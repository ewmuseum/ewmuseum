From Some Things That Are Good Sometimes Turns Evil

23 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/11 Evil eyes lurk over the Thunder ranch as they watch the Thunders come home from picking Jimmy up at the airport after another unsuccessful night at World Wide. The binoculars go down and the close up vision of Jimmy exiting his SUV with his family following, turn into unclear images caused by the distance that separate the evil eyes from the Thunder family. It’s bad enough that Jimmy can’t pull a win and is enduring, clearly, the worst losing streak of his illustrious career, but he now has an evil presence around him. The worst part about it all is that Jimmy has no idea……yet. Slowly lifting a cell phone to his ear, the lurker says in a soft deep tone, “I can see them. What do you want me to do?” “Nothing, just make your way back here, we need to discuss a few things.” A voice said in return. The lurker turns the ignition on his vehicle and slowly pulls away from his vantage point, reluctantly leaving the Thunder family entrenched inside the sanctuary of their ranch house. Inside the ranch house, Jimmy is soaking his head under a hot, steamy shower. Not really needing a shower for his own personal hygiene, the time is instead used for Jimmy to regroup his thoughts and think of how he can shake that wretched monkey off his back and finally get a win at World Wide. After his shower, Jimmy dries off and gets dressed into some comfortable clothing. He takes a seat next to his bed and opens a drawer to find a type of after shave but instead comes across a medallion he use to wear. This was no ordinary medallion either. It was on the larger side and had a really bright silver pattern weaving through it, enclosed with an equally bright silver rim and the letters “LTL” inscribed on it. “I’ve always meant to ask you about that medallion.” Emma mentioned when she unexpectedly walked through the bedroom door. Jimmy put the medallion around his neck and has another look before saying, “This was something that I won in a match against Chris Neptune. He had won it from it’s original Thomas Dsousa, who had it made specially for him.” Jimmy explained. “Legend has it that Thomas had it made after his girlfriend had died in a car wreck. To him, this was a symbol of the struggles he encountered after her death. It was suppose to help him heal and deal with what ever life threw at him. He called it The Demon Jewel” He added while looking at the medallion. “I started wearing it soon after I won it from Chris Neptune and it started my three year undefeated streak.” He said. “I’m starting to wonder if I wear it again that it might change my luck, you know?” He ends with while looking up at Emma. Emma smiles and walks towards him and gives him a hug before saying, “Baby, I don’t really care if you win or lose, I just want you to be healthy.” “It matters to me if I win or lose.” Jimmy quickly replied. Not understanding Jimmy‘s way of thinking, Emma asks “Why would it?” “When I retired, people thought of me as a winner, I left an awesome legacy, my name meant something.” Jimmy explained. “If I keep losing everything I achieved and did will mean nothing because everyone will remember me as the loser who couldn’t do anything but lose at GCW. He ended with frustration in his tone. Several hours had passed since the lurker was ordered back to where his leader is currently situated. An old, derelict looking detached home with dirty yellow bed sheets covering all the windows. The lurker walks into the home and sees his two friends sitting on an old raggedy green couch. At that point, the leader walks in and all four men are in the same room. The strange things about this situation is that the four men in the room are the men who are devising an evil plot to be perpetrated against Jimmy Thunder and his family are in fact known to Jimmy. The two sitting on the old raggedy green couch are former Bloodbox superstars, the imbecilic pair of Christopher Stryfe and Bryan Deas. The evil lurker, non other than Anthony Taylor and the leader, the one and only, Ian Cross. These four men competed with Jimmy at a company called Bloodbox where Jimmy was the main attraction there. For months, Ian Cross and Anthony Taylor raged war with Jimmy for the Bloodbox World Title and for months Jimmy was able to fight off the pair and hold a mortgage on the World Title. Once Bloodbox had closed down, Jimmy thought that everyone else, like him, had moved onto greener pastures. Everyone but Deas, Stryfe, Taylor and Cross, that is. Holding a grudge like a woman scorned, the former Bloodboxers had blue printed a plan to bring down Jimmy in a way that would wound and scar him for life. Much like the scars they bare from the embarrassing way Jimmy had dominated them at Bloodbox. Unfortunately for them, their stocks as free agent wrestlers dropped when Bloodbox had closed and they lay the blame squarely on Jimmy. “What’s it like out there?” Cross asks. “Perfect” replies Taylor with a sinister smile. “Alright, we’re at the last stage. We’ve planned way too long now to turn back.” Cross declared. “The way I see it, Thunder has literally cost us hundreds of thousands in lost potential.” Cross says as he pauses and nods while lips turn in. “It’s time that son of a bitch pays the Piper.” He ends with while the others show how much they like what they’re hearing by smiling and looking at each other as Cross’ comments give their adrenaline a huge kick start. Meanwhile, back at the Thunder ranch, Jimmy is sitting with his young daughter and reading her a book. With all the time he has spent on the road lately and also being so engrossed in his own world, Jimmy realized that he has totally neglected to spend any time with his precious little girl. A vibrant brown haired, brown eyed little girl, Chloe posses the raw energy and enthusiasm that Jimmy is starting to realize, is feeding his drive to succeed in his return to GCW. Finally falling asleep, Jimmy covers his little girl and gives her a kiss before turning her light out. Jimmy heads towards the kitchen and finds Emma sitting down at the table, drinking a hot coffee and reading a magazine. Hearing Jimmy walk in, she looks up and smiles at him before saying, “Do you realize how cute it is watching you read to your daughter.” Feeling himself blush slightly, Jimmy smirks and says, “I really haven’t spent much time with her lately and I missed my time with her.” “Let’s plan a vacation some time soon?” Emma suggested as Jimmy takes a seat opposite her. “Where would you like to go?” Jimmy asks. “I want to go somewhere the four of us will enjoy.” She replied then adds. “Let me think about some places and we’ll talk about it another time.” “Ok, let me know and we’ll plan something.” Replies Jimmy. Turning around in his chair and about to stand, he says “I’m going to go watch World Wide 143 before going to bed.” Emma quickly stands up before Jimmy and walks over to him and as she is about to sit on his lap she says, “Not so fast there mister.” She sits in his lap and puts her arms around his shoulders and smiles at him. “I’ve got some plans for Thunder From Down Under.” Jimmy smiles and asks, “What’s that baby?” “You, me and a nice hot tub with some music and your favorite chilled Merlot.” Emma explains just before Jimmy picks her up and starts walking towards their bathroom with a smile on his face as the scene fades. The next afternoon, Jimmy is found finishing up his daily work out. Even though he still hasn’t had a win, his routine stays the same. For someone who hasn’t had a win since returning to active competition last month, Jimmy is feeling strangely confident that he can beat Micah St.Clair. Although his workout routine stays the same, his mental approach to this match changes. The last few weeks the he has come to World Wide with a hint of self doubt and second guessing himself. This week his plan is to arrive at the arena, loosen up and just let it come out. Sooner or later these cob webs will blow out and a win will happen. Once that win happens, Jimmy is confident the rest will fall into place. “The show is almost on baby.” Emma announced as she stuck her head inside the gym door. “What show?” Jimmy asks with a curious tone while wiping the perspiration off his body. “The interview you did with ESPN.” Emma answered. “Ok baby, I’ll take a quick shower here and head on over.” Replied Jimmy. After taking his shower, Jimmy heads over to the house and finds Junior and Chloe seated on one lounge while Emma is on her own sitting on another separate. “Has it started yet?” Jimmy asks while taking a seat next to Emma. “It‘s just about to start any second.” Junior replies while taking a drink from his soda can. John Buccigross: The world of professional wrestling is a brutal and unforgiving sport. There are many low lights and highlights in a sport where men are men and wimps are, let’s just say, shown the door. Wrestlers sometimes taste the sweetness of success and at other times experience the sour taste of failure. GCW’s Jimmy Thunder is one individual who has experienced an emotional roller coaster during his time as a professional wrestler. He has experienced the highs as a world champion and the lows of being a widower. Tom Rinaldi traveled out to Murphy Idaho and had a rare, sit down interview that is revealing and shows you a side to Jimmy Thunder, you thought you’d never see. Tom Rinaldi: In the outskirts of Boise Idaho, a small town called Murphy is the residence of one of the gentlest giants you will ever meet but also one of the most dangerous. Inside this ranch type compound lives Global Championship Wrestling’s Jimmy Thunder and his family. Everyone just knows Jimmy Thunder as the wrestler and the showman. Very few get a chance to look inside the private life that drives one of the most successful wrestlers to ever grace the squared circle. In a rare and revealing interview, Jimmy will make you laugh and make you cry. He’ll let you inside his private life and share his elation and his sadness. What you’re about to watch is the raw side of Jimmy Thunder. TR: First of all I want to thank you for sitting down with me today and giving this interview. You’ve agreed to a tell all interview, now these types of interviews are rare for you, why’s that Jimmy? Jimmy Thunder: I’m a fiercely private person Tom. I don’t mind talking about what I’m doing in the wrestling business but when it comes to answering questions which involve my personal life, I kind of like to keep that personal. TR: You live in a tranquil ranch just outside the Boise city limits which possesses all the quirks of modern living. You have some expensive toys so financially you seem to be doing alright for yourself. JT: I guess I’m doing ok, let’s just say that I’m lucky enough where I wont have to worry about living on the streets tomorrow. TR: You have your second wife Emma, a son to your first marriage and a beautiful little girl to your second marriage. Now everyone that I talk to in the industry say that they’re always on the road, partying and see their family on the rare occasions where you’re always with your family and party with the rest of the roster on a rare occasion. Would it be fare to say that you’re showing your age there? JT: HA!! HA!! HA!! Hardly! The thing for me is that my family comes first, second and third. Anything after that doesn’t really matter. My son is a college student learning medicine and I spend the majority of my free time with my wife and little girl. They keep my feet on the ground and just fill my body with youth. I sometimes forget that I’m get along in years. When I’m with my daughter she just makes me feel so alive inside and when I see her accomplishing new things in her life it just makes me feel like I’m the luckiest guy in the world. TR: Let’s talk about your first wife here. Now for the viewers I want to remind them that you have never spoken publicly about her till now. Pick up this story if you will and let us know what happens. You and your brother-in-law Theodoric Solomon had just finished a major paper-view event at Dynasty Wrestling Alliance where you defended your World Title. You and Theo just landed in Boise and a family member meets you at the airport. What happens next? JT (Pausing and looking down while blinking fast and biting down on his bottom lip): Well, both Theo and I were told by a family member that my wife Aurora was involved in an auto accident with a drunk driver. Theo was Aurora’s brother, anyway, he and I were then rushed to an awaiting car and we were given a police escort to St. Luke’s. I have always tried to do charity work for the police and I still do, maybe more now than ever but back then when they had heard that it was Aurora in the accident they couldn’t have done anything more for me. Anyway, I was quickly taken to her room where she laid and there with all these tubes coming out of her. I sat by her side for three days and three nights and on the third night a doctor had explained to me that there was no brain activity and that there was no chance of recovery. He ahhhh (pausing to fight back tears) suggested we turned off her life support system. TR: Did you? JT: No, not at that time. Theo was with me and as much as I fought the decision he made me realize that it was the only option. We agreed to do it the next morning, mainly because my son was still so young and he was emotionally drained from it all and we thought that it was best he had a good night sleep. The next morning we called family members and gave them all a chance to say goodbye to her. TR: What was the fall out from all that? I mean, you suffered a tremendous loss and you also had your young son to consider, how do you pick not only yourself up but also your son as well? JT: They were my darkest days, no doubt about it. I was lucky enough that the company I was working for gave me all the time and space I needed. I voluntarily gave up the World Title, which the company said I didn’t have to but I felt that I did. I took six months off, Junior and I spent a lot of time together, we laughed and we cried but we stuck together and we managed to get through it. TR: You also suffered another tragedy, this time a financial one. What happened there? JT: At the time I was retired and was doing a few little things here and there just to keep myself from being bored. Anyway, I went to Harley Davidson spare parts place to get a few things that I needed when my cards didn’t work. I went to the bank and found out that the IRS had put a freeze on my accounts. Oh man, if I could explain to you the shock that was running through me it was unbelievable. So I rode on down to my accountant, a guy I knew for many years and someone I trusted. I got to his offices and found a police car there with a large group of reporters surrounding the front door way. He was being escorted to an the police car, under arrest, for fraud and embezzlement. I found out he used all the money I had in a real estate development that had gone belly up. As a result I lost every penny I had and the IRS were in the process of taking possession of my house and basically leaving me with nothing but my son and a toothbrush. Thankfully, I managed to do a deal with the IRS and I got to keep my house and belongings. TR: It was then you decided to go back to the ring, right? JT: As part of the condition of the deal I had done with the IRS I had to make some substantial payments and the only way I could make some serious money was to get back in the ring. When I decided to do that I had outlined which companies I wanted to start negotiating with and wasn’t really sure on what kind of reception I would get considering I had been out of the spotlight for such a long time. I was expecting to work some independent circuits and some low budget companies but I couldn’t believe it when I got a call from GCW’s Steven Caldera himself! To cut a long story short, I met up with him, he offered me a decent contract and the rest, as people would say, is history. I have to say that Steven Caldera and the GCW family have been nothing but great to me and my family. TR: Your first run at GCW was a successful one, wasn’t it? JT: You can say that again. It was probably the most unbelievable run I have ever had. Even though I had won world titles at other promotions, GCW back then was regarded as one of the very few companies that only employed the elite. To be winning major singles titles and mixing it with guys like LeStatt Knight and Jordan White, back then were regarded as probably the best wrestlers on the planet, was a real buzz for me. TR: Ok Jimmy, I’ll set the scene and I’ll get you to take over, you’re in Australia and it’s the day of the Tsunami pay-per-view. You’re scheduled to take on Shaman for the US Title and you’re in your hotel room, what happens from there??? JT: What had happened was I arrived at the hotel a few days before the Tsunami show and word had gotten out that I was staying there. In a matter of hours the crowd outside built from a few people to a few thousand people. It got to a point where the hotel security couldn’t handle them and had to call in GCW officials who booked the room and also the local police. The day before Tsunami a senior GCW official suggest to Steven Caldera that they take full advantage of the pandemonium and use it to hype up the match between Shaman and I. So Steven Caldera decided to turn my ring entrance into the biggest one in history and organized a convertible for me to sit in and drive me to the arena. I have to say that the adrenaline was pumping through me like never before. There was no way I was going to leave Sydney without that US Belt. TR: What’s the difference between your first run in GCW and your second run? JT: Well there was no doubt that the first time I was at GCW it was one of the leading promotions in the world. Today, GCW is the leading promotion in the world. No other promotion comes near it. All the elite are here, the best of the best. Before I was squaring off with talented individuals now I’m dealing with guys who are the best at what they do. A lot has changed between my first run and my second run, the competition is a lot more fierce, there’s and unbelievable amount of people on waiting lists for a spot and to give you a clearer answer, back during the Tsunami show, Jay Terror was just a TV Champion and I had won the US title, now Jay Terror is the World Champion and I’m just a chump who cant win a match at the moment, so what does that tell you Hahahahhahahaha!!! TR: We touched on your personal side but what we failed to mention and this is another thing you don’t really talk about much is your tireless charity work that you do. What is it that drives you to do all this kind of work away from any cameras and fanfare? JT: Before I started out in the wrestling business I barely had a dime to my name. How I ended up like that will be explained in my book that will be out soon. Anyway, I landed a job as bouncer at a local Boise night club. While I was working there I was basically sleeping at friend’s houses, just going from house to house to take a shower and get a decent meal. The wrestling business gave so much to me and my family and the best way I knew to pay it back was to do some good in the world and try and help out people who are struggling with various issues in their life. Issues like financial, health, mental or just to put a smile on someone’s face. When I can make someone smile I cant help but feel I’m paying back, in a small way, the good fortune that came my way. TR: What’s next in the life of Jimmy Thunder? JT: To win a match at GCW hahahahaha!!! TR: HA HA HA!!! JT: No, seriously, at first I just wanted to have some fun but now I want to leave a legacy for anyone else who comes to GCW and show them that with dedication and hard work, anything is possible. I really love being in the GCW and when I finally do retire I want to stay involved with the company in some form. TR: Jimmy, I just want to thank you for this interview, you had every opportunity to balk at some of the questions I asked and you answered them all. You’re not only a credit to the wrestling business and this city but also to your family and yourself. JT: Thanks so much, I appreciate your kind words. I had to open up because that is what my book is all about so I thought that this interview was as good as any to start. TR: Thanks again. “That son of bitch!” Snarls Ian Cross. “Look at him, being painted like a fucking martyr!” He says. He throws a can of beer at the television and yells out, “What about the lives and careers he’s destroyed you fucking idiots!” He looks to Anthony Taylor while Deas and Stryfe are playing their X-Box in the other room. Cross leans towards Taylor and says in a soft voice “I’m really relying on you for this plan to be executed perfectly.” Taylor nods while listening. “Those two nitwits in there have no concept of how one plus one equals two. All I need them to do is for their brain to clearly and correctly tell their mouths to explain to you and me what it is exactly they see with their own eyes, then everything will work out.” Cross explains. “I don’t need them to think, move or do anything, just tell us what they see, surely that isn’t too hard for them.” He adds. “I’ll make sure everything goes to plan.” Taylor declares. “Thunder has to pay for what he’s done to us and pay he will.” He says with a smile. “I’m thinking we start this sooner rather than later.” Cross suggests. “I agree” Taylor says while smiling and nodding. The scene fades.
