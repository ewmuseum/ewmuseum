Tacky-Tachyons Part 2

1 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/11 Previously on "One Life to Cruz" Bradley was feeling down about the current state of his career, and began to obsess over past matches and former glory. His cousin on his Mother’s side Slider, decided to go give Bradley a nice gift he purchased from a neighbors estate sale, all in the hopes to cheer Bradley up, but what Slider thought was a nice piece of Sci-Fi memorabilia ended up being an actual Tachyon Emitter. Slider left to pick up some takeout after warning his cousin of dangers of exposing the emitter to any radiation, but as always the troublemaker Bradley ignored Slider’s warning by turning on the emitter and placing into the microwave set on popcorn. The radiation caused the tachyon Emitter to transport Bradley to a unknown location where he was confronted by a unknown voice. Where is Cruz? Who is the Mysterious Voice? Will Cruz make it home? Will Slider remember to get extra napkins? Some of this and so much more on "One Life to Cruz: Tacky-Tachyons! Part 2 : Inter-Dimensional Boogaloo" ----------- Cruz is sitting down in the dirt cross legged with his head propped up by his arm as his chin rests in the palm of his hand. Brad’s face reflects one of boredom. "So you’ve decided to just sit there and sulk?" Brad rolls his eyes hearing the mysterious voice. Brad: "Yes, I’ve decided to sulk." "...and you believe this course of inaction will benefit you how?" Brad: "I suppose it doesn’t much benefit me, but it doesn’t indulging you in anyway, so it has that going for it." "Oh my stars and garters, how quickly you’ve become so cold and evasive." Brad: "I’m evasive? You won’t even tell me who you are." "I told you, I’m…" Brad: "Yes, yes you’re ‘You’!" "Me." Brad: "Whatever! Do you at least have a name?" "My kind has no use for labels." Brad: "Your kind being?" "Unlabeled…" Brad: "…oh, hey, by the way… who’s on first?" "Pardon?" Brad lets out a sigh. Brad: "Never mind. Can you at least tell me what the hell you are?" "I am non-corporeal sentient entity existing in several separate dimensional planes. I hope that answers your question." Brad simply nods. Brad: "Yep, absolutely." "Really?" Brad: "Sure, surprised?" "A little, how did you-" Brad: "I watched a lot of Star Trek as a kid. You tend to catch on to the cliche buzz words that make up cheap science fiction. Not to say that you’re cheap or cliche." "Of Course not, I understand." Brad: "So why don’t you believe in names?" "Understand my kind had nothing against assigning designation, but we really had no use for it." Brad puts on his trademark smirk as he tries to emotionally cut into mysterious being. Brad: "You know, I think the real story is that mommy and daddy just didn’t love you enough to be bothered to give you a name." "Love did not factor into this. Besides labels are far more adept to telling the contents of a jar of preserves, then they are at telling you the contents of a person. Besides naming someone is far crueler then leaving them nameless." Brad raises and eyebrow with curiosity. Brad: "How so?" "Names have deeper meanings. They have cultural and often religious significance. Some names are humble and common, while others are bold and regal. A name is something that should sum up a person perfectly after they developed a unique personality or an age of enlightened, but instead your species decides the name at birth, often before the child has even drawn its first breath and shoehorning its deeper meaning upon a life form that is not yet even able to open its eyes much less understand its place in the world. Can you explain that?" Brad thinks a moment and simply replies. Brad: "Children are like pets. You name them because eventually you’ll have to scream their names across a crowed park …because it’s almost dinner time and you want to get home before "Hot for Cleveland" comes on, and you’re too cheap to spring for a DVR. In the end you name what you want to be able to call back… I.E. why your parent’s didn’t name you… because they didn’t love you." The voice chuckles as it responses "My kind doesn’t form bonds with our parents, and we never resorted to shouting." Brad: "But how does your kind distinguish who they’re talking to?" "Well we don’t. My kind does not traditionally verbalize messages to one another. We communicate through strong sensations or feelings; your kind may know them as premonitions." Brad: "So you translate these feeling into words, into ideas?" "No translation, there is only feeling and then understanding." Bradley rises to his feet and looks to the sky as he speaks. His eyes once again catch a glimpse the dark spirally clouds. He can’t help to feel as though he was staring at badly produced green screen effects. Brad: "Speaking of understanding… where am I?" "Nowh-" Brad: "-and don’t say nowhere!" "There is no better way to simplify the answer." Brad: "Well then get a little complicated." "You are the product of your reality, and somehow you have removed yourself from your reality and came here." Brad: "So I’m in another reality, another dimension?" "Not quite…" Brad: "What do you mean?" "Your more in-between dimensions, caught between open empty space separating one reality from the other, but it’s not entirely empty. You see each reality produces large amounts of energy that radiates and where we are positioned is right middle of the largest current of energy. Now this energy is the fuel that makes a reality possible. Think of it as a chimney full of wood and no match. This wood is potential unused energy, at least until you the match stumbled your way in." Brad: "I created all this? I mean I always thought I was rather god like, but creating my own reality is a bit much. How is this possible?" "Anything could have triggered it. The energy burst during your arrival, a strong burst radiation or partials, it could also be triggered by electrical impulses or nervous system." Brad takes this all in trying make heads or tails of, but latches on to a part of the mysterious beings statement: ‘Partials or Radiation’. Brad suddenly blurts out under his breath. Brad: "Tachyons… could tachyons do this?" "Tachyons?" Brad begins to think snapping his fingers trying to grabs the subject matter. Brad: "What did Captain Picard say Tachyons were… ‘something something’ that was able to move forward and/or backwards passed the speed of light." "Particles." Brad: "Could that have caused this?" "Well I suppose it could, but it would have needed to be severely aggravated by an outside force." Bradley grins and innocent grin as he lets what he knows slip. Brad: "What about microwaves?" "Definitely.… but who would be dumb enough to… oh." Brad: "Yeah." "May I ask why?" Brad: "I don't know… looked like a over sized novelty paperweight. I thought It was the worst gift ever! I didn’t know it was actually a Tachyon-" Brad’s eyes widen as his jaw nearly drops. Brad: "The Emitter!" Bradley quickly runs around searching frantically for the small little glowing pyramid. "What are you looking for?" Brad: "For my ticket home, the Tachyon Emitter!" "Perhaps you could describe it, and I could help." Bradley is still frantically searching for his ticket home as he describes the object to mysterious voice. Brad: "It’s not really big. It’s small enough to fit in a microwave. It glows blue, made of metal and glass. Shaped like a pyramid." "Like this." Bradley looks up to the tachyon Emitter floating a ways above him. Bradley’s face lights up as he reaches for it, but his hand is nowhere near reaching it. Brad: "Yes, that’s it! Bring her down a little lower." Bradley continues to reach, but Tachyon Emitter just floats there suspended in air. Brad: "Hey come on, I’m waiting. Help me out." "I am sorry, but no." Brad looks angry and annoyed. Brad: "… and why the hell not?" "Do you know how often I get company?" Brad: "With your winning hospitality? I bet all the time!" "No… hardly ever… the only consolation is that they usually never leave." Brad: "...but I can right?." "No…" Brad: "Listen, I have to go back. I have a fiancee, an idiot cousin, his equally idiotic best friend, a job and a life. I’m asking, please… let me go." "No." Brad: "Okay, you formless nameless inter-dimensional piece of shit! I’m no longer asking, I am telling you to GIVE ME THE GODDAMN EMITTER, NOW!" The mysterious voice replies with a roar. "NO!" The being’s voice knocks Bradley back. He pulls himself back together, recomposing himself. Brad: "What do you want?" "I want what you clearly want, but for myself." Brad: "Fine, come with me." "It’s not that simple" Brad: "It never is, but we can work something out." "Alright, I want to learn as much as I can about you." Brad: "Alright…" "Make it through that… and I’ll consider giving you the emitter." Brad: "…and if I don’t make it through?" The mysterious voice begins to chuckle. "Then you stay here… and I go, in your body." To be continued.
