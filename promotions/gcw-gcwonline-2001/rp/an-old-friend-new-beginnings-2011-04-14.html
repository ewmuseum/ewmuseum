An Old Friend & New Beginnings

14 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/14/11 So this is it. Back in the high life again. The sweet pleasure of achy muscles and the aroma of sweaty wrestling gear as a group of professional athletes’ move from one arena to another entertaining the millions of fans who support the paycheques. Daniel Cruz couldn’t help to think of the upcoming challenges he was about to take on since he has signed on with GCW. Was he running low on money? No. Was trying to regain the feeling of stepping into the spotlight again? Maybe. Or was he just crazy? Most likely. It was somewhat ironic that "The Canadian Icon" was having his debut match with GCW in America’s Capital. There was no doubt in Daniel Cruz’s mind that when he walked out in front of the live crowd at the National Mall that he was going to be instantly targeted with hatred from the American GCW fans. He didn’t mind though. Daniel Cruz was all to use to being public enemy #1 when he walked out in front of an American wrestling crowd. After all, Cruz spent 90% of his career wrestling in front of an American audience. This crowd in Washington would be no different. Daniel Cruz was mentally prepared and ready for his upcoming challenge. But before Daniel Cruz made his flight out to DC, he had one very important stop to make. Air Canada flight AC8702 was just getting ready to land in Edmonton Alberta. Daniel Cruz was sitting in the first class section trying to send some quick emails before the stewardess would tell him it was time to put away his laptop. "….Sir? Could you…." "I know I know" Daniel Cruz said with a courteous grin as he glanced at the Stewardess. Cruz folded down his laptop and stored it under his seat. He finished the last sip of his scotch on the rocks as he looked out the window of the airplane. As the clouds started to disappear, the tiny view of trees displayed below him as the Captain came across the intercom. "Ladies and Gentlemen this is your Captain speaking. We are about to make our descend down to ground. Please keep your seatbelts buckled at all times until the light above you is turned off. Also please turn off all electronic devices for the remainder of our descend. Thank you for flying Air Canada and enjoy your stay in Edmonton Alberta". This was it. Daniel Cruz’s last stop before he took the next flight out to Washington DC. You may wonder why Daniel Cruz was making a quick stop to Edmonton Alberta before he flew out to Washington? One simple reason. Eddie Gilbert resided in Edmonton Alberta Canada. When Daniel Cruz first started out in the professional wrestling industry, he did much training in Toronto and Calgary. While in Calgary, Daniel Cruz met his mentor, "Ruthless" Eddie Gilbert. Gilbert was one of the stiffest hard knocks on the Canadian Wrestling circuit. But what impressed Cruz so much about Gilbert over the years was his technical wrestling ability. Daniel Cruz admired and learned so much from Gilbert over the years. They continued to remain friends as the years past. In 2005, Eddie Gilbert relocated to Edmonton after retiring from the wrestling world at the age of 44. However, Gilbert hasn’t totally walked away from the wrestling business. Gilbert now operates a seasonal wrestling school for all the juniors out there who have the dreams of being on top of the mountain in wrestling. That’s why Daniel Cruz was heading to see his long time friend. The plan was to meet with the students and share some advice while having a good workout with the gang to shake off any last minute ring rust before his GCW debut against Dynasty. The plane eventually landed on the runway and made its way over to the terminal. As Daniel Cruz gathered his belongings and walked off the plane he couldn’t help but to feel adrenalin run through his body. He was minutes away from seeing a great friend from his past. As the passengers walking ahead of Cruz ran into the arms of their family and friends who waited for them by the terminal gate, Cruz walked into the center of the Terminal looking at the surroundings around him. There was no sign of Eddie Gilbert. "….Hmm… looks like your starting to go a little thin on top their kid…" A thick hand slammed down on Daniel Cruz’s left shoulder. Cruz quickly turned his head as he heard the arrogant remark that was made to him. Cruz smiled once he seen Eddie Gilbert standing behind him with a cocky smirk on his face. "Starting to go a little thin on top?? Well it’s better than having none at all like you, you old bastard!" The two shared a loud laugh as they gave each other a quick hug as passengers walked by them. "It’s great to see you man. Come on, the truck is outside" said Gilbert as he patted Cruz on the side of his face with a gentle tap. "Do you need any help with your bags princess?" mocked Gilbert "Shut up!" laughed Cruz as the two began their exit out of the airport. Daniel Cruz and Eddie Gilbert shared a few laughs as they made their way to the parking garage of the Edmonton International Airport. They approached Eddie Gilbert’s aqua blue Cadillac SRX. "Nice rig" said Cruz as he threw his bags in the back. "But does it come in a man’s color?" Trying to hide his laughter Gilbert replies "Boyyyy your looking for it already aren’t yah?" The two both shared another laugh as they hopped into the Cadillac and began to drive out of the parking garage. "I got to ask you a question" said Gilbert as he stopped for a traffic light. "Here we go…. What is it?" replied Cruz "Are you frigging nuts? I thought you were done with this game? When you emailed me back in February telling me that you were in talks with GCW, I thought you were carrying on! I mean you’re definitely still in your prime… but what ever happened to getting out early? Reaching success, enjoying the success, and then leaving it behind? You are younger than me, but I still didn’t think you were serious about going back to the sport." "A lot younger than you" replied Cruz sarcastically with a smile. "Don’t get smart!" said Gilbert as he shot Cruz with an evil look. As they cruised along the streets of Edmonton, Daniel Cruz looked out the window watching the trees and buildings quickly zip by. "I don’t know" said Cruz. "I couldn’t take it anymore. The time was starting to become too long and boring for me. All the days just blended together. Between constantly watching wrestling on television and recalling all the success and fun I had a few years ago, I decided I had to go back before I lost my sanity. I still have lots of great years ahead. I still feel great. I want to go for another run. Don’t you ever feel that way?" "HELL NO!" shouted Gilbert as both began to laugh out loud. "Don’t get me wrong. I loved every minute of it. But the days of going on the road and banging myself up again are long gone my friend. I had my success and I had my fun, but it’s MY personal time now. I got money in the bank, the wrestling school keeps me busy and I got a ranch up north that I can escape to whenever I want. If I never seen the inside of an airplane again, fine by me!". "Yeah but I’m sure a lot of the fans would love to see you back again someday Eddie. You were one of the best!" "And that’s why you’re here right?" "Exactly" said Cruz as he smiled. The two continued to drive throughout the city heading towards ‘Eddie Gilberts: Extreme Dream Wrestling School’. They continued to talk away about funny stories from their past and laid out the plans for when they arrive at the Wrestling School. Although it has been a few years since Cruz and Gilbert seen each other they just picked right up where they left off like they always do. After forty minutes of straight driving, Gilbert and Cruz arrive at Gilbert’s wrestling school. The building resembled the shape of an old airplane hangar from the 1940’s. Although it’s an older building the outside exterior appears to be completely renovated. Cream yellow vinyl siding covered the building along with new vinyl windows and roofing shingles. However even though it was an old building that looks semi redone, Cruz had to throw in his arrogant remark to his old friend. He stared at the building as he got out of the car and slammed the passenger door. Casually throwing the strap to his laptop briefcase over his shoulder, Cruz says. "This is it? This is where you take money off innocent kids and train them to become superstars? I’ve seen homeless shelters that were in better shape than this!" "Keep running your mouth!" warned Gilbert as he grabbed Cruz’s luggage from the back and slammed down the trunk door. "This building may look old, but I fixed it up properly to run a legitimate training school here my friend" claimed Gilbert. "It took me three years to fix this place up the way I want it. It may be big and old but only the tough can survive here. Not just any punk comes off the road with the dream of becoming pro. I put them through every damn vigorous test known to man. Just like I’m going to put you through today!". "I look forward to it" said Cruz as he squinted his eyes from the blinding sunlight. "So this is where it all takes place! How is the class looking this year?" he asked. "Too hard to tell at this point" responded Gilbert as both men held onto Cruz’s luggage staring at the building from the parking lot. "We have a group of 12 right now but I expect that to decrease down to 8 next week once we up the cardio and strength training. Then once we start working them stiff in the ring and show them the ropes I expect that 8 to be cut down to 4. Many apply to be here but only a few last until the end. You’ve seen it all before. You know how it is" stated Gilbert. "Yup… I sure do!" chuckled Cruz as he looked down at the ground scuffing his shoe on the pavement. "Come on!" said Gilbert as nudged Cruz in the leg with his luggage bag. "Come on in and meet the gang. Everyone is waiting for you. Some of them are exactly excited about meeting you. Can you believe that? They said they are huge fans. Makes me sick!" "No need of getting jealous now" laughed Cruz. "I’m sure they all knew about your wrestling career too. I bet their grandfathers were probably huge fans!" Gilbert immediately shoved Cruz’s luggage bag into Cruz’s chest as both men laughed and walked into the building. As soon as Gilbert opened the door, Cruz was welcomed with a round of applause from the students and staff of Eddie Gilbert’s wrestling school. Cruz smiled and politely nodded his head in appreciation as he made eye contact with all the students who were so happy to meet him. Once the applause stopped Cruz took the time to shake hands with each student and allow them to introduce themselves. "It’s really great to meet you guys" stated Cruz. "I know some days can be very tough. I know what its like to workout to the point where you vomit in the washroom. I know how it feels to be twisted and locked into submissions to the point where you lose feeling in your limbs for hours or even days. This is no cake walk but you’ve all made the first correct decision in your careers. That decision was putting your future in the hands of this man" Cruz pointed to Eddie Gilbert who never made any facial expression. He just kept his eyes locked on the students standing in front of him. Daniel Cruz continued his speech. "Now don’t get me wrong. I know Eddie can be a real hard ass. So if he gives you any trouble, just let me know and I will deal with him myself." said Cruz as the gang of students laughed. Eddie just rolled his eyes and chuckled as he let Cruz finish his speech. "As you all may already know, I have decided to return to this business because I just love it way to much. This sport has given me too much to walk away now. And like you guys standing in front of me, I have an up hill climb as well. I need to prove myself to each and every person on the GCW roster that Daniel Cruz is the Canadian Icon and the best technical wrestler that ever graced the squared circle." A loud cheer came from the group of students as they smiled and began applauding once again. Cruz nodded his head again to the guys before holding his hand in the air to continue the speech. "Now I will be joining in on your session today. Both as a student and as a trainer. At World Wide 148 I plan on introducing the Canadian Icon to Dynasty. This Rampage tournament is the perfect way for me re-launch my career and make a huge impact in the GCW locker room. I refuse to walk away from this empty handed. You’re going to see Daniel Cruz in the greatest shape he has ever been before. And I look forward to working with you guys while I make my way to the top." Once again the group began an applause as Daniel Cruz grinned and nodded as to signal that he was finished. That’s when Eddie Gilbert jumped in. "ALRIGHT LADIES. Enough listening to this chump talking about his future goals of GCW. We all know he’s going to get his ass kicked anyways." The group laughed as Cruz chuckled and shook his head. Gilbert smiled as he slapped Cruz on the back and gave the group their instructions. "Group A.. I want to see you guys cycle some cardio. Group B… get in the ring." The group of students turned around and headed for their assigned areas. Daniel Cruz and Eddie Gilbert watched them walk away. "Looks like a fine crew" said Cruz. "I’m not sure about the guy in the red bandana though. I know I just met the guy but he looks like he was exhausted. They only just started the workout session a few minutes ago didn’t they?" "You’re totally right" replied Gilbert. "The guy talks big but he has no heart. I constantly catch him taking breaks and falling behind on all exercises. He looks too awkward in the ring. He wrestles like a 90 year old woman." "He’s definitely learning everything he knows from you then!" said Cruz as he laughed. "Alright big mouth. You’re going to pay for all those remarks. You said you wanted to work off any last minute ring rust? Well here’s your chance cowgirl. Locker rooms are down the hall past the ring. Go get changed and meet me in the ring. That’s of course if your ready?!" "Oh yeah" said Cruz assertively nodding his head. "…… I’m ready!"
