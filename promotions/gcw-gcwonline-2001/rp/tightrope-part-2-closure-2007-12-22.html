Tightrope (Part 2: Closure)

So whatever tomorrow brings I'll be there with open arms and open eyes - "Drive" by Incubus ---- Act Four: The Devil's Advocate ---- Click... Click... Click... An avalanche of memories flooded her mind as she walked down the halls. It was now so long ago it almost seemed like a different lifetime entirely. Things were so different then... her future seemed almost preplanned into a freefall of choices that weren't hers. Back then the hallways were so warm though, not like now. Now they were dark and cold, almost devoid of all compassion and humanity. Much like the look in his icy cadet blue eyes. How fitting that even the business he loved more than anything would seem to have had it's soul sucked from it by his devouring personality. Every hallway was grand, giving you a sense of wealth and accomplishment. Gloating to you with their meaningless and shallow existence as you marched down their marble lined corridors. Clinching her black leather bomber jacket closer to her body, her surroundings sent a chill down her spine. Everything about him did these days. The transformation from the young vibrant revolutionary she met five years earlier to the leper messiah he was now was startling as she stopped to take a look at his picture. Just his image made a sneer of disdain cross her lips. This was probably the last place on earth she wanted to be... but she had to know the truth. It was time she stopped putting off the obvious and get the answers for questions that had been on her mind ever since the blaze that took the love of her life. Everything was so unnatural. There had to be something more than an "electrical fire", and Christian Ulrich Zenith was the only man on earth she knew that had the resources to get away with something as extravagant as a preplanned arson. Pausing at the door she began to reach out, but hesitated halfway through the action. Her hand hanging there as she took a deep breath, knowing very well that evil incarnate resided on the other side of the door. Her heart skipped a beat as she touched the cold metal doorknob and turned. There was no turning back now as the door alarmed her existence to everyone around with a high pitched squeak. Cerulean blue eyes navigated around the dimly lit room in which she spent much of her early adulthood. Slowly turning to her right she could see his silhouette in the moonlight as he looked out the window that encompassed the entire back wall of the office. Even though he knew she was there he didn't bother to turn around, he just continued to look across the city scape before him. It was obvious that even evil had a lot of things on its mind. Vivica J. Valentine was just one of them. "I knew that you'd come back here before too long..." Christian Zenith spoke as Vivica opened her mouth, almost as if he knew what she was about to say, "Ever since I heard the news about Markus Blaire." "What do you know about Markus?" Vivica asked him, almost as if she was interrogating him. "I know that he was killed in a fire," Christian spoke as he turned around... his cadet blue eyes pierced into her soul as he continued, "I'd almost say it was karma if I believed in that kind of thing. While it's unfortunate that it had to happen, I can't help but to think it was deserved wasn't it Miss Valentine?" "You did it didn't you?" She snarled back as he casually paced back and forth in front of the window. "I didn't do a damn thing." Christian responded immediately, "I was sitting right here in my office at the time of the fire. I read a report on it from a Las Vegas news site. Although I'm sure blaming me made you sleep better at night didn't it Viv? That he was stolen from you by a big bad wolf so you never got the chance to tell that asshole how you felt. Always passing the blame for mistakes you've made. I don't think it's surprising at all that you've lost all your friends." "You're not really in a pos..." "FRIENDSHIP..." Christian raised his voice interrupting The Fearless Phenom before she could finish her line of thought, "is equal parts trust and responsibility. Two qualities I must say you've lacked since you stepped foot on this earth. Your ability to sucker people into trusting you is something I admired in you when we were together. What a great asset it was to have the ability to wield influence on people like you do... only to stab them in the back as soon as you get the chance." "We're not here to talk about me." Vivica rebutted back at Christian Zenith, "I'm here to talk about Markus and what you did to him. I don't give a damn what you think about me." "You don't give a damn at all. That's the problem Miss Valentine." Zenith calmly replied, "You completely shrug responsibility onto whatever host you can attach yourself to. Like a leech you find a strong leader like myself or Mrs. Ambrosi to come along and attach yourself to them. Hoping that they can lead you to the promised land of whatever your 'destiny of the day' is with you having to do as little work as possible." Vivica clinched her hands into fists as he continued on his rant. Completely ignoring the subject at hand. "Then when you fail... like you always do. You have someone to blame. Someone to pass the buck off to when you come up short so you don't have to take responsibility for your actions right Viv?" Christian continued to speak, "If you want to know what your destiny is I can tell you. You're destined to be a failure. You were a failure as VP of this company, you were a failure as a lover to me, a failure of a girlfriend to Markus, and a failure as a friend to Lia." "You'd know a lot about that wouldn't you Christian?" Vivica sarcastically answered. "You've got quite the resume yourself." "Ah... but I've accomplished something in my life." Zenith argued back, "I own a Fortune 500 company. I'm one of the most power and influential men of the city of Chicago. I'm a two-time GLOBAL World Heavyweight Champion. They immortalized my image in their Hall Of Fame. You're just a walking disaster with no real accomplishments to balance out your history of failing at everything. It's time you face the facts Vivica. You've failed at life... and disgraced everyone whose ever given a shit about you. Then... you're going to fail at winning the championship. And I'm going to enjoy every minute of it." "Well I'll make sure to come back and pay you a visit when I prove your sorry ass wrong..." Vivica retaliated with a scowl, "and when I do, I'm finding out whatever in the hell you did to Markus and he's coming back with me." "If I had your little mutt I'd let you have him back." Christian said, "If I ever saw the son of a bitch I'd kill him myself. If there is one thing I hate more than failures, it's snakes. That's what your little boyfriend is... he's a snake. And do you know what I do to snakes?" Vivica's eyes narrowed as he continued... "I set them on fire, because I love to watch them burn." "You're a sick fuck... you know that?" Vivica responded. "But aren't we all Miss Valentine?" Christian spoke as he stood up, "Aren't we all?" As he finished his sentence Vivica suddenly found herself surrounded by the security. Pushing her up against the wall they secured her arms behind her back and fastened her in handcuffs. She didn't resist because she knew it was coming... she'd gotten inside of his head. The fact that she was on to him was eating away at him and he'd do anything to watch her suffer a little bit in return. "Now if you'll excuse me..." Christian said in the smarmy sarcastic tone that has been perfected by him after years of bullshit, "I have success to attend to. Good luck in your third attempt for that World Championship Miss Valentine. You'll need it." "I'll be back Chris!" Vivica grunted as she was pulled away, "I know you're hiding something. Markus is going to come home with me one way or another... and I just want to see the look on your face when you see the championship around my waist." ---- Act Five: Miseria Cantare (Origins) ---- "Get the fuck off of me!" She screamed into the night as she was forced out the door. Powered to the ground Vivica J. Valentine rolled over onto her back just in time to see security slam the door in her face. Jumping up to her feet she rushed towards the door and grasped the handle. Desperately she tried to pull it open to no avail. It was locked. "Son of a bitch..." she spoke to herself as she stared at her reflection in the tinted glass door. Embroidered with the Zenith Corporation logo she spit right in the middle of it, furious at the events that just took place. He knew something... he had to have done something. That look and that demeanor, she's seen it a million times before. He was lying. Plain and simple. Licking her bottom lip she suddenly felt the bitter taste of blood. Slowly she ran the side of her finger across her lip and looked down at the red stain under the street light. The Fearless Phenom was livid. How dare he treat her the way he does after all the things she did for him against her will. It was times like this that Vivica desperately wished there was a God. Even if she did get sent to hell, she'd enjoy nothing more than to watch that soulless asshole burn with her. Just to hear him scream. With the rumbling of thunder in the distance, you could smell the scent of rain descending upon the city. A sudden brisk wind blew her red-lined, golden blonde hair across her eyes in the breeze. Slowly a trickle began to come down from above. Bit by bit the drops began to moisturize the blackened streets once again. Faster and faster the rain started to beat down around Vivica J. Valentine, creating a crescendo as they beat against the earth. "Let it go..." Vivica said out loud to herself, turning around and taking a step toward her car, "...that bastard is just trying to get under your skin. You've moved on, you're better than that." Taking a step forward her black combat boot splashed into a puddle. She couldn't help but feel like she was trapped in her own Groundhog Day. An overwhelming sense of Deja Vu came over her as she took another step forward, now completely soaked from head to toe from the downpour. Looking up at the street light she paused on her way to the car, then she looked over her shoulder. It was an abandoned tennis court. The very image of rusted out chain-link brought the memories back. Three years ago was it? Turning around, her beautiful cerulean blue eyes focused on the scene. The only thing to her left was what appeared to be an abandoned tennis court. Her hand slowly eased off the door as she backed away, getting a better glimpse of the tennis court through the sheet of water that continued to fall effortlessly from the heavens above. Her beautiful eyes peered through the rain, as her curiosity began to lead her towards the tennis court. Through the raging tempest, she marched towards the old haggard tennis court. A series of events flickered through the mind of The Fearless Phenom almost as if she was watching an old home movie as she reached out and opened the gate. With a gritty scrape it was hard to push the rusty forward, but once inside she looked around... almost like she thought Lia Ambrosi would be there waiting for her. This was the place in which they first met. On a night just like this one. “What’s a matter Vivica, you don’t believe in vampires?” A woman said from directly behind the confused Vivica J. Valentine. Vivica spun around just in time to get a slap across the face from a woman with long black hair. She grinned as Vivica held her face and stumbled backwards. Vivica looked back at the woman with disgust. She remembered the icy cold slap across her face. The sting of the impact hurt almost as much as the look on Lia's face when she questioned her loyalty on WorldWide. That night she immediately knew there was something special about Lia. She was so much more than a crazy eccentric. In her eyes she could see something different than the rest of GCW. Vivica saw the eyes of a mentor. A sometimes cruel but unexplainably intellectual teacher. Lia Ambrosi was the type of person who you could be around every minute of your existence and learn something new everyday. A bottomless well of knowledge and gifted talent. How do you begin to plan a strategy against a flawless opponent? A flawless opponent who knows absolutely everything about you. Maybe that was the part that hurt Vivica the most. She'd put more trust in the arms of Lia than she'd ever given anyone in her entire life. Every deep, dark secret buried deep down inside of Vivica's soul rested in the mind of her adversary. It was much more that unsettling, it was frightening. The things Lia could do with those secrets could crumble Vivica's entire world around her. Yet all it took was the slightest bit of meddling from spineless coward like Steven Caldera to make Lia take all those things and turn her back. She felt so angry. So scorned. So vulnerable. “There is nothing you can do to stop me Vivica!" Shadow declared as she pulled another throwing dagger out of her pocket and looked at it with a smile before aiming it at the back of Vivica’s head. Spinning around in place Vivica almost thought she could hear her voice ringing through the choir of water splashing against the asphalt. Yet she wasn't there. Lia was never there anymore. The respect that they had earned for each other that night in the middle of the tennis court was now just a crumbling relic of opportunities gone wayside. Perhaps Vivica's biggest mistake is that she put far too much stake into Lia's friendship. Christian may be an arrogant mind-fucked stooge, but one thing he said rang clear. Maybe she did put too much faith in Lia to carry her to her destiny. Maybe she cared far more about Lia Ambrosi than Lia did about her. The revelation sent a bigger chill down her spine than any crisp Chicago breeze could have. She felt taken advantage of... and she was going to make damn sure that no one did it again. This feeling of perpetual bitterness began to eat away at her as she just stood there in the rain, staring up at the top of the fence that Lia Ambrosi fell from that night. Unlike that night however, she stood in the rain soaked streets alone in the darkness. It was so quiet and a little scary... but that was the way things were going to be done from now on. If you don't want this feeling to strike you ever again... you have to keep the ones that can hurt you away. If she was going to walk out of NC-17 as GCW World Heavyweight Champion against all odds... she had to do it alone. No more excuses this time. No one to fall back on. It was all or nothing, because another chance may never happen again. Shadow pulls the knife away from the neck of Vivica J. Valentine and Vivica drops down onto the concrete. Opening the rusty chain link door, another screech erupted through the stormy symphony as Vivica J. Valentine left the old tennis court... along with her memories behind. The world felt so cold now as she splashed through the puddles that gathered on the moistened pavement. Never did she ever think her destiny would play out quite like this. Then again... the best things in life never come easy. Some things are just worth fighting for. It's something that Lia Ambrosi would probably say to her... if she even still gave a damn. Or if she ever gave one at all. Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one of us Love your hate, your faith lost You are now one, one of us ---- Act Six: Surprises And Revelations ---- Ten Days Later... ZIIIIIPPPPPP! With one last flick of the zipper, she was done. Everything was packed and ready to go. "Patrick!" Vivica screamed from her bedroom as she hoisted her carry-on messenger bag over her shoulder and grabbed the handle of her red rolling suitcase. "Yes Viv?" He echoed from the other room, obviously making last minute preparations for her departure. "Wish me luck." The Fearless Phenom spoke as she walked out into the hallway. Patrick stood in the hallway and greeted her with a smile. Next stop was O'Hare. After that... destiny. "Good luck Viv." A smirk crossed the lips of her housekeeper as Vivica began to roll the suitcase behind her. Although it was not by choice, Vivica had grown accustomed to traveling to shows alone. While it was not recommended by GCW she didn't really see herself as having much of a choice. That and she'd rather kill herself then spend any amount of time in a car with Banned & Exiled~! All she needed was an iPod and a clear mind. No more distractions to derail her from her heart's content. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship, having Markus back---everything could be her's with just a little focus. With a sigh she reached out for the door knob. Anxiety struck her now. It was the biggest night of her life and she was a little nervous. It was nerve-racking not knowing what the future has in store. Her entire world could change in just 24 hours. Her next step would be the next step towards the rest of her life. Turning the door knob she slowly opened the door with her eyes closed. Sunlight beamed down upon her on the other side, it almost felt like destiny shining on her as the air filled her lungs and... SURPRISE! ...right back out again as a deafening roar took The Fearless Phenom off guard. Jumping back in shock Vivica's beautiful blue eyes shot open from under her golden locks. Dozens of people were gathered on her front lawn. Holding signs of support and friendship. As they all cheered, every ounce of air escaped her body. Breathless and speechless, The Phenomenon looked much akin to a deer in headlights as she looked around. Tessa Windsor and the kids were there. So was her former Hunting Grounds partner Jason LeMaster who'd left Zenith Corporation over a year ago. Her parents were there. Friends from high school, teachers, neighbors, family members that she hadn't seen since she was a little girl. All holding up posters made with neon colored boards, markers, glitter, and love. She couldn't move an inch as Tessa ran up to her. "So what do you think?" Tessa said with a grin from ear to ear holding her arm out towards the crowd. "I..." Vivica's brain refused to move at this moment in time. After everything she'd gone through she was taken off guard. How did all this come about? How did all of this happen? Just a week ago these people were ignoring her existence and now they're... cheering. "They all wanted to apologize." Windsor replied as Vivica's mouth was frozen like stone. Their roars died down to silence as The Fearless Phenom just stood there. "I..." Vivica stuttered again trying to gather her thoughts, "Wow." Laughs rose from the crowd at Vivica's inability to put together a simple sentence. "Thanks?" Vivica stated with a bit of a curious smirk across her face. Just that one word sent the crowd into cheers once again. The entire thing was just so unbelievable. It was amazing that one little thing such as a gathering on her lawn could completely send her mind to a different place. Suddenly a world that was so lonely and cold didn't feel quite so lonely anymore. "WE'RE MORE THAN CARBON AND CHEMICALS!" Her father Jack Valentine yelled out above the crowd. A man who never approved of a thing she did in her life. Especially her career choice of professional wrestling. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The crowd roared back in response. It was almost like an out of body experience for Vivica. She couldn't hold back the huge smile that was on her face now. People who had given up on her a long time ago, and people that she'd turned her back on now gathered... both giving and asking for a second change. Uniting under a single banner. Out of love and dedication to a person who although makes mistakes... has touched all their lives in one way or another. "FREE WILL IS OURS AND WE CAN'T LET GO!" Shouted Jason LeMaster... a man who she hadn't spoken to since she broke up with Christian Zenith. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The jubilant crowd roared in response once again. Vivica and Tessa Windsor looked at each other, trying to hold back giggles of their own. "WE CAN'T ALLOW THIS THE QUIET CULL!" Tessa Windsor shouted before pointing at the crowd in front of herself and Vivica. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" The response made Vivica's heart skip a beat. Afterwards they waited in silence as The Fearless Phenom glanced at Tessa Windsor and then back at the crowd. "SO WE SING OUT THIS OUR CANTICLE!" Vivica screamed out at the top of her lungs to the deafening response of... "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" Followed by a roar that may not rival that of the everyday cheer from a capacity audience in GCW, but meant just as much as that ever did. If all these people could come together and forgive her for all the mistakes she may have made... maybe she should consider forgiving Lia. After all, everyone makes mistakes right? But first Lia needed to act like she wanted it and second... Vivica knew she had to earn back her respect. There was only one way to do that now. In the middle of the ring. "We love you Vivica!" Someone shouted from the back of the crowd as Vivica stepped outside away from her porch with Tessa by her side. "I love you too!" Vivica shouted back with a grin from ear to ear. Many hugs and handshakes were shared between her front porch and the car. Her grandparents, old friends from high school, principles, teachers, neighbors, ex-classmates, family friends... but all of them paled in comparison to the one at the end. Her father stood at the door to the car and extended his arms. For the first time in nearly 8 years, Vivica wrapped his arms around him. "I'm proud of who you've become." He said as he patted her on the back, "I couldn't be a happier father than I am today." It felt like a million burdens were lifted off her shoulders and suddenly all the little things didn't mean to matter so much. For the first time in 25 years... her father was proud of her. To her right she looked over to see her mother crying and she almost did the same. Tears welled up in her eyes and it became hard to see straight, but she remained strong. "Go knock em dead Viv." Jack said patting his daughter on the back on last time as Vivica sat down in her car to a thunderous round of applause. Various shout-outs echoed from the gathering on her front lawn as she closed the door and rolled down the window. "I will dad..." Vivica replied, "I will." Rolling the car window up Vivica backed out of the driveway leaving behind dozens of fans and loved ones. Maybe dreams aren't ONLY about winning championships and having a long list of accomplishments next to your name. No matter how great those things are... living your dream is about moments like this. The ability to touch the lives of people everyday. Vivica realized she lost touch of how much of a hero she is to people. Sometimes you forget what it's like to be a fan. Vivica felt deep in her heart that the GCW World Heavyweight Championship was still her true destiny. But even if she came up short at NC-17... she had no regrets. If this is how just succeeding in the eyes of her father felt, she couldn't possibly imagine the emotion of holding that belt for the first time. Without this... destiny just wouldn't be worth it. ---- The End
