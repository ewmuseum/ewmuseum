Oh, my aching head

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The day after Everything hurts. My head hurts, my back hurts, my arms hurt, my legs hurt. I went through hell last night, so it's understandable. But I won, someone ran down Johnny Lexicon last night. I have no idea who, I have no idea why. But someone had a reason for mowing him down with a car. I don't know if it was someone with a vendetta against Lexi, or someone wanting to help me. I doubt the latter and am guessing on the former. Either way, I remain undefeated. I pulled myself from my bed and shuffled to the bathroom and winced as I turned on the light. After letting my eyes adjust to the light I surveyed my face. I looked like hell, felt like it too. I carefully peeled back the bandages on my head where Johnny had cut me last night. They still looked ugly, I applied fresh bandages and tossed down a couple of asprin. I shuffled back to the bedroom of my hotel suite and started looking for clean clothes to change into as I set my mind on a nice refreshing shower. The buzzing of my cell phone pulled me away for a moment. I had set it on vibrate, afraid that the ringing would make my head hurt even more. The phone bounced across the nightstand and fell to the floor. I groaned in pain as I bent over to pick it up and answer it. 'Yo.' 'How you feeling?' Jeff Wylde, former GCW wrestler and my business manager asked. 'Like handmade shit. I have aches in places I didn't even know I had places.' I said, sitting down on the bed as my head started throbbing again. 'Any idea who it was that ran Lex down?' 'Not a fuckin' clue.' 'Well the reason I'm calling is because you have a title defense on the upcoming WorldWide.' Jeff said. 'You gotta be kidding me.' I groaned, 'I just went through hell last night and Caldera wants me to fight again?' 'Yeah, against Chrissy-Poo.' 'Who?' 'Chrissy-Poo, one of the newer signings.' Jeff said, 'He was in the Cruiserweight title tournament.' 'Am I supposed to be worried?' I asked, 'With a name like Chrissy-Poo he sounds like he's still living with his mother and hasn't seen a naked woman in his life.' 'Just thought you should know, and you should know better than to underestimate anyone. I did that in TPW and got my ass handed to me. That and I've already taken care of your travel arrangments, rental car and hotel room arrangments for Vegas.' 'I'll worry about it after my headache goes away. Lex put me through the ringer last night. You did see the show right?' 'Yeah, of course. You think I was gonna miss that? Miss watching LeStatt and Stevens tear each other apart? Miss you tearing into Lexicon? I wasn't gonna miss it for the world. Anyway, I'm gonna let you go, just remember, don't underestimate this Chrissy-Poo. His name may sound wimpy, but he's in GCW for a reason.' 'Yeah, yeah, I'll worry about it later.' I said, waving it off. Chrissy-Poo was the furthest thing from my mind right now. Getting a shower was foremost on my mind. 'Later bro.' 'Later.' Jeff had offered me the chance to stay at the ranch and I had turned it down, I wasn't in the mood to go from Toronto to Vancouver after the hellacious match I knew I was going to have with Johnny Lexicon. I snapped the phone shut, fished out some clean clothes and showered the pain and funk away. I felt slightly better after emerging from the shower and started packing. Jeff said he had taken care of my travel arrangments so I figured I should get to Vegas. I hadn't been there in a few years, and maybe I could get in a little gambling before WorldWide. I stood in the living room of my hotel room looking over everything. I had given the rooms a final sweep making sure I didn't leave anything behind. I had my suitcase, my equipment bag, and the TV title belt. All set. I opened the door and the wire thin man on the other side almost hit me in the face as he was about to knock on my door. 'Mr. Savitch?' 'Depends. Who th' fuck are you?' 'My name is William James, I'm one of Mr. Caldera's lawyers. I was wondering if I could have a quick word with you?' 'You have until I get to my rental car to ask me questions.' I said closing the door behind me and heading down the hall, not looking to see if he was following me. 'Well as you know,' he said running to keep up with my long strides, 'The Marauder was hit by a car last night during your match--' 'And you want to know if I had anything to do with it. Right?' I said cutting him off as I reached the elevator bank and punched the down button. 'Well, basically yes.' 'Because Caldera wants to make sure that I didn't try to kill the bastard just to keep the title?' 'Again, yes.' The elevator arrived and we stepped inside, I hit the button for the lobby. 'Well allow me to clarify everything. I had nothing to do with that, I don't know who did it, I don't know or care why they did it, and if even if I did have something to do with it, do you really think I would admit to it?' He cleared his throat nervously and shook his head, 'Well, I that is...' 'You're covering Caldera's ass. I get it.' I said, 'Look, I hated Johnny Lexicon for sticking his nose into my business, and I asked for the parking lot match because a match like that is a pay-per-view style match and a feud ender. My only intention in that match was to beat the holy living shit out of Johnny Lexicon, nothing more, nothing less. I don't know who tried to turn him into roadkill.' The elevator reached the lobby, I checked out paid for the movies I had rented, the food I had ordered and the beer I had drank from the mini bar. I headed towards the parking lot, James following me out, realizing that his questioning of me was nearing it's end. 'And you have no idea who would want to do something like that?' 'I already said as much. I don't know who ran him over, and I don't fuckin' care. All I care about is winning matches. I'm done with Johnny, you might wanna look into his past, maybe someone resurfaced with a grudge against him. You tell Caldera and York I had nothing to do with what happened to Johnny. If he wants me to tell him personally I will, right now,' We had reached my car, I tossed my bags in the trunk, and the TV title belt on the front passenger seat. 'I have a plane to catch.' I got in the car and started it up, pulled out of the parking lot and headed to the airport. To whoever ran Johnny over, I thought smirking, ...thanks.
