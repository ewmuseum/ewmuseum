What we need to do right here is go back ... way back ... back into time.

23 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 April 08 13 MARCH 2001 “RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIING “. RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIING “. RIIIIIIIIIIIIIING?” The damn phone, it had become a curse to me since I had come out of “hiding”. Not that too many people called me anyways, and I could almost certainly guess who this was at the other end. I tossed the black sheets off my body and just laid there for a bit longer as the screeching noise of the phone continued to interrupt my attempt at sleep. Eventually though, as everyone knows that fucking sound can drive anyone nuts. I groggily grabbed at the phone and placed it to my ear. “What is it David ?” David Sandy also known in the wrestling world as RaZiel. Every since he found me, basically rotting away, he had been trying to get me back into the wrestling scene, a place I hadn’t been in a little over a year, since my brother’s death. He figured that if anything could help me harness all of the uncontrollable emotions in me, it would be wrestling. He was my best friend ? my only friend. When everyone and everything turned it’s back on me, he reached out yet again and I am eternally in his debt. “Roberto, I think I finally got it ! I know you’ve been growing tired of these little independent one match stops we’ve been trying and I finally got a small opening that you may be very interested in.” This obviously was news I had been hoping for, so I forced my body to move its way to a seated position at the side of my bed. With my free hand I began trying to wipe the sleep from my eyes, it had minimal impact but it was the effort that mattered most. I patted my hair down and back from my eyes. “You got my attention D, what do you got ?” “Okay well last night I was at a club, when I got approached by a man named John Reed, know him?” “Isn’t ringing any bells amigo.” “Alright well, anyways, he asked me if I was RaZiel, and of course I figured he was just some moron trying to start some shit with a “wannabe” tough guy since I wrestle. Instead he told me that he knew I helped manage you, Rob. My interests peaked then, and he told me that he was a member of Global Championship Wrestling?” “GCW ? It’s a pretty fresh place right ?” “Fresh and endorsed big time Roberto, I mean this Steve Caldera guy is huge right now. Well anyways, Reed said he was tasked to help with recruiting and knew that management would be impressed with your work. He took my contact information and shit, then this morning he called me.” “Well shit Dave, what the hell did he say?” “You have an interview with Steven Caldera next week in Columbus, OH. All we need to do is bring a tape of some of your work and a brief bio. The rest is up to your way with words and ability to impress.” The smile on my face was one of a kind, I hadn’t smiled this way in quite sometime. It just seemed as if things may finally be getting into a mode where I can enjoy life again, where all of the demons of my past may be able to stay buried. Of course I am not a fortune teller, thus I do not know what really is in my future plans, but I can always hope. “Thank you Dave?” It was all I could manage to say at first, as I was still caught up. I mean It wasn’t often that someone would go through all this trouble to help me succeed. Quickly I regained my composure though, as I couldn’t allow my emotions to get the best of me, I mean as great of an opportunity as this was, I still had to do the interview and get hired. “I better go and begin getting ready, buy the plane tickets and everything.” “Yeah you do that man, I’m going to get a bite to eat and I’ll be over within the next hour or so.” “Alright Dave, see ya then” I hung up the phone, but didn’t really move much more than that at first. I only allowed my gaze to tilt forward a bit, and look outside of my bedroom window. The sun was shining bright in the early morning sky, with not a cloud in sight. I couldn’t help but think that after a horrific few years of my life, that maybe it was really time for a change. Perhaps life did have funny ways of turning out the right way, and maybe, just maybe that happy ending that I only had a glimpse of would really come true for me one day. Or it could simply be the calm before the storm ? Only time would tell? 25 MARCH 2001 “From myself, and everyone on the crew, we here at American Airlines would like to thank you for flying with us. If Columbus is your final destination then welcome and we hope you enjoy your stay, if you are moving elsewhere have a wonderful flight from here.” The voice of the pilot overhead woke me up, and obviously David as well, as in his half conciousness kicked the seat in front of him, which more than startled the woman seated in front of him. At 7’1? and 350lbs, even your slightest touch can be ground shaking I guess. After apologizing to the woman he looked over at me with a half grin, then rubbed his eyes to get the sleep out. “I guess we’re here then eh?” David mumbled as he undid his seatbelt. “Yeah, should I be nervous?” “C’mon Rob, this is what you love to do. You look good, you wrestle good, and you are a great speaker ? just go in there and do what you do best.” “yeah you’re right ? let’s get out of here.” I got up, lifting my gym bag from beneath the seat and began walking through the aisle, until I heard a loud thud, and turned around quickly, and began laughing loudly. David was standing there holding his head, as he hit it hard on something. “Ay yi yi.” “Shut up Roberto, that shit hurt.” “Stop fucking around Dave, we got some business to take care of.” I ducked off the plane, and David followed closely behind as we slithered through the masses of people that engulfed the airport. After some struggle, some luck, and some pushing smaller people around we made it to the baggage claim, which is the most annoying part of this flying process in my humble opinion. After what seemed like an eternity of watching bags go round and round, almost making me dizzy I saw my bag, quickly behind that was David’s. We took up our gear and made our way to the doors, which slid open and let in a burst of cool air. It was spring time, so the sun was shining pretty brightly, and the weather was pretty fair about 60 degrees or so. The slight breeze whipped my hair about a bit, along with the tail end of my trench coat. I reached into my coat pocket and pulled out a pair of sunglasses and slid them on to cover my eyes from the light. David looked around a bit, until he saw a Taxi coming our way, and chased it down. The driver, who was a middle aged black man with streaks of grey in his small’ish afro and beard, popped the trunk to allow us to place our bags inside, then we made our way inside the cab and shut the doors behind us. “Where to ?” “Do you know where Caldera Enterprises is ?” “Sure do, have you there in about fifteen minutes.” So we took off towards what could be a career booster for me. I could feel David’s eyes staring at me, probably wondering why I had gotten so quiet since we landed in Columbus, although it couldn’t have been too much of a surprise. After fighting for so long to make something right I was going to be given a chance to do what I loved, and in front of a man like Steven Caldera it was quite nerve wrecking. He never asked the question of what was wrong, but I knew he wanted to ? I turned to him, and said what he wanted to hear. “I’m alright D, I’m just anxious.” With that we just both turned our heads and looked out our respective windows. We both knew how important this business meeting was, so we didn’t want to jinx anything with too much confidence or too much blabber. Although we both claim to not be superstitious, that fear is always there. Only minutes went by, but it felt like an eternity when the cab driver stopped in front of a huge building, that read “CALDERA ENTERPRISES” and under it “Home of GCW”. Dave paid the cab driver as I scrambled to my feet from the cab, which was smallish so I was very relieved to be able to stretch my legs, at 6’6? that’s a disadvantage, and even more so for the 7’0? friend with me. I tossed my gym bag over my shoulder and began walking towards the front doors, with David walking right to my left. No words were exchanged as we approached, me because of my nerves and David because he knew that talking would probably only make me worse ? but I knew as soon as I sat down and began talking with Mr. Caldera, that I’d make it through just fine, but its that initial uncertainty that kills. It was about time to tempt my fate ?
