Dangerously Wrecked

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Whenever “I’ve had enough of you,” Twisted shouted into the mirror. Kind of like the opposite of that Michael Jackson song. Instead of making the world a better place by starting with yourself Twizzy is trying to break it down. Emotionally. He’s a wreck you know. It’s been a long time since The Junkie Man felt pretty good about himself. At least he wasn’t doing heroin though, right? Heh? hehe? eh? pathetic. Actually there’s one congressman who thinks Twistodium is far worse than most illegal drugs out on the market. Truth be told it’s worse than every other steroid in most respects. Twistodium: don’t cure the common cold, but whatever. He slammed his fist into the mirror. Seven years bad luck. Seven years. 7. Se7en. SSSSSSSSSSEEEEEEEEVVVVVVVVEEEEEEEENNNNNN. Woah, mon friar. That’s depressing. Twizzy thought so too and fell into the wall before sliding to the floor. A little puke drooled out of his mouth. Yummy. Hasn’t eaten in days. Hasn’t won in months. Hasn’t achieved anything in years. Dangerous Games is going to be FUN!
