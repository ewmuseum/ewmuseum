Deliver me from EVL : Part Five- Mystra's Dream

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/2005 In the world I was transferred into there was a woman named Mystra. She was a woman of great beauty and even greater strength. But these are not what made her unique amongst all others. She was a woman gifted with visions, prophceies if you will. Today I will tell you of a dream she shared with me. A dream that forever changed my life, not only in that world, but in this one as well. This is the story of Mystra's dream.... A clouded dream on an earthly night Hangs upon the crescent moon A voiceless song in an ageless light Sings at the coming dawn Birds in flight are calling there Where the heart moves the stones It's there that my heart is longing All for the love of you.... The night was cold. Fog hung suspended in the air like death's cloak merely waiting for us to falter so that it may swoop in and take our souls to the other realm. My heart was thudding so fiercely I feared that the others would hear it as we carefully crept through the fog. We had been instructed by Mystra to climb the mountain path which lead to the mystic fortress of Apothcera. It was Mystra's birthplace. A place known for the birthing of women with the power of foretelling the future through vision both waking and dreaming. It was unlike any place in the kingdom of Calderia. The journey had been dangerous. Along the way we lost many men leaving only four to face that we had been told lay within the fog. In order to reach the fortress we would have to pass through the misty cloak and face our strongest fears, our deepest hates, and things not even knew we had imbedded on our spirits. And so there we stood looking into the fog for a breathless moment before looking at each with an uncertainty we each felt. ' Well Cap'n?' My eyes fell on the masked face of Cedric as I struggled to answer his partially asked question. He wanted to know if we were to go in to the thick of the magical ward or if we would give up our journey and return home once again without a victory. I found myself reaching deep within fighting to remember all those things that frightened me. I wanted to know what I would be facing and somehow I knew that despite what I knew, what lay within that blanket of thick air I would be unprepared for. Turning to Cedric I merely shook my head as I faltered taking a step back. I was not ready to face all those things in my life. I was terrified of what the fog would find in my soul and force me to face. ' I don't know Cedric...' ' Eve...Mystra said...' I turned to face the only other survivor of our journey. Eve's brother Alrhick looked at me imploringly, silently urging me to have faith in Mystra's words. ' I know what she said Alrhick..but what if she was wrong?' ' And what if she was right. I have never known you to back down from a challenge placed at your feet. I am hard pressed to believe you would not face the challenge of yourself.' He was right of course. Even I had never refused to face a challenge head on, how was this any different? Drawing a deep breath I glanced at them both and nodded. ' Let's go. We've a mission to complete.' A painting hangs on an ivy wall Nestled in the emerald moss The eyes declare a truce of trust And then it draws me far away Where deep in the desert twilight Sand melts in pools of the sky When darkness lays her crimson cloak Your lamps will call me home And so it's there my homage's due Clutched by the still of the night And now I feel you move..... From the moment I stepped within the thick of that fog I felt the weight of all that was part of me come bearing down as if to crush me beneath its imaginary feet. I drew my sword out of the need to feel protected from whatever it was I was to face. It did not take long for the first vision to come to me. She seemed to materialize out of no where. The vision of my beautiful mother coming back to haunt me. Her eyes mirrored the color of mine but within them I could see the disappointment. ' If only you had paid more attention Eva..if only you had opened your eyes and saw the way I suffered. You could have helped to save me....you could have helped to save me.' My heart broke as I choked back a sob. It was as I feared. I would not have Eve's fears haunt me but rather my own and it sturck a bitter cord in my heart. I raised a trembling hand reaching for her only to have her melt as she had done many times in my nightmares. My own ragged scream nearly peirced my ears to the point of deafness as I struggled to grasp at her and keep her there if only for a moment longer, but it was no use. She blew away like sand on the wind. My shoulder's sagged as I wept balefully and took another step. Nothing stopped me from further advancing for a good few yards. Then, I saw him. My own father in a sea of blood reaching out for me as he blamed me for his death. I squeezed my eyes shut wishing it would all go away as I sank to my knees and dropped that sword from numb fingers. Of all the things to face, the guilt I felt for the death of my parents had to be the worst...or was it? Another voice penetrated my whirling mind. I lifted my tear filled gaze to catch sight of Demitri standing there with his lover at his side. He mocked me. He laughed at my foolishness for thinking any man could possibly love a woman such as me and then before I could move he launched forward wielding a knife and drove it deep into my heart. A biting chill sunk into my chest as I collapsed to the ground gagging from the pain I felt as my heart crumbled once again. Every breath is full So it's there my homage's due Clutched by the still of the night Even the distance feels so near All for the love of you... I was a fool. I knew this. When I managed to finally pry my eyes open and face the next of many things that were coming to haunt me I saw the vision of Lestatt. I watched as he took mary into his arms and kissed her before me. I screamed my rage as she turned a snide smile toward me and told me that I could never have made him love me. I was not a woman worthy of a man's love. I was not a woman at all but a mere shell barely existing. A parasite who thrived off others for survival. My fingers clawed at the earth. The dirt embedded beneath my nails painfully. I felt the warmth of my own blood as I lurched to my knees and arched my back to the sky above. The bellowed scream which erupted at that point caused the world around me to tremble as I released my pain. Once my scream died down I fell to all fours again and reached out for my sword. Clutched within hand I used it to get to my feet. I was past my fear and my pain now. It was time to face my rage. First Rollins appeared his eyes glowing with crimson fires as he barreled down on me with spear axe in hand. I roared like a feline protecting her nest as blade met with blade. We fought fiercely and in the end I won cutting him down like a dog. A noise caught my ear from behind. I whirled to face it and spied the vision of Mike Savige. He swung his sword up and I swung my down to greet it half way. We fought like crazed bezerkers. Each screamed at the other as we met metal against metal again and again. Then I thrust my sword into his stomach and watched as he smiled and faded away. A pain stung at my shoulder and neck that staggered me to a knee. Turning my head to look over my shoulder there stood Lestatt once more. My hatred for him boiled to the surface as I released a banshee's cry for death and attacked him with everything I had. He had my love and he betrayed me with it. The battle ensued and he quickly gained the upper hand as he used two swords vs my one. My blood ran over my arms with each cut delivered. Sweat stung my eyes despite the cold atmosphere. I swung wildly, unable to see him as he danced about me and toyed with my mind and heart. Then in a moment of brief clarity I felt his approach from behind. As he came in high as I dropped to one knee and swung my sword in a downwards arch bring it back and thrust with everything I had. The impact of his false form on it knocked the wind from me forcing me to the ground face first. I laid there for many minutes unable to move as I struggled to regain my fighting spirit and return it to the surface. With great effort I managed to regain my feet and slowly turned to face the final phantom who incited my rage. Johnny Borealis. I saw the belt which was rightfully mine draped around his waist like a stolen trophy. I felt the rage resurface and, yet, this time I made not a sound. He grinned knowingly and I managed to smile back. I wasted no time with that fight. Instead, as he came hurtling forward I flipped that sword in my hand like a spear and released it forward into the air like a spear. Once it impaled in his chest I felt a release of the rage..then I felt nothing. I was left alone. I was left to face the sightless scene. I was left to stuble blindly in a fog that tightened its grip and I had no one to save me. No one could save me. It was the deepest fear that I had imprinted on my soul. The fear of being alone... I fell to the ground once again. My fingers clutched at nothing as I sobbed pathetically. I wept for what seemed an eternity. I screamed. I thrashed. I raved. I got up and swung my word wildly at the air in a beserk rage caused by my own mind...my own loneliness. I was crazed by it all. I was paniced and enraged by the knowledge that I could depend on no one but myself. I was livid with myself. Then finally I came to accept it all. Slowly I allowed that sword to slip from my fingers. I stared at it for long thoughtful moments. Then I found my spirit again and picked it up. My eyes looked forward and from the blanket of the mist I saw not one pair of hands but three reaching out for me to help me to the other side. As soon I as I was free of the soul seering mist those hand faded away and all that was left was Mystra. She stood there with a soft smile on her face as she came forward and embraced me. ' You have faced all those things that have tainted your soul and you have come to terms with it all Eve. Now you are truly ready to face your destiny.' I relaxed against her and melted in that warm embrace of comfort and knowing. She was speaking to Eve but it was I who was listening. It was I who had faced my fears, my pain and my rage. It was I who had come to terms with myself and dug down to pull my true spirit free once more. I had found the warrior within and with that I found the strength to follow the path of my destiny... The path of Mystra's dream.....my dream. I would get that belt, and I would get others as well. I would become a force in GCW to be recognized, respected and recokoned with. And somehow..I knew I would not be alone but I am able to stand alone. Others would stand with me. Three. And together...we will change the face of GCW for the better. It is time that good conquered evil. It is time....
