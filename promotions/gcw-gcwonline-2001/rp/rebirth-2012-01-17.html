Rebirth

17 January 2012

Date: 01/17/12 December 29th, 2010 – 7:45 PM Quick Loans Arena Cleveland, Ohio Cars steadily stream throughout the streets as a backdrop of snow blankets the city of Cleveland. For a man about to make his debut to the world of professional wrestling, I am rather calm. My back story is a simple one: former standout athlete, turning to the ring as a second career. Not a new concept, but one I can try to work with. Lawrence and I have been through our career game plan time after time now. Only a few hours a week I have to be this person. Hide the world from the alcohol. Hide the world from the drugs. Hide the world from the women. Hide the world from the real Theo Palmer. Only show them a small portion of my past, and an even smaller portion of my personality. The alcohol, drugs and women to this point have ruined all other aspects of my life. My wife is long gone with our two daughters. My friends, minus a handful, grown up and moved on. My parents not wanting anything to do with their failure of a son. My twin brothers, Russel and Duncan, ashamed of who their old brother is and has become. All I have left is Lawrence, and I can't fuck that up. My thoughts are broken by the sound of the arena doors opening, and Lawrence exiting onto the entrance steps, recently salted to combat the current downfall. While smiling and slightly nodding my way, Lawrence doesn't even have to say a word to communicate as his eyes say everything, yet does so for the sake of the cameras flowing out behind him. “Your match is next. You ready to do this.” “I haven't slept in days Lawrence. This is all that has been on my mind, and it's time for Theo Palmer to leave his mark.” My first words spoken and it already feels forced. The invisible noose on my necks feels tight. I am easily uncomfortable in the skin Lawrence and I have decided to portray. As I react in most situations of the sort, I reach into the front pouch of my Under Armour hoodie and pull of my trademark Putter cigarettes, the classiest of rez butts. “Theo, your match is next, as in any minute now. The only mark you're leaving right now is the one on your lungs. Fight the temptation of cancerous glory and get the fuck in here.” Running the fingers of my free hand through my beard, Lawrences wide eyed expression says it all. Already I am lapsing, not wanting to commit to this persona. I want to be me. I am not the smooth athlete we want the world to see. I am rough around the edges. I am a borderline lowlife. The only thing about me that screams Theo Palmer right now is the greasy beard and nicotine stained fingertips. “Get your head out of your ass and focus on the task at hand.” Lawrence says with a bass to his voice. I can see the desperation in his eyes. He is the only one who has stood by his loser of a brother. This is our chance to make something of ourselves, to salvage an otherwise shitty life and show those who are left I can be something, someone. Tossing the cigarette into a snow bank, I say the first thing that comes to mind. “It's fitting my debut coincides with Christmas, you know that?” “Why's that?” Lawrence asks, the bass replaced with a tone of confusion. “Christmas is the birth of Jesus Christ. The newborn king of our faithful Christians.” As I think to myself where exactly I am going with this, I can see Lawrence is of the same mind. “Well tonight, GCW will be bestowed with their own newborn king. The debut of Theo Palmer will rock the very foundation of this company.” Yes, I went there. Straight up comparison to Jesus, two minutes into my live television debut. He wanted cocky. What is more cocky than paralleling yourself to the savior himself? “Did you just compare yourself to Jesus?” He says softly, not intending for the cameras to pick up but is loud enough that they do. “I've received the shit end of the stick far to often in life thus far. It's my time to start handing out the shit end to everyone else, starting with Alexander Redding.” You know, my best friend since childhood who I am now supposed to forge an “alliance” with, limiting the public's knowledge of our roots. As I slowly begin my quest into the building, Lawrence simply shakes his head as I pass by him. “Pretty sure Jesus never handed out the shit end of the stick to anybody...” he mumbles. We'll see how long this last. We'll see how long the world is treated to this generic bore of a persona. It can only be a matter of time before the real Theo Palmer comes face to face with the world... November 22nd, 2011 – 4:48 A.M Hotel Max – 620 Stewart St. Seattle, Washington “I love scotch. Scotchy, scotch, scotch. Here it goes down. Down into my belly.” The smooth liquor slowly glides down my throat, creating the warm, comforting feeling all alcoholics strive for. The red leisure suit from last nights portrayal of Theodore Burgundy reeks of sweat, cigarettes and much more than just scotch. My spectacles still firmly in place, minus a lens, the other cracked. Sleep has been the furthest thing from my mind. Eleven months have passed and my evolution into reality is nearly complete. The world has seen me for exactly who I am. They have seen my at my all time low, losing the only family member I had left and almost my job to go along with it. Now it's time to simply embrace these demons. Make them popular instead of pathetic. Make them my strength instead of a weakness....
