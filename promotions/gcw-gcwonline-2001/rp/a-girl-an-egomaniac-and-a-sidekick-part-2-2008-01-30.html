A Girl, An Egomaniac, and A Sidekick: Part 2

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/30/08 (takes place in 2002) August 2002 Under the circumstances how Chris Storm’s GTT run ended, Markus Stone knew his friend was going to be pissed. And sure enough, he was. Storming (pun totally intended) through the backstage area, Chris threw the curtain aside and stomped his feet. "This is bullshit!" he screamed mid stomp. Markus shook his head. He saw this coming from miles away, "I expected more than that kind of crap from a PTC event." Markus just laughed, "should have read up on your history". Not caring for that kind of answer, Chris turned away, "maybe I’ll go read it up at home, which is where I’m going." "Ah come on man," said Markus, "I want to watch Tessa’s match. I heard she has Paul Harrington in the third round. It starts soon." Chris certainly did not look like he wanted to go, but Markus seemed intent on going with or without Chris, so Storm decided he would tag along with his friend anyways. It was, after all better than going home by himself to study up on the history of his opponent. Heading toward the nearest monitor to watch the match, the two discovered that there was already a gathering around the monitor to watch Tessa Windsor wrestle. It seemed Tessa’s popularity had taken a great upswing from her exposure in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Then again, this was always one of the high selling points of the tournament; making no ones into household names. Markus rubbed his elbow into Chris? ribs, "she’s quite something, isn’t she?" he said as Tessa picked up the victory. Though there were no big cheering or outward expressions of excitement, it was easy enough to tell that she had been the favorite backstage. Markus turned back to the monitor to watch a replay of what had just transpired, his partner however, made a point to turn his head away, "yeah she’s ok." Markus slapped Chris? shoulder as he stood up to leave, "for a chick." "She lasted longer than you did" his friend reminded him, to which Chris gave Markus a dirty look. The two walked away from the group as the match drew to a close, ready to leave. Passing by two other wrestlers from XCWF, where Tessa was from, Markus nodded to them for Chris to look. "You know, I heard XCWF was having some problems. Like there was some talk of a strike among the wrestlers." Chris snickers, "you won’t see anyone acting that way in WIW." "Hey, maybe you should offer her a contract with WIW." Markus suggested out of the blue, acting as if the idea had just dawned on him out of nowhere. Continuing to walk, "who?" "Who" he stammered, not sure if Chris was jerking his chain or not, "who else? Tessa!" Shrugging it off, Chris replies back with, "I doubt it". "You doubt it?" Markus fired back, "what about the other day? You said you would marry her. Since then you haven’t said as much as a word to her. " Stopping at a catering table, the two grabbed more than a handful of sandwiches. They were never one to pass up free food. After the match he had, it would likely be the highlight of Chris? day. The thing that softened the blow to his ego. He remained silent for a bit after Markus had hazed him about his odd stance on Tessa, but as he turned away from the table, he looked to his partner. "A woman can’t be successful in WIW." He looked him dead in the eyes, "and a woman will never be successful in WIW. We’re not full of weak men like where she’s from." "What’s that Chris?" Markus questioned, raising his voice slightly, "I didn’t hear you." "I said, she’d never succeed in WIW. " Chris repeated, raising his voice some as well, "It’s pitiful that you even think she could?.." Markus snickered to himself, as the voice of this “her” called out from behind them. "Hey guys." "Hey Tessa!" Markus delightfully called out, as he turned around to face her. "Congrats chica! You totally kicked ass out there." Chris continued on his way, realizing that Markus had got one on him this time. After all the years the two had spent together, Chris should have realized Markus was up to his old tricks, after all Chris had done the same to him countless times. "Thanks," She replied, brushing a lock of her sweat drenched hair back behind her ear, "let’s hope for a repeat performance next round." "Hey Chris!" Markus called out to his partner in crime who’d drifted away towards the exit, just inches from making his escape, "Where you going buddy?" Continuing to walk away, Chris just shouted out over his shoulder "out". The two double stepped it to catch up to him. Tessa kept her eyes down for the most part until she finally managed to connect the words in her head in just the right order, "so I saw your match with Draven." That brought Chris to a halt. Markus? eyes started to grow wide, having known Chris for much longer than Tessa. "You had a great match, I’m sorry it didn’t go so well." Chris spun around on his heels to face her, leaning right in to almost go nose to nose. "I didn’t lose. I was cheated. Those punks from DWO are just a bunch of little shits and you better believe I’m going to pay them back for it very soon." Heat just about seethed off his teeth and caused Tessa to draw back, wondering whose grandmother she just insulted. Markus tilted over to her to clue her in, "he doesn’t like to be reminded of his loses." "Oh," She mouthed as Chris turned his back towards her once more in attempts to pull Markus away, "I’m?" Markus abruptly silenced her by lifting his index finger up to his lips, in hopes to save her from the can of worms she was about to open, but it was too late. "You’re what?" he asked, "sorry?" Chris cut his eyes away, "I don’t need your sympathy. I’m a champion in the WIW, I don’t need anyone’s sympathy." By now, the magnitude of her win tonight was starting to settle. Not only did she walk out victorious, but she proved, twice now, that she was capable of beating a guy. While it may not have been that big of a deal to Markus, and definitely not Chris, this meant the world to her. She’d finally proven herself. Now the next step was to look for bigger fish to fry. Lucking for her, that big fish was standing just feet from her, begging for a comeback, "Then why so pouty?" "Did you just call me pouty?" Chris looked to Markus. "Did she just call me pouty?" "She called you pouty," came the answer. Chris focused his attention on Tessa, eyes razor sharp. "I am not pouty, I do not get pouty," his words came out in between heavy breaths, "what I am is pissed." Maybe it was the fact that she was only twenty at the time, or maybe it had something to do with trying to gain some respect around these parts, either way she was bound to make a statement."Pissed that you lost? Wait, champions don’t lose?." Chris fired back, "and women shouldn’t wrestle." His eyes trailed off to search for any other hint of interest. "So I guess everything is just fucked up today." For his credit, Markus Stone hung back and watched the two go at it like a Saturday Night Fight. "Women shouldn’t wrestle?" She stated rhetorically, "Let me guess what comes next, the whole barefoot and pregnant part?" Tossing back a lock of blond hair, he kept his eyes off her. "Preferable." Now his eyes drew to her to try and further drive home the point, "it might get them to shut up." "A bit chauvinistic, aren’t we?" Markus wrapped an arm around Chris? shoulders, "that’s our Chris" he stated. The man in question continued to stare at his opposition with fiery intent. "You face some lag-about shit stains in this joke and suddenly you think you belong here? Go crawl back to that pathetic excuse you call a wrestling promotion. I hear next week you get to have a bikini match in chocolate pudding." "You know, I happen to love chocolate pudding." Markus added, hoping to ease some of the tension between the two. It hardly helped though. "You’d like that, wouldn’t you?" Her eyebrow lifted up, before exchanging another blow, "You and Markus, half naked, wrestling. Yeahhhhhh, I bet that’s what your deal is. I was wrong. It’s got nothing to do with Draven does it? Markus simply declined your offer, didn’t he?" Even Markus shut up on that one, wondering how he had gotten dragged into this mess. Chris pointed a stern finger in her face but never said a word. Turning on the edge of his heel, he made an about-face from her and started back down the hall as he had done before being stopped by Tessa. Passing Stone, Storm nodded his head in his direction, motioning for him to follow. "Come on Markus, we’ve got better things to do than to waste our time with a second rate like her. Let Deville show her what it means to step into the ring with a man." Though he was done with the conversation, Chris still made sure he said it loud enough for her to hear. "What’s the hurry?" She continued on despite him continuing on his way, "hot date?" As the two rounded a corner and left Ms. Windsor in a huff, Chris turned to his friend "This is why I don’t want women in the WIW." Markus could only laugh though, "I don’t know man, you two already sound like a married couple." The fact that the joke couldn’t even crack the slightly smile from Chris? lips made Markus laugh all the more harder. He was in denial. But Markus saw right through him. And given that in Chris? life, he had plenty of say, he wouldn’t stop until he landed his best friend the woman of his dreams.
