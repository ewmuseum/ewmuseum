All part of the Plan

5 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5 2005 Johnny's hand fell from Eva's arm only to shove her forward into the darkened room.She winced at the pain caused from that irate grip on her arm only to cry out as he literally hurled her into the darkness of his locker room. The force at which he sent her forth caused those normally agile feet to stumble breaking one of the heels of her spiked shoes.With a click and a slam pale illumination spread throughout the locker room and the door is closed tightly. Every inch of his muscle bound body is tense with anger, his eye brows furrowed together tightly and his lips pulled into a tight grimace. Collapsing to the floor she stifled the urge to cry instead grinding those teeth in anger as she reached out for the bench she had narrowly missed to pull herself up. Pale eyes locked on Eva as she used the small bench in the center of the sparsely decorated room to get back to her feet. ?I leave you alone for fifteen minutes and you're fucking somebody else?? He groans through gritted teeth. ?And of all people it had to be Trey fucking Tremendous. Icy blue eyes would lift to stare daggers at Johnny's angry face as he spewed off about her latest choice of a cheap lay. ' You do NOT own me Johnny Lexicon.' She could almost feel adrenaline pounding through his veins with a vengeful fury, something a woman scorned could aspire to, but a task she had done purposefully. That piercing gaze watched his stride towards her. He looked like a hungry predator every step agonizingly slow and calculated. Johnny's teeth were planted together so tightly she sore she hear them grind as he spoke in a grating voice full of bitterness and irony. ?Own you?? Another step taken. ?You expect me to own something as worthless as YOU?!? She had no time to react as his hand suddenly shot out of it's own accord and connected with the soft flesh of her cheek in a force he likely never used on his opponents in the ring. 'CRACK' Her head reeled to the side pain exploding behind her eyes and cheek as she let out a loud cry of pain. For a moment it seemed as if everything slowed down while she recollected her jumbled brain, then, before he could react, her own hand would snap forth to deliver a harsh open palm slap in return.His head turned with the force of her blow, she was stronger than she looked. A snide smile threatening at the edge of lips only to fall at the sight of those blue eyes snapping back to glare at her. It was all going to plan. Eva watched for a moment as his tongue dart out to wipe away the blood trickling form the corner of his lips before they curled up in a most macabre manner. It was here she broke the heavy silence in the aftermath of violence. ' WHO the HELL do you think you are Johnny? A prize? You best look in the mirror, you are nothing to be proud of yourself. In fact, you know,what? We are through!' It had been a calculation made to look the mistake as his hand snaked forward with all the speed of a cracking whip and took her arm again spinning her to face him. Oddly she was surprised by the force with which he grabbed her arm as it had her whirling to collide with him chest to chest as he growled in her face. ?I'll be the one to say when we're through.? He growls. ?You don't walk away from this that easy.? Slender brow arched as she growled back, ' Let go of my arm Johnny.' She knew he wouldn't. The drugs in his system were likely in full force by now with the elevation in his blood pressure...all part of the plan. Inwardly she was smiling despite the pain. It was the same every time they fought, one got mad, they screamed at each other, the fists flew..but in the end they always ended up in bed. Again....all part of a carefully laid plan. Next step.... Snarling with disgust she wrenched her arm from his grasp rendering his hand empty empty of her flesh. Ice met ice for a second as she noted the curling of tight fist with bittersweet amusement and spat out,' God Johnny you have turned into a regular junky you know that. Clean yourself up.' And with that she turned and began to hobble towards the door on broken heels. She smiled at the sound of his heated breathing quickening. Puurrrrrrrfect. She knew what would happen now. A battle within him would begin. He would think 'let her leave' but something that egomaniac side of him would win out. He had to have control, the last word if you will and she knew this all to well.BAM! There is was the sound of him stalking up behind her as his voice boomed in the silence of the locker room. ?It's not me who needs to get cleaned up.? In an instant he was behind her, his left hand caught in her hair before she could open the door. Her screech of rage and surprise shattering against the walls as he whipped her around and flung against the cinder block. He was fast and forceful, far more so than he had been the last time they were physical. It had left her breathless but not broken. No, he couldn't break, perhaps that was all a part of her strange effect on him. His face was inches from hers when he spoke again. The tone, a mixture of anger and something huskier. ?How does it feel to be a notch in the belt?? He was taunting her with it all as his free hand found the top button of her blouse. Hot breath washed over her face as tears of anger stung at the back of her eyes from that tight hold on her head arching that lovely neck at an easy access angle.It was beginning to excite her. Still, it was all part of the plan. His taunt was met with a husky laugh and a jab of her own. ' I don't how does it feel to be a notch on my lipstick case Johnny?' Before he could answer a hand snaked up behind him wrapping fingers tightly in his hair as she forcibly pulled him to her for a brutal kiss which would leave them both panting for more. She had won. Johnny's hand ripped open her shirt and the heat of his anger was taken out on her in the same way it always was. How easy it all was, Johnny lusted after her like a dog in heat and she manipulated him like a master. In the aftermath of their rather tumultuous joining he pulled her down across his chest. Somewhere along the way they had ended up on his bench. She offered no resistance allowing him to pull her down with him, that human succubus purring like a kitten against his neck. ' That was lovely, though we really need to try to have sex without anger every now and then.' A chaste kiss was placed on his check as she shifted gears once more. The ice on her exterior coming back in full mode as she untangled herself from his grip and hoisted that lithe frame up. Quickly she kicked those heels off as she pulled her skirt back to rights. Those icy blue eyes would linger upon his locker as she non nonchalantly moved to the metal closet and tugged it open to retrieve one of his white teeshirts quickly tugging it over her exposed bosom. With a simple knot tied at the midriff she moved towards the door and called back airily. ' I am going to get a coke, you want one?' ?Just get out.? It was always that way after they fought and fucked. She would get up acting like nothing happened and he would tell her to get out only to call her back in a few minutes later. Of all the drugs he was on, she was his number one addiction next to the ring. Secretly she loathed that. She never liked playing second fiddle, but in this case, she would play along. Slender fingers tapped on the edge of the door a moment as she struggled against laughing at him. Fool. Instead, she merely paused at the door with that all knowing smirk and cattily replied in kind. ' Fine. But don't expect me to coming running back to you if you call me. You want me? You know where I will be.....' Turning to face him with an evil gleam in those eyes.. ' Trey's locker room....' And with that she stepped out and slammed the door with a grin. She knew two things. One, Johnny would never call her, but then that was all part of the plan, right? Second her taunt would piss him smooth off....it was her intention. Chuckling wryly she waltzed down the hallway to the predetermined meeting place with her brother...the plan had gone off without a hitch...as usual. She would pause only a breath as she heard the telltale signs of Johnny's frustration in the way of his locker room door slamming and him walking off in the opposite direction. Perfection, really it was....or was it? Feet hit against the cool linoleum floors as she rounded the corner to find Erik waiting for her as usual. One look at his face and she could see the moment of anger in his eyes at the reddened blotch on her cheek. Larger finger gripped her jaw angling her head so he could examine the damage as he murmured in neutral tone. ' How did it go?' ' Perfect as usual.' ' He got rough this time.' She shrugged lifting her chin out of his grip and smiled. ' He is always rough. I played him like a fiddle.' He chuckled and shook his head planting a small kiss on her brow. 'Good girl.' ' Take me home. I need a bath.' She smiled at him as he took his jacket and draped it over her shoulders. Soon enough she was tucked beneath her twin's powerful arm protectively and they were headed out the door. Everything was perfect.....all part of the plan.
