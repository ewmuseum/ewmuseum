Cuatro - Treat Him Like A Lady

26 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 26th 2009 In the wake of the Masked Luchador’s devastating loss to Shaman WorldWide 119, both he and his unofficial agent/manager/boot polisher were in need of some rest and relaxation, particularly as the man from south of the border was set to be in action again this week, squaring off against not one, but two opponents, before taking his place in the infamous Dangerous Games main event. Not only was M3 craving a physical break, he also needed some mental downtime and a smooth build-up to a match for once; near riots in theme parks don’t exactly help focus the mind on the job at hand, after all. All of which explains why the most Mexican man in GCW was at this very moment being checked in at a secluded spa resort just outside San Diego, his heart set on a day of pampering without the continual distractions that seem to crop up everywhere else. So, how do you think it turns out? *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘So, are we going for the seaweed wrap or the volcanic mud mask first?’ The beauty therapist was everything you’d expect – mid-twenties, tanned, blonde and exceedingly friendly. Exceedingly friendly. ‘Or perhaps you’d like the back massage and sea salt scrub?’ Jorge was grinning like a specific breed of cat, one from Cheshire, to be precise. Clad only in a complimentary white bathrobe, the chest hair periscoping over the top of the fabric is not a pretty sight. ‘This es the life, eh mi amigo!?’ M3 is as stoic as ever, his shrug this time saying nothing more than ‘Eh, it’s okay.’. ‘We will take the works chica!’ Her face registered surprised for the merest instant before professionalism returned. Usually men had the basic package, even flamboyant queens only took one or two treatments. Yet here were these two, taking the full luxury ensemble. ‘Okay then! If you two gentlemen just lay down, we will be with you in a second.’ Jorge eagerly obliges, hopping up onto the table and laying face down as the beauty technician laid a towel over him before removing his robe. M3 wasn’t quite as keen, the sturdiness of the table under his considerable bulk as much a concern as anything. ‘Oh come on ese! Es all bueno!’ Cautiously, warily, M3 climbs onto the table, wedging his face into the hole in the table as he and his friend await the arrival of their masseurs. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ 53 Minutes Later ‘And we’re all done!’ The smile of contentment and relaxation on Jorge’s face told the story as he started to get off the table. ‘That was fantastico!’ ‘Well I’m glad you enjoyed it!’ Unfortunately at that precise moment, she saw just how much he had enjoyed it. ‘Oh my god, sir, your robe!’ Not to dwell on it but it wasn’t pretty. For some reason though, Jorge didn’t realise his faux pas. ‘Es okay baby, we all friends here, no?’ It may have been the accompanying wink that did it, it may have been the sight of Jorge’s little man coming to attention, but the high-pitched shriek as the masseur flew from the room was one of unmistakeable panic. Jorge, bemused, could only call after her. ‘This no es happy ending chica! What es going on?!’ On the other table, M3 sighed in exasperation. So much for a quiet day. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ After managing to track the terrified and quite frankly, offended, young masseur down, Jorge managed to make a reasonable job of the apology, although his attempt to ‘hug it out’ didn’t go down too well. ‘I’m sorry amigo, we can still relax now eh?’ The headphones covering M3’s ears clearly meant nothing to Jorge. As they lay on the sun loungers that surrounded the large, circular pool at the centre of the spa complex, the Unknown Luchador and his pant-forgetting associate made a bizarre couple, which in a way they were. Jorge, for instance, knew exactly what was his friend’s mind. ‘Don’t joo be worried about the match, es going to be muy facil! Joo just gotta keep low, take all oportunidades, joo know?’ Again, the headphones meant that M3 could get away without giving an answer. Jorge, however, didn’t take the hint. ‘I mean, joo just need to stay outside, then jump in. Es perfecto! All those idiotas are gonna be looking dumb!’ Still not getting any reply, Jorge decided to give up, at least for the time being. ‘Okay Senor Gruñón! Time for sauna!’ Jorge gets to his feet (robe closed tight shut), and makes his way inside the building as the Masked Luchador savours the sound of silence. He should have known it wouldn’t last long. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ It had probably been about 5 minutes since Jorge had left, but it seemed a lot longer. Peace and quiet becomes a much more valuable commodity when you spend time with the inimitable Mr Ramos. He was just about to get into the pool for a quick dip when he was disturbed by lots of loud shouting. Shouting coming from the direction Jorge had gone in. Shouting coming from lots of women. This couldn’t be good. ‘No, no, es an accident! Es an accident!’ The sight that greets the Unknown Luchador as he turns round is not an everyday scene, to say the least. It looks like a stampede. At the front of the group is Jorge, thankfully still wearing his swimshorts. Behind are 6 angry women in various states of undress. Although he has no clue what has just gone on, M3 can make a fairly good stab at it by listening to the shouts that are chasing the terrified Mr Ramos. ‘Pervert!’ ‘My husband’s gonna kick your ass!’ ‘You put that thing near me again and I’ll rip it off!’ At this stage Jorge has around a 20 yard lead and as he stops momentarily at a fork in the path, he spies GCW’s masked superstar and makes a beeline for him, all the time apologising to the lynch mob that is in pursuit. ‘Praise dios! Quick, get the keys, vamos ahora!’ M3 looks at his manager for a second in confusion, before snatching his phone and keys off the sun lounger and sprinting for the car park. The gap had closed now to about 10 yards and it appeared that some of these ladies had played softball in the past, as they were now being pelted with all sorts of objects, from stones, to soft drinks, to flip-flops. As they ran, the Unknown Luchador looked at his compatriot as if to say ‘What did you do?’. ‘It was…an accident…huh…I walk into the…huh, huh…wrong room!’ He was slowing down but the women, driven on by estrogen and adrenaline, were showing no signs of fading. There was nothing else for it. With one smooth motion, M3 picked Jorge up, tossed him over his shoulder and kept running towards their car, a rented convertible. As Jorge looked back, the panic was increasing. ‘Rápidamente! Rápidamente! They are catching!’ The car was only a few feet away now so M3 simply tossed his friend into the backseat and vaulted into the driver’s seat. Seconds later, with the smell of rubber lingering behind them and Jorge’s head stuck somewhere between the front seats, they were gone, leaving an angry mob and an unpaid $400 bill. Just the kind of preparation you need before a big match, no?
