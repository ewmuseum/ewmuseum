Operation: FOX VI

26 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/10 Previously on Operation: FOX... After failing yet another mission, and accidently slugging his father with a sledgehammer, Clyde Fox and company returned back home to strategize their next move. They certainly could all feel the pressure, knowing that this was going to be the most crucial decision of the operation. However, our heroes were at a disadvantage when The Prince of The Playroom decided to forgo his responsibilities, and drown his sorrows away at a bar with Jay Terror. Fox developed milk poisoning after consuming what was rumored to be in the neighborhood of six gallons of two percent milk. After searching for their leader for an extended period of time, Suzy and Clowl finally secured Fox. It wasn’t long before the moment of truth came, and Clyde Fox was in front of all his friends, telling them that he was finished with everything. Clyde was convinced that his inability to succeed was just going to further complicate things, and that the best course of action was for him to just go away. However, after the amount of support his underlings showed, Fox couldn’t resist to stay. Now Fox and company are more energized than ever! They have their sights set on ending this mission once and for all, by any means necessary. The only question that remained was how? How can our heroes make sure Clyde doesn’t die by the way fate has it written out? If indeed, fate has him dying so far ahead. Why, it isn’t a matter of going back at all. It’s by doing something they had never thought possible; traveling to the future... This is the beginning of the end. Awwwww sh*t. CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! There stood Crayden Ragner, Kyle Bold, Suzy Yates, Onjin Wiznaaski and Clowl as they clapped invitingly in front of their skittish leader, Clyde Fox. "Peeze, peeze. You are all too kind. I don’t deserve this kind of tweatment," Fox humbly accepted. They all eventually moved in and embraced Fox. They knew that without him, their chances of chance were greatly diminished. Clowl even wiped a rare tear from his feathery eye. They sure were glad not to see Fox go. Don’t forget Cyrus, too. The talking transport was parked safely in back, balling like a young woman watching her emotional soap operas. "We’re so glad you’re staying, Clyde," Suzy celebrated as she pecked him on the cheek, "We just wouldn’t know what to do without you." Fox pressed his lips together as he looked up to the hangar’s cavernous ceiling structure. He noticed how the diagonal bars supported each other, just like his group backed him. If even one of these supporting beams weren’t there, the entire roof would cave in. Fox knew that he couldn’t just leave his team behind. There was still work to be done. "I know, I know," Fox recognized as he embraced his team back, "But wisten. We don’t have time to waste being all sappy and stuff. Usually, I like me some emo cakes, but that’s only when it’s Fwiday night and I’m in my jammies watching Steven Seagal films. It’s not Fwiday, nor is Steven Seagal on so let’s get on task guys." The group slowly broke away to their workstations. The nerdy Onjin hunched over the control panel as Crayden just sort of looked around while taking sips from his coffee mug. Suzy snatched the coordinates for their next desired mission as Clowl and Kyle conversed. "So then boss," Suzy started, "What exactly is the plan? Fox rubbed his chin in deliberation. He wasn’t quite sure himself, but he did have one rather radical idea. "The future," Fox muttered. Everyone stopped and turned back to Clyde. The future? No way! Did he just propose time traveling to the future? But that sort of thing had most certainly gone untested. Every other mission was back to the past. But why the future, and why now? "You see... we’ve dinked around too much in the past, messing everything up. Why don’t we just travel to the future, right before I get hurted or something?" Fox proposed. At first the faces of his friends looked anything but reasonable. But as Fox’s words sunk in, they slowly glanced back and forth at each other, conceding how much sense their leader was actually talking. "That... that could work," Crayden mentioned, "But the key word is could work. You see, Cyrus isn’t properly equipped for future time travel. So the only way to do that would be to open up a time-managed wormhole and to go through it manually. As in, just you go, Clyde. But, like I said, we don’t know the ramifications of such an event." Worried looks overtook the many faces around Clyde. He wasn’t quite sure what to do after his good friend informed him of the unknown risks. Banking on that fact that there were probably going to be many risks, Fox had no choice but to go for it, especially if he truly wanted to change the world’s fate. "I understand Cwayden," Fox acknowledged, "But I think the ends justify the means. I have to take this risk, just like Madonna does each time she goes on stage, what with her bladder problem and all." It took a moment before Ragner nodded in agreement. He put his opinion aside and rolled over beside Onjin and began configuring a personal time traveling device. Suzy placed a hand on Fox’s shoulder in support. "Are you sure you want to do this?" Her sweet voice rang. "Are Trix for kids?" Fox nodded in his reply. Just then Fox turned and noticed that Kyle was rather far away from the group. He was intrinsically inspecting one of his wide-angle lens cameras. Clyde made his way over to his time traveling companion as the rest of them remained by Crayden and the control desk. "Heto," Fox piped up, grabbing Kyle’s attention. Bold looked up at Fox for a brief moment before continuing tinkering with his camera. "Oh hey," Kyle lobbed, "So I guess everything is set then, eh? You’re going to be the hero of your own story once more... going to the future to save yourself from getting killed. Kind of poetic if you ask me. The only part that sucks is that I guess you’ll be going alone, eh?" Clearly bummed out that his time traveling escapades were over, Kyle kept his attention on his camera. The journalist had been a great compliment to Clyde throughout their time together, but the unfortunate truth was that it really was a solo job now. "Hey Kyle," Fox cheered, "I wewy sowwie. If I could take someone, I would have taken you again bro. If you want, you don’t have to stay here. I’m sure you have a ton of material to create lots of good stories for da newspaper." "Yeah, I guess you’re right, man," Kyle agreed, "Well then, I guess I’m going to grab my gear and head out. Clyde, it was really nice working with you and I hope we meet again in the future... thanks... friend." Kyle walked up and patted Fox on the shoulder before walking off into the distance. Clyde was most certain they’d meet again. "FINISHED!" Ragner’s voice sprung with excitement. Fox cycled back and saw Crayden standing tall atop the control panel, waving a small crystal-like device around. It’s light blue shine made it transparent to a certain degree. Clyde skipped back up to the rest of the group and nearly slobbered all over the magical crystal. "How do you like it, man?" Ragner bragged, "I managed to compile data and remnants from Cyrus’ memory banks in order to create it. I’m not sure how useful it’ll actually be, but it’s the only shot we got." But just before Fox could open his mouth and reply... KA-BOOM! The earth around them shook as dust and debris shuttered from the ceiling. "What was that?" Suzy screamed in fear. A choppy helicopter propeller could be heard whisking around the building. The evening sun fell off the radar as the hangar lair was overcome with a bright spotlight. Synthetic light blazed through each crack, door and window. Everyone inside covered their eyes as they heard an all too familiar female voice over a PA system. "Prepare for trouble!" The she lady spoke, "Give it up Clyde! We’ve got you surrounded!" "What? Who in the world is that?" A couple of them questioned. "It’s... it’s that mega bitch Candace Fulmont," Fox informed, "You know, that self proclaimed professional bitch who I turned down on a date and now she can’t move on." "That’s not true!" The lady’s voice rang through the PA, "I turned you down! And no one turns down the great Candace Fulmont! NOBODY!" The group huddled together in hopes to defend against the untimely and unexpected attack of the super bitch. It really didn’t matter why she was there. All she cared about was preventing Clyde Fox from changing his fate and living a peaceful life. "I’ve brought my army with me, and this time they won’t go down so easily!" Fulmont taunted from within her helicopter, "Now meet your true doom CLYDE FOX!" The buzzing of the helicopter got closer as it created a surreal panic. Fox’s forehead dripped sweat nonstop as the spotlight peaked through the open cargo doors of the hangar. The helicopter finally came into full view, and along with it came row after row of heavily armed infantry. "Oh no! They’re here!" Clowl indicated as he huddled firmly against Fox’s leg. Knowing that if this scared him; he’d have no way of facing his true destiny. So Clyde took a deep breath and stepped forward. "I WON’T WET YOU HURT MY FWENDS! YOU’LL HAVE TO GO THROUGH ME FIRST!" Fox shouted. The array of firearm bolts locking into place accompanied the soldiers converging on Fox and friends. The helicopter’s spotlight drew downward, allowing for the troops to see their way to Clyde. EM-ER-EM-ER EMMMMM! The clanging and clunking of twisting metal echoed behind our heroes as chills shot up their spines. They collectively turned their heads just in time to witness a mechanoid version of Cyrus sprawling right overtop of them. Clowl hoots oddly before flying off towards Cyrus. "HOOT! HOOT!" Clowl hooted. And just like that, Clowl morphed into the back of the humanoid shaped Cyrus, giving it a feathery undertow. "Hokey smokes! Did Cyrus just totally, like, transform and then, like, Clowl just totally morphed into him?" Clyde deducted, "This is totally like Pokemon meets Transformers!" "You leave my friends alone," The silvery-blue mechanoid defended, "For my name is Clyrus, the transforming android owl type thingy..." "This is even more epic than a Rikki Roxx concert!" Ragner noted. The heroes looked on in amazement. They never knew that Cyrus had such ability. And after all they had gone through, Cyrus, the talking dump truck that was useless to everyone else, had finally come in handy. "Pfft. Pathetic! Is that your last line of defense, Clyde? Well boys, knock ‘em down!" Candace directed from the cozy confine of her helicopter. Just like that, the foot soldiers moved forward, opening fire on the newly morphed beast. Bullets deflected off of Clyrus’ shiny metallic armor. Their tiny guns were certainly no match for the two-story-tall mechanoid. A battle so crazy, that it could only happen on this plane unfurled before everyone’s eyes. "Get them Clyrus!" Fox shouted from below. It wasn’t long before our heroes noticed many of the soldiers rushing by the much slower Clyrus. There were more than enough soldiers to keep Clyrus’ hands full, allowing some to filter on through. They had a deadly look in their eyes. It was a look that said they would take absolutely no prisoners. Our heroes scrambled back, not sure what to do. "I think I wet myself!" Onjin screamed as he clung onto Crayden for dear life. "LISTEN! WE HAVE NO TIME!" Crayden reasoned. They were getting closer. Time seemed to slow down as each soldier climbed the stairs one by one towards their prey. "CLYDE! YOU’VE GOT TO TAKE THE CRYSTAL AND GO BACK RIGHT NOW!" Ragner yelled. Our heroes turned and saw soldier after soldier ready their guns. They proceeded to unleash hell. BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- BRAKKA- Bullets rained down like fire from the heavens as they all scrambled for their lives. It was too dark to tell who got hit and who didn’t, but somehow, someway, their adrenaline kicked in. "QUICK! TAKE IT! TAKE IT AND BE FREE! NOT JUST FOR US, BUT FOR THE REST OF THE WORLD! I believe you can do it... friend." Crayden spoke as he placed the crystal into Fox’s hand. PIT-CHU! PIT-CHU! PIT-CHU! "AHHHHHHHHHHHH!" Ragner screamed as he fell to the ground. One by one Fox looked around him. Onjin got his teeth knocked out of his jaw as a soldier came up and smashed him as hard as he could right in the face with the butt of his rifle. Crayden was shot numerous times, all in different places. And lastly, Suzy was thrown over the railing, surely sent to her doom far below. He knew he had to run but his knees became weak. Clyde Fox stared death right in the face before forcing himself to move. "I won’t let you die Cwayden!" Fox cried before huddling over one of his best friends ever. Ragner’s blood soaked right through Fox’s shirt. He knew that there was nothing he could do to help his fallen comrade. "G-get going ya little twerp," Crayden said heroically, "I’ll be okay." As Fox could hear the soft notes of a violin being played, he rose to his knees. He wept like a baby. His cheeks were speckled with blood and soaked in tears. He didn’t want to play anymore. He had enough. He just simply wanted to take his ball and go home. No more of this fate stuff. No more of this death stuff. He just wanted to sit in his backyard and eat grilled cheese sandwiches. With extra ketchup. And maybe a pickle. Clyde Fox was done. A small group of soldiers made a half circle around him, basking in the moment. They knew that they were going to get what they came for. Or were they? SW-SW-SWOOSH! Suddenly, the same blue light that the crystal embodied seemed to appear directly behind Clyde. Tiny electrical bolts reached out and latched onto Clyde. He didn’t want to go, but he knew he had to. Fox locked eyes with the other soldiers, as they were only helpless to watch what would happen next. Clyde glided backwards, into the wormhole, leaving his friends battered, bloodied, and bruised. "I’m going to fix this... friends," Fox spoke softly. A massive white light cascaded throughout the hangar. Clyde Fox had made it into the wormhole. His last mission-- his greatest mission awaited him on the other side. A final date with the death of his future self... and his future killer. To Be Continued...
