Date: 06/12/06 Standing around in the kitchen was accomplishing nothing, but a look at the world outside the window didn't exactly fill Dawn with an overwhelming need to go outside. She'd always felt out of place in America no matter where she went, and being in the capital did very little to assuage her feelings of being an outsider. ?Not that being here gives me warm fuzzies either...? She scowled, looking over one cream coloured shoulder into the living room of the flat that Dies Irae had rented for Warpath. The TV was off and the furniture was unoccupied and were it not for the play station plugged into the front of the television set, she would have sworn that no one at all had so much as entered the room since they arrived. And by ?they? she meant Seymour and herself. It was possible that Vivica was lurking about somewhere nearby, but Dawn rather preferred that the stables own blonde bombshell be elsewhere most of the time anyways. She didn't much care for being looked at like she was some particularly loathsome type of fungus you'd just scraped from the bottom of your hiking boots. There were days she could convince herself she didn't care, but on days like today it was an act of extreme will not to explode into random violence despite being mostly alone. Folding her arms around her midriff, she hugged herself as though for warmth, despite the pervasive heat of the world outside. The weight of things she'd been unable to say to either Lia or Grey wasn't so heavy, but in the absence of that weight was a feeling so stark and so familiar, it made her heart ache to feel it...isolation. She resisted the urge to sniffle, catching the back of her hand as it was halfway to her nose and staring at it in disgust. ?Get a fucking hold of yourself.? She growled, ?Aren't you a little old for the woe is me routine?? Stalking from the kitchen, Dawn skirted the door to Seymours room and strode down the carpeted hallway to the room she'd claimed for herself. Not quite slamming the door she flung herself into bed with a frustrated growl, hands knotting in the still sweat damped sheets. Releasing her grip she flipped over onto her back, arms spread wide as she stared up at the wood and brass ceiling lamp that spun lazily over her bed. The gentle beat of the fan blade was almost soothing with the backdrop of Seymours Final Fantasy music, Dawn's eyes dropping lower as some of the tension she was holding in her shoulders bled out into the mattress. Her mind was spinning, the chaos of being wrenched from sleep still made her mind ache from the strain of it. As if that wasn't enough, she was on the eve of something great, a chance to do something so monumental that it would shock wrestling fans the world over... ...a chance to defend against another has been. She smirked, that maddening half smile that had become burned into the brains of so many of her opponents. It let her ignore the pangs of uncertainty in her belly, the voice in the back of her mind that whispered he was different somehow...that Lestatt Knight could be ?the one?. In truth her pride still stung from her first lost, handed so casually over by a woman who wasn't even supposed to be a part of her life anymore. A familiar arm looped around Dawn's thigh from behind and then the unfamiliar sound of a hand slapping the mat three times...coupled with the look of disdain that had been laid down by her ?partner?. ?Damn him...? She growled, thumping one fist into the mattress for emphasis. She didn't feel any better for having hit the bed so she did it again. Even three solid blows did little to assuage the feelings of frustration welling up inside her. If Tessa Windsor could get the drop on her, what about Lestatt Knight? And to top it all off, the mystery aches and pains that kept flaring along her back and neck left her doubting whether she was going to be one hundred percent for Warpath. Rolling onto one side she gingerly reached around her shoulder, trailing her fingers over the aching spots that ran just parallel to the sides of her spine. ?Why couldn't we have been closer to Ontario?? She groused, resting her head in the crook of her arm and staring absently at the cream coloured stucco walls that made up her bedroom. It had been long since she was out of the Estate for any length of time, and the truth was she'd become something of a home body. She longed for the familiar twists and turns of her home and the sounds of life that had sprung up around it. And in truth she longed for Cyrus' counsel, even the carefully manicured condescension in his tone would have been a welcome change to the confusion she was feeling... *************************************************************************************** ?You look a little lost Lady McFarlane, are you looking for something?? Cyrus' voice was low and rich, sliding over the carpeted floor of the Estates library like cream, drawing a startled yelp from the Estates owner as she fumbled to keep the book in her hands from careening to the floor. ?Jesus christ on a cross you scared me...? Her eyes were wide in a mixture of startled fear and surprise. She had known Cyrus spent much of his time in the library, but hadn't thought he would have said anything to her while she was searching. ?My apologies Lady McFarlane, I did not mean to startle you so.? There was a hint of amusement in his voice, like he had in fact intended for exactly that to happen and was only saying otherwise to be civil. She stared at him hard for a moment, his apparent amusement not lost on her...before she decided it was all in her head and shrugged easily. ?It's no biggie, I was pretty absorbed in what I was reading.? ?Mmm yes, so I had noticed.? He glided over to the table, his misshapen form moving with a speed and grace that Dawn had always found disconcerting. Too long fingers flipped idly through the books she had spread out on the table. ?Hmmm...'Nightmare Creatures', 'The Hunters Hunted', 'The Damned and the Fallen'...really Lady McFarlane, I'd never pegged you for an interest in Demonology.? His black eyes moved from the pages on the table to her own wide eyes, fixing there with a momentary intensity that stole most of the breath from her body. For a long moment she could only wait, her throat as dry as sand, and hope that his next words weren't accusatory. ?And what is it, pray tell, that a woman like you could possibly want from the study of demons and devils...Lady McFarlane?? ?I...need to know...? She stammered, taking an involuntary step back. Cryus didn't hesitate, taking a step forward as she stepped back, his posture low and hunched as though he was stalking her, his entire seven foot frame seeming coiled in preparation for sudden movement. ?And what exactly is it that you need to know? Certainly if you're interested in arts of a...sorcerous persuasion, Lady Vlaszy could explain them to you...dabbling with demons is far beyond you little one.? She bristled at that, taking a step back forward and regretting it almost immediately. ?It's not like that at all! I'm not stupid you know!? He smirked then, standing a little straighter and laying one long finger against the curve of his cheek. ?Oh really? Then what exactly is it you're doing in a dark corner of the library researching a subject that's seen many young ladies burned at the stake?? ?I...? She faltered mid sentence, then looked into his bottomless eyes and steeled herself. ?I need to know what's happening to me.? His left eye got a little larger, as though he were lifting the hairless eyebrow above it. ?Happening to you? You need to research demonology in order to find out what's...happening to you? My dear, if you've had truck with demons then the physical transformations are the least...? ?No!? She said, slashing one hand down for emphasis. ?I've not had ANY truck with demons I...I...think I might...be one.? He snorted at that, throwing his arms up and turning away from her. ?Preposterous! In the extremely unlikely event that you were of a demonic nature, somebody would have surely noticed something by now...likely yourself. These kind of things don't just sneak up on you Lady McFarlane, it's not like going through puberty.? ?You...know something about demonology?? She tried not to sound too hopeful, already sensing a brush-off in his stand-offish tone. ?I know enough to know that you do not want to be meddling in something as...? ?Please.? She cut into his sentence, the one word earnest enough to stop him mid-word and draw his dark gaze back to where she was standing, clutching the book to her chest. ?Please I need to know.? He stared at her for a long moment before sitting back slowly into one of the library chairs, steepling his long fingers in front of his face. ?Perhaps you had best tell me what it is that's made you so...determined to find out if something demonic dwells within you....all of it.? She moved to the table and sat down, placing the book down in front of her. The story came slowly at first, starting with the dreams she had of her mother and the strange pains that often came when she woke. It expanded to include her meeting with Lillith in Japan, and the visit from Roshael... ?...who said she was my mother, and that I had inherited certain things from her.? It all seemed so unrealistic, so fantastic as to defy reason. For his part Cyrus appeared largely unmoved, gazing at her impassively over the back of one slender hand. ?And what reason do you have to believe this woman was something more then human?? ?Wings.? Dawn said simply, ?She had huge leathery wings, and little tiny horns on her forehead.? He leaned forward sharply, eyes drawn to one of the books lying on the table. ?Wings you say? And Horns?...tell me...did she have a tail that you noticed?? Dawn shook her head slowly, almost unable to believe that she was really having this conversation. ?Not that I saw no...her wings were pretty attention getting.? ?Hmm...and was she attractive this Roshael?? ?Well I guess s...? Dawn started to say before stopping herself, nodding slowly. ?Yeah she was...and not beautiful or anything she was...hot.? ?Indeed.? He seemed to mull that over in his mind, staring at a point in the library just over her shoulder. ?And what makes you think her claims to your parentage have any weight?? ?She remembered what I said the day she left.? ?Hmph.? The non-committal sound was his only reply. He stood suddenly, brushing off the front of his shirt. ?Take off your shirt please.? She blinked, a fiery blush creeping up into her cheeks. ?W-what?!? He favored her with a look that brooked little argument, his tone sounded much the same. ?Your shirt please. If you want me to assist you, then I must be able to examine your physical form and see if any changes are taking place that we cannot see.? Still blushing she stood, turning her back to him and slipping her black t-shirt off over her head. She stifled a gasp at the feel of his hands on her back, managing to do nothing more then jerk slightly when he touched her. ?Hold still.? He commanded, fingers tapping down the length of her spine. She grimaced, the cool touch of his skin a little too reminiscent of some of the other men in her life. She closed her eyes and started to count backwards from one hundred, thinking of anything other then standing here topless with strange hands on her bare skin. ?Hmmm...does that hurt?? Expert fingers probed into a spot on either side of her spine, making her shudder suddenly and jerk her body away. ?Sorta...it feels kind of raw when you do that, like a muscle I've pulled or over strained.? ?Interesting.? He murmered, hands moving down to the small of her back where they poked and prodded at the skin around her tail bone. When his long fingers slipped into the rim of her jeans she jumped, spinning in mid air and fixing him with a furious look. ?Now LOOK!? She started to say. ?Miss McFarlane please. If I am going to determine what may or may not be happening to you, then I am going to need to examine the vast majority, if not all of, your body. The sooner you accept that, the sooner we can move on from these distractions.? ?Look, I appreciate what you're trying to do here, but there is NO WAY I'm going to let you start putting your hands down my pants. That's about as likely as...? ?A vampire walking in daylight?? He smirked, ?Fine, but I will need to examine your tail bone at some point. For the moment come here please.? Reluctantly she crossed the space between them, stopping where indicated as he stepped up to her. ?Open your mouth please.? She complied, barely able to hold back a shiver of revulsion as he pushed her upper lip away from her gums and touched the exposed red skin with his thumb. Applying pressure with his thumb, he squeezed the roof of her mouth with his index finger. There was a jerk of pain and the unpleasant sensation of bone against bone, followed by a slight popping noise. ?Aha...? He murmered. ?Amrha?? She mumbled around his fingers, trying again after he'd removed his hand from her mouth. ?Aha?? ?Yes. Were you aware that your eye teeth extend much further into your gumline then normal? In fact it's as though they're sitting in a set of...? ?Gumsheathes.? She finished for him, trying to talk around the pair of sharp teeth that were suddenly poking into her lower lip. It gave her a lisp that rasped on her nerves, but Cyrus didn't appear to have taken notice. ?Lia and I discovered them shortly after...? She coughed, ?...we started seeing each other.? ?Indeed....can you make them extend like that on command? They seemed firmly in place when I massaged them into extending.? Dawn shook her head slowly, wishing she could will them back into their sheaths. ?Not that I've discovered. They only extend when I'm mad or when I'm...ah...welll...? ?Aroused?? He said the word like any other, choosing not to notice the fiery blush that spread along her cheeks. ?I had suspected as much.? Turning back to the table, he spoke over one shoulder.?I will need to examine you in more detail of course, but that can wait until I have access to a more appropriate facility. And of course Miss Vlazsy will be able to lend her considerable expertise...? ?No!? The force of it brought his head around, that same fleshy eyebrow raised. ?No? Why not?? ?I don't want Lia to know yet...I don't want anyone to know until I have something solid to tell them. After everything Lillith told me about what I could represent...I don't know what anyone will think.? She ducked her eyes to the carpet, feeling his sardonic gaze bore into the top of her skull. ?Very well. You have sheltered me while I was insensate, and so I will do as you ask...but I should tell you that I think it is folly to try and keep this from anyone around you. If you are what you suspect yourself to be, eventually you will manifest in a way that is impossible to hide. It would be best for everyone if you were honest before that ever happened.? His voice wasn't disapproving, but it was certainly brutally honest. ?Thank you.? ?Hold you thanks Miss McFarlane, you may not wish to thank me so much when this is over...? ****************************************************************************************** Dawn sighed explosively, hopefully expelling a cloud of negative chi with the cloud of breath. At some point in her reverie the music had stopped, leaving the sound of the fan beating the air as the only sound in her bedroom. Rolling her neck to one side, she sighed at the bright red display on the front of her alarm clock. ?Time to go,? She sighed, ?Everybody wants their piece of flesh before the big show.? She heaved herself out of bed and set to digging in her bag for something appropriate to wear with a title belt.
