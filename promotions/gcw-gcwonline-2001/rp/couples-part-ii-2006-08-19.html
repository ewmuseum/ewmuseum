Couples: Part II

19 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/19/06 ~The Brand-Northsky Residence~ ~Las Vegas, Nevada~ Dani sat at the kitchen table, browsing the Internet using her own laptop that she hardly ever used. Ukyo sat next to her. Josh, sulking still after being goaded into something he had to deliver on, was seated in the back of the room, watching one of the many TV's on a loveseat as the girls were planning his rather busy afternoon. ?So, it looks like the only plans we really need here are what time to make sure the chapel will be ready. Which one we want to use and of course is there anything else we need to get?? Ukyo looked at her. ?Blaise and I were considering at one point in having two ceremonies. One for my family in Japan and one for his over here. This was before we got access to the jet and could arrange for his family to fly to Japan for one ceremony. From what we found, it looks like the best one for your dollars was the Chapel inside the Aladdin Hotel.? Ukyo put her hands onto the keyboard and slid the laptop over a bit. She brought up the website for the Aladdin's chapel and started showing Dani the plans available. ?Those are nice. Hey, this one isn't too bad. It's not like we want a huge reception. It's got two nights in one of the big suites, so we could leave Gray alone afterwards, the bouquet and every thing. For only a couple grand.? Josh looked at her as if she were on fire or something and then went back to watching the TV. ?You have the your dress right?? Dani nodded. ?I have the one my grandmother made me. It was my mothers tribal dress that's been fitted to me now. Josh has a suit, I know this because I made him keep it after taking me out to dinner for my birthday a few years back. I know it still fits him too. So that would leave only the Rings left.? ?But he had an engagement ring?? Josh spoke up. ?That was my mother's. She gave it to me to use on Dani.? ?Okay, so you need rings. There are quite a few nice jewelers around Las Vegas. You can imagine with the wedding traffic they get here.? Dani looked to be tabulating things in her head. ?Okay, so it's reservations for the chapel, rings, license, and then later tonight get married. That sounds like it shouldn't be too hard...? Josh just shook his head as the two girls continued talking. He got up and walked away. Ukyo watched him as he headed to the bedroom he and Dani shared. ?You know he's not liking this right now.? Dani hit a few keys on her keyboard and the printer in the living room came to life. ?Well he'd better get happy really quick. Cause I'm not in the mood to wait forever.? Ukyo could only watch her as she got up and headed for the printer. ?Well, I'll have to say I'm with Blaise on this one, I don't think he'll do it. You sure you don't want anyone else there?? Dani shook her head 'No'. ?I'll not give him an excuse or any reason to crap out on it. We'll give a call when it's done. I'd love to have you all there if this was a more planned out thing, but in all reality I'm done waiting for the right moment or the right time. It's now... I'm sick of not knowing what is going to come tomorrow. The last stunt he pulled that almost got him or me killed was when I realized that things aren't always going to go our way. We can have a bigger ceremony later on, if everything works out right. In the meantime, it's something he and I need to do.? Ukyo nodded and then headed for the door. ?Well in that case, I'll let you two get to it. Give me a call if you change your mind or need anything else.? Dani gave her a thumbs up as she got onto her phone to call the chapel. ~A couple hours later~ ~Tower of Jewels~ Josh and Dani had been out for a few hours, and had gone to a few stores. And it wasn't getting any easier. They now came into the most well known and most well stocked jewelers in Las Vegas to find a pair of rings that would do them right in the ceremony they had booked for later on in the evening. They'd been in here for twenty minutes already and Dani had looked at five sets of rings. And so far Dani hadn't said a thing of support for one of them. Josh looked down into the case and spied one that he thought she'd like. ?Hey, can we see that set? Just the bands...? The guy knew that, he had seen the antique rock that was on Dani's finger that his father had given his mother, his second wife. The clerk reached in and pulled out the bands in question. They were a matching pair, one obviously larger, but not as pretty for the male side. The other was a band that was covered in two rows of diamonds going around it. Dani's eyes got big when she saw them. ?Whoah...? The clerk started pointing out to them. ?This men's ring is a platinum band done in the same manner as the woman's. They're both 18k platinum. The woman's band has 3.5ct of diamons in two rows going around the baguette.? Dani looked at the tag on the display and noticed the ring should be the right size. ?Do you mind?? The clerk looked at her and smiled. ?No, not at all. Go ahead.? She picked it up and slid it on. It fit just about perfectly. Josh spoke up. ?Do you have the men's band in a size larger? I've got big fingers from working out a lot.? ?Of course.? He reached down and grabbed a different display and held it out for Josh to look at the men's ring. Josh slid it on over the large knuckles he had from cracking them all the time. ?Nice. So, how much for the both of them?? The clerk almost chuckled, he could see it. Both were still in Jeans and T-shirts and it wasn't for sure if the clerk recognized them as celebrities. ?Well sir, the woman's band has all the diamonds so it actually runs at about $4,000 retail. The man's ring is the same platinum, but there are no diamonds. It usually runs about a suggested of $500. But I could probably let them both go for just the $4,000.? The man smiled, almost expecting them to put them back down. Dani, the ever frugal one who refuses to really spend the money she has looked to be having second thoughts all of a sudden. ?Josh, we don't need to spend this much on rings. Let's go with that other set, the one that was only about $500 from the marked price.? Josh turned to her and smiled wide. ?Look, if I'm going to be en-trapped by this talk you had with Blaise, I might as well give in. And if I'm going to marry you this evening, you're not getting some cheap ass 14k ring with a fake stone in it. You're worth more then that.? ?But...? He shook his head no to her. And then took off his ring and put it down onto the counter. ?So the gentleman would like these?? The clerk seemed surprised. ?Yeah, I'd like them. And you don't really need to do any fancy wrapping, they're being used in a few hours as it is.? The clerk looked at him, as if trying to study him. Josh reached to his back pocket and pulled out his snake skin wallet. He opened it up and pulled out one of the credit cards. The guy looked at it and then walked over to the register, as if he thought it wouldn't have enough money on it to make the purchase. Dani turned to Josh, looking up at him. ?You don't have to buy those rings. We can go cheaper.? Josh shook his head no. ?Look, you an Blaise had a plan with that little talk about a bet. And I fell for it hook line and sinker. I'm a man of my word. I said if I could I'd marry you today, and I will. But, if you think that means we can skimp on it when we don't really have to, you're crazy. It's not every day a man can marry an angel.? He smiled at her, wide and white while she blushed a little and took her face into his chest to hide it. The clerk returned with the receipt for the purchase and the card. The clerk looked to be quite a bit happier at this time. ?Here you go sir, if I can get you to sign this please and show me an ID?? Josh moved in and took the pen and the little tablet with the receipt on it. He opened his wallet and showed his Nevada Driver's License to the man. ?Ah, Mr. Brand. Well since you said these are being used in a few hours, I shall wish you both a wonderful life together.? Josh handed the receipt to the man and put his credit card back. The clerk took the carbon copy off the paper and handed it back to Josh with the box that contained both rings in it now. ?Now, let's go to the courthouse.? ~Many More Hours Later~ ~Aladdin Resort Chapel~ (soon to be the Planet Hollywood Hotel and Casino Resort Chapel) Dani walked out of the provided dressing room, wearing a long white traditional dress with fringe and beads on it in the design of the Oglala and Brul? Sioux peoples. It had been originally her mother's dress, one of the few things that had been recovered after the woman had been murdered. Her hair was pulled into two 'squaw' braids that were much tighter then she usually wore to control her hair in the ring, with white flowers and feathers at both the ends and in the main back of her hair. As soon as she stepped out, a woman walked up wearing a pretty sharp suit in charcoal grey. ?Ms. Northsky?? Dani spun around, holding the long skirt of the dress down. It looks like it had been done in a more silk or satin then the more traditional materials. Something of a 'little bit old' on the design, but a 'little bit new' on the material. Some lace could be seen as you got closer on the breasts and arms. ?Yes? Can I help you?? ?Yes, I'm your wedding co-ordinator. I just wanted to confirm everything with you really quick. Mr. Brand already dropped his credit card off for payment before he headed to his dressing room. But he listed you as the one to check everything with.? The woman held her clip board up for her. ?Okay, you reserved a 'This I Promise You' wedding package.? She started to point at things as she ran down the list. ?You've already provided us with the wedding license. This package will include the ceremony, where you are using standard vows. There will be traditional wedding music. You will be receiving a two night stay in the Panorama Suite up in the Resort Hotel. We are providing the Bridal Bouquet of Stargazers and Roses. Also the wedding garter and Groom's Boutonni’re. A $75 dollar gift certificate to the spa in the resort, a video of the ceremony, and bottle of champagne as well as the photo package.? Dani smiled as she looked at it. ?That's perfect. Small but still elegant.? The Coordinator returned the smile like a good little customer service worker. ?Well there are just a few more things we need here. The minister's fee is $50 in cash, and that is payable right before the ceremony. And I noticed that you and Mr. Brand showed up alone?? Dani nodded. ?Yes.? ?Okay, the state of Nevada requires at least one witness to be present. Do you have any friends or anyone else that could come over? I see your residence is listed as being here in town.? Dani looked worried. She checked the clock up on the wall. ?No, not right now. Ukyo would be on her way to get Blaise from the auditions. And I have no idea what Gray would be up to. Alexis doesn't have her own car here.? The lady smiled. ?Well we can provide a witness to sign the certificate for a nominal fee.? Dani's mind hit the sound of more money coming out right away. Josh had already dropped four grand on the rings and this ceremony was running a couple grand in it's own rights. She was all for it, but they were after more money. ?Have you spoken to Josh about this yet?? ?No... he's currently getting ready.? ?How long do I have?? ?About an hour?? Dani started to walk away. ?Give me an hour then. Hopefully less.? She walked out of the chapel and into the large hallways of the Desert Passage Shops. She looked around at the people walking around. Most of them were obviously tourists. She wandered a bit, following some people who were not dressed fully like the others. They were more in the line as to things that Josh or Blaise would wear on the outside. Jeans, rock t-shirts, and the occasional club-fetish type wear. There was a club here... And then she saw a familiar face. Two of them to be exact. She looked into the face of Lia Vlassy and Virgil Ambrosi as they were walking towards that club also. ?LIA!? Lia, Shadow in the ring, turned with Virgil's arm still around her waist from behind. She looked at Dani and actually looked a little surprised. ?Dani?? Dani came up at a trot, trying not to fall in her heels under the tight fitting dress. Lia looked her up and down and got a confused look on her face. ?Nazdar, Dani. What are you up to? Especially dressed like that?? Dani stopped, remembering fully how she was dressed. ?Uhm, what's it look like?? Virgil was the one who answered. ?If I didn't know better, I'd say you were getting married. But this is no church.? Dani smiled. ?No, but there is a chapel over there where Josh is getting ready. So yes, I'm getting married.? Lia stepped forward and started to check out the dress. ?This is beautiful. Where did you have it made?? ?It was my mother's. I had it tailored a few months ago after I got it from my grandfather. ? Lia looked it over as Virgil interjected. ?So why are you out here, and not in there getting married then?? ?We hit a snag... You see, we kinda tricked Josh into getting it over with. But in doing so, I didn't want to scare him too bad and the others were not going to be at the ceremony.? Lia saw the look of concern coming onto Dani's face. ?Go on...? ?Well, we've got the license and everything. But apparently we also need witnesses. They can provide them, but you know how that would go. I was going to look for someone else to stand in and witness it, but I didn't see anyone I'd offer that too, until I saw you two walking into the club.? Lia's eyes sparkled a bit. ?You're asking us to be witnesses to your wedding?? Dani nodded. ?Well, you're here, and out of all of the people here, I'd not prefer anyone else. Would you two do us the honors? It stars in about a half hour and they do a new ceremony every half hour. So it won't take long.? Lia went to open her mouth, but Dani interjected really quickly. ?That is if we're not going to interrupt anything.? Virgil grinned. ?Just Dinner, but it appears to be too early to get anything that would be really good.? For some reason that put a shiver down Dani's spine as his grin flashed his fangs at her. Lis hooked Virgil's arm. ?We can delay by an hour or so. Give some time for some better choices to come out and about. In the meantime we can help a friend in need. Right, Virgil?? He kept his grin, knowing full well that it made Dani nervous from her looks towards him. ?Right this way then.? Dani walked off, leading Lia and Virgil behind her back to the chapel. ~Half and Hour Later~ Lia Vlassy and Virgil Ambrosi stood in the main chapel at the Aladdin's Wedding Facility. Josh was standing up by the minister, his face was stoic as he looked at the small hispanic man who would be the one to deliver their vows to them, his eyes darting over to the woman he once asked to embrace him as a Vampire of her kind, and her own husband of twelve-hundred years. As the music started to play from the elaborate organ setup on the side wall, and Dani appeared in her dress and holding the bouquet of stargazers and roses... Lia felt a quick need to do something. She pulled out her thin cell phone and dialed a semi familiar number. ?What are you doing?? Virgil asked her as he tried not to make too much of a scene. They were not dressed for a wedding lke Dani and Josh were. And they weren't standing as best man and maid of honor. ?Causing trouble.? The phone was apparently answered ?Blaise...? She gave a pause to wait for an answer. ?Yes, it's Lia. Where am I?? She chuckled. ?Well listen to this.? She held the phone out at arms length to allow it to take in the tune of ?Here comes the bride? played by the Organ. After a few seconds she brought it back to her ear. ?Yes, we ended up running into Dani and they needed a couple of witnesses. So Virgil and I volunteered. We're at the Aladdin's wedding chapel.? She listened in with a wide grin on her face as Blaise showed what he thought of it. ?Yeah, it won't take long. Where would you like us all to meet?? ... ?Yes, we know the Club here. We'd checked it out. That's where we were going to find some dinner at.? ... ?Interesting, I'll give a call around up to our penthouse over at the Wynn and see if anyone else wants to come. We'll meet you there.? She hung the phone up. ?What's that all about?? Lia looked over at Virgil. ?Looks like we're doing the club here, Krave, for the reception. Blaise is getting ahold of Gray and Alexis. I'm going to give a call up to Dawn and Viv and see if they'd like to come down also.? ?Hmmm.? ?Oh, Blaise is buying. Something about a bet.? ~To Be Continued~
