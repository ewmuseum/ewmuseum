Date: Aug 26th, 2005 The last week had come as a shock to Dave Montgomery and Jim Brandt. No matter where they went, eyes and whispers seemed to follow. This on it’s own wasn’t out of the ordinary as wherever their usual daily rituals took them, they were regularly regarded with disdainful stares and distant insults (they used to be up close insults before Dave and Jim figured out that the only thing that scares a bully more than a little guy that won’t back down, is a fat guy that won’t back down) from people whom believed their obsessions a sign of weakness; or even worse, geekness. But for the last week, the stares had been something entirely unexpected. Whispers and smiles, and even a few friendly waves which turned into a pack of mildly obsessive observers whom followed them throughout the mall as they made their rounds. This really shouldn’t have been unexpected, as who’s the last person of any importance that you can remember coming out of northern Missouri? Still, for a couple of self-realized social misfits, it was a total 180 and it threw them for the proverbial loop. At Barnes & Noble, where normally they would purchase lattes and hang out in the sci-fi aisle, waiting for someone to look at D&D books so they could tell them all about their favorite characters and invite them to the next gaming session.; instead they found themselves under assault by a legion of early-teen girls hell-bent on getting LeStatt Knight’s phone number. The only thing that worked in driving off the mob was to resort to conversing in Klingon, which is fortunately equal parts crude and nerdy. The weekly trivia session with Melvin the Star Wars Toy geek at the toy aisle at Wal-Mart had to be cancelled when the assistant deputy manager (whom coincidentally used to be the high school football team captain and picked on Dave mercilessly) made a PA announcement to meet and greet GCW’s newest superstars in aisle 10. Their escape was so hastily beat that Meat (Jim) nearly ran over the deeply wrinkled and mostly narcoleptic greeter (whom was actually trying to block him in). Meat would have felt bad, except that the guy had only two weeks ago told him to “Stay out of the snack aisle, fatty”. The only normal stops were at Mailboxes Etc. because honestly, who actually goes there outside of the Christmas rush; and Things for Thinkers, which has a powerful enough geek aura to keep away even the most glassy-eyed of reality TV fame-chasing wannabes. It is here, amongst the myriad of books, battlements, and dice-bags of Things for Thinkers that we join The Rogue Paladin and Meatshield Trainwrecker; collectively known as “The Guild”. Meat’s sizable shadow was cast across a table covered in long-boxes, as he scoured the bagged and board archives that Greedo (The shop owner, who’s real name was Gary) had brought in from the last con that he went to. Meat had already pulled 2 dozen issues and wasn’t even through the third box. Over at the counter, TRP was excitedly opening a package that he’d picked up earlier in the day at Mailboxes etc.. Greedo stared intently, trying to guess the contents by the British postmark. “What’s it this time” More gauntlets? A new dagger? Coat of arms? ? asked Greedo in rapid succession. “Maybe that set of Hogwarts wands you were obsessing about?” “Nope,” replied a smirking TRP, “even better than that.” Meat waddled over to the counter a set down a stack of roughly 50 comics. “I’ll take these, what’s in the box?” TRP finished his meticulous opening process (hallmark of any toy collector if they ever get around to actually opening anything) and held aloft his prizes. “They’re so awesome.” Greedo and Meat stared alternately at the items and each other for several moments not really knowing what to say, while TRP gazed dreamily at his prized possessions. A pair of patent leather wrestling boots with a gold ?+5? emblazoned on the side. “Umm, you already have a pair of boots.” was all Meat could manage once he finally found his voice. TRP set the boots gently on the counter and turned to his friends. “You don’t understand, these aren’t just any boots. They’re magic.” Meat’s eyebrows attempted to join with his hairline in surprise, while Greedo was lucky not to bruise his chin on the counter when his jaw fell open. “The hell?” was all he could utter. “It’s the truth. I sent them to a sect of druids in Britain to be blessed. It took forever to convince them to it, and then another week to complete the rituals. It’ll be hard to miss if they’ve done the incantations properly.” said TRP in all seriousness. Greedo’s jaw continued to linger somewhere on the counter, and made a fine pivot as he turned his gaze alternately from Paladin to Meat and back. Meat seemed to be assessing the boots with a keen eye. Slowly he turned his gaze to TRP, as a slow grin formed on his face. “Plus 5 boots of ass-kicking it is then. Don’t suppose there’s anything in that box for me.” Now it was TRP’s turn to grin. “Of course there is!” Reaching into the box he retrieved something that looked like a giant ox horn with a cork wedged into the tip and leather straps dangling from it. This would be because it was a giant ox horn with a cork wedged in the tip and handy leather sling straps wrapped around it. “One Viking drinking horn for the best bezerker I know.” said TRP, handing over the horn to Meat. Meat stared at it intensely for a second before looking his friend in the eye. “Grab those boots, we gotta get to the Quik-Trip. I need to fill this thing up and it’s the only place with Mountain Dew on the fountain.” ~fin~
