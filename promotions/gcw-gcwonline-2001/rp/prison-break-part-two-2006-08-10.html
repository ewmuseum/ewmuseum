Prison Break - Part: Two

10 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/06 written for archive - United States District Court for the District of Nevada, Las Vegas, Nevada, November 2005 - As Jeff sits there in a black suit, white shirt, black tie and his black hair shorter than it was in Bloodbox, beads of sweat begin dripping down his cheeks, obviously nervous about the outcome that awaited him, knowing in his mind that killing Simon Caine was the right thing to do, it’s a fact that most justice that happens in the world is illegal, Jeff didn’t care about that, he didn’t even care if he got the death penalty, Jeff was happy dead, despite the fact that the only thing he can remembers was that it was warm and a lot of screaming could be heard around him, he truly felt like he was at home. Jeff did not fear the reaper, he embraced death but a fate worse would be locked up in a cell. Jeff had always been so used to the freedom, being locked in a cage is something very few people have ventured into before. Once Jeff realises he can’t escape from anyway he becomes vicious and almost begins to foam at the mouth like a rabid dog. If he was to be given the death penalty then Jeff would finally be free for all eternity, it was the one thing he really ever wanted. Jeff sat slumped in the chair as the prosecution and the defence attorneys battled it out, the judge was having a hard time keeping them under control, Jeff on the other hand had to bite his tongue. “You cannot execute this man, you know who this is” Jeff Fucking Harris that’s who, he’s a fucking legend!” Jeff’s lawyer yelled, not knowing that Jeff was actually hoping for death. “This man is a murderer and should be treated as such, what he did to Mr. Caine was inexcusable, it was deliberate and just plain heinous, if you let this man go free you might as well let out all the other rapists and murderers in the world.” The prosecution council fired back. “Since when did Jeff become a rapist” No one’s said that, you’re putting words into the judges head! Caine was an evil man and where the police failed, Jeff prevailed. Jeff isn’t a murderer, he’s a hero and that’s how he should be treated.” Jeff’s lawyer responded. “He doesn’t have the choice of who gets to live and who gets to die, it’s not his job, it’s the laws job. He’s a mercenary, in my personal and professional opinion, he only reason he did this was for the money.” The prosecution replied with a harsh tone. “Luckily for us all, it’s not your opinion that counts is it?” Jeff’s lawyer shouted back. “Right, that’s enough the both of you! I’ve had it, calm down now or I’ll hold you both in contempt!” The Judge shouted. Jeff looked at the judge, clearly an aging man, grey hair and liked to crack his gavel on the wooden stand he had on his desk. Both lawyers sat down, Jeff’s lawyer gave him a reassuring nod as if to say that everything will be alright, Jeff was not a fool however, he knew that whatever the decision was going to be, it wouldn’t be the one Jeff was hoping for. “Alright good, now, we will take a short recess while the jury makes their decision.” The Judge says as he slams his gavel once more, a number of people leave the court room, Jeff stays put however, he turns around and sees Adam Masterson, Theodoric Solomon, Jimmy Thunder, his three greatest friends, he felt happy that his brothers were there for him, Jeff and Jimmy had just retired from Bloodbox a month earlier, next to them was the Bloodbox Belt of Excellence Champion, Shawn Harris, his mother Stephanie and finally for some strange reason, Bryan Deas. They all looked hopeful for Jeff’s release, Jeff nodded at them all and turned facing the front. Jeff knew deep down that he’d never see his family again and that’s what those people were with Deas being the retarded cousin everyone hates. Minutes passed by, the lawyer then walked back into the court room and took his seat next to Jeff. “Well, I think it’s going pretty good Jeff.” The lawyer said. “Don’t piss down my back and tell me it’s raining, tell me the truth.” Jeff replied. “Alright, well it looks like they might be going with the death penalty.” The lawyer replied. Jeff had a grin former at the edges of his mouth, “Excellent.” “You want to die” You truly are insane.” The lawyer exclaimed. “I’d rather die and be free of all this mental torture I’ve been carrying around for years on end instead of being cooped up in a shitty little cell with a bunch of homosexual retards. The doctors should have kept me dead.” Jeff replied. Before the lawyer could say anything back the jurors re-enter the room, one of the workers then announces the return of the honourable Judge Matthew StClaire who proceeds to enter the court room and take his place. “Has the jury reached a verdict?” The Judge asks. “We have.” Says one of the male jurors. “We have come to the unanimous decision that Jeffrey James Harrison the third, is guilty of murder of the first degree.” At that moment the court room was silent as a graveyard, no one spoke, out of shock but mostly out of fear because of what punishment would be applied. Jeff looked disheartened. “Very well, I have however decided not to inflict the death penalty due to the unorthodox circumstances surrounding Simon Caine’s death, therefore Jeff Harris, you will be given life imprisonment with no chance of parole.” The Judge said. While Jeff’s friends and family looked somewhat relieved he wasn’t going to be put to death, they didn’t like the fact he’d spend the rest of his life behind bars, what Jeff did was for Rachel Ryan, not for his own personal amusement like all the other murderers. Did Jeff feel screwed? Royally, not only was he not going to be free, but he also wasn’t going to die, this was the worst possible outcome for Jeff. - Clark County, Nevada, April 2006 - He was his dark to his light, his hate to his love, his sadism to his normality, his Ben to his Jerry. After facing each other almost a 100 times over the course of 7 years Neu Generation had basically won the vicious feud after Neu attempted to kill Jeff back in GCW, he succeeded to but what everyone didn’t know for nearly half a year was that Jeff was revived and went into hiding, waiting for the right time to come back and kick major ass. He was Neu Generation to his Jeff Harris. They started out as enemies and ended the same, it was Jeff that sent Neu over the edge, had it not been for Jeff, a lot of pain and suffering wouldn’t have happened. Jeff had the last laugh however by manipulating Neu Generation and his son Shawn Harris to team up with him with Simon Caine. The Syndicate were supreme until it was revealed that Jeff had been playing them all along, once he aligned himself back with Jimmy Thunder, the Syndicate was done. Jeff then decimated both Neu and Caine in Jeff’s final match, Neu didn’t visit Jeff for the pleasure of seeing him incarcerated, it was something far more special than that, perhaps uncharacteristic for Neu Generation. “So, all this money that Rachel paid you for your services in Bloodbox really did pay off didn’t it” I mean seriously, this is truly a mansion.” Neu said as he smirked. Jeff sat there and glared at him, ready to jump up and choke him with his handcuffs. “What is it you want?” he asked. “Now don’t look at me like that Jeff, you and I have known each other for a long time, friends, enemies, brothers” mostly enemies although I don’t know why, you and I have done more damage to each other than anyone else ever could, something keeps drawing us together because we just can’t leave each other alone can we? It’s either me coming for you or you coming for me, yeah we’ve made a lot of money from it but was it truly worth it?” Neu pondered. ??Yeah.” Jeff answered. “When I supposedly killed you at the end of 2004, I didn’t expect you to come back ever again, when I ended you in America, I didn’t stay in America, I got the fuck out and stayed away from civilization for a hell of a long time, well six months but it seemed longer, you just had to go back to your house didn’t you” You should have known the police would have been looking there. But when I thought I ended you, something inside me changed, you turned me into the psycho everyone came to know me as, it was you constantly attacking me and that went on until something inside me snapped and I bit back. When I thought the cause for my insanity had disappeared, so did all the hate inside of me, I wasn’t the same man I was before, I became like you, a shell of my former self. Sure, when you returned it was obvious that you had broken through the shell but I hadn’t, attacking Shawn in Bloodbox was me trying to get noticed again, then you showed up.” Neu said, shaking his head afterwards. “You’re point Peter” Because seriously, you’re really making me wish I leapt up and hit the judge square in the jaw when he convicted me.” Jeff snarled back. “My point is, I would have never followed you like I did in Bloodbox if I wasn’t a shell of my former self, I may have killed you Jeff, but it was me that suffered the long term damage.” Neu said. “Luckily for everyone, you’re retired.” Jeff exclaimed, almost with a smile on his face as he stared into his eyes. “That’s what I wanted to talk to you about Jeff, since we were kids living back in Duckmanton, Chesterfield, a village that wasn’t special by any means, we had the normal shops in the town centre and everything but it wasn’t high profile like Manchester or London or anything.” Peter said, Jeff responded by shaking his head. “Nothing special but it was our home, since we were kids Jeff, we’ve been competing, we used to compete over better gifts for Christmas and birthdays, you beat me most of those years, no matter what I got you, you always got me something better. The best gift you got me Jeff was back in 2004 when I ended your life. Our seven year struggle to get the upper hand came to an end in GCW, no one has ever had a feud like ours, yeah feuds of the same intensity have happened but did they rage on for seven years?” “No they haven’t.” Jeff answered. “Exactly, seven years brother, that’s a huge chunk of our wrestling careers used on kicking each others ass, we did more damage to each other than anyone else has. Well anyway, the greatest gift I have ever gotten from you was winning the feud and taking your life and now I’m going to win the competition because I have the better gift, I took your life, now I’m giving it back to you.” Neu declared. Jeff sat there looking confused, how could Neu Generation possibly give his life back to him when he’s in prison, as far as Jeff was concerned, his life was over. Jeff hadn’t seen Neu since the Simon Caine match back in October, maybe this was Neu trying to screw with his mind. “What the hell do you mean you’re giving me my life back Neu” If you’re just here to piss me off then you’re wasting your time, I’d love to kill you where you stand but I know I wouldn’t get very far before these bitch guards took me down.” Jeff growled, trying to keep his voice down so the guards don’t hear him. “No need to be like that Jeff, you’re just mad because I’ve won the competition once and for all, as I said, I’m giving you your life back for one night only.” Neu replied. “One night only” What the hell am I going to do? Get totally hammered, try to forget my troubles only to return to prison afterwards? Yeah it’d be good tasting alcohol again but surely you could just sneak me some in.” Jeff said. “No you won’t be getting hammered, not in the drinking sense at least you see there’s this wrestling event that you’ll be participating in, name is WFW Encore and your match” you’ll be facing Adam Masterson and Theodoric Solomon in a triple threat match, I hear it’s Masterson’s and Solomon’s last match as well.” Neu said. Jeff sat there, trying to not crack a smile, “A reunion show” Why isn’t Thunder going to be there?” “I don’t know, he couldn’t be fucked or something, he wasn’t in WFW for long anyway. You’ve beaten him before anyway, you’ve never faced Solomon or Masterson, so I’m giving you one last chance to shine for one night by defeating your greatest friends. Bloodbox wasn’t really a challenge for you, you dominated the entire roster, Masterson and Solomon are the real challenge I think.” Neu said. “Who are you facing?” Jeff asked. “Me” I’m taking on Scotty Phillips in the opening match, I know, me curtain jerking but I attribute that to you sabotaging my WFW run. I remember your debut like it was yesterday, you smashed me in the face with a steel chain, instantly taking the spot light, winning the Canadian championship from Moss. That’s probably why your match is the second to last match on the card. The main event hasn’t been announced yet.” Neu answered. Jeff sat there thinking for a moment, his match being mainly a main event match despite the fact that none of the three were the World Champion was a great honour, while former World Champions such as Revolution, Anson Drake and Nick Kelly would be placed in mid-card matches.” “Naturally, I’m going to do it.” Jeff said smiling, “but how the hell am I going to get out of this shit hole?” “Well that’s all been taken care of, you see, I’ve had discriminating pictures of the governor of Nevada for awhile now, so I put those to good use asking for your release for just the one night, then I paid the governor of this prison quite a bit of money to put towards whatever he liked to sanction it, plus he’s a wrestling fan so I gave him a couple of tickets to the show.” Neu replied. “When is the show?” enquired Jeff. “May 14th, so that leaves you plenty of time to get yourself into shape for this thing, hell, maybe throw some people around the prison. Have you done that yet?” Ask Neu with a knowing smirk on his face. “Well, just before I met you I threw someone over the railings. He didn’t die though” But May 14th? That’s three weeks away, it should be enough time I guess, but? Why are you doing this?” Jeff asked. The reason Jeff asked that was for the simple fact that neither man has done a thing for each other for nearly fifteen or twenty years, yeah they’ve aligned with each other in the past but they’ve never really done anything for each other out of the goodness of their heart. What Neu Generation was doing now could only be a thing out of Neu Generation’s heart, despite the fact Jeff always believed it was black and cold he though Neu was simply getting old and sentimental. Jeff and Neu were very different men when they started their feud, neither man ever expected it to get to the point where it got, now they’ve become old guys, true veterans of the sport and both had promised to never be one of those types that wrestled until they were 70 and only worked until that age for the money and to hold other wrestlers down. Maybe Neu felt sorry for Jeff, whatever the reason it did give Jeff one last chance to truly shine in front of the fans, his run in Bloodbox wasn’t challenging in the slightest, he was the top man at the top of the tree because he was simply the best, guys like Ian Cross and Anthony Taylor were complete jokes compared to Jeff. Adam Masterson and Theodoric Solomon, his greatest friends, Jeff never even thought of wrestling them before until Neu made it possible, it would show the fans who would be the best of the best. Neu sat opposite Jeff still, pondering the question asked. “Why” Because despite our past Jeff, I loved WFW as much as anyone else in that place. Jay Moss, Bryan Deas, Anson Drake, Revolution, The Chosen One, Nick Kelly, Scotty Phillips, Isabella Drake, Flood, Tornado, Solomon and everyone else helped make that fed what it was and even though I had varied stints in that place I helped add to it, so did you Jeff. You were one of the most known wrestlers in that fed, not for your skills but for your attitude to backstage politics, you were always the guy that fought for what you thought was right, you’ve quit several times and got fired several more, the end of the promotion came when you were screwed over and decided to buyout and dissect the company. People will be coming to this show expecting to see the biggest hothead, the biggest asshole, the biggest absolute prick that ever walked those halls and because I loved WFW, I feel it’s my duty to deliver you to them. It’s not time for a Neu Generation anymore Jeff, it’s time for the Jazzy One.” Neu responded. “Well Neu, I am very grateful for this and I’ll admit, you’ve won the so called competition we’ve had since we were young ones. I can’t wait to step into a WFW ring one last time.” Jeff said thankfully. “Time!” The guard shouted to all the prisoners and visitors. “I guess this is where we make our goodbyes until the show.” Neu said. “Indeed.” Jeff grunted back almost disappointed knowing that he’d have no one he knows to talk to until he went to the show. The two of them got to their feet, Neu held out a hand to Jeff, Jeff looked at Neu, the hand and then back at Neu again, he grinned and slapped it away, almost immediately after Jeff wrapped his arms around Neu’s torso and gave him a brotherly hug. “Thank you Neu” thank you brother.” Jeff said. “Just do me one favour and kick ass out there.” Neu replied. “I’ll meet you on the 14th right outside this building, then we’re going to Detroit, Michigan.” The two separated, this marked a new level in their relationship, neither man said sorry for their past actions towards each other because they both meant everything they each to one another, but now, it was like Jeff and Neu were trying to get past their differences. Neither man wanted to be bitter towards each other at their age and sharing the first brotherly hug in nearly 20 years was a true testament to that. Neu Generation and Jeff nodded each other and Neu went out of the exit, Jeff walked back towards his cell, he looked around thinking that unlike the rest of them in the prison, he’d be getting out, even if it was for just the one day, he was getting out. Jeff had thought about making a run for it directly after the show, try and make it back to his hideout in Northern England, live out the rest of his days alone instead of rotting in jail until he dies. Jeff always hated the fact that humanity relies on the life for a life rule, people like Simon Caine need removing from society and the people with the balls to do it get punished for doing it. Jeff decided that he wouldn’t run, he’d be the martyr for tyranny and injustice. As Jeff made it back to his cell he looked at his surroundings, he thought that it wasn’t so bad, if there were ever any people that needing an ass kicking, it’d be the people in prison, Jeff saw two paths in his future, a violent one or a very quiet one, which one he was going to take was still uncertain. Jeff pushed those thoughts aside, as the cell doors closed Jeff got the photo of his son and ex-wife from under his pillow and looked at it. He placed it in his pocket and began doing push-ups. He began training for his last wrestling match ever.
