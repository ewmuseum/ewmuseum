Brotherly Love

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-9-2005 5 September ,1988 It was a dreary day in Minato-Ku ,Japan and that was made no more evident than when you see a lot of the people using umbrellas to keep from getting soaked by the torrential downpour that was coming down that day and people were wondering when/if it was in fact ever going to stop! At the household in the Yujikawa district of Japan we find Joshua Curtis ,then 9 years old, getting ready for the school day ahead of him by making sure that he had one of his uniforms ironed and hung up in his closet and double checked to make sure that his glasses were cleaned so he could see the chalkboard otherwise he’d have a hard time reading the blackboard. Joshua was dressed and ready to go when he heard a noise coming from down the hall that he normally never had to worry about hearing? “A’A”ACHOO!? Upon hearing this Joshua went to the bedroom that his sister ,Mizuno Ami, occupied and Joshua knew that his usually illness-free sister must of come down with something but when he didn’t know. “Ami” Joshua asked worriedly “are you feeling ok?” Joshua finished as he sat next to the bed and the look on Ami’s face told him all that he needed to know. Bloodshot eyes, flushed cheeks and a nose that was running worse than a leaky faucet were some of the major symptoms that Joshua worried about the most not to mention after he took her temperature and found it higher than any normal fever he ever had he ,Joshua L. Curtis, at 9 YEARS OLD had to act like the parent because mother was away at a medical conference. Upon dialing the number to the school and getting the school nurse Joshua explained the situation about Ami’s sudden illness to the nurse ,whom told Joshua that the best he could do was stay home and take care of her ,which wasn’t a problem seeing as how he and Ami were like 4 chapters ahead of everyone else in the class they didn’t have to worry about catching up on any studies and if anything they could use the time to get further in their studies and Joshua could take care of Ami. “You know you don’t HAVE to stay brother. I?” Ami’s thought was interrupted by a sudden urge to sneeze and Joshua quickly handed her a tissue before speaking his piece. “Sis” Joshua began “I’m not about to let someone whom is very sick with a fever stay home by themselves with no one to care for them. Mom is away at a conference and she specifically told me that if something like this happens to you to do my best until she comes back and take care of you. I intend to keep that promise and all I want you to do is rest ok?” Joshua said somewhat pointedly but Ami got the message and got it good! Joshua wasn’t leaving for ANYTHING and if Ami wanted him to go to school Joshua wouldn’t do it because he knew he was needed there more. Joshua got up from the bedside and went to the bathroom to get the medicine for Ami to take and luckily their mother had gotten the “Cherry Flavored” kind although the thought did occur to Joshua to give Ami the nasty tasting stuff that he usually takes but he wanted to help Ami and not kill her so he got the “good stuff” and proceeded back to Ami’s room with a glass of water just in case she needed it. Ami took the medicine and Joshua was happy that she didn’t fuss about it because he knew that she needed the medicine or else it could get worse. On any other day off Joshua would have been downstairs playing his “Famicom?(Nintendo) playing Super Mario Bros. but today he knew what was more important and when it came time for lunch Joshua made the chicken soup and grilled cheese for Ami and so he didn’t make her feel bad he had the same thing ,which was just fine for him cos he wasn’t that hungry for lunch anyways. The rest of the day for Joshua was spent making sure that Ami got the medicine and studying with her and even talking about the boys in school that Ami met. It would be another four years before Joshua and Ami discovered the truths about themselves but in that time they still enjoyed each others company! Present day? Its now 17 years later and sometimes the more things change the more they stay the same. Joshua is still taking care of people in his “family” when they get sick and today that is none truer when it comes to his “One and only” ,the incomparable Zack Johnson! Oh it seemed like it was going to be a day for the couple to go out as they had planned’that was until Ami was called into the room by the San Francisco native and examined him thoroughly only t find that he had a vicious migraine that was something like what Dr. Mizuno had about 2 years prior. When Joshua heard this he did what he did for Ami when she had a fever but the only difference was the medicine that Joshua would have given Ami then wouldn’t work now because of the severity of the condition. Thankfully for everyone Joshua had Dr. Mizuno’s pager number and when the good doctor saw the number to the “Complex” on her pager she immediately went and called back irregardless of the fact that it was about 11:00 P.M. her time! “Joshua what’s wrong?” Doc Mizuno asked immediately and Joshua explained the situation before she then told him about some very strong medicine that she had given Ami when she went to go live with him and that it was in the medicine cabinet. The catch was that it would make Zack very sleepy but he would be fine just as long as he didn’t **** up and give Zack the wrong dose and no more than one time! Joshua took down the instructions and dead sprinted from the kitchen where the phone that he called from was located to the bathroom where the medicine cabinet was. Want to know how fast Joshua ran up the stairs? Quatre was coming over to practice the violin with Michiru and the young Arab was nearly knocked over by a brown-haired blur racing like he was holding his piss! When Joshua did get the medicine to Zack he took it instantly and sank down into the covers before his eyelids got so heavy that he couldn’t fight the sleep anymore and as his breathing evened out Joshua smiled for he knew that when Zack woke up he’d be better and he had his mother and his sister to thank’after all that’s what family is for! Good Night and God Bless! :)
