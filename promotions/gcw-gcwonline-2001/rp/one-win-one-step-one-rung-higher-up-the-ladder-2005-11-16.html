One Win, One Step, One Rung Higher Up the Ladder

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/2005 [The camera slowly fades into what appears to be a dark, dank, arena that is literally packed with onlookers. The location and current time is unknown. The sound of hundreds maybe a couple thousand screaming, screeching, and yelling maniacs can be heard piercing and echoing throughout the arena air. At this time it appears a battle is taking place down below on the arena floor and all the onlookers are literally salivating at the intensity of the fight. The camera catches an angled sky view of the two combatants battling it out way down below and quickly zooms in. As the camera catches a better look at the two pitfighters, one of them appears to be a black man with a black bandana on his head, a well-trimmed and now bloodied up goatee on his face, a black tanktop t-shirt that is drawn very tight around his muscular frame, and a pair of black jeans on. He is seen draped almost lifelessly against the black barricade that separates the combatants from the screaming masses in the stands. The pain and agony is deeply etched across the warrior’s face as blood drips from a small gash on his forehead coloring his face a crimson red. The man that we see is none other than “The Violence” Vince Jones, the man that the wrestling world has learned to fear; but, at this moment in time he is being pummeled into submission by his opponent, a rather massive man in a white t-shirt and black denim overalls with a dog collar around his neck and a bearded face that is soaked by his own blood that is slowly streaming down. As Vince Jones tries to summon the strength to stand up to his own two feet his opponent gazes at him and lets out a loud booming laugh and just shakes his head.] Bearded Man: Look at you! Just look at you! You supposed to be the man that’s rising to the top of this dump?” All I see right now is a soon-to-be dead piece of New Yawk prison yard crap! [The monstrous, bearded man’s chest heaves up and down with laughter as he spits in the face of Vince Jones. This sends the crowd into a frenzy. Vince Jones looks up at the bearded man with defiant eyes and snarls back.] Vince Jones: You think you gonna finish me off tonight, HUH?” Then do the damn thing, b*tch! C’mon on wit it! [The bearded man chuckles to himself as he reaches down on the littered arena floor and grabs a lead pipe.] Bearded Man: Don’t mind if I do! [The bearded man raises the lead pipe high over his head and begins inching towards Vince ready to strike.] Bearded Man: Lights out, b? [All of a sudden Vince Jones springs forward and nails the bearded man square in the gut with a swift kick out of desperation that leaves the big man stunned and makes him drop the lead pipe to the floor with an more than audible cling clang sound. Vince Jones drops to his knees but somehow manages to muster enough strength to lunge forward with a powerful right hook to the big man’s kidneys that sends the man reeling backwards clutching his side in pain. Vince slowly rises to his feet, grabs the man by the back of his head and slings him face first into the barricade as the whole arena erupts and becomes amped by the sudden turnaround. He quickly whips the big man around and begins laying into him with vicious lefts and rights to the face over and over and over again in succession. Suddenly, the large, bearded man manages to get a hand up and block one of Vince’s oncoming punches and swiftly nails Jones with a low blow that sends Vince teetering to the arena floor in agony. Vince Jones grabs himself and begins rolling around on the floor in pain as a loud “Ooooooooooooooooooh” comes from the collective voice of the blood thirsty audience. The bearded man slowly reaches down and snatches up the lead pipe once again and looks down at Vince Jones in satisfaction as he is prone on the arena floor. The big man belches out a roar of laughter as he raises the lead pipe high above his head.] Bearded Man: Its over for you, boy!! [The big man begins nailing Vince Jones in the ribs with shot after shot from the leadpipe he is wielding. The crowd goes nuts at the carnage in the arena as the big man continues to beat Vince Jones into submission and begins taunting him as well.] Bearded Man: How you like that?” What?” I can’t hear you! How the hell you like that, huh?” [The bearded man begins nailing Vince Jones in the kidney area as well over and over again making Vince Jones start to cough up his own blood on the floor.] Bearded Man: Look at you! You’re garbage! And to think they call you The Violence! Well, I’m done playing with you! Its game over time! [The bearded man raises the lead pipe high above his head and measures up for the final deathblow.] Bearded Man: Lights out, boy!!! [With a quick swing, the bearded man strikes Vince Jones across the skull. Vince instantly rolls over on his back and just lies there literally motionless in a heap on the floor to the satisfaction of the boisterous crowd. The large, bearded man immediately drops the lead pipe to the arena floor and raises his arms in the air as he rejoices in the praise. All of a sudden a loud, booming, low, deep voice blares out over the speakers throughout the arena.] Announcer: Your winner of tonight’s NYUFC Friday Night Prize Fight is? “The Dog of Death” Drew Winthroop!!!! [Drew Winthrop begins circling Vince Jones in victory and pounding his massive chest. He then looks down at Vince Jones and gloats.] Drew Winthrop: Time for me to go collect my paycheck, b*tch! Looks like you gonna be a lil hungry this week! I’m sure there prolly some scraps layin? in some dumpster for ya! [The big man roars with laughter and just walks away. All of a sudden a well-built man appears with long blonde hair, a black leather jacket with the words “The Hellraiser” written on the front in crimson, and a pair of black leather jeans on. Vince Jones? expression intensifies as the man smirks at Vince’s state of defeat and just chuckles to himself.] The man: Well, well, well. Would ya look at this. I like this. I’m really likin? this. With all the sh*t that’s been spewin? out your mouth recently I guess you never woulda thought you’d find yourself laying on your back looking up at the ceiling. Did ya, punk?” One question for ya, how’s the view from the floor?” [The blonde-haired man lets out an evil laugh and just shakes his head.] The man: It’s a shame. It’s a damn shame. I’ve been hearing a whole rack of people talking as if you’re supposed to be the next big thing around NYUFC. People actually come up to me [leans over Vince and points to himself] and have the gall to allow your name to roll off their tongues! [Vince Jones coughs and some more blood spurts from out of his mouth. The blonde-haired man appears a bit outraged by this and swiftly kicks Vince in his side. Vince clutches his ribs in agony as the blonde-haired man’s expression intensifies a bit.] The man: HAVE SOME RESPECT WHEN YOUR CHAMPION IS TALKING TO YOU!!! [slowly begins breathing in and out trying to regain his composure.] Now, look at you! You’re a bloody mess! And to think that you’re supposed to be The Violence of NYUFC! People think you could be the most dangerous man to ever set foot in the NYUFC. Is that right?” Well, I don’t give a damn what the people might think!! The way I see it, you’re still nothing but V.J in my eyes, the NYC prison yard trash that wandered his way down here to the NYUFC hoping to make a name for himself. [The man shakes his head and just laughs to himself.] The man: Well, you don’t look so dangerous to me right now. In fact, until you can call yourself the champion of the NYUFC, you and the rest of these jackasses that wish to one day get on my level, you and the rest of these fools are nothing but dirt beneath my feet. [The man raises his arms and circles around Vince once more.] The man: You see all of this? This the NYUFC! This my house! This my world! This is my hell! Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright runs this! And you?” [The man we now know as Jack Wright stops in his tracks, looks down, at Vince and just chuckles.] Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright: You just exist here because in my world because I allow it! No ifs, ands, or buts about it, V.J! You got that?” You only exist here because I haven’t decided to wipe your sorry carcass off the face of this map yet! One of these days though you’re gonna overstay your welcome and when that day comes, I’ll be more than happy to end your career around here, for good! [Jack Wright kneels down beside Vince and Vince keeps his eyes steadily locked in him with an intense glare.] Jack “The Hellrasier” Wright: In the meantime, you oughta do yourself a favor and just leave this place! You’ve lost! You’re down and out! It’s over for you! You’re done! [Jack Wright stands to his feet and turns his back on Vince.] Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright: Hey! Someone get over here and peel this human blood stain off my arena floor before he starts stinkin? up the joint! I’m gettin? sick and damn tired of lookin? at him! [The camera slowly fades to black as the words of Jack “The Hellraiser” Wright continue to echo through the air.] You’ve lost? “You’re down and out” “It’s over for you” “You’re done” You’re down and out? “You’re done” “You’ve lost” “It’s over for you” **RRRRRRRING** [All of a sudden the sound of a ringing phone can be heard as the camera switches to what appears to be a dark bedroom. The camera slowly pans around the darkness and catches sight of the man known as “The Violence” Vince Jones, who is fast asleep at the moment barely wrapped up in the black sheets of his bed.] **RRRRRRRRING** [The source of the ringing appears to be a small cellphone which is sitting on a small nightstand next to the bed. As the camera continues to ring the sound of Vince Jones slowly stirring to life can be heard as well as the rustling sound of his bed sheets. Vince Jones finally rolls over and sits up at the edge of his bed and begins to mutter to himself.] Vince Jones: The Violence is neva down, out, or done in this game. Hell with that sh*t! **RRRRRRRING!!** [Vince turns around and eyes the ringing cellphone in fury and just shakes his head as he tries to wipe away the sleep from his eyes and shake of his grogginess.] Vince Jones: Damn, man! Who the hell callin? the celly now?” [Vince quickly snatches up the ringing cellphone and turns towards the digital clock on his nightstand which now reads “3:45a.m” ] Vince Jones: This betta be good! Its almost four in the damn mornin?! V oughta be sleepin? right now! [Vince flips open the cellphone and jams it up to his ear and answers.] Vince Jones: Yo! V here. Whatcha want? [The camera switches to a split screen and we see the man known as Reno Banks “The Agent of the Stars”, the man who is in charge of taking care of business negotiations, arranging endorsement deals, and the whole nine yards for The Violence. He is seen sitting at his neatly arranged work desk with his trademark sly grin plastered across his face. ] Reno Banks: Ah, Mr. Jones, I bring you a lot of good news. Vince Jones: [rolling his eyes] And this good news couldn’t wait until the daytime? Nahmean? The time that normal people be givin? normal people good news and all. [Reno Banks chuckles to himself, leans back in his desk chair, and props his feet up on the desk.] Reno Banks: Yes, but I’m not normal. I’m the best damn agent money can buy. You know that for yourself, Mr. Jones. I take care of your business 24/7. I keep you informed 24/7. That’s what I do. Its my job, sir. Vince Jones: [irritated] Enough with the bullsh*t! Let’s cut to the chase here, Banks! Its four in the mornin?! [The smile vanishes from Reno’s face momentarily as he sits back upright in his chair.] Reno Banks: Okay, okay. I’m sorry about that, Mr. Jones. Well, I just wanted to call you and inform you that business is running smoothly at the moment. The Violence is on the verge of blowing up in GCW for sure. [Vince rolls his head at the news.] Vince Jones: That's the sh*t you always sayin', Reno! V. Jones always Reno Banks: Well, there was that loss to? [Vince’s eyes open wide with fury.] Vince Jones: That sh*t was a fluke win by Michael Stevens, b*tch! Why we gotta talk “bout the bad. Let’s talk about the positives here. Reno Banks: Positives? Now we're digging a little deep, Mr. Jones. Vince Jones: Hey! Reno Banks: Alright, alright. I’m sorry, my mistake, Mr. Jones. Your performance was smashing, you just came up short ;but, I do have good news though. Vince Jones: Go ahead and spit it, Banks. Reno Banks: Well, it turns out that you have been scheduled to participate in a singles match. [Vince Jones smirks at the thought.] Vince Jones: So, V. Jones gettin? some random sacrificial lamb tossed his way to get slaughtered. Who the b*tch V gotta silence? Reno Banks: Rei Tiu. [Vince Jones nods his head, rolls his eyes, and appears a bit disinterested with the news he’s hearing.] Vince Jones: So, V facin? some Rei Tiu? Aiight. Anything else you got to say here? Reno Banks: Now hold on, Mr. Jones. I don’t think you understand what’s on the line in this match this week at WorldWide. You win this match and you could be one step closer to making a name for yourself. A win against could mean you moving one step closer to the title scene. Any win for you at this point is good. [Vince Jones hears the good news and rises to his feet with a grin across his face.] Reno Banks: Mr. Jones? Vince Jones: Aight! V’ll see you at WorldWide Reno Banks: Good, Mr. Jones. That’s just what I wanted to hear. I look forward to the day that you’re sitting on top of the world in GCW with the belt around your waist. Well, I? **CLICK** [Vince Jones hangs up the cellphone and the camera switches back to a regular view of the bedroom of Vince Jones once again. He shuts the cellphone and tosses it back on the nightstand. He stands there in silence as he contemplates his situation this week.] “So, all V gotta do is take out this Rei Tiu and he movin” on up. Almost sounds too good to be true?” [The camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones takes a seat once again at the edge of his bed.] ?” V been waitin? for a helluva a long time for this first win, a helluva a long time. V been waitin' to sit on top of the world as a champ for a helluva long time too. You’ve been waitin? for this moment a long time to, Vachon; but, don’t worry. In due time, V. Jones’ll be wearin? the strap around his waist like you always wanted, but neva got the opportunity to. This win this week’s for you’one win, one step, one rung higha up the ladder? [The camera slowly fades to black as Vince Jones remains sitting there in silence.]
