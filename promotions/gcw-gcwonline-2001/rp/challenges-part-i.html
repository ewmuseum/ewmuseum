Date: In the Weeks Before WW110 Challenges Part I: A Special Challenge for a Special Guy Everyone seeks to challenge themselves in life. After all: a life without challenges is dull – full of meaningless, satisfactory living. And so, people set goals in various facets of their lives. The avid learner seeks out a master’s degree. The gamer looks to beat a high score. The sprinter chases after a new personal best time. The business man tries to expand. Often, these challenges are seen as admirable goals by others. They can appreciate the accomplishments, even if they don’t seek them out themselves. And then there’s serial killing. * Face stands in front of his make-shift American flag, his arms crossed behind his back – his scarred hands clasped together. “So...what do you think, Dan?” asks Face, turning to face his critic. Dan looks to be in his twenties and his head is neatly shaven. His jeans and t-shirt, torn and bloodied from an obvious struggle, do not hide his tremendous physical condition. His eyes – both being of the black variety – are opened wide in a mixture of rage and horror, with a dab of amazement thrown in. Dan is left speechless, while Face simply tilts his head – studying his critic’s response. “I know the red is a few shades darker than the actual flag...but I thought you would appreciate the symbolism of it all...” says Face, slowly stepping over to Dan, who – if his increasingly slanting eyebrows are not of the deceiving variety – is starting to exhibit more and more rage and the seconds pass. Face stops behind Dan, turning and resting one of those nice, scarred hands on Dan’s shoulder. Dan tries to recoil, but finds he can barely budge in his predicament. “Now, this was a spur of the moment sort of thing...I felt like doing a tribute to the American Dream match and creating an American flag out of a man’s flesh and bones was the first thing that came to my mind...” continues Face in an entirely too casual, yet still very raspy, tone. “Now, for you, I’ll make sure to plan things out before I begin to make those first cuts...” And there’s that horror coming back again, telling rage to take a backseat. After all: until now, Dan couldn’t have known he would be another victim. For all he knew, Face could have legitimately wanted some criticism on his creation. Sweat; Darting eyes; A slight shaking of the head characterizing disbelief. Face observes the tell-tale signs of a terrifying realization in what can now be assumed is his next victim. Liquid begins to percolate around Dan’s black eyes. He wants to scream, but he’s past that point of fear now. Death is staring him in the face and those black crosses aren’t very comforting. “Oh, don’t worry Dan...I’ll make sure your piece has a much better quality. You have a much better build than the guy I used for my flag.” Face’s promise of high-quality flesh and bone art doesn’t seem to entice Dan, who is now shaking his head at a higher frequency, attempting to tell himself this isn’t happening. Unfortunately, it is. Seeing Dan in such a terrified state persuades Face to laugh harshly at him. After a few moments of what could be the most disheartening, maniacal laughter ever heard, Face takes his hand off of Dan’s shoulder and begins to pace around the room. He begins to conjure up images of a chair, or a footrest – or even both, if he can manage his resources well. However, after musing of possible applications of Dan as furniture, Face gives in to his enormous well of pity and decides to give the supplier some say in the matter. “You know, since you’re the first person to knowingly take part in my new hobby...I think you should get some input on what exactly you are made into. What do you think? Hmmm?” Face waits for Dan’s input, but Dan has now gone into that silent, unmoving phase, which would take several years – possibly even decades – of psychiatric help to fully break him out of. However, Face won’t let him go through all that, being the nice and caring man that he is. “How about something like a dresser in the shape of a ‘D’...for Dan? Hmmm? You’re a pretty large guy,” says Face. “I could probably make you into a Big D...” Face sizes Dan up with his hands and turns to project that size onto a nearby wall. He double-checks and nods, approving the idea. And it is at this point that Dan surprisingly breaks from his catatonic state of hopelessness and speaks. “What...the Hell is this? Wh...Why? Why are you doing this? HOW can you do this?” questions Dan in a very perplexed tone. Face turns to face Dan once more, tilting his head with that look of intrigue he has from time to time. “Well, ideally, I’d slit your throat and allow for your heart to naturally pump your blood out of your body so that the ‘resource acquiring phase’ isn’t as messy as it was last time...Then I’d carefully remove the meat from your bones, so as to keep it for further structuring and decorating. After that, it would simply be a matter of drilling and sawing the bones into the correct places to create the structure of the dresser – you know...what IKEA most likely does with all their sets of furniture...” “NO!” is what Dan abruptly shouts out, cutting Face off from his step-by-step guide of how to convert someone into an IKEA dresser set. “How can you just do this to people? How can you just kill them and...just...” “Incorporate them into the decor of my secret bunker?” Face adds in. Dan looks like he’s about to burst, but Face cuts him off, taking on a much more serious tone. “Alright...let’s cut the foreplay here, Dan. By now, I would assume you would have figured out that I don’t possess the same set of morals that you – and the majority of the world, for that matter – do. I don’t play by the same rules... I don’t see a point to them. Killing people...is quite satisfying and fun for me. And being a serial killer really...spices up my life.” Dan tries to cut Face off, but Face doesn’t let him get a word in. “Of course, as with everything that starts off fun and exciting...being a plain old serial killer has become a little dull. And so, I seek ways to make it more interesting. To be honest, you should consider yourself lucky that I am not making you fight to the death with someone else. That got dull as well...” Face points towards his home-made American flag. “But that...and this (Face points at Dan, who is most likely being referred to as a dresser in this context) is my next solution. I always have these bodies just...decomposing away in another room down here. Why not do something with them? Why not make a new challenge for myself that just happens to intertwine nicely with the whole murdering hobby I have?” “Because that’s sick!” intervenes Dan, finally getting some words out before Face can. “Don’t you feel guilty for killing all these people?” Face sighs to himself, furrowing his brow with one hand and reaching into his back pocket with another. Dan looks on warily. “What...what are you doing?” Face stops furrowing his brow and pulls out a dagger from his back pocket. “Answering your question,” says Face as he plunges the dagger into the side of Dan’s neck, the point of it poking against the opposite side. Face steps back and takes a look at what he’s just done. “No.”
