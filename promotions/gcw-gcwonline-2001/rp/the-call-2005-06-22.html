~The Call~

22 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/2005 A hefty groan would break the silence in the dark hotel room as Erik VanLorne rolled to his side ignoring the ringing in his ears. ~ Ring ring.....ring ring~ Groggily one eye popped open to peer first at the alarm clock to see the time. 3:34 am. Slowly that same icy globe would roll to peer at the room phone watching the red light buzz to life with each ring. Who in hell would be calling him at this time of the night? Eva. Tossing the covers aside roughly he slowly rolled to sit staring at the phone hard enough to burn holes in it before that meaty hand reached out and snatched it off the cradle. 'This better be important Eva....' Assumptions ..they always make one feel like an ass when they are wrong. ' Sleeping well Erik?' The German's world just turned to black and white and icy cold at the sound of that thick Russian accent on the other end. Sighing heavily he slumped against the wall running fingers through his white blond hair as he growled at the voice. 'What do you want Petre? I still have another week to get you what you want.' ' I know you do my ~friend~. This is just a friendly reminder. Remember no diamonds...and that lovely sister of yours will wish she were dead.' ' Listen you piece of Shie--' ~Click~ The reception on the other end had gone dead. Erik yanked the phone from his ear staring at it a moment before slamming it on the reciever and sitting on the edge of his bed with head in his hands. Heaving a heavy breath he fell back on the semi comfortable bed and stared at the ceiling. He had found the diamonds but the person who had them would not sell them at any price. This was causing a major dilemna. Muttering an oath he launched off the bed and stalked to the bathroom. A hand slapped the light on as he stepped to the sink and wrenched to cold water faucet on only to cup his hands beneath the flow and wash the cool liquid over his face. After a moment or two of doing this a hand blindly reached out for a small towel rubbing it briskly over that handsome face before he pressed palms on the counter and stared into his own eyes with disgust. None of this would be happening if they had never taken that job...if he had never.... He pushed the thought from his head hurling the towel at the far wall with another curse. A light knock on his door had his whipping about only to cringe slightly at those bruised ribs. Cautiously he stepped from the bathroom to go peer through the peep hole and spy on whom it was on the other side. It was Eva. Muttering beneath his breath he gathered his calm and slowly began the task of unlocking the locks and tugging the door open wide. Leaning against the frame with arms folded his stared hard at his sister who in turn stared back stoically before he finally moved to place his back at the door allowing her to enter. 'Come on in.' ' I didn't wake you did I?' ' No, Eva for once, I was already up.' She moved through the room until she was standing in the center and said nothing. In fact, niether of them said anything in that akward moment of silence. Finally she turned and looked at him with a look he had not seen since.....well, let's just say it was a long time ago. ' What is it Eva? What is wrong?' ' Erik....I..' He closed the door and slowly approached her. Quietly those blue eyes searched the matching set of his sisters as he questioned her quietly. ' You what...' She took a deep breath and exhaled, turning her back to him to cross arms over her robe. ' I just wanted to know if we are still a team Erik? I need to know I can depend on you tomorrow in the ring..' The man's eyes hardened as his jaw tightened slightly. Hands curled into fists for a moment as he fought the urge to drill her about her mystery date on that night. After a long moment of heavy silence..they relaxed again as he stepped forward and gently took her shoulders within his grasp. ' Da, little sister...da.' Eva turned to wrap her arms around his waist sighing as she rested her cheek against his chest. ' I know you are angry with me..I am angry too. I will tell you..I promise.....Just..' ' Then tell me..' ' Not now Erik. Later...I promise.' As he stared past her head at the window he sighed and merely nodded giving her a tight squeeze before setting her back and murmuring quietly. ' Go get some rest. We have a match tomorrow night.' Nodding she leaned up and kisses his cheek before moving past him and showing herself out the door. As it closed Erik just stared at in silence. She was in danger and she didnt even know it. Was he doing the right thing by not telling her? He didn't know....lately he felt like anything he had known was a lie. Cursing quietly the big man moves back to his bed and crawls beneath the covers to stare once more at the ceiling. Outside the door Eva did not move merely leaning against the frame and looking up with a soft sigh. Slowly that head would roll to the side ear pressed against the frame as she whispered. ' Don't you worry brother dear. I will find out who did this to you...and they will pay.' Shoving off she moved down the hall and opened her own door to enter inside and try and rest for the night, but like Erik, rest would not come for her. Eva's was a mind haunted be conflicting emotions...emotions that revolved around one man....
