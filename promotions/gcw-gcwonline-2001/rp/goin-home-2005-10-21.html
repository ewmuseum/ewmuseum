Date: 10/21/2005 [Scene fades up on a hotel room at the Scottsdale Links Resort in Scottsdale, Arizona. Coming out of the bathroom is Andrea Scabbia. She is brushing her long, red hair when she hears a knock at the door. She heads over to answer it and sees that Sam Wolack is standing in the doorway.] Wolack: What the hell do you think you're doing? [Andrea is quickly taken back by this aggressive question.] Andrea: I came to see you. After all, the person that bank rolled everything that we did in GCW wasn't very pleased by the fact that you left the project, then you up and disappeared. Wolack: Yeah, well I didn't like the deal anymore. I finally was able to get my attorneys to break that god forsaken contract that I signed also so I could legally be made a part of Global's talent roster. Andrea: That's good to know. Wolack: So don't even think about trying to convince me to get back in there. Looks like someone else in Global would be more than willing to take over the role. Andrea: That's not why I'm here. Wolack: Then why in the hell did you come? I know that you didn't come to see me just for laughs. I know that my leaving effectively killed the project, so I know that you're probably now back where they found you. Waitressing at some LA hotspot. Andrea: No, I came to see if you were alright. I knew that you'd come back to where you lived before getting signed up for all of this. I wanted to make sure that there were no hard feelings between the two of us. Wolack: You want to know if there are any hard feelings? No, there aren't. Happy. Andrea: Well, I did have something to ask you? Wolack: What? Andrea: I wanted to come see you, in the ring. I wanted to see what you would have done if you were given the chance to portray X in a match. Wolack: You want to come to WorldWide? When you found out that the whole production was for a wrestling show, you initially didn't want anything to do with it. Now you want to be there in the crowd? Andrea: I want to see what you were are like in your environment. I never got to see that. I want to know what you do for a living. Wolack: You didn't need to ask my permission to buy a ticket. Andrea: I just wanted to know if my being there was going to be a distraction. Wolack: Only if Keiko or Dan would be there would I be distracted. Andrea: You miss them, don't you. Wolack: More than I missed being in that ring. But I can't have them back. So I'm going to do what I know best. Acting isn't it. Wrestling is. Andrea: So, what's next? Wolack: I get to purchase a Holloween Costume and then face some new guy Bronx Mahoney. Andrea: Well, I'll be cheering for you. Wolack: Yeah, well don't get too involved in it. I don't intend to be in that ring very long on Thursday. I intend on looking for that sack of s*** O'Conner so he can answer my challenge. Andrea: You do that. But also, I want you to be careful. [Wolack starts to head for the door.] Wolack: If I did, I wouldn't be true to myself in that ring. [Wolack exits the room as the scene fades to black.]
