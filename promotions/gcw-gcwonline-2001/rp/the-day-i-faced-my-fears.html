The Day I Faced My Fears

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/25/1999 "Leave me alone Carter!" {...This was something I heard more often than anything living in my broken home. The normal R'epertoire, consisting of many such phrases found in the Domestic Abuse hand book. This barrage usually gathered around the obvious Ouches, or numerous threats of calling the authorities, topped off with the occasional blood curdling scream. It's stated that the years of early childhood are the years that are most vivid to remember in a persons life. Awesome. That will work wonders for me...} "I told you the corner store was closed. You will just have to wait till morning." Kendra expresses with fear in her tone. "Why can't you do anyting right?! (hiccup) I couldn't have married a bitch that smokes or drinks?! Now I have to....(hiccup)... wait till morning to smoke. Fuck You!" Carter replied, nearly tripping over his drunkenly confused limbs. "Go to bed will you! You are going to wake up my son. There will be a pack of cigarettes waiting by your bed side by the time you awake." Kendra pleaded as she goes to check on Trey's room. (Carter, completely boozed up, continues on his tirade of habitual nonsense as Kendra gains access to Trey's room where he appears to be in his slumber. Kendra walks up to him and lightly kisses his forehead and pulls the blankets up higher around his shoulders and walks out the door, lightly closing it behind her. As she walks out, Trey's eyes shoot open as he continues to listen in on the situation.) "Listen Carter, clearly you are drunk right now. You need to go to bed. Tomorrow is another day." Kendra states. {There is something I learned this particular night that stuck with me forever. Never call someone drunk when they have been drinking.} (Carter, now with rage in his eyes, stands up and wobbles his way towards Kendra who cowares into the corner of the living room sliding slowly down the wall into a ball with every step Carter takes.) "You fucking slut. I am not drunk. I am completely fucking...(hiccup)...fucking sober. I'll show you to speak out at me..." Carter stumbles a few feet from her as he spills his flask full of Jack Daniels all over the carpet. (Kendra, by this time is aware that she is seconds away from more bruises and story telling, so she quickly jumps to her feet and makes way for the kitchen. Before she can make it even two steps, she is dropped by a hand around her left ankle and comes crashing to the floor below. Carter slowly slugs his deadweight body onto Kendras as he mounts her and lifts his fist as he mocks her before preparing to connect with her cheekbone.) "You useless piece of shit. All you fucking do is cook and clean and stay at home while I...(hiccup)...I make all the money to keep us alive. I think I deserve one...(hiccup)...or two cocktails when i get home! It's my fucking right!" Carter proclaims. (Kendra closes her eyes and prepares for impact as the sound of steel suddenly rings through her ear canals. She opens her eyes and sees Trey [13 years old] wielding a steel fold up chair as he rears back and takes one more hack on the slightly dazed alcoholic. The second swing connects with the forehead of Carter as it shoots a quick paint splatter of blood across the living room wall. The open gash sends tremendous amounts of blood rushing on to the carpet as Carter lies motionless and unconscious in a pool of his [O type positive] blood. Kendra [completely shocked, yet secretly impressed and thankful] rises quickly to her feet...) "Trey! What are you doing?!" Kendra exclaims. (Trey doesn't respond as he hangs his head in disgust as if he has done something horribly wrong. Kendra takes notice of Trey and quickly grabs him and pulls him in for a hug and rubs his head as she continues on...) "You shouldn't have hit him Trey! I brought this upon myself." (She continues hugging him with a slightly tighter grip now.) "He was only trying to prove a point. I shouldn't have snapped on him like that. I was wrong Trey...Do you understand?" She remarked. {I understood loud and clear. That day I nodded my head as to let my mother believe that I didn't know what was taking place around me. But I knew, I always knew. Except this time I wouldn't stand for it any longer. This day played a significant role in changing my life. This was the day I found the courage to take on just about anything. I haven't had or shown any feelings of fear since that day. All I found was my extreme thirst to place harm on to those who seek it. This was the day I faced my fears.}
