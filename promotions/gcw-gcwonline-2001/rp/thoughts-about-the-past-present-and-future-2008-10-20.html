Thoughts about the past, present, and future.

20 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-20-08 Mr. Danger had just returned to his hotel room in London, England after a workout at a nearby gym. He had decided to stay in England for a week instead of making the trip back home. The GCW office had definitely been keeping him very busy in the past week with radio appearances, autograph signings, and charity events. He sat down in a chair exhausted, and for the first time since his stay in London he finally had an afternoon to relax and get his thoughts together. Dangerous Games was less than a week away. The no disqualification match with Face could very well be a defining moment in Danger’s career. Danger’s strategy for the match was very simple, hurt him. He wanted revenge so bad for what Face tried to do to him. Face had tried to injure Danger’s already bad knee, with the intention of ending his career for good. In the back of his mind, Danger wondered how well his knee would hold up in the match? Afterall, the doctors had advised him years ago to never wrestle an active schedule again. But he didn’t care. He just wanted to hurt Face. He just wanted to pull off a win at the pay per view, any way he could. His thoughts then turned to the Dangerous Games match itself. A concept that he had put together on a pay per view over seven years ago as the commissioner of this company. Boy how things have changed. He recalls the early days of GCW, when a young and inexperienced Steven Caldera would often rely on his professional opinion. Together, they finalized the plans and the Dangerous Games pay per view was the result. They decided that in order to draw huge media attention to the new event, Dangerous Games would take place in Mr. Danger’s hometown of Kansas City, and that he would also play a huge role in the show itself. The commissioner delivered, as he was the special guest referee in the GCW World title match that night. The show turned out to be a huge success, and today is no doubt one of the most anticipated shows of the year for competitors and fans alike. Now its seven years later, and Danger never imagined he would have a chance to compete in the very match he created. Now he had a new opportunity. The same opportunity and dream that the other 29 wrestlers in the match had, and that is an instant rise to the top. Now it was all clear. Danger has remembered why he returned to GCW in the first place. This business needed him as much as he needed it. Or at least he thought so anyway. Danger had been away from GCW for so long it seemed that many of the new GCW generation didn’t even remember him. Not only did they not remember him, his legacy, or his accomplishments, but he felt they also didn’t respect any of it. Respect was something very important to Danger. He had worked so hard his entire career to leave his mark, to leave an impression on this business and it was starting to seem like he had failed. This is why Danger felt he had something to prove at Dangerous Games. He needed to make an impact and most importantly earn the respect that he needed. Danger reclined in his chair, and realized he wasn’t as mentally ready for Dangerous Games as he had thought. He had so many emotions running through his head, so many thoughts, and even some doubt. But no matter what, he was determined to give it everything he had come bell time. He felt he had so much to prove, so much history to defend, he was prepared to do anything to come out on top. And that is exactly what he promised himself he would do. XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX It’s the next day and Mr. Danger is in a local London radio studio for an interview to promote Dangerous Games. Danny Dupree hosts a daily sports talk radio show, and the conversation has turned to GCW. Danny: In just a few days time GCW will present their annual pay per view Dangerous Games at the O2 arena here in London. Our special guest today is a man that is credited with creating this event, and that man is Mr. Danger!! Danger: Thank you Danny, it’s my pleasure to be here. Danny: Now I understand you played a huge part in the creation of this event, is that correct? Danger: Well Danny back in the early days of GCW I served a much different role than I do now. I did a lot of work behind the scenes with creative back then, and was the commissioner of the company on the air as well. So we came up with the concept of the battle royal match, but we just needed a name for the pay per view. I suggested Dangerous Games, because afterall on TV and in the fans perception I was making the matches and running the show. So I came out and said I had come up with a way to crown a new number one contender, and thus the name Dangerous Games was born. Danny: Interesting. And this year you are wrestling in a match with GCW newcomer Face. Care to elaborate on this current rivalry? Danger: Face and I have had quite a history the past couple of months. He’s tried to take me out on numerous occasions since my return to active competition, but he’s failed to finish the job. This Sunday our match isn’t just any ordinary match, it’s gonna be a fight with no rules. There will be no disqualification in our match, and that’s gonna make it even more brutal for both of us. And trust me, I will do whatever it takes to win this damn fight! Danny: And that’s not all. In addition to the Dangerous Games match itself, the GCW World Title will also be decided in a brutal fassion. Champion Jay Terror will defend the GCW World Championship against challenger Jordan White in an ACWF match. Who’s your pick? Danger: The ACWF stipulation was just added last week on WorldWide, and will definitely make this one of the most brutal title matches in recent years. And as much as I hate to say it, I’ve gotta side with the champion on this one. Terror has one major advantage on his side, and that is he’s got The Establishment in his corner. In a match where anything goes and with Establishment members sure to be lurking, White is definitely in trouble. Danny: Okay Mr. Danger, let’s turn the tables a little bit and get a more broad interpretation of you’re thoughts on things. How do you think the business has progressed over the last 5 years, and what do you see in the future of the business? Danger: Well we have definitely come a long way with GCW. The business as well as GCW has grown to heights I would have never imagined. There is so much more competition these days, and I mean quality competition. We have so many talented stars on our roster we have the tools to create nothing but success. As for the future? I see a long and prosperous future for the business. Once we reached a certain level, we can only keep getting better. Danny: And just one last question for you personally. What prompted you to return to the ring in the first place, and how much longer can we expect to see Mr. Danger around? Danger: As you and everyone knows I took a long time off. A long time. And I realized that this business was one of the great joys in my life and I just wanted to be a part of it all again. There aren’t many things in life that people do that they truly have a passion for. GCW is one of them for me. I have a passion for this business, and you can bet that Mr. Danger will be around for a long time to come. Danny: Well there you have it folks. Once again you can tune in on pay per view in just a few days to watch Dangerous Games, which will take place from the sold out O2 arena right here in London. I genuinely thank you for your time Mr. Danger, and best of luck to you this Sunday. Danger: Thank you Danny, the pleasure has been all mine. Danger shakes hands with the radio host and quietly leaves the radio studio. As he walks back to his rental car, he thinks about how much truth there was to what he had said on air. Deep down he was full of rage and aggression, and he couldn’t wait to unleash it all inside the ring. He knew that there was a good chance he could severely re-injure his knee, but he didn’t care. He just wanted to get his hands on Face and beat some respect into him so he could finally have justice served. As he continued to walk, he thought more about his life, his future. He really didn’t know what the road ahead had in store for him. He would have to look deep inside himself after what could very well be the fight of his life and find a reason, a purpose. What the future holds? Only time will tell..
