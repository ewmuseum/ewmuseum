For All The Right Reasons

9 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 9, 2007 ---- Foreword: In My Shoes ---- I remember as a teenager, sitting in a class room not too far from where I am standing right now, listening to everyone talk about what they want to do with their lives. Some of the girls had it all figured out. They were going to be vetenarians, or doctors, or lawyers, or nurses. Some of the guys had aspirations of being famous actors, athletes, and scholars. Some of the people didn't know what they wanted to do in life. I was told I didn't have a choice. Back then I was a scared little girl, afraid to make the wrong choices because it might piss off mommy or daddy. Constantly telling me who I was, and what I was supposed to become. I was going to be rich someday. I was going to be somebody special, someone who did good for the world and everyone would know their name. I was going to change the world, and mommy and daddy were going to be quick to take all the credit. Things didn't go as planned for them. I ran away to make my own life, away from them. To blaze my own trail without any assholes dictating what I wanted to be. I wanted to be a fighter. Here I stand today, outside my old high school gymnasium with butterflies in my stomach. I guess mom and dad were right. I was going to become somebody special. I was going to be rich one day. I'll be damned if they take all the credit for something they didn't achieve. It was my blood, sweat, and tears that brought me to this highly unlikely event. My vision still blurry, and my balance still off from the beating I've taken over the past couple of weeks. I have butterflies in my stomach and my bones throb in pain. With every step I take the pain grows harder and harder, but it's the path in life I choose to follow. I'm a fighter, I'm everything I've ever wanted to be. I'm drug and alcohol free... and I don't need any cigarettes to "calm my nerves." I'm straight-edge. It's more than the cool thing to do. It's a life I chose before I even knew the meaning of the phrase. I've watched many people in Chicago fall victim to drug and alcohol abuse, some of them are no longer with us today. I don't advertise my lifestyle, it's not something that you force down someone's throat like those pamphlet weilding heretics that come banging on your door force-feeding you God and his morals. Fuck them, and fuck everyone who wants to tell me what to do with my life. I'll do everything on my own. I don't need anyone's help anymore. I don't need my parents to point me in the direction my life should lead. I don't need Christian Zenith opening doors for me and using his retardedly large amount of power to get what I want. I don't need Dies Irae to intimidate people or me, or have my back. You can call me stubborn all you want, but Highland Park High School wants me in their athletic hall of fame on my own merit. It's something I never asked for, but something I'll proudly accept on behalf of my hard work and dedication to get where I am today. My vision my not be the best, and my body might be trembling in pain... but I don't need any pain pills to get me through the day. I don't need the help of anyone or anything. Just like the Vanguard Championship in a few weeks, I'll walk into the gym and take my award. Something I've busted my ass for. Something I deserve. The only drug I need, is the cheers of millions of people. People who know what it's like to be told that you can't do something. People with hopes and dreams. People who have been waiting far too long to have their time in the sun. People just like me. ---- Chapter One: Broken ---- A thunderous ovation is heard in the Highland Park High School Gymnasium as another participant in the Hall Of Fame ceremony raises his plaque and smiles. He takes a seat behind the podium as Principle Jim Swanson smiles and gives a round of applause. The people in attendance quiet down as Swanson leans into the microphone. "The next athlete I'm introducing is a very... special cause." Swanson speaks, "As many people know this young lady didn't actually graduate from Highland Park High School and caused quite a stir in the community when she ran away five years ago. Yet her talent and dedication to her craft... whichever you may know her by, is equal to if not greater than everyone else in the Hall Of Fame. When Vivica Julian Valentine puts her mind on something, she's been known to accomplish it." The crowd claps as he continues on. "She brought two state girls gymnastics championships to Highland Park High School. Her GPA while she attended never dropped below a 4.0. She's a former prom queen and homecoming queen, and her impact on her classmates could be seen all over the school the day she came up missing." Swanson looked around the gym, "Today, Vivica is on television with Global Championship Wrestling. She's achieved her unlikely dream of being a professional wrestler on the highest level. Just like when she attended gymnastics meets, Vivica's determination in the ring is unrivaled. Vivica Julian Valentine shows us all that if you dream and you believe... you will achieve. Ladies and gentleman... Vivica Valentine." The Highland Park High School Marching Band plays a sheet music version of "Image Of The Invisible" by Thrice as Vivica J. Valentine walks through the door. Wearing a small black dress and a plastic smile to hide the pain, The Fearless Phenom slowly made her way across the gymnasium floor to a standing ovation. In the crowd she saw familiar faces from her past. Old teachers, friends, classmates. Looking at them all again was almost humbling in a way... almost. Her heart raced as she approached the podium, her sight grew blurry and her knees quivered. Vivica showed no signs of weakness though, standing tall behind the microphone holding the plaque in her hands. A handful of wrestling fans who somehow made their way to the ceremony began a "Thank You VIVICA!" chant followed by a clap. "No, no..." Vivica spoke for the first time, "Thank you all for coming out tonight. My road back to Highland Park has been a long, winding, and dangerous one. Yet for all the bad times, there were just as many good times. Every mis-step came with a lesson, and from every lesson I learned and grew as a person. I wouldn't trade any of my experiences along the way for the wol..." The Fearless Phenom grabbed onto the podium as her right knee bent out from under her and the room began to spin. Closing her eyes she tried to remain focused on the task at hand. Everyone in the room began to talk, rumors began to swirl almost immediately as Vivica took a deep breath. Trying her best to remain cool and confident. "I wouldn't trade them for the world." Vivica spoke, "When I left Highland Park I was a scared little girl. Afraid of what my life had in store for me, afraid of what I might bec..." Suddenly the crowd gasped as Vivica collapsed onto the floor. Her Highland Park Hall Of Fame plaque skiddind across the hardwood. Before anyone could go over and check to see if she was okay a hooded man jumped out of the audience and raced to her side. "We need an ambulence!" He shouted. His long black hair stuck out from under his hood as everyone in the room paniced, grabbing their cell phones and dialing as fast as their little fingers could take them. The Fearless Phenom looked into the white glassy eyes of the concerned stranger. Though he was a stranger to everyone in the room, he wasn't a stranger to Vivica J. Valentine. Yet what he was doing here eluded her, as her eyelids became heavy and everything arounded her blurred out into darkness. Vivica J. Valentine enters the gymnasium of Highland Park High School to thunderous applause, as the newest inductee of the Highland Park High School Athletic Hall Of Fame for gymnastics. As she goes forward to shake the hand of the principle she collapses onto the floor. A mysterious man in a black hoodie runs down from the bleechers to get her help as everyone watches in shock. ---- Chapter Two: Confessions Of A Vampire ---- "I need to get the hell out of here..." A girl's voice grunted across the room. Violently pulling out drawers and ripping clothes off of hangers as she moved about. She stuffed them all into two different suitcases, packed on a bed. "Won't this whole thing just blow over Vivica?" A man said on the far side of the room, sitting in a chair by the window. His face was covered by long stringy black hair, but you could assuredly tell that he was looking over at The Fearless Phenom as she packed. Vivica stopped for a moment to take a breath, something that usually didn't happen with her overly brash personality. "I don't think so this time Markus..." Vivica spoke, "I've had enough of this bullshit. Ever since I lose to Brandon Youngblood I've been treated like garbage. No matter what I do, or what I say I'm wrong. I'm treated like a fucking child and I'm sick of it. I tell them exactly what's going to happen and they don't believe me. Mayhem fucked them up real good, didn't he? Just like I told THEM." Vivica continued to rip clothes and possessions off in any direction she could in a fit of rage. Although Markus couldn't get a clear view of her face from across the room, he knew she was crying. So badly he wanted to reach out and comfort her. Deep down inside he knew it would be the wrong thing to do. Just listening to her voice strained with anger and tears made him want to change the world. He'd felt like this before... but not since the accident. Things were different now, much different. "So what are you going to do?" Markus stated, "We can't just go running back to Christian." The mention of Christian Zenith stopped Vivica in her tracks. "Fuck Christian Zenith." Vivica said before slamming one of her luggage bags shut with a grunt, "WE'RE not going anywhere. I'm going to go stay in a hotel and look around for houses. In the meantime YOU are going to stay with Lia. Where she can take care of you, the way you need to be taken care of." "I'm not a child either you know." Markus blurted out from the corner, "I'm not a little kid you can just toss onto the babysitter whenever you don't have the time." "It's not fucking like that Markus and you know it!" Vivica screams back, "I know nothing about what you are or how you tick. Some weird vampire side-effect could come up any day now and I just can't run you to the doctor. I have a very limited supply of blood packs and then what? Seduce Christian again? I don't think so. We need a solution to this." "Well I'm not killing anything... if that's where you're getting at." "It's not where I'm getting at." Vivica snapped back almost immediately, "What I'm getting at is you need specific lessons in your new life that frankly I can't give you. I can't give you advice, I can't give you tips, I can't teach you anything. Lia is perfect for this sort of thing. She knows exactly what you're going through, and with her you don't have to worry about a thing." Markus shook his head in the corner, he couldn't believe Vivica would just up and leave him with Lia and Virgil. One of the same people she wanted to run away from so quickly. Why couldn't she just understand? "So you're just going to leave me here... with them." Markus finally spoke, "You know sometimes there are a lot more important things to my life than what I am. I'm a vampire... KIND OF. We don't even really know if it's 100% true. We don't have that kind of equipment and I highly doubt it exists. Plus some things are more important to me than being able to learn about what I am." "Like what?" Vivica said shoving the last bit of her personal items into her suitcase. "Like..." Markus hesitated, "Like I love you." The frantic pace of The Fearless Phenom came to a very sudden halt. Wiping the tears away from her eyes, Vivica looked across the room at Markus who just glared back at her. Awaiting a response, anything. A million throughts raced through the mind of Miss Valentine... including the tragic death of Markus' last girlfriend. A thought that had always settled into the back of her mind whenever she was in a room with him. "Now I know I have to go." Vivica picked up her suitcases and looked back at Markus one last time before slamming the door shut behind her. The darkness slowly began to disappear, and the faint dim of lights began to consume Vivica's consciousness. The image started out as a series of lights, and slowly objects came into focus. A flourescent light hovered above her head and quickly she looked around the room. Laying in an adjustable bed and dressed in hospital garb, The Fearless Phenom sat up and rubbed her head in confusion. "How did I get her..." Vivica began to speak aloud to herself before a conversation outside the door interrupted her silence. "Let us in, we're her parents. We want to see that she's okay." A man yelled from outside the door. "She's not awake yet..." Another man responded. "Don't you know who in the hell I am? My name is Jack Valentine. I'm a lawyer, and if you don't let me in..." He was cut-off in mid thought. "You'll what?" The familiar voice of Markus joined the conversation, "You'll ruin this doctor's life the same way you ruined your daughters?" "Who in the hell are you anyway?" Jack Valentine responded. "Who I am doesn't matter." Markus retaliated before going on, "You've known where Vivica is for well over three years now and you've made zero effort to contact her and you sure as hell didn't give a damn about her well being. At that Hall of Fame ceremony back there though you had no problem going around introducing yourself as Vivica's father... quick to take credit for everything she's done with her life. You havn't even talked to her in FIVE YEARS." "I have better things to do than to sit around here and listen to some... gothic punk tell me about MY daughter." Jack responded, "It was some asshole like you that probably talked her into running away from home in the first place." "Oh it was an asshole alright. It was you." Markus' words burned deep. "I think it's best that you leave Mr. Valentine." The first man said. At this point Vivica was almost positive this man was the doctor attending to her. "I'll have the whole lot of you fired." Jack threatened, "Just you wait and see." Inside the room The Fearless Phenom closed her eyes and laid back in her bed again. It was such a surreal moment for her. Last thing she remembers she was standing in front of a crowd at her High School, and now suddenly two men she thought were gone from her life are outside arguing in front of her hospital room. One thing was for certain. Tonight was going to be a long, long night. ---- Chapter Three: Clearing The Air ---- The steril, prisoner like atmosphere of the hospital was starting to wear down at Vivica J. Valentine as she laid in bed. Restless, she tapped her fingers on the table beside her while staring up at an episode of Law & Order. So badly she wanted to just get up and leave, she felt perfectly fine. For the life of her she didn't understand why they were holding her. Like a convict on death row she felt, waiting impatiently for the call from the governor to come through. The more she waited, the longer she thought she was going to have to stay. The only thought that crossed her mind was that she'd drag the bed to Las Vegas for the Vanguard Championship Match if she had to. The sound of clicking grew closer and closer to her room, she knew someone was on the way. Her cerulean blue eyes fixated on the door, staring impatiently. So badly she wanted the doctor to walk through and tell her to leave. Conversation soon accompanies the clicking noise. Markus began to speak as they approached, with great concern in his voice. "So what's wrong with her exactly?" Markus asked. "Her body is finally caving in on her." The doctor said, "The human body can only take so much punishment before it just gives out from under you. I'd say she collapsed due to severe fatigue and lack of pain treatment. She has to have been in constant pain for well over three weeks, but she refuses to take pain killers." "She's straight-edge." Markus said as the doctor stopped in front of the door and turned the knob carefully. "What's that mean?" The doctor said as they stepped inside. "It means I don't do drugs. Period." Vivica stated, nudging her way into the conversation as only she could. They both looked at The Fearless Phenom, still in hospital garb drumming away at the table set up next to her bed. Trying to get out of the bed you could see Vivica wince before laying back down again. Frusteratingly she grunted shaking her head. "Well if you don't want any pain medication..." The doctor continued, "I guess there is no reason to keep you here." "Great, well it was nice seeing you doc but..." "Hold on a second there Miss Valentine." The doctor interrupted as Vivica was already halfway out of her bed. Unwillingly The Black Mariah sat back down, with an emphatic sigh. "I don't recommend you wrestle this weekend." The doctor said, "I'm to understand from talking to some of our resident wrestling fans here at the hospital that you're in a particularly violent match. You're in absolutely no condition to be fighting. You can hardly move without grimacing." "I'll be fine." Vivica stated, "Just like I always am. No one knows my body better than I do... and I don't need to pop any pills either. I just need a couple days rest, and I'll fly out to Vegas first thing Friday morning. Take a night out on the town..." Markus looked back at Vivica J. Valentine. "Alone." Vivica added staring back at Markus Eaux D'Artifice. "Well I'll go fill out the paper work, get yourself ready to go Miss Valentine." The doctor said as he turned around and walked out of the room, taking a hint from the menacing stare from Markus on the other side of the room. Closing the door behind him, the doctor left Markus and Vivica in the room... alone. For the first time since the night she left The McFarlane Estate. The silence in the room was noticeable as Vivica pulled the curtain shut and started getting back into her normal clothes. What was there left to say? Markus poured his emotions out to her and she just turned away. He couldn't much blame her though, not with what happened with his last girlfriend. The terror-filled images still haunted his mind. It was that grim scene that often made him wish he were dead. "I'm going with you." Markus spoke, finally breaking the awkward silence the emerged once the doctor had left. "No, you're not going with me." Vivica retaliated, "I'm not a little girl Markus, I don't need anyone to hold my fucking hand wherever I go anymore. I'm sick of people thinking I need someone to protect me. I'm just FINE on my own. Thank you." "You know back at The Estate..." "Let's not talk about it..." Vivica said, pulling the curtain back aside and throwing the hideous lavender colored hospital robe down on the bed. She had put back on the black dress she was wearing when she collapsed, and one couldn't help but to realize how stunningly beautiful she was. It was a rare occurance when Vivica wore a dress, and she looked amazing. "Quit staring." Vivica said as she slipped her shoes back on, sitting on the side of the bed. Each bend was accompanies by a small grunt. One she was trying to hide from Markus. Trying not to let him see any weakness in her. "I'm going with you, whether you want me to or not." Markus spoke, taking control of a conversation with Vivica for the first time in their friendship, "I know you think having someone by your side makes you weak, and I know you can do just fine on your own. You know I get that... you're a tough girl. You're a professional fighter Vivica. Everyone knows that. You don't have anything that you have to PROVE to me." Vivica looked down, knowing Markus was speaking the truth. "There is a lot more to life than how tough people think you are," Markus continued on, "You have the opportunity of a lifetime this weekend. You can become the first woman ever to win the GCW Vanguard Championship. That's pretty tough Viv, and whether you like it or not I'm going to be right there in the crowd cheering you on... and there isn't a damn thing you can do to stop me." Vivica looked back up at Markus, slowly from the ground. "I'll be damned if anything stops me from watching you live your dreams." It might've been the way Markus stood up for himself, or it might've been the words that he said... but something inside Vivica made her forget all those doubts she had about him at that very moment. The fear of history repeating itself subsided, and a smile crossed the face of The Black Mariah. It seemed like her entire life people were holding her down and telling her that she couldn't get the job done. That she couldn't pursue her dreams. For once, Vivica thought, it was nice to have someone believe in her. Just like the cheer of the crowd, it was invigorating and uplifting. At High Stakes, it wasn't about going out there to win the Vanguard Championship to prove the critics wrong. For once in her life Vivica wanted something more than that. She wanted to win the Vanguard Championship for Markus, and all those other people that believed in her now too. For the first time in her life, Vivica J. Valentine wanted to prove everyone right. These are the things dreams are really made of.
