A good yet strange night

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/05 'Really? No shit? That's fuckin' awesome!' I said into my phone, my excitment growing by the second. 'Who else? Really? This just gets better and better!' The knock at my door pulled my attention away from the phone for just a second, and I moved to answer it, keeping the phone plastered to my ear. I pulled the door open to see Jeff standing there, the look on his face perplexed, as he remembered the mood I was in the other day. I waved him in as I continued on the phone, 'You're serious? Oh my God, this is great. Look, I gotta go, keep me updated, and I'll talk to you later.' I snapped the phone shut, a big shit-eating grin still stuck on my face. 'I take it things are looking up?' Jeff said closing the door to my hotel room behind him. 'After the mood you were in yesterday, I expected to find you curled up in the corner sucking your thumb and crying for your mommy.' 'That was before the phone call I was just on.' I said, 'Paramount just picked up my movie! 'The Smuggler' is going to see life as a live action movie next summer!' 'No shit?' Jeff asked. 'No shit.' I said, 'I guess you were right, being a succesful wrestler does have it's advantages!' 'Well it also helps that you're a damn good writer...for a wrestler.' 'Fuck you.' I laughed, punching him in the arm. 'So come on, give me details. You can't leave me hanging!' 'Calm down, have some dip.' I said, recalling a line from a George Carlin routine. 'Well first and foremost, the lead role of the smuggler is going to be played by The Rock, James Marsden, the guy who played Cyclops in 'X-Men' is Lex the Marauder, Uma Thurman is gonna play the character I based off of Eva VanLorne and Dennis Hopper is gonna play the president of Global City.' 'And what about the coolest character in your movie? You know, that Wild guy,' Jeff said, trying to act nonchalant, 'Who's gonna be playing him?' 'Well they're up in the air about that.' I said, 'It's either gonna be a guy in a suit or CGI. Either way, the role's yours if you want it.' He seemed taken aback by that, I guess he wasn't expecting to get the role, but was expecting someone cool to take the role. 'Really?' 'Yeah, so it's up to you. You can either wear a really heavy, hot suit, or provide the motion capture and voice for a CGI version of Wild. Either way, you'll be in the movie. The only reason I'm offering you the role is because unlike me, you don't have a full wrestling schedule.' 'I'll have to think about it.' He said, 'I hear those suits can be damn uncomfortable, but CGI can sometimes take longer to get just right.' 'You don't need to give me an answer right now, but Speilberg wants to know so he can either get the costume department working, or call ILM and get them started on the GCI.' 'Wait, did you say Speilberg? As in Steven Speilberg?' 'No, I meant his less talented brother, Ira Speilberg. Of course I meant Steven Speilberg! He's agreed to direct!' 'Well you know this calls for a celebration!' Jeff said, springing up off the couch where he had planted himself. It always amazed me how agile he could be for a guy who was nearly seven feet tall and a shade shy of 300 pounds. He glanced at his watch, 'I think, it's beer-thirty!' 'Beer-thirty you say?' I asked rubbing my chin. 'To the nearest drinking establishment!' I said, pulling on my shoes, and locating my keys, wallet and cell phone. We headed downstairs and out to my rental car, I glanced over at him as we drove. 'Are you sure you wanna do this?' I asked 'What? Drink?' 'No,' I said, smirking, 'Be here, be at the show tomorrow. I mean it is the LeStatt Knight ego show, and as much as I'm concerned about Rivero and his gimps jumping me, I'm a big boy, I can take care of myself.' Jeff sighed, 'Look, Mike, I know what you're worried about. You're worried about me confronting LeStatt again like I did the night he was your partner, right?' I nodded. 'Don't. I know there's a lot of history between me and Knight, a lot of bad blood. Most of it spilled in arenas across the country, but there's no real point in me confronting Roberto. Verbally or physically. I don't hate the man anymore, but he's not on my Christmas card list either. It's like I told him in 3W, there's no point to me and him fighting anymore. There's no point to the Wylde/Knight feud anymore even if I was still wrestling. It's like a dog chasing it's tail, no matter how fast it runs, it's never gonna catch it. Same goes for me and him, no matter how much we chase each other, no matter how many matches we fight, whatever stipulations there are, there's no point to it anymore. I'll be at the show, but I'll do my best to avoid Roberto for the simple fact that there's nothing more to be said between the two of us. But do me one favor.' 'What?' 'If you see him, tell him Happy Birthday from me.' he said smirking. I smiled back before I glanced at the speedometer and the gas guage next to it. 'Shit. I need gas.' I spotted a gas station on the next block and pulled in, 'I'm gonna run in to grab something to eat real quick, you want anything? Because remember what happened the last time you drank on an empty stomach.' He grimaced, 'Yeah, that was ugly. Grab me a couple of hot dogs or microwave burgers or something.' I headed inside and paid for the gas before looking around for something for the two of us to eat so we wouldn't get stupid drunk. I snagged some microwave burgers and cooked them up. 'I'm telling you it's him.' Great. Fans. 'Over there.' As I put another burger in the microwave I noticed out of the corner of my eye two guys looking at me. One of them had on a yellow and black jacket and a black stocking cap, the other had on a long black trenchcoat and a backwards baseball cap. The two were looking at me as if they were expecting me to do something other than make microwave burgers. I turned towards them and raised an eyebrow. 'Can I help you guys?' 'See!' the one in the yellow and black coat said, hitting the other one in the arm. 'I told you it was him! Snoogans!' 'What is it? You guys want autographs or something? I gotta get going. Just a warning though, I only autograph the body parts of females.' 'No, no.' the one in the yellow and black jacket said, 'Allow me to introduce ourselves, I'm Jay and this is my hetero life mate Silent Bob.' Silent Bob just waved at me, still staring at me as if he expected me to jump into promo mode or something. 'Hi.' I said, putting the third burger in the microwave. 'We were actually thinking that we could help you.' Jay said. I looked back at them, were they kidding me? Jay looked like he'd blow away if a strong breeze came along, and Bob looked like he didn't miss any meals if you catch my drift. 'You? Help me?' I decided to humor them, 'How?' 'Well maybe not Lunchbox here as much as me.' Jay said, 'But I'm betting you could use a manager.' 'Look guys, I appreciate the offer, but bringing a manager to ringside for a wrestling match went out with the 80's.' 'Yeah, but we know that fuckwad Dan Rivero and his fuckin' masked butt-buddies have it in for you. You have us along, if they fuckin try to fuckin jump you, me and Lunchbox we fuckin jump in and represent! With a Snootch and a Bootch,' these were represented by Jay shadow boxing with a cardboard cutout of Dale Ernhardt Jr on the beer cooler, 'We knock them the fuck out, leaving you to go to the ring and fuckin kick some fuckin ass.' Silent Bob nodded so much I thought his head was gonna fall off. 'Again, I appreciate it,' I said, realizing that the third burger was done and put in the forth one, 'But personally, I think Rivero and his goon squad would eat you two for breakfast. Plus, I already have a manager, someone to watch my back if Rivero and his boys do try and jump me.' Jay turned and whapped Silent Bob in the chest. 'See, I told you it wouldn't work!' Bob flinched and a look crossed his face as if to say 'Me?'. However, he remained just as quiet as his name suggested. 'Well, then,' Jay said, 'Could you maybe hook us up with some free tickets?' I pulled the last burger from the microwave and collected up the other three I had made and grabbed some ketchup packets. 'Sorry guys, but not on such short notice. I'd have to give the powers that be at least a week's advance notice and the show's tomorrow night.' I reached the counter and fished out my wallet to pay for my burgers. Jeff poked his head in, 'Come on man, how long does it take to microwave some burgers? The beers not gonna drink itself man!' 'I'll be right there!' I said, paying and heading outside. As I reached the car I saw Silent Bob come out after me, Jay was arguing with the clerk over the price of a pack of gum. 'Good luck.' he said. I nodded, and Jay came out. 'Come on Silent Bob, I think there's a Circle K up the road.' The two took off Silent Bob looking at me and shrugging as if to say 'Wherever he goes, I go.' I shook my head and got in the car. 'What the hell was that all about?' Jeff asked taking the burgers while I started the car. 'I don't know, there's something about those guys though. Like I've seen them before.' 'They look like a couple of mallrats to me.' Jeff said taking a bite of one of the burgers. I brushed it aside and took a burger as I pulled out of the gas station. There were beers out there with our names on them. It was after all beer-thirty.
