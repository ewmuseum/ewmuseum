AXEL in Time- Chapter II: Big Trouble in Ancient China (a.k.a. The Great Wall Incident)

25 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 25, 2009 There’s an ancient Chinese proverb that says, ?May the bird of Paradise shit on your head.? Those words have never rang more true for anyone than they have for AXEL Action over the last few months. He’s been a part of a faction that was going absolutely nowhere, he’s been battered, bloodied and bruised at the hands of Kenny Mercury and cheated out of the American Dream by Jacob McKail. Most recently, he’s been sucked through a time vortex with no idea where he’s going to end up. So when it comes to being shit on by “birds of Paradise”, AXEL has been hit by a raging storm of projectile diarrhea. Our hero (or zero, depending on how you want to look at him) comes to in the middle of a bamboo forest somewhere in the heart of China. The only problem is that he doesn’t know he’s in China, nor does he know that he’s lying in the middle of a patch of thorns and brambles. He begins to stir, prickly vines tearing at his flesh, a sharp scream echoing through the bamboo stalks. Deciding to remain still for a moment, he attempts to gather his bearings. Blood pools underneath him on the forest floor, coating the underbrush with viscous, red liquid. “Okay, AXEL, get it together?” AXEL takes a deep breath, the vines ripping into his flesh more and more with every expansion and contraction his chest makes. In his mind, the only way out of this predicament is to just get up as quickly as he can, but what he doesn’t realize is that those vines are actually peeling the flesh from his body. It’s having almost the same effect as the barbed wire had on him back in his days of wrestling in Japan, so it isn’t like he’d never felt this kind of excruciating pain before. It’s just that it has been so long, and his mind has settled back into the less brutal style of American professional wrestling. He takes another deep breath, exhaling raggedly as the jagged shrubbery rends the flesh from his body, sinews nearly visible. Intuition tells him that he needs to get up before parasites and bacteria from the soil can cause infection to set in. He braces himself for the inevitable and sits up, peeling himself off of the vines. The sound of flesh tearing away from the bone resounded throughout the forest, coupled with a bloodcurdling scream so loud that it would most likely rouse the dead if there were any around. He tries to take deep, calming breaths, but all he can do is pant and wheeze as he tries to gather his wits about him. “Alright” Now comes the hard part.” He places his hands on the forest floor, doing his best to avoid anymore of the green, terrors that struggle to keep hold of him, but to no avail. As soon as he touches his palms to what seems to be the forest floor, he feels the stinging sensation of thorns puncturing his palms. His eyes shoot wide open, and he knows he’s in trouble if he doesn’t get up. Pain notwithstanding, he forces himself to turn his body, peeling his lower half off of the thorns, the fabric of his pants tearing noisily. Now that he’s to his knees, it won’t be quite so hard to get up. However, those vines just aren’t cutting him any slack, as they dig into his knees, pressing against the kneecaps. Fighting back another scream, half-worried that he might alert any savages that may be around; half-protecting his slightly bruised ego, he grits his teeth, his brain scrambling for a way to block out the pain he’s feeling, but it’s of no use. There simply is no easy way to do it, so he forces himself to a standing position, brambles clinging to his clothes almost as if in a last ditch effort to drag him down into their prickly grasp. That is quickly remedied when he takes hold of the portion without thorns and peels the brambles from the fabric. He takes a good look at his surroundings for the first time since coming to, and he’s not quite sure what to make of his situation. In fact, he’s not quite sure where or why he is where he is. His mind hazy, he attempts to navigate his way through the bamboo, but has a hard time doing so without a machete. He remembers the Swiss Army knife he received from his grandfather on his twelfth birthday, but realizes it would never cut through those tough stalks of bamboo. As trusty as it was, it wouldn’t suffice in a situation such as this. His only course of action, he decides, is to try and muscle his way through the stalks, being as careful as possible to not get tangled up in anymore vines. Trekking through the forest without a clue where he’s going, The Unholy One pushes branches and vines aside, trying to make his way to some sort of civilization. As he trudges through the thick forest, his surroundings grow brighter and brighter the nearer he gets to the edge of freedom. The smell of blood is heavy in the air as his wounds continue to gush, his white t-shirt a dark crimson from all the blood he was losing. He knows that if he doesn’t find some help soon, that he will die from blood loss. There’s just no way around it. ?Come on, AXEL, you can make it. Pain is only weakness leaving the body, and you have no weakness. You’re the Unholy One for fuck’s sake!? He repeats those words over and over inside of his head, trying to battle through the pain that is wracking his body. Strangely enough, the row of trees and bamboo on the outside edge of the forest is much thicker than the rows deeper in, almost as if they’re trying to keep him from leaving. He backs up just a bit and takes a running start, barreling through the bamboo, managing to break through. AXEL topples to the ground due to the force of his momentum and rolls on over to his knees where he then regains his vertical base. Taking a moment to take in his surroundings, he’s suddenly enveloped in a whole new culture, one he’s never seen before. Multi-tiered, pyramidal pagodas with upward-curving roofs over each individual story are scattered throughout the area. The streets are crowded with pedestrians whom are going about their everyday lives without any knowledge of his presence. For those who aren’t crazy about walking, rickshaws dot each and every street corner as far as the eye can see. It’s almost as if he’s really in ancient China. That can’t be, though. Maybe he just fell asleep and dreamed the whole thing with the time portal in his pantry? He pinches himself to find out whether he’s awake or not, and much to his dismay, he finds that he’s wide awake. Not only that, but the pinch hurt immensely, as well. Speechless and in complete awe, he makes his way through the town while absorbing the culture around him. By now, he’s completely forgotten all about the searing pain that’s resonating through his body, his eyes locked on the ornamental lanterns and the lovely, silken robes that the peddlers are hawking. Suddenly, he’s pulled inside of what appears to be some sort of ancient medical facility of some sort. “You huht(hurt)!” AXEL looks at the strange female, his eyes showing signs of slight bewilderment. Why this woman is speaking in broken English is beyond him. Could it be that by breaking through the barriers of time, he’s altered the past in some way? That can’t be, can it? After all, he hasn’t actually directly affected anything. He speaks up, his voice a little shaky. “Wh-what?” “Huht! You huht!” The woman points to his shirt, which is absolutely reeking of blood. In her frantic pointing, she accidentally presses her finger against one of the many, MANY wounds on his body and he cries out in agony. ?YEEEAAARRRGGGHHH!!!? “Huht! You huht!” He begins to feel really stupid and inadequate once it finally dawns on him what this woman has been saying this entire time. ?Oh? She was saying “hurt.”?, he says to himself, nodding in her direction. “Yes, I’m hurt. Badly.” “I take care you!” And take care of him, she does. Almost immediately, she has his shirt peeled off of him before pushing him face-first onto one of the makeshift beds. He doesn’t question her methods; for fear that she might kill him if he does. Suddenly, the sharp sting of what seems to be an ancient anti-bacterial medication surges through him and he gives a sharp gasp for air. It hurt, and badly, but at least he doesn’t have to worry about dying from some sort of weird bacterial infection. Who knows what kind of infections and diseases are running rampant in old China? “Drink this!” “What?” AXEL’s unable to get a word in, edgewise, as she already has the cup to his lips, pouring the liquid down his throat. The only choice he has is to swallow or drown, so he swallows the fluid. Coughing and sputtering, trying to get the taste of the rancid fluid out of his mouth, he attempts to ask what it was. Before he can get the words out, though, everything goes hazy. His vision blurs, his speech becomes slurred and he slowly passes into unconsciousness. While he’s out, the woman begins peeling the leeches from the wounds on his back. Those would explain the stinging sensation he had felt before being anesthetized. If he were awake at this moment, he would recall something he learned in high school history classes about the use of leeches for medicinal purposes in ancient China. It’s a good thing he’s unconscious at the moment, because if he wasn’t he would be in a world of hurt right about now. The woman pulls a scorching hot iron out of the fire that was built in the fireplace and began touching it to each of the cuts on AXEL’s back. The sound of sizzling flesh emanates from the building, the sound pouring into the street where everyone can hear. AXEL comes to a few minutes later, groaning quietly as he shakes off the cobwebs. His back feels slightly hot, and he cranes his neck to try and see why. All he can see are the welts and blisters from where the strange woman seared his flesh. “What did you do”!” The anger rings clear in AXEL’s voice, and it’s obvious that he is rather disturbed by what she’d done to his back. He turns over and sits up on the bed, his eyes burning with a fiery passion. He addresses the woman, whom seems to be ignoring him, once more. “Hey, woman! I asked you what you did to my back!” “I fix! I fix!” “What, exactly, did you fix” All you did was burn me!” “You get infected. I kill it!” Why this woman is speaking like a caveman is completely lost on him, all he knows is that she’s crazy. Everyone knows you don’t kill infection by burning someone. You just? burn someone by burning someone. It’s clear that AXEL is rather annoyed, and is tired of this silly woman, so he gets up, pulls his bloodied shirt back on and heads for the door. He stops for a moment and turns towards the woman. “Thanks for the” um? help.” He turns once more to leave, but she calls out to him before he can. Turning around once more, he lets out a frustrated sigh, his shoulders slumping. He can tell that she is in the mood for conversation. “Wait! Wall is complete! Celebration tonight!” AXEL finds this woman’s lack of grammar and proper sentence structure to be rather appalling, but what can he do? It’s ancient China, after all, most likely sometime between the fifth and sixteenth century, B. C. She isn’t supposed to speak English at all, yet somehow, she’s been doing so this entire time. “Look, lady. I don’t give a damn about any celebration or anything. I just want to go home.” “Home” This is home.” “No, this is YOUR home. MY home is in the future. MY home is in Hollywood, Florida; not ancient China.” Frustrated, his patience spent, AXEL simply turns and leaves. Upon exiting the building, he takes a deep breath and looks around for a moment. Unsure of what to do or where to go, he decides to do the most logical thing and backtrack. He makes his way down the street, dodging pedestrians and rickshaws, careful not to be trampled by all the people. He finally arrives at the edge of the village only to find that the bamboo forest is no longer there. He rubs his eyes, blinking fiercely as if trying to verify that what he was seeing was reality. He can feel his heart drop into the pit of his stomach, and his first thought is that there’s no way out of this Hellhole known as China. ?Where could it have gone? It was just here a few hours ago; It couldn’t have grown legs and walked away, could it?? It’s at that moment that he finally realizes that the forest was never really there in the first place. Was it an illusion created by the time portal? Or was it the time portal, itself? Either way, there is one thing that’s for sure. Those injuries he sustained from the bramble patch are real. They’re so real, in fact, that he can still feel the stinging pain from the combination of the gashes the thorns made and the welts of that scorching hot iron. Defeated, AXEL makes his way back into the village and decides that he might as well go to the big party that the woman was talking about. What could it possibly hurt? There’s only one problem, however. He has no idea where the “Wall” is, or even which “Wall” she was talking about. Could it be the Great Wall? It almost certainly has to be; as that seems to be the only “Wall” that anyone would put on a celebration about. AXEL considers asking for directions, but he knows that it won’t get him very far. Besides, he doesn’t have the patience to put up with anymore of that horrid, broken English. Deciding that the best course of action to take would be to try and locate it on his own, he turns around and begins making his way back through the city. Once again dodging pedestrians and rickshaws, he makes his way to the other edge of the village, only to be met with expansive stretches of mountainside and forest. He stops for a minute to take in the beauty of the trees and the mountains when suddenly, he feels something brush by him. Snapped from his trancelike state, he looks in the direction the sensation came from, only to notice what seems to be everyone from the village heading into the forest. “Come! Party this way!” “Look, I can’t come just because you tell me to!” Innuendo! And the voice that called out to him? You guessed it; it’s the woman from before. AXEL sighs and nods, following the group of people through the forest, careful to avoid any of the brambles and thorns that litter the foliage. When he finally reaches the inner edge of the trees, his breath is taken away by what he sees. From left to right, as far as he can see is a vast stretch of wall that appears to go on for miles and miles. He finally realizes which “Wall” the woman was referring to. She was referring to the Great Wall of China. “Wow! That’s the most beautiful thing I’ve ever seen!” It’s the kind of thing that would bring a tear to AXEL’s eye. That is, if he were the type of man who cries. Though he doesn’t want to be in ancient China, he can’t help but feel completely overwhelmed by the wall. There is so much history within its structure, and he is getting to witness it firsthand. A short, chubby man with a bald head and a moustache that hangs past his waist steps onto a soapbox and clears his throat. He begins speaking in what seems to be Chinese, and quite frankly, AXEL hasn’t a clue what the man is saying. He looks to the woman who “doctored” him previously, who just happens to be conveniently standing beside him. “What’s he saying?” “He says this is momentous occasion. Great Wall finally complete, now we celebrate!” AXEL looks up, only to see the man point at him, then to the pile of fireworks sitting at the base of the wall. His eyes widen and his head tilts from confusion. “What’s he asking me to do?” “He wants you light fireworks. You are foreigner. Only foreigner to come in years. It’s a big honor!” He shrugs his shoulders, thinking to himself, ?How bad could it be? I’ll just light the fireworks and leave, and no one will be the wiser.? AXEL heads over towards the base of the wall, and is handed a small torch that is surprisingly, not lit. He cocks a brow at the torch, wondering how, exactly, he’s supposed to light the fireworks when there’s no fire to the torch. His question is answered soon enough, as the torch is taken from him and struck along the side of the wall like a match. Fire erupts from it with a loud roar, and the crowd surrounding the wall cheers loudly. AXEL kneels down and picks up the rather long wick that is attached to several different sets of fireworks and lights it. He hands the torch to one of the guards standing around the area and walks away, heading back towards the city to look for a way out of this era. ?Fssssssss?! FWEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE~!? The fireworks sizzled and squealed as they went off, giving a loud, ?POP!?, every so often as they go off in the air. What he doesn’t realize is that there are explosives in a storehouse on the top of the wall, and the door to the room is open. One of the fireworks happens to fly into that room, careening into the explosives, triggering an explosion of MASSIVE proportions. ?BOOM!!!? He turns around just in time to see debris and body parts flying. He folds his hands behind his back, kicking the dirt with his feet. “Oops?” He walks away, whistling innocently, resuming his search for the way out of ancient China and hopefully, back to his own time. That ancient Chinese proverb rings inside his mind once more. ?May the bird of Paradise shit on your head.?
