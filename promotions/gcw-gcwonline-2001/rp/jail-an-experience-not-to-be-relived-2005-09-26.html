Jail:An Experience Not To Be Relived

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 26 2005 Confined, a word meaning to keep within bounds otherwise restricting. Trapped, a word meaning a confining or undesirable circumstance from which escape or relief is difficult. Although I was confined I felt trapped, you may be thinking well what’s the difference, I felt as though even thought I’d gotten myself into this disaster that somehow, someway I was forced to be here. You may be confused as to why I felt this way because I did indeed my fate, the fact that I was sentenced to nearly life in a federal prison. You may be confused thinking that I was forced here because truly I wasn’t. I was trapped because of the events that you are soon to witness; my mind was trapped into a space in which it wouldn’t release itself from unless I lost it. The daily routine of sitting in my cell, working out, showering with 50 other naked men, eating, sitting in my cell, eating, visitation on some days, and eating one last time, an occasional time of television, and then finally staring at the ceiling of my cell until I could finally fall asleep. The fact of the matter was this process got old, very quickly, I began to wish that I had never committed the crime, it was exactly what the jury wanted me to be doing, grieving and feeling sorry for what I had done. I had a world of regret on my shoulders and it wasn’t passing anytime soon. The visitation was nice; it gave me a sense of freedom, reality at that. Mostly, my brothers came to visit, I was glad I hadn’t been outcasted by them because I sure as hell knew that the rest of my family hated me, it was different with my brothers though because I gave the man Michael Mason what he deserved, I shouldn’t of but I did it was exactly what my brothers wished they could have done themselves and had one of them done it I would of loved them even more than I did. My brothers and I were a tight knit group, I knew they were always constantly attempting to find a way to get me out of the hellhole, and soon they would. Jail, a word meaning a place for the confinement of persons in lawful detention. Escape, meaning to break loose from confinement; get free. It wasn’t true that I was actually going to escape from jail, those brothers that I had, the ones who cared and loved me so much were my escape from this place, seeing them for 1 hour, for 5 minutes was my escape from this place it kept me going. On ward with the actual story behind this though, this story will be possibly the most hectic, crazy, emotional one. The last two of this series will match up to each other equally. The scene opens in a room, a lineup of divided walls one side signifying freedom and one side signifying entrapment, for me at least. My brother sat on one side of the glass wall picking up the phone and looking at me, his eyes red as though he had been crying. I picked up the phone and began to talk to him. “Hey” I said to him softly, I knew how much it disappointed him to see me in here, he never wanted me to be here and I let him down, he was like a father to me after my father died. “Hey, how you been?” Jesse said, Jesse was my eldest brother of the three brothers I had, when he found that my parents died he told us, and he embraced us taking us in as his own, he used the money from my parents business to continue it and support us and he did it just fine. “Its better knowing everyday I’m a step closer to being out of this place, I keep seeing odd things though.” I said to him. The tone of my voice had lifted slightly now that a smile grew on his face; I knew that he was glad to see me. “I know what you mean man, we miss you more than anything bro. What do you mean seeing odd things?” he replied. “Michael Mason.” I said to him. “Michael Mason” He’s dead bro, you killed him remember?” he said back to me, you could tell by his voice that he was slightly confused yet intrigued by what I said to him. “Yes, I don’t know, a ghost or something, the man is haunting me I swear it on my life. Maybe it is just a figment of my imagination something I’ll have to live with the rest of my life because I took his, maybe it left an imprint on me or something, a constant reminder or something I’m not sure.” I said. “Aiden, you have to fight through it, it is only your imagination. If you don’t believe you can conquer this image in your mind, you can get over the fact that this man ruined your life and you ended his.” He said to me but he went to continue. “Aiden, bro, you know that we are all glad that this man’s life was ended he went unpunished until you found him and took him out. We didn’t want you to go to jail we wanted karma to get his ass but it just wasn’t gonna happen I guess, maybe God wanted him to suffer for his actions, going through life knowing that he heartlessly murdered two parents one night in hope of money.” He said. “Time’s up.” Said the Guard standing there behind me. “I’ll G U O, love you bro. One of the twins will be in here next time; he’ll give you the next word unless you figure it out before then. Love you bro.” he said to me quickly and quietly. “Wha...Love you.” I said quickly as the guard rushed me to get up so that he could cuff me once again. I was walked down the long hallway, walking past some of the worst men in this world, I had a reason to kill and some of these guys just killed for the sake of killing. It sometimes disgusted me that I was in this place will cold heartless men that had no motive, they just wanted blood. I wanted blood, but again I’ve said it many times before, another day another story another time. As I walked down this hallway, I thought about the words my brother had left me with. “I’ll G U O.” I whispered quietly to myself. We reached my cell, he threw me in there shutting the door and locking it. I walked over to the bars and he uncuffed me, I then walked over to the bed and laid down staring up at the ceiling wondering about the words my brother had spoke to me. I finally came upon the assumption that I’d have to wait until the second word from one of my brothers came. That would be nearly a week later. Although this is something very off of the topic, it is a necessity to the events that follow the time in jail. So for one moment we will sidetrack from the story of “I’ll G U O” and move on to this one. I spoke of Michael Mason, the man I murdered. I informed all of you that I saw the ghost of Mr. Michael Mason. It was one of those moments whether you aren’t sure whether you are dreaming or if you aren’t. A moment in life that often causes you to confuse reality. It was very much indeed possible that I had been dreaming but everything seemed so real, it was unexplainable but I will go on to explain to you everything that happened in this time that I saw Michael Mason. The series of events began as follows; I awoke from the night and began to look around the dark and cold space of my cell. The facet trickled down and at the moment I peered over at the facet, I saw the face of Michael Mason. He stared directly at me with a look of anger in his eyes, as though he were about to murder me himself. This look instantly made me think of my parent’s and this MUST have been the look he had given them the night he relentlessly murdered them. “Michael?” I spoke softly. “Aiden, you betrayed me. You were family to me, and you literally jabbed that knife into my back. It was a painful experience but not as painful as you will now experience suffering in this hell hole.” He said to me. “You asshole, you should have been here for murdering my parents, you should of MICHAEL MASON!” I began to scream at him. As the guards heard this I could hear them already running down the hallway, my heart began to pound knowing that I was about to receive a brutal beating from the guards and later that day I would receive another from the men inside of the prison, I had disturbed their peace, at this moment I wasn’t quite sure of anything though. I couldn’t believe that Michael Mason was actually standing in the cell with me. “You’re right, little Aiden Jackson used to cry himself to sleep every night because his rich ass daddy and his bitch of a mother died. He was quote unquote robbed of a childhood. Son, I did you a favor by killing that scum from the face of this earth. Hell, you got your vengeance on me didn’t you?” He said to me angrily. “You asshole, You would have never been half the man that my father was, he walked with dignity, if he had to provide himself for our family, he did he built his empire from the ground, you had to murder a man and a woman coldheartedly to get your life on track, you asshole. Michael Mason I’ll Murder You again.” I screamed at him. Just as I screamed these words I felt the presence of others in the cell, I then felt the nightstick striking the back of my head. I hit the floor with a thud, the cold stone floor laid against my face. They then threw me onto my bed and left the cell, I couldn’t speak then I finally blacked out waking up the next morning to the sunrise. Now that you know about something that will be key to this story in the future, I will move along to the “I’ll G O U” mystery. But before we move along, I’d like you to know that that week was the worst of my life, I constantly got the visits from Michael Mason he kept saying the same things, it was followed with the beatings from the guards, and more beatings from some of the inmates. The second meeting was with my brother, Jamison, he is a twin to other brother Parker. These two are the second oldest to the family. The meeting between me and him was really rushed so instead of only one word I got the whole thing and I got information that seemed to please me. “I’ll Get U Out, I don’t have much time, but Jesse has connections at a mental institution and we know with the actions that have happened recently we might be able to get you transferred there. The prison has called us on several occasions telling us that you have been experiencing some events that have come to make them believe that you are not actually sane. Aiden we know you are but this is your shot on getting out of here, we’ll get you out.” Jamison said to me quickly. “Alright, I love you brother, send them good words.” I said to him “Alright, they are already talking about a reevaluation, the judge is going to see you again we already know it. You’ll be out of here quickly.” He said to me as he got up quickly and left the prison. For one moment, I had hope in my life, hope of seeing the light of day once more. The words confinement, trapped, and jail were words that were freed from my mind, they were words no longer a part of my vocabulary. My mind was free from the nightmare that I was living with the man Michael Mason, in which I was walking a thin line of reality and fantasy. Finally, my life had been renewed with the power of Christ. I knew that he had blessed me even though I had taken the life he had put on this earth, the creation of his own, the creation made in his figure. Although I betrayed him he confided in me and he helped me through the toughest year of my life. And with that I leave you this from the Lord himself. “Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight.” ? Proverbs 3:5, 6 Although, I didn’t always trust in the Lord, when I did he did guide me through the straight path with forgiveness. Finally my brothers have found a way for me to be free and I was thankful that I had the brothers I did, powerful ones. Nearly a month later, I was brought up for a reevaluation and they categorized me as a mental patient and issued me to be immediately to High Valley Mental Institution. Any person knows that the dreams I was experiencing were actually quite normal of a person who holds any regret. Think about it, have you ever had a recurring nightmare of something that you regret, you probably have and it probably haunts you to this day. I am in fact haunted by the man called Michael Mason but he will not take me from what is real. I am sane, and although I endure some harsh treatments in the next 2 months, everything will go according to plan and I will find my way to the place known as Core Wrestling. Dear Aiden, The owner of the Mental Institution knows that you are now here in this place, you are going to have to take part in some harsh testing that might make you slightly unstable but you will be out of there in two months max. Max, the owner of the institution will see to it that you are not harmed and that you get out of there safely and in one piece. We just have to make the jail forget all about you before we can get you out of there in a good fashion. Sorry to cut this short but I have arrangements to make; you will be home soon my brother. Love and with all Sincerity, Jesse I, now have a quote of my own? “If you trust in the lord, and find that he does certainly guide you down the right paths, he will cause you to experience life events that will change you forever but they will only benefit you with strength and the ability to cope with problems in your life. The lord will bless you with a party of brothers, blood related or not that will sacrifice their lives for you.” “Aiden Jackson, we have chosen that you are mentally unfit for the facilities presented in front of you therefore we have chosen to suitably fix the situation to your needs, you will be transferred to a maximum security mental institution where you will be cared for there.” The Judge said to me. “Yes your honor, yes yes yes.” I said to the Judge as he spoke to me loudly, acting although I actually was mentally ill. “Thank you Mr. Aiden Jackson, May God have mercy on your soul. You will die someday a sick and cold and lonely man. Aiden Jackson, before that point in your life has been reached I hope that you will realize that your actions were wrong and I hope you gain sanity for that your proper punishment will then be realized.” He said in a slightly firm yet mellow tone.
