Date: Timeless Without question, it eats at me. Blood spilling down my face...a somber realization catching in my throat...I am mortal. Flesh and tendons that fold and snap. I feel pain. No. Pain is too tame a term. What I feel is outright agony every single time I step outside of a wrestling ring. I don't have my pills anymore. I'm clean. I thought this night was made for me. Dangerous Games...yet another showdown in GCW in which I would walk away victorious. And then I would grunt and growl like an animal and scream at the top of my lungs about my superiority. Then crash as the adrenaline wore off. Shadow. One on one, I put the onus on her that she couldn't top me. But I'd be damned if I didn't realize deep down that she could tango with me step for step. She had picked herself up from things that nobody has. Her chest was torn ragged by my knife edge chops but she kept coming. She gave me a concussion. She battered and bruised me like nobody else since Mike Randalls. I'm the bully. I am not used to someone, especially no lithe little lady doing something like that. It was a stark reality. The Angelo Deville's, the Karina Wolfenden's, the Nova's...I guess there was an inherent sense of superiority. That the only thing that separated wins and losses with them was a mistake or two on my own part. But with Shadow...it was different. Equality...knowing that even if I wrestle the perfect match...she could still top me...it scares me. Maybe I am losing my edge. My sense of self confidence going out a window. Maybe this big bad ass invincible power to the people persona is fictitious. Because God knows I certainly don't fucking believe in it. And I do not understand how these legions of fans can possibly cheer me, especially knowing what I have done. The lives I've tried to ruin...the hypocrisy I have touted...the things that I've done. Put your chin up and don't back down from the enemy and you become a hero...even if you are undeserving in your own mind. Let them think what they want. Let them buy my damn merchandise and put more money in my pocket for retirement. The sting of rubbing alcohol touches off an electric reaction in my nerves. Ironically, the issue I have to deal with is simple: I'm used to it. I'm used to the pain, the misery, the sacrifice. I am so goddamn used to jumping on plane after plane and burning the end of my wick just to satisfy the bookings I get. I always say to myself that I am going to wane it down a little bit. I never do. I take more and more. More countries and all that. It all flashes back to me. The toiling I did paying dues. A kid with just a simple dream, without a cent to his goddamn name and a broken down car. I lived out of a vehicle for nearly a year to save money. Worked two jobs, one as a clerk at a gas station and the other as an overnight stock guy at a Walmart. I carried appliances into the area's of the store that they needed to go and made a seventy-five cent shift premium on top of my minimum wage. I've made a dump truck full of money in wrestling but I've spent a landfills worth. I got the nice secluded house in the desert that was a base of operations. State of the art through and through. I had the gas hog Hummer that I drove around the country. I was buying pills and supplying my wife's habits of Tiffany's shopping. Great big suits tailored to fit the contours of my skin with alligator's. I came from my self made nothingness and accomplished my dream. I became a big time wrestler, like the guys I watched growing up on TV. But the feeling is gone. The rush disappeared. 2005 I sold my soul for immortality, and before the year was done the Devil enacted his tab upon me. My wife was gone, my money thinning out, and I couldn't find a place to feed my habit. Winning Dangerous Games...I dunno. I'm angry. I'm pissed off. But there is nothing I can do. Shadow showed me up. I'm on the back burner. Second place is not something to be ashamed of...well...unless you are me. And needing the money...or simply needing that one elusive World Title to finish completely your dreams. Dust yourself off and pick yourself up. It is my creed. Never let them see you cry, always stare into their eyes with fury. I do that with Lia right now. My brain trying to turn it on. Just seeing this group of fucking mongrels celebrating and talking behind my back about my inadequacies... No...you know what? Fuck them. Fuck them all. But I get a different sense from her. Her of all people. My sworn enemy. The rest of them essentially cower in fear of me, turning silent. They're too scared to say anything to my face because even through their smug arrogance they know that I can slice and dice them...even in the state that I am in. None of them have the capability to stare into my eyes. They always turn away, or look somewhere else. Allude to the contact but never truly make it. She quivers and shakes as I growl at her. Turn it up a notch Brandon. Make her back down. Everyone does. They never look you in the eye when they challenge you. They shut up when you look at them. They are all just like everyone else. The bastards that kept me down for six months before GCW gave me the shit offer it did to bring aboard. I took it in desperation. But I still hear the whispers of PTC, calling for my head. I can hear the Chet Worth's of the world laughing at my expense. But in the end they can't even look me face to face. Politics. Addiction. All caused by wrestling. Or rather...because I have become professional wrestling. And for so long I am merely a slave to it's whims. I do not even remember who I am most of the time I've become so enamored with this business. Sport. Business? She doesn't flinch. But I can't help but sense a mirror of lies being reflected to each of us. Lia. Shadow. Brandon. Diamond. We're the same person. I realize that now.
