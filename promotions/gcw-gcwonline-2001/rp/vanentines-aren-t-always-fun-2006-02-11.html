Vanentines aren't always fun.

11 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/06 ?Alright, are we here to be taking pictures or drinking coffee?? What? What did he just say? Josh looked up from his can of hot Georgia? coffee he'd taken out of the vending machine not a few moments before and stared the man down. He looked Japanese, just like everyone else here. But his accent said ?New York? all over it. He always hated it when some producer would start thinking they owned the place or ran the show. ?I'm not part of the crew.? The small man, New Yorker with a lisp actually, backed up a bit when he looked into the green eyes of the half native wrestler. Josh hated east coasters who always thought they needed things done right now. They say people in Seattle are hyped up on caffeine. If they are, then EVERYONE in New York does speed or something. They make Seattle-ittes look placid. ?Then who the hell are you?? Josh smiled. ?I'm one of the bodyguards for your models. Any of your photographers try taking any liberties and it's my job to ensure that they don't leave without a camera loaded suppository.? Josh always loved it when he gave an answer like that. He was the smallest of the two Brand brothers, but he was obviously someone who worked out and had something in the line of talent in the way he moved. He had been trained by the Order's best in the fighting arts, that's how he got to be where he was. And he was feeling pretty good about things, with the exception of being drug out to this place for the events for Dani and Ukyo. It was a distraction, but one that Dani needed. ?Oh...? The guy had no answer for that. It might have been Josh's long duster style coat with the full built in cloak. It might have been his long hair, or just the look of pure carelessness in his eyes, but the small guy with the money turned and left him alone. Just the way he liked it. He turned around and put two one hundred yen coins down the slot of another machine, a row of something like 8 of them were situated along this wall. Quickly a cup dropped down and then filled with steaming hot water right in front of him. He took the bowl of ramen noodles out and put it down onto one of the picnic style tables nearby, grabbing a pair of chopsticks from a holder on the side of the machine. He sat down and started to eat the noodles as he watched the hell go on about him. ?I see you take to this stuff the same way I do.? His brother had almost stuck up upon him. Fortunately for Josh, that really wasn't possible. As long as it had ever been, or currently was, mortal, he knew where it was. He was working on his empathy more and more each day. Those who were close to him were easy to detect. Gray sat down across from him at the table. Gray was a wary type. But then again, when you had his senses, things were rough to. Gray, who has wrestled before under the name of the ?Gray Ghost? lived up to that term. That was a term that the Native Americans had for a creature of the forest that you never knew was there until it was too late. A side that Gray Brand had that not many people knew about. ?What do you have for me big brother?? Josh was to the point. He was the one stuck there while the girls were taking part in this fiasco. Since the photographers had decided to use the very Japanese resort they had decided to stay at, it was pretty messed up. Even if he wanted to, Josh couldn't go back to the little hut that he and Dani were sharing to get some sleep. It just wasn't possible. ?Well, apparently our tryout match impressed them.? Josh sighed. ?I would hope so. Rumor has it that Team Baked had been promised the same thing we were by Core and Global... a shot at the Tag Belts. And we beat their ass. As I understand it, Team Baked were tagged for their next challengers as it was.? Gray nodded his head. The larger man had a worried look upon his face. Josh was picking it up. It wasn't an easy feeling as it is. ?What are we booked for this week?? Gray swallowed a mouthful of spit down before answering. ?Apparently Team Baked aren't the favored anymore. So they've decided to run a gauntlet to decide the number one contendership for the vacant belts this week. We're apparently entering third after The Guild and Team Baked.? ?The Guild, that's the guys who are nerds like you, playing Dungeons and Dorks through their High School Days. Only these guys never got beyond that, right?? Gray smiled. ?Well for one, they're not whale penises. But yes, they're worse then Almasy and his video games. They think they're in an RPG constantly.? Josh nodded. ?We're not the only three teams, are we?? ?... No.? Josh adjusted his seating as he took another bite of the noodles. Gray continued. ?Not giving the order yet, it looks like Ambrosi and Valentine are forming a team here too. They're in it. As well as a team that has Rich Rollins in it.? ?5 Teams only?? A bead of sweat on Gray's forehead told his brother more then he wanted to. ?I'll take that as a no?? ?Almasy was accepted, he gets to pick a partner before the show. No one knows who it is.? ?Is that it?? His brother's uneasy feeling was enough to tell him otherwise. But he wanted him to say it. ?No, there is one more team. However, Almasy being in the match is already hard on us. He's a former member of the Brigade. He's got no partner. But he gets to pick. He could pick up any singles wrestler and they'd end up being his partner. He knows about our honor code. He could pick someone in the KB as it is, and it would be a done deal.? ?We have two singles people. You think he'd pick Blaise?? ?No... That would be an impossibility for this match.? ?You think he'd pick Dani?? ?I think if he asked Blaise to give him some help, Blaise would ask Dani to do it.? Josh suddenly had a previous comment hit him like a ton of bricks. ?Wait, you said that Blaise would be an impossibility for this match? Why? He's...? Gray interrupted him. ?He's already in the match in the fourth seed.? Josh stopped dead in his tracks. ?How?? Josh's head turned as he saw the obvious answer. Standing over near the photographer, still dressed in her normal clothing was another member of their stable. The lavender dyed hair stood out, not to mention the six foot perfectly proportioned frame that it went with. Blaise's girlfriend and their current acting manager, Ukyo Kushinaga. ?She's been cleared by the Order's docs. She's good to go. FutureShock is forming again. That means that Things could get interesting. If Dani were asked to join Almasy, that gives us three chances at being the number one contenders.? ?No it doesn't! It gives us a hard ass fight. You and I wouldn't get a slip through on that.? Gray frowned. ?Did I say you and me? I was talking about the KB:NAN. I wasn't saying that they'd lay down for us. That would violate one of the rules of the Brigade as it is. Any Brigadiers that face each other in competition will not violate any rules of the match and will ensure that the challenge is met and is carried out in a fair fight.? Josh glared as his brother. ?And after everything we've been through over the last year, I'm sick of worrying about others. We went to Core, and they promised us a shot at the Tag Belts. Then when we got close... they killed the tag division. Then Global, we beat the champs in a non title match, got booked to face them. They then hightailed it out of the fed. Instead of giving us what we had earned, they killed the division to restart it AFTER we had left. After we had left there. We're a laughing stock of the federations because we're so fucking gullible. We just beat the highly acclaimed Team Baked, and yeah it secured our contract. But dammit, when are we going to get ours?? Gray shook h is head. ?It isn't always about US, dearest brother. Sometimes it's about the team. Don't get me wrong there. Right now, after what you've proven in that ring. All that you have learned over the last year... I would have it no other way. I'm your partner. We are Unite. But sometimes you have to take everything in stride. We have a chance at this. We've beaten top contenders all over the place now. It's only a little bit of time until we get ours. The really good news is, we have enough teams here now, that there is no chance of them cutting this division out. We got here for the restart that Global didn't even bother to book until we were gone.? Josh nodded. He hadn't been happy at all over the last year. Every time he and his brother got close to getting the recognition they had earned... it had been stripped from them. ?Yeah, you're right. But Ukyo shouldn't be ready. She had her neck almost broken by her brother. It's not time yet.? Gray nodded. ?I can assure you that she's ready. You've got to remember. Not all of us are human around here. Between me and Blaise, it should be enough. But Blaise's other half as well as a few others in the Order. She's healed and ready. So you'd better be ready. We beat the one who wins the first seed match up, and we've got them. And by the rules of the order, if you let our friendship with them get in the way of winning, then you've lost already. Be ready. Be careful too.? Josh smiled at his last comment. ?Me? Careful? You've been listening to too damn many stories.? ?Okay, don't get worked up over anything in the near future?? Josh looked down at his bowl of fast cooling noodles. ?Yeah, nothing but next week. Tuesday to be exact.? Gray didn't show any emotion to that one. He just made a statement. ?At least you have someone to spend it with, so don't knock it. And it's someone that on your own admission looks pretty hot in lingerie. And I know that she and Ukyo have been going shopping. So consider yourself lucky.? Josh's trouble making organ that seems to be inherent to himself kicked in. ?Yeah, could be like Almasy whose wife left him. Or you, who left your wife. Oh, wait. Maybe you could both send roses to the same girl and see who she thanks.? Gray snapped like a dog at him... ?And what's that supposed to mean?? Josh retorted. ?I saw the tapes of the show, Gray. You found your lady love again. The camera even caught you checking our her backside. Not that I blame you for doing so when she's wearing leather pants, but if you also saw the holiday show of Blaise's old fed, you'd see that she seems to have a thing going with Mr. Almasy, also. That sicko has already tried to fight Ambrosi over her womanly lover, you think he'd not take it up with you over who would get Dawn?? Gray swallowed to clear his mouth out again. ?Dawn and I have nothing romantic going on. If she'd rather be with Almasy, that's fine...? ?But..? ?But, nothing! Yes, when given the chance I did check her out. I didn't know who it was. I can admit to many things when it comes to taking a subversive look in her direction. But we're friends, not even really that yet... We find that we can talk to each other. I've shown her a side of me that only my closest friends have ever seen. She's seen what I can become. I've told you that. And she took it very well.? Josh took the shot. ?You know you'd toss one in her if you got the chance, and you know it.? That brought the two brothers face to face, quickly. Gray reached across the table and grabbed his brother by the shirt and pulled him across the table. It's not like Josh, being fully mortal with regular strengths, had any chance to avoid it. ?You could do well, in learning tact my brother. I'm sure that if Dani ever heard you explain your relationship with her, when you're still fumbling over how to ask her hand in marriage without swallowing your own tongue or tripping over your own feet, as tossing it in her. However, you of all people should know, that I would not give anyone an ultimatum of it's either me or no one. It's not right.? Josh struggled with the words. ?But you're in love? Right?? Gray let him go. ?We don't know each other. So I can't call it that. Am I attracted to her? Yes. I can't lie to an empath about that.? Josh fixed his shirt a bit before taking a swig from the can of coffee. ?Gray, there a saying on these types of things. I think even our ancenstors knew this on Father's side.? ?What's that?? Josh just looked his brother in the eyes. ?Nice guys finish last, old Wolf. Nice guys finish last.? Gray shook his head. ?That may be your saying on these matters, Bodaway. But mine is more along the lines of: 'If you love something set if free, if it doesn't come back...' I'm sure you can fill in the rest.? Josh choked on some of the coffee for being called his middle name, the native word for 'Fire', making his nick name of 'Firebrand' even more fitting then most took it to be. Gray continued as he grabbed his jacket from where he'd thrown it down on the table when he walked up. ?I'm off to go work on some stuff with Blaise. Keep the girls taken care of while they're doing this, okay? All these people around here are irritating my senses. Especially the ones who haven't washed in some time. Tell them to go use the bathhouse or something.? At that, Gray turned and left, without any interruption from his brother. Josh looked over and saw the small, traditional, paper house that served as Blaise and Ukyo's residence while they were in Japan. He picked up his makeshift lunch and tossed it away into a waste container. He then walked over to the door, and slid it open, quietly. He walked in and headed towards the back. Not saying anything. At least not until he turned the corner. Standing there, in front of a mirror, doing some make up was the woman he'd come to talk to. However, he didn't know that she'd only be wearing a small black thong when he came around the corner. ?Ukyo? Oh, shit. I’m sorry!? He turned his eyes, as she quickly grabbed what looked like a red vinyl or faux leather jacket, covering her breasts with it. ?What are you doing?? Josh stumbled over his words. ?Well, I'm in need of some help.? ?Okay?? He had turned his back. ?Well, you're a woman and all.? Ukyo looked down at her chest and then back at him. ?I'm glad you noticed, Josh. But you're taken, and so am I. I also have a maturity requirement for whomever I date.? Josh's face turned a bit red at that. It didn't show if the last comment cut into him, or if it was more in the line of the embarassment of finding her like this. ?No, it's more like I need the advice of a woman. Particularly one who's engaged to be married.? Ukyo's face went a bit soft. ?Oh, that type of advice. Let me get dressed and we'll talk. The living room is that way.? He started heading back out towards the main living area. ?Alright.? He found one of the bamboo mats to kneel down upon and wait for her. What will they talk about? That's to be seen at a later time... To be continued...
