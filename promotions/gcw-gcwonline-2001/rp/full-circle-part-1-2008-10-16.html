Full Circle (Part 1)

16 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/16/08 (Takes Place @ High Stakes 2008) High Stakes 2008 4:30pm "It’s do or die time." There comes a time in everyone’s life when that single phrase is uttered, often followed by an uncomfortable silence. For Tessa Windsor, that evening was tonight. She sat straight up, hands folded in her lap, as LeStatt Knight continued his lecture. His eyes remained glued to her every movement, desperately seeking for any sort of sign that she might leave him high and dry. That is, if he allowed her to. "You’ve put up with enough shit in both your personal and your professional life. It’s time that you finally put your foot down and make someone listen. And tonight will be that night." He paced to the right three steps and her eyes deterred to the left just enough so that he could tell exactly what was running through her head. His voice let out a fatherly roar, "DO NOT take his words to heart Tessa, he’s not in this for you. He hasn’t been there once for you since you’ve gotten back, remember that." Still, GCW’s US Champion was in denial about her complete and utter dependence on her fianc’e, Chris Storm. She could tell LeStatt over and over again that she no longer used Chris as a crutch until it made her blue in the face, but it still wouldn’t make it true. She needed to prove it as far as he was concerned. "So, you ready?" He inquired with a raised eyebrow. Without thinking she answered, "I’m game." "Good." LeStatt responded, before the reality of what she just confirmed sat in, "I trust you won’t let me down." "Of course not." Tessa replied, as if the actions she were about to perform were part of her everyday routine. After all, she was ONLY about to screw her fianc? and his best friends of their tag titles. Why should she show any hesitation? Patting Windsor once on the back of the shoulder, Knight had been well aware that Tessa needed that extra bit of support to make this all count, Knight assured Tessa for one final time before making his leave, "You’ll steal the show, trust me. This is going to do you a load of good. I’ll see you in a bit, Tessa." Absolutely nothing was meant by his parting words, but as LeStatt walked out her locker room door Tessa felt as if she were baring the weight of the world on her shoulders. "Great." She whispered to herself as she curled into fetal position on the bench, "I better not screw this one up." Oddly enough even though LeStatt had grabbed the doorknob as he was heading out, Tessa had never heard the door shut. This peaked her curiosity enough to pull her out of her head out of the clouds and see what the reasoning for that was. What she saw didn’t ease her nerves any. "I swear to rod that guy has the biggest hard on for you." You would have sworn Tessa had seen a ghost with how wide her baby blues had grown to be. But it wasn’t a ghost; rather her fianc?, Chris Storm, who had waltzed into her locker room. Talk about the last person she wanted to see right now. Especially given that he had seen LeStatt leave, so he’d no doubt go on a tirade about how Knight was getting into Tessa’s head. That’s what Storm had always done the minute any other guy had crossed her path. "Who doesn’t?" Smoothly a teasing smile crept across her lips giving the impression that she had been completely comfortable with Chris being there. Of course it was quite the contrary. Chris smiled back, plopping himself down as close to Tessa as he could. "Good point. Great thing that I’m as awesome as I am or I’d be walking around like Sir Knight not so Bright trying to pretend like I matter because I won a World Championship ages ago against some forgettable Joe Schmoe." His comment did not elicit the response Chris had hoped it would. Instead, Tessa’s arms folded up against her chest. She stepped in to defend her friend, "He’s bound to be a legend one day. It’s a shame people can’t put aside their petty problems aside and admit that." Obviously Chris was not going to win this battle. So instead of fighting tooth and nail for the answer he wanted, he plopped down beside his fianc’e and shifted to matters that were far less upsetting, "Knight aside, how are you? I haven’t seen since you left the hotel this morning." Small talk. It was an absolute killer. And right about now if felt like that one little question had jabbed Tessa right where it hurt the most about a dozen times. Why did he have to put her through this tonight of all nights? Why? As she normally did when the weight of the world was on her shoulders, she allowed her head to fall onto somebody else’s shoulders, this time it being Storm’s. Only this time the weight grew heavier. Somehow Chris always seemed to know just when the going was getting tough, even if she never uttered so much as a single syllable. "Been keeping busy, that’s all." She finally replied after a brief moment of silence, "I figured it’d be best to get out and do stuff while the kids aren’t around." That was complete and utter bullshit right there, even Chris knew that, but he never bothered to call her on it. There had to have been a damn good reason she was lying to him. He figured she simply left out because her nerves could not handle hanging around the hotel room while he and Markus bounced Knight jokes off one another . It had to have been why she would lie to him, to keep from hurting his feelings. She was always quite sensitive to others emotions. She’d never do or say anything that would hurt another human being. She simply wasn’t capable of being mean. "Good idea." His head nodded along with his words, "I figured you would need a break before you own big match tonight." She half heartedly smiled as she lifted her head back up to where it was supporting itself, "It’s really not that big of a deal. It’s Thom Lestrange. He’s much more arrogant than he is talented." Naturally, Chris had to try to push her buttons, "Ooooo, someone has her claws out tonight. Stay away from me. You might end up gouging my eyes out if I say the wrong thing." Instead of whining back with a response as she normally would, Tessa’s head dropped some. As much as Chris and Markus worked on her nerves, they really were always there for her at times like this. And yet here she was about the drop them like a bad habit. And in a god awful manner at that. Regardless of what anyone said, it really did kill her to have to look him in the eyes and pretend that everything was fine. "I just might." She tried to tease back, but it came out of a half par attempt. The ironic thing was she was speaking the truth. She really was about to do quite the equivalent of all that, for the sole reason of her pride. Yeah, it really sucked for her to have to look him in the eye and play the same old coy Tessa that had disappeared somewhere behind the shadows cast by all her close friends. Rising back up to his feet, Chris patted Tessa on the shoulder and smiled, "Go get “em Tiger. Just be sure to save some of the spunk for me later, ok?" A grin forced it’s way across her lips. Never once did she bother to look back and acknowledge her fianc’e. It was just too hard for have to look him straight in the eyes and promise him something she knew he’d never get, "K." Before leaving he stamped her forehead with a kiss, just as he always did before all her big matches, "See ya later hot stuff." She never bothered to look up and wave before he left. If she was going to do this, she wasn’t going to shatter her heart into a million pieces beforehand. That would totally kill the purpose of breaking out of her own. And that was completely unacceptable. Mark her own words, Tessa Windsor would break out of the shell she’d been entrapped in for so long. Even if it meant costing her own fianc’e the one thing that he treasured almost as much as he did her, his tag team title. It was no longer a matter of what was right and wrong. It was a matter of necessity. And if Tessa ever wanted to make it on her own, it was something that had to be done, regardless of the consequences. -- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- High Stakes 2008 11:30 pm (After the Show) Tonight was an evening that would certainly never be forgotten. It was damn near epic proportions, at least for one person. Back in the locker room of LeStatt Knight there was most certainly a party going on. Having captured the Tag Team championship on his own, there were plenty of reasons for LeStatt to celebrate. Of course, he was not alone in his celebration. Along side him was his mentee Tessa Windsor, who had pretty much been glued to his hip as of late. She too had plenty of reasons to celebrate, though hers were much more sinister than LeStatt’s. "I did it!" She shouted aloud for what was probably the hundredth time since she had turned her back on her fianc’e and his tag team partner earlier that evening. LeStatt grinned from cheek to cheek as he laid an assuring hand on her shoulder, "It was the wisest choice you’ve ever made." The smile that crept across her rose colored lips was not nearly as confident as LeStatt’s. The instant he turned his head the slightest bit away from her direction, Tessa curled her bottom lip in and bit down, a nervous habit she never quite got to shedding. While she nodded her head in agreement, inside her mind was another story. If she had ever bothered to make a list of the all the people who promised her the same thing, chances are it’d be a mile long. They all promised her the world, and in the end all she ever seemed to get in return was a speck of dirt. LeStatt was different though. It was difficult for her to pinpoint whether it was his overwhelming confidence in his abilities that led her in his direction, or if it had something to do with the amount of power he seemed to posses in terms of having people’s respect regardless if they liked him or not. Regardless of what the factor was, there was one rumor that had been validated earlier that evening, it was LeStatt who had finally driven Tessa Windsor away from her so called demons. "So senorita, may I ask what’s next?" Plopping down beside Windsor on the bench propped up against the wall, Knight reeked of confidence. That was a damn good question. What was next? Tessa had never really gotten around to thinking about that part. It was a large enough challenge to finally muster up the courage to take someone else’s hand and step away from what felt like her destiny, supporting her friends on their quest for gold and keeping out of the way. "My US title?" She suggested, clearly clueless as to what the appropriate answer was. Knight shook his head in clear disappointment. Tessa’s head quickly turned in search of something to focus her attention on; anything other than Knight. Clearly he had higher expectations for her than just the US title. At least he had a straight forward response for her that time, "You set your expectation far too low for someone of your caliber." His head continued to shake slightly from side to side as he tightened the grip on her shoulder. The pressure seemed to ooze from his fingertips straight to Tessa’s brain. "I could prove a lot if I held on to this belt long enough." She suggested. The grimace on LeStatt’s face served as evidence that she still had yet to provide him with the answer he was searching for. Clearly there were still plenty of lessons to be learned by the bright eyed, bushy tailed Windsor. "I’m sure that’s what Chris has told you over and over again." This time LeStatt was not going to dance around the issue, he would beat it into Tessa’s head until she repeated his words in her sleep. "After all, that’s exactly how he earned his fame, isn’t it? By beating half par competitors? And now look who has their belt." On command Tessa’s glare directed itself in the direction of the two belts. One would think that after her actions this evening Tessa would beam in pride after costing her fianc? and his best friend those belts. While out there that seemed to be the one thing Tessa was bound and determined to do, to steal the spotlight away from the self proclaimed duo of awesomeness. Back in LeStatt’s locker room it was a completely different story, however. While she reeked of arrogance on the outside, on the inside she was filled with the same old panic and doubt she always had. Right now she would have killed to have someone to fall back on while the going seemed to be getting tough. Falling back on someone was not an option right now, though. Like a true woman, she let her fears sink into the deep pits of her stomach and forced a smile for her admirer, "Good point. But what exactly do you have in mind for me?" Cue LeStatt’s trademark grin. "Don’t play humble with me Tess, you know damn well what I’m in reference to. It’s what you’ve always wanted, isn’t it?" Her baby blue eyes shifted to the side opposite Knight as she answered, "I’ve never really thought about it." Knight was quick to jump to accusations, "You’re lying! I’ve seen the way you look at Vivica whenever you two are hanging out. Every time you see her with that belt, I can see that little glitter of envy in the corner of your eyes. You want people to stop looking at you as a sidekick to the stars. When people mention the best there is, you want your name to be mentioned. You want to be the World Champion. There’s no use in putting on that coy cutesy wutesy act of yours. It’s what everyone wants, and if you don’t there’s no point in you being here." Her defense was about as believable as her lack of desire to win the big one, "Not right now. Vivica deserves her time to shine. Besides, I think I’m proving enough with the run I’m pulling off right now as US champion." Enough had been said. Knight’s glare had shifted from one of sheer cockiness to one that demanded Tessa to fess up, and that’s just what she did, albeit a bit hesitantly, "I guess it would be nice to win the big one though." Obviously he wasn’t getting far with his action. It was time for another approach. "You guess?" He questioned as he slowly lifted himself from the bench, "Then I guess maybe you really don’t need my help. After all, you’ve been treading in the midcard for how long on your own? Obviously you’re doing quite well for yourself." Ouch. Talk about rubbing salt in an open wound. It most certainly opened up Tessa’s ears. Right then, the last thing she could afford was to lose Knight’s assistance. "Wait!" She cried out, jumping off the bench to follow behind him, "You’re right. I do want to win the big one." While it was not visible on the outside, Knight was most certainly smirking on the inside as he knew he’d finally managed to win Windsor’s trust over. While his intentions were far from using Tessa to get ahead like most believed, Knight still was damn proud of what he had managed to accomplish, "I trust you know what you need to do then." Tessa obediently nodded her head. Knight acknowledged her compliance with a smile, "Good. Then I trust you’ll show up here next week with that goal in mind?" Again her head nodded. "Good, then I’ll see you next week." ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- High Stakes 2008 12:30 A.M. "Hey Tessa!" Trudging along with her overstuffed pastel pink gym bag slung over her shoulder, Tessa looked back dreading the worst, "Please god don’t let it be a crazy fan." Thankfully it was only Vivica J. Valentine. Granted it looked as though this would not be any easier of a conversation that it would be had it been a fan instead. Judging by the look in Vivica’s eye, she had plenty of questions for one of her closest friends in the business. "Hey," Tessa began by meekly replying back, "What’s up Vivi?" Never one to dance around the truth, Vivi hit Tessa with an unexpected response, "I think I should be the one asking you that." That right there was exactly why Tessa had been trying to keep a low profile all evening long. She was aware well ahead of time that Vivica would not be approving of any plans that she made that coincided with Knight. Now it was time for the inevitable guilt trip. There was no possible way she could sneak away without it. Tessa’s shoulders bunched up as she began to recite the excuse she had played out in her head all evening long, "They never took me seriously Vivica. No matter what I did, it was all about fun and games to them." Having had to deal with both Markus and Chris plenty of times herself, Vivica understood plenty where Tessa had been coming from there. It did not, however, make her action any more acceptable, "He’s your fianc?, Tessa. You can’t just do that to a man who’s been by your side for as long as he has. If you really needed that much of a break why didn’t you come to me for advice?" Tessa’s shoulders curled further in, "I didn’t want to waste your time. You’ve been busy with autograph sessions, hanging around with Lia, and all of that other stuff. Besides, I’ll be fine. It will do me some good to have some time alone since chances are Chris probably is not going to want to speak with me for a good long while." "You’re not yourself Tessa. I can tell you right now it’s not good different either." "I’m fine, Vivi, really." Tessa assured, starting her path towards her car once again. "Tessa!" Vivica shouted, this time jumping in front of Tessa so that she could no longer dodge Vivica’s bullet, "You turned on your fianc? for Christ’s sake! Don’t tell me that everything is fine. What did Knight do to you? What did he tell you?" "Vivica!" This time it was Tessa shouting back, "At least have enough respect for me to not view me as some kind of mindless zombie that’s being controlled by Knight. We’re just friends! Okay?" Complete and utter silence filled the garage. Neither woman was sure of what to say at a moment like this. They were after all, the closest of friends. It almost seemed pitiful to let something like this drive a wedge between them. Vivica was the first to speak back up, letting out an unnerved sigh before doing so, "Just be careful Tess, okay?" "I’ll be fine, trust me." Tessa assured once more. She resumed her path to the 2008 Nissan Maxima she had rented out for the evening, turning back to Vivica only to ask, "We’re still friends, right?" "Of course." Tessa breathed a giant sigh of relief, "Good." "Tessa." Vivica cued once more before her friend was out of earshot. "Yeah Vivi?" "You know I’m always here if you need to talk." "Yeah, I know." "I’ll see you around. Call me later, ok?" "Okay." Tessa complied, hoping it would finally get Vivica to leave. Thankfully, it did. But being that around GCW nothing ever seemed to happen in privacy, Tessa was then interrupted by someone else. This time it was LeStatt who snuck up on Tessa, a tag belt hung over each of his shoulders. "What did she want?" "Nothing." Tessa shrugged, sliding her hand into the gym bag to grab the keys to her rental. "Good." Of course Knight didn’t believe her, but there was no sense in pressuring Windsor anymore than she had already been tonight, "Don’t let her feed you any BS either. What you did tonight was good. It’s the start of a bright future for you, mark my words." Even under pressure Tessa could not help but to be flattered by LeStatt’s words, "Thanks. Hopefully I’ll live up to words." "Oh you will, trust me." Knights hand slithered over Tessa’s shoulder once more, taking firm grasp as if he were about to shake some sense in to her. Thankfully, he didn’t. Rather he continued to feed her words of support, "I won’t allow it not to." Tessa grinned from cheek to cheek, "Good." Really there was nothing more she could think to say. "I’ll see you next week Ms. Windsor. In the meantime, good luck with all the shit that is going to flung your way this week for sticking with me. I’m sure you’ll need it." Sarcastically Tessa chuckled, "Yeah, thanks for that." Without another words, Knight dismissed himself from Tessa’s presence. Finally, alone in her car with only her own voice to listen to, Tessa let out the biggest sigh she had all evening. Her only remaining thought rolling off her tongue the instant her car started. "This is going to be one hell of a ride."
