"Time waits for no one"

19 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/19/10 Time was merciless. It slowly ticked away from seconds to minutes, minutes to hours, hours to days. It held no sympathy towards the people each passing second hurt, killed or worse. There was never any chance for a do-over. As the saying goes, "You can't get a second back." When you rely on your body to perform, it doesn't matter if it's television, movies or sports entertainment, each second perches itself as more of an enemy than anyone standing across any ring. That must be why, "Time is of the essence." When sports entertainment is your second career after competing professionally in various Mixed Martial Arts tournaments. Then moving onto K-1 and finally ending up inside an octagon some may say you've added years to your actual age. In this case, "Time heals all wounds", doesn't apply. That is exactly where Oliver Ranken stood. No less than five months after his epic title match with Shawn Jessica Hart at Ready, Set, Wrestle he now stared himself down back in Los Angeles. His personal home/dojo/gym had "seen it's better days". His travel bags from his last trip to Florida still sat right on the wooden bench just by the hand free weights. His last wrestling attire still sat neatly folded inside, boots just on top. Ranken's system had, "stood the test of time." Across from them he stood staring into the wall mirror, or mirror wall whichever. He was studying the new wrinkles in his face. A hard realization for anyone is to admit they are getting older. The folding of LoC was almost a guaranteed banishment back into the B-leagues. In this case, "His time seemed to have passed". so you're back for more The Mauler nodded, "What else would you 'ave meh do? Ay've burnt meh bloody arse to ah crisp down in the sonshine state. They folded. Befor' that Aye toiled touring the east coasts less than desirable promotions." exactly, now we've seen you back to the big time "Ay'hm not there yet", Ranken quickly fired back. "An' who said Aye was listenin' tah yoo? In fact the last time Aye even considahed listenin' tah yoo..." it wasn't that bad was it "Perhaps not", he agreed locking his icy gaze on his reflection looking back at him in the mirror, "then again perhaps Ay've taken too many lumps on the ole noggin." is that what you believe "Ay'hm not sure what tah believe right now." His reply was too quick to be untrue, "Aye mean 'onestly Ay'hm ah little old tah be havin' imaginary friends am Aye not?" now you think we're imaginary "aye." that hurts, who's been there for you when you've been down "Noh one." and who's been there when you've needed a boost "Nohbody." seems to me like you have nothing to lose The confrontational Brit snapped back, "Meh last shot at respect." whose respect "You bloody well know", snarled the Mauler, "the fans... the 'istorians... people." the same fans who've time and time again turned their backs on you "W'o else should Aye be lookin' fer approval from then?" Ranken sarcastically snapped back. This was usually the place where the voice went silent. ... "Juss as Aye thought", Ranken smirked, "same as each an everytime... smug little twat of an innah voice yoo are. As soon as Aye ask you ah real question yoo shrivel up an disappear." my approval "Yoo?" Ranken's smirked turned into a full blown laugh, "yor juss the voice in meh 'ead." or maybe i'm apart of you that's never been released... the successful part "Bollocks!" the words echoed angrily off the mirror. THAAAAWHACK The cinderblock like hand of Oliver Ranken explodes a close-by paper towel dispenser into scrap metal. "I'll nevah seek yor approval again." so you do remember me "Git outtah meh bloody 'ead!" Ranken shouted before slamming his head into the other towel dispenser. "Yor nothing tah meh anymor... it's been years since Ay've even whispered yor name." i'm not a boogey man my presence here isn't a curse on you "Ahcourse not, yor jus 'ere tah put the final nail in meh straight jacket." At this point it was the best attempt at humor one could expect from the brit. "If yor not 'ere tah drive meh into full all lunacy then juss why are yoo 'ere?" to bring out the best in you "Ay've 'ad the best trainers, came from ah background ah 'and tah 'and combat." Ranken paused to survey his swollen hands. "Face it, Ay'hm thirty-three years old... if meh time 'asn't already past..." then why even bother Ranken drew in a breath. He knew the answer to the question. In a gruff almost whispered voice, "One more chance." excuse me "One more chance", Ranken said much more sternly. so somewhere in there still beats the heart of a predator "Aye." then i will be here for you one. more. time.
