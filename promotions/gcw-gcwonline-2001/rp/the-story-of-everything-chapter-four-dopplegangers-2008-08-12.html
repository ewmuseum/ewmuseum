The Story Of Everything, Chapter Four: Dopplegangers

12 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.12.08 (AKA ONE DAY THE FUTURE) There was a formless, shifting mass in my doorway, and I was trying pretty successfully to ignore its presence, although I realized I had to leave the bathroom at some point. I could play the waiting game better than most, so I thought I'd roll the dice and take my chances. "Johnny," I heard, and I assume it came from the mass because there was nobody else in the room, and I was damned sure that I didn't say it. Talking was an automatic disqualification according to the official rules of the waiting game. Another victory well earned. Unfortunately, I had become so immersed in the overwhelming excitement of the waiting game that I forgot it was my objective to ignore this frightening, amorphous blob in my doorway until it decided to leave me alone. "Ah! You talked!" I paused and reflected. "Shit." "I'm going to find you, Johnny." "What the hell are you talking about? You found me. I'm right here." I changed directions. "First, who or what the fuck are you, and how did you get into my fucking bathroom?" "I'm your doppleganger, Johnny." "But you look nothing like me," I puzzled. "In my earthly form as a homunculus, I look exactly like you, Johnny. I am just a spirit, Johnny." "Look, you don't have to use my name in every sentence. You and I both know what my name is." "Our name, Johnny." "Right; and also, only a fool would believe in ghosts, so get out of my bathroom." "I will find you, Johnny, and you will die." The figure retreated back out the door, and that was that. "Well," I said, standing up and brushing my hands. "That was ominous." "What was ominous?" A female voice called out from the living room, and I rushed out, and my suspicions were correct. Eryn was there, and she was in the earliest stages of redress, dragging from a cigarette. "Are you talking about the sex? I thought it was amazing." "What sex?" I inquired, my interest perked. "Seriously?" she questioned, turning up her palm towards the ceiling. "It was only like ten minutes ago, Johnny." "It couldn't have been," I calculated. Though I couldn't remember precisely what I was doing ten minutes ago, I was sure it had nothing to do with copulation or I would've remembered it. "Fuck!" "What?" "The doppleganger!" I cried, and it seemed to strike a chord of possibility with Eryn. We looked at each other for a brief moment, but I was unable to read her eyes. "Well, it was still amazing; much better than our first time." She emphasized the word 'first' rather than the word 'our,' which did relax me, if only incrementally. "Well," I shuffled. "Are you ready for another round?" "Not right now; I'm worn out," Eryn rejected. I sat down in the recliner next to the futon she was stretched across, and she put a hand on my knee, noticing the defeat in me. "Don't worry about it, Johnny. It happens all the time. The afterlife is full of dopplegangers." I continued to stare forward. "Oh, if his homunculus finds you, you're going to die," she added. "I don't know what that means," I admitted. "The doppleganger has a living counterpart called a homunculus. It looks exactly like you, but it doesn't have a soul. I'd say about ninety percent of the living are just homunculi, so you've probably seen other people's homunculi before. Once the entirety of the living is populated by homunculi, the dopplegangers can gain control of both the living world and the afterlife and link the two." "That sounds like a real pain in the ass thing to do." "Yeah, your activist buddy Mark David started a group called 'Stopplegangers.' It's about the gayest name a resistance group could have. I ran into him the other day, and he told me he wants to see you about some money you owe him." She took another drag from the cigarette and put it out on the table. "I told him to fuck off." "If you see him again, tell him I'll go talk to him. Maybe there's something he can do for me." "Like what?" Eryn lit another cigarette. "I'm going to kill my homunculus, and maybe Stopplegangers can help me." "That's suicide, Johnny. You'll die if you see your doppleganger." "Who says I'll have to see him?" I had prepared. I thought myself the sly dog at that one. "I'll wear a blindfold." "That's the most retarded thing I've ever heard," Eryn put down while ashing her cigarette. "Hey, I need some money from you to help with Lennon." "Oh, alright," I reached for my checkbook and made it out to 'cash.' "Where is he, anyway?" "Your grandparents are watching them. I met them the other day. They're really nice." "Oh yeah? Send them my regards." "Your grandma is worried that you're not eating enough." "Go figure." I light up a cigarette. "You ready for another go yet?" "Sorry," she frankly replied. "I have a headache." *** By some means or another, I had made my way back to the bathroom. It looked a lot different than the way I remembered it. For starters, the toilet had been removed completely, and instead, there was this monstrous contraption with a seat above an initial chamber full of some sludgy liquid mixture connected by a tube that first curved upward and than back down into a second chamber full of something that resembled water a little more closely. A tube extended up from the second chamber. I saw that a large wooden container was open in my bathtub, and an assembly manual was open on the floor. I picked it up and took a look. "It's all in Dutch or something," I realized. "There's no way I could've put this all together." I dropped the manual back on the ground and turned towards the door, but as I shifted my body, I ended up catching a glimpse of myself in the mirror, and the very sight of my own reflection sparked apprehension in me. I remembered something vaguely about having to blindfold myself and kill the homunculus version of my doppleganger, so I studied my face in the mirror while feeling it through the tangles of beard. I would have to train myself to recognize myself without the luxury of vision. I found a dictionary that I was in the process of composing. It contained all of the words I knew and found necessary for conversation without crowding the language with any words I deemed useless. Phase one of this training required that I record myself reading the entire list of words onto a cassette tape. Overall, there were 3,333 words that I had to record, though I'm sure some of them were repeats, and I listened to the tape on my portable cassette player with my eyes closed. In phase two, I would say a word and then play the tape, and then I would have to guess whether the word came from my mouth or the cassette player. My accuracy was well over 70 percent, which I felt was good enough. I saved the rest of the afternoon for the toughest part of the training, which was learning to operate a samurai sword while completely blindfolded. I pirouetted through the room with what I thought was an unrivaled grace, slashing and cross slashing like it was the seventeenth century. There were several cassette decks placed about my living room, and I had them all playing at once. I recorded several tapes of myself with varying lengths of pre-roll before I started talking. If I could cut through all of the tape players before any of the tapes reached their end, I would consider my training complete. I pressed play on all the tapes, applied my blindfold and unsheathed my sword. I waited. "Aardvark, avalanche, beeswax, beryllium . . ." I took a mighty swing, but I think I only hit a wall. "Boondoggle, butthash, cactus, chrysanthemum, colonoscopy . . ." "Damn it! Where are you?" I turned and swung again, inadvertently lodging the sword into the floor. And then another tape player started going. "Aardvark, avalanche, beeswax, beryllium . . . " "Elephant, endoplasm, fichus, firefighter . . . " "Colonoscopy, dementia, doppleganger, eggplant . . . " I was being attacked on all sides, and it was pure and utter mayhem. I spun in a circular motion with sword outstretched, and part of the ceiling fan hit me in the back. "Shit!" "Aardvark, avalanche, beeswax, beryllium . . . " "Gerrymander, gingersnaps, hallucinogen, hamburger . . . " "Flagellation, furnace, galoshes, gazpacho, gerrymander . . . " "Elephant, endoplasm, fichus firefighter . . . " It was relentless, and it was coming from everywhere. The sons of bitches wouldn't let up, not even for a second. I dove for cover, and I had either ended up under the coffee table or the futon, but I could hear the voices still taunting me. "Flagellation, furnace, galoshes, gazpacho, gerrymander . . . " "Muscles, narcotics, ninja, nomenclature . . ." "Mastication, meadow, meander, mirth . . ." "Insect, lethargy, liquor, lumber . . . " "Meadow, meander, mirth, muscles . . . " All I could do was wait, trapped under some piece of furniture or another. One by one, the voices calmed, and I held tight to my sword. Each popped out of existence with tape fuzz and a mechanical clicking. The last one came to a halt as well. "Xenophobia, xylophone, yeti, zamboni, zigzag, ziggurat, zygote." The last of the play buttons disengaged, I threw off the blindfold and sat defeated on the futon. At least I could enjoy the silence. I couldn't stand the cacophonous illogical arguments of my own competing voices. The voices on the tapes sounded nothing like mine. Gone was my soothing baritone; soothing like ocean sounds or a hot vapor rub. I looked around the room, and curtains were tattered, chunks were missing from walls. Everything was slashed except those damned tape recorders. I made fast work of changing that, and soon enough, sliced tape player parts, cut wires and speaker pieces were strewn about the room in my wake. "I need someone to train me," I admitted. I found half of a phone book underneath the coffee table and began thumbing through it until I found a list of personal trainers. "How personally would they train me?" I wondered, because this was about to get all types of personal, but most specifically, doppleganger personal. I embarked on a series of calls. "Hello, is this Body By Jenny? I'm looking for a personal trainer," I started. "I need someone who can train me to kill without using my eyes." I heard a click. "Hello?" The line was dead. I tried the next one. "Hi, is this Cardio Ricardo? I'm looking for a personal trainer." I continued, "I need someone who can train me how to kill with my eyes closed." Another click. Someone has been selling faulty phones to a lot of naive personal trainers. "Is this Uncensored Mike D'Addendum? I'm looking for a personal trainer," I started. "I need someone who can train me to kill someone without having to ever see them." There was a pause, but there was no click. "I like your guts, kid." "So you'll train me?" "Yeah, I'll train you, but if you're going to train under my roof, you're going to train under my rules." "What are those rules?" "You show up at pier fourteen at midnight, and I'll explain them to you in person." It seemed like a setup, but I agreed. I had set my first appointment with my new personal trainer, and I was anxious to meet him. I didn't gleam much about him through the rest of our phone conversation except that he was willing to train me to kill my doppleganger, he had claimed to have trained people to kill before, and he charged $240 an hour. "How close do you think I can get to finishing my training in an hour?" "About an hour closer, I reckon." I was in for the toughest hour of my life. *** Uncensored Mike D'Addendum was a yeti of a man, and even though he made no attempt to hide his old age, he was still in peak physical condition. My God, he had to be at least eighty-five, and his pecs chiseled through his sweater like a pair of muscular chisels. He wore a tight knit cap to cover his balding head from the lakeshore breeze, and he curled his bottom lip up around a fisherman's pipe as we stood on the empty pier where he specifically requested I meet him. He also wore a cape, which I found particularly peculiar. Uncensored Mike paced back and forth, making sure that I was aware that only he had the right to pace without permission. "Do you really think you have what it takes to kill your doppleganger?" I nodded, examining Uncensored Mike. His skin was leathery, and his voice was leathery. Everything about him was leathery. "Well," he said, still pacing. "Did you bring your sword?" "Check," I said through the cigarette dangling from my mouth while I tapped the sheathed sword that hung at my side. "Do you have your blindfold?" "It's in my pocket," I stated, taping on the pocket opposite my sheath. "Now, killing your doppleganger is no easy task. It's like killing yourself, in a way, and you and your bony knees don't look like your prepared to kill yourself." "I'll kill myself, alright. Let me at me." "If you're ready to kill your doppleganger, someone who looks exactly like you, you've got to prove you can kill at all. You can't rightly kill yourself if you can't even kill someone else." He had a point, but I didn't like where this was going. Unfortunately, I was locked in. I had already paid him, and all payment was non-refundable. "I want you to put on that blindfold, take out that sword and stab old Uncensored Mike right between the ribs." "That doesn't make any sense," I said. "Why would you make me pay you to kill you?" "Because if you don't kill me," he said, revealing a fine cutlass from underneath his cape, "I'm going to kill you." "That wasn't part of the agreement," I gulped. He had already fitted his blindfold over his eyes before drawing his cutlass and holding it towards me. "You got your blindfold on yet?" the old man said, dancing left and bobbing right while stabbing his sword at the air around me. I tried backing up, but he was onto me. He could hear me moving. He could feel the distance between us, and he kept it at bay, knowing that I could still see him. "Put that blindfold on, boy, or I'll put it on for you." I didn't know what that even meant. "I'd like to see you try!" "Maybe I'll just cut those pretty eyeballs of yours right out of their sockets." His argument was convincing. I reached into my pocket and put the blindfold over my eyes. "Now draw your sword," Uncensored Mike commanded. I don't know why he called himself Uncensored Mike. He never said anything too vulgar or objectionable. Maybe they called him Uncensored Mike because there was no need to censor him. I drew my sword and deeply regretted what I figured might have been my last thought. I felt the contact vibrate through my hand as steel met steel. We batted swords, and it was obvious that he was the superior blindfolded swordsman. He quickly backed me up against a wall of a nearby warehouse. "Now hold on, one second," I said, breathing heavily as he pinned me against the wall with his sword against mine. The tension on my sword held steady. "This is all just a dream, right?" He answered with a quick flick of his wrist that sent the point of his sword into my shoulder. "Did you feel that?" he said, backing up and dancing back and forth. I could tell that blood was dripping down my bicep. Enraged, I came out swinging, but I was only cutting the air. The son of a bitch was like a ghost. "I'm over here, Johnny." I turned towards the voice and swung, but I was quickly knocked to the ground with what felt like the handle of his cutlass. I toppled to the ground, and I could hear the man laughing above me. I tried to shield my body, but a kick to the side rolled me over onto my back, and Uncensored Mike was on me fast with his boot dug into my throat. "Any last requests before I cut off your skin and wear it like a Johnny suit?" "Before you kill me, can you at least take your balls off my fist?" "What the --" I aborted the confused question forming at the tip of his tongue with a punch aimed towards where I thought his groin might be. He wailed, and I could tell I had hit my mark close enough. He fell backwards, and I scampered back up to my feet. We circled each other again. "That's it," growled Uncensored Mike. "I'm not going to go easy on you now." He flew at me with cutlass bowed towards me, but I dodged to the right and swung with my sword. He readjusted and blocked, and when he got to his feet he began forcing me to backtrack with a series of blows I was able to block. My skills were sharpening, and I wondered if this was all part of the lesson. Make a man fear for his life, and he'll learn a lot faster. Or maybe it was the cape. Maybe it was encrusted with magic. "Is that all you got, old man?" I spat back. Uncensored Mike laughed, and I felt behind me. It felt like barrels, and then he lunged. I stepped aside, and his cutlass pierced the barrel. I heard something sandy sifting out as Mike grunted and struggled to free his sword from the barrel. It smelled like gunpowder. I charged at him with my sword, and he lifted the barrel to block. My sword wedged in and clanked against his, and we stood there, fighting over the barrel as it emptied gunpowder. It splashed all around us as we tugged and pulled for control of the barrel. As the barrel emptied, the weight of the gunpowder bogged us down less and less. Our struggle was taking up more space, leaving more room for error, and we both lost our grip on the barrel and along with the swords that were stuck in its splintered wood, the barrel flew into the lake. "What now?" I asked, hoping that we'd agree to part ways and never speak of the ridiculous sequence of events that had just unfolded. He laughed, and with a whirl of his cloak, unsheathed a dagger and held it against my throat. "Plan B." "I think you might want to reconsider," I stated. "We're standing on a barrelful of gunpowder, and I'm still smoking. I'll probably drop it if you kill me." "You son of a bitch." "I call it Plan C . . . for cigarette." I smoked American Spirits. They lasted damn near forever. He lowered the knife, but not completely. "If I let you walk away, you'll blow my old bones to smithereens anyway." "Then it looks like my training is complete." I flicked the cigarette into the lake, and he let me walk away. After I had gotten some distance, I heard a terrible explosion behind me. I heard all they found was a skinless corpse, a burnt cloak and a fisherman's pipe. But I didn't see anything. I kept my blindfold on the whole time.
