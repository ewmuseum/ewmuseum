Return of Horror

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ?? This is where The Belmont House of Torture use to lie. Almost a year ago the most menacing structure in England came crashing down. For months, the rubble smoked, left untouched. The citizens of Belmont decided to vote one day during a town meeting on what to do with this mass of destruction. They once feared this house, but it was private property, so its destruction was never once debated. Children ran from here, parents never took Belmont Court to get home. They would drive the long way, just to stay away from this place. Every town has its own haunted house, this was Belmont’s. Parents and children alike know this house of horrors for its atrocities. Belmont spit venom and vomited blood. Elizabeth Bathory, the blood countess, lived here. Years of silence, followed by years of screams when the O’Neils occupied this place. Anton would bring home prostitutes, often torture them, than bury them in his own makeshift cemetery. This area was the ghetto, this was where the police never dare venture into. It was complete anarchy here, and the town knew it. Mothers would sometimes fill out missing persons reports, and when asked where their child was last seen, they mentioned just outside the Belmont Court. The police would nod, and in the back of their minds, closed the case. Since the infamous match with Lestatt Knight the town has filled in the empty foundation with concrete, and hoped the death and fear has left their small old town for good. Belmont’s largest celebrity Bryan “Mayhem” O’Neil has visited this site on numerous occaisions, but the violence never followed him. Now Mayhem stands on the concrete slab, and stares down the hill to the town below. Some houses billowed smoke from the chimney, some has faint light through their windows, while others are blacked out. Belmont is an extremely poor town. Most houses reminded visitors of old mud huts found in the congo. There is not one single house that is a double level. Kitchen, bathroom, often only 1 bedroom, and a center room, or a family room is all these houses consist of. Most families only have 1 working parent, and that parent, usually the father works for chump change at the coal mine 15 miles outside the town. Mayhem stares, and snarls at the town below. He has a hatred for everyone of its citizens. They often scoffed at him, talked behind his back, belittled his whore of a mother, and never once made a friend of him. Human nature teaches us to hate everything we can’t understand. Being a hypocrite, Mayhem hates them as much as they do him. Mayhem has returned here to relive his youth. He has set up a camp just outside the cemetery, and intends to live there as long as it takes. He feels like he has lost his edge, and has decided to come back to the land where he made an impact. Life was cruel for him here, and now life will be cruel for those in his path. Mayhem wishes he could just run through the town, torch in hand and watch them all burn. He wants to watch the faces of the adults as their children’s flesh is lifted from their bones. He wants to hear them all scream in agony. Their agony is his content. Mayhem has become obsessed with fire. It is the only thing on earth where you feed it more oxygen, more life, it can destroy just as much. Most say drowning has to be the most painful death on earth. Mayhem disagrees. Sure you feel your lungs collapse, your body being crushed, but once its done, its painless. Being set afire must be excruciating. The heat overcomes you, you can’t breathe, the flames rip and tear at your body. You feel your flesh melt, your insides start to boil. You flail your burning limbs wildly, but you only fuel the fire even more. It’s the only form of death where you can actually hear yourself die. You hear the pop of heat blisters, you hear the slide of melting flesh, you hear your gaia screams, only to collapse, motionless, paralyzed, but still succumbed with pain. This intrigues Mayhem, he loves fire, he loves torture. Staring back into that town, Mayhem decides the torture must continue. He must live up to the boogeyman moniker he adopted years prior. The people of Belmont will bow to him, and they will fear him. His loss, will be other’s gain. He lost his title, he lost what was dear to him. Now the town of Belmont will gain the fear they once possessed. Ashes to ashes, dust to dust, face the enemy, manic thoughts, Belmont will burn in hell. Mayhem picks up the container of gasoline, the liquid of his new life and begins to dribble it around the outside of the concrete slab. His laugh has become giddy, almost a marijuana style laugh. His brain is being overloaded with thoughts of peril, his heart is swarming with emotion. The Lunatic has returned to the mother ship, and the annihilation of man, the procreation of the wicked has turned a page unto a new chapter of angst. It takes him almost 3-4 minutes to finally circle the slab, and drench it with purity. He pulls out a match, looks at the foundation of his once childhood home, than back into the town. He sparks the tip of the match with his thumbnail, and the blaze begins. He stares into the flame for a brief second, pondering his future, his new life force, and than drops it. Like dominoes stacked up against one another, the flame begins its trek around the slab of concrete. It makes its way all the way around, and standing in the middle of the ring of fire, laughing maniacally is The Lunatic Bryan Mayhem. The citizens of Belmont hear the laugh and begin to congregate outside their homes. It 9:12PM and dusk is beginning to settle. They all look towards the laugh, its on the hill. Mother’s cover their children’s ears and stare at the towering inferno just 1 mile north of them. The laugh continues, and the yelps of the town begin to surface. They didn’t think this could ever happen again, but it has. In shock they watch as a small fireball begins to move down the hill. What the hell is it? Still in disbelief they realize that it’s their monster, he has returned, and is running straight towards them, torch in hand, smile on his face, death in his eyes. Oh God, the horror has come back to Belmont, Oh God the agony?.
