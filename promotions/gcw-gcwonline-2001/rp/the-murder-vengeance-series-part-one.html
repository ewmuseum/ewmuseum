The Murder: Vengeance Series Part One

Date: Unknown Prelude In order to fully understand the man known as Malachi, you are going to have to witness a series of life-changing events. You are going to witness events that include death, a trial, the paranormal, and a man who cries out to the world with emotion and ultimately acts through those emotions. And now, the story begins? “Aiden!” My father said as he called to me from down the stairs. “Yes Sir?” I replied back to him. Now you may be thinking, “Yes Sir?” Hell, I never had to say that to my parents. It is quite different here you see, Aiden and the rest of the Jackson boys would go with punishment if they had not addressed their parents by either sir or ma’am. Jackson parents were both Catholic and raised the boys on it. The Jackson family owned a Jackson Lumber and Mills, near Saint Louis, Missouri. So you could say that they were well off money-wise. The children underwent manner lessons at a young age and were always taught things the appropriate way, hence why they referred to their parents as ma’am and sir. And back to the story? “We are leaving now; Mabry will have dinner on the table at 6. I love you son.” He said to me as I walked down the stairs. “Alright, I love you too. Have a great time.” I replied back to him. The night went as normal as it could have, my brothers and I joked around and wrestled with each other just horse playing around while eventually calming down to watch a movie. Eventually, we received a phone call that would change our lives forever. The phone call that would lead to the demise of one man, this phone call would leave 4 boys orphans. “Hello?” my eldest brother Jesse said into the phone. “Hello, May I speak to the eldest of the Jackson sons?” the mysterious man said into the phone. “This is him speaking, I am Jesse, May I ask who is calling?” Jesse replied. “Hey Jesse, I am Officer Jaymeson. Tonight something terrible happened, your parents were walking out of the Fox Theatre to their car when they were apparently mugged and both were shot.” My brother had become awestruck; finally tears began to stream down his face. My other brothers and I sat there worried and staring intently at my brother. He set the phone down on the hook and stared back at us. “Mom and Dad...” He said to us. “What!”” We all said in unison. “Mom’and Dad are ddd..ead.” He said to us. We all did just as he had nearly 2 minutes before he told us. We sat there for a minute soaking it all up thinking about what our life was going to be like with this happening. Just as Jesse had told us what had happened to our parents Mabry walked in teary eyed and he walked up to each one of us embracing us in his arms. “I will take care of you boys, I assure you of that.” He said to us. [Scene fades] Now you know if you ever have loved someone and that someone has died, you often lose faith in everyone around you; it takes time to mesh the wounds that have punctured your heart. If the case is extreme enough you may even be mad at God for the actions that you feel he has taken on the earth. The anger that I felt after that night is indescribable and there is no way that you can imagine it. I was fifteen at the time of my parents death and questioned God from then on, Mabry raised us until Jesse was 21, I moved in with Jesse and me and him took care of each other while I went to the Church studying to be a Priest and he eventually got married. And now the next chapter unfolds? End Prelude The Murder: Vengeance Part One In order to fully understand the events that will proceed we might have to take a few trips back in time to unlock some answers that are held deep within Malachi’s mind. The only way to fully understand what happened to the Jackson Parents that night was to go back in time and go with them to the show, then walk out with them that night and witness the murder. Of course there is no such thing as a time machine so we cannot go back and stop what was inevitable. Malachi went on in life and studied hard, knowing that his parents were looking down as angels above witnessing his every move being proud of him. The truth was deep inside anger, hate, and vengeance was brewing in Malachi’s mind. You may be now thinking, oh this seems like the typical story, someone in the family dies and as a result it affects the whole family deeply. The truth is this story is beyond that, what you will witness in the next three chapters of this sick, demented book is disturbing and I warn you now, the actions that Malachi takes are not just, they are nearly unforgivable. {Flashback} I was nearly 15 years old the night of the murder, the police identified the killer as Michael Mason, but he eventually “disappeared” and was never found. That night will remain in my heart everyday that goes by, just as slowly as the last’just as my parents will remain in my heart there. “Do you think they made it to heaven?” I said to my brother. “Of course, they were great people Aiden, there is no doubt in my mind that they are looking down on us in heaven right now.” He replied to me. “I see, do you think they will ever find him?” I questioned my brother. “Who knows I hope so, I hope he gets what he deserves. We can’t go searching for him though, shit kid we don’t even know if the guy is still alive anymore. Anyways, if we did go searching for him we’d probably end up killing him in spite of anger and that’s the last thing we need you know?” he said to me. “Yeah.” I replied thinking of how sweet revenge would be. The truth was that 5 years later I would be in a library, supposedly studying for my last test to become a priest, that day I would go, take the test, become a priest and find the man who killed my parents. No I wouldn’t meet him, I would meet his wife, a woman who had no clue what her husband had done 5 years ago. Forward 5 Years Later “Hello, how can I help you sir?” She would say to me in her soft, sweet voice. I looked down at her name tag to identify her properly and looked back up, speechless as I saw the name on that name tag. It read “Georgia Mason”, I instantly freaked out running away to a table and sitting there then walking down the aisles in some sort of panic. “Ma’am, I apologize for my actions earlier. It’s just; it’s just something frightened me.” I would say to her around 5 minutes later. “And what might that be sweetheart?” She replied. “Your name?” I said slowly. “Yes, what about it” It’s not all that uncommon.” She replied. “Is your husband’s name Michael?” I instantly said. “Yes, how did you know that?” She replied. “Good guess, Sorry for bothering you I must get back to my studies.” I said back to her walking away. If you have lost a person in your life once you will understand the hate I had for this man. My life was a wreck because of this man, because he needed money or whatever he needed he had to murder my parents. Every time you think of the person(s) that died you cry, it is like an uncontrollable feeling that you have because of that scar that was left on your heart. I sat at the table, hiding behind the book tears streaming down my face; I was a twenty year old man crying behind a book in a library. I was confused and anger filled hoping to find answers, hoping to see vengeance. I grew into a regular relationship with Mrs. Mason to gain access to Michael Mason. I saw Michael on occasion never acting, until one day I snapped, I couldn’t keep the anger built inside anymore Michael had to pay for the things he did. Now we are going to side track for a bit before I kill Michael. By now you are probably thinking, “Oh My God”, how can you do this? You’ve grown close to Mrs. Mason and you are going to make her suffer for this? Now you are probably wondering why I pulled you aside from me murdering Michael Mason. How was I sure that this was indeed the Michael Mason that killed my parents? Another question you may have been asking. Then again you think Malachi is studying to be a priest what the hell!” Here are the answers for you. In the police report, apparently my dad fought the man off until the man pulled a gun out. My father always carried a knife when he went into Saint Louis, and I wouldn’t blame him the place is quite scary. My father stabbed the man that had slain my mother and father that night. This relationship with Mrs. Mason was not something for the reason that I wanted to be her friend, I wanted revenge I was just waiting for my moment to act, regrettably I didn’t wait long enough, I couldn’t, I felt as though this man could have slipped from my grasps. Mrs. Mason informed me of the scars that Michael had because apparently he had lied to her and said it had happened at work, he was a construction worker. He held the sign when oncoming traffic would drive through a construction zone, there was no way that he could have been stabbed doing that unless some freak accident happened. I couldn’t do what my parents wanted anymore; I couldn’t follow the ways of Christ I had to dispose of this man. And onward with the story, I visited the Mason household for dinner as Mrs. Mason went out to get ice for the ice maker they had in their house; I decided it was time for me to make my move. Why exactly do you ask? This is the first time I had ever been alone with Michael Mason. “I’ll be right back I’m going to grab a soda.” I would say to him as he sat in his recliner intently staring at the television. “Alright Aiden.” He would say back. I walked into the kitchen and pulled out a knife, trembling with fear and hopes that the nightmares of the phone call, the nightmares of not having a life with my parents would go away. I walked into the room, and began to scream in a fit of rage. “Michael, you killed my father.” I said I grew into a devilish figure. “What are you talking about Aiden?” He said to me. I revealed the knife and began to speak some more. “Michael you murdered my parents down by the Fox Theatre my father stabbed you’TAKE YOUR SHIRT OFF!” I screamed at him. Sure enough the scars were revealed there; at this point Michael trembled in fear himself. You have no clue how it feels to take the life of another person, the way that you feel afterwards, the constant reminder that you took a life away from another person just as you had. I was the man that would suffer in this situation. “Please don’t Aiden; I’m sorry for what I did to your parents.” He said to me. “You weren’t sorry for them, you weren’t sorry for me?” I looked at him devilishly. I will not going into the gruesome details of the murder, in the long run I strangled then stabbed Michael Mason 3 times and it was over. I escaped from the house before Mrs. Mason had arrived back to the house. I knew that she would call the cops finding her husband dead and me not there, screaming and crying over his body. The cops would discover the man who murdered my parents and eventually realize that he was a murderer and inform Mrs. Mason of it. Eventually, they would come searching for me. They would indeed find me. I was so unsure of what I had done, it wasn’t revenge at all I felt no better about my parent’s death. I still felt as though I was empty and alone in this world. I turned to the only place I knew, the place that I had just abandoned. I walked into the church going into a confessional. “Dear father, I have committed many sins in my life, those of which I cannot be forgiven, one so unbearable that the lord himself would frown upon me. What I have done my father is wrong and I shall pay for it.” I said softly “Son, the father will forgive any of your actions that you have taken. Trust in Jesus and he will guide you. Please Son; tell me of your sins.” The truth was at this moment I had not been trusting in Jesus and I would pay for it and I would eventually go to jail, just not yet. I began to speak to the father hoping he would provide comfort and guide me through this situation. “Father, I have committed murder. I slain a man out of vengeance tonight, trying to refill my heart with the yearning I had for my parents. I took the man from a family.” I said to him. “Dear Son, leave this place immediately and turn yourself into the authorities, God will justify your punishment. Good luck in your quest my son.” He replied to me. I took his advice running from the church, and to my house. Was I going to fully take his advice and turn myself in? No, because they were already on the hunt for me. They came to my residence and from there it all became a blur. “Mr. Jackson, Please open the door with your hands up!” the officer standing outside the door said to me. I stood there in the hallway, in a state of panic, everything around me moved in slow motion as a SWAT team smashed down the door tackling me and strapping the handcuffs around my wrists. The only thought in my head was “Karma is a bitch” a phrase my brother had once used to inform me that Michael Mason would get what he deserved whether it was when he faced God in the heavens above or whether it was on Earth. I grinned as the men took me into the armed vehicle on my way to the police station. “Karma is a bitch.” I yelled out as I sat in the armored vehicle then I remained quiet for the rest of the time. We arrived at the police station and I was instantly taken into questioning. “Aiden Jackson, Have you committed any crimes before this?” the officer said to me as I looked up at her dazed and confused. “Aiden Jackson! Have you committed any crimes before this?” she repeated. “No Ma’am.” I replied. “Aiden, did you kill Michael Mason tonight?” She said to me firmly. “Yes Ma’am.” I said to her. “Why did you kill Michael Mason?” She questioned. “I killed Michael Mason because he ruined my life; he murdered my parents when I was 14 years old.” I said firmly as I stared into her eyes. She looked at me speechless. She leaned over and began to speak to the guard standing there next to her. The guard instantly walked out of the room and then came back with a file folder. She began to read from the file folder. “Listen, Aiden if you plea guilty to the crime we can get you off on 7 years, with probation and about 2 years of house arrest.” She said to me looking slightly sad. “Okay, I need to speak to my lawyer.” I said just after she said that. Upon me saying this, the large guard walked forward and began to speak with the small woman officer. He then began to speak. “Aiden Jackson, you are being charged with the murder of Michael Mason, you will be tried in a jury of your peers.” the large cop said to me. I was then taken from the room and put into solitary were I sat for what seemed to be days. {Flashback End} You see, there was a point to this story. Should I tell you the reason? No, you should think for yourself and try to formulate my reasons for telling you this story. Perhaps, it was to scare my opponents, perhaps it was to show a small piece of the history of a man known as Aiden Jackson. Maybe it was to send a message to the people of this world. You decide what it was. As you can see here, I show no mercy for any man. I have just begun to unravel the stories of HATE, anger, and ultimately the destruction of one man and the change in this same man. Malachi will undergo a trial, he will witness some sort of paranormal activity that will have a bearing effect on him, he will be transferred to a nut house, and eventually he will wind up where he is at now telling this story to you. I underwent a series of events that taught me the life I need to live. I was taught to let God control the way that people are affected, to let God run his own course and to not disrupt his path because Karma defiantly is a bitch. I’ve come to learn that I have the ability to destroy a man, will I always use it? No, Not necessarily. Will I use it to satisfy a man who helped me out? Of Course. Will I use it for reward of persay’Gold? Of Course. And Now I leave you with this my friends? “For everything there is a season, And a time for every matter under heaven: A time to be born, and a time to die; A time to plant, and a time to pluck up what is planted; A time to kill, and a time to heal; A time to break down, and a time to build up; A time to weep, and a time to laugh; A time to mourn, and a time to dance; A time to throw away stones, and a time to gather stones together; A time to embrace, And a time to refrain from embracing; A time to seek, and a time to lose; A time to keep, and a time to throw away; A time to tear, and a time to sew; A time to keep silence, and a time to speak; A time to love, and a time to hate, A time for war, and a time for peace.” ?
