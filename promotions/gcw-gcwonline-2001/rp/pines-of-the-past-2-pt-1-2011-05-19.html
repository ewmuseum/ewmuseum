Pines of the Past: 2 (pt. 1)

19 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/19/11 The car hissed like a snake, ticking and tapping as it slowly idled outside of the small apartment complex. The air was calm and felt a bit oozy, with lingering molecules of water drifting along endlessly without detection. The driver side door swayed open with force and teetered back and forth slowly with life before being stopped in its place. The exhaust fumes bled out of the pipe, lofting upwards at first until becoming dematerialized into micro matter, minute and undiscerning to the naked eye. Two legs kicked through the wet vapor as a man slowly emerged from the car, without a desire to kill the mechanism of its life, he slammed the door and stepped onto the curb and out before the rattling mobile. He listened on at the engine, which was resonated by its incarceration within the car itself, under the hood to be exact... He looked down slowly, glancing at the concrete which was riddled with weeds and cigarette butts and followed it down as his eyes rubbernecked the curb joint, following it until his grimace swiveled into a slight grin, and then back to a glare. He stuck his foot out and began to saunter his way over to the walkway, which branched off perpendicular to the curbside. His brown, Montrail hiking boots were thick, equipped generously with sound traction and stability with comfort as well. He kicked forward with a heavy load on each foot and found himself looking up at the complex as the morning air sent chills down his spine. From the leather jacket he wore, he retrieved a small cell phone and flipped it open discretely. With his right hand, he began to scroll through his contact list until he found a name which he seemed to fancy, and without hesitation clicked OK to initiate the call. His eyes were raging waves rushing in a large body of water, blue as the skies of our sphere, clouds as the icing in corners. "Hello?," he spoke, pushing his focus onto the large apartment complex. He was still residing in the same location as he had been prior to the phone call. His face flipped with concern, or it may have just been his 'serious' face. Either way, he continued, his lips slowly parting to reveal words, blanketed by the brisk air, enveloping around the unseen but heard words like a spittle larvae resting within its accumulation of 'spit'. "What's you status?" The wind pushed across his face and he snapped his head the other way, shielding his fair skin from the darting arrow of morning's unseen teeth. Those co called teeth continued to nip away at his cheeks, but quickly withdrew as the passing breeze faded away. "Well, I'm here. Can't you just leave?" By his expression, it had seemed like a problem had arose, but it could only be speculated from his tone, which echoed traces of quaintness. "Tell her you're with me, she likes me." His eyes shifted over towards his car, which still ticked and tapped with life, almost to the point where he felt driven to disengage the engine. He felt it was best, and as he spoke to his friend, Tony, he was under the impression that this trip was going to be delayed, but for how long was anyone's guess. If there was one thing that William had come to realize, it was that his friend Tony had chosen a real bitch to marry. In her defense, however, William also noted that Tony was stubborn and could be very impetuous. "Well, you did tell her that you're going on my behalf...because of my father, right?" His foot dragged up and down the joint which divided the individual slaps of concrete. Each drag caused the uprooting of small weeds from their position within the minimal soil depth found within the joints, along with various articles of trash and pebbles. The green juices inside bled out and left their trace behind, slowly leeching out seeking unclaimed concrete to dye green. "I'm not really trying to walk all the way up there, man." His foot worked the plant matter further into the ground as he held the phone to his ear, conversing slowly with a dull look plastered to his mug. He wasn't in the mood for the ear load of drama that was usually dished out by his dear friend, Tony Zichichi. "I've been standing out here for nearly five minutes, and I've been talking to you the whole time. You're getting nothing done, all I hear is bickering between you and Susie. For the last time, hurry up... I'll be waiting." With that, he snapped the phone shut abruptly, leaving Tony at the other end in a fit, most likely. William had his last good grind of the plant carcass that he seemed thoroughly enjoyed with. The air was beginning to calm, and the morning was still young, but the sun was just beginning to preheat the Sphere's of matter which orbited its very existence, the very entity of life itself was focusing in on Earth, and the ball of fire continued to radiate its contents onto William and his black leather coat. The dark surface almost seemed to reach out with hands, embracing the rays of heat and openly accepting them. This was something that William was not very pleased with, so he ceased the process and whipped off his jacket, slinging it over his shoulder as he once again peered over and up at the complex. He furthered in on a door which was perhaps the room of Tony and Susie and let out a grunt, followed by a rather large mass of spit, which he slung from his mouth and outward until it splattered against the cool concrete before him. "Tony, hurry the fuck up." He said to himself, shaking his head and beginning to pace back and forth repeatedly. He shook his head again and dug his hand into his pocket, retrieving the phone with quick, deliberate motions. With all of his rush, and the anger beginning to mount, he dropped the phone to the hard surface below and with wide eyes, watched as it exploded into pieces. It was only three, however, and being it just the battery, phone itself and battery cover, made his brow lower and turned his disappointed look back into rage, as he kneeled before the scraps and reassembled the device. He snapped the final piece back on and quickly dialed Tony. "What are you doing?" He screamed, as he faced his car and had thoughts of leaving. "Um, I'm right behind you." William turned around and sighed. He shut the phone and gave Tony one last look before placing his foot ahead and taking off. He opened the door to the car and Tony followed. "So how far is Oregon?" "Don't talk. Just sit there for five minutes, that's all I ask, OK. "OK." "Now...buckle up bitch, here we go."
