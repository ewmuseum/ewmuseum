Haunted Minds ( Prt 3)

18 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/18/2005 The dream was still fresh in her mind. It was a frustrating experience to be unable to push something out of that perplexed brain. Her arms pumped harder raising the weights into the air as she did horizontal butterflies. The frustration was showing with the exacting movements which ended abruptly each time above her chest. In through the nose, out through the mouth. In through the nose, out through the mouth. One hundred repititions just did not do it for her today, by now she was on one and fifty before finally sitting up and slamming those weights down harshly. ~ CLANG~ 'You can return but you can never go back once you step through the gate...' It was like a haunting voice echoing continously through her brain, a leech that refused to let go even if burned. Grimmacing she flipped a leg over the bench and head to the ski machine. Climbing on fingers danced over the settings before she grabbed the faux ski rods and began to pump away again. Those legs would slide back and forth with a grim determination altering that of her well toned arms. One, two, three, ........ninety nine, one hundred... The count went on, each stroke matching with her heartbeat, a beat that was like an echoing drum to that voice which refused to quiet down. ' It is here that you will meet yourself. An ending is a beginning...' Louder the voice became. His voice still having that rich timbre, which was quickling becoming annoying. Faster she pumped limbs hoping that a hard workout would take her mind off the disturbing dreams. ' Only when you understand beginnings will you conceive endings...' ' DAMN IT!' She screeched hoarsely slamming the off button on her equipment to climb off as she stalked towards the punching bag. Muffled sounds of flesh connecting with leather bag soon echoed throughout the room as she swung at it over and over again all while invisioning that man's face. Backfist, side kick, jab, palm strike....it didn't seem to matter what she struck it with, his face was still wearing that smile mocking her. This workout simply wasn't doing it for her anymore. Nothing seemed to be working and the only thing she knew would work she was hardpressed to do right now. ' God damn you Lexicon.' She would have to break the silence sooner than she thought. Heaving a derisive sigh she glided across the matted floor of her private gym and lifted the portable phone off the wall. For a moment she would merely stare at the buttons allowing them to blur away as if someone had taken a had and swiped it across a chalkboard. Finally she just gritted her teeth and pressed the digits for Johnny's cell phone. Ring...ring...ring... ' Thought you weren't going to call me?' ' I lied. You know I can not resist you for longer sugar.' Ugh, she hated playing this game at times, but at least this time she was doing to it for a reason other than the mark. ' I am not so sure I am ready to see you yet. Besides don't you have Tremendous sniffing at your skirt still?' ' Give me a break Johnny, he is old news. You know it is you I want baby. Let me make it up to you?' ' You expect me to forget it happened Eva? Go back and wipe it from my memory?' ' You can return but you can never go back....' It echoed in her mind once more. ' No Johnny, I don't, but I am asking you to forgive me? Please let me come over and make it up to you.' The phone went silent on the other end. Panic momentarilly seized her heart before he finally made a noise letting her know he was still there. ' You fuck up again and it over. Got it? No more fucking around...you are mine.' ' Yes Johnny I understand.' ' Get your ass over here.' ' On my way.' The phone was shut off and slung back into its cradle as she trotted out of the room heading for the shower. 'Your journey has only just begun...' ' Shut up.' Who was she talking to? No one...just a voice in her head.
