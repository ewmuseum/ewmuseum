Pawn :: Chapter 5

3 May 2008

Date: May 3, 2008 Angry, depressed, and alone, the three most unwanted feelings in the world that could dampen the spirit of one Kyra Macaria, Kaden Calix's lady friend, at any given time, but for the last four months, she had been feeling all of them at the same time. She was mad at Justin Michaels for getting the one reason she was in America taken away from her, and also at Kaden's parents for obviously knowing what was going on the whole time. These hateful thoughts towards multiple people is what caused her to slip into her second depression over the last year, the first coming during the time when Kaden seemed out of control while hiding from his parents, who at the time, were trying to hunt him down for reasons unknown. Then, of course, the hole that was left inside of her, the emptiness of being without anyone else, only aided all the negativity she had been going through. It was on Friday, May Second, Two Thousand Eight, that a small glimpse of light broke through the Berlin Wall that was the proverbial depression, keeping her from being as happy as she deserved to be. The scared little girl was curled up on the couch in the living room of the Lansing, Michigan home that Kaden had purchased for the two of them late last year. Just looking through the windows to the outside world, you could tell it was a beautiful day, one of which Kyra was having no problem ignoring as the same thoughts rolled through her head, much live every day preceding this one. A high pitch jingle echoed through the house, and Kyra sat up on the couch, almost confused, and not expecting the door bell to have been ringing at that exact time. She continued to listen to the silence that followed the ring, almost as if waiting for it to ring again to make sure she wasn't imagining anything. The ring came once more, and the pupils of Kyra's emerald green eyes grew two sizes as she made her way to the front door with haste. Standing on the wrap around front porch was yet another surprise for Kyra, as she was face to face with Lindsay Matthews, the fianc? to the man who took hers away, in a sense. There were no words spoken right away, Kyra's resentment for Justin being taken out on Lindsay at first glance. "What do you want?" With a roll of her eyes, Kyra asked. Lindsay's long blonde hair pulled back into a ponytail revealed the tiny beads of sweat that had balled up on her forehead, and with her hands stuffed into the back pockets of her blue jeans, Lindsay glanced at the ground. "I'm really sorry." Unable to find the right words to say, she said the first thing that came to her mind. "What do you mean you are sorry?" Shaking her head, Kyra questioned back. "Are you and Justin here to pick me up too?" Asking as it would be relief from her misery, Kyra smirked at Lindsay. "You and your conceited, good for nothing, fianc? have already taken just about everything from me, why not take my life away too?" Pushing by Kyra, her eyes still locked at her feet, Lindsay made her way inside the home, and Kyra's face filled with confusion. "Justin isn't with me, and Kyra, rest assured, I had nothing to do with them taking Kaden away." Lindsay admitted, making eye contact with Kyra for the first time. "I'm here to help you out." Smiling, Lindsay confessed the reason to her visit. There were still a hundred questions going through Kyra's head, made obvious by the look still plastered on her face. "Unless you can get Kaden out of that God awful place, so we can try and start living a normal life together for the first time, then I don't see how you being here s going to help me at all." Making her way back to the living room, Kyra stated. Lindsay followed Kyra into the living room, and watched her sit back down on the couch, where she had been just moments earlier, and buried her face into the palms of her hands. "I'm not saying me being here is going to get him out of Southwind Gardens by the end of tonight, but it's a step in the right direction if we are going to get him home." Starting to feel more comfortable around Kyra, Lindsay began to explain. "You see, I love Justin with all my heart, and it takes one Hell of a situation for me to do what I'm doing right now, but I thought it was necessary for me to make him feel like you do as a first step to getting Kaden home." She admitted, and Kyra looked at her with a surprised, yet questioning look. It was almost as if the idea had shocked her, Kyra stood up, and looked at Lindsay dead in the eye. "You just up and left Justin to help a guy you've only known for a couple years, and came here to tell me, someone you've met a handful of times before, about all of this?" With tears forming in her eyes, Kyra questioned once more. "Why didn't you just tell him you were leaving him until he made it right, and just be done with it?" Curiosity in overdrive, Kyra continued to ask. "He would have never taken me seriously if I had just told him I was taking off and staying somewhere at random." Lindsay explained. "I left him a note saying he needed to fix what he messed up, and that if he needed to talk to me, I'd be with my family, and he could do it in person." Tears forming in her eyes over the whole situation, Lindsay continued to explain herself. "So why don't you just go stay with your family?" Kyra quickly questioned back. Letting out a heavy sigh, Lindsay walks over to the couch and sits down. Her attention drawn away from the drama, and she stared outside one of the windows in the living room for a few moments. "I said I was staying with family, because I know Justin won't even attempt to go there to look for me." Looking back at Kyra who had taken a seat at the opposite end of the couch now, Lindsay goes on. "He and my brother haven't ever seen eye to eye, and we've all known each other for years now." She admits. "But then again, if I do show up and say that we are having issues, then I'm just going to cause even more drama that I don't want to deal with." Finishing her statement, Lindsay has to wipe a few tears away from her cheeks. All thoughts of Lindsay being partially at fault had left Kyra's mind at this point as she watches her hold the water works at bay as much as she can. "Are you asking if you can stay here with me?" Kyra asks Lindsay, almost excited that she won't be alone, even more so to the fact that Lindsay wants to help her get Kaden home. But before Lindsay can answer back, a phone starts to ring in the other room, a cell phone to be exact, and Kyra's attention is taken away from Lindsay. She stands up and makes her way out of the living room, through the dining area, and into the kitchen where Kyra's phone lay on the counter top. "Hello?" She answers, and can be heard clear out in the living room. There is a long pause, however, and a heavy gasp comes from the kitchen as Lindsay begins to wonder what’s going on. "Kaden, is that you?" ******* Alec and Mary Calix, after being escorted off the campus of Southwind Gardens last week in their attempt to visit Kaden, had made their way back to Lansing, Michigan, a town in which Kaden would reside outside of the looney bin, but also the hometown of Mary Calix. With nothing better to do than wait everything out, they took the time to grab a bite to eat at a local diner. Sitting in a booth right next to the window, their empty plates sitting in front of them, and their half empty coffee cups held in their right hands, the same thing comes up in conversation that has every day since December of last year. "Money just doesn't seem so important anymore Alec." After taking a sip of her coffee Mary said to her husband. "I know we needed it when we agreed to go through with this, but what were we thinking?" She asked. He shook his head, unsure of how to answer, and he took a sip from his steaming cup as well before sitting the cup down on the table. "If I could go back and start over, don't think I wouldn't, Mary, but a lot of good it does for us to think of being able to do so, we've got a bigger issue to worry about now." Alec confesses, a bit of worry in his deep, crackly voice. "As much as we would like to tell Kaden, or anyone else, we can't, or he may end up in that funny farm for the rest of his life." Setting her cup aside as well, Mary placed her hands in her lap and started to look out of the window. "What about Kyra?" She queries. "Should we go see how she's doing, or fill her in on any of this?" A big gulp followed the question as Mark looked back to Alec, her expression as full of worry as Alec's voice. "I don't think she needs to know about any of this." Looking down at the table top, Alec replies uncertainly. A smile came to Mary's face, and she shook her head. "You don't honestly think she's stupid enough to not already have an idea of what happened, or what's going on, do you?" She questioned to Alec's response. "Though, I'm sure it’s not the best time to drop this bomb on her right now, but we should at least pay her a visit." With a nod of his head, Alec agreed with Mary on that point, and he grabbed the ticket sitting on the edge of the table. ******* "Kyra, that's great and all, and you can tell her I appreciate it, but if I get caught, I'm only going to make this harder on myself." Whispering to his girlfriend on the other end of the phone, Kaden peaks out from behind the receptionist's desk to see if anyone is coming or not. "I love you too; and trust me, I'll be getting out of here soon enough." He said to Kyra before hanging up the phone, and once again, checking to see if anyone would catch him or not. With the coast clear, Kaden made a break for it, running as fast as he could by the double front doors to the institute to make sure nobody outside could see him. He sprinted down the hallways and took corners as fast as he could without spilling to the ground, though, a familiar voice could be heard echoing in the distance, that of Dr. Damian Sorrow. Taking one last corner, this time slightly faster than the other, Kaden slipped and fell flat on his stomach, but quickly recovered and pushed himself back to his feet. It was here, forty feet from his cell, that a young female, along with the steroid looking freak twins, Franklin and Robert Alexander, stood, almost as if being a lookout for Kaden, holding the door open, and hanging onto a large key ring. "Thanks Lia." Kaden said as he dove into his cell, and she quickly closed and locked the door. The tiny sliding door used to serve meals opened, and the young female, whose name must be Lia, peaked into Kaden's room. "No problem Christopher." She said back. "Just let me know if you ever need anything, I'll be happy to help in anyway I can." Admitting with a smile, she gave Kaden a little wink. "Besides, Dr. Sorrow admitted my Steven here a few years back before he lost contact with reality, and that Kyra you talk about, she sounds too nice to lose you the same way I lost him." Finishing what she had to say as quickly as she could, she closed the small sliding door, and jogged off down the hall, the sound of clanking keys fading off into the distance. It wasn't but a few seconds later that the sound of Dr. Sorrow's voice got closer and closer, until finally, he was right outside Kaden's door, and it unlocked. "Has he caused any commotion yet this afternoon you two?" Damian asked the two institute assistants, but there was no answer, at least one that Kaden could see or hear. "Well that is unusual; Seven Twenty-Seven usually starts to fuss around this time in the afternoon about one thing or the other." Curious to the "unusual" behavior by Kaden, Damian says out loud as he opens the door to Kaden's cell, and steps inside. "That is all for now, you two can go on break now." "You know, those two should lighten up some, they don't laugh at any of the jokes I tell 'em." With a smile on his face, Kaden greets Dr. Sorrow not, but goes right into conversation. "So how was your three hour lunch break Doctor?" Sitting up in his bed with his arms crossed, Kaden still wore a cocky smile as he questioned Dr. Sorrow’s long absence. With a shake of his head, Damian ignored Kaden's sarcasm. "I would have you know Seven Twenty-Sev..." Stopping himself mid sentence, Damian refrains from calling Kaden by his patient number, and continues. "Excuse me, Kaden, but I would have you know that I had some urgent matters to attend to at an alternate location." It was Kaden's turn to shake his head this time as he swung his legs out to the right side of the bed, and placed his feet flat on the floor. "As long as you were gone, I sure hope you got the good stuff." Still full of sarcasm, Kaden says, making a joke, one that Damian must not understand as the confused look on his face says it all. "But enough about you, what about me?" Kaden asks. Grabbing the only other object in the cell that could be used to rest on, other than the toilet in the corner, Dr. Sorrow sits down, and places his briefcase on the floor next to him, opening it. Per the norm, he pulls out his clipboard that has several pieces of paper clipped to it, as well as an attached pen that he takes in his right hand. But things took a turn for the worst, and Dr. Sorrow only had one thing on his mind to talk to Kaden about. "So, tell me, who helped you out of your cell today?"
