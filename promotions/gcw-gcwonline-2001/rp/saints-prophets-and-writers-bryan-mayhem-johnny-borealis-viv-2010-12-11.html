Saints, Prophets, and Writers? (Bryan Mayhem, Johnny Borealis, Vivica J. Valentine, and Jay Terror)

11 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/10 *The scene starts in a seemingly random arena parking garage. It’s dark, but well lit with white florescent lights. Tires squeal in the distances as few cars roar to life pulling out their spaces leaving the arena for the night. The camera wonders aimless until coming across two young men talking back and forth standing with their backs to the driver side door of a sliver Cadillac. The first of the two being a young slim man of Latino decent with dreadlocks (Slider), the other a young well built African American with a shock blond for a fade (Eko) stand there and continue their conversation.* Eko: “Oh, man I already told you… I don’t want to hear it.” *Slider reacts with distressed look on his face* Slider: “… and why not?” Eko: “Because I already told you… this big picture… where we are in the grand scheme of things in the universe and who decides it… kind of talk really creeps me out.” Slider: “…but it’s important.” Eko: “I don’t care!” Slider: “How could you not?” Eko: “I just don’t.” Slider: “Well then what do we talk about?” Eko: “I don’t know, anything!” *Slider places his hands his hips tilts his head and raises and eyebrow* Slider: “Anything? I’m trying to have a real conversation about EVERYTHING and the best alterative you could come up with was anything? Everything includes anything.” *Annoyed Eko places his hand in his palm.* Eko: “Well then we’ll just talk about something el-” Slider: “Everything also includes something…” *Slider face slumps lower into his palm* Eko: “Fine we’ll talk about nothing!” Slider: “Everything includes nothing.” *Eko pulls his face from his palm like a mad man and looks in sliders direction. * Eko: “FINE, FINE, FINE, FINE!!! It’s obvious that you don’t care I don’t want to hear about it so just tell me already! Tell me who runs the cosmos or whatever!” *With a brief pause and a grin on his face Slider simply replies.* Slider: ”A writer…” *Eko just stares at him. He opens his mouth to respond but no sound comes out. He darts his eyes to the side… as half a thought begins to forms in his head and he tries to speak on it… but it’s lost and once again he falls silent… this time looking towards his shoes. A moment passes as Eko looks up at Slider simply says.* Eko: “…go on.” Slider: “A writer dictates what we say… what we do and he just types away in his little typewriter… kind of like that movie ‘Delirious’ with ‘John Candy’.” Eko: “Typewriter?” Slider: “Well Laptop if you want to get yuppie.” Eko: “So this writer is writing for everyone?” Slider: “Well I suppose everyone has their own… I’m sure you and I share.” Eko: “… and he’s writing… everything… including now?” Slider: “Ideally yes.” Eko: “… but why?” Slider: “Huh?” Eko: “I mean if he really does control what we say why be so reveling about it?” Slider: “I – I never said he was good.” *Before they continue their conversation the beautiful young blond Sally Ford approaches. She looks baffled and concerned.* Sally: “Huh excuse me gentlemen, what are you doing by my car?” *Slider and Eko turn their attention towards the young lady.* Slider: “I’m sorry Miss Sally Ford; we didn’t mean to startle you. I’m Sylvester Montoya but you can call me Slider and this my associate-” Eko: “Edward Kole, but I go by Eko. Together we’re the Dogtown Saints, and we were sent by our boss to steal a moment of your time.” Sally: “Steal a moment for?” Slider: “An interview…” Sally: “With who?” Eko: “Brad Cruz.” Sally: “The guy who just had a tryout?” Slider: “The one and only.” Sally: “Well couldn’t this wait? I’m really looking forward to calling it a night.” Eko: “No Miss, our boss was quite insistent.” *Sally gives a tired sigh and nods reluctantly.* Sally: “I guess I don’t have a choice.” Slider: “That’s the spirit, right this way Miss.” *Eko and Slider usher the young GCW interviewer back towards the arena entrance.* Eko: “On the way, Maybe Sly can tell you about his higher power theory.” *The Scene fades and transitions into a locker room where a young lady in a red blazer miniskirt combo with long silky black hair. She is packing her carryon when there is a rap at the door. She quickly tucks away the last of her odds and ends before rushing to the door. She composes herself and her outfit before she proceeds to the door. She opens the door for GCW interviewer Sally Ford and a cameraman who is already filming. Slider and Eko stand to the side of her.* Crystal: “Sally Ford, I’m Crystal Robbins. Bradley is looking forward to meeting with you.” *Crystal looks toward Eko and Slider.* Crystal: “I see that you two aren’t completely useless, nice job.” Eko: “Thanks?” *Crystal hands them the carryon she was packing moments before.* Crystal: “Get the car ready.” *Slider and Eko nod as they turn their attention to Sally.* Eko: “Pleasure meeting you Miss” *Sally shakes hands with Eko and then Slider. They both then proceed to walk away. Slider glances back* Slider: “Good luck… you’ll need it.” *Sally watches as they walk away and disappear around the corner with concern regarding Slider’s parting sentiment.* Crystal: “Well let’s not keep you waiting any longer.” *Crystal ushers Sally into the dressing room where a man sits on a sofa facing the other directing watching a large LED TV. From behind all that is noticeable is his trademark shock of blond hair. The man shuts off the television and proceeds to stand up.* Crystal: “Sally Ford I would like to introduce to you ‘The SoCal Prophet’ Brad Cruz.” *The man just stands there with his back to Sally and Crystal.* Crystal: “Well I’ll give you two some privacy.” *Crystal leaves the room closing the door behind her leaving Sally, the cameraman, and Brad alone. There’s an awkward silence until Sally decides to speak up. Sally reaches into her coat pocket and pulls a wireless microphone and clicks it on.* Sally: “Well Brad, maybe we would should get started… would you like to introduce yourself to the fans… say little bit about yourself?” *Bradley seems to ignore her words.* Brad: “The reason I chose you Miss Ford is because you have a certain aesthetic appeal and you’re the only interviewer from California, which makes you bearable… so with no further delay, on to my message.” *Brad draws closer to her making Sally very uneasy, he leans forwards closer to the microphone and simply and begins to speak.* Brad: “This message is to all the GCW wrestlers, officials, and fans… Global Championship Wrestling is a federation that has had a long life… that has compiled lists and lists of memorable moments… of event… of heroes …of villains …of champions … and of LEGENDS. It’s quite remarkable; minstrels should sing your praises … but unfortunately as of this moment all your accomplishments don’t mean A DAMN THING!” *Cruz’s smirk fades as he continues his rant.* Brad: “…because the moment I set foot in this building the bar of excellence was raised tenfold. You see, what you considered in the B.C., Before Cruz as great is just mediocre by standards set in the A.C., Anno Cruzini which is Latin for ‘In the year of our Cruz’. …but fear ye not for I ‘The SoCal Prophet’…‘The Golden Cruzsader’… ‘The Silver Screen Seraphim’ am willing to become the beast of burden and carry the ruins of this crumbling promoting on my back and lift it to heights of success the likes of which you have never seen before.” *Cruz walks away from Sally and approaches the coffee table picking up a mug and takes a drink from it. Sally waits for him to continue but he doesn’t.* Sally: “Was that it?” *Lowers his drink just long enough to say.* Brad: “No, GCW… you’re welcome.” *Sally Becomes livid she tries to hold it back but all her natural filters give away all at once.* Sally: “You’re telling me that you sent your goons to scare the living daylights out of me, so you could keep me from my warm hotel room bed and then just insult me and GCW?” *Bradley lowers his drink taken back by Sally’s fortitude. He tries to respond.* Brad: “H-” Sally: “Where do you come off? This company is great and it doesn’t need your elitist mouth spewing or your delusional demeanor of grandeur. You may have had success in other companies, but when it comes to GCW you haven’t done a damn thing, and need I remind you they have yet to sign your contract, so whether or not you even get a chance is highly suspect. This company has done fine before you and will survive not having you.” Brad: “Survive without me? Have you seen your last few champions? Your hardcore champion Brian Mayhem looks like deranged off color Raggedy Andy Doll, your Television Champion Johnny Borealis looks like Tom Cruise with a post apocalyptic Justin Bieber hair cut in sex offender sunglasses, your former United States Champion Vivian Valentine looks like a possessed Avril Lavigne who judging by her dye job fell asleep in a puddle of peroxide, and then there’s your World Champion!” *Brad looks nostalgic as he shakes his head* Brad: “You know I go back in my mind pulling from the vast archive of wrestling knowledge I have, and I think of all the greats that never won a world title and then I look at Jay Terror and I realize the only thing that was holding all those greats back was apparently basic hygiene. Seriously he looks like something the Plumber would charge 70 bucks to plunge out my sink. I mean I’m just glad he decided to cover himself up with a bandana… seriously I bet there’s only shame and a total abomination due to lack modern dentistry under there. ” *Cruz draws closer to Sally Ford* Brad: “You say I don’t even have a contract. That’s only a formality that will be rectified at any moment and when it is… I will become the shot adrenalin this company needs, and I will carry it to greatness as easily as I ‘Cruz through life!’ because I’m O.C. ‘Original Cruz!’” *Cruz aggressively points to the door * Brad: “Now leave, both of you!” *More then happy to leave Sally Ford quickly rushes out the room with her cameraman slamming the door behind her. Cruz just stares at the door a moment as a small smirk forms at the corner of his mouth. Scene fades to black. *
