Date: 11-05-05 Going Home - Part I Before I get started a little disclaimer for you: This seriers of RPs in particular deals with “AL’s” and that means “Alternative Lifestyles” meaning gay and lesbian relationships(Yaoi and Yuri for Japanese faithful) and if that makes you uncomfortanble then stop here please otherwise don’t IM me telling me I offended you with the content cos this is your warning NOW! Still reading? Good it means I can get started now. Shinoku, Japan. Sound different enough for ya? Good cos this is where we are going to be for a little while to kind of ease you into the world of one Joshua L. Curtis. Now I know you all must be thinking *I thought he lived in New York City?* and you would be right but Japan is his birthplace and he never forgets that by visiting during the months of the American Thanksgiving and Christmas because he has friends and family there that “get it” about him that aren’t wrestlers or anything of the sort. They are people that help Joshua keep the stress level down by communicating with him when he’s off via e-mail and IM and yes for those Zack Johnosn fans out there Zack knows about the two young men that Joshua talks to and knows that Joshua developed a small harmless crush on one of them at one time. Now today in Shinoku its a pretty bad day weatherwise but not bad enough to where Joshua couldn’t wear jeans, a t-shirt and oh yeah some British Knights! The reason for this visit is simple: Joshua wanted to do some promoting on his own without the whore of an owner in Steven Caldera telling him where to be and when to do it. He wasn’t a slave to the system and he wanted to make sure that Caldera knew that by making this trip. So where did he go in Shinoku? Well his good friend Ranmaru Samejia had invited him to come for a little “retreat” from the American media and he KNEW that no press would bother HIM around there. If anything Ran was the media darling there so he got to “lay low” for a little while and that was something he enjoyed thoroughly! As Joshua drove his Dodge Viper through the streets of the city to the parking garage at the complex there were some people that recognized him and waved and Joshua did his part to politely wave back but believe me when I tell you it wasn’t because of his stature in GCW...no in this country Joshua was pretty much looked at as a GOD in Wrestling and people that didn’t know him were basically stupid to the world there. As Joshua got his things out of the car’s back seat a cionversation was going on about 2 floors up in 17-C “Think he’ll really come?” The one young man asked as he moved a pawn on the chess board up two spaces to start the chess match that he and his opponent started. “I know Joshua Kei and he’ll be here. He likes it here too much to not want to come see us because he knows when he’s here he can just escape.” The other man said and at that time there was a knock at the door and the blonde stood up and walked over to the door and looked through the opening that the chain would allow to see who it was and boy was he surprised! As Joshua peaked through the opening he cleared his throat and spoke in his most gentlemanly of tones. Is there a Ranmaru Samejia in? I have a package for him from America.” Joshua said and Ran quickly unhooked the chain and practically threw his arms around Joshua! “JOSHUA! OH MY! Kei come here you were right!!” Ran exclaimed and Kei smiled as their good friend stood at the door with a couple of bags which Ran helped with as well as Kei and that made him blush a little bit. “Come on guy you KNOW you don’t have to do that right?” Joshua asked and Ran blushed as he put the duffle bag down that he had in his hand. “Know it...Yes, but I feel obligated to do it as someone whom is happy to have you in our presence again. How’s America treating my favorite assistant from University days?” Ran asked and Joshua didn’t look too pleased to talk about it and they both got the hint so Kei changed the subject. “You and Zack still together” Last I heard you and him were serious but Zack was going to clubs looking right?” Kei asked and Josua KNEW that was a rumor and he addressed it. “Darling Zack never did that. It was something that American papers printed because they had a slow day and wanted to break me and him up.but it didn’t work and they feel REALLY dumb now!” Joshua told them both as he sniffed the air “Ramen Noodles?” “I see you haven’t lost your observation skills Joshua” Ran started “We were just about to have some when you came. I made enough to feed like 5 people so I hope you’re hungry.” Ran said and Joshua laughed before nodding and explaining that he hadn’t eaten on the plane and the trio enjoyed some Ramen and Joshua brought an “AMP” Energy Drink over for his drink so that he didn’t even THINK about alcohol! Has Joshua ever done that? Nope and he wants to keep it that way! As the dinner wore on the conversation was light and Joshua asked only once about Ran’s injury which his good friend kindly answered and told him that believe it or not it was almost healed and he wouldn’t mind Curtis giving him a match at his specialty which was Kendo. Now Joshua is a complete virgin to the sport of Kendo. Oh sure he watched it once or twice but as far as having a match that’s a negative and it was somewhat evident in the manner that Joshua fought with the Kendo stick the first time but then something happened when they went at it a second time: Joshua outmaneuvered, outwitted and outlasted Ran in a 45 minute epic that had his lover Kei Enjyoji glued to his seat watching the fight and by observation noticed that Joshua had become a lot quicker from his training in a wrestling ring, which is something you don’t normally see. “You’ve trained before like this haven’t you Joshua?” Asked a breathless Ranmaru and Joshua just smiled as he took off his mask and sat cross-legged on the wooden floor. “Surprisingly I haven’t but I’ve been watching matches that you had and matches with Kai to know things I can and can’t do. Its always a treat watching those matches and I wouldn’t mind facing Sagano sometime.” Joshua admitted and Kei nearly spit out his tea! “You’re kidding right Josh” Kai is only the number one ranked Kendo star in the country and you want to face him?! Do you have a death wish?!” Kei asked and Joshua quickly waved off the concern not as arrogance but rather as a matter of wanting to really intensify his training while he was home. “Being the underdog is something I pride myself on Kei. I like the challenge of facing someone better than I am and if/when I beat them its two things: A notch on my belt and proof that on any given day I can face someone whom is the top of the class and beat him or her on any given day and two its busts there ego a little bit.” Joshua told Kei and the man acknowledged that he had been beaten in that argument. The rest of the night was spent just talking and having fun and oh yeah Joshua still had his old room at the apartment and he enjoyed it immensely...not saying that he doesn’t like being around Zack because he loves that man but sometimes its good to have a little peace in your life and he knew he’d get it here. Ran and Kei respected Curtis and both knew that Joshua wouldn’t want to hear them if he wasx on the computer typing in his blog so when they went to bed they really went to bed and Joshua didn’t have to hear anything for the whole night.... Its good to be home again!
