Just a Smile: Finale

26 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 26th, 2007 Arriving at the hospital, things quickly became quite hectic. Getting Tessa out of the car, nurses and other emergency workers were there to help her out of the car and into a wheelchair. Rushing into the hospital, Tessa barely had time to know what was going on. Workers came up to me, wondering who I was and holding me from following her. I must admit that I hadn’t planned on it being this crazy here. It was not within my plans to get separated from her. I tried to catch up with her but it seemed I would be needed for information while the doctors sedated her. One nurse asked me who I was. "I’m the father!" I cried out, trying to move past her. "What’s wrong with Tessa is everything alright?" At this announcement they allowed me to move past the lobby and pointed me in the direction they wheeled Tessa. Though her cries for "Scott" helped me follow her much more than the directions did. Catching up with her wheelchair, Tessa looked over her shoulder to me when she noticed my presences. "Where is Scott?" she asked me with just as much worry in her voice as panic. "He’s not coming" I told her, helping them to push her down the hall, "he doesn’t seem to care." Little did I know that a few miles away at the jail Markus Stone was bailing out his long-time friend and partner, Scott Drake. It didn’t need to go without saying that Scott was quite livid with me. I do believe it most likely involved ripping off my head, hallowing it out, and stuffing the rest of my remains in it. Though even with the urge for my blood boiling in his veins, concern for Tessa most likely was his chief thought. "Push!" the doctor ordered, "push!" as nurses and other medical staff gathered around Tessa. The moment of truth was finally here, the baby would not be held back any longer. Tessa’s grunts and shouts filled the air, slamming against each other and bouncing off the walls. Sweat drenched her skewed face as she did her best to follow the doctor’s simple orders. Then, piercing through the screams was the cries of a newborn girl, taking in her first glimpse of light. Then there was that moment of silence, when the mother was united with her child for the first time out of the womb. I stood back and smiled as I watched the expressions on Tessa’s face. For the first time in days she seemed truly happy again. But those moments could only last for so long before the baby needed to be moved into its own room and the mother given some time to rest. I’m sure if she could, Tessa would have held onto her baby forever. It was hers, her first though she had cared for four other children. But now she had one of her own blood, from her own womb. Watching the nurse carry the baby out, I gave one last look to Tessa before I slipped out of the room behind the nurse to follow her. My plan had reached the next step and I couldn’t waste time simply admiring Tessa from afar. The timing on my exit couldn’t have been any better either, as I noticed Scott down the hall and heading toward Tessa’s room. Most likely there was a very upset desk clerk downstairs who had just gotten an earful from Drake when he arrived with Markus Stone at the hospital from the jail. I could hear Tessa’s surprise to see Scott from even in the hallway. She thought he had left her, yet there he was right in front of her. But he had little time to explain everything to her now. He needed to know where the baby was, where the children were, and most importantly, "Where is Rob?" "I don’t know" Tessa told him, still trying to grasp at the situation, "he brought me to the hospital because you weren’t around. But then he left a few minutes ago. Why, what has happened?" Panic started to set in, "is the baby alright?" By the time Scott had bolted out of the room, I had already slipped around the corner and started to disappear down the hall and after the nurse that had carried off the baby. He had left Markus there to protect Tessa, like I would be so foolish as to return so quickly. It would take Scott much longer to actually find the room where they kept the newborns than normal in his state of heightened panic. I easily used that to bide my time until I was sure the nurse had left Tessa’s child alone with all the other infants. "Excuse me" someone said over my shoulder. My heart sank. My gut rolled into knots. Sweat formed on my knuckles. Turning my head ever so slowly, I glanced over to notice a nurse standing politely near by me. "Can I help you?" she asked with an honest smile across her pink lips. I smiled back, pointing to the newborn cooing in its crib, "my first child. She’s beautiful." The woman in turn looked with me as we admired the infant as it looked around its strange new world. I once again turned to her, "do you think it would be too much trouble if I showed her off to my parents, just for a bit?" Coxing her with a smile, I laughed "they have been dying to see their grand-baby." Taking a quick look around, the nurse gently opened the door so as to not disturb any of the infants in the room. Nodding her head for me to follow, I made my way into the nursery with her and watched as she scooped up the child in her arms, cradling it. She turned on her heels gracefully and presented the baby to me like a crown to a king. Scooping the child into my own arms, I held it against my chest and stared dead into its crystal blue eyes. She had her mother’s eyes. With the warmth of the smile from the nurse as my blanket, I slipped out of the room. True to my calculations, it didn’t take long before Scott arrived at the nursery following my exit. His screams were likely to be heard from all corners of the hospital as he realized his daughter, who he had yet to meet, was already gone. "What in the blue hell do you mean she’s with her father?" Scott screamed, "I’m her father!" The poor nurse, who had been so kind to me, now had to suffer from Scott Drake’s verbal abuse like he was going mute. "What kind of bullshit is this, did you even fucking ask for an ID?" The woman didn’t know which way to turn, which way to even blink. Scott stayed right in her face. I shouldn’t have hung around to listen to his verbal berating, though, as Scott looked down the hallway and happened to spot me. With the child in my arms, I couldn’t very well run off and endanger the baby. It was a part of Tessa, and in a sense a part of me. Thunder-footing his way over to me, Scott was more than ready to bash my head in with his bare hands. Let him think that though, as he failed to realize I hold all the cards. Stopping a few feet short of me, he stared at the infant in my arms, realizing that she was his daughter. "Rob! I don’t know what the fuck is going through that sick little mind of yours, but I swear if you touch her in any way I’ll smear those brains all along the walls" he shouted, doing his best too look threatening. Scoffing, I held the child secure to me. Though I didn’t expect Scott to get out of jail so soon, I can’t say I hadn’t thought it would come down to this. I knew that at some point it would come down to me versus him. The situation just came sooner than I would have liked. But I planned for this. As a man of the mind, I knew not to rely on something as simple as Justin or something as overly complicated as a legal system. I would be the one to bring him down; I would do it with my bare hands. "So Scott, here we are intertwined by fate of a woman. You may be a physical match for me, but know that I am a much greater mental threat to you, and thus shall be your down fall." That pompous fool didn’t appreciate the speech I had been working on, rather he shook his head. "No, this is you handing over my daughter to avoid severe pain." From behind me I could hear a multitude of footsteps. Turning my head slightly, I noticed a pair of cops coming in from the other end of the hallway. Trapped, I focused my attention back on Drake. It looked like I would be denied my one on one confrontation. Time for some plan B action. Pulling out a small handgun, I pointed it directly at Scott’s chest. "You may be stronger, but I’m smarter!" "I’d suggest replacing smart with crazy and you most certainly are." I was surprised. Even looking down the barrel of a gun, he managed to keep his sharp tongue. But I fear I’m giving him too much credit. He’s most likely too stupid to realize the situation he found himself in. "Look around Rob, you are surrounded by cops. What do you even hope to accomplish? A lifetime in jail?" In a bold move, Scott moved slightly closer to me, though kept a soft foot about it. "Look at them; they are ready to gun you down if you blink the wrong way." Again he stepped forward, continuing to gore me into a conflict. But I stayed sturdy in my way, holding the gun. "Come on, what the hell is the plan anyways? Murder me and kidnap my daughter? Are you that gay that you want a piece of me so badly?" His insults tried to wound me, but I wouldn’t allow it. He didn’t know me. He didn’t know what he was talking about. He just wanted to raise my ire. "Man, you might as well put that gun down. If I die, who’s going to give it to Tessa at night? Surely not your limp dick self. The last thing that gave you an erection was the Baywatch calendar featuring David Hasselhoff." Against my will, my body started to shake. "What the hell do you know?" I shouted. "You know nothing of the love I have for Tessa! You don’t even deserve to be in the same room as her! You treat her like shit when she deserves a man who would love her and cherish her like I could." My hand rose up to bring the gun directly into Scott’s face. Blood pumped through my veins at an accelerated pace. Never in my life had I ever been so angry. Sliding into the scene, Scott Drake’s friend Markus Stone showed up. "Rob! Thank goodness you have the baby!" Both Scott and I turned to look at him, trying to see what he was talking about. "Tessa’s wanted to see her baby but the nurses couldn’t find her!" He kept trying to leave, motioning for me to follow him, but I stayed in my place. Markus looked to see that I hadn’t budged yet. "Come on man, what are you waiting for? She sent me personally to go find you and the baby. She’s been calling out for both of you." "Look, I can see you are busy with the whole?" he stopped and examined the situation around us, "thing. So I’ll go ahead and take the baby to Tessa and tell her you are on your way." I’m not as stupid as they would like to believe. The infant is my leverage in this situation. Without her, all is for naught. Still, Markus refused to give up his claims. "I’m serious man, she’s stressing out. They told her that they can’t find her baby" he said. "How do you think she took that?" Without waiting he added in, "nuts! She went absolutely nuts. I could barely understand her between the sobs." Markus started to come near me, his arms out for the baby. I whipped my gun from Scott and pointed it at him. That caused Markus to stop, who held up his arms in defense. "Look, she sent me to get her child for her. She’s scared man. She’s scared for her child. And besides that, it looks like you have some shit to handle with Scott. You two need to deal with that." My eyes casted their hateful glance back over to Drake, who helplessly stood back as a bystander. "I do need to finish my business with him" I said as I turned the gun back to Scott. With my attention elsewhere, Markus Stone was able to surprise me by taking the infant from my arms without me being able to stop him. Cradling the baby in his arms, he used one free hand to wave to me, "thanks man, I’ll tell Tessa you are following me" and he disappeared past the police officers. The loss of the child was bad enough, but it was even worst when I turned back around. WHACK! It felt like a brick wall had hit me right in the face. But I knew better. It wasn’t a brick wall; it was Scott’s fist coming at me faster than ever before. I don’t think he’s ever thrown a punch that hard in his life, and I don’t doubt he would agree with that. In a matter of seconds the cops were on me and had the gun out of my grasp. They pinned me to the floor, screaming and shouting. My head bounced off the cold hospital ground and in that moment it was like some evil demon had been exercised from my soul. As they pulled me back to my feet, my hands cuffed behind my back, I looked over to Scott with all the shame in my eyes. "Why?" I asked him, causing a confused look across his face. "Why do you smile the way you do to Tessa?" I think Scott figured out what had just transpired over the past few days as he looked back at me and gave that smile he so often reserved only for when his eyes fell on Tessa. "If you could only see the way she loves me, then maybe you would understand why I feel this way about our love, and what I must do. If you could only see how blue her eyes can be when she says; when she says she loves me." Love. What an odd term. How is it that such a powerful word can have all the meaning in the world, yet be so empty and painful? It can drive a man insane or inspire a man to greatness. It can ruin a nation and build a future. Love? Is it even real or something we created within ourselves to try and give our lives meaning? Love. Because of love it looks like I’ll have a long time to think about the word; a very long time.
