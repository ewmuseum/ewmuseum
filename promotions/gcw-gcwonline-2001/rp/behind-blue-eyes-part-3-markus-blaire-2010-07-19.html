Behind Blue Eyes (Part 3: Markus Blaire)

19 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/19/10 Memories are a funny thing. Some people cherish them eternally. Others would do anything to try and forget. Sometimes I wish I could take control of my own subconscious and pick and choose the memories I'd like to keep and the ones that would go away. Unfortunately lately its been mostly the latter. It's not something I'm proud of but something I have to do. I do it because victory is the closest thing I have to respect anymore. In professional wrestling I can beat respect into people in the ring. They may not like me, but I can make them believe I'm the greatest fighter to ever walk into this business. I was born for this. There's something about proving people wrong that's always brought a bigger smile to my face. It makes me want something more. I'm a like a child when you tell me I can't have something or that something is impossible, because proving you wrong is the first thing I want to do. Even if the people in those seats don't like me they know every night I'm going to show them something they always thought wasn't possible. I've made a career out of shocking the world. Even after all the pain and suffering I've been through in that ring, I know someday it's going to be the real world that's going to be the end of me. It's hard to turn off the switch once it's been turned on when you leave that arena. Going into the real world I still have to wear that plastic smile while everyone tries to burn me down. My friends. My family. Journalists. All of them want their chance to bury me alive under the sins of my recent past. No matter what I do I can't stop them... and I can't keep them from eating away at me. Forced to fight back like a bird asked to fly with clipped wings. I just don't know if I could ever make anyone happy ever again. At a point where I've grown accustomed to everyone stepping up to the plate to take their shot, for some reason I never saw this coming. After everything I did... putting my life in danger to ensure his existence, I never imagined he would still turn his back on me. Lie to the world like I never meant anything. At one point in my life he was my entire world. I placed him up on a pedestal like a martyr for my sins. He was supposed to be better than this, but I guess even the cynical ones like me can be taken by surprise sometimes. In a world full of lies and deceit, my fondest memories are all I have left. The only time I'm happy is when I'm replaying those memories in my dreams. Heh... what's that say about me? BEHIND BLUE EYES (An Exploratory Narrative Into The Life Of A Former Phenom) Part 4: Markus Blaire ---- The room reeked of production. Fake bookshelves. A prop desk. The American flag. Dark, natural tones. The set couldn't have been more stereotypical if they were attempting to shoot a parody. His mind wandered as the crew barked out instructions. Never being one to much care for the rules and regulations of society, Markus Blaire tended to march to the beat of his own drum. Some people called him a freak, but on television they'd state he had an "eccentric personality." His biggest fans would claim that's the way all the brilliant literary minds worked. Staring across at a middle aged woman in a business suit, he couldn't help but wonder how much he was getting paid for this. The way his agent was pushing, it must be a lot. While the technicians latched a microphone to his black denim jacket, he stood uncomfortably biting his lip as the television lights flashed on. Looking away from the camera he took a seat once the wiring was finished. "Is everything alright?" She asked while she took a seat, the technicians scattered as everything was in place. He didn't respond, knowing full well he wasn't contractually obligated to acknowledge anything at all until that camera started to roll. "You are okay with doing this, aren't you?" He only responded with a nod before taking a long drink of water, trying his best to keep the cup steady as the countdown started. FOUR. THREE. TWO. ONE. "This is Jessie McCoy with a rare sit-down interview with world renowned author Markus Blaire. His only book thus far, entitled 'When The Levee Breaks', was the best selling fictional novel in the past five years. An espionage drama that captivated the world is currently in production to be full length feature film." She pauses for a moment, smiling into the camera, "Tonight he's here to announce his next book, the much anticipated sequel to 'When The Levee Breaks.' Which will be called?" Looking at Markus, Jessie watches as he hesitates. Opening his mouth but nothing comes out. What appears on camera as choosing his words wisely in reality translates into something much, much more. He's lost the ability to speak. "It's called 'Run Like Hell'." He finally managed to pronounce, listening as a collective sigh of relief was made by the crew. "So we're continuing the trend of well known classic rock titles I see." She stated with a smile, "What can your fans expect from your upcoming novel. More of the same espionage thriller?" "Not quite." Markus responded while running his thumb in circles around the point of his index finger, "It's not so much a thriller as it is an action-packed love story. The story of David, the main character from the previous novel, and Julie, the woman he grew close to at the end of the last novel are together living in the consequences of their actions. Running from a far more powerful network of professionals than they ever expected, while meanwhile experiencing the truth that love and relationships bring." "I'm assuming we're not talking about butterflies and rainbows here." She responded. "Sometimes? Yes. Mostly no." Markus adjusted himself in this chair before fixing the oversized sunglasses that were falling down his nose, "Love is beautiful and love is deadly. Love can make or break a man. That woman who you used to worship as a Goddess sometimes turns out to be a succubus." "So I'm to understand things don't turn out happily ever after?" "That depends on your definition of happily ever after." Markus leans back in his chair a bit, "I may feel happiness is different by definition than you do." "Well do they find happiness together?" She digs deeper. "They may, they may not. I have novels I need to sell." Markus responds, "In my personal life, being the way I am, I sometimes feel true happiness is being content with who you are. Without having to live your life in a way to appease a person, a society, or an ideal." "So is Julie a controlling character?" "Julie has a strong personality, very emotional." Markus nibbles a bit on his lower lip before rubbing his hands together, "She doesn't take no for an answer. She never gives up. She won't allow you to tell her something is impossible." "She's a dreamer." "Not particularly." Markus hesitates, "How do I put this... she's stubborn, because sometimes things are in fact impossible but she refuses to concede to that. She'll, for instance, change the rules to meet an expectation if the expectation can't be met. She very much manipulates the situation around her will." "So do you believe she's devious?" "Depends on the person." Markus explains, "Some people view her as very devious, I'm sure. Perception is..." "Is it fair to say Julie is violent?" Jessie interrupts, clearly with an agenda in mind. "Well she's a professional killer in the first novel, I think that's a fair assessment of anyone trained in that line of work. Studies show it does tend to bleed into their lives outside of that line of work as well. A recent study from Harvard actually shows that..." "Well how do you perceive the character? Do you draw from any personal experience when dealing with certain character archetypes?" "Of course," Markus hesitates, suddenly feeling even more uncomfortable in his own body, "I think the backbone of any good piece of literature is life experiences." "I mean, not to jump to any conclusions Markus but it seems clear to me at least that Julie and your ex-girlfriend Vivica Valentine have a lot in common. Physical features, attitude, and even preferences in music and clothing styles." "Again, you tend to draw from life even if you try not to. The will of the writer is a direct result of life experiences. If there is a similarity between Miss Valentine and Julie, it's a natural progression." "So did you know Vivica to be a stubborn woman?" "Well, this isn't really about my personal life." An uncomfortable laugh escapes his lips as he readjusts in his own seat before running his hand through his thick, black hair. "It's about a piece of literature." "What is your relationship with Vivica Valentine?" She pushes the subject, "Are you still good friends?" "Miss Valentine and I no longer speak." "Why is that?" "I simply don't agree with some of her personal beliefs and ideals. We're very different people." "Would you say she's violent?" Jessie asks abruptly, watching Markus' reaction as he looks off screen like he's trying to hide from the question itself, "Would she hit you?" "I have no further comments about Miss Valentine or my personal life." "But don't you deserve closure?" "This interview is over, thank you for your time." Markus states as he jumps up to his feet and pulls the wiring off. Jessie looks over at the crew as her subject quickly turns his back and marches off set. Running both hands through his ravenous long hair, he trembles, feeling the eyes of the world squarely upon his back. His teeth grind as he kicks the exit door open and disappears from sight. A return to sanctuary, far away from the public eye. Just as he liked it. ---- You would think a girl like me would be used to knives, but it wasn't the knife sinking between my shoulder blades that hurt. It was who pushed it into me. Our lives have gone different ways and we don't exactly speak any more, but that doesn't mean I don't care... ---- "Again, you tend to draw from life even if you try not to. The will of the writer is a direct result of life experiences. If there is a similarity between Miss Valentine and Julie, it's a natural progression." "So did you know Vivica to be a stubborn woman?" Watching all by herself in the middle of a hotel lobby, she tapped a pen against her legs, which were propped up on he coffee table in front of her. Even though she was in fact all by herself, she could almost feel the glares of the hotel staff around her as if they were hanging on every word as much as she was. Waiting and watching for a reaction. "Well, this isn't really about my personal life. It's about a piece of literature." "What is your relationship with Vivica Valentine? Are you still good friends?" "Miss Valentine and I no longer speak." Sticking the pen in-between her lips, she bit down before placing her hands together. Even though she'd read every quote that was made, she was still in shock watching the words leave Markus' mouth. It was definitely him. Nervous twitch and all. Perhaps it was just something she didn't want to believe. "Why is that?" "I simply don't agree with some of her personal beliefs and ideals. We're very different people." Resting her forehead on her hands, she almost couldn't watch anymore. She knew what was coming next, closing her eyes she hoped by not watching that those words wouldn't leave Jessie McCoy's lips... but she knew the media better than most. You give them an inch and they'll take a mile. It wasn't even Jessie's fault in the end, Markus practically lobbed her the questions like a softball. "Would you say she's violent? Would she hit you?" "I have no further comments about Miss Valentine or my personal life." CLICK. Running her hands through her hair, she was thankful to hear the sound of the television shutting off. Spitting out the pen, she watched as it rolled across the coffee table and took a deep breath. Counting backwards from 100, she put her hands behind her head and opened her eyes. She was still alone, but couldn't shake that feeling that she was still being watch... "Miss Valentine?" Those words almost made her jump thirty feet into the air. Turning around she recognized the man as Jeff Umberger, part of the travel staff that arranged caravans and sleeping arrangements for every stop of the GCW tour. Still catching her breath a little bit from the shock, she tried to compose herself. She had a reputation to uphold. Even though they used to call her the Fearless Phenom, deep down inside she still felt as if she had to live up to the expectation. "What?" Vivica asked, taking a deep breath and trying to remain calm. Her cerulean eyes couldn't help but wander back to the television as she spoke, curious as to if his face was going to appear again. "We can't hold off any longer, we have to leave now if we want to make our next flight." As he spoke, she knew he was right. Japan was a nightmare. If you thought it was hard navigating the streets, then you've obviously never been inside the airport. Trying to keep a schedule in Tokyo especially was a mess. "Your bags are already on the bus." "Are they gone?" She asked curiously, her eyes navigating towards the window shielded by blinds. "No." He answered, "There's more." "Shit." She muttered under her breath. "It's not pretty out there Viv. They're really out in full force for this one. It's nothing you're not used to, right? Media blitzes. NC-17 lead ups. You've been in the main event twice, this kind of thing should be second nature to you." "Not the same." Vivica took a step forward with a little hesitation, "Ever after I knocked Murray's teeth down his throat those people didn't want to kill me. They just wanted to watch me lose." "Well I suppose beating up a wrestler is one thing, but beating up an author is completely different." The man realized how stupid his comment was as soon as it left his lips, resisting the urge to drop his jaw and cover his mouth with his hands. Well, at least he didn't cover his mouth with his hands. "Excuse me?" She stated. "Well that is if you did it..." He laughed nervous and took a step forward, Vivica followed realizing they just couldn't wait another moment, no matter how stupid his comment might have been. "...you didn't, did you?" "Keep walking or you'll find out." Vivica snarled between her teeth as they moved along. Approaching the doors, the rumbling that inside the lobby sounded like a train passing by grew louder and louder... like a tsunami of conversation as they came to a stop. Looking back at Vivica, Jeff knew that behind that door was a pack of wolves. His hand reached out but didn't grab the handle. He grew a bit pale waiting for her to give him the signal. With a nod, the floodgates opened. The approached tsunami crashed around them as the floors shook. A stampede of Japanese media quickly surrounded them like wolves on the kill. Using his smallish height to his advantage, Jeff burroughed under the invasion of photographers, news cameras, and microphones as she looked around at the spectacle in awe. It seemed like only yesterday when the mob would rise to their feet for a glimpse of The Fearless Phenom, worshipping the ground she walked on as she graced them with her presence. Those times were long gone. The worshippers replaced with vultures, circling the skies before descending down upon their not-so-helpless prey. Closing her eyes she lowered her head and tried to force her way behind Jeff in his path. Turning and squeezing between walls and walls of humanity, she was constantly smacked in the mouth by microphones and had cameras documenting every agonizing inch of her journey back to the bus. She knew how dangerous it was to live in the past, but if she could just go back in time and never try to save Markus from the never ending hell he endured. If she just could've watched the embers of the place he used to call home rise through the air and not question the integrity of the flames, she'd be trotting to the bus right now with a cargo bag over her shoulder. She still wouldn't be liked, but she'd travel in peace. And so would he. He'd probably be dead. With one last shove she grabbed onto the handle inside the door of the bus, pulling her body through the mob with every ounce of strength she had before the doors slammed shut behind her. Quickly coming up the stairs, she looked down the center of the charter bus as every pair of eyes were focused squarely on her. She could see their accusing stares, labeling her as an abusive woman when she walked past. The last seat was one that she was going to have to herself, falling down into it she looked out of her window to see the heathens still trying to get one last shot through the heavily tinted glass to no avail. Even with the cries and screams for answers that were happening outside of the bus, it was the whispers that she heard inside the bus that rang the loudest. She couldn't go on living like this. This isn't how things were supposed to go. It all needed to end. Now. ---- It just means that we're both human, something that I know you've always questioned within yourself. Sometimes humans fall apart from each other for unexplainable reasons. I'm sorry that you don't seem to cherish the same memories that I do. ---- The tranquil hallways of Zenith Manor seemed like a far cry from the same world that brought the stampede of press towards her in Tokyo. Her sneakers were the only shoes in the house that didn't announce her presence with a click on marble flooring. It was a silence that brought a smile across her face as she glared down the hall towards the frosted glass door, lined with silver and a Z emblem embedded within a circle. "You have to do it..." She whispered to herself as she took another step forward. Desperate times call for desperate measures, and nothing is quite as desperate as making a deal with the devil. Behind that door lies the beast himself, Christian Zenith. Philanthropist. Billionaire. Business and engineering prodigy. Narcissistic asshole extraordinaire. Putting her hand against the door, she couldn't help but think of the last time she was in this building before she came back home. That last she saw this door, she was running away. Far, far away with Markus Blaire... to live happily ever after. "That depends on your definition of happily ever after." His voice rang through her head as she pushed the door open. As quietly as possible, she slid her tiny figure into the door before closing it gently behind her. "Lacrymosa" filled her ears as she peered down towards the far corner of the office, where Christian Zenith sat in front of a chessboard. They were all still in place as started towards him, he never as much as lifted his head to acknowledge her presence. "Some people say chess is the ultimate game of skill and intellect." He speaks, still not lifting his head. Grey eyes staring back at himself in the glass chessboard. The pieces are of the finest quality, made of silver and gold. Vivica examined the board as her stomach felt like it was lifting up into her throat. She knew this man for so long, it shouldn't be so difficult to speak with him... but she's also known him long enough to know she was in the presence of evil. While there were multiple deeds she could reflect back upon to establish that theory, the fact of the matter is that was her first impression of the man so long ago. There had always been something so imposing, yet fascinating about the young man's presence. Your gut was screaming at you, letting you know you were in the company of a very dangerous man... but your mind was in awe. There was something about Christian that made you want to study him, learn the ways that made him tick. "Sit." With the instruction, Vivica slowly pulled out the chair across from him, trying her best not to screech it across the expensive flooring before gently sitting. Looking down at the pieces in front of her, it certainly wouldn't be her first chess game... but it had been years since she sat down at this very board. She was sitting across from a man she could never beat. "I disagree." He stated, looking up at his company for the first time. "I never said anything." Vivica looked back confused. "Chess is not the ultimate game of skill and intellect." Christian spoke again, getting his point across before going on, "That would be life." "We've had this conversation." Vivica answered, "Life is not a game." "But it is." He responded, "Just do as I say." "I didn't come here to play a game of che..." "White to e4." He interrupted, looking up at the woman once committed to be his bride. It seemed like almost a lifetime ago as she slid his piece across the board. "Black to e5." Hesitant she placed her hand on the pawn, pressing it forward she began to move it before stopping. "This is just stupid Chris..." "Black to e5." Glaring back at him, she almost defiantly moves the pawn forward in frustration. "I inadvertently introduce you to Lia Ambrosi. She is someone who I would one day consider my friend, and you would one day consider your mentor." "What are you talking abou..." "White Queen to h5." Christian cuts her off again, moving his Queen out from behind the pawn wall. "Black Knight to c6." Doing as he says, she takes a second to remember where c6 is on the chess board before picking up the only knight that can get there and laying him down in the correct spot. "Markus Blaire introduces himself to you on your first day of your new promotion in Zenith Corporation. I go the other direction, thinking nothing of it." "I think I know how this stor..." "White Bishop to c6." He moves his bishop out through the opening in the wall of pawns, expressionless. "Black Knight to f6." Moving the piece, she now expects the explanation. "Lia Ambrosi and Markus Blaire meet each other. You learn of his sob story. His abduction on the battle field. The experiments he was put through in our labs in order to turn him into the perfect soldier." "You tortured him." "I tried to turn him into a God." Christian doesn't even look at Valentine as he responds to her accusations. "I know you're smart enough to know better than that." "He failed because of you." Christian stated, "When you came along he suddenly wanted to leave. He wanted to move into a house with a picket fence, settle down with the cute blonde girl and start a family. Someday maybe have little freak children running around the house, writing terrible books for the ignorant public to eat up as fiction." "He's allowed to choose his own life, Chris." "But he was not allowed to choose my wife." "I'm allowed to choose my life, too." Vivica argues, "I was never your property." "White Queen takes Black Pawn on f7." Christian moves the piece to the desired spot, taking off the pawn and sitting off to the side of the board before flicking over the black king. "Checkmate." "Scholar's Mate." Vivica said out loud. "You remember." Zenith looked back at her, "How fitting was it that after he stole you from me, you turned around and stole him from me, too." "You locked him up like an animal." Vivica defended her cause. "He was a solider." Christian spoke, "If he was not willing to fulfill his obligation, he should have never signed on the dotted line." "He was just a kid." "He was an idiot." Christian responded. "No..." Vivica interjected, "He's a liar. A thief. An asshole." "And.." Christian finished, "He is a deserted of the United States Military. That is why you came, is it not?" For some reason she wanted to scream yes from a mountain top, but she just couldn't force the words out between her lips. She knew the second she did, that Christian would have her just where he wanted her to be. Asking him for help. She knew it was what she wanted to do, but just the thought of asking him for help, after everything he's put her through... "No need to say anything." Christian spoke, getting up from his chair, "I saw the interview. He burned you just like he burned me. He lied to them. The last thing in the world you are is a manipulative, abusive harlot." The thought of that brought a smirk across the lips of The Leader Of Men. "Oh, and have I watched you try..." "Shut your mouth Christian." Vivica defended herself, "Don't talk down to me like I'm one of your employees, I don't take orders from you anymore. You treat me with respect or you don't get what you want." "And what would that be?" "I know where he is." Vivica replied, halting the pacing Zenith in his tracks, "I know where he writes. Where he sleeps. Where he shops for groceries. I know everything. The man you could never find, and I've known all along." "Well, well, well..." Christian smiles looking back at the Bulletproof Blonde, reaching over the chess board and picking the Black King back up and placing him upright in his starting position "Let us talk business then, shall we?" ---- I've made mistakes in the past and I've tried my best to cleanse the blood from my hands. I've seen and done horrific things that haunt my dreams... ---- "It's a good thing I'm not scared of the dark..." she spoke to herself, holding only a flashlight in hand as she marched through the dense summer fog. Crunching her way up the hill, downed limbs and twigs snapped under her feet as she peered into the mist to see a dim yellow light distorted in front of her. She felt her heart jump as she flipped off the light in her hand, her steps became slower and shorter. Taking a long sigh, she looked down at her hands. They were trembling. "Pull yourself together, Viv." Mumbling to herself she closed her eyes and lifted her hand up into the air. She was cut out to do a lot of things in life, naturally gifted to be an athletic young girl... but this wasn't one of them. She turns her head before she slams her shaking fist into the large wooden door. It was too late to turn back now. Listening inside she heard movement. Her heart races, pumping harder and harder inside her chest as she turns her head and attempts to look down the hill. Tears began to well up in her eyes as the footsteps inside go closer and closer. Her shaking hands became shaking fists. Biting her bottom lip, her nostrils flared as the door swung open. "Hello..." He spoke before looking into the cerulean blue eyes of his ex-fiancé. Besides the brilliant shades of blue, they were pink and bloodshot. Rubbed raw around the eyelids, obviously filled with several sleepless nights full of distraught. "Why did you do it?" "What are you doing here..." "Why the FUCK did you do it, Markus?" With the words she invited herself in, pushing past him and entering the cabin. Staying off the grid, the cabin was currently lit by seven candles placed strategically around the room. He said nothing as he closed the door behind him. Pacing back and forth, Vivica couldn't make eye contact with him. "I'm not good at this kind of stuff..." Markus stated. "Obviously." Vivica responded, "What in the fuck were you thinking?" "I was thinking I had to answer her questions do I didn't look like an idiot on national television." "So instead you made me look like a psychotic, abusive, violent killer?!" Vivica screams back at him, "How could you?" "Oh, so you care all of a sudden?" "I've always cared, Markus." Vivica retorts, "Just because we aren't lovers doesn't mean I don't CARE." "Maybe you should've showed it sometime. A phone call once every... I don't know, year? That would be more than I've got. I haven't seen you since I was in rehab over a year ago! The only reason I knew you were alive is because I'd see you on television when I was in town. What happened to you? You're not the girl I saw on TV two weeks ago." "People change." Vivica responds, "I have to do things I'm not proud of every now and again to keep my job. I can't just put out a book burning bridges once every three or four years and make millions." "It's fiction!" "BULLSHIT IT'S FICTION, MARKUS!" Vivica screams back at him, "You can't pull that shit on me! I was there. Those are OUR memories inside those books. Our memories that you just feel like you can change on a whim to make me out to be whatever you want me to be so you can sleep better at night." "I'm trying to sell books." "You're selling my identity!" Vivica says through the tears obstructing her vision, "Why can't you see that?!" "I was running out of money, I have nothing else Vivica." He looks down at the ground, trying to compose his thoughts... circling his thumb around the tip of his index finger. "I didn't think it mattered." "Because you weren't the one getting hurt, you selfish bastard. You never made any attempt to tell those people who I really was. About how they burned your house down and said you died and I didn't believe them. I spent over a year of my life Markus, a year of MY life with Lia Ambrosi trying to find out what happened to you. We broke you out, we could've been killed and what do you do?" Vivica glares back at him. "You write a 'fictional' novel about it and make millions. With those millions you proceed to run my fucking name in the dirt and I don't even get as much as an 'I'm sorry' from you. I got NOTHING." "I'm sorry." Markus interrupted, "I am. I'm sorry. It wasn't supposed to be like that." "But it is." Vivica looked away, "It's too late." "No, no it's not too late Viv. I can get another interview, I can set the record straight about you. I just need to make a phone call and..." "No, you don't understand." Vivica closed her eyes, "It's too late, I'm sorry." "You're... sorry?" With those words she listed as the door to the cabin was splintered by brute force. Flashlight swarmed around the cabin as Markus turned around just in time to be tackled down onto the ground. Dozens and dozens of elite military officers filed into the room, piling onto the ground as Markus Blaire tried to shake his way loose. "WHY?!" He screamed, watching as tears flowed down her cheeks and she grimaced, "WHY DID YOU DO THIS TO ME?!" She didn't have an answer for him as they began to tie a gag around his mouth. Turning her back she couldn't watch anymore. "VIV!" He screamed out in desperation, "HELP M..." She covered her face with her hands as his screams became muffled. She couldn't watch. She couldn't listen. It was at that moment in time that she realized there really was no turning back now. She was all in for a deal with the devil. Almost as if on cue she suddenly felt a hand on her shoulder, patting her on the back as she sobbed through her fingers. "These type of things..." Christian's voice said her ear as she could hear Markus being drug away, "They are not easy. But... they are right. You did the right thing Vivica. We are all proud of you." ---- ...but nothing quite like this. I couldn't even look into your eyes as they came. I turned my back because I couldn't bare to watch. The memories we share can never be replaced my heart even if you weren't around. You should've left well enough alone, Markus. You should've just let me drift away.
