Time: Midnight Place: Munich, Germany 1875 It was a different time then it was today. In 1875 Munich was a little more beautiful than it was today. With its Victorian like houses, and the sounds of late Classical Music and early Romantic Music filling the air, Munich was simply picturesque during the day. Yet, at night, much like this night, Munich was a city of pure fear. Heavy fogs would sweep the night air, making summer nights feel like it was the deadest of winter. The people began to talk about how the beautiful city was under some kind of spell from the devil. Deaths also were running rampant throughout the city. People who would be found with bite holes in their neck were completely pale skinned that looked like their soul was sucked out of them. Days later, others saw these same people waling at night, even after they were buried, and the death count was raising more and more everyday. Some wondered though, that it was rather odd, more women were dying than men. Many were lead to believe this was the works of a man who was sick of mind, yet little did they know that the citizens of Munich was sadly mistaken in their toughts. This night, when the day was renewing itself, a lovely brunette was walking the streets. She had left a tavern and was trying to walk home. Just like many nights before, a heavy fog was wrapped around the city. The woman, who was named Lyssa walked the streets letting her heart try to guide her home. Every once in a while, she would look over her shoulders, because she felt she was being followed. Yet, every time she turned around, she was rewarded with more fog. “I’m just being crazy,” she thought to herself. “Actually you are not,” a soft lustful voice said to her, “I thought I was the only one crazy to be walking alone at this time of night.” Lyssa jumped out of her skin as a woman suddenly appeared beside her. She was dressed in a purple cloak, even though in the fog, and dim light, it appeared to be black. The hood of the cloak was pulled down to reveal this woman’s long blonde hair. “I didn’t mean to scare you,” the beauty told her, “I am lost in all this fog, yet I’ve lived in this city for years. I never thought I would find someone else out here as well.” “It’s okay,” Lyssa responded, “You just came out of nowhere. I’m lost as well. I thought I knew these streets but I appear to be as lost as you are.” “Not a good thing with all the murders going on.” “I agree, but I can’t spend all night singing in the tavern either.” “That was you, you have a lovely voice.” “Thank you, you know usually I have to fight the men off of me after they say that. They always want something for me after that.” “I can assure you all I want is some company walking down the street. Let’s see what’s down this way.” Lyssa nodded and the two walked hand in hand down the street. Lyssa noticed but didn’t say anything that this mysterious woman’s hand was cold to the touch. After the two walked a way without saying anything, Lyssa decided to cut the silence. “My name is Lyssa, I didn’t catch your name.” “Oh, I apologize. My name is Leandra/? “You say you lived here for a while now, I don’t recall seeing you before.” “I’ve only been here for a few months. Fairly new to town.” “So you were here when all this weird weather was starting up and the deaths?” “Yes, I was. You know, you don’t strike me as one of the brave crime fighters here.” “I’m sorry, just my sister was one of the people that died as well as my dear mother. I’ve been trying to find who would kill them in such a horrible way.” “Someday, the killer will be caught. You shouldn’t worry about me. Do I look a blood thirsty killer to you?” “Actually, you don’t, I’m sorry for thinking that way. You look more like a bar winch despite your cloak.” “Considering things, I’ll take that as a compliment. Oh no, looks like a dead end.” Sure enough, the street was actually and alley they were walking down. The two had came up to a stone wall. Lyssa began to shake as she saw this development. “Is there something wrong, we can just backtrack a few steps.” “This was the alley my sister and mother were killed in. I didn’t even realize it until now.” “Oh I’m so sorry, Lyssa.” Leandra wrapped her arms around Lyssa and hugged her. Lyssa tried to break away after a few seconds but the blonde held her tight. Soon the brunette’s lips were locked with the blonde’s. The blonde’s tongue probed into the brunette’s mouth which caused Lyssa to break away. “What the fuck are you doing,” Lyssa shrieked. “I was trying to make you feel better, the only way I knew how to do so. Besides it seemed to relax your sister, and boy your mother just melted to my touch.” “How the hell do you know them.” “Because,” Leandra said cackling stepping closer to Lyssa, “I saw both of the them the night the died. Even a few minutes afterwards.” “What the hell, it..it was you. You killed them?” “Oh, kill is such a dirty word. I prefer to say I put them at ease. Now it’s your turn.” Lyssa screamed punching Leandra in the face. She turned and ran away from her, only to be slammed back to the ground after she ran a few feet. Lyssa had ran into a wall as she thought she was rounding a corner in the dense fog. “Oh, help me, I found the killer of Munich! She’s right behind me,” she screamed into the night. “No one can hear you Lyssa,” Leandra said joining Lyssa. She picked up the burnette by her hair and spun her around to face her. Lyssa screamed as Leandra’s beauty was changed by long fangs dangling from the mouth of the woman. Her fingernails were longer than they were just mere seconds ago. Blood streamed from the blonde’s nose from where Lyssa had hit her earlier. “What the hell are you,” Lyssa cried, “Are you some sort of demon?” “I prefer to be called a vampire. A creature of the night, if you will. My kind, will give you kind what they always wanted, everlasting life. A chance to remain beautiful, much like your mother and your sister. In just a couple of days they will arise from the earthly prison you call death and walk the Earth again. I can give you that kind of peace, to be reunited with your family.” “I don’t want to die,” Lysaa cried. “Why do you humans, say the same thing every time” You won’t die, it’ll just hurt for a few minutes. However for you, I’ll make you last a little longer since you are the one who hurt me!” Lyssa went to scream, but Leandra slashed at the throat of the brunette. It wasn’t a deep cut, just enough to silence Lyssa a little. “What’what are you going to do to me,” Lyssa gasped, the fear making it harder for her to breathe. “I’m going to give you peace. Don’t you want to see your family again?” “Yes, I do.” “Then afterwards the three of you will serve your Queen Leandra, like you should’ve tonight when I kissed you.” Leandra wasted no more time and plunged her teeth into the succulent neck of Lyssa. The woman screamed, but soon that was stopped as the loud slurping sounds of Leandra was heard in the night. “Hope, what the hell are you doing!” Hope raised her head up and saw no fog. It wasn’t even Munich?.she was in Gerald, MO holding some woman she didn’t even know. In front of her was Christopher Clarkson sitting on Ariel. Hope was naked as the day she was born. She then looked down at the woman, trembling in fear. Her neck was bleeding and when Hope raised one of her hands, she found out that there was blood coming out of her mouth. “Christopher, how did I get here,” she cried. “I don’t” know my love. One minute you were lying in bed with me. Next you were gone. I was worried and I called for Ariel. She picked up her scent, and we found you here biting this woman’s neck.” “That’s impossible. How the hell did I get here” There’s no way I ran 5 miles from the country naked into this town and no one stopping me to report it! “Hope perhaps you should turn around.” Hope did that, and she saw a line of four bodies lying on the ground holding their necks. All of them were crying and asking for this madness to stop. “Christopher, get me home now. I need to speak to Shadow. I can’t wait for her to contact her. This is getting serious. I’m on the verge of killing people now! There’s to many witnesses here, and I could be sent away.” “You don’t need to worry about that. Who is going to believe a dragon appeared in some country town with a naked woman acting like a vampire. You’ll be fine.” Christopher grabbed his lover and the two sailed into the sky. He held her tight as she softly whimpered. He didn’t know what to do or how to save her. All he could do was pray that she was able to control her inner anger.
