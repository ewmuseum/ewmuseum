Crucified for no sins An image beneath me What’s within our plans for life It all seems so unreal I'm a man cut in half in this world Left in my misery... ~x~ January 15th, 2004 Carson stood at the bus stop waiting for the bus, wishing that Keegan would’ve tagged along. She knew that he wasn’t into the whole mall scene but since the murder of Amber Ryan, it made her edgy. Amber was only a year older than she was, it made Carson think that it could have been her. She let out a deep sigh as she saw the bus approach. She slowly climbed the steps of the El Sol bus looking around cautiously before she took a seat behind the driver. On any normal day she would have sat in the back seat but a very disturbing looking man with greasy black hair occupied the seat. Carson pulled out her Walkman and put the ear buds in hoping to drown out the whirling of her own mind. Since the words of accusation spilled from her father’s mouth about the young girl that was raped and murdered Carson couldn’t help but wonder if he would say the same thing if that had been her. She kept looking at all the people on the bus; she couldn’t help but shake the feeling that she was being watched. She reached up and pulled the chain signaling her stop a block early. She quickly got off the bus, her heart pounding like a bass drum inside her chest. Before heading up the block she stopped and looked at the bus as it pulled away from the curb. She saw the face of the man that was sitting in the back seat pressed against the window. Tears streamed down her cheeks as she ran to the nearest payphone. Her fingers trembled as she deposited the coins and dialed the number for a cab. She had no desire to go to the mall, no desire to be raped and murdered. She would go home and that is where she would stay until Keegan came home. She would never leave the house alone again. ~x~ The reverend he turned to me Without a tear in his eyes It's nothing new for him to see I didn't ask him why I will remember The love our souls had Sworn to make Now I watch the falling rain All my mind can see Now is your (face) ~x~ Present Day He looked up into the long fluorescent bulbs of the Camp Recovery Center; their bright light shone across the beige colored walls providing a soft contrast to the harsh fire engine red tiles covering the floor. For many months now Keegan O’Conner had been coming to outpatient therapy as ordered by the city of Los Angeles. The court order came about when Keegan was busted for the use of Steroids in Encore. The young GCW Superstar had quite the criminal record. He followed the red tiled road for what felt like miles, the florescent lights against the beige walls illuminating the hallway to the point of a glaring brightness. His heart felt like it was about to explode. The shouting voices from the Emergency Department team slowly started to fade away, replaced with a loud beeping sound in his ears. Keegan lies on the stretcher just looking up at the lights, each light flashing a memory of his younger years good and bad. Memories that were the reason for him lying there. One that continually haunted his mind was that of Carson. The medical team jolts Keegan back to reality as they quickly turn to the left busting through a set of double doors. He felt the prick of a needle and his heart felt relieved as the pace slowly decreased. Keegan slowly started to regain his hearing and could hear the doctor and nurses voices. “Blood Pressure two ten over one eighty” slowly decreasing.” The female nurse’s insipid voice replied. “His color is slowly returning to normal” perspiration to a minimum.” This voice made Keegan turn his head as he felt her hand on his forehead. She looked like an angel with the florescent lighting revealing a halo around her blonde hair. She lifted her head and Keegan remembered the face, it was Dianna’s face. Keegan blinked against the penlight that she directed at his eyes. “Still very light sensitive” pupils still not equal and reactive.” She looked down at Keegan and smiled. “You’re gonna be fine Keegan. I still love you, I couldn’t let anything happen to you.” Keegan’s could hear the heart monitor start to beep faster, his body reacting to the shock of her of all people being there. “I will never regret what went on between us. You are a good person Keegan, you do a lot of bad things, but you still have a good heart. Please never forget that.” Keegan felt a sharp pain in his chest as the monitor went ballistic. She smiled at him and he felt a stinging pain in his right arm as his eyes slowly started to flutter closed. He could hear the doctor screaming orders at the nurses but he couldn’t really make sense of it. He could hear his heartbeat pounding like a bass drum in his ears. He strained to open his eyes but he just couldn’t fight sleep any longer. “Mr. O’Conner” you need to stay awake?. Mr. O’Conner.” Keegan looked up at the blonde nurse and smiled slightly as his eyes slammed shut. He felt a slap against his face as he slowly started to open his eyes. He felt another sharp slap to the other side of his face. “Mr. O’Conner?” Snapped back to reality, Keegan found himself standing outside his destination. Dr. Copeland eyed him curiously, her blonde hair falling over shoulders. “Mr. O’Conner. Are you alright?” Dr. Copeland asked. “Huh?” Was the first response that came to mind. Keegan took a moment to recall his surroundings and when he did he felt rather ridiculous. “Uh” yeah? I was just lost in thought for a moment.” She could sense his embarrassment but chose not to acknowledge it, Keegan was a very reclusive patient and to acknowledge his embarrassment would make him clam up during their session. So, nonchalantly Janet Copeland stepped around Keegan and opened the door into their session room. Janet slipped past him and slid into the room, “Then let’s get this session started.” With the soft whiff of jasmine in her wake Keegan followed his therapist into their room. ~x~ Well I guess You took my youth I gave it all away Like the birth of a New-found joy This love would end in rage And when she died I couldn't cry The pride within my soul You left me incomplete All alone as the Memories now unfold. ~x~ January 15th, 2004 The marijuana smoke traced ladders to heaven, drifting up the bedroom's ceiling before disappearing into nothing. Keegan, Michael and Alec had been friends since grade school. The three went through everything together, and drugs were just one of those things. Keegan took a long drag off of the remains of a dutch master, holding his breath and the smoke in his lungs he handed the blunt off to Michael. Keegan could feel the smoke in the back of his throat and he knew what was coming. In a rush of air the smoke flew from his mouth as the young O’Coonner’s hand found itself clamped to his mouth. As quickly as he had begun, Keegan stopped coughing and sat back in the computer chair, a smile formed across his face. “Fuck me, this shit is awesome.” “I told you didn’t I?” Michael replied, handing the drug to Alec. “My boy John takes care of me.” Alec hit the blunt in three quick puffs. Looking at the blunt Alec rose from the chair in the corner and butted the roach out in the incense holder. While the other two kept talking Alec pulled a small bag from his pocket, placing it down on Mike’s dresser. “How much is he selling this shit?” Keegan asked, gazing up into the white ceiling. “Fiften a gram.” Replied Mike leaning back across the bed to prop himself up against the wall. “But this shit is worth it.” “Yeah, no doubt.” He responded, as his high began to overcome him. “Dude, this is the most mellow I’ve felt in weeks.” The contents of the bag laid out across the dresser as Alec busied himself separating the substance into three pinky wide lines. “Hey guys, I got something for us to try.” Mike pulled himself up from the bed coming to see what Alec had, Keegan however remained seated too stoned to move. “Yayo?” The over-eager Mike asked. “My cousin gave me this bag.” Alec replied, confirming his buddy’s ascertainment. “Yo Keeg.” Mike hollered, snapping Keegan back to attention. Not knowing why he was doing it, Keegan rose to his feet and walked over to his friends. Satisfied with the thickness of the lines Alec pulls a dollar bill out of his pocket and rolls it into a tube. With the tube firmly in place Alec sniffed the white substance into the bill and up his nostril. Shooting his head back, Alec pinched his nostrils between thumb and forefinger. Taking from Alec’s lead, Mike took the tube and followed suit, finally handing the tube to Keegan. “What’s this for?” Alec chuckled, “you stick it up your nose and sniff the coke with it.” “Coke?” A perplexed Keegan asked. “Yeah, the mother fucking bug shug”.” Mike added. “It really gets you revved up.” “Oh?” The room spun around Keegan as the thrill of a new adventure overcame him. To sniff, or not to sniff, that was the question before him. Reluctantly, but excitedly Keegan closes the distance between him and the substance.
