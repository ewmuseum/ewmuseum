The Golf Dream

15 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-15-05 Marcus Williams had spent twenty three years on the force, signing up for the squad right after he finished his tour of Vietnam for the United States Marine Corps. Marcus knew all the trials and tribulations that went with the career choice he had chosen, but as time passed on’he relished the thought of finally retiring and spending the majority of his time on the golf course. Marcus was not a bad golfer either, he just had to focus more on his backswing. Marcus often found himself daydreaming about those days. He could almost feel the perspiration building up on his brow as he lined up a fifteen yard putt for Eagle. He inhaled gently as he drew the club back a few feet, before exhaling as he swung with just the right amount of force. He smiled as he watched the ball glide over the luscious green grass that surrounds the eighteenth hole of a near perfect game’The bragging rights a fifty nine would bring brought a smile to his face. “The golf dream again?” Marcus sprung up in his chair, knocking over an empty coffee mug that was set close to his paper littered desk. “Yeah,” he sighed, as he ran his fingertips over the smooth patch of skin where hair once resided. “Won’t be too much longer.” Marcus just smiled, “You know you can’t wait for me to retire. Maybe you’ll get that pretty little partner you’ve been fantasizing about.” “Yeah right,” shaking his head as he came into view, Robert Slade took a seat at the desk directly across from Marcus. With his smoke gray sport coat draped over the back of the seat, Roberto pulled it in closer to the desk. A red, long sleeved dress shirt takes the place of the powder blue one he was wearing the other day. “With my luck, I’ll end up with Davis over there.” Marcus glances over in the direction Robert motioned toward with a tip of his head, chuckling softly as he pictures the two of them together. For reference sake, Alan Davis is a slightly overweight detective with a sweet tooth for strawberry filled donuts. Proof of this usually dribbles down his chin and on to his shirt, as evident by the various faded pink stains that he has no problem wearing. “Yeah, you can wipe up his chin like you did Bobby’s.” Robert shakes his head, grinning at Marcus? little joke. “Yeah, but Bobby’s two and a half. What’s Davis” excuse?” The two continue their laugh for a bit, before Robert throws in the mood killer. “That Bradley punk walked.” Marcus nodded his head slightly, the grin that once filled his face with joy now faded away. “I was sure he would crack,” Marcus started, as he began to fumble through one of the files that was stacked on his desk. Robert leaned forward, whispering to his partner, “He knew we didn’t have that piece of trophy as evidence’I saw it in his eyes. He knew we were lying to him, and he played along. But why?” “I don’t know, Rob,” Marcus said as he flipped the file closed, sending us into complete darkness. “But I intend to find out.”
