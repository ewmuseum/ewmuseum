A Warrior

14 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14 Nov 05 It started as any day would start for me. The alarm clock buzzed me awake, which of course had me cursing at myself for setting it so early. I laid in bed; Mary’s arm draped over my chest and looked up at the ceiling with that blurry, morning vision. The bed was warm, as was Mary’s touch, so I really had no desire to move. I brought my hand up and placed in on my forehead and let out a sigh. I turned my head to the side and looked at her face. Mary was sleeping soundly. I could hear the light sounds of her breathing and I watched as her back slowly moved up and then down again. Her face was almost completely covered by her wild curls that rested about her head. She was beautiful that was for sure, although I still sometimes wonder how two people as different as we are fell in love. This thought makes me chuckle softly. I take her hand and slowly move it from my chest, careful not to wake my sleeping beauty. Without much of a sound I turned my body to the edge of the bed and let my feet touch the carpet. I attempted to rub the sleep from my eyes; it works to an extent although things are still a bit blurry. I looked at the curtains that were right in front of me and could see the light of the rising sun beginning to creep into the room. I stood up and stretched my body out. I could feel every muscle twisting and turning and it felt damn good. I turn my head to each side, letting my neck crack in the process. I made my way around the room and towards the bathroom, flipping the light on. I was momentarily blinded from the sudden change in light. I didn’t wait for my vision to come back fully before I walked towards the sink with my arms outreached as to not knock anything over. Just the thought of what I may have looked like at the time made me smile and shake my head slightly. My sight came back just as I reached the sink. I looked into the mirror and saw what I could only describe as what I’d look like if I were in a George Romero Zombie movie, looking back at me. My hands roamed until they found the faucet, turning the cold water on. I cupped my hands under the cool water for a moment before bringing it up and splashing it on my face. A chill ran up my spine, jolting me a bit more awake than I was a moment ago. Again I let the cold water fill up in my hands before covering my face in it. My eyes moved back to my reflection in the mirror when ? WHERE THE FUCK WAS MY REFLECTION!” Haha No just kidding. Having the name LeStatt didn’t mean I was some blood sucking, creature of the night like many had labeled me. I laughed out loud before opening the cupboard and pulling out my toothpaste and brush. I went on to brush my teeth, put on deodorant, and brush my hair out and tie it back. As I finished my morning ritual I looked back into the mirror and gladly wasn’t staring back at a zombie version. I flipped the light off and then left the bathroom. My next stop was at the closet. I slid the door open and walked in. It was Sunday and I had nothing to do really so it was just a day to relax. It had gotten a tad chilly standing with no shirt and boxers so I pulled down a pair of white pajama bottoms and a black “wife beater”. I rotated around to a drawer and pulled out a pair of black socks. I closed the door behind me as I left the closet, moving to a chair that was at my computer desk in the room. I slid my bottoms on, followed by my socks. The shirt rested on my shoulder as I stood back up. I looked down at Mary and smiled just at the sight of her. I leaned down and kissed her on the cheek softly before turning and going out of the bedroom, throwing my shirt on as I walked out. The house was new, so it still had caught me off guard a few times. After Mary and I burned the last one down it was kind of in necessity that I bought something else, although I would probably sell it in a few weeks anyway. I have no real desire to own one place for very long anymore. I found the stairs and moved down them, trying to find my way to the kitchen for a snack before sitting in front of my television for a day of football. The kitchen tiles were cold even with the socks covering my feet. I moved to the refrigerator and opened it up; peering inside to see what the hell I could eat. The thought of cooking me some Chorizo con huevos was tempting but the thought of actually cooking wasn’t one I was big on at the time. I shrugged and pulled out our gallon of milk that was now half empty. On top of the refrigerator were a few boxes of cereal. I pulled down the Golden Grahams and set that and the milk on the counter behind me. I turned again and went to the cupboards next to the refrigerator and pulled down a bowl along with a spoon. I fixed my cereal and made my way to the living room where my big screen and NFL Ticket were waiting for me. The games weren’t on yet so I just flipped it to ESPN to watch the pre game show and enjoy my breakfast. It was the first time I had gotten to really relax in quite sometime. Even on my birthday I was busy making people wish they had never crossed me. World Wide was a complete and utter success if I had ever seen one. Stevens was worse for wear and even some of those ignorant fools that had been getting on my nerves got what was coming to them. Of course Mary also beat Eva in that match so that made the night even sweeter. Life was going well that was the one thing that I could agree with on this day. Mary and I were never better, my career had really taken off and all of those years of fighting and clawing for my respect had finally paid off. I could actually be happy for David and his relationship with Wylie, even was planning on going to dinner with the two of them someday soon just to get everything out in the open. Not since Cindy was alive had I felt so happy and I could tell. Even as I rewatch the shows I can just see that I am having more fun out there. Sure there were still people like Michael Stevens who could almost ruin a perfect night, but I have learned to deal with that. I even openly wonder now if I really do even hate Stevens? I dislike him and cannot wait to embarrass him at NC-17 once again, but I am not sure if hatred is the right word. Yes things were fine and would continue to be. How could they not be? Then the article that changed my day flashed on the screen. “No signs of foul play in WWE wrestler death.” By this time I have seen many people in the world of wrestling die suddenly. I remember being far younger than I am now and watching the Pay Per View where Owen hart had his fatal fall from the rafters, I remember the moments of silence for the likes of The British Bulldog, Mr. Perfect, and Brian Pillman. They were all legends in their own right. I remember Owen beating his brother Bret Hart, just as I remember Bulldog beating the same Bret Hart for the Intercontinental championship at Summerslam 92 in London. I remember the Sharpshooter, the perfect plex, the Rude Awakening, and the power slam. I shook my head, wondering aloud who had passed this time. When his picture flashed on the screen my heart dropped and I could feel the color from my face flush. I leaned forward, placing my bowl on the table in front of me and turning up the volume. “Authorities said Eddie Guerrero didn't respond to a wake-up call Sunday morning. His nephew, fellow WWE wrestler Chavo Guerrero, and hotel security at the Minneapolis Marriott City Center forced their way into the room and found the 5-foot-8, 220-pound wrestler on the floor. Attempts to revive him were unsuccessful.” Eddie Guerrero? Is that really the name I had heard and the face I had seen? One of the only real reasons to even pay attention to WWE programming? DEAD? My mind was cleared of any other thoughts in that moment as I listened to more and more about the things that Guererro achieved in his career. Many of those accomplishments I had followed. I had much respect for the man, being a very successful Hispanic wrestler does really give you honor and respect in our community. I remember my uncle Juanito turning me onto wrestling when I was a child; it was all about the Luchadores or later on “cruiserweights”. At the time it was almost completely what our people were in wrestling. From ECW’s craziness, to WCW’s dominance, to his intro to the WWF as “The Radicalz” and even his World Title reign that brought a smile to my face. “In February 2004, Guerrero became the second wrestler of Hispanic heritage to be WWE champion when he defeated Brock Lesnar, a former University of Minnesota wrestling standout. Guerrero lost the title four months later.” Only the second Hispanic wrestler to be crowned WWE Champion. That stat alone showed what kind of career the man had. I met him once when I was just getting into the business. Myself and my brother were the tag champs of a small Indy fed and he agreed to come and give the kids pointers. His knowledge was invaluable even thought I only spent about ten minutes alone with him. “His nephew, Chavo said Guerrero was open about past drug and alcohol abuse but had been sober for FOUR years. He was married and had three children.” It was this had had hit me the hardest. The man was not a saint this everyone knew. He made mistakes just as every other man had. Yet he had the courage and the strength to pull himself from those dark places and make himself a better man. He fought, clawed, and scraped to get where he was. Did his sins finally catch up with him? Did God not see his incredible turn around in his life as good enough? God seems to have a way of taking those that seem to deserve a second chance, yet leaving the sorry bastards who would be better off eating dirt to poison the free world. He gave me a second chance. Everything seemed so trivial in those moments after I had heard the news. That ordinary day didn’t seem so ordinary. Everything that seemed important just did not anymore. World Wide, Michael Stevens, Steven Caldera ? none of it. It was life that seemed important. A life that I had taken for granted, hell that almost everyone took for granted unless forced not to. Did a man who had a family to take care of not deserve a second chance? A man who exorcised his past demons and learned to stand on his own two legs, did he not deserve to live a full life? Was I one of these poisonous snakes that are left to plague the world? I couldn’t answer these questions, nobody could. Instead I swallowed hard and stood back up on my feet. The day had just begun but there were things that could not wait. I threw on a pair of all white shoes and then tossed my black jacket over my shoulders. I left the TV on and walked outside in the sunlight. It was a beautiful morning, a crisp breeze, a bright sun, bird chirping. I couldn’t help but frown slightly, thinking that a good man would no longer enjoy days such as these. Life had to be more. It had to be special. Eddie Guerrero. The meaning of his name suddenly came to my mind. Guerrero meant WARRIOR. What a Warrior he was. R.I.P.
