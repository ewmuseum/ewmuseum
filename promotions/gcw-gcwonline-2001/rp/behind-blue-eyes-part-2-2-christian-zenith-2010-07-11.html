Behind Blue Eyes (Part 2.2: Christian Zenith)

11 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/10 "How far we all come. How far we all come away from ourselves. You can never go home again." -- James Agee A Death in the Family As the doors to Zenith Manor opened in front of me, I couldn't help but repeat that old quote to myself in my head... You can never go home again. Yet, here I was years after I'd ran away from this palace screaming and the exact opposite had happened... it felt like home. Following Jacques down the hallway it felt like I belonged, like I was wanted, like it was meant to be all along. Even at this very moment the thought of this place brought a taste of bile up the back of my throat, but the feeling was irresistible. The passing of a mirror even revealed to me a smile across my face that I was trying so hard to hold back. Jacques noticed too, smiling back at me without a word as looked around the awe-inspiring entrance that didn't seem to change one bit. With all the money Christian Zenith had, this estate was the one true mark that he had it all. There was not one upgrade within the house the past couple of years. Not because he wasn't willing to spend the money, but because the upgrade simply didn't exist. With my bag slung over my shoulder, every clack on the marble floor and every picture on the wall brought back a memory. Some good, most bad... but the memories were mine and mine alone. Even with all the bile I still hold in my heart towards Christian, the time we‘ve spent apart has only made me grow fonder of the memories we share. I remember when I was young my mother explained to me this would happen, but never in a million years did I believe her until this very moment. As I came closer to my destination, I couldn't get over how surreal everything seemed to me. Almost like I was trapped within my own dreams, or if Christian shows up my own nightmares. Yet in this dream the weight of the world was lifted cleanly off my shoulders. Despite the heavy bags weighing me down I felt light as a feather, like I was a teenager discovering this beautiful mansion for the first time all over again. How could something that felt so right, feel so wrong? As excited as I was, I almost would've rather felt nothing at all. I guess I sound more and more like Christian every day, it's no wonder that I ended up here. Maybe I did belong. Looking back, I should've known this was all far too good to be true. Everything just seemed to fall into place so easily. At the moment the only thought I could manage was that Jacques was right, this was exactly what I needed. I felt alive again. ---- "Your room, Miss Valentine." Jacques opened the door with a smile from ear to ear, almost as if he'd been waiting years to say that line again. A smirk crossed her face as she looked at his reaction. Jacques wasn't one to show emotion once, always working and never getting a single thank you. He was a loyal servant to the entire Zenith family for years, becoming Vivica's confidant and almost father figure during her stay there. He brought a calming presence over her, even during this chaotic time in her life. "Thank you, Jacques." She spoke as she pulled her rolling luggage behind her into the room. Almost immediately she felt as if she had stepped into a time warp. Everything was decorated exactly the same way, precisely how she left it. As a matter of fact, it was almost like she'd never left at all. Laying her suitcase down on her old black comforter, she couldn't help but look around the room in suspicion. "How, did you do this?" Vivica asked obviously struck by the replication. "I kept it very well for you." Jacques nodded with a smile, "I was waiting for you to come back." How strange. As she opened her suitcase she couldn't help but feel like this was all a little too clean, a bit too concise. Surely with the hustle and bustle of big industry and the whores that Christian liked to keep around, somebody HAD to have used this room for something. If not, why even keep it together? It could be used as an office, or even an upgrade to Jacques room. God knows he needed it. "Well, I mean no offense... but I was never planning on coming back..." Vivica hesitated as she walked over to the closet and pulling it open, revealing the same expensive dresses that Christian had bought her during their years together. Even the jewelry box sitting on a shelf just inside the door was in precisely the same place. Not a speck of dust, and everything inside was still just the way she organized it. "You didn't need to keep it like this, you could've used this room." "Oh, no Miss Valentine, I couldn't do that." Jacques shook his head as he walked over to the slightly distraught woman, placing his hand upon her shoulder, "I think it's much better suited like this. Welcome home." Even though she was uneasy about the whole situation, it was a lot better than living out of a suitcase and going from hotel to hotel. She'd yearned for a bed to call her own, and sure enough there it was right in front of her... her bed for years. It was comparable to moving back in with mom and dad, except there was no mom and dad. Just her and Jacques. That type of thing was eerily... normal. "Thank you, Jacques." Vivica forced a smile patting her confidant on the back before he turned around and speed walked away, just like he always would. The man kept so busy, it was no wonder he had no time for emotions. Closing the door behind him, Vivica looked around at her old room one more time before sitting on the bed. No matter how many times she closed her eyes and tried to picture the room in her head, whenever she opened them it was exactly the same. In a way it crept her out that after all these years not a single object had moved but then again... it did feel like home. And in that way, as she fell back onto the bed and a smile crossed her lips, it was everything she'd ever asked for. ---- The problem with being alive however is that you aren't always in control. When things feel too good to be true, they normally are. Life tends to be that way, but it wasn't until that night when reality struck me light a lightning bolt... ---- You're even more useless than I thought. "I did my best, what do you want from me?" I want the champion everyone said you were. The greatest of all time... what a joke. You're nothing compared to LeStatt. Nothing. "I can beat that son of a bitch, I've done it before..." That doesn't matter, you're not good enough now. Now he's gone and you're only as good as your last match. It's over. You're a failure, just like you thought you were. A flash of light jolted eyes open from the depths of her nightmares. Lightning in the distance. Looking around the room she felt alone, but knew someone was there. Maybe they had the answers she was looking for. "You... told me I would beat him." The strain in her voice told the story above a backdrop of raindrops and distant thunder. She was told that it was going to be hell, and at one point she honestly believed that she knew what that was. It wasn't until it was over that she learned hell isn't something that is overcome. Enduring hell didn't end with trumpets and fanfare. Hell is when you've given everything you have to give, and your efforts are scoffed at and tossed aside. Hell is when you realize that you didn't matter. "You... took everything away from me." A flash of light showed the figure of a young woman slumped at the end of a bed. Her long hair flowing down, covering her face from the light. She didn't deserve to see the light of day. Deep down in her heart she knew it to be true. She'd seen and done unforgivable things. Burned the bridge to everything that she knew for a shot at immortality. To be the best the world had ever seen. "You... lied to me." Her swollen upper lip curled into a snarl as she ran her fingers through her hair. Impatiently she waited for an answer that would never come. How coincidental was it that whenever she wanted answers it was gone and whenever she wanted silence she couldn't get away? "You... are not going to answer me are you?" Huffing the words between her lips she inhaled deeply, keenly feeling the deep bruises and scars that cover her otherwise immaculate body. Reluctantly she lifted the dead weight of her battered body to a vertical base. Slowly taking steps towards the window, a grimace crossed her face every time she planted her bare foot onto the cold hardwood floor. "Of course not..." Answering her own question she looked into the window, but not out into the beautiful thunderstorm that was taking place just a few inches away from her face... but at her face itself. A swollen lip was nothing compared to the bruises and scratches that were etched along her otherwise breath-taking features. She once remembered looking at these battle wounds with a sense of pride, morally satisfied with the sacrifices she'd made for the cause. This time however, she could only think of one thing to say: "You're a coward." "I will admit, I would have preferred to hear hello..." A familiar voice rang from just a few feet behind her, derailing her train of thought with a startling answer. She was so involved her own mind she'd forgotten where she was, and immediately realized she should've known better than to trust things would go as smoothly as Jacques said they would. A favor to an old friend is what he'd assured her, but she had a sneaking suspicious that this was how it was going to end. It was foolish to think that he'd pass up the opportunity to kick her while she was down. Hindsight is always twenty-twenty, they say. She looked into the mirror and watched as he slowly paced his way behind her, placing his icy cold hand on her exposed back. A chill ran up her spine as he moved his hand up and down just like old times. His best attempt to sooth the pains of a woman scorned. "...But I cannot say I am entirely surprised by your reaction. You sure seem to be though. See a ghost?" "You're not supposed to be here." Her answer was blunt but expected. Anyone who has ever been close to her wouldn't have expected anything less, and he was no different. "And you were not supposed to lose to LeStatt Knight," A smile crept across his reflection in the glass, "but I suppose we are both full of surprises." "No, I'm full of surprises." Vivica snarled from between her teeth, "You're full of shit." She jerked her own body out from under the touch of her ex-boyfriend, spinning around and looking at him in the eyes. Even the often emotionless Christian Zenith couldn't contain a smirk that crossed his lips as he looked upon a girl from his past. She was once meant to be his wife, but that's when things went downhill. "Why does it always have to be that way, Viv?" Christian looked down for a moment, shaking his head before slowly glancing back up at her, "Why is it that my presence is always met with such scathing hatred? I remember you were in love once. They were good times..." "They were good times for you, maybe." Vivica responded before Christian even finished, "I spent my time sitting in this place all alone, waiting for weeks for you to come home because I wasn't 'competent' enough to work for your company anymore. So you locked me away here with Jacques while you went around and fucked God knows what..." "I assure you, they meant nothing." "They should've been me!" Valentine roared, "That's the problem with you, you're not even fucking human anymore Christian. It's like you don't even process in your brain what a committed relationship is. It's not okay to go around screw other girls while you don't even as much as call me to let me know you're alive." "Things have changed." "They have." Vivica agreed taking a step around her former lover, standing perfectly still except for his face, moving wherever she moved, "I don't give two shits about you anymore. Goodbye." "I will not allow you to leave in this weath..." "Fuck you." That was the only response Valentine gave as she marched across the room. Pulling the door open she walked out and slammed the door behind her, shaking through the house like thunder. In the hallway she continued her warpath, cerulean blue eyes only fixated on one thing and one thing alone... the exit. "Miss Valentine!" A scream from behind fell on deaf ears as tunnel vision locked in. Her fists clinched in an effort to contain the venomous hatred she felt in her heart for the man she left behind. Treating her like a prisoner in her own home, fucking anything with a vagina that fell into his lap, the hell he put her and Lia Ambrosi through after she had left. She had done some terrible things in her life that she'd never repeat to a soul... but her list was child's play compared to the biblical length of Christian Zenith's actions. "Miss Valentine, please stay!" Suddenly a hand grabbed her on the shoulder, preventing her from her destination. Instinctively, Valentine turned around and swung, watching her fist just barely graze the top of Jacques' slicked back hair. His jaw dropped as Vivica took a step back, realizing who the man she almost knocked unconscious was. Frightened, Jacques looked back at her like a deer in headlights, not quite being able to formulate words that would equate to the English language. "Oh, it's you Jacques... I'm sorry! I thought you were..." "It's... fine." He managed to speak as he calmed down. It was ironic that the keeper of Zenith Manor and Christian Zenith's personal affairs was quite the pacifist. Vivica jogged her memory to try and remember a time when Jacques ever showed emotion before now. Nothing. "I'm sorry, but I have to go." Vivica responded, "I can't stay here." "This weather is horrible, Miss Valentine. I won't let you leave." "I'm not asking permission, I'm telling you I'm leaving." She took a few steps back as she said it and waved goodbye, "Thank you for the thought, anyway." "Please, don't go Miss Valentine." His words stopped her in mid-turn. "I had no idea Mister Zenith was going to show up tonight, but I assure you it's very temporary. He has a meeting scheduled for very early tomorrow morning, he can't possible stay here more than a few hours tonight. He is already quite a bit out of his way." "Well, the few minutes he's been here has already managed to fuck up my stay." "I'm aware of that..." Jacques responded, "But I'll make quite sure you don't see Mister Zenith for the rest of your stay. I guarantee it." "We both know you can't do that." "I will, promise." He looks back at the only company he used to ever have in this place, "For you." Pausing for a moment, she looked into the eyes of a man who had given up everything he'd ever known to serve another man. He never asked for much, not even from Christian himself. Jacques had gone years without a pay raise, not to say he wasn't paid handsomely, and had never taken any time off. His dedication and quiet nature is what got him this job in the first place, it certainly wasn't experience. Before this job, Jacques had done nothing like it before. Formerly an apartment manager in his native Quebec, Jacques dedicated his life to Christian Zenith after the horrific murder/suicide of his parents. It was Christian's father Ulrich who had given him the chance to start a whole new life, and he felt as if he owed the same to Ulrich's prodigal son. After all, Jacques had experienced a similar murder/suicide himself when his wife stopped taking her medication and murdered their six year-old daughter before hanging herself in the closet. Yet he kept strong and silent, a real man's man despite his taste for rather exquisite things. It was one of the very few things he shared in common with Christian, but the only thing Jacques had ever asked Vivica for was one more night in the Manor. How could she possibly say no? "I see him again..." Vivica laid down the law, "I'm gone." "You have my word, Miss Valentine." Jacques reassured her as she looked over his shoulder back down the hallway. Leaving her room, Christian Zenith slowly closed the door behind him almost as if he had no idea that anyone was watching him. Her eyes narrowed as he looked back down the hall at her. His expression was once again cold, emotionless. It was nothing more than a glare, however, before turning his back to his former protégé and disappearing into the darkness of the long, darkened hall. ---- I was that close to walking out that door, but something kept me from pulling the trigger. Whether it was the fact that I was a glutton for punishment or if Jacques really meant that much to me, I'll never know. It was destiny if I'd ever seen it before. If only the people who rant on the internet about how absorbed I was in myself could've seen me at that moment. They would've seen me for who I really was. Maybe they would've understood why I do the things I do, and why I am the way I am. I just want to feel loved. After everything I've done for them, why is that so much to ask? ---- Swipe. Red. Swipe. Red. Swipe. GREEN. "About fucking time..." She grunted to herself as she pulled the stainless steel handle on her hotel room door. 512 it read as she threw her weight, not quite as intimidating as it sounds, forcefully into the door jarring it open. With wheeled luggage in tow, she carried a black cargo bag and a backpack inside with her like a pack mule. Struggling to forced her doubled girth completely through the doorway, she tossed her cargo bag into the nearby bathroom opening and stepped through, letting the door close itself behind her. Another WorldWide. Another road trip. Another shitty hotel room. Never in a million years after the events that lead her to joining Global Championship Wrestling in the first place did she think that she would honestly miss Zenith Manor. These road trips used to be so exciting. The chance to go out on the road and see cities she'd never seen before, meet new people, make new fans... but the older she got the less she cared. Another city. Another group of fans. Another show. Another group of people who stopped believing. The last few months in GCW had turned the most fan-friendly wrestler in the history of the organization into a cynical, bitter ice queen. It was amazing the difference a few months could make, sometimes Vivica herself couldn't even believe the thoughts going through her head. It was almost like she had a different mind. A different voice. A different soul. It wasn't something she was particularly proud of, but it was also something she made no attempt to hide. At first GCW staffers attempted to set her up with special rooms in hopes of raising her spirits, it was after the loss of her house that everything changed. Every room looked the same. Generic, lifeless, and boring. The same lamps with the same switches adorned the walls of every hotel room from New York City to Los Angeles. Simply another quick shell constructed around a bed. A shell was something she could very well relate to. Just looking into the mirror she didn't see the same "Fearless Phenom" that everyone saw last year. That creative spark was gone, behind blue eyes was... nothingness. "What happened to me..." She said to herself dropping her bags on the ground. Running her hand through her hair she tried to make things right, forcing a smile back at herself... but it just wasn't the same. "No..." She stopped, "What have they done to me?" CLICK. The sound of her door opening startled her as she spin around and quickly tried to fix her hair and adjust her top. Who could possibly get into this room? "I don't need any housekeeping, I'm fine and kind of busy so if you could pleas..." "Heh, I do not do laundry." A familiar voice rang through her ear drums. Her eyes adjusted to the man behind the Gucci shades. Pulling them off ever so gently, he folded them up and placed them into the pocket of his suit jacket. The color of the suit? Black. Just like his soul. "I don't need you either." "I just need a moment of your tim..." "Don't make me call the front desk, I'm sure you're not supposed to have my keycard." "I have ways of getting whatever I want," Christian Zenith stares back at his former lover, dumbfounded, "you know that." "Well, I don't want you. You can't have me." Vivica explains taking a step towards him, he takes a step back to keep his distance, now with his back against the door. "You think that is what this is all about?" Christian's words halt her in her tracks, "You think I 'want' you? Is that it?" "I honestly don't care." "But I do." Christian replies, "That is why I am here. You see... I understand that whatever there was between the two of us is gone. My personal feelings for you are long gone. I do, however, feel that you have been wronged... by more than just myself. By the fans. By Garbage Bag Johnny." "You expect me to believe you feel sorry about something Christian?" Vivica almost laughs out loud, "Have you forgotten what I know about you? Do you think I'm an idiot." "No, I know all about you." He speaks confidently, "This is why I have to talk to you. Even though we will never work as lovers, you have to admit in the past we worked amazingly together. I think I can help you. No one here knows you better than I do, Viv. I knew you before all of this." He motions his arm around the room. "Private hotel rooms. World Championships. Fame. Fortune. Disdain." Christian takes a step forward, now more confident about his ability not to get struck by the brash Valentine, "When I met you, you were just a woman looking for something to call her own. Outcast by her friends and family, all she needed was a little motivation in her life to become something more. Something special. I saw it in you from day one, why else do you think I moved you all around Zenith Corporation in different jobs? I knew one day that you would be a star." "Some star..." Vivica huffed, "Those people don't love me anymore than I love you." "I noticed." Christian took another step forward, "Then they insult you, place you in the ring with people like AXEL Action. They ignore you in the main event picture, they think you are washed up, Viv. We both know you deserve better. I may have did some nasty things to you in the past, and I am not here to apologize for them, but I have respected you as a competitor and an athlete from day one. I would force you to groom the next big stars, the people paid money to see you... until Garbage Bag Johnny came along." "Are you getting to something?" Vivica looked towards the hotel room phone, "Wait... you cut it didn't you?" "You know me too well, Viv." A smirk crossed the face of The Leader Of Men, "What I am getting to is that I want to make things right by you again. I just want the chance to regain your friendship. I offer you back my home, no... your home again. Would it not be nice to have a place to come home to? I know it would mean the world to Jacques. Before you came back to visit all he would do is ask how you were doing, if you were okay. He looks at you like the daughter he never got to keep." "Yeah..." Vivica said, "He would've been a great father." "He still can be if you come home." Christian states, "Just a small token of my appreciation for everything that I am sure I have put you through. We can start over again. Wipe the slate clean. If you just give me the chance." "Thanks, but no thanks." Vivica glares back at Christian, unconvinced, "I don't make deals with the devil." "Well, I will let you sleep on it." "I said no." "I think it is best that you sleep on it." Christian said, pulling a key out of his pocket and laying it down on top of the television, "Here is the key to the house. Keep it. My home is your home. Sure beats living out of a suitcase, right?" She doesn't respond, her cerulean eyes just fixated on the golden key sitting on top of the television. "Have a nice night, Viv." With those words, the man affectionately dubbed by the United States Military as 'The Death Dealer' took a few steps backwards trying to measure up his ex-fiancé before turning his back and leaving as suddenly as he came. Vivica never knew him as 'The Death Dealer', only as Chris... and much later as 'that son of a bitch.' Picking the key up off of the television, she looked down at the circled-z chiseled on the top. He plasters his image all over everything. He was very proud of his work. A few minutes passed in silence as she examined the key to Zenith Manor. She had one exactly like it once and the same thing ended up happening to that one. It went into the trash. ---- All I'd ever want was a chance to take everything back again. A chance to start over and wipe the slate clean. In this business, that's asking far too much. In this business the lucky ones get worshipped like Gods among the people. Their mistakes are excused as an honest mistake that anyone could make. Heh, if only I could be so lucky... ---- "Hello?" Her voice echoed though the long, empty corridors. Despite what she was told, she seemed to be all alone inside the labyrinth. The sound of her footsteps on marble floors bounced off the walls around her, repeating back into her own ears as she moved her head back and forth on her shoulders looking for anyone... or anything moving in the distance. "Is anyone here? I let myself in..." There was no answer. Moving on, she found her path stopped at a tinted glass door lined in stainless steel. Her memory reminded her that this was the kind of tinted glass that you could see out from the inside but you couldn't see in. Balling her hand into a fist she banged on the glass three times before putting her ear up to the door, listening for an answer. Still, there was nothing. A frustrated grunt left her lips as she looked down to see if the door was locked. Much to her surprise the steel handle turned and she slowly pushed the door in, speaking as she slipping inside. "Where the hell is everyone?!" She'd hoped that her exclamation would be answered on the other side of the door, but alas it again was heard by no one except herself. "The hell!" Throwing her arms up in the air she turned around to leave, reaching her hand out towards the door before freezing in place. Blinking for a moment she turned back around and examined the room she found herself in side of. A giant bed laid slightly off center against the back wall of the room with a nightstand next to it. Against the opposite wall was a couch, chair, and a large entertainment system with a projection screen. The wall farthest to her right was entirely a tinted window with a beautiful view of the entire estate below. To her left, two doors. One was a large walk-in closet but the other... "That must be new." Vivica muttered to herself as she walked towards the mystery door. It certainly wasn't there before she left. Everything else in Christian's bedroom remained exactly the same, perhaps he ran out of room for his extensive and expensive wardrobe. Maybe he'd even built himself a second office that connected to his bedroom, or even a gym would make sense. No longer wrestling, much more of his physical training would have to be done at home. As she pulled the door open, she found herself completely surrounded by darkness. "There has to be a light..." Speaking under her breath to herself, Vivica ran her hand along the wall to find a light switch. The walls felt as if they were covered in craypaper, it was an odd sensation before she remembered Christian's fondness for clap-on lighting. CLAP. As the fluorescent lights above her sparked to life, her eyes adjusted to find... eyes staring back at her. Not just any eyes however, but her own. Quickly her breath left her lips and her heart jumped out of her chest. Her jaw dropped to the floor as she slowly spun around the room to see every inch of wall space covered with pictures of herself. Magazines, photoshoots, personal pictures... everything she could ever remember doing was there, right at her fingertips. Closing her eyes for a moment it was all just a little too much to take in. Opening them once again, she still found herself in the middle of a shrine. A shrine dedicated to her. "Heh..." She finally managed to vocalize, "Welcome home." ---- I can already hear the whispers in the crowd when they'll see him by my side. "What is she doing? She's made a deal with the devil." Maybe they're right, maybe I should know better than to trust the Antichrist. But in my darkest hours he has been the only person willing to give me the only thing I ever wanted... unconditional acceptance. I am who I am. I can't change that. I'm emotional, I'm bitchy, I have a short fuse and a dirty mouth, I'm opinionated at all the wrong times, and I put myself in situations where I'm in way over my head. I'm a girl who has always lived her life a on the edge and perhaps I am prone to making bad decisions, but they're my decisions to make. I just hope someone special in your life gave you more than you've ever given me. Everyone deserves a free pass every once in a while. Just remember that next time you turn on your television and wonder how things ever could have come to this. It all could've been avoided if you just would've believed in me... but you didn't. For some reason, I wasn't good enough and because of that: The blood is on your hands now.
