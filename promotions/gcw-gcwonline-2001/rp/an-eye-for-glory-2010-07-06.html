An Eye For Glory.

6 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/06/10 [Back at Larry's gym I'm in the locker room lacing my boots up for another day of training as I see Ben and Larry walk in. The two of them walk towards me and follow me to the punching back where I starting slamming into it.] Larry: Well kid I must say you were fantastic in that ring last week. Donny: I told you I didn't need as much training as you said I did. Larry: Your right! You need more! You were only a moment away from defeat until you caught Giles off guard. And don't get me started about you injuring Equinox before the match so you wouldn't have to watch both of them. [As those words come out from Larry's mouth I punch the bag one final time. I look to Larry just for a second before heading over to the speed bag.] Donny: Larry I know I did some bad things in my day man, people remind me of it all the time. That thing with Equinox though had nothing to do with me at all. Instead of being a grumpy old man accusing me of assault, you should be congratulating me on winning a title shot at Rampage. Larry: Congratulating you on a title shot? That's like telling Payton Manning congratulations on winning the Super Bowl in the second quarter before New Orleans came back to kick their ass. You need to remember that winning a title shot means jack shit until you actually become Champion! Donny: Whatever man. I got Mayhem's number, the only reason he was able to keep that title from me last time was because Bishop Steele didn't know what the Hell he was doing. Not only is Steele now gone, but Commissioner Johnny also gave me the advantage, of invoking my title shot any time during the night, so Mayhem will never see me coming. [A smile crosses my face as I mention the rules of my title shot to Larry, and a few beads of sweat drip from my chin. The speed bag slows down as I turn around to head towards my next training spot, that is until a chair comes down hard across my head knocking me down onto the floor. I drop to one knee holding my head in pain as I see Ben drop the chair to the floor, but before I can move he hooks me in a cobra clutch.] Ben: Ha! After all that blabbering about how you got Bryan Mayhem's number, I think you forgot that this is in hardcore rules! All it took was a chair shot and now your in a cobra clutch, if this was Mayhem you would of just been hit with an Oblivion Bomb and your title chance has just been wasted. Larry: You get one little win and all of a sudden you're the greatest wrestler in the world? That cockiness is what you need to lose, or else you will never see that title with your name on it. [Ben's holding me tight in the cobra clutch which is causing me to breath slower, and if that wasn't enough Larry is now slapping me in the face. I try to force myself from the hold, but all I do is waste energy as Ben drags me out of the locker room. As we near the ring the color of my face begins changing due to the lack of oxygen I'm receiving. While holding me in the hold he lifts me up to get me in the ring, but I catch him around the neck and pull him down instead for a front face DDT to hit my setup move "Diamondized". I quickly turn to look at him down on the ground with a smirk on my face as I start to catch my breath.] Donny: See what happens when you underestimate... [Before I can even finish my sentence I fall to my back as I feel something knock me across my thighs. As I look up I notice Larry is there with a kendo stick in his hands. He knocks it across the floor close to my head, purposely missing me as both Ben and I grab hold of the ring apron to bring ourselves back to our feet.] Donny: Since when did you start getting involved here Larry? That shit hurt! Larry: Well remember last week when you asked about Shaman? Well at the time Shaman wasn't a problem, but since you like to chew off more than you can eat he has become a problem. Because of your constant interference in Shaman's GCW business you can bet your money on the fact that he will sign himself up for a fight against Mayhem during Rampage. Donny: Like I care what Shaman signs himself up for. You think I'm just going to stand there and let that sneak attack go without consequences? He has to pay for what he did to me! Besides it's not like Shaman has the talent to actually beat Mayhem, he had to carry Shaman like dead weight the entire time time they were together. Ben: Sneak attack...Yeah... No matter what you classify it as Donny, the fact is you have managed to back yourself into another Bishop Steele situation. Even if you do have what it takes to beat Mayhem you need to remember that there is no rules here. The entire time the match is going on you will need to make sure to keep an eye out for any possible conflicts from Shaman. Larry: Now the two of you get your asses into the ring so we can start some proper preparation for this match. [Pushing my self up onto the ring apron I sit for a second before rolling inside from under the bottom rope. Before I'm able to get back to my feet Larry presses the kendo stick down onto my neck holding me down as Ben makes it into the ring and grabs me by the ankles. Just as Larry releases me, Ben drags me into the middle of the ring. Before he's able to release me, I use my legs to pull him in and hook him almost in a complete Gogoplata. He's able to pass his arm inside the hold preventing me from locking it completely in.] Larry: During this match even though it's not in the ring you will have to remember that you will be on your back most of the time due to the size difference. So instead of training you for technical skill this week, you need to work on your submissions because there is no way your going to be able to lift him up for a tombstone piledriver. Ben: And using the Gogoplata is a bad idea for this match, because its a great opening for the Oblivion Bomb. [Right as those words come out of Ben's mouth he pushes up and begins lifting me into the air for a power bomb. As he lifts me up I release the hold on his arm and pull myself around his leg where I hook him around the ankle and start elbowing him in the back of the knee. He stumbles forward where I'm able to grapevine my legs around his, tripping him down onto the mat and I'm able to properly hook in the ankle lock.] Ben: Damn this shit hurts! Come on let go already! [I release him from the hold and he hobbles up to his feet using the ring ropes. A grin crosses my face as I see Ben actually feeling some pain from that submission hold, but it soon fades as I notice Larry starring at me from outside of the ring. To catch my breath for a moment I lean back against the turnbuckles as I listen to what the old man has to say.] Larry: While you're on the right track with chaining the submissions, a leg submission will not likely make Mayhem tap out. And taking his size into factor once again, he could still attack you with his free leg. No... You need a much stronger submission hold to beat him with. Donny: I think I have the answer for that. Come on Ben! Unless you're going to let that little ankle lock be the end of you. [Looking over to Larry I laugh a bit until Ben meets me with a kick to the stomach. He pulls me into a front head lock and lifts me into the air for a suplex, but I quickly slip around him once more and hook him into a dragon sleeper. My legs wrap around his waist and he quickly falls back to the mat to try and shake me off, instead it allows me to lock it in deeper as I roll onto my side. Just as the color of Ben's face begins to change I release the hold and climb back up to my feet where I give him a helping hand.] Donny: My "Forever Night" submission will surely choke him out. Even more so since it takes more energy for someone his size to move around. Ben: Nice move, but did you really need to lock it in so tight? Larry: Nice! I see you have been paying attention to everything we've been teaching you here. Donny: Hey now Shaman isn't the only person who can read books on human anatomy to better his wrestling career. [We all share a common laugh as Larry tosses us some bottles of water. Stepping out onto the ring apron I take a seat, sipping the water from the bottle. Over the gym's door I notice Larry has a replica of the PTC Global title from when I won it so many years ago. Looking at it I can't help but smile a bit as I know I'm only one match away from becoming Champion again.] Ben: So what's it like? Donny: Wrestling? Ben: Being a Champion. I've followed the careers of so many different people, helped some break into the business even. Out of them all though I never met someone with such a high profile like you. Donny: It has to be the greatest feeling in the world. To know that after all of the injuries, all of the countless hours of training, and all of the effort to get there you finally have something to show for it. It demands respect from the fans, from your peers, from everyone. And even if it's not the top gold in the company, the honor and respect is still there, because not everyone has what it takes to be a Champion. Ben: So that's why you made this thing with Shaman so personal right? Because he has no respect for you and for what you have done in your career. Donny: No. Because in GCW past accomplishments don't mean jack, and to earn respect in GCW you have to be Champion. But some times that isn't enough... I mean I have tons of respect for Bryan Mayhem, for Vivica Valentine, even for Tempest and all of them are Champions. But some times it takes a lot more than a belt for that to be earned, I mean take a look at Andy Murray. The self-proclaimed "King" of GCW I have no respect for trash like that, I have more respect for AXEL Action's dog than I do for him. Ben: But he is one of the most acclaimed talents GCW has. How can you not respect someone like that? Donny: Because respect has to be demanded! Vivica is the perfect example of someone demanding respect, because she made sure that everyone remembers Murray as being her personal bitch. Ben: Then what's the deal with Shaman? Donny: Shaman believes that the time I spent dealing with Bishop Steele was a waste of time for GCW. Shaman demands respect from me, and there is no better way to show respect than to show Shaman I will not back down from him. But when Rampage is over and I am holding Mayhem's title high in the air it will be me who is then demanding respect from Shaman! Ben: Yeah that is surely shaping up to be a really nice pay-per-view for GCW. The fans for Rampage are going to be on fire with everything that is planned for the event. Who you think has what it takes to win the Rampage tournament? Donny: I have had a real blessing in my career to travel the world and face every type of person you could imagine... Well maybe not someone like Rasa but no one is like Rasa... But I never faced a woman with as much discipline and desire like Tempest has. She has amazing talent and has what it takes to win this thing, and besides how can I go against someone who beat the living piss out of SurReal? Well I better get going for now. Thanks for the session guys, I'm going in as Donny Diamond, but thanks to you two I'm coming out as Champion! [I push off the apron and toss the water bottle into the trashcan and make the dunking motion as it falls in. Shaking the hands of Ben and Larry I thank them again before heading to the door to go home. Before I can place my hand on the doorknob it opens and a woman walks in. The outside wind has blown some of her raven locks into her face, and after brushing them away with her hand she tilts her head up. I drop my bags to the floor as I'm shocked to see the woman standing before me.] Donny: ...Katrina...? - Some times in life you stop doing things because you feel like there is nothing left to achieve. Then as the years pass you tell endless stories for endless hours of your fondest memories. As more time goes by you start to wonder if you still have what it takes to do it all over again. Can you still hang with a new generation of hopefuls? So you decide to give it another chance. Then your hit with defeat and you start to question if your time of being great has passed, but you decide to not give up. You understand that until you get back into the swing of things there will be set backs and people will try to talk you into quitting. Some may even tell you that old dogs can't be shown new tricks, but like the mean dog you are you bark back and keep fighting forward. Use their negativity as fuel and push yourself even harder than before. Everyone deserves a chance at being happy, and now with a chance to reach glory one more time you know your chance at happiness is there waiting for you to reach for it!
