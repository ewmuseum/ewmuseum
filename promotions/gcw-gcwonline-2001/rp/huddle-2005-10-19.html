Huddle

19 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 19, 2005 Varsity’s hotel room. It’s small, quaint, but good enough to serve its purpose. One of the newest members of Global Championship Wrestling is currently on the floor doing his morning exercises. Music is blasting in the background, something from the local radio station probably but it sounds like a rip-off of Breaking Benjamin’s latest hit. Who knows. The important thing is that Varsity’s keeping himself in shape. The muscles in his chest and arms contract and expand with each push-up, since he’s shirtless. His black sweatpants are blessed with a Philadelphia Eagles patch, showing his loyalty to his home team, whilst he finishes up this set. It takes him a few moments, but soon he’s done and rolling over onto his back to let his breathing regulate. “Yo! V!” The room’s door slams closed and enter the Styles. As usual, he’s dressed to impress, all the way down to his sneakers and what not. He’s got some paperwork in his hands and sunglasses on his face. He moves right on over to the kitchen, tossing the folder on the counter and proceeds to help himself to some orange juice. “Hey Styles.” Varsity utters, craning his head back some to look at his friend. Styles finishes his glass and sinks it before turning to address his client. “Two things. One” What the hell are you doing on the floor?” Varsity rolls his eyes some and sits up, getting to his feet. “Working out.” Styles nods a bit and snatches up the folder again, moving to take a seat somewhere safe, like the sofa. “... Right. I forgot you do that. So. Number two. Title shot.” Varsity’s right in the middle of sipping some water when he has to cough and shake his head. “What”!” Styles flashes a smile and holds out the folder. “Title shot. This week. All you, baby. Am I the best manager in the world or what?” Varsity’s caps the water bottle and slides it under his arm. He starts flipping through the paperwork in the folder, skimming and reading and all of that good stuff, at the same time. “How’d you...”” “Skills.” “But this is my...” “First match.” “And it’s a...” “Title shot.” “And you did this how?” “Skills.” Styles chuckles a bit and hops up to his feet, moving over to Varsity and throwing his arm around his best buddy. “V, listen. Relax. This is cake, man. Chocolate cake, with chocolate ice cream and no dinner beforehand. All you gotta” do is win and we’re on our way.” Varsity flips the folder closed and hands it over to Styles. “It’s not the winning, I’m worried about. It’s the drama. I mean, I’m the new guy. I haven’t even got settled in yet and I’m already goin” for the gold?” Styles chuckles and holds up his hands. “Whoa. Slow down, V. It’s not that simple. Peep game...” Leaning up against the back of the sofa Styles explains, “It’s gold, yes, but it ain’t the gold we want. This is the TV title. When you take this from Mikey” You’re gonna? have to put this thing on the line every damn week.” Varsity winces in mock pain. “I don’t like the sound of this...” Styles smirks, “That’s right. You love the sound of this. Cuz this is exactly what we need to make you Rookie of the Year, my man. Think about it. Varsity comes into the GCW, his first ever major federation. He completely dominates The Machine... taking the Television Title Belt in his first match in the major leagues. After that he goes on to a gripping television title reign that makes Julius Ceaser look like Bill Cosby.” Varsity chuckles and shakes his head. “You’re insane, you know that?” Styles laughs. “I’m serious, though. This is opportunity, V. This is our time. You’ve been down in the little league so long you forgot that you were born to be the main event, baby. Who were you in high school” The Man. Who were you in Pee Wee football? The Man. Who were you in college? The Man. Who were you in Summer League football? The Man. Who you gonna? be in GCW?” Varsity and Styles both answer this one, “The Man!” The two friends slap hi-fives and Styles pulls his cell phone off his hip. “Now, go... shower... get dressed and meet me downstairs in twenty. I went and picked up the rental early this mornin” and it’s a Benzino. So we GOTSTA go cruisin? in this thing. The honeys’ll be all over us.” Varsity rolls his eyes, heading towards the balcony doors. “Styles. Are money and girls the only things you ever think about?” he teases. Styles pulls open the door and gasps in playful shock. “... Of course not! I also think about sex.” He winks and disappears out the door. “20 Minutes!” As the door slams, Varsity chuckles to himself, pulling open the sliding door and steps out onto the small balcony. He breathes in the fresh air, letting the cool air of the city cool him down from the earlier workout. This is it, Alex. Your debut. Your WorldWide debut, in more ways than one. You can do this. All you have to do is play to win. Don’t worry about the fact that Mike Savige is undefeated. Don’t worry about the fact that you’re the new guy and everyone’s expecting you to choke. You’re Alex Winters. You went All City and All State four years in a row. You don’t choke. You don’t lose. You win. Varsity sighs, feeling better after his internal pep talk and a small smile creeps across his lips. He whispers softy, “Put me in, Coach. I’m ready to play.”
