The Trial

undated

Date: Unknown Now, where do we begin from the last story? Ah yes, the trial. You know, this was probably the most emotional part of the story for me. At this point in time I realized what I had done, I realized that I was going to go to jail and suffer for what I had done. The scene opens in a large courtroom filled with members of the Mason Family and some of the Jackson family, many of the Jackson’s were far too ashamed to come and watch a relative of theirs be sentenced to murder. All of Aiden’s brothers arrived there in support of him although they knew what had happened and secretly they had wished they were the one holding the end of that knife. “Everyone in the court please be seated and we will get this underway” the Judge said loudly after he pounded his gavel to the table. “Today, we are here to witness the accounts of several suspects in the murder of Michael Mason, a father to 3 children, and a loving husband.” the judge said. “Would the defendant please rise and take the stand.” the Judge said. I rose from my seat and walked up to the stand, placing my hand on the bible and swearing to tell the whole truth and nothing but the truth. I sat in the seat which was slightly uncomfortable and cold. I felt as though I had an audience with everyone staring at me, some crying and some just glaring at me. Instantly the prosecution had begun. “Mr. Jackson did you or did you not consider yourself a friend to Mr. Mason?” he said to me firmly. “You know, you don’t have to do that did you nor did you not crap, just ask me if he was my friend. No, I was not friends with him but I did associate myself to their family.” I replied. “No need to get smart, you associated yourself with the family how?” he said to me. “I went over there for dinner; I was friends with Mrs. Mason. She is a librarian, and a very intelligent woman.” I said looking at her. “We know what Mrs. Mason is. If you adored Mrs. Mason so much then why did you murder her husband, the love of her life?” He said to me. “Because I was seeking vengeance for my parents, for the people that that man stole from me. He took them from my life; he took them from the lives of me and my brothers. We grew up without parents, now I’ve got a question for you. Do you know what it is like to wish that you had a mom to tuck you in at night, to kiss you on the forehead, a father to tell you he loved you and that he was so proud of you for whatever you did, did you have that did you”!” I screamed at him. “It is none of your business about my family matters, but for the record I did have a mother and father. The fact is you stole the life of a man, and you stole the man from the woman he loved.” He replied to me. “What did you do to that man that night” Please give us all of the details.” He then followed his statement up with. “I’d rather not.” I replied to him. “Jury, please note that the defendant is not willing to give his account of the night that Mr. Michael Mason was murdered.” He said. “Listen, jackass, everyone in this room already knows that I murdered Michael Mason. Why do I have to go into details” Hell, why don’t you just haul me off to jail right now?” I said firmly looking at the man. “Judge?” he said as he looked up to the Judge. “Continue with your questioning, this man has a point.” he replied. “I have no further questioning your honor.” He said looking back up at the judge. “Alright, Aiden Jackson, please take your seat next to your lawyer.” The judge said to Aiden. “I would like to call Mrs. Georgia Mason to the stand for questioning.” my lawyer rose saying to the old woman who looked slightly distressed. She rose from her seat and walked slowly to the stand, agreeing to her vows of honesty over the Holy Bible. She sat down softly, her eyes red looking as though she was a raccoon from crying, she stared directly at me. At this moment, I felt remorse for her, I DID regret that I murdered him because I stole the love of her life from her’the past cannot be changed though and I have to live with my actions, I mean it’s only fair right? He DID kill my parents and rob me of a life for I don’t know how many years. “Mrs. Mason did you or did you not know that your husband had murdered the parents of Aiden Jackson?” He questioned her. She hesitated then began to speak, her lip quivered as she talked and you could sense the nervousness and sadness in her voice. “Y..yyy..eee’sss.” She said to him as she shook softly. “When did your husband tell you this?” he followed up. She finally gained her composure to talk and began to speak. “Listen, I understand that my husband was a murderer but that’s not something that’s going to bring him back is it” Why do I have to answer this question? What does this have to do with his murder? I want justice!” she began screaming at my lawyer. “Ma’am it is only a question that I asked you please calm down. The question makes perfect sense and this questioning makes perfect sense considering this is the reason that your husband was murdered.” He said to her firmly. “Okay, He told me around 2 years after the murder, I don’t know if he could have gotten up the heart to tell me before then. We were in huge financial debt and we needed money, unfortunately he killed for the money that’s how desperate he was.” She said. “Well, How did he escape the cops?” the lawyer questioned. “That is what I am not sure of, I mean he never really told me he just explained to me that he had killed to prosper us, I was quite disgusted with him but eventually it was past me and you know” I just moved on with my life, which was all I could do. As a matter of fact, I was actually scared to leave him, scared he would kill me but eventually all of that rotted into the back of my mind.” She said. “Did you know the name of the people he murdered?” he said. “No I didn’t know the names of the people he murdered.” She said. “You mean to say that you had total disregard for the lives that he stole away that night?” He questioned. “Well’No..I mean yes’I mean..I don’t know.” She said. “I have no further questioning.” The lawyer said. Hours later of questioning debating and etc. the sentence finally came. I laid my head into my hands and I was breathing heavily as my brother patted me on the back from behind. I was nervous knowing that I was going to go to jail, there was no doubt that this jury had found me guilty. The question for me was how long, would I have a shot at life after this murder? “The jury has reached their verdict please rise Aiden Jackson.” The representative of the jury said. “We the jury find you guilty on all charges.” He then followed up. “The sentencing for the crimes committed with taken consideration into the motive will be 20 years in a maximum security prison.” The Judge said following what the jury representative had said. I thought to myself, I would be forty by the time that I got out of prison, I would not be able to live a life’Look what you’ve done to yourself you’ve ruined the life that you could have had. “Brother, be strong for the lord will guide you; remember that my brother, we will see you soon I promise this isn’t over you’ll get out trust me.” Jesse whispered this into my ear as I was cuffed and they began to walk me away. The truth was I had no faith in the lord at this point in time, he had gotten me into the biggest mess of the world or so I had thought I was wrong though, God was dealing out my punishment for my crime, I thought I was punished enough when he took my parents away by fate. But, I was driven by the motive of an eye for an eye and I murdered, I should be losing my life right now so maybe I should be thankful that the lord has served me justice. I feel as though I am in a confused state not sure whether to trust in the lord or to betray him once again and walk in the light of the devil. Only time will tell, only time will show which path I will walk. The End of “The Trial” [fade] A wise man by the name of Winston Churchill once said “We shall show mercy, but we shall not be shown it.” I feel as though that quote defines me to this day, I now show mercy but I will never be shown mercy. Why won’t I be shown mercy? I won’t be shown mercy from this relentless world because I once murdered, and I found my way out. How did I find my way out? That’s another story for another day, the fact of the matter is that I am here in Core Wrestling, the fact is you guys have a man who murdered in your federation, does that strike fear into your body? You may say it doesn’t but the truth is I often scare myself, so I must scare some other people. “I tell you the truth, if you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there' and it will move” ? Matthew 17:20 Maybe, just maybe that will be enough for you to believe that I do indeed walk the hallways of Global Championship Wrestling, as a murderer, an acclaimed nutcase, and ultimately a man coming to destroy everyone in his path, the man who was in my path was Enigma, the future of Enigma is only a second away from you all and I hope you wait around long enough for that. I in fact do trust in my lord now, knowing that he blessed me and got me through the toughest time of my life, but like I’ve said once and I’ll say it again’Different story for a different day.
