Date: 8/10/06 written for WorldWide - Clark County, Nevada, November 2005 - Day One It was only a couple of short weeks when it was announced that Jeff would be sent to prison for the rest of his life due to the murder of Simon Caine, this left Jeff heartbroken as he either wanted to get off or be given the chair/lethal injection. He had only just been transferred to the Clark County jail in Nevada, Jeff’s home state. It was here where the police picked Jeff up after he had wanted to make a quick visit to pick up some things before he goes on the run, but with the police waiting there for him he didn’t get very far at all. Jeff is now stood in a line of convicts, handing in their valuables to a guard behind a desk. Jeff left most of his valuables at the house prior to being sent to jail, it was decided that if he was never going to see them again then he'd let someone else with access to his home have them. Jeff gave the keys to his house to Shawn at the court hearing. There was no point wearing much jewelry considering he had heard rumours that the guards only take them for themselves. Jeff looked down the line and saw a guard holding a shotgun, the guard was looking back at Jeff, he had noticed that the guard hadn't taken his eyes off of him since first laying his eyes upon him, Jeff squinted his eyes back at him and looked in front of him at the rest of the convicts trying not to start an incident, the guard stood about six seven and weighed around the same as Jeff except he had a lot more body fat then Jeff did. Behind Jeff stood a man around five six, looked like a small guy, he had short, spiked brown hair and was trying to get a look at Jeff's face, he kept looking either side of Jeff, he then stood out of line for a moment, looked at Jeff's face and got back in line after, he then began grinning from ear to ear. One thing Jeff was mainly worried about was being recognized, he had wrestled all over the world, there may be guys inside the prison maybe looking for a fight so it gives them something to brag about when they got out of prison. Jeff was by no means looking for a fight, but he had always been the type of guy never to back down from a fight. “You're Jeff Harris aren't you?” The man behind Jeff asked. Jeff took a look behind him and looked the man up and down, he seemed about 25 at the most, Jeff turned away from the man and continued looking to the front. “I used to be, now I'm just a number like the rest of everyone else in this hell hole.” He said. “Nah man, you'll never be just a number, I've seen you before in wrestling shows, FAW, Bloodbox, that one time appearance for Lights Out Wrestling, xCw, hell I even saw your debut match with WFW against Jay Moss where you took the Canadian title, your world title victory against Marsham in FWF, I've been to a lot of your shows man.” The man said. Jeff had been recognized already, it was like meeting his greatest fan or an obsessed fan, there wasn't much room for debate on that aspect, Jeff just smiled. “You've seen a lot of my matches” Well... good on you.” He said. “Yeah man, you totally fucking kicked ass. What are you in here for anyway” Little surprised to see you here.” He asked. “I crushed Simon Caine with an 18 wheeler in order to save the integrity of Bloodbox, little did I know the company shut down weeks later, it kind of make it a pointless task but it was a task that had to be done. So I've got life.” Jeff replied. “Ah that sucks man, I was falsely accused of murdering my girlfriend.” The man said. “Falsely accused” I guess a lot of murderers say that.” Jeff grunted back. “Nah dude, I know who it was, while I was away training to be a wrestler she always went out to some other dudes house and was fucking him on the side, you know how it is.” The man exclaimed. “So you think it was him that killed her?” Jeff asked, almost pretending to be interested. “I know it was him that killed her, that's why I killed him, although the police don't know that.” The man answered, “Name's Tony Cornell by the way.” Jeff smiled slightly, “So you were training to be a wrestler?” Jeff asked. “Yeah man, I know I'm not a big guy, always wished I was at least six three or something but I guess I'm doomed to be a cruiserweight for the rest of my life, it's cool though, copied some of your moves a bit actually, used the XTF as my finisher, it's a brutal move, you should have used it more often.” Tony replied. “I can get more damage done with the Chain Reaction, wouldn't you be serving a life sentence if you're in here?” Jeff asked. “No, I claimed temporary insanity, next thing I know I've got ten years. I guess it'll be a good gimmick or something once I get out of here, what about you?” Tony answered and asked. It was at the moment that Jeff knew he should have gotten a different lawyer, although Jeff wasn't temporarily insane, he was at the very least, insane. He'd rather spend the rest of his days in a mental institute than a prison, it was too late to cry about it now though and so Jeff pushed that thought to the depths of his mind where the rest of the rubbish is. It was also at this moment he felt like ignoring Tony, but it seemed like this was going to be the most normal conversation he was going to have. “I have life, it's a fucking joke, Caine deserved to die.” Jeff replied. “True that man, true that.” As Jeff got the desk he pulled out his wallet and set it down in front of him, he then took off two gold rings he had on his left hand, he then took out a picture of his son and his ex wife and put it down in front of him with the rest of the stuff. The only possessions Jeff brought him with him, Jeff turned around for a moment, noticing the guard was still eyeballing him. “Two gold rings, one black wallet with... 47 dollars inside, you can keep the picture.” The guard behind the desk said. Jeff picked up the photo and put it back into his jeans pocket. The guard then put his belongings in an envelope labelled “82342 Jeffrey James Harrison”. “That your son man?” Tony asked. “That's right.” Jeff answered. “Cool dude, I've seen him in the ring before too, not live or anything, he's a great wrestler, he'll do you proud I think.” Said Tony nodding his head at the same time. “Quiet in line!” The guard who had been eyeballing Jeff shouted. Jeff turned his head and glared at the guard, he then turned back to the desk, the guard walked up behind Jeff and growled at him, “You got something to say you fucking little bitch?” Jeff just stood there, his back turned to the guard, biting his lip Jeff signed a piece of paper for his things. “I didn't think so faggot.” “Hey man, you can't talk to him like that, do you know who that is” That's Jeff fucking Harris that's who, show some respect fucker!” Tony yelled, the guard turned his attention to Tony, smiled at him and then smashed his nightstick into the side of his face, busting Tony's lip wide open. He picked Tony up by the throat and stared into his eyes. “You ever talk to me like that again, I'll have no reservations in making you someones bitch, you understand me maggot” Your life here will be a living hell.” The guard growled at him, he then went to hit him in the face with the nightstick again but was stopped, Jeff had stuck his hand out and grabbed the nightstick, the guard yanked it off of him and turned his attention to Jeff again. “That was a big mistake boy.” The guard went to hit Jeff with the nightstick, Jeff grabbed the nightstick before it hit him, took it off him and punched him in the face, he then hit him in the side of the head with nightstick knocking him to the ground. Another guard came at him, Jeff hit him in the face with a big boot knocking him down, he then slammed the nightstick into his throat, the rest of the inmates remained calm as they didn't want to attract attention on the first day, an alarm went off as guards came running from both sides of the hall. Jeff hit a superkick to one of them then hit another in the face with the nightstick coming down the other way. Jeff took the shotgun off of the guard he first knocked down and hit one guard with the blunt end of it, he aimed it at three guards who stopped in their tracks, sensing their were people behind him, Jeff threw back an elbow hitting a guard in the face, he quickly turned around and smashed him in the face with the shotgun, he then dropped the gun and hit another guard with a spear. The guards quickly got in formation and ganged up on Jeff, hitting him with nightsticks, Tony Cornell spat blood on the floor and got to his feet, he ran up behind one guard and hit him with a back cracker. Tony got up and punched another guard who was obviously bigger than he was in the face, the guard unphased went to swing back, Tony ducked and hit another punch to the mouth before being finally caught, the guard grabbed him by the throat and began choking him, Jeff fought his way up taking two nightsticks off of two guards and hitting them with them, before Jeff knew it however he was being shot in the back by two taser guns which soon began pumping electricity into him. Three more guards then shoot him with taser guns as well, the tall guard got back to his feet and ordered the guards to stop. He stood over the angered Jeff, who couldn't move due to his muscles still convulsing. “Welcome to Clark County bitch.” The guard said before hitting Jeff hard in the face with a boot. The next thing Jeff saw, was darkness. Day Fourteen After spending the past fourteen days in solitary confinement, Jeff hoped that his scene was enough to give him the death penalty, it wasn't however. Solitary wasn't really a punishment for Jeff though as he spent most of his life alone. After being forced to leave his son Shawn and wife Stephanie he spent a long time being alone. Even before being thrown in prison, with his son rejecting him as a father and Masterson, Solomon and Thunder all moving on with their lives, Jeff had been alone after Bloodbox had ended. All Jeff kept thinking of was that he was never going to train at his gym again, never sleep in his own bed, see his favourite prostitute, never see his friends, he missed Adam Masterson the most. Jeff also had been sat there looking at his photo of Shawn and Stephanie and also thinking back to the glory days of wrestling, just running his matches and interviews through his head. Now that Jeff thought about it, he wished he cherished it a lot more in his later days, he never expected it to be gone so quickly. The last thing Jeff knew was being in front of thousands of people, being the owner of Bloodbox if only for a short time and being the last ever World Champion, now he's sat on a dirty floor with an uncomfortable bed, no longer a multi-millionaire, just inmate 82342 Harris. Jeff had not long woken up, it was quiet just how he liked he, he then spent some time doing push-ups, Jeff got to his feet and walked over to the sink and took a drink of water he cupped in his hands, he heard keys jangling outside of the door, he knew that his solitary was about to come to an end, he was being released into the wild, as he hears an unlocking sound, the door is pulled open and one of the guards he hit with the nightstick is there, still with bruises on his face. “Come on hero, time to get you settled into your new home.” The guard said as Jeff looked on disappointed. Jeff got to his feet and began walking towards the door, knowing it was time to meet the rest of the inmates, Jeff felt like cracking the guard in the face so he could be thrown back in solitary, he decided not to though and used it as a future option. Jeff walked through the halls and could hear the rowdy prisoners already, if Jeff wanted to be in a cell with anyone it'd be Tony Cornell, the guy backed Jeff up in the fight he had 14 days ago. Jeff and the guard then walked into the main part of the prison where all the cells are, the other prisoners looked at Jeff and gasped at how big he was compared to them, not to mention the visible scars all over his face and body. Jeff was taken to the fourth level, walked down a walkway and was stopped outside a cell, the man inside the cell was quite short, greasy black hair, looked like a redneck just from appearance, the guard opened the door. “Hey Billy, got you a new roommate. Enjoy.” The guard said. “No, no, no, I don't need anyone else in this place, it's small enough as it is boy. Take this little fucker away.” Billy shot back. “Tough shit redneck.” The guard responded, he then pushed Jeff into the cell and locked the door behind him. Jeff looked around the cell for a moment before looking at Billy who was laid on the top bunk. “The fuck you looking at son?” Billy asked aggressively. Billy Shaw was the type of guy that no one messed with that often unless they had the power to back themselves up. Billy was known worldly for having money and paying those people that stuck close to him, he also made it his business to rape various young inmates with the help of said friends, little did Jeff know that Billy had been picking on Tony for the first week before ultimately gang raping him in the showers, Jeff squinted his eyes at Billy. “Yeah you better take that glare elsewhere before you become one of my many bitches, you understand?” Jeff growled, grabbed Billy by the throat and pulled him from the top bunk, he then threw him against the wall, “I don't care who you thought you were or who you thought you are, but you piss me off little man and I'll fuck you up, do you understand that” I'm here for a really long time, killing you would be no sweat off my dick.” Jeff said to him quietly so the guards outside his cell didn't hear, Billy nodded his head, “Good. Top bunks mine.” Jeff said, he then threw him on the bottom bunk before jumping up to the top bunk. With Jeff already signifying who the alpha male in the cell was, Jeff laid their staring at the ceiling, Billy who was still laying on the bottom bunk, flipped Jeff off and rest on the bottom bunk. Jeff knew that he wasn't going to get along with Billy at all, he didn't want to either, in Jeff's mind, he wasn't a criminal he was a mercenary, but someone like Billy Shaw must have been something terrible, if Jeff found out about Tony Cornell, he'd probably take Billy's life right there and then, Jeff was anxious to see him thinking him as the only thing close to normal in the entire prison. It was now that Jeff was beginning what was to be, a very long stretch. Never again would he see the outside world, Jeff couldn't get the thought out of his mind that it was day 14, only another 6000 or so days to go. Still it could have been worse, he could have been in South Carolina when he got arrested. - Clark County, Nevada, April 2006 - Day One Hundred Sixty Four It had been two days since Neu Generation came to visit Jeff and give him the news of WFW Encore. In that time, Billy Shaw had returned to his cell keeping well away from Jeff, Billy was the type of guy who never knew when to shut up, he had to take something just that little bit further, usually he had people to back him up, but that didn't work with Jeff. Billy had never seen anyone quite like Jeff before, he was an animal, Jeff's time in Bloodbox turned him into a raving psychopath, the years of wrestling must have taken a toll on his mind, in Bloodbox he was nothing short of a fierce war machine, losing only one match to Ian Cross, some say it was a very lucky victory and was unable to defeat Jeff again after that. Jeff had captured only the one championship in Bloodbox also, the World Championship, he was undefeated and retired in a manner fitting of a championship, on top. Now Jeff was to return to the ring thanks to his step brother, facing Adam Masterson and Theodoric Solomon in a triple threat match gave Jeff chills, to face two of his greatest friends in the ring was something special. No one anywhere had ever seen a match between Jeff Harris and Adam Masterson, Adam Masterson and Theodoric Solomon or Theo Solomon and Jeff Harris. Many fans had wondered for a long time if Masterson and Harris were ever going to feud, after Masterson quit the first time in WFW, he blasted Jeff over the head with a steel chair and wasn't seen for a few months, Masterson eventually returned and they rekindled their friendship when many thought that they'd be feuding. Over the years Adam and Jeff became the best of friends, Jeff had even moved down to Florida when they were both in DWA so they could train together for matches, they had often joked that a Jeff Harris/Adam Masterson match would draw huge ratings and could make a promotion a lot of money. It wasn't just the fact that Jeff was facing him that made him so excited, it was the fact that he missed his brother in arms. Theodoric Solomon was the same, Jeff and Theo became better friends in DWA when Jeff asked him to join Dangerous Intentions, Theo jumped at the chance and became a major player for the company, many might have seen this match as a mismatch of styles with Jeff being quite a bit bigger than the other two, but Masterson and Solomon were fierce in their own ways, all three men were technically sound. There was only one thing Jeff could have wanted added to this match and that was the addition of Jimmy Thunder, it turned out that Thunder had turned down the chance to be in a match with his three greatest friends due to the simple fact that he was only in WFW for a few weeks before it closed and felt that he didn't truly signify the true essence of WFW. Jeff, Adam and Theo weren't the only competitors in the Dangerous Intentions faction that were facing each other, as Anson Drake was to take on long time friend “The Chosen One” Seth Stevens in a one on one match, if there was one dream match Jeff could have hoped for it would have been Theodoric Solomon, Adam Masterson, Jeff Harris and Jimmy Thunder taking on Nick Kelly, Seth Stevens, Anson Drake and Post. Jeff could barely sleep since Neu Generation visited him a couple of days ago, it took 162 days for someone to actually visit Jeff and when someone finally did, it turned out to be his nemesis. He had at the very least expected Rachel Ryan to visit him, he didn't blame anyone else for not visiting him though as he knew that they all had their own problems, Jeff wasn't going anywhere any time soon, he had thought about making a run for it after the WFW show, doing nothing all day does give a man a lot of opportunity to think about these things. But he knew the prison warden wasn't just going to let him out unsupervised. Jeff didn't have anywhere to run to even if he did make a run for it, there was no way he'd be able to get out of the country undetected, another factor was Jeff's age, he was becoming an old man, he had spent his 38th birthday inside, March 10th, he didn't tell anyone but he did get a packet of smokes off of Tony Cornell who knew his birthday anyway just from looking it up on the internet. Jeff was grateful to have someone he could talk about wrestling with inside, most people were either into football, baseball or other dumb American sports, Jeff has yet to learn about Billy “Six Inch” having his men hold down Tony while he raped him in his cell, Tony was going to tell him but thought it wouldn't have accomplished anything, Tony felt it was his fault why Jeff was thrown in solitary in the first place and didn't want to cause him anymore bother. Tony decided to put the whole thing behind him, he was safe now at least with the fact that Billy now knows about their friendship, Billy wasn't scared of many people in the prison because if there was one man he did fear it was Jeff, Jeff was in for life, Billy knew that, Billy also knew Jeff wished he had been given the chair, if Jeff was going to kill Billy, it wouldn't be any skin off of Jeff's nose, it was because of that reason that he kept his distance. Jeff spent every spare moment he had since seeing Neu training for his match, over the past 160 days or so Jeff had become lax on his training schedule, WFW Encore gave him a resurgence, it gave him a desire to get back into shape, it was like he was forever doing pushups, running the steps in the prison cell block, sprinting along the field outside, a lot of of the prisoners had wondered why he was trying to keep in shape, some were already told by their visitors, who happened to be avid wrestling fans, Jeff spoke to no one, no one apart from Tony Cornell who often trained with him. As the day raged on, the blistering heat was getting too much for some people, Jeff wanted to stay inside but was told to get some fresh air, Jeff stepped outside of the prison into the yard and began stretching as he planned to do a lot of running, Tony Cornell ran up to him and nodded to him. “Going for a run?” Tony asked optimistically. “Yeah, the screws made me step outside so I might as well do something while I'm out here.” Jeff answered. Before anymore could be said, Billy Shaw and his crew came walking up to them. “God damn boy, can't believe you're outside for a change, maybe you can hang around with us.” Billy said to Jeff. “Sure.” Jeff responded. “...Really?” Billy asked, shocked. “My God, you rednecks are slower than a spelling bee full of stutterers.” Jeff replied, obviously being sarcastic, Billy looked disappointed, one of his men glared evilly at Jeff, Jeff looked at him back, unimpressed. “You do not want to go down that road with me again, trust me.” Jeff said sadistically, the con then looked away thinking better of it. “Yeah well, didn't want to hang around with a goth anyway.” Billy said, he then turned to Tony, “Hey hot cheeks. I'll be seeing you later.” Billy said, before winking and walking off, the rest of his posse followed him. Tony stood there looking angry for a second. “What was that about?” Jeff asked. “Nothing man, forget it, let's do some training.” Tony replied. “If anyone causes you any trouble in this place, you come to me alright?” Jeff asked him, almost taking the role of a father figure. Tony nodded his head. “Hold on a second.” Jeff said. Jeff then walked over to Billy, pushing his friends out of the way to get to him, he spun Billy around and gripped him tightly by the throat. “You know, if I ever find out that you did something to Cornell, make no mistake about it, I will kill you and the rest of these anal raping faggots. Understood?” Jeff exclaimed quietly, trying to keep his voice down, Billy nodded his head, Jeff let go. “Don't worry son, never touched your boyfriend.” Billy answered, he then walked off briskly, Jeff walked back over to Tony and nodded to him. Jeff knew at that point that something was up, something that might have gone down a long time ago, Jeff made it his business to keep his eye on Billy since the first day he moved into his cell, Jeff had always despised rapists, when Jeff saw someone being violated in that way he went ballistic, this was the case when his ex-wife was attacked and raped four years ago when Jeff trying searching for Shawn. To rape another person is different from most crimes, it burns that image into their mind, it burns the feelings they felt into their subconscious, Jeff had seen people commit suicide for being raped, it wasn't just women either, it was men as well, Jeff knew this sort of thing happened in most prisons, Jeff wasn't about to make himself the superhero of the prison, he was done helping people, especially where it got him the last time he helped someone out. After the reunion show Jeff knows he'd be going back to prison to spend the rest of his life doing absolutely nothing with his life, he couldn't wait for what would essentially be his official retirement match, little did he know Masterson and Theodoric were also retiring after the match, it was going to be something special to be sure. Jeff wasn't about to commit suicide over the fact that he'd never see his friends again, Jeff never quit in anything he did, if Jeff was have his life taken away, it wouldn't be by his hands. WFW Encore would be Jeff's one last chance to truly shine before fading away forever. Jeff was ready to begin running, he turned to Tony. “You ready?” Jeff asked. “You bet I am dude.” Tony replied, “Let's do this, we need to get you in tip top shape before your match.” Jeff smiled, maybe for the first time in over six months, the two then began doing laps around the field as the sun blazed on.
