This is me, for forever

28 March 2006

Date: 03/28/2006 Easing the door to her bedroom shut, Dawn stepped onto the deep pile carpet of the hallway, her bare feet sinking in with each tentative step. Placing her hands on the railing she scanned the stairwell and the hallway she was in, finding nothing but deepening shadows and pale shafts of moonlight. It was a clear night and the first real spring weather since she’d returned fro Japan’but something niggled at the back of her mind, a shadow and a doubt that had woken her from an otherwise deep sleep. Pale skin flashed in the moonlight as she moved to the stairs, having not bothered to dress for her trip downstairs. It wasn’t that everyone was asleep, but rather that they had all seen each other in various states of dress and undress since moving in, her sleeping panties and tank top weren’t any cause for blushes anymore. And with the weather turning nice it was a refreshing change to be able to wander about her own house without the need for a heavy overcoat. Shivering involuntarily as her feet touched the smooth tile of the front hall, she hugged her arms to the think garment protecting what little of her modesty she had left. “Okay maybe it’s not as warm as I thought.” She grumbled. The living room was quiet, the television having been silenced many hours ago and those watching it having retired to bed or other past times. All the lights on the ground floor were off, the only illumination was the faint beams of moonlight that crept in through windows and under doors. A faint clicking sound reached her ears, the familiar sound of carefully manicured claws against polished tile. Hands on hips she waited until the owner of the sound came into view, too intelligent eyes shining brightly in the darkness. “Damek!” She scolded, knowing exactly where the “dog” had been. “You’ve been sleeping on the couch again! What have I told you”!” The male half of Lia’s hellhound pair hung his head a little, whimpering softly in the gloom. With a sigh Dawn crouched low, beckoning him forward until she could reach the soft spots behind his ears. Rewarded with an animal groan of pleasure, she shook her head while grinning. “I do love you, but that still doesn’t mean you can sleep on the couch.” The massive creature wuffled irritably, sitting down by her feet and gazing reproachfully up at her. Rising back slowly she shook her head, looking from Damek back to the darkened floor. “I dunno big guy, something just doesn’t feel right’like a sense of dread, or pressure.” She shivered again, and it wasn’t from the chilled tiles beneath her feet. Placing one hand on Damek for reassurance she crept forward into the hallway, following a faint sensation of being pulled toward the back of the house. The large bay window at the back of the kitchen illuminated the entire room, the bright light of the moon chasing away the darkness. “Nothing.” Dawn murmured, her brows knitting together in confusion. “But I was sure I felt someth?” Her voice caught in her throat as she moved forward, eyes fixed on a point outside the window. A figure stood by the pool, face turned away from the house. A long, velvety black cape pooled around the figures feet and seemed to blend with sheets of red-black hair that cascaded down past it’s shoulders. Dawn reached down to reassure herself that Damek was still there, and then moved towards the back door. Licking lips gone suddenly dry she eased the door open, gathering her breath as well as her courage. “Hey! What are you doing there?” The figure, a woman, turned slowly towards Dawn with a smile. “Waiting for you.” She replied, her voice rich and husky, sounding to almost smoulder in the night air. Wishing she’d through to grab a weapon of some kind, Dawn stepped back to stand beside Damek’only to find the dog lying on the floor, his flanks heaving gently in and out. “Your friend is just sleeping. I didn’t want to hurt him, but I don’t think he could have said the same for me.” She smiled then, a pleasant expression with a touch of sadness about it. “Who are you?” Dawn refused to be cowed, especially in her own home. Trying to ignore the strange feeling pressing her mind she stepped off the step and onto the back porch. “What do you want”!” The woman inclined her head, slowly bending at the waist in an elaborate curtsy. Pale skin caught the porch lights, the sinuous curve of breasts pushed up by a pvc corset. “I am Roshael. It is?” Her voice caught and she struggled to clear it. ??nice to see you again.” “Again” What the hell are you talking about?” Dawn was incredulous, and her anger was growing. “I’ve never seen you before in my life!” Roshael reached up to tuck flows of black hair back behind her ear, smiling a sad smile that went all the way to her entrancing emerald eyes. “Maybe not that you remember dearheart, but I knew you when you were a baby.” That paused Dawn, staying the anger in her breast and the snap in her voice. She mulled her next words carefully, studying Roshael as best she could without seeming rude. “Why should I believe you?” “Because you know I’m telling you the truth.” She moved away from the pool, taking one hesitant step onto the porch itself. “Search my face Dawn, tell me what you see.” “Look, I dunno what you want but?” “Please.” The earnestness of her voice caught Dawn off guard, cutting her off mid sentence and drawing her reluctant eyes to Roshael’s pretty face. Pretty wasn’t the right word, nor would Dawn have called her beautiful’she was striking, with high cheekbones and skin the colour of stirred cream, but this was not a woman you imagined taking out on a date. Rather this was the woman of the most carnal, intimate fantasies. Black hair glinted an odd reddish tinge in the porch light, hanging in waves around her face, throwing into contrast the entrancing green of her eyes’eyes? Dawn gasped suddenly, hands going to her mouth in shock. Eyes the colour of polished emerald gazed calmly at her, underlined by the high cut of pale cheeks and the sensual pout of full, red lips. It was almost like??A mirror. You’you look like me!” Chuckling, Roshael took another step forward, spreading her arms to show she was unarmed and meant no harm. “Actually Dawn, if you want to be technical’you look like me.” Taking a step back, she shook her head slowly, disbelief flooding her mind and her voice. “But’why” Who ARE you?” “Is it not obvious by now?” Roshael’s voice had softened, losing some of its fire but gaining a sort of gentle promise. “You’are my daughter.” *Thump* Dawn sat down hard on the deck, staring up in disbelief at the woman across from her. She wanted to deny it, wanted to shout for her friends or to drive this’woman from the Estate’but something stopped her, something in Roshael’s face cooled that fire. “H-how can that be possible”!” She stuttered, managing to find her voice again. “Why does it seem so improbable?” The other woman held her ground, hands folded primly in front of her. “You said yourself that we look the same.” “So” A lot of people look similar! I’ve met fans who looked almost exactly like me’and you’re not even blonde.” It sounded weak, but Dawn’s mind was still reeling at the implications. Roshael chuckled at that, smiling genuinely. “Well you do have a lot of your father in you, and he was very fair.” Seizing on that, Dawn asked the first thing that came to mind. “You knew my father?” “Of course I knew your father’he is my husband Dawn.” “Liar’if you were telling the truth you’d know my father is dead, you’re talking in the present tense.” Roshael turned her head to one side, grimacing. “The gulf of death may separate us now, but I still love James. He’s just as much my husband now as he was the day we were married.” Dawn gritted her teeth and pushed up off the deck, indignation at Roshael’s intrusion flooding her mind and forcing her to action. “Then where were you when he DIED”!” She was yelling now, but didn’t care, her carrying as much thunder as possible. “If you loved him as much as you say then why did you let him die alone”! Why did you leave ME alone?!” Turning back to Dawn, Roshael fixed clear eyes on hers, eyes that wavered with tears unshed. “Don’t you remember?” She whispered, “You were sick’I had to leave so that you would be well again, and I had to stay away.” “I?” Dawn’s voice failed her again, memories of the dreams that had been haunting her since Global coming back to the fore. “Dawn sweetie” You need to wake up, even if only for a little bit okay?” She was floating in a haze of white light, light so bright that it obscured everything around it, leaving only a play of fuzzy and indistinct images. A woman with long hair sat at the side of her bed, one hand gently cradling Dawn’s cheek. “Muh’mummy?” Her voice was small and high, not the sultry tones an adult Steel Rose had learned to anticipate coming from her own lips. “What’s going on?” “You’re sick sweetie, daddy and I are just going to give you some medicine okay” It’ll make you feel better about everything.” “Are you going away Mummy” I heard you telling Daddy’that you had to go.” The woman turned away, a silvery tear sliding down one cheek to splash onto Dawn’s fevered face. “I have to go sweety, or you’re going to get sick again. When I go you’re going to be all better, and you’ll stay that way forever.” “I don’t want you to go mummy’I don’t want to be better!” A small hand came into Dawn’s vision, grasping weakly at her mothers arm. Gently her mother freed her arm and took Dawn’s small hand between both of hers, pressing it up to her own cheek. “You have to get better Dawnie, I could never live with myself if I knew you were sick because I stayed. Once I go you’ll feel better, and then?” Her voice caught, a choked sob sticking in her throat. ??And then someday you’ll forget’me.” “NO! No mummy I don’t want to forget! I won’t EVER forget you!” Dawn flung small arms around her mother, squeezing tightly. “It’s okay Dawnie, no matter what happens, you’re always going to be my little girl?” She pressed her lips to Dawn’s forehead and there was a flash of pain, so sharp it felt like a nail had been pounded into her skull. Dawn clutched her head in both hands, wavering on her feet at the all too familiar feelings. Roshael started towards her, eyes wide with concern when and outstretched hand stopped her. “No’I’I’m fine?” Dawn looked up with tearing eyes, “What’What did I say” On the day you left me?” Roshael’s eyes closed and she took a shuddering breath, her own memories of that day as raw as if it were only yesterday. “You told me no mummy, I won’t ever forget you.” For Dawn that was the key, the key to the door she’d locked those feelings behind. The door that had been slowly breaking, more and more of it’s precious cargo leaking out for all to see’until it was opened with the right key. She flew to Roshael, her arms around the other woman’s waist as her face buried in the spot between her neck and shoulder. Sobs wracked Dawn’s body as gentle arms encircled her shoulders, holding her close in a maternal embrace. She poured her heart onto Roshael’s bare shoulder, tears leaking down the other woman’s skin and pattering like rain onto the treated wood of the deck. It seemed like hours she cried, sobbing until her eyes went dry and her body simply heaved until exhausted muscles ached with the strain. Only then did she loosen her grip on her mothers waist, taking a half step back to swipe clumsily at her eyes. “I-I’m sorry?” “Hush.” Reaching out, Roshael gently stroked the tears from Dawn’s face with her thumb. “There’s nothing for you to be sorry for dear heart’I’m the one who has a lot of apologizing to do.” Dawn snuffled piteously, her heart aching for the promised explanation. “But I’m afraid I don’t have a lot of time?” Her heart froze, eyes wide at the suggestion. “B-but, you just got here and there’s so much for us to talk about and I was hoping you’d come and stay and please don’t go we just got back together and?” “Shhh.” Laying one finger across Dawn’s lips the other woman smiled. “I’m not leaving forever, or even for a long time Dawnie. Remember why I left the first time” I still have to protect you from that’but I can come back often now that you’re stronger.” “Stronger?” Dawn sniffled again, “Did you leave because I was weak?” “NO! Gods no!” Roshael couldn’t hold in a rueful laugh. “You’re impossible, just like you’ve always been. I bet you drive Lia crazy with an attitude like that.” “You’you know Lia?” Roshael nodded, unable to conceal a small smile. “Just because I went away doesn’t mean I stopped making sure you were okay hun. I know all about Lia and your band of friends.” Dawn had the grace to blush, a horrified thought creeping into her mind. “You havn’t?” “Watched you?” Another laugh, this one deeper and more honest. “No dear heart, I’m not some kind of perverted voyeur’I do know you love her though.” Sighing, Dawn plopped down onto the nearby bench, resting her chin in her hands. “I do’but I’m not very good at showing it. It’s all so?” ??complicated? It doesn’t have to be that way you know.” Turnining her head away from her mother, Dawn gazed out into the woods behind the estate. “There are things I don’t know’Lillith told me that Lia only loves me, because of what I am’and I don’t even know what that is.” “Lillith says a lot of things, most of which aren’t true.” Roshael’s voice had enough snap to drag Dawn’s head back around. “She likes to twist the truth to her own ends, so she can use it to twist people too’you’re smarter then that.” “But how can I be sure”!” Dawn’s voice was desperate and pleading in equal degrees. “I don’t even know what I’ve become! How can I know it’s not affecting the people around me”!” “You’ve not become anything, you’re who you have always been. You’re Dawn McFarlane, you’re my daughter and you’re the GCW World Champion.” “You’you know about that?” “That’s why I came hun.” Roshael cocked her head to one side, smiling. “I wanted to say I was proud of you’and I’m sure your father is too.” Dawn hung her head, averting her eyes from her beaming mother. “I really hope so’I’ve wanted to do well for so long and’and’I think I screwed up’things with Lia.” “Nonsense.” Roshael waved one hand dismissively. “If I’m right about her, then you’ve not damaged anything between you. But you’re going to need to be honest with her’make sure she knows you’re not playing.” “How?” “This is going to sound terribly clich’d’but follow your heart.” Roshael smiled ruefully. “No matter how ridiculous it seems in your mind, follow the ideas your heart is giving you and you can’t go wrong.” Dawn nodded slowly, looking back up at Roshael. “What’s happening to me” What does Lillith mean when she says “My nature?” Why does being around you make me start to get sick?” There was a pregnant pause after the question, storm clouds brewing behind Roshael’s eyes before she finally answered. “There are some things you’ve inherited from me that make you’different. Not bad, not worse, not weaker’just different.” She sighed. “This is difficult to explain, it seems so unreal sometimes, even to me.” “Unreal?” Dawn raised one eyebrow weakly. “Well?” She sighed again. ??do you like my cloak? Look closely.” “Your cloak” What’s that got to do with’any?” Her voice trailed off as she leaned forward a little, really looking at the garment slung over Roshael’s shoulders for the first time. A simple clasp of what looked like ivory held it closed at her throat’a clasp that looked for all intents and purposes like it was two claws hooked together. What she had mistaken for velvet was more leathery up close, with long rib like protrusions that ran the length, making the entire garment look an awful lot like? “Wings.” Her voice rang with disbelief and Dawn couldn’t tear her eyes from the “cloak”. “That’s’a pair’of?” “Wings. Yes.” Roshael nodded slowly, sighing. “And there’s more, but I didn’t want to alarm you with too much too soon.” “Do I have?”” A chuckle. “Have you noticed any” I assure you they’re extremely hard to miss.” “But what does all this mean” Who ARE you?” “I told you, I’m Roshael and I’m your mother.” Came the cagey reply. “That’s not what I mean’I guess’What are you?” “There’s a lot of explaining I owe you Dawn, but I cannot tell you tonight. I’ve stayed too long as it is, and the longer I’m here the worse it could be for you.” Roshael took a step back, regret etched on her face. “Wait! Don’t go please! I have so many questions!” Dawn came off the bench, reaching one hand out. “And I’ll answer them I promise you’but it will have to wait until my next visit.” She wrapped one hand around Dawn’s fingers, squeezing lightly. “Trust me.” With that her “cloak” unfurled, dark, batlike wings stretching enormously to either side of her. With a final look she sprung from the deck, one massive beat of her wings propelling her into the sky. “I love you Dawn.” She called, disappearing into the black sky in moments. “I still have so many questions?” Dawn whispered as she sank to her knees, staring up into the sky.
