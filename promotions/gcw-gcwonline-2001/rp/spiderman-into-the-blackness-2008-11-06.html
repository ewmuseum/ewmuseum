Spiderman: Into The Blackness

6 November 2008

Date: 6 November 2008 10/30/2008 Westbound on 31st St., New York City. 11:07 P.M. It’s dark and tense, the way all of their rides were to be honest. Something was different about this trip however, something just felt more important about this one. Although it is cold outside, the six people in the dark here are heating up because of the body armor and ammunition that’s draped over their protective clothing. Sitting in the back of one of the S.H.I.E.L.D. armored trucks has become second nature, almost like a second home to the soldiers. The sweat on their brow, the nerves, the fear however? All brand new feelings to this group of brave people. Normally there’s an excited feeling, joking, laughing all around on these little trips. Tonight however they just take everything in, the sound of the city moving frantically on the other side of the walls, their bodies bouncing around because of the tires hitting potholes on the street ? the pounding of their hearts. There has been a war going on for a very long time, a war that humanity never believed would end. Now? Here they are, on the brink of winning the war against a very determined and dangerous opponent. The numbers game would seem to favor the S.H.I.E.L.D. team, Six trained soldiers going in to find one vicious creature but they know better than to be fooled. The team knows better than to underestimate the power of their opposition. Humanity as a whole did it once and they were almost destroyed. It can be finished tonight, the fate of the world rests on S.H.I.E.L.D.?s shoulders. Talk about pressure. None of them have any real idea that they are close but somehow, they feel it. If someone would listen closely, they’d hear the thud of their hearts crashing around inside of each and everyone of their chests. Thump!Thump!Thump!Thump! As the truck makes a final turn the thumping increases speed. THUMP!THUMP!THUMP!THUMP! Their bodies jerk forward as the driver hit’s the brakes. They all listen to the brakes screech as the truck comes to a halt. Now the thumping is as hard and fast as it can be without hearts ripping outward from their chests like some alien from the old movies. The sound of the big door holding them inside being unlocked causes them all to latch onto their weapons, their fingers wrapping around the triggers. The door swings open. "LETS MOVE!" A deep voice screams out. They all pile out of the truck, into the night. Towards a monster. Towards freedom. 10/30/2008 Atop Trinity Church, New York City. 11:15 P.M. He is perched high above the city streets, New York’s red and blue savior. The boy who got bit by a radioactive spider who became a hero to a city that desperately needed one. Peter Parker has seen so many strange things in his time underneath his colorful garb, some funny and some dastardly. He’s seen people do amazing things and he has seen the most terrifying things imaginable. He’s been loved and he has been hated but through it all he has remained true to the city that he loves. He has made sacrifices, like all of the people similar to him have to do. He can never take credit on a personal level for the good things he does, tries his best to keep his secret from those that he cares most about so they aren’t put in danger. He’s given up the love of his life, any kind of free time he could possibly have and sometimes he questions what future he really has in this whole “super hero business” as he calls it. But he has remained, a symbol of hope. Knowing that kids are safer because of him makes everything worth it. But Lately? Things have become much harder. The city had become overrun with an evil that is unmatched. “The Black Plague” is what they’ve called it in the media. Peter’s most bitter rival, Eddie Brock lost complete control of the symbiote and it began to spread, mutating and replicating at an alarming rate. Soon New York became a feeding ground for these people who became cannibalistic monsters, children of Venom. The strain weakened the original symbiote to such a state that it could barely sustain its own life let alone Eddie’s cancer filled one and it forced itself away from Brock ? dying on the spot. Brock went insane and was locked up. Venom was gone but his children remained. After five years of battle it was close to being over. Peter’s right ear is filled with the song "Sexy Back" by Justin Timberlake. He nods his head to the music for a moment before answering his phone using a technologically advanced head set. "Go ahead." "Peter, it’s Tony." "Sup Stark?" "I’ve just got word from Fury, they’ve found it. The final Symbiote." Parker wraps his free arm around a stone gargoyle and leans forward. "How long ago?" "You have to hurry, they are already going in. A team of six." "SIX!” Are you kidding me? This thing will tear them to shreds! Why didn’t you get a hold of me sooner? Hell why aren’t you here?" "Cool it kid, I just got the word myself!" Tony Stark takes in a deep breath. "I’ll be there as soon as I can. You just need to get to the Warehouse on 31st and Brooks." "Alright, on my way." Peter presses a button and the phone goes dead. A sick feeling forms in the pit of his stomach. "I just hope I’m not too late." He pushes off the statue and leaps off the roof, diving into the night. 10/30/2008 Abandoned Warehouse, New York City. 11:16 P.M. Delylah leads the team towards the worn and battered wooden door that will give her and the S.H.I.E.L.D. team access to the Warehouse. All six members are stacked, weapons pointed in all directions with Delylah up front and Sergeant Samson bringing up the rear. Directly behind Sergeant First Class "D" is Specialist Stevens, Scott Stevens. Attached to Stevens? hip is Corporal Stone and behind him are Sergeants Axel and Fox. SFC D. calls for the team to halt right before reaching the door. "Stevens, unlock the door." SPC Stevens smirks and moves up to the door, over exaggerating his movements, obviously mocking the military motions. Stevens places his back up against the Warehouse wall near the door and puts a worried look on his face. The other members of the team are tempted to shoot him themselves but going to prison isn’t really worth it. "It could be dangerous in there!" He proclaims in the loudest attempt at a whisper in history. "Scott, get the door." "Yes Ma’am! Right away!" "Open the fucking door!" Samson’s voice booms from behind. Stevens glares back at the hulking form that makes up Paul Samson but says nothing before turning to the door. As the rest of the team focuses their weapons towards the door, SPC Stevens slides to a kneeling position and grabs a hold of the handle that’ll open the door. "On three." Delylah tells him. "Fuck that, last one in gets called a bitch on national television." And with that last comment Scott Stevens pushes the door open and rushes into the dark warehouse. "SCOTT!" "FUCK!" The rest of the team, led by Delylah move into the warehouse, immediately turning on lights that are attached to their rifles. Clouds of dust fly all around the area, making things difficult to see. About a hundred feet ahead they catch Scott Stevens? light flicker on and despite his asshole’ish ways, the team is relieved. "This place is probably fucking empty." Stevens yells back as he turns from left to right trying his best to catch a glimpse of every corner of the room he is in. Delylah leads the team towards Steven’s light, all of them on high alert, knowing that at anytime the thing they are after could show its hideous face. "Stevens, you prick." Axel spits out through gritted teeth. "Could we not cuss so much?" Fox asks. "Quiet!" Samson tells them forcefully. "Pay attention or we’re all dead." They, very slowly, walk towards Scott Stevens? light. Their own green beams desperately searching for what they came for. Perhaps a part of them hope they do not find it though, the consequences could be deadly. They continue to scan the area but find nothing. Crackle-ANYTHING?-crackle The voice on the radio cuts through, Delylah reaches up and presses on her radio. "Not yet. We’re still looking Sir." Crackle-Let me know when you have contact.-Crackle "Understood." D gets a tighter grip on her weapon. "Stay focused guys." So the team pushes forward, their lights creating an eerie, shadowy world. Spider Webs create visions of hellacious creatures, coat racks make for frightening ghouls and curtains bring to life the ghosts of people who might have died in the area. The wind picks up outside and its howl sends a chill up and down Fox’s spine. "Kind of creepy." Clyde says with a great hint of nervousness in his voice. "This is fucking stupid." Stevens lowers his weapon and walks away from the group again, frustration showing. "What is this thing so afraid of!” COME ON OUT YOU BASTARD!" "Steve! Stop!" "Quit it fucker before I shoot you myself!" "Prick." Stevens ignores the team and walks a few steps further out, The other five members lock their rifles onto him to allow their lights to shine on him. Stevens spreads his arms out fully. "C’Mon! I’m right here! Come face the ELITE BITCH!" Silence. Everyone looks around the room. When nothing happens, Stevens smirks. "Exactly what I thought, pussies." He lifts his rifle again, taking in in both hands. Clyde is the first person to notice the movement above SPC Stevens. At first he just thought it was a mixture of the dark and the dust getting into his eyes until it moved twice more. Problem is that it moved quick, snake-like speed even. By the time Fox raised his rifle to get a better look, it was hovering over the very arrogant SPC Stevens. "LOOK OUT!" "Huh?" Stevens looks up buts far too late to react when a dark green arm thrusts out from behind the veil of blackness and latches onto Steven’s face. Sharp claws penetrate the skin and bones on his forehead, blood spurts out and the once overly confident soldier now shrieks, terrified beyond anything he’s ever felt before. "FIRE!" Just as the team pulls their triggers, lighting up the area that Stevens was attacked, the disfigured arm pulls Stevens out of sight. His howls of terror and pain intensify and eventually you hear a sickening gurgling sound before the screaming stops. The team ceases fire and listens? "Where is it!?" Samson yells out. "I don’t fucking know!" Axel responds. CLUNK! In between the five remaining soldiers falls the lifeless body of Specialist Scott Stevens. A pool of blood quickly forms around his head and Axel kneels down to check the wounds. Fox points his light in that direction as Axel turns him over ? and his brain spills out from his head and onto the floor. A huge chunk had been savagely bitten from Steven’s head. Fox turns and pukes. The rest of the team gets to their feet and to a defensive position as fast as they can. Delylah gets on the radio. "WE HAVE CONTACT! I REPEAT! WE HAVE CONTACT! WE’VE SEEN PAYNE!" -Crackle-TAKE HIM THE FUCK OUT! END THIS THING ONCE AND FOR ALL!-Crackle- She handles her rifle again and swallows hard? "Let’s kill this ugly son of a bitch." Then the beast roars! It’s animalistic growl echoing throughout the entire warehouse? 10/30/2008 The Whitehouse. Washington, D.C. 11:28 P.M. Standing near the window of his office, the Oval Office, President Steven Caldera glares out of his window and out into the night. His eyes admire the full moon that hangs in the sky, he basks in its faint glow. He takes in a deep breath. Things were going according to plan but tonight his nerves were somewhat on edge. He hated failure, the people who fail him are not treated kindly. Of course failure rarely happens under his watchful eye but still, the possibility lurks on this occasion because of how volatile the situation is. "Mr. President?" A woman's voice calls from the intercom. "Yes Ms. VanLorne?" "Mr. Octavian here to see you." "Perfect. Send him in." "Yes sir." A few moments later The President hears his office doors swing open but he doesn't bother turning around to greet his oldest friend, he remains locked in a stare down with the moon. The sound of Octavian's expensive Italian shoes slapping the floor stop just a few feet behind Caldera. "Any news?" The President asks. "There is." Octavian moves up right beside Caldera. "They've made contact." "Any survivors?" "Not sure yet but Spiderman was sighted a few blocks away, probably involved in the altercation now." Caldera nods his head as he turns to look at Octavian. "Good." "What's next Steven?" "Prepare a conference, the world will want to know the good news." "What if they do not succeed?" "Spiderman won't fail, he is too stubborn." "Very well." Octavian turns and walks back the way he came, leaving The President alone once again. Briefly his eyes go back out the window, into the darkness. "Good Night Spidey." 10/30/2008 Inside the Warehouse. New York City. 11:41 P.M. "LOOK OUT!" Axel screams just a little too late. Axel turns hard to his right, taking aim at Samson where their Monster has crept up from behind. The creature swiftly takes both of his clawed hands and digs into Samson's shoulders, dark blood begins to flow immediately. Paul's entire body tightens up as he tries to hold off from screaming into the night. A low chuckle escapes the monster's throat before he moves his body right up against Samson's knowing that the rest of the team wouldn't fire at him. "You motherfucker!" Delylah hisses, tears welling up in her eyes. "Let him go!" Clyde, Axel, D and Stone aim their lights, getting the best look they have been able to get of their prey. At first glance he looks like some sort of lizard from hell, dark green, distorted skin. Clawed hands. Long, thrashing tail. Razor sharp teeth. Flicking tongue. "Noooo." The creatures whispers. "I'm hungry." In one motion IT opens its mouth, rows of vicious teeth extend out like a Great White Shark and sink into Samson's neck. Now he howls in pain, blood splatters all over Samson, the floor, the walls and the beast. With a violent tug and shake of his head, The creature rips a huge chunk of flesh off and releases his latest meal. The four remaining team members stare blankly as Samson's body crumbles, motionless, to the floor. "FIRE!" Axel roars after snapping out of the trance. Clyde and Stone both raise their weapons and pull the triggers. Bullets fly all around the not so abandoned Warehouse but manage to hit only old furniture and walls .The Symbiote has disappeared again. "God Damn it!" Stone curses to himself. "We had it." "D!" Fox yelps as he notices Delylah looking faint and almost passing out. "Got ya!" He catches her just as her knees buckled. Stone and Axel collapse on either side of Fox and Delylah, weapons pointed outward. "You Ok?" Of course he knows she isn't, her husband was just murdered in front of her "Guys, we can't be fucking around. This thing will kill us." Axel says, frustration in his voice very apparent "Shit I could use a smoke about now." Stone calls back, not to anyone in particular though. "Look, you stay with her Fox. Markus and I are going to go find this thing and kill it." "Fuck yeah we are." "Fine, I'll get her on her feet soon." "Good." Axel turns and puts his light forward and greeting him is that demonic smile, those razor sharp teeth. He can't react, there's no time. The Symbiote, Payne, swipes at him with an uppercut, his claws ripping in right under Axel's chin. The force rips Axel's head off, most of his spine sliding out as well. Markus lifts his weapon to fire but Payne throws the head and spine combo at him and it smashes Stone in the face, instantly breaking his nose. Payne lowers his body and adjusts, whipping himself around and slashing Markus with his powerful tail. Splitting Stone in half at the torso. Intestines, guts and blood fall to the floor in a steamy pile. "NOOOO!" Clyde fires, squeezing the trigger relentlessly. The bullets spray and begin to connect with the monster, causing it to screech in pain. "TAKE THAT!" The bullets continue to hit and Payne backs up further and further until he falls, limp and lifeless. Fox's weapon quits firing but not because he stops pulling the trigger, its because he runs out of bullets. "WOW." He says out loud. "I GOT HIM!" Delylah gets up to her knees and looks over at the dark form of Payne's body. "You think he's dead?" She asks. "Has to be." Fox smirks. "I unloaded a whole damn clip." "Let's be sure." "Alright, cover me." Delylah picks her weapon back up and shines the light towards the hideous monster. Cautiously Clyde approaches, hands trembling. He reaches the monster and kicks its leg and instantly backs up, awaiting it to attack again. Nothing. Clyde kneels down beside it and pokes it with his rifle. Nothing. "Dead?" Delylah asks. "Has to be." "Thank god." Her eyes shift to her husband. "Paul ." She fights back the tears and turns to look back at Fox. He's gone! So is Payne! "No." She can hear a loud 'CRACK' . Clyde's Neck being snapped like a twig. She thinks about getting revenge for her dead lover. She thinks about the end of the war. She thinks about freedom. She decides on running. She makes her muscles pump as hard and fast as they can. She knows Payne is right behind her, almost on top of her. Doesn't matter! RUN! RUN! RUN! Is what she tells herself, get outside . try to stay alive! The door gets closer.. Payne's growl does too! Just as she reaches the door she turns and dives, she fires off her rifle, letting her back hit the door. Payne goes airborne! The Animal pounces on Delylah and they both crash through the old wooden door, splintering it all to hell. She's stuck, the monster trapping her. He digs his claws into her arms. He loves the smell of her fear and he cannot wait to taste her. "I'm so hungry!" He growls. His entire body shivers in anticipation of the meal to come. And he bites down on her face, ripping her head in half and leaving very little to be recognized later. 10/30/2008 Outside the Warehouse. New York City. 11:52 P.M. Spiderman releases his grip on his webbing and gracefully flies through the air before landing on the ground in a kneeling position. He stares straight ahead at Payne who has just finished off a young S.H.I.E.L.D. woman. The scene is so vicious that Peter has to swallow hard to keep from puking inside his mask. "Let’s go you sick freak!" Payne’s tail begins to sway back and forth, obviously enthused from the challenge. He growls before leaping over the mangled body and lands right in front of Spidey. "Spiderman!" His low voice frightening and distorted. "Now I’ll finish my father’s job!" "Don’t count on it Lizard Boy, I’m sending you to hell with him." Payne screeches and dives at Spiderman but he is far too quick for the Symbiote infected man and dives out of the way, quickly reaching into a container in his belt. With a press of a small button on his wrist a cartridge of webbing flies out and he replaces it with the special batch he just pulled out. Payne pounces again! Spiderman leaps into the air, flipping backwards over Payne and landing behind him. Spiderman goes airborne and just as Payne spins around again he has Spidey’s boots implanted into his chest. Payne’s whimper of pain turns into an angry growl quickly as he rolls over and back onto his feet. "Too slow Fatty, maybe too many flies out by the swamp?" Payne charges again, Peter tries to anticipate his move but he is unsuccessful. Spider does a back hand spring to avoid the charge not knowing that Payne stops midway, spins and whips his tail out. As Spiderman lands back on his feet, the middle of Payne’s tail smashes into him. Spidey goes hurling through the air and crashes into the side of the S.H.I.E.L.D. Armored truck, denting it big time. Spiderman falls to the concrete, pain surging through his body. "Well not the right move Spidey." He starts to push himself back up to his feet immediately but his brain is still rattled so he isn’t fast enough. Payne charges again, lowering his head and bulldozing Spiderman sending the blue and red clan super hero back into the armored truck, the force tipping it over to its side. Payne growls and leaps up and lands on the wreckage, a few feet from Spiderman. Slowly Payne crawls to him, hovering above like a vulture. Spiderman remains still, catching his breath and regaining his wits. As soon as Spidey attempts to move however Payne lashes out and wraps his hand around Peter Parker’s throat and brings him off the ground. "You’re not so special," Payne’s long tongue flicked out and back in again. "Why did he want you so bad?" "B-Because," Peter choked out. "I’m better than you!" Spidey rolls away from the blow and bounces up to his feet and shoots regular webbing at Payne, wrapping it around the beast’s neck. "Come over here!" Parker can only turn his head to stare at the carnage as the symbiote burns alive and desperately attempts to leave its host. It’s not meant to be ? the symbiote burns up, dying right there.
