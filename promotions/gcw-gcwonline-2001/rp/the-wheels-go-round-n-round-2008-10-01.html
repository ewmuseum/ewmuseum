The Wheels Go 'Round 'n 'Round

1 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-1-08 Life’s funny. When you’re young, the whole world seems out in front of you? It’s a blue mountain range calling to explored, scaled, and conquered. But time can do a lot? it decays the mind, breaks the spirit, and can tear down those mountains. Life’s funny? like a bad joke. Even after the mountains are reduced to sand, we still endure. Left to wander the desert; lost children on a harsh alien plain. The sands of time tear at us further? clawing at that face that once shone so brightly? that our mothers once looked down upon with such love? scarring what was once virgin pulp. Life’s a cruel joke. A young man once shone like a newborn star, like a prodigy in a land of gold. But time, as it always does, destroyed that promised land? yet the man remained. He is Kenny Mercury. The star that shined so brightly, yet fell so hard. “I’m the shooting star?” A moniker once know throughout the world of professional wrestling. ?” “n I don’t take no shee-it. Not from anyone?” Mercury is plopped down in a dark corridor, surrounded on all sides by boxes. Upon his cardboard throne, he decrees to his court. A finger points out into space, at some imagined audience. “You all were witness to the return of a wrestling DIETY! Kenny Mercury slew Rikki Roxx” and in less than five minutes? what does that have to say about KENNY MERCURY?!” Kenny’s head tilts backwards, exposing his bearded neck. He chuckles to himself. “Perhaps it says more about your opponent than you, my dearest wearer of the rancid poncho?” Kenny’s head shoots to attention. He grits his teeth, eyes darting back and forth? “You” Manalishi?” “Beloved advisor” jester? and all around swell guy to have around your kingdom of shit, eh, Ken?” “I told you not to call me that” besides? this poncho was free, it was a gift. What do I know of laundering cloth?” “Apparently nothing” you’ve been wearing the same tights since 2001... Have you ever taken those off?” “They have been removed before” by force, of course.” “And taking some pocho off a dead wayward son isn’t exactly receiving a “gift?” “HE DIDN’T NEED IT ANYMORE” I was COLD? so cold?” Kenny hisses. “He was just like you” a lost soul, trying to scrape by--? “NO ONE IS LIKE ME! You lie” your words reek like bile.” “Oh, that’s right” you’re something special, aren’t you? Well, how special were you before I came to you? Hm? Do you recall? Naked and drunk? sick and dying? out on the street, in some alley? like a rat that no one wanted. A dead dream, rotting--? “Please stop” please? I’m sorry? I know? I know now that I am nothing, I admit it? I am nothing? without you.” Kenny actually cracks a smile? black as the soot lining a politician’s pockets. “Heh” I’ve done everything you asked’I found Global Championship Wrestling? I got a contract, though? turns out “The Renegade” is worm food. The wee maggots got to “em before I could” but no matter, no matter. You picked me up? lifted me from my tomb, and now like Lazarus and back and want to skull fuck EVERYONE. I have risen from the dead? and Phil Atkin will be the next to achieve self realization? Well, that he’s a mere stain on my boot? yes, shiny shiny? my shiny boots of leather?” “Just make sure we don’t have another episode like last week” that was too close. I want PROFICIENCY? I want PRECISION? I want the technical wizard? I want the ring skills of the Kenny Mercury of old? combined the your current blood lust?. I want that unholy matrimony to turn the GCW upside down? and Phil Atkin will be the first vessel to take it all in.” Mercury chuckles to himself and licks his lips. “Technical” ecstasy? the best of everything? the thought’s titillating.” The entire room shakes with an awful sound. “The wheels on the truck go “round and “round” When do we get to the arena? This semi-trailer smells of dead skin and stale urine?” ? Mercury looks around? no response came. “Or maybe” IT’S JUST ME! GYAHAHAHAHA!” Kenny takes hold of his poncho, and brings it upon to his face? he takes in a large breath and exhales, sliding down the wall into a broken heap on the floor. He continues to laugh to himself, his cracked voice bouncing off the aluminum walls.
