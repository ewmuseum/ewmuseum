The day after

Date: The day after...cant'cha read? I woke up with a groan. My back ached, my arms ached, my legs ached and everything else that could ache, did. I got out of bed quietly not wanting to wake the sleeping form of my wife next to me. I had gotten my rubdown last night, and hadn't really watched the replay of Annihilation. Hadn't watched much of anything because the rubdown turned into something more. A lot more. That's why I was so damn sore. I looked at my half asleep face in the mirror and gingerly pulled away the bandages covering the cuts on my forehead and rubbed my fingers over them. They looked pretty bad, but I'd live. I put fresh bandages on them and took a leak and made my way back to the bedroom where Cindy was sitting up now looking dishevled and bleary eyed at me. 'Come back to bed honey.' she muttered, holding her arms out to me like a sleepy child. A sleepy child with a nice rack. 'It's almost 11am.' I said, 'I'm hungry and the hotel only serves breakfast for another half hour.' She dropped her arms and pouted at me, 'I don' wanna get up.' she mumbled. 'I was gonna order in.' 'Oh...' she rubbed her eyes, 'Good.' She then flopped back on the bed as I sat down and picked up the phone. 'You want anything in particular?' She rattled off her breakfast preferances, and I related that, and my preferences to room service, telling them to leave the cart outside the room. I slipped back into the bed next to her and she rolled into me snuggling against my chest. She looked up at me as I wrapped my arm around her. 'Feeling better?' she asked. 'I'll feel better once I get some food in my system, but, yeah, I'm feeling better than I did after the show last night.' 'You'd better be.' she said, 'I don't give good sex like that to just anybody.' 'Oh, so I'm special?' Truth was in the back of my mind I was still thinking about the Maraduer and how I'd like to insert my foot so far up his ass that he'd have to open his mouth so I could tie my shoes, but there would be another time for the two of us. I made a damn good showing in what was only my third GCW match, and on a pay-per-view no less. I had done exactly what I said I was going to do from day one, and that was put on one hell of a match. It may not have been the best match ever, and it may have paled in comparison to the match that LeStatt Knight and Michael Stevens put on (what I managed to see of it anyway, which wasn't much). But considering it had been at least six months since I'd been in a match of that caliber, it was damn good, if I do say so myself. I had noticed the missed call on my cell phone when we got back to the hotel last night, it was Jeff congratulating me on putting on a hell of a match, and telling me to call him back when I could. I decided to wait on calling him back. Besides, I didn't think Cindy was going to be letting me go just yet, and any man would have to be a damn fool to let a naked woman go to make a phone call. I stared at the ceiling and ran my fingers along her back as she adjusted herself in the crook of my arm. A few hours later after we'd woken up some more, eaten, showered, dressed and were getting ready to leave we were both feeling a lot better. Visions of the Marauder being beaten half to death at my feet still dancing through my head, but still at the back of my head. In the front of my head was making sure we weren't leaving anything behind. We left our bags by the front door and swept the room making sure that nothing of ours was staying in LA. Various GCW officials were in the lobby making sure that everything was coordinated, and that we weren't sticking Caldera with the bills we ran up. I left Cindy to go and talk to one of the officals while I paid off the bill. She came back to me with a shit eating grin on her face that I hadn't seen in a long time. 'You are not gonna fuckin' beleive this.' she said, in a barely controlled squeal of delight, waving a sheet of paper around in her hands. 'Pete went on a diet?' 'No.' 'Jhub went to sex counseling?' 'No!' 'You just saved a bunch of money on your car insurance by switching to Geico?' 'NO!' She said hitting me in the chest with the sheet of paper, 'Read!' I took the sheet of paper from her hands. It was the card for the next WorldWide. My eyes widened. 'Is this right?' She nodded, a smile showing nearly all of her teeth spread across her face. 'Now you tell me last night didn't mean shit!' she said ready to burst. I just matched her grin with one of my own. As we headed out towards the parking lot I pulled out my cell phone and called Jeff. 'Wylde.' 'Hey fugly. Guess what?' He used the Geico joke on me. 'No, something much better.' 'It turns out last night was just a bad dream and you actually beat Marauder?' 'Not quite that good,' said as I started the car and eased into traffic heading towards LAX. 'But I impressed someone last night, because yours truly is going up against Eva VanLorne for the GCW Television title this week on WorldWide.' I could almost hear the grin spreading across Jeff's face. 'Sweet. You gonna try and break my record for longest title reign?' 'If need be. After all I am the saviour of GCW.' 'Hey, you don't need to lay that shit on me. I know what you're capable of. Save the promos for the fans. But yeah, it looks like Caldy knows what's dropped into his lap. Make me proud Mike.' 'Oh, I plan to.' I said, nodding, despite the fact that he couldn't see me, and I was grinning from ear to ear, 'I fuckin' plan to. Eva wants to play with the big boys, then she's gonna play with the big boys. Besides, Cindy and I think that title would look much better around my waist then it does hers.' Jeff chuckled, not something he does often. 'It ain't the world title, but it's a start. Remind me to call Caldera and negotiate a pay raise for you for when you win.' Again I nodded, remembering that he couldn't see me. 'You take care of the business end, I'll take care of the asskicking end.' After saying our goodbyes, I hung up and Cindy and I started planning my strategy as I speed off towards the airport to get to Portland for WorldWide.
