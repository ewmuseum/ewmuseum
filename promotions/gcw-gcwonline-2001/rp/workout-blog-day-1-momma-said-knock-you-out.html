Workout Blog Day 1: Momma Said Knock You Out

Date: WW113 Workout Blog Day 1: Momma Said Knock You Out The “Heat of the Moment” blares over the crackling computer speakers as Nic Nero furiously curls the biggest dumbbells he can find. The room is pitch black, but the light beaming in through a small pane of glass shows that this is obviously some sort of underground basement. Nero is positioned in front of a wall completely covered in mirrors glistening in sweat, ah, the lubrication of hard work. He is completely absorbed in his lifting; eying every ripple and crevice in his heavily muscled exterior. “Ah that’s the pump, BABY! I’ll show you the fucking heat of the moment!” Miraculously his curls line up exactly with the stomp/clap rhythm of the verse. DO YOU REMEMBER WHEN WE USED TO DANCE? STOMP - CURL STOMP - CURL STOMP - CURL CLAP - SQUEEZE! Out of nowhere the lights flicker on and DeSean Bleak’s massive frame is illuminated in front of the cinderblock wall. Startled and slightly embarrassed Nero quickly drops the weights and lunges for the computer, cutting off the music. Huffing and puffing, Nero puts his hands behind his back, acting as if nothing has happened. “Are you seriously lifting in synchronization with Asia again?” Nero lowers his eyes to the floor. Shame. “Well,” Nero stammers, knowing he’s been caught, “it gets me really juiced up..” DeSean grunts and shakes his head, walking over to Nero. “Listen, I got a call today from the HQ and they wanted us to stop by before we get on the plane to World-Wide. The Boss said something some promo he wants us to do on GCW TV.” Nero rolled his eyes at the thought of making the trip all the way to the Team Projection supplement warehouse and superstore in New York. Each one of their trips seemed to end with them being pushed out the door with absolutely absurd instructions. The last time they made the journey their boss had asked them to track down a rare type of bird that his daughter wanted as a pet for her birthday, before telling them that pet shops don’t carry them. They were forced to hunt and shoot the bird with a stun-gun (but that is a story for another blog). The train ride to 1st and Main in Manhattan was mostly uneventful except for the now common bewildered stares their physiques garnered. After paying the fare and squeezing their hulking frames through the turnstile they made their way down the bustling sidewalk and into the front of the TEAM PROJECTION superstore. The building was four stories tall with each story dedicated to another facet of the team. The first story was the reception area, housing all of the members of the team’s offices. The second was dedicated to the TEAM PROJECTION gym, which was open to the public from 9-5. The third story was reserved for all the sponsored athletes of PROJECTION INC to both workout and stay when they were competing in the city. The fourth story was where the owner of PROJECTION INCORPORATED lived and commanded his army of athletes from. The wrestling subsection of PROJECTION INC was made up of DeSean and Nero as well as a host of singles wrestlers that were currently training to make their GCW or professional wrestling debut. Our duo made their way up the stairs and into the office of their boss whose chair was turned around facing the wall. As funny (or creepy) as it seemed, neither DeSean nor Nero had ever seen their boss, even the day they had be made part of the Projection Inc family. Nero cleared his throat to signal their arrival. “Your throat sore, son? I have some special instructions for you.” “Eh, what's the problem boss?” “Listen. This is important. I want you to take the segment that GCW has given you TV time for and hype our new protein powder.” Both of our heroes did a double take. “But sir, we were going to use that time to talk about how awesome and dominant we were in our match!” “Exactly, you WERE going to do that. NOW you’re going to hype PROJECTION INC’s new protein EXTREME CHOCOLATE SUPER WHEY INFUSED NITRIC BERRY EXPLOSION. Or you’re finished, and by finished I do not mean fired.” Our duo about faced and walked solemnly out of the office, arms crossed over their chests. “Dude this is so beat I was talking to myself in the mirror coming up with all this cool shit to say after we annihilated Holy Hell. I mean this about all the good material. - Holy Hell? They stink, we should call them Holy Smell from now own! - Did you see us give them the PROjection INjection? Holy Hell was so bruised up we should call them Holy Swelled. - Holy Hell SUCKS! See man I came up with all that good shit, I spent like four hours brainstorming this shit before even putting pen to paper. Gold, this is what comedic gold looks like and its all going to waste so we can hype our stupid EXTREME CHOCOLATE SUPER WHEY INFUSED NITRIC BERRY EXPLOSION.” “Yeah, hyping this stuff is going to make us look stupid.” Our members sheepishly glance at the camera.
