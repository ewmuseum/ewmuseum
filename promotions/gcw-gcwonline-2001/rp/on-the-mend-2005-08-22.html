On the mend?

22 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 August 2005 Mary sat up in her hospital bed confused, drained and thirsty. She looked tired, like she hadn’t slept for days. Of course she had slept because the lovely doctors and nurses had pumped her full of enough drugs to force her to, but she never slept soundly. Her dreams were fraught with nightmares now but not just of her Uncle and what he had put her through as usual, but of Lestatt Knight also. Her fears played out short movies inside her head so many times that she had lost track of how many she’d seen. She’d lost track of how long she had been in hospital too but for some reason she hadn’t been bothered with finding out until today. “Go, Go Gadget Mary!” She heaved her legs over the side of her hospital bed but was met with Arael’s firm hands gripping her ankles and almost forcibly flinging them back the way they came. “oh nenhum voc” n’o.” Arael tucked her in while Mary pouted with her arms folded, he seemed unfazed. She furrowed her brow and grabbed his hands to place over her face. Arael’s fingers brushed over her face lightly and he smiled a slight smile while shaking his head. “Now you know that pouting will not get me to disobey Miss Skye’s orders, or the doctors for that matter.” Mary huffed then sighed. “Mary gotta get outta here Eddy, she don’t like hospitals.” Arael perched himself on the edge of her bed and rubbed his brow. He couldn’t remember the last full nights sleep he had had. “I know Pequena, but you need to give yourself time to get better. Miss Skye has been out of her mente` since she has been back.” Arael turned to face her. Mary sat up in bed with some effort. Pain spread over her face and Arael could sense her discomfort, although he didn’t offer to help, Mary could be stubborn like that. “I need you to tell me what happened Chica. I am your protector and I haven’t been doing my job very well. I need to know what happened so that I can begin to do my job better.” Mary looked at him, the memories playing over her face. She shrugged, painfully. “Mary don’t know Eddy. One minute was all exciting and hide and go seeksy and the next thing Mary finds Stattybear in the dining room all evil Angelus and shit. Mary dint even get to screw his soul out first. He was speaking all funny, kept talking like Mary had done something bad, real bad, like he didn’t love Mary no more. Said Mary wrote a letter or something that meant that Mary dint love him no more. But that’s a lie cause Mary only wrote one letter and there was like 3 lines to it and none of em said nothin like that Eddy, none of em, you know how much Mary digs her ChaCha mucho?” “Si Mary I do.” “They fighted, they fighted bad Eddy. Mary thought he wanted to kill her at one point, but it wasn’t that. When she looked in his eyes was like he wasn’t fully there like he was at the bottom of the ocean and all Mary could see was the blue at the surface. He dint wanna kill Mary but he wannid to hurt her and she didn’t unnerstand why. She still not fully sure, but she knows it got summing to do with this note he read, she just dunno where it came from or why someone would do that to them.” “Do you think The Six might have something to do with it?” Mary shook her head, “Don’t think is they style. The Six all like on that Jacuzzi honour shit.” “Yakuza Mary.” “Ya what now?” “Yakuza Mary, not Jacuzzi.” “Oh’yea well yea, them peoples they usually not so like sneaky, they come out and spit in ya face, not behind ya back like that. Besides after what they did to Mary in Mexico they woulda said something.” “You said you were fighting badly Mary, but when I came back I found you both beside each other, your arm was over Senor Abitua.” “Yea, Mary tried to make him see at the end that she still loves him, that she never writed no stooped note, that she never would. She thinks she made a breakthrough too but?” Mary trailed off and looked down at the sheets. “but Mary still don’t know. How does she know he wasn’t just saying that to lie to Mary, to hurt her again.” “Say what Chica?” “He said he loves Mary, and Mary believes him, she believes that he does, somewhere inside him, but she don’t know if all of him does and if that’s true then is only part of him loving Mary enough?” Mary glanced up at Arael with big brown questioning eyes. The swelling had gone down considerably over the last few days she had been in hospital and she was starting to look like a person again not a cabbage patch kid. “I’m not sure if I am qualified to answer such a question Mary but I do know this, that if you don’t at least let yourself find out, you will always be wondering. It is your way Chica.” Mary slumped back into the pillow behind her. “Why everything gotta be so difficult Eddy?” Arael remained silent. “Mary loves him Eddy, loves him lots, she would do almost anything for him. Even when he was hurting her she still loved him and when the house was burning, she didn’t want him to die, she couldn’t stand to see him hurt like that Eddy. Is warped Mary knows considering she smashed more than one vase upside his head but still. He’s still here and that still makes part of Mary smile.” Arael felt uneasy at that moment. He got up and stood by the door, his face gave nothing away to the conflict he felt inside. But after all a job was a job and feelings were never supposed to be involved. He had made that mistake before. He would not do it again. “Have you seen him Eddy” Is he ok?” Arael turned to face her but stayed by the door. “Si I have seen him. He was here but he checked himself out shortly after being admitted and patched up Chica.” Mary’s face fell. “He did” Well, was he alright, he’s not like freaking ironside is he?.” “The doctors say that he has some deep lacerations, some bruising and spraining and he should be in hospital longer, but nothing life threatening. He will recover eventually.” Mary exhaled. “Well that’s something.” She shifted in her bed. “And ya know what’s worse” Mary misses like being with him. She misses the way he sounds and the way he smells, not just the hot hot circus sex, but the noises he makes when he’s’well’ya know, she misses being around him, feels like something’s missing a lil. OH SHIT!” Arael’s hand instantly went to his gun. “Que?” “Shit, Mary nearly frogot. She kinda sorta looked into joining G dub while she was in Mexico.” “You did?” “Yea, Mary wannid it to be a surprise for Statty, figured that maybe if they whuddent spending so much time apart that things like that Eva wouldn’t be hoverin round her man like flies round shit. Not that Statty’s shit, well, he’s hot shit, but you get what Mary means. Mary thought it would be good to be in the ring again too and she was gonna join HSW with Kagerro but then she would have to move to Mexico and she cant put more strain on her relationship with something like that.” “So what will you do now?” “Mary’s gotta go home, she gotta check her messages, see what the G-Dub is saying, and if she’s in. Well, then she gonna rock the freaking casaba and try and fix whatever broke with her and Statty.” Arael smiled. “That’s the Mary I remember.” He looked thoughtful for a moment. “Mary, will you tell Senor Abitua what happened to you in Mexico, what they did to you?” Mary pulled the sheets up around her and her knees up under the sheets and rested her chin on her knees shaking her head. “No, no, He don’t need to know, not right now. Would only confuse everything. Once we back to normal, when things is sorted out then, maybe, maybe Mary will tell him.” Mary pressed the buzzer by her bed. “Right now, Mary wants to go home. Um, Eddy, could you give Mary a minute?” “Si Chica, I will be outside the door until the doctor comes.” “Obrigado Eddy.” As soon as he was gone Mary sat back against the pillows again. She was alone and everything in the room seemed so much louder. It was dark outside and the wind blew at the leaves in the trees outside her window. She wrung her wrists and noticed the hair band she was wearing that was once Roberto’s was still there. She took it off and played with it for a moment before heat burned the backs of her eyes. Then the tears came. She hadn’t really shown any emotion since the night of the fire and she was exhausted from being so numb. She held the hair band to her nose and it smelled like Roberto’s hair. She turned onto her side, her wet face facing the window and curled up under the sheets like a child. “Mary just wants us back.” Mary stared out into the night half wishing there was an answer out there. She got no reply.
