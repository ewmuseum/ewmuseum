A Look to the Past!

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 17th, 1999 Human beings have long aspired to be stronger, faster, better than their enemies. Some ancient warriors such as Hercules and Achilles sought to roam this earth on an equal footing with the divine. However, none has ever compared to those group of people who trace their lineage back to the last Trojan warrior, Aeneas. Roman blood has always been something of a unique life force. It has dominated the world for over twenty five hundred years. But through the ages, the purity of this blood line has been corrupted by infinitly inferior blood. Inter-marrying with lesser races has led to a sharp reduction of the real Roman spirit, the true Roman bloodlust. The quest for blood was what defined Ancient Rome and enabled her to conquer the known world. And yet, despite hundreds of years of decline, one man still has the pure Roman spirit coursing through his veins. As long as he lives, Rome endures. This man's father, however, restrains his son to the boredom of running a multi-billion dollar global corporation. Preparing him for the day when he will die and his son becomes the leader of this vast empire. But something in the son fails to obey his father's wishes. Secretly, he waits for his chance to break free. This young man is currently seen behind a desk in a huge corporate office. He is wearing a black Gucci suit and his hair is slick back. His assistant Vincenzo is also in the room by the door. 'Sir?' 'Yes?' 'Those projections you wanted are in. Third quarter profits are at an all time high.' 'I did not ask for those profits. My father asked you to bring them to me for my analysis.' 'Well...uh...here they are.' Gaius takes the papers and reviews them. 'Excellent, well done Vincenzo.' 'Thank you Sir.' Instead of enthusiasm however, the young man sinks back into his chair with a look of sheer boredom over his face. He lets out a sigh as His executive assistant turns to leave but Gaius stops him. 'Vincenzo?' 'Yes, Sir?' 'Does Niccolo Giganti still work here?' 'Yes, he is the vice-president of marketing.' A smile ascends across Gaius' face. 'Get him on the phone!' 'Right away Sir.' Vincenzo leaves the room and Gaius continues to smile. Within five minutes Vincenzo is on the intercom. 'Giganti for you, Sir.' 'Patch him through.' After a second, a voice comes through. 'Yes, Mr. Octavianus?' 'Niccolo, congratulations! Your going to Japan to our two week conference as my personal representative. You leave in three hours. I suggest you go home and pack.' The man on the other line is ecstatic. 'Thank you, Sir!' Gaius grins sadistically. The man on the other line hangs up. Gaius turns his chair around and looks out toward the city he loves. After a few moments, he turns around and lights a cigar. He has a very unique look on his face, one that looks like the embodiment of boredom. Gaius presses the button again to contact his assistant again. 'Get me Niccolo's wife!' 'Sir?!' 'Now!' The phone rings a minute later. 'Pronto?' 'Valeria?! It's Gaius Octavianus!' 'Ciao Handsome. If you want Niccolo he is upstairs packing.' 'No, I called for you!' 'Really?' 'Yes, your gonna be alone for the next two weeks and I was wondering if you wanted to have dinner tonight.' 'Wow! I don't know what to say!' 'Say yes.' 'Ok, Yes.' 'Perfect. My driver will be there at 8. Ciao Bella.' 'Ciao Gaius.' Gaius leans back and smiles again. His assistant comes into the room. 'Sir, did you just...' 'Vincenzo, do you know how incredibly boring this is. I need alittle excitement around here.' 'Fair enough boss. But he will be upset if he discovers this.' 'Who?! My Father?' 'No. Niccolo!' Gaius just laughs. 'Please. Like he will even have a job when he returns. This is purely a setup for my amusement. Now Vincenzo, get back to work and keep your mouth shut.' Vincenzo leaves the room and Gaius continues to puff on his Cuban cigar. He turns on the huge television in his office. A commercial is on the Italian TV station RAI. 'Do you have what it takes to make it in the wrestling world? Do you seek adventure? Excitement? The spectical of battle? Then contact Danger Enterprises to try out for a new wrestling federation known as ACWF. Call [detail withheld] or visit our corporate office in Atlanta for your career opportunity.' Gaius eyes light up. He summons Vincenzo. 'Yes, Sir?!' 'Pack your bags, we're going to Atlanta...'
