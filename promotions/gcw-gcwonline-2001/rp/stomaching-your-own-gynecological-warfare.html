Date: Once upon a time... A chance has been thrown to me. Like a steak to a hungry dog I'm devouring it without even taking a chance to breathe. My unsettled stomach feels like its been thrown inside of fastest, craziest roller coaster that rolls on this planet and I'm trying my best to keep it settled. Moving quickly and rapidly through the twists and turns I can feel an uneasiness residing within the confines of my digestive tracts and that throwing up feeling remains at the top of my throat ready to purge the day's feast at any moment. If I COULD manage to eat. Life's moving faster than a junkie in a stolen Porsche and all I can do is hang on tight and hope that we don't spin out into oblivion, colliding in the guard rails to keep us in, safe and sound. Whether or not those rails can keep me from plummeting to my doom in the ravine 50 feet below is not up to me. Fate has a funny way of making us want to clutch tightly to the Jesus handle sitting above the door, double check the tightness and security of our seat belts and wrap our knuckles on the fiberglass casing of our helmet. But none of that means anything to fate itself. Your time comes and they say good things happen to those who wait. Does that mean if you're impatient bad things happen? Does that mean if you want a hurry along something God awful will happen? The bad things HAVE happened in my life and I've stood up tall against them. I've gritted my teeth, collected my sword and shielf and gone into battle against them and my courage has proved the tests thrown at me. My bravery is second to none to NO man and that's not even arguable. That's a fact. Many a man would not be able to deal with the complications thrown in my direction and finally this dog has been thrown a bone. Given a second chance at life inside something Global and it feels good. The blood coursing through my veins now feels fresh and revitalised and I feel I could take on the world with my sword and shield. But I could still throw up. That feeling's still there. - - } = = ] s h e p h e r d [ = = { - - We sat sitting in the waiting room of the local hospital, just to check doubley on the verdict the little piss-on-a-stick come up with. It told April she was blue, whatever that meant. It meant something along the lines of she was carrying our child but the box it come in said if it were blue to come into see the doc and he'd make his mind up. And that's where we were. April sat in her chair, knees up around her chest and reading a magazine from 1996. Apparently Tom and Nicole were having troubles. I think Scientology is classified as a little more than a trouble but then again, I'm no expert. The magazine's experts thought that the power couple would overcome their problems and April was reading madly like this were the latest gossip hot of the rumour mills. I let out a sigh as these waiting rooms made me nervous. Last time I was hear they warned me it would cost me an arm and a leg. I got a good deal. They just took a hand. That antiseptic smell was enough to spin anybody out, especially those who had shitty reminiscences of days gone by where they laid in a hospital bed and the doctor went through a heap of technical terms for "we're gunna cut your hand off". So forgive me for feeling a little wonky at the knees. So we'd come, as per instructions of a little cardboard box, to see a gyno. The feeling that some rich prat Rolex deep in my missus made me feel a little edgy, and I hoped that I didn't have to watch coz I could feel a whole heap of jealousy overcoming me with a warm flush even as I thought about it. Do they buy the lady a drink first or just get straight down to business? Probably a little bit of an unlearned view on the whole situation, I'm sure he doesn't get a kick out of it but there's been the odd fish finger surgeon being done by the police for not putting on the gloves. Trying to shake off the feeling of wanting to punch April's gyno I scanned the room to look at the other expecting couples. A man sat across from me rubbing his wife's pregnant stomach like a tourist in Thailand, rubbing the belly of the Buddha. Its funny how many of us Westerners go to the lengths to find a Buddha whose belly you can rub. I think the Thai locals would find enjoyment in watching us whiteys rubbing Buddha's belly and taking happy snaps. I wondered if this guy would get any luck. He put his mouth to her guts every now and again speaking to the expectant child, like he were going to get an answer. Half expecting a little hand to reach out of her belly button and say "Dada?" It was really annoying and I hoped I wouldn't get so awestruck by the fact my April would be smuggling a human of her own if the gyno said the little piss-on-a-stick were right. Seeing people talk to things that won't answer them annoyed the Hell outta me. Like when chicks spoke to their dog. Chick: "Who's a good boy then? Who's a good boy?" Dog: "I am bitch now gimme my God damn biscuit!" I stared at the guy and tried to figure out his name. I liked how some people looked like their name and this guy looked like a Brian. Which made me chuckle. Am I the only person that finds the name Brian humorous? Maybe. Probably. A chubby nurse waddled her way from behind the desk with her clipboard, looking down at it and then at us and the other couple in the waiting room. She stared directly at me, as April was engulfed in her magazine. "Phillip and Tanya?" she asked. I gave her one of those what-the-Hell-you-lookin'-at-me-for looks. Did I look like a Phillip? I hoped not coz no offense to any Phillip's but that is one of the most annoying name's ever. I mean, most guys I've met named Phillip are educated twats or geeks who try to claim a little coolness by shortening it to Phil. I was neither. I was a God damn Brock. Starin' at me like I was a Phillip made me wanna punch the nurse for some reason and I put down to the fact that the antiseptic was getting to me. The baby whisperer put his hand up when he realised I wasn't going to admit to the name, and he helped pull Buddha to her feet as she clutched her back and let out a groan. The chubby nurse tilted her head and smiled as she spun on her heels to lead them into the prying fingers of the gyno. He turned to me, like we'd created some sort of bond by sitting in a waiting room. "Good luck!" he burst out, giving me a thumbs up. I let out a nervous chuckle and the first thing that came into my head came out of my mouth. "You too. Hey, tell him to warm his hands up first!" I called down the hall. He gave me a disgusted look as I felt a sharp boney jab connect with my rib, causing my to jolt and smash my elbow on the arm of the hard plastic chair. And the chair's arm directly found its target in my funny bone, which was never funny at all. I drew breath through my teeth and rubbed at my elbow in agony as April stared at me with a little bit of a chuckle but a little bit of a disgusted look all at once, which made her look quite ugly but I didn't want to mention anything. She'd be destroyed considering she were so excited about the whole ordeal. I didn't wanna ruin her moment. "Brock!" "What?" "That's rude!" "It was the first thing to come outta my head!" I admitted. She shook her head, enjoying my stupidity, which frequented our lives from time to time. "Well, think before you open your mouth," she said, turning back to the escapades of Tom and Nicole's marital troubles. Sighing with discontent for the pain my elbow had been put into I laid out a little, extending my legs out to relax as the anti-septic smell was getting to me now. I was trying my best to keep my wits about me but the anti-septic wasn't helping me maintain my sanity. Wish ol' Mr. Fish Finger would hurry up and get this over and done with. - - } = = ] s h e p h e r d [ = = { - - I sat sitting opposite my manager's desk. Not only had he been a prime candidate for Hall of Fame's for numerous promotions but he was still active within some, most recently as the colour commentator for Spinebuster. Harry Balkin, the Playboy, could still get inside the ring if he wanted to but he was a lot smarter than most guys I'd met. He saw his calling and that behind the announce table and to help people like me be the best that I can. I'd represented myself before and it'd just been a nightmare trying to continue after the injury. Since I've returned to the ring Harry's gotten me into some paid work and the breaks between have been far shorter than when I did it on my own. Harry is a god in my eyes. The door behind me opened and immediately the smell of his cheap cologne soaked the air thickly, even causing me to cough dryly into my sleeve. His confident swagger to his executive style plush leather seat made me chuckle as his ginger mullet swayed with his steps. It'd become somewhat of a patent hair style for Harry and he'd confided in me that he was desperate for a new image but if he did he'd probably have to give up his heelish ways or get in the ring and do one of those "I need a new hair cut so let's have a hair style gimmick match". He wasn't interested in either. He sat down and rested his hands, intertwined in each other, upon his belly. He smiled at me a knowing smile which instantly made me feel all giddy inside. "We've reached an agreement," he confidently tells me. BINGO! "GCW?" I asked. "GCW," he replies, handing across a sheet of paper and tapping it as he places it on the desk in front of me. With a bold Global Championship Wrestling header on the page I scroll through, kinda brushing past the boring B.S. to find what I was looking for. A bold caption which read WorldWide 67 and there was my name. "Brock Shepherd versus Alec Osgoode". "Oh snap!" I called across the desk. "Snap indeed, Brock. Snap indeed. You ready for this? I mean, Spinebuster was a small time promotion, we're talking about a global scale again, Brock. I'm talking BIG time coz if you can't handle this it's gonna hurt both of us. "Are you ready?" Balkin asked me. It was probably the first time I'd ever seen him so serious and that shit-eating grin of his had been wiped off of his face and made me feel a little uncomfortable. The probing questions toward whether or not I was ready to hit the big time again. Was I? Could I handle a program? I mean, walking into a promotion like that would be facing demons all over again. I'd be walking into the mouth of Hell itself when it came to my demons. I hadn't done anything this size since Action! Wrestling. The promotion that stole my hand from me. "Am I ready?" I found myself asking, trying to gather more time so I could figure out exactly if I were or not. I found myself staring at the wall in complete and utter fear, so much so that a bead of sweat had began to make it's way from my forehead to my chin. I brushed my forearm across my forehead in contemplation of whether or not I DID have what it took to take someone of a capability that Alec Osgoode might have. He would be in GCW for a reason, just like me. He clearly had the talent to be the best otherwise he wouldn't be in GCW. There's a reason why people are on these rosters and its because the company thinks they can make money out of them. Simple as that. Could I take down Osgoode? I didn't know but I'd sure as Hell give it the best that I could. I'd try my best to be the best and he was the first step in the GCW ladder for me. The first rung. On my way to the top. Looking at it like that I could feel my confidence recoiling for a strike, ready to pounce and take down whatever stood in my way. I could feel the fear subsiding and my ego inflating as I sat in the chair. A wicked grin rose on my face and I was nodding at Harry. "Oh yeah, Harry. I'm ready. For sure!" And I was. I WAS ready.
