Playing with the Boys

16 March 2009

Date: 03.16.09 Playing with the Boys "Psssh," Mindy hissed as she clutched her pen and stared at the book in front of her on her bed, "I dunno where ta staaart." The young woman whimpered as she stared at the blank page of her diary. One would assume she had plenty to write about; hell, she hadn't wrote in it for ten months. She was finally done rehabbing her leg, had her first wrestling match in years, and came out on top, she should have had plenty to write about. And yet, something was keeping her from getting started. "Come on, it can't be that tough!" she mentally prodded herself. She couldn't remember having so much difficulty with it before... and with that in mind, Mindy flipped a few pages back to some of her past entries. A relationship; a family situation that led her out of Italy; a world title reign under a now defunct wrestling organization. All of it- even the bad parts- seemed interesting and worth missing. Now, everything was bland; forgettable. Mindy won her first match back from her three year hiatus with wrestling, but she still wasn't impressed. She wanted to win more definitively. She wanted it to mean more; not particularly to the people watching or to her employers but to herself. She wanted to find a valid reasoning to what she was doing going back to wrestling to begin with. She wanted more. Mindy thumbed to a random page and began to read. "Gawd I can't believe how long ago this was," she murmured to herself. She could feel herself blush as she read over the entry; while surprised that what she'd wrote about on that page in particular was over an event that really happened, it didn't surprise her that she could remember the distinct joy and excitement she felt when the pen touched the paper. It was that moment that helped the whole thing sink in for her back then. Heya, Diary, Well, I've done it! Who woulda thought that in between the last time I wrote in here and now that I would have won the biggest, bestest title in wrestling? Certainly not me. I still can't believe it myself... in fact it's sooo unbelievable that I've gone this far in such short time. Sheesh, it's unbelievable that I haven't freakin' died yet. That Train... well, he wanted to hurt me, alright. I dunno, though... I just feel as though I'm in some kind of dream that I can't wake up from. I mean, if this is a dream, I wouldn't exactly want to wake up... but I haven't had something so great happen to me without something bad take place in return. I'm just waiting for the part in the dream where I get scared out of it and wake up. But until then... I'm gonna enjoy every second with my belt that I can; it's the only proper thing to do. I don't think Markus expected it, either. When I came home he told me that he practically fainted when he saw that I won! ^^; So cute. I have to admit that I was kinda nervous when first getting back together with him. I guess I'm naive enough to forgive easily. That, or the fact that I realize that he could have turned out worse. Either way, I think I did the right thing; he wouldn't have stayed here with me all this time, supporting me through all my troubles, and doing everything in the world to make me feel better if he didn't love me, would he have? And would I really have been focused enough to win that big shiny title if I didn't believe in the encouragement he gave me? It felt good to read that again, even if there was an underlying bitterness that came about going back to such a memory. Markus played a big part in her getting to where she was at that point. Not in the direct ways like interfering in a match, giving her relentless training exercises, or sucking the Global Wrestling CEO's cock, but by motivating her; reminding her that everything she had done before then meant something to someone. Someone who cared for her and loved her and wanted to see nothing but the best in her, even. And now that person was gone along with the feeling of joy and excitement. No matter title, no more boyfriend, no luck. Of course she didn't expect everything to be perfect from that point forward, but for Mindy to have everything going the way she wanted it and then suddenly lose all of that was frustrating... especially considering that she had no idea how the downslide had started in the first place. No idea how the downslide started, but clear-minded of the things that fueled it along anyway. "Jus' wish tha' win woulda turned things around for me," she huffed to herself. Of course it was a nice start and she wasn't going to quit, but the win seemed pointless; just a way of letting people know that she was still alive. Fortunately for her, her cell phone began to vibrate in one of her pockets before she could allow herself to be bothered by it any more. Mindy fished the phone out of her pocket and checked to see who was calling her. Seeing Markus' name appear on the small screen made her force a small- though perhaps, too hopeful- smile. "Hullo?" As she expected, it was indeed Markus' voice on the other end to speak to her. A little less cheerful than she was used to hearing him, but it was still him. "Heya, Mindy." Mindy sat up on her bed, swinging her legs over the edge. "What makes you call?" she asked curiously. "Heh, are you kidding? Saw you on TV about a week ago. Just, you know... thought I'd say congratulations. I wanted to tell you earlier, but I've just been so busy." "You're not upset to see me back to my old antics?" Mindy asked, her voice perhaps a bit more spiteful than she meant for herself to sound. Markus allowed himself to laugh it off, though. "No, no. I really am glad you got yourself a win. It must feel good, yeah?" "It's a start," she replied. On Markus' end of the phone, Mindy could hear a bit of commotion. "What's going on over there? Sounds biiiizzy." "Hm?" Markus questioned with a brief pause. "Oh, naw, just got some friends over. Drinkin', watchin' the telly, you know." Over Markus' voice, Mindy could hear one of those friends ask who Markus was talking to. He must have covered his phone to answer his friend as Mindy briefly had difficulty hearing anything. However, she finds herself thinking he took his hand off the phone a second early... "What's her cousin been up to? Busy sucking dick?" Mindy blinked. "What was that?" Judging by how Markus sounded then, she could only assume he was as red as a tomato. "Oh... ugh, yeah. Jimmy's just being a douche." "Tha' sooo?" Mindy jested, "Mikey's not gonna like hearin' 'bout thaaat." "Which is why you shouldn't tell him." Mindy allowed herself to laugh. She didn't take kindly to anyone making fun of her or her cousin, but hearing Markus sound defensive made her smirk. "But I have so much fun doin' that to ya." Markus huffed. "Hmph." "Don't ya 'hmph' me, boyo," Mindy teased. "Why not?" "Because I said so!" "Funny, that's exactly why I did it to begin with." Mindy pouted softly. "Oh don't do that," he continued, "I start to feel guilty when you pout." For some reason, while the pout was only playful the comment Markus made about it caused her to feel somewhat worse. "Sure it does." Markus paused at the tone Mindy's voice took then. "I guess I should've expected you to sound like that one way or another." Mindy sighed. She felt apologetic for being so childish, but she didn't say as much. She just remained silent, letting the lack of communication do all the talking until she could find something else to say. "...I'm sorry I disappointed you," she stammered finally. Immediately she regretted the choice of words. Damn it, why can't I really say what I mean? "Disa- disappoint? No. No, Mindy, you didn't disappoint me." Markus tried to sound comforting. "You and I just... I dunno. We were peas and carrots except on one issue and it's more of a problem with me than it is with you. I care 'bout you, Mindy, more than I should. 's why I have a tough time lettin' you go on and keep doin' what you do and just standin' by feelin' helpless." Mindy nodded silently, even though she knew he wasn't there to see it. "I know all that..." she mumbled. "It's just still... tough ta deal with." "I know, Mindy." She let out a soft sigh. This conversation wasn't likely to improve. "Anyways, I gots some stuff ta take care of." "Oh... alright, then." "Yeah... look. I'm sorry to be all bummed out talkin' to ya, hon. Just... ya prolly know how hard it's been fer me an' all. I'll call ya later, if'n ya want me to." "For both of us," Markus corrected. "Don't be sorry. If you need someone to talk to yer more than welcome to call me, alright?" Mindy nodded her head again. "Yeah... okie, then, I'll do that. Thanks, Markus." "Sure thing," Markus replied. "Just take care of yourself, alright?" "You know I always do. Bye, hon." Mindy hung up on him then. It was tough to be able to hear his voice and not fully express how upset she was, but she had an idea of how she could cheer herself up. Instead of putting her phone away, she dialed another number. It took several rings, though eventually she ended up reaching the voice mail. "Oi, this is Michael. I'm prolly busy so if yer family or a friend, leave a message; if yer a female fan leave yer name an' number, if yer a male fan ye better stop fuckin' calling m-" Beeeeep. "Mikeeey, Jimmy insinuated you were busy sucking another boy's peniiiiis." Michael was even quicker answering the call than Mindy expected then. "The fuck did he say?" "Jimmy said you were busy s-" "I know what he said. I just... can't believe he said it." Michael paused for a moment; he sounded like he was breathing somewhat heavy. "That's alrigh', though. I'll get a hold of tha' little shit eventually." Mindy managed to squeak out a small giggle. "Jus' thought you should know. Anyways, you doin' okay? Sound kinda tired." "Yeah," Michael said with a grunt, "yeah fine, was jus' exercising when you called. Why, what's up? Or was that all you wanted ta tell me?" "Pretty much," Mindy said, grinning sheepishly, causing her cousin to groan. "Guh. Alright then. How're you doin' anyway?" "Hangin' in there." "You fuckin' better be, after that match ya had last week. You did great." "Heehee... thanks a lot." "You took off for the next show, right?" Mindy exhaled deeply. "Yeeeah... didn't feel up for gettin' right back in there just yet." "Why not?" Michael questioned. "Figured ya'd be anxious to get back in there while you got some momentum goin'." Mindy waved her free hand dismissively and flicked her wrist. "Eh, I jus' wanna be sure I dun' burn myself out again is all." "Mm, guess I can understand that. Deserve a break after the beatdown you put on Ngozi anyways." "I appreciate that, really," Mindy giggled again. "I'll be good ta go next week fer sure, just need ta make sure mah head's screwed on straight." "Fair 'nough, cuz. Any idea who yer facin' next?" Mindy nodded slightly as she replied. "They're thinkin' of puttin' me against two other newcomers. Brock Alyas an' Ash Bombay I think their names were?" Michael's voice grew more intrigued suddenly. "I hope you didn't jus' say the name I thought I heard ya say." "Which one?" "The second one." "Ash? Whut about'im?" Michael went silent for a long moment before answering. "...I stole that fuckwit's sneakers in the middle of a match once. You better win." Fortunately Mindy wasn't drinking anything, because she probably would have spat it out then. "You what? I never knew 'bout that!" She wasn't complaining about knowing about it now, however. It would actually give her a reason to show up next week. "Yeah yeah," Michael said, "wasn't really much of a challenge so I didn' think to tell ya. Figured it'd be like excessive abuse to a retarded kid or somethin'." Mindy tilted her head. "But stealin' his sneakers was perfectly good natured?" "Oi," Michael cut in, "tha' little shit deserved it. Actin' like some fat oompa loompa who watched one too many John Cena interviews." "Well at least he's got good taste?" Mindy teased. Michael didn't like that very much. "I suddenly want something very bad ta happen to ya." Mindy grinned widely. "Oh I'm jus' joshin'. Seriously, though..." Mindy trailed off then, finishing the sentence in a hushed tone as if she were afraid someone else would hear, "...did joo really steal tha' guy's sneakers?" "You're fuckin' A right I did," Michael answered sagely. "And you better put a hurtin' on him somethin' fierce. Gotta do our family name good an' all that jive." Mindy shook her head, snickering to herself. "Since when did ya care about that, huh?" "I don't. I'm jus' sayin' you oughta win this one, barrin' Brock havin' more similar traits to Brock Lesnar aside from the name." "Such as?" Mindy inquired with a quirked brow. "Steroid abuse." "Oh. That, uh, I dunno. I dunno who either of'em are, really. Jus' what you told me." "Right... well, you still better win. Yeah." Mindy smirked. "Uh oh, I dun' wanna bring yer wrath upon me for losin'! I really better win now, 'cause I was totally lookin' ta lose until you threatened me." "Oh you laugh now," Michael warned, "but you'll rue the day you did if you lose. You will rue." "Wha's tha' word mean?" "Means don't fuck with me or I'll fuck you up. Know what I'm sayin'?" Mindy blinked. "Right... aaaanyway. Done swearin' now?" "Yeah... yeah. Think I'll let ya go worry about that. I need to track Jimmy down and break one of my feet off in his asshole." "Don't hurt'im too bad," Mindy scolded, "you remember whut happened last time." "Sure sure, I'm not payin' anyone's hospital bill this time 'round, I ain' worried about that. Just gonna pay him a li'l visit." "Whatever ya say, Mikey. Anyways, I'll seeya later." "Yep. Lata, cuz." Michael put his phone back down on the coffee table and moved to the center of his living room, laying on his back over the carpet. "No one says I suck dick without getting into a whole world of shit. Fucking bastards." As Michael said this to himself, he thought nothing of being shirtless and sweaty while doing stomach crunches in front of his television, which just so happened to be playing the infamous volleyball scene from Top Gun. Anyone who passed by his house then could likely hear 'Playing with the Boys' blasting from inside if they listened close enough. Sad thing, though, was that Michael was taking his workout seriously. He just hoped that his cousin wasn't slacking off for her next match, either.
