Date: 02/20/10 Things weren't always so easy for The Bulletproof Blonde... January 31, 2006 (WorldWide #38): Vivica J. Valentine makes her debut in Global Championship Wrestling as part of a stable known as Dies Irae. Teaming with superstars such as Lia Ambrosi, Dawn McFarlane, and Seymour Almasy... Vivica manages to find her way in with little-to-no wrestling training. Learning the bare basics from Christian Zenith and being trained by newly acquainted Lia Ambrosi, Valentine fails to show much of anything to the GCW fans. Expectations from the GCW faithful are low for the beautiful, petite blonde from Highland Park, Illinois... but Lia Ambrosi would become a best friend and mentor to the green superstar. February 21, 2006 (WorldWide #40): In her first ever singles match, The Black Mariah shocks the world by defeating GCW mainstay Sam Wolack for the United States Championship. The victory is very controversial, and eventually led to the exit of several roster members. July 9, 2006 (Warpath 2006): In the first and only LiveWire match in the history of GCW, Vivica J. Valentine successfully defended her United States Championship against one of the greatest US Champions of all time in Eva VanLorne. Withstanding electricity shooting through her body, her valiant effort is saluted by a standing ovation from the sold out crowd. October 28, 2006 (WorldWide #53): Brandon Youngblood makes an example out of the rising star, breaking her record breaking US Title reign at 247 days. Her reign is saluted by fans and staff alike, granting her a World Championship shot against Hall Of Famer LeStatt Knight. She would come up short, which started a streak of losses that would become as infamous as her title reign. January 14, 2007 (NC-17 2006): Tensions in Dies Irae boil over. Having not won a match since her loss to Brandon Youngblood, Vivica becomes the laughing stock of her team. Finding some self-confidence she becomes brash, violent, and defiant... standing up to stable leader Virgil Ambrosi. Her aspirations to prove his criticisms wrong end on that night however, when her losing streak continues at his hands. There were times where it seemed like it just wasn't meant to be... February 8, 2007 (WorldWide #60): Bryan Mayhem viciously attacks Vivica J. Valentine in the Dies Irae dressing room, giving her a concussion and making her pass out due to blood-loss. The next week on WorldWide, Valentine would reveal her attacker and finally say the words to the rest of Dies Irae that the fans had wanted to hear for months. "I Quit!" The ovation was thunderous. June 3, 2007 (Warpath 2007): With a change of heart comes a change in outcomes. The losing streak of the newly dubbed "Fearless Phenom" is snapped and victories over Markus Stone and Jayce Bradley put her into a well hyped rematch against LeStatt Knight. This time Valentine is victorious as things come full circle. Later that night Vivica is defeated in the finals by Jordan White, but her imprint as the next big star in GCW was already sealed. August 12, 2007 (Tsunami 2007): After threatening Lia Ambrosi with fire and tormenting her all the way up to taking away her World Heavyweight Championship, Bryan Mayhem steps into the ring with the crowd pleasing aerialist who'd been waiting to get her hands on him for months. Mayhem successfully defeats Valentine and nearly sets her on fire for her troubles. August 27, 2007 (Dangerous Games 2007): Facing Bryan Mayhem in his own element, an Inferno Match, Valentine steps into the show as a massive underdog. Fighting adversity as well as her own fears, Vivica still doesn't manage to defeat Mayhem. She would have her arm set on fire on her way to a tragic loss. Written off for the Dangerous Games match due to a severe injury, Valentine shocks the world by entering the battle royal anyway and wins with only one-arm... last eliminating long-time nemesis LeStatt Knight to become #1 Contender for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship held by Lia Ambrosi. November 16, 2007 (WorldWide #77): A victory over recently debuted wrestler Clyde Fox isn't enough to sway the fans in Valentine's favor as President Steven Caldera has successfully convinced the public that Vivica has betrayed her best friend and joined The Establishment despite her protests of the opposite. Later it would be revealed that she was setup by Peter Maxwell, who took the spot she rejected in the stable. Proven innocent, the crowd was behind her as she took care of Maxwell on her own terms, defeating him in a singles match the week before NC-17. December 23, 2007 (NC-17 2007): Tears flow down the eyes of The Fearless Phenom as she becomes the new GCW World Heavyweight Champion, defeating Lia Ambrosi to a standing ovation and a heartfelt show of respect in the middle of the ring. On the day before Christmas Eve, her dreams come true. At last, her destiny of winning the World Title was achieved. But at the end of the day, dreams were meant to come true... August 13, 2008 (WorldWide #95): An ever-growing battle against Steven Caldera and the Establishment wages on as Valentine walks into WorldWide after arguably the most impressive victory in her career to date: a successful defense against the newly "Established" Jay Terror and would-be 2008 Worker of the Year, Jordan White. Upon her arrival, she's blindsided by an immediate title defense against Jay Terror, where she loses in shocking fashion. One of the most memorable world title reigns in GCW history comes to a startling end, after 235 days. February 4, 2009 (NC-17 2008): Her bids for a rematch are denied and her dreams of once again becoming World Champion are shattered by The Establishment and new member Chris Bagwell, who is looking to make a name for himself by taking out "the face of the company." After months of verbal abuse and attacks on her closest friends, Valentine defeats The Human Hype Machine and thwarts yet another Steven Caldera attempt to break her. Although the world calls for her rematch to gain back the World Championship, it would never come. They said it was an incredible string of good luck... February 14, 2009 (WorldWide #105): Valentine, being snubbed in her plea for a title opportunity, takes the fight to The Establishment just 10 days after NC-17 by facing Tyson DeBough and Brian Fisher in a handicap match. Tessa Windsor volunteers her services, turning the match into a tag team affair. Establishment members and their most heated enemies all join in the action before Windsor's husband Chris Storm and Banned & Exiled~! tag team partner Markus Stone shock the world by attacking Valentine and joining the Establishment. They would bring with them the GCW World Tag Team Championships. June 21, 2009 (Rampage 2009): Vivica J. Valentine and newly recruited tag team partner and close friend Lindsay Troy defeat Establishment members Banned & Exiled~! for the Tag Team Championships. The victory is bittersweet, however, as immediately afterwards Steven Caldera ruins the party by terminating Lindsay Troy's contract. July 26, 2009 (WorldWide #114): After the firing of friends Lindsay Troy and Tessa Windsor, as well as the pushing away of new close friend Andy Murray in fears of the same, Valentine is forced into her second handicap Tag Title defense against FishBough. Unlike the first, she is not victorious. They said that she was building an empire destined to fail... October 28, 2009 (Dangerous Games 2009): After her impressive performances in past years, commentators and fans alike being to anoint Valentine "Miss Dangerous Games." She would have a match against former Man In Black and friend Digital Mortality, who returned after a long absence to make a name for himself. Unfortunately for Mortality the battle against Valentine was an uphill one and he fell short, almost as if foreshadowing Valentine's Dangerous Games performance later in the night. It seemed as if she had the match won until Jay Terror made his GCW return to keep her from becoming the first ever two-time Dangerous Games winner. November 25, 2009 (WorldWide #121): After giving up his World Title shot to pursue "bigger things," Jay Terror moves on and Vivica is slated to face Clyde Fox in a #1 Contender’s match for her now-boyfriend Andy Murray's GCW World Title. In a very controversial finish, Valentine grabs the ropes to become #1 Contender and earn her long-awaited World Title rematch. But for the first time since 2006, the fans are not pleased with her victory. Instead, they find favor in the very lovable Clyde Fox. December 23, 2009 (Christmas Chaos 2009): Two years to the day after her only World Title victory, Vivica J. Valentine is eliminated in a Deck The Halls Match by an "accidental" shot from Andy Murray. This incident, coupled with Murray forfeiting on her behalf in a match against long-standing rival LeStatt Knight, embarrassing her with revealing outfits, and handling her with kid gloves, causes Valentine to finally snap. She drills him in the face, costing her team the match. Shocked by her own actions, Valentine watches as her entire world falls apart around her. Three years worth of hard work, injuries, and sacrifice to gain the love of GCW audience goes away with one well placed boot to the mug of the Scottish King of Cool. At the end of the day, they shouted the tragedy from the rooftops like it was never meant to end. Her inevitable fall from grace was much harder to watch than they ever imagined. The children averted their eyes, hiding from the scenes that brought tears to their impressionable faces. As the harsh reality of her collapse set in, the masses began to speak of bringing closure to her legacy; a quick and painless end to a rapidly tarnishing image. How do you properly snuff out the brightest light of them all? For in the shadows lurk the most sinister of intentions. When the light is gone, they are free to roam however they please.
