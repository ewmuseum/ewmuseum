Training Day Part 1

12 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/07 START Since we last left Clyde and his companion Squirtle, they were previously engaged in a rather emotional discussion. Clyde, now ready to take on the world, has prearranged an aerobic workout just to make sure that he is in optimal wrestling form. Little did he know; Clyde was in for a rude awakening. The local city bus pulls up to the curb on a rather hot and sticky afternoon in the city. The doors screech open and out bustles The Young Fox with Squirtle in hand. "Gee Squirty Squirtle, I sure hope all those burritos you made me eat last night don't hurt my performance today!" Fox exclaimed. Squirtle said his usual silence.... it is a plush doll after all. "Okay Squirt Squirt, in the words of the Japanese; let's get on it!" Fox uttered, "Beeee teeee double you, that stands for by the way Squirt Squirt, I have been meaning to learn Japanese! Maybe someday you can help me!" The future superstar and up-and-coming manager progressed to the front sliding doors of the Gold's Gym facility. With a prompt wiping of his shoes on the entrance way carpet, Clyde slowly creped into the lobby filled with a shyness about him. "Hi there," The lady at the front desk welcomed, "Welcome to Gold's Gym, I'm Satin." "I like cashmere better," Fox returned. Satin stood behind the desk and paused with a puzzled look on her face. "Um, so I'll just need your membership card to swipe you in," Satin asked. "Double you teee eff? You have to be a member to play here? But I know plenty of jungle gyms that are free!" Fox pouted. "This is no jungle gym," Satin replied. "But.... but, but I made an appointment!" Fox begged. "Um okay, what's your name sir?" Satin said as she took her seat and starting to click around on the admin computer. "FOX!" Clyde stated with pride. "Oh that's a neat name," Satin conversed. "Yeah it is," Fox led on, "It's cool I guess. I personally think that 'Bunny' would have suited me more considering I'm such a sexy pervert." And with that, Fox tilted his head back and caught some of his drool which was escaping from his mouth. "Excuse me?" Satin turned to Fox and asked. "What did I say?!" Fox recovered, "I mean, I'm such a slave for women! Or no, I meant I'm a salt lover, or I meant I'm a sex man, sexy man, pervert of sex.... SEX PERVERT!" Everyone in the area stopped and looked at Clyde. "Oops.... ummm so.... how's the appointment coming?" Fox shly asked. "It doesn't exist, now GET OUT!" Satin enforced. And with that Satin got up, slapped Clyde across the face and signaled security over to throw him and Squirtle out of there. "No lemme go you big muscle heads! IT WAS SQUIRT SQUIRT WHO SAID, HE FILLS MY MIND WITH DIRTY THOUGHTS, TAKE HIM, TAKE HIM!" Fox screamed. Regardless of whatever he blabbered, Fox and Squirtle ended up on their backs just outside of the entrance. "That t'was rough eh Squirtle?" Fox said as he brushed himself clean. The Kid proceeds to gather himself and Squirtle again when they spotted a gym across the street. "Lookie! Maybe we can still get our aerobic workout there Squirt Squirt!" Fox approached the road to cross it. It wasn't very busy at all but there were procedures to be taken. "Okay Squirtle, you look left and I'll look right and then we can cross," Fox directed. After a moment Fox assessed the situation. "Clear on my side," Fox checked in. Squirtle didn't appear to say anything. "You say clear too Squirt Squirt? Okay so we are off to the other side of the road.... last one across is a rotten eggie.... BIG TIME!" Fox said. And with that, Fox, holding Squirtle's hand, starting walking across the street to the other gym. However, on their way there, a car came driving by. More specifically, from the direction that Squirtle was suppose to look after. Clyde turned his head and was within a foot of the car that was driving by. Ironic, a Fox giving a 'deer in the headlights' look. "SHOOTY!" Fox yelped as he dived out of the way. The tires of the car screeched as the front bumper slammed into Fox's diving feet. "OWIE, OWIE, OWIE!!!" Fox shrieked in pain. The car stopped and the man driving it got out to observe the wounded deer fox. "Holy shit man, watch where you're walking! Are you okay?" The man asked with concern. Fox lay silent, crying, eyes open.... sucking his thumb in the fetal position. "Hey man, I'm talking to you!" The driver investigated. Clyde slowly crawled into a sitting position. "S-s-s-sir," Fox said. "Yeah?" The man answered. "I HAVE A BOBO ON MY KNEE!" Fox cried. His knee was slightly red. "Are you serious?" The driver asked confusingly, "That's all that hurts? I mean I hit your feet when you were diving!" "THAT'S NOTHING COMPARED TO THE PAIN YOU PUT ME THROUGH!" Fox screamed as if he were accusing the driver for murder. Clearly Clyde has no concept of what actually hurts and what doesn't. "Go away you mean man, you scraped my knee!" Fox said. "Yo dude, I don't mean to be rude, but you walked in front of my car. You got issues man," The driver said as he went back to his car and drove away. Fox still sat on the ground with what few tears left still on his face. "You know what Squirtle," Fox started, "When life scrapes your knees, you gotta keep on plugging. Dat's exactly what I'm going to do. Let's get to that gym!" FINISH
