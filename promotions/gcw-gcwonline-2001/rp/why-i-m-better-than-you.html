Why I'm Better Than You

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Everyday Don’t get me wrong, I love this world; I love women, sports, and many other things…I also love winning, a lot. I have taken it upon myself to become the best at everything possible and thus far it hasn’t failed me yet. What I am trying to say is that I’m better than you. Yes, you, you, you, and you. All of you, I am better than every single person in GCW, PTC, rW, SCCW, PRIME, any place, EVERY PLACE, yet these places mean nothing to me, nothing at all. They are acronyms, random words pasted together, scribbles on the wall in the men’s toilet, useless little letters that stand for something…nothing at all. I can’t tell you how good it is to get this off my chest; I thought I was going to explode there with madness for a moment. No matter, it’s all a matter of perception. What you think and what I think are different, but you need to realize in every situation, I am always right, I am better than you, no matter how many times I say it, no matter how many times you hear it, I will undoubtedly be better you than you. Don’t get me wrong, you may talk to talk, but you certainly can’t walk the walk and that’s why I am here, to give a lesson in my ways, a chance to look at the method from my madness… * * * …2 Nights Ago… Another typical Tuesday afternoon; I’m just sitting here minding my own business and I am struck by inane ramblings from someone that I am contractually obligated to call my manager. “Let me lay down the facts for you…” “Alright, hit me Mr. Big Shot. I would love for you to set me straight.” “Okay, listen, you are a nobody here.” “Uh, wow, what is this all about? Did I happen to strike a nerve in that head of yours, mate?” What is it of his business to call me out like that? Apparently he knows nothing, if he is going to speak about me like that, to my face no less! “Lucas, I am just trying to give you the facts, you may have been well known in England, you are basically their Paris Hilton.” “How....how dare you?! How dare you compare me to….who was that again?” He makes no sense to me, yet again. He tries to get me to do and understand these mundane things that I am not accustomed to. I don’t really care for all the attention that I seem to NOT be getting, but for his sake I will play along. “You drink, you fight, and you say things you are really not supposed to.” He shakes his head at me. He knows me better than I thought. “What do you mean? I haven’t drank or fought in literally days, mate; it’s all good I know what I am doing. Your concerns are dually noted, Chris.” He left me to my own accord. What he really needs is a swift slap to the face, he may be damn good at what he does, but he still has a lot to learn. Maybe that’s my problem too, nah, can it? Am I just full of myself? No, no that can’t be it, I am good…right? Yeah, no need to get myself worked up over anything. I am good at what I do, I am the best, and there is absolutely no doubt about that. * * * …Last Night… Time… Time… Time… Who has time anymore? Who has the time to do anything? All we do is sleep and shit and sleep some more. We are supposed to be animals, the top of the food chain, the dominant thing on this planet, yet we sit around a twiddle our thumbs like some sort of circus side show. What are we means to our end? Do we stand around and work for eight hours a day for monetary value that has really no justifiable meaning to it? Why? It’s all a waste of time, we should be getting back to our primal urges, our status as the top dogs has come to an end, and I am here to fix that. Our inner most deepest urges are suppressed by our morals and our obligations to one another; I am here to fix that. Don’t believe everything you are told and don’t regret a thing for a single moment. There are things that I just can’t get out of my head. My mind is buzzing with memories of the past and how I would have done differently. The past can’t change, but the future hasn’t been set in stone. I am going to change things, I am going to be the biggest, most graceful athlete to grace this place since…well I can’t really think of anyone important off the tip of my tongue, but rest assured my time is coming, I can feel it, in fact I just don’t feel it, I know it. All this time wasted on petulant family feuds, stabbings, shootings, have no effect on my in regards to the upcoming weeks. I know what I have to do, I know what’s at stake, what’s at store. History hasn’t been made, but when it does it will be great. They will be shouting my name from the rooftops and I will be what I set out to be…a god. A god among men, ha, I would turn this world upside down in a second…in a flash…by the snap of my fingers. I can feel it, can you? Can you feel this power, I am not talking about wizards or demons or anything of that sort, this power of being alive, of being your best, being the one everyone else looks up to. Having those goals set in sight for me, seems rather reasonable. “Damn fool, have you no shame?” I was already feeling pretty divine like. This is me in a nutshell. Whenever I can’t get what I want I will tell point blankly what is wrong with them. I have no trouble with speaking my mind; I never have, unlike most people in this world. They are too afraid to speak, to speak their minds, to get what they want, not me though, I am what you see here today, and I am not afraid to show it. “I tell you, you have no idea what you are doing. Did your mother teach you how to do that? I mean come on, mate.” I don’t mean to be crass…well of course I do. I don’t like nonsense that could very well be avoided. “Lucas, every single month you come in here, we go over the same exact thing, you insult me every single time, and I tell you every single time, you are healthy. I draw your blood, you complain. Coming in for a physical every month isn’t going to change the fact that you are healthy. Most of my patients come in for a physical one or twice a year, but you seem to beat that average by a long shot, why is that? What are you afraid of?” The doctor asked a lot of good questions, why do I do these things? “Doctor Sanchez, I am going to be frank with you, if I can. I want to be the best, not just the best, the very best, if I find even one imperfection on me, I will lose it, you have to be careful sometimes when you go out there into the real world, because you can lose that edge, you know.” He was nodding along with what I was saying, I think he could care less what I thought, I knew he was annoyed with me, my constant jarring and rambling about things that could not concern him. “Well, Lucas, I know what you mean, when you are a doctor such as myself, you have you become the very best at what you do, it is not enough that you get good grades in school or have the highest clientele in the tri-state area, you need to be on top of your game.” That was one thing we could agree on, I don’t respect a lot of people, but I could tell where he was coming from. “Anyway, Lucas, again I would like to tell you that you have a clean bill of health. Though, I would recommend laying off the alcohol a bit, else you might have extensive liver damage.” “Yeah, I’ll do that, Dr. Sanchez.” No, I wasn’t going to do that. That was the only sane thing I had left to do. “Call me Lucien…” he says as he extends his hands to me. I shake his hand and nod in agreement. I get up off the examination table and shake his hand again. “I hope I won’t be seeing you anytime soon, buddy.” “No, mate, I don’t think you will.” Of course I had already booked my next appointment for next month, much to his disarray. He was correct in his words though, you need to be careful, because you can lose that edge, and with that edge gone, you have nothing, absolutely nothing. You don’t just become better than everyone else in an instance, you have to work on it, you need to build up your foundation to reach the next step, then you reach the next step, and so on and so forth until you come to your peak, and when you reach that peak you have to do everything imaginable to stay there, because if you get knocked off, you are done, you are a shell of your former self, you have no reason to live anymore. Luckily for me, I don’t have that problem, I’ve hit my stride and it is time to show the world who I really am. * * * I need to have a plan. If I don’t have a plan, I can’t concentrate, and if I don’t concentrate then I won’t be on top of my game, what am I supposed to do? I can’t sit around here and do nothing all day while everyone is out there living their lives. I need structure, balance, or else I can’t function any longer. I am going to go mad with blind rage if I can’t get over this. I got it. I know what I am going to do. I have always known what to do, all along. I just need a drink, oh sweet ecstasy, won’t you bit my tongue and call me a bad boy. I need your sweet release, my whole body is aching. To hell with that doctor, I am a capable person. I walk over to my bar and get myself a sweet everlasting drink. “Cheers.” I down one after another after another. Why hasn’t anything kicked in yet? Where are my mates when I need them? I could use someone to confide in, to tell my innermost sanctimonious secrets to. All alone, was this what it is like to be at the top, to be the person everyone looks up to? To be alone, no matter how many people you have around your shoulder, no matter who you have around your campfire at night, you will always be alone, you will always be by yourself. Has it been all for nothing? I reach under the bar and pull out my university diploma. It reads “Lucas Quinton” at the top and “B.S. Philosophy” at the bottom. One of my first achievements, it’s funny; I didn’t have to work as hard as I do now to achieve success. It was important to me that I complete it, which is how I operate. Once I get into something, I will work and work at it until I become the best, the alpha male, the top dog, the head of the class, so on and so forth. Some might say I have a tendency to overdo and over think things… Fuck them… I know what I have to do. So what if I go overboard? That is how I operate. Just because you don’t like the way I work things doesn’t mean you have to criticize me. The world is a game, and I found a way to get to the next level, the next path, to reach the top…to reach number one on the leader-boards, that is who am I and make no mistake about it. * * * …Morning… It was cold outside. The breeze met my lips and gave me a cold slap. I could tell it was going to be a bad rest of the day. I pause and take in the long deep breathe. As I sucked in that air, the ice crystals snare inside me and protrude my throat I cough a few times while rubbing my eyes, I could see where this was going. “I’ve had better days than this.” I paused again for a bit and looked out through the horizon. I could barely see the sun. It was fighting the clouds for dominance. I knew exactly how it felt, fighting your way to the top, I could sympathize with it, going around in circles until finally you let loose and have you best moment shine through. I take a sip of my coffee, I hate the stuff really, but it only keeps me away for a little longer each time I drink it. As I walk back into my house, I hear this whirring noise coming from some sort of device and then a loud cracking noise and then a… CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! BOOM! I run towards the end of my property line and peek over the divider. I find a stepladder nearby and ascend to the heavens like the deity I should be looked up as. I can see my neighbor holding his hands up in victory and he just cut down a tree with his chainsaw. My neighbor, my fucking neighbor, of course he has to do something as stupid as this. Cutting down a tree, at this time of day? What are you mad? Of course I wouldn’t tell him this face to face as I do like living here, don’t like pissing off those neighbors anymore than I have to. “What is going on?” I enquired to my neighbor. He didn’t hear what I was saying, his chainsaw was going full blast and any attempts to contact him were drowned out by that infernal machine. He was a shorter fellow than I, dark hair, think horn-rimmed glasses, a heluvalot older than me, and a grin you can shake a stick at. He powers down the chainsaw and removes the eyeglasses. “What did you say?” “I said, what is going on, Mr. Lapidus?” “Well as you can see, as clear as day, I just chopped down this friggin tree. The leaves are driving me nuts, the Misses didn’t want me to cut it down because we had it since we moved into the house, oh I say about 20 or so years ago.” I didn’t need to hear his story. This guy’s background was a lot more interesting than mine, in a twisted way, he made his big payday day-trading, but I think he face tells a different story, I could see he was a liar, a manipulator like myself, he probably made all that money illegally, and he was happily shoving it in everyone’s faces. He was the kind of man who didn’t give a shit about anything. Not my problem though. My ears are still ringing from that chainsaw. “You saw how I took down that tree, right? You saw?” “Yeah I saw, Lapidus. Great job I guess.” I really didn’t have anything else to say, I guess it’s nice that he is happy for something. “Yeah, I knew you saw it. I took down that tree like a bear. It was begging me to cut it down, to end its life. They would have been cruising for a bruising! A clamoring for a hammering! It woul-…” I cut him off; I didn’t need to hear all that. “Good…good for you, mate. I am happy that you accomplished something so…great.” Ha, another lie carefully constructed. “A victor I am, a mighty warrior, a god, all you worthless heathens watch out. Lapidus is on the warpath…Isn’t that right Mr. Lucas Quinton, wrestler extraordinaire…You know I watch that wrasslin’ you guys put on every week, if I was a few years younger I would scrap with the best of ya…” If there is one thing I hate, it’s fans, not that I hate these people, I just can’t stand their constant questions and belligerent attitudes about how they could be the best if they just had that one shot, that one chance to show them all. I put on the best fake smile I could ever muster…show time. “That’s wonderful, Lapidus. Listen, I have to get inside and do something other than this. See you around. Congrats on that tree victory by the way. You sure showed him who the boss is.” I roll my eyes and continued back into my house. He didn’t know a thing, that was the problem of most people these days, they didn’t know. They had no clue about the inner workings of things. They had no idea what the meaning of honor and sacrifice meant. People are really now all about the nonsocial interactions like the computer or video games, it wasn’t there nature anymore to go out there and live and become one with the earth. I try to avoid this hippy bullshit, but I do believe in karma. Bad things happen to happen people and good things happen to good people, this all went into a big dramatic circle, messing with somebody could have any effect on you…ten times worse coming back as it did going in. I feel like I have this stronger connection, this connection makes me one with everything, not in the fantasy sort of sense, but when you get that feeling that you need to do something, and you’re the only one who can do it, that type of feeling. I rest my coffee cup on the nearest table and hop on my couch and turn on the television. I think it’s quitting time already; I cover myself with a blanket and shut my eyes. “I feel a whole god damn lot better…” It was time to get some rest. The day had already started, but I was ready to end it. Forward on I go. * * * …FINAL THOUGHTS… Subtlety wasn’t one of my better suits. I had to be in the middle of things all the time. As much as I liked to see other people I know and like succeed, I just have to be become better than. I would go up to their faces and say ‘Hey, nice job.’ But I was also adding in ‘and let me show you how it is done.’ It didn’t matter to me anymore who was my friend and who wasn’t, but if they were going to try and one up me, I was going to show them up twice fold. That was who I am; I could be an asshole and have no qualms about it. I like to see myself as some sort of hero, a majestic, a king, a lord. I know people who look up to me and know I mean what I say and I say what I mean, if I am going to do something, I was going to do it, no questions asked. When we are dead and gone, how are people going to remember us? You can’t take account of other people’s actions, they might be good or bad, polar opposites. What we have to offer society is our blood, sweat, and tears. What would it say if we forgot who we really are? We may be humans, but we are the true animals by heart. Our animal instincts leads us to our automatic self-preservation which is our key to surviving and living on this world, our emotions drive our actions, but at the end of the day is what we have to account for. This is how people are going to remember us, by what we gave to the world at the end of the day; how we affected each and every person we come in contact with. It was my turn, finally my turn to make this world a place of my own, to shape it like my own ball of clay and mold it into what I would a perfect society. We all take things for granted, and I am not a stranger to that. I could easily be a part of the fray like a sheep. I am not sheep, I am a wolf and I am hungry for more, more action…more adventure, to prove to everyone that I am not just another ordinary person, I am greater than that, greater than you. I’m going to turn this whole god damn place upside down… I’m not going to take anyone’s shit anymore, not this time around. The recognition, the fame, the fortune, they can have that all back. I know who I am…I know what to do...I know how to do it…I know where to do it… Herein lies the million dollar question, here lies the whole point of anything anymore. Why am I better than you? Because I am…and that is all you need to know.
