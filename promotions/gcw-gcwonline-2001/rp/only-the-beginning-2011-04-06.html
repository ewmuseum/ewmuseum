Only The Beginning

6 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/11 (Losing has always been my best trait. I am usually one who can take a loss with a grain of salt, but to not even have a debut match, I became a bit frustrated.) (I have yet to enjoy a single nights rest without the constant remembrance of NC-17. To be taken out of a match before the bell sounds is a moment of embarrassment, and a shot to a man's dignity. How am I to be a force to be reckoned with when your debut leaves you walking directly back to the locker room with no experience gained, and all pride lost. That's more of a double edged sword.) (Flashback to NC-17 VIII) Van Jahan Surgoth is trying to power out of the clothesline, and for extra torque, Trey Arbor leaps up and wraps his legs around Surgoth's body. In response, Surgoth lunges forward and drops Trey Arbor over the top rope and to the outside! 0:01 Bryan: And Trey Arbor might not make it back to the ring in time! Terrence Kingsley quickly whips through the ropes and into Ring B as Surgoth turns around. Both looks at Aimz on the canvas before Terrence Kingsley shakes his head at Surgoth, pointing at Aimz and declaring "she's mine." DING! DING! Bryan: And it's official, and Josh Briggs is telling Trey Arbor that he is no longer eligible for this match! Yale: Trey doesn't seem to be taking too kindly to that. (Fast forward the flashback) Ring B Van Jahan Surgoth is too busy waiting for a good time to jump in on the brawl to notice that Trey Arbor is back in the ring and behind him. Bryan: Van better look out! He doesn't. He finds himself quickly upside down as an inverted Mitchinoku Driver plants Surgoth face first on the canvas. Bryan: Thorn Driver by Trey Arbor! Yale: Looks like that'll be the end of him! Josh Briggs gets in Arbor's face and ejects him from ringside. Arbor, satisfied with his work, brushes off his hands and storms off. (End flash back) (I remember an overwhelming feeling of success that coursed through my body when I drove Surgoth face first into the ground. I'm not particularly happy about my actions, I can let my anger get the best of me sometimes. But still, I am no push over. I will do what it takes to prove a point. The point in this case being that I will contend. Whether it be today, tomorrow, a month from now or a year from now, I will contend.) (Here I sit on a pull out couch in a run down motel. This place is so beat to shit that cock roaches would check out before even considering checking in. The plaster on the wall bares holes the size of watermelons with the vision of plywood beams with termite rot supporting the stability and insulation behind it. The carpet lies doused in just about any human bodily fluid imaginable. CSI wouldn't even want to investigate this carpet. Who's to know what has taken place on this slab of shag rag. Fucking raunchy. The bathroom toilet is stained with the smears of shit rotted finger prints on the sides of the outer bowl while the tap inside resembles the sight of a witch doctor brewing a voodoo blend. Human hair with small chunks of regurgitated remains, topped off with a splash of internally released colon pudding is all that is mostly visible in this porcelain death trap. This place is so fucked over that espestice poisoning would be the best means to an end. This isn't exactly the life of luxury I dreamed of.) (The day I inked my name on multiple dotted lines of large quantities of perfectly headed, double spaced, arial font GCW stationary, was the day I felt legally bind to a new life of luxury. Some maybe called this a contract, but I called this a purpose to life. I will work harder than I have ever worked before, and I will fight to be the face of something beautiful. I will be the face of the GCW. This is only the beginning...)
