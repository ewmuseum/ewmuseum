5. A New Start

21 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/21/10 At first, I had no idea what to say to Pat when he came into my locker room. See, my brother isn't the loving brother type that will just come in and pop in to say hello...he also isn't the type of brother that is there only to get you feeling better about yourself. Pat wanted money. Pat wanted advancement in the sports world. Pat wanted notoriety... He can achieve all of this through me, ergo, he is sitting in the locker room with me after a bullshit fight against some rookie to see if wrestling has made me soft or not. In summation... My rap isn't getting any better since I started in GCW. Once thought to be the next rising star, but now my name sinks lower than the new hires of Global Championship Wrestling. I thought I had what it takes to tackle both sports, but once I excel in one, the other crashes and burns. I won this, wont that, but then lost my chance at the Television Title and then it was downhill from there on in GCW. GCW doesn't want me to be apart of WEC and WEC laughs at the fact that I am in GCW. Laughing=Shitty Fucking Matches. While we are on equations, let me spell out the new developments for you... Pattilac (my brother, dietitian)=P Smitty (The most amazing MMA trainer)=S World Extreme Cagefighting=WEC Global Championship Wrestling=GCW This Asshole=K Alright so... In a Perfect world, my performance in WEC should increase my ability in GCW. So we get this equation... K + (WEC X GCW)= 2K With that equation, you throw in the best trainer in MMA and a fucking amazing Nutritionist/dietitian, I will become unstoppable... (P + S) + 2K= 10K (Unfortunately, the result of the equation is not even half of what is going to come out of my paycheck to pay these guys.) So, I am unstoppable with them, but then there is the last part of the equation that I am trying to not factor in. See, she is a fucking TEN on the scale out of ten, but in my equation, she is a 0. I'm not good at math, but I remember what I learned in Elementary school and anything multiplied by a 0 equals 0. That is what I'm afraid of... OK, so we have Pattilac sitting in a chair with a smug smile, but if he were any businessman, he would never have brought HER into the room. If he were smart, he would have just let me take the offer and run with it, without ever running into HER in the arena... ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ "I can't believe you are here! How did you, when did you..." "I work for the arena's PR department. I knew you were going to fight tonight and I couldn't miss it!" Oh, wait! Now I know why he brought her back into my life. She can hype my career, she can hype Smitty and Pattilac as my new coach/trainers... He really only cares about himself... "I've been here before though, and you never came to see me?" "No, well I have..." "Alright, come on you two, you need to break it up so we can get the hell out of here and rest." Pattilac was in the middle of us as I try to keep my eyes on HER'S. "Kris, you need to get in the shower and ice up. We will meet up in the morning. Now, love," his whole attention was on HER, "I will give you a call in the morning. I will let you know when we can set something up and I will forward you the details, but for now, Krissy here needs to get changed and get some rest." "She can stay! We need to catch up, right K...." "Absolutely not! My little brother isn't going to mack it with the chick in PR under my watch! Do you know how much bad publicity that will give us? In your locker room no less!" "Kris, it's alright. I will talk to you later. Let me give you my number so you can..." "ARe you trying to distract my fighter? We need to set some boun..." "Pat, shut the fuck up! I haven't seen her in years." I don't know what pissed him off more. The fact that I told him to STFU or that his plan of bringing her in was much less professional than he anticipated. "I don't care if you can go ten rounds, I will still knock you the fuck out where you stand!" I guess it was the former... "Kris, I'll be around. Pat has my number, when we need to, er talk business I will be free." She leans in for a hug only to get a throat clearing from Pat. I grab onto her and hug her anyway. "Pattilac, always good to see you..." "Likewise..." She leaves the room. "Now don't tell me you are going to fuck her." "Jesus, man. You haven't changed a bit in three years." "Four, and I am just looking out for your well being. I want to see you get back on top of the division and I want you to kick the shit out of that fuckin whackjob in a couple weeks at that No Contest-17 shit." "NC..." "Whatever! Get showered, changed, and I will meet you in the car so I can take you back to my place." Back to his place? Who did he think he is? "No, I'm going back to my apartment." "Noooo, you are going back to my place. Or should I say Dad's place." Dad left me his house when he passed, but I didn't like being in the place by myself. The house was cool and all, it is where I pretty much grew up in high school, but I gave it to Pat before he became a professional bodybuilder to help him save on money. "Fuck it, I need to get my things first..." "Shower!" ~**~*~*~*~*~*~*~**~*~*~*~*~*~* I couldn't stop thinking of HER. Her Ashen Blond hair and her cloudy grey eyes. She had a smile that made the girls on the Maxim's Hot 100 List look lackluster. It didn't matter where I was, when I closed my eyes, she was there... I walked through my father's house and dropped my bags where I always left my equipment when I was younger. The room still smelt like gun cleaner and oil from all the years my father used the room off the garage as his armory. When i looked over my shoulder I noticed Pat added to the collection. The house felt as warm to me as it did when we first moved in. No matter how much time will pass, my feelings will never change. I was never home as a kid, nor did I really know where home was. My father moved from military base to military base, taking me and Pat with him, but this is the house that he settled on for retirement. Because my father was home more, I was out training and fighting more. I walk into the main hallway that leads to the kitchen and I look into the rooms as I walk past. I see most of the furniture that dad left, but the decorations on the walls have changed. No longer are there pictures of our mother or paintings she did, but now there are hot ring girls and fitness models seductively staring at me as their name is scripted across the bottom with a little personal message. "You're so big, Kristy." "I want to oil those abs, call me, AVA" "I want to give you my pro card, Tina. It is amazing how the smallest endearing message can sound so sexual. Now, in no way am I a prude, but I think of myself as a romantic and hate how my brother goes around just to fuck. Not my thing, but more power to him until he gets another STD. "Where the fuck are ya, kid?" I stop in my tracks. I can't believe that Smitty is here. There is nothing more in the world than I want to train with Smitty, but there is also sleeping, and after a fight, sleeping trumps all. I look at the clock in the family room: 1:21am. "Kris, get your ass in the game room so we can talk." If it wasn't for my brother's voice coming through the air like lightning, I would actually hurry to the game room to meet Smitty. Reluctantly, I get over to the room and see the two giants playing a game of pool. "Ah, there's the little champ. Get over here so I can get a look at you..." I step further into the room and cut into the center where my heavy bag used to hang, but I guess my brother thought a stripper pole would be better... Smitty stands at an impressive 6'6 and has barely any fat on him. Smitty is a BJJ fighter by discipline but he can fight in any way he seems to fit the fight, and he can train you in anything you desire. His twilight black hair and amber skin, not to mention his stoic personality, make him a guy you don't want to fuck with. His demeanor can win fights. "Well, you've put on wait since I last saw ya, some good size too...I guess that wrestling bullshit hasn't made you totally useless. You did win a fight tonight after all." "Yes, sir." "Oh, stop with that bullshit. Smitty, Kris. You know my name, now use it. Don't give me that bullshit fake respect. Show me respect, don't fake it." I always feel intimidated by Smitty, but I'm not sure why. I've been in the ring with the best MMA has to offer and I trump everyone, but Smitty is a guy that I don't feel comfortable in my own skin around. If there is one thing I fear, it is punishment for letting him down. See, I met Smitty through my brother years ago. They grew up together in a respect and were the closest of friends. When I was about nineteen, my brother challenged Smitty's fighter to fight me. Smitty laughed and said no contest. I stepped up and challenged the fighter myself after I heard that. The fighter was Chris Lopez and he was a weight class above me. Chris took the challenge and so did Smitty...I choked him out in a minute twenty five... "Can I ask you something, Smitty?" He nods. "Of all the guys out there, why train me?" Smitty raises his eyebrows and my brother steps in, but before he can say anything, Smitty raises his hands to stop Pat. "You are a fighter that I can rely on. You are someone that looks for challenges for the sake of proving yourself. Most of all, you do it to prove to yourself, not for others. I respect that. Pat says you are in a rut, and I say I can get you out of it." I think my jaw ht the floor. Smitty was a good judge of character, he has always been, but I never expected him to actually want to train me. I do wonder who approached who though... "Are you done sucking my nuts now? Go upstairs and sleep, Kris. Smitty has a big day planned for you tomorrow." Pat laughs to himself but Smitty and I just stare at one another until he motions for me to go. I climb up the stairs and know that from now on, I am Smitty's fighter, and I will never lose again...
