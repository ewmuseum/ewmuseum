31 August 2005

Date: August 31, 2005 Days after Annihilation, caught up where he always resides (his weight room), Coyle O’Conner takes a well-needed water break before continuing on. After going through an intensive upper body workout cycle, his body is starting to get strongly dependent on water already at this time. It’s quite early in the morning, in fact, the birds have just started chirping; and this is when Coyle gets ready for his daily jog. Without hesitation, but with a little struggle, he has completed strapping on his running shoes. “Good mornin” Newport!” “As he steps outside, he takes an unconditional moment to view his surroundings. “Such a beautiful place?” Ensuing that, he lowers his right foot off his steps quickly, only to follow it with the left, which begins his race to Stevens Ave. and back; a relative distance to 3.6 miles. Since the announcement of the newly desirable Cruiserweight World Championship title, and the out-of-whack dog pile that consisted of multiple lightweights such as himself, Coyle realizes that he has a lot to work for, and that it’ll be a tough journey. Just passing by the upper corner of his road, and turning right onto Rabbit Rock Rd., the fitness test that he is about to sprint is going to prove whether or not he even has a shot. Without any sign of failure so far, all that streams throughout this 6 foot tall Irish man’s head is that he has a shot to finalize. Each slap of the foot on the pavement sets a ticker to click within his head; for some reason he is keeping track of it. He knows later on in the day he won’t really care, but as he runs from street to street and block to block, he is starting to notice that he is absolutely, and completely bored. In fact, every little thing is starting to distract him, whether it be a car, a bark, an ant walking, an oil spill, a stick on the ground, you name it, he saw it. 3.6 miles is a long way to run without getting bored, as Coyle could tell anyone. Coyle tried thinking of his 1st Round Tournament match to distract him, but his mind just wouldn’t budge. He was starting to get so bored that he was starting to breath heavier; it was deeply impacting his mental state of how tired he was for this jog. Then, there was a car. A black Lincoln LS started to trail behind Coyle, slowly. He hadn’t picked up on this until about 5 minutes later when he noticed the hum of the cars engine, the same way he noticed everything else. He started to weave through yards, but to no fail, the car was still following along side him in some possible way. As minutes went by, then turning into 10 minutes, and so on and so forth, Coyle got fed up. He completely stopped in his course and turned to the car. He stared at the car and no personality made itself show yet. As Coyle started his way angrily to the car, with his right fist to his side, a black tinted window wound down. Anonymous Voice- “Get in the car...” ?”” Anonymous Voice- “Trust me, get in the car, it’s about your brother?” Coyle pauses and thinks. Before agreeing to get in, he looks around to see if anyone is watching, and opens the door. Anonymous Voice- “Hurry up!” He leaps into the car as the door slams shut behind him. The cars? wheels spin and the car runs off. Just a few moments later, the two stopped in a free commuter parking lot. The anonymous man shut his car off, then looked over to Coyle. Coyle started to feel uncomfortable and drew a face to show it. “Listen, I have no idea what’s going on here, but if I could just unlock the door and leave?” Anonymous Voice- “Listen dickhead, it’s only me, Kenny. Remember, your brother Sean’s friend?” “Oh yeah! Wow, I haven’t seen you since I moved to the States. How has everyone been” Do they know I’ve been accepted into a wrestling federation?” Kenny- “That’s kind of one of the reasons why I’m actually here.” “Whata’ya talking about?” Kenny- “Listen, Sean is in trouble. He heard you had a title shot, and thought there was no way you had a shot, he had no trust in you. I know it’s all good things for you, but Sean has always been a gambler, and well, I think you can take it from there?” “Hrmm, can’t be that much, aye” Just a few hundred dollars maybe? I can pay fo?” Kenny- “Try 40,000 on whether or not you’ll win your first match. He bet against you. Francine left him, she took the kids, he’s been drinking heavily now. You know, even Mrs. O’Connor has been worrying about him.” “How is my mother?” Kenny- “She’s still keeping” on.” “Good, good, well listen, if I win this match, I’ll be getting a slab of money from sponsors and what not, tell Sean he doesn’t have to worry. Tell him, I know what’s in stake with this match, and that I have no intention on letting this one slip through my hands. Let him know, this is something I want more than anything!” Kenny- “Alright, alright.” “So, umm, you came all the way to the States to say that?” Kenny- “Aye is right Coyle.” “And are you kidding me?” Kenny- “Not a wee bit.” “You are an idiot” Kenny- “Oh, and your mother said take some depression classes, it could really help!” Coyle suddenly motioned out of the car, telling Kenny to have a safe trip home. As Kenny drove away, Coyle turned around and thought. Now, his own brother is in trouble. This wasn’t good, this was the same reason Coyle left Ireland, and he started to worry. This whole dilemma means he has to win this match just so he pay for his brothers hole; Coyle “the O” O’Conner understands about being in the hole. All of this means he has to defeat X. Coyle must eliminate X from the tournament to pay a whole debt, and it became a recognizable challenge. Here is a fighter, equipped with decent skills, a huge morale within the fed, fighting against him. It’s not going to be easy, but Coyle has a prominent attitude of optimism. Showing himself his happiness, he turned around, and started jogging back on his way to his home.
