Lies

8 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/8/2009 Deep Thoughts… Deeper Lies… It’s what I have become accustomed to. * * * I am a man. An ordinary man. I have my own ideas, my own willpower, my own sacrifices. I spend each and every moment thinking about things that once were and what could be. I wasn’t about to let myself fall behind. I was…no I am, I am going to become something great, something that has never before laid eyes upon. I was sitting alone in the darkness. My head lay in shame, I had no where to go. Not that I needed a place to go, I was safe here, my solitude, the only thing that could really save me now. My worst nightmares have come true… “Fuck…” protrudes from my mouth in epic proportions. I had not given much thought into how this would have affected me. In my deepest darkest depression I had created myself an altering skew on things. No…no my father died, of course that happened, I wouldn’t lie as something as bad as that, no, no as I sit here brooding I come up with the worst possible scenario to make myself feel better. He didn’t kill my father, he didn’t tie me up. He didn’t concoct some type of scheme to inherit millions of dollars by rewriting my father’s will by forging his signature. No, nothing happened of the sort. It was a lie built upon deeper lies. It was all a part of my nightmare. He still died, I went to the funeral, it was nothing special, I came, reconnected with some old friends, had a moment with my family and I came back. Now while I sit here, it finally has a chance to sink it. I have a chance to really reflect on my life from then on and forward. There is only one thing that came to my mind that could really change my outlook on things. It was… Her… * * * I lay on my bed. Thinking…wondering. Changes. What do they all amount to? Once you are dead, you are nothing. You soul is ascended into the heavens reaching out for a new calling, but back here on earth, your rotted corpse is just a shell, just a meal for the worms in the dirt. “Damn…” I reach across the bed and pull out an old photo of my family from my dresser. It was taken around 10 years ago when we actually liked eachother and had no bad blood. It was a simpler time, I wish I could go back and relive those moments. That was a lie… I was ready to move forward with my life, start anew day, a new journey. MY own quest in life wasn’t going to be stopped by pettiness. It wasn’t going to be shattered by people who don’t even know the real me. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK The door hammers on. I didn’t feel like talking to anyone right now. I was in my own world; my depression was getting deeper and deeper by the minute. The overwhelming feeling makes me want to stay here in this one spot forever. I could care less if I am brooding, screw them all. I had to do what I needed to do. It was all I could ever inquire of myself. My confidence was at an all time high, but my dedication was at an all time low, why do it anymore? I don’t know. “I am coming in whether you like it or not, Lucas.” It was apparent to me that I was not going to be left alone, not now, not ever. It was also apparent that I didn’t lock the door, because I can see she opens the door with a swift tug. He pops her head in and looks at me, she gives me a big grin, but it soon changes towards sorrow. “I’m sorry…well, no I am not really, you have been cooped up here for days sleeping and crying like a little bloody school-child. You are Lucas Quinton, for god’s sake, you can get by this, but not sniveling here like a noisy little boy.” I love her… I hate her… Her long black hair flowed in unison with the breeze that came through the door. She fiddled with her hair as she always did, she had this way about her, and she would know exactly what to say and how to say it to get anyone out of any situation. Truly a master manipulator, she knew what I was thinking and played off that like it was nothing at all. Her ever ecstatic grin was what got me through the day. She slowly inches towards me and joins on the bed. She crawls and crawls until we are met face to face. She laughs. I show no emotion. Her hand starts at my shin and slowly moves up my leg…to my kneecap, up my thigh, to my inner thigh. I try to suppress these thoughts, but can’t resist for much longer. She slowly moves in her head, her lips almost touching mine. She blocks my attempt to kiss her with her index finger. She laughs again. “Not today, cowboy...you need to get up out of this bed and do something, but right now it isn’t going to be me.” She laughs again. A cruel temptress, she knows the game, and how to play it. She uses her hands to push her off my chest and pulls me up with both of her hands. “I am in no mood for your games, Lucy. Not today, I have enough on my bloody plate.” I stand straight up and head towards my closet. I needed to put on something decent. A walk or a ride someplace could set me straight. Maybe. “Tsk…tsk…tsk. You are a somebody, you continue digging your nose in that pillow like a little boy who lost his pet dog. You need to man up and take action. You need to let out some aggression, because I am not going to stay here any longer with you being a toddler.” Bitch… * * * Entrusted with a goal, I set out today with Lucy in tow to alleviate some of this madness that has been ripping apart at my innards. The breeze relaxed me, the gust of wind was like a morning cup of coffee, it energized me and eases some of the pain that was buried deep within. I sucked in the air like a vacuum and sat that for a moment. Thinking… Pondering… Wondering… Was this my life now? If I wanted to I could keep walking, I could go the distance and look back, I could become a drifter…a loner, although I was already well accustomed to that. I could walk for days on end, not giving a turn of my head backward, I was going to go on forward. Wishing… Pondering… Waiting… Waiting for that moment that I have dreamt for so long to come true, but all I have been given were nightmares. These nightmares… I can’t control them, yet they feel so real, I can touch and taste the air, feel the breeze between my skin, the kiss of the wind on my lips. It was all there. Every single little detail was there, every follicle of hair, to every blink of an eye. It was all a lie. A Dirty Lie… I look back at her. She smiles at me, I give her a quick smile back. Everything I wanted was right here, was right here holding my handing, the interlocking of two people, one on one, a connection. I could feel her, when she was happy or sad, or when she got scared and got goosebumps. These are the only moments where I feel I am truly happy. But I must know. Is this real or another lie? Only time will tell.
