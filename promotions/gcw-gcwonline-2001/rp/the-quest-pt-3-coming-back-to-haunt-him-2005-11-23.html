The Quest Pt. 3: Coming Back to Haunt Him

23 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/2005 [Scene fades up as Sam Wolack is getting off of a Gulfstream G550 with the 'Damage Inc' logo on the tail. Standing at the bottom of the stairs is a rather attractive looking blonde in business attire and glasses.] Blonde: Well, Sam, what did you think? Wolack: For a heartless bitch, you do take care of things. [The blonde is taken back by this comment.] Blonde: Just remember, this heartless bitch is going to be paying you rather well for this add campaign. Wolack: Kelly, for that I thank you. I also thank you for allowing me to choose where we shoot. Kelly: Where to next? Wolack: United Arab Emirates. Dubai. Kelly: Very well. When are you planning on leaving? Wolack: Monday, with arrival back in the US by Wednesday. Kelly: I should just have you fly commercial. Wolack: Now you know you aren't going to do that. You have two corporate jets and use only one of them. This way, you get your money's worth. [Wolack and Kelly start to walk towards an awaiting car.] Kelly: So, I heard that your brother just got back from Japan. Wolack: What would you care? Kelly: Even though we got divorced.... Wolack: Twice. Kelly: I only wish him the best. Wolack: Getting him suspended from NWC Atlantic, taking his company and motorcycles away from him. Leaving him damn near broke the second time. Kelly: That was the lawyers. I had nothing to do with that. Wolack: He was willing to end the marriage so you could run away with your 24 year old pool boy. He nearly went bankrupt after that. Your taking him to the cleaners did help. Kelly: He didn't tell you then. Wolack: Well, seeing as he got back from Japan after I went to Thailand, nope, never had a chance to talk to him. Kelly: We started talking again. Started to patch things up between the two of us. Wolack: That's good. Kelly: I gave him part of the company that he helped to build. Wolack: That's good. Kelly: He's also thinking about making a comeback to the ring. Wolack: Even though.... Kelly: His doctors cleared him. The surgery on his elbow was a success and he's looking to get back in the ring. I thought that maybe you could talk to CEI to get him a tryout. Wolack: No. Kelly: No? Wolack: Can't do it. Kelly: Why not? He got you your shot in MSW, remember. Wolack: Because I'm already stretching their leniancy by doing these shoots. Maybe after the ad campaign. Kelly: I know why you're doing this. Wolack: Why? Kelly: You want to prove that you can attain respect on your own, outside of an organization that knows you only as Jay Draven's brother. Wolack: Point being? Kelly: You've had more opportunities since you were a rookie than he did at the same time. You've travelled the world. Your brother was 29 before he ever wrestled in Japan. You were wrestling in Europe at 20. He was 27 before he worked there. Wolack: Yeah. It worked those places because of Jay. The Japanese promoters that booked me did it because they thought that I would be as good as he was. I only went to England because I was his tag team partner. [Scene switches to a hotel room in Philly. Wolack is unpacking, his wrestling gear laid out on a bed. There is a knock at the door.] Wolack: Gimme a minute. [Wolack closes the bag and heads to the door. He opens it. There is a man in a long black trenchcoat standing there. He pulls a gun and shoots a tranquilizer dart into Wolack's chest. We fade to see Wolack waking up in a darkened room. Sitting across from the table is the man that shot him.] Wolack: You son of a..... [Wolack goes to lunge at him, but finds that he's tied to the chair, which is riveted to the floor.] Man's Voice: You're playing a dangerous game, Mr. Wolack. And if you want to see your pretty little girlfriend alive, you're going to play by my rules. Wolack: Who the f*ck are you? [The man walks out from the shadows.] Man: You would like to know that, wouldn't you? Just so you can tell the FBI who I am. Read the paper lately. [The man tosses a newspaper on Wolack's lap. Headline reads 'FBI Informant Found Dead'] Man: He tried to play the game as well. He got you involved, and now he's dead. You were going to go to Dubai to look for your pretty little girlfriend. Cancel the trip. Tell them whatever you want, but you aren't going. You attempt to go, she dies. Wolack: Fine, Dubai is out. Man: Good. [The man that had initially shot Wolack pulls the tranquilizer gun out from his jacket.] Man: This is for our protection. [Wolack is shot again. We fade to find him waking up on his floor, by the door of his hotel room. He opens his shirt to find that there are no puncture wounds. Wolack heads to the hotel phone.] Wolack: Hi, this is room 414, did anyone at the desk see anyone wearing a long jacket entering or exiting the hotel in the last....[Wolack looks at his watch.] two hours. No. Thanks. [Wolack hangs up the phone and grabs his cell phone. He dials a number.] Wolack: Kelly, yeah, cancel the trip to Dubai. You think that it's possible to have your driver bring me some fruit. Don't ask. [Scene fades to black.]
