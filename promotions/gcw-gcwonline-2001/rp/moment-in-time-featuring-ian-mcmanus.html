Date: today Tabloid and Ian McManus aren’t exactly what you’d call good friends or buddies. Sure, McManus is hired by Tabloid to provide some muscle, but the only real tie between them is a paycheck. Thus it’s not so surprising, really, that the self-proclaimed First Amendment of GCW is alone on his day off, sitting in an Irish pub in the middle of Chicago, watching football matches play out on the big screen with a pint of Guinness in his hand. “Oy! Come on! Three minutes left!” He was still getting used to the whole America thing. He’d grown up his whole life on the Emerald Isle, in and out of gangs and low level soccer hooliganery. Now, he was a professional wrestler, because really, there weren’t a lot of professions out there for hooligans who liked to fight and do so legally. A pause, and he pondered his situation. “Mebbe I should have signed up for that UFC shite.” Then again, Tabloid paid pretty well. Better, he imagined, than a starting mixed martial artist. Money let Ian drink. Drinking made Ian happy. “World’s so complicated for everyone else. Me” I just drink and see what the fuck happens! Easier that way!” Truly, a philosophy to live by. Tabloid did all the mental heavy lifting for the new duo of Entertainment Tonight, while Ian handled most of the physicality. His first match was imminent, and the big man smiled to himself as he took another big swig of his brew. Why, soon he would be on GCW television in front of millions of fans. He smiled, and began to deadpan deliver a promo, imagining a camera in front of him. “Pleasure and Pain, tonight, you’ve got the task of takin” on the toughest sonuvabitch that the Emerald Isle’s ever produced! I’m gonna rip ya in “alf, I will, and there ain’t nothing ye can do “bout i?” “PIPE DOWN, DAMNIT! THE GAME’S ON!” With that rebuke, Ian falls silent, opting for the silent security of his Guinness. Perhaps Tabloid’s got to handle the talking now, too?
