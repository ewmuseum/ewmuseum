Brad Cruz, SoCal Man of Mystery

20 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/11 2ND Annual Sacramento Pro Wrestling Convention 2011 Come meet your favorite superstars live and in person! See them, talk to them, take their picture, and have it signed! All this and so much more at Wrestle Con at the Sacramento Convention Center! Superstars in attendance: Former POW North American Champion and current GCW performer. "The SoCal Prophet" Brad Cruz Former POW Superstar "The American Mad Man" Haze "Brothers in Strife" Former FPW US Tag Team Champion Gabriel Strife Former ECWNG South Eastern Champion Eric Strife Former POW Tag Team "The Dog Town Saints" Slider and Eko The last ECWNG Southeastern Champion and Former APW World Champion "The Mad Cat" The Panther …and many more! Wrestle-Con 2011 We open in large convention hall in Sacramento, California where 2nd annual Wrestle Con is being held. The convention center is packed as pro wrestling fanboys and fangirls young and old clamber from booth to booth trying to catch a glimpse of their favorite superstars. The commotion is almost too much, but through the crowds and booths is a lone star that shines brighter than all the rest… a star from the west… who’s light shined so bright it was beyond comparison! A star that pulls the masses like a glowing beacon of biblical proportions… his name…" Fanboy: "Brad Cruz! Oh my god It’s really you!" The young man is wearing a Cruzifix T-Shirt can barely contain himself as he jumps up and down with excitement. Cruz sits there at his booth legs propped up looking down at a latest issue of variety, sipping a cup of coffee all the while oblivious to the young man’s fandom. Fanboy: "I have every match you ever wrestled… since MOW:RTW when you were still the Hurricane!" Brad: "Why are these American superheroes being played by Brits?" Fanboy:"I remember when you fought Hawaiian Bryan in a table match!" Brad: "I mean are they really that hard pressed to find a proper American actor?" Fanboy: "I remember when in P.O.W. you pinned Sebastian Roach for the North American Championship!" Brad: "First Batman, then the new Spider-man, and now Superman!" Fanboy: "Oh, oh and I remember in P.O.W. when you when you dove off the Titan-tron at the Civil Brawl PPV!" Brad: "I say as a country we retaliate by taking Dr. Who, Sherlock Holmes, and James Bond show those Brits we mean business with the Boston Tea Party 2 Electric Boogaloo!" Fanboy: "I’m your biggest fan!" Bradley continues to read his magazine as his one fan stands there and gushes a man in black pressed suit with a matching tie approaches from behind the fanboy. The well dressed stranger stands around 6’1 with black parted hair and a large faded X shaped scar on his forehead that wraps around his face like a mask. Stranger: "Mr. Cruz, I was hoping if I could take a moment of your time for a brief word." Cruz doesn’t looking continuing to read. Brad: "Uh-huh, please wait in line sir… or miss… you’ll get your turn… thank you for your patience." Fanboy: "He spoke to us! Wait till my wife hears about this!" Excited the Fanboy runs off leaving Brad and Agent Mercer alone. Solomon approaches the booth table leaning closer and speaks in a tense whisper to an oblivious Cruz. Sol: "Listen, I’m working for a small government agency known as Seraphim, and you’re the only one that can help us." Bradley turns a page and doesn’t look up. Brad: "Listen guy, I’m not into the whole secret agent LARPing scene, so step aside so the next fan can get a turn." Solomon looks behind him to the supposed line and sees no one. Confused he turns back to Brad. Sol: "I’m the only one here." Brad looks up at Solomon then turns his attention to the nonexistent line. Brad: "What happened to all the people I was supposed to neglect?" Sol: "I don’t know, but about my agency." Brad jumps to his feet. Brad: "Listen I already have representation I’m happy with. In fact I’m so happy; I literally plan to marry it." Sol: "I’m not a talent agent I’-" Brad: "I know, I know, you wanna be less of an agent and more my pal, but my current agent is my fiancé, so that trumps pal, pal." Bradley walks around the table and is about to walk away, but Mercer grabs a hold of him. Sol: "You need to come with me." Brad looks at Mercer in disbelief. Brad notices the young man’s scars on his forehead. Brad: "Hands off hatchet-face!" Brad pulls away and Mercer once again grabs him. Sol: "I’m sorry but you don’t have a choice in the matter." Brad tries to pull away a second time, but Sol quickly pulls him back in and comes around with a hammerlock. Brad instinctively reaches up grabbing Sol’s head dropping his own weight dragging Sol over with a snapmare. Sol rolls on the carpeted ground quickly to his feet. Mercer reaches into his coat as if going for a gun but Brad has already grabbed a unfolded chair and slings it at Solomon. Mercer ducks down as the chair hits a display behind him. Sol gets back to his vertical base pulling out a black pistol with a blue glow and points it at Cruz, but the moment Sol gets Cruz in his crosshair Brad’s foot kicks the weapon from his hand followed by a front kick to Sol’s gut causing him to double over. Cruz grabs Mercer by the back of his belt and coat hurling him over his booth table. Sol hits table taking it with him as he hits the carpet. Bradley sprints running away from his attacker as Sol eventually pulls himself to his feet recollects his weapon and gives chase. Brad runs with little to no regard for the well being of anyone as he pushes his way through crowds of fanboys while knocking over booths and displays. Sol cleverly jumps over tables and cutting between booths has makes up some major ground. Both Brad and Sol break away from the main exhibit area coming out to an open stretch that leads to the outer ring hallways. Brad really digs deep pumping his legs trying to keep his distance but Sol is close behind. Slider: "Hey Cuz!" A distance away near the main door to the convention hall stands a familiar face, Slider. Slider waves to Brad who b-lines straight to his cousin. Sol trails behind slowly creeping up on Brad, who finally reaches Slider and with no wasted movement Brad grabs Slider pulling him back into him tucking his cousin’s head down and throwing him into Sol driving Slider’s shoulder into Mercer’s midsection. Slider and Sol go crashing to the floor and Brad bolts out the main door. Brad exits to the main hall of the building and realizing he has to get down stairs, so he rushes towards the elevator. He makes it to elevator door and pushes the button anxiously waiting for it to open. While he waits Brad sees something in his peripheral vision. Startled he turns t confront his maybe attacker only to see a smiling man wearing a Cruzifix t-shirt and a young woman holding on to the Cruz fan’s arm. Brad turns his attention back to the elevator. Fanboy: "Bradley, It’s me again your biggest fan! " Brad: "Uh-huh…" Fanboy: "This my wife L-" Brad presses the button more adamantly. Brad: "Uh-huh…" Fanboy: "Well I talked it over with the wife and we decided…" The fan and his wife smile as she squeezes her husband’s arm. Fanboy: "We want to fill my wife with your seed!" Brad stops everything he’s doing and looks at this odd couple. Brad: "You have my attention…" Fangirl: "We want a little Cruzsader of our very own." Brad tilts his head in confusion and disgust unable to express a single response. Suddenly Sol stumbles through the main door. Brad panics and looks the couple. Brad: "Stop him and I’ll consider it!" The couple rush into battle as Brad runs through a door that leads to the stairwell with two interlocking stair cases. Brad runs down the stairs and moments later Sol makes his way into the stairwell. Bradley with a dominate lead decides to pull the old switch-a-roo jumping of the railing onto the second staircase and begins to run up the stairs. Brad climbs and climbs until reaching a door. Bradley opens in and stumbles outside onto a large open roof top. Bradley walks out to the middle of the rooftop. Bradley gasps a sigh of relief catching his breath. He cracks a smile feeling that he’s in the clear. Sol: "Don’t move!" Brad: "Damn, that stairwell bit worked in Back to the Future 2" Brad turns his attention to the rooftop doorway where Agent Mercer now stand weapon drawn. Sol: "This was a simple recruitment mission and you just had to go an drag this out." Brad: "You pulled a gun on me!" Sol: "How about you? You threw chairs, your fans, hell you threw family!" Brad: "Yeah, I’m not too proud about some of that. It was a really nice chair, it deserved better." Sol: "Shut up, give it up or I’ll shoot!" Bradley shakes his head and looks the scarred faced man in the eye. Brad: "Why don’t you come and make me yourself? No guns." Sol hesitates he could just end this now with no fuss. Brad: "Come on, aren’t you a hardboiled agent, or are you just a scarred face ruined pretty boy?" Sol’s brow furls as its becoming clear that Brad is working his last nerve. Brad: "How did you get those scars anyways? Did daddy teach you to shave with the weed-wacker, or did mommy put those notches on your face because you had a twin and she wanted to able to tell you apart?" Having heard enough Sol holsters his weapon and charges toward Brad with a big right hook which Brad manages to sidestep. Brad: "You know, If you want to clear of that complexion of yours I would start with some spackle and a puddy-knife" Sol spins around with his left and Cruz avoids that, but is caught with as small jab followed by a flurry of rights and lefts to the head and body that Cruz does his best to block. Sol goes for a round house kick to the head but Brad manages to duck just in time, the kick cuts the air over his head and Cruz shoots and tries for the double leg takedown. Cruz manages to break down Mercer driving to the ground, but Sol defends himself with his legs shoving Cruz off of him. Cruz is knocked pretty far back and quickly jumps up to a vertical base. Sol already to his feet charges Brad with a flying knee to the jaw. Cruz manages block the knee with his arm but the force of the blow knocks him down flat on his back. Cruz quickly rolls to his knee and places his hand up as if begging for mercy. Brad: "I’m sorry! I didn’t mean for this to get so out of hand. I give! I give!" Sol just shakes his head as he approaches the beaten Brad Cruz. He draws closer to Cruz pulling out pair of handcuffs. Sol leans in to pull Brad to his feet, but Brad lurches forward grabbing Sol’s belt buckle pulling him in close and delivering a crippling uppercut into the lower extremities of Sol Mercer who gasps and crumbles to his knees in pain. Brad jumps to his feet with a cocky grin. Sol: "What the f-" Brad: "I know what you’re thinking, This guy just gave up and he used that to take advantage of the situation. I know that’s not fair and it’s against the unwritten rules of honorable combat, but there’s a super secret rule that you may have over looked, and it’s as follows…" Bradley kicks Sol clean in the sternum doubling Sol over. Brad: "NEVER!" Brad comes to the side of Sol and kicks him in the midsection with a soccer style kick rolling across the floor. Brad: "TRUST!" Brad begins to stomp away. Brad: "A MOTHER!" STOMP! Brad: "FUCKING!" STOMP! Brad: "HEEL!!" Bradley satisfied with what he’s done Brad slowly begins walking toward the rooftop door leaving Sol lying. Cruz makes it to the door reaching for the handle when a burst of energy hits Brad in the back. Bradley falls to all fours. Brad tries to pull himself up but it’s difficult. With great effort he manages to pull himself up and turn back to Sol who lays there pointing his weapon out toward Cruz. Brad tries get to his feet, but Sol fires again sending a second shot straight to Cruz’s chest. The impact knocks Cruz back into the door. He hits with hard thud. Cruz is unconscious as he slumps down slowly until coming to rest. He lays there motionless as Solomon gets to his feet and stands over him. Sol pulls out a cell phone and begins to call someone a moment passes and someone picks up. Sol: "We got him."
