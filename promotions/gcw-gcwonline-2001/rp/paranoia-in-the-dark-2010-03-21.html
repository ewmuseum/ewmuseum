Paranoia In The Dark

21 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/21/10 It is a dark and stormy night; a cliche if there ever was one. Rain pours hard, and every so often a lightning strike followed by the "BOOM" of thunder shakes Rick Mad's house. "God damnit," Rick said. "What an awful night." Rick is sitting on his black leather couch, with a Wii controller in his hands. His long red hair is tied back in a ponytail, and he's wearing black jeans and a plain brown shirt. Beside him on the couch is Logan, wearing a black shirt and bluejeans, and also holding a controller. "Look on the bright side," Logan said, "at least we have off tonight and we're not in some middle-of-nowhere town getting our asses beat." "Well, I think we'd be the ones beating ass, but- ...wow that sounds wrong." Rick is moving left and right, very interested in whatever game they're playing. "Alright, almost there, okay.." Thunder strikes and Rick jumps, the controller flying out of his hand. From the TV we can hear the familiar sound of Mario getting killed by some foe. "DAMN IT!" Rick stands up and kicks a nearby coffee table. "WE ALMOST HAD IT! I'm so damn tired of this level, raarrgghhhh!" Rick is shaking his head in frustration... and Logan is just laughing at him. "You DO know you're overreacting to losing in a video game, right?" "Yeah, Logan, I know. It's just... I'm kind of bored. We haven't been working a lot lately and I'm a pro wrestler. I'm used to being angry a lot. And I just kind of realized I'm an old man playing with his Wii with another guy on a Saturday night, and that is kind of sad." Again, Logan chuckles. "Yeah.. well.. when you put it THAT way.. you're right. Its kind of depressing. Let's not think about it. I'm gonna get a hot dog, you want one?" Rick shakes his head no. Logan shrugs and heads to the kitchen. Rick sits back down on his couch, slouching back... until thunder once more rocks the house. Rick jumps a little. "Heh heh.. you'd think I'm some little girl, jumping because of a little thunder-" Suddenly, there are two loud knocks on the door. Rick's eyes go wide. "I'm a grown ass man! Why did THAT scare me!? But really, who is here at this hour?" Rick gets up and peers out the window, looking to see who's at his door. A man is standing there in a black raincoat with a black hat, looking generally menacing. "...Logan?..." Rick yells to the kitchen. No one answers. "LOGAN? You all right?" Again, no answer. The man knocks again. Rick looks around nervously, not sure what to do. "Ugh. This is stupid. Rick, just answer the damn door," he says to himself. He heads to the door and opens it. "YEAH?" he says to the man, somewhat aggressively. "You Rick Mad?" "Who wants to know?" "Me. Because I've got something for him." Rick detects a hint of menace in the voice, panics, and slams the door in the man's face. "Okay, Rick, get a hold of yourself. That was stupid. If he wants to kill me, slamming a door in his face isn't going to help. Hell, why would anyone want to kill me anyway? God I'm stupid." Rick opens the door back up. The man is still standing there, looking very annoyed now. "Look mister," he says, "I don't know what you think this is, but I'm just trying to deliver a goddamn business document. Time sensitive, very important. I'm sorry its raining and that you must be on acid or something, but please, if you're Rick Mad, and I know that you are because I'm a wrestling fan but my boss says I have to ask anyway.. if you're Rick Mad.. please, just sign for this package." Rick's stares blankly at him for a second, realizing just how stupid he's been acting. "Uh.." Rick stammers, "Yeah. That's me. I'll sign." The man hands Rick a clipboard he'd been holding under his coat, and Rick signs. The man then hands Rick the package, a paper-sized brown box. "Thanks. And good luck," the man says, as he turns around and heads back to his car. Rick closes the door. "Wow.. what could this be?" Rick says to himself as he grabs a pair of scissors from his end table and opens the package up. He pulls out its contents, several pages of paper stapled together.. "LOGAN! LOGAN OUR GCW CONTRACTS HAVE FINALLY ARRIVED! WE'RE IN!" Rick shouts with excitement. Until he remembers that Logan hasn't returned from the kitchen yet, and didn't answer him before. "..Logan? Are you okay?.." Rick's sense of paranoia has returned. He sets the papers down on the table and slowly, reluctantly, enters the kitchen. Rick gasps and he sees Logan on the ground, holding his head in agony. Rick drops to his fallen friend and helps him up. "LOGAN! CAN YOU HEAR ME!?" Rick slaps Logan's face a few times, and eventually Logan comes to and wakes up. "LOGAN! WHAT HAPPENED! Is someone in the house? Did you get attacked?" Logan's eyes open up and he slowly remembers what happened.. "SOMEONE spilled some damn mustard on your floor and I tripped on it and hit my head! Fuckin' mustard..." Rick can't help but laugh. Until lightning strikes once more and his electric goes out, leaving everything black. "God damnit. I hope this isn't a sign of things to come," Rick says in the darkness. "Huh?" responds Logan. "Our contracts came, Logan. GCW. We're in." "HELL YEAH!" shouts Logan, and we can hear him jump up in excitement. A half second later we hear the sound of him hitting something, yelling in pain, and then falling back to the floor with a loud CLUNK. "Logan?..." Rick says into the darkness. ".....Logan?...." No answer. "I'm scared."
