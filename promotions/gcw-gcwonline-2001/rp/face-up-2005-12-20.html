Face Up

20 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20 He was kinda young. Mid twenties. A hopeful scholar, successful businessman, good father, and proud friend. His little boy at home gets great grades in school and his wife of six years is a veterinarian. Dinner every night at five thirty, except when he's off sealing a deal, and then he's not home till late. But everybody understands because he's got the big deals to make. The big money to make with them. And he was just out celebrating, right? Just out having a good time, just getting off the merger between his company and some small time shin-dig that they don't ever have to worry about again. Gin and tonic. Scotch. Martinis galore. And a little trickle of blood split three ways as it ran down his chin. The Tigris, the Indus, and the Euphrates, all blood red, going down his chin. And one eye was shut and wouldn't open for at least two days. And one tooth was gone. Did I have to? He would have. 'He might press charges, John.' That's Kate saying, 'I don't want anything to happen to us, Jack.' She's not concerned about Jim Everyman. She doesn't want anything to happen from our far-from-perfect family unit that we've got going on here, that's what she's concerned about. She couldn't know. 'I got witnesses that say he started it.' 'Fuck him. I'm a God damn celebrity.' Isn't that what you really mean, Johnny-boy? 'But there's the chance that he might press charges. And then what happens? You get in trouble with Caldera and you set a bad example for Sarah and she's got to be known as the girl in school that has a daddy that beats up negotiators in bars.' 'Your job and your daughter matter, not the guy you destroyed.' Not the guy who's gotta crawl home to his family defeated, humiliated, and has to explain to his kid what happened to him. 'Look, Kate, if he presses charges I'll just hire you.' 'You can't afford me. You're still in debt, remember?' grrrrrrr... 'Kate, you're not being reasonable. This guy...he came in off the street to my bar and picked a fight with me. I'll say it again, he picked a fight with me. Average Jim Everyman came in and saw Johnny Borealis, that guy he knows somewhat from the TV, and he came up to me. And a few words were thrown around, and the booze made him think he was tougher than he is.' 'And you annihilated him.' 'I gave him what he needed. A good sobering up of the attitude and the bloodstream, by beating both of them out of him. And if it wasn't me, then what would've happened? He and his little friends would just get drunker and drunker until they thought they could take on the world. Then he wouldn't have tried to be more of a man by taking on a professional athlete. They would've taken on somebody who wouldn't think twice about murdering his ass. Then he'd be dead.' Dead instead of humiliated. Take your pick. You want the actual, truthful story? Johnathan Fitzwillis is in a bar, getting something to wet his whistle. Not to get drunk or to forget who he is, but a couple of nice Miller High Life's to chase the Sabres game. By the way, suck it, Philly. Anyway, in comes Guy in a gray pinstripe suit of indifference to celebrate the big deal that went down. The big success at the office. He and three of his big office friends with their hair cut the way they like it and their shoes buffed to a mirror shine. I think that that was actually the thing that caught my eye first; their shoes. Shined so high and bright that I could almost see a glimpse of the future in them. So mirroring the world, but distorting it with their curves, I knew that the order that they tried to show would get broken down in the chaos of violence as the four adults strode to the bar and ordered up a bunch of high class to kick the night off. Best friend fronts the first couple. Cheap Friend gets the next one, but only the next one. Guy Down the Hall that is only Friend by Association doesn't buy any and acts nervous but happy to be included. And Guy who made the deal shines like a fuckin diamond, and to tell the truth, I feel pretty good for him. After all, this business deal that was struck, it's the epitome of success. Real defining of the human race. A true contribution to the world. He went in there and laid down the law, and the Devil himself crumbled and ran and minds were freed and the people rejoiced. Even the Gungans and Naboo, because George Lucas feels the need to ruin the Original Trilogy as well as the new one. How much later? How many mixed drinks in? I know that they saw Johnny Borealis, that guy from the TV, not too long into their celebration. Sitting off, watching the hockey highlights, wishing something that they'd never know. A small smile flicked across Johnny's face, remembering a joke from earlier in the week that one of his coworkers had told him. But they'd never know what that joke was. Because Guy that made the deal felt like showing the world what wrestlers truly are made of. 'Make your excuses, John. But what if you were ever the one to make the wrong choice? What if he had a knife? Or one of his friends had a gun? What if you ran into someone that didn't think twice about murdering you?' 'I don't want to be lonely, Jack.' She doesn't want to be alone. She doesn't want our daughter to be alone. 'I wouldn't.' 'I didn't.' 'You could.' 'I know you didn't.' 'I'm way too careful, Kate.' 'I'm indestructable, woman. Didn't you know?' And she gets that look in her eye. The look that tells me that she doesn't want to speak about it anymore. Because her mind's made up and my mind's made up and we're not gonna do anything making progress because we both know everything that we're talking about. So, we might as well stop talking about it, start smiling, have sex, and wait till the sun rises and washes all the wrongs away. Isn't that what couples do? AndI remember it clear. Well dressed businessman has a few too many drinks. Sees a celebrity. God knows why he wants to hit a celebrity. Maybe he doesn't like me cuz I'm a heel. Maybe I'm not heelish enough. Maybe he remembers me from back when I was a babyface and tried to please the crowd and he didn't ever like that and he hasn't forgiven me yet. Maybe he misses that. But his problem was that he missed the first punch. I'm not gonna pretend that it felt good to beat the shit out of him. And I do mean that I beat the shit out of him. I mean, between being a businessman, not a body builder or athlete, and being drunk off his ass, it was almost as easy as picking on Jordan White. No effort. I mean, it was so easy that it couldn't even be considered a contest of any kind. One to the gut that makes him throw up the last four hours of drinking his life away, one to the face, two to the face, three to the face, Hell make that four to the face. His friends get a lot less happy and the bar gets a lot less noisy. And the guy had three rivers of blood coming out of his mouth. And when he got all cleaned up by the police, the guy had to go home to his wife with the short, sexy blonde hair. She just got that hair cut for him, to surprise him, to inject their sex life with a little bit of pizzaz because she felt that he deserves the attention after working so hard on the business deal. And when he came in, she knew he'd be drunk. But she figured that she'd light a warm fire and send Junior to bed early and the two of them could try a few new toys from their secret closet that the world doesn't have a clue exists. And she wants to because she loves him. 'Mr. Caldera did say hello and asked how I was doing. I was amazed at how he even knew who I was. I've only met the man once.' Subject change. She's shaking as she's cutting some carrots and trying to lead a normal life's night. 'Yeah. He's a good guy. Real caring businessman.' I can almost see her heart stop a beat on that word. Businessman. I say it and she thinks about the same thing I do. Guy that made the deal stumbles in the door, and his wife's been up for hours with his little boy because they got a phone call saying he was hurt. I mean hurt, too. Missing teeth, eye swollen shut, stitches on his face, fractured wrist, whiplash, all the minor bumps and bruises of a good ass kicking that this guy doesn't know about. It's not his fault that he doesn't live our life. That he doesn't know how to grit his teeth and pop a dislocated shoulder back in. It's not his job. I admit that I wouldn't be a good sight to look at after trying to make the big deal. He wasn't much to look at after taking on Johnny Borealis for the title of the Midweight Champeen of Teh Bar!!! And he's got to explain to his kid what humility tastes like. His little boy who will never be a Borealis fan, even if I came over and kissed his ass like nobody's business. Because I wrecked Daddy on the big day. Mommy even spent fifty dollars on this new haircut and she sent him to bed early so that she could show him she loved him and instead the kid's got to look at three little rivers of blood, that kind of look like the Tigris, the Indus, and the Euphrates, and he's got to know that his dad is not immortal. His dad isn't the strongest man in the world after all. Hell, his dad got his ass beaten by somebody who isn't necessarily the strongest man in the world after all. Kid's gonna look at my picture and he's gonna see refinement, not size. He's gonna look at my weight and he's gonna say that I'm just over being a cruiserweight. I'm nowhere near the strongest man in the world. And he's gonna know what I already know. His dad's a pussy. 'I...I think I'm going to go check on Sarah.' On my words, Kate turns around already nodding. She doesn't care what I do, as long as I don't embarass her again. As long as I don't do what? Act like I'm in my twenties? Act like I'm a free man? Oh, I remember...act like I'm not a father. A responsible, calmed down individual. Who contributes to society. Who makes the big deals. 'You wish you married that businessman instead of staying with me, don't you, Kate? That's why you're upset. You're pissed off at my because I beat the living shit out of the guy that you wish you married. You had six years, and you didn't use them. You could have gone with anybody. But you didn't think anybody was good enough for you, because you had had me. You'd had your one guy. The one that you needed. And you hate me for not being in a pinstripe suit of gray indifference and making the big deals. Instead, I beat the Hell out of the guy that should have had your kid. The guy that should be bringing home the money instead of leaving you every single week.' I wish I could say that. 'Go...check on Sarah. She misses you.' 'I can't even look at you, Jack. I know you're right.' And without another word, I'm going upstairs. I'm leaving town on a bad note. Again, mind you. Out of here in three hours, and the last day has been spent with police and with lawyers. Guy that made the deal came at me first. Challenged somebody that he couldn't take, and I took him down. Showed him that he's not untouchable. And I sealed one of his eyes shut for two days. And I broke his wrist. And I made him throw up all over his pinstripe suit. And I made him bleed three rivers of blood out of the corner of his mouth, down his chin. That's what I know about him. His son? His wife with the sexy haircut? The big business deal? I don't know. I don't know which are real and which aren't. In the end, I guess that they don't matter, do they? Because no matter what he had to go home to, I'm aware of what I had to go home to. And opening the door to Sarah's room, I'd trade any day. I'd trade. I would rather tell my daughter that some jerk who fights for a living, and I don't, just beat the crap out of me...rather than trying not to tell her that I was that jerk. And that I messed somebody's life up. And that not I'm going to try to tuck her in bed like nothing happened. Story of my life.
