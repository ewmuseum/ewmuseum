Spiderman: Into The Blackness: Villain/Hero

15 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 Dec 08 ** I'm going into the Future here ... it'll all be explained in future episodes! ** 11/21/2023 9:49 P.M. Central Park, NY "Looks like big, bad Venom has gotten slow in his old age." A long black tentacle comes flying at Spiderman so fast that even he cannot react quick enough. The large black mass drives into his chest, forcing him back into a tree with a loud thud. The tree groans because of the pressure, threatening to topple over. The Symbiote monster, Venom, uses his new found powers to keep Spiderman pinned between him and the tree as he walks forward, getting closer to his adversary. "The famous, Spiderman," His voice is deep, scratchy? frightening. "Hardly what Venom remembers of you." The hulking form moves closer, his head getting extremely close so that he can take in deep breaths. His long tongue slides out of his mouth and makes a trail up Spiderman’s head. He shakes his head. "You’re a fake." He transforms the black tentacle back into his hand and he grips Spiderman’s throat. "You are not the one that Venom has followed all of these years. You are weak!" He turns and tosses Spiderman through the air with great ease but maximum force. Spiderman crashes to the ground hard, the back of his head bouncing off of the frozen ground. Venom leaps into the air, pouncing on the fallen hero and using his clawed hands and feet to hold Spiderman in place. "Peter Parker. Where is he?" Spiderman’s hands ball up into fists, anger fills him up quickly. He takes in a deep breath. "Dead." "WHAT!?" The symbiote howls. "He can’t be dead!" "Well he is. One of your creations caused it!" Venom growls. "NO!" He pulls Spiderman up closer to his face. "Parker was mine!" "You can’t kill him anymore Venom." "Kill him?" Venom pushes Spidey’s body back down to the cold ground. "I wanted to bond with him. I wanted us to be one. He was powerful, we could have done whatever we wanted!" Venom adjusts to stand up and he pulls Spiderman up with him. His massive hand reaching up and clawing away the mask, revealing Spiderman’s face. Venom laughs, sounding more like a cackle than anything else. "You’re so young, just like Peter was when Venom met him." His mouth opens wide, showing off that devilish smile filled with rows of razor sharp teeth similar to a shark. "You want to be a hero? You want to save people? You want to stop ME? Is that it?" "It crossed my mind, yeah. Things as ugly as you shouldn’t be able to just wander around the streets of New York." "Well hero, let’s see if you can fly." With all of his strength, Venom spins as if competing in the “Hammer Throw” at the Olympics before tossing Spiderman through the night sky. Fear rushes through Spiderman’s body, not knowing where he could land. He goes back first into the bare branches of a tree. The cold makes the edges even more sharp and his skin is gashed open all over his body upon impact. The tree stops the hero from going any further but now gravity kicks in and whatever goes up, must come tumbling down. Spiderman begins his descent, hitting most every branch on the way down and flipping over one completely before smashing to the ground, his entire body aching. "Damn. Any frequent flyer miles for that?" Venom is right back on Spiderman, driving his foot into his side. The force of the blow lifts Spidey off the ground and flips him over. The symbiote covered menace rushes in again, lifts Spidey up from the ground and brings him over his head only to slam him down onto a huge boulder. Spiderman screams in pain. Venom growls in delight. "There’s one lesson that Peter must never have taught you." Venom digs the claws on his index fingers into the shoulders of the red and blue wearing hero causing his body to tighten and the blood to flow. "Heroes never win. You’ll die alone, you’ll wish you were never born." That Evil Smile ? the Devil’s smile forms on his face again. Those gruesome rows of teeth shining in the moon’s light. "But not yet." "I’ll kill you!" Spiderman yells out. "For Peter!" "Honorable but foolish." Venom yanks his right claw from Spidey’s shoulder and brings the hand up to his face. He admires the blood. "You’ll be a hero, Spidey. But to be a Hero you will need the one thing that every great hero needs." "And what’s that?" "A Villain." Venom’s hand begins to move but not in the typical sense. It’s as if something is crawling beneath that black skin until a piece of the symbiote separates from the back of his hand but moves back in quickly. He smile grows once again and he turns his hand over, revealing his palm. In the palm of his hand the symbiote moves again and eventually it breaks free. Venom looks at his offspring fondly. "Just as Peter had Venom ? you’ll have me." He turns his hand over again and lowers it down onto the open wound on Spidey’s shoulder, letting the small piece of symbiote inside. The pain inflicted upon the World’s newest Spiderman is immense. The symbiote crawling through his body makes him rigid, his hands shake. His skin begins to change, his teeth sharpen. Then he goes back to normal ? only for the transformation to start up again. The veins in his head and neck bulge, appearing ready to explode. Then he blacks out. Venom laughs as he places his hand back over the wound ? the small piece of symbiote crawls back out and bonds with him again. He takes in a deep breath and looks down at Spiderman’s lifeless body. "I’ll see you soon ? Jordan." And he takes off into the night, leaving Spiderman alone. 11/22/2023 01:35 A.M. Peter Parker’s Lab Jordan stumbles into the lab, clutching at a table for support. He pulls himself up and rolls onto it. His vision is blurry and his muscles ache. He can taste his own blood and it makes him want to vomit. He can still smell the stale breath of Venom, like a mix of human flesh and bile. "He probably does eat shit" he thinks, even if his current physical state the thought makes him flash a smile. "What the fuck was I thinking?" He asks himself out loud. He thought he was ready to face the Symbiote. Who else was there to do it? Peter was gone and news broke that another Symbiote had been sighted in Washington D.C. Jordan was tired of sitting around and doing nothing. Peter tried preparing him the best he could but Peter wasn’t there anymore to give him guidance. And he didn’t have radioactive induced super powers. Jordan didn’t understand how powerful this thing is but now he knows. Now he knows that he cannot fight it the way Peter did. "How the hell did he fight this stuff with a suit made of spandex and cloth!?" Another thought that brought a painful smile to Jordan’s face. A very real and sobering thought hits Jordan’s mind like a freight train. Venom is still out there! His sore muscles beg him not to move but Jordan knows he cannot just sit and wait. He is the Hero the World needs and he cannot allow it to succumb to a monster. He sits up on the table and looks around then down at the suit that he is wearing. Slowly he pulls the mask up to eye level and stares at it. "It’s time to lay you to rest Peter, once and for all. The world needs Spiderman ? but not this one." There is work to be done. Jordan and Peter had been friends since the night that Peter was paralyzed by Payne. Over the years Peter had taken Jordan under his wing, taught him everything he knew. Together they began work on a new project. Something that would help save the world, give it the hope that it had lacked for many years. Now is the time to finish that project. Jordan moves his body off of the table and stands up on his own power. "Time to get to work." Then he collapses, the pain and exhaustion becoming too much. Saving the world could wait until morning. 11/22/2023 12:49 A.M. ?”””””””””””” He sits in the shadows, the evening’s events flipping through his mind like a photo album. He looks as his hands, the blood stained claws look like art to him. His breathing is heavy but very slow, showing that he is very calm and at ease. "Amazing. Brock was right, there’s nothing like this feeling of power." A voice inside of him calls out. No one else can hear it, not that there is anyone around to hear it anyways. Parker ? "Forget about him!" He groans outwardly. "He’s dead, there is nothing we can do about that." He was the ultimate host, the one that could make ME stronger and not just the other way around. He belonged to me. He opens his mouth, baring his teeth as a way of intimidation ? even if it’s only himself. "He’s dead! He is not coming back! But you can still have a purpose. With me. Together we can do all that you wanted with Parker. I know what you’re feeling, Parker rejected you, time and time again. Then the one being that wanted you was weak and could not sustain your life. Eddie Brock was nothing! But I can give it all to you. Together, Venom, we can be all powerful." But who will oppose us? There is nothing left. "Spiderman still lives." That is not Parker ? he is just a man. "He’s a man who is fighting to be a Hero. If we let him build himself up before tearing him to shreds ? the world will fear us. We will slowly destroy that pathetic creature and then we can feed off of fear." Jordan White? "It’s time to think BIG. Forget Parker, forget New York City. What I am talking about is bigger than that. You and I? We can take the WORLD." And step one? He laughs that low, frightening laugh before forcing himself to stand up. The symbiote begins to morph, slowly revealing the man underneath the monster. His icy blue eyes penetrate the thick darkness? "It all starts with Jordan." Jordan ? White. "Then we’ll take the World." TO BE CONTINUED “.
