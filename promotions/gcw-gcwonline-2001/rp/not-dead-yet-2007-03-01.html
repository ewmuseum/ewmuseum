Not Dead Yet

1 March 2007

Date: 3-1-07 "What the hell do you think you're doing?" Jayce never bothered to look over his shoulder, he knew who was standing there; he recognized his voice. "I have a match." "You're in no condition to be in that ring, Jayce," Mario Trellis had his arms folded neatly across his chest, further accenting the perfectly chiseled form of his upper body. "Last week you ignored me and went out there to defend the Cruiserweight title, and you los-" Jayce winced as he turned, but he never cried out from the extreme pain that poked at his spine. "I was cheated out of that match, Mario. You know that, so don't try to lecture me." "You're hurt, Jayce," he began, "You've been hurt since SHaman and Mayhem attacked you. I know you have a hell of a lot of pride in you, kid, but you're going to get killed out there." "I never asked for a father, Mario. I paid you to help me control my urges, to help me refine my skill, and teach me a few new things to add to my repertoire. No where in that did I ask for someone to baby me and cuddle me when I look hurt." "It's just the way I am, you cocky son of a bitch." Mario had unfolded his arms, and now stepped toward Jayce with his eyebrows furrowed and a sneer on his lips. "You didn't ask for it, but I'll be damned if you're not going to get it. I don't care if you're pushing fourty, if you're not in any condition to get in that ring, you better believe I'm going to tell you." Jayce shook his head, then turned around and continued to pack his clothes into a duffel bag. "You think they're going to forget you again, don't you?" Jayce closed his eyes. He had tried not to think like that, but the thought was always in the back of his mind. He had not been doing that great lately, and it seemed as if he could not buy a win when he needed it the most. It was silly, or at least he felt others would think it was silly, but it seemed to be his greatest fear. "They're not going to get mad if you tell them what the doctors told you, kid." "I can't let them know they won, Mario," Jayce said with his eyes closed, still fighting back the thoughts of failure that had clouded his mind for the last few weeks. "I need to be there." "You were supposed to be there yesterday signing autographs and doing that radio show, what makes you think they're not already pissed at you?" "I need to be in that ring, Mario, why can't you just understand that." "Because I've been there, Jayce. I know what kind of toll those matches can take on you, and with your cracked rib, you can hardly breathe.. much less wrestle." "I don't care, Mario. I don't care if I didn't do the PR crap, I don't care if I'm going to be running through the doors when the match starts... I'm going to be there, and dammit, Shaman and Mayhem are going to know that I am there." "You're going to end up out of the business forever at the rate you're going, worse off than me if you don't watch out for yourself." "Does it really matter, Mario?" Trellis had no response, he just threw his arms up in defeat and walked out of the room. He knew there was no getting through to him, he was much to set in his ways to listen to common sense. It just wasn't in his programming.
