Importance Of Wrestling: Family

5 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 Feb 08 I don’t understand mommy It’s what I try to say as I sit on her lap and watch the TV. I don’t normally get to watch TV with mommy because I sleep a lot but sometimes I do. Mommy calls the show “WorldWide” I think, something where people hit each other a lot. It’s really fun to see! Mommy and me, we only watch for one person though, the only who makes everyone fall. “Daddy!” It’s the only word I can say at all. Mommy looks down at me with a big grin. “Yes it is your daddy, wave to him.” I wave but for some reason he doesn’t wave back. I don’t understand mommy On the TV Daddy looks so different, like someone I’ve never even seen. He beats people up, why is he so mean!” Maybe it isn’t really Daddy? Maybe it’s a clone! He could’ve snuck up on daddy! Maybe when he was alone? Then the people on the TV talk about Daddy, they don’t have nice things to say. They talk about bad things; they wish that he would just go away! I don’t understand mommy! Why don’t they like him? Did he do something wrong? Is Daddy just a faker and he has been mean all along? I don’t think so, he has always loved me! We have had many good times together, if only I could make them see. They talk about Daddy doing awful things like being a great big liar! They even say he almost caught his friend on fire! That doesn’t sound like him at all Mommy now does it? Do you remember when I was first born and how bright his eyes were lit? I could barely see anything and yet I could see his blue eyes. So how could a man that caring tell such horrible lies? He always wakes up with me when I have a bad dream, no matter if he is weary and tired. No way did he almost catch Jason Dread on fire! It makes me angry mommy! Why do they have to say all of this? Can’t they see Daddy like I do? Can’t I make that wish? And why do they call him by that name mommy? LeStatt Knight? That is not right! I feel tears in my eyes! I don’t understand mommy! They call him Knight, LK, LeStatt, “Hall of Famer” and a big, big baddie! But he isn’t those things! He is only ? “Daddy!” ____________________________________________________________ I watch as he sleeps, comfortable in his crib. Watching his tiny back move up and down as he breathes during his sleep is soothing, like the one calm thing in my life ? my anti-drug if you will. Before Joaquin Roberto Abitua was born I wasn’t sure exactly where my life was headed. I had Mary but she isn’t exactly the most stable person around ya know? I love her with all of my heart but I wasn’t sure if she would be the person capable of pulling me back from the edge if things got too bad. But Joaquin is. There is something amazing about the way a child can change your life. It’s a feeling I’ve had once before, feels like a lifetime ago really. As charmed a life as I have led, the day my daughter was taken from me my life was ripped to shreds. Money, fame, glitz and glamour can only take a person so far. It’s the same with inflicting physical harm on others. When I hold him in my arms I still get choked up at times. I never believed I would be blessed with such a feeling again, didn’t think I deserved it neither. Now that I have it though, other worries have crept in. I am no longer just making a name for myself; I have someone that will look up to me someday. Will my son look at me and feel I am a person that he can be proud of? Will all of my victories, my records and my legacy really mean anything to him once he is old enough to really understand? It’s hard to say really. I know what they mean to me, I cherish my moments inside of the ring. I learned to because it was the ONLY thing I had for years. I can honestly say I’ve grown to appreciate it even more over the years though. In the beginning it was just because I could hurt others and I was a natural ? but now it’s become something of a quest. My goal is simple really, to be the brightest star in the sky. I don’t care if you’re a wrestling fan or not, I want the name LeStatt Knight to mean something to you. Deep down, I always wanted that approval. Deep down I know that I’m selfish. And that is what kills me! I cannot afford to be selfish anymore. Mary and Joaquin depend on me, they need me. Wrestling keeps me away from them for long periods of time and I cannot ask Mary to drag our son around from place to place with us, no matter how much I want to. They deserve a stable life, a home. Sadly I’ve never been sure if I could offer that to a family. So in circles I continue to go. My love for my job, that adrenaline rush, the legendary status and worldwide fame that goes along with it on one side. The satisfaction of being a family man on the other. Family Legend Family Legend “. FAMILIA. They are more important to my survival, Mary and our baby are my life. Wrestling will end someday when my body is broken and battered, maybe when my drive has dwindled. A family’s love is something that can never be taken away. So the choice is easy. But right now? There is unfinished business. Right now my drive is as high as it has ever been. My body is in the best shape of its existence! My heart? I have never been so sure of something in my entire fucking life. One day I will be Roberto Abitua, family man. But until then? I am LeStatt Knight. Future GCW World Heavyweight Champion. I lean down and kiss Joaquin’s head softly. “I’ll make you proud.” A smile moves across his sleeping face as his body adjusts. Which forces a smile to my face.
