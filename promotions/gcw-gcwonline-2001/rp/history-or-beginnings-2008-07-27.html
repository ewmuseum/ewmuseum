History or Beginnings?

27 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-08 A dark cubby hole of concrete looms before you. Occasionally a hand reaches out and seemingly flicks something with the twitch of the wrist. A voice is heard talking yet slightly disturbing. The voice is familiar yet just a bit different in a tone of cynical bliss. The voice seems to talk at nothing yet at everything all at once. Right at this moment the voice is angry at pigeons. It is yelling at the pigeons and laughing the whole time. "Come here you shit with wings!" Suddenly a loud snapping sound is heard as a dead pigeon flies out of the darkened hole. Deep guttural laughter is heard as a deep release of air is heard. The pigeons no longer come near the hole as several of them lay dead down below. A hand reaches out and grabs the edge of the concrete and Shaman sticks his head out looking at the world around. "I like my privacy and my little concrete hole in the wall." Shaman waves his hand around and points to bridge abutment around him. he then points down to the river below. Then smiles as it is only about 40 feet below him. Shrugging he kicks his feet out and sits down dangling his feet over the edge looking down at the river below. "Guess you can say somethings are like the river below me. Everything moves through its course and changes. Sometimes you meet little punks who want to prove more than what they are worth. Win or lose i enjoy a good fight regardless of how cocky some punk really is. I don't kiss someones ass and never will. I am not some damn corporate lackey. That had passed when i quit the last place. Yeah yeah in know i returned. Point is i did it to prove a point. What point is that if i lose you wonder? Simple i made it known that i can and will no longer be controlled." Turning his gaze it settles upon the shadows that are being cast from the bridge above. " Everyday something is changing and something is coming yet again. Darkness, change, and isolation, None of these mean anything. I am here one way or another i do what i want to do and when i want to do it. Ask Steven Caldera if i ever listened to what he wanted. Ask that punk kid Azrael in the WWA if his words mean anything to me. Truth is i got what i wanted there Azrael. I got a match with you and it stopped you from doing anything more important hasn't it. Oh i know what my plan is in that wretched place. They do not hold reigns upon me any longer and thusly i got what i wanted and when i wanted it. So go believe you have already won your battle Azrael. I have won the war by strategically doing what i wanted. Placing the pieces where i want them placed and even the WWA Corporate haven't even realized it yet. Yet just like the WWA many in GCW forget and think they know what i am going to do." Shaman stops and looks over his shoulder a moment and touches a place where red spray paint is seen drying, but still drooling down the wall. "Which brings me to my next issue. The more important one in my eyes. The man who abandoned me and changed his plan. You turned your back on a brother in Oblivion Mayhem. You turned your back on everything you are and were. Look what you have become Mayhem. You have become nothing again. I am going to give a little damn history lesson for those who do not know nor understand what i am saying." Shaman turns his palm up and grins as he points a finger down into it poking. "Two years ago Bryan someone came to GCW and slapped you off your high horse of terror and shall i term the phrase loosely Mayhem. You see two years ago Bryan you came face to face with me. The one man who refused to back down from you. The man who would bring the fight to you in all the ways you have never thought possible. We had a third member of our little game his name was Ciel remember him? I am sure you forget him as most do once someone is gone. Months you and i faced off and continuously beat each other. For what at that time was your little Tv Title. NC 17 2006. You, me, and Kyo Kushinaga remember him? I beat you and him for the Tv Title. Took away your precious little gold trinket and we continued our brawl for a little while longer. Why many people wonder Why did Shaman come to GCW and face off against the man monster Bryan Mayhem? The answer is simple really. All you have to do is listen." Shaman stands up and grins as he leaps off the bridge pylon from his perch down into the water. A large splash erupts as he cuts into the water like a knife. Shaman breaks the surface a few moments later after the water stills a bit. he then reaches up to a boat parked to the side that was out of view. Reaching up he pulls himself up into the boat. He grins and turns as he sits himself down. He reaches to the side and grabs a towel and dries himself off a bit. "Shaman and Mayhem the most dangerous duo in GCW. We wrought havoc to every person who got in our way. Nobody was safe not even Caldera himself. Caldera the man who was frustrated at not being able to control the two of us. Then Mayhem gets a shot at Lia and over months i helped Mayhem achieve the one thing he could not do. I helped him gain the confidence in himself. The same confidence that allowed him to become world champion. Not too long after wards there was a buried alive match against Ambrosi and Valentine. Bryan bailed on me after wards. He ran in fear of failure and could not comeback and show his strengths. Now he comes back with Lia Ambrosi. Hiding behind her skirt and he wonders why i am so dangerous right now. Truth is simple Mayhem i have always been this damn dangerous. Where have you been hiding?" Shaman turns and looks around the boat and walks to the back and looks out over the lake as the sun sets in the western sky casting peaceful evening shadow across the water. "Bryan do you remember when we were robbed by Banned and Exiled? When we defeated everyone in the ring for the Tag Team Championship. Markus Stone come out of nowhere and dropped and hooked the opponents leg. Effectively screwing us at that moment of our Tag team right? Did that break your spirit? If i remember correctly you had gained the World Championship just after that. Damn it Mayhem i showed you the way out of Dies Irae and how they were using you as a tool. Now you go running back to Lia as if she is your Momma. Truth is your momma is dead." Shaman rubs his hands together as his eyes dart from side to side as he gets a sadistic smile upon his face. He turns and looks around once again and then claps his hands together. "Hot damn why did i not think of that." Shaman slips down into the cabin of the large boat and sounds of rummaging around can be heard. He looks out as he climbs back up onto the back deck of the boat. "You know Bryan which names are you going by in the past. Bryan Lunatic or how about Bryan O'Neill your real name wasn't it? In fact your first match or one of them was against the Dark Messiah wasn't it. The guy was a joke and didn't stand up to who you were." Shaman grins and pulls a magazine out of all of his matches. "You see Bryan i have faced many wrestlers over my career. Including guys like The Magician, Steihl, Black Scorpion, Angel Reaper, Chris Rogers, Shadow aka Lia Ambrosi, to name just a few. Yet here we are two forces of nature about to collide again." Shaman lets a breath out through his nose slowly as he sits down on a large seat in his boat. "You see Bryan you and i are like the proverbial immovable object and the irresistible force. We keep getting drawn back to one another. Violence, blood, pain, and downright torture. They all go hand and hand with us doesn't it. What are we going to do this time to top ourselves? Do we beat each other so senseless that we will comeback and want more or do we do the right thing and go after everyone like we are meant to do. Simple thing is Mayhem we gotta take each other out first. We are in each others way and that just won't do will it." Shaman grins and picks up a short fishing pole and swings it around like a sword. He steps forward imitating a fencer as he speaks. "I am like Captain Hook to your Peter Pan. Your like James T Kirk to my Kahn. How about my yellow M&M to your red one." Shaman stops simply to look around as he eyes the shadowed tree line. "Your hiding behind lies Bryan. Your going to have to come out of the shadows and understand who it is you face. let me give you a short history lesson." Shaman wipes his face and tosses the towel to the side. "1997 i joined the WWA, First opponent was a man named Steihl. It was a loss and then i went up against a man named Chris Rogers who insulted me and my heritage. I crushed him as i did many others through out that time. Then two years later i won my first title. My first of seven successful captures of the Extreme Title there. Then i got the Television title twice there once beating Troy Walcott and then beating Captain Amazing. Then during that time as well i beat Jackson Kraven and Craven Moore for the US Title. Finally i felt the exilerating feeling of taking advantage of a situation to gain the World Heavyweight Title by someone elses misfortune. That was my first WWA World Heavyweight title. It was not very gratifying. You know why? Because later on i beat Grunge and several other people for the World Heavyweight title and crushed them. To only be cheated by corporate bull. I was cheated and knew it. That was my decline and leading to my departure there. Where i only just recently made a return." Cracking his neck from side to side Shaman looks at his huge arms. "Bryan the point is never ever underestimate me. I have a goal and the goal is simple. I am going to do what i want, when i want, and how i want. You cheated me Mayhem and i want some revenge for it. People think Azrael has my number truth be told he has no talent and couldn't fight his way out of a wet paper bag. He claims he gained it faster, but there are nobody worthy of the competition levels back in the days when i earned it. You your a different beast Mayhem. The only man i trust in all of the business and you did me wrong by deserting me. Do you think i will let that go unpunished? You better believe i won't Bryan." Shaman points upwards at some scaffolding on the bridge where they are doing some work. "Scaffold match. One of my favorites Bryan. Do you not think i am not going to enjoy every minute of that match? I am going to enjoy hitting you and dropping you off the highest points down onto your empty mime loving head. Bring your matches to me. I cannot wait till the death match. Because your going to wish you were dead when i am through with you Bryan." Shaman leans closer to the camera his eyes all that are seen. " Look into these eyes Mayhem. There is no fear here, Hatred is all that there is waiting for you. You betrayed me and i will make you pay for each and every minute where i felt i lost a brother." Shaman flexes all of his muscles in his upper body and arms as he groans. "Never stop making them pay." Camera pans over the lake away from Shaman and the boat as the suns sets beyond the horizon as the sky slowly begins to turn and the stars begin to appear.
