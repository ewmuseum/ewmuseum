The Cell: Burial

27 November 2007

Date: 27 November 2007 He stares out of his window enjoying the view that accompanies the positioning of his office. For years he has stood at this very spot and looked out of this very window. Unlike the other times however, when the sky was black and life was nowhere to be found, the scenery is much more appealing. The sun beams down, lighting up his world like he had never experienced before. Birds fly this way and that, singing their songs of happiness and freedom. The sound of children’s laughter echo through the air. A calm breeze winds its way through the trees causing them to sway back and forth as if they were at a concert. The view, the sun, the laughter ? it all brings a smile to Roberto’s face. It wasn’t always like this however and that very notion is what makes Roberto appreciate it even more. For years he was played and manipulated by someone who did nothing but feed off of people’s less desirable traits. Anger, despair, sadness, illness ? all things that this monster could sense and use to his advantage. Above all other gateways however, FEAR was his most usable trait. Roberto’s fear of losing all that he cares about, his fear that people would never respect him for all that he has accomplished in his career, fear that he’d lose control for good. All of this cast a black cloud over his life causing him to only see the horrific and the violent. It wasn’t until Roberto fought back that this sinister cloud dissipated. From the day of his Revolution Roberto has done everything imaginable to keep this man from escaping. This monster. This entity. LeStatt Knight. With each passing moment he has dug Knight’s grave a little bit deeper and a little bit wider. He forgave his most hated rival, Alex Grey, becoming close friends and confidants. Love found its way back into Roberto’s heart through Typhoid Mary. A child’s laughter and warmth and safety came too. Roberto Abitua also learned how to enjoy and love his job, not just for the brutality of it but for the joy of it ? for the purity of it. Each positive thing in his life was more or less another lift of a shovel, just inching closer to the day that he could bury his demons once and for all. There is a lot to bury, a lot that went on in the very room that he stands in. “I’ve waited too long for this day.” He whispers The slew of twisted bodies, the firing of the shotgun, his time locked away as LeStatt’s slave. These memories will never dig themselves out of Roberto’s memory. Roberto won’t let them. These hardships have formed the man that stands in front of that window today. Strong, caring, confident. As much as he hates LeStatt he knows that it was his pushing and his viciousness that kept him on his toes. If it wasn’t for Knight? Roberto may never have achieved the greatness that he has. But the risk is far greater than the reward. LeStatt Knight is empowered only by suffering, death and destruction. “Mr. Abitua,” A voice calls from behind. “It’s time.” Roberto doesn’t turn around as he is too caught up in the moment. Today marks his first true defeat of LeStatt Knight. And that is a beautiful moment. “Mr. Abitua?” The voice calls out again. Now he snaps out of it. “I’m coming.” Roberto now takes his eyes from the window and looks around at the office for what he feels should be the very last time. He smiles again. “Time to bury this shit once and for all.” __________________________________________ “Mother fucker.” The words escape easily although hardly a surprise when thinking about that do-gooder prick, Roberto. Where would his sorry ass be without me!” He’d be wearing that ridiculous mask and calling himself Khris Krowe, that’s where. He’d have to suck Caldera’s dick to ever even get a sniff of what the world title was like. He’d be just another one of those idiots you see come and go in your life. Never enough to make an impact but just enough where you could laugh about how bad they were. Hell without me he would probably be dead by now. If it wasn’t Payne it would have been RaZiel and Demonio not long ago. How does he repay me? By locking me away in this god forsaken cell and hoping that I either die or go in-fucking-sane. Well if he really believes that I will be taken down that easily he is in for a rude awakening. I have been engulfed in darkness for over a year now, no light at the end of this tunnel. All I’ve had to keep me company is the slow drip of a leaky pipe somewhere up above and the stench of rotted corpses, puke and pile that lingers in the air thick enough that I could probably tear it with a swipe of my hand. A normal man would probably have already gone insane or worse. But I am no normal man. Instead I sit here patiently, waiting for my time to rise again. As strong as Roberto has been the last year I know he cannot hold up. He is too weak to keep this mask of perfection up for long. He will grow angry and afraid, vulnerable and weak ? and that is when I will pounce. That is when I will get my revenge on everyone who has ever done me wrong. That is when I will take back the life that was stolen from me. The life that I deserve. “I will ?” What’s that? A door opening? Foot steps? Yes ? yes it is. The sound of hard soled shoes clapping off of the hard floor continues to get louder and louder, causing my heart beat to grow faster and faster. I can hardly hide my excitement, perhaps this is the light at the end of my tunnel. Then the light turns on and flashes into my eyes. It feels like my eyes are on fire. “ARRRGHH!! You son of a bitch!” I howl as I attempt to cover my eyes. Laughter! Those assholes are laughing at me! ME! LeStatt fucking Knight! “Move over to the bars you sack of shit, back up against the metal, your arms behind your back.” I don’t give them much of a struggle even as they grab me by my hair and slam the back of my head into the steel. I feel the warmth of my blood dripping down my neck and onto my back. The anger begins to rise inside of me as they tightly wrap the handcuffs on my wrists. “I’m going in.” One guard says to the other. He moves into my cell, his light shining near my chin and threatening to burn into my eyes if I make a wrong move. His fist crashes onto my cheek, forcing my head to the side. “It’s time to meet your maker you sick fuck.” He whispers to me followed by a chuckle. He shackles my feet before ordering the other guard to undo my wrists to allow me to stand. A massive hand wraps around my throat and brings me up to my feet and then drives me back into the steel bars causing pain to wash over me. “Let’s go.” He leads me outside of the cell and then cuffs my wrists once again. “Time for a walk pal.” The second guard tells me. “The last one you’ll ever take.” The first says with a laugh. I say nothing in return because finally I see the light. It’s time. _________________________________________ In the distance Roberto could see his two guards surface from his maximum security wing. The wing built especially for this one prisoner. It almost makes Roberto giddy to see LeStatt in such a weak state, after all of the years of him being superior. The guards have to almost drag him across the field because his body is so lifeless. Roberto narrows his eyes, focusing on the face of Knight, almost calling out to him. LeStatt must know it too because now he forces his neck up and those black eyes of his lock horns with Roberto’s icy blue stare. In that moment anger fills Roberto as every last memory slams into his brain with the force of a freight train. Roberto brings his hand up immediately to rub his temple, trying to massage out the pain of the sudden headache. His breathing quickens and his muscles tighten. Maybe worst of all he can just feel LeStatt smiling at him, knowing that even in his weakened state he can always cause such a problem within such a calm man. It seems to take a lifetime for Knight to reach Roberto’s spot but when he does the guards lift his weak body high so that the two can meet eye to eye. You can liken Knight to a weakened predator, never willing to accept defeat, defiant to the bitter end. Even through the sharp pains from the headache Roberto can smile at this. “LeStatt Knight,” Roberto says with a sigh. “How long has it been now?” “Too long.” “Perhaps,” Roberto chooses his words carefully. “I can promise you this though. After today it will be even longer before you me or the light of day again. I hope you take this all in because for you this is the end of the road.” “You don’t frighten me Roberto. Never have.” “I don’t need to Knight.” He says confidently. “Look at you! You are done, finished, destroyed. You always enjoyed talking about how you were ushering in some brand new era in the world but guess what” Today I end it with no chance of revival.” Roberto takes a few steps backwards and to the side, revealing what is directly behind him. “Your burial ground LeStatt.” He says with a smirk. “The place where you can finally be put to rest. I don’t need you anymore; the world does not need you anymore.” The hole in the ground seems to make a path straight into hell, that’s how dark it is. Perhaps if you look close enough you can see the fires of hell burning bright, hungry for their latest meal. Knight’s blank, emotionless gaze leaves Roberto’s and takes a glimpse of his eternal resting place but quickly returns back to Roberto. “You will never be rid of me Roberto. I will always be here, waiting for you to realize who you really are ? preparing to take my throne back again. You can try burying me, hiding me like some dirty secret but we both know the truth. You are nothing without me, I am what made you a legend, and I built your legacy.” “And?” “Legends do not die.” Roberto moves in close and grabs Knight’s chin and yanks it upwards so that the two are directly in front of each other. “You listen to me! This war between us is over! I’ve won, you’ve lost and there is no getting around it. I have watched you destroy people’s lives, wreck their careers and alienate everyone I’ve ever held dear. I will not allow you to take away what I have fought so hard to gain once again. This ends now.” “What you have?” Knight says with a weak chuckle. “Mary” The only person in this world who is more fucked up in the head than you? Your friendship with Alex Grey? A man who tried to kill you on numerous occasions. How about that child of yours? The one that will never fully replace that lovely daughter of yours that YOU killed!” Maybe you’re talking about that illustrious wrestling career? The one that would be nothing without my influence!” Give me a fucking break!” “LeStatt ?” “SHUT UP!” Knight booms surprisingly. “Do what you want to Roberto but my time will come again and when it does I hope the rest of the world is watching. The world ain’t seen anything yet.” Roberto has heard enough and drives a hard right hand across Knight’s face and quickly pulls out a silver pistol from his coat pocket drives the end of the barrel into the forehead of LeStatt. “Any last words?” Knight pauses but never falters. “See you in hell.” “Indeed.” The pistol slams against the head of Knight, knocking him unconscious. Roberto watches as his body slumps to the ground at his feet. “Sir ? are you going to?” “NO!” He answers before the question is asked and looks up at his two guards. “He deserves a slow death.” Roberto turns and begins walking away, leaving the guards standing over Knight’s body confused. “Bury him.” Roberto spits out as he walks away. I let out a gasp of breath and open my eyes suddenly. Or do I? Blackness again surrounds me but this time it’s different. This time not even the sound of a leaky pipe is there to keep me company or the stench (which is a welcomed revelation). I lift my head. CRACK! My head slams back down to the ground underneath me, something slamming into my head and forcing me down. I squirm, struggle and move but all around me I’m boxed in, unable to budge much more than a few inches on either side. I know what’s happened, I understand. Roberto thinks he has won, that he can bury me and forget that I’ve ever existed. He thinks that he can have his perfect life and not worry about ever losing control again. But a man cannot fight his true nature. You can decorate it, hide it and try to lock it up as best as you can but nothing works. Everyone has a dark side, an evil side and I believe we all know one thing to be true about pure evil. It doesn’t die. It just rests for awhile. But it continues to grow stronger. _______________________________________ I shoot straight up in bed, sweat pouring down my face and chest, breathing erratic. No office No cell No grave I look around at the lavish hotel room, compliments of Steven Caldera and breathe a sigh of relief. Mary’s soft touch comes next as her hand runs up my arm, showing me that she’s awake. “You Ok?” She asks is her quiet, tired voice. “Ye-Yeah Mami,” I smile. “Just a dream.” Fact is I haven’t had a dream like that in a long fucking time. I thought I was done with it all, moving on with my own life by my own rules. Mary sits up, pulling the sheet up with her to keep her bare flesh covered up. “Statty-Bear” You can talk to Mary.” “I know love,” I shake my head. “It’s nothing, just go back to sleep.” She frowns for a split second, knowing I’m lying but not wanting to push the envelope any further. That frown is quickly turned into a mischievous smile. I lift an eyebrow up in response. “What is it Mary?” “Well since we is BOTH awake “.” Slowly she runs her hand across my arm and onto my stomach where it stays for about a fraction of a second before sliding down lower until she reaches what she wants. I lean down and kiss her passionately which she takes as an invitation to adjust and straddle my hips. Her hand slides down again to put me inside of her ? both of us let out a soft moan. I close my eyes and enjoy the moment. Those black eyes stare back at me. My eyes fire open as I reach out and forcefully grab Mary by her arms. She looks surprised for a moment and slows down her rhythm. “What is it?” She asks again. “I-I don’t know.” I loosen my grip and control my breathing again. She regains her rhythm. I try to block out the dream ? But something tells me it’s just the beginning.
