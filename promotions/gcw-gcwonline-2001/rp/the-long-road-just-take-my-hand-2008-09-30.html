The Long Road: Just Take My Hand

30 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30 Sep 08 A familiar setting by now, Roberto on one knee, his body bruised and battered. The old man, Joaquin, stands in front of him, again victorious, his disappointed gaze burning a hole into Roberto’s soul. Roberto does not look up at the old man but he sure can feel the glare. Roberto’s long hair, sweaty and messy, sticks to his face, making the GCW icon look more monster than man. Joaquin places his staff under Roberto’s chin and forces him to look into his eyes. “The big day is here and you still continue to disappoint me.” Roberto doesn’t answer. “This is your time to speak boy.” And still nothing. “Very well.” Joaquin moves his staff, letting Roberto’s body slump to the floor. “It’s over.” NOW Roberto’s eyes find their way to Joaquin on their own as if pleading his case. “It can’t be.” Roberto whispers. “It isn’t.” “I knew this was a mistake,” Joaquin turns his back. “You’ve been a complete and utter failure since you were young. Now that you’ve grown you’re still a failure ? just a rich one.” Roberto pushes his tired body to one knee again. “I’ve changed.” “No, no you haven’t.” Joaquin turns around swiftly, anger in his eyes. “You are still that young, pathetic boy who MURDERED his family and then ran off to America never to be seen or heard from again.” The old man could have ripped Roberto’s heart out from his chest and couldn’t have have hurt him anymore than those words did. Joaquin sneers at Roberto’s facial expression. “Do not try to give me that sad puppy dog look. Save that fgor someone who might have sympathy for you. Cindy was my daughter!” “It was an accident!” Roberto’s voice is weak, defeated. “I never wanted?” “What” Never wanted to kill my little girl!” Steal my grand daughter from my arms?” Inside Joaquin’s eyes is something frightening but yet oddly familiar. “Well you did. Do you have any fucking idea what that felt like?” “Of course I do!” Roberto screams, his voice raspy and horse. “She was my wife!” “But I guess having those drinks with your fuck-up pals was waaay more important.” “FUCK YOU!” “Fuck me?” Joaquin laughs. “No, fuck you!” The old man unleashes a hellacious kick to Roberto’s face that sends him sprawling to the floor. Joaquin stalks Roberto until he is standing directly over him and places his boot on the back of Roberto’s neck. “I’ve waited a long time for this boy.”” He grinds his boot deeper, causing Roberto to gasp. “You deserved to die the night Cindy did ? NOT her!” “Y-You can’t.” Roberto pleads, hot tears forming in his eyes, prepared to spill out. “I loved her too.” “Save it.” Joaquin lifts his boot but reaches down, grabbing Roberto by the hair and pulls him up to his knees. “Revenge is very dark and brutal Roberto but sometimes it is so very delicious, wouldn’t you agree?” “This’This’I’Please’I” The old man shakes his head before leaning back, taking his staff in both hands. He cocks it back. “You won’t be able to hurt anyone else.” Joaquin swings as if hitting a fastball that’s over the middle of the plate causing the staff to snap in two as it collides with Roberto’s face. Roberto is sent backwards awkwardly, his legs beneath him but his eyes staring directly up into the black ceiling. It only takes a few seconds for Roberto to feel the burning sensation from the gash on his forehead and the hot blood that pours from it trailing down his face. Joaquin tosses the broken staff to the floor, amazing himself that he hit Roberto with such force. “Now you can fully appreciate what you put that poor girl through. She was helpless Roberto. Helpless, bleeding to death, alone and afraid. Before you breath your last breath and the life in your eyes fades to darkness I want you to suffer as she suffered.” Roberto manages to roll to his side, trying to alleviate some of the discomfort. “Please, I have a family.” Joaquin’s evil scowl melts away instantaneously, his face becoming blank and emotionless. “Don’t you worry Roberto, not at all. I told you that you’d suffer the same way my daughter did. She was not alone in her fate was she” “Joaquin’s despicable grin returns. “I’ll make sure once I’m through with you that your son joins you in hell.” There are moments in life when you have to let go, accept your destiny. Some people find ways to duck and dodge these moments, afraid of whats to come. Some people just don’t know when the moment is even if they’ve searched their entire life. In this one moment, for Roberto Abitua, a lifetime of loneliness, guilt, depression, sadness, heartache, stress and hopelessness transforms. In the blink of an eye Roberto focuses every ounce of emotion he’s shown through his past traisl and tribulations into one very powerful emotion? Anger Yes, Roberto is at a high level of pisstivity The old man moves forward, ready to go for the kill shot. Roberto reaches out in front of him, gripping one half of the broken staff, eyeing the jagged edge the whole time. With one rush of adrenaline Roberto bounces up and lunges at Joaquin, driving the jagged end of his weapon into the old man’s gut. The two meet eye to eye, Joaquin’s eyes wide and in pain but Roberto’s bright and full of hate. Roberto releases an animalistic growl as he drives his former mentor back into the wall behind him. “GO TO HELL!” Roberto pulls the weapon from Joaquin’s body “GO TO HELL!” And drives it in again “GO TO HELL!” And again, this time twisting it and snapping it in half, leaving one half stuck inside of Joaquin’s body. With the final break Roberto takes a step back from the wall and allows the old man to crumble, lifeless, by his feet. Roberto’s eyes are wide, glowing bright blue because of his rage. His breathing is erratic, his hands tremble. Then, as if being smashed into by a freight train, all of his senses come back all at once. Roberto keels over and throws up. A million thoughts race through his mind. What do I do? Should I call the police? It’s self defense right? What will Mary think? Is my career over? Can Caldera help? I’m fucked! Amidst all of the confusion, thoughts and questions that are going on inside of Roberto’s head, one pops up to the surface ? I’m not alone. Clap-Clap-Clap-Clap Applause? The sound of the slow clapping distracts him, forcing him to turn his head and try to focus from behind his mask of blood, tears, hair and sweat. It takes just a few moments for him to realize who his fan base in. “LeStatt?” “Bravo, Bravo.” LeStatt walks closer to Roberto, still slowly clapping until he stops. “I’m proud of you.” “I killed someone!” “NO!” LeStatt roars. “You survived! He was going to kill you ? and Javier.” “But ? I?” “No, don’t say what you’re thinking.” Knight moves closer still. “There are no apologies needed for saving your own life.” LeStatt paces back and forth a couple of times, collecting his own thoughts. “Look at you,” Knight smirks. “You look like hell.” “What do I do now?” “Nothing. It’s already handled.” “Are you kidding me” LOOK!” Roberto turns his head and points towards Joaquin but his voice is taken away from him for a moment. His heart begins to pound in his chest. “He’s gone?”” “Indeed he is.” LeStatt reaches into his pocket, pulling a lighter and a cigarette out. He proceeds to light it up. “If he was ever there at all.” “That’s impossible, how’d he..”” “A lot like me,” Knight takes a puff and releases the smoke into the already very sour and stale air. “Your mind is quite powerful.” Roberto shakes his head. “No damn way, he was right there!” “Rob, listen to me. Joaquin has been dead for over eight years now. Killed himself a couple of years after Cindy died. Couldn’t handle the stress I suppose.” A pause. Roberto clenches his hands into fists. “So I’m nuts.” Roberto says matter of factly “Perhaps.” LeStatt kneels next to Roberto, giving Roberto his first good, hard look at LeStatt. A feeling of anxiety and nervousness rushes Roberto as he finally realizes that Knight is no longer tied up. His first instinct to try to subdue him, put him back in his place. But he doesn’t. “You’re free.” Roberto whispers even if a whisper wasn’t what he was trying for. “I am,” Knight brushes his hair from his face. “I told you it was inevitable. Fate can be a funny thing. You know whats to come yet its always surprising when it actually happens.” Roberto takes in a deep breath. “So what now” Kill me? Lock me away?” Knight smiles. “No, believe it or not I’m going to offer you a choice. A chance to make things right.” “What kind of choice could possibly make this right?” “It’s simple. You can rot in this cage forever or you can allow me to do what you created me for.” “And what is that?” “To make things better. You’ve fought me off for so long now, afraid of what I might do, allowing yourself to completely forget that there was a reason you brought me here.” LeStatt stands back up, appearing as some kind of dark god to Roberto. He drops his cigarette and crushes it with his boot. One last cloud of smoke is released into the air. “You think I’m the reason you become angry and out of control but it is not true. We both just witnessed what you are capable of on your own. I’m your balance, I am what some might call “controlled chaos”. I am your strength, your drive, your god damn PURPOSE!” Roberto quickly steals a glance towards the spot where Joaquin’s body was. “I thought he’d help me.” “The hell with that old bat. The only person that can save your sorry ass is you. Like it or not ? I am you.” LeStatt offers his hand. “You’ll get this opportunity only once. If you give in we will be unstoppable.” Roberto’s eyes, lost and afraid, look out towards LeStatt’s “peace offering”. His mind takes him back to all of the times he has denied LeStatt his existance. Those battles with his other half, his stronger half have helped mold him. Roberto thinks about Knight’s words, about GCW, his family ? about Cindy. “I’m a time bomb aren’t I” Just waiting to explode.” LeStatt Knight grins that famously sadistic grin. “It’s time you exploded.” After years of fighting it, Roberto doesn’t have it in him anymore. He knows now that he cannot do it alone. He has looked for answers from outside sources, Caldera and his Establishment, Mary, the mirage of a normal life. Now perhaps its time to look inside of himself. He shuts his eyes tight and extends his hand towards LeStatt’s, which the dark figure grabs eagerly. “Boom.” I-ya, I against I, flesh of my flesh, and mind of my mind, two of a kind but one won't survive...
