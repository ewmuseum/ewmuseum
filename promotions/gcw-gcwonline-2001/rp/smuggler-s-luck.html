Smuggler's luck

undated

Date: Timeless This had to be a joke. 'I said prepare to be boarded.' I almost laughed. Then I saw the face on my viewscreen and I did laugh. This guy was human and he looked to be about 12 years old. 'You're kidding me right?' I asked, putting up my shields and dropping into a barrel roll as he started shooting at me. It turns out I didn't need my shields, this guy's aim was so bad it looks like he couldn't hit the sun with a heat seeking missile. I was torn what to do as he chased me around shooting at me and missing by miles. I could easilly outfly him and turn him into sub-atomic particles, or I could easilly circle around him and cripple his ship with repairs that he could make easilly, but would provide me with plenty of escape time, or I could simply outfly him and jump into hyperspace. Ever since the citizens of Global City on Caldera had placed a 50,000 credit bounty on my head for an accidental computer virus wiping out every record in the city everyone was after me. I had taken one piece of advice the late Lex the Marauder (at least I was pretty sure he was dead) left me. I avoided the Western sector of the galaxy, and Caldera more specifically. I had also had my apprearance slightly changed, but the StarSkimmer was still with me and I was not getting rid of my baby. I arced away as this newest fresh faced bounty hunter shot at me and decided that I should just leave him out here while I casually got away. I dropped my ship down and went into an emergency stop and he shot past me. A few well aimed shots left everything but the life support in need of serious repairs. 'See ya later, looser!' I laughed as I checked my navigation systems and tried to figure out where I was and where I could go. 'Now this looks interesting.' I said finding a planet that offered more vices than you could shake an arm/tentacle/antenna at. In fact that was the name of the planet, Vice. 'My kinda place!' I bellowed out in a singsongy voice. And aimed my ship towards it. Once I'd gotten landing clearance and had landed and changed out of my flightsuit into something more suited to the atmosphere of the planet I decided to check out the main strip of the portion of the planet I'd landed on. It was all bright lights and noise. Beings from every sector of the galaxy congregated here. I saw signs in windows in lanugages I spoke and didn't speak. But all of them said pretty much the same thing. They didn't care what planet your money was fron, it was all good here. While I was changing I had grabbed one of my many strongboxes in the hold of my ship and loaded myself down with a few thousand credits which I was more than eager to spend. When you've stolen---er, I mean acquired as much money as I have, you don't worry about loosing some of it on games of chance. I finally settled on a casino/hotel that was so damn tall I couldn't even see the top of it. I could however see the guard ships that circled it at intervals going up. High class place. I walked in and a green multitentacled tuxedo wearing Greel greeted me with a sound that sounded like it was about to spit on me but translated into 'Welcome.' I nodded at it and made my way around. The usual vices were here, just in a more high class setting. I almost expected to see Elvis walking around. That was if this was in fact his home planet. Ever since he 'died' on earth he's been buzzing around the known galaxy, of course that was over a hundred years ago. I spotted something that looked like it was right up my alley. Holo-poker. I changed out some of my credits for chips and hit the table sliding into one of the three emtpy seats, and dropped the five hundred credit buy in. The robot dealer tossed two cards to me, I watched as the faces changed before settling on 11. The invention of computerized cards that randomly changed faces when they were dealt put an end to card counters. I played a few hands before I moved on. I circled around the casino playing games here and there. I even stopped to play a couple of slot machines. All told by the time I headed to the bar for a drink, I was down 5,000 credits. Not too bad considering I still had plenty left. I sat at the bar and scanned the drink list, all three pages of it. I finally settled on something that looked like it would agree with the human side of me and ordered it. The bar was round and took up the middle of the casino and was just as gaudy as the rest of the place. The barmaids were all attractive, I don't care what planets they were from there were a few that I couldn't deny I wanted to find out if they were compatable sexually with me. As I was about to finish off my drink I felt someone else come up behind me and the unmistakable feel of a blaster barrel being shoved into my ribs. 'Fuck.' I mumbled. 'Get up real slow and don't do anything stupid or I'll pull the trigger and at this range I won't miss.' Well I'll be damned, the little idiot from earlier fixed his ship a lot faster than I gave him credit for. I thought I'd left him with hours of repairs, well at least more than three hours worth. I got up and he moved next to me and slung an arm over my shoulder like we were old pals. 'Just smile and nod and act like were good buddies. If I have to shoot you I can get lost in this crowd easilly.' 'There's at least fifteen cameras on us right now, and if you don't get that fucking gun out of my ribs I promise you I will kill you before you can pull the trigger, and no one will know for at least ten, fifteen minutes while I make my escape.' The pressure from the blaster eased up but the 'friendly' grip on my shoulder didn't. He guided me out a side entrance and then down a side street. 'Now hand over the medallion and I'll be on my way.' 'What is it with you bounty hunters and my medallion? Is that supposed to be proof that you killed me? I thought they wanted me brought in.' I asked turing to face him, his blaster raised at me. 'It's been promised to whoever brings you in. It turns out it's worth quite a lot.' 'And it's been bounced around the galaxy.' I said, 'I didn't just take this from wherever it came from you know. I got it from Eva VL who got it from...someone. You want it, take it like a man. Put the gun down, there's nothing between you and me except air and opportuinity.' 'Nice try,' my baby faced bounty hunter said to me, 'But no dice Case.' He assumed I was Case Morgan, one of the many alises I traveled under. 'You can't blame me for trying to keep my head attached,' I said rasing my hands and taking the medallion off. I handed it to him. He looked at it and placed it in a pocket, and motioned for me to turn around. Suddnely something was jabbed into my neck. I grabbed the point of impact and staggered forward. 'Son of a Alterian whore!' I yelled looking back at him. He had a vial in his hand filled with my blood, or at least what I passed off as blood. 'They say to truly understand a being is to partake of their blood.' he said emptying the contents of the needle into a vial and then tipping the vial to his lips. 'Bottoms up.' He took a sip and then spit it out. I guess he didn't know I was a cyborg and there was more than just blood running through my system. As he gagged on the motor oil tainted blood I swung a right that caught him on the side of the head, he staggered to the side and I grabbed him by the front of his tunic and tossed him across the alley into the wall with spine jarring force. He slumped to the ground, looking dazed, I pulled him to his feet and slammed my steel lined skull into his non-steel lined skull. I heard the audible crack and dropped him. He'd be alright in a couple of hours. Of course he'd have a wicked headache and be in serious need of a good doctor, but he'd live. I didn't kill unless I needed to, and this was one situation where I didn't need to. I recovered my medallion and headed back to the casino. That four breasted barmaid had been giving me looks earlier, I wondered what time the sun rose, and when the barmaid got off. And how.
