Finding the Lair

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/19/08 date written Paul slowly walked along the streets of Paris; enjoying the beauty of everything around him. He knew that Del would be nervous waking up and finding him not next to her. He just couldn't lie there anymore, staring at the ceiling and pondering why Vassago had shown up and what it was he wanted. Paul hated the way he would stare at Delylah, lust filling his eyes as if he was undressing her with his cold, hard, calculating eyes. Paul knew that Del's plan wouldn't be easy. As much as she hated the idea he knew that the only way Cleo would stop torturing her was to kill her. Then Daniel would be completely free of the world he hated. The world that his mother absolutely adored. Paul stopped in front of a gym and had to repress the notion to go inside and pump his thoughts away. He knew that it wouldn't make the situation any different; he just wanted the thoughts to quit racing around the proverbial race track in his mind. Paul looked behind him having the feeling that someone was watching him. No one was there but Paul still quickened his strides to return to the hotel as quickly as he could. When Paul entered the lobby of the hotel the woman at the front desk stopped him. ?Pardon sir but you 'ave a message.? She said dreamily as she batted her dark eyelashes at him. Paul didn't pay her no mind as he quickly took the slip of paper and headed toward the elevators. The golden doors of the elevator opened and Paul stepped in and looked down at the piece of paper. I have seen where you sleep. I will eliminate you from the equation and make her mine. Paul shuddered and cursorily ripped the piece of paper to shreds. He couldn't let Del find out about this, she was already on edge just knowing that ?The Gatekeeper? was in Paris. ******************** Vassago sat and watched over the sarcophagus as Cleopatra and Daniel slept. Thoughts of opening Daniel's coffin and spiking him through the heart made a smile play about his normally taught lips. There was something about the kid that he just didn't like. Cleo proposed that he was intimidated by him but Vassago scoffed at the idea. Why would he be intimidated by a boy that would perpetually be twelve years old? Vassago's face twisted with thought. Maybe it was because he is who truly brought Paul and Delylah here to Paris. If it weren't for the boy he and Cleo would be free to continue their reign of terror on Paris. There had to be a way that he could convince Cleo to free herself from the boy. His plans did not include a child. The sun slowly crept up over the horizon as Vassago lifted himself from his post. He knew it was time to go and put part of his plan into action. He would do whatever it would take to get Paul and Delylah in a state of hysteria. If they weren't thinking clearly they would become easy pray. Vassago wanted to make sure that what happened before, in the Nguyen home, wouldn't happen again. ********************* A young man about the age of twenty was escorting a lovely young woman. Her ebony hair flowed in the light breeze. He looked down on her with his cerulean blue eyes with lust. Her ruby red lips curved into a seductive smile as she led him into an alley. He pressed his body close to hers as their lips engulfed each other. He ran his hands up and down the length of her body, settling on her large breast. She let a small moan escape her lips as her fangs slowly came into sight. His face twisted in fear as he struggled to free himself from her embrace. She sunk her fangs into the tender flesh covering the carotid artery. Blood sprayed into her mouth in rhythm to the beat of his frantic heart. His body slowly starts to go limp and she releases her grip, tossing him ruthlessly farther into the darkness of the alley. A young face peers around the corner, his face twisted with disgust and contempt. She smiles at him as if she didn't notice that he hated her. ?You see my son, it isn't hard to capture your prey.? Cleo's voice took on a motherly tone as her eyes softened, ?All you need to do is use your abilities. Glamor them, make them believe that they want to go with you,? Cleo's eyes narrowed her voice becoming harsh, ?Then you take what you need from them. Take their blood into your body, it is so much better than blood from a bag.? Cleo laughed as Daniel's jaw dropped with shock, ?How... how did you know?? Daniel stuttered. ?I know what you do, you are a part of me and I a part of you.? Cleo patted him on the head as her lips curled up in a forced smile. ?Your auntie Delylah is here and you will need to get a strong stomach to be able to tolerate what we're going to do to her and her lover boy Paul.? Cleo spat, a fire blazing deeply in those emerald eyes, ?She has done enough to ruin my life and I plan to put an end to it all.? Delylah's eyes fluttered open as the golden light from the rising sun filtered through the blinds. She ground the sleep from her eyes and looked around the room. She turned to her left side to curl up to Paul but he wasn't there. Delylah jumped up out of bed and rushed to the door that led to her mother's room. She was there still fast asleep. Delylah let out a sigh of relief but still wondered where Paul was. She returned to her room and searched for a note. She found nothing. She began pacing the floors trying to think of where he might be. She prayed to God that they didn't take him in the middle of the nigh and she didn't even notice. Tears streamed down her cheeks as her anger grew by the minute. If they did take him they wouldn't win by getting her upset she would use her anger to fuel her, to make her stronger. The soft click of the lock on the door snapped Delylah's head around. She poised herself and was ready to put up a fight. The door slowly crept open and Paul slid his large frame into the room. He saw Del pacing in the center of the room seething, he slowly walked up behind her and wrapped an arm around her waist. Del brought back an elbow connecting with Paul's jaw. Paul stumbled back and Delylah spun around to face her ?assailant?. Delylah grinned and then narrowed her eyes. ?Serves you right John Paul Samson, you had me worried to death.? Delylah screamed. ?I'm sorry Del. I couldn't sleep and I thought I would be back by the time you woke up.? Paul's voice took on the tone of a little boy being admonished by his mother. Delylah's eyes softened, she couldn't stay mad at him for too long. ?I thought they took you in the middle of the night.? Tears shone in Delylah's eyes but she held them back, ?We're going to have to be more careful Paul. They are going to try and get inside your head as well as mine. They want us to be completely frazzled so we will be easier target.? The tears broke free of their dam and slid down her cheeks. Paul walked forward and kissed each cheek, trying to stop the tears from flowing down her beautiful face. He hated to see her cry, it tore him apart inside. ?I didn't want to tell you this but...? Paul stopped and guided her to a chair so she could sit, ?I had a message when I returned from my walk. They know where we are Del. He left me a message at the front desk, I know it was him.? Paul's eyes gleamed with anger and frustration. They both knew it would only be a matter of time before Cleo would know Delylah was here and thus revealing their exact location. Things were going to be much harder now. There were two of them to worry about instead of just one. Del knew that Cleo was going to try and turn Daniel against her before they struck. That way they would have the upper hand. The door to Nebt-Het' room swung open and she stepped through. She looked like a fashion plate, her dark hair pinned back in a bun and looking like she was ready to conquer another day. ?Good morning sleepy head,? Nebt-Het smiled brightly, ?Don't tell me you forgot you have your final dress fitting today.? Nebt-Het's eyes filled with displeasure and her smile faded. ?No mother I remembered. I just slept in a little longer than I expected,? Delylah flicked a smile to Paul signaling him to not say anything about all that had occurred this morning. ?Let me shower real quick and then get dressed. Why don't you and Paul go downstairs and have a quick bite to eat and I will meet you downstairs in say fifteen minutes.? Delylah beamed a smile at them as she tried to hide her true feelings. ?If you're not down there in fifteen minutes I am coming to check on you,? Paul whispered in her ear before he kissed her soft lips. Delylah nodded and headed directly for the shower. She had to put on a facade for her mother whom she knew would worry terribly if she knew the whole truth of everything that was going on. She had to protect her and keep her from falling in to another state of disillusionment and melancholia. It had been a rough road for all of them since that night in her family home. She still has trouble believing that her father is dead. Delylah dressed hastily and rushed down to meet Paul and her mother. They had just finished having tea and croissants with currant jelly. Delylah declined anything to eat, her stomach was still in knots. She placed a kiss on Paul's cheek and headed out the door with her mother. Paul stood and watched them slide into the back of the taxi and speed away down the streets of Paris. Now Paul would begin his research. He had to find out where her lair was. With the tidbits that Del had provided he should be able to pinpoint a place where she would hide out for the day. ******************** Paul headed down the street with a quick pace. He had walked by a library and was determined to find it again. When he did a small smile graced his lips and he walked through the front door. The library was massive, bigger than anything he had ever seen in the States. A petite brunette stood behind the desk watching everything and everyone. As soon as Paul stepped up to the desk her face softened and she permitted her lips to turn upwards. ?Excuse me ma'am but could you tell me where the computers are?? Paul asked in as hushed a voice as he could muster. ?They are all the way in the back. Follow this line of bookshelves and then take a sharp left and you will see them.? The woman whispered back. Paul nodded and headed in the direction she told him to. Paul sat at the first available computer and typed in Jardin des Tuileries. He scrolled through all the pictures and found that it was located near The Louvre. He brought his hand up to pinch his chin between his thumb and forefinger as he pondered the notion that they might be there somewhere. He rose from the chair and set out toward The Louvre museum. He would go there and check things out before he told Del a possible location for their lair. He had a gut feeling that they were there. He would know the minute he set foot in the place. Then they would be able to devise a plan of attack. When all of this was through then he would be able to marry the woman he loved. Everything would be peaceful at long last.
