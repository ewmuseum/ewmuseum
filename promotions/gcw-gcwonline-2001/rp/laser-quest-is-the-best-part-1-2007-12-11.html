Laser Quest Is The Best Part 1

11 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/07 START The scene opens to a relaxed downtown atmosphere of the next hosting city to a GCW WorldWide. However there is one “Kid” roaming the streets tonight that isn’t so relaxed. Clyde Fox churns his feet frantically as he pounds the pavement on a local sidewalk. Clyde then notices a big clock just above a nearby bank. 8:55 pm “Gosh darn it! I’ve got five minutes before my game!” Clyde calculated. That’s right. Clyde Fox is going? LASER QUESTING! “Now didn’t I book my game for 9:00 pm” or was it 9:05 pm?” Fox questioned, “Wait a minute Clyde, it doesn’t matter, you just gotta get there” BIG TIME!” Clyde quickly crosses the street even though the do not cross sign was lit. “Oh my ginger snaps! I just broke-ed the law! No time to care, I have a date with a laser gun!” Clyde insisted. 8:56 pm Hurry Clyde, hurry. Four minutes until your game starts! But wait, he better look out for that bum on the street who is hustling people for change! “S-Spare change sir?” The bum inquired as he holds both hands out in a bowl shape. Clyde kneels down to get on the same level as the trench coat wearing disadvantaged homeless gentleman. “Sir, I’m sorry, I don’t carry a second set of clothes with me. But if I pee my pants after my laser quest game, which I usually do when I am in dark places for a really long time, then I’ll give them to you on my way back, okay?” Fox suggested. Offended, the bum retracts his hands and huddles into his coat. “But here, you look like you could use some money,” Fox said as he tosses a few pennies at the man. 8:57 pm He’s only got three minutes. Will he make it? Stay tuned for the exciting conclusion? right now! Ring, ring. The buzzer on the door rings as Clyde compiles himself in the main corridor of the Laser Quest building. Victorious, Clyde struts on up to the main desk where a lovely lady awaits to serve him. “Heto,” Clyde greeted. “Hello,” The lady responded. “Heto,” Clyde stated again. “Hi” again. I’m Charlotte and welcome to Laser Quest. How can I help you?” Charlotte inquired. “I play Laser Quest in three minutes Charmin!” Fox proudly stated. “It’s Charlotte. Can I have your name please?” Charlotte asked. “Charlotte,” Fox said. “No that’s my name, I need your name,” Charlotte confirmed. “Oh okay. EFF O EXX. Fox,” Clyde delivered. “First name too please,” Charlotte nudged. “Charlotte,” Fox recited. “No” your first name. Is it Clyde?” Charlotte asked. “HOW DID YOU KNOW CHARMIN?” Fox said with conviction. “Because it says your first name in the appointment book, I just have to double check that you’re not stealing someone else’s game,” Charlotte explained. “Oh icy” icy. So Charmin, are you as soft as the toilet paper?” Fox played. “Excuse me” For the last time my name is Charlotte!” Charlotte encouraged. “Like chill girl. You don’t gots-ta-be all up in my grill,” Fox deflected. “Anyways, your game will start once the game that is going on now finishes. So, where are the rest of your players?” Charlotte investigated. “Come again?” Fox asked as he leaned on the front desk. “You booked a game, where is the rest of your party?” Charlotte stated. “I just wanna play Laser Quest,” Clyde said. “Have you ever done this before?” Charlotte asked. “Yeah I SO have. The last time was in like, 1997 for like, my, like, 10th birthday, like,” Fox boasted, “My mommy booked a game for me and I was even allowed to invite Tommy Bridges from school even though in the previous month, Tommy didn’t invite me to his birthday party at the bowling alley so I stuck gum in his blonde hair and he had to get it cut!” “I see--? Charlotte gets cut off. “No it’s “icy” my dear,” Fox informs. “Okay well whatever. But if you just wanted to play by yourself, all you had to do is call and we would set you up in someone else’s game. So now you booked the whole place for an hour by yourself,” Charlotte explained. Just then, the game that ran from 8:00 pm to 9:00 pm let out. A bunch of fluttering six year olds and two parents emerged from the game zone. “Hmmm” IDEA!” Fox thought to himself. “Well Clarinet, I have a group. I’ll just let these kids play with me!” Fox frolicked. The male parent then walks up to the front desk. “Thanks for the game, we’ll see you again sometime,” The man stated as he started to head for the door. “Wait sir!” Clyde begged, “How would you like to let your kids play another game?” “Well I don’t see why not,” The man replied. “But there is one thing that I need. I gotta play too!” Clyde popped. The man just stood there. He scanned Clyde from head to toe as if with his eyes he was saying: “Dude, do you see how big you are” So many questions come to mind. Mainly, aren’t you a little old for this? And a little big? And a little weird, I mean, who wants to go into a dark sweaty room and run around with a “big gun” chasing little kids all night?” “Are you serious?” The man asked sarcastically. “Serious as Snapple!” Fox enlightened. The man then turns to the woman parent. “What do you think dear?” The man asks. “What’s he going to do Robert” Kill them? I mean we’re right here. I think it’s a very nice offer; a free game for Greg and the boys,” The lady pitched. “Well alright, I guess so,” The man agreed as the children went wild and rushed into the equipment room. “Okay! Now I can brush up on my skills to show my big brother who the heck he’s messing with!” Fox exclaimed. “Pardon me?” The man asked. “Oh well the whole reason why I’m here is to become a little more fierce because my brother has been pushing me around lately. And the last time I played Laser Quest was when I was ten and boy did I ever hurt one kid so bad. I figure that I should try and reignite the fire! Oh and Clarinet, my code name is “Child Killer?” L-O-L. Just kidding! It’s really “Star Fox?” BIG TIME!” Fox joked. Stay tuned for the exciting conclusion of “Laser Quest Is The Best”. END
