Setting The Standard

25 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/2009 Setting the Standard’ .: High Impact Training Facility (Atlanta, GA) October 25, 2009 2:00 pm:. (Scene One) {The camera comes into focus as a training session in going on inside the ring area. There are about 12 guys doing some drills with one of Atlanta’s most premiere wrestling coaches/trainers in the area. The camera zooms in on Roger Powers as he shows his students different drills that will get them ready for some local shows and maybe even a chance to compete in the big time one day. Meanwhile, as Roger is training his students the front door opens as the bell goes off on the door letting the front desk that someone has entered into the building. The camera pans over to the door and zooms in on a man walking into the facility. The man looks to be about 6’4 238lbs. He has a physique that is well defined with very big muscles and a nice six pack. He is wearing some Timberland Boots (tan), a white LRG designed long sleeve thermal shirt that is tucked in the front. He has both ears pierced with sparkling diamonds, a long diamond chain; he is wearing Gucci sunglasses and is sporting an Atlanta premiere uptown fade. He stands there and then takes off his sunglasses and looks around. Meanwhile Roger spots the man and stops his class. He climbs out of the ring and walks over to the man. The man smiles as he speaks.} BS: "Roger Powers." RP: "Bishop Steele. How are you doing man?" BS: "Well as you see I can’t complain one bit. How is everything holding up here?" RP: "Couldn’t be any better these days. I see business is booming as usual." BS: "You know it man. I have just opened my 10 store here in the states and plan on opening one in Italy soon." RP: "Well I must say you still look amazing." BS: "Well you know I owe my physique to High Impact. You guys are the one who made me look this way and I thank you." RP: "How is Chainz doing?" BS: "Well you know he runs and owns the SWA now out of Atlanta and is doing quite well for himself." RP: "So do you still have the skills in the ring?" BS: "Are you serious Roger. I will take on your top wrestler to prove to you that I still got it and then some." RP: "Oh really. Well since you say that I got the perfect person for you." BS: "Bring him on. I need a little tune up before my debut." RP: "Your debut what are you talking about." BS: "I have a PPV match next week in the GCW." RP: "Really that is cool. Well go get dressed in the locker room and we will get this little exhibition started." BS: "Alright." {Bishop walks back to the back locker room and gets changed. Meanwhile Roger talks to his students about Bishop.} Student 1: "Who in the hell was that?" RP: "That was one of my students a few years back and now he just got signed to a big contract with a major company." Student 2: "Is he any good?" RP: "You guys are going to find out what happens if you really want to make this dream of being a professional wrestler come true. Michael you are going to wrestle Bishop in an exhibition match." Michael: "Alright I got this one in the bag." RP: "We will see." {Bishop comes out and looks focused and ready to showcase his skills to Rogers’s students. As he walks to the ring he locks eyes with his opponent. He smirks at him with a cocky look and climbs into the ring. He walks straight up to the kid and speaks.} BS: "I hope you are ready for this." Michael: "Huh I was born ready." {After the comment from Roger’s student Bishop turns his back and then quickly turns back around landing a right hand to the side of Michael’s face. Michael stumbles. Bishop pounces wasting no time. He drops a serious of elbows to Michael’s chest as he lies on the ground. He then locks him in a rear naked choke, squeezing the life out of him for a brief moment. He then let’s go and then heads towards the ropes. He springs off and hits a furious forearm shot to Michael’s face, dropping him once again to the mat. He then walks over to the corner and waits for Michael to get up to his feet. Michael stands up slowly stunned from the forearm and starts to turn around. As he turns around he is met by a Superkick straight to the nose and jaw. Michael flips backwards and hits the mat. Bishop then smirks over at Roger and begins to set Michael up for a devasting finisher. He speaks.} BS: "This is what I call No Remorse." {Bishop then hits his flipping piledriver on Michael leaving him motionless on the mat. He then covers him and Roger counts. 1……2…..3…….. The bell get rung and Bishop stands up. He then walks out of the ring and heads towards the locker room. Roger yells.} RP: "Hey Bishop?" BS: "What." RP: "Good luck and well done." BS: "I don’t need luck. When you look like me and are considered True Perfection the only person that needs luck is my opponents. What you just witnessed is me Setting The Standard for the GCW." {The camera fades away with Bishop walking into the locker room, with not one drop of sweat falling off his forehead……….}
