Smuggler's run Part 2

undated

Date: Timeless Well this is just peachy. If you can't tell, I'm being sarcastic, this downright sucks. I avoided being captured by Lex and Eva. I avoided being killed in the Fallout Zone and managed to loose Meatskin T-Wreck (well actually he got himself killed), got my shield generator upgraded, and even spent a couple of days relaxing. Now this. This just in case you were wondering was me being cornered by Lex. The bastard had somehow, somewhere slipped a tracking device onto my ship and had been watching my every move and following me everywhere I went for I don't know how long. He had taken the long way around the Fallout Zone and tracked me to the planet where I was getting my ship's sheild generator upgraded. So natuarally he paid off the mechanic to sabatoge it a bit. Then he tracked me after I left the planet and shot me down, he did just enough damage so I could land without my ship (and me) blowing into several thousand pieces, but not be able to effect quick repairs and take off. And he chose the most inhospitable planet to chase me to. The planet had been dead for at least 50 years if memory served me correctly. It had been caught in the tail end of a comet and all life was instantly wiped out. Every man, woman and child of seventeen different species were wiped out in seconds. The only consolation for any of them was that it was quick and painless. Relatives of the deceased had visited the planet but that was fifty years ago, and most of the relatives of the deceased were deceased themselves. The area where Lex forced me to land was a large six story complex used to park all manner of ships. The parking complex still held all manner of ships, all of them abandoned and mostly falling apart due to a half century of neglect. The top of the complex where I landed held what looked like it used to be a civilian low level transport, a couple of freight cruisers, and six bulk transport ships. And now my crippled StarSkimmer and a StarJammer class ship that Lex came out of. His white eyes glowed from his black, featurless face, and I could see his smile, all teeth as he walked towards me, casually, knowing that this time I wouldn't be able to use tricks to get away. Just for good measure though I'd found the tracking device and was busy stomping it into spare parts. 'Just you and me now, Mike.' he said as he reached me, but stood well out of arms reach. 'Nowhere to run, nowhere to hide. No lives to bargain with, no one else to interfere. Just you and me.' 'Tell me something,' I said kicking away the last remnants of the tracking device, 'Who exactly put this bounty on my head? It's been bothering me for the longest time. It's not Eva is it? Because you did work with her for at least one attempt at capturing me. And I did take the medallion from her. I just wanna know.' 'The people of Global City.' Fuck. Global City was the largest city on the largest planet in the galaxy, the planet Caldera. Home to over ten billion beings of all life forms, shapes and sizes. I had been there once before, breifly, and made off with a few million credits in ill gotten gains. I didn't think that was enough to warrant my head on a silver platter. 'All of them?' Global city itself held about 100,000,000 beings. 'No, but a good chunk of them. You see when you stole those credits, and set up your little computer backup to erase all traces of your being there, it erased everything.' 'Define everything.' 'Everything, as in every computer in Global City crashed and everyone's information was erased. Everyone from the lowest level factory worker to the President of Global City himself. Every birth file, every bank account, every tax record, every property record, every ship and vehichle registration. Everything, you turned the entire economy of that city into nothing with one little keystroke.' he said, frowning at me now. 'So wait, if I destroyed all that, how did you know it was me?' 'Well not everything was on the network. Eyewitness with a hand held video recorder saw you leaving the scene. Good thing they had night vision on because just as you made your escape is when the entire city collapsed due to your computer backup.' 'Well it's not like I meant for that to happen. I just wanted to shut down the bank itself, and at that just the security systems, and the video files. I don't know how it fried the entire grid of the entire city! You can't tell me they think I did that on purpose!' 'Someone did and offered the ten thousand credit reward for you to be brought back alive.' Lex said, 'But it turns out getting the network turned back on was more difficult than they thought. Slowly but surely backup systems have been kicking in, the first ones in the hospitals, good thing for you because there would be millions of deaths on your head if they'd stayed offline.' 'So why the upgrade to fifty thou and the dead or alive?' 'Because of the problems getting the network back online. There are still millions of people without their identities intact. The backup files are corrupted and it's taken a team of hacker and data recovery experts from a dozen planets to slowly clean up all the files. Millions of beings are still without their identities because of you.' 'I didn't do it on purpose! Why can't you understand that?' 'That doesn't matter to them! They don't know, and I doubt they would accept that as a decent excuse. You ruined lives and now you have to pay for it.' 'So you gonna shoot me and take me in?' I asked. 'I'm not a killer, you know that.' 'I never said kill me Lex, I said shoot me. I know your gun has a stun setting.' 'No, I'm not going to shoot you Mike.' 'Then what?' Lex dropped his weapons, he took off his flight jacket and cracked his neck. 'The only fair way to do this. One on one.' 'You wanna fight me? And if I loose? What? You'll take me back to Caldera? They'll kill me before you even count off your first fifty credits!' I said, 'Screw that, I'm not going back!' 'I won't take you back there. How about that medallion?' 'You're crazy. I should shoot you right now and get it over with.' 'There will just be another one after me, you know that.' Lex said, 'You give me the medallion and I'll even go so far as to fake a video of your accidental death.' 'Wait a sec,' I said, 'Why would you do that? You've dogged me all the way from one end of the galaxy to the other, shot at me, nearly blew my ship up, and for what? To get fifty thousand credits over a fake video of me dying?' 'Under any other circumstances I'd bring you in to face justice, but like you said, you'd be dead before I even left the planet. So I'm giving you the option, if you fight me, hand to hand, man to Marauder, I'll leave you alone. If I win, I take the medallion and fake a video of you accidentally dying during our fight.' 'And if I win?' I asked. 'Then you keep the medallion and I'll fake video of your ship blowing up. Either way you win. You'll still be alive, although I'd advise not going anywhere near Caldera for the rest of your life. Heck, staying out of the Western sector of the galaxy might be a better idea.' I mulled this over for a few seconds. He had a point. I'd be alive, and I knew some people here in the Northern sector who could help change my appearance. I looked back at Lex. I dropped my weapons, took off my flight jacket and cracked my neck. 'Let's do it then.' I said taking off the medallion and dropping it on top of my jacket. We moved towards each other fists balled up ready to fight, man to Marauder.
