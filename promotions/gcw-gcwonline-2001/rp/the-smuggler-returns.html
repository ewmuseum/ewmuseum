The Smuggler Returns

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A while ago I once swore I'd never come back to this planet, but considering the fact that I was 'dead', I could risk it. Plus I had left a few things behind that I'd need. And there was the tournament. Caldera loomed before me like a huge marble in space, and as I broke the atmosphere and headed towards New City the planetary secrurity team contacted me in the form of two blue and white security ships on either side of me. 'Do not deviate from your present course.' one of them said, 'Please state your business on Caldera.' 'I'm here for the tournament in New City.' I said, and then fired off one of my many aliases. My navigation console beeped and some numbers flew across followed by a map to a landing platform. 'Please follow the pre-programmed course.' I locked onto it, and followed the path in. I landed my fairly new StarBurst 300 and was met by the security personel who checked out my fake ID and let me through without a problem. New City was just a few miles away from Global City, which as I understood was still undergoing reprogramming after a computer virus left by my former self wiped out the entire city's databanks. Not on purpose mind you. Hey, everyone makes mistakes right? Anyway, that had earned a ten thousand credit bounty on my head and I was followed by Lex the Marauder who almost got me, but was run over by a myterious assailaint, I had no way of knowing if Lex was alive, so I left him for dead in his ship. Turns out he was alive after all, I saw footage of him on the SpaceNet News. After it was found out that I was responsible for wiping out Global City's mainframe, the bounty was upped to fifty thousand and my 'Alive' status was bumped up to 'Dead or Alive'. But then some Dark Ones offered a chance for the Ultimate Prize, which didn't exist apparently. I landed on the planet where they led me, and was forced to fight for my life with five other guys, we had to literally bury each other alive, and I buried one before a guy with fire on his head buried me. Wild had followed me and dug me up, helped me change my face, and helped get me the StarBurst. All my money and other goodies were in the StarSkimmer that the Dark Ones had taken for themselves. I had a couple of my fake ID's on me, and lost the rest. I heard about the tournament in New City and decided that I could partake in it, the alias I was using was one I had used before as a fighter. He even had history to him. I wasn't sure what part I'd be playing in the tournament, I had to find someone in charge of it and get information on how I could get in and make my presence known to the other competitors. I passed by the arena where the tournament would be held. It looked bigger than I thought, but when you make an arena to hold 20 million beings it should be big. As I mentioned I had left some things behind on Caldera and being no dummy, I had left them somewhere where I didn't need to be myself to get them out. At least not on the outside. No fingerprint scanners, no retinal scanners, no facial recognition programs were needed to get what I needed out of lockup. I veered off the beaten path, and when I was sure no one was looking ducked into what looked like a sewer grate. I dropped down to the floor and walked along before finding the door. I stood before it and a scanner in the lock read signals from my cybernetic implants and then hummed and clicked open. I grinned to myself as I stepped into the small room where I had boxes of clothes, money, fake ID's and equipment hidden. I gathered up some clothes in a bag, took a healthy sum of money and some weapons (I had left my other weapons on the ship when I went to the Dark One's tournament), and of course some fake ID's. I exited the room and froze at the sight of the blaster stuck in my face. 'Freeze.' 'Very funny.' I said shoving the blaster out of my face and pulling the person behind it towards me. 'I missed you too.' Syn looked at me and smiled before wrapping her arms around me and kissing me. 'Where in the hell have you been? Wild said you died, but I knew better.' 'You know me babe, I'm too stubborn to die.' Syn was the only woman in the galaxy I cared about, ah hell, why mince words, I loved her. Once, a long time ago we had worked together, and when you're with someone for as long as we were, an attraction forms, and in our case it turned into love. Just because we both had cybernetic implants didn't mean we didn't have hearts. 'How'd you even know I was here?' 'When you left, I had my CPU keep a low level scan on for your operating signal so that if I came within twenty feet of you I'd be able to find you.' she said. 'I missed you.' 'I missed you too, I was going to come looking for you after I got done here.' 'You look different.' 'I had to die.' I explained as we exited the underground tunnel by another exit. I explained to her where I'd been, why I had to 'die' and why I was back on Caldera. 'The tournament huh?' she said as we emerged into the early evening. 'That's a big deal you know.' 'I know, trust me I know, twenty million beings in the arena itself, plus Caldy's gonna have it beamed out to every inhabitated planet in the galaxy. Every sleazy bar, every transport ship, everywhere there's a viewscreen. Whatever happens in that arena will be bouncing off every satellite in the galaxy.' We reached a transport and she opened it and we drove through the now dark streets. I suspected she was taking me to the apartment we shared. At least the apartment I remember us sharing from the last time I'd been here. It had been a few months. 'So if you're not Mike Savige anymore, who are you?' 'I am now known as Mac Hine.' 'You haven't used that name since your days on the underground fighting circut, right after you got your implants.' she reminded me, 'I'd almost forgot about that.' 'I heard a lot of big names are going to be at the tournament. Including Borealis.' 'I heard that too. He's the United Planetary champion you know.' 'Not for long.' I said, 'I intend to challenge him for the title and take that pretty little medallion of his for my own.' 'It might not be that easy.' Syn said as we reached the apartment building that I remembered. 'I hear Evvl wants it too.' I sighed, 'I already dealt with her once, in fact it was Borealis running interference on her the last time she and I met up that allowed me to get away with the medallion I'd aquired from her the last time we met.' 'You think she'll know that Mac Hine is the same guy she ran into before?' 'Consdiering that as far as she knew it was Case Morgan that ripped her off, I doubt it. That and I look totally different.' I said. 'There's gotta be more to it,' Syn said as we exited the elevator and entered the apartment we shared. 'There is, like I said, this things gonna be beamed out to every planet in the galaxy, bounced off every satellite. Seen by billions of lifeforms on every planet from here to the other side of the galaxy. This tournament is where mortals become immortal,' I smiled. 'And immortals become legends.'
