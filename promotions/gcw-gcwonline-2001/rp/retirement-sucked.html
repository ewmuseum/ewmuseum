Date: so I came back The arena is empty, abandoned for the night by all except those chosen; diligent few who remain after to clean up the mess. In the morning they would come, the road crew, to tear everything down, pack it all up, and move it somewhere else to make another mess. The lights, the giant screen, the floor seats and barricade. By this time tomorrow it will all be gone, never to stand erected as grand as it is now for another month. Until the next big night when titles, reputations, and careers are put on the line. Johnny walks out of the back with no swagger, no cocky grin and no taunts. He looks around at the empty seats, listening to roar of an imaginary crowd, bobbing his head to non-existant music. Walking down the ramp he takes his time, it's been awhile since he'd thought about coming back to this. Back to her. Lexicon stops with a reverent sigh before his mistress, his deviate delight. Fingers come gently to the edge of the ring, tentative and fearful, moving with care as one would a lover. 'What exactly are you doing?' 'I'm... re-introducing myself.' Johnny smiles at the man's voice, deep and bored, but without taking his eyes off the mat. 'The ring is like a woman, when you first meet, when you're young and hot, she's good to you. She makes all your dreams come true and asks for little in return, she gives you everything without thought, and without thought we take it. Until you take it for granted, you start to think that it was all you, all your hard work, you forget you wouldn't be anywhere without her, and she becomes angry. As quickly as she gives she can take it all away.' 'A ring?' 'She's not just a ring.' 'Well I know, it's a wrestling ring.' 'No, it's an ideal, it's a ticket, a filthy industry built on bloody dreams. It's my home, she's my gal.' 'You some kinda whacko?' Johnny scowls and snickers at the cleaner. 'It's a moment man, a chance for me to ponder my years of service and dedication that has built inside of me the strength of character to do it again.' 'Whoa, I was askin a question bud, didn't mean to interrupt yer moment or anything, but I got some blood to clean off o your lady friend here. But I will say this, hell may have no fury like that of a woman scorned, but heaven has no splendor as sweet as her love.' 'You married?' Johnny asks with a breif smile. The man raises a thick, hairy finger, flashing his wedding band and Lexicon realizes he's looking at the man for the first time as a person, a balding hairy fellow with a thick black mustache and kind eyes, and not simply a cleaning man in blue cover-alls with a bit of a belly. 'You?' He asks, leaning his back against the ring as Johnny does the same. 'Me? Naw, once but... no.' Crossing his arms with a shake of his head Johnny provokes a laugh. 'How long have you been with her?' 'Twenty three years.' A chest swells with pride. 'How did you make it last?' 'Bah, see women aren't like the rest of the world, they're not covered in any laws of rationality. Think of it like a fire.' The man brings his hands together as if holding a small fire. 'A fire needs air like a woman needs reassurance, it's not enough to say I love you everyday, every so often you gotta prove it.' Johnny nods a bit and half grins. 'You're a wrestler?' 'Yeah.' Lex nods a bit and tests the bottom rope. 'I've been out for a bit, it's something like a come back.' 'A grand return to the ring.' Johnny shakes his head with a scoff. 'No, that's not my style. I'm not a fame hungry rookie anymore, I'm like a lion returning to claim his savannah. I don't need to show boat and make an ass of myself to be an ass. I know what I'm doing this time around.' 'You were some kind of champ then?' 'Well, no.' Johnny reasons. 'That wasn't so much my style either, but I'm an ego maniacle ass-hole in the best shape of my life and lookin to pick a fight.' 'Well you're a good lookin lad, I'm sure you'll do alright.' 'Oh I know, I just wanted to make sure.' 'Make sure of what?' 'Make sure the ring would have me.' 'And?' Johnny smiles and turns to look at the cleaner with a shrug. 'You have kids?' 'Yeah, three boys.' He responds eagerly. 'Sixteen, twelve, and ten, haha, or as I like to say, quarter back, goalie, and pitcher.' Johnny humors the man with a chuckle. 'you?' 'Yeah a little girl, she'll be thirteen in october. I haven't seen her in awhile, she lives with her mom in Japan. I'm a better wrestler than a father, so I figure I better be the best damn wrestler now, and work on being a dad later.' 'That's, well that's.' 'Self serving and reprehensible, I know, ego maniacle ass-hole remember? We all make mistakes, just so happens I don't make any in there.' Johnny motions to the ring. 'In there I don't have any questions, no fears, no doubts, just the motions. The battle that's become the only thing I can remember. I think that's when somebody can safely say that it's become their life. If this is my life, then I'm going to make something of it.' 'Being a father and a husband is my life, my girlfriend thinks I'm a banker.' He gives Johnny a wink and a chuckle. 'Do you love your wife?' 'Of course.' 'I love my job.' Johnny looks to ring. 'If you love your job, you'll never work a day in your life.' 'Kenny Rogers said that.' Johnny replies a nod. 'And I'd have to agree.' 'What's your name anyway?' Lexicon chuckles and turns to leave. 'You'll find out soon enough.'
