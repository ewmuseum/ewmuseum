The beginnings of the Viking Monster!

9 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/9/10 1985 Trondheim, Norway. In the familiar home of the Samuelssons, there is a a bit of a disagreement going on. One that in a matter of fact will influence the future of a young Jorge Samuelsson. The fact his father had returned to be part of his sons life. The only thing standing between him and a part of his sons life was his Grandfather. Here is how it was seen from Jorge Samuelssons eyes. Sometimes birthday reunions aren't all that happy. Here is the story of Jorge Samuelsson as he grows into adulthood. The path to become a Viking Warrior is fraught with dangers that are as simple as falling down, but can be hidden as easily as ones misguided love for a family member. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Rain falls battering the cobblestone styled street where the Samuelsson family own a rather large home. Upon the door resides the family crest seemingly shining more than the rest of the doors fixture. Pride has been given the crest in its polished surfaces. Voices can be heard raised just beyond the door. As it is clearly heard that anger is replaced by clear headed conversation. The voices seem to get louder and louder. Suddenly the door opens and walks out a man dressed in Levi's bluejeans and a blue jean jacket with a red flannel shirt underneath. He turns and looks at the older man with the young 11 year old boy. "You cannot keep being this way!" He stares hoping for an answer. The young boy looks on kind of confused. He looks up at his grandfather and then back at the man he has never known. "Mister who are you?" The man looks at the old man and then to the boy. "I am your father Jorge." The young man looks on at the man and then to his grandfather. "Grandfather is this true?" The grandfather simply stares at the man with no word. Simple contempt in his eyes. The man looks at the boy and smiles. "I am Sven your father. I left for the Americas to become a Rock star. That kind of changed after i got there and became an American citizen. i now work at a factory making good money now." Jorge looks at the man strangely and then asks a simple question. "Why'd you leave Momma behind?" Jorge looks at the man curious as to what the answer was. The grandfather smiles waiting for the answer he knows will be hard. A small feminine hand is placed on Jorge's shoulder as a woman appears in the doorway. "Yes Sven please answer your sons question. Why did you leave me or should i say us behind?" Sven looks a bit off guard seeing his old flame Eula. He does not look into her eyes as he is ashamed. " I...i...Was scared, That you'd see me as a fake and a coward." The grandfather looked at Sven. You are a coward leaving them behind. Your heritage alone should have given you strength of family." He looks at his daughter and grand son with pride. Eula gently places a hand on her fathers arm. "Sven we do not hate you, We are merely disappointed in your lack of faith and waiting to see your son. The choice is now Jorge's on whether or not he wants you to be part of his life." The 11 year old boy steps forward out into the street. He places a hand upon his fathers shoulder. The strength in the young man is evident in the grip he has put upon the shoulder. "You have to earn me now Father. Look at me you have missed 11 years of me growing. While Mother and Grandfather have watched and observed my growth." A tear flows down Sven's cheek as he looks at Jorge and his kind words. Jorge looks at his father with strong and fearless eyes as he speaks his next words. "Do not disappoint me again Father for the consequences are not going to be pleasant for the future of our relationship as father and son." Sven looks at Jorge with a shocked look on his face. Wondering where a child has any authority to tell him what he can or cannot do. He decides to not say anything to Jorge, But he will put his son in his place as he knows he can and will for he is the Father. Sven shakes his jacket a bit knocking off some of the gathered rain as it begins to soak his shoulders. He looks up at the Grandfather. "Well can i stay here?" The Grandfather shakes his head as he speaks up. "No i still believe you a coward and not a man of your own word. You can stay at the Inn down the road. I will call ahead and let them know you are coming." Jorge watches as his father walks away. He eyes him not sure if he will return or not. He turns his gaze back to his Mother and Grandfather. His Mother smiles and walks into the house as his Grandfather looks at him sternly. "Jorge he will comeback, Because this day he has seen that his own son has outgrown him in manhood and he is merely a child. Let us see what he will do next shall we?" Jorge looks over his shoulder as he turns and walks up the steps into the house that Samuelsson had made. 7 years later.... Sven hit the bottle once again as he always had done on his common visits three times a year to Norway. His company had paid him well and wanted him to be the machinists liaison to the Norwegian benefactors. Sven has grown impatient with the Samuelsson family and he is on his way to meet with his son at the tavern. This meeting will be a tense one, because he had beaten his son severely four years ago and walked away. Leaving Eula and the boys Grandfather angry. The one thing that continued to haunt him was Jorge's look at him as he departed. It was a look of pure disdain and hatred. Sven slowly came to the bar and looked in. He felt kind of relieved he didn't see Jorge. He walked in and looked about and saw several people and a large man at the end of the bar with a full beard and long blond hair pulled back and braided. The large man looked over at Sven and stood up. He casually made his way to Sven and towered over him by a full foot and a half. Sven was a stout man, but this man was a monster. A Norwegian monster of a man stood before him and Sven couldn't help, but feel intimidated. Sven reached a hand out to the very large man. "Greetings friend my name is Sven!" The large man simply looked down at the hand and raised his eyes. "I am no friend of yours Sven." Sven looked up at the large man and stepped back a bit. The man raised his head and showed him his chin. He reveals a scar as Sven looks on in horror as a sudden realization comes into play. "Jorge is that you?" Jorge simply stares at the man waiting. "No Father it is your sister. Who the hell did you think it'd be after the way you left me bleeding and beaten." Sven quickly looked at all the possible exits and ways out of here if things got messy. He turns back to Jorge who simply stood straight and looked down at the man. "You seem to have forgotten something Sven." Sven looked at him and growled a tone at Jorge as he spoke. "You will not call me Sven boy i am your father and you will respect it or i will.." Sven was cut off as Jorge looked at him with a cold hatred. " Or you will what Sven? Teach me a lesson and beat it into me?" Sven backed up a couple more steps this time grabbing a bar stool. Noticing the bar stool Jorge quickly stepped in and attacked. He grabbed the swinging bar stool with his right hand, as he grabbed Sven by the throat with his left. Sven was hoisted up off the floor with ease. Jorge jerked the bar stool from Sven's hand and slung Sven across the room into the stone wall. Jorge stalked over slowly as Sven slowly attempted to get back to his feet. Jorge swung huge lefts and rights, literally staggering Sven side to side, from each massive blow. Sven fell forward down onto his knees and chest not moving. Busted open Jorge looked down at Sven his eyes filled with hatred and disdain. "You didn't have to worry about me or my mother. You should have stayed away this time. I was going to destroy you, but Grandfather asked that i simply teach you the lesson you tried to teach me. Simply put you were too weak to ever be considered a Samuelsson. Now go father and know forever more that your son beat you with ease and little to no effort." Jorge turns towards the barkeep and hands him some money and walks out the door not even looking back. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Jorge had turned eighteen years of age and sought revenge on his own Father. This was just the beginning of many things that were to come in his life. In fact Jorge did owe one thing to his Father. His Father had beaten into him his destined path. Follow thy own destiny for it may lead you to your own demise or into the hands of some monster. This poem is for you. From the Eggjum grave stone, Sogn, Noway ca. 700 C.E.: Hverr of kom Heráss á hí á land gotna? Fiskr ór fjanda vim svimandi, fogl á fjanda lið galandi. (As whom came War-god hither to the land of men? A fish from the torrent of enemies swimming, A bird against troop of enemies screaming.) Sleep well children.
