Schr’dinger's Cat: Chapter 6

3 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 3, 2008 It was a brand new day in the same old world that she lived in. The sun shone through a corner of the blinds as an annoying reminder that she had to get up. The sheets seemed to have stuck to her body with really strong glue as she had all sorts of trouble getting out of bed. She stood up and her body glistened with the sweat of a difficult night. The tossing and turning had begun once again after years of their disappearance. She knew that the stress of the situation was getting in her nerves. The door to the bathroom was already open as she walked inside and turned on the faucet. The water felt good on her face. It worked well in waking her from her daze. Justine grabbed a robe after drying her face with a towel and walked towards one of the three doors in her room. Living a double life was one of the most horrific experiences of her life. For years she had put up with a man she despised. Worse yet, she had put her son in danger every time the two were in the same room. She had learned to compartmentalize her fears so as to not show them at all. It was hard work. When she walked into her son’s room, the surprise of seeing Burton standing over the sleeping boy felt like a punch to the stomach. After what had happened with Antonio, she felt more apprehensive about their position in Burton’s house. The fear that Burton would find out what happened was like a rain cloud threatening to ruin the day with torrential waters. “Ah, so the sleeping beauty finally awakens,” he said in a rough morning voice as he gently caressed the little boy’s hair. “I missed you last night.” Justine felt just a little bit of bile rise up to her throat but she tried hard not to show her revulsion. As much as she wanted to get out of that place, she knew that her life and more importantly, her son’s life were in play and she would not gamble with them. Like a professional poker player, she smiled and moved slowly towards Burton. “I’m sorry. I was just feeling too tired,” she said as she started massaging and kissing Burton’s neck. His arm fell to his side and with a smirk on his face; Burton slowly moved it up her leg. Her body instantly reacted when he reached the place he was looking for. He felt her muscles tense up completely for a couple of seconds before they relaxed once more. “You seem so tense,” he said as he turned around to face her. Burton had not gotten to where he was by only being the most ruthless. He was a very good observer and he saw in her eyes that something was wrong. There was a worry there that he had not seen in her eyes in a long time. She tried to smile and shake it off. “Stress. Everything that’s happening seems to be falling hard on my shoulders. I just wish it would all end,” she said truthfully. “Don’t worry, it will be over soon. Once we rid this world of Antonio, there will be nothing else for us to do but enjoy this life,” he said as he grabbed her hand and kissed it. He smiled at her and tried to reassure her. There was something in her eyes that felt out of place. She slowly squirmed her hand out of Burton’s grip and moved back towards her room. “I’m going to get dressed.” “Going somewhere?” She turned around and smiled. “I thought I’d take a walk since it’s a beautiful day. Maybe Milo and I will go to the park or something. I just feel so suffocated in here all the time.” She grabbed some clothes from the walk-in closet and started to get dressed. She was hoping her excuse would be good enough for him to believe. “That’s good. That will help you relax a little bit. I was thinking though, I’m going to take Milo with me today. Since you are so stressed out, some time for yourself would be good,” he said with a strange smirk on his face. She stopped dressing herself as she heard what Burton had said. “Er” Sure? why not?” Justine finished dressing herself quickly and went over to Milo’s bed without looking at Burton. There were many thoughts running through her head and she hoped that Burton could not see any of them. “Milo, wake up,” she said softly close to his ear. He had always been a heavy sleeper but as soon as he heard her voice close, he stirred. Milo’s eyes opened slowly and he held out his hands to his mother. She hugged him before standing up. “Good morning, Mommy,” he said as he tried to take the sleep away from his eyes with his hands. Milo sat down on his bed. “Honey, you and Daddy are going to spend the day together. I have to go now. I will miss you a lot,” she said as she hugged him again. She wiped her moist eyes away with her hand so none of them saw it. “I love you, Mommy.” “I love you too,” she said before she disappeared out of the room. Burton put his hand on Milo’s shoulder and gave him a big smile. “We are going to have fun today. Get dressed. We’ll have some breakfast and then we’ll go have some fun.” Milo did as told and went to a stack of clothes that had been neatly folded there for him on top of a chair. Meanwhile, Burton took out his walkie-talkie from his pocket and turned it on. “How does it look out there?” “Everything looks good,” the voice on the other end answered. Burton moved out of Milo’s earshot. “Justine will be passing your way soon. I want you to follow her.” “Sure thing, boss.” --- The Night Before... “Are you here to end it” To finally finish me off?” Antonio tried to get up but was unable to because of the gunshot to his shoulder. He had never felt so defenseless in his entire life. He was expecting it any moment now. It would be soon before he would experience the particular sound of the gunshot, the peculiar smell of gunpowder in the air and the sudden and terrible pain. The man walked into the room and looked around methodically. He made sure that everything was in order. He reached into his pocket. Antonio braced himself. He had gone through so much in his life that death didn’t seem so bad. Plus, he might be able to see her again. “Here you go. You have to keep hydrated,” he said as he took out a bottle of water from his pocket and after opening it, put it on top of the night table at the side of the bed. Antonio looked at him questioningly. “What’s going on” Why didn’t you kill me? I thought Sheila had betrayed me.” “She did betray you. I knew the Antonio from this dimension thanks to Justine. We have been friends for a very long time. When I heard what Sheila was planning to do, I decided to tell Justine,” he explained to Antonio. Because of the efforts of Justine and Anibal, he was still alive and breathing. He couldn’t help but feel grateful that they had given him the chance of meeting his son for the first time and for seeing Justine after death had separated them. Antonio took a sip from the water. “Thanks.” She was almost breathless when she arrived at the house. Even though the streets of San Juan were almost deserted, she had to make sure not to be seen by anyone or her life and her son’s life would be at risk. She walked over to where she heard the voices. “So, how are we going to proceed with this?” “The whole idea is to really put Sheila and Burton at odds. That way they will take themselves out and leave us to do what we want. The important thing is to take Justine and Milo away for safety.” “But weren’t the both of them already at odds?” “There is no love lost between Sheila and Burton, but they have been working together for a while now. We have to destroy that trust between them and have them duke it out in the streets with firepower.” Justine entered the room where Anibal and Antonio were discussing the problem at hand. She ran towards a surprised Antonio and kissed him passionately. The feeling of Burton’s touch completely disappeared as she fell deeper into Antonio’s embrace. Antonio wasn’t sure if the tears streaming down her face were of joy or what, but he was glad to be holding her in his arms. When she finally let go, she hugged Anibal as well and sat down on the other side of the bed. “We need to move faster. I think Burton suspects something. He took Milo with him today somewhere. I fear what he might be planning if he found out that I helped you escape,” she said before starting to sob quietly. The thought of losing the light of her life was too much for her. Her son was the only thing that had kept her going when she thought she could live no more. “I will have to go and Sheila and plant the seed we need. It won’t be that hard. I will just have to steal some of the stuff from the supply stash and claim Burton’s men did it. You need to lay low for a while, Justine,” Anibal told her with concern in his voice. --- Meanwhile? “Come on, boss.” “Yeah, what’s up?” Burton’s man put the device closer to his mouth and delivered the news. “So she is in some house” With who?” “I don’t know, sir. But I’m pretty sure I heard a male voice.” “Find out who it is in there. Now.” The man could feel a stab of pain when he heard the order. The tone of voice had turned into a cold and slightly murderous pitch. He had heard it before, and he knew that if he didn’t find out who it was, he would die. He put the device in his pocket, breathed deeply and went off to find out who it was. Burton pocketed the device as well and walked towards were his bodyguards and Milo stood. His murderous look was quickly transformed into a smile. “I just spoke to your mom! She says she loves you and that she is fine. She also said to do everything I ask you to do,” Burton told the little boy. Milo just smiled at the mention of his mother and nodded. “Now, you see that pig squealing in Ramon’s hands?” Burton pointed to where one of his men held a medium sized pig. Milo nodded excitedly. “Well, I want you to take this and do exactly as I tell you.” He handed him what looked like a small toy gun. “Now, get very close to it and aim directly at the head. Don’t worry, it won’t bite.” Milo held tightly to the gun in his hands and moved towards the pig. He hesitated a little when he was close as he could hear the human-like squeals of the pig. Burton gently pushed him closer to it. “You can do it, son,” Burton whispered in Milo’s ear. Milo began to pull on the trigger of the gun. It offered almost no resistance. The gun fired and the bullet hit the pig on the head, killing it instantly. Burton applauded. “Very well done! You did very well, Milo. I am very proud of you. You mom will be as well when she hears about it.” Burton moved towards the pig and put his hand on the bullet wound. His hand was quickly drenched in blood. “Now, you killed a living creature that we will use this night as our meal, but you must have respect for the hunted. Put this on your forehead and cheeks.” Burton softly grabbed Milo’s hand and put some blood in it. He then instructed Milo how to do it. “You must always be the hunter and never become the hunted. I hope you take that with you today.”
