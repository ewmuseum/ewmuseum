Bouncin' Back (Part 3)

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/12/2005 [The camera slowly fades into the interior passenger bay of the long, black, stretch limo that was sent to pick up Vince Jones and the lovely Jasmine earlier on. The Violence is seen sitting there with a nasty scowl on his face as he takes a sip of some champagne from his glass. Jasmine is seen glancing out the window at the city streets as the limo speeds by. She then glances at Vince Jones who sits there motionless like a statue taking an occasional sip here and there from his glass.] Jasmine: V? [He takes a quick sip from his glass and slowly turns towards her direction.] Vince Jones: What? [She pauses and takes a deep breath.] Jasmine: I don’t think I’ve ever asked you this before and all’but, do you ever get nervous when you’re starting over again? [Vince looks at her in confusion.] Vince Jones: Startin? ova again? What you talkin? “bout, girl” Jasmine: You know what I’m talking about, this whole transition phase from Lights Out to Global Championship Wrestling. Don’t you ever get just a lil bit nervous knowing that you’ve gotta start all over again in a new environment? [Vince sits there in momentary silence and finally just shakes his head.] Vince Jones: No. Jasmine: Not even a little bit, V? Vince Jones: No, not at all. [He lifts his glass and takes a long sip of his champagne while an awkward silence passes between the two of them.] Jasmine: So’V, why’d you run away? [Vince stops in mid-gulp and slowly sets the glass of champagne down at his side.] Jasmine: Why’d you run off after that match against Donny Diamond at UnEarthed back in LOW? [The tension can slowly be seen building in Vince Jones as he clenches his fist in anger.] Vince Jones: V didn’t run anywhere! [Jasmine leans over and rests her head against Vince’s shoulder and looks up directly into his eyes. She begins rubbing his chest affectionately.] Jasmine: Its okay. You don’t have to lie to me. Just tell me the truth. That’s all I wanna hear, the truth. [Vince shrugs her head off his shoulder and shoves her to the side.] Vince Jones: There ain’t nothin? to say! Aiight?” Nothin?! V had some issues he had to take care of and that was that, end of story! [Jasmine shakes her head and rolls her eyes at Vince.] Jasmine: What do you mean end of story, V? I want some answers! I wanna know why! Why couldn’t I get in contact with you?” Where were you?” Why didn’t I see you until about a week ago when you got signed to GCW?” Why didn’t Reno hear anything from you? [Vince just turns his back to her and tries to tune her out.] Vince Jones: The Violence don’t wanna hear it! It’s been done! Just drop this sh*t! [She places a hand on his shoulder and tries to get his attention.] Jasmine: Listen to me, V! Listen! I was worried about you! Reno was worried about you! We tried to call you for days and you weren’t returning our calls! When LOW closed down who do you think was the first one to get in touch with the right people to get you set up and going again in GCW? It was Reno. Who do you think was sitting on that couch by the phone waiting for it to ring each night? Who was the one standing by that door watching and waiting for it to swing open again? It was me. Vince Jones: Yeah, yeah, yeah! Enough with that sh*t! V tired of hearin? it! You two need to step up off V’s business! [Jasmine leans back and folds her arms across her chest.] Jasmine: V, we ask because we care about you. That’s why? [Vince chuckles to himself and just shakes his head.] Vince Jones: You care. Well, ain’t that sweet. You and Reno care about V. Jones. You two oughta put that sh*t on a Hallmark card or somethin?. But ya know, sometimes V gotta wonda. Sometimes he gotta wonda “bout you and him. You two really care “bout V or is it love for the money V makin?” Jasmine: Look, its not about the money, V Vince Jones: Then what’s all this sh*t about huh? Why Reno got you trackin? V. Jones down? Why Reno wanna get in touch with V so bad? Jasmine: V, this meeting with Reno is for your benefit. He thinks that he could put you back on top. I let him fill me in on his ideas and I kind of felt he might have been on to something. [She pauses and looks over at Vince Jones and awaits a replay or feedback of any kind; but he just sits there motionless mumbling profanities under his breath.] Jasmine: Hear me out on this, V. Reno Banks is a business man. They don’t call him the Agent of the Stars for nothing, right? I really believe he might know what’s best for you. He can turn things around and push you in the right direction; but, he like myself, wanna know what was going on with you when you suddenly decided to vanish on us. [Vince Jones just shakes his head in disgust and looks at her in fury.] Vince Jones: The Violence would’ve thought that if anyone was on the same page with him it would’ve been you. We go way back. We go way back even further than all this wrestlin? sh*t, the money, and the fame in this game! You should know V more than anyone here. After all this talk it startin? to look like you don’t even know where the hell Vince Jones comin? from nowadays. Ya know somethin?” You sound more and more like the rest of these cats in this game that think they know what’s best for The Violence and think they know about the life V lived when you know jack sh*t! If V. Jones don’t wanna talk “bout his business V don’t have to! [Jasmine glares at Vince in anger.] Jasmine: How can I know where you’re coming from when you never tell me anything? I ask you questions. I try to find things out. I try as hard as I can to figure you out, but you give me nothing. Are you hiding something? What are you hiding anyways, huh? I just don’t understand. I just don’t understand you at times. I’m on your side. I’m always on your side. We’re a team. You know that’s fact. Remember the first day we met, V? [Vince Jones turns away from her and looks out the window of the limo.] Vince Jones: Enough about V’s business! It ain’t important! And when the hell we gonna finally be at his offices? [Jasmine sighs as she leans back against the seat.] Jasmine: Yes, I understand, V. Well, be patient. I’m sure the ride won’t take too much longer. Vince Jones: Yeah? Well, you betta be right! V. Jones hates long waits! [Vince Jones turns and looks out the window at the bright lights of the clubs and streetlights that line the downtown area as the thoughts begin to rush through his mind.] “Whateva happened to the girl V once knew” This ain’t the same girl he met all those years ago. She all about the money and the fame V got now. Back in the day this chick was the one that was never impressed by status and all that crap. She was never down for that sh*t. She knew Vince Jones and was down for him and him only. Damn! She askin? if V can remember the first time we met! How the hell could he forget somethin? like that?' [The camera slowly fades out and fades back in and we are inside a small grocery store of some type. The camera pans around the store and a few shoppers here and there can be seen walking up and down the aisles looking at various produce and grocery items. The camera continues to pan around the small grocery store and catches sight of a young version of Vince Jones who is bagging up groceries for a young black woman with long, dark brown hair. He hastily bags up the items and hands them over to the woman and flashes a quick smile as she smiles back at him. She takes her groceries, walks away, and exits the store. As she exits Vince Jones snarls in disgust and rolls his eyes. All of a sudden some laughter can be heard nearby and Vince Jones looks up at the source of it in anger.] Vince Jones: What the hell you laughin? about, huh?”” Shut the hell up, K.J! [The camera turns and catches sight of a boy that appears as though he may be of some relation to Vince Jones. He tries his best to regain his composure and finally manages to put on a straight face once again.] K.J: Man, I can’t help it. That smile was classic, V. How many more of these broads you gonna smile at today, huh? Man, you lookin? like a punk b*tch over there, big bro. [Vince Jones shakes his head and glares at his younger brother.] Vince Jones: You know somethin?, K.J? Sometimes you gotta play the game to get by. [K.J hears this comment and bursts with laughter.] K.J: Well, you sure playin? the part damn good, kid. I gotta give you props on the performance. You had me really believin? that sh*t for a second there! [Vince Jones slams his fist on the counter and glares at K.J.] Vince Jones: Okay! That’s enough with this sh*t! Damn! Keep it up and I’m gonna whup your ass when we get home! Got it!” [They cease their conversation as the manager of the store comes walking over with an expression of disappointment across his face.] The manager: Boys, you two really need to tone it down in here. Could you two do that for me, please? [Vince and K.J both roll their eyes at the manager and nod in agreement.] Vince Jones: Yeah, whateva man. [The manager nods in approval.] The manager: Thank you. Now you two can get back to work now. [The manager turns and walks away as K.J begins to burst with laughter once again.] K.J: Who the hell do he think he is, huh? How the hell he think he gonna tell Keshawn Jones what to do and get away with it? Man, I oughta beat his ass one of these days for some sh*t like that! [Vince Jones shakes his head and laughs to himself about his younger brother’s na’ve mind.] Vince Jones: That so? Well, I hope you ain’t plannin? on doin? that while he still writin? our checks, kid. That’d be some dumb sh*t right there, Keshawn. K.J: Yeah. You’re right. Damnit! I just wanna shove my foot up his ass! You know what the deal is, V? [Vince Jones nods in agreement.] Vince Jones: Yeah. I know what ya mean; but sooner or later I’m out of this joint and I ain’t neva comin? back. K.J: Yeah. Whateva man. You ain’t goin? nowhere. You need this job. We need these jobs. How the hell we gonna help moms out, huh? Just face it, V. We gonna be tag teamin? on these groceries for foreva and a day. Betta get used to it now. Vince Jones: See, you don’t know what I know. It’s like this. You know a guy named K-9? [K.J nods in agreement.] K.J: Ain’t he one of those EDW cats that been tearin? some sh*t up around here? Man, I hear them cats be runnin? things around here. They makin? waves. Everyone been talkin? “bout them. [Vince Jones nods in agreement.] Vince Jones: Yeah. I know “bout all that; but he ain’t just any EDW cat. He Mr. EDW himself. Man, I got into another fight with some of his people and then he shows up out of nowhere” K.J: ? and you got that ass of yours beat, V? [K.J laughs out loud at the thought of his brother taking a beating.] Vince Jones: Shut the hell up! He caught me off guard! Okay!” Well, he had a quick talk with me and said he was lookin? to expand his clique a little. He told me to meet up with him and I might get a shot at runnin? with the pack, kid. Yo! I took that spot like it was nothin? and things are gonna be lookin? up for us quick. I can leave this damn job and get some real money now. [K.J grins at the good news.] K.J: Now that’s what I’m tryin? to hear. My bro “bout to get paid up in here!! He up in the EDW! That sh*t is tight! [Vince Jones puts a finger to his lips and tries to get his brother to quiet up a bit.] Vince Jones: Shhhhhhhhh’what the hell you tryin? to do, huh? You tryin? to broadcast this sh*t or somethin?” I gotta keep this under wraps as much as possible, got it? Don’t tell anyone else! [All of a sudden the voice of a young girl can be heard.] Girl: Hey stranger! So, you’re in the EDW? Sounds kinda interesting. You must be a tough guy or something. [Keshawn and Vince Jones turn around in surprise as they spot a pretty girl standing at the edge of the counter with a bright, brown eyes, and long brown hair staring back that looks somewhat familiar to us, but from where?] Vince Jones: You didn’t hear sh*t, kid! Got it!” [The girl shakes her head and laughs to herself.] Girl: Oh, I heard everything. And my name’s not kid. Get that? [Vince Jones looks on at her in surprise and then in impatience.] Vince Jones: [looks at her suspiciously] Well, while you’re runnin ? off at the mouth about stuff that don’t even concern you what the hell do you want? [The young girl grins at Vince Jones nervously.] The Girl: Uh?.I?.just wanted to say hi. That’s all. [Vince and K.J look at her in confusion.] Vince Jones: So, what’s your name, huh? [All of a sudden the front door of the grocery story swings open and tall, black man, comes walking in through the door with an impatient expression on his face as he turns and looks at the young girl standing in front of the counter talking to Vince and K.J.] The man: Girl, quit flirtin? with those guys and let’s go. I’ve been waiting twenty minutes for you to get out this damn store! You’re burnin? up all my damn gas! Now come on! [The young girl turns around as the man walks over and snatches her by the arm. She turns and looks at Vince Jones and smiles.] The Girl: Uh, I gotta go, but I’m sure I’ll see you around again sometimes. My name’s Jasmine. Bye. [Vince Jones waves goodbye as the young, black girl and the older man who might be her father walk away and leave the store. He continues to scold her as they leave and walk on down the street. Vince Jones watches the girl and the man in curiosity and K.J waves his hand in front of his brother’s face and begins laughing to himself.] K.J: Come on, V. Snap out of it. [K.J snaps his fingers in front of Vince Jones eyes and then he hauls off and smacks Vince in the back of the head instantly getting his attention.] Vince Jones: Yo! K.J: Looks like somebody in love. [Vince rolls his eyes at K.J and shakes his head.] Vince Jones: In love? Hell no! That’s for punks! Man, I thought I knew her from somewhere. [K.J laughs and just shakes his head.] K.J: Yeah, whateva man. Vince Jones: Hey. It’s the truth, kid. [Vince is about to turn around and get back to his work and then he stops and turns back to Keshawn in anger. He slaps him hard in the back of the head and K.J whips his head around in surprise and holds it in slight pain.] K.J: OWWWW!!! What the hell was that for? Vince Jones: You know damn well what that was for, b*tch! Don’t you ever slap the sh*t out the back of my head again! Got it!” [Vince Jones turns and goes back to his work as K.J nods in agreement.] K.J: Okay. I’m sorry. [K.J turns and walks away and goes back to his work in the store as the camera slowly fades to black.] To Be Continued...
