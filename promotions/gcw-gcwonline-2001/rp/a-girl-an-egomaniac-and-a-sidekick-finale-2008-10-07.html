It’d be just me and you.” Chris could not help but to roll his eyes at her comments. Her naivety only added to her charm. Then again, it only made his next message all the more important, “Well I meant what I said. I love you Tessa.” Cue what was probably about the 100th awkward silent moment between the two. “Oh?” Totally not the response Chris was hoping for. But it would do. For once it was Chris who was shying away, “Look, we’ll talk about it tonight. Okay?” She nodded her head, continuing to try and digest what had just occurred. Faster than frazzled cat, Chris cued the engine of his Coup Deville and began to pull out of the driveway. Just then a thought FINALLY crossed Tessa’s mind. She had to scream o ensure that Chris heard it. “WAIT CHRIS!” Looking like a maniac she chased his car down the driveway, flailing her arms all over the place. Thankfully he stopped. His driver side window rolled down, “Yeah?” “I love you too.” And then came all the traditional love story stuff. The big romantic kiss. The many nights spent together in bliss. The proposal. And hell, even a kid. But there’s no such thing as a happy ending in the real world, is there? That is a question that still has yet to be answered ;)
