Broken

18 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/2005 There was no escaping it. The bitter taste of copper stained her mouth almost like a token of her defeat at the hands of the man she had lost her heart to. The smell of her own blood seemed to flood her senses as it continued to run freely despite the medical team's best efforts to stop it. Still, through it all she just sat there on that hard cushioned examining table staring numbly into nothing. Pale blue eyes looked glazed and far away as hands kept working fervently to patch her up. ' Eva, we don't have the facilities to do what needs to be done for the gash on your forehead. It needs stitches.' Almost mechanically her head rolled to look at the head of the medical staff. For a moment she said nothing merely staring through him. ' How bad?' ' He really opened you up.' ' I see.' A cotton swab was passed over her head into his waiting hands. Words were spoken but lost to her as her mind blurred distorting reality from fantasy. ' This is coagulant, Eva. I need to shove this up your nose to stop the bleeding long enough to get you to the hospital for xrays. It is going to hurt. I won't lie.' Eva merely stared at him again. Pain? He must not realize who he was talking too. She had been dealt enough pain, both physical and mental to last most people a few years..and hers had come in a matter of only a couple of weeks. ' Just do it.' Looking over her head at his assistants he nodded. Immediately her arms were seized and held down as another set of hands clamped down onto her head forcing it to remain still as the medical officer set to the task. As soon as she felt the swab shoved hard up her left nostril a shrill scream shattered the room. Her body immediately tensed up and began to struggle wildly against the staying hands as he pinched her injured nose over the swab and pulled it out. Within a breath another swab was brought forth and shoved up the right one igniting an even harder struggle from the diva. It was over in a matter of seconds, but as soon as her hands were released a fist connected solidly with jaw of one of the men who had held her down. ' Eva calm down. It is over!' She wasn't listening, she was beyond listening at this point. The only thing she could do was react, and react she did. She was reacting to the weeks of not knowing, the doubt, the physical torture she had been through and the mental and emotional stress that had finally just caused her mind to fracture for that moment as she attack those people who were actually trying to help her. The struggle between the injured lioness and the medical team would last for a few moments as her adrenalin pumped body was hard pressed to go down by any means. Through it all she didn't even feel the slight pinch of a needle entering her thigh. All she knew was the sting of her own blood dribbling in her eyes from the gash on her forehead and the rank pain of her nose that fueled that boiled rage released on these innocent people. Then...she knew nothing. Her world began to spin as eyes crossed slightly. Limbs weakened, joints turned to jelly and before she could conclude they had shot her up with a tranquilizer she was slumping towards the floor. She was unaware of the arms that caught her keeping her from the fall. She was unaware of being placed on a gurney and rushed through the halls to the back entrance. Eva did not witness the flash of cameras as reporters managed to press past security in hopes of getting a good story. There was simply nothing but the same numbing blackness she had known while she was awake. -- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Slowly those lids would flutter open. Icy blue's would have trouble focusing as the blinding track lighting above had her wincing and turning her head to the side in hopes to keep them from causing more stinging pain to an already troubled head. Vision blurred she could only barely make out the shape of a rather large humanoid being. It took several blinks to work the moisture back into those eyes well enough to finally focus on the face of who it was at her side. ' Johnny..' She breathed groggily. Johnny Lexicon, not looking all that great as far as shape went at this time, merely half smiled at her scooting his chair closer to the bed. Standing over her now he frowned smoothing away her platinum bangs to look at the stitches there. ' I'm here doll.' His words must have done something to her. They seemed to be the final crack in her built up walls as that dainty chin trembled, body quaked and she quietly began to weep and blubber. ' Why Johnny? Why me? What did I do to him that was so horrible that he would do this to me? Haven't I paid enough for what I did to you...to anyone? ' They were the words of a seemingly broken woman. Lex said nothing merely stroking her brow softly in repetitive motion as if to sooth her troubled mind. ' I loved him Johnny. Did you know that? I actually loved him. I wanted so much and...and..' She could say no more. Not able to stand seeing her in this much pain Johnny actually moved to sit on the side of her bed gathering her up in his arms to hold her close as she quietly sobbed mourning her losses. She had lost her heart. She had lost her man, but most of all it felt as if she had lost her faith in herself. ' Shh. Don't do this to yourself babe. He isn't worth your tears.' Sniffling heavily she drew a ragged breath and pulled away from him slowly shaking her head as she looked down at her trembling hands. ' I was a fool to think he felt anything for me. Never again Johnny. You hear me? Never again am I going to let a man get near me like that. I let it happen it twice and both times....I lost.' He merely nodded and kissed her brow before gathering her up again. Unknown to her, he was now only half listening, it was as if he had his own haunted thoughts. Together they sat on that uncomfortable hospital bed for what must have been an hour before he finally withdrew. ' I have to go doll. I need to take care of some things.' Eva nodded. ' Thank you Johnny. You are a true friend.' He nodded not meeting her eyes as he got up and walked to the door. Once there he turned to look back at her one last time. ' Take care of yourself babe. Never know who is out to get you.' Confusion became obvious on her face as she nodded. ' Johnny, be careful. I don't want anything happening to you. You are the only real friend I have.' Then...he was gone. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- She was staring into the mirror and what she saw staring back at her was enough to nearly drive her into a rage. Tapered fingers gently brushed aside her hair as she looked upon the nine stitches with contrite anger. Lestatt had done this to her, and Johnny Borealis had helped in the end. A rush of air left her lungs as she whipped around and stalked out of her bathroom heading into the living room once again. Those eyes stared down at her unsigned contract to wrestle against Seymour Almasy. She was not one hundred percent and the powers that be saw fit to pit her against the one man who has been unstoppable since he debut in GCW. It almost felt like a conspiracy, yet deep down she knew better. Sighing heavily she sat down on her couch and lifted a pen from the table as she dragged the contract towards her. Idly that pen thumped against knee as she considered whether or not to take the match. The recent events that have taken place during her matches had Eva seriously considering not signing it. Pursing her lips she flipped through the pages wondering who else had been placed on the card for the upcoming show. ' Taylor Made, Stockbrokers, Ehren Moore, Jayce Bradley...lots of new names on this card..hmm...' She almost stopped scanning the card when suddenly a name seemed to jump out at her..two in fact. ' Well I'll be god damned...' Johnny Borealis versus The Marauder. The decision was made. Immediately she went back to the first page and signed her name away. Seymour would have his match...and Eva...well Eva would have the beginning of her retributions. Johnny Borealis wouldn't know what hit him.
