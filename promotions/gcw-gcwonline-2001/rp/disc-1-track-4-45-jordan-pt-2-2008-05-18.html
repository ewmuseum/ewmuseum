Disc 1: Track 4: .45 (Jordan Pt. 2)

18 May 2008

Date: May 18 2008 ~x~ Send away for a priceless gift One not subtle, one not on the list Send away for a perfect world One not simply, so absurd In these times of doing what you're told Keep these feelings, no one knows What ever happened to the young man's heart Swallowed by pain, as he slowly fell apart ~x~ Recent Past A startled and stationary; Keegan stares into Jacque’s eyes undecided if what he saw was reality. What he saw however was the gaze of a man in epiphany; and that man was carrying his child. “Jacque?” Keegan managed to mumble. “What are you doing here?” “I thought that Jordan should know his father.” From the moment Keegan opened his apartment door to find Jacque and Jordan standing in the hallway he felt like pinching himself, but by now the reality was finally setting in. Still too befuddled to comment Keegan backed into the apartment giving Jacque room to move with the car seat. The native Canadian carries the car seat over to the couch while Keegan shuts the door behind him. “Do you want something to drink?” “A water please.” Jacque replies, glancing up. Keegan heads for the refrigerator grabbing two bottles of water, by the time he returns Jacque has gotten Jordan unbuckled. His head leans against the side of the car seat with the strap from his corduroy overalls under his little chin. With his eyes still on the little guy he hands Jacque his water, then takes a seat in the chair opposite. “I’m a little surprised to see you here.” Keegan comments, once both men have sampled their respective waters. “I’m a little surprised to be here myself.” Jacque responds, “if someone had told me a month ago that I’d be here with Jordan I would have thought them crazy.” “Yeah.” Keegan adds, still fixated on the sleeping boy. “He’s a year old now.” Jacque states. “Why now?” Keegan finally asks, the question that had been burning him from the moment he saw them. “After a year, why now have you suddenly changed your mind?” Jacque glances away, the Canadian doesn’t display shame well as anyone who’s ever crossed his path already knows, and even rarer still does he admit when he was wrong. Despite that, genuine regret surrounds him as he replies, “I had to make right what I did wrong.” Jordan begins to stretch, his two tiny hands curled up beside his cheeks, slowly his little lips curl downward and he utters a whimper. The soft cry draws both men’s attention, immediately Jacque reaches down at his side but doesn’t find a diaper bag. Keegan notices concern crossing Jacque’s face and inquires. “What’s wrong?” “I forgot his bag.” Jacque replies looking back up. “Will you hold on a minute, while I go get his bottle?” “Huh?” Keegan asks, feeling a little dumb-founded. “His bottle.” Jacque responds rising from the couch. “It’s in the diaper-bag, and I forgot it in the Escalade downstairs.” Jacque leans over picking Jordan up, “he’s probably hungry, will you hold him while I go get the bottle?” “O-Okay.” Jacque hands the child to Keegan and hastily exits the apartment. Keegan glances down at his boy and he can’t help but smile. He was so small and helpless, so fragile and so innocent. Was Keegan really a man capable of caring for him? ~x~ And I'm staring down the barrel of a 45, I'm swimming through the ashes of another life There's no real reason to accept the way things have changed Staring down the barrel of a 45 Send a message to the unborn child Keep your eyes open for a while In a box high up on the shelf, left for you, no one else There's a piece of a puzzle known as life Wrapped in guilt, sealed up tight ~x~ January 16th, 2004 He kept his hands in the pocket of his green hoodie, with his right hand resting across the .45 caliber handgun. His head lay tucked against his chest, but his eyes darted across the dirty streets of the California ghetto. Just ahead he could see the black Monte Carlo, with the large Confederate flag decal resting in the rear window. His prey was near. Keegan made his way down the cracked sidewalk to the driveway where the car was parked; he could hear loud music coming from inside the house. He made his way up the paved driveway, and turned into the short path to the door. His heart was racing, and his hands were sweaty as he stood at the front door. Keegan rang the doorbell, and the man that answered looked the part with his greasy black hair hung over his shoulders. In one swift motion, he pulled the .45 caliber out of his hoodie pocket and swung the butt of the gun into the side of the man’s head. He stumbled back, and Keegan shot through the door, slamming it behind him. Despite the man’s seven-foot frame he was not the intimidator this time around, instead he stared shockingly at the brash teenager before him. “What the fuck are you doing in my house?” He demanded with false bravado. Like the blood running through his veins Keegan’s ice-cold stare bored through the man. “I might have asked you the same thing earlier today.” “What do you mean?” He asks, slowly stepping back. The resemblance hits him, but he feigns ignorance. Keegan glances around, noticing what a shit-hole this guy lives in. There is trash all over the place, with numerous photographs of naked girls, none of which can be older then Carson had been. “You know what I mean...” He says, as he locks his gaze on the man again. “Now tell me your name.” The man chuckles, feeding the rage that builds within Keegan. “Who do you think you are, coming into my house, and demanding I tell you my name... who the fuck do you think you are?” Keegan plunges his hand into his back pocket producing his wallet. Quickly he sifts through it and yanks out Carson’s most recent school photo, tossing it at the man. He glances down to look at it, and then returns his gaze to Keegan, now scared more than previous. “My name is Keegan O’Conner, and that was my baby sister you raped and killed today...” He chokes out, trying to maintain his composure. “Now, tell me your name!” “Its Roy...” he responds, reaching behind his back. “My name is Roy, and what can I do for you?” Keegan crosses the distance between them, getting right into Roy’s face. “I want to know why you did it...” Roy smiles, and instead of replying pulls a six inch hunting knife from out of his back pocket plunging the knife into Keegan’s stomach. Keegan grimaces in pain, and swings the butt of the gun at Roy again, but Roy ducks out of the way and yanks the knife out of him, trying to plunge it in again. Keegan doesn’t think, he points and fires... the next thing he knows Roy is laying in a pool of his own blood, and Keegan is bleeding from his stomach. “I...” but Keegan can’t say anything else. He backs toward the door, fondles for the handle and backs out of the house leaving the door wide open. He’s killed a man, and though he deserved it he can’t shake the disgusted feeling in his stomach. He doesn’t think he’ll ever be the same... ~x~ What ever happened to the young man's heart Swallowed by pain, as he slowly fell apart ~x~ Recent Past Carson had been this little once, but back then Keegan wasn’t responsible enough to hold her. Frankly, he wasn’t certain if things had changed much since then. Despite his initial awkwardness the one year old laid curled up in Keegan’s arms once again asleep when Jacque finally returned to the living room. Regardless of their history with one another, it warmed Jacque’s heart to see the slumbering child with his real father. “It’s hard not to fall in love with them.” Jacque comments. Keegan glances back and nods. “I remember my sister being this little once.” It’s Jacque’s turn to nod, as he returns to the couch placing the diaper bag beside his feet. “Some of the best stuff is still ahead.” He takes the bottle out of the side pocket and places it beside Keegan’s feet. “His first word was “ba-ba”.” Jacque explains, “but he hasn’t started walking yet, but he crawls pretty well.” The little guy’s eyes begin to flutter open, and Keegan cracks a smile. “He hears ba-ba and wakes up.” Jacque chuckles, “it doesn’t take too much to make him hungry, that’s for sure.” As though a natural Keegan reaches down and grabs the bottle of milk, Jordan cradling snugly in the crevasse of his arm. The one year old reaches his hands up to accept the bottle and greedily chugs away at the moo juice. “Oh wow!” Keegan exclaims startled at first, but quickly grinning instead. “He can really chug.” “If you think that’s good,” Jacque begins. “You should see him with a French fry.” “He can eat solid foods?” Keegan asks taken by surprise. “Already?” “Some.” The Canadian replies. “French fries, mash potatoes, applesauce, or anything that’s soft or mushy.” Jacque pauses for a moment, “in fact why don’t we go grab something to eat” We should really sit down and talk, and frankly I’m a little starved from the drive here.” “You want to go out to eat with me?” Keegan inquires, still adjusting to this complete turnaround. “Well it doesn’t have to be anything fancy,” Jacque explains. “Maybe you know a good diner where we can get some burgers and some fries for Jordan?” “Okay.” Keegan responds, “let me go throw a shirt on.” “If you hand me the baby I’ll get him ready, and you can get dressed.” Jacque reaches across and after a moment’s hesitation Keegan hands Jordan over before heading into the other room to get a fresh t-shirt. Jacque buckles Jordan up and has the car seat already in his hand when Keegan returns. “I can’t believe your letting me around him?” Keegan begins, his voice haunted with painful memories. “Despite knowing about my past.” Jacque meets his gaze, his expression solemn matching that of Keegan’s. “I know why you did it” and I’m proud to tell you that I would have done the same. That bastard deserved to die.” Lyrics from “.45” by Shinedown
