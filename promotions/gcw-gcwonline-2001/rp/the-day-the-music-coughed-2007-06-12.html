The Day the Music Coughed

12 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/07 Twizzy lay on his back, spread eagle, arms stretched out, feeling sorry for himself. Waah, waah. Poor Twizzy. Oh, he isn’t in the Hall of Fame. Waah, waah, waah. I’m crying, because Twizzy is oh so lovable and better than The Establishment. Cry, cry, cry. Cry it out so that EVERYONE feels sorry for Twizzy. Maybe we should all riot and charge GCW headquarters with placards and bumper stickers and a bunch of hippies. We’ll get the cops to arrest Steven Caldera by the power of the law! “You, Mr. Caldera, are charged with being a dick!” Yeah, and then Twizzy will be happy again, because he’s earned it and deserves it. Yeah, right. Janine tried to console Twizzy all week, but had to leave and get some fresh air today. He’s just too depressing. “When Twizzy was in Egypt-land. Let my Twizzy go?” he sang. RING-RING! The buzzer rang and Twizzy turned his head towards the door. “Who is it?” RING-RING! Now he remembered that before Janine left she ordered him up some Chinese food. This must be it. It’s probably pea pods and orange beef. She likes the pea pods. Twizzy thinks it’s only because of Seinfeld. In only his boxers he climbed out of bed and stumbled. He hadn’t walked around much since Warpath or as he has referred to it: The Day the Music Coughed. He answered the door and sure enough it is a man of Asian descent. I don’t like to assume people’s nationalities. Twizzy thinks he’s Thai. Either way he comes bearing food. “2B?” the delivery man asks. Twizzy nods. “Let me get some money.” The money for the food is being covered by the toaster. Just so it doesn’t blow away. She left nine dollars and the bill is for $7.65 so Twizzy gives him the full nine. “Good day,” the man says. “Hold on a minute.” The delivery man stops in his tracks and turns with eyebrows raised. “Yes?” “What should I do with Steven Caldera” The Day the Music Coughed was awful. I’m miserable. I’m not in the Hall of Fame. I’m not number one contender for anything. I’m humiliated.” Waah, waah, waah. Poor baby. Let’s all pray for Twizzy, because he deserves the help of Jesus, Buddha, Ganesh and The Dude. “What should I do” That damned LeStatt Knight. I hate him so much.” The delivery man doesn’t understand too much English. “Soy sauce?” “I’d like some, thank you.” The delivery man reached into his pockets and pulled out five packets of soy sauce and handed them to Twizzy. “Good day.” Twizzy watches him go and then closed the door once he’s down the stairs. He sighs. “I think I’ll order a pizza. Maybe he’ll know what to do.” WorldWide is soon and Twizzy knows that eventually he’ll have to take the Hall of Fame title from LeStatt Knight, most likely in a match. But that fact isn’t one that he wants to admit to. Certainly not now. If the music coughed last week then he was afraid that if he fought LeStatt Knight then Knight would fucking kill it.
