Is This The Beginning of the End, or is This The End of a New Beginning? (Part 1)

Date: 04/03/10 New York City Post-Rampage 2009 12:05am Three hours removed from a grueling match with Jay Terror the only things Tessa Windsor looked forward to were a heating pad and a bottle of aspirin. Walking away the winner was great and all, but given how sore she knew she’d be about noontime tomorrow, any kind of rest and relaxation sounded absolutely fabulous. Of course the chances for a little R& R were few and far between for the pint sized diva. While she laid sprawled out across the over sized bathtub inside of the two bedroom suite she had rented out for the evening, Tessa prayed that the kids would finally crash after all the excitement the evening had brought. Between begging every person they found backstage for an autograph and taking a couple hundred photos, it was hard to believe they’d have any energy. Tessa could only hope the silence that had been hovering throughout the suite for the last ten minutes confirmed their exhaustion. If not, the four of them were undoubtedly up no good. Sinking her tense body further into the mass of bubbles, her eyes sealed shut as her imagination drifted off to a world without responsibility. A world where she wasn’t raising four kids on her own, a world where she did not have to watch her back constantly, and a world where her contributions to both her family, friends, and career were actually recognized and praised. A world that was far out of reach, and even further away when the generic ring tone of her cell phone shattered that thought. RING-RING! Tessa groaned, still caught up in a world where if she shut her eye tightly enough, it would stop the phone from ringing. Unfortunately in that fantasy, reality was able to creep its way through, in the form of her internal debate of whether to pick it up at the chance of good news, or to ignore it and the more than likely bad news. The ear piercing sound of the phone ringing ended up winning out. Crawling out of the bathtub with a groan, Tessa strategically grabbed the towel hanging on the wall rack beside the tub and then hurried her way over to her phone, right outside the bathroom. "Hello?" She snapped into the phone, catching it at the very last moment. The person on the other end seemed hesitant to even breathe after catching such a harsh greeting. "Uh…Tessa?" Nanoseconds is what it took for Tessa to recognize the voice on the other end, which only made her tone ten times harsher, "Do you know what time of night it is?" The voice answered back quite sincerely, "almost one." Even a simple answer like that at this time of day was capable of further fueling Tessa’s rage. It helped none that the man on the other end was her on and again off again. She figured if anything, Chris Storm was merely being a wise ass when he answered her rhetorical question, which is why she had no problem ripping straight into him without hesitation, "Exactly. And you’ll never guess who has to watch the kids tonight? I’ll give you a hint, it’s not you." "I know, I’m sorry." He answered with only partial remorse. It had nothing to do with him not wanting to watch the kids and everything to do with the fact that he wanted to move past that part of the conversation as quickly as possible. "Hey, you haven’t heard from Markus tonight have you?" Tessa groaned as she used her free hand to rub her forehead. The only times Chris ever called about Markus, the two had gotten themselves into some kind of mess, and being that it was about closing time for most bars, this time was likely no different. "What did you two do this time?" "Nothing!" Chris automatically shot back, though raising his voice did little to convince her otherwise. "It’s just that, it’s just I haven’t seen or heard from him since he left the arena." Tessa paused for a moment to gather her thoughts while she slid out of her towel into her favorite white bathrobe. Upon connecting the dots, she came to the conclusion that Chris was merely trying to outsmart her, so she hit him right back with her own piece of knowledge, "and you think he would call me?" As stubborn as the self proclaimed Rock Superstar was, even Chris realized how unlikely that was nowadays given him and Markus affiliation with the man Tessa had done battle with earlier in the evening, though it didn’t stop him from inquiring once again, "I dunno, I figured maybe he would call you if he couldn’t get in touch with me. Are you sure he didn’t leave a voicemail or something? Or talk to the kids?" Waltzing out of the bathroom with her hair still a wet tangled mess, Tessa sighed as she informed him, "I’ve had my phone with me the whole time. I’m pretty sure I’d hear if he called." "Oh," was all Chris managed to mutter in response. Awkward silence filled the line as each waited for the other to say something. It was Chris who ended up caving, "do you think… think that… maybe you could call me if he does get in touch with you?" "Fine," Tessa answered with a sigh, "I’ll let you know if he calls, though I’m doubting it." Her eyes rolled at the thought. There was no way Markus would ever call her before he called Chris, no way at all. Besides that, with how much trouble the record breaking duo of Storm and Stone were capable of getting into, Chris really should have tried the NYC police department first. Chris, slighted by Tessa’s comments, awkwardly chirped in his appreciation, "alright, thanks." "Yeah, you’re welcome," was all that Tessa sarcastically responded with before hanging up. She whispered a plea to the phone gods shortly after clicking the end call key on her blackberry pearl"please don’t let him call again." Anything would be better than getting dragged into another one of their messes, anything at all. ---- New York City Post-Rampage 2009 3:25am Living on the go for nearly eight years now, Tessa Windsor had long since come to accept the sour fact that getting more than six hours worth of sleep was few and far between. Needless to say, she savored every opportunity she had to keep her eyes closed longer than an hour or two at a time. Tonight was no exception, even with the knowledge that her kids would probably be awake and bouncing around at the crack of dawn. Being aloft in peaceful dreams was an experience that Tessa had gone months without. Needless to say when she was halfway there, she was none too thrilled to be startled awake by a series of knocks on her hotel door. With her eyes half open, she groaned as she rolled over to her right, pulling the mass of white cotton sheets over her head. As unlikely as it was, she prayed, that the person on the other side of the door would grown bored and disappear into thin air. The moment Tessa was able to force her eyes the rest of the way down, another series of knocks echoed throughout the room. This time Tessa was not the only one to stir. The sudden break in silence startled her youngest, Aislinn. In a matter of thirty seconds, Tessa’s grogginess now had to contend with a sobbing toddler as well whoever was on the other side of the door. Thankfully, motherhood had made Tessa a champ at multitasking. With Aislinn tucked tightly between her arms, Tessa cautiously crept towards the door, her eyes only half open. She had only made it to the door by the time her eyes started to sink back shut. Quickly losing her battle against the sandman, this was just about the only times lately she was thankful to see Chris on the other side of the peephole. She opened to the door only to pass Aislinn off to her biological father. His eyes wide, Chris looked over to Tessa for some kind of explanation as to what to do next. The only thing he got in return was a scowl. "Hi," was the only word he had to offer as his broad arms cradled their only biologically shared kin. Tessa was not nearly as receptive to Chris’ presence as Aislinn, who had finally ceased her wailing. With her arms folded over her chest, Tessa questioned with plenty of attitude, "what do you want?" Chris answered a lot more defensively than he had earlier in the evening, "I promise I’m not trying to bother you. I know you’re busy. I just need you to answer me honestly." Her hands planted firmly on her hips, Tessa took to Storm with an overwhelming amount of authority, "what?" Regardless of the front Tessa was trying to put on, Chris felt it was necessary to disregard any barriers the two had set at the moment. He brushed past her to get into the room then turned to face her the moments he got through the door’s threshold, "I need to know where Markus is." Flabbergasted by the anger surging through her at the moment, Tessa had to take a moment to recollect herself before answering, "Do you really think I know? And even more important, do you really think I care?" Judging by the way his blue eyes widened when she asked that, the answer was ‘yes’. Regardless, he made himself clear, "I haven’t heard from him in a few hours." It did little to sway the opinion of Tessa, who had been the butt of far too many their endless pranks. "Call the police then. They ought to be able to tell you if he’s been locked up yet. And if he’s not locked up, then god knows what kind of trouble he’s getting himself into." Which is exactly what Chris was concerned about, "That’s the point!" It still was of little concern to Tessa, who brushed it off as merely Markus trying to score some girls number without Chris getting involved, "I’m sorry he didn’t want to bring you along on his little adventure this time. I’m sure you can manage without a wingman for one night." "I don’t know." Chris muttered with a bit of a waver in his voice, "I just…" he paused for a moment as if to try and figure out the best way to phrase what he wanted to say, "…I just have a bad feeling." "It’s nothing," Tessa responded without so much of an ounce of concern, "it’s Markus we’re talking about. You really should not be surprised with anything he does." Tessa turned her cold eyes onto Chris, "Besides, it’s not like he’s married to you, he doesn’t owe you an explanation of where he’s going every hour." Chris shuttered at the icy words, littered with plenty of backlogged issues that were being slung his way. "I know," his ego advised him it’d be best to back away now, "well look, if you hear from him can you just give me a call?" "Sure, whatever." Tessa responded with an icy glare as she reached out for Aislinn, who hardly seemed thrilled to be passed off again so quickly. Chris knew he had it coming. Waving only to Aislinn as he headed back for the door, he kept his voice to a whisper as he made a beeline for the hallway, "Thanks Tess." "You’re hardly welcome." The door slammed shut and Tessa made yet another attempt to get some well deserved R&R. ---- New York City Post-Rampage 2009 5:21am Any other morning, Tessa welcomed the sun creeping through the blinds of her hotel room. The pint sized diva absolutely despised any night on the road she had to spend alone. This morning, however, the former U.S. champion was fortunate enough to have the company of her four children. As much of a blessing as this rare treat was, Tessa dreaded the moment where all four were due to sprung to life with a fresh bout of energy and never-ending questions of where they were going to go today and what they were going to do. Lying in bed with her eyes half closed, Tessa had plenty to contemplate given everything that had transpired in the last twelve hours alone. Last night alone she had managed to walk out victorious against Jay Terror, address a bombardment of questions from reporters as to what was in her sights next, and make a little extra time for a celebration with the kids. And then there was Chris. There were plenty of questions inside of Tessa’s mind regarding his true motives. Redemption, harassment, the kids, affection, or merely looking for a quick fix were his typical reasons for showing up, but none of them seemed to be feasible this time around. The genuine concern for the well being of his partner in crime, Markus Stone, is what shone through on Chris’ face this time. As great as it was to see that softer side of Chris again, it seemed far too overly sentimental for Tessa to want to buy into. They’d been down that road before, it always ended along the lines of Tessa being duped. Yet Markus was another story. As much as she hated his antics, as well as his current direction, he’d always been like a brother to Tessa. Had Chris actually been telling the truth, there’d be plenty of guilt to pass around. Perhaps that’s why her head ceased to explode when for the third time in the matter of a just few hours there was a knock at the door. "Come in." She sighed, knowing full well that there was no sense of her resisting contact from the man on the other side. Chances were either one of the receptionists at the front desk would be plenty willing to give him a spare key to Tessa’s suite if he coaxed them long enough with his ‘charming father trying to win back the kids he misses so much’ routine. Sure enough, the door clicked open and in waltzed Chris Storm. She had completely guessed it. Tessa let out a long and loud sigh to let Chris know just what she thought of his intrusion. "You never heard from him?" Chris kept his head down, looking at the floor, "no, and I take a guess youhaven’t either." He lifted his eyes up to see what her response would be. "No." "You don’t think that’s kind of weird?" He asked, almost pleading for her to agree with him. As he expected she finally started to cave some, though she’d never admit the minor defeat. "Perhaps it’s a bit out of character for Markus. Not worth calling out the search dogs over though." Collapsing down at the end of the kids bed, Storm let loose with another sigh, "He ALWAYS checks in Tess. Even if he’s out fooling with some girl." That last bit was clearly too much info for the pint sized diva, who clinched her teeth as she scrambled for a response that did not involve chewing Chris out for offering up that kind of information. All she managed to come up with was a semi snappy, "well, what do you want me to do?" "Just come with me." Those were words she was not willing to work with. "I can keep an eye out for him while I’m out with the kids today." He hated to argue when he was desperate for help, but he was well aware of the loophole she was trying to sneak herself into. "Please, just come with me?" He was sure to lock his eyes with hers while pleading, "I promise I won’t bother you about this ever again if you give me just an hour to look." Tessa’s head shook side to side yet again as she fired back with a damn good question, "Why should I?" Chris leaned in forward toward her, his hands clasped together. "Please," he begged, "I know you don’t owe me a thing with everything Markus and I have done lately, but just do this for me." A part of Tessa wanted to give in, seeing how much it was bothering Chris. The other part of Tessa wanted to shove her foot into his face and tell him to get lost for daring to ask her a single favor after all he had ever put her through the last year alone. "You are damn right I don’t owe you anything!" He was on the verge of tears, "please…" The little bit of defense she had left shattered. Just as Chris had when he entered, Tessa let loose her own sigh, "You’re lucky I’m senseless." He reassured, "An hour. Not so much as a second more." Leading the way to the door, Tessa disregarded his last bit, "Let’s just go." ---- New York City Post-Rampage 2009 6:03am "So, what have you been up to?" Twenty minutes had passed since the two had set forth on their journey, yet not a word had been exchanged until the man who commonly referred to himself as a Rock Superstar finally tired of hearing nothing but his own anxious thoughts regarding the wellbeing of his best friend in the business. "The kids doing okay?" Even his attempts at small talk did little to extinguish the angry fire raging inside of Tessa’s mind. Though she was the one to cave, she still saw it to be entirely Chris’ fault that she was out aimlessly wandering the unfriendly streets of New York City first thing in the morning. Tessa was none too amused with his small talk attempts, "Do you really need to know? Or are you trying to pass time?" "Both." Chris suggested with the slightest of shrugs, "I figure you have to have some kind of news you want to dump on me." Tessa joined in the game, if only to find an outlet for her anger, "Well….let’s see….I slept about an hour last night between your interruptions and Aislinn’s night frights. How about you? You get any sleep?" Chris nervously let out a laugh, realizing just how upset Tessa was. "Sorry," was all he managed to mumble out to her, "shouldn’t have asked." As the seconds ticked by and steps added up, Tessa’s nerves began to slowly grind away. She absolutely hated the internal battle that resided inside her head anytime Chris was around. Without any sort of probing event, Tessa was reduced to a short lived rant, "What exactly do you plan on gaining by wandering around the entire city? Do you actually think we will find him?" At this point Tessa stopped walking and was in full rant mood. "I don’t know if you were aware, but New York is a very big city." The only thing Chris could offer was his zillionth shrug of the past twelve hours. Coaxing Tessa to come along was really began to feel like a bad idea. Even if they had found Markus, she was unlikely to one eighty it and suddenly spring a delightful smile. He turned to face her, dread etched across his face. He almost fell to tears yet again when he pleaded, "Just please cut me some slack? Imagine how worried you’d be if anything happened to Vivica." The instant Chris turned back to face the path ahead, Tessa’s head dropped. He had a damn good point there, one that deserved no sort of disagreement from her. In his own mind, Chris came off a bit too scornful for his own liking. Quickly he backtracked to save some face, "I promise I won’t keep you any longer than I need you. I’m already pretty damn aware that being here with me is killing your pride." Tessa’s bottom lip curled in as she desperately sought for anything appropriate to say. The only thing she stumbled upon was a meek, "Mmhmm." Her head dropped back towards the ground, her eyes carefully tracing every step made by her gray and pink running shoes, until a quick yank in the direction of Chris threw her sight off balance. Her eyes shot wide open as she glanced over towards Chris, who kept a tight grip on her arm as he stared off into the distance. "Jesus Christ." Chris whispered under his breath, thinking it would not be loud enough for Tessa to hear, but she had and her eyes desperately maneuvered around from side to side to catch a glance of whatever he was staring at. When she finally caught glance her own mouth fell slightly agape. Sprawled out across the street on a park bench much too small for his massive self with a half drank bottle of jack on his side, Markus Stone looked anything but classy. To add to the case, the puddle of vomit by his side certainly did warrant any sort of warm welcome reception. If anything, Tessa wanted to vomit herself purely out of disgust. Right then Markus Stone looked far more like a bum than a man with a six digit salary. Without hesitation, Tessa turned on her heel, but ended up halted mid twist by the massive arm of the man beside her. She did not bother to bite her tongue this time, "You can’t be serious Chris." "Just stay with me for a second. Please?" He finally asked in a normal tone of voice. "One." She took a step back proclaiming on her way out, "Alright, I’m done." He had no humor when it came to her acting childish, "You know what I mean." She knew full well what he meant; it was much more the fact that she had absolutely no idea of how to cope with the situation that scared her. The only difference between her and Chris at this moment; she had the guts to admit it. "Seriously Chris, what am I going to do? Smack some sense into him?" "Do you really think I have any clue, Tess?" "You’re his best friend. I imagine you’d have a better idea than I do." Chris let out a sigh and grabbed Markus’ shoulder, trying to shake him awake. Markus snorts, rolls over, and seems to go right back to ignoring them. "Look, he’s fine" Tessa told him as she tried to leave again. Once again Chris had to stop her from going, "come on, look, just give me a hand getting him upright. Maybe he just needs to sober up for a few minutes." "Then he can sleep it off." Tessa fired back. Chris looked at her with those big round blue eyes, absolutely pleading with her to help out. As much as she tried to turn her head away, somehow Storm managed to keep his eyes aligned with hers to where she could not help but feel guilty. Under her breath she muttered, "I hate you." Even if his time of need, he could not help but point out the irony in her words, "Yet you’re staying." "For Markus. NOT for you." "Thank you regardless." He said tenderly, reaching out for her hand. Tessa made sure to yank it back before his fingertips could even brush up against it and Chris could almost feel the ice coming off her. Shaking it off he turned his attention back to Markus Stone and grabbed him around the upper body to try and get him to sit up right. "BLARGH!" What he got instead was Markus throwing up over Storm’s shoulder, all over his back. Tessa tried to stifle her laughter with her hand but it slipped between her fingers. "Now that almost made it worth it." Chris looked none too pleased, but he had at least managed to get Stone to sit upright. Plopping himself down beside his best friend, his eyes glanced back up towards Tessa in a search for suggestions, as if he’d never gone through a night worth of drinks himself. "I’ll get him some water." Even managing a simple cooperative answer from her seemed miraculous. "Thanks." He whispered under his breath with a faint smile. By this point he had sat down on the bench beside Markus, in attempts to keep his partners head propped up. It failed to dawn on Chris that he had absolutely no clue what to do, until Tessa disappeared from the corner of his eye. It was then he sat in absolute silence. There was not a thing he could say to Markus that did not sound either overbearing or far too emotional for a guy to spieling aloud in a public setting. Instead the two sat in silence for quite awhile before Chris finally prompted with a question that had the utmost obvious of answers. "You alright man?" Stone groaned before pulling himself up on his own power. He blinked twice, groaned, then looked over at Chris with the utmost puzzled expression, "the fuck we doing here?" With a shrug, Chris answered back, "Wish I knew, Marky, really wish I knew. It was a long fucking night." Keeping his emotions under tight wrap felt damn near impossible for Chris. Much as he hated to see his friend like this, he was far more concerned about walking away with his macho persona still intact. He dreaded the bullshit explanation of things he’d have to draw up the minute Tessa showed back up on the scene. "I don’t remember you there." Stone pitched in a mutter, barely managing to pull himself up to his feet without stumbling. "I was late." Storm’s story would have been much more believable had Tessa not finally strolled back into the scene with a bottle of water in each hand. Again Stone blinked and threw another question in the way of the route Chris Storm sought to take. "Why’s she here too?" Storm hesitated long enough that even Stone, in his morning after stage, could pick up that something was up. Regardless, he still tried to cover his tracks. "She came along with us. Oh man…." Number one tip to remember when lying, make sure all parties concede to the story being told. It was obviously the point where Chris went wrong. Catching the tail end of it all, Tessa was sure to jump straight in with the real deal. There was no way she was going to be unwillingly dragged into all of this, and then made out to be the fool. "ACTUALLY…" Chris quickly sprung to his feet, his arms wide open for her to jump into, "You’re back!" He was completely denied. Instead, Tessa’s eye bore straight into Markus as she went straight into preacher mode. "You’re damn lucky to be alive from what I hear." Grabbing her by the arm, Chris skipped straight back into desperation mode. He lowered his head down to her ear level, whispering his plea, "Tessa….don’t…." She spat back venomously, "Don’t what Chris? You don’t even know what happened!" It didn’t stop him from trying one last time. "Tess….please…..I’ll talk with him." "Talk? Or lie to him?" Finally Stone chirped in with a pint of agitation, "The fuck you guys talking about?" "Chris…" Tessa pressured, hoping she’d be able to wrangle control of him the same way he always managed to do so with her. Markus glanced over his direction, obviously searching for the truth. A truth his best friend avoided talking about at all costs, even now. "Don’t worry bout it man. Things are fine." "But Tess said…" "She’s a chick. Chicks worry bout everything." In reality, there was little Tessa could do but let it slide away out of her control. The likelihood of Markus believing anything she had to say decreased every second that Chris spent around him. Neither one of them would ever want to admit to being the weaker half of their record breaking team. Forced to wave it off, Tessa’s conscious nagged her to at least let Markus know, he was far from off the hook. It wasn’t easy to do, but ever so meekly before walking off, Tessa made sure to stand her ground, regardless of how angered Chris would end up, "We’ll talk later Markus. Please call me?" Markus, caught in the middle of it all, apathetically murmured, "If that’s what you want." "Yeah." She conceded, having to bury the exasperated sigh residing within her lungs, dying to escape, "Guess that’s what I want." "Guess I need to then." "Eleven, no later." "Sure. Whatever you want." That said, Tessa took her cue to leave. After all, time had shown her many times that no good ever came from her sticking around in troublesome situations. The only thing she could do now was pray.
