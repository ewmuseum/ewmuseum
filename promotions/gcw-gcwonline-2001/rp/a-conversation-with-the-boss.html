A Conversation With The Boss

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today The call had come to Seymour Almasy’s Tokyo hotel room promptly at 8:00 AM. Really, picking it up and answering it was a formality, because Seymour Almasy had known who it would be from the second the ringing began. It wasn’t like one of the boys was calling him up asking to go drinking. Not at 8 AM. Besides, considering Seymour’s current sorrows, such an invitation would have been rejected anyway. No, the only person that both had his phone number in the room, and had business to discuss was the man that called. “We need to talk,” Steven Caldera, owner of GCW, had told him, in no uncertain terms. “I’ll be in a limo downstairs in forty-five minutes. Be there.” And then, he’d hung up. “Touchy,” Seymour had thought, though he could understand why. One of Caldera’s biggest stars was about to stand trial for a quadruple homicide, in addition to a laundry list of other charges. Rich Rollins was GCW through and through, a man who had been there from the beginning. Those were the reasons why Caldera had posted bail to the tune of two million dollars. And that was why Seymour stood outside his hotel on the streets of Tokyo, at 8:40 AM, waiting for Caldera to show up, rather disgusted. “I can’t believe Caldera has two million dollars to throw around on shit like this,” Almasy said, shaking his head. “And on fucking Rollins to boot.” Considering what he’d discovered about the Renegade, Seymour was rather looking forward to Rollins? trial. He’d watch every minute of it on Court TV and exult. “He deserves to die for the line he crossed. I hope they give him the chair.” Rollins had slept with Seymour’s ex-wife, Laura. Gave her a gun and told her to use it on Seymour. Poisoned her mind with lies. As it turned out, though, his plan didn’t go quite as planned, because it was Laura who ended up pushing up daisies, not Seymour. Almasy was alive, ready for their contest in the GTT5 semi-finals, a match that was likely to be Rollins? last. Seymour smiled at that thought, but before he could revel in it too much more, a long black limousine pulled up and parked curbside. The driver got out, and walked around to Seymour’s side, opening the passenger door to reveal the spacious interior. Seated on the leather was a grim-looking Steven Caldera, as serious as Seymour had ever seen him. “Get in,” Caldera said, and considering his tone of voice, Seymour Almasy was not going to refuse. He got in the fucking limo. The driver closed the door, and at first, it seemed pleasantries were in order. “Hello,” Almasy said, trying to sound cheerful. “Hello,” Caldera echoed, before going a step further. “Care for something to drink?” “No thank you,” Seymour replied. “Too early.” By this time, the driver had gotten back into the driver’s seat, and the limo had begun to pull away. Caldera waved his hand, and the window between the front and back of the limo went up, leaving Seymour and Steven alone. Instantly, Caldera’s face hardened again. “Here we go,” Seymour thought, knowing deep down in the pit of his gut that this was going to be a highly unpleasant conversation. “Let’s get straight to the point, Seymour,” the businessman said, looking him dead in the eyes. “Rich Rollins is in deep shit, and you’re the only person who really knows what the fuck happened.” Seymour nodded at that, calmly, prompting a veritable explosion from the owner of the company. “The idea of him kidnapping you,” he stated bluntly, “is bullshit.” “Of course it is,” Seymour replied. “Why do you think I didn’t press charges against him on that count” He’s got enough to worry about as it is.” “How helpful,” Caldera replied, in an utterly patronizing manner. “If you didn’t get kidnapped, then what the fuck were you doing there?” “Sorry, Steven,” Seymour said, a bit flippantly, “but my lawyers have advised I not speak to anyone on this subject.” Caldera reached into the limo’s minibar and grabbed a bottle. Pouring a shot-glass full, he took a stiff swig. Judging by Caldera’s reaction, it was probably something hard. “Shit, Seymour, I know you don’t like the guy, but this is serious business! He’s standing trial for four murders! If he gets convicted, he could get the chair!” “WILL get the chair,” Seymour corrected. “I’ve seen the case against him. It’s airtight.” “You WHAT”!” Clearly, Steven hadn’t expected this. “You already gave a statement?” Seymour nodded. “Yes. Although, they told me they weren’t going to call me to testify. Pretty lucky, huh” I’ll be able to make all of my GCW dates.” “What the fuck did you tell them, Seymour?” It didn’t seem that Mr. Caldera was especially concerned with Seymour and his GCW appearances right now, not with one of his other top stars potentially about to fry. “I told you, Caldera, I can’t talk about it. But trust me when I say it doesn’t matter.” “Of course it matters!” Caldera was thunderstruck, grasping at straws even as he spoke. “Rich told me you shot the detective in his hand. Did you?” Silence reigned in the back of the limo for a few moments, before Seymour nodded his head. “Yes. Yes, I did. I also shot him in the kneecap. Same for that other guy, Frank, or whatever his name was.” Mr. Caldera let his head fall into his hands. It was truly worse than he could have imagined. “Then, if you SHOT TWO MEN, how the fuck are you gonna be able to make GCW shows” You’ll be on trial too!” The look on Seymour Almasy’s face as he shook his head “no” would stay with Mr. Caldera for a long, long time. “Nope. The state of Illinois has decided not to prosecute me, so long as I keep my mouth shut. I figured that was a pretty good deal for me, so I took it.” “You know something,” the owner replied accusingly. “You bastard! You could get Rollins off, couldn’t you”!” Again, Seymour shook his head no. “Even if I told the whole story, it wouldn’t matter. Illinois is after its pound of flesh, Steven. Surely, being a businessman, you know how the world REALLY works, right?” Caldera sat there, unable to respond, simply watching as Seymour continued spinning his tale of doom and gloom. “They’ve already created evidence to show Rollins fired the three shots I fired at the two men. What evidence they couldn’t find at the crime scene has been fabricated to point the finger at Rich Rollins. For those reasons, the case against him is airtight. That’s the reason they don’t need me to testify. They don’t WANT me to testify. A good defense attorney can poke holes in testimony. There’s not a whole hell of a lot you can do against forensic evidence.” Caldera’s question was simple, to the point, and borne from a sense of defeat. “Why?” “Why do they want Rollins to fry” Easy. He killed a cop. No matter what the circumstances were, the police don’t take kindly to that sort of shit.” “But Rollins told me--? “What, that Frank and the detective kidnapped his pregnant mistress and set her on fire” That’s what he told me, too. But really, who the fuck is a jury going to believe? The state, or some stupid wrestler with long white hair?” Really, Steven Caldera felt like pulling his hair out. Bad enough that Rollins had been caught in a compromising situation, but now, to listen to Seymour, the entire state was aligned against him. “There’s something here I’m missing,” Caldera said, looking at Almasy expectantly. “You had absolutely no reason to be there in that cemetery. None at all. If you’re not going to tell me what actually happened, fine. But at least tell me what the fuck you were doing there that got a gun pointed at your head.” Seymour took a deep breath. Answering Caldera’s question would tear open the wounds that he had been trying to heal. But Caldera was asking for it. By God, it sounded like he was lionizing Rollins. Trying to get him off, after everything he’d done in his life? The Final Fantasy could not stand for that. “I was looking for Rollins,” Seymour spat. “Because, as much as I know you love him because he makes you millions of dollars, he’s a soulless fuck with no remorse for everything he’s done.” Tears started to stream slowly from Seymour’s eyes, and it was clear to Caldera that he’d hit a nerve. Furthermore, he suspected this whole story was about to get a whole hell of a lot more complicated. “What happened, Seymour,” he asked, throat dry as he watched one of his top stars slowly break down in the confines of the limousine. “Tell me.” Hatred eminated from Seymour’s gaze as he attempted to speak, swallowing the bile in his throat long enough to do so. “Laura’my ex-wife’she killed herself. You know that. What you don’t know is’that Rich Rollins’slept’with her. He gave her the gun she killed herself with. Only problem is, he meant for her to use it on me. He told her I didn’t care about her. All of these lies’to get her to kill me.” Seymour looked at Caldera. Caldera looked at Seymour. “I understand the investment you have in Rich Rollins, Caldera,” Seymour said, “but I hope the bastard dies slowly and painfully.” Steven Caldera took another swig of alcohol. He had never heard Seymour talk this way, never seen the usually kind-hearted and good-natured Seymour so utterly defeated, yet resolved. “I understand you’re upset, Seymour, but why not take it--? “To court” There’s no evidence. Never mind the fact that I found one of the asshole’s bandanas IN HER FUCKING HOUSE. There’s not enough evidence to convict him on it. Besides, what are they going to charge him with, conspiracy to commit murder? Rollins has gotten away with shit for way too long. You know Frank and that detective? Rich killed their daughter in a car accident. Wasn’t prosecuted. Slept with my ex-wife and tried to get her to kill me. No way I could get him to be prosecuted. I see this whole thing as karma coming back to bite him in the ass.” Caldera opened his mouth to respond, but Seymour cut him off. “No,” he insisted, “you’re done talking. You’re done trying to defend this asshole. You disgust me. The only thing you give a shit about is GCW. Your ratings. All the money Rich Rollins makes you. You don’t give a shit about me, or my feelings. THE MAN WANTED ME DEAD, STEVEN! And you know why” Because he thought he was going to run into me in GTT5 and wanted me out of the way. HE DID THIS TO ME OVER WRESTLING! IT’S BECAUSE OF HIM LAURA’S GONE! BECAUSE OF RICH ROLLINS, I DON’T HAVE A BEST FRIEND ANYMORE!” Suddenly, he stopped screaming, and looked at Caldera. Almasy’s shoulders slumped forward a bit, and he sighed. Perhaps he’d been a bit too aggressive. “Look’I’m sorry for yelling at you. But you have to understand where I’m coming from. If it was you’you’d do the same thing. Please, Mr. Caldera, tell the driver to pull up to the side of the road and let me out. I’ll call a cab back to the hotel. Please’I can’t deal with this shit anymore right now.” Steven Caldera took a slow, deep breath. If what Seymour Almasy was telling him, he was in for a migraine and an ulcer simultaneously. Rich Rollins was in deep shit, and there didn’t seem to be anyway out. Furthermore, regardless of what Rollins had or had not done in relation to the quadruple homicide, he HAD fucked with another GCW talent, one of the top ones to boot. Even if Rollins did the unthinkable and made it out of this clean, there’d be backstage problems for the rest of either man’s tenure. Caldera sighed, looking back at the man he shared a limo backseat with. Clearly, Seymour wanted out of the vehicle, and he wanted out now. There wasn’t anything to be gained by making the Final Fantasy any less happy than he was, so Caldera replied, tapping on the window to get the driver’s attention. “Pull over,” he said, as the window came down. “We’re going to be letting Mr. Almasy off here.” The driver complied immediately, and the limousine came to a stop. Casting a look over his shoulder at Mr. Caldera, Seymour exited with a few final words. “I know this isn’t easy for you. Wish there was something I could say or do to help.” And with that, Seymour Almasy was gone. Rather than call a cab immediately, he disappeared in one of the dozens of noodle restaurants that dotted the Tokyo landscape for a quick bite to eat. Back in the limousine, Steven Caldera vowed to have a flunky get him some aspirin. Of the extra strength variety. -------------------------------------------- This is my escape. GCW. Rampage. Competition. When I’m in the ring, I can forget about what Rich Rollins did to my life. What he did to Laura. In the ring, I still have control. Eva, Taylor, Kimbusa’it’s nothing personal. Any other week, I’d give you guys a great shot at beating me. You’re three of the best in the world. But this week, I need this. This week, I will have this. By any means necessary.
