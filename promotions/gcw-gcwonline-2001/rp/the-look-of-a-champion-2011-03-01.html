The Look Of A Champion

1 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/11 The scene opens up inside of a fashion store. Currently, they are in the Men's Formalware Section. Lollie is walking around looking at various styles on display, while Toga follows him with the GCW Hardcore title around his waist. He appears to be holding it up... maybe it's too big for him? His focus is mostly on his crotchal-area, due to the fact that that is where the belt rests. He's, surprisingly, paying enough attention to follow two-steps behind Lollie as she browses. Lollie: "Hmm.... this one isn't bad...." She makes a mental note of the style before progressing on. She glances up at a long velvet, purple overcoat, with a frilly shirt underneath, a tan bow-tie, and a light brown top hat resting on top of it. She makes an ucky face, as she looks onto the next. Toga glances up, and immediately his eyes start to sparkle. He stops and stares at the purple coat outfit. Toga: "Woooooooooonka!!!" The gal in green turns back around to Thomas, with a quizzical look on her face. Lollie: "Huh?" The skirt-clad champion points at the purple overcoat, while still holding the belt up with his other hand. Toga: "Willy Wonka's suit!" Lollie: "Oh gawd........" She rolls her eyes and continues on. Toga continues to stare at it for a few seconds longer, before VERY reluctantly scampering back over to his manager. He once again starts watching the lights bounce off his new prize that is wrapped around his waist. Toga: "Lollie....... um...... may I go look around....?" Lollie: "Sure. Just come back in a couple minutes, so we can try some of these on and see how sharp you look!" He grins, gives her a quick hug from behind, then scampers off. The camera stays focused on her. She spots another suit that catches her eye. Lollie: "Ooooooo!" Once again, she jots down a mental note. In the background, Toga can be heard asking someone, probably a store clerk, a question. Toga: "Sir, where is the fitting rooms?" Clerk: "Over there." The shuffling of feet can be heard as Toga scurries into the dressing room. The camera pans over just in time to see him slip into one of the rooms. Lollie looks curious. She wanders over there and stands just outside of the curtain to the fitting room that Toga is in. Lollie: "Tommmmmyyy...... what are you doing?" Toga: "I found this amazing shirt that I needed to........ ugh....... ack................ OWIE............... uh-oh." Lollie gets closer to the curtain, having her curiosity piqued even more. Lollie: "Tommy, are you okay?" Toga: "Uh... ow.... yeah.... just.... errr..... fine......................" She places her hand on the curtain, contemplating opening it. She hesitates. Lollie: "Do you.... need help?" Toga: "Umm.................. yeah.... I'm stuck." She pulls the curtain back far enough to squeeze in, the camera starts to follow... slowly... Lollie: "WHAT THE HELL?!...... WHERE ARE YOUR PANTS, YOUNG MAN?!" The camera stops approaching the curtain. It takes a couple steps back, actually. Toga: "I took them off." Lollie: "Whyyyy?!" Toga: "Because I'm getting changed... Duh!" Lollie: "Yeah... but just your shirt!..... of which is.... Hamtaro?!.... It's a children's shirt, dumbass!" The curtain rustles a little bit... Lollie: "What the hell is on your underwear?" Toga: ".... my little pony..." Lollie: "You're wearing girl's panties?!" Toga: "Pfft! NO!" Lollie: "Yes, you are! They don't make My Little Pony undies for anything except little girls!" Toga: ".................. oh." There's some more rustling, before Lollie storms out, with the children's-size Hamtaro shirt in hand. Lollie: "And put your damn pants back on, boy!" Toga: "Yes ma'am." The gal in green rolls her eyes as she walks over to an employee. Lollie: "I'd like my friend to get fitted for a suit." Clerk: "Which style did you have in mind?" She wanders over to a suit that she had looked at before. She points to it. It is quite snazzy. Lollie: "This one." Clerk: "And where's your friend?" On the cue, Thomas stumbles out of the dressing room, with his pants on one leg, trying to get the other one up. He hops around a bit, trying to keep his balance, before finally crashing to the floor, with his Pony undies on full display! Lollie sighs. Lollie: "... that's him." The clerk glances at her with a "You're with THAT guy?!" look. She slowly nods. Clerk: "Um................ ooooookay. Let's see if we can work miracles." Lollie: ".... thank you." She wanders over to help Toga back up, as the clerk goes to get everything ready. Lollie: "Tommy... let's get you some new undies while we wait..." Toga: "... transformers?" Lollie: "We'll see........" Toga manages to pull his pants up, as Lollie grabs the Hardcore Title from the dressing room. She hands it to him, before they both walk off screen towards the MEN'S underwear section...
