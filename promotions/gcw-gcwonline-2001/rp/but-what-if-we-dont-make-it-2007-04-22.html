But what if we dont make it?

22 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22, 2007 The door shut as she walked into the room she could tell there was something wrong, you know how you get that feeling once you enter a room that maybe you shouldn’t have walked in but it is too late to turn around now? Well this was one of those times for Tiara Belle. As she strolled into the door she was not feeling all that well, she had been fighting a cold for sometime but she really had a headache today. She carried her bags into the kitchen where he husband was watching Sports Center and drinking a bottle of Gatorade. The sweat was pouring off of his head he took his towel and wiped his brow and looked at his unpleasant wife. “Hey baby, how was your day?” You could tell he knew that it was a loaded question but he had to ask it anyways to make the small talk. He wiped his whole face down after asking the question before looking back at her. “Well” Ok I guess. The stores were packed and I couldn’t find the shoes I was looking for.” She could tell Wayne had just finished his daily work out and that he was not satisfied with his performance. It was just the look in his eyes that gave it away. But she had to ask the question anyways, “How was yours?” Taking another sip of his water before responding to her question he thinks hard. Then he places the Gatorade bottle back on the counter and looks at her. “Well I tried upping my max today, but to no avail. I pushed only 415 on the incline and the 500 on the flat bench, I really wish I could get up to that next level.” She could tell the disgust in his eyes. She wanted to comfort him but he was all sweaty and she was wearing a fairly new shirt. “It’s ok honey, those are still amazing numbers. It just goes to show that you really are on top of your game.” Wayne slams his fist down on the table, it startled the slender Tiara Belle. ? If I was on top of my game we wouldn’t be facing the new team!” “What do you mean?” Tiara deep down knew what he was getting at. They had gone from being Tag Team Title contenders to being the opening act for all new teams in the last month or so. “You know damn well what I mean, this is bull shit, we were the title contenders hell we beat Fiery Passion before they even got the titles. And a month removed we lose to Banned and Exiled~! Over some fucked up mind games shit, and now we once again we are facing a team that doesn’t deserve to be in the ring with us.” Wayne’s face was getting all red, not from the sweat or from the work out but from his temper starting to flare. He knew he needed to calm down, but right now he couldn’t even if he waned to. Trying to be all sympathetic or at least acting it as she felt Wayne was over reacting, “So what we have to face this new Japanese tag team” I mean it will be an easy win, it will just be another notch on our belts.” “I know, I know” but we should be facing Banned and Exiled~! Not some japs! It just sucks, I think we should go straight to Caldera’s office when we get to North Carolina.” Wayne knew there was no other way but at the same time he wasn’t sure if they had the grounds, I mean yeah they had been number one contenders, yeah they had beaten the champs in a non title match but then they had disappeared from the card completely, they were non existent until their loss against Banned and Exiled a week or two back. “Why don’t we give it a week, if we win this week and nothing comes of it then we will do something, but first we have to ensure that we win this match and this match alone, baby clear your head, and win this match.” Wayne nods in agreement as he looks at his gorgeous wife, he knew she was always the calmer of the two, she could be a complete bitch but she did have a knack for being calmer. “Why don’t you go take a shower, when you get out I will have dinner ready and then we can sit down and watch a movie and I will give you a back rub.” Tiara had tried to deal with his temper the best she could sometimes, mostly in the ring, she was unable to and had to let him go freely but the point still stood that she had done her best today and it looks like she had calmed him down enough that he wouldn’t do something stupid and get his self kicked out of GCW or better yet thrown into jail with that temper. She starts cooking dinner as he runs up stairs to take a warm shower trying to cool himself down. Tiara starts putting the different things she purchased onto the kitchen table as she puts on some water so it can start to boil. She is going thru the different things and allowing the water to boil she can start to make dinner. Meanwhile upstairs Wayne is getting ready for his shower allowing the water to run for a good five minute as he likes to have his water scolding. He rubs his hands over the top of his hand bringing them down over his face. He couldn’t help but feel that the reason Pleasure and Pain hadn’t been succeeding was his fault. In reality he knew it wasn’t completely his fault but in his mind he was placing the blame on himself and on himself alone. This week though he knew that he needed to step up, and he planned on it. Wayne takes his shower while Tiara cooks his dinner and puts everything she bought today away. Their home in the suburbs of Massachusetts was not a mansion but it was a good size home that was well taken care of. The two finish doing their own things and meet again at dinner for another wonderful conversation. Wayne sits in his chair enjoying a wonderful dinner his wife had just cooked for him. He places his fork down after taking a bite of a meatball and takes a sip of his wine. Tiara had something she wanted to talk to him about but she wasn’t sure how to bring it up to him. He thought about it while she watched him closely, he thought she was just admiring him and showing he love, but in a few moments he will find out that she really has other intentions with that subtle look in her eyes. Wayne takes another sip of his wine and that is when she makes her move. “Baby?” Tiara started the conversation slowly but getting the response she expected from him she continued, ? I have been thinking if our wrestling career doesn’t take off like we want.” “What do you mean if it doesn’t take off like we plan” It will don’t you worry.” Wayne was not looking forward to where this conversation was headed. But he allowed her to continue anyways. “Let me finish first please, If our career as a tag team does not take off I had another idea.” Tiara’s idea wasn’t really an idea it was more of what she really wanted to happen but she had to get him to think that this was only going to happen if they didn’t work out like they want. “Oh yeah what is that?” “Well if it doesn’t work out and we keep getting the shaft I was thinking you could go on and become a singles wrestler while I can be your manager.” Tiara had a lot more on her mind but she had to feel Wayne out, she had to see which would be the best way to continue on with her idea. “Then what would you do” Just walk me to the ring, hit my opponent with a stick when I am not able to win a match?” Wayne was disgusted with such a thought but he had to let her talk otherwise his night might not go as planned and he knew he might be sleeping on the couch once again. “Well I was kind of thinking that we could start our family.” Tiara had never really brought this up to Wayne before, she knew he wanted a family but they had always talked about their wrestling careers first, and well they just got their second gig ever in the industry. Wayne’s fork hit the table, not the response she was hoping to get from him but still it was what she got. “But baby I thought we were going to work on our careers and once we were settled and had accomplished what we had wanted to we would work on that?” She expected Wayne to get mad but he didn’t he kept his cool, something he was not usually very good at so he at the same token was happy that he was able to keep it. “That’s why I said if we don’t get our career going soon, I could get the family started and you could emerge on the scene as a singles wrestler. It would work wouldn’t it ?” Tiara knew it would work but she also knew that they had met in wrestling school and that was really where they found the love for each other, when they found the love for their sport. ? I know but ever since we meet in the ring I had always expected that one day we would be holding gold up over our heads, be claimed the victors and the newest Tag team champions.” “We can still do that but what I am saying is if this time it doesn’t work then why don’t we try something else I will still be involved by managing you but you will be in the ring by yourself that is all.” Wayne knew what she was saying might be the right idea, she always had a knack for knowing what to say and when to say it. He didn’t always want to hear it but oh well. “Give me another couple of matches and we can take it from there.” Wayne went back to eating his stomach was still upset but not nearly as much as it was before. Tiara went back to eating as well but not before she could throw those last words in, “That is fine.” She expected he would remain quiet and keep eating but instead he placed his fork down took a sip of his wine and grabbed her hand, “Baby you know I Love you and you know I want to have children with you but first lets try and make it in this wrestling world of ours.” “Ok,” she said as she placed her fork down and looked back into his eyes. She looked at him and they were both in unison getting up kissing each other ever so softly making their way upstairs. They had forgotten all about the found but not about their love for each other.
