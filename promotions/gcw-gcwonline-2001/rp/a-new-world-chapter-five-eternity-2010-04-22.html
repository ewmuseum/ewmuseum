A New World. Chapter Five: Eternity.

22 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/10 “Donny Diamond is dead...” The Society. [The door to his holding cell slides open as Donny Diamond is seen sitting on his bed. He looks up towards the door where Jason Nickles can be seen walking into the room.] Donny Diamond: Are you so corrupted that you would have me kill my twin brother? Jason Nickles: I admit we knew the true identity of Dagda, but we also knew you would never listen to us if we told you who he was. Donny Diamond: You are more of a monster than I could ever be. You have no morals for anything, nothing is sacred to someone like you. Jason Nickles: That's where you're wrong, because our purpose is making sure humanity remains sacred. By fading out the people who cannot see our vision the world will become a perfect Utopia. And now is the time for it to continue with your next task. [Walking closer to Donny, Jason opens up a folder he was holding that has a profiling of his next target. Grabbing the information sheet Donny skims it over for a moment before noticing the name on it; “Pope Benedict XVI”. Before words can escape his lifts, Jason nods in assurance that this is indeed the correct target. Behind the profile is some pictures of the Vatican City in Italy, which is where Donny must go.] Donny Diamond: I guess it's to bad I never had the pleasure of visiting the Holy See. Jason Nickles: That is of no worry, seeing as you have just glanced over the photos that is all we need in order to establish a connection for you there. Donny Diamond: Why would you have me kill this man? He stands for everything The Society represents, he is the sole servant of God. Jason Nickles: Because he has lied to God! He used his power in the church to cover up several cases of rape by priests! Someone with such great sins does not deserve the honor of delivering the messages of God and once you rid him from this world we can rise out of hiding. The world will then be able to see the truth and know that only the purist souls can enter Heaven! [As Jason continues on with his preaching Lola enters the room where she begins placing the bracelets once again on the wrists of Donny. He rises from the cot he was resting on and follows Lola as Jason closes the folder and follows them. As they walk down the hall, Donny takes notice of the room where his daughter is being held. Lola opens the door to the transporter room, and Donny makes his way into the center as the machines are turned on.] Donny Diamond: You will be punished for all this Jason! [Jason just smiles to Donny as Lola closes the door to the small room. Everything in the room begins to shake and screams can be heard coming from Donny as the room fades in and out. Soon the surroundings transform and Donny is standing in a dark corner of a large room. Darkness covers everything that the moonlight doesn't hit. Off in the far side of the room is an open door leading to the balcony, and Donny slowly makes his way towards it.] [On the balcony stands Pope Benedict XVI looking up towards the sky saying a silent prayer. A few moments pass before he walks back into the room. Donny quickly grabs hold of him and forces him down onto his knees, and he reaches onto a cabinet where he grabs a small dagger. Forcing it against his throat in an upward direction he holds it there.] Donny Diamond: Forgive me, but I have no other choice than to end your life... Pope Benedict XVI: There is always another choice my son. Donny Diamond: Not this time Father Ratzinger. This society holds power of controlling souls, and plans on rebuilding this world in their image. They hold my daughter as hostage unless your life is put to an end, they already had me end the life of my brother. [Donny pulls the knife forward then swiftly cuts the bracelets on his arm. Everything begins to shake and soon he finds himself back in the room with Jason and Lola. Walking towards the small room he kicks the door in makes his way towards Jason. Lola stares Donny in the eyes as he lifts Jason up against the wall by the throat and punches him in the stomach before dropping him to the floor.] Donny Diamond: No longer will you be carving the world into what you believe it should be! Jason Nickles: Donny... Wait... [Using the knife he took, he slashes the controls in the transporter room. Machines begin to move and clash causing small flames to erupt. Lola escapes from the room as Donny kicks Jason in the ribs a couple of times more before doing the same. He walks down the hall looking for the room where his daughter is being held as smoke begins to fill the area. Several people are seen running in a panic as he enters the room he had been searching more, guards become aware of his presence and he makes quick work of them.] [Walking into the room he drops to his knees as his eyes locate the small chamber where the soul of his daughter is being held. Tears fill his eyes with joy, just as he makes it to his feet he walls forward against a wall in pain. From behind a knife comes stabbing into his shoulder, he turns around to see Jason standing there who punches him in the face knocking him down onto the floor.] Jason Nickles: Did you really think it would be this easy? Did you really think I would let you ruin everything I have worked for? Even a monster such as yourself has a weakness and look how easy it was for me to exploit it! [More explosions can be heard going off in the distance as Jason pulls the knife out and stabs it into the chest of Donny. He rolls over onto his back in pain as he coughs up blood and once again the knife is pulled out. Jason stabs forward again but Donny manages to grab him by the wrist and fights against him, he then punches him hard across the jaw as the knife falls.] Jason Nickles: Don't you understand that this is the end for you? You should not even be alive to begin with! [He leans over and slaps Donny across the face a couple of times mocking him. Grabbing the knife and in one swift motion Donny slices it across his throat. Blood immediately starts to gush out as he falls to his knees and more explosions erupt. Slowly Donny gets up as more blood is choked up and he makes his way towards his daughter, he opens the chamber and her soul slowly vanishes as Donny falls to the floor with a smile on his face.] Donny Diamond: Forgive me Father, for I have sinned... [Laying on the floor his eyes slowly close as he see's a white light beaming around him. As his eyes close for the final time an explosion is heard and flames come soaring into the room. Outside Lola is standing in the shadows as a small quake can be felt. She watches on as the ground caves in and flames fly upward to the Heaven's.] - If given the opportunity to shape the world into an image you believed perfect, would you do it? Would you go crazy with absolute power? One thing is known for sure, inside every human being there is good and there is evil. And in order for life to be perfect there needs to be a balance of the two. What you see as a perfect world, may not be seen as perfect by others.
