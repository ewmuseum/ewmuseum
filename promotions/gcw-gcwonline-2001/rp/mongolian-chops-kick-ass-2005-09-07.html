Mongolian Chops Kick Ass

7 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-7-05 'So... You're SURE that I need to use Mongolian Chops?' 'Yes! YES! OF COURSE! The fucking rule man... Best... Move... Ever.' 'Okay... whatever you say...' I didn't feel like arguing... but damn... I mean... Mongolian Chops? Come on... I had always thought of Andy, my college roommate, as an intelligent person. This was tarnishing my mental portrait of him. 'Wouldn't it be funny if you just Mongolian Chopped your girlfriend after you blow your load in her?' 'Er... Well... it would definately be SOMETHNIG...' Damn it... he's not just tarnishing it... he's taking a hot steamy dump on it... Actually... he brings up a good question... What is the best wrestling move to preform just after having sex? Victory Roll? Spew Green Mist? Palm Strike? Monkey Flip? Northern Lights Suplex? Oh well... Andy isn't a wrestler... just a casual fan... maybe I should take suggestions from people who actually know what the hell they are talking about. Oh yeah... where the hell are my manors? Ha. I don't have any manors, I live in a dorm. But my manners... yes... I forgot to introduce myself. I am Chrissy-Poo. Chrissy-Poo am I. I am a professional wrestler. Well... I'm about to be... my first match is coming up... and well... I'm a little nervous. But I guess there's nothing to be afraid of... unless of course you factor in physical injuries... broken backs... broken necks... broken eyelids... the fact that a man named Aiden Jackson is going to be trying to come as close to murdering me as possible without actually doing it... well, at least I hope so. But really... maybe this is going to work out... I mean... its a free ticket to beat the living fuck out of someone without any reprecussions. Why the hell aren't their more pro-wrestlers? Because everyone's a pussy! You pussy! Holy shit... It's Wednesday! Wednesday World Wide... fuck! I'm going to miss my flight to Seattle! 'Andy... I gotta go!' 'No way, man... stick around for a bi--' 'NO!' And then I dropped him with a kick ass Mongolian Chop... Hey... I guess those really do work!
