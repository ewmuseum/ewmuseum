Date: 11/18/11 Kathryn Shaw had to be at rock bottom, because if this wasn't as low as it got, she'd sooner put a bullet in her brain than find out. Just six hours ago, she was evicted from her penthouse apartment. It was the closest thing she'd had to a real home in years, a luxurious place she'd redesigned and redecorated more times than she could count. It was the perfect honey trap, a movie set she could live in, and when she returned from Rampage, she found her keys didn't work. They'd been paying for her rent by accident, they explained, and requested that the contract be terminated immediately. They even paid an exorbitant fee to do so. No problem, she thought. Her backup account at the credit union had the same story -- hell, in their footage, she'd even given her phone number to one of the security guards. The same held true everywhere she went. The storage company where she kept her things. The post office. They'd even canceled her fucking Nordstrom card. As horrible as it was to admit, she was now reliant on her wrestling gig to get by. She sighed, smoothing the circular across the seat. If worst came to worst, she could trawl the bars on Ladies' Night to find someone horny enough (or drunk enough) to be led into the kind of indiscretion they'd pay to keep quiet, but only if she were desperate.
