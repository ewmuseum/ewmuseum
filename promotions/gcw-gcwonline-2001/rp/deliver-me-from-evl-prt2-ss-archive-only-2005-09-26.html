Deliver me from EVL(prt2)(SS ARCHIVE ONLY)

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/26/2005 ---------------Part two-------------------- Hello again. I wasn't sure if you would come back or not. I admit, it does make me feel a bit better knowing you are here eager to hear what happened next. Sit down, make yourself comfortable. Would you like something to drink? Perhaps tea? Soda? Coffee? No? Alright then...now where was I? Ah, yes..... my destiny. As I said yesterday, we marched to the capital city. It was a long and rigorous march and the weather was rough at times. Terrible rain storms would slow our progress more than we liked, but they would not stop us. The determination and faith these people had was admirable. I found myself endeared to them as well as jealous. What they felt for this one goal, I honestly have not felt in my entire life. It made my life look empty, devoid of true existence, and it sickened me inside. The men I traveled with only numbered around sixty but they had the will of three hundred men when one looked into their eyes. Still, I found myself wondering how they hoped to take on this great army I had heard so much about. So...I asked. ' Cedric.' ' Yes Cap'n?' ' We've only sixty men in number. Do you honestly believe we can defeat an entire army with such a small number? Seriously, this is a suicide mission.' He chuckled at me and shook his head. ' It seems you still haven't regain all o'yer memory Cap'n. There are others who will be meeting up with us to launch the attack.' ' How many?' ' Hundreds..from all over.' ' Should I know any of these people?' He stopped walking so abruptly I had nearly run into him. The look on his face was something akin to mingled concerned and shock. I couldn't do anything but stare at him expectantly. ' Cap'n one of yer most trusted allies is going to be there. Mystra.' ' Mystra?' ' Aye. Don't ye remember her?' What? Was I supposed to do lie? These people still thought I was this Eve, Captain of the Devil's Nightmare and if I was to continue to keep their faith..I needed to know everything I was up against. I had shaken my head at him. ' Mystra is the woman who helped to change your mind about the revolution Cap'n. She taught you things you lacked before. You have a great respect for the woman. As do I and many others. She is the one who prophesied you would be our savior.' Me? Savior? HA! I played along nonetheless. Appearances were important here. I mean I was stuck after all. I figured it was best to learn to fit my part and play it for all it was worth. I did a good job as time went on. To be honest, if it had been a movie, I could've earned an Oscar for my performance. Over the next few hours as we trudged along through the rain and mud Cedric told me about each group and its respective leader. I learned how I knew each of them. What I felt. My past concerning them..or rather...Eve's. Among the small battalions joining our rebellion there was Symorus, Mick Stevis, Mystra and Boragard. Evidently I trusted most of them. Boragard, however, was under suspicion. His actions and nature over the past month had been slowly changing. Many thought he was beginning to fall under the spell of Jordius. Others thought his own greed for power was changing him. I found out eventually, but that is another story for another day. The march took a grand total of three weeks and four days. When we reached the designated gathering spot I was tired, hungry and dying to have that bath I had been dreaming of ever since I woke in that god forsaken world. Granted, the rain helped, but after awhile, not even it could cover up the stench of unclean bodies. I was probably the only person who noticed it. I suppose that is to be expected considering they had lived in that filthy place all their collective lives and knew nothing else. Mystra's army was already there. A rather extensive camp had been set up not only for her own men but for others as well. I was immediately greeted with a great enthusiasm and taken to Mystra's tent to speak with my 'old' friend. Upon entering that tent I remember stopping dead in my tracks to stare at the luxury inside. Mystra traveled in style... a woman after my own heart. Within her tent the canvas walls were draped with silk swaths. The floor was raised six inches from the ground by wooden supports designed to help keep the inside dry. Over the planks she had furs scattered about. There were rod iron candelabras spread in different areas with brightly burning candles. It was all beautiful, but the things that stand out the most in my mind to this day ...the rather inviting looking make shift bed and the sweet smell of incense in the air. ' Eve! You finally made it.' I saw her for the first time. Mystra was a beautiful woman. Dark hair, amber eyes, elegant bone frame...a classic beauty. I liked her instantly. I was greeted with a tight hug and quick chattering of getting food, and clothes for me. I appreciated it all, though I saw no point in clean clothes if I couldn't bathe. Mystra saved the day. ' I took the liberty of ordering a bath for you. I can not guarantee the warmth of the water, but it is clean and the soap is scented.' I no longer liked this woman. I loved her! ' Mystra you are a peach. Thank you so much.' She smiled. I smiled. I bathed, ate, slept...it is time to move on with the story. The next morning there was a meeting to plan strategy. All those mentioned earlier were in attendance. As everyone spoke I had taken the time to study each of them in turn. I started with Symorus. Symorus was a man of short stature when compared to the other men I had come to know, but his stature paled in comparison to the inner strength that seemed to shine through. He did not say much but when he did, everyone listened attentively. The man was a tactical genius and a great asset to the cause. Next I studied Mick. He was a likable fellow, though he seemed more a brawn over brains type and I should know...I am surrounded by them everyday in the wrestling world. It is his bright red hair I remember the best, it kind of reminds me of Michael Stevens as far as color went. Last but not least was Boragard. This man was like a splinter under my nail. There was something off about him and fact he immediately began to attempt wooing me did not help his status either. It was something that had set me on edge immediately and had me being rather clamp lipped. Well, at least that was my excuse when questioned about my lack of participation later that evening. I still had no idea what I was doing, so the man had inadvertently assisted me with my dilemma. We had agreed to strike before first light. Symorus and Mick were set to take the left flank. I was supposed to work with Boragard on the right flank. It was not something I liked very well, but Mystra felt Eve was the only one who would see an ambush coming before it actually happened. Too bad I was not Eve. Mystra had the largest force and as a result, she would storm the front gate and draw as much attention to her own battalions giving the rest of us the opening needed to launch our second and third wave of surprise attacks. The plan seemed solid enough to me, but then again, what did I know? Everyone had gone to bed early that night, well everyone but those set on guard. Even I had climbed into that comfortable pillow bed Mystra had set up for me, but unlike many, I could not sleep. Every time I closed my eyes it felt as if someone was reaching into my chest and squeezing my heart. I couldn't breathe when that happened. I pretty much just tossed and turned for half of the night, and stared at the canvas ceiling the other half. I wanted to sleep, I truly did, but even the sound of a gentle rain storm could not sooth my anxiety enough to lull me to slumber. It was a hopeless cause. By the time I had managed to close my eyes without that eerie panicked feeling in my chest it was time to rise and prepare for battle. The preparation is somewhat fuzzy in my memory. I barely remember putting on the clothes provided for me by Mystra. I had Eve's wardrobe, but Mystra had insisted I wear the ensemble she had provided. Something to do with the special protection woven into the cloth. I remember having a rather nasty tempered horse brought to me as my mount for the attack. I had no problem with riding as I am an expert rider, but this beast was huge. His name was Titus apparently named after the titans due to his large nature. Mounting him had been a trick. Next thing I can remember is riding up to the top of a tree covered hill and waiting for the signal. From this spot I had a perfect view of Mystra's, Symorus and Mick's forces. It was a breathtaking sight. I watched as Mystra marched on the castle her army moving forward large cannons latched onto rudimentary dolly carts. My own crew had four of these cannons hidden within the tree line that would be used to launch the third wave. The moments before the battle started can only be described as the entire world holding its breath. Even the creatures of the forest fell silent nervous and awaiting what would be one of the bloodiest scenes I have ever witnessed. It was Mystra's voice that shattered the absolute stillness in the air. Her voice held a commanding tone that makes me smile to this day. She was a woman of great confidence, and even greater composure. I do not know if my words will do justice for what I was privy to next, but I will try to paint the picture as best I can. ' FIRE!!' The air exploded with the sound of cannons firing in a domino effect. I had to cover my ears just to try and ward off the pain from the shrill whistling that followed as the cannon balls arced with deadly precision towards the fortress. Her shots were received and answered by cannon fire from the fortress walls. How anyone did not go deaf from this kind of battle I will never know. Personally, I was praying for some ear plugs. It took only ten minutes for the battle to be in full swing between Mystra and Jordius's forces. The time had come for Symorus and Mick to launch the second wave from the east. ' FIRE!!' The roar of cannons from the east had caught Jordius's men off guard only a few moments before they managed to answer the deadly force with a force of their own. So much was beginning to take place I had a difficult time keeping up with it. Mystra's army suddenly cried almost in unison as the men on foot rushed the gates that had successfully been shattered open by a particularly lucky cannon shot. The sound of that battle cry had given me goose bumps. The memory of it still does on some days. Jordius's men rushed out to meet them as the two forces met just outside the castle walls in a mighty clash of steel and muskets. Piercing cries of wounded and dying men seemed to create a morbid symphony in the air. I was horrified at the sight of swords completely running through bodies and I wanted to close my eyes. Human nature took over, however, and I watched it in wide eyed wonder and morbid curiosity much like one would watch a train wreck or stare at the mangled bodies from a sixteen car pile up. It was not long before the smell of blood and death was lingering into my nostrils. It made my stomach turn violently. I reacted accordingly, leaning far over the side of my horse as I relinquished the morning's earlier breakfast onto the earth below. It was embarrassing to say the least, but I could not help myself. Cedric had checked on me asking if I was ill. I lied and had told him that I might have eaten some rotten food. In that world, it was a high possibility. I managed to watch for the next few minutes without much repercussions other than the macabre images forever being burned in my mind. My time to watch was cut short thankfully as the signal was made and Cedric murmured to me to make the order. I hesitated only a second as I looked to him then back to the battlefield. Those people needed my aid and some deep rooted sense of honor I had not known existed forced me to do just that. ' FIRE!!!' I covered my ears the second before the fuses hit the final spark and those cannon thundered to life spitting out their fury upon the unprepared forces inside the keep. The ground actually trembled enough that I felt it through my mount. We reloaded and fired three more times before Cedric told me it was time to join the others. My face must have turned ten shades of white at his words. I had not realized I was supposed to actually go toe to toe with the enemy. In fact, I was quite happy right where I was. Cedric was having none of it as he pulled his curved blade from it's scabbard and slapped my steed's withers sending him careening at breakneck speeds down the hill. I tried in vain to stop him. I have never had a horse that would not stop on my command, but this was not like the horses I was used to. This was a destrier. A war stallion bred and trained for this kind of thing. It explained the nasty temper. He would not stop until we were trampling through the enemy lines, much to my dismay, I had not been informed of that little tidbit. So there I was clinging for dear life to the saddle horn as I rushed into battle. You would think that considering I face men twice my size on a regular basis in the wrestling world I would be okay with this. I wasn't. Truth be told I was as scared as a five year old with night terrors. I had always thought that conquering a man in the ring would be one of my biggest challenges, but then again, I never thought I would end up in a different world. These men I would be facing had weapons. Rifles with bayonets, swords, daggers, muskets....all things that could end my existence. My death...it was something I simply did not want to have to face and it seemed it had become unavoidable. Titus crashed through the first line of enemy soldiers. He was like a force unchecked. Mighty hooves would raise in the air and come crashing down stomping a man to death beneath his feet. I could actually hear the bones snapping like twigs just beneath the strangled cries as he continued on his tirade. All I could do was hold on tightly and pray I wasn't thrown off or worse yet...shot. The previous octave of the battle had been nothing compared to the volume it had there in the center of it all. I could hear steel clanking against steel. Sword would scrape along sword piercing my ears in the same manner nail scratching a chalk board would do. I was terrified. My eyes were wide with the wild fear I felt as my mount continued to cause enemies to crumble beneath his vicious hooves. I couldn't keep up with all the faces that surrounding me. They seemed to just wither and melt into a surreal picture as I felt my body and mind begin to shut down. The terror had finally made my body succumb. I was shutting down. As you are probably thinking, that was the last thing I needed to do considering my situation at hand. My knuckles had turned white from the powerful grip to the horn. My eyes wouldn't focus. All I could do was listen and silently pray. Then it happened. My destiny came crashing down. My body suddenly lurched sideways driven from the saddle by an impacting force. I landed on the ground with such a force my breath was stolen from my lungs. I gagged for air as I slowly rolled to my hands and knees. My eyes were focusing on the blood streaked earth beneath me as I struggled for that much needed gulp of air. I should have been focusing on what it had been that had dragged me from my perch. I saw the shadow move just off to my right. I remember turning my head in surprise to get a look at what was coming on. I never got that look. What I did get was pain exploding in my face as an armored boot met with it dead center. Blood sprayed for like firework from my nose and lips as I was sent tumbling backwards from the force of the kick. My eyes crossed from the pain. Tiny white particles were clouding my view as I writhed on the ground trying in desperation to clear my abused head. I simply did not want to die. Not there. Not like that. I can remember thinking that, feeling it, willing it not to happen. It was in that moment I had my first realization. I wanted to taste love. Not the detached kind I was used too, but a true unwavering love. I wanted to get married possibly have a child. All my childhood dreams I had shoved into the darkest closet of my mind suddenly erupted forth. It is what gave me the strength to regain my feet and finally get a look at the person responsible for my mangled face. Treyial. It must have been instinct. I had never seen the man yet I knew who he was the moment I managed to lay eyes on him. In afterthought I have to say it must have been eyes that gave him away. They were hard eyes, cold and filled with a hatred that surpassed even that of Lestatt in his darkest moments. For an instant I swear I could death's hand gripping at my soul. ' Eve..I should have known it was you.' Even his voice had an edge to it. It was like rough steel stabbing at your essence. I hated him. I suddenly felt a surge of rage build within me that made everything I have ever felt before look like child's play. My arm lifted swiping the bloody rivers from the front of my face as I stared back at him. ' Nothing to say old friend?' ' I am not old and I am definitely not your friend Trey....ial.' ' Still the same bitter bitch I see.' ' You should know you created me.' ' I did nothing of the sort.' I was rapidly losing this battle of words and I knew it. How was I supposed to fight him with words, when I was not truly Eve? ' That looks like it hurts.' ' I like pain.' ' Ah, well I am glad to hear that because you are going to feel a lot more of it. ' He was removing his sword from its scabbard. I took a long hard look at the piece and very nearly ran. His weapon was not like the others I had seen. This blade as a little wider with jagged edges that looked to made to rip a man's body apart from the inside. A cut from this blade would make stitches a nearly impossible task to stop the bleeding it could cause. I think he knew the fear seeing it had caused. His smile was sadistic, arrogant even. ' So tell me are you going to die like a good girl?' ' I have never been a good girl.' He chuckled. ' Excellent. I honestly wanted a good fight. Hopefully this time you will do a better job.' ' I only get beat once Treyial...' Now why would I say that? Eve had faced this man once already, but that was Eve, not me. I had never faced him before. Still I couldn't take it back so I did what any person would do in the situation. Well, any person with courage. I drew my own sword and held the blade before me rather precariously. ' Bring it big boy.' Treyial must have noticed something off about me because he hesitated. For a split second his face melted from arrogance to doubt. It is quite possible he knew in that moment I wasn't Eve. Whether he did, I will never know. In the next breath his face was full of arrogance again as he grinned and lunged with a forward thrust. He wanted to end this fight quickly! I barely managed to turn off to the side knocking his sword away. Our eyes met. We slowly began to circle one another, each looking for that one opening we could capitalize on. He had the advantage here due to his knowledge of these awkward weapons. My only assets were my will to life, my lightning fast reflexes, and my instincts. Treyial rushed in again. this time his sword coming from below into a upward arc at angle. There was no way I was going to block that from my stance so I did the only thing I knew how to do. I thrust my sword downwards in hopes to catch his on its way up and I rushed him. Our bodies collided with colossal force as we tumbled to the ground. I lost my grip on my sword and lucky for me, so did he. We tumbled about landing in a tangled heap. The crash had caused us both to lose the ability to breath for a moment as we just kind of lay there looking almost like lovers who had just had a tumble in the bed. He recovered first. Rolling to his feet he commenced to viciously thrusting a boot into my midsection. The force of the blows had knocked the ability to breath out of me. It took me a moment or two of concentration when he paused to regain my wits long enough to retaliate. The next time he sent a boot towards me I was ready. It connected with my waiting hands. With a vicious twist and thrust I sent him to the ground and flipped to my feet. An eye for an eye I always say. With brutal retaliation I stomped a mud hole in him and finally bent down to haul him to his feet by his hair. Without thought I had slipped into wrestling mode. I couldn't beat him in a sword fight, but I would be damned if I didnt get a nice piece of him an all out brawl. As he stood up looking a bit winded himself I greeted him with a sharp elbow to the nose before grabbing his head and hooking it on my shoulder. Treyial was about know what it was like to be stunned. CRACK! He flopped back writhing in shock and pain as I rocked back to my feet and turned to look at him with that fire in my eyes. I felt the burn in my stomach, the want to win, the burning focus that made a determined soul dangerous. ' What's a matter Treyial? Can't hack it?' I probably should not have taunted him like that. He roared like a raging animal as he leaped to his feet and rammed into me with a spear tackle. I didn't get the chance to move out of the way. It felt as if my feet had been magnetized firmly keeping me rooted there as his shoulder collided with my ribs and carried me on my perilous flight to the ground. The landing was something I never want to feel again. Upon impact I knew I had sharp rocks imbedded in my flesh. Stars exploded in before my eyes in blinding flashes as my head connected with a rather large stone. My skull had been cracked. I knew it. I felt it. The warm sticky feeling of my own blood weaving through my hair caused me to panic as I lay there helplessly. I was going to lose this fight. I was going to lose because I lost my focal point. I was going to die because I was over confident. Treyial had been underestimated by me and because of that I would die in this miserable place far away from home, far away from my friends....worse yet far away from my life. No longer could I focus my eyes, the injury to my head too much for me to handle. Treyial was nothing more than a shadowed blur, his sword once again in hand was like a displaced light. He was going to kill me now and there was nothing I could do to stop him. ' Time to die Eve. I want you to focus on me. I want to know you can see me when I kill you.' I squeezed my eyes shut. I did something I had not done in sometime. I prayed. I prayed for a second chance. I prayed for this to all be a bad dream and for god to forgive me. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ' Focus on me Eva. Come on Focus!' No. I didn't want to focus on his face. I didn't want to ....wait. Eva? It took a second for me to comprehend the change in the voice. Even the air smelled different. I could smell the smog, the exhaust of cars, and even better, the smell of gasoline. ' Come on Eva I need you to open your eyes hun. Focus on the light.' Furrowing my brows I slowly blinked my eyes open only to snap them shut at the bright little pin light spotlighted into my left eye. Again I would try it. Eyes opened and this time I managed to keep them there as I was instructed by the EMT to follow the light. ' Good girl.' ' What happened?' ' You had an accident.' I winced. ' How bad?' ' You have a nasty gash on your forehead. Your car is mangled in the back end.' I groaned in dismay as I turned my head to take a peek. There is was my car, ruined by my own carelessness. I wanted to cry. ' Is anyone else hurt?' ' Luckily the other driver's air bag deployed. He should be fine.' Thank god he was alright. ' It is odd. Your own airbags didn't deploy upon impact. You are lucky you didn't suffer more injury.' By this time I was only half listening to the man. My mind was drifting into a fog. I could feel myself spiraling into oblivion as unconsciousness once again took hold.... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ' Think she will make it?' ' Only the gods can answer that now.' Oh god, no no no. Not again. Not here! I was home again and now I was back here. Groaning I tried to sit up only to find hands gently pressing me back into the soft surface. ' No, Eve, do not move. You need to lie still and rest.' ' I need to get home.' ' Eve, you are home.' ' No, I am in hell.' I knew the male chuckle that drifted into my ears immediately. ' She will be fine methinks...if her orneriness is any indication.' I merely smiled and slipped into a heavy sleep once more. Speaking of sleep, I had not noticed the late hour. If you want to hear more just come by tomorrow. Perhaps if you are lucky I will tell you about my conflict with Boragard. I think you would like that tale. I know I do.
