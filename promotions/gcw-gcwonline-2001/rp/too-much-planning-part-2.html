Too Much (Planning part 2)

Date: Shortly after. The little prick Lance, glared at him with hate-filled eyes as he stepped out the room in which RaZiel was being kept. Arms folded across his chest, sitting in a chair the kid looked like some bizarre cross between angel and demon. Golden hair falling around deeply bronzed skin with sunken cheek bones, and dark shadowed eyes. Malice twisted the corners of his mouth. That look erased all the good feelings he had leaving the room. A moment ago he’d been smiling even laughing at the monstrous RaZiel’s plight. However, one look into that face so full of darkness was like a icy cold splash of reality in his face. Trying to acknowledge the kid as little as possible, Stevens walked towards Alex. The big man leaned against a wall, his head hung low, arms slack at his sides. After the adrenaline rush of the show he was crashing hard, realizing that nothing they’d done could possibly make up for losing Sheena in the first place. Again that cold splash of reality, as the thought finally occurred to Stevens as well, taking a bodyguard wouldn’t even begin to level the playing field. Everything they’d done tonight was just catch up in a foot race, and they were still a quarter mile behind. For what it was worth, Stevens considered how well everything had gone off earlier in the evening. The two men had followed his instructions to the letter, and they’d succeeded in their goal. Almost a minor miracle considering how low spirits were, at the moment. The kid looked nearly ready to shove a knife in Michael’s back, while Alex could barely be motivated to get out of bed in the morning. Stevens was desperately trying to hold them all together. He hoped once they’d got Sheena back things would turn again for the better, but all things considered he doubted it. The kid straight up hated him, there was no way to get around that, even before Sheena was taken that point was made clear enough for even the blind to see. Still, he’d done what was asked of him, at least the kid could take an order, that was something to be said for him. Alex, on the other hand was simply crushed by the events of the last couple weeks. Getting him back up to working shape would take a lot of work, but that work had to start now if they were going to be an effective unit going into Annihilation. The kid? Let him rot for all Stevens cared. “Alex, we’re going to grab a bite to eat, you ready?” “Uh, Mike I don’t really feel like..” “Screw it, you’re coming with me. We’ve got some things we need to talk about anyway. Kid, you’re on guard duty.” He almost regretted saying it the moment the words crossed his lips, almost. Lance hated being called Kid, and Stevens knew it, he just couldn’t resist getting a rise out of the little bastard. He should’ve thought that this was no time to joke around, no time to poke the caged tiger, his sister had just been kidnapped after all, but with everything else already preying on his mind, he never even thought about it. “You fucking prick, I told you not to call me Kid!” Slowly Lance rose to his feet, staring across the room at Stevens. Face flushed, his hands curled into fists so tightly his knuckles quickly grew white. He was ready for a boil over, all the rage had simmered too long, and Stevens had just turned up the heat fifty fold. The slightest taunt would push him over the edge now, and that wasn’t something Stevens had time for, he’d had to be delicate. That was the worst treating someone with kid gloves had never been his strong suit. “Listen, Lance, just do it ok” You, don’t like being called kid, sorry I forgot. I’m not looking for a fight here, I just need you to make sure RaZiel doesn’t get loose and wreck the place. Or worse yet go running back to his mommy Vampire or whatever. We can’t afford to lose the only bargaining chip we’ve got for Sheena’s safety. You get that right?” The tension was thick for a moment, and Stevens almost believed he was going to have to deal with the kid physically. While he didn’t relish the thought of thrashing some too-skinny junkie boy, the work out would probably be one hell of a stress reliever. Lance seemed to stew over the words, analyzing them carefully for any hidden dig. Eventually finding none, his fists slowly loosened falling slack. Carefully sitting back down, he lapsed into a sullen silence. “I’ll take that as a yes. Come on Alex, I’m starving.” Almost robotically Alex slide away from the wall onto the balls of his feet. Walking towards the door, head still hung low, he opened it and stepped into the hallway. Turning his head to look at Stevens he for all the world appeared to be a scolded puppy, obeying out of shame more than willingness to serve. Sighing Michael followed him into the hallway, closing the door behind them. They walked in silence for quite some time. Stevens wasn’t exactly sure where to even begin, but he definitely knew he didn’t want to start until they were somewhere more comfortable than a hotel hallway or even lobby. Alex for his part just seemed morose, content to silently torture himself, with hands buried his pocket, unwilling to verbalize his pain. Eventually, after many awkward moments through the hotel, they arrived on the street. It was dark, lit only by the street lights, many of them burned out and not yet replaced by the city’s street crew. Glancing out from the corner of his eye, Stevens watched Alex, desperate for some sign of real life. Even during RaZiel’s kidnapping he’d been stiff and slow in everything he’d done. Almost as if he’d retreated so deeply into himself, that he was merely sleepwalking through existence. Damnit this was going to be tough. “This place looks alright, lets eat here.” The place in question a small twenty-four hour diner. The type of place Stevens had always been naturally drawn to, they were quiet, and out of the way. Plus they were almost always comfortably muted, like a home after a big family dinner, everyone having their own hushed conversations, unwilling to intrude upon the others. “Sure, not really hungry but whatever.” As they entered the establishment, Stevens flagged down a waitress, ordered two cups of a coffee, and asked for a table as far away from everyone else as possible. Receiving a negative answer to his question about whether or not he could smoke in the building he cursed under his breath. California was like New York in it’s smoking ban, figures, and that wasn’t a mistake he’d be making again. The two men sat at the table they were directed to, and for a moment simply stared at one another. The pain of his perceived failure was etched horribly across Alex’s face. The smile lines around his mouth had deepened into lines of worry, almost overnight it seemed. In that moment, Stevens heart nearly broke to his cousin and closest friend in such agony. This had to be resolved quickly, for both their sakes. Unable to meet that gaze any longer Stevens turned his attention to properly doctoring the coffee the waitress had just brought out. “For the record, I’m not really hungry either, I just figured we both needed a break.” “Maybe.” The word was mumbled, almost completely lost in the near silence of the diner. The place only had a couple other customers, who spoke quietly to one another, but still so low was Alex’s voice that the sound would be swallowed by even the most minimal background noise. “Look, Alex, I think we both know there is some stuff you need to get off your chest. I think the best place to start, is you just telling me what exactly happened?” Having looked up from his coffee and back at Alex, he found the big man staring down at his own cup. Still as a statue he seemed to not even breathe. “You know, that night, what happened?” “I should’ve stopped him, that’s why I hit him so hard, I should’ve been able to stop him the first time.” “RaZiel?” “Yeah, him.” For a long moment a silence hung between them. This wasn’t really news after all, Stevens had assumed it was RaZiel who’d done the bulk of the kidnapping, Lestatt had been occupied with pretending to care for Eva at the time. Or was he? “Alone?” “Not sure. It all happened so fast.” “What, what happened” How did he get the drop on you?” “It was my fault, I shouldn’t have done it, and I didn’t not really. I was going to though.” Now he was starting to get a little confused. Did what? Certainly Alex couldn’t be implying that he had some hand in helping with the kidnapping, could he? The idea was ludicrous of course but it was still there, worming away instead Michael’s brain. The blood drained from his face, and his voice was cold and hallow as he spoke. “Alex, what were you going to do?” “Fix.” So much shame and anguish tied up in that one word. It fell like a bombshell, and continued to hang heavy like the Sword of Damocles. So that was it, he was going to get high, turn his back on his sobriety and in his moment of indecision RaZiel had entered. Still the story wasn’t finished yet, there was much more information Stevens needed, and he had a good idea of where all this was leading now, and who had almost convinced Alex to do it. “Ok, but you didn’t do it. Right?” “No, I never got the chance. I had the needle in my hand when the knock on the door came. Lance was already out of his mind, and opened the door. He thundered in, dropped Lance, I yelled for Sheena to hide. She’d gone to take a bath. Then he was on top of me. I couldn’t stop him, I was too worried about that damn needle. I had to get rid of it, I turned and threw it out the window, and that’s when he got me.” And there it was, all laid out on the table. The two guys were getting high, that’s how RaZiel was able to get in so quietly. Get the jump on all of them, it all made sense now, and Stevens’s blood boiled. He couldn’t believe Alex would not only turn his back on his sobriety that he’d been so proud of, or that he could be so damn oblivious to let all this happen under his nose. It took every bit of will power he had not to leap across the table and strangle Alex, but he held back. The guy was easily influenced, and he had to make sure he knew where that influence came from, because he just couldn’t believe it’d been Alex’s idea. If he were right he’d have an excellent target for his rage. “Alex, whose idea was it?” “What?” That almost pushed him over the edge. If Alex’s voice had been more than the quiet squeak it was, he probably would have snapped. However, he could see how much this had been eating the big man, that meant guilt, and that he was punishing himself enough already. “You know what I mean, whose fucking idea was the goddamn needle?” “Lance.” Confirmed, if he’d been with them Stevens would’ve decked the kid on the spot. “I thought so.” Slowly standing Stevens began to leave when he felt a hand clutch his arm. “What, don’t he’s just a kid, it was my fault.” Turning Stevens glared at Alex. For the first time since Sheena’s kidnapping there was actual emotion in Alex’s voice. He honestly felt sorry for the kid, felt he should have been the voice of reason. He wanted to throttle the kid into unconsciousness, but seeing Alex’s reaction began to reconsider. Perhaps now wasn’t the best time to deal with Lance, and his hand in what happened. His blood was burning to hot, he may well do something he’d regret. Sitting back down Stevens placed his head in his hands. This was all too much to deal with right now, everything had gone to hell in a hand basket, completely spun out of control. Still he had to be the level headed one, he had to keep things running as smoothly as possible, or everything would come crashing down around his ears. He didn’t know how to handle Lance, and worse yet he had no idea how to deal with Alex. Too much too fast, and he still had to worry about Sheena’s safety and his upcoming title match. Nearly trembling he let out another sigh. “Too fucking much.” ~To Be Continued~
