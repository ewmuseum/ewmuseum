Date: Later that night Richard never gave me a time to meet him, but I figured my unfamilarness with the city would give me some time to actually find the place. However it was less complicated than I thought, of course checking the map I had helped. I made it a point to buy a map of every city I was in so I could get around easier. Just in case situations like this came up. Well okay, situations like this never came up, but there's a first time for everything. The place looked like what you'd expect out of a typical titty bar. A huge neon sign outside adverstising the name of the place, Hi-Heelz, next to a neon stiletto shoe with the heel bouncing up and down to the sounds of the music thumping from inside. Richard hadn't told me anything other than the name of the place. Didn't give me a password or anything like that so not knowing what else to do I just walked in. The big bouncer at the door glanced up at me giving me the once over, and nodded to a window to his left. 'Five bucks.' The bored girl behind the counter took my money and gave me a stamp on the back of my hand in case I left and decided to return. I headed down a short hall lit in a red that made everthing, including my black t-shirt, glow. I could hear the music thumping even louder as I moved down the hall. I passed through a door covered with just beads and into the club itself. Again, if you've ever been in a titty bar it was exactly what you'd expect. Dark, lit only by the stagelights and some less than tastefull neon lights running along the walls all around the club, and the lights from behind the bar so the bartenders could see the drinks they were making. Up on the stage was a redhead with long hair that ended just above her heart shaped ass. Nice tits too, but I doubted they were hers. As my eyes adjusted to the low lighting I scanned the place for Richard. Not seeing him I sat down at a table and one of the girls came over and asked if I wanted anything. I ordered a beer and kept scanning the place for Richard, occasionally turing my attention to the stage and watched as the redhead was replaced by a brunette. Now those tits were real. My beer was about half gone when I heard Richard's voice. I followed it and saw him coming out of the VIP area with another brunette. She gave him a peck on the cheek and then disappeared into the dressing room. He scanned the room, spotted me and waved me over. I grabbed my beer and moved across the room to him. 'Glad to see you could make it.' he said, slightly yelling to be heard above the music. 'Well like I said, I didn't have anything planned for tonight, and anything is better than sitting around my hotel room bored out of my skull watching cable.' I said, shrugging. He led me over to where the stripper had gone and knocked. The door opened a crack and a face peered out. The door opened enough for us to slip through. The door muted the music somewhat, and the bright light of the locker room made me squint. 'Hey Richard.' a female voice said. It was the redhead that was on stage when I came in. 'Ooh, who's your friend?' 'Hey Amanda, this is Mike.' he said, 'He's off limits though, he's married.' She mock pouted, 'Oh well, I still got you right?' That was mental image I didn't need thank you very much Amanda. Past the door that led to the dressing room were two more doors. The one on the left led to the bathroom, and the one on the right led to the poker game. Several of the strippers were in the room, looking over the shoulders of the four men sitting around the table. Everyone looked up when Richard and I walked in. 'Mike, allow me to introduce Bob Jones, the owner of this fine establishment, Walter Jones, aka J-Cool, Paul Capriatti and Officer Robert Daniels. Guys, this is Mike, the guy I was telling you about.' Now talk about an unusal group. Bob, the owner was dressed in a Hawaiian shirt and khaki pants, Walter looked like a rapper (I found out later he was), complete with do-rag and a R.I.P Tupac shirt, Paul was a biker and looked the part, shaggy hair and beard and a worn Harley Davidson t-shirt and denim vest, and the cop, Daniels was dressed in a simple t-shirt and jeans. Much like I was. Richard had on the same clothes from when I saw him at the pool hall earlier. 'Have a seat, as soon as we finish this hand we'll deal you in. Granted of course you have the buy in money?' I nodded 'Five grand right?' It was his turn to nod. I reached into my leather jacket and pulled out an envelope and opened it, showing the green inside. He nodded and ushered me to a seat. He took a moment to introduce the girls, whose names I promplty forgot. They all had the typical stripper names anyway. Hope, Diamond, Faith, Alyssa, Destiny, Desire, shit like that. Walter took the hand they were playing and then Richard and I were dealt in. It turns out the five grand wasn't a flat buy in fee, it was to ante in. A hundred bucks from each of started the pot at six hundred bucks. It was up to two thousand by the time Bob won with aces over eights. I bluffed my way to a win with the next hand, at seventeen hundred with a pair of sixes. As I finished my beer, another one appeared in front of me by one of the girls. They were cirulating in and out as they went out for their sets. There was a girl behind each one of us, just watching our cards. They didn't seem to be trying to cheat for anyone, just watching the game and keeping the drinks filled. Richard won the next hand, and Walter won the next two hands, Paul won a hand, Robert won a hand, I won a hand, and so it went. Each of us picking up a hand here and there, I pulled out a royal flush at one point raking in $4,700. It wasn't the highest stakes game in the world, but considering I still had some of the money I came in with I wasn't doing too bad. I watched the other guys to gague their reaction to me, and to make sure I could take them if the shit hit the fan. The only guys I figured would be a problem were the cop and the biker. They were both big and well built, of course I wasn't a slouch myself, and I was a professional wrestler, and knew where to hit people where it hurt. But I guessed officer Daniels did too. They seemed not to mind my presence, and considering I knew how to play poker they made a few good natured jokes at my expense before picking on each other. I soon joined in and was treated like one of the guys. Bob was in and out of the room, he had a walkie talkie next to him on the table and he would occasionally get messages on it, and run out to check on things in the club. One sounded like a report of a fight, another of a guy exposing himself to one of the girls. I barely noticed the time pass, but before long Bob announced it was closing time. I counted my money, not too bad, I had only lost $2,500. Last time I was in Vegas I lost at least double what I started with in this game. So loosing half my money wasn't a bad thing. Plus I did have that bonus of being the GCW Television champ. Out in the main part of the club, the lights were up, the girls were dressed in street clothes and were getting paid. 'If you wanna stick around for a few, we usually walk the girls out to their cars. You know, creeps and all.' Robert said to me. I nodded, I knew all about obsessed fans. There was more than one occasion where I had to get arena secuirty to walk me to my car when my actions earned me more than just the boos of the crowd. Apparently there were a few stragglers in the parking lot who obviously didn't get that everything the girls did inside the club was just an act to empty their wallets. However, not even the sight of myself, Robert and Paul did little to convince them to leave. That would prove to be a mistake. One of them came up to one of the girls ready to procalim his love for her. He looked like he had stepped out of 'The Sopranos', he slicked back his hair and adjusted the lapels on his dress shirt before stepping up to her. I stepped in front of him. 'Can I help you?' He looked up at me. He stood about 5' 5' if that. 'You can get out of my way so I can talk to Faith.' 'Don't think that's gonna happen. Get in your car and go home.' I said. The others were watching me to see how I settled this. 'Why don't you let her tell me that?' 'Let's not and say we did.' 'You're starting to piss me off, you know that right?' he said, actually getting up in my face, not an easy feat since I was a foot taller than him. 'Like I care. Go home.' 'Look paisan, move.' Paisian? Did this idiot just call me paisan? I didn't know people actually said that. I pointed towards his car. 'Go.' 'You need to get the fuck outta my way.' he said poking me in the chest. Then he grabbed my arm and tried to move me aside. I grabbed his wrist and twisted it backwards. He dropped to his knees. The other stragglers in the parking lot quickly got in their cars and tore ass to get out of there before I decided they were next. Vinnie Goodfella had a look on his face that showed the extreme pain he was in. I leaned down into his face, 'A little more pressure and I could snap your wrist like a twig and you won't even be able to jerk off when you get home. Now your choices are go home with a sore wrist, or go to the hospital with a broken one.' 'Okay man! I'll go, just let go of me! Jesus, that hurts!' I let him go, he scrambled away from me and got in his car and burned rubber to get away from me. 'You ain't too bad kid.' Bob said to me as the parking lot emptied out. 'Handled that a lot better than I thought. I figured you'd bodyslam him or something.' 'The old me would have. I save my fighting for the ring now though.' I said, 'No sense in wasting my moves on an idiot like that, at least not without an arena full of 20,000 screaming fans.' 'Yeah,' Walter added. 'You should come around more often dawg.' 'Well I don't know how often I'll be in town.' I said, 'That's the thing about wrestling, takes me from town to town on a weekly basis. I'll be here through thursday though.' 'We got another game tuesday night.' Richard said. I nodded, 'I'll be here.'
