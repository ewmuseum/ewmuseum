Confused

23 November 2006

Date: 11/23 Many times in this world we have to fight confusing battles. Most of the time, it’s just the way of life we go through at the time being. Do you go to Florida for the holidays or do you stay or go and visit family? This is just one example of the questions in life. Nothing at times is more confusing than that of the Bushido code. Because of this code, I have had to answer some difficult and confusing questions in life. For instance, with my partner Hope Clarkson, I’ve had to meditate on many issues in life. We were both battling out for the affections of Kawanaha Kuno, our Mistress and one of the Captains in our life. She rules over us. Not like Blaise McClancy and Ukyo Kushinaga rules over the Knightmare Brigade. Now those two are two people of honor, but I’ll get back to them in a few minutes. Right now this is about me. Kawanaha is an incredible woman. Little did I know that I won her affections from the beginning of Hope and I feud. Now that I know that I’ve won, I should be happy. I should be jumping in and out of the volcanoes of the damn Hawaiian Islands . However there is the other part of the Bushido Code. Sure you must respect and honor any wish your Captain says, but in the same time you can’t dishonor your friends, or anyone that is apart of your actual house. Hope is all those things and more. She’s my student, my friend, at times my lover, and she is apart of the House of Yoshimoto just as I’m apart of her house. I’ve done things to her and even her own family that I’m not proud of. I’ve hurt her with my words and my actions that I should be killed for. I’m left wondering what I should do with my honor, if that is indeed what I have anymore. Sadly I have no honor anymore. I have no idea how to even get it back. Then there’s the dishonor in my own family. My sister Umi’my dear sister Umi. The things I’ve said to her and my actions of hurting her, I don’t know how I can live with myself anymore. Perhaps I need to go away. Go into hiding and think about just what I’m doing wrong. It’s just that I can’t just up and leave. My commander needs me as well as my fellow partner in crime. Hope is going through some kind of change where she seems possessed by an evil entity that is causing chaos in the house of Kuno. Kawanaha needs me to keep her stable and from killing Hope. Again tied between a rock and a hard place, not knowing what to do. So here I am, lost in translation. So you ask yourselves, with all this on my mind how I can be happy. I’ll tell you how I’m happy. I’m happy that Fiery Passion is finally on top of the word and in the running for the Tag Team Titles. I’m happy that the first time I along with my student will be facing our commander in McClancy and his wife, so that we can truthfully show them and everyone else in this league, that Fiery Passion deserves to be in the Knightmare Brigade and to have the Titles! I’m happy that my student has found love in this world. She has found the one person who will fight for her tooth and nail, and even go to the brink of death for her. Christopher if you are watching me rant and rave, then you will know this’I forgive you for everything you’ve done. I want you to protect Hope. She needs you and you need her, the two of you deserve the other. I don’t know why, but the two of you do. As for me, why am I really happy, because while all of you people go home to your loved ones, I’m in the bed of a Hawaiian Goddess who showers me with everything I could possibly want in life. All I have to do is follow her orders, however sadistic they are. Now back to you Blaise and Ukyo, this will be a match based on honor alone. Hopefully my actions will not make you look at me in a different way. There is still honor in me somewhere, I believe. I follow the code to the letter, even the loopholes, that my Mistress and her bitch of a sister has found. Yet, a part of me screams to get out of there, U just don’t know how. So win or lose this match, I have to ask the both of you something, I need you to help me. I need to know how to restore my honor. I need to know how to stay good and not to hurt people. I do know that the path I’m on right now, I know that I’m borderline evil. I can’t go down that road again! I won’t go down that road again! I’m going to win over all these obstacles, and I’m going to start by defeating you two and winning the number one shot for the Tag Titles. Then when I move on and win the titles, Hope and I will defend them by the honor of the Bushido Code! I’m tired of being walked over and doing things for others because they can find flaws in the code. Perhaps my student was right when she said to the hell with the code. Yet, if I do that, what do I have to live for? I hate this land of confusion that I’m in, and that I’ve put others in. Time: Noon Place; Seoul , Korea : Keiko’s House/Dojo Just a couple days before her tag match at Worldwide against Future Shock, Keiko Yoshimoto decided she needed to return to her home in Seoul to check up on her sister, Umi. Just a couple of weeks ago, she was here before the Dangerous Games event, where she and Umi had got into a heated argument about Keiko’s life and current state of honor. It ended with Keiko getting violent with her sister, spraying her mist and superkicking her sister square in the face. Umi went down, and even though she wasn’t moving, Keiko went on to stomp her sister into the floor cursing at her. It wasn’t until Kawanaha Kuno and her sister Kit along with Faith Von Almen stepped in that she stopped. “Well, Keiko you must be some kind of mind reader,” Faith smiled. “What are you getting at,” Keiko snarled. “I was coming here to take Umi out of here. You see I needed her to convince you to do something for me and the Kuno’s, but you seem to have disposed of her already. Pity, I didn’t get do this myself.” Keiko looked down at her handiwork and she screamed. Umi was bleeding and her neck was bent at an awkward angle. It scared her that her sister wasn’t even moving. However, the way Faith was talking made The Dragon worry even more. “I’ll do anything for you, you know that,” addressing Kawanaha, ? You need not use my sister to get what you want from me.” “Even if your friend Hope was the one I was taking my anger on,” Kawanaha hissed. “I’ve pledged my allegiance to you time and time again!” “Very well then, tend to your sister and then return home immediately!” Keiko hurried to get Umi to a hospital and then left Seoul as she was commanded. Now its two weeks later and Keiko came back to see her sister. As soon as she set foot in her house, Umi met her at the door. Her sister’s natural beauty was replaced with bruises, a black eye, and a neck brace. “Well look who’s come home,” Umi said, her blue losing its twinkle, her black hair hung down low to try to cover some of her injuries. “Big sister come to beat me up even more” You can go straight to hell where you belong!” Keiko was rewarded with the door slamming in her face. The wrestler waited a few minutes and then opened the door to see her sister sitting in the middle of the floor crying. Keiko lowered her head, and had tears streaming down her check as well. “Umi, I’m sorry,” she tried to say. “I know you are, Keiko,” Umi replied, “I know you did what you did, because I provoked you. I questioned your honor.” “But I shouldn’t have done what I did. I used my wrestling training to harm you. I could’ve blinded you with the Mist.” “You didn’t though! Everything is fine now, Keiko. Just leave here, you are no longer welcome here!” “But this is my home,” Keiko cried. “It’s no longer your home; you gave it to me months ago to keep me out of the acting community. I bought it from you and I tend to the grounds here. You have no land anymore, to go along with you lack of honor. Now, LEAVE!” “You don’t mean what you are saying here! I’m your family! I came here to beg for forgiviness.” “You did mean what you did, Keiko. Otherwise you wouldn’t have done it. But you’re right about one thing, this isn’t you of course. It’s all because of what Kawanaha and Kit have done to you. They are evil, I tell you! I’ve told you this all along; they do things that make you and Hope become hurt in the long run. Look what’s happened to Hope, and now it’s happening to you as well. You aren’t the same woman that I have loved to call my sister. I don’t even know who you are anymore!” Keiko collapsed to the floor on her knees. She didn’t know what to say. Apart of her wanted to cry out at her sister for forgiveness. Yet, she knew that would fall on deaf ears. Another part of her wanted to plunge her dagger into her chest. Thankfully she didn’t have it on her at the time. “Keiko, I will not ask you again,” Umi spoke softly amongst her tears, “Please leave or I will call the guards that I’ve hired. I don’t want you here anymore. I don’t want you in my life anymore. Just leave.” “Okay,” Keiko tearfully said getting to her feet, “I’ll leave if that’s what you want. What about my things here?” “I’ve already talked to Hope, and she and Christopher are arranging for your things to go to the farmhouse. You are to live there if you want to run away from Kawanaha like you are today.” “I see you had this all planned out,” Keiko whispered, “Before I go, I will tell you this. You don’t truthfully mean what you say. I know you value family over everything else in life. You don’t really want me to go despite what you say and are feeling at this time. One day you’ll want me back here.” “The only day you’ll be allowed back home is when you finally realize that Kawanaha is evil and you leave her. Until then, you are exiled from this house. You do know what exile is don’t you.” “All to well,” Keiko responded. Keiko went to hug her sister, but she had already turned her back on her. Two large guards then came into the room and grabbed Keiko by the arms. “I know the way out of here,” Keiko yelled at them, “I did use to live here!” The Dragon then left Seoul without looking back. The plane ride back to Oahu had Keiko crying softly to herself. Once home in Oahu , Keiko went to Kawanaha’s room to let her know she was home. She was rewarded to be the only one in the room. She then laid down on the bed and awaited her Mistress. She didn’t have to wait to long for someone to appear in the room as Hope Clarkson appeared naked to the bone. Keiko looked at her questionably, wondering just which version of Hope she was seeing. “Its okay, Keiko,” Hope sighed, “It’s me. Although I’ve heard from Christopher what I did, and no I don’t remember any of it.” “What are you doing in here naked,” Keiko asked. “Mistress Kawanaha has made me her servant, and she thinks because of my actions I don’t deserve clothing unless I’m doing something that will be making her money for Kit’s plastic surgery. I was actually sent up here to see if you were home or not.” “I am, and well Hope, why didn’t you tell me Umi was kicking me out of my, well her house?” “She asked me to keep it secret.” “Do you agree with her then” Do you believe I have no honor?” “Keiko, I know you have followed the code of the Bushido through and through so I know you are with honor. However I know now that if you want Kawanaha, you can have her, you won.” “Why the hell is everyone telling me that!” “Because you always made this a game. You wanted me to fail and not win her affections. Because of my own stupidity?” “You’re not stupid, Hope!” “Yes, I am. I’m stupid! I’ll never be taken seriously, even now with this thing trying to come out more often now. Yes I love Christopher and I don’t regret it, but I do regret saying it out loud. I have nothing now. That’s what you wanted all along when you and I were fighting over that bitch! You wanted me to have nothing, well now I have it! Are you happy now” Are you?” Now Hope was full blown tears as she looked at Keiko. Keiko reached out for her, but Hope smacked her hands away. “Hope, you don’t mean this,” Keiko slowly whispered. Hope looked at her than sat on the bed. She whipped her eyes and then looked at Keiko. “You know,” she said regaining herself, “Your big problem is that you keep telling people they don’t mean what they say. If we didn’t mean it, we wouldn’t have said it. Yes, you won Kawanaha; you got what you always wanted. The only problem is you don’t know whether to be happy or let the guilt take over you. Right now, I would think that the guilt is plaguing you right now.” “Perhaps you are right,” Keiko sighed, “I would be lying if I said it wasn’t feeling any guilt. Hope, I don’t know what to do anymore. I don’t even know who I can talk to.” “I’m feeling the same way Keiko. That’s why I sought help. You got all these things going against you know, like me. Shadow has been a tremendous help to me. I don’t know where she can help you though. But maybe Ukyo can help you, or Master Skylar?” “Master Bruce, is out of the dojo for a while. I don’t know exactly where he his at. As for Ukyo, how do you ask the help of someone you are wanting to take their number one spot from.” “For Christ sake, Sensei, they are our stable mates and comrades. I don’t think they are seeing us as enemies in this fight. Besides, remember I’m the one with vampire tendencies here. I would be more afraid that they try to stake me then worry about your lack of honor and guilt.” Keiko smiled at that one, “Buffy and Xander will have to get through me if they even try to stake your ass. Besides I get first dibs on that.” “There, now I can see that Asian smile of yours. Do you feel a little better now?” “A little, you can go and tell Kawanaha I’m home. After the card, I’ll start asking Ukyo for some advice.” “And if you don’t get what you want to hear?” “We’ll cross that bridge when we get there. I think you are becoming on the point where I don’t have to protect you anymore. I think it’s you that has to protect me.” “Well protect each other, Sensei. That’s how you and have always survived and how will continue to fight on.” Keiko grabbed her student and held her tight against her. The Asian’s head resting on the American’s shoulder as she cried a little more. It only lasted a few seconds as Kawanaha came into the room. “What the hell are you doing here,” she said sharply to Hope. “I’m sorry, Mistress, the Lady Keiko wanted to talk to me. She was under some stress.” “Then you should have got me and had me talk to her. Now leave here!” “As you wish Mistress.” Hope looked at Keiko with her eyes showing a hint of red. “Now’s not the time’Leandra,” Keiko whispered remembering what Hope called herself when she was angry. “It’s alright, Keiko, it’s still me,” Hope sighed, “See you at Worldwide.” “I’ll be there, and we will be victorious.” Hope soon left the room and Kawanaha snarled as she left. She joined Keiko on the bed. “So what is your problem,” she asked the timid Asian. “It’s my sister, she banished me from my own home. She says that you and Kit are evil. I tried to tell her otherwise, but she just wouldn’t listen to me.” “That’s awful! One thing that I live for is family. Your sister can learn from me and Kit. Should I talk to her?” “I don’t think that would be wise, Mistress. She doesn’t want anything to do with you eiher.” “Well’what about Kit, then” I’m sure Kit could’persuade her to see things in a new light.” “She wouldn’t hurt her, would she?” “You have my word that no harm would fall on your sister, from Kit’s hands.” “And Faith’s?” “What about her?” “I don’t want Faith to hurt her either!” “The feelings between you and Faith are none of my concern. However I can suggest to Kit that Faith doesn’t need to go around her.” “Fine, I guess I can live with that.” “Now then, is there anything I can do for you to help take your mind of things.” Keiko smiled nodding. She began to take off her clothes and get under the sheets. As the scene fades out Kawanaha too is beginning to remove what little clothing she has on as well.
