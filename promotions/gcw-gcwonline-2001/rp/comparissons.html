Date: Today Main Entry: drag?on Pronunciation: 'dra-g&n Function: noun Etymology: Middle English, from Anglo-French dragun, from Latin dracon-, draco serpent, dragon, from Greek drakOn serpent; akin to Old English torht bright, Greek derkesthai to see, look at a mythical animal usually represented as a monstrous winged and scaly serpent or saurian with a crested head and enormous claws Main Entry: monarch butterfly Function: noun : a large migratory American butterfly (Danaus plexippus) that has orange-brown wings with black veins and borders and a larva that feeds on milkweed The Dragon's throughout time have been called many things in life. Beasts, destructive animals, icky, murders, and myths. I think people call them myths for the simple fact they do not understand just what a dragon is. Yes, it is true dragon's are murderous creatures that would kill even the smallest dare I say butterfly without any remorse. However dragon's are not icky as one would think. Dragon's can be a loyal pet and protector for any human that treats the creature as an actual creature and not a destructive animal. The same applies to me Katie O'Brien. I seen your debut match and I must admit I was slightly impressed. You're the crusierweight, high-flyer submissionist like I am. I'm sure you would even throw in some martial arts skills to see that I stay on my A-game. However the only difference between you and me is I'm the murderous animal and you are this cute little insect that really the world doesn't care about. Let's compare here. Out of the two animals in us, the dragon is talked about more. The Dragon is powerful and strong. The Butterfly has colored wings. The Dragon could care less about it's competition and eliminate it's threats with a single Fiery Mist. The Butterfly is content on eating it's milkweed and trying to woo children. The Dragon is a creature who haunts nightmares. The Butterfly is all sunshine and lollipops with candy kisses. A Dragon fights until it's final breath. A Butterfly is easily squashed! You are no threat to me Katie, and being this is your second match, I see the lovely committee decided to give you a challenge in me. It's not everyday that a butterfly, mind you a monarch butterfly can go against the power, skill, and charisma of a dragon. To be honest with you this Dragon's competition would be best with the family that has caused her harm in the Zenith's, but she would also like to see if she battle another predator in this chain of animals. A predator that thinks she is the alpha bitch in this league. I'm talking about the Wolf, known as Shadow. But alas that is for another time. The Dragon will take the appetizer before going in for the kill. I do see Katie like me you have had success in other fields of work. You've been a multi-time champion in another fed. From the looks of your records you have been quite successful in your life. Should I give you your cookie now because of all of this, or should I beat you first than give you something sweet to think about? However I too have been successful in my life. I've followed the Bushido code of honor, I've mastered the art of Kimagure, I've became one of the rare line of Samurai-Rita's and have become a seven time Weapons Use Champion. Like you I compete not only to better myself, but to most importantly to survive. I thrive on hurting people, it's my passion. It's my passion to kill off all the beautiful perfect things in life. After all all the prettiest things in the world is ugly on the inside. There has only been one I couldn't beat, one that was so perfect that I joined her army and helped her when called upon, and the one I learned from. That woman was Patti Anderson. You Katie, are no Patti Anderson. You are more like a woman who not only fell to my physical assault but also my sexual assault. Diana King, better known to the wrestling world as LLL. From what I understand she is still nursing her wounds. Such a pity that she tried to beat me by becoming my blood sister, in the end she was left in the ring, aroused and beaten, just like you soon will be Katie. So now the question remains; when the time comes and you know you are against the ropes facing a power, hunger, and anger you can not possibly comprehend are you going to spread your wings and fly away? Or are you going to fight me? Your prior success and mine won't matter in this match. All that will matter is two animals locking up hell bent on destroying each other. I will take great pleasure swatting you away like the insignificant pest that you are.
