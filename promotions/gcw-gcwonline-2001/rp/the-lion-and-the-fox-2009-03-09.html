The Lion and the Fox

9 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/9/09 The space around Zacharia Taylor clings to him like the darkness of space, constricting his ability of sight. The dark vacuum around him leaves his body in paralysis, unable to do anything but listen to the ghostly whispers of his other self. "You must be with me....or become me...." "You are a lost soul in a sea of deceit and submissiveness..." "Nobody understands you...nobody but me..." "There is no God, no Book, no reason for madness...." Zacharia struggles against the absence of strength to move and then suddenly gasps for air as if his feet were tied to a cinder block and thrown into the bay. His deep breath and arched back is restricted by the tight bands across his arms and chest, holding him against a gurney. His adjusting eyes fight against the bright sun above him, EMT's and police officers standing around him in front of an ambulance. "What's going on? Where are you taking me?" The police seem to be ignoring him but a young EMT near his head takes full attention to him, raising a small flashlight to his eyes. "Father, please relax, we are taking you to the hospital. You fainted on the alter inside, we want to run a few tests before..." "No, son, I'm perfectly alright. Everything all at once, the police and the sight of medical...I'm ok, I assure you. If you could please just untie me so I can go about my business." The young EMT looks across Zacharia's body to what seems like his superior. His superior then looks to the police officer that was talking to Zacharia inside the church which seems like years ago. The police officer stares deep into Zacharia's eyes and then nods to the EMT. "Ok, sir, we need you to sign a couple of things before we can let you go. We want to assure you that we think it would be good for you to go to the hospital, but we cannot force you. Signing here will say that we expressed our professional opinion and explained what we were going to do. This next one explains that we are no longer responsible for your health condition once we leave upon your assurance that everything is fine." Zacharia's right arm is freed from the bindings and he reaches up to a pen held out by the superior and signs the two different papers. The rest of the bindings are released and Zacharia sits up, but holds his hand above his eyes out of the sudden rush of pressure behind his eyes. The young EMT goes to say something, but he is silenced by his free hand. He gets off the gurney and looks at the police officer. He gives him an assertive stare. "Officer, would you kindly come with me inside the church so we could talk?" Zacharia attempts at a comforting smile, but it comes across as a more sadistic grin than anything. The police officer follows Zacharia through the courtyard door and sits on a pew. The police officer remains standing. "Sir, what is your name?" "Officer Bynes. Father, I think there is good reason for you to go to the hospital." Zacharia looks down at his hands and runs his fingernail through the winding curves of blemished skin. He looks back up at Officer Bynes without much attention to his expressed concern. "Officer Bynes, are you a religious man? Do you believe in God? Do you believe in His word? There is no right or wrong answer, and you can answer truthfully or not, it is of no judgment." The police officers stares at him discernibly and then touches his nose with his right hand. His actions show the uncomfortable reasonings behind his eyes. Zacharia holds up his hand. "I can see you are uncomfortable with the question. You do not have to answer. If I were to tell you that your whole life, you were taught be believe one thing, but when certain things happen and different events unfold, the question of those teachings and beliefs start to wither beneath the unrelenting reasoning of some other belief..." Zacharia contorts his face and tries to reason out what he wants to say. "I guess what I want to know is that if everything you believed for your whole life started to crumble before you, what would you do?" Again, Officer Bynes is uncomfortable and starts to move in an erratic way. He sits down beside Zacharia and finally opens his mouth to speak. "Father, I am not a religious man, but I do believe in certain things. I think most of the Bible is a morality tale for people to interoperate for themselves to get some good out of the thing. I don't think that everything in there actually, truly happen. I think that if you are taught that the Book is all truth, and then you hit a stage in your life where you don't believe it, then that is just the way our brain works, you know? Human's are defiant until the end..." Zacharia nods and takes in what the police officer says. Officer Bynes takes out a card and pushes it into the view of Zacharia. When Zacharia looks up to take it, Christian is standing directly in front of him against the wall. Zacharia freezes and stares. Officer Bynes looks up at the blank wall and then back at Zacharia. "Father, I know that you are going through a hard time. If you ever need help, any help at all, you can come down to the station and show them this card, they will get a hold of me and I will help you out. Father, just don't worry, help is always there for those that need it." With his short little dialog, Officer Bynes stands up and purposefully stands in front of where Christian is. He reaches out a hand to shake Zacharia's which breaks his trance, and he reaches out for a respectful handshake. "I will see you soon, Father." The police officer walks out of the courtyard door and disappears with the echos of radios from the other police officers waiting for him. The door slowly closes and the echos fade away, but Christian Thomas' footsteps break the ambiance and he leans up against the pew in a very casual manner. He motions for Taylor to get up, and he obliges, and follows Christian to the alter. "You know, Taylor, you and I are one in the same, in more ways than you want to admit." Christian walks up to the candle next to the alter and hovers his hand over it. Slowly, redness starts to appear on Zacharia's open hand. He rubs away at it, but it becomes more red and pain starts to ripple through his nerves of his hand. Christian removes his hand from the flame and smiles at him in a very Christian-esque smirk of defiance and superiority. He takes the paper of written notes from the open book on the alter and tears it up, throwing the pieces back onto the book. "Taylor, you are just as much of a fox as I am. You deceive because it is easy. These, sinners if you will, are so caught up in their own ideals that it is so easy to use it against them. If we are two parts of a whole, you are the lion and I am the fox. I deceive and have people follow, but you, yes Taylor, you are the lion. anger and strength, not words and reason." Zacharia looks over at the alter to see that several pieces of paper sticking out of the book on the alter. He does not remember them being there before. He slowly walks over to the book, Christian still talking. "He who seeks to deceive will always find someone who will allow himself to be deceived. The lion is easily deceived by the fox. Your sorrow and rage after Father Emanuel's death made you the cookie-cutter lion that you have always battled with. You made me the fox, you made me into what I am..." Zacharia flips the book to where one of the pieces of paper are with more notes scribbled onto it. What Christian just said is written across the page. "He who seeks to deceive will always find someone who will allow himself to be deceived." Underneath the words it said Machiavelli. Zacharia flips to another piece of paper and words are sprawled across it in long sentences with the name Trumbo underneath. Zacharia looks confused at these pieces of paper, for there are several with different lines and different names. "Zacharia, you have a greater role in this than you think. You are me, and I am you. You thought of me, my words are your words, my knowledge is your knowledge. I do not understand why you cannot grasp this concept yet. You will either become the man you have created, or you will learn to embrace both sides of the coin, and work side by side with him. And, by him I mean, Me, not God. He's too far out of your reach now..." Zacharia looks up at the cross on the wall behind the alter and cringes as he sees himself on the cross. He tries to wipe away tears that are not present on his face. He looks over to Christian, whom is smiling, but tears stream down his face. Zacharia starts to go into a panic, but then falls to his knees at the alter, begging for guidance. "Why? Why was he taken when I needed him so badly?" "Father Emanuel did not die by your hands, he was taken by God because it was his time to leave. If you want to blame anyone for the fucked up situation you are in, blame yourself, Taylor." Christian mimics Zacharia's body by falling to his knees and imitating the prayer. Christian then laughs and throws his hands out from him. "Who killed Father Emanuel, if I didn't, and Fenning is dead..." "Some perp killed him. It was just as you remember. Some masked man came in and killed him after attempting to rob the place. You made up the chase of Fenning and made me up so you can have closure. You wanted some answers, and I was the answer. You and I created a wild goose chase to keep your mind busy, to have closure. You didn't want to believe it, and you wanted to know who I was. Instead of being a good little alter boy and doing as you were told, you wanted to rebel and find out what made me tick. You didn't like what you saw, so you tried to kill me. You know, suicide is a sin and condemn you to hell...." Christian begins to laugh harder and Zacharia finally starts to expel tears. "The messenger, the picture..." "How many times do we have to do this song and dance? You find me the picture and I will disappear and this would never have happen. THERE IS NO PICTURE! You made it up. The messenger, however, did exist, but he is nothing more than worm food at the bottom of the grave that you so generously dug for me!" Zacharia runs to the courtyard and picks up a shovel and starts to hack away at the ground around the unmarked grave. Christian stands behind the grave shaking his head and waiting. After several minutes, a half decomposed hand is visible. Zacharia falls backwards and sits, sulking. "Would you give it up? He died for the cause of your own sanity, or lack thereof. He was a sinner like the rest, you didn't go against your morals. You watched him, and saw what he did, and when you paid him to bring stupid letters to yourself, you then chased him after the last one, found him, and killed him. He left him under a tarp until you dug the grave that you thought was for me. Amazing what the mind can do, turn on one person or shut off another. I had control over you at that point, not you. That is why you would think of it all as a mere dream, nothing else...." Zacharia wipes his eyes and tries to come to sense of it all. "You say he died for a cause? The dead don't know what they died for! Corpse's don't know causes or honor or reason. They know dirt and decomposition. Their reasoning expels into the earth around them. This man knows nothing of what I did, because he is dead." "Well look at you, Zachy got his gun!" Zacharia looks perplexed. "Trumbo, Taylor, Trumbo. You read it just a minute ago at the alter, in your handwriting. You do more than you know, and know more than you think. What you just said is something I would say, not the holy Zacharia Taylor. You are using your head, not following mindlessly. This is why Father Emanuel died. God, if there is such a thing, took him so you can develop into your own. Your path has changed." "I don't know anything anymore, Christian." "The sooner you accept this, the sooner you accept who you are becoming, the quicker we can move on and do some good. I made you aware, I unleashed you from the troubled confusion that you developed over the years trying to understand where you stand in this great plan of His. Father Emanuel, just like Jesus Christ and God, are all foxes, like me! You are the lion that we need to do the dirty work, and you need to accept that." Zacharia walks over to the street in front of the church and looks at the people passing by in cars or on foot. He stares at them and tries to think of what it would have been like if nothing happen, if he died in the fire or the fire never happen. Christian appears in front of him and holds up The Modern Testament. "We need to talk about where we go now, not where we could have been or where we used to be." Zacharia takes the book from his hands and stares at the cover. "I think for the first time, I understand... I need to do what I want to do, come into my own... I do need you after all, Christian." Christian smiles and pats him on the shoulder. Zacharia wipes away the remaining tears on his face and flips open to the picture in the book that he has stared at so many times in the past. The picture of the Mercenary of God standing over his victim...
