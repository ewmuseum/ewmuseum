Ashes of the Wake

Date: 3 decades ago A young man, tall beyond his age, strong beyond his ears, and strange beyond the most peers into the hole. Is it really six feet deep? Could they have messed up and made it 5 feet 10 inches? He stares into that hole and knows one day his time will end there. Could it be tomorrow? Next year? 50 years from now? All he knows is that he will be lying in silk, inside that kind of box. The box...catches his attention... You spend nine months trying to get out of a cramped little area, live your life in a overcrowded cluster fuck of a world, than die and your afterlife takes place in this cramped box. Existence is claustrophobia. All around him there's rock protruding from the earth, each of them says a little something about the person six feet deep, buried in that cramp little box. That person's whole life is carved into a 3 foot stone. Existence is short. The wind blows against him, he lets out a small gasp and a shiver. The hand holding his clamps down on it. He looks up and notices it his mother. He looks back at the box, confused, than back at the hand, than its owner. He's holding the hand of some old man. Red eyes, forked tongue, a pungent stench something furious. The hand clamps down even harder, squeezing his little fingers. His knuckles crack, and the pain shoots up his arm. The old man begins to gring. Missing teeth everywhere. A mist emitting from his mouth. He tried to scream, but its a silent scream. No one else attending the services seems to notice the giant boy on his knees crying from the pain in his hand. No one seems to notice the old man squeezing his hand laughing something hideous. He tries to avoid looking at this man, but the masochist in human nature looks. They say don't look down, you do. They say dont look at the blood when you cut yourself, but you do. His conscious told him not to look but he did. Thats when the burning feeling in the pit of his stomach began to appear. It attacked him like a wolverine, fast and furious. The pain, the burning begins to move through his stomach and up his esophagus. The buring sensation turns into vomit. Hand still being crushed the boy keels over and sprays the earth with vomit. Trickles of blood add to the kaleidoscope of colors on the ground. He feels a tug at his hair, than a rip. He hears some of his hair actually being pulled out of his head. He looks around, vomit dribbling from the corner of his mouth. No one is paying attention to this old man carrying him by his hand and hair. The minister continues his sermon, his father stand with his head down, Jason in front of him sobbing. It's as if this is not evening happening in their realm of life. It's as if he and the older man were sucked into some dark chasm of another world, a world with windows where you can still look through and see those of your previous realm. That's when it hits him. Oh my God, am I dead? 'Not yet.' Scowls the old man. 'Soon my young chap, quite soon.' The old man throws him down in front of the alleged 6 foot deep hole in the earth. He begins to nurse his crushed hand and burning scalp. The old man slithers his forked tongue and points to the stone sticking out of the earth it says: Here Rests Bryan O'Neil. Bryan looks at the old man and gasps. That hideous laugh erupts from the old man's pus infected mouth. It gets louder and louder, almost echoing in this world. Bryan once again looks for help from the congregation and to his dismay no one notices him. He begins to hear gurgling and bubbling sounds. He looks into the deep hole and notices a liquid begins to rise. It reeks of death, and is steaming. It bubbles up and pops. It's rising in the hole and it has Bryan trapped. Between the pool of death and the hideous laugh young Bryan is stuck. He doesn't move, he just stares into the pool. It has reached the top and is beginning to bubble over. The laughing stops. Bryan looks over at the old man, his red eyes peer into him. Bryan's hand begins to burn, the pool of death is touching his hand. He looks at his hand, his skin begins to peel away, the pool is eating away at him. Bryan once again stares back into the red eyes, and leans over and falls into the pool, sinking to the bottom......... Sitting on the bed in his hotel room Bryan Mayhem looks at himself in the mirror, than at his wrist, sporting his new tattoo. DeadPool.
