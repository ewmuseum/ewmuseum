Just a Smile: Part 5

18 July 2007

Date: July 18th, 2007 The motel I used as a makeshift apartment looked bad, I know. I hadn’t had time to clean it; I’ve been far too busy with my research. Though it was a mess, I could still find whatever it was I was looking for, typically after some searching. Sure, there are a lot of photos of Scott Drake, it should be expected. He is, after all, the person I am researching. But there some photos of Tessa Windsor as well. I made sure to collect photos of her as well. There are some photographs of how she looked now, as well as some how she looked before getting pregnant. It’s amazing what you can find after a few minutes of searching on the internet. As a scholar, I am able to look at my research with an unbiased eye and see just how much information I had gathered. It is impressive no matter the comparison. It piece was painstakingly sorted and organized. They were noted, listed, and filed away exactly where it needed to be; which made it even more of a headache that there were still mountains of remaining research that still needed to be sorted away. It cluttered my desk, my bed, the nightstand, and any other location that would hold papers. I made a mental note to run out to Wal-Mart later to pick up another filing cabinet. The three I had were full, each lining the far wall of the motel. But not everything I had in the motel room came from research. One piece came into my possession by accident. Yet that piece could hold the key to the secret to my entire thesis. The only problem was that I couldn’t touch it. My foot tapped the box below the desk. The lid tipped but didn’t fall off. Peering down, I couldn’t see what was in the box, though I knew it was there. As soon as I had gotten back to the motel with it, I instantly threw it into the box and closed the lid. I wasn’t supposed to have it to begin with. As a researcher, I knew I should study it and file it away just like all the others, but I felt guilty for even having it. My foot hit the box again. This time the lid did fall off and the box nearly fell over. My eyes could spy the contents now. Silk, teal blue, and recently used. I pulled my eyes away from Tessa’s panties. They weren’t mine to gaze at. I needed to get back to my work anyways; I couldn’t spend my time staring at a pair of panties, even if they were dirty. I had plenty of new material that needed to be cataloged. Recently I decided to take a new direction with my research, now including his career into my source material. I had watched hours of Scott Drake’s career, spanning from his beginning in WIW to his partnership in Banned & Exiled~! I could see his progression from being a cocky upstart rookie to’well a cocky upstart veteran. Not exactly the greatest change, but it stayed close to who the man was that stepped into the ring. Normally I just watched the recordings from TiVo, as I have no care to actually watch a whole wrestling promotion. I’m not a wrestling fan. I am a fan of the human mind, and in my years I have never met a mind like Scott’s. That is why I do all this. Why I catalog every moment, every thought of this man’s entire life. So that I may understand his mind and learn how it works. My foot tapped the box again even though the lid was already off. Letting out a sigh, I knew this pair of panties kept distracting me. I reached under the desk into the box and pulled out the panties. Tossing them onto the desk I let them lay where they fell. I wasn’t really interested in them; I mean I never even meant to take them. I was looking for evidence to my theory on why Scott smiled the way he did whenever he looked at Tessa. To some, those panties were something dirty. To me, they were evidence. I just needed to connect the dots that drew the picture. I grabbed the panties, tossing them back into the box. I felt like I had gotten that out of my system. I would file it later. It wasn’t even important at the moment; I had others that still needed to be filed away anyways. The stack of DVDs on the corner of the desk sat beside a portable DVD player. At the moment I had the Best of PTC DVD in there. Among the matches was one that interested me; Chris Storm vs. AWS Man (Also Known as Bill) vs. Global Champion Seymour Almasy. The match could easily be called one of his career highlights as Scott Drake climbed the mountain and took his place at the top. I had already watched the match in its entirety. I was planning to watch it for a second time, though just the ending. I wanted to see the moment in which he is handed the title. The event would be paused frame by frame and sketched out. Those sketches would be organized and filed away with all my other sketches after studied. As a person who focused on facial expressions, I had mapped out that unique smile Scott had reserved just for Tessa. Each sketch would be compared against the sketch of his smile to see how many points matched. It was painstaking long but well-worth it. You know though, maybe I should file those panties away first. This way I would stop worrying about them. Grabbing them from the box, I placed them in front on me on the desk and grabbed a sheet of paper. I hit pause on the DVD player and closed the screen so I could focus just on the underwear. The sheet of paper had a section for each bit of information I might need from it. There was a list for description of the item, date for when it was logged, and other such information that I might need to mention. Picking them up with one hand, I took a pen in the other and started to write down the information. Description: Silk woman’s underwear. Teal in color, appears to have been recently wore, and in style of Brazilian-Cut. Date: July 18th, 2007. Other Information: The blank area stared at me. I really didn’t have any clue as to what to put in that field. It isn’t like I had planned to put them under a black light to check them for any stains they may have. Though thinking about it, I may need to do that, for research purposes of course. One never knew what piece of evidence could lead to a major break. I brought the panties up to my face to sniff them, not for anything sexual. I wanted to see if maybe I could place how old they were. I’m sure any man of science would agree that every angle needed to be looked at. The smell didn’t seem odd, least not from what I could tell. Under normal light they appeared to be in fine shape. The only evidence I even had on to go on it being used is where I had found the object, in the dirty hamper. Why, had it not been for that, I might assume that they were in fact still clean. Other Information: It is insanely frustrating how close I feel to breaking this case study. Every piece of information I log feels like it could give me that last clue I need. Yet the pieces have yet to click. Holding the panties in my hand, letting the fabric rub between my fingers, I held it up against my skin. These were very sexual underwear; there was no doubt about that. Though I couldn’t tell if Tessa Windsor had bought them before or after getting pregnant, it didn’t matter. They were worn to show off the body, to reveal silky curves and ample bottom. I would imagine on Tessa, the underwear did just that. A man like Scott, with all his aggressive nature, would not be able to control his urges if she ever strutted out in front of him in those. He would thrust his body against hers, ravaging every inch of her skin. Then, after the act was through, he would turn to her in smile in that special way that he only gave for her. That was my Smile Theory. Tessa Windsor sexual pleased Scott Drake in a way unlike any other, satisfying his needs on a level that brought out euphoric pleasure and created the smile. That is how a person who was so cold and uncaring could smile so passionately and lovingly. All that anger, the distain, all of it washed away in the sea of calmness. It was the only thing that could explain it, and in my hand was the key to unlocking the mystery. But I needed more proof if I wanted my theory to be a reality. When I had asked her about it, Tessa clammed up on me. So I knew I wouldn’t be getting any information from her about the kind of sexual acts her and Scott engaged in. Scott may admit it in a sense of pride, but he may also be too stubborn to ever leak any intimate details to me about his relationship. He was, after all, very private with personal information. Looking closer at the underwear, I tried to see if there were any markings on it. I searched for anything that might give me a clue as to what happened when Tessa wore that pair. I dabbed it against my tongue, thinking that maybe there might still be a salty residue left on it. There wasn’t, and afterwards I felt shamefully embarrassed. I threw the panties into the plastic bag with the sheet of paper, sealing it shut for now. I would get a black light and test it later. But for now I was just going to get some sleep. This case would not get solved tonight, and not by a man who was so tired that he licked a woman’s private underwear. Tomorrow, surely I would crack the case tomorrow.
