Enter Moore

10 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.10.05 'I'm almost there, Mom.' a voice says into a cell phone. The voice belongs to the blonde haired man sitting behind the wheel of a 2004 charcoal grey Chevy Monte Carlo. He is smiling and shaking his head as he listens to his mother on the other end of the phone. 'I'm serious mom. I'm like, three blocks away. I'll be there in a few minutes.' He says. 'Okay, bye.' He barely gets out due to his laughter. He closes his black Nextel flip phone and sets it in the cup holder to the right of him. The man is clad in a black pair of mesh shorts that have the ARMY logo on the left leg, as well as a black shirt with the word ARMY on it, the American flag being the letters' background. He takes one turn right slowly, and stops the car, shifting it into park. He shuts the ignition off and opens his door, immediately met with hugs from an older woman. 'Ehren! Oh my goodness! It is so good to see you again!' The woman yells as she continues to hug him and kiss him on the cheek. He laughs a little bit as he pushes her away and closes the door. 'Hi Mom.' He says, still laughing. Ehren moves towards the back of his car and presses a button on his keys. The trunk pops open, and Ehren reaches in, coming back out with two standard issue ARMY duffle bags. He sets them on the ground and reaches up and slams the trunk closed. He picks his bags up again and begins making his way up the driveway towards the single-level house. The house is beige in color, with an attached garage. As Ehren walks up the drive and by his mom, she grabs him and begins kissing him again. 'Mom! I'm glad you love me, but could we please continue this unusual and disturbing show of affection inside?' He says. 'I'm sorry Ehren!' She exclaims. 'It's just that you haven't been home for a few months.' She walks up the drive after Ehren, attempting to catch up, but his 6'4' frame carries him faster than his mother. Ehren steps up the four wooden stairs leading to the porch, and as he does, the front door swings open and an older man steps out. Ehren simply drops his bags and grabs the man, giving him a huge bear hug. 'How's it going, Dad?' Ehren says squeezing his father. They stay like that for a few seconds before Ehren lets go and backs up, sizing his father up. 'Damn! You got old, and fat.' Ehren's father just laughs at these comments as Ehren leans down to pick up his bags. His father holds the door for him and his mother, who has caught up now and then walks in behind the two others. Fade... 'So what's this new job you had to drive all the way up here to tell us about?' Ehren's mother asks as she hands him a bottle of water. The three of them have gathered in the living room where Ehren is sitting slouched down a bit, and his father sitting in a recliner across the room, reading a paper. As Ehren takes a sip from his water, his mother plops down next to him and looks toward him. 'Well....' Ehren says with a long pause. He takes a deep breath and rubs his hands over his face. 'I've become a professional wrestler.' Silence. That’s all he hears. His mother looks at him with a confused look on her face, while his father has dropped the paper down and is staring at him. 'A what?' His father asks forcefully. As Ehren looks for the proper words to explain it, his father folds up the paper and sets it on the floor next to the chair. Ehren's mother has finally realized what her son has told her and begins to speak. 'But Ehren, honey, you've got a good career in the military. Why throw it away?' She asks him as she stands. She makes her way to the kitchen as Ehren sips on his water. He screws the cap back on and sets it on the side table next to the couch and looks to his father. 'Son, you're twenty-four years old. I'm in no position to tell you what you can and can't do. But I can say that I'm disappointed.' Ehren stands as his father speaks, a little taken back. 'Dad, they told me that the chances of me getting called back to active duty in the next year or so were very slim.' Ehren says defending himself. He seems to have been aggravated by his parents' reaction. 'This arm injury has changed things. They're going to take the people who haven't been injured yet first. I figured I'd better get job, and join a gym to stay in shape. So I decided to kill two birds with one stone. I'll be able to stay in shape in case they do call me up.' He says defensively as his mom walks back in. 'Honey, now you can get hurt bad doing that.' She says in her sweetest voice. It doesn't get to Ehren though. 'Remember what happened to that guy at the WWF thing. The one who fell and broke his neck.' 'Owen Hart.' Ehren says trying to help his mother out. 'Yes! Remember that. And then there are other people that have been injured.' His mom says as she continues to pace around the room. Ehren's father is still sitting in his chair trying to believe what his son has told him. 'Mom, look at what happened with my arm!' Ehren exclaims. 'I wasn't supposed to get shot. But I did. I've been training for this for a while now, and I'm doing it. I've already got a contract signed and everything.' 'Oh dear.' Ehren's mother says as her husband rises from his chair and makes his way towards his son. She simply exits the room into the kitchen and begins unloading her dishwasher. 'My first match is Sunday night.' Ehren says as his father reaches him. 'I've got two tickets. Would you and mom like to go? I’m sure to have a match. Just come and see how athletic and physical it is.' Ehren asks. A few quick seconds of silence pass before his father opens his mouth to speak. 'I'll never step foot anywhere near one of those things.' His father says with a stern look on his face. 'Go ahead. Ruin your life, Ehren. I don't really give a damn.' He says as he pushes past his son, patting him on the back. The screen begins to go dark as Ehren's dad walks into the kitchen, and Ehren drops his head and takes a deep breath. Fade...
