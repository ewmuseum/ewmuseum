Busted?

10 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/10/07 With the steaming hot water trickling down his old, tired body, Jimmy slaps the palm of his hand on the wet tiles in front of him in disappointment. Although this was his second outing, Jimmy was hoping to last longer in the rumble, in fact, he was quietly confident to be one of the last to be eliminated. One thing is for sure, Jimmy looked at the rumble as a yard stick as to where his ability rated amongst some of the GCW roster. One thing was clearly evident to Jimmy and that was he had a long road ahead of him if he was to get anywhere in GCW. Wrapping a towel around his waist as he walks out of the shower room to the adjoining dressing room, Jimmy sits down and ponders his next move. The more he thinks about it the more he is convinced that his road to the top has plateaued somewhat. His reality is that he needed to finish higher in the rumble to get noticed by the booking committee and perhaps a potential sponsor or two. Finishing middle pegging simply doesn't cut it in this cut throat business which has Jimmy starting to think that his glory days as a main eventer may have passed him by. While packing his bags, Jimmy's train of thoughts were broken by a loud sudden knock on the door. Not caring who it is, Jimmy continues to pack his bags as a backstage official pokes his head in and spots Jimmy packing his bag. "Thunder!" The official says loudly to get Jimmy's attention, who doesn't turn to the official and continues on his mission to get his bag packed. "You've been booked for World Wide next week my friend." The official announces which still fails to get Jimmy's undivided attention. "Who am I facing next week, some defenseless, paralyzed guy who can't fight back?" Jimmy says with a dejected tone while zipping up his jam packed bag. The official smirks before saying, "Close, you got Shaman" Jimmy quickly turns and looks at the official in amazement. "Yes, it's for the Television title too, good luck to ya." He ends with a smile before leaving the room. Jimmy slowly turns his head until he sees himself in the mirror. His disbelief at his sudden change of luck numbs his entire body before it triggers an unprecedented wave of emotion from Jimmy who starts jumping around the room from both feet as he continuously yells "YES! YES! YES!" Sure, Jimmy has fought in high pressure World Title matches in the past but this is a totally different situation. Back in those days, Jimmy feared no-one and was always confident in beating anyone who he faced. Today, his own confidence and self belief is tested day in and day out. The way his adrenaline is pumping rapidly through his muscular frame reminds him of his days when he was starting out in DWA. His first title match ever was for the DWA Extreme title which was held ironically by his very own brother-in-law, Theodoric Solomon. Jimmy's excitement that day turned to euphoria when he beat Theo to not only become the longest reigning Extreme Champion, Jimmy also went on to win every title in the DWA and become that promotions first ever Grand Slam Champion and the first ever inductee into the DWA Hall Of Fame. The comparisons are not paralleled in situations but the feeling Jimmy is experiencing now is eerily similar. Feeling like this gives Jimmy every right to believe in himself that he could pull this off and become GCW's newest title holder. Jimmy picks his bag up and rushes to the parking lot area, when reality hits Jimmy in the head harder than then wooden baseball bat swung by a Major League player hitting a home run. There is no limo waiting for Jimmy, no fan fare, nothing! It would be safe to say that in the last five minutes, Jimmy's emotions has experienced one wild roller coaster ride. From a low wave to a high wave and back down low. He drags his feet along the bitumen like a ten year old kid forcing himself to walk to school. One thing is for sure, Jimmy can't wait till his first payment is deposited into his dry bank account so that he could live like the way he is accustomed to. Jimmy manages to hail down a cab from the busy main street of Cincinnati. He wastes no time jumping in and tells the cab driver to take him to the airport. The cab driver looks back in his rear view mirror and can't believe that he has Jimmy Thunder in his cab. He spent years being a huge Jimmy Thunder fan, watching him on television and seeing him, on the odd occasion, live in an arena, never did he imagine he would be driving him to the airport. As much as he wants to strike up a conversation with Jimmy, unfortunately for the cab driver, his nerves gets the better of him and keeps his mouth closed. With the miles to the airport rapidly clicking down, the driver uses every ounce of courage like he was facing Jimmy himself in a street fight and forces himself to say something to him. "You're Jimmy Thunder, right?" He nervously asks Jimmy. "Sure am, mate." Jimmy replies. "I can't believe this, man I use to watch you on television and I've seen you at a Bloodbox event here in Cincinnati." The driver excitedly rambles out. "That was a long time ago, mate." Jimmy responds nonchalantly. "Man, why'd you come back to the ring? You have nothing else to prove." The cab driver asked with much interest. "I guess I missed signing autographs." Jimmy said while smirking in a sarcastic manner. The cab driver eventually brought Jimmy to his destination where Jimmy was able to exit the cab with just a simple autograph for the driver. Expecting to get his photo taken and asked all sorts of crazy questions, Jimmy felt relatively lucky to have managed to escape relatively low keyed. With the smell of aviation fuel prominent in the air, Jimmy's fear of flying is immediately in panic mode, even though his flight doesn't leave for another twelve hours. Jimmy's current financial position only allows him a seat inside the airport. The only thing Jimmy has in his pocket is an empty leather wallet, two one dollar bills, a plane ticket back to Boise paid for by GCW and his cell phone. With a little less than twelve hours to kill, Jimmy starts searching through the phone numbers in his cell phone to pass the time, deleting people's numbers he doesn't call anymore. After sifting through several numbers, Jimmy reaches the "E" section in his phone and comes across the name, Emma. It was less than two weeks ago since Jimmy had last seen her and he hasn't been able to stop thinking about her. He still remembers talking to her like it was yesterday, the striking fragrance from her perfume's fresh aroma sits strongly with Jimmy's sense of smell like she was seated next to him. His heart skips a beat from his nervousness, like he was a teenager with butterflies in his stomach before his first kiss. Jimmy can't take it no more, he knows he has to call Emma but what does he say to her? It's not like he can ask her out on a date, he'd have to tell her she would have to pay and his pride would never allow that. Then again, she might not even be in Boise, she may have went back home. Then it dawned on Jimmy, he doesn't even know where she lives. All these negatives pushing Jimmy into a corner while the one big positive keeps pushing him back out, his intense attraction to her. With her number shining brightly on his screen, Jimmy's thumb hovers above the "call" key on his touch pad. The force pushing against his thumb is so great that it could stop a freight train touching the key. Jimmy, summoning up all the courage and will power inside him forces his thumb down to press the "call" key. The shock waves riding through Jimmy's body is brought on by him seeing Emma's number on his screen with the word "connecting" flashing underneath as bright as the neon sign in Las Vegas. "Holy shit." Jimmy says quietly under his breath. "It's ringing." He adds as the tone of Emma's phone ringing paralyzes Jimmy's body. "Hello?" Emma says in an uncertain tone. "He-He-Hello" Jimmy stutters nervously. "It's me, Jimmy, the guy you met at the cab stand around two weeks ago in Pittsburgh, remember?" He finishes nervously. "Hehehe I was wondering if you'd ever call me." Emma replied with a sense of relief in her voice. "How are you?" He asks. "I'm fine. How are you?" Emma asks. "I'm doing pretty good. Are you still in Boise?" Jimmy asks praying that she is. "I sure am, where are you at the moment?" She asks inquisitively. "I'm out of town right now but I should be flying back into Boise tomorrow." Jimmy replies with his confidence starting to gain some momentum. "That's cool, you seem like you travel a bit." Says Emma. "You gotta do what ya gotta do. I was thinking, while you're still in Boise I'd love to catch up with you sometime before you leave." Jimmy asks as he closes his eyes hoping he'd get the answer he's seeking. "Yeah for sure. When do you fly in?" Emma asks with a hint of excitement. "Tomorrow morning at about nine o'clock" Jimmy replies curiously. "I suppose you have a ride then." Emma asks in hope that Jimmy would say no. "I do." Jimmy says slowly as he realises that Emma has left the door of opportunity wide open for him as he scrambles his mind to come up with an alternative. "My brother-in-law is suppose to pick me up, I was about to call him and confirm my flight time." Replies Jimmy as he opens his own door of opportunity for Emma to seize. Emma wastes no time when she quickly says "Just call him and tell him he doesn't have to come because you already have a ride." Jimmy smiles when he says "I'd appreciate that." "Then it's settled. I'll be there to pick you up at nine o'clock." Emma says with a cocktail of confidence and relief, added with a touch of excitement thrown in for good measure. Jimmy and Emma say their goodbyes before Jimmy ends the call. He sits in the airport lounge with a huge smile on his face, like he was ten your old kid that was given the biggest candy bar he's ever seen. It's funny how someones life can turn upside down on the flip of a coin. A few hours ago Jimmy was doubting himself and his future in GCW, next thing he knows is that he's getting a title shot in his second singles match in GCW and his personal life seems to have some rays of sunshine trying to break through the dark black clouds that were engulfing him so much that he felt like he was suffocating. While Jimmy sat in a world of fantasy for the last five minutes, he quickly forgot that he had to call his brother-in-law that he didn't need him to pick him up from Boise airport. After trying unsuccessfully to reach Theo, Jimmy felt his empty stomach rumble. The last time Jimmy ate was on the flight this morning to Cincinnati and his stomach is screaming at him to be filled with some food. Jimmy stuck his hand in his pocket and pulled out two one dollar bills. All Jimmy could afford was a cheeseburger from McDonalds. He walks to the rather empty McDonalds counter and asks for a cheeseburger. After paying for it he walks to a quiet corner in the airport and starts eating it like he was a savage from the wild jungles of Borneo. Two bites and the burger is well entrenched into Jimmy's starving stomach. Now with that burger gone, Jimmy has to wait till he gets home to Boise till he could eat again. One thing is for certain, when he does get back home, he'll be calling GCW's offices and finding out when his payments will be deposited into his account. He's largely avoided all contact with boys from the locker room area mainly because the guys get together for a drink after the shows. Usually the heels stick together and go to one bar and the faces go to another. Jimmy hasn't been to one gathering at all, simply because he can't afford it. When ever he's asked to meet up for a drink, he always manages to come up with an excuse. He'd love nothing more than to sit back with a cold beer in his hand and enjoy hanging out with the guys instead of being couped up in an airport over night. After finishing his burger, Jimmy's thoughts become caught up with Shaman again and the shot he has at really making a name for himself. To be able to get a shot at a title in GCW is an achievement on it's own, to actually win the match and be known as GCW title holder is something worth savouring. Being so caught up in his own little world, Jimmy soon forgets that he is in an airport and as the tiredness from the rumble rapidly catches up with him, Jimmy starts to slowly doze off to sleep. This is when Jimmy's worst night of sleep commences. To him, it seemed like every ten minutes either a loud noise or someone talking loudly near him would wake him. It gets to the stage where Jimmy is so tired that nothing is able to wake him from his slumber. He sleeps so hard that when he wakes up the next morning, he notices his bag had moved from in between his feet to under his chair. He assumes that his bag moved in that direction from his feet moving during his sleep. He leans forward and picks the bag up and places it on his lap and notices from the clock on the wall that he has an hour and a half to board his flight. Once Jimmy collected his boarding pass from the ticket counter, Jimmy makes his way through to the security area where he drops his bag on a conveyor and walks through a metal detector. While waiting at the other end for his bag, Jimmy is surprised when the security guard picks up his bag and runs it through the machine once more. Once the bag comes out the security guard motions for Jimmy to walk to where the guard is standing. "Is this your bag Sir?" The guard asks. "Yes it is." Jimmy replies while he looks the guard in the eye. "Do you mind if i search through it?" The guard continues to ask. "Be my guest." Jimmy replies confidently. The guard rummages through the bag for a few minutes and finds nothing. Jimmy smiles when the guard zips the bag closed. The guard then searches through Jimmy's bag pockets and pulls out two minute bottles containing a liquid substance. The guard looks at Jimmy before ask "Do you know anything about these?" Jimmy just stares at the minute bottles in shock as the screen fades......
