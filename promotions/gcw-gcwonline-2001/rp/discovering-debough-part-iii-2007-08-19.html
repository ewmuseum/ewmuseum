Discovering DeBough: Part III

19 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 19th, 2007 It had been a few weeks since Tyson's confrontation with Nick. Since that time, quite a bit had happened. He was now the owner of the GCW Television Title (whether legitimately or not, Stephen Caldera didn't seem to care), and was now on a plane to Paris, France to see someone he had hoped to never see again in his entire life. Jenna Todd. A ruthless, manipulative whore. Jenna and Tyson had dated for about two years, and lived together for one. The first year, they may as well have been married. Attached at the hip, where-ever they went. Then, the hell-ride set in. Jenna had just had Gastric Bypass surgery when Tyson met her, and had lost about a hundred pounds or so. The first year, she was still working through that. Then, she started getting attention from guys that she hadn't ever gotten. Attention from old high school boys who paid her no mind while they were going through high school. That's when the infidelity started. She cheated on him left, and right, and left again. There was no trust, but Tyson was so co-dependant on her, that none of that mattered to him. Until he wised up, at least. He'd broken up with her about a year previous. In that year, she had tried her hardest to get him back, but he'd been strong. The scariest part about this trip, was not the deal. It wasn't the large amounts of cocaine that were about to be pedaled to and from the United States. It wasn't even the fact that Nick had Chloe. He knew Nick, and knew he didn't have it in him to kill an innocent. It was more of a play card. No, the scariest part was not knowing how Jenna would look, or act around him. He knew he could get back in with her very quickly. She still claimed to love him. It was a bullshit lie, but there none-the-less. The hardest part of this job, was going to be not falling for her manipulations again. She was very, very good. .~. He checked into his hotel, the De Lausanne, at about nine o'clock in the morning, which meant it was around two in the morning back home. It was his least favorite part about flying, and traveling in general for that matter. The time changes. He had trouble doing a three hour time change from coast to coast, let alone an overseas trip. His room was very nice, which didn’t surprise him. Nick tried to make it seem like deep down, he was a really great guy, so he splurged. A penthouse suite, overlooking the Eifel Tower. He made his way into the bathroom, which had a tub the size of an olympic pool (or so it seemed), complete with water-jets. The idea came to him that maybe he deserved a relaxing bath after such a long flight. A knock at the door made him think otherwise. He walked to the front door, and looked through the peek-hole. It was a very, very scantily clad woman. “Figures.” he thought to himself. Nick was just trying to keep the image. “I’m not interested.” He called through the door. “Je suis d’j” pay? pour.” she responded. Fuck if he knew what she said. “No espeko, french-o.” he said back, starting to get irritated. “I said I’m already paid for, dickhead.” Came the sharp reply. He flung the door open, and opened his mouth to retort, but was instantly met by a silenced pistol. He mockingly put my hands in the air, waving them. “Oh god, please don’t hurt me.” He said slowly, so that the sarcasm of each word would bounce off of her tiny, french brain. “You think Nick trusts you that much?” A perfect english accent. Fucking cunt. “So he sends a girl,” He paused, smirking, “to do what, exactly?” She jabbed the pistol into his chest, as if to threaten him. “Fucking Christ, you kill me, Nick’s out of the deal he wants.” He rolled his eyes. “You french people are so godamn na’ve.” She lowered the gun. “He didn’t mention that you were extremely good looking.” A smile crept over her mouth, and her eyes scanned him seductively. He wasn’t phased. “Fuck off lady. I’m not new to this, ok?” Her eyes narrowed into a glare. “Charming.” “Oh, and I’m supposed to treat you like a queen, after you shove a gun in my chest, that you’re not even intending to use?”” He said back, eyebrows raised. No response. He had won, at least this battle. He had a feeling she wasn’t going away very easily. “What’s your name anyways?” He asked. “Michelle.” It was almost spat. Tyson had always had a knack for being able to get under your skin quickly, and efficiently. Michelle was learning that very fast. Her eyes narrowed, then she threw up her hands in disgust, and walked quickly back towards the door. “Leaving so soon?” He called after her. Alas, it was not to be so. She came waltzing back in with a suitcase. Apparently the plan had just been to get into the hotel suite. She waltzed into the bedroom, threw her belongings on the bed, and started unpacking. “Um’what are you doing?” Tyson asked. “Unpacking’what does it look like?” She responded, with a sarcastic innocence in her voice. “Aw, fuck no?” he said, while pulling out his cell phone. It rang a few times, and then someone answered. “Hello?” “Nick.” “Ah, Tyson’I see you’ve arrived?” “Yea, look “bud,” what the fuck is with the girl?” “Michelle?” Merely there for your entertainment.” He paused. “Is there a problem?” Tyson allowed himself to collect, ?Of course there’s a problem you fucking asshole! She pulled a godamn gun on me!” He could hear Nick chuckling on the other end of the phone. “She is feisty, isn’t she?” “Get her, her own room Nick. And out of my way.” He spat. “Not possible, I’m afraid. She stays with you.” Came the response. Tyson seethed. “You want this job pulled, she stays out of the way.” There was a pause on the other end of the phone. Perhaps he had won. “And if you want to see Chloe alive again, you’ll do what I say.” Nick said, cooly. “She stays.” Tyson snapped his phone shut. “Fuck!” he yelled at the top of his lungs. Michelle poked her head around the corner, wearing only a towel. “Um, could you quiet down” I’m getting ready to take a bath.” He hurled a lamp at her. .~. [Static.] [Cue Audio.] DeBough: I am the GCW Television Champion. [Cue Video.] [DeBough sits in a suit, behind a large cherry oak desk. In front of him, is the GCW Television Championship, freshly stolen from the hands of Jay Terror.] DeBough: Terror, you should really watch your things more closely. [He taps the nameplate, which now reads “Tyson DeBough” on it. He’d had it changed since Tsunami.] DeBough: So now, here’s what I forsee happening with you Jay. You’re mad, understandably so. I’d be pissed if I’d lost this. But you see Jay’I can’t let you get your hands back on this. [He shakes his head.] DeBough: No, no. Phil Atken will want a fair shot at this belt. Granted, Phil will not come even close to winning this belt, he still deserves a good, clean fight. [Smirk.] DeBough: Right Jay? [He folds his hands on the desk.] DeBough: I’m going to have to ask you, to refrain from your antics. For Phil, Jay. For Phil. Think about his dreams. Put aside your selfish wants for a week, and let him try for what he deserves. [His eyes narrow.] DeBough: Besides’you’re probably going to want to pay close attention to the type of thrashing Phil recieves this week. You will see this week, exactly why I consider myself the rightful owner of this belt. And, exactly why I told you I let myself lose to you. [He relaxes back into his chair, and puts his hands behind his head.] DeBough: This all goes for you too O’Shea. I don’t want either of your greedy little mugs around my title. [He gazes at the belt.] DeBough: It is beautiful though, isn’t it?” Although Jay, you really should have it polished more often. I couldn’t see my reflection. [He picks it up and breathes on it, polishing it with his elbow, before looking at himself in the gold.] DeBough: Ah, much better. [He grins.] DeBough: See you at World Wide boys! [End Video.] [End Audio.] [Out.]
