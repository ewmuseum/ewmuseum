A questionable decision

16 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/16/08 ?Fuck.? His mind was reeling from the past few months. Dealing with Cami's family, helping with funeral arrangements, going to the funeral, talking to the police about what had happened, and convincing them that it wasn't him who did it. Lucky for Tyson, his fingerprints were all over the bathroom, so that made him a prime suspect, as irrational as that sounds. At least until they found some skin underneath Cami's fingernails that wasn't her own, nor was it his. That cleared him pretty quickly. Mix in the fact that all this was affecting his career in GCW, and things were starting to weigh down on him pretty heavily. He'd developed an ability to block out all emotion at a very early age, so dealing with Cami's death wasn't exactly the most difficult thing for me to do. He could brush off most of the emotions, and maintain a strong, yet gentle demeanor around her family. It was definitely more difficult, being that he couldn't just up and run away this time. He had to deal directly with the funeral, and her death, but he had trained himself well growing up. Nothing could penetrate his ?wall.? The funeral was past, family gone home, and now he laid alone in his bed, staring blankly at the ceiling. ?What now? He thought. Obviously the answer that came screaming to the forefront of his mind. Find the fuck who killed Cami. That was the easy part of the equation though. How do you find something when you have no idea where to start? It's like finding a needle in a haystack. He threw his legs over the side of his bed, sat up and walked towards the bathroom. He hadn't been in that bathroom very much at all since the day he found her. Every time he walked through the door, he could see exactly how it had been that day, including Cami, slumped against the shower wall. He opened the door slowly, looking around the room, his eyes avoiding the shower. Everything was spotless, the police had sent in a cleaning crew after they were done with the crime scene. Now it was just his bathroom, but one he felt incredibly foreign in. He forced himself to look at the shower. It was still difficult to see that area, but he knew he needed to get over it. He walked out of the bathroom and into his living room. DeBough's mind changed from Cami, to his current situation at work. It was starting to weigh on him that he wouldn't get a partner, nor would he be able to do much for the Establishment anymore. Which obviously meant Caldera wouldn't need him, which meant no more nice place, no more money, and no more power...possibly no more job. He grinded his teeth, and stared straight ahead. And where the fuck was Fisher? He'd called him almost a week ago, and he still hadn't even gotten back to him, which honestly was his concern. Fisher was notorious for being the biggest flake in the universe. Why he wanted him as a tag partner, Tyson still wasn't sure about. He pulled out his phone and pulled up Brian Fisher and hit send. The phone rang a few times, and then the voice mail kicked in. The automated version. That should give an idea of how lazy this guy really was. Tyson hit the 'end' button without leaving a message. If he was worth anything, he'd call him back. He shoved the phone down into his pocket again in disgust and almost instantaneously, it rang. He pulled it back out and looked at the caller ID. "Restricted Number." "What the fuck?" Tyson breathed, and hit the answer button. "Tyson here." "Who the fuck is this? Tyson who?" Tyson instantly recognized the tone. "Fish, it's DeBough." "I don't know any Doughs." Tyson rolled his eyes, and thought to himself. ?Ugh. Idiot.? "It's Tyson DE-BOUGH. Like bow and arrow." "Hold on a second." He could tell Fisher pulled the phone away from his mouth but he could still hear him clear as day. "Baby, baby, stop for a second. Get your tongue outta there." Tyson sighed. "Alright. Come again. No, baby, not you." "Pay attention shit-head. It's Tyson. DeBough." He spat the words into the phone with as much diction as he could possibly muster. There was a pause. Not too long. Not too short. But very noticeable. Tyson could only imagine what Fisher was thinking before he said: "DeBough? Fuckin' Dice?" Tyson heaved a heavy sigh, feeling more and more like this was not a good idea. "Yea you tool, Dice. I don't go by that anymore though, so you can just call me Tyson." "You'll always be Dice to me, homeboy. 'Member that time we rolled on them cats in Summer Games?" "Fuck yea man." His mind thought back to those days. Back when he was on top of his game. "Those were some good times back then." He paused. "Actually, that's a bit of why I called." "I know why you called, Dice, but I was hoping you'd forget." Another pause. "He knew?" He thought to himself. "Um...what do YOU think I'm calling for, because I'm pretty sure you weren't a mind reader last time I saw you, and I'd bet money you still aren't one." "Don't play coy with me, homey. It stopped snowin' and you need some of that winter wonderland. But I don't act in that scene no more." It took Tyson a minute to register what he meant, but when he did he immediately righted Fish's thinking. "Nah dude, I'm clean. Been out of that for a few months now and going strong." "I'm applauding you in an alternate universe." There's an awkward pause before Fish gets curious. "So, you don't want coke. What the eff you want then, dude? Don't tell me what I think you're gonna tell me." This time DeBough was pretty sure Fish knew what he was actually thinking. He was already falling off the edge at this point, so he just dove in headfirst. "Ever think about makin' a comeback Fish?" The line went dead silent. There weren't any out-of-place sexual comments. No wisecracks. Not even the faint sound of a breath. All DeBough heard was the static-y void from cell phone to cell phone. But Fish is a big cat wearing the guise of a human. And curiosity has gotten him every bit of success and failure he's ever seen. "No. But I'll always listen to some bullshit proposals." For Fisher, that was a positive response so Tyson plunged forward. "Look, long story short I need a tag partner. Shits not going so hot for me in the fed I'm currently in, and I need your help." That wasn't the most convincing argument ever, but he knew it would at least appeal to Fisher's curiosity, which meant he'd be able to explain further. "Explain further." Tyson takes a deep breath. "I'm involved in the powerhouse stable in the company. We run the entire place, do what we want, when we want. The president of the fed is our backer, so as you can imagine, that leaves us with quite a bit of play room." He pauses, letting that sink in. "Well, I'm sort of what you call the weak link of the group. Haven't been too focused because of some personal shit, so Steve got in contact with me and gave me the freedom to pull someone new in to work with me. So, naturally, I called you." "That's very flattering, Tice." Fisher paused, probably for effect. "You see what I did there, with your name and Dice? Combined the two?" Tyson didn't respond. He just tapped the phone on his forehead in irritation. "Yeah, I thought it was clever too. So, DeBough, was I your first choice?" Tyson had though this through. Obviously Fisher wasn't his first choice, but he'd rehearsed this answer because he knew he'd ask. "Yea man. Of course." And he pulled it off. Smooth, no hiccups. Sold it well. He held his breath to see if it worked. "You're not a very good liar." Tyson shook his head and thought about hanging up the phone then and there. "But..." Fisher interjected into his thoughts. "Well, here's the thing." Tyson removed his finger from the 'end' button, and perked up a bit. "Could he seriously be considering this?" he thought to himself. His optimism deflated a bit, remembering who he was on the phone with though. Fisher wasn't the most reliable person so even if he said yes, that still didn't mean he'd show. "Even if I say yes, you know that doesn't mean I'll show, right?" He smacked his forehead. "Yea Fish, I know." "So with that in mind, you need to convince me to not only say yes, but to show the fuck up. 'Cuz I'll be honest, Dice, not only have I not been in a ring in years, I've started this nice little film production company and I keep quite busy with it. Perhaps you've heard of it ? Lost Toys Productions? Yeah, we make porn. And you wanna pull me out of this little fetish fantasy camp I've been in since 2006? You, sir, have your work cut out for you. How do plan on convincing me to do this? And choose your words carefully, my friend. I've become quite the connoisseur of lame pitches, as you might imagine being in this business." Tyson walked around his counter to one of the bar stools in his kitchen and sat down. "Free promotion of your porn in GCW?" That was a flat out lie, and he knew it. Caldera would never agree to something like that, but he had no idea how to respond with anything that would pique Fishers interest so much that he'd not only say yes now, but show up. "Tice, you should know porn promotes itself. Have you been on the internet lately?" Fisher paused. It sounded like he moaned. "Tell ya what..." And here it came. The patented "Brian Fisher Promise." The one he gave verbally, but never followed through on because, well, he didn't have to. Tyson slumped his shoulders a bit, and awaited the empty words. "You tell me when. You tell me where." Fisher paused, definitely for effect. "And I'll be there." "..." "On one condition." His hopes didn't go up at all as he said, "And that would be?" "You have to be in one of my movies." Tyson's excitement rose a bit, but then dropped again quickly. "I'm not doing a gay porn Fish." "Don't act like you've never considered it, Dice." "Fuck off Fish. I'm not doing it." "Chill out, ese. It's not gay porn. I had Mr. Marcus signed, sealed and delivered and he backed out last minute. Everyone's booked and I need someone. You do me a solid. I'll do you a solid." Fisher paused to give DeBough a few moments to think. "Deal?" Tyson paused for a moment, mulling over any other loop-holes he needed to cover. "Girls hot, right?" "Definitely. One of 'em has a lazy eye, but she gives the best foot-jobs in the business so that makes up for it. We cool? We straight?" Tyson was a bit more encouraged by this type of an offer from Fish. If he did something for Fisher, and it turned out to be legit help, then Fisher really would owe him. "Yea, we straight Fish.? "Goody gumdrops. I'll talk to you soon then, Dice." DeBough was ready to say thanks and goodbye and whatever else but Fisher cut him off. "Oh, and Dice." "Yea?" "Your fluffer is a guy. War out." And he hung up. Tyson gripped the phone harder as Fish's last words echoed through his brain. "FUCK!"
