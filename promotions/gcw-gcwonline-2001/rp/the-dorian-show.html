The Dorian Show

Date: Today “Well, Mr. Ryan, even though I don’t approve of you continuing to wrestle while recovering from your knee injury, I’m happy to see that you are taking a little off of your workout schedule and that your knee is as healed as it ever will be.” The orthopedic surgeon that initially wanted to cut into Dorian Ryan’s knee is now telling him that it isn’t going to heal anymore. The partial tears in his knee ligaments have recovered. Finally, for Dorian Ryan, he gets some good news in his life. “So, that means that I don’t need the hard brace anymore?” “I would recommend that you continue to use some sort of brace on that knee whenever you’re in the ring, but you don’t need that bulky piece of plastic and steel.” “Good.” Dorian stands up from the chair that he was sitting in and shakes the doctor’s hand. “Thank you very much, doc.” “Just be sure to take it easy for right now when you’re not in the ring.” A little later in the day, Dorian returns to his apartment and is looking through his mail. Bills, coupons, newest issue of Superstar magazine with that contains an interview with Dorian taken before Dangerous Games, latest issue of Sport Bikes magazine, and a package marked URGENT! As Dorian doesn’t take too well to envelopes are that are marked in all caps, he tosses that one aside first and foremost. “Let’s see how much the utilities are going to run me this month.” Dorian says aloud, not realizing that there is a camera hidden in his ficus. Dorian then sets the magazines and his keys down on his counter and opens the first bill. “Shit. That’s it, I’m switching to satellite.” Dorian then proceeds to open the next bill. “What the fuck is a USF?” Dorian throws that bill down and walks over to the couch in his living room, where he lies down and closes his eyes to try and take a nap. But this just wasn’t the time for that as his phone starts to ring. Dorian, slightly cranky gets up and answers the phone. “Hello?” He takes a moment to hear what is said on the other end, and then responds. “Sorry, but I’m pleased with my long distance service. I don’t think that you can offer me a better deal than what I’m currently getting.” Dorian doesn’t take too much time in hanging up the phone and heading back to the couch to take a nap. Again, Dorian is denied a chance to get a little bit of extra rest, by the sounds of his upstairs neighbors? creaking bed, and their moans. Now Dorian is getting pissed. He gets back up and walks into the kitchen, opening up the pantry, and grabs a broom. He then goes into his bedroom, and can be heard pounding the broom on the ceiling. “Hey, there are people trying to sleep down here. Can you please fuck quieter?” If there was anything that Dorian Ryan wasn’t good at when he is trying to get some rest, it would be subtle. But that didn’t bother him too much, as they got quiet. “For fucks sake. I hate my neighbors.” For a moment, there was silence, that is until you hear a door close and the sound of a stream of water hitting a larger bowl of water. You could only imagine where Dorian Ryan was at this very moment. Then again, with the sound of a toilet flushing and then running water, you didn’t need to. Dorian came back into his living room and again tried to get in at least a few minutes of a nap when his phone started to ring again. Dorian rolls his eyes into the back of his head as he heads back over to answer it. “Hello.” Again, Dorian is silent as he listens to the person on the other end. “Listen, I don’t care what you’re offering, I’m not going to change my internet service. I’m sure that it’s faster, but I’m not some loser that sits at home pretending to be a pro wrestler in some fake internet wrestling league, while downloading Hentai and playing World of Warcraft. So if you don’t mind, I’ll stick with my slow ass DSL connection.” Again, Dorian hangs up the phone and tries to get a nap. He doesn’t even get halfway to the couch when his phone rings. Right about now, he’s thinking that he should have invested in an answering machine, caller ID, voice mail, NyQuil. He heads back over and picks up the phone. “Hell??listen, I do not want to subscribe to the paper. I’m home two, maybe three days a week, if I’m lucky. And when I’m home, if I want to read the news, I go to CNN.com, or if I want a fair and balanced view of the bullshit that the media presents, I watch the Fox News Channel.” Dorian again hangs up the phone and unplugs it from the phone jack. He then reaches into his pants pocket and pulls out his cell phone, opening it and holding down the power key so that it will shut off as well and places it on his counter. Dorian then walks into his bedroom and is in there for a moment. He then comes back out carrying the phone that was originally in the bedroom. Dorian then lays back down, shuts his eyes, and for a moment thinks that he’s finally going to have some peace and quiet. But that wouldn’t make for something interesting for the viewers at home, or the people in the IWC reading the recap, so you know that there is a knock at the door. Yes, we are evil. Dorian, of course, is now really cranky. He’s probably going to walk up to that door, answer it, and rip whoever is on the other side’s head off. Even if he wanted to, he couldn’t. As he answers the door, the three most feared words in the English language are heard. “Honey, I’m pregnant.” OK, so we were lying, those weren’t the words he heard, they were even worse. They were words that not even Satan himself could stand up to without submitting. Yes, these are the words that he heard. “Girl Scout cookies.” Yes, we are going to hell. “Thin mints.” Awwww, he bought some. He did his good deed for the day, giving the young lady selling the cookies a ten and receiving a couple of boxes of Thin Mints. She walks away happy as her father walks up to the door. “Thank you sir, now she?” Dorian doesn’t care, as he shuts the door in the man’s face. He walks back into his kitchen, opens the freezer and tosses the two boxes of cookies in. A steak that he was going to have for dinner for tomorrow night falls out. “Fuck it.” Dorian closes the freezer, picks up the steak and places it in the refrigerator. Closing the refrigerator, he starts to hear the upstairs neighbors going at it again. He rolls his eyes into the back of his head again and walks back towards the door of his apartment. He leaves the apartment and a few moments later, you hear a pounding on the door upstairs. “Will you two please give it a break?” They stop. And a moment later you hear a door opening. “You sick son of a bitch, put some clothes on.” Hmmm, I wonder, when we go to hell, will I see Elvis there. You hear a door close and again, there is silence for a moment. Then you hear the sound of a locked door trying to be opened. “SHIT! Where’s my phone” OH FUCK!” Nope, I’ll be stuck listening to the Spice Girls. The sounds of the two people making? wait, this is already rated R for language, the sounds of the two people fucking upstairs starts up again. About ten minutes later, the sound of the door unlocking is heard as Dorian is finally able to get back into his apartment. He just finally submits to the fact that he isn’t going to get a nap, grabs his keys, his cell phone, and leaves the apartment. Dorian Ryan is coming out from the barber shop, his hair much shorter than the shoulder length hair that he had less than a half hour ago when he entered. In fact, the shorter hair looks better. Of course, we took control of the traffic cameras near it. We love our jobs here. Dorian is walking towards his car and gets in. He turns the key, but the block doesn’t turn over. I’m sure that if we had a camera in there?.oh wait, we do. Cue up camera two. Much better, we are embedded in his speedometer. This is like the Truman Show, isn’t it? Only, our ending is much better. Trust me, we’ve seen the Truman Show a million times to learn where to hide cameras. But there is Dorian, pissed. Not because his car won’t start, but because he cancelled his roadside assistance yesterday. Then again, he remembers, he forgot to cancel the roadside assistance that came as a free trial on his cell phone. He goes to call it. Ha! You think that with the day that he’s had that was going to happen. I don’t think so. “Where the fuck are my bars” Largest network my ass.” This is what we call a karmic bitch slap. Isn’t it great. Dorian opens up the car door to step out. FOOL! Of course, it’s ripped right off the hinges by a car exceeding the speed limit by at least ten miles an hour. “Great.” Oh, and by the way, he now has full service on his phone as it rings. He doesn’t recognize the number, but recognizes that it’s a Minneapolis area code. “Hello?” Dorian’s eyes grow wide as he realizes who’s on the other line. I told you the ending was better than the Truman Show.
