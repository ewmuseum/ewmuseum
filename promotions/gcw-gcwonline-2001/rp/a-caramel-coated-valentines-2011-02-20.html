A Caramel Coated Valentines

20 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/11 ~~Valentines Day 2006~~ A roaring fireplace casts flickering light upon the beautiful voluptuous body's of Jenny and Gabrielle Ignito. The two of them radiate happiness as they hold hands while Gabrielle rests her head on her wife's shoulder as they gaze upon the slowly burning fire and sip from their wine as they quietly (for now) celebrate Valentines Day. Upstairs in Micah's bedroom he sits on his bed with his step sister Carmella by his side. Carmella grasps the TVs remote in her hand as she flicks through the various Valentine themed movie's that currently play out on every channel. Carmella: There's never anything on this time of the year. Micah just nods his head as Carmella finally settles on a channel which broadcasts a movie where the two love birds to be sit upon a bed in a state of undress and confess their undying love for one another. Carmella: How sweet. Micah: Awww little Carmella getting all soft on me is she? Carmella: No! Carmella turns to her brother and sticks her tongue out at him. Micah: What happened to my tomboy sister? Carmella: She was showing her big brother up! Again Carmella teasingly sticks her tongue out at Micah who responds by throwing his arms around her and tackling her to the bed. Micah: That'll be the day sis! Carmella giggles before kissing her brother on the forehead and admiring those diamond like eyes that had always confounded...and tempted her. Micah gently caresses her arms and finds himself slowly looking her body up and down, only a pair of short shorts and a baby tee currently cover her deliciously caramel coated and curvy figure. He cant help but lick his lips as his eyes follow up along every contour that is a blessed gift from her mother Gabrielle. She smiles up at him and giggles prompting him to lean down and kiss her...on the lips, their tongues wrestling against one another's in an embrace that was just the first of many. ~~Present Day~~ Carmella stirs from her blissful slumber with a broad grin upon her face, a yawn that sounds more like a moan escapes her lips as she presses a kiss to one of her lovers chests. This gentle embrace causes Amanda to stir and run her hands through Carmella's long brunette hair, the two women smile before turning their attention to Micah who proudly watches the two beautiful women in their loving embrace, bodies intertwined as they tease him. Micah: You two are insatiable. Amanda: You're one to talk Micah. Carmella: I'm surprised you let us sleep brother. Micah: Never say I don't look out for my little sister...and Alex's sister as well. Carmella: One big happy family...right. Carmella and Amanda both playfully giggle before Micah leans over and picks his sister up off from Amanda and plonk's her down on his other side, happily sitting between the two lovely girls who cuddle up to him. Amanda: Just with a few more perks than than the average family. Micah: Well I'm glad one of you considers them to be perks. Amanda: I don't think she enjoyed her spanking as much as we did. Carmella: Well I never said stop did I? Carmella giggles again as she rubs at her posterior. Amanda: Naughty girl! Micah slides his arms around both women and they kiss him on the cheek. Amanda & Carmella: Happy Valentines Day Micah.
