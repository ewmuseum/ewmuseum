Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/04/11 :: The Following is a MTV Production:: Voice : Welcome to MTV Cribs :: The Camera Shows the blueprints for a House Outside of Las Vegas in the desert, Surrounded By a 10 foot Lion Claw fence that reaches around the 100 Acre estate. The Land has a Man Made Lake in the Back Surrounding it and The Home includes a ten car garage 10 bedrooms 4 bathrooms and 4 Guest Houses, As a commericial approaches It States the 2 million dollar home was purchased by none other than Wrestling great "PlayBoy" Johnny Davis As the scene fades to Black:: :: Scene reopens as the tour Begins at the front Door :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Im Playboy Johnny Davis and Welcome to my Home. As You see Here it's very Spacious and I went with the standard Saguaro cactus out front and the three traditional Bird Baths. As I take you inside you see the 4 Story spiral Staircase with Replica's of all the titles I won throughout my career, Cause I had to give the real one's Back and to You kids at home, The Titles are real Gold. As you see the front Dining room has classic Portraits of Wrestlers that Ive meet on my wonderful wrestling journey. The one over on the left wall is of me and my former tag team champion partner James the Ladies man slade. And on the right, Is a Picture of my comeback from Injury, It shows me with my brace on going through rehabilitaion. And now lets enter the Kitchen. :: Camera Pans to show a All marble counter top that goes through the whole kitchen, extending to the bar. On the backside, With a all stainless Refregirator and stove, With Matching Microwave :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Well let me show you inside the Fridge, And as You can see theres nothing but Bud Light and Crunk Juice. Thanks to my friend Lil Jon from the ATL for the Hook Up. Gotta have Crunk Juice before Every Match. And it's Real Good after Sex Too. Woops Can I say That. Well lets move on to the Master Bedroom. :: Camera Pans up the Stairwells all the way to the top level which is the 4th floor of the Las Vegas Mansion as the camera enters there's a slew of Historical Championship Belts on the wall along with Magazine covers from ages Past. Playboy Johnny Davis : Well as You see here I have trophies from the GWA, WWA, And JPW when I wrestled in Japan but my favorite is the 3rd place trophy from the 2000 King of Extreme tournament with 64 of the best Hardcore Champions of all Time and ill give my props to Boston Strangler who defeated me.As the rockstars say it, This is where the magic happens .As You see there's leopard skin cause ya'll know im a true ladies man. The Carpet in here is Gucci carpet which is limited, The Picture on the nightstand is of me and Hugh Heffner at my first Bunny Party at the PlayBoy Mansion back in 99', That was a fun time. But enough in here, lets go to my clothing area. Voice : Well see were the Champ Keeps his clothes when we return. :: Camera Fades into the scene in the hall way :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Well since I live alone and have 10 bedrooms I made one for nothing but clothes. As You see I added shelving through the whole room, and to my pleasure I have 5 clothing brands that ship me my warddrobe. And 3 NBA teams and 2 NFL teams that make sure i have whatever I need when it comes to fashion. I get 10 outfits a week from, Nike, same for Adidas, Gucci, Versace, Phoenix Suns Appearal which is through reebok. Shoes. I have every Pair of Adidas Shell toes ever made along with my 600 wrestling outfits, Some dating back to 1997 when i was a rookie. As You seen enough in here I have a Special Surprise. :: Camera follows Johnny Davis Who is walking quickly to a Pantry upstairs where he pushes a secret button that appears to open shutters to a hidden elevator as the elevator opens he tells everyone to come on in :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Ok so I lied there's 5 floors but the first floor is underground. As you can see where to my special area. :: Camera Reveals a Full size Unground gym/arena that is gracious enough to hold a house card. Surrounded by Big screens and Weight Benches Is a full size wrestling ring in the middle. There's a PA Stage where it looks like it is used for concerts :: Playboy Johnny Davis : This is my Spacious area where I dwell, I practice and get ready when Im here which is rare, I hold parties and concerts in here, But this isn't nothing let's hurry and finish this up I have a Promotion to do for WorldWide in 30 minutes. Follow Me. :: The camera follows PlayBoy into a door which appears to be a seaworld type tunnel that is under the pool :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Yes we are in a glass pool, look in so if your at a Playboy Party with me sneak down here, And take a Peek because MI CASA es Tu' Casa And this is definetly the best view for the Party. But Lets Go Outside. If you follow me the tunnel turns into a Stocked underwater Bar but there's now way out this way and we will go outside through the elevator to the 1st level and then to the Pool Lake area. :: Camera Goes to a commercial :: Playboy Johnny Davis : We are Outside here on my Massive Property, It's a little to early for the pool and Grotto or a ride on the Jet ski's or Barge but Let's go to the Ten Car Garage and look at my Toys :: Camera Follows into the massive Hide Away were there is 5 cars under covers :: Playboy Johnny Davis : The First one is my Escalade 2001 with the 26'' spinners that is for when me and the Fellas want to get some ladies, we scoop them up in here and there's plenty of Room. And This next one is my Beast! MY 2005 H2 with the 20'' rims with the off road tires. This baby takes You anywhere, And the next one is my " FRIENDS " Birthday gift she is turning 21 next month, As it is a 1998 SE Dodge Viper, Now for all you punks that want to Race on the streets, I run the Baddest of all baddest which is my 69' chevelle SS Fully Loaded, The Motor is Borred over along with Nitrous into the 454, This thing is amazing and of course the Lamborghini Diablo every man needs one. Voice : But why's there a 10 car garage, Playboy Johnny Davis : I loan my other 5 cars out to rappers for music videos along with my 2 CBR'S and helicopter. Well you seen my Crib Now get the hell Out. :: Phone Rings and Johnny Davis Answers it :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Hello Voice : Hey Man it's me Playboy Johnny Davis : Who? Voice : Man It's Damian Nigga. Playboy Johnny Davis : Aright Bro, Wassup. Damian : Did You see the Promo that GCW Cut about you being 0-2 in GCW? Playboy Johnny Davis : Nah Not Yet, Ill Check It out, Thanks Bro Damian : No Problem, Stay Up Nigga. Playboy Johnny Davis : Talk to ya Later. :: PlayBoy Hangs up the Phone and goes to the Kitchen and takes a Shot of Patron , He Then Picks up his Phone again and dials :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Hey I need a Film Crew in My Basement in 20 Minutes Dale Gettem here NOW. :: Playboy Throws his House Phone across The Room and Goes down into his Basement and changes his outfit for TV viewing, He now is wearing Full Wrestling Attire From Pants to Boots as he then Gets the GCW promo on his big Screen, After Watching it he then Jumps into his Ring and Tosses around some Steel Chairs and Begins to Hollar, He then Picks Up his Phone and Dials Again :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Dale GET THEM HERE NOW!!! :: As soon as that is said the Crew rushes through the Front Door and Prepares to Broadcast :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Alright this goes out to all you Punks in GCW, Johnny Davis is just now getting in the swing of things, I can't stand to see Shaman prancing around in a loin cloth posing as the GCW Hardcore champ, My Plates full right now, I just won round 1 of the cwc Asscencion Tornament 3 rounds to go, But that has nothing to do with my GCW record, Im here to stay, Shaman Ill get back at ya real soon,Don't be upset when i kick down your teepee door and Choke you out next to a Indian Rain dance ceremony, But enough with him,This Week it's Brad Cruz and Toga, First Toga, FRAT BOY?!?!? WHAT THE HELL. This is gonna be a pleasure, And Brad Cruz, Im gonna put it out there right now, You rookies stand no chance against the king of Extreme, THE LADIES Dream!!! Playboy !!! Johnny!!! Davis !!!. I in myself am a throwback, But I take a Lickin' and Keep on tickin, Just like your sleezy women, HAHA.... All I can say is come Worldwide, The shit is gonna hit the fan!! and I will be victorious and then from there NC-17 Shaman, YOURE,NEXT and if anyone once some they can get it!!!! Im Pissed and Now it's on... :: Johnny Davis stands up and does a backflip landing on his feet and then points at the camera :: Playboy Johnny Davis : Im a Vetran Wrestler who has Traveled the World Wrestling in Places You can't Pronounce. And Im a Extreme Legend in the Ring and in the Bed, Ask Your Woman She Knows.... That's all I have to say for right now. IT'S TIME TO GET X-RATED! Your next Hardcore Champ has spoken!!!. :: The Camera Crew Rolls out as Johnny Davis Checks His Schedule fo see whats in store for tommorow ::
