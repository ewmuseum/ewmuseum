The walls were exposed concrete, the floor tiled but patchy. Old posters of boxers splattered the walls, along with announcements for upcoming fights. Keegan’s taped hands pounded away on the bag, working on his punching power. The 6'11' frame of Paul Samson was holding the bag, providing added tension to the already stiff chains of the bag. Keegan rattles off a two-piece and a biscuit, before Samson began to sense something in him. At first he holds back, watching as Keegan threw a few wild haymakers at the bag, obviously frustrated about something. Finally, Paul asked, “Why you so tense today Keeg?” “It’s Complicated.” Keegan rattled off, throwing a few hard rights to the bag. “How complicated can it be man?” Paul asked while the bag plunged into his stiff frame. “Complicated.” Concerned, Paul pressed on, “I’m only asking cause you don’t seem like yourself.” “Yeah, well they have a tendency to do that to you.” Retorted Keegan vaguely. “They?” Paul inquired, as the bag struggled to move despite his iron grip. Throwing a stiff cross, “Yeah...” Keegan paused, before deciding to elaborate a little. “Women. They tend to make things complicated.” “Oh” How so?” “Well see, I’ve been seeing this woman for awhile now...” Taking a moment to recount the time in his head. “Six weeks now.” “Okay, so what’s wrong?” Paul asked curiously. “She’s pregnant.” Smiling, “Dude, you’re gonna be a daddy?” “I don’t know.” “What do you mean, you don’t know?” Paul asked, becoming more confused by the moment. “I don’t know if it’s mine.” “Oh, so it’s a trust issue then?” “Yeah, but not on my part.” Perplexed, he pressed a little further, “What do you mean?” “Well, she has a boyfriend, and well we... we uh, just...” Frustrated Keegan hauled off nailing the bag with a heavy-handed right hook. “It’s complicated.” “Yeah, so I’m beginning to see.” He fell silent, and Paul decided it was best to leave the situation alone until Keegan decided to say more. The wait wasn’t long however as after throwing only a few more stiff left hooks, and finishing off with a right cross body, he shot up erect, the frustration evident in his expression. “It’s just all the sneaking around, it’s killing me.” Stated Keegan as the floodgates began to open faster now. “I think I might love this chick... but being with her is wrong.” “Why don’t you tell her this?” Paul suggested, “Tell her how you feel, if this is bothering you so much; why not ask her to choose?” “I can’t ask her to choose...” Replied Keegan, “I wish she would, but I can’t ask her to... it would be wrong for me to force her to choose me over her boyfriend.” “It’s not any worse then what you are doing now.” Keegan’s face formed into a scowl, but before his temper could flare he takes a moment to calm himself. Paul is his friend, and he is trying to help him. “Yeah... but, I know the guy.” “You’re not fucking Del are ya?” Keegan chuckled, grinning widely. “Del could kick my ass, and I won’t sleep with a woman that could kick my ass.” Paul grinned, “She is a tough bitch huh?” “I bet’cha she could kick your ass Sam Wise.” This time it was Paul’s turn to smile. Keegan liked to compare him to the supporting hobbit in Tolkien’s books. Ribbing tends to be common amongst friends in this business. “Only when I let her...” Paul began, “Usually it involves a whip, some handcuffs, a little whip cream, and a lot of...” “I think I get it...” Keegan interrupted. “How are things going anyways man?” “Couldn’t be better.” Samson stated with a grin, “She’s a real tiger where it matters.” “I bet she is.” A little calmer, Keegan returned to his test of strength and stamina, laying a few quick right / left combos onto the heavy bag. “Since I’m doing all this to get ready for our big New-Found debut, what have you got up your sleeve Sam Wise?” “I was thinking of watching you tire yourself out for another hour or two, then maybe watching a good movie with the woman, then maybe some extra-curricular activities.” Paul responded, with a hearty chuckle. “They say fifteen minutes of that is just as good as running a mile.” “So you get about an eighth of a mile in at a time then?” “Funny.” Paul responded, grinning ear from ear. “Jackass.” “Yeah, well it’s what I’m good at.” Replied Keegan, delivering a faster combination of rights, tossing a left in every now and then. “Boxing, and being a jackass.” “Too bad you couldn’t be a professional jackass, it might have went better then your boxing career.” “Touch”.” Replied Keegan, with a hint of a smile. “Seriously though, how do you plan to get ready?” “I’ll do some power squats, maybe go jogging, some cardiovascular stuff. I’ll be good to go in there.” Commented Paul, “What are you going to do about the girl?” Keegan shrugged his shoulders, dropping his arms to his side. “I guess I’ll have to talk to her...” “Good idea man, damn good idea.” “Yeah, I’m given decent ones sometimes.” “Only when you talk to me.” “Yeah, you’re one of a kind Sam Wise, one of a kind.” ~x~ You're something beautiful A contradiction I wanna play the game I want the friction You will be the death of me You will be the death of me ~x~ November 27th, 2006 Keegan sat upon a wooden bench in the gym locker room, cell phone in hand. He appeared to be at the end of a telephone conversation. Well it’s been nice talking to you. I got some stuff to do but gimme a call sometime and I will definitely go to the gym with you. Keegan commented, as he rose from the locker room bench. All right man, take it easy. I will probably call you sometime within the next week or so. The man on the other end responded, before terminating the call. With a deep sigh Keegan placed the cell phone into his black duffel bag, and then places the duffel bag into a combination locker, quickly shutting it. Keegan had been on the phone with Global star Jacque Noreault, and Noreault had been talking about the upcoming Global Pay-per View Showdown in Tokyo, along with other current events in his life. The only thing Jacque hadn’t mentioned was his soon to be fatherhood. Keegan adjusts the drawstring on his green jerzee shorts before heading out into the actual gym. The first thing Keegan headed for was the treadmill, to begin the first part of his training exercises, his five-mile jog. Usually he would start with a five mile jog on the treadmill, then make his way to the bench press to complete ten sets (ten reps a piece) at two hundred fifty pounds. After this, he would usually make his way to the speed bag, for forty-five minutes before heading to the exercise bike. He would pedal for an additional five miles worth before making his way to the heavy bag, where he would spend another forty-five minutes. Then, he’d finish in the ring with Paul for a little sparring / wrestling, to work on his ring awareness and ability to anticipate his opponent. This was not a normal day however as Paul was out with Delylah. Sometimes it sickened Keegan to think that Paul was in a happy relationship, and it was at these times that he found himself jealous. Not because he himself didn’t have love, but because his love had to be hidden. Keegan’s special someone was involved with another man, so his relationship had to be hidden. She had promised him that she would come clean, but that was a while ago... now things were different. He had tried to reach her, as Paul had suggested he do but he couldn’t reach her. That didn’t make life any easier for him. After only ten minutes Keegan gets off of the treadmill, coming nowhere near his usual mark of five miles. Skipping all the speed and agility training, Keegan heads for the heavy bag. Keegan loosens his shoulders and begins to box with the bag. While petty jealousies and moral frustrations corrupt his thoughts, the right and left hands he throws land with forceful tendency. A long way he has come from wrestling in Pico Mundo, not much professionally as personally. Wrestling in a small time promotion he got to know the community, so women were a constant, but no one serious. When he left Pico Mundo he found himself struggling to meet anyone. The only two people he got close were the reason behind his problems. This was all a new and different place for him still. Paul had been lucky enough to bond with more people than he had. Paul had bonded exceptionally well with Delylah, the Egyptian beauty. Keegan had only Paul, and because of Paul had Delylah around from time to time. It was one of those 'friend of a friend' kinds of things. Paul spent more time with Delylah with every passing day. Keegan’s tempo calms, as he finds himself uninterested in the heavy bag any longer. He glances around the gym to discover he really didn’t feel up to this today. His muscles feel sore, and his body lagging. He opted for a little R& R instead. Getting out, and getting his mind off of everything else would probably be best. Without taking the to time change, Keegan grabs his duffel bag, and tosses on his coat. Slipping out the locker room exit, Keegan hops into his metallic green Neon. The speakers thud when he starts the car, pulling out of the parking lot. Tonight Keegan was going to do what his heritage did best. Glancing at the wall clock, it reads two thirty in the morning. While stumbling through the house Keegan noticed Paul isn’t home yet, not much of a surprise to him to be truthful. Paul is rarely at the apartment any longer. Feeling the hermit, Keegan sat on the olive love seat, pulling his cell phone from his duffel bag. He flipped the phone open, and scrolled through the address book, until the highlight lands on the name ?Jacque“. Keegan pressed the button and holds the phone to his ear, until the other end answers. “Hello.” A rough and exhausted males voice answered. Keegan became embarrassed and hung up the phone quickly, stuffing it back into his duffel bag. Keegan shot to his feet and stumbled toward the refrigerator. There is never anything good on TV this late at night, so Keegan switches the channel over to Nickelodeon, featuring some of the shows he was familiar with when he was a child. God bless Nick at Nite. Keegan took a long swallow of his beer, as the image of Dan Goodman and Roseanne Barr rolled across the screen. “Dis” is dat epi... *hiccup* episode where they get “runk in the g’rage *hiccup*? Noon Keegan awoke with his hand over his eyes. Falling asleep on the love seat by the window was not a good idea when he had been drinking. Waking up with a killer hangover already, and then the bright light... not a good combination. Keegan rose from the love seat, stumbling into the bathroom. He got a glass of water from the sink, and a bottle of Tylenol from the medicine cabinet. He popped two Tylenol and gulped down the glass of water. His head was still pounding, and he felt like he may throw up. He left the bathroom and makes his way to Samson’s room, knocking on the bedroom door. Se ya later mate. - Samson Keegan snarled, crumbling the paper up in his hand.
