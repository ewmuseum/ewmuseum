Getting Ready for Christmas

19 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/2005 The camera slowly fades into the hallway of Vince Jones' apartment complex. He can be seen hauling in his hands a massive box full of Ricky Thunder's water bottles. Vince reaches his door and snarls at the thought of his days work hocking Ricky's water for his business. Vince Jones: V. Jones gettin' sick and damn tired of dealin' with this water sh*t! Why the hell did V have to make that damn bet with Ricky Thunder anyways? Well, atleast V's home. Time to relax a bit. Vince Jones opens the door to his apartment and looks inside with angry scowl. He drops the box of water bottles on the floor and takes a good look around the apartment. All he can see around him are Christmas decorations, wreaths, mistletoe, Christmas lights, ribbons, candy canes, the works all lining his walls and everything in sight. In the corner of the room sits a large Christmas tree decorated from top to bottom with an assortment of colorful decorations. Christmas music fills the air as well. Vince closes the door behind him as Jasmine enters the room with a bright smile on her face carrying a tray of Christmas cookies in her hand that she shoves in his face. Jasmine: Have a cookie, Vince? Vince Jones: What the hell did you do to V's apartment, Jasmine? Jasmine: Well, you do know Christmas is coming up pretty soon, V. I thought it'd be a great idea to brighten the place up and all for the season. Vince Jones takes another look around his apartment in disgust. Vince Jones: This place was lookin' pretty right to V before you wrecked it, Jasmine. So, what the hell were you thinkin', huh? This place was a playa's pad before he left. Now this place lookin' like one of those damn Christmas shops in the mall or somethin' here! Jasmine rolls her eyes at Vince as he trudges across the room to his leather sofa and plops himself down in a heap. He grabs the remote and turns on the t.v. Jasmine immediately makes her way across the room, stands in front of the t.v, slams the sheet of cookies down on the coffee table in front of Vince and puts her hands on her hips. Vince appears a bit angered by Jasmine standing in front of the t.v, but tries to keep his cool and motions her to move out the way. Vince Jones: Hey! V hopes you know you ain't a glass window or nothin'. He can't see the damn t.v. Slide ova. Jasmine snatches the remote out of Vince's hands and turns off the t.v. Jasmine: No, you and I need to have a heart to heart for a moment here. Vince just shakes his head, folds his arms across his chest, and nudges Jasmine to the side with his boots as he props his feet up on the coffee table. Vince Jones: Aight! Spit it! What you got on yo damn mind that's so important, huh? You found a new dress down at the mall you tryin' to cop? You had a bad hair day or somethin'? Jasmine's eyes light up with fury and she immediately slaps Vince across the face catching him off guard. Jasmine: No! I wanna talk to you about how I did all of this for you? Vince looks around at all the decorations in disgust. Vince Jones: You wrecked V's pad as a Christmas gift or somethin'? What kinda sh*tty gift is that , huh? V liked the place the way it was! Jasmine: (lets out a sigh of frustration) You just don't get it! I decorated this place thinking you'd love it. I thought you might appreciate it. Vince Jones: What the hell is there to appreciate about this, huh? Do tell, Jasmine! Jasmine: Well, I know for a fact that you never really got the chance to celebrate Christmas the same way other people do when you were coming up. It must've been hard. I know you had to grow-up hearing all the stories from other kids about what their Christmases were like. That had to have hurt you deep down inside. Vince just sits there in silence. Jasmine: And I know Christmas must've been especially rough without a father around... Vince Jones: Hmph! Like that son of a b*tch was a worth a dime when his b*tch ass was around anyways! His ass can rot in hell for all V cares! Jasmine: I'm sorry if I hit a nerve, V. I just wanted you to know that I did all of this for you, V. I baked this cookies and decorated the apartment hoping you'd appreciate it. I wanted you to be able to celebrate a real Christmas for once in your life. Vince snarls at the thought. Vince Jones: Christmas'll be on point if V. Jones makes it out of Christmas Chaos on top. Jasmine sits down on the sofa next to V and puts her arms around Vince and gives him a kiss on the cheek. Jasmine: Don't worry about Christmas Chaos right now. That's not important. Just relax a bit. Vince Jones tries to calm himself down and is almost to that point when he suddenly notices a letter with his name on it sitting on the table. He reaches out and rips it open. He looks it over. All of a sudden he rips the letter to shreds. Vince Jones: Ah hell nah! Hell nah! Jasmine looks at him a bit surprised. Jasmine: What's wrong, V? Vince Jones: V just got his latest water gig from that b*tch ass Ricky Thunder! Jasmine: So, what's up? Vince Jones: This b*tch think he funny or somethin'! He got V. Jones slated to work as a damn mall Santa! Jasmine: Well, that shouldn't be so bad, V. Vince Jones: Are you out yo mind? V hates dealin' with lil snot-nosed kids! Damn! Where the hell those cookies at, Jasmine? V might be needin' one of 'em! Vince lowers and shakes his head in disappointment as the camera slowly fades to black.
