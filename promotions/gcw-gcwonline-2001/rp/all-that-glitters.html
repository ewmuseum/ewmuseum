All that glitters...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: On a dark night All that glitters is not gold; Often have you heard that told: Many a man his life hath sold But my outside to behold: Gilded tombs do worms enfold. Had you been as wise as bold, Young in limbs, in judgment old, Your answer had not been inscroll'd: Fare you well; your suit is cold... The city. Cold. Dark. Restless. It's unforgiving and always forgetting. One day you're the hero. Then, when people's personal wants criss cross with the law, you're the villain. Just like that, no warning, you've been changed from being held up on a pedestal to being scorned in the streets. People say my name with hate, with a sarcastic twist. 'Johnny Heroic.' And why? What is their excuse for their change in actions and their so fickle swaying of their opinion of me? What is the reason that I have now lost all the respect that I have worked so hard to gain? I change with the times. That's what. I lost my powers three months ago. I used to have a grasp on the very world itself, on the cosmos around me. I could control electromagnetic fields on just about any scale, and thus had any and every power that I could imagine. And then I was attacked by my arch-nemesis, Destiny, and his two hit-women Fortune and Fate. The unthinkable happened then; my infinite luck left me. The universe itself no longer answers my requests. No longer listens to my begging or demanding to be able to do whatever I want. And so, to stay a hero, to stay alive, I've had to alter my methods. Change with the times. And people can't accept that. They've forgotten who I am. And they won't forgive what I do. But that doesn't change the fact that there are crimes out there that someone needs to stop. 'He's got my purse!' 'I'm being mugged!' 'Somebody rigged the election!' 'The world's about to end!' 'My baby's cross-eyed!' 'They fail to believe in what I believe in, and thus we are at odds!' Twenty million people live in this city. This one city. And no two of them can get along whatsoever. A million cries from a million voices all at once, all distinct. There's a war for every year, a bullet for every tear. A shout when twenty cries are silenced. Love is an art while killing's a science. But I hold up the truth. The cops do what they can, but they're merely human. And, aside from being weak as such, they are subject to fallibility. Their laws and their views are imperfect. They do not know what it is to keep true order. They are forever biased, one way or another, by their lives when they are not upholding the law. I have no life outside of this business. I have no life but a hero's life. No life but upholding truths. That is why I have no problem enforcing it by whatever means necessary. Even if it makes me a villain in their eyes. 'Somebody help!' The shriek is at the same time the scratching of glass and music to my ears. My hatred and my love: the call of duty. It is half a second into running to the sound that I realize that I should take action. Someone in this city calling for help is someone in this city asking for me. They need a hero. Stat. It is true, by the way. I have no powers anymore. I am not lightning fast, and I don't have incredible hearing or sight. I won't have super strength and I won't be impervious to harm. I am no longer a super-hero, just a regular type hero. But, if I've learned one thing, it's that the presence of powers shouldn't be what makes a hero what he is. It's what he holds to and how he adapts to the ever-changing world. And above all, it's his bravado to not let anything, not even those that he is to protect, stand in his way of upholding the law. I reach the scene remarkably fast for one without super speed. I was wise to use my powers to perfect my bodily shape before I lost them. I might as well be superhuman with the shape that I'm in. 'I came as soon as I could, ma'am. I-' 'That you for saving me!' Her joy takes me by shock. I haven't been thanked in ages. Especially since I didn't do anything. And I realize that I haven't quite taken in the entire scene just yet. A voice from above booms, resonating in the alley and bouncing off the bricks and rattling inside my brain. 'No need for thanks, citizen! The Masked Avenger watches over his people!' ... ...that son of a bitch... The giant shape of the woman's savior disappears before I can do simple math and put one and one together. And I am left standing in an alley with a woman looking after The Masked Avenger. Two would-be rapists are handcuffed to a street-lamp while I hear sirens going off and approaching. Two steps toward the woman give me all the reward I can handle. 'Monster! Get back! Get back!' Her words are my only weakness. Daggers of truth. A monster. Is that what I am? 'Whoa, whoa, whoa...let's calm down here. I'm here to help.' 'Here to help? I don't know if you've noticed, but I've already been helped. By a real hero. One that shows up in time. One that I'm not as afraid of as any other thug in this city.' 'Thug? What are you afraid of?' But she doesn't give me the dignity of an answer. She's afraid of me. She's afraid that I'll save her one minute and condemn her the next. But she doesn't realize that the only one that controls who I save and who I damn is the person themselves. You break the law, you pay. It's the only universal constant. The fact that I've had to change how I enforce it shouldn't alter judgement of me. Fuck it. Let her go. We'll see if The Masked Avenger will save her at the next alley that she comes across. We'll see how perfect he is. And I head over to inspect the thugs. He hasn't killed them, of course. He is above killing, isn't he? The perfect Mr. Masked Avenger, not believing in ever taking a life. So that these two can go to jail and get out and continue to ruin lives until he sends them back to jail and it starts over again. I check their bonds. The fucker even has his own handcuffs. A skull and crossbones style and it looks like the keyhole is a pressure-sensitive-slash-fingerprint-identifying device inside the eye-sockets. Stupid fucker. And his stupid fucking custom handcuffs. Eyes narrow as I look after where The Masked Avenger dashed off to. When I had my powers, I could do any number of things to locate where he was and confront him, kick him out of my city, in an instant. But the way it is now, I'll have to track him like an old-fashioned bloodhound. I do have that power. Learned it from an Indian Chief in '96. *** *** Five hours. Five fucking hours of tracking where The Masked Avenger was in this city. I think I've only grown to hate him more over the last five hours of having to run after him. When I got tired of running I began getting cabs. When the cabs stopped taking me, once they figured out who I was, I ran again. Five God damn hours. And I end up running into him out of blind luck. I happened to be right next to some lady as she was getting mugged. Her shouts for help drew him right to me without hesitation, and now I stand face to face with my anti-hero, The Masked Avenger. Towering over me, wearing his skull and crossbones mask, The Masked Avenger stands in front of me, ready to spring into action and tackle the purse snatcher, and ignoring the fact that I'm standing there, about to save the day myself. 'You've done your last evil for the night, hooligan!' He dives in, his cape flourishing behind him, toward the simple purse snatcher as I jump in his way and he stops short of bowling me over. The purse snatcher, seeing his chance, turns and breaks out into an all-out run while I confront this anti-hero in front of me. Johnny Heroic and The Masked Avenger square off. Two titans of heroism with two entirely different ideas of what being a hero means. 'Excuse me. Masked Revenger. I think you and I need to speak-' 'One side, citizen! There is a crime that is in need of being JUSTIFIED!' The bastard gives me a stiff arm to the shoulder that pushes me to the side and pisses me off pretty good. I grab ahold of his cape and he seems dumbfounded for a moment before realizing the reason for his not moving forward. 'In need of being justified? Are you honestly that much of a fucking moron? That means the complete opposite of bringing justice to an evil-doer.' He looks confused. Even behind the mask. '...please let go, citizen. I have a crime to stop.' 'There are more important things to deal with than some woman with a purse. And don't call me 'citizen!'' Using his superhuman strength, The Masked Avenger rips his cloak out of my hand and turns back to me, literally breathing down on me. 'Who are you to restrain The Masked Avenger!? Hero of The City! Beloved by its people!' 'Umm...can one of you two please catch that purse snatcher?' The meek voice of the people is barely heard by us heroes. We have scores to settle, and thus they must wait. 'This is hero business, ma'am. We'll be a moment. Now, Avenger...maybe you're just a bit too slow to comprehend this, so I'll say it slowly. This...is my city. I protect it. Me. Johnny fucking Heroic. So why don't you and your fancy little cape and your idiocy go someplace else.' The giant of a man puffs out his chest and puts his hands on his hips as he lets out a deep laugh. 'HhhhhhhhhhAAA! So you are the pretentious, pitiful excuse for a beneficiary to the well-being of this metropolis! And I suppose the citizenry that inhabit this land have you to thank for the less than appropriate standard of living and non-comparable amount of maliciousness that is herein.' '...yeah... ...that's me...' 'Ohhhhh, I am right loathed to meet you, Johnny Heroic... And yet, at the same time, it has been something of a bit of a desire of mine for some time. For too long have I held in my own breath, watching over my populace in my native land whilst crimes 'gainst the very heart of this country, it's people, are being perpetrated in the distance. For too long have I heard their cries! And for too long have I done nothing, hoping against all hope that someone else out there would be able to take up the cup of helping those in need and using his abilities to carry his people to a proud Utopia of the times! For too long, Johnny Heroic, have I waited for you to join me...IN OLYMPUS!' ...holy shit this guy's long winded. 'Uhhh...that's cool and all. But did you ever hear the saying, 'Too many logs in the river make the lumberjack cranky?'' 'Are either of you guys going to get my purse back?' The downtrodden cannot be heard by those in a duel. A joust for the right to call himself this city's hero. Besides, lady, it's a fucking purse. Get over it. 'Johnny Heroic...from that which I have heard to mine own ears, you are now but one of the mundane multitude! Stripped of your strengths, your powers ripped from you and how many then should cover your faults now that you stand bare? You presume yourself an unearned dignity, claiming to be something above and beyond what you are. Denying the simple fates and destinies and fortune's cruel hope and...and...and such odd sayings, you stand as a pillar of what is misguided about the human spirit. Cold...aloof...alone.' His melodrama is all but killing me. What he needs is a good, swift ass kicking. That's what he needs. 'Listen you poetic piece of shit, I don't need powers. I've been keeping this town clean for a long time without them, and I can continue doing it without idiots like you running around, not even knowing what a hero is.' 'Ah yes. Your debased actions in the name of righteousness. Is it not irony in itself that one Johnny Heroic believes himself to be saving the citizenry from the crimes 'gainst humanity that he sees. But his actions speak not heroically at all. He has degraded to nothing more than a vigilante, a rogue without a purpose who delusions himself to be a hero. And furthermore-' 'I lost my powers, Avenger. I have to make up for my lack of super strength and super speed with efficiency now. There is no cutting of corners. There is no bullshitting around. Like those two you left back there to be brought to jail. What were your hopes? That they would go in and come out reformed? Changed men? I've probably put them away ten times myself already. The only way to solve the problem is to get rid of the source.' His jaw drops in revolt as he realizes what I'm saying. 'You killed them! You are a monster! A murderer!' The Masked Avenger is visibly shaking, his muscles quivering beneath his skin, as I can tell he is barely holding back from attacking me. It looks like somebody's got some demons that are rather easy to provoke. 'That's right. And here's your fancy, custom-made cuffs. I want you and all of your fancy shit out of my city, away from doing my job, by the end of the night.' If there's one thing I know, it's how he's going to react. I turn my back on him because I know his kind. He won't attack me from behind. He won't take the obvious chance and the required action to stand up for his own beliefs. And that's what separates him from me; we both have solidified views on what's right and wrong, and we both adhere to the law and to the ideas of this metaphysical idea of 'goodness,' but he will not change his way of attaining it as the times have changed. In today's world, though, chivalry is a luxury I can't afford. He is in front of me in a second, rather than tackling me from behind like he should have. Predictable idiot. That's his main weakness. 'You are a vagabond and a scoundrel! A murderous, ravenous, despicable, hollow man! How can you hope to hold up justice when you have none?' 'Because I am doing what needs to be done! I am putting my life on the line and putting my balls where they count. Because that's all I have to work with! You stand there, high and mighty, and you dare to judge me. Who the fuck are you, Avenger! You hide behind your mask and you fight crime by night. Well who the fuck are you? After your shift is over, after you go home and hang up the costume, what do you walk around the streets as? How do you live your everyday life when you're not running around in your underwear playing hero? Huh!' I turn the tables. I get in his face. 'Don't you dare fucking judge me, Avenger. I don't have a secret identity. I don't wear a mask! I don't have a day-life and a night-life. I am Johnny Heroic twenty four hours of the day for my entire life! And being what I am and where I am, I don't have the luxury of enjoying life without my powers. A bullet can kill me. A knife can kill me. I don't have anything but my balls anymore. So, if cleaning up this town and being The City's hero using means that you do not agree with is what I need to do, then you can believe that I will do it! And if killing a couple of rapists so that they do not commit their crimes again is what I deem to be the needed action, I will do it.' 'You play God.' 'I live the life!' 'SOMEBODY GET MY FUCKING PURSE!!!' The Masked Avenger and I are both ripped from our little world as we look over to the woman standing ten feet from us, mud all over her, in tears over her purse being stolen. I notice that the Avenger and I each have clenched fists and were about to come to blows. And he looks at me. And I look at him. And he looks at the woman. And I look at the woman. And the two of us take off at almost exactly the same time. The bastard is faster than me, having super speed and all. But for all his powers, his strength is his weakness. He believes himself to be faster, stronger, and better. And thus he doesn't think I'll overtake him. And maybe I won't. But throwing a lead pipe with just enough force will be enough to catch up with him and nail him in the back of the neck. With an 'ACK!' he goes down, and hard, and I spring past him as he lay sprawled on the ground and off to a night's work of finding a purse snatcher. *** *** 'Please! Please Johnny Heroic! Don't kill me!' Guy McGuyerson. I don't really know if that's his name or not, and I really don't care either. He's a criminal. He's unjust. He stole one person's purse that I saw. How many things has he done that I haven't seen? The problem with people is they always take more than you're willing to give. They get away with a crime once or twice and they feel that they can step it up. They feel that because they're more skilled than the law enforcers that that gives them some sort of right to do it again and again, only on a bigger scale. If Guy McGuyerson isn't handled in the here and now, he is responsible for the downfall of all of society that witnesses his slippery slope. The gunshot to the leg brought him down. The gunshot to the arm kept him from fighting back. The kick to the skull was to teach him a lesson. But that lesson is a moot point. The rotten apple must be taken out of the bushel for the bushel to survive. 'Death...is inevitable. A true man doesn't fear it. For you to fear it is to prove that you are not a true man. Anyone less than a true man cannot understand the law. Anyone that breaks the law must know death. Do you see your fate at the other end of this barrel?' I used to hate guns. Then I lost my powers. Now I love guns. End of story. He lets out another scream, hoping against hope that I won't pull the trigger. In actuality, I am tackled from behind as I take the shot. The bullet hits the ground near his head and bounces off the pavement and whizzes by my ear. I'll put a few grand on what dumb son of a bitch almost got me killed while trying to save a criminal... 'Misled simpleton! Johnny Heroic, your name is a pitiful excuse for what it means to be a hero in and of itself! You would have killed me with that blow to the back of the neck had I not the super strength and enhanced healing ability that I possess. You have left me little choice, my misguided friend! I shall have to challenge you to Fisticuffs! Upon your loss, you shall be arrested and brought to JUSTIFICATION!' Oh my God I hate him. He pushes his knee into my back as I bring my palms under me, determined not to give in to him. 'Did it ever occur to you, you blundering moron, that I only threw the pipe at the back of your neck because I knew it WOULDN'T kill you?' My words strike true. He considers that I did not try to murder him and his attention slips away from holding me down. Unlike him, I don't hesitate. Ahh, the need for thought and monologues...easily my second advantage over him. In one fluid motion, I roll to one side and pull him in the same direction, then switch to roll to the other side. He falls off me and I am on my feet. But it's too late. My criminal has dragged himself away. Two small trails of blood could be used to track him and finish him off. But not with Sasquatch shoving his head up my ass like he has been all night. To be blunt, I lose it. 'You fuckin idiot! That's twice tonight! God DAMN it, why can't you just go and mind your own fucking business!' 'In my state of unconsciousness I was able to formulate a hypothesis that I should wish to use to combat your prior question with. Yes, I have the power to formulate coherent thoughts while I am unconscious too.' 'You just stole AND botched my quote, you bastard!' He ignores me and places his hands behind his back. He stands so that his profile is facing me, rather than being head on, and recites his lucid, well formulated thought that he must have taken the entire time of the two hours he was out to put together. 'You claim that I wear this mask because I cannot bring myself to disclose to the public my true identity. This may be true. YET!...You, Johnny Heroic, hide your true feelings, your very nature in and of itself, from the people you see every day of your life. Your entire life is your duty, and yet your duty is flawed. And thus your life is but a charade! A CHARADE, Heroic. A CHARADE!' He is silent for a moment. Somewhere, right now, there's someone dying. There's someone getting raped. There's a crime happening and there are two heroes battling each other to see who can fail at saving them all. The sick cycle, as I always call it. Ignore the obvious solution and let's throw down on the things that are really important here. 'Avenger-' 'A charade!' '...Avenger, I-' 'Not an ounce of truth in it that could be used to fill the insatiable hunger of-' 'Shut the fuck up!' The night is dead silent behind my yell. It isn't the yell of a courageous hero, nor a violent vigilante. It isn't the shout of a man who is standing up for his beliefs. It is the frustrated scream of someone I barely know. A scared child. A person pulled too thin, stretched too far. I barely even know where I am, let alone what the hell the two of us are even fighting over. What is it? The title of who is the hero of this city? Which of the two of us are the good guy? Which one is the villain? He is my villain. I am his villain. Yet we both claim to be for justice, for the law, and for the people. I protect those that hate me but follow the law. He protects those that love him but break the law. And right now the two of us are clashed in a struggle that has no end, because you can't change someone's beliefs... You can't change the way someone thinks. At least not with words. But that doesn't stop me from yelling. 'Just shut up! Just shut the fuck up, you imbecile! You stand there and you preach and you go on and on and you never shut the fuck up! And you're here, telling me how to live my life. Well I've lived your life, Mr. Hero! And I've lived it through all hours of the day and night as far back as I can remember. You have the ability to go home and take off that mask and walk around and people don't know who you are! And you have the blessing of ignorance, to put criminals in jail and still feel good about it; to still call that a night's work! The law is the only constant. Death is the only thing that's permanent. One MUST be the end of the other, or we have chaos! And if you would just SHUT the fuck UP once in a while...maybe that idea would go through your head!' He stands there, staring at me. I imagine what I must look like to him. A madman. Someone at the end of his rope who has been pulled too thin. But I no longer care about that. And that's what he doesn't get. I don't care how he sees me. And I don't care how the citizens see me. And I don't even care how I see myself. What's important anymore is not the safeguarding of the citizens' liberties or happiness, but their very safety. I am no longer a hero for people's smiles, but for the law that they must adhere to. And not the laws of books, but the universal truths that more than half of them will never understand! The law that is eternal. And I must be eternally vigilant to uphold it. And without powers, I have to do whatever I can to do so. Disgusted, I turn around. I wish only to take my leave of The Masked Avenger once and for all. 'Johnny Heroic, I-' He puts his hand on my shoulder to stop me. He hinders my attempt to follow through with upholding the law. I cannot waste any more time on this man. It's a reflex action, not much different than springing into movement when hearing someone call for help. My knife enters his side faster than either of us expect it to, and the pain on his face is visible. That healing factor of his doesn't work for mortal wounds. He can't save himself from death. ...nobody has that power... I can feel the clenching on his hand on my shoulder tighten. And tighten. I feel like he may shatter every bone in my arm with his grip, but it doesn't change me, it doesn't affect my resolve. I've been through worse. But then...his grip weakens. It fades. It dissolves into nothingness as he falls to his knees, unable to breathe. I lay The Masked Avenger's head down on the pavement. Nobody deserves a painful or cruel death. Just a just and true one. His eyes search for mine, but I can tell that his vision is going fast. He needs something to cling on to. Part of him doesn't even know if I can hear his words. '...I just wanted to say...that...I understand... ...and I am...sorry...' His words stab me even more than my knife stabbed him. Daggers of truth and light pierce everything in me as The Masked Avenger, a hero in truth, even if he is an idiot, lay dying at my feet. He was a hero. I am a hero. I killed him. And only one thought can come to my head. '...what the hell have I become?' ...Cold, indeed; and labour lost: Then, farewell, heat, and welcome, frost... - William Shakespeare
