The Chase: Part IV

27 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/10 Just a few months ago Aaron had one thing on his mind. Getting into bed with who most considered the most attractive girl in the Meteorology program at NIU. Since that time things had changed drastically. Aaron wasn’t expecting to begin falling for Chloe. That was exactly what was happening. Originally he had only envisioned another notch on his belt and the reputation that went along with it. Gone were the times he’d stare at her ample chest during conversations. Now he’d find himself getting lost in her gorgeous green eyes as they spoke. Something was coming over Aaron that he couldn’t explain; something he could only assume was the feeling of falling in love. Things had been very different between the two of them since Aaron sacrificed comfort and privacy to give his chase partner the only available hotel room. Chloe was definitely impressed by the gesture and had been a lot more open to the meteorologist-slash-wrestler she was travelling with. Friday May 28th 4 P.M. The two of them pulled up to a roadside gas station that was just booming with business. One other car sat at one of the gas pumps. The mid-day Oklahoma sun beat down on the fields, slowly ripening the atmosphere for a late afternoon severe weather outbreak. As Aaron pulled the car to a stop the only other car in sight pulled away from the station leaving Aaron and Chloe with whatever creepy Oklahoman was running the station. The tired engine of the SUV silenced like a giant sigh of relief. Both Aaron and Chloe hopped out of the vehicle and stretched after their already two-hundred mile trek for the day. “I’m going to the little girl’s room.” Chloe turned to Aaron as he entered the door of the gas station. He nodded and then moved into the station. As the only customer, Aaron spent only a couple of minutes in the store before moving his frame back through the open door and to the front of the Blazer. He set two bottles of water on the hood of the SUV and began unwrapping one of two gas station sandwiches. He took a bite and grimaced a bit as he tasted it. He took the time to check the package for an expiration date. He shrugged his shoulders and took another bite before reaching into his pocket for his phone. He pressed a few buttons and checked his text messages. Only one drew his attention. did you screw her yet? Aaron sighed and swallowed the food in his mouth. He snatched a bottle of water up and took a big gulp before checking the side of the gas station for his red-headed partner. Chloe was still nowhere to be seen, much to Aaron’s relief. He turned his attention back to his phone and began texting a message back. He got the message typed quickly and looked at it before sending it off. Dude eff off. Its not like that Aaron sent the message and slipped the phone back into his pocket. He scoffed and took another bite of his sandwich. He knew his friends wouldn’t understand and there was no point in trying to explain things to them. The last month had made Aaron realize that there was more to girls than just boobs and vagina. The friends he had chosen back home had made a bet that would last the four years they were at college; who could sleep with the most women? A cool five hundred dollar prize waited for the winner. Aaron was tied for the lead with just two weeks left of school. Surely Chloe would seal the deal. “What did you get me?” Chloe’s voice rang out from over Aaron’s shoulder, startling him to attention. He quickly brushed the crumbs from his t-shirt and turned to face her. He smiled and pointed to the sandwich and water sitting on the hood. She casually walked over and took the sandwich to unwrap it. Aaron smiled and held a hand up to stop her. “Be warned, it’s not the greatest thing ever.” Aaron retracted his hand and took another bite form his sandwich as Chloe opened hers. She walked around the front of the Blazer and leaned against the hood in a position right next to Aaron. The two stood there in an awkward silence for a few moments. In reality it was probably only awkward for Aaron. He was standing there staring off into the Oklahoman landscape feeling as though Chloe knew about the text messages that Aaron had just received and sent. Aaron reflected on the last month he and Chloe had spent together and what it had meant to him. Chloe was now somebody special to him and he wondered if he’d be a fool if he spoke his feelings to her. He could never settle on any thoughts that assured him one way or the other. On one hand she had just spent the first month of her summer traveling across the open plains of the United States with him. On that same token Aaron had to decide whether it was an interest in him or the weather that tempted her to do so. “You’re right; this thing sucks.” Chloe laughed and tossed the rest of her sandwich to the ground ahead. Aaron laughed and did the same with what remained of his ham and cheese. He looked over at Chloe and took a drink from his bottle of water. Chloe turned her head and looked at Aaron as he guzzled down the clear liquid. She laughed and lifted her hand up to Aaron’s chest. “You’ve got crumbs on your shirt.” “What?” Aaron didn’t think and fell for one of the oldest tricks in the book. He looked down and was immediately met by Chloe’s index finger running up his sternum into his face. Chloe began to laugh as did Aaron as he put the cap back on his water and set it on the hood of his truck. He then turned and began tickling the beautiful redhead. Chloe laughed uncontrollably as Aaron continued. Chloe never tried to get away from the “attack” but instead just leaned back and laid down on the hood of their chase vehicle. Aaron didn’t let up for another few seconds and when he did, he was position over top of her, face to face. The laughing trailed off and the two just stared into each other’s eyes. They each slowly began to move closer to each other and finally came together in a long, tender kiss. The affectionate gesture broke away and Aaron ran his hand through Chloe’s silky soft red hair before standing up and offering his hand to her. She took a hold of it and he hoisted her up to her feet. “Come on. Let’s find some place to get some real food. My treat.” She walked around and hopped into the passenger seat of the SUV, waiting for Aaron to do the same. He grabbed both bottles of water from the hood and walked around to the driver’s side. Aaron decided the car ride wouldn’t be as awkward as it once seemed. December 26th 12 P.M. Amongst all the crazy, deal seeking people heading out the day after Christmas to get wrapping paper, bows and scotch tape for next year’s gift giving festivities Aaron had plans of his own. He had spent the first six months as a full-fledged meteorologist job hunting and considering his wrestling career. Aaron locked the doors of his Dark Titanium Metallic Dodge Charger and shoved the keys into the pocket of his Khaki dress pants. He buttoned the top button of his black overcoat as he carried himself down a sidewalk amongst a few aged buildings. He stopped as he approached a sign standing in front of one of the buildings and read it to himself. Davis Hall Geography/Meteorology “Back home.” Aaron spoke to nobody but the cold December air that surrounded him. He took a deep breath and looked to the front door of the building he earned his degree in. It had been six months since he was handed the most important piece of paper of his life and he had nothing to show for it. Two summers lost and given to taking general education classes. A lot of hard work and determination to do what he was told he would never do; earn a Bachelors of Science degree emphasizing in Meteorology in just two years. Countless job applications had gone unanswered. Dozens of first interviews had seemingly left employers unimpressed. Aaron stayed awake at night wondering what he lacked that other people possessed. With no answers he turned to the one place he thought he could; school. It was time he began considering going back to school to attain his Master’s Degree. Then he’d have the credibility to get a job. Then he’d have the answers. Then he’d be able to sleep at night. At least that’s what Aaron told himself. December 26th 1:30 P.M. Aaron cleared his throat as he buttoned the final button of his overcoat and slid a pair of knit gloves onto his hands. He moved to exit the building but stopped short as he saw snowflakes falling from the sky. Aaron pressed his forehead against the cold glass of the door and peered out at the falling precipitation, almost mesmerized by the beauty. He slowly pushed the door open and stepped outside. The flakes were large and falling rapidly which Aaron knew meant a low snow to liquid water ratio. He stretched his arm out and allowed a number of the flakes to collect. He studied them intently for a few moments before estimating it would take five inches of the snow to equal one inch of liquid water. He sighed and brushed nature’s beauty from his coat sleeve before turning and heading for his car. “Aaron?” The voice called out and stopped him. A small smile came across his face as he slowly turned around to see her. The beautiful redhead stood at the front door of Davis Hall with a bright smile on her glowing face. She rushed towards him with her arms outstretched and the two embraced in a hug. For the first time in a while a genuine smile enveloped his face. Aaron held on to Chloe and spun her around before setting her back down and hugging her tightly. She laughed as they released their embrace and took a step back from one another. “How have you been?” He asked the question as he peered over her shoulder to another young man still standing at the door. Aaron had an authentic interest in how Chloe had been but found himself only half paying attention as she responded. He finally snapped to as the male figure approached from behind and grasped her arm. “Oh Aaron, this is Tyler. Tyler, this is Aaron, a former classmate of mine. She wrapped both of her arms around Tyler’s left forearm and seemed to be clinging on for dear life. Aaron and Tyler shared generic greetings and shook hands as Chloe continued to speak. “So what are you doing back on campus?” “I came to see Dr. C. I think I’m going to come back in the spring to begin work on my Master’s.” She smiled at his answer and began speaking about how weird it would be if he worked as a Teaching Assistant for one of her classes. This triggered something in his head. What if the awkwardness hadn’t escaped them that day? What if it had only been temporarily masked by lust and the adrenaline involved in chasing tornadoes? “By the way, how was your semester from Hell?” “Oh you have no idea. We had a test in Atmospheric Dynamics once a week and you know how Dr. Song’s tests are.” She laughed as Aaron simply nodded. Chloe looked to Tyler as did Aaron. The two males locked eyes and Aaron immediately sensed some animosity. He could tell Chloe had spoke of Aaron in detail multiple times before. This brought another genuine smile to his face. “But I’m managed to pull an A.” “I knew you would. So what are you up to?” Aaron was finally beginning to block Tyler’s presence from his mind. It was comforting to know he meant enough to Chloe that she had taken memories of him to her new boyfriend. The snow was falling heavier now and Aaron peered up into the sky. Chloe did the same and stuck her tongue out in an attempt to catch a few flakes. Aaron laughed and joined in. “I was just showing Tyler around campus. He’s from back home. We’re heading in to look at our research posters from Advanced Synoptic Meteorology.” Aaron nodded and looked to the ground. The snow was beginning to accumulate rapidly now and travel was going to be horrendous. “Would you like to join us?” “No thanks. I had better get going. My hour drive back home is going to turn into two soon.” Aaron raised his hands into the air to blame the impending slow drive on the white flakes. Chloe nodded as she laughed and released her grip on Tyler’s arm. “It was great seeing you, Aaron. Text me sometime Mr. Soon-to-be-Master’s-Degree. I could use your expertise sometime.” Chloe raised her arms and the two embraced in another hug. They held onto each other for a few moments and released. When they did Chloe planted a soft kiss on Aaron’s cheek before stepping back. Aaron reached into his pocket and retrieved his keys as he turned. Chloe waved as he began down the sidewalk that would take him to his car. Before he got far he spun around and took a few steps backwards. “Make sure you show him my project on the lethality of nocturnal derechos.” Aaron clicked his tongue and turned back around. Chloe laughed and said she would as her and Tyler entered the building. Aaron unlocked his car and fell into the driver’s seat. He started the car up and sat there with the sound of Bert McCracken signing the chorus of “I Caught Fire” reverberating throughout the car. He flicked the wipers on and they brushed the snow from his windshield. It was going to be a long drive back to Plainfield. Aaron didn’t think he’d mind. He had to decide whether he’d return to Northern Illinois University. For a split second Aaron had a glimmer of hope. He hoped it wouldn’t fade too much.
