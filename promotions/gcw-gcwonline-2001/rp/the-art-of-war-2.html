The Art of War

undated

Date: The past "Ultimate excellence lies not just in winning, but defeating your opponent before the first punch." The heavens opened up early in the morning, giving Zacharia a hell of a time getting over to the gym. No cabs were in sight and he needed to get two towns over in less than an hour. Toting his bag across his chest, he tightens the strap and makes a mad dash outside of the church and into the park. His pathetic rags he calls clothes play as tight hugging spandex against the pounding rain. Zacharia jumps from tree covering to tree covering trying to keep his bag as dry as possible. He hops the low iron fence of the park and turns to the empty bus stop, but to his dismay, the bus is halfway down the street. His skinny frame hunches against the iron fence, waiting for the next bus in about a half an hour. He slowly trudges over to the garbage can and sifts through it for a newspaper. After nearly going head first, he finds one and opens it up. he makes his way back to the fence where there is a small branch hanging over, providing minimal cover, but more than what the open sidewalk provided. Zacharia looks at his gym bag as it drips with acidic rain. He takes a deep breath and stands up straight to take off the bag around his chest and places it behind him, against the fence. He takes most of the newspaper and places it over top of it. He opens up the small entertainment section over his head and waits. **** The doors shut behind Zacharia as he pulls out a clump of wet bills from his swimming pool of pockets. He tries to divide the bills as the bus driver waves him on with a smile. Zacharia musters a smile as he shivers down the isle to find a seat. He makes his way to the back and sits alone, rubbing his arms against his chest to warm himself up. A few more stops go by before he can warm himself up to the ability to function. He removes the drenched newspaper that has conformed to the bag. The light at the end of the tunnel is that the bag is nowhere near as wet as he anticipated, the contents still dry. He zips up the bag and moves it onto the seat next to him instead of the floor. He stares at the name on the side of the bag in impact lettering: Bagwell's Gym. Each letter seems to find a home in each window of the bus once it comes to a full stop. Zacharia stands up, and so does a man with a brown hoodie and similar bag, towards the front. Zacharia makes his way outside, walking into the chilling wall of rain. Chris Bagwell is waiting outside the building for him. "You look like shit kid..." Zacharia takes a few steps closer to get out of the rain. "Thanks Chris, you really know how to make me feel better. Can I go change?" Bagwell motions towards the door and Zacharia enters the gym. The hot teenage receptionist smiles at him at first, but then drops her bright blue eyes to the ground. It wasn't that she just felt sorry for him, because that was part of it, but everyone knew that he was dirt poor and lived inside a church. Her eyes mustered the strength to meet his once more and she smiles with feeling, making Taylor feel more secure. Small ghostly footsteps remain behind him as he walks down the hall towards Bagwell's private training facility. Once the main part of the gym is out of sight, the boxing gym opens up, showing two men sparing in the ring while another man, the same man in the hoodie from the bus, punches and kicks a heavy bag in a strict repetition. Zacharia forces his feet to keep moving through the door. Zacharia makes his way to the end of the long room and gets into the small locker room. He puts the bag down and promptly unzips it. His clothes stick to him like clinging plastic, forcing him to struggle out of everything. He pulls out dry boxers and training shorts, sporting the name "BAGWELL" down the side. He looks up into the mirror at his small frame and cowers at his own image. He looks sickly and cold. He grows frustrated at himself and marches his way out of the locker room, only in his shorts, dragging hand wraps on the ground behind him. "Taylor, do your stretches in the ring." Bagwell yells from the closet, pulling out several sparing pads and other workout accessories. "start with your leg stretches and then go into arms." Zacharia mouths his words in a mocking fashion. The image of himself takes a long time to die in his mind, every time he looks at himself. He lazily slides into the ring and starts his exercises. After twenty minutes, he starts to jog and do lunges in the ring, hitting against the ropes and starts to bounce on his toes, ready for his training session. Bagwell jumps into the ring, holding two guerrilla pads. Zacharia pulls over the two wraps and begins to tangle his hands with cotton wraps. Bagwell comes over next to him and he jumps up in anticipation. Bagwell gets into the center and raises the pads. "I want you to get into stance. Good. Now, I am going to call out something, and I want you to do what I say, understood?" Zacharia nods, raising his hands up. "Head Jab!" "Body Cross!" "Head Cross! Come on Taylor! Pay attention!" "Body Jab!" "Body Hook!" "Body Straight! What was that?" "Head Straight!" "Head Uppercut! Head! Head! Head! Clean out your ears, kid!" After a few more punches, Bagwell gets frustrated and throws down the pads and points to the mat. Zacharia drops to the ground and starts to do pushups. After ten, Bagwell puts his heel into Zacharia's back, forcing him to keep his back straight. "You need to stay in control of your body! Every muscle, even this one," he taps on Zacharia's head, "needs to be in your full control." Zacharia stands back up after Bagwell removes his foot from his back. "Let it be know, Taylor, only the strong stay in power!" Zacharia nods, and then throws a strong straight into the held up pads. Bagwell continues to call out punches and Zacharia follows. Bagwell's words remain with him throughout the day. He tenses up his body as he is walking through the park at night, his gym bag tight around his chest. He throws a couple punches into the stillness of the night, but he stops one he hears someone walking behind him. He cuts to the right near a tree and then turns around, but doesn't see anyone. He quickly turns around and sees a tall figure in front of him in what seems to be a cloak, prompting him to yell. Zacharia wakes up on the bus, in a cold sweat. He looks next to him, where a very large black man is staring at him. "Is something wrong, sir?" The man just shakes his head and looks out the window. Taking in a deep breath, he raises his arms to stretch and then looks outside. "Oh no! Stop the bus please!" The bus pulls over and stops on the corner of the street as Zacharia hurries down the isle and thanks the driver. He sprints down the street, where the bus came from, holding onto the bag tightly. After running for several minutes, he makes it in front of Bagwell's gym. He looks at the side parking lot and can't find Bagwell's car. He takes a deep breath and enters the gym calmly. "Hi, did Mr. Bagwell come in yet?" "No, Mr. Taylor, he is running a little late." Her oceanic blue eyes calm him down a little and he motions over to the private facility and takes the long walk down through the gym. When he makes it over to the boxing area, he notices the man in the brown hoodie is the only one in the room. He looks over at Zacharia once he turns the corner. Zacharia looks towards the front door and then drops his bag and walks over towards the man, whom is still standing, watching him. "Hi." The man doesn't say anything, just motions to the bag. Zacharia nods and starts to hit it, the leather stinging against his bare hands. The man pushes him gently to the side and then takes a step closer to the bag. He throws a quick three piece combo and then kicks the bag. Zacharia's eyes light up. He's never done any kicking before. "Why don't you try?" His voice was a lot more familiar than he expected it to be, but never talking to him, he wasn't so sure what to expect at all. He throws a quick combo, but it lands sloppily, and kicks, nearly falling over while doing so. He looks back at the hooded man and he nods back. Zacharia tries the combo again, but his hands slide on the leather on the third punch, ripping open his skin. The sudden pain makes his kick even more unbalanced, making it more of a knee shot than a kick. "Taylor!" A stern Chris Bagwell stands at the edge of the room. Taylor licks his knuckle and then walks towards Bagwell. He looks back at the hooded man and nods in thanks, and then turns back to Bagwell, holding his bag for him. "I thought I told you not to train out here on your own. You are going to hurt yourself. Why didn't you wrap your hands? Now tell me how you are going to punch with a torn up hand? You need to think shit through, kid!" "I was waiting for you, figured I could get a couple shots in on a bag, you know, as a warm up." "If you wanted to warm up, you should have wrapped your hands and stretched waiting for me. Now go change." Zacharia hangs his head in shame and changes quickly in the locker room, and walks out to the ring while wrapping his hands. He freezes when he sees a man, in full body gear, standing in the ring. Bagwell is standing next to the man talking to him just loud enough for Taylor to catch: "...no, you have to watch out for him, he's a lot quicker than you would think. No, I mean, he's still a kid, but he lets his emotions take over...yeah, he's not so great at kicking, so use that...No, just kickboxing..." Taylor slides into the ring behind Bagwell and finishes up the wraps, and begins to stretch. He looks up at Bagwell, and stares him down, trying to figure him out. Something just isn't right about him, but he's not sure as to why. Was he running late because he was waiting on this guy? Who the hell is he? He is stockier than Bagwell, and a little taller, but looks older, like a veteran. "This is Mike Stanz. He is an old friend of mine from when I used to train. You are going to spar with him today, but I don't want you to focus on it as training, I want you to look at it as one of your, adversaries which you are trying to take down. He is in full gear, but I want you to remain in your shorts and hand wraps." Zacharia just nods and continues to stretch everything out. He tries to size up Mike Stanz, trying to figure him out before he has to stand up against him. "This is going to test your endurance and strategy. I want you to think of this as kickboxing, if someone falls, you back away, waiting for them to get back up. Try to keep your punches sharp, Taylor...Whenever you're ready." Taylor jumps up and instantly attacks Mike, who is not expecting the onslaught. He quickly moves into a defensive stance and blocks most of Taylor's punches, but one catches him in the kidney, which sends him to one knee. Taylor backs off respectfully and he prepares for his next attack. "Slow it down kid, Christ!" Zacharia takes offense to him using the Lords name in Vein and he charges in once more. Bagwell circles them, studying how Taylor is striking. "Use kicks, Taylor." Zacharia jumps to the side, picturing the heavy bag and kicks Mike in the leg, but the strike knocks himself off balance, leaving himself open to a strong cross by Mike. Zacharia hits the mat, but is up immediately. He charges in and jumps, landing a Superman punch on Mike's face. Mike falls backwards, into the rope, and then slides to the mat. "Very good, Zacharia. You can feel weakness as you strike. I can see you don't have the confidence in your kicks yet though, so try to use them a little more. I want you to attack again, but this time, think defensively." "No..." "No? Excuse me, Taylor? You do as I tell you." "If I am winning, why should I go defensive?" "Sometimes to win you must dig in and get ready to see an opening, instead of putting yourself at risk trying to create one." Mike gets to his feet and changes his stance to be a much stronger offensive stance. Zacharia lunges forward as Bagwell yells, "TAYLOR!" but he doesn't pull back. Mike throws a sweeping kick at Taylor, knocking him off his feet, and smacking hard to the mat. He jumps up quickly and charges once more but a jab stuns him, and a strong torso kick knocks the wind out of him. Mike goes to pull back but Bagwell signals to continue. Mike throws another jab and then follows it with a hook, spinning Taylor around, causing him to fall into the ropes. He brings his knees into his chest and tries to catch his breath. "You aren't even thinking about what you are doing! You just run in and hope for the best. Haven't I taught you anything about strategy?" Zacharia coughs out blood onto the mat and stands on his feet, raising his arms above his head, still trying to catch his breath. He nods at what Bagwell is saying, but doesn't listen. "Who is this kid, Chris?" Mike stretches against the ropes opposite Taylor. "You said he can fight! He can strike, but he can't fight. Kid, fighting isn't about how many punches you land, it is strategizing each strike to do the most effective damage, not the most! You grab this kid off the street?" Taylor can no longer take it and lunges a final time at an unsuspecting Mike. He fakes to the right and jumps to the left and throws an elbow into Mike's nose, busting it wide open. "TAYLOR STOP!" Bagwell tries to yell, but Taylor continues to attack. He grabs onto Mike's shoulder and starts to knee him in the ribs. "TAYLOR STOP!" Taylor than throws a solid punch at the defenseless Mike Stanz, hitting him in the chin. Mike stumbles and Taylor turns towards the voice of Bagwell that just met his ears, but it is too late. Zacharia is hit in the mouth with a lunging punch from Chris Bagwell, knocking him onto the mat, and out cold. It is so dark that Zacharia can barely see the cement path in front of him. He continues to run the best to his ability, but trips, sliding through dirt. He gets up, as quick as he can, but the man has already caught up. He stands over him, still cloaked. Taylor tries to confront him, but no punch lands. The man backs off and then throws his own in retaliation. Right before it connects, Taylor wakes up in the corner of Bagwell's private facility. The door creeks open next to him, but nobody is there. He looks around and doesn't see anyone in the gym. He stands up and stretches, then walks over to the door to close it. He massages the lump on his lip that is nearly healed over and walks over to the closet to grab a jump rope. He flips on the light and looks on the shelf. Hanging over the jumprope is a ratty brown sweatshirt. He turns around and sees Bagwell behind him. "Today we are going to go over style and variations. The piss poor job of your performance in style last week made me think that you needed this more than the kicking shit I wanted to show you." Zacharia turns back around and grabs the jumprope off the the hook and then looks back quickly to see there is no sweatshirt. He looks on the floor and on the other hooks, but nothing. He takes the rope and brings it out to the center of the floor and starts to jump, single leg jumps. Bagwell goes on to talk about style. "Speed. Speed is the essence of fighting. You need to keep your opponent guessing, and by using speed to your advantage, they will have less time to react and plan out their own attack." Zacharia starts to swing the rope faster, jumping once for ever two spins. "Defensive. Defensive styles are used when you are going to be backed up into a corner. Instead of being cornered or surrounded, you make a stand until you find the opening you need to get out of the corner and go back onto the offensive." Zacharia goes back to the single jumps. "Offensive. Offensive goes hand in hand with speed, but sometimes it is more about a strategy than how fast you can land your attack. As Mike said last week, you need to see what will be the most effective move, not the biggest move." Chris reaches out his hand and catches the jumprope. "Last week, you let your emotions get the best of you. You need to be able to use that to your advantage. Let the adrenaline go through you, but you can't let it take you over, you need to keep your head clear or else you will get yourself killed. You're not training for a wrestling match, you are fighting for your life against men that are going to kill or be killed. Do you understand?" Zacharia thinks for a moment about the man in the park trying to strangle him during the fight. Another image of another guy comes into thought, pinning Zacharia against a tree, choking him out. "Do you understand? I care for you kid, I wouldn't be spending all this time with you if I didn't..." "Yes, sorry. I'm kind of out of it. My mind is elsewhere, but yes, I understand." Bagwell drops the rope to the ground and stands in a southpaw stance. "You can change up how you stand. It will take away from your power punch, but it gives you the opportunity to take a defensive with your strong hand up front to give you room." Zacharia mimics the stance, but zones out from what Chris is saying. He keeps thinking about the walk home. The last two dreams that he's had, the man in the gym, the hoodie that was in the closet... Is God trying to tell him something? What does it all mean? Chris notices that he is out of it, and lets him do some bag training instead of anymore lessons or drills. After the bag training, Zacharia goes over to the weight room and does a couple exercises until it starts to get dark. Chris comes over to him as the sun starts to set. "Hey, I know you have shit on your mind, we all do, but make sure you take care of yourself kid. You can come back tomorrow if you want to, I will be here at noon. I am going to do a couple things, but if you want to do some sparing or whatever, I'll be here. Need me to pick you up?" "No, sir, I will be alright. I just need to clear my head a little bit. Father Emanuel told me I shouldn't be training with you, because I don't really know you. I think I'm a good judge of character though..." "I'm only trying to help, Zach." "I know, and I greatly appreciate it." Zacharia shakes Chris Bagwell's hand, but for some reason he looks up into his eyes and stares for a moment. He doesn't understand why, but he feels it could be the last time he talks to him. He shutters at the feeling and turns away quickly and goes out the door. The bus pulls up right in front of the gym as he makes it to the sidewalk. Zacharia takes a front seat and looks out the window, and then puts his head into his arm. His eyes begin to burn and without warning, he begins to cry. His eyes pour out hot tears, but his breathing remains normal. In what feels like a matter of minutes, he is at his stop in front of the park. He gets out and looks around, now a lot more calm than he was before he left the gym. The park was pitch black. For some reason, none of the lamps were on, the city's lights were not penetrating the small woods, and the moon and stars were shrouded by dark storm clouds, but there was no rain or lighting. Zacharia tightens the bag around his chest and struggles to look at the sidewalk at his feet. Suddenly, he realizes that this is right out of the nightmare he has been having over the last week. He quickly scans the darkness around him and holds his breath to better hear his surroundings. nothingness.... Zacharia closes his eyes and blesses himself. He quickly says a prayer to himself for protection and guidance. Once the prayer is over, he feels eyes on him. He slowly opens his eyes, but still, he can see nothing. Every step he takes resonates more like an echo than the simple grumble of crushed rocks and dirt under his foot. He struggles with whether he should run or continue to walk. He attempts to remember what Bagwell has taught him, but then a figure of a man starts to approach him. "Who is there?" Nothing.... "Who are you?" Still nothing.... The man stops about five feet from Zacharia, and takes off his hood. Although his features are still shrouded by the darkness, the man still seems familiar. "Zacharia, I've been watching you for quite some time." The man's voice is very familiar, yet unknown to him. "I have to say, Father Emanuel has come up with someone worthy enough to carry out his bidding." "No more. How do you know me?" "You think you know where you stand in the world, but you are just as lost as everyone else, misguided just like all your victims." "I am not like them..." "That is where you are wrong. You are just as much a killer as they are. In the name of a Man you have never known. God is a word, not a person, and you killing in the name of a word is just as evil as killing for one's own pleasure." Zacharia's heart begins to race. His breathing starts to grow heavy and explosive. He makes a fist and clenches his teeth together. "See, anger. Anger is not an emotion you should feel, by your precious Modern Testament. I know how you feel. You want to silence me, to kill me. You want me to lay down and take my last breath in front of you, but you will get no such pleasure..." As he finishes his last line, Zacharia is off of his feet and at the throat of the speaker. He struggles against the strong grip of the man's hands. Zacharia tries to clear his head, but all he can think about is the mocking voice of the man before him. The man grabs the bag around his chest and rips Zacharia down from on top of him. Zacharia takes off the bag and charges the man once more. This time, he fakes one way and attacks the other. After a couple more evasive moves, he is able to gain the advantage and knock the man to the ground. The man struggles to speak, but he cannot, for Zacharia's forearm is against his throat. The man begins to plead, but Zacharia continues to choke him. The man is able to gauge at Zacharia's eyes, forcing him to let go. Zacharia throws a punch and catches the hood of the man, and pulls. The hoodie comes flying off and the man falls forward, and onto the ground once more. Zacharia stomps his foot down on the man's face and kicks once more, falling on top of him. His emotions now running rampant, he falls to the guys throat and chokes him against the pleads of the man. "Don't you compare me to people like you! Don't you ever say my intentions are not true! Do not mock my Lord!" After a few more moments, the air is once again clear and silent. The man no longer shows any resistance and his eyes stare blankly up into Zacharia's. He stands up, his senses finally coming clear. he looks down at his hands, now covered in the blood gushing from the man's nose and mouth, and he begins to feel sick. Zacharia tries to wipe the blood off of his hands and onto his shirt, but his shirt is saturated in blood already. He takes several steps away from the body and then trips over his fallen bag. Out of impulse, he grabs the bag and runs. He makes it into the back of the church in the courtyard and rests against the wall of the church. The blood lays in darkness as the rest of his raggy white shirt glisten in the moonlight as if the shirt were brand new. He rips the shirt off of himself and throws it to the ground. Tears flow into his eyes. "I...I...I killed him. No, he deserved it! No, he didn't even attack me. But words are just as damaging as fists...Oh Lord, what have I done?" Father Emanuel comes running out the door next to him, nearly hitting him with it. "My son, what is wrong?" "Father, I killed him. I killed him..." "Who? Who did you kill? Was it in line with the Modern..." "No, he didn't even fight back. He taunted the bible, he taunted it Father. He challenged me... He tried to get me to fight him, and I did. I killed him..." Father Emanuel picks up the shirt that Zacharia threw to the ground and holds it up to the moonlight and turns it over. Zacharia grabs the shirt out of his hands and runs over to the dumpster next to him and throws it in. Father Emanuel stares at him in surprise. "My son, if you cannot deal with..." "I can, Father. Please, just... I need to deal with this..." Father Emanuel bends down and picks up a brown hoodie off of Taylor's bag. He lifts it up. "Is this his?" Zacharia stares at the hoodie and goes wide eyed. He attempts to speak, but he cannot. He struggles with words, but his lower lip trembles in substitute. "I see. Well, my son, you should wear it in his honor. If you punished him under the wrong circumstances, you need to pray for him as well as yourself, and honor him. Seeing that you through out your shirt, I do believe that you need something in substitute anyhow." Zacharia slowly walks up to the shirt and takes it. He looks at it and then throws it over his head and puts it on. Once it is one, he feels empowered. He feels a sense of remorse, but as well as power. He falls to his knees and begins to pray silently. Father Emanuel kneels down with him and says a prayer of his own. Zacharia stands up after a few moments and wipes away his tears. "Thank you, Father..." Zacharia walks inside... ***** several weeks later ***** Zacharia Taylor steps out of the showers, sporting a dark brown hoodie and dark jeans. His bag is tightly fastened around his chest and back. Bagwell is just finishing up cleaning the gym up. Taylor walks over to a water fountain and takes a quick sip before getting hit in the side with a guerrilla pad. Taylor winces in pain and holds onto his side where the pad hit. He tries to stand up through the pain while kicking the pad back at Bagwell. "You know, Chris, you have no remorse." "No, I can't say that I do..." Zacharia walks out of the gym with Bagwell in tow, locking up the door. "You know, Taylor, that's a good look for you. A lot better than the dirty orphan look you had when I first met you." "Come on, Chris, you know I have no money." "Don't the donation boxes have some cash in them?" Zacharia smiles and shakes his head. He gets into the car and they pull away. "Same time tomorrow right?" "Yeah, I guess I can make some room in my schedule for beating your ass." Zacharia waves off Bagwell as he walks towards the oncoming bus. He pulls a couple crumpled dollar bills out of his pocket as the doors close.
