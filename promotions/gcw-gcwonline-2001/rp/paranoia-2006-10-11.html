Paranoia

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11, 2006 ---- Prelude: Losing Grip ---- No. Not again... How in the fuck could this happen? I thought this was over, I thought I laid her out forever. That night I grabbed the United States Championship from above the ring and looked down at her lifeless in the middle of the ring. A smirk crossed my face as I dropped down to the ring. I watched as the EMT's ran down to the ring and carefully carried her away from the ring... never to come back again. After everything she'd put me through at the beginning of my professional wrestling career, it was my just deserves. My happily ever after. It's not allowed to end like this. It was already fucking over. I splashed little bit of water into my face to help clear my thoughts. Looking up into the mirror in front of me, I didn't see the same girl I saw back then. Confident, Talented, Carefree. Successful... everything I prided myself on. Not anymore. The person looking back at me was nervous, battered, and bruised. I havn't had stitches since I was 11 years old and I fell off my bike. There they are like a symbol across the top of my forehead, courtesy of Archibald McGregor. They might as well read FAILURE. I couldn't stand to look at myself anymore, I was falling apart at the seems. It seemed like my stable and my United States Championship was all I had left. Eva VanLorne... was out to destroy both. My constant comparisons and beratings at her expense just wasn't enough. She wants to take my title, she wants to end my stable, she wants to ruin my fucking life. I can't let that happen. Snap out of it Vivica, get your head on straight. You have to be strong, you did it before you can do it again, put that bitch out of your misery. I worked up the courage to go look at myself in the mirror again, just for reassurance that I was me. When I took a look into the reflective glass, I jumped backwards and closed my eyes. What I saw inside the mirror wasn't myself... but her, staring back at me. She was everywhere I went, she had begun to consume my every thought since I'd left Las Vegas, Nevada. The faster I got this over with... the more quickly I can get on with my life. 'Get control of yourself Viv...' ---- Chapter One: Homecoming ---- Closing the door behind her as she walked in, she began to peek around the house. It was a place that she'd been to many times in her life, but never did it ever feel quite as much like home as it did right now. After all, she had no place left to go... and no where else would be quite as accepting of her, accomplice. The place was perfect for him. The windows were darkly tinted, and there were many people just like him who lived inside. People he could talk to about his problems and his condition that would know where he's coming from. Sure, she thought to herself, they won't be so accepting at first... but just like on her, she was sure he'd grow on them. He has a bit of quiet charm that often goes unnoticed. 'Is this the same place...' Markus Eaux D'Artifice spoke up, looking around at the building that seemed a little familiar with him. 'Yeah,' The Black Mariah responded, 'This is The McFarlane Estate. Where Lia and Virgil live.' 'Where is that girl?' Markus responded, not quite remembering the name of the friendly girl that had greeted them at the door. He had just noticed that no one was sitting at the desk that she was at the time. 'I don't know where Alexis is...' Vivica responded, looking around for the only person in the house besides Shadow who could honestly say she formed some sort of bond with. She looked back at Markus with a shrug before pointing over towards a stairwell on the far side of the room. Quietly they approached, no one in sight. You could've sworn the place was vacant, that is until they came a little closer to the stairs. Murmurs descended the staircase in front of them. Following the voices, they began their way up. Click... Click... Click... Atop the stairwell the noise of an on-going conversation grew louder and louder. Although the specifics were not audible due to the distance, the feel and rhythm of the talk sounded much like that of a debate. Knowing the people which inhabited the house... a philosophical debate was far from out of the question. Click... Click... Click... 'I heard there was a vacancy?' Vivica smiled a little bit looking around the room at some familiar faces... and some not such familiar faces. A slightly surprised Shadow nodded as she glanced over towards the hallway that leads to the room where Seymour Almasy used to reside. Suddenly from the same hallway Dawn McFarlane walked through the door, and quickly gave a look that can only be described in three words. Deer in headlights. 'I'm home!' An jubilant smile crossed the face of The Black Mariah as she locked eyes with The Steel Rose. Markus, per usual, took this time to stare at the floor and avoid eye contact with anyone in the room. An uncomfortable silence filled the room as Dawn McFarlane took a glance over at Lia, who sat in the chair and simply responded with a glance of her own. You could almost sense an uneasy feeling in the pit of Dawn's stomach as Shadow quickly interrupted the akward moment. 'I think now would be a good time to show Vivica and Markus to the rooms that are available...' Dawn looked over at Vivica J. Valentine and Markus Eaux D'Artifice, standing in the middle of her living room head to toe in bags of clothes and various other knick knacks. You would thinking moving into a house would be something you'd at least get asked about. This time though, Dawn wasn't familiar with the circumstances surrounding Vivica and Markus' quick departure from The Zenith Manor. The only person in the room that really knew the truth outside those directly involved was Shadow, who'd simply mentioned after hearing Vivica's story during a phone conversation earlier that she was sure she was welcome in the Estate. The look on the face of The Steel Rose wasn't welcoming, it was more frusteration. Then again, not even Shadow expected Markus and Vivica to be there so quick. Dawn McFarlane took a deep breath before finally addressing the new tenants. 'Follow me...' Dawn said, supressing her own aggrevation for the betterment of Dies Irae, 'I'll show both of you to your rooms.' With a smile, Vivica followed Dawn McFarlane down the hallway with Markus in tow. Finally, she was home. No more fighting, screaming, having to take care of numerous things she didn't care about on a whim whenever Christian felt like it. She could go home and relax, and talk to her friends. Things for the first time all week had started looking up for Miss Valentine. Now if only she could get her out of her dreams... maybe this was just the trick. ---- Chapter Two: Sweet Dreams Are Made Of This... ---- 'It's over.' Shooting straight up out of bed, she sprung into a panic. Her heartbeat raced. Looking around the room she pulled her blanked up to her neck. A dresser. A lamp. A closet. A television. A window. Those were the things she saw, trying to take deep breaths to return to normal. 'Get out of my head...' She said between breaths, 'I FUCKING HATE YOU.' That last phrase she heard in her sleep began to repeat over and over in her brain as her breathing began to turn to normal. Her heart still raced as looped in her mind. It's over. It's over. It's over. Burying her head into her heads, she felt the stiches in her forehead across her fingers. What in the hell was going on in her head. She was beginning to lose her god damn mind. She'd gone months without having a house, she'd lived a life where she held a gun just to live, she'd watched people who she's formed relationships with die in front of her. Now, her entire world is being rocked by a german blonde getting over a concussion. 'How fucking weak am I now...' Vivica J. Valentine said putting her hands over her head and taking a deep sigh. She'd conquered every test that had been put in front of her until Dangerous Games. Just seeing her face again sent her into a frenzy. She thought once she beat her it would be over. She'd keep her title, people would stop saying her name, and she would never have to deal with her again. She could move on with her life, out of the shadow of Eva VanLorne. Things almost never end up like planned in this world. Eva was back, and Vivica saw the look in her eyes. She hated Vivica more than she ever had before. No matter what she tried to do, Eva saw it coming and did one better. She left on a stretcher... and came back immortal. Taking a step out of bed, Vivica was wearing a huge white Aerosmith T-Shirt and a pair of black panties underneath. She was safe here... Eva VanLorne could never get into the McFarlane Estate without the full force of Dies Irae coming squarely down upon her head. It was in that fact that The Black Mariah found sanctuary. Still a bit uncoordinated from waking up, Valentine quickly stumbled and fell down onto one knee. Looking around she was trying to see her face, expecting Eva VanLorne to crawl out from under the bed and beat her to within an inch of her life. The culprit wasn't Eva, but a Final Fantasy X Action Figure of Yuna. Courtesy of the departed Seymour Almasy, who'd apparently left it behind. Picking up the action figure, she threw it out of the open window where it landed on the dew-covered grass below. Back up to a verticle base, The Black Mariah opened the door. She chuckled a little bit in the hallway, picturing Seymour Almasy weeping in some corner of a hotel room in Arizona about the loss of his action figure. Anything to get her mind off her immediate GCW future... and Almasy was so easy to make fun of. As The Fearless Phenom approached the bathroom, she noticed a ray of light shining from under the door across the dark hallway floor. It was occupied. Taking a lean by the wall across the hallway from the bathroom, Vivica looked both ways back and forth across the hallway. Just to make sure it was safe. One thing was for certain, she had to end this before it gets any worse. The sooner she got into the ring with Eva, the better. This time though, she had to get the job done right. The door opened across from her, the light from inside hurt her eyes as the silhouette of a woman appeared before the light was flipped off. Rubbing her eyes, The Black Mariah responded... 'Ow, that's bright.' 'You get used to it.' A woman responded. The voice was instantly recognizable for Vivica, it was the voice of Dawn McFarlane, 'Speaking seeing the light, it's about time you finally left that crapbird Christian and came to your senses like I did back in Global.' 'He hasn't always been this way...' Vivica responded. 'I'm sure he has Viv, you've just been too blinded by power and wealth. I used to think like you, I used to think Christian was a brilliant man. The more I was around him, the more I realized how much of a joke he really was. Nothing more than a paranoid, insecure man compesating for it with lots of money.' Dawn went on, 'I've been trying to tell you for over a year now, you just wouldn't listen to me would you?' Vivica just responded with an irritated stare. She had more things on her mind than trying to win a petty argument with Dawn McFarlane, no matter how much she wanted to. Before she could have any type of rational thoughts, she needed to clear her head and get it on straight before she really did go crazy. 'Got nothing to say then?' Dawn said with a bit of a smile before she walked away, 'Maybe you should open your ears a little more often. You'd be surprised what you can learn if you closed your mouth long enough to do it.' 'Just get out of my way...' Vivica said before walking past The Steel Rose and into the bathroom. To this day they still don't completely get along. That rift has been there since Hunting Grounds, but she knows how much Dawn cares about Shadow... so she humors her. Although if Dawn were to leave Lia like she left Christian back in Global... Vivica wouldn't hesitate for a second to beat her face in. She'd always hoped that one day her and Dawn would get along. That day wasn't now, and it looked like it wasn't going to happen anytime soon. But some things... happen at the most unexpected of times.
