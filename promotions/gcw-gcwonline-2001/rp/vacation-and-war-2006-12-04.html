vacation and War!

4 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.04.06 The Mountains of Colorado in Early January. The snow falls lazily from the sky as it is still heavily overcast. The snow drifts are dee[ as a set of rabbit foot prints seem to head nowhere. The sounds in the distances are muffled almost to a dull silence. A peaceful easy type of feeling comes over those who walk in the snows falling this time of year. The quiet muffled type of feeling, the feeling that you are under a blanket being protected by Mother Nature. Panning across the area the camera focuses on a cabin several hundred yards away with an orange glow in the windows. The camera comes into focus inside the cabin as Shaman’s huge form is seen stoking the fire as he looks over his shoulder at Suzy his longtime partner and he smiles. He walks back over to her and sits down with her on the love seat. She wraps a blanket around him as he looks at the fire and smiles softly. “It is nice to have a romantic moment with you after all these years of craziness Suzy. I mean with all the big time wrestling events and the coming year I think everything is going to be hectic. I have a big business to run now and a wrestling career to still manage. Sometimes I even wonder why I never retired from wrestling.” Smiling softly Suzy leans in over into Shaman. She grins as she closes her eyes and speaks. “Yes it is nice for once, but you know why you never retired. You can’t handle doing something that you do not know dear. You purchased the casino because you could and were tired of people saying all you had was wrestling. You should mention the fact that you face Bryan Mayhem and Kyo Kushinaga for the GCW TV Title.” Shaman frowns and gently lets her go as he stands up. Walking across the room he reaches up and places a hand upon the mantle of the fireplace. He stares down at the fire his eyes focusing at it deeply. “Kyo Kushinaga simply got in the way of my fist. I am tired of people assuming they know what it takes to bring me down. Mayhem and I have been going at it for months and guess what there is unsettled business and now they throw an idiot amongst the wolves. Kyo thinks he is dangerous. I have seen his kind before. Braggart and very annoying to the point of vomiting, He doesn’t impress me in the slightest, in truth he will feel what it is like to get his ass handed to him come NC-17. Take for example he skipped into this slot for the TV Title because Ciel retired. He would have gotten another shot if he was patient, but this just means I get to hurt him even more.” Suzy stands and walks into the kitchen area of the cabin and looks over at Shaman. She smiles knowing his anger and rage is running through him and he is hiding it well. “Shaman dear you should feel free to cut loose in the ring. You have unfinished business with Mayhem and Kushinaga is just simply in the way. What do you usually do when something gets in your way?” Shaman grins with a twinkle in his eye. “I usually remove it. You know I am curious if those two even have a clue what they are up against. It isn’t everyday you run into someone like me. Mayhem has a clue, but Kushinaga hasn’t the slightest on what he has put himself into. I could care if the little boy cries or wets himself. It is time to shut him the hell up.” Shaman reaches up over to a coat rack and pulls a jacket off. He pulls on the big winter coat and puts on gloves and reaches down grabbing a big double bladed axe. He turns and makes his way towards the door looking over at Suzy. “I’ll be back inside in a bit. I am going to go chop some wood and then bring some back towards the door. I knew I should have moved that pile earlier.” Suzy nods as she puts a pot of coffee on the huge woodstove in the kitchen. She quietly lifts the handle of the stove and stokes the flame inside to bring it up to a roaring flame and adds another log. She closes the top and leans back and watches Shaman as he heads out the door. The door closes and Shaman tromps through the snow outside as he looks around smiling. He likes the peacefulness of this place he thinks to himself. Making his way over to the snow covered log pile he looks down at it and frowns. Reaching down he pulls a huge stump hat seems to have been cut off flat and sets it up. He then grabs another short piece of log and stands it up. He sets it solid and grasps the axe solidly and then raises it bringing it down with such force that the piece splits in two with ease. Shaman stops and reaches down and throws the logs to the side as he looks at the axe. “I remember a time when I would hate this work. I seem to rather enjoy it now though. It gives you a great work out and gives you satisfaction of achieving something on your own. As a kid I’d suffer through all kinds of ignorance and prejudice. It wasn’t easy growing up the way I did. Playing football, baseball and Boxing all because my dad wanted me to be more aggressive in defending myself. Getting into professional wrestling in Mexico then somewhere in the southwestern US. It hasn’t been all fun and games either. Took me nearly two years to get my first title. Then I joined the CWC a huge organizational umbrella. That is where I found the NCW later to become the WWA and I won every title afforded me there including the Canadian expansion at that time. Took me almost two years again in the WWA to get my first title as well. That title was the Extreme title. Some people wonder why I stuck it out it was simple really. I stuck it out because I never quit on anything.” Shaman lifts another log and throws it onto the stump and stands it up and brings the axe down hard as the piece of log splits in three. He clears the stump off and throws the log over into the new pile he created. Looking up at the lightly falling snow he closes his eyes and listens to the silence. “Man is master of his own domain. You throw three men into a ring and all want to be master of that domain. It is like the time I faced Tracer Bullet for the first time for the WWA Heavyweight Title. He got hit with a chair and I took full advantage of his weakness and took the title. I am honorable, but I was tired of the people in the WWA underestimating me. I took advantage of it and took the title. Then I lost it to Tracer Bullet shortly there after. During that time I also beat Troy Walcott for the Tv Title and had gone back and forth with him for a little while. Beat Jackson Kraven and Craven Moore for the Us Title. It went slow for a little while and then with Darken reformed The Four Horsemen of Apocalypse and I captured the tag team titles over Havoc one of the most dominating tag teams in the WWA. I had another run with the Heavyweight title shortly there after in a split with the WWA. I went with the bosses and moved forward. I beat out Kash and someone else I really forget to take the title from Grunge and was crowned the new champion. Shortly there after I got screwed by Kash and he got the title and then Matt Warr stripped it. After that corporate screwed me from getting a title again. Life happens and I got tired of it.” Shaman lifts the axe and drives it hard down into the wood of the stump and leaves it there as he turns and looks out over the snow covered landscape. Staring out at it he frowns and his face becomes serious. “Hiding in the past is for lesser beings. I came to GCW to find competition and beat the better wrestlers. I left WWA enough said about it. I am tired of little cretins like Kyo Kushinaga and Bryan Mayhem assuming they are the most dangerous and violent men in the game. They should know more about there opponents rather than think they know how things will go. I am not one to be taken lightly. Kushinaga you were in my way and you are in my way now. Your fate is sealed get over yourself and prepare for a real fight.” Shaman reaches down and grabs a few of the chopped logs and carries them to the door and enters. He places them down by the fire place turning to look at Suzy who comes in with some hot coffee. She hands Shaman a cup after he removes his coat. He begins to look around and takes a large piece of wood and places it into the fire and sits back on the couch and drinks it slowly. “You know Suzy I am rather amused right now about how things are going at the casino.” Suzy smiles and sits down near Shaman and grins. “Yes it seems you have taken a rundown casino and hotel and turned it into a goldmine. The road was tough, but you worked hard and made her into something to be proud of. Your wrestling career has gone on the next stage and chapter. Besides you are wrestling in a federation that has more Native Americans than ever seen before in any fed.” Shaman turned his head and frowned looking at Suzy. “Suzy not a one has approached or even talked to me. They have their own little world and a simple hello or greeting seems to be beneath them. I hold no ill will towards them. They come from somewhere tough much like I have. I won’t worry about it. They will come and speak with me in time. At the moment I have more things to be concerned with.” Shaman drinks more of the coffee and simply looks down into the cup of coffee observing the rolling steam. “Kushinaga has made a blood vow, a vow that will go unanswered. I am going to break him into pieces. I am going to break him more than any man should ever be broken. He thought that threatening me would shake or rattle me. He has proven nothing to me other than getting in my way after Mayhem. Mayhem is my target, but Kushinaga opened the door to his own pain. He will suffer greatly at my hands.” Suzy smiles and stands up extending a hand towards Shaman. “Let us go release some of that stress. It is around 9 pm here so we can go to bed early.” Shaman smiles and stands up going with Suzy to the bedroom of the cabin and the door closes. <<3:03 am in the morning.>> Suzy is seen sleeping comfortable on the bed as the moonlight shines through the window as a huge Shadow assumingly Shaman is silhouetted through the moonlight as he stands near the window. One arm is resting against the windows framework as he simply observes the world outside. He watches a deer walk across the snow, but his mood is somber and emotionless as he observes. He quietly mumbles to himself. “So what is it that makes them assume they know me. I am not some cake walks for these two men. I do not play these games that they are so inclined too. NC-17 is a battle ground and they are taking each and every bit of it lightly.” Shaman raises his hand and looks at it as if the moonlight is highlighting it. “It isn’t as if the spirits are against me and my goals. If only men understood their place in the greater scheme of things. All around the world no matter the belief or creed. There is a place for everything. This battle that is coming is a battle for more than a piece of tin and leather. It is about dominance and a soul.” His eyes turn and look upwards at the moon. Looking at how clear the craters and impacts look on the moon he simply smiles. “With all the changes to the landscapes something’s just stay the same. People are going to underestimate opponents, such a shame that some of them are simply idiots. You have a great run somewhere else and you think coming here you’re going to roll over everyone else. There is a fact in this matter. You’re going to have to earn every bit of it. Kushinaga you got a hand me over and lucky to get that title shot with Mayhem and I. I don’t care who you think you are or where you come from. To me you’re a little man with a big mouth. You can and will be beaten just like the rest. I have seen your kind come and go Kushinaga and I will watch another come and go.” Shaman turns and heads towards the door and opens it slowly. He walks across the living room area and lifts a log and stokes the fire as it becomes more of a flame and burns brighter than before. He watches it for a few minutes and smiles softly to himself. “Fire has a beautiful dance all its own. The raging flames jerk and twist as if in a convulsion yet constrained by the confines of its area. It relies on its fuel to keep it burning. You take away the fuel it dies and does not burn any longer. Feed it fuel beyond its measure and you have something that will get out of control.” Shaman stares at the fire once again as he pokes the fire a bit with the poker. “Mayhem is a flame burning out at the moment. His title reign is coming to an end. I am going to make sure of that fact. Kushinaga you are a flame burning out of control and I am the wall that is unburnable you have reached.” Shaman turns and walks to the couch and sits down upon it looking out at the window and trees beyond the glass. “It is nice and quiet this evening and I should be enjoying the serenity, But I had to lose that peacefulness over ignorant remarks and tactics by my opponents. As Sun Tzu once said “let your enemies choose the killing grounds.” I allowed Mayhem to choose the way and Kushinaga you simply agreed that NC-17 is the killing grounds. There are worse fates to expect Kushinaga and Mayhem, but there aren’t many.” Shaman grins sadistically as he reaches over and pulls up a rather large knife from a table nearby. He looks over at the fire once again and then gently runs a dinger across the blades sharp tip. A slight path of blood trails down the edge of the knife as he smiles continuously staring at the fire. “I shall have several trophies from NC-17. The trophies of hearing my opponents groan in despair and pain. Listening to bones crack beneath my hands and feet. Allowing you to believe you have the upper hand. Isn’t grand to feel the fear creeping up your spine. You show a brave fa’ade. In the end it is all false for fear rules you both and in that is where my victory lies.” Shaman grins as he brings the knife edge to his lips as he gently licks the blood of the knife. He stares at the fire the whole time doing this. He then licks the blood off of his finger. He smiles as something changes in his demeanor. He seems more at ease relaxed yet a fire rages in his eyes that is very seldom seen. “Oh yes there will be plenty of blood. I will enjoy taking these men to their limits and beyond. Has it ever occurred to both of you that your time has come?” Placing the knife back down upon the table he leans back. “You are going to be tested in ways that no mere man will ever be tested. Your mind, soul, and spirit will yearn for release. When you scream out in pain I will twist the broken bones even further and attempt to rip it out of your body. I will attempt to separate your limbs from your body. Have I ever been afraid of either of you” Not in the slightest bit. As you face me in the ring you will realize how much your efforts will be worthless.” Shaman looks up and Suzy is leaning against the door naked with only a blanket wrapped loosely about her. She smiles and speaks to him. “Dear it is late comeback to bed and mull over this in the morning.” Shaman smiles at her and nods as he gets up and walks to the room as she wraps her arms around him and her blanket drops down hanging barely over her shoulders. He kisses her softly and caringly as if he was another man yet the same raging beast within. The door shuts and all is quiet. << 8:30 am later that morning.>> Waking as the sun shines through the window freely. Shaman wipes the sleep from his eyes as he smells breakfast freshly cooked. He sits up and looks to his left and notices Suzy is up with the sun as he smiles and leans forward stretching a bit and rubbing the last vestiges of sleep from his eyes. He slowly rises to his feet and puts on a pair of pants and a t shirt and walks out of the bedroom and into the living room and then looks to the kitchen where Suzy is sitting at the table with a cup of coffee and freshly cooked food on the table before her. He slowly walks over to her and sits opposite her at the table and looks at the food and looks up at Suzy. “Looks delicious Suzy.” Suzy looks up over the rim of her coffee cup and smiles at him with the bright loving eyes she has always given him. Shaman smiles and slowly reaches down and begins eating the food as Suzy speaks. “We are a day closer to NC-17 dear and it is time for you to prepare mentally and physically. They are going to come at you full force and nothing is going to stop them from achieving their goals other than you.” Shaman looks up at Suzy, but says nothing as he finishes his food and then drinks a glass of orange juice as he leans back in the chair and looks at her. “This time things are going to be different for you. You have always faced the odds. Now you have two men who truly want to hurt you and get some revenge. What are you going to do about it?” Shaman smiles as he stands up slowly and looks at Suzy. “I’ll have to say the odds are finally even for them, two of them and one of me. They couldn’t ask for anything more than that. I am going to have fun with this matter at hand.” Shaman smiles and seems to brush it off. “Well anyways has Bryce called from the Casino this morning and given you the numbers from the last week?” Suzy smiles and nods. “Yeah he called this morning about an hour ago and gave me the numbers. We seemed to have done better than expected.” Shaman nods as he opens a newspaper on the table. “Looks like it is going to be a great weekend doesn’t it then. Making money and kicking ass all within a week. What else could anyone ask for. Other than total and absolute victory and that has to be worked for.” Shaman quietly reads the paper as Suzy goes back to drinking her coffee as Shaman looks out the window as the sky is becoming cloudy once again and the snow begins to fall slowly and lazily as if nothing has ever impeded its advance upon the ground.
