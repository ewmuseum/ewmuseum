1.2 The Streets of San Diego (An El Moreno Episode)

28 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/10 "I have learned that the novice can often see things the expert overlooks." - Tom Peters 1 The sun had already set beneath the horizon but the air was still warm in Downtown San Diego. The streets were dimly lit by the faux gas lamps as crowd noise slowly started stirring. It was a Saturday night in the Gaslamp District, the heart of the city, and Miguel Erazo was strolling down E Street with his hands in his pockets. His face was dressed in stubble, as if he hadn't shaved in several days, and his body blanketed with a rustic red sweatshirt. His hair was hardly parted and his shoes seemed as though they were seven/eight years past their prime. Miguel hardly seemed like an officer of the law. Between his appearance and newly acquired slump in his walk, he could just as easily have been passed off as a city bum on a mission for loose change. But that was the point. Captain Phillip Toms was irate with the antics of the FBI. They waltzed into his jurisdiction and all of a sudden had a platter of leads handed to them within mere minutes. Toms had been on the job long enough to know that either he was slipping or something was up. But Toms was a man of balance - he didn't slip. It wasn't often that Captain Toms disrespected higher authority and that's what made this particular case all the more meaningful. He was laying his long career on the line and in the hands of Erazo and Tommy Chase. "This looks like the place." Erazo stutter-stepped to a stop and peered up at the forest green canopy along side of the building. Double Deuce, it read. It was a country western bar in the Gaslamp District known for its mechanic bull and its gorgeous bartenders. The Deuce was particularly notorious for getting to be a bit rowdy afterhours, when the Billy Ray Cyrus albums were packed away. Miguel wasn't there for the good drinks, the beautiful women, or for a ride on a robotic bull. Instead, he had arranged a meeting with a man who'd been undercover on the streets for the last six years. He'd never seen the agent before, nor did he have the slightest idea what he would look like. All Erazo knew was that the informant called himself Sabra. "You Miguel," said a soft yet stern voice. Alarmed, Miguel jolted his head towards the entrance of the Double Deuce. His eyes nearly rolled out of his head when he was greeted by a tall, thin, strawberry blonde with a set of abs she could wash your clothes on. Without making a sound, he nodded his head. "You're late," she shouted. "Now come with me." Erazo didn't budge. He gleamed at the girl, not quite knowing what to make of the situation. "Do I know you," he asked with hesitation. She smirked, "Sabra. Now let’s go." 2 "Andy Teater." The words escaped the lips of Officer Tommy Chase as if they'd slipped on a banana peel. Like Erazo, Chase adjusted his normal dress to meet with a second informant to see what sort of information he could get on Los Aztecas. However, instead of getting paired with a strawberry blonde sex icon, he was in the company of a short, overweight and balding middle aged man. "Samuel Suhn," Chase exclaimed. "You may have not been blessed a beauty queen, but you sure as hell have a knack for what you do." Samuel, better known to friends as Sam, nodded his head. He was a quiet man and often conveyed his feelings through gestures. Sam had been undercover for only three years when he was able to infiltrate Los Aztecas. Until this point, most people that knew of his existence assumed that his undesirable physical traits got him killed. But despite frequent ridicule, Sam muscled himself respect. His rough demeanor and intimidating silence earned him a position as an Aztecas enforcer. Though his rank was extremely beneficial to his assignment, it also called for him to execute orders (pun intended) that were hardly acceptable as an officer of the law. "So Teater's the tie-in between the Aztecas and the FBI," Chase began mumbling to himself. "That's how Erwin was able to scrape together so many leads in such a short time." At the sound of Erwin's name, Suhn's eyes widened. "Erwin's on the case?" "He's the lead investigator," Tommy added. Suhn shook his head frantically, "You didn't mention that." Sam began to grow anxious, rocking back and forth in his seat. "You didn't mention that," he repeated. Chase stared at Samuel Suhn, perplexed. What about Erwin could've made him so uncomfortable by a mere mention of his name? Though the pieces weren't all quite fitting together, they were certainly there. Chase knew that Teater and Erwin were connected, but a mere informant-to-unit connection wouldn't have been the cause to so much dismay. "I must leave," Samuel Suhn commanded as he stood from his chair. Without even grabbing his coat, he marched to the doorway, looking over his shoulder with almost every step. "Something's not right," Chase whispered to himself. BANG A loud shot pierced Chase's ears...and Samuel's chest. At the doorway, Suhn dropped to the floor with blood spewing through his shirt. Chase had nowhere to go. 3 Miguel stared at her with a twinkle in his eye. He was like a teenage boy with an infatuation for a celebrity. But Sabra was hardly a celebrity - nor did she have much patience left for insinuative stares. "You need to move fast," she encouraged. "If Los Aztecas have truly infiltrated the Bureau to the level that I think they have, it's only going to be a matter of time before they take you and your unit down." Miguel gave her a nod and slowly worked himself out of his daze, acknowledging the severity of the situation. "I just don't know what I should be looking for. I mean, if this Teater guy is really supposed to be an FBI informant, wouldn't the double agent in the FBI have taken him out already?" Miguel scratched his chin. Nothing made any sense. Why would a double agent allow an informant to survive, let alone report to the FBI the minute they enter the case? It'd virtually be like they were working in opposite directions, to the disadvantage of Los Aztecas. And that's when it hit him. "Wait a minute," he exclaimed. "How do you know of Teater?" "I overheard it in conversation," Sabra responded. "You found out Andy Teater was an FBI informant by ease-dropping on a conversation between two Aztecas?" "Yes," she said curiously. "What don't you understand about that?" "Fuck." Miguel dropped his face into his palms and gave a deep exhale. "It's a fucking setup," he whispered. With his head still in his hands, Miguel rolled his eyes up and began to take notice of some of the other patrons inside the Double Deuce. There were more than one of Mexican descent whom had their eyes fixated on he and Sabra. She began to panic, "What do you mean a setup?" Miguel placed his hand gently on her thigh. "Relax," he said. "You didn't overhear anything. They wanted you to hear about Teater to see if you'd approach him and break your cover. Gangs don't just allow informants to meander within their ranks. They take them out." Sabra shakes her head, "I should've known. How could I be so naive?" "Relax," Miguel insisted. "Don't let anyone know that you're upset. There are a ton of them in here right now, just watching us." Sabra glances around the room, taking notice to some of the men Erazo was speaking of. She takes a deep breath and tries to focus. "Alright," she says. "What do we do? We can't just leave." "You head to the bathroom. Lock the door behind you and then kick the window out. In the mean time, I'll order a drink and drop the glass. In the midst of the commotion, I'll book it. We'll rendezvous around the corner outside the Whiskey Girl." Sabra gave Miguel a nod and stood from her stool. "I have to go to the bathroom," she said in a convincing tone. "Another Jack and Coke," Miguel added after locking eyes with the bartender. 4 "What the hell happened to you?!" Tommy Chase stumbles up to Miguel Erazo, a bloodied mess. They're a few blocks from the Whiskey Girl rendezvous spot, hidden within the Horton Outdoor Plaza Mall just outside the Gaslamp District. Sabra, who was previously dressed scantily clad, is draped in a dark overcoat. "Suhn..Suhn..." Chase struggles to get his words out. "Suhn? Suhn what?" "He's dead." Erazo wears a confused look, "who the hell is Suhn?!" "The other SDPD informant," Sabra interjects with urgency in her voice. "Who killed him?" "It's Erwin," Chase exclaims. "Erwin's running the gang..." The sound of screeching tires encompasses the open hall. A blacked out Lincoln Navigator whips up to the three of them aggressively. The window drops to reveal Captain Toms in the driver’s seat. "Get in," he shouts. "They're coming after us." Miguel anchors Chase over his arm and helps him into the back seat. Sabra scurries over and climbs into the front, slamming the door behind her. The four remain silent, staring at each other blankly, as they listen to motorcycle engines roaring through the streets of downtown San Diego. A war is about to erupt and they're right in the middle of it all.
