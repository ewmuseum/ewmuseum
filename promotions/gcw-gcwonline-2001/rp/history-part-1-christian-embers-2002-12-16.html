History: Part 1
Christian Embers
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #13
Date: 12.16.02
Location: .My mind... your eyes.

“SUBMIT!” taunted Christian Embers as he locked his opponent in his crucifix shoulder lock. He taunted once more, “SUBMIT!”

“NO! NO! NO!” screamed his opponent replying to Christian’s demand. Almost simultaneously Christian locked the shoulder locks harder with his arm and leg. “NO!” screamed the trapped man once more.

“FUCK OFF THEN!” screamed Christian as he raised his free elbow and started to obliterate sections of his opponent’s face with huge elbow shots.

Second by second his opponent fell closer and closer to the gray mat. His opponent started to bleed from his nose wildly and his mouth was blown out of proportions. Christian loosened the arm locks as he focused harder on the elbow shots to the face.

“FUCK OFF!” he screamed at his opponent as he slammed his elbow once more into the opponent’s face, sending it slamming hard onto the gray mat.

“STOP IT CHRISTIAN! STOP!” screamed the referee as he jumped on and smothered Christian’s opponent with his body to protect him. Christian rose up proudly and raised his arms up as he ran around the ring in circles. The crowd, which was always behind him, roared in ovation as they stood and cheered on for their favorite fighter.

Christian looked up the sky, kissing his hand and raising it to the heavens. Only recently had his father, the most important figure of his life, passed away. “That was for you dad!” he quietly and emotionally whispered to the heavens, “I love you always!”

The referee came over to him and raised his hand for the knockout victory. Over the loudspeaker Christian heard, “And your winner by knockout… Christian Embers!” He felt proud that the fans could hear his name but he was even prouder of the fact he could hear them, their spirit and their love for him. He could HEAR their love.

He jumped out of the ring and onto the floor, sweating heavily but radiating with happiness inside his body. He smiled at his fans and exchanged some hi-fives as he headed to his locker room.

When he arrived, he saw the other fighters as they congratulated him. They smacked his back and offered their congratulations.

It would have seemed that Christians life was exciting and fulfilling. He made good money from these fights and he had fans to support him for emotional support.

However, assumptions are never 100% true, no matter how good the evidence looks like. Every moment a fighter met Christian, they grew a bigger hatred for him. The seed of malice grew and grew until it entrapped the fighter in jealous envy.

In one man would this seed grow into a vine entangled with jealousy, hatred, malice and above all, vengeance. The vine bared the sour fruits of a blind viciousness and everlasting envy. The sour fruits bared the seeds of corruption and illegitimacy.

The vines and fruits would grow uncontrollably until this one man knew that Christian Embers was suffering. He would continue to nourish his hatred with false assumptions and propaganda.

This one man took it way too far.
