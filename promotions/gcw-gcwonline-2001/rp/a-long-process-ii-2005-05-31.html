A Long Process II

31 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-05 “Rich Rollins. I’m familiar with him, and of course Caldera himself. I’m quite certain I’ve out lasted them both either in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament or the Universal Title Tournament or whatever, they have all run together.” Ryan Stephens leaned way back in his black leather office chair. His black loafers were propped up on his large mahogany desk and he was donning a suit per usual. His secretary, friend, business partner Shelly Tallent sat across from him in a smaller cushioned chair. When they weren’t too busy Stephens frequently entertained her with wrestling talk. She felt obligated to listen and pretended that she cared. Stephens was certain she didn’t, but it didn’t matter either way to him. “So then you should win this television title without too much trouble right?” she asked. “That remains to be seen. I don’t want to get ahead of myself. I could careless if I win as long as nobody messes up this gorgeous mug shot of mine; however, I could use the initial extra face time that would accompany a win,” Stephens said. “Which would in turn aid you in your business propositions as they arise.” “Precisely. I’m concerned though. I don’t think Caldera realizes who I am. You see none of these guys were familiar with the NWC because we kept to ourselves until we let a few guys like Almasy, Hancock, James Edwards, etc come over and mid-carded for us. Cisco Milloy was the only one of them all that could compete in our circle. And when we sent Aidan over to represent us in the UTT, what did he do” He waltzed through the competition to win the damn thing. Indeed, it’s a damn shame these guys are so ignorant.” Her act was convincing. Only a guy as perceptive as Stephens would be able to tell that she really could give two shits, but Shelly had long since convinced herself that because he had saved her ass she would tolerate his. That and she envisioned the old Ryan returning eventually. She pondered for a second before responding. “Why be concerned then” The element of surprise will catch them all off guard, won’t it?” she asked. “Indeed maybe at first until Caldera wises up. Then I assure you my brash cockiness will lead to Caldera trying to put me in my place if you will. He’ll try to treat me like some no-name coming in and spouting off at the mouth.” He smirked. Clyde himself would be intimidated by a smirk so deceptively evil and cunning. “I look forward to all those that think I’m just some hot shot ego spewing bullshit for their amusement. Hell I was pretty successful in my short stint over in PCW. Just ask Jordan Shabazz or Multiple Man or dare I say it, Violence Jack. Ask those guys how full of shit I am, and that was long before I became the enigma I am now. I was kid then Shelly” “I miss that kid,” she said without hesitation. Their relationship was much different a long time ago. He ignored the comment and continued. He had never been interested in her in an affectionate scenario. “If they really want to see spewing forth at the mouth they have to look no further than that Marshall guy. How many times have you seen his type show up in a fed” I hope he’s resilient because until his attitude changes he’ll have his ass handed to him a few times. And it’s going to suck eating his words week after week until he gets some promo skills. For someone who tries to put emphasis on more than just talking, he sure does a lot of it. But never mind Brian Marshall. I’m more concerned with Borealis or maybe one of the VanLornes?,” Stephens explained. Shelly wasn’t even listening. She nodded. Truth be told she was remembering the young kid that she so admired. Even once he quit law school and went on to wrestle in the NWC she had always admired the sheer heart and courageousness he possessed. Stephens eyed her meticulously. “Shelly, have you heard anything I’ve said?” he probed. She nodded negatively. There wasn’t any point in lying, and she knew he could careless. The nostalgia was written all over her face. “I need a smoke,” she said excusing herself from his office. Stephens interlocked his fingers and folded them behind his head. He looked around at his elaborate set up, the fish tank, the bookshelf, the memorabilia. He enjoyed the life he’d made for himself. He never wanted to relinquish it. Part of him was somewhat alarmed because he couldn’t figure out why he was unusually fired up for this Thursday’s match. It wasn’t like him to rattle on and on about wrestling unless it correlated strongly with whatever business venture he had going at the time. A tiny frown differed from his usual indifference. ******** The meeting with the Barrata’s had went as planned. He could tell that the elder Barrata John was preparing himself for a mental battle of one-ups with Stephens, but he was confident in his plan. He knew when it was all said and done he’d have upper hand on the Barrata’s and 51% of Fox Sports Net’s television rights. It would be the first step among many. He was thankful that Blaise Barrata hadn’t brought his ex-wife because though he had tried to prepare himself for anything, he wasn’t sure he could handle the awkwardness of that situation. He was certain she couldn’t either and that’s why she hadn’t been brought along. He hated Blaise’s spoiled, arrogant ass. It made working with his father that much harder because not a second went by that he didn’t want to take the kid into the shadows and end him. There were a lot of uncertainties surrounding his debut in GCW. He hadn’t wrestled actively since his last NWC stint, and he hadn’t been involved with wrestling since he fucked over the Wisoming roster. He knew for the first time since his PCW days he would have to prove himself, and he didn’t know if he wanted to exert the energy necessary to do so. He had thought about calling an old acquaintance and a competitor he respected in Syko Arsonist, but he couldn’t come across with any indecision. He had done extensive research when he decided that PTC was the place for him, and then even more months when narrowing it down to PRIME or GCW. It would have been nice to fight alongside some of his fellow PCW fedmates over in PRIME, and others like Deville and Karina, but after such a long layoff he couldn’t afford to get lost amongst all the names. His face had to be on TV, people had to see it whether he was winning matches or not, that didn’t matter. It would be a long process and he knew it, he also knew that he ever decided he wanted to claim the top spot in GCW, he could arrange that chance. First priority would be not getting injured in the clusterfuck battle royal. He would be elusive, he would avoid contact and fighting. They would leave him be, thinking he’d be easy to eliminate at the end. Surprise cockfags! This wasn’t his first rodeo. ******** Robin Boca knew he was up to something. “Blaise. Listen to me. He’s a scoundrel now. Nothing matters to him unless it benefits him. This won’t be a partnership. It will be a hostile takeover,” she pleaded with her new boyfriend, John Barrata’s son, Blaise. Ryan didn’t marry a blonde bimbo to spend his money; hell he didn’t have any then. He had married one of his best friends. She was very successful in pharmaceutical sales and it afforded him the opportunity to wrestle and not worry about the shit salary the first couple of years. She was a classy broad. Her piercing green eyes watched Blaise as he ran a hand through his slick black hair and down his long ponytail. “You don’t have to worry about anything.” He stuck out his chest. “My dad and I have it covered,” he replied putting great emphasis on I. She knew her ex husband better than they did though. She had witnessed his transformation. They suited one another perfectly and were living the good life when he finally attained main even status in the NWC, and for a period of time when he worked cross promotionally with PCW as well. They had thought about having a family. Then one day Ryan won the Atlantic Heavyweight Title. It was the heavyweight, main event belt that had eluded him for so long, five years give or take. She was promptly kid napped unless he laid down for the belt and somewhere in the midst of it all he couldn’t bring himself to do so. He tried to save her, and eventually he did, but she’d been roughed up a bit and it killed him. He pushed her away claiming that she was a liability, still very much in love with his wife but unwilling to give up a belt he’d spent the better part of five years trying to obtain. He didn’t know any better then. She never came back. “I’m just saying. He almost killed the man that was his prot’g” and tag partner a mere 2 years ago. He shoved me away for a gold belt. His eyes have always been on the prize. He’s always wanted the top spot and if he couldn’t wrestle his way there, he’d find a way. His new way is screwing people like you and conniving his way to the top,” she explained. Blaise smirked. His beady black eyes exuded confidence. “I bet I could take his pansy ass. Besides, the top of what” You have to be kidding me if you think GCW, even PTC is the pinnacle of wrestling.” Blaise stated. “No, it certainly isn’t, but it’s a stepping stone. He’s patient and he’s meticulous. Just don’t say I didn’t warn you when it all comes crumbling down,” Robin said. “Now you listen here,” Blaise raised his voice. “That scum bag can’t outsmart Blaise Incorporated. I don’t care what kind of meathead wrestlers he’s taken advantage of. I’ll break him if he tries anything clever.” She just nodded. It was useless. ******** Ryan shuffled through some papers on his desk. None of them held his interest. He got up and shuffled over to his fish tank. Still not interested. He heard Shelly bustling about across the hall. He could call her in and make her listen to more bullshit, or see how long it would take for him to rile her up. Nah, even that was getting old. He returned to his desk and opened the top drawer. He looked around the room suspiciously and then lifted up a tray filled with pens, pencils, rubber bands, etc. Beneath the tray was a small sheet of paper. He lifted the paper from the desk. He sat it in front of him and looked around a bit more. He wasn’t sure why, it was only he and Shelly. He took his right hand and carefully ran his long bony fingers over the back of his left palm thinking about the little blonde girl that had spilled coffee all over one of his favorite suits. He picked up the phone and dialed a few numbers, then promptly put it down again. Again he looked around and after a long pause he picked the phone up again and begin to dial the numbers on the small white piece of paper. ********
