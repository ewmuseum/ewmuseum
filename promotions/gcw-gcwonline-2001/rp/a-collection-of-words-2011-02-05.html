A Collection of Words

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Voice of God: We (and by we, I mean you) find ourselves backstage at GCW’s latest house show is Rogers Dick, Townsville. Or is it Rogers Town Dicksville. It could very well by Dick Town, Rogersville. Anyway, it was one of those places where the women are free and the men are goats. The capacity crowd of twelvity billion is on hand to enjoy a spectacle of sports that no man nor woman nor goat nor horse nor boat nor mouse nor Chris Bagwell has ever seen. Ring announcer Pablo Escobar the Third is announcing some form of match but that is not important which is important is backstage there is a man. Man: Hello, I am backstage. Voice of God: The man was popular late 90s UK Light Entertainer and Quiz Show host, Les Dennis. Man: My name is Les Dennis, popular 90s UK Light Entertainer and Quiz Show host. Voice of God: I wasn’t lying. Man: I’m not sure what I’m doing here is Dicksville, Rogertown… VoG: That was it, I knew it was some arrangement of those words. Suddenly, as if by wizardy magic, Philip Martin Atken (known by some as PMA) appears in front off popular 90s UK Light Entertainer and Quiz Show host Les Dennis. PMA (Philip Martin Atken): Popular 90s UK Light Entertainer and Quiz Show host Les Dennis???? WHAT ARE YOU DOING HERE???? Les Dennis (Popular UK Light Entertainer and Quiz Show Host): RP padding. PMA: What? Les Dennis (A man who was popular in the 90s and may have have a quiz show host): Yeah, some guy thought if we listed my accolades, that of me being a popular 90s quiz show host and light entertainer that it’d pad your RP word count and make it look like you’d done more work than you actually had. What with you completely drawing a blank on how to approach this gimmick show and everything. PMA: Kayfabe man. Kayfabe. Les Dennis: I totally sexed up Kay Fabe. PMA: That is a reference that I, Philip Martin Atken, PMA for short, don’t even get. Les Dennis: Why isn’t this thing colourful? Everything else was colourful. PMA: Well, you see Les… Suddenly a dinosaur appears from nowhere and eats the head of Les Dennis, bloody is gushing everywhere, oh the horror, the horror. PMA: NO! NOT POPULAR TV ENTERTAINER AND LIGHT SHOW HOST LES DENNIS! HE WAS TOO YOUNG AND/OR OLD FOR THIS SHIT! VoG: The dinosaur disappears as quickly as it appeared, which in my opinion was pretty damn quick. I blame warlockry, the natural enemy of wizardry. Suddenly a cat walks by the camera. PMA: WHAT A GAY CAT. THIS IS THE GAYEST CAT I EVER SAW. Cameraboy the Fifth: Why is it gay? VoG: PMA (Philip Matthew Atkins) shrugs his shoulders. PMA: I read something about gay cats yesterday, it is a reference. Do you know what references are? CtF: Yeah sure, it’s when a prospective employer… PMA: SHUT UP WITH YOU AND YOUR FANCY WORDS BOY! THIS IS PHIL’S TIME! VoG: And so PMA (Phyllis Diller) did kick Cameraboy the Fifth in the nuts with his world famous NUTSHOT TILDE EXCLAIM and low CtF did slump to the ground. Good thing for tripods eh? Good thing indeed! PMA: Hello GCW viewing audience who watch the GCW Promo Channel on your local cable provider, call now to order. It is I, The Smartest Man in Wrestling Today, The Real Ratings Grabber, The Best Man in the World, YOUR Entertainment Saviour and winner of the Lord Sexypants Contest 2002, Phillycakes Atken and I am here today at a GCW House Show because… VoG: There was a pause that could’ve held a wet fart. Not a dry fart. A big stinky, noise, wet fart. PMA: Actually I’m not sure. I’m pretty sure no GCW talent actually wrestles at these things. Do people pay money exclusively for in-ring promos? That doesn’t seem like a worthwhile investment in my opinion. VoG: Bonny Jorealis suddenly appears on the screen BJ: Splork. VoG: The screen appears back to that guy with the three names face. PMA: I don’t get it. VoG: People like name dropping and references. PMA: Am I meant to be able to hear you? VoG: Probably not. PMA: LEAVE THE MEMORIES ALONE. VoG: I don’t get it. PMA: It is a reference. VoG: What happened to that wall in front of you? PMA: I think it collapsed. VoG: Jay Terror stumbles around in front of the camera and then disappears again. The next noise heard is that of very loud dry heaving. PMA: Do we have permission for him to be here? VoG: No. PMA: Do we need it? VoG: I’d say so. PMA: CHEESE IT! VoG: And low, Terrance Stamp (PMA) did run away from the scene, hands flailing in a panic. Suddenly we cut to. PMA: HELLO! I AM IN THE RING NOW! VoG: PMA standing in front of the crowd of at least two billion, many of them confused at this turn of events, partially because it is not a GCW event but rather some kind of birthday party for the King of Spain. PMA: What? How is that even possible? That literally makes no sense. VoG: Okay, maybe it was a GCW event. PMA (Pregnant Mothers Association) has two microphones in his hand and a single turntable, he really puts his own spin on things. PMA: HELLO GCW! VoG: I am pushing a button labelled “crickets.sfx” right now. PMA: IT IS I! PHIL ATKEN! REMEMBER ME????? VoG: I push it again. PMA: Anyways, just to let y’all know I have a match with a woman and I’m going to beat the women up good and proper then prolly rape her in the ring, because rape is funny and I’m an evil bad guy. So… by that logic you will hate me while laughing at my attempted rape. WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU PEOPLE. Anyway, that’s all I gots to say, I’ma beat a woman at Worldwide then maybe I’ll force her into sexual acts with my giant penis. ENJOY THE REST OF THE SHOW! VoG: This is the main event. PMA: Wow, these people got jipped. Wait, is that racist. VoG: I dunno, probably. FADE TO BLACK. PMA: You know yelling that does nothing. VoG: Oh, what do I need to do? PMA: Brackets for action! VoG: I see. (FADE TO BLACK)
