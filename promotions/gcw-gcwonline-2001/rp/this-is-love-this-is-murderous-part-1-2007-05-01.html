After the sun goes down the rampant heat quickly cools, and chilled breezes sweep across the desert sands. This night was no different than any other. A desert enveloped in a blanket of cold with winds that feel as if they could slice the skin. Many would not dare venture out into night. Vivica J. Valentine was not many, she was one of a kind. With her 2006 black Chrysler Crossfire floored, she sped through the unsupervised desert road with speeds exceeding 100 miles per hour. Yet her mind sped even faster. A million things went through the mind of The Fearless Phenom as she watched the flame-licked skies ahead. Glowing in hues of orange, pink, and violet. It could only mean one thing: someone she was very close to was in a lot of trouble. The exclusive estate now owned by Lia and Virgil Ambrosi was the only thing even vaguely in the area. A thought of possibly never seeing her best friend again cycled through her mind over and over again as the smell of thick smoke and embers began to infiltrate her nostrils. There were so many things she needed to say that she never said. There were so many moments that she never got to thank the one formally known as Shadow for. A choir of lights came into sight as the flames towered above the stone gate that enclosed the home. Beacons of red, blue, yellow, and white together formed a symbol of distress. It could not end like this, not after everything they've gone through. The home came into sight as Vivica threw her sports car into a screeching halt that scared some of the nearby medics. The home appeared to be fine. This is when reality began to set in. "Not Markus..." Vivica said aloud before leaving her car, "Please not be Markus." Her eyes began to swell up with tears and her throat began to close and dry up like the desert she found herself surrounded by on the outskirts of Las Vegas. Sprinting as fast as she could, she made her way towards the medics and police officials. Who quickly intercepted her path and held her back. "I need to see what's going on!" Vivica tried to keep a calm demeanor about her, but it obviously wasn't working, "My best friends are in there! They need me!" As she was held back by a Las Vegas police officer, the sight of a guest house on the premises etched itself into her memories forever. Burning bright into the barren skies, it certainly appeared like there was no way in hell anyone would ever get out. She watched the roof collapse as water thundered down upon the flames from the hose of a fire engine. "You need to calm down ma'am" The officer said, "We're all trained professionals here." "But you don't understand!" She screamed out, tears now streaming down her face like a waterfall as she shoved the police officer aside and began to make a move towards the building. With her heart pounding, The Black Mariah threw her own well being by the wayside just a week away from her first round Warpath Match. Yet destiny had other plans as her attempt to save Markus was short-lived. Quickly she was tackled to the ground by another Las Vegas Police Officer. "He's not like most people! He's going to die!" Vivica tried to claw her way out unsuccessfully as she watched her hopes for the future burn in a pyre into the night. Inaudible screams and moans began to escape her lips as she gave up and buried her head into her heads. "It'll be okay..." One of the officers said putting his hand on her back as she made it to a sitting position on the dry, cracked earth. "Get the fuck away from me!" She screamed out. Lashing out at everyone around her was the only way she new how to react. Her usually energetic and lively cerulean blue eyes were haunted by grief and horror. Someone important to her was lost. Someone who she could even say that she loved. The part that hurt the most, was that she never could tell him the truth... ---- Chapter One: Memoirs Of A Past Life ---- Awaken. Sitting straight up in his bed, a man frantically looks around his room. The white stone walls. The stainless steel tables. The one-way mirror. The adjustable bed. The IV in his arm. Where was he? What in the hell happened? Ripping the IV out of his arm, a stream of blood shoots out of the needle and begins to run down his arm. His eyes widen as he sees the sight of blood squirting all over the place. Quickly he grabs the needle and holds it into his mouth. He rocks back and forth quickly, the feel of blood running down the back of his throat is soothing. The rocking begins to slow down as he closes his pale colored eyes, hidden behind a layer of long black hair. His breathing goes from a near hyper-ventilating gasp to a slow deep pace when he throws the IV off to the side of his bed. Putting his hands over his head he tries to regain control of himself, but couldn't quite shake the feeling he was being watched. Watched like an animal. A sideshow attraction, locked in a cage for the sake of someone elses entertainment. It wasn't the first time he'd felt this way, but the last time he had that feeling seemed like a lifetime ago. Things had changed since then. His life now was a life that some people find scary, but others find fascinatingly romantic. Ever after over a year, he still didn't quite grasp what was so romantic about this curse. Cursed to roam the earth forever. Cursed to watch the ones you care about die and you can't do a damn thing about them. Except pass the curse on, and make them just as miserable as you. Maybe that was his reluctance in telling her. Getting out of bed he walked over to a full sized mirror hanging on the wall nearby. Looking back at his reflection, he didn't see anything that even closely resembled the man he was once. His once scrawny body now looked like that of a Greek god. His once mesmerizing green eyes were now pale as the moon. His boyish face had become almost like a porcelain doll. His hair was long, dry, and out of control. His teeth were piercing. He was everything he wasn't before. It was indeed a lifetime ago. A life that ended in the very room he stood in today. The boarded and painted window reminded him of where he was. He tried to look away, hoping if he ignored it... it wouldn't be true. He continued to stare back at what he was in the mirror. What he saw to this day wasn't a man. No, it was a monster. An animal. A sideshow. A freak. Closing his eyes he turned his back to the mirror. He once thought that as time passed it would eventually become easier... even accepted. The day had not yet come, and he feared dearly that it never would. Learning what he was still came slowly to him, mainly due to the lack of desire to do so. Yet it was something that had to be done, whether he liked it or not. She knew it too. That's why she left him behind. It was something that he had to do. If not, how would he ever control the monster that lurked deep down inside? After opening his eyes, a simple gaze across the room revealed to him something out of place. So much so that it seemed preplanned to catch his attention. A black notebook, with a leather cover encrested with a foil stamped logo. The logo was a Z inside of a circle, silver embossed on black leather. The top of the notebook was a serial number, AZ2660-01. Intrigued, he opened the notebook to read what appeared to be a detailed log of events. Events that seemed all too familiar. Logged by the woman who has been in his every thought for the better part of six months. As he read along, he could almost hear her voice again. PROJECT FORSAKEN Operations Manager: Vivica Julian Valentine PATIENT: Markus Blair. College Student. 25 Years Old. We were contacted by his fiance, Jessica Montero (24 Years Old) whose father is an executive for Zenith Corporation. MISSION STATEMENT: The patient has contacted an extremely rare unnamed disease that effectively destroys the immune system, but does not follow the consistent patterns of HIV or AIDS. Our mission is to boost the immune system by splicing the DNA of the patient with a donor who shall only go by "Shadow." The donor is of the vampire species. The desired effect is to effectively boost the patients DNA so he may live. We will also monitor his progress as far as his own mortality. We hope to have great results with this, hopefully giving the patient immortality, inhuman speed, and strength without the side effects of the vampiric nature, namely the need to feed. FIRST ENTRY: The patient was evaluated by the medical team who will be working with him for the first team. They give him no longer than 7 days to live with the current progression of his disease. He is being kept in solitary confinement, breathing through a respirator. Any contamination could greatly impact our project for the worse. Many samples were taken from both the patient and the donor. SECOND ENTRY: The patient has become significantly weaker, and was unable to move during the beginning of the splicing process. Will monitor his progress beginning tomorrow. THIRD ENTRY: The splicing process has been completed, and we have seen minimal success. The patient was more responsive. FOURTH ENTRY: Significant improvement has occurred since the process began. The patient is now able to walk on his own and has begun to eat on his own. No longer needing a respirator or an IV, the patient has begun a diet of soft foods. The only feedback that has occurred is these foods "taste different". FIFTH ENTRY: The patient has said he "feels better than I have in years." So far the project has been a success. The patient has been able to converse with loved ones in supervised telephone conversations and has moved onto a normal diet. SIXTH ENTRY: Thus far the experiment has been a roaring success. The disease has seemed to now be a distant memory and body functions have returned to that of a man at his peak physical condition. Only side-effect reported was the patient has seemed to add 10 pounds of muscle over the past couple of days with minimal physical activity. SEVENTH ENTRY: The patient has began to complain about the lighting in his room. His window has been taped off due to minor sunburns that have begun to form on his body. The sensitivity to the sun is an unexpected side effect from the vampiric DNA. It was a strand previously thought to have been spliced out. We will have to further examine the donor's DNA sample to better understand. EIGHTH ENTRY: The patient has requested a change in diet, eliminating everything except steak, cheeseburgers, and chicken. His request has been granted due to his health taking a turn of the worse. We hope a change in diet will better satisfy the patient and regain his heath. NINTH ENTRY: Even with the diet change, the patient's health has continued to decline. His immune system has almost returned to the state in which he arrived. We have returned him to a quarantine for safety measures. The medical team have been evaluating many samples, but cannot seems to find the source. Patient has complained about his meals being overcooked, although they are being prepared just the same as the rest of his stay. TENTH ENTRY: An IV has been reinserted into the patient's arm and meals have been ended. While the patient seemed angered by the decision, we believe it is the best for his health. The patient is too weak to chew his own food. ELEVENTH ENTRY: A recent sample has found the DNA of the patient has been changed, and now more resembles that of the sample of the donor. We are worried the splicing has not taken and the immune system seems to cease to exist. Patient has continued to order meats, and has become increasingly hostile with myself and the medical team. TWELFTH ENTRY: The patient is now under twenty four hour surveillance and has entered a comatose state. Recent samples have many similarities to samples of cadavers. Multiple organs have shut down and emergency surgery is required. Without replacement organs we give the patient no longer than 48 hours to live. ---- Chapter Two: The Final Days Of Markus Blair ---- THIRTEENTH ENTRY: We came in this morning to fine the patient's heartbeat to be slow, then non-existent. After a flatline we were unable to return the heartbeat. The estimated time of death was 10:12am. Immediate friends and family will be contacted tomorrow. Many observations to come tomorrow, to find out what happened. As of right now, our medical team is baffled. Date: January 28, 2006 Location: Zenith Corporation Medical Facility (Chicago, Illinois) beep... beep... beep... You could cut the tension in the air with a knife. More than a dozen medical personnel filled the tiny bedroom sized room. Most of them formed a semi-circle around a bed directly in the middle. The sound of a heartbeat was what motivated all of them. After all, their paychecks were in the balance. You could accuse Zenith Corporation of just about everything except for not paying well. Although it was known to the doctors that you get paid more if the project is a success. beep... beep... beep... The slow beep was that of Markus Blair, comatose, almost dead looking laying in bed. His eyes closed, his shallow breaths and lengthy pulse made him look like a wax figure laying on the bed. "We need to operate now if he has any chance to live!" One of the doctors shouted. He wore black, thick rimmed glasses. Obviously an older gentleman, from his greying eye brows. "Cut him open? With next to no immunities?" One of the other doctors retorted, "He'll die in minutes!" "Well we can't just stand here." A nurse said from behind the semi-circle. Tapping her fingers on a stainless steel cart sitting next to her, loaded with operation tools she looked concern. beep... beep... beep... From behind a one way mirror a look of worry crossed the usually jubilant face of Vivica Julian Valentine, fresh off her termination from Global Wrestling. Her cerulean blue eyes looked on with concern, sitting at a rather large desk... her head rested upon her hands. Behind her stood a large man wearing a black custom made suit. His pale cadet blue eyes gazed emotionless at the scene. "I don't think he's going to make it Christian." Vivica said aloud, moving her hand from under her chin over her forehead. Taking a deep breath and closing her eyes, she tried to collect her thoughts. "Failure isn't an option." Christian spoke confident and to-the-point, "Don't make me regret putting you in charge of this project Viv." "I won't fail." Vivica responded, "Not if I can help it." beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee... "We need to do something now!" A doctor screamed as the heartbeat of Markus Blair flatlined. He looked over at a nurse as she scrambled across the room as fast as she could. Struggling to open a closet door with panic, she finally pulls it open and grabs a machine from inside. Wheeling it across the room, she's obviously shaking. "CLEAR!" The shook a volt of electricity through the limp body of Markus Blair, making him uncontrollably spasm into the air. Still unconscious he lands with a beep, followed by another flatline. One of the nurses puts her hands up on her head, nervous and helpless. Once again the doctor listens as the machine powers back up. "CLEAR!" Another shot to the chest of Markus Blair sparks and makes his body jump into the air. Another beep, another flatline. One of the doctors begins shaking his head in disappointment as a different doctor re-powers up the machine. His heart races as high pitched whizzing of the machine reaches a climax. "CLEAR!!!" eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee... "Apparently you can't." Christian spoke, finally breaking the awkward silence between the stressed couple. Vivica wasn't paying attention though. Her heart raced along with those of the doctors inside. She wanted to so badly to succeed. Something to hang her hat on and say 'look at me, look what I did.' Biting her fingernails thoughts of inevitable failure flooded her mind, and she could no longer watch. Burying her head into her arms on the desk. The gaze of The Midnight Messiah into her back shook into her soul. She could feel it tingle through her spine. This wasn't what she was meant to do. Deep down inside she knew it didn't feel like her. It was such a feeling of helplessness. Especially being around one of the most successful men in the United States. Everything he seemed to touch turned into gold. An extraordinary businessman, good looking, a talented fighter, and more money than he'd ever know what to do with. Why couldn't she be like that? It was so frustrating to behold his greatness, and not know what your calling in life is. eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee... "CLEAR!" One last bolt to the chest sent shimmers through the bones of Mr. Blair. Frustrated, the doctor threw the instruments to the ground and let out an audible scream of anger. Now it wasn't the hit on his paycheck that upset him, it was the feeling of failure. "He's gone." One of the nurses stated the obvious, as some of the others started to cry. The panic of the room suddenly came to a halt, and melancholy rushed through the air like a passing breeze. The flatline still sounded in the background, a painful reminder of their loss ringing true through the silence. "We did our best." The older doctor with the thick rimmed glasses spoke, breaking the silence, "It just wasn't enough. It wasn't mean to be. It wasn't in God's plan." eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee... "Congratulations Miss Valentine." Christian Zenith spoke, "You killed a man." "It wasn't MY fault." Vivica spoke out, "I didn't volunteer for this you know. It was something you told me I HAD to do. I HAD to prove myself to you. After all these years I'm STILL being forced to show my worth. I'm not a fucking employee Chris. I'm going to be your wife someday." "What did you want me to do?" Christian spoke, not allowing the woman who would months later be known as The Black Mariah rattle him, "Let you keep going out and getting your ass kicked in front of millions of people? I can't just sit back and watch that happen." "Why? Because you can't stand to watch anything associate with you LOSE every once in a while?" Vivica was fired up, on the other hand. Clearly rattled from the incident that had just occurred. "We're HUMAN Chris. FUCKING HUMAN BEINGS WITH FLAWS AND WE MAKE MISTAKES. You make mistakes, I make mistakes, we all make mistakes. We're not Gods." "And we're certainly not getting any closer by fucking up everything are we Vivica?" Christian made his way towards the door, "The death of Markus Blair is unacceptable. It looks like I'll have to find something else for you to do around here." "Why can't I just wrestle Chris?" Vivica interrupts his departure, "It's what I WANT to do. Sure I may struggle a little at first. I'm willing to work hard and train. Lia has already been looking at employment offers from GCW AND PRIME. She's offered to help me, train me, make me be able to do what I want to do in life." "Shadow is very good at what she does..." Christian speaks before walking out the door, "I'll consider it." Closing the door behind him Vivica picks up the pencil sharpener on the desk and hurls it at the wall. Watching it shatter into a thousand pieces on the ground. Her breathing is unstable, her face turns red, and her fists shake with rage. "You're not my fucking father..." Vivica muttered under her breath. Tears of anger began to stream down her face as she watched them pull the white sheet over the body of Markus Blair in the other room. A man she got to know quite well in the time they spent together. He didn't deserve to die, and she didn't deserve to live in this hell. You don't always get what you deserve though. This proved to be a learning experience for Vivica J. Valentine. One of many to come. The haunting sound of the flatline still chimed in her head from time to time. Driving her harder to succeed. Driving her not to be a failure. Driving her path to what she honestly believed... was destiny. ---- Intermission: Rise Above ---- As told by Vivica J. This business had made me weep tears of joy and tears of anger. This business has made me bleed, given me a concussion, and has riddled my body in pain to the point where I've needed help out of bed in the morning. When both your asses are at home watching us compete for a World Title shot. You keep punching that punching bag Markus thinking you're going to maim me. The difference is, I'll punch you back... and knock you the fuck out.
