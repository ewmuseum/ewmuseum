Promises

21 September 2005

Date: 9/21/05 If I closed my eyes titled my head back and took a deep breath I could almost forget my surroundings. However that was impossible. The day may have been sunny, but I knew why I was here. It was kind of hard to forget. I felt the hand on my arm. 'You okay?' 'Yeah, it's been ten years but it never gets easier.' I said, opening my eyes and turning to face her. 'How about you?' 'It's hard, but I made her a promise, and I intend to keep it.' I took her hand and we walked a path we had tread once a year for the last ten years. We stopped when we reached the spot. I felt my chest tighten, as I looked down on the headstone. Jennifer Denise Russell 1975-1995 Beloved Daughter and Sister 'We're back sis.' P.J said quietly. 'Just like we promised we would be.' P.J laid her flowers down at the foot of the headstone, then her mother did, then I did. None of us tried to hold back our tears, if there ever was a time for crying this was it. 'It never gets any easier baby.' Julie said as she looked at her daughters resting place. 'But I just take things day by day.' It never did get any easier to visit a graveyard, but the three of us had made a promise and it was one we kept for the last ten years. Once a year on the same day we visited Jennifer. The same day we buried her. It had gotten to the point where we just stood there and didn't say anything. We saved the reminescing for later. I had never been much of a religious person, but I believed that Jennifer could hear our thoughts and was looking down on us, smiling. Jeff had been kind enough to loan me his jet so I could take the trip from Canada to Los Angeles. I let Steven know that I would be back in time for my match at WorldWide, but that I had a standing engagment that I could not get out of. The worst part for me was remembering what had happened ten years earlier. ********** I was a college student at UCLA, and was dating a lovely young woman named P.J Russell. Jennifer was her twin sister. One day while sitting in my dorm room working on a paper, there was a knock at my door. I found P.J standing there tears running down her face. 'Mike, it's gotten worse.' was all she said and fell into my arms sobbing. She didn't need to tell me more, I knew what she meant. Jennifer was dying, had been for months. She had AIDS. She'd contracted it from her ex-boyfriend. P.J's mother Julie lived in LA, not more than an hours drive from campus. My paper forgotten, we hopped in my car and headed to the hospital. Jennifer was sitting up in bed her mother by her side, Jennifer had a smile on her face despite the fact that things had taken a turn for the worse. That was the thing about Jenny, no matter how dark the cloud, she could see the silver lining. 'Hey.' she said as we walked in. Julie and P.J were the only family Jenny had. Their father had gotten out of the picture as soon as he found out Julie was pregnant, they weren't even married yet when she had gotten pregnant. He wasn't ready for a commitment, especially not to children, so when he found out she was pregnant he simply took off. Bastard. It was bad enough that Julie was an orphan, but she had a strong support system of friends that were able to help her through things even going to far as to take her to LaMaze class and be there when the girls were born. And there was always someone to babysit while she worked hard to keep clothes in the girls mouths, clothes on their backs and a roof over their heads. She was a strong woman, and managed to be a single mother of twin girls and did a damn good job of it. P.J and I met my freshman year in college, it was almost love at first sight. Julie and Jenny liked me as well, which relived P.J to no end. During the same time P.J and I started dating Jenny met Mario. And I swear, upon seeing this guy you couldn't help but think that his name was Mario. He looked like a Mario. This guy just screamed 'Italian'. But it turned out Jenny was not the only girl he was seeing. There were at least two more that I found out about later from some friends of mine. Mind you I was only eighteen and still several years away from becoming a wrestler, but I wanted very much to kick his ass when I found out, seeing as how Jenny was madly in love with him. Then the worst part came when Jenny got sick. At first she thought it was just the flu, but things got worse and thats when she found out she had AIDS. I wanted to beat Mario to death when she told me. 'I don't know if it was him, after all he's not the first guy I ever slept with.' 'But Jen, he's been sleeping around on you!' I protested, 'What's to say one of those skanks he has on the side didn't give it to him and he gave it to you?' 'If he did, then that means he has it too, and that would mean he's got enough problems as it is without you pounding his face in. I appreciate the gesture though.' Jenny said as I visited her in the hospital not too long after she was admitted. 'But Jen--' 'Mike, beating him up isn't going to solve anything.' 'It'll make me feel better.' I muttered. 'But it won't cure me, and like I said, if he does have it, he's got something bigger to worry about than you.' Jenny said, 'Mike, I know you're mad, but I'm the one laying here in a hospital bed. Just promise me you won't confront Mario, let the bastard find out for himself. Take care of my sister. She needs you.' I nodded, she was right of course. Beating Mario up wouldn't solve anything. Jenny would still be sick, and I would probably be in trouble for beating his ass. So I left him alone and concentrated on being emotional support for P.J. But the day it got worse I was just as much as an emotional wreck as she was. 'How you holding up?' I lamely asked as we sat in the hospital room with her the day we came to see her after getting the news that things had gotten worse. 'Like shit.' Jenny said, 'I can barely lift my arms up, they got so many tubes in me, I think I'm more machine than human, and I'm sweating like a pig. Other than that I'm just peachy.' she offered a weak smile. I could see it was taking a lot of energy for her just to be talking. 'Doctors say that there's not much else they can do.' Julie said, 'The drug treatments aren't doing diddly shit, and since her immune system's shut down, the disease is turing her body against her.' 'Have you heard anything about...you know who?' P.J asked, not wanting to even mention Mario's name. 'No,' Jenny said and paused as a coughing fit wracked her body. 'And I don't fuckin' care either. Like I said, if I did get this from him, I'm content with knowing that he's gotta be as miserable as I am.' 'Jenny--' Julie said. 'Mom, I know I shouldn't feel that way, but I can't help it. I mean he was sleeping around on me! With at least two other people! I loved him, but he didn't love me. There was no way he could have! Not if he was cheating on me. And there's no telling how long he'd been with those other girls. For all I know he could have had them on the side when he met me. Which means I was just another notch in his belt.' 'I even had to promise not to beat him up.' I said, 'Seeing her point that if he is infected, then my beating him up would be a moot point.' We steered the conversation away from him and the grim situation at hand, just because we were all getting agitated. P.J and I missed the next two days of school, taking the time to sit by Jenny's side, not wanting to leave until the doctors could tell us something, anything. But each day it was just more of the same 'We've done all we can do.' crap. I felt so frustrated at one point I wanted to scream. I didn't beat up Mario, but I did find one of his other girlfriends and told her the news. I wasn't going to go near him, I didn't trust myself to control myself if I did see him, so I did the next best thing. I warned off the girls that he was sleeping with. She looked at me with mute shock. 'I don't know who you are, or why you're telling me this, or why I should believe you--' 'I don't give a flying fuck if you believe me or not. I'm just telling you what I know, and that is you're not the only girl he's got, and one of them is sick. She has no idea that I'm here, so you can take this any way you want. But if I were you I'd get myself checked out.' I didn't bother following up on her, because I had said my piece, it was up to her to either believe me or think I was just some asshole telling her things about her boyfriend behind his back. The next day in the hospital the three of us sat gathered around talking to Jenny and making her laugh. Then she said she needed a nap. She closed her eyes and drfted off...and never woke up. I still remember one of the last things she said. 'Just promise me, after I'm gone, you won't forget about me.' The funeral was held a week later. Julie, P.J, myself and a few of Jenny's friends were the only ones at the funeral, a small quiet ceremony was all we had. When it was down to just Julie, P.J and myself we all made a promise. We wouldn't forget about her. And once a year for the last ten years we hadn't. I had missed shows to keep my promise. I had even ducked out of a title defesne just to make sure I would be there for her. All this time later it still hurt. I stayed until late into the night before I left LA to fly to Calgary so I could make WorldWide. I had kept my promise to Jennifer, and I'd be back next year. I made a promise and I don't break my promises.
