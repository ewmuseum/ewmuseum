a way out

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/05 “Yeah I’m sure. Tonight at 8pm at the eastern docks. Look, you asked me to find out, I found out. I’m done now ok?” Ricky hangs up the phone, and glances out onto the busy streets, people moving about with their groceries and the likes. Ricky pulls his jacket up around his neck and lowers his hat before jumping into his car. He reaches down and grabs his cell phone from the seat next to him and punches in a few numbers before lifting the phone to his ear. “Hey it’s Ricky. It’s goin down tonight at the docks.” “Tonight”!” comes the voice from the other end. “Yah, I couldn’t delay any longer. If I had they would have suspected something. But I need you to meet me there, I don’t think they want me to walk away from this one” “Ricky this is insane. You need to get out of there. Skip town” “You know they will find me.” “Christ, how did you get into this” Even more importantly how do you get out of it?” “I don’t know how I got into it, and honestly I think tonight is my out.” “That don’t sound promising.” “Look just meet me at the eastern docks tonight at like 7-ish. I want to get there early to make sure everything is in place.” Ricky hangs up the phone and tosses it into the passenger seat, and floors the gas pedal. He winds through the city streets before squealing to a stop outside of a local pawn shop. Ricky leaps from his car and heads inside. He approaches the counter and points at the pistols. “I’d like to have a look at those.” “Sorry Ricky, Mr Tiantes has a block on them.” “How do you know me?” “Ricky, we know more then you could ever imagine. Mr Tiantes don’t want you handling no firearms for some reason.” “Sonofa”.” Ricky leaps across the counter and grabs the man’s collar and slams him face first against the hard counter top. He leans in close to his ear and whispers “You are goin to sell me a gun. You aren’t goin to tell Tiantes nothing. You haven’t seen or heard from me” “I can’t do that Ricky” the man mutters under the pressure of Ricky’s weight pushing his head against the countertop “Tiantes will kill me” “If you tell him anything’I will kill you” Ricky grabs a handful of the man’s hair and slams his head down on the counter rendering him unconscious. Ricky the n reaches over the gun case and grabs a pistol from inside, standard glock 9. He then grabs some shells from behind the counter and leaves the store, flipping the sign on the door to “Closed” before hopping back in his car and heading off. ~*~ 6:58pm Eastern Docks ~*~ “Come on where the hell are you” Ricky is standing on the docks smoking a cigarette when a set of headlights can be seen pulling in. “Excellent” Ricky mutters as he watches the car move closer. Ricky reaches into his belt and pulls the pistol from his waistline. He looks around the docks, where the sun in starting to set, and notices just a few shadows caused by the warehouses located there. He cocks the gun when suddenly he is hit from behind as the car pulls up. Ricky collapses in a heap his world going black from the knock on the head. ~*~ 8:15pm ~*~ Ricky slowly opens his eyes, having no idea of the time or place he is in. It’s dark. Ricky goes to sit up and hits his head again. He clenches his fist in pain and he holds his head with his other hand. Ricky soon realizes where he is. A clicking noise is heard as the roof of his surroundings is raised up. A large man yanks him from the trunk in which he had been occupying. He is slammed against the side of the car as the trunk is slammed shut. “Ricky you disappoint me” “Tiantes?” “MR Tiantes” The man slams a hand into the midsection of Ricky. Ricky doubles over but is soon stood back upright by the other men. “You tried to screw me over Ricky” “No I didn’t’I was just looking out for me. I wanted to ensure MY safety” “Obviously you have done a great job of that. You were safe with me Ricky. You were alive with me. You had no fears, or problems in life. Was it the money” Is it the fame?” “I just wanted out.” “Out” There is no out? Ricky glares at the man in front of him wearing his high dollar Italian suit, and black leather gloves. His hair slightly grayed yet sophisticated. He speaks with a hard Italian accent as he continues grilling Ricky. “Why Ricky” Why The gun? Why the friends? Why were you here so early? Why Ricky!”” “I was?” Mr Tiantes silences Ricky with another shot to the gut. “Shut up you maggot” The man spits in the face of Ricky, following it up with a hard slap. “Speaking of your friend, have you seen him yet?” The man cracks a sadistic smile “You better not hurt him. He has nothing to do with it!” “I better not do what” Ricky you obviously failed to remember who is in control. In control of your destiny, in control of you future, and in control of your life.” Ricky struggles to free himself as a body is drug from the back of the car and tossed on the ground in front of him. The man lays on the ground, blood dripping from his nose and mouth, not moving. An obvious fracture across the cheekbone can be seen. Ricky’s eye well up with tears as he stares at his broken and battered friend. “Ricky, this is what happens when you screw me. There is no out. Well, let me rephrase. There is one way out, and it looks tome like you found it.” The man pulls a gun from his waist pointing it at Ricky’s head, Ricky closes his eyes, as the thought of his friend lying there, bring s tear steaming down his cheek. ~*~*~*~*~ Ricky jerks awake and sits straight up in bed, sweat streaming down his face. He looks around frantically, and grabs the phone lying beside his bed punching in some numbers. A groggy voice on the other end picks up. “Hello?” “Jimmy” Is that you?” “Yeah. Ricky” You ok?” “Yeah I think so. I guess it was a “never mind” “Dream?” Jimmy finishes the sentence for him. “Yeah, sorry to have woke you. I’ll talk to you tomorrow man?” Ricky hangs the phone up and collapses back on the bed, running his hands through his hair, breathing out deeply.
