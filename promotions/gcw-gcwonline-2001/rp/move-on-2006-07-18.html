Move on

18 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-18 You don't know. You don't know because I haven't told you. Time changes everything, doesn't it? Time heals and time hurts, time degrades and time strengthens. Give time enough time and things will start to repeat. Some things will even start to show patterns. Every eon is a new day, sun up and sun down, and every cycle has one purpose; to continue into the next one. No more, no less. And she wouldn't know that even if I did tell her. 'Coffee, John?' I nod. No words form. Somewhere deep inside I'm kidding myself into believing it's because I haven't had my first cup of coffee of the day, that I need some sort of kickstart. In some deep recess within Johnathan Fitzwillis, I still think that a bitter black and brown mug full of steaming caffeine is what I need to get motivated to go through the motions another day in a row. Fooling myself and fooling her, maybe I do one in order to accomplish the other, my taste buds nearly fry off at the first gulp of black acid that touches them. And for a brief second, I can't taste regret or the past. 'So, your flight leaves pretty soon, right?' Another nod. I don't mean to be quiet or rude or any of those other things that I guess I am. I'm just...being Johnny. Being. Living. It's not that my mind is elsewhere. It's that I feel like my mind should be elsewhere. I have a re-debut match against someone that, frankly, I would like to leave in a heap in the middle of the ring. I have a few loose ends to tie at GCW, and on the other hand I have a few threads to pull on. I've got a plane ride. I've got the airport. I've got the drive there, the trip home, the hotels, the aching and the beating that I'll probably take, and everything in between. And honestly, my problem is that my mind isn't on any of those things. It's on the fucking cup of coffee. And it's on what I hide from Sarah. 'Well, John...you're having a quiet day.' One of the things I hate and accept at the same time is repetition. I don't think I've made any attempt to hide my belief that life is constantly repeating itself, whether by Hindu ideas of reincarnation or by modern approaches like algorhythms, and even in attempting to break the chain do I remain held fast by it. Try to stop the repitition, you're just adding to it. Overthrow one dictator and be ruled by another. Just like the wise man once said...in with the new boss, the same as the old boss. Sarah's problem is that I don't talk to her about a lot of things. She thinks I'm uncomfortable sharing anything that goes on inside my head because I'm embarassed of it, or because I think I'll scare her. She's accepted my ex-girlfriend and my daughter, and she's even gotten over the fact that the most important woman in my life is named after the woman I'm currently dating. And, I'm sorry, but there's something about that whole love connection that even screws with my head. I mean, Kate, the girl that I spent so much of my life with, has a daughter from me that I don't know about and names her Sarah. And why? Because when we were together, I used to call out the name 'Sarah' in my sleep. She asked why, and I told her it was my mother's name. And it was. But I was calling for this one right here. 'You excited about facing off against Seymour Almasy? Going right to the top of the card your first day back?' '...nope...' 'Really? Don't you recognize all the parallels and interesting inuendos with going against him when he went against you at his debut and you went against him in a tag match just before you left and all that?' '..I do.' 'And?' 'You asked if I was excited about it. I'm not.' Keep staring at the coffee. Its swirling loses me, makes me lose myself for a moment. I can remember drinking what looked and tasted like the same cup of coffee with Kate, with my sister, and even with my mother. Maybe it's something Johnathan Fitzwillis does with women he cares about at some sort of apex in his relationship with them. Or maybe it's just one of those things that aren't actually anything and we only notice them because we go searching. 'I would be. I mean, I can't imagine the feeling of being up there, in front of so many people, fighting tooth and nail against someone else. It's like...gladiators of Rome or something. Something so...barbaric. So primal.' 'It's not making you hot or anything, is it?' She raises an eyebrow and smirks a bit. 'What if it was?' And that's definitely tempting. 'Wanna...fool around real quick?' 'You've gotta get to the airport as soon as you finish that coffee.' 'I am in no hurry to finish the coffee.' She just smiles and shakes her head. The quiet times are the ones that I hate the most. Mainly because they're the ones that I don't want to ruin. These little perfect moments that ripen and blossom like fruits in the sun, rotting and wilting but being beautiful in their own way. And shattering them ends it, and I don't want it to end. I want this perfect moment to last forever, and not be lost in the shuffle of the past. But we all know that's not true. We all know that everything gets left in the past. Victories, defeats, changes made to changes and pleasure and pain. And I know Seymour, for instance, dwells in the past. Not that he is miserable there and is dreary about it. But that he lives there...that he uses the past to fuel his deeds of today. Old victories translate to new ones. Oh, the poetic justice of who was established versus who was getting his feet wet. Nevermind that I was off the wagon at the time. And conveniently forget when I beat him and Michael Stevens when I was partnered with a joker who barely showed. But I suppose the rose colored glasses that give Seymour a bonus to his ability to look at the past allow him to bypass all the true atrocities that he's faced. He sees the injustices that are done and over with and that he cannot do anything about, and those are what he lives in. He lives in the very realm of wanting what he can not have. And me? 'Don't worry, Johnny. Win your match, and I guarantee you'll have a nice welcome home present. I'll even be on top all night, just so you don't have to hold your sore, bruised body up.' 'Wouldn't that be if I lost?' 'Well, when you win, you're going to have plenty of time on top. On top of the world, on top of Almasy for the count, and on top of GCW. So, understand that a girl's got to have her time as well.' '...fair enough.' I plain and simply recognize the irreversible. And move away from wanting to change things that not only cannot be changed, but have already transpired and no longer exist, save for their effects. The pain of a loss, the loss of a love. A dead mother or a championship that slips away. They're already dust to me. I stated earlier that I don't make the same mistakes with Sarah that I made with Kate. I realize what those were; they were telling her what goes on. In dreams, in thoughts, and in prayers. I told Kate, through word and action, who exactly Johnathan Fitzwillis was. She heard, saw, or experienced every single thing that happened to me, and was nauseated and horrified by what I was. That I was not only a human, but by all accounts a pretty awful person. And deep down, she couldn't deal with it. Ever. Not Sarah. This is my clean slate. Just like a clean start at wrestling, she is my clean start at life. She sees Johnny Borealis as her dream man, the one that she knew so many years ago, and I intend to give her what she wants. If that means not being honest, and if that means not even being honest to myself, I don't give a damn. I've been down that road already. I've got to get running. Plane leaves pretty soon. One hand grabs the suitcase handle and I'm heading to the door. 'Later, Johnny.' 'See ya.' The other thing I love about Sarah. No long goodbyes. No ceremonial bullshit. No rituals. Nothing like that. It's just two people who can live together, living together. Just like the ring, in a way. Except the opposite. Two people who fight each other, fighting each other. Sometimes weapons, sometimes not. And no matter the rules, it's always what one person has against what another has. No other way to put it. I have the drive I used to lack. I have the ability I used to doubt. And I have the want to see this through. And I cannot wait to see the look on all their faces. Just like before. The cycle continues. Just like before.
