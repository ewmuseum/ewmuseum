The Feel Good Hit

4 August 2010

Date: 08/04/10 Nicotine WorldWide 134. Anaheim’s Honda Center. Before, just. Terns circled overhead, their incessant screeches scratching at your ears. Keh, keh, keh, they accused. Keh, keh, keh. Silhouetted against the setting Anaheim sun, the more judgmental of the flock swooped down to pick at the discarded junk-food peeking out of the dumpsters. Their bills snatching at soggy French fries and wilted lettuce, you remained fixed in the gaze of their beady eyes. “You know what I‘m about to do, don’t you?” you muttered to one who sternly sneered at you while pecking a morsel from a moldy, sesame bun. “I can see it; you want to claw my fucking eyes out.” A flicker of flame gave the dulled, dusk world a fleeting, amber-tinged glimpse of your scarred face, shadowed by the gaping hood of your sweatshirt. Your lungs filled with itchy smoke for the first time in… you couldn‘t remember. The sweet aroma of tobacco was blanketed by a charred stench. Your diaphragm fired off a rapid volley of short, shallow coughs, just like when you were a teenager, sneaking your first draw of a cigarette huddled in a bathroom stall. “You won’t be the only one,” you told it as a thunderous flutter propelled the tern back towards the circling, screeching jury. You exhaled a fine plume of charcoal smoke in its wake. Keh, keh, keh, they judged. Keh, keh, keh. The mellow nicotine buzz massaged your skull; a warm embrace welcoming an old friend back after all these years. “Thought you’d quit?” His words startled you, plucking at your tensed nerves and spitting the cigarette - mid-draw - from between your dry, split lips. You spluttered, heavy lungs jettisoning their tainted air. He’d come. You’d called, you think, and he’d come. “So I di-” “You did call me, yes - drunk - at 4am this morning,” he softly growled. “Could damn near smell the liquor on your breath through the phone line. “So, you out? ” From the shadowy passage between two dumpsters, he emerged, looming over you. He was your safety word, your panic button; the one friend - the only friend you had left - who could be relied upon, who was strong enough to pull you out when you felt the dark barbs of Rasa sink into your soul. You looked up at him. Thumbs tucked into your hood, peeling it back so that he could see your face; pale, scarred… and scared. It was the face you were about to end a career to keep hidden. “I’m… I’m going to stay,” you confessed, words flushed of emotion. Scratched and scuffed fingertips, their nails bitten down, retrieved your cigarette from the asphalt and returned it to your peeling lips. “Sorry.” He was your safety word, your panic button; the one friend - the only friend you had left - who could be relied upon, who was strong enough to pull you out when you felt the dark barbs of Rasa sink into your soul. If only you’d called him back then. Your hood slid back down over your face as you exhaled another lungful of smoke, watching it swirl and dissipate into the evening air… just as you knew your friendship must. You’d let Rasa grow too strong, too fast. It had seized upon your weakness, aggressively purging your will, cell by cell. So long as Rasa was hiding behind your face, speaking with your sober tongue, you could no longer ask him to pull you out. To rid you of this demon, he would need to reach deep inside and tear your soul apart… and you wouldn’t have what violent darkness would erupt upon him on your conscience. “Your choice,” he grumbled, voice straining to hold back any condemnation. He sensed a landslide of that was soon to bury you. You inhaled another lungful of smoke, drawing the cigarette to its filter. The novelty of the nicotine buzz had long worn off, leaving only tar and ash. You tapped another from its carton. “I remember in health class they told us how these things kill,” you spoke, words detached, left to wander aimlessly into the night. “Showed us a picture of black, festering lungs. They looked like… well, they looked like death. They looked like every bit of me Rasa has touched. My grandfather had died the week before; lung cancer. I got told he’d died peacefully in his sleep… then had to sit through class as they told me how the cancer spreads, suffocating the lung and collapsing it, infecting it so you drown in blood and puss. Or how the cancer can spread to the bone, flushing calcium out into the blood and short-circuiting your nervous system. I remember every fucking word of it, but I’m still going to finish this carton before I go back in there and do what’s planned for me.” Keh, keh, keh, they sentenced. Keh, keh, keh. “Because when this night is over…” dead words spoke as smoke trickled from your nose, “that kinda death will be too good for me.” You peered back up from beneath your shadowy hood and he’d vanished; in a puff of smoke. Valium Cheap-as-shit, rat-infested motel. Anaheim’s gaping asshole district. After. GYARGHHHHHAGHAGHAGHAGH!!! The piercing wail of Sean William O’Neill stabbed pin-pricks through your bursting, blood-shot eyes. It rang in your ears, a maddening hum reverberating through your skull. You lay in bed; cheap, itchy sheets kicked off into the dirt, grease and semen-stained carpet. Your raw, tired eyes strained at their nerve bindings as your gaze gave darting chase to shadows, which you could only pray were rats or cockroaches. “Ugh, ugh…” prodded against the porous walls from the next room. She sounded thoroughly bored reading from the cliché script. “Oh, yeah… ugh, ugh… don’t stop… fuck me, fuck me… ugh, ugh…“ Clamping a limp, coarse pillow to your ears, you screwed your eyes shut. Breathe in, breath out. Breathe in, breath out. Breathe in, breath out. As the macho, thrusting grunts turned to withering wheezes, you timed your pulse to that of the neighboring room’s headboard. It was just sordid enough to be a little arousing. Exhaustion gave your aching muscles a fierce, deep massage. The shadows lost their grip on your dry eyes and slipped out of your peripheral vision. Your eyes crept shut. The gasping, limp man next door ejaculated after precisely one minute and fifty-seven seconds of passionless ‘lovemaking‘. You felt the blanket of sleep begin to envelope you; warm, cozy… forgiving. GYARGHHHHHAGHAGHAGHAGH!!! And Sean William O’Neill’s deafening wail tore it to shreds, leaving you naked in the unforgiving night. You peeled clammy skin from sand-paper bedding, lashing out with your feet at the sheets which had desperately clung to the end of the bed, condemning them to wallow on the soiled carpet for the night. The casing of the pocket watch on your dresser coughed up a trio of powder blue pills; then two more at the second time of asking. It had great sentimental value, you’d claim. One more for luck. Two since you were feeling extra lucky. A sip of stale, tepid water from a bubble-beaded glass flushed the pills down your throat. Nestling back into the prickly bedding, grating from side to side, you stared at the featureless ceiling until you eyes throbbed a deep, dull ache. Chemical-induced slumber tugged at your eyelids, sparing you the harsh truths and consequences of the waking world. Sean William O’Neill continued to wail into your ear, only his mouth was clogged with cotton wool, muffling his piercing cry. As you pictured his face, contorted in agony, the detail was scrubbed from your eyes, leaving a blurry, faceless watercolor. You would have your peaceful night’s sleep. But when the valium wore off, Sean William O’Neill would be your alarm call. GYARGHHHHHAGHAGHAGHAGH!!! Vicodin Blurry, cheap-as-shit, rat-infested motel. Anaheim’s, spinning, gaping asshole district. After the Before that comes After. The shards of glass lodged in your knuckles pierced through your drunken invulnerability. Plunging your hand into a sink of steaming, sedating water, the fresh wounds seeped flowing ribbons of crimson which your blended into a gaudy fuchsia cocktail with a fierce splashing. “Fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck,” you winced as tweezers extracted the cerated shards, uncorking jets of blood which rippled the pink, steaming broth. “Fuck.” With your head pounding, senses misfiring and nerves searing with pain, you staggered out of the bathroom and collapsed back onto the lumpy bed. A fresh gash across your cheep wept crimson tears as the stuffy, rank air stung your mangled, exposed flesh and shredded nerves. You were sweating, head stuffy and buzzing, sending agonizing grenades of salt-shot trickling down your face. This was not the life that was planned for you. All you wanted was to retreat from the pain, to let the cheap scotch take its fiery hold and drag you heavy-headed from the conscious world. From the veritable pharmacy which lay in the wreckage of the bedside table, your bloody fingers popped at many Vicodin as you had streaming wounds from their blister pack. The last dregs of scotch flushed them into your system, taking your consciousness down on its torrent. You saw only shadows. The violent hammering on the motel room’s door was no more than a hollow pop, even as it flung off its hinges. You sensed the world looming over you. “Now, now,” a sharp, sneering voice trickled down into your ear. Belial; his demonic silhouette cast before your blurry eyes. “Is this any way to prepare for your first day at work? You’ve a big day ahead if you. Oh, yes you have. It’s a good job Mr. Zenith had me keep an eye on you, now, isn’t it?” The shadow floated off, but its words lingered. “Pump their stomach; don‘t be gentle.” Marijuana This ain’t a stoner piece. I think we’re a little past that, now, aren’t we? Ecstasy The last hours that you would ever enjoy in Anaheim, California. The night before. Within the heaving throng of euphoria, Belial was a malignant, cancerous - not to mention, humorless - mass. All around him, sweat-drenched bodies were slaves to a bass line, a hive-mind abuzz. “Preferred sneakin’ about in the fuckin’ sewers,” the goblin-esque henchman hissed, hunched body buffeted by the pulsing crowd. “Much better class of person down there…” Where Christian Zenith sent Belial, Belial would go. There was a comforting reliability to it. He would, however, piss and moan about it. Frequently if not incessantly. “Ah, there you are.” The corner of his mouth peeled back with an eerie hybrid of sneer and smile, unsheathing a mustard-colored, incisor, built on a foundation of plaque. He had found Rasa’s - or rather, its host’s - scent; it hooked his nostrils, even through the sweat-sodden mass of bodies, spilled drinks, and stale, static air. The order was simple; keep an eye on Christian Zenith’s new pet. It was relayed with the kind of unnerving pride, or misguided love, that a pitbull’s owner might lavish upon it the eve of a dog fight; he didn’t want his new toy broken before he got to enjoy it. Narrow eyes, squashed under a heavy, wrinkled brow, gazed with curiosity at the host. In all the time he’d doggedly trailed them, this was the first time he’d seen them… smile? No. It wasn’t just some basic tensing of the facial muscles. Their whole body glowed, free of the dark, deathly aura which usually encased it. “Must be off your fuckin’ face,” Belial scoffed to himself. “Won’t be long until whatever shit you’ve stuck up your nose, down your throat - or in your fuckin’ ass for all I know - wears off. Enjoy if while you can, ‘cause we both know what happens tomorrow…” Alcohol Blurry, cheap-as-shit, rat-infested motel. Anaheim’s, spinning, gaping asshole district. Before. “Fuck you, Christian Zenith,” you toasted with a sharp slug of scotch. “Fuck you for what you’re making me do.” The whisky was blended; each of its malts as cheap, raw and devoid of character as the others. It was bordering on pedophilia to glug on something so young and immature. Its vapor had began to rot the eyes from your skull, tears streaming down your scarred cheek. “And fuck you, you.” Through the mirror, you shot a glare of such spite and revulsion that it almost shattered and stabbed your reflection through the eyes with the cerated shards. “Fuck you for going along with it, you pathetic, spineless...” The bottle emptied down your throat. Drowning in cheap liquor your gargled it back up your throat and scorched nostrils bare as it erupted back out of you like watery lava. Coughing violently, respiratory tract scalded, you hurled the bottle at the mirror… missing by a good three feet, to the goading delight of your reflection. One bruised, split-nailed, uncoordinated foot in front of the other, you stumbled across the motel room, barely feeling the stabs of sterilized, liquor-tipped glass shards which peppered the smudged carpet. You hawked a wad of tacky phlegm up into your throat’s chamber, irritating your parched throat, before firing it with vitriol into your reflection’s eye. Your knuckles followed, shattering the mirror; phlegm-slobbered shards lodging themselves in your fist as blood began to gush down your arm. The pain sobering, you stood unnervingly still, staring at the grotesque, mangled flesh… before punching yourself in the face with glass knuckles. You staggered back; or was it left, or right, or forward? You were laughing, you knew that much. Laughing, until you collapsed onto the bedside table, its corner stabbing you just under the ribs. “Uck… ugh… uck hyuck…” you spluttered, the fragrant citrus hint of vomit tickling the back of your throat. There you lay; drunk, weak, crying, bleeding, cursing. There you lay, beside the telephone. Too young to hold its liquor, Rasa was no longer flooding your ear with its dark, toxic wishes. It was the only number your memory held. “The fuck time is this to call?” barked down the line at you. “Who is this?” “Me.” You wondered if you spoke how your voice sounded. You were very drunk. “I… I want out. I need out before I do something I ca-… I just need out. I‘m on the road. Anaheim for tomorrow‘s… today‘s show. I jus-” “Explainin’ weren’t part of the deal,” he growled back. “I’ll be there.” Roleplay title and chapter headings are from Feel Good Hit of the Summer by Queens of the Stone Age.
