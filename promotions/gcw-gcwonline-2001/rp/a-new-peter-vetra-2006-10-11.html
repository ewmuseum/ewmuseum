A new Peter Vetra

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.11.06 ~ February 14, 2005 ~ 9:15 am, currently in my room at Rehab Today is the first day of this so called experiment? They want me to sit here and write in this journal. As if they is going to help me surpass the depression? and even worse the addiction to drugs. I guess I will have to give these doctors a chance. But I still don’t believe them. This seems like a bunch of bull shit. 12:30 pm, eating lunch in the common room. I’m still new here, no one knows nothing about me nor do they seem to want to learn. Fine by me, these people are nothing to me just as this place is nothing. Do they think I actually want to be contained like this? Do they actually think I want to be jotting down all these stupid emotions like a fucking first grader! What am I supposed to do? Nothing? sit here and take it like a man, and only hope to come out a better man. 10:21 pm, about to go bed in my room. Sitting here getting ready for bed I ponder if these little journal entries are going to do any good. Do they think they are going to help me remember my past? Do they really believe that these entries are going to help me in the future? I guess they have spent all this money on these degrees they need to get their money back some how? I just happen to be the poor sap. Competition notebooks and pencils are going to be my new best friends they told me. I’m supposed to become more demanding on such things and on my emotions, thus trying to take the place of the liquor and the drugs. I guess time will only tell but the fact of the matter I think it is a load of doctor bull shit, getting as much money from the working man. They are almost as bad as insurance agencies. I don’t know about this but for now it is time for bed. ~ Current Time ~ Looking back at all the notes I took I laugh, I read my very first entries I am filled with disguste. I thought nothing good could ever come from them but I was completely wrong. The wrong end of the spectrum, if it wasn’t for those notebooks, those doctors, hell even my emotions I probably would be alive today. They saved me. I get ready for my next big feat in GCW; I get ready to take on Shaman who has seemingly taken upon himself to start a war with not only myself but what seems to be the rest of the Ascension. What am I to do? That is the only question left to ponder. At Dangerous Games he threw the first couple of blows, now it is time I throw the retaliation blow, it is time I speak my mind on the situation. Is Shaman my real concern? Is this figure I know nothing of, know nothing about, nor care about really the truth behind my anger, behind my quarrels in GCW? Probably not, hell I wouldn’t give him that much credit, but the truth is he is going to get the brunt; he is going to get what I give him. My life has taken the turn for the worse, I lost my father, I lost my life, I lied, I have cheated, hell I have even stolen! But now I am on the right track,. Now I am working with the forces of good, I am looking to take all this bull shit out of the world, I am looking to help those and most importantly help myself. I have wronged the Vetra name; I have proven to my father he was right. Most would love to do that but I however would love to prove him wrong. I should have not followed in his footstep I should have done what I have desired to do all my life, I should have wrestled. I took his dream and tried to mix it with my own, I lied to the wrestling world, a CARDINAL SIN. Now I am spending my time trying to repent. I am spending my time trying to prove to the fans; Peter Vetra is the real deal. He is not he phony he was three years ago, that he was when he busted on to the wrestling scene at GTT4. He wants the world to know the only reason he ever did such a thing was because he didn’t want to let his father down, he didn’t want the family to be torn. Now a year after his death? Only a couple of months after my near death? The truth must be revealed, I must not lie any further I must allow Peter Vetra, to be Peter Vetra. And it all starts this week on World Wide 53? It all starts with you Shaman?. You are just the beginning?
