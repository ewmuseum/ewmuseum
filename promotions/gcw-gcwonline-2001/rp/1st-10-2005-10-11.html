1st & 10

11 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11, 2005 Welcome to the Winters home. For all your visualization needs, it’s your classic upper middle class home. The only thing that missing would be the white picket fence. The only reason this two story abode isn’t sporting one of those is likely because those that live inside have decided to not sport the same style as everyone else on the block. Speaking of which, the block seems to be quiet as we make our way down it. It’s late afternoon and the sun is in the process of setting, sending the front yards of these houses into a calm and peaceful evening. At least, that’s where everything was headed until we move around to the back of the Winters home... As we move over the fence and into the backyard, it’s fairly obvious that there’s a party going on. From the way the pool is being splashed into by all sorts of hot girls and cute guys to the people shaking this and shaking that to the music pounding out of the strategically placed speakers. The backyard is packed, but not overly crowded and everyone seems to be having quite the good time. The majority of this crowd seems to be in their college years and older. A banner hangs over the glass doors heading into the living room of the large home. It reads: “Good Luck, Alex!” And through those same doors comes the man of the hour, Alex Winters. He’s dressed in nothing out of the ordinary; dark denim jeans and a white t-shirt. Tossed on his head is a green Philadelphia Eagles cap, twisted to the side a little for some style. His sneakers are white, but green laces have been threaded through the holes to add to that fashion decision. Alex just flashes a smile as he comes out of the house, throwing a wave here and handshake there to the people that greet him. A quick glance around points Alex towards his father, Jonathan, over on the grill. He tugs his hat a little bit and makes his way on over there. “Hey Dad!” Jonathan’s a little bit busy turning the burgers over and dancing (horribly, I might add) to the new generation’s music that’s playing. In the midst of shaking his booty, Alex’s hand grabs his shoulder and he laughs, turning around. “Hey son! How’s it going?” “Dad... I told you it’s just an audition. You didn’t have to throw a big bash.” Alex says, though it’s very clear that he appreciates this gesture. Mainly because of the big smile that’s on his face. His father pokes at a couple of burgers and waves his hand dismissively. “Psh. I got a good feeling about this one, Alex. A good feeling. You’re gonna” walk in there and you’re gonna? knock “em dead and then you’re gonna” call your mother because she’s still a little wary about you fighting professionally.” He winks. Alex smiles and nods, “Yeah. I sure hope so. I mean, TK says that if I put on a show, that this federation is willing to sign me to a six year deal. The money could really help you guys out...” Jonathan frowns and raises the fork. “Alex. This is your life. Your mother and I can take care of things back here. I don’t want you doing this because you feel you need to help out. You want to wrestle” Then you wrestle. Don’t do it for us. Do it for yourself.” Alex just shrugs a little bit and looks around. “You just work so hard, Dad. I want to make sure that my family’s taken care of. That’s all. If...” Jonathan chimes in quickly, “When!” Alex smiles, “When, I get picked up by a federation, I’m going to be traveling all over the country... maybe even the world. I won’t be around to help you guys out or keep Jess out of trouble. I just... I won’t be around.” “Which is something I’ve been looking forward to since you started this wrestling stuff!” The female voice comes from behind Alex and it belongs to his younger, but oh so hot (hey, she’s a wrestling babe. duh.), sister Jessica. Alex whirls around and then flashes a smile. “Oh shut up, Jess. You know you’re gonna” miss me.” Jessica chuckles and throws her arms around her brother, squeezing him tightly. “Maybe. I just wish you’d hurry up and move out so I can have your room.” Alex gasps in mock-hurt, “You are so not getting my room.” This would be the part where he starts to try and wrestle his high school senior of a little sister into a noogie! Jess acks and struggles, immediately going for her ace in the hole when she can’t get out of Alex’s grip. “Mom! Mom! Tell him to stop!” Of course, that causes Alex to let go and he gets a punch in the arm for his troubles, before the family just laughs it off. Which brings everything to a bit of a close, considering that everything else that happens just tends to be natural party stuff. The Next Morning... Philadelphia International Airport. It’s big, no large, and it’s definitely crowded. However, as the black family SUV rolls up to the curb, ready to unload the newest and brightest star in professional wrestling, things seem to be a lot smaller. As much as this is a happy time, this is also a pretty sad time. The family’s losing a member. Which is definitely going to be a pain to replace. But let’s not go down that train of though because... ew. Anyway, the family pretty much piles out of the car and Alex is the last one out. He throws open his door and moves to the trunk, pulling out his duffle bag and his other carry on, before stepping up onto the sidewalk and dropping them down there. He turns back to look at his family, as it’s now time for each of them to say goodbye... The first is Jonathan, Alex’s father, of course. Being the man of the house, he feels its necessary that he lead off these final moments. Strolling right up to his son, he places his hands on his shoulders and proceeds to come out with the words of wisdom. “You’re going to be great, Alex. You’re going to go out there and you’re going to knock “em dead. Don’t worry about winning. Don’t worry about making me proud. Just go out there, do your thing, and the victory will come.” He pauses for a moment and smiles. “Piece of cake, right?” Alex smiles and takes his father’s hand, as they do that Winters Men handshake that his father showed him as soon as he was old enough to remember it. “Thanks, Dad.” As Jonathan steps back, his wife steps up. She’s all teary eyed and semi-emotional. Hey, Alex is her baby boy, even though he’s all grown up. Diane sniffles some and immediately starts fiddling with Alex’s jacket collar and everything. “You call us as soon as you land, okay” And make sure you don’t stay out at all times of the night. You’re headed to the big city and things are dangerous there. I heard on the news that a bus flipped over twenty seven times, so try to catch a cab if you can, okay? If you need anything, and I mean anything, you call us. Don’t try to be the Lone Ranger out there. We’re your family and we love you and we’re here for you.” There’s more of this rambling, but it finally tapers off in the midst of Diane planting her Motherly Kisses of Protection upon Alex’s forehead. She squeezes her son to her tightly, “I love you, Alexander.” Alex blinks back a couple of tears of his own and squeezes his mother back, “Love you too, Mom. I’ll be careful. I promise.” Jonathan has to peel Diane off Alex, in order to get her away from the boy. Which leaves room for Jessica to take a couple of steps towards her brother. She’s got a smirk on her face and is trying her best to put on the front that she’s happy he’s leaving But, alas, that charade doesn’t last too long. Alex is surprised as the girl wraps her arms around him and squeezes, rubbing her tears into his shirt. “Don’t get broken, Lex. Kay?” Alex smiles, one tear falling from his eyes as he can’t hold them back anymore. Jessica has been his worst enemy and best friend ever since she was born. You’ll never find a pair of siblings closer. However, Alex just hugs her close and kisses the top of her head. “I won’t. I promise. I’ll fly you out as soon as I find out where I’m going to be staying, okay?” Jessica pulls back and wipes her eyes, before punching him in the stomach. “You better.” She shares a smile with her brother, before stepping back towards the others. Alex sighs. This is a sad moment. He’s never left his family in such a capacity before. He doesn’t want to, but he knows if he’s ever going to make it in this wrestling business, he’s going to have to put in the work. “Welp. I better go. Don’t wanna” miss my flight.” He picks up his bags and offers them all one last wave, before turning his back and heading towards the entrance to the airport. SCREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH! Tires squeal and screech and everyone turns to see what’s going on. Jammed haphazardly into the space in front of the Winters SUV, is a Candy Apple Red Benz. Inside, trapped in the backseat between a handful of hot girls, is... well... I’ll let Alex tell you. Alex’s eyes go wide and he blinks at the sight, “Styles”!” The young, African-American guy in the back, comes up for air and peers over the tops of his sunglasses. “Yo! V! Wassup!” He pushes the girls off him and hops out of the back of the car. He spins around to the trunk, popping it open and brushing the imaginary lint off his suit. Yes, he’s wearing a suit. Probably why they call him Styles. He starts digging a couple of bags out of the trunk. Meanwhile, Alex just keeps blinking, trying to figure out what in the heck is going on. “Hey um... what’re you doing here?” Styles tosses one bag onto his shoulder and holds the other one in his hand. He hits on the trunk and signals for his women to drive off, whilst he makes his way to the sidewalk. He tosses a nod towards his second family and then turns back to Alex. “What you mean what I’m doin” here? We have an audition tomorrow morning, don’t we?” Alex blinks again, still confused. “We?” Styles raises his sunglasses up for a moment. “Yeah. We.” He seriously sounds like this shouldn’t be news to Alex. “You ain’t think I was gon” let you head off to become rich and famous without your manager, did you?” Alex blinks. One more time. “Manager?” Styles laughs and pulls his glasses back down. “That innocence. I love it. C’mon, V. Let’s go make you a star...” With that, Styles slaps Alex on the shoulder, then turns to the family to wiggle his fingers. He even blows a kiss to Jess, who just rolls her eyes. With that, he yanks out his ticket and starts towards the door! “Hey! Professional wrestler comin” through! Can we get some skycaps or suntin?!” Alex watches Styles make a spectacle of himself and shakes his head. With a shrug of his shoulder strap to get his bag on more, he steps off after him. This is going to be a long plane ride...
