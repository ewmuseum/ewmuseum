Date: 6/12/06 ~The Land of dreams~ The desert, covered in mesa's. A place that would remind anyone of the American South West, in the New Mexico or Arizona ranges. The sand was white, with large reddish mesas coming out and heading up to the sky. Dani could feel the hot wind pass buy, blowing up the skirt to her native sewn dress as she walked along. She didn't know why she was out here. She could make out the tracks of various animals that had been by, especially all of the sidewinders and other snakes of the area. A few tracks for kanagoo rats and others feeder vermin could be made out as well. She walked along, until she came up onto a mesa that was a good mile or so in the distance. Around it were what appeared to be birds flying about... Except that birds don't have swords and glow in the daylight air. Angels... Dani had to get closer, she had to see them. She pulled up the long dress over her ankles and started running towards the high rocks. As she approached, she could make out that it appeared to be more then one angel on another. It looked to be a good three on one beating. The three were three different types. One had six wings, as Dani had learned this was a Seraph, the highest rank of angels in heaven. Another had four wings that appeared to be caught in a bluish flame, as well as a matching, flaming sword. The last was barely there, it's shape was that of an angel one moment, and that of a large wheel of fire in the next. All three, in their human features looked like the traditional take on the angels of the bible. White skin, maybe a little tanned, long flowing hair and wings of the purest white. The one angel they were attacking was another Seraph. Only he had darker skin tone, Almost the same as Dani's. His wings were an ashen gray, with white designs in them, just like Dani knew her two wings to be. She ran as best she could, feeling the dry, hot air, filling her lungs. She didn't know if she'd get there in time. It looked like the single angel needed her help. It had to be the good one. Nothing good would ever converge in such an out matched battle. She was getting closer, unlike other dreams where things never seemed to get closer, this one was. She got close enough to see a strike of the sword by the four winged one crack the mesa in half! Rocks fell from the impact point, and cracks formed that traveled through to the other side. She moved out of the way, expecting it to fall in her direction, but it didn't. It slid off towards the side, falling in half. Dani continued her sprint after it was down on the ground, through the dust cloud that had come up. She came up upon the four angels as they were now on the ground. The single one was down on one knee, his side cut from one of the other swords. ?Go ahead, Mikhail. Get it over with. Prove to these people that they can never follow us our our message.? The Seraph on the aggressors side stepped forward. ?You broke your own covenant. It is you who should be ensuring our God's laws are followed. Yet you break them without worry for your self?? The single angel looked up. One of his wings was broken and bloodied. As he spoke the dust cleared enough that Dani could make out details. ?You try to put the hypocrisy upon me? I am the one in charge of ensuring those who turned their backs on us are punished! Yet you prevent me from doing this when they have run off to a new land, where we don't have any sort of control? Would we let them regroup, get stronger and then come at us again? We need to strike while they're down, and eradicate them once and for all!? The Seraph pulls his sword up high. ?That is not for you to decide. Our role is only to carry out the orders of the Most High. This is the land of other gods, let them help their people.? ?But we're the ones who unleashed the monsters of the Nephilim and the Noephilim upon them. Why would we not fix our mess?? ?That's not our decision to make. The war is over, get used to it.? The man on his knees looks up, and gives a proud look as he says his answer. ?No.? Just then Dani realizes, the angel with the broken wing looks just like... Josh. ?JOSH! NO!? She starts running for him as the angel it had called Mikhail brought the blade down. It cut it's target from the shoulder down to it's navel. The rebel angel fell backwards, onto the white sand, next to the fallen rocks. Dani ran towards the area. The other three angels all looked at her before they flew away. ?Josh!? As she approached where the body had fallen, things suddenly begin to shift. The fallen rock becomes worn through time and weather, the body deteriorates to sun bleached bones, it's six wings splayed out in the sand. ?What?? Next to it, is a small house. Nothing more then a cottage, painted white in the hot sun, to reflect the intense heat away from it. Dani looked down at the skeleton, the ribs broken from the slice with the sword. ?Who are you?? ~The Real World~ ~The Human Side of the Veil~ ~Washington, D.C.~ The KnightMare Brigade had finally made it to Washington D.C. To join Gray and Alexis. His preliminary report on the run in with the Society of Leopold, the unofficial sponsors of the Den wasn't one that had Annie and Blaise happy at all. Josh wasn't back yet, and Dani was having problems. You could put it that way. She didn't know why, but the longer Josh was gone from her the worse her dreams would get. It never failed. She started to eat less, and get moody. And sometimes she could swear that her stomach was hurting. As soon as he'd be back around, she'd be fine though. But he wasn't, and that meant that Dani didn't want to really do more then sleep all day. And this was something that Ukyo noticed rather quickly. A few hours ago, she'd been so bubbly and apt to call Shadow to finally setup a meeting between her and her grandfather. And then she'd gone back to bed. This left Ukyo all alone though. Blaise was with Annie still, talking with other big wigs about all of this stuff, Gray with Alexis, and Josh gone. Ukyo sighed as she stretched. She was wearing a pair of Blaise's sweats, and one of his old ratty Nirvana t-shirts. Time to be comfortable. She was in a rather extreme lotus position on the couch, in front of a blank and off television. She was trying to clear her head, and take care of her own thoughts on the things going on when there was a knock on the door to the suite. Ukyo cracked a single eye open and looked in it's direction. As she had taken accustomed ton, she shifted her sight over to the veil, and could make out the dark, black aura of the being on the other side. It wasn't an evil aura, but that of a soul trapped in a body that should have been dead a long time ago. One of the walking undead, it had to be Shadow. Ukyo leaned forward, her hands hitting the floor as she rolled out of the lotus and then slowly flipped over and up to her feet with an acrobatic grace. The six foot tall woman then went over and peered through the peephole. The flash of black hair and emerald eyes show her what she thought... it was Lia. Quickly she removed the locking bar from her head level and then opened the door to her. ?Hello, won't you come in.? Lia did so, coming in and looking around the relativity business class suite. Ukyo, acting on what had become instinct for her, looked Lia from foot to head. She wasn't wearing much really. A pair of black velvet hip huggers and a matching corset comprised the vampire's attire. There was no way she was hiding any weapons. ?You're here to see Dani and her grandfather, huh?? Lia was a bit nervous, but she answered confidently. ?Yes. I said I'd be here.? Ukyo nodded, as was almost tradition for her upbringing to her. ?I'm sorry. Dani laid down after calling you. She's not herself when Josh isn't here. I'll go tell her that you're here. Can I get you a drink?? ?Can I ask what you have?? ?Heh, you want just a drink or a real drink?? ?I'm fine with whatever.? ?Beer? Wine? Saki?? ?Saki, please.? ?Coming up.? Shadow stayed standing as the taller Japanese woman walked away towards the bar in the suite. She started working some things, it looked like she was going to make sure the drink was served properly. Lia waited patiently as she did. ~Dani's Room~ Dani had been awake for about fifteen minutes. The dream had been real to her, just like any of her others. For the last week or so, they'd been nightmares, repeating the surfaced memories of her family's slaughter at the hands of the man she knew as Jaymes, and the fallen angel she now knew as Barbatos. She couldn't fully make sense of it all though. To them, the revelation of the little boy, Adam was big. But to her, she never knew him. While she felt sad, she wasn't quite sure they were real. She wasn't sure that it had ever happened. For all she knew, it was a fantasy she'd made up in her head. She only knew of one person she'd ever called big brother, and his name was Gray. Right now, she couldn't put anymore more then a figment of her imagination. But then again there was a darker figment in her mind. Suriel kept appearing in the strangest places of her mind, and in her journal. She really didn't know what to think of it all, the keeper was a strange man but interesting to be near. But it wasn't him she was drawn to, it was more the HIM the one that touched her. In her half-sleep her hand slid to her stomach. She remembered the feel of the surge of power that Suriel fed her, causing her to show her truth self to him. As she did she woke up, fully, her mind on fire... her back burned.. She knew what it meant. Carefully she rolled out of her bed standing in the middle of the room she allowed her body to over and didn't fight what was happening. The questions in her mind: Who was she? Who is she? What will she become? Her answers back were logical She is the Granddaughter of Kokabel, the Morning Star. She was Sirius Star. She would become something that no one would expect. Not even she knew for sure. But one being seemed to know, Suriel. She knew only one thing: she had to see him again. Her mind screamed for him. As she stood there she opened the door to her room walking towards the door that lead to the living area of the hotel suite. She stopped only when she heard Ukyo's voice. ?Dani? Where do you think you are going like that?? Danielle turned around. Her eyes vacant, her voice flat. ?I'm going to him.? ?Danielle? Going to who?? Dani turned towards the door. It was almost as if she wasn't fully here with them. Almost as if she was more sleep walking then anything. Shadow turned around, only to find she was looking right at the woman she knew as Danielle Northsky. Only that same woman was standing there in nothing but her underwear and no shirt, and a pair of ashen gray wings were sitting there, attached at her shoulder blades. ?Dani?? Ukyo put the saki down and headed for the door. ?Lia, can you make sure she doesn't get through?? Lia moved towards the door, keeping the young woman in her sights. Ukyo reached for a small recorder on her belt, if anything she was going to get some tape of this encounter. With each new one the Order learned more about Danielle. ?Please, he calls to me. I must go to him.? Ukyo spoke up, as Lia appeared to be quite speechless. ?Dani, Josh is in Seattle you cant go to him. I don't even think you could fly there on your wings fast enough.? Dani stopped and turned to face Ukyo. ?He calls for me..? Ukyo reached out and grabbed her shoulders. ?Think! You know you cant leave like this. You know you would be exposing yourself and the Order to great danger.? Shadow spoke under her breath. ?Exposing yourself in some way, that's for sure.? Dani was almost like a zombie. ?He calls for me....? Ukyo shook her a bit. ?Dani! Anyone that would call for you isn't thinking of your safety. You can not go to him.? Dani was holding her abdomen. ?He needs me. I need to go to him, the pain.? Alone with Danielle, Lia and Ukyo where not sure what to do. For Lia, it was strange enough to be called here to speak to Dani's grandfather. But this was getting to the weird. She'd heard what Dani really was, and it was hard enough to believe. But seeing was most assuredly, believing. Ukyo could only think of one thing and she prayed it worked. She needed to call her grandfather he might be able to get here in time before something happened. She spoke sternly, looking Dani right in the eyes as she did. ?Kokabel... If you can hear me, get here now before I have to subdue her.? From when she finished her sentence, until the room was filled with a light, it was less then a second. Dani tried to turn back towards the door, but Ukyo stayed in her way. Behind them, in full view of Shadow appeared a man. A young man by looks. He had long flowing black hair, and a tanned complexion that matched that of Dani's. But, also like her, he had a pair of wings on his back. His granddaughter turned towards him, her face confused. ?You're not him. You're not the one I need.? Danielle takes another step towards the door, and Ukyo moves to block her. Kokabel stepped up, pulling himself to his most upright. His wings at fully extended showed the negative of hers: white with ashen makings. ?Sirius Star! You will not leave this place.? Danielle stopped and turned to look at the man standing near her. Her wings extended to their fullest. ?Grandfather, why do you stop me? You know he needs me and I need him.? ?It isn't time. Not yet at least. You are not ready young one. And nether is he.? Kokabel folded his wings down, Danielle looked at him with a hurt in her eyes... ?Suriel needs me, I must go to him...? At that last sentence, Ukyo's eyes darted from Dani to Kokabel. She was wrong about her desires, it wasn't to go see Josh. Dani's wing disappeared, leaving her standing there half dressed. Her face was no longer blank, she fell to her knees and began to cry. Ukyo, thinking of a guest being in the place, grabbed one of the jackets that sat on a chair next to the door. She put it over Dani's shoulders. Kokabel took a few graceful steps forward and wrapped his arms around his sobbing Granddaughter. As he did, his wings covered her also. ?It's okay My little Star, things will get better.? Shadow was wide eyed with it all. ?Can't you feel it? He calls to me, He needs me. He can't feel without me.? Her mind couldn't focus on anything, names kept seeping in ones she wanted to call out. Kokabel picked her up and moved her over to the couch. He motioned to Ukyo for a comforter that was folded on a chair, the one that Gray would have been using to sleep on this very couch tonight. Ukyo covered her and then went to go get her another shirt. After seeing this, Shadow stepped away from the door and spoke. ?I'm going to assume that you're her grandfather?? Kokabel stood up and then turned to the woman. He eyed her from head to foot, and then back up again. ?Yes, Ms. Lia Vlassy? Or are you taken to being called Baronessa yet? I'll apologize for this scene, but we've got surprises all around here lately. In the meantime, before I introduce myself, let me make a mental note first? He looked up at the ceiling, almost as if he was trying to project his voice. ?Suriel, you and I need to have a talk...? He then brought his attention back down to Shadow as she stood there. ?Now, allow me to introduce myself since my six-times-great-granddaughter is indisposed right now. My name is Kokabel, Star of God, and Chief Astronomer of Heaven. At one time I was a fallen one, Grigori who fell out of love for a human woman. But now, after her death, and all of that, I'm once again the Virtue of the movements of the stars.? Shadow rolled her eyes. ?Why does everyone want to roll off all of their titles and history when they introduce themselves?? She still put out her hand to shake his. He took it like a fine European Gentleman and kissed her knuckles. ?Warning, I've heard we can brush off on people. Don't want to damn your soul, if you have one to spending time with one of my former lovers.? Kokabel smiled. ?That's exactly why I wished to speak to you.? Shadow's sly grin went away. ?You know who, then?? ?Oh, I know him well. I'm sure you'd know that all of my kind would. The Poison Angel, as his names means, is well known to us.? ?Well Sam...? Kokabel put his finger to his lips. ?No! Do not mention his name.? ?Why not?? He shook his head. ?For one who has drank the blood of the first fallen from grace, and has had dealings with the Black Lady who stole our secrets, you're very open to dropping names. Have you ever wondered why the mother of your kind always seems to be able to find you at the most inopportune time?? Shadow just nodded. ?You see, for those of my kind, infernal or holy, or even those who have stolen our secrets... saying our name, our true god-given name, allows us to know where you are, what you're talking about and what you're thinking.? Shadow again just nodded. ?Go ahead, try it. Just say my name.? Shadow did so. ?Kokabel.? He smiled and then replied to her. ?Well, what a mind you have on you Ms. Vlassy.? She smiled at him. ?Didn't work did it?? ?No, let me quote exactly what you were thinking... Jsi v hajzlu, nedomrdĕ.? She was visibly shocked. Not only did he get what she was thinking right at that moment, but he's quoted it in perfect Czech. ?Okay, I'm convinced.? He smiled wide. ?This also goes for the mother of your race. Which is the other reason why I've called you here. I have reason to warn you about what is going on here.? ?That's fine, but why not Aine or Blaise telling me?? He smiled wide. ?Cause they have to follow orders and get things cleared. I have no such restrictions. And I know more then they do.? She smiled, this had the feel of things Blaise would condone. ?Go ahead.? ?First of all, as you can see, the story about Dani is true. She is a celestial angel born on this earth. She has enemies that now move to get to them.? ?Great...? ?One of them I know. She's the daughter of your former lover, and your kind's mother.? Shadow's face went long. A being born of both Sammael and Lilith? ?She's one of the beings you would call a succubus. I will give you her name if I had something to write with.? Ukyo quickly grabbed the man a pen and a piece of paper. He wrote it down on the paper, and then showed it to Shadow. The name on it: Layla. ?The Angel of Night, and of conception. She has sworn a covenant to her mother and her uncle.? Shadow's eyebrow raised. ?Her uncle?? ?The main one who wants Dani. The one her kind called the Evening Star.? Her eyebrow didn't go down. ?Why are you telling me this?? He smiled wide to her. ?You are infused with the power given to you by the Poison Angel himself. You're no weakling. And I know you and your ilk will come in to play here. You're destined for it. The prophecy mentions you, and your allies.? Shadow's eyebrow finally lowered, but then her forehead creased as she tried to understand what he had just said. ?I've got news for you. I'm a vampire. My kind are damned for eternity when we die.? He chuckled. ?Is that so?? ?Yes, don't you read your own book? You're an angel right?? He openly laughed at that. ?The book you speak of is written by man, to control other men. It's nothing more then propaganda. Mostly influenced by the people who have maintained control over it's content for centuries. Namely, in today's world, Saint Cassius Longinus and his Society of Leopold.? Shadow froze. ?Yes, they're part of the church, yes? They have some say when their leader votes on what is scripture and what isn't when they have their meetings in Rome. And when a paragon, an immortal says that this is how it's going to read, that's how it reads. No one will openly oppose his band of soldier priests.? ?But Cain's sin, didn't it...? ?Cain's sin should have had him laid out, and his entrails thrown to the four winds. But he was let go, his families immortality returned. A mark put upon him to show others that messing with him was the wrong thing to do. Tell me something Lia.... does it make sense for the son or daughter to pay for the crimes of the father?? She actually tried to think on it for a bit before she answered. ?No. Everywhere else in the bible, the man who sins has to pay the price.? ?Exactly. That part was put in there to try to ensure your kind were hunted. Don't for that. If you are ultimately good, then who knows. But out of all of these people, other then Ukyo and Blaise, you're the only other one to have dealt with something from my realm. On more then one occasion. They will need your help. And I ask to make sure that you will be there to give it.? Shadow had only one thing to answer for that. ?Okay, but what would be in it for me?? Kokabel knew the only real answer for that question. ?My dear, I'm an inside man. If anyone is going to speak to Gabriel the Judge, on your behalf when you pass from this world, I'm the perfect one to do it. After all, you have resisted temptation from the best tempter of us all.? Lia nodded to him. Just then Dani spoke up. ?Grandfather?? He turned to her. ?Yes, Little Star?? ?She needs to know how to identify the enemy. She needs to see the marks, the seals.? Kokabel nodded to her. ?Very well.? He walked over to a table that was strewn with books, and pulled up one. He flipped the pages open to one that had a marker on it. ?The brother to your former lover, and uncle to the half breed Angel of Night.? He handed Lia the book, open to the page on Astaroth. But it wasn't the knowledge of who was hunting Dani that intrigued her. It was the marking on the page that denoted the sigil or seal of Astaroth. She flipped through, to the other pages that were marked. ?I've seen markings like this recently.? ?I know... when you were trying to find your brother down south.? She glared up at the angel before her. ?I was following you. I get bored watching the same things over and over again. Sometimes I follow you and Virgil. Sometimes I watch them from afar. Sometimes I go surfing on the beach in Hawaii.? He turned away from her as she flipped through the pages. ?Can I keep this?? He nodded to her. ?Sure, I'm sure your fey friend back in the library could use that to help you out. It'll put more then a few brains looking into things.? Lia closed the book and tucked it under her arm. ?You know, this is really a shock to me. I was raised in a very religious world until I was embraced. Everything you've just told me has really broken doctrine. It's kinda hard to know what to believe after all I've seen. Which god is the real one?? Kokabel turned to her after picking up a cup of Saki. ?You know, Ms. Vlassy. When I went to Japan to watch over my Dani, I ran into some beings, people to me, who I'd never met before. And I got to have sushi, saki, and tempura for my first time ever, while feeling up the lovely Ms. Ameratsu Omikami, and listening to the poetry of Hachiman.? ?What's that supposed to mean?? ?It means there are somethings that have become too damn complex in today's age. Things were much more simpler in the old days. Why limit yourself to one god? Believe what is true, and what is right. We came to this world over here, as outsiders. It doesn't matter if our god has control right now. Being the god of three major world religions. The others can be just as necessary. Especially in a war. Cause you never know when they'll be ally or enemy.? With that, he downed the saki by throwing it back into his mouth. As he slammed the cup down, he suddenly cursed. ?Ah, crap!? ?What is it?? ?JOSHUA!? Dani perked up. ?What about Josh?? Kokabel's face got twisted, something none had ever seen before in it. ?He's not where he said he'd be.? Dani spoke up again. ?Where is he?? ?At your mortal Grandfather's place.? ?What?? With that, Kokabel faded out, right before their eyes. Dani got up and started to try to find her phone in her purse. Shadow looked at Ukyo and nodded. She started to turn towards the door. But Dani spoke up to her. ?Lia?? Shadow turned as Dani held the phone up to her ear. ?Yes, Dani?? ?We'll help you find your brother. I promise.? Shadow nodded to her and then turned to leave the room. ~To Be Continued.~
