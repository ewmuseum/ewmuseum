Date: December 20, 2007 __Start Sitting on his throne, he watched their fingers step along the strings of the harp striking note after note. The harmony spilled into the air and carried to his eardrums creating a whimsical masterpiece. A smile stretched across his lips as he leaned against the golden backrest taking in the true beauty of the music. ?You always have a way to soothe my soul.? He bellows. Two women step onto his raised portion of ground with grapes in hand and feed him graciously. He takes their offer and crushes the grapes, one by one, under his tongue swishing the liquid around. A voice came from behind the throne resonating until it reached the god's ear, 'Everything ok today master?' 'Archaeis, you know better than to call me master.' 'Of course.' 'Yes though, everything seems to be in order.' The god answered. Opening his eyes, he signals for the angels to leave with their grapes. They do as he wishes leaving the god and the new figure alone. The owner to the voice comes out from behind his master and looks him in the eyes. 'Very good. Anything you need?' 'I seem to be taken care of. And you?' 'Nothing at all.' 'Any news on the compromise from below?' 'Not yet, no. We seem to be encountering problems with contacting him.' The god sighs and moves his body to a more comfortable place in his chair. 'Should I be worried?' 'Not the slightest. It is being taken care of and will cause no damage to the kingdom.' 'Very good.' Archaeis nodded and felt his stomach tightening. His vocal chords twist and a heavy knot forms in the center of his throat leading him to make a heavy gulp. The god notices nothing and both of them stand in silence as the music lulls them to sleep. The Last Supper The Oceanaire Seafood Room Sitting within the confines of the world renowned Houston Galleria, The Oceanaire rests comfortably in the center. Its face, a curved wall stylized to replicate shear elegance, is decorated by a line of green plants stretching from side to side. Just above them, on the wall, sits the custom marlin emblem which guides the way for both Jay Terror and Caroline as they walk along the designated pavement. Hand in hand, almost as if they were dating or something of the sort, they walk admiring the layout. 'Looks fancy.' Says Caroline. Jay nods, 'A bit. Fancy a problem?' 'Not the least bit. It's been a while since I had to throw on a blouse anyways.' 'It's a shame it takes an event like this to get them out of the closet.' She smiles, as she has become accustomed to do, and releases her grip on his hand. Tucking her body against his, she nuzzles close. They reach the glass double doors and Jay tugs on the steel bar running horizontally across them. Like a gentleman should, he allows Caroline to step inside first. She does so and he follows with a hop in his step as he attempts to reach the next door first. As he extends his arm, he finds that there is no need for the rush seeing that a man is being paid to let the guests in. 'Good evening.' A false smile crests over his lips and he bows his head down allowing them to walk inside. Jay nods and walks his guest into the main lobby. 'Looks more like a hotel than restaurant. You know?' 'I like it.' 'I never said I didn't.' She giggles and a man in a black suit approaches them. His eyes are hidden behind a pair of thin rimmed glasses and by the look on his face it's easy to tell that he's not one for giggling. 'Good evening to the two of you.' 'You going to check us in?' Jay joked. Caroline laughed again but it died back fast as the man shot her a quick look, 'Do you have a reservation?' 'Should. Look up Jay Terror.' The man rolls his eyes at the outlandish name and steps backward until he reaches a wooden booth. A thick bound book rests on the mantle and he pages through it until he finds what he needs. 'Terror you said?' He verifies. 'That's the name.' Looking behind him, the snooty man signals for another employee to step forward. In that instant, a stocky man with slicked back hair comes to his side holding a pair of menus in his right hand. 'Where will they be seated tonight sir?' 'There is an opening in the rear portion of the restaurant; you should be able to find it well enough.' 'Of course.' Looking at Jay and Caroline, the stocky man opened his mouth, 'If you'll please follow me.' Jay held back his laughter and instead went the extra mile by poking fun at their escort. He puffed his stomach out and ran his fingers through his hair making it stick behind his ears. The man behind the wooden booth caught attention of it but did little to stop it. Caroline, unable to hold back anything, let out a slight chuckle. The escort turned to face them and she stifled her laugh with a thick cough. 'Is this your first time joining us?' He asked. 'We're both virgins, yes.' Jay answered. He wasn't amused by the answer but showed no sign of it. 'I hope this isn't your last time. People have told us that they've never had better food.' 'In that case, I doubt it will be our last. Hell, if we ever come to Houston again we'll have to look into you. Nothing better than the best food in the world, right?' 'We stretch further than Houston you know? Between Washington, California, and North Carolina, we serve quality food all over the United States.' He said in a dignified sort of way. Jay held back another burst of laughter and shook his head. It didn't matter where they were, he didn't have plans of making a return visit. Even without a viable sample of food, he had made up his mind. He had made a bad decision by choosing the Oceanaire. Nevertheless, he held the conversation hoping they would reach their table soon, 'Interesting.' Thankfully, the conversation was dead as they walked up to a circular booth nested in the corner of the room. The table itself was shell shaped and coated in a fine white tone. He led them into their seats and watched as they scooted inward until they found the center. Placing the menus on the table, he gave them both one last look, 'Your waiter will be with you momentarily.' As he left, Jay turned to Caroline and smiled a bit, 'You think he ever got laid?' 'By the guy at the front.' 'You think?' 'Definitely. You see the way they looked at each other?' 'Not particularly.' 'Just trust me then.' __________ The fire jumped around his throne sprinkling flecks of soot and ash over his naked body. His skin, an awful red hue, twitched upon being touched by the powders causing his body to dramatically shake. He grins showing his yellowing teeth and shuts his eyes as he enjoys the sensation pulsing over him. The fire bursts behind him again hissing angrily. 'Oh hush. We have to be patient.' He spat. The fire died down slowly and the red man swiveled his body in his chair. His focus was cast on a small telephone which leaped up and down waiting to be answered. 'Only a little longer.' __________ 'You like seafood, right?' He asked looking away from his menu. Caroline dropped hers on the table and eyed up her date, 'Of course. We wouldn't be here if I didn't.' 'Right.' Feeling a little embarrassed, Jay let a smile slowly come over his lips, 'Would you mind helping me then?' 'With what?' 'What to order.' Her jaw stretched and her eyes bulged, 'You mean you never ate seafood?' 'I've had shrimp and crab cakes.' She shook her head and put her arm around his shoulder. Pulling him close, she kissed him across the forehead, 'Why are we here then?' 'Because it's fancy.' 'And if you hate the food?' Grinning wide, 'They have room service in Houston too.' She punched him in the arm at the wise crack and pulled back. 'Ok, seeing that you're a complete virgin, how about we stick with Tuna?' She offered pointing to the Ahi Tuna selection on the menu. He eyeballed it and read over the description. Luckily for him, they had a screenshot beside the text showing off a pink filet smothered by crispy onions and other articles of food. 'So it's not like tuna fish?' 'Not at all.' 'Ok good. Bad experience.' Trying to think of a way to describe it, she came up short. 'Basically, it's a safe fish for someone like you. I bet you love it and join the Oceanaire in all its locations.' She said in a mocking sort of way. Jay and Caroline shared a laugh which slowly lead to a kiss. Their lips touched and moisture gathered between them creating a suction. Like any great occasion, the kiss is ruined by the entrance of their waiter. 'Hello, I'm Garret and I'll be your server tonight.' They let go of the kiss and turned to look at a lanky man with thick rimmed glasses. His tie was red, as was everyone else’s, and his shirt was tucked into his pants messily. 'Sweet.' Jay spat. Garret picked up on the sarcasm but kept to his job making Jay believe everyone working there was unable to stir confrontation; 'Are you ready to order?' 'We haven't even gotten drinks.' 'Typically our guests order the drinks when they order their meals. If you'd rather me bring them out now I can do that for you.' 'Wait,' Jay paused. 'You mean that people sip on water for an hour before their food comes out with their drinks?' 'Our food doesn't take an hour to come out.' 'Still.' Caroline was amused by the bickering but her stomach wasn't. Clapping her hand over his mouth, she ordered, 'Chilean Swordfish for me.' He scribbled the order onto his pad of paper and, once done, glanced up at Jay. Terror looked to Caroline to confirm his order and she pointed to the label on the menu. 'The Ahi Tuna for me.' 'Very good. Would you like us to bring our handpicked wine with each of those?' 'Wine?' Jay mused. He wasn't accustomed to formality in a decent restaurant and upon learning about it he wasn't a fan. 'You have Jack Daniels?' 'Jack Daniels doesn't make a wine sir.' The waiter corrected him. 'I didn't ask for Jack Daniels wine.' Jay responded. The waiter looked to Caroline hoping she could help but soon saw that she wouldn't be able to do much at all. His hand moved up his side and to his head where he found an itch. 'I can check to see if we have that, yes.' 'That'd be awesome.' Walking away, the waiter raised his chin. He wasn't used to the kind of demands Jay made and he surely wasn't willing to conform to them. He knew for a fact that they supplied all types of liquor and that whatever the customer asked for they were supposed to provide. Something about Jay made Garret feel less inclined to fulfill his wishes though. Back at the table, Jay turns to Caroline, 'Anyways-' __________ Stepping along the cloudy ground, the god walked beside Archaeis admiring the tall steel gate. He'd watched it many times before and during each occasion he felt as if he enjoyed it more and more. There was something special about watching the new guests enter that kept him mesmerized. ?Where are we going? Asked Archaeis. ?I believe that rain is in order. It is my understanding that people below are getting worried due to the lack of fertilization. Archaeis shrugged, ?Very well.? They continued until they reached the edge of the clouds. Looking over, the god admired his work below him and took a moment to stop and stare. ?You know, it's really something now that I stop and think of it.' 'The greatest thing to have ever occurred I'd say.' He bobbed his head in agreement and raised his hands into the air. Shutting his eyes, a mist began to appear between both of his palms. The water cultivated and transformed into a spinning orb which slowly crept away from him. It edged over the side of the clouds and started to spread out into a flat object. The once orb grew immensely and began dripping liquid out which fell to the earth with a steady rhythm. 'That should do the trick.' Archaeis watched the rain fall, stupefied like usual, and spoke, 'You know -' He started. Unfortunately for him, he wouldn't be able to finish. A loud thud echoed through the cloudy domain and a sudden burst of heat came over the realm. The god and Archaeis both turned to look at the gate which was slowly melting away. 'What is that?' Archaeis gulped, he knew exactly what was happening. __________ 'So this is it? LeStatt Knight and you will finally get to have a go at each other?' She asked while twirling her fork in the fishy sauce. Jay nodded and took another bite from his tuna. Much like Caroline had said, he was beginning to fall in love with the fish and even felt like opening his own version of the Oceanaire. Then again, he also contemplated the fact that he could cast his line off a dock and reel in his own tuna. Both ends were tempting. Nevertheless, he looked to his date and nodded some more, 'Yeah, the big showdown. Kinda' exciting.' 'Id' say. The guy's been a complete dick.' Jay shrugged, 'I think he's just trying to compensate for something. Rumor has it that he sold it to Caldera.' 'Shit.' 'Double shit.' Caroline took another chunk out of the swordfish and mixed it around in the gravy substance, 'What happens if you win?' 'He'll deny it.' 'No, really.' Jay placed his fork on the table, beside his plate, and picked up the glass of Jack Daniels. It wasn't the best glass of liquor he had ever bought but it was good enough to get him buzzed. 'If I win then I guess I'm some sort of prophecy. They've been talking me up to be the next big thing in GCW and if I beat him then I suppose I am that big thing.' 'You're already huge though.' 'To him his own. I like to look at it like I'm pretty good. If I beat him, I'm better than pretty good. That way, I was never horrible and I'm never at my full potential.' She cocked her brow sideways and puckered her eyes up a bit in confusion, 'How's that make things better?' 'Makes me want to keep climbing. It's one of those things that Daniel taught me.' 'Daniel?' 'Long story.' Jay picked his fork up from the plate and stabbed the last slice of tuna with the three prongs. Sliding it into his mouth, he chewed until it dissolved to mush. 'You want to go to the show?' 'You think I'd like it?' She had never been to a wrestling event before, even though she was an avid fan, and had always wondered what it would be like. 'You think you like the TV version? Seeing it there is like a real big orgasm I hear.' 'Well shit, sign me up.' 'Already did.' Jay sat back against the large cushion behind him and shoved his fingers into his pockets. Coming out, they hold onto a box of 27s and a single ticket. 'I figured you'd want in.' After handing it to her, she read the matches on the card with a smile. Jay found two cigarettes from the box and lit them both with his Zippo. He handed her one and took a drag off his own, 'It'll be right in the front.' 'Thank you.' She took a puff on the filter end and leaned over to kiss Jay. As she did, Garrett returned making perfect timing once more. 'How is everything?' He asked. Jay looked up to Garrett and flicked the cherries off the end of his 27, 'Better than I thought.' 'I'm glad to hear it sir. Anything else I can get for you?' Caroline shook her head, 'You have ashtrays?' 'We're actually a smoke-free restaurant.' Jay showed his teeth through an open smile, 'I haven't seen any signs.' 'Our guests know of our policy and therefore we don't typically need signs.' Resting his cigarette on his plate, he eyed up Garrett in an annoyed manner. He'd heard enough degrading remarks and petty gibes throughout the evening that one more would surely drive him over the edge. Jay took a deep breath and let out a heavy sigh, 'It's a shame. We just started these.' 'Hopefully you can just end them then?' Caroline could sense the frustration brewing in his eyes and decided it would be best for her to take over. 'Look, no one's around us and in five minutes they'll be down to the butt anyways. Let us have a quick smoke?' It was against the company's policy but he too could see the unease in Jay's demeanor, 'Keep them out of sight and finish them quick. I'll be back with your check in a few minutes.' Jay thanked him and took another shot of poison through his nostrils, 'You're some kinda' cunning linguist you know?' 'I'm just hott.' 'Touch?.' __________ The steel twisted and bent over itself crashing into the wisps of cloud. The heat grew until a full fledged fire broke free. The flames rose as a figure stepped out of them. His body was naked, painted red, and his smile was far too demonic for someone to misinterpret his reason for coming. Two horns rested beside one another on his head and a long tail whipped to and fro as he walked. The god gripped the air tight and shouted, 'What is the meaning of this?' The devilish being looked at the god and raised his chin high, 'I figured I'd relay my answer to your proposal by word of mouth.' 'And?' 'And,' he paused creating a sudden sense of drama, 'I don't accept.' A sick laugh came out from behind his lips and reverberated through the heavens. 'You see, I'm quite fond of my world and I'm not exactly willing to give it away.' 'Fine, you can leave here then.' The god answered. 'On the contrary, I just showed up.' Archaeis wanted to do something but any attempt he could make would surely be futile. The god shook his head and opened his eyes for the devilish man to see, 'You have no right to be here and you know that. Leave.' 'Roberto, you know I cannot just leave after such a grand entrance. Be kind and show some hospitality toward your guests.' 'Hospitality?' He let the question linger, 'I've shown you enough hospitality by staying in my own world. I have never entered your realm and have never planned to. If that's not consideration toward you then I'm not sure what is.' 'You're right.' Roberto looked amused by the answer and was curious as to what he meant by it. The devilish man wouldn't give him the chance to ask though, 'Before I leave you be-' He stopped and raised his hand. A fire was brewing in his palm and as it moved higher into the air it gained volume. 'I have something special for this world of yours.' He released a fiery blast toward the clouds below them and a gap opened up. Shooting another fireball from his palm, the horned man hit open another patch of cloud. 'What is the meaning of this?!' The god shouted. The red man ignored the question and pressed on with his dirty work. The fires spread and the floor of the heavens began to wither away. Roberto tried to use his own power to heal the ground but nothing seemed to help the diminishing clouds. Fires raged along the bottom of the god's throne and lit the pieces until nothing was left of it. That same sadistic smile he had entered with still shined bright on his face as he enjoyed every second of his work. 'Stop this!' The god bellowed. He raised his hands and tried to summon his energy to take out the devilish figure. Another orb gathered in his palms and as soon as he aimed it he found a wave of heat and fire engulfing him. The flames bit his ankles and wiped away the orb leaving nothing but useless energy. He tried again but the devilish man was on top of his game and spat a fireball from his palms. It headed straight for Roberto's chest and caught him off guard as it tore through. 'It was good seeing you again my friend.' The red skinned man said. He lowered his hands and looked around at the decaying heavens. 'I believe my work is done.' With that, he turned and disappeared under the clouds leaving the fires raging. The god recomposed himself and looked around as well. The place that he had created as a safe-house was slowly dying. His kingdom was crumbling. __________ Walking through the restaurant and back to the main lobby, Jay and Caroline stepped passed the same man that had greeted them earlier. He stood in a grandiose sort of way, looking like he had something shoved up his ass, and smiled as the couple walked past, 'Have a goodnight and thank you for coming to The Oceanaire.' Jay turned around to look at him and simply smiled, 'You sure got that line down.' 'Excuse me?' 'I can tell the dick's in your ass but I can't see one in your ears.' Looking real upset, the man started to walk up to Jay and Caroline. 'You know what.' Jay didn't care what. He and Caroline pushed through both sets of double doors before the dignified son of a bitch could finish his sentence. They had better things to do. __End
