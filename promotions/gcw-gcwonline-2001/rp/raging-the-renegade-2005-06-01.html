Raging the Renegade

1 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 1st, 2005 “Evolved into what I am now, I can clearly see, there’s just no rest for me. Every path I take, every move I make, there’s someone watching, waiting, ready to attack. Little do I remember getting a bulls eye tattooed on my god damn back! Just let me do what I do best. Stop getting in the way, stop causing me to look like the damn fool. You jealous pricks cheat me from the top and not matter how hard you all want me to fail, it’s never gonna happen! Ever seen anyone attempt snatching the prey from a vicious wild animal” That’s something you will not see frequently. It’s pretty much like facing death directly face to face. I’m not much different. I have my mind, my mentality set on the Gold. And now you wanna-be’s try to take my prey from me? You don’t know what your getting yourself into. This frustration is ready to burst out of me, and I’m not joking when I say this’Someone is going to get hurt.” (My face dripping wet with sweat, my teeth grinding together with pure hatred, I am clearly pissed off more to an extent which I’ve never truly reached. It takes a lot to set me off, but somehow, that barrier has been breached. The sound of cracking cartilage is heard from the intense pressure I am putting on my knuckles. Pure adrenaline mixed with synthetic hatred revs through my engine pumping body. My mind is set on overkill and this machine of a man is ready to take the next level to something drastic.) “Once again, I am about to finish off another victim who blocked my road to success, and what happens” Another bump in the road seems to swipe my axel clean off and now I once again have to stop my journey to fix the problem. And that problem is you Renegade. You had to go and stick your nose where it didn’t belong. Was it the smell of recognition, or was it a smell of desperation. You see, your nothing more than a has been. You washed out of GCW quite some time ago. And now you’re back looking to make a name once again. So there you sit, commentating matches for the business you once knew and loved. A business you once reigned supreme in. You may be the only hall of famer in GCW history, but I’m gonna be right up there next to you. Cause you see, unlike you I have everything to gain and nothing to lose’Where as in your case you have everything to lose and nothing to gain. You have been the best in the business before, people like me use to look up to people like you. But you see, nowadays it’s the youngsters who hold this business together. I have learned so much and times have changed since your time. I honestly don’t think you have what it takes to sit in my league now. I am everything you use to be, and everything you honestly want to be. It’s ok to wish to be young and powerful again. But I’m not gonna let you try to punk me out in front of the whole world and play me off as a pussy. Renegade, you just bit off more than you can chew?” (I release the grip on my sweaty hands and simply brush the loose strands of hair from my face. My eyes glow with a glimmer of pure confidence as I release a statement so deep, it makes the dead turn over in their graves?) “Prepare for the worst”..Bitch?”
