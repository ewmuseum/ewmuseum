The Surgoth Story - Chapter 4 - A Technicality in Justice

12 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/12/11 Beep beep beep. Beep beep beep. I reached over and shut the alarm off for one last time as I decide to finally crawl out of bed after hitting the snooze button about five times already. I swung my legs out from under my blue silk sheets and threw them over the bed as I sat up. I rubbed the sleepiness from my eyes and looked over at the clock to see that it was already a little after six in the morning. Normally I was in the office at this time but I had worked so much over time that I figured going in a little late today wouldn’t hurt anything. I stood up from my bed and headed to the bathroom that was only right across the hall from my bedroom. The first stop once in the bathroom, the toilet. As I stood their urinating I thought about the case I had been working on. A Vamlisk, part vampire and part basilisk, had been killing a gang known as The Tribals. Apparently he was created by a scientist that had recently been put in jail. I could only figure it was the one that I had put away for killing some innocent people with a deadly virus. I next turned on the water for my shower, turning on the hot water first to let the water warm up. I put my hand in to check the temperature of the water and quickly remove my hand as the water was scalding hot. I waved my hand in the air violently to quickly cool it off. I reached around the water to turn on the cold to cool the water before I decided to burn my ass off. I finally got it to the perfect degree and got inside the shower, closed the shower curtain and stepped under the water. My mind wasn’t completely on the case however. I kept thinking about her, the girl who usually brings my lunch. Even after getting the information I did from her grandmother, I still haven’t got up the courage to ask her out. I thought I did have the courage, but when she came around and I saw that beautiful smile of hers, I chickened out. I can deal with things like Vamlisks without as much as flinching, but when it comes to her, fear gets a new meaning. I thought about trying to make today the day I finally ask her but I knew that I would probably just get all, how do you say it, wishy –washy, yet again. I turned off the shower water after rinsing all of the soap from my body. I moved my green shower curtain aside, grabbed my light green towel and began drying myself off. It was quite the refreshing shower to say the least. Once I finished drying off, I walked back over to my room, got into my dresser and pulled out all the clothes that I would be wearing today. I grabbed out a pair of black boxers, blue jeans and a blue, Diamond City Police Department T-shirt. I threw on my clothes, headed to my kitchen and turned on the television that I had sitting on the counter. It was only a small twelve inch tv so that I could at least listen to the news as I prepared my breakfast. Right now they were talking about the weather. “It looks to be a mild day today with a chance of some thunderstorms starting in the early morning hours, then tapering off to a light drizzle later this evening,” said the woman on the news. A few seconds later a loud thunder boomer could be heard rolling across the sky. “You couldn’t have called that any better missy,” I said out loud with a little laugh as I grabbed the eggs from the refrigerator. I took the eggs over to the stove and threw a frying pan down on one of the burners. Oops, I did it again! Forgot the butter! I walk back over to the fridge, grab the butter and a knife from the silverware drawer. I walk back over to the stove and put everything down next to it. I grab a spatula from above the stove as well, then I open the butter, grab the knife and dip it into the butter. I grab a nice glob of butter and throw it into the pan and turn the burner on the stove to the high posistion. In seconds the butter begins to melt. I pick up the frying pan and begin to move the butter around. The butter crackled a bit and some jumped out of the pan and landed on my arm. “Owch! Son of a bitch!” I cried out. I put the pan down kind of hard as I was a bit angry at the situation. Kind of a stupid reason to get angry, but I wasn’t completely awake yet. I threw five eggs in a pan and they began sizzling instantly. Once the actual news came on I began listening more closely as they said there was a special report. “This just in,” said a man on the news broadcast. “Today, Celis Grom, a former scientist who worked for Gildus Labs, was left out of jail today on a technicality. Mr. Grom had been put in jail due to the murders linked to an unknown virus that had been let loose on the city.” I looked over and seen a live broadcast of Celis Grom being brought out of the prison being escorted by a few police officers. A few seconds later, the police officers fall to the groud face first and then ‘it’ appeared. Celis Grom had been grabbed around the neck by the Vamlisk. You could actually hear the other police officers around yelling at it. “Let him go!” yelled on of the police officers. “Were not sure what we are seeing here folks,” said a live news caster on the television. “It looks as though Mr. Grom is being held hostage by a... what the hell am I suppose to call it!?” I shut off my stove and headed for the door instantly. This was my chance to stop this creature. Sure, he had a killer in his arms and was moer than likely going to get justice for those that were killed by him, but I still couldn’t let it happen. I quickly left my apartment, and instead of taking the elevator from the third floor of the apartment building, I quickly ran down the stops to the first floor. I jumped into my black Sadan and headed for the prison which was only a few minutes away. I couldn’t believe that they had let that murderer go free! What the hell were they thinking!? I promised myself that once I saved Celis’s asa from this Vamlisk, I was putting him right back in jail and mkae sure that this ‘technicality’ wouldn’t happen again! I had finally made it to the prison where three police cars had already been parked and at least seven police officers stood behind their cars with their guns pointed at the Vamlisk. Chief Gilthor, was already there as well and quickly rushed up to me as I got out of my car. “I knew that if you were watching the news that you would be here in a matter of minutes,” began the Chief. “What the hell are we suppose to do about this thing!? Why the hell has he even got a hold of that scientist!?” “That scientist is the one who created that thing that is holding him hostage. Somehow this Vamlisk has an unbelievable hero complex,” I said a little sarcastically. “What do you think he wants?” “To kill Celis.” “To kill... Wait! How do you know!?” “He told me,” I responded to the Chief. “What do you mean he told you?” asked the Chief “I guess I kind of failed to mention I had a conversation with that thing. Figured you wouldn’t believe a story like that, so I figured I would wait until I caught this thing before I told you the story to prove that it was true. Didn’t want you to think I was nuts or anything,” I kind of snickered as I told the Chief about it. “You are nuts! When we get back to the office, remind me to write up a report to the psych. to have you evaluated, that way she can make you a certified psycho!” “Thanks Chief,” I said with a laugh. “I think those are the kindest words you have ever said to me. You have to be a little crazy to work for you.” “Why thank... WHAT!?” “Never mind that right now Chief. How about getting me a megaphone so I can try to reason with this thing. Then again, last time I tried that, he sort of got away.” The Chief shook his head at me, kind of laughing to himself while doing so. I was only telling the truth though. The things he puts his men through, you have to be a little crazy to put up with it. It was good to see the Chief laugh and smile a bit. Last I heard his home life wasn’t going the greatest. I guess it would be hard to keep a marriage together when you don’t really remember anything about each other. Even though he couldn’t remember their wedding or anything about her, the Chief still ahd feelings for his wife, that he could never forget. They had two kids, a boy and a girl, and he loved them as well. When the Chief woke up on the Day of Awakening, he found that he had it great and didn’t want to lose any of it. I had hoped that someday I could be so lucky as to have a wonderful family like his. The Chief finally returned with the megaphone, I put it up to my mouth and pushed the button, “Let him go and we won’t be forced to kill you!” I wasn’t pissing around. I didn’t care if it wasn’t by the book but my methods usually worked. “Chill out Van,” whispered the Chief to me. “There is way to many witnesses here.” “He needs to die Van, you know that as well as I do,” began the Vamlisk. “How do you get released on a technicality? Don’t you understand what is going on Van? There is more to this than you realize!” I pushed the button on the megaphone to try and reason with it again, “I will find a way to have him retried again! You are already in enough trouble with killing all of those men at the warehouse! Trust me, Celis will get what is coming to him! You are endangering another being and I will shoot if I have to!” “If you protect this man then you are just as guilty as he is and I will drain you of every ounce of that sinners blood that runs in your veins!” “That’s fine! Come after me and all these men will shoot you down! If you let that man go and come with me now, I maybe able to get the judge to go easy on you!” “Isn’t going to happen Van, this man needs to die,” said the Vamlisk as he began to let his tougue out. It twirled around Celis’s neck , began to go down his chest, and began to enter into Celis’s body. Van raised his gun, and with a well placed shot, hit the Vamlisk right between the eyes. The Vamlisk fell to the ground and Celis Grom ran towards the police. Clapping could be heard all around but I didn’t feel it was a time to celebrate. I knew that I had just helped a guilty man, but I did what had to be done. The Vamlisk was a lot more dangerous than Celis could ever be. “Great job Van,” said the Chief as he patted me on the back. “Chief,” I began in a sort of sad tone. “This wasn’t a victory. Sure, I killed a killer, but another has been saved.” I had almost felt like crying at that moment. The Chief could tell that I was upset and he put his arm around me. I could tell that he had sort of felt the same way. “Don’t worry Van,” began the Chief. “We will find a way to put Celis back in jail. It will only be a matter of time before he slips up again, and then...” “And then we will have more innocent people dead!” I yelled at the Chief. “It’s not worth another human life in hopes to put Celis Grom in jail! I will not rest until I find this ‘technicality’ that got hi off the hook and put his murdering ass back in jail!” After I had finished yelling at the Chief, I jumped into my car and took off like a shot for the office. I really wasn’t in the mood to go to work after dealing with all that had just happened, but I knew the only way I was going to catch Grom was to find more dirty on the bastard. * * * I had made it back to the office around eleven hundred hours and I had alerady been working for an hour trying to find whatever dirt I could to put that bastard, Celis Grom, back in jail. It was where he belonged and I was going to make sure that is where he was going to return. DING! There was the elevator bell. It was now lunch time and I did get myself some lunch, but I had no desire to talk to ‘her’ today. Don’t get wrong, I still wanted to ask her out, but right now putting that guilty man behind bars, AGAIN, was a little bit more important. She laid down my turkey on wheat and mountain dew in front of me, crossed her arms on top of the cubical wall as they weren’t very high walls, placed her head down on her arms and began to talk. “I seen you on the news today,” she began. “It was great how you took charge in that little standoff with whatever that freak was.” “That freakcreated by the same man that he was holding hostage,” I replied as I continue to dig through a stack of files on my desk. “My name is Lilly by the way. I had heard you were asking my grandmother about me. I keep looking for you every day so I can actually talk to you, but you haven’t been around the office much lately. I was beginning to think you left.” “No,” I replied to her still not looking up from the files. “I’ve just been really busy on a case and most of what I’ve needed to do has been outside the office.” I understand. Your job is a very important one. I just hope it doesn’t keep you from the things that you want most in life. I’d hate to see such a handsome guy as yourself end up alone for the rest of his life,” said Lilly as I looked up at her beautiful face and she smiled at me. “Look,” I began. “I really like you Lilly, and ever since the first time I laid eyes on you, I have tried so hard to get up the courage to ask you out. Now, you are right in front of me. I’d love to ask you out on a date and do the whole mating dance routine with you, but right now I am swamped with work.” “Well, you know where to find me if you actually decided to take a chance,” she said in a disappointed tone as she walked away from my desk and finished delivering lunch to the rest of the people on our floor. I really didn’t want to talk to her like that but right now I had to be focused. I know I might have come off a little harsh to her, but I made sure she knew how I felt about her and when work isn’t so busy, I’ll do everything in my power to act on my feelings for her. I just hoped that when that day comes, it wouldn’t be to late. There it was, the thing I was looking for that should be damaging enough to put Celis behind bars. I closed the file, got up from my desk and started my way toward the Chief’s door. This time Celis Grom wasn’t getting out of jail on any sort of technicality. I don’t even think he’s going to have a chance at a trial once I put things into motion. But first I had to show the Chief. I knocked on his office door once I had made it to it. He waved his hand for me to come in. Once inside I threw the file down in front of the Chief. “What’s this?” asked the Chief a little surprised. “Evidence that is going to put Celis Grom behind bars for good.” * * * Meanwhile, in an undisclosed location and in a rather dark room other than a few monitors, a man sits in a chair. The man can’t be seen clearly as he has his back to the monitors. A voice is heard somewhere in the rom. “Anything to report on our little experiment?” “Everything is right on schedule and going according to plan,” said the man in the chair. “Good,” began the other voice. “If there is any changes, make sure to report them to ‘HIM’ right away.” “Yes sir.”
