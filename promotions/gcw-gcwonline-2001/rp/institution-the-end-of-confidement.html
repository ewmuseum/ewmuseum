Institution: The End of Confidement

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Unknown This next story, is possibly the most gruesome of them all, this story is not for the weak stomached. Aiden will undergo grueling tests of his physic and his mental state. Although, every single thing that Aiden goes through he understands because he comes to the belief that the lord is doing this as punishment for what he had done, to end all of the punishment. Aiden overcomes every obstacle put forth in front of him and he narrowly escapes death. “Freedom, finally.” I said to myself as the day came for me to be transported from the jail. Although freedom would soon follow this encounter, I had no clue of what was about to happen. As I walked from my cell outside the fences, tears of joy were streaming down my face. Two guards, of decent stature walked by my side, escorted me to the gate of the prison and began to enter the code into the first of the two gates in which you had to go through to actually exit the prison. Although, there were these two fences there was another fence following this fence that had two large towers on it. The two men entered numbers into each of the keypads then unlocked my handcuffs after we exited the second gate, they stuck me into a Hum-V and we began traveling towards the other fence. The feeling of freedom wasn’t so fresh in so long, but freedom was farther away than I had thought. Upon exiting the gates I was transferred into another van, a large black armored van in which I was strapped into a straight jacket and they sat me in the van and began the questioning. “Alright, this is patient 296344000.” The smaller of the two men in the car said. “His name is Jameson Myers, then correct?” the larger man replied to him. “No, I am Aiden Jackson.” I screamed out to both of them. “Sure you are buddy.” They both said in unison They would never come to believe that I was actually Aiden Jackson; they thought I was truly a mass murderer, a crazy man who murdered 12 people in various sick ways. It would be nearly 2 months later that they would find after all of the mal treatment that I truly was Aiden Jackson. [Forward 1 Month] “Jameson, Are you ready for your treatment?” the doctor said to me as the two large men dressed in nurse like outfits picked me up for my daily treatment. I was strapped into the straight jacket and tied down to a chair so that I couldn’t move as they questioned me. The doctor then reached into his jacket pulling out a bottle then twisting off the cap, then pulling out the small pink pill and then forcing it down my throat. I felt as though I was being treated like an animal, this substance they fed me was a truth pill of some sort. The only problem was I was actually telling the truth. “Now, Mr. Jameson as we have been asking you for the past month. What is your true identity and why are you here?” he said to me firmly as he stared at me intently, he adjusted his glasses slightly as he said this to me. “I am Aiden Jackson, I saw a ghost and they brought me here from the prison.” I said to him firmly. “Now Jameson, you know the punishment for lying.” He said back to me. He then leaned to one of the guards and signaled towards me speaking quietly to him. Although he spoke quietly I could hear the words that he spoke to him slightly. “It seems as though this medication we are using on him is having an adverse effect, we should have this documented immediately. Get a box ready we are gonna throw him in there as a punishment.” He said to the guard. “You’re not putting me in there you asshole.” I screamed at him. At this moment, the two large guards grabbed me and I let my legs go limp in an attempt to drag myself away from this box, this box was hardly big enough for me to fit in the box was the opposite of everything my brother had said. I felt betrayed by the lord my father and my own brother. Maybe this was the appropriate punishment though, something to finally teach me of what it means to corrupt the cycle that God has put before each and every one of us. The two men slammed the door shut to the small compartment area. I began to scream, and slam myself against the walls around me. I was engulfed by darkness soon I faded away crashing into a nightmare. The house of Michael Mason surrounded me; he sat there deviously in the chair with his vindictive style. I loathed the man as I stared at him intently with the knife in my hand. I knew what was about to come next, following the murder Georgia Mason walked in, she stared at her husband on the ground weeping then holding me tightly. “I should have told you sooner son. I knew you would find out sooner or later and I know you would have wanted revenge. Here take this, it is the diary of my husband it might be able to aid you in seeking vengeance against the others involved in the murder of your parents.” She said to me as she let go of me. “Georgia...there were others” Dear Lord, Thank you Georgia, I am sorry.” I said to her. Hours had passed by this time and finally the light crept in through the door as they opened it I was awakened from the dark slumber that I had encountered. The two large men pulled me from the cold hard steel; they took a rag to my face wiping the drool and sweat away. “God save me.” I said quietly as they withdrew me from the compartment. I stumbled around in the straight jacket as they released me; I walked forward two steps then fell forward smashing my head against the cold hard cement floor. The guards then grabbed me up and I felt the pounding sensation in the temple of my forehead. “What the hell is your problem!”” I screamed out to them. At this moment they covered my mouth shoving pills into my throat then forcing me to swallow them. Minutes later, I awoke in the darkness of my room, I got up from the bed that I was resting in and gazed outside of the window, wishing that freedom would come. The downfall of snow glistened around the one streetlamp in the distance. I walked back to the small cot like bed that sat adjacent to my window, close enough that the dim moonlight of that night shone in ever so beautifully reflecting from the snow-covered ground. I gradually drifted into a slumber, a deep slumber. [Forward 2 Months] As we left off the last events were seemingly cruel and unjustifiable yet the actions seemed as though they were necessary for the doctors to conduct. The actions that these men took against me continued onward, every time I had a different dream. Each dream was related, intertwined like a story too. The time came for me to be thrown into the box once again; this is another key dream in finding out where “Michael Mason’s Journal” is if it truly does exist. The two large guards who had taken me there took me there once again, shoving the pills into my mouth and slamming me against the wall as I resisted. They finally forced me into the box and shut it. My usual ritual of hyperventilating then passing out into an unconscious state, a dream filled state. I looked around, I was in the library I had been in several years before. I walked up to Georgia and began speaking to her. She looked horrible, her face pale and she walked droopily around the library placing books on the shelves and browsing on the computer. “Georgia, Can you help me find this book?” I said to her softly. She looked up at me at this instance and a face of horror shone back at me. She turned away from me and began to weep, as she looked back at me she had the face of Michael she then fell to the floor in convulsions. In a state of panic, I ran around the counter and began to conduct CPR, hoping and praying to save her life to obtain the information that I needed to find the diary of Michael Mason. “A-i’den, look beneath the heart and you shall find the”..di?” she pushed this out with her last dying breath then lay there lifelessly. I awoke in the box screaming, slamming against the box. For some reason, I had come to believe that Georgia Mason had truly died for at this moment she was lying on the floor of her home or the library or some place dying. Although I wasn’t sure whether or not she was, I got that gut instinct the one you feel when you absolutely know that something is wrong. After an hour of slamming against the walls, they released me from the box. Taking me out of the straight jacket was possibly the hugest mistake they made. I went into a fit slamming the two guards into the wall. One guard turned around to meet one of the steel poles lying on the floor. The other guard turned around meeting my left fist. After around 10 minutes the guards finally gained control of me going into a fit of rage. The doctor finally made a decision and advised I not be hurt by them. [Forward 3 Months] I sat there in a chair staring forward to the television in the small, cold room. The other patients, the true nutcases roamed around me screaming cusswords often and sometimes coming and talking to me about their issues. “Do you see him?” a small man leans in and utters this to me as he stares at the walk with a blank expression cast onto his face. “See what?” I said to him calmly. “Jesus. Do you see him?” he replied to me. “Are you kidding me” Jesus is not there buddy.” I said to him in reply. “He is so, you’re a crazy man you can’t see him but all these other people can!” he said to me. As he spoke these words from his mouth, I blanked him out nearly half way through the sentence. I saw a sight that I thought I would never see again, a man walking from the hallway looking as though he was an angel to me. Jesse walked down the hallway with a worried look on his face, he entered the room and ran towards me staring for a moment then beginning to utter words. “Ai’Aiden are you okay?” He said to me softly. “Yeah, I’m fine I just need to get the fuck out of this shit hole man, I can’t take it in here anymore. The mal treatment, these people are trying to kill me here and then there’s all these nut jobs around me talking about how they see flying purple monkeys and how Jesus is standing right over there.” I said to him with a hint of anger in my voice. “Aiden, I’ll be right back I swear, I need to go talk to the owner.” He said to me quickly. “Okay.” Jesse disappeared only for a moment; he rushed back into the room and pulled me up from the chair as the owner appeared with him. Both of them helped me walk from the room and set me in a wheelchair as we made our way through the large doorway. “Aiden Jackson, I am so sorry for the mistake. The doctor who conducted these experiments on you will be punished for you are truly a sane man. Please Aiden forgive me and I will be sending a large sum of money your way for the troubles that have been caused.” The large owner said to me as he pushed me down the hallway. “Don’t worry about it. I can walk so just let me get the hell outta this wheelchair and walk down the hallway myself.” I said to him firmly. Finally the true freedom that I deserved, after all of the cruel treatment and such, it would never matter again because I would be a free man. I repaid my debts to the Lord and I feel as though I am near the representation of freedom as an eagle is, I feel as though it has been a lifetime in jail. Finally, this nightmare is over but another book is about to be opened and another story told for the men and women who walk before me on this earth. Blessed is the man who trusts in the Lord and has made the Lord his hope and confidence. - Jeremiah 17:7 Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge Him and He shall direct your paths. - Proverbs 3:5,6 “Thank you, God.” I muttered to myself as I left the Institution [Fade out]
