Master Plan

10 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-10-2007 V/O: I’m going to do something that I haven’t done in quite a long time. [Fade up.] [DeBough sits in a large executive chair in front of a camera, dressed in a black suit, white shirt, and a cornflower blue tie. His eyes sparkle in the artificial lighting of the small room, as he almost eyes the viewers, ignoring the fact that there is in fact a camera.] DeBough: No one seems to take the time to do a good old fashioned promo anymore. We’re all to goddamn focused on our personal lives to give a flying fuck about our jobs, save when we’re in the arena. [He smirks.] DeBough: I realize that I haven’t made a very prominent stand in GCW as of yet, save for toying with Terror and his precious Televsion title. That’s just a way of fucking around for right now. Play dumb. Act like I give a shit about the TV title. [Click.] [A plume of smoke exits his mouth as he lights up a cigarette and takes a drag.] DeBough: That’s just a filler. My eye is really on what we all really want. [His eyes narrow.] DeBough: The GCW World Championship. [Fanfare!] [Fireworks!] [Other things that exonerate excitement and prestige!] DeBough: Now, don’t get me wrong Jay. I won’t lose to you this Sunday at Dangerous Games. [He pulls out the TV title that has since been returned to him, and lays it on his lap.] I will continue to tote this around with a shit eating grin on my face, and make you feel like a fucking douchebag for letting me steal it. [Blink.] DeBough: Oh yea. I know I stole it. I know that it in all actuality, this is your title. Jay Terror is the legitimate GCW Television Title. But who is looking at legitimacy right now? All anyone can see is that for the past month, Tyson DeBough has been the man that has carried around this belt on his shoulder, and that Tyson DeBough is the man that has evaded Jay Terror for a month, and managed to keep his title from him. [Shit. Eating. Grin.] DeBough: What does the world think of a man who is “legitimately” the title holder, but can’t seem to get his hands on his own gold?” [Pause.] [Pause.] [From dramatics mostly.] [Pause.] DeBough: Absolutley, fucking, nothing. That’s what. You can piss and moan all you want about how I’ve been under Caldera’s protection, and how I’ve run away from you, and you haven’t been able to get ahold of me, and all that shit. But the truth Jay? The truth is that if you really were as good as you fucking claim to be, you’d have gotten your belt back. You’d have figured out a way to outsmart me. [He takes another drag.] DeBough: Which is exactly why you won’t win at Dangerous Games. [He feigns mock surprise.] DeBough: Why you ask? Why won’t you win?” You sit there watching me, and yell at the screen “What do you mean I won’t win?” [His eyebrow raises a smidge.] DeBough: Because I’m smarter. That’s why. So as soon as Dangerous Games is over, I’ll have solidified to the rest of the GCW roster that I am in fact that man who deserves to hold on to this title, and you are nothing more than a cheap version of the Marlboro man. And then? [Another sarcastic grin.] DeBough: I’ll do it again, when I’m the one who tosses your sorry, wheezing, smoke fatigued ass out of the ring in the main event at Dangerous Games. And then? When I am the last one left standing in that ring, and have earned the right to main event at NC-17’you’ll be left asking yourself how the fuck the man that was supposed to be a coward, and hid behind his boss ended up in the main event, with a shot to fight for the GCW World Title. [He takes one last drag and flicks his cigarette off-screen.] DeBough: Mark my words. It will happen. [He shifts his weight.] That goes for the rest of the GCW roster as well. [The screen goes black.] See you at Dangerous Games. Fuckheads. [Cut.] [Fin.]
