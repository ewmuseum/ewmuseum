What Goes Through His Mind

10 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/2008 The sky is a strange orange; it’s not quite the end of the day and it’s not quite the beginning of the night. A car rumbles down a bumpy gravel road in the middle of what looks to be a farm that was once prosperous, but has long since been abandoned. The fields are filled with weeds and wildflowers twisted together in such a way that would leave you to believe there was a tall tower with a long-haired princess in it on the other side. As the car continues down the rocky road, an old, abandoned barn comes into view. It’s so beat up it looks like a two year old could blow it over. Yet, the car continues to hustle down the road like it’s on a mission. Then, as the car comes within one hundred meters of the barn... BOOOM!!!!! Without warning, the barn suddenly explodes in a HUGE fireball! The man inside of the car suddenly hits the breaks, bringing the car to a screeching halt. He then ducks under the dash as hundreds of pieces of lumber ? many still on fire from the blast ? begin to rain down on and everywhere around the car. Some of the falling debris lands in the field of twisted flora and it doesn’t take long before fires begin to pop up all over the place. It appears that the area is a dry one as the fire spreads quickly through the weeds and wildflowers, causing heavy amounts of smoke to plume up into the air all around the deserted farm. As the initial explosion finally subsides and the debris finally stops falling, the man in the car gets back up into his driver’s seat and adjusts his glasses. He then looks out at his surroundings from within his car, unable to see anything but smoke and flames all around him. The man decides to get out of the car to get a better look, but leaves the car running in case he has to get a move on. He takes a look around and doesn’t get much more information from his venture outside his vehicle. Unfortunately, the information he gains isn’t pleasant; a large piece of flaming lumber from the barn landed about a dozen meters behind his car, blocking a backwards escape. There’s no way he can go out to the left or right, as the fields on either side of him are rapidly becoming even more enshrouded in flame and smoke. No, the only method of escape seems to be, ironically, to head towards the barn and hope there’s another road going away from it. The man turns to go back into his car. However, before he can get back in his car and go that way, he hears a harsh, raspy, chilling voice coming from behind the smoke in front of him. “Like what I’ve done with the place?” The man quickly spins around to find himself staring face to face with one of GCW’s newest additions to the roster: Face. Face would probably have a devilish smirk on his face if he were able to show any emotion through his mask. Of course, the question is dealt with a motionless visage that makes it that much more creepy. The question is then followed up by a flick of a lighter that Face pulls out of one of the pockets in his ragged dress pants ? if you can still call them that. The man, already practically shaking in his boots from the fires that surround him, now looks quite terrified as Face paces methodically around him. Face is apparently waiting for an answer to his question, flicking his lighter on and off like clockwork as he does. However, Face gets no answer from the man, who is unsure of what to do now with the flames and smoke growing larger and higher and this strange, scarred, masked man pacing around him. Face decides to cut the silence with his nails on a chalkboard voice. “What” Are you a little...” Face grabs at his tie, pretending to do it up tightly. “Choked up” The place was run down...and I thought you’d love to be the man to see this disgrace of a farm come tumbling down. No? I was wrong? Shame.” The man goes from being terrified to extremely angry as he can’t believe a man would just blow up a barn like that. He suddenly snaps out at Face. “What the HELL are you doing!”!”!” Are you fucking crazy!” How the Hell do you expect us to get out of this place now!”” The man stares harshly at Face, who just stares him down with his seemingly sightless eyes after the outburst directed at him. After a few moments, Face responds, a sudden outburst in flames coming within a couple meters of the two men as he does. “Oh...I’m sorry; you wanted to get out” Hmmm...I’m afraid that can’t happen now that you know I was the one who blew up that barn and burned away the landscape...” The man’s harsh stare suddenly turns back into a look of deep fear as Face looks down at his lighter, continuing to flick it on and off every few seconds. He then continues to speak; every chilling word is another nail the terrified man in front of him can feel piercing through his heart. “Isn’t it funny how much a couple barrels of gasoline and a lighter can do...” Well...I suppose not for you. I suppose this is quite the abrupt and tragic ending for you my friend...But you’ll have to come to terms with it in the next few minutes that you have to live. Oh, and don’t worry about your squeeze back at home. Sure, she’ll grieve for a while...but she’ll eventually move on and find a new stud to play house with.” Face’s rather nonchalant nature about the subject of a fiery death sends endless chills down the spine of the man in front of him, who is just frozen in shock, fear and awe. Face suddenly steps towards the man, causing the man to jump away. Face takes another sudden step, pushing the man further away with the invisible bubble of force known as fear. However, Face never intended on reaching this man as he suddenly opens the man’s car door and gets into the driver’s seat. He shuts the door and, before the man can fully grasp what is happening, he floors the gas pedal, causing the car to lurch forward! The man dives out of the way as Face drives off towards the barn. Just as he leaves, the fires surrounding the poor man suddenly become more intense. The end seems imminent...but it is never reached as everything ? all the fire and smoke and debris ? suddenly disappears. It is then replaced with what was seen before the explosion, as if nothing had ever happened at all. The car makes it to the barn, stopping about a dozen meters away ? the driver clearly not wanting to get close to the horribly held together structure. A man gets out of the driver’s seat, an unsure look on his face that pretty much says “what the Hell am I doing here!”” Nonetheless, this relatively short man with curly black hair and glasses steps forward out of the car’s driver’s seat. He has a notepad in one hand and a pen in the other. He looks at his surroundings, his face not displaying any more cheer as he does. However, he soon puts his thoughts of this location aside and calls out: “Hello! Is anybody here!” I’m Jeremy Myers, from The Wrestling Tribune!” Jeremy waits patiently for a response, keeping a lookout all around himself. He doesn’t have to wait long as the now all-too-familiar Face steps out from inside the barn, his twisted dream unfulfilled...for now.
