Vince Jones: A Humiliated Man, A Broken Man...A Lonely Man

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/2005 The screen is pitch black and all is silent. Suddenly a message fades in onto the screen in white text. 'One week ago...' The camera slowly fades in to the outside of the arena in which WorldWide 30 took place in Denver, Colorado. The air feels warm and cars can be seen slowly rolling down the streets outside of the packed arena. Denver natives can be seen making their way inside, outside, and up and down the block outside the arena going about their usually daily business. Vince Jones steps out through the front doors of the arena gazing off into the Denver night. His valet, Jasmine, comes running out the front doors after him. She grabs him by the arm trying to get his attention, but he just stands there in a daze. Jasmine: Vince, what's up? Vince Jones just shrugs his shoulders and stands there in silence as his latest defeat at the hands of Coyle O' Connor has finally sunk in. Jasmine looks on at Vince with worried eyes. Jasmine: V, so you lost again. Its no big deal. There will be other matches, V. C'mon. Let's just get going and head back to New York. Vince breaks away from Jasmine's grasp and just shakes his head in disappointment with himself. Jasmine just looks on at his demeanor in surprise. Jasmine: V, it'll be alright. Vince totally ignores her words and remains silent. All of a sudden a low, but gentle voice begins to speak in the background. 'The night was October 28th, 2005 and 'The Violence' Vince Jones had once again left without the taste of victory in his mouth. The once threatening Vince Jones had not only been defeated at the hands of Coyle O' Conner, but had experienced the ultimate humiliation by being caught prancing around the backstage area in a Peter Pan suit. Adversity had finally reached its peak. 'The Violence' Vince Jones who had never in his career experienced such an awful slump of misfortune would now leave the arena and Denver, Colorado a humiliated man, a broken man...a lonely man.' Vince Jones lets out a long sigh, lowers his head in shame, and begins slowly lumbering down the street with only his black gym bag to comfort him. A piano can now be heard playing a familiar tune in the background, 'The Lonely Man' from the old Incredible Hulk t.v series of the late 70s and early 80s. As Vince Jones trudges down the block a group of GCW fans can be seen standing there watching him in amusement and laughing outloud. GCW Fans: (in unison) Hahahahahaha!!!! Vince Jones, you suck!! You suck!! You suck!! Vince Jones stops in his tracks looks up at them in sorrow, sorrow for his own current state of being. One of the fans looks down at his gym bag and sees the Peter Pan suit barely sticking out the top of Vince's bag. GCW Fan: Hahahaha!! Hey look!! He's still got that Peter Pan suit with him!!! GCW Fans: (in unison) Hahahahahaha!!! Peter Pan! Peter Pan! Peter Pan! Vince Jones lowers his head once again and continues on down the block as the camera slowly fades to black. A message in white lettering slowly fades in on the screen. A Few Days Later At A Restaurant in New York City... The message slowly fades out as the camera fades into a restaurant in New York at lunch time as the sad piano theme music continues to play in the background. Where Vince Jones is seen sitting at a table all alone with his head hung low. A beautiful blonde haired waitress stops by his table with a smile on her face and a hot bowl of soup in hand, but her smile slowly fades from her face as she spots Vince and notices his apparent sadness. She sets the bowl of soup down in front of him and walks away. Vince Jones looks at the bowl of soup barely interested in touching it. The camera zooms out and we see two young kids, a boy and a girl, at another table across the room staring intently at Vince Jones with curiousity in their eyes. Young Girl: (points at Vince Jones) Who is that guy? What's wrong with him? He doesn't look like he wants to eat. Young Boy: Him? Oh, he's that Violence Vince Jones guy. He's a wrestler in GCW. He sucks though. The camera pans and catches sight of the bearded, glasses wearing father of the two children. He looks at the two of them with a stern expression on his face as he sets his fork down on his plate. Father: Alright. That's enough out of you two. Leave that poor guy alone. He's obviously having enough problems right now as it is. Now quit staring at him before his sorriness rubs off on the both of you. We all know he sucks. There's no use making a big scene out of it. The slowly zooms back in on Vince Jones who reaches for his glass of water and in the process accidentally flips over his bowl of soup. Vince Jones looks down at the overturned mess of soup, picks up his spoon, and slams it down in frustration. Everyone in the restaurant notices Vince's predicament and begins laughing outloud. Restaurant customers: (in unison) You suck! You suck! You suck! You suck! Vince holds his head and lowers it in shame. The camera switches back to the table with the two children and the father who were eating together. The kids are laughing outloud at Vince. Young Girl: Wow! He does suck, Tommy. I'm only five and I never spill my soup all over the table, cuz I'm big girl. Young Boy: Yeah! He does suck! Father: Hey! Enough out of you two. What did I tell you before? Now if you don't wanna grow up to be a bum like him then eat your green beans. The kids immediately start scarfing down their green beans as the camera slowly fades out. Another message slowly fades in on the screen. ...At A Playground The message slowly fades out and the camera fades in as Vince Jones can be seen walking with his head still hung low past a small playground in New York where kids can be seen playing and having a good time. All of a sudden a little red-headed boy on a swing turns around and spots Vince Jones walking down the block. His open wide and he hops off the swing, runs across the playground, out the gates, and rushes over to Vince Jones' side. Red-Headed boy: Hey Mister! Vince Jones stops in his tracks, turns, and looks down at the boy in silence. The little boy looks up at him with a large smile on his face. Red-Headed boy: Aren't you 'The Violence' Vince Jones? Vince Jones nods in agreement somewhat happy to know someone recognizes him and isn't about to bash him for his recent slump of bad luck. Red-Headed boy: Cooooooool! Well, there's something I've been wanting to tell you if I met you. The smile quickly switches to an angry look on the face of the child. Red-Headed boy: You suck! All the kids at school make fun of me now because I cheer for you, you big fat loser! The little boy kicks Vince Jones square in the shin and runs back into the playground. Vince Jones bends down and rubs the spot on his shin where he was kicked. All of a sudden all the little children in the playground run up to the fence and begin laughing at him. Playground Children: (in unison) You suck! You suck! You suck! You suck! You suck! Vince Jones stands to his feet and flashes his middle finger at them in fury. He then lowers his head once again and slowly walks away as the sad piano music continues to play. The camera fades to black as another message flashes up on the screen. ...At a Nightclub in New York City. The message fades out as the camera fades into a nightclub where Vince Jones can be seen sitting in the V.I.P section leaning back in his seat with a half empty glass of Hypnotiq sitting in front of him. A lovely hispanic woman passes by his table without even giving him a glance. Vince slaps the table getting her attention and motions for her to take a seat next to him. She just shakes her head and laughs. Hispanic woman: Yeah right! I can't be seen with a loser like you! Forget it! Win a match for once! The woman leaves as a beautiful black woman with short hair passes by. Vince motions for her to join him, but she just passes him by without even acknowledging him. A group of white women pass by and he motions for them to join him, but they just laugh and keep on going. A hot Asian woman passes by and Vince tries to get her attention, but she picks up her speed and rushes past his table. Vince Jones lowers his head in shame once again. All of a sudden the owner of the club approaches Vince's table and taps him on the shoulder. Vince slowly raises his head as the owner folds his arms across his chest. Owner: Excuse me, sir, but you are no longer considered V.I.P around here. I'm gonna have to ask you to get up and move. Vince Jones shakes his head and slowly rises to his feet and leaves the table taking his glass of Hypnotiq along with him. Owner: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Not so fast, buddy. I'm gonna have to ask you to leave that glass. That's a V.I.P only glass, sir. Vince looks down at the glass of Hypnotiq, sets it down on the table, and bids it farewell as he is ushered away from the table by the club owner. The camera slowly fades out and fades back in. We see a sign which says 'Losers Section'. The camera moves downward and catches sight of Vince Jones who is seen with an angry scowl on his face after being booted out of the V.I.P section. A small, half empty plastic cup of Hypnotiq sits in front of him. The women in the club continue to pass by his table without even paying him any mind at all. All of a sudden a fat woman passes by his table and looks at him with a rather seductive look on her face. Fat Woman: I bet you want a piece of this, big boy. Don't ya? In Vince's sorry state of sadness and desperation he looks up at the fat woman with hungry eyes. She sticks out her booty in Vince's direction and begins patting it. Fat Woman: Oh, I bet you want a big slice of this rump meat. Don't ya? Oh, I know you do, Big Daddy. Vince nods his head in agreement as she turns towards him and begins laughing outloud. Fat Woman: Well, too bad! You can't have it, ya big loser! I can't be seen with bums like you! The music suddenly stops and everyone in the club turns towards Vince Jones and begin laughing and pointing. Clubbers: (in unison) Hahahahahahahaha!!! You suck! You suck! You suck! You suck! Vince Jones can't take anymore humiliation. He quickly rises to his feet and trudges out the club with his head hanging low. The camera slowly fades out and fades back in to a overhead view of the streets of New York. Vince Jones can be seen walking down a lonely city block of New York City alone with his head hung low. The low, gentle voice of the narrator from earlier on begins to speak over the soft piano music that continues to play nonstop in the background. 'Vince Jones was in a sorry state. He felt as though his world was crumbling around him. Will this downward spiral continue? Who knows? Not Vince Jones. Tune into GCW WorldWide 31 this week when Vince Jones, Rich Rollins, and Mike Savige take on Seymour Almasy, Michael Stevens, and Ricky Thunder in a main event 3-on-3 tag match to find out. Until then 'The Violence' Vince Jones continues to tread through life with a heavy weight on his heart as a humiliated man, a broken man...a lonely man' Vince suddenly stops in his tracks, looks up at the sky, shakes his fists and begins yelling. Vince Jones: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!! All of a sudden a light is turned in one of the apartments and the window opens up. A bald headed, beer-bellied man in a t-shirt sticks his head out the window and looks down at Vince Jones in fury. Bald headed man: Hey! Shut the hell up with that noise down there! People tryin' to sleep around here, ya loser! The bald headed man slams the window shut and turns off the light. Vince Jones lower his head once again and continues down the block in silence as the camera slowly fades to black.
