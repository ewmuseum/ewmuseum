Date: 03-Nov-2006 Archived for storyline purposes ****************************************************** GTT6 Round 2. Danielle Northsky vs. 'The Fallen Angel' Angelica Dominique. Angelica is a free agent in PTC, who calls a federation that uses the name R4GE her home. She's supposed to be pretty good, and to come up from some federation that no one has ever heard of and take the shot, and make it past the first round? It would seem to prove that she is. But there are things she's not good at. And that is portraying herself to someone like Dani as a threat. 'The Fallen Angel'? Please, Dani is a member of the KnightMare Brigade. Their parent organization is head by a man who wrestled under the name of 'The Fallen One' Nephilim. For those that don't know, that means that the main trainer of everyone in the Order already knows someone with that name, since Nephilim means 'Fallen Angel'. To top it off, his late first wife, Xian, went by the name of Angelique when she was in the sport. While the talent may be there, there is nothing here that doens't point to just someone trying to make a quick name for themselves and using one of the most common monikers in this sport today. But what is there to truly fear? Should she be feared? Heh? She's got a brother in law who is a garou, or werewolf. She's got a good friend who is not only garou like Grayson is, but is also descended from the Faery people of Ireland. His fianc?, her best friend within the Brigade is the same thing, but from Japan. And let's not forget people like Virgil Ambrosi, a vampire. Or even GTT6 favorite and former Global Champion, Shadow, whom Dani calls by her real first name of Lia, who is also a twelve hundred year old vampire. Shadow's lover? Dawn McFarlane? They don't even know what the hell she is yet. But the biggest one of all is the one that makes this match that much more interesting. One one hand you have a woman using the name of 'The Fallen Angel'. On the other hand? A woman who hides the fact that she's? an angel. Danielle Lynn Northsky is the sixth great granddaughter of a Sioux Legend. The legend of a Wakan Taken, a white skinned man who came down from the sky, who took a Sioux woman as a wife. His name to the Sioux was 'Morning Star', as just like the Angels of the bible, all of the Sioux Wakan Taken were represented by a star in the sky. However, Dani, has found out the truth about her multiple time great grandfather, whom she has met. His real name is Kokabel, meaning 'Star of God' and is the astrologer of heaven. At one time with two hundred other angels, he fell from heaven to take a woman he had lusted after as his wife. Their child, Fallen Star, had been a giant and a great hero to the Sioux People. But all of that had changed. Kokabel was the only one of the two hundred who was allowed back into heaven after having defeated a great evil in the new world. And now Dani is the seventh generation in this line that has passed down through the Northskys. Named for the region of the sky that the Morning Star resides in to the Sioux people. One of these women wears a pair of dark wings attached to her jacket when she comes to the ring. The other hides them, for if she truly showed people what she was? how would the world take it? ~Just off the Rosebud Reservation~ ~Black Hills, South Dakota~ Steven Matoskah, the cousin to Danielle Northsky on her mother's side had been in a foul mood for weeks, and the cause of a lot of it was his cousin. The few days she'd been in town on the 'rez', everyone could only talk about how successful she'd become. They could only talk about how the daughter of the Wakan Takan legend had come home for a bit. A legend that he didn't believe to be true. How could it be that Dani was born on this earth with the traits and attributes of what the white man called an angel. He couldn't believe it. No one in their right mind would. For all he knew, it was all a story that people like his stupid grandfather had shown her after he'd brought her home. And he remembered when they had brought home the ten year old brat as it was. He'd been the baby of the family, and suddenly, that had this girl who'd been raised in the white man's religious orphanage coming in. And everyone pampered her. Everyone loved her. And everyone WORSHIPPED HER! He took another swig of the bottle that he'd been drinking from all day. It was labeled to be the worst that the white man could have. But it was something that he drank whenever he could, Jack Daniels' Kentucky Whiskey. The bottle was only seated in front of him, and more then half of it was already missing. The bag he'd brought it home in this morning. He'd brought it not to the home he shared with his grandfather, but to the home that his woman, Terra, had given to her by this organization he knew as the Order from both her and Dani. Terra was his long time girlfriend, and the Order was paying for her to finish college, and held her under retainer as a resident expert on Sioux myths. They had taken the story of Dani and her mythical blood line, hook, line and sinker. 'BABE?' It was Terra as she was in the other room. 'What?' Her head poked into the dining room and kitchen combo. The woman was dressed in her usual hot topic style. He loved her and she was a minx in the sack, but she was not one to dress the role of a full blooded Sioux. But then again, she'd gone to the white man's colleges and was getting an education to make her a better person. 'You didn't hear the door bell, huh? You're hunting buddies are here.' As she said that, two others came into the room. They were dressed as Steven was in dirty t-shirts, jeans, bandannas and cowboy hats. One of the two, smaller with his hair in multiple brave braids walked over and sat down next to Steven. He immediately grabbed one of the glasses and started to pour himself some Jack. Steven's hand shot out to cover it. 'Who said you could?' 'I did. It's the least you could do with what I've got for you.' Steven pulled his hand back and let him pour. 'Speak the words then.' The guy finished pouring and then took a gulp of the firewater. He then passed the glass to his friend, who upon closer inspection could be a sibling to the other one. 'Well, I got hooked up with that chick that wanted to talk. She took me to her employer.' He was getting anxious and it was annoying Steven. 'Say it!' The man almost jumped out of his skin as Steven yelled. 'Hey, this guy looks like that crazy Nakota that used to hang around here, Jaymes. Well, he's full blooded and has offered us not only cash, but gear if we wanted to raise some hell.' 'Gear?' He'd peaked Steven's interest. 'Yeah, Gear. As in right now I've got an SKS made AK-47 sitting in my trunk with three boxes of ammo to try it out. And he said he'd support us if we wanted to do it.' Steven nodded at this. 'So, what do you want to do?' 'Get back in touch with them. I want to meet him first. In the meantime, you've got how much ammo for that thing?' All three smiled wide as they drank down the warm liquid. ~Dallas, Texas~ ~The Location of the last GCW Show~ ~Maple Manor Inn~ Dani had been awake for hours as she laid there next to Josh. He'd been snoring for some time after they had finished for the evening, but not her. She could not find sleep, in fact she hadn't in the last week. Was probably the reason why she had messed up in her first ever world title shot in her career, having tapped out to a hold from Lestatt Knight. However, while she had failed to bring the world title home to the KnightMare Brigade: Dies Irae, but she had looked good doing it. But then again, as stated, it had been the first time in her now two year career ever getting a shot for a title that high in standing. Soon in a few days though, she'd be traveling to face against another woman in the GTT6 tourney, and then coming back to Houston to take part in a stable war match at GCW's Devil's Night Super Show. She stood up and stretched, smoothing down one of Josh's old t-shirts that she'd stolen as an over sized night shirt for herself. She didn't know the band, but she usually tuned out some of Josh's choices as it was. It didn't take long for her to be up, when she felt her husband's eyes upon her. 'You okay?' She didn't answer right away, and walked over to the large cage and perch combination that had come with them. Gray and Alexis would be taking it back to Las Vegas while Dani headed to Madrid, Spain. On the perch was a large bird of the macaw family of birds. It's feathers were electric blue with an occasional yellow highlight, especially a small mark under each eye. A Hycanith macaw that Dani has kept for over a year, an original gift from Josh to her. It's name was 'Angel' that she pronounced as if it was Spanish. The bird was an easy thirty inches from head to the tip of it's tail feathers. She reached up and started to scratch the bird behind it's feather covered ears and watched as it extended it's feathers into a crest to allow her access to the skin underneath on the top of it's head. She continued to scratch as the bird spoke to her. 'Morning, beautiful!' She smiled, Josh had taught it that. 'Dani, are you okay?' She looked over her shoulder to Josh who now sat up on the bed. She finally answered him. 'Yeah, I'm fine. I guess I've just got a lot on my mind. Especially the situation with back home. Steven's bid for tribal council is upsetting me.' Josh didn't hide the fact that he rolled his eyes at the mentioning of her cousin's name. 'Anyone who would vote for him is an idiot. It'd be like voting for Ashcroft for president in 2008.' While she couldn't disagree, she just shook her head though. 'If grandpa just appointed him, there would be no election.' Josh got up out of bed and headed over to wrap his arms around her waist. 'Look, your grandpa is one of the smartest man I know. He'd not do that. You saw how he handled everything.' She nodded and then leaned her head back to kiss him. 'I know that, it's just? I don't know, I'm just not happy with it all. Steven was partially right. While I've been off wrestling, I've lost touch with the rez and the people there. But as the last in the Northsky line, I should be there for them. If I'm some sort of legend come true for them, I should be there as a symbol of hope at least.' 'You are. Did you see them and hear them during the ceremony while we were there? They couldn't get enough of you. I think you signed more copies of that old FHM you posed for that night then yo have most nights at wrestling events. You give them hope that you can succeed in this world even being one of them. You got off the reservation and you're making a name for yourself in the rest of the world. That's just as much a symbol of hope as the statues and graves at Little Big Horn.' She knew he was correct. But there were things that were still in doubt with her. She turned around and looked up at his green eyes. 'But how can I by this mythical symbol of hope when I really don't even know what I'm here for. Hell, I don't even know why I keep wrestling sometimes. I prefer being a model. Then there are the other things going on.' 'You're not getting down for loosing to Knight are you?' She shook her head no. 'No, I half expected it. He's twice my size, and has three to four times the experience. Someday I'll be a world champion. But it was nice to get a shot while I'm still considered a rookie.' 'You're no rookie.' She smiled, actually pulled away from him a bit. 'I am for another year according to all the magazines, Josh, so are you. Even with your two tag team title belts you carry around right now. We're anomaly in the business. We've done more then most do in their whole careers and we're still eligible for rookie of the year awards.' He chuckled at the comment. But she was right. The next thing that she brought up was not what he wanted to discuss at 4:00 AM in the morning. 'At least it currently gives us an excuse to give to the normal people as to why we're not perusing having a family.' Josh's chuckle turned to a sigh. He knew that eventually she wanted to be a mother. She wanted to continue her blood line. But her unique blood made her immortal, while he was still quite mortal. He'd seen her survive a head first plunge from hundreds of feet in the air while battling the fallen Archangel that still wants her, Astaroth. He'd seen her also kill another angel named Baraqiel whom she now apparently had control of his domain as the victor from that fight. Although it was now, to them at least, a proven fact that Angels and humans could mate, and had not only in the biblical times detailed in Genesis and the books of Jubilees and Enoch, but even more recent, like when Astaroth had made the man they knew only as Jaymes in the late 1800's. 'Well you can't blame me for trying? according to Kokabel and the others, you're the key in that. You have to decide you're ready to get pregnant. You've got a condition that overrides my attempts.' His attempted jokes did not comfort her. 'Josh, I need focus. I need to get things right in my mind before we head to Madrid. We're near the Academy, aren't we?' She was asking about the Four Winds Academy that was about an hour out of town. It was the home of the Order's North American operations, and the place where G. Ian Childress III, the current head of the Order and VP of GCW held his court outside the norm of regular society. It was also the next training path in the four winds path of fighting training. Both Josh and Dani were graduates of the West Wind Academy in Chicago. As they had gone through one of the four individual 'wind' academies, they could go to and train at the Four Winds Academy at any time, and when they finished, they would become graduates of that school also. They knew of only one graduate of this program, and that was their captain, Blaise McClancy. Josh guessed that it was more then her want for training that was there. She needed to find out more, and it would be an excuse to get access to the library, and maybe speak to Ian Childress again. Her dreams had subsided since she made the choker necklace from the bones of the angel's skeleton the Order had recovered, but when it came down to it, there were still too many things that were unanswered for her. 'Yeah, it's about an hour out of town. If we head there, maybe we can get them to let us use one of Ian's planes to get to Spain and back, instead of commercial.' She looked to be thinking it over for a few moments. 'Yes, can we go there? I need to see some things, and it would be the best place to get some real practice in to build up my confidence. I doubt there will be really good facilities in Madrid.' Josh looked to be leaning back onto the bed again. 'Okay, we'll go after we sleep a bit. Come to bed.' She shook her head. 'Nope, it's 4 AM in the morning. Lets go now. I'll not be getting any sleep tonight. Maybe after a bit of soul searching I'll be able to get some, but not now.' Josh started to getup, mouthing his protest in his sighs and grunts. Dani slid a pair of sweat pants over her legs while he got fully dressed. ~A bit over an hour later~ The two had rushed to get out and everything before the Dallas traffic picked up. The sun was now coming up and Dani could only see desert in every direction as they headed down the less then well traveled roadway that led to what was looking like a large building in the distance. She'd only heard of it before. It was a complete copy of a place she'd been in before, in Spain. The original is the home of the entire Order of the Hammer operations. It was originally one of the temples that once served the Knights Templar when they had so much control in France, Spain and the rest of Europe. Now, it housed things that you just didn't speak about in the real world. And this copy was the same. Although one thing stood out as they drove up. 'What the fuck is that?' Dani could see the statue in the front cul-de-sac in front of them. It wasn't small in the slightest. It was of two people. One a woman, and she was barely clothed. The other was a man who appeared to be mounting her? and that is meant to mean the sexual manner. He was mounting her from behind, as they embraced. The thing that really set this apart though was the large angels wings that came from his shoulder blades. 'Holy shit?. is that how your family line started?' She punched him in the shoulder for that comment, no matter how true it could have been. As they drove up and parked the car, she couldn't take her eyes off the statue though. Something about it was off, and she couldn't quite place it. She got out of the car quickly, leaving Josh to worry about things and walked over to the statue, walking around to the front of it to see if there was any type of label upon it. Josh joined her about a minute later. 'So, I'll get punched again in the shoulder, but I have to ask. When we going to join the mile high club under Dani's wing power?' She snapped him a look that told him to drop the subject. She wasn't to up on flying in an airplane, let alone using her own wings for anything like that. And his thoughts of carrying out a sex act while she was carrying them both aloft was not actually her idea of fun at this time. She finally came around and found a name plate for the statue. She ran her fingers over it as she spoke it out loud. 'The Second Fall?' The statue was about the second fall of the angels, two hundred of them to be exact. The Grigori, or watchers who had fallen out of lust for human women. The tale that has been broken down to only a few verses in Genesis 6 still in the bible. When the 'Sons of God' fell out of lust for the 'Daughters of Man.' She reached up and touched her hand to the foot of the statue, the right foot of the male angel. It's touch was that of cold stone, but it was almost electrifying. Wait, it was electrifying. She saw the arc come from her fingers. Like it did back in Spain when she had fought the head of the Order's Hunters to gain access to the angel's skeleton, of which the final remains she wore as a native style choker now. She'd destroyed Baraqiel, the 'Lightning of God' and had taken some of his abilities. And now blue electrical arcs came from her hand to the stone of the statue. But why? She'd not wanted to. 'Ah? The earth born angel has come' She heard the voice, but couldn't place it. 'Did you hear that Josh?' 'Hear what?' She looked up at the statue, craning her head to try to look the male figure in the eyes. 'Was that you?' A simple word came back. 'Yes.' Josh's eyes suddenly showed signs of recognition. He was an empath, and he could sense when other entities were nearby. He looked up as she did. He could not hear it, but he knew it was there. The entity continued on. 'Finally someone else who can hear me. I can only speak to the Keeper inside because of my brother that he houses in his physical form. But you, you can hear me? there are many things I could tell you.' She had only one question to ask. 'Who are you?' 'I am the one that was blamed for it all. I'm the one who bore the brunt of the violation my brothers, including your great grandfather had done. I'm the one who was bound for eternity in the sand stone of the Arabic desert for eternity. The one that Ishram himself found, and dug the stone out to have this statue made. I am the Scapegoat for the crime. I am Samyaza.' She could only nod to it. She'd heard the story, but did not know fully. But this was interesting to say the least. Something else had changed, and that meant that things were happening again. Dani just needed to figure out what. ~To Be Continued~
