Saints: I Don't Think So

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Newfoundland removes his’ black Under Armour sunglasses off his’ face as he looks up at a huge golden sign hanging off the front of a large, red brick building. The golden sign reads, “CITY OF ATLANTA POLICE”. Newfoundland sighs as he places his sunglasses into one of the pockets inside his black, leather jacket as he begins to climb the granite stairs toward the large front doors leading into the Police department. KJ’s meeting with Rox never happen because he was force by Kodiak to go somewhere else and not talk to her thanks to his feelings about her. As Kodiak began to push him down the street away from Rox, KJ hands her a card with his number and told her to call him later. KJ received a call from her later the day before his main event match at World Wide 144. He tells her that he would meet her down in Atlanta, Georgia alone without Kodiak couple days after the show. Kodiak was not happy to learn that he was going to New Jersey with all of their stuff and Newfoundland was heading back to his’ hometown. Before KJ left to get onto his airplane bound to Atlanta, he specifically told Kodiak do not take The Requite out of the box or he will be in a great deal of trouble when KJ gets back from his trip down south. Kodiak may be a kleptomaniac, but when he was told not to touch something in the fear of getting hurt. He will avoid the item if it was his ex-girlfriend. KJ chuckles slightly to himself as he opens one of the large doors. Kodiak would probably place a sign on the box the was holding The Requite that warns all those who touch this box that they would be risking their life if this doesn’t reach the next airport. Newfoundland takes couple of steps into the Police Station before freezing in the middle of the lobby. A reason why he didn’t bring The Requite with him was that he didn’t want any of the police officers trying to take it by force. Another reason is that The Requite would ask for all of the souls of the corrupted in the waiting cells. The Requite would inform KJ of anyone that had darkness in their souls that need to be snuffed out. If someone asks KJ to explain how The Requite can tell him, he would tell them that it was a feeling he gets and cannot explain it to them. But now without The Requite, he still could feel the feelings of hatred and pain that fills the station. The older woman in her mid-forties at the front desk asks, “Sir, do you need to report something?” “I am here to have a meeting with Rox,” KJ says with a faint smile as he walks toward the front desk trying his best to avoid making eye contact with the people in the lobby, “I am really hoping she is here and not out on a call. If she is not here then I have to reschedule another date and that would cause me to go prematurely grey.” “Young men like you wouldn’t have to worry about getting grey hairs for years come. I would page her and if you wouldn’t mind would you wait in the lobby for her? By the way my name is Joan.” Joan says as she press some buttons on a hidden computer under the smooth black counter. “I wouldn’t mind at all, Miss Joan” KJ says smoothly causing her to giggle as he turns to wait for Rox. KJ sits down in a chair away from anyone else in lobby or at least the best he can in the half filled lobby. Not even a minute later, a muscular African American walks about to KJ and says with a deep voice, “You may have been ask this a lot, but are you, Mr. Newfoundland? You know that guy who works for GCW the wrestling company?” KJ tilts his’ head to the right as he looks at the man, normally when an African American talks to him. That person did something evil and is about to be reborn by The Requite. But, this guy is pure or at least not guilty like most. “Are you okay?” The man asks as he slowly takes a step backwards “Oh sorry, I zoned out there for a moment. I just got off an airplane not even an hour ago and I am trying my best to stay awake.” KJ says as he sticks out his right hand to the man, “I am KJ Newfoundland and you are?” “My name is Tri,” Tri says grabbing KJ’s hand and giving him a firm handshake, “I have watch you throughout the Indies until you finally got a chance in the major league were the truly talented wrestlers are at. You have improved a great deal since you first start three years ago.” Newfoundland shrugs his shoulders, “I have a great deal more to improve before I am happy. But, I will never be happy with being good wrestling.” KJ continued with a slight growl in his voice, “I want to be one of greats to ever step into square circle. I want to be legend with my own line of energy drinks.” Tri simply smiles and says as he pats KJ on the right shoulder, “If you work hard enough at anything you want you would get. I have worked hard in my life to get to where I am at now.” Tri says sadly as he looks down at the floor, “But, now I am jumping through hoops trying to file a missing person report on my wife of ten years.” KJ asks as Tri sits down next to him, “Wait, they’re not letting you? How long has she been missing for?” “Couple months now” Tri says as he tightens and untightens his knuckles on his pants, “They keep giving me the same answer that she may just left you and you haven’t been ready to move on. One day, a lady told me that I was not the only person who lost a love one in these few months. She said before she left that a recent increase of missing people reports have been a worry for the police. They think all of the missing people may have something in common.” “Is Mr. Maddox talking your ear off too?” Rox says standing in front of the two with her hands on her hips. She continues as she pulls KJ up and leads him toward the back, “Mr. Newfoundland don’t worry about him, we will be taking care of his problem as soon as possible. We currently have too many other problems in this city to be worrying about single missing person.” KJ tears his arm out of Rox’s grip as he ask, “What is your wife’s name, Tri?” Tri looks up with glassy cover eyes, “Her name is Hope. Her name is Hope, KJ!” Newfoundland smiles to himself as he thought as he steps into the lioness’ den, “Hope. Everyone needs little hope in their lives.” [Couple Minutes Later] KJ and Rox are sitting across from each other at a simple wooden table with a tape recorder lying in the middle. Rox says as she lights up cigarette, “Mr. Newfoundland, the reason why I ask you for a meeting is that I want to make sure that we have the current information about the death of your Father. For the record, tell me how you think your Father died.” KJ clears his throat, “I was eight years old when my dad passed away, Miss Rox. So, what I was told by the detective that was on the lead of the case told me that he was killed by hunting accident when he was hunting for gators out behind our house. How old are you, Miss Rox? Twenty?” Rox puffs out small smoke circles out of her mouth before she answered, “I am twenty-two years old and-“ “So, that would make you age of three when he died?” KJ said cutting her off, “Why would I expect someone younger than me to figure out a break in a case that was closed and sealed away so many years ago. What are you gunning for, Miss Rox? Are you going say that my dad was killed and I should be looking for killer to bring to justice?” Rox chokes on the next wave of smoke circles as KJ began his rant on her. She says coughing up a lung, “Woo, calm down there, Mr. Newfoundland. Closed? Your father’s case was never closed. It was placed on the unsolved section in archives.” KJ stares at her with a confuse look as she said that his father case wasn’t actually solved. “Wait, you didn’t know?” Rox asks as she leans onto the table with her elbows KJ looks down at his hands lying on his lap as he spoke, “I was taken to my grandparents before I could actually here the final verdict with my own ears. They just sent my grandparents couple pages explaining what they found.” Rox says as she leans back into her chair and says off hand, “I think your dad was murdered. But, the question is who and why they did it?” KJ says as he lifts his head up, “Did they ever locate my step-mother? She disappeared the same day my father was found with the large hole in his chest by a single buck shot round from a shotgun. The funny thing is that they never found his gun he was using for hunting.” She stares at KJ, “I was going to ask if you saw your step-mother since the shooting. The police didn’t find her or any signs that says she is even living anymore.” Rox slides down in her chair, “They were right and said this case was not solvable. You’re free to go, Mr. Newfoundland. I have no more questions for you.” KJ stands up out the chair and heads toward the door. He says as he opens the door, “Rox, you need to look for someone that has experience military because my dad told me couple weeks before his death. That some guy tried to hire him to help do a job and need a guide for the swamps around my house.” “Why didn’t you tell everyone this before?” she asks “Because I was a kid and I was scared.” KJ lets out a deep sigh as he walks through the doorway and closes the door behind him. [Outside the police station] KJ is sitting on the back of a large wooden bench as he watches people walking past him only about their own worries. “Okay, I may be forced to be tagging with Mr. Palmer against a small group from this fraction that calls themselves The Saints. But, it doesn’t mean we’re going be best of buddies after this match. I will do my best to make sure that we win this match against The Saints.” Newfoundland continued with a slight growl, “To get my first win here in GCW.” “Anyways, I am not one for using a large group of men to beat down a single person because that would ruin hunt. The hunt of bring down a large predator down by your lonesome and to tell the world that you’re the new predator on this food chain. Saints, your name doesn’t reflect your actions you’re probable going be doing. Saints are people who have a good soul and won’t do anything evil. So, I think I have a better name for you….” KJ says with a snarl “Darkfriends” Newfoundland face softens as he asks, “What do you think, kid?” A kid is staring at him with eyes filled with terror, “Where is mom?” KJ deeply sighs as he hops down from the bench and takes the kid by the hand, “Let’s go find her before something bad happens to you.”
