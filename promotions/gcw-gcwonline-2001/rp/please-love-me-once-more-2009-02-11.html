'Please Love Me, Once More'

11 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/09 I’ve realized these thoughts may be in vain, but the burden has become too much to bear. Through the many years of my life, I’ve seen horrible atrocities that the human mind cannot even begin to comprehend. I’ve seen those I’ve loved slain before my eyes, and my own heart slain at the deceiving hands of trickery, betrayal, and lust. Time is supposed to heal all wounds, and until the deepest and worst wound I’ve experienced, it ultimately has. Countless sleepless nights have been spent on my knees before the stars and the moon, begging for some form of solace and mercy. Unlike previous emotional wounds laid upon me, or scars left by those departing their earthly vessel, I find myself in a situation where nothing is improving. Months go by and the calendar moves on, but yet my heart and soul cannot. Your actions have mortally wounded me, and the icy cold response of negligence and apathy only adds salt to the proverbial wound. What does it say about who I am when the death of my daughter doesn’t haunt me nearly as bad as what you did to me? Why is it that losing the one person who loved me unconditionally has been dealt with in time and mourning, but somehow there’s something different about losing your wife? By no means am I claiming to be perfect, but I have yet to understand what happened, why it happened, and why there cannot seem to be a way to rectify it. Marriage is an eternal commitment that we made to each other, and it took you less than two years to break it. Yet for some reason, despite your vow being shattered in the lustful sin of adultery, I’m still as compelled to uphold my end of the bargain as I was the day I made it? A part of me has wanted to hate you for what happened between us, but that part of me will never permanently reach the light of day. I cannot hate you, I can’t even truly be mad at you. I love you to the end of this earth and beyond, no matter what was in your heart when it happened and to this day. There are days I spend around you that I want to hold you in my arms, to kiss you, to love you, and it kills me to know that I can’t. Even worse is the fact that there’s a slim to none chance that it ever will again, and it often has me contemplating the purpose of surviving. It’s irrational and completely ludicrous, but I can’t deny that it’s been crossing my mind with some frequency. There are times that I hold my sword in my hand, the sharp edge taunting me with the easy way out of this misery. For my entire existence on this cursed planet, I’ve been told to carry through the dark times, for the eventual horizon of happiness would one day make the journey worth it. Yet every time a wave of optimism reaches my sandy shores, it is tainted with the blood of those I love, or merely that of the dripping from my own wounded heart. The blade could make it all go away, and sometimes the temptation becomes too much. I’ve gone so far as holding the sharp pointed end to my temple, dragging it gently across the sensitive skin from whence my feelings can be envisioned by those who touch it, and wonder what there is left in this world for which to wait? My kin transcended, my love is dead, and I’m the last of my kind. The only comfort left in this world is of the warfare, the competition that is the wrestling organization that seems to be my safety net of sanity. Where once I drew great strength and pride from civilized warfare, I now merely find myself escaping from the horrific reality that is my own mind. Nothing any human could do to me inside of a wrestling ring can even hold a candle next to the nightmares that decorate the cinematic masterpiece that are my eyelids each and every time my soul tries to rest. The true testament to your actions lies not in the pursuing of them, but in the lack of emotion attached to the effects of them. How is it you can so calmly sit next to me as my heart destructs each time I see you and not even bat an eyelid? How is it that my eyes can cry rivers of untreated pain spanning months, and the expression on your face doesn’t even change a twist? Why is it that I can pour my soul through words and actions, but a response is not only not given, it’s not even attempted? Yet somehow, what I want more than anything else in the world is to return to it, to give you another opportunity to destroy me. I love you that much, and I’m not sure if that makes me a strong, courageous warrior in the battle that is love, or a hopelessly oblivious fool setting himself up for yet another miserable failure. Either way, I no longer understand the path on which I walk, and how this is supposed to lead to some story of righteousness, where this pain no longer condenses my soul with the thick fog that has become my own survival. I’ve been to lands where the dead still walk the earth, continually repeating the cycles that were their own departures from their world. Oblivious to the ever-lurking danger that devours them each and every night, they fall right into the same path where the train hits them, where the ex-lover shot them, or where they made the choice to leave this world on their own. There was a time where I couldn’t imagine where someone would have to be in their mind to want to end their existence over the loss of a love, but the longer this festering of misery continues in my heart, the more rational it seems. Humans spend most of their lives attempting to achieve immortality with their perceived creator, and the only path to that horizon is through their deaths on this earth. While on this earth, they’re taught that walking this planet among the droves of their own species, one of them is destined to ultimately fall in love with them, and for the remainder of their short stay in the Hotel Suite de Earth, they will be accompanied by someone whose love knows no bounds, who will reproduce with them, raise their children, and ultimately love each other until the relief of death finds them, and they ascend to their ultimate destination. When you are given these kinds of expectations, is it no wonder that when the lifelong companion that is supposed to be yours fornicates with another individual and leaves you clutching at the shattered pieces of your own broken heart that the rest of your existence doesn’t seem worth living? How is this perceived as irrational behavior? For hundreds of years, I’ve seen the blood spill, I’ve felt the sting of betrayal, and I’ve seen lifeless eyes staring back at me, but the one thing I know about each drop of blood left to glimmer on a blade of grass, of each eye no longer attached to the soul I once knew, is that they found their eternal peace somehow. Wherever their spirit has transcended, ultimately they have found happiness and eternity. The Elves are given what humans must experience death in order to find; immortality, and yet we are cursed with the innate vulnerability of emotion that wounds us as a bullet would wound human skin. An interesting paradox arrives of these situations; humans experiencing pain and eventually death in order to find their immortality. On the other hand, the Elves are supposedly blessed with immortality, but the perceived wounds of emotion festers in their spirit as a physical wound would to a human, to the point where continuing to exist with what humans would consider their ultimate destination seems less rational than making the choice to not exist anymore. Clearly, the motives behind the Earth’s creations had a sense of humor much like the creators of the humans, and sometimes it seems like we’re all pawns in the chess game that is life, and something beyond our capability of understanding is getting a huge kick out of this. What is our own personal Armageddon of the soul is merely the climactic scene before the credits to the supreme rulers of this tiny universe that exists on this stupid little planet. Do you realize that you have solely brought me to this point? Are you even out there to hear, or are your ears, not to mention the rest of your body, occupied in the thoughts and actions of another? Through blissful ignorance, are you participating in the sacred acts that are supposed to be reserved for those with the bond that we conceived some time ago in the bonds of matrimony? Likely, even if you are, those words don’t have the impact upon you that they do to me. Something inside your head gives you the ability to discard the emotions and souls of others in order to achieve your own personal objectives, no matter what the ultimate consequences are to anyone around you. The irony of this is the faith that you follow teaches that you will be forgiven for your sins and brought before your heavenly creator, and cast into the light that will bring you the immortality that has been taught through books for centuries, one way or another. Those sentiments still hold some solace for you, yet the penned chapters on adultery, bearing false witness, and betrayal seem to slip your mind in order for your own personal preferences and whims to be fulfilled. Through your choices and decisions, my soul is dying. The karma from your actions is what is becoming the destruction of my very own existence and well-being. I defy you to find someone who can understand this logic, because it is clearly escaping the realm of my comprehension. When the gods created their representations in humans, they endowed them with the gift of free will. Through this deity bestowed ability, such acts of betrayal and deception are allowed to freely take place, often without consequence, retribution, or justice. The victims are left to suffer with the mortal wounds of the ignorant and deceitful while the perpetrators continue on their journey of self-fulfilling prophecy and retain their happiness. These actions caused the living Earth so much pain that she shed the tears that became the Elven beings, brought to existence by Mother Earth herself in order to protect humans from each other and begin the necessary healing processes so the Earth would not ultimately die from all the suffering. The Elves were given life free of sickness, the effects of Father Time, strength, intelligence, and the abilities to feel and envision others? emotions and fears. The drawback of such immortality is the vulnerability. The empathetic beings are susceptible to emotional wounds and scars, and have provided some of my ancestors with their final transcendence to the plane of existence I have discovered, and will one day be confined. With my knees gently pressing in the blades of grass, lightly damp with the condensation of the clouds above, a wistful wind gently blows my blue strands of hair into my field of vision. Alas, the soothing illumination from the moon has been disrupted temporarily by the passing of thick passages of visible moisture. The time reflects how long my thoughts have been confining me to this clearing in the woods, as many hours have passed and events have likely occurred. Humans suffer many of these same inquiries and self-perceived tragedies every day, yet one that took place in another realm some time ago still haunts me to this very second. Five months have passed since I discovered her misdeed, and despite everything in my heart, all the pain, sorrow, and longing for the cold passage of life itself at times, my sole desire is to return to the arms of her grasp, to know her loving gaze once more. In a letter to my future self, I’ll be wondering from this time what will transpire of such a deep emotion with such staying power. When I unfold the pieces of paper penned by the feelings circumventing through the annals of my mind in the future, will I find myself in a similar conundrum, still pondering the pure desire to return to the only solace from reality I’ve ever known? or will I even survive long enough in order to discover said written time capsule? While my thoughts had been spinning around in my imagination, my hands had subconsciously written them, and I carefully placed them in the appropriate container with a timed release date. It was somewhat depressing that the initiative to survive was to merely meet the date where the letter would be unlocked, and I’d be able to read and reflect upon what I had written this night in order to compare to the clusterfuck that had become my existence. For a being promised eventual eternal happiness, the slope sure did seem to be getting deeper. Could the curse of immortality be to spend one’s whole life following a supposed treasure of peaceful bliss, but never truly attain such seemingly simple measures? It seemed that only the slow measuring stick of time would provide me with possible answers to such an inquiry, but I wasn’t placing my wages on the odds of that coming true, seeing as how the passing moons had yet to provide me with any true remedy to this seemingly eternal quagmire that was the Elven life. ? "Your thoughts on me have changed, and I can't change that. I will never lose how I thought of you, how I imagined you, and the feeling I had driving with you or the look on your face when you were ringside. "Tong Hua" translated means "Fairy Tale", and perhaps that's exactly what that was, and if this is the last time I speak to you; it's how I'll always remember you. Regardless if you ever tell me what I did to hurt you, I won't hold it against you, and hope that shall you pass, all will be forgiven. I'll just try to remember the Fairy Tale, and the good things that changed who I am, and led me to pursue something I'd been avoiding my whole life. I've gone after a dream, and I've succeeded in doing so. And I owe part of that to you" ? Several hours earlier As the days counted down to another debut in a professional organization that neither particularly knew or cared of my existence for the time being, I spent quite a bit of time trying to make sense of the position in which I’d been placed. Within the context of the nearly parallel planes of this universe, somehow through a ripple in the crack of time, I’d been placed in this world. The last ten years in which I’d spent wrestling and raising a daughter, many questions had been answered, but yet led to many more questions that made this life even more confusing than plain oblivion would’ve been. It hadn’t crossed my mind that this seeming coincidence in the ways of the universe may hold the key to unlock my eternal struggle for a small measure of peace. Fortunately, arriving again soon would be an acceptable distraction in the form of competition. My eyes closely followed Devon Drake’s eager, impatient pacing. Surely she’d been left without a hobby since the company of Evolution Pro Wrestling had departed our employment. With the emergence of Global Championship Wrestling on our radar, I was once again returning to a world that I thought I would no longer need in wake of the reckoning. However, in the other reality, seeming holograms could not help this ineptitude condemning my soul to eternal questioning and suffering. "Would you just get on with your meditation already?" Devon finally snapped and screamed. "You’re driving me batshit insane here." "Wouldn’t that imply that you were indeed sane at one point in time?" I sarcastically inquired. Devon turned her head and shot me her typical look of death. "Fair enough point, but I should still kick your ass for saying that. Would you stop keeping me in suspense already, what’s our first objective here?!" "Fellow native Brit, it seems," I responded. "Lantlas, you spent all of your infancy in Britain and haven’t returned since," Devon groaned. "How the hell is that going to assist you in this contest in any way?" "It likely isn’t, was just making a point of noticing." "Is there anything relevant you might have to add to this dissection?" Devon rolled her eyes and muttered. "Seems like me, he knows what it’s like to lose his parents," I noted. Devon seemed particularly intrigued by the latest retort, and brought her hand across her chin in a thoughtful manner. "The guy’s name is LeStrange, but he’s from Britain. That is indeed “the Strange”, but I digress? So Mommy and Daddy no longer carry on the family name and he’s off on his own trying to prove a name for himself?" "I don’t know, Devon. We just got here. And you certainly have a non-chalant way of viewing one losing their birthgivers, don’t you?" "I call it like I see it, Elf-boy," she cracked. "You of all people should know that by now." "I suppose I’m just a bit distracted," I admitted. "Lantlas," Devon menacingly replied in a warning tone, "if your mind is going back there, so help me I’ll throw you back there and never let you return." "It’s hard not to think about it, even now Devon." "I realize this, and I’ve known you long enough to know the way your mind? and heart, for that matter, works. It’s a relative pain in the ass at times like this, but fortunately I give you what I can’t give many others, and that is patience. You’re lucky, because with anyone else, I’d have excommunicated you years ago," she laughed. "Glad I’m held in such high regard," I laughed in return. "You damn well better be grateful, Elfy. I put up with a lot of shit at your expense. "I know I don’t need to repeat any of the story, for you’ve heard it all before," I explained. "Lantlas?" she began, "I know this isn’t easy to hear, but the time will come where you’ll have to accept what happened and attempt to move on. The slim to none chance that exists that things will go the way you want them to isn’t something on which you can base the rest of your life. She’s likely already moved on and not even told you, and you have to understand that. I know how much it pains you, but deceiving yourself from reality isn’t going to aid in the healing process. You need to come to the point where you can accept something else, and continue to live." "Devon," I responded, "I will not give anyone a silver medal. It would blaspheme the very emotion that is love, and I would spend the entire time trying to convince myself that I could feel the way I do about her about someone else, and it’s just not going to happen. I’d rather be alone and honest than cause someone else the unnecessary pain that she’s caused me. Just because she hasn’t stayed true to her vow doesn’t mean I’m not going to, and I accept that." "That’s? incredibly depressing, Lantlas." "I can’t apologize for how I feel, but don’t misunderstand my situation as giving up. You’ve said that I need to accept things, and that is my acceptance. It’s either going to be her or eternal solitude, I accept that. There’s nothing in this world that can change how I feel, and either the storybook ending will occur, or I’ll be given some purpose to journey alone for the rest of my life. Sometimes the stories don’t end the way we want them to, and I’m all but prepared for that inevitability, because the only difference between knowing that and the way it is now is that I’ll know for sure, and truthfully, what difference is that going to make in perpetual loneliness? None." "Except the knowledge that the loneliness will be eternal with that decision," Devon countered. "If that’s the result of the choice that I’ve made, I at least take comfort in the fact that I withstood the temptations that could’ve been, and remained loyal to the vow I made. If nothing else, I’ll always have that integrity." "If that incredibly depressing mindset is the one you choose to take, I suppose there isn’t anything I can do to change it," Devon shrugged. "All I really have left is that hope and foolish optimism, Devon. It’s what keeps me going, it’s the one thing that’s helping me survive. I hope, if nothing else, you can understand that," I continued. "Maybe someday, but for now, I’ll never be able to comprehend why you would choose solitude over the proverbial Cupid arrow striking twice again," Devon responded. "Cupid has struck, and for better or worse, I’m stuck with it. So shall the rest of my life tell the story of the effect of the arrow, not journey toward the chance for a new one. That’s not why I got married, and I’ll be damned if I let that change." "I think you’re a fool," Devon admitted. "You may be right," I retorted, "but a hopeful one I shall remain." "For how long?" she asked. Looking through the hallway window at the darkening horizon, it was clear some comfort in the seclusion that was my forest was needed. As my gaze interrupted the flow of the conversation, the tapping foot of the heel of Drakey’s left show temporarily brought me back to reality. "Until I know," I answered. "And when that day comes, I’ll either know happiness or be void of it. Either way, so be it." Turning away from a likely bitterly confused friend of mine, the comfort of the trees was calling my name. The fading sunlight provided the inflection of the moon’s light to soothe the growing wound inside my spirit, at least enough that I could keep going? for a little while. Could the rest of my life really be a series of “for a little while” comforts? In the hands of time, the true answer will eventually emerge. "Please love me, once more?" ? "In every match I ever wrestle, in every step I ever take, and any time I look into my daughter's eyes, I will see you. When I return to the ring, you will be on my mind, watching there from ringside like you always used to, your eyes lighting up when I'd emerge victorious... You'd be there next to my ecstatic daughter, jumping up and down, and being there for me to wrap my arms around you when it was all said and done. You will never disappear from my thoughts, and you will always be there with me. I love you." Forever With You, -Lantlas Evenstar Musada Durin Diee Anduril
