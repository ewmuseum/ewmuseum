It Takes Time

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now [Scene fades up on Robert Larabee's office. Sitting in a chair in front of a desk is a man with long black hair, wearing a suit and a black mask. This man is known as the Enygmatic One. He is looking at a world of hurt. We call him X. Sitting across the desk from X is Robert Larabee, stroking his chin, thinking.] Larabee: X, I know what you're thinking. I know that the Storyteller told you that you are going to be this major star here in the United States. Well, you are going to find out what it really means to be the top dog here with me representing you. Letting me talk for your actions. You make the money, I process it. Simple as that. X:..... Larabee: You don't need to talk. That's what I'm here for. The Storyteller trusts me to keep your best interests in mind. That is why I accepted this Street Fight on your behalf. I know that you can dispose of Bryan Mayhem easily. I know that after the match is over, that you are going to be victorious and then you are going to tackle the Cruiserweight Division. X:..... Larabee: Yeah, I know what you're thinking, how can I possibly think that you're ready for this. Remember when you hit the Power 9 on both members of the Guild? That just shows that you are ready. You saw an opportunity to pull off something amazing and you did it. That's what cruiserweight action is all about. That's also why you are going to go out there and take care of business against Mayhem. That means that you are going to leave him a bloody mess. You were hired to do a job and that's all there is to it. You're going to have to trust me on this. The Storyteller wants this to happen. I know that you look up to the Storyteller like a father, and I think that you are feeling betrayal by his leaving me to guide you, but he knows that this is for the best right now. When you're ready for his guidance, you know that he's going to return. Then you might not have need of me. X:..... Larabee: Don't look at me like that. I know what I'm talking about. I've known men like you my entire life. One of these days, you're going to trust me like the Storyteller has. That's when you are going to take off and be one of the top stars here. That's when you are going to be bigger than LeStatt Knight. That's when you are going to be bigger than Michael Stevens. Bigger than Trey Trememdous, Rich Rollins, Eva Van Lorne. That's when you are going to be the biggest, brightest, most marketable star here in GCW. First step is going to be beating Mayhem. Then you are going to go on and take over the Cruiserweight Division. Then, the sky will be the limit. [Scene fades to black.]
