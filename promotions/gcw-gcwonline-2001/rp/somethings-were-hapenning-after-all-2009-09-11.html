Somethings were hapenning after all.

11 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/11/2009 Somewhere in Northern Ohio, The two large members of Mortifera, Jorge Samuelsson and Shaman are sitting in a small town bar. Lots of fans are cheering the victory over Navy. Shaman shakes his head as he watches them. Samuelsson just simply drinks the beer he has from the pitcher. Shaman looks over at him shaking his head. Pointing at the pitcher he looks up at Samuelsson. “Not everyday you see someone drinking from a pitcher.” Samuelsson stops drinking and burps loudly looking at Shaman. “I thought this was a mug. Those cups you call glasses are pathetic.” He turns and points out the college football fans. “How can they celebrate when their team nearly blew it all against a supposed subpar team? I had heard this Ohio State was supposed to be the monsters of the Midwest. Looks to em this Michigan team is better.” Two fans look over at them and both come over and look at Samuelsson and Shaman glaring. “Did you just say Ohio State is bad and Michigan is better?” Samuelsson looks at both of them and responds “Did I stutter?” Shaman raises his hand between Samuelsson and the fans. He grins slightly. “Well they sure aren't the SEC or BIG 12, but I guess you can be proud of something.” One of the fans swing at Shaman who steps back a bit as he stands. He suddenly steps forward swinging the stool his was sitting on bringing it crashing down on one of the fans. “You see the only way to deal with a cockroach is crush it completely.” Suddenly a bouncer comes over and grabs Shaman. Shaman looks over his shoulder at the man grabbing him. Samuelsson grabs the bouncer and launches the bouncer over a couple of tables. “This is fun.” Shaman grins at Samuelssons response. He turns and grabs he other Ohio State fan. “You'd think LSU had won another national title or something. Damn man lets get outside to the truck. I want to tell you a story about something.” Shaman and Samuelsson walk around the truck as a man in a suit walks up to them. “Was all that necessary?” Shaman and Samuelsson look at one another and then look at the man. “Yes” they both respond simultaneously. The man steps back as Samuelsson opens the door to the truck. Shaman looks over at the man. “You know Jacob you do your lawyer mumbo jumbo and take care of the bar owners issues. You will be covered expense wise. Hell tell ya what take care of it and I may even include a bonus in it for you.” Jacob turns and mumbles as he pulls a cell phone out of his pocket. Shaman chuckles as he slips into the truck behind the drivers seat. Shaman reaches over and starts the truck as Jorge Samuelsson pulls out a CD and pushes it into the cd player. He looks at Shaman and grins. “ Nothing like a little Amon Amarth for mood.” Shaman rubs his temple looking at Samuelsson and simply shrugs as he turns and hits the gas driving out of the dirt parking lot. Shaman listens to the music as he drives down the highway. His mind remembering the past. He wants to remember this clearly as he looks at Samuelsson through the corner of his eye. "You remember when we first met?" Samuelsson nods as he looks over at Shaman listening. "You may have been the most powerful and dangerous opponent i had ever met. You set your sights on me and wanted to topple the man that was as dangerous as they came. 7 Extreme titles in the WWA, Heavyweight reign, Two TV title reigns and of course a US Title reign." Looking up momentarily at a highway sign he then looks back forward down the road as he continues. "You did defeat me soundly, but i came back better than ever. I owe this to my past from which i had lost before then. You were able to defeat me a man without purpose and direction at that time." Samuelsson looks over at Shaman and shakes his head. "I hope this isn't going to be a woe is me type of conversation i might have to puke and throw myself out of this truck and hope to die." Shaman looks at Samuelsson speaking in a serious tone. "Shut it while i am talking, We are a team, but disrespect doesn't happen between us ever. Got it?" Samuelsson tilts his head at Shaman's serious tone. "Alright alright i get it!" Samuelsson leans back in the passenger seat as Shaman continues. "June 1995, the month was hotter than usual and i was on tour in Japan in my early portions of my wrestling career. There was this one guy named Kajimiru Toru. He was one tough son of a bitch." Smiling a bit Shaman taps the steering wheel. "Anyways the guy was psycho and he went out of his way to taunt opponents and get them angry. Well i was young and fell into this trap. He defeated me soundly and left me bloodied in the ring. I took one lesson away from that. Never fail even if you lose. Failure is the worst type of defeat it means you have given up completely. Losing just means you can comeback and fight again." Shaman grins and looks at Samuelsson who seems to be contemplating this. "These last few weeks we have not done too well. Last show we lost and in truth be told neither one of us should have. In fact i should have beaten Surreal a few weeks prior. Oh and trust me he and i will speak on the matters at hand that happened during that match." Shaman slows down and pulls to the side of the road. He taps the navigation device and looks at it for a few minutes before pulling back on the road and continuing the drive. "Sorry about that kind of distracted with some inner thoughts." Shaman looks ahead as the begin to creep up on a semi truck. He cracks his neck from side to side. "You see Jorge this is a match of importance to us. Not some little time period where we will sit here and tell each other stories about what we will do. Nope we will guarantee what we can do. Team PROjection is our partner in this. This means we get to battle it out against the other two teams and move forward upon winning. Am i guaranteeing a win? Nope i am just confident in our ability to win." Samuelsson burps as he reaches down towards his feet pulling up a beer. "Well at least the beers cold. I am not one to work strategy too much. I just like to kick somebodies ass and the Warpath match allows this. You think the strategy and i will listen. We both have enough muscle between us to crush a heard of stampeding cattle." Shaman laughs at the remark as He looks over at Samuelsson. "Man you never quit surprising me with your remarks." Shaman narrows his gaze and groans slightly as he remembers something. "We'll stop here to continue the conversation." Shaman slows the truck down and pulls into a gas station. He opens the door and steps out and walks inside the gas station. He seems to ask the clerk something and the clerk points to a magazine rack. Shaman walks over and grabs a few magazines off the shelf. Samuelsson eyes Shaman curiously as he watches. "What the hell is he doing?" Shaman exits the gas station after paying for the magazines and he walks towards the passenger door. "Here Jorge come on out and have a look at these with me. I wanna point out a few things." Shaman opens one of the magazines and points out something. "You see Jorge Oblivion is still a ranked tag team even after Mayhem has departed. Comments nearby state that These two Behemoths Bryan Mayhem and Shaman could still return at any moment and wreak havoc upon the tag team scene again. People need to get it through their thick skulls Oblivion is gone." Shaman throws the magazine behind Samuelsson's head into the back seat. Shaman seems obviously agitated with this. He looks at Saemuelsson. "It just pisses me off that no matter how we do it they are still comparing Mortifera to Oblivion. We are different, we are a completely different type of beast. We are ourselves with no make up and no blood pools or hell even satanic rituals. Mayhem has been gone for a longtime now. I moved past it, but god damn it i will not sit here and continuously get reminded that Mayhem and i held the tag team titles together as Oblivion. You and i can do this without some sort of reputation from Oblivion." Samuelsson looks at Shaman and taps his fingers. "You know this whole thing you being obsessed with ridding yourself of the Oblivion image shouldn't be a problem. In fact you should use it to your advantage. For example..." Samuelsson snaps one of the magazines from Shaman's hands and begins flipping through it. He finds something and holds it up showing it to Shaman. " Mortifera is not being compared to Oblivion in that way. They are comparing the fact that we could be and are as dangerous as you were with Mayhem. In fact you are stated as being more dangerous than before. Now teamed with me you have become something not unlike a mythical danger to the tag team divisions. Look at them here compare us to tag teams of the past and present. In fact they have gone so far as compare you to tag teams you have been part of in the past. Impressive that you have been a tag champion several times." Samuelsson points at several of the pictures and then runs his finger down the page to the dates and times. "1996 Shaman teams with Jason Flintlock to win the SCCW Tag team titles. 1997 Shaman wins tag team titles with the Chinese Mafiaman." Samuelsson looks at Shaman. "The Chinese Mafiaman was he serious?" Shaman laughs a bit shaking his head. "Oh yeah he was real, a Chinese guy wanting to be Italian it was classic." Samuelsson pulls the magazine upwards as he turns and shows Shaman. "June 2002 The Horsemen pull the mask off after defeating Havok for the World Tag team titles and revealing that Pestilence and Famine are none other than Magician and Shaman. Then we come to this one, the one you should be proud of achieving and showing that you can move on and do it again with a different partner!" Samuelsson holds up the magazine Showing Oblivion (Shaman and Mayhem) holding the GCW Tag Team titles. "October 9th 2008, Oblivion wins Tag team Titles from Samson and Delylah. You had a respectable run as title holders. Your problem is that your angry about people referencing that or are you angry that Mayhem bailed again leaving you to clean up the mess?" Shaman looks at Samuelsson his eyes moving downwards looking at the magazine. "Time to end this shit Shaman. Move on use it to your own advantage. This should fuel you not make you weak and angry. Use your anger as the fire to ignite the fuel burning deep within you." Samuelsson closes the magazine as he opens the door stepping out standing next to Shaman. "So here i am standing right next to you. In this god awful state called Ohio. A state where its college super team keeps choking at National Titles. Manages to win a few here and there, but still chokes when all the chips are against them." Shaman raises his hand shutting Samuelsson up. "Alright you are right. I am harboring anger towards Bryan Mayhem who ran himself back to England. I was angry he stopped me from getting revenge for losing to Banned & Exiled. I am angry he ran like a scolded dog. No more brooding over the past. I am going to do this and do this the right way. You are my partner Jorge and we are going to go get ourselves some GCW Tag Team Titles. How does that sound to you?" Samuelsson smirks and looks about. "Suits me fine, but a reminder first. Don't forget Warpath we got two tag teams to beat and then we face our team partner at Dangerous Games." Samuelsson smirks and sits back in the truck on the passengers side. Shaman nods and walks around the truck and climbs in the drivers side starting up the truck. "How can i forget this event Jorge. We are going to remind people why Mortifera will live up to tis name." The truck spins gravel and dirt as it speeds out onto the highway driving away. Leaving the thought for everyone to consider. What is going to happen next with these two.
