Happiness

12 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 Dec 05 Happiness Now before I continue I will allow you to let that word soak in for a moment. The reason being is that it is a word that is thrown around all the time but how many people REALLY find it? Sure people may seem happy on the surface but does that mean that they are showing you every side of them? Of course not and I know this better than most. You see happiness is almost as hard to find as the Loch ness monster or Big Foot. Sometimes you are fooled by fakes and tricks but no one is REALLY sure if they exist or not. See the similarities? There was once in my life that I was ONE HUNDRED percent happy. It seems like a lifetime ago now and in a way it was. Not because so many years have passed since then but because of how different a person I am now. When I was living in Spain with my Tia and Tio things were perfect. My Uncle was rich and bought me anything my heart desired which surprisingly wasn’t TOO much. I always felt bad taking their money once I found out I wasn’t really their son. I was the model child getting straight A’s in school, never got into any altercations with other children, never spoke out, nothing. They loved me like I was their own and I never questioned it. They made me happy. Then I met Cindy and my life seemed to have a brand new meaning. When I would talk to her my voice would get caught up inside of me, my palms would sweat, and I couldn’t help but smile. I didn’t know what love was then but now I know even then at the age of 14 I loved that girl. The years went by and we got closer, dated, had sex together for the first time. Years later we even had a child, Starr Abitua. They made me happy. Then of course my new life began, a life full of secrets, mistrust, lies, violence. The story has been told numerous time and I really have no desire to tell it again. The short version is that I saw a lot of blood shed in a very short period of time. Most of that blood came flowing from the veins of those that actually did make me happy. In that time I cried so much that my tears eventually dried up, it was all I knew how to do. For a whole year I remained in exile in some run down apartment complex that now a days I wouldn’t even wish for a $3 whore to occupy. It was the only place I knew that no one would come try and find me. I thought a lot about that blood that was shed in that period of time. I thought all kinds of different scenarios in my. “What if I woulda been there?” or “Maybe if I would have just?” and everything else in between. I might as well have been blind in that year because all I could see was red. The red of that sea of blood. In that sea of blood I could see the bodies of Damen, Cindy, Starr, My Mother and Father, My Aunt and Uncle. Everyone that I ever lost was there reaching out for me to save them but my fear kept me from. In that river flowed my happiness. I stood alone for a long time until David “saved” me. He brought me back into this world of wrestling that I find myself in now. That brought something I never knew I could feel back inside of me but was I really happy? I thought I was but as I said it is easy to be fooled by happiness. Wylie fucking Bick was a mirage in my way of true happiness. I once asked her to marry me and thought that I was in love. At the first sign of trouble she went out and fucked my most hated enemy ? and now is fucking my best friend. It’s funny how things work out sometimes. Surprisingly I don’t mind that she is whoring around with David now because she would never have made me happy anyways. She was too plain, too normal ? too weak. Sure she was beautiful and she gave a nice blow job but hell you can find that anywhere. No if she makes David happy then I will allow them that, there is no point in me interrupting such things anymore. Since her there was never anyone who really made me even feel close to that again. Sure I had a fuck fest with Ashura and that was nice if I do say so myself. Her problem was she took herself far too seriously, that and I don’t think she could even come close to being happy. I figured she wanted to hear that ridiculous “dark prince” deal and so that’s what I gave her, yet she still fucked me, kicked me, and then left me. Women. Then don’t even get me started on Medea Hardy, that was just the mistake of a lifetime. Now I am all for a little bit of change here and there but this one was expecting me to turn into some Family Man-Zombie thing and that certainly wasn’t happening. I still wonder what the hell I was thinking by agreeing to go out to that damn Farm or Ranch or whatever the hell it is. Those people drove me nuts, it was like a bad episode of the Brady Bunch with a sprinkle of the Twilight Zone. I mean when Jeff Wylde is the most entertaining person around you KNOW you are in for a long weekend. For a long time I figured I had my one shot at happiness and that my time was done and over with. Usually it is these times in life when happiness will find you. That is when I had Mary walk back into my life. She was always the X-Factor if you will. In the beginning there was a strong sexual attraction but she was wary because I had just gotten out of that “thing” with her cousin Ashura and she had just been crushed by that son of a bitch Damien Black or Faust whatever. As the story goes I left for about a year, met Medea and then ran into Mary. It is actually quite similar to GCW. Met someone, Mary comes in to help and then Mary and myself end up together. I almost forced her away too and now I know what a mistake that would have been. Mary is that piece of me that I have been missing since Cindy had passed. That piece of me that I never thought that I would find again. She is my happiness. I have come to realize that most people only get one chance at TRUE happiness. Me on the other hand have now been given a second chance. I now get the chance to make right the wrongs that I have done in the past and this time I will not fail. I will not fail in love, in life, AND in my profession. For once my stars seem to have aligned. Mary will be my wife My Life is seemingly whole And I WILL become a legend in my profession None of these are guesses or hypotheses These are FACTS. I have had a lot of hiccups lately professionally but that is going to change here and now. Chris Kannon is first but he will not be the last. I will make a splash in the coming months until Steven Caldera is forced to give me another shot at MY World Title. Then Mary Abitua comes into my life forever. THEN my happiness will be complete. Happiness? Let that sink in for a moment.
