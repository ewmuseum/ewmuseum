Dusting Off

13 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13 Losing was never an easy process for Jason Warr. It usually would entail more of an effort in his training for the following week, more adrenaline. That was before his life fell apart. --- He sat behind the wheel of his used Mercedes, greasy mexican takeout in hand, surveying the scene that laid before him. Every night, for the past week he'd drive to her apartment and watch him wisk her off for a night on the town. Her now blonde hair fell past her shoulderblades where it stood straight. The face belonged to a woman he once loved, once thought he'd be with forever. However, those thoughts were crushed by an unyielding, unforgiving, bitch. And she stood before him, gallivanting around town with this new guy, some piece of trash. He had no reason to hate his guts, but he did, and he sought no further persuasion. She couldn't just leave, no. She had to rip off a piece of his soul first, and she had. He couldn't handle it anymore, driving to the same spot, every week, and watching. He didn't care about anything in the world but them, they raided his thoughts and controlled his dreams. Every waking moment the images of them would pop into his mind. He'd no showed two AWC bookings because of it. But money was no longer an issue. No. Tonight, it'd be vengeance. The difference between tonight and every other night he'd spent in this position were the vodka shots he'd done an hour previous to help him forget. Rather, it clouded his judgement to the point where he found himself manuevering the vehicle onto the darkened street, crooked smile creeping onto his face. Every week, Warr watched the new man pick her up, drop her off, and walk slowly across the street to his car. And as he did so, Warr threw reason to the wind, throwing the car into Drive and screaming down the road. --- The Player had a thing about him, women just couldn't resist. Charm, good looks, edge, whatever you want to call it, he possessed it. His new prospect was this blonde he'd picked up in a bar a couple weeks ago. He'd been about ready to seal the deal back at her place with some freak with blue hair barged in, the boyfriend it seems. Whatever, didn't matter really. The Player just climbed out a window and called her in the morning, and for the past week they'd been going out. After a reasonably good night he began to slowly cross the street, only to be shocked as headlights sprung on. By the time he turned to look at the driver it was too late, the black mercedes clipped him and sent him tumbling to the ground. Before The Player passed out he saw the Mercedes slammed into a parked car. He heard sirens, and then everything went black. --- We all make mistakes, sometimes. Apparently the NYPD saw it my way, and since the victim decided mysteriously not to press charges, I got a revoked license and sume bullshit fine. Do I feel bad? Not really. The only regret I have is that she hadn't been crossing the street with him when I stepped on the pedal. A dick and a Whore, two for the price of one. That was three months ago. After my impending release from AWC I dissapeared, but didn't retire. Moved to a shabbier apartment in Harlem and worked for an Underground wrestling league with no real name. Just a bunch of crazy guys duking it out in nightly tournaments, winner got paid, the other 15 went home with empty pockets. It was that sort of environment I needed to get my edge back, to pull myself out of the emotional trap I'd fallen into. I'd trusted another far too deeply, and it's a mistake I'll never make again. As my mind wanders toward Rampage, and my second competitive match since leaving the underground wrestling scene, I feel I need to win. I need to prove to myself that no matter how much they took, how much SHE took, she didn't kill me. If I win, I win. If I lose, I go back to my cheap hotel room and a full bottle of Jager. Somehow the latter seems less and less appealing every night.
