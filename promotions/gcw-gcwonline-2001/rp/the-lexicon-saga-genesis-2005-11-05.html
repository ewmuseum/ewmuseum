The Lexicon Saga: Genesis

5 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, Nov 5th Tales of the gifted ones are varied and plenty, their exploits, deeds of valour and crimes so heinous I prefer not to speak them. But always has a code of conduct been followed, set down by ancient fore fathers forgotten by time. A single law that is rarely needed to be enforced, a single law that sets the Gifted ones indeffinetly apart from their mundane brethren. A Gifted One shall never rule over mortal men. It has been debated by a hundred tongues in a hundred times in a hundred lands and always the conclusion is the same. The Gifted Ones, while smarter, stronger, faster, and longer lived, are driven by an otherworldly hunger. It's a drive they are powerless to resist, the lure to power too great. Belts, most commonly, fashioned of precious metals long ago lost to the smiths of modern man. These tokens of glory are sought and defended with savage jealously, and for that reason, the ancients denied them their voices of politics. Mortal man should never suffer in the endless feuds of the gifted. But always there is one who will defy authority, always there is one who will take matters into his own. THE LEXICON SAGA Created by Robert Kristian Genesis The land is lush, spring blooming in all directions, shouts of men who's blood runs hot fill the dreamy afternoon air, energizing the small grassy meadow. A group of peasants, one hand in the air and the other trading coins shout with glee as two more men step into the human ring. 'A glorious day Lexicon!' 'Exactly what makes this day any more glorious than the last? Or the one before?' Lexicon replies with a quick glance, intense blue eyes alight with mischeif. The larger man's face splits in a grin. Light glitters and sparkles across Lexicon's silver half breast plate, his left arm coated in armour while his sword arm holds only a bracer. A squire making quick work of detaching the long blue cloak that pours from his shoulders like liquid velvet. The other's armour though is not so expensive, straps of leather and metal bind at awkward sections, scratched and dented from previous battles. His bald head catches a ray, cleaner shaven than his face which still holds that malign cocked grin. In fact, the only thing not grungy and offensive of the muscle bound gladiator, is the gilded golden belt strapped firmly about his waist. 'Today is the day I get to crush the dreams a pansy noble.' His voice is rough and intimidating. Lexicon laughs audibly, lowering his head as the lad pulls shoulder length blond hair back and ties it tight with a blue ribbon. 'You've not the ability to understand my dreams, Lord Draven.' Lexicon's voice loses all amusement as he lifts his head and squares his shoulders. 'C'mon and let's get it done already, Lexy, you dragged me out here, at least come prepared.' Lexicon merely grins. The squire returns with a pair of hilts protruding from a cloth bundle. A shout catches his attention. Draven laughs as men armed with pikes and halberds invade the circle. 'What is this Draven?' 'This is my insurance, I can't trust the likes of you.' 'The likes me? You're one to speak half blood!' 'Your kind sickens me, so attracted to these damn belts you'd sell your own mother for the chance at one.' Lexicon bites down his reply, he grabs the hilt of a long sword in his left hand and rips it from the bundle, his feet digging into the earth and sending uprooted clumps high into the air. He charges with a quickness belying size and armament, the weapon glitters onto the offensive and bits into a wooden haft. From nowhere Draven catches the blade in the middle of a great axe, throws his weight forward and swings a savage chop that Lexicon barely manages to duck. The axe spins overhead and comes down again at blinding speed, Lex throws himself to the side and catches in a low stance before diving forward with a thrust. His momentum following back around Draven catches the assault and slaps it aside. There's an electric moment before Lex sweeps a pair of left underhand chops into the axe haft and slams a hard right handed fist into Draven's chin. The larger brute stumbles back a step , Lexicon's blade circles and flips into his unarmored right hand and he charges. The axe comes around and crashes against Lexicon's armoured shoulder, he stumbles to a knee and takes a boot to the chest that sends Lex sprawling. His legs curl over and he springs to his feet as that heavy axe buries itself in the ground where he'd been a moment earlier. 'Quicker that I gave you credit for.' Draven cracks. They clash again, Draven's axe against the shoulder guard, Lexicon's sword out of reach, better prepared this time Lex throws his weight forward and the axe goes wide. Crimson arcs into the air as Draven wheels and stumbles a few feet away. Lexicon presses his advantage but realizes his folly too late. The axe swings back around with a mighty roar and cleaves into the armored limb. Lex cries out and takes a knee, the blade stuck, Draven uses it to hold his challenger down. 'Don't you get it? I'm better than you, the Emperor chose me over you, he told me so himself. You'll never hold this belt.' Blue eyes flash with rage as Lexicon meets Dravens arrogant facade, a bead of sweat tracing down his forehead, the effort needed to hold the weight starting to stretch his features. Draven blasts a boot into his chest that sends Lex to the ground hard. Blood sprays as the axe comes free and Draven saunters over his fallen enemy and looks down with a grin. 'Don't be discouraged Lexicon, I'm actually bleeding, best challenge this kingdom's put up yet.' Lexicon only sneers as he forces himself to his hands and knees. Draven collects his weapon and turns his back. 'I will hold that belt!' Draven only laughs. Squires rush to their fallen fighter, who swats them away in frustration. 'Do you hear me half blood? I will hold that belt!' The scene fades indoors, a fire crackles lazily in a brass brazier, illuminating the unmistakable Lexicon pacing back and forth. His armor gone he stands in a brown leather tunic and grey cloak, his hood drawn back and his hair let loose to hang free, framing his broad jaw and thick neck. Seated at a table, hood drawn over his face carefully, the only other figure in the room speaks, his voice heavy and smooth. 'It is risky of you to even ask such things.' 'Our people are being championed by a foreign half blood.' 'His blood has nothing to do with it, you want that belt.' 'Just as badly as you do.' 'If I were to agree to this, if you actually see this through.' 'I am completely aware of the consequences my actions may bring, but I am even more aware of the growing need to replace the leadership of our people. The emperor is far too lax on who he allows to compete. A half blood... I've seen kingdoms where half gifted bastards weren't even considered of the talent.' 'You're behind the times, Lexicon, the Emperor has gone so far as to extend his protection to Draven's holdings.' 'So it's not just one law we're breaking.' 'You're breaking.' 'I cannot do this without you.' 'You'd better be able to, you cannot count on me as I cannot count on you. I'll not be your puppet.' 'I'd never presume-' 'You would. If I help you do this, what do you offer me in return.' 'Riches, land, women. Real power.' 'Now you're presuming to know what power is.' 'Don't patronize me.' Lexicon's baritone grows threatening as he turns on his guest. 'The truth is you have very little I want that you could offer me.' The hooded figure rises. 'You will have my answer in five days.' 'I attack on that day.' The hoods nods in acknowledgment. 'You trust me with a great deal of information and yet you know little about me. Curious. I heard rumor you were a talented strategist.' 'I am. I chose you.' The hood waits a moment before turning towards the door. 'Five days Lexicon.' The voice floats in the wake of his exit. A grin comes to Lexicon's face. The scene fades to a lavishely decorated bed chamber illuminated sparsely by covered candles. The bed surrounded by drapery parts as a half naked Draven emerges from the comforts within. He crosses the room slowely, pulling a house coat across his shoulders, pausing only to touch the diagonal scar across his chest. 'Draven, what are you about?' The feminine voice filters from the hidden heaven. 'I don't know.' His gruff voice responds thickly. 'I sense something.' 'What kind of something, sweetheart?' 'Something bad...' He pauses, looking back over his shoulder as the curtain around his bed parts and she emerges. An angel made for man, her dark hair spilling around powerful shoulder and sleek arms. 'You're only with me for my belt, Zodiac.' He snides. 'Of course.' She winks. Turning away Draven pulls at the handles of two large doors and they swing open with gusto, the broad balcony before him looks out over a sparkling city scape. He steps out into the light western wind, barefeet slapping the cold stone. He takes in a deep breath through his nose. 'Draven.' Her voice loses the amusement. 'Draven what is it.' 'I smell...' He frowns. 'I haven't smelled that in years...' 'You're scaring me.' He turns around and looks her full in the face. 'What!?' Zodiac steps up to him. 'It smells like war.' Zodiac frowns in confusion and smells the air. 'Who would we be at war with? It's impossible, none of our neighbors even have titles let alone the power to invade US!' Draven's silence is his only response as he looks long and hard into the darkness surrounding his valley. 'Maybe it's not an invasion...' Slowely he turns his eyes to meet Zodiac's and it's obvious they're thinking the same thing, when in the distance an alarm sounds, and then another, a frantic chain of events until the bells are at the doorsteps of Draven's mansion. Draven sniffs the air again. 'Fire.' Zodiac says the moment before they both turn to the room and march in opposite directions. There's a knock at the door. 'Champion!' A panicked voice calls. 'We know!' The gifted ones snap in unison, pulling on peices of armour in hast. 'We have an hour till dawn.' 'We won't last until dawn!' Draven snaps at his general. They stand in the immaculate; open entrance way. Draven with Zodiac by his side, her sparse plate mail covered vital regions and every where else hung silver chain mail fashioned so fine it looked like a gown in the right light. And one of the largest most imposing non-gifted individuals you're likely to see. 'Rally the militia, whoever it is we have to fortify the mansion, start the evacuation immedietly.' Zodiac snaps with cold precision. 'With all due respect, I am in charge around here.' The soldier states boldly. 'We're in this together!' Draven growls. 'A gifted shall not lead men to their deaths.' 'So that man can lead man to their deaths, this is the defense of your people!' 'They are MY people, you can grab a sword and get in the ranks or evacuate with the women and children.' There's a moment of tense silence, the human's carefully trimmed beard doesn't move as he meets the golden champion's glare. 'What are your orders Rook?' Zodiac interjects. 'We fortify the mansion and start the evacuation immedietly, we have less than an hour till dawn.' 'You arrogant son of a-' 'Let's move!' Rook barks in his sergeant voice and one of the two heavy main doors flies open. 'They attack!' 'What?!' Rook cries and starts walking for the entrance. 'Before dawn?' 'The fire spreads out of control captain, we need to retreat.' 'Did you catch sight of them, a banner?' 'It is too dark my lord, I could hardly see the soldier I did before he was nearly on top of me.' 'Call people here.' 'They're already on their way.' 'Gather every man with a sword in my courtyard.' Draven suddenly shoves past the thoughtfully quiet Rook. 'Hey!' Rook barks. Draven grabs the shoulder of a young boy running past. 'Grab a sword and get your brothers! Get everybody you know!' 'What are you doing?' 'I'm defending my home!' Draven shouts as he collects an axe from a weapon rack, and three swords in one hand before rushing off to find hands to put those swords in. Rook, face flushed red with anger, wheels on Zodiac as she storms past him out the door. 'You can grab a sword and protect YOUR people or you can hide in here.' Zodiac says with an emotionless glance, pulling the slender silver bow from her shoulder and breaking into a run for the stables. Rook snarls in anger and turns away, charging back into the mansion and out of sight. The horizon. The sun breaks the crest and spills merciful morning upon the eager landscape. 'Hold the gate!!' Draven's voice roars above the din of battle. Throwing himself forward he slams his shoulder against the barricaded front gate just when the battering ram on the other side slams forward. The gate holds and a dozen more men put their weight against it. Peices of lumber find purchase and prepare for the second, immanent blow. It thunders into the gate, the wood crackles and splinters but hold it does. 'Draven!' Zodiac's voice cuts the clamor. She appears on her horse, glorious and adorned in her winged helmet. 'We're ready for them!' She cries, an entire flank of men and women with bows and arrows stand ready, rag tag and dirty, steadfast defiance etched into every feature. The crash, the creak, cries and shouts to hold, to hold! 'Get off the gate!' Draven shoves the nearest to him. 'It won't hold another!' There was a moment, a long drawn out second where the music pauses in it's epic jingle. CRASH! Jagged splinters fill the air, the ram smashes through the barricade and retreats quickly. The music hits a hard note and the defenders spring into action as the first of the invaders appear. Arrows belt men and sheilds, silver streaks cutting through the torrent with hammer-like force. 'Cease!' Zodiac cries before another volley is launched. Draven's infantry closes at the gate, there's a sudden cry of urgency, a crash and two men sprawl into their allies, Zodiac yelps as Draven suddenly reels several feet, his heels digging rivuts in the earth as he reverses his momentum. 'Lexicon!' Zodiac curses as they clash, Lexicon's throws the axe wide with his arm guard and hits Draven again. He throws himself away as a silver bolt shimmers past his face. His arm guard comes up and deflects a second arrow, he adjusts and blocks a second he throws it down and charges. Zodiac's eyes flash, wide, she holds her aim and fires, drawing another. The bolt glances off of Lexicon's sword, he spins and drops into a stance twelve feet away. Draven comes down from a mighty leap, his axe spins and clashes against Lex's thick bastard sword. Again and again he spins the great hafted weapon, precise and powerful, Lexicon parries the first, ducks the second and leaps back, catching an arrow on his arm guard and then crossing it under his blade to catch that deadly axe. 'I'll kill you!' Draven screams in fury. Lexicon grins. He throws the axe into the air, blocks an arrow, side steps a second and slams his sword into the side of the axe as it swings down in an overhead cleave. His armored limb swings about savagely and crashes into Draven's ribs. The champion goes sprawling and Lex levels his weapon on Zodiac. 'This is a deul for the belt!' He cries. 'This is no duel.' She says too quiet for him to hear and releases an arrow, Lexicon's head snaps to the side, a trail of blood hovers in a line; a peice of his ear has vanished. 'Help!' The unfamiliar voice draws Zodiac's attention away from the scene just as Lexicon spins, pulling his sword up and across to deflect Draven's weapon. A little girl cries as she stumbles towards the mansion. The scene rises to show collums of smoke climbing into the air. 'The refugees...' She mutters and pulls in the reigns on her mount. She starts towards the little girl when the tree line behind her erupts, black armoured infantry charge the mansion from the behind. The little girl screams and starts running faster. A horse breaks from the bushes, his rider cloaked and hooded. Shaking her head again Zodiac looks to the battleing pair and turns her mount away, spurring it forward. More of Lexicon's troops push forward but the determined and desperate infantry Draven put together is holding, barely. Draven sweeps his axe wide and Lexicon moves to block it with his arm guard, Draven drops his shoulder and lets it fall underneath digging into the earth quickly before he snaps it back up. It catches Lexicon under his arm and digs past his one sided breast plate. Draven turns his head at the last instant, when the sound of thundering hooves comes up behind him, the cloaked rider sweeps a savage blow with a wickedly jagged falchion and the champion is sent sprawling under a blast of crimson ichor. Lexicon stumbles and takes a knee, his chest heaving with each laboured breath. Pulling his hand from his wound it drips with blood. Black armoured soldiers rush the defenders from behind, weapons crash and voices dry out in agony and victory alike. Lexicon's eyes look up to his savior as the hood falls away. 'You made the right choice Lestatt.' Looking back on that brief skirmish, in the grand scheme of things, the battle was not overly fierce. Larger wars will be waged. 'Where's the belt?' Lestatt asks. But it was probably the most significant moment in Third kingdoms long history. The catalyst of something far greater than a golden strap, or the Emperor's favor. Faith and loyalty is so easily put into the Gifted Ones. 'And remember, none can survive to bring word to the capitol!' Lexicon's voice fades with the scene. The Lexicon Saga
