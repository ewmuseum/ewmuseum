The Devil Made Me Do It

30 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/30/10 Belmont in October is something like you’d read in a horror novel. The trees have changed from an ugly-not-so-lively green to the death bloom of orange red and brown. Most prefer looking at the foliage in the fall than they do during the summer. The wind is sharp and severe because of the current from the channel to the east and the ocean to the west. This time of year the sun is never out, but most say the sun has never risen on Belmont, well ever since the aura of that house on the hill began to lay its rights to the land. Someone once wrote in a town paper that Belmont is the aborted child of Liverpool and England. They went on to say that Belmont was the blood clot left floating in the toilet after a pregnant whore went on a drinking binge and fell down a flight of steps. How Belmont became this way is no mystery. The Blood Countess Elizabeth Bathory once used this land as a summer home where she would take the lives of virgins and shower in their pure blood. It’s also the former home of the O’Neil family. The O’Neil name strikes fear in the hearts of those that say it, and if you are not scared when you speak this name it is because you are either one yourself or a sick demented freak that deserves to rot in the hell with the very same family we are speaking about now. There are a plethora of nonfiction, and well fiction pieces written about this very same town. None of the stories are ever positive and none ever perceive Belmont as some flourishing European city with wealthy important people. Instead Belmont is written as a gutter slime fucked town that no one in their right mind would ever consider visiting. Belmont is belittled moreso than anywhere else on earth. Oslo, Norway the town known for Black Metal, Church Burnings, and Satanism is a utopia compared to Belmont. Besides the serial killer cannibal woman and Europe’s version of the Manson family, Belmont’s claim to fame is author Stephen King once visited the town because a friend of his recommended he do so because he needed a dormant death of a town for his newest Dark Tower story called ‘The Wastelands.’ He claims to this day that Belmont, England had nothing to do with the location of this best selling novel, but the citizens beg to differ. Belmont’s most famous son, and it’s most famous bastard stands where his house once stood. Most tend to forget that this very plot of land he stands upon was made famous by the blood of it’s citizens because most are a ‘what have you done for me lately’ type, and 500 years ago is history. Bryan O’Neil is the town boogeyman, he is the devil incarnate, and he has no care for the town that he calls home. Bryan Mayhem stands where his house once stood staring down at the dead town which is only about a half mile from where he’s standing. He hates the people, he hates the land, and he even hates the name. His dad often joked with him that it sounded like a war ship. Bryan has no clue where he ever got that idea from, but it most likely came from the alcohol. Bryan hated it because it was a goddamn castle in a video game called Castlevania. The citizens feared him not only because he towered over everyone else but because of the rumors floating around about him. Most feel he murdered the Barnes child 20 years ago because Barnes was an infamous bully, and Bryan’s younger brother was the infamous bullie. Barnes body went missing one day after school and not an iota of evidence was ever discovered placing Bryan as the murderer. There was no evidence because no body was ever found. Bryan begins his trek into town, the very same trek he use to make with Jason to get groceries because his father was too drunk and his mother either had a needle in her arm or a cock inside of her, sometimes both at the same time. During their youth everyone either felt bad for them because of their namesake and parents, or some actually thought it was cute seeing 2 boys holding hands as they crossed the street to get different groceries. As they got older and the rumors started to circulate about molestation, drug abuse, and violence most began to avoid the O’Neil children. Because of the stigma of Bryan O’Neil’s popularity overseas and his “troubled” past most avoided him as an adult. The town had its own celebrity living amongst them, but they seem afraid and disinterested in the phenom. The main road in Belmont is called Berg. Berg is about 3 miles long and is the home of downtown Belmont. The shops and stores that once flourished so many years ago are either boarded up or is a haven for whores. Sure there’s Smiley’s Deli and Whittier’s Food-4-Less, but everything is else is shit. Berg does lead to Pervert’s Pass which is the bridge that is rumored to be where Anton O’Neil would take his children and do unspeakable things to them. Bryan, even has a giant, still gets goose flesh walking over this bridge. As he moves in a methodical pace Bryan is greeted in town with open eyes and mother’s grabbing their children tighter than they did before. This makes him smile. Belmont’s fear is the fuel to his fire, and the more they scatter like cockroaches in the light the more he enjoys it. He looks to his left and stops in front of what use to be Baltzly’s drug. The old man always had a lollipop for the lil un’s as he called them whenever they came to get the meds to help bring their mother down from her high. Old man Baltzly was very involved in the town attending every town hall meeting and every high school football and rugby game. He was the face of Belmont for so many years, well that’s until his face was crushed with a shovel. Bryan came home from school one day to a loud moan coming from upstairs. This was not unusual because he usually came home from school to his mother in the middle of “servicing a client” but this time was different. He heard moans followed by what seemed like screams for help. What the town did know was that Old Man Baltzly liked to beat women, and O’Neil’s mother was no different. Bryan caught him one day outside the bathroom at a rugby game and smashed his face so hard that if it wasn’t for the same shirt he wore week in and week out, no one would have ever figured out who it was. The entire town went to his funeral, even the O’Neils. Under his breathe Bryan hoped the old man burned in hell. 13 years old, a murder, and no sympathy. Bryan Mayhem was on the Eve of Destruction. He stopped himself in mid chuckle and continued down Berg, not knowing why he was here or where he was going, but he continued his determined pace. He moves up Berg, the chill snaps through him, he shoves his hands in the pocket of his trench coat and continues on. A block north was the Florist. This crack head whore use to make bouquets for every wedding and prom in town. Bryan once watched his father smoke a white rock from a pipe with this woman, than they both proceeded to head upstairs where screams and moans were heard all through out the night. Ms. Haden the florist ended up selling her store for a few rocks to a man named Alex Flatts who ended up selling sex from the very same building. Flatts was eventually arrested and his whores were all either arrested or killed. Bryan never had an interest in buying sex, so this building was always just something he passed by on his daily ventures. However, the next building was what made his thirst for blood, and his interest in Satanism peak as a young adult. The Backlist was an old book store ran by a man known to the town as Barker. They didn’t know if Barker was his first name or his last name, but nonetheless this man made Bryan hungry for blood. Whenever Jason ran off he came to this place and hid himself amongst some of the world’s greatest minds. Jason was not the most intelligent child on earth, some may say he had somewhat of a learning disability, but despite all that he steal yearned for knowledge. He would sneak into the back grab something by Twain or Emerson and just sit and read and read and read. Most of the time he had no clue what he was reading, but sitting in that book store made him feel important. Important people were smart and knew how to read. Bryan ignored this store, and rarely even paid it a lick of acknowledgement that it was even there. The only time he walked in was when he had to come and get Jason before both of them received a beating. Bryan came in one day wearing all black. His hair was getting longer and his face becoming paler. He received looks of disgust and death as he walked by, but he paid no mind to them. He opened the door to Backlist, a bell jingled letting Barker know he had a visitor. Bryan stood at the door and yelled for Jason three times before Barker walked out, his graying hair in shambles, wearing a black cloak. “Jason is not here. The boy has not been here for a few days.” Bryan stood still, quietly taking inventory of his surroundings. He goes to turn around and leave when he feels an old feeble hand on his shoulder. Bryan quickly turns around and slaps the hand away. “Calm down my son, I am not here to hurt you. I can only help free you.” Bryan looks at this old man, confused and scared. The fear causes his testes to rise into his body, a shiver runs through him, and his teeth rattle. “Free me?” “Child, you are young and weak minded, I shall save you from this burden just like I do for Jason.” Bryan is perplexed. A wave of thoughts and emotions begin to rattle his brain. What are you talking about?” Barker waves his hand in a follow me motion. “Let me show you something boy. Don’t worry I mean you no harm.” For some reason Bryan begins to feel an overwhelming level of trust with this man. He shuffles his feet and regains his balance as he walks towards this man. He stops him before a doorway that is illuminated with an eerie red glow. The man puts his hand up in front of Bryan and asks him, “Do you believe in Jesus Christ?” Bryan stands there silent not knowing his own beliefs. Entering that room behind Barker very well might have changed Bryan O’Neil……. Later that day, long after Bryan left “renewed” Jason walked into Backlist yelling for Barker. Jason’s lip was swollen, a large gash was on his head, and his shoulder was sore. Anton became very drunk and began to beat on his younger son. After the beating was done and the pain subsided Jason walked as fast as his beaten body would allow. The door was still unlocked and Jason rushed in ringing the little warning bell as loud as he could. “Mr. Barker! Mr. Barker!” There was no answer. Jason ran into the backroom and saw the horror laid out before him. Barker was nailed to a large cross like object. The cross was lying in the middle of the floor in the center of a circle . Barker’s face was mangled, bone and teeth exposed. Blood was caked to his forehead from the crown of thorns he had on his head. Lying at each point of the upside star which is painted on the ground are the removed organs from the old man. The stench was insane, and the visual was even worse. Jason drops to his knees and begins to cry. Barker’s hand was removed cleanly with a sharp item. Written in blood on the mirror over the makeshift fireplace was the word “Oblivion.” Jason would later find out that the finger prints were of Barker himself. Jason screams his brother’s name as loud as he could, knowing once again big brother has taken his only friend from him…..Jason however, never once accused Bryan, or even asked him why he did. Jason told himself that the devil made Bryan do it….Little did Jason know that his brother was the devil….and Belmont was his playground…
