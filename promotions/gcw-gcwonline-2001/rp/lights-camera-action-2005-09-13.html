Lights, camera, action!

13 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/05 'And...cut. Print that. It's a wrap. Great job Mike.' I walked off the set eager to get out of the singlet. I had just finished shooting a commercial for Vault energy drink. It had changed from the premise of me getting beat up by a bottle of the stuff, to me being the physical representation of a bottle of the stuff. A local wrestler from an indy promotion played the Average Joe who took a sip and then was transported into a wrestling ring where I played the part of the drink that kicked your ass. There were a hundred people playing extras in the arena we filmed the spot in. I had to toss the guy around for a half hour as they filmed him falling from every angle. Then I pinned him and the 'referee' made the three count and raised my hand. Then I had to look into the camera and make the most menacing face I could and shout 'Kicks like an energy drink!' Later an announcer would be added in saying 'Vault. Drinks like a soda...' and my part would be tagged on. I changed and met Jeff and Cindy as I exited my dressing room. 'Why did I let you talk me into this?' I asked him as we left the lot. 'Because it's good for your career.' Jeff said smiling at me. 'How come you never did any commercials?' I asked. 'I'm not the most photogenic man alive.' he argued. 'Bullshit. You're just lazy.' 'That too.' The fact that the next WorldWide was being held in Vancouver made lodgings pretty easy. Cindy and I stayed in the guest room of Jeff's house out on the Kenson family ranch. It was good to see his extended family again. I couldn't help but notice that Jeff's wife Hunter was pregnant, and Rancid's wife Kimber was pregnant again for the third time. Sisters pregnant at the same time. That couldn't be a pleasant household. 'I still can't believe you're teamed up with LeStatt Knight.' Jeff said as we got into his SUV and headed back to the ranch. 'What the hell is Caldy thinking?' 'The fact that the Marauder is teaming up with Stevens.' I said, 'So the natural enemies for them are myself and Knight. I mean after all, Spitfire and Knight are going into a submission match at Fallout and Johnny-Boy and I are going at it in a street fight.' 'Why in the world did you ask for a street fight?' Jeff asked me, 'That's just asking for trouble.' 'For him, not me. I grew up on the streets of New York, I'm gonna take Lexicon down a couple of pegs. He stuck his nose into my business pretty much from day one, so I'm gonna break it.' I said, 'You know better than anybody, I've never backed down from a challenge and I've never taken shit from anybody, I don't care who they are or what they've done. Like the old saying goes, you fuck with the bull, you get the horns.' He shook his head, 'I know, but a damn street fight? Couldn't you have just made it a hardcore match?' 'Actually I wanted a cage match, but there was already one scheduled, and this is a pay-per-view. You know how it is. For a PPV you need to go bigger and better. A hardcore match would have been good for an episode of WorldWide, but for FallOut we need big. We need bad. We need bloody. We need a street fight. Actually, I would have asked for ACWF, but that's the Jhub's baby, and I don't wanna fuck with that. I've seen those matches on the tapes you showed me and dangerous doesn't even begin to describe that shit.' We hit the ranch about twenty minutes later with Jeff changing the subject. I don't think he wanted to talk to me about the street fight anymore. Not because he looked at me as a meal ticket, but because of our friendship. He knew how far to push before I started pushing back and started yelling at him. And he was bigger than I was and knew where to hit me. His daughter Kiara came bounding out of the house and flung herself into my arms. Despite having seen me every day since we landed sunday afternoon. 'Hey uncle Mike. Come see what I drew!' she pulled me by the hand into the house and I let the eager eight year old pull me in. As best I could see it was a scene from 'Beauty and the Beast', and for an eight year old it was pretty damn good. Although Beast looked like a large dog rather than well...a beast. 'They didn't let him on camera did they? I mean the man is criminally fugly.' I turned to see Rancid come into the house and Jeff smirked at his brother-in-law. 'You know, one of these days I'm just gonna punch you dead in the face.' 'Might improve his looks.' That comment came from Hunter Kenson, who was easing herself down the stairs, her belly leading the way. 'Nothing could improve his looks.' I said 'He's got a face only a mother could love.' 'Whose mother?' Jeff asked. Later that night after almost everyone else had gone to bed I stood on the screened in back porch, a beer in hand looking out into the night. 'What's wrong?' Cindy asked from behind me. 'Nothing.' I said, 'Just thinking.' 'You're not worried about this tag-team match are you?' 'Well maybe a little.' I said sitting down on the swing. When Cindy had found out about the Vancouver WorldWide she packed and came with me for a chance to see Jeff again. She hadn't really seen him in person since his stint in GCW ended, and he 'retired' for the first time. 'I guess it's what Jeff said about LeStatt.' I said, 'I mean I know the man's a sadistic fuck. I mean for crying out loud he thretened to set Jason Dread on fire.' 'But he's your partner.' 'That's what worries me.' I said, 'I mean I don't know if I can trust him. Sure he hates Stevens, that's a given, and I know there's some history between him and Lexicon from the 3W. But I don't know if I can trust Knight. I know Jeff doesn't. He respects the man, but he also hates his guts. He and Knight have never seen eye to eye, and I'm just hoping that doesn't extend to me.' 'Hey,' Cindy turned my head to face her, 'You are Mike Savige, the Machine. You are the GCW World Television champion. A title you won after a month in the company. I know you'll get through this. You'll take care of the Marauder and Stevens, and if need be LeStatt Knight. Just remember what I taught you back when I was your manager. Take it one match at a time.' Then she kissed me. 'You taste like beer.' 'Funny that.' I said holding up the bottle. 'Thanks babe.' 'Anytime.' she said and got up, streched and looked back at me. 'I'm gonna head to bed. You coming?' I looked at the half empty bottle of beer and nodded. 'Soon as I finish this.' I said and she nodded and headed back in the house. I sipped the beer and looked back outside. I saw Rancid's dogs Malone and Capone heading towards Jeff's house from Rancid's. I let them in when they stood and the screen door and looked at me. They wandered into the house and left me alone again. Could I trust LeStatt Knight? Probably not, but I had a feeling his hatred for both Stevens and Lexicon was greater than what he felt for Jeff. After all he and Jeff respected each other and as far as they were concerned their battles were over and done with. They'd fought their wars, they'd shed their blood, and there was nothing else to prove. As for the Maruader...I mean Johnny Lexicon and I, there was still much to be settled. I'd take my measure of him at WorldWide, and then at FallOut...I'd finish the bastard off.
