Torture (Pt 1 The Vanishing)

Date: Hours after... Another week left laying, oh well at least he was screwing with Knight’s head. He was really starting to feel the mental ware of a full scale Stevens assault on his mind that was pretty clear. The guy even had to call up his bodyguard just to contend with it, that was great. Sure maybe the two of them were able to put Stevens down, but it took the two of them. Oh well, a few bruises were nothing compared to the mental exhaustion Knight was feeling. The plan was working, and that was good enough. Sitting down for a cup of coffee in a little dive diner, Stevens had a smile on his face thinking about the end of the show. He’d told Sheena he was going to head back to the hotel right after the show, but when everything was said and done, he hadn’t felt like it. He wasn’t exactly looking forward to spending more time with that asshole Lance. The kid was just plain ungrateful, always complaining, even though Stevens had been nice enough to let the him travel with them. The lawyer had taken his sweet time getting Stevens out of the holding cell, but it was ok, gave him some time to think. He wasn’t sure at first how he was going to handle talking to Sheena’s brother, he just wasn’t even sure how to deal with the kid at all, he’d made an immediate bad impression on Stevens. But taking the time in the holding cell to think he’d decided he’d just try and be civil to the kid, after all Sheena and he weren’t that close, at least it didn’t appear so, and it wasn’t like they’d be running into each other too often. At least that was the thought. Turns out Lance was in a lot more than just legal trouble around New York. He’d just gotten kicked out of his apartment, and was now homeless. He’d chosen to drown his sorrows with a couple drinks and had ended up in the bar fight that landed him in jail. In relating all of this to Stevens and company he’d remained aloof, even antagonistic. Still they couldn’t just toss him to the wolves on the streets, well that wasn’t true if it had been up to Michael solely, they probably would have. Sometimes you just can’t help someone until they’ve reached bottom and the kid seemed like he had a lot more falling to do, but it was Sheena’s brother and she flatly refused to go anywhere unless she knew he was going to be safe. Stevens couldn’t really blame her, if it had been his sister Cindy in the same situation he knew what he would have done. Although chances are he would’ve just put her up in an apartment somewhere, instead of dragging her along for the ride. Sheena however had different ideas, she thought maybe the group could be a good influence on the kid who had a really bad habit of making stupid choices. Michael guessed she didn’t want him going down the same road she was heading down before he’d found her and pulled her out. Understandable all things considered. You’d think the kid would at very least be thankful, they were giving him a place to stay, even if he did have to travel all the time. There was always something wrong though, he didn’t like the music or the food, he couldn’t sleep in the hotel beds, whatever. Lance was like a freakin two year old, if things didn’t go exactly how he wanted them to he’d make sure everyone knew about it. Bastard was really starting to get under Michael’s skin, but he tried to grin and bear it, for Sheena’s sake if nothing else, and Alex seemed to like the kid. Maybe he saw his past reflected in the kids shadowed eyes. It wasn’t exactly a big secret that the kid was a junkie. That fact was made clear enough the first time he wore a short sleeve shirt. The track marks stood out really plainly against his tanned skin. Which was odd in and of itself, most junkie’s Stevens had ever met hated the sun and were always pale. If you factored in the kid’s musical taste, well the tan made even less sense, but hell he was just different. Finishing up the last sip from the coffee cup, Stevens checked his cell phone. The show’d been around almost three hours now. Strangely, under normal circumstances she would’ve already called him four or five times to figure out where he’d run off too. Maybe he was just too busy dealing with the latest complaint from Lance. Either way, it was starting to get pretty late, and Stevens decided it was time to call it a night. Pushing the chair away from the table and standing in one fluid motion, he tossed some cash on the table, easily enough for the coffee and a decent tip. The waitress had been really good to refill the thing whenever it got empty. She deserved it. Stepping out onto the sidewalk he started heading down towards the hotel. It was only a block away, he’d decided on that particular diner because it was so close. At first he’d planned on calling up Sheena and having her meet him here, but with her would’ve come the brother and he’d decided against it. Burying his hands his pocket he walked. The foot traffic wasn’t too heavy at this time of night, something Michael was exceedingly grateful for, he hated having to push his way through the sidewalk mod. One of the many reasons he preferred the night to the day. Lighting a cigarette while walking he tried to clear his mind. Best not to think too much, if he did he’d just start dwelling on how much he didn’t like the kid again. That probably wouldn’t be the best thing to have floating through his head when he got home, he might end up saying something about it and ruining the night for everyone. With any luck the kid would already be asleep. Arriving outside the hotel he tossed the nearly finished cigarette in the gutter. Hotels in this price range frowned on smoking in their lobbies. Normally Stevens wouldn’t have cared and just smoked anyway, but after that little bit of trouble in New York, he was a little leery of smoking regulations. Oh well, he’d probably get over that soon enough, the worst thing that came out of the whole ordeal was Lance anyway. Great, didn’t mean to start thinking about him again. Apparently it was inevitable, until the kid found a permanent place to hang his hat Stevens was going to keep thinking about him off and on. Oh well, such is life. Through the lobby and up the elevator, he tried to put a smile on his face. Best to not antagonize the little prick by looking unhappy the moment he stepped through the door. Once he reached the door to the room, he was looking something resembling happy, at least not annoyed. That was about the best he could hope for at this point, he was going to have to straighten the kid out sooner or later, but tonight wasn’t the night. Sliding the keycard into the door, he turned the handle and stepped inside. The breathe caught in his lungs as he witnessed the carnage before him. Furniture overturned, or broken, the room was a disaster area. Painted across one wall in red “COPS KILL’Think about it”, Alex and Lance lay on the ground, unconscious maybe worse. Wasting no more time he sprinted into the room, he should check on the two men, but only one thought consumed his mind, where was Sheena? Throwing open the door to the bedroom of the suite, the bed linens were torn away from the mattress, a lamp was smashed. Continuing from there he stormed into the bathroom, nothing. “SHEENA!” His voice echoed through the suite, tearing out back into the suite proper, he preformed a quick search turning up nothing. “Uhhhh” The grunt sounded like Alex, maybe he saw something, knows what’s going on. Leaping over the couch Stevens ran to his fallen cousin. Kneeling beside the larger man, he cradled his head, feeling the warm trickle of blood from the back of the big man’s skull. His eyes were fluttering, he was almost conscious, Stevens couldn’t wait, had to know what happened. She was gone, who’d taken her? “Alex, ALEX! What the fuck happened” Who?” “Ugh, uh, nh, don’t know, didn’t see. Came from behind, hit me with something. Didn’t see.” Just like that the big man was out again, he probably had a pretty serious concussion he was in need of medical attention badly. Lance probably wasn’t doing much better. Quickly Stevens considered his options, he should call the police, but the note on the wall, stopped him. Kidnappers were dangerous people, he’d been taught that at an early age. The children of the wealthy are always a target. He just didn’t know what to do, none of this made any sense. Slumping over into more of a seated position he buried his face in his hands. What was he going to do? ~To Be Continued~
