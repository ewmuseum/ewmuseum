Sleeping With the Devil

20 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 20, 2008 She was a reporter and found that the best way to get inside his mind would be to have him inside her. Thrust after thrust she mentally jotted questions in her memory banks for the after talk while trying her hardest not to mention one mid-moan. It went on this way for another half hour before Jay let out one last gasp and rolled off the side of the bed onto the hotel rug. He grabbed up his boxers in one swipe and followed with his left hand by grabbing his Marlboro’s and a lighter. "Where are you going?" asked the young reporter. "Need a smoke s'all." "Me too actually." Jay opened the sliding door and stepped onto the porch. "Yeah?" He left the question dangling as he shut the door behind him creating a barrier between the conversation. Curious, she propped her petite frame onto her elbows and peered outside only to watch him light the end of the cigarette. Leaning back, he exhaled and kicked his bare feet onto the railing. The reporter found her phone on the side table. Dialing. "Hey, what do you think's more important: Andy Murray or Caroline?" She waited a second and listened closely. "I'm in his room, just answer." Another pause, "Yeah yeah, c'mon." She listened a little longer before, "You think? I figured it'd be pretty big to break open the whole Caroline egg." Jay took in another drag. "Ok, I can do Murray." She clapped the phone closed and found Jay's shirt lying in a pile on the floor. Pulling it on, she walked to the door and stepped outside. Jay paid no attention. She took a seat in the chair beside him and looked out at the moon across the way. It was full and lit the streets below better than the overhanging lights. "Beautiful night out, huh?" He nodded and sucked in nicotine, "Yeah." "Not into post coital talk?" "Never was. Makes things awkward." "You think?" "I said it, yeah?" "True." An audible gulp came from her throat as she collected her thoughts. "You know...what we had in there - well, I liked it." "Good." "You don't have a girlfriend or anything, do you?" Jay turned to face her and blew a cloud of smoke into her face. "I thought you were part of the media." "You're a mysterious man. The last I heard was that you and a girl named Caroline were hanging around places." For a second he waited, took a drag, and shut his eyes. It was a name, yes, but it was more than that. It was an idea. A past. It was a symbol of what should have been only to be later dismantled by greed and misfortune. He could still see her eyes when he heard her name. He could still smell her perfume. Opening his eyes and releasing the smoke, he shook his head. "She's doing other things." "Jail I heard actually." "Here for the story or the cock Candace?" "I'm sorry." She sunk her head into her shoulders and looked off the balcony again. The moon captured her attention for a short while before she tried to crack in again. "Am I the first to go this far for -?" "No." "And how did they do?" He smirked and flicked the dead butt off the porch. Jay lit another. "You said I was mysterious, right?" "Touch?." "Did I leave something out during the press conference?" "A lot actually." He ashed the cigarette, "You want more White talk?" "Murray actually." He shot her a perplexed look, "Murray? What'd I miss?" She turned her head onto her palm and looked into Jay's stony eyes, "Everything. What's next for him? How do you plan on keeping him back? Is it official for you two to fight at NC-17? Will you -" "Why's it all matter?" "It's news and it's hot. As much as the people despise you, they also love the fact that you're messing with their favorite man." "Go figure." "I just want to know a little Jay. Enough to satisfy a couple people back in the office." He laughed it off, "Why should I? If they're interested they'll watch Worldwide." "Some need an incentive." "Look, Caroline -" "Caroline?" He froze a second as the realization hit him, "I didn't mean that." "Of course not." She picked her head off her palm and allowed a wide grin to spread. "When's the last time you saw her?" "Forget it." "Jay," Candace turned the chair to face him as if interviewing without the microphone down his throat, "Off the record. How long?" He took a drag on the cigarette again and shook her off, "Forget it. It was a mistake." "I know but," He looked at the burning tip of his cigarette and then back at her flawless skin. Without a second's hesitation he tossed the smoldering piece at her creating a sharp searing sound in the air. Her cheek turned red and her shirt began to catch ablaze. "Holy shit!" Jay watched as she took the shirt off and tossed it onto the balcony floor. He stood and opened the sliding door leaving her outside. "You son of a bitch!" He ignored her and grabbed a pair of jeans. Sliding them on, he could here her entering the room behind him. "Jay Terror!" He left her behind with the slam of another door. Candace dialed into her phone and sat on the edge of the bed. "Hi, yeah, I'd like to report an attack. I'm in room 345 of the Marriott."
