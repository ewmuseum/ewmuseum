Date: 01/07/11 :: Scene opens up Thursday January 6th 9:30 pm at a hotel in Downtown Columbus Ohio. Johnny Davis is wearing a Gucci shirt half buttoned and some blackish jeans along with some all black air force ones. Johnny Davis has his hair in a tight pony tail with his mr.t like chains hanging below. One chain is the playboy bunny logo thats silver along with a huge diamond studded chain that says Johnny D. Playboy has a wad of cash on his bed as he sprays on some cologne and flex's in the mirror. As he kisses his bicep his cellphone appears to ring with the Ring tone, "Im a playa" By tech N9ne his new entrance music. After a minute long ring Johnny Davis answers the phone :: Johnny Davis : Hello. Champ here. Voice : Yo, Kid it's Damian. Johnny Davis : No Shit Caller ID Homeboy. Damian Dawkins : Hey im here in Colombus at the Dollhouse, Where we set up a champagne room, and a Place for you to hold a private press conference to speak about your match on the upcoming card. Johnny Davis : Cool. Love it when a homeboy comes through. Any Ladies there? Damian Dawkins : Shit, Kid Women Galore, Best Gentleman's club in town, and there all waiting for you, I brought your table stand, with the life size Johnny Davis stand up. I got tons of your merchandise, including your brand new GCW Shirt. Johnny Davis : Well, Make some calls the party is on it's way, You can invite other GCW cat's if you want, Big party, Try to get some of those wrestling honey's, Like Aimz, Alexis O'Ryan ,Carmella, and definately Vivica Valentine, And Dynasty ? Damian Dawkins : DYNASTY ? that's a dude ? Johnny Davis : My Bad, I keep forgetting that, You can even invite Guys like Shaman if you'd like. Even though he probally won't be able to get off the indian reservation. Damian Dawkins : Your'e funny kid, You been drinking already ? Ill see ya in a minute. :: Johnny Davis leaves the hotel room and heads down to the lobby where he hops in the first available cab, After a very short drive he arrives at The Doll house , There's a red carpet and camera's waiting as Playboy hop's out the grimmy amigo taxi, then the pop is amazing, flashe's by the thousands as Playboy waive's and enter's the crowded gentleman's club. As Playboy enter's he is escorted by 4 beautiful ladies, 1 of each nationality, he is then met at VIP by the 6'8'' 310 pound bodyguard / Personel Promoter and wrestler himself Damian Dawkin's. :: Johnny Davis : SHOT'S OF PATRON FOR EVERYONE !!!!!!!!! :: The cocktail waitress then announces over a intercom PLAYBOY JOHNNY DAVIS HAS JUST BOUGHT OUT THE BAR AND WOULD LIKE TO TOAST TO HIS UPCOMING MATCH AGAINST KJ NEWFOUNDLAND AND MICAH ST. CLAIR :: Damian Dawkins : Before you get to drunk homeboy let's talk. So Wednesday ill be in the crowd, cheering you on, Would love to be in your corner but no deal has been worked out, still coming off that ankle surgery, but anyways, how are you gonna fight 2 guys at 1 time, Johnny Davis : Well ill start by isolating the weak link which physically i believe is Micah St. Clair. Damian Dawkins : Because he's shorter ? Johnny Davis : No Because he's Canadian, You know there always weaker eh ? KJ seems to me to be a well prepared athlete. Did some research he's a very qualified competitor but aswell so is Micah, Im not going to underestimate either of these guys, Im going to isolate and attack, ::Johnny Davis then situate's himself as two women begin their private dance as Playboy is still conversating and then swigs from the bottle of patron the waitress dropped off. :: Johnny Davis : i s o l a t e. Wow im about to isolate myself from you Damian and go into a private room, but i need to keep my cool. Damian Dawkins : Damn. these girls put in work kid. Johnny Davis : Im gonna put in work here in GCW, First I will put KJ and Micah out and then it's on to bigger fish, Bryan Mayhem , yeah that's right Bryan Mayhem, Bryan needs to know I am the King of Extreme, From the adult industry to the 30 foot high cage diving,chair shot receiving , barbwire piercing my fibula, im a BAD MAN, Damian Dawkins : You Don't have to tell me sucka. I know, Iv'e seen you powerbomb Jade shocker through six tables from a two story cage, Iv'e seen you Playboy plunge 30 feet off the ground, You've been there, and done that. Johnny Davis : Well It's about party time, im getting a buzz. Damian Dawkins : Same Kid. Johnny Davis : Damian my final thoughts about Worldwide 143, KJ, Micah, We your only rated PG it's not a good idea to play with a X-Rated main eventer, TOLD YA IM DRUNK, NOW.As for Mayhem, Wrestling me would be legendary . Recieving stitches will be priceless. Damian Dawkins : Let's pass out these Playboy's Back shirt's Johnny Davis : Sounds good....Gosssssssh darn WHERE'S VIVICA IS SHE HERRRRRR YETTTT?
