I watch the game with a sense of satisfaction. It seems that my life has finally come together, or as much as it’s possible for my life to come together when I’m in hiding. Over the past few weeks the two men and I have bonded over our mutual love of the UFC and our mutual distaste over the state of the high school that we teach in. Adam is a short, balding man with a beer belly and a sizable nose. He teaches American history, and continually complains that most of his students’ sleep in class but ace his tests by reading the textbook. Brian is Adam’s polar opposite. He is an abnormally tall stick of a man, and has a way of walking that makes him seem a great deal more clumsy than he actually is. He has the opposite problem of Adam, as well. I suspect that she had motives other than the safety of her students, but I am not about to pry. “Well it’s a good thing they decided to head out,” I reply with a shrug, “I’d rather not try to deal with kids who are high on one thing or another.” At that, Brian and Adam immediately burst out laughing. I stare at them confused, wondering what was so funny, but then I recognized the sound of that laugh. And somehow…I doubt she wanted me to get rid of them for the good of the community. But fuck it, I don’t get paid anywhere near enough to worry about it.
