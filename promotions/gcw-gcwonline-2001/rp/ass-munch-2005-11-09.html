Ass Munch.

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-9-05 The night was cold and dry, autumn’s fingers strangled the earth. The dead leaves swirled around Chrissy-Poo as he walked down the street. Chriss quickly scuffled towards his destination, his body under attack as the frigid wind cut into him like a barrage of arrows. Poo snuck into his dormitory. His cheeks turned red as he thawed in the lobby. He made his way up the stairs, until he finally reached his floor. Poo fiddled with his keys in the door, but finally after several moments the door swung open. Chrissy tumbled in and plopped down on his couch. His dorm was small and stuffy, like a human shoe box. Chrissy Poo sighed? then groaned. He held his side. Chrissy-Poo: “That fucking dick?” Chrissy ran his fingers through his dirty blonde hair. Chrissy-Poo: “Why is everyone so god damn stupid?” Chrissy was in pain. After defeated Ehren Moore at World Wide, you’d think that the Master of Poo-Fu would have been riding high? but that was anything but the case. After his victory, Chrissy had been assaulted by a sour Bryan Mayhem. Chrissy-Poo: “This” this means war? So this seven foot freakin? MIME wants to mess with me, just because he’s a sore loser? Fine? FINE? but there’s god damn consequences? I’m going to fight back.” Chrissy-Poo closed his eyes. He ached inside. Chrissy-Poo: “Bronx Maloney” I can’t believe that guy tried to save me? and he got crushed. I’m sorry, man? You didn’t have to do that, really. This is between Bryan Mayhem or O? Neil or whoever he is? it’s between he and I.” Chrissy-Poo laid down and closed his eyes. How in the hell could he beat Bryan Mayhem? the guy was a monster. He dwarfed Chrissy, and made him look like a kindergartener. This was, by far, going to be the biggest obstacle Chrissy-Poo had ever had to face in the ring. And then there was the X factor? Chrissy-Poo: “And Coyle O” Conner? nothing person, man? but I think you might just get loss in the mix this time? I mean? Mayhem is going to go through anyone and everyone to get to me? he wants revenge? and you know how dickweeds like that get when they want something?” No, not that X factor? not Coyle O? Conner? the OTHER one? Chrissy-Poo: “But good thing I’ve got the X Mask” Chrissy-Poo is but a man? but Chrissy-X? Chrissy-X is something more. I’ve got a good feeling? I think I can pull this off. Bryan O’Neil is a storm of emotion, he’s not thinking clearly. I beat him at Devil’s Night once? I can do it again. His head is obvious not screwed on right. I can do this? I just need to stay one step ahead of the big idiot.” Suddenly, a ghastly apparition appeared before Chrissy-Poo. Figure: “Chrissy-Poooooo” Chriiiisssyyy-Pooooo?” Chrissy-Poo: “Yeah” Who’s there?” Figure: “Chrissy-Pooooo” it is I? The ghost of Randy Rhoads!” Chrissy-Poo: “No shit” Really? Wow? this is a first.” Randy Rhoads: “Fuckin” A. right, man.” Chrissy-Poo: “Yeah” I mean, John Bonham and Bon Scott drop by now and then? but I don’t believe we’ve ever met.” Randy Rhoads: “No, we haven’t.” Chrissy-Poo: “So what can I do you for?” Randy Rhoads: “I just want you to know, man” That you can do this, man? I mean like? Have you listened to Diary of a Madman lately?” Chrissy-Poo: “Well” not since this morning?” Randy Rhoads: “Well, fucking A., man” what did you learn from it?” Chrissy-Poo: “Umm?” Randy Rhoads: “Damn it, man” dig deeper. Look inside yourself, you will find all the answers!” Chrissy-Poo: “I learned” I learned that? that? That the madman? the madman is ME. I’m the mad man, not Ozzy! Oh my god? it all makes perfect sense now? this whole time? my eyes have opened! It’s as clear as day! I’m a crazy fucking bitch! I’M CRAZY! I’M A FUCKING 160 POUND PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER! WHAT THE HELL AM I DOING!” I DON’T KNOW, I’m CRAZY!” Randy Rhoads: “Right on” looks like my work here is done.” Randy Rhoads gave Chrissy-Poo a thumbs up and disappeared into a swirl of purple smoke. Chrissy-Poo: “Wow” I feel great! I feel like? like? Like bashing in Bryan Mayhem’s skull in with a tack hammer! This feeling? it’s better than any drug! It’s better than sex! It’s better than sex with other people even! I’m going to freakin? beat the hell out of Bryan Mayhem! Wow? just wow? I can’t believe that just happened.” Chrissy-Poo thought for a moment. Chrissy-Poo: “Well” actually? I can. I’ve been strung out on peyote for hours? shit.” Chrissy took a deep breath. Chrissy-Poo: “So Mr. Mime Mayhem is pissed” well I don’t understand why? all I did was beat him in the Buried Match at Devil’s Night? but I did it within the rules? I don’t know what the hell his problem is, and starting now, I don’t care. It doesn’t matter what his problem is? I would have reasoned with him, but no? no, he’s crossed the line. Now it’s not about you anymore, Mayhem? it’s about ME? it’s about me defending myself? about standing up for myself. So you’re a sore loser and you’re EMBARASSED that I eliminated you? big whoop? you little baby, why don’t you cry home to MAMA! There’s a big fat black and white doodie in your pants and you need to be changed, I know? but don’t get in my face about it? I may be small, but I’m a crazy little motherfucker? And you want to provoke me, go ahead? Go ahead, because I’ll be the one to reap the benefits. I’ll be standing over your fallen body with the GCW Television title around my waist? and you? you’d have been beaten by a little shit like me? your ego and your career would never be the same?” Chrissy smiled. Chrissy-Poo: “And it’s going to be all” your? fault.” ? ? ? “Ass munch.” The End.
