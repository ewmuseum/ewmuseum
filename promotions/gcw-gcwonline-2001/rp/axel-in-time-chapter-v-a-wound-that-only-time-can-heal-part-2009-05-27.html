AXEL in Time- Chapter V: A Wound That Only Time Can Heal, Part 2

27 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27, 2009 ‘Wait and see’- the words of the day for one, AXEL Action. Being stuck in a place like ‘Prohibition City’ for six weeks can put a lot of things into perspective for someone. After sitting in one spot and waiting nearly twenty-four hours for his exit from that wretched place, he had begun to grow a little restless. His patience long gone, he stands from his seat and paces along the floor, his excitement getting the best of him. AXEL has never been one to just sit around and wait. He’s the type of person who prefers to just reach out and take what he wants, no matter how he has to do it. In 1377, William Langland said, “Patience is a virtue. The ability to wait for something without excessive frustration is a valuable character trait.” This is very similar to the Latin, “Maxima enim patientia virtus. (Patience is the greatest virtue.),” and the French, “Patience est une grant vertu. (Patience is a great value.)” Chaucer, in ‘The Canterbury Tales’, wrote: “Patience is a high virtue,” followed by the wry rejoinder, “But virtue can hurt you.” Never have more prophetic words been written. AXEL Action has never been one of the virtuous sort, always favoring chaos and destruction over order and peace. Patience has never really been his cup of tea, either, but that just comes with the territory. To him, patience is nothing more than a fleeting feeling, something that parents attempt to instill in their children in hopes of ending their incessant nagging about things they want. It’s something that AXEL learned a long time ago, and it’s helped him achieve his goals on more than one occasion. However, his current goal seems almost impossible to achieve and it’s likely that his efforts will end in disappointment. With his stomach full of proverbial butterflies and his mind plagued with doubt; he paces, paces and paces some more without end. Unsure of just how accurate his research was, or just where he’s going to end up if he manages to divert the flow of time enough to change the path of the rift, he decides to get some sleep. After all, nothing clears the mind like a good night’s rest, right? Wrong. Curling up on the loveseat in the room, he closes his eyes and slips into a rather fitful sleep. Tossing and turning quite violently, his subconscious mind drifts back to past events, most notably the more horrifying ones. Between the memory of his parents’ murder and his time spent in the rat-infested, shithole dungeons of England, his slumber wasn’t a very comfortable one. Waking with a start, his body drips with a cold sweat and his heart beats ninety to nothing, much faster than it normally should. Making a mental note to see his shrink once he gets home, AXEL shifts himself so that his feet rest on the floor, leaning forward to bury his face in his hands. In an attempt to clear his head, he breathes deeply before exhaling slowly. Usually, it’s in with the good air and out with the bad, but due to the circumstances at hand, all he’s getting is stale air. He chokes on that stale air, quickly uncovering his face so that he can breathe properly. “Okay, that wasn’t the greatest idea, after all.” The room is filled with an unearthly chill, the icy grip of which envelops him like fierce blanket of cold. He places a hand on the wall to hold himself up, because that chill sends shivers down his spine, the likes of which he’s never felt before. It takes everything he has in him to keep from dropping to his knees, his mind racing for an explanation. His breath appears in front of him as a dense mist as he exhales, a sure sign that the temperature in the room has dropped and that it isn’t just him. That’s when the realization strikes him -- supernatural things such as spirits and time rifts need a certain amount of electromagnetic energy in order to manifest. These supernatural occurrences generally cause a sharp chill in the area they manifest in, because when they suck all the electromagnetic energy out of that spot, it takes the heat with it. This causes what is known as a ‘cold spot’, or an area of localized coldness or sudden drop in temperature that is claimed to be the result of paranormal activity. “Get it together, AXEL. Get it together. It’s just a bit of a chill, nothing to worry about.” Shaking his head, he manages to pull himself together and focus on the task at hand. He senses that his exit from this dreadful period in time is nearby; he just has to figure out where. Taking one, last deep breath, he steels himself for what’s to come, and begins his search. Scanning the room with his eyes, he’s able to deduce that the rift he’s looking for is going to be in one of two places: either the closet or the bathroom. The problem was figuring out which to check first. In fact, it could be in an even weirder spot than the closet. After all, the first portal appeared in the weirdest, well, least expected place -- his pantry. AXEL sighs, running his fingers through his hair, brushing a few strands out of his eyes. He decides that the closet is the best place to begin his search. Making his way towards that oaken door, he takes hold of the knob with the tightest of grips. He turns the knob and suddenly, his mind drifts off into thoughts of his dearest Alysea. His subconscious mind begins replaying the events of that fateful day. He sees himself scaling the walls of the castle and climbing onto the balcony of her room, watching her sleep. She always looked like an angel when she slept, and he recalls slipping into bed with his ‘angel’, as he so affectionately referred to her as. His mind drifts even farther back to all those nights they spent consummating their relationship. AXEL reminisces about the day he asked her to marry him, and he can still see that look of genuine happiness on her face when she said yes. His mind takes him back to one final moment, and that’s the moment he becomes separated from his love. He clearly remembers stepping through the rift with her in tow, only to find she’s not with him when he steps through the other side. As betrayed as the thought of that day makes him feel, he still can’t help but love that woman. “Hang on, Aly… I’m coming back to get you.” Snatching the closet door open, he breathes a sigh of relief as he’s managed to find the portal in the first place he looked. Taking hold of the solid steel headboard of that bed he dismembered, he takes a final look around the hotel room before stepping into the rift, waiting to see what’s on the other side. As the portal closes behind him, he closes his eyes, allowing himself to be swallowed in the energies of the vortex. They say that patience is a virtue… but virtue can hurt you.
