Changes, Part I: Josh's story

11 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/06 Joshua Bodaway Brand is a man of action. A man who knows what he wants and goes out there and gets it. He's a man who knows exactly what he's after. And what he wants is simple stuff. Like getting by his first tag team defense against a couple of guys he would have beat up back in high school. D&D Nerds and their ilk who either chuck dice on a table or play with their Magic of Yu Gi Oh cards. People like the Guild. But then again, he's got something that the two misfits that make up the Guild could never have. He's been living a real life version of the game they hold so dear for years. He's seen real life trolls, demons, and a few other things, like his brother being a werewolf. Including being able to sleep with an angel, or as their little game calls them, celestials. These boys don't get what they're getting into. Then again, they think that their little fantasy world is real. Got news for them, it is real. It's realer then they'd ever imagine. Especially the parts about all of these outside entities playing around with people's lives, making deals and generally being major pains in the asses. Something wasn't right, and that's what Josh wanted to fix now. He could worry about The Guild and act like he was afraid, or he could get business taken care of, and make sure that Dani was going to be okay. Given the circumstances, he didn't love anyone in the Guild or the Ascension for that matter. But Dani meant more to him then anything. Even those tag team titles. Yeah, he finally won a title, but there would be more, he was sure of that. But in the end, he only had one chance with Dani, and he had to get it right this first time. Things just keep piling up, or at least that is what Josh began to think. Everything was pointing to a coverup for Dani, and it was beginning to make the Da Vinci Code look like child's play. Some wanted to sit around and wait for the enemy to attack, but that was not one thing that Josh agreed with. What he knew? There was more in Danielle's past that anyone could imagine. Most knew about the ten years in the Catholic Orphanage. Most knew about the death of her mother and father, that was supposedly done as a hate crime against Native Americans. But what most didn't know, including Danielle herself, was she wasn't a only child. She was the younger sister of a very brave little man named Adam. He took his duties of protecting his little sister to his grave. But it bothered Josh that her grandfather allowed her believe she had been the only child all her life... never to tell her about her brother. Josh decided only one thing to do and that was to travel to meet her grandfather and face to fact talk to him about everything. Only one thing bothered Josh even more: the fact that his mind was filling with snapshots of memories. A lot of them he wasn't sure about or who they where. But now he had to act to find out and connect the dots. But to do that he had to leave Dani for a while. He didn't need to take care of his brother though, he was assigned to Alexis now. Dani was fully Josh's responsibility. But she'd never support him doing this. So he had to figure out how to take care of things. And that meant leaving her with Blaise and Ukyo. Josh had set everything up, his mother was covering for him in Seattle. if anyone was to ask he was out visiting a old friend that wasn't doing so well. It had become a 'faked family problem' that he had to go take care of, a really close friend who was hurt. And he's be 'staying with his mother'. Josh had pooled his money plus a little extra for a private plane to take him to South Dakota. He even had his mother call him with a story of a old friend injured in front of Danielle, who told him to go and visit him. He hated to lie to her but it was the only thing he could think of he had to get there with out being stopped, and the Order had enough people to keep him from doing anything. He'd even paid for a plane ticket he'd use to Seattle and back. But the private plane was chartered from there to the area near where Dani's reservation was in the Black Hills. He was breaking more protocols then he should, and if he got caught, he'd be in for a major case of trouble, but it was something he had to do. The flight to Seattle had been comfortable, to make it look really good, he'd flown first class with all the trimmings. But once there, he'd walked down the terminal at Sea-Tac and then took the shuttle to the business airport on the side. From there it was a little 'puddle jumper' that would take him backwards towards his real destination. He was a man on a mission... ~Rosebud Lakhota Reservation~ ~Todd County, South Dakota~ The fight took forever, it was slow and bumpy as an older plane would be. When they landed there was a car waiting for him just like his mother said there would be, but only until he promised to keep it below ninety miles per hour on the Reservation. It was a pretty long drive from Mission to here, the plane had to land at a small airport. It was a good hour, hour and a half on these very poorly maintained roads. He pulled up at the lone house on this road, and climbed out of the car. He looked around, seeing the stable that had Dani's horse in it, as well as all of the others, and the relatively larger house that housed three generations of Singing Eagle's family in it now. An old, weathered man stepped out onto the porch, using a cane to support himself. He smiled a partially toothless smile. One of the younger members of his generation, one of Dani's cousins, stepped out with him. It was the only cousin he'd ever met, the one that didn't like Josh because he was a half-breed. ?Greetings young cub, how are you doing this day? I was not expecting any company. Did you bring my granddaughter with you?? Josh glared at the man, and the younger man who helped him stay upright when he walked. ?We need to talk, there is something that had come up and I want answers. And you're the only man who can give them to me right now.? Danielle's Grandfather, Singing Eagle, raised his eyebrow at josh, and then smiled. ?Really young man, and what would caused you to come this far out of your way?? Josh reaches into his car and pulls out a rolled up piece of paper. ?Two things Actually, one something for you and two something for Danielle.? Josh hands the first picture to Grandfather. The old man stutters as he says the one word that Josh was expecting him to say. ?A... A... Ad... Adam...? His eyes welled up and he stood there staring at the picture. He turns and moves towards one of the chairs on his porch. The young man helps him to sit down to what appears to be much in the line of trouble. ?How do you know this?? ?Dani had a dream, and this young man was in it. It was a dream of his last night alive. And it was pretty detailed.? The grandfather looked at Josh with a worried face, then looked back at the picture. Running is fingers along the face lines of the young boy. ?He was a smart little guy, he knew his destiny long before we knew it.? Josh got a bit livid. ?Destiny? What Destiny? There is no such thing as Destiny? He was led to a slaughter.? The grandfather looked up at Josh his face hardened ?You have no idea, what happened!? Josh slammed his fist down on the railing to the porch. ?Yes I do... I saw it though her eyes.. I saw the dream that she did. I don't know how but it was strong and came through to me as more then just the emotions she was feeling.? Singing Eagle shook his head. ? But she was too young to remember. Now Josh shook his head. ?No she wasn't, she was just to young to process it. You of all people should know that the mind can store everything you ever see. But in this case, her meeting with The Keeper and Suriel, allowed her to remember. Even to the point she now knows that Kokabel knew what happened to her. You allowed her to live all her life alone, unloved!? The grandfather smiled, and stood. He started towards the door. ?Come on, I'll let you know then. It's obvious that things are moving that are prophesied. The time for hiding is over.? ?Prophesied?? The old man moved pretty good. He had a stride to him now. It looked like the younger man couldn't really keep up with him too much. He headed over to the stone hearth fireplace that kept the house warm in winter. Next to it was an old chest, a footlocker almost. It looked like it dated back to World War II. The old man sat in the chair next to it and then started fumbling with the combination lock that was on it. As soon as it popped open, he pulled the lid up and reached in. He Pulled out a folder and handed it to josh. ?Joshua, you have to understand. Adam did what he was supposed to do, by giving his life he allowed Danielle to live.? ?Bullshit, you know as well as I do old man, Kokabel could of saved them all, why didn't he? I know it was him that saved her out of the car. The nun's recognized the drawing of him, that I did. Danielle is suffering, she has memories, she has seen what happened to her family.? The old man looked troubled at that. ?The touch of the greater Angel that resides in your Keeper must have allowed her to have... what is the term? Total Recall of that night.? ?Yeah, we're guessing that he wanted her to remmeber. Cause we're being followed. But this whole hiding things from her got her scared. She tried to run, and she ran almost into the waiting arms of BARBA...? Before Josh can say the name, he's interrupted by someone yelling at him! ?ENOUGH JOSHUA, you do not speak that name here!? Josh's head snapped into the Direction of the voice, as did Singing Eagle's. Josh saw Singing Eagle almost fall out of his old chair at the site of the man who now was in the room with them. But it was no man. It was the Morning Star himself. Josh recognized him. His wings were out, but relaxed on his back. Before them stood the Virtue known as Kokabel, Star of God... Dani's sixth times over great grandfather. On his face was a look of concern, almost fright as how he just barely made it in time. ?What the hell are you doing here? Kokabel gave him a stern look. ?Well since you're going around dropping names. Mine a few times, as well as the Wrath's... I wanted to make sure you stopped before you dropped another one. Looks like I got here just in time.? Josh wasn't to responsive. ?What ever, you're part of the reason for this mess.? That cause Kokabel to approach closer to him. ?If you didn't learn anything from this, you can not go around dropping the names of those who are either in league with our creators or our would be destroyers. To say the name of an angel, whether in heaven, or on earth in any rights or aspects, is to alert them to not only your presence, but those around you. And you just almost said the name of the bearded fallen angel we call the Treasure Hunter.? Josh didn't answer him. Kokabel put his arms across his rather cut chest. ?Mr. Brand you are suposed to be in Seattle, according to my granddaughter. But yet you are here showing disrespect to an elder, and her maternal grandfather. I though your Older brother would of taught you better.? ?Will you but out of this? This is as much as your fault as it is his. Suriel's meeting with her that caused this, he touched her, he forced her manifestation. ?He knew what he was doing, I am sure there was a reason for it.? ?And what about this?? Josh ripped the picture from Singing Eagle's hands. ?You could of saved Adam!? ?Yes I could of, but that wouldn't have saved Danielle. I could only save one of them. And the prophesy said that the Angel Born of The Earth would be saved then the predators came. Adam did what he was supposed to do. He gave his life so his little sister would live on.? ?Live on how? In Loneliness? Feeling unwanted, unloved and Hated? If had loved her, HOW could you have done that to her?? Kokabel was unmoved. ?I did what I had to, for her own protection. Even our most holy creator of our kind had to allow his only avatar son be sacrificed in his name on this world. She had to live on holy ground, it was the same reason that The Wrath had you meet on holy ground. It is safe. And it was also part of the prophesy.? ?Thats bullshit! And you know it! This game has almost cost her everything. She isn't a toy for your kind to play with. She isn't some messonic icon! She is a woman that has a life!? And that was it, Kokabel, the Virtue of the Stars got into Josh's face. ?Young ?Fire? Brand... Hear me now, and heed my words. Young Adam, did what he was supposed to do. He gave his life for his sister, in which allowed Danielle to grow up and come to her own. With out him my young friend, you would not have the woman you love do much! She would have been dead!? The words stung him like sea salt in a open wound, he knew the words to be true, but he gritted his teeth. In his heart he knew it was so, but his mind couldn't work past the visual of the little boy falling to the ground. Josh didn't flinch. this mind screamed out to say what he felt, for how much longer will he have her? 20 years? 30? While she will never age what will happen to him? Do you think she will want to keep him when he would grow old and she is still young and beautiful? With out Kokabel's blood they would have had a long normal life together. His blood has made this impossible. Josh gritted his teeth even more. ?But for WHAT? Some prophecy? Some fable? Some 'destiny'? What the hell is that all about, there is no such thing as pre-destiny to do something.? Kokabel studied him... and then calmed down as he spoke to him. ?You are using a white man's definition of a prophecy. Not ours. Not definition of those we came over here and taught when the likes of Cassius, Ishram and Cain having their little battles, and altering that which was true faith in this world, things have fallen to shit, literally shit.? ?So what's this definition of prophecy?? ?It's nothing more then a recipe. If you do these steps, this will outcome. It's like baking a cake at on a cosmic scale. Adam had to die to protect her. We know this. As did her parents. You think Daniel and Lynn didn't know what was going on? You saw the vision. Do you remember their reaction to Adam's drawings? That was me! I gave him those clues to draw! He was my messenger to him that they were close!? Josh was silent. ?And when they were busy falling for the trick of fate... thinking that just like everything else in the lies they had spread, that the male was the Destroyer reborn, I saved HER! And then I sequestered her in the only place that would hide her from them. They thought she was dead, until your people contacted Jaymes, the son of the Evening Star, as an expert on Native myths and gave her name!? ?Jaymes was what?? ?Is, he's still alive. I know this. But your allies have made him useless. Jaymes was the mortal born halfbreed son of the Evening Star and a woman he took by force. A witch, warlock, whatever you want to call them. And he was the one that killed Adam and the others. But that was also, part of the prophecy of the Destroyer Reborn. Now they come for her, and she'll awaken even more. The path is set, and the markers are there. When all is said and done, the recipe will be completed and...? ?What is this Destroyer Reborn thing?? ?Dani...? ?Destroyer? Why such a dark name for her prophecy?? Kokabel shakes his head. ?It's a very literal translation. When some of the fallen ones came to the new world to escape the hands that hunted them, Mikhail, Uriel, Lawrence, the captains of the holy host, some of us came after them. There was one in heaven that didn't like this. Heaven would not allow him to come forth under their orders to take out the enemy... this was a land of savages, heathens who did not fall under our god's protective hands. He found that to be worse then the sins of the fallen, themselves. To unleash a plague upon such simple people, and not protect them from it... he saw it as hypocritical of even our creator himself.... And he fell on his own, denying the Command of God. And he became a great Destroyer of the forces of the Evening Star and his ilk.? Josh actually looked intrigued. ?And what happened to him?? Kokabel shed a lone tear. ?Because he had defied them, they sent their forces. The archangels themselves came here and rended his body. It was in the south, near the white sands in the desert. Mikail... Micheal himself, tore his body asunder and left the remains on the earth. Dani is supposed to be this angel, this savior of her people's oppression against these beings and their followers who came from Europe. She is this angel reborn on the world by a small fragment of him that my son had found and injested.? Josh was shaking his head in denial. ?This is crap, this has to be just pure crap!? ?No, it's true. As long as the other signs continue on, it will happen. Dani is a blank slate...? ?What's that mean?? Kokabel, shirtless as he normally was, turned his head away from Josh, straining his neck. He pointed at what looked like a tattoo or brand on his neck. It was a stylized symbol, that had a lot of stars represented in it. ?This mark denotes me to my kind. It's my name in our angelic script. It's my ID Card if you will. Dani doesn't have one.? Josh hesitated. ?She's the first angel to ever be born without one. She has no angelic name. She is a blank slate, a wild card. She's the one.? He couldn't believe it. ?And you, my boy have to keep your head on straight if you're going to help her get that far. Some of your other endeavors will need to stop. All will work out in the end, I promise. Now, since I already told her that you weren't where you were supposed to be, go back and catch that plane, and go visit your mother for a day or two, then get back to my granddaughter. She needs you.? Josh listened and turned around to walk outside. He didn't remember much else that day. At least not until he hit the car rental place at the airport to turn the car back in. He had been in a stupor, absorbing all that he'd taken in. He was getting ready to get onto the golf cart that would take him back to the puddle jumper when he saw someone he recognized. ?YOU!? A woman who looked like she could be Dawn McFarlane's sister, with a deep, rich black mane of hair about her stepped into his view. She smiled and waved at him. He tried to resist, but found he couldn't. ?So, I see that things haven't been going well for you?? Josh's eyes narrowed. ?Oh, don't be such a worry wart. I came looking for you myself. Wondering if you'd finally got the vampire to embrace you, or if you were still willing to negotiate?? ?No, and I don't know. There is so much going on.? She smiled a purely seductive smile at him, reaching up and moving some of his long hair from in front of his face for him. ?Well, if you're interested, I think we could work something out. My boss would be very interested in meeting with you.? ?I don't know...? ?all you'd have to do is speak with him. See what he has to offer.? ?Okay? How do I get in touch with you? Cause I've got to be catching this plane.? She smiled wide with perfect white teeth. Although if Josh didn't know better, he'd think they may have points to them, almost like fangs. ?Well that's fine. Just say my name when you want to talk to me.? ?What's that?? ?Layla, just say my name and I'll know.? Josh looked at her suspiciously. ?It works for good purposes too. No cell phone needed.? She smiled at him again, and Josh just nodded in agreement. It going to be a four pill night to kill this headache. ~To be continued~
