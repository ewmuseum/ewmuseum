Behind Blue Eyes (Part 2.1: Christian Zenith)

15 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/15/10 "You... told me I would beat him." The strain in her voice told the story above a backdrop of raindrops and distant thunder. She was told that it was going to be hell, and at one point she honestly believed that she knew what that was. It wasn't until it was over that she learned hell isn't something that is overcome. Enduring hell didn't end with trumpets and fanfare. Hell is when you've given everything you have to give, and your efforts are scoffed at and tossed aside. Hell is when you realize that you didn't matter. "You... took everything away from me." A flash of light showed the figure of a young woman slumped at the end of a bed. Her long hair flowing down, covering her face from the light. She didn't deserve to see the light of day. Deep down in her heart she knew it to be true. She'd seen and done unforgivable things. Burned the bridge to everything that she knew for a shot at immortality. To be the best the world had ever seen. "You... lied to me." Her swollen upper lip curled into a snarl as she ran her fingers through her hair. Impatiently she waited for an answer that would never come. How coincidental was it that whenever she wanted answers it was gone and whenever she wanted silence she couldn't get away? "You... are not going to answer me are you?" Huffing the words between her lips she inhaled deeply, keenly feeling the deep bruises and scars that cover her otherwise immaculate body. Reluctantly she lifted the dead weight of her battered body to a vertical base. Slowly taking steps towards the window, a grimace crossed her face every time she planted her bare foot onto the cold hardwood floor. "Of course not..." Answering her own question she looked into the window, but not out into the beautiful thunderstorm that was taking place just a few inches away from her face... but at her face itself. A swollen lip was nothing compared to the bruises and scratches that were etched along her otherwise breath-taking features. She once remembered looking at these battle wounds with a sense of pride, morally satisfied with the sacrifices she'd made for the cause. This time however, she could only think of one thing to say: "You're a coward." "I will admit, I would have preferred to hear hello..." A familiar voice rang from just a few feet behind her, derailing her train of thought with a startling answer. She was so involved her own mind she'd forgotten where she was, and immediately realized she should've known better than to trust things would go as smoothly as Jacques said they would. A favor to an old friend is what he'd assured her, but she had a sneaking suspicious that this was how it was going to end. It was foolish to think that he'd pass up the opportunity to kick her while she was down. Hindsight is always fifty-fifty, they say. She looked into the mirror and watched as he slowly paced his way behind her, placing his icy cold hand on her exposed back. A chill ran up her spine as he moved his hand up and down just like old times. His best attempt to sooth the pains of a woman scorned. "...But I cannot say I am entirely surprised by your reaction. You sure seem to be though. See a ghost?" "You're not supposed to be here." Her answer was blunt but expected. Anyone who has ever been close to her wouldn't have expected anything less, and he was no different. "And you were not supposed to lose to LeStatt Knight," A smile crept across his reflection in the glass, "but I suppose we are both full of surprises." Behind Blue Eyes (An Exploratory Narrative Into The Life Of A Former Phenom) Part 2: Christian Zenith ---- There is a time I remember, not so long ago, where life felt like nothing more than a cruel game of building up dreams and sending them to the wayside. I remember not wanting to wake up in the morning, knowing by opening my eyes that I'd be reminded of how depressing my life had become. A life where a vacant building was the closest thing to home I'd ever have and a long forgotten dream of professional wrestling took a backseat to a dream of becoming something more. Anything was better than what I'd become: A drifter, deprived of any dreams or emotion. Living just to live. Mindlessly passing the time until I ceased to exist. That was until he came along. Like a knight in shining armor he saved me from the shambles and made me believe that I deserved something more. He lit the spark inside me that would soon glow brighter than anything he'd hoped for -- for the light would strike him clean and reveal him for what he truly was. A false prophet using charisma and influence to gather an army of the weak for his own personal amusement. I remember when I was a little girl, my mother made me memorize biblical passages so that I may one day "see the acts of God." When she wasn't busy telling me who I was or how I acted, she was spending her free time teaching me about the end of the world. She swore I'd see it in my lifetime and desperately wanted me to "be with my family" up in heaven. While I'm far from a Christian today, passages hammered into my tiny, developing skull still persist. I can remember clear as day... "...a king of bold countenance, one who understands riddles, shall arise. His power shall be great, and he shall cause fearful destruction, and shall succeed in what he does, and destroy mighty men and the people of the saints. By his cunning he shall make deceit prosper under his hand, and in his own mind he shall magnify himself." Every time I think about it, I'm certain I've met that man before. He saved my life. He helped me become what I am today. I've slept beside him and felt like I was immortal in his arms. I was mesmerized by everything he was and everything he promised me I could become... but all it took was a little time away to realize his true nature. He's no antichrist. He's just a petty, selfish, insecure man with a desperate need to play God. If you look up narcissism in the dictionary, you'll surely find a picture of Christian Zenith. ---- She could feel every set of eyes around her, following her as she walked past. With rolling suitcase in tow, none of them dared to speak a word. Halting on-going conversations and business instructions in mid-command at the very sight of her moving past. Her glare told the entire story. The bitter taste of defeat never sat well on the pallet of The Bulletproof Blonde, and this taste worse than most. So sure of her ability to get the job done, but at the end of the day she was just another victim of LeStatt Knight. Vivica acted like she couldn't hear and whispers and snickers as she passed, trying to keep her head up high the best she could. Keeping her body in one-piece was the bigger priority. This was the kind of match she was sure to feel in some form for the rest of her life. Just like her body, her life was also falling apart without any sense of control. When she walked out that exit door there was no place to go, no one to talk to. Another airplane flight, another hotel room. And no one to keep the staff from laughing hysterically at her expense. Her dark blue Ford Mustang was a rental, but it was recognizably hers. The backseat crammed with tiny keepsakes and personal belongings. If you were to open the trunk you'd realize it smelled distinctly like smoke. After all it was the only place she had to let her rescued items dry out. Perhaps that's why it was so easy for Jacques to pick it out of a lineup all marked by Enterprise logos. Leaning against the rental, he couldn't help but grin from ear to ear as Miss Valentine approached her vehicle. Not because he was relishing in defeat like her peers were so manic about, but because it'd been such a very long time since he'd seen her face. Jacques was a busy man, but also a lonely man. No wife or kids, he'd spent his entire life caring after the personal affairs of the Zenith Estate. He was far too busy for anything like a family. Vivica could certainly relate, looking up for the first time as her body was in so much pain she struggled to muster to strength to pull her keys out of her right jeans pocket. If she was capable of doing so, she would've jumped a thousand feet into the air when she spotted Jacques. It was almost like seeing a ghost, especially due to the fact that she'd never seen him outside. "That smile..." Vivica spoke immediately, relating Jacques smug expression to those she'd passed on the way out, "...you don't agree with them, do you?" "Never been a wrestling fan." Jacques answered, "So I wouldn't have the faintest clue what you're talking about." "Then what are you doing here?" "Well, I'd heard the bad news about your house on the local news." Jacques jumped right into the topic at hand. Being such a busy man usually meant a lack of time for catching up for formalities. Plus, if things were to go smoothly, he'd have plenty of time to catch up. "You're probably getting tired of staying in hotels every night, it was always the part you hated most about traveling... I thought I'd offer you a place to sleep. Still have a room with your name on it." "Heh, thanks but no thanks." Vivica scoffed as she dropped the massive cargo bag off of her shoulder, "I think I'd rather stay in a hotel." "You can't be serious." Jacques answered, "I know you and Mister Zenit..." "Chris." She interrupted. The name rolled off of her tongue as if she were talking about Satan himself. "Chris..." Jacques corrected himself, "...don't exactly have the greatest relationship, but your presence at the Zenith Manor has been greatly missed. I often find myself fielding questions about what you have been up to, or if there is any chance of you coming back." "There's none Jacques." Vivica used her hand to brace herself as she walked around the vehicle, managing to bit her lip to silence the cry that would've screamed off her tongue after turning the key into the lock on the trunk. "I'm sorry." "It's not exactly like you'd have to speak with him, or even see him for that matter." "There's always the chance." "But you lived there long enough to know how slim that chance is." Jacques retaliated quickly, "Between out-of-state conferences, business meetings, production, day-to-day operations, and Global Championship Wrestling... he doesn't sleep in his own bed more than once a month at most." As the smell of fire wafted up into the air and through Valentine's nostrils, the painful memories began to return. "I took a boat to get here in order to pick him up for a business meeting he had planned in Los Angeles tomorrow about a new project." Jacques continued to work on the now frozen-in-thought United States Champion, "He instead left early and boarded a flight to Tokyo. Said there was some kind of emergency stock conference or something like that. I'm afraid I'm thousands of miles away from home and the trip back would be awfully lonely." After the last few weeks of hell, it was those words that hit her in a place she could relate to. With the way things have fallen, the road was a lonelier place than normal. Often times her instinct to pick up her cell phone and arrange a get-together at a local restaurant brought tears to her eyes. There was no one to call anymore. "Tell me about it." Vivica responded, slamming the truck door shut. "Look at it as a favor to an old friend." Jacques walked up behind the woman he grew to know as The Black Mariah. The familiarity of that bearded smile made her feel a little more at ease. "It sure beats being lonely all the time." Taking a deep breath, she looked up into the light green eyes of the French-Canadian. Looking even deep down she didn't see the cynicism that seemed to accompany the stares of everyone else she knew. His eyes were friendly, warm, and welcoming. Taking a deep breath and a sigh, she slung the cargo back over her shoulder with an audible grunt, almost losing her balance and toppling back down onto the ground. "I guess one night wouldn't hurt anything." Vivica tried to force a smile but it wasn't happening, "I certainly can't stay here. I'll never hear the end of it." ---- Yet when I walked into Zenith Manor years later, it felt just like home. Like nothing had changed at all. There was something strange about it all that I just couldn't place my finger on at first, but I should've seen it coming a mile away. ---- TO BE CONTINUED...
