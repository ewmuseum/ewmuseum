History, Planning, and just strange!

3 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/09 The light of the moon shines down on the rocky coastline which lies before you. The waves crash over and over again as two figures simply stand watching them as they continue to roll in. The larger of the two simply does not move. His eyes focused on the moons reflection on the water. His eyes focused as he turns his head. “Let me tell you a story of my past and perhaps a past that many know all too well.” Clearing his throat he looks down at his feet. “Once there was a child who had to fight. Not because it was his destiny or his heritage. No it was a fight for survival on the streets during the cold winter. Nobody believed he would survive.” The other figure turns his head and looks. “Look Jorge is this going anywhere other than the mushy whoa is me?” Raising his hand Samuelsson cuts off Shaman. “Are you going to listen or whine like an old woman?” Shaman narrows his gaze and waves his hand nonchalantly. “Go ahead and finish then.” Samuelsson turns his eyes back to the sea. “You see that boy was me. Survival of the fittest and all of that happy stuff. It was cold, I can remember every single detail of those days. The grey cold, The slush, and dirty alleyway. There is one thing I want you all to remember. There will be no clown in a wolfs head claiming to be a hero of heroes or a simple clown. If he wants to prove himself he can prove one thing to me. He can prove he can bleed.” Shaman grins and looks at Samuelsson as Samuelsson nods. “The destiny of these people who try to make jokes out of others is a painful reminder of reality. Nothing ever goes according to plan does it. Let me finish my story.” Samuelsson clasps both of his hands together. Clearing his throat he wades out into the water. “Back in 1986 I watched as my grandmother withered away from a cancer. This cancer ate away at her spine. Literally eating one of her vertebrae to nothing, Leaving just a gap. I looked inside myself and pondered whether or not I was going to waste away at this moment.” Looking down at his opened hands he closes them into tight fists. He sneers slightly. “School came and went as I seemed to be the quiet gentle giant. It was a fight and I was not yet the warrior I am today. Oh they would soon be awake to see the beast within open up.” Raising his arms he looks towards the moon high above in the sky. He looks forward as a ship slowly appears and rolls into view. Its massive wooden dragon bow is clearly seen in the moonlight as it moves to shore slowly. Stepping forward Samuelsson places his hand upon the mighty wooden vessel. Over the side someone appears looking as Samuelsson nods and the person disappears again. “Senior year two men approached me and violently attacked me. I am huge and I thought to myself in that moment why do I cower. Suddenly I broke through the chains of fear and attacked.” holding his arm up he touches the hammer on the one. “I put both these men in the hospital and one is still recovering and that was back in 1992. I was recruited by a wrestling organization in late 1997 and after working out to become a strongman like the great Magnus Samuelsson I saw my calling. It was to be a wrestler, the scourge of all who come before me.” Samuelsson looks over at Shaman his eyes narrowing. “So that brings me to you where we had our first bout. You had never faced anyone like me and you never will again. I crushed you put you out for several months. In fact I was the one and only man to ever put you out for a long period of time. An accomplishment I enjoy showing why I am to be always underestimated.” Samuelsson reaches over the rail and into the boat pulling out a potato. He holds it up and points at it. “Years ago in your former home that recently closed you knew several people who worshipped this. A great potato wasn't that man once called Y.E.S? One of the managers for The Overlords of the Dark?” Shaman nods and looks around. “yes I forget you were one of the famed Four Horsemen. Then again you don't wrestle some lame ass idiot named the Wolfeinator who is 300 lbs of pure ass and wearing a wolf head that looks like it was stolen from some failed Disney project.” Shaman breathes deeply as he narrows his gaze at Samuelsson. Smirking slightly Samuelsson looks at Shaman his eyes focusing on him. “Didn't you ever pay attention to the idiots on the radio shows impersonating you? How about the mocking like you were theirs to use as a court jester? Shaman you are going to have to hurt someone seriously to make them understand. No truth is you have held back for too long. Where have you been hiding?” Samuelsson reaches back and slaps Shaman hard across the side of the head. “Smarten up and do something about them. You are not some sort of fool nor are you too proud to handle the situation like a warrior.” Samuelsson looks at Shaman who is emotionless looking at Samuelsson. Samuelsson reaches back again and swings. This time Shaman grabs the arm in mid swing. “You are playing with death Samuelsson.” A loud booming laugh can be heard. “Good you now are coming around and your focus is coming. You see my story is slow and incomplete. Your story on the other hand is grand and ever building. Now I have a purpose as a warrior. I am teamed with a warrior who knows what he has before him. I know what we want as Mortifera. Are you prepared to do what is necessary?” Shaman rubs his chin and looks at Samuelsson. He nods and simply looks forward. “This is a kind of torture isn't it?” Samuelsson looks at Shaman as he wades in the water near the wooden vessel. “No torture is wood splinters under the fingernails, hot pokers to the testicles, an enema with overfilled water balloons, needles through the eyelids, and of course my favorite hammer to little toes. That is torture what we have is a game of waiting and positioning.” Samuelsson looks at his hands again grinning. “Back to this guy called the Wolfeinator. Question though is there even forests in Kansas?” Samuelsson looks at Shaman. Shaman chuckles as he looks back at him. “Not really more plains than forested lands.” Samuelsson nods and looks around. “It is kind of funny when you come in here thinking that someone who plays the clown well gets placed into a battled with a beast like myself. You then question the sane aspect of the people who put him there. Truth is you want to know who the hell he has pissed off. Is it important or is it something one must be punished for. Idiocy is its own punishment. A man mocking beliefs of people and the paths they have chosen. Maybe knocking his teeth in will teach that lesson." Samuelsson pushes the bow of the wooden vessel away from him as he grunts pushing the weight as he yells towards the guy inside. "Turn the rudder and steer her towards the sand portion of the beach. Softer landing." Shaman walks down the beach a ways as he looks over at Samuelsson. "You may be right. I may have been holding back since Mayhem left. You are my ideal partner and it is time to show some of those nay sayers that we mean business and have an ultimate goal." Finally Shaman seems to be coming to his senses as he cracks his neck from side to side."When your done playing with your toy boat Samuelsson we have some business to attend." Shaman turns and walks off into the darkness as Samuelsson stares at him as he disappears. "Makes me wonder just what type of monster i have awakened. This will be good for us." Laughing deeply he turns and walks out of the surf. "Bad for those who stand in our path." Samuelsson slowly disappears up the beach in the same direction Shaman had headed off in. Moments later in a cabin near the sea..... Shaman is seen sitting at a table rolling out a large piece of rolled paper. Samuelsson walks in behind Shaman and comes over and takes a seat. He looks down at the paper as he eyes it carefully. "What is this?" Shaman looks up grinning. His eyes go back down to the paper. "New battle plan. A path from which very few choose to travel. One i have traveled before and would again with open arms." Tapping the paper Shaman looks it over carefully. "Remember Samuelsson i think on broader terms of combat and strategy. Everyone of our moves has got to be calculated and made known we have a plan. If we go in blind something will be a failure eventually." Samuelsson looks over the paper and nods recognizing some aspects. "So these are all the matches of Oblivion?" Shaman nods as he runs his finger down the list of matches. Each match seeming having several foot notes and such along with it. "Each match has our weaknesses and strengths. Each one has the exact timing and weak points of our opponents." Shaman pulls out another scroll from under the table and reaches under once again and pulls out files and videos. "These are our opponents in the tag division. These show the weaknesses and the strengths of each team. In fact the vanity of each team can be used against them. Take this one for example!" Samuelsson looks over at the tag team name and nods. "Too bad we don't have this in the singles department. I could use the inside track on Wolfeneitor." Shaman looks up and shakes his head. "You don't need anything against a guy who mocks us and does it in a blatant way. Time to shut him up Samuelsson and trust me when it is said and done all will be pleased." Shaman lifts the paper and holds it up as he motions for Samuelsson to step over and look. "One second Samuelsson we need to shut out prying eyes." Shaman reaches over and covers the camera lens. Camera suddenly goes to static as Shaman chuckles. "Nobody can see nor hear what we have planned." Camera fades to black. To be continued....
