One Master, One Apprentice. No More, No Less.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A Long, Long Time Ago Every so often, an epic discovery is made. One that rocks the very core of the multiverse, shaking the foundations and bringing forth near ruin to the lives of all Americans and the alternate reality counterparts. Sometimes it begins with just a small pebble, rippling across the fabric of reality until it creates an unstoppable tidal wave of destruction and despair. This is NOT one of those moments. "Two there shall be. No more, no less. One to embody the power, and the other to crave it," Markus Stone's voice escapes his lips almost as a whisper. The room bare of any furnishings, Markus sits cross-legged in a meditative pose. Candles placed in the four corners of the room provide the only illumination, yet the light fails to penetrate the shadow of the hooded cloak he wears. "Are you prepared?" Markus calmly asks. "Yes," comes the reply from Chris Storm. To the side of Markus sits Chris Storm, identically dressed, mimicking the same pose. "Yes, Master," Markus sternly rebukes his apprentice. "Do you know the Sith Code?" "Peace is a lie, there is only passion," Storm begins, monotonously reciting the ancient teachings. "Through passion, I gain strength. Through strength, I gain power. Through power, I gain victory. Through victory, my chains are broken. The Force shall free me." That may satisfy the casual instructor, but not when dealing with the Dark Side of the Force. One should remember, the correct answer is not necessarily the right one. "Do you understand the Code?" Markus asks almost simultaneous to Chris' completion of the code. Chris pauses and thinks for a moment, tilting his hooded head towards his master. "The Code is more than just a mantra. The Code is the way. The one true path. Without understanding, you will never be able to overthrow me. If you cannot do that, you are not a worthy apprentice," the Master states, upset by the pause Chris takes. "Master, my emotions, my passion, give me the power to call upon the Dark Side. The Jedi are emotionless, and therefore unable to fully wield the powers of the Force," Chris quickly recites. Markus pauses for a moment, then stands. "Good, you are beginning to understand," he compliments his apprentice, while beckoning him to rise. Chris slowly stands, facing his master. Reaching into his sleeve, Markus pulls out a silver handle. With a press of his thumb, a red beam extends. Chris' face, bathed in red light, beams. "Fucking awesome!" exclaims the Last Great Technical Wrestler. "Right?" Markus replies, grinning. "EBay, seventeen grand!" After a moment, Chris Storm frowns a big. "What about me?" he asks in a sulky voice. "Dude, c'mon. Don't cry about it," Markus answers with a shrug. Pausing for dramatic effect, Markus tosses a second lightsaber handle to Storm. Catching, Chris almost dances in his excitement. "SWEET!" they simultaneously cry. Flicking his blade on, Chris jumps in the air with a triumphant fist pump as he also owns a red blade. "It's like a Christmas decoration, only dangerous and deadly!" Chris barely contains his joy. "Yeah, but dangerous dude. We gotta be careful," comes the warning from Markus. Along with a sarcastic chuckle, of course. Seriously, do you expect the two of them to be careful? Hells no! The two Sith practitioners assume battle ready stances, pulling their lightsabers horizontally across their shoulders, two handed grip, blades pointed away from each other. "I see you also plan on using the Shii-Cho form, my apprentice," Markus states, returning to his Sith Master persona. "Yes Master, and I will strike you down," Chris states as a matter of face. Without another word, Markus swings his lightsaber horizontally, clashing against Storm's blade. Spinning away, Chris lets his blade swing in a low arc, but Stone catches it with his own deflection, then shoots a riposte towards the ribs of his apprentice. With lightning reflexes, the apprentice halts the attack with a thrust kick, knocking his master back. "Very good, but can you defend against the Makashi?" the Master asks, adopting a single handed low guard. Pointing his blade down, and placing his legs at shoulder guard, Markus pulls his hood back and smiles at his apprentice while changing his grip on his flat lightsaber handle. Darting forward, Markus unleashed a diagonal overhand sweep, nearly decapitating his opponent. Lucky for him, Chris rolled to the side and quickly reassumed his ready position. The smell of singed hair decorated the air. Without hesitation, Storm immediately went on the offensive, mixing high and low swings, left side, right side, trying to whittle down his Master one charred limb at a time. Slowly sliding his feet back, Markus met every attack with a parry, waiting for the opening he knew that his apprentices' Form I would give him. Storm continued his advance, moving to his sides with each strike, cutting off his master's angles of attack. Not being knowledge in the Makashi, Form II, Chris was painfully oblivious to the fact that his master's style was solely intended to counteract Shii-Cho. This slowly became apparent as Markus began to counterattack. Deflecting a high thrust aimed for his shoulder, Markus spun to his right, striking to Storm's left arm. Not waiting for the block, Markus immediately spun back left, aiming low this time. Using the slowly combat form, Chris barely turns his blade down in time to block the blow. The instant the blades connect, Stone arcs his blade almost directly straight up, nearly ending the battle. Leaning back on his heels, Storm avoids the strike, but does not avoid the palm strike that lands on his sternum. Flying off his feet, Chris crashes to the ground. "Good, you are improving my apprentice," Markus praises Storm while shutting off his lightsaber. "Perhaps there is hope for you yet." Spring to his feet, Chris charges towards Markus, who holds out his hand in a gripping motion. Storm's lightsaber clatters to the ground, deactivating, and he clutches at his throat. "St... stop... stop..." Chris gasps in between choking sounds. "It? It worked?" Markus whispers, eyes bulging out of their sockets. Markus lowers his hand quickly, not wanting to kill his tag team partner. He stands still for a moment, completely shocked. Chris' coughing soon fades, giving way to outright laughter. "Dude, I so had you!" Chris says while doubled over in laughter. "Ah man, I thought it was gonna work this time!" pouts Markus. Storm looks at the sadness on his partners face, and almost regrets fooling him. Almost. "You should have seen your face. Almost as bad as the first time you saw a pair of tits!" Chris points out immediately before breaking out into uncontrollable laughter again. "Dammit, it's gonna work one day..." Markus begins to trail off. "I hope." "It that, A New Hope, AHAHAHA!" Chris continues to mock. "No, it's on old one. I've wanted to do that for years," Markus answers, oblivious to the joke Storm just made. Storm looks at his partner, straight face. "Dude, are we both that retarded?" "No dude, retarded people wouldn't be able to learn lightsaber combat forms off of the internet," Markus states. Scratching his head, Chris wonders if that's true. "I wonder if that's true," Chris wonders aloud. "I wonder," Markus begins wondering as well. Soon the two are both wandering in the wonder, wondering if mentally challenged people really can wield a wonderful lightsaber. Soon the wondering ceases, and the Greatest Tag Team Eva~! wanders back to reality, no longer wondering about anything at all. "That was wonderful," Markus thinks out loud, still enjoying the Star Wars act the two just went through. "I wonder," Chris begins. "No, no more wandering or wondering or one-dering," Markus interrupts his friend before the sad mental journey can begin anew. "Did you just use the number one as a wondering?" Chris asks his partner. "Yeah, like that band from that movie, That Thing You Do. It's gonna catch on," replies Markus. The two stand still, shuffling their feet. "Markus," Chris begins. "What?" Markus interrupts his beginning friend again. "Did we just pretend to be Sith in a room full of nobody but us, for no reason what so ever?" asks the Last Great Technical Wrestler. "Well, duh~!" Markus says, adopted a facial expression mimicking that of a mentally challenged child holding a lightsaber. "Why?" Chris asks, voicing his doubts. "Well, you know... It's what cool kids do," Markus answers, trying to dismiss the question with a bit of bandwagon peer pressure. "Um... Well, yeah, I guess if I'm doing it then the cool kids probably wanna do it to," Chris replies, realizing just how many kids in the world will lightsaber battle just to be like him. "Besides, this way I finally get to act like top dog in our dynamic duo," adds the Man Formerly Known as Zero, or MFKZ for short. "You know, MFKZ has a good ring to it," Chris says dismissively. "What? MFKZ? Wtf are you talking about?" Stone asks his partner, utterly confused as to what the hell he is referring to. "Dude, you just text-talked me," Chris accuses his partner. "No, I most certainly did not," Markus denies. "Yes you did." "Na-uh" "Uh-huh." "Na-uh" "Uh-huh." "Na-uh" "Uh-huh." "Na-uh" "Uh-huh." "Dude, what the fuck are we talking about?" Chris asks. "Not sure man, it felt kinda like filler to me," answers the other half of Banned & Exiled~! "I agree, maybe we need to sleep on it a bit," suggest Chris Storm. Markus pauses, and thinks for a moment, then nods his head in agreement. "Just don't forget. Peace is a lie, there is only passion," Markus reminds Chris Storm. "I agree," Chris agrees. "You do?" Markus asks incredulously. "Damn right, I'm SO full of passion, I'll be the master in no time!" Chris answers with a triumphant fist pump. "Yeah, the Master of Bates," Markus shoots back. "Hell yeah, I will own the Bates-es! Air Five!" shouts Chris Storm. Rearing back the two shoot their hands forward to high five each other, instead dishing out the coolest air five in the history of the multiverse. Will the dynamic duo achieve mastery over the Dark Side? Will the apprentice rise up to strike down his master? Will the two members of the most dominant tag team in the history of history have an intelligent conversation with meaning and depth? Well, that one is certainly a resounding no, but stayed tuned for the next installment of "As The World Turns." Er... I mean, Deep Space Nine... No, wait, Gladiators! Shit... Dude, we gotta find a name for this shit.
