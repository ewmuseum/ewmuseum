Stoop

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/05 Europe is a big place, but to dumb Americans who think it’s all the same you can say things like, “He sat on a stoop in Europe,” and no one would think twice about the vague statement. He sat on a stoop in Europe. See? You don’t care where in Europe. You just want to know what he’s doing. Oh, you probably also want to know who he is. Yeah, that might help with your deductions of the situation. Like if he was an accountant he would be sitting on a stick while sitting on a stoop or if he was the Slacker Hero Heeeeeey-Man he would be slouching. But this man who is currently sitting on the stoop is not either of these souls. He is a lost soul. At one point in time he was the darkest of the GCW dark gimmicks. The only man who would ever come close was The Dark Messiah, but when this man was surrounded by gloom and black clouds TDM was simply The Ticking Time Bomb. In the archives of GCW HQ in Columbus, Ohio lie tapes of a GCW stable that was second only to System-X. It was the Militia and they carried with them broken hearts and scrambled brains. They were crazy kids. And later the man of darkness lived his destiny to become a GCW World Heavyweight Champion! The Dangerous Games battle royal was his throne and he was the king! On top of the world, he was. Forever plastered in the main event. That man is the man sitting on the stoop in Europe right now. His name is Twizzy and he is holding up a New York Times. “Oh, Satchel Pootch, you’re so funny!” he squealed while kicking his legs at the ground in childlike glee. A main eventer. Now that spot is coming into question by a young gun. Will Twizzy be able to hold on or has his time to go out to pasture finally come up? Only Warpath will tell?
