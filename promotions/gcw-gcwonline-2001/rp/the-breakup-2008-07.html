The Breakup

July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July Phil Atken and Dirk Dickwood were sitting in the offices of Dirk Dickwood's Totally Awesome Talent Agency. The room was smoke laced, as Dirk chomped down on another cigar. Dirk sat at the head of his maple wood desk, hand crafted by orphans over twenty years ago, Atken guzzled down his freshly poured cup of coffee, he slammed the empty cup down on the desk, satisfied and refresh. The tension was so thick you couldn't even cut it was a katana. On one side of the desk sat Dickwood, vexed, confused, lost in a world of his own, on the opposite sat Atken, focus, determined, he knew what must be done. Atken: You know Dirk, this has been a long relationship, but I think we both know it's flawed. It's riddled in failure, riddled in errors and riddled in all manners of STDs. Dickwood: Sorry about that last one, Candisqua SWORE she was clean. Dirk gave a reassuring smile, but it wasn't reassuring enough, not this time. Phil could see through the lies, he knew he could no longer trust the man, he knew that Dickwood was nothing more than a web of lies, a web that he must single handedly attack with the duster of justice. Atken: Yes, always a good idea to trust to word of a hooker. They are known for their innate morality after all. Dickwood: She seemed trustworthy to me. Then again, the always do. Even that one who tied me up and stole all my money. She seemed even more trustworthy the second time I saw her... Dirk attempted to finish his though, but Phil had heard enough of the thought. The thought was a distraction, a derailed train on the rail track of justice, and Phil Atken was just the man to put that train back on the tracks. Atken: You know this is exactly why your current long term relationship was with a fucking Viking right? Dickwood: Don't you dare badmouth Helga, she is a goddess. The only thing that ever cause me any harm was her metallic pointed bras. Atken: That's because it was the SIZE of you. Dickwood: Oh sure, make fun of the midget. Dickwood attempted to gain sympathy for being a man of a shorter stature than many others, but Atken knew better. He'd seen enough James Bond films to be aware that midgets were evil creatures, the kind that would attempt to blow up your speed boat while you screwed the brains out of a busty blonde. He wouldn't let Dickwood sink his ship. He would screw the brains out that blonde. Atken: Would you rather I make fun of your video tape collection? I mean seriously, what sort of man needs eight mace based porn tapes, and I don't mean to OUCH OUCH MY EYE KIND, I mean the HOLY SHIT THAT METAL BALL HAS SPIKES kind. Metal balls, just like Atken had to confront Dirk Dickwood, who was perhaps one of the most powerful men working for the Dirk Dickwood Totally Awesome Talent Agency. Dickwood: A renaissance man. A man of the world. Atken: A man who is about to have one of his clients leave him? Dickwood: You're breaking my heart Phil. The broken heart of Dickwood was of no concern to Atken, he needed to press on. Dickwood had broken many more in the past on his own accord, perhaps this was just a slice of the sweet justice pie that Phil Atken had just cooked up. Atken: I'm sure that the new shipment arriving on Tuesday will somehow mend that broken heart. Dickwood: You will not believe the things those ladies do to a John Kerry inflatable doll and I thought I'd seen everything with that Michael Dukakis doll. Atken: Any reason you enjoy videos on ladies enjoying sexual intercourse with failed Presidential candidates. Dickwood: I enjoy failure? Atken: This is exactly the reason I'm breaking up with you. Dickwood: What about our Brosef Rings? Atken: ... those don't exist. Little did Phil know they truely did exist, in the heart of true brosefs. They however never found their way to the ring fingers of Mr. Atken and Mr. Dickwood, as the rings have the power to detect true brosefs, of which just like Phil, they knew they were not. They knew of the evil secrets hidden within the darkest parts of Dickwood's soul, the things he had seen. They too were aware of his vast porn video collection. They are the most advanced rings human culture had ever seen. Dickwood: Then what is this I'm wearing right now? HUH? Why don't you tell me that Mr. Smartyrings? Atken: Your wedding ring. Dickwood: Oh... I forget about that. Atken: You forgot you were married? Dickwood: Well, when Helga is away on long raping and pillaging voyages it slips my mind. She sent my a postcard the other day. Seems she was having a great time burning her local villages down to the ground. Atken: Oh that crazy Helga, what will she do next. Dickwood: That's the thing that terrifies me the most. What Helga actually did next was something both Dickwood and Atken had made a solemn vow never to repeat and despite the end of their business relationship, neither has broken to this vow to date. It was only referred to as "the unspeakable evil" when either man was questioned about the events. Rumour has it, it took an entire armed patrol just to stop her for a mere few seconds. That however would be a different story for a different time, this was the story of good triumphing over evil. The mastermind Dirk Dickwood finally been taken down by the Saviour, Phil Atken. Atken: Anyway, I'm leaving the agency. Dickwood: So you said. That kinda sucks. Atken: Well, I just want to try it on my own for a while. Dickwood: Well, you know I'm always here if you ever feel the need to return. That or just to watch my video tape collection. Atken: I think you are the only one who will ever watch those videos Dirk. Dickwood: You'd be surprised. Atken: Anyway, I've got to head off, got a plane to catch. Catch up for drinks next week? Dickwood: Sounds good. Atken grabbed his trenchcoat and fedora, and bolted for the exit. He had taken down the evil empire but he wasn't going to remain in the building to admire his handy work, he knew that Dickwood may rise again, he knew Dickwood would never truly be defeated, especially with Helga lurking around the corner. Now you know the tale, the tale of Phil Atken triumphed over the evil Dickwood Empire. The day he finally brought it down from the inside.
