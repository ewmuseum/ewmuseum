EVL Introductions: Erik

28 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/2005 A rythmic beat fills the air, dancing and playing among the spiraling embers drifting skyward from the the massive bonfire. The sun dissappearing on the horizon watched on with it's last sleepy glances as two men took center point within a circle of spectators. The two men, one white and one black shook hands with a nod and a smile. Dressed in a pair of loose black pants, the dark haired and fine featured european gives his shoulders a quick stretch. His opposite bounces in tune to the beat, the crowd stomping in pace, they act. Bouncing from side to side a kick is ducked and a backflip landed. African women and children start to sing as both men roll into a stance, they dance more than fight, moving and flowing in perfect harmony, master and pupil. The pupil rolls his shoulders and leans back under a spinning heel kick, dropping to his back he springboards back onto his feet in time to spinning away from cresent roundhouse, they come about fast, as the musics tempo increases. Faster and harder until the european catches his opponent with a leg sweep and he falls to his back. The drums stop and the villagers continue to chant. Speaking in his native tongue, the black man smiles and accepts the white mans hand. 'I didn't teach you that.' 'I just taught it to you Umbuwama.' He replies in the same foreign dialect. The pair are siezed by hands and led towards the warmth of the fire, bowls of water placed in their hands. The orange glow cast by the dancing flames casts a majestic tone to the evening. 'Today your training here ends.' Umbuwama says in english, his accent heavy but his mastery of the language apparant. 'There is nothing more I can teach you, Capoeira is not ment to mastered, it's ment to be felt. In your soul, and in your heart.' A great necklace of beads and teeth is strewn about the white man's neck, and then another as the entire village bustles about the two men. Bidding his pupil to kneel Umbuwama pulls an iron rod from the coals of the massive fire. 'You fight like a lion, with courage and honor. And so the lion shall always be close to heart.' With a sickening sizzle the brand is pushed against the europeans left pec, wisps of smoke coiling into the air. His face twists in pain but he does not cry out, and when the offending instrument is pulled away the circular design of a roaring lion is forever burned upon his heart. The edges are burnt and the center raw, smoke still lingers upon the scorched flesh. The edges soften swell up as the center becomes coated in a thick scab. It lightens and heals into a scar before being covered by a thick cotton vest of the darkest black. Bouncing from foot to foot his fists snap up before him defensively. The asian man before him darts in quickly and launches a quick pair of jabs, the white man blocks the first, catches the second and sends a hard knee into his opponents chest, the asian man stumbles back and barely rolls out of the way of a flying jump kick. The european lands and they circle slowely. Both dressed in the fighters gee, both with black belts around their waists. The man enters with a high side kick and the black clothed warrior side steps easily, jumps and launches a spinning back heel that connects hard with the asian warrior's shoulder. The european harries him with lefts and rights, he struggles to keep the offending fists at bay and catches one, pulling hard he hip tosses the european down to the mat. But the dark fighter is well versed, rolling with the impact he's on his feet, not given the time to gain his balance he continues to roll and corkscrew spins away from a brutal straight kick. He catches and lands, drawing his left arm to deflect a jab and sweeping his right down to catch the expected side kick, hooking the leg he grabs the man and pulls him around and slams him into the mat hard. With a yelp of pain a word in chinese is shouted and the european releases his victim. Drawing himself up straight he helps the light clothes asian to his feet, they square up; put fist to palm and bow. 'You have a been an amazing partner.' The european says in crisp chinese. 'I will take the test again against another student, until then I will continue my training.' An older man dressed in long colorful robes approaches the two, the asian fighter bows and takes his leave. 'You have mastered everything I could teach you. You have been an exemplary student.' The student and the master bow in turn before the old man passes a black and orange belt to the european. 'You have a dragon in your spirit young warrior, and it has accepted you as the host of its strength. Never abuse this privilege and it will always be there to deliver you from harm.' The european bows low, his long locks of dark hair falling about his face, stringy with sweat. Straightening his back he throws the vest from his shoulders and lets his pants fall to his ankles. He stands in boxing shorts and pulls a pair of bright red gloves over his hand as the crowd snaps pictures and scream for more abuse. He weaves left and launches a low body shot against his larger black opponent. He weaves right under a south paw hook and launches another body shot. He comes back around with his right and slams a cold one across the mans jaw. Hopping from foot to foot as the man stumbles back and falls against the ropes. The ref counts but the screaming crowd already know what to expect. Wiping sweat and blood out of his right eye he waits for the ten count and raises his gloved hands in the air. His hands fall and the big red gloves are replaced by smaller tighter ones, his hands are spread farther apart as he hops in close against a lanky brazilian. They connect hard with side kicks but the european lands the first right punch to his opponents face, he grabs a hold of the dazed man and throws him to the ground. Falling on him he lands a triplicate of ferocious right hands into the man's head before a bell is rung and a referee descends on the pair to pull them apart. His bloody fist is raised in the air as the crowd around the outside of the octagon cage scream and stomp and chant. 'And your winner...!' 'E-V-L! E-V-L!' 'Erik Van Lorne!'
