State of Your Sanity

June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: First Week of June "This is my state of your sanity" What...What have I become? By the Lord HImself, please, answer me! I am YOUR CHILD! How can you deny me in this moment of need? Tears develop in Zacharia Taylor's eyes and fall onto his blood stained hands. The tears rejuvenate the crimson flakes on his hands and turn them into small red streams running through the crevices of his scarred hands. Do You expect me to suffer? Have you known that You would turn Your back on Your child? What is it that You ask of me? What is it that You want me to do? I've never betrayed my Lord...I've never betrayed You. Taylor collapses onto the grass of the park that he has come familiar with as his home, his sanctuary he resides to every night that he spends in Clifton. The furious stinging in his hands causes concern in the back of his mind. Despair is God's remedy for pain. They are reciprocals, canceling one another out. Pain for another Pain. His despair is running thin, only one can take their own sympathy enough before he becomes enraged. I repent, my Lord. I repent... Taylor's hands become fists, powerful entities of his might, ponding into the grass with all his strength. Every time he lifts his hands a trail of freshly spilt blood flies through the air. It is not until the warm crimson liquid touches his face that he realizes what the stinging in his hands was from. He raises his hands in the air, presenting them to the light of the lamppost to the left of him. Seeing the blood run down both arms ignites a rage inside of him. "He hath no fury until his own blood is spilt. Hath rage and mercy to all those that are evil in his presence. Blood of the innocent shall no longer be spilt." Rage. Rage is no expression as to how I feel Father Emanuel. You... You have given me this rage. You have suppressed this rage inside of me by the wrong means, Father. No, no longer shall I submit to your teachings. It is my turn. It is my turn to act as I please. Taylor stands up, letting his veins release onto the grass. He walks down the concrete path that leads off into the street. The moon becomes partially covered by the unfamiliar clouds, casting disfigured shadows onto the world. A man walks past Taylor, his image no more than an outline dancing to the moon's light. He takes no form but that of a shadow that is uncomfortable with its own form. Taylor feels the man's stare emanating from this shadow. After a scuffling of feet behind him, Taylor feels cool steel press up against his neck. "If you fucking keep still, and do what I say, there will be no harm. Put your fucking hands up!" Taylor closes his eyes and looks towards the sky. HIs rage slowly building up inside him, his heart pounding blood throughout his body. Adrenalin streamed through his muscles, making him shake with power. "It is my turn to act as I please." The blade slowly falls away from his neck and presses against his back. Taylor grinds his teeth in anticipation, playing possible actions out in his mind. "Do you have anything in your back pockets? Money, wallet, jewelry?" "My back right pocket is a book that you may be part of." Taylor says in a cool voice, still with his eyes closed. "What? No, your money, pal!" Taylor feels the point of the knife enter his skin so slightly that if felt like a mosquito bite. The hot blood flows from the wound, down his back. "That was a mistake..." "Give me your fucking money, turn around and slowly reach into your pockets and give me your money." Taylor turns around and opens his eyes with a smile on his face. He punches down on the guys hand, knocking the knife to his fee, and reaches forward and grabs the neck of his sweatshirt. Taylor thrusts in and head butts the guy in the knows, exploding his nose with blood. As tears filled his eyes and blood soaked his shirt, Taylor stood there, transfixed by the blood and the pain that plagued the man's face. "Get on your knees." The man complies and cups his hands over his nose. Taylor pulls out his cross from under his sweatshirt and starts lipping a prayer until the man calls out. "God, help me. I'm sorry, God. Please, don't kill me..." Taylor stops and looks down at the man, astonished. "You commit sin, let the greed overcome you, instill fear into your innocent victims, and then you ask for His help? His salvation? Do you really think that you deserve what the Almighty God can give you? Repent! Repent and follow His creed and you shall live." "I do, I do! Please don't kill me. I am sorry. Please forgive me!" Taylor blesses himself and then the man on his knees before him. He reaches down and puts his hand on the trembling man's head. "Father, please forgive Your child that has chosen the life of sin. Accept him back into Your graces and show him the life of virtue and righteousness. Protect him from the de..." Taylor stops as the knife that was on the ground next to the man enters into his forearm of the hand that was resting on the man's head. Taylor pulls out the knife as the guy stands there, thinking he dominated Taylor. Rage builds to the point of explosion in his head and he lashes out by stabbing the man in the chest, opposite his heart. The man falls to the ground, gasping for air, wincing as he takes every breath. "...Protect him from the devil and accept him on the path to the Almighty Lord. Let him be saved by Your Son, Jesus Christ. Hear his prayer my Lord. In Nomeni Patri Et Fili Spiritus Sancti." Taylor walks away after blessing the hurt man in the grass. In the distance he notices his bag sitting against the tree he was once sitting by and grabs it before he heads out into the street. ******* The walls of Saint Mary's Hospital in Passaic is decorated just like any other hospital; plain. The white walls with the rubber bumpers on the sides for the gurneys occasionally throwing in a pastel teal and pink streaked wallpaper brings out calming moods of insanity. The room that Taylor resides in, waiting for the release of the doctor is in the ER, being graced with the decoration of green walls and green tile. The thought of it being green makes it seem easier to clean off any sprays of blood. Taylor's arm is stitched up and now wrapped excessively. Both his hands have stitches in different places. The doctor wouldn't release Taylor until he eats something and proves he can walk on his own, the only problem being that there was a car accident and nobody is paying much attention to Taylor in his bed. They had to order a blood transfusion because he collapsed when he entered the ER, blood soaked his clothes and was all over the floor of the lobby. Taylor took the opportunity to grab something to eat from the refrigerator and got his bag from the lockers on the side of the ER. He pulls out his book, the pencil that is stuck on the side, and reaches for the phone. "You better have a good fucking reason to be calling at this hour because..." "Bagwell, its Taylor." "What's up kid? Pay phones work this late at night?" "Real funny, Chris. Actually..." A nurse runs by the open curtain of Taylor's room screaming for an IV. "Where the hell are you?" "I'm in St. Mary's Hospital. Some shit happen in the park tonight, got a little cut up." Taylor flexes his hands, watching blood seep into the clean, white bandages. The sight of the blood makes him tilt his head in curiosity. "Must have been a hell of a night for you to admit yourself there. What the hell happen?" "I called you to find out about my next match. I was out of the loop after the PRIME match, haven't been in touch..." "Oh, you didn't hear? Caldera didn't tell you after the last World Wide?" "Tell me what?" "You're defending the belt against the winner of a Battle Royal! That's sick man it should be exciting out in Vegas with all the fuckin hookers and shi..." "So you don't know who I'm facing?" "<Laughing> Well, it's one of the nine poor bastards that is still sitting up after the match!" Not knowing who he is up against. But, he has to know. How can Taylor prepare mentally for the match if he does not know who he is facing? Why would Caldera do that? It was ok for the Television Title Match with him and three others, but it was really just a match against Fox. This was against who? He doesn't have to physically face them, just one. What if it's not who he expects? "Bagwell, how could he do this? How am I supposed to..." "Get ready? Just do your thing and you will be fine. Pray a little, it usually helps." "This isn't funny. I have to know what I am up against, I have to meditate on it, have to make sure that I am doing the right thing." "What is a right thing? You are your religious shit. Wake up! You are the Television Champion, you are going to have to fight people that you don't want to. You will have to go up against some that you will never consider evil. That is how it is. THAT'S SHOW BUSINESS!" "You wanted to put me in here. You said it would help with pent up aggression. Its not helping, I think its actually making it...worse..." "You are getting a hot head, Taylor. You need to make amends with that. You have to embrace who you are. You are not only the Mercenary of God, but you are also a GCW fighter." Bagwell pauses for a moment. Conviction is in his voice, but conviction of a fighter or friend? He isn't his side-kick in the Movement anymore. "Once you come to terms with that, you will take on the challenges ahead." A nurse steps into the room that Taylor was in and looks at him on the phone. She lips "I will come back" and steps out once again. He looks back down and stares at the words written in the book on the open page. "There is no sin in following one's own heart, unless it falters into the realm of great sin." Taylor looks up once again and fires back at Bagwell. "So what is my challenge? Who is in the Battle Royal?" "Alright, its: Kaden Calix, Phil Atken, Dan Black, Jacob McKail, Keegan O'Conner, Jake Reed, Rikki Roxx, Patrick Vander, and Daria Ngozi. Hope I didn't miss anyone..." Taylor wrote down the names. The list seemed like it would never end. He played the names over in his head, only recognizing a few. He remembers writing down Calix, Roxx, and Atken, but not the others. He stares down at two all too familiar names on the paper, Dan Black and Keegan O'Conner. Keegan gets his rematch while Dan Black gets his opportunity. "Ya there Taylor?" "Yeah, I was just going over the names in my head. We don't need to talk about your boy Black or Keegan, but who are the others?" "Alright, we can take off Rikki Roxx, Patrick Vander, Jake Reed, and Daria Ngozi off the list. Vander, Reed, and Ngozi are all brand new, so I wouldn't expect much of them, especially being up against a pretty stacked Royal. And I think Rikki Roxx was thrown in as a joke, so its the other guys that you have to worry about." Bagwell hesitates waiting for some kind of reaction, but all Taylor did was cross off the names he mentioned. He patiently waits for Bagwell to start again, not wanting to feed into his games. "Alright, some stuff that you may want to know. Reed is up McKail's ass for whatever reason, something about advancing careers and some garbage. So that might take out some of McKail's flair. But McKail is no champ by any means. He seems tough, but really hasn't proved himself around the ring. But his animosity for Reed and towards Calix for his last loss might take him out all together." "Alright so I'm not so worried about them, I know that Calix is doing really well, undefeated I think?" "Yeah, I think he is being overshadowed by O'Conner and Black in this match, so you might have to watch out for this one. I don't really know anything about him though, he doesn't really do much in GCW yet. He won a championship over at Global, and he is 3-0 here so far. The part that you might have to look out for is that him and O'Conner could team up and take out Black and Atken. They were on a team against Lestrange and DeBough and won, nobody saw that coming. That might take some flair out of those two." "He's a big guy too from what I remember. I have written down that he is 6'5 and about 270. Why is everyone so much bigger than I am?" "I hear that if you pray on your knees too much it stunts your growth." With a silence that was painful for Taylor to maintain, Taylor moved on. "Atken isn't much of anything either. He really hasn't done much in a while. He made a pitiful attempt for the US title, and really hasn't done much. Keegan beat him a while back as well. So, it comes down to Calix, Black, and O'Conner?" "Yeah, pretty much, and they all outsize you by quite a bit. You know how tough it was facing O'Conner. He nearly beat you if it wasn't...But you might have..." "Yeah, your Movement interfered with my match. You let Black get into the ring and take me out. Everyone thinks that Keegan should have won and Black stole the match from him. So He might team up with Cailix to take out Black. I can't picture him getting much of a chance against me. Alright, so it looks like it goes down to Keegan, I can deal with that. If I have to face him, I know all about him already, he is just as bad as I thought. Alcohol takes precedent. He looks for the easy way out." "Alright, sounds good then, but, in all honesty, don't be surprised when you see who wins this. You can easily lose your title." "I know Chris, I know." "Just, take it easy alright. Make sure that you are ready for the match. I will see you in Vegas. Make sure you nurse those wounds." "I know man, you don't need to get.." "Zacharia, I am saying that as a friend. This is a big match for you and you are sitting in a hospital bed. I will see you in Vegas, alright?" The phone hits against the receiver on the nightstand. Sympathy is not something that Taylor wants to hear. All he got was sympathy from Father Emanuel. Sympathy got him where he is today. He thinks back to when he was little, everyone wanted to be a crying shoulder for him, but what he wanted was to move on. He knew his place, he knew where he stood in God's eyes, but he couldn't get there with Father Emanuel consoling him all the time. He closed his book with the pencil stuck in the page. Taylor stands up and changes into a fresh pair of clothes. He puts the cross back around his neck and kneels beside the bed in prayer. Once he stands and turns around, the nurse came in to check on him. "Oh, sir, I am sorry, but we needed to see you eat and make sure you're alright to get going." "I ate, the wrappers and carton are right over there on the nightstand. I obviously can walk fine. Can I go?" The nurse is taken back by his forwardness and reaches out to take his hospital gown. "Yes, you may go sir. Please go out the right door and then down the hall to the right to exit the building. Stay clear of the nurse station, it is full of kids from that awful car crash." Taylor nods to the women and picked up his bag. He couldn't help but smile at her when she gave him a smile. He walked out of his little room and headed around the doctor's station. He hates hospitals. He has very rarely gone to them, only when he absolutely had to. Father Emanuel used to patch him up when he was hurt, but he rarely needed to go. He has a hard time coping with death of innocence. He looked around a hospital and saw nothing but grief and sadness. The last time he was here was when he severely burned his chest on the burning barrel so many years ago. The scar tingles at the thought of that night. Right before he walked out into the hallway, he heard the painful sound of a flatline in the bed that was next to the door. Nurses rushed over to the curtain and ripped it open. Taylor hesitated and looked into the bed. When the nurse moves out of the way, Taylor becomes stiff and unable to move his body. The teenager laying in the bed was himself. His chest wears the same scar across his chest, his arm had the same stitched gash on his forearm, his hands were similarly scarred and bandaged up. His eyes were peaceful, yet strained with pain and anguish. His body lay loose, yet his muscles were tense and his veins popping. The boy's hand falls open as a nurse presses down on his chest, trying to necessitate him, revealing a cross with a matching scar on his open palm. The boy is him, he is the boy. This is a sign from God, but what is He trying to say? Taylor is dead? The old Taylor is dead? Or was He trying to say that if he continues the path he is on, he will die? God communicates with Taylor in very significant, but enigmatic ways. Whatever is said or done, has a lot of meaning on the events that would follow. "My son, you need to learn the value of life, and what it means for the innocent to lose their life. You have had a taste of it with the passing of your parents, but that was God's will to bring you closer to Him. You will accompany me to a Wake today over at the funeral home down the street from our church. I am to give a prayer and a blessing to those that greave. You will see what it is to look in the eyes of the families gathers, to look at the lifeless body of the innocent that has passed. You will see what it is to be at loss." "Father, what does this have to do with my studies?" "Everything, my child. Everything indeed. You can not value the life of the innocent, the ones you are destined to protect, until you see what it is for one to lose their life. Even though you shall not see them pass before your eyes today, you will see the last effects that the loss has on the world. This is one of the fundamentals of the Modern Testament. You must see that the Modern Testament is not only about the punishment of the offenders, but about the value God has for the innocent." "We have gathered here today to honor the passing of our loved one, Gary Oldrich. Through the eyes of our Lord, he is a precious child of His that has lost his life before his rightful time. Gary is in the loving embrace of God now, something that we all strive for, but many take for granted. Gary talked to his Lord and asked for forgiveness from sin, asked for salvation, and asked for guidance from his Lord. All what a good man should do. I look at the passing of Gary as unfortunate, but this adds definition to our lives. As we sit here, tissues in hand, tears in eyes, we stand together. We put our differences aside and comfort one another and pray together to get through this hard time. I stand with you here today not as your priest, but as a pillar in the community that stands for faith. I hold up the goodness in our hearts and extend the love to God's children. We use faith to get us through this hard time, to get us through this grieving time. If you open your hearts to the Lord, he shall touch them and help you through this time. As Gary enters the embraces of God's arms, we need to embrace one another to support one another and remember the greatness of Gary Oldrich. In Nomeni Patri Et Fili Spiritus Sancti." Taylor Shakes his head and looks back at the bed. The teenage is no longer himself, but a boy that is badly bruised and cut up from the crash. The nurses put a breathing mask over the boy to help his breathing. The nurse that talked to Taylor before walked up to him once again. "Mr. Taylor, it is unfortunate that you had to see that, he is better now, as you can tell" < The nurse looks over at the boy and smiles. Taylor smiles and says a prayer of protection in his head before walking out the doors. He gets outside and sees the sun beginning to rise. He over at the glow next to the mountain and smiles; it is a new day. Definition to life, state of sanity... It is time to take hold on one's own life and make it into what they envision...
