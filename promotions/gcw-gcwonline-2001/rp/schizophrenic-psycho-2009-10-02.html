schizophrenic psycho

2 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2, 2009 It had nearly been a month since I stepped into a wrestling ring, the weight of the world resting firmly on my shoulders as I hoisted the Just Wrestling Championship belt over my head, screams of accomplishment spinning uncontrollably from my chapped lips, previous nights residue still clinging to life. I had beaten two men, both of which deserved many kind words thrown in their direction, but I fell silent like a victim being stalked by their killer. I took up residence behind a tree and shuffled down to my bum, and held my breath, snot and tears combining into a swift river of emotional breakdown. I tried my hardest not to breath, the cold still air giving away every position I had paved roads for in my mind. And then it was over. My very foundation to a pitiful life finally flowing freely from the smile that had been carved from ear to ear. I had nothing left to prove, let alone words of adornment towards my fellow peers. To say that was a great match wouldn’t do myself just, so I just walked away with a smirk on my face, the musky hallow smell of my breath deteriorating every living breathing creation that had or has yet to walk in my line of sight. I was the Just Wrestling Champion, but here I was just a rookie starting from the ground up trying to establish myself as a force that couldn’t be reckoned with, but that was my faith speaking. I had very little these days, each line of cocaine was just another image of salvation I pushed farther and farther into the backgrounds of my mind. The steal cage linked fence held tightly by my brittle fingers, a cold hard stare that could pierce the very soul of a man only if he would turn around and take it all in and gain some sort of enlightenment. And I was looking for enlightenment. I wanted something to show me that this life I had dug myself into, the dirt smeared on my face, and the shovel slung over my shoulder would be nothing compared to the overall picture I was trying to paint with blood, sweat, and tears. I had very little tears to give these days. My only accomplishment in life clung to my waist, every other image flashing from one reel to the next measured zero in my mind. I had bled for days you could say, each time the crack of the whip made contact with the scared flesh of my back forced me to gasp for air, the warm iron pouring effortlessly down the mid of my back. I was a work horse and damn it if I wasn’t going to be worked. And work I did. I rain circles around the opposition, my very demeanor inside that ring wasn’t a hardened veteran that dominated foe after foe. I was always the underdog even if the chips where stacked in my favor. People knew that when they stepped into the field houses, auditoriums, theaters, open venues, that Aaron Nothings was going to be beaten to a pulp, but if little else was said, the spark kindling inside of my body left me raw and emotional, the very last spec of life blowing up into an atomic bomb that laid waste to the wastelanders, my record boosting another check under the win column. And that’s what I did best besides shove a needle into my arm, the toxin rolling my eyes into the back of my head. I had wanted to stay clean since I signed the dotted line on the contract that was handed to my by Global Championship Wrestling, but the addictions of an addict just seem to eat away the part that wants to quit until finally all that’s left is the core of the apple, the meat of sorts traveling effortlessly down the pipes and splashing into your gut. The fire that had been dormant for months finds its fuel for the long winter its predecessor is about to embark upon. I strolled down the sidewalk, the wind against my back almost forced me face first into the cement undertow of my current situation. I had left Just Wrestling to give the bigger picture a try. I wasn’t holding hands anymore. I just needed the time and space away from home, hope lurking in the shadows that I’d find some peace and mind along the way. I watched cars pass by me, the subtle looks that cut through my core ate away at what I was trying to become. I wasn’t some lucky prick living his days out with fame and glory. I had no home, no car, barely any money, and the clothes on my back came from laundry mats that had the least susceptible victim with his back turned and the right timing led me to the restroom with a fresh coat of paint upon my body. I had been walking these same streets for days, playing in and out of my head the events that had taken place back in Michigan. I gave it all away when I dialed that number. Any man with half a right mind could see who the killer was, the sourdough bread crumbs leading back to the little kid who suffered from withdraws, the cookie jar wide open with a face full of chocolate residing from ear to ear. You couldn’t help but grow a smile on your face at the innocence, but a true blooded killer had not a spec left in his soul. You could say in sorts he had crossed the line between human and inhuman, but these days it was getting harder and harder to find these so called humans in a vast landscape of met morphing mother fuckers. I was just another black sheep in a sea of mangled sheep trying effortlessly to have the bigger picture grasped in their short stocky grubby fingers. I was quite as greedy, but I’d take what I could get if there was still anything left to get. I remember it like yesterday, but little specs of the plot left my mind. The kind of specs you’d want to forget, but with the flashing images on the television, you couldn’t help but see the hand that feeds you. The slicing of the throats, perfectly sprawled out positions, an intricate display of perfection, not to mention the flammable liquid that engulfed the entire second floor before sirens rounded out around the corner, the beating hearts of a soul breaking down the front door, his first instinct isn’t about himself, but saving the lives of those trapped behind the flames, yet little did they know that lives had already been taken. The crypt keeper of sorts claiming this souls for the after life. The mighty mounted buck above the fireplace mantle ringing out with triumph and discloser. I had to deal with the last images tattooed on their faces. It wasn’t hard to kill someone in their sleep, but harder to do such when the soft sterile eyes of innocence clings to the art of love. And I loved Heather probably more then any man could ever think about loving a woman. She was the air I breathed, the water I drank, even the heroin that coursed through my veins on a daily basis couldn’t topple any effects that woman had on me. I remember being in the hospital the most, the pain and agony I saw in her eyes, it just tore me apart, but then the agony turned into love, the screaming of a tiny infant rang louder then any scream could penetrate the wall of displeasure. For a moment there we had a moment, but lost in the shuffle of wrestling and addictions, I left it all behind even though my heart stayed in Michigan, my name of sorts left imprinted on an unlikely foe of all foes. I rounded a corner to a stop sign and took a brief moment to deeply breath in the autumn air. I never bothered to look both ways as I took that first step out into the road. I didn’t really care if I was going to get smeared on the pavement, my last sight of the clouded sky above me. Hallow with shades of grays. It matched my person completely. There where no rainbows or soft fluffy white clouds. Not even the sun would shine on a dogs ass. That’s when I heard it… “I know what you did.” I jerked my head around, looking behind me only to find nothing. A few cars passed by as I stood there in the middle of the road, my eyes darting in every direction like a scared little child in a department store looking tirelessly for his mother, the warm pockets of her hugs could only bring the child back into the heart of safety. I started to take a few more steps. “Baby killer!” I jerked around in a three sixty darting my eyes, penetrating the darkest shadows for some sign of life, the faint echoing voice that trailed off before I could get a fix on it. The wind ripped by, the shrill cold of it sent a shiver down the mid of my back. “Who the fuck is there!” I raised my voice, something I had rarely done in my life besides when I was buried behind the trenches, flashes and gun fire ringing out over heads of men sought to serve and protect. The many men that died, the sound of my voice trailing off as they traveled towards that supposed white light. I waited a few seconds and got no reply, my mind was surely playing its god damn tricks on me. So I took to walking away. “It takes a real monster Aaron, to burn a baby…” I was furious now, my body started to gain uncontrollable shacks, something that over came my body when I was in a sea of fury. “WHO THE FUCK IS THERE!!!!” I screamed that the top of my lungs, the strain on my vocal cords would leave me days later wishing I had just stayed cool, calm, and collective. Still there was no answer. I was started to believing I was loosing it, the winds travels at multiple digits just didn’t help the fucking matter at hand. I tossed my hood over my head and buried my head between my shoulders and continued to walk. It had rained the night before, the murky water residue clung to my shoes as I passed in and out of puddles, the chill air did nothing for the cough I had accumulated over the past few days. “I cant believe you Aaron…” I stopped in my tracks and closed my eyes for a split second. I had no need to yell at the top of my lungs again, the horse dryness was already eating away at my throat. I turned around, and peered out from under my hood. I still saw nothing out of the blue, my face melted just at the thoughts of the crimes I committed and for what purpose? A jealous rage of sorts? Was I really the killing type? To save myself and others that cared about. Reasons for war I participated in. A few tears rolled down my cheeks, nothing that couldn’t stop a sleeve from wiping away. “What the hell…” It was soft and barely audible. Only me, myself, and I could of heard it, but when I took my eyes to the ground, I knew right then and there who it was talking to me. “Getting all soft on my now are you Aaron?” I stared at my reflection in a fairly large puddle, the infamous smirk of a gritty yellow teethed man blankly looked back at me. My own image of a killer blanketing me with disapproval and praise at the same time. “I’ve lost my mind…” I was fucking talking to myself! How far gone does one man have to be till he becomes a schizophrenic psycho? “Maybe, but who’s to say you had a mind at all to begin with?” The statement held come baring. After all, when I was stationed in the middle east I had lost a part of that mind, the twelve year old child holding that 9mm proved the to the world that nothing is what it seems to be. My better half was left in that empty alleyway. “This isn’t happening. I know I’m not this fucked up. Or am I?” I had question my stability in my head on numerous times, but I always thought I was level headed. I might of done some fucked up shit in the past couple of years, but nothing ever seemed this far off the map. “You’ve always been fucked up! Why you think you did what you did?” I had nothing to say. For the first time, my lips seized to spatter words. “Come on, you know the answer as well as I do Aaron! You couldn’t handle it anymore! The drugs where starting to lose their effects. Granted you’d still get high or drunk as fuck, but the middle man was missing. There was always the thought in your head.” He had a point. What the fuck was I saying? He had a point? I had no point! I didn’t even have the slightest clue as to what was happening. The uncontrollable doing the controlling. “Maybe you killed them!” I was arguing with myself. Not a good indication as to what was going on in my head. A battle of sorts raging war against each other. And I was losing every last inch of my sanity… “But I am you Aaron. So either way you want to justify it, you slashed the throats, you burned the place down, you…YOU!!!!!!!!” The last straw of straws. “FUCK YOU!” And I ran my foot through the puddle, the image bleeding seamlessly a color that creates me vanished as I turned around, the blaring of a car horn sunk into reality, and within a blink of an eye I was on the hood, my shoulder smashing into the windshield, and up in the air till I landed intricately on the pavement ten or so feet behind the maroon impala. I laid there for seemed like eternity, my vision coming in and out of focus, the older blonde hair woman running up beside me, the hesitant look on her face of the unpleasant, stinky mother fucker before her eyes. There was no pity in them, but shocked annoyance that some dirty ass bum ruined her newly acquired vehicle. The only thing that crossed my mind at that moment before I black out was wishful thinking that Zacharia Taylor would be less merciful that I.
