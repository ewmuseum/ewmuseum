A Conviction of Revenge

8 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/08/11 The sun beams down in the desert and midday hits. In the distance several police cars drive across the mix of sand and gravel road on their approach to the weathered down house. They come to a stop, each at a different position around the house as the officers all step out. Leading them all is Detective Jordan as he motions for his fellow officers to get into position. “I want everyone to cover any possible exits from this place. This has to the be the place where Ben is being held.” After surveying everyone's position Jordan walks towards the front door where two other officers are. Jordan pulls his gun out and motions for the other two officers to go inside. They kick the door down and slide inside making sure the entry point is safe and secure as Jordan walks inside. A little bit of shock sets in as they notice the inside of the house is actually really clean, and completely opposite from how it looks on the outside. “Detective... Over here, we found a door leading down into a basement.” As the officers lift open the door, alarms go off down in the cellar where Donny, Ben, and Katrina are. Muffled screams can be heard slightly escaping the strap across Ben's mouth as Katrina pierces different needles through his eyelids. Donny is standing off in the corner watching the police officers upstairs on the small television monitors on the wall. “What good does it do for you to scream? The doors are shut so no one could ever hear you. Instead of crying like an infant you should be embracing the pain. Pain is a reminder to your soul that you're still alive, and because of that you should be grateful to the both of us.” Donny walks around the table towards Katrina with a rare smile on his face as he watches her yank a needle out from his eye. He places his arms around her and leans against her as the rate of her breathing increases while she hovers over Ben's trembling body. "I'm sorry to say my love, but I believe our play time must end prematurely.” “Just a few minutes more please?” “Okay, but only for a bit more.” After falling to her plea Donny watches as the officers try and find a way to open the steel door. He reaches for the strap across Ben's mouth and removes it as Katrina grabs a knife from the side of the table. Ben screams out in pain as she uses the very tip of the knife to cut down the middle of his chest slowly. Donny notices Jordan is on his radio and he makes his way towards the door in the back of the room. Katrina notices Donny motioning for her and she casual walks towards him, knife in hand and a gleaming smile in her eyes. Just as Donny goes to press the button for the door to open Katrina urgently pulls back. “Wait! I can't leave the CD we made here! It will make for a good listen on the trip home.” Walking back towards Ben she grabs the small external CD recorder from alongside Ben where she recorded him during the torturing. With Katrina at his side once again Donny presses the button and the door opens. The two of them walk down a long dark tunnel as the door closes shut behind them and the door to the room opens before Detective Jordan. “Ben!” Jordan lets out a scream of panic as he rushes towards Ben in the center of the room. All of the officers hurry into the room to secure it and aid in the rescuing of Ben. "Help...” “Save your energy man. We're going to get you out of here first and then we can figure everything out from there.” As the officers work on removing the steel restraints one of the other officers notice a clock counting down over the exit door. “Um Detective sir, I think you need to see this.” Detective Jordan leaves the side of Ben and heads to the officer calling for him. The officer nervously points up towards the clock that is counting down from seven minutes and twenty-three seconds. “What is it counting down for?” “I don't mean to cause a panic sir, but from the wiring I believe this place is going to blow.” “A bomb? Hurry and get those restraints off of Ben now!” Frantically everyone begins to try and get Ben freed from the table while keeping an eye on the clock. It doesn't take long before the clock begins to reach under two minutes. “Sir we can't get these things off, they're bolted down to the table with some form of lock. We got to out of here now!” “We can't just leave him down here when this place explodes!” “There is no other choice sir!” As the officers begin exiting the room Jordan continues to pull and tug on Ben's body in an attempt to free him. An officer runs back into the room and pulls Jordan off of Ben and out of the room as Ben cries and pleas to not be left there. Everyone is quick to make their way upstairs and out of the house as Jordan takes a final look back at the house. Everyone knows the clock has finally reached zero when flames erupt from the house and pieces of wood and sand fly into the air. Jordan drops to his knees in a state of helplessness as he knows Ben has become the second victim of the case he has yet to close. - “I just love how his voice hits such a lovely note each time the needle punctures his eyes.” “For being your first you did a very nice job my dear. However my darling, now that we're alone and the only thing we can kill is time I must ask you something.” “Yes?” “I don't really care how you found me, but I need to know how you became like this. When we met there was no way you would do such a thing.” Donny is completely focused on the road as Katrina turns in her car seat and starts digging in her purse. The sun is slowly starting to go down as they continue to drive back towards the city limits. He notices Katrina pulling a small black book out from her purse. “Well a few months before I moved back in with you this guy ran into me while I was at the mall. He dropped this book, but when I looked up I didn't see him anymore. So when I got back home I flipped through a few of the pages and became really interested. Her preachings of life's truth filled me with so much power and confidence.” He continues to glance over at the book while continuing to drive. Katrina thumbs through a few pages as she talks. “Then a couple of weeks passed and I decided to visit the address in the back of the book. It was a beautiful church filled with people listening and living off each word being told from this guy behind the podium, as he read her word out loud from this book. When service was done, he came to talk to me and introduced himself as Dorn Tracer.” “Father Tracer?” “Oh my God you know who I'm talking about?” Before he gives her an answer he takes a moment to compose everything she has just told him. “When I was lost on my path Father Tracer showed me the light. As I recovered, he sat beside my bed every night and read to me her word. It is because of Father Tracer I am the way I am now, and I thank him dearly for it.” “He is a very nice man. I must thank him next time I see him, for saving my Donny-pooh.” “Indeed he did save me. And Katrina we must now devote the rest of our life to The Following so that the entire world can know her word.” “And together it will be epic, nothing will be able to stop us baby.” - Noise fills the air as the elevator doors open and out walks a woman with flowing red hair dressed in a plain black dress. While she walks down the hall she notices all the police officers making sure the criminals are kept in check while they wait to be booked. She stops in front of a door with the words “Detective Jordan” painted on it, she opens it and walks inside. Like normal Jordan is slouched over his desk smothered in paper work. "Mrs. Fox, what are you doing here?” “Well now that the funereal is over and everything has calmed down I've had some time to do some research.” Concerned for state of mine Detective Jordan pushes the paper work aside to make sure he gives her his full attention. “Mrs. Fox with all due respect this job should be left to me. You have been through a lot over the last couple of weeks.” “What am I suppose to do? Sit home every day and cry my eyes out as I explain to my daughter why daddy won't be coming home? No! I'm going to do everything I possibly can to find the bastard that did this to Ben!” He moves his from his chair and takes a seat in front of her on his desk. His arms cross in front of him as he shuffles possible ways to calm her down. “The old house in...” “Mrs. Fox come on now, you really need to stop.” “As I was saying; my friend in real estate looked up the owner of the old house where Ben was being held. It was leased to a man named Danny Bolds, but him and his wife have been dead for years. So after coming across that bit of information I did some more snooping and those two adopted a kid around fifteen or so years ago. Guess who it was.” He rubs his chin for a moment while Robyn takes a seat in a chair alongside his desk. “Well the only person that is connected to this would be Donny Diamond, but I interviewed him myself. He was very cooperative and seemed anxious to find the killer as well.” “Exactly. It has been him all along and you have what is needed to prove it.” “I do?” “Remember when Larry was murdered, the news stations reported that the only evidence forensics were able to recover was a portion of a burned glove. After looking over some of Diamond's photos of his career, he once wore the same type of glove. He just recently started wearing different gloves.” “You don't say... We have to get right on this before he has time to cover his tracks then. You go on home and get some rest, I'll give you a call if I hear anything else.” The two of them talk a bit longer before Robyn gets up from her seat and shakes the hand of Detective Jordan. Robyn exits the door and heads towards the elevator as Jordan closes the door behind her and picks up the phone to get more information after what was just explained. A few hours pass and after finishing off a few errands Robyn pulls her car back into the garage. She gets out and walks towards her front door, where she proceeds to walk inside. “Mommy! Mommy!” Robyn is a bit startled by the yelling of her daughter as she comes running down the hall with a smile from ear to ear. “What is it Katie?” “One of daddy's friends came over to play with me!” “What? ...Where's Mrs. Joyce?” “Oh, don't worry. She's taking a nap upstairs. Me and Katie here have been having a blast.” Robyn slowly turns around to see the door closing behind her and Donny Diamond standing before her with no emotions on his face.
