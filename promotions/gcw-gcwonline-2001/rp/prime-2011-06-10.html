Date: 06/10/11 INT. THE FOURTH WALL NIGHTCLUB – NIGHT Neon running lights of every imaginable color outline every corner and crevice of this hole-in-the-wall club, situated somewhere in the heart of the concrete jungle of downtown Chicago. The garish lighting illuminates very little, making it hard to see the faces of the young and beautiful silhouettes of the Fourth Wall patrons as they mingle amongst tables surrounding a circular bar. The lines and ridges of the bar glow with soft blue lights, evoking a rather TRON-esque mystique, but due to licensing and copyright laws this striking similarity is merely implied. Cheap techno music BLARES from hidden speakers, enhancing this not-unintended motif. The BARTENDER, an attractive twenty-something woman in tight leather pants and a House of Blues t-shirt, notices a quiet, rather tan MAN sitting by himself, presumably waiting for a drink. She grabs a clean glass and walks over to greet him. BARTENDER What can I get you, stranger? If it tastes funny and gets you drunk, chances are we got it. It's hard to see the Man's face clearly in the half-light, but from the blue reflection of the bar it is obvious that something is on his mind. He waves his hand absently. MAN I want to be left alone. BARTENDER (flirting) Do you? Well, you came out here and sat down in, you know, a public bar. Coulda fooled me. MAN (quietly) You should leave. BARTENDER Excuse me? The Man stands up, towering over the tiny blonde bartender. He is wearing an ominous-looking brown trench coat that is buttoned up to his chest. He unsnaps the top few buttons. MAN (louder) I said, you should leave. Right now. The Bartender GASPS as the Man opens his coat, revealing a metric fuckton of C-4 EXPLOSIVES tied around his chest with a bunch of technical wires connecting everything to a big RED BUTTON. MAN (CONT'D) 'Cause it's about to get hot in here! The Bartender screams, starting a domino effect around the club. Heads begin to turn; people start yelling, pushing, and causing general havoc as the Man takes a few steps away from the bar and spins around, displaying his deadly payload to everyone. With one hand he holds the flap of his coat open, and with the other he motions for people to remain calm, though most of the club-goers are too busy, you know, crying and running the fuck away to notice. MAN There's no need to be alarmed! I'm just a harmless terrorist armed with enough explosive to kill us all, no big deal! (pause) You know what, the hell with it. All this politically correct pussy-footing around rules and barriers has gone on long enough! Tonight I destroy the Fourth Wall! For PRIME! The Man slaps the RED BUTTON with the palm of his free hand and squeezes his eyes shut. JUMP CUT TO: EXT. Taking off a pair of ornate reading spectacles, Garbage Bag Johnny closed the tiny manuscript in front of him. His head shook a little, not quite a nod or a spasm, just a movement of muscle. Quietly, he pushed the script away from himself and fixed his eager colleague with a dead-pan stare. "This is the dumbest shit I've ever read," he said flatly. A light flickered out of Rich "The Renegade" Rollins' eyes. The Renegade deflated, his eyes cast down at the manuscript. "Did you at least get to the part where you and me and Murrr stop a massive dildo... in space?! I mean, c'mon Johnny. That shit is solid gold!" Johnny snatched the manuscript up and shook it like a rattle, causing some of the pages to flutter down around his desk. "This is going to get us both fired, you idiot! The minute Caldera sees this, not to mention Devin Shakur! Holy shit man. I mean, holy shit." "Whatever," Rollins snapped, snatching the remaining pages right back. "I've got a pay-per-view to get ready for, and your Negative Nancy bullshit is bringin' me down. You have to admit though, we would make for some bitchin' buddy cops, wouldn't we?" Try as he might to hide it, a hint of a smile managed to tickle the bushy mustache on Johnny's lip. Clearing his throat, the GCW Commissioner gathered up the loose pages and shoved them at Rollins. "Show me a second draft, then we'll talk. Maybe," he added, trying to hide that smile again. Rich Rollins gathered his pages with a smirk, shooting GBJ a little salute. "See you on the moon, John-boy." God help us all. ~Fin~
