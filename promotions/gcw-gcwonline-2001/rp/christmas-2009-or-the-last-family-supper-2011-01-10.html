Christmas 2009, or The Last Family Supper

10 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/11 Christmas dinner, 2009. The camcorder turns on. "Alexis be careful." Colin speaks past the camera. "Hell I just got the thing for you, don’t break it." The voice of a young female speaks gleefully. "I love it Colin! Thank you." "Yeah yeah, I know how much you love those things. And considering I sort of broke your last one." Colin says, semi-apologetic. Alexis laughs as she moves the camera away from Colin towards her father. It was the first time in ages that Ashley O’Ryan had both of his children visiting him for Christmas dinner. Ever since the divorce he didn’t see either much. Alexis lived with Moira and Colin was out on his own. So this was a special moment for him. He didn’t have many of those. Alexis and Colin sat on the couch together laughing and sipping eggnog while the flames of the fireplace bounced off them with a healthy glow. Any other time and this perfect Hallmark Card moment would make Ashley sick. But it made him happy. Another thing he didn’t experience much. He looks at the leather covered flask with etched O’Ryan Crest on the front that Colin gave him with a smile. Alexis moves the camera around a bit, showing the fireplace and the tree and all the other festive stuff. Of course she was the one that decorated her father’s place so she has reason to show it off. However, both children are caught off guard by Ashley’s next words. "’ows yer mother been?" Without thinking about it, Alexis swings the camera past Colin, who is looking at her, and at her father. He never asked about her. Ever. "She’s…she’s good dad." It’s obvious in the inflection of her voice that this is an awkward conversation to have. "I hear she’s seeing somebody." He says. "Ben." Is all she says. He tries to keep from sighing and almost succeeds. "Bastard leached onto her the moment the papers were signed I bet..." Ashley say’s under his breath. Alexis turns the camera back to Colin. "Did you tell him yet?" she asks. Colin looks like a stunned deer as he looks at Alexis. He tries to shush her. "Tell me wot?" their father inquires. "Its noth-," Colin is about to say before Alexis cuts him off. "Colin is going to be a wrestler, like you." Silence… … .. . "Isn’t that exciting dad?" … .. . "Dad? Isn’t that exciting?" Alexis says again. Ashley stands and walks over to the fireplace, resting his head on his arm on the mantle he stares into the fire. Colin looks at his father then over at the camcorder and Alexis annoyed. "What?" she says. Colin is about to tell her off but their father speaks. "You’re not wrestling, Colin." Colin turns his head towards his dad. "That’s hardly your choice." Colin protests. Ashley turns towards Colin with a laugh. "That bloody so? You don’t know a damn thing about wrestling me boy. Not a damn thing. Yer just going to get ‘urt like a damned idiot." Once again Alexis speaks up, much to Colin’s chagrin. "He’s been trained." Ashley looks coldly at Colin, "Who?" "Never mind," Colin says. "Stu," Alexis says matter-of-factly. Colin rubs his face muttering the name of every deity he can think of in vein under his breath in about one second. Ashley says nothing. He just turns back to the fire, staring into it as if all the answers lay deep in its flames. "Dad.." Colin is about to speak. But the old man will have none of it. He turns, all his weight going into the palm of his hand as is swings around and strikes Colin in the face. Colin stumbles back, falling to the sofa. He holds his hand more in surprise then pain. "Don’t," Ashley voice breaks slightly,"don’t call me dad." "But I..." "You disobeyed me. You went be’ind me back. You trained with Stu St.Clair?! No son of mine would ever do that." Colin tries to protest once more. And one more Ashley won’t have it. "I want you out of this house. Go to your precious wrestling. But don’t expect my sympathy when it treats you like a whore and throws you to the streets!" Colin stands. Fists clenched. The tension between the two is thick. He stands ready to strike. But then relaxes his shoulders and looks back and the camera, back at Alexis. Then he walks out of the room. Alexis follows Colin to his room. Colin is only there for a few minutes before he emerges, bags packed. He looks at Ashley, and spits at the floor before walking out. Alexis turns to her father. "Dad, Do something!" Ashley does something alright. He takes a single swig from the flask Colin gave him, looks at it for a brief moment, and then chucks the flask right into the fire. Alexis runs into the street. By that time Colin is gone. ------ The scene fades to black and we turn from the television we have been viewing it on. "That was over a year ago now." Speaks a slightly older Colin. "I would love to say that I got right into the ring and proved everybody who doubted me wrong. I would love my father came to me and said "I was wrong, forgive me." There are many things I would love to say. But I can’t claim to be able to say them. Not yet." He reaches into his coat pocket. The coat is army green, with signs of wear and tear. He pulls a small white cylinder out and places it in his mouth, lighting the end on fire, and taking a drag from it. He pulls the cigarette from his mouth and looks at it as he lets out a slim long puff of smoke. "We all have our vices. My father’s was drinking. So I’ll be damned if I had the same one." He takes another drag, and lets the cigarette dangle from his lips. "Some might say gambling. But to me poker is nothing but an easy way to pay the bills. For the last year I wandered. I claimed no home. No address. I found money where I could. It wasn’t too hard. One good poker match and I were set for months. But I kept moving. I was a drifter. Until I found this place." Colin looks around with a smile. "This. This is what I want. I want to be in the ring. I want to feel the energy of a crowd. I want to battle. To feel pain. To feel victory. Hell, to feel loss. This is me doing what I damn well want because as much as it might bring me close to death, it makes me feel alive! Sadly, that bastard who shagged a dope dealing crackwhore twenty something years ago disagrees with me. He even disowned me. Not legally but for all intents and purposes it’s the same. But regardless of that, my true family is here. Here in the GCW are the two people that mean the most to me. My brother in arms, if you will, Micah St.Clair. We both trained under his father. We suffered together. Laughed together. Bled together. There is no man I would trust more than him. The other is Alexis. My dear sweet sister. Who I was delighted to hear decided to also join the family business. Thank you Auntie Raquel. It delights me that this week me and my sister will join up and face Palmer and Redding. I can’t wait to see what moves she has up her sleeves." Colin stands, taking a last drag from the cigarette. "We are sons and daughters of bastards. Make no mistake about it. There is a certain deviance that runs through our veins. But know that whatever we do, we do it with the utmost honesty and purity. Even kicking the shit out of you. We are the Saints. We will be venerated."
