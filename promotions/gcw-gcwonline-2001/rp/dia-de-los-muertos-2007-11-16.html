Dia De Los Muertos

16 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 November 07 2 November 2007 Spain It’s a cold crisp day, colder than a normal November day around here. The breeze causes me to dig my hands into the pockets of my coat as I walk along the grass. My eyes stare only at the few feet in front of me as I walk, somewhat to protect my face from the cold but mostly because I’ve been here so many times that there is very little reason for me to look up. I know every inch of this place by now. As is the case every year as I get closer all of the background noises melt away into obscurity. Once when all I could hear were the cars in the distance, birds chirping or the construction workers right down the street now turns into just two things. The beating of my heart The exaggerated breaths going in and out of my lungs It’s almost like the feeling you get when your ears pop or if water floods into them. It’s almost a dream-like state. Sadly it’s not one of those good “having the best sex EVER” kind of dreams. More like one of those “FUCK IM BEING CHASED!” kind of dreams. God damn I hate those kind of dreams. The feeling hits me like a sucker punch to the gut. All of the typical symptoms hit at once, shortness of breath, shaky hands and the forming of the tears in the corner of my eyes. I swallow hard and turn to my left. In that moment its as if the wind stops blowing and my heart stops beating. Side by side they sit in front of me. Cynthia Yvonne Abitua and Starr Maria Abitua. I take two steps forward before my legs just become too unstable and I drop down to my knees. The tears come next, as the always do, They feel like ice crystals rolling down my cheeks from the cold air surrounding me. In this moment I see their faces ? but not the smiling and loving faces that I had known. I see the cuts and bruises. I see the blood and tears. I hear Cindy’s screams Starr’s cries. The entire chain of events flashes before my eyes. I try to block it out, closing my eyes tight but it doesn’t work. It never does. The argument The alcohol The car The crash? All my fault. If nothing else ever remained branded into my memory that night it’d be the Officer telling me the last words she ever spoke. “I hope he is ok ? I love him.” It was typical Cindy, always caring about others even if they hurt her. She was the most beautiful person I have ever known, inside and out. She was always smiling, always caring, always giving. If you were in trouble it was her that’d drop everything to be with you. She was my wife. Then there was my sunshine on a cloudy day. Starr. My baby girl. No more than a year old but she had more personality than most people my age. She had her mother’s smile and laugh ? the kind that could make anyone’s day brighter just by being around. She was my daughter. They died ten years ago now but it feels like only ten minutes ago. Every time I step between those black gates of the cemetery and walk on this cold grass It’s like I’m moving through a time machine. People try to say that time can help to heal wounds ? or at least make them hurt less. What a crock of shit. There is a part of me that will never completely heal or forgive myself for what happened. That’s a hole that can never be filled. NEVER. I try to compose myself. The sounds of the world around me begin to come back and fill my ears. My breathing goes to nearly normal levels. The tears stop flowing, instead they stay at the corners of my eyes ? ready to show up again if called upon. The harsh wind hits me again, sending a shiver down my spine. I take in a deep breath and sit back on my knees. “Hello my ladies,” I begin with a sigh. “I’m sorry I missed your birthdays this year. Coming all the way to Spain is tough ya know” Especially with the schedule I have to follow. Maybe I can get My boss to do a tour of Spain eh? Then I could come see you both more often.” I do see them sometimes though. When I lay down at night and go to sleep. In my dreams they are both with me, happy and alive. Starr is 11 years old and going to school. It’s almost time to begin those very awkward discussions about the birds and the bees. I’m already having to scare off young boys who are calling the house. She’s a straight A student ? she makes me proud every day. Cindy is still as ravishing as ever. Her cooking is incredible and her sense of humor great. And the rest of her? Well those I keep locked away for rainy days. “I thought about bringing Mary with me today,” I pause to wipe away a lone tear that slides down. “I don’t think I’m ready for that though. She means so much to me Cindy and I hope that you are okay with the route my life has taken. I miss you so much it hurts sometimes ? especially around this time of the year. My birthday isn’t quite the same anymore. My life isn’t quite the same anymore.” “And my son?” I can’t help but smile. “He is nearly a year old now. I can’t even begin to tell you how happy he has made me. He brings that same kind of light that I’m sure Starr does to you everyday that you are together up in heaven.” I reach into my coat pocket ? “I have something for the both of you.” I lay down a heart shaped charm near Starr. “This is for you baby girl. Something for you to remember how much I love you. I hope you like it and wear it everyday.” I turn my eyes to Cindy’s headstone ? all emotion leaves my face. “And this is yours.” A ring. Her wedding ring. “I have had this since the day you died. I have never been able to part with it ? thinking that if I ever did it’d make everything that happened even more finalized than it ever had to be. Silly I know and after ten years you’d think I’d have realized that nothing will ever take that pain away and make it any less a reality. I guess deep down none of us wants to believe something horrible can happen to us.” But it does. To some people, bad things happen more often than not. I’ll never fully understand why it started happening to me. “Today I think I can give it back and know that you understand that I’ll always love you. No matter what else happens in my life you are always my girl ? both of you. Nothing can change the love I have for my family. I just hope that after all of this time you can forgive me for what I’ve done. I hope that when my time does come that you both will welcome me with open arms. That we can be together as a family once again.” The tears come falling once again. “Until that time I can only ask that you watch over me. Make sure I don’t screw things up again ? help me make the right decisions.” Oh and how can I forget? “Oh and another thing” Help me win at NC-17 this year. It’s been a long time coming.” I place my hands up against my lips and kiss the tips of my fingers softly before placing them on the two headstones. “I will come to see you both again soon. I love you.” And I always will.
