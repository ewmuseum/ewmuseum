The Valentine Revolution (A Beautiful Lie)

21 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 21, 2006 ---- Prelude: Tempers Flare ---- 'Hey!' Vivica screamed across the crowded backstage area. Her pace was frantic, especially considering the fact that she was walking with a slight limp. Cluster matches are hell, but they're part of the job. Holding the back of her head throbbing in pain, she tried to keep pace with the figure quickly maneuvering through the traffic ahead of her. It was hard to keep track of him being all of five foot and a half, but his much larger size made it a bit easier for her to see from a distance... ever with her now messy blonde hair blocking the vision from one of her eyes. Frustrated she tucked the strand of hair in front of her right eye behind her ear and shoved aside a GCW audio worker into the nearby wall. Unbeknownst to him, he was standing right in the middle of The Black Mariah's warpath. Up in the distance she saw the object of her chase come to a stop at a door. 'Virgil stop!' Miss Valentine yelled over the masses. Perhaps Virgil Ambrosi didn't hear her over the hysteria going on around them, preparing for the night's main event. Regardless, he walked into the room and slammed the door behind him... almost in the face of Vivica in tail. Reaching out she grabbed the doorknob, but it didn't turn. Trying to crank harder with no avail, she let out a frustrated grunt? 'You locked me out?!' Vivica screamed into the door, 'What in the hell is wrong with you?!' Without even thinking of the consequences or what condition she may be in, she began to kick the door. Hoping it would fly open with every strike, Vivica eventually fell after the fourth kick, weakened from the match she'd just finished. Normally the door might've fell, but not in her current condition. It was pointless... there was no way she was getting into that room unless someone let her into the room. It didn't look like that was going to happen anytime soon, but The Black Mariah was more stubborn than most. 'Let me in...' Vivica said from her knees outside the door, 'Please?!' Knocking fruitlessly, her calls were never answered. Perhaps Virgil Ambrosi was distracted with other matters, or maybe he was doing it intentionally out of frustration. Regardless one thing was very clear to Miss Valentine, she wasn't getting into that room. Which means, she wasn't getting her stuff to go home. After a high pitched scream that some fans admitted hearing out in the arena during the main event, she finally gave up. For the first time in her life, Vivica left the arena in Japan alone... without any belongings besides the ones on her back. Hopefully Virgil would have the decency to gather them for her and give them to her next week, but in the back of her mind... she highly doubted that. ---- First Passage: Breaking And Entering ---- Clouds whimsically pass in front of the full moon, hanging in the sky alone. Above the metropolis of Chicago, Illinois it shone. So distant and so peaceful it looked from the streets below from an observer. Walking through a maze of steel and concrete... the moon above looked so tranquil, it was no wonder she wanted to go there so badly as a child. Didn't all children want to be an astronaut and land on the moon? Make footprints in the dust and stick a flag in the rock, to prove that you did what everyone else said couldn't be done. The great Space Race might be over, but the children of today still dream... just like the children of yesterday. One thing was sure... her reality definitely was a constant reminder that she wasn't a little girl anymore. Her breath visibly danced in the frozen air in front of her, she watched for a short while after each breath. Looking at how they were all different... she needed to think about something, anything to get her mind off of wrestling. A cold walk was just the trick. Bundled up under a black leather bomber style jacket and match black scarf and toboggan. Her blonde hair waved in the arctic breeze that passed her, turning her face a light shade of red. Looking around at the buildings around her, it had been so long since she spent every waking minute walking the streets. About two years ago is when she left the city for New Mexico in search of Die Erntemaschine, but it seemed like a different lifetime. It was amazing how much things could change in two years. Going from being the perfect girl, to a hoodlum on the streets, to being a professional wrestling. Years ago no one would've ever saw what her future had in store. Yet years had passed since the last time she stepped foot in this neighborhood. Looking around at her vaguely remembered surroundings, she pondered what brought her back here in the first place. She never particularly thought this side was beautiful, nor did it have any sort of emotional attachment. After passing under a street light and kicking an empty beer can into the sewer, she looked up at the building next to her. The old event venue was vacant and decrepit, but it wasn't always that way. Once full of life, it was a third venue for the city. Many young bands in Chicago got their first break and the much smaller arena. She had went to many conventions with her mother there when she was a little girl. Slowly, against her better judgment, she made her way across the parking lot towards the condemned establishment. Farther and farther from the usual light of the city she walked. Why was she so fascinated by this place? It was hazardous, she should just stay away. Many thoughts like that crossed her mind but she continued to walk, no matter how much her mind told her to turn back and go home. Maybe it was trying to teach her sometime... a memory lost a long time ago. ---- Second Passage: Broken ---- 'Now taking the floor... Vivica Valentine.' The capacity crowd in the small arena rose to their feet and clapped as the eight year old Vivica Julian Valentine walked towards the blue mats, nervous as can be. Her blonde hair was pulled back in a pony tail, as for it not to distract her during this event. Dressed in a white tight spandex gymnastics outfit, she took a deep sigh before reading the sign that hung from the arena ceiling. UNITED STATES JUNIOR GYMNASTICS CHAMPIONSHIP The arena grew completely silent as Vivica rose her arms up into the air. Her teal blue eyes were fixated on the vault and horse in front of her. With an explosion the young Valentine took off, running towards the vault with an attempt that would clinch a junior gymnastics championship. Her mother and trainers would be so proud... every one would shower her with praise and would be so excited. She couldn't wait to see the look on her daddy's face after she landed. She almost smiled on her way to the vault where she jumped with incredible force... for an eight year old girl. Flying through the air she reached out for the horse... but it wasn't there. Desperately she reached out for it, trying to grab it as she flipped out of control through the air. While spinning she saw it pass... along with her chance to bring home that championship. As she violently spun on her way to the ground she pictured the disappointment on her mom, dad, and trainer's face. Would they accept a failure? So close... yet so far away. Landing awkwardly on the ground with a thump the crowd gasped as little Vivica erupted into tears holding her shoulder, crying out for her mommy. Without hesitation her parents jumped out from the crowd along with EMT's who had been watching the events unfold before them with an intent eye. Her shoulder was separated and she couldn't move it. Yet no matter how much physical pain might've been throbbing through her small body... nothing compared to the amount of pain failure inflicted onto her soul. ---- Third Passage: Second Chances ---- 'Hmpf...' Vivica J. Valentine said aloud, rubbing her shoulder and looking around inside the decrepit old building. She stood on what was once the arena floor, in almost the exact same place that she broke her shoulder in over ten years ago. She hadn't been inside the building since. Her eyes were now adjusted to the darkness and age had done a number on the building in the six years since it closed down. You could still see the memories though, they were still there. The giant video screen and scoreboard that once hung high above everyone was now crashed and shattered in the middle of the arena floor. A startling reminder of how dangerous walking into such a place may be. If something were to happen, no one was ever going to look there for you. Pardon a few cats, that now call the arena home. It was part of the thrill for Vivica Valentine though. 'So this was the last time I had a shot to be a champion...' She said aloud, talking to herself. Adrenaline had become like a drug, the more she wrestled the more she wanted it. The cheer of the fans... the feeling of flying through the air towards someone, not knowing if they were still going to be there when you got there or not. It was risky, sometimes painful, sometimes joyful, but always addicting. Her weekly fix was just not enough anymore... but she knew deep down inside part of being a professional wrestler was shying away from such demons. Afterall, it was why so many wrestler's careers were ruined by turning to other forms of getting a rush. Like drugs... or death defying games. She would never turn to such things though... she'd seen on the streets what it does to people. It wasn't pretty. 'I'll have to do something about that.' Vivica said before staring up at where the United States Junior Gymnastics Championship banner once hung. It was times like this that it crossed her mind what her parents were up to since she left home around the time this arena closed. Not that she'd ever admit it of course, she didn't necessarily care about them... she just wondered what happened to them. Christian Zenith's whole 'caring is a sign of weakness' philosophy had kept her from voicing such opinions. She figured fate would tell her if it was to be. She also knew inside, that fate had bigger and better plans for her than finding out what boring ass life her parents are having these days. ---- Epilogue: Put The Kiddies To Bed ---- The hustle and bustle of O'Hare International Airport in Chicago could be a bit overwhelming to the first time visitor. As some patrons look around in complete confusion, trying to find their way around the massive complex Vivica J. Valentine shoved her way through the crowds on the way to her destination. A private airplane on the airstrip headed to Japan for the next GCW WorldWide event... and her first shot at the GCW United States Championship. Suddenly an obese man stepped in her way and she bumped into him, almost dropping her cell phone on the ground... 'Move it fat ass.' Vivica said aloud before walking around her and putting her phone back up to her ear, 'No not you Christian... the fat ass that I just had to walk 20 feet to get around. Needs to lay off the damn twinkees!' Vivica said the last line hopefully loud enough the Shamu behind her could hear her remark. Unsuccessful, she settled by listening patiently as Christian replied, giving her advice. 'Yeah I know all about Wolack.' Vivica replied, 'You know as for Virgil... I don't know what in the hell his problem is. He thinks just because he's a vampire everything is my fault for not being a vampire. You know this title show couldn't have come at a better time for me Christian... I don't need the tag team titles. I'll go win a championship without him. Then maybe we'll see if he can still blame it on me, and I want my duffel bag back too.' Vivica listened to Christian again, rolling her eyes as he spoke to her about material possessions. 'I don't care if you can buy me a new one it's the point...' Vivica said, she was going to continue on but suddenly she was interrupted. 'Excuse me.' A small voice said from behind her, she turned around to see a little girl and her father standing there. 'Hang on Christian...' Vivica said before putting down the phone, 'Sorry... I don't do autographs. Maybe you can catch me at a convention sometime.' 'You're Vivica from Global Wrestling right?' The little girl said looking up at her. 'Well... yes, but I'm not with Global Wrestling anymore, I'm with Global Championship Wrestling. It's like Global Wrestling, without the crappy management.' Vivica said with a smile. 'My daughter thinks you're great.' The man said, 'Are you still on television?' 'I have to go Christian...' Vivica said before hanging up the phone, not allowing Zenith to reply, 'Yes... I am. I'm actually on my way to Japan to do a televised show right now. GCW WorldWide... Thursday Nights at 8pm, on XTV.' 'I'll see you then.' The man said with a smile and the little girl waved. 'Can you do me a favor?' Vivica said before she walked away... 'Don't let her watch the show this Thursday.' 'Why?' The man asked confused, 'Is it because you don't want her to see men hitting women? If so it...' 'No...' Vivica interrupted with an arrogant smile, 'I'd hate to see her witness a grown man cry like a baby when I immaculate him on television.' The man looked on in confusion as Vivica just walked away. Confident in her step she made her way towards the airport terminal with dreams of triumph and gold. Last time in gymnastics, she fell just short but this time... it was different. Some in wrestling win titles because they're lucky, some wrestlers win titles because they're good, but for Vivica J. Valentine... it was fate. Her fate awaited her on Thursday across the Pacific. The United States of Wolack was just about to witness The Valentine Revolution. Nothing in Wolack would ever be quite the same again.
