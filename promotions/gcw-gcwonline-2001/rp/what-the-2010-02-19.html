What the....!?!?!

19 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/19/10 Quietly the screen opens to a dark room where the only light seen is streaming in through closed curtains. The sunlight streams through the dust as a highlighted form of two men sitting in chairs with hands both folded. The first man to speak is Shaman who's voice is obvious. He leans forward as the little sunlight lights half of it. Samuelsson is leaning back in the chair grinning as he listens. “Now you know the story of Mortifera a bit and you know the story of the rise of Oblivion while myself and Mayhem were at the peak. Yet once again Bryan Mayhem departed and ran away off to wherever he hides. He thinks this is some kind of game to himself. He is convinced he will win this battle. In fact maybe I will just plot a little course of history between us shall we.” Shaman reaches back and lifts a remote up. Pressing a button as a screen flashes to life. His eyes focused on the screen. The screen begins to play as Shaman leans back looking at the screen. “Bryan you seem to forget that I can beat you. I am more than capable of it. Yet your convinced that you will walk away from the war unharmed. Since when have you ever walked away from a confrontation with me unscathed?” Shaman smirks as he turns his gaze towards the screen as a video montage of Shaman's and Mayhem's confrontations from the past. On the screen shows Shaman's and Bryan Mayhem's matches in order from the past to present. The last one being NC-17 with a question mark. Shaman looks up and grins as he begins to explain a bit of the history. "Remember this date Bryan?" Shaman smiles sadistically. "This is the first day we actually met in the ring. I kept you from losing your title so i could in a surprise take it from you at NC-17 a place where we meet again in the ring. I took your coveted TV Title at that time. I made it mine and made a point known right then and there to everyone in the GCW world." Shaman stands up as he tosses the remote over to Samuelsson. Samuelsson catches it looking at Shaman shrugging. "Hey what is the big idea?" Shaman looks at Samuelsson and shrugs himself. "You'll get over it as i speak now." Shaman paces back and forth across the room looking at the screen. "Bryan you and i have a history so pay attention as i explain it to everyone else. This isn't about what we are going to try and do to each other, fact is it is about what will we not do in our glorious paths of destruction." Shaman cracks his neck from side to side as he has a little glint in his eye not seen in a longtime. "My first title bout here in GCW was for the TV title that you held. We fought in a three way and we found ways to brutalize one another. I introduced tacks and roofing nails to the ring. Such fun at that time. Then i hoisted you and dropped you with the Desert Spike. No man had ever powered you up like that ever. You even told me once while playing chess that that had even shocked you." Shaman steps backwards leaning back a bit in front of the screen looking at the World Wide number. "Damn have i been in GCW that long? Damn man anyways that's not the point." Shaman clears his throat and looks around. His eyes focus looking at Samuelsson. "World wide 62 where Oblivion officially formed unofficially. Bryan and i had realized it was better to cause chaos as a group rather than watch it be spread thin. The guy realized at that time i was a controlled threat. Only to be released when i deemed it necessary. That day we defeated Lia Ambrosi and Virgil Ambrosi in the Main Event as Bryan was looking to tear Lia Ambrosi to pieces." Shaman grins as he walks around and holds a hand over towards Samuelsson who slaps a beer into it. Shaman pops open the beer and takes a long slow swig off of it. "So here comes World Wide 65 where Shaman and Bryan Mayhem come together and make it official. Our war against all in GCW. Did we hide no we made sure everyone saw us coming." Shaman clenches a fist looking at it. His eyes focused on some point not seen to everyone. "Then you gain your coveted World Championship Bryan. You started to lose your way after that. Do you remember which direction you headed shortly there after?" Shaman opens on hand palm up looking across it as the camera focuses close upon his eyes. "Emptiness Mayhem you lost your way within the corporate world of GCW. The tool to be used for the greater good of greed. Caldera marketed you as the Monster Champion at the time. You forgot the path of Oblivion then didn't you?" Shaman paces the room like a caged animal. The muscles in his neck tense and relax as he walks back and forth. "Soon you lost that title didn't you Bryan you lost it abit. Then comes Devils Night as we began regaining momentum. You got beat by Valentine and Ambrosi two people you had owned for a longtime. Then you get buried alive and disappear leaving Oblivion hanging out to dry as i had to fend for myself. I had to get rolling again on my own as we were supposed to be a team and had a plan." Shaman grabs a chair and slings it across the room. His eyes focused and angry this time. Samuelsson looks up at him and grins. "Use that fire you have kept buried. Looks to em that there is unfinished business there." Shaman narrows his gaze towards Samuelsson and then turns around once again. "High Stakes Bryan remember that we met again one on one. Brutal match made me have one of the most brutal memories relived in my whole career. Also made it know that neither of us were going to ever back down from anyone not even each other. You returned after abandoning the formation of Oblivion." Shaman shakes with rage and anger. He focuses and swallows hard as his cheek twitches as the muscle flex in his jaw. "What happened then Bryan? Neither man prevailed did they. Guess you had to realize right then and there our fates were intermingled. The future would soon come to us thought wouldn't it Bryan. Not everyone could keep avoiding us." Shaman drinks some more of the beer as he stays focused on his task at hand. "Then comes World Wide 94 where i beat you with then partner Axel Action. You got to run with James von Doom in that match. Kind of sweet justice if you think about it. Yet it lead us into the most brutal match we had ever had. The Russian Roulette match perhaps the sickest match in GCW history involving two of the most dangerous men. Remember how it turned out Bryan?" Shaman grins as he drinks some more of the beer. Pacing back towards the window he reaches up with his free hand and tears the curtains off the windows. The light shines down now as dust and sunlight fill the room leaving Shaman as a huge shadow walking around the room. "I beat you then Bryan in a brutal war of three separate matches. Not you Bryan i won straight up." Shaman turns throwing the now empty beer bottle through the glass window. Samuelsson dodges the glass and stands on his feet. "We gained the Tag team titles Bryan as Oblivion and then we lost them. We lost them to Banned & Exiled in the third man rule. We were against adding that big old bastard Kimbusa. He became a hindrance always getting in our way and he cost us the tag titles. Then you bailed again like a coward. Now your back. Whoopdeee fucking doo. Your not impressing me or the dog and pony show this time Bryan." Shaman walks to the window and looks out over the snow covered yard. The sun shining brightly over the surface. Shaman walks over to the chair and lifts one one the GCW Tag Team Title belts. Holds it up high and walks to the camera holding it close. "These titles don't say Oblivion now Bryan it says Mortifera. You know what that means ya freaking mime? It means Dangerous things in Latin. You created a fucking monster now get ready to deal with it. In fact i want you to watch something closely. You know where i am?" Shaman pulls the camera and forces it out the window as it focuses on Belmont, England. Shaman points over the grass area to a grave yard. He points at Samuelsson and steps through the window down into the yard. He makes his way across it trudging through the snow. He laughs as he pushes the iron gate open and walks into the graveyard. He walks past a Mausoleum as he begins looking around. Samuelsson walks behind him with a huge Igloo cooler. Shaman stops and looks around. "Found the bonnie lass right here Jorge." Camera pans and notices the name Brenda O'Neil. Shaman reaches into the cooler and pops open a beer sitting down next to the gravestone and puts an arm around it. "Hey toots you seem a bit stiff? Ahh hell don't give me the cold shoulder let's talk about that bouncing bubbly painted mound of flesh known as your son Bryan." Shaman begins to laugh hard as Samuelsson looks at him confused. Shaman looks at Samuelsson and laughs harder. "Sorry man this isn't Mayhem's mother. This is just some poor schlobs mom i picked out oft he graveyard. There are some secrets i wouldn't desecrate or give." Shaman stands up and looks around. "We better get out of here Jorge. I am not sure when the groundskeeper will be back, but i am sure he won't like finding out a Native American and a Viking have come to England and have been tromping around the graveyard drinking." Shaman stops and looks around. "This seems oddly familiar i better call Surreal and see if he remembers something like this." "Point is simple Bryan. You and i always have unsettled business. We aren't going to be any different this time. It is time we both kick the ever living shit out of each other and pray that you have enough to get a double dose of ass kicking. Welcome to hell Bryan because this time i guarantee you will wish you hadn't been down this road with me." Shaman turns and walks away as he pours a beer on the graves as he passes. "Cheers ya stupid rejected Nazi's" Shaman disappears as Samuelsson looks around. "Seems to me there is something that needs to be settled. Rest assured i won't be handing this belt over without the fight of a century Mayhem. Shaman isn't me and I'd be willing to simply break you because i can. There is no greater feeling than breaking someone. Ask Shaman i had done it to him before." Samuelsson walks after Shaman humming and begins to say a nursery rhyme he once heard Shaman say. One , two, gonna break you!! Three, Four, Coming back for more! Five , Six, The children will be sick!! Seven , Eight, It is way too late! Nine , Ten we are gonna do it all over again!! Samuelsson disappears around a Mausoleum leaving nothing but snow tracks showing where they were.
