Date: When he wakes up A cigarette rested between his lips as he waited for RaZiel to wake up. The big man had passed out in the trunk, or possibly Alex’s blow to the back of his head had knocked him unconscious, either way he was still out. He waited, there was going to be a big surprise for the monster of a man once he woke up. There were probably all manner of ways Stevens could have tortured him, but in the end he’d settled on this one. Not exactly torture really, but it’d be annoying, and would probably drive him crazy. That’d at least put a smile on his face, and would certainly take his mind off Sheena. That was making everything unbearable, how in the hell was he supposed to function when every second of every day he was worried about what kind of evil things Lestatt might be doing to her. He’d sworn at Worldwide that if Knight harmed a hair on her head he’d kill the man. It wasn’t an empty threat, he was just now beginning to realize that. Prison would seem like a vacation resort provided he knew Sheena would be safe and her attacker had been taken care of, yeah just like a vacation. At least he was finally back on top. Taking Sheena must have been easy for Knight, nothing but violence and intimidation, the only thing the asshole was good for, Stevens had actually used his brains. That felt good, outsmarting someone always gave Stevens that warm tingle in the pit of his stomach. Locking Knight in his dressing room, well that was just a bonus. The asshole had made things personal, and he was going to find out just how vicious Michael Stevens could be. RaZiel snorted and twisted in his chair, well he tried to anyway. Stevens and Alex had bound him in pretty tight, it wouldn’t do to have a monster like him running around unfettered. Still he didn’t regain consciousness, he was still out cold. Either there was an exhaust leak in the car, or that had been a vicious shot from Alex, neither would be surprising really. Alex had grown to really care about Sheena, and Michael knew he was probably taking her kidnapping hardest of all. When you’re a man like Alex and you only believe your good for one thing, and you fail in that one thing, it’s got to be difficult. Stevens would have to have a talk with him about that soon, probably once RaZiel woke up. After his knee injury Alex had thrown himself into the bodyguard job with an incredible zeal. It was almost as if he felt he’d finally discovered his purpose in life. For the most part a pacifist, the one thing he would always fight for, was the people he cared about. The bodyguard job must have been a blessing for him, he only had to get violent when something he was supposed to protect was in jeopardy. Plus he got be around wrestling, which was something the big lug had loved since he was a kid. A smile twisted at the edges of Michael’s lips as he thought of the huge grin on Alex’s face every time Michael brought him back into the fold. It was almost worth firing him over and over again just to see it. Maybe that’s the reason Stevens always fired him only to hire him back a month or so later, just to see that smile, make the big man happy. God, he must be getting soft. He wasn’t supposed to be thinking about stuff like this, especially not now when everything was so tense. Still he couldn’t help it, everything was just seeming so much clearer after that night with Sheena in the hotel room. Gritting his teeth, he thought back to that horrible night, weeping, feeling like the scum of the earth, held tightly in Sheena’s arms. It was the best awful feeling in the world. And it had made everything so clear, he just couldn’t go on acting the way he always had, things were just different now. But he couldn’t focus on that now, not yet. First he had to get Sheena back, that was his number one priority, and after that of course he’d teach Lestatt a lesson. That was going to be the tough part. He was almost certain Knight had seen the fire in his eyes, and realized just exactly what he’d gotten himself into by daring to touch something that belonged to Stevens. He’d made a mistake, and one that could very well prove fatal if he screwed up. Honestly, he felt that Knight must have seen that, so he tried not to worry too much about what he may be doing to Sheena. Unfortunately that feeling was very small comfort. There was something truly scary about Lestatt Knight. The limits he went to in gaining the world title would be nothing compared to what he’d go through to keep it. For christ sakes the guy nearly set another human being, a former friend on fire for a piece of gold. Just thinking of what he may do to protect that gold sent a small shiver down Michael’s spine. It wasn’t a pleasant thought by any means. Knight was very nearly a sociopath. Whatever the man was that used to dwell in that shell was completely gone, destroyed by his lust for championship gold. There was a glimmer of hope in Eva, certainly she was a cold hearted bitch, but she didn’t deserve the way he treated her. Imagine that sicko sat in her hospital room feigning worry while plotting to steal away the woman that Stevens loved. The very idea sent Michael’s stomach turning. Even at his worst, Stevens had never been that bad. Even if he did use people he always remembered they WERE people, not just objects. Knight doesn’t seem to be able to make that distinction any longer. Stevens would have to step up his game if he wanted to compete with that, and that’s exactly why he was sitting here, right now. Never before had he done something so cold blooded as ambushing another person only to steal them away from their lives. This was dark territory and something Stevens would have to tread carefully around. In an effort to one up Knight, to keep the playing field level he’d stooped to an unheard of low, at least for him. He would try not to enjoy holding RaZiel, well, at least not too much. Another groan from the captive. No, his name was RaZiel, must remember it’s a person, certainly a sick individual, someone who almost certainly had a hand in kidnapping Sheena, but he was still a human being. Of course that meant he had weaknesses to be exploited. It wasn’t difficult coming up with the idea, how exactly he’d “entertain” his guest. Everything about the guy screamed for it. The same way guys walking around in business suits just scream “throw a pie at me”. Yeah, this was going to be good, and he’d enjoy doing it. Maybe he shouldn’t, but hell, alls fair in love and war, and Lestatt had been the one to turn this into a war. So his soldier would have to suffer the consequences. “What, the fuck?” “Oh, good, I’ve been waiting for you to wake up.” “Stevens.” The word was a low growl. The monster was angry, but for all his might he couldn’t move. Perfect the straps were going to hold, and that meant he had the perfect little captive audience. “Yup, that’s me. It appears you’re going to be my guest for the next several days. I hope you find the accommodations comfortable, I’ve gone to a lot of effort. Unfortunately I’ve had to bind you to the chair, so safety purposes you understand, wouldn’t want you to get hurt or anything you know. After all Lestatt did trust me to take good care of you. Oh, you are house trained, right?” The response was unintelligible, just another low throaty rumble. “Well, I guess I’m just going to have to trust that you are. Anyway, Razzie, I’d love to stay and chat, but I have other matters to attend to, but don’t worry, I’ve prepared you some entertainment. Everyone loves music right, I figure I’ve got something right up your alley.” Standing and heading for the door, Stevens flicked on the light switch. Fully illuminated the room was adorned with various posters, mostly boy bands, and other cheesy pop acts. Pressing a button on a nearby CD player the room was filled with those incredible earth shatteringly sublime lyrics. “I’m a Barbie Girl, In a Barbie World, Life In Plastic, It’s Fantastic.” Opening the door Stevens stepped to the outside a smile on his face as he watched RaZiel struggle vainly against his bonds. “I hope you like Europop, that’s a hundred disk changer filled to the brim with Aqua to Yanni. Enjoy asshole.” Laughing, Stevens closed the door behind him as RaZiel threw his head back and roared at the top of his lungs still struggling against the bonds. ~To Be Continued And Shit~
