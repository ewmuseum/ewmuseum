Radio Free Cowboys

5 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-5-08 “Mark Matthews and the Depraved are the opponents for our next guests,” the smarmy host of the Smart Mark Radio Show says as the Cowboys From Hell are seen sitting across from him. “They are Wyatt Colton and Jake “Don’t call me Connor” Ennis, the former FUSE Fatal Faction Champions, the Cowboys From Hell. Welcome to the show guys.” The Cowboys From Hell pull their chairs up to the mics. “It’s good to be here, tonight, Rancid.” Jake pulls his mic closer as he says this. Rancid, the host, looks at the two of them, “So, the two of you are now in Global Championship Wrestling, after what some would say is a rather short, but memorable tenure in FUSE Wrestling, in which the two of you held that company’s tag team gold before the eliminated the Tag Team, or Fatal Faction in their case, Division.” Wyatt looks at the host, “That’s right. Unlike what happened with FUSE, we came to a place where Tag Team wrestling is not only appreciated, but is thriving. You see, me and Jake?” Rancid interrupts, “Jake and I, you mean?” Wyatt gets a rather disgusted look on his face, “I know what I said. Me and Jake, we’ve been up and down the road together for quite awhile, even before we were put together as a tag team. That’s something that most people don’t think about.” Rancid takes this opportunity to jump in again, “What do you think about your opponents, Mark Matthews and the Depraved?” “Well, Rancid, Wyatt and I,” Jake made sure to emphasize those words, “we look at them, and we see ourselves when he teamed up for the very first time. A couple of punks that don’t deserve to be in the ring with a team that rightfully has never been beaten for their titles.” “If you don’t mind, Rancid, I would like to add in a little bit more. You see, we didn’t come to GCW to open shows. Eventually, we are going to get to the top, and be the team that knocks Banned & Exiled”.tilde-exclamation point for the GCW World Tag Team Titles. That means we have to knock around a few other teams in the process. That’s all Matthews and Depraved are. Just a stepping stone.” Wyatt has Rancid laughing at saying tilde and exclamation point out loud. “Now, don’t get us wrong,” Jake adds, “They are probably good, decent singles wrestlers. But in this world, the tag team world, that doesn’t mean anything. You see, being a tag team wrestler is much harder than being a singles wrestler. Not only do you have to worry about yourself out there, but you also have to worry about your partner. “You also have to worry about the fact that instead of one guy out there who wants to separate your head from your shoulders, that you have two guys. That’s why some people can do this, and others can’t. “I guess we’ll see if the Depraved and Matthews have what it takes to be a tag team in this world.” Rancid hits a button on his control panel, “Well, for those of you just joining us, this is Rancid and this is Smart Mark Radio on the World Internet Radio Network. I am joined in studio with GCW Tag Team wrestlers Wyatt “I’m Not a Fucking Martian” Colton and Jake “Shove Brokeback Mountain Up Your Ass” Ennis, and we will be back after this commercial message from our sponsor, Ryland Premium Ketchups.” Rancid hits another button and the commercial starts to play. He takes a drink from the glass that he has sitting next to his control panel. “I thought the two of you were bad asses, I want to hear more about how you hate your opponents.” Rancid takes another sip from the drink. “Where’s the passion, or would you rather talk about how Smitty T. Duluth screwed the two of you?” “Son, if you value the ability to breath, you won’t bring up Smitty. Savvy.” Jake has a rather threatening tone in his voice upon saying this. “Got it.” Rancid doesn’t look too worried. “We’re back, and with us this evening are the Cowboys From Hell, who yes, for all the ladies out there, look good naked. Before we went to break, they were talking about their opponents for the upcoming edition of GCW WorldWide on the X:TV network.” Wyatt again gets a little closer to the mic, “Like we were saying, these are a couple of guys that don’t know what they are getting into when they step into the ring with us. This, to my knowledge, is the first time that they’ve teamed together. Jake and I have been teaming for over a year. We’re “former” Fatal Faction Champions, and we’re the future of GCW. People talk about the Establishment, but they don’t mean much to us.” Wyatt turns it over to Jake, who adds, “After we defeat Matthews and Depraved, we’re going to start to work our way to a match with Storm and Stone, Banned & Exiled’tilde exclamation point. But, we have to defeat Matthews and Depraved first. I don’t want these two little boys to think that we’re going to look past them at a tag title shot that we haven’t gotten yet. No, we’re looking right at them. “They should be scared. You hear me, boys, you should fear us. Not because of what we can do to the two of you, but because of what we will do to the two of you. In that ring, we’re the Sheriffs. At the end of the night, boys. You’re going to find out that we’re also the judge, the jury, and the executioner.” Wyatt adds one last thing, “Because, GCW, We’re Taking Over This Town!” Rancid decides to wrap the interview at that last sentence. “Jake, Wyatt, thanks for coming.”
