The Lost Seg!

28 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 Sep 05 ** This was a seg I wrote and turned in for Fall out but considering the serious nature of the night, Ben felt it would be a bit out of place. I wanted to put it here because I had a blast writing it. ** The cameras come to a scene of a wall, yes a WALL. The crowd’s reaction is pretty non existent and then confusion comes in when a voice is heard. The voice seems very sophisticated, deep. “Welcome Fans!” Slowly the camera travels downwards until it rests on a very, very short person. He has very little hair on the top of his head, which is odd shaped and has just a small patch of hair resting on top of it. A smug smile is on his face as he points to the camera. “GCW let me introduce myself, I am Stewie Griffin and I have recently been hired to get the inside scoop on all of the “superstars” around here. First I have gotten an exclusive interview with the one credible human being here.” Stewie takes a few steps to the right until he is near a door, he then points up to it, the camera travels up a moment to see the name on the door. “LeStatt Knight.” The crowd bursts into a chorus of boos which brings a smile to Stewie’s face. Stewie: Yes I know that you all hate this man, but lets face it ? you all are worthless scum that will one day be slaves in my masterful plan to take over this disgusting word. Now let us knock and chat with the man.” Stewie knocks and await’s the answer with his arms crossed. The door swings open and the camera pans up quickly to see LeStatt staring out a bit confused. He sees the camera and gives a dirty look, then sticks his head out and looks from left to right, but not seeing anyone else. He goes to head back into the room when Stewie screams. Stewie: Down here you big ape! Knight jumps back and looks down, when he realizes who is standing in front of him, a slight smile comes to his face. LeStatt shakes his head as he kneels down to go eye level with Stewie. LeStatt: What the hell are you doing here amigo? Stewie: As I expected ? you BOW TO ME! LeStatt: (chuckling) Whatever you say, but that still doesn’t answer my question. Stewie: Steven Caldera loved me, and compared to the other dead weight he has around here he needed an upgrade. Of course ? (Stewie grabs Knight by his shirt and pulls him closer, looking left to right before speaking.) The DEVICE is a very powerful weapon. You are now sworn to secrecy though ? if you speak, I shall eliminate you! LeStatt: (rolling his eyes) This is suppose to be an interview right? Stewie: Very well then, (he reaches into his pocket pulling out a cue card.) First question, what do you think about your chances to beat Stevens here tonight? LeStatt: Piece of cake Stewie. There are people who are winners, champions ? then there are people who come close but just cannot quite get it, Michael Stevens is the latter. I will make him tap, and make him squeal like a little girl. Stewie: I knew there was a reason why I liked you ? but question number two is, Eva Van Lorne? LeStatt: Next question. Stewie: Excuse me!” LeStatt: Next question, all will be answered in good time. Stewie: Very well then, but you interrupt me again and mark my words ? your uppance will come! LeStatt: My apologies Stewie, anything else. Stewie: Yes question number three, what do you think about your ground break’WHAT THE DEUCE IS THIS WRETCHED GARBAGE!” Who the hell writes this stuff? You know for all the money that GCW makes you’d think they would have a better creative team. One moment LeStatt. (Stewie rushes towards the camera, and gets right into it, his face taking up most of the scene.) Listen here “Powers that Be”, I give you GOLD, ratings, MONEY! I require creative control, world domination, and a vanilla/chocolate swirl ice cream cone ? but NO SPRINKLES! Oh god help you if I find sprinkles! (Stewie backs up a step) Now back to our regularly scheduled program. (Stewie walks back to LeStatt) Alright good luck LeStatt, win, win! Rah-Rah! And all of that ? this interview is over. Stewie walks away from Knight, leaving the camera resting on a confused LeStatt Knight. Knight stares into the camera and gives an awkward smile before Stewie yells out. “MOVE IT IMBECILE!”
