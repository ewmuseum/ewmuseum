Date: 12/27/10 CHAPTERV “Where y’all headin’?” the cute brunette asks Danny. Taken back a bit, the GCW rookie glances back to the black Escalade and shrugs before looking back to the pretty little thing with a sexy Southern drawl as smooth as sweet tea. “Nowhere really. Thinkin’ about a trip to Windsor. What about you?” She parts her lips and her bright white smile seems to stun Danny for a few seconds, “Same place! Aww, shucks. Think you guys could spare a girl a ride?” Danny steps back from the situation, thinking about if he should bring her into the thick of this shitstorm, and then figures she might be more of an asset if things go South. He lowers his designer shades and gives her another up and down. Her olive eyes and golden skin are enough to draw Danny into her tight curves and ample breasts. He winks, “Grab your gear, darlin’.” She jumps up and down and gives Danny a hug, kissing him on the cheek. She darts off to her last ride, an eighteen wheeler, and grabs her brown leather backpack. She waves to the bearded trucker before heading off toward the Escalade with Danny. “So what’s your name?” he takes a gulp of water. “Please tell me it’s Daisy…” She turns back over her shoulder and flashes a bright smile. Danny shakes his head and figures it out quick - the trip to Windsor just got two-hundred times better. CHAPTERVI “Danny motherfuckin’ Francisco!” Big C’s loud, booming voice is known from border to border simply for his contributions to the American drug trade and his infamous Samuel L. Jackson impressions. He’s looking to make his name as an arms dealer now and the Trick is the one connection to let him make that move. Cesar and Roach bolt into the crowd, making their way to Big C, who sits in the corner with a gaggle of gorgeous geese. Cesar reaches the table as Big C eyeballs Danny and Lacy from across the room. Danny wanders toward the bar as Lacy bounces onto the dance floor when the music comes back to life. “Jack and Coke,” Danny nods sliding onto a stool. The bartender smiles and heads off for the order. Unbeknownst to Danny, Big C gives the bartender the nod and continues the introductions with Roach and Cesar. The GCW rookie gets his drink and is waved off when he offers up some money. Suspicious, he turns toward Big C’s corner. “Big C?” Danny leans in to the bartender. The man tries to hide it but can’t fight back a shy smile. Dynasty wonders for a few minutes if this guy is gay but figures it’s his extra gay way of playing hard to get and shrugs it off. Before Danny knows it, a group of young girls is at his back and his Slut Senses begin to go bonkers. “Can I…” he flashes a handsome smile. “Help you girls?” The blond in the front, the Viper (feisty, sexy, thinks she’s in charge), locks eyes with Danny in that lusty gaze; “Are you… a wrestler?” “That’s right,” Danny takes a gulp from his glass of goodness. “You girls fans?” The blond smirks, “Of yours? Heh.” “I love that Jay Terror,” one of the hot girls from the sides says in a dull roar. “Those tattoos are so hot.” Danny smiles at the thought she might be into tattoos. She catches sight of his forearms and then shoots him those Fuck Me! eyes, biting her lower lip. He grins, realzing she’s too easy, and moves onto the next. The blond demands his attention yet again, throwing her hair back. “I would be your fan,” she snaps. “You don’t win though.” An awkward hush sweeps through the group of girls which puts a mean little smile on the blond bitch. Danny nods, taking the blow as best he can, and slams the rest of his drink down the hatch. He calls and motions for another cocktail in a smooth, sly gesture before turning to face her all the way. “Tough guy doesn’t have a comeback?” she weaves her head opposite her hips and coaxes Danny into the proverbial parking lot for a verbal roundabout. “Can’t think of anything?” Danny slowly reaches inside his jacket and pulls out his smokes. He reaches onto the bar and picks up a matchbook and lights one of the smokes. Taking a drag, he exhales it directly in her face and stands up quickly. “Listen little girl,” he takes a long drag before continuing. “I know Daddy didn’t love you or Mommy but I ain’t the one you wanna pick a fight with. In fact, I’m the last fuckin’ guyu you wanna call out ‘cause I’m no gentleman. I’m the opposite. I’ll knock your pearly-white teeth right down your dick-chuggin’ throat if you keep givin’ me that tone and you know damn well I’m not bluffin’.” The blond girl lets her drop hit the floor as Danny takes only the third drag from his cigarette. He nods his head, scanning her up and down, and then looks back at her through a half-hearted glare. “It’s a damn shame, too, ‘cause those boots and that dress…” Out of nowhere, their brunette hitchhiker flies out of left field and cold cocks the blond before she throws a few more closed fists at her entourage of gold diggers. Bouncers try to tame the mob of maidens but can’t contain the overhand, overhead slaps that fly from all around. The brunette lands most of them, showing her fiery side and loyalty to the kind guys who gave her a lift. Danny and her lock eyes, the brunette flipping her hair out of her face and smiling. “Nice to meet ya,” she extends her hand out with a swollen right hand. “I’m Lacy.” In shock and pure happiness, the young American reaches out and catches the soft, gentle grip of the southern girl sent to them from the open road. Seconds float by as the two get lost in thought and translation between body language. His head picks up and she twirls on the flat part of her boots. Danny turns quickly when he feels tapping on his shoulder to find Cesar and Roach standing next to him. Roach eyes the girls as Cesar conducts the business, wrapping his arm around Danny. Before he gives Cesar his full attention, Danny turns back to the blond but she’s long gone. Cesar turns Danny’s head by the neck with command, “Ready to take care of business?” Danny looks up and notices the lack of a sizeable black man in the room. The bartender sets a full glass of Tennessee whiskey and Coca Cola in front of Danny’s cautious eyes. He takes a good amount off the top before setting it down and looking at Cesar. Remembering the fat stack of cash in his pocket, Danny steps away from the bar and thinks about his deal with the Trick as he eyes the remaining whiskey-cola blend. He grips the glass, Let’s finish this. CHAPTERVII Danny steps outside the hotel room with a cigarette pinched between his lips. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a lighter. He lights the flame and cups his hands against the brisk Canadian nighttime breeze. The door opens and Lacy pops her lovely mug outside, wearing Danny’s old Arizona State University hoodie. “I hope you don’t mind,” she says, smiling innocently. Danny grins as he leans up against the balcony and laughs at that old rag worn by a number of nameless sets of legs before her. He shakes his head indifferently, inhaling for a few silent seconds before gazing out into the night as a cloud of smoke billows from his lungs. She leans on the cold steel next to him before pointing at the smoke, “Is that a cigarette?” Danny exhales and shakes his head, laughing lightly. “Don’t tell Teacher, okay?” “Got anymore?” she laughs. “I could really use one.” “Sure…” Danny studies her facial expression. “Everything alright?” She nods and he waits a few seconds before pulling out his metal cigarette case, opening it with one hand and letting her pick her favorite. He flicks the flame to life and cups his hand over it, letting her lungs fill with fun and nicotine-flavored goodness. She takes a relaxing drag and her shoulders fall back without tension. “Thanks,” she sighs. “I needed this.” Danny waits again before saying anything and decides to change the subject, “Where’d you learn to throw a right hook like that?” Lacy locks eyes with the sarcastic schmuck and can’t help but laugh. Danny grins as he motions his hand out to the limitless night sky with the smoke pinched between his fingers. A plume of smoke escapes him and floats over his head. “Lady’s gotta learn how to keep herself outta trouble,” Lacy explains. “Especially when trouble happens t’be findin’ it’s way into her life.” Danny knows what that means and takes another assessment of the girl to his right. She seems alright but to the untrained eye that might seem like a sign that she’s ok. Danny knows any girl this small, this pretty, and this strong doesn’t live on the road by choice. What’s she running from? “I suppose you’re guessin’ why I’m so far from home,” she breaks the silence. Danny shrugs, “I suppose you’re about to tell me.” She looks at him with her big eyes beginning to water up. She jerks her head away, throwing her hair around her head and in her face before Danny can read her anymore. He knows that she’s gonna need a shoulder soon and she knows it’s gonna be him. Lacy turns back to Danny, arching her brow, as Danny exhales into the night’s sky yet again. He flicks the butt over the edge and watches it fall before turning back to Lacy, with a strong look on his face. “I don’t wanna know,” his tone is stern. Lacy shakes her head, “I really don’t-“ “Look Lacy,” Danny cuts her off and turns, facing her and looking her deep in the eye with a sympathetic look. “I don’t care. You don’t wanna know who I used to be and I don’t need to know who you are either. Right now, I’m just a man lookin’ for a livin’ and if I happen to come across a beautiful little Southern belle I can help out on my way to wherever I’m goin’… I’ll lend her a hand if I can.” Headlights light up Lacy’s face full of shock and draw Danny’s attention toward the parking lot. He notices the black El Camino pull up toward the staircase. The passenger side window rolls down and Leon’s head pops out. He nods at Danny and exits the car. He stops at the bottom of the stair case and crosses his hands over his waist. “I’ll be right back,” Danny whispers in a serious tone. “Get inside and lock the door, alright? Don’t open it for anybody. I have my key.” Lacy watches the man flash his hardware from under his jacket and then looks at Danny. “Be careful.” Danny stomps off but her words register after a few seconds. He turns back around and offers her a charming grin, “Never.” He shuffles down the stairs rather quickly to get this midnight rendezvous over with. It’s not often that somebody in the public realm can meet with a known drug kingpin out in public but, after all, this is Canada. Danny notices Leon as he comes down the final few stairs and then looks at the car. He hops in the passenger door and looks at the driver. “Big C,” Danny nods. Big C grins, “How ya doin’ Danny Boy? Take a ride with me.” Danny watches the curb float away from the car as the ground starts to pass beneath his feet. Through the spots of melting ice on his window, Danny watches Lacy head back into the hotel room. He wonders where she’ll be when, and if, he gets back. “So what’s new D?” Big C pulls a tightly wrapped blunt from against the speedometer. “Still gettin’ down with tha chronic?” Danny shoots Cecil a curious eye, “You still hit it like a bitch?” Big C hesitates briefly before burst out into a boisterous laugh. Danny shakes his head and finds a suitable radio station as Cecil’s side of the car clouds up with smoke. He takes in a deep breath before holding his lips shut. Big C passes it as his raises a finger. “So I been thinkin’ Francisco,” he lets the smoke escape his lungs. “You ‘n me make a dynamite team. You got tha flash, tha flair and tha style… ‘n I got tha connections and tha street smarts to make us a fortune. Why don’t you come up here, settle down for a lil’ bit, ‘n come work for me?” “As crazy as it sounds Cecil,” Danny nods, smiling because he likes to sound crazy. “that might work. I’m a one man show these days though, been wrestling with Global Championship-” “You think you gon’ make any money workin’ as a ring jockey?” Cecil shakes his head. “Maybe… maybe not,” Danny takes a whack of the fine piece of smoker’s art. “I’m just tyin’ up some loose ends and get back to work. I should have known puttin’ my face back out there was gonna bring up some bad memories. I’m just here to take care of this one.” “So you know why you up here?” Big C takes his eyes off the road, trying to read his passenger’s face. “Tha Trick sure wasn’t too happy when you went Balboa on that dude from St. Louis. I think he said you cost him close to four-hundred thousand G’s?“ “There’s more I wish I knew about that deal,” Danny jerks his head. “What I do know is that I’m not stickin’ around to get that lost again.” Big C grins, “There’s a lot to be said about a man who can admit that, Francisco. Ya know?” “I’m gonna see this thing through,” Danny knows enough to not say anything too telling. “After that, I’m out. I met a man in Mexico… Diego… and he opened my eyes. I used to think I had no choice, had no options, and that I had to be who the people want me to be. Now I know I’ve got to be who I want to be.” “Who’s that D?” Big C laughs at his own jab. “The guy who pisses everybody off?” An ambitious glint spreads over Danny’s eyes, “If that’s what it takes…” He looks out the car window and watches as Cecil wheels the car into an empty parking lot. Oblivious to the change in setting, Danny watches the moon stay constant as the stars spin around it. He thinks about his opportunities to really put the world in his palm when he takes on Shaman. Whatever it takes. Cecil stops the car, “Now about this exchange, Francisco.” Snapping him back from his high hopes of owning speed boats and posh penthouse sweets, Danny realizes he’s got a long road ahead before he can claim himself the best thing the wrestle in the ring. No matter how the cake gets cut, he knows that there would be no parade if he’s dead or broken thanks to his black past. The two lock eyes. “Where is it?” Cecil asks. “Where are we?” Danny changes the focus of the situation. “A safe place,” Cecil says, unconvincingly. “where’s the cash, Danny?” Danny examines his surroundings over silence and then looks Cecil directly in the eye, “A safe place.” Cecil’s nostrils flare and he shakes his head with a bitter smile. “You done fuckin’ around yet?” “No. I’m not,” Danny points to the proverbial trap. “You think I’m stupid? I know what happens when I give you that cash. I become expendable. Fuck you and fuck off. You’ll get the cash when I-“ “Chill, chill brotha,” Cecil taps Danny on the shoulder. “That ain’t how this works, D. The game has changed since you been ‘round here. The Trick wants to do business so let’s do business. As soon as I see the cash, you guys head off with the goods and the partnership begins.” “Take my word for it,” he flashes a giant smile at Cecil. “It’s here. Trust me… and make it happen. You’ll get the cash when I get the product.” Cecil snarls and wrenches on the key, bringing the car to life for a few seconds before looking over at Danny. Both wait to hear what the other has to say before breaking eye contact. “You gonna be all right, Danny,” Cecil nods. “you gonna be all right.” The car pulls out of the darkness and back onto the road. Danny knows that Big C already has tabs on him judging by the black sedan that’s been behind them ever since they left his club. He knows that this is no casual deal. The sedan pulls into the hotel parking lot after a silent ride back. Danny spots Lacy still standing outside the hotel room as he steps out of the car. The passenger side window rolls down as Leon slides into the car. Danny turns around after lighting a cigarette. With one foot on the staircase and the other in mid air, Big C locks eyes with his old customer one last time. “Go treat that girl to a fine time,” Cecil laughs before pulling off. Danny knows he should, he knows he could, but as he looks up at Lacy a new thought runs through his head. I gotta get her the fuck out of here. CHAPTERVIII Big C sits down and unbuttons his suit jacket. His eyes hide behind thick, big black sunglasses and behind him stand two massive bodyguards. Big C finally cracks a smile and throws his big mitts on the table, his fingers banging on the table from all of his gold rings. He puffs and pulls on the flame, lighting the end to an orange glow. “This is Leon,” he thumbs to the other, “and his brother, Deon. Deon shoves him down and Leon tromps over to the clearing with his brother. Both men rip open their jackets and reveal an array of throwing knives. Each man unleashes into the giant corkboard behind Cesar and Roach. The Trick’s thugs give each other a wide-eyed stare before slowly turning their heads back to the knife-chucking brothers, Leon and Deon. Danny snatches up the Zippo and lights a cigarette. I’ll take my paycheck and penthouse in South Beach, thank you very much.” “Funny you should say that,” Cecil puffs on his Cuban. “I heard you was down in Mexico last?” Danny nods amusingly and works out how to answer this in his head. “I was, yeah. Last time I checked, you were in DC still. Things change though and people move… it’s all a part of the game.” “Is part of the game workin’ for the Trick, too?” Big C looks on with his watchful eyes. “You come up here to do his work then?” Against his better judgment, Danny remains silent. The first is that they are both right-handed, an oddity in twins, but an important piece of information if things should get froggy. The second thing it tells him is that they’re young, antsy, and anxious for some action. “Fuck you, Cecil,” Danny grins. “I gotta eat, too.” Big C keeps the stone face up long enough to trick everyone other than the man across the table and he throws his hands back. We got some business to talk about.” As Deon does his bidding, Big C points to Cesar and Roach. “So which one’a you got tha cash?” Danny taps his chest, “Two hundred and fifty large…” “Ooooohh shit!” Big C nearly hits the roof. “Where’s it at?” Danny glances to Roach and Cesar, whose expression isn’t normal and then looks at the hunger in Big C’s eyes. He knows that no matter how many times he asks her ‘are you okay?’ or ‘you alright?’ -- if he’s not careful she may not be able to answer those questions. “Where are we going now?” her voice is soft and afraid. “Is it over?” Danny looks over at her sweet olive eyes before slightly expressing some sort of hope with a smile. “We’ll be alright.” Get to Cleveland... beat Shaman... Danny's eyes burst open with the answer. Get some fuckin' sweet justice.
