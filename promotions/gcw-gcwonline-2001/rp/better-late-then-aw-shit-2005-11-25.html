Better late then...aw shit!

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-25-05 'Where do I begin?' We slowly fade in from a blank screen into a world full of color and life. We actually find ourselves inside of the apartment of one, Jayce Bradley. The fact that the place is a bit cleaner doesn’t change that fact. We move along with the camera crew around the apartment until we see an open passageway, where a few rays of sunlight are shinning through. Finally reaching this passageway, we turn and look to see a light almost blinding the camera. The glare is too great to see anything. All of a sudden the light disappears and we see a bright and shining face. That being of course Jayce Bradley. He smiles at the camera and then seemingly returns to the stool in his kitchen. He leans against the counter looking toward the camera. “I guess I could start with the most obvious. That of course being that I will be getting my first cruiserweight title shot. What does that really say about me” Maybe I’m not good enough to be given one in the first place. Who really knows.” Jayce sits up and stops leaning to his side. “Something a little more obvious and a little more well-known. My opponent, the current champion, is Alex Winters. A man that enjoys rubbing his hands inside the inner thighs of another man. Playing it off as some football game. Yeah, right.” The sarcasm flows perfectly through his voice. “I guess I could come out here and talk about meaningless facts of my life, but I don't want to bore myself. If you wish to do that I can tell you later, football. Now how about we move on to some observations I have made while looking over you... Now if I happen to say something wrong, don't be a silly shit and make a big fuss about it. Just correct me and I will accept that.” Pause. “There is one thing that I have noticed, and that is that I'm completely different from all of the other opponents you've faced throughout your run here, but yet I'm somewhat similar. I’m sorry if that is a bit too confusing for someone of your caliber. You see I've gone down the same route as all those other guys. I've tried that destiny bullshit and it failed miserably. I mean in all seriousness, it messes with your head worse than a cheerleader on Prom Night.” “I believe that I have tried just about everything I could to intimidate, confuse, and shock an opponent. Nothing really works. You see you are going to think whatever you want anyway. So why should I bother trying to impress you with past glory. I don't think that will scare you. If it does... let me know... that'll be a first.” Jayce puts his fingers up like quotation marks. “It seems that you like to rely on luck a little more than others. You rather use that than any talent that you may have. That's fine, I'm cool with that. It's your thing. I just don’t deal with luck, I prefer to count on my talent in the ring, not some game that I played as a kid.” Jayce shakes his head. “Lets be honest with each other. We really don't know jackshit about the other. If you even seem to think you know one thing about me, I will just laugh at you and assume you smoked some bad shit.” “With that being said why don't you be a pal and tell me a little bit about yourself. Nothing too personal please. I don't need to be ill from whatever stories you provide for me. So just give me the gist about you. I really don't want to have to put any effort out about trying to locate information about you. That could be quite troublesome and I am not in the mood for childish games like that.” Jayce rolls his eyes. “I'm probably asking a lot of you, but I have one last thing to ask for. Would you please not bring up the past” That is such a boring topic. The fact that you had two title shots before I held one has no relevance, and neither does the fact that I ran down your jungle bunny buddy. Hopefully you will be the first intelligent?” “...let me retract that statement and say this one instead?” “I’m hoping you will be the first person, you really don't need to be intelligent seeing how that would hurt you more than ever, to not bring it up. If you don't I thank you, but if you can't seem to get past that then go on your merry way and I will be the one to stop your goal line drive and take that title from you.” “Speaking of your title. I will be like the general public when facing an opponent and say I will beat you. What could be better for me to do now” I get to make amends with myself and put my own demons to rest.” Jayce stands from his seat and takes a peek out the blinds in his home. The light comes back over to screen the camera again. Jayce quickly turns back to see the light directly at the camera and then closes the blinds. “Sorry I forgot about the lighting. And to football, I apologize to you for jumping to any conclusions on you, but that's what seems to always happen. Oh well, what are you going to do” I suppose you could cry about it, but I would prefer you didn't because I don't deal too well with whiny jocks that don’t get their way.” “All I can hope for is that you focus on the match and not on your past accomplishments. That would be a damn shame to see such a great champion get whooped because he thinks he’s running for the Heisman.” Pause Again. “I'm sure I will be hearing from you soon sunshine. If anything I have said has hurt your feelings or bothered you in any fashion’don't worry, I’ll be out of your hair as soon as I snatch that title from around your waist.” Jayce turns to the camera and winks. “And there’s nothing you can do to stop me.”
