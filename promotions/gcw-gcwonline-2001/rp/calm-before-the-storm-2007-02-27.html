Calm before the Storm

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/2007 Katie’s breathing slowed, and she stretched her arms to her sides as she balanced lightly on the balls of her feet. Her chest was barely moving, her shoulders didn’t twitch at all, yet her breathing came in a deep, even rhythm. She bent over and touched her knees... with her forehead. Her hands wrapped around her ankles. For a full minute she didn’t move. Slowly her knees bent. She lowered herself to her knees and rested them on the mat. Her torso slowly arched back; her spine bending like a gumby doll. Her arms went over her head and she touched her palms to the floor behind her. Her breathing continued in the same slow, deep, even rhythm. Slowly her legs came out from underneath her, as her feet rested on the mat and her whole body was arched backward like a bridge. “Haaiiiiiii.....” With seemingly no telegraphing movements from any part of her body, she vaulted up into the air, her body twisting counter-clockwise into a 180-degree turn as her left leg and foot swung in an upward-left arc. Her left heel raked across the small suspended speed bag, bouncing it uncontrollably back and forth. “...yyyyaaAAAAAAHHHHH!!!” The Monarch Butterfly landed on her feet and danced around the bag, arcing into it with rapid, accurate savate kicks. Her feet flew through the air so fast that nobody could determine the exact left foot – right foot sequence. The colors on her hi-top athletic shoes seemed to form streaks of color around her body, almost bathing her in an eerie, dark blue and dark yellow light. Lightning! Cartwheeling backward, Katie lunged in again with a driving high front-snap kick. Her foot planted down sharply to provide a stable base as she whirled clockwise on it. The blade of her right foot blasted across the bag with an audible smacking noise, rocking it wildly from side to side on its attachment hinge. Thunder! Now the diminutive Irishwoman’s gloved fists went to work, peppering the bag with savate punches and elbow strikes. Fast as her legs and feet moved, her arms and hands moved even faster. The orange stripes across her gloves seemed to blur around her in a pattern of orange color. It was all optical illusion, of course, but it made watching Katie in action a mesmerizing experience. Katie’s body pulled inward like a coiled spring. Tension snapped into her every sinew. She pushed off on her left foot, hurtling forward in a dazzle of blazing fast motion. Her right foot snaked outward, forward in a side thrust kick that tore open the leather outer covering of the speed bag. Stuffing fell liberally out of the bag as it bounced wildly back and forth on the hinge, showering the immediate vicinity with foam. Rain! A light sheen of perspiration sparkled across Katie’s skin as her diminutive body finally came to a stop. She was starting to breath a little hard, and she believed that one should train, but not strain. She pulled the orange scrunchie out of her hair, letting it free. A small shake of her head fluffed her hair around her, and her fingers kept it away from her eyes. She grabbed a bottle of Gatorade and drank down a big gulp. “Hi Katie,” said Stuart, Katie’s agent as she stepped out of the workout room in the gym. “Ready for your match?” Katie rubbed a small towel across her skin and nodded curtly. “Sure and I am ready,” she said. Her voice held a hard edge to it. Stuart read the edge in her voice quickly. “Payback on your mind, I see.” “Oh yes,” Katie answered. “Remember that I have taken on storms afore. Like Summer Storm back in 2002.” Stuart grinned briefly and patted Katie on the shoulder. “I remember... probably one of your proudest events ever. Worldwide 62 won’t be as big an event, but you don’t need to be at a pay-per-view to write your name on the map, do you?” Katie shook her head. “All I need is a little vendetta... and a big victim.”
