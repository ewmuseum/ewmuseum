Fuck My Life

21 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 21, 2009 June 21, 2009 New York City, New York Madison Square Garden Markus and Chris sit across from each other. Still covered in sweat. Still unchanged after their match. The television in their locker room plays the live feed of Rampage 2009. Neither man has said a word to each other after losing to Vivica Valentine and Lindsay Troy. Chris and Markus have worked together for years, and know each other better than most life long friends. Right now, Chris knows better than to try and talk to Markus. He's seen his friend this way before, dejected, depressed, and angry at the world. Markus briefly looks up, sighing heavily. Chris hopes this is the perfect time to break the silence. "Dude.." he starts, softly and full of apology. Holding a hand up, Markus stops his friend before he can fully commit to the act of comfort. "Not now man." With a whisper Markus brings the silence crashing back down. Looking at his friend, Chris can't help but feel helpless. Everybody in the world knows the inner child these two men bring to the stage. Aside from Tessa, nobody saw through Markus' facade besides Chris. Not even Alicia, the on again off again love of Markus' life. "Then we're just gonna sit in silence? Sweet," the joke flows smoothly, and Chris waits to see a familiar grin on Markus' face. Markus stands, grabs his bag, and walks to the shower. Chris turns his head to the television, having his attention drawn by the words Tessa and Windsor. A faint smile appears around his eyes. _________________________ Drying off quickly, Markus hurries to dress himself. Grabbing his bag, he walks back to the seat he occupied before. Glancing at Chris, Markus sees the happiness and longing mixed on his face. Turning to the television, he is unsurprised to see Tessa on the screen. Markus' heart drops. Maybe I shouldn't make things so hard on my friends, he thinks to himself. After all, Chris already tried to pull him out of his funk. Then again, wouldn't a good friend try harder to console his friend? Wouldn't a good friend ignore the ex-girlfriend on the television and help Markus get past his depression. Then again, maybe Markus is the shitty friend? Here we go again, Markus tells himself half sarcastically. Reaching into his bag, he grabs the fifth of Jack Daniels he bought earlier in the day. "Here's to Banned and Exiled," he raises the glass to Chris, trying his hardest to fake a smile. "Tilde, exclamation.." Chris stops when he turns to look at his friend. The hope that Markus would be in a better mood after the shower is gone, replaced by genuine concern. Life is generally only filled with two types of unhappy Markus: sulking Markus, and angry Markus. When Pretend-to-be-happy Markus shows up, bad things happen. "Are you gonna be okay man?" Markus looks at the bottle on his hand, and opens his mouth. Something catches in his throat, refusing to let the lie slip out. Markus looks down, refusing to make eye contact with his best friend. Instead, he raises the bottle one more time before walking out of the room, searching for the arena exit. Chris sags in his seat, watching Tessa's hand raised in victory. _________________________ Markus soon finds himself on 33rd and 12th Ave, only a few blocks from The Garden, staring at the water. The bottle in his hand nearly empty, Markus is already feeling the effects of the alcohol and decides that sitting down by the water would be a good idea. Nothing quite as calming as watching green slime move around New York. As he stumbles across the street, Markus is amazed at the general lack of traffic. Not for nothing, but a desire to be hit by a moving vehicle isn't exactly missing from his mind at this point in time. Luckily for those who still care for him, he manages to make his way across the two streets without incident. One chain-link fence later, and Markus is sitting on the edge of the waterfront, legs dangling above the water, reflection dimly dancing back at him. Markus takes a swig from his bottle, and lets the whiskey burn in his mouth for a moment before swallowing. Looking at his reflection, Markus shakes his head. I can't believe we lost. Guess that's what happens when you get real tag team competition. Like that time LK beat us. One fuckin' man. And Tessa, don't forget about her. Yeah, Tessa. Gotta wonder if Chris would still be so smitten if she made him the victim that time, rather than me? Probably not, those two are smitten. True love and all that. Why can't we find that? With Alicia? Yeah. Because we fucked that up. More than once. Yeah. And then we made sure she'd never come back. Yeah. And then we secluded ourselves from the world. Forgot about that part. The part where we sit alone at home every night we aren't flying to an event? Yeah, that's the one. ... ... So now what? Maybe we should jump? I don't think we'll make it to the top of any buildings around here. This is probably just a phase. Yeah, a phase. Like the last ten years of our life. Yeah. Do we ask too much of our friends? Probably. We can't even take care of ourselves. But shouldn't they help us? They have to help themselves, too. But shouldn't.. I mean, friends are more important to me than me. Yeah, but that's because we suck. We suck. Yeah. ... ... Markus takes a drink of his bottle. I am pathetic. Markus can't believe he's having this talk with himself. Again. Leaning forward, Markus tries to study his reflection. "Fuck you." Lifting the bottle, Markus carefully aims, and drops the bottle on his head. The image shatters and ripples into a swirl of colors. "Shit." Without taking the time to think rationally, Markus pushes himself off his ledge. Determined to get his whiskey back, Markus submerges himself in the water. The water chills him instantly, but Markus pushes himself deeper. Can't be too deep? GET THE FUCK OUT OF THE WATER! Markus ignores himself, pulling himself down. Jack's my only friend tonight, we gotta save him. GET THE FUCK OUT! Markus swivels around, trying to find the bottle on the black and green darkness. We can't give up on him. Markus opens his mouth and sucks in, searching for air. Water rushes in to fill the gaps, and panic sets in. Fuck. Oh shit. Ohmygod. I'm dea... The thought fades to darkness. _________________________________ Stone floors, colored a creamy white, match the stone walls, also creamy white. Markus looks around the room. Though he is unable to recall ever being here before, the house he stands in feel eerily familiar. The windows are frosted, snow piles blanket the sills. Markus looks out, but all his vision is met with is white snow that eventually fades into blue. Home. Not sure how he knows this, Markus turns to walk towards a chair he shouldn't know exists. Sluggishly, he moves, as if struggling against an invisible force. The dark red chair faces a fireplace. Though the logs a presently burning, Markus feels no heat, and notices no light being given. In fact, he feels quite cold. Markus glances about as he sits, searching for the source of the light. He is quite unsuccessful. In fact, the house is completely bare. No pictures, no ornaments, nothing save for the chair and the flame. "Welcome home." Markus is comforted by the unfamiliar voice, welcoming and sultry at the same time. Turning to face her, Markus recognizes the face he has never seen before. Standing easily as tall as he, with raven hair and sharp features, the woman advances towards him. Markus says and does nothing as she reaches forth, placing a milky white hand on his shoulder. The cold pierces through his shirt, tingling his fingertips. "Thanks, good to be back." "Not yet." Markus understands, but doesn't have a clue what she's saying. "I feel strange." She smiles at him, completely unsettling. Markus sags in his chair, and looks back at the flame. Above it, on the mantle, rest a shining, golden belt. Plates of shining gold, reflecting light and projecting it back towards him. Correction, producing light. Markus realizes the belt is the source of the light in the room. Somehow blazing forth through all the windows, providing illumination for all the world. "Is it always this quiet here?" He turns back to her, already knowing the answer to the question he's never asked. "Always. It's comforting, isn't it?" She blinks, slowly. Her hands slides from his shoulder as she advances towards the mantle. Shivers ripple through him. "Yes. No." Markus stands and follows her. Her graceful movements glide through the atmosphere as Markus trudges towards her at half-speed. "A dream, no?" She turns back to him, one hand caressing the mantle. Markus stops trying to catch up with her, and follows her hand as it slides across the gold. A jealous tinge ignites in his chest. "Always." Through gritted teeth he speaks. "At what cost?" "Everything." "I accept." Hand dropping, the light begins fading as she moves away from the belt. The flames retreat into the wood that produced them. She takes two steps towards him, and places her now glowing hand on his chest before Markus can manage to move. In the span of a blink, the house is filled with water. As he reflects on what she said, Markus can't help but notice how beautiful she looks submerged. This is it then. This is the end? She shakes her head, and smiles condescendingly at him. Markus doesn't understand how she heard his thought, though he knew she would. Violently, he convulses on her hand. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, fifteen times. Chest expands once, twice. Convulses once, twice, fifteen times. Markus tries to raise his hands, tries to push her away. The water is too heavy, his limbs won't respond. His chest expands once, twice. He opens his mouth to protest. No words come out. Only history. He watches in disbelief as he retches in imagery and metaphor. Thoughts and memories taking shape yet nonexistent. Nothing makes sense to him, yet he understands with crystal clarity. Tears run down his face, thicker than water. "Don't cry, you've no time for that." She reaches up, brushing at his eyes. Warmth floods through him. _________________________ Heaving to his side, Markus vomits. Water, bile, and whiskey splash the ground next to him. Looking around, Markus makes eye contact with the woman kneeling over him. She stares shocked at him, water, bile, and whiskey dripping from her mouth and chin. Turning her head, she vomits with him. As his eyes focus, he briefly wonders if she is the woman he just saw. She turns her head back to him, pushing her blond hair back from her face. Markus' mind races, grasping at what he just saw, fighting through the remaining haze of the alcohol. You alright? Yeah, we're fine. Let's go. Markus stands, pushing the woman's grasping hands away from him as he walks away, water dripping from him.
