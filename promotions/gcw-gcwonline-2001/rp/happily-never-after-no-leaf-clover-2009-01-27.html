Happily Never After... (No Leaf Clover)

27 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 27, 2009 "For all you 'When The Levee Breaks' fans out there, Markus Blaire's release from a local rehabilitation facility is 'soon' one spokesperson from the facility told our staff today. Many witnesses have been quoted as saying Blaire looks 'at peace' and 'relaxed' since entering the facility. He's even been spotted sporting a new haircut. Gone now are the long, mysterious black locks that made up so much of his enigmatic persona. Now he's sporting a short black 'fauxhawk.' We'll give you more news on the Best-Selling Author when it's available." -- News 3 Las Vegas "Next we have an update on the Markus Blaire story we brought to you a few days ago. Officials at the local rehabilitation clinic that spoke with our staff on the conditions of anonymity told us that Markus Blaire could be released as early as tomorrow, and that work has already been started there at the facility on the sequel to Best-Seller 'When The Levee Breaks'." -- News 3 Las Vegas "Why should we even care about this guy, really? He wrote a good book, so did that wrestler guy Mick Foley. Mick Foley isn't a writer, he's a wrestler. And that isn't what Markus is famous for either. He's famous for the parties at this point. He's famous for being a rock star. He's going to rehab because it's the cool thing to do. In reality the Markus Blaire guy is a waste of space and is polluting our television screens. I mean this guy is just Kevin Federline famous. He dates Vivica J. Valentine, and then we're supposed to care about this alcoholic scumbag? Who really gives a damn? You say he's going to rehab but I know this guy's type. He'll be back in the bar in 2 weeks partying like a rockstar. In 5 years this guy will be dead on some street corner and you know who'll care? Sure won't be me." -- News Radio Pundit A horde of photographers and media surround the outside of a building made of glass and cream colored plaster. The searing bright sun reflected off the blue-tinted glass entrance of the building, causing most of the observers to shield their eyes in protection from the rays. Blindness hardly affects their desire to get the good shot, the big scoop, the picture that'll start the speculation that Markus Blaire is off the wagon before he even leaves the rehabilitation property. There are thousands of dollars just on the other side of that glass lobby. That's what the world has come to, unfortunately. Publishers of smut magazines forking out hundreds of thousands of dollars for any sort've picture they could use to distort the reality into something to make headlines. Any quote that can be taken out of context in order to perverse its intentions to make somebody look like someone they're not. The view outside of the rehab clinic was a sick, sick sight into the harsh reality of today's society. It was time to see if Markus Blaire could build his fences and set restrictions. It was time to see what the kid was made of. On cue the automatic doors slid open and security braced the barriers for the exit of Markus Blaire. Accompanied by personal friend and member of the Ambrosi Estate, Alexis Key he walked into the sunlight. His hair now in a styled black fauxhawk, but never losing the sunglasses he used as his window to the world. He wore a black and red affliction shirt and a pair of tattered and torn blue jeans. His silver wallet chain flickered with the reflection of flashbulbs all around. Alexis tried her best to block the view of the obsessive media. But her tiny frame could only do so much. His reaction was one of apathy. Not a single facial feature of false step appeared in his stand against the gauntlet of media personnel who'd succeeded in making his life a living hell in the previous months. Carrying a black rolling suitcase behind him he walked with a purpose, following Alexis to the black Mustang that would take them back to the Estate. "Markus! Markus! What do you plan on doing next?!" "Markus! Can you give us an update on the next book?!" "Hey Markus! Have you heard the comments from Fallout Shelter?" "Mister Blaire, if I could have just a second of your time..." "Why was Vivica J. Valentine here?! Are you two a couple?" The screams from the on-lookers didn't seem to cease as they approached the Mustang. Running around the back Alexis opened the trunk so the center of attention could put his black suitcase inside. If anything the people trying desperately to get him to mess up couldn't say he didn't look healthy and refreshed. "I never understood how Vivica could deal with this so well..." Alexis stated under her breath, just loud enough for Markus to hear as she slammed the trunk door shut. "This would drive me insane." "You're telling me..." Markus said, with a bit of a smirk, "But I'm learning I suppose." "You sure are creating quite the scene." Alexis replied with a smile of her own, before making her way over towards the driver's side of the car. Markus moved towards the passenger side with a few slow paces before looking back into the eyes of the beast. Flashes engulfed his sunglasses as the boisterous crowd once again began to shout for comments. All their screams faded into one chaotic noise as he reached for the passenger side handle, but one voice rang out over the rest... "How's it feel to finally get out of there?" Stopping in mid-motion Markus Blaire stood back up and looked into the crowd as they awaited to see if he had a response to their questions. All he could do was smirk and said one line... "It's a good day to be alive." ---- And it feels right this time On his crash course with the big time Pay no mind to the distant thunder Today fills his head with wonder, boy Says it feels right this time Turned it 'round and found the right line "Good day to be alive, sir. Markus is stated to be in fair condition in a Phoenix, Arizona hospital with several injuries. We'll keep you updated on the story as it unfolds." -- News 3 Las Vegas ---- Chapter One: The Morning After ---- The morning after. It was some superstars most precious moments, and others most dreaded. It was the morning after where all the fans collected outside the hotel the wrestlers were staying in, hoping to get a prized handshake, autograph, or picture with their favorite superstar. For people like Chris Bagwell and Christian Zenith, it was a wave of jeers and profanities as they made their exit towards their vehicles. The morning after was a constant reminder of just how little the paying public cared for them, and often had to deal with them on a much more personal level than during the show. As for superstars like Vivica J. Valentine... it was a fond memory. It was the morning after where she got to say her last goodbye to little Mary Venton. It was the morning after when she got to be waken up by the colossal roar of her adoring fans. It was the morning after when all the media scrutiny and backstage relationships took a backseat to what really mattered. The fans. Their hopes. Their dreams. Their desires. She remembered attending a morning after as a little girl, skipping high school to try and get the attention of Rey Mysterio, Jr. From the other side of the tinted glass doors she stared out at the crowd held back by security banners. She watched as the crowd reacted somewhat violently towards Tessa Windsor. A smile crossed her face as she watched the events unfold before her. She'd be a liar if she didn't say she took a little pleasure from the results of Tessa's actions... no matter how distorted her perception of reality may be at this point in time. "There are two press crews out there." Someone suddenly said behind her, startling her as she watched the other wrestlers leave to go to a different Phoenix hotel closer to the Stadium for NC-17. Turning around she was relieved to see the face of James Bryan behind her. "We've talked it over... David Yale, Kevin Jacobs, and I... and we're pretty sure they're out there to talk to you. Might be about NC-17. Might be about what happened to Markus. Either way I've never seen them at a morning after before." "Me neither..." Vivica said, obviously disappointed. This was her escape from all of that. Was there really no getting away anymore? "Just hang in there champ." James Bryan said, patting Vivica on the shoulder as she walked up to the door with her backpack on. Her image could barely be seen outside by the fans, but they could tell who it was none the less. A rumble began to emerge from the crowd as she touched the door handle. Taking in the feeling while she still could. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" "They're chanting for you out there." JB said with a smile from behind The Fearless Phenom, "You better not keep your adoring public waiting." Closing her eyes she listening to the chant, bringing a smile to her face before taking a deep breath. A last shot at taking it all in before the frenzy. With the air escaping her pink lips she pushed the handle forward and took a step out into the open. A deafening cheer greeted her arrival, looking out across the crazy crowd through cerulean blue eyes. In appreciation she threw up a pair of Ronnie James Dio rock horns, and much of the crowd returned the action. As fanatics raced to grab their Vivica J. Valentine posters and a sharpie, she scanned the masses trying to pick out the media crews. While fans snapped pictures of the blonde-haired Aerial Artisan, it didn't take long for her to find what she was looking for. A male reporter could be seen trying to push and shove his way through the crowd towards the front, like a rock concert packed from wall to wall. Holding a tape recorder he screamed over the pleads from her adoring fans. "VIVICA! VIVICA! This is Scott Stevens from News 3 Las Vegas!" He raised his voice, trying to get the attention of the superstar who was obviously ignoring him, "Do you know of any in-house consequences that'll happen to Chris Bagwell as a result of his attack on Markus Blaire last night?!" Looking back at the reporter, Vivica decided to use this opportunity presented to her to get a rise out of her fans. "I don't associate myself with Chris or Steven, but I assure you..." Vivica paused, "...whatever consequences that could possibly be handed down to Chris Bagwell by means of management in GCW is only going to be a slap on the wrist compared to the punishment that I'm going to put him through at NC-17." The crowd roared as Vivica swooped the red streaked hair out of her eyes. "TRUST me." With those two words the crowd started a new chant. One that started small but didn't take long to rise. "VIVY'S GONNA KILL HIM!" "VIVY'S GONNA KILL HIM!" As she walks away with a smirk from ear to ear, she doesn't even notice the second media crew making their move near the barricaded parking lot to her left side. She almost forgot that she was dreading having to face them. Keeping stride she kept on just like it was any other morning after, before suddenly a voice from the crowd penetrated her consciousness like a bullet. "So you're going to play up this little publicity stunt to get some more buyrates for your upcoming Pay-Per-View?" It stopped The Fearless Phenom in her tracks. Her cocky smirk of satisfaction suddenly turned very serious at the accusations of her using her former 'true love' as a punching bag to hike up the Pay-Per-View buyrates for GCW. The last thing in the world she wanted was to get Markus Blaire, Ayake Sonoda, or anyone else involved. The smirk soon became a snarl as her icy cold stare met the eyes of the female news reporter who struck her by surprise like a rattlesnake. "Get the fuck out of here." Vivica responded without thinking, "This morning is for them... NOT for you." Very smug and condescending, the way the brunette news reporter reminded her a lot of Chris Bagwell. Infallible, the world revolves around the decisions that she decides to make in the morning. Her word in her eyes were gospel, and she'd gotten The Fearless Phenom to get into a bit of a trap. "Way to be a good role model Vivica." She responded, "I'm sure parents all over the world want their little girls saying that. That's not very befitting of a woman in your position." Furious all Vivica could manage to say was one last thing before leaving all of her adoring fans hanging, waiting desperately for their pictures and autographs that just wouldn't come this particular morning. She had a hospital visit to make, where a very real Markus Blaire was laying with very real injuries. "I'll tell Markus you send your best regards." ---- Chapter Two: Heartbreaker ---- Staring up at the ceiling was just about all there was to do these days. Sometimes it still felt like the room was spinning, surely a side-effect from the concussion he'd received the night before. Staring at the ceiling helped him try and focus his vision though, and turning his head really fast still made him quite dizzy. He'd been hit over the head with a few things before, but nothing quite like a Chris Bagwell chair shot. That man swung a chair like Baby Ruth swung a baseball bat. Maybe it was because he'd had so much practice using them, because Markus still through he was a coward. He'd heard from a few of the nurses assistants who were big wrestling fans that after he was attacked from behind that Chris Bagwell had admitted to sending him the note, and telling him it wasn't from his 'girlfriend.' If only. That was a mistake he made a long time ago. As each day passed he felt more and more wrong about his snap decision to break everything off after a magazine article. He now understood the harsh partial-realities of the media. Unfortunately, he couldn't spend too much time thinking about things like that... the pain in his upper neck hurt too much. With the opening of his door the mood in the room was about to change. The bustling of the hallways instantly grabbed his attention, making him slowly move his head to his left to see what was going on. Much to his surprise in walked one of the nurses assistants, along with Vivica J. Valentine. "Sorry about the hold up in getting back to you last night and again this morning Miss Valentine." A woman with the name badge 'Sophia' said, "As I'm sure you're aware of the news media and other outlets have been clogging up our phone lines trying to get information about what happened. Our staff got used to shooting down any questions about Markus. If it wasn't for one of our wrestling fans on staff recognizing your voice, you may have never gotten through at all." "It's okay..." Vivica said, looking over at Markus laying helpless in bed, "In this line of work you get used to things like that." "Well I'll let you two be so you can catch up on things. If you guys need anything just give us a ring, we'll be right there to assist you." Sophia answered with a smile while she looked over at Markus now holding his buzzer in the air to show her he knew what to do. Uncomfortably Sophia lightly closed the door behind her, and Markus tried to focus in on the figure of The Fearless Phenom in front of him. Amazingly enough he was still a bit woozy, but other times he felt perfectly fine. "You look like a wreck." Vivica said walking closer to his bed, noticing his once fashioned fauxhawk was now just short, flat hair on top of his head. Removed were his sunglasses, exposing his emerald green eyes to the world. Now surrounded by the rings of a sleepless night, they weren't as brilliant as they used to be. "I feel like I got hit by a freight train." Markus stated, trying to laugh of his honest statement. There was no point in trying to make jokes of honest statements in front of Vivica though. She had a knack for seeing right through those kind of things. "I'm sorry about all this... really I am. I never EVER wanted to get you or anyon..." "Drop it, it's fine." Markus interrupted, not realizing how guilty Vivica felt about the statement that the news reporter made earlier. The car ride had given her a little time to think things over. About some things that she'd been putting off for a long time. Things that aren't easy to talk about. She didn't know how to approach the topic exactly. "I'll hit him back twice as hard though..." Vivica stated plainly, "I promise." "You know, I have something I've needed to..." Markus grabbed his head and blinked his eyes before going on, "That I've needed to get off my chest for a while now." "Yeah?" Vivica felt maybe she wouldn't have to be the one to approach the subject after all. "I was wrong." Markus stated, "Back when Lia helped me get back home again, and I read that magazine about what you'd done I was so angry and I felt so betrayed that I made a really dumb decision." Vivica felt her heart clinch inside. She knew how hard it was for Markus to admit defeat or make mistakes. His inherent defiantness is partially why he's still alive today, but she didn't know what to say in return. What do you say to someone who admits to you that they're flawed as a person and made a terrible and somewhat massive mistake. "Okay..." is what Vivica decided to go with. Not the most emotional or well-spoken of answers, but clear-thinking is trait that she seems to develop on somewhat rare occasions. Thinking before speaking isn't something she's exactly known to do. "I didn't know about the media... and what they can do." Markus explained, "I know now. I've learned first hand I guess. Do you forgive me?" "Of course I forgive you..." Vivica stated, somewhat reluctantly because she could feel deep down inside what was coming next and she really didn't want to do it. "I want to try and make this work again..." Markus looked away from Vivica for a second in a blur before looking back up at her, "You want to give this another try?" Biting her bottom lip Markus immediately knew that the answer he was looking for wasn't coming as easily as he thought it was. There was no jubilant screaming of yes, her running over and giving him a big hug, and holding her in his arms made all of his ailments magically go away. She just bit her lip ring and looked at the ground. Conflict. Distant. Uncomfortable. "What?" Something was terribly wrong. "You're not with somebody are you?" "No..." Vivica still couldn't look into his eyes, "It's not that at all." "Then what is it?" Markus was anxious and a little hurt. He didn't know what the holdup could be. He thought this is what she'd always wanted. "When you're Vivica J. Valentine..." She says somewhat reluctantly, "...you have expectations on you. You're held up on a pedestal and under a microscope at the same time. You're the face of a company, an ambassador of a business. You're a role model for little girls and boys all over the world. When you have so much responsibility..." Vivica runs her hands through her hair, trying to hold back the tears... for her own good. "...I've been doing a lot of thinking." Vivica started over again, trying to look up at Markus but can't seem to get her eyes all the way there, "I'm on the road a lot. When I'm not doing shows I'm doing media appearances, or autograph signings, or stuff for the Make-A-Wish Foundation... other charities. When you're me, you have to make a lot of sacrifices. Even some really hard ones that you don't want to make..." "Like what?" Markus said quietly. "Like love." Vivica finally looked up at Markus, her eyes were red but she wasn't crying, she was fighting them back, "I'll never be around Markus. You can't go on the road with me, look at you. This isn't the first time this will happen to you. We all know you're not a fighter, as much as you may like to think you are. You're a lot smarter than I am, you think your way out of things you don't kill them in the face." "But if we just give it a shot..." "I'm not putting you through this again." Vivica verbally slammed her foot down, "It's too painful for me to see you this way, and it's way too painful for you to be getting hit in the head with chairs by fucking cowardly egotistical cockstains like Chris Bagwell. What if it was Bryan Mayhem?! He could've taken your fucking head off your shoulders Markus!" "He didn't take your head off your shoulders." "It sure as hell fucking felt like it Markus!" Vivica exclaimed, "Do you know what that really feels like? Do you? You really DON'T want to know." Heartbroken and defeated Markus looked away from the woman he'd re-fallen in love with. "I'm sorry." Vivica spoke softly now, "I really am. I want this as bad as you, I really do." "Then just do it! It's that easy." "It's not that easy." She responded immediately, "You don't understand and you probably never will and that's exactly what the problem is. You'll never understand. This business, this sport is my life Markus. MY life. Not yours, or anyone elses." Vivica closes her eyes and takes a deep breath. "I'm sorry... I really am." "Just go." Markus said as he looked back up at the ceiling again. She went to apologize again but realized that she'd stayed past her welcome, it was better off that she did leave. Turning her back to Markus Blaire she opened the door, but looked over her shoulder one last time and what she was leaving behind. How desperately she wanted to say 'nevermind' and start all over again, but she knew that she couldn't. This business was her one true love, and if it were any other way it'd just end in heartbreak. One way or another. ---- Chapter Three: Peace And Quiet ---- Looking up at the moon, it was almost like an entirely different reality than earlier in the day. The only sounds that filled her head were the sounds of traffic moving down below her feet. As the cold desert wind blew through her hair she felt a feeling that she hadn't all day. She felt relaxed and at peace. What is the world coming to where the only place you feel you can get away is hundreds off feet off the ground. She could see University of Phoenix Stadium from her hotel rooftop. It was only a matter of time until NC-17... even with all the distractions leading up she still felt giddy like a child thinking about it. The biggest show of all time, and she'd be there on a triple main event card. Five years ago the woman who has become a household name for arguable the greatest GCW World Heavyweight Champion didn't even know how to wrestle. Vivica J. Valentine of five years ago probably would've laughed in your face for mentioning to her it was possible, even if it really was what she'd always wanted. She had a facade to establish. Trying to be just like mommy always wanted her to be. The perfect little girl. Sometimes in order to accomplish great things you need to take great chances. Like dropping everything you've ever known (including a million-dollar wedding) to train to be a professional wrestler. Luckily the gamble paid off, Lord knows what else she could be doing right now if it didn't. Probably teaching gymnastics to some kids in Chicago somewhere. Yet she was always the risktaker, the daredevil, the woman without fear. Even know she looked down at the ground hundreds of feet below her, sitting on the edge of the hotel, feet dangling down. She wasn't scared of heights. Never was. She supposed that's why she was called The Fearless Phenom, she wasn't scared of a lot of things that most people were terrified of. Heights, flying, zombie movies, pain, clowns... but something as simple as watching a little girl could rattle her sometimes. Looking down at the ground actually made her feel great. She felt strong and free... like a superhero. Oh how much she wanted to be a superhero at this specific moment in time. She'd have the power to win all her matches effortlessly. Super speed to make all the hectic appearances and arrangements scheduled for her. Time travel to give her all the hours in the day she'd ever need. But she wasn't a superhero. She was just a girl. A highly successful living her life out of a suitcase like a gypsy. Vivica couldn't even remember the last time she'd spoken to one of her neighbours or slept in her own bed... “What are you doing out here V?” The familiar voice broke her daydreaming. Scooting away from the edge a little bit she looked over her shoulder to see the sight of Andy Murray. “The staff has been looking for you. Something about a schedule change for NC-17 arrivals?” “I just need a little piece and quiet...” Vivica said, the sight of Andy Murray reminded her that she wasn’t the only one living this kind of life. There were people like Andy right there with her along for the ride. Lia Ambrosi and Andy Murray were the only people she felt like she could talk to that would understand her. “Getting my mind all settled.” “Bags got you that frazzled?” Murray said as he approached The Fearless Phenom, “I know all about that.” “I know it Murr...” Vivica said with a bit of a smile, “Just pisses me off that he can just waltz around just hitting whoever he wants to, you know” Who the fuck is he? Who died and made him Jesus Fucking Christ? If it wasn’t for assholes like him I wouldn’t have half the problems I got now. There’s Chris Bagwell’s fucking everywhere. In the media, in the line at the drive-thru...” “At the airport terminal, when you were in school, there’s always one at a concert.” Murray added. “There is always a Chris Bagwell at a concert.” Vivica chuckled a little bit, “Just you know... this life never gets any easier. You think that you’ll just get used to it and everything will be second nature and it’s not. You’re always running late. You’re always doing something wrong... or you’re doing something people don’t like. It’s hard to believe that you walk into the arena and hundreds of thousands of people rise to their feet in complete joy that you’re there. That they’re going to get to see you perform and do what you do best. But at the same time... there’s always someone like Chris Bagwell.” “Looking for that quick reward.” Andy Murray added, “The easy way out. Making it more difficult for everyone else.” “Right... looking for that quick reward.” Valentine nodded, “I guess that’s why there’s people like us right” Someone has to knock those jackasses down a peg.” “Hey, you don’t get the biggest pop I’ve ever heard for no good reason, right V?” Murr stated, “You must be doing something right.” “Yeah... I guess everything is going alright.” Vivica said as she got up to her feet, “Thanks Murr, we better be getting back inside... apparently I have a schedule change I need to okay.” With those words the two new friends made their way back inside the building, leaving the cool desert air behind with the slam of a door. In just a matter of days, the city of Phoenix, Arizona was about to witness a show to the likes of which had never been seen before. A spectacular which had never been equalled, and contests that’d go down in the history books as some of the greatest that’d ever occur. That’s what the hype around NC-17 was about. Through the years it’d never disappointed to be the greatest of all time, no matter how impossible people would say it was. Unfortunately for Chris Bagwell, the same could be said for Vivica J. Valentine. Just like the others had to learn before him... it was time for him to believe the hype. The soothing light at the end of his tunnel towards championship glory is really just a freight train coming his way. One of the likes in which he’d never seen before in his wrestling career. Even the most finely laid strategic plans couldn’t prepare him for the fight he’d gotten himself into for NC-17. He’d made his battle with Vivica J. Valentine very personal. Real life doesn’t work like chess. You can’t call checkmate before you kill the Queen.
