A government investigation now?

20 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/11 Lighting a cigarette in the darkness the two individuals standing in the darkness. Only lights the pierce the darkness of where they are standing is the city far off in the distance. The city beyond with its glowing neon and flashing lights is non other than the city of carnal sin and delights known as Las Vegas. The two men look at each other and neither make any sudden moves, both seem in deep thought as one breaks the silence. “Cristoff seems like we have been going in circles for weeks. How'd the interview go with the Casino slash wrestler owner go?” Reischmann rubs his hands together as he looks around, He then reaches forward and grabs Cristoff's cigarette and drops it on the ground snuffing it out. “Nasty habit there, even though I smoke we both should quit.” Packing his cigarette pack, again he pulls another out and lights it, his eye focus on the small task at hand as he raises his eyes looking at Reischmann. “There more than a million ways to die, I'll choose my own way thank you.” “Yeah your choice, so anyways back to point. The details on Mr. Whitecloud giving us any support here involving his military exchange is rather disconcerting. He seems to be adamant about not getting involved with anything to do with the military.” He zips his jacket slightly in frustration as he looks over towards Las Vegas. “Our leads are running low.” Kristoff responds with a somber disappointed voice. They both look at each other curiously as a small red dot of light is seen on the hill in the distance. Almost unseen. Kristoff puts on his sunglasses in the dark and looks in that general direction. He motions for Reischmann to kneel low as he does the same. “I'd say we are being watched.” He points towards the hill as he removes the sunglasses. Both men quietly observe over the bushes watching as the dot flashes and disappears. They remain where they are just watching. Both men stay low as they bear crawl around to some rocky outcroppings. They both lean back as Reischmann looks over his shoulder and around the rocks at the area where the light was seen. “They are watching us as we check for more serious leads. I hate being watched, perhaps it is time to turn the tides.” Nodding Kristoff pulls his pistol from inside of his jacket. He places his other hand underneath the handle. Reischmann removes his side arm as well as he continues to watch the hill. “We are pinned down basically, but they still think our locations is about 50 yards at our previous location.” Kristoff clears his throat slightly as he smiles. “Let them think they know where we are, may be in our favor at the moment.” At a rocky bluff around 400 yards away two men are observing the two men. They both stop and look at each other. One stands up and uses the night vision binoculars to figure out where the men went. “They simply disappeared.” He slowly scans over the area spotting nothing. He turns and looks at the other man and motions his hands downwards. “We have a helicopter coming in.” Quickly they both duck down and climb in between the rock outcropping surrounding them, soon the helicopter flies overhead nearby with search light brightening the area. Fearful to move the two men blend in as the helicopter flies overhead and begins to descend in the desert around 500 yards away. The helicopter an old UH-1 Huey slowly comes to the ground kicking up dirt and debris hiding anything from any type of camera or sensory device. Reishmann and Kristoff take note of this and notice that the helicopter is government and they run to it quickly and hop in. Before the two in the rocks recover the helicopter is off and heading away from the area towards Nellis Air Force Base. 2 Hours later inside a hanger at Nellis AFB... Reischmann slowly walks out of a door with Kristoff behind him. They both look a little agitated as they stop and look at the helicopter that picked them up. Kristoff looks at it and shakes his head as he looks at Reischmann. “You would have never thought that Mr. Whitecloud would come and nab us from the desert. Guess we were being watched a lot more than what we figured.” Kristoff goes to light a cigarette but a large hand reaches over and grabs it from him. Shaman otherwise known as Andrew Whitecloud looks at the both of them shaking his head. “Not around my helicopter you don't smoke. You can blow us sky high with all this fuel around.” Kristoff grunts as he shoves the cigarettes back into his pocket. He turns and pokes Shaman in the chest as he stares him in the eye. Looking down Shaman observes the smaller man as he simply looks at him. “Look here Mr. High and mighty wrestler, I don't give a damn who or what you think your doing. You are impeding an investigation and we need your help in solving it!” Standing up proudly with his chest poking out Kristoff gets a serious look on his face as he nods his head. Waving them off Shaman turns and goes over to his helicopter and begins looking over her with a fine tooth comb. “I am probably going to have to strip her down and clean her out before she flies again, had to go out there and get you two sorry idiots.” Reischmann walks forward waving Kristoff off as he faces Shaman. “You don't have a clue about who was out there do you?” Raising his eyes looking away from everyone Shaman shakes his head. “No clue and don't really care, I wasn't out there to save you. Someone else asked me to go get you two clowns before you get in over your head.” Shaman turns the rear rotor blade slightly and then steps away. As Reischmann reaches for Shaman a voice behind them speaks. “Stand down Reischmann it is about time we worked to share some information.” An Army Major steps around the hangar doors looking at both Reischmann and Kristoff. Kristoff comes forward and stands next to Reischmann. “And you are?” Commands Reischmann quickly. Smiling as he clears his throat. “ I am Major Scott Thadiuss Thompson, I have been investigating some weapons dealings here in Las Vegas and unfortunately it has fallen hand in hand with the drug trade. Your agent got killed by being in the wrong place at the wrong time.” Shaman turns and looks at everyone. “Well guys its been real, its been fun, but it hasn't been real fun.” Clearing his throat he turns and heads for the hangar doors. “I got a wrestling show to get to for GCW and I don't need you guys hanging around. You don't need me and I will be paying attention to what happens around my casino.” He raises two fingers points them to his eyes to Reischmann and Kristoff then back. He turns and exits as the major shakes his head. Passing the two men, Major Thompson motions for them to follow. Both Kristoff and Reischmann follow, as they enter an officer there are maps and files all over the place. Kristoff looks up quickly as he points at the maps and files. “Why didn't we get them a few weeks back? This would have been valuable information.” He looks around frantically looking at everything. Major Thompson shakes his head and lifts a file. “You didn't ask nor you the need for them at the moment. So I ordered the base police to give you what you asked for specifically.” The Major walks to a chair and pulls it out from under the table. He sits himself down slowly while reaching forward and pulling a file towards himself. Reischmann and Kristoff follow suit as they observe him closely. “So why all of the sudden share this information now?” Major Thompson leans back and taps the desk. He slides a folder across the table and looks at the two men. “We are being undermined on all fronts, Someone is literally taking every piece of information we gather and changing it and sending us in circles.” “Who would do such a thing and yet continue to be pointed in a single direction.” Reischmann pulls a picture from a folder showing Shaman in a military uniform. He slides it across the table to Kristoff. Rubbing his chin Kristoff lifts the picture. “Yet once again we are drawn to Mr. Whitecloud.” “No I think it is made to look that way since he has a a lot of ties to the military and the public. What the connection is to all of this is very dangerous to say the least. “ The Major stops short and then taps a folder. All three men begin going through all of the folders as Kristoff stands and pins a picture of Shaman up on the bulletin board. He turns sitting back down as he thumbs through personnel files.
