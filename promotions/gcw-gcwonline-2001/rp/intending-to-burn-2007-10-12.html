Intending To Burn

12 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 12, 2007 ---- Introduction: And The Flames Licked The Sky... ---- Music filled the air with an almost calming demeanor. A much welcome escape to the hectic life on the road she was accustomed to. It was homecoming. A small breath of fresh air in the midst of chaos. Kicking back might've been the only thing on her mind as she basked in the glow of the red-light hovering high above her, swaying back and forth almost rhythmically in the breeze. "Burn" by Alkaline Trio encompassed her while the dim glow changed from crimson to a soothing emerald green. There's a lightning storm each and every night Crashing inside you like motorbikes We toss and turn, sleep so loud Grind the teeth in our empty mouths Our empty Slowly she opened her cerulean blue eyes, but it felt like pulling anchors out of the ocean. Exhaustion had settled in as a crisp October breeze whispered through the driver's side door. Small surface scratches and fingerprints littered the exterior of the car. Street lights emphasized the imperfections on her windshield as she passed, illuminating the toll in which life on the road had taken on the vehicle. The smell of autumn in air mixed with the poignant smell of sulfur which suddenly alerted her attention. Looking over in the distance above the tree line a large cloud of ash rose, blocking her view of the beautiful twilight. With a blink she attempted to double-check her sleep-deprived reality. Almost in a second it was gone, nothing but a trail of brilliant stars speckled the sky on the roadside. "I'm losing my mind..." she said aloud, continuing her midnight drive. There's a forest fire burning bright Spreading quickly towards our last rights Nowhere to run, pointless to hide Just lay there and scream, pretending to try Pretending Alkaline Trio continued to provide the soundtrack to the venture as she rounded the corner onto a familiar street. The horizon provided a faint glow in front of her, as if giving a hint of the dawn ahead. Looking down at the sapphire lit stereo planted in her dash, the clock clearly read 1:00AM. There was no dawn in sight for the next few hours. Returning her focus to the road, the faint glow seemed to disappear in front of her. "What is going on?" She whispered to herself, wondering in the back of her mind if this could be some kind of dream. As a stop sign approached she slowed, focused on the skyline ahead. Hoping to see any sort of sign that what she'd seen just a few seconds before was truly real. Intending to burn, pretending to fight it Everyone learns faster on fire Things took a turn, lost all desire You live and you burn You live and you burn Taking an extended wink, the effects of many sleepless nights had taken their toll on her consciousness. The cadence of the song kicked in and became one with the sounds around her, resulting in an ecstasy that she had to fight away in order to remained focused on the road ahead. With each foot she traveled her vision seemed to become more blurred as water filled her eyes. Ahead was a much welcomed sight, a mailbox labeled 3390. Upon seeing it she took a deep breath, followed by an extended sigh overpowered by the melodies of "Burn". It was home. This impending doom is left deep inside And it's haunting you each and every night Light starving wolves, drowning sheep We close our eyes, pretending to sleep Descending The headlights struck the white picket fence like the spotlights of a prison break. Ascending up the drive she could see the image she'd been waiting nearly 6 hours for ahead... home. Outside of the pulsating beats emitting from her car, Old Mill Road was enveloped in silence. Tranquility over took her body as she came to a halt near the end of the small stretch of drive. Closing her eyes, she tilted her head back. Her long blonde hair lied like a throw on her shoulders as she stretched her arms high into the air and her legs straight out, pushing the seat back. The once bitter October skies ignited as she opened her eyes. Her fruitions engulfed in flames sat in front of her as she looked on frozen in time. A funeral pyre awaited her. The glow from the embers rose into the air igniting the skyline. Dust and ash towered above her, littering the air around her with the bitter smell of sulfur. All she could do was watch, unable to do anything but think about how fitting of an end it was to the events that had taken over her life. Intending to burn, pretending to fight it Everyone learns faster on fire Things took a turn, lost all desire You live and you burn You live and... Like hell, we are anxiously waiting Like hell burning silently strong Somehow we fell down by the wayside And somehow this hell is home "This is hell..." the phrase escaped her lips slowly in a faint whisper. ---- Chapter One: Pain Runs Deep ---- "Vivica!" A harsh knocking noise suddenly broken her tranquility. Her eyes burned as she opened them for the first time in what seemed like an eternity. Trying desperately to focus through the intense light, she wiped the sleep out of her eyes and peered over to her left. Through the smudged windows was the blurred image of her housekeeper Patrick, waving hello with a look of concern on his face. It was at this point in time she realized where she was and sat straight up behind the wheel of her Crossfire. Everything was... normal. The house stood in front of her in one-piece. The white picket fence still stood in place, the symbolism of suburban perfection. The smell of sulfur replaced by the autumn breeze. Towers of ash replaced by birds flying by over head. Confused, The Fearless Phenom looked back over at Patrick who waited patiently outside the driver's side door. "Are you okay?" He finally spoke again as Vivica J. Valentine flipped open her cell-phone to seen it was damn near noon. "You've been out here since about 1:00 this morning." A sigh of relief escaped from deep down inside of Vivica's body as clinched the handle to the car door. Opening the door she stepped out, a bit off balance from just waking up. A huge yawn made her stretch out her body. Nearly every vertebrae in her back cracked as her piercing cerulean blue eyes looked back at her housekeeper. "Yeah... I'm fine." She spoke groggily looking around at her surroundings, still a bit taken back. "I figured you'd fallen asleep and it was best I didn't disturb you. I wasn't planning on it until your car ran out of gas." Patrick spoke, almost as if he was covering his ass. Sure enough she looked back to see the keys still in the ignition of her car. "Dammit..." Vivica put her hands over her head in embarrassment, "I really need to get away from this thing." Slamming the car door shut she slowly made her way across the lawn, littered with orange and yellow leaves crunching under her shoes. It was all just a dream After all... but it seemed so real. Then again, it always does. Everyday her dreams seemed to be more and more like the movie Groundhog Day. Ending with a blazing fire, the image was emblazoned onto her sub-conscious. It got more and more real every night. Thoughts of what could've been with Markus were never far behind upon awakening. Plunging her key into the socket she opened the door with a twist. The scent of Pine Sol filled the air while she kicked her shoes off. This was the house they'd share if he was still alive. A picture of him and her together at the Ambrosi Estate in Las Vegas still sat upon the coffee table in the middle of the living room. On the television was the local news at noon. Sure enough another shooting, another rape, another robbery. What a great time we live in. Walking into the kitchen she quickly notices a pile of paper sitting on the dining table. They all appear to be missed letters from her most recent road trip, but it'd only been about a month since the last time she was home. A much shorter turn-around had almost 6 times the fan mail. She picked one up off the top of the pile and began to read: Dear Vivica, I just wanted to say thank you for all that you've done. You're my hero and my inspiration. You were the focus of a recent report I did at school where we had to tell the entire class who our role models were and why. This is why I'm writing you. I don't know if I'll be watching you at Dangerous Games because I'm scared. I'm scared of what's going to happen to you once you fight Bryan Mayhem again. You got beat up so bad last time and this time in an inferno match? You have more important things to live for than getting him back for some things he's said to you. Don't you? If you get set on fire... I just don't know what I'll do. Please reconsider and just enter the Dangerous Games match. Whether I order it or not you'll be in my prayers Vivica. Be safe. Sincerely, Michelle Mattingly Slowly she laid it down onto the table and reached out for the other one before she was interrupted. "They're all like that." Patrick said from behind her. "Hundreds of fans of all ages from across the world. Worrying about you. Your safety. Your future." Vivica turns around and looks at Patrick, as if debating the fan mail. "I've done this a million times before! You know I..." Vivica began a rebuttal but was interrupted again. "No you haven't." Patrick spoke, "I have the upmost faith in you Vivica but... you just haven't. Do you realize what can happen to you out there? It's one thing to wrestle that sick bastard with security surrounding the ring and EMTs waiting for anything at all to go wrong. But surrounded by fire? If he goes berserk or if something happens to you how are they going to get to you? Can they even get there in time Vivica? He can KILL you in an Inferno Match." It was something she didn't think about often. The Aerial Artisan was never one to think about anything that wasn't victory much, but Patrick's concern really alerted her. Since she moved to Highland Park and hired Patrick he'd been completely supportive of her in every way. Confidant that she could do whatever she set her mind to and overcome any obstacle that was put in her path. She could tell by the look on his face that he was more than a little concerned about her well being. He was scared. He feared for her life. "I won't let that happen Patrick." Vivica said as she picked up another letter. Vivica, What are you thinking?! An Inferno Match with Bryan Mayhem... on the same night that you could be #1 Contender to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship?! That takes some guts but as one of your biggest fans I really think you should reconsider. It just isn't worth what you could lose. I can't sit back and watch you throw your destiny away without saying anything. - David Young "They're all like this?" She said as she laid it down on the table. "Every one." Patrick said confidently as The Fearless Phenom backed away from the table. She knew that she could beat Bryan Mayhem, it was only a matter of when. Maybe she had raised the stakes too high... what if there wasn't a next time. It was one of the only sports where your favorite didn't get a next year. Everytime you step into the ring it could be your last, so you have to make it count. What if there wasn't a next time... had she made the proper arrangements if she was critically injured and couldn't ever wrestle again? What would she do with the rest of her life? What happens if he DID kill her? Sure he'd go to jail and probably get the chair but what about Patrick and his family? What about Lia and Tessa? After what she'd gone through with Markus... she knew what kind of pain that brought people. The pain of loss ran deep, maybe a little too deep. What if she did just throw away her destiny? ---- Chapter Two: Looking Through The Glass ---- Click... Click... Click... Clouds whimsically pass in front of the full moon, hanging in the sky alone as footsteps resonated around her but were lost in the sounds of the bustling city. Above the metropolis of Chicago, Illinois it shone. So distant and so peaceful it looked from the streets below from an observer. Walking through a maze of steel and concrete... the moon above looked so tranquil, it was no wonder she wanted to go there so badly as a child. It was almost history repeating for The Fearless Phenom, almost two years had passed since she stepped foot into Global Championship Wrestling but the more things chance the more things stay the same. Here she was... walking through the streets of Chicago trying to clear her thoughts just as she did two years ago. Looking around she saw familiar places, and some familiar faces. Downtown Chicago had always been her safe haven. As dangerous as the place was... it was her second home. She'd lived on these streets for years, and just going back made her miss home. As a swift breeze past and blew her blonde hair with a red steak into the wind, she clinched her black leather bomber style jacket close to her. It didn't take long for her to remember some of the harder times. Times she'd go up to a week without eating, not knowing if she'd ever have any type of meaningful relationship ever again. Once again, the more things change the more things stay the same. The match for Dangerous Games was signed... it was an Inferno Match and as so many had pointed out, it could very well be her last. Tonight was a time for reflection, if it was going to be her last match... she was going to enjoy all the good times she had. Her United States Championship win will always be a good memory, as was her victory over Eva VanLorne in the first ever LiveWire Match. So many missed opportunities. Middle of the pack in Eliminated by Dawn McFarlane in Rampage 2006 Round 1, being eliminated middle of the pack in Dangerous Games 2006, maybe the runner-up in Warpath 2006 may be the most painful. She was so close she could taste it... As she walked, she was just another face in the crowd of hundreds walking the streets this Friday night. It was that little taste of glory and the support of her loving fans that pushed her on. What situations she'd put herself through just to taste it again and put smiles on their faces. She was such a sucker for affection... Stopping in her tracks Vivica J. Valentine looked high above her... at the towering Zenith Corporation building in downtown Chicago that lied in front of her. So many years of her life she spent inside that building. Tied and bound by the chains of a business suit, pretending to be someone she wasn't just for a little affection from Christian Zenith. Willing to throw all of her dreams and aspirations away just to find true love in all the wrong places. The more things change, the more things stay the same. Vividly the conversations with Christian Zenith play through her mind like they were just yesterday... "So you're just going to lose to Jake Hix and walk away... like nothing happened?" Vivica J. Valentine replied, taking her eyes off the Manor to shoot daggers back at Christian Zenith across from her. "I can take care of Jake Hix when I so please. One on one I would've walked away from that place a champion." Christian replied, "Jake Hix will get his you can count on that Vivica." "When?" Vivica replied, "When are you going to do anything about it? After you're done playing monopoly? What in the hell is wrong with you Christian? This isn't like you at all, these little things always work themselves out." "Enough." Christian said, narrowing his eyes as he glanced back at Vivica, "This will be fixed, this company is my life and my first priority. Always has been. Global was nothing more than a game for me... a hobby, a pastime. Like collecting baseball cards or playing video games. Nothing more. Maybe you're the one who got her priorities messed up somewhere." "A stupid little game..." she mouthed to herself as her expression turned into a cowl looking up through the glass windows. She could almost feel his icy cold gaze looking down at her as she looked into the sky. This game had taken away so much but given back so much more... "Zenith Corporation was never a priority of mine." Vivica replied, "You are my priority, not your business or your money. What in the hell do you expect me to do now Christian?" "You'll always have a job with Zenith Corporation." "I don't want a fucking job with Zenith Corporation." Vivica's reply seemed to take Christian a little off guard, "I'm not a business woman Christian, I'm not cut out for this world. It doesn't interest me... it's not what I want to do for the rest of my life." "Then what brilliant idea do you have without Z Corp?" Christian said, taking his eyes off of The Black Mariah as the limo came to a stop. "I don't... know." Vivica said, "I really don't know." "This is my brilliant idea." She said to herself staring through the glass entry to the Zenith Corporation building. "Look at me now." All the pent up frustrations of years past came tumbling down upon her all at once as she stood in front of the Zenith Corporation skyscraper. All the times that Christian prodded her into thinking she was incompetent and not capable of thinking on her own. All the times people like her parents told her that a little girl like her didn't belong in a wrestling ring. All the times people like Brandon Youngblood told her she sucked. All the times people like Bryan Mayhem thought they could just push her around like a nobody. Proving people wrong is something The Fearless Phenom had done her entire life... the adrenaline rush of the competition and the satisfaction of proving to everyone that she COULD do whatever she put her mind to. Maybe a trip home was just what she needed After all... a chance to see what she would've been if she didn't take chances. Didn't try to pursue her dreams. She had to do this not only for herself... but for all the people that assholes like Christian Zenith, her father, and Bryan Mayhem had pushed around. A job that only a person whose worthy of being Michelle Mattingly's role model could do. They may be scared, but in the end they'll see it was worth it. That you really can do ANYTHING you put your mind to. Even the impossible. NOTHING was impossible. She was going to avenge the name of Markus Blaire, who'd been desecrated by that asshole who'll stand across the ring from her at Dangerous Games. A smile crossed the face of The Aerial Artisan as she looked at the Zenith Corporation logo screened onto the glass door. A reflection of herself was inside... and she could almost see the GCW World Heavyweight Championship on her shoulder already. It was almost as if Markus Blaire was looking down at her right now. A fire lit inside of her as he imagined what he'd think watching her become the #1 Contender, and Bryan Mayhem walking out of Dangerous Games a burnt shell of his former glory. Vengeance was so beautiful. ---- Intermission: A Shot Of Clarity ---- Bryan Mayhem can be described as many things. A demon, a monster, a champion, a hallmark of pain and destruction in GCW. Seven feet of pure evil... the only man ever to defeat Lia Ambrosi for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. The mere sound of "Babylon AD" now strikes fear in the hearts of hundreds of current and former GCW roster members... many of them never came back. He's ravaged a path through GCW that can not be ignored, he's a walking horror. Now I find myself next in line. People tell me to give it up. They tell me to move on with my life before I get injured... or even killed. I'm not like anyone else though. I don't just curl up in a ball or run away in the face of danger. I won't back down from assholes like you Mayhem. You play with fire... trying to set me aflame to prove a point. Try to play with my mind and make me cry like so many have before. You get some kind of sick fucking pleasure looking at the look on my face in agony. Name dropping the love of my life and his unfortunate death, like you need to remind me what can happen. Every night in my dreams I see the fire Mayhem. I look on helplessly as my dreams of a family and true love incinerate into the night before I could even tell him that I felt the same way. I wake up practically in tears, wishing that is WAS just some really bad nightmare. Yet every morning I call the phone... the bill isn't paid, and he's gone. Those nightmares have made me stronger, they've made me realize that there is NOTHING worse you can fucking do to me than what I had to watch that night. There is no doubt in my mind that there is only one asshole who deserves to burn... that's you. That's what happens when you play with fire Bryan, and I'll enjoy every god damn minute of it. Watching you scream out, making you go through only A FRACTION of the pain I've had to endure in the past couple of months will be worth more money that I have to my name. I've told you time and time again... you keep knocking me down and I'll get right back up again, harder and stronger than before. If this little revenge ruins my destiny so be it. If there is one thing I've learned over the past couple of years in this business, it's that no matter how hard you try to... you can't just control destiny. Destiny just happens. It does what it pleases and there isn't a damn thing you can do about it. I've played conservative my entire career, taking short cuts to make my path to glory as easy as possible. At every turn I've found a dead end, but have found a little bit of myself in each one. In every loss I've grown better, stronger, faster, and smarter. My failures have made me who I am today. Each one of them just another brick in the wall. A wall that has become a much larger than myself. An image of the invisible, a crowd of the nameless at my back pushing forwards towards my destiny. An uncanny clash of heart, tenacity, speed, and intelligence. A ticking timebomb of brilliance, waiting anxiously for the day to come... the day when it's my time to shine. Destiny works in mysterious ways Mayhem... but in the pit of my stomach I know. This isn't a fairy tale, but dreams do come true. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship will be mine, and I'm NEVER going to give up on my dreams. I've lived my entire life for this moment so whether I'm perfectly healthy or burnt to a crisp... I'm going to be there. I'm going to take what's mine, and there isn't a damn thing that Bryan Mayhem, Steven Caldera, George W. Bush, or even Jesus Christ himself can do about it. I'm walking out of Dangerous Games #1 Contender to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship... or I'm not walking at all. ---- Chapter Three: This Is Love, This Isn't Over... ---- ... Darkness had blanketed the room in a veil of unknown. The silence had encompassed his entire life for the past couple of months. Looking around he'd grown accustomed to the dark once again, it had keened his senses. After all what better do you have to do when your shackled up and a broken image of what you once were. A freak on a leash. A black hooded sweatshirt covered the scars of rebellion lost. He tried so hard to fight, but eventually just like the others he was broken and tamed. At least, that's what he wanted them to think. Just another number is what he became despite his valiant efforts. AZ2660-01 is what they called him now. No longer having a name to call his own. All he had were his hopes and dreams that flooded his dreams every night as he faded away to sleep. Most of his days now consisted of watching Kenneth Anger films that the dictator who made AZ2660-01 call him "father" was so fond of. One named Eaux D'Artifice has especially gained his interest. The fountains of Italy were so beautiful. One day he'd get out of this hellacious prison and be free again. Oh what he'd do to see the sun and the blue skies. What he'd do to breathe fresh air and hear the sounds of the birds chirping. So many little things he'd overlooked in life that now he missed so dearly. Things would be different this time. This life he'd appreciate so much more. He'd found out through various experiments that he could walk out during the day again... even on the highest frequency the sunlight didn't hurt so much any more. Everyday he'd become more and more human. The cravings had begun to subside although they weren't completely gone. Yet he'd give it all away just to see her face again. He wondered everyday if she knew, or what she was doing, or if she still looked the same. He'd hoped she didn't find someone else... oh how heartbroken he'd be... knock knock knock A small tap through the dark had caught his attention. He's emerald green eyes peered out from under the black hood covering his face. Listening intently he tried to figure out who or what was trying to get his attention. Or perhaps they didn't know he was there at all... "Markus..." A woman's voice whispered through the air. She'd finally come. Just as she'd promised many weeks ago. "I thought you'd given up." Markus tried his best to whisper but his anxiety got the best of him. "Quiet down..." She spoke softly, almost like a passing wind. "I just came by to tell you to be ready, it's almost time." It was the message he'd been waiting what felt like centuries to hear. For the first time since he'd been locked in the shackles that now wrapped around his wrists, ankles, and neck a smile crossed his face from ear to ear. "What of Vivica?" He spoke as quietly as he could. "She's doing well... she still speaks of you everyday." The voice answered. He could almost feel his heart skip a beat as his hands clinched into fists in joy, for he could not make a sound or he'd surely alert the guards. "Dangerous Games is coming soon... she's fighting for your honor against Bryan Mayhem, you should feel very proud of the woman she's become in your absence." The voice continued, "I know I am. I'll come back soon. Don't give up hope Markus. You will be out of this hell hole." He could almost feel her presence fade away in the shadows. Once again he was alone but suddenly the dark room didn't feel so lonely anymore. Hope began to rise from inside of his bosom. Freedom. Blue Skies. No chains. No guards. Excitement was so much he had to say it aloud to himself... "It's almost time." Vengeance was so beautiful.
