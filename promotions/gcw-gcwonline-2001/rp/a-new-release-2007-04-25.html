A New Release

25 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/2007 ...What truly lies beneath the agony of one mans life? A fame so staged, so plotted to genius standards that the thought of it would make even the most arrogant of all well known celebrities shutter. Could it be that this mans fame was truly another mans fate? Who really knew that this would be the most perfect time to stand out once more... (Waves stroke gently onto a welcoming yet peacefully sandy surface, almost as if it were content with its life. Warm midst feeding through the air as the moon illuminates the heavy clouded starry night sky. The sound of relaxation rests on the waves as they continue to pamper the sand. It's a getaway to some, yet more like a home to others. It can speak to you, only if you are willing to listen.) ...How could failure feel so painless? Why should it not come as a surprise anymore? Is it really the fact that I'm merely prone to failure?... (The clouds continue to sway through the night causing large gaps to form, setting the mood to perfection. The glow of the moon on the silent water peers into the distance, highlighting the true beauty of this ocean.) ...Why do I continue to feel this hole in my life? As if I have yet to fulfill my goal, or reach my peak. Is there something still worth fighting for?... (The clouds rumble quietly as if it were warning that something big were about to happen. A premonition of things to come, an awakening of something big.) ...Do I push along? Do I continue my ever so long journey? Or Do I just fade away into this heavenly abyss? I wonder... (The rumble continues to grow as the ocean starts to gain fury. The waters, in an outrage, fear for the worst.) ...I have a reason. I still serve a purpose... (The rumbling turns into slight cracks of lightning. A storm is brewing. The Clouds are scattering frantically to open the wrath of this once suttle beast.) ...I will fight on... (The lightning begins to multiply as the Thunder begins to amplify. Soft rain begins to tap the wavy ocean.) ...This is a path I have chosen. I will obide by my decision... (The rain becomes stronger as the lightning grows longer and louder.) ...And all those who stand before me shall fall, just like the rest of them... (Heavy rain falls as this once peaceful ocean water gets drilled by what feels like two pound bricks.) ...And noone and nothing will stop me this time around... (The storm calls out a mighty roar as it releases hell on this forsaken ocean. The rain taking its toll on the water as the skys light up in anger and fury with its every crack and boom effect. It seems the worst is at hand when suddenly a body walks slowly out fromt he depths of the waters....And he walks onto the sand and stands tall in all this mystery. Looking once drained of all emotion, yet looking motivated. His moist lips slowly separate.) "At peace once again."
