Bouncin' Back (Part 4)

14 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/14/2005 [The camera slowly fades into the dark driver's side of the long, black, stretch limo sent to Vince Jones and Jasmine by Reno Banks on a wet and rainy night in the city. The windshield wipers furiously run back and forth trying to whisk away the pouring rain as the chauffeur tries his best to focus on the road and get his passengers to their rightful destination as quickly as possible. Outside of the limo a wide array of automobiles can be seen steadily driving by as the rain continues to pound everything in sight. There are even a few brave city dwellers that can be seen through the thin layer of running water on the windshield making their way down the street with umbrellas and raincoats. The camera switches to the passenger bay of the limo where Jasmine can be seen gently nudging Vince Jones who is taking a nap.] Jasmine: V, wake up. We’re almost there. V? [Vince Jones begins to stir and his slowly open.] Vince Jones: Huh? What’s that? Jasmine: We’re almost at Reno’s office. Just down the block or so, I assume. [Vince Jones sits up and leans back against the seat and nods as he glances at the wristwatch on his arm.] Vince Jones: You said we were almost there a helluva a long time ago. Jasmine: [rolls her eyes] That was just to shut you up. I really mean it this time. We're almost there for real.] Vince Jones: Yeah. This sh*t should be interestin?. We shouldn’t even be doiin? this. Reno and V. Jones ain't got a thing to say to each other, but whateva. [Jasmine nods in agreement.] Jasmine: I know. I know that, but there’s no turning back now. We gotta see what he's got in mind He might have some interesting plans for you. [Vince Jones snarls at the thought of having to meet up with Reno Banks.] Vince Jones: Yeah, whateva! Let’s just get this sh*t over with! V. Jones ain’t tryin? to spend too much time up in there with Mr. Agent of the Stars, himself. Things are simple. V got this damn debut with this b*tch made named Ricky Thunda or whateva the hell his name is. V goes in, beats his punk ass, and moves on to the next chump that needs to get silenced! [Jasmine smiles and nods in agreement.] Jasmine: Don’t worry. I’m sure we can handle business quick and painless with him. Then, you can go on and get ready for the big match. [Vince Jones begins cracking his knuckles.] Vince Jones: Betta be quick for his sake! V ain’t in the best of moods! [The camera then switches to the outside of the black, stretch limo as it slowly makes a right turn and pulls up alongside the wet sidewalk of a small office complex in the downtown area and comes to a halt. The chauffeur exits the limo, stops beside the passenger door, and opens it. Vince Jones steps out and looks on at the office complex in front of him in disgust. He begins to make his way towards the building as Jasmine quickly exits the limo as well, covers her head, to shield herself from the rain, and quickly dodges around the limo, down the rain-soaked sidewalk, up the stairs and through the glass doors of the office complex behind Vince Jones. The camera slowly fades out. The camera fades back in and we are inside a small, cozy office away from the rain outside. The camera pans around the room and an assortment of pictures of wrestlers from all across the nation can be seen. In every picture the man we now know as Reno Banks, The Agent of the Stars, can be seen posing with each and every one of his clients from past and present. In each one of the pictures he has that same trademark sly grin. The camera continues to pan around the room past some filing cabinets, a few small bookshelves, and a large screen t.v. The camera stops on the t.v for a brief moment as a GCW recap show from the past year is seen playing. The camera begins to pan around the room until it stops and catches sight of the man himself, Reno Banks, who is seated at his neatly arranged desk looking over some important paperwork concerning one of his clients. All of a sudden a small knock is heard at the door. His eyes rise from the paperwork and glance at the doorway.] Reno Banks: Come in. [The door slowly opens up and Jasmine is seen standing there. Behind her is a rather unexcited Vince Jones. Reno Banks sees Vince Jones standing in the doorway along with Jasmine and looks at him rather uneasily. Reno stands to his feet and tries to force a smile.] Reno Banks: Please, have a seat. I’ve been waiting on you two to show up. Make yourselves comfortable we have a lot of catching up to do. [Jasmine and Vince Jones step inside the office. Vince closes the door behind himself. Jasmine walks over and takes a seat in a chair sitting in front of Reno’s desk while Vince Jones leans against the wall with his arms folded across his chest.] Reno Banks: So? where do we begin? Where do we begin? [cups his chin in thought] Hmmmm....Mr. Jones, are you ready? [Vince looks at him in confusion.] Vince Jones: Is V ready? What the hell do you mean by that, huh? Reno Banks: I mean what I say, Mr. Jones? Are you ready? Are you ready to get back on track in GCW? Are you ready to make an impact? [Vince Jones chuckles to himself and just shakes his head.] Vince Jones: Where the hell were you at, Reno? Didn't you catch that G-C Dub footage from this past week? V. Jones is more than ready to make an impact. The impactin' has already started. [Reno Banks lets out a sigh and just shakes his head in disappointment with Vince.] Reno Banks: Yes, I saw the footage. I saw your little escapades this week at Worldwide and while you may consider that an impact, I see it as small change. You know how many guys I've managed that have felt they can waltz into new territory and 'bust up a few heads' only to falter? Its a hard job being an agent of my status. Everyone wants you on their side. Everyone wants you to carry them to new heights. You on the other hand, have always brought the intangibles to the table and I don't want to see you changing that at all. [Vince Jones slides the dark shades on his face downward a bit and rolls his eyes at Reno.] Vince Jones: Ain't nothin' changin' here. V the same ole G. Reno Banks: But there is something different about you. You've already expressed it. What? You're suddenly the type of guy that will settle on beating up on a few nobodies in Global Championship Wrestling and calling that a day? Vince, you pounded on a few nameless GCW employees. Granted you did quite a good job at it, that's still small change. No one cares about them. Mr. Jones there was a time in which a man like you was hungry. Vince Jones: V still is hungry. Reno Banks: No, I'm talking about real hunger. You were the type of guy that would stop at no end to be known as the greatest in the biz. The time in which you really start doing some major damage around GCW is when people name guys like your LeStatt Knights, Trey Tremenouses, Seymour Almasies, Michael Stevens and know that... Vince Jones: ...that V. Jones put the boots to they punk asses and left them in a pool of they own cold blood. [Reno shakes his head in disagreement.] Reno Banks: No, Vince. When people start running through the list of those big names as well as others in GCW and know that you've pinned them, defeated, them and in your famous words... Vince Jones: ...silenced them! Reno Banks: Correct, Mr. Jones. That's when you know you've done some real damage. That's when you know you're going somewhere. Mr. Jones, you can wound a man, but his wounds will eventually heal for people to forget; but, when you snatch things that people really uphold, value, and appreciate that's when you've made a real impression. That's when you know you won't be forgotten. So, are you ready, Mr. Jones? Are you ready to once again rise to the top? Are you ready to once again be a force to be reckoned with in the wrestling world? [Reno Banks stands to his feet and extends his hand to Vince for him to shake it.] Reno Banks: Are you ready to be a star once again? Its your choice. You know my track record, Mr. Jones. I guarantee results. Remember your run in 360WE? Remember the name we built together there? Remember your run in LOW? It could all become a reallity all over again if you and I are on the same page. [Jasmine turns towards Vince who is still leaning against the wall with his arms folded across his chest.] Reno Banks: Its up to you, Mr. Jones. Right now you're scheduled to face a lowly opponent by the name of Ricky Thunder. He's the bottom of the totem pole. He's small change, Mr. Jones. You know you're capable of being way more than a curtain opener. C'mon, Mr. Jones. [Vince ponders Reno's words with his hand cupping his chin. Finally Vince walks towards Reno's desk and shakes Reno's hand.] Reno Banks: Good, good, Mr. Jones! Good choice. With you and I back on the same page you're gonna do big things in GCW. Vince Jones you're about to blast the roof off of GCW...SKYHIGH!! Now please have a seat. We need to iron out a few details... [Vince Jones takes a seat next to Jasmine. Reno takes his seat as well.] Reno Banks: There's been one question burning on my mind for awhile now. Before I say anything please keep a level head about you, Mr. Jones. Alright.” Vince Jones: Aiight. What the deal? Reno Banks: I ask you this because as your agent we have to see eye to eye. There cannot be any secrets between the two of us. Mr. Jones, why did you run out on us back in LOW? And where did you run off to anyways?” [Vince sits there in silent frustration.] Reno Banks: Mr. Jones, I need answers as well as Jasmine. What was going on? Where was your head at? [Vince suddenly rises to his feet and makes his way to the door.] Reno Banks: Vince?” Jasmine: [turns towards Vince] V?” Where you going? Vince Jones: It don't matta! V out! This meetin's ova! 'Nuff said! [Vince exits the room and slams the door behind him. Reno and Jasmine look at one another in confusion as the camera slowly fades to black.]
