Emergency

3 November 2007

Date: November 3, 2007 I think we have an emergency I think we have an emergency If you thought I'd leave then you were wrong Because I won't stop holding on So are you listening? So are you watching me? If you thought I'd leave then you were wrong Because I won't stop holding on This is an emergency So are you listening? And I can't pretend that I don't see this It's really not your fault And no one cares to talk about it To talk about it 'Cause I've seen love die way too many times When it deserved to be alive (when it deserved to be alive) And I've seen you cry way too many times When you deserved to be alive Alive The scars they will not fade away And no one cares to talk about it To talk about it 'Cause I've seen love die way too many times When it deserved to be alive (when it deserved to be alive) I've seen you cry way too many times When you deserved to be alive Alive -- "Emergency" by Paramore ---- Chapter One: The Morning After ---- BUZZ BUZZ BUZZ Suddenly a hand slammed down on top of the alarm clock sending the room back into silence. The silence was temporary... "Son of a...!" A girl could be heard screaming with a bit of a crack in her voice from under the covers. With a grimace on her face Vivica J. Valentine falls out of her bed and onto the floor, still tangled in a nasty web of white covers and a midnight blue comforter. Grasping her left arm with her right, you could see sheer pain shoot across her face from under the tangled red and blonde locks of hair. Wincing she rolls over onto her back, taking deep breaths and facing the ceiling. Through the pain her cerulean blue eyes open slowly, letting the light in for the first time this morning. An extended blink gradually brought her surroundings into focus. It was home. For a moment her thoughts were in a frenzied jumble but became clear as she began to situate herself on the bedroom floor. Slowly hoisting herself to a sitting position with her right arm, she looked down at her injury from Dangerous Games. Her left arm was still burnt but didn't sting to the touch as much as it once did. That doesn't mean it doesn't hurt when to slam it down on top of an alarm clock. The Fearless Phenom leaned against the night stand next to her bed, where the alarm clock read 2:00pm. It had been a long night and lord knows she needed the rest. She'd spent the entire day before in a New York City area hospital, the name escaped her at this time in the morning. Constant tests and prodding made her arm hurt almost as bad as the attack by Tony Gamble that put her there in the first place. They'd told her it was necessary but she was still skeptical, as always. Maybe it was the same bullheadedness that made her take that late night flight back home to Chicago as soon as she was released instead of renting a hotel for the night. Across the room she saw her travel bag still unpacked. Didn't even have time to unpack it before she passed out in her bed. The past couple of weeks have been nothing short of a nightmare on her body. Destiny wasn't exactly supposed to feel like this, but when you want something bad enough... you'll deal with it. More than anything what she wanted right now was a new bandage. The scars on her arm weren't exactly the most flattering thing to wake up to in the morning, some of them the doctors said will never go away. One day she thought she'd wear them like badges of honor, but right now they hurt like a bitch. Crawling across her tan carpeted bedroom floor on her right arm and both knees, Vivica reached out for the black carry-on bag she'd taken on her flight with her. Zipping it open she began to rake through her belongings. T-shirt, t-shirt, jeans, iPod, Pill Bottle? Slowly she pulled the bottle out of the bag and examined it. They appeared to be pain killers from the hospital. She simply shook her head in frustration before launching them across the room. No matter how many times she told them she didn't take medication, they keep insisting. The straight-edge lifestyle certainly wasn't glamours, and sometimes it was painful... but it was hers. Digging back through the bag she began to start taking things out looking for a fresh Ace bandage. Jeans, earplugs, headphones, toothpaste... cell phone. The red light on top of her cell phone blinked trying to get her attention. On the screen it read "One Missed Call" as she flipped it open. Without as much as reading who it was she checked her voicemail. She was expecting a call from PRIME to notify her that Tony Gamble had stopped being a chicken shit and accepted her challenge. "Vivica? This is Lia. I don't know where you are right now, but I've had something come up. I need to discuss plans with you about Devil's Night as a result, and... just give me a call back when you get this message, okay? I'll be here most of the day." "Lia..." She said with a sigh, flipping the phone shut abruptly after the message. Suddenly the search for an Ace Bandage wasn't so important anymore. Closing her eyes she leaned back against the bedroom wall and slowly began to bounce the back of her head off of it. "You've really done it this time Vivica..." She said to herself opening her eyes again and viewing her own reflection in the mirror on the other side of the room. Wearing a red tube top and a pair of black fleece pajama bottoms she looked like a wreck. Her blonde hair with one red streak was all over the place, covering half of her face as she moved it out of the way to get a better view. At least the outside matched the inside as a sudden sense of failure swept over her body. She finally remembered what happened after she left the arena at WorldWide. Steven Caldera had ordered The Establishment to attack Lia Ambrosi... and as she hung in there and waited for Vivica to show up, no one came. Typical Vivica J. Valentine, as soon as someone who you care about needs you... you're gone. First Markus, her lover, burns in a fire as she stands by in tears, frozen in time like a deer in headlights. Now Lia Ambrosi needs her to come out and save her... and she's off to the hospital, never stopping to think if she should stick around incase Lia needs her. What excuse could she possibly give? Sorry Lia, I wasn't there to save you because I was only giving a damn about myself. You were getting your ass beat the other night? Oh, I was sitting down listening to the new Avenged Sevenfold album on my iPod. "I'm such a fuckup sometimes..." she said out loud to herself looking down at the cell phone before clinching it in her right fist and knocking it up against her head in frustration. So she had to make it up to her. She had to take the chance she never got to against Markus. But what? Suddenly the tune of "Misery Business" by Paramore began to play in the room and a vibration shook her hand. Looking down she saw an incoming call from... "The doctor..." Vivica said out loud, "OH SHIT I'M LATE!" Scrambling up to her feet Vivica grabs the t-shirt and jeans she'd pulled out of the bag and scrambled to get them on. She'd had a doctor's appointment scheduled at 2:00 to get a final say on whether or not she'd be able to compete at Devil's Night. Putting on clothes one-handed was a challenge to most, luckily it'd almost become natural to Vivica over the past couple of weeks. With a final zip of her pants she jumped into a pair of flip-flops, grabbed her wallet and keys, then was on her way out the door. As she sprinted down the hallway, you could hear the tune of "Misery Business" by Paramore playing again. She didn't bother answering. ---- Chapter Two: Doctor's Orders ---- The sterile room had the familiar feel of discomfort. Bright white lights and uncomfortable padded tables covered in wax paper had become the norm in her life. The paper crunched as she adjusted herself for the millionth time. Looking in the mirror across the room, she continued to fix her hair. She didn't have an excuse for why she cared so much about what her hair looked like... perhaps it was just an excuse to not think about everything else going on around her. The fresh Ace Bandage on her left arm made her feel like a new woman as she sat alone in the doctor's office. The doctor had gone on to fetch her test results and as excited as she should've been to find out her status for Devil's Night... she couldn't seem to get her mind off the phone in the palm of her right hand. The red light blinked at her mockingly. One New Voicemail the text read. Her sight hadn't left the front of her cell phone for nearly five minutes since Dr. Nordstrom had left the room. Never in her life had she been so hesitant about something so silly as a voicemail. It was her guilty conscious getting the best of her. She should've been there. She didn't have an excuse. What do you say when you finally pick up that phone? You could say you're sorry, but that just doesn't cut it. When you're slated to face someone at the end of the year... it's hard for them to believe that you're really sorry for them getting their ass kicked. So much had changed between them since she'd won Dangerous Games. Every decision that she's attempted to make she'd second guessed herself in the eyes of Lia. She was so worried she was going to give the wrong impression. Worried that with the slightest mistake in words or actions, she'd lose the best friend she had in the entire world. After she managed to kill off Markus, and then stab Lia Ambrosi in the back, what would she do with her next trick? Run over Tessa Windsor's dog. Her mind had gone too far, she HAD to listen... "Vivica? *sigh* This is Lia again. It's been about six hours since the last time I called you, so I'm wondering if the first message even went through. So ah... just calling back to make sure. I've had a change of plans this week, and I really need to talk to you about how we should handle it. Can you give me a call?" Flipping the phone shut she knew deep down inside, that she had to return that phone call. She had to clear everything up. What if she fucked up again? Then what do you do? It's all over... Suddenly Dr. Nordstrom opened the door sidetracking Vivica's stress fit. "Well Vivica I have your tests here and..." He paused for a moment, typical asshole doctor suspense move, "it doesn't look good. You're not 100% yet. Your arm is very tender and with the type of wrestler Tony Gamble is... he's going to sniff that out like blood in the water. He can put you down for a long time. I do not recommend, in the slightest, that you attempt to wrestle at Devil's Night." "I can beat that fucking coward if that's what you're worried about. Heh, Tony Gamble... if that's all that's keeping me from being able to..." Suddenly she was interrupted. "It's not." He stated cutting her off abruptly, "Steven Caldera sent papers over and you're booked in GCW's final match of the evening. You and Lia Ambrosi in... a Buried Alive Match? Against Bryan Mayhem and Shaman. Last time you stepped up against Bryan Mayhem you got that injury in the first place. Give this man No Disqualification an..." "Lia Ambrosi?" Vivica asked as her mind went to one-track in a hurry, "I'm booked as Lia's tag team partner?" "Yeah. Choosers Of The Slain. That's what it says..." He stated before going on, "but I simply cannot allow you to..." "I have to fight." Vivica answered flatly, not giving Dr. Nordstrom an opportunity to tell her otherwise, "I can't hang her out to dry again. If I don't show up he'll make her fight them alone. I know how he is, I know what he does to people. No. No way. I'm not fucking this up doctor I'm sorry." "You're putting your title shot in grave dang..." "I. DON'T. CARE." Vivica raised her voice. You could almost see the fire light inside of her brilliant cerulean blue eyes as she stood up, "I'm going. I'm not fucking this up again. That's the end of this. Call Steven Caldera. Tell him he has his match." She couldn't do it again. That would be the end of it for sure... she had to show up. Much to the doctor's chagrin he watched helplessly as The Fearless Phenom pulled the door open and slammed it shut behind her. Her subconscious was telling her that Steven Caldera was playing both of them like a fiddle, but deep down inside she knew. Although she didn't have the words to save their relationship... she had the actions. Injury or not injury... she was showing up to Devil's Night. Even if she had to do it on fire. ---- Chapter Three: This Is An Emergency... ---- WHACK As the punching bag sways away, a grimace crosses her face. The punching bag comes swinging back and she bites her lip and swings. WHACK With another shot she pulls away with her left arm a little bit. Trying not to move her right arm over, she concentrates on the punching bag coming back at her. WHACK A grunt escapes her lip as the punching bag swings back farther than before. As it swings back a squint crosses the face of The Fearless Phenom during her wind-up. WHACK Holding her left arm she backs away from the punching bag in obvious pain. Taking a moment to gather herself she attempt to shake out her left arm, with a bit of a grunt. Obviously disappointed in herself Vivica J. Valentine sighs with frustration when suddenly... "Vivica?!" A familiar voice interrupts her mindset. In the corner of the room GCW Rizing Starz columnist Ayake Sonoda steps into the room. With a look of concern on her face she jobs over towards The Fearless Phenom who begins to stretch out her left arm. "Oh... hey Ayake..." Vivica responded trying not to initiate conversation to the point where it distracts her from her goals. She'd been at it for a little over an hour now, but if she wanted to have any shot at surviving Devil's Night... she had a long night ahead of her. "I found this outside on a bench." Ayake help up a black cellular phone. Vivica immediately recognized it as her own. A red light blinked at her from the corner of the phone. Just when she seemed to get focused and get her mind off of all her problems... they keep coming back to get her. "It rang about an hour ago, and it's been blinking ever since. I can't imagine who'd call you at one in the morning though..." Looking up at the clock The Fearless Phenom was shocked to see it was getting near 2:15am. Maybe it'd been a lot more than an hour. She'd lost track of time at some point. "What are you doing here at this time of night?" Vivica asked while reaching out to grab the cellular phone from her fellow employee. "Honestly?" Ayake said, "I'm worried. About you." Vivica didn't have an immediately response to make Ayake satisfied. So instead she flipped open the cell phone and it was just as she feared. One Missed Call. Lia Ambrosi. Every day since WorldWide the name had been harder and harder to look at. Her best friend... a name that once brought a smile to her face now made her heart sink to the pit of her stomach in guilt. Hitting the talk button it immediately took her to her voicemail... "Vivica, I left you two messages, but couldn't get in contact with you. I don't know what's going on, but I would have liked to at least know that you got them even if you didn't have time to talk... I don't have much time to get into details here, but I've changed my plans for this week. I'll be at Devil's Night, but I probably won't see you before then. If you need to leave me a message, call Dawn- I'll be with staying with her." As she flipped the phone shut the guilt continued to eat away at her. Bottling it up inside it began to take control of her every action. Every thought had become not fucking up again. Ayake could see on Vivica's face how distraught she was as she slipped the cell phone into her pocket. "What's wrong Vivica? Why are you like this?" Ayake questioned, breaking the uncomfortable silence between them. "Ayake this is something I have to deal with on my own." Vivica answered. "But you're hurt." Ayake said looked at Vivica's bandaged left arm. "Why don't you just rest for the night? You're pushing yourself so hard you're going to end up killing yourself at this rate." "When I'm in a match eventually the adrenaline is going to wear off Ayake..." Vivica said, "and I'm going to have to deal with this for what's going to feel like days. If I don't get used to it, I'm fucked. Your father was such a great fighter I'm sure you've seen him go through much worse." "Hai." She said under her breath, "But that's also why I didn't want to follow in his footsteps." "It's a tough life..." Vivica spoke as she tried to stretch out her left arm again, "but I have a lot of people's expectations to live up to. I can't continue to let snakes like Tony Gamble have free reign and do whatever the hell he pleases to me without getting back at him. I can see the look on his cocksucker now smiling if I don't show up. Then there's Bryan Mayhem, I haven't beaten the guy in MONTHS. I can't let the entire world think he owns me, that he has my number. Then Lia..." She paused for a moment looking disappointed in herself. "She's gone through enough shit... she's got to have something to depend on. Someone to depend on. I can't let her think I'm just going to up and leave her behind. Turn my back just because of this title shot." Vivica spoke quietly as she looked at the ground. "Someone else depends on you too you know." Ayake looked over at Vivica, who slowly raised her head to acknowledge Miss Sonoda was talking, "Your fans." For the first time in recently memory the fans never crossed the mind of The Fearless Phenom until just now. The very people who were behind her almost the entire way. Besides Lia Ambrosi and Tessa Windsor... they were the people who she could always count on to have her back. To cheer her on to victory. "They don't want to see you go out there and kill yourself. Then you'll never reach your destiny." Ayake said, "What do I know though right? I just saw my dad's career end in the middle of the ring when I was a teenager. Because he pushed himself TOO HARD. He cared too much. Just like you do." "I can't fail Ayake..." Vivica said, "Not like this. I've come way too far to throw everything away." "Then don't throw everything away." Ayake said, "Take your bag, go back to your hotel room and go to bed. If the phone rings for God's sake answer it Vivica. Tell Lia the truth. You're like sisters, I'm sure if anyone will understand it'll be her. Go get some sleep, before you kill yourself." Vivica met Ayake's reasoning with complete silence, almost as if she was lost for words once again. Maybe it was that same stubbornness that caused her so many problems that wouldn't allow her to admit she made a mistake. "Take care of yourself Vivica..." She said as she walked away, "It's not such a selfish thing to care about your well being." The Fearless Phenom just watched in silence as Ayake Sonoda left the room. Looking down at her phone she still saw Lia Ambrosi three times on her missed calls list. There some bit of foolish pride deep down inside of The Black Mariah that kept her from dialing her best friend right then and there. She was still going to show up to Devil's Night... and she was still going to give Tony Gamble the ass beating of a lifetime, but everyday was a new lesson. With every step came experience. Still very young... The Fearless Phenom was learning. Getting better and better everyday. For those who care very much about her it was marvelous to watch what she was growing into and becoming. From a brash little girl who could barely do a textbook armdrag in January 2006 to the woman she was today. She truly was a Phenomenon. To those who stand in her way it has become very scary to realize that she still had a lot to learn, and reaching her full potential was just a matter of time away. Tonight, she grew even closer. ---- Chapter Four: An Unexpected Message ---- I'm in the business of misery, let's take it from the top She's got a body like an hourglass that's ticking like a clock It's a matter of time before we all run out The tune of "Misery Business" by Paramore cut through the silent night. The red light on the corner of the black cell phone began to blink as the verse resonated through the ear drum of The Fearless Phenom. A lump was in the throat of Vivica J. Valentine as her eyes opened and glanced through the blackness of the early morning at the red light, mocking her on the night stand. That lump in her throat was her pride, she swallowed deeply as she reached out for the phone. Flipping it open she took a deep breath, and just started out pouring whatever words and emotions came to mind. "Lia I'm so sorry about everything I just..." "Vivica J. Valentine?" An unfamiliar voice interrupted her confessional to whom she thought was her best friend. Taking a moment for the question to set in Vivica's heart skipped a beat, who in the hell was that? "...Yeah?" She answered slowly, still a little groggy from waking up. Her mind was trying to get up to speed with her heart. "Does the name Markus Blaire mean anything to you?" He spoke slowly with a raspy voice, emphasized by the use of a distortion device. It was quite possibly the last name in the entire world she expected to hear spoken to her. Her heart beat faster and faster as she finally answered. "It means everything to me." She said. "Don't give up hope. The end is at the beginning." The distorted voice spoke out before the sound of a phone hanging up on the other side quickly ended their conversation. Setting the phone down her heart skipped a beat. Just hearing his name again brought back the feeling she had for him. The feelings that had taken a backseat to all the other stress that was going on in her life. For just a few moments anyway, Markus was the only thing in the world she cared about. The rough and tumble of sleepless, terror filled dreams of fire and stress ended that night. She slept peacefully. All the medication in the world couldn't have done for her what that one phone call did. Love is a wonderful thing After all.
