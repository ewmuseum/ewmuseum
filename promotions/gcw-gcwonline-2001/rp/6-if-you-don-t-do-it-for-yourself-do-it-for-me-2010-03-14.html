6. "If you don't do it for yourself, do it for me"

14 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/10 For the first time in a long time, I actually feel like myself again. I'm not going to sit here and tell you about my issues or my high powered reasoning into what I had become, but I will tell you this, if it wasn't for my brother stepping in, I would be jobbing for Shaman or even worse. I think WEC is coming to a close for me. I've already missed the biggest card they ever produced, and I don't think they are going to let me just slide back into where I was. I have to fight tooth and nail to get to where I was, and Smitty is going to help me get there. GCW, watch out, because The Executioner is about to take names... ---------------------------------------- The small room that was once his own when he was in high school is now the epicenter of Kris Michaels. It is important to remember where you come from, and Michaels nearly lost focus of that. It took a lot to get to the prestigious level where he was at in WEC, not to mention the first couple remarkable wins in GCW, but everything started to change because HE changed. The room is definitely a reminder of the man that once took down the giants of the cage. The bleach white walls with hand written notes, autographs, and fight history. The Black ceiling reinforced to hang a heavy bag in the center of the room. The tempurpedic bed and pillow covered with white and black sheets because "the most important point of a fighter is sleeping." The room seems plain and quite unspectacular, but there are small things that would make you smirk, like the spotlight hanging from the top center of one of the walls pointing at the opposite for shadow boxing. Or the reinforced bar in the closet to hang upside down and do inverted sit ups. Or even the assortment of boxing/workout gear hanging everywhere. The most important part of the room of all is where Kris sleeps though. No, not the bed, it is much more important than that. Nearly right next to the pillow on the wall is a small note with a beautifully signed name. No, it isn't want you might think. It isn't something corny like "You will always be mine." or "Forever yours." It is much more important than that. Yes, it is from a girl. Yes it is THE girl. But it is a message that started one of the most successful careers of any man in the WEC. If you don't do it for yourself, do it for me." Now, you might be thinking that it is the corniest fucking thing you ever read, but to a kid going into high school, thinking he can no longer fight against some of the semi pro fighters he is being pitted up against, this is a huge deal. He didn't want to let down his father, he didn't want to let down himself. But most of all, he didn't want to let down HER. Every night he would read that note and stare at it until he fell asleep. Every night he would envision himself winning. Every night he would see her in the crowd cheering him on. "Kris! Get your ass out of bed! You've better get a meal in you before the first session!" Smitty took up residence in the house for the time being to get Kris back on track. There is nothing more motivating than having the best world class trainer watching your every move trying to get you back into the game, besides maybe a hand written note by your lover... "Get up you fuckin...fuck!" Grumbles and remarks fly downstairs between Smitty and Kris' older brother Pattilac. Smitty is all about professionalism, and Pattilac wants money and fame. Pattilac makes a game out of everything until something steps in front of his name. Kris gets out of the bed and leans over his knees. He starts with his routine stretching and cracking starting with his hands and then goes into cracking his back and neck. If you are a fighter, then you know why this is important. Kris walks over to his nightstand and opens a tackle box of pills and begins to grab one out of each of the twenty three slots. As you can imagine, it takes nearly a whole water bottle to swallow them. "jesus fuckin' Christ and his pathetic saints. How heavily medicated are you?" Kris turns over his shoulder and stares at his brother for a brief second and then goes back to drinking his water with a shot of vitamin c. "You know, when Smitty first approached me about staying here, I thought it was a good idea. You know we go way back, so I thought it would be like ol' times. But you have him in such a fuckin funk that all he wants to do is cook food for you, plan to train with you, and then book shit for YOU!" "What's the matter, afraid I'm going to steal another one of your friends?" Pattilac (now only going to be written as Pat) tenses up in anger and then takes another step forward before Kris points to the side wall. Pat stops instantly and shakes with anger before backing through the door. Nearly right next to Kris is a decent size hole in the wall with a indent just above it. The hole is from Pat's head and the indent is from the rest of his body flying into the wall upside down after he tried to attack Kris back in high school. Kris makes his way downstairs as the smell of eggs and wheat toast fill up the kitchen. Smitty is standing behind the stove with two plates ready to bring them to the table. Pat walks in from the game room and notices the two plates. "Thanks for breakfast, Smitty! I was just going to say-" "It is for me and your brother." "What..." Pat looks both perplexed and pissed the fuck off. "What about me?" "Aren't you on some kind of diet for your show? Make your fuckin grits and leave us alone, we have a long day." Pat gets to the cabinet where his food is and begins to pull things together while keeping a silent eye on Kris at the table. "I have the day planned and your meals are already cooked. You are going to need two sets of workout clothes, a towel, and some street clothes. Two workouts throughout the day and we have to meet up with a gym owner tonight for dinner. Nothing fancy. I just want to show your face before I buy the gym from him." At these words Kris looks up and just nods. he knows how Smitty is. He will choose a locale, figure out the best place to train, and then buy it or rent it out. He must have really liked this gym. "Do I need to bring any gear?" "No, I have new stuff for you." Kris nods as he pushes some cooked egg whites onto a piece of wheat bread. The kitchen is silent until Pat slams the microwave door closed with his grits and cheese inside. He sneers at Kris as he looks up but Kris clears his plate. "Ready Smitty?" Kris stands up and tosses his paper plate in the garbage and heads upstairs to collect his clothes. ------------------- "...so you understand the stations?" Kris, shirtless and glistening with sweat beads, wipes his brow and nods, slightly out of breath. Smitty points to five different stations in the order in which he wants Kris to take them. "We will start with just thirty seconds in each station because it is new, and then we will kick it up to a minute. Ready?" *whistle* Kris drops to the ground and places his hands on a 45lb plate and begins to push it across the floor. He reaches a line and then goes in reverse, obviously straining a lot more. *whistle* Kris runs over to a squat bar and lifts it off and puts it resting nearly on his collar bone. He squats with the bar and then upon reaching the top, he shoulder presses it. He begins to pick up the pace... *whistle* Kris runs over to a medicine ball and squats down to pick it up and then suddenly launches it backwards over his head to the other side of the room. He runs after it and then picks it up again... *whistle* Kris runs over to straps hanging from the wall and puts his feet inside of them. in a pushup position, he begins to move his legs in mountain climbing motion... *whistle* Kris runs over to a palliates ball and lifts up a 25lb dumb bell in each hand and begins to punch with them while supporting his upper back on the ball. "TIME!" Kris drops the weights and stands up, wiping his brow again. He doesn't seem to be any more winded than he was at the start of the circuit. "Alright, now that was pretty good, but you need to focus on form a little bit more. I want you to visualize squeezing each group of muscles while doing the exercise. You should be able to make ten pounds feel like a hundred. Alright, now again!" ***** "Good. Very good, Kris. Now, I want you to go to the heavy bag and do your punches first. Left right's and then do your kicks. Just body kicks for now. I will catch up. I just need to make a call." Kris makes his way over to the bag and slides on his bag gloves over his damp wraps. This is his favorite part of the work out. When a fighter gets to the level that Michaels is at, there is no thinking going on in these routines. Instinct takes over. When instinct takes over, it leaves the fighter to think. Right, Left. Right left. Right....Fuckin Pat always on my case. Now he has Smitty pissed at him. I just don't understand his problem. HE approached ME. Pat wanted ME to train and all. I mean, what has he really done for me? Pat writes the diet, and makes Smitty cook it? Smitty had enough on his plate, no pun intended. The bag now moves like a metronome between kicks from Kris. He begins to faze out his eyes go blank. Smitty gets off of his phone call and walks over seeing this happen. The kicks are perfect in form, and devastating in power even after all the workouts from the day. Smitty leans up against one of the pillars and watches, also slightly getting entranced by the bag. After about five minutes, Smitty begins to get bored and walks over to the bag. "Kris. KRIS! Enough! That's good. Take a breather." Kris walks over to his bag and begins to drink out of his liter bottle. Smitty watches him as he stares at the wall past is bag. He looks at his watch and sees that they have plenty of time. "Kris, what's bothering you man?" Kris looks at him and shakes the blankness out of his eyes. "Huh? What?" "See, that's what I'm talking about. You went through the workout, perfect I might add, but you aren't there. What is it? Your brother? Me? STD? What the fuck is bothering you?" Kris smirks a little before answering. "Smitty..." Smitty looks as concerned as he can. "I know you don't have an older brother, but can you take a look at the situation from my point of view?" "Your brother is..." "...a total asshole. I mean, I do all the work, he wants to take the pay cut and the name. He doesn't care about anything but himself. I want to do this, don't get me wrong, I do. But I don't want to do it for him. I don't want to do it for the money..." "Then don't do it for him. Do it for yourself!" "'if not for yourself, then for me.'" Smitty looks at him slightly confused and looks in the direction that Kris is looking, trying to figure out the ME. "Smitty, would you put your life on hold, sacrificing everything you have to make it in the ring? Well, I have once, and I don't want to do it again. I really don't! Pat wants everything to be exclusive, but I need the things I care about. I need to be, me." Smitty looks at him with a sunken sympathizing face, but it is something he's heard from many fighters before. Before he says anything at all he thinks of all the different people that have said something similar, but then thinks harder, back to the last ten years of Kris' training and fighting history, every since middle school. He starts to sympathize with Michaels... "I just...I want to know where I stand in the world. I want to know where I am going, but then again, I want to know that it wasn't all for nothing. I've had fame. I've had some sort of fortune. But there is so much more. I don't want to be the only one that lives and breaths fighting. I want a life." With that, Kris stands up and goes back to the bag. He looks at it, still swinging back and forth and then kicks it as hard as he can, knocking it off of its chain. The bag falls with a loud thud which echoes off the walls. "I want her..."
