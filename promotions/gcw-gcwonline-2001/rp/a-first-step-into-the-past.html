A First Step Into the Past

Date: 2003-2005 Flash. September, 2003. I hadn't felt this way in years. Since the moment I lost Rebecca, I'd been numb to feelingand apathetic to life. She had been absolutely everything to me. My air... my blood... My life. But finally...finally, I found myself an escape. It was a resurgance that was running briskly through my veins. A resurgance that had otherwise been at a stand still since the day I'd stepped through those ropes and into that squared circle. Poor career choices paved this path for me. I never wanted to do what I'm doing. Who the fuck wants to be a professional wrestler for a living? A professional fighter. Can you even consider it a profession? Its a savage sport that shows sympathy to no man. It doesn't care if you lose somebody. It just tells you to get over it. It doesn't give a shit if you get hurt. It just shows you the drugs and tells you to forget about the pain. A profession? More like a death sentence. It barely pays the bills and it barely lets you by...alive. Its the worst choice I've ever made in my entire life. But it did give me Rebecca. Just as quickly, it took her away -- but at least it gave me her to begin with. And those moments with her changed everything I would've ever thought about life. But she's gone. Its taken me a while to accept it. But I am now. I must. I'm going to end up killing myself if I don't. This chapter of my life is over. My career as a savage is through. So for the sake of kayfabe, let it be known and let it be sworn ... this epic is over before I'm irreputably torn. - CB FORWARD January, 2005 "I can still see her face when I close my eyes," Chris whispers while encompassing his attention on a small picture frame atop his fireplace. He sits slightly reclined on a black suede love seat in the heat-powered warmth of his New Jersey home. "Its better to remember and weap then to ever forget, Chris," replies his long-time friend who is seated across the room. "Sometimes I feel like I should just be over all this already, Tommy." Chris lowers his head in distraught. His eyes glaze over for a minute before continuing, "like ... I could be stronger." "There is NOBODY that can deny you the right to sorrow on the very day that changed your life. You spend the other three hundred and however many days of the year forgetting. This day is yours... .. its yours to remember." SCRIPTURE Tommy's words rang through my head that night. I don't think I could ever forget the sternness in his voice and the demand in his eyes. He definitely wasn't letting me take him lightly. I've gotten better though. I reshaped my life. I got myself a real estate license and I was doing well in the business. Granted, the housing market still hadn't hit rock bottom yet, either. But there was something about January second that crushed me. I suppose Tommy was right. I will never be able to forget the day I lost the only one I had left. Getting off the road helped the healing process. Settling down and not depending on meager meals and alcohol to get by allowed me to see what else life has to offer. I couldn't be happier to say that I left that disgraceful business. I would probably already be dead if I had not. Nevertheless, I have to give credit to the new lady in my life. I met Michelle on one of my first sales and we instantly hit it off. We've been exclusive for near a year now. Having someone new in my life is certainly amazing. Although I will never truly and fully get over .. Rebecca. - CB
