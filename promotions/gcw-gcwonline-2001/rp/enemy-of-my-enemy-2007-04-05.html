Enemy of my Enemy

5 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/2007 Forgive, sounds good. Forget, I'm not sure I could. They say time heals everything, But I'm still waiting High Spirits Casino Resort, Las Vegas, NV 4/5/2007; 0915 hours Gambling may not really be a sin... as long as one didn’t overdo it. As many people lost their shirts in the many casinos of Las Vegas... as many people got robbed of their life savings by one-armed bandits... as many people had to hock precious jewelry and family heirlooms to pay off the prices needed to be paid, to learn that it’s a BAD idea to try to bluff one into folding their Big Blinds in Texas Hold’em competitions... there were also the conscientious and careful people who set strict budgets and stuck to them; who knew when to QUIT and not try to recoup further losses. As long as one was responsible, gambling wasn’t really a sin. But don’t tell that to Katie O’Clare. “Dastardly... sure and truly vile,” she mumbled under her breath, passing by two casino rooms on her way to the hotel restaurant for breakfast. Of course, the casinos weren’t open at this early hour, and that suited the diminutive Irishwoman just fine. She was a guest at this hotel strictly because of the High Stakes supercard, and her involvement in the four-way match for the Vanguard championship. The hotel was opulent almost to a fault, including the restaurant. The ceiling candelabras were faceted lead crystal with silver trimming, and each table was covered with a tablecloth of the finest linen, and a silver candlestick stood on each table with a deep blue candle measuring a foot long. The candles burned with a small, slow, steady flame. The cup that held Katie’s coffee (brought without her needing to ask for it) was hand-shaped high-quality china delicately painted with ornate designs. The utensils were all sterling silver. “San Diego omelet, the breakfast special,” Katie requested when the waitress came to take her order. “And a bowl of oatmeal with milk, and topped with blueberries.” The Vanguard match weighed heavily on Katie’s mind as she ate her breakfast. She knew that neither Peter Vetra nor Rufio were the least bit pleased to see her placed as a participant in the match. Not that the diminutive Irishwoman cared one bit what either of them thought, but it meant she would need to watch her back at all times. The two men probably would think nothing of ganging up on her in an attempt to take her out of the match early. Then there was the wild card. Vivica J. Valentine. I'm through, with doubt, There's nothing left for me to figure out, I've paid a price, and I'll keep paying... The Catholic in Katie told her to forgive Vivica for what had happened between them many months ago... but the pragmatist in her screamed out warnings against trusting the Fearless Phenom. Those do not remember the past, are doomed to repeat it, and Katie had enough to worry about with Peter Vetra and the chauvinist pig Rufio, without extending the hand of friendship to Vivica and have that hand nearly sliced off. The question was, would Vivica slice it off... or clasp it in alliance? Enemy of my enemy. Vivica hated Rufio even more than Katie did... and with good reason. The two had been at each other’s throats since she beat him in an open invitational he held. Enemy of my enemy. The quintessential chauvinist pig, Rufio was a belittler of the female gender and the abilities of women... something Vivica would never take kindly to anymore than the Monarch Butterfly would herself. Enemy of my enemy. Vivica was hungry for her next championship belt and had no love lost for Peter Vetra, either; they’d wrestled a brutal bout the last WorldWide for the Vanguard Championship and the Fearless Phenom was hankering after some payback at how it ended. Enemy of my enemy. Finally, Peter Vetra and Rufio were likely counting on Katie’s continued grudge against Vivica. Katie still not trusting Vivica was probably the very thing the two men wanted the most, because if they struck any kind of alliance, it could potentially spell disaster for both the champion and Rufio. The two women’s wrestling styles were not at all dissimilar and pretty complementary; if they teamed up against their other two opponents, the tempo of the match would likely be turned on its collective head. Trusting Vivica could also be exactly what the Fearless Phenom herself wanted, also... so she could set another trap for the Monarch Butterfly. Did Katie have the luxury of leaving herself unguarded against that potential threat? I'm not ready to make nice, I'm not ready to back down! I'm still mad as hell And I don't have time To go round and round and around! “Thank you, come again,” the waitress smiled as she placed the check down on Katie’s table. “Be sure to try our blackjack tables during normal casino hours; all first-time gamblers at our casino get a complementary $200 in chips toward a round of blackjack.” Having given the obligatory promo for the hotel’s gambling establishments, the waitress turned and strode briskly toward another table; unaware of the two shamrock green eyes that glared sourly at her upper back. Katie left the restaurant without leaving the waitress a tip. Her morning already soured, the diminutive Irishwoman decided to change into athletic clothes and hit the Bally’s Fitness Center on the 15th floor of the resort hotel. The Fitness Center was fully equipped with state of the art machines for every muscle group, as well as dozens upon dozens of assorted free weights; over 25 exercise bicycles, treadmills, and staircase exercisers, 10 rowing exercisers, and a sauna and steam room for relaxing after a workout. The Monarch Butterfly indulged in some time in the steam room to relax her muscles before exerting them. She did some stretching and deep breathing in the steam room. The warm, heavy air opened up her bronchial passages and stimulated her lungs. Katie was feeling better and ready to proceed with her workout... but little did she know what awaited her on the warm-up mats. It's too late to make it right, I probably wouldn't if I could! Cause I'm mad as hell Can't bring myself to do what it is You think I should... Katie stopped dead in her tracks on sighting Vivica J. Valentine on the warm-up mats, as she entered the workout area of the fitness center. She had to fight to completely stifle a groan. Jawing with the blonde bombshell was pretty much the last thing Katie wanted to do right now... there would be time enough for that come High Stakes. Those blackjack tables were actually starting to look like not so terrible an idea after all—a train of thought that had Katie fighting back a wave of nausea. Vivica and Katie stared at each other for a few seconds, before the Fearless Phenom turned slightly and resumed her stretching exercises. Apparently she had decided to deal with Katie’s presence here by simply ignoring her—treating her as if she were any other stranger come into the fitness center. That suited the diminutive Irishwoman just fine and she picked a spot on the mats a respectful distance away from Vivica, to commence her own warm-up routine. Still, Katie found she was incapable of stopping herself from stealing the occasional quick, stealthy glance over her shoulder at Vivica from time to time. A lingering mistrust that the lithe blonde might try to cheap-shot her from behind; a lingering desire to throw caution to the wind and try to break the ice in hopes of actually establishing some sort of dialogue; a combination of the two... Katie couldn’t be entirely sure. More than likely it was a little of both of the above. Whatever the reason, however... the more Katie proceeded with her warm-up stretches, the more she knew that one thing was certain: she couldn’t simply ignore Vivica’s presence the whole time here. And when she happened to glance at a mirror-like piece of metal, Katie knew... Vivica was thinking the same thing. The blue-eyed blonde was taking the same quick, stealthy glances over her shoulder at the Monarch Butterfly that Katie was taking at her. I know, you said Why can't you just get over it, It turned my whole world around And I kind of like it... But what to say to each other, that was proving a stumper. Simply walking up and saying, “Hi Vivica, I forgive ye for the incident in the locker room,” was absurd and out of the question—forgiveness was pretty much a moot point right now, and the Fearless Phenom wasn’t likely to think she needed to be forgiven, anyway. Katie walked over to one of the free-weight benches. She would be here at least an hour or more, so there was plenty of time to decide how to initiate some kind of dialogue. She adjusted the bench-press barbell to a comfortable weight level for her and lay down on the bench, gripping the long metal shaft in her dainty hands. The cool steel bar rested in her palms as she prepared to lift. Just as Katie drew in breath for the first rep, she felt the bar shift ever so slightly in her grasp. Her eyes opened, and the bar nearly slipped back down onto the rest hooks. Vivica had walked over to the bench and gripped the same bar, preparing to spot Katie. That was certainly one way to break the ice. Katie looked up at the blonde athlete, her shamrock green eyes unblinking. Vivica had decided to make the first move... she had seized the initiative. Now she was calling the tune and Katie had to dance to it. It was an unwelcome, awkward feeling. But Vivica said nothing. She gripped the bar, her hands closer to the weights than Katie’s; watching, waiting for Katie to begin the press exercise. Her eyes weren’t watching Katie’s face, but Katie’s hands. It seemed they had each other over the same barrel, but it just seemed that way, Katie knew. She pushed the bar off of the hooks and pulled the bar down, preparing for the first press. Vivica kept her grip even and loose, continuing to spot Katie. The diminutive Irishwoman slowly released her breath and pressed the bar upward, launching into the first set of repetitions with a smooth, easy rhythm. Still not a word was spoken between the two women. After several sets, Katie deposited the bar back on the hooks, and Vivica slowly grinned. “Still don’t trust me, do you?” she asked. I made my bed, and I sleep like a baby, With no regrets and I don't mind saying, It's a sad sad story that a mother will teach her Daughter that she ought to hate a perfect stranger! Without waiting for an answer, The Fearless Phenom walked over to an adjacent bench and adjusted the weights to a level that was comfortable for her. She lay down on the bench and gripped the bar, then glanced over at Katie, inviting the Monarch Butterfly to spot her in turn. Katie blinked slowly, rising from her bench as she watched Vivica. The atmosphere was anything but threatening, leaving Katie feeling a bit silly to find herself keeping such a heavy guard up. She silently walked over to Vivica’s bench and lightly gripped the bar outside of Vivica’s grip, preparing to spot her. Vivica lifted the bar off of the hooks and prepared to begin pressing. Vivica’s bench-presses proceeded at a slightly brisker rhythm than Katie’s... either a side-effect of Vivica’s somewhat greater strength, or an effort by the blonde to one-up her shorter counterpart. Just her contemplating which was the case, told Katie that Vivica was still calling the shots on any dialogue. With any feeling of control out of her hands, Katie simply said nothing. Vivica set the bar on the hooks and grinned again. “Wasn’t so bad, was it?” she asked. Katie gave a small gesture conceding Vivica’s point, and the two women commenced a round of squats at adjacent benches. Dumb bell flys and curls, and a round of good-morning exercises, followed in simple quiet. Finally Katie opened her mouth in an attempt to get some feeling of control over the conversation, back. “So why are ye being so nice all of a sudden?” she asked. “Sure and are ye trying to win me trust by trusting me?” Vivica’s answer seemed a bit elusive, yet entirely relevant to the subject at hand. “Someone’s got to take their finger off the trigger if we’re going to talk at all. It might as well be me.” And how in the world can the words that I said Send somebody so over the edge? That they'd write me a letter, saying that I better Shut up and sing or my life will be over! The two women hit adjacent treadmills and began at a brisk walking pace. Resistance was set to the equivalent of a hike along a park trail. “Sure and there can be only one champion,” Katie said. “Ye can’t win if I pin Peter Vetra. I don’t win if ye pin Rufio.” “I don’t plan to let you WIN, Katie,” Vivica answered flatly, “nor do I expect you to let me win. But you can bet Peter and Rufio will be more interested in making fools of us first, before they settle the issue of the Vanguard title between themselves. My best bet to beat them at that game—is you. Your best bet... is me.” “And then we settle the title between ourselves, woman to woman, face to face?” Katie’s tone was still dubious. “Why do you have such a problem with that?” Vivica challenged, getting a little irritated at Katie’s stubbornness. Katie was silent for a little while. Vivica waited, determined to get an answer out of the Monarch Butterfly, whatever it might be. The two women finished the treadmill exercise before Katie answered. “I don’t,” she said. “What ye still need to sell me on, is that it is nay just a pipe dream.” “No, Katie,” Vivica replied. “You do.” I'm not ready to make nice, I'm not ready to back down! I'm still mad as hell And I don't have time To go round and round and around! They went to the various machines next; always using the same machine at the same time, always seeming to compete against each other in their own sets as if to one-up the other in them. The bicep curls, the tricep press, the abdominal crunchers, the neck machines, the calf raises; all were done side by side, at competing rhythms. A few more words were exchanged as they began pedaling adjacent exercise bicycles. “So you’re more interested in competing with me in the workout, than talking about Rufio and Vetra?” Vivica asked conversationally. “If I can beat ye here,” Katie answered simply, “then sure and I can beat ye anywhere... including in the ring.” Vivica grinned. She seemed very satisfied with Katie’s answer. Still, Katie knew that her answer skirted around the issue of Rufio and Peter Vetra. “Sure and I have no quarrel with Peter or Rufio right now,” Katie said. “Other than their being opponents in the match, of course.” “That’s what you think,” Vivica snorted. “Just wait ‘til you climb in the ring with them, and you’ll find plenty of quarrel with them. What, do you have to wait until they put you in a locker and kick it a few times, too?” “Exactly why I find the prospect of narrowing the competition down to ye and I, no more appealing than narrowing it down to meself and Rufio... or meself and Peter Vetra,” Katie retorted. Vivica chuckled sardonically. “Except that those two slime pits will stop at nothing to make sure that none of those choices happen.” “So now all of a sudden, simply because ye third opponent is a woman, that makes me into ye ally?” Katie’s attempts to punch holes in Vivica’s argument were relentless. Fending off the logical assault was proving almost as daunting as the Vanguard championship match would be. Vivica said nothing, and Katie summed up her challenge. “I still have no reason to think that suddenly ye like me. So why would ye want to face me one on one, instead of either Peter Vetra or Rufio?” The Fearless Phenom smiled as though she’d been waiting the whole workout for Katie to ask that question. “Because I don’t like either of those two chauvinistic swine... I don’t like their smug, misogynistic faces... I don’t like their more-person-than-thou swagger... I don’t like their a-woman’s-place-is-in-the-kitchen tones of voice. I don’t like Vetra or Rufio, a hell of a lot more than I don’t like you.” It's too late to make it right I probably wouldn't if I could! Cause I'm mad as hell Can't bring myself to do what it is You think I should... They stepped off the exercise bicycles and commenced calisthenics that were part exercise, part cool-down. Vivica was quiet for a while again, letting Katie absorb everything that had just been said. The diminutive Irishwoman was one tough, tough nut to crack, but at least she hadn’t said, ‘hell NO,’ and she seemed to be running out of ammo to use against Vivica. From what Vivica knew about Katie, the pure-defense approach—simply blocking all her shots and waiting for her to exhaust herself—was the best strategy to use against her; both in the wrestling ring... and apparently in a verbal debate as well. Finally Katie spoke up again, and her tone of voice was sarcastic, as though she wasn’t entirely buying into it. “So I do nay have to like ye... I just need to dislike Peter Vetra and Rufio more?” “By George, I think she’s got it,” Vivica quipped lightly. “Sure and how do ye know I don’t already dislike them more?” Katie countered. “I have no quarrel with them right now, but that does not mean I am a member of their fan clubs, if ye catch me meaning.” Vivica’s answer came in a slightly lurching tone, as she fit it in between sit-ups. “Then why do you still have such a bee in your bonnet?” Katie smiled tightly. “Because the enemy of me enemy... is not always me friend,” she said. “The wolf is an enemy of the lynx, and the lynx is an enemy of the jackrabbit, but that does not mean that the jackrabbit invites the wolf over for dinner.” (I'm not ready to make nice,) I'm not ready to back down! I'm still mad as hell And I don't have time To go round and round and around! It's too late to make it right I probably wouldn't if I could! Cause I'm mad as hell Can't bring myself to do what it is You think I should... Cool-down stretches were done and completed, and the two women reached for their towels to wipe away the light sheen of perspiration that sparkled on their skins. Vivica invited herself to follow Katie to the sauna for a well-deserved relaxation period. Vivica’s parting words came as she exited the sauna to change back into street clothes and be on her way. “I’ll see you at High Stakes, Katie... and we’ll see how you feel about all this then. You don’t have to invite me over for dinner... there’ll be enough of Peter Vetra and Rufio to go around. Just come hungry for gold, and hungry for shutting their big mouths. The rest will fall into place.” With that, Vivica left Katie alone with her thoughts, and Katie closed her eyes and took in a few deep breaths to help fully relax herself. One thing that had never been discussed was Katie’s complete lack of interest in the Vanguard Title. She was not going to tell Vivica about that by any means. The whole fed would find out about it soon enough, if Katie were to win the match... and just because she was disinterested in the title, didn’t mean she was going to give anything less than 200% of her effort to win the contest. To the Monarch Butterfly, it was simply a match for bragging rights and honor; nothing more. Changing back into her street clothes, Katie went to the hotel concierge to pick up some maps and inquire about department store shopping. While she loathed the city of Las Vegas and everything it stood for, clothes were clothes, and picking up a few gifts for her family was always a nice idea. Studying the maps and asking the concierge for a few directions, Katie went to catch a bus. She put The Fearless Phenom out of her mind. Just like Vivica had said... there would be time enough to bring the discussion to a resolution, at High Stakes. Forgive, sounds good. Forget, I'm not sure I could. They say time heals everything, But I'm still waiting... Not Ready to Make Nice; The Dixie Chicks, ? 2006
