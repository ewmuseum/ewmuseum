The Resurrection

13 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/10 Dejection. Disappointment. Rock bottom. That is exactly how Jimmy feels as he sits in his dressing room at the end of Dangerous Games. Still in his wrestling attire and not even showered an hour after the show had finished, the thought of moving from his chair is a distant one as he reminisces on his efforts during Dangerous Games. Sure, he threw some guys around like rag dolls and yes, he stood toe to toe with guys like Mayhem and the eventual winner Andy Murray without taking a backward step, which raised a lot of eyebrows and drew respectful comments from some of the guys in the back. In Jimmy's cold hard reality, tonight was another failed venture in his return to professional wrestling. After eventually showering and ready to leave, Jimmy's thoughts were disturbed by a knock at the door. He turns his head as he hears the door open and is surprised to see Emma walk in. She takes two steps and looks at Jimmy. Gone is the sparkle in his eye that she always saw. Gone is the smile on his face that he was famous for. Gone is the strong, confident husband and father that she came to expect. All that stands before her is a broken, soulless man. A shadow of his former self. A tear falls down her cheek as she knows the lifeless man standing in front of her is a product of her own selfishness. "I'm sorry." Emma says in a loud whisper. "Me too." Replied Jimmy in a similar tone. "I want to be with you, no matter what." Emma adds "No matter what." Replies Jimmy The two give a long, warm, embrace. They hold each other for a moment when Emma looks up at Jimmy and says with a smile on her face, "Your dreams are important to you and what's important to you is important to me." Jimmy smiles and holds Emma even tighter. Her words released the shackles on him that he felt were holding him down since he returned to GCW. Suddenly he can breath again, like the vice grip that he felt around his throat, was now released and the air from his mouth and nose to his lungs began to circulate freely again. The next day Jimmy walked through his front door to have his little princess, Chloe, run up to him and give him an almighty hug. He picks her up and holds her close to him. Her love filled embrace is just the tonic Jimmy needed on his return back to his ranch after being on the road the last few weeks. Chloe leads Jimmy into the kitchen and shows him the drawings she had done while in school. All he can do is sit there and marvel in her crazy art work that only a five year old can accomplish. The laughing is suddenly silent when Jimmy Junior walks into the kitchen. Both Senior and Junior stare at each other. Junior walks around the table towards his father and the two embrace. "I'm sorry, Dad." Junior said in a soft tone. "Me too." Jimmy replied, as he looks to Emma who is smiling even though her eyes begin to water. The tender moment is broken by the telephone ringing. Jimmy picks up the phone and briefly talks into it and ends the conversation. "That was Theo, he just wanted to see if I was home." He announces. "He wants to come over and talk, he said he has an idea for me." He ends with in a confused tone. After an hour or so, while Jimmy is watching a replay of Dangerous Games, in particular, his time in the ring during the Dangerous Games match. With his eyes were glued to the television, standing against the door frame is Theo. "You're missing something." Theo said which caught Jimmy by surprise because he had no idea Theo was standing. "Holy crap Theo, where did you come from?" Jimmy asked surprisingly. "Through your front door." Replied Theo in a quick and witty tone. "You look lost in that ring. You move around that ring like you need a map." He added "It's just ring rust." Jimmy said nonchalantly. "It's more than ring rust my friend." Theo announced. "You need a voice at ringside to tell you where you have to be." He says. Suddenly Jimmy realizes Theo is suggesting he'll start being at ringside with him. "Are you saying you want to be at ringside?" Jimmy asks in hope. "It's time to get the band together." Theo says as he starts to walk into the room and stands in front of Jimmy who is now leaning back into his soft lounge. Giving Theo a confused look, Jimmy asks "What do you mean it's time to get the band back together?" Theo says with a smile, "DI baby, DI." A shocked look sweeps across Jimmy's face. He has no words or questions. Theo steps in and says "I know this is a bit too much for you to take in right now, sleep on it and we'll catch up tomorrow. What have you got planned?" "I'm suppose to be visiting a terminally sick teenager for the Make A Wish Foundation at St Luke's in Boise." Jimmy replies, still in shock with Theo's proposal. "I have some planning to and calls to make. Call me when you're done and we'll discuss our next step." Theo explained as he gives Jimmy a hug. Typically, when a major moment hits Jimmy's life, a sleepless night awaits him. The thought of reuniting with Jeff Harris and Adam Masterson is filled with mixed reservations. Jimmy hasn't been in contact with both Jeff and Adam for several years now and has no idea how the chemistry will be between them all. Back in the day they were tight, that was because they traveled together for years and watched each others back. Since then, they have all gone their separate ways. The fact that they have not been in contact is testimony to Jimmy's belief that the chemistry wont be there. Just as Jimmy's eyes finally close, the piercing sound of his alarm pries his eyes open. If it were an ordinary day, Jimmy would just turn the alarm off and fall back asleep but the fact he is visiting a terminally sick teenager, for visits, Jimmy tries to spend several hours with the person making the request. As Jimmy walks into St Luke's foyer he notices a woman walking towards him with her hand stretched out to shake his hand. Thinking she is a representitivefrom the hospital, Jimmy stars to smile. "Hi Jimmy, Vicky from the GCW office said you'd make it here this morning. I'm Julie from Coke and I was hoping to have a word with you, considering you dont have a manager, about doing some commercials for Coke." Julie asked with a what Jimmy found to be a fake smile on her face. A little perplexed by her braisen introduction, Jimmy takes his hand back and with a serious tone he says, "If you and Vicky from our GCW office had have listened to GCW Live last week you would have heard our "Emperor" announce that we have now signed a sponsorship agreement with Pepsi, knowing that I will have to unfortunately say no." Jimmy smiles to be polite but walks away, frustrated that someone from the corporate world would approach him about doing a commercial, even though he's walking into a delicate situation with a terminally sick teenager. He makes a mental note that he has to speaking to Garbage Bag Johnny and get him to remind his staff to use a bit of common sense in the future. Jimmy heads to the information desk and is introduced to a representative from the hospital who leads him through the maze like hallways to a ward where the sick teenager is. Jimmy introduces himself to Daniel, the surprised teenager and to his equally surprised parents. Jimmy also takes the time to sign autographs and poses for photographs with the other sick children and the nursing staff but his entire focus is spent on Daniel who requested to meet him. Sitting down and listening to some stories from Daniel, telling Jimmy how his father took him, when he was younger, to Bloodbox events when Jimmy Thunder was the biggest draw card at Bloodbox. He even showed Jimmy a photo that Daniel took with Jimmy in the car park of the MGM Grand after a Bloodbox pay per view. Taken aback by it all, Jimmy promptly signed the photograph and asked his parents if they could send him a photograph of his visit today for him to keep. With the excitement of Jimmy visiting, Daniel feels the need to nap and try and get some of the energy that sapped from him back. In the mean time, Jimmy steps out with Daniel's parents for a bite to eat. While sitting with Daniel's parents, Jimmy listens to their stories of struggling to deal with Daniel's illness and knowing that the doctors have said that Daniel will be lucky to see the end of the year out. Jimmy Thunder, six feet nine inches, three hundred and ninety pounds of muscle. One of the strongest and most powerful men to ever to grace the squared circle of Global Championship Wrestling is now a broken down man and crying like a baby. The sadness he feels now for his new found friend is enough to bring Jimmy to heartfelt tears. Upon hearing that Daniel has now woken from his short slumber, Jimmy and Daniel's parents head back to Daniel's bedside. Jimmy had originally agreed to spend an hour with Daniel but four hours later, he was still by Daniel's side. The two had started to forge a special friendship. One in awe of the others achievements and what he stands for, the other mesmerized by the strength and courage shown while staring death in the face. After spending another hour with Daniel, Jimmy realizes he has get back to prepare to leave for World Wide this week. Jimmy stands up and says goodbye to Daniel's parents then looks down to Daniel and tells him how much he enjoyed visiting with him. "Can you do me something for me, Jimmy." Daniel asks quietly. "What is it, buddy?" Jimmy replies. "Go and win the world title." Daniel says with a smile. Jimmy smiles back as a tear falls down his cheek because he knows that it's not possible for Daniel to see Jimmy win the world title alive. Jimmy looks to Daniel's parents then back at Daniel and asks "I'll win it if you do something for me." Daniel's face lights up as he says with excitement, "I'll do anything?" "Keep cheering for me." Jimmy says as places his hand on the side of Daniel's pale cheek. "It's a deal!" Daniel announced as the excitement runs through his voice. Jimmy says his goodbyes and walks out. Daniel's father comes racing out and thanks Jimmy for everything he has done for their son. Jimmy writes his cell number on a piece of paper and hands it to Daniel's father and insists he calls and updates him on Daniel's condition. Daniel's father starts to cry as he gives Jimmy a big hug. Jimmy takes a few moments to console him before leaving. While making his way home, the strength and courage shown by Daniel was so infectious that it has now hit Jimmy. Now the whole complexion of Jimmy's return to wrestling has changed. At first, it was all about having fun and enjoying the ride. Now, for the first time in nearly five years, Jimmy is setting his sights on the biggest prize of them all. The Global Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship. Jimmy slowly walks into his living room and sees Emma sitting on the lounge watching TV. "Geez you were gone for longer than what you said you would be." Said Emma. "Yeah." Jimmy says softly. "I know." He adds. "How was your visit?" Emma asks while still watching the television "I'll tell you some other time." Jimmy replies as he takes off his jacket. "Babe, Junior and I were talking. We want to come with you to World Wide." Said Emma as she diverts her attention to Jimmy's reaction. Jimmy can't help but smile as he says, "That would be awesome!" Just at that moment he hears a car pull up outside his house. He looks and he sees it's Theo. Jimmy steps out onto his deck to meet him out there. Theo climbs out of his car and says, "Jimmy I made some calls and I..." "Don't worry about it." Jimmy says while interrupting Theo. "What do you mean, don't worry about it?" Theo asked with a confused tone. "I thought we were getting the band back together, come on!" He continues with his confused statement. "We are." Jimmy replies with a confident smile. "You and I are." He adds. With one eye brow raised and confirming what Jimmy had "Just you and me." "Everything has changed now Theo." Jimmy says "It's only going to be you and me." He says with a confident wink as Theo continues to look curious. The scene fades........
