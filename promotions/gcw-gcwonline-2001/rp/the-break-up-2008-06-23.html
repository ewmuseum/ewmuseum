The Break Up

23 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/23/08 Monday, June 23, 2008 7:50 p.m. After what has been the best week of my life up here in West Yellowstone, Montana, things have all of a sudden taken a turn for the worse. I just accidentally picked up Amber’s cellphone instead of my own (they both are the same model and color so it’s an easy mistake to make) and answered a phone call. “Hello,” I mumble in a quiet voice. “Hello Amber,” the voice on the other line replies. But just as I am about to respond to tell the person this, he starts talking. “It’s Brian. I had such a great time in Vegas with you. I loved it when you came over and made love.” At this point my eyebrows raise up toward the ceiling as he continues talking. “I’d love to continue doing this on a regular basis. Just don’t let James find out. If he finds out, we’ll both be in deep.” He pauses on the other line and then continues again. “Is he there?” I think for a moment about what to do and then cough into the phone. “Oh, ok. Just get off the line sweetie and I’ll talk to you later. I love you.” The other line goes silent and I shut off her cellphone. Now I have a dilemma that I’m not too happy about. My girlfriend has been cheating on me and we’re on vacation together. Now I must decide whether I should bring it up or let it slide until she brings it up. I decide to think it over before making a decision. * * * 9:45 p.m. “So it’s over?” I ask. She turns to me and nods her head. “Yes,” she mutters out quietly as she packs her suitcase. “It’s over, James.” This is totally blowing my mind right now. This is the happiest I’ve ever been and then all of a sudden I get sucked into a black hole of despair by this woman who I thought was the one but turned out to be a rat. “Why is it over?” “Because I’m not feeling it anymore.” “No, you’re just in denial.” She turns and gives me a weird look. “In denial of what James?” “You’re in denial that you had an affair with one of your friends and are in denial that the affair is the reason for the break up. You can say that you don’t love me all you want but the truth is you had an affair and got manipulated by someone else.” “Yes I did tell you that but I did love you but not anymore.” “So you had an affair with one of your friends instead of just breaking it off” I would’ve been fine with a break up but then you did that stupid ****ing bull**** and now we’ve come to this point.” “What point is that?” “The point of no return.” We both give each other a look at this point. She’s looking at me like I’m nuts whereas I’m looking at her like she’s a disease infested whore. “James, this is not just all my fault. You played a part in this too you know.” I put my hands on my hips. “Oh really” And what part is that? The part of the person who got cheated on?” She glares at me. “If you would’ve spent more time with me then this wouldn’t have happened.” I motion around. “WE’VE BEEN IN THE WOODS FOR MORE THAN A ****ING WEEK! WHEN THE HELL HAVE I NOT BEEN SPENDING TIME WITH YOU”!” “Back in Vegas?” “So you mean to tell me you started ****ing him BACK IN VEGAS”!” She turns away and looks down at the ground before nodding her head. “Yes,” she whispers. “WELL THAT JUST MAKES ME FEEL ****ING GREAT ABOUT THIS WHOLE SITUATION! You’ve been cheating on me for more than a week and that just makes me SOOOOO happy.” I turn around and start heading out of the bedroom door only to stop before turning back around. “Just pack up your **** and go.” I then leave her to finish packing in the bedroom as I head down the hallway and walk straight for the kitchen. I need some alcohol after this night. This has not been a good day at all. * * * Journal of James von Doom 24 June.- It took a few weeks but I finally have driven Amber away. Well, actually, no I haven’t. **** that! She left me and had an affair with one of her friends in the process. That is not the idea of what I had in mind when we came up here. This is just another incident in a long line of incidents that have left me single yet again. I am getting sick of this stupid dating ****. Every time I let someone get close to me I always end up getting hurt and this is just another case of that happening. I’m probably destined to be alone. However, I still have my wrestling career to look forward to. I have two championship belts to defend and I plan on keeping them both. That is my mission in life now. No one will stop me. No one. * * * June 24 4:45 p.m. Today has been really depressing. I’ve basically just been driving around Yellowstone National Park all day sightseeing and eating at various restaurants that are scattered throughout the park whenever I got hungry. This is the kind of solitude that I’ve been wanting to have ever since she left me and now I have it. Alone all day with only my thoughts. The heartbreak of lost love can do some strange things to a person. Some people get depressed and suicidal. Other people stop thinking about it and move on with their lives. However, I on the other hand have had a strange reaction to this event. The anger about what has happened has made me motivated to win now. The anger over what has happened has turned into motivation to take it out on my opponents and that is not a good thing for either of them. As I look out into the wilderness, the rage overcomes me. I know what I have to do now. I have to stop worrying about the past and start worrying about the present. I have to worry about my title defenses. I must win. And no one will stop me from doing so. Nobody. * * * June 25 6:30 p.m. I have been having a really weird week and this is just another incident that has happened in it. First the Church of the Unholy breaks up officially and then Amber leaves me after having an affair with one of her friends. But hopefully my luck is not going to get any worse. What has transpired has motivated me to be the best there is in the world at the TEAM Super Show. Instead of crying and moping around, I’ve been training? Training a lot. Ever since she left all I’ve done is work out and go on jogs or walks. I’ve also had a lot of alone time to get myself in the zone for my matches. That is not a good thing for my opponents. I’m going to be ready. And they won’t know what hit them? * * * 7:45 p.m. “Frankie Scott and Larry Tact.” The feed starts coming in on my scheduled TEAM interview session for the Super Show. I am wearing a white “Ghostbusters” T-shirt along with some black basketball shorts I begin talking. “Me and Otaku are the Lethal Lottery Tag Team Champions of the world here in TEAM. Now those titles have a lot of prestige to them. A prestige that needs to be upheld with the highest regard by the holders of those championships.” “As being one of those title holders, I need to keep two guys who hate each other from winning and destroying the elite nature of these championships. If Scott and Tact win these belts, they will kill each other as the champions.” “So, in order to prevent this from happening, me and Otaku have a plan. We are going to win this match hands down and prevent that from going down.” “Tact, Scott, you two guys can bicker and fight amongst yourselves all you want but there is no way in hell you guys are winning.” “Just come out to the ring and surrender before you two kill each other in the middle of the ring. That would not be good for either of you guys or TEAM as an organization. Two dead wrestlers in the center of the ring would not be good at all.” “So this is what I’m proposing.” “You two guys can stay at home and just not show up so that you can spare your lives from the inevitable double homicide that we all know is coming. So stay home, don’t die.” “You’ll both be doing yourselves a favor.” I smile at the camera. “It’ll be for the greater good. Trust me. And you’ll both be alive at the end of the day so everybody walks away happy.” “So think about it. Think about it real long before you two make a decision.” “Trust me. Your lives will depend on it.” I then shut off the camera and the feed fades to black. * * * Journal of James von Doom 25 June.- With the distractions over with, now I have had plenty of time to devote to my title defenses. Everyone in the wrestling world is thinking that my wins were flukes. They’d better think again because this is not a one show fluke. This is the start of some big things for me. People are going to start fearing me like they should’ve done years ago but were too busy making jokes at my expense to notice. This is going to be my revenge and their worst nightmare. My plan is to take on any and all challengers with both titles and hold both belts for as long as I possibly can. That is my goal and I plan on accomplishing it. No one can hold me back from my destiny. I am on a mission to become the best in the world and no one is going to hold me back anymore. No one can hold me back. No one. * * * 9:20 p.m. The feed comes back on and I smile after watching Larry Tact running his mouth earlier in the day on the television so I feel it is only necessary that I respond to him in kind. "Tact, you seem to think you are the **** but you're not from the way you're running your mouth. Just because you weren't in either title match doesn't matter. The same thing would've happened, a big loss for you and a big win for me." "Me not handling the pressure? Well, reality check Tact. I will come into Super Show IX with two titles and I will leave Super Show IX with the same two titles I came in with. You should be more worried about your upcoming match with your partner with the Loser Leaves TEAM for 5 matches stipulation than worrying about winning any gold in this match because it's not going to happen." "You may be confident but you don't have the heart, the inner guts in order to pull this out. See you're all about the ego, I'm not. I have the eye of the tiger so to speak. You're only worried about gaining even more reputation. I could care less about my reputation. All I know is that I am going to be keeping both my championships on this Super Show." "You're a seasoned veteran, yes, but so am I. Six years of surviving the wrestling wars will do that to you. So I know what it takes to survive and win in big match situations. I've been doing it for years and will continue to do it until the day I retire from wrestling for good." "You can show me no respect whatsoever, that's fine. The only problem is that kind of attitude will come back and bite you in the end when you and you're little buddy lose this match. See me and Otaku are on the same page whereas you and Scott are not. That's the difference. You seem to have forgotten that during your little rant or temper tantrum or whatever that was." "I will not surrender, Tact. I repeat I WILL NOT SURRENDER. I will fight until the bitter end and then I will leave you with a stunned look on your face after having your shoulders pinned to the mat by someone who 'can't handle' the pressure in your mind." "You also seem to forget that you are not the only one with motivation to win this match. I have YEARS of motivation to stick it to all the people who said I would never amount to anything in this sport. That is my motivation. I don't need a lecture from you about how motivated you are. I could care less. The bottom line is there will be two winners and two losers in this match." "I don't plan on losing. However, you should." "Words are cheap unless you can back it up so invite you to try and back up your words. Because, if you don't, you will lose some of that prestige that you are so proud of." "In fact, just quit talking. Let's get this fight over with. I'm ready for it." The feed fades out.
