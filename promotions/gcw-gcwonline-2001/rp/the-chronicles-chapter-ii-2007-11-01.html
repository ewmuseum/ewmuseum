The Chronicles: Chapter II

1 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 1st, 2007 Chapter 2: Pegasus's Pain before Devil's Night. Edmonton, Canada Nov. 1st, 2007 Pegasus is leaning on the side of the pier. Pegasus is wearing a all black suit with thin white stripes, black shoes and a platinum watch. Pegasus is wearing a pair of black shaded sunglasses. Pegasus is standing on the pier with his hands cuffed in his pocket and then he pulls his left hand from out his pocket to wipe his left eye. A light snow shower starts to twinkle down. Pegasus is focus towards the reflection in the water. Another figure appears to be in the water and it happens to be Interviewer Kevin Jacobs. Jacobs pats Pegasus on his back. Jacobs nods his head and wonders what’s happening with Pegasus. Pegasus takes a deep breath and a slow swallow. Jacobs pulls out a old looking stop watch and it reads 12:20 AM. Jacobs: You alright kid? Pegasus: Yeah Kev, I’m fine Jacobs: You know you got Branson King & The Nashvillain & Elijah Keeling this week, right? Pegasus: Yeah I pretty much found out. I’m just gonna destroy them and that’s that. Jacobs: Great to hear you talk like that. Keep a positive mind and you can get through anything. Pegasus: Yes but of course? Jacobs:: Umm Robin, I see that you’re career is jumping pretty high’and I don’t think you need m- Pegasus looks at Kev and nods his head. Pegasus: Kevin don’t be a fool, you are a good advice giver and nothing will change that. I’m feeling pain because I lost a friend. It hurts when you loose someone special. I looked up to this man, this man showed me the way of American Mat Wrestling. Just never thought it would end so quick. Sunday it’s my Tribute to my best friend Cory Chevelle. Its me, Dorian, and Clyde verse Branson King, Nash, and Elijah. they're is not really my problem, my problem is focusing on the match. If I make one false move I just might lose that match. [laugh] for a man who battles demons, fight bigger opponents, and still is hated and loved by many. I feel like one lucky son of a *****. I actually knew Cory Chevelle, I’m pretty sure Keeling didn’t know the guy. Keeling hasn’t been around long enough. a lot of people think my intentions is going in the ring and destroy Elijah and his crew. But no my intentions are to defeat them all for Chevelle and I know their goal is to do the same. Brandon is one lucky bastard to have so many friends. I have you Michelle, and you Kev’and quite frankly that’s all I damn well need. Jacobs: thank you for your words of kindness Pegasus. But right now i need you to be the beast we all know. Because if you moan now, yo will defiantly lose to Team King. You will be Television Champion and I feel it coming very soon. Pegasus: Yeah Kev maybe you’re right’but right now I don’t care about the TV Championship. Jacobs shockingly looks at Pegasus? Jacobs: What did you just say!” Pegasus: You heard me correctly Kev! Jacobs: Wow you don't want to go down in history with people such as Tyson Debough, Jay Outlaw, Shaman, and Dan Rivero? Pegasus: Kev, I came out here to pay tribute to my friend Cory and you come out here to talk business. Life isn’t always about the wrestling business. These men have other lives and my friend Cory lost his, so please give me my hour alone. Jacobs: pathetic! Look at you! Pegasus nods his head and looks down. Jacobs: Where’s the Edge!!” Pegasus: OK! You want Edge Kev!” Jacobs: Yes I want Edge!! Pegasus: Here’s a message for Brandon King’King Sunday isn’t just about honoring Chevelle but it’s also about me kicking your ass. When you face hit the cold mat and your body shuts down thanks to my swift maneuvers I will declare myself king of the ring! This was about me fighting for Chevelle but Kevin and the fans wants Edge! So you’re going to get the worst beating you’ve ever received in a wrestling match. We can take this fight to the highs and to the lows. I wont stop until one of us is lifeless. Bring Elijah, Bring Nash, Bring God! I don’t care! If anyone steps in my way its curtains! that’s a promise! Jacobs: nice! Also Nash says you’re nothing more than a cute little exhibition match, they put him against someone he can dominate for the tribute. Is that what this is? Pegasus: Nashvillian is overrated! He talks about his bitchy life. Where’s the fresh new shit!” Oh yeah he ran out of them. We came to see athletes fight and not actors rant on about there friends. He’ll mention his opponent here and there but he usually talks about his “friend”. I haven’t met a guy that loves his friends more than his career. You’ll only find that in gay porn. Jacobs: Yeah I know what you mean’About your opponents of course! Pegasus: [laugh] I know what you meant. Jacobs: I’m gonna sit in the car ok..” Pegasus: Yeah sure. Just don’t play with the radio Kev. Jacobs: I wanna hear smooth jazz? Pegasus: Alright go ahead. Kevin Jacobs walks over to Pegasus’s New 2007 Black Escalade. The Truck is sitting on 24’s and the Truck has gold and platinum trimmings. Pegasus leans forward towards the lake. He sees Some sort of reflection that only appears to be in the water. Pegasus looks around to see if anyone is near. The figure starts to give him a winter chill in his chest.[/i] Pegasus: Cory’I know you can hear me, I want to say thank you for everything. I mean you put your body on the line every week for the fans and in return you get to visit Jesus. Man, I almost cried when I heard of your parish. But I know I’m stronger than that. I had a feeling if I didn’t win the rumble, you would but I guess I have to win it. Sunday is my supercard debut. There’s no limits to what I’m gonna do to them. Hell I might even F-5 him [small chuckle] but yes we will all miss you and believe me you are not forgotten. Its just your backstage presents isn’t with us anymore but everyone will get through this like every other great death’people leave back a legacy. Pegasus wipes his last tear from his eyes and head towards his car. Pegasus gets in his car. Jacobs: you done? Pegasus: Afraid so Jacobs: Good lets go to that little Chinese Restaurant down the block. Pegasus: Lets Roll.. Pegasus puts the gear in drive. Pegasus is driving through the streets of Edmonton. The Car stops at the restaurant Chu Shi Xiao. Pegasus Kevin Jacobs walks out of the car and into the restaurant. A small Asian male escorts them to there seats. Jacobs: I know you guys are going to win the match against Keeling, Nash, and King. I just have the gut feeling. You know what I mean? Pegasus: Yeah, Nash is going to get planted. Sometimes its better not to talk about people like Nash and is better to talk about people like you Kev. Jacobs: what about me? Pegasus: Basically you are a hell of an interviewer, plus a good friend, thanks for being here for me man. Jacobs: Yes but of course. Where's Michelle? Pegasus: She's in Boston. Waiting for the GCW crew. Jacobs: Oh great, also i see there's no one in your way of getting the TV Title. Pegasus: Actually you still got that punk kid Phil atkins looking for a small title run which he’s not going to get from this point on. Brandon King is a hell of a challenge but as a number one contender, this fight will be war. Jacobs: I Totally agree with you on that one. But he thinks you have no chance of beating him. I find that very insulting. Pegasus: He doesn’t know where his foot is stepping in. Team Pegasus and Team King are going to give the fans a great match. Because that’s how Cory would want me to do it. Jacobs: Spoken like a true Champion Pegasus: Yes indeed. Jacobs: Do you think you can out wrestle them. Pegasus: Out wrestle? Jacobs: Sure, I know you can do it! Pegasus: Well i'm glad i have dorian ryan, and clyde foxx on my squad this week. So i can expect good things from them. Jacobs: I know The waiter comes over to the table. Waiter: What can I get you guys? Jacobs: Umm I’ll have the shrimp and broccoli with the white rice. Waiter: O’ok umm and for you sir? Pegasus: The Television Championship? Waiter: Television Championship? Pegasus: Yeah Waiter: Sorry but that’s not on the menu Pegasus: but it will be on my waist. The Screen comes to a closing with Jacobs, Pegasus and the Waiter in the restaurant.
