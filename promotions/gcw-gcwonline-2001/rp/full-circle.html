Date: 4 Setpember 06 “Have you seen him since?” “He’s been a ghost ? a lot like the last six years.” Life can take some strange and unexpected turns. That’s a line that I have used over and over again. As clich?” as it may sound is it not the truth? A few years ago I would have probably laughed and then drove a knife into the chest of someone who told me that I’d be sitting across from Alex Grey, drinking a beer and having civilized conversation. If any two people had put each other through hell it was us. Yet now with the exception of Mary he is all I have left. Irony at it’s best. “RaZ fucked that boy’s mind up beyond belief. I really don’t know what he told him or where he kept him but it couldn’t have been good.” I said in between sips of Corona. “Just be careful Roberto, who knows when they will come back.” I just nodded and finished off my third beer of the night and immediately ordered another round. It had been a long time since I had gone out. Mary had stopped following me on the road a few weeks back so when I was home she needed me there with her. I was more than happy to be there for her and our unborn baby. The very thought of Mary and our baby brought a quick smile to my face. “Thinking of Mary?” Alex asked after seeing my smirk. “As always amigo.” “You’re lucky to have found someone. Mary, while very ? different, for lack of a better word, is loyal and loves you. Life is good for you.” That’s what worried me the most sadly. Life had never been great to me for long periods of time. Just when things seem like they are clearing up for good something gets tossed in and rips that happiness apart. Now life was at it’s peak. I had a beautiful fiance?, a baby on the way, and of course there is GCW. Or what’s left of GCW. “Hopefully it lasts.” A few minutes and the rest of a beer later I said my goodbye to Alex and left the small bar. Then again aren’t they all small in a place like Oregon? The hotel room that I was staying at was only a few blocks away so I decided to walk. Figured I could use the quiet of the night to actually think. Something else that I hadn’t gotten a chance to do lately. So much had been going on that parts of my life seemed to be going on autopilot. More specifically my professional career. GCW Or ? What’s left of GCW. Sure the place still makes its money and has continued to attract talent but something isn’t quite the same anymore. Sure the World Title looks great around my waist but it goes far deeper than that. GCW has lost its identity. It has the same name, the same owner and the same World Champion as before but little else is the same. I have felt so out of place since for so long now that I even forget when it began. Groups like the Knightmare Brigade and Dies Irae have come in and tried to tear down what a lot of hard working people fought to build. Hot shots like Brandon Youngblood bring their arrogance, newbies like Ciel and La Sombra are here for someone else. The problem? No one is here for GCW. We have a group that are doing everything in their power to show that GCW needs to be put to sleep like a dying dog but where are the ones fighting back? The Ascension? I have my doubts on the capability of this group. Separately they are talented and perhaps even in a small group they can do damage but they are missing something big. What they are missing is the appreciation of what they now represent. To them GCW is simply a place to come wrestle and earn a paycheck. To these people who haven’t been around long they only fight against GCW’s enemy because someone with a cute face and a curvy figure has batted their eyes and flirted just enough. The problem with that is there is no passion, no fire, no drive. In other words: GCW will fall. GCW is more than a wrestling promotion, more than three letters, and much more than a place that I earn a paycheck. GCW is my very essence, it is what I AM. I came to GCW back in 2001 as Khris “The Legacy” Krowe. A Cocky, arrogant, and masked man wanting to take on the world. I don’t know how many times I challenged then world champion, The Jhub to a non-title match just because I figured it would boost my career. I was determined to be heard. I didn’t quite understand the game but at the same time I didn’t care to listen to others. It wasn’t until months later when after careful consideration that I knew something had to change. Before a PPV I decided that I would ask to lose the mask and change my name. Steven Caldera loved the talent and was willing to work with me. I chose a name that I felt was powerful, dark, and when you heard it you’d know you were in trouble. I became LeStatt Knight. The PPV? Dangerous Games. Really throughout my career in GCW Dangerous Games has been a proving ground of mine. In that first attempt I battled into the final ten before being dropped. A few years later as GCW was trying to resurrect itself under the moniker XW I lasted until the final four ? and it is my belief that it was then that Caldera really took notice. It was then that the idea of how good I could be really sunk in. After XW went down I went other places and honed my skills and got to know myself but GCW will always be known as the birth place of LeStatt Knight. It was for this reason that about a year and a half ago I came back after a call from Caldera ? a man I never thought I’d hear from again. GCW was reopening and he wanted LeStatt Knight. My career and my life had come full circle. I wouldn’t fail this time ? I WAS going to make an impact. That I did. Dangerous Games 2005 Just like before it ended up being a huge night for me. You could call it a rebirth of LeStatt and an eye opener to the rest of the world. I toppled Jason Dread and became the World Champion. I earned the glory that I had always felt I deserved and it was a great feeling. GCW had never seen a man do the things I was doing and they loved to hate me ? I was doing my job wonderfully. GCW was at incredible heights I was at incredible heights I was proud to be GCW. So what happened? When exactly did GCW stop being a cohesive unit and become the battle field that it is now? Are Dies Irae REALLY to blame for this whole thing? Is that even the real question people should be asking? Actually no it isn’t. The question should be where will GCW’s savior come from? This Ascension has a lot to learn. As hard as Tessa works and as talented as some of it’s members may be, they just don’t understand. They go after Dies Irae just because they were asked or MAYBE they have a personal vendetta against one of more of the members. It has to be more than that. It cannot be about impressing Caldera or a woman. It can’t be about moving up the ladder for a title shot and it can’t be about getting back at one particular person. It has to be about defending GCW. But will they ever understand that? How could they? Well as good as I am I cannot perform miracles. What I can do is go out at Dangerous Games and bury Dawn McFarlane once again. I can show what GC-fucking-W is all about. Perhaps even more importantly I can stop being just a fly on the wall. Someone needs to step up and MAKE them understand. Perhaps that someone is LeStatt Knight The man that the GCW fans hated just a few months ago. The man they LOVED to hate. The MAN that bleeds GCW. GCW’s Savior ? Maybe. Irony at it’s best indeed.
