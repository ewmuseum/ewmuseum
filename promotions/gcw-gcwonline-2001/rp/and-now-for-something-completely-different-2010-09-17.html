And Now for Something Completely Different

17 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/17/10 If you were looking for Chris Storm, you would be best to search Hershel’s. If you were searching for Johnny the Kid, you would be best to search where Chris was. Scott Drake, the man known to the world as Chris Storm, was getting ready for the start of a new day. That meant he was down at his favorite gym. With him as he has been for the past four months, was Johnny the Kid. Scott was already in the ring doing sit-ups as Johnny came in with a brown bag. Scott wouldn’t stop his warm-ups until Johnny called for him. “Breakfast’s here.” Pulling himself up, Scott exited though the ropes and sat down on the apron. His protégé joined him, reaching into the bag and handing Scott his food. “That’s nasty.” Scott looked innocently at his food, two power bars sandwiching a meal-replacement bar. “Good protein to start a day” Scott said as he took a large bite. Johnny just shook his head and helped himself to his plain bagel. He would have loved to have some real food, like some sausages or pancakes, but Scott had him on a strict diet. “Good way to be shitting early in the morning.” Laughing, Scott patting his own stomach, “good way to keep the weight off then.” As much as Johnny would have loved to continue to discuss Scott’s morning bowel movements, he had more pressing issues on his mind. “Hey, how come I haven’t had a match yet in GCW?” It was something that had been bugging him for a while. While he certainly was still getting use to being in a major wrestling promotion, it always disappointed him when he never saw his name on the cards posted backstage. Seeing the look Scott was giving him, though, tipped Johnny off to the fact that he needed to try and save it before Scott had him doing one-hundred push-ups. “Not that I’m complaining, because I’m not. I’m just wondering, cause you know, Wall is already in the main event and I haven’t even had a match yet.” Ah snap, he just made it worst. “You want Knight to train you?” “No.” Johnny’s answer hadn’t had the convection Scott was looking for. “I can call him, see if he’ll take on another student. We use to tag up for like a week. Hell he might due it cause of how much he wants to bang Tessa.” “Look, I’m sorry, I’ve just been annoyed because it feels like I’ll never get a chance to use all this training.” It was Johnny’s best attempt at sucking up. It wasn’t going to work right now. Scott was upset, it was visible by the look on his face. “That rook is backed by Knight. I think you’ve watched enough GCW to know that Caldera has to wear knee pads when he’s in the same room as him. I’m not the least bit surprised they’re already giving him a shot.” Scott turned and looked him dead in the eye. “I don’t know what Knight’s been teaching that boy, but he’s baptizing him with fire and I promise if I get him in the ring with him, I’m going to fucking incinerate his ass.” Johnny sat quietly on the ring apron and took small bites from his bagel. The last thing he wanted to do was upset Scott, but that’s exactly what had happened. He tried to change the subject, something to ease the situation, even if only for a short while. “To be honest, if I finally got on national television I would probably vomit all over the place. I would just be so nervous.” His comment, which was meant to soften things between the two, only served to make Scott look at him as if he had just grown a second head. “Why in the blue hell would you be nervous?” Johnny shrugged his shoulders. “I dunno, never been on television before. Wouldn’t want to mess up.” Scott hopped down from the apron and walked in front of Johnny to look him over. “When you step through those curtains your ass better be confident. I don’t want no bullshit nerves. I’ve trained you too hard for that. When you do get your match, you’ll be better prepared than whoever you face.” Scott slowed for only a moment, long enough to wipe his mouth. His anger had caused him to spit out his words with a fury. “No one works as hard as we do in this business. If you’re nervous than I’ve failed in my job.” He pointed at Johnny dead in the chest. “Know how I was before my first match?” “I’m going to guess ‘not throwing up’?” Scott nodded his head. “Damn straight I wasn’t throwing up. I was pumped, ready to smash some fucking skulls.” Scott paused for a moment, remembering something. “Granted, that might have been because I had just did an ounce of coke at the time. I was fucking wasted during that match. I can’t believe they even let me compete.” “Regardless” he said as he waved his hand in the air, dismissing his last statement, “do you know how I was for my first title match?” “Again I’ll go with ‘not throwing up’?” “And you would be correct again. “Was you high on coke again?” Johnny tossed out there. Scott had to stop, thinking back. “Nah, well, yeah. I mean I wasn’t on coke. That was later. I don’t remember what I took but I know I was tripping hard. Boy they fired my ass like a week later.” He let out a laugh despite himself, remembering back to his younger days. Johnny just couldn’t believe the words. “Do you know what I was doing before my first World title match?” It hadn’t take Johnny long to answer the question. “You were not throwing up and high on drugs?” Scott shook his head. “Nah, I was with Tessa back then, she would have killed me if I was doing that shit. Though, if I remember correctly, I probably had about half a bottle of Southern Comfort on my stomach.” “You don’t suppose all those drugs and alcohol is what kept you from being nervous, huh?” Again Johnny had two heads to Scott. “The hell you talking about, I just liked drugs back then, a lot. I mean I was retarded with that shit. But nah, I knew I was the best. I was confident, cocky even. I had no reason to be nervous. Hell the poor bastard across the ring from me should have been nervous. He should have been pissing in his fucking pants with the amount of ass-whooping I was going to put on him.” Johnny laughed, in part because he found Scott’s rant unintentionally hilarious and in part because he was relieved to see that Scott had already put his previous comments aside. Finishing his rant, Scott hopped back onto the apron and resumed his protein sandwich. “Scott?” He turned and looked at Johnny. “Have you ever been threatened, by like a fan and stuff?” Finishing the bite in his mouth, Scott let out a sigh. “The fuck is this, twenty questions?” Johnny patted his dangling feet together. “I dunno, just been thinking about it. I mean I might not be right now, but soon I’ll be on TV. And I’ll hopefully have some fans, and well, I dunno. Just been thinking about it.” “You think of some weird shit.” “That still doesn’t answer the question, have you?” Scott had to put down his sandwich for that one. He leaned himself back against the ropes and allowed his head to tilt back. “Yeah, I’ve had a stalker.” Johnny’s jaw dropped and his eyes bugged. Sure he had asked the question, but you never expected to hear an answer like that. Scott tried to make him feel better. “But hell, everyone has stalkers in this business.” Scott really sucked at making people feel better. “It’s not always serious. Vivica gets emo fucks stalking her, she can’t go to Hot Topic without like five of them fucks following her around. Murray gets stalkers, their like Euro stalkers and shit. Hell even Shaman gets stalked by people who think he’s the Indian from the Village People.” Johnny let out his breath. “Ah ok, so it’s not like any kind of ‘life or death’ stuff.” “Hell yeah I have, I’ve had a gun shove a gun into my chest. Fucker was crazy as batshit, was totally going to blow me away.” Scott really, really sucked at making people feel better. He could tell he was blowing it by the fact that Johnny’s feet were tapping against each other much more rapid than before. He cleared his throat loudly to draw attention back on himself. “Not to worry though, I was a rare case. It’s not normally that serious. I have one of the hottest wives in professional wrestling, and my personality doesn’t exactly lend itself to others.” Johnny scoffed at that one. “Ok, so I’m an ass.” Johnny nodded in agreement when Scott corrected himself. “And I have a feeling you won’t have to worry about either of those effecting you.” The Kid chuckled, knowing that Scott most likely meant it. That was the thing about Scott, he really was an ass. Finishing off his protein sandwich, Scott rolled under the apron and back into the ring. He dusted his hands of the crumbles and motioned for Johnny to follow. “Come on kid, no more talking. Finish your breakfast and let’s get to work. I got places to be.” Johnny’s mood dampened instantly. He shoved the remaining bagel into his mouth and followed suit into the ring. He popped up to his feet, swallowing the food. “Europe again?” Scott nodded, as pointless as it was. They both knew what it was. It didn’t mean Johnny had to like it, but whether he did or not wasn’t going to change anything. “Aren’t you supposed to be training me? You’ve been doing that a lot less ever since you joined that place.” “We’re working out right now, aren’t we?” Scott got into his starting stance, crouching down low to the canvas. Johnny followed suit. “Yeah, but I can tell you’re in a hurry. And who knows when you’ll be back.” “I’ll be back in time for WorldWide, no worries. And after the show we’ll have another training session.” Johnny rolled his eyes. “I’m sure your wife would love to hear that.” It was too much talking for Scott. He shot in and easily took the distracted Johnny down with a Double Leg Takedown. Slapping him across the head, he pulled himself up and stood back. “Look, I may be teaching you, but let’s not forget that I’m a professional wrestler first. I still have to do what’s best for me professionally. I don’t owe you shit.” Johnny dropped his head in shame. Scott actually had a point there. He wasn’t even paying for the training and Scott had gotten him a job with GCW, even if it hadn’t cultivated in a match yet. It was still better than others and much better than his old indy bookings. While Johnny was in thought about what Scott had told him, the Rock Superstar took the opportunity to shoot in with another Double Leg Takedown on him. Johnny’s head bounced off the canvas hard. He could hear Scott laughing over him. Scott was, after all, an ass.
