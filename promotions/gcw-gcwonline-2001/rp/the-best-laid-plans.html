The best laid plans

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Day after WorldWide I sat up with a groan, as usual everything ached. Cindy mumbled in her sleep next to me and I quietly slipped out of bed making my way to the bathroom. After I was done, I made my way into the main room of my hotel room and squinted. The sun was shining in just right to catch the faceplate of the TV title. I moved towards the ten pounds of leather and gold that I had tossed on the couch upon returing to the hotel last night. I picked up the belt and looked at it with a bit of disbeleif. Not even a month into my GCW contract and I already had gold. Some may have argued it was a third tier title, but it was a title dammit. It was MY title. Eva put up a good fight, I'll give her that, but it wasn't good enough. Then again Johnathan Borealis hitting on her in the middle of the match didn't hurt either. I could have beaten Eva on my own, but I'm not gonna look a gift horse in the mouth. I had the title and that was all that mattered. At least for right now. There was still the deal with the Maraduer. This guy was seriously starting to piss me off. I carefully placed the belt on top of my equipment bag and made my way to the kitchenette and grabbed an energy drink from the fridge and walked over to the windows and looked out at the sunrise. I don't know why the Marauder chose me to pick on, but he made a serious mistake in doing so. And he had the balls to rain on my parade just when I was all set to teach little Eva a lesson. It's not like I was gonna hit her again...much. Just a little bitch slap to let her know she had been outclassed. But then again that should have been obvious from the second we stepped into the ring together. As for the Maraduer, well his day would come, it was just a matter of time. As the old saying goes, every dog has his day, and that masked freak would get his. The more important point was I had delivered. I said I was out to take the GCW by storm and by God that's what I did. I took the Marauder to the limit at Annihlation, and we nearly killed each other, and then just four days later I unseated Eva VanLorne as the TV champion. All in all things were working out even better than I expected. People may have hated me, but that was okay, I never said I was in this to glad hand the fans and be everybody's friend. I came to GCW to beat people up and win titles, and I was living up to the hype. People may have hated me, but they couldn't deny I was doing just what I said I was gonna do. And doing it well. I heard a shuffling behind me. 'Mike? What are you doing up?' Cindy asked, voice heavy with sleep. 'I had to go to the bathroom, and then I caught sight of that.' I said turing and pointing to the title belt. She shuffled over to it, and picked it up, brushing her hair out of her face. I could see a smile forming on her face. 'Feels good to have gold again, doesn't it?' she asked putting the belt back down and making her way over to me, she stood on her tip toes to kiss me. 'Yeah,' I said hugging her, 'It feels damn good to hold a title again. Although the Marauder had to rain on my victory parade.' 'Are you still worried about him?' she asked, 'I know he beat you up a little after the match, but he can't change the fact that you beat Eva.' 'I know, and I've already got an idea of how to handle him.' 'Do tell.' I told. 'I like.' 'You think Caldera will go for it?' 'Well that's really not up to him is it?' she asked, looking up at me, and I could see the glint in her eye that she used to have when she was just my manager back in the IWO days. The same glint she had when we were planning on taking out someone. The same glint she had in her eye when either myself or Damange, Inc had the advantage. I smiled back, 'No, it's not is it. Matter of fact when I get done, the Maruader or Johnny Lexicon or whoever is under that mask, will be begging Caldera agree to my terms.' She led me back to the bedroom, and we slipped back into bed, but at this point sleeping was the last thing on our minds.
