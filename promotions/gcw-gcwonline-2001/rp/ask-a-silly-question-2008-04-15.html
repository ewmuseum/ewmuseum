Ask A Silly Question.....

15 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/04/08 BANG! BANG! BANG! Blake Scott quickly opens his eyes. BANG! BANG BANG! He immediately sits up in his bed and wonders what that banging is. He turns to look at his clock and it says 4pm. "Who in the hell makes this kind of noise at FOUR FUCKING O'CLOCK IN THE AFTERNOON!!!" Blake says as he takes the blankets off himself and puts his foot in a lump of shit that his dog did while Blake slept. "God damn fucking dog!" Blake yells out as he throws a shoe at him. "You do that outside Max! Do you understand OUT-FUCKING-SIDE!!!" He continues to yell as the dog who runs through his little hole in the back door. Blake begins to hop to his bath tub, cursing at the same time, where he washes off the dog shit on his foot. He then gets his robe and looks outside as to what the banging was that woke him up. He sees some workmen leaving his neighbours house and instantly tries to run outside to catch them only to watch them drive off. Although he was pretty frustrated with not being able to catch the workmen, Blake's need to satisfy his appetite quickly became his main focus. He quickly rushes back inside and gets ready to head to his favourite caf? for one of his favourite burgers for breakfast/pre-dinner feed. Once inside the caf?, Blake notices that the place is jam packed with old age pensioners. Feeling frustrated again, Blake manages to find himself one spare seat in an area filled with these pensioners. Taking the only available seat that is within hearing distance of an elderly couple, Blake gives them a fake polite smile as he sits down. While reading the menu, Blake manages to hear the little old man say to his wife, "Remember the first time I met you over 50 years ago? We left this cafe, went round the corner behind the gas works, and I gave you one from behind." "Why, yes, I remember it well, dear," replies the little old lady with a grin. "Well, for old time's sake, let's go there again. and I'll give you one from behind." The two pensioners pay their bill and leave the cafe. Blake sitting near them, overhears the conversation and smiles to himself, thinking it would be quite amusing to see two old pensioners at it. He gets up and follows them. Sure enough, he sees the two pensioners near the gas works. The little old lady pulls off her knickers and lifts up her dress. The old man pulls down his pants and grabs the lady's hips, and the little old lady reaches for the fence. Well, what follows is 40 minutes of the most athletic sex Blake has ever seen. The little old man is banging away at the little old woman at a pace that can only be described as phenomenal. Limbs are flying everywhere, the movement is a blur, and they do not stop for a single second. Finally, they collapse and don't move for an eternity. Well, Blake is stunned. Never in his life has he ever seen anything that equates to this -- not in the movies, not from his friends, not from his own experiences. Reflecting on what he has just seen, he says to himself, "I have to know his secret. If only I could shag like that now, let alone in 50 years' time!" The two old pensioners have by this time recovered and dressed themselves. Plucking up courage, Blake approaches the pensioner. He says, "Sir, in all my life I have never seen anybody shag like that, particularly at your age. What's your secret? Could you shag like that 50 years ago?" The pensioner replies, "Son, 50 years ago, that fucking fence wasn't electrified." Once Blake realised what had happened, he walked back inside the caf? feeling like a total idiot, he sits back down and ponders on what he had just experienced, Blake orders his food and tries the block the entire experience from his head as the scene fades.
