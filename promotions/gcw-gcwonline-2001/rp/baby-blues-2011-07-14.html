Baby Blues

14 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/14/11 “Well, what does it say?” A nervous Jimmy Junior asked his girlfriend Amanda. Amanda looks up at Jimmy Junior in shock and says, “There’s two blue lines.” “Are you serious?” asked Jimmy Junior as he snatches the pregnancy test kit from Amanda’s hands. He looks at it to confirm Amanda’s conclusion. He slowly lifts his head and looks at Amanda with fear in his eyes and says, “my dad is going to kill me.” Later that day, both Jimmy Junior and Amanda walk into Jimmy’s house to find Emma sitting down at the kitchen table, drinking a hot cup of coffee and flicking through a magazine. Surprised to see Jimmy Junior there, she smiles as she stands up and walks over to give him a hug. “What are you guys doing here?” She asks. “Aren’t you guys suppose to be in school still?” “We’ve got no classes this afternoon so we thought we’d come home early for the weekend.” Replied Jimmy Junior. He takes a seat at the kitchen table with Amanda. “How have you been?” Jimmy Junior asked Emma. “Your dad took me and your sister out to dinner and I’ve been feeling sick ever since. I woke this morning and threw up and my stomach has been turning all morning. I‘ve got a Doctor‘s appointment tomorrow morning. I just hope it doesnt get worse between now and then.” Replied Emma. “Remind me to never go that restaurant.“ Jimmy Junior said with nervous tone in his voice. Jimmy Junior‘s facial expressions and demeanor is sending alerts to Emma that something is not right with Jimmy Junior and Amanda. “What’s going on guys?” She asks with a concern tone. “Where’s dad?” Jimmy Junior asks. “He’s gone to his final physical therapy session before he flies out to Columbus Ohio.” Replied Emma. “Yeah, I read he’s got a shot at the HDTV Title. How has his been feeling lately?” Jimmy Junior asked cautiously. “He’s really put himself under a lot of pressure since the card was released. What’s with all the questions? Is something wrong Junior?” Asked Emma. Junior turns and looks at Amanda, looks down briefly then looks at Emma and says, “Well, ever since my mom died, you’ve been the closest thing to a mom I’ve had and I feel comfortable telling you this.” He pauses for a moment as he takes a deep breath. “You’re the first person that will know but we just found out that Amanda is pregnant.” Jimmy Junior confesses. Emma closes her eyes as she falls back in her seat. She opens them again and looks at Jimmy Junior and says, “Your father is going to freak out when he finds out, you know this right?” “Yeah, I figured as much.” Replied Jimmy Junior. “He’s got such high hopes for you and he’s going to see this as holding you back.” Said Emma. Jimmy Junior just shrugs his shoulders and says, “What’s done is done now, we’re just going to have to deal with it all.” Emma stands up and walks over to Jimmy Junior who stands up as well. “I’ve been with your dad since you were a little boy, you know I consider you to be my son and I love you just like I love your little sister Chloe.” Emma says as her eyes start to well up with tears. “Despite what your father says, I’m on your side and I’ll be by your side no matter what.” Emma says before hugging Jimmy Junior and Amanda. “Thanks Emma, I love you.” Said, Jimmy Junior. “I love you too son. I love you both.” Replied Emma. They all sit down again when Jimmy Junior says, “While dad’s gone, we’re going to go and tell Amanda’s parents. “How will they take it?” Asked Emma. “They should be great, I really get on with them both so it should be a piece of cake. Replied Jimmy Junior. “A piece of cake?” Emma responds as she sits back in her chair and raises her eyebrow. “You’re very much like your father and I just got the same nervous goose bumps from you as I do when he says to me that things are a piece of cake.” She adds. “How about you wait and go there with your father?” “It’ll be fine, it’s dad’s reaction that worries me the most.” Said Jimmy Junior. Emma gives Jimmy Junior the straight face, agreeing with what he had just said. Jimmy Junior and Amanda now find themselves sitting in Amanda’s parents living room. Amanda’s dad Ron and mother April, have for the most part, approved of their daughter’s relationship with Jimmy Junior. The fact that Jimmy Junior is studying medicine at Boise State University is a huge plus for them and knowing he comes from a very respectable home is a pleasant relief for both Ron and April. Jimmy Junior and Amanda walk into Amanda’s parents house and are greeted by April in the living room. “It’s so good to see you both, take a seat guys.” April said invitingly. After sitting down Amanda turns to her mother and asks, “Where’s dad?” “Where else would he be? He’s in his shop as usual. I swear that man spends more time in there than what he does in this house.” Replied April. “Well Mom, you better call him inside, we’ve got some news for you guys.” Explained Amanda. “Oh, well, ok. I’ll go get him.” April’s joy at seeing her daughter suddenly turned to concern as she heads out to call her husband. “Are you ready for this?” A nervous Amanda asks Junior. “Everything will be ok. Your parents are cool.” Replied Junior. “I know, I guess I’m just nervous.’ Said Amanda. “Do you want me to tell them?” Junior asks. “It’s ok, they’re my parents’ I’ll tell them.” Replied Amanda. At that moment April walks in with Ron. “Hey Pumpkin.” Ron says as he kisses his daughter on the cheek before sitting down. “I believe you guys have some news for us.” Said Ron. “Yes Daddy, we do.” Replied Amanda. “Well, what is it? Did you guys get the results on your finals?” Asked Ron. “Not exactly.” Said Amanda with a nervous tone. “Well, what is it?” Ron asks as he starts getting anxious from that anticipation. “Mom, Dad, I’m pregnant.” She announced. April and Ron look at each other in shock with what they had just heard. April then jumps to her feet and hugs Amanda and starts crying with joy. Ron slowly stands up and walks over to Amanda to give his little girl a hug. He then walks over to Jimmy Junior and gives him a hug as well. “I’m really happy for both of you.” Said Ron with a smile on his face. “Thanks Dad.” Said a smiling and teary Amanda. “Thanks Ron, thanks April.” Said Junior Ron sits back in his chair, as does Junior, while April and Amanda head into the kitchen to get some snacks out for a small celebration for the four of them. Ron turns his head to make sure April and Amanda have left the room then leans back in his chair and says, “Junior, I’ve always thought you were a good kid that comes from a good home. You’re going to be a doctor and you seem to have your future set out.” Ron leans forward and turns to see that the women are still out of the room before looking Junior in the eye and saying, “Now you’ve knocked up my little girl which means your priorities need to change. I couldn’t careless who your father is and what you’ve got planned but I’m telling you now, you do anything that upsets my little girl, I promise you, I’ll come looking for you and as God Himself as my witness, I will make sure you walk with a limp for the rest of your life.” At that point, Amanda walks in with April holding a tray with some snacks and cold drinks. Ron leans back in his chair and looked up April and smiles at her and says, “Thanks Baby Girl.” Amanda sits down next to Junior who seems frazzled. “So Junior.” Says Ron. “What are your plans now?” Ron curiously asks. “Relax Ron, give the boy a chance to breathe.” Said April. “What do you mean, give the boy the a chance to breathe?” Asked Ron in a stern tone. “He’s going to be a father to our grandchild before he knows it and I just want to know what he’s got planned.” “It’s ok, April.” Said Junior. “Once I’ve spoken to my dad, I’ll have a better idea on what we’ll do.” Replied Junior. “So you haven’t told your folks yet?” Ron asked. “Just Emma so far.” Junior replied again. Ron just nods as then keeps quiet as the women keep the conversation going. Later that evening when Junior and Amanda lay in bed and get lost in their own, personal train of thoughts. Amanda breaks the silence and says, “I don’t think my dad was that happy.” “You can say that again.” Replied Junior. “Why, what did he say?” Asked Amanda. “It doesn’t really matter. I’m more worried about what Dad is going to say.” Said Junior. “Well, we’ll find out tomorrow afternoon.” Amanda responded. The next afternoon, Junior and Amanda arrive at Jimmy’s and Emma’s house with Jimmy just finished packing and ready to leave for his flight to Ohio when Junior and Amanda walk into the house. “Hey guys, Emma told me you guys were around.” Said a cheerful Jimmy. “Yeah we arrived yesterday. We had no classes so we thought we’d come up early.” Replied Junior. “Where’s Emma?” He asked. “She went to the doctors, I’m waiting for her to come back before I leave so I can say goodbye.” Said Jimmy. “Dad, you better take a seat, I’ve got something to tell you.” Said Junior with a serious tone in his voice. “What’s up mate? Did you get kicked out of school or something hahaha?” Replied Jimmy as he sits down. “No, not at all.” Replied Junior. “It’s ummm well, I really don’t know how to tell you this.” Explained Junior “This sounds serious.” Jimmy said. ‘Well it is serious but it’s not at the same time.” said Junior. “I’m just going to let it out.” He added. He takes a deep breath in and says, “Dad, Amanda’s pregnant.” He announced with a smile on his face. Jimmy blinks a few times and says, “She’s wha what?” “She’s going to have a baby.” Junior answered. Jimmy closes his eyes, tilts his head back and says, “Oh my fucking God in heaven.” Jimmy leans forward and opens hi eyes again, looks at Junior, then stands up and starts pacing. “Well, say something Dad.” Said Junior. Jimmy keeps pacing back and forth before saying, “How do you plan to handle this one son?” “Well, I was kind of hoping you’d give me some advice.” Replied Junior. “Advice?” Jimmy asks. “Or is it more you need both Emma and me to help you guys raise this baby?” He continues. “Son, your life was all planned out, you were going to be someone and now that all changes.” He says as he looks at Amanda. “I don’t mean that in a bad way there, all I’m trying to say is both you kids have got your whole lives in front of you and this happens.” He continues with. “Dad, it’s not like we planned for this to happen.” said Junior. “I know you didn’t plan for this to happen. I really don’t think you realize how much this is going to change your lives. You guys are just way too young for this, I’m way too old to be having babies in this house now.” Said Jimmy while pacing backwards and forwards. At that moment Emma walks in the room which breaks Jimmy’s train of thought. “What did the Doctor say?” Jimmy asks “I’m pregnant. Replied Emma. Jimmy quickly turns his head to her for a quick moment before falling backwards and passing out from the shock of the news. To be continued……
