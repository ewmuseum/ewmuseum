Date: Timeless The PTC representative shifted uneasily in his seat, not able to stare Steven Caldera in the face. “It’s more a question of’um’you know’feasibility.” The man seemed like he had trouble in personal contact given the stream of his sentences was constantly being broken by his own nervousness. In situations like this, it would be prudent and a whole lot less embarrassing to simply send something over e-mail, or correspond over fax. In that way, it was easier to be a cherry picker and hide behind the umbrella rather than go out on their own and be prima donnas. But Caldera had no such qualms staring down anyone. “What do you mean feasibility” Could you’per chance’elaborate on your choice of words?” He knew where the talk was going. He knew that this PTC representative would question the viability of holding a PTC event on GCW show, for fear that it might not even make it to air in a timely fashion. “We just think that a PRIME’maybe even a FUSE--? Caldera cut him off. “Youngblood earned that Silver Ticket. Part of what goes with that involves selecting where the match takes place. He has selected his home organization. There really is nothing more to discuss about it.” The PTC representative frowned, but just as quickly as he saw hope slip away he grabbed on to its coat tails, attempting to bring it home in a manner that would make “everyone” happy. “But he won that ticket over a year ago’as part of a rival organization! Ergo, being that he was released from the company he earned it for, the choice belongs to us!” Caldera merely smirked. “I went over this with executives before I even signed the man. The ticket, IN WHOLE, was part of the package. Nothing from it was annexed. Just because he was involved in another organization does not mean we get shortchanged--? Steven caught himself in a moment to politically correct himself. “Er’does not mean that HE will get shortchanged.” The PTC executive snapped his fingers, shaking his head. “Mister Caldera’I do not think it is prudent to continue to be pushing the buttons of this governing body.” The uneasiness dissipated, replaced by a resolute demeanor of informing in a manner of which to force a strong man to back down. “You’ve bullied and bought your way to title shots, started and helped instigate general discontent within our community’you’ve jumped organizational lines to settle scores--? “From talent poachers that apparently NEVER do any wrong.” Caldera retorted. “Er?” The PTC executive did not know where his thought was for a moment, having been caught off guard by the statement. “Er’you know Caldera’it’s an attitude like that that will get GCW thrown out of PTC!” Caldera rocked back in his plush chair and let out a hearty laugh. “With AWC farting in the night” With XUW closing in just over a year? YOU CAN’T AFFORD TO LET US GO!” “We might not have to if you keep on falling apart at the seams.” Caldera sneered at the biting comment. “And it’s a malignant attitude like that that has allowed monopoly’s to be built at a time when the talent pool is thinning.” “The fact is?” The PTC executive choked out through a cough. “The fact is’it’s more than about scheduled times going up. We just’do not want to see Brandon Youngblood hold a belt of ours.” Caldera smirked at this. “Because he’s under contract for me.” The executive shook his head in the negative. “No. But you crossing the picket line on a talent that was branded, as off limits’well’that hasn’t gotten you many friends. Youngblood did not burn his bridges with this community. He napalmed them.” Caldera punched his desk, startling the man before him. “The past is the past! How about you let the track record speak for itself” He’s one of this company’s top performers. Hell’he hasn’t even LOST.” “Talented” Sure. But ask yourself why someone with those kinds of abilities couldn’t get a job anywhere. It goes beyond anything about in ring talent and desire’marketability or whatever. Youngblood isn’t cancer’he’s AIDS. And whatever he touches’it goes to shit.” Caldera rubbed his hands together anxiously, trying to mask the disdain of agreeing with the man. But he had done the research. He knew what he had on his roster very well could be a nuclear warhead ready to go off inside a bunker. And worst of all’it would be HIS. “He has not even remotely come near to violating a term in his contract for us.” The PTC executive, having found a topic he could look any person in the eye on, tuned in to Caldera’s pensive movements. “We know this. We also know the amount of incentives he is about to reach. Merchandising’behavior’even something about bringing a PTC Title match to GCW?” “So WHAT”!” Caldera sneered. “It’s my money’he’s achieving items in it that increase our profits all the way around.” Shifting in his chair, he put his hands on the desk, looking Caldera eye to eye. “Steven’seriously ask yourself how someone as bitter about his termination from PRIME is willing to take a PTC Extreme Title match to GCW’when he could well go onto their show and embarrass him” You put one million dollars on just getting the match here.” Caldera eased back in his chair, having a knowing smirk. “You see that guy in the ring” It’s not about the money to him. He just wants to crack skulls. You people are going too deep thinking about a possible conspiracy here.” The executive somberly replied. “For your sake’for all our sakes’I hope I’m wrong.” *** Title: Rebirth of Divine Nepotism It had been over a year since Brandon Youngblood could honestly say he felt himself. And by himself, that meant as the Only Diamond in the Sport. His nepotism had capsized him in PRIME, one step away from the grand altar of which he stated day one he would stand up and shout triumphantly. Their bodies washed away from him as he made his ascension. Names that now stand as false idols in a since dated promotion. Karina Wolfenden. Nova. Angelo Deville. Youngblood came from minute beginnings in the professional wrestling world as a fake cowboy. Years later he would have perhaps the most impressive list of bodies defeated in professional wrestling. And the sky was the limit. But something crashed. And Duke Williams interference, sending a steel chair in the skull of a prone Youngblood began a downward spiral of which he had tried to pull himself from. *** It wasn’t short and sweet. Being amongst the Englanders, Brandon looked at them through his tinted Oakley sunglasses as he continued to sign autographs with a smile on his face. The sky was overcast with clouds, the norm in this part of the world. Pen firmly in hand, he signed newly purchased merchandise, as well as older stuff. All for a fee. “Ten dollars a signature guys’don’t push’plenty of time to spend.” He just did not seem to care that was coming or how deep they were. Like a bank teller, he took their money and rendered his service. All making his faded jean pockets fatter. He tried going quickly, to get through as many as possible. One of the fans huddled around Brandon looked his cohort in the eyes. “Where the heck is that accent?” He simply shrugged, elbowing to try and keep his position. “I dunno. I thought he was supposed to be bald.” *** The reality was simple. Hell, it was out there in front of everyone. Brandon Youngblood had built a wrestling career on his tremendous acumen for the sport. An inaugural member of the Texas based Squared Circle, Youngblood’s ring ability was obviously something of mention. But in those dog days as nothing more than midcard fodder, Brandon became more and more dangerous. He learned from the best the sport had to offer. He had to as a means for survival. The crucial lesson though was to keep your mouth shut. Brandon, to this day, has rejected that lesson. But it was in tSC where he found out the punishment for his nepotism. Wrestling legend Mike Randalls butchered him and put him on display for the world to see. Coral Avalon, a man known more for his slight whimsically romantic nature than his killing instinct bludgeoned him and made him submit. Brandon Youngblood changed from a loud bird to someone who was a force defeating the vaunted Alias in three consecutive contests, making him submit as well. The Spirit of 2004 had been exposed. But as quickly as he had dominated Alias, it all slipped away. He learned his craft in the single greatest WRESTLING promotion in the world. But Brandon had also built a career out of lies. Lies of being a National Championship winning amateur wrestler. Lies about his clean cut nature when taking on a supposed evil. These lies came back and bit him, as if the wrestling environment was akin to a parent. *** Since Dangerous Games, he’d been the hardest man on the planet to get a hold of. Almost as if he was a ghost. He’d won the United State Championship and dominated those that challenged him. He still had not lost a match beyond a gimmick battle royal and a tag team contest where someone else took the fall. “I’m untouchable.” He said with a smirk to the mirror. Low and behold, Youngblood had lost a chance to be champion at that Dangerous Games show. And with that loss seemingly came a sense of grand disappointment. Shadow was in the NC-17 spotlight, not him. And for whatever reason, he could not rationalize it. “I’m unstoppable.” Nobody in GCW was on his level. NOBODY! Jacob McKail had his ass beaten so badly that he went to FUSE rather than have to face off against him again. Seymour Almasy’s legacy died in a GCW ring from a knock out loss to Brandon. Baltasar’s wrestling career was ended less than two minutes into a match. “I’m a monster.” Nobody who stepped in a ring with him had ever beaten him in GCW. So why come back and truly care until now? A shot at the World Title was on the line, and for Brandon Youngblood that meant more than the world. What was there left to prove in GCW but add a few bodies to the list? What was there left to do but win that piece of gold? It didn’t matter to him who, where, or how. He just knew that someday’someday VERY soon’he’d finally be able to don the one thing that had always alluded him. “I’m Unbeatable.” “Unbeatable” Brandon Youngblood. Current United States Champion. Future World Champion. And the most dangerous man in GCW.
