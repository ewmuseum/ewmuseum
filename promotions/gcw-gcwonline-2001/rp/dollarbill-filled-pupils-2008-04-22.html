Dollarbill filled pupils

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22, 2008 Dinner, compliments of Steven Caldera himself, was in fact greater than dinner paid for by Jay Terror's wallet. Mouthwatering steak. Mashed potatos. Fine wine. Cuban cigars. It had everything that established personnel expected plus the luxury of harps playing a mere five feet away. Living the moment, Terror shoved a fatty piece of steak onto his tongue and bit it loose from his fork while tapping his cigar on a gold-plated ashtray. 'Good food, yeah?' 'Better than I've had in a while, yeah.' 'Something wrong?' Caroline, struggling with a loose bit of pepper in her front tooth, shook her head. 'Nothing. Just thinking is all.' 'Thinking is hardly nothing.' 'It's hardly something too.' He set his fork on his plate again and brought the cigar back to his lips. After an inhale, 'What's on your mind?' 'It's nothing. Just typical stuff.' 'Let's hear what's so typical about it, huh?' She sighed and twirled the bits of leftover potato around with the prongs of her fork. 'Things are just different. I don't know. I mean, like dinner and all. Why did we go here over the Wendy's around the corner. Or why'd we fly in first class instead of coach after leaving Japan? That kinda' stuff.' 'That's what's eating you?' 'I guess. Things are just wierd.' 'You saw the stack of hundreds Steve gave us, right? Remember?' The twirling continued as if she hoped to mash the mashed potatos out of existence, 'Yeah. I saw it.' A pause. 'Just wierd who it's from too. I didn't see it coming, that's it.' 'That was the point, no one saw it coming.' He took another bite from the steak and looked off toward the harp players. A grin formed on his lips shielded by the back of his head. 'We had to do it so no one knew. You understand, yeah?' 'Yeah, I mean. Sure. It's just - I figured that I wasn't part of that circle and that -' 'You're not part of that circle.' 'Then how come you couldn't tell me?' He wiped the grin away and slowly turned back to his cigar and Caroline. Her eyes radiated a dose of sincerity that temporarily weakened his defenses. After another puff though, he could feel his system rebooting. 'Steve told me not to. He said that no matter what, no one could know.' 'Even the girl you're fucking?' 'You're more than that.' She shut her eyes, 'Uh huh.' Jay's fingers crossed the flower arrangement between them and touched her hand, 'I'm serious. I wanted to tell you.' 'I understand. It's fine.' 'You hate fine' She opened her eyes again and leaned back in the mahogony chair. 'It's fine.' 'Baby -' 'How's Jordan?' Jay shrugged it off, 'Dunno, haven't talked to him since that night. I figure he's good enough though, I mean - I didn't hit him that hard.' 'You shouldn't have hit him at all.' 'Caroline, you don't understand.' 'Understand what?' Terror shook his head and dug into his pocket. Coming to the surface of the table, Jay pulled loose a stack of hundred dollar bills. 'Didn't you see my prize? Remember?' Her eyes tilted down to a stack of green paper which Jay placed between his plate and hers. 'I know Jay. I saw.' Her lids closed slightly creating a tight gap for her to see through. 'It's just that, well -' 'Well what?' 'Nothing.' 'Caroline, we can talk you know. I'll listen.' 'Let's just get out of here. I'm getting kinda' tired.' Silence takes over as Jay studies his lover. Her eyes remain tranfixed on the stack of hundred dollar bills on the table and follow them as the owner tucks them away in his pocket again. 'Yeah. Same.' Terror turned around and called down their waitor signifying he wanted the check. Turning back to face Caroline, Jay smirked at the look Caroline had when eyeing the money. It grew wider as he thought of using it to his advantage. +=+=+=+=+ 'Where are we going?' 'To the gym.' 'The gym?' 'Yes, the gym. Mr. Caldera's orders.' Jay blinked a few times and sucked off the end of his cigarette. 'What's at the gym.' Seeming peeved, the driver sighed and sat up straight. 'I'm not sure what is there. I was told to drive you there. That's all.' 'Right.' He squirmed on the leather seat cover and found a niche after curling his body slightly, 'You going to drive me everywhere from now on?' 'Wherever I'm informed to bring you, yes.' He grinned and blew a circle of smoke out of a hole between his lips toward the driver. With a cough and a wipe of the nose, he acted as if nothing happened and continued to drive onward. 'You know, you are a lot like the drivers in movies. Stoic and such. Kinda' in a world of their own. Ya' know?' 'Not exactly, no.' 'I'm just saying,' he paused, 'you're reserved.' 'I'm paying attention to the road.' 'And you're doing a damn fine job at it.' Jay nodded and finished off the last bit of nicotine in his cigarette before shoving in the window button on the door and flicking the butt off into the wind. A gust of air swept through the limousine and passed over both Jay and the driver's heads whipping their hair slightly. An awkward silence hung over the two of them, neither knowing what to say, and continued to roam along the small confines of the limo until they reached the gym. The driver maneuvered alongside the curb and depressed the brake. 'Here we are.' 'Yeah? Here?' 'Here, yes.' 'Will you be here when I'm done?' 'I will, yes.' Jay nodded and let the silence return for a moment. Avoiding any formalities, Jay grabbed his stuff and stepped out into the brisk Oregon air. His eyes caught the gleam from the sign above the gym reading "Gold's Gym" in thick gold letters. He shrugged and grabbed another cigarette from his dwindling box of Marlboros before stepping through the automatic doors and into the sweat-drenched air. 'Jay Terror?' Jay looked off to his right, following the voice, and noticed a shirtless man covered with Celtic tattoos looking his way. 'That's me, yeah.' 'Put that smoke out.' 'Ha. You the pope or something?' The man crossed his arms across his chest and shook his head, 'Hardly. There's no smoking when working out.' 'To be fair, I'm not quite working out yet.' 'To be fair, that's because you're late.' Jay cocked a brow and sucked at the cigarette paper again allowing the flame to burn the end more. 'That's not my fault. I had a driver.' 'Just drop the cigarette and follow me.' The man turned his back to Jay giving him another two quick drags before tossing the cigarette into a nearby trashcan. Obediently, Jay followed the man at a safe distance as they snaked around the corners until they reached a poorly-lit room with a wrestling ring. 'You wrestle?' 'I fight.' 'One of those types, yeah?' 'Climb up there, I want to bury the bullshit.' 'Bury it?' 'Just get up there.' Jay followed the command like a trained puppy and stood in the center of the ring looking over the scratched paint on the walls around him. The canvas below his feet was dyed red thanks to a large blood stain dried long ago. 'What's on the agenda,' he paused looking for a name. 'Call me Jack.' 'Jack seems like a good name.' 'I was hired to help train you in the ring, not talk about my name. So let's see -' 'Hired huh? Steven Caldera think I can't fight or something?' Jack shrugged, 'He thinks you could use some fine tuning. From the looks of it, I agree.' 'I may not be the biggest guy out there but I bite harder than they do. You should see, I'm like the little bullet that could gnawing through the ribcage and spinal cord out the back of the body.' Jay smiled coyly and crossed his arms in a mimicking pose. 'I don't care what you think you can do and who you think you are, you're going to be working under my conditions.' He uncrossed his arms and leaned against the apron of the ring. 'No matter what.' 'Not to be judgmental but who are you exactly? Jack, I know. But what else?' With no emotion visible, Jack slid under the ropes and into the ring where he proceeded to lift Jay off the ground for a slam of some sort. Half through, however, Jay manages to avoid the mat and counter with a slip. For his efforts, Jack levels Jay with a firm forearm shot to the nose. 'I'm a three time Mixed Martial Arts champion who's been told that you need help with your career. A world champion will duck that forearm shot and will surely avoid the slam I nearly had on you.' 'Ever hear of the comeback?' 'Who needs to comeback when they don't fall behind?' 'Touche.' Jack leaned backwards on the ropes behind him and grabbed them with his baseball-sized fists. 'These next few weeks we will travel with the GCW schedule and make sure you're in top shape to take down Vivica J. Valentine. You'll do what I ask and act as I say. Agreed?' 'So pushy.' 'Agreed?' 'Just make me a monster and we're solid. That belt's gotta' be around my waist.' 'It's in the plans. +=+=+=+=+ I'm not quite sure what to write in this thing but Caroline said that it would help me relieve stress and look at my life in a different perspective. Not sure why I need that. Not like I'm stressed or nothing. Her though, that's different. She's been weird since I sided with Steve, almost like she'd rather me stay stagnant. Who knows? She visited someone the other day though claiming it was an old friend. Not really sure what old friend she knows in Oregon. Anyways, fuck this. I'm not stressed enough to fill any more space up. +=+=+=+=+ 'Remember when we met Jordan, that night in Japan?' 'Yeah, I remember. Was a good night.' Caroline nodded and inched her way up to a sitting position in the hotel bed. 'I was just thinking, I mean who knows but - does Caldera make you go on dates with random girls?' 'Steve you mean?' 'I'd rather not.' Jay huffed, 'What do you mean random girls?' 'Remember Jordan,' she paused as he jogged his memory to the night he and Caroline ran into the man Jay had recently belted in the forehead with a chair, 'he was with some snooty random girl.' 'He wasn't on Steve's side though. Not for a long time.' 'I know that but - I mean, I figured that with all that money, you'd be taking out random hookers for the sake of taking them out.' Her eyes were orbs of fire burning Jay's soul with pressing questions. Jay shook his head at the statement, 'Unless you consider yourself a hooker, I wouldn't fret. The money's for us. That's all.' 'I'm just saying -' 'Do you not trust me?' Jay asked with a defensive bite in his words. Caroline shook her head, 'I do. That's not it. I was just checking.' 'Uh huh.' +=+=+=+=+ You know what, maybe I am frustrated. I don't get it. Why can't she see that what I'm doing is for the best. I'm going to be the world champion in no time and with that comes all the benefits in the world. Free booze, cheap cigarettes, private jets, global recognition, the whole shabang. We'll be richer than Richy Rich and happier than Elmo. I just wish that she'd look at it the way I do. The way that everyone should be looking at it. Maybe I'll add more later.
