The Adventures of Jordan White and Sylvia the Hooker!

10 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/06 'It's just, fuck it, y'know? Like everybody I know, they're all high schoolers. Not, not, not really high schoolers but, I mean, they act it. Dig?' She nods, sends cascades of blonde hair rippling across my chest. 'Just got tired. Like, dudes and bitches and everybody, still stuck on some ol' drama shit, or stuck on some quickchanging personality shit, like. Flip. Fuck 'em.' 'Fuck 'em.' She laughs smoke. 'Yo. Shit. Where's the pisser at?' 'Pisser?' The word rolls over in her mouth like a toy. I laugh air. 'Y'know, the bathroom.' With a grin she points at the only door that I didn't walk in from. Drag my pants behind me, toss 'em over my shoulder and dig for cigarettes, dig for fire. I hate these fucking toilets. European trash with no deep water to aim at, no hope of that plooshing, streaming splash that's half the relief. It hits the ceramic shelf. It lingers for a second, it finds rivulets to run in. And they want me in Spain before next week? Oh, fuck. I feel that happening. She's laying in the mirror, sprawled out with a sweat glaze and falling into the deep cushion of a thick red comforter in a thick red room in a thick red world; red in molecular interaction, red in the blind angles and swerves we take, red at soul. Wrapped in smoke that tries to turn the crimson box to pink, she arches her back. 'Take your time, Jordan. You've got all night.' Her words are clinical, try their best to avoid slipping into a real accent. Northern European, some Scandinavian country. Whatever. Little useless details that populate a little, useless place. Coarse, like her throat's maybe sore. With infection, with virus, with disease. Maybe. Three shakes and I'm done. Even washing my hands. See? I'm an alright person. Staining her lips with more hot pink lipstick, smacking them together to bring it into equilibrium. 'Can I have one?' 'Mm?' 'A cigarette.' Two fingers, totemic, push to her lips. I toss her the pack and the lighter and, well, fuck it. She can do the rest; she does the rest as I'm forcing myself down further into the bed. It devours me with satin fangs, her hands pressed against my chest and feeding me to the beast. I watch the smoke rise to flail, curl in a barely ever real puff of wind, wrap a loosening spiral to the gold and light gold ceiling to join the herd. 'Jordan.' ' 'Yeah.' 'My name's Sylvia.' 'Well, I. Fuck, alright. Cool, whatever.' 'Do you want me?' 'Sure. Just gimme a few.' Her hands drift. 'A few what?' 'Minutes, probably.' 'I thought you Americans were stallions.' 'Got a good advertisin' campaign.' She's going to laugh at anything I say. Fucking. The carotid artery of Amsterdam. I think I could maybe bleed distilled depravity right about now. 'I like you, Jordan. Are you staying?' 'In the city?' 'Yes.' 'No. Passing through.' 'Business?' 'Real work's for suckers.' 'Then what do you do?' 'Eh, not much. What about you?' The corners of her lips peak and her lower lip rolls and she giggles. 'You are a man who is very funny.' She'd call me Zebuluque if I told her to. So, well. It's sort of a hollow compliment. 'I get by.' 'How do you survive? I'm not a poor hobby.' 'Workin' in a coalmine.' 'Hahh hhhah. I love that song.' 'It's a song?' 'Be serious, Jordan.' Serious means pushing my shoulders up against the headboard, just so I can slump forwards. Means brushing back scraps of hair that needed to be shaved a few weeks ago. Means cocking my head back to look for that soft spot in the ceiling. Being wistful. Bellyaching. Browbeating. No. 'Shit, not the serious type, not a serious mood.' 'What type are you?' 'Not the type you'd think.' 'Oh? I am very good at reading people.' 'Yeah?' ''Yes.' 'Oh.' I don't wanna force the issue’she might be right. 'Could you take your cross off? It's kinda bugging me.' Big green eyes slip down to the glinting gold of a t nestling in her chest. She lifts it between two fingers, spins it to face me. 'Why?' 'Hell, why not?' 'Because I wear it proudly.' 'Then wear it inside.' The tip of her nose pitches down to obscure the rise of her top lip; she slips it off and hangs it around a crook of a branching lamp on the nightstand. It sways on thin gold links, it pitches erratic shadows of messiahs on walls. It's. Heavyhanded. I find silk at my feet and dig my toes in and go for my ankles. 'Is this your first time?' 'Well. Like, first time first time, or first time paying for it?' With a laugh that's too quick and soft to even fuck with stifling, I'm closer to back on her good side. 'Paying.' 'Oh. Shit, yeah.' 'That's cute. What made your decision?' 'Fuck, it's cheaper than keepin' around flunkies to tell me how great I am.' 'You talk like a rich man.' 'Nah, I talk like a shmuck. I do everything else like a rich man.' 'What is shmuck?' 'It's, like. Okay, it's like a loser that just doesn't understand his real place in the world. Sorta.' 'You seem to know your place.' 'See? Marriage is way more expensive, and they're not even guaranteed to be nice.' 'You're married?' 'Uh. I don't know, really. It's like, it's an issue. Y'know? Up for debate.' 'Hehee. Which side is hers?' 'I dunno. Lines ain't drawn real deep. I'm figurin', fuck it, I'ma know an answer whenever I get back home.' I smear fake tan, I fade a jaundice yellow under a bronze thigh. 'I meet a lot of married men.' 'Doesn't surprise me. One relationship's enough.' 'Ah, all you men are the same.' 'Fuckin' pigs?' 'No, babies that want us to think you're pigs. It's cute, yes, but I wouldn't be with one.' 'Hah. What?' .'How do you say, date. I only date women.' 'Y'know, Amsterdam's an odd place.' 'It us a good city, yes? Why you're here.' 'S'a nice visit. Feels like fallin' back into gold. Dust, gold dust. Nuggets or fuckin', bricks, yeah, that shit'd hurt. Scrooge McDuck was a tough fucker.' The curtains are too heavy to stare out at moaning neon signs, gawkers gawking by. I can't smell myself over the perfume of a thousand flowers and a million dead whales. Thoughts can't pour over the sounds of a lurching city. 'You never told me your job.' 'Fuck. You wouldn't believe me if I did.' 'Haha. You big rockstar, actor?' The cigarette smokes. The cigarette is smoked. 'Nah. Kinda. Famous. Well, kinda famous, yeah.' 'Mmhm. Yes?' 'No, you'll laugh.' 'I won't. I promise.' 'Did I really pay you that much?' 'Enough,' she laughs. 'Enough. Go on.' 'Okay. Okay, I'm a professional wrestler. People beat my ass for money.' 'So you're a whore. Does it pay well?' 'Christ no. You can't survive doing this shit unless you're already rich.' 'Can you survive doing this shit?' 'I'm here, aren't I?' 'Hah, oh. If you can survive, then why would you do something you are so ashamed of?' 'I. Well. My old lawyer signed the papers to make some money back outta me, and. Shit. I just never signed the ones that'd make me quit. Gets in the blood.' 'An addiction, I see.' Five fingers trail down my sternum, find my xiphoid process and tap. 'Shit. You like your job?' Her lips arc into a perfect parabola, draw a perfect smile and pause. 'I love my job.' I'm halfway into a syllable when she starts back. 'The pay is good, the work is fun, it is a beautiful country, what's not to love?' 'Salient points, all.' I hope salient means what I think it means. 'Shit, there are shirts with my face on them.' 'That's something to be proud of.' 'Nah. Nah, you're fuckin' crazy. It's real weird, I mean, seein' my face on someone's chest in like a fuckin' grocery store. Not anymore, nah, people don't really give a shit about a guy that can't get his head into it. But way back. Haha, shit. I almost never win against chicks. I just can't stand to hit something with tits, I guess.' Her back mirrors her lips on the horizon, draws up and out. 'You have to wrestle girls?' 'Crazy shit, I know. It's all a big joke, really, but I just can't swing right at a chick, y'know? Like, like I can't put any follow through in it. Y'ever had a nightmare where like, I don't know, some great unspeakable evil shit was just bearing down on you and you keep throwin' punches at it, but they're all stopping dead on the surface with this impotent smack? It's like that. Like wind resistance is suddenly an epic motherfucker.' 'Your wife, how is she?' 'Breathing, probably.' 'No, joker. As a person.' 'She's alright. I mean, shit, she stuck around through a lot of stupid shit but she also caused a lot of stupid shit and’y'know’no matter what, I'm here right now and that probably says somethin'.' I'm just not really listening, is all. 'You think I don't understand you.' 'I think I need some more smoke. Where'd you lay that'?that thing right there?'right there, cool.' I grab the little glass bong by the stem, pull through and fill the bowl for a second round. She jumps the gun. Smoke wafts as her mouth shoots in pulses. 'My mother knew the Beatles.' 'All of 'em?' Sucking her lips into her teeth, nodding: 'yes, she dated George in the sixties.' 'Shit. Cool.' 'Yes, and the Sex Pistols.' 'Perseverance.' I rip, I cough. Shit, that's life. 'How was your mother?' 'I dunno. Grew up with an aunt. My pops is pretty cool, met him a few times.' 'That's sad.' 'Nah, it's alright. I've had a pretty swank life this way. And it's better than dealing with a teary divorce. Probably.' 'You should just stay here with us.' 'Hah, plural?' 'Yes, my friends and me. All the girls are wonderful, wonderful. You would love it.' 'Totally not the first time it's crossed my mind. But, shit. It is a really good idea. Like, incredibly fuckin' good, dig?' And shit, why not. I hear the windmills turn all year round. Maybe fjords and, wait, no. Fjords are further north. Shit. 'I dunno.' 'Think about it, for me.' 'I have a huge fuckin' wallet.' 'I know.' 'You're a pretty good marketing department.' Maybe she blushes, maybe she's flushed like it hurts, maybe there's finally direct light against her makeup. 'Fuck. I need a drink.' 'There's a bar down the street.' 'How far?' 'Two blocks.' 'Fuckin', where's the nearest store?' 'Two blocks.' 'You be here when I get back?' She bites her lower lip and nods. Groovy. 'Want anything?' 'Red Bull.' 'Sure.' Pants go on like this, and shirts like this. I grab the fur coat like maybe it's still alive and I'm gonna have to take it down. And it goes on like this. Shoes and socks, somewhere. 'Hurry up.' And I open the door. And I'm into the blue world. Huddled in the doorway, lighting a cigarette off the butt of the last and pushing out. Wreathed in a cracking cold, and it's never all in my head. Thoughts turn to a frosty mush, to an infinite number of infinitely sharp ice crystals that sludge past neurons. Fucking cold. Killing cold. I bathe in cherry and lime and apricot tubes that read 'GIRLS!' in different tongues that all translate instantly to that one root point; but they don't hide the blue world. It has its fingers in me. They wrap around the base of my spine and work there way up. I'll die, yeah, and it'll suck. I'm a person, and people don't have a natural coating that is thick, fluffy and highly insulating. Or, well. Holy shit. A coating that is thick, fluffy, and highly insulating. Cigarette sticking to my lips, pulling away tiny bits of flesh so the night can stab in without a fight. Like I shouldn't be breathing. The gunk, the slime, the ooze and mucus in my throat and lungs freezes solid, or into semisolid chunks that break and rip and slice. Maybe breathing's a bad idea. I'll just take a good deep inhale and hold it until I get to Cozumel, or California, or somewhere I don't find myself cheering on global warming, somewhere I'm not tempted to light two tons worth of aerosol cans ablaze, somewhere I don't want to lead a train’nay, a fleet of Hummers everywhere I go. When I don't dream of beach front property in Wyoming, then I can take a nice deep inhale or. Fuck. Lightheaded. Breathing's a necessary evil. This place is empty. People apparently know something I didn't. Huh. Nice that this sudden approach to heatdeath's taken my mind off that shitty job. Oh, fuckin'. Just one slip. Just one slip and I don't think about explorers millennia from now finding my body clutched to a lightpost, perfectly preserved and still a little angry about it. It's all back to the job, and that's just a step away from getting back into all the problems I was supposed to have left in America. Fuckin' Danielle, and Casey, and Trey, and god damnit. Why couldn't everyone just play by the rules and not go Al Pacino dramatic? Guess I did need a vacation, anyways. Not bad so far, gotta say. Fuck it. I can dig.
