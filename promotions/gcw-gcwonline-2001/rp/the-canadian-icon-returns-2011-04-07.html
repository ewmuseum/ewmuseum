The "Canadian Icon" returns!

7 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/07/11 ::: So many memories. So many legendary matches. Tears of joy and tears of pain. A legendary career that was suppose to be over but has somehow resurrected for another run. All these facts and thoughts run through the mind of Daniel Cruz. As Cruz walks through the trophy showcase in the basement of his home, a black and white video montage of his wrestling career flashes on the screen. Moments of Cruz winning the World Heavyweight Championship in FATE. Short video clips of Cruz wearing a full crimson mask as he applies a Liontamer on a unworthy opponent and other clips of Cruz having his arm raised in victory. The black and white video montage slowly dissolves off the screen as we see Daniel Cruz once again standing in front of his showcase. Cruz's eyes are focused on the beaming shine from the replica championship belts that he held throughout his career. ::: Daniel Cruz: Just when i thought i was out...... they pull me back in... ::: Quoting the famous line from one of his favorite movies, Cruz casually adjusts his polo short as he neatly tucks it into his black slacks. With a confident grin, Cruz continues his concentration on his showcase, focusing on former promo pictures and amateur wrestling medals as he says ::: Daniel Cruz: For those of you who may have been living under a rock for the past decade, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Daniel Cruz... but you can simply refer to me as The Canadian Icon. The greatest of all time! I have proven time and time again that I'm truly the most technically sound wrestler in the world. My matches are historic, my career is legendary, and as you can see.... my appearance is simply irresistible! Needless to say you can see the impact I have made on this business over the years. I thought i did everything i needed to do in this sport to truly claim myself as the true Canadian Icon of Professional Wrestling. I retired from this sport five years ago. During that period i simply laid back and enjoyed the millions of dollars that I earned. I spent time in Las Vegas. I worked on my tan at my condo in Hawaii. I ate the freshest sushi in Japan. And i kept myself in shape here at my mansion in Canada. I've got it all. But for guys like me... all is not enough... ::: Daniel Cruz finally loses his trance into the showcase of accomplishments as he locks his brown eyes directly into the camera standing in front of him ::: Daniel Cruz: I have been watching Global Championship Wrestling for quite sometime now. I've been tuning into the shows. I own the DVD's. I buy the magazines and i purchase every single Pay Per View event. I have to admit... I'm very impressed. I always was. But I always noticed as good as GCW is... the company as a whole is missing one very important item. That item is me. The Canadian Icon. The highest rated technical wrestler in the WORLD. Hands down! But ladies and gentlemen your concerns are now over. Your hero is officially announcing his return to the world of wrestling. And where else to have a better return than GCW?! So many punks, so many nobody's, and so many wanna-be's that i see on GCW every time i turn it on. Now don't get me wrong. I'm a fair guy! Some talent around here are impressive, but they are in no means the quality of yours truly. So speaking directly from my heart.... i'm giving you fans and talent of GCW a quick introduction to introduce myself to you. But from this moment forward, I'm officially asserting myself as the most dominant male in the GCW Kingdom. One by one i will begin to take everyone apart and assert myself as Alpha male #1. For all you one hit wonders that enjoyed winning streaks and championship reigns in this company.... your time has run out. The Canadian Icon is here.... and i plan on seeing you all... very VERY soon! :::Daniel Cruz displays his arrogant grin for a second time as he walks off from the camera's focus. The camera slowly rotates back to the showcase of accomplishes and displays a case that is completely empty. As the camera zooms in on a gold name plate above the case, we see that the engraving on the name plate has three letters already engraved..... GCW Scene Ends:::
