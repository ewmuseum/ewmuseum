Date: Today “This week I’m faced with a grave challenge. Being able to walk away from that fight last week was almost impossible. But stepping into the ring this week is almost unimaginable. Kyo took a lot out of me. Hell he may have taken just about everything out of me. But when it comes down to it, I retained the title, my title. But some how this week at World Wide 61 I am faced with not only match for my title and Jayce Bradley’s Cruiserweight Belt, I’m also placed in a Battle Royal for the contender ship of the number one belt. This week I face the grave danger of my belt being taken away from me. Title vs. Title a match I would seemingly be eager for any other week. But this week I fear the result is not going to be one I cherish. I fear I might be losing my title, not gaining a grasp on a second. Any other week my confidence would be thru the roof, followed closely behind by my excitement for newly found gold. But this might be one task, let me rephrase that one risk I might not be able to face the challenge of.” Posted by Peter Vetra 2-09-07 @ petervetra.com/blog Inspiration: “Only those who dare to fail greatly can ever achieve greatly.” Robert F. Kennedy Mood: Nervous Song of the Moment: Memories by Emerson The submit button had been pressed the world would once again get a chance to enter the head of Peter Vetra. I always wondered what my obsession was with these blogs, but it was a way to express my feelings not only to my family and friends, although now a days they are a few and far to come. But also with my fans, some might sit back and wonder why I would do such a thing because it also gives my opponents an advantage. Well truth be told they need all the advantage they can get. I sat in another hotel looking for something to do. Posting those blogs always passed the time but once I was done there really wasn’t left for me to do. I mean I could look up the competition’s recent results and download the transcript or the show itself but honestly the only competition is PRIME and I despise them. Some might say FUSE but I find that to be more of a joke then a real member of competition. So with that I shut the lap top down walked over to the mini refrigerator and pulled out one of my energy drinks. “Cincinnati, Ohio just another stop in many of my tours,” I opened the top and took a sip, I let out a little chuckle. In my head I was reminiscing about the olden days when I was on the road. If I wasn’t stoned or drunk there was something wrong. “Those might have been the golden days but hell I am glad I am not on that shit anymore.” I walk over to my bed lay down and start to close my eyes. Shaking the sleep of I look over at the clock and notice that it is only 8:30 pm, “I really need to get a life.” I look over into the corner and notice that my Hardcore Title is slung over the chair in the corner. My eyes start to close but once again I shake my head vigerously, I need to stay awake. I slap myself upside my head a couple of times, once to the left and once to the right, “there that should do it!” Still feeling a little sleepy I decide to get up and go for a walk with my drink in hand.
