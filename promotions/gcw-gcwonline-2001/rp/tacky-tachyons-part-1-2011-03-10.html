Tacky-Tachyons Part 1

10 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/11 The scene opens in beautiful sunny Beverly Hills, California in the midst of breath taking multimillion dollar homes as a gray metallic Scion xB pulls up to the curb and comes to a rest. A moment passes and the door finally swings open, and out steps the long dreadlocks of Slider. He closes his door and walks to the back of his car opening the back hatch. Slider reaches in pulling out a midsized cardboard box placing it on the sidewalk for a moment, and then closes the back of the car. Sly arms the alarm and the car beeps in conformation and Slider picks up his package and heads towards the house. As Slider makes him way up the drive way a neighbor watering his plants yells a friendly greeting over the hedge. Mr. Hobbes: "Sylvester! How have you been, my boy?" Slider smiles as he sees the kind older gentlemen and decides to approach the hedges. Slider: "Hey Mr. Hobbes, I’ve been good. How are those Grandkids?" Mr. Hobbes: "Oh they’re great. They’ve been spending a lot of time with us lately. In fact they were watching your cousin on the TV the other night. Little Calvin was disappointed to see Brad lose." Sly lets out a tired disappointed sigh. Slider: "Me too…" Mr. Hobbes: "I wish my grandson would be better at picking his heroes, but what can you do? So how’s your cousin holding up? I haven’t seen much of him since his loss." Slider: "Well you know how when he won his first match he sang it in the streets?" Mr. Hobbes: "Uh huh." Slider: "Same sort of thing just to the other extreme." Mr. Hobbes: "Ah well, little Calvin wanted to make sure I send him our best, and hopefully he’ll feel better and get back to his feet." Slider holds up his box. Brad: "Don’t worry Mr. H, I picked him up a little something that should lift his spirits." Mr. Hobbes: "That’s good, if you got time stop by later, we’ll watch the Lakers and have some brews." Slider: "Maybe Mr. H, depends on the amount of damage I’m dealing with in there." Mr. Hobbes chuckles. Mr. Hobbes: "I understand, good luck, and don’t let him push you around. You do way too much for him, just so he can turn around and treat you so badly." Slider: "I know, you have a good day Mr. Hobbes." Mr. Hobbes: "You too, kiddo." Mr. Hobbes walks towards his house shutting off his water and disappears into the side yard. Slider takes a deep breath and proceeds to the porch of the large multimillion dollar home. Slider unlocks the door with his key and enters the house. As he enters the foyer Slider yells out. Slider: "Hey Cuz!" Slider walks from foyer to the kitchen that flows right into living room where "The SoCal Prophet" sits watching a Sci-fi drama. Slider sets his box on the kitchen island and heads to the living room. He approaches Brad sitting in his lazy boy from behind. Slider: "Hey Cuz, what are you watching?" Brad: "Don’t know… needed a break… I was watching like twelve hours worth of match footage." Slider: "Of your match with Theo?" Brad: "Everything…" Slider: "All your matches?" Brad: "Starting with my Indy days." Bradley presses play on the remote and the sci-fi drama is replaced with a match from Brad’s RTW days. Bradley watches a moment. Brad: "You know you could tell from the very beginning I was something special. I wasn’t some skirt clad idiot with sticky fingers, a homely jolly green girlfriend, and title I don’t deserve." Slider is beginning to understand. Slider: "Is this what this funk is all about? You had one bad night against Theo Palmer and Toga had a great night winning the hardcore title? Is it really that big of a deal?" Brad: "Is it that big of a deal? For over a year in POW I kept that little spaz under my boot where he belongs, knowing that full well that the slightest hint of success out of that fucktard’s sad little career would mark the end of the wrestling business as we know it, and low and behold, Toga won his first title and the seas have begun to boil, the magnetic poles are starting to shift, and the ground has begun to tremble, and yet everyone is just accepting that little skirt wearing dullard is a GCW Hardcore Champion!" Slider decides to change the subject as he makes his way back to the kitchen grabbing his box. Slider: "Listen Cuz, I know you’re bummed, and nothing I can say could make you feel better." Brad: "I’ll say…" Slider: "…so instead I got you a little something." Slider walks back to the living room handing Cruz the box. Cruz looks at the box then back to Slider unsure what to think. Cruz hands proceeds to work open box as tears at the tape holding it together. Slider: "It isn’t much, but I stopped at one of your neighbor’s homes, the ‘Neryses up the block. They were having an estate sale." Cruz begins stripping the tape off the box. Brad: "Ben Nerys, the physicist?" Slider: "Yeah big trekkie too, know him?" Brad: "Met him during the 4th July block party that Crystal forced me to attend. Guy wouldn’t stop talking my ear off about theories of strings and loops… it was like Nova on public broadcast, without the benefit of a mute button, so why the estate sale? Ben kick the bucket?" Slider: "No he was committed to mental health clinic." Brad: "Oh, the loony bin. Maybe he can finally work out the conflicts between his loop and string theories and mix them together and make himself nice necklace." Slider doesn’t care much for Brad’s off color remarks, but is quickly distracted seeing Cruz finally pry open the flaps of the box. Cruz’s eyebrow rises as he looks down into the box. He reaches in pulling out what looks to be a glass pyramid with a chromed metal frame. Cruz turns it upside down looking at the metal triangular plate with a small black button. Bradley looks to Slider who simple nods with a excited expression on his face. Slider: "Go ahead push it." Brad places his thumb of the black button pressing it. The small pyramid suddenly lights up glowing a pale blue as it lightly hums. Brad notes it’s slowly becoming warmer in his hand. Bradley sets it on his coffee table. Brad: "You got me a paperweight?" Slider: "Paper-? No, no it’s a ‘Tachyon Emitter’." Brad: "Tachyon Emitter?" Slider: "Yes!" Brad: "Which does?" Slider's hand dives into his pockets looking for something. Slider: "One sec I wrote it down…" Sly pulls out a small pocket sized notebook and flips through a few pages. Slider: "Ah, here we are! It Emits… Tachyons… … … neat right?" Brad looks at Slider in disbelief and turns back to Emitter just in time to see the light turnoff and fall silent. Slider: "Ya like it, Cuz?" Brad opens his mouth but his words seemingly fail him and he says nothing. Rather than drag this on Brad just nods. Slider: "Awesome! I’ll tell you want, seeing as you’re starting to feel better, why don’t I go and pick up some Chinese food and we can binge on episodes ‘Firefly'. Slider is about to head out just a he remembers something important. Slider: "Hey Cuz, just so you know… the lady that sold the emitter to me had some very specific safety precautions that were worth noting. Slider refers to his notes." Slider: "Make sure not to expose the emitter to extreme temperatures, and radiation like microwaves." Brad: "Yeah, yeah I also won’t feed it after midnight." Slider: "Alright, I’ll be back." Slider walks out leaving the kitchen. A few moments pass and the door can be heard opening and then closing behind Slider as he leaves. Brad: "Idiot!" Brad slumps back into his chair. Brad: "I don’t need some damn geometric paperweight. I need my edge back!" Brad glances back at the television screen as his passed highlights continue to play. Brad: "If only I knew all this wasn’t for nothing. If only I knew for sure that I was going to make history. That’s what I really want." Brad looks down at the emitter. Brad: "Instead I got this hunk of crap." Brad picks it up. Brad: "What am I supposed to do with this? Dumb myself down to Slider’s level just to be in awe of its ‘glowy shininess’. I don't know, but I know what I’m 'NOT' supposed to do with it.. expose it heat or-" Bradley’s domineer brightens into a devious smile. He turn around to face the kitchen looking at his large counter where his microwave oven. Brad: "-Microwaves." Bradley grabs the Emitter and walks into his kitchen. He slowly walks around his island stopping in front of his microwave. He opens the microwave oven door and presses the button on the emitter before placing inside. He closes the door but can still see the blue glow through the plastic window. Cruz ponders what time to set. Brad: "When in doubt, go with popcorn." Bradley sets the time to popcorn and follows up by pressing start. The little microwave roars to life as the emitter spins inside. Cruz is smiling ear to ear as the metal in the emitter instantly starts to react with sudden sparks that dance over the small pyramid. Suddenly large surges of electricity grips the emitter followed by bright white flashes. Bradley reaches to stop the microwave but receives a slight shock causing him to pull his hand back. The white flashes begin to strobe and become disorienting. Cruz tries to once gain to shut the microwave off, but feels as though a force was pushing him back. The strobe of white light becomes for frequent and gradually becomes a solid blinding white light drowning everything thing out. . . . Cruz wakes up face down on the floor in a pile of dirt rather than the cool granite floor of his kitchen. Realizing this Cruz’s eyes open widely and quickly gets to his feet. His vision is blurry and he’s having a hard time making anything out. He rubs his eyes and eventually they’re able to focus, but what he sees is not his kitchen, but instead instead a large un-landscaped dirt lot. He stand there confused seemingly in the middle of nowhere. Cruz looks to the sky to see a gloomy gray drab skyline packed with gray swirling clouds. Brad: "I’m in the middle of nowhere with an overcast of dark gloomy weather… how did I get to NorCal? "Silly, you’re not in Northern California!" Cruz spins around looking for the person who said that, but sees no one. Brad: "Who said that?" "Well, I did of course." Brad keeps probing the general area with eyes not seeing anyone. Brad: "…and you are?" "Me." Brad: "… and that would be?" "Me… Is it really such a hard concept to grasp? I am ‘me’, you are ‘you’, and we are ‘us’. It’s simple really." Brad looks rather annoyed and confused by this whole mess. Brad: "Alright we’ve established that you’re ‘you’ and I’m ‘me’." "Well technically I established I’m ‘me’ and your ‘you’, but I suppose It’s all really relative. I just love splitting hairs" Brad: "How about I split your skull!" The voice chuckles aloud. "That maybe difficult considering I don’t exactly have a ‘skull’ at the moment." Brad lets off an annoyed sigh. Brad: "Can you at least tell me where I am." "I would have thought that was obvious my young friend. You’re nowhere… but don’t worry … you’re not alone… you have me … and we’re going to have so much fun together."
