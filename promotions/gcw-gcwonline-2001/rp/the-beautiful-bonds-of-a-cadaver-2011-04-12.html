The Beautiful Bonds of a Cadaver

12 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/12/11 The Beautiful Bonds of a Cadaver Forrest Lawn Gravestone. Engraved, John J. Swinn. 1958 - 1988 "Here lies a father, a husband, and a damn good man." Thunder roars behind the ghostly clouds, above the granite tombstones, and over Covina Hills. The storms in California continue to roll in with such force and ferociousness But that doesn't stop me from seeing you No, nothing in this world ever could. Whether I want it to or not. To my father To the man I wish was here today Standing beside his only boy Watching every small step I make Taking pride in every big leap I take I miss you, dad I love you, dad. Your substances They Ripped you from the stickiness of a candy indulgent boy's small hands Those hands now large, calloused and cold due to lack of warmth, guidance and care. Your warmth. Your guidance. Your care. You are no longer here to clutch my palm into yours. Forever in the ground, you now lay And for now, on the surface I'll stay Until we meet again My father Forever in my heart Forever a part of my soul. I gazed up into the dreary clouds Painted over the once blue sky I kneel, knee deep in the slimy auburn soil Thinking Pondering. What if I came home that night? Why did I have to leave? Had I not, dad would still be alive. Damn his weakness Damn that needle. Staring into the engraved tombstone Reading the epitaph, over..and over. I finally push my head up toward a hint of beauty A whiff of good My wife My heroin in white satin armor She keeps me from falling off the edge She keeps me smiling, and happy. She keeps me loved. When I fall into a deep, dark ditch; she rescues me with her long, and gorgeous brunette locks of hair When I stop smiling, she puts on her Jester's hat and dances a good comical role until it makes me cackle till' death do us part And for that, I always feel loved. And with love, I feel human. Sane. We're incredibly different I cheat She plays by the rules I lie She tells the God's honest truth I'm scum She's pure. I'm imperfect, to say the least. But I suppose, we're perfectally imperfect. And I'm fine with that "Why do I put myself through this, Dar?" I gazed up into Darlene's eyes Almost seemingly at mercy For there I kneel, right beside her feet In front of a grave site that could very well be my own. "B'cause you love him. He's your dad. There's nothing wrong with what you're doing, Jimmy." Love? Only she could make the most simplistic answer seem so complex How is it I love a man that I never knew? And why is it that I feel this..bond? As unnatural as it is, it's a bond. Me and a cadaver. A cadaver containing shares of my DNA. "Ahh, let's go." Death. The look of it. The smell of it. The utter depression makes my stomach turn. "You sure, babe?" Hey eyes, her eyes. Twinkle in a grave site She makes this place seem..so alive, so alive. "Yeah, there just isn't a point." I push myself off of the top of the granite tombstone Her eyes follow up as I glare down at the tombstone. The rock. That's all we're remembered by in the end. A bitter pebble wedged into the mud. "Not like he's gonna fuckin'... reach out from the maggots and dirt and climb his ass outta' that hole to tell me he's a'right. That everythings gonna' be okay. Nah, once you die..you're dead, man. Cut and dry, ain't nothin' to it." That's my belief That's my word That's my gospel. As a devout Christian, She loves the lord. Follows his ever word Just as much as she reads it. The disappointed glare in her eyes Though she says not a word Her eyes speak her inner emotions My reaction did not leave her unsettled No, this happens every time I visit Dad. I kick I scream I whine Like a child. "Which is why I just don't understand how you can stand there and think otherwise. I mean..really? God?! Divine powers?! Who are you kidding? Dr. Fucking Seuss could've easily have written the Bible!" Still, she says not a word. Just a pity smile Or perhaps she knows something I don't Maybe she's just following her lord's word. "I'm sorry..I just, can we go?" She fixes her soft, frigid yet soothing hand upon my cheek Her blush lips gravitate toward mine Just as we are opposite Like magnets, our lips bond together. A kiss as tender as a soft, subtle breeze in the fall. Compassionate Loving. We depart. Leaving the gloomy clouds to keep a watchful eye over Dad. Hoping the rain feeds the vegetation surrounding the grave site. Hoping for just a little bit of life.. For the damn good man buried underneath the mud. For the damn good man that I once called: "Daddy." For the damn good man that made me. Me. The piece of shit that I am today.
