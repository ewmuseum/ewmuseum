....A Threat....

18 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 18, 2005 “I can’t think straight. All this glory got to my head you know. I saw everything coming my way, I thought I was on the top of my game. But my feet unknowingly fell out from beneath me.” Caldera- “Come on Mike, don’t be so harsh on yourself. Get over the Iron Man Match. You were up against the Iron Man himself. He’d never lost in that environment before, but you were the closest to breaking that streak.” “I just wanted it so bad you know.” Caldera- “Wanted what?” “I just wanted to be GCW’s icon. GCW’s headliner. I wanted to be the best. I thought I could prove the odds wrong and hold my weight for once. I feel as if I were just a jobber on a lucky winning spree. Like everyone always says Steven, I’m merely Cruiserweight Material.” Caldera- “Listen Mike, I had you put that weight on to get you away from what was holding you back. The Cruiserweight division held you back. You are destined for so much more right now. Believe it or not Mike, your name is becoming a Legacy here in GCW. I don’t know anyone with more Determination, more Courage, ah hell”.more BALLS, who has been or run through this company. You have a name to uphold. YOU HAVE A GOD DAMN REPUTATION TO CONTINUE! I’m not gonna see you hang your head in my presence one more time! Cause damn it Mike, you’re a warrior! You will fight the good fight. You are becoming something you and I never thought you could be?..” (Steven’s eyes grow wide with pride as he spits out a word I never thought possible for me to be labeled as.) Caldera-?”..A Threat?..” (To the sound of this, my heart skips a beat, my neck now pumping with adrenaline, My head comes lifting from the solemn position it once slumped in. I have been enlightened, and for once in my life, I felt something truly come over me in a cold shiver??Pride?..)
