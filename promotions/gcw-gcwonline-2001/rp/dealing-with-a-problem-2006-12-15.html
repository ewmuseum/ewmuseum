Dealing with a problem.

15 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.15.06 Before you lay the expanse of a large casino bustling before you. The casino is active and is lively and three security guards are escorting a man to an elevator as above the elevator is a huge glass window as a large man is shadowed in it from some lights behind. The elevator opens and the man seen is Shaman with his hands clasped behind his back as he looks down at the casino below. He speaks as the security guards release the gentleman. “Have a seat Vinnie. I am sure our visit will be enlightening.” Shaman turns around slowly and looks at Vinnie with eyes that would stare coal to diamonds. He walks to his desk and picks up a few security pictures that are sitting there. It clearly shows Vinnie tampering with some slot machines and then he turns another few as Vinnie is obviously seen counting cards. “Seems to me Vinnie your bosses are up to their old tricks. You’re trying scam the casino again when I warned you last time. I told your bosses they weren’t getting their fingers into this pie. Now they are sending you into sabotage my games and trying to cheat your way into some of my money. You see Vinnie I am a nice guy up to a point. There is the phone call your boss in. I have a few words to say while you make sure he gets here.” Shaman turns the phone on his desk around and shoves it towards Vinnie. “You better make the call because your little worthless thing called physical well being is at stake.” Shaman stands up and looks at Vinnie and then waits as he taps his fingers on his chair nearby which he spins around the back towards himself. Vinnie dials the phone and then holds it as his hand is visibly shaking. “Hey boss come to the Spirit Casino. Yeah he is waiting to have a meeting with you. Ye..Yeah all went according to plan heh.” Hanging up the phone Shaman looks at Vinnie and grins walking over to him and reaches out and grabs him by the throat and lifts him up and pulls him close and looks him straight in the eyes. “You seem to have gotten in my way this time Vinnie so I will do you a big favor this time. I am going to give you a plane ticket to Jersey and to never return. In fact the plane is waiting for you. Gentlemen escort Vinnie here to the airport outside of town. The one with the little prop DC-10 waiting for him and be sure he enters with nothing and I mean nothing. Also be sure he gets there soon. I don’t care on what he looks like or is feeling when he gets there. Just be sure he is alive when he lands in New Jersey. Go away Vinnie I am waiting for your boss Tony.” Shaman turns and walks to the desk and bends down slightly and taps the computer bringing up his emails and then turns and watches the security guards roughly grab Vinnie as he panics and thrashes and the guards shake their heads as one pulls out a hand taser and shocks him as Vinnie falls limp on the floor as they both reach down and grab him by the arms. Shaman looks up and notices this. “Guys make sure the patrons do not see him either. The back door is free and there is a van waiting there.” Shaman reaches over and pulls his chair over to where he is and sits down leaning back as he rubs his chin looking at the screen. “Vivica J Valentine that is a surprise, Well Vivica my gripe isn’t with you truthfully it is with your stable mate. You know the quiet guy. The one with some psychotic tendencies. Disturbing aspects almost as much as I am within my own sadistic tendencies?” Shaman grins and leans further back kicking his feet up on the desk and places his hands behind his head. “Anyways back to our match up. I have seen you have had your ups and downs in the matches within the world of GCW. Truthfully I don’t care. Really I could careless. You are my opponent and I will not treat you any less than I would any other opponent. If you bleed you bleed. If you get hurt no skin off my back, I am going into the ring to do a job. It is business plain and simple.” Shaman leans forward looking at his screen. Then he shakes his head removing his feet from his desk. He slowly stands up and looks out over the casino floor again as six security guards go walking to the main doors. The two way on his desk chirps as someone speaks. “They are here boss.” Shaman reaches down and lifts the radio up and speaks. “Show them up here I am sure they have a few things to say. I am curious as to what Tony has to say. ? Shaman watches as a man in his 40’s walks forward with two guys in grey suits by his side and six of the Casino’s own guards wearing black suits with ear pieces and looking a lot like secret service guys. Shaman observes and then sits back down. “Well that was obviously what he expected. Anyway back to you Vivica J Valentine. You’re a great athlete and a respected woman wrestler and you have achieved a lot in your goals and achievements. I respect where you are going and where you have been.” Shaman clasps his hands together as he turns his chair towards the elevator. “Don’t get me wrong here. The respect stops at the curtains of the ramp way. When it comes straight down to it, it is going to be strictly in the ring business. I do what ever is necessary to win the match. You can be ten foot tall and bulletproof for all I care doesn’t mean I am going to take it any easier on you. When I enter the ring I change. I become a beast of rage. A warrior who controls every aspect of what is going on even when you think you have the upper hand I change my tactics and move forward and start again with a new plan. I will see you soon Vivica I am sure you are wondering on what is going to happen in the ring, but rest assured you will wake up eventually. The nightmares usually end at some point.” Smiling sadistically he looks to the door as Tony enters and Shaman motions him over to an empty chair as his own personal protection step in with him and takes up positions around him on his right and left. Shaman leans back in his chair and places his hands together and listens to Tony speak as he smiles. “Shaman I knew you’d come around eventually and see things our way. I am prepared to offer you a little less than what you paid for to get this establishment. Now let me tell you the simple demands. Me and the boys here want you out of this office now. This will be our little party spot and secret endeavor. All paperwork will be drawn up in the morning and we want you out of Vegas by tomorrow afternoon comprende?” Shaman smiles as he looks at them. “Anything else?” Tony looks at Shaman enraged as he looks at him raising his voice. “Are you hard of hearing your freaking moolie” I said out now and do not comeback or things are gonna get dangerous for you.” Shaman shakes his head and looks at Tony. Tony gets angry and looks at his two personal guards. “Escort this man out of my building now.” Snapping his fingers Tony’s guards go to reach in their jackets but several clicking sounds are heard as the guards freeze in their tracks and Shaman’s casino security had drawn their own guns and have them pointed at Tony and his security entourage. Shaman grins and stands up and looks at Tony and then walks in front of the window and looks out at the casino. He then turns and looks at Tony. “Tony your boys should drop their weapons really. It would be good for their health.” Tony motions for his guys to drop the weapons as the remove the guns and drop them to the floor as one of Shaman’s security guards walk over and pick them up and Shaman motions for Tony too. The guards search Tony and his guards and pull two more guns and three knives from them and then Shaman motions for the wallets and removes all of their cash from them and drops it on the desk and then smiles as he cuts their credit cards. Shaman looks at Tony and smiles. “Tony your old days of bullying people don’t work here anymore in Vegas. You should know your way out of your element now. Your being here has nothing to do with what you have been trying to bully me into doing. In fact it has everything to do with the tactics you have gone about trying to do.” Shaman stops as Tony raises his arms and speaks. “I have no clue as to what your talking about. I am a legitimate businessman and I was out right looking to buy this casino which is nowhere near the top in Vegas.” Shaman clears his throat abruptly and lifts a magazine off his desk and throws it into Tony’s lap. He smiles and speaks. “You see right there on the cover Tony where it reads Spirit Casino a gold mine made by a professional wrestler. You see Tony I have been onto you for sometime. In fact the locals here had warned about your little split from Mafia stuff to get your own power in Vegas. Truth is Tony the Mob hasn’t been strong footing it around here since the late sixties to early seventies.” Tony looks at the magazine and is beginning to get nervous obviously. He begins to run his mouth to Shaman who turns and walks to Tony as Tony stands up and points at him as he speaks. “Do you know who I am you Native American reject” I control this area and I control you. You are nothing and will be nothing i?” Shaman suddenly backhands Tony up and over the chair Tony holds his mouth and jaw in shock as Shaman walks around the chair slowly and grabs Tony by the throat and drags him to his feet and then releases the throat and grabs the tie and pulls him in close nose to nose. “Tony shut your old pie hole up and listen. You own and you control nothing. You have no business footing in the city and you are less than anything you thought you’d ever be. You’re a thug and a bully and nobody is backing you. You see your boys here they already came to me today and told me about your bullying plans. Your boy Vinnie was caught in the casino trying to cheat and undermine my casino.” Shaman shoves Tony down onto the floor and then looks at him as he looks at his two guards. “Gentlemen your services are terminated please leave my casino where there is a cab outside waiting to take you to the airport for you to go to New York. Thank you for your services.” Shaman turns and looks at Tony after the two men leave as Shaman grins and snaps his fingers as two guards walk over and begins tearing Tony’s cloths off and leave Tony in shredded tatters of clothing. Shaman walks back and forth in front of Tony. He looks at him and then grins. “Tony your going for a walk in the desert. I have decided that your going to receive some of your own strong arm tactics. You see my people have a little thing we used to enjoy doing to our enemies. Not specifically my tribe, but you see I am going to modify this little bit of punishment. You see there is a helicopter on the roof waiting to drop you off in the deserts nearby. I am going to give you two canteens of water. It is more than enough to get back to the city and for you to think about what you were trying. So i am being merciful here. I want you to know to never step foot into my casino. That will be evidently clear I assume. You trouble me again and things will get much much worse.” Shaman sits in his chair and looks at Tony. “Get him out of here. I am tired of looking at the piece of shit.” After several security guards walk out with Tony Shaman turns and looks at the last two remaining guards and nods to them. “Tell the FBI where they can find him and let them know he is all theirs. Let the New Jersey guys know where Vinnie is and the same with the New York guys.” The guys nod as Shaman turns and goes and sits back down and looks at the computer screen once again. He leans back in his chair propping his feet back up over his desk and places them down upon it. “You see Vivica J Valentine. I am a man with many plans going on all at once. I think way ahead. In a fight I know what I am going to do. How I am going to hit and where. I have thought out my scenarios clearly and will be watching each and every movement you make carefully so I can adjust my time well ahead of time. Everything I do is thought through and the outcomes known regardless whether they are good or bad. I don’t care what people think or do. In the wrestling ring I do what I want when I want and how I want. I hunt Mayhem and I do not stop nearly as fast as those would think. I back down from nobody and that is why I have been known as the most sadistic son of a bitch you will ever know. Sleep well but not too soundly. The nightmare has come early and after our match it will be over.” Shaman stands up from the sitting position after he kicks his feet off the desk. Standing he walks over to the glass windows overlooking the casino floor he watches closely and observes the patrons below enjoying their time in the casino.
