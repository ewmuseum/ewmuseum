Brotherhood

12 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 12, 2009 Brotherhood is a concept that has been around since the birth of humanity, even dating back to the very first man and woman. According to ancient Jewish and Christian religions, Cain and Abel were the first and second sons of Adam and Eve, making the duo the first historically recorded brothers. The Islamic Holy Book, the Qur’an contains the story as well. Though in the Qur’anic account, the brothers’ names are changed to Qabil (Cain) and Habil (Abel). In the New Testament of the Greek Bible, Cain is referred to as εκ τοu πονηροu; or "from the evil one." in some translations. In others, it translates to "of the evil one." Some people believe Cain to be the son of the tempter, the serpent; the same being who tempted Eve into breaking God’s law. Jewish legend states that the Garden of Eden's serpent (Hebrew: nahash נחש) was the father of Cain. Regardless of one's beliefs, Cain is a crop farmer and Abel is a shepherd in all versions of the tale. Cain is viewed as sinful, having committed the first murder by killing his younger brother after God rejected his offerings of produce but accepted Abel's offerings of animal sacrifice. After the murder of his brother, God cast Cain out into the world alone, cursing him with alienation and the task of walking the earth for the rest of his life as a restless nomad. Never again would he be able to successfully farm the land, nor would he be able to have human contact without being persecuted. At the same time, those who persecuted him would not be allowed to kill him, and if they did, they would 'suffer vengeance seven times over,' according to the Holy Bible. The oldest known copy of this Biblical story is from the 1st Century Dead Sea Scrolls. Appearing in numerous other texts, the story of Cain and Abel is the subject of several different interpretations and beliefs. Due to being the very first historically recognized murder victim, Abel is often considered as the first martyr; whereas Cain, being the first murderer, is sometimes seen as an ancestor of evil. Some scholars think that the pericope may have been based around a Sumerian story about the conflict between nomadic shepherds and settled farmers. Others believe it could refer to the days of agriculture replacing the ways of the hunter-gatherer. No matter where the story originated or which language it's written in, the fact remains that the story of Cain and Abel is a tragic tale of what happens when jealousy runs rampant and mankind covets what they cannot have. Whether it's true or not remains unknown, but what has been discerned is that each and every one of us can and should learn from Cain's mistakes and strive each and every day not to repeat them. The bond between brothers is strong and at times can seem unbreakable, but it’s certainly not infallible. Then the Lord said to Cain, "Where is your brother, Abel?" "I know not," he replied. "Am I my brother’s keeper?" God then said, "What have you done? Your brother's blood cries out to Me from the ground! So now you will be cursed from the ground that opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood you have shed. If you work the land, it will never again give you its yield. You will be a restless wanderer on the earth." Cain answered The Lord, "My punishment is too great to bear! Since You are banishing me today from the soil and I must hide myself from Your presence and become a restless wanderer on the earth, whoever finds me will kill me." The Lord then replied to him, "In that case, whoever kills Cain will suffer vengeance seven times over." God then placed a mark on Cain so that whoever found him would not kill him. Then Cain went out from The Lord's presence and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden. - Genesis 4:9-16 Ring! Ring! Ring! The sound of a cell phone's ringtone shatters the silence of the early morning hours. The shrill pitch of the music is enough to wake even the dead, figuratively speaking. As the device rings and vibrates against the hard wood of the bedside table, a sleeping body stirs beneath the sheets. An arm flails lazily at the table, trying in vain to slap the snooze button on the alarm clock only to find that the sounds aren’t coming from his alarm clock. Ring! Ring! Ring! Finally sitting up and tossing the covers back, AXEL Action groans and picks up the ringing phone. "Ugh, it’s too fucking early for this," he thinks to himself as he flips the phone open and places it to his ear. "Hello?" AXEL's voice is shaky and the tone is that of slight frustration. It was five o' clock in the morning, and he didn’t have to be up until nine for his autograph signing in the hotel lobby. After a moment of silence and quite a bit of listening, he finally recognizes the voice on the other end of the line as that of his older brother. "Hey, Ace. It’s been a long time, bro. How's life been treating you?" After a few moments of listening to the words being spoken to him on the other end of the line, AXEL's eyes widen in shock. Something Ace said had caught him off guard, and he struggled to find the right words to say. "You're what?! You drove more than 1500 miles just to watch my title defense? What would possess you to do such a thing?" The idea that his older brother would do something like that was just something he couldn't wrap his mind around, especially given the fact that they hadn't seen or spoken to one another since June of 2008. Feeling slightly confused about the situation, he simply shrugs it off and listens to what Ace has to say. "Alright, then, we’ll get together after the meet and greet with the fans is over. There's something I wanted to talk to you about, anyway. I’ll see you in a little while. Bye." He takes the phone away from his ear, gazing at the screen for a few moments. "Call ended,", it read, the words soon leaving the screen as its light fades. Folding the phone together and placing it back onto the nightstand, AXEL takes a deep breath and sprawls out on the bed before pulling the sheets over his head. "I can already tell that this day is going to be one I won't soon forget.", he says before closing his eyes and allowing himself to drift off into sweet, sweet slumber once more. At nine A.M. sharp, the buzzing of the alarm clock echoes throughout the room and resonates within the sleeping AXEL's ears. Waking up with a start, he sits up in bed on full alert as he looks around the room. Once he realizes that it's only the alarm clock making that awful racket, he rubs the back of his head and laughs at himself for getting so worked up. Tossing the sheets back, he swings his legs over the side of the bed and puts his feet on the floor. He yawns and stretches before shaking his head to rid himself of the grogginess he was currently suffering. He really wasn't looking forward to the meet and greet. The thought of signing autographs for hours upon hours didn’t appeal to him at all. He knew that before all was said and done, he’d be lucky if he didn't contract carpal tunnel syndrome. Nonetheless, he was a man of the people and the GCW United States Champion. He had an image to uphold, and he fully intended to maintain it. He gathers his things together and makes himself look presentable, even applying his trademark face paint. Just as he's applying the finishing touches to his paint, he hears a light knock on his door. "Are you ready, AXEL? Your fans are waiting for you in the lobby." The voice that calls to him from behind the door is none other than that of James Bryan, GCW's play-by-play guy and the event co-coordinator for the meet and greet. Taking the United States Championship in hand and laying it over his shoulder, AXEL heads to the door and opens it, stepping out into the hallway. As he nears the top of the stairs, the sound of the fans' excitement rings within his ears. "I'll have hand cramps for a week, but just seeing how excited they are makes it all worth it. Well, JB, here goes nothing.” AXEL descends the stairs one by one, the crowd's excitement growing louder and louder the nearer he gets. Once he sets foot on the floor of the lobby, the waiting fans nearly blow the roof off of the building as they show their love and admiration for their hero, their United States Champion. Upon taking his seat, he raises a hand in acknowledgement of the throngs of people who had come to see him before laying his championship title out on the table out of the way. Taking his pen in hand, he starts autographing pictures for the fans, one by one. After about three hours of shaking hands with his fans and signing autographs, the line of people had thinned to one last fan. AXEL sat in his chair, opening and clenching his fist in an attempt to work the cramp out of it as his gaze was stolen by a hand being extended towards him. AXEL reaches out and takes hold of the offered hand, shakes it firmly and asks, "Would you like an autograph?" "Hello, AXEL." The familiarity of the voice catches AXEL's attention, and he gazes up at the man in front of him. The man standing before him is his older brother, Ace who was there to see AXEL, as promised. The GCW United States Champion rises from his seat and the two brothers embrace each other in a hug for the first time in over a year. Pulling out of the embrace, AXEL reaches down and takes his championship in hand, laying it over his shoulder once more. "I've got to go upstairs and put this back in my suitcase. You’re welcome to come, if you’d like. If not, you can wait down here. Either way, I'll only be a minute.", he says, heading back upstairs. Just a moment after AXEL re-enters his room, Ace appears in the doorway with a rather curious look on his face. Placing his championship back into his suitcase, AXEL closes and locks it before turning to face the older(by three years) of the two. "What's the matter, big brother? You seem worried about something." "You told me you needed to talk to me about something when we spoke on the phone earlier, but you didn't mention what it was. Is everything okay?" AXEL's expression changes rather quickly at the mention of the important matter he’d brought up earlier. He shakes his head as he takes a seat on the one chair in the room, motioning over to the bed. "You can sit there, if you'd like. I''m sorry there isn't another chair, there was only one chair in the room when I checked in." Ace nods and seats himself on the edge of the bed, crossing his arms over his chest as he looks to AXEL, trying to scan his face for any signs of what he might be thinking. Shifting his gaze to the floor momentarily, the younger of the two Action brothers finally manages to gather the courage to speak his mind. "Ace, I'm scared that I'm going to be a flop as United States Champion, just like I was as Television Champion. Winning it was simple. All I had to do was pin Tyson DeBough’s shoulders to the mat for three seconds or force him to submit. Now, not only do I have a gigantic bulls-eye on my forehead, so to speak, I’ve got to live up to the reputation of what a United States champion SHOULD be. I don’t know if I can handle all of the pressure." Ace sighs quietly as he listens intently to AXEL, a sympathetic smile curling his lips as he reaches over to pat his little brother on the shoulder. Though he tried to force it down, a slight chuckle emerged from within him as he listened to AXEL worry over what was practically nothing. "Is that what you're worried about? Listen, you may think you were a failure as Television Champion, but at least your entire career didn't flop. Remember when I won the OWA World Championship? The only reason I became champion was because Eli the Eliminator twisted his ankle and fell from the scaffold. Not only that, but I lost it back to him in a rematch the following week.” AXEL lifts his head to look Ace in the eyes, his head tilting from left to right as he tries to figure out just what his brother is getting at. He remembered those events well, and while they were rather humorous, he just wasn’t in a laughing mood at the moment. "You're twenty-seven years old, in peak physical condition and at the top of your game. Not only that, but you hold one of the most coveted championships in this industry. You didn't get to where you are by being a flop or failure. You are in the spot you're in because you bust your ass night in and night out to put on the best show you possibly can for your fans. Win, lose or draw, you've always given it one hundred and ten percent every time you step in the ring. That's what has brought you to this point in your career." "But Ace, I…", AXEL starts, but is interrupted by Ace. "There are no buts about it. There is no doubt in mine or anyone else's mind that you deserve to wear that belt around your waist. The wrestling business has granted you its favor. Take advantage of it while you can and be the best you can be, because in the blink of an eye, it'll turn its back on you. I'm a walking example of that; a thirty-one year old, washed-up has been whom everyone claimed had worlds of potential, but was never given the chance to shine. You have been given your chance. Stop whining, pick up your ball and run with it." Nodding affirmatively, AXEL manages a faint smile as he takes a moment to let everything sink in. Taking a deep breath, he rises to his feet and smooths out his jeans as if brushing the non-existent wrinkles out. "I suppose you're right." "You're damn right, I'm right! Now come on, let's go get something to eat. I'm starving!" As the two share a laugh, Ace slips his arm around AXEL's shoulders and the two exit the hotel room together; happily reunited and as close as ever. Much like God smiled on Abel and turned Cain away; the wrestling business looked favorably on AXEL and cast Ace aside. The difference is that since they were teenagers, the two Action boys have had no one but each other. The bond between two brothers is a very special bond and is only surpassed by that of a mother and child. If that bond is nurtured and given the time to grow properly, nothing can break it or tear it asunder, not even jealousy. Brotherhood (noun)- the condition or quality of being a brother or brothers.
