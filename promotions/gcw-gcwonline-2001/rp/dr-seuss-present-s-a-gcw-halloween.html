Kissing his mother, telling her he’ll be back She shrugged him off to go freebase some crack. The White house that belongs to Mr. Taylor, Zachariah Known worldwide for his LSD laced Jambalaya. Oh how much did Chrissy love this treat Last year he recalls Elephants with 6 feet. Next house on the block belonged to Christian Zenith Pardon my lisp, but this man has a small Penith. Known worldwide for his obsession with boys Luring them in with trinkets and toys. Remembering the pain Chrissy skips this place Off to the house of the man with the painted face. Mr. Mayhem wont you please get a clue People are no longer afraid of you. Shaman, big ugly Indian, who lost his land. This man says Metallica’s a metal band. Look at me Fee Fi Fo Fum The funny Indian, oh so dumb. The brick house, the family Ambrosi. That face on her head, oh so grossy. What happened to her the talent is lost? Her career now looks like a book by Faust. Oh yeah baby, bent over and lookin fine Oh my god, it’s Vivica J Valentine. What the hell, standing behind her is Kimbusa It looks like he’s balls deep in her Caboosa. Spray painting, B&E acting like they are four Let’s be honest, these two are a bore. Transformers, Pokemon, and Eeyore too. What will they do next? How about eat their own poo? Hey looks its Dorian Ryan Sam Wolack X Let’s guess which name he will use next. So confused on this supposed gimmick Sorry to say, even though I’m not a critic. Not black nor white, but something in between Next house belongs to GCW’s own resident bean. GCW icon that goes by Lestatt Knight Unfortunately owned by Mr. Jordan White. The next house is draped in a red bandana Feared in 4 states including Montana. Jay Terror had his chance and blew it. Grabbed Icebabe by the mouth and screwed it. GBJ are you Cheech or you Chong? Wow another wrestler hitting the bong. Unknown up until two weeks ago His career has 3 months left to go. After peeing in Tessa’s Mailbox The next house belongs to Clyde Fox. This part of the story is not very fun Because there’s nothing exciting Clyde has done. Fuck Clyde and his stupid brick place Let’s go bother the man they call Face. Who is this guy, and where is his career? The last house on the street is near. The last house resides the creepy Caldera Steve Always has some stupid trick up his sleeve. In charge of the Net’s biggest fed, Yes it’s true, Eva gave him head. Heading back home with a sack full of fun The world wishes Chrissy would play with a gun Looking to the sky, there is no more light Turn on the tube, time to enjoy Devil’s Night.
