A debutant Report

4 December 2006

Date: 4th December Is the End only the Beginning? A report by Justin Herron The End was a song by the Beatles which in the early Seventies signed off a great illustrious musical career. They bowed out from the world stage with grace and dignity, showing the world that throughout time they will not easily be forgotten. In March 2005 Wes Sanders exited from the WWA stage in a complete opposite to the Beatles, the last remaining segment saw one of the biggest fan favourites shoved into a boot of a car and carted off, never to be seen again. He was like so many wrestlers, forgotten in the shade. The dust had settled and neither Wes nor his kickboxing legacy made it through. Was Wes finished? At 29 without a job Wes began touring the Indy circuits once again looking to find a star among the space. Nothing came for over 9 months until Wes found GCW. Not an Indy fed oh no, Wes had once again found himself among the best, some of the most famous names state side. GCW or fully named Global Championship Wrestling have taken a chance on the high flying daredevil hoping that he can regain his once stirring form and entertain the crowds once again. I for one am looking forward to seeing the former number one contender in the WWA perform once again, seeing some of the most innovative offensive manoeuvres since Brett Hart back in the early 90?s. However this reporter is certainly concerned over a few things. Wes has now been out of action for almost a year, even though his training for one might still have carried on, the ring rust is bound to be present. His first match comes against a relatively experience GCW Rufio. At roughly the same height and weight both stars neither have speed or power advantage yet both pose two completely different styles, where Wes likes to stay on his feet using them as his two main weapons, Rufio likes to keep his opponents grounded, working on certain body parts hoping to retain a submission. This complication between the two varying styles could be the lead to a very interesting match but I have to say that I’m against Wes on his return, the odds are stacked against him and I feel that the lack of action will keep him out of the Win box. Next week I will hopefully get a chance to speak to Wes about his return to action. Justin signing out
