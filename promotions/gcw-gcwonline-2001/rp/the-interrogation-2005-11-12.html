The Interrogation

12 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-12-05 Before we get things started, let’s have a bit of a recap for those not really following everything that is going on. Jayce Bradley entered Global championship Wrestling, and came out of his debut match with a victory against Macavity Frost. The following week, Jayce was not on the card. That was not much of a big deal to Jayce. What was a big deal, was the week after that when he was placed in a match against a man by the name of Ehren Moore. Ehren Moore was some military punk who came out on top in their match, but that really is not the problem either. No, the REALLY big problem was that some high school jock came in at the same time as Moore and was handed a Television title shot. A Television title match in his debut match, when Jayce had been on the roster for three weeks and had a victory under his belt. Jayce felt short changed about the situation and tore through Varsity’s locker room and left nothing unturned. Jayce even went as far as destroying one of Varsity’s prize trophies from his high school days. Jayce enjoyed watching Varsity near tears about that, and ended up with a match against the title-less Varsity in a past episode of World Wide. Jayce come out of that match with a victory over Varsity’surely entitled to some sort of title match now. Of course, this is no fairy tale, and Jayce is not a hot princess that is destined to live happily ever after. No, Jayce was placed as Michael Stevens? first opponent in a Gauntlet match. Now as for Varsity, the man must be blowing the right people, as he was awarded with a Cruiserweight title match after losing to Jayce Bradley on the previous episode of World Wide. Has it even been mentioned that Varsity had not won one match prior to that Cruiserweight title match? Yes, I said “had”. Turns out, Varsity finally won a match. Varsity is now known as the Cruiserweight champion. Jayce had a bit more bothering him now, eating away at his insides like a Cancer. He was furious, especially after Varsity refused to fight back after Jayce landed a solid punch, that staggered Varsity back against a wall and busted his lip. Something else happened that night too. Something that may have gotten a bit more trouble for Jayce than he was expecting. This is where tonight’s story begins? “I’m not even sure why you brought me here.” Jayce Bradley sat on one end of a large oak table, his arms folded casually across his chest as he shook his head. On the opposite side of the table, there were two men who did not look to be in a good mood. One of the men, the balding one wearing the white shirt with gray pinstripes, was tapping a white handkerchief against his forehead to dab away the perspiration that was beading up. “We know what you did, punk,” the anger seemed to drip off of the words that escaped the balding man’s lips. In no way was this man playing any games with Jayce, but Jayce remained calm under the pressure and laughed it off. Waving his hand to dismiss any knowledge of what the men were talking to him about. “Do you think this is some sort of a game, son?” The other gentleman, the one with the wet look in his slicked back, light brown hair, spoke up in a quizzical manner. The sleeves of his powder blue, long sleeved, button-up dress shirt were rolled up above his elbows. The powder blue and yellow tie dangling about an inch over the table as he leans forward toward Jayce. Jayce leans forward as well, pressing his hands on the table in an aggressive manner that disturbs his interrogators a bit. “No, I don’t think this is a game. What I think, is that you guys are wasting my damn time.” “Wasting your time?” The balding man stands from his seat in a huff, attempting to get around Mr. Powder blue shirt. He is of course, stopped. Jayce just smiles as he leans back in his chair. “You little shit. You could have killed him.” Jayce stretches his arms out, waving them in front of himself. “Whoa’back it up. I could have killed who?” “Styles,” powder responds. “The kid you ran down with your car.” “Now wait a damn minute!” Jayce pops up from his seat, a bit taken aback by the whole line of remarks from his hosts. “I didn’t do a damn thing to anybody. I’m innocent I tell you.” Balding man shakes his head, a smile creasing his lips as he seems to enjoy what he is about to say. “We have witnesses that put you at the scene, and we have your little trophy, too.” “You got the wrong man?” “Not so much of a big shot now, eh,” the balding man replies as he dabs a bit more sweat from his brow. “Thing is, punk, we got witnesses that saw the whole thing. They even saw you toss that broken piece of trophy out the window.” With a slight chuckle, powder blue guy interrupts his partner’s statement. “You’re going down for this, punk.” Jayce pushes back in the chair, separating himself from the table before rising to his feet. The balding man hops to his feet, as powder blue shirt guy gets into a defensive position. “This has to be some sort of misunderstanding.” “Oh yeah, please resist,” the balding man says as he reaches toward his hip where his service pistol is located. In case you don’t know how to read between the lines’these men are police detectives. Powder blue places a hand on his partner’s, motioning towards the large mirror that takes up almost a full wall to their right. The balding officer sneers as he pulls his hand away, shaking his head at Jayce as he begins to speak, “We’re going to give you a few minutes to think about what you’ve done. Maybe you want to think about getting yourself an attorney.” “But I haven’t done anything,” Jayce argues, his voice raised a bit to the point where it may be considered yelling. “I’m innocent!” His words fall on deaf ears, as the two officers walk out of the room and slam the door shut behind them.
