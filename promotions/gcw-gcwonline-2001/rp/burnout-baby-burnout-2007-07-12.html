Burnout Baby Burnout

12 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 12th Phil Atken Esq, not to mention BA, was sitting in his newly refurbished New York apartment. The reason for the refurbishment was of course due to his and Dr. Dick Dirkwood PhD and superagent complete destruction of anything in their sites after their recent series of career humiliations. Both could not believe the embarrassment that happened to them, Phil had never been fired in his life, he was the destroyer of worlds. Well if "worlds" means wrestling promotions. He brought death to those who mildly irritated them, he wasn't a man to be ushered out the back door in a hush hush moment. Phil sat, unemployed for the first time in his life against his will. He had went into hiding in the past, he lived the life of a hermit for a good few months but those were all his personal decisions, never had this sort of decision been made for him. It was all so new to him and Phil as quite possibly one of the most ignorant people in the existence of the known and the unknown universe was not at all used to dealing with the unknown. Almost comatose looking to an outsider looking in, Phil leaned back in his chair as thoughts of his failings raced through his mind. "Why am I not just getting acknowledged as the best talent in the history of wrestling and just getting handed awards?" "It can't be burnout can it? I'm still in my prime, how can it possible be burnout? A talent like me comes once in a lifetime, like Jesus! I can't possibly be going downhill! It just can't be the case." He couldn't do anything but think of his failings. These feelings were completely new to him, he never sulked in the past. He was god's gift to the industry from his charming personality alone how could the old "nudge and wink" technique have disastrous results? He used to get out of evening the biggest scandals he caused in the past through a mixture of persistence and charisma. Also the fact he had his bosses wrapped up around his pinky didn't harm him much either. However this time was different, this time he hadn't even caused a scandal, not even a tiny one involving a dock, a sailor and a bevy of whores. This time he was just simply told his services were no longer needed and they wished him the best. No hours of lawyer lectures, no punishment title losses and heck he didn't even get a chance to cause internet uproar by bitching to them this time. He was there one day and gone the next. With that running through his mind he wondered if it was time to call it a day. He continued to sit, ridged and tranced, with his eyes shut in deep thought for another hour before he was snapped out of it by the ringing of some form of telephonular communication. His mobile phone to be more exact. Phil answered the phone and answered with a monotone and ran together "Whatyouwant?" "Wait, what? Phil is that you?" The voice on the other end of the line belonged to Phil's manager and oddly enough superagent, Dr. Dick Dirkwood PhD. "Yes of course it's me, I'm just sad and depressed for crying out loud, I'm all emotional! I answered my phone like a mentalist under treatment because I'm an emotional wreck! Couldn't you tell of my heartbreak just from how I answered the phone Dick? For crying out loud I'm pouring out my soul before you and you are taking a big stinky turd in it." "It sounded more to me like you were half asleep." "I'm a very complex individual I'll have you know." "Don't lie, I've known you for three years and I can say without a shadow of a doubt you have no emotional depth whatsoever." "Says you." "Yes, says me." "Okay, I may also have been napping but trust me when I say that I am going through emotional hell over here." "Oh stop pissing about, you got fired it happens to people every day. Thankfully you are not as worthless as those awful common people with their scurvy and their public urination." "Well don't push it too far Dick. Everyone has pissed on a trash bag in an alley every once in a while. You shouldn't judge them for that. Judge them due to smelling of shit if you want to judge them on anything." "As much as I would enjoy to consider the mockery of people who make very little money and indeed a lot less than we make individually never mind combined. Yes, as much as I do indeed enjoy making fun of those poor people who I have ordered my manservents to hunt for fun on occasion..." "You did what?" "Never mind that. I called with a reason and a purpose. There is a reason you pay me a wad of cash every month and this is it. I've already found you another job!" "Already?" "Already!" "MAYBE I WANTED THE BREAK DID YOU EVER THINK ABOUT THAT JERKSQUAD MCGEE?" "I considered it, but then I realised that would be exceedingly stupid and we need to not lose any momentum. The longer you're off my beautiful beautiful TV screen, the less value your name has. Strike while the iron's hot is what my mother used to say." "Explains how she spent the last years of her life in the burn ward I suppose." "She did tend to take her advice a little too literally. Anyway GCW wanted to scoop scoop scoop you up, so I signed the contract already by forging your signature. You start in a promotion blitz in two days." "You forged my signature?" "Well yes, strike while the..." "You JUST said that. May I say that you are an awful awful manager." "Well I thought you'd want to job." "Of course I want the job I want to swim in my pool of money like Scrooge McDuck once more but it would have been nice if you had at least asked before signing my life away." "I'm sorry but I'm very excitable." "God should I fire you." "Can't, I put that in your new contract." "The one I didn't even sign?" "Well technically you signed it." "I'm glad you're on the phone because god do I want to punch you in the face at the moment. Anyway, now that I have to do it anyway I might as well get ready." "Excellent! I'll be over in an hour to explain the details so make sure you have some god damn pants on by then." "God you are such a pants nazi. I'll see you then." Phil disconnected to call and let the glory of himself sink back into his mind. He then danced quite possibly the most embarrassing victory jig never witnessed. He sat back down, leaned back into his chair and uttered two words to himself. "I'm back!"
