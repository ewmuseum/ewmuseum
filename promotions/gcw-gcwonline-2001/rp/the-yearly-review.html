The Yearly Review

Date: Well, before WW 83 More concrete. More asphalt. More glass more steel more marble more tile more slate. Frank's head rang. He slouched down into black leather cushions and waited across from the secretary. He tried not to stare at her, but he'd already taken in everything else the room had to offer and was finding a malaise in recanting them. She, dark and local, had welcomed him and told him to take a seat under a soft accent and softer lighting. He was dressed to listen and obey without responding, was dressed to keep to himself and hold his thoughts close. He stuffed his hands in his jacket's pockets and wondered if her eyes were more cerulean or aqamarine. Something about the angle. And this was Frank still waking up from his flight. He'd stretch his legs out across the couch and he'd amble tepid to the coffee machine, but they were just slight permutations on how he approached the consistent wait. That ate hours unabated, without that hungry appreciation it had when it would pick up scraps of seconds and spend time savoring the minutes thrown its way. It had grown enough that he had to regard it with some forethought, approach it with a sense of understanding and keep his hands away from its mouth. The wide world had schedules that he had to fit into. He existed in its downtime, he felt; had less and less of his own and struggled to do anything productive with it’it was too easy to relax, with a mind to escaping the grind of training mingled with physical therapy, of constant motion, of subordination and of the public's hounding recognition disguised as appreciation. That is, nothing in him disliked the life or was na’ve to the point of damning the conseqences and praising the causes. He accepted and he trudged through with a disconnect almost stoic. He took his jacket off and laid it over the back of the couch, got his pack out before she could call his name. His voice hung in his throat, dormant, vestigial. Slow to rise. "Yo." "Sage is free. Now." So he tossed his jacket over his shoulder on the up, took the forward down a hallway that he could've lived in’given walls and amenities. The sun hung through a wall of glass and pinned his shadow to the wall a halfpace ahead of him. He was surrounded; beset upon by plants with long stems and palm leaves, swarmed by the haunt and glow of the sun refracting through offset/offangled windows, encrusted in light prismatic that passed over him qicker than he could crawl under it. It was a long walk, past a row of hanged and mounted artists he knew on sight, or at least on consideration. He gauged the distance by era, crept through the fall of Rome and picked up his pace through the dark ages, rode casual from the Renaissance on into modernity and postmodernity and stopped at white marble faced doors with JXW dug deep into whatever their black framing and guts were made of’he laughed. It took art a few thousand years longer than it had taken him. The side he tried gave, half of an X and a full W swinging in with him. Another long room; he sensed a pattern of replicating dark floors in glowing white rooms for an eternity if he was dumb enough to keep moving. The low desk, the wide chair behind it, the cabinets, the monitors, the trinkets; it was all shadowed and blurred against the sloped hills and rising mountains beyond the glass of the far wall. Sage coughed, entrenched below the fur coat wrapping the back of his chair. Black vested, white shirted. Tieless. Sunken eyed. Clean shaven. They registered one another. "Want a seat?" And Frank advanced. He dragged toes, sqeaked rubber against the floor and cast glances slow like glares. Cases. Gleaming. Metal. Angles. Treasures. Dinosaur bones. Weapons. Glass. Hesitant, maybe, he dropped his jacket over the back of a chair like a gutted warehouse; he dragged it back and sat, let the arms dig into the flesh under the caps of his elbows and eyed a silver cigarette case and a large crystal lighter like great dividers. "Whats good?" "Pussy. Beef. Drug abuse. You?" "Shit." "Hows the hometown?" "Ashs been stayin there. I been avoidin a little. She brought up marriage and shit. I mean. Damn." "Fuck it, Frank. What can you do? You got what, a few good months out of it? Not that Im not a romantic or anything, but Im generally happy with a couple of weeks. Doubt youre too different." "True. Yeah, yeah. Ima have to cut her off." He looked at his shoes. "Fuck, sprobably what she wants. Girl knows whats gonna happen when she starts talkin about that shit." "They arent stupid, no. Hows the rest of the place?" "Charlotte, still? Shit, you know. Cant find a single motherfucker I like, the kids I wanted to avoid are all over the place. Went to the college while I was around last week, bumped into this dumbshit stoner I used to smoke with. Kid was speakin on politics, what life was like for Muslims in the Middle East. Bunch of bullshit he didnt know and, goddamn, everyone around him was actin all impressed like, shit. Like watchin CNN and readin a book or two means fuckers oughta listen to you. I tried to call him on it, he started on this, this 'Frank knew me before I changed' noise. The hell. Like what youre interested in is who you are." A stop. "And thats Charlotte. Pretty much." "How old is he?" "Shit. 20, 21? Somethin like that." "Probably just started reading Hunter Thompson. Fuck it. They all get over it." "Yeah. Yeah. You votin?" "Doubt Im around the polls come time for it." Frank stared out the window, cast eyes on foreign soil. "Hah. True nough. If you were votin, then, have any preferences?" "Fuck no. Fuck that. I havent voted since I thought it could change the world’feel too dirty even talking about betting on it these days. Gave up that bullshit, started relying on myself to fix things. Hah. Like a real dumb motherfucker. Righteous, dumb motherfucker though." "Its somethin. Lotsa people dont give it that much." "Yeah. And look where it got me." The intentions were mixed; Frank saw the slump in Sage's shoulder and the dry stare in his eyes less than he saw the sheen of the desk, the glint of the glass, the decorations, the ornaments, the secretary, the hallways, the view. He didn't know which of the argument he fell on, he knew even less which side he was meant to fall on. He shrugged. "Could be worse." "People always say that. Its a trade’I wont call it a sacrifice. Fuck, if I found anyone I could trust with it, Id be paying a ridiculous amount just so I wouldnt have to deal with all the Jordans." "I as bad off as them?" "Christ no. Its just. I just cant deal with these people. This one guy, knew he was a test run for the market, knew he wasnt guaranteed a spot, and still went out and made the name look bad. Then, he has the audacity to come to me’not call me, not see if I had a fucking thing to say to him’but actually fly over here and demand an appointment." "Helld you do with him?" "He lost. I mean. Fuck it. He lost every match for two months straight. I asked him abo, or, well. I told him to fuck off’so he started trashing the place. Started trashing my fucking office, screaming this tired bullshit about how worthless I am. Then he gets tired or bitchy, or, I don't know. He just drops and, all teary eyed and shit, told me that he agreed, and how he was just a surrogate, just a product to pass the time until I find a better deal. I mean, what the hell?" "Thats like, thats somethin Ive only even seen people that just turned into exs do." "Guess you cant expect everyone to keep cool. I need to start asking these fuckers for resum’s." He took a cigarette from the case, lit it against the desk lighter and leaned his chair back. "But, if that doesnt answer your qestion: what the hell will? Youre reliable and sane enough. In some great sense of balance, you alone bring in enough profits to cover all our losses. Theyre not worth shit, most of them. They just. They suck and they suck, and they try to suck me dry. Assholes. Worthless fucking sluts." "Hah. So Im the best whore in the stable?" "Yes. A fucking thousand, a million times: yes. You breathe fire while the rest guzzle gasoline. Pale imitations. You? Brilliant imitation." Frank reached his hand into his coat; Sage perked up and slid the case skittering across his desk. Frank took it, took a smoke. "Hows Sageton? Sageville? Whatever." He slid the lighter to himself, bent in over it. Fire. "Blacksburg, last I heard. Its around. I just. I needed to get away from those people. I needed an office. Land. Isolation." Sage swiveled his chair around, turned to face the windowwall. "You planning on staying long?" "Depends. What you got to do around here?" "Bullshit. Tons of bullshit. Piled to the sky." "That, uh. That dont tell me much." "Yeah. I do that shit sometimes. I dont think youd like my scene very much, honestly. Its all, or, we are all assholes and dirtier than our money. There are something on the order of three weeks worth of parties, or events, or whatever, tonight and Im pretty sure we could at least fund a serious action against Sudanese militias. But, no. Theyre going to be eating fish eggs and, sucking coke off the rims of wine glasses. Fuck them." "You doin anything yet?" "To help? Not yet. No. I will." Frank tried to wait the silence out, like the rest of an answer could materialize. He had to break it. "So. Dont think I got a real answer. Whats good?" Sage kicked the floor and spun back to Frank. Once. Twice. Three times. Shoe to the desk, he stopped. "Never get tired of that. Anyways. Whats good? Thats, whats what? 'Get on with it; why the hell am I here?' Right? Right. Its, its this direction thing." "Change your mind on me doin my own thing?" "Not like that. Doing your own thing, whatever the fuck it is, is working something like, 80 percent of the time. Thats good enough for me, fuck. Blew up the last tournament you were in, blew up Viv to finish it, then blew up Knight when it came up." "Hah, that dont mean shit. Rollins beat my ass when I was one and nada with him, and I got a win over Lia after shed beat me." "Empirical evidence is all we have to go on. Like I said: 70, 75 percent. Variables, something. I dont want to get into that’just leave it that I trust you to wrestle and promo, and that I wouldnt interfere even if you fucked up miles above your head. Thats not what I do." "So what youre sayin is." "Good qestion. My heads a little. Elsewhere." "You forgot why you sent me plane tickets and told me to get here as qick as I could?" "When you put it that way. No. Youve been with JXW just over a year’I believe that, if nothing else, an annual review of employees isnt the worst idea; get a head on where they are, where theyre going. You agree?" Frank shifted against the seat, pulled an ankle up and rested it over his knee. "What kinda review?" "Some qestions to answer, a few tests." "Tests?" "How fast you can run a mile, how much you can piss in a cup. Things like that." "So. Drug test." "Its more for curiousitys sake than anything. Dont worry about it." "Thats just. Thats fucked up, man. Could just ask me what I was on." "Trust no one talking about what drugs they use. Trust the urine, the hair, and the trained professional that tells me what the results mean." "I gotta talk to one of those trained professionals too? Answerin the qestions, I mean." "No, no. She isnt trained. Only vaguely professional, either." "Whens it start?" "Whenever. Can start now same as you could do it tomorrow or the day after. Im not involved with it. I couldnt give a fuck when you schedule." "Shit. Can run the qestions now." "Why not?" Sage tensed his fingertips against the desk and stood, sliding the coat off his chair and across his shoulders. He raised a finger, found a pocket for his cigarette case, and headed for a side exit with an: "Ill get her" and no second glances. He slid the door into the wall, lingered in the doorway and called out a name muffled by music, piped into the hallway louder than the office. Frank hefted the crystal lighter in his hand, baseballed it against his palm and tested its heft. He framed a mountain in its fragmented eye, twisted it and contorted the peak into a wave in the fluid. The door shut again behind him; he replaced the chunk of glass and set his chin over his shoulder. He recognized her, the third Jordan White if he was the second. She was gold, wore spring sky and stepped tight. He credited Sage when it was deserved. She sat, shrinking into Sage's chair, all a silhouette against the day. Smiling. Pulling a small digital recorder from a plaid bag he'd given no attention to. She sat it on the desk and turned it on. Red light. She read. "Whats your name?" He cleared his throat. "Frank Nelson Black." "Enunciate." He leaned forwards, aimed his mouth at the microphone. "Frank Nelson Black." "Whats your birthday?" "Was the sixteenth. Of January." He got nothing from her. "1980." "Hows it going?" "Been better. That a qestion?" "Yes. Would you consider your tenure with JXW a disappointment?" "To who?" "Anyone." She stared blue. "Probably. Someones always let down." "You?" "Ive done aight. Shit. Little, uh, little disappointed, was it? bout shit here and there but, I mean, overall? Did better than I thought I would. Fuck it. Done better than I hoped I would." He put his weight against a hand on the arm of his chair, moved back into the seat and tapped his cigarette into the ashtray as he situated. She didn't posture. "What have you accomplished, by your estimation? If he wanders around," and she coughed qiet. "Excuse me." "Lets say I wandered around." She breathed out once, a sigh that was no more than a breath: "fuck you." Her eyes narrowed. "What is your greatest accomplishment?" "Beat LeStatt Knight." She scanned ahead. "Biggest disappointment?" "I am stuck in some nickel and dime bullshit tournament instead of wearin the belt. That count?" "Where would you be without us?" "ANC, still. Probably." "If you found out Sage wanted to kill you for losing to Lia. How would you react?" He sat forwards, crooked his head. "Lemme see that list." "No. Answer." "Nah. I dont think its on that sheet you got." "Its not. Sage told me to take a qestion off’I replaced it with another one. Now answer." "Bitter fuckin chick." Pink lips snarled. "Go to hell, Frank. I was just letting you know." "That real, then?" She dropped the papers to the desk, stabbed her elbows down on either side of them and leaned in. Her mouth opened, closed; she shook her head and fell back into the seat, dragged an outstretched thumb backwards across her throat. "He didnt like it." "Never did like Lia. Never told me why." "He wont. Hes too responsible." Frank laughed, laughed again when he tried to drag words up from the bile in his throat. "Aint expect that. Whatever, though. Heheh. Responsible." He stamped his cigarette out, ground tobacco away until it was filter on glass. "Wanna know? If that qestion you asked, I think Id feel real bad about losin." "Hm." She had to find her place in the list. "Are you sober?" "Yeah. Any way yall could do somethin bout that?" "No. Whats your record?" "Singles?" "Sure." "Shit. Somethin like 9 and 5. Fuck." He laughed. "Relaxed schedule. And I fuckin suck, thinkin about it. Loss to Kyo Kushinaga’the fuck does somethin like that even happen?" "Outside of singles?" "No fuckin clue." "Have you eaten today?" "Huh." "Eaten. Today. Have you?" "Sage got a sorta, odd rhythm with these qestions." "Just answer." "Well, yeah. On the plane." "Was it any good?" He shrugged. "Enh." "Album of the year?" "Ghost and Wu were alright, but shit. Saul, Rise and Liberation was crucial. Uh, Aes Rocks last too. None Shall Pass, yeah." "Movie?" "Fuck that. Movies aint really my thing’I mean, I went to see 300 in theaters, enjoyed it. Im a shit film critic." "TV show?" "Reruns. What, uh. What do they gotta do with a yearly review?" "I think I can skip some of these’who is your favorite opponent from the past year?" "Huh." He dropped his chin against his hand. "Shit. Probably LK." He put his face to the mic again: "LeStatt Knight." "Why?" "Uh, dudes a solid wrestler, a one man legacy, and a hell of a lot less into bullshittin than anybody else I been up against. To the point, direct’gotta respect that, specially outta someone been around as long as him. I mean, he could just go out and demand title matches and bitch and all that other mundane shit’but he dont. Motherfucker deserves a title shot no doubt, and hes still goin out just to prove he can earn it’thats sick." "Why do you wrestle?" Frank dropped his foot to the floor, fought the corners of his lips down and cocked his eyebrows with the impact. He recoiled, then; coughed somewhere in his ribs and let it simmer. "Whyd I start, or whym I still around?" "Whichever." "Motherfuckers; aight then. I started this shit cause, see, there was this point where a new semester of college came back around and my ass wasnt there with it. Needed a job, wanted somethin I could maybe travel off of’get away from Charlotte, get away from those people. Was a lot, or, was enough people had looked up to me’one way or another’that I wasnt just gonna stick around, just some college dropout with no more real goals. Stupid fuckin idea, freal: ended up with a bunch of promotions around Charlotte, so its like, I didnt get away from shit for awhile. All those cats, fuckin, all those cats used to respect me got to see me get my ass beat for like, 15 bucks. 20 on the weekends. Was fucked up." "What was your" "I aint done. Chill. So, so Sage found me, hired my ass as the first replacement Jordan’but everyones gonna hear this knows that, so it aint what youre looking for and it sure as fuck aint a real answer. Im still around cause, cause fuck it. Im not but so good at this shit’enough to maybe get noticed, but not enough to just steamroll my way to the top’but its still just about what Id been hopin for. The drama is all some old bullshit, but its fun. I always liked watchin it, I mean, but livin it? Gettin to wrestle as the replacement to one of my fuckin idols from back in the day? Free flights around the world? Fuck yeah." He exhaled sharp, a half a chuckle. "I get paid to fuck people up. Yknow how good that feels?" "No. I never wrestled." "Oh, yeah. Wait’not even before Sage hired you?" She shook her head, smile stretched on a slight sideways dismissal. "What was your best performance?" "Ironman. Rollins." "Worst?" "First couple weeks, somewhere. Northsky or Kyo. Fucked up against both of em." "What is your opinion of Steven Caldera?" He sucked his lips into his teeth, knotted his brow. "It legal to ask this sorta stuff?" "Why wouldnt it be?" "Like I need a lawyer or somethin in here. Shit." He nodded. "Caldera, huh? Hes a boss, used to be a badass wrestler’taught me some shit when I was around, at least. Cant say I like him, cant really say I respect him. Too whiny, I guess. Dramatics, yeah; he likes getting dramatic about shit. Is who he is, though." "Is there anything he did that stands out as," she coughed, slapped her fist to her upper ribs. "Sorry. As particularly immoral, unethical, or illegal?" He laughed. "Aint tryin to fuck anybody over here." "I dont think Sage means to overthrow GCW." "Still. Maybe dudes tryin to make sure I got sound treatment on the job’just some shit crosses lines." "You could just say no." "It dont deserve a no. But, fuck. I trust Sage. I guess." He looked down, put his palms together and brought his eyes back to the red light. "Caldera didnt do anything bad. Dudell, dude will turn a blind eye to a lot of shit so long as youre in his company. Dont matter whose side youre on. Youre gonna see my piss test so, fuck it. Youll see what he dont care about. Maybe shit worse than that, I dont know. Hes all business. Hes not a monster, I mean. Not from what I saw." Eyes back to Jordan; he huffed. "That wasnt hard." "Wasnt right, either." "Hm. Do you have any children?" "Cant prove it if I do." "What do you know about Leslie Dawkins-Thompson, Mike Starr, Zoe Williams, Erika Nichols, and Jimmy Hall?" "What the fuck?" "Should I repeat the qestion?" "Nah, nah, no. I dont. Sage. Stay out my personal life. Dont dig up my shit." "That your response?" "I guess." "Thanks for your time, then." "That the last qestion?" "The last qestion you needed to answer, at least." "Heh." He laughed, then, rolled his neck back and parted his lips for a smirk like a sneer’or vice versa. "Dont even know who the fuck Jimmy Hall is. Sure as hell isnt yalls place to know." "Erika Nichols' exboyfriend." "Oh. Yeah. Well. Fuck you for that too." "That all?" "Shit. Can I ask you somethin?" She turned the recorder off and slid it parallel to her papers; she looked up to him. "Sure. I dont have to answer you, though." "Sounds fair. Aight. The fuck is up with Sage?" "Hm?" "He. Aight, aight. Last time I saw him was, it was you and him, when I went to his crazy little, fucked up town. Fucker was strungout; and now, now, this shit? The fuck?" She arranged her bag; stacks and files and rows. "He had a company to run." "Who was runnin it when Sage was mayor of drugsville?" "Sage was." "So its. So its, he just changed his setting and figured that he would be a better businessman?" "Something like that." "Huh." Frank thought. He watched the desk only for the inevitability vision was to him. Something about order, something about the continued relevance of method acting to his world; he grasped and shook his head. Nothing was coming together as a conclusion, but things rarely ever will; just a whole, a culmination of anecdotes and untested hypotheses. Themes, then. He couldn't expect answers. "Fuckin Sage." She was standing, then, out of focus against a horizon obscured by the slow crawl of tectonic motion. "He told me to pretend to hate you." "Hnh?" "Before you came. He told me that I should start off like that, then be less of a bitch as we went on." "Thats. Thats weird." "I think he wanted those last qestions to really hit you. Just thought you should know."
