Bringing out the best in pain **Investigations cont.**

31 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/31/11 Pacing himself around the men while they are bound and restrained, he slowly turns and looks down at the man. His eyes piercing, as if he was looking into the souls of each person tied to the chairs. He looks at Reischmann and Kristoff. Smiling he brings a long thin cigarette to his lips, lighting it slowly he turns his attention back to Kristoff, Gladstoff smiles as he comes closer looking at the two.   “Ahh Kristoff and Reischmann should give you a slight bit of history about your names. Both German names, also descending from old German aristocracy. Now Americans living in the capitalistic society, you must so miss the old socialistic ways.”   Pulling on leather gloves Gladstoff opens and closes his hands stretching the soft leather, looking at the gloves as if he was an artist looking at his canvas imagining his work before he begins.   “Heinrich Himmler tortured thousands through interrogation; He wanted to know who was out to kill the Fuhrer. Of course Hitler was the man who was paranoid, but Himmler knew if he did as told he would be placed at the top of the pile.” He runs his finger across his chin in a thoughtful fashion, leaning his head to the side slightly he stairs at Kristoff's right hand.   Grabbing Kristoff by the hand he pulls the palm upwards as he looks down at the fingers. Nodding with his own satisfaction he turns. Walking to a nearby table he reaches out and grabs a box of toothpicks from it. Slowly he opens the cover and looks back over at Kristoff.   “Wooded toothpicks are such a nice tool, used to pick little pieces of meat or other food objects from your own teeth, but they can also be used in a very creative fashion. Want to know what I am thinking of Agent Kristoff?” Chuckling he pulls out a handful of toothpicks.     Running the toothpick over the finger nail of Kristoff he slowly and deliberately pops it underneath the nail itself and presses inward. He presses hard as the wood of the toothpick tears through flesh. Kristoff roars out struggling against his bindings. Gladstoff stands up and looks to his left.   “Dmitri a little anesthesia please.” Dmitri hurries over and hits Reischmann with a solid right cross. Gladstoff shakes his head and looks down at Kristoff.     Continuing his work, he slowly shoving toothpicks underneath Kristoffs nails as Kristoff lets out a roar of pain underneath his tape mouth. Patting Kristoff atop his head he clears his throat. “Forgive Dmitri for his over excitement.”     “You see Agent Kristoff, Dmitri is just excited to punish Capitalist dogs from America. After all his father died protecting him in Iraq in your first Desert Storm.” Twisting one of the toothpicks he raises his eyes noticing the sweat on Kristoff’s brow.   “It wasn’t just Iraqi soldiers you killed there. Just like how we crushed the Socialist pigs in Germany. I simply regret we did not put a bullet in Hitler’s head ourselves.”  Chuckling as he finishes up. Looking over at Reischmann, he turns and carefully steps across the floor. Grabbing Reischmann’s chair he drags it over the top of a drain. The chair sits directly across from Kristoff, as Reischmann slowly raises his head looking at Kristoff who is breathing deeply.    Looking at Reischmann Gladstoff stares bullets through him. “Did you think I forgot about you Comrade Reischmann or should I specifically call you Herr Reischmann as your grandfather fondly preferred to be called.”    Spinning quickly, he strikes Reischmann on the side of the head. Reischmann falls hard onto the cold wet concrete. Standing over Reischmann he adjusts his gloves. Slowly he draws a syringe from the inside pocket of his coat. Reaching down he drags Reischmann and the chair back to all four legs on the floor solidly.   “Now this will only hurt for mere moments.” He smacks Reischmann’s forearm drawing the veins up. He slowly pushes the needle into the largest of them emptying the contents within quickly. “Heroin should make this whole experience for you interesting. Shall I say very enjoyable as well for myself.”    He turns his attention back to Kristoff who’s fingertips are dripping blood from the fresh wounds. Taking each finger one by one he breaks off the toothpicks leaving splintered ends. He stares into Kristoff's eyes as he does so trying to elicit another reaction other than pain and anger out of him. “Come Agent Kristoff you must be feeling a little bit of despair at my hands, knowing you will never get out of here. I have been waiting years to get some revenge against you Capitalist pigs for what you had caused to happen in Berlin.” Throwing the last pieces of shattered wood onto the wet floor. He turns and looks at the little table nearby. Lifting a bowl he eyes a brackish yellow liquid in it. Rolling the bowl slightly he mixes the liquid a bit, then turns as he pours it slowly onto the finger tips of Kristoff. Kristoff roars through the tape a muffled howl of anger and pain as tears roll down his cheeks from his eyes. “Yes yes Kristoff that is what I want to see. Sit absorb the pain, ponder upon your failings.” Laughing loudly as he turns his gaze back towards Reischmann. Slowly he takes careful steps towards the man. Reaching his hand forward he grasps the chin of his prisoner. Turning Reischmann's head roughly as he leans in and speaks in his ear. “I was there in Berlin as a young man, I watched the city get destroyed absolutely by weapons fire from my fellow countrymen. Where was your Grandfathers Fuhrer? He was dead, he committed suicide rather than face ultimate defeat. He chose a cowards way out. Now you shall suffer for your grandfathers transgressions.” Sharply turning the head of the bound Detective, he strikes another closed fist as he looks to Dmitri. “The girl is yours to do with as you please Dmitri, I will handle Reischmann here.” The door slowly closes as Dmitri exits the building out into a thunder storm, that has seemed to pop up out of nowhere. He walks across the open broken asphalt area. Looking up as the lightning explodes above his head and the rain begins to pour down upon him. Reaching an officer area he opens the door and looks around. Noticing someone is missing, but his female prisoner still bound and gagged. Morales watches with a bit of fear in her eyes as Dmitri begins removing his shirt in front of her. His eyes focused on her. He then slowly removes his belt as she struggles against her bonds. He wraps the belt around his hand twice. Swinging it as wet leathery slap is heard echoing through the room. Dmitri reaches forward and grabs Morales by the face with his free hand as he looks her straight in the eyes. “This is going to hurt you a lot more than me, in fact it will end up quite pleasurable for me.” Leaning in close he licks her cheek as she closes her eyes and shakes from utter revulsion. Standing straight up he reaches down and begins to tear her shirt off leaving her only in her bra. Taking in the sight of her as he suddenly realizes something. Turning he opens the door yelling out into the rain. “Stefan you fool, where the hell are you??” Slamming the door in frustration he turns his attention back towards the woman in the chair. This time he will make her pay. He will make them all pay as he advances towards her as she struggles and screams only drowned out by the thunder and lightning. He looks over his shoulder at the door then back to Morales. “Where has Stefan gone?” Morales looks at him and spits at him. The spittle landing upon his cheek he turns and backhands the woman causing her to land hard on her side upon the floor. The cold concrete feeling good against her cheek as she turns slightly looking up at Dmitri. Dmitri smiles as he looks at the blood on his hand from her busted lip. “This is only beginning for you woman, you will learn your place.” Outside the building a shadowy figure moves quietly away from the building. Behind him he is dragging a man beaten and bloody, back towards the tall grass. Moving in black tactical clothing and a mask pulled over his head he drags his own prisoner through the mud. Staying low he drags the man up over the hill into a gully between two dunes of grass. Reaching into one of his pouches he pulls out black tie wraps and zips them tight onto the mans hands. Then follows suit with the mans feet. Nodding at his own handy work he stands up and heads off back towards the perimeter fence near the office where Morales is being held. Quietly he observes the happenings and proceeds to a higher vantage point. Stopping his movements he stops and stares at the compound getting his bearings. Like a large animal stalking his prey he slowly moves in behind a guard. Tapping the guard on the shoulder the guard turns and is met with a heavy punch to the temple crumpling him. Reaching down he grabs the guards radio and then drags the guard off into the grass once again. He raises this time to the top of the dune where he can clearly see a spotlight shining out into the grass as several men are hurrying around quickly. They haven't noticed his presence nor the guards missing, but he is curious as to what has them all in an uproar. Watching the gate he notices four Us Army Humvees pull into the area. Several men in black step out as do three US Army Officers. He watches them carefully. Watching them head into the warehouse as well where the FBI Agent and Las Vegas Detective is being held. He checks over at the office and then looks to his left following the set of power lines. Making his way to the small transformer there he reaches into his pocket and grabs a survival knife and draws it forward. Sticking it in the pole nearby he wraps copper wire around it. Turning he heads back towards his dune and simply watches and observes the actions of everyone at this abandoned base. Back in the officer Morales is lifted by Dmitri as he looks her in the eyes, He grabs her close and forces a kiss upon her lips as she suddenly thrusts a knee upwards into his precious stones. He crumples and falls to his knees as he vomits from the excruciating pain of the kick. Morales hurries and runs towards the door kicking it open. She turns the corner of the building and runs for the grass, up and over the dune she quickly scrambles running right into the man dressed in black tactical gear. He spins quickly putting a gun to her head. Morales closes her eyes and accepts her fate suddenly. “Go ahead and do it you bastard.” Lightning flashes and lights up the area as Morales defiantly stares down the barrel of a 50 caliber Desert Eagle. The man does not move nor flinch as the stare down has begun, as fate will only test the wills of these two individuals, the unknown man and Morales the Secret Service Agent.
