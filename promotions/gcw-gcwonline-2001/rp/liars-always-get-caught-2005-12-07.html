Date: 12/7/05 [ :: Fades in. :: ] [ :: In Jaden’s family Chinese restaurant, Dragon Express, it was unusually packed for a Sunday. The restaurant was low on staff, but they still manage to take and deliver their customers? orders. Aunt Mei stands behind the front desk, handing out menus to the hostess who was escorting the customers to their seats while talking on the phone with her nephew, Jaden. :: ] Aunt Mei: [ :: exhausted :: ] I don’t know. One minute, we were getting little customers and now we’re getting lots of customers from the woodwork. What do you think happened, Jaden? Jaden: Maybe they finally realized that Dragon Express is the all-time best Chinese restaurant in all of San Francisco. [ :: Aunt Mei sighs as she rests her head and elbows on the desktop, taking a small breather before anymore customers enter the restaurant. :: ] Aunt Mei: How’s GCW? I hope you’re behaving yourself there. Jaden: Well, you know me. [ :: Aunt Mei’s glasses glows a bright white as she was in Ultra Piss Mode. :: ] Aunt Mei: Jaden? Jaden: [ :: laughs nervously :: ] I’m behaving! I’m behaving! [ :: laughs nervously more :: ] But I have to say GCW is a fun and challenging place to be. [ :: Her glasses returns to normal as she closes her eyes and looks with content. :: ] Aunt Mei: Have you decided to apply to this college? Jaden: [ :: flustered :: ] Uh? Well? Aunt Mei: Only asking because it has been at least four weeks since you left. Jaden: I’m still thinking about it. [ :: content :: ] Oh, I forgot to tell you that I’m coming home tomorrow. Aunt Mei: [ :: looks content :: ] Really? Perhaps I should prepare your favorite dish then. Jaden: Don’t have to do that. Aunt Mei: [ :: smiles :: ] Nonsense. You’re going to be going away to college soon and this could be the last time you get to have your favorite dish. You know what would be better, a party. I’ll invite your friends because I know they want to take part of your great opportunity. Jaden: [ :: modest :: ] Really, there’s no need for a party. Besides, I haven’t decided if I’m going to college in the first place. Aunt Mei: If you’re not going to college then why are you where you are now? Jaden: [ :: flustered :: ] I’m? [ :: grunts :: ] Aunt Mei: You’ve been grunting in my ear since we started this conversation. Are you sure that everything is all right over there? Jaden: It’s nothing. [ :: chuckles :: ] I’m just popping bones. [ :: mutters :: ] Literally? Aunt Mei: Did you say something? Jaden: Nothing. [ :: A customer enters the restaurant, as Aunt Mei stands up and stares at the customer with a smile on her face. :: ] Aunt Mei: I have another customer. But sure to call me before you leave for the airport so I can get everything ready for your party. Jaden: [ :: distant :: ] Yeah? [ :: She carefully watches the customer approach the front desk. :: ] Aunt Mei: Ok, bye. Jaden: [ :: still distant :: ] Bye? [ :: She hangs up the phone and handles the customer with politeness. :: ] [ :: Meanwhile, in a New York hotel room, Jaden sits at the head of the bed, staring down at the phone, wondering how long he can keep up lying to his Aunt and what will happen when she finds out that he hasn’t applied to college, but he has really applied to a wrestling federation. He knew he would have to tell her sooner or later, but he would prefer on telling her when all his injuries are completely healed because he also knew that his Aunt was going to beat him worst than any other wrestlers could. Jaden puts the worrisome thoughts aside and heads to the bathroom. :: ] [ :: Turns on the lights with a flip of a switch. Jaden stands before the mirror, staring at all his bruises on his chest that he sustained at the recent event. Closes his eyes and lowers his head, as his mind starts to think back to the events that happened after the scaffold match. :: ] Is he dead? No, he’s still breathing. Hey kid, can you hear me? [ :: Jaden opens his eyes and lifts his head, seeing that he was backstage of the Madison Square Garden with two trainers and a doctor. He sits up from a flat surface, favoring the back of his head, but he winces as he quickly places his hand on his aching side. :: ] Jaden: [ :: wincing :: ] Damnit! Doctor: Take it easy, kid. You just took a nasty fall off the scaffold. Jaden: [ :: looks at the doctor curiously :: ] Fall? [ :: remembers :: ] Oh, that’s right. Who won the match? Trainer #1: Wolack and you won the match. Trainer #2: Wolack didn’t have time to celebrate after the scaffold fell on him. Jaden: [ :: blinks and looks at the trainers curiously :: ] What the hell are you talking about? Trainer #1: After you got eliminated? Jaden: [ :: snaps his head at the trainer and cuts the trainer off :: ] Eliminated? [ :: humphs :: ] It was more like being stabbed in the back. I can’t believe he let me go like that. The nerve of that punk! Trainer #2: He had no other choice. Wolack was wide open for an attack let alone for an easy elimination and Moore would’ve won the match not you and Wolack. Jaden: [ :: looks away with frustration on his face :: ] Whatever? Trainer #1: As I saying, Wolack and Moore were locked in a bloodbath that would soon turn into a nasty wreck. [ :: The trainer gotten Jaden’s attention. :: ] Trainer #1: One of the scaffold’s support snaps causing the scaffold to swing to one side as Wolack was hanging on the rail while Moore was hanging on Wolack’s legs. There was only thing that Wolack could do. [ :: Jaden stares at the trainer with a raised brow. :: ] Trainer #1: Wolack released his grip which was enough to make Moore release his grip on Wolack’s legs. Moore hit the mat first, and then Wolack followed. The scaffold’s support completely snapped as the entire structure fell on both men. Jaden: Oh man? [ :: looks down at the floor with concern :: ] Doctor: You only have a couple of bumps, bruises, and perhaps some bruised ribs. You’re very lucky compared to Wolack and Moore. Trainer #2: You still think badly about Wolack. [ :: Jaden silently stares at the three men. He turns his head and sees himself staring back at him, as he was back in the hotel room’s bathroom, facing the mirror. He turns on the water, gathering water in his hands and splashes the hot water in his face. Lifts his head and looks in the mirror, watching the water drip from his face while he listens to water run. :: ] Jaden: [ :: in thought :: ] I know sacrifices have to made to get the “W” in combat? [ :: Turns off the water. Jaden turns and leaves the bathroom. :: ] Jaden: [ :: continues in thought :: ] But I still think Wolack had a smile on his face when he let me go and watched me hit the ring. [ :: Stands in the doorway with his back turned on the bathroom. :: ] Jaden: Punk? [ :: Turns off the lights with a quick flip of the switch. :: ] [ :: Back over in Dragon Express, the cooking and serving staff was relaxing in the front, as the restaurant was closed for the day. Aunt Mei sits at a table with a three waiters, chatting while playing mahjong. :: ] Aunt Mei: [ :: sighs :: ] What a day? Waiter #1: I’ll say. [ :: leans back in his chair :: ] Waiter #2: [ :: looks curious :: ] I believe this has been the busiest day we had in the last four weeks. Waiter #3: [ :: stares at the second waiter with a dull look :: ] You could tell? Aunt Mei: [ :: leans forward in her seat :: ] Business haven’t been like this for months and now we’re booming. Waiter #2: Maybe the Business Gods finally answered our prayers. Waiter #1: [ :: dull :: ] You and your Gods. Waiter #2: [ :: glares at the first waiter :: ] Don’t mock the Gods, or you’ll bring the restaurant bad luck. Waiter #3: Pfft, there’s no such thing as bad luck. Aunt Mei: [ :: shakes her head at the waiters :: ] Regardless, this is very peculiar. [ :: looks cautious :: ] Don’t get me wrong I love the business that the restaurant is getting. Just don’t love the fact about not knowing how we’re getting this business. All we could know that this could be the doing of a loan shark, and then we have to pay the leech money for his unwanted help. [ :: looks serious :: ] And we’re not having that. [ :: They nod. :: ] [ :: A cook approaches Aunt Mei’s table with a newspaper in hand. :: ] Cook: [ :: nervous :: ] I think this could be the reason for the sudden interesting of the restaurant. [ :: Cook hands the newspaper to Aunt Mei. She takes the paper and begins reading, but her eyes widens after she read a shocking article. The waiters stare at Aunt Mei curiously. :: ] Waiter #1: Mei, what’s the matter? [ :: The lens of Aunt Mei’s glasses glow a bright white as she was in Ultra Piss mode. :: ] Waiter #2: Mei? Aunt Mei: [ :: sets the paper on the table :: ] It’s nothing. [ :: Her glasses returned normal, as she stares at the waiters with a modest smile. :: ] Aunt Mei: [ :: sets a mahjong piece on the table :: ] I win. [ :: smirks :: ] Let’s play again shall we. [ :: She begins setting up for a new game. The waiters look at one another, and then back at her, wondering what was in that newspaper to cause her to act differently. :: ] [ :: The next day. :: ] [ :: Jaden was back in his hometown. The young wrestler was walking down the road alone with his head down and his knapsack over his left shoulder. He should be thrilled to be back home with his friends and his Aunt, but there was an unsettle look on his face. As he walked, he begins thinking to his somewhat pleasant and unpleasant meeting that he had with his friends. :: ] Hey, did you all miss me? [ :: Jaden lifts his head and sees that he was at the “stomping grounds” better known as the Blazer Dragons? hangout, standing at the entrance. :: ] Jaden: [ :: smiles :: ] Did anyone miss me or not? [ :: His three friends stop what they were doing and looked at him. :: ] Spray: [ :: smirks :: ] Well, look who has come back from being a badass wrestler. [ :: Jaden manly embraces his friends one by one along with giving them the gang handshake. :: ] Tank: Welcome back, Leader. [ :: Jaden takes a seat on small stack of old dirty tires. :: ] Jaden: It’s good to be back. [ :: looks at his friends seriously :: ] Have there been any troubles since I been gone? [ :: Spray shakes his head. :: ] Spray: It wasn’t anything that we couldn’t handle. Jaden: That’s good to hear. But no one is crazy enough to miss with the Blazer Dragons anyways. Tank: So, how long are you staying, Leader? Jaden: Not long. I have to be back on the road in two days. Speaking of being on the road? [ :: Jaden goes in his knapsack, as he takes a stack of papers and hands them over to Tech. :: ] Tech: [ :: blinks and stares at the papers curiously :: ] What’s this? Jaden: It’s all the homework from the past four weeks. [ :: smirks :: ] I must thank you, Tech for hooking me up with the lesson plans. If you haven’t hacked into the teachers? laptops then I would be behind. Tech: Hey, what are hackers for. [ :: grins with an embarrass blush on his cheeks :: ] So, what is wrestling like, Leader? Jaden: What is it like? [ :: ponders a bit :: ] I must say it’s like being in high school? Tech: [ :: looks curious :: ] Like high school? Jaden: [ :: nods :: ] Pretty much. I guess that’s why I fit in so well. [ :: Tank stands at Jaden’s right. :: ] Tank: [ :: whispers :: ] Hey Leader? Jaden: [ :: glances at Tank :: ] ? [ :: Tank leans over to Jaden slightly. :: ] Tank: [ :: glances at Jaden with a slight perverted look on his face :: ] Are there any hot chicks there? Jaden: [ :: slowly nods with a grin :: ] Oh’yeah? There’s this girl name Eva. I haven’t talked to her, but I seen her around backstage. She’s a fine looking thing with quite the trunk if you know what I mean. [ :: playfully nudges Tank’s ribs :: ] And then there’s Mary. She’s a looker, but she’s off the market. Tech: [ :: smirks :: ] Like that will stop you. [ :: playfully nudges Jaden’s ribs :: ] [ :: Jaden grunts and winces as he slightly favors his bruise ribs. :: ] Jaden: Careful, I’m still a bit sore from my match. Spray: It would’ve been a lot worse if you didn’t relax your body at the last second. Jaden: Yeah? [ :: stares at his friends curiously :: ] Hey, did my Aunt come by around here to talk to you guys? Tech: [ :: shakes his head :: ] No, she didn’t. Why? Jaden: Well, she was saying that she was going to hold a party and was going to invite you all to it. Tank: [ :: looks at Jaden curiously :: ] Party, what party? Jaden: [ :: looks distant :: ] Of being accepted to college? [ :: His friends stare at Jaden with surprise. :: ] Tech: You mean? [ :: looks with great shock :: ] YOU MEAN TO TELL ME THAT YOU HAVEN’T TOLD YOUR AUNT THAT YOU’RE WRESTLING!” Jaden: [ :: twirling his thumbs with a nervous look :: ] I’m getting around to it. Tank: Jaden, don’t you know your Aunt is going to kick your ass even far worst any wrestler could if you continue to lie to her. [ :: Jaden gets off the stack of tires and drapes his knapsack over his shoulder. :: ] Jaden: [ :: looks distant :: ] I’m just waiting for the right time to tell her. Spray: [ :: folds his arms over his chest :: ] When? When your arm or leg is in a sling, on crutches, in a neck brace, or in traction in a hospital bed? [ :: stares at Jaden seriously :: ] [ :: Jaden gives Spray the same look. :: ] Tank: Don’t sweat it, Leader. I bet your Aunt will understand. Just remember to say that you’re sorry and you love her very much. Tech: [ :: nods :: ] Yeah, I believe that your Aunt will support your decision on being a wrestler. [ :: Jaden stares at his friends in deep thought. He sighs with settling blinks of his eyes and sees he wasn’t in the hangout anymore, as he was standing outside of Dragon Express. :: ] Jaden: [ :: sarcasm :: ] She’ll support my decision all right. When Hell freezes over? [ :: He soon sees the close sign through the glass door. :: ] Jaden: [ :: looks curious :: ] They’re closed on Monday? [ :: Unlocks the door with his copied key and enters the empty restaurant. He closes and locks the door behind him, but he jumps in slight fright when seeing his Aunt sitting quietly alone at the front desk. She sipped on a hot cup of tea as she faced her nephew. :: ] Jaden: You’re scared me, Auntie. Aunt Mei: [ :: rests her tea on the desk :: ] I’m sorry about that, dear nephew. [ :: She stands up from her seat and slowly approaches him. :: ] Aunt Mei: How was your trip? Jaden: [ :: stares at her suspiciously :: ] Umm? It was fine. The in-flight movie sucked thou. [ :: looks around the restaurant :: ] I see you decided not to go through with the party. Aunt Mei: [ :: smiles :: ] Nah, I just want to have you for myself. Jaden: [ :: still suspicious :: ] Ok? [ :: looks at her curiously :: ] I tried to call at the airport, but there was no answer. Aunt Mei: [ :: smiling :: ] Oh, I was getting you your surprise. Jaden: Auntie, you didn’t have to give me anything. Aunt Mei: Oh sure I did. It’s something that you really deserve. Jaden: [ :: blinks :: ] Oh? [ :: Aunt Mei moves a bit closer to him. :: ] Aunt Mei: [ :: looks modestly with a slow nod :: ] Yeah? [ :: Without another thought, Aunt Mei goes in Ultra Piss mode and grabs hold of her nephew’s arm and throws him on the hard floor with an over the shoulder throw. Jaden yells out in pain and arches his back. Aunt Mei holds out yesterday’s newspaper before the fallen Jaden. :: ] Aunt Mei: [ :: smiling :: ] You just checked into Whoop Ass Station via Dragon Express? [ :: Jaden sees he was facing a black and white picture of himself performing in the scaffold match that gone with the title “Enter The Dragon”, as the article was talking about Jaden along with his match he had so far in GCW. Jaden slowly sits up while he was favoring his back. :: ] Jaden: [ :: wincing :: ] Is it too late to say that I’m sorry and I love you very much? Aunt Mei: [ :: pretends to think :: ] Uh? [ :: glares at him with a nod :: ] Pretty much. [ :: Fades out :: ]
