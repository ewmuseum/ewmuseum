The Surgoth Story - Chapter 2 - A Mystery Meeting and A New Case

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 I was surprised to see I still had a job come Monday after looking into the unknown virus. As I walked over to my cubical, I saw the Chief standing there waiting for me. I thought for sure I was fired. As I approached closer, the Chief began to get a big smile on his face. Once close enough, the Chief shook my hand. "Great job Van," said the Chief. "I'm confused," I replied. "Thanks to you, Doctor Argath no longer needs to hide nor be protected. We caught the guilty party responsible for the deadly virus attack on the citizens of the city. I apologize for going off on you like I did Van. I thought a good friend of mine was going to end up sitting in jail." I removed my had from his and said to the Chief,"I was only doing my job. This had nothing to do with protecting a friend or anything else. I would have done the same thing no matter who it was. I'm about getting the guilty party, nothing else. Now if you'll excuse me, I have work that needs done." The Chief just looked at me shocked. He thought for sure that I would just forget all about what happened in that room behind the glass just because the guilty man was caught. I don't easily forgive, and I never forget when a wrong has been done to me. The Chief walked away and went into his office. I sat down at my cubical and began to go over some of the paper work at my desk. As I was doing my paper work, I reached over without looking and turned on my computer. Once I was donegoing though some of the paper work I looked up at my computer screen and noticed a yellow post-it note stuck to it. On it was a phone number, '5552121'. I picked up my phone and dialed the number. The phone rang three times then someone picked up. "I hear you're the man to go to for help," said a raspy voice on the other end of the phone before I had a chance to say anything. I could tell the man, or woman, was disguising their voice. "Depends," I began. "What is it you need help with?" "Go to the ally just outside the building your in right now. Wait there for a black limo. Approach the limo and get in the back on the passenger side. I will fill you in with all of the information when we meet." "I don't..." before I could tell the stranger that I don't think it would be a good idea to just take a casual stroll with someone I don't know, he or she hung up. My curiosity was peeked though, which is probably one of my biggest downfalls, but I to know what was going on. I knew I should probably tell someone about this before I left, but I knew as soon as I told them the situation, they would prevent me from going. I collected my gun from my top desk drawer and preceded to the elevator to take me down from the fifth floor, to the lobby. Once the elevator doors opened up to the lobby I walked up to the glass doors, pulled the right side open and stepped out of the building. Outside traffic was a little crazy and it wasn't even rush hour. It was about seven-hundred hours. I walked to the side of the building and into the ally way. There I waited about five minutes, then a limo appeared on the opposite end of the ally that I had entered. I walked up to the limo, got in on the back passenger side of the car and closed the door. In front of me was a person wearing a black ski mask to hide his identity. He then began to talk to me in the same raspy voice he talked to me in on the phone. "So you actually came," he began. "I wasn't sure if you would, thank you." "Just get to it, what is it you called me here for?" I asked a little annoyed that he didn't get right down to business. I had work to be done in the office and I didn't have all day to take a joy ride with this man. Also, I wanted to get back in time for lunch, because of her. "Punctual man I see, I like that in a person. Anyway, I heard about what you did for the good Doctor over in Gildus Labs and I thought maybe you could do something for me. Something has been killing my friends and I'm not quite sure how to explain it." "What do you mean 'Explain it'?" I was a little puzzled about what this man was trying to say. "Their skin becomes cold and purple in color. By the looks of it you would swear all their blood has been drained and the decomposing process has completely stopped. Whatever is killing my friends, I don't think that it is human. I heard that your main practice was Paranormal Activity. Is this true?" "You have defiantly come to the right man but... I usually don't work for someone that has to hide his face from me." "I'm sorry but I must keep my confidentiality." Again I was beginning to get annoyed very quickly. My job is more or less solve mysteries yes, but when the person I'm doing it for is a mystery themselves, I usually don't like to take on the job. Yet, I was intrigued a bit. I hadn't seen anything paranormal since the Day of Awakening, and now looked to be the perfect time to finally experience something exciting. "Can you show me one of the bodies?" * * * The limo pulledto an old warehouse where both the masked man and myself got out. We walked up to the warehouse and the masked man knocked on the door three times, loudly. The door opened and we walked in. Inside there were moer men wearing ski masks, some with tribal designs painted onto them. Almost all of them had machine guns in hand and I had a strange feeling none of these guys followed the law. In the middle of the large room was a table with a sheet across it and by the looks of it a body was laying underneath it. Once up to the table, the man that I rode with waved his hand and the man standing next to the table removed the sheet. I couldn't believe what was before my eyes. It was unlike anything I had ever seen before. The man was telling the truth when he said that the skin had turned purple. The skin was almost prune like which would probably be due to the lack of the body having blood. I searched all over the body and while I did so I pulled out my portable tape recorder. "Body is purple in color. Time of death is undeterminable due to the decomposing act being completely stopped," I scanned down a little lower and noticed a small puncture wound on the victim. Veins spider out in a darker purple color from the wound. Cause of death is unknown but may have been done by whatever made this wound." I stop my tape recorder as my scan of the body is complete. I had no idea what could have done this but I was very intrigued and would defiantly be looking into this. "So what do you think?" asked the man I had ridden in the iimo with. "We are going to have to transport him to the morgue to have and autopsy report made up. I'm going to have to call an ambulance to take him down there." "I would rather you not do that." "If you want help then you are going to have to let me get him to the morgue. There isn't anything else that I can do from here." "Take him yourself." "I can't dop that," now I knew for sure these men were taking part in something illegal. Now I felt like I was helping criminals I had sworn to put away. I promised myself right then and there that not only would I find out what is killing these criminals, but I would also put them away where they belong, in jail. "Where did you find his body originally?" "Right outside the building. He was one of the men that usually stand guard," replied the man. "Fine, place him back there. Get all your men and get the hell out of here. I'll say that it was an anonymous tip that I had gotten. Whatever it is you guys are doing, you will get found out one way or another. This place will be swarming with cops with in the hour and trust me, they will do a complete search of this property. I don't care how well you clean this place up, they will find something." "That won't happen because you're going to keep your moth shut, right?" "Don't worry, your secret is safe with me, but I can't stop the authorities as soon as they find you out." "MOVE OUT!" said the man in the mask to the rest of his men as they began to clear the building. Outside you could hear all of their cars starting up. The man I now took as the one in charge, grabbed the dead body and I followed him outside. He placed the dead body up against a crate sitting up. "This is how he was when we found him. His fun was missing so I haven't a clue where it went." "That's fine," I began. "Now get out of here because I'm about to call the authorities and an ambulance." "Thank you." "Don't thank me just yet." * * * Blue and red lights flashed all over the area at the warehouse that I had been. Cops and CSI Agents swarmed all over the site. Even the Chief had shown up and had approached me. "We've got a problem Van," Said Chief Gilthor. "What is it Chief?" I asked. "Apparently this man belonged to a gang known as The Tribals. How did you find out about this man being here?" "I got an anonymous tip." "And why didn't you tell anyone?" "Because I already knew this sort of case was my cop-o-tea. The man had told me that there was something amiss about this mans death, that it couldn't have been done by a human being. He heard that I was the man for the job." "How did you get here?" asked the Chief. "I don't see your car here anywhere." "Someone picked me up and brought me here," I answered truthfully even though I knew I would more than likely get in trouble for doing so. But I didn't really care, I was doing my job and that was all that mattered. "Are you insane!? First you follow an anonymous tip without telling anyone what you are doing and then you get a ride from some stranger!? One of these days Van I will have your badge! I'll ask you more questions about it later. Right now I want to get this body to the morgue and I want you to go down there with it. I want you gathering as much information on this case as possible," the Chief began to walk away but stopped, and without turning around asked me another question. "Have you found out any more on the Day of Awakening?" "Nothing sir," I answered. "I know what everyone else knows right now. We all woke up with a memory of our name and the jobs we do, nothing else." "Keep looking into it. We need to find out what happened to us before we woke up." The Chief walked away from me and headed over to talk to a few more of the officers. I knew the Chief would be pissed about what I had done but he knew that I was good at what I do. He knew I had my own way of doing things and it always brought results even if he didn't like the methods. As I stood there, I watched the medics put the body in the back of the ambulance and they motioned for me to go with them. I was now to go to the morgue to find out more about what killed this man. WHatever it was, I knew it had something to do with that puncture wound on the side of the dead man. * * * Evening was already drawing near when we finally reached the Diamond City Mortuary. It was now eighteen-hundred hours and we were finally wheeling the body into the lab. I stood in the lab with the dead body for about twenty minutes before the man finally came in to look over the body. The man walked up to me and extended his right hand to shake mine. I obliged. He then put on some gloves and activated his portable tape recorder, then placing it on the metal table beside him. "I'm Rohan Talis, I'll be the one looking over the body," he said to me and then went right into his work. "Skin purple in color and body completely drained of blood. Decomposing process has stopped. Puncture wound on right side." After finding the wound he placed his finger on it and when he removed it, strings of something slimy came with it. I had only missed that because I wasn't going to touch it without a glove. He reached over next to him where his metal table sat and grabbed a petrie dish and a cotton swab. He swabbed the slime from the mans body and rubbed it into the petrie dish. "A slimy substance seems to be surrounding the wound the looks to not only be an entrance wound but also and exit wound. I don't believe the wound is the cause of death. Possibly a parasitic organism burrowed in then back out. Hopefully the culture will show more. I think I will also grab some to put under a microscope." Rohan reached onto the metal table once again to grab a vial that had only a cottom swab in it. He untwisted the cap of the vial and used a cottom swab on the slime again. He placed the swab back in the vial, placed it on the table and shut off the portable tape recorder. "It looks as though the information I was given was right," began Rohan Talis. "Whatever killed this man isn't human. Hopefully these labs will give us some sort of idea what we are dealing with." "How long do you think it will take to get some results?" I asked Rohan. "Give me a few days and you will have all the information you need. They told me you came with the ambulance, want me to call you a cab?" "No," I answered. "It's a nice evening and the station isn't that far from here. I think I'll take myself a nice walk." "Suit yourself. I'll get a hold of you in the next couple days with the results of the test." "Thanks." I walked out of the room and began to head for the exit to the building. I already had a feeling that whatever was killing these men wasn't just some typical parasite. Something tells me that it goes a lot deeper than that.
