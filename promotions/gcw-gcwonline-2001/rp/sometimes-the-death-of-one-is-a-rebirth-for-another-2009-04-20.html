Sometimes, the death of one is a rebirth for another

20 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/20/09 “Our life is made by the death of others” The tires of all the cars in the funeral procession struggle against the puddles in the cobblestone leading up to the mountainous black tents and black chairs hovered around the dirt and rock tomb, awaiting the vessel of a resident to be lowered in. Six men get out of limos and black cars wrapping around the cemetery like the Black Momba, but not ready to strike, ready to mourn? The back of the lead car swings open, casting some white into a world of grey. The inside accents the fact that there is a coffin inside, but more importantly, a fallen friend. The coffin is lifted off of the rollers with ease. Six arms barely strain under their perfectly pressed coats, carrying their comrade to his final resting place. Nobody would expect such an intricate mix of bodies at the same funeral, all sharing the same emotions; heartache? A Scott lying down is rival for the last time? A President lying down his biggest ratings booster? A loyal muscle lying down his employer? A former right hand man lying down the Movement’s creator? A former masked man lying down a mentor? A bestest friend forever lying down his ring mate? Andy Murray, Steven Caldera, Kiljaden Kincaid, Dan Black, AXEL Action, and Clyde Fox all walk with somber faces to a platform set up in the center of the arena of seats under the tents? A priest is now standing, right hand over the coffin, talking to the crowd, but the words are mere mumbles to Zacharia’s ears. The Cemetery of Holy Sepulchre? the cemetery of The Church of the Resurrection, East Orange, NJ. I can barely remember how I came to be sitting here. I look around to see endless faces in sullen sloops, angel white tissues and tightly knitted fabrics raising to the corners of eyes for those too ashamed to show their emotions while others let the salty splintered streams torment their cheeks. Human emotion has always caught my eye. The damaged beauties of God’s creation unable to hold back their emotions, so they turn to a way of breaking down all control of their bodies. It is amazing how hard it is to control anything when you give in. I try to think of it as more of an overwhelming sensation of emotion, for it is hard to resist a sudden shock like your best friend, your mentor being found dead in a hotel room without any leads or conclusions. It is hard to look at all of these faces and fathom being loved, even through all of the hate and exasperation one may feel about a person, it always comes to the summation that we are all human, and human life is so precious. I could only imagine my funeral having a quarter of the people in attendance. Nobody outside of GCW knows me or loves me for that matter. I am truly alone, and it took until this moment to realize this? The priest’s hand is raised in Zacharia Taylor’s direction with hundreds of pairs of eyes looking in his direction. Someone nudges him to catch his attention. His eyes rise to look ahead, his eyes full of despair. The priest motions towards the coffin and steps away with a comforting smile. He stands up, everyone’s eyes still on him. Several whispers begin to join in with the faint conversations of cars whisping through the shallow puddles on the highway behind the cemetery. Zacharia sets a steady pace for the platform the coffin is resting on. Fitting for the ceremony, Taylor is no longer in his familiar hoodie and jeans, but in a very crisp all black suit and black tie. His presentation is a shock, but the words to follow, nobody knows what to expect? “Chris Bagwell came to my side when I needed someone most.” Taylor walks around the coffin, eyes glazing over. “He saved my life in everyway someone can be saved. He stood in as my father, and my mentor, but most importantly, my friend. The man that is lying in front of me was a great man. “Whether you knew him as the Bagwell in the ring with GCW,” he makes eye contact with Andy Murray, “or if you knew him as Chris outside in his troubled life,” he makes eye contact with the grieving Rebecca in the front row, “you were at least fortunate enough to know him. He had a way with words’Let it be known, let it be sworn” Words so strong that he could make you think you can take on the world or enrage you to want to go on a killing spree. Trust me, I have been on both sides of these tactics.” Zacharia stares at the coffin for a few moments. A cough breaks his concentration and he looks out into the crowd. He catches so many eyes from those in GCW, and many look away shaking their head in shame or breaking the eye contact out of sympathy. It is hard to look someone in the eyes that you have never really been able to get along with in the company? He catches a familiar face in the crowd, but is baffled by who it is, until several strands of damp hair cover his face in the wind; AXEL Action. “We are all human, and we are all vulnerable?” AXEL nods his head, not breaking eye contact, but Taylor’s eyes start to wallop in tears again, so he looks out into the mystifying rain. “Tears of angels fall upon us today, shedding the external layer of our differences to reveal how similar we all actually are. I look around and see the faces staring back at me. We are all hurting, and we are all in need of comfort. Death is a cycle of life that I am all too familiar with, and it truly pains me?” Zacharia struggles with his words. His struggles are accented with sobs and coughs in the crowd. The priest comes over to give him support, but he stands up straight and wipes away a tear on his eye with his finger. He clears his throat and continues: “I look at death as another part of the cycle of life. No matter if you believe in the same religion as I do or not, there is a cycle or plan for every person on Earth. We are born, we live, and we die, but it is what we make of our lives that truly matters. When I look down at this coffin, it reminds me that our life is made by the death of others. It is the wisdom and lessons that we take from the dead that make our lives thrive. “Chris had this idea of motivation to settle problems. If you had the motivation for change, there will be change, if you had the motivation for truth, you would be granted truth. Sometimes, even our masters fall back on vices to settle problems, but I don’t find this vice to be the death of Chris Bagwell. I do have the motivation for truth, and I will find out?” Once more, his emotions get the best of him, but his emotions turn to anger to push through the tears. “Let it be known, let it be sworn” “I’ll never forget you, Chris’I’ll never forget you. I’m sorry, I’m so sorry. You only wanted to help and I pushed you away, thinking I was too good” I’m so sorry?” As Zacharia falls away from the coffin in pain, Andy Murray and AXEL Action jump up near the casket and help him up. Many people in the crowd break down in his pain as he is brought back to his seat off to the side. Taylor rocks himself back and forth until all thoughts fade out into the crowd and rain. ***** Zacharia is standing beside the open grave of Chris Bagwell, casket already lowered to its final depths. The priest is standing beside Taylor, holding up a hand to the cemetery crew ready to put dirt to the final chapter of the former GCW wrestler. Taylor’s eyes are swollen and red with tears, hands trembling to hold onto his hoodie draped around his strained fingers. “Chris, it was you who helped me into the man I am today. I always looked up to you, even when we went out different ways in GCW. I should have been there for you when you needed it. I should have been there, Chris.” Zacharia’s weak fingers let go of the hoodie in his fingers, allowing it to faintly glide through the air in what seems like slow motion down to the casket. Zacharia begins to break down again, falling to his knees. The priest grabs his shoulder, afraid he is going to fall into the grave. “I’m retiring my hoodie for you, Chris. A new Zacharia Taylor will rise from your ashes?”
