Do You Believe?

12 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-12-06 In our so-called everyday life, we have people all around the world in the wrestling business striving to become the absolute best. They strive for everything they believe in, because in their heart, they know they can accomplish anything if given the proper opportunity. In our everyday life, there’s plenty of opportunities just waiting to be fulfilled. Waiting for the right person to come along and take advantage of absolutely everything they can. When a certain opportunity arises that can help you prove yourself worthy to all mankind, you know damn well you’re going to take it, whether you believe in yourself or not. If you’re the only person believing in yourself, though, it tends to throw your confidence off-track. You don't focus as much as you should. You can’t help but think what other people are thinking about you. If you’re being laughed at behind your back? Are people gathering around in a little circle, saying? “This guy has absolutely no chance in hell, no matter how hard he tries.” People are the meanest of the mean around this so-called world. Sure, they’re nice and perky to your face, because you’re nice to them, but once you turn around, you can feel the knife digging in inch by inch. You can’t wait for the knife to come out so you can feel better, so the pain can be forgotten. But that’s just it, the pain is never gone. The unbearable tolerance stays with you for a very, very long time. The longer you’ve been around here, the more you realize it. There’s no doubt that you see more, experience more, and eventually learn more. You see more backstabbing in one day than bumps in a wrestling ring. You truly begin to see the two sides of every person, some you can trust, and others you aren’t so sure about. But that’s life, and supposedly, there is nothing you can ever do to change it. Supposedly. =?=?=?=?=?= “Title versus title,” he whispered softly to himself. Sitting on the edge of the bed, the off white linens thrown back messily, Jayce Bradley stared down at the flyer that had been faxed over to his rather lifeless hotel room. It was a rundown of the various matches scheduled for the forty fourth edition of WorldWide, and it informed him that he was scheduled to face off against Jason Warr in a match for both, the Television and the Cruiserweight championships. He wanted to smile, happy that his abilities were finally being noticed by those in charge. For once, he did not have to remind people that he was on the roster. The moment would have been perfect, if he was not in Mexico partaking in the 2006 Total J Cup at the moment. Then again, it could very well be perfect timing, if his presumptions about the Global Championship Wrestling booking committee having it in for him were actually true. Still, there was no way he could let this opportunity pass him by. He would be stupid to worry about a place in a tournament more than a title in his home fed, and he knew it. He balled up the paper and tossed it toward the waste basket in the far corner of the room, banking it off the wall to watch it fall a few inches short of its intended destination. “Damn it,” he said with a sigh, hoping that would be the only time he would fall short this week. So much of his future depended on the outcome of this match. Not just for the fact that his title was on the line, but because he had been overlooked for so long. Someone had taken notice, and not only could he not afford to disappoint whoever it was that granted him this opportunity’but he could not bear to face the fact that Jason Warr had done nothing in GCW to warrant the title that he wore proudly as his own. Just another one of the new faces that was catapulted to the front of the line, passing up the wrestlers that had paved the road they were speeding through. Something that needed to be changed, and fast. He stood up reluctantly, clutching at his chest a bit as he struggled to remain upright. Tugging at his shirt, he managed to lift it up over his head; revealing a few bruises in the shape of hand prints he obtained from his latest meeting with Evan Jackson. Sixty three wrestlers signed up for the Total J Cup this year, and Jayce managed to end up drawing the man he defeated to move on to the fourth round of GTT5, and solidly defeated him again to move on to the second round of this tournament. Angelo Sinclaire seriously needed to hand him the FUSE Universal title, if for no other reason than just being better than Damage...Hands down. He smiled at the realization that entering various tournaments over the last few months’and actually doing quite well in them’had not only brought himself recognition throughout the business’but brought recognition to GCW as well. Something that surely weighed heavily on the booking commitee's sudden change of heart. Rich Rollins winning GTT5 vastly overshadowed Bradley’s run in the tournament, but not to the point where he could not be remembered at all. Even now, during the Total J Cup, his name has been tossed around as one of the names that could make it very far’if not take it all. GCW Cruiserweight Champion. GCW Television Champion. Total J Cup 2006 winner. PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion. The possibilities were endless. All he had to do was believe in himself, and in doing so make others believe in him as well. Not an easy task, but one that Jayce planned on tackling head on at WorldWide Forty Four. Where winning is not an option’it’s a necessity.
