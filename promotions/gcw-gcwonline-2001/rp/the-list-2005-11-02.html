The list

2 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2/05 As soon as the GCW crew dug me up and I recovered enough to remember what happened I was.. mad? No. Angry? No. Pissed? No. I was fucking livid. 'I'm gonna fuckin' kill that piece of shit!' I screamed as I reached my locker room. I tore it apart in a fit of rage. I collapsed in a gasping heap as I destroyed everything I could get my hands on. I had taken the monitor that was in my room and smashed it to the floor, I had broken the table in my room, I had tossed the steel chairs around denting the lockers. I smashed the mirror. I finally settled down enough to take a shower and wash the dirt and blood off of me. I got dressed as a line from an L7 song ran through my head. 'When I get mad and I get pissed I grab my pen and I write out a list Of all the assholes who won't be missed You've made my shitlist!' And right now Michael Stevens had made his way onto my list. I already had Bryan Mayhem on it. Along with Dan Rivero, Caldera Mask and the other masked flunky that was running around with them. Who the fuck did they think they were targeting me? And now on top of all that I find out that I'm in a six man tag with Vince Jones and Rich Rollins going against Seymour Almasy, Stevens and Ricky Thunder. I finally realized my cell phone was ringing, I pulled it from my bag. 'WHAT?' I screamed into it. 'I don't blame you for being mad, but it's not my fault.' 'Mad?' I said, 'Jeff, I'm not mad, I went beyond mad twenty minutes ago. I'm way beyond mad here man! I am fucking livid! Michael Fucking Stevens cost me my shot at immortality. I was all set to bury his Fruity alter ego when he buried me! HE FUCKING BURIED ME ALIVE JEFF!' 'Yeah, and that's not my fucking fault!' Jeff yelled back at me. 'I had nothing to do with it! I wasn't there! Now calm the fuck down and listen to me and listen to me good. You've got other fish to fry, I know you wanna rip Steven's head off and shit down his neck, and I don't blame you. But Dan Rivero and his masked cronies threatened you, they targeted you, and to make sure that doesn't happen, I will personally fly out to Minneapolis and keep an eye on you at the next WorldWide.' 'I don't need a fucking baby sitter.' I said grabbing up my gear and heading out of the locker room. A few crew members looked at me uncertainly as they had heard the ruckus I had made in my locker room. 'What the fuck are you looking at?' I snarled at one who backed off and found the floor very interesting to look at. 'No, but you do need someone to watch your back.' Jeff said, 'You've got a target on your back and Dan Rivero is aiming for it.' 'Fuck Dan Rivero.' 'That's what I say, but I'm not taking any chances. You're my friend first and foremost, and you're also a valuable investment in Wylde Tyme Entertainment. If you think even for a second I'm risking either of those things you're in for a motherfuckin' surprise. I'm gonna be at WorldWide and if Rivero and his asshole crew come a calling, I'll be there to intercept them.' 'Fine.' I said reaching the parking lot and working my way toward my rental car, 'But I'm not taking any prisoners in this six man tag match that the booking comittee put me in. Stevens is gonna be my main target and if Almasy and Thunder get in my way, they're gonna find out why I spent my first ten matches in GCW without a loss on my record. I'm gonna see to it that Stevens never makes it to NC-17 in time to loose to LeStatt Knight again. I may see to it that he never makes it to a wrestling ring ever again. I don't care if he does make it to NC-17 and does somehow manage to beat LeStatt Knight. I want that son-of-a-bitch in a ring, so I can dismantle him!' 'Don't look past Rivero. He's gotten more dangerous. Just like LeStatt has. Like I said, if you had told me back in the first incarnation of GCW that LeStatt Knight would one day be the World Heavyweight Champion, I would have laughed at you until I was blue in the face, but I've watched him evolve from a joke to the most dominant wrestler of all time. Rivero seems to have turned up his game a notch and for some reason has his sights set on you.' 'He's set his sights on the wrong motherfucker.' I spat as I reached my car, 'I'll gouge out his eyes and skull fuck him if he thinks even for a second that the Machine is gonna sit by and take a beating from a halfwit like him.' 'Mike, you're better than this. Just take some time to calm down and think this thing through rationally. Stevens and Rivero are no pushovers, and I have a damn good idea who's behind that Steven Caldera mask. And if it is who I think it is, you're in deeper shit than you can possibly imagine.' 'Yeah?' I asked, 'And who might that be?' 'I'd rather not say,' Jeff said, 'You never know who might be listening.' 'Nobody's listening, I'm in my car.' He told me. 'But I thought he was--' 'So did I,' Jeff said, 'But I've been wrong before. I wasn't there, I don't know what happened, and I don't know if he was there. And after all, those are rumors, and rumors have a tendency to take on a life of their own, especially ones involving wrestlers.' 'We'll discuss this when you get to Minneapolis.' I said, finally calming down enough to drive without thinking that this was a live version of Grand Theft Auto. 'In the meantime I'm gonna go back to the hotel room, and entertain homicidal thoughts about several GCW Superstars.' Jeff laughed, 'You do that. I'll see you in a couple of days.' 'Later.' I snapped my phone shut and headed back to the hotel, somewhat calmer than I had been when I had been dug up nearly forty minutes earlier. I was still mad, but it was just mad. Michael Stevens would get his. So would Bryan Mayhem. But first I had Dan Rivero to contend with. No one makes a target out of the Machine and gets away with it. Dan Rivero and his cronies could come after me, but they'd find me ready. And they'd find out they've made the biggest mistake of their lives.
