Date: 3/1/06 Prologue: ---- ~Osaka, Japan~ ~Somewhere~ The man looked across the table at his 'business partner'. Other tables around showed this to be some sort of bar or pub. The patronage was sparse, still early evening or late afternoon. He was older, showing the signs of age. His golden brown skin and darker hair showed him to not be like everyone else in the room. They were all Oriental for lack of a better term. He was dressed plainly, a simple suit and tie in a navy blue motif. The woman across the table from him looked like a reject from a goth punk video, her deep olive eyes painted with the most garish pink that could be found. She was dressed in black vinyl,PVC clothing. Jewelry all over her body showed that she was very much into the entire fetish and Gothic scene. Introspective: ---- You know, there are some things that people look for in this world. Some want a nice, easy ride to the top. Then they can sit there and laugh at all of those who are toiling below them. Some want everything to be given to them. Some want to rest on their laurels and constantly live in the past: Victories, loves, trials, and tribulations. I'm the cousin to both the infamous Sean and Fey McClancy twins that are associated with another wrestling entertainment company. I'm the son of Conner McClancy, twin brother to Duncan McClancy. Conner met a woman that he fell head over heals for, Rosemary. She was a full blooded Moapa Paiute Native from the Valley of Fire area near Las Vegas. Conner made the decision to leave the team, and leave his family over her. They married and shortly thereafter I was born on the same reservation that my mother came from. There were of course things from their joining that even I didn't understand. Many years later, I got a special appointment to go to Japan to learn the art that my father had done also. I was invited to attend a special place known as the South Wind Academy. I learned under two people, a married couple at the time by the name of Ian and Xian Childress. I learned upon my graduation from that school that it was also being used as a recruitment center for an organization known as the Order of the Hammer. This organization was more then what he had expected. At the time I thought they were just a bunch of freaks and I left them to their own devices. He's had an entire federation put upon his shoulders to hold it up and support it. So have I. But where I surpass him, is that I not only had a federation on my shoulders, but I had an entire fucking inter-fed. No, it wasn't PTC. No, but it's biggest competitor ever, Revolution Studios. I held it up on my own, along with the Superior Championship Wrestling Federation. But I wasn't just a world champion. No, I was the Superior Champion. Then you have to ask, what happened? That was years ago, right? What happened was something that I've since learned from. My big mistake was letting it all go to my head. I got an ego. I thought I was 'da shit'. I had contracts to promote soft drinks, shoes, t-shirts. I had bands being paid to appear in my entrance videos while they sang their songs for me to come to the ring with. I had the cars that cost a quarter of a million dollars. I had millions of dollars in the bank. I had it all. And then, what happened when I got injured? I lost almost everything. All the way down to my dignity. Eventually I even lost the mother of my two children, my twin girls. You learn that you can't bank on your past anymore. You can't sit there and tell someone that because your a former world champion, you're the best. Because guess what, if you're not the dawg standing at the top of the hill, you're nothing but a has-been. Admittedly that is much better then being a never-was. This is a lesson that many can learn though. Especially someone with a French name, and an English last name for his in ring nome de guerre. Mr. LeStatt Knight, the FORMER GCW World Champion. Interesting isn't it. That GCW has never given him a rematch? Instead they book him into the tourney with everyone else. They don't bother to sit there and loud the fact that he's the former champion. Instead it's all about the newcomers in Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, Vivica Valentine, even in comparison Seymour Almasy. Knight doesn't realize that it's all in the past for him. It's all yesterday's news. In fact, the only person who's been seen tooting Mr. Knight's horn recently are some poor fans who don't seen to know better, grasping at the previous career highs for the large man from Mexico, himself, and well, not to many others. A man who has taken it upon himself to walk around wearing his sunglasses, berating others as to if they're there to service him in some manner... all because at one time, he was on top. But he's not anymore. He's in the exact same boat as I am. I'm not the top here. I'm far from it actually. But I have a chance. A chance to become that which would make me great again. For me it's been years of looking at the others out there make it to the big time. Especially the year and a half I was in my cousin's federation. Constantly I was pushed aside for someone else to allow them to get the rub while I waited and waited. But the playing field is far different now. And this is GCW. Some would say that I just walked into LeStatt Knight's back yard, his playground. I'm going into his ring as the challenger. I say that his payment for that land, that property, that backyard, that playground is long overdue. And I'm the man coming to repossess it, and take it over. I've had years to think about what mistakes I made during my time at the top. They won't happen again. I'm still young, I'm still a solid worker. I know I have the tools to get there again. This time, I have the attitude to get there, stay there and hold onto that brass ring. The puzzle to success is finally complete. I've fixed my personal life, and no longer am I alone. I have Ukyo beside me. I have custody of my daughters. I've dropped my facade, and not only have I embraced the Order which trained me, but have worked my way up to a field officer position. I've embraced both sides of my heritage, becoming more then you would ever hope to be for something so... human. But now it's time for my career to be returned to that which it once was. Knight, you're the next step on a long road for me back to the top. Win or loose, you will know you were in nothing but a fight. And the rest of the GCW, PTC and the wrestling world will know that Blaise William Todd McClancy is back, and the time to take him lightly is no more. He watched as she assumed her full heritage in front of the man who'd spawned her line. The Angel of Heaven known as Kokabel. Blaise watched as the Angel pushed Gray aside like a paperweight and then undeniably forced him back to his humanoid form. Blaise took the hint and changed back on his own. As he combat suit returned to it's normal form, he reached down and flipped a switch on a small device on his belt. Dani yelled at the man again. ?MORNING STAR! Grandfather! That is enough! This is unneeded and dangerous. How dare you risk my friend's safety for your little game.? He moved towards her and opened his mouth. And then the two started to talk. If you could call it that. He could see the look of confusion on both Ukyo's and Josh's mouths. Gray was still confused in his own right as he was coming out of a blood rage. But Blaise had heard this voice before. When he had been an Assassin hired out to the ArchAngel Zadikiel, the angel that he ended up killing and taking his divine sword from him, he'd heard this language when Zadikiel had been visited by others of his kind. It was known only as the Divine. Or some would say... Babel. The original language of the world when all races were one. Angelic language that was the true, pure form of speech. And Blaise was recording every word they said. He waited while the spoke, Dani looking angry to a point, and Kokabel looking like the protective grandfather who was worried about his grandchild. Blaise waited while they spoke. He wasn't the main player right now. This had all been a setup though. When they were done, Kokabel approached him. ?I am sorry my friend, Blaise... or Turren, yes... that's the other name for you. Prince Turren MacOgma. I know your kind now. I'm sorry for this test, but I had to make sure you where worthy of protecting my granddaughter. She's very special to me. She's... one of a kind.? Blaise just nodded his head, trying to show respect to this being. But he was pissed off for being used like that. It was the one thing that he'd always hated having been done to him, ever. However, Kokabel apparently didn't want a confrontation with him. In a rush of air the angel had taken to the air and sailed out of the window high on the wall. Blaise looked around at the dead bodies around them. They were unnatural, they would disappear on their own. But maybe not before morning. Ukyo came across the device on his forehead to him. ?I've already signaled for a cleanup crew. Kogagawa-san wants to know what shit you've gotten into now.? Blaise looked at his girlfriend and suppressed a laugh. The Order's commander in this area was his old teacher at the South Wind Academy in Okinawa. ?Did you tell him?? ?Of course.? Blaise just shook his head. He pulled a small round item off his belt and tossed it out into the middle of the room. ?Locator set. They'll be able to find it.? Ukyo nodded at him. ?Holy Shit!? Blaise turned as Dani answered the voice of Joshua Brand. ?What?? Dani had returned to her more normal looking form. Gray had started to stand back up on his own. ?Hey, I might not have been able to move, but damn. I did get to see. Fucking 'A' Blaise, you weren't kidding when you said that Ukyo was the artillery. She's a fucking tank!? Blaise again tried to suppress a laugh. He knew that Ukyo was usually embarrassed when she received any praise other then from him. He walked over and took his arm around her small waist. ?Yeah. People usually don't believe me when I say she's a real firecracker.? He then watched as Dani moved to open the large door to the warehouse. He reached down and turned off the recorder unit he had turned on. If everything went right, he had their entire conversation recorded. He would send it back to Annie, the Order's chief researcher and the actually office officer to this case. But he knew what she'd do with it if it was good. She'd take it to the husband of his cousin Fey. The man known as the Keeper of Secrets. Colonel G. Ian Childress III. The man who was also known as ?The Wrath?. He'd know what to do with it. That's for sure.
