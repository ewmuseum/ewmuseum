Date: Blah She’d be waiting for him inside the room, and he knew she wasn’t going to be happy. They hadn’t spoken in almost two weeks, since Lance had really taken his downswing. He should’ve answered her calls, but hadn’t, he should have called her back, but again he hadn’t. There were a million things he could, and should have done to make the next few moments easier, but he’d been unable to bring himself to do them. Truth be told, he was afraid to speak to her, afraid of how her grief would reflect his own guilt back on him a thousand times stronger. So he stood, hands buried in pockets, head hung low. He felt like dirt, if dirt could feel, trod on, abused, the lowest of the low. In a perfect world, he’d boldly walk through the door before him, take her in his arms, comfort her, tell her everything would be alright in time. However, this was far from a perfect world, and right now, he wasn’t even certain if he should knock or not. Either way he’d feel like an intruder, her grief was something that should be sacred, private, and he was about to shatter her peace. The easy thing to do would to just hold her, and let her grieve but he couldn’t do that, not now. He’d spent too much time with his own guilt weighing him down not to alleviate it. He’d come to his decision that he’d tell her what really happened, today. She had to know before the memorial service. He couldn’t deny her that, she had to send her brother off knowing the truth. He’d gotten all of his information from Alex, he could at least speak to him. After Lance’s brain activity had slowly declined to nothingness, Sheena had been fairly quick to make the decision to pull the plug on his life support. From what Alex had said it wasn’t an easy decision for her, but then again how could it have been for her or anyone else? He said she’d decided the way she had, because she couldn’t stand to see him suffering any longer than he already had, made sense. It made everything so much worse knowing that he was responsible for having her go through that. If he’d have just been able to hold his temper none of this would have happened. He was a monster, and she had to know that. He had to step up and take responsibility for his actions, he could only hope that she’d understand how many other people going to the police could hurt. Maybe they deserved it they had lied to protect him, but he hoped she wouldn’t be that vindictive. Pulling out his wallet he produced the keycard for the door. Briefly he wondered how much money this room had ended up costing him. Sheena had been staying there for almost a month now, and the bill would be astronomical. Just another thing he’d have to deal with, it was his fault after all. Stepping inside his eyes immediately began to sting from the thick cloud of incense that choked the room. The vapor made a valiant effort to overcome the stench of stale cigarette smoke, cheap liquor, and old vomit that pervaded the room, however it was obviously a losing battle. The blinds were drawn and the lights off, the room wasn’t pitch black, but it would difficult to see for a few moments, especially with the haze in the air. Gritting his teeth against a wave of nausea Stevens continued forward letting the door shut behind him. Whether it was the odor or the situation that made him feel sick, he wasn’t exactly sure, but he wouldn’t let it stop him. Telling Sheena was the only thing that mattered now. Hopefully after he got it off his chest he could begin living again, with or without her. “Sheena?” He spoke quietly, but such was the dead silence of the cavernous room that he heard it echoed back as a whisper. Patiently he waited, he didn’t want to wake her up, as most likely she hadn’t been able to get much sleep lately. That would account for the liquor smell, she’d never been much of a drinker, passing out after only a few drinks, of course that would also account for that unhealthy vomit smell that clung to everything. For as bad as the room smelled, it looked remarkably clean. Everything was a little too neatly placed however, almost as if someone had been making an effort to conceal the decay that rested just beneath the surface. He made an effort himself to tread lightly as he walked through the living suite towards the bedroom area, almost afraid that if he took a wrong step he’d break through the exterior revealing the seedy underbelly of corruption. Reaching the doorway he paused. He wasn’t sure whether or not he should proceed. Waking her up would be an awful way to start out the conversation at hand. Still he was afraid that if he put it off any longer than he already had, he’d loose the nerve. It had been hard enough to work up enough guts to come here in the first place, and every second he waited wore away at his resolve. Trying to compose his face into something resembling a comforting smile he opened the door, and stepped through into the bedroom. She lay on the bed, covers askew. Fully dressed she was curled into a ball, one pillow beneath her head, another clutched tightly at her breast. She was still save for her breathing, she could’ve been asleep, but it seemed her breathes were too uneven for that. Perhaps she was just taking a few moments to herself to quietly grieve. “Sheena?” He spoke a little more firmly this time, and at the sound of his voice she rolled over to face him. She looked ravaged by time, as if she’d aged ten years in the last month. Deep dark circles hung beneath eyes red and puffy from constant tears. Upon seeing him, she attempted to smile, but she couldn’t hold the expression long. However, her eyes filled with a strange type of hope as he looked over at him. “You came back.” Of all the things he expected her to say, that was one of that last. She should have been furious, he’d ignored her in a time of tragedy. Yet, it seemed she was more surprised that he’d returned, rather than angry at his absence. Wearily she rose from the bed, and practically fell into his arms, sobbing. “I know why you’ve been gone. You hate me because all this is because of me. He was getting better, and I made him get me something. If I hadn’t done that he’d still be alive, I set him up to get killed. It’s all my fault Michael, I’m so sorry, so sorry.” Worst fears realized he froze. He could’ve handled angry, or heartbroken, but guilt was something he just couldn’t deal with. Instantly he felt worse than he ever thought possible, not only had he caused her to loose her brother, but she blamed herself for his mistakes. How could he have possibly done this to her, he was something worse than a monster. Yet there was a part of his mind working that screamed for him to take this as the way out. He could just agree, say she’d made a mistake, it could happen to anyone. He could get out of this free and clear, and they could remain together. Well, not really free and clear, he’d still have to deal with the guilt gnawing a hole in his gut. He’d still have to deal with everything left unresolved eventually. No, he couldn’t take the easy way out, no matter how tempting it may seem at the moment. Quietly he made calming noises, cooing softly into her ear. He had to tell her, but he couldn’t right now, had to calm her down first. Until he did that the conversation wouldn’t go anywhere. Directing her back towards the bed he sat, and allowed her to curl up in his lap to cry. Hopefully she’d be calm soon, he didn’t know how much longer he’d have the guts to tell her what he had to. ************************************************************************************ He wasn’t even aware he’d fallen asleep until his eyes opened. Sheena was still curled up in his arms, looking completely peaceful. In sleep the lines of worry had smoothed from her face, and she looked like herself again, if only for a moment. He was loathe to move for fear he might awaken sleeping beauty. She must be so tired, he couldn’t bear to drag her out of what looked to be the most peaceful sleep she’d had in weeks. Still his entire body was beginning to hurt from staying in such an awkward position so long. Years on the road, and old aches told him that if he didn’t move soon, he might not be able to move for quite some time. Deciding future mobility was more important than another few minutes of sleep for her, he as gently as possible tried to remove her from his lap. If he were lucky she wouldn’t wake up anyway. Everything seemed to be going right until he moved his hands away from her. Almost immediately she cried out, grasping at the air. Despite himself Stevens chuckled, she’d never been able to sleep once he moved. She was kind of like a child that way. As she began to awake Stevens reached down and lifted a lock of her hair away from her face. “Sheena, it’s time to wake up.” As she continued to drag her way into a wakened state, Stevens drug a chair to the side of the bed and sat. This was going to be tough, but he had to do it right now, just like a band aid, quick and as painless as possible. Once she’d sat up, he reached across the gap between them, and held her hands. Steeling himself with a deep breath, he looked into her eyes. “Listen, sweetie, there’s something I’ve got to tell you.” That probably wasn’t the best way to start off, he could already see she made the wrong assumption. He should have thought back to what she’d said after he’d first walked in. She looked crushed, apparently having already decided this is the speech where he would tell her to get out of his life once and for all. He had to speak quickly, lest she tear up again. “Michael I?” “No, don’t say anything this is really important. Shit, I don’t even know how to say this, it’s so fucked up. The thing is you’re not responsible. I am.” And there it was. Finally out there, not the whole story, not yet, but he’d at least started it. Honestly he was already starting to feel a little better, that huge weight being lifted from his chest. Still he realized that the next few moments were going to be much more difficult, as he could see already she didn’t fully understand the gravity of his confession. “Mike, that’s sweet but you can’t taken responsibility for?” “Oh believe me, I can. Look, I’m not proud of it, and you’ve got understand that I know I fucked up, like big time. This has been eating me up inside ever since it happened, I can’t believe I did something like this.” It was starting to sink in for her, that much was clear from the dawning terror in her eyes. He had to look away quickly lest he lose his resolve. He couldn’t bear the thought that she’d be afraid of him. “It was an accident, I was upset lost my head. When Alex showed up at the restaurant, with you and him like that, I was so pissed off. I couldn’t believe he’d put that trash in his own sister’s arm. I went crazy, I didn’t even realize what I was doing until it was done. Honestly I barely remember it, but I know I did it, Alex had to pull me off?” She pulled away in the moment, the fear in her eyes quickly turning to shock and revulsion. Taking a deep breath the caught his eyes and held them, and he could tell deep inside she was pleading that she was wrong in believing what he said. “No, no, you’re lying. You’re just trying to make me not feel so guilty. You can’t be telling the truth, I don’t believe it.” Sighing, he hung his head, quietly excepting the rebuke. He wanted her to be mad, angry, furious. This questioning, this desperate foolish trust in him was something he didn’t expect. It hurt, like flaming daggers searing, slashing into his soul. He couldn’t take much more of this, nothing he’d done to prepare could have gotten him ready for this. “It’s true Sheena, I’m so fucking sorry, it was a mistake, that’s all I can say.” He felt the tears start to come then. He couldn’t stop them, and buried his face in his hands. After all the time he’d worried about her becoming too emotional, he’d never stopped to consider how much emotion he was feeling. Never stopped to consider how much this all had really effected him. In the end he was a victim, of himself. The force of the slap across his cheek send him out of the chair on onto the ground. He’d been hit harder before, but he couldn’t remember something ever hurting so bad. “YOU MONSTER! YOU SICK PIECE OF SHIT! HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO ME” HOW COULD YOU DO THAT TO HIM?” She was already up and standing grabbing whatever small objects were handy. Each of them bounced off his prone body. She threw them with a force that belied her small stature. She was much stronger than she looked but then again he already knew that. With each object that struck him, he could almost see the ties that bound he and Sheena snap. This had been his ultimate betrayal, it was all over, and all the pain he’d ever caused her was now bubbling over. The flurry of knickknacks continued to pelt him, as he curled up. Desperately he thought back to that night, realizing how Lance had been in this same position. Only instead of knickknacks thrown by a ninety-five pound woman, he was being beaten by a two-hundred-fifty pound trained fighter. The helpless of his own situation frightened him, he could only imagine how terrifying it must’ve been for Lance. Eventually the hail stopped, and he was able to look up. She seethed in barely contained fury. Her hair stood out wildly around her head, and there was a look in her eyes almost fiercely animal. She stood glaring down at him, as if trying to make a decision, she wanted to kill him that was obvious. But there was something else, something almost more frightening. “I’m calling the police.” There it was, he’d expected that. He had to stop her from calling, not for him, he deserved it, but there were others to think about. He couldn’t let more people suffer for his mistakes, they’d only been trying to help him. “You can’t?” “Why the FUCK not Michael” You beat my brother to death, with your bare hands. Then you lied to me, and the police about it. Tell me exactly why the fuck I shouldn’t call the police and have you sent to jail for what you’ve done?” “Cindy, her husband, Alex.” That stopped her, as she’d already began moving towards the phone to make the call. Obviously she’d been expecting some sniveling response to save his own skin. He couldn’t blame her for the thought, after all in all the time she’d known him, he’d looked out for number one constantly. “What do they have to do with anything?” Slowly he got up from the floor and too his knees, he didn’t want to stand, not yet. Leaning one arm on the chair he’d been sitting in only moments before he lowered his head, being unable to maintain any sort of eye contact. “I know I deserve to be punished. If I could take it all back, I never would have let them lie for me. But I was barely aware of what was happening. I shut down after I realized what I’d done Sheena. It was Cindy and her husband that talked to the police first. They made up the story to protect me. They covered up a crime for me, they could go to jail, at best he’d be disgraced and loose his job, Sheena would probably have the company stolen out from under her. I’m so fucking sorry all of this happened, if I could change things I would, but I can’t it’s done. I want you to understand that Sheena. I should go to jail, but I don’t know how to do that without hurting the people I care about it. I don’t want anymore people to have to suffer for my stupid mistake. Please, can’t you just stop and see that?” He’d meant every word he just said, and he hoped Sheena could see that. Cindy, her husband, Alex, they shouldn’t have to suffer for what he’d done. On top of everything else that might be the final straw, he wasn’t sure he could take it if she did end up calling the police. Still there wasn’t a whole lot more he could do to stop her than he’d already done. The ball was completely in her court, she had all the power. “You mean that don’t you?” “Yes. I feel guilty enough, I’ve completely destroyed one life, don’t make me responsible for ruining three more. Please, I know you’ve got to hate me right now, and I deserve that too, but don’t punish them to punish me.” “I almost believe you, I must be a complete idiot. You’d say anything to save your own skin. You’ve just gotten smarter about it, that’s all.” She turned away from him, but didn’t move further towards the phone. Wrapping her arms around herself she stood trembling slightly. Stevens finally stood taking a couple steps to stand behind her. He placed a hand on her shoulder, and at first she began to pull away but stopped. She was waiting for him to speak. “Look, you’re not wrong, normally I’d be trying to save myself. But I fucked up bad this time, I know it, and I’d change it if I could. But I can’t, and I can’t be punished for it without hurting people who only wanted to make sure I didn’t get punished. Don’t you think that eats me up inside” Sure I can be a bastard, but I’m human, you know that right?” She turned to face him, arms still crossed over her chest. The trembling had been from the silent tears rolling down her face. “I know Michael, and I don’t think I would have called the police really. I was just so mad, I mean, I still am. I don’t know what to think really. I want you to see something though.” Turning she walked across the room to where he’d been laying. From the ground she picked up a note book. Glancing over it quickly, she found what she was looking for on the page it had fallen open to. She walked back to Stevens and held it out, pointing to about mid-page. “What’s this?” “It was Lance’s, I think he was making a journal of his addiction recovery. Just read where I’m pointing, I think you need to see it.” “Um, ok.” It struck him as an odd request but he’d do anything to humor her. She still held his fate, as well as the others in her hands. Right now he’d give her anything she wanted. Looking down the page he caught his name, ah, Lance had been writing about him. That’s why she’d wanted him to see it. Reading over the paragraph his knees began to feel weak. What he was reading couldn’t be right, it didn’t make any sense. “This can’t be true?” ~To Be Continued~
