Fox Diaries: Super Bowl XLII

10 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/10/08 START Flashback to Sunday, February 3, 2008. 1:00 pm Super Bowl Sundae? Sunday. Having the status that the lucky TV Champion, Clyde E. Fox has, it’s no surprise that our hero has scored himself a pair of Super Bowl XLII tickets. The sun is up on a crisp, but somewhat overcast day as Super Bowl Sunday has finally arrived in the wonderful city of Glendale, Arizona. No, his favorite Cardinals will not be playing in today’s game, instead, two teams on the brink of history. To be honest, Clyde was more worried about scalping the second ticket he acquired. In the one and a half star Motel 6, we find our hero. Clyde exits a nearby elevator and into the front foyer. The sun sparkles through the horizon and lays claim to the desert below as Clyde faithfully checks out. Fox: Heto. Front Desk Lady: Hiya. Thanks for your stay sir. Fox: Oh thanks! I appreciate you letting me check out so late. Front Desk Lady: We change our hours every Super Bowl for more convenience. Fox: Well thank you very very very very very very very very very much! Front Desk Lady: Okay, bye now. And have a great Super Bowl. Fox: Yeah? it shouldn’t be a problem; I’m swinging by that kitchen d’cor store. Front Desk Lady: For what? Fox: I’m buying a super bowl. The lady stands there with an awkward look on her face. Fox: No not funny? Humph, I might be losing my touch. Oh well, ta ta! Clyde makes his way out of the building and over to a nearby coach bus. With his clothes breezing in the wind, Clyde grasps the cargo handle on the side of the bus and proceeds to open the hatch. After tossing his very lightly packed suitcase in the storage compartment, Clyde climbs aboard the big white bus in hope to make it to the stadium within a decent amount of time. The bus was empty except for the driver who was checking seats for any garbage. Fox: Heto sir. The bus driver stood up and looked at Clyde. Driver: Hi? I’m Loretta. Fox: Oh Martha Stewart! I’m sorry; you’re BTM blatantly screamed male-ness. Loretta: BTM? Fox: Bad teenage mustache. It’s okay though, it looks good on you. The 45 year old Loretta shakes her head in disgust as she bumps her way by Clyde. Loretta: Kids these days. They have no respect for their elders. As the burly Loretta exits the bus and heads towards the hotel to carry on with her business, Clyde rushes to the vehicle to get the best seat. Fox: Yay, I got a back seat! Now hurry up butch lady and drive me to the Super Bowl! After a few minutes of waiting, Loretta emerges from the Motel 6 with a large group of senior citizens. The walker filled chain gang slowly funnels into the bus. Fox: Oh poke-poo! What the heck is going on here? Clyde tries to get up to investigate but just as he grabs the back of the seat in front of him, an elderly frail lady blocks the aisle and sits down beside him. Old Woman: Oh hello young blood. Ready for the Senior Bowl? Clyde sits back down reluctantly with a sour look on his face. Fox: The Senior Bowl? Old Woman: Yesh shunny. It’s a special party for all of the Super Bowl grandparents. Oh I’m going to tear the ruff down this year! Fox: You mean you’ve been here before? Old Woman: Yesh. My grandshun is Brady. Fox: WHAT? Old Woman: Kyle Brady. He’s got a tight end. Oh dang nabbit, I mean he plays at tight end. Fox: Oh so he’s a no name guy. I understand. Old Woman: The name is Gladis by the way. Fox: I’m Clyde. Loretta fixtures herself in the driver seat, turns the bus on and begins to drive. Gladis: And who is your grandson on the team? Fox: Oh actually I don’t even have kids. Yeah? you see? I got these tickets for cheap through a super secret dealer because I’m kind of a big deal. Gladis: Oh yesh? In what? Fox: I wrestle. Gladis: You look like a pip squeak though. My grandshun Brady is five time your size! Fox: Oh yeah? Well is he the TV Champion? Gladis: He gets on TV. Fox: Okay Glade, STPU! Gladis: Excuse me shunny? Fox: It’s an acronym for shut the pansy up! Gladis: I don’t like your attitude shunny. I’m going to go sit with my girlfriends, the grandmothers of Joe Buck and Troy Aikman. Even though Gladis exiting her seat looks as if she ruptured her spleen, she manages to propel herself away from Clyde. Fox: What a bee eye tee see ayetch. The bus comes to a stop at its destination, the site of Super Bowl XLII, The University of Phoenix Stadium. Clyde pastes his face against the window and drools at the spectacle. Fox: THE TOASTER! I’ve never been in a University before. Pushing all of the old people out of his way, Clyde gets out of the bus first. Fox: Ahhhhh! It smells way better out here than with those seniors? BIG TIME! Clyde rushes to the nearest gate as he struggles with his pockets to get his ticket. Turnstile Lady: Ticket please. Fox: Sorry my junk is in the way! The lady looks confused as Clyde finally pulls his ticket out of his pocket. Fox: I should have listened to my Daddy and bought so free forming undies. These ones chafe my trouser snake! It also doesn’t help that there are holes in my pockets! The lady jolts back and very carefully grabs the ticket by an edge to scan it. Turnstile Lady: Umm have a good time I guess. Clyde snatches the ticket back as he enters the stadium. The already bustling crowd consumes Fox as he tries to make his way to section 269. Everything seems to be going so well? until the pee monster hits. Suddenly Clyde remembers that he forgot to pee before leaving the hotel! Luckily Clyde jumps out of the crowd and into a nearby washroom. Five minutes later. Clyde emerges with a big smile on his face. He tries to catch his breath as he puts his hands on his knees in a bent over position. Little did he know, but Mike Tirico and an ESPN TV crew had just set up shop for some of their pre-game coverage nearby. Tirico: Folks today is the big day. Everyone is so relieved to be here at Super Bowl forty-two. Fox yells from the bent over position. Fox: Oh hell yes I’m so relieved! Mike Tirico stops his thoughts as he turns around to see Fox gathering his breath. Tirico: Excuse me sir? Where live on ESPN right now, if you don’t mind, could you just not be so loud? Fox stands up and walks over to Mike. Fox: Well you know, Mister, we worked hard to get to the washroom undefeated today. I feel so much more relieved that I got the pee out of me but then was under attack by an unsuspecting number two, followed by a fierce hot rod that needed rubbing out! Fox’s forehead gleams with beads of sweat. Fox: I just feel like we had an overall great team effort out there today and look forward to other challenges as we move forward. Tirico: What the hell are you thinking? Fox: Oh it’s okay shorty, I’m the Television Champion. I can go on TV anytime I want to. Tirico: No you can’t, this is ESPN! Fox: Yeah it’s cool. I’m usually on X TV so I guess you’ll have to ask them for my rights to actually air this. Tirico: X TV? Fox: Yeah I’m a wrestler with GCW. Tirico: Cut the camera guys! NOW! The cameraman turns away and sends his live feed to the control room. Looking extremely pissed off, Mike lowers his microphone and talks to Clyde. Tirico: YOU! Fox looks around and then points at himself. Fox: ME? Tirico: Yeah you! And then in a surprising twist? Tirico: I LOVE YOU MAN! You’re Clyde Fox from GCW! My kids love you man! Why didn’t you just say it was you man! I watch you on WorldWide every week! Fox: Oh okay cool! Why were you being a hard ass on the TV then? Tirico: Little man syndrome? you know, I instill fear in the viewers. But enough about shitty ESPN; could I get your autograph? Fox: Sure. Got a camera? Tirico: For an autograph? Fox: Well how the hell do you expect to take one? Tirico: No no Clyde. And autograph is your signature. Fox: Then why didn’t you ask me for a signature? Tirico: Well? here, sign my show notes. Mike hands Clyde a pen and his show notes as Clyde follows up with a signature. Fox: There. Tirico: It’s a picture of Pikachu!” Fox: Yeah I’m doing the whole Prince thing. Tirico: I see. Well thanks? I guess. Hey, shouldn’t you be with the celebrity seating? Fox: Where is that? Tirico: Section 222. Here, take my extra backstage pass. I carry two in case I lose one. Fox: Will Jessica Sampson be there? Tirico: You betcha! Fox: SHOW ME THE WAY TO THAT TEET! Flash forward to Sunday, February 10, 2008 Clyde Fox sits in a comfy chair in his locker room on his Gizmondo taking a call. Fox: And that was my adventure at Super Bowl ex ell eye eye. I can’t wait for next year! Person on Phone: So you have nothing to say about the football game itself? Fox: Well sure I do. There was a brown ball bouncing around the field that these big sweaty men wanted. And at the end, they gave the red and blue team the silver football trophy. People were cheering SO much at the end! I wasn’t? you’re not supposed to cheer yourself on. Like, it was just little old me at the Super Bowl. Person on Phone: They weren’t cheering for you. They were cheering for the Giants. Fox: Nah, it was for me? you had to be there. Person on Phone: I think you’re a pretty big bull shit artist you know. Fox: No, I’m just an artist. The person on the end of the Gizmondo hangs up. Fox: Okay goodbye to you too! Last time I get into a personal conversation with a phone sex lady? or was that a man? Who can tell anymore these days? And with that, Clyde switches over to the game application on his Gizmondo and fires up an intense session of Luxor 2. Fox: Alright! Level 12! Jingles and bells ring vibrantly from the game as Clyde gives his thumbs some exercise. Gizmondo Voice: You’ve got mail. Clyde exits the game and flips over to his mail application. The screen shows a message from GCW Booking Staff. Fox: Let’s see what this bad boy has to offer! Gizmondo Text: Congratulations! You will be appearing in the upcoming Rampage tournament! You’re first round match up is set for WorldWide 85 against Docta Z. Please rendezvous with the nearest GCW flight agent to get your itinerary for our Japan tour. Fox: OH MY FUDGING GOD! I’m in a tournament! This is so cool! It’s like I was watching the NFL playoffs, and now it’s like total role reversal! I’m in the Whampage playoffs! Oh em gee. I have a chance to go undefeated like the Patriots! Take a moment there Clyde. Fox: Oh wait. They lost at the end. Well I’ll just succeed where they failed! At least it will serve me as perspiration. No, wait. Inspiration! And on the plus side I get to go to Japan and celebrate the Chinese New Year! Clyde takes a second to read over the mail again. Fox: Oh eight bits! Why do I have to face Dr. Z Z Z again? I just had a checkup like a few weeks ago! I’m sleeping fine! Well I guess they just want to see if the time difference will have any affect on me? BIG TIME! As the scene fades, Clyde shuts down his Gizmondo for the night. He’s got a huge adventure ahead of him, and if he is going to make a stunning run through the Rampage tournament, like the New York Giants, he better get his rest. Fox: I said Patriots Mr. Narrator. Yeah but Clyde, they lost okay. It makes more sense for the story to compare you to the Giants. Fox: I have a giant-- No one wants to know Clyde? no one wants to know. END
