A Parting of Ways

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/10 Wednesday, April 21, 2010 (2:00 A.M.) It’s with a heavy heart that AXEL Action lay in bed in the rather small guest room of his older brother’s house. It’s been another sleepless night in a bed that isn’t his own, and things don’t seem to be getting any better. Almost a month has passed since AXEL was discovered eating rotten food waste from Ace’s dumpster and even now, he can still taste the garbage on his palate. Sighing softly, AXEL sits up and shakes his head, one hand moving to clutch the back of his neck. “Fuckin’ lumpy mattress… He should really look into replacing it.”, he thinks to himself as he takes a moment to allow his eyes to adjust to the palpable darkness of the wee hours of the morning. The darkness becomes less and less of an issue as he sits there, the Angel of Vengeance soon able to see well enough to make his way around. Turning to place his feet on the floor, he stands up and stretches before feeling around for the light switch. Sliding his hand along the wall, he manages to find what he’s seeking. With a flick of his wrist, the room is filled with incandescent light that assaults his maladjusted eyes. His vision blurred by the spots that light caused, he blinks furiously in an attempt to ease the ache that ravages his poor oculars. As his eyesight clears up, he tugs his only outfit on, a pair of blue jeans and a t-shirt along with a cheap pair of sneakers from the nearby clearance outlet. Rifling through the small desk that stood against the far wall, AXEL procures a sheet of paper and a pen. Situating his grip on the writing utensil, he places the tip of it to the page and begins to write: Ace, Ever since we were children, you’ve been all the family I’ve had. You’ve always been there for me when I needed you and you’ve always been here to pick up the pieces when I would screw things up. Always the responsible one, you made sure that I took care of my business the right way and that I never forgot to do what was important. Well, tonight, that’s exactly what I’m going to do. I know I can’t depend on you to take care of me forever, and this is where it stops. I’ve tried so hard over the past few weeks to swallow my pride and just accept the kindness and hospitality you’ve shown me, I really have. No matter how hard I try, I just can’t seem to wrap my head around the fact that my livelihood is gone. That doesn’t mean that I don’t appreciate everything you’ve done for me, big brother. There’s no one else on God’s Green Earth that would do the things you’ve done for me, and I’ll always be grateful. I just feel like it’s time for me to man up and take back everything I lost; time for me to take control of my life. You have no idea just how much your kindness has meant to me, and I don’t think I can ever explain with words. In fact, I’m not even going to try to. All I can do is offer my sincerest apologies for running out on you like this. I wish I could stick around to tell you face to face, but I just can’t. I hope you’ll forgive me. Thanks for everything, AXEL P.S.: I’m going to borrow your car and drive to the airport. I’ll be using the spare key. I’ll leave it in the glove box when I’m finished with it, and your car will be waiting for you in the parking lot. Dropping the pen onto the hard wood of the desk, AXEL exits the bedroom and heads for the back door of the house. Making his way through the kitchen, he stops long enough to place the note on the counter. Taking one last look around, he walks out the back door after grabbing the spare key to Ace’s car off of the hook beside it. Closing and locking the door, AXEL heads to the car and climbs in, cranking it and pulling out of the drive. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Wednesday, April 21, 2010 (3:30 A.M.) Pulling into a parking spot at the Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, four miles away from the center of Hollywood (Florida), AXEL shuts off the engine to the car. Taking a deep breath, he pulls the key from the ignition and opens the glove box, tossing the key inside. Closing the compartment just as quickly as he’d opened it, AXEL opens the driver’s side door and steps out into the early morning air. Locking and closing the door, AXEL steps away from the car and heads toward the terminal. “Well, here goes…”, he says as he stops just outside the front door, standing on the rubber mat that causes the automatic sliding door to open once it’s stepped on. As the door slides open, AXEL walks into the facility. Fortunately for him, the line to the front desk isn’t very long, so it won’t take long for him to get his ticket and be finished with his business. Sauntering casually up to the front desk, he places his hands on the edge of the counter, resting them there. “Hello sir, how may I help you?”, asks the woman behind the desk as she types away at her computer’s keyboard. “I’d like to purchase a ticket to Honolulu, Hawaii, please; round trip and coach.” The woman nods, glancing down at her keyboard and typing in a few words. The clickity-clack of her fingers on the keys is the only sound in the surrounding area, outside of the occasional whispers of the passersby. “Your total is going to be $569.00, sir. Will that be cash, check or credit?” “Credit,” says AXEL, reaching into the back pocket of his jeans and taking out his billfold. Opening it up, he pulls out his Visa Mastercard and presents it to the woman. Placing his billfold on the counter, he waits patiently as the clerk swipes it through the machine then runs the numbers on it. AXEL knows he can’t afford the charge right now, but that’s where credit comes in handy. After all, he can always pay it off later. “There you go, sir. You should be billed within the month. You’re on Flight 123, and it will be leaving at 9 A.M., sharp.”, says the woman, handing the card back to AXEL, which he promptly puts back within his wallet. Stuffing the leather object back into his pocket, he decides to take a seat in one of the many chairs around the room. Sinking down into it, he tilts his head back and closes his eyes as he heaves a deep sigh. Feeling more relaxed than ever, he manages to drift off to sleep for the first time in weeks. Wednesday, April 21, 2010 (8:55 A.M.) “Flight 123 from Fort Lauderdale to Honolulu will be boarding momentarily! I repeat, Flight 123 from Fort Lauderdale to Honolulu will be boarding momentarily!” Awaking with a start, AXEL shakes his head, trying to clear the cobwebs as he gathers his wits about him. Stretching after his first decent night’s sleep in quite sometime, he stands up and brushes the wrinkles out of his clothes. “Man, I’m still so sleepy…”, he mutters, rubbing the back of his head as he made his way to the gate. Stepping into the line that had formed, he looked out the window at the planes as they moved into position. The line slowly moved along, each person stepping through the gate and onto the plane. As he walks up to the gate, the person behind him places a hand on his shoulder, stopping him. “Excuse me…” “Yes, ma’am?”, AXEL asks, turning for a moment to fact the person behind him. What he’s met with is an older woman, seemingly in her late fifties, early sixties. She was a petite little thing, though seemed to be in fairly good shape. Tilting his head to the left, the Angel of Vengeance cracks a smile that could warm even the coldest of hearts. “Are you AXEL Action?” “That’d be me. I’ve got to get on the plane in a couple minutes, but I can spare a second. What can I do for you?” The woman reaches into her purse, pulling out a pen and a little slip of paper. Pushing them in his direction, she smiles up at him. “I was just wondering if you could give me your autograph. I’m going to visit my grandson and his parents in Hawaii, and he’s a big fan of yours.” Taking the pen and paper in hand, AXEL nods, signing his name to it before handing it back to her. His left eyebrow perks as he hears the usher calling out to him. “Come on, folks. It’s time to board the plane. It’ll be leaving any moment now,” the man says with an almost indignant tone. “There you go, ma’am. Give your family my regards.” “Bless you, young man.”, says the old woman, smiling and waving to AXEL as he darts off through the gate, nothing but Hawaii and the Rampage tourney on his mind. “Honolulu, here I come!”
