'If love be rough with you, then be rough with love. Prick love for pricking you and you beat love down.'

21 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st August 2005 They say love isn’t supposed to hurt. The drip in Mary’s arm, the beeping monitors, the smell of antiseptic, the bandages and the chart at the foot of Mary’s bed would suggest otherwise. Although they don’t mean to say love “doesn’t” hurt, just that it “shouldn’t.” Mary had been living by this mantra since she was 5 years old when her uncle forced himself on her for the first time. She reminded herself again and again when lover after lover seemed to use her up and then spit her out. She’d burned inside, not with anger but with pain because they had loved her in their own special ways. Her Uncle being the exception to the rule. He hadn’t loved her, he’d wanted her, completely. There is a fraction of a difference between the two. Love can consume you. What her Uncle felt for her, consumed everything and everyone like a California forest fire and eventually, it consumed him whole. Scar and Skye were Mary’s true loves in The Institute and both were taken away from her, eventually she ended up hurting them. She often wondered if it was some kind of karma that she ended up where she did and the way she has. Lazlo meant well, he made her happy and in the end that’s all she really wanted, it’s all she wants period and never really gets. Then came Damien Black. Damien Black the man who kissed the boo boo and made everything outside of the two of them seem like a distant voice whispering for their attention. Damien Black, the man who stopped her harming herself, helped build her up after Scar threatened to tear her down and Lazlo went missing without a word. Damien Black the man who loved her so much his own self couldn’t stand it. Damien Black the man who beat her and caused her to miscarry a child she didn’t even know she could conceive let alone know that she had at the time. Damien Black the man who made winning the unified title of the Dragon Wrestling Association, one of the single most painful memories Mary has the misfortune to carry with her and lord knows there is stiff competition in that head of hers. The man came close to killing her with malice alone but she survived because she promised Marianne after her Uncle had died that she would never let anyone hurt them like that again. Typhoid Mary was the Calvary, the enforcer she was supposed to be the strong one and she was, she is. Then came Roberto Abitua or LeStatt Knight, however you want to paint it. Mary was warned off him, as was her cousin Ashura when she, well dated is too strong a word, fucked around with him is more accurate. Some things you really shouldn’t keep in the family. Yea Roberto came along and made even Typhoid Mary, the strong one, the enforcer believe that maybe it was finally her turn. Thing about turns is, eventually, it’s someone else’s. Arael heard Mary move gently in her sleep but nothing more than that. He sat back in the hospital issue armchair and rested his ankle on his opposite knee with his head leaned back and facing the ceiling. Well where else do you look when you see everything and nothing all at the same time. Being blind spared him the discomfort of seeing Mary in the state she was but it also meant that the mental images were stronger, bigger, brighter, deeper and lasted a hell of a lot longer. His brow furrowed at his self-disappointment and he exhaled long and silently. 8 years ago he got personally involved with the daughter of a client. That cost him his job and his sight and would have cost him his life if the people sent to kill his clients daughter hadn’t wanted him to live with the image of her death being the last thing he ever saw. They figured that would be more painful than death, more of a punishment. How poetic. Now here he sat with another client he got too close to, albeit not as close as the last one he got too close to, but damn near close enough. He wasn’t supposed to leave her alone but over the few years he had been guarding her, he and Mary had come to an understanding that she was fine as long as she was with Roberto. He never liked Roberto but he respected him as a man that cared for Mary. Arael sat there shaking his head over and over telling himself that if he had have insisted he stay, Mary wouldn’t be lying in a hospital bed. The same way if he had insisted he protect Mary instead of her cousin Kagerro over in Mexico, she wouldn’t have been captured and tortured by the sick freaks who are after Ashura. Tangled webs much? A knock at the door caused Arael to leap to his feet and pull his gun from its holster on his left hand side. He was by the door before the person on the other end could announce themselves. “Eduardo” It’s Skye.” You would have thought he would sigh a sigh of relief hearing his boss’s daughter’s voice but he didn’t. He held his breath for a split second and shook his head again. “Si Miss Skye.” He holstered the gun and opened the door expecting a tirade of abuse for the job he’d done protecting Mary but he got nothing. Skye’s face was blank. She looked tired and worn out. Her dark golden complexion looked slightly pale. She had a huge bag slung over her shoulder and dark glasses over her eyes. She stared up at Arael with a blank expression. She looked over at Mary, removing her glasses, and dropped her bag by the door before approaching the bed. She took Mary’s hand and smoothed some of her wild curls from her face that now seemed limp and lifeless rather than exuberant and playful and wild like they usually were. Mary didn’t move other than the rise and fall of her chest. She was breathing on her own and Skye said a silent thank you to anyone who might be listening for that small mercy. Mary’s face was bruised around her jaw and more so down the left side of her face. Her one eye was almost swollen shut and there was a gash above the other. Her right arm was bandaged and lay flat against the sheets that covered her stomach. “What have they done to you O’Anjo?” Skye’s voice was barely a whisper as she leaned down and kissed Mary’s forehead and then her lips with the barest of brushes. She kissed her forehead again and leaned back up. “You’re tougher than this bebe ok” You just remember that.” Skye’s glassy eyes turned to Arael who stood by the chair again in the far corner of the room. Her eyes narrowed in anger but then her face became neutral again like she was too tired to even be mad at him right now. “What the hell happened Eddy?” Arael dragged a hand down his face and shook his head. “After we got back from Mexico I wanted to take Mary home but she insisted she go to see Senor Abitua. You know how stubborn she can be. I thought maybe it would do her good after what she went through with The Six in Mexico, to be around Senor Abitua for her first night back. So I agreed to pick her up in the morning and left her at his door. Half an hour down the road I realised she had left one of her bags in the cab and I called her cell phone to tell her but it was still turned off from the flight so I had the cab driver turn back. By the time I arrived back there, the house was on fire and Senor Abitua and Miss Mary were on the lawn out front, bloodied and unconscious. Sirens were already in the distance but I called an ambulance too just in case.” “I don’t understand, why is she in this state, what happened?” “I’m not fully sure Miss Skye. Mary was in and out of sleep while we waited for a ambulance and on the way to the hospital. She kept saying that she didn’t write it. That she loves Senor Abitua, that he was wrong. She didn’t make much sense.” “Were they robbed” Mugged? Was this The Six? I don’t get it Eddy, she looks like she’s been in a fucking hardcore match!” Arael glanced at the floor then back up at Skye. She noticed. Arael was never one to give anything away in his composure so this was something big. “What aren’t you telling me Arael.” “It would be merely speculation Miss Skye and now isn’t the time to”.” “Fucking tell me Eduardo!” Arael tried to think of how best to say it. “When the cab and I returned to find the fire and Mary and Abitua on the grass I could feel they were surrounded in shards of glass.” “So?” “The cab driver told me that the window at the front of the house was smashed, like they had been thrown through it together. There was no sign of any body else being there according to him. I could only see the shadow of the flames. I felt for serious injuries on them both and had to ask the cab driver to confirm what I was feeling.” “which was?” Skye was growing impatient. “Which was that it seemed like they had been in a fight.” “I just asked if they got mugged.” “No Miss Skye, a fight with each other. Apparently the fire brigade and the police say there was no sign of forced entry and nothing was missing as far as they could tell but the hall, the dining room and the living room all look like Beirut.” Skye braced her hands on the edge of Mary’s bed to steady herself as she took all this information in. She took several deep breaths before saying, “So you’re telling me that Roberto, Lestatt, whatever he wants to call himself, you’re saying they fought, and he did this to her?” “Well we don’t know for sure but?” Skye let loose a string of Brazilian profanities that would make a sailor blush. “I fucking knew it, I knew he was too good to be true, I knew he was no good for her, I fucking told her, I warned her!” Skye paced up and down the room. “Where the hell is he now” Please tell me its jail Eddy.” Arael walked over to the window and leaned against the frame. “Actually he is in hospital also. Truth be told, he was in just as bad a mess as Miss Mary here, if not a little worse, I do not know for certain.” “Which hospital” Here?” Arael shook his head, “I do not know.” That was a lie, he knew where Roberto was. Hell he and Mary had travelled in the same ambulance. Of course he knew better than to give the fiery Skye that lil tid bit of information in her present state of mind. Skye was fuming. She looked at Mary then back at Arael, then back at Mary again and tears threatened to spill from her eyes if she didn’t fight them back with all she had. She shook her head while her fists gripped the bed sheets so tight her knuckles paled. She let go and marched over to the far end of the room, Arael followed her footsteps. “I want him gone Eddy. For good.” She was so matter o fact about what she said that Arael almost didn’t understand what she was asking him for. “Miss Skye?” “Don’t play dumb with me Eddy, I want him gone, forever. I’m not watching Mary go through this shit again.” “Are you sure you realise what you are asking of me Miss Skye?” “I wouldn’t be fucking asking if I wasn’t sure now would I?” “What about your father. He is my boss and as much as I am at your disposal, all hits have to go through him Miss Skye.” “Don’t worry I will talk to Papa, he will okay it, then I want you to do what your supposed to be oh so good at. I want Roberto Abitua dead and buried where no one will ever find him. I want him to vanish. Do you understand me Eduardo because if you don’t I’m sure Leviath would take the job gladly.” “Si Miss Skye, I understand. He will be taken care of as soon as your father gives me the permission to do so.” And then there was silence. Skye was the first to break it. “What” You don’t think he deserves to pay for what he’s done?” “It’s not that Miss Skye. I just think you’re letting anger speak for you right now. You didn’t order a hit on Damien Black when he harmed Miss Mary and surely causing her to lose their child and nearly her mind and life was worse than what has happened between Miss Mary and Senor Abitua?” “Maybe it was worse but see we didn’t know that Damien was a bitch. We didn’t know that he would turn on her, he fooled us all and at the end of the day Mary rose above it and he fell into obscurity. That was enough for me. Roberto on the other hand he knew we didn’t trust him. He knew what Mary had been through and he even had the decency to leave her alone. Then he came back and he dropped that Medea ho for her. I mean, I thought maybe this man really is the one she’s been waiting for. She was so happy with him. He knew we were all watching. That time when she was in hospital and he came to visit her he promised us he would never hurt her, he looked me in the face and said he would never hurt her Eddy. Now look at her. She is tortured in Mexico and she comes home to this” No he has to pay Eddy. With his life. Fuck him. I hope he rots!” Silence again. There were times when Arael along with everyone else who knew her could forget that Skye was the daughter of a notorious crimelord but this was not one of those times. Mary was moving in her sleep, her head rocking every so often from side to side. She eventually was tossing and turning enough to alert Skye who rushed to her bedside. She stroked Mary’s hair to soothe her as Mary’s eyes fluttered open startled and confused. “STATTY NO!” “Shhh?” Skye cupped Mary’s cheek and tilted her head towards her. “Skye blue?” Skye nodded, “Si Anjo, Skye blue and Eddy. No Statty, he’s not here, he can’t hurt you now bebe.” Mary nestled her face against the warmth of Skye’s hand as Skye leaned her face down to Mary’s to hug her to her. She looked over at Arael who stood by the foot of the bed then kissed Mary’s cheek and whispered into her hair, “He’ll never hurt you again isn’t that right Arael?” He just nodded. “Si, Miss Skye, Si.”
