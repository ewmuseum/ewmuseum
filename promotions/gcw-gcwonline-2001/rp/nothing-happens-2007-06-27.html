Goodbye, David Byrne and Tina and, man. Whoever the fuck else was in the band. Out of the room, in with the creep. Zombie, deaf and dumb and blind, running the same stupid routines I ran back when I was alive. Rent a hotel room, get wasted, stagger into the hotel room, get wasted, stagger out of the hotel room and try to find something outside that feels like gold. The shame of it all being that "feels like gold" doesn't really describe much of shit. Might as well be "tastes like brains" for all it really seems to come up. Jordan's at some bar. It has a name, I just don't think that'd help me find it. A block away, maybe. There was a big statue. I'll find it. It'll be swarmed by people, loud and just as far out of their heads as I am. People really are the same all over, I guess. Or similar. I'm still having problems telling the difference between Lou Reed and Television. Other than that, I think I'm better now. Just throw a heads up to him, keep walking. They're not totally amazed by the celebrities anymore, I guess. We, or, well, we as in "my group of three people which contains exactly one person that matters to the company" have sorta lost our sheen. I think that guy smoked with me and called it a spliff; can't really expect him to worry 'bout us much when he sees the glimmer of celebrity going dull up close. Repeat a thousand. The inside consumes, pulls you like depressurizing and sucks you down into bright and loud. A clean, well-lighted place. The people look like me; maybe they're all actors or artists or athletes. The song's, I don't know. Fucking shit pop music with an Aussie accent. Not Wolfmother, at least. The Troggs were Brits, right? "Sage!" This one I know. Jordan "Frank 'Jordan White' Black" White. How the fuck is he wearing furcollared denim and shit? Stupid blood, dammit. They've all been shouting or bitching. "Frank. Yo. Hoped you'd still be here." "Dude, you've only been gone like, fuckin', what, muhfuckin' ten minutes?" "Twenty, thirty maybe." Fuck you, Hemingway. You're trying to trap me in your roles. "Shit, ain't long. S'up nigga?" Nothing fun this time. I guess, now and then, people just happen to talk and, well. Not much else. I've earned this inactivity. "And, basically, I just got sidetracked on all of that shit for a minute. Getting things worked out with Caldera, that bullshit. Fuck, you know. You were there." "I spaced on a lotta it." "Well. Okay, I got distracted for awhile. That's all that matters. Now that shit's all settled for a minute, I had time to get on with that whole McJordan thing." "Oh shit." His eyes go wide, half his mouth smiles. "That's word like, shit, word. When's he showin' up in the Dub?" "GC? He's not. And he's she." "What?" "I mean, I thought about bringin' her in for a big GCW debut, get the fame out, but’just being fucking on the level here and all that tripe shit’there is fucking no way I can trust Caldera with two Jordans. It's just very important that I make it obvious who owns the name so it's never not obvious. So, well. No her in GCW for now." "Oh yeah. Her?" "Yeah, actually found her when I was lookin' for the replacement job you took. Her name was Jordan, fuck, something, so I figured I'd see if she didn't suck." "And?" "She didn't suck." "Smooth on. "Man, I kinda expected you to use Jordan 2 as a stand in for my ass. Shit, fuckin' glad I ain't gettin' fired." "You should be. And, Frank, don't forget that you're Jordan 2. She's 3." "Aight, I kinda expected you'd be usin' Jordan 3 as a stand in for my ass." "That's. Better, yeah." "So, shit. What you're sayin' is I'm gon' be stranded in fuckin' Australia?" "Yeah. Basically. He's staring into the mug he brought with him. Wonder if he's making eye contact. "Shit. So I'm just another dog in the pen now?" Digging in my pocket, holding out a pack of smokes to him. He pushes them away. "I quit, man." "Fuck. I didn't." Fire. "Don't be a prick, you fuckin' prick. You're the plan A, you're the bastard I actually have to keep, y'know, copasetic. "Least I'm consistent with it. No one'd confuse me for a person wasn't out for himself." Sometimes, I wonder if they believe the shit I'm saying.
