The River

Date: ?? Children are the most precious things on earth. They are brought into this world in nothing less than a miraculous fashion. They learn faster than adults, because their little brains are empty, there is so much room to learn. It’s amazing how fast they catch on. Menial things like finding your toes, or discovering your hand shows just how special children really are. You can’t wait for that first smile, that first giggle, that cute soft little giggle that melts every parents heart. Than they learn to crawl, than they are walking, and before you know it they are calling your name, calling their “Sissy, or Biggie Bro” They grow so fast that before you know it they are in school, doing homework. It’s all so fast, amazing, and beautiful. With all that being said it’s even more amazing to see an adult abuse their child. To beat them for wetting the bed, to beat them for misspelling a word in school. Why would someone take something so precious, and destroy it physically and emotionally. Why?”. “If I ever catch you playing in the chicken coop again I will beat you until you piss blood you pathetic piece of trash!” Anton began to swing and belt his oldest child with a fury of fists. Thuds being heard off the skull and body. There is no precision to skill to the flying fists. Anton is just flailing wildly. A bottle of Beam, and some pills can turn any man with violent tendencies into a monster. Peeking around the corner is the middle, but now youngest child Jason. He watches his big brother, his hero beaten to a pulp. Bryan is a big kid for his age. 11 years old, he stands 6’2? and weighs already close to 200lbs. He’s bigger than his pint size father, but alcohol induce rage can bring down the biggest of men. Jason feared his father most. Jason has not spoken a word in almost 2 years, ever since that day he and Bryan had to do the unimagineable. Bryan and Jason are downstairs building a puzzle of Big Ben. Bryan has a knack for things of this sort. They do things like this when they become scared. Upstairs mother is screaming at the top of her lungs. The two brothers think this is another common beating from the hands of their father. His lashes are usually far worse with their mother. Bryan thinks its because she’s an adult, and you hit adults harder than you hit children. Both boys are away from the outside world, they think all children and mommies are beaten. They haven’t figured out that their father is raging drunken monster, and its not in the norm to harm a child, not even in the slightest. The screaming upstairs came to a stop, and all that was heard was silence, than followed by a wave of crying. Their father mumbled something, and their mother screamed even more. What was going on up there? They hear some stumbling around, than their father says something that they can’t quite make out. Anton begins to head down the steps yelling for the boys. Bryan and Jason quickly stand up, afraid of a beating if they do not listen. Anton approaches the two boys with a shoebox, British Knights to be exact, but there’s spots of red all over it, and the hands around the box. He looks a mess, like he’s just seen a ghost. “Take this to the water, and throw it in. Whatever you do, do not, and I mean do not look in the box.” He had that look of death in his eyes, also this glimmer of fear, something the boys have never seen before in their lives. Something that scared the living shit out of them. Bryan and Jason quickly hurried out of the house, the box in their extended arms. They carried it this way probably to avoid looking inside. The bottom of the box started to get damp, and something kind of shook inside. Their curiosity began to peek. Out of breath Jason chugged “Maybe-we-should-look-just-to-ease-our-minds.” He kept running trying to keep pace. Bryan stopped, and turned back around when he got to the river. The house was now out of view. Bryan put the box on ground. The grass was stomped away. This is where the boys, and sometimes their father, when sober, would fish. They never caught anything worth while, but it was fun. A lot of fun. But that fun was not now, and after today, will most likely never will be. Bryan, hands on his head to catch his breath, looks at his brother, towards the house, than at the box. A faint little squeal came from the box. Jason’s head perked like a dog’s ears and looks deep into his brother’s eyes. He nods, almost in a telepathic agreement, that maybe they should take a peek, but will never tell a soul. Bryan squats down in front of the box and looks at Jason. They both seem to be thinking the same thing. “You know, after we look in here we may not ever be the same again.” Jason looks on in confusion. “What if it’s nothing Bryan, what if it’s just something stupid, and we are just acting like foolish kids.” Bryan contemplates what Jason says, flicks him in the ear, which causes Jason to flinch. “Don’t be stupid. He made sure we did not look in here. It’s something special Jason. But we must never tell anyone, you hear me, or I will pound your face.” Jason nods. Bryan cracks his knuckles, and grabs the folded end of the shoebox and slowly opens it. Bryan falls back, his eyes wide and face pale. “What is it Bryan” What?” Bryan turns over and vomits. His mouth is wide and his chest expands at the impact of the vomit. Jason picks up the lid himself and stares in amazement. It’s a baby? “Close it Jason, damnit close it!” Bryan stands up and shoves his brother. Jason falls and scrapes his knee, winces in pain. “Father said not to fucking open it!” Jason holding his scarped knee asks, “Did you see it’s face Bryan” It looked deformed. Like when father beats a pig with a iron rod.” Bryan, squatting, head almost between his legs replies back, “Yeah, but Jason it’s our little brother. Mother was pregnant, but the baby was born bad, father wants us to kill it.” Tears well up in Jason’s eyes. He almost seems to forget the blood seeping from his knees and just stares at the box. “We can’t Bryan. It’s our brother, we have to take care of him.” Bryan’s head bolts up. “Did you see it fuckhead” It’s bad, it’s no good. That’s no way for a child to live. Let’s do what father says and get out of here.” Jason looks back at his brother, mouth agaped, and starts to shake his head. Bryan’s hand quickly makes contact with Jason’s face. Jason falls to the earth, tears leaking from his face. He looks at his brother, and that’s when he realizes the end is near. His eyes are empty, just like when father beats him. Jason’s only friend and idol is turning into the worst person on earth. Bryan picks up the box, his face now blank, his eyes empty, his mind lost in the moment. He turns, faces the river, squats down, and places the box in the water. It floats away with the current. Bryan stands up, looks as the box floats away from view, than turns around and faces his brother. Jason, sobbing heavily, stands up and hangs his head low. Bryan walks towards him, lifts his head up with his finger tips, and puts his arms around his younger brother. Jason is sobbing frantically into the shirt of Bryan. Bryan runs his fingers through his hair. “Jason, it’s for the better, I promise it’s for the better.” Head buried into Bryan’s chest, Jason wonders if Bryan is right, or if it’s gotten worse, way worse?
