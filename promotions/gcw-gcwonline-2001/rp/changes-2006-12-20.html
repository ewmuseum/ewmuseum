Date: 12/20/06 *credit to Jen for her allowing me the use of Patti Life always seems to be constantly changing. First, Hope and I go from being virtually nobodies in a small independent federation to the big time. 4WW was the place to be, but like all good things, bad things are abound to happen. The fed unexpectedly locked its doors, when the current owner found out that his darling wife was caring the next generation. This upheaval sent us to our new home, the GCW. Here, our lives took a surprising and what some believe, a well deserved turn. Hope and I battled through the Jackson Brothers, La Sombra and Cecil, and even the team of Future Shock to earn our shot at the tag team champs Unite. Unite, a team of two brothers who tend to be more concerned about the goings on with one, Dani Northsky then concentrating on the real issues at hand. But, who can't blame them when one of their own has transformed into something that they can't quite understand. Or, maybe it's because one brother is too busy getting his groove on and the other is afraid to get that groove on. I guess in that aspect, I can't really talk that much. Hope's and my life changed just as much. Hope went from sweet natured, shy student to someone I don't know. She's embraced the heterosexual world with bright eyes, attempting a career outside the wrestling world, while reaping none of the benefits. Every red penny she makes goes right into the pocket of our mistress's sister, Kit. Hope accidentally scarred her, so now she's got to fix the damage she's done. I, well, I can't really say that much myself. I gave up my identity to be nothing more then what I thought of Hope's ex-lover. Little did I know, Kawanaha did love me more then Hope and what she claimed was a test? With all that was going on, I failed to realize I had lost the most important thing to me with all the changes that had occurred. I had lost my home and my sister. What was a girl to do? She turns to the person that everyone would least expect. Date: December 20, 2006 Place: Athens, Greece The beauty and the splendor of the house that stood before Keiko, was just overwhelming for the little Oriental. She thought she had seen beauty before in the form of Kawanaha's home, but this place was far more elegant then what her mistress has. The columns that stood out front were a throw back to the Parthenon days when the Greeks worshipped their gods. The white foretold of the purity of the love that was sure to be plentiful on the inside of the home. Suddenly, a quick movement caught her attention. A dark-haired child popped out from behind a set of dark purple rose bushes. “Who are you and are you here to see my mommy and daddy?” The child asked with an air of innocence. Keiko couldn't quite speak. Her mind tried to search for reasoning on where she had seen this child before, but she couldn't quite place her. The child's bright blue eyes stood out in contrast to her dark black hair. If Keiko hadn't known any better, she could have sworn that the child was her without the Oriental eyes. “I…I'm here to see your mommy. Is she here?” The raven-haired child nodded her head. “She's resting out by the pool. She and Daddy are gonna have a baby.” Keiko bent down to eye level of the girl and stared into her blue eyes. The little girl smiled and brushed the bangs out of Keiko's face. The little girl giggled, “Your eyes are funny looking, mommy doesn't have many people who look like you.” Keiko blushed and stared more intently at the child. The more she looked at her, the more she struggled to figure out who she was, so she decided to take a guess. “Are you Raven by chance?” The little girl nodded and smiled.. “Would you take me to your mommy? I'm afraid that I'd get lost in this big place.” Raven smiled and took Keiko's hand. She led Keiko through the big double doors that led into a spacious parlor. The parlor was decorated in rich hues of dark greens and reds. The furniture was mahogany build and looked as if it stood the test of time. The chairs were draped in hues of darker red, making it seem that there was no covering at all. The walls were hung with curtains that managed to seal all available light out of the room. The paintings on the wall showed Keiko the history of the owners. She marveled at the riches that lay before her. Keiko secretly wondered if Kawanaha was trying to compete with the others around her. The richness of this home would just make any person just as jealous as the next. Keiko rested her hand on the back of the chair and let loose with a deep sigh. “How about instead of you wishing fer what you don't have, you could at least help a pregnant woman to the chair.” Keiko turned her head and caught an eyeful of the very pregnant Patti Anderson-McClancy. The rumors of her caring the heir-apparent to the McClancy dynasty were true. Patti could barely walk, let alone keep her balance for long periods of time. “Oh! I'm sorry, let me help you.” Keiko rushed over to help Patti to, what she hoped was one of the more comfortable chairs. Keiko noticed that Raven was on the other side and helping her mother out. Patti groaned as she sat down in the chair and raised her partially swollen ankles up on the foot rest that Raven and quickly placed for her. “See, I told you mommy was pregnant, she and daddy are going to give me lots of sisters.” Raven gave Keiko a cheeky grin. Patti patted Raven on the head and pulled the child up onto her lap. “Be careful poppet, why don't you give mommy a kiss, a hug, and then go tackle daddy. Tell him mommy's got her strange cravings again.” As Raven gave into Patti's request, Keiko felt something tug at her heartstrings, a feeling that she wasn't quite sure what it was, but it was a really odd feeling. Just as quickly as Raven came she was gone. “So Keiko, what do I owe the pleasure of this visit? “I'm having a problem and I'm unsure what do to.” “What the life of luxury not working out for you?” “No, that's not it. I was just wondering if you've ever done anything that you've regretted. And I don't mean anything small; I mean something that you felt guilty enough over that apologizing wasn't enough?” Patti raised an eyebrow at Keiko. To her it almost sounds as if Keiko had asked her the impossible question. Patti had done a lot of what others had considered regrettable at times, but she never once apologized for her actions. “Which time?” “This is the reason why I sought you out. How do I get my sister as well as my home back?” Keiko fell to the floor in almost a defeated motion. Tears spilled from her almond shaped eyes. Her last hope was in Patti's advice. She had heard rumors that she had gone through it twice before with her brother and both of her sisters, so she hoped for some words of comfort. Patti looked confused for about half a second. Not many came to her for advice. Most visitors just left her to her own devices and her family. Others would come to pay their respect to the future heirs to the throne, but hardly anyone from the wrestling world graced her doorstep. Patti took a few unsteady steps towards Keiko. She wobbled a few steps, as one of the triplets purposely kicked her harder then expected. “Get up! Let me tell you something about the Fates. Their job is to play with you, manipulate you into things that you don't normally do. They are the ones that will determine if, and when your sister forgives.” “But why are they doing this?” “Because they're bloody savages. The best advice I can give you, is to wait it out. The damage has been done, a lesson is to be learned and when you've learned what needs to be taught, then and only then you will have your reconciliation with your sister.” Keiko frowned even more. Patti was just as cryptic as her sensei. She knew that was the wisest answer she was going to receive. “So in other words, wait and see and it will all work out. Caitlin was just as stubborn, and she came around… eventually. I'm sure Umi will come around too.” Patti wiped the tears from Keiko's eyes and gave her a reassuring smile. Her smile, however, began to fade as she started to double over again. “Patti? Are you ok?” “One of the bloody little ones is being a little over-active tonight. It feels like they're having their own rumble in there.” Before Keiko could respond, the tones of “Mind Freak” can be heard coming from the back pocket of Keiko's jeans. Keiko rolled her eyes in annoyance. She quickly reaches behind her, and answers it. “What did I do now?” A few minutes elapse as Keiko seems stuck in a repetitive “uh-huh” mode. Keiko kept one eye on Patti. Her second sight giving her a clue that something was about to happen. “That's just great, mistress. I'm sure I'll love the house, but I've got to go…” Just as Keiko hangs up, Patti doubles over in pain and lets out a loud ear-piercing screams. Servants come rushing out of every crook and cranny. Some even pushes Keiko out of the way. As the scene fades to black, Keiko gasps realizing what's going on. “Oh SHIT!!!”
