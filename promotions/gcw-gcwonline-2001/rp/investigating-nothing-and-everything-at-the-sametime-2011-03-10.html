Investigating nothing and everything at the sametime.

10 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/11 6:45 am 10 miles outside Las Vegas. The sun beats down mercilessly upon the desert sands as the heat rises quickly. Slowly sitting up two men look around rubbing there faces and head. Kristoff and Reischmann both look at each other and then down to two gallon jugs of water. “Well fuck!” Kristoff stands up looking around and off in the far distances he sees glittering glass. He turns and offers Reischmann a hand. Reischmann grabs the hand and pulls himself to his feet. He looks around and reaches down lifting one of the water jugs. He turns his gaze towards the mountains. He then looks over to the left noticing tire tracks driving further off into the desert. Reaching into his pocket he pulls out a pack of cigarettes. Slowly he packs the cigarettes against his palm. He tears the wrapping off the package and then shoves the pieces into his pocket. Pulling a cigarette out of his pocket he lights it, as he then takes a long drag off of it. “Well this is what drinking gets us doesn't it?” Kristoff shakes his head as he reaches down and grabs the other jug of water from Reischmann, looking at him respond by a questioning look. “What? I can hold a jug of water and smoke a cigarette, besides there is another jug.” “So are we gonna have another helicopter flight from Mr. Whitecloud?” Raising his hand to shield his eyes from the sun, as he watches the sky. Shaking his head, he reaches down grabbing the other jug of water. Turning on his heel he walks on towards Las Vegas, looking over his shoulder he watches Reischmann inhaling his cigarette slowly following while dragging his feet in the sand. Kristoff thinks hard as he walks along plodding through the desert. His mind was in deep thought only broken by Reischmann who speaks up. “You know the weird part was that we were simply dumped out here. Leaving us with everything including our guns.” pointing at the jug of water he raises an eyebrow. “Then leaving us with more than enough water to get back to the city, either someone cares or is simply making it a point that anything can happen in Vegas.” Kristoff reaches into his pocket and pulls out a bunch of folded paper and hands it over to Reischmann. “Well Mr. Whitecloud seems to be avoiding us with a passion. Every time we attempt to open his military records is met with lawyers at every turn, Is he hiding something?” Looking at each paper carefully he looks over at Kristoff as he coughs to clear his throat. “Remind me again why we went out drinking last night?” “Truth is we needed to be out and relax, We haven't even had a chance to take a break.” Kristoff chuckles slightly as they walk up over a sand dune of sorts and stumble down the embankment right onto a road leading towards Las Vegas. Kristoff looks down and chuckles a bit. “Well I guess we are closer than we thought.” “Yeah yeah laugh it up, but on a serious note though, why would Whitecloud make it a point to leave us out here in the desert?” looking around Reischmann lets out a deep breath. “Perhaps he is attempting to make a point and we aren't seeing it.” Looking over his shoulder he nods towards his partner in the investigation. “Look Reisch he is a key witness in all of this. Even if he isn't involved he knows who is. The guy is hard headed and won't open up to anyone and all we are asking I his damn help.” They both look at each other in silence as they walk down the road a ways. No cars or any type of activity. Reischmann stops as he seems to have suddenly realized something. “National Security, that is the only thing that could make sense in this matter now.” “Come again?” Kristoff looks back quickly. “He was in Special Force with the Air Forces Para Rescue unit. He had to do and deal with things others normally wouldn't. He has a National Defense ribbon and a combat medal.” Smacking his own forehead as he looks over at Kristoff. “How did I miss it?” Rubbing his chin Kristoff considers this in deep thought over this for a few moments. “I missed it as well, but I think I see where your going with this. Also we were at the High Spirits Casino last, it would only make sense he was the last one to see us.” Down the road a black vehicle speeds down the highway towards them. It is a black van, in fact a black Ford Econovan. The van slows to a stop as the double side door opens and someone inside speaks. “Get in I believe you will be interested in what we have to say and offer.” Both men look at each other and slowly get inside the vehicle. The passenger climbs out of the front and shuts the doors behind them. Flat screens turn on inside the van as they cannot see into the front seat area of the van, only blocked by what looks like dark tinted plexi glass. “My apologies for Mr. Whiteclouds treatment of you last night. He is not very tactful when he is trying to get you to leave him alone.” The man chuckles slightly. “ You see he has a major wrestling match that is most brutal. He feels he does not need any distractions. That is where we come in and we will provide you with assistance.” A small mail slot sized door opens and a large sealed envelope is slid through. Taking the envelope Reischmann opens it. “You know by doing this you are betraying every piece of government security you can think of.” “You see Shaman aka Andrew Whitecloud is unaware of changes going on around him.” Suddenly a noticeable female voice is heard clearly. “ He decides to try and blind himself with reality and stays in the light rather than dwell in the shadows as he used too. It is time to reveal some of the shadows to the light.” The Van begins to drive as both men go over the file carefully. Noticing there are specific areas blotched out with black in and marker. “You know we need all information.” “Tsk tsk really gentleman do you really think we would sacrifice all national security? You only need what is needed for your investigation.” A soft chuckle is heard as she sounds like she is adjusting her position in her seat. Kristoff grins a moment recognizing the voice finally. “So what does the Secret Service have at stake here Agent Morales?” Suddenly the breaks on the van slam as both Kristoff and Reischmann are thrust forward hard into the plexiglass type material. As they recover a van door slams as footsteps are heard as the two side doors are whipped open quickly as a female about 5'10” straight jet black hair looks at them. “What is my stake? That is my damn brother, How in the hell do you think I want to take it.” She reaches in and grabs Kristoff dragging him out looking him in the eyes. She reaches back with everything she is and slaps Kristoff, she suddenly drags him out of the van. “You put him undercover and got him killed.” Reischman quickly pulls his side arm out pointing it at Morales. “Lady you have got five seconds to let the man go if you know whats good for you.” As Reischmann readies to shoot he is distracted by a gun pointed at his head from inside the van. Leaning from the front seat is another man staring him down. Reischmann looks at the man and nods to him as they both slowly lower their weapons. Kristoff stands to his feet dusting himself off as he looks at Morales. “Dammit Morales you know your brother was my best friend.” Rubbing his cheek he looks at her accusingly. “We already established that Mr. Whitecloud isn't involved in that, but we want to get his assistance somehow.” Morales smiles a moment as she steps towards the van and reaches in. She pulls out the envelope and pulls out some photos. She carefully holds up a specific photo and taps it. “You want his attention use her, if there is anything he is loyal to family no matter the cost.” “Did not want to even go there Morales, guess you are right the game has changed.” He frowns and turns climbing back into the van. 4:35 pm High Spirits Casino.... Grunts of exertion are heard as Shaman presses up around 500 lbs of weight on the bench press. His eyes concentrating on the bar as he presses it upwards slowly, then slowly lowering it back down to his chest. As he presses upwards again a foot is propped on the edge of the weight bench nearby as a woman's lower form is seen from the corner of his eyes. “So when did those two recruit you Morales?” Shaman slowly presses the weight up and placing it into the holders. He sits up slowly looking at the three staring at him. Morales tosses his records file down onto the open space on the bench in front of him. Reaching down he opens it slightly. “Nice to know my personal records are so safe in St. Louis, perhaps I should contact a lawyer now. I mean even the Secret Service isn't above the law.” He wipes his face with a towel and then lays it over his records at the end of the bench. Reischmann walks over to him pointing an accusing finger in his face as he speaks. “Hey jack ass you left us out in the desert fending for ourselves.” “Fending for yourselves less than ten miles from civilization with two jugs of water, that's a gallon a piece by the way.” He leans back and lifts the weight off his chest and begins benching it again. “A donut....every......half.....mile...” Morales laughs as she looks at Reischmann as he turns a shade of read normally only new born babies show. He goes to grab Shaman as Kristoff grabs his arm and shakers his head no. Kristoff holds a finger up towards Reischmann turning towards Shaman. “So you thought that little stunt in the desert wouldn't cause you to have any problems?” Kristoff walks over and presses down on the bar pushing it down towards Shaman's chest. Looking up at Kristoff Shaman smiles as he presses upwards lifting Kristoff up as well as he places the weight on the bar. Standing up he looks over at Reischmann. “What I did was simply remind you two idiots anything can happen in Vegas. Then again if Morales here didn't have her head so far up some Democrats ass she would remind you about simple cause and effect.” Morales jumps up and points at Shaman. “If you didn't leave us hanging at Kelly Air Force base back in 98 Andrew we probably wouldn't be having this conversation.” Turning his head he looks at Morales. “Abandoning you eh? You always think that a guy you fell in love with abandoned you Morales.” He jerks his thumb over his shoulder at the other two. “These two dug a hole for your brother, and yes I left the air force, I even spent two years in the Navy afterward. Don't give me any crap about leaving, only person I left behind was you, You should remember why as well.” He stands up fully cracking his neck from side to side as he walks over towards the punching bag. He begins throwing heavy rights and lefts into the bag. “You know what pisses me off most here, I gotta go deal with four jack offs in a wrestling match this week.” Morales opens her eyes a bit wider realizing what he is talking about. “ Yeah you know the one Morales its called Belmont.” “You know Bryan Mayhem, Donny Diamond, Toga, and Dorn Tracer or was it Tracer Dorn, it doesn't really matter does it.” throwing a right hook into the punching bag he continues to throw punches. Morales walks up and places a hand on his shoulder as Shaman stops and looks over his shoulder at her. “Look Andy I am asking this to help bring peace to my family for my brother. You know he was killed under the FBI's watch.” Shaman for once is caught struggling with his conscious. Kristoff steps forward and hands him three envelopes with a serious look on his face. “These are the last resorts if you do not cooperate with us.” Opening the envelopes slowly he looks inside as he raises his head after looking at the first one, followed by the second and then finally the third. Kristoff nods his head and then grins. “We can't touch your brother since he works for the government in the military, but your sisters and wife are a whole different story.” With no chance for reaction he lashes out grabbing Kristoff by the throat and side stepping in and around behind him. With a quick gripping motion his huge arms are ready to snap his neck in one quick motion. “Threatening my family will cost you more than your job Kristoff.” Gasping for air, Kristoff grasps at Shaman's huge arms struggling. Morales and Reischmann quickly spring into action. His rage boiling over and throws Kristoff to the ground as Reischmann reaches down and picks up the envelopes. “When were you going to tell me about this Kristoff?” “National Security none of your business!” Kristoff gasps as he stands up, Reischmann steps back and belts Kristoff. Kristoff staggers backwards and trips over a weight bench. His eyes opened wide in surprise. “My ass Kristoff your just playing with people just like Morales over there.” Reischmann turns and looks at Shaman as he hands him a business card from the inside pocket of his coat. “Call me kid, I think we need to talk and bring your lawyer along, I'll have the governor in the office waiting.” Reischmann turns head for the door as Kristoff gets up realizing he had just screwed up big time in this investigation. “Resichmann wait a minute...just wait a damn minute.” Hurrying he follows Reischmann out the door. Morales stands up as Shaman turns and looks at her as well. Morales stays silent for a moment, she sighs softly as she looks at Shaman. “ Look Andy I didn't mean for any of this to happen. I was simply told you were part of everything and wanted you to answer for the past, I was ...was so damned angry at you!” “Look Kristy this whole mess you got caught up in is part of the major drug problems we are having here in Las Vegas, it starts in town and for some reason rolls into Nellis. You want to help then help fix this issue.” He reaches down and grabs the towel off the weight bench. He then points to her wedding band smiling. “I am glad you could move on from that back then.” looking down at the wedding band she nods, She holds it up smiling. “Yeah I married Marcus.” “Wait a second Marcus Davies?” He laughs a bit as he looks around. Shaking his head he motions for Morales to come. He turns opening the door to his officer as he slowly enters. “You can tell me why I am a central point in this investigation.” “There better not be any tequila involved, that is what got us in our mess the first time.” She points at him and then to herself as they both laugh. Several Hours later at Nellis AFB.... Kristoff is sitting at a desk with papers and maps laid out before him, His eyes going over everything. He doesn't hear the door open as someone walks in. Turning at the last possible moment he is struck over the head from behind with no struggle. Before he passes out he hears a few voices. “Will he wake up?” Someone says whispering. The other person stepping into view looking him in the eyes as he finally succumbs to darkness. “Not unless he is superman.” Slowly he begins to come too again, how long was he out he thinks to himself. The road is bumpy, a lot bumpier than he would have thought. Opening his eyes he notices Reischmann and Morales unconscious laying near him bound up. He slowly rolls his head with the roll of the vehicle towards the front, but in the darkness he cannot make out who the drivers are. Through the windshield he can see clear stars in the sky. Whispering so low nobody could hear him. “We aren't in Vegas anymore.” The vehicle slows down and somebody leaps out of the van. A dragging sound is heard and then the sound of chain link fence. Someone climbs back into the front of the van speaking. “The Colonel said this is a secure facility, nobody has been here since the late fifties, they even have old air craft sitting in the warehouse here. The Military doesn't get rid of its secret toys do they?” The van drives for a few more miles on the bumpy road as they come to a stop. Kristoff looks over and notices Morales eying him as well as Reischmann questioningly. Shrugging to the other two he rolls over and sits up as the side door slides open. “Nice to see your awake old friend. Glad it was you truthfully because it would have been hell to try and convince Mr. Whitecloud to come on out here without casualties.” The tall figure in the dark motions for someone as a flashlight is turned on blinding all three. “Get them to the warehouse, it is time for some of our own questioning.” All three were roughly dragged into the old warehouse, Kristoff recognized two B-29 bombers with tarps over them and well preserved. A little confused he looked around and noticed a few World War 2 German aircraft there as well. He turned his head and noticed a man walking towards them removing his military beret. “Well Kristoff, don't recognize old Colonel Gladstoff?” He chuckles deeply as he turns around showing three chairs with heat lamps sitting above each one. Kristoff is pushed down into the chair and tied with extra care. “Now we know how this works don't we Kristoff? You sit here and roast like Marshmellow and I question you, while taunting you with water for relief.” Morales looks at Reischmann, Reischmann in turn looks at Kristoff. Gladstoff looks at all three. “Feels like roulette wheel doesn't it? Who is going to make me the great winner?” One of the soldiers drags forward a large battery looking device with cables hooked to it, Gladsstoff takes one of the cables in his hand and pours water on Kristoff. “Just going to get thing started slowly comrade, going to soften you up for the main course.” Shoving the cable into Kirstoffs calves he laughs loudly as Kristoff roars out his pain and shakes from the shock of the electricity. The lights in the old building dim a bit as the screams echo into the night mixed with laughter.
