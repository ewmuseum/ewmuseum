Maniacal?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless -=Mayhem stares into the mirror. His image is dark, satanic, evil, and every other adjective you can use to describe the dark side. There's a large crack running down the center of mirror, but his face is not distorted. He's made history, and ended it all in the same night. The very thought brings a smile to demented face. Its a dark grin, not quite meeting the cheekbones. He closes his eyes and lets all the hype soak into his body. Once again, like he did years ago he's made news in the ring. All three major networks are all planning another 'Violence in Wrestling' special to air within the next few months. And like before, it was at the hands of 'The Lunatic' Bryan Mayhem.=- Mayhem:'They keep telling you you're old and washed up, yet completely insane. I may have proven the latter, but I get better with age.' -=His smile gets bigger, finally meeting at his cheekbones. He smiles as if hes a child that hears the door open and knows Daddy is home from work.=- Mayhem:'You know I heard the skull crack when I dropped him on his head, I felt his legs snap. I even heard him say 'God help me' when I had him up for the 5th Oblivion Bomb in a row, the one that fractured his skull. Now they call me Maniacal and dangerous. I warned them didn't I? I told you all that Oblivion is coming. And they thought Jason was insane.' -=The image in the mirror shakes his head left to right slowly, showing that sadistic grin of The Lunatic.=- Mayhem:'You haven't seen anything yet.' -=Once again, he begins to beam. The mental state of Bryan Mayhem is beginning to show. He almost destroyed the life of a young upcoming talent, and he seems happy about it.=- Mayhem:'I received a letter in the mail from a mother, and I want to read it to you.' -=The image seems to wait to hear the words, even though it's his image mimicking his every move. Bryan grabs the piece of paper.=- Mayhem:'Dear the man they call Mayhem. I am the mother of 2, and my eldest son whose name I won't give you the honor of knowing, is 13 years old and absolutely worships you if you want to call it worship. Posters of your painted face cover his walls, and I'm beginning to find it disturbing. Now I'm old enough to know that this is sports entertainment and it's all intended to entertain the masses. It's suppose to be fake. Well when my son heard of your return to the ring he got excited so I waited in like for 8 hours to get tickets to WorldWide here in Phoenix this past week. Your match came up and his eyes glowed. It wasn't until your brutal beating, and nonstop beating until the ring was a puddle of blood that I realized you are not entertaining, you really are hurting people, you really do work for a darker Lord than the rest of us. Because of you, my cable was shut off, and I hope mothers across the world do the same. Maybe if mothers across the world decide to boycott this product of violence, and your immensely large ratings dip, your owner Mr Caldera blames you and fires you. I will sleep better tonight knowing that your face and sadism will not rot my son's mind ever again. May God send you to Hell to learn your lesson Mr. Mayhem. Sincerely, a loving concerned mother.' -=Mayhem puts the letter down, and once again smiles. No one has ever seen this man smile so much in their life except for today.=- Mayhem:'I'm glad you care so much about your son's well being. But you're too late. As you sleep tonight your son will have dreams of fire and blood. He's already crossed over, and because you're too busy getting your nails and hair done, too busy caring about what people at the office think of you instead of your young adolescent son, he's decided to cross over and become, well me. Mom and Dad never paid attention to me, and now look. So instead of writing letters, maybe you should be a mother and not pretend bitch.' -=Thunder crashes outside, and a wind blows hard through the open windows blowing the candles out. It's pitch black.=- Mayhem:'GCW it's only going to get worse. Jake Summers was the beginning. This is the end of everything, and I'm the one to blame.' -=Mayhem strikes a match and lights the candles. This time staring back at him is not his own image, but the face of battered and beaten old man. Bryan's father stares back at Bryan though the reflections of the mirror.' Mayhem:'SONOFABITCH STOP!!!' -=In either anger or fear Bryan smashes the mirror with his fists. Blood from his knuckles begins to ooze down the mirror dripping onto the letter. He puts his fist to his face and tastes the blood, the salty stream of life. He looks down, and dripped onto the paper in blood is 'O NEIL'=- Mayhem:'NO!!!'
