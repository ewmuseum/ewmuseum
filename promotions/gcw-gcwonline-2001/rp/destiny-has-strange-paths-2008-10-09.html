Destiny has strange paths!

9 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/08 Diary log ... Have had the US Title twice now and no worthy opponents other than Brad Jackson have stepped up. Jackson has my respect because he brings the fight to me. Mayhem has brought the fight to me as well and i have enjoyed that. Soon someone else has got to gain a pair and face me. Us Championship title is beginning to just weigh on me now. Shaman diary entry during his second US Title reign. Prologue 6-17-02 Tag Team Champion. Shaman and Magician known as The Horsemen had won the WWA World Tag Team Championship. Being in the WWA they had to get a foothold on the titles. A dominant tag team duo. Inner turmoil destroyed the tag team and the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse fell apart. Tag titles were lost a month and a half later. Shaman and Magician parted ways to never join forces again. GCW 2007 WorldWide 70: Banned & Exiled vs. The Establishment vs. Oblivion vs. Choosers of the Slain. Oblivion was screwed out of the titles by the former cowardly champions Banned & Exiled. Shaman and Mayhem had begun their reign of terror only to see it come to an end later that year. Mayhem disappeared and Shaman was left to fend for himself Oblivion sunk back into the shadows to be unheard of for awhile. Talk swirled around to see whether or not they would ever return. GCW 2008 Shaman and Mayhem reunite reforming Oblivion opening the road for destruction and discord through GCW once again. Only roadblock they see before themselves is themselves. Times wheel continues to turn.... Somewhere unknown the world is only wondering what is to come. A match flits to life as it is raised and lights a huge torch setting the oils upon the torches tightly wrapped clothes and fabrics. The torches flames flicker and cast odd shadows around a cave. On the wall is cave paintings seemingly hundreds of years old. One specific cave painting shows two men side by side standing before a huge drawn black circle. As you turn you see Shaman standing there the flames casting an odd color upon his deeply reddish bronze skin. He observes the painting as he cracks his head from side to side. His eyes narrow at the vision as he smiles slightly. "I never would have believed it unless i had seen it with my two eyes. Someone told me these painting seemed to mimic Shaman and Mayhem as Oblivion. The circle is the darkness of the unknown. It is what awaits those who cross us in Oblivion." Shaman stops and rubs his chin. "Eagles reach Australia has some unique just discovered painting not too long ago. Lemme see if i can remember this correctly." Shaman turns and places his hand upon the stone wall. "Eagles Reach rock art includes stencils of hands, arms, ancient boomerangs and hafted axes, which were probably created by spraying ochre with the mouth over and around the objects. The stencilled hands are regarded as the oldest paintings at Eagles Reach, and anywhere between 2,000 and 4,000-years old." Shaman grins and pulls out a piece of paper as he holds it. "Sad truth is i had to remember this damn information. I had a point to make with it, but hell those paintings are older than hell." Shaman crumbles the paper and tosses it to the side as he turns and makes his way over to another stone and sits down upon it his hands resting on his knees looking around. "Truth is i wanted to be here in this spiritual place. I needed to quietly step back a bit and put my career into perspective. This place and my heritage seem to be some of the few things i have precious to me now. I have Mayhem who is unlike any real brother anyone could imagine. He and i are comrades in arms if you will. One moment after another we seem to be thinking on a similar level. All goals must be achieved. We both are driven for the fact of achieving these goals." Shaman brings his hands together hard as a loud slapping sound is heard. "We are like metal and a magnet. We come together no matter how many times we go about each other in brutal dangerous matches. It is our way of making up. Violence is a useful tool in reconciling differences. Shit happens and we move on. Thus Oblivion is much like the mighty Phoenix we will always be reborn and forge the flame of Oblivion as never seen before. Yet here and now there are still lingering questions as to how this path had been laid before us and where it leads." Shaman waves a hand across the ground as he drops some sand. The sand a sign of change and the ever moving earth as it changes constantly. This destiny is represented in the sand that he dropped. A symbolic sign of the changing sands of time. "A path is one we choose to walk. We all as mankind have this choice. It is our greatest gift we have the right to choose." Shaman turns and looks out over the cave and steps out grabbing the torch as he walks away. He steps through the cave entrance revealing the starred sky outside as he looks up at it. "You see the thousands upon thousands of stars in the sky. My people have observed them for centuries. I have observed them numerous times in my 34 years. Losing your mother and father. Watching your siblings disappear and never return. No communication and yet i have a goal. This goal is to take something because we wish too." Spreading his arms wide Shaman seems to embrace the sky as he watches the stars above. His eyes twinkle in their ever ongoing glimmers high above the earth. He looks up at them as he speaks. "Spirits of my ancestors shine down upon this world and guide those less fortunate who do not know the path of destiny. Guide my brother and i into Oblivion's dark embrace and show us the way to the embraces of our chosen destinies." Shaman slowly lowers his arm as he simply throws the torch out over the cliff as he turns and begins to walk down the path which just happens to be to his right. He slowly descends the plateau he was standing on. As he walks down the edge he ponders things to come. "Samson and Delylah our paths are crossing. Brad Jackson you and i will dance once more. You will never feel the pain for which i have in store for each of you. I will unleash Mayhem upon you. Oblivion will reign supreme as we crush those daring to oppose us. The destiny is set for you. Grasp at the little gold belts like straws. You will feel the force that is Oblivion. Destiny shall show you that it will repeat by being conquered by those with the power to do so." Shaman grins sadistically as he walks. "Apples of Discord were sewn when Banned & Exiled screwed with us cheating us out of our grand prize." Shaman rubs his chin and looks at the stars. "Perhaps i can explain this with the source of its beginning." Shaman leans against the stone face of the plateau and speaks calmly. ""This story begins at the wedding of the hero Peleus and the water-nymph Thetis, parents of the famous Achilles. All the gods are invited to the party, saving one: Eris, goddess of discord - an understandable omission. Eris, didn't see it that way and resolved to get revenge." She stole one of Hera's golden apples and inscribed it "Property of the Fairest" and tossed it onto a banquet table "where it was squabbled over by every goddess on hand." Turning Shaman pushes off the wall as he turns and begins walking down the face of the plateau. "History has weird ways of showing itself again. I faced a pair of men in the past who claimed to be Greek gods. They are no longer running around preaching their supremacy. I defeated them when i was one of The Horsemen. Apocalypse does not whiter and die, but it does give way to Oblivion. All shall stand before Oblivion and be judged. Another phrase from Greek mythology comes to mind in this place." Snapping his finger as he raises his eyes staring straight out across the landscape. "Oblivion is like a Triton amongst Minnows. For the less educated i will give you the explanation as i have before." Shaman chuckles disturbingly as he begins descending once again. "Poseidon and Amphitrite, represented as a fish with a human head. It is this sea-god that makes the roaring of the ocean by blowing through his shell." The expression means: "A great man among a host of inferiors.In this case great men." Shaman stands at the bottom of the plateau as he looks back up at it and he turns and walks across the desert. His eyes focused forward in the darkness of the night sky. He knows that the time has come for Oblivion to stand and make it know that they are here to stay. "Many have been forewarned for two years. Bryan Mayhem and Shaman have formed the most deadly tandem in the history of GCW. You step into the path of destiny like a freight train you get rundown. I do not claim to be the best nor do i claim that i am unstoppable. I am merely the Irresistible force and Mayhem is the Immovable object. We slammed together twice and each time our destiny came together and turned in the right direction. This force will come crushing the enemies we have laid before us. We have a list of names and upon our path we will reveal each of them as we continue." Shaman stops and looks forward. "Lia you and i have two different views on the same subject. You and i will butt heads until the day is done. Only problem is the fact that you and i have faced off numerous times over the years. Do you think i will be some lackey before you? Not in the slightest so go and handle your business with Zenith. The time has come for me to do what i do best. I will walk on that hard path and grasp something that i determine belongs to Mayhem and myself." Turning once again Shaman walks to an old jeep and climbs in it as he starts it up with a loud roar. "Oblivion has come. It doesn't matter if NME, The Establishment, Samson & Delylah stand before us. Victory is to be had and it will be ours. Suck it up little boys and girls for our time is nigh and you have no say in the matter." Shaman laughs as he turns the jeep and leaves a trail of dust to where he is heading to mask only what he left behind.
