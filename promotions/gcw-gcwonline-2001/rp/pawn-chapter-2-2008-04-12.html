"This wasn't a part of our deal!" . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . "What deal?" . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . "This is your punishment for breaking the rules!" Tossing and turning in a pool of his own sweat, Kaden dreamed in a deep, dark, and awful place. His thoughts had been taken further into his mind during this slumber, a place he would soon wish he hadn't been taken to. It was still in the early hours of the morning, well before he had scheduled himself to rise and shine, but something tore him away from his inner thoughts, causing him to wake and rise in bed. "What the fuck?" He asked himself aloud in the daze of being brought back so suddenly, breathing heavily and still dripping with sweat. His restlessness, along with his sudden jolt in bed and speaking aloud, started a two domino effect, and Kyra soon came to as well. "What's wrong?" She asked Kaden, whose breathing started to calm down. He didn't answer right away, and it concerned her. "Are you alright?" She asked him, checking on him, still waiting for a response. A few more moments of silence went by before Kaden finally snapped out of his daze. He wiped the dripping sweat from his forehead with the palm of his right hand before rubbing both of his hands together to dry them of the perspiration. "Yeah Ky, I'm fine." He finally answered. "Just one of 'those' dreams, you know?" He responded with a question back to her. "Don't you mean a nightmare?" Kyra asked back, but not letting him answer. "You never sweat this bad, or wake up breathing like that after just any dream." She said, still sounding concerned. Swinging his legs out to his right, off the side of the bed, Kaden stood up, and walked around the front of the bed, moving towards the bedroom door. "It's nothing to worry about really, just another one of those things your mind does to fuck with you." He said to her, trying to stop her from worrying, almost as if he, too, needed convinced. "More than likely just games my mind is playing on me because I'm nervous about this Friday." He admitted as he opened the door to the bedroom. "It probably doesn't really mean anything, anyway, so there's nothing to be concerned about." ******* Plain, dark, and dismal; just a few words that described the halls, rooms, offices, and pretty much everything else pertaining to Southwind Gardens Mental Institute just outside of Wichita, Kansas. However, down one of the patient quarters hallways was a sixty-something male, decked in navy blue slacks and a patterned sweater of many different colors. He stood, bending over, outside one of his patient's rooms, talking through a small sliding door that ever door down this hall was equipped with. "Soon, Patient Six Sixty-Six, we will have another to work with." He said to the mental patient who merely sat motionless on the floor of his quarters, unable to keep saliva from escaping between his two lips. Crossing his arms, and standing upright, the elderly man, identified by a name tag pinned securely to his sweater as Dr. Damian Sorrow, continued to speak with the patient of his, one who he's obviously grown attached to over time. "You know Six Sixty-Six, for the longest time I thought you would be the only special one who came here that would ever deserve my attention." He speaks again, looking around this time. A small chuckle comes from the man shortly before he continues. "Now I do not mean to make you seem less special to me, but there will be another who deserves the same special treatment as I have given you these last two years." Leaning up against the door across the hall from the room of the patient he speaks with, Damian lets out a heavy sigh. This was no ordinary sigh, though; it was almost one of pure pleasure, coming from a man who deals with very sick patients every day. "Things are finally starting to come together Patient Six Sixty-Six; if only you were well enough to understand it all." He confesses to the mental patient, obviously not aware of anything this man is saying. "Though, I'm sure if you were well enough, you'd recognize..." He was cut off by a young woman before finishing what he had to say. "Dr. Sorrow?" The young woman asked, trying to not be rude by interrupting his one way conversation. "There's someone on the phone for you in your office." She said to him, looking concerned, as if she knew she shouldn't have interrupted him. "Lia!" Dr. Sorrow raised his voice, but did not look at her right away. "I appreciate the job you do as my secretary, but everyone who calls to speak with me who says that something is of the utmost importance does not mean that you should always bring it to my attention." He explained to her, unfolding his arms from in front of him, and putting his hands into the pockets of his slacks. "So just write the name and number down, and I will get back with them later." He finished. His secretary, still standing there, moved the stringy, black hair from her bangs out from in front of her face, placing them behind her left ear. "Well, he said that you'd probably want to take this call, and that you've been waiting to hear from him for a few days now." She explained. "But I'll just take down Mr. Michaels' contact information and let you get back with him." She finished as she turned around and obviously started to head back to his office. The information processed quickly in Damian's head, and he quickly shut the small sliding door to the room of his patient, and took off for his secretary. "Lia!" He raised his voice to her once more, this time not out of disappointment. "On second thought, I will take this call." He admitted to her now. "Have you already transferred the call to my office?" He asked, and she nodded her head. "Thank you so much." Damian thanked her, and she smiled at him. He then began to explain the importance of the call aloud as he headed towards his office. "This IS a very important call about an appointment I have with this man Friday afternoon." He admitted. "The future Patient Seven Twenty-Seven is a friend of his and lives somewhere in Michigan, so we have to discuss our plans for admission of this very special individual." ******* If tensions were a fog inside of his Lansing, Michigan home, visibility would be zero. Kaden paced back and fourth in the middle of the living room of his home, while his parents, and Kyra, were seated. His parents, sitting together on the couch, looked just as uncomfortable as Kaden did walking back and forth, while Kyra, playing with her hair, sat on one of the two recliners looking lost. "Oh Christopher, would you just sit down already!" Mary, his mother, shouted. He didn't open his mouth, and his pace was undeterred. Kyra did her best to blend in and look pretty so she wasn't forced to talk, but it wouldn't be much longer before the awkward silence would be broken by Kaden's father, Alec. "Damn it Kaden, we didn't come here to make thinks worse between the three of us!" He stood up from his seat and yelled, upset at the fact that nothing has really been said since Mary and he arrived. "We've got some things we need to tell you before its too late." He finally admitted, still standing. Kaden, too, stood still after what had been said, but quickly moved himself closer to his father, almost as if he wanted to get into Alec's face. Despite Kaden's size, and the four inches in height he had over Alec, his father still managed to make him look small. This did not prevent Kaden from bursting out in anger. "What the fuck do you mean, 'before it's too late?'" He asked, raising his voice. "Don't you think it's ALREADY a little late to be 'explaining' things to me?" He questioned. "Don't you think it's a little late for me to change my opinion of you two?" He queried yet another issue. "Please let us..." Mary tried to butt in and calm the situation down, but it only seemed to make things worse. Kaden's glare turned from his father to her, and being the person she is, did not say any more, as she didn't want to make anything worse. He turned his attention back to Alec and proceeded with his violent attack of words. "You two pathetic souls don't even really belong here right now!" He confessed. "I wasn't in the right state of mind when you caught me in the hospital for Kyra's incident, and I should have never made it seem like you were welcome here!" He finished before going back to pacing. Finding the right words to speak, Alec remained motionless. He opened and closed his mouth in succession two or three times as if he wanted to say something, but no words ever came out. Then, just when he was actually about to speak, the doorbell rang. Kyra was quick to her feet, and out of the living room to the front door. The silence in the living room continued as Kaden listened to the activities at the front door. "Hey Justin!" Kyra exclaimed. "It's been so long. How have you been?" She asked, and there was no answer heard. "Where's Lindsay at?" Kyra asked yet another question concerning Justin's fianc?. "And who are these people?" She asked again, still not receiving an answer right away. "Sorry Kyra, I'm here for business, and the four of us don't have much time." Justin's voice could be heard as footsteps could be heard entering the home, right before the front door was closed. The looks on both Alec and Mary's faces could tell a story, but Kaden didn't seem to notice as he was too busy pondering the situation himself. Just moments later, Kaden's friend Justin Michaels came into view, and entered the living room followed by the much older Dr. Damian Sorrow, and a couple of rather largely built identical twin males. "What the fuck is this all about Justin?" Kaden questioned Justin, at the same time questioning the friendship the two of them have. "So this much be Christopher?" Damian spoke, stepping towards Kaden, giving him the once over. "You are very impressive Christopher." He said to him, and Kaden just glared at him, his parents doing the same, only their glares were more in shock than frustration. Getting a bit physical, Kaden shoved Dr. Sorrow away from him, and the twins stepped forward as if ready to pounce. "Kaden, this is the man who I wanted you to meet. I'm sure in time you will thank me for this, and realize that he is nothing more than a man wanting to help you out." Justin admitted to Kaden, as well as everyone else. Just about everyone's jaws on the opposite end of this deal dropped open. "Don't be alarmed Christopher, we will take VERY good care of you at Southwind Gardens." Damian spoke once more. "And maybe in due time, you will be well enough to return to those who care for you so much." He finished. "What the fuck is Southwind Gardens?" Kaden asked, but not allowing an answer before letting his actions speak louder than words, giving Dr. Sorrow a nice taste for his right fist. The twins quickly jumped on Kaden, however, and before his father, Alec, could respond, he was met with Justin's right fist, surprising him, and knocking him to the ground. The brute twins lifted Kaden to his feet, and Damian staggered to his feet. Kyra and Mary began to cry, and Alec, still on the floor, spoke up. "What are you doing?" He asked, almost whining. Wiping the drips of blood from the corner of his mouth, Damian answered. "We are taking him to a place he belongs until we can cure him. Southwind Gardens is a very lovely mental health institute in Kansas that studies the regrowth of normal brain functions, and implements the research in everyday life for its patients." He explained. "Justin was very kind and brave to inform us of Chris' situation, and we would be glad to aid in curing him, so if you will excuse us." He said, very rudely to finish, and the four of them began to walk off, Kaden being carried by the twins. "Let me go you bastards!" Kaden yelled, trying to break loose of their grip. "I don't belong there!" He continued to exclaim, pleading to not be taken away. "I'm not fucking crazy!" As the four of them continue to walk, Justin explains to Kyra, Alec, and Mary who start to follow them. "He needs help!" He says with a raised voice. Mary cries out for her son who continues to struggle to break free. "He's done nothing to deserve this!" This causes Damian to stop, and actually turn to Mary. "I'm only following procedure." Damian speaks, not really explaining anything all, or discussing what procedure he is speaking of. He doesn't get far before Alec grabs a hold of his arm, and turns him back around. Pulling the doctor closer to him, he speaks softly, under his normal voice level. "This wasn't a part of our deal." He said, trying to plead with the man. A look of confusion comes across Kyra's face, and she questions. "What deal?" But her question does not get answered. Still in the grip of Alec Calix, Damian motions for Justin, and the twins, to continue outside, and to the vehicle they arrived in. He smiles at Alec, and then looks at both Mary and Kyra, before turning his attention back to Alec. His smile grows even more this time before he spoke. "This is your punishment for breaking the rules!" Whatever the comment entailed to Alec, it caused him to let go, and Damian brushed himself off quickly, before exiting the home. Alec, Kyra, and Mary were all left standing there in shock of what had just happened.
