'Memories are just where you laid them '

31 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/31/06 Memories are just where you laid them Dragging the waters til the depths give up their dead What did you expect to find? Was it something you left behind? Don't you remember anything I said when I said, -Fuel, Hemorrage (In My Hands) ~A Week Ago~ ~Enroute back to Charlotte~ ~Coming from South Carolina~ The meeting had made things into a very long day with Dani. She had gone on what she thought was an honor ceremony, but it turned into a meeting on sacred ground between her and, ultimately, their boss right now. But he seems to have taken a personal interest in Dani also, and what or who she really is. But what happened has set Dani off a bit. She couldn't help but get by this uneasy feeling from it all. And as she flipped through the stack of papers and books that had been given to her. They had been watching her for weeks, almost months, but had let her try to do it on her own. It hadn't been until her many-times great grandfather had stepped in and tricked her into saying a name that everything had finally fallen into place. That name still rang through her mind, still called out to her to say it again. Suriel. She flipped through the many pages, browsing through them. Copies of pages from old texts, and other tidbits from scrolls and drawings. She knew of her enemy now, but she did not know him. She could read about Astaroth, and his many relations. About his followers and the legions of demons they once commanded. And about some of the others who have been known to have sided or worked with him before. All of it was pretty boring though, so she only glanced it over, she'd get more in depth once she was home and safe. Well at least what a wrestler would call home on the road. She looked up at the front seat of her Highlander SUV. Josh was driving, which was interesting enough when you thought about it. The man had just gotten his License back a few months ago, and it wasn't exactly in the most legal ways he'd got it in this instance as it was. Gray was seated, half asleep in the passenger's seat. He was looking over some charts from the work he'd started with that new girl, Alexis. She seemed nice enough and all, but so far had been pretty offstandish with everyone but him. But that meant that Gray was in scientist mode, and every waking moment would be put to his tasking with that bit of his 'case'. Dani hit the last page of the stuff that had been given to her, at least the last page of the loose papers that had been stuffed into this many inch thick manila folder, and saw something she wasn't expecting. It contained what looked like an entry from an old encyclopedia, Suriel- ?The Wrath of God?, Angel of Guidance. Archangel of the Seraphim who was responsible for meeting out punishment for those angels who strayed from God's law. Under it, taking up the rest of the paper was the stylized symbol of Suriel. Innocently, Dani traced it with her fingers. ?So, what is your stake in all of this?? Something snapped in her. She would normally not do this, especially in her car. She picked her journal out of her backpack and opened it up. ?Hey, Gray, you have an extra pen? I have to write something down and I don't want to break out the India ink in the car.? Gray handed an expensive gel ink pen back, without really looking back at her. She took it, pulled the cap off and put it onto the butt end of the pen and started to write. Coming back from the meeting/ceremony. Well it wasn't any sort of ceremony. HE was there. Yes, He as in the Keeper. But this time I could truly feel the other presence inside of him. In fact I think that was the person who was in charge of this whole meeting. Well, I don't know if I should really be calling him a person. He's much, much more then that. I can see why they call him the man with two souls. And, his wife is just wow, she has a command about her that just takes over what ever you might be thinking. You can see the love they have for each other, and feel it when they are in the room together. The others have been pretty much quiet with me since we left. Josh and I had a connection on the match this week and what I much do, but that doesn't mean that he's accepted it fully. And then having to get naked in front of this man we met with so that I could show him my true self, he's just out of it. Gray, well he's in scientist mode with that new girl. I've noticed that he's been actually hanging with her instead of us. Then again, she's able to look at a petri dish and understand the damn thing. I can't. I don't even know what they're working on, I just know it deals with blood. Blaise and Ukyo? Well, they were there, but they didn't get involved. And now they're riding in their truck, following us. Its not like I'm expecting much. I know that they reported their findings to him, he's got to know that I was trying to find this stuff without any help. But his silence on the whole thing is pretty unnerving. Then again, I can understand where he's having issues trying to get this whole thing with Shadow and the others to work. In the end though, I am not sure I can say much about it, everyone seems to want to see me but yet no one seems to be able to understand me. I really need grandfather, I miss him. There are just times that it seems he is the only one that remembers me. Although, I will say this. When he... HE as in the second soul, reached out and touched me to help me manifest... I've never felt safer or more powerful. It was almost as if he could touch the very inner core of what I am and push it out to the surface. ------? The journal entry ended there, and Dani drifted... as it to another place or time. ~Somewhere~ The rain was making pitter-patter noises on the roof of the car. Dani enjoyed it a lot, reminded her of her favorite rattle. Where am I? She thought, looking around. A man sat in the front seat, his long black pony tail tied back tight. Next to her was a boy who looked like a perfect mini-me to that man. The boy was in a booster seat for traveling in the car. The man turned around and looked at him, smiling. ?Just a little while longer Adam and Mommy will be off from work. Then we're going out for ice cream, okay?? The boy nodded as he was drawing with a set of crayons, on a tablet in front of him. He suddenly holds the picture up. ?See what I finished Daddy?? Dani recognizes the man when he smiles at them both, although she's only seen him in pictures. Including a picture that she recently got and kept about a year ago. His name was Daniel Northsky, the man she was named after. Dani's heart sank. ?Well, where did you see this? At church?? The man held the picture up for both to see. It was a crude crayon job, but the shape could be made out. It was a man with dark skin, a pair of wings coming off his back. It was an angel. ?No papa, outside my window last night. He was looking in at us.? The man just smiled, but it looked almost forced this time. Before he could say anything else, a woman opened the passenger side door and climbed into the seat, setting a ladden purse down between her legs. She wore what looked like a waitress' uniform for the Caf? sign that Dani could barely make out in the window. The man and woman reached towards each other and embraced, kissing each other on the lips. The boy, Adam, interrupted them. ?Mommy! Look what I did! All Danielle could do was drool she isn't big enough to help draw yet. But, I put her name on it too for you.? Proudly the little boy handed his mother the drawing. Upon looking at it, the woman, whom Dani now knew as Lynn Northsky, looked suspiciously at her husband. She then to the pile of pictures that sat next to the boy in the backseat. ?Well, Adam, looks like you have been very busy waiting for me. Can I see?? ?Sure, Mom!? The boy tried to collect up the entire stack of loose pictures he'd pulled from his tablet and hand it to her. She tried to collect them as much as possible, her husbands hands coming in to help when needed. They get all but one, which Dani makes out pretty well as for some reason its drawn close to her... and it looks like she starts to chew on it. The boy reaches up and takes it from her, the corner of the paper coming away soaked with the spit from her mouth. ?It's not for you Dani. Babies are not allowed to have this, Mommy has said so.? Dani suddenly hears the sound of a baby crying, only it seems to be very close. It is close, because its coming from her. Suddenly a stuffed horse with a big goofy looking grin is put in front of her. Adam has pulled it out and set it down. He keeps his hand on it, as he trots it in front of her, making horselike noises. Dani hears the sound of a babe's giggle emanate from what would be her mouth. ?Don't worry Dani. You've got other toys that Mommy allows you to have.? He then looks up at his mom, a worried look on her face. ?I drew them for you and Grandpa.? Lynn looks at her husband and before she can say anything, he already answers her pending question. ?To your father's then?? The woman nods with out saying another word. ?Grandpa's? But you said we were going to the movies tonight. You said we were going to go see 'Back to the Future'!? The young boy started to sob. ?No, we're still going. But I need to go by Grandpa's first. I forgot the tickets there. Need to get them before we go, alright?? The boy starts to sob a bit less. He wiped a loose tear from his eye. ?Promise? ?I promise.? He tried to look up and over his mother's shoulders as she was looking at the stack of pictures he'd drawn. No one even noticed that the car was moving, and moving pretty quickly at that as it traveled down the freeway towards the reservation. ?This has to do with the man behind the house last night doesn't it? He kept looking at my bed room window. He was a angel he had to be. He looked like the ones that Grandpa told me in the story of the angels. But his wings where not white like Grandpa said they should be. And he looked like he was mad at me.? Lynn gave Daniel a worried look as they sped along.... **CRASH** Dani could only scream, she couldn't do anything else. The entire front end of the car suddenly collapsed in as what looked like a truck plowed into them. She could see her mother and father laying there, they looked okay, but they were bleeding from cuts on their head. The passenger's door opened, and a man's hands reached and and grabbed her mother's limp body. ?See I told you I could find them. No one can keep a treasure hidden from me. No one.? ?Of course, I'd never doubt you Barbatos, you've served our master well.? She recognized that last voice... Jaymes. The man who'd tried to take her against her will a bit over a year ago. The man who was now mentally a baby, thanks to her friends. The body of her mother was pulled from the car, a few moments later she could hear the driver's side door open. ?Don't forget the boy, either. He's the one we need to get rid of.? ?What about the infant?? ?She won't last long, not out here. Besides, with how much gas is leaking from these two, it wouldn't take long for something to happen anyway.? Dani was scared, and looked over at the boy named Adam. He whispered to her, his voice weak and jittery. ?Don't worry, Dani. You have the angels watching over you. Don't worry 'Mitaksi-ki'.? She heard the name in Lakhota. Her native language. He'd called her ?Little Sister?. And then she saw his door open, and a man who looked native, hair white and flowing reached in and pulled Adam from his seat. Dani could only think of one word to try to say. ?Ciyebaye-ki? But it was too late. She watched as the boy was taken over to a clearing next to the road. One man, the man she saw as Jaymes as running some rope up from the branch of a tree. But that was not the most horrific part. The man with the white hair, he set Adam down and then stepped behind him. She heard the gun's hammer cock back. And then she saw the flash and heard the bang... and saw the silhouette of the little boy fall at his feet. Then, the door next to her open, and hands reached out to grab her... ~Back to the Current Time~ Dani awoke with a start, in the backseat of her own car. But she didn't see that. Gray had reached back to try to wake her as things had gotten a bit vocal from her as she had dozed, her journal still in her lap. ?Dani? Dani? Are you alright?? Her eyes snapped open and tried to focus on the situation. What had just happened? She felt her heart pounding in her chest, the sweat dripping down her forehead. The others could see the paleness of her face. Dani looked at Gray, and in the blur that was her forward vision right now, she only saw one thing... white hair. Even though Gray's had a tinge to it that separated it out, the glow of the lights coming through and Dani's feel of 'snap back' from being awoken only showed her white. ?Why did you do it? Why did you kill him? Why did you kill Adam? Why did you take my Ciyebaye-ki!? Gray spoke Lakhota Sioux. He knew the word she'd just spoken. Dani lunged at Gray, and struck him in the arm. As she came up partially into the front seat, Josh slammed on the breaks and headed into the dirt at the side of the road. Instantly the radio came to life. ?Josh, what's up?? It was Blaise trying to get in contact with them, he was in the truck right behind them. Josh reached down to use the radio but could barely get a word in as Dani was going off on Gray. ?LET ME OUT OF HERE!? Josh looked and noticed that Dani's face and eyes were almost like they weren't fully here. And the it hit him... she was feeling sadness, anger, despair all at the same time. And he almost could pull something more then that, a face. The face of a little boy. ?Let me out!? Dani's eyes darted back and forth from Gray and then to the door. She was looking for a means of escape. ?Dani, stop. You know me, its Big Brother Gray. I'm you're Ciyebaye-ki.? From over the radio, since Josh was just holding down the button on the push to talk. ?Don't let her leave the car, whatever you do. She does a flight, and I mean a flight, we could loose her.? Gray answered him, very sternly. ?Blaise if you have an idea whats going on I would suggest you let me know. I don't know if I can force her to stay if she wants to go, with out hurting her. Get your furry ass here now!? Outside, the Chevy Avalanche the Blaise drove had spun out on the road up ahead and was on its way back. As soon as it pulled up, both Blaise and Ukyo hopped out of the truck. **Click** Josh moved and hit the button to make sure the doors on the Highlander were still locked. The mechanism moved to make sure they were, the tell tale sound coming out. But that wasn't really good to do. Dani lashed out and struck the driver's side window with the heel of her palm. Normally that wouldn't do anything, but right now, it was enough. Glass erupted outward, heading right for Ukyo who stopped and covered her face to protect herself. A few more hits and the rest of the glass was mostly gone from the window. Those were done with more traditional hits with a fist. Dani took the option and dove from the car window, a few obvious cuts going down her arms and legs as she did. She immediately came up and started to bolt across the road and into the woods on the other side. Ukyo called out. ?Blaise, she's rabbiting!? That's all he needed to hear as he started to run from the other side of her SUV after her. She was fast, faster then he should be. But a slight change in his mind and Blaise became the four legged hunter that he was born to be. He could see her aura, and she was almost in full manifestation as it was. If she got to the point where her wings came out, she could try to really fly away. Blaise was hoping she remembered that she's afraid of heights. He stayed a few feet behind her, as she tried to dodge him through the woods. Dani kept going until she suddenly stopped. Blaise had pulled back, and stopped when she did. He didn't want to press the issue. And then they both saw him. A man stepped out from the trees, looking, not at Dani, but almost through her. Blaise recognized the aura configuration on him, and something of the accent and language in which he spoke to Dani. Dani though, heard him as if he was speaking to her without any issues. ?So, my little treasure. I've found you at last. The master will be so proud of me.? The man had white hair, hair that would match the wings on his back if he had them out. She recognized him, fully. ?Barbatos...? ?Well, the question now would be, how to return you since you've bolted from your friends and protectors.? Blaise heard him draw what could only be a sword. Unmistakable grinding of metal on metal as it came from its sheath. And the particulars of this metal were pretty unique. Dani dropped to her knees. ?No.... You're the one who killed them.? ?And Jaymes was a nothing more then a stupid monkey for leaving you alone in the car. He got the wrong brat from the looks of it.? Blaise shifted, and then leaped out into the small clearing, pulling the two spirit blades he kept in his denim jacket out. ?You have a problem there?? The white haired man looked up at him. ?Maybe? Maybe not? You must be the wolf man who's got some older stuff mixed in with him. I've heard you're pretty hot shit.? Blaise nodded to him. ?I wouldn't put it that way. But then again, I'm my own worst critic. You might go try finding what's left of Zadikiel and ask him.? The man stopped his approach on Dani and looked Blaise fully in the eyes. ?What do you know of Zadikiel?? Blaise just smiled wide at the man. ?You're an angel, figure it out. But before you do, just know that I'm prepared to take you out if I have to... Angel. And I don't mean that as an indication of you having wings. I mean that as in your choir rank.? The man Dani had called Barbatos took a step back, Blaise stepped forward. He pulled his sword up, and Blaise snapped his left hand out, sending the spirit blade sailing. It made its mark, right between the ribs on the man's right side. That was enough, the man howled and his pure white wings came forth from his back, ripping the jacket and shirt he wore. He turned tail and headed way. ?Not tonight, not tonight. The master will know where she is. You can't hide her from me... you can't hide anything from me.? Dani was in tears, Blaise could see that. Her muscled corded and ready to bolt again. She went to do just that.... Until Blaise got up next to her. And then her vision went to black. ~Almost a Day Later~ ~Charlotte, Her Inn Room~ Dani woke up, her head pounding. Sitting next to her, a damp cloth in his hand was Joshua. She could feel the bumps and bruises she had from the night before, and it looked like she had a change of clothes on. In fact, she was in her night clothes she normally wore. A few bandaged were covering the deeper cuts on her arms and legs. In fact, Dani found that she couldn't move her hands too well. They felt almost bound. She could move them, but not her fingers. She pulled them up and both of them were wrapped in gauze. ?You're healing fast, but you're not done yet.? It was Gray, who was standing on the other side of the room, trying not to look in her direction. ?What happened?? ?You tell us? All we know is you fell asleep, woke up, started screaming to let you out. When we stopped you punched the window of your truck out, and bolted into the woods. In there, Blaise said you ran into someone who knew you, threatened you and apparently works for the Evening Star.? She felt her head, it was pounding. In fact her whole body felt like it had been through a meat grinder or something. Especially a spot on the back of her head. ?It's mostly a blur for me. I remember having a dream. I was young, in a car... there was a wreck and the men there took them and... killed them. They were leaving me to die on my own, cause of how young I was. But something grabbed me from the car, before it caught on fire and blew up. But that's where it stopped... after seeing the white haired man, the one from the woods shoot him in the head.? Josh spoke up as he was looking at removing her dressing on her right hand. ?Shot who?? ?Adam.? ?Who?? ?I don't know. I'll need to speak with grandpa. I've never seen him before. But in the dream, he was my little brother. He was the one they thought was going to carry the line. They didn't see me as a threat. It was Jaymes and this angel.? Dani was holding the back of her head with her other hand as Josh unwound the bandages from her right. He looked at the cuts that were accelerated in their healing right now. ?Well they're looking good. Guess its a really good thing that Angels can't get infections or other stuff like that.? She looked down at the almost fully closed cuts. But the bandages were deep red with what had been leaking from them before. ?I'll take those and toss 'em. Get her cleaned up. She's got a match in a few days. Need to make sure those things have time to get healed up, even at a fast rate.? Josh nodded as he put the current batch of soaked bandages into a zip lock bag. Gray took the bag and then turned to leave the room. He stepped out to the common area, noticing Blaise standing there. ?You trained at the South Wind Academy, didn't you?? Blaise looked up at him. ?Graduated. Why do you ask?? ?Your technique. If Josh ever found out you hit her, he'd take you on. You'd probably kill him, but he'd take offense. I've seen that type of nerve strike before on guys who washed out of South and tried to come to West due to our more lax discipline. Its effective, but only if you've mastered it.? Blaise nodded to him. ?So what do you have?? Blaise shook his head. ?Trouble. This guy, white hair, sword, white wings. Apparently one of the last remaining servants of this Evening Star fellow. His specialty? Finding lost treasure. She knows him, she said his name. I'm not going to tempt fate. He's only an Angel, lowest Choir. But he's got his tricks. They're on to us, Gray, and they're going to find us soon.? Gray looked a bit worried. ?We'll take care of it. I'm worried about Dani. What did she say in there?? Gray held up the baggy. ?Well, I'm going to do some tests on this. The baseline tests from when she's not manifesting are normal human. But we've only gotten a manifested sample that was coated with dirt and sand from the desert before. Only thing i know that makes her different then human is that when an Angel, she's got super DNA. Her DNA strands triple in length, improved senses, healing, strength, agility.? Blaise nodded. ?You do that. You've got the lab equipment now. Keep me up to date. I'll need to turn over some of this stuff to Annie.? ?What do you think caused it?? Blaise shrugged his shoulders. ?I can only speculate.? ?What? She mentioned something about a dream, a boy. This angel and Jaymes killing her family after a car crash. Including someone named Adam. She called him 'big brother' in Sioux. A very endearing version of the word, since they have six versions of it.? ?What do we know about her background fully?? Gray shook his head. ?Not a damn thing her grandfather hasn't told us. And he didn't go after her until she was ten. She spent nine years in that orphanage, learning that 'little red skinned girls' don't get a family. I'm half thankful that it was a catholic orphanage though. The initial investigation I did was that she was left there by a man, younger looking. He'd left her with a name and some money to take care of her.? ?Catholic?? ?Yeah, nuns.? ?The bastard left her on Holy Ground for ten years.? Gray startled at Blaise's outburst. ?What?? ?You heard Ian when he spoke. Well when Suriel spoke to her. On Holy or Sacred ground, consecrated to some god or higher power, she's safe from their finding her. This is beginning to make a lot of sense. I need to go find some stuff. Get working on the lab results. Kokabel must have been the one. He put her in the Nun's care because it would be safe from this guy's tracking.? ?And after ten years, they figured it was safe. They thought she was dead and went about their own ways. That makes a lot of sense. Okay, but tell me one thing, why would she suddenly have this dream, and why right when this guy was near us. He was tracking us.? Blaise grinned. ?Because Suriel wanted her to know he was tracking us.? Blaise reached down to the manila folder that had the notes on their enemy and his forces. He opened it up to the last page. He also opened up Dani's journal to the last page she'd written on. Both were adorned with the same thing... The line drawing of the symbol of the Angel who was in charge of dealing the punishments for those of its own kind that had defied the laws of the Creator. ?Holy shit.? ?That's what I said. Let's get cracking. We may have a war to prepare for. I've got to call Lia as it is. I wonder what Kokabel wants to have a meeting with her about as it is.? Both men nod at each other and walk away. Back in the room, Dani lays there, allowing her lover to dress the rapidly healing wounds. Her mind racing with thoughts. Was it a dream? Or a memory? Who was this Adam? Who saved her from the car? And why would she remember it now? She didn't know. But others had their own ideas.
