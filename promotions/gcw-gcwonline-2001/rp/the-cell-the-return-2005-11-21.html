The Cell: The Return

21 November 2005

Date: 21 Nov 05 * Okay anyone who was in 3W MIGHT remember my 'Cell' series. I am going back to it here (and for GTT5). This one will be much longer than 3 parts, especially if I get a bit further into GTT5. This is just a short intro to it all. Plus it goes against YOU PHYL! ;o) Enjoy * He stood in his office staring out of the window. It was something he did everyday, no one knew why because all that could be seen out of that window was darkness. Yet each and everyday those cold and lifeless eyes watched nothing, saw nothing out of that window. His face was always emotionless which matched those eyes perfectly. His hands were clasped behind his back. A nice black suit covered his body, a suit that was similar to every other one he would come in wearing. LeStatt Knight turned from his spot at the window and moved into his office more. The events that went on in that office about a year ago were still fresh in his mind. He could still smell the blood that was splattered on the carpet, hear the thud of the body slamming against the floor, and most of all the anger that ran up his spine as he watched as the one man he hated most tried to take away his glory. It brought a smile to his face. He walked around his large marble desk and sat down in his chair. So much time had gone by and it was amazing to him that everything had settled down. He knew better than to completely relax. The quiet of the eternal night that plagued this world of his was quite deceiving. One day he would have to face his adversary again. The day would come and perhaps far sooner than he wanted it to. Everything about that man made LeStatt sick to his stomach. Roberto Abitua stood for everything that LeStatt did not. Roberto could be kind, caring, happy, and energetic. LeStatt saw most of this as weakness or openings for failure and failure was something LeStatt would not tolerate from himself. In LeStatt’s world, failure meant death and he wasn’t quite ready to die. Knight always thought about why he kept Roberto alive after that gunshot to his own head. He could have just allowed Roberto to melt away and never would he have been able to come back and ruin his plans. Even though he hates admitting it LeStatt thrives on that feeling of knowing that Roberto is really the only person who can stop him. Where was the fun in letting him die on his own accord? Knight was far too proud to allow that to happen. So for over a year LeStatt has taken good care of his enemy. Giving him food, shelter, clothes, health care, you name it. Even if Roberto’s shelter was the same dark and gloomy cell that held him captive for years. LeStatt took over for Roberto after his escape. A daring escape at that, an escape that took the lived of ten ordinary people but that was neither here nor there. LeStatt’s eyes traveled along the desk that he sat at. Scattered on the desk were pictures. The pictures depicted happiness, joy, and life. It was Roberto’s life. LeStatt decided against taking Roberto’s pictures down. Knight decided that the best way to destroy him was to become him. He looked at pictures of Roberto’s brother, his wife and infant child, Wylie, RaZiel, the original GCW, etc. LeStatt shook his head when thinking back at it all. In his own mind he had convinced himself that if things would have been under his own control that things never would have gone out of control the way they had. Perhaps he and Roberto could have become partners in this quest for dominance, instead of bitter rivals. Over and over again Knight told himself that if it weren’t for Roberto’s foolishness that he would not have had to lock him away in a dungeon. It was out of his hands now he continuously thought to himself. Events had been set in motion that even Knight could not stop. No one wanted to see Roberto Abitua taking charge anymore. Why the fuck would they want to? Steven Caldera certainly didn’t want Roberto back at the helm. The first run in GCW is proof of that. If LeStatt were not running things then everything he has gained would have been in someone else’s hands and Knight could not stomach another Octavian or Jhub run at the top. What about that incredible United States Title reign? Would the world have really wanted to see Luke Hanson with the title for 105 days? There is no possible way. Without LeStatt, Roberto was mediocre at best. Michael Stevens, did he want LeStatt Knight out of control? Probably not. Michael Stevens has not had anything close to this kind of challenge in his entire life and deep down he loves every moment of it. Before LeStatt Knight came into his life Michael Stevens was a immensely talented but underachieving competitor that would just as quickly fade into the background as get a cheer or two. NOW? People fear that man because he has a real purpose inside of that ring and in life. LeStatt has always brought that out of people; just ask Payne, Johnny Lexicon, Jason Dread, Jeff Wylde, and now Stevens. Maybe Mary would want Roberto back? This was more comical to him than the other two actually. LeStatt gave her the things that Roberto could not. LeStatt is confident, passionate, and determined. Not to mention that he fucks her seventy ways from Sunday, giving her pleasure that Roberto or any other man could only wish to be capable of bringing to a woman. LeStatt sat back in his chair to think more about Mary. He imagined having her finger nails digging into his back, sweat beads forming on her forehead, her beautiful skin draped on his, her heavy breathing, her perfect breasts bouncing as he fucked her. The passion of it all was enough to make LeStatt breathless himself. The best part? He knew that Roberto loved her and perhaps the only thing that kept those two connected. LeStatt for as badass and emotionless as he claimed to be, had strong feelings for Mary. The knowledge of Roberto’s love was icing on the cake. So the days and nights would continue to simply brush on. LeStatt would rule over everything that happened and bring pain to those who did him wrong. He had plenty on his plate, with NC-17 coming up, GTT5, and continuing to please Mary. It was all secondary though, knowing that soon he would battle for his very existence once again. This time he would be completely ready, this time he invited the challenge. Challenges were what drove Knight to get to his lofty status. Just like when the United States Title was placed in his grasp. LeStatt made sure to battle any and everyone who wished to step up and if not for one slip up he may STILL be U.S. Champ as well as World Champ. Sometimes the notion of being the U.S. champ and being considered better than the world champ was enough to satisfy him. That was until absolute power was given to him. That power is intoxicating and LeStatt knew that now. For the longest time titles and small trinkets to glorify himself meant absolutely nothing. It wasn’t until the power that came with being the Global Championship Wrestling World Champion was bestowed upon him that everything came into perspective. LeStatt knew that no one in GCW was prepared to handle such power as he is and that is why he would not, he COULD not lose to Michael Stevens at NC-17. If Roberto did not stand a chance, then Stevens had no chance what so ever. The world would be Knight’s soon and who could stop it? Things were going so incredibly well that he didn’t see a way that a beaten down Roberto would be able to match him blow for blow. LeStatt Knight was becoming more than just a man. LeStatt Knight was becoming legend. And Legends just don’t die.
