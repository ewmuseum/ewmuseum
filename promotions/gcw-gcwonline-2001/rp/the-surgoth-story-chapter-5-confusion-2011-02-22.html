The Surgoth Story - Chapter 5 - Confusion

22 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/22/11 I was under attack in my own apartment! I had the kitchen table tipped over and was hiding behind it because I was being shot at! The man was only on the other side of the counter, which was the livingroom. The man finally quit firing at me so I peeked up over the table with my gun drawn and I waited for him to come back up to shoot at me again. A few seconds later, his head did appear again and I took my shot. The bullet found its mark and I heard the man’s dead body hit the floor. I came out from behind my kitchen table and walked up to the spot the man had fallen, pointing my gun out in front of me the whole time. Once on the other side of the kitchen counter, I pointed my gun directly toward where the body had fallen. He was definatlty dead. I picked up my phone which was mounted on the column that stood between the kitchen and living room, right up against the counter. I called the police and after two rings someone picks up. “Diamond City Police Department, how may I help you?” asked the lady on the other end of the phone. “This is Van Jahan Surgoth and I’m with the Diamond City Police Department CSI Unit. I was just attacked in my apartment and had no choice but to shoot the man down in self defense.” “I’ll send someone right away Mr. Surgoth,” said the lady as she hung up the phone. About ten minutes later I could hear the sirens from a distance. It was around two-hundred hours and I was making a late night snack because I couldn’t sleep. That is when I had noticed that my livingroom window was open. I knew I had shut the windows before I went to bed so I decided to look around a bit. Before I could even get into the livingroom, someone started shooting at me. I tipped the kitchen table over and hid behind it until I could take my shot. I had come so close to dying at that moment. The only thing I could think of in that moment was Lily. She knew how I felt about her now but all I could think of was not taking the steps to actually get a relationship going with this beautiful girl. The sirens had stopped and a few seconds later there was a knock at my door, “Open up, this is the DCPD.” I went to the door to open it up and on the other side were two police officers. I didn’t recognize either one of them, but then again I didn’t associate with to many of the cops. The Chief was really the only one I had anything to do with, and that was because he was my boss. I lead them over to the body to show them who ahd been shooting at me. One of the police officers put a radio up to his mouth and began to speak in it, “Can I get an ambulance down here. We got a stiff one in here.” The other cop looked at me and said, “Would you like to give me your statment?” “I came into the kitchen to make myself a snack when I noticed my living room window open. Next thing I knew I’m being shot at. Thankfully I carry my gun everywhere so at least I had a way to protect myself,” I answered to the police officer. A few minutes later an ambulance could be heard making its way to the apartment. While we waited for the EMT’s to make their way up to the apartment, the police officers took a few pictures. “Does he look familiar, like someone that’s been wanted?” I asked the police officers. “Nope,” began one of the officers. “Do you have any enemies that might want you dead?” “Well I do work for the police force. It wouldn’t be a normal day unless someone is trying to kill you because you either put them or someone they know in jail once upon a time. But other than that, no, I don’t think I have any real enemies.” The EMT’s finally made it up to my apartment and threw the guy onto a stretcher. They threw a sheet over the guy and wheeled him out. I didn’t get a real good look at the guy unfortunately so there was no way I was going to be able tofind out who the man was. “We’ll keep in contact with you Mr. Surgoth,” said the officer that I had been talking to as him and his partner left my apartment. I decided that it was time to go to work early. I knew the CSI team would be here soon to look for evidence, but I was going to find it myself before they got here. I walked over to the spot where the man had been laying dead. There were a few drops of blood on the floor and the counter top. I found me a cotton swab and a little baggy in one of my kitchen drawers and dipped the swab into the blood. I then placed it into the baggy, walked to my room and placed it on my bed while I got dressed. I dressed in my usual blue jeans and DCPD t-shirt. I placed the baggy into my pocket and that was when I heard a knock on the door. I returned to my kitchen and opened my apartment door. On the other side were three men, all of which I recognized from work. Two of them were about as tall as I was and the other, she was only about five foot, four inches tall. Normally I find short females attractive, but not her. She might as well have been a man herself. “Good to see that you’re alright Van,” said the tallest of the three. “I almost wasn’t,” I began. “I’m surprised to see that it is you here Halgar. I had heard you got fired a couple weeks back.” “No, I had a family emergency that couldn’t be helped. Unfortunately I won’t be having a vacation for quite some time though.” “Well, it’s good to see you back. I’ll let you guys get to work around here, I think I’m going to head into the office pretty early today.” “I’d defiantly say it’s early,” replies Halgar. “It’s only three in the morning!” “I need to do more work on that Grom case. I’m making sure to put him back behind bars. That murderer don’t deserve to be walking the streets free. After what I found though, I don’t think he will be getting out of jail any time soon.” “Yeah, why did he ever get out of jail?” “All of a sudden they are saying there wasn’t enough evidence to keep him in jail,” I answered. “Truthfully I think some of the evidence went missing. Someone is pulling some strings behind the scenes for this guy. Whoever it is will be sure to share a cell with Grom once I find out who it is.” I left my apartment and headed for the first floor of the apartment building. I got into my car and headed for the office building where I worked. It was then that it started to bother me. My hand began to itch something terrible. On my right hand was a mark. It had been there since my ‘Awakening’ and I still had no clue where it had come from. It was in the shape of a trident, the spear that the God, Poseidon, once held. The next think I knew it began to glow red, as if it was burning, and the pain was ubearable. The last thing I saw was a lamp post, then, nothing. * * * White pillars were to the left and right of me and I found myself kneeled on a red carpet. It front of me was a large, muscular man who held a spear with three points on the end. It looked like the trident. The man holding it had a stubble like brown beared and a mustache to match. On his head was very short brown hair, that was no longer than the hair on his face. The only thing that covered his body was a white robe that covered him from the waist, down. “Do you promise to hold up all laws of our land and to use the power that is to be given to you for the purpose of good? The man asked me. “I give you my word,” I said as I bowed my head. I had no control over myself.What I really wanted to do was ask, what are you talking about? What am I swearing to? “Then, with the power of the trident, I mark you as one of my loyal subjects. Hold up your right hand with the back of it facing toward me.” I did what the man had asked without hesitation, though I tried to, but I had absolutly no control. The man had then raised his trident, pointed it at me, and from the tip came a bright white light. It hit my right hand and the next think I knew I was screaming. It burnt terribly! When would it end!? I couldn’t take it much longer! Just when I thought it couldn’t get much worse, I blacked out. * * * “AAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!” I sat straight up in bed. I wasn’t quite sure where I was at first but I knew that I was in a bad. Once I collected myself, I began to look around and realized I was in a hospital room. “How did I get here?” I asked myself out loud. Juast as I had said that, ‘she’ walked into the room. “You were in a car accident,” said the beautiful Lily as she approached my bed and held my hand. “We didn’t think you would ever come to.” “What do you mean you didn’t think I would every come to? How long have I been here?” “A couple of days. The whole time you seemed to be having a nightmare. You would scream at the top of your lungs and hold onto your right hand. At times you would open your eyes while this went on but you didn’t respond to anything. The Chief was actually one of the first ones here to see you. When he left he wasn’t very happy.” “Where’s my clothes?” I asked her. “They are in one of the closets, why?” “Bring me my pants. There is something in them that is very important.” Lily left go of my hand and walked over to one of theclosets in the room. She pulled out my jeans and brought them over to me. I reached into the pocket I thought I had put the plastic baggy in with the cottom swab. It wasn’t there. I began looking thought all of the pockets in my pants, but it was no where to be found. I was pissed! I threw the jeans across the room. “Damn it!” I yelled loudly. “It’s gone! The damn thing is gone!” “What is?” Lily asked me. “I think I know why the Chief left here pissed off! The son-of-a-bitch went through my damn clothes!” “What are you talking about?” “I was attacked in my apartment! I killed the bastard that was trying to kill me and before Forensics could get there, I took a sample of blood that was laying on my counter top! I was going to analyze it myself and see who the guy was! Well now the damn thing is gone!” “You’re damn right it’s gone!” yelled the Chief as he walked into my hospital room. “What the hell were you thinking!?” “I think I have a right to find out who the sick prick was that tried to give me a nice dose of lead!” I yelled back at the Chief. Anyone that knows me enough already knows I haven’t got a problem yelling right back at an authority figure. I think that might be one of the reasons why the Cheif keeps me around. I’m the only one in the whole office that has the balls to actually stick up to him. “And you kniow as well as I do that you will find out as soon as we do! For your information we did find out who the culprit is but right now I can’t disclose that to you. Not until we get a bit more information on the whole incident. What the results came back with made absolutely no sense what so ever.” “What do you mean?” I asked the Chief. “Just keep on the look out Van, I have a feeling that your life might still be in danger.” “Oh that’s just great! My life might still be in danger but you aren’t going to give me any information? What the hell is the matter with you!?” “Sorry Van, but I can’t tell you right now,” said the Chief as he left my room. I went to get out of my bed to chase the Chief down but Lily placed her hands on my shoulders. “What the hell are you doing?” I asked her. “You need to relax and get some sleep,” she began. “I understand you’re upset, and you have every right to. I’ll tell you what. If you be a good boy and stay in bed, I’ll try and do a little investigation of my own to help you out. How does that sound?” “But how...” that was all I could muster before she placed a finger on my lips. “All you need to worry about right now is yourself, I’ll take care of the rest,” she finished as her face got close to mine, she removed her finger and kissed me. I couldn’t believe it. For so long it seems like, I have loved this woman and now she was kissing me. It was to bad it was in a hospital bed and not in my own. She would have to wait on doing her investigation. She stood up from the bed and left the room. I tried to speak but I just didn’t have the words. It was seldom that I was left speechless, but with her, it is a completely different story. It wasn’t long before I feel back to sleep. This time my dreams weren’t filled with riddles. This time it was full of thoughts of Lily. I finally got the one thing I had been waiting for. * * * I awoke and found that it was dark in my room. The only light was what came in from the hallway outside my hospital room. I looked out my window and I could see rain drops falling onto it. I wasn’t sure what time it was but it had to be at least be after twenty-two hundred hours. Lightening also flashed from time to time and the thunder rolled gently, as if trying to lay a blanket of comfort over me. To the left side of me, where my door was, there was a click. I looked over and seen that my door was now closed. Lightening flashed and I saw a figure in the room with me. “Who are you?” I asked the figure. “This time you won’t be so lucky,” said the figure. The next thing I knew, someone was on top of me. The lightening flashed and I seen that it was the figure that was in the room with me. I also seen a quick flash that came from something else other than the lightening. The lightening flashed again only a second later and I saw what the lightening was flashing off of. It was a buck knife. I grabbed a hold of the figures arms and tried to keep him from bringing the knife down on me. I soon kicked him off me and I heard him hit the floor with a loud thud. The lightening flashed again and I used that opportunity to see where the figure had fell. I jumped on top of the figure and began to throw left and rights into the guys face. The next thing I knew, my room door swung open and the light came on. A nurse began screaming for an orderly. Before the orderly could arrive and pull me off, I had already beaten the guys face in so bad that he bled to death right there on the hospital floor. * * * About a half hour later the police showed up and the Chief was right behind them. Quickly, he walked up to me and said, “Are you alright Van?” “I’m fine Chief,” I answered. “Just another attack on me is all! How come so many people are trying to kill me is what I would like to know!?” The Chief turned around to look at the dead man on the hospital room floor. A shocked and horrified look came across the Chief’s face. I was half afraid to ask him why he looked so terrified. A few seconds later I didn’t have to ask the Chief what the problem was, he came right out and told me. “This doesn’t make any sense Van,” began the Chief in a terrified voice. “I just came from the mortuary looking at the guy who had tried to kill you before. This guy laying here on the floor... He looks identical to the other guy!” “Are you saying they are twins?” I asked the Chief. “I’m not real sure what I’m saying. All I know is we need to put you under protective custody. You aren’t going anywhere without some sort of escort.” Great, I thought to myself, I guess they are going to find out what I do n my leisure time. They were about to find out all about Global Championship Wrestling. The only thing I feared is how they would react to me actually leaving Diamond City and going to the United States. I guess I would be finding out soon enough.
