Disappointing Decisions

27 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/11 The front door bursts open, nearly shattering the glass. The students all freeze in their training and heads snap toward the entrance. Noise subsides in an instant, leaving only the sighing of the cold Seattle wind to be heard. Markus stands just inside the door as it slowly swings closed behind him. He drops the bag from his shoulder and it thuds to the ground, an exclamation to the silent anticipation in the room. “I’m gonna need all of you,” Markus’ outstretched hand waves across the room, “to get out of here. Now.” Markus calmly walks into his gym and stops in front of one of his students. Jeremy, the only one who Markus honestly believes has the combination of ability and determination to actually make it on the business of professional wrestling. “Except you,” the icy stare and monotone voice freeze Jeremy where he stands. Within moments, every student in Markus’ facility has changed and hurry out of the door. He hasn’t budged. He stands with a gaze leveled on Jeremy, who’s feet have remained just as frozen while students file past the two. One chances a look back and smiles at Jeremy. He can’t tell if it’s to try and comfort him, or if it’s because the larger man is simply happy to not be in Jeremy’s position. He doesn’t blame them for feeling that way. Especially since they can’t see past the façade Markus normally places squarely in front of himself. Markus slowly starts to smile. “Saw your match.” Markus’ smiles turns into a full fledged shit eating grin. “You have any idea how good it felt to get back in there?” Markus slaps Jeremy on the back with a friendly hand. “Yeah? Even those chair shots?” Markus pulls back in feigned offense. “Well, I forgot all about it until you decided that some salt needed to be rubbed into those wounds.” “Hey, gotta keep you grounded right man?” Jeremy lets slide out as he casually walks to the ring. “Grounded?” Markus grunts as he plops himself onto the apron. “What the hell would I need grounded for? I beat Jay Terror in such a way that he had no choice but to grab a chair to try and stop the punishment he was enduring!” Leaning over the top rope, Jeremy shakes his head with a chuckle. “You make it so hard to figure out whether or not you actually believe half the shit you say. So what are you doing until the next WorldWide? You got William Curr this time.” “Who?” “William Curr.” “Who?” Markus looks over his shoulder in genuine bewilderment, eyebrows arching as if to find an answer in his hairline. “Curr. William. GCW employee and professional wrestler. Won a match on the same WorldWide you just competed at. Took some hits of acid on accident and God knows what he did next. You really don’t know who he is?” Jeremy’s volume elevates to convey his mixture of disbelief and disappointment. “Nope. I don’t really watch much wrestling these days. It’s always the same thing. Hulk Hogan version 3.0 gets beat down, makes a comeback with four moves that are somehow unblockable, irreversible, and completely devastating. Somehow nobody ever learns how to counter said moves, and the Hulkster of the new generation wins more titles than Ric Flair ever did in half the time.” “Uh…. What are you talking about? Vivica’s not a man.” Markus looks at Jeremy, shrugs his shoulders. “Could’ve fooled me,” he adds with a chuckle. “Jesus Christ man, can you take anything seriously?” Jeremy’s frustration edges through the cracks as Markus’ wall of nonchalant begins to suffer the assault of seriousness it is receiving. “Curr has gone two and two in GCW. The dude has only lost to Triumph Frost and Brad Cruz. He beat Cameron Garret and Zip Gordon. You shouldn’t be taking him so lightly.” “Jerbear,” Markus hops off the apron and turns to face his friend. “You think I’m even going to this event? I’m sure by now I’ve got tons of job offers on the answering machine. I’ll bet my twitter has blown up and I have no doubt that my Facebook has a SHIT. TON. Of new friend requests. I BEAT JAY TERROR!” Markus literally THROWS his hands up in some sort of celebration. Face beaming with enough joy to light a small Alaskan village, and genuinely filled with ignorant bliss. “You think some four match punk is going to beat me? I’m not even showing up! I’m sure somebody out there wants to hire Markus Stone, and I’m damn well sure they’re going to pay me a sum that Caldera doesn’t want to match.” “You know that GCW is one of the wealthiest companies in the world?” Jeremy asks rhetorically. That doesn’t stop Markus from answering, “Yes.” Jeremy shakes his head, the burden of trying to get through to his friend almost too much. “Jeremy, you ever wonder why they’re so damn wealthy?” Pause for dramatic effect. Not too long of a pause though, Markus refuses to make the same mistake Jeremy did. This rhetorical question will NOT be answered by some wise ass trying to get the last word in. “Caldera is a cheap ass! I know, for a fact, that I can get a better contract if I wanted. They only thing GCW has going for me right now is exposure baby. And I plan on exposing myself in somebody else’s front lawn this week.” “You’ll probably get arrested.” “When has that ever stopped me?” Jeremy doesn’t bother answering. One, because it really hasn’t ever stopped him. And two, even if it had Markus would simply find some other way to expose himself in front of millions of families the world over. “You know, this sounds like an oddly sexual conversation. Almost like I’m some kind of child molester.” Jeremy shakes his head with a laugh, “At least you have one redeeming quality. You’ve never slept with a teenager.” Markus’ eyes open just a fraction wider, and he stops fidgeting. Standing statue still he tries to appear extremely innocent. He fails. “Man I don’t even wanna know,” Jeremy sighs as he turns around in the ring. He paces the mat, glancing down at some of the stained blood, and the thinning material in the center where the fabric has taken the strain of dozens of bodies wreaking all manor of havoc over it. “So how’s this gonna work man? You gonna fly to a match, then fly back just to kick everybody out of the gym?” Markus meets Jeremy’s eye as he turns to face his teacher. “Gym’s closed dude.” The sledgehammer falls on the conversation, driving a wedge right through the happy mood and striking a geyser of ill temper. “WHAT!?” Jeremy springs from the ring and plants his face inches from Markus’ before the veteran can begin to brace himself. “Look man, I don’t have time to worry about all these guys and their silly ass dreams. I gotta worry about me right now, and I gotta get back on top of the world,” Markus turns to walk away from Jeremy as he talks. He hopes that creating space will ease the tension. He’s wrong. “You piece of shit. You are such a… piece of shit!” Jeremy’s anger blocks his mind from working out a better insult than repetition. “You sit there on your fucking pedestal looking down on everybody around you hoping that you can somehow make yourself better just by imagining that it’s true!” “Jerbear, I imagine it’s true because it is. I can’t sit around here and foster all their dreams. The only worth a fuck student I have is you, and you don’t even want to make a serious go of this. You don’t want to leave your wife here at home while you fly from on arena to the next for over half of the year. Don’t fucking talk to me about a pedestal when you try to pretend that this is some kind of fucking hobby you can just do on your spare time you arrogant little shit.” Jeremy’s hand clamps on Markus’ shoulder. Markus has just enough time to turn his head toward the hand when his body is jerked one hundred and eighty degrees. The world turns bright for a split second, followed by a thump. By the time the stars clear from his head Markus is laying on the ground, an angry red face looking down at him. “You piece of shit! I should’ve listened to Dad about you. I should’ve listened to everybody about you. All you ever do is burn all these bridges, and spend enough time to make some half hearted apology hoping somebody else will pick up the pieces for you. You did it Alicia, with Chris and Tessa, Mom and Dad, and now me. Me? ME!? I’m your fucking brother you piece of shit, and you want to sit on your fucking throne a rain shit down on MY dream?” Markus finally sits up as Jeremy takes a step back. “You done now?” Jeremy glares at his brother. Reason seems to enter his mind as he relaxes his tense body. “I can’t fucking believe you man. There’s no getting through to you, is there? You are the most selfish man I’ve ever met. And if you weren’t so goddamned self-destructive you might actually get somewhere on day.” Jeremy shakes his head in disappointment, resigning to the fact that he really can’t say anything else in this situation. Markus stands to his feet and watches as his younger brother walks out of the gym. Normally Markus would feel a stab of regret, and maybe even a bit of remorse. But right now… He’s got an answering machine to check. “He’ll be back in a few days anyway.” Markus walks past the ring, and down the hallway in the back of the gym. Past the storage locker, but not anywhere near the locker room, he enters his office. God knows you don’t want to walk past a wrestling gym’s locker room every single day. That shit will rot your fucking nosehairs. Markus looks at his answering machine and sees a flashing glimmer of hope. New message, it says. Flashing: hope, hope, hope. “FIRST UNHEARD MESSAGE:” A woman’s voice take’s over. “Mr. Stone, this is WWA talent relations returning your phone call. I’m sorry to say that we aren’t currently interested in hiring your services-“ “MESSAGE DELETED. NEXT UNHEARD MESSAGE:” Another woman’s voice. “Mr. Stone, I regret to inform you-“ “MESSAGE DELETED. NEXT UNHEARD MESSAGE:” This time a man’s voice follows. The southern drawl makes it seems as if he is speaking through a mouthful a tobacco spit. “Markus Stone! We’d be damn glad happy to have you son! You just come on down here to the SWA offices on Thursday! We’ll figure out how to get Chris Storm to return my calls, I’ve got big plans for the tag team division down here that could use Banned & Exil-“ “MESSAGE DELETED. NO MESSAGES.” “FUUUUCK!!!!” the profane outburst is exclamated by the answering machine shattering against the wall. Looks like Markus is going to have to wrestle William Curr after all.
