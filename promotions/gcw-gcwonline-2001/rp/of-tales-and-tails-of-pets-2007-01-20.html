Of tales and tails of pets

20 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/20/07 ~The Order's Main US Facility~ ~Outside Dallas/Ft. Worth Texas~ Aine ?Annie? Leanan walked into the room with a disinterested look on her face. She wasn't sure what was going on, but she knew it was enough to wake her up at only eight in the morning. So it was important, but it would have her in a bad mood until she had either a couple pots of coffee or some strong Irish stout. She surveyed everything. It was all researchers, wearing their 'uniforms' of lab coats. They were her people, but they were also people from the European side of the Order that were here to observe and note things for Lord Ishram in this upcoming war. One of them was a mousy woman, she looked like a weaker and meeker version of Alex with dirty blonde hair. The other was a guy who looked not unlike Dan Akroyd's character, Egon, from Ghost Busters. ?Captain Leanan, how good to see you.? He rose to his feet and extended his hand. Annie took it and shook it readily. The other girl didn't get up, which meant she was a worker bee. This guy was going to take credit for what she'd found, but she was here to explain things, since she had actually found it and Egon here didn't know shit. He was just a boss who sat around observing until something came up. ?Nice to see you too. I understa'nd tha' ye be 'avin' somethin' o' interest fer me?? Annie didn't try to hide her accent. She didn't like bosses like this. And she could read it in his every move. As soon as she said something, he looked right at the small woman. He'd given himself away. Annie knew who would get the commendation in her book if it was something of she needed to know. ?Yes, if you don't mind I'm going to let Cindy here take the presentation of what we found while going through the battle recordings from the assault on the Cleveland Den and taking of Geoffri LaBatte.? Annie sat down. ?Please, go riogh' on a'ead.? The girl, Cindy, got up and walked over to the large flat screen that adorned the wall. She pulled up a remote control and the screen came to life. ?I'm not one for doing big presentations, Captain Leanan. So I figure straight and to the point would be best.? ?I would'na agree more, young lady. Especially when it's well known I'm a nigh' owl, an this is very early in th' morn fer me. The sooner it's done the better.? ?Very well, M'lady? Hmm, the proper title according to the courts for her. This girl was good. If she found something really good, she might even be asked to move to Annie's team. ?Yes, please continue.? The screen comes up to full resolution with a montage of pictures that Annie recognizes. A bipedal wolf, white of fur with green eyes. The garou or werewolf form of Captain Blaise McClancy, leader of the KnightMare Brigade's militant side and soon to be crown prince of Tir Tairngire come next month when Oberon proclaimed Maeb Fey McClancy as queen of the ?Land of Promise? and proclaim the new world a new haven for those Faery that come here. ?As you know, M'lady, this is Captain McClancy. You're quite familiar with him and his garou form being one of two garou that work for the Order, and both in the KnightMare Brigade unit. Mr. McClancy's wolfen form is considered to be a throw back to a lost tribe of werewolves known as the White Howlers that were prevalent in the northern UK isles centuries ago. These white furred garou became almost extinct because of the hunts that went for them after about 80% of them became infected from the entity they call the Wyrmm and became the Black Spiral Dancers, evil creatures that share traits with garou and vampires.? Annie nodded. ?Yes, Blaise's grandfather is a Utkena garou, of the Native American blood from over 'ere. It's thought tha' Blaise's Celtic side allowed the traits o' th' old kind t' come through.? Cindy nodded back at her. ?Yes. Well, I'm going to bring your attention fully to this picture over here.? She pointed to the one on the far right. ?This one in particular is from his fiance's battle recorder from that night when he showed up to help her take on the two heads of the Giovanni clan in Cleveland. You'll notice the same white furred creature we've become used to.? Annie nods again. Cindy changes the screen. It becomes something of a moving scene. Annie knew this footage, it was from when Blaise was dealing with a group of men, three vampires or ghouls who had stopped them. While Ukyo continued on, he fought with them. One of them had taken his battle recorder and taunted him with it. ?Yes, I know this scene. This is where he was fightin' th' three lesser vampires. 'E lost 's battle recorder in it.? ?Yes, he did. While I know we've been through this, it was just last night that I found something in it. Something of great interest to us.? The fight goes on the screen until it gets to the point where Blaise broke free and started to take the three men on . The man with the battle recorder is taken down by the unseen McClancy as he had turned to run away... And as he fell the recorder fell off of his ear and laid there, staring the vampire in the bloody and rended face, his sunglasses askew. She stopped the scene there. ?And right here is where we have our anomaly.? Annie tries to find it on the screen, but her eyes don't pick it up. And then the woman puts the cursor on top of the man's shades and then zooms in. Annie can see a black blob form on the screen. ?It's not pretty right here. So let me enhance it digitally.? Cindy smiles, getting ready for her big revelation to the head of the Order's research department. Annie's jaw drops when the enhanced image takes shape. The reflection in the man's glasses are that of a large wolf-like creature. It's muzzle is long and sharp, and full of teeth. A pair of red eyes stare down at the man. The key thing here though, is the fur. Jet black, almost oily in appearance. ?Wha' is this?? Cindy turned to the woman and shrugged her shoulders. ?We don't know. It doesn't even look like any garou we know. It would come close to a Black Spiral Dancer, but it doesn't have the webbed wings under the arms or the mutated appearance. It's a large black dog, with huge teeth and red eyes. We don't think it's coming from his Garou side at all.? ?Hmm, tha' could na' be. We' only 'ave one legend like tha', and Blaise's mantle is tha' o' a Seelie Prince. 'E's na' an Unseelie like me. There would be only noble things 'e would become. Tha' looks like vengence walkin'. Bu' ye 'ave peaked me interest and shown me somethin' tha' I'll need t' lok int' more. Do ye 'ave 'ard copies o' this?? Cindy handed Annie the large folder of papers she had brought with her with a wide smile on her face. ?Thank ye.? ?Your welcome, M'Lady. It was a pleasure. I don't know what drew me to it while going through the tapes one more time. But it jumped out at me all of a sudden.? Hmm, she could be a sensitive too. ?Thank ye, again. I'll get riogh' on this after a pot o' coffee. Looks like I need t' go talk to Queen Maeb an' find ou' a few things.? ~Columbus, Ohio~ ~Courtyard by Marriot Hotel~ Ukyo looked down at the papers, reading them again as she sat at the stiff table. Another night in a hotel and another night thinking things through. A noise caught her attention and she looked over at the two small beds that the hotel had brought in for the now four year old twin girls that she was soon to be the adopted mother of when she and Blaise marry near the end of April in Japan. She sighs and then looks up at the door to the balcony where Blaise is standing in just his workout pants. His hair is growing back out, and he has it pulled back into a small, tight pony tail. She watches as he leans against the railing and does a quick set of incline push ups. Seeing his muscles on his back cord up like that really gets her thinking other things, instead of something that has turned into business. She could see the thoughts going through his head though. He wasn't doing well after the way the match with La Sombra had ended. He knew that they had won another battle. In fact, for the most part the KnightMare Brigade had won every battle at NC-17. Virgil has shown Vivica the door the way the KB would toss someone on their ass, by beating them. Blaise won. Fiery Passion kept the tag team titles with the KB. Dani took the US Title. And Lia was now the GCW World Champion. All but the Hardcore title was theirs to control again. And if other indications were right, the third attempt by the Ascension to get things going, was stomped into submission again. Only Tessa Windsor remains, and there was no telling as to just what condition she was in. She sighed again and got up from her seat, wearing only one of his t-shirts as far as anyone looking at her could tell, she stepped out on the balcony behind him and wrapped a pair of arms around his waist. He jumped, almost out of his skin, and then stopped when he realized who it was. ?Did I scare you?? ?No, startled yes. I thought you had gone to bed or something.? ?No, mulling over the contract still.? ?Getting cold feet?? She smiled as she put her chin down onto his shoulder. ?No. Just want to make sure it's all right and there isn't going to be any problems. I've also had lawyers looking at it, to make sure that no one can say anything about me doing it when I go to get joint custody with you of Kaylee and Kylie.? Blaise nodded. He knew that the slightest stuff could get Becca's family again trying to get his daughters. Becca was locked up in Clark County, Nevada jail. So she was out of the picture, but she wasn't the only one. Her family had begun to try to play the legal cards, but so far they'd been held off rather well. ?You look like you're thinking about things? You are booked this week, you going to be good to go after that match with Sombra? Youv'e got Jayce Bradley this week. If I didn't know better, I think they're after making you double up to try to get that belt off of you.? He nodded. ?Probably, but I'll be good. I'm tougher then almost anyone else on that damn roster. I could probably give Youngblood a run for his money right now. But I'm happy where I'm at too. Bradley doens't scare me. He came in what? Last, damn near last in that cluster fuck match? Once again he fell to Dani, and she's got the belt he was after. If I'm worried about Bradley, then it's time for me to get out of the game, know what I mean?? She kissed him on the neck. ?I know. What's got you worried then?? ?Things.? She pulled him in a bit tighter. ?It's the deal with Lia and Ian isn't it?? She had him, he could only nod in agreement. ?You're not liking part of this whole thing?? ?No, the open assault that we took part in. I think it was a trap. He tried to lure someone out.? ?Who would he need to lure out?? Blaise turned around, so he could face her, leaning against the railing. He reached up and moved some of her lavendar colored hair out of the way of her face. ?Ian has many enemies. But he's taken care of most of them. There is one who has so far eluded him. One that he's not been able to take care of because of his position.? ?Who is this?? He sighed and exhaled deeply right after. ?The Hand of Longinus.? ?A paragon's hand? Is that smart?? ?I doubt it. But this is a man whom Ian owes a lot to. This guy destroyed the PWW from within, and when it comes down to it, has tried to kill Ian a few times. Ian once destoyed the man's knee, and now he walks with a cane.? ?Who is he?? ?His name is Joseph Sinclair. He's the bastard half brother of Ian Childress.? Ukyo's grip loosened around him and she almost took a step back. ?Ian's brother? He's a paragon's hand?? ?He's with the church, like his father. He became the hand or hound of Longinus out of chance and the fact that he knows Ian better then anyone but Fey who is alive. I once mentioned to Lia that Ian doens't do anything without asking for something in return, and she said she knew that. And that she'd been told that when she repays him, it'll be dealing with her talents and ths kills he's honed.? Ukyo brought a hand up to her mouth to cover it. ?Yes, Lia is a trained supernatural assassin. She's the only one I think he knows who would have a chance at finally ending that little issue in Ian's life.? ?What would be the ramifications though?? ?A war like no other. Longinus would mobilize the church against us if he could. Otherwise we'd end up looking out for the likes of the Society of Leopold all over the fucking place. That's what I worry about, not people like Jayce Bradley. Hell, as it is it looks like Longinus has a couple groups of mages and at least one vampire clan working with him. It's getting pretty dangerous.? ?When do you think he'll send her after him?? ?No telling. I could see advantages to doing it both before and after Fey's coronation. I guess it's depending on what needs to happen. I just don't feel too good about it. Ian's got me worried with this stuff with Dani and that.? She crooked her head sideways. ?What?? ?Ukyo, dearest. One of the symbols that Dani keeps drawing, is one that I've seen before. They just don't know it.? ?What's that?? ?Suriel.? ?Suriel?? ?The angel who's spirit hides within Ian, keeping it alive. Dani and Josh mentioned they destroyed a skeleton of a seraph. That skeleton was Suriel's, I'm sure of it. I think I've found what's going on. Especially with what Josh had given me. But I've got no proof yet.? ?So what do you think is going on?? ?I think Ian's made something similar to a deal with a devil. But Suriel is instead a disembodied former favored of heaven. I think they're going to try to use Dani to take him over, and in return for it, Ian is going to become something else.? ?Something else, like what?? ?Ian's taking actions to take on two 'Hands?, take that for what it's worth.? ?What? Who?? ?Lia for his left hand. The hand of deceit and trickery. She's a trained assassin and thief.? She nodded at him. ?And for his right hand, the hand of a war... me.? ?You have agreed to this?? ?No, but my position is where I have to follow orders. I'm bound to him and my queen. When it comes down to it, I think we're in the middle of seeing Ishram purposely making another paragon in his grandson. Skewing the sides. He and Cain are in a truce. The lady and Longinus are close. One more kinda tosses the power scale over.? She shook her head. ?This may be getting over our heads right now. Let's try to think about other things. Tell me, my lover and future husband. Do you have any reservations about that contract sitting on the table in there?? He looked at her and smiled. ?Only one.? ?Oh? And that is?? ?Yes, you get to be called a pet and I'm the one who turns into a fucking dog.? She busted out with a chuckle at that. ?You have no issues with me doing this? None at all?? ?Hey, I've supported your little modeling endeavor with Patti and Dani. If you want to take it a step further, I support you fully. Just as long as people remember who's attached to that rock on your finger and who gets to have you in his bed at night.? She leaned forward and put her head on his chest. ?I just wish people like Kyo would understand things about this all. I've given myself to you, and thus I'm infected. If you ever left me, I'd go crazy.? ?Literally.? ?What?? ?Heh, remember how Patti was without Sean around?? ?Oh, yeah. I remember that, super bitch from hell time. I'm glad you have never done that to me then. I know that even when you go away to a meeting I feel it in my gut that you're not there.? ?Believe me, while your kind may not have the 'infection' like mine own, I've found that I need you just as much. You're the only one I know of that can control the other side. Are you looking forward to a few months from now?? She smiled and appeared to be listening to his heart though his bare chest. ?Yes.? ?Good. I'm bored out here. Time to do some practice.? ?Practice for what?? The next sound she made was a squeak as he reached down and pulled her up to wrap her legs around his waist as he held her by the now bare bottom with a baby blue thong showing as the t-shirt bunched up. ?What do you think you're doing, good knight?? He smiled. ?I think I'm taking the next Penthouse Pet to bed.? She giggled a bit as he walked into the hotel room. She wrapped her arms around his neck as he carried her over and kicked the door open to the bedroom area. As soon as he had her next to the bed, he let go and let her fall backwards down onto it She giggled as he started to undo the tie at his waist for his workout pants. ... ~Back in Dallas area~ Annie came up to Fey's room and knocked on the door. She knew the guards were near, but didn't bother to even look for them. The door opened and she pushed her way in past the serving girl. She saw Fey sitting there. ?Annie, can I be 'elpin' ye to'day?? ?Yes, Yer Highness, ye can. It's abou' yer cousin.? ?What is it?? Annie put the folder down in front of her. ?I need t' know everythin' ye do abou' Prince Turenn's mantle.? Fey opened the folder and pulled out the two pictures. ?Who's this black one?? Annie sat down next to her. ?That's the thing. It be 'im. We got tha' by chance. It's the reflection from the vampire tha' stole 'is battle recorder durin' the assault on th' Den. When Blaise went feral on those three, 'e was na' the Garou we know.? Fey shook her head, trying to take it in. ?The thing is, we don'na know much abou' 'im. Blaise is a unique person, an' a unique McClancy. And no one 'as ever 'eld the mantle o' Turenn in milenia. They didn't even know 'e was available, 'er who 'e was fer the most part. The only thing we really know is that 'e and Lugh are mortal enemies on a political level.? ?We don'na 'ave anythin' else?? ?No.? Annie couldn't believe it. ?With yer permission, yer highness. 'E is the Crown Prince once the coronation is done. Would ye let me look int' this?? Fey looked at the picuters. ?Yes. Please do.?
