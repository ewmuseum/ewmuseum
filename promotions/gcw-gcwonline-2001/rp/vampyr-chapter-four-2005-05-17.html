Vampyr: Chapter Four

17 May 2005

Date: 17 May 05 Roberto pushed the door open that would lead him to his small apartment. Although there were no lights on inside the shine of the moon put a light glow onto the room. The room was simple; having a dirty and noisy wooden floor, off white walls and beat up wooden doors leading to the bathroom and a closet. A dusty mattress laid on the floor which looked to be decades old. Next to the mattress stood a wooden dresser with a large mirror and a small lamp resting on top of it. There’s a window on the far side of the room, away from any of the decorations that filled the place up. Roberto’s trench coat slides off his back, falling to the floor near his mattress showing off the arsenal of weapons that hung from a black utility belt. His fingers find the buckle and snap it off allowing the belt to hit the hard wood floor, joining the trench coat. Roberto’s body flops down on the mattress bringing up a thin cloud of dust, he hardly acknowledges this though and simply begins to untie his boots. After a few moments of working at the laces the boots easily come off. Roberto was drained, emotionally and physically as he usually was after a night such as this. Sleep was what this body craved most at this point in time but Roberto had a ritual that he could not do without. He forced his body back off from his place on the mattress, stretching his muscles out. Reaching back with his right hand he pulled the rubber band from his hair, letting his dirty black hair to fall about his shoulders. After taking a deep breath in, Roberto slowly walked to the window and sat down on the ledge, letting his icy blue eyes stare out at the horizon. As usual his face was cold, emotionless almost as if he were a statue or a gargoyle. Pulling his right knee up towards his chest Roberto took in the sight that was unfolding before him. His fingers clasped together, resting on that knee that was pulled tightly to his chest. Since he was thirteen years old Roberto would sit at his window sill and stare out into the darkness knowing what was out there lurking, and at the same time thinking about the night that his life was altered forever. He hasn’t slept too often since that night, and never at night when a person is supposed to do so. Fear keeps this man awake at night, a fear that he may not awaken the same as he was when he fell into his slumber. To most people the every thought of life and death could not be contemplated. Very few people ever actually are forced to look death in the eyes, and even fewer do so and live to tell about it. Roberto had seen death too much for his liking though, he understands what life and death are all about. Life is something that humanity has taken for granted for years, never believing that “it can never happen to me”. Roberto has learned that it doesn’t matter if you’re old, young, black, white, Mexican, Chinese, handicap, gay, straight or any other assortment of person ? if your time is up then death will come find you. After everything that has happened in Roberto’s lifetime some may have crumbled, as a matter of fact almost anyone would have. Events in one’s life happen which rip the meaning of life right from your body. That’s when you have a choice to either give in and die or fight through the pain and flourish. Roberto has fought for the life that lives inside of him. He feels that he owes his family that much, the family that was taken from him seven years ago. Roberto’s eyes begin to sparkle as the tip of the sun begins to peek up from below the horizon. The reds, oranges, and yellows from the new day begin to spread across the once black sky. It was truly a beautiful sight and inside Roberto smiled but outwardly his face remained still. His eyes alone told the excitement that was usually kept bottled up inside of him. Roberto began staring out and watching the dawn when he was going on twelve years old. After he lost his family and began his transformation into the man he is today, the sunrise was something special to him. Not only was it a spectacular sight to behold, not only was it the only time he felt 100% safe ? it was also the one thing that reminded him what being human was all about. A Vampire he was not, but there was something inside of Roberto’s body that just did not seem completely human. Perhaps after seeing death so often has pushed this young man to the limits. With each and every creature he slays he expects that hole in his heart to be filled, and while small bits of satisfaction come from it, it’s merely a hollow feeling. As each victim of his burns up, falling into a pile of ash and bone, a part of that humanity seems to burn up with it. Roberto’s lust for their lives is only matched by their lust for blood, which sometimes makes him wonder if he really is any better than they are? Vengeance can be a powerful tool in a man’s life but if not harnessed in the correct fashion it can also be his downfall. Roberto understands this but every time his bullets rip into one of their bodies or every time his blades gash their flesh, anger mounts inside of that body of his. Roberto not only kills for his own personal vendetta but for the family that he hoped now rested in heaven. They were caught up in an evil being’s sick game and unfortunately they could never have been prepared for what happened. Roberto has felt responsible for their deaths every since it happened, so much so that they invade his every dream ? turning them into intolerable nightmares. Vividly he remembered the blood shed, the screams, and the loneliness. After seven years these things still eat away at his soul. Every time those heavy eyelids close the memory shows itself like some kind of cruel and sadistic movie that is played over and over again. He tries to get away from it, he tries to forget it but it is embedded into the very essence of his being. Like it or not Roberto knows that it was that night which defined him forever. On that night the blood that flowed from those bodies might as well have been his own. In that mind of his, Roberto still questioned how he pulled off what he did that night. How did he remain alive and how did he kill with so little remorse? It was almost as if that side of him was just laying back and waiting, waiting for something or someone to trigger it off. Roberto was like a loaded pistol in the hands of an insecure youth. When things became too much or when the right vision or scenario was placed before him, the pistol came out, the safety was turned off and the trigger was pulled. NOVEMBER 10th 1998 Roberto Abitua was the birthday boy on this day just turning the ripe age of thirteen. It was at this stage in his life that he felt he could take on the world as every young teenage boy does. He was rather thin but fairly tall for a thirteen year old. His dark hair was short, usually cut in a skin tight fashion but it had been a few weeks since his last cut so his locks were getting a bit shaggy. Those blue eyes had a glow to them, mainly because of the candles that rested on the cake in front of him. Roberto’s outfit consisted of a blue Dallas Cowboy’s jersey and a pair of blue jean shorts. On his head was a homemade party hat which read “Happy Birthday Dummy”. Those eyes were fixed on the candles in front of him and a goofy grin formed from his lips. Mom and Dad stood on both sides of him with smiles on their faces. Dad was a short man with shaggy brown hair, glasses and a thin mustache. Roberto’s father was far from a heavy set man but he did have a slight beer belly. Mom was just a tad taller than Dad, her hair was about shoulder length, brown with blonde streaks running through. Mom wasn’t a small girl but too overweight, with some exercise and maybe dieting she could get down really fast. In the chair diagonal from Roberto sat his older brother Victor. Vic was fifteen but that really didn’t separate the two much as they never went anywhere without each other. Victor was a few inches taller than Roberto and a few pounds heavier with brown eyes instead of blue, but other than that they looked fairly similar. Victor smiled wide at his big younger sibling who was being celebrated on this day. Roberto and Victor were as close as two brothers could possibly be. Although Victor was the older of the two and hung out with an older crowd he took Roberto wherever he went even if he got grunts and moans from the other people in his crowd. Roberto looked up to Victor, held him such high esteem that in Roberto’s mind Victor could do no wrong. The good thing about that was, Victor did no wrong. He was a great student, cared for his family, and also a pretty accomplished athlete. The way things were looking, Roberto would follow suit. Roberto’s mother bent down to whisper into Roberto’s ear. “Make a wish sweetie.” Roberto flashed a smile to his mother before blowing out the thirteen candles that stood tall on his cake. When the candles were all out Roberto took off the party hat and put it down on the table in front of him. “So what’d you wish for you knuckle head?” Victor asked. Roberto sighed and rolled his eyes at his brother. “Vic you know I can’t tell you that!” The family laughed together knowing full well that was how Roberto would respond. Roberto got up from his chair and did his rounds, hugging his family one by one. After hugging her son, Roberto’s mother left to the kitchen to grab a knife to cut the cake with. This family certainly appeared to be a happy family which enjoyed the company of each other. The rest of the day was fun, consisting of eating cake and of course smearing cake in each other’s faces when the opportunity presented itself. The family talked about a wide variety of topics ranging from how Roberto enjoyed his gifts, school, sports, girls, etc. As the day became later Roberto’s dad put in a movie for the family to enjoy. By this time Roberto was a bit antsy because he had somewhere to be tonight. He loved his family and all but he had been secretly seeing this girl from school for a few weeks now and was suppose to sneak out to see her later this evening. The movie flew by and Roberto could probably barely tell you two things that happened in it. The sun set at about 10:00 PM that night and Roberto, after a series of over exaggerated yawns and stretching said that he was ready for bed. He kissed his family goodnight and trudged up to his bedroom. He waited up just long enough to hear the door of his parent’s bedroom shut. This was the signal that Roberto had been longing for so that he could make his “great escape”. A trembling hand reached out and grabbed onto the silver colored door knob and turned. Slowly the door pushed open making an old creaky sound which for the life of him, Roberto could not remember ever being there before. Roberto smiled at this thought remembering when Victor had told him that when you’re trying to sneak out the world seems like it makes so much more noise. Roberto cupped his mouth with his right hand fighting to let a chuckle escape his throat. Staying close to the ground like an undercover detective trying to capture an unsuspecting bad guy, Roberto tip toed past his parent’s bedroom and stopping right as he reached the steps to the bottom floor. Slowly a foot came down on the top step and just like the door made an unbelievably loud noise. Roberto froze and turned his head to make sure no one was awake. When everything seemed clear Roberto quickly went down the steps. Without making too much noise he walked to the front door and just as he was ready to turn the nob a voice came from behind him. “So who is she?” Roberto leaped as if he had seen a ghost and fell into the door behind him. Victor stood in front of him leaning up against the wall. Roberto clenched his chest as he tried to return his breathing back to normal, smiling at the same time. “I’ll tell ya later Vic! Just please don’t tell mom and dad!” “haha don’t worry Rob I won’t ? have a good night, and happy birthday.” Roberto grinned as he turned and went out the door with little other noise. Victor walked back to his place on the couch and flipped the TV back on. He chuckled to himself remembering the days that he did the same kind of stuff, considering he was only 15 he still did them on occasion. This thought made Vic chuckle to himself as he put his head down and let the movie “Independence Day” be his “bed time story.” Roberto reached his destination about twenty minutes after leaving his house which was a small elementary school’s playground. He hadn’t been to the school in a few years, making sure that he stayed away every since he “graduated”, because he was an “adult” now so why would he want to be around a bunch of little kids? At first he didn’t see anyone around, the only thing keeping him company were the chirping of the crickets nearby. The moon was full and glowing beautifully in the night sky. Roberto never could explain why exactly but the night time was always his favorite. Not only did everything have a more forbidden feeling to it but strangely enough Roberto always felt more alive when the sun dropped below. Also the scenery was much more gorgeous, including the girl who sat alone on the swing a hundred or so yards away from where Roberto stood. He was not alone as he once thought which made him more nervous than before. Getting caught for sneaking out wasn’t on his mind, the night time chill, or even if the police were to catch them there ? the unimaginable beauty in those green eyes consumed his thoughts. Picking up a slight jog, Roberto moved towards the swing set to greet his company. She turned her eyes to him as he approached and smiled a radiant smile which Roberto could have sworn made his heart melt inside that chest of his. When Roberto approached the beautiful girl stood up and the two hugged quickly. “Glad you could make it Roberto.” “Come on Kari do you really think I would have missed this?” She giggled and leaned in, kissing Roberto’s cheek lightly. She was a few inches taller than Roberto, which was expected for young people this age. Besides her wonderful smile and bright green eyes, Kari had shoulder length brownish red hair. She wore a pair of black slacks that Roberto would have guessed had to have been painted onto her body, but of course he didn’t mind, also because of the chill a white hooded sweatshirt covered her top. The two began to walk, going nowhere in particular just enjoying each other’s company. For hours they walked, talked, and laughed together, telling each other some of the most personal things that they each had stored in their memory. Finally the young couple made it to Kari’s house to say their good byes. Both of them instantly got nervous, Kari fiddling with her hair and Roberto with his hands jammed in his pockets. “I had fun tonight Kari.” “Me too, we should do this again sometime maybe?” “I am at your service.” “well I had better get back inside before my parents find out I am gone and flip out, but before I go I just wanted to say something.” She didn’t give any warnings, just leaned in and kissed Roberto on the lips. Roberto’s whole body tightened up as this happened, lips went into a pucker and his eyes were wide eyed from shock. After a few moments Kari pulled back, opening her eyes and looking at Roberto who still had that pucker on his lips and his blue eyes were still wide open, which made her laugh softly. “Happy birthday Roberto.” “uh’huh?” Roberto watched her walk around her house to go in the back way. At first he didn’t move just replayed what had happened in his mind over and over again. Snapping him from his daze was the flicker of Kari’s bedroom light. Roberto shook his head and blinked his eyes, regaining his composure. A bright smile flashed across his face as he turned and began running home, more lively than he had been all night. He ran as fast as his legs could carry him and keeping that smile the entire time ? or almost the entire time. Three blocks from his house a sharp pain came to his stomach and head. It gave no warning sign, and Roberto couldn’t explain it. The pain brought a fear into Roberto’s body, but it wasn’t a fear for himself but for some reason he feared for his family. He could not explain why he felt this fear but all he knew was that something was happening. The front door was wide open as he returned home and instantly Roberto’s stomach turned over inside of him. Dashing up the three stone steps, Roberto flew in the door but there he stopped. Everything appeared to be the way it was when he left. The lights were all out in the living room although a blue screen covered the TV and Victor was nowhere to be found. Roberto ran to the kitchen where he picked up two large kitchen knives for protection. Roberto then proceeded to the stairs which lead to the bedrooms. The first couple steps were taken slowly but his anxiety and worry got the best of him and he leaped his way to the top of the staircase and to his parent’s bedroom door. Kicking the door it busted open and the scene that Roberto witnessed was horrific. Blood was splattered all over the walls, the sheets and the floor. The windows were busted out and the two lamps were broken over the floor. On the bed is where the true horror came from however. Although they looked peaceful, the blood soaked bodies of his parents and brother lay sprawled. Instantly Roberto fell to his knees and puked on the floor. Tears began streaming down his face, knowing no other way to react. His hands were clasped around those knives tight as he placed his knuckles on his temples and began rocking back and forth. Roberto’s entire body shook as he rocked his body back and forth, trying to make sense of everything. Suddenly his tears stopped, not because he wasn’t in pain or his confusion was over, but because he heard a voice. “Roberto.” The voice was weak and ghostly but it was a voice he knew he hadn’t gone crazy yet. Slowly his head turns upwards, the sight of His family now standing in front of him caused him to scream and jump backwards hitting hard on the floor as he landed in the doorway. “YOU’RE DEAD!” “No Roberto we are very much alive!” His father hissed. “DEAD!” Roberto screamed “Roberto my darling don’t be afraid ? we love you.” Step by step his family walked closer, but somehow Roberto knew that this wasn’t his family. They moved differently, they talked differently, they even smelled differently. “STAY AWAY MOM, please STAY AWAY!” “Roberto! This is Victor talking here man ? what’s wrong with you?” “YOU’YOU?” Roberto couldn’t finish his sentence as tears began flowing again. Victor’s eyes flashed red and he almost floated towards Roberto, but Roberto was ready and he screamed and thrust a knife forward, driving it deep into his brother’s chest, causing Victor to howl in pain and to Roberto’s surprise burst into a pile of flame and ash. This caused Roberto’s crying to stop, a look of anger filled his face. “NOOO! He killed our boy!” Roberto’s mom bellowed. Mom kneeled down by the ash that was her son Victor and Roberto’s father glared into Roberto’s eyes. “Roberto ? children this bad need to be punished!” His father’s fangs glimmered in the moonlight as he leaped into the air for an attack on his son. Roberto fell backwards letting his father fly over his body, but as he did Roberto drove his two knives into the chest of his father sending him to the same fate his brother faced. With a snarl the mother attacked hitting Roberto with a thunderous smack, sending him on his back with a slight nose bleed. Quickly Roberto sat up and through one of his knives and it dug deep into his mother’s shoulder causing her to grimace and screech at first, but that soon turned into a smile. Roberto wasted no time, using this distraction he got on two feet and lunged at his mother, placing the blade into her chest, destroying her. Fist clenched around the handle to that knife Roberto stood over the carnage and glared. His eyes never blinked nor did his body even tremble a little bit. Amongst the rage and violence the boy now seemed more in control than ever ? as if this was something he had done day in and day out. Roberto had to kill his family, and now had to wonder why he was spared. TODAY The sun had finally fully shown itself to the world and Roberto finally smiled at the new day. Something about that sunrise told him that there was still that piece of humanity still driven in that body that his soul inhabited regardless of how many of those things he killed. Roberto took in a deep breath before moving off of the ledge and flopping down onto his dusty mattress. That slight smirk still on his face, Roberto closed his eyes and drifted to sleep, knowing that when that sun went back down there would be even more lives to take.
