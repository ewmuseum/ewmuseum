What is Family?

19 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-19-06 Jayce Bradley's crystalline blue eyes focus on themselves, as he stares into the reflection of himself in the mirror. Leaning heavily on the cold porcelain of the bathroom sink, his eyes droop wearily. His sweat drenched wifebeater clings to his chest, his blond hair glistening with perspiration. His eyes appear completely devoid of emotion, as he watches the mud rinsed from his hands as he holds them below the faucet's steady stream of water. The stinging of fresh, bright red cuts make themselves known up and down his hands and forearms as the grime is washed away, yet he doesn't even flinch. A combination of blood and sweat mixes with the water to drip from his hands as he examines the welt just below his eye, as well as the knot on his nose. ?The son of a bitch broke my nose...? Taking a firm grip of his nose, tears well up in the corner of his eyes as he twists it back into place. The pain causes him to chuckle softly to himself, finally lifting his head to take another look at himself in the mirror. The muscles in his arms contract as he twists and turns, removing the dingy wifebeater from his well defined frame. Welts have already begun to rise around his rib cage, a slight shade of purple already evident on his light complected skin. Grabbing a towel on his way out of the bathroom, he makes his way over to the bed of his nondescript hotel room. Draping the towel over his head like a cowl, he leans forward with his forearms resting more heavily than he would like them to. Allowing his mind to drift, he waits patiently for the aching of his body to become a dull throbbing. The pain coursing through his body was nothing though, compared to the pain he felt in his heart. A life he once thought implausible, had become attainable in the briefest of moments. For the past few years, he has believed that his family was dead. That his Mother and Father had since passed, but it had all been lies. Lies told to him by a man that craved something, a man that used him to get what he wanted despite the pain and anguish that not only Jayce would feel... but his family as well. It had taken him quite a bit of time to work up the courage, but he finally decided to track them down... if for no other reason than to let them know that he was still alive. If they even cared anymore. When he approached the home in a suburban neighborhood in up state New York, he knew something was wrong from the get go. =?=?=?=?=?= A silver Chevy Malibu eases up the drive to the house located in the center of a Cul de Sac, it's tires gripping against the asphalt to come to a stop in front of a two story home that is easily worth more than he could ever afford. Stepping out, he straightens his black short sleeve button up shirt, and places his keys in his jeans pocket. Walking up the front steps, that are wide enough to drive a truck up easily, he stops at the door and uses the heavy gold door knocker. He waits for an answer, but one does not appear to be forthcoming. The heavy oak doors shake in the frame as he slams the knocker down over and over again. ?Dammit, there has to be someone here.? Pacing back and forth, the frustration slowly builds in his gut. He knows someone is there. Why would they want to see him though... as far as they knew, he had abandoned them. His eyes narrow slightly as the door slowly eases open, and a large man in his fifties stands there staring at the former Cruiserweight champion. ?Can I help you?? Swallowing the knot that had formed in his throat, Jayce replies the best he could, ?Ye- Yeah. Is Mr. Or Mrs. Bradley in?? Shaking his head, the old man responds, ?Sorry, but the Bradleys moved out of here about a month or so ago. I moved in here just last week.? The words were not meant to harm him, but Jayce felt their sting nonetheless. After so long, he had come so close... only to fall short. ?I see,? Jayce mumbles. ?If you'd like to step inside,? the man starts, ?I may be able to find some sort of mailing address. If it's important of course.? A sign of hope lifts Jayce's head to stare into a man that could lead him to a family he thought to be lost. ?It's very important, sir.? Bradley's emotions are running out of control now as he follows the man into the home. A wall that had sprouted up in his journey to find his family had been knocked down by a sliver of hope, and there was no way he could let this opportunity pass him by. Watching as the old man thumbed through a few stacks of mail, his heart began to flutter. ?Sorry to get your hopes up,? the words alone caused his heart to skip a beat. ?but I can't seem to find anything.? Dejected, Jayce nodded politely. ?Don't worry about it, sir. It probably wasn't meant to be.? ?Any message you want me to give them if they happen to come back?? ?No thanks,? Jayce shakes his head from side to side. ?They probably don't remember me anyway.? =?=?=?=?=?= Back in the hotel room, Bradley sits icing his black eye with an ice cold Labatt Blue, the bottle glistening with condensation. He can't believe he got into a bar fight. Especially since he insinuated the fight when some Italian prick refused to give up a pool table. In any other condition, with nothing on his mind, there is no way Jayce would've done that. He had got past that part of his life and had moved on... but where did he stand now. He acted foolishly in going to this man's home to try and find his family, only to get slapped in the face by reality. They have moved on, rebuilt their lives without him. How could he ever expect them to just open their arms and accept him, after all this time he was he kidding. Now, there was two men who had absolutely nothing to do with this in the hospital, using their good arms to call their lawyers. This day is turning out to be one of the worst days in his life. ?I should have just let the past go...? he thought to himself. He still doesn't understand why he became so hell bent on coming up here to New York in the first place. A wiser man would have let things go, and accepted the fact that he was lied to and had his family taken from him. No matter how bad he wishes it could have happened otherwise, he will never be able to change the past. Not now, not ever. He would change many things in his past if he could, but then again, that's the bitch about life, you're stuck with how you play your cards when you get them. There's no going back into the deck and looking for the right one later on in the game. ?I've got more important things to be worried about right now anyway. Vivica... Dangerous Games...? He tried to think of something else, concentrate on work and forget about all the rest. Downing the last of his beer, he gets back to his feet and walks into the bathroom. Dropping the bottle in the trash can under the sink, he twists the knob and splashes his face with some cold water to try and clear his head. Lifting his head, watching as the cool liquid streaks down his face, he cannot shake this feeling that his mind is laughing at him. It's amused by the fact that he has failed at yet another thing. ?I know, I know... I'm stupid. I get it. You want to wipe that shit eating grin off your face?? He runs his fingers through his hair, realizing that he is standing in a run down hotel room somewhere in upper New York arguing with himself. You can tell just by looking at him that he is a defeated man. His foolish pride brought him here, his ego dragged him into a mess that should have remained dead to him like it was before he got the phone call months ago telling him that his family was alive and that he had been lied to. Hating the man in the mirror is something that we all know something about. At one point or another there is a time when you want nothing more than to punch that man in the mirror with everything you got. Hate is what we hold for the bastard in the mirror, because he doesn't lie to us. No matter how bad we don't want to hear the truth he is going to tell us anyway. The truth hurts, and the mirror tells no lies. Ain't that a bitch.... =?=?=?=?=?= Jayce returned from his venture into unbridled insanity in New York weeks ago. For the most part he has confined himself to his apartment ever since, not really wanting to face the reality that he lost it all once again. Not too long ago he was rising up in the business and making a name for himself. The money was good to him, and he was happy for the first time in longer than he could remember. One simple phone call made him lose his mind completely. Mad at himself more than anything, he has been trying to figure out what to do with himself now that he has given up on anything from his past. No reason worrying about someone you put to rest in your mind. Slowly walking into the living area, he drags his feet heavily. His face covered in the growth of not shaving in nearly a week's time. His upper torso is still well defined, but it looks as if he has lost a little bit of weight. His jeans hang off his hips slightly because the belt is undone, showing black boxer briefs underneath. The only reason this bears mentioning, is because he put them on inside out today. Nearly a year ago, he walked into Global Championship Wrestling. It came to him in the early days of being a part of GCW, the simple truth that once you compete in these rings you can never be satisfied with anything else. Even if you don't make it here, at least you know you went down in a fight with the best there are in this business. He can't help but think that he has only truly been happy when he is inside a ring. It has been this way for so long now, that he never realized that it had become so much to him. It has been his family, his love for the past few years. Sitting down in the leather straight backed chair near the open front windows, he stares out at the children running around having a grand old time. Full of joy without a care in the World. He needed to feel that again, to feel the adrenaline coursing through his veins. He glanced over to where the phone was, noticing the card that he was given not too long ago by a woman who said she shared his feelings. ?I guess it couldn't hurt,? he thought to himself as he stood up, making his way over to not only the car, but the phone as well. Flipping the card end over end in his fingers, he idly takes another glance out the window. Listening to the joyous laughter coming from the children running around in the street as he dials the phone, his eyes drifting back to the business card frequently. After what seems like an eternity his eyes light up as the phone is answered on the other end. ?Hey, this is Jayce,? he says to whoever it was that answered on the other end. ?I'm ready to stand beside you... all of you.?
