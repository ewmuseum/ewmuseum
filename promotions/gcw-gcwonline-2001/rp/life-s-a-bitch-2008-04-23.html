"What the ..." My mind had a strange way of playing with me, and today was one of those times.” I did not wander to my wife and child's grave like I thought I had.” No this name was still familiar to me ... but it was a name I had hoped to forget.” A name that had brought nothing to my life but sorrow ... "Mi...Michelle?" I didn't need to be told why i was here now, fate just plans thing for us.” My body didn't tense up like it once would have, because I expected what would happen next.” In many ways I invited what would happen next.” I rose to one knee, and took in a deep breath before letting it out into the crisp cool night air.” "So we meet again Alex ?" I looked into his empty eyes, trying to find some sort of human features, but for some reason his eyes seemed to be black, as black as my hair and my coat.” It was almost as if he weren't there at all. "Oh Roberto...of the many things i've shown you how to do over the years haven't i shown you that sometimes it's more delicious to cause misery, to play those wonderful games we used to play? To see which one of us could burrow into the others head the deepest" Suddenly the sounds of chains rattling together can be heard above the patter of the rain, in Alex's hand is a chain "You hurt others so badly by playing with them that death would have been a mercy...but sometimes I was the only mercy, isn't that true..." he jerks the chain and a figure flies out of the darkness and lands at my feet with a muffled cry of pain, I look down and my eyes widen with surprise as my once Lover Wylie stares up at me through frightened eyes, a choke chain cruelly digging into her neck, her mouth bound and gagged by an S and M device, her clothes tattered and torn exposing her bare flesh and dignity to the mercy of the cold rain "Isn't that true...Wylie."
