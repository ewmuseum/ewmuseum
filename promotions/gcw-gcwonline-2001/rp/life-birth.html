Date: 8/1/87 PRELUDE I stood there. Contemplating what to do. This was the pinnacle of my life. That point that you get to and you know you have to make a choice. One that will impact the rest of your life. One that could very well reshape everything you’ve ever known. He had a gun in his hand and no sympathy in his eyes. Fearless eyes. The alley was dark. I wasn’t sure what to do. All of this over that tiny squabble. Was it really that insignificant then? Apparently not. Should I just give in? The rain poured down fiercely. That I know I can remember. It was the hardest rain I’ve ever felt. Each drip had the potential to pierce my clothes like bullets penetrating amour. I’m just a feeble old man. It sucks that my final moment had truly arrived. "Give it up retard!" No. No I can’t. I’m sorry. I refuse. I REFUSE! His hand shook fiercely as his sweat overtook the rain? at least on his body. Or so? that’s what it looked like for sure. His leather jacket was ruined for sure. I mean? no one would find out about it? right? It’s a dark alley. Ain’t anybody coming down here in this storm. What does he have to lose? The last thing I remember before he shot, was just staring off into the distance? like I did so many times before. The emptiness took over inside of me? again. BANG! The bullet. He shot me! My? my eyes? what’s happening? START Fondest Memory: Birth Saturday, August 1, 1987 9:56 am Boy? it sure was dark in there. Not even my claw attack could get me out of that horrid contraption of life-giving. Picture an oven? but attached to the front of a woman. Now picture that woman attached to a hospital bed. And picture all of these wires attached to her. Scary stuff isn’t it? It shouldn’t be. Child birth was down to a science? even in 1987. I think. Who knows? That’s why they have Wikipedia for those sorts of things. Anyways? where was I? Oh yes. The womb. If you’ve ever been; you know what I’m talking about. It’s sticky, messy and potentially dangerous. Kinda like prostitutes but minus the STDs. So there I was. Creating quite the little ruckus in Mommy’s womb. Who knows what kind of mischief I got into? But it was all about to come to an abrupt end. "Okay Mrs. Fox, I’m Dr. Hooligan. Let’s have a baby," the doctor said. I was born in one of those typical semi run down hospitals. One with pasty green walls and decrepit side tiling. The happy face decals on the windows were all but peeled off. The floor hadn’t been cleaned in nearly five months. But still? it was home. My home. I wasn’t even sure if I was ready to come out yet. I mean? that place was the only thing familiar to me. I didn’t want to go out into some unknown place and start all over. I’m established here. I have a pretty damn good set up if you know what I mean. I get fed on regular intervals. Granted? it might not always be the best stuff ever. Like that time she ate Mexican food. But let’s not go there. I got treated pretty well. A warm cocoon with no worries. Who would want to leave? "Okay Mrs. Fox. The preparation has been completed. You’re quite dilated? should be happening really soon now," his voice rang. I still remember the sound of his scruffy voice. He looked just like Fred Penner. Hehe. I loved his show. But that’s not for a few years. He was a good doctor. For the most part. So there I was. About to be born. My spaced out Father was watching through the mesh grid window. He would have been better described as a zombie. All of this happened because of an accident he had in the ring back in 1975. I have him to blame for this. "Push Mrs. Fox! PUSH!" He screamed as he coddled his hands closer and closer together. The sound of a spleen splitting isn’t always the most comforting tune. Especially if a seven year old boy hears it. That was my brother for you. Apparently Chris threw up at the ear splitting sounds made when I came out of the chute. Sorry bout that little scar I left on your life. Ell oh ell. I remember reaching out with both hands and screaming loudly. The doctor almost dropped me. I guess that’s why my Fox 40 is so effective. God gave me great lungs. "Congratulations! It’s a boy!" Hooligan stammered. Boy? it; scratch that; I was a boy. "Oh. How lovely. Casey would be so proud," my drained Mother exclaimed. Casey, aka my Father didn’t have a clue what just happened. He stood there facing the window with both eyes reaching out in different directions. "Daddy!” Daddy. Is that my little brother?" Chris inquired cutely. My dad looked down at Chris and nearly drooled on him. That’s how gone his head was. I was being cleaned up and prepped for a little one on one Mommy session (like I wasn’t already with her enough for nine months) while Chris questioned our Father. "Duhhhhhhh. Rainbows like daffodils!" My Father replied. "You don’t even know what just happened Daddy!” Another person flew out of Mommy! Can I play with him Daddy?" Christopher asked. Back in the birth room Dr. Hooligan was cleaning me with an apprentice nurse. Damn bitch was so stupid that she didn’t know the difference between a dust buster and a lollipop. And trust me? THERE’S A BIG DIFFERENCE! So the doctor thought to use me as a test subject for his little apprentice. "This is how you properly clean a newborn," Hooligan instructed. She didn’t get it right. That bitch nearly drown-ded me! Maybe my retardation came from that and not my Fathers accident. Or maybe it was a combination of both. All I know is that I got brain damage from it. It’s because of that event that I blurt out random profanities? FUCK, SHIT, COCK, BITCH! Sorry? see what I mean? "Pull him out! Quickly now!" Hooligan took over. "What’s wrong? Is everything okay?" My immobile Mother worried. "Yes! We have everything under control? now that is," Hooligan declared. Dr. Hooligan fixed me up and got what little water I swallowed out of my stomach. As fucked up as everything was? I was ready to go. With a half working heart and a mental handicap? I was ready. "Hope he’s not like his brother," Hooligan muttered to his apprentice. "What do you mean?" She replied. "Chris? is a very special child. He’s rather slow. And plus he threw up all over me when I delivered him nearly eight years ago," Hooligan explained. PLAT! Take that you evil doctor man dude! Yeah that’s right. Almost on cue I upchucked all over that sunuvfabitch! "Perfect! Just perfect!" Hooligan complained as he wiped his pure white lab coat. So there I was world. Whether you were ready to accept me or not. My zero minute had passed and it was truly time to start living my life? "His name Mrs. Fox?" The apprentice asked Mommy. "Clyde? Clyde Evan Fox." Mommy stated. END
