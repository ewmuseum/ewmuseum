Drops Of Jupiter (Part One)

23 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/23/10 The deep hum of a Gibson bass still rattled the ribcages of dozens as they hovered around the stage, optimistic that the previous dim of the lights was nothing more than a ploy to trick them into chanting their name. "We Want Train!" The masses obliged, hoping... "We Want Train!" ...wishing... "We Want Train!" ...praying... "We Want Train!" ...that the jukebox wouldn't be the next chord that they heard. House lights dimly lit around them as disappointment set in. Neon lights flickered to life all around the bar, forcing docile bartenders to scramble in preparation for the onslaught of sweaty, drunken patrons that was sure to follow. Having already claimed her seat at the bar, Vivica J. Valentine circled a red stirring straw around in her Red Bull and cranberry juice cocktail. Enter the jukebox, in the form of "Paralyzer" by Finger Eleven. The once hushed masses turned into a textbook example of calamity, heading every which way but the same. Like herded cattle they pushed and shoved their way towards different destinations... the bar, the exit, friends, family, restrooms, backstage. Contrary to the bartenders, however, the wrestling icon wasn't watching in amusement as drunks tried to find their way to alcohol. It was a group of people that entered through a side door that had piqued her interest. Lead by a tall man with a shaved head who seemed to be sharing a funny story vastly overpowered by the sounds of drunks in mass force) they circled around the bar almost oblivious the chaos around them. Long black hair almost completely covered the features of a skinny pale man to his left, whose gaze was fixated on much smaller girl with a multi-colored bob cut. As the trio headed towards her, Vivica couldn't help but stare as she desperately tried to place why they looked so familiar. Not even a Red Bull induced caffeine high jogged her memory enough to put her churning mind at ease... at least not enough to look away. "Excuse me?" The raised voice of the smallish girl startled Vivica, who until that point in time remained stuck in her own mind trying to place the woman's face. "Did you need something?" "I... uh..." Vivica stammered through her words in a volume that surely couldn't be heard over the rush to the bar, shifting her eyes quickly to her drink she needed only a second to come up with a worthwhile response. With a raised voice over both music and masses, she proclaimed, "I really like you hair. That color green is something I've been thinking about doing." A smile crossed spider-bite pierced lips as the woman sat down on the barstool next to her. With a porcelain complexion in contrast of emerald green eyes, she was the type of girl who seemed to stick out in a crowd. The black and lime green dyed hair seemed to only draw the eyes in further, exposing anyone who came across her to a magnetic aura. Something about the girl was open and comforting despite her alternative appearance. "Thanks, I just did it last week." Her response was quickly followed by a tap on the bar, a signal that even the over-worked bartender acknowledged immediately with a shot glass. "What's your name?" Vivica's expression turned vacant for a split second, taping index finger against the side of her glass. "Vee," Finally spurted out from between her pink lips. "Just call me Vee." "Nice to meet you, Vee." The woman responded, looked down at the shot glass of Lemon Drop, and then threw it down the back of her throat like a pro. "My name is Jupiter. Jupiter Skye." "Legally?" Viv couldn't help but chuckle. "Now? Yes." Laughter was contagious. "Didn't used to be." "What did it used to be?" "That..." Jupiter tapped the shot glass against the bar. "That's a secret. Did you want another... what is that?" "It's cranberry juice and Red Bull." "That's no fun!" Jupiter shook her head. "You driving or something?" "Yes, but I don't drink anyway." "Why not?! If you don't mind me asking..." "Not really my thing." Vivica took a sip of the cocktail. "Bad experiences." "Haven't we all?" Jupiter giggled before throwing back another Lemon Drop and pounding the shot glass down on the counter. The shaved guy tapped her on the shoulder and pointed towards the exit, a move she acknowledged with a nod before she waved the two of them away. "Failing isn't a very good reason to quit. It's something we all do sometimes, is that why you stopped?" "I never started." "You've never drank, EVER?!" "No. Never. Not my thing." "You said that!" "I did." A smirk crossed Vivica's face as Jupiter began to laugh. Perhaps it was simply that infectious or the caffeine was beginning to sink in, but she couldn't help but laugh along even if she didn't think it was at all funny. "But talking with another girl is nice. It's different for me." "How so?" "I spend all my free time with those bastards." Jupiter pointed towards the exit. "They're cool as fuck but you know... they're men. They talk about man things. Sometimes I just want to be a girl dammit!" "Roommates?" "No, bandmates." Jupiter tapped her glass loudly on the counter top again. "Fallout Shelter! Did you get here late?" Suddenly like a switch it clicked in Vivica's mind. She was in the band that opened the show earlier in the night. A girl drummer, it was so rare she couldn't believe that she'd forgotten so easily. Pretty talented girl too, if her memory server her correct. "No, I saw. You were pretty good out there." "Thanks! We just signed to Sony! I'm fucking FLOORED." Jupiter put her hand on Vivica's shoulder. "We're going to have an album come out at the end of the year, and a US Tour. I love this job." Vivica simple glanced down at the hand on her shoulder, at first wanting to brush it off but... it grew on her. "That sounds amazing, grats." "Thank you so much!" Jupiter threw back another lemon drop and smiled from ear to ear. "What about you? Are you on vacation? Do you live here? What do you do for a living?" That might've been the last question The Bulletproof Blonde wanted to hear. "I..." Again a loss for words takes the breath right away from her lips. "I... work at a record shop not far off the strip. Vintage vinyls, that kind of thing." "Here in Vegas?!" "Yeah." An uneasy smirk crossed her lips. "What did you think I did?" "Fuck if I know, but we should totally hang." Jupiter quickly grabbed her hand. Vivica's first instinct was to jerk it back, but for some reason, she hesitantly watched as Jupiter pulled a sharpie out of her pocket and began to write on Valentine's flesh. Biting her bottom lip, Vivica looked away as the green haired drummer finished her inscription. "That's my number. Call me. I need some time away from the guys! They're driving me INSANE." As she looked down at the number on the back of her hand, doubt and uncertainty flooded her thoughts. What had she gotten herself into... "Yeah." The professional wrestling icon forced a plastic smile. "Definitely." When Jupiter clapped her hands together in a fit of giddy drunkenness, Vivica could almost feel her heart sink down into the pit of her stomach. It was going to be a long week in Las Vegas. I didn't know if I was more scared of being recognized "Hurry up, I don't have all da..." CLICK. With one touch she stopped the incessant whining on the other end of her Palm Pre, but I suppose when you're traveling with Christian Zenith, such experiences are expected. With a bit of a pep in her step, she jogged down the stairs outside the world famous Caesar’s Palace, over 20 floors below where her room looked out over Sin City. Cerulean blue eyes navigated the scene from behind golden blonde locks of hair, searching out for what was sure to be an extravagant Zenith Corporation limo. There it was, almost as if on cue. As it came around the curve, it didn't stop, almost as if the driver never saw Vivica as she hustled down the staircase. It pulled away ever so slowly and her jog turned into a bit of a sprint while she shoved her phone into her pocket. He arm raised into the air... "HEY! SLOW DOWN!" She screamed before she reached out and grabbed the back of the limo, using it to pull herself forward next to the back door. With a quick tug, she jerked the door open and jumped inside, slamming it shut behind her. With a sigh she turned around to look straight into the eyes of the devil himself. "Were you planning on fucking stopping back there?!" "Never saw you." Christian's monotone voice responded as he looked back at her from behind black sunglasses. "You were late." "I didn't even want to co..." Snarling she stopped herself and gritted her teeth. "Forget it." "We can take you back." The Bulletproof Blonde only replied with a glare, which Christian expertly avoided when he pulled his blackberry out of his pocket and began punching away on the keys. From behind the tinted window, Las Vegas unfolded in front of her. The lights. The sounds. The excitement. Hello. Again. Friend of a friend, I knew you when... A burst of sound erupted from the pocket of the blonde haired daredevil. Slowly Christian Zenith looked over at his passenger, watching as she struggled to jerk the cell phone out of her pocket. While she looked at the name, you could watch the color leave her face. Our common goal was waiting for the world to end... "Are you going to pick that up?" Christian's voice raised as he glared back at Valentine, who didn't respond at first, as she stared holes into her phone. "I... don't... know." Vivica slowly spoke, reading the world "JUPITER" on the front of her phone. "If you aren't going to answer, could you at least ignore it?" Christian went back to his Blackberry, "That song is atrocious." With a last minute decision, Valentine flipped her phone open. She took a deep breath and placed it up to her ear, glancing over at Christian as she spoke... "...Hello?" "Hey Vee, what's happenin?!" "Not much, just... relaxing." "That's fantastic! Hey, did you happen to listen to that single I asked you to look up?" "Yeah, it was really good." "Awesome! We have another show here in two weeks, you should come out." "A... show?" Vivica repeated, the tone in her voice was enough to get Christian's attention next to her. Zenith couldn't help but put his phone away and observe the rest of her conversation. Something was definitely up. "Yeah, same place! We decided to stay in Vegas for another few weeks." "I would but..." She looked around the inside of the limo, trying to get some kind of inspiration as Christian looked on, nodding. "I work a lot, and I work weird hours, Jupiter. I'm not sure if I could get the time off." "Time off?" Christian asked and a smirk crossed his face, everything was beginning to piece together in his head. "Bummer," Jupiter responded. "I'm sure we'll be able to work something out. What are your hours this week?" "I..." Pausing again, she caught a glimpse of Christian Zenith glaring back at her like the cat that ate the canary. "Yes Vee, when do you work this week?" Zenith asked, trying to contain a chuckle. "I don't know, I'll have to look." Vivica began to bounce her leg up and down as she glared back at Christian, "But I have to go." "Alright, call me when you find out, Ciao." "Bye." With that said, Valentine flipped her phone shut and took a deep breath. She didn't want to make eye contact with the billionaire sitting next to her now completely aware of her lies. He took a moment to formulate the perfect response before he spoke. "I nev..." She already knew it was coming. "Fuck off, Chris." "I ju..." Before he could finish his sentence, her hand had already made its way to the base of his throat. Moving her hand up to his Adam's apple she shot a glare back at him that would bring most men to their knees. Christian far from considered himself most men. "I..." Her grip tightened and her nostrils flared. "Try me." Silence. Or if I was just feeling like I wouldn't be accepted A turn of a rusted handle and a small shove brought forth an ear shattering squeal from the door hinges. Used to the usual greeting, she entered the door with a finger in one ear as she swung the rust bucket shut behind her. Her entire body ached, a head to toe throb that can only be remedied by a nice relaxing bath. A nice relaxing bath that would never happen, thanks in most part to a shut off noticed posted on the fridge. Dated yesterday. Trudging over mountains of laundry, she passed towers of dishes raising high out of the sink. A pair of jeans obstructed her view of what may or may not be a coffee table, but definite sent her stumbling forward and landing haphazardly on an old, beat down green sleeper sofa. She landed with an audible grunt, and let out a long sigh before adjusting herself into a slightly more comfortable position. A position in which she could kick off her shoes and reach back to turn on the box fan in the window at the same time. Success is soon found in the form of a size six Adidas flying across the room and bouncing off the wall, soon followed by another. As the fan blows through strands of green and black, she stretches out as far as she can, cracking every single vertebrae on the way up to her shoulders before erupting into a yawn. Reaching into the pocket of her khaki pants, she pulls out her cell phone and flips it open. No missed calls. She'd already called twice that day, and the urge to call a third time was almost unbearable. Her entire shift she seemed distanced from the rest of her co-workers. Inside jokes and teasing that often resulted in vengeance went without any real response. She had spent more time staring at her cell phone that at the condition of the shelves. Surely she was in no position to become employee of the month as she pulled the blue polo up over her head and tossed it on top of the pile. Trying to enjoy the new found escape from the blistering desert sun under her box fan, she closed her eyes and tried to imagine she wasn't there. She tried to imagine the events at the bar that night had never happened. Whether it was the alcohol talking or her own shallow sense of self-worth, the new friend she had made wasn't worth the nerve racking guilt that she'd felt every day since. As she opened her emerald green eyes, the blue Blockbuster Video polo laid on top of the mountain of clothes mocking her. Grabbing her cell phone she dialed the number for the third time that day, trying desperately to reach her new friend. Trying desperately to make thing right. RIIIIIIINNNNNGGGGG. Her eyes couldn't help but move down the color towards the name badge. RIIIIIIINNNNNGGGGG. A silver badge reflecting under the sun, reading just one name in bold capital print. RIIIIIIINNNNNGGGGG. AMANDA HARPER. RIIIIIIINNNNNGGGGG. "You've reached the voicemail box of..." CLICK. She tossed the phone on top of the blue polo before running her fingers through her multi-colored hair. Staring straight up at the ceiling she couldn't help but say her feelings out loud... "Fuck my life." But something kept me from being... well, me. I guess it's hard to be yourself, when you don't know who you really are. The only thing I know for certain, is that I can't go on living a lie. Vee has to know the truth. I have to make things right.
