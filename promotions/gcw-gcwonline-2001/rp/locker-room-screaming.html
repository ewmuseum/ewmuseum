Date: Timeless Jesus, this has been going on for...check the watch...a good half hour now. You'd think he'd run out of things to say by now. Guess that's why he did the commentary thing for awhile...loves to hear his own damn mouth being shot off. Tonight, we got outplayed and embarassed...duh. Any jackass with one eye full of cataracts could catch that. To tune back in shortly... 'You sons of bitches are pathetic. I gave you...no, I gave US Jason Dread on a silver f*cking platter! What the hell is the matter with this picture? I don't think his damn music stopped the whole match. It never got cut off because the whole pathetic ordeal was over in two minutes...' It's late. Something tells me an odd now would be ill-timed. Steven signs the checks, but between Danger and Gaius is where I actually get money to play with. Still...my patience is starting to wear thin. To have been quiet for this long is pretty damn impressive, I think. I can understand why Gaius is still here. He and Danger have that symbiotic-type relationship going now. Coddling each other the majority of the time, 'pushing' each other in the gym-though they both seem to be putting on a little padding-and making 'world-altering' business decisions all the damn time. Damn married couple. Now Alex. He confuses me a little bit, I won't lie. Guy's silent as a damn Royal Guard...the ones with the big furry hats. Real stoic. I think deep down, he's scared shitless, feels like he's in over his head. Knight's not lacking in the rage department though...can't say I wouldn't be a little worried if I had him gunning for me. Except I won't say that because, well, I'm better than Alex and Knight never scared me...just beat me. That one still stings a little bit. And then there's the question of why I've- 'Luke?! Dammit, you braindead gorilla, pay attention, you're bringing down the team. We've got to strategize all over again thanks to the combined f*ck up you all pulled tonight. Now-' Teamwork? Combined? I signed up for this thing 'cause it got me a little more respect in the booking. Now this week I'm up against Jonathan Borealis with Jhub and Warpath still a couple weeks off. So teamwork...no, this isn't a high school football team, I think that's about all I'm going to take for tonight. Gaius and Danger are going at it verbally, but looking stately but wrecked in Armani. Alex is in his chair busting his balls internally to show nothing externally. Think it's about time I shake things up in here. 'Hey, 'boss?' Shut up for a minute and let me say something so I can get out of here and find a damn room for the night.' I've got to say, the looks are priceless. Alex has totally morphed into the deer in headlights. Danger's jaw'd probably hit the the table if it were an inch higher. Octy looks down right incredulous. At least I know I finally got there attention. Only took two months. 'I didn't sign on for this team bullshit. I'll overlook the fact that when it comes down to it y'all recruited me to be some kind of a damn enforcer, because I milked the situation and got some of what I wanted out of it. Now let's face it. Were any of you to be hit by a falling piece of space station and die in the next hour, I wouldn't give a damn. I'm sure you all feel the same. I didn't sign up for team meetings and strategy sessions, and I sure as hell didn't sign up for sloppily organized attacks that would put Gaius Octavian back in 'his' spotlight. I'd rather be jerking the curtain if it puts me in a decent match that'll garner a little respect via a pinfall. Now, if I'm going to be in the ring with Jason Dread, I want it to be for me. And while I have no problem crippling the sumbitch, it really ain't worth it for me if it's not for his belt. I don't want it yet, Octy, so untie your tubes. I got closets full of unfinished business with some of the other fellas here. I plan on dealing with some of it before I start seriously going for the gold. Gaius, I hope you haven't forgotten that. And I really hope that you haven't forgotten that, if I thought it would help me, I'd slit your throat while you were sleeping to get more acclaim within GCW. I'm in this for me. You're in this for you. Occasionally, we help each other out. I am an individual. I don't known when we got labeled as your damn 'PG.' But be damn sure that it rubbed me in all kinds of wrong ways. So in case you were wondering, no, we ain't cool. From now on, when you need some muscle. Call me. No more attempt at a community dressing room. And when I need money...well, I'll keep using the beautiful piece of plastic you pay the bill for. This week I'm fighting that new kid, Jonathan Borealis. I don't know who he is. I haven't paid attention to who these guys are, who they're aligned with, who they're pissed at, or which ones of them are nailing that hot new piece of ass Eva. But this week, I'm going to look into it. And when WorldWide comes around again, I'm going to break the kid, on my own. If you need some help with something, call me. Otherwise, do what worked before this thing became the 'Gang Bang Jason Dread Show': leave me the f*ck alone. Now, I'm tired, I'm sore, and after tonight and that pathetic thing we just called a 'main event,' I'm embarrassed. I'm going to go get drunk and pass out in a nice hotel bed. Good evening.' Well, that felt good. Blaq still doesn't have a clue, but Danger and Gaius sure are pissed. And I'm out the door. Maybe pissed is what they need. Maybe now they'll finally get something productive done. But as far as I'm concerned, f*ck 'em, it's their shit to deal with. Like I said, I've got my own problems right now. And there's Danger letting me know just how he feels... 'Hey! Hey! Get back here, asshole, I'm-we're not done with this by a longshot! We've got...' End.
