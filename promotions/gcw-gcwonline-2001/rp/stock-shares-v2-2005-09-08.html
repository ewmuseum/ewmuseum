Date: 9-8-05 “I got them,” she said. “I know.” “How do you know?” “Never mind that now. Meet me at Kutac’s Koffee and Kookies at 9:00 this evening. We’ll sign them over then and it’ll all be ours.” “Wait,” she hesitated. “What makes you think I trust you” Who says you won’t run off as soon as you get them, and after all this hard work I put in.” “I’m anxious for our reunion. It’s been a long time, but it’ll all be worth it. I’ve missed you!” “You better not fuck me Ryan.” “I won’t run off with the shares without taking you with me, as for the other connotation, I make no promises.” CLICK. “You fucking dumbass! What have you done?” screamed the elder Baratta, John. He raised a hand to hit his youngest son, but Blaise stumbled backwards out of his father’s reach, body trembling in terror. “I’ve” I’ve? been with her for 14 months now father. We’ve slept together. She hates Ryan, her allegiance is to our family,” said Blaise. “Which is why she’s meeting Stephens tonight to give him control of our company. Control that YOU GAVE HER!” yelled John. “But she loves?” “She doesn’t love you, you fucking idiot. I warned you! She’s conned you out of our majority share of FSN.” “There’s still time, I’ll get them back.” “You know nothing is beyond Stephens. Go check the papers, I bet those two are even still married. The bastard would let his wife fuck another man for fourteen months to gain control of our television network.” “I’ll get them back Dad, we haven’t lost it yet.” “You’re right, we haven’t. That bitch was smart enough to con you for fourteen months and then fuck it up by using her cell phone to call Stephens. We’ll kill them both tonight.” “Give me a chance to get them back first Dad. I know she loves me.” So we had The Regulators this Thursday on Worldwide. I wasn’t overly concerned. I felt confident my boy Jake could single-handedly dethrone team Weiners and Piss Pants or whatever they were called in High School. I missed the beginning of my career when I could nonchalantly make pointless references to high school, college and the likes. I missed being over confident and sprinting to the ring, where it seemed like every match was life and death for me. Oh wait, I didn’t miss that at all. Very soon I would have control of the whole Fox Sports Network where I would then attempt to bring every PTC federation on board and broadcast PTC all the time. I might even let that Malice Man guy have his own show, the thought had crossed my mind. Business was booming and I was one half of the tag champs in one of the best, and definitely the fastest rising, hottest commodity fed in PTC. Life was on the up and up, especially if Summers would come around. Jake had always been a great wrestler, but his mindset didn’t suit me much. He was one of those fighting champions, and I thought that was over rated because all that wrestling made me tired and put me at risk of injury. I didn’t want to run my company from a wheelchair or on crutches. Jake didn’t mind doing the grunt work though to get us the titles so I decided I didn’t much mind being his partner either as long as he picked up some good cheating techniques in the next few weeks I figured we could retain as long as we wanted. It was obvious the fans preferred watching us to a female champion anyhow. And besides, Jake owed me. I got him back into wrestling, and I put the tag strap on his shoulder in his FIRST GCW match. He was going to be making the money necessary to feed his family, and he had to attribute that success to me. I owned Jake, I hope he knew it. Oh, and best of luck Regulators!
