Calling Out For Help

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today After WorldWide went off the air? “Yo, you gonna be okay, man?” Seymour Almasy’s eyes blinked a few times, then came back into focus. Standing over him was the legendary Toad, GCW Hall of Famer and now Head of Security for the company. “Ugggh”.yeah, just give me a minute.” Needless to say, Seymour Almasy was not at his best. Getting your ass kicked 4 on 2 tended to do that. “I thought you knew those guys,” said Toad, as he looked down at Seymour’s battered form. “That’s some way for them to get reacquainted.” Yeah, really. Especially when Seymour had no idea what the fuck they thought they were doing. For once, Seymour Almasy was completely and utterly in the dark. It wasn’t a feeling he held any great affinity for. “Tell me about it. Wait’where are Stevens and Kannon?” “In the back already,” came the reply. “We’ve got medical personnel attending to Stevens. Kannon’s fine. Basically, he saw you two guys get your asses kicked and figured he’d better play dead for a bit. Good call, in my humble opinion.” A much less good call, of course, was Michael Stevens running out to try and save Seymour’s ass. Almasy knew it, too. Why the heck HAD Stevens come to his rescue? Was it that one conversation they’d had, where they said they’d have one another’s backs? GCW pride? Whatever it was, Seymour owed Stevens one. If not for the GCW World Champion, he’d have been? He didn’t especially want to think about it. “You should get the Hell outta here,” Toad advised. “No point in sticking around this place. Get a drink or something.” It wasn’t often that Seymour Almasy took the advice of Toad. He was a rude and crude man on occasion, though Seymour DID rather enjoy the way he made Scott Taylor’s life miserable. That was always fun. Tonight, though, a drink seemed like a very good idea indeed. “Oh! Before you go, take this. Boss told me to give it to you, it’s about next week.” The Final Fantasy sat up slowly, taking the sheet of paper from the Head of Security. His weary eyes scanned the paper for a few seconds, before it fell from nerveless fingers. “Fuck.” Two hours later, McKinney’s Pub? Rum was not a beverage that Seymour imbibed often. The 151 was known to him mostly by reputation, mentioned by wrestlers in locker rooms around the world as their drink of choice. The Final Fantasy wasn’t out to get drunk tonight, but the warming qualities of hard liquor were certainly welcome. “You okay,” he heard the bartender ask. “You look a little down.” Popular question tonight, that one. Almasy sighed in response, then raised his eyes to meet those of the graying bartender, even as the hustle and bustle of the bar continued on around him. “Yeah, I’ll be okay’just had a bad night.” The bartender nodded, and Seymour laughed. Undoubtedly, the man tending bar had heard that statement a few thousand times in his career. “Care to tell me about it?” Seymour thought for a few moments. It was a ludicrous story, really. The tale belonged as the plot of a soap opera. But really, with no one else to talk to, he had to say something. And so, the Final Fantasy engaged in a game of generalities. “You ever get into a huge argument, or fight, or something like that, think it’s over, and have it come back at you worse than ever?” “Of course. Haven’t we all?” Yeah, but did *your* situation involve two vampires, the girl who loves one of them, and the girlfriend of one of the people you absolutely despise? Didn’t think so. Instead of saying that, he merely nodded. “Sucks ass, doesn’t it,” he queried, raising the shot glass to his mouth and taking the contents down in one quick gulp. Seymour shivered slightly, before the warmth spread throughout his body. “Ahhh?” “Rum does the trick every time,” the bartender said, with a knowing smile, before turning off to go take care of another customer. Suitably alone for the time being, Seymour pulled the piece of paper Toad had given him from his pocket, and began turning it over in his hands. “Gauntlet match,” he groused, shaking his head. “And I enter last. Ordinarily that’s a good thing. Assuming I can find someone in GCW to be my partner.” Unfortunately for Seymour, he’d stayed rather to himself over the course of his GCW career. Eva VanLorne was one of the few people he’d met and gotten to know even slightly, and she was injured, presumably out for at least the near future. The only other person who knew was Michael Stevens, and the GCW World Champion was already booked. As if that wasn’t bad enough, it seemed most of the rest of the roster was booked in the goddamned gauntlet, too. “Team Baked, those would be the Herbheads,” he said, with a slight chuckle at his own reference to marijuana as a common healing item in his RPGs. “The Guild’hmmm, maybe I should talk to those two. They play D&D, and I haven’t found a good DM since I got to GCW.” Then came the not so fun parts. “Unite’that’s Josh and Gray Brand, I think. Didn’t talk to them much, if at all in Global.” In fact, the only thing he knew Gray for was taking a liking to the charms (if he could really call them that, considering that most of his contact with her had been anything but charming) of one Dawn McFarlane. It continued getting worse with Future Shock. “A McClancy,” he groaned, shaking his head at the sight of the last name. In UWF, Seymour had been part of the Knightmare Brigade, an organization loosely associated with the Brands and Blaise. And the other members had left him high and dry. Whether he would admit it or not, there was a part of him looking forward to stepping in the ring with Blaise McClancy and settling something the man’s family had finished. Of course, that provided Blaise and his--“wife” Was that it?--partner survived long enough to face him. At the sight of the next team he swore, a rather obscene obscenity that I don’t feel at liberty to transcribe. “Hunting Grounds: Version 2,” he moaned, unable to believe that they’d followed him here seemingly for the sole purpose of making him suffer. His feelings for Vivica Valentine and Virgil Ambrosi were not friendly ones in the least bit. Virgil was the significant other of Shadow. Both had tried to kill him on multiple occasions, although he supposed he really couldn’t blame the male vampire for that. After all, he HAD made out with Lia. And it had been wonderful, up until the point where her hyper-mercurial personality kicked in and she sicced her attack dog of a boyfriend on him. Valentine was another story. She was Christian “Hey! Look at me, I surround myself with pretty girls but don’t do anything with them so I can cover up being a flaming homosexual!” Zenith’s significant other, and Seymour’s feelings towards Zenith were slightly between “fuck off and die” and “fuck off and die, please”. Hell, he wasn’t even mad at the prospect of having to face Rich Rollins, you know, THE PTC GLOBAL CHAMPION, and Jason Dread, mostly because he was simply too annoyed with a pair of simple facts. Fact A: Virgil and Vivica were in the match. Fact B: SEYMOUR HAD NO FUCKING TAG TEAM PARTNER. Which was par for the course, really. Not that it mattered if he found one. Why, Shadow and her gang of vampiric misfit fuckheads were just going to run in and beat the shit out of him four on one, like they tried to do on WorldWide. Which was a shame, really, as he rather liked Dawn, but she didn’t return said emotion. “Fucking everything,” he mumbled, clearly ready to just say the Hell with the evening. “Why can’t they just let whatever silly grudge go?”” ? Suddenly, in that moment, it came to him. He re-read the piece of paper, just to be sure. “SEYMOUR ALMASY MAY PICK ANYONE TO BE HIS PARTNER.” Anyone. Anyone at all. That meant that this silly, this irrational idea’could actually work. Calling the bartender over, and pulling out his wallet, the Final Fantasy made an odd request. “I know you don’t usually sell whole bottles, but could you give me a bottle of your finest scotch?” To show the seriousness of his request, he threw several bills of sizeable denomination on the table. Slowly, the bartender nodded. “Sure, kid.” At that, the Final Fantasy grinned. Now all he had to do was overnight this peace offering tomorrow morning, and see if his erstwhile partner would accept the offer. * * * * * The bottle of Scotch came yesterday around noon, and it was already gone by midnight. I don't understand how he still does this to himself, especially on the eve of his first child being born. I love him with all my heart, but sometimes I just try to put myself in his shoes and wonder. When he looks at me, I can tell he loves me. The way he'll gently rub his hand around my expanding midsection, feeling for a kick from his son. The way he repeatedly tells me how my hazel eyes are the most beautiful he's ever seen. There is honesty in his voice, but I think it only buries the pain he's always drowning away with alcohol. Last night will be nothing but another one of the 'lost days', as he calls them, to add to the overall tally. After losing his match against Cereal Killer and Tessa Windsor last year, he vanished into an alcoholic stupor that would put any normal person into rehab. In fact, it was so bad that he can't even remember the match, he only knows he lost because of what I told him. His biggest match, albeit a loss, wiped away by Bourbon. Sometimes I pray to God, I pray to let him stay away from alcohol when our son is born. If there is one day in his life that he should remember, it should be the birth of his first child. Our little boy, which he repeatedly says will be very important in life. I don't know why he believes our son to carry such a weight, but he's believed it since I first told him of my pregnancy. Occasionally a few strange things happen around these parts, but I just blame it on the small-town mentality of urban legends and gossip. There were whispers of a long-dead Vietnam veteran walking the streets last spring, walking around as a door-to-door alcohol salesman, but I don't recall seeing anyone. Although if he made his way to this house, the salesman sure as hell got his money's worth. The baby is due in less than a month, and I just wish his father would get his life back together. When he was wrestling in the Cruiserweight tournament, he managed to stay away from the alcohol until the very end. Even though he doesn't remember the loss, it never devastated him’it seemed like some extraneous force was pushing him towards the bottle. He visited his grandmothers house for some reason, a place he hadn't gone to in years. Cereal Killer pinned him, and he shrugged it off. On the ride home from the arena, he simply said 'Defeat is part of the sport, babe, it happens to everyone.' And he smiled and ran his hand through my hair. Then, the next morning, I found him asleep on the couch with the empty bottle of Bourbon in his hand. His memory wiped clean, his most prolific match to date. God, if you're listening’get him back into wrestling. His desire for alcohol seemed to diminish when his desire to succeed in the squared circle was his goal in life. Please’I'm begging you, our unborn son is begging you’let his father support his family by succeeding in his passion. I don't care how. I've heard of this Infinite Gauntlet thing, so maybe that. I've heard of other wrestling federations out there, so even if it means moving across the country’please get him back into wrestling. When Aaron is born, I want his father to be sober and to remember it. I want Aaron to admire his father, both as a father figure and as a competitor. I don't want our son to shun him because of his uncontrollable urge to drink his problems away, to bury himself in the bottle. So please, send me a sign. I don't even know if you exist, but please oh please, help me. I don't want to watch my son grow up with an alcoholic father. I don't want to be the wife of an alcoholic, regardless of how true the love actually is. When I become Mrs. Archibald MacGregor, I want to know that our son has a future. That our family will not be torn apart by the desire to drink. Send me something, please. * * * * * Thousands of miles away, Seymour Almasy picked up the telephone. Unerringly, nervous fingers punched out a phone number. The Final Fantasy took a deep breath, before speaking into the reciever. On the other end, Heather Davis picked up the phone. “Hello. This is Seymour Almasy. Is Archibald MacGregor there?”
