Just a Smile: Part 6

24 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24th, 2007 I’m not sure if I should mention this, as I’m still not sure myself what it means. Last night I had a dream about Tessa Windsor, it was a sexual dream. She wore those teal silk panties, waving her every curve in my face. Her sexual body begged for me and I gave into my animalistic urges. For a moment I felt like I was Scott Drake. That I could feel what he felt, taste what he could taste, and sense what he could sense. Our bodies blended as one as we danced the night away under the sheets. That’s when the phone rang. I nervously jumped out of bed, not knowing who would call at such an hour. Rolling over, looking at the illuminated numbers on the clock on the nightstand, I noticed it was three in the afternoon. Those all-nighters really threw off my sense of time. Picking up the phone, I reaching into the pocket of my sleepers to make sure the panties was still there. They were. Rubbing them between my fingers I answered the phone, "hello?" "Rob! How’s it going buddy!" The voice on the other end damn near blasted my ear to deaf. It was no mistaking that voice for Justin Talos?, and by the sound of it, he’s had a few drinks already. The former college buddy of Rob’s was looking for some fun, hoping to once again catch up with his old drinking pal. But I had more pressing issues on my mind than simply getting trashing with my friend. I needed to see her. "Hey look, I would love to hang out with you, I mean I feel bad that I’m in town and we haven’t seen each other much. But the thing is; I’m heading out to see my girlfriend in a bit." That caught Justin’s attention. I had just came to Florida not long ago, how could I already hook up with a woman? The pieces of thread linked in my head and I realized what I just said. "Oh yeah, uh with that. Yeah some crazy shit. You remember that wrestling show we checked out? Yeah we met the chick backstage? Yeah her. I know man, crazy shit, like I said." I let out a sigh, glad that my friend believed my story. The amount of alcohol he had already consumed might have had something to do with that. "Well when I left at the restaurant it was because I bumped into her. She remembered me from the show and we just hit it off. She is just absolutely in love with me, totally digs the brain." My eyes filled with terror as Justin asked the one thing I should have thought about when I told my crazy story. Now Justin wanted to hang out with not just me, but my new girlfriend as well! I highly doubted Scott would be down for that. In fact I doubted if I would even be able to breath after even suggesting such a thing. "Hang out? Uh, we really can’t. We already have plans. Tomorrow night, yeah sorry that’s no good either. I mean we’re pretty busy all week. She wanted to show me around and all, sorry man. But look, I’ll definitely have to hook up with you so we can do something before I leave, promise." Hanging up the phone quickly, I didn’t even wait for Justin to reply to my last comment. Pulling the panties out of my pocket, I brought them up to my face and plunged my nose into them, taking in the sweet aroma. "Ah, I need you." Finally out of the bed, I grabbed a quick shower before packed up my usual stuff; a notebook, a few pens, a pencil, a voice recorder, and my camera phone. I wanted to make sure I could record any moment in nearly any way. With all my things as they should be, I headed out to the motel to stop by Scott and Tessa’s condo. Normally I would just be interested in trying to take down each moment of Scott’s life to try and decipher his thoughts, but this time I couldn’t care less about what Scott thought. No, I just needed to see Tessa. As I shut the door, I once again reached into my pocket to make sure the panties were still there. I would need them as well. At their condo, a twenty minute drive, my nerves continued to build. I could almost sense Scott’s distain for me, as if he seeped into the air and choked me. Could Scott see me? Was he possibly watching me now? Did he know? I laughed nervously, admitted it was all in my head. Looking over the door I gently rapped my knuckles against the wood. "Yeah, what the fuck you want?" My hand shot into my pocket to rub the panties as sweat beads already formed on the corner of my forehead. He knew! Scott just had to know. That look he gave me showed he absolutely had to know. From behind Scott appeared Tessa in all her wondrous glory. "Would you be nice, Scott?" she asked, trying to move him aside. Scott refused to budge through, keeping himself in the frame of the door to block me and Tessa from each other. “He didn’t appreciate her”, I thought, “not like how I appreciates her.” "Nice to this little creep?" Scott asked, giving me a hard brow. "You’ve got a better chance of me being nice to those Blue Collar Boys or Steven Caldera than him." Tessa let out a soft laugh, trying her best to make me feel more comfortable. "Don’t mind him," she told me, "he’s just upset because Rich Rollins returned to GCW." "Damn that Renegade!" Scott yelled out, having a Storm moment. If not for his rather odd personality, I would have thought that to be a scripted moment for relevance. "I can’t stand him. He’s nothing but a washed up has-been riding the wave of past success." Again Tessa let out a laugh, though this time a deeper laugh. Facing her fianc’e, she just had to remind him "says Mr. “I Was Once a Global Champion?" "Well I was." Sadly, at this moment, I had all but been completely forgotten as I stood out in the hall just a few feet from them. "And so was he, Scott!" Tessa quickly fired back to try and snub out the argument before Drake made himself sound any more like a fanboy. Scott though just rolled his eyes, "he beat a girl. Doesn’t count. I mean at least I beat Seymour’oh wait. Scratch that." Though trying to get a laugh out of Tessa with his humor, all he got instead was an elbow to the ribs. "Don’t start with that whole “women can’t wrestle” business again. Let’s not forget that I’ve beaten you a number of times." "That’s because I enjoy you on top" Scott said, flashing her a huge grin. He again received an elbow to his ribs. I interrupted them by clearing my throat, letting them both know I was still standing there. Scott looked to me, showing his annoyance. "You still here?" Tessa pushed Drake aside, making room for me to finally enter into the condo. "I’m so sorry Rob, please come in." My hand still in my pocket, rubbing her panties, I entered into the condo to see even more boxes packed now than last time. Now nearly everything had been packed away into various brown boxes. Tessa tried to move a few of the boxes out of the way for me, but it seems no matter where she moved them, there were still more. "I’m sorry," she said again, "we’re planning to move before the baby comes. No sense in staying here anymore with Legacy gone. And besides, we’d rather be back home with the baby anyways." Though unnerved by the news that they were speeding up their trip back home, I knew I couldn’t lose sight of what I had set out to do today. Turning to Tessa I held up my notepad for her to see. "Uh, Tessa, I was wondering if I would be able to interview you again today. To finish where we left off at." Tessa shot Scott a look. She hadn’t told him about what happened last interview for fear of his temper, but she didn’t want to be put in that situation again. I could tell by the look she was giving him. Scott picked up on the signal and stepped in to my annoyance. He just needed to give us time. I would have helped her stop feeling so uncomfortable. "Nuh uh," Scott told him, cutting between me and Tessa. "We can’t play your little games today. We’re busy. I’ve got a phone conference with GCW about Markus and I’s suspension. We still have to pack. Plus other things, so we’re just too busy. Try back a different time. Like the next time Haley’s Comet passes by." Taking the hint, I knew not even Tessa would speak up for me again. My notepad and pen dropped to my side as I filed them away in my bag. My free hand slipped into my pocket, needing that sense of relief. Though it made Tessa sad to see me hurt like that, she didn’t stop me from leaving. She still, after all, felt uncomfortable around me alone. Maybe it was more pity than sadness. Back at my apartment, I threw my bag onto the bed and let everything shatter about. I never bothered to turn my head to see the direction things flew. I just wanted to be alone right now. No, not alone. I needed to make a phone call. To say Justin was surprised to get a phone call from his buddy so soon would be accurate. Having just been blown off by his former college roommate, he couldn’t believe how chummy I sounded on the phone. But Justin didn’t have any plans for the night and didn’t mind hitting the bar with a buddy who was willing to pick up the tab. To Talos, there was no finer friend than a drinking buddy. True to my word, I kept the drinks coming. And like that, Justin kept drinking them. "You know what" Justin said, leaning over onto me, "you’re a good pal! I like you!" I just laughed, passing another drink on down for him. Shooting it back, Justin finished it off with a heavy victory sigh and a smile on his face. "Yeah, you’re a good friend too Justin. There need to be more good men like you in this world." Justin beamed at the compliment, flexing his muscles. "You ain’t half bad yourself kid. What with the girlfriend and all." Playfully, Justin jabbed me in the shoulder and gave me a thumbs up; though in his state of being inebriated, he accidentally hit me harder than he intended and nearly sent me off my barstool. "Yeah about her," I started, but purposefully stopped. My light comment had been enough to spark even the drunken ears of my companion. "Uh oh, don’t tell me there is trouble in paradise already" Justin told me. I nodded my head, letting my buddy know that there was something wrong. Sobering up for a minute, Justin leaned in toward the bar with me to lend me an ear. He should have lent an eye as well to see the smirk I had hidden on my face. "Well" I began, "do you remember the guy from the wrestling show? The jerk that shoved us aside?" Justin’s blood began to boil, remembering the embarrassing moment of being brushed aside as if he was nothing. It was just like in college. "Well he’s been trying to steal her away from me. The guy is just a complete jackass. He’s the worst kind of lowlife; he’s rude, selfish, self-centered, hot-tempered, and it wouldn’t surprise me to see him hit her." Justin slammed down the drink he had in front of him, spilling its contents all over the bar counter. I was quick to have another sent down to him. "That’s horrible, dude! He sounds just like those frat boys from college." "The ones that always picked on us?" I mentioned, though I knew the answer to the question. I just wanted to remind Justin. The snarl on his face showed that Justin did remember. "I can remember the time they duct taped you to a tree out in the quad one night and poured beer all over you." "Just because I beat them at a drinking contest" he muttered, looking down into his Scotch. Throwing it back, Justin hissed as the alcohol burned the back of his throat. "Yeah, I remember that." I looked to Justin, the smile shining through, not bothering to hide any longer. Justin instantly picked up on it, wondering what was going on in his friend’s head. "Hey Justin," I said, "how about we turn the tides and pull a prank on the frat boy?" The frat boy in question was none other than Scott Drake. "It will be the ultimate prank to end all pranks." Justin could easily get on board for that. All those unpleasant memories of his college years had rushed back to him and now he just wanted any way to try and extract some kind of small revenge. Slamming back another drink, Justin could hardly stay on his bar stool. Using the corner of the bar to keep his balance, Justin turned to me, "so what’s the plan?" "Oh nothing much" I said with a devilish grin. "Just the perfect prank." Moving in closer, Justin tilted his ear toward me, "I’m all ears." Passing another drink to Justin, I started to reveal my “perfect prank”. "Well I’ll lead the stupid frat boy out so that you can jump him. Then, in mask, we’ll mock kidnap the poor sap. Once we get back to your place we’ll tell him what a jerk he’s been and shit. About him being rude, mean, and a complete jackass. Then once he is scared for his life we’ll pull out water guns and pretend to shoot him. He’ll be so scared he’ll piss all over himself." The smile on Justin’s face couldn’t be any broader. "Classic." Sipping the last of the alcohol he looked to me, "just like the good ol? days."
