Who I am Flashback (3 of 3)

8 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/08/05 Ricky is sitting at the kitchen tale silently. He flips through the newspaper that is unfoldeded in front of him too quickly to even be browsing it. Almost in a robotic state. Sean enters the room and sits down at the table beside Ricky, not speaking at first then slowly begins to converse. “Ricky I know you’re hurting. I know that but we need to talk man.” “About?” “What you plan on doing. You have no other family that is any count. You are only 10 years old, the government isn’t going to let you live by yourself. We need to come up with a plan.” “Why” My entire world was wiped out in one night, I’m sure I’m next. This is a part of lifes great plan! Everything that lives dies, I’m no different.” “Ricky, come on man, just help me out here for 10 minutes. I understand that you feel like you have lost everything, but me and mom here are willing to help you get through this?” “What do you mean?” “She will file for custody, if that is ok with you.” “What?” “Don’t look at it like trying to replace anything you have lost, but look at it as friends trying to be there for you. I always promised your brother that if anything happened to him I would look out for you. That time has come in the most unfortunate scenario possible. We don’t want to replace your family Ricky, we just want to be there to help you get through it all.” “What do I have to do?” “All you have to do is show up in court, and state that you agree and want to live with us. I would be lying if I told you that it was a guarantee, but these things normally go pretty smooth if all parties, including the child are in agreement. Assuming the child is old enough to make a legitimate decision, and you are.” “What happens if I don’t” I mean don’t get me wrong I know what it is you all are trying to do, but this shouldn’t be your burden?” “First Ricky it’s no burden. Secondly, if you choose not to, which is definitely your call, then you become property of the state so to speak. You will probably be sent to live in a boys home since most people aren’t looking to adopt a 10 year old. You have no family to speak of’aside form us Ricky.” At this time Alma enters the room. She looks at the 2 sitting at the table and slowly starts to speak. “Ricky, I know we could never replace what you have lost, and we would never try. Please let us try to help you through these times. I don’t want to see you in a home, I will do all I can to make you feel like part of our family I promise you this. I know your parents, and they were some of the kindest, most caring people I have ever met. Sean was best friends with your brother, and I’m sure he cares just as much for you Ricky. Please give it some thought.” Alma turns and leaves the room, as Ricky stays at the table and stares down into the newspaper. “Sean, I have a question.” Ricky starts. “What’s up man?” “What’s in it for you all?” “Nothing. Aside from your well being not a thing. Friends don’t help others because there is something for them to gain. Friends help those in need. That is something you will learn fast and hard Ricky. So please, consider this offer and choose to stay here with us. I will leave you to yourself to take some time to think it through.” “Wait?” Ricky reaches out and grabs the big man’s arm before he can leave the room. “If you are sure it’s ok’I would like that?” “Of course it’s ok Ricky” comes the voice of Alma who has re-entered the room. Alma walks over to the table and gives Ricky a hug, as she cleans up the pile of newspaper in front of him. She gathers it all up and notices a small piece of newspaper in the chair beside Ricky, as she reaches for it Ricky grabs it away. He looks at her who has a puzzled look on her face, as he looks back at the paper. “Can I read it to you?” Ricky asks. Catching Sean’s attention and causing him to take a seat back at the table he nods in approval as Alma takes a seat at the opposite end. Lost. Shane Thunder, also known as Thunder by the fans of wrestling’s CCW Entertainment, has passed away at the age of 22. Shane was diagnosed with malignant mesothelioma, early this year according to his doctor. The cancer was in fact untreatable. Shane was a great asset to the company in which he wrestled, where he was the multiple time Tag Team champion with one, Sean Aitkins, also known as The Supreme Machine. Mr. Thunder’s family was called to his bedside, as he faded away into the afterlife. Shane was taken at such a young age, and never was able to fulfill his life long dream of becoming a Veterinarian. Ricky stops for a moment before looking up. “Shane hated animals?” He continues to read? Shane left behind his parents Rick and Louisa, and younger brother Ricky. The tragedy did not end there for the Thunder family. After leaving the hospital that night, Rick & Louisa were hit by a drunk driver and both pronounced dead at the scene. Young Ricky was not in the vehicle at the time. In a matter of hours, a young man’s life was turned upside down, as his brother and parents were taken from him. Ricky is reported to be staying with some friends of the family, until further arrangements can be made. Please take a moment to reflect on your life, your friends, and mostly your family and be thankful for what you have, for you never know when it may be you, who becomes lost. Ricky sets the paper down on the table still lying underneath his hand, not allowing anyone to take it from him. “Can I keep this clip please?” “Absolutely Ricky, but let me say, that line about “until further arrangements can be made”. These are the arrangements as far as I am concerned.” Alma speaks. “Ricky?” Sean starts “come on, let’s go out and grab some food’little brother?”
