Do Not Enter Pt 2

14 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/14/10 “Holy shit man check this shit out!” Ryan and Ty both stop their snooping around the basement and run towards the sound of their friend’s voice. Mat is standing in a hallway, the room in front of him is illuminated by a single light bulb hanging from the ceiling. It was still bright enough to show the group the exact use for this room so many years ago, or maybe not even that long for all they knew. The boys stared in awe at the old devices that crowded this room. The room smelled of wood and death. It was warm and musty, almost an intense humidity succumbed the room. There were chairs set up with holes in where the hands rested, the white pine soaked red. People , or victims as they seem to have been were nailed to this chair. In the back corner stood a table with chains and pulley. It’s obvious this was a drawing and quartering table. The Autopsy Table was of extreme interest to the boys, yet none of them left the doorway. They all stood in shock, in dismay, and even in a level of curiosity. Ryan was the first to open his mouth. “Guys, look, I ain’t gunna lie, this place is flipping me the fuck out. So can we grab whatever the fuck we came in here for get outta here. Please?” Mat looks at his friend and nods his head in approval. “Yeah man, I think that’s probably a good idea.” Yet even with the fear nipping at their nerves like a cold October wind, they stayed and stared at these….these tools of death. Ellen looks at her friend who is still circling the main room, running her fingers through the mountain of dust taking residence on the mantel and most of the furniture in this larger than most room. Robyn is walking around almost in a daze, almost in a drug induced trance, oblivious to the world around her. Oblivious to the fact that in a matter of seconds she will split open like a pig only to watch herself bleed out like a Koshered calf at a slaughterhouse. Mat, Ryan, and Ty had no clue what was going on inside the house. They had no clue that their friends were being skewered, bludgeoned, and decapitated. In this very moment they forgot all about their friends in the upper floors. Ty made shoulderd Mat, than Ryan and moved his head to the motioning his friends to get the hell out. “Guys, let’s move. Grab something and fucking move.” Ryan and Mat escape from their trance that overtook them and started to scatter throughout the basement to look for something to take. There was a $150 pot at stakes, the best item stolen wins the pot, and $150 was nothing to snicker at. What was going on upstairs however, no person on earth could ever fathom what was going on…no one. Ellen nor Robyn saw it coming, neither heard a thing, and neither felt that awkward presence most feel when they are being watched. Ellen dropped to her knees when she felt a blunt object hit the back of her neck. Had she not moved a foot forward she might have been knocked out cold or even killed, and not dealt with the torture and pain that was awaiting her. This giant man moved with amazing swiftness and quickly grabbed the throat of Robyn and began to squeeze. A silent scream escaped from Robyn, but silent it was. Ellen lied on the ground still trying to comprehend what was happening. The man was of freakish nature in size. He looked 10 feet tall yet she knew that was impossible. His hair was down his midback, dirty and stringy with dreadlocks. The locks where black and almost seemed to not move with his movements. His body was covered with tattoos, most of them devil worship in nature. She does not know how, but she recalls the one that said “Shemhamforesh” which translates to “Hail Satan” in Enochian. His was built like a wrestler, and his face was painted like the dead. She remembers the white background, the raccoon eyes, the black lips, and the upside down cross on his head. Even with all this she could not find it in her to scream. She watched as this giant man squeezed the life out of her new found friend. She watched as he licked her blue face, licked her tongue that was lolling from her mouth, and licked her eyes that seemed to bulge out like discs. This giant let his grip go and Robyn dropped her knees. She put her face into the old shag carpet that was all over the room and started to heave in every last breathe she could. Her coughs were loud and violent and her body shuttered with each one. Her hand found the knee of the this giant and began to pull herself up, still gasping for whatever air she can get. The giant looks over at Ellen and shows a sadistic and evil grin that sends a shutter through her body. The giant blows her a kiss and grabs Robyn by her hair and yanks her to her feet. Robyn is still trying with all her might to breathe. Her eyes are soaked, her face is apple red, and her vision keeps blurring in and out. Ellen watched as this giant pulls her friend up and plunges something deep into her stomach. Robyn’s body twitches as the knife is plunged so deep her in that she can feel the giant’s hand halfway in the opening made by the knife. She can’t scream, only quite mewls are leaking from her crushed voicebox. The pain is so intense that she feels herself growing limp and can feel herself slipping into consciousness, or maybe she’s dying? Ellen let’s out a small yip, almost like a new puppy trying to get his new owner’s attention, but she quickly covers her mouth as the painted giant, teeth gritting, stares over at her. She knows screaming will get her nowhere, so she makes an attempt to stand up and run out the front down but it quickly knocked out by the butt of the knife that just seconds ago was plunged deep into her friend. “Ok guys, got what we need?” Ryan and Ty almost murmur yes. Ryan grabbed an old rusted wood sheathed knife, and he knew he was not going to win, but that was OK, he just wanted to get the fuck out. Ty grabbed a bottle of centuries old scotch from the wine cellar that contained nothing but three bottles of brandy, some wine, and this bottle here. Sure he may not win the money, but tonight when he puts his lips around this bottle he will definitely feel better than the rest. Mat on the other hand, what he found was by far the most disturbing thing they’ve ever seen. “Really guys? A knife and a bottle of liquor? That’s all you can conjur up?” Ryan and Ty both nod, than wait to see what their friend grabbed. Both guys jumped when Mat pulled a human skull. “HAHA I bet you motherfuckers I win this time. This thing has to be atleast 100 years old.” Ryan starts to back up and stops when his head bumps against one of the pillars holding this massive house up. “Mat dude, maybe you should put that thing back.” Ty nods in approval. “Yeah I think that’d be a good idea bro. Put it back, you win.” Mat stands holding the skull that once houses a brain of a livng breathing being that was at the wrong place at the wrong time and met his/her maker here in this house of death. “You fucking serious? This thing is fucking cool!” Ty and Mat heard the thud and both looked in the direction of the sound. Buried deep into the post was an axe, Ryan’s lifeless body already sloping to the ground, blood squirting straight into the air from where his head once resided. His decapitated head, left marred with the very same expression he exhibited seconds before he lost it all, rested on the metal of the axe before falling to the cement with another thud. Ty screamed but his cries were met with a pickaxe through the side of the head. The pick went through his left temple and ended up exiting through the right side of his neck. The pickaxe was elevated and shook violently until Ty’s lifeless corpse slid and slurped off. Mat dropped the skull, pieces of it cracking and shattering as it hits the cement. What stood before him, coming in and out the shadows was the largest man he’s ever seen in his life, his face painted and the rest of his nude body covered in tattoos and soaked in blood. “HELP!!!!!!!” “Your screams fall on deaf ears.” The voice was deep and booming. The bass knocked Mat backwards a step. “What did you do with them? Where the fuck are my friends?” “Dead. Some more creative than others.” Mat begins to sob, tears fall from his eyes as he looks to the ground. A goo like substance is leaking from the hole in Ty’s head,and the blood is still exiting the neck of Ryan. “Who the fuck are you motherfucker? I will fucking kill you.” The giant lets out a silent yet manaical laugh. “WHO THE FUCK ARE YOU??” The giant takes a step forward towards Mat. “Bryan O’Neil.” Mat’s bowels let loose. Urine begins to run down his leg as he’s met on the left cheek with a violent punch that folds him like an accordion. Ellen comes to, her arms and shoulders are burning, and her neck is heavy. She knows she is stripped naked because she doesn’t feel her bra digging under her breasts like she always use to. She has no clue where she is and makes an attempt to pull her hair from her face but she can’t move her arms. She realizes she is bound. She shakes the hair and blows the rest away and staring right at her his the blue and slumped face of Robyn. A smile comes across Ellen’s face, that is until she looks down and see’s an enormous hole in her friend’s stomach and chest. The hole is so large she can see Robyn’s spine. Ellen screams and begins to twist and contort herself. With every twist she hears a rattle. Where the hell is that sound coming from. She moves her stiff neck towards the sky and see’s her arms are bound with chains to some kinda cage. She starts to shake again, a desperate attempt to remove the chains. She feels something slither from her shoulder, lands on the bottom of the cage with a wet smack, followed by a slurp than another hollow wet slap as it falls further. Ellen moves her head and looks at her nude body and notices there is something wrapped around her. She uses her chin to move what’s on her other shoulder and watches as a long tube falls to the bottom of the cage, slips through and lands far below. She realizes two things. She’s caged high above the ground and two, well when she looks at the hole in Robyn agains she realizes she is wearing the inner guts of her former friend. Ellen finally screams and begins to shake the cage violently. The screams are so loud that it wakes Mat below. He is cemented to his neck in a large bathtub. The cement is starting to thicken and become heavy. He can’t move his legs or his arms(he never does figure out that he has no arms or legs). He tries to move but the cement is thick enough to hold him still yet loose enough to allow him smalls gasps of air. A slimey tube like thing falls from above and slaps him in his head. The screams, the rattling, and this fucking thing 2 inches from his face make Mat look up. Directly above him are two caged beings. Mat looks up again and yells, “Who’s there?” Ellen stops her screaming and realizes that someone below them is still alive. “Help! It’s me Ellen. Robyn is dead! Who are you?” “Robyn’s dead? It’s Mat! I can’t help you I’m stuck. Where are we??” “I don’t…..” Ellen was never allowed to finish her sentence becase she feels a sharp pain in her side, followed by another and another. Standing on a platform shoving a spear into the cage was the giant painted man. With each thrust Ellen screams in pain. Mat hears her but is helpless. Blood begins to drip down on him, slowly as first. Small drops like the ones you feel sporadically in the beginning of a rainfall, but they eventually increase, and become thicker and heavier. Ellen is being jabbed at a pace she can’t bear any longer. The story of Bathory runs through her head. Mat is down below showering in her blood, just like the story. Ellen while being jabbed and prodded begins to shake even more violently in attempts to avoid being stabbed further. Her violent jerks are causing the cage to move and sway. While the cage moves from the painted man and avoids one poke, she is stuck deeper as the cage swings closer. The cage must be old or not made efficiently because with one violent jolt it falls from the rafters right on top of the tub. The bottom of the cage lands on Mat’s head splattering his brains all over the place. Ellen feels her legs snap, her shoulder fly out of place and her head bust open. She lies there staring up at the ceiling of this torturous hellacious place she’s in. Her arms are still chained, both wrists are broken, her femur snapped, her collarbone is yanked from her body. Ellen wakes up in the exact same place she was before she passed out. Her body is numb all over, her body is covered in the guts and brains of her friends. Her vision loses its blur and as she focuses she see’s the giant that has put her and her friends through so much hell, that she let’s out such a loud violent scream that she can hear her vocal chords tear. The giant looks down on her and grins again. “I’m not going to kill you.” Ellen begins to sob heavily, the tears burning her facial wounds. “I want you to tell the world about me.”
