The rest... you'll have to do on your own.” “The rest” But what do you mean the rest? What can there possibly be left to do?” Her jade eyes widened, begging him not to leave without giving her answers, as he was wont to do. Vaclav squeezed her shoulder gently. “Don't be concerned about this. After all this struggle, you're finally going to get a reward for your efforts. You've gotten somebody's attention... don't sell yourself short, that's all I can tell you’don't sell yourself short, and this journey will be like any other day for you.” He started to move away from her, and she stood up a little straighter. “What... what's that supposed to mean?” He tucked an errant strand of hair behind his ears as the wind wafted gently through it with a languid, feathery-kind touch. “You owe your friend Ian a favor... take care of that obligation soon, as soon as possible. And Laszlo’I'd be ready for a visit if I were you.” “Why?” Vaclav gave her a sardonic smirk. “I'm sure he wants to congratulate you, especially now that he's met your husband and approves of him. He seemed quite approving of the ceremony, in any case.” “But he wasn't...” Vaclav shook his head. “Not inside the church... at the door. I presume he didn't want to disturb you. Your brother cares for you, Lia, even after all this time absent from him. Don't shut him out. Spending time with him is well worth the investment, in all possible ways.” It was a cryptic statement, and she was left to muse on it for a moment as Vaclav moved still further from her. The wind ruffled his hair once more as he turned back to her. “In any case, rest while you can... you won't have much of an opportunity to do so once you get the call.” And that was all he spoke to her. Suddenly, the earth shifted, and the sky was her canvas again. In their hotel room, Lia frowned very slightly. Neither she nor Virgil woke, but Virgil seemed to sense the disturbance in her countenance. He pulled her closer, and she nuzzled into his chest. The lines on her forehead slowly smoothed until it was once again implacable, all else blunted in comparison to her security in this moment. She sighed deeply, a smile tugging at her lips, even in dreams.
