Interview with a Captor

26 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/26/2005 You injected me with what? Truth serum? What the fuck did you do that for? Great, now I am a labrat..just fucking wonderful. FINE..ask away. You want to know what it is like to be a woman in the wrestling world? Well, I will tell you from first hand experience it has its ups and downs. Respect. Loyalty. Trust. Three things that you need in this career. Respect. Now there is something that is hard for a woman to gain in this career. If you are beautiful, they often forget the drive, talent and sheer skill you may simply because of that pretty face. If I had a dollar for everytime I have been hit on, I would own the world. I am always regarded more as eye candy for ratings than as a formiddable opponent, well mostly. I came in to this federation on the arm of Johnny Lexicon, then not too long after that I became involved with one of the biggest names in GCW, Lestatt Knight. What do you mean did I do for a career move... ..... The answer is no. Lestatt intrigued me. He was mysterious, handsome, and had a penchant for being a bad boy. I have a thing for bad boys, call it a fatal flaw in my character. Unlucky for me, his bad boys ways were deeper than I thought. Even worse, I was on the receiving end of those ways after having fallen in love with him. ..... No, I will not go on a date with you. Are you insane? Does it hurt? Yes it hurts. I fell in love with a man who still has a deep love for another woman and became his personal punching bag, so to speak, as a way for him to inform me it was over. I have nine stitches in my skull courtesy of a microphone to the head.And now I am a pawn in your sick little game with Marauder. I am getting tired of all this. I have a broken heart and a need to get vengence. Do I hate Mary? Hmm, interesting question. The answer is yes....and no. I don't hate her for what Lestatt did to me. No, that is all him. I hate her for coming back and ruining it after she left him like she did. I hate her for having a stronger hold on him. However, at the same time, I have to respect the fact that she was his love and still is. It still doesn't answer the question why he couldn't just tell me and let me go without all the extra pain. What do I plan to do? Nothing...yet. Revenge is a dish best served cold. Lestatt will get his. I promise you that. Not only did he break my heart, but he broke a promise. We made a promise to each other in the beginning, I kept my end of the bargain why couldn't he? A man is only as good as his word and in this case he is no man but more a monster. I almost feel sorry for Mary. Johnny Borealis. Hahaha, now that is a subject I do not mind talking about. He is a snake. He is nothing more than a parasite who infects people with his lies then turns on them like a rabid dog. You know what they do to dogs with rabies? They kill them and cut off their heads for testing. Considering Johnny Borealis is a dog, I am thinking about bringing an ax to my next match with him. Never can be too sure if he has rabies or not. US Title? Yes, I want it. Yes, I WILL have it. And, no, I no longer care how badly I have to hurt him to get it. I have worked far too hard in this wrestling federation to simply stand on the sidelines while an undeserving piece of shiesse walks around with that belt on him. Kind of like Lestatt. Those two are like two peas in a pod. Both of them are snakes. Both of them are untrustworthy, and conniving. They both cheat, lie, and purposely harm others. Personally I think they should become a couple. Niether of them are true champions. They are frauds. They are a stain on GCW's name and I intend to wipe that stain out one way or another. My relationship with the Marauder? We are nothing more than friends. We have definitely had our moments of mistrust, deceit, backstabbing, and subterfuge, but all of that is in the past. I honestly think we are both better people for it today. He has defended me on more than one occasion, despite my quick temper. I trust him. I am loyal to him, and.....I respect him. And he does the same for me. You know...when he gets his hands on you, you are going to wish you never did this, right? Zodiac? What about her? I don't even know the woman on a personal basis. I have heard of her. Johnny has spoken of her on many occasion. I know she is a former champ from another federation. She is a talented wrestler, and if my intuition is correct...Johnny has a huge thing for her. Why do you ask? ....... I see. Curiousity. Well curiousity killed the cat. So tell me how long do you intend to keep me here anyway? Eventually someone will find me you realize and when they do? You will rue the day you crossed my path. ~ SMACK~ SSSSS....that stung a little. Tell me do you always slap a helpless woman around? Is that all you are capable of? Pssh, if so I REALLY feel sorry for you when Lexicon gets his hands on you. Hey...what is that? No, don't do that again. I hate needles. NO! Damn you. Damn you to hell. You son of a ..bi..t.......ZzzzzzZZZZzzz
