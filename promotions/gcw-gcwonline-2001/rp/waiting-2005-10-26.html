Waiting...

26 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/26/2005 The camera fades in and we see 'The Violence' Vince Jones sitting on a bench in the middle of a crowded mall in New York with a rather angry scowl on his face. He is wearing a black New York Yankees baseball cap turned around backwards on his head, his trademark dark shades, a black Rocawear fleece jacket with a white t-shirt and a chain underneath. He is also seen in a pair of baggy black jeans and a pair of black Timbaland boots on as well. The time is about 5:57p.m. Shoppers pass by left and right occupied by their individual shopping needs while taking in the sights around them from stores like EB Games to Victoria's Secret. Every once in awhile the occasional excited wrestling fan passes by and notices Vince Jones sitting there with an astonished look on his or her face. Vince Jones appears totally oblivious to the people that pass him by. He takes a quick glance at his wristwatch and just shakes his head in disappointment as he begins muttering to himself. Vince Jones: Where the hell this chick at, huh? Jasmine said she'd be out in a few. Damnit! Every time we go to the damn mall she be pullin' this crap! How much sh*t is there to be lookin' at in a damn costume shop anyways?? Vince folds his arms across his chest in frustration as the camera fades out. A LITTLE WHILE LATER... The camera fades back in and we see Vince Jones still sitting on the same bench with his Timbaland booted foot propped up on it now and an even angrier scowl across his face. Vince lets out a grunt of frustration as he checks the time on his watch once again. Vince Jones: Aiight! C'mon Jasmine! V ain't got all damn day here! Hurry up and waltz yo ass on out this damn store! Camera fades out once again EVEN LATER... The camera fades back in and we see Vince Jones pacing around back in forth in front of the bench about ready to blow up with rage over his long wait for Jasmine to return. Vince quickly glances down at his watch once again. Vince Jones: Aiight! This sh*t gettin' on my nerves! V been out here foreva and a day now! Where the hell she at? V ain't gonna be standin' around outside this joint all damn day! All of a sudden a young teenager with a Vince Jones t-shirt, blue jeans with a hole in one knee, and a black baseball cap turned backwards on his head passes by and spots Vince Jones. Fan: Hey! Its 'The Violence' Vince Jones! Sweet! Can I get your autograph, man? The fan shoves a pen and Vince Jones GCW trading card in Vince's face. Vince stops in his tracks and looks down at the fan in fury. Vince Jones: WHAT?? Vince snatches the pen and the trading card out of the fan's hands and rips the two apart before his eyes. Vince Jones: Get the hell on kid!! The teenager runs off in fear as Vince Jones turns around and kicks the bench in fury. Some onlookers stop and watch Vince Jones with astonished looks on their faces. Vince finally takes notice of all the people watching his outburst and turns towards the crowd. Vince Jones: Hey! What?? You people ain't seen a man a lil p*ssed off before?? Quit yo damn starin' and get the movin' before V. Jones really goes off! Ain't nothin' to see here! The onlookers immediately lower their heads and try to go back to their normal shopping as Vince Jones just shakes his head and mutters to himself. Vince Jones: Sh*t! Can't get no kinda sense of damn privacy these days without some nosy b*tch mades all up in yo Kool-Aid! Vince Jones turns around and sees his close friend and valet, Jasmine, standing in front of him with a pair of shopping bags on either arm and her arms folded across her chest. Jasmine: V, what was all of that about? Why you makin' such a scene out here? Vince Jones folds his arms across his chest and looks down at her in disappointment. Vince Jones: What you mean what was that all about? What you think that sh*t was all about, huh? V obviously was gettin' frustrated with waitin' around for you to come out that damn costume store! You were up in that joint foreva! Its only Halloween, Jasmine! Jasmine leans back and rolls her eyes. Jasmine: Excuse me?? I was in there forever? Are you kidding me? I was only in there for about fifteen minutes, V, just fifteen minutes! That's it! Vince glances down at his watch which now reads 6:12p.m. He glances back at Jasmine in anger at the fact that she is right. Vince Jones: Yeah! Well, ya know somethin'? That was fifteen minutes too long! Jasmine just shakes her head in disappointment at Vince's cop out of a reply. Jasmine: Uh...yeah whatever, V. You know that I'm right. I didn't have you waiting that long at all. You just don't want to admit it when you're wrong. That's okay though. [smiles at Vince] That's okay. You gotta protect your ego like always and I'm more than fine with that, baby. Jasmine pats Vince on the cheek which really set him off, but he is at a loss of words. Jasmine turns around and begins walking away. Jasmine: Well, I'm ready to go. I'm finished with my shopping for today, V. Vince Jones comes trotting along behind her and eyes her in frustration. Vince Jones: Aight! First off, you was mistaken 'bout somethin'. V is neva wrong and you best be rememberin' that! Jasmine nods in agreement barely paying attention to word of his ranting. Jasmine: Alright. Go on, V. Vince Jones: Second off, V got a match 'gainst this Coyle O' Connor b*tch made at WorldWide this week and V gotta be focused... Jasmine nods once again as she begins looking through her two bags from the costume shop. Jasmine: Yeah, focused is good,V. That's the way to be. Vince Jones: Nah, you ain't followin' V here. Focused ain't enough. V needs to be more focused then he has been the past few weeks. V don't be talkin' like this on the regula', but he gettin' sick and damn tired of this slump he in right now. We both know for damn sure that V. Jones ain't accustomed to comin' up short like this. Jasmine nods Jasmine: Yeah. Coming up short is no good, V, no good at all. Vince Jones: Are you even listenin', Jasmine? This some serious sh*t here and you busy lookin' in yo damn shoppin' bag! Jasmine stops in her tracks and looks up at Vince with loving eyes. Jasmine: I am listening. I've listened to every word you've said; but, you've been saying the same thing for the past few weeks now, V. Vince Jones: What? Whatyou talkin' 'bout? Jasmine: Its true, V. You've been obsessing about having to get that first win in GCW so much the past few weeks that its making you lose your edge. You've been going out there to the ring with the wrong attitude, not the same kind of attitude that's made you who you are in the wrestling world. (Smiles and grabs a Vince's hands) Baby, loosen up a bit, because if you don't you're only gonna bury yourself in failure. Vince nods his head at the words spoken by Jasmine. Vince Jones: Ya know somethin', Jasmine? Jasmine smiles brightly in anticipation of a 'You're right, Jasmine' Jasmine: Yes, V? Vince Jones: Reno was tellin' V the exact same thing just a few days ago. Guess V be underestimatin' the man sometimes. He actually knows what he talkin' 'bout sometimes. Maybe V outta take his words into consideration. Jasmine's face drops, half in disappointment, half in frustration with Vince. Jasmine: (points to herself) What about me? Vince Jones: (in confusion) Huh? What about you? Jasmine: I'm the one that made that suggestion to you a sec ago. Where's my appreciation at, V? C'mon. You gotta give me a lil credit. Vince Jones: Reno said it first. Jasmine: But I...oh, forget it! I just think you need to start having a little more fun sometimes, V. Yeah. I know wrestling is your life and all, but you've gotta find times to just chill out and have a bit of fun. You're way too uptight. Vince Jones: And how do you figga V go 'bout doin' that, huh? This sh*t ain't a game! Its real up in G-C-Dubb! You think any of those fools in the locka room gonna give a damn if they gotta take V. Jones out this game to get by and make it to the top? Hell nah! That's the way it is and that's the way its always gonna be! There ain't no changin' that! V gotta be ontop of his game and ready for anything at all times. Jasmine: See, now there you go again. Remember what I just said? You need to chill a bit. You can worry about all of that other stuff at a later time. Just try and chill and have some fun this week. For starters, you could...dress up for the Halloween edition of WorldWide this week. Jasmine smiles rather sheepishly as she presents Vince with one of the shopping bags she has. Vince lowers the dark shades on her face and just stares at Jasmine with a blank look on his face. He suddenly bursts with laughter. Vince Jones: You've got to be kiddin' here! Dress up in a costume for WorldWide? You mean you actually think V. Jones'd be caught playin' dress up for WorldWide? V got a rep he gotta keep intact here. It ain't happenin'. It ain't V. Jones' style. Jasmine: Oh, c'mon, V. Its just for one night. It'd be fun. I got a costume too. Vince Jones: No Jasmine: Pleeeeease!!! Vince Jones: No, hell nah! Jasmine: Well, will you atleast look at the costume I picked out for you? Jasmine hands Vince the shopping bag. Jasmine: I thought you'd look really cute in it. Vince appears hesitant at first but finally decides to peer in the bag. Suddenly the smile vanishes from his face. Vince Jones: What??? Ah hell nah!!! 'The Violence' Vince Jones is not wearin' this sh*t, not at WorldWide, not anywhere! There no chance in hell you gettin' V in this! What the hell were you even thinkin' when you picked this sh*t out, huh? Jasmine: V, I always used to fantasize about him. Vince looks up at Jasmine with a puzzled expression on his face. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa! You mean to tell V that you've had fantasies 'bout this cat? What the hell you been smokin', huh? Jasmine appears a bit embarrassed by Vince's reaction and folds her arms across her chest. Jasmine: Alright. You know what? That is not funny. That is not funny at all. Why you gonna joke about my fantasies like that, huh? I can't help it. Ever since I was young I always fantasized about him coming to visit me at night, sweeping me off my feet, and whisking me away in the night. Vince Jones: Aight! V ain't tryin' to hear no more of this. That's got to be the most crackish sh*t V's eva been told in his life. Jasmine: See,t hat's not even right. Who do you think you are calling my fantasies crackish? I mean I try to share something intimate with you and all and you laugh. Well, you know what, V? I don't laugh about your fantasies about... Vince Jones immediately cups Jasmine's mouth. Vince Jones: Hey, hey, hey! 'Nuff 'bout that sh*t with yo loud ass mouth, girl! You tryin' give away all V's secrets or somethin' here? We out in public! Damn! People ain't tryin' hear all that! Vince removes his hand from around Jasmine's mouth and she just pouts. Jasmine: V, just think about it. Try it on atleast. You never know it just might grow on you. Vince peers into the bag with a look of disgust on his face at the sight of the costume inside. Vince Jones: Hmph! That crap ain't growin' on V and that's fo damn sho! You best believe that. The camera slowly fades to black as Jasmine and Vince Jones continue on their way to the mall exit.
