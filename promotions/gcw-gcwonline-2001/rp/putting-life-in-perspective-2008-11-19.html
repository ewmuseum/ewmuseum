Putting Life in Perspective

19 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/08 “What do you think” I mean if you’re going to start over you might as well start like this! Am I right?” How many times was he going to “Start Over” Good God he wasn’t getting any younger, While he was only 28 years old there were times when he felt twice his age, when the weather grew cold, when he pushed himself to go that extra mile in the gym, afterwards he’d feel those aches and bruises, yeah there were times when he felt like an old man. “Sony Fifty-Two Inch Ten-Eighty P LCD’High Def! How’s that for a Television?” He didn’t even know what Ten Eighty P, LCD, High Def meant, all he knew was that the Television was Obscenely large, he would have been happier with something a quarter of its size. “As far as the d’cor goes’I had them go for something warm, something that felt like home, the brown really captures the light, makes the whole room almost glow, I wasn’t sure if you’d want Carpet or just bare flooring in some places so I went for both” Home, did he even know the definition of the word? He couldn’t remember the last time he’d been in a place that felt like home, a lifetime of living “On the road” running from City to City, crummy Motel to crummy Motel, grab a cup of coffee from the lobby and hope it was still relatively fresh, fighting against the nagging urge that he’d left something important behind while he endured the check-out process. “Alex?” He blinked and looked over at his Brother, Draven Masters was wearing a pleased grin on his face, he motioned for Alex to follow him. “I said come check out the bathroom” Taking his silence for permission to continue Draven flicked on the light and spread his arms out “I went for the marble look here, spacious, a tub fit for two’or if you’re like me three or four” He took it all in while Draven chuckled, while he was put off by the extravagant feel of the place he did enjoy being clean, he could recall too many times In his life where he’d felt dirty, one of the luxuries he freely allowed himself these days was long hot showers. “And finally the bedroom!” Draven concluded the tour by throwing open the double doors to a lavishly furnished room that again made Alex feel uncomfortable by it’s sheer size Draven moved about the room expounding on various points of interest and details he felt Alex should take notice of. “I had a bed just like that once” Draven informed him “Trust me you’ll never sleep in anything more comfortable” Alex believed him. Finally Draven stopped speaking, Alex looked around the room and the silence began to drag on, finally Draven uttered a sigh his previous enthusiasm bleeding away. “Look Alex, I know you prefer a simpler kind of lifestyle, but I wanted you to have all of this” Draven said spreading his arms out “I want you to have a place where you can relax” he said emphasizing “Relax” and I want you to have a place where you can feel like you’ve come home? Beginning to feel guilty Alex looked around the room, it was lavish to the extreme but it “Did” look relaxing “You probably feel like you don’t deserve it” Draven said “I don’t understand that but I can’t force you to live here, I just want you to give it a chance” Opening his mouth to respond Alex was suddenly cut off by a deeper voice. “Of course he feels like he doesn’t deserve it” the voice rumbled “Alex wouldn’t feel comfortable unless he was living in a Monastery of Self Flagellating Monks” Joshua Freeborn loomed in the doorway, ducking under it as he entered, self proclaimed “Bodyguard” to Alex he was one of the few men Alex could call friend, but one of his more annoying qualities was pressuring Alex to open up when confession was the last thing he wanted. Alex both appreciated and Hated Josh for it. Alex narrowed his eyes at his friend and Josh returned his gaze and crossed his arms not willing to back down an inch, Draven saw the look passing between them and decided it would be best if he left. “If it doesn’t work out let me know” he said to Alex as he moved past Josh and out the door “I’ll set you in something different, but I really think you deserve this Alex” Without waiting for a response he left, Josh and Alex listening as the door opened and shut, and then they were alone. Josh wasted no time “What the hell is wrong with you!” the seven foot man growled Alex let his hands fall to his sides balling them into fists “I don’t deserve this?” he said with as much defiance in his voice “Bullshit” Josh told him “You used to live like this all the time and it never bothered you” Shoving past Josh Alex made his way into the living room “That’s just it Josh! I used to live like this! Martin kept me in the lap of luxury and I didn’t deserve it then!” Josh followed Alex into the living room “I’ve got a newsflash for your dumb ass, Draven Masters is NOT Martin Smalls! He’s not trying to use you! He’s your brother Alex! Did Draven ever once do anything other than try to help you while you were in EPW?” “No?” Alex sighed “He was only there to help, but I?” “You shot him down nine out of ten times he tried to help” Josh finished for him “He’s been reaching out to you for years Alex! And this is another attempt!” he said gesturing around him “Just because you’ve got a huge ass Flat screen TV doesn’t mean you’ve sold your soul to the devil!” Keeping his back turned didn’t make any difference, Josh kept the verbal barrage going, and worst of all Alex knew he was right, the man was always right! “The only thing Draven is guilty of is being a stubborn thick headed Moron when it comes to certain things, a trait I will go as far as to point out you share with him! I swear to god it’s a genetic thing in your family! You two would just march yourselves right over a cliff if I wasn’t around to keep you safe!” “I don’t deserve this Josh!” Alex repeated more loudly “After all the things I’ve done in my life I don’t de?” The blow struck him in the back of the head, the sound of it reached his ears as he flew forward over the couch and rolled into a tangle of limbs on the floor, his head rang from the force of it but he knew Josh must have hit him with an open hand, if he’d hit him with a fist his head would have been split down the middle, anger overriding common sense he leapt to his feet and advanced on Josh, to his surprise his friend pulled his jacket aside showing Alex the Custom made Desert Eagle Pistol he kept there. “I don’t care?” Josh began his voice low and dangerous “If I have to pistol whip you all over YOUR apartment, you are going to listen to what I tell you! I’ve had it with your self doubts and your self pity! It’s time to WAKE UP Alex!” Stopping in his tracks Alex clenched and unclenched his hands at his sides, nails bit into his palms as he struggled to not leap at Josh regardless of the situation not being in his favour. “You are not Payne anymore!” Josh said keeping his defensive posture “I’ve watched you trying to convince yourself of that during your whole tenure in EPW, and it’s become painfully obvious that you now need help figuring that out, it just so happens I have a degree in Psychiatry” Josh held up his fists “These are my tools, and believe me I’m really good at sorting out the messes in peoples heads with them, that little tap I just gave you” That was a consultation, free of charge? Josh let his coat slip closed and Alex breathed a sigh of relief feeling himself begin to relax “But if you continue to be this brain dead stupid then I’m going to get down to some serious work, and I’m going to charge you “Asshole Tax” for my efforts? Josh said letting his hands fall to his sides Rubbing his head Alex found himself chuckling softly, turning he sat down in a leather recliner “Alright Josh I’m listening” Josh nodded, moving over to a stack of unpacked boxes he began to root through them “Is life so bad Alex?” he asked as he looked through one box “You’ve got friends’those belong to Alex Grey, you’ve got an apartment most people would kill for’it belongs to Alex Grey” Finding what he was looking for Josh pulled it out of the box and tossed it across the room, it landed in Alex’s lap “That belongs to Alex Grey”.Not Payne, you earned it Alex!” Looking down at his lap Alex saw the EPW Evolution Championship “Sure EPW is closed but that should serve as a shining example of what Alex Grey can accomplish” Josh said “You should hang that up on your wall right over the TV, but leave plenty of room for the others you’re going to win” Looking down into the polished gold surface of the title Alex had to admit he still felt Pride at having won it. “Look around Alex, Really look around this room and tell me if life is really so bad for you right now” Josh said Looking around the room long and hard Alex couldn’t find a reason to say that it was. “It’s not” he said after a moment of silence “I guess I just needed things to be put in perspective for me” he said rubbing the back of his head “Though next time you could just hit me with a chair’I think it would hurt less” “Don’t tempt me” Josh said zipping up his Jacket and heading for the door “Josh?” Alex called after him “Thank you for?” “Don’t you dare go all “I love you man” on me!” Josh said without turning around “You can make it up to me next Sunday, have plenty of beer in your fridge and don’t plan on watching anything but football, I’ve never watched football on a fifty-two inch screen before, I’m anticipating it to be an experience on par with that of meeting Jesus Christ in person” Laying back in his chair Alex sighed to himself as the door closed, looking down at the Belt in his lap he looked up at the empty space over the TV. The belt would look good up there. Josh was right, it was time to stop feeling sorry for himself and move forward.
