Medieval Unholy Church vs. Cult War, Part 1

26 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/26/2008 In The Morning... [In the back of the Church of the Unholy, in one of the meeting chambers, some of the Church members are chatting about their plans for world domination and unholy desecration. Some of these members include the Demonically Possessed Roid Freak, the Satanic Nun, Evil James, AXEL Action, C.G. Murder, and their leader the demon Belial. Under the cloud of darkness they chat about their plans.] Belial: The dark armies of the North are preparing for the war to end all wars. The war of armageddon. Evil James: So you're saying that we're going to have to fight in some Holy...yet, Unholy War in the near or distant future? AXEL: That doesn't even make sense! How can we fight in an Inholy Holy War? Are we just going to murder and sacrifice everyone in our paths? [Belial has a sickening smile on his face.] Belial: Yes we are. And the war will be started by the Antichrist. [The Church members look very pleased with this development. The Roid Freak, however, is too in love with himself to notice and quickly makes everyone notice too.] Roid Freak: HAVE YOU EVER SEEN THIS MUCH MUSCLE ON A MAN? Evil James: Yes. [Roid Freak starts flexing his muscles.] Roid Freak: MY GOD WHAT A MAN I AM! I WOULD PUT HONEY ALL OVER MYSELF AND LICK IT OFF. I'D CUM ALL OVER MY BODY. I WOULD FUCK MYSELF SO FAST... [Evil James clears his throat. Roid Freak looks over at him and looks displeased.] Evil James: Dude, shut the fuck up you ass ramming fuckstick. Nobody wants to hear that shit. You sound like you're half queer! Roid Freak: YOU HAVE NO IDEA WHAT A REAL MAN IS! Evil James: Dude, I will kick your roid using ass all the way back to the BALCO lab you came from you butt munching ass fuck. AXEL: What the hell is a butt munching ass fuck? [Roid Freak turns and leaves.] Roid Freak: You guys suck... Evil James: Not as much as you on a Saturday night pussy face. AXEL: Ooh!! The fag got BURNED! Belial: Alright my minions. That's enough. We have more pressing matters to attend to later on tonight. However, I do hear that you guys are going to a... [Murder interrupts him.] Murder: A, uh...murdering salon. AXEL: Murdering salons are fucking awesome. Evil James: Got that right. I want to murder some mother fuckers here tonight. AXEL: I'm going to murder a fat man and then use his fat to fry some chicken. Scrump-diddly-umptious! Evil James: Murder, the best recreation in the world. AXEL: Murder is my hero. Murder: We will, uh, kill people. We will just go around and fucking kill and murder and kill some more. I used to LOVE the old days when me and Tioneb would just go around with my brother and we would just fucking murder sons of bitches. Those mother fuckers deserved it. Evil James: I'm SO marking out right now. AXEL: I think I just creamed my pants. [The three of them then leave the room to talk some more about killing people and this leaves Belial alone with just the Satanic Nun. She looks at him and he looks at her.] Satanic Nun: Have you been reading your Satanic Bible? [Belial rolls his eyes.] Belial: We'll talk about this issue later. [Belial turns and leaves the room. The Satanic Nun then looks around and pulls out a cross.] Satanic Nun: Well, there's always masturbation. [The Satanic Nun then walks off with her cross to do some Exorcist style masturbation.] * * * That Night... [The Church of the Unholy is back from their hunting expedition at the murdering salon and are back inside the Church. Evil James and AXEL are pissed at each other. CG Murder and Captain Carnage are just watching as they argue. Devil Dan runs off to go prank some humanoid subcreatures. Belial doesn't seem to pleased with them but Yautja and Xenomorph are on his good side for acquiring the Hand of Satan.] Evil James: You dumb stupid FUCK TWAT! This is not what we had planned! We didn't get to kill anybody and we saw other people do our killing for us! THIS SUCKS! AXEL: Dude, cool your jets! Did you really want to risk our own lives by getting in the middle of that insanity?! I wanted to murder someone, too. But I wasn't going to get in the way of Yautja and Xenomorph. You know how they get when they start murdering...! Evil James: What the fuck were you thinking just sitting there with your thumb up your ass just watching them? AXEL: You know what? FUCK YOU! Next time, you can walk your ass into the middle of slaughter if you want, But when you get murdered, don't expect me to revive you! Evil James: You moron! We have a big problem! [Belial slams his fist on the pulpit and starts walking over to them. The demon of the unholy seems more psychotic than usual. His hooves clack on the floor as he walks over to his minions. He has a newspaper in his hand as well.] Belial: YOU IDIOTS! We are on the verge of becoming the second most unholy organization in all of the world! Another organization has almost stolen our thunder and has been taking forth our unholy brethren from the rankings of our Church and have joined this new organization! [Evil James raises an eyebrow.] Evil James: What are you talking about? How can there be some organization that is more Unholy than us? AXEL: Yeah, I thought that WE were the Unholiest of Unholies...? Belial: What I'm talking about is THIS! [Belial holds out the newspaper and it has the headline "Obama on a roll."] Belial: The Cult of Obama. [Evil James grabs the newspaper and takes a look at it.] Evil James: What the hell is this shit? Belial: This man, Obama, is taking our power hold. His followers, his "Fellowship," if you will, is taking our hold of our power over Unholy people across the nation and is building up his forces even as we speak. AXEL: This Obama character seems like a real jackoff... He should know better than to try and take something that isn't his in the first place. Evil James: Obama seems to be someone who will give us some problems. He is uniting people all across the American nation into his fold and creating his plan for world takeover. He preaches to his flock and now thay are on the verge of a complete world take over. AXEL: The Unholy peoples of the world belong to us! What right does he have to come bursting onto the scene in an attempt to take our people from us? What do we have to do? Hold a fucking debate of our own? We need to stop this asshole before he gets out of hand. Evil James: We need to make a statement that shows that we are the bad ass unholy mother fuckers on this planet. We need to kick some ass. Need to show the world that we still are the most unholy organization on the planet. We need to make a statement and piss some people off to show our unholiness. AXEL: Pissing people off... It's what we do best. But in order to do so, we need propaganda... UNHOLY propaganda... [Belial smiles and nods his head.] Belial: Yes, I like this idea a lot. There is a rumor about there being a giant egg hidden in a cave inside Mt. Fuji in Tokyo. With the destruction of Mecha Streisand and with Mecha Stein and Mecha Dio inside the Church labs for repairs, we need to send in our other monster. Evil James: Which one? The monster we sent to New York is gone into some underwater cavern and we don't know where he is. Damn that video tape footage of him getting out! But at least it's good publicity. And any publicity is good publicity. It'll take all the spotlight off of Obama. AXEL: Indeed! We've got all the publicity we could ever ask for right now, and it's like they say, "The only bad publicity is no publicity." Wait... What about Satanzilla? Evil James: Well we can send Satanzilla. Belial knows how to call him forth from the darkest depths of the abyss. [Evil James and AXEL turn to Belial.] Belial: I can make it happen. The unholy beast shall come forth and unleash his HELL upon Tokyo! Then we shall have the egg and all the treasures locked inside. [Evil James, Belial, and AXEL all are smiling as Yautja and Xenomorph head out of the room and down the hallway toward the Church armory. Captain Carnage quickly follows behind them as Belial is now drawing a pentagram on the floor. Once finished, he starts chanting in the old Hellish verses.] Belial: Nakatu zama de'lial! Homa scisca deniab! Zula maki ata karra no satina monstro! [The lights fade out in the Church and all the candles inside suddenly burst out in red flame! Evil James and AXEL look on in awe as Belial cackles maniacally as the lights come back on. Evil James quickly goes over to the Unholy television set on the side of the room and then pulls it over to the middle of the room. He turns it on and puts on the Japanese news channel so they can watch it. Suddenly, a hole from the abyss opens on the outskirts of Tokyo and the giant beast of Satanzilla appears from the void of Hell! The giant monster has red scaly skin, fins on his back, a big long tail, claws on his hands and feet, and stands forty meters tall. Satanzilla screams out in a primal scream. Satanzilla then breathes atomic fire on the buildings in the city and then starts heading toward Mt. Fuji. However, Tokyo is in the way. Satanzilla starts walking through the city destroying buildings. Back at the Church, Belial is pleased.] Belial: Excellent. Our minion is already on his way. Murder: That things know how to kill people. I need to hang out with him on a Saturday night at a, uh, murdering salon. [Evil James has been thinking about what is going on and has some very innovative thoughts on what is going on.] Evil James: Is it just me or is Obama the Third Antichrist? Belial: You are correct. Evil James: So does this mean we're allied with Christ against the armies of Satan? AXEL: The thought of being allied with Christ sickens me... Belial: Are you insane James? Obama is allied with us but we are not allied with him. Evil James: How the fuck is that possible? Are we against him or not? AXEL: I'm slightly confused... Murder: I would, uh, kill him for five dollars. For ten I will fuck his wife and then kill her. [Evil James and AXEL start laughing over this.] Evil James: This guy fucking rules. AXEL: Indeed, he does. Evil James: But seriously, why are we not allied with Obama? He's the most evil person on the planet. His Cult is taking over MY home country. We need to be an allegiance with him if he is the Antichrist. [Belial shakes his head.] Belial: No. He won't ally with us. He thinks he's the Messiah. He thinks he's ruling the Church and Nation of God. We are the enemies of the Church. Therefore he will be against us even though we know who he is. Evil James: That dumbass. It'll be like 'They Live' before we know it. AXEL: But if we know who he is, wouldn't HE know who he is, too? Belial: He does not know what he is. He would join us if he knew but he is blinded by the thought of his power. Evil James: What about when he starts his war? What should we do? Belial: Nothing, absolutely nothing. Murder: I will do nothing...except for, uh, kill people. Yes man, yes. I will fucking murder the dumb son of a bitch who comes near me in the war of armageddon. AXEL: Yes, murder the bastard! And pretty soon, Tioneb will return, and he can murder with us. He can strangle with electrical cords and smother with pillows. Evil James: I wonder what the rest of us will do? What will our roles be when the end time comes and the shit hits the fan? How will we participate in the end of the world? AXEL: We'll do what we always do, try to take over the... Murder: What we will do is, uh, kill people. Those dumb sons of bitches will deserve it for trying to attack our Church. The mother fuckers. It'll be like the old days when me, Tioneb, and Eidde would just go around and we would fucking murder and kill. Those were the days. Belial: Yes you will. James, however, is on a different path from the rest of us. [Evil James looks a bit puzzled.] Evil James: What do you mean? Belial: All in due time. AXEL: What about me? What about AXEL? Belial: Same goes for you AXEL. AXEL: Oh... Okay. Evil James: So, in the mean time, what do we do? [Belial smiles deviously.] Belial: I've already sent the Unholy Power Rangers and Unholy Ninjas out on their next missions. Evil James: Where did you send them? Belial: To America. AXEL: What are they going to do in America, Lord Belial? Belial: They are going to attack Cult buildings within the country. They want to take us on in our turf we will give them a war. we are the few rare birds that Nostradamus talked about that would stand up to the Cult. But, then again, he said those birds would be extinguished. Evil James: Oh. Well that...sucks. AXEL: Wait, are these the birds that shit in peoples' mouths? Those birds are awesome! Murder: We'll murder all those birds and the cultists too. The fuckers. Those fucking sons of bitches. Belial: All in due time... [Belial looks over at the Hand of Satan sitting on his podium of unholiness.] Belial: All in due time. [They all four turn and stare at the Hand of Satan as the scene fades out.] (To be continued...)
