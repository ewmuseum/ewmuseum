Thus I Will Live Forever

2 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-2-09 I'm not some cartoon supervillain. I thirst for unparalleled power, but I'm pragmatic. I know I won't be here forever. It happened to Mr. Danger. It happened to the Krotzers. They were all on top until I came along. They all were the best at what they did until I came along and did it better, made them obsolete and sent them from the pinnacle of the business to emptying my garbage bin. Right now there's nobody in this business who is better than me. And though you may hate me for it, you also know deep down in your heart that it's true. It's why people who hate my guts keep coming to work for me. They cringe every time they cash a paycheck with my signature on it, yet they keep right on cashing them. Because they know they're in the presence of greatness, and they know there's nowhere they'd rather be than GCW. Yet you cannot label me a power-hungry sociopath, you can't call me naive. Since the moment I opened GCW I have been preparing for the day that someone would take over, someone would come along and do all the things I couldn't do. Some day I will join all those I stepped on and over to get to where I am. But there will be no janitorial work for me. No. Never. There's something that sets me apart from all those like me who had power and lost it. I realize that power is fleeting. I've held it longer than any before me because I am simply better than them at what I do, but no matter how good you are at something, you'll always get surpassed in the end. And my time is coming. So when I look around and take stock of those in my employ who might some day wrest control from me, I realize that there is only one sane thing to do. I've often been accused of attempting to crush those who threaten my power. Nothing could be further from the truth. Vivica J. Valentine, Andy Murray, Jordan White, these people threaten my business, but not my standing. They're not the ones I need to fear. No, when I identified the one who could surpass me, I did something most people in my position lack the resolve to do. I made him an ally. From the moment I saw Jay I knew he was something special. When he battled LeStatt Knight at NC-17 in the match of the year, I was sold. This young man was me, but the me that never got the chance to see his career through to the end. Jay Terror. Steven Caldera version 2.0. Improved, faster, stronger, better. Those with power often forget the basic facts of their humanity, and convince themselves that they are immortal. But true power comes from accepting that the end will come, and when it does, we will choose the manner of its coming. This power that I have will be mine until the bitter end. Anyone who attempts to wrest it from me until the proper hour will find that Steven Caldera is still the best there is. And when the time comes, Jay will find that I will resist his rise with every ounce of spite and malice left in my body. That's the way it works. You can't have a proper revolution without bloodshed. But until then, Jay Terror can learn how to be the best. He can learn how to harness his once-in-a-generation gift. I will give him all my knowledge, all my wisdom, all my power, and in assuming my position as the greatest wrestling mind alive he will ensure that my legacy lives forever. I'm not immortal. I cannot change the ways of nature. But in Jay Terror, I can create myself anew, and thus I will live forever.
