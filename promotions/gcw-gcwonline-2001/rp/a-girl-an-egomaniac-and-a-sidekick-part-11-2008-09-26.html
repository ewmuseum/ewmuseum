A Girl, An Egomaniac, and a Sidekick (part 11)

26 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/26/08 (takes place 9/2002) September 2002 The line at the Dunkin Donuts arena in Providence, Rhode Island seemed almost never-ending. While in reality it was probably a two hour wait at most, it most certainly looked longer, wrapping around three corners of the building. To a normal person it almost felt insane to wait outside for this long on a day where the sky seemed ready to open up at any second. All for what? One little signature, and maybe a minute at most to talk to a person who was pretty much as average as them. “I can’t believe how big this crowd is.” Tessa thought as she peered out from the limousine that had been rented out for her by WIW’s head honcho Jason Chae that dreary September day. “And just think, they’re all here to see you.” Peering back at her, her chauffer for that evening was amused by just how amazed she was. She was clearly new to this. Her eyes grew as wide as those of a child in a candy shop, “Wow!” she mouthed. She had to quickly regain herself as the limo slowed down and came to a halt. Her heart fluttered and automatically her brain cut off any sort of logical thought. She looked to her limo driver with her face questioning if this is where she was supposed to exit. Of course it was. He winked back at her, his thumb sticking high up, “Good luck.” Taking a deep breath in before she planted her white four inch pumps on the concrete, she turned and thanked her driver, then made a giant leap of courage out of the limo and toward the small sea of people waiting for her. “Let’s do this.” Tessa was forever thankful that the walk between her limo and the table they had set up for her to sign autographs was no more than a minutes walking distance away. By now she’d been so overwhelmed by the amount of support this Rhode Island crowd was willing to show her that she felt she might’ve been overcome by her excitement and hit the floor at any second. “You ready for them?” The security guard standing beside the table inquired. She nodded her head, “Let’s get this started.” Seconds after saying that the crowd began to cheer as the wooden barrier was lifted off the ground.It was a short lived relief. It was almost immediately after that some of the cheers turn to jeers and a voice cued up from behind Tessa, “Not going to wait for me?” Tessa’s head quickly turned and her jaw nearly hit the floor as WIW’s World Champion Chris Storm waltzed up to the desk as if it were nothing. Again she has to recompose herself quickly to keep from seeming too unprofessional, “What the hell are you doing here?” she growled in a whisper. The closest thing she had to a nemesis arrogantly grabbed a chair and took a seat right beside her, “I heard this was your first actual autograph session so I asked Chae if I could teach you the ropes. Of course he couldn’t turn down his favorite person.” “Teach me the ropes?” Her voice nearly cracked as she struggled to keep herself from shouting, “How hard can it be to sign my damn name on a paper?” “You’d be surprised.” He heckled, obviously pleasured by her reaction. “Seriously, do I deserve to be punished like this every week?” Furiously, she grabbed one of the many pens Chris had brought along with him, “What have I done wrong to deserve this?” “I don’t know.” Chris shrugged, a giant smile plastered across his face, “Why don’t you ask them?” Tessa peered out of the corner of her eyes towards the large mass of people awaiting the two, “Them?” “Of course. They know you the best.” Signaling for the first set of people to march forward with his left hand, Chris turned to Tessa and played as if he were some kind of wise old man with infinite wisdom about the wrestling business though he was only 25, “Hell, I bet some of them probably know more about you than you know about yourself. They’re creepy like that.” Quite stupidly Tessa let his words set into her head, and only became more overwhelmed by the sea of people waiting. At least she cued him in on her frustration with him, “Just for the record, we still aren’t friends. And chances are we never will be.” “Good.” He sassily mocked back on purpose, “I don’t want to be your friend anyways. Friends never get any action.” She wanted to desperately to reach out and smack him, but before she could do so she looked up to meet the face of a boy who looked to be no older than her eight year old niece. Instantly, Tessa’s motherly instincts kicked in and she forgot all about Chris. “What’s your name?” She questioned the boy as he handed over one the many 8x10 glossy pictures showing Tessa in action inside the ring. This one was from Tessa’s debut match against Vito Winn. She happened to be right in the middle of kicking Vito square in the chin. “Joseph.” The timid little boy responded as he looked up at Tessa with wide brown eyes. Of course, Chris just had to jump in, “Joseph, let me ask you a question.” If his eyes weren’t already wide enough, they only grew wider when the World Champ had called his name. Instantly Joseph’s eyes glittered as the looked over at the championship belt that sat aside Chris on the ground. “Yes?” “Joseph, don’t you think that Tessa needs a new boyfriend.” Joseph stood there speechless, clutching on tightly to the picture that Tessa had handed back to him after signing. “Chris!” Tessa hissed, not approving of this question. This only intimidated the little boy more, who looked down at his feet as if the answer would be written in a dust pile that blew by. “What” I’m being serious here. Joseph, do you think Tessa should come out with me on a date tonight?” “I already”.” Chris slapped a hand across Tessa’s mouth quickly, hoping that Joseph would finally supply an answer. Although not too certain, Joseph finally did, “I guess so”.” “Thank you!” Chris removed his hands from Tessa’s mouth and shot it high up in the sky in victory, “Joseph, you’re my new best friend!” His hand came back down low to give little Joseph a high five. The boy did so, and then looked over at Tessa who did her best to force a smile. “Get this boy a picture with my belt!” Chris ordered the man with the Polaroid camera in hand as he surrenders his belt to faithful little Joseph. Joseph smiles while Tessa lowers her head to disguise the heavy sigh she let out. Clearly this was going to be the longest two hours of her life. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dunkin Donuts Arena 2 Hours later If two hours with a flirtatious Chris Storm was not bad enough for Tessa, the fact that he wanted to hang around more afterwards drove her even more insane. “You still haven’t told me what you’re doing tonight.” He harassed as he followed her towards her limo, both hands buried in the pockets of his baggy navy blue jeans. She did not bother to show him any sort of respect when she answered, “Really it’s not any of your business.” “I don’t see why not.” Unfortunately for Tessa, her smaller legs couldn’t quite carry her as fast as Chris could carry himself, so her chances of speeding up ahead far enough to lose him were slim. “I’ve got more than a handful of reason, unfortunately for you I’m running late for a date so I can’t stick around to list them off. Chris? ears perked up at the mention, “A date?” “Not with you.” Chris sarcastically snapped his finger, “What a shame.” Tessa did not bother to even joke along with the matter. Instead, she responded back with every tinge of annoyance intended, “Not really.” “Not really?” Chris laughed, “Please, a date with me would be much better than all your dates with Erik combined.” Now he had Tessa’s interest. Did he really think he was that much better than Erik? “An hour with you makes me want to blow my head off. Why would a date be any different?” Chris, desperate to make a point, started counting on his fingers the reasons why, “Because I’m more mature, much better looking, and I’ve got much more money than him. Not to mention so many women would kill to be in your position right now. ? Tessa, clearly unimpressed, nearly doubled over laughing, “More mature” Please. And what do I care about other women? I’m in a position where I can PICK who I want to date. Right now, I’m picking Erik.” “So you’re a cougar”.” “What?” Tessa blinked erratically, confused as to what he was leading into. Chris made sure to raise his voice, to call attention towards the two of them, more particularly Tessa, “You hear that guys” Little cougars going on the prowl tonight!” Embarrassed to the point where she lost any kind of contact with her morals, she shouted aloud “WOULD YOU PLEASE JUST SHUT UP?” Satisfied with how riled up he’d gotten her, he smirked as he answered, “Okay cougar!” If she could have killed him right then and there she would have. Instead, she had to resort to quite the opposite, “Stop that! I’m not a damn cougar!” “You’re right, you’re not?” “Thanks?” Before she could finish her sentence, he was back at it again. “You’re a pedophile instead!” This time her anger could not be controlled. She lunged at him claws out, not thinking about just how much bigger than her he was. “I swear to god if you don’t”.” Before she could finish her sentence he had her wrists in his grasps. While he should have been pissed, he could not help but to be completely and utterly amused at just how much he was able to get under her skin. “If I don’t what?” She growled, but didn’t bother to answer this time. Instead of using this to his advantage, Chris lets go of her hands and tries to stifle his laughter. Quite wisely Chris dropped his own hands to his side and did his best to return to a neutral face, “I’m done with the jokes now. I swear.” Angered, Tessa didn’t bother to look up at him as she continued her walk towards her limousine. Despite knowing how angered Tessa was with him at this moment, Chris continued to try and get her attention, “But honestly, I really don’t think you should go out on that date with Erik tonight.” She walked another ten steps or so before he continued his pleading. “He’s using you Tessa. You’re being way too na’ve.” Offended, Tessa turned around and snapped, “You’re not any better! What if I were to go out on a date with you tonight” Chances are halfway through you’d be reaching for things you shouldn’t be touching.” Chris certainly didn’t help his case any when he started off, “Maybe I would, if you came off as that type of woman,” luckily his mind went quickly to work and fixed things, “thing is, you don’t.” For the first time since the two had started talking (well more like bickering) two months ago, Tessa actually saw some concern radiate from the icy blue eyes of Chris Storm. Still, she felt the need to stand her ground, “I’m not going out with you tonight.” “It doesn’t have to be tonight?” This time she cut him off, “Or tomorrow, or the day after that. I’m not interested in you.” “Just one date.” He pleaded sincerely this time. She denied him once more, shaking her head as she responded, “You’ve had one date. In fact, you’ve had more than that. You talked me into joining this place. Then, you harassed the crap out of me daily. Then, you came to my house for another date and showed absolutely no sort of responsibility around my niece. What am I supposed to do, waste my time by giving you a third chance?” Then, the truth broke loose from Chris? mouth, “I’m not worried about that. I just don’t want you going out with Erik tonight.” Her eyebrows perked up slightly after hearing this, but she would not surrender, “He’s not going to hurt me, he’s a good guy.” “Now you’re sounding like your kid.” He stopped right outside her limo as she began to climb in, “This isn’t a matter of good and bad.” “Then what is it?” It took Chris a minute to come up with an answer, “It’s a matter of you being blind to what is going on.” “Then tell me what’s going on.” Finally tired of this back and forth, Chris waved her off as he started to walk in the other direction, “Just forget it. Go out and do your thing.” “Wait”.” It was too late though, Chris had already marched out of earshot. Tessa sighed as she slammed the door shut, “He’s a manipulative piece of shit.” She struggled to convince herself. But it just didn’t seem right. There’s was something about Chris Storm that didn’t sit right with her, something that she was bound to get to the bottom to, just not tonight. Tonight it was time to kick back and relax, then tomorrow she’d put her mind to work. Her mind would not rest until she figured out the enigma that was Chris Storm.
