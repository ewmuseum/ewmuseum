Introducing...

28 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/10 * The cameras flicker to life to show two people, one male, one female, walking along a downtown sidewalk somewhere. They both have ice cream cones, and the woman is holding the leash to a large golden retriever who seems quite fascinated by everything around them, but well-behaved nonetheless. * * The woman has an average body size with well-defined curves. She has short green, spiky hair, green-rimmed eyeglasses, a matching tanktop, jeans, and green sneakers on. The man is skinnier than average, but not supermodel-size. He has slightly longer black hair, that seems to be going in just about every direction but down. He also wears a tanktop, black, and jean shorts. He has tube socks, with two red stripes at the top, that stop halfway up his calves, that fit inside of his black sneakers. * Woman: "So, Tommy, what do you wish to do now?" * Tommy licks his chocolate ice cream, with a child-like smile shining in between licks. He is obviously distracted by the icy, chocolaty goodness in his hand. The woman watches him, with a small smile, as they continue to walk down the sidewalk. * Woman: "You have the mind of a 6-year-old boy, don'tcha?" * He takes one more lick of his ice cream, getting a bit of it on the tip of his long nose, before he glances up to her, not even noticing his dessert-face. She giggles. * Tommy: "What? What did I do, Lollie?!" * She stops walking, and he follows suit. The dog takes this time to sniff around a bit. Lollie winks at Tommy, before she wipes the ice cream off with her finger, then licks it off her hand. He smiles and blushes a bit. * Tommy: "Ooopsy!" * She giggles a bit more, accidentally dropping her vanilla ice cream on the ground! The dog is quick to the scene! * Lollie: "Oh!!!" Tommy: "Aww...." Lollie: "I guess Daisy's getting a treat today, then..." Tommy: "Yup.... sucks to be you!" * Lollie frowns playfully, as Tommy goes back to his ice cream, paying no attention to his female counter-part. He enjoys it selfishly. Daisy looks up, after scarfing down the fallen ice cream, with hopeful eyes of more. Lollie takes of a bite of her cone, before kneeling down besides the golden retriever. The "Green Goddess" pets her golden-furred friend. * Lollie: "Sorry Daisy, but that's all you get!" * Lollie stands back up, and glances over to Toga, who is almost done with his entire cone by now. She smirks, as a muffed sound starts playing from her pocket. The eco-friendly-colored girl reaches into her pocket and pulls out her cell phone. The sound becomes recognizable as "Walla Walla" by The Offspring. She answers it. * Lollie: "Yeh-llo?.... Oh! Hi, Waldo!.... Yeah, he's right here... though you really should get used to calling him Tommy. He doesn't wrestle anymore, so can quit calling him by that ridiculous ringname, Toga......" * Tommy frowns at her calling his old stage-name "ridiculous." She doesn't notice. * Lollie: "You want to speak with him?.... Alright. Here he is." * She passes the phone off to her XY-Chromosomed friend. * Tommy: "Bacon." * Lollie rolls her eyes at the way he answers the phone. * Tommy: "Yessir.... Mmhmm.... Y'don't say?!... Really?!... THAT'S AWESOME!!!.......... No. I'm not listening.... Huh?!" * Lollie smacks Tommy in the back of the head, lightly. He frowns at her, as she gives him a stern look back, telling him to start listening to his friend! She then kneels back down and returns to petting her four-legged-friend, Daisy. * Tommy: "Alright....... Huh? Scouting?!" * Lollie gets a surprised look over her cute-but-nerdy face. Tommy's excitement level seems to be rising. * Tommy: "By-diggity-dumbo, yeah! I'd love to!... I've been meaning to get back into it!... This civilian life is killing me!... I needs ta hunt me some Charlies!" * Lollie looks slightly offended by her cohorts last statement... * Tommy: "Nah... Chaplin. He's hil-LAIRY-ous!... But anyway, yeah, I'll dust off the ol' wrestling boots, that I don't wear!" * Lollie stops petting Daisy and stares at Tommy with a look of disbelief. * Tommy: "Yeah... 8 O'Clock, Sunday? Gotcha! See ya there!" * Thomas hangs up the phone, and hands to back to Lollie, who slowly takes it, as she stands back up. * Lollie: "Wha.... what was that about..?" Tommy: "Wally wanted to rent the Hannah Montana movie." * She stares at him blankly. * Lollie: "Liar." Tommy: "Nuh-uh! That's a great movie!" Lollie: "YOU might think so, but I assure you, he would disagree." Tommy: "So... doesn't make it any less believable!" Lollie: "Actually... it really does." * Tommy shakes his head. * Lollie: "What was that REALLY about, Thomas?" *He looks around frantically. * Tommy: "Um.... fire hydrant." Lollie: "Nope." * The skinny man looks up. * Tommy: "Clouds!" Lollie: "Try again." * He looks right. * Tommy: "Shiny Cars!" Lollie: "Nuh-uh." *He looks left, at a poster in the window of a local shop. * Tommy: "Wrestling...... Errr.. oops.... um... no... not wrestling.......... um........ wrestling!!!..... DAMMIT!!" * He frowns, realizing his ultra-super-secret cover is blown. * Lollie: "Did Waldo mention something about a wrestling scouter?" * Tommy nods his head yes. Defeated. * Lollie: "For what company?" * His voice is soft and quiet. * Tommy: "Global Championship Wrestling..." * Lollie nods slowly. * Lollie: "And you want to go?" * Thomas lets the word slip of out his lips, still ever so softly. * Tommy: "...yes...." * There's a moment of silence. Tommy stares at the concrete below him, as Daisy stares up at him. Lollie is watching them both. * Lollie: "Then do it." * Toga throws his head up, with a twinkle in his eyes and the largest, cheesiest grin on his face, which perfectly shows his crooked and missing teeth. * Toga: "REAAAALLY?!" Lollie: "Yes, Tommy...... but you're taking me with!" * The man with the mind of a child screams like a schoolgirl meeting Edward (or Jacob) for the first time, as he throws his arms around Lollie and gives her a huge hug! She struggles to breathe. * Lollie: "Tom...my..... I... need......... AIR!" * He releases her, then kicks his heels together and starts skipping around in circles around Lollie and Daisy, all while singing some unrecognizable song, that he probably just made up. Daisy seems to be getting excited watching him. * Lollie: "Settle down, Tommy... before Daisy-" * In the middle of her sentence, she is cut off, as Daisy starts chasing after him, effectively wrapping her lease around her master. Tommy seems to pay no attention as he continues to sing and skip around, with a golden ball of fur following him. He glances down at the curb-gutter and stops dead in his tracks. * Toga: "Oooo! Shiny coin!" * He bends over to pick it up, as Daisy slams into him, knocking him over, and tripping Lollie (who was wrapped in the leash)! He glances over to Daisy, who seems a bit disoriented, then to Lollie, who is VERY disoriented, then back to the coin. He picks it up. * Toga: "Yoink!" * With that, the scene fades to black, leaving the audience wondering what's next. *
