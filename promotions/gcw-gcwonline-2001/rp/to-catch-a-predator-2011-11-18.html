''I'm not kidding, okay? This kind of shit just makes her close in. I just… I really need a friend right now, you know? One who doesn't know her. Someone who can help me figure out what I'm doing with my life, no bias. Someone who cares about me without some other motive.'' ''I can do that,'' he nodded. ''You still have my number?'' ''Of course,'' she smiled. ''Even when I think you're a dick, it's nice to have someone I know I can call to watch out for me.'' Ryan shrugged, ''That's just the way I am.'' ''Alright, so I'm gonna hug you and go back to Al now, acting like I just pulled one over on you. Don't be too pissy if she gloats a little, just keep acting kind of pissed off and let us on our way. You'll hear from me in a couple of days.'' Pierce watched from beside Campbell's Cadillac, straining to hear without much success. Maybe if they'd been talking at a normal volume or if they'd been indoors, she could have made something out, but out in the open, she could only wonder. At least she didn't have to keep her face neutral. She let her scowl run free, trying not to stare. That proved even difficult, especially when Amy wound her arms around Ryan, a smile curling across her lips. Hinton returned the hug cautiously; Alex watched the man's eyes flicker down, knew what he was thinking. Her fist balled up tight, a sigh hissing through her nose even as the little redhead extricated herself from the embrace.  Amy led the man back, giving Pierce a brief smile before she glanced back over her shoulder. "So we can go then?" she asked.  Alex's brows came up. "Does this mean that I don't have to walk in a straight line or breathe in a machine to prove to you that I'm sober." "Al..." Campbell sighed. "He's being nice. Can you maybe try for a minute to--" "No, it's fine." Ryan forced a grin. "She... Amy convinced me that maybe I was being a little harsh."  "I see." Pierce set her jaw. "So no sobriety test, no trip to the station?" "Not even a ticket," Amy put in, looking hopefully to Hinton. "Am I right?"  He scratched at the back of his neck. "I guess not. Drive more carefully next time, will you?" His smile stretched a hair with his brief glance to the little redhead. "I'll see you around." Ryan picked up the case, backing away. "You're lucky Amy was around." "I often am." A bare grin touched her lips. "Have a good evening, Officer. Thanks for all your hard work tonight." He grunted, stepping towards his car, a bit of a whistle on his lips. Alex turned, brows lifting. "What did you--"  Amy didn't respond, slipping back into the passenger's seat. "Can we just go, please?" she asked with an exasperated sigh. "I'm fucking exhausted." "All right, all right." Pierce slid in behind the wheel, the car lurching away from the curb. She flicked her eyes to the mirror; Ryan made a U-turn and went in the other direction. "Uhm, Amy?" The passing streetlights illuminated her stark features in flashes of yellow-orange light. "Mind explaining?" ''Maybe when we get home,'' Campbell shrugged. ''O… kay.'' A few seconds of (rather irritated) silence passed before either spoke again. ''Al?'' ''Hm?'' ''So, let's say I was planning to blackmail a cop…'' To say three years with the Spider had changed Amy Campbell would be an understatement.
