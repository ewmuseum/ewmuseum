Born Free

10 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/10 I. Never has such a simple sound caused either man’s heart to beat so fast. That grinding sound of metal digging into wood has both LeStatt and Roberto captured in the moment. Clockwise the revolver spins, fate taking it’s time to decide who gets to start this particular game of life and chance. The spinning slows down to a crawl. The crawl turns into a stop. Fate has made it’s choice. Roberto’s breath is stolen from him as the pistol points directly at him, beckoning him to pick it up and use it. He feels a knot form in his throat and senses LeStatt’s dark eyes fixing on him. Roberto brings his eyes up to meet Knight’s gaze. LeStatt’s grin makes Roberto sick, knowing that this dark half that he created could be moments away from taking everything that he has worked for away … that pisses him off. “Take it.” Knight whispers. “End this.” Roberto extends his hand and grabs a hold of the weapon. The cool metal feels good on his warm skin as he lifts it off the table and brings the revolver closer to his body. He’s surprised by how heavy it feels. Roberto allows his eyes to trail up and down the shiny silver. “Now!” Knight growls, his impatience showing through. “What is your rush?” “What is my rush?” “Yes.” “Mother fucker, my rush is that I have been chained to you for nine god damn years.” Roberto nods his head but does not look back at Knight. “This won’t be the end LeStatt. I will not lose to you here today. I have things to live for, people who care about me. Who need me. You? Well you’re an abomination.” “Fuck you,” Knight’s hands form tight fists while on the tabletop. “When you are taking your last breaths I want you to imagine something for me. I want you to imagine me living your life the way it is suppose to be lived. Picture me fucking your wife for months before dumping her ass to the curb to focus on what is really important … MY future. I’ll leave Mary with that bastard of yours and concentrate on further cementing MY legacy in this profession, in GCW.” Roberto snaps to attention, his eyes locking with Knight’s. “That’s right.” Knight smirks again. “I’m going to kill your marriage. I’m going to destroy this whole perfect existence that you have tried so hard to build. Mary isn‘t fucking important to me Roberto. I‘m more focused on Andy Murray.” Roberto’s anger bursts, he points the .357 at LeStatt. “Don’t you dare touch my family!” “And how will you stop me?” Knight laughs. “By shooting me? We’ve already established that it doesn’t work that way. YOU cannot kill me anymore than I can kill you.” Roberto knows it is the truth but his arm seems frozen in position, pointing the weapon at Knight’s forehead. The sound of Roberto’s teeth grinding together echoes through the empty room. His breathing becomes deeper. The weapon shakes more and more violently until Roberto releases a groan and brings the pistol back to him. He stares at his monster for one more long moment… Then puts the pistol to his head. “I’m going to stop you.” “No,” Knight’s eyes narrow. “You won’t.” Roberto releases one last breath before letting fate do her thing. He pulls the trigger… II. He wants out. I can’t let him. I’m losing my ability to keep things under control. I hardly have any warning any more before he makes an appearance. Not everyone can see it, Joaquin notices it right away. A visit from LeStatt always frightens him. Others only catch glimpses of the monster, perhaps when the light shines on him in just the perfect way. This is the category that my wife, Mary falls into. She may sense that something is not quite right and maybe she catches a quick glimpse when his mask is pulled back in very brief moments but his disguise is still nearly flawless. One thing that I do know is that I feel when he comes but by then it is much too late. Prior to these recent times it would begin as a small, nagging headache. Soon after the pain would trickle down until into my jaws and slowly move to my chest, burning away at my heart. There were precautions that kept him from coming up and showing himself to the world. Most of the time I took them and kept him suppressed. Only letting the monster see the light of day when it was absolutely necessary. But now? It hits me like a fucking freight train. The pain is automatic and it does not give me the luxury of being in one place at a time, no the pain is everywhere and throbs three times as hard as it ever has before. I vomit most times, typically until there is nothing in my stomach and I am dry heaving. I feel as if my veins are going to explode. Then he comes. My monster, My darkness, M y LeStatt. I always seem him. Even now as I look at myself in the mirror of this shit hole of a motel bathroom, those aren’t my blue eyes staring back at me. Those black, beady eyes glare back at me … taunting me, laughing at me. He thinks my weakness is funny, he thinks I cannot stand up to him. He fucking thinks that I am failing at keeping my life in order. FUCK YOU LESTATT! FUCK YOU! It doesn’t help. His smile grows wider. I turn the water on and run my hands under it. The water’s cold, quite the contrast to how hot the rest of my body feels. I cup my hands under the cool water, creating a fleshy drinking gorge and taking all of the liquid into my mouth. I close my eyes, allowing the water to run down my throat. It seems to help, the water doing it’s best to put out the fire that rages inside of me. Doesn’t last long. I open my eyes to check my reflection again, hoping that perhaps my bright blues will look back at me. I’ve never hoped so much to see such a sad image looking back at me in the mirror. My hopes are unrealized however and those black eyes and arrogant smile greet me again. His lips move, the sound isn’t audible but I still know what he says to me. “You can’t be rid of me.” FUC-! The pain hits without notice, just as I knew it would. My hands tremble, my insides burn. I fall to my knees, my strength failing me. My breathing becomes erratic right before the vomit travels from my stomach to my throat and out of my mouth, splattering on the off-white, tiled floor. I hurt. I dry heave. I make a fucking mess of myself. When it’s over, I roll away from the mess that I’ve made and stare up at the ceiling. There’s nothing left to fight. Not now anyways. LeStatt’s here. III. -CLICK- Roberto’s eyes shoot open and look around the room, scanning for some proof that he is, indeed, still alive. Slowly he lowers the pistol from his temple and places it on the table in front of him. He can feel his heart pounding inside of his chest, he cannot remember it ever being so loud. “Your turn.” Roberto slides the weapon across the table, it grinds on the table again until LeStatt’s hand stops it from moving. “So you think this makes you brave?” Knight takes the weapon into his possession. “You think you are now capable of beating me?” “Just shut up and shoot.” LeStatt’s head slightly cocks to one side, a very subtle and very brief smile cracks his stern facial expression. “I don’t think you fully understand what it is that you created Roberto.” “Please,” Roberto shakes his head. “You aren’t difficult to get. You are everything that’s wrong with me. Every dark place, every shadow … every terrible thought I’ve ever had. That’s you.” “Perhaps that is true, very possible in fact. Problem with that thinking? If that is ALL that I am, then why am I still here? You seem to think you have things compartmentalized just fine now correct? Well you can take care of yourself. Why am I still here? Once you got control I should’ve disappeared right? Right?” Roberto rises from his chair and uses his palms to keep him balanced as he leans forward across the table. His blue eyes shine from the candle light. The red flame dances in those eyes. “I am finished listening to you. You talk too much LeStatt, you always have. Your games are nothing more than pathetic attempts at keeping your life going for a little longer. Up until this point? I’ve given in, afraid to really lead my life without this mean streak. To let others suffer so that I can become rich, famous …” “Respected. Feared.” “Yes,” Roberto nods his head in agreement. “I’ve been selfish. I’ve done things, terrible things all because I thought it was the best way.” “Because it IS the best way. It is the ONLY way.” Knight’s eyes look down at the pistol in his hand. He smiles that dark smile again. “I’ll show you.” He brings the gun to his head. “Even destiny and fate are on my side Roberto.” -CLICK- Knight slams the gun on the table and pushes it to the center before removing his grip on it. “You see? I can’t be rid of. It’s natural selection amigo. I’m stronger than you in every possible fashion. Fate does not side with the weak. You created me because you NEEDED me, not the other way around. I can live a life full of greatness. What I have accomplished is nothing compared to what is ahead. Let me free and I will stomp Murray into the ground. Take the World Title BACK.” “Your road ends here.” “Then pick up the gun and let us find out.” Two shots fired, four left. It’s the thought that is constantly playing out through Roberto’s mind. He doesn’t want to die, especially not under these circumstances. He fears for Mary and Joaquin. He also fears for his career. Global Championship Wrestling could be torn down from its foundation the same way his marriage could. “Stop being afraid.” Knight’s voice slices through Roberto’s thoughts, distracting him. “I’m NOT!” “It’s your turn! So either pull the trigger or let me out of this fucking cage!” “Fuck it.” -CLICK- Three shots fired … three left. IV. This is my throne. This is my Kingdom. Roberto’s body is becoming weaker with each passing moment. He’s losing his grip, on me, on his life, on reality. Creating a life is easy isn’t it? Being responsible for that life? Not so easy. Taking a life? Well that’s not easy either. At least not for a typical person. Yes there are people out there who don’t give a shit but the majority of people are just like Roberto, they care too much. He’s grown tired of fighting me back. Now I can feel those shackles of mine loosening and the light at the end of the tunnel is becoming brighter every time Roberto uses energy to resist me. This last one was particularly brutal. The sour taste of vomit is still strong, the acids stinging my throat. My eyes are still watery, my body still weak. So weak in fact that I find it tough to breath, let alone move around. The tiled floor is cool to my cheek even though the rest of my body feels like it is on fire. What a throne. What a Kingdom. It is in me to rule with an iron fist, to make sure people fear me in order to get what it is that I crave. Glory. Roberto has always been too weak to fully understand. I didn’t ask to be created, I wasn’t given a fucking choice. If I had any say in the way my life has gone I would never have been under the thumb of some fucking asshole who constantly feels sorry for himself and blames his own mistakes on everyone else. No, I had no say in this … yet here I am. Now all I want, all I’ve ever fucking wanted was the opportunity to live just as everyone else does. And Roberto hates me for this? He hates that he created a powerful being and now he cannot control it anymore. He played GOD and now he must suffer the consequences. I watch as my hand slowly rolls up tight, creating an open fist. I have to concentrate to get my strength back. It wouldn’t be long until Roberto tried to lock me away again. The time is now! He must learn that he still needs me. That he can’t make it … not this week … without me. In fact, there is a King that needs to be over throne. What a Kingdom indeed. This fucking King is sitting in MY throne. How dare he make a mockery of what I built!? No, fucking Roberto cannot have this body back yet. I won’t fucking allow it! I have a legacy to protect! It’s a goddamn legacy that will flush away if Roberto is allowed to be rid of me. I’ve worked too long and too hard for that to happen. My blood, my tears and my sweat are embedded in everything that is Global Championship Wrestling! THAT is my Kingdom. The World Championship is my fucking throne. I have the chance to right a terrible wrong. Just give me that Roberto! It’s ALL I’ve fucking got! Suddenly the light at the end of the tunnel doesn’t seem so bright. The shackles feel like they are wrapping around my throat. My hands shake and my stomach churns. He’s not going to just lay down. Roberto, that fucking bastard, wants to FIGHT for the first time in his life. But so will I. For my chance to rule … I’ll die fighting. I will win. V. “YOU CAN’T BEAT ME!” Roberto’s voice booms. He throws the pistol down onto the table with authority. A sense of accomplishment washes over him. LeStatt just glares for a moment before leaning back in his chair. He notices something in Roberto that he cannot recall ever seeing at another time. Courage. This war between the two had been completely dependant on Roberto being unsure of himself, unsure of his ability to be successful. In this moment, a monster feels a very real chill run down his spine. “What? No witty comeback LeStatt? No words about my wife or my career? No talk about how you’ll fuck my wife or how without you I’d be nothing more than a curtain jerker in my profession of choice? How about you sit there and lecture me about how destroying people is the only way to get the respect of everyone around me. Why don’t you do me a favor LeStatt? Why don’t you tell me how much of a coward I am!” “GO TO HELL!” Now it’s Roberto’s turn to smile, right before he tosses the pistol back to LeStatt. “Don’t be afraid LeStatt. Remember, fate is still on your side.” Immediately LeStatt begins to clap, applauding Roberto’s new found strength. “Bravo. Bravo.” He claps one last time. “Real impressive that it takes you putting a gun to your head, with your very fucking life on the line for you to actually grow some balls.” “That’s right, MY life. Not yours.” “But let me tell you something Roberto. Let me just tell you that without me, you’d never have broken from that shell of yours. Without me? You’d still be whining about Cindy dying. Maybe dead. You sure as fuck wouldn’t be a multi-millionaire, three time World Champion and a living legend.” “I’ll show you.” Roberto sits back down. “When you’re gone … I’ll flourish.” “When I’m gone? When I’m fucking gone?” Knight lifts the silver beacon of freedom. “I’m not going ANYWHERE!” He pulls the trigger … -CLICK- “I told you.” The Monster and The Creator stare at each other with both knowing and fully understanding that there is still one bullet in that gun and only two more shots to go. Roberto’s turn is next and 50/50 chances are enough to make the sweat flow from his body. Roberto puts his hand out and Knight cautiously hands it over to him. “It’s time to end this Roberto.” “I think so too.” Roberto readjusts his grip on the weapon. “I guess I just have one thing left to say.” “What is that?” . . . -CLICK- 5 shots down … 1 to go. LeStatt Knight’s world has just been turned upside down. Roberto’s trembling hand pulls the pistol away from his head. Knight’s eyes widen, that chill comes back. Roberto looks at Knight. “Go to hell.” LeStatt, for once, is speechless. Never did he figure that fate would choose Roberto. Since he can remember he had felt no fear, only angst. Anxious to break free of the shackles that have bound him to his creator. Knight’s plans had never completely worked, he couldn’t find a way to get rid of Roberto. But he also never believed that Roberto would be smart enough to best him either. His eyes follow the gun as Roberto slides it back to the center of the table. “There is no more running, no more hiding. This is your game LeStatt, it’s time that you finish this.” “I …” Knight clears his throat. “I’ve lost…” “It was inevitable. The bad guy always dies.” In one rush of movement Knight launches the table out of the way and he charges Roberto, latching onto his throat with both of his hands and driving him to the floor. Roberto’s hands grab Knight’s as well as he attempts to alleviate the pressure. “JUST DIE!” Knight roars, the sound coming out like a cry from a wild animal. “I’M BETTER THAN YOU!” Off instinct Roberto brings his knee up and it connects with Knight’s groin. Of course this blow forces LeStatt’s grip to loosen and Roberto flips him off. Swiftly Roberto rolls away and pushes himself up to his feet. Knight is quick to his as well and he charges, crashing into Roberto and driving him into the nearby wall. “It’s MINE! All of it! The fame, fortune, glory! GCW is the house I BUILT! No one will remember Roberto Abitua! They’ll remember LeStatt Knight!” Roberto had never seen this emotion in Knight’s eyes before. For once, those black eyes were not lifeless. It is in this moment that Roberto finally realizes something that he should’ve known all along. As powerful as Knight is and has become … it’s all because Roberto has fed him. All of his fear and all of his insecurities have built Knight to this point. He’s strong but he isn’t without fear. He fears dying just like any other creature. And Roberto is the only one who can kill him. This brings a genuine smile to Roberto’s face. “WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU SMILING AT!?” “This.” Roberto brings a hand up and shoves Knight away with incredible ease. Knight doesn’t know what to do or how to react. Roberto closes in again and this time he drives his right fist into Knight’s face, splitting him open. He follows the blow with a knee to the gut and then another punch to the back of Knight’s head that sends him face first into the dust covered floor. Blood as black as Knight’s heart splatters on the ground beneath him. “I know the secret now.” Roberto circles around, locating the .357 Magnum and picking it up. “It is so clear.” He leans down and grabs Knight by his hair with the free hand and lifts him to the point that he is on his knees. “I will not do it!” Knight screams. “I will not take my life.” “But you will.” Roberto leans down and hands Knight the gun. “You will because you have no free will.” “I can’t.” Roberto kneels down in front of him. “You don’t have a choice. I’m not afraid anymore. I don’t need you to fight my battles. I am ready to be my own man. I’m taking your power … and I‘m taking mine back” Knight brings the pistol up to his face. “What the fuck...” Roberto nods. “Freedom.” LeStatt places the gun into his mouth. “Good bye …” A single tear falls down Knight’s cheek as he shuts his eyes for the last time. He pulls the trigger. BOOM! Blood splatters. Knight’s body crumbles to the floor. To Roberto it seems to all happen in slow motion, almost like it isn’t really happening. Could it really be? After all of these years is LeStatt truly gone? Roberto stares at the fallen body wide eyed. Cautiously he backs up until he comes against the wall and slowly slides down until he is sitting on the floor. His blue eyes lock onto Knight’s … somehow those black eyes, the ones which always appeared lifeless, are even further gone. Like staring into a black hole and a chill runs through his body when the thought of disappearing into that blackness forever creeps in. Roberto slams his eyes shut. “He’s dead. He’s dead. He’s ….” He pauses, his breathing stopping momentarily. “Dead.” The icy blue eyes pop back open. He looks into that nothingness again. Roberto smiles. He chuckles. He laughs. Hysterically. “Ding-Dong,” The laughter echoes through the room. “The witch is dead.” VI. The world is a blurry mess. I taste blood and vomit and I’ve got a splitting headache. I place my hands flat on the concrete in front of me and push my body up until I am on my hands and knees. My muscles feel as if they have not been used in ages. It takes every ounce of strength that I have to get myself to a vertical position. Even when I’m there I feel as if I could fall at any moment. The evening’s events flood my memory bank. Knight wanted free … more than anything. And for what? To get at me? To ruin MY life? I’ve had blood on my hands for a long time now. I’ve done some unspeakable things to people in my life, both personally and professionally. I’ll never be able to make everything all better, there is no magic fix or giant band-aid for life’s fatal wounds. I can only move on. Honestly, I do not completely regret my time with LeStatt Knight. He was everything that I thought that I wanted to be. He was everything I truly BELIEVED that I needed to be. Now, while his methods were too sinister for me I did learn plenty from him. You cannot be afraid to go after what it is that you want. You have to take life and live it no matter what. I’ll always admire his passion for what he did, no matter the morality of it. I’d never have become who I am today without his presence and for that? I am grateful. But now things have to change, my world is different. I do not have to live by his rules or his code anymore. I can just be me, Roberto Abitua … the real man behind the legend. I hold the power to be every bit the legend and the icon that LeStatt helped create and even MORE. I have to take things one step at a time though, as they say, Rome wasn’t built in a day. Soon the time will come to right the wrongs that Knight caused. I can fight to rise above the labels that have been put on me, mostly regarding GCW. I am determined to give them, to show them a man reborn. I will be the kind of the Legend that they truly deserve. But not yet. First things first, I take back what is rightfully mine. From the corner of my eye I see the first sliver of sunlight peaking over from outside of my hotel room window. Dawn has arrived. I cannot remember the last time I actually watched a new day be born. I’ve seen the sun fall from the sky many times in my life … darkness has always been what I’ve felt most comfortable in. I take in the image as if it is for the very first time. It is quite beautiful. It’s in this moment though that another thought hits me. I wish Mary were here to see this too. I run my hand across my chest only stopping it when it is hovering over my heart. I Smile. “It’s time to go home.” I don’t know exactly where things will lead me next. I don’t believe anyone does. Fate doesn’t exist. Life is what you make it.
