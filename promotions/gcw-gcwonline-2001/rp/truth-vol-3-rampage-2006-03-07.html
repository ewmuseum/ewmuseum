TRUTH: Vol. 3 (Rampage)

7 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 Mar 06 I Investigation 26 Feb 06 “You heard what I said mother fucker, on the ground!” Something I had learned from my business with Dominic, people tend to do what you ask them when you have a gun to their heads. In the case of this particular situation right in their mouth. I had been all around Detroit for a couple of days and had learned bits and pieces of what I really wanted to know. Better believe though that on February 26th I was going to know every last damn bit of what I wanted. “Now this is going to go one of two ways my friend.” I cocked the pistol. “One is that you are going to tell me exactly where I can find this David. The Other” Well the details can be varied a bit but it will consist of your fucking brain matter spread all the way across this damn room of yours. Now tell me ? which one will it be?” I slowly pulled the pistol from his mouth and pressed it up against his head. His entire body shivered and his eyes darted back and forth. This asshole was afraid. And in this case? He had better well be. “Okay ? I-I’ll tell you.” When it comes to getting justice for someone that you love how far will you go? I think it is a question that many of us ask ourselves over the course of our lives but it isn’t a question that many of us ever really get the chance to answer. In my lifetime this is the second time that I have gotten to answer this question. My answer by this time is simple. Whatever it takes. “Start talking puto.” “He lives in the suburbs of Detroit, not in the inner city. Every weekend he throws parties for anyone and everyone with a little money and the urge to get high.” “Have you ever seen him?” “Yes.” “WELL!”” “He’s a big mother fucker alright” Got this weird black and red hair.” Now of course it was at this point that something should have triggered inside of me. It was at this point that the smart part of my brain would have kicked in. Of course when it comes to being this emotional you rarely think with that portion of your brain. “What else” Do you know a last name?” “No last name ? He also has a weird nickname that he goes by sometimes. Something that sounds German or some shit like that. I can’t remember it right now. I can give you the address though.” “Then give it to me.” Earlier in this story I spoke of strange twists and turns. Well it was here and now that I was going to take one hell of a turn. When this thuggin? piece of gangster shit handed me that address, things became so clear ? yet again so cloudy. My life just became complicated even if some parts of it made so much more sense now. “Are you fucking kidding me?” “Look at me! What would I ?” I whipped him with my pistol. “You better not be.” I pulled the pistol away from his head and walked away. I had places to go and someone to see. I was after so much, like evidence, justice, revenge, but most importantly? The truth. The truth is never something easy to obtain. Sometimes the truth is stranger than fiction. Sometimes the truth can kill you. In this instance the truth could destroy me and not just physically. Everything I had ever known and cared about was stolen from me by David. Now as I’ve said life does take turns but it is only a matter of time before those twists cause an accident. A Fatal one. So as I headed towards my final destination in search of the truth I had only one thing left to do. Make a phone call. “Hello?” The voice on the other end was raspy, tired, but very familiar. “Hey Amigo I have a bit of a situation. I need your help ?” Then I told him the entire story from front to back just as I have just told you. The man on the other end was one that I knew I could trust with my life. No matter what kind of fucked up shit had went down he was always the constant. He never sugar coated anything and he never lied to me. When I was finished with my story I remember taking in a deep breath. There was quite an awkward silence between us that seemed to last forever, even if it was only a few moments. “Are you serious?” “Yes ? Can I count on you?” “I’ll be there.” “Gracias.” And I hung up. There was nothing left to say. Destiny awaited. Revenge awaited. The TRUTH awaited. II Revelation David met me with a sinister smile that I had never seen from him before. He knew I would come for him and he knew I’d be alone. I did my best to control exactly what I was feeling and not kill him right there. Just like when I turned on Jason Dread last year, the people kept asking me “WHY?”. Ironic isn’t it? That when I had a knife driven through my back it is the same question that I asked to myself. I think we all do. “Roberto, welcome to my humble home.” He stood up and chuckled. “Make yourself at home.” Perhaps it isn’t quite clear at this stage but this Drug King-Pin’s name was David Sandy. Why is that name significant? It is also the real name of one, RaZiel. The man that saved my life, the man that protected me and stood by my side. He hid behind a mask of lies and pretended to be my friend for years and now was the time that I had to find out why. “David ?” I swallowed hard, trying my best to think of the right words. No words were the right words however. Nothing he could say would make me understand. “What have you done?” “Whatever I wanted Roberto, whenever I wanted it. Do you think it was an accident that I found you all of those years ago” Do you think that I did not know what I was doing in making you what you have become? Give me a fucking break. I knew exactly what I was doing. You are and were an asset ? sure you had your rough spots but I had so much money to gain from your success. Indirectly you have helped my business ventures flourish and for that I must thank you.” I was floored, angry, hurt. Pissed the fuck off. “Thank me” You helped destroy my life! You had Damen killed!” WHY!”” “His purpose was fulfilled Roberto.” He said with barely a shrug as if my brother’s life didn’t even matter. This was far from the man I grew up with and trusted. This was an evil man, the devil himself. “With Damen you were never going to reach your full potential. That was not a risk I was keen on taking. But if he was taken from you” Then your anger would take over. You would ride on raw emotions. THEN you could become profitable. Now granted I never knew that you would kill Victor or his men. That was a pleasant surprise. But you see it was exactly THAT fire and rage that attracted me to you in the first place.” “We were nothing to you?” “Oh but that is not true Roberto.” Those dark eyes of his seem to flash with red for just a moment. “You have been EVERYTHING.” “You son of a bitch. I should kill you right fucking now.” “No you shouldn’t Roberto ?” His face went completely emotionless. “Because if you do then you will never know what really happened to your brother.” “HE DIED!” I screamed at the top of my lungs ? although it probably came out as more of a roar. “I WATCHED HIM DIE!” Then he laughs, perhaps the only part of David that I recognized anymore. It was a laugh I had heard many times before when we joked, wrestled, or played games together. It was genuine. Normally this would soothe me but tonight it scared me. “You are wrong amigo. You didn’t watch him die. You couldn’t have.” “And why not?” “Because ? he’s alive.” This would be when I felt the butt of a pistol collide with the back of my head. At first it didn’t really hurt but then again it all happened so fast that it probably just felt like it was in slow motion. The pain shot up and down my body. I tried to fight it but my view of David began to change as I fell forward, slowly losing my vision. I crashed into the carpeted floor, felt the blood rushing down my head. Slowly but surely everything began to go dark. The last thing I remember seeing? That fucking smile. God I hated that fucking smile. III Resolution? So here I sit staring into his eyes again. Eyes that I hadn’t seen in years. Eyes that last time I saw them, were empty, hollow, DEAD. It’s not everyday that you see your brother whom you believed to be dead. Yet here he stood with a hatred in his eyes that I could never imagine. “Damen?” “No Mr. Abitua,” He says while circling the chair I’m sitting in. “Your brother died a long time ago.” “What are you talking about?” “My name is Demonio.” I looked back over at David who was standing in the doorway with his arms folded over his chest. His face was cold and emotionless. It sent a shiver down my spine. BUT at least he wasn’t smiling. “How did you survive?” I say while adjusting my gaze back to my brother. “I saw that bullet go into your head.” “I TOLD YOU!” He growled at me. “My name is Demonio! What I can promise you though is that you will pay for what you did to Damen. He was a good kid. Yet you let him die! For years I have been waiting until I was strong enough to make things right. All who did Damen wrong while he was alive will pay the price. Roberto ? you are simply the first.” “What has he done to you Damen?” I ask ? almost pleading really. “What have you become?” Annoyed He turns and goes over to David, whispering something into his ear. Damen disappears out of the room, leaving me alone with RaZ. Something that normally wouldn’t bother me nearly as much as it does now. “What is wrong with him!”” “Well amigo why don’t you try being in a coma for years from a bullet in the head and see how “THERE” you come out.” David pushes himself off the wall and approaches me again. “I have cared for him since that night. Sure I wanted him dead but that asshole just wouldn’t die. I knew that he was strong but hell not many people can survive that kind of trauma. I mean it was like a page straight out of a Quentin Tarantino movie or something!” “So you play into his fantasy world?” “You have no idea how ruthless he has become Roberto.” David shrugs. “He kills without thought or remorse. He steals, lies, and causes pain like no one I have ever seen. Of course he doesn’t remember anything so regardless of who I tell him harmed “Damen” while he was alive, he will take them out. How do you think I have stayed away from the police for so long?” “He is nothing but a pawn to you.” My eyes meet his again. “He is MY brother!” “He is nothing but a killer now Roberto. Just like the last time, when his usefulness dries up ? I will dispose of him once and for all.” He chuckles and leans down to move closer to my face. “Just like your usefulness is up now. I am sorry Roberto but there will be no Rampage tournament for you, no marriage to Mary, and no more Pay Per View matches. You will be nothing more than a distant memory in a few weeks time.” “There is something that you forgot.” “Oh really” And what would that be?” The look on his face when he heard the shotgun being loaded in the background was priceless. I let a smile form on my lips. The shadowy figure in the background moved up and put the barrel to the back of David’s head. “Hello RaZ.” When he recognized the voice it was even better. A ghost from both of our pasts ? but an unsuspected ally of mine in the present. “Alex!”” David asked. “Well actually YOU can call me Payne.” With all of the strength I could muster I pulled the ropes apart, freeing myself from their restrictions. Although my body was weak and worn, I forced myself to a standing position. “What you forgot David is that just like what you have fed to my brother for all these years ? Roberto died long ago. YOU can call me LeStatt Knight. I am the man you wanted am I not?” “FUCK YOU!” He spit out, venom in his words and fire in his eyes. “Since we are good friends David, I will not shoot you. Neither will Alex. Instead you will burn in this house like Victor did all those years ago. You will burn with your lies and your deceit. Tonight TRUE justice will prevail.” Alex blasted him over the head, knocking the giant man out cold. I look up at Alex. “Where’s Damen?” “Who?” “Fuck.” IV Truth We didn’t find my brother but somehow I knew that I would eventually. He wouldn’t rest until he made me pay for what he believed that I had done. The truth can kill a man. Tonight a piece of me died. A friendship was torn to pieces and the Devil exposed. One thing I did know was that my life would never be the same again. Another thing I knew? I had a lot to live for. My life has been fucked up and twisted but I finally could write my own chapters. The story wasn’t anyone else’s. I still had a legacy to keep up. My success in the ring, one of my only places of peace, could not be taken away. Funny Through all of the chaos that has been my life the two things that seemed to be the most unpredictable have become the things I could count on the most. Wrestling and Payne. So while this story may be over, a new one was just beginning. The End
