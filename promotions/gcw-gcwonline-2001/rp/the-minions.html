Date: Timeless -=Mayhem sits atop a large stone that stands due east of the mansion. This is what he calls his contemplation stone. Most would call it a thinking rock but Mayhem does his thinking all day, throughout the day, and wherever he stands. He sits atop this rock when an idea comes to him and he contemplates on whether or not he should go through with it. Mayhem feels lost, lost in his thoughts. His nightmares during his slumber are filled with the images of Octavian, Jeff Hardy, Stevens, Rollins, Knight, Eva, and Borealis. Those that are at the top, those that everynight are worshipped by either cheers or boos by the millions and millions of viewers. Mayhem use to be on that pedestal, he use to be the boogeyman that feared children and excited only those of sick mind. Now he's middle card against people that are struggling like himself. What happened to Mayhem? He's just referred to as Bryan Or Mayhem, never is the moniker 'The Lunatic' used. Mayhem sits here contemplating his next move, until its made for him.....=- -=Noises startle Mayhem. He looks around trying to place the sound, but it's all around him. He stands up, ready to spring upon his enemy, but it's friend, or should we say friends not foes. First just one, but than 2 more appear, than 3 more, than another and another and finally the last two come from his behind. They all look the same. Black cloaks, pale complexion, bald heads. They all look dismal and beaten. They stand in front of Mayhem and just stare.=- Mayhem:'Who are you?' -=Yet they still stare. Mayhem stares them back, deeply anger, yet deeply confused. His expression doesn't change, but his aura seems too. He's about to approach them when all of sudden in sync they all bow to him, as if they served him in a past life. Mayhem begins to grin, that devilish grin that made him famous years prior. The grin turns into a deep smile.=- Mayhem:'He sent you didn't he? He sent all of you.' -=They all sit back up and stare at their new master, waiting his command.=- Mayhem:'Oblivion starts Thursday at Worldwide, and it begind with the Marauder, but doesn't end there. He's the plan...................'
