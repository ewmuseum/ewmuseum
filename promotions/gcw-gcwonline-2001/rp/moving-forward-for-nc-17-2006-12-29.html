Moving Forward (For NC-17)

29 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 29th 2006 PART ONE “Are you kidding me” Professional wrestling!” Oh and make no mistake, I use the word “professional” VERY lightly in this case.” Coach Tom Ryan. He is very passionate about his job and more importantly the actual sport which his job pertains to. Over the past year I have grown to respect the man even if my ways of showing respect have always been questionable to say the least. “It pays Coach, simple as that.” You could have zapped him with enough volts of electricity to light up The Horseshoe and he wouldn’t have looked anymore shocked than he does now. That look goes from shock to disappointment to pissed off beyond belief in about 1.23 seconds. I’m pretty sure that’s a new world record. “WHAT THE FUCK!” This school brings you in here and gives you an opportunity to be somebody, to gain an education, and to do something that you supposedly LOVE to do and this is how you repay it?” I think I see fire ready to burst from his eyes. “Listen to me you Alex, you don’t want to throw this away! You have a bright future ahead of you.” “Coach I appreciate what you and the University have done for me. I have been a fan of Ohio State since I was a baby but I Ju-? “What you don’t get is that you ARE still a “baby” Alex.” Now this is where I usually run into issues. I respect Coach, I love THE Ohio State University, I absolutely adore wrestling BUT what I DON’T love is someone cutting me off. I don’t care if it’s the President of the United States. “Fuck you Coach.” The words glided off of my tongue so effortlessly that I was almost startled from hearing them. It’s one of those moments where you think to yourself, “Self, did you really just say that?” after you actually speak the words. Plus Coach’s face has turned so red now that I wouldn’t be surprised if someone told me he had been out in the sun for a couple of days. “GET OUT OF HERE!” Coach screams at me. I just smirk back. “WE DON’T NEED ANYONE WITH NO HEART HERE!” I want to yell back maybe send a few insults his way just for fun. I want to tell him how I am the best thing that happened to OSU wrestling since it’s creation. I want to tell him that he should go back to Hofstra and leave my beloved Buckeyes alone. Most of all though? I want to punch him in his fucking mouth. But I will not. I have my reasons for leaving and they have much more to do with money. Well technically it IS for the money but it’s bigger than that. I’m not out to get rich quick or have nice things and fancy cars. Material things can easily be replaced but there are others that can’t. I’m sure that Coach would understand my decision if he knew the truth but I will not tell him. I don’t need anyone’s pity or condolences. “Goodbye Coach, I’ll be sure to write you a letter when I buy my first million dollar pen. I’ll be sure to wait to read about your firing.” And that is that on that. Ohio State Wrestling is now a thing of the past. To save my present I have to look to the future. I can’t lie and say that I’m not excited to see where this new road takes me because I am but at the same time I worry. “Get it together, Alex.” I say to myself. First things first and that is a trip home. PART TWO “ALEX!” My little sister yells out when she opens the door. She immediately lunges forward with her version of a bear hug. “How are you doing?” “Fine Ashley.” I say with a smile. It seems every time I see my baby sister she looks less and less like a “baby”. Sure she’s 14 now but in my eyes she will always be the little girl that always wanted to do what “Big Brother Al” was doing. She gets more beautiful everyday that’s for sure. Her shoulder length bleach blonde hair and fair skin make her bright blue eyes stand out even more than they normally would. I hate to admit it but shes also growing in all the places that women are suppose to. Fucking teenage horn dogs are going to be all over her like white on rice. I’ll kill someone ? and I’m not even kidding. “Where’s dad?” “With Grandma.” Her smile fades and her eyes seem to dim just a tad. It was the typical reaction. “She’s not doing do well.” I look down a long hallway knowing that my Grandmother’s room is at the end. I hate that fucking hallway. “Go on Ash, I’ll come talk to you in a bit.” My heart pounds louder with each step and my stomach twists more and more as I get closer to the door. When I reach the room I swear everyone in the house can hear the thump of my heart hitting my chest. It doesn’t stop me from going inside though, it never does. The room smells of a mix of vanilla and roses, two fragrances that I love under normal circumstances. However when you know the most influential person in your life could very well die in that very room among those very smells it alters your perception a bit. My Dad and Grandmother both shift their heads towards me when they hear the door push open. “Hello son nice to see you.” He says while standing up. “You too Pops.” The silence was more awkward now, then again with him it always was. I didn’t really like the guy, hardly REALLY knew him. “I’ll leave you two alone.” He says and walks by me and out the door. I follow his figure out the door with my eyes, only looking back at my Grandmother when the door closes. Her breathing is heavy, her eyes red, and its obvious she’s in pain. Yet she still smiles at me and just like every other time she has smiled at me before, I feel safe. I move next to her and take the seat my father was at and I grab her by the hand. “How are you feeling Grandma?” As If I didn’t know. “Better now dear, better now.” Her voice was no more than a whisper. In a way it was almost eerie. I hate seeing her this way because it is not the woman that I grew up loving. She was so energetic, caring, and happy. The strongest woman, NO, PERSON! That I have ever known. “Have the doctors-? “The doctors can shove it up their asses.” I can’t help but laugh softly. She still had that fire about her. At least part of her still remained. “I take it they haven’t been back.” “No money.” This is why my decision isn’t a hard one. “Grandma I promise you that you will get the care you need. I am going to make sure of that.” I fight back the tears that begin to form in the corners of my eyes. “I have found a way to make it happen. To have all of this handled.” “How baby?” “Well I quit school.” She begins to say something, no doubt a lecture about getting an education. “PLEASE! Listen first. I am going to use what I have learned so far with wrestling and take it professional.” “Oh Alex,” She lifts a weak hand up and lightly touches my cheek. “Just live your life. I’m old and my life has been lived already. You have so much more ahead of you, don’t blow it because of one old lady.” “Without you, I would have no life.” “Alex?” “I’m going to finalize my contract tomorrow. I’ll be gone a lot but I’ll send letters, call, and get you money as soon as I can.” I get up and hug her as tight as her body will allow me to. “I Love you Grandma.” “I love you too baby.” PART THREE “Welcome to GCW headquarters.” “Thanks, good to be here.” “You may think that this is just some line that we use but Mr. Caldera is actually very excited that you chose to accept our offer. It’s not often that GCW gets homegrown talent, you being from Columbus and all.” “Only good thing about this city is the college.” “Ha well you and Mr. Caldera should get along nice.” “Wait he’s here?” “No he’s overseas promoting NC-17. As soon as you sign today you’ll be flown over as well to get started. You’ll meet him face to face then, he likes meeting all of the new guys as soon as possible.” I didn’t get this corporate jerk-off’s name; well that or I just didn’t care enough to listen. The latter being the most likely. The building was quite impressive, no doubt about that. Seeing the seemingly endless lines of pictures of former and current GCW stars makes me want to get started sooner. “Here we are, come right in.” The office was lavish, complete with pool table, plasma TVs (yes plural), it’s own bathroom and a beautiful view. “Is this Caldera’s?” “No, Caldera’s is much bigger.” He says with a chuckle. “Come sign your soul away.” I move over to the desk, grabbing the pen from his hand. “So does this make Caldera the devil?” I sign. “Depends on who you ask.” He checks my signature quickly and grabs the pen back from me. “Thank you Alejandro and welcome to the team.” “Appreciate it.” Well if I signed my soul away to the devil then that asshole better pay well over the rest of eternity. I don’t have the time to fuck around and neither does my Grandma. Today I’ve taken the first step in saving someone’s life, my Grandmother’s and maybe, just maybe my own as well.
