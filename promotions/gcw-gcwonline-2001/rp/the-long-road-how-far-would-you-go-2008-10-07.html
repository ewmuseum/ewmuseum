The Long Road: How Far Would You Go?

7 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 Oct 08 I remember how dark it was under there, how uncomfortable as well. I almost dozed off a couple of times waiting for my time to move into the light and take care of business. I remember thinking "if only I could see what was happening above!". It was suppose to be quite the spectacle, at least that’s how Steven described it. GCW’s first and only Coliseum Cage Match for the GCW World Heavyweight Title. Jason Dread defending against GCW’s greatest champion of all-time, Octavian. Til then at least? I waited patiently for my chance until it finally came. I pictured it in my head a thousand times, how it would look. I guessed that I’d look like some vicious vampire rising from his grave to take a life or two. Something so frightening that it’d make Octavian piss his pants. A creature so vile and terrifying that the crowd would explode in disgust and fear. But then I realized that the people thought I was there for them, for their pal Jason Dread. They’d cheer me, play right into my hands. I smirked as I clutched the cold steel of the chair in my right hand. My heart was pounding, the excitement of what was to come getting the best of me. I took a deep breath and closed my eyes before driving the steel chair upwards, slicing the canvas like butter and light flooded my dark hole beneath the ring. I heard the crowd begin to murmur, wondering what was happening. I thrashed around with the chair a few more times until the hole was big enough for me to push my way out from. And I rose up, that sinister vampire from his grave ? and the people cheered! The flashes from the thousands of cameras going off at one time blinded me for a moment, luckily neither Dread or Octavian had gotten a glimpse of me yet. Dread was in far too much pain to pay attention and Octavian was engaged, applying the Roman Rapper to Dread, trying to steal the title from him in the process. Jason, my "friend" was in a bad way and any moment he’d finally give in to the pain by either quitting or passing out and regardless of how it ended, I couldn’t allow it to be that way. I had worked far too hard to make things perfect and Octavian winning the title just was NOT part of the fucking plan. I moved up behind them, raised the chair high above my head and crushed Octavian’s head like a god damn grape. Again the crowd roared in approval! Funny how a man’s actions can be seen as heroic only if they are helping someone the public likes. If I had bashed the head of Dread and allowed Octy to take home the title that night they’d have booed my mercilessly. With things back as they should be I backed off, allowing things to play out from there. Dread does as I expected, he took advantage, immediately locking in his own signature submission move, "No Moment". Octavian’s howls of pain were like music to my ears, a beautiful tune that signaled the fact that everything was going according to plan. The grin from behind my mask of black hair was as wide as it had ever been once I saw Octy’s head nodding, confirming the fact that he had nothing left and Jason Dread was the winner. Andrews: Here is your winner? and STILL GCW World Heavyweight Champion. . . JASON DREAD!!!!!! Beautiful ? just beautiful. I waited to move in again until the referee tried to check on Dread and hand him his title. I snagged the title from his hands and shoved him aside, he wasn’t needed anymore. I reached down and helped GCW’s Champion up to his feet, my "pal" was victorious. Again the flashes from thousands and thousands of cameras blind me, the cheers baffle me but at the same time make me even more excited. They played right into my hands, just the way I knew they would. I grabbed Dread’s wrist and lifted his hand into the air! Right before I pulled him in close to me and delivered a Knight Fall, letting his face smash into his own World Title belt. Those cheers I was telling you about? Instantly transformed into something I have been much more comfortable hearing over the years. The infamous chorus of boos. I’d have smiled if I wasn’t so focused. My eyes were locked in but not on Dread. It was on what he now used as a pillow ? the World Title. I kneeled down, ripping the title from beneath him and now find a reason to smile. Dread’s blood now stained the belt. I had been through so much bullshit in my time in GCW that its amazing that I had made it that far. Caldera found countless ways to insult me and my talents, even making sure that I was NOT apart of the first match to crown a new champion when GCW reopened in 2005. Now that I had destroyed everyone, made the United States Title even more prestigious than the World Title and was universally regarded as GCW’s top wrestler he STILL gave the title shot to some fucking reject like Octavian. Well my plan wasn’t going to allow that to happen. No the plan was to see Dread defend and then rip out his god damn heart. The plan was working. And as I stared down at the bloody World Title, I knew that it would one day be mine. Dread couldn’t beat me, nobody could. At Warpath 2005 I sent a message to GCW. Things were about to change. _________________________________ Roberto finished his story and smiled. The memories of his first year in GCW always comforted him, it has been what drives him because he yearns to feel that way again. Roberto, for lack of a better term, is a glory hound ... he needs it like an addict needs his fix. "You did change things." Now, LeStatt Knight is a completely different animal. Some would call him an animal, literally. He also loves glory but his main vice is making people afraid. He feeds off of fear and insecurities, basically he is one sinister son of a bitch. Roberto has always known this, thats why he fought him off for so long. As they say, however, desperate times call for desperate measures. Now Roberto's smile fades, replaced by a look of concern. "But that feels like a lifetime ago now." Roberto shakes his head. "I'm not sure if I can be that guy again." "I know you can, because I know that I can make you that way," Knight takes a quick drag of his cigarette. "You just have to trust me." He exhales, the smoke floating towards Roberto. "I'm trusting you. We wouldn't be here together if I wasn't, but I still have some reservations. I mean, what will you do?" "That's not really the question here. I'll go as far as it takes to get the job done. The real question, that you must ask yourself is ... how far are YOU willing to go?" Knight adjusts himself in the wooden chair, which he has turned backwards, putting his hands on it's back. "Without you wanting it badly enough, I cannot take you where you want to go. Fuck, where I want to go." "I..." Roberto moves around uncomfortably. "I don't really know." LeStatt rolls his black eyes and leans forward in his chair. "Wrong damn answer. It's time you remembered exactly what it takes to be the man at the top. Back then you were just about willing to do anything to win, fuck do you not remember what happened with Dread?" "I remember." Roberto replied quickly. "Well refresh my memory. Maybe talking about it will get you fired up again. How'd you feel going into that match?" "Nervous." ________________________________ A wrestler’s entrance is vital to his success. It is the time that you get to show off some, maybe intimidate your opponent. Up to that point, I had never put much thought or effort into my entrance but now things were different. Sure I’d won the DWA World title against my most hated rival a few years before but this was different. Against Payne I was fueled by rage, I could only concentrate on breaking his face so the realization of going for my first ever World Title never quite came to me. GCW changed all of that because then I wasn’t battling someone I hated, I was battling someone to win the World Title. I was proving the world wrong about me. Butterflies did crazy things inside of my gut, sweat poured from my face and my entire body trembled. Nerves sure got the best of me early that night. Here I was, about to go face to face with Jason Dread for the GCW World Heavyweight Title in an "I Quit" match! Never had the stakes been any higher for me. I remember saying a prayer, hoping God would listen to me for once in my life. My eyes were closed tight. My mind focused. "O’Fortuna" cued up, followed by a round of boos that made the entire arena shake. Everything went dark around me. My nerves heightened, my trembling worsened and my fear grew. I took in a deep breath in an attempt to steady my heart rate. I lifted my head, my closed eyes facing the sky. I could smell the smoke from the fog machine I could faintly see the red lights all around Then ? BOOOOOOOOOM! My eyes popped open when the wall of fire erupted from beneath the stage and my music of choice was at its peak. I clenched my hands into tight fists and I remember smiling. "This is it." I told myself. My chest heaved up and then back down because of one last deep breath and then I stepped through the curtains, stepped forward towards my destiny. Andrews: Introducing first, the challenger. From Mexico City, Mexico, weighing in at two-hundred eighty pounds. . . LeStatt Knight!! The intensity of the crowd was breath taking. To be honest, I’ve always told anyone that would listen that they don’t mean a damn thing to me but that is not the truth really. Where would I be without them? Would LeStatt Knight even matter? Even if I don’t care if they like me or not, I care if they react to me or not. If the fans didn’t give a shit about me then LeStatt Knight would be in the junk pile with Khris Krowe. There’d be no legacy. Pete: There's no doubt Jason Dread has his hands full in this one, JB. Knight is on a mission, and he's more determined than ever. We've known this moment has been coming for a long time, and tonight is the night LeStatt Knight gets his shot at the GCW title. After all this time, after all these months of dominance, can LeStatt Knight take that last step into history and become the champion? I, personally, hope Jason Dread burns him alive and spits on the ashes. The line that had made quite the impression on me. Pete had no clue when speaking those words how ironic they would prove to be. Fire would be the most memorable thing of this night ? but not quite the way he wished it. I took my time getting to and inside of the ring. Part of me wanted to make Dread sweat and another part needed the extra time to keep myself together. I couldn’t afford to come out flat or tentative, it wasn’t the time for bullshit half assing. LeStatt Knight NEEDED the World Championship. Then I dropped to a knee to wait for the one who’d go to war with me. I didn’t have to wait long. "JUST GOT WICKED." Kicked in. The fans cheered. Dread appeared. My nerves vanished, replaced by the hunger than has stayed with me since I first touched the World Title. It’s never been about the fame or the fortune, money really doesn’t interest me. I’ve always wanted to be the best, to look down at everyone else from my perch atop the fucking mountain. And I knew I was the best and seeing Dread and his fake smirk walking down the aisle with MY World Title just made me even hungrier. The fact that he chose to not look me in the eyes and concentrate on the crowd? Made me want to rip his head off. I felt myself getting irritated, angry. But it was exactly what he wanted. Dread’s little mind games almost got to me. I took a step back and took it all in. When I realized what his game was, I smiled ? it was such a pathetic plan. Funny really, did he honestly believe that pretending to care more about the crowd than the man inside the ring would make me think that he was care free? That he wasn’t afraid? That he didn’t want to destroy me? That he didn’t know that he was losing? Maybe? But then he locked eyes with me and they told the story that he was too afraid to admit. He tried to look strong but I knew better. Even as he came and stood nose to nose with me, I knew it was all an act. The crowd’s cheers would only motivate him for so long before the realization that I was just better than him in every single possible way really set in. He wasn’t the champion, he was merely a bump in the road. Yes he was afraid of ME and I knew it? Even when he punched me square in the face. _________________________________ "And he should have been afraid of you!" Knight yells out. "Every person that you work with should be afraid of you. Andy Murray should be at home right now crying himself to sleep because he has to face you in the one match that he has no advantages. Do you think he is doing those things?" "Probably not." "And why not?" "Because I haven't made him." "Exactly," Knight tosses his cigarette to the floor near his right boot and smashes it. "You've changed, your plan has changed. You haven't been dead set of achieving perfection in a long time. You let Steven Caldera jerk your chain and throw you into meaningless match after meaningless match. You've lost to more people than I care to count ... it's about time you MAKE them understand just who you are." "I know. You're right, it's just that I can't..." "Stop right there. Never say you can't, alright? You can and you WILL. The first step in taking back what is rightfully yours is to ensure that YOU know that you're good enough. If you can't see the truth then what chance do you have against Andy Murray? Or 29 other sumbitches that will be going after the same thing you are at Dangerous Games?" Roberto stands up, obviously not enjoying what he is hearing but not because Knight is insulting him. Because he knows what he is saying is true. "We don't have alot of time." Roberto turns to look Knight in the eyes. "Make me believe." LeStatt smirks. "Well I need you to do something for me." "Which is?" "Take me to the end of your match with Jason Dread." "Why?" "I think you know why." Roberto nods and takes in a deep breath... ___________________________________ The pain was immense. The No Moment sure does hurt like a bitch. Even though my eyes watered, I could tell that the entire crowd was on their feet just waiting to get a glimpse of something they thought they would never see, LeStatt Knight quitting. I did not realize it until I would watch the replay days later but I was screaming in agony; yes Jason Dread made me sound like a bitch. But it didn’t matter. No Moment or not, I wasn’t going to fucking tap out or say "I Quit." He’d have had to kill me and guess what? There is no man that has what it takes to make me quit ever again. Dread wrenched back further, the pain even more excruciating, my howls of pain becoming even louder. Then a miracle happened, Dread lost his grip and tumbled to the mat, his bad ankle giving out on him. My body felt like it was on fire but there was no way I was going to let things end that way, never was quitting part of the plan. I checked inside and reaffirmed that the hunger was still present and stronger than ever before. How could I deny that hunger? I pressed my hands to the mat and tried to push my body up but I didn’t need to do it myself, Jason Dread, weak ankle and all managed to get up first and pull me to my feet. We’ve talked before about there being moments in your life when you have to stand up and become the man you were meant to be. Well this was that moment for me ? Dread, foolish as he was tried to toss me into an exposed turnbuckle. Even as it was happening I knew he was too hurt to finish it. I may have been locked in his favorite move and been through hell prior to that move but I had two good ankles to battle on. So I reversed it. Now if I had anything left inside of me before, I was fucking drained after tossing Dread into the turnbuckle. I used so much force that I basically dove forward. And the ring collapsed under us. In all honesty, it frightened me how powerful I could be. It was then that I finally saw what I was capable of. I had always known that I was powerful, cunning and smart .. but fuck if I knew that I could beat The Incredible Hulk in an arm wrestling match. I caught Dread, limp and lifeless, in the corner of my eye. I knew it was time to act, time to seize my moment. Barely able to stand, I crawled down at an angle towards Dread. How bad he was hurt didn’t register in me until I knelt beside him and lifted him into my arms. The Villain and The Hero The crowd became unusually silent as the two of us shared in this moment. I allowed my mind to wander, thinking about what to do next. I had a plan of course, but for one instant I wasn’t sure if I should follow it. Was I really willing to go that route? I smiled when I made my decision. I carried Dread around the ring, much as I’ve carried GCW for all these years, and placed him against a ring post. Those eyes that once oozed with false hope and machismo now couldn’t hide is true feelings anymore. The scent of his fear was enough to rile me up even more, make me stronger! As I wrapped a broken ring rope around his body, the fear grew ever stronger. When Dread couldn’t escape, I took a few steps back to admire my work. Dread’s eyes searched my own, looking for answers to questions that he had. But I assure you that all he found was ? something dark. I shot him a wink and began to search for what I knew would end this entire ordeal. The infamous black canister was just where I had left it, along with the lighter that would be quite the weapon. "This is it, Jason. The moment of truth. Just how serious am I? Just how far am I willing to go? Is your "friend" really willing to KILL you for the GCW title? You'd better ask yourself if you're really sure I won't do this!" I remember the words like I spoke them just yesterday. I also remember the eerie silence from the crowd and the stinging of my nostrils as I doused the GCW Champion with gasoline. And everyone remembers the small flame from the silver lighter. It truly is remarkable, the survival instincts of man. Dread was dead to the world until that tiny flame came out. Once he had it in his sights however, Dread’s eyes widened and his muscles tightened. Through the pain and agony he must have felt, Dread began to thrash about to get free. But soon he found that it was no good, he wasn’t escaping this fate. "This is it! If you REALLY think I'm still your friend, then I won't do this. But I'm telling you right now that I will set you on fire right now if you don't say the words!" Silence? His eyes lowered. Tears formed in the corners. He swallowed hard. "I?" He looks back up to me, pure hatred in his eyes. "I Quit!" Destiny had finally found me. LeStatt Knight was the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Many questions still loomed over me but none that needed answering right then. It was my time, my moment and nothing could take that away from me. GCW was under my control and I knew exactly what had to be done. Everything went according to plan. BUT there is one question, it really is the ONLY question. Would I? Jason Dread obviously believed so? Would I have burned someone alive to become the World Champion? Would I? ________________________________ "I know the answer to that question." LeStatt says, interrupting Roberto's story. "I know you better than you know yourself." "You think so?" Roberto says with a bit of irritation in his voice. "Then whats the truth?" "No way in hell would you have thrown that lighter on him. As heartless as you try to pretend to be on-camera, you're nothing like that for real. It's all a mirage." Roberto places his hands on his hips and tilts his head so that he is looking straight up into the ceiling. "The truth is that I didn't even know what I was doing." Roberto's head drops down and he stares straight ahead. "I needed that win and I figured no sane person would take the chance that I wouldn't do it. I didn't even both to contemplate what I would do if he refused to quit still." LeStatt now stands up from his spot in his chair. Slowly he moves towards Roberto, only 3 paces separating them. "Well either way, things are different this time around. Andy Murray is not Jason Dread." "He doesn't even compare." "Perhaps, but that's not what I mean." Knight inches closer. "People have seen your tricks, they know what you will or will not do. You've shown your cards and now the other players will call your bluff every damn time." "They know nothing." "No, you're in denial." Knight comes face to face with Roberto, his black eyes searching Roberto's icy blues. "You want the truth? Well here it is. You can't beat Murray the way you beat Dread. Andy Murray is older, wiser than Dread was. He's talented and very confident that you cannot beat him." Roberto sighs and drops his gaze for only a moment before Knight reaches up and grabs Roberto by the chin, forcing him to stare into his eyes. "DO NOT FALTER!" Knight snarls. "To win you have go above and beyond. You must be willing to throw the lighter and burn these mother fuckers until there is nothing left but ash and teeth. You have to be willing to listen to Andy Murray's screams as the fire engulfs him. You'll have to stomach the smell of burning flesh and you will have to live with the monster that you'd become." The two stare at each other, Roberto's blue eyes wide and bright. Slowly, Roberto's lips curl and form a devious smile. In a few short moments his entire body shifts, somehow he looks more confident. "I want this LeStatt. I am tired of being looked down at, especially by Jay Terror, Jordan White, Chris Bagwell, Andy Murray and that bitch Vivica Valentine." Knight backs off "So ... how far would you go Roberto?" Roberto's smile grows wider "I'm going to tear Andy Murray's fucking spine out." LeStatt Knight throws his head back and laughs a maniacal laugh, something that comes off more as a cackle than anything else. "WorldWide 100." Knight shakes his head back and forth. "Let's make history."
