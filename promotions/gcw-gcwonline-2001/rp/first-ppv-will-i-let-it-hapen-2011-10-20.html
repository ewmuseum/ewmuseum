First PPV.... Will I let it Hapen

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/11 [ WorldWide 154... ] The last bit of popcorn had just been thrown away, the cold beers had all been drank, the crowd had enjoyed the show. Everyone who was able to had left the building except a couple of sound technicians that were still cleaning up. Cameron Garret had once again had a chance to become a known superstar, however once again failed miserably. Playing the roll of a baby face who was chasing his dream had com so easy to him, that's who he really was. However Cameron was not only getting angry but was starting to get very disappointed in himself. Sulking around the corner Cameron was not looking to speak or interact with anyone, h was aware that was part of his contract but going into only his second week as a professional wrestler he was still not ready to handle it. His face showed defeat, his face showed the anger however there is one thing his face showed DETERMINATION. Cameron was not ready to give up, he had just begun and he started off on the wrong foot, that was all. [ Two Days Prior to Rampage... ] Five more reps, push yourself just five more. Okay that's it just one more. Great work! Cameron's mind was busy racing what he wanted more then ever was to be a professional wrestler, he wanted to be somebody. That chance had come, that chance might almost be gone if he does not step up his game soon, that is why we find him here in the gym at 11:00 at night. "I know my opponents, I know their lifestyles. They are out, they are enjoying the town. This is where I need to be different, this is where I need to be leaps and bounds a ahead of them." Cameron wiped the sweat off his brow with the towel near by. He went back to the free weight rack and was trying to get his momentum going, he had none currently. Grabbing the first set of 35 lb weights he could he started doing a couple hammer curls. Not really focusing on the weight but the repetition and the proper form is what will put him over his opponents. Knowing that the only reason Curr beat him the first time around was because he wasn't ready. He was out of shape, he was a giddy little school girl to be in the ring for the first time. All of that will be different this time, so he told himself. He knew that only one thing mattered, his drive. He spent the last couple of nights and days since World Wide 154 where he lost to Brad Cruz thinking. That's all he did was think of what he was doing wrong, what had he done right? I mean think of it he has yet to win a match in GCW, yet they decided to keep him on board. He was doing something right, he was still wanted. Better then that they had awarded him with a match he never expected. Being on the dark card was one thing but being the opening match for Rampage was another. Hell they even through a chance at a title, whether it be contendership or it be a title match it didn't much matter it was a chance. [ Day of Rampage ] The music played thru his headset, mind over matter is what he kept telling himself. It didn’t matter that his opponents had more skill, more experience, hell these two might even deserve it more. The one thing I have going for me is that I WANT IT MORE. Tonight it may happen as long as I want it to happen. Now I only question will I let it happen.....
