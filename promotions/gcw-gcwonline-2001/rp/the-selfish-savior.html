The Selfish Savior

undated

Date: Today We didn't know her name. I've sat with her for two days now, while I wasn't gathering the dead or working at the sandbags. Bryan Mayhem, Lestatt Knight, Mike Savige, Johnny Lexicon... They all seemed so far away from here, they all lived their lives sheltered from all of this. And praise them their ignorance, because ignorance is bliss and they are truly the blessed ones. Fighters and warriors they may be, none of them should have to hold a little girls hand while she coughs so hard she may lose her lungs. I fished her out of a house two days ago, stricken sick and practically dead already.I've only prolonged her agony. There was nowhere else for her to go, there was no-one else to care for her. We could only assume her father had died protecting his house from looters, but I can never be sure. All we know is that he is dead with two shots in the chest, his wife, her mother, with one in the head. One in the head. From behind. Tragedy descends all around and people turn in on each other like predators. The strong destroy the weak and survive, natural selection is a bitch. She settles and I wipe a cloth across her forehead, tuck the covers to her neck and whisper a short prayer. The tent fades away quickly, replaced by the moon, the all seeing moon set within its mosaic of stars. I could rage at that moon. Scream at the heavens and chastise God for his lack of compassion. But what is compassion, could it be to let the little girl die as she was ment to, too diseased to be sent to Eva, doing what she could to help. More than what people would expect from her. Everybody but me. A wet nose finds my palm and sniffs furiously until I drag my hand across velvet ears and a scruffy head. I'd have never found the girl if not for the stranded pooch on the roof. Maybe it would have been better I collected the dog and left instead of searching, Eva had told me to do just that. Nobody could be alive down there. She'd repeated that a few times, even the moment before her face went slack and I handed a pair of skinny legs through the hole I'd made... Looking back at that tent tears my heart open, gazing into my dirty palms could almost bring a tear to my eyes. The dead these hands have carted, so many of them black, so many of them simply too poor to go anywhere else. They struggle and they fight every day to survive the evils of man and in the end it is God's own devices that would take them. Limitless mercy. Even in the divine scheme, the ends bear justice to the means. I grip my shovel like a weapon and assault the earth for its careless betrayel. It takes me a long time to dig it deep enough, but I save on size. She's not very big. But pulling up heaps of wet dirt gives me plenty of time to think. Plenty of time to plan. As far away as the entire world might seem right now, it will be alot closer tomorrow night. I stopped researching my opponents when I took the mask. It gave me an insight on things they would assume I know, I became reliant upon it, the advantage can turn sour though. Because once you know somebody, you underestimate them. In that respect you could say I've known the major figures of the last two years pretty damn well. Lestatt Knight, Eva Van Lorne, Jordan White, even Trey Tremendous. It's a mistake I hope never to make again. Bryan Mayhem. If anybody can sum up a gimmick in a name better than me or Mike it's him. I don't assume to know anything about him except that he might just beat me. The dice fall as they will, I just get into that ring and I do what it is I do best. I may be out here helping a lost soul find peace, but it's self gratification, it's not who I am. I am The Marauder. I live on the high, the agony of defeat is only bitter sweet in comparison. Down here, elbow deep in the sewage of life, I can be something more for just a few people. Down here I can be remembered by a precious few for all the right reasons. I back away from the grave. Wrapping Sasha's rosearry around my dirty hand twice I plunge into the dimly lit den of death and gather the poor creature in my arms. She's not warm anymore. I wonder if she'll remember me, I hope she does, I will always remember her cherubic face and it may always twist my stomache this way. I kiss her chilled forehead. The sheets hide her from sight as I lower the bundle into the blackness. The dog watches as I shovel dirt. Respectfully seated. I enjoy his company even though I can't tell his breed, maybe that's why I like him so much. I stake the site with a cross and a prayer. 'She died with her eyes closed.' This one deserves to get in. When I die, I know my own will be open. What I do now and in the future I do not do in the name of redemption, I do not plan to be saved. My reasons are far more selfish than that. But if granted a request for the work, I'd ask the gates be unlocked for just this one, so at least one person in heaven might remember my name. The name that I whisper into the cross dangling from my clenched fist. Sasha's cross. She wore this cross through so much, the beatings, the prostitution, and the drugs. She gave it to me with a promise she wouldn't need it anymore. A kiss on the cheek, a hug, and she told me she would never forget me. I didn't know it just yet, but when I got back from World Wide I knew she couldn't bring herself to wear it when she slit her wrists. She was only fourteen. Her eyes were open when I found her. She didn't leave me a note. I left LA that night and came here, to save a little girl and then watch her die. As hard as I try to save the ones that deserve it it seems the worse I make it. For Sasha the hope was too much to bear, the thought of a better life, the struggle out of this one. Perhaps my own fault, to offer hope without a means. Her blood on my hands is as bad the blood already there, could it only be that was enough to save her suffering, but she had a whole lot more blood on her own hands. 'When you get to the gates baby doll, just don't mention my name.' My voice feels dry as I stand up. The dog senses the new tension and offers a bark. I like that dog. I pat his head, it takes me until the sun starts peeking over head to collapse my tent and strap up the small amount of gear I'd gathered the last few days. Wash my hands in the inky water, my mask reflecting back at me; haloed by the morning sun. I cock a grin at myself and can only chuckle before rippling the image into obscurity. I don't pretend to be anything I'm not. It only takes a couple hours by canoe to get back to the nearest camp, the pumps had started the task of draining the city yesterday and it was a bustle of activity. I must have become a regular sight because only a few turned their attention to the masked freak. 'Looters got some guy up the road, best watch yerself.' One of them says. I start unloading my bags. 'If today is the die I am ment to die, then gentlement, I plan to meet death with with nothing but my courage to live and the word of God.' 'Oh geeze.' Another comments. They laugh. I grin and heft my microphone from heaven. 'Amen.' They add. I turn the rifle and pass it off. 'Anybody heading up towards Eva's camp?' 'I think we can fit you in.' One of them nods me to follow and I do just that. With a quick whistle the dog jumps from the boat and falls into step at my heels. I like this dog. 'Got yerself a friend?' 'I figure he owes me one.' 'What's his name?' 'Dunno.' 'Ain't named him yet?' 'No, he hasn't told me it yet.' 'Well he ain't never gonna talk...' I shrug.
