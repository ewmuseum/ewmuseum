Field Goal

3 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-03-2005 Field Goal “Yo! V! Wake the hell up, man!” Styles yells as he slams on the door of his client’s hotel room door. He’s dressed in his classic attire, most of which seems to be bearing the Roc-A-Wear label and he’s definitely styling, much like his name sake states. He pounds a couple more times, rolling his eyes and everything. “Yo! Come ON! I got great news and it ain’t got nothin” to do with car insurance!” The door finally opens, slowly but surely, and Styles shoves it the rest of the way open. As he busts into the room, he shakes his head and glares at Varsity. “The hell, man”! Why you all cooped up in here by yourself? You havin? PMS symptoms or suntin?”” Varsity rolls his eyes and heads back to the small sofa, dropping himself back down onto it and snatching up the remote control. His reply to Styles is merely a shrug and then the flipping of channels as he gets back to his life of channel surfing. “This is pitiful. I’m glad Devil’s Night is over cuz I don’t think I could take another week of this Mopey Taylor shit you’re pullin”, V.” Styles says, moving over towards the sofa and tossing down a folder onto the chest of his client. “You’re booked for WorldWide this week.” Varsity shrugs and shoves the folder off himself and down onto the floor. “That’s nice.” He continues channel flipping for a moment or two and that’s when it hits him. Styles just said that he was booked for WorldWide. This week. “Wait... what?” Styles rolls his eyes, spinning off and heading towards the kitchen area, ready to rummage through the fridge like the freeloading bastard he is. “I said you’re booked for WorldWide. You and Jayce Bradley. Goin” toe to toe. One on one. Mano y mano y mayo. All that shit, man.” He sighs and pulls out an empty carton of orange juice. “How you gon” drink up all the juice, man?” Varsity sits up, snatching up the folder and flipping it open. Sure enough, there’s his copy of the contract and everything. He flashes a big ol? smile and hops up from the couch. “Woo! I’m back! Yes! I’m gettin” a second chance!” At this point, Varsity begins to actually get jiggy with it. Which, by all means, is a very scary sight considering the fact that he’s a middle-class white boy from the suburbs. If you can visualize such dancing, you probably need to go and wash your brain out with a 357 magnum. Go ahead, we’ll wait for you to get back. Styles sighs, snatching out a bottle of water and pops the cap. “V. Please. Every time you try to dance, a kitten dies.” He flashes a smile to show that he’s joking and cops a seat at the table, tossing his feet up there and everything. “So. You done mopin”, right? Cuz this is your second match and if you don’t win, I’m gonna? have to seriously pull some strings to get you an endorsement deal. You gotta? give me somethin? to work with here, man. And if you can’t get your head on straight, we’re not gonna? make any money.” Varsity swings himself over the back of the sofa, tossing the remote control and folder back down behind him and he stretches some. “I was never mopin”, Styles. I was just bored. I mean, I know I had to step back and let the veterans shine but... it’s my time, now, man. I can feel it. This is my shot.” Styles smirks and guzzles some water. “So what’s first” Shall I set up an interview or something? Get you some exposure?” Varsity shrugs. “Up to you, man. I’ll let you handle the fame and the fortune. Me” I’m gonna? go level the playin? field...” And with that, Varsity takes off for the bedroom, closing the door behind him. Styles blinks a little bit and looks around. “So um... wait... what” I hate it when you go Riddler on me!” Later... The football field of the local high school is empty. That is until Varsity steps out onto field, large radio in one hand and his duffle bag tossed over the shoulder of his other arm. He takes a second to look around and smiles to himself, before he nods and heads over towards the bleachers. He’s totally dressed in his classic workout gear from back in the day. One of his old high school jerseys and some black sweat pants. A bandana has been tied around his head, holding his hair back from his eyes and giving him some style. He reaches the bleachers, tossing the bag down. He puts the radio on the bleacher seat and with a flick of the wrist, slides a cassette into it. Play. Cue Training Montage: Let’s Get It Started by Black Eyed Peas Varsity’s foot taps a bit to the beat as the song cues up and he smiles. Oh, he’s definitely about to send himself into quite the workout. He stretches his arms out some, before he steps back out onto the field and works his shoulders. He drops down, starting to do some push ups and getting those arms back in shape. He spent two weeks on the sofa and he’s got to get back into shape, as far as he’s concerned. There’s no time to waste. None at all. The next moment he’s running laps around the field. The look in his eyes is one of extreme focus and determination. He’s got one thing and one thing only on his mind right about now. Victory. That can be practically read in his eyes with the way he pushes to make it around the field. Sweat dripping from his brow the entire time. The duffle bag is unzipped and a fury of workout equipment is exposed. Varsity’s hand yanks out a football. In the middle of the field, he lines up with an imaginary offensive line. Hiking the ball to himself, he rolls out left, dodges an imaginary pass rush and hurls the ball down field. 70 yards. Touchdown! Again, Varsity hikes the ball to himself, this time he spins off imaginary defenders and leaps over another one. Tucking the ball to him, he leaps, somersaulting over the crashing linemen and lands in the end zone for another touchdown! Quarterback sneak, one can presume. Off near the radio now, Varsity’s working the jump rope. Single time. Double time. Triple time. Crisscross. As he jumps, he closes his eyes, mouthing the numbers so that he doesn’t lose count. 45... 46... 47... Sit ups! In the middle of the field, a shirtless Varsity proceeds to get his six pack back in order with some odd number of sit ups. All that can be seen is the continuous appearance of Varsity’s face as he sits up, touches his elbows to his knees and goes back down. Nothing’s gonna? stop him now, apparently. Nothing. Once again, Varsity grabs the football off the ground. He looks up, staring down field at the tire that’s hanging on the goal posts. He hikes to himself, rolling right, then left, then back left! His arm goes back and the football is launched! It soars through the air with tremendous hang time and begins its descent towards the swinging tire... Taped to the front of the tire is a blown up picture of Jayce Bradley. It’s not there long, just long enough for people to recognize him, because the football rips through the flimsy paper like it wasn’t even there. And with that, it’s about time that this montage ends, don’t you think?
