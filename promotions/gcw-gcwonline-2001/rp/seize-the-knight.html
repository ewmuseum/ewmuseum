Seize the Knight

Date: Timeless Staring into the mirror in his quarters Mayhem contemplates. When you reach his age all you do is contemplate. Retirement. Life. Death. Career. Love. Hate. All of the above. It's all in your head, it all weighs heavy on your heart. His image staring back at him seems younger, wiser, more handsome, yet more experienced. He's seen this image before, it's his conscious, that little devil over your left shoulder. Do you know why the devil always appears on your left shoulder? Legend has it that Satan when he was an Angel of God sat on the left hand of God, jealous of the Angel Gabriel whom sat on the right hand of God. Fuck God. 'Who hath knocked on the door of hell with the intention of no good? Lestatt Knight. Awfully peculiar for a man named for a Vampire. The walking dead. You have knocked loudly my friend, but the person you thought would answer did not. INstead the Lunatic hath greeted thee with smite and anger. Anger is a powerful sin. Its acted upon with revenge usually. I'm not going to condemn you to a life of hell out of revenge dear Knight. That's too easy to manifest. Instead I shall introduce you to 13 years of Pain followed by 40 more of torment according to the Lord of the Underworld out of sheer amusement. I'm the underdog in our little confrontation because you have owned the longest title reign in GCW history. They call you the most dominating force on earth. Good. Keep building this up even more. The Lunatic stands no chance against this force. Not even God himself can defeat Lestatt Knight. I dare you God to challenge me. How much pain you willing to endure Mr. Knight? How much blood you willing to donate to the cause? How deep into hell are you willing to walk through to say you beat me twice, something that has NEVER been done? I will cripple the walking dead. Before its all said and done, my prediction, is collapse, your career. Welcome to oblivion Mr. Knight. Shemhamforesh nosfera aus lundam ostera nottos sen.'
