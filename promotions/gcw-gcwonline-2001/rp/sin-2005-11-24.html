Sin...

24 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/05 Have you ever had one of those days where you really don’t care if you live or die? The kind of day where nobody in the universe exists except for you. Nobody to care whether you exist, nobody to talk to about your emotions. This is one of those days. Ricky flips his phone shut as the clock reads 1 minute later then the last time he looked at it. He lies on the couch, unable to think, unable to move, unable to motivate himself to do anything at all. He flips up the phone again looking at the clock. The next minute has yet to approach. He sighs heavily as closes his eyes. Ricky shivers a bit as the icy air chills him to the bone. He wraps his arms around him trying to hold in the heat, as he looks at his surroundings. “What the’where am I?” he wonders aloud. “Mount Gagazet” comes a reply from behind. Ricky spins to face the voice from behind him. The voice is very heavy and has a certain rasp to it. Ricky jumps back a few feet as he is shocked at the being in front of him. The “man” in front of him has green colored skin, and what appears to be tree roots for hair. His hands hang from his exceptionally long arms, with the fingers looking like tree roots as well. “What the?” “I’m sorry, if I startled you. The maester said we should be expecting you. Please Sir Thunder. Follow me” “What are you?” “Sin’s toxins are indeed great. I’m a of the Guado people. Please come along.” “How did I get here” And what’s a Guado?” “All questions will be answered once we return to the palace. Now please come along.” Reluctantly Ricky begins to follow the seemingly harmless Guado. The pass through the base of a massive tree, which leads to an underground palace belong to that of the maester. The Guado leads Ricky inside and motions for him to have a seat on the ruby red sofa in the foyer. Ricky looks around and notices something odd. That sofa is the only item of furniture there. No pictures, no flowerpots, no nothing. A man appears at the top of the stairs and motions joyfully to Ricky. “You must be Sir Thunder. Please allow me to introduce myself. My name is Seymour. Maester of Yevon. It is an honor to meet you.” “Thanks, I guess. Where exactly are we” Even more importantly, ho w did I get here?” “Sin has carried you here Ricky.” “Sin?” Ricky ponders before answering “So this is hell?” “Sir Thunder, surely you jest?” “I’m really confused as to what’s going on. This talk about sin bringing me here, is why I assumed it’s hell.” “The toxins?” “Toxins?” Ricky questions. “Sin is reborn every 1000 years. It is formed from the misdeeds of others. The only way to truly eliminate Sin is to create a utopia. We Guado have tried to achieve this for quite sometime, but continue to fail. Sin produces a severe toxin that can cause the body to fall into a massive septic shock. Many people die from Sin’s encounters. Most people do.” “So, for every bad thing someone does Sin grows a bit more?” “Basically. We have awaited the visitor that can defeat Sin.” “Why bother if it’s just going to spawn again in 1000 years?” “Those 1000 years of peace, are the best 1000 years ever. Sin is essentially the punishment for straying from the teachings of Yevon.” “Yevon?” “Surely you jest, Ricky.” Seymour asks shocked. “I apologize. I must remember with Sin carrying you here, your people may not recognize the teachings of Yevon.” “Yeah’I guess so. So why am I here?” “Sin will always deliver a person to help us get through the darkness. Sin always brings us a savior.” “Whoa” Ricky retorts “I’m no savior. I just want to go home?” “Sir Ricky, Sin will deliver you to your homeland once your goal is achieved.” “Goal?” Seymour leads the way down a long corridor into a smaller room, with several spheres set into the walls. He approaches them, his hand waving in front of them but not touching them. He reaches out to touch one and a small hologram or a Guado appears. “1000 years has passed since the last calm. The time has come for us to deliver Spira from Sin. The one that Sin delivers to us shall aide in our battle, to absolve the Mayhem. Sin causes Mayhem. You will know this soul, as soon as they arrive. They will not e from this world. They will be the only ones who can aide our high summoner in delivering the calm.” “Whoa! What was that thing”!” “Basically people can trap messages in these little spheres. All of these spheres here have messages in them. Most from former masters. Ricky, come with me to the temple. I want to show you a bit more of the Mayhem, Sin causes.” The two exit the underground castle, and take a small dirt road towards the temple. Several trees shield the pathway from the sky overhead. Ricky takes notice to this as they seem almost pruned to cover the path. “These trees?” Ricky starts. “Yes, they shield us from the dangers of fiends in the air.” “What’s a fiend?” “Fiend’s are the souls of the dead, that have not been sent off to the farplane. They come to resent our life, and wish to bring us harm.” “Farplane?” “Ricky, there is much you will learn about Spira, and our ways, but first, and most importantly, there is Sin. In order to defeat your enemy you must know your enemy” The two stand facing a omniscient looking temple, that slowly opens its doors to the awaiting maester. Ricky stares in awe as the walls roll back to reveal the entrance to the previously sealed temple. Seymour leads the way into the temple with Ricky close behind. Upon entering Ricky sees several huge statues. Seymour motions for him to follow as he heads towards one statue in particular. “Whoa! I know him!!!” “Of course you do Ricky. This is the one that Sin has consumed. He brought you here.” “Wait, you said I was brought here to defeat Sin” “Sin always delivers the one that can defeat him. Sin does not enjoy destroying life. Sin is simply all consumed by evil, and cannot control the destruction it brings.” “So this is Sin huh?” “Not as you knew him. Ricky, when a body is consumed by Sin, their soul is what fuels Sin. There is no end to the destruction that Sin is capable of. The man that once was, is no more.” Ricky stares at the statue of Bryan Mayhem, as his world goes dark. Ricky jerks upright rubbing his eyes and looking around his living room. “Goddamnit! I need to turn off the playstation sometimes!!!” ***Dedicated to Sean. Good luck to all GCW’ers in GTT5***
