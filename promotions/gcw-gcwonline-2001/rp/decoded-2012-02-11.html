Decoded

11 February 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/11/12 “What would you say if I told you that in the backstage of GCW, it isn’t really all fists and fury? What if I was to tell you that wrestling is predetermined, fake; and has been since its inception? What would you do if I told you that beyond being predetermined, GCW wasn’t real at all? I’m Brad Meltzer, and this is Decoded.” Cue the flashy intro, annoying music and spiffy connection of letters that let you know, hey, these people really must be smart. That’s enough of that, let’s go to a commercial. Yeah, you love those, right? Just a couple seconds of an intro after you just sat through two minutes at the end of the last show. Get up, grab another drink, sit back down and hope that the annoying, get your attention method employed in the commercials you’ll see ends soon. Argue with the wife just a little, but shut up, we’re back. “Buddy, McKenzie and Scott are in Kansas City, the site of the most recent World Wide, the title of the semi-monthly program that airs on XTV.” Cut to the three standing in the hallway of what could be anywhere. They look all inquisitive and ready to get down to the bottom of this question. Or at least rehash old ideas and present them like something new. Who should happen by them but the recently made former US Champ, Alexander Redding. His size and build has him tower over most, and looking as imposing as the worst that could ever happen on December 21, 2012. “Excuse me, but you are Alex Redding, right?” says the short, skinny guy. Scott. “That’s what they named me.” “Who are they?” “Shit, right into it, huh? No hello, nice to meet you. I see. They: my writers.” “Oh, okay. So you mean to tell us this is just a stage name. Do you have another name? What is on your birth certificate?” “No. I’m Alexander Redding. I don’t have a birth certificate. I was born inside of some guy’s head, and really, most of me still lives there. He hasn’t been the greatest at getting my personality out and forefront. Shit, the guy has had me losing for the past 5 cards. What a dick.” Time for the girl to chime in. McKenzie. “What are you talking about? You’re just as real as any one I’ve…” she gets cut off. She scrunches her nose is disapproval. “The address Meltzer gave you was an internet address.” “You’re saying this place doesn’t exist beyond an internet site?” “I didn’t say that. We occupy a corner of the mind of about 20 people. Used to be a lot more, but then people got all grown up and gay and said fuck this shit, took their dicks and went home.” “Wow.” Stunned silence. It’s something they fake a lot, but this one looks more fake than the rest. Cut back to the green screen that has the lame repeating background, and Meltzer’s bald head shining into the camera. “Well, that sucks. We were supposed to dedicate an entire show to decoding this myth. I think we got maybe two minutes of footage. This is useless… unless…” Back to the hallway. “But if you want to know something that is real: werewolves.” Red says plain as could be. “Werewolves?” they all ask in unison. “Stop talking like creepy ass little girls. And yeah, well, I knew of one that was real.” “Tell us, what happened?” “Get this, right? I’m driving along in some shithole town in Colorado to get to a house show which doubly does not exist. Next thing I know, the engine overheats, damn thing blows up in my face when I pop the trunk. Knocks me out cold. When I wake up, there’s a group of about twelve standing around me, which was awkward as fuck, I must have pissed my pants when I got knocked out. So I change my pants, and they can’t stop staring at my dick,” he slows up, shifting to see McKenzie looking down at his pants. He smiles a little. The smile fades when he sees Scott doing the same. “Anyway… I get changed and they say they need my help, and since they helped me, I should help them. Didn’t help they held a gun to my head. They said they were going werewolf hunting, and I ought to tag along. I was going to be their good luck charm. Whatever. So I tag along, but the fucked up thing of it is they kill off the cute one that looked at my dick like she wanted to ride, ya know? It was fucked. They said it was a sacrifice, asking for good luck from whatever thing they called god. I asked how many times they did it, they said 30. These people didn’t start getting the clue after at least 3? Really?” “Go on,” Buddy gets two words in to interrupt. “So we’re stalking around this wooded area and I’m thanking fuck this ain’t the forests of Kansas.” Confused faces. “There’s this big fucker of an Indian that lives there with his gay Viking.” “Oh…” “So we’re back hunting, and these people are really getting more unlikeable be the minute. We finally happen upon the hairy bastard, but I give the dude a heads up. The wolf does his wolf like thing, killing a bunch of them, making the leader run like a bitch, and than happened upon me. He walked up all slowly, than just stuck out a paw. I instinctively gave him some knuckle bumps and like that, we were buds.” “That’s amazing. But you said you thought there might have been just one?” “I’m getting to it, sheesh. Alright, so we’re chilling, trying to get the fuck out of the forest, we run across a Yeti. The wolf calls him a fag, but the Yeti just continues on, ignoring us, like a fag. The sun is coming up around this point, and the wolf is changing back into a guy. He remembers everything we did, asks for something to wear. I was lugging around my bags the entire time. He gets unnaked and we head to a bar. I’ve give Buttfuck, Colorado props for one thing; they were serving liquor at 7 am. We get there; he sits down in the booth, all tired for the shit he goes through to become and wolf and back to a dude. He tells me to grab him a beer. I walk up to the bar, order myself a Jack Daniels. Bitch tries to poor me a shot glass, I take the entire bottle. I remember Wolfie, I never did get the dude’s name, and ask about the draft specials. Coors Light on for $1.99 a pint. I figure, fuck, that’s a good deal, and take it. I leave a fifty and walk back to the booth. I sit down and we start talking about who he is when he takes one sip. He collapses on the table, not breathing. I try my best not to freak out, but I’ve been awake for 35 hours at this point, and all I want to do is get wasted, so I take the JD and slowly move away, until I’m out the door, drinking in the streets. It isn’t until I’m halfway done the bottle before I realise what I’ve done. I killed the guy. Coors Light, the silver bullet.” “Wow.” Useless as tits on a guy, these three. Back to Brad in studio. He’s hung himself from the rafters. I guess this is the end of the show. RIP Wolfie, Brad.
