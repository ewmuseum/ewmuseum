3. Paradise Lost

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/09 Paradise Lost I got another letter in the mail today... That is four this week already and it's only tuesday. What is it about people that don't even know you, having to criticize you and scrutinize you for every fucking thing? Letter fucking one: "Mr. Michaels, I am just writing in regards to your last match in WEC against Pierre. I just wanted to let you know that after a lackluster event, I came to the conclusion that your other career is making you soft and you should decide to stay in one or the other. I am a huge fan of yours, but I do not think you have it in you anymore to pursue MMA..." ...and that one was the nicest one... "The Executioner, The only thing you executed was ticket sales. Be proud of yourself that you were able to get a guillotine in on a fuckin rookie..." "Michaels, You suck...." "Kris "The Showboating" Michaels, I want the old Kris back. Give up that fake shit and stay with WEC! You looked winded, fat, and your technique was sloppy as shit and all you were looking for were pops from the crowd. Fuck that shit, fight like you always did and get over yourself..." .... No, the match didn't go the way I anticipated, but I've been trying to balance GCW and WEC in the same breath. Nobody knows how hard it is to do both! First, I'm told to bulk for GCW, then I need to cut for WEC. One work out is strength, the other is agility and speed... Then there is dieting and events and shows and training and... Don't you understand that I am trying to be what you're dying to see? I try to do it all for you! The stress is worth it for those that appreciate it, but who the fuck appreciates it anymore? WEC vs. GCW. I don't know how many fans try to be loyal to both like I do but I know the fence is beginning to clear... ...this is life which is so thankless... I know that once it is all over and I am dust in the long history of the ring, they will all know they loved us, but until then, we are nothing more than entertainment for the here and now in the ring... All the same, I trudge on with another day in this thing called life. *** A steady heartbeat... A faint sound of the crowd going wild... The sound of tape ripping off the roll... The saturation of marker on taped hands... The Vaseline covering his face... ...Two minutes till Michaels' WEC match, less than a week before GCW's WW airs... Just another match to add to my career record, just another match. I only need to walk through those doors, down the ramp, and into the ring. All I need to do. Walk. No need to exert any extra energy before the match. One minute before I'm let out into the arena... One minute. "KRIS! Listen to me! You are a fuckin' champion! Fuck what everyone is saying. You are a fucking champ! Go out there and give it all!" I'm a fucking champion, a fucking champ. Nobody stands in my way. A new division, a new opponent, but the same style. I'm a champion... Jessie knows it, and I know it... "REMEMBER! You are the heart for the heartless..." I''m the heart for the fucking heartless... "You are the guts for the gutless.." The fuckin' gutless...Potter will feel my aggression tonight... "The means to an end, an end to this fight tonight!" I will win this... "And remember, Kris..." Kris looks up at Jessie for the fist time "You are the fucking EXECUTIONER! Now come on baby and show them what we're all about in Jersey! COME ON!" The doors open and it is a rush of adrenaline all its own. Nobody knows what this feels like, unless you are a fighter, like me. Rows and rows of fans in Las Vegas all waiting for the gladiators to clash in the center of the ring. They want their money's worth, but more importantly, they want to see blood and guts... ...all of which I'm ready to give... Down at the ring, they check me for anything illegal as always, but all of this will be an afterthought once the bell sounds. Tedious things like this that last but a moment feel like an eternity when you want to hear the bell ring. The ok... All I need now is to take this shirt off and I am ready to start swinging... Please take it off. He is right there in front of me. Frank Potter of New York, New York. A pretty boy who thinks he knows what he's doing around here. Requesting this fight to get noticed for the UFC. It will take a lot more than losing to me to get noticed by them! Fucking mouthpiece...he can't hear me, but then again, I'll let him hear it when he starts to lose consciousness before the end of round one. Come on, let's get this on! I know these damn rules. Touch my glove and let's get this shit over with. Please! I need this! I need to hear the bells. I need to rip his fucking head off! Come one, please! Let me go. Let me loose! I need to hear the bells! My calves are in a constant spasm, waiting to be unleashed! COME ON! Jekyll and Hyde, Jekyll and Hyde, JEKYLL AND HYDE!!!! LET'S DO THIS! The bells ring in nearly slow motion Alright, back to Jekyll... Calm, clear, and collected. He has no standing game. Ha, nice attempt at a jab. Test the waters a little more, one more. Jab, Jab, Cross! Nailed it... Take another step back, good. Another one. Jab to push him back, Jab again. Deep Breath aaaannnnnddddd EXPLODE! Flying knee to his chin, and he's down. Drop with a superman punch, jab, jab, jab. Come on ref! STop the fight! Ah, he recovered. His mistake. Full guard, dominate position. Jab, Jab, straight. Stop holding my back you bastard... Take a minute to breath...breath... Hammer punch! Hammer Punch! Hamm...nice grab. Alright he's getting strong. Hes breaking my guard, alright he's nearly up, but this is my chance. Wrap the leg, slip around... Alright, back control. He's done. The fans know it, the commentator's know it... ...he knows it... How should I finish it? What kind of choke? Should I lock his arm, or work the body? Legs? Ugh, he's starting to panic, getting desperate. He's another punch to the temple, and sweep the arm... Rear naked choke half the way in...three quarters... Oh, I can feel it in his throat, the air is getting cut off and I can feel his pulse getting stronger but slowing... Just tap... That a boy... Don't worry, you aren't the first to tap, nor will you be the last... I get to my feet and casually walk to my corner raising my hands, waiting to be picked up by Jessie. Yup, like clockwork! The fans loved that one.. Where's the clock, where's the clock... Ha! One minute and twenty three seconds in the first round. And you want to be in the UFC? Keep trying Potter... Alright, announce it, call it, and get me the fuck out of here so I can get some sleep for GCW training tomorrow... **** I hit the alarm clock, nearly breaking it. I'm pretty sure I just made the mechanical hands slide a couple hours and minutes ahead. Another day, another routine, another step closer to the next show... This isn't everything I thought it was going to be. I don't know how long I can juggle both places, but I do feel a strong urge to stay with GCW. I thought everything was going to be amazing and simple. Please the crowd, get the fans... Schedule a match in WEC, train, and kick some ass... No, that's not how my paradise works. I feel run down and my body is in constant change. It fluctuates with the routines, going up and down, flat and hard, and not to mention my stomach... Diet, diet, train train... The match against Potter was simple and quick, but I felt tired. The match didn't last long, but choking him out, landing those strikes... I don't remember being hit, but my chin is bruised... I wish I could stand strong like I picture myself, but how the hell do I keep up with the likes of the World Champion? I'm a far ways off, but I don't even have time to get shit going with people from GCW! I'm rarely even on the shows! I thought I had a strong fan base in GCW, but I haven't been booked, nor have I even been given the opportunity... I guess I really screwed it up in the last match, huh? Well, my Paradise is lost, and I need to set things right again but all I have is time... ...but time is an expendable luxury...
