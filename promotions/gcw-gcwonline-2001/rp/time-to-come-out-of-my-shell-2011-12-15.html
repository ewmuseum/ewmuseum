Time to come out of my shell

15 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/11 The lights and sounds going off around him we unknown to him. Cameron Garret was taking his first steps into the High Stakes Casino in Nevada. It was only about a month or so ago Cameron had made his first trip out to Nevada. Now he is witnessing another first in his life, entering a casino. Taking a deep breath then releasing a very hard sigh, “ This time we can only hope for a better outcome then the last time I stepped into Nevada.” Although his match was his main priority the noises of the machines, the freshly pumped oxygen all got his blood running the second he walked in the door. Not wanting to look like the proverbial “newbie” Cameron started to make his way around the casino. So many things he had never experienced before, so many things he felt he had been missing out all those years. Cameron made his first stop over to the cigar bar, looking at the variety the casino had to offer Cameron could not make up his mind, talking under his breath he decided that he would return to this place later on once he has seen the whole facility. Staying the same area he knew it was time to grab his first drink. Cameron was not a bigger drinker, being in the wrestling industry made him very conscious of the poisons that could be entered into his body that would hurt his career and his chances for getting ahead. However he felt it was time to live a little on the “wild” side, he was now touring with a professional wrestling organization Making his way over the bar Cameron looked up at the gorgeous bartender and did everything he could to keep his jaw from hitting the bar. “What can I get you sir?” she said it with the brightest smile he had ever seen. “What would you recommend?” Cameron was not doing it to hit on her as she probably thought he was, Cameron really didn't know what would be a good drink of choice. Laughing she responded, “ Well sir, that is really what type of liquor you like. You could be a simple beer or wine guy for all I know. Or you could be a manly man who enjoys his whiskey or scotch, only on the rocks of course. Then you have your other men who would enjoy their vodkas, their martinis, their mixers. I guess it really is all up to who you are.” Cameron took this as his opportunity, he knew that he really didn’t have much in this aspect in regards to knowing what the hell he was talking about but he could at least sound as if he did. “Whiskey on the rocks please.” The nice lady knew she was dealing with someone who really was not sure what they are doing with, “ You want the house whiskey, Jack Daniels, Crown Royal, Jim Beam?” Not wanting to give to many options. Without hesitation Cameron looked up, “ Crown.” Not really sure how it tasted but knew the name sounded good. The bartender turned around, the backside was as good as her smile. The only problem was you could tell she knew it as she walked. Before she had come back Cameron already had the money and the tip on the counter and wished he a good night as he turned around. Not wanting to watch her witness the first sip he was facing away, however once he tasted it he knew that was not going to be as bad as he may have thought. It was time to start working on the machines, he wanted the full experience. Walking around he was not sure where to start, he sipped slowly on his Crown on the rocks as he watched others lose all their hard earned money in this hopeless dream to make more by pulling a lever. Knowing that slots might not be the answer for him he stepped into the side room where the roulette tables were located. Once inside a light bulb went off, he hard all of his college friends talk about the odds and how this was the best chance for winning money. However that was not Cameron’s concern. He was more worried about how much he was going to lose. Stepping up to the first wheel he saw the minimum bet was $10. Reaching into his pocket he placed a $100 bill on the table and asked for $10 chips. Waiting the first round out he watched as the others bet. The tension and anticipation was building on the table as the dealer called for no more bets. The ball landed after the wheel spun around numerous times, 00 green. Most at the table were disappointed however there was two gentlemen that had played that number so were content with the payout. The dealer cleared the bets, and reopened for another round. This was his chance,taking another quick sip of the liquor made him feel ready, even though he was not. Grabbing the chips he placed a $10 spot on the number 22. While at it he also played the outside first row. “Go big or go home.” Cameron said with a smile to the man playing next to him. However that was not really all that big to most of these gamblers.The dealer called for no more bets, and that is when it happened. Sweat started to form on his brow, he could feel his heart start to race. He almost felt as if he was back in the ring for his first ever wrestling event. It was a feeling he loved however he did not get to feel it often. Taking the last swig of Crown Royal, Cameron watched eagerly as the ball started to spin around the wheel. “No more bets.” The dealer waived his hand over the betting board as a universal signal that the betting round was over. He loved the feeling he was exonerated he was living the life of someone on the road. It was short lived however. He faced the same reality that many people faced, he had lost the number went on Black 3. Neither his number or column was called. Not wanting to give up so easy Cameron repeated the bet. Not really getting any success for the night this went on for a couple of hours as every time he seemed to be down and out of money, he somehow won a little to get back into it. After spending almost all of his money Cameron had enough of the up and downs that roulette brought to him. He decided he wanted to go back and see that pretty lady at the cigar bar. Making his way back after having a couple of drinks was not as easy as he had thought. Cameron made it, took probably a little longer then it should have however once he arrived he noticed they have changed shifts and he was now stuck with an overweight bald man. Oh well the eye candy would have been nice, but he was still having a good time. Ordering another Crown on the rocks and getting the cigar this time Cameron paid the man and walked over to one of the plush leather couches that surrounded the bar. Random college football was on and Cameron was not looking forward to it but it would pass the time. Lighting the cigar seemed seem less for someone who had never smoked. Paying so much attention to his actions he wanted to make sure that was not making it known this was the first time for this. He light the cigar and inhaled the first puff of smoke, exhaling Cameron slouched down into the chair. A cigar, a Crown on the rocks, and a career was only thing he needed right now, it brought him to ecstasy. Siting in the chair enjoying his drink, his cigar, and his memories of how he got to the point he was in his career was only the beginning as he knew that his time was coming he would be viewed as the superstar he actually was, not as the one that had been shown over the last couple of weeks since signing with the GCdub.
