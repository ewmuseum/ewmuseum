When You Wish Upon A Star (Part 1: Innocent)

26 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 26, 2007 Satan, you know where I lie Gently I go into that good night All our lives get complicated Search for pleasures overrated Never armed our souls For what the future would hold When we were innocent Angels lend me your might Forfeit all my lives to get just one right All those colors long since faded All our smiles all confiscated Never were we told We'd be bought and sold When we were innocent This prayer is for me tonight This far down that line and still ain't got it right And while confessions not yet stated Our next sin is contemplated Never did we know What the future would hold Or that we'd be bought and sold We were innocent -- "Innocent" by Fuel ---- Introduction: A Haunting Foreshadow ---- When you're young the world is such a happy place. A place where anything is possible and dreams come true. A place where you can do anything with a little hard work and dedication. I suppose in that sense I never really grew up... Bright lights shine all around, glistening off the sterile white walls of the backstage area. The fluorescents accent the golden blonde hair of The Fearless Phenom, interrupted only by a single red strip in her hair. Hanging in her face, it covers her beautiful cerulean blue eyes as she looks down at her hands. If you could get a better look, you could see the pain building inside of those piercing baby blues. In her hands she tightly clings onto a black cloth. It certainly holds some sentimental value. But I suppose I was one of the lucky ones. My destiny was what I always thought it would be. I sit here today as GCW World Heavyweight Champion like I always dreamed of. I couldn't imagine being anything else. I couldn't imagine life forcing me to make a different choice. I couldn't imagine a time table being set on when I could achieve my hopes and dreams. A timetable that would fall far too short... Unfolding the black cloth in her hands she pulls it apart and slowly reads the three letters in her mind: MSV Looking at the letters is like releasing the locks on the dam. Memories flood her mind of the events that have transpired over the past couple of weeks. Laying the black band across her leg, a stream of tears begin to fall from those beautiful blue eyes. It was still quite painful looking back. The scars were still fresh. Vocal sobs begin to emit from her thin pink lips. She tried her best to hold it back, but how does one deal with the loss of someone they hold dear quietly? Perhaps I was raised jaded in the moral values of Mighty Morphin Power Rangers and 1980's Professional Wrestling. A world where there was a clear line between right and wrong. The bad guys were easy to identify and always got what they deserved in the end. The good guy would undoubtedly come in at the last second, just in time to make everything okay... Slowly she fights through the tears and the sadness to wrap the cloth around her left leg, tying it in the back like a headband. Standing up, the World Champion examined herself in the mirror. She was emotionally unstable... but she was alive. She was still breathing. Things could be worse... they could always be worse. Wearing her black band proudly like a medal of honor, she picked the gold plated belt up off the bench next to her and swung it over her shoulder. Possibly for the very last time. Through the tears and the sadness, she knew there was a fight to be won. Things don't work like that in the real world... but that doesn't keep me from trying. There are things in life worth fighting for. Win or lose, there are sacrifices to be made for the greater good. No one ever said that the life of a champion would be easy, I knew that. Never would I have imagined though, that I'd be having to deal with something like this during the most important moments of my career. Vivica J. Valentine looked at the championship gleaming on her shoulder in the mirror. Through the festering emotional wound that still took its hold on her psyche, she managed to force a smirk of determination. But this is my legacy. This is my place in the history books. No matter what happens in my life I can't let this slip away. My legacy has grown to be much bigger than myself... it's an embodiment of an entire generation of wrestling fans. My story is the story of legends, and this match will define it all. These are the matches that you tell your grandkids about. It could be my biggest moment or my greatest defeat. Whether or not I have other problems I'm willing to step forth and be judged, because they say what doesn't kill me only makes me stronger. If that's true, may God have mercy on the souls of Jay Terror and Jordan White tonight... because I sure as hell won't. ---- CHAPTER ONE: Make-A-Wish ---- Weeks earlier, hours before WorldWide 93... THE UNITED CENTER CHICAGO, ILLINOIS Tick.. Tick... Tick... A round battery operated clock hanging next to the exit sign above the door sounded much more like a hammer pounding a wall than a quiet second hand ticking the time away. Maybe it was just the silence in the room that made it sound that way, or perhaps it was the anxiety that rattled her brain. It was 5pm... the night of WorldWide 93. It had been a week since The Fearless Phenom had last looked at her reflection in the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Something that was taken away from her by Jay Terror in the middle of the ring. Several phone calls and not-so-friendly demands to Steven Caldera's office hadn't produced a damn thing. Still a week later there she was. Her hands were empty. Two and a half years of blood, sweat, and tears shed... for something that was just taken away in a matter of minutes. There was no pinfall, there was no disqualification. No entrance music, no announcement. Nothing. The horrible sting of betrayal could still be felt between her shoulder blades on her back. Almost like a knife, stabbing to kill her hopes and dreams. Yet today wasn't about her as she watched the second hand on the clock slowly count down to showtime. It was about someone else. A 9 year old girl named Mary Sue Venton was coming to spend the day with her today. It was a true wish being granted by the Make-A-Wish Foundation. Vivica was her hero. It was her life's goal to meet the woman she idolized on the television, and even though Vivica hardly felt like a hero at this particular hour she had to put on that face. She had to force a smile and work through it. The cameras would be rolling. This would be a day both of them remembered for the rest of their lives. Even though she had been expecting something to happen for almost an hour the knock on the door startled The Fearless Phenom, who imagined to lose herself in much deeper thoughts. With a deep breath she ran her fingers through her hair, doing some last minute primping before it was time to meet her bright-eyed fan. Getting up off the bench it almost felt like the room was spinning. Vivica would be lying if she didn't admit to be just a little nervous about the whole situation. Turning the doorknob slowly she pulled the door open with a forced smile from ear-to-ear across her face. Imagine her surprise when a 46-year-old man walked into the room. In his hand were two groups of red, black, and silver balloons. The champion assumed they were to go about the room, but she still didn't have the slightest idea who the man was... but he sure was dressed nice. "Miss Valentine, I presume?" He said as he extended his arm forward. The Aerial Artisan tilted her head to the side as he spoke... not reaching out to answer the handshake. She was quite baffled. "...Yes?" She answered slowly, showing obvious signs of confusion. "Oh how silly of me." The man chuckled to himself, it was a forced laugh. The same one all businessmen use when they fuck up, "My name is Mark Bingham. I'm a representative for the Make-A-Wish Foundation. I just stopped by to add some decoration to your locker room. You know... to make the special little girl feel appreciated and comfortable." Uncomfortable? Vivica could relate. In all her years in this business and... well... every other business she's been involved in for that matter, never has she had to entertain a little girl before. She's never even had to babysit. She'd certainly have to watch her language around the youngster, and probably sit a little more ladylike too. Damn, there was a lot she'd have to think about. "Oh, hey..." Vivica stated looking around the room awkwardly, "...just sit them around anywhere you want I guess. When the show gets started I'm assuming we probably won't be spending much time in this room anyway. It's kind of boring." "I'm sure she'll get a kick out of anything you do Miss Valentine. She idolizes you." Mark spoke as he started to tie a set of balloons around her closet door handle. Nervous Vivica began to scratch the back of her head looking back up at the clock again. Her blonde and red hair moving up and down with every tick of the clock. "You're pretty nervous huh?" Mark said with a smile, "That's surprising from a girl like you. You're in front of millions of people every night. You look like you're at home out there too." "But most of them aren't children." Vivica responded with a weary smirk, "Going out and kicking boys in the mouth and entertaining a little girl isn't exactly the same thing." "You'll be fine." He assured The Fearless Phenom as he tied the last group of balloons to the leg of a table on the far side of the room. "She's a great little girl." "I guess so..." Vivica said, doing a terrible job of reassuring herself that everything would go smoothly. "I'll go get her and her family." Mark said with a smile before leaving the room. A sign escaped the lips of The Fearless Phenom as she began to look around the room quickly. There was a lot of stuff she had for this. The brand new FEARLESS T-Shirt, an autographed replica of the GCW World Heavyweight Championship, a poster to go on the girl's wall, and the elbowpads she wore when she won Dangerous Games last year. They were all sitting in the corner, in a nice little pile. If everything was ready... why in the hell was she so damn fidgety? "Hello?" She heard a woman's voice on the other side of her door. Vivica almost jumped a mile into the air at the sound. Turning around Vivica made sure the door was mostly closed and no one saw what was definitely an embarrassing moment. The whole title thing had put her on edge lately, and all... THIS... certainly wasn't helping things any. "Yeah?" Vivica said. Once again she looked into the mirror on the far side of the wall quickly messing with her hair and forcing a big cheesy grin from ear to ear like a freakish circus clown. Vivica thought she looked like a looney, but she thought maybe Mary Sue would want her to look that way. "You can come in." Slowly the door pushed open and in walked a pretty 32 year old woman. She looked around the locker room decorated in Make-A-Wish memorabilia like a kid in a candy store as she was followed shortly by a man in his mid 30s, holding the hand of a little girl. Mary Sue wore a black baseball cap with the GCW logo on it, and a white and pink floral patterned dress. The look on the little girl's face almost made Vivica feel like a job well done. Almost. She was still nervous as hell. "Oh... my... God." The little girl said before her mouth fell open in surprise. Vivica waved uncomfortably as the woman walked over with a huge grin and grabbed Vivica's hand and shook it. The Fearless Phenom put on her PR face and smiled back shaking her hand warmly. "Vivica it's so great to meet you. Mary thinks the world of you." The woman said, "My name is Jackie Venton... I'm Mary's mother. Over there is Michael Venton, Mary's father. He's the reason that she's here. He's a big wrestling fan and got our daughter into it as well." "It's a pleasure to meet you champ." Michael said with a grin waving from behind the little girl. "I can't believe it's you!" Mary said with a HUGE smile. Vivica's heart began to beat quickly. Things were going smoothly... right now. "Well it is!" Vivica said with a smirk, "You must be Mary right?" "I can't believe you know my name!" Mary responded. "Haha, well I've been waiting for you all day!" Vivica responded enthusiastically, "And I have a bunch of stuff over here for you. Let me go grab i..." The Fearless Phenom stopped in mid-sentence seeing a bracelet around the little girl's tiny wrist. It was blue and white, and had a logo on it familiar to the World Champion. "Can I see that?" Vivica said pointing at the little girl's wrist, "Where did you get that?" "From school." She said with a grin, holding it out like a badge of honor. "You go to Wolf Branch Elementary School?" Vivica said questioningly. "Yeah... how do you know my school?" "I went there when I was a little girl..." Vivica finished examining the bracelet and let go of the little girl's arm. Mary Sue Venton was still taken aback from everything. She couldn't believe her idol was standing right in front of her very eyes. She was real, and she was amazing. Mary's dad thought she was 'quite amazing' as well... but Jackie watched on like a hawk. There was no enjoyment to be had. "No way!" Mary responded, "I live right up the street!" "I live pretty close myself..." Vivica said looking into the eyes of the little girl. The girl had been through so much, but she still stayed optimistic and you couldn't tell she'd been through a thing. She was so... happy. It was almost inspiring, even to a GCW World Heavyweight Champion known for her own tenacity. "I live on 2506 Red Oak Lane. It's the new big house with the long driveway, I had it build about a year ago." "I'll have to come visit sometime!" Mary looked back at her parents. "I'm sure Vivica is way too busy for you to come and visit Mary, I'm sure with her road schedule she's hardly ever home." Jackie consulted her daughter, who responded with a bit of a frown, "But me and your dad have a tour of the facilities we're taking with Ayake Sonoda. Now you better be good for Vivica." "I will mom!" "Try not to worry..." Vivica tried to reassure Mary's parents, "Mary will be fine. I beat up bad people for a living." "I suppose that's true..." Jackie responded, "Let's go honey, they're waiting on us." Vivica watched as Michael and Jackie walked out of the room. Now was the time she was so nervous about. It was just her... and a 9 year old little girl. Mary looked up at Vivica with her big bright eyes and a grin she couldn't hide. Vivica couldn't help but feel a little warm inside seeing how much her company was appreciated by the little girl, but it didn't keep her from shaking the feeling she'd had nearly all day. She looked away from the little girl up at the clock, as time continued to tick away. As it counted down, she said something under her breath so that Mary couldn't hear... "What in the hell do I do now?" ---- CHAPTER TWO: Enjoy The Show ---- "Come on now!" The sound of a woman's voice echoed through the corridors of The United Center. In the middle of the hallway a little girl stood, completely engulfed by the sights and sounds that surrounded her. Frozen in a state of awe. Mary Sue Venton found herself in the Mecca of professional wrestling... the backstage area of Global Championship Wrestling. "Mary?" The Fearless Phenom never took her eyes off the little girl, watching her much like a guard would watch a precious diamond. Mary turned around quickly with a big smile on her face. Her hands were full with free merchandise, she hugged them against her chest as she ran over towards the World Champion, trying her best not to drop anything along the way. "I'm sorry Miss Valentine." Mary said as she stopped along side her hero. "Mary, please don't call me Miss Valentine..." Vivica said for what seemed to be the hundredth time, but still said it with a smile. In a way it was sort of cute, "It makes me feel like an old lady." "I'm sorry Miss Valentine. I'll try not to do it again." Mary responded, who was reminded many times by her parents to address absolutely anyone as mister or misses. The response made Vivica chuckle a little but, but the advice was well she assumed. You never know what kind of shady figures you'd run into backstage at a professional wrestling event. Shaman could be raising himself from a vat of blood around any particular corner. "So what's your favorite color?" Mary asked with a smile. Her intrigued little mind had a lot of questions that she'd been holding back for quite some time. A bunch of questions she had to get off her chest. "Red." Vivica returned. "Favorite band?" "Rise Against... I think? I don't know. I like a lot of bands." "Favorite movie?" "I honestly don't know if I have one..." "Do you like Miley Cyrus?" "Who?" "Hannah Montana! She's amazing. Almost as amazing as you." "Hannah Montana?" Vivica looked down at Mary questionably, "Is she a cowgirl?" "No, she's a rock star!" Mary giggled, as Vivica couldn't help but smile. She just skipped alongside The Fearless Phenom with a big smile from ear to ear and her arms full of free stuff. Vivica could tell she paid special attention to the autographed World Title replica. Vivica couldn't help but be a little impressed with Mary's optimism. "So why are you with the Make-A-Wish Foundation?" Vivica questioned, "I don't think anyone ever told me." "I have cancer." And ever with that phrase, the smile never left her little face. "Cancer?" Vivica didn't know what to say in response to that. Cancer? This little girl has cancer? That can't be right. Cancer is terminal and life-threatening. How can she possibly be so happy all the time with something so serious? Vivica said the only thing that came to mind, "I'm sorry." "It's okay." Mary said with a skip in her step, bobbling the souvenirs in her tiny arms, "I got to meet you." "Yeah..." Vivica said with a grin on her face, "I guess that's right." "I'm not interrupting am I?" A low, mellifluous voice caught both of them off guard from behind. "NO WAY!" Mary said, "YOU'RE LIA AMBROSI!" "I am." Lia smirked, stooping to shake the little girl's hand as she looked over at Vivica. "Who's your charming little friend here?" "Her name is Mary, she's with me today for the Make-A-Wish Foundation." Vivica responded, patting Mary on the head. "You're awesome!" Mary said looking up at Lia, "I remember this one match. This guy was totally being a jerk, right? You kicked him in the face and it was all like BAM! Then you got up on the top rope and you flew through the air with all kinds of spins! I wish I could do that." "Well maybe one day you can." Lia said looking down at the little girl with a bemused smile. "Vivica was like you once. She always used to tell me stories of wrestling matches she watched when she was a little girl. If you REALLY want to do it and you work hard at it, I'm sure you can too." "Really?" Mary asked looking up at Vivica. The GCW World Champion just nodded in agreement. "I can be a champion too?" "You can be anything you want..." Vivica said, remembering the words that she'd always wished her mother spoke to her growing up. It was at this point in time that Vivica realized something. She had a chance to change a little girl's life for the better. A chance to open up the world to her, and not pigeon-hole Mary into something she didn't want to be. "...with a lot of hard work." Mary looked down at the replica World Championship in her arms, as did Vivica... who was missing her own. For a moment there, all the problems of the week seemed to go away. Even if it was just a little bit. All the back-stabbing, all the favoritism and politics... gone. "Anyway, I just wanted to wish you luck against Steve tonight." Lia said to Vivica, folding her arms over her chest. "I'm sure you're going to need it. He has to have something planned." Yeah... her match. Wait, what was she going to do with Mary during her match? "Shit!" Vivica said outloud. "SHOOT! I MEANT SHOOT!" The Fearless Phenom looked down at Mary hoping the impressionable little girl hoping that she didn't just hear that. Vivica looked over at the clock hanging in the hallway wall. Her match was in about 10 minutes. She came to the arena ready... but... "Lia!" Vivica said, before her best friend started to walk away, "Can you come here for a minute?" The former World Champion felt almost like she was walking into a trap, taking tiny steps towards The Fearless Phenom as Vivica looked down at the little girl and said "I need to talk to Lia for just a moment, okay?" Mary nodded and sat down on the cold concrete floor. She began to rummage through her autographed items as Lia walked over to Vivica and they took a few steps away from the little girl, trying not to let her in on the conversation. "I need a HUGE favor." Vivica stated, with a little bit of a worry in her voice. "You don't want me to watch her... do you?" Lia said, as if she could read Vivica's mind. "Just during the match... I SWEAR I'll be fine afterwards." Vivica spoke with a smirk, "I mean it's Steven Caldera! He's got that bum knee right?" "Yeah but I don't know how to watch a little girl!" Lia retorted. ?Do you realize... I mean... do I look like the motherly type?!? "I didn't know either Lia, I swear it's EASY! She's such a nice little girl." "The only kid I've ever had to watch is Seymour!" Lia said, "I don't know..." "You'll be fine!" Vivica said putting her hand on Lia's should, "I REALLY have to go." "But..." "Mary!" Vivica looked over the little girl, whose head shot up off the ground... her expression almost looked like a puppy with its ears perked up, "Lia is going to watch my match with you from the backstage area! It's coming up soon so I have to go. BE NICE TO LIA. Okay?" "Yeah!" Mary said getting up off the ground and picking up all her stuff. "Good luck Miss Valentine!" "Thanks Mary! Thanks Lia!" Vivica said as she scampered off towards the gorilla position for her upcoming match. Lia just stood there slightly in shock watching Vivica disappear down at the end of the hallway. It wasn't long before Lia felt the little eyes of Mary Sue Venton fixated on her. In awkward silence Lia just stood there and looked back at the little girl, before finally Mary decided to break the silence herself. "So... what's your favorite color?" A sly smile came over Lia's face as she considered the answer. ?...pink.? ---- CHAPTER THREE: A Hero's Welcome ---- Victory felt great. The sounds of "Image Of The Invisible" still rattled through the arena as Vivica made her way up the ramp, with the GCW World Heavyweight Championship now firmly back in her hands where it belongs. She couldn't hide the smile on her face as she turned around on the stage and lifted the title above her head to the cheers of thousands in attendance. Pointing around the arena, she could feel the adrenaline rushing through her veins as the sweat dripped down her forehead. With a deep breath she turned around and pushed the black curtain aside leading into the backstage area. "WE ARE THE IMAGE OF THE INVISIBLE!" A large group of people chanted loudly as she found herself shocked in the backstage area. Mark Bingham was there from the Make-A-Wish Foundation. As was Lia Ambrosi, Mary Sue Venton, both of her parents and Ayake Sonoda. All chanting in unison. A laugh escaped Vivica's lips as Mary run over to her and gave her a hug around her left leg. "You were so amazing! You taught those bad guys who were boss!" Mary said excitedly, "You're my best friend EVER." "I try my best." Vivica spoke through heavy breaths. The match against Steven Caldera was hard fought... probably more so than she thought it would be. She made some mistakes, but Mary didn't see any of those. She looked right past all of her flaws... big or small. "That's the real world title!" Mary said pointing at the strap now hanging from Vivica's shoulder. "Can I touch it?!" "Of course." The Fearless Phenom got down on one knee and Mary Sue reached out and touched the plate on the GCW World Heavyweight Championship that read Vivica J. Valentine. It looked almost exactly like hers. "Mary..." Jackie Venton spoke up, "I think it's time we go hun, they're starting to pack up around here and I'm sure Miss Valentine probably has to leave pretty early in the morning so she can go to another town and meet other little girls." "Do I have to?" Mary whined as her mother simply nodded. The little girl put her head down in disappointment. She was just a normal, happy little girl... and her hero was Vivica J. Valentine. Little did she realize that Vivica herself was inspired that day. She'd faced some adversity in her time... Christian Zenith, The Ascension, The Establishment... and she'd let them get the best of her from time to time. But never had she faced anything quite like cancer. Vivica was one tenacious competitor but Mary... Mary might've been the true hero in that room. "Hey wait a minute..." Vivica said, "Do you have a pen and paper?" Mark Bingham stepped forward with a pen and a notepad, handing it over to the World Champion. Vivica started jotting down on the paper with the pen as everyone watched in curiosity. "This is my address and cell phone number." Vivica said with a smile, ripping the paper off the notepad and handing it over to Mary, "If you ever need anything... just write or call and I'll get back to you as soon as I can." "Wow..." Mary said looking at the paper. "That's very nice of you Miss Valentine." Mary's dad said as the little fan turned around and handed it to her father. "But we have to get going now, Mary has to get some sleep because we have to go to the hospital for a check-up on her cancer treatment tomorrow." "Awww..." Ayake Sonoda said. "It's okay..." Mary said with a smile, "They give me stickers." "Get one for me, okay?" Vivica said. "I'll get you a bunch!" Mary said as mom and dad started to wave goodbye. "Say goodbye to Miss Valentine!" Jackie told her daughter. "Bye Miss Valentine! I'll miss you!" Mary said giving Vivica a big hug. The Fearless Phenom patted the young fan on the back, responding with "I'll miss you too." Letting go Mary waved good bye as she followed her parents and Mark Bingham down the hallway. As she sat there watching Mary walked away... something occurred to Vivica. In the past couple of months being the greatest champion of all-time meant so much to her. But in the eyes of Mary Sue Venton she was something so much more than a champion. She was something that Vivica herself would tell Jordan White the following week that she wasn't. She was a hero. Even with all of her imperfections Mary Sue Venton accepted her as the single greatest human being walking the Earth. If she was so inspirational to a girl as strong as Mary... there was no limit to what she could do. She felt like she could feed the world, cure cancer, establish world peace. What she had was a gift. A gift that could touch millions... and the World Heavyweight Championship was her medium. Retaining her World Title was about so much more than her own personal gain. It was about the responsibility that it held. She couldn't let Jordan White take her title away to teach all the little girls out there that it's okay to disregard the rules as long as you're getting paid more for it. She couldn't let Jay Terror take her title away and teach all the little girls that if you want something just bully someone out of it. In a world full of the horrors of reality, she had to fight for the millions and millions of people out there who looked up to her for something to believe in. Even if for one day they forgot about all the people dying in a war, or all the people dying of starvation, or the fact that cancer would one day take their life... she'd helped them all. The weight of the world was on her shoulders... and if someone wanted to take the championship from off of her shoulder, they would have to rip it from her unconscious body. Because no matter how hard the obstacles that stood in her way seemed to be, or whatever unexpected twists and turns life would throw at her Just like Mary Sue Venton... she would never give up.
