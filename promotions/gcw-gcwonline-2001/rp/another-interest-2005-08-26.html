Another Interest

26 August 2005

Date: August 26, 2005 Now with a victory notch severed deep into his belt, Coyle O’Conner has great feelings and a great attitude towards all of life’s entity’s; despite how maliciously life treated him prior to the match. Coyle doesn’t necessarily feel invincible, however, no one can bring him down, not at this moment. Feeling amazingly ambushed with energy and ready for his next match, whenever that may be, he is looking to continue on with his warpath with beautiful success. Not only does he want to pursue his career and make a name for himself, he wants to start fighting for something incredibly valuable. Acknowledging the return of the Cruiserweight Championship title, Coyle has been considering giving this bout a shot. “Cruiserweight Championship, eh” That just might not be a bad idea, not a bad one at all?” Pain, anguish, suffering, attacking, success; it’s all going to be part of the path to the Cruiserweight title, but Coyle still isn’t thinking twice. His mind is set now, and without halt. “I can see it now, Coyle O’Conner, the Cruiserweight Champion of the World?” Coyle emergently springs out of his chair, and at a quick pace, he gets to his weight room. Yes, in a house with just a small kitchen, one bedroom, a bathroom, and a laundry room, he has decided that having a weight room is more important than having a living room. Alone in his bachelor house, there is no need for something fantastic as he says, thus is why what he has is just fine. Resting in his spacious weight room are 4 things; the bench, squat, leg press, and dip station. He also has free weights for cleaning and dead lifting. Forming a thick border around the room are tons of tubs of whey-protein. You can probably guess Coyle takes fitness seriously by now, and you would be right. When he worked out last week, this is where it happened. When he works out this week, this is where it’ll happen. And if he grabs hold of the Cruiserweight Championship title, it’ll be because of here, where it starts to happen. Getting a good-stretch in, and also cracking some knuckles and what not, Coyle looks around. His desire is now steadily consuming him, and it is now clicking within him. As soon as he completed his stretches, he calmly walks over to his stereo in the room, and hits play. Intensely and loudly, “Happy” by Mudvayne streams out of the speakers. “Exactly what I’m in the mood for.” Starting his way over to the squat station, his head starts filling with rhythm to the song. He squats under the bar and positions himself into the right formation, still beating his head to the song. Just as the chorus chimes in, Coyle is lifting the bar on his back from his legs, and starts yelling the song as he does his reps. “Peel me from the skin, tear me from the rind!” Yelling very loudly, lowering himself? “ugh, Does it make you happy now!” Now raising himself to work his legs? “Tear me from the bone, tear me from myself!” Still yelling with a voice sounding adrenaline-filled? “Are you feeling happy?” Three sets of 8 repositions later, Coyle rests outside of the squat station, stretching his quads. “This is for the Cruiserweight Title, that’ll make me happy.” The song is just ending, and “Seasons” by Sevendust starts to fade on. Still with the mindset of getting better, Coyle unmercifully works himself out some more, taking his career seriously now, and that championship belt. It’s what he does now that leads him to another victory, and hopefully, to a reward. “No more dicking around.”
