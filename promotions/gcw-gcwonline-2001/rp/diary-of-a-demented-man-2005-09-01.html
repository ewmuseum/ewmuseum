Diary of a Demented Man

1 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/1/05 *A dark silloute of a large man stands before you in a dameaning manor, the only thing that can be seen in his eyes. Dark Green eyes peer into your own, and seem to be looking straight into your very soul, and as the silloute blinks the once dark green eyes not are a very bright red, and that one time look of nothingness has now turned into a look of utter hatred towards you or towrads someone else* *A white flash can be seen off in the distant, and all of a sudden the flash travels down the street illuminating it all, and as it gets to you, you are blinded, and seem to fall to the ground with a sudden pain in your head* ----- Flashback Starts ----- *Last time i wrote to you I was running from the polce for what i thought was for the murder of my father in our home in vegas. That is what I get for thinking that, as I open my eyes in my room my father, who I thought was dead stands over me, his eyes red, and bloodshot, the anger in his eyes would scare even a convicted felon in prison on death row.* *As I try to stand up he pushes me back down with such force i hit my head on the head board of my bed, and it busts me open, as blood begins to pour out of this gash in my head I actually get htis feeling in my head of anger towards this man who raised me, and brought me up to show my true emotions, but the thing is how can i show my true emotions to a man who could probably kick my ass as much as I could kick his* 'I thought you were dead, Dad', I said 'That's what you get for thinking you young SOB', he said *As he says this he lifts his right hand back, and gives me a nice staright jab to my mouth, as blood begins to pour from mouth. I feel something moving freely around my mouth, as I spit it out I see that it is a tooth that has been broken in half.* 'You broke my tooth', I said *He begins to laugh, and trys to real back, and hit me one more time in the mouth, but this time I ctahc him of guard, and block his hit, but i wasn't expecting he to come with a left uppercut which knocks me off my feet, and drops me down to the floor with such force i crack, and break some of the wood flooring. As I begin to stand up, I get a shooting pain in my back, as i reach around, and try to touch where the pain is I feel a piece of the wood flooring sticking out* 'Your trying to kill me aren't you', I said 'Good guess son, but the thing is I want you to feel the pain i felt when you knocked me out cold, and I wound up in the hospital' *With that said he reals back for another hit, but this time i'm ready, and when he trys to swing I duck out of the way, and give him a right uppercut of my own, which sends him to the floor with such force he breaks the wood flooring. I stand over him, with the same look he looked at me with, then I start to laugh as he sits on the floor looking at his own son, who just clocked him in the mouth* *He stands up, and walks out the doorway, but before he does he turns back, and looks at me. His eyes show the same hate, as they did before but this time one eye is swollen shut, and blood in trickling out of his mouth. I look straight back with the same hatred. Both of use are bloody, battered, and brusied, but in my mind I had one hell of a time* ----- Flashback End ----- *I stand in the same roomwhere that fight happened, the two dents still in the floor, the blood all over the place like it just happened yesterday, as I stare at the mess a single tear rolls down my face, as I remember my father, and the man who I looked up to once in my life, and a man that now is six feet under i think* 'I havent talked to you in awhile dad, if you can here me. I'm glad we had that fight that night it should me something, you should me something. That something is that no matter how much pain your in, no matter how much blood you loss or are lossing at the one point in time. When you fight with your heart, and your emotions you can never lose, but if you do you know you did your best to put on a show for everyone to know' *I get a starnge feeling in my head, and I black out only to wake up to a strange place where I have never been before. A tall hill stands before me, nothing around me, as I look around a single oak tree stands on top of the hill, with a stone object. As i move up the hill, the stone object starts to take shape, and when i get right up to it I see that it is a headstone.* Here Lies a Man Who Died with One Son He truly Loved Him Please Pray For Me, Rick *Was this my fathers grave?* *Was this the place where the man I looked up to is finally buried, and is now resting in peace?* 'Rick?' *I look around there is nobody there, and there is nobody * 'Rick?' 'Who are you?'. I reply 'I am you, or I was you back then' 'Your me', I replied 'Yes' *I shadowy figure steps out from behind the tree, and yes it was me, but a lot younger. I looed to be the same age when me, and my dad had that fight. The figures face is still brusied, and bloodied* 'That fight, dad and we had was the turning point where dad respected us more, and we respected him more' 'I can't here this right now. You see i try to put that point of my life behind me, and i thought I did, but now I see that putting it in the back of my mind was a bad idea' *all of a sudden the figure disappears, and I wake up in real life* 'Now with all that down the drain I can finally talk about my match, Meatshield where the hell did u get that name I mean come one it sounds like a pornstars name, but other than that. Meatshield Trainwrecker we are both big men, and yes this will be a battle of the giants, but the thing is what do i have more than you.' 'Weight, No' 'Height, probably' 'Experience, you damn right' 'Meatshield Trainwrecker the last time we met you had your little tag team partner the rogue paladin to help you this time the two big men will get to play in the yard without the little kids getting in our way, but I got to say why get yourself involved with a man who thinks he is stuck in a roleplay game. I mean The Rogue Paladin come on, get out of your mom's basement, get a job, gorw some facial hair, and job the real world. Meatshield Trainwrecker I will see you at worldwide, and don't bring your RPG buddy'
