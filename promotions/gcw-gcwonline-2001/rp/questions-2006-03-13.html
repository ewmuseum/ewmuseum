Questions...

13 March 2006

Date: 3/13/06 Tag Team Action at Rampage. Someone will leave, well some team, as the new number one contender to take on the team who wins between either Almasy and his soon to be picked partner, and the Guild. All nerds in the main tag team event? Oh, it's obvious. Well it could be nerds and one other, since the other three can't even be compared to Almasy's partners for not being nerds. But the match for the new number one contendership fares with for teams who are just that... teams. Not like Almasy and some makeshift partnership. It almost goes to wonder why he got the final seed in that gauntlet a while back. It's almost as if they're still trying to push him like he was when we got here. Seymour Almasy, the golden boy of GCW. When we got here he was in a contract dispute, mostly because he wanted to make up for the money that he lost when Global folded and then reopened. He let the cat out of the bag, and showed the world who Emoticon was. But then his contract was signed. He either got what he wanted, or they did. Or both... But then look at his recent history in the ring. How can they bank on him to overcome even a couple of simpletons that still play Dungeons and Dragons at their age? They should see a meeting in one of the Order halls and get to see what a real troll looks like, or a real redcap goblin... Let alone look across the ring one day to see someone whose Irish side comes from a race that was the basis for Tolkien's, and subsequently Gygax's elves... That's right the Sihde of Tir Na Nog, the Tautha De Dannan as they call themselves. The family that gave birth to Blaise McClancy, or as he's known as in the Seelie Courts of Tir Na Nog, Turren MacOgma, Prince of the Tautha. Blaise is a Hunter of the Order of the Hammer. He's fought creatures to help enforce that which the Order does enforce upon the 'creatures of the night'. The fact that they will not violate the rules presented upon all of the immortal and immoral races outside of humanity. They will not violate the veil and show their true selves to things with fragile minds. When they do, the Order comes and cracks down upon them. It's spawn from it's history, from the days of it's oldest conflicts. The children of their enemies are the most likely to show themselves. Hell we have a few in GCW right now. But they've worked around the rules, because they don't really show their powers to others. And if they do, it's in a ring, on Television. ?It's all camera trickery! They're not real.? The Lord of the Order has given that the Okay. But he's ordered his own followers to infiltrate and watch them. And that they have. In GCW alone you have a collection of what some would call a ?rag tag band? of people. But they've come under the banner of the KnightMare Brigade, the Order's special forces group. And they've come also under the banner of the Native American Nations. Team Baked? You think you're funny? You think you've got what it takes to stand in front of the Order? Of the Brigade? We've had members like you before. You're a rehash of a team that is on the UWF hall of fame! And they did it better! Old news, old ideas. Tropical Storm was much better at it. The Foundation? Who? What? Some say that the Foundation is the strongest point of a building. That may be true, but if you want to tear it down in one fell stroke, you take that part down. You bring it all crumbling down after that. But then you have the other team in this Number One Contendership match up... Got news for the rest of the boys, it's two teams waving the same banners in the same match. If you think you have a chance? You're either too stoned or too stupid to realize it. We can't discount Unite though. Josh and Gray know us better then anyone else on the roster. They train with us. We all know each other about the same. But we have the experience of being former two time tag team champions. We... Blaise ?Hazard? McClancy and ?Tenshi No Arashi? Ukyo Kushinaga FutureShock! (Source: Webster's Online Dictionary, modified by me) In this case, we are the superior team. Get used to it. ~Ryokan Inn~ ~Mt. Fuji, Tokyo Side~ Dani woke up with a start. Josh had already left the bed. He had started letting her sleep in, and she hatted it. What was worse was the fact that his brother had been letting him do it. That wasn't good. She didn't like getting special attention. Then again after the last few nights, particularly the night down in Osaka, they had been letting her do just about anything. She had nothing lined up for Rampage now, after Bradley had cheated to get the win over here in the tourney. Good to see that Shadow put him down again. She sat up and looked around the inn room they had now shared for almost a month together. Josh and Gray in this one. But Gray had taken to sleeping in the living room area, away from the 'two lovebird' as he called them. All was fine, soon they could head back to the states. She could take the information from her Grandfather Morning Star and figure out his riddle to her. She had to find the man with two souls. She had to figure out who this new enemy was, and figure out how to defeat him. ?I really hate the feeling of sleeping in. Too groggy. I'll turn into a coffee freak like Josh if I keep this up.? She got up and looked around a bit; no notes, nothing showing that anything was going on. In only her boy short panties and t-shirt, she headed out to the common area. ?Gray?? No answer from him. Time for a cup of Josh's special blend coffee, guaranteed to be illegal in most third world countries due to caffeine content. As she poured the cup of java, she noticed something that peaked her interest. Gray's laptop computer was out, sitting on what made for a coffee table here in Japan. He'd apparently been working on it when they left. And of course, Gray was a computer user, but wasn't perfect. He'd left it on and it had gone to a screen saver. ?I wonder...? She walked on over and took a seat down onto the mat that he'd used, a small cushion under her backside. As she touches the keyboard, it quickly goes to his desktop. ?What's this?? Sitting on the desktop is an icon, one with the coat of arms symbol for the Order of the Hammer. Under it sat a very weird way of saying what it was for ?Order.net? was all it said. ?Time for curiosity to kill the cat, I guess.? She took the mouse pad over and double tapped, a splash screen with the coat of arms came up, asking for a user name and password. The user name was already filled in, ?Brand, G.S.? The password was blank though. ?Oh, man. What would you use?? She started looking around. ?Wasn't his wife's name Jean or Jeannie? His son was Benjamin, I know that.? She typed a few things, each one giving her an access denied error. ?Damnit!? That's when she saw the small journal book under the table, apparently it had fallen down. She picked it up and opened it up, flipping through until she got to a middle page with many thing scratched out on it. It was a list: jeannie benny benjamin april10 december18 february14 All of those scratched out, the easy stuff, ex-wife, son, her birthday, his, and their wedding anniversary. But there was one that wasn't struck out: Catherine. ?Who? What?? A name she didn't know. But she typed it in all the same and was greeted with a message that she'd successfully logged into the Order's research center for Field Operatives. ?A research center? A library...? She immediately brought up the search feature and started typing in what she wanted to find out about. ?Evening Star.? ~Okinawa, Japan~ ~The Order's South Wind Academy~ Blaise climbed out of the car and took a deep breathe. He'd flown down here overnight on an Order provided Jet, alone. Ukyo was back at the Inn, watching things. He looked around, longingly. This was his home away from home as far as he was concerned. This was the first Order Academy he'd graduated from, under it's headmaster, Kenji Kogagawa. Now a Captain under the Order, Kogagawa had offered the use of his facilities if Blaise needed it while they were in Japan. And now, he needed the facilities that a permanent location provided. He looked on as one of the trainee's, an acolyte by rank walked out to greet him. The man bowed deeply. Blaise was wearing his hunter's gear, but marked for his academy school and rank... he'd come on business and had to look the part. ?McClancy-san, you are expected by the headmaster. If you'll follow me. He said you were on a tight schedule and to take you directly to the main uplink.? ?Thank you, lead the way please.? Blaise followed the small, wiry man as he headed into the compound. An old Feudal Era Castle, now a training center for the Order's South Asia operations. They walked past a sea of people in different colored robes and martial arts guis. Each marking their style, rank and if they were already ordained into the Order or not. Some only came here to learn how to ?kick ass!? But some, some showed promise and were introduced to the Order, just as Blaise had. Originally he'd denied it, and refused to join. But that is a thing of the past. We all learn from our mistakes, right? It didn't take them long though, either that or Blaise's reminiscing made time fly here. Soon they were in a room in the main building with a large electronic setup inside. A few terminals hooked to a main computer network, a secured and encrypted network. Blaise looked up at the control room for it all. They were also near the main training facility for those who were... special. This was the place where he'd awakened and accepted his path as a Garou a year and a half ago. And now he was back, but this time, not as a new selectee for the Hunters, but as a special assignment leader. A voice boomed over the loudspeaker. ?Ah, Blaise-san, terminal two is ready for you. Leanan-sama is already waiting for you.? ?Damn, you guys move fast.? The acolyte showed him to the terminal in question. As soon as Blaise sat down the screen came up with the face of a rather striking brunette woman. Aine ?Annie? Leanan, the former betrothed of his cousin, his commander on this case, and head research officer of the Order under Fey and Ian. Her thick, Irish accent cascaded out of the speakers. ?Blaise, 'ow th' 'ell are ye. I 'ear ye ga' somethin' fer me?? ?Yeah, I do. Did you read the report I sent in?? ?Aye, I did. Interesting tha' the Morning Star 'as revealed 'imself. Ye say ye could'na understand Dani when she was talkin' t' 'im.? ?Nope, it was like she was speaking in tongues or something. So was he. They spoke like they could understand each other though.? Annie looked to be thinking a bit. ?I've 'eard th' Keeper when 'is 'guest' speaks sometimes to some of the others. It's a language like no other. I take it yer 'ere t' transfer me th' file ye ga' through the network, riogh?? ?Of course.? Blaise reached down to the belt that held a lot of his tools for when out on the hunt, one of them a device not unlike a digital recorder, but with a small SDCard loaded into it for quick removal and replacement. He pulled the card out and inserted it into the computers terminals card reader. A quick few clicks of the mouse sent the file streaming across the network with a status bar indicating it's delivery rate. ?So, if you guys have heard this speech before, you've got a means of translating it, right?? Annie looked up from her second monitor on a similar setup to his. ?No. The Sereph tha' co-habitates with our Keeper o' Secrets 'as told us tha' it's basically Babel. The Angels never stopped usin' the one world language. Bu' there is nothin' on this planet tha' can translate it. Bu' we'll try. I've never 'ad a recordin' o' it either.? The status bar indicated that it had fully transferred the file over and Blaise closed the window for the program. ?Quality check...? Blaise knew the procedures. He could hear Annie's speakers playing the gibberish that was in the recording. ?Appears t' be fine. I skipped through it, I ga' abou' a five minute recordin'? ?That's correct.? ?Good. So, wha' d' ye think?? ?I think it's a warning.? Annie smiled. ?Why?? ?Because of a few things. The first thing is the guy we found jumped in Oklahoma, he stole something, right?? ?Riogh', my mental recordin' o' th' nigh' tha' Dani first manifested.? ?The other thing is the fact that he openly admitted to me that he had done it to test Ukyo and I. He had seen both of the Brands do their deeds before, but we were unknown to him.? ?So he was a' testin' 'er new defenders.? ?Yup. And after I caught the same signature hanging out around Dani after one of her dreams, he's trying to tell her something.? ?'E's capable 'o speakin' our languages though, why would 'e use their own tongue t' give 'er a warnin'.? ?I think he wants her to figure it out for herself.? Annie laughed. ?But yer goin' t' 'elp 'er ou'?? Blaise smiled back at her. ?As much as I can. I'm not the researcher, you are. I hate books, remember. Not unless they were written by Tolkien, Lackey, Norton, Gemmel or Eddings.? ?Some o' those are good research material. Ye'd be surpised 'ow many o' our books Gemmel used fer 'is stuff.? Blaise ignored her comment the best he could. She took the hint. ?Well I'll get workin' on this, as best I can.? She looked at her other screen and then smiled wide. ?Hmm, Gray is logged int' th' network. What work is 'e doin'?? ?Gray is?? ?Yeah, usin' our version o' the Encyclopedia Mythica, searchin' aroun'.? Blaise's eyes narrowed a bit. ?Well? I thought Gray was heading out for a bit of alone time.? Blaise pulled out another thing off of his uniform and put it to his ear. It buzzed as he called out to his partner. ~Back at the Inn~ Ukyo was working on her own stuff, and interrupted by the sound of beeping. She was sitting in a lotus position in the common area of the inn cabin, looking to be meditating. She rolled a bit to pull her heels out from under her and then headed to the open bag of gear sitting on a chair. Pulling out a similar device to what Blaise was using she put it up to her ear. ?Hai!? On the other side, Blasie spoke to her. ?Hey, Dear? Is Gray around?? ?I don't know?? ?Can you do me a favor and check?? ?What? Why?? ?It's important. Don't let them know you're doing it either. Use your goggles or something.? Ukyo frowned, Blaise was up to something. She pulled the communicator away from her ear and set it down. Reaching back into the bag she pulled out a pair of what looked like electronic goggles, shaped like swimming goggles to the untrained person, but the lenses were black with some red lights on them. She slid them over her head, not worrying about her hair as she did. Her right hand came up and pushed a small button on the side of the right eyepiece. The red lenses then lit up as she looked around towards the other cabin. A few more sweeps and she pulled up the communicator again. ?Your suspicions are confirmed.? Blaise chuckled on the other end. ?No Brand in sight. But a figure that looks not unlike Dani is seated in her common area. Only got heat, but neither of them has that hourglass figure. In front of her is what looks like a laptop computer? ?Thanks, baby.? Ukyo hung up the communicator and took the goggles off her head. ?Okay, what are you up to now?? ~South Wind Academy~ ~Okinawa~ Blaise put up his communicator as Annie asked the obvious. ?It's not Gray is it?? ?Nope.? ?Well then for you, the first search term is stored in the cache on the server.? ?Alright? Annie looked at him between the two screens. ?Evening Star.? Blaise just nodded as Annie started working her magic from her end. ?Evening Star, the enemy that was vanquished forever to a cavern by the Morning Star in the Sioux Mythos.? Annie stopped. ?Ye sure o' tha'?? ?Yup. You won't find much without that.? ?No, they aren'na like the Cherokee with a long tradition o' writin' down their stuff. But if this Evenin' Star is the mortal enemy o' Mornin' Star.? Blaise nodded again. ?Then he's one of them. One of the Angels that fell from the realm of Heaven.? Annie's last word was something that Blaise expected. ?Bloody 'Ell!? ~Back at the Inn~ Ukyo had decided that she needed to go check on Dani, and maybe get some more information for Blaise. She headed over and knocked on the door to her cabin. ?Coming!? Dani quickly exited the application she was working in and closed down the top of the portable computer. She headed back to her room and tossed on a pair of sweat pants before answering the door. ?Yeah?? Ukyo couldn't help but grin at her. ?Busy?? ?Not really, doing some reading.? ?Sounds like a bore.? Dani didn't answer her. Ukyo noticed the uneasiness. She saw Dani looking at her strangely. They'd not talked much since that night in Osaka a few days ago. It went on for a few moments until Ukyo decided to break the silence. ?You won't see anything unless I either manifest or you do.? Dani blushed. ?Sorry. The other night was a shock to me. Here I thought you were just a sweet little woman...? ?I am. To a point. But when I need to, I'm pretty good at doing what I'm trained to do. I'm a hunter like Blaise is. We're partners in that too.? ?I can tell, you broke that woman's neck. Why?? Dani backed up and let Ukyo come in a bit. ?Because she was going to cut your heart out. You couldn't understand her. I'm Japanese. She had been paid by your grandfather to test us, but I'd assume he told her that you were not to be harmed. So she decided to double cross him. See what made you so special.? ?Oh!? Dani was stunned. Ukyo had actually saved her. ?What are you, Ukyo?? ?What am I? Like you're an Angel? Like Blaise is a mutt of two cultures?? ?Yeah.? ?I'm a Kami, an Earth Spirit of Japan according to the Order. My family always said we were special. Both me and my brother had special abilities when we were young.? ?And you didn't see it any different?? ?No, we're Shinto, I've always seen spirits, or Kami as natural things. Up until just after World War II our emperor always claimed to have divine bloodline to our Sun Goddess, just like the Pharaohs of Egypt. For us, like your people, the Sioux, spirits of nature who intermingle with humans are commonplace.? Dani just took what she said as fact. She'd never tried to compare the peoples of the world. ?However, my powers are impressive over here, but they're not so back in the States. We're linked to the realms here. Although Blaise is the same way with his Faery half. In Ireland he's very powerful. Not as much as his cousins, but all the same...? She trailed off as she looked around. ?Ukyo... tell me something?? ?Yes?? ?What is the Order?? The question took Ukyo a bit off guard. ?Huh?? ?What is the Order? I've worked with them for my training, but I've never actually joined them. I know that Gray took me under his wing then they told him I was different, they assigned him as my Guardian. But I'm not one of you... I want to know about it.? Ukyo had never expected such a question. She sat down on one of the chairs. ?Thats a big question. Because to tell y' about the Order I have to start way back at the begining.? Dani pulled up another chair. ?Well I'll start some tea, you start talking.? Ukyo smiled at that, and as Dani got Tea she just that. ~End For Now~ Note: Dani Northsky appears with permission from her handler for the parts at the Ryokan Inn.
