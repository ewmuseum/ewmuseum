Getting Even, Part E / Adding to the Arsenal, Part A

Chapter XXV The three suits accompanying Buffalo’s Bad Boy were no bigger than he was, which is why he never saw the point to the security detail, but there are times they prove to be useful. He often wonders if Caldera mandates them as damage control – since most of the roster has legal issues and violent tempers – or is he manufactures henchmen from an underground lair. "I’m taking a piss," Danny realizes. "See you girls in a minute." His escorts all eye him, then each other, and then focus on their assignment again. The tension grows when he pulls out a pair of sunglasses, smirking before sliding them over his suspicious eyes, and pats the closest man on the back. "Relax Alice," he jokes. "I’ll be back in ten." He peels off from the group and heads for the doors. One of the guards spots the pack of cigarettes and lighter in Danny’s hand and gives the assuring nod to his teammates. They all disperse to keep track of the target. Danny makes sure to check their position as he gets through the doors but notices they’ve since separated. Stepping onto the sidewalk, Danny’s freedom is rewarded with the smell and sight f busy city traffic and the noise of everyone and their mother cruising the busy streets. It amazes him how different two parts of the same state can be, how Buffalo compares to the Big Apple, and shakes his head. He never thinks of himself as a city boy but he knows why others might. "Cowboy killers?" a familiar voice says in Danny’s ear. "You really do have a death wish." He refuses to turn around because of the eyes on him nearby but recognizes the southern, sweet sound of a young girl’s drawl that he could never forget, especially after their past together. Francisco shrugs, "I don’t fuck around." "Jesus," the girl says under her breath. "Is there someplace we can talk?" Danny takes a slow drag, turning against the grain of the pedestrian traffic, and shakes his head quickly. He exhales slowly, spotting an odd amount of policemen on the adjacent corner from them. "Not here," he looks around for any sign of his surveillance squad and takes another drag. "Where are you staying?" She steps out in front of him, raising her hand toward the onslaught of taxis approaching the curb, and keeps her back to Danny. The mere sight of her is enough to give him a rush of blood to the head and sends his heart into a frantic, sporadic beat. Of all the things he wanted to get done in New York City this weekend, he never thought a rendezvous with his past was in the cards. "The Hotel Deluxe, Room 68," she says as a cab pulls up. He recites it three times in his head before glancing over his shoulder. He spots one of the guards just outside the door with eyes on him. Unaware, Lacy spins around and flashes her pearly whites at the budding celebrity. She cracks open the cab before turning away, "I’ll be there." He pitches the butt to the ground before looking through his loose bangs at Lacy’s big brown eyes. The moisture in her eyes resembles stars twinkling for a brief second. Danny’s hair flies into the air thanks to a giant gust and it serves as her cue to dive into the cab and speed off into the mass of glass and steel. "Old friend?" the guard asks with less sincerity than a text message. Danny’s keen to the tone and wonders which route to travel. He decides on the more invigorating and drives his fist into the man’s gut and rocks him with an elbow to the jaw in a fluid motion. With all the people on the sidewalks, the action is not easily noticed, and even so, this is New York City. "That’s none of your damn business Fletcher," Danny slowly rests the man against the outer window of the lobby. After straightening the man’s collar and removing his earpiece, Danny stuffs it into the inner pocket of his jacket, and then watches to see if his unconscious guard sways one way or the other. When the body seems to be simply standing against the glass, he heads to the curb and raises his arm to signal a cab. A yellow taxi comes to a stop at Danny’s feet with screeching wheels. He jumps in quickly and then spots the rest of his entourage racing towards the car, one of them shouting emphatically into his radio, and the other stretching his hand out in all efforts to do his job. Danny can only be modest and offer them a middle finger, mouthing the words that go with it. Chapter XXVI Lacy jumps the second she hears a pair of heavy knuckles wrap against the door. She hesitates for a second before setting down her glass of whiskey. She looks through the peephole and spots something worth sporting a satisfying smile over. "Danny," she practically whips the door open. "Lacy," he says when they embrace emotionally. "I wasn’t sure if I’d see you again." Before the catching up and fun times begin to roll, she steps back, and pulls him into the room. She slowly closes the door and guides Danny into the room. The silence builds a tension in Danny’s gut that makes him feel uneasy, unsure if this is a set up or not. He takes a step back. "That’s what I want to talk about," she picks up another glass of whiskey and hands it to Danny. "I’m not here just to catch up." He cocks his head and decides to mull it over with a sip of sour mash. The sweet burning sensation warms him from the inside out. He eyes Lacy, her figure, her bare shoulder, and then finally decides to bite the bullet. "What is it then?" his tone is soft. "Its money isn’t it?" Her eyes burst and roll, "Oh my Gosh! I can’t believe you think I came all this way for money. What kind of gal do ya take me for?" He says nothing but keeps his eyes locked on her. The lack of response and dead stare in her direction gives a general feeling of disbelief from Danny’s side of the room. He simply sips his whiskey and keeps silent. "Okay," she smiles. "It’s money… kinda." A proud smile stretches over his face briefly, before it returns to uninterested and suspicious. She sips her drink and then heads for a suitcase by the head of the bed. "Yeah," he pauses. "I’m not sure how you think this works but, I’m not paying you anything unless I have a reason to. Hell woman, we haven’t even slept together and you want money from me?" She pulls out a gun, "See?" "Holy Christ," he spits up some whiskey and tries to duck the shot. "Settle down," she snaps. "It ain’t even loaded." Danny finally sits back, relaxes, but still wonders what the hell she’s doing. "I wanna be your bodyguard," Lacy demands with fists on her hips. "So yeah, I want money, to be your bodyguard." For the first few seconds Danny takes her seriously. The boisterous laughter that booms from within after those seconds is what bounces off the walls. Lacy can tell this isn’t going to bean easy sell. He finally gathers himself, "What makes you think I need a bodyguard? If I did, and I don’t, what makes you think I’d hire you? I’ve got a whole-" Lacy shoots out a kick that connects with Danny’s bruised ribs and knocks him down. He does all he can to save the amber spirit from spilling but she grabs that same wrist, wraps it around to his back, and then puts upward force on it. The whiskey drenches his shirt when he yelps. "I said," she lets off the pressure slightly. "I’m gonna be your bodyguard. You’re gonna pay me. I can help." Danny shows her a dumbfound look from over his shoulder, "Help? How?" "Think about it…" Lacy bends over and leans into Danny’s ear. "I know a lot of girls. I can tell which ones are nuts… and which ones are… what’s the term?... DTF?" Danny’s eyes widen. He smiles a familiar smile and nods his head. When Lacy releases the hold, Buffalo’s Bad Boy stands up and eyes her up and down. "You are pretty hot," he thinks aloud. "So fuck, why not?" She smiles and jumps into his arms. He enjoys the gesture and wraps his arm around the small of her back, the most lethal and surefire way to a woman’s heart, and in the young hours of this partnership, things get awkward when Lacy presses her lips into Danny’s. They pull apart to assess how this might work. "You said you were going to find me chicks," Danny says slowly, trying to figure out if this is a setup. Lacy flashes a bright smile and wraps her arms around his neck, "Yeah, I will." Chapter XXVII His eyes slowly roll open, locking onto the burning amber digits the fine people at the Whatever This Is Hotel call an alarm clock, and he immediately jerks backward when the bright red lights burn his unadjusted retinas. A hollow thud comes when he knocks his head on the headboard, though it doesn’t feel anything like a headboard or wood or anything inanimate. Danny sits up slowly, still sore from his run in with Frost and Fujita at WorldWide 146, and clutches his head when he reaches the upright position. Note to self… he squishes his face, closing both eyes. Fuck Triumph Frost up next time you see him. He opens one eye, finally awake enough to think thoughts, and checks the clock – reading 4:10AM – before shaking his head. He can’t believe he’s awake, which reminds him why he woke up in the first place. Figuring he’ll find the TV remote, Danny reaches back but finds something else – something that distorts his expression – something flesh and bone. He looks back to find Lacy, who doesn’t budge when Danny’s hands lands on her bare back with a clap, and he thinks back, trying to piece together the last few things he remembers. That is when El Moreno’s stench runs through his nose and Fujita’s ugly mug flashes through his mind. He shakes his head and refocuses his eyes, casting his gaze out into Times Square. Beyond the building just before him, he spots the sight of NC-17 VIII and the Big East Conference Tournament, MY Madison Square Garden. Lacy tosses over and seems to smile softly and sincerely at Danny. His eyes continue to adjust as he watches her to see if she wakes or not. Suddenly, the feeling of wholesomeness rushes through his veins when his eyes lock onto her luscious, full lips. He thinks about what this partnership might be like in the future, if they become an item, but tells himself not to think about any of that right now. Who the fuck cares? Danny quizzes himself. It finally registers in his recently rattled brain that, though it looks like the ceiling in his room, he is her guest. Danny notices dozens of shopping bags, cocktail dresses, and countless brochures about the Big Apple. When he spots the closest pamphlet, he picks it up and gives it a once over before looking back at the girl. He smiles at the brunette beauty and her sexy style. Struggling through soreness and poor lighting, Danny stands upright and locates his clothes before heading toward the door. He slides his jeans on, his boots, and drapes his shirt over his shoulder. When he gets to the girl’s side of the bed, he kneels down, presses his lips against her temple, and runs a longing finger through her hair. His face looms over hers briefly, trying to think of the right words to whisper, but remembers that, right now, he’s got someplace else to be to ensure they live comfortably for a long time. Danny wraps his hand around the handle and slowly draws the door open, trying to soften the sound of the click of the latch. He looks back one last time and finds Lacy’s brown eyes thanks to the breaching light from the hallway. "It’s okay darlin’. I understand," she smiles. "This is only gonna work if you make it work. Take that bag. Consider it a starter pack." He seems to lock up, "A starter pack for what?" When Danny looks down and spots a black Nike duffel bag at his feet, he picks it up, which takes some effort, before ripping it open to find fresh shoes, workout clothes, and something he didn’t expect. At the bottom of the bag, underneath everything else, his hand comes across an iPod. He turns back to thanks Lacy but realizes she’s already back to sleep. The door clicks when he steps out into the hallway completely. His eyes aim straight ahead, looking for the nearest gym that can get him in shape to kick the shit out of everyone in his way for a GCW championship. Chapter XXVIII The hiss of the shower head dies with a twist of the wrist. Danny whips the curtain back after a grueling workout and getting himself ready for war on Sunday. He grips his towel from overhead and dries off, heading back toward his locker. The familiar sound of Saliva’s Badass comes form his locker as he nears. He flips it open, "Hello?" "Danny Francisco," the scratchy voice on the end starts. "I believe you placed a bet with Mickey Green last week?" Danny pauses for a second and waits to see what happens next. Even though he believes that he was into gambling, drugs, and hookers, it’s somewhat hard to remember how he did it so effortlessly and exactly what he did. "Yeah," he finally replies. "Thirty-five large," the voice continues. "Is that right?" Danny swallows every instinct to lie in case it saves his ass, "Yeah, that’s right." "Congratulations kid," the old man chuckles. "I didn’t even see that one comin’. I thought that Dynasty kid was gonna win. Sure enough. How ya wanna collect your winnings?" Danny lets out a sigh of relief, unsure if they knew it was him or not, and he takes a second to think it over. Showing up there looking like Dynasty was out of the picture so he needed a partner. A partner… Danny stops and smiles. "I’ll be sending one of my associates to collect. A woman. Carmen is her name." "Good. I’ll contact you with the location later in the week," a few seconds pass before the conversation is ended. Danny looks at the phone and smiles. He slides it back into his jacket. He begins to change into his street clothes and occupy his mind by running through a mental strategy of the match. His eyes close from time to time and he shadow boxes briefly, getting lost in the preparation phase, before he finishes getting dressed. Confessional No. 1 'The Prodigy' sits at a table under poor lighting in front of a small video camera. Smoke clouds the room, obviously from cigarettes, as he is smoking one right now. A bottle of Jack Daniels sits to his left, the cigarette smoking ensemble (light, cigarettes, ashtray) and glass of whiskey on the rocks to his right. He slowly exhales more smoke toward the camera. A gold belt is what I came here for… …a gold is what I’m leaving here with. No matter what. The wins, the losses, the ups and the downs all go out the window when GCW takes over Madison Square Garden on Sunday night. Moreno’s streak… Fujita’s recent surge… Messiah’s unpredictability… All of that means jack shit when we step under the bright lights of NC-17 - the biggest night GCW has to offer - and everyone witnesses the birth of the true American Dream, born through the fire of a Fatal Fourway for the chance to become the United States Champion. Not the Mexican-American Champion. The United States Champion. NC-17 has the reputation of being the grounds where legends are born, names are made, and champions come out of contenders. The 8th chapter will be where Dynasty separates himself as the cream from the crop. Those who have what it takes to become champion. Those who don't. then there are those who are given the chance to be given the chance to go from the middle of the road to the express lane. A one way ticket to the top. A ticket with my name all over it.
