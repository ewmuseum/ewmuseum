Sometimes You Have to Break Routine.

25 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/25/07 At 4am every morning, Aislinn Elizabeth Drake, would crack open her eyes and catch a glimpse of nothing but the glow of the nightlight her father had planted in the socket nearby the door. Her eyes would squeeze shut, trying to block the distraction out, but it never worked. It wasn’t the light that kept her up; it was the grumbling in her stomach that brought her to shrieking for her mother every morning by 4:15. Her mother would then reach over to the monitor perched on the nightstand beside her, crank the knob a bit to the left, and prayed that Aislinn would eventually fall back to sleep. It never happened though. Instead, every morning at 4:30, Tessa Windsor would have to climb out of bed, flip on the light, and listen to her fianc? grumble about how early it was, while she tried in vain to find her slippers. Then, once she finally found them, the light would go off and Scott would fall back asleep while she slipped off to the nursery to feed Aislinn. By 5:00, Tessa was struggling to stay awake in the rocking chair, as she wrapped Aislinn up in her velvet pink blanket and tried desperately to rock her two month old back to sleep. Once 5:30 rolled around, Scott would finally be up, but never to help her. Instead, he’d slip into the shower, leaving Tessa catch her last hour of sleep before the other kids needed to be woken up. By 6:30 Scott was heading out the door for the gym, and Tessa was stuck trying to rally the three oldest up for school, which was never an easy task. Then by the time they were gone, Aislinn was up again and Tessa would have to wait until midnight to catch another nights worth of sleep. Until then, she’d have to pick the kids up at 3:00. Then she’d have to play chauffer, dropping the boys off at practice and Christa off at a friend’s house. Every day had grown to be the same. Now, she knew why every woman she knew dreaded becoming a housewife. She took a peak at the Rolex Masterpiece watch Scott had bought her from her birthday, to find that 6:00pm had finally rolled around. In an hour, Scott would finally stroll home, and Tessa could finally hit the gym. Until then, she kept her self occupied desperately trying to cook a decent dinner for the three oldest. Tonight main course: Pepperoni Pizza. She’d already slid the pizza into the oven, now she’d have fifteen minutes to kill before it was finished. There was plenty she had to do in that time. "Evan, Chris!" She called out to her two sons, "Come set the table." At least the children provided her with some kind of support, sometimes. "But mommmm?." Evan whined from the living room, where he planted in front of the 42" inch television to watch the Fairly Odd Parents. Chris hadn’t said a word, but looked up at his step mother, with the same kind puppy dogs eyes as Evan, "Can we wait until the commercials?" Tonight obviously wasn’t going to be one of those times. "Just forget it." Tessa sighed aloud, slamming the cabinet doors shut They boys had no clue that their mothers actions were meant to drop a hint. After all, their father wasn’t quite the master of picking up on her cues either. "Maybe you ought to just take out some paper plates," Tessa’s thirteen year old niece turned daughter hinted, as she doodled a pattern of hearts and flowers on the side of the notes she’d taken down inside her Mathematics class, "you know they’re not going to sit down at the table and eat anyways." Again Tessa sighed, though this one was much milder, "I suppose I ought to." Why she ever agreed to take care of all three of them was beyond her at this point. Christa was understandable. She was her niece after all. Her brother would have easily preferred Tessa to take care of Christa over any other. Chris was Scotts, not hers. Naturally, she had to take him in anyways, or suffer Scott guilt tripping her for not forgiving him for his “mistakes”. And Evan, well, she was too kind hearted to pass him down. She had, after all, saved his life, only to find out he had no family. His father had been arrested after killing his wife, Evan mother. How could she possibly tell him no after going through all that? Aislinn’s shriek interrupted that thought. She, just as all the other kids, was yet another surprise. She was certainly a welcomed surprise, definitely not something Tessa would classify as a mistake, but a big surprise nonetheless. Immediately, Tessa halted her course to the pantry, and headed towards Aislinn’s bouncer in the living room, "I’m coming, hold on." "Mom, she’s crying too loud! I can’t hear the TV." Evan complained, snagging the remote off the glass. 35??40?..the volume bar on the television continued to increase until Tessa could manage to muster up enough of her own volume to be heard above it, "Turn it down, NOW." 40?.35?.it jumped all the way back to 25 before Tessa was satisfied with the level of noise emitting from the living room. "Thank you." With Aislinn now in her arms, Tessa retreated back to the kitchen. "You really need a vacation," Christa’s deposited her pencil on top of her notebook, and scooted out of her seat at the kitchen table to help her mother in anyway she could find. "GCW’s my vacation." Tessa replied, one hundred percent serious about what she was saying. All the while, she was trying to juggle Aislinn in one hand and prepare her bottle with the other. Christa dove to the rescue, peeling the bottle of powdered formula away from Tessa’s grasp, "No, that’s your career." "I get to go plenty of places though." Tessa was quick to defend. She’d waited almost a year to step back into the ring again. The last she wanted is to step away from it again, just to take a mental vacation. If she wanted, or more precisely needed one of those, she would have slipped into the oversized bathtub inside her and Chris? bathroom and soaked in a mixture of bubbles and warm water. Of course what kind of thirteen year old girl wouldn’t have a response to that? Certainly not the type Christa was, "You wrestle at those places. You don’t relax like you need to." "Look?" Once again her thoughts would have to wait, as she was interrupted one more. This time it was the oven’s timer going off. "You and Scott hardly ever spend time together." Christa continued on, over the ear piercing noise. This sprung forth Tessa’s catty side, "Well that’s certainly not my fault. It’s your father that won’t take any time off." Had Scott been home and within four feet of her, he likely would have met a face full of sizzling grease, pepperoni’s, and cheese. While Tessa had come to accept that this was the lifestyle she chose to live, nights like this where she had to play the role of both mother and father certainly didn’t make it any easier on her. "Make him." Christa shrugged. Being only thirteen she supposed that all a woman had to do is snap, and the man be there to serve her. If only things were that easy. If they were, Tessa wouldn’t be captivated by the envelope she’d received in the mail the day before. Inside that envelope, she had more than enough to go on that vacation, and then some. Hell, if she wanted, she could buy her own damn place to vacation at. "Who’s that from?" Christa’s eyes caught glance at the envelope which Tessa’s free hand was hovering above. "No one you know, darling." The opened envelope instantly disappeared into the back pocket of Tessa’s hip hugging jeans without any further discussion from Tessa. Christa wasn’t one to be played a fool, "Why’s there so much money in there?" "It’s a bonus. For all I’ve done in GCW." "But you’ve only?" There obviously wasn’t any way Tessa was going to break down and tell her daughter how if she sold out on her fianc?, so for now she’d have to hold off on the topic until she could think of an elaborate enough lie, "We’ll talk about it later." "But?" Christa tried to protest once more, but Tessa pretended not to even hear. "Dinner’s ready." Tessa called out to the boys, who in all likelihood heard the alarm go off several minutes ago and just chose to ignore it. "I’m not hungry." Evan shouted back. "Me either." Echoed the younger Chris. Tessa was not having that as an answer. Not after spending her valuable time in the kitchen, a place she loathed almost as much as the doctor’s office. "Come to the table, NOW." She demanded in a commanding tone, which was quite unusual for her. Without a second protest, Evan clicked the television off, and headed straight for the kitchen with Chris following closely behind him. Handing Aislinn off to Christa to feed, Tessa headed for the oven, while the boys pouted at the table. It figures Tessa would jump into a routine when their favorite television show was on. She always seemed to have these mood swings at the worst times for them. "Man, why do we have to sit down at the table?" Evan whined inadvertently loud enough to where Tessa could hear. "Because, we’re a family. And tonight, we’re spending family time together." Tessa fussed back at him. This time the younger of the two boys, Chris, had a protest, "But dad’s not home yet." "We’ll then he’ll just have to miss out." "Not fair." Evan muttered under his breath quietly enough to where he wouldn’t get snapped at again. Chris on the other hand, didn’t seem to care so long as there was pizza to makeup for it. He grinned from ear to ear as Tessa deposited his paper plate covered with two pizza slices in front of him. "We’re going to start having more dinners together. We don’t spend enough time together as a family." Tessa announced much to Evan’s dismay. Now he’d never get to watch TV while she was around. Just as Tessa went to sit in her seat at the head of the table, there was yet another interruption. This time it was the front door opening. As expected in strolled Scott, with his hair neatly combed and dressed in a fresh change of clothes. "Dad!" Both boys cried out, seeing this as perfectly great excuse to escape from the table. A rare genuine smile spread across the lips of Scott Drake. Wrapping his monstrous arms around the two boys, he kissed both of their foreheads, and stood to the side of the dinner table, looking over at his fianc’e, a bit bewildered, "Wow. Why’s everyone eating at the table?" Tessa’s lips crumbled down into a snarl, "Because that’s what families do!" Scott’s arms assumed a defensive position in front of his chest, as if he expected her to pick up the knife she’d cut the pizza with, and stab him multiple times, "Sorry, didn’t know we were doing the whole family thing now." "Of course you wouldn’t know!" Her arms flailed up in the air, "You’re never home!" Evan, though frightened by his mothers temper, tried to at least take some joy in the fact that her anger wasn’t directed towards him now. Taking a bigger bite of pizza that what could fit in his mouth, he sank back in his chair, and prayed that his adoptive father would tame the beast his mother had become. "Look, I’m sorry." Tip toeing his way over too, he stamped her forehead with a kissSo and hoped that would ease her nerves some, "Why don’t you go hit the gym some. I think you could use some time out of the house. I’ll watch the kids." "Did you miss the whole family time bit?" She snapped, not caring that she was the one wasting their time with her shouting. Again he apologized, this time back away a step, "I’m sorry. You just seem like you need a break." By now everyone’s focus was on Tessa, hanging on what she’d say next and not daring to challenge it. Silence lingered in the kitchen for a moment. Anyone who wasn’t Tessa was scared to even breathe too loud. Finally the guilt had begun to set in and Tessa’s shoulders slowly lowered, "Let’s just eat." Scott nodded to that, and sat down at the opposite end of the table. Beside him he noticed Christa mouth the words,"What was that all about?" The only thing he could offer he was a shrug of his shoulders. He had not the slightest of clues as to what was troubling his fianc?. It was almost as if there was a demon crawling around inside her body, which viciously took a hold of her at the most unpredictable of time and dared not let go until it felt threatened. Something just was not right, and Scott would stop at no end to figure it what it was plaguing his angel.
