The Eve of Something New.

21 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 21, 2005 I’m stepping into uncertainty. This coming WorldWide. Zodiac. I know just enough about her to admit that I know very little. My modified variation of the words of a wise man. “I wasn’t aware cross-gender contention even existed in wrestling.” Jacob Finnergan. My best friend. I weigh that thought carefully, peeling it away to its core. He’s driving. The background cacophony of the radio and muffled harshness of his voice sacrifice that. I’m on speaker phone, which I hate. “Of course not. You’re out of date. The fact that women can compete in a sport with men in total equality frightens you.” Jacob laughs, slapping the dash. He enjoys my small rants and raves. Claiming that if he’s managed to get a rise out of me, he’s accomplished something monumental. Baronial, he says. Maybe so. I just think he’s among the elite few that can make my skin crawl. Call me Rei. Lecturing the roaches that crawl on my food. “That’s harsh.” But it’s true. Jacob’s a convicted man of the past. Women cleaning the house. Women not working. The man wearing pants and the women ironing them. What’s funny is how the women flock to him, regardless. I think he only expresses his views to me. He’s a good man, overall, however. A personality everyone can love, as I’ve said before. Like all men, though, the detriments seep through his pores all too often. Me? I’m Rei. That’s all I need to say for you to understand. “Okay, fine. Truth,” A game we play. Used to play. I smile as it resurfaces. The days of old at my humble abode jack hammering my brain. “Men are generally stronger than women.” Jacob with a possible point for masculinity. A game played with friends. All of us would sit in a circle, tackling a certain subject. “Truth,” as it was labeled by us, is a fond memory of mine in a mental world where the beast feeds. Sorry. I’m good at ruining the mood. My turn. “Truth, Jacob, is that women are generally smarter than men. Therefore physical strength is rendered inadequate.” Defeated, Jacob takes a breath. The idea of men versus women is a debate and argument that neither of us can win. I can’t make him understand my point of view, though I desperately want to. It takes a woman to do that. “Truth, women analyze situations too much. Men are already three steps ahead.” I laugh. Jacob’s waiting on a response. With Jacob’s remark, I’m a wo-man hybrid who thinks too much, and is six steps behind. I can’t argue with that, besides the obvious. I can cogitate whole days away. Day dreaming about the normal and having a vicious mental shellacking with the sudden arrival of my dream’s antagonist. I think about what the psychologist had told me, again. My active imagination proving to be lucrative. Not for me. Maybe for him and his kind. Perhaps that’s what he meant. “Truth, women are surreptitious. Skillful vampires with a dangerous power of seduction. Men are generally pugnacious, often falling minion to a woman’s artistry.” Nothing but net. I congratulate myself for being a traitor. I’ll be hung in the courtyard on the morrow. Call me Rei. A sheep among wolves. “Rebecca would be proud.” The piano player controlling my thoughts strikes a wrong note. Magnum opus ruined. Shifted to neutral while traveling at high speed. Jacob’s the heel who strikes a low blow every chance he can. I’ve got more, but I’ll stop. Point delivered with firm execution. Truth: Rebecca came back and we talked for hours. Discussing our lives and our possibilities. The future and the past. She stayed the night and we talked through most of it. When she finally fell asleep, I covered her with a blanket and guarded her hopefully peaceful dreaming. No sleep for the wicked. When she left the next day, the stitches of a heart in the process of healing broke. She promised to call me next week, after my schedule calmed, and kissed me tenderly. Elated and depressed. Buoyant and sinking. I miss her even more. Jacob doesn’t know about her coming down. I still have quarrel with the fact that he spoke to her about me whenever I looked away. As soon as it fades, he’ll know. Unless Rebecca tells him first. Beating me to the punch like always. Rebecca equals fox. Rei equals turtle. At least I have a shell. “But you win, Rei. Only because I’m bored of the topic.” He laughs. I hear him mutter something under his breath about a passing motorist. Jacob’s road rage is horrible to the point of being comical. “What’s on your agenda for the evening?” I ask. I already know what’s on mine. Long afternoon filled with deliberations and musings. Dissecting Zodiac. Before Global Championship Wrestling, the independent scene was my playground. I’m leaving out most of the details, for everyone’s sake. Many bad gimmicks I had to portray coupled with even worse ones. With independent wrestling, what you see is what you get. Most of the wrestlers are simply average joes and janes who learned how to execute a suplex two weeks prior. Slap a horribly “creative” personality on them and set them free. Usually, the only worry that tugged my brain there, was wondering how early I was going to pull off my finishing move. It was often tough, sure, no denying. An experience all wrestlers should have to go through. Demoralization was a formidable opponent. But this ? Global Championship Wrestling. It’s entirely different. The spotlight shines wide. Thousands of fans. Eager contenders. Zodiac has me wondering “what if.” What if I don’t make it past this round? What if I end up face down on the canvass? Not even allowed the honor of staring up at the ring lights. Call me Rei. I carry the burdens of the world on my narrow shoulders. “I don’t know, honestly. I thought about checking out William and his band, tonight. They have a gig at some nightclub around here.” Jacob’s in San Diego. I’m in Canada. Calgary, Alberta. The area of tomorrow’s WorldWide. “They told me to tell you good luck, since they didn’t get to talk to you the day you left.” Luck. No such thing. “Yeah, I was busy with preparations and such. Hopefully I can catch their next gig.” Their fusion of jazz and metal is both intricate and imaginative. Often sending me on a journey throughout the depths of my perspicacity. Not hard to do, though, considering. Commercials do the same. “That reminds me ? How’s Canada?” Jacob’s never been. Once he moved to San Diego several years ago for college, he’s never left. “Slightly cold. I haven’t been here long enough to do anything besides get comfortable in my hotel room.” Which I admire. Stephen Caldera booked a first class flight and a top rate hotel room. I almost feel like I owe him money. There’s something ingenious about that. Jacob snickers. “I imagine.” He curses, again. “Well, I need to get off this cell phone before I kill everyone around me with my growing kamikaze desire.” I laugh, happy to hear hints of soberness in Jacob for the first time in quite a while. He was quickly becoming an alcoholic. Seeing him drunk was a constant in an equation. “I’ll talk to you when I get back down to San Diego. Give everyone my love, Jake.” “Will do. Take it easy and bring it all home to us, man. See ya.” The almost imperceptible severing of cell-phone contact. And I’m alone. All by myself. Thinking of you. The collective. I am Rei. The fast forward button on your VCR. The hotel room is certainly very nice. Soft colors brighten the room. Positive vibes goading temptation and eulogistic thoughts. A mahogany table sits offset from the bed, chairs like thrones situated around it. A bowl of fresh fruit welcomes me on the table, along with a small bottle of champagne, which I won’t drink. Incredibly clean and tidy. Even the pillows are new, still wrapped in slick plastic like a present waiting to be opened. Still, I can’t quite relax. The week has been another blur of activity. A week of children all talking at the same time and screaming difference. Rebecca. Global Championship Wrestling. Zodiac. Every week seems to have more potential than the next. I can only imagine what will happen if I go on to Global Championship Wrestling’s FallOut and capture the title. Edit that out. I’ll deal with that if it happens. Let’s not confuse worrying with clinical depression. I’m thrilled beyond words, but knowing my conflicting mind, negativity is on the defensive. I know nothing about Zodiac, therefore I can only plan to achieve. Which I do very well. When it comes to Rebecca ? that can wait. I find myself wondering what I would do if Rebecca’s face morphed from Zodiac’s. An entertaining mind excretion, to say the least. I hope I don’t screw it all up. Right now, WorldWide takes precedence. My thoughts and ideas drifting with a sail toward my GCW future. A plot outline for my career. A master plan. Unfortunately for Zodiac, she’s plunging head first into the scheme of the design. Drowning slowly in the depths of my madness. I’ll shake her hand and welcome her to my world. It’s not a place where everybody knows your name. Pleasantries dispensed and traded for its antonyms. Sometime in my younger years, I wanted to be a narrator. My voice the focal point of the story being told. Just recently, I wanted to hide from the prying eyes of GCW. Now, I want to be a champion. Funny how the tides of me have changed. And I’m still reeling. Still feeling the bottomless pit of the responsibility and the life that GCW is handing me in doses. Small for now, but getting larger. If I beat Zodiac, Jackson’s next. If I beat him ? GCW fame. First month and a champion. It’s happened before, but not so often that it isn’t considered an accomplishment. Call me Rei. Acknowledging a possible future. Refusing to be blinded by its light. I decide on a bath. Some of my most labyrinthine ideas are concocted in the wake of nudity and steam. Take that for what you will. I strip my navy blue t-shirt depicting a rabbit cartoon and toss it to the floor, watching it crumple. Tomorrow would be another test. The small champagne bottle can be a toast to hoping it goes well.
