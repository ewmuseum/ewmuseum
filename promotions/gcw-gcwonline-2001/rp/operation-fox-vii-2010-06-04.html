Operation: FOX VII

4 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/10 Previously on Operation: FOX... That bitch, Candace Fulmont showed up out of nowhere to spoil the joyful celebrations of Fox and friends. And this time, the bitch brought a loaded arsenal. Along with her fully equipped helicopter, Fulmont converged on Fox’s time traveling HQ with a fleet of foot soldiers with an order to kill anything in its path. Cyrus ended up stepping in by revealing a power no one even knew he had. After transforming from an unsuspicious transport truck into a mechanoid fighter, Cyrus gave his best effort to hold off the barrage. Even Clowl got into the act as he permanently morphed himself into Cyrus’ inorganic being, creating a much fiercer, and feathery combatant. While Transformers dabbled with Pokemon, our heroes had one last ditch effort up their sleeve. Clyde was thrust into the time vortex by an all-powerful crystal created out of the remnants of trips past just before Fulmont’s crew could get their hands on our hero. Now, with the rest of his team battered, bruised and bloodied, Clyde must trek through the uncharted waters of the time vortex alone in order to take on his destiny, face to face. Will Clyde make it safely through the time vortex? Will Lord Transcepticore make an all too convenient appearance? Will Clyde ever see his friends again? Has Candace killed them all? All the answers and more will be revealed as Operation: FOX begins its conclusion... "Whoa." BLURP! BLOOOP! The sounds of underwater air pockets bubbling around accompany the kind of feng shui found in a sixties toke pad. It’s almost like being trapped in a kaleidoscope. Minus the drugs, of course. "This pwace is AWESOME!" Fox’s shrill voice enlightens. I’ve never ever never seen the time vortex like this. It’s so different with its purdy colors and all. The walls almost look lick-able. Lord Transcepticore has really outdone himself by redecorating this pwace. I remember when this pwace looked worse than a public washroom. Hey, I wonder where the heck Lord Transcepticore is anyways... Clyde floats there, ogling all the psychedelic colors around him. Of course he’s been in the time vortex before, but never outside of his trusty truck companion. Fox can’t even believe he can even breathe. And on top of it all, everything seems so touchable. "Ummm, otay. So I’m here now but what do I do next? How do I get to the future?" Fox ponders, "Time travel was so much easier in Cyrus because he had gizmos. Maybe if I just click my heels together umpteen times and pray, I’ll go to the future!" Fox brings his legs together, tighter than a stiff chick. He closes his eyes and begins to tap the heels of his fresh white shoes together. "Future, wooture here I am. Future, wooture can, can, can. Future, wooture says the silly man!" Fox rhymes, or rather, attempts too. Nothing. Oh rats nests and testicular cancer! It didn’t seem to work. I’m still stuck in limbo. Maybe that time traveling crystal bwoke or doesn’t have enough power to get me to the future. Maybe I’m going to be stuck here FOREVER! In that case, I better call my tag team pawtner in crime and tell him I’m going to be late for our matchy match! Fox pulls out his trusty Gizmondo and begins typing in the longest combination of numbers. "I’ll call him collect. That way my Mommy won’t get mad that I’m paying for a call from another dimension," Fox plans out. BEEP! "We’re sorry, but your call cannot be completed," The automated operator rings, "Please try again or travel to the closest service zone. Goodbye." CLICK! Fox mauls his face with an open palm in frustration. How in the time vortex world can Clyde make contact with anyone? "I’ll twy one more time! This time I’ll use my super secret code," Fox says optimistically, "It’s winging! One ringy-dingy... two ringy-dingy... HETO!?" The magic of his Gizmondo pays off as it only makes sense that Clyde could make a multi dimensional phone call with such an odd device to begin with. Static fills the voice on the other end but Clyde still manages to tune his hearing. "Clyde, hey. What’s going on?" Jay Terror welcomes. "Ummmm not too much. Just hanging out. You know. You might not beweave this but, uhhh, I’m kind of stuck in a time vortex. Surprising, eh? What are you doing?" Fox continues with small talk. It’s not enough for Clyde to cut to the chase. He has to get the most out of his monthly minutes that his Mommy pays for. Otherwise he can’t maintain that schoolboy image. "Time vortex? Sounds... unreal," Terror remarks. Fox flicks his wrist and rolls his eyes like a typical teenage girl on the phone after school. "Oh beweave me, it’s something you’d only see in a Disney movie. But, uhhh... you... you don’t hear that? You don’t hear that annoyingly loud pulsing sound like I’m in a meat cellar?" Fox says in reference to the strange gurgling noises around him. "Yeah, no. I’m busy, Clyde. You should be too," Jay deflects. Clyde pretends to wrap the invisible phone cord around his free hand before replying. "I am totally busy, silly! I’m twying to uhhh, ugh... hey, listen. There’s a postcard shop here. Did you want me to send you one? ‘Wish you were here’ from The Time Vortex. Oh this ones cute. It has a bunny picture on it," Fox relays as yes, he stumbles upon a floating post card stand in the time vortex. CLICK! BEEEEEP! BEEEEEP! BEEEEEP! Fox pulls his Gizmondo down from his ear and shakes it. "Hmmm, must’ve lost the connection because my Gizmondo only beeps when... NO, WAIT! JAY TEE HUNG UP ON ME! But I need... your... help, I’m not going to make... it. DANG NABBIT!" Fox screams. Suddenly the bright and cheerful colors that surround The Prince of The Playroom begin to change. Light yellows turn into dingy oranges as sparkling blues transform into lame purples. What did Clyde do now? POOF! Some sparkling magical fairy dust sweeps by Clyde as a dark figure closes in. Chills crawl up and down our hero’s spine as his creepy old man pervert radar kicks in. A gassy dark cloud looms overtop of Clyde. The black mist slowly dissipates to reveal a crotchety wizard, sporting a pointy white beard and gown. "Have I died and gone to Pee-wee’s Playhouse?" Fox mentions as he pinches himself, "Because that man sure looks like he’s got a wicked wand!" The wizard glides in beside Clyde. He’s a rather smiley guy for someone who makes Bob Barker look young. His wrinkles have wrinkles and his ears droop more than Dumbo The Elephant’s. Yet after opening his mouth, the wizard’s youthful voice soothes Fox’s skepticism. "Well if it isn’t Clyde Fox! My old buddy! What brings you to town?" The wizard asks. I’m pretty sure I haven’t taken any mushrooms. I know haven’t smoked any oregano joints. And I’m almost positive that the glass of milk I drank earlier today was only expired by three months. Just what the heck is going on and how does this guy know me? I only know one being in the time vortex and that’s Lord... oh gee whiz! It’s Lord Transcepticore! The keeper of the time vortex! "Is... is that you, Lord Transcepticore?" Fox asks as he extends his hand. The wizard nods and takes Fox’s hand. "I was worried that for a second there that you wouldn’t recognize me," Lord Transcepticore admits, "I’ve really taken what you last told me to heart. I’m working on becoming something cool, something entertaining. For reasons that are still beyond me. Before I was just an ordinary time vortex keeper, but now, now I am a wizard! Check this out!" Lord Transcepticore turns his hand with Fox’s as a bouquet of fake flowers appears in their grasp. "Pretty neat, huh?" Lord Transcepticore brags. "Nah, I think being a wizard is lame," Fox says, "To be honest, you had me shaking in my booties with that entrance you made. Maybe you should go for the whole scary aspect." "You think? Hmmm I knew that entrance would overshadow my act. Damn shame too, I have balloon animals and the whole bit lined up," Lord Transcepticore says defeated. "Unless... maybe if you appeared as a dilly bar or something, more people would like you. I mean, I love dilly bars. I think they are fun!" Fox suggests. POOF! And just like that Lord Transcepticore transforms into an irresistible dilly bar. His chocolate coating glimmers in the light. Never before has Clyde laid eyes on such a crotch-hardening dilly bar. Fox so desperately wants to take a bite out of it but he knows that dilly bar is actually Lord Transcepticore. "My goodness you look rich in sugar," Fox notes as he drools. "Ah, you like that, don’tcha? You wana take a chunk out of my sweet ass!" Lord Dilly Bar Transcepticore taunts. "Well, this has been fun. Really. But I should probably get going and, you know, save the world and all," Fox refocuses, "Any chance of you pointing me in the right direction?" Lord Transcepticore flings his wooden handle over and points out a dark hole off in the distance. "That opening will lead you to where you need to go," Transcepticore guides, "But beware, going to this moment in your future might have more ramifications than you might expect. The outcome might be different, your involvement might change things and you might not even be able to come back." "Pfft. What do you know? You’re a freaking dilly bar for crying out loud," Fox laughs as he swims his way over to the transport point. "I’m being serious right now, Clyde. I may be a dilly bar, but I’m the dilly bar that runs this time vortex. Heed my warning or you might end up like a case of DQ sandwiches," Lord Transcepticore warns. "And that is... ?" Fox questions. "On the bottom of the freezer because everyone’s eatin’ dilly bars!" Lord Transcepticore fires back. A moment’s pause. Fox nods his head. "Touche," Clyde acknowledges, heading closer to the transport point. So there you have it, folks. Clyde makes it successfully through the totally unrealistic land of the time vortex with his trusty (and now cool) friend, Lord Transcepticore. Now there really is only one loose end left to tie down and our main man, Clyde Fox is on the job. But does he really know what’s waiting for him on the other side of the transport point? Does Clyde really have what it takes to face his destiny once and for all? And most importantly, can Clyde really be involved with something this epic? Only time will tell. To Be Continued...
