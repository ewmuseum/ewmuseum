A question of dreams!

1 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/1/08 ......Destiny was the word used most. Here i am standing on the edge of something. Was it greatness or was it something different. Where was my vaulted strength and courage. Am i scared of what has yet to unfold. Asking myself numerous times what was yet to come. I was afraid of change and indifference. My decisions and actions had heavier consequences and burdens. Where was my future bringing me. Shaman's Diary entry after winning the GCW TV Title...... Somewhere in silence and dreams...... In the quiet swirling darkness of this mist. The ground seems moist and yet very warm. It seems like grass after a morning dew on a warm summers day. Your feet are wet and kind of soggy. No shoes on bare feet will kind of do that when you are searching for something in an unknown place. As you turn your head you always see a shadow or figure in the swirling mist, but never able to discern who this person or what it is. As if your lost, you feel a little pang of fear. Will this world envelope you and trap you? Why are you here in this world? You ponder these many questions as you continue to search what you are seeking. Can this world really have the answer you are seeking. You continue to walk on in the mists as you come to finally an open air area. The mist circles around it as if in a tiny vortex that is unfeeling and unseen. You look up and the sky is full of stars like little beacons of light in a far off place. Yet the world is lit with a bright white light. Are you dead you begin to ask yourself. You look up and see a figure emerging from the shadows. You see yourself at a younger age. Around 24 to be exact, This kind of throws you for a loop. You wonder what you have to say to yourself. You have always wondered what you would say to yourself if ever presented with the opportunity. You do not get the chance to say anything before you are hit with a sudden feeling of unanswered questions. You begin to speak, but no words are coming as your younger self begins to speak. "So this is who i have become in ten years? It kind of frightens me. I do everything for my people and my pride. The fans love me and yet you they respect and at the same time are repulsed by who you have become. What about your people? Where is the pride of who you have become? What are your strengths and mighty beliefs in what is right and what is wrong??" You circle around yourself in disbelief at facing your own demons of the past and yet this one is not like a demon. It is like a living breathing extension of yourself. It is as if something deep and buried is asking many questions of you. You stop facing yourself and begin to answer. "My people failed me! The pride was a tool that was never working and i had to choose a different path to survive! My beliefs are still there, but they have changed!" Part of your answer has kind of made you sick. You ponder what this bit of regret is and why it is bothering you. What is it that you hold deep inside that is on the back of your mind. You look at yourself again as he stares at you eyes blazing with passion that you had turned from long ago. "Remember what the dying words of our mother was years ago? Do you even wish to remember how she was strong for ten years with that beast eating at her on the inside. The uncontrollable beast that ate at her very core being?" A coldness creeps over you as you hear mention of your mother. You narrow your gaze at the younger version of yourself as he stares back at you with equal intensity. You know deep inside that this really is yourself. You can see that fire that you held so long ago. Where did this fire go you wonder. You look at yourself as he begins to speak to you again. "Cancer was a cruel mistress of mother. Taking every bit of energy she had. One thing though she never took away the love she held for you and our brother. Our sister left only to disappear and never return. Yet you stand here powerless and pathetic in my views. What have you gotten in return in your journey to the top of the mountain as you stand there looking down at the rest of the world?" You look at yourself wondering why he is even questioning you. You got to where you are by your own hard work. You had to bleed to get where you are. You opened doors that nobody would open for you. You look back at your younger self and begin to reply to his unwavering questions and determined stares. "Mother died and with her some parts of myself. She had the strength that you and i will never possess. I am no longer you and yet i am being questioned about changes i had to make to survive, No i never forgot who i was. Powerless and pathetic is what i viewed myself as i used to be, As i was in you. My real brother is still out there somewhere. Have i gone to search for him? No it was simple he needed to lead his own path. I have found a new brother in Bryan Mayhem. He and i went through many of the same trials and tribulations in life. Our father was killed by a drunken white man at a bar and what was it that you remember in the past? I remember the late night crying. I remember the funeral and watching as everyone had pity upon us. I was not wanting the pity i was angry and wanted to release untold amounts of pain and suffering on the man and his family for what he did." Looking at yourself there is a bit of surprise in the younger versions face. he seems to remember what it was to feel like this. He stares at you wondering why you feel this way about this happening way back then. He begins to speak to you again this time calm and collective as if having a point to get across. "You are lost. That man killed himself over the guilt and grief of killing our father in that accident. He begged forgiveness he never received from our hatred. You never forgave that man until after he was dead. You found destiny and pride in what you did. You wanted to end the worlds evil and deeds upon your people and our family. You even went so far as spending time with alot of unfortunate children. Where is that sense of pride now?" You seethe with anger. An inner rage boils up deep inside of you as you stare at the younger version of yourself. You know that he has valid questions. He had been the epitome of all of these things. Yet the world changed around you. It changed with all of your good deeds as they went punished. Every time you stepped up to help another person stabbed you in the back. Even the organization and home you had come to respect, protect, and cherish. They all turned against you. The fans began to boo, your people began to turn in revulsion as to who you were. You were called a joke a dinosaur of the past with no place in the future. You look at yourself as you begin to speak. "It all failed me. They turned and destroyed everything i built in merely a simple case of reactions and revulsion. You stand at the precipice of what i was. I achieved many goals as i was you. Yet here i stand changed and not the same man. I clung onto those beliefs and changes and where did it get me. It made me the martar of a bygone time and era. The golden ages of a time when being the good guy, being respected was what i wanted to be. Now it is different, I don't care what people think i am working on something so much different. So much more of the past has comeback and i want to be the one to show that i am nothing as i was." Your younger self looks at you kind of disgusted. He speaks and steps forward looking you in the eyes. You stare back with alot of intensity. Neither is budging as would be expected. He speaks to you as he does not waver and stares deep into the soul of who he is yet to become. "What is it that caused you to change who you were? Why is it that you are not even looking back and feeling remorse of who you have become?" You do not move, you look back at him as you respond. "Betrayal, hatred, revulsion, and the list shall go on and on. Trust nobody and you will be spared the tortures of becoming one of the sheep we fought so hard for. You do not become one of the sheep that struggle for notice. You are the one that makes them notice. You do not speak of how great you are. You show them what you are by doing. You are my past and that's where you will stay. That past is dead and gone buried with everything you stood for." The younger version of yourself shakes his head as he steps back. He looks at you disappointed. "Where is the good that you stood for?" You look at yourself and answer with a simple response. "It died a longtime ago!" Your younger version of yourself begins to turn back into the mist that swirls around you. You watch closely as if intrigued by what has transpired here. The younger version asks one question as he completely disappears into a swirl of mist and colors. "Who are you?" This question echoes in your mind over and over again as you leave the open swirling vortex of mist and enter the thickened bank of mist and disappear into the white again as shadows and figures swirl around you over and over again. The real world.....Morning The sound of a blade whipping through the air is heard as you open your eyes and see a ceiling fan above you. The warmth and softness surrounds you. You blink your eyes and sit up as you push the quilt and comforter off of yourself. You rub your eyes and then stretch a bit as the early morning rays of sunlight come through the blinds. You stand and walk over to them and open them looking out at the world beyond. You stare at the buildings and turn your head as there is a knock at the door as you hear a voice. "Room service!" You turn and walk towards the door and open it. Standing before you is a young kid who seems nervous at being in your presence. You see that he is a stout kid and you have seen him somewhere before. You remember as you slide the tray into the room and hand him some money. You speak as you look at the kid. "Kid piece of advice. If you are going to follow your dreams as a wrestler learn to do things your own way. Too many people are going to exploit you and try to take advantage of your dreams. Be strong and never waiver. Things will change as you go, but stay true to what you know you need to do whether it is changing completely or simply staying true to your word. There is going to be betrayal guaranteed. Thanks for breakfast kid and that 50, put in your pocket it is for you, Good luck." Shaman closes the door as the kid is staring at him shocked. Shaman smirks as he looks at the steak and eggs breakfast he ordered. He sits down and pulls it close to the edge of the bed. He grabs the fork and knife and begins to cut the steak into pieces. he stops and places the knife down as he picks up the television remote. Turning it on he flips through the channels coming to a channel talking about GCW and the upcoming dangerous games. They flash up pictures of Bryan Mayhem and Shaman. He listens as they speak. "zzzzzz....You know Jimmy Oblivion will be in this thing together it seems...... zzzzzzzz......Yeah but can they co exist??...zzzzzzz.....Shaman and Mayhem have proven they can co exist anywhere anytime....zzzzzzzz.......The unknown factor here is Lia Ambrosi.......zzzzzzz.....Yes she could be the catalyst to put these two men over the top......zzzzzzz......There are three different personalities in Oblivion, which one will be the dominant one?.....zzzzzzzz......First Oblivion has a tag team match at World Wide 99 and World Wide 100.....zzzzzzz....They are a formidable force to eb reckoned with in the ring.......zzzzzzz......Shaman and Mayhem have slipped what two or three times in matches recently??.......zzzzzz.....With these two it only takes one moment to snap and they will click.....zzzzzzz.....Well i am going out on a limb here and state there can be many surprise possibilities with these two.....zzzzzzz.....I will just state we are going to wait and see Eric......zzzzzz....Next we will look at the system in which Oblivion has come to be.......zzzzzzzz....The history of Shaman and Mayhem and where it all started.....**Click**......." Shaman shuts off the TV as he goes back to his food and stabs the fork into a piece of steak and eats it as he speaks while chewing. "Been there and lived it." Shaman eats his food carefully one bite at a time. You know deep inside that there are burning questions and desires. Shaman as a whole seems like a being on a path to completion now. It is as if someone opened the flood gates and you are gaining energies and strength to tackle a bigger problem. It is like David versus Goliath and in the end you need to grasp at something more than just simple straws. You raises your eyes towards the window as you speak. "So Joaquin Pierre and Brad Jackson eh?" You take a bite of the food and then chew it for a bit. You swallow the food as you watch the sun rising outside. "Brad Jackson and i have faced off before. Pretty good opponent and truthfully a worthy one. He just forgets i don't play by anyone's rules. Pierre has come in at the wrong time and screwed with the wrong people. One fact will be simple. The Movement will be moving along in another direction." Shaman eats the last of his food and stands up as he walks to the window and leans against it. "Enough of the Movement and my opponents coming up this week. I am tired of seeing them and dealing with whoever gets in the path and then they are going to cry and whine on how they are going to beat and defeat us. I don't give a flopping shit about anything they say and wish to do. I am here to do what i do best. That is to do my job at the best of my ability. I will try to win and when and if i win with mayhem we move on and look towards the next opponent after this one is done. There is a tough road ahead for us to achieve and reach the goals that we have set before ourselves in Oblivion. Even Lia Ambrosi is working on plans and details that we share week in and week out." Shaman taps the window with his fingertips as he stares out at the wakening world before him. "What is getting to me is the dreams i have been having lately. Each one is passing a message to me and yet i cannot seem to grasp what that message is. Why is that answer hiding behind a veil of shadows? Am i hiding it from myself?" Shaman looks down at the cars driving by on the street below. His mind pondering the questions running through his head. Turning he walks towards the bathroom stepping in he turns and reaches into the shower and turns it on. He undresses and then steps into the shower allowing the water to cascade over his head and face. he enjoys the warmth. The water had always enveloped him with warmth and it seemed perfect for him to enjoy some of the world little gifts. He relaxes a bit as he opens his eyes. The water flowing down his face freely as he reaches for the soap. "Why was mother brought up in all of these dreams? I thought i closed that chapter of my life? Was there a message i forgot that she had given me? perhaps i should call my brother and ask him what mom had left to us in her last words. It is like i am being forced to remember my past. That is what i find quite disturbing. I thought i had come tog rips and settled on what had happened and accepted it. Mom died of cancer and Dad was killed in a bar fight, but a drunken man who showed remorse in the following years. It was true i forgave him later. Why am i hating the man again." Shaman puts a little bit of shampoo in his hand as he scrubs his head washing his hair. He runs a hand through his long hair washing each length carefully. He then rinse and then makes a fist around his hair as he pulls alot of the water and soap out of it. Then he rinses his hair again repeating the same process several times. he stops and looks up as if questioning himself again about something. "Is it i could be hating the man again for stealing something from me? Like my mother's cancer stole something from us? Why do i constantly see myself speaking to me in these dreams. Damn this annoys me. I have little patience especially when my own mind is doing it." Shaman finishes washing himself in the shower and steps out into the steaming bathroom. He grabs the towel and dries off. He turns hanging the towel he just used and reaches for another larger one as he wraps it around his waist. He walks out of the bathroom and stops looking over at a bible laying upon the table. It seems to himself that it symbolizes something in his mind. Reaching down he lifts it and looks at it. He remembers a priest trying to speak to his mother about saving her. She would have nothing to do with what she believed was a religion that destroyed her people. Turning he placed the bible down as he walked and sat in a chair as he looked over at the bible laying on the dresser. "People can believe in whatever they want. Just don't ask me to become part of what i am not interested in. My mother was the strongest person i ever knew. She was dying and it took ten years to finally sap that strength out of her. The doctors gave her three months and she lasted Ten years. She wanted to see myself and my brother grow into young men. Her two strong men as she saw fit to state. My sister she was strong as she had always thought she had to be. When mother died she disappeared. Maybe i need to go seek her out and find a bit of reasoning behind the dreams i have been having. See if she and my brother have been having these same dreams." Lifting his wallet he reaches down and pulls out a black and white picture of a man. He looks at it and smiles as a single tear rolls down his cheek from his right eye. he speaks to the picture as he looks at it. "Hey Dad been missing you quite a bit lately. Wondering how things could have been if you were with us still. You left me with a big gift. I am successful by having the drive that you had while you were alive. You worked hard to keep us fed and free of sickness. You put food on the table even long after you were gone. Mom did well using the inheritance you had left behind. Who would have thought insurance money and what little bit of savings would have kept us fed and strong long after you were gone, Thanks Dad!!" Gently putting the old picture back in his wallet it seems he has one piece of his past he does treasure. Turning he begins to get dressed as his eyes gaze at the world beyond. His attitude changes slowly. All aspects of his own human nature and gentleness of person slowly disappears as he seems to run back into the man we all have known as Shaman in GCW. The member of Oblivion with a no nonsense take on his actions. What is it that he refuses to show the rest of us and yet he is so willing to show to himself. There are many questions rolling through his mind. What is it that drives this man so hard that he is willing to put himself in harms way day in and day out to prove a point that no matter what he is the best at what he does and nobody will ever challenge that. Turning Shaman pulls a shirt down over his head as he speaks. "Back to business now. World Wide is coming and Oblivion has plans, Very big plans!" Shaman turns and exits the room as the door shuts behind him and the camera turns and focuses on the bright reddish orange sun shining upon the new day.
