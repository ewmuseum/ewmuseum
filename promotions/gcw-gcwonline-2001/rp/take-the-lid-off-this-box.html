Date: Today Fingers scrape through the dirt. Dirty, blistered fingers. Poor cuticles, shorten nubs of fingernails. A beauticians worse nightmare. The fingers are cupped, like a shovel, a hor, some sort of garden tool used for digging. Breathing is loud and stuttered. The fingers dig rapidly. The epidermis of earth is easy to get through. The inner core requires some prying loose. He’s about a foot deep, maybe two feet across. Bryan Mayhem, sans shovel, is digging into the earth. He’s about a quarter mile from where his childhood home once stood. This is his first time visiting the land of Belmont, the home of Elizabeth Bathory, and much later the O’Neil family, For the first time, atleast to his recollection, the sun is out. The land around the castle’s land is now green and fertile. The apple orchard he once played in as a child now has apples. A cloud looms over the former foundation and land of the Bathory/O’Neil house. The Devil still looms over this 3 acre land mass. Bryan is on his hands and knees digging with his hands into the earth. He’s at the Belmont Cemetery which is also on the O’Neil land. His father took over control of the cemetery and funeral home when it’s former owner was found slained in the mortuary. His bloodline rests here. His mother, his poor diseased beaten whore of a mother rests here. Bryan still does not know why he loved this slut so much. No matter the neglect, no matter the drugs and alcohol binges, he loved her more than life itself. Watching her die at the hands of his own father was life threatening. He’s still haunted by this vision. Her eyes popped from her head. The vein at the center of her head stood almost an inch out. He tongue dangled, it’s bluish black death of a color. Watching a person go from a slight flesh hue, to red, to purple, to pale is the most disturbing transformation on earth. He thinks his father, that sadistic sonofabitch, went to hell with her fingernail scrape scars on his hands and arms. Bryan will go to hell with this image in his head, this fucking image that will never escape his brain. Traumatized. Bryan does not realize that by digging with his hands that his fingertips have worn off a layer of skin. The skin under his nail begins to bleed. Some of his fingernails split down the middle. With all the pressure the underskin swells and seeps through the cracks. Bryan still digs on. He remembers the day his mother was put into this hole. He wore his best suit, his only suit. Daddy said, in a drunken stupor, “Don’t you two embarrass me by looking like a buncha trailer niggers. You are O’Neils.” Jason’s eyes welled up with tears as his mother was lowered into the abyss of hell. This image of his mother’s burial would forever haunt the Dark Messiah. Not as much as the strangulation did Bryan. But even in his biography Jason O’Neil says he wakes up every morning to that off tan casket being lowered. It was cold and rainy. The wind was howling. He said in his book. “Fuck that whore, she never loved me anyways.” Bryan felt the same way. “Fuck that whore, she never loved me anyways.” She did, and she didn’t. She loved them when it was convenient for her. If her Heroin or booze was low, she would play stupid games with them. Her favorite was “Now boys, whoever can fetch their mommy Daddy’s Scotch can stay up an extra hour tonight and fill in the crossword puzzles with Mommy.” Both kids would rush to the basement. Bryan would always win because he’d land an illegal elbow to his baby brother’s face, slowing down the now legend. He’d return with a bottle of Lauder’s or J&B. There was never staying up later, and the crossword puzzles never existed. She played this game daily, and for some reason the two boys would fall for it everytime. Five feet deep, and probably 4 feet wide now. The pain at his fingertips becomes unbearable. But he must dig. He knows not why he chose his hands as shovels. The shed, which was not destroyed in the most watched match of wrestling history against Lestatt Knight (over 350 million people have viewed this match. The DVD was the fastest selling DVD of all time) stood only 500 feet behind him. There was shovels, pickaxes, and other tools strewn about. The process would have been easier, and far less painstaking. When the dirt was pushed into the hole Bryan through a Halloween mask she once gave him. It was the only time he ever saw her smile. Now she’d smile when she was nodding out from a fix, but never on her own accord. During his digging he never saw the mask. He must have thrown it more towards the bottom of the casket. He finally reached the rock. He stands up, and looks up at the shed. He climbs out of the hole and heads towards the beaten down woodblock of a shed. When he opens the door a wide array of dead things are spread over the flood. A 3 foot deep hole in the middle from when his father made him bury his deformed youngest brother. Bryan buried him at sea in the back of a semi moons later. He grabs a sledgehammer, and heads back toward his excavating. Bryan climbs back in the hole and begins to whale the sledgehammer at the casket. A loud ping, followed by a crack. He swings again, and another crack, followed by another crack. He throws the sledgehammer back onto the mound of dirt and starts to pull at the crushed pieces. What seems like ages is actually only a few seconds. Mayhem pulls back the tethered remains’She’s gone.
