Over and Done With

8 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/08/10 It was early November, the air was brisk, and the wind had a bite. The ground was snow free, but still hard as a rock. Late Fall/Early Winter in England was always bitingly cold, but the roads were clear as day. Traveling was not difficult, but for some reason in the small town of Belmont, most chose to walk. Jason and Bryan both walked to and from school daily. Well, to be honest Jason mostly walked by himself as his older brother was usually still at home or out causing destruction. Depsite the physical and verbal abuse Jason was a recipeint of at school, he enjoyed going. He enjoyed his teachers even though they would constantly tell him how unintelligent he is. He loved doing homework even though he never knew where to begin. He most likely enjoyed school because his brother was with him. Even though the ridicule and abuse hurt him, Bryan would always hurt them back, even worse. The O'Neil boys were the talk of the town, and in a town of only 115 or so residents the townies are always talking. The giant castle on top of the hill is an absolute eyesore, and when people are out and about mowing lawns, planting flowers or just front porch sitting, an eye always finds its way to the O'Neil residence. Everytime a mother walks by the house with their child, they will grab their hand a little tighter, and pull them a little closer. Jason is actually well liked amongst the elderly in town. He will be the first to help an old lady cross the street, or to climb a tree to get a cat out of it. It's his violent brother and father that make the O'Neil name the town's boogeyman. Most men in the town have also paid for Jason's mother's services. In a family of demented, sick, violent, Jason is the light at the end of the tunnel. It was a horrible day at school. Jason failed Math and Language this morning, he was beat up for his lunch, and they did it so badly he pissed himself, so he was stuck in class smelling of urine the rest of the day. He walked home at a faster pace, because the wind was blowing right at him, freezing snot to the edge of his lip. His eyes were watering, and his cracked lips hurt immensely. He put his head down and trudged forward. "Hey Bitch!" Jason keeps his head down and keeps walking. Once he crosses the mini bridge in front of his house he's home free. The bridge is aptly named "Pervert's Pass" and no one in town is brave enough to enter the O'Neil's property. "Bitchboy, don't ignore me!" Jason kept telling himself to keep moving, don't turn around and it will all be over soon, or so he thought. Jason hears footsteps and than is met with a fist to the back of his head sending him down to the ground. Jason's wrist is caught under his own body and he hears a snap. Jason screams and winces in pain. "You answer when I fucking call you little boy!" Jason still can't see who it is, but he feels his hand on the back of his head as the attacker begins to push Jason's face into the hard dirt. Blood begins to run its course from Jason's nose. It begins to mix with the dirt and smears his face. The attacker kicks Jason hard in the ribs, and the wind is knocked out of him. Jason rolls to his back and see's who his attacker is. Andrew Covik, the town bully, begins to lay a mountain of kicks into Jason's side. He's gritting his yellow rotted teeth with each impact into Jason's body. Jason begins to cry and that causes Andrew to kick him even harder. "Crying? I am barely touching you, and you are fucking crying!" Now with the gritting of his yellow teeth he begins to wind up and grunt with every kick. Jason in an act of desperation rolls himself and lands a kick to the back of Andrew's knee that causes him to buckle to the earth, giving Jason time to get back to his feet and hobble away. "You sonofabitch now you are dead!" Andrew lurches at Jason. Jason turns and runs as fast as his injured leg will carry him. He stops at the bridge and turns around expecting a punch to the face, but nothing. Instead he see's Andrew on his knees begging for mercy in front of...Bryan! Jason's brother came to the rescue again! "I was just messing around Bryan, I didn't mean to, just let me go I will never bother him again!" Andrew is begging, and Bryan is towering over this bully. Bryan motions for Andrew to stand up. Andrew does as he asks. He stands in front of Bryan, hands clasped in front of him almost in prayer. "Andrew, 4 years you have tortured my little brother. 4 loooong years. I have waited 4 years to get my hands on you, and to give you what you deserve." "Wait man, look, I promise I will leave him alone! Look let me go please!" Bryan's hand as quick as lightning grabs the throat of Andrew and begins to squeeze. Bryan's face is clenched tight as Andrew's hands grasp Bryan's wrist trying to pry his paws from his throat. Andrew's tongue begins to loll out, snot leaks from his nose, and his eyes begin to bulge. Bryan starts to squeeze tighter, as Jason looks on with his blood and dirt smeared upper lip. Bryan lets go of Andrew and shoves him back to the ground. Andrew inhales deeply and starts to cough. He grabs his own throat and is gagging and coughing violently. His face is greeted with a boot . One of his teeth fly right through his upper lip splitting it in two, the left piece hanging like a thread from a poorly sewn shirt. Bryan stands over him, chest heaving in anger. Andrew puts a shaking hand up in protest, Bryan grabs the hand with both of his hands and twists violently. Andrew screams in pain as his wrist is completely separated from the rest of his arm. His detached wrist hangs there limp and unmoving, all bones, joints, ligaments, and nerves completely severed from their base. Andrew's scream is curdling and raspy. He has never been in so much pain in all his life. Jason is watching his brother once again come to his rescue. He cracks a smile because he know's Andrew will never ever bother him again, little did he know that Andrew will never mess with anyone ever again. Bryan grabs Andrew by his severed hand and drags him like a child does a doll. The pain is so intense Andrew passes out escaping the light and entering the black..... Andrew comes to, but realizes its still black. He's lying on a hard floor, his head aching and his arm in a numbing pain. He goes to sit up but busts his head on something. He begins to panic and realizes he's surrounding not only in black but something more physical as well. He begins to scream, but no one can hear him, they never will. Jason looks down on the pile of dirt that is now the resting place for Andrew Covik, and smiles. He turns around and starts to skip back home, whistling an unknown song.
