The Surgoth Story - Chapter 7 - Look It's A Bird, It's A Plain... Wait No... Who The Hell Is That?

27 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/27/11 Things have been getting a little crazy in my life recently. First I was attacked by a half vampire, half basilisk made by a sick scientist who is now on the loose after being let out of jail on a so called technicality. Now I’m dealing with clones of myself, or maybe I’m a clone of someone else. One thing I knew for sure is I am Van Jahan Surgoth, no one else is. Just recently I made a trip to the United States and surprisingly I was welcomed with open arms. GCW gave me one hell of an opportunity and I gave it my all but it just wasn’t enough. But that’s okay, because it isn’t over. I have a new opponent and I plan to give him more than I did at the Pay-Per-View. I was back in Diamond City today, back to work at my normal job, if you want to call it normal. I sat at my desk looking through some papers, trying to get Celis Grom put back in jail where he belonged. The clones and Grom seemed to be connected somehow but was he the creator or was he just there to look over the progress. The files really didn’t tell me much. It was time to go visit my friend over in Gildus Labs, Doctor Argath. * * * I pulled into the parking lot of Gildus labs and parked my black 2011 Mustang. Wasn’t my choice in cars but the company had given it to me recently. I got out of my car and headed for the labs building but I didn’t get a chance to get in. Doctor Argath came out of the labs, stood outside and lit a cigarette. I started walking toward the good Doctor and he noticed me coming. "Hey Van, great to see you," said the Doctor as I reached him and he put his hand out to shake mine. I returned the gesture. "What brings you here today?" "I think you might be able to answer a few questions for me. It’s about your former partner Celis Grom," I said to Doctor Argath. "Shoot." "When he was here, did you or him look into cloning?" "The subject had come up a few times yes, but I refused to take part in the project. The creation of life isn’t meant to be in the hands of the human race. That belongs to whatever higher power exist out there. All he said to me after that is that he was going to one day surpass that higher power and create a perfect being." "This might just be what I’m looking for to put this son-of-a-bitch back behind bars," I said as I shook Doctor Argath’s hand. "Thank you very much Doctor. I may need you to come down to the station and give a statement about this. I’ll be in touch if you’re needed." "Any way that I can be of help Van, I owe you much," answered the Doctor as he threw his cigarette on the ground, stomped it out and walked back into the building as I headed for my car. I knew Grom was into something even deeper than the virus and his failed experiment known as the Vamlisk. As I placed my hand on the door handle to my car, I heard someone behind me. I turned around only to be knocked out cold before I could see who or what it was. * * * I finally woke up only to find that I couldn’t see. It felt like I had a blind fold on so I went to remove it only to find that my hands were tied behind my back. I was sitting in an upright position on a chair, at least as far as I could tell it was. "Hello!?" I yelled out. "Is anyone here!?" I could hear foot steps right after walking toward me. Whoever it was walked up behind me, stopped, grabbed the hair on the back of my head and yanked it back roughly. "So you are finally awake," said a snake like voice I had heard once before. "I thought you were dead?" I said to the creature that I knew as the Vamlisk. "I seen you get shot down." "Ah so you recognize me without seeing me. Aren't you perceptive," said the Vamlisk as it let go of my hair and removed my blind fold. He walked to the front of me so that I could see his face. He wore a black trench coat and a rimmed hat. He now carried a wooden cane with him, walking with a limp as he walked over to a desk and moved a chair over to sit in front of me. "What do you want with me?" I asked him. "I just want to talk Van. I thought the only way you would listen to me at all is if I captured you and brought you here. Doesn’t this place look familiar?" "I’m not answering anything you ask until you answer me. How did you survive?" "I guess you should have come look at my body after you shot me. Where do you think you got me?" "I shot you in the head." "Do you know that as a certainty?" "No," I answered honestly. "But I’m going to assume from that limp I got you in the leg." "Lucky for me I guess. Now I answered your question Van, how about you answer mine. Does this place look familiar to you?" asked the Vamlisk. "Yeah, it’s the former office for the CSI unit a few months ago when we moved into the precinct with the rest of the force. What about it?" "I’m hurt Van that you still don’t remember me." "Why would I remember you?" I asked. The Vamlisk stood up, walked up to me, placed his hands on the arm of the chair I was in and put his face next to mine so that I could look into his eyes. I couldn’t believe my eyes at what I saw in his. "Ronin?" I asked unsure. "BINGO!" said the Vamlisk I now knew to be my former partner that had gone missing a few months ago. "No one has been able to find you. What happened?" "You already know what happened Van. Grom kidnapped me and turned me into his sick experiment." "But why you?" I asked. "Because I know everything there is to know about you," answered Ronin. "He wanted me to kill you, get you out of the way." "Why?" "Celis Grom knows allot of things no one should know. He knew that you would put him behind bars and he also knew that he would be left out long before it happened. But he also saw his own demise and it was by your hand." "So why kidnap me? Why not just kill him yourself or let me alone to do my job if that is what is meant to happen?" "Because, before you kill him he succeeds in what he’s trying to accomplish. He works for a higher power Van, whoever it is pulling the strings of Diamond City." "And when am I suppose to have killed Celis Grom?" "A month from now, but you have to kill him sooner if you want to stop what is to come. You only have two weeks to do so," said Ronin. "What does he do that I need to stop?" I asked Ronin. "He unleashes some sort of weapon that would make everyone sleep again. This time though, we all wake up a week later and become an army for the higher power of Diamond City. They are out to take over the entire planet and beyond if possible." "What do you mean beyond? As in other planets?" "It goes much deeper than that. The higher power wants to take over Heaven and Hell." "That sounds a little crazy," I replied. "Yes, it does sound crazy but according to Grom’s vision, it happens," said Ronin. "So what am I still doing here? Untie me so I can stop him." Ronin the Vamlisk walked behind me and began to untie my hands as he said, "Van, we use to be partners once upon a time. I want to be your partner again, but it can’t be like it was before. I’m a monster now and the precinct wouldn’t give me a chance to explain anything, just like you wouldn’t have which is why I brought you here. Please Van, don’t let anyone know where I am. Let me help you with Celis Grom. I think that if we stop him there may be an antidote for what he has turned me in to." Once I was untied I stood up and extended my hand to my former friend and ally. He shook my hand as I said, "Don’t worry Ronin. You will become you again. We are going to stop this ‘Mad Scientist’ once and for all. We are going to protect this city like we did before." "Thanks Van," began Ronin. "You have always been a great friend to me." "So where do we have to go to find Celis Grom?" "I’m not sure, but I will begin the search. I can cover more of the city than you can a hell of allot quicker. Once I find him I will be in contact with you. Wait for a sign. When you get that sign, meet me here." "How will I know what the sign is?" I asked Ronin. "Trust me, you’ll know," he replied. * * * I made my way back to my car that sat in front of Gildus Labs. It was an hour walk from where the Vamlisk I now knew to be my former friend and partner, Ronin. I got into my car and began to drive for the office. It was about three in the afternoon now and I still had some paper work to go through. Lily would have already delivered lunch so I would see her until after work now. Things in Diamond City were getting crazy. Doctor Celis Grom turned out to be someone who could see into the future, his future at least. Grom wasn’t very smart, however, telling his creation his plans and what he had seen. I was now one step ahead of the sicko. All I had to do was wait for Ronin to give me the sign that he found Celis Grom. So many questions, so few answers. Our city was a bit crazy. Vamlisks, mysterious viruses, ‘Mad Scientists’ that can see into the future. That wasn’t all that had happened since the ‘Day of Awakening’. So many things had gone on before. This was just the craziest. It was hard to tell what would happen next. I found out from Ronin that a higher power runs our city. A higher power that once to take over not just our world but the spiritual as well. Hopefully the answers I’m looking for will be revealed once I understand more about the ‘Day of Awakening’. Those answers will hopefully be in the United States where my investigation has led me.
