The Dream Of The Insomniac

5 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/05/2006 04.45am Tuesday 2nd May 2006. Some motel room. Pick one. They all look the same...still. Somewhere. Anywhere. Nowhere. McKail sat on the uncomfortable standard issue motel room chair, his feet raised, resting upon the window ledge, glaring out into the dark and gloomy motel car park in complete silence. The TV was on in the background, but he wasn’t much watching it. Some old episode of Friends. He would’ve probably found the show amusing if he were in the mood to laugh, but these days it never seems to be in the mood to laugh. McKail hadn’t even tried to get any sleep that night. He knew it wasn’t going to happen as soon as he rented the room from the greasy looking Hick that worked the reception the afternoon before. He didn’t feel the least bit tired and without his wife to share a bed with him, sleep wasn’t much of an option. From his position a rich beam of moonlight washed over him, illuminating his face from the surrounding darkness and he smiled to himself realising the irony in conceivable potent metaphor a casual observer could extrapolate from this scene. It would suggest that he is the only beacon of light ambushed by the darkness, when it reality he considered it to be the direct opposite to that romantic notion based on a common natural occurrence. McKail gently massaged the bridge of his nose and leant back in his chair, allowing his hand to lower past his nose in order to massage the his stubbled chin. He realised his mind was running away with itself again, finding meaning where there really is no meaning to be found. It happened often on nights where there wasn’t much chance of sleep and highly successful TV shows played in the background. He shook his head, hopeful that such extrapolations derived from innocent and natural happenings had eroded into the night air. Making sure that his brain was now in check, he focused upon some of his other, more important, problems. It seemed like the sensible option to him; it always seemed to wipe his mind of unnecessary thoughts and memories. He was due to meet up with that damned councillor again to go over his anger “issues” and the like. Though it seemed as though this councillor - Miss Flannigan, she’d told him to call her - only seemed to want to piss me off and she was very good at it. She wanted to rehash the past, go over details that McKail really didn’t want to discuss any longer. He figured precisely why she was pushing so hard for him to relive it. The long and painful road well being and all that crap. McKail had no time for it, never would do, but it is court-appointed and there’s not a chance for him to get out of it unless he actually wanted to face some real jail time. He just had to bear it, close his eyes and grit teeth until it was all over. He needed to last the distance desperately attempting to avoid the subject of his deceased wife at all cost. He removed his feet from the window ledge stood from the seat, stretching his arms high above his head wearily - a lot more wearily than he thought he would be. He then slumped down onto his equally uncomfortable bed and lay on his back looking at the dreary motel room ceiling, the rich moonlight still soothingly washing over him. ---- In only my second match within Global Championship Wrestling I was given a shot at the GCW Television title, but I sure as hell don’t know why. The nearest I can figure is, they allowed it nto happen because they thought I wasn’t ready for it. Weird logic, I know, but who in the hell gives a rookie a Television title shot and expects him to win? I guess the GCW officials thought it would be okay to give me the title shot because they figured that I wasn’t in the same league as Jayce Bradley or Virgil Ambrosi so I definitely wouldn’t win. I guess they severely underestimated me, because guess what, I won and guess what, I’m going to keep winning until I realise my destiny and not only dominate the GCW, but dominate the entire wrestling industry. If I slept long enough to dream these days, this eventuality would be their content and would lay out before me a path that I would have to walk. However, these days I don’t dream much and if I do it’s more fragmented images of my wife frozen in time, fuelling me with the determination I must utilise in order to succeed. Maybe the GCW will continue to underestimate me right up until I win the GCW World Heavyweight title? I guess that would be a good dream to have.
