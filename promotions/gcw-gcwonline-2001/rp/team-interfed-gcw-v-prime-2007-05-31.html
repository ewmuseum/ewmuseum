Date: Written: May 31st, 2007 Breathe. With a single word.. in a single instant.. your hands go numb and your heart skips a beat. The hair on your arms stand on ends and your nose falls victim to a slight twitch. You tell yourself you're not afraid, but for some reason you've still got this nervous feeling racing through your veins. Breathe. The lights go dim. Breathe. And when they're bright again, you look at destiny. Breathe. You reach for hope. Breathe. But not a hope of faith. Breathe. A hope of harmony. You hope that he has some compassion for you. That he'll find it somewhere in his blackened heart to stop himself from stopping you. Breathe. In a blink of an eye, you bare witness... Breathe. ...to Bagwell. ---- San Diego, California Post-WorldWide, Episode 90 11:21 PM "You ready for a beer?" It had become common-place for Chris to close every night at work with a few adult beverages at whatever the local establishment may have been. Call it a new pasttime. The taste of a cool Coors Light rushing down his throat always seemed to be the best medicine to help harbor the aches and pains of being a professional wrestler. Chris' short-term experimentation with drugs had left him fearful of the effects of taking any sort of painkiller, from Oxycotins to Advils. He'd much rather swallow the stinging and wash them away with the weight-gaining alcoholic alternative. Lifting his bag from a shiny steel chair inside the confines of his locker room, Chris Bagwell bounces his eyes from the bag to his buddy. The right side of his lip curls up with an enticing grin. "Am I reay for a beer?" he questions rhetorically to his long time friend, Timmy Phils. "The hell kind of question is that?" "Hey now, I figured you'd be all sorts of shot out after tonight," Timmy responds. "With the big win and all." It's been almost three months since the last time Chris had seen Phils. Timmy had a short stint traveling with Chris upon his arrival in the GCW, but the added miles on his body were starting to wear him down. The look on Chris' face at the sight of Timmy could best be described as a litte boy in a candy store. "Now here's a face I haven't seen in months," Chris shouted ecstatically. "What'd you finally decide you'd come out because I was in Cali?" Laughing, Timmy replies, "You know me all too much. How the hell d'you think I'd pass up a night out in San Diego." Chris struts over to Phils and extends his right hand, where Timmy greets it with a handshake and a hug. "And go figure," Timmy continues, "Your ass actually won a match, too!" Chris' face lights up with a smile, sensing Timmy's sarcasm. "Yeah, I'll knock your teeth in too, talking like that!" With GCW WorldWide 90 coming to a close, Chris leads Timmy out of his dressing room and down a length corridor to the parking lot. Throughout the journey, they pass by a number of Chris' colleagues. James von Doom... who gives a subtle wave. LeStatt Knight... who cracks his knuckles, as if ready he's ready to brawl. Thom LeStrange... who gives a gentle nodding of his head, probably in reference to their chat earlier in the night. Tessa Windsor... who slips a smile, despite not always seeing eye to eye with Chris. Brad Jackson... who rotates his shoulders counter-clockwise, flexing his pecks. "Zacharia," Timmy interjects, grabbing hold of Chris' wrist. Bagwell stops and looks back at Phils, shaking his head. "Nahh, man." Confused, Timmy asks, "What do you mean?" "He doesn't roll with us anymore, my man." "No? What happened?" Shrugging, Chris answers, "Some washed up Native American got into his head and convinced him that he was living under my shadow. You know how easy it is to get inside Zacharia's head and get him thinking wacky shit." Timmy just laughs. "Just forget about him," Chris asserts. As the two pass by the dressing room reading ZACHARIA TAYLOR, the door creeps open. Taylor pokes his head out, watching Chris and Timmy the rest of the way, as they walk through the door to the parking lot. "So where we headed, Chris?" ---- San Diego, California Local Pub 11:43 PM "WHOOOOO!! THAT SUCKER STUNG!" Chris and Phils slam their shot glasses to the counter, eyes squinting, and mouths watering. "Wow," Timmy exclaims. "I haven't touched tequila in a long damn time." Timmy's face screams 'I feel like I'm going to puke as he desperately chugs his beer, trying to subside the tequila from making its way back up his throat. The bitter taste stings and lingers for a few more moments. "What'd you grow a vagina in the last three months?" "Nahh, man! I just don't date the tequila anymore." "That's no excuse. You can always get a booty call from an ex-girlfriend!" "Ha! I guess that was a bad example then." Chris smiles, shaking his head at Timmy. "I'll get you a Southern Comfort and Lime this time, then." Chris leans forward on his chair, waiving his hand at the bartender. Once he grabs her attention, he's able to flag her down. "Just a second," she says. Chris leans back in his chair, taking a look around the bar. In his survey, he notices a slightly overweight man dressed in a well-groomed black suit sitting alone in a booth. The man looks middle-aged, possibly in his mid-to-late 40's, or even early 50s. A black fedora fits around his egg-shaped head and his eyes gleem through his black-framed glasses at a small glass on the table. The man takes a rather large sip, nearly devouring all thats left of the drink. As he places the glass back on the table, he peers up at Chris and locks a gaze. "So what can I get for you boys? Another shot of tequila?" The bartender interrupts a rather awkward moment for Bagwell, startling him some. "Ohhhh no!" Timmy exclaims at the mention of tequila. Chris gradually falls out of his trance. "That tequila nearly kicked my ass before," Timmy continues. Chuckling, the bartender asks, "So then what'll it be? Maybe a Cosmo for you?" Timmy's eyes nearly bug out of his head. "You hear that Chris? The girls got jokes!" Chris finally snaps out of it. Smiling, he interjects, "Well you shouldn't be such a little pussy!" The three of them laugh together, with Timmy obviously feeling a bit embarressed. "How about I bring you something special," the bartender suggests. Timmy looks a bit weary eyed. "I promise it'll taste good and still give you a swift kick in the ass," she reassures. Timmy opens his mouth about to speak, but Chris interrupts. "We'll take it." With a smile, the bartender says, "Good! Thats what I like to hear." Bagwell's eyes are locked upon her small, tight, firm behind as she scoots away to make them their shots. "These shots are going to end up being the death of me," Timmy says worryingly. "Oh stop it. We have much to celebrate tonight!" "Damn right we do. I can see the Pro Wrestling Illustrated headlines already. BAGWELL PINS VALENTINE!!" "It just goes to show exactly WHO should be in the title running for that belt." "Yeah! Screw Jay Terror or Jordan White." "Ehh, Terror isn't a bad guy. I'm starting to respect the kid a little bit. I just don't think he's ready for the big-leagues yet." "Yeah, I hear that," Timmy sympathizes. "What I'm really glad that I accomplished," Chris says, "is that I made a statement." "A statement to whom?" "To people like Lindsey Troy." "Who in the hell is that?" "Its some little cum dumpster that wrestles in another organization. There was some inter-promotional thing where Troy went up against Valentine." "So whats that got to do with you?" "She came on GCW Radio and, well, you know how well I take to people who don't immediately acknowledge my superiority." Timmy just laughs, shaking his head. "You're too much, man. Too much!" Chris gives another quick scan around the bar, catching another glimpse of the slightly overweight, black suited man. The man's buggy eyes are locked on Bagwell, so much so that he hardly even blinks. "Here you are boys!" The bartender brings over a couple shot glasses and a shaker filled with some wild, fruity, alcoholic concoction. "The Susy Special!" Susy pours out the shaker across five shot glasses. "And here I felt priviladged that you were making us a special drink. And you go and make it for three others!?" Timmy mutters. Susy winks, "Nope! Two for each of you. One for me." Chris, although feeling uncomfortable with that man staring at him, turns his attention back towards the bar. Timmy nudges Chris, "See what I mean, man? These things will be the death of me!" Chris smiles, "Just take the shot. To Susy!" Chris lifts one of the shot glasses from the bar and raises it into the air. Timmy and Susy both join in on the cheers. They each pound back the shot and slam the glass to the bartop. "How'd you like it," Susy asks. "Awesome!" Timmy shouts. "Yeah, it was pretty good. What was in it?" asks Chris. "That's my little secret," Susy winks and starts to walk away, "and those were on me boys!" This time, Timmy's eyes are locked on Susy's behind and she scoots away. "She wants us, Chris." "Don't get yourself too worked up yet, my man. You've still got another shot sitting in front of you." Timmy and Chris both grab their last shot and toast. Then pound it back and Chris lets out a gentle sigh. "I've gotta take a leak," Timmy mumbles. He stands from his barstool and stumbles his way to the bathroom. Across the bar, Susy catches a glimpse of Timmy's trouble walking and starts giggling to herself. She looks over at Chris, and he merely smiles and shrugs his shoulders. There's a sudden tap on Chris' shoulder. "Mr. Bagwell?" The overweight, black-suited man walks up next to Chris and takes a seat next to him. He takes Timmy's seat, to be precise. Chris looks at the man with an awkward stare. "Yeah?" The man leans in and, in a whisper, says, "We've got a few things to discuss." In the same movement he slides a folded piece of paper in Chris' direction. Chris' eyes lock on to the paper and he reaches for it. "I'll be seeing you soon," the creepy overweight man assures. ---- A Few Days Later Undisclosed Location 11:52 AM A First Person Account (A Chris Bagwell narritive) My limo driver pulled into an empty parking lot and came to an abrupt stop. I looked around through the tinted glass, trying to see if I could locate the fat guy that shadily made his presence felt at the bar a few nights prior. I was nervous. I was curious. And I was even a bit afraid. The note didn't say much. Rather, it said little at all. It gave me a time and a location and said that it'd be a meeting that could potentially catapult my career. I'm not even sure why I came. I just pinned Vivica J. Valentine's shoulders to the mat. My career has been catapulted. I just have one last barrier in Andy Murray and I'll be gunning for the most prestigious World Championship left in the business. The GCW Championship. But I'm also egotistical. I like to get my hands dirty in whatever I can. And thats probably why I came. Thats probably why I'm here. And then in plain sight, I saw. It was a guy whom, at one point, I'd called a best friend. But he seemed to have faded off in the distance. He fell under my shadow, looking for guidance. And I suppose I did a good job. I always told him that some people stand in the darkness because they're afraid to step into the light. Finally, he wasn't afraid. He stepped out of the darkness -- out of my shadow... and into the light. As he approached the limosuine window, I scrambled for the right words to say. He tapped lightly, obviously unable to see through the tint. So I creeked the window down just a smidge. Zacharia Taylor. I'd started to wonder what the hell we were both doing here. Was this some sick plot by Caldera to try and frame me, using Taylor as the pawn? The irony of both Zacharia Taylor and myself being sent to the same abandoned parking lot was knee deep. And that was about the time I'd say it went up to my waist. In the not-so-distant distance, I caught a glimpse of another man whom I'd become quite profoundly familiar with. Zacharia was rambling on about something, but I just zoned him out. Completely not paying attention to a single word he had to say. I was dead focused on a guy whom, only a few days ago, I'd encountered backstage at WorldWide. We didn't have the prettiest of words for each other. But we weren't total enemies. Zacharia finally realized I hadn't been paying attention to him and so he tried to follow the path of my gaze. He turned to see who I saw. He turned to see Thom LeStrange. And for what reason? LeStrange joined our little rendevouz, asking the same questions that had been lingering on my mind. Why are we here? Whats this about? And why the hell is it us three? I kept my cool on the outside, in front of Taylor and LeStrange. I didn't want them to realize that I was just as skeptical of what was going on. My skepticism hit an all-time high when a fourth entered the picture. Two hundred and seventy seven pounds of solid rock built into a six foot four statue. Brad Jackson. It slowly started to come to me. I'd seen each of these men just moments before I left the arena in San Diego. It felt like a fourth installment of a Final Destination movie. Here we were, for a reason -- a purpose. And not a single one of us had any idea what it was for. I wouldn't even particularly say that any of us liked each other. But hell, maybe that made things all the better. All the more interesting. About eight or nine minutes had elapsed since I'd pulled in and finally a black Lincoln Town Car pulled up next to my limosuine. It was just a shrunken down version of what I was already driving... or being driven in. There stepped out the overweight man, still sporting his black fedora hat. It was at that time where he filled us in on the details and the selection process. Most of what he said went in one ear and out the other. But I did pick up a few things. I heard the name Jason Natas. I heard the name Simply Beautiful. I heard the name Union Jack. I heard the name Jimmy Bonafide. It finally clicked that. It was a battle of pride between PRIME and GCW. A battle of brands. A battle that could finally end the war and determine a victor. Caldera's intentions had become clear. He'd sided me with The Establishment for weeks to ensure my safety. He knew I'd be able to carry a team into battle against his ultimate rival. He knew I'd be able to do what Valentine couldn't do. And hell, he knew exactly what troops he needed to supply me with. When the overweight man left, I still hadn't known his name. I hadn't known who was sponsoring the match or what the stipulations even were. I wasn't really paying attention, so I really didn't know a thing about it. What I did know ... was that the parking lot I was sitting in was just an extention of The Philips Arena. And that tonight .. I'd catapult the GCW into infamy.
