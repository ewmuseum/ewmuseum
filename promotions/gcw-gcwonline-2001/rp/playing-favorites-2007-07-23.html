Playing Favorites

23 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/23/07 "God, he always cook this good?” The petite blonde laughs as she takes another bite of some shrimp that surely died in a grave sacrifice, prepared bizarre and edible most likely for some mixture of luck and chance. Her lipstick doesn't smear, her mascara doesn't run, her clothes don't fit just awkward enough that they’re stuck in an eternal drag to one side or the other: the newest Jordan White. And Danielle's just shaking her head with a soft smile. Jordan perks her thin eyebrows, leans on the table like she has to show how well she's listening as Dani drops the smile. "I didn't know he cooked." Sage: "I spent years studyin? Jack Tripper. It was like, a sensai thing. Except on television instead of, y'know, an actual relationship." Jordan: "You like Three's Company? Holy shit. What about the later seasons?" Sage: "Fuck Don Knotts." Danielle: "Could you please please please please not get him talking about that damned show?" Jordan: "What? Come on, how can you not like Three's Company?" Danielle: "I don't dislike it, but seriously. It's just a 70s sitcom and he's picked it up for idol worship and, could we just avoid it?" Sage: "She's been bitter ever since the 24 hour marathon." Danielle, dropping her fork to her plate with a clichink: "It was one fucking season of the show, Sage. One, repeated and repeated and repeated. One." Sage: "And it was the best season too. What's the problem?" Danielle: "Just drop it." Sage: "Hah, if you're gonna be all insistent about it at least like, try to move us onto some other topic instead of just bitching." Danielle: "Okay." She's idle, fucking with her food and staring at the plate. "What about music?" Jordan: "Oh yeah, we've gotta pick out my theme song sometime." And, like serendipity or manna from heaven or whatever the loquacious say to emphasize timing, Sage's phone starts ringing. Some MF DOOM track still, but has it ever mattered? Dani glances over from her plate, maybe seeing that it's not quite ceramicing up to her standards. “Fuckin' Frank again." “Bullshit. You’re fucking with me. You’re not fucking with me” Sage's nodding slow; she intuits and sighs. Jordan slides her chair further back from the table, lets those in the know do their complaining. "Fuck does he want? You just got off the phone." "Cats, his whole generation really, s'always on the edge of offended. Probably got pissed that I suckered him." "You mean the whole jumping him and leaving the country thing?" "No, no. Sending Sobias in without mentioning it. At any point." Danielle's got a vacant stare; Sage recoups. "Today. Hold on, lemme answer this." He straightens his face, rubs his eyes red and coughs as he flips the phone open. "Yo. What's up?" Shaking his head. "Oh yeah, yeah. Yeah, I sent Sobias. Yeah, to do the whole covering your stupid ass thing." Stabbing at a shrimp. Too dead to scream. "I mean, I told him to go do my job. Because, like. You really wanna see me right now?" Sage's eyes go wide. His food gets colder. "Okay. Well. Maybe I'm just busy. That could be a good excuse." He glances around. Jordan’again, the chick one’meets eyes with Danielle and points her thumb. She and Sage both nod, solemn, slow. Like funeral processions. "It's. I'm eating right now. Is there anyway you could like, hit me back up, later, sometime?" Jordan's resting his arm on a payphone, closed up in the booth and cradling his cellphone. People outside wait, stare’mainly at the big Mexican man wearing the horizonwide sneer. "When? Yeah, yeah. I can manage that. Shit. What you grubbin' on?" There's just the faintest beep; he stares at the screen and breathes deep. Over his shoulder, outside the scuffed plastic door; he stares at Sobias until Sobias stares back. Throwing his cin up and pocketing his phone, he opens the accordion doors and makes it outside. "That went well." "Hmmph?" "Sage." "So he did send me. What a tremendous surprise." "Ain't that, I mean. Like, after that shit the other week I wasn't really expectin' to see him anytime soon an' shit, but I mean, muhfuckin?, dog said he was about to land like, five minutes “fore you showed up. Just, shit surprised me a little, y’know?" "What a motherfucker. It's not surprising, though. He's a bit, passive aggressive when he's not going psychofuck on someone. But I guess you're a little more aware of that now." "Shit. You an' him chill?" "Hefh. Sage and I have a few issues. Not exactly close." Sobias hefts his suitcase and starts with a stomp to the nearest street. Trudging down the asphalt in the bake of the sun; Sobias sweating golden in khakis and a wifebeater, eyes squinted behind expensive sunglasses that just look cheap. Jordan hangs in his shadow with long strides, struggles against melted rubber soles for each leap. Hyperbole, maybe. "Where we headed?" "I'm going to a fucking hotel. This isn't a bonding experience." "I mean, yeah, but. Figured we could grab a beer, chill out for awhile. Ain't like you've got shit else to do, y'know?" "I have to go to a hotel." "Well. After that." "I'm going to fucking sleep." "C'mon, Sobias’I can call you that, righ'? No like, 'Mr. Torm' bullshit or nothin'?" "I don't care." "Cool, cool. Aight then, Sobias, you go get checked in, do your shit, then we hit up a few clubs an' see if you can't tag some Aussie ass while you down here." Torm stomps to a stop, turns enough to pitch his watch into shadow in the name of actually reading it. "It's not even two." "An' I still ain't had shit to drink, so you gotta tell me somethin' quick." "Hah. I like the way you think." The chicano laughs into himself, rumbles and roars and slams an open palm across Jordan's shoulder blades. The kid staggers. "Fuck it, I've got time." "Shit yeah, that's what I'm talkin' about." Pause. He breathes in deep, fills his lungs and holds it as he rolls his arms back in their sockets. There's gotta be something the slap's still echoing against. "Sage is never down for that shit." "What, getting fucked up beyond his means and making an ass of himself?" "Shit, nah. That's like, his thing, ain't it? Heh, shit. I mean like, just bummin' around, tryin' to pick up a little pussy an' alla that." "That's certainly new." "Dude's married, I mean, but shit." Sobias shakes his head with a tight sneer, runs his hand back and fights his slicked hair back down. "I've known him a very long time. Him changing at all, for anything, any reason, or anyone, is surprising." "Changed his name." "Phah. Only because he can't change himself. Trust me. He's as Goddamned stubborn as he is blind." "Ain't that bad." "Jesus, man. He beat the fuck out of you. He can't be paying you that well." "It's good money, I mean, but I know I had that shit comin'." "Bullshit." "Nah, nah; seriously man. Been bein' a sorta dick an' all, an' it's like, everybody's gotta be put in their place now an' 'en, y'know?" "That's bullshit, too." He shoves two tree branch fingers into his breast pocket, pulls out cigarettes, boxed. Can you guess what he does with one? Cool. "Sage, Sage'll make a person crazy. If you listen to him, that is. Not many people are that stupid, and even the stupid ones figure it out eventually." "Dog ain't that bad." "No? Huh. Guess I've just been wrong about him for the last seven, eight years." "Maybe he just don't like you." "Hah. Sage doesn't like anyone." "Shit. Nothin' personal, but either way? I don't really wanna talk about dude all day, y'know?" "Can't say as I blame you. Man of your stature should be talking business." "Thanks. Righ??" "No." "Oh. Then, well. Fuck you. Shit got hectic." Sobias snorts, laughs, smirks as a singular event. "Don't blame yourself, kid. Sometimes a manager'll leave your mind in a bad way." "Man, I know difference 'tween shit bein' Sage's fault and shit bein' Rollins fault. Ain't gonna get confused on that." And then, then it's like: things happen. Greatly insignificant’really’and of no import, or consequence; totally predictable things that've already been mentioned, laid out, and are absolutely zilcho but the routines. Less than zilcho, even; nothing that matters even comes up, much less gets said. They bullshit, they move. Several hours later, they're headlong into a drunk. "Sage ever talk about the small days?" "Small days?" Jordan’or Frank, or whatever’stares at the head on a metric unit of beer. Maybe lager; too late to start trying to figure that out, though. "Old times. Back before he was fucking rich and pompous." "Nah, nah. Dude ain't tell many stories." "Hanh. Not surprising. He make it out like, implying he was some sort of great?" Jordan shakes, no. "Humh. Ah, least he's not lying." "Man, like, what kinda stories? Small times, or whatever." "Well, he worked in the White House for awhile." "What? Fuckin', no fuckin' way. No way they'd hire him. Dude's got a spider. On his fuckin' forehead." "O Jesus, it was before that dumbass tattoo. He wasn't, he wasn't hired either. There was a show in the area, maybe Maryland? I'm not’too’sure. That doesn't matter though. He wanted to check out the capital and, see, back then, Jordan’fuck, Sage, whatever’spent a lot of time really got damned lit. Bad into smack when it wasn't bad into meth, y'know? But alright, we take the tour and I'm more or less just fucking babysitting him by the time we make it to the White House. I think he was about, four hours into a few mesc pellets?" Sobias breaks, stares under the shadows to the woodpaneled ceiling’it's so insincere. "Yeah, that sounds right. Anyways, I turn my back on him, go to take a piss or whatever, and when I get back, the jackass has completely disappeared." "What? No way." "Oh, no, this is. This is very serious. Back before this Goddamned fascist lockdown, late Clinton years. I assumed he'd, well. I assumed he'd tried to wrest control of the country away from psycho Billy, but, I'd hoped he'd just gotten lost, joined up with another tour group." "Had he?" "I started this out saying he worked as a tour guide. Do you think this ends with him waiting for me outside?" "Well, not really." "Alright, then. He, he wasn't outside of course. I spent a little time looking for him, but you know, it's not like he was functionally retarded’he'd always sober up and track me down. This time though, he just straight up fucking blew the show entirely’didn't get in touch with anyone, just didn't show up. Almost rolls around time for the next show, still haven't heard from him, still haven't seen news of him being arrested for treason or mutiny or anything; I go back to the fucking White House and this son of a bitch is giving out tours." "You're not jokin?" Sobias lowers his head, drops his brow; raises his brow, smirks; blows out in something like a chortle; shakes his head. "No. He had the whole uniform; didn't seem to recognize me until a little into the tour. Turns out, the Abecave entrance is hidden behind the bookshelf." "Shittin' me." "Well. There is no Abecave. That was just, part of Sage's little speech." "Man, how the fuck did he pull that off?" "He's, he's good at not getting arrested? I don't know. Fuck. It's not like any of those tour guides back then were sober or anything. World was different. Eight years ago." He laughs, mainly at himself. "True." Cocking his jaw, eyes wide and flitting to the distance; finding some pleasing curve and zoning out with a zen dedication. He comes to with a cutoff laugh, toned down and almost disappointed. "Goddamn. That's fuckin' crazy." "Oh, yes. Sage has always had a few problems like that." "Haha, how the hell's that a problem, man? Shit's pretty smooth, I mean." "He'd. He'd pretty much acclimated himself to being a tour guide. Don't you get how that's an issue?" Shrug. Noncommittal and indecisive. "Sage used to adapt to things very quickly. He could be almost anything’then it's almost like he figured that out and realized he, that he wanted to be a real boy or something. He got his head on something that felt solid and started to level out. Which is why he's such a Goddamned uppity bitch today." "Hah. That sounds pretty alright though. I mean, I ain't see what you got against dude at least." "He's fucked over a lot of people and, quite frankly? I liked a lot of these people. Fuck. I liked Sage. He was a hell of a lot like a puppy." "Shit, no way. Sage 'on't seem like the type take orders, y'know." "A puppy, not a dog. Christ, if you yelled he'd look at you’not like he could really understand, but he'd give in and he'd make consolations and he'd back the fuck down when he was wrong." "Huh, guess some shit do change." Jordan's eyes wander, he grips the mug and pantomimes actual drinking. Setting it down, breaking wide that contented grin. Follows with actual drinking. "You can't stop progress." The kid shrugs. "So, aight then; how'd this fuckup kid end up all rich, ballin' an' shit?" Torm laughs into his hand, pulls a lit match to a wide Camel. "Wrestling got him some money, it didn't get him rich." He waves the match dead, sulfur fading into the general stench of a dark bar malaise. "Yeah, I mean. I know all that takin' a company an' runnin' fraud an' shit." "Hey, hey. Keep your voice down. We don't say thing's like 'fraud' in public. Unbecoming." "Hah, well, aight then. But, I mean like, how'd he even get like, where he was at in wrestlin'? I remember dude's career, alla that shit, but it ain't like he was established or nothin'." "I got him good matches, back when he listened to me at least. Doesn't really matter when you're in the boss' camp though’forgoes a few song and dances in convincing." "Well, you bringin' that same advice now?" "What the hell are you talking about? Jesus, kid. You know what you've gotta do." "Yeah?" Torm nods. Jordan braces forearms against the table, leans in close. "O, Lord. You've got to beat the everliving shit out of Rollins." "Oh yeah, yeah. Sorry, shit seemed a little, Iunno, obvious?" "Yeah. You'll get your shot though; look, could you go grab a couple more drinks?" "That's word." "And buy a round for that little blonde table over there, too. I'll cover it." "Hah; aight, aight." Kid follows his headsup, sliding out of the booth and fading into the scenery as the bear maintains a constant sigh through the finding and dialing of his phone, letting it fade to a hum and out during the long ring. "Dude. I'm busy." "Huh. For some reason, I just don't believe you." "Yeah? That's cold. Fuck are you two doin' anyway, eating donuts?" "Why the hell would we be doing that?" "Well. I am, and. I don't know. Just sorta figured you might be too. That's all. Hey, gimme a second." "Okay." "Not, not you, Sobe. Was talkin' to Danielle. Gotta step outside." "Where are you?" "Krispy fuckin' Kreme, prick." "Oh. Of course." "You're fuckin' sanctimonious, you know that?" There's that flick of a lighter. Heavier breathing, longer exhaling. "I’ve got an idea about Jordan." "What? Make it quick, I'm sorta, tryin' somethin' very precarious and don't wanna fuck up." "Hm?" "Don't even worry about it. Now, make with the talking about Jordan." "Wait, who are you with?" "Fuck's it matter?" "I'm your attorney, Sage. It's important that I know these things." "You just wanna know what I'm doin'. Admit it." "Eh." "Don't gimme that passive bullshit. I swear, I will rip your throat out." "Yeah? Huh. Alright, have you talked to Caldera about the Rollins situation?" "Wait, Rich? As in, the motherfucking Renegade and shit?" "Yeah." "Shit. Didn't realize he was back around. That's pretty cool." "He. He knocked Jordan out and made him lose the title. The other day, actually." "To, to fuckin' Shaman, was it?" "Didn't you see it?" "I don't watch wrestling, man. That shit's sorta. Well. Y'know." "How could you not watch your prot’g? defend his title?" "You have no clue how fuckin' deliberate and wellconstructed this plan I'm on is. I spent a lot of time plotting, and considering, and other assorted things like that. Because this is way more important than if Frank has a belt or not." "What" "Quit fucking this up, Torm. I'll. Shit. Fuckin' kid lost the belt?" "Yes." "Mother of fuck. Look, man. I'll get you Stevesy's number tomorrow and you can talk it out with him. I'm. Very very busy and I'm sure a lawyer of your caliber can find someone else's teat to suck for awhile. Oh shit, that reminds me. You know magic?" "Have you gone stupid?" "Fuck. That's a no." And Jordan comes back. "Man, that Sage? Lemme holla at him." "Kid wants to talk." Sage wasn't lying about being very busy and has already flipped his phone shut. It's hard to hang up a cellphone angrily’it's all about swiftness. Pocketing it and puffing away at his Newport; brings it down to halfmast before stomping it out and heading back into the yellow glow of an allnight run. He breathes a slow plume of smoke on his way to the table, lets it trail behind him like steam engines and dying cars. The seat's already out, still waiting on him. "What a chump." "Come on, he can't be that bad." Danielle cuts her eyes to Jordan; Sage's laughing it off. "Which one?" "Out of who? Okay, I'm confused here." "Sobias, lawyer, chump in question." "Oh, okay. I thought you were talking about, um. Frank." "Frank's fuckin', a fuckin' cokehead of the worst kind. And totally goddamned unreliable." "God, how late is it?" "Now it's a how early is it." "Oh, fuck me. I never got around to checking out a room." Drops the half of her donut that still exists in warm, crispy, glazed form; mouths out more "fuck"s. "Well." Heads turn/rise to Danielle. She is the one given all the good ideas. "We've got a spare room. You could stay with us." Big blue eyes, striated skyblue/oceanblue, spread glossy. "You sure I wouldn't be, uh, imposing?" "It's fine. I just need to have a little talk with Sage first." "Oh, okay." Sage digs into the box of donut holes. Jordan shines a smile unto the world. Danielle. Sits. "The kind of talk where you go to the car and wait for us." "Ohh." She pushes away from the table, chair legs screeching castrato against checkerboard linoleum. "Ohhhhh." Stumbling away, watches the reflection of her being watched in the reflection. It straightens her step, pushes her hands down her sides and straightens out her dress too. Through the glass door and to the Impala’unlocked. She crawls into the backseat sprawl, splayed; lights a roach of Sage origin and voyeurs on conversation.
