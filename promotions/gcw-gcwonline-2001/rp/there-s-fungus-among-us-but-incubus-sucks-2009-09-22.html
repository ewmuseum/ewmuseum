There's Fungus Among Us but Incubus Sucks.

22 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/22/09 Autumn, the changing of the seasons, the dying before pure white death, the sacrifice before life anew in the spring. Dead leaves, a blaze of firey colors, crunch beneath feet. Bonfires light up the hills like beacons: here be man. Farmers finish up their last harvests and the stockpile is added to before winter’s frigid fingers comb across the land. There’s many reasons that make autumn a special time of year… but it’s most special reason is not listed here. Oktoberfest? No… Football? No… Eating turkey? Nay… Pumpkins? Closer… Halloween? Nope… you’re colder… … Eh… Frost? Now you’re freezing, I’ll just tell you… … Squash. “SQUA-ASH!?!?” A roadside farmer’s stand on a dusty country highway. This is Americana. Chris Kannon and Kenny Mercury stand before a few wooden crates filled with assorted autumnal veggies. Pumpkins, corn, apples… and the squash. “This is their last hurrah, Ken. This may be our last chance… soon these denizens of the dirt road will be all mold and mush. There’s fungus everywhere… spores. They take what’s good in life and they spoil it, turning into soil. DIRT.” Mercury leaned over the vegetables, his face pained, “But why… why do we have to EAT this crap?! This is feed for COMMONERS…” “To know the squash… you must EAT the squash… for that is their primary objective in life. To be eaten… they are sustenance…” Kenny grit his teeth. He panicked, “BUT WHAT DOES THIS HAVE TO DO WITH US!? WITH ME!?” Chris remained calm. His associate was infamous worldwide as one of prewriting’s biggest hot heads. Kenny Mercury was a short fuse… and he’d lost his mind. The small amount of demented rationale that he once possessed had been lost forever. He was a ticking time bomb… He was a wild animal, but a wounded one. Mercury needed Kannon. Chris’ guidance and nurturing could be the thing that could bring him back from the brink of oblivion and resurrect his career… his life. Chris, of course, knew this well; he had dictated it. “We must DEVOUR the squash before it spoils and in turn spoils everything else around it. The GCW tag team division is chock full of nothing more than squash. You leave the squash out too long and even though it appears resilient…. Eventually… the spores get all over and the FUNGAL take-over is already upon you. The fungi of the federation will breed at an alarming rate… and soon you have nothing left but dirt… shit.” “So… Primetime Enterprise’s nothing more than a moldy pair of fungi infested squashes?” “Yep.” Chris lit up a cigarette and took one fully confident drag. “Right.” A sinister grin slashed across Mercury’s face. In one fell swoop his hands shot out around the sides of the wooden crate, gripping the corners. Kenny tore the crate from it’s legs and cackled like a mad coyote as he carried it off to the opened back door of a navy blue full size utility van. “You of all people should know the inner workings of a squash match, monsieur… Tonight we dine on squash… on WorldWide, we dine on Joe Milone and Defoe Smith… bringing a beginning to the end of their ilk before they ruin the fruit of the GCW tag division before we even taste its sweet sweet juices…” Like many country produce stands, it was unattended. Left out for the good ole country folk, the ones with morals… a code of country honor, to leave money in a little box. Paying what they thought was right. Not that it mattered if the stand was manned… they would have dispatched the attendee in a moments notice. Chris held up a pair of bolt cutters as he exhaled a plume of gray smoke. CRACK The padlock hit gravel… Kannon emptied the contents of the box into his torn jean pockets. “Fuel economy.” A three round burst of doors slamming shut and the van was back on the road. Now this… this was freedom. The Schizophrenic Demonoid Mobile itself, carrying its horde of invading Viking warriors to their next destination… to sack its next town… to defile its next populace… to lay waste to its guardians… Primetime Enterprise. Destination: Pennsylvania. Chris Kannon always wanted to be in a rock band. It was poetry, it was romance, it was pure destruction and violence. It was freedom and it was direction. Get the fuck in the van and destroy every town in your path. It was a war machine. It was Black Flag. I'm about to have a nervous breakdown My head really hurts If I don't find a way out of here I'm gonna go berserk cause I'm crazy and I'm hurt Head on my shoulders It's going... Berserk! I hear the same old talk talk talk The same old lines Don't do me that today Yeah if you know what's good for you, you'll get out of my way Cause, I'm crazy and I'm hurt Head on my shoulders Going... Berserk! I won't apologize For acting outta line You see the way I am You leave any time you can cause I'm crazy and I'm hurt Head on my shoulders Going... Berserk! Crazy! Crazy! Crazy! Crazy! I don't care what you fuckin' do! I don't care what you fuckin' say! I'm so sick of everything I just want to... Die! Nervous Breakdown ripped through the shitty speakers of the Schzoid van as it tore a gash down the middle of the highway. Mercury slept in the back on a bed of multicolored squash and bedding not fit for stray dog. Visions of broken bones and blood-curdling screams danced in his French-fried yam head. Chris Kannon pushed down on the gas. No fucking way would he trust Mercury behind the wheel… the man was created for one thing and one thing alone and that only occurred within a elevated square stage surrounded by a trio of cables. This was his both his dream, his escape, and his revenge. This was rock and roll and all the ugliness that that entailed. Mercury was his instrument. GCW was his stage. The tag division; their victims and the audience… the fucking schmucks that just would never “get it.” But what’s their to “get?” Pure… unadulterated… destruction… that is TRUTH. The green mask of the Manalishi rest upon the dash. It’s empty eyes stared up at Kannon’s baby blues, watching the sleep collect in the corners. What was behind that mask when Kannon didn’t wear it? It was never a disguise… it didn’t hide anything or anyone. Behind that mask was Chris Kannon. But by itself… The Manalishi… it was both ghost and devil… it was a demon… boiling up from the netherworld to extract its revenge on the world of man. Chris was a mere specter of what he once was… at his best he stood victorious against the devil himself, Bryan Mayhem, as Television champion at NC-17 for all the world to see. At his worst… he was “Poo”… the skin-and-bones kid whose entrance was accompanied by Boy George and Culture Club. Who was sicker? GCW’s writing team for coming up with the idea… or Chrissy-Poo for accepting it? Accept nothing. You beat the Devil… and became a demon yourself… You’ve faded from imaginations of the people. But now, you reemerge in their nightmares. The Manalishi is truth… The Manalishi is revenge. The Manalishi is retribution… The Manalishi is power and MYTH. The Manalishi is everything you wish you could be… The Manalishi is A MASK. You parasite. You louse. You LEECH. USER. USER. USER. Keep telling yourself that. Keep telling yourself that you’re a foil: a catalyst. You’re a right hand man. You make things happen, you’re a Moses leading the lost out of the desert. Keep lying to yourself. Keep sucking the blood until you’re so bloated off of someone else’s spark until it fizzles out and you have nothing left to do but detach. Fall off… fat and full of hot life itself, leech. You incubus. You second rate demon leech… you’re a fucking NIT on the dreamer’s greasy nut hair. Keep… SUCKING.
