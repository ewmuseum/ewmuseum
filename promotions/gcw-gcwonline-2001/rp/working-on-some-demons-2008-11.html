Working on some Demons

November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November “Sometimes our demons get the best of us, Other times they become us? The ring is empty. No soul is in sight of the million dollar square. The Orlando heat swelters inside of the vacant arena. The turnbuckles feel warm like blood. The mat feels sticky like the hardwood floor after a Friday night rave. Without anyone to break its silence, the faint screaming of departing planes echoes through the halls. Tears break through the stillness of the air and smash into the canvas like kamikaze planes into the still water of Pearl Harbor. Zacharia Taylor wipes his eyes dry to give way to another stream. He sits up against a turnbuckle, his bare back against the warm padding. He has complete composure over his body, no real signs of the act of crying, yet there are still tears. He lifts his head up to reveal wide eyes and a grin that would put Chris Bagwell to shame at his own game. Tears of joy? Not quite Taylor’s thing. He looks down and sees the wet mat below him and runs his finger through the small puddle. Violently, he starts to punch the mat, making it sound like Kimbusa stomping to the middle after Andy Murray. He jumps up to his feet and turns around. He looks at the turnbuckle like a child looking at computer for the first time, not knowing what to do with it. Taylor suddenly starts to punch at the turnbuckle, moving the stiff pad slightly side to side with every left and right. He grabs the top of the turnbuckle and starts kneeing into the pad. He starts with just throwing his knee into the center and escalates into jumping knees to the top of the pad. Tears stop flowing from his eyes. His eyes become narrow slits of rage and his nostrils flair with ever deep, reverberating breath. Taylor turns around and runs towards the center, jumping into the air in dropkick fashion. He is suspended in the air for what feels like an entire match. His head becomes clearer with the wind cooling the beads of sweat on his face. His maddening eyes relax and his face is relieved. His body releases for a matter of seconds but his muscles contract once again bracing for the fall. He does a backwards roll and lands on his feet. He motions around an opponent that isn’t there and slams him down in a suplex. He rolls to the side and gets up to fake a sit down power bomb. He sits there after the power bomb and starts to envision the arena populating itself. The announcers make it to their table and the ref comes into the ring. The fans are silent, but their bodies show contrast to the noise as they are on their feet screaming. Chris Bagwell appears grappled up with an invisible body. Clyde Fox appears holding onto the bottom rope near the turnbuckle. LeStatt Knight lays beside Taylor, wincing in pain. Andy Murray appears opposite Digital Mortality. Vivica Valentine appears in the grasps of Chris Bagwell. Taylor looks around and sees himself laying where he just power bombed, completely out cold. Taylor tries to get up, but a police officer runs up to him. Confused, Taylor hesitates and then realizes that the Police officer wants to help him. He tries to reach out with his hand to grab what the man in uniform is holding out, but his arm is stopped. He looks own and sees his hand handcuffed to Knight. He grabs the key with his other hand and unhand cuffs himself from Knight. Everyone is moving in slow motion. Taylor stands up and walks over to Clyde Fox, eyes closed and muscles flaring under the tension of holding on. He then walks over to Chris Bagwell and pokes him. To his surprise he feels real. Taylor looks over and sees himself still laying down, out cold on the mat. He looks up to the megaton and the words “Dangerous Games 2008” are very slowly strobing. Taylor looks around for Garbage Bag Johnny but he is nowhere to be found. Taylor closes his eyes and runs his hands through his hair, but to his surprise, his hair is really long and dirty. He opens his eyes immediately and looks down. These aren’t his hands. He IS Garbage Bag Johnny. He yells out in a fit of rage and starts to throw punches. He hits Chris Bagwell in the face, but he doesn’t move. Instead, an imprint of his knuckles appears on his face. His skin slowly rights itself as the slow motion wrestler moves forward in time. He throws an elbow into the side of Vivica’s head and then runs over to Clyde and stomps on his face. Clyde goes unchanged, but the bottom rope he is holding onto goes back a foot and slowly moves back to place. Taylor steps in front of a falling Digital Mortality and unloads a barrage of punches and kicks to the falling former champ. Only indentations come of Digi. He turns around and stands before a life size, grinning Zacharia Taylor. “What are you doing Johnny?” “I’m not Johnny. I’m?” “Who” Who exactly are you? Me? No, that’s impossible. You are the good for nothing scum that steals belts from men when they are down and unsuspecting. You are the bastard that goes behind people’s backs and?” “I am Zacharia Taylor! I’m no thief!” The Taylor before him side steps, showing nobody laying in the center anymore. All of the wrestlers disappear from the ring, leaving the two talking. “You’re not me. You will never be me again. You lost that. You lost that when you lost your belt!” “I’m no different than what I was before Dangerous Games. I’m no different than?” ??than me standing here? On the contrary.” A mirror appears in front of him. He gets behind it and the full fledged figure of Garbage Bag Johnny stands in reflection, smiling back at him. “So, you were saying” Sometimes when we are so angry, our rage embodies us in our enemy, or the one reason for our uncontrollable emotions. You, my friend, lost yourself because of Garbage Bag Johnny! But hey, let’s face it, there’s only room for one Zacharia Taylor, and you lost that.” Embodied in Johnny’s body, he backs up into the turnbuckle, shaking his head in his hands, tears again start to stream down his face. Zacharia Taylor puts his arm around the mirror and looks at the image, unchanged of Johnny even though “he” backed away to the turnbuckle. The mirrored Johnny smiles and looks at the Taylor standing next to him and points at the turnbuckle, laughing. He opens his mouth to talk, but it is Zacharia’s voice. “You said it yourself, how could you be mad for compromising on the situation presented. I mean?” The voice changes to Chris Bagwell. ??it is a competition, as well as a business, am I right? You are no stronger than the little?” The voice changes to Dan Black. ??bitch that walked through the doors in January and into the Movement, or should I say into Chris Bagwell’s shadow. But I do have to hand it to you?” The voice changes into Jacob McKail. ??you have come from a nobody, to somebody. Even if you don’t want to face who you really are right now. I mean, come on Taylor, you are still trying?” The voice changes into Axel Action. ??to work out the same demons you had when you started. Nothing is ever good enough. Well I will tell you something you can live by?” The voice changes to Garbage Bag Johnny. ??You will never be better than me!” The mirrored image starts to laugh, being echoed by the Zacharia Taylor standing beside the mirror. Taylor picks up the mirror and moves it into the turnbuckle at the opposite corner. He goes to the center of the ring and gets down on one knee. “So, this is where we are at?” He sits down on his ass and brushes off his knee. ??You need to admit defeat, my friend. Or else you are going to have some serious issues to work on. There can only be one Zacharia Taylor?” He lays down on his back. ??so both of us can’t exist. Either, you embrace your rage and take it behind stage and in the ring, or you take yourself out and let us all close our eyes. All you have to do is pin me. Just like Johnny did at Dangerous Games, the best, worst day of our lives. Just throw your right hand over me, and we will call it a day.” The image of Garbage Bag Johnny in the mirror nods and Taylor comes out of his corner. He walks over to the motionless Zacharia Taylor and falls to his side. He looks down at the helpless man before him. “You’re right, it’s time for change?” Taylor throws a hand over the body before him. The two men switch positions, Johnny laying on his back in the center of the ring, and Taylor taking his real form. A ref appears next to him, throwing the belt around his chest. He fans go wild, yet there are still no audible sounds out of their mouths. Taylor throws the belt at the mirror where an image of himself is sneering at him behind reflective protection, but the glass explodes in every direction as the belt hits. Taylor climbs out of he ring and starts to walk up the ramp. “Hey Taylor?” Taylor turns around and looks into the ring. Chris Bagwell, Jacob McKail, Vivica Valentine, and Garbage Gag Johnny, with the belt around his body stand leaning on the ropes in their own way. Chris Bagwell has his arms crossed in a smirk worth millions. McKail holds his cigarette between his two fingers, ember towards his palm. Vivica Leans up against the turnbuckle examining her finger nails. And Johnny holds the belt tight to his chest. He looks at his fellow ringmates and smiles. “We will be waiting for you?” Zacharia Taylor smiles and walks up the ramp and out of sight.
