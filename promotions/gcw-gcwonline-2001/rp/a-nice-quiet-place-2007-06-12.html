A Nice Quiet Place

12 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 June 07 A wave of silence hits the bar when he walks in, everyone’s eyes fixing on the large figure that strolls across the floor and towards the bar. Of course there are only like six people in here anyways. His bright blue eyes meet mine when he gets no more than ten feet away. “How ya doin LK?” I ask. “Fine I guess.” He slides off his jacket and places it on the chair before sitting down. “What’ll ya have hun?” “Two things senorita, a Bud Light and a shot of Patron.” I didn’t mean to but I know I gave a bit of an awkward look. I have been working at this pub for 10 years and serving this customer for four of them and only three times has he ever ordered anything besides a beer. “Problem Joyce?” I come back to my senses quickly and reach below the bar to pull out the beer and then above the bar to grab the bottle of Patron. “Nah just am not used to ordering anything but beer.” I say with a smile as I pour the shot. “Something wrong?” Of course I knew what it was; I’ve been following the career of LeStatt Knight since we met those four years ago. He knew it too based on the look he gives as he puts back the shot of tequila. “Come on now, I’m pretty sure you saw what happened.” “Yeah, Yeah I did.” One of the first things I learned after meeting Roberto Abitua was that he loved his job and he wanted nothing more than to succeed. It wasn’t tough to figure out that he was no ordinary person the moment he first stepped into this place, I mean how often do you meet 6’6? Mexicans weighing 270lbs.” Not to mention the fact that every muscle on his body cuts through like a knife on butter. “What’d you think?” He asks me between sips of beer. “Honestly” You’re rusty.” I answer. “Honestly?” “Honestly.” “I thought the same for a moment actually but then it just seemed like some kind of excuse.” His eyes lower. “But you don’t make excuses LK and we both know that.” That was another thing I learned about this man upon meeting him. Whatever he does, he owns up to and will never accept someone else protecting him and he’ll never apologize to anyone. Especially not when beating up a certain bar tender’s boyfriend ? namely my own. Sure my boyfriend started it because he caught me smiling at Knight but who is into details? “Yeah.” It sounded as if he had more to say but just know how to express what it was. Instead he fiddles with the beer bottle on the bar, pushing it from one hand to the other, grinding it along the counter top. “It’s something I wanted bad.” He answers softly. “I don’t doubt that.” I reach up and lift his chin softly. “But you can’t be too hard on yourself, you know these things happen.” He forces a smile my way, even his fake smiles can make a woman melt. But that’s neither here nor there. “I’ve just been sitting around ya know” Wondering exactly what it is that keeps me coming back.” He cocks his head to the side slightly. “I’ve done it all in GCW, what if it IS time for me to move on?” “Oh bullshit!” The authority in which the words came out of my mouth not only caused Knight to jerk his head straight but it almost frightened me as well. “Pardon?” “That’s bullshit and you know it.” I say as I pour another shot of Patron. “I have known you for awhile and I know that you are not tired of this just yet.” “The issue isn’t me getting bored, it’s if I can still win at the level that I was before. It seems like every chance I’ve gotten to prove myself at a grand stage, I’ve failed.” He shakes his head. “I lost to Michael Stevens at NC-17 2005, Shadow 2006, and Vivica fucking Valentine at Warpath ? and that last one is just fucking humiliating.” I have heard Knight discuss retirement before in his visits to my bar, a few times actually. The first time I believed him completely since it was the first time but after the third time I was more than likely not going to believe him. Sure he’s taken time off but he has never REALLY considered retiring but this time could be different. I see it in the way he looks, talks, sits and even breaths. Not to mention he just got back from a break a few months ago. This time it’s also not because of family issues that he is considering retiring ? its wondering if he still has what it takes. “You know what I think?” his eyes meet mine. “I think that you are scared.” He jumps back as if someone just hit him in the mouth. “SCARED!”” he yelps, his voice even cracking because of the surprise in it. “Yeah scared. There are a bunch of new and hungry people stepping into the scene and you are afraid of being put down by them.” “I think you have been drinking too much my friend.” He says with a smile. “Because you and I both know I don’t fear any man.” “But I got you to smile didn’t I?” “That you did.” He tips his glass to me and kills his first beer. I grab the bottle and replace it right away, Knight never drinks any less than three on his visits here, also something he taught me being his bartender. I grab the shot glass next and lift it, signaling the question “would you like another?” “Yes ma’am.” He answers. I pour the shot and quickly slide it over in front of him. “So tell me,” Knight puts the tequila down as fast as I put it in front of him and slams the shot glass down to the table. “What’s this really about” It can’t be about you second guessing what you got.” “Partially.” He shrugs. “There are just some things I’ve been doing that I’m not too sure about.” “Like joining The Establishment?” His head jerks my way. “Actually no.” He says quietly but yet very emphatic. “You don’t think your image could be tarnished?” “Look I didn’t sell out to anything or anybody to get where I am. People can be ignorant and call me a sell-out if they want to but it’s only because they do not see the full picture yet.” “The full picture?” I ask. “The Establishment isn’t about taking people by storm with sheer numbers and brutality. No anymore, I am not that pussy Octavian or any of Caldera’s old cronies ? not by a long shot.” “Then what?” “Purifying GCW.” “Purification” Sounds like some Nazi shit to me.” Knight chuckles a bit. “Come on now Joyce.” He takes a swig of beer. “The GCW has been overrun by people with barely any of the talent that courses through my veins and let me say that I intend to weed them out as soon as possible.” “But how can you do that by retiring.” The bar grows quiet again. This is when I realize that the people in the bar are still keeping their eyes gazed upon the figure of the 6’6? monster of a Mexican. It brings back the fond memories of his first talked. This makes me smile. My smirk causes knight to turn as well and he too notices all of the looks and stares, probably from a group of people who may not know who he is exactly but DO understand that he isn’t just ANYBODY. “Very true.” He responds back. “Can’t you ever just pretend to believe that I’m going to retire?” “It’s not in me to let you quit.” “Not even when we’re fucking.” “Especially not then.” We share a laugh together. Most people wouldn’t imagine that such a big celebrity would find his way to a place like this one, maybe thinking all of them prefer the glamour of Hollywood but Roberto Abitua AKA LeStatt Knight is a different person. No one knows he comes and I’m sure that is the way he likes it. He may never be able too “blend in” with a crowd but he does his best here. “So what now?” I ask as I put his final beer in front of him. “Well,” he picks up his beer. “I finish this beer and then I head out to Australia for WorldWide.” “Who ya got this week?” “Chris Storm.” “Who?” “Exactly.” He says with a wink. “Well good luck with that.” The man didn’t need luck though and he knows that. But Chris Storm? He might need a miracle Or Jesus.
