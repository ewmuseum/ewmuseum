A Recap of Events

21 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/08 The rancid smell of blood and mold mixed together assaulted her nostrils. Her eyes were covered by what felt like a thick piece of satiny material. She could hear screams but couldn’t tell where they were coming from. Delylah tried to get up but the tethers on her wrists and ankles held her bound to the stiff backed chair. She twisted and pulled but could not free herself. A familiar voice speaks to her. “So you have figured out my little plan have you” Do you actually think you can stop me?” Her sister’s voice resonated against the solid walls. “I have our father but he is quite reluctant to talk. He won’t give me the answers that I so desperately need. So now he must suffer.” Delylah heard footsteps closing in on her but they stopped about five feet in front of her. She heard the whimpering of a man whom she knew was her father. Then there was an agonizing scream. “Leave him alone. Don’t hurt him.” Delylah pleaded. “Don’t worry sister dear. I wont drain him of his blood completely. What better way to have my father for always than to make him one of my children.” Cleopatra cackled. “NO! Please don’t.” Delylah bolted upright in her bed. She looked around and noticed she was still in the bedroom of her childhood. Her plan had worked. She knew that if she slept, even if for just a little while Cleopatra would give her the answers she needed. Now that she knew her plan she had to stop her. Her father had done a lot of horrible things to both of them but he didn’t deserve to be the monster that her sister was. “Come in.” Delylah answered the tapping on her bedroom door. The door slowly crept open and there stood Paul. Tears came to her eyes partly in happiness and partly in terror. “What are you doing here” Why didn’t you just stay home?” Delylah choked out through her sobs. “I thought you would be happy to see me. I know you told me to stay home but I just couldn’t Del. I knew you were going to get yourself in trouble somehow.” Paul told her as he crossed the room. “I know where she is Del. I can help.” “How do you know where she is” How did you find out?” Delylah asked with wide-eyed fear. “You know she is in my mind as much as she is yours Del. I know what she has done and what she plans on doing. If you go there by yourself she will kill you and get exactly what she has been after.” Paul told her calmly. “I know you were going to take off without me to protect me. I made a vow to you Del, a vow to protect you for always, and I intend to keep that promise.” Tears streamed down Delylah’s cheeks as she wrapped her arms around Paul’s neck. Deep down she knew that she wouldn’t be able to leave him behind. She knew that Cleopatra wouldn’t let that happen. “I need you to make one more promise Paul. If something should happen to me you get out of there. Save yourself. If she has me she will leave you alone.” “That is a promise I’m not sure that I can make Del. I love you too much to see anything happen to you.” “If something were to happen to you Paul I would never be able to forgive myself. That is why I didn’t tell you what I was planning after I found out she had my father.” “Can you be sure your mother will be safe if we leave her here by herself?” “Cleopatra will have what she wants as soon as we are within her grasp Paul. She doesn’t want my mother. She knows mom didn’t have anything to do with her being given away. If she wanted her she would have taken her a long time ago.” “We should leave soon then. It is about a two hour drive from here and it is almost dusk.” Paul explained. “It won’t matter if we wait till morning. She will still be able to function where she is located.” “My father will be safe for tonight Paul. She knows that we are both going to be coming for her. She needs to sleep sometime. I will know when she does and that is when we will plot our plan of attack.” Delylah told him. She knew that her sister wouldn’t make things easy on them. Cleopatra wasn’t dumb enough to leave herself vulnerable. Delylah and Paul sat for a little while and made the last minute plans that were necessary. They would plot their plan of attack in the morning when she knew her sister would be asleep. That would give them enough time to make sure that someone, anyone would be available to be with her mother. She didn’t think that Cleo would stoop as low as to take her mother from under her nose but at this point in the game she wouldn’t put anything past her. Paul and Delylah slipped from her bedroom and headed down to the kitchen area for a small snack before dinner. Delylah would speak to her mother and make sure that she would be with someone at all times tomorrow. If need be her and Paul would take turns and stand watch over her for tonight. After finishing their snack Delylah found it weird that her mother hadn’t came to check on them. “Paul... how did you get in the house?” Delylah asked quizzically. “The maid let me in as she was on her way out for the day.” Paul said as he took the last sip of his bottle of water. “How did you know I was upstairs?” Delylah asked panicky. “The maid told me she thought you were taking a nap as well as your mother. She told me how to find your room and I came straight upstairs to see you.” Paul knitted his eyebrows together. “What’s wrong Del?” Delylah jumped up from the table and headed straight for her mother’s bedroom. She scaled the stairs two at a time. When she reached the top of the stairs Paul was right behind her. She made a quick right and headed toward the first door as she reached the door a wave of nausea came over her and she clutched her stomach. She flung the door open and dropped to her knees. The oak canopy bed was empty just as she feared it would be. Paul rushed past her and pounded on the bathroom door. There was no reply. Cleopatra had done the one thing that Delylah didn’t think she would do. Her mother was gone. Paul helped Delylah to her feet and handed her the small piece of paper he found on the floor beside the bed. Delylah read it aloud through her tears, “Now I have them both sister. Not something you expected. I know what you’re thinking even while your awake. Never underestimate me sister. Love, Cleo.” Delylah screamed and tore up the piece of paper. Now what was she to do? How was she supposed to take her by surprise? They would have to wing it and hope for the best. They had to save her parents. They had to put an end to Cleopatra’s destruction and they had to do it now. Paul would not tell her where Cleo was for fear that she would leave with out him. They had to leave now there was no time to waste. Paul had packed a bag full of things for the trip and they were on there way. They had no clue what they were going to walk into but Delylah knew it wouldn’t be pretty. She had more drive now than ever to kill the monster that was once her sister.
