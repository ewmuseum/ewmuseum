Back in Boston

30 June 2005

Date: June 30th, 2005 “Forty-seven?”.Forty-eiiight?..foooorty-niiiine?.ffffiiiifffftttyyy?..” (Steel collides with steel as the press is lowered back to its frames. I sit up sweating profusely as torrential downpours drip down my face and splash upon the cement below me. I reach for my towel that lies below me and wipe my face. I stand up and pack up my carry bag when suddenly a very familiar voice starts from behind me.) Ronnie- “So, Mr. Hardy how does it feel to be home?” (I turn to see my best friend and words can’t come across my defined vocabulary. All I can do is hug him at that very moment.) Ronnie- “So’you’re at the gym”. that has and will always be?. two miles from my house and you can’t say hi?” “I’ve been thinking so hard about my match coming up. It’s hard to get my head straight.” Ronnie- “You talking about Rich and the Iron Man Match?” “Yeah’I mean I am so ready to step in that ring, but I’m not sure how prepared he will be. You know?” Ronnie- “Yeah’I understand you know.” “Yeah, but what are you doing nowadays” You working, have a girlfriend or two?” Ronnie- “Well I’m making about fifteen bucks an hour over at Sherwin Williams and I’m about to move up to management.” “Not bad”.” Ronnie- “I had a nice little Italian girl too. She’s like five foot two and she’s got that dark Italian tan skin tone. She’s gorgeous and she’s got BOMBS too! She’s SLAMMIN”! “So what happened to you guys?” Ronnie- “She was in a long relationship before ours and realized she missed being with him. So I let her go.” “That’s horrible I’m sorry man.” Ronnie- “Don’t sweat it. I can do better.” (I pat him on the back as I pick up my bag and walk to the exit door with Ronnie.) (As we left the bar, I felt as though I were right back where I wanted to be. Back home with my friends. I mean being on the road you meet some people who are nice and all, but no one can compare to the friends you’ve always had. Ronnie and I began our walk down memory lane. And honestly, It’s something I really needed. Help me get my mind off business.) “Do you remember Mrs. Monroe?” Ronnie- “HAHA! Old Mrs. Monroe”.She was like in her Nineties!” “Remember we use to shovel my driveway and then shovel hers. Once we finished shed open her window and pass of old rotten fruit through the window! Haha!” Ronnie- “Poor lady was senile!” “But the better part was when we would go back in my house and my Mom would have her awesome fudge waiting for us!” Ronnie- “Oh I know! How is your mom’Well’Not your mom”.but your’Grandmother?” “Nah I know what you mean”.She’s doing alright. She’s had a couple of minor heart attacks. But she’s a soldier’she’s pushed right through them and fights on.” Ronnie- “I’m sorry to here about the heart attack but I’m glad she’s ok.” (A silence comes over us until Ronnie bursts out with another memory.) Ronnie- “Yo’Remember the stink pit!” “Yeah I do! Peter Orcil use to always go in there and like sit down!” Ronnie- “What was wrong with that kid”!” “Actually I think he was autistic. Right?” Ronnie- “Oh yeah”.That’s right.” Ronnie- “Remember when you were standing on my garage roof hitting rocks with a baseball bat and you hit the huge apartment complex and broke a window and the cops came and wrote you up for that.” “Well you’re the dumbass who pointed out the broken window to them ass!” Ronnie- “No I wasn’t!” “Dude are you serious! The cop took Marc, yourself, and me over to the apartment complex and searched for the window. The whole time it was right above us. He didn’t end up seeing it and then you pointed it out to him! You jackass!” Ronnie- “Haha oh yeah I did didn’t I! HAHA I’m such a dick. Which makes me wonder something” Why didn’t you try to become a baseball player’You were really good at that! You were the only one to make the All-Stars on your varsity team!” “I tried, but mostly gave up. I figured it wasn’t truly for me.” (Baseball was my life as a kid. It was all I wanted to do. But now, I’ve found the true me’And baseball will never cross my mind again.) “Hey haha! Remember when the tree was blocking your basketball hoop and we were breaking off all the branches we could reach.” Ronnie- “And I cracked a 2x4 over your head full of nails and cracked your head open. Yeah I remember that.” “Dude this is making me re-think why I stayed friends with you’Hmm.” Ronnie- “Cause we are like brothers?” “Yeah we pretty much are.” (It was so great to be home. It eased a lot of tension in my life. It made me feel like I would never be forgotten. That no matter what’I always had a home in Massachusetts. It got my mind off my match tonight, and it showed me that wrestling is not something worth stressing over. I would either come out victorious or with the loss. But no matter what’Back at home, I was always victorious. My confidence is complete, and tonight’Tonight, will be my complete evolution.)
