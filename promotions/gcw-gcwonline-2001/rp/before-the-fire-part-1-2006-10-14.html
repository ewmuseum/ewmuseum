Before The Fire (Part 1)

14 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14.06 Cutting back the throttle on her custom Ninja, Dawn eased the motorcycle into a narrow parking space and snapped the kickstand down. Pulling the helmet off she shook her strawberry blonde hair out, trying to rid it of the sweat of the road. Swinging one long leg over the bike she dismounted smoothly, stretching aching muscles. 'Man I really need to buy a car.' Grumbling softly she moved to the saddle bags, unclipping the one containing her ring gear. On the other side of the parking lot turned social haven for both wrestlers and fans alike, stood the woman the beautiful blonde, Dawn McFarlane, would be facing later that evening, Tessa Windsor. Much like her fellow female colleague, Tessa felt a bit disheveled, having absolutely no clue how she was going to pull off one of the biggest matches in her career later this evening, with so many other things do. Still the five foot four beauty tossed her keys into the yellow coach bag she kept glued to her shoulder, and offered one bit of advice to herself, “Just breathe, you’re overreacting yet again.” Dawn was offering herself the same advice as she fell into step towards the arena, her thoughts whirling around her head and threatening to choke off everything else. She offered weak smiles and waves to the assembled fans and obsesses, never having been terribly comfortable around rapid fans of the sport. Falling into step beside a much smaller woman she flashed a shy smile. 'It's like a zoo out here eh? Not used to this kind of crowd.' “Tell me about it, I’ve never seen this many people before. Not even for GTT” The woman offered up in agreement, as she lead the way into the arena which had even less space to offer, considering it had been where the press set itself up. “Is this your first Primetime event?” Dawn nodded nervously, her blonde hair falling around her fair face. 'Yeah...I'm really nervous about this. Might have to pay a little homage to the porcelain god before I go out there.' She offered up a wry smile. 'Sorry, that's a little TMI.' “Don’t worry about it.” The pint sized brunette chuckled in sheer amusement. “Believe me, we aren’t all the prissy little things they make us out to be. Though I can openly I wish I had the courage to be that honest.” 'Hey you've got the courage to be here right?' Dawn punched her gently in the shoulder to make her point. 'You've got to have some courage to brave a legion of fans looking for up skirt photos and blood thirsty press. Speaking of which...' Dawn ducked her head and squared her shoulders, pushing past eager reporters looking to steal a phrase they could misuse from people new to the sport. “Well you’ve got a point there?” Falling a few steps behind Dawn, the smaller woman had to fight a bit harder to squeeze through the line of vulture like creatures, especially considering the size of the heels she’d decided to wear that day, ah how she never learned from her mistakes. Shoving her way through the assembled mass, Dawn took point for her petite new friend until they had managed to push past the assembled vultures. 'I don't think I could ever get used to this kind of think.' “It’s not so bad once you get used to it.” It sounded a bit awkward coming from a woman nearly eaten alive by the press on several occasions, but then again, after awhile it almost became a daily routine. “You’ve just gotta make sure you don’t run into the wrong people.” Dawn couldn't help but flash a small smile. 'Sounds like wrestling as a whole eh? Got to make sure to steer clear of the wrong people.' “That’s for sure!” Her strong sentiment echoed throughout the crowded hallway, thankfully the multitude of stars still yet to arrive kept both press and fans alike from looking back their way. Once they were away from the larger crowds and could speak more quietly Dawn looked over her shoulder at her new found friend. 'So are you live tonight then?' “Yeah, but not till later.” Not that it gave the young woman much less to worry about, but still she tried to stay optimistic, “Hopefully getting here early will give me plenty of time to shake the nerves.” 'I'm not on until later either, but I won't be able to shake my nerves until my music hits. I need adrenalin to clear out my nervousness, I'm such an underdog here.' Dawn smiled nervously, almost wringing her hands “Eh, I’m sure you’ll do fine.” While the two might have just met, it only seemed like the right thing to say, especially considering she had been in a similar spot at one time and still managed to shock the world. 'Thanks.' She smiled in return. 'I'm nervous but I think I have an okay chance.' She walked a few more steps before stopping in realization. 'Oh my god, I forgot to introduce myself.' She extended one hand. 'I'm Dawn, pleased to meet you.' “Dawn?” The stranger questioned, perking up one of her darkly painted eyebrows. “As in Dawn McFarlane?” 'Yeah that's me.' Dawn looked pleased, a little pleasantly surprised that her name was known by a bigger star. 'I'm flattered you know of me.' The surprise was equally felt by the stranger in her presence, who finally balled up the nerves to introduce herself. “I’m Tessa”.Windsor?.your opponent tonight?.” 'Oh.' All the wind went out of Dawn's sails and she was left staring at her outstretched hand, feeling a little like she'd been caught with her hand in the proverbial cookie jar. 'Uhm...pleased to meet you.' The tension, while not negative, was certainly enough to keep Tessa quiet for a moment, as the contemplated the right thing to say, “Nice to meet you too.” 'Uhm...wow...this is awkward.' Dawn offered a more genuine smile, trying to break the tension. “Yeah, you could definitely say that.” Letting a chuckle fall from her strawberry colored lips, she also offered her own ways of trying to break the tension, by offering up this one piece advice. “But don’t worry about me, believe me I’m harmless.” 'DOn't sell yourself short hun.' Came the fast reply. 'You're great, I've followed your stuff since I found out I was facing you. You're an inspiration to someone like me, coming up from the indys.' It was hard not to gush really, but Dawn preferred that to the uncomfortable tension. “Eh, I prefer to think of it as just luck.” While she hated to brush off her talent, she also hated to sound like all the egomaniacs she had been brought up around in the business. “But either way, I’m looking forwards to getting into the ring with you. I’m sure we’ll put on a great one.” 'Definitely.' Dawn gave her the thumbs up, flashing a rare grin. 'Us and...whoever that other guy is.' “Don’t worry about him, I’m sure we’ll steal the show.” Patting her in the back in an assuring matter, her conversation with her new friend slash opponent, helped provide a solution to her biggest problem, the match. Now at least she knew if she lost, it would be to someone well deserving, not just someone who’d take the match as some kind of bragging right. 'Oh I don't doubt it.' Came Dawn's reply, followed by a light and reassuring touch on Tessa's upper arm. 'I've got to go get ready, get warmed up. I guess I'll see you in the ring?' A warm smile is all she had to offer to the genuine Dawn McFarlane, as she too started to tip toe her way towards her own locker room, “You sure will.”
