Depressed “R” Us

23 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/23/08 START 2 pm The fluorescent lighting radiates through the aisles of everyone’s favorite toy shop; Toys “R” Us. Remember that feeling when you were a child? The feeling of walking into the store and being bombarded by dazzling colors, sharp toys, clever games, and seemingly endless amounts of fun. For the old, those were the days? for the young, those days are being had. For the Fox? those days just don’t feel the same anymore. It’s a rather slow Wednesday afternoon as a few families swing throughout the store. You know it’s pretty well dead when you can hear the buzzing of the lights high above you. Two kids come running out from the video game section, laughing and playing until they stumble into the main toy aisle for boys. They stop immediately in awe at the sight of all the sparkling toys. Everything from Sponge Bob Frisbees, Wall-E movie tie in harmonicas, Easy bake ovens (male edition) to Transformer action figures, Yu-Gi-Oh! figurines, and even GCW wrestling buddies? this toy aisle had it all. One of the kids walks over and picks out a super cool Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle action figure. He can barely contain himself as he bursts out in excitement! Orange Shirt Kid: WHOOOOOOA! Check this out! He’s got grappling action! Blue Shirt Kid: Do they have Raf? He’s my favorite! The kid in the orange shirt hangs the toy back up and scratches his head vigorously while the blue shirted kid starts to peek through the line of TMNT toys available. Orange Shirt Kid: I don’t know! I just picked this first one I saw. Wonder if they have the new Iron Man toys? The kid in the blue shirt pulls out a Raf version of the toy. Blue Shirt Kid: This is like the best day of my life! Go see if they have Iron Man! I want to see if they got Leonardo! The orange shirted kid departs, leaving his friend to rummage through the toys. At first the kid in orange heads down the aisle in an all out sprint but as his stamina quickly fades, his run turns into a slow wander. The kid reaches the end of the aisle without finding an Iron Man action figure. He rubs his head as he ponders what to do next. Orange Shirt Kid: Oh? I KNOW! The kid turns and yells to his buddy at the other end. Orange Shirt Kid: I’m going to check the next aisle! Make sure my Mom knows that! The kid in blue nods his head in acceptance as he is now heavily surrounded by TMNT toys. Leaving his friend behind, the kid in orange walks over to the next aisle. But to his dissatisfaction, he enters into the girl’s toy aisle! After the initial shock wears off, the frightened boy ventures deeper into the aisle, joining the only other person already standing in it, engaged with a product. This other person was an adult no doubt. The kid looked at the man for a brief moment before realizing how vibrant all the pink toys around him were. Everything was there? all the toys you could imagine; Hello Kitty, Easy bake oven deluxe editions, Polly Pocket, and the most preferred girls toy; Barbie. Orange Shirt Kid: Wow? girl’s toys rule! But? that’s odd. The man standing there is holding a package of "Street Safe Barbie." The kid tries to get closer to all the pretty pinkness but before he can, he accidentally bumps into the man. The kid is sent tumbling backwards as the man twitches in shock. Orange Shirt Kid: I’m sorry! I’m sorry! Please don’t hurt me! The man steps more into the light as his face is completely revealed. It’s our lovable hero Clyde Fox! With depressed look in tow, Fox slowly looks over at the kid, and then glances back to Street Safe Barbie. His lips quiver as he tries to put together words. Fox: You know? I used to love coming here. Fox nods his head up and down as he bites his bottom lip. Fox: They just don’t do it for me anymore. The kid gets to his feet and cleans off his hands on his jeans. He gets a little closer to Clyde before engaging in conversation. Orange Shirt Kid: Are you okay mister? Sorry I scared you like that? you kinda scared me too. Fox pulls the Barbie package to his side? then behind his back as his eyes lock onto the child in some weird serial killer type of way. Fox: That’s okay Timmy. You? you like girls toys too? The orange shirt boy clasps his hands together as a sense of fright can be heard in his voice. Orange Shirt Kid: My name? isn’t T-Timmy. And yeah? I like the pretty colors. Fox looks upwards to the ceiling. He swallows and then looks back down at the kid with an even blanker stare than before. Fox: So why is Street Safe Barbie so safe? I mean really. Look at her. Fox places the doll box in front of him for the kid to see. He then twists his head from side to side and continues. Fox: What’s so safe about how she dresses? She’s wearing a hand cut, red square wool shirt with cut off blue bum shorts made out of jean material. Not to mention that her hair looks like it’s full of product. She looks like she lives in a trailer. She looks like she is a drug user. She looks like she isn’t Street Safe Barbie? she’s Depressed Druggy Barbie! By the time Fox finishes his sentence, he realizes that the kids face is pasted to the front of the package. He pulls it back nervously and begins to tap his fingers on it. The kid almost begins to cry as he is frozen in fear. Orange Shirt Kid: Are y-you o-o-okay mister? Fox stands there, unblinking and unresponsive. He doesn’t even twitch. It’s as if he had become a wax sculpture. Orange Shirt Kid: Mister? Just then the blue shirted boy along with a woman walks by the end of the aisle. They stop as the woman calls the boy. Mother: Brandon! Oh there you are! Are you alright? Come on, we’re leaving! Who is with you? The woman walks down the aisle and sees that her boy is frightened. She then looks at Clyde in a confused state. Mother: Hello? Brandon? were you talking to this man? The boy nods his head emphatically as the mother peers into Fox’s empty eyes. Mother: Is everything alright? No response. Fox’s nostrils flair ever so slightly. The mother then turns to her son and friend. Mother: C’mon Brandon, Dillon? let’s get out of here. This guy gives me the creeps. Just as they go to leave the mother turns to Fox and says one last thing. Mother: I don’t know what’s wrong with you, but stay away from my child, okay!” This is Canada; people don’t do that kind of shit here. And with that, the mother makes sure that Brandon wasn’t touched in any bad way (like a good mother would) and they went on their way. Right after they vanish out of sight, Fox awakens. He tilts his head down and blinks rapidly. He sighs very depressingly. He opens his mouth? Fox: He stands there with it open waiting for the opportunity to talk. Fox: There’s no one else around. He can say whatever he wants. He’s got ample time to say anything from a stupid joke to a serious comment. Fox: He sticks his fingers in his mouth momentarily just to make sure his mouth is open. He pulls out his saliva covered fingers. He looks at his hand? it jitters uncontrollably as the clear liquid runs down to his palm like a cascading ocean wave. There were words in his mouth but nothing to push them out. Not his motivation? not his rage? not even the healing spirit of buying a little girl’s toy could do it. Fox: His depression was holding it all in. For those few moments that he could talk; he knew it wasn’t really him. It all changed him. The fire changed him. Tyson DeBough changed him. Fox is changed. He closes his mouth as he softly weeps. He rubs the back of his neck with his free hand as the other clutches the Barbie. Did she say Canada? I don’t even know where I am. Who? am I? Why must it all hurt so much? I can fix this? I can’t fix this. Words continue to pile up on top of each other as Fox’s mind tries to juggle it all. It’s your fault! It’s my fault! It’s our fault! You're a child! No one likes you! You should just fuck off! ?: Sir? Sir? Are you alright? The sudden burst of light sears Fox’s eyes. He shakes his head as he tries to get his bearings. Fox finds himself lying on the ground, covered in saliva and puke. A good looking teenage guy stands over Fox with a concerned look on his face. Employee: It’s okay sir. I saw you convulsing on the ground and I came running over. Are you alright? Fox shakes his head in a yes notion. He then pulls himself up to a sitting position. He huffs radically as he wipes various liquids from his forehead. The teenage employee goes to put back the Barbie doll but Fox stops him briefly. Employee: You want this sir? Yyyyyyyyoooooouuuuuuu wwwwwaaaaannnnnnttttt ttttthhhhiiiiiisssss sssssiiiiirrrr?””” Fox: ? No. Fox lets go of the employee’s arm. The employee then proceeds to place the doll back on the shelf. Employee: Anything else I can do for you? Like call the hospital? What happened? Did I just have a panic attack? I feel? different. Fox pulls his groggy self back to his feet and begins to walk away confidently. Fox: No. As he walks past all the items he’s cherished for so long and holds so close to his heart, he could only notice how his heart kept on going, even without those things near it. He was motivated by what’s inside it. My heart won’t stop beating. You can strip me of everything. You can take my toys, friends, candy, fun, spirit, soul, self, life, and everything ever about me? but I Will Survive. Fox walks out of the Toys “R” Us and looks around. The country that he calls home. And he knows he must defend it against all evil doers. You may be able to take the Fox down? but he’ll be there to build himself back up? big time. Fox begins to walk down the street before stopping in front of a homeless person. Fox pulls out some spare change and tosses it in the man’s cup. Fox then smiles? Fox: My name is Clyde Fox? nice to meet you. END
