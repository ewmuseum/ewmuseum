1. A bed meant for two

11 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/09 I had that dream again... You know, the one where... Ah, never mind. It's not really worth going into it. I get up and go over to the radio, the first thing I do every time I wake up alone, and flip on the mp3. The opening chords of "The Red" by Chevelle blast throughout the speakers arranged in my apartment. "They say freak, When your singled out..." Something about this song just really does it for me in the morning. All I think of is the octagon, the cage, the fans. All eyes on me and my opponent, my my opponent will only be an after thought after I'm done with him. Give me three minutes, just three and I will contort his body in ways contortionists on the Las Vegas Showboat stage would cringe and three faint taps on my leg, arm, or torso will end it all. I will stand, I will rise, I will be victorious.... But that's not the way, the reason, anymore. It's not about the cage, the ring girls, and the blood and violence all ending in short rounds under the command of a ring clock. No, now it is a show. Entertainment. No longer will I be going for submission of the night, knockout of the night, or fight of the night. Now, it is about the show. The long lasting, display of skill and charisma. ...not something I'm accustomed to... See, I know what you're thinking. Brock Lesnar wannabe. Well, if that's what you're thinking, you're wrong. I envy him, don't get me wrong, but we are two different people with a very different agenda. See, I'm not leaving the WEC. I'm not giving up my first career of being a warrior of the ring No, I'm staying in the cage. I'm just stretching my legs a bit. I will still be an active member of the WEC, but I'm moving into GCW. I want to entertain, not just get paid. Lesnar is a legend. He took wrestling to new heights, and then he crossed over into the UFC because he wanted to prove he is a true king of the ring. He wanted to show the world that he's not just some asshole in tights that can throw someone around a ring without the intention of hurting him, but with full intention of pleasing the crowd. Lesnar is an animal, and animals deserve to be kept in cages. Lesnar took the UFC Heavyweight Title with ease, and he's not giving it up. I've been doing this for a while, MMA that is, and I've always been intrigued by the wrestling fan base. I want to show the world that I'm not just a mindless drone that inflicts pain for pleasure. I'm the opposite of Lesnar. I want to show the world the other side, yeah, but the side that Brock started his career with. Enough of the six foot three, two-hundred and sixty-five behemoth... "Something's gone terribly wrong with everyone; All the world is mad. Darkness bring terrible things; the sun gone- what vanity! our sad, wretched fires." Isn't that the damn truth. Thrice really hits it on point here. I've done some pretty terrible things, it comes with the territory of being an MMA fighter, really. You talk shit, you lay threats on the tables to help promote fights, but sometimes, the only way to dull the pain of a loss, the broken bones of a hand or rib, is alcohol. The shit is terrible for anyone with pent up aggression, not to mention those that know how to fight.... "We can't medicate man to perfection again; We can't legislate peace in our hearts..." The hell we can't... We all have our reasons, and some have different ones, but mine are for the right ones... One half Deltasone pill... One part Tylenol.... Two parts Vitamin and Mineral... Maybe we can't medicate to perfection again, to the state in which we were born, but nobody is perfect, not even when they are born. The Deltasone is for arthritis of sense, but mostly because I've been fighting all through my growing existence in this fucking life. Everyday it is pain, but I mostly don't notice it. Pain is what you make of it. Pain is an everyday thing. Pain is something that can be put away with medication and patience. Patience, you ask? Well, when you are as fucked up as I am, when everything hurts, you learn to live with it. Eventually, your brain registers it and your nerves build a tolerance to it. Eventually, the pain is a minor annoyance that you put to the back of your mind while you go on with your everyday agenda. I take Deltasone, but a really low dosage. It takes out some of the swelling to manage the pain to a workable level. I only take it a week after and a week before a fight. Now that I'm wrestling, who knows when I'll need it. Eat... Five egg whites, one whole egg, a cup of oatmeal, a glass of OJ, a shaker of Mass Gaining protein... Pre-game... Documents, documents, documents. I never knew that signing up with GCW would be so much homework. Read this, do that, make sure you meet here, make sure you smile there. Dot the I's and cross the T's. Then there is that oh yeah moment when you look at the daily planner when you forgot it is Jeff's birthday or you forgot that you were supposed to be up three hours earlier because you were supposed to meet with a wrestling coach three hours away.... Fuck it.... Workout... Weights, cardio, weights, cardio, sit-ups, sit-downs, push-ups, push-downs. Punch, kick, slap... Eat... Ten ounces of chicken, a gallon of water, steamed broccoli, a cup of undercooked brown rice... ..... Have you ever looked at your life in a book? No, not an autobiography or biography...I always forget which one you write and which one someone else writes... Anyway, I mean when you look at a fucking piece of paper in an agenda book and have your whole day planned out hour by hour, calorie by calorie, and if you deviate from the agenda, you are no longer disciplined... I am my own worst enemy when it comes to the daily agenda because if I feel good, I work out longer. If I feel shitty, I push through it, even if I don't think I can do it anymore, but I push through. I get done, shower, and relax, even if the agenda tells me I need to do this or that... Sometimes one bullet point in an agenda must be sacrificed for the good of another... As the twilight of the still darkness sets in around my apartment, I turn to my bed. A bed really is a sanctuary. Not only is it a place of comfort, but also security. Some of the most intimate moments happen in your bed/bedroom. No, not just sex, but the intimate moments of your mind. You are alone, locked away, stowed away from the threats of the world, where only your mind is left. Again, I'm my own worst enemy. When left alone, alone to my thoughts, I can truly do the most damage to myself. I would take a beating in the cage or an embarrassment in my debut match at GCW over being left alone to myself at night. My mind runs. All I do is think. I very rarely fall right to sleep when my head hits the pillow. Shit, it's usually a fucking process to get to the pillow. Something about sitting/laying on your bed with your eyes closed... It's so... ...self-mutilating... Forgive my use of words, I don't mean actual mutilation, but of the mind, or being? absolutely. I don't just lay there and think how amazing I am, or how great I did in the match. I'm not a very vain person for an MMA fighter. No, I think about that moment in high school where I should have asked out Kate or that moment in a pathetic excuse for a college career where I should have taken something that may have benefited me in the future. Or I will think of her... Yeah, there is always her. The one, the one that got away, the one that changed your life... No matter how you put it, it is still Her... You know that girl that you see in pictures that is just, amazing. Her smile makes you smile, her eyes inviting you into her world. The very presence of her in the picture makes the picture the most amazing thing you've ever seen... Well, that was her, that was "the one" but that's not how it started. Well, yeah, it started that way, but it was complicated. I saw her around town, but never said a word. I can step into a ring with someone that wants to kill me and show the world how loud I can squeal in a submission, but I can't walk up to a fucking girl reading a book in Barnes and Noble, sitting alone at the bar while she waits for drinks, or standing in line at the local super market... No, I'm not a pussy, it's just... well, lets get back to the picture... Ever see a picture of someone, and think how happy they look and how amazing they are, but then you flip to the next picture when they are in the background, not knowing the picture is being taken? That one is something real. You see the person for who they really are, unless they are faking something else for another picture or the sloppy fat guy that thinks he's hot shit in front of all of his friends but they only come around because he has money and supplies the party spot and alcohol... No, the picture where she's pouring her own drink or turning away from someone after a fake laugh at something that wasn't any more funny than a political joke you didn't get on late night tv. That image, the one where she is pushing her hair over her ear and looking to the side. Her eyes tell a much different story than the one previous when she is with three of her girlfriends... Happy? Not quite.. The smile disappears from her eyes and you see loneliness, money problems, loss, etc. Yeah, maybe I'm a creep, but I'm trained to pay attention to detail, and this is something that caught my attention, and I was forever in love. I wanted to be that guy that she wanted. I wanted to be what her eyes were missing. I wanted to show her that there are other moments to be truly happy besides the pictures you take with three friends you probably won't even be in contact with in three more years. I wanted to be that guy... Another day has gone pass and I still haven't said a word to her... How fucking pathetic... Another night alone in my bed for two... Am I afraid? Maybe I'm not ready for the step into GCW. Maybe I'm making a fucking mistake? Everyone thinks they should tell me this. Everyone tells me that what I am doing is career suicide. I'm told I won't ever be allowed back into the cage. I beg the differ, but what do I know. My debut match is days away so anything can happen. Anyway... ....I had that dream again.... Do you really give a shit? Well, let me give you access to my head for a minute... There is a blur of different things going on. Banging this chick or fighting that guy. I really don't remember, but then it happens. A girl, a fucking girl with a cross dangling from her neck. A wooden cross, like one of those things you see in church or a movie of a girl from church. Well, something happens. We are talking, and I'm not the only one in my bedroom, but she is there and I'm joking around with someone. Then... I look over... The girl isn't the same anymore. She's all possessed and fucking weird. She turns into a real pale looking bitch that is screaming, but the scream isn't like a rape scream, but more of a painful, depths of hell type scream and she slowly makes her way over towards me. She's not crawling, or walking, but it is something in between. The movements aren't natural. She is moving with this wretched stutter and forcing her legs down into the ground with such power, but then freezes while her upper body catches up.. Now I'm alone. I know that I'm alone, and you know how? I know that I'm sleeping... It's me laying there in bed, staring over at my bedroom door where this possessed bitch is, but I know that it's a dream, or should I say that I hope it's a ream. Either way, I struggle to wake up. I smack myself, hit myself, yell and try to convince myself that I'm awake. The more I try to wake up, the harder it is to keep my eyes open. I struggle to keep them open, trying to keep a fixed position on where the crazed bitch is. Finally, I decide to clap. I'm clapping really fucking loud and yelling to myself. My eyes are barely open. I'm looking through fucking slits. I take long blinks like right before you fall asleep and when I reopen them to myself clapping and yelling, I see her closer and closer. Then... I'm awake! It's totally fucking dark and I'm in a cold sweat looking back and forth for the fucking girl, but she's not there. Nobody is there. But I knew I was sleeping. I look at my hands in the moonlight and they don't look anything different, but they sting like I was smacking them really fucking hard. After all that, after my panic subsides...I take a deep breath and try to fall back asleep, eventually succeeding. And here I am, telling you the fucking dream. What is the significance? No idea, but I keep on having it. Usually it doesn't go down just like that, but you get the idea. I know that I'm alive, I know that I'm dreaming, but I can't get myself awake, and then when I am, I don't want to fall back asleep. We're talking about a total mind fuck here. Either way, the dream weighs down on my mind all day and sometimes days at a time if I don't have the dream every night. I think it's a metaphor or something for this road in my life. Not the crazy fucking girl, but the whole sleep and not asleep thing. I'm trying so hard to change things in my life, making a career change in a sense, and all I hit is road blocks and people that tell me this or that. My fear of the girl is a fear of disappointment or failure. I keep trying to work it out and everything, but all I see is that fucking failure. Even thought I try to put myself back on track (waking up) no matter what I do, failure stares me right in the face. The only thing I'm scared of in life is fucking failure. My father drilled that into my head until the day he died. I stay in this fucking apartment more than I stay in his God damn house because I'm afraid to face my failures. The house is a failure to me. I've been on my own so long, going back to where I grew up makes me feel like I did something wrong and need to go back. Well, not yet. Until then, I'm just going to work for that girl in the picture and sleep in my bed for two.... ...because... I won't accept failure...
