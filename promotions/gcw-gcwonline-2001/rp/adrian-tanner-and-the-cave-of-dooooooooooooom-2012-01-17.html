Adrian Tanner And The Cave Of DOOOOOOOOOOOOOM

17 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/17/12 [The scene opens up to a nice two-story house in the usually-sunny town of Tucson, Arizona. In a change of sights from the norm, it is actually overcast out, the grey skies leading to a slim-but-still-possible chance of rain. After a few passes around the outside area, the camera changes to show inside the house, in the large living room on the first floor of the house. Various photos of the men and women who live there and/or have lived there in the past line the walls, a large big screen tv sits on a black entertainment center in one corner while on the other side of the walls sits a couch and two recliner chairs. Sitting in one of the chairs with the recliner popped back and his feet on the table in front of him is a young man in his early-mid twenties reading a newspaper. Another figure, a hispanic man with long curly black hair steps into the shot carrying a bunch of envelopes.] Adrian Tanner Jr: Well Brandon, another new fed another multi-man battle royal for me to win, eh? [Adrian Tanner Jr, otherwise known as the Arizona Assassin, chuckles a bit in greeting to his best friend Brandon as Brandon enters the shot. Brandon doesn't respond right away, setting the mail on the table while glancing through it before finally looking up at Adrian.] Brandon Young: Ah, well, actually... Adrian: What? [The Young Gun rubs the back of his neck slightly as Adrian quirks a brow.] Adrian: What is it..? Brandon: Well, you're not in a battle royal. Adrian: I'm not? Brandon: Nope. Adrian: Ahh, the ol' multi-man one fall-to-a-finish match then? Brandon: ...Nope. [Adrian cants his head to the side, staring at his friend.] Adrian: No? Then what is it? Brandon: It's a singles match. [Adrian's eyebrows raise as he sets the newspaper down, reaching for a bottle of water on the table instead.] Adrian: For real? An actual single match to start out a new company with? I didn't think those existed anymo- Brandon: Against Brad Jackson. [Adrian spittakes, water flying all over the table.] Adrian: WHAT? Brandon: You heard me. [Adrian's face goes from shock, to thoughtful, to an outright grin as he hops to his feet.] Adrian: Come on Bacon, we've no time to waste! To the Assassin Cave! Brandon: ..wut? Adrian: I've.. got.. a plan! [And with that, the Arizona Assassin is off! Brandon stares after his friend for a long moment before shaking his head.] Brandon: Oh dear... --------------------------------------------------------------------- "Brad Jackson." [Cut to Adrian, dilligently working away inside of a large cave which is decorated by giant replicas of his fallen foes. A ridiculously huge gold necklace with the initials "JS" in blinged out letters on it hangs on a Museum stand behind him, along with the remains of a giant pokeball. What? You thought he was joking? Perish the thought. Adrian, wearing black pants, a black shirt with a gold S shield on it and a pair of goggles over his eyes to keep the sparks out fiddles away with something in front of him. Judging by the sweat coming off of him it's obvious he's been at this for awhile. A voice rings out behind him but he pays it no mind.] Brandon: Adrian. [Still working away, he either doesn't hear his best friend or just doesn't care. Either way, the Young Gun's going to have to do better than that, so he moves closer.] Brandon: Adrian! [That gets his attention, and he finally turns the torch off long enough to turn and acknowledge his friend. With the sparks still shooting up and the goggles on his head he looks more like some kind of evil supervillain than any sort of Batman-themed hero like he was probably hoping to come off as, but he doesn't care.] Adrian: ..You bellowed? Brandon: ..The hell are you doing? Adrian: I do believe my good chum, that I am putting into motion Operation: Omega. [Brandon quirks a brow.] Brandon: What? Adrian: I'm rebuilding Soundy. Brandon: WHAT? Adrian: You heard me. Brandon: You... what? Have you LOST YOUR MIND? That thing almost murdered Cecil! On like a thousand different occasions! Adrian: Oh that, pssh. I fixed that. Brandon: How can you be so sure? [Adrian ponders that for a moment.] Adrian: Okay, so I didn't, but eh.. Cecil's not around much anymore anyways, right? Brandon: ...THAT's your justification? [Brandon just STARES at his friend.] Brandon: What... exactly do you plan on doing with the murdering psychopath you're about to unleash on the world then? Adrian: ..What else? I'm going to use it to destroy my enemies and make the world mine. MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Brandon: ... Adrian: ... Brandon: ... [Both men stare at each other for a long moment.] Brandon: ... Adrian: ... Brandon: ... Adrian: ...Okay, I'm not going to do anything of the sort. Brandon: Thank GOD! What the hell, man? [Adrian sets the goggles down on the table.] Adrian: I dunno, I'm in a wrestling company where none of the wrestlers actually want to talk about THE WRESTLING. So what the hell right? I'm just going to do the most ridiculous things I can think of at the time and send them as promos, because, why not? I mean here I was thinking I was gonna face BRAD FRACKING JACKSON in a wrestling match, a battle of wits with one of the best around, finally a real test to my abilities, and yet the more I look around GCW's offices, watch their promos the more I realize.. I don't belong here. So, if I can't have a verbal spar with a guy like Jackson, a guy who I'd love nothing more than to have a real war with, then I'm just gonna do shit like this. [He shrugs.] Adrian: Jackson, you are a bit of an urban myth from what I've seen and heard. Oh I've heard your name uttered all over during my travels in the last year, but I've never actually seen you in action until I looked up a couple of the GCW shows. Gotta say, I liked what I saw, more than I thought I would, and I look forward to getting the chance to crush those myths between my boot heels, to prove the almighty Jackson isn't so much almighty as just.. mighty. My name is Adrian Tanner Jr. I am the Arizona Assassin. I'm not a Lifetime Movie Special like the rest of the roster. I'm a fricken WRESTLER. And I am very, very good at what I do. [He smirks.] Adrian: I like to think you and I are of like minds, so it's going to be an honor to step into that ring with a guy of your calibur, and I hope we light that place on fire. Just don't be.. tooooo shocked if the guy standing atop the ring with his hand held at the end is ME, not you, capiche? [Fade.]
