Date: 10/19/05 I left Cindy alone in the hotel room because I needed to get in a workout, plus she knows I like to workout alone. No distractions, just focus on getting my body into shape. I've been doing this for eight years now and I'm in what I consider to be in the best shape of my life. But I'm the TV champion and I have to wrestle each and every week to keep the belt around my waist, so I need to be in better than the best shape of my life. There's no Wylde World gyms in Phoenix, which kinda sucks, after all, Jeff is my business manager and I do have a lifetime VIP membership to any of his gyms anywhere in the world. So, like I do whenever I'm in a city devoid of his gym, I try and find something low key. I don't need all the flash and balls of a gym where everyone comes to workout so they can look good to come and workout. I don't need to be around people who spent more time picking their workout clothes than they do actually working out. I find the right place after a half hour of driving around. It's a small unassuming place with the simple, plain assumimg name of Brian's Gym and Fitness Center. It's all exposed, weathered brick and a plain, hand painted sign. No neon here. I walk in and the place is just what I expected. A bunch of guys and girls in t-shirts, sweats or shorts who are here to work out. Not to show off, but to get themselves into shape for whatever reasons they may have. There are several free weight stations, a few weight machines, and the usual assortment of workout stations and equipment that you'd expect to find in a gym. I'm already dressed in my workout clothes, a plain black sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of gray sweats. The only other things I brought with me are a towel and a water bottle. I move over to the bench press, set up 280 pounds and get to work. I'm barely ten minutes into my workout when I notice the guy watching me. My first thought is a fan, someone who notices me when I don't want to be noticed. It's a common problem. But I glance over at him and I realize it's not that. There's something else. He's looking at me like he recognizes me for another reason. And I recognize him as well. He smiles and walks over to me as I take a sip of my water. Then it hits me. The way he walks, the way he carries himself. Then I see the tattoos on his arms and it hits me all at once. 'Brian?' 'Hey Mike, I thought that was you.' We give each other a man hug, lots of pounding on backs and not holding it for too long. 'Damn man I haven't seen you since the IWO went belly up seven years ago.' he says. Brian Kelso, better known in his ring days as Killer Keslo. A homage if you will to the man that trained us both Killer Kowalski. Brian and I were in the same class together at the Killer's school many years ago. He and I had some vicious wars back in the IWO. I was managed by Cindy and Brian was managed by a real bitch of a woman named Simone, better known as the Angel Of Darkness. 'Has it really been that long?' I ask, realizing, yes, it has been that long. 'So what the hell have you been doing with yourself?' He makes a broad sweeping gesture around him. 'It ain't much, but it's mine.' Brian's Gym and Fitness Center. Of course. 'I opened it up a couple of years ago. After the IWO I worked for a couple of other companies before a nagging back injury forced me from the ring. Not that I was sad to leave the wrestling behind. Simone was becoming more and more like her character. I mean she was always bad, but she got a lot worse as time went on. Even Jimmy and Alex quit just to get away from her.' 'Damn,' I laughed as we walked off to the side out of the way of the other patrons and sat on some equipment that wasn't being used, 'I remember how bad she used to be. Everyone thought it was all an act until you got backstage and realized that was just the way she was.' 'Yeah, so what about you? What've you been up to?' 'Well after the IWO, Jeff, Mal, Cindy and I moved on to a place called the SWO...' I regaled him with the story of my career until I came around to GCW, '...and within a month I was the Television champion and I'm currently undefeated.' 'Nice.' Brian said, 'I wish I could still get in there and mix it up, but the way my back has been. Hell, I'm lucky I can bend down to tie my shoes in the morning. So you and Cindy, huh?' 'Yeah, it's funny. One day she was my manager and suddenly there was a change, I looked at her and realized, 'My God, how come I never noticed how beautiful she was before?', so I asked her if she'd be willing to quit as my manager and become my wife.' 'And she actually agreed? Did you slip her a roofie?' he laughed. I punched him in the arm. 'Nah, I just dropped six grand on a ring, hell if you'd seen the ring I got her, you probably would have married me!' We both got a good laugh out of that, and for a moment I could see the man I once faced in the ring. Brian and I had a four month feud for the IWO World Heavyweight title, that ended in a steel cage match at the Spring Fling PPV. 'So whatever happend to Jimmy and Alex?' I asked refering to his two partners in Simone's Wings Of Darkness. 'Last I heard Jimmy moved back to California and opened a surf shop, and Alex...well shit. Last I heard Alex moved back to Cali as well, but I don't know whatever became of him. We lost touch shortly after I quit wrestling for good. What about Jeff and Malcolm, you keep in touch with those two?' 'Yeah, in fact Jeff is my business manager.' I said, then I added in Jeff's gyms and his entertainment company. 'No shit.' Brian said, 'Well damn, looks like Jeff went big time. Parlayed his wrestling career into something bigger.' I nodded. 'Yeah, he wrestled for quite a while after the SWO shut down, and won major titles all over the place, but now he's married again, settled down and about to be a father for the second time. In fact, that's why he's retired from the ring, he'd like his second kid to know what he looks like while he can still walk without a great deal of pain.' 'To this day I don't know how we did it, or how you still do it.' Brian said sighing and glancing out the window to his right. 'What's that? Wrestle?' 'Not just wrestle. Work hurt. I mean you know as well as I do that every wrestler works hurt, there's never any time to recover, you go from show to show, week to week, aches and pains covering every inch of your body, some more prominent than others, but you manage to block it out and go on. Getting dropped on our backs, our heads, our necks. How in the hell did we do it?' 'Twenty thousand people in the stands every night screaming our names, or screaming for our heads.' I said, 'Millions more turning on their TV's every week hoping to see us kick ass or get our asses kicked. The money's nice too, don't get me wrong, I wouldn't do this shit for free. But the real buzz is when you step through that curtain and walk down that ramp and step into that ring and you've got twenty thousand people either screaming your name, or screaming for your opponent to tear you limb from limb. Even though the people hate me now, I love stepping onto that ramp and looking out over the arena and knowing that I'm partially responsible for filling those seats. Knowing that I'm partailly responisble for all those TV cameras beaming my face into millions of homes across the country. Knowing that some of those people are actually cheering for me to win, and tear the goody-goody in the ring into small bite sized chunks.' Brian looked back at me, looking every day of his thirty years. 'Yeah, that's why we did it.' he smiled. 'So,' he said clapping his hands and standing up, 'You need a spotter?' I stood up and took a swig of my water, 'Sure what the hell why not? Want some free tickets to the show tomorrow night? If I ask real nice I might even be able to secure you a ringside seat.' 'Sounds good to me.' 'Alrighty then,' I said, 'You help me work out, and I'll help you get in.'
