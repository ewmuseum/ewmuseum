Going back to the beginning...

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 June 05 The most annoying sound to wake up to has to be the telephone ringing. At first the sound seemed as if it were just a dream, a very annoying dream but a dream none the less. It took a few moments for the ringing to register but when it did I growled in the phone’s direction. I dug my head deeper into the pillow, trying to knock the sound from my ears but it was to no avail. I swung my fist down on the soft pillow, letting it sink in like vampire fangs to flesh before forcing my head up. Sliding my body over I could feel my muscles trying to get themselves together just like my senses were. I reached a shaky hand out and snatched the phone from its base. Slowly I pulled the phone to my ear, clearing my throat before speaking didn’t help my voice sound any less raspy. “Hello?” “Roberto?” The voice was very familiar, so familiar in fact that my eyes shot open from their seemingly drunken state. I forced my muscles to cooperate and help me sit up right. I hadn’t heard this voice since she popped up at my door step at the hotel in Europe. “Kari ? how are you doing?” She didn’t answer right away which immediately heightened my alertness. I have grown to understand people, more importantly the communication aspect of people. Her voice was soft almost as if she had been crying or was whispering so that someone else wouldn’t hear the conversation. “I don’t know Roberto. I, um ? need to talk to you?” “Well what do you need?” “It’s about Cindy, and your baby.” My body felt weak and my stomach began flipping and flopping inside of my body. I could feel my eyes begin to burn from the mix of tears and anger that were building and boiling. I could see my lip quiver a bit, but it was nothing I could control. This happened every time her name was mentioned. “Wh’What about her Kari?” “Listen Roberto there are things about her that you need to know, things that you should have known a long time ago.” “What are?” she cut me off quickly, her voice came back with an intensity that startled me a bit. “LISTEN! It’s not just her, its about your uncle too. Things that happened that you just never knew about.” “Maybe I should just talk to you when you are a bit calmer Kari.” “Please Roberto ? her death ?” Her words were cut short by some sobs. I listened intently now as she tried to find the words to use. I knew somewhere that perhaps I didn’t want to know anymore than I already did about Cindy’s death. I have learned to live with the guilt, the anger, and hatred. It is easier for me to just hate GOD and what he stands for than to have to change my entire philosophy around. I am what I am. On the other hand though, how could I not wish to know what she was going to tell me? Even though it was my fault perhaps my visions of what transpired that night aren’t accurate. I don’t remember the accident, all I remember is the aftermath. I remember being wheeled over to where she was pinned inside of her own vehicle and hearing her final words. Of course death is final ? even if I learn something new about her death, she is still in fact dead. There is no bringing her back to life like in “Pet Sematary”, there is no sour ground and there is no supernatural force that will link me to her. As the saying goes ? if you dig up the past, all you get is dirty. “Kari ? maybe you shouldn’t tell me.” “What” Why?” “Listen I have my memories of Cindy and they are lovely memories. I have even learned to live with the fact that it was my fault, that she would be standing right next to me as my wife. Starr would be eight years old, and making us laugh every day. It wasn’t easy ? but I know their blood is on my hands.” “That just it ?” “What’s just it!”” “You didn’t kill her, Roberto.” Like a jolt of electricity, fear ran through my body. I felt light headed and began sweating, yet it was not very warm in the hotel room. My muscles became weak and almost completely useless but somehow I kept the phone pressed up against my ear now hanging on every word she would hopefully say next. What did she mean by this? I was there! There is no way something or someone else killed her, there just is no way. “I saw her die Kari ? I watched as she gasped her final breath.” “Yes you did ?” she took a deep breath holding in the tears. “BUT do you remember anything after the bar that night?” No? but listen to me Kari don’t do this.” “You need to?” “NO! I can’t ? don’t do this to me, please. My life has built itself back up as strong as it has ever been. I don’t need to revisit old ghosts ? I don’t know if my body can take it.” “Your uncle knew about it?” This time my muscles tightened up and it certainly wasn’t depression and sadness that was filling up. Tears again began filling up in my eyes and a few began spilling over, but these were different tears, these tears were full of rage. “DON’T LIE TO ME KARI! My uncle was a good man! He loved me until I FUCKED up my life! Don’t proceed to drag his name in the mud! He’s dead! Just like Cindy so leave it alone! I DO NOT need this ? not at all! So leave the past alone, and don’t come at me with these lies.” “I have proof.” “LIAR! I will not let you do this to their names or ME. Do you think you can just come back into my life after almost eight years and just stir up the worst part of it” If you think I am going to go along with this little charade that you have or your fantasy land ? then you are sadly mistaken! I am not some “super sleuth”, I am not McGyver or Walker Texas Ranger ? I don’t look for questions when I already have the answers!” “Please just hear me out ?” “Leave me alone Kari.” I hung up the phone with as much authority as one can and sat at the edge of my bed. Everything began floating through my head once again and it scared me. She couldn’t be telling the truth! Theres no fucking way. My Uncle was a good man, why wouldn’t he have told me if Cindy would have died some other way? It’s ludicrous to even fathom. The nerve of that bitch! Yet ? What would she gain from lying to me? I drove my head into my hands and just let the tears flow, it as all I could do. It was too early for me to be on my toes and being “the rock” was just impossible 100% of the time. I have weaknesses, and I hate myself for it. I try so hard to hide them, keep them hidden in the dark corners of my mind, but every so often they pop out ? just like HIM? “Yeah just like me ?” His voice was expected ? LeStatt always showed up when I didn’t want him to. “Go away LeStatt.” “Oh but we are just getting started.” Things seemed to always fall apart at the wrong times for me, and now with the GCW World title so close, I could not afford an emotional break. My past would have to wait, Kari and Cindy would have to wait. The future is much more important.
