Hysteria

17 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/17/10 BOOM Vivica J. Valentine slams the door shut behind her and walks over towards the window again, looking down at the press still standing dozens of stories below her hotel room window. Grabbing the curtains she pulls them shut violently, ripping them halfway off the curtain rod. Frustrated she turns around to see the laptop still sitting on her bed, open and playing a video from earlier in her career. She watches herself hold the GCW World Heavyweight Championship high in the air. It was taunting her. She swore she flipped that laptop shut. "I'm better than you are..." She said out loud looking at the video of herself, "I'M BETTER THAN YOU." Her scream resonated back to her again off the walls as she grabbed the computer by the screen and hurled it across the room with a primal scream. Sparks flew from the casing as it shattered against the closet door. As the pieces rained down onto the hotel room carpet she felt good. She felt relieved... She felt alive. A smile crossed her face and her breathing staggered as she glanced at the smoke rising from the heap of circuits and breakers that used to be her PC. Ripping her black cargo bag off the floor she jerks it open, looking inside before throwing her "Bulletproof" shirt across the room as hard as she cool. The pants were next, she beat them against the bed repeatedly before tossing them across the room on top of the television. Pulling her elbow pads she threw them hard against the headboard. Next were the boots, pulling one out she spun around and heaved it at the wall listening to a whack as it made contact with white drywall. The other had a special purpose, water began to well up on her eyes as she saw her reflection in the mirror across the room from her... "FUCK YOU!" She screamed before hurling the boot at it, watching the glass shattered and shard off in different directions. Looking down in the bag it was empty, a blank space where her GCW World Heavyweight Championship should've been. Valentine slapped the bag off of her bed and the ripped the covers off and tossed them onto the floor. Grabbing onto the dresser handles, one by one she jerked on them as hard as she could... ripping them out of the dresser before slamming them down onto the floor. Wood splintered in impact as she kicked the scraps out of her path on the way to her floor lamp. Grabbing it off the floor she held it like a baseball bat, winding it back before whipping it around right into the screen of her television. The glass imploded and sparks flew out across the room as she dropped the lamp, letting it bounce off the hotel room floor before dropping down to her knees. Running her fingers through her blonde hair she looked around at the carnage around her and just screamed as loud as she could, probably waking up the entire building as she released months of pent up emotions. The faint echoes of the scream could still he heard in her ear drums as a small smile creeped across her face and she wiped the tears out of her eyes with shaken hands. She didn't know who she was anymore, she'd lost complete control over her own body as snot began to drip down out of her nose. She whispered to herself... "I'm better than this..." "I'm better than this..." "I'm better than this..." "I'm better than this..." It wasn't long before the hyperventilating started and she sat down rocking herself back and forth to the horrors that went through her mind. The Fearless Phenom she wasn't anymore. There was no going back now... because Vivica J. Valentine made a beast out of herself. It was the only way she could look at herself in the morning. ---- It's bugging me, calling me And twisting me around Yeah I'm endlessly caving in And turning inside out Cause I want you now I want it now Give me your heart and your soul And I'm breaking out I'm breaking out That's when she'll lose control Yeah it's hurting me, morphing me And forcing me to strive To be endlessly cold within And dreaming I'm alive Cause I want it now I want it now Give me your heart and your soul I'm not breaking down I'm breaking out And I tend to lose control And I want you now I want you now I feel my heart implode And I'm breaking out Escaping now Feeling my faith erode -- "Hysteria" by MUSE ---- 4:52 AM The alarm clock on the nightstand was a constant reminder of nothing more than the fact that she wasn't sleeping. As the sun began to rise, not much had changed from the night before. The aches and pains all over her body made her feel as if she'd been hit by a large truck, and the ice bath she took as soon as she got home from the arena didn't help much. She couldn't eat, she couldn't sleep, she couldn't do anything but lay there... looking at the ceiling as her mind still ran around in circles. What do you do now? Where do you go from here? How did everything go wrong? With a small yelp she rolled over onto her stomach and pushed herself up. Her arms buckled under her weight and the feeling of moving around made her want to scream at the top of her lungs, but thus was the life of a professional wrestler. Her physical scars were nothing compared to her emotional ones, not to mention the mental ones. As she dragged her almost corpse like body across the hotel room she made her way over to the window, wincing in pain as she looked down below. The next morning the media was still there. News outlets from all over the wrestling world wanted comments from NC-17's competitors. Even if she wanted to grab a bite to eat she couldn't leave without being assaulted by those vultures. Oh, how she hated the media. They'd do anything to watch her fall, watch her squirm. They were the sickest individuals on the planet, exploiting people in their weakest moments for their own personal gain. The very sight of them turned her uncomfortable grimace into a scowl. She bit her lip as she backed away, running her hands through her hair. She had to be able to do something about this. She used to be on top of the world, unbeatable, some wondered if there was anyway to ever grab the GCW World Heavyweight Championship out of her hands... but Vivica J. Valentine felt so far away from those days. If she could repeat those 235 days all over again, hardships and all, she'd give her life away now in a second. There had to be something she could see, something she could change, even the smallest detail had to be corrected. Pulling her laptop out of her gear bad at the foot of her head, Valentine laid it down on the bed and dragged her body next to it. Turning it on, she waited impatiently as it loaded up, hitting the space bar repeatedly until it did. She clicked Internet Explorer and went right to http://youtube.com.... Vivica Valentine World Heavyweight Champion She typed into the search window before hitting enter. Thousands of results came up, most of them being fan made videos posted around the beginning of 2009. Curious, she clicked on the first one she saw and watched as the sound of "You Can't Stop Me" by Guano Apes played over her speakers. The girl who appeared on the screen in front of her seemed long gone, a shadow of what she once used to be. The smile of her face long eroded into a violent snarl. Clicking back on the browser she turns the video off, moving along to the next one and starting it up. The video shows her on her knees in the middle of the ring at NC-17... crying, grasping the GCW World Heavyweight Championship against her chest as Lia Ambrosi applauded her. She listened at Joey Andrews announced it on the video. The words she hoped so desperately to hear hours ago... "Winner... and NEWWWWW GCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION..." He stops with a dramatic pause, "VIVICA JJJJ VALLLLLENTINE!" The crowd roared on the video as she watched herself smile and bow to them in appreciation as "My Sacrifice" by Creed began to play. Highlights flashed across the screen before her eyes. Matches against LeStatt Knight, Bryan Mayhem, Shaman, Jay Terror, Jordan White... the list goes on and on and she watches them all fall to her. The smiling face on the screen has started to become something much more than a fond memory. As her cerulean blue eyes made eye contact with her now future self, it almost seemed like she was taunting her. She daring her to be as good as she used to be. The anger rose up inside of her before she flipped the laptop shut. "What the fuck is wrong with me..." She said out loud, forcing herself up and out of her bed. She needed to move around and be free. Get out of the hotel and clear her mind... but that wasn't going to happen. Maybe a brisk walk down the hallway would do the trick? Her knees buckled as she limped her way over to the hotel room door, while reaching down into her pajama pants pocket and making sure her keycard was with her. Grasping the doorknob she pushed the door open and stepped into the silent hallway. Closing her eyes for a minute she let the door latch behind her, taking a deep breath of not so fresh air. Opening her eyes she sees a man wearing a black polo shirt walking towards her down the hallway, maybe he was one of those mall walker types... or maybe he was lost? Regardless she didn't feel like dealing with anyone so she turned her back to the man and started to limp away off to her own devices when suddenly... "Vivica!" She name echoed down the hallway, stopping her just a few steps into her exodus. Turning her head around made her grind her teeth in pain as the man started a trot to catch up to her. Even as he approached she didn't recognize his face, something was wrong... "I've been looking for you." "Who are you?" She answered, taking a look into his eyes. Maybe it was the pain or perhaps the lack of sleep... but no matter how hard she stared at the man nothing came to mind at all. "My name is Jack Walters." He spoke up, "I'm a reporter for the W..." "This is a closed floor." Valentine quickly answered, trying to get away from the press. "Well, I'm staying on this floor so I'm allowed he..." "This floor is closed, you're not staying here." Vivica answered, "So if you could pleas..." "I just have a few quick questions." "I have no comment for you." "You had an amazing performance last nig..." "I said I have no comments for you." Vivica sternly interrupted him before turning her back to the man and again limping away from confrontation before the man grabbed her by the shoulder. Spinning around Valentine back-handed him across the face sending him down hard onto the hallway. She looked down at her hand and then down at the man trying to pull himself back up. He looked up at her in stunned silence, as if he had something he wanted to say but it just wasn't coming out of his mouth. Vivica on the other hand, had no problems speaking her mind... "You had it coming." Vivica responded. "You bitch..." The man responded pushing himself up, "I'd heard rumors bu..." "What did you call me?" She snarled back. "I... er..." Jack stuttered as he reached his feet, back-stepping away from The Black Mariah as she took a step forward. "You think I'm a bitch? Huh? You think I'm a bitch?" Valentine balled her hand into a fist as she approached, "You fucking people made me this way. I was fine, I was happy until you guys had to push me around. You had to break me. You had to watch me fall, didn't you?" "We're just doing our jo..." "You're a fucking coward is what you are." The Bulletproof Blonde wouldn't let him get a word in edgewise, "Get the fuck off my floor..." "But I'm sta..." "NOW." She winded back and almost swung before the man jumped backwards, turned around, and jogged back up the hallway towards the elevator. Her shoulders rose up and down as her eyes narrowed. Her fist began to shake as she blew her blonde and black striped hair out of her face. She wasn't the same Vivica as before. This girl had snapped. ---- "Rise" by The Cult echoes through the arena as she laid staring up at the lights. Her body felt like it had been shattered into a million pieces, but that was nothing compared to her shattered dreams. Rolling over onto her stomach she watches as Andy Murray held the GCW World Heavyweight Championship to his chest, clutching onto it like a beacon of vindication. He had the title she'd given up everything for... Her title was gone. Wiping the blood out of the corner of her lip she watches helplessly as it was fastened around the waist of her former lover. Watching him soak in the adoration of her former fans only made the wounds deeper as she forced herself up to her feet. Under her weight her legs wobbled, reminding her of the violent struggle she'd just lost. She felt like it was all for nothing, everything she threw away and all the criticism and punishment she'd taken from everyone was for absolutely nothing. Seething, she choked back the tears as an amazing thing happened... the crowd was on their feet, but it wasn't for Andy Murray. It was for her. The adoration and respect that she'd longed for that started this whole thing was right there in front of her. She'd gotten exactly what she wanted as the man who should hate her more than any other on the planet stepped aside to give her the moment she do desperately needed. She thought the cheers from the crowd would take away all the pain and agony she'd gone through the past few months. The disrespect that made her feel so slighted would simply be overcome as she held that GCW World Heavyweight Championship in the air and forced the world to take her seriously again. Even without the title she got the reaction she'd hoped for, but it didn't go away at all. As she looked around at the crowd she felt insulted as they cheered for her out of sympathy. She didn't need their pity. She didn't need their charity, anymore. Fuck them. With a sneer she fumed before dropping down to the mat and rolling out of the ring, without the thing she came there for. To the people in the crowd she was a joke, a failure, the punchline of an epic fall from grace. For the people just loved to watch their heroes fall, just pick up the latest tabloid and read as they ridicule the people who put their lives out in the public just to entertain the masses. Watch as their private lives are exposed and the public soaks up every minute of their failure. As she turned her back to the crowd and limped up the ring, they completely ignored her in favor of Andy Murray... and perhaps that was for the best. She had been ignoring them for a while. Their sick and twisted ways wouldn't drag her down to hell with them. They can laugh now, but she already had redemption in her thoughts. She didn't want their cheers or smiles anymore, and didn't give a fuck about their chants. It wasn't the crowd's approval that made her feel incomplete, and it wasn't the love she lost from Andy Murray. It was the gold plated and leather piece of validation. All she wanted was her title, and she was still willing to do absolutely ANYTHING to put it around her waist again. Anything. ---- "He who makes a beast of himself, gets rid of the pain of being a man." -- Samuel Johnson
