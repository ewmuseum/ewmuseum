Every week he would come in, take a urine test and meet with his psychiatrist. They would go into a little room, and Keegan would sit across from her as she scribbled notes on her pad. Keegan was one of many patients she saw through out the week, which made him feel more open to talk to her. The likelihood that she would really remember anything he said provided an air of comfort. Her name was Dr. Janet Copeland. She was middle-aged, tanned with long brunette hair. She wore thin wire frame glasses and iced honey lipstick that accented the beautiful curves of her face and lips. Like most Californian women, she looked very young for her age, but no visible signs of plastic surgery were present, she was all natural. Today she surprised him by bringing up a recent event. "So I see your working again." Keegan nodded, he was surprised they got his paperwork already. Then again, the wrestling industry has played it extra careful in the last year. "Are you glad to be back to work Mr. O’Conner?" Dr. Copeland asked, probing for something to talk about today. "Yeah." Keegan responded lacking enthusiasm. Dr. Copeland nodded and scribbled a little on her pad. "I thought you might be a little more enthused then that Mr. O’Conner." "I am enthused." Keegan replied, "I just have other things on my mind, is all." Her petite hand scribbled a few more notes across the ledger. "What might that be Mr. O’Conner?" Her direct and indifferent approach always amused him, and today was no different. Keegan cracked a smirk, suppressing a chuckle and decided for the thousands of dollars he was paying these people he might as well put her to use. "I’m just sort of reminiscing, ya know?" Keegan asks, non-chalantly. "It was over a year ago I really got my start in the business moving up to mainstream wrestling, and now I’m getting another opportunity." "I see." She replied, furiously scribbling across the notepad again. "Are you concerned of how your peers will perceive you because of your problems?" Keegan actually had put some consideration into that, and had his answer ready. "Not really. I’m here getting help, I’ve been clean and I’m basically starting fresh again." Dr. Copeland continued to scribble down her thoughts as she asked the next question, "And what about your criminal record? Didn’t you say in a previous session that it has caused concern before?" The question caught Keegan off guard, after months of coming to Dr. Copeland she had never made reference to remembering anything he had ever said until today. He began to feel less comfortable talking to the woman with every passing second. "Mr. O’Conner?" Dr. Copeland asked, taking her glasses off and setting them on the metal table. She leaned across the table to examine his expression closer. "Yeah." Keegan replied sharply. "There was concern before." "Are you concerned of the repercussions it might provide in your new company?" Dr. Copeland inquired, a quizzical expression across her otherwise innocent looking face. "I?" Keegan began; it felt as though the room was closing in on him. "I never thought about it." "Well," Dr. Copeland began with her focus remaining on him. "Would you like to discuss that?" "No." Keegan responded sharply. Dr. Copeland nods retrieving her glasses and pad before leaning back in her chair. "Alright, what about the issue with Jordan? Have you had any contact with him, or his mother in the last year?" If ever there was anything he didn’t want to discuss this was it. Keegan rose from his chair and began to head for the door, Dr. Copeland hollered after him. "Mr. O’Conner, you really shouldn’t leave." Hesitating at the door for a moment he listened to what she had to say. "I know someone of your nature has a difficulty in discussing matters this personal, but if you have any hope of recovery we need to tackle the tougher issues." Dr. Copeland explained, also rising from her chair. "You need to work out the problems that put you here before you can get better. Ninety percent of all addicts become so because they are unable to face problems in their life." Slowly Keegan turned away from the door to face her. "Have you ever felt like your life was nothing but one mistake after another?" Perplexed; Dr. Copeland probes, "How so?" "Let me put it this way." Taking a step closer to the table Keegan continued, "Just about everything I’ve done in my life was a mistake, but out of all it there is only one that I truly regret." ~x~ Are you listening? whoa oh oh oh oh oh Sing it back, whoa oh oh oh oh oh So tell me what do I need (tell me what do I need) whoa oh, whoa oh When words lose their meaning (When words lose their meaning) whoa oh, whoa oh I was spinning free, whoa oh oh oh oh oh With a little sweet and simple numbing me. Yeah, stumble till you crawl, whoa oh oh oh oh oh Sinking into sweet uncertainty ~x~ October 30th, 2007 Keegan sat at a round oak table in the lounge of A New Beginning, the inpatient drug rehabilitation facility he had been checked into. The various drugs in his system kept him doped out, kept him calm and were meant to keep him from fiending. So far, they weren’t working. He had know idea when Jacque was supposed to be there, more than likely the idiot Canadian got lost on his way, sure would serve him right anyway. These false hopes were shattered however when Sammie the receptionist brought the Canadian Jackass his way. Keegan couldn’t help but stare blankly, imagining what it would be like to be done with this man forever. If ever there was a man he couldn’t stand, Jacque was definitely it. "Keegan?" Jacque asked sitting directly in front of him. Keegan never saw him sit down, must be the drugs were finally kicking in. After a brief startle Keegan settled down focusing on his visitor. "Hi Jacque." Keegan replied with a hint of disdain in his tone. "I wanna make this as quick as possible. Just gimme the damn papers and I will sign them." He could hear himself over annunciating each word, but apparently Jacque paid his overstatements no mind. Jacque’s jaw dropped as he stared at Keegan, he began to say something but Keegan could hardly hear him with all the screaming going on in his head, he wanted nothing more than to reach across the table and choke the life out of this insensitive prick. Keegan finally came to towards the end of Jacque’s response, "?more of a fight over this." Keegan just stared at him blankly, "Why fight Jacque? I know I am not capable or worthy of being a father. I know this is something that you wouldn’t expect from me but in a situation like this you’re more of a man than I am." Keegan choked back any sign of emotion even though his heart felt like it was being crushed. Jacque pulled out the papers and pushed them across the table to Keegan. Keegan signed them without a bit of hesitation. He knew if he hesitated that he wouldn’t be able to go through with it. This was his son, a part of him he would probably never know. Jacque stood and so did Keegan, with a nod both men walked away from each other. Jacque headed toward the door as Keegan watched him. He knew he was making the biggest mistake of his life but it was for the best. ~x~ (Are you listening? Are you listening?) As much as he hated to admit it she had been right. Until he was able to face his problems he’d never get better. He walked across the living room placing his house keys on the kitchen counter, a glance at his answering machine showed he had two new messages. He pressed the play button as he walked towards the refrigerator. "Message One, 4:03 PM Today." The automated voice ended, and a very familiar voice began. "Hey Keeg, it’s Paul. I just wanted to call to say it’s great to see you back in the business, I just know you’ll do great against Atken. Anyway, call me back when you can it would be nice to get together again." The message ended with a loud beep, Keegan smiling reached into the fridge and grabbed a bottle of Aquafina. "Message Two, 3:35 PM Today." As Keegan approached the living room to turn on the TV the voice on the other end stopped him in his tracks. "Hey Keegan, it’s Jacque." Four words he never thought he’d hear again, nearly collapsing he dropped to the couch as the memories of their last meeting came to mind. "We gotta talk, get a hold of me as soon as you can?" Jacque’s voice pauses on the machine before adding, "I hope you’re doing okay man. I’m sorry." As the beep sounds through the desolate apartment Keegan remains seated. Jacque was the very last man he ever expected to hear from again, on the bright side he'd have something to talk about next week in therapy. ~x~ I was spinning free, whoa oh oh oh oh oh With a little sweet and simple numbing me.
