Footieprints In The Sand

22 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/22/11 My dreams are a desert, balmy and endless. In nothing but my lime-green onesie, I tread toward the shining bush, because it is the only object in sight. My foot sinks into the sand with every print I make, a memoir to this hellish terrain that I had stepped upon it. A man walks next to me, neither leading nor following, but accepting the path that I chose that led to the shining bush. He has flowing locks of brown hair and a pair of rawhide sandals, and I suspect that he is jealous of my pajamas. His plain white robe looks pretty cool on him, but the exuberant amount of comfort I was experiencing must have made him envious. He had traveled with me for many miles in the eternal desert that WAS MY LIFE. He never once complained that he was tired, thirsty, or famished. It had never been brought up as to "why" he followed me, either. He was just my sidekick, my shadow. I go there, he goes there, I go here, he goes here, me there, him there, and there we were there. Well, except for this ONE time. "Jesus?" I ask him, mainly to break the silence that had fallen within our sheets. "Yes, my friend?" Jesus responds, looking over to me with his eyebrows arched. "Look behind us," I tell him, motioning over my shoulder to the trail that we had created. "We have made many footprints together, you and I. You have walked beside me throughout this entire journey. Well…almost the entire journey." Jesus nods, turning his head back forward and gazing toward the bush. "But," I continued, "what about those times where there's only one set of footprints? Those were some of the toughest parts in the journey: rocks and shit, and I'm wearing feetie pajamas! Where were you then?" Jesus chuckles and shrugs his shoulders sheepishly. "Hot Mask, I was carrying you." I glance over at him and stare as we continue to walk. "Are you high? Like, there weren't any footprints next to mine a half-mile ago, which was less than ten minutes ago. You weren't there, and I wasn't floating." I could tell Jesus was thinking this one over, as I had just tapped into one of the worst lies ever told by man. Fairly quick with a snappy parable, I notice his pinky finger go into the corner of his mouth as he rationalizes his previous statement. "Yes I was." "You goddamn dick-drink," I fire back, "no you weren't!" Jesus lets loose an extravagant exhale and shakes his head knowingly. "Look, you're just saying that because you don't have faith in me. You don't believe in what I say." "Because you are full of shit," I respond, and with certainty. "Sick burn!" A smiling cloud above exclaims, reaching down a puffy white hand and high-fiving me on the break point. Jesus, his authoritarian rule being challenged, reaches into the front fold of his robe and pulls out a deck of cards. Shuffling them as he walks, he calmly retorts without making eye contact. "I'll show you I have magical powers, man. I'll show you." "With a card trick?" I ask, sneering and scoffing at the notion. He continues shuffling the cards: under and over, under and over, under and over, under and over, under and over. "Not just any card trick, but the ULTIMATE card trick. You'll believe in me then," he states with confidence. "Because I was carrying you." Under and over, under and over, under and over. "FAGGOT!" A booming voice bellows from the sky. "HEY! You BUTT!" Jesus exclaims as the cloud retracts his arm after knocking the deck of cards out of his hand. When people recant the greatest moments in professional wrestling history, a few matches immediately come to mind. Hulk vs. Andre, and the body slam heard 'round the world. The epic hour-long battles against Ricky Steamboat and Ric Flair. Mankind taking on the Undertaker in one of the most breathtaking Hell In A Cell contests ever seen. Shawn Michaels defeating Bret Hart in what would go down in infamy as the Montreal Screwjob. Bastion Booger taking on two pizzas, a Renaissance Faire turkey leg, five bags of Lay's Sour Cream & Onion, and a liter of cola. All of these matches are absolute shit compared to the epic battle taking place on Hot Mask's latest investments: a crisp-looking flat screen and the best professional wrestling video game ever made. WRESTLE HOOD: MEN IN TIGHTS Seated at the couch with respective controllers in hands are Hot and Scott Mask, the Czar of Spank's on-screen offspring and off-screen pal. Both are intensely focused on video-smashing the dookie out of one another, so much that Scott's tongue hangs out much like Charlie Brown's did during a difficult task. "Prepare to lose, toots," Hot quips, maneuvering his wrestler off of the top rope and connecting with a deadly diving head-butt. In real life, this particular head-butt would kill an individual, as Hot Mask had taken almost a half-hour to perfect the look of his Create-A-Wrestler character. A head that was the size of the wrestler's entire body, and a right hand that was triple the size of his left. At the top of the screen, The Elephant Man's health bar was nearly full, and now had two "FINISHER!" icons still left in the reserve tank. Scott frantically began to tap buttons as The Elephant Man makes the cover. "ONE! TWO! BULLSHIT!" Hot screamed, putting emphasis on the final exclamation as Scott's character, the Gossip Kid, manages to kick out. "Yesssssssssss!" Scott remarks, unintentionally doing a perfect Home Alone impression. His celebration was short-lived, however, as Hot Mask had Elephant Man lift the dazed Gossip Kid to his feet. Scott tries desperately to move his man out of position of The Elephant Man's second finishing maneuver, but was outwitted by the smooth-talking 128-bit John Merrick. A devastating paintbrush slap with the genetically-enhanced hand sends Gossip Kid crashing to the mat, almost knocking the "lips with index finger covering them" design off the ass of his trunks. "Did you feel that, you stink-factory? Because I felt it," Hot Mask laughs. Pressing the R2 button, the Elephant Man responds by standing over the Gossip Kid and executing Sexy Taunt 74 (single breast jiggle). He then lies down vertically for the pin, prompting Hot to count along with the game's referee. "ONE! TWO…" The machine-gun style tapping of the buttons never fail, however, as Scott releases a cry of amazement as Gossip Kid kicks out once again. "CHEATER," HM yells directly in Scott's ear, which only makes Scott laugh and throw a kick to the Elephant Man's gut. Regaining his focus, however, Hot responds with a kick of his own, then engages a collar-and-elbow tie up. "But seriously, how the fuck are you kicking out of these?" "I don't know," Scott responds. "You're asking me to explain the schematics of a video game. I'm just mashing buttons." "Well, I got something for your button-mashing ass," Hot fires back, sounding really tough in the process. Performing a scoop slam onto Gossip Kid, the Elephant Man tromps to the near turnbuckle and mounts it, then leaps off for ANOTHER awkward diving head-butt. "FAGGOT!" Hot Mask booms, knocking the controller out of Scott Mask's hands as the ref makes the count. ONE! TWO! Just before Scott can snatch the controller back to his hands, though, a miracle of modern day wrestling takes place: the Gossip Kid kicks out of Elephant Man's finishing maneuver. For the third time. As Scott looks up from retrieving the joystick, he cannot help but giggle at the scene. Hot Mask, however, stares directly at his counterpart as tears begin to form underneath his eyelids. "Are you crying?" Scott asks, in shock at the sudden turn of events. "Man, are you crying?" "…no," Hot answers, casually brushing his forearm against his eyes. "I'm just sweaty." Scott leers toward him. "If you're just sweaty, then why'd you shut the game off?" "BECAUSE I'M SWEATY," Hot squeals, throwing his controller haphazardly behind him. The sound of smashing glass soon follows, prompting the startled Masks to turn and look. "Dammit!" Jumping off the couch and nearly stomping Scott's knee into the carpet, Hot darts over to the bookshelf to find the controller, the remnants of a frame, and an emotionally significant family photograph from his youth. Sighing, HM bends over to pick up the picture, holding it in between thumb and forefinger. It was the happiest of days in that spring break of 1988 spent at Myrtle Beach when the snapshot was taken. A brown-haired man with a tinted handlebar mustache hoisted a child on his shoulders. The child wore a pair of neon yellow swimming trunks and a matching mask with the image of a green caboose over the face. The train phase, Hot would reminisce, the point in life where he wanted nothing more than to grow up and become a train. Although the notion of a human becoming an inanimate object was silly, his father had always encouraged him that if he worked hard enough, then one day he might just become a train. HM had many photographs of himself and his father on their various beach trips, but this particular day stood out strongest in his memory. He'd treasured this picture, as it was taken an hour before his dad became his hero. Sure, most people look up to their father as a birthright, but the event in question had solidified Mr. M as a bona-fide SuperMask in his eyes. Scott, who had just retreated to the kitchen for a bag of Cheetos, creeps up next to his pal and gazes at the photo along with him. "You and your dad?" "No, a child molester who claimed to be my uncle. YES, you cheating shit, me and my dad." "Cool mustache," Scott remarks in between snacking on the cheese-coated corn knobs. "Yep," Hot nods. "Did I ever tell you the story about this picture?" Scott digs down into the bag and pulls out another handful of Chester's pride. "You never tell me anything about your childhood, except for the time you put a pinecone down your pants in first grade and made a shit joke on the playground." Hot Mask chuckles. "That was a good one. But this picture here, this was taken on the day that one of the most important events of my life happened. That man in the picture? He was more than just Therbert Daniel Mask, Jr. He was a teacher, a hunter, a savior, and an idol. And a bearer of great genes to boot." "What was so special about that day?" Scott Mask asks, a boyish curiosity in his voice. "Well," Hot pretends to recall (although he had recited the story to several women in order to fuck them several times before,) "I had spent the rest of that afternoon working on my very first REAL sand castle. I mean, I'd dig around and shit when I was younger, but I was determined to build a fucking masterpiece. And it ended up shaping up to be just that: like three-foot high walls and everything! But, as I was just putting the finishing touches on it, squaring up the towers and getting ready to go down to the ocean to collect water for the moat, something happened." HM sighs, switching gears. "These two kids who were older than me, well, we'd had an earlier altercation in the hotel arcade. I don't really remember what exactly happened, but I do remember that they spoke French, so the kicks to the shins we exchanged was their fault. Anyway, they were walking along the beach and happened to take notice that I was the one building the sand castle that looked better than anything in Montreal ever did, so they very rudely began to stomp it to smithereens." Scott glances over at Hot, his mouth forming an angry frown. "Who DOES that?" "Me," Hot replies, "to a kid the day before. But, I didn't have any concept of karma at that point…and that kid's sand castle sucked." "Ah." "So anyway," Hot continues, "as I dropped my little pail and balled my fists up for American beach architects everywhere, the sight of my dad swooping in was like a bald eagle telling those kids to go fuck themselves. I'm pretty sure they didn't understand English curse words, but they certainly understood the universal language of him pointing to my forlorn little frame next to the sea, my tiny little shovel, the pile of destruction they had just caused, and then his titanic biceps." Scott lets out a half-laugh. "So he made them rebuild the castle for you?" Hot nods and grins. "The whole thing. But, the crowning achievement…" "Huh huh," Scott interrupts. "Crown…castle…" HM stares back at Scott, shaking his head. "You asshole. The PINNACLE achievement was as the two frogs put the finishing touches on the last turret, my dad gives them a thumbs-up. He says, 'Great job!' And then, without a moment's hesitation, he kicked the shit out of the castle right in their faces. BOOM," Mask animates, making a sweeping motion with his leg, "sand in the eyes and mouth!" "Darn," Scott quips, not sure whether to laugh or be horrified that a grown man kicked dirt in two kids' faces. "He DID that?" "Goddamn right, he did. Of course, the two pussies went and told THEIR pussy father, who pressed pussy charges since it's an entire family of pussies." "Is that why it was one of the most important events in your life? Because your dad went to jail?" Scott asks. "Nah, he was in and out of there fairly regularly," Hot muses. "The event was important because I ended up learning three valuable life lessons as a result. First lesson: lying under oath to protect a family member is OK when you're a kid and the accusing party is French, because South Carolina judges hate those types. Second lesson: muscles are intimidating and can make people smaller than you do things for you. And the last and most important lesson?" HM beams, emphasizing the point by lifting an index finger. "The best punishments are the ones that immediately follow punishments when the person doesn't expect another punishment. Like taking a freshman's virginity and then reciting your sexual history during the post-coital cuddle session." Scott blinks twice. "That…that actually explains a lot about you." "Just like the old saying, Scott," HM grins, wrapping a friendly arm around his broad shoulders. "The tadpole doesn't fall too far from the dick head." "That's certainly one way of putting it," Scott agrees. "And this picture here is my reminder that I owe everything I am to my dear old dad. Well, him and faulty condoms." "Wait," Scott realizes, crinkling his face and shooting him an accusing glance. "You actually cuddle after sex?" "I sure do," Mask boasts, not afraid to show his feminine side. "I may never be a world champion wrestler, but I'll always be a world champion snuggle bear. Now let's go to Hobby Lobby and get a new frame for this thing, you homo." "Put the goddamn cards up," I bark at Jesus, who had taken his sweet time in collecting the mess out of the sand. We were closing in on the bush, which was where my instincts instructed me to go. "Are you sure you don't want to see it?" He asks, offering up the Holy deck for me to pick a card (any card). Ignoring him, I continue to walk, noticing the way the dunes seemed to roll on into eternity. As years of watching garbage television had no doubt rendered my dreams less creative, it was still insane to think that my own mind could have come up with a landscape so vast that I'd been walking for miles on it. An arid, excruciatingly long journey, all to reach a bush. It was a pretty accurate metaphor, if anything. Finally, Jesus and I stood only twenty feet from the piece of desert foliage. We both simultaneously notice a twisting trail of smoke begin to rise from the top of it. "Is it about to catch on fire?" I wonder aloud. "No." Both of us were startled by the sound of what must have been the bush, because it sure as hell wasn't the cloud. Without time to comprehend its answer, the bush continues. "Psyche!" ARUUUUUUUU-GA! ARUUUUUUUU-GA! (Not necessarily the sound you'd normally hear when something bursts into flames, but instances are funny like that.) Both the Son of Man and I stepped back from the inferno as the bush laughed maniacally at his little joke. Nervous, I pipe up. "Are you…God?" "YES, MY CHILD. I AM THE ALPHA AND THE OMEGA, THE CREATOR OF ALL THAT IS EVERYTHING, THE…ET CETERA. But you already knew that, which is why you sought me out this evening inside of your dream!" To me, the bush/God had a very intimidating presence. "Hey Dad," Jesus remarks dryly. "Hey kiddo," responds the bush. However intimidating he was, though, was almost immediately nullified by his comical tone. Even though I'd read a few Bible verses in my time, I never knew that God sounded exactly like Sam Kinison. "Well, I wasn't really seeking you. I was just kind of bored and figured, 'eh, there's a bush, let's walk to it,'" I note, shrugging my shoulders. The bush burned on. "So, you were just dreaming about walking in a desert. To a bush. Really?" I nod in response. "That's cool, I guess," God muses. Although he did fine work with creating sarcasm, he wasn't too sharp at hiding it; at least not in bush form. "Well, you're already here, and since you went to the trouble of walking all this way…anything you want to ask me?" Here I am. In the middle of a desert with no one but God and Jesus at my side, and my mind races with a million different questions I could ask. Who would take Best In Show at this year's Westminster Kennel Club dog show? Would I ever find true love? Would Kelly Clarkson ever stay at one size? Would I still fuck her if she were a blimp? Is Garth ever going to get his Sports Illustrated Football Phone? Who really DID have the biggest ding-a-ling in professional wrestling? "Millicent the Pekingese, probably not, are you joking, absolutely, there was a mix-up in the shipping department and it should arrive tomorrow, and Rod Stewart, Jr." God answers without me having to even speak. Of course, these responses begged even bigger questions… "He's a meat-and-potatoes wrestler in a northeast territory. Not actually Rod Stewart's son," God answers, without me even having to think. "Ah," I respond, grinning. "Well, I've got just one more," I continue, finally forcing up the real question that had plagued my mind for the past fifteen seconds. "Fire away," the burning bush jokes. "ENOUGH WITH THE FUCKING WORDPLAY JOKES!" Jesus screams, kicking at the sand angrily. "Calm down," I tell him, giving him a backhand across his mouthy cheek. "God," I stutter, feeling the douche chill up my spine as the next words came pouring from my mouth, "what am I going to have to do to achieve true happiness?" My truths are a desert, balmy and endless. In nothing but my lime-green onesie, I walked away from the shining bush with the knowledge that I wasn't necessarily prepared for. My foot sinks into the sand with every print I make, a memoir to this hellish terrain that I had stepped upon it. When I awake the next morning, I'm not sure if I can chalk this up to being just some ridiculous dream.
