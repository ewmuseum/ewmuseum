The demise of Kid Cool part 1

19 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/19/10 Failure... That's all you are... You'll never be a true champion. Francis Eriks (Kid Cool) kicked a tin can against a curb on the street, the echo reverberated over the dark night as a dog barked at the noise. The walk home was always going to be long one, GCW didn't pay enough for the taxi fare and Kid Cool had to walk the last two miles alone, thinking about his loss, as he nurtured his wrist gingerly. The match was over quicker than expected which disappointed the crowd, the GCW bosses were not happy with his performance and quickly kicked him out of the arena after the show. Frank was used to this kind of treatment from a company, nobody seemed to care about his passion for the business and the idols he looked up to. A cars headlights turned into the street soaking up Franks body with illumination, Francis froze still for a moment trying to soak in the engine of the car, the car slowed down as it approached him. Cocking his head in interest Frank held his hand up to shield his eyes as the engine switched off. The was a moment of utter stillness, neither Francis or the car moved, the dog stopped barking and for once, no crickets could be heard anywhere. It was if someone had pressed pause on time. The door of the car opens and a polished black shoe steps out along with a neatly pressed trouser leg, finally the full body of a immaculately dressed individual looks inquisitively at Kid Cool running his fingers through his slicked black hair. “Frank Eriks?” He asked coyly adjusting his tie slightly “Maybe... Who wants to know?” Kid Cool tried to sound brave yet cracks in his voice could already be distinguished “Arn't you fed up with nobody giving you a break Mr. Eriks? Angry at the world that just mock you, instead of cheering your talents?” The man asked Cool bluntly, his voice deadly serious. “I am a wrestler, I can handle myself” Kid Cool snapped back, clearly not in the mood to discuss his failures “You can handle yourself? That's not what I saw tonight, I've been sent over to watch you Francis, keep an eye and make sure you succeed” The gentlemen extends a hand gesturing towards the car. “Would you like to go for a drive” Kid Cool raises an eyebrow then shakes his head in a no fashion “Look I don't even know you. How do I know you're not going to just kidnapp me and hold me to ransom. I've seen the documentaries!” Kid Cool retorts with a certain sense of knowledge. The man paused for a moment just staring intently into the eyes of Kid Cool who glances away in embarrassment. “I'm offering you an opportunity to drive yourself forward, you either want it, or I can just walk away” Kid Cool looks around at the neighbourhood he is walking through taking in a deep breath of cool night air. He looks back at the impressive gentlemen before looking at the black car. “How are you going to make me succeed?” Kid Cool asked. “We have certain methods of improving your style and... should we say uniqueness” The man let a small smirk grow out of the corner of his mouth. “Well that was vague, if I'm coming with you I need answers, who wants me to succeed? Who are you working for” The man gestured towards the car once more and Kid Cool took a step forward, sweat seemed to appear from nowhere all over his body. A cold shiver ran down his body, Kid Cool looked down at his hands for a moment it felt like they were being controlled by someone else. “Are you okay?” Asked the driver looking concerned “I... I don't know” Kid Cool paused. “Something is happening to me” Failure... That's all you are... You'll never be a true champion. Ran through Kid Cools head before nothing. === 20 minutes later === Kid Cool found himself looking at the passing city of Los Angeles laying sprawled out in the back of the car, a pounding sensation ran through his head, a groan comes out from his mouth as he attempts speech. “You woke up I see... You took a nasty fall Francis” The driver looked in his rear view mirror now sporting some dark sunglasses “Wh... Where am” “Well you need to see a Doctor Francis, you just blacked out. So I'm helping you out” The gentleman paused for a moment. “Just relax we'll be there in ten minutes” Francis attempts to sit up but finds it too much of a strain and ends up lying back down again. Thoughts echoed through his mind driving through his brain. “You nearly unleashed me Francis” “What?” Francis called aloud. The driver slowed down and turned around “I didn't say anything Francis.” The driver turned back to look at the road once more as the engine revved and changed gear. “I would have stopped you entering the car, that idiot driving you would have been punished” Sprang throughout Kid Cool's brain, his hands clenching into a fist and his blood boiling. A bead of sweat dripped off the nose of Kid Cool as he found himself beginnning to lose control, all of a sudden the lights of Los Angels begin to blur. The driver skidded to a halt. “STOP PANICKING FRANCIS!” but the voice was distant, muffled and undistinguished. Francis' eyes rolled into the back of his head and once again there was just black. === A short time later === Warmth, a pleasant feeling, like a warm breeze gentle lapping up against the face like wave after wave from the ocean. The warm feeling was then suddenly replaced with a deep cold, cold and burning. Francis took in a huge gulp of breath, almost as if it was the first time, he coughed feeling a tickle coming from his chest and opened his eyes. “Oh... *cough*” The car had hit a tree and the driver was lying against the steering wheel sporting a black eye, Francis began to feel overcome with fear and confusion. His hands were cut from dragging himself out of the car onto the hard gritty road. His mind was racing a mile a second. “You did it Francis, you broke free” Francis caught himself smiling. A bizarre feeling of satisfaction drowned his body with pleasure as he once again looked at the wrecked car. “But I didn't do any...” Kid Cool began to whimper to himself But you did, ran through Kid Cool's head, he couldn't quite remember but something seemed his fault. Kid Cool dragged himself to his feet and although unsteady, soaked in his new surroundings, the street looked off the beaten track and there were only a few small lights lingering in the distance. “What the hell did I just do...” Kid Cool called out to nobody in particular. “The right thing” Came a voice from behind a startled Cool, arms flew quickly grappling Kid Cool from behind, the mysterious mans' strength suffocating any movement from the trapped Kid Cool. “Calm down Francis, and everything will go a lot easier, we need the both of you fit and ready for Shockwave” The man paused “Besides its thanks to me that you got into the GCW after all.” Kid Cool stopped jerking, things were starting to go blurry again but the more he relaxed the more focused things became. “Bu...” Muttered Kid Cool. Kid Cool's eye rolled upwards trying to get a sense of his attacker, but it was no use, the area was too dark, looking back at the car he could see the gentlemen still out cold on the steering wheel. “So just relax and let me prepare you for battle ahead, I needed to find a way back in Francis and at last, your my ticket...Strange really” The man paused for added effect. “Strange that it would be my son that would be the catalyst. Thankyou Francis, thankyou” And with that the man squeezed harder on the neck of Kid Cool causing him to pass out once again, and only one thing raced through the mind of Kid Cool before the dark. Failure... That's all you are... You'll never be a true champion.
