Date: Somewhere in Mexico 'You think of the craziest shit stuck in the desert.' 'Tell me about it, I live here.' 'Yeah that's gotta suck.' 'It has it ups and downs. How did you get lost out here?' Johnny shrugs non-chalantly and looks at the setting sun. 'It's a long story.' 'Hey we got time.' 'Aren't desert fox's nocturnal hunters?' 'I'm a spirit fox dummy, don't you see the wavering lines? Why do you think I'm talking?' 'I thought that was the drugs.' 'It's been weeks, the drugs aren't even in your system anymore.' Johnny crosses his legs and leans back on his hands, his shirt hangs from his head around his shoulders and his pants rolled up to his knees. His bare skin was a deep red color and his once thick mucles are looking lean and malnourished. He holds a months worth of stubble across his chin and a new set of tattoos on his arms. More than once he'd feared death, but it took more than a little sun and a nearly crippling amount of peyote to keep Johnny Lexicon down. 'So how did you get here?' The spirit fox queries once again, perched nobly on a rock in front of Lexicon. 'I guess in the end it was a woman.' 'Isn't it always?' 'I suppose, this one was bad though, let me tell you. Cold hearted bitch from hell she is, took everything from me. My house, my car, my life, and then gave it all to a fucking crack head and his pet wrestler. It was my fault, I let it all happen, before Eva and before GCW. I was the man on top, the high roller with all the right cards. I played a fresh hand with the devil and took the house, I gave Trey his ticket into the big league, I gave Jordan a fighting chance to fend off the mass of enemies he accumulated. I didn't hog the spotlight, I tried to share it with the most talented people I could find and in the end I got by fucked by the ungrateful bastards.' 'Sounds like a happy little family.' 'The name of the game was Anarch Agencies. I signed their contracts and I put them to work, I never forced anybody to sign the line, but they did. Maybe they expected me to offer them compassion, to be their mentor, this ideal leader who would make sure everybody got equal fame and money. They didn't understand that everybody has to work for what they get, they want something for nothing and I'm not prepared to pay. When Trey came back scarred and said he wanted to fight, I put him out there to fight. When Jordan was declared unfit to wrestle and asked me to help him, I gave him a new contract and let him fight. I bent over backwards to make sure those bastards got every chance they could to get famous. And once they do what do I get? A big dick in the ass, that's what.' 'Sounds painful. How did they lube?' 'With contracts, it's always with contracts. I had Trey's and I wanted Jordan's, but they didn't want me to have them, even though I offered above premium benefits.' 'What was the catch?' 'There wasn't a catch, together we stand divided we fall and all that shit, haven't you been listening? All I ever wanted was to wrestle, I dedicated my life to that bitch, I've bled, battled, won, and lost on every square inche, I was good to her and in return she was good to me. I just wanted people to be able to find what I'd found, that unity with the ring, the love of competition, and all the money and fame that came along with it. I shared my success with all those I worked with, allied or against me I'd make somebody famous and nobody ever once said thank you. All they ever wanted was more, all I ever gave was more, and now they took everything I have ever worked for. Eva took it from me. I never should have gotten with her, never should have smiled, never should have talked, never should have fucked.' The fox wavers a bit. 'Go on.' He urges. 'It was the second glance that got me, I caught her eyes and I couldn't let go. If I had kept on walking I'd still be a millionaire, I'd have a hundred girls just as beautiful and not nearly as cold. I'd have my car... damn I wish I had my car...' 'What happened to your car?' 'I don't remember exactly, I was with Jordan one minute and then the next I woke up alone. The rest is a colourful blur of self pity and blinding rage.' 'You seem pretty calm for a guy who's lost everything, sleeping in the desert at night and wrestling with those mindless thugs in town.' 'I can't live without the fight... The fight calms me down, now that it's me fighting again. The drugs I took, they made me mean... I was nearly unstoppable. Trey stopped me though, he took me to the limit, he wanted it more than I did. He hated me so bad that the thought of working with me drove him harder than I've ever seen. And I've seen Trey wrestle for a long time now. But out here, out here I'm real again, I'm not Johnny Lexicon, I'm the Canadian. I can wrestle with these guys for the passion of the fight and the money to eat, winning means a feast while losing is a night gone hungry. It's the heart of the sport that addicted me so long ago. There's no contracts, no obligations, I show up, I fight, I get paid.' 'Seems like you've got it figured out.' 'I have to appreciate the complexity of my situation. I was completely put over, and for all the bravado I spewed about being a mastermind and a wrestling legacy in the making. I completely lost sight of what I'd set out to accomplish and maybe somewhere along the line I did some things I'm not proud of. I was a career ender, for all the people I tried to help I hurt many... many more. I sacrificed my family life, I sacrificed the love of my life, I even sacrificed my morals to make it, and in the end I sacrificed everything without even knowing it. The meek shall inherit the earth or some shit like that, God said it I think, but it's true. Those that I thought meek have inherited my life's work and now enjoy the fruits of my labour. The spoils of their victory, they earned it I suppose.' 'This doesn't make you angry?' 'It should, and it does a little, but the fruits of my labour was sown and reaped on the pain of others. 3WA was a rough place, you either made it or you didn't, and the way to the top was littered with broken dreams, and dreams are alot like glass, when they break they shatter. For all that it's worth, they can have it.' 'So what are you going to do now?' 'Now I'm going to sit here and wonder how I lost tonight and plan on winning tomorrow night. One night hungry is a learning lesson, two nights is just plain incompitence. What else is there to worry about? I will survive and for once I'm at peace, there's no confusion about it, no forks in the road. For once my goals are straight and attainable.' 'But is survival all?' 'Is there more?' 'Is there?' There's a long moment of silence, Johnny uncrosses his legs and looks to the sand in quiet contemplation, his expression relaying none of his inward thoughts. 'There will always be more.' Johnny says solemly. 'The trick is figuring out how much is enough.' The fox replies, and for a moment Johnny can swear he saw the spirit fox smiling, and in the blink of an eye he was gone, leaving Johnny to ponder if he'd ever truly been there at all. After a long time, the sun had set and brought with it the chilly night air, plunging the landscape into an ominous and dangerous darkness, Johnny collected himself and got to his feet. The twinkling lights of the small desert town guiding his bare feet through the sand. Perhaps this isn't enough, he thinks when, I hope I don't step on a snake, is what he should be thinking. But there is little he can do about it now, peniless in Mexico with no way to prove his identity let alone his Canadian citizenship. If he wants more, he's going to have to do what got him here in the first place, he's going to have to play the game, he's going to have to fight harder, hurt more, and win bigger. But playing the game means risking it all over again, it means sacrificing everything all over again, it could mean ruining peoples lives all over again. Whether he is capable of making that leap again is a thing for deeper thought. Tired, hungry, and in pain, it's not something that Johnny can think about right now. What he is capable of figuring out right now is the means to an end, he needed to get out of Mexico and into a wrestling ring and there was only one person he could think of. The person responsible for all of this, the one who left him out here to die, the one fucked up enough to know what to do, the one Johnny might kill on sight. He had no way to get a hold of the guy, to make contact, but for some reason it had never been an issue before, and something told Johnny wouldn't be now either. He just had to look for him, look for... Jordan fucking White.
