WorldWide 67 Comments, some steak, and a tiger

6 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6th, 2007 The camera opened up in a lovely house. The walls were brilliantly white, floors were made of marble, and there was a staircase on both the left and right side of the entryway. The camera pushed forward as loud bass thumps came from somewhere in the house. Entering the living room, smoke was still in the air. As we round the bend we spot Global Championship Wrestling’s newest tag team duo, Class Act. The team is consisted of two unknown international stars, High Roller from Ireland, and roux, from France. They have been employed by H.G. Davis & Co. to wrestle in GCW, kind of like a sponsor. Sitting next to them on his own bright yellow futon is the founder of H.G.D.Co., the Wonder, the man they call Daddypants - H.G. Davis. Next to him is Antonio, his pet Siberian tiger, who was wearing a red handkerchief around his neck. Some may remember Davis as Ultimo Rudo in the 70’s and 80’s in Stampede Wrestling and the Royal Wrestling Alliance. Some of his sleuth of talentless chimps - Tyler Smells, The Butcher, and Julio “the Fudge” Montoya ? were also hanging around. They were playing cards with Roux. The Butcher: Go fish? could you put that out, please? I’m- High Roller, the larger and Irish half of Class Act, was smoking a cigar and reclined back onto the white leather couch. Next to him was The Butcher, coughing and gagging. He looked at Smells, exhaling a large cloud into his face. Next to H.G. sat Roux, Class Act’s French representative. High Roller: Yoo hear anything from Gloobal Championshep Wrestleng? His thick Irish accent as kind of hard to comprehend, but Davis was getting used to this. He leaned forward, throwing down three 5’s. He was dressed quite oddly enough; a gold bubble vest, a pair of ski goggles, and a pair of sweats. His hair was in pigtails, too. He was stylin?. HG: Take “em. (looking to High Roller) I have actually, my potato lovin” lackey. Roux: Does you have any zevens? The frenchman threw his cards down, lunging forward into the conversation. Antonio didn’t think it was cool though, and swiped his giant paw at Roux tossing him through the table and, ultimately, ending the game of Go Fish! All players groaned as Roux lifted himself off the splintered table. HG: Antonio, chillax. Sorry broseph, can’t make those kind of moves around him. Roux: Who has a freaking Ziberian tiger as a pet, honestly? You’re lucky your not lunch. High Roller reached his hand out and ran it across Antonio’s neck. HG turned around, giving it a T-bone steak. HG: You kiddin? Antonio’s stoned out of his mind, man. He’s probably seein? eight or nine of us right now. The Fudge: Yeah, he’s so stone- AHH! Antonio pounced on Julio, sending the couch tumbling backwards and the two into a scramble on the ground. Fudge ran. Antonio laid on the ground, pretty high still. High Roller: So what’s the plan? Davis: They’ve got you booked against the Takashi brothers on WorldWide 67. Roux: Zat’s what I’m talking about. HG picked up a joint, sparked it. He smoked it, too. HG: Whoa the colors, man! The colors! This is some dank shit... High Roller and Roux turned to one another, letting Davis do his thing. Roux: Alright laddy, we have a lot to do from here on out. Our first match of ze Class Act era. High Roller: Yoo got thet right. En fact, I think we should use thess match to warn all these bastahds just what’s in store for them once we touch down oover there. We’ll be bashin and smashin our way to the top sooner than they can spell your lest name. Roux: Zat’s not going to be so easy’actually, it most likely will. Haha, ze Takashi brothers might be oriental but zey certainly are not oriented in delivering pain on zose lame egg rolls. I once broke ze bartender’s nose for calling me ze shit. Stupid imbecile? Davis: But really, dudes, this is big. Don’t think for one minute that you’ll get paid if you lose. Just keep that in mind. I gotta keep my pimp hand strong. Both foreigners gave each other a look of concern, but shook their heads as Davis dove into a pool of Cheetos that had been set up by his scantily clad roommates. Roux: We’ve been training for months and months; we’ve got ze best teamwork I have ever seen in my life. Ze Chief has given us all ze resources to be ze best. Really? ze best. High Roller: The last partner yoo hed was found unconscious and bleeding from the eyes. Roux: Yes? well, he sucked. It was his time, as zey say. Anyway, back to ze match. I sink we really have a shot. High Roller: Of course we doo, ediot. First off, the Takashi’s are nootheng to sweat over, my web-footed French friend. Secondly, look at us. We have one of the greatest wrestlers of all time leading our campaign toward those gold belts- Roux: Ze belts! Oui! High Roller: Meanwhile, we’ll get to take out anybodah we damn well please along the way. You and me, hem (pointing at Davis) and Claire. All of us, to the top. The Foodge, too. Both members turn to the camera, focused on one thing. High Roller: Gloobal Championshep Wrestleng, we’ve got a message for yoo. If yoo thenk for one menute that there’s one team to stop, yoo better realize that et’s Class Act. Whoever you thenk is the best, simply put; isn’t. Roux: We’re going to dissect ze Takashi’s like they are part of ze ninth grade biologee lesson, piece by piece and limb by limb. It’s going to be quite ze show when we do hit ze ring, zat’s for sure. So keep your eyes peeled and your pants dry ? it’s going to get hot in here before all things are said and done. A crash was heard off camera as both members stood up quickly. Davis? legs were in the air kicking as he was grasping for emptiness. Antonio was sitting on top of HG. Davis: DUDES! Get me a teeee-booooone. The Chief hollered as both members scrambled around for a drug-laced T-bone steak. The camera faded to black as Antonio was chomping down on the piece of meat.
