Date: 12/27/09 Disclaimer: I hope that you are reading this, so you hear the news first hand. I regret to inform all readers and eyes of GCW that this will be my last RP with Zacharia Taylor. As many know, my time has been dwindling and I just do not have the extra time to write massive role plays and matches any longer. I am going to be stepping back completely and will allow my character to be used through NC-17. I wish that I had the time, because Zacharia has had a great run and I have worked so hard to get him up to the level that he is. After many changes in the fed and huge events coming and going, I know that now is a good time to leave. Someday, Zacharia Taylor may return if GCW will allow it. I know that Taylor has been through a lot, and it is hard to let go, but I hope that his accomplishments will be recognized and remembered. I hope that everyone enjoys this final role play, and I sincerely hope that you laugh at the Parody of A Christmas Carol Sincerely, Christopher Stuart ______________________________________________ Preface: It would be foolish to say this was a tale that was told to honor someone. It would also be foolish to say that this tale was told to scare all those that come in contact with the text, but I am bold enough to say that if anyone that reads this tale takes offense to their own person… Well, you are probably Scottish and I am surprised enough that you can read this text at all… ___________________________________________________________________ A Christmas Carol ___________________________________________________________________ The big doors opening up into North Pole Arena are beginning to frost up. The crystallized individual snowflakes cake onto the doors struggling for a look inside the warm sanctuary of entertainment. Through the doors, down the halls and into a locker room, Zacharia Taylor waits against a large space heater. His feet are up on a lavish foot rest and he is stretched out in an overstuffed chair, molding to his body. With a scowl on his face, he reads through “Wrestling Magazine” Zacharia Taylor has never really been a personality in GCW. He’s hung his head down through the halls and lifted them up at opportunity to threaten and scare. He’s had very few alliances and very few friends, mostly only for personal gain, never coming to the aid of others unless it resulted in personal or monetary gain. Regarded as a face, he really isn’t much of such at all. Around the corner, in the kitchen is a personal assistant appointed to him for the remainder of the day to show him around the frigid complex and let him know of his different appearances throughout the day. His name is Dan, and Dan is incredibly close to a very small personal heater which overheats every five minutes, prompting to be unplugged for a few minutes to cool down. Dan curses Taylor’s name every time it happens. Taylor took the large heater for himself, refusing to let Dan in the same room as him to benefit from the heat, instead he pointed out the small heater covered in dust on the kitchen counter. The heater stalls, and Dan curses as he unplugs the heater once again and rubs his hands on his chest to warm himself up. Suddenly, there is knock at the door and Dan stands up, his joints creaking as if frozen solid. He gets to the door and opens it up to see Kris Michaels at the door. Dan smiles at him half heartily and waves him in. Kris, a very happy friend of Zacharia’s comes around to Taylor whenever possible, but is usually shooed off before he can get even three words out. This being around the time of Christmas, his favorite holiday, he wants to extend a hand, once again, to Taylor for him to join his family for the holiday. “Zacharia, how are you doing today?” Taylor looks up from his book and grunts in response to the chipper young wrestler. Michaels, already expecting this, shrugs it off and pulls up a nearby chair to stay in conversation. “Zacharia, you know what time of year it is? Well, of course you do, but I want to extend an invitation that lays on your desk every year. Seeing you are nowhere near a desk right now, I am happy to give you this handwritten invitation to my house for Christmas Dinner!” Michaels hands over the envelope and Taylor takes it and tosses it over his shoulder. The envelop flies through the air and lands on a small desk in the corner of the room. “Oh, well, I guess I didn’t see that one there.” Kris smiles, and looks at the article that Taylor is reading. “GCW’s World Title Scene Heating Up…” Kris throws on a smirk as he sees “Zacharia Taylor” highlighted throughout the article. Either this is the second time that Taylor is reading it, or he had Dan go through the magazine and highlight his name for him. You are so self-infatuated! All you care about is yourself! I am getting sick and tired of it and you are going to come to the holiday party!” “Bah Humbug!” Kris Michaels looks up as if smacked across the face. His face instantly goes form hopeful to infuriated. “Bah Humbug? You don’t mean that! In fact, you probably do because you are a good for nothing lackluster wrestler with his sights set on a belt that he will never attain! A very large table is set with plates and food as the door opens and James von Doom and AXEL Action come on through. Several people run up to greet them with an overjoyed expression and they all make room so the two superstars of the family can make their way to the table. Once everyone is seated, Taylor makes his way to the corner of the room so he can see everyone equally. “Well, who wants to do grace this year?” A women closest to von Doom asks around. “I think I will,” Von Doom takes the responsibility and a few faces cringe around the table as they close their eyes. “Dear little boy that became a God. I want to thank you for everything this year, especially this little guy over here getting the TV and US titles! He brings a lot of joy to our hearts! He is the inspiration under my wings! He’s the stubborn hair on my right testical. He’s the overbite of Uncle Charlie!” Taylor laughs as the children begin to chuckle around the table and AXEL begins to blush. “He is truly a great guy that came out from under my wing and was able to fly out on his own! God willing, let him get that belt back in the next couple weeks so he can bring honor back to his family…AMEN!” Everyone around the table begins to eat as AXEL thanks von Doom for his kind words. Taylor focuses in on AXEL and he suddenly feels guilt for the way he treated the two of them a couple days prior. He treated them terribly, yet they still go on with a smile and hope. It feels like Taylor hasn’t had that in so long… “I wish I could say that…I wish…” “Wish ya could fuckin’ say sorry? Dumb bloke! Your words did shit else to them, even if you told them to fuck off!” “I know. I shouldn’t have done it… I know I shouldn’t have…” AXEL digs into his meal and his uncle and other stand up to pat him on the shoulder in thanks and appreciation. Taylor walks up to AXEL, ready to do the same when the room begins to spin in a vortex once again and the scene changes. Von Doom and AXEL disappear and so does the table and family. They are all replaced with a different table where a different crowd is sitting. It looks like three families are sitting together at a long table, finished with their meals. At the one head of the table is Kris Michaels, and his family and friends are around him. At the very end of the table is an untouched chair and setting. “Sweetheart, I don’t think he’s coming!” “Well,” Michaels raises his eyebrows in hope, “you never know with Zacharia. He might! One of these days he won’t be able to refuse my offer. He has a lot on his mind.” “Oh please, he’s just a son of a bitch!” “Jake! He has a lot on his mind!” Taylor goes over to the table and goes to sit down where he was supposed to, but he cannot move the chair out to do so. He looks up at the intimidating Christmas Present in front of him. “He’s making excuses for me, even though I pushed him away and cursed him off! He still set a spot for me and hopes that I will show.” Taylor is nearly in tears once more and Christmas Present just nods at him. “I messed up, Christmas Present. He didn’t want anything but my company, and look what I did! I threw him away from me and didn’t think anything of it!” “Aye, now you understand you aren’t the center of the universe!” “I know…now…” Christmas Present puts his hand on Taylor’s shoulder and goes to turn, but Taylor jumps up to his feet and steps in front of Christmas Present. “Do we have to go? I’d like to stay here for a while!” Christmas Present looks around and then nods. He takes a few steps backwards and rests against the wall. Taylor turns towards his would be family and watches them talk about gifts and trips that involved everyone. He watches Kris Michaels hand out gifts to all the children and then to the adults. Everyone opens up gifts, big and small as Kris sits back and enjoys the whole thing. He notices that all these gifts are from Michaels. Taylor didn’t get anything for anyone because of his greed and selfishness, and Taylor has more money than Michaels, the newcomer to GCW. Taylor watches the families give some cards to Michaels, but he just sets them aside for now. He gets up and starts to clear plates and bring out desert. The whole day begins to fly by as Taylor happily watches the party he should be a part of. This is where he should be. This is what he should be doing instead of just focusing on GCW and the struggle to get the belt! This is what Bagwell was telling him to do. This is the life he is missing out on… Taylor turns to the wall and sees Christmas Present getting older and darker. Christmas Present looks like he has a burden greater on his shoulders than Taylor could ever dream of. Taylor watches him out of the corner of his eye, not wanting to lose the family he now feels so a part of. After a few more moments, it is unbearable and he feels the urge to stand up and go up to Christmas Past. ___________________________________ Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come ___________________________________ Taylor gets over to Christmas Present and the scenery changes around him. He’s no longer in Kris Michaels’ house. Christmas Present doesn’t even look like he’s the same person anymore. The once proud looking Scott is now a falling apart, decrepit old man. Taylor looks down at his legs which seem to be moving, but not in a normal way… Taylor takes a step back and two children fall from Christmas Present’s legs. The two starved looking children are Ignorance and Want. They are withering away, just as Christmas Present is. Taylor stares at them, and they stare back. His eyes feel like they are falling out as he looks but they suddenly disappear and Taylor is standing there alone. He frantically looks around and then sees a shrouded figure out in the distance. He feels entranced and walks closer to the figure. The figure comes in better view, but the face cannot be seen. The figure raises a hand and points behind Taylor. All happiness is replaced with fear. He turns around and sees a bunch of people gathered around something in the center. Most of the people are standing coldy, where others talk among one another, but it doesn’t seem like any of them are genuinely upset or happy. Taylor gets closer and sees a coffin. The casket is closed, so Taylor looks all over for some kind of indication as to who it is. He listens to the idle chatter, but picks up on nothing. “Who is in there?” The shrouded figured doesn’t say a word. Taylor looks all over for any indication, but the more he looks, the more he listens, the more he has a feeling he knows who lies inside the casket. Someone is talking about finances and begins to laugh at the word “millions”. Taylor hears “died of stress” and “kept to himself” and “drove himself mad” from people gathered around. Finally, Taylor falls to his knees in front of the tombstone. Zacharia Taylor Died 12/23/13 Taylor begins to lose it, crying hysterically. He turns to his right to see everyone is gone, but the shrouded figure is standing right next to him. He reaches for his ankles, but something unseen prevents him from touching the figure. Taylor now understand what he has been put through. He understands that Past and Present showed him happiness and what matters in life. He knows what he must do. He know what he must do to end up alive. He know that Chris Bagwell wants him to learn from his mistakes! HE WANTS TO LIVE! Taylor is then lifted up by the shrouded figure and is thrown backwards at alarming speeds. Taylor flies through a blur and then hits a soft surface. _____________________________________ The New Me __________________________________ Taylor jumps up and looks around, noticing that he is on the couch in the locker room. He frantically looks around for any indication that someone is around when he hears someone cough in the other room. He runs out to see Dan sitting there next to his broken heater with chattering teeth. Dan sees Taylor and pretends to be busy, but Taylor gets in front of him wish a panicked face, alarming Dan. “What are you doing?” “Um, working for you, Taylor.” Taylor looks confused as if he asked the wrong question. “What time is it?” “Noon, sir. Noon on Christmas Day…” Taylor smiles a maddening smile and puts a hand on Dan’s arm. “Really?” “Yes. Sir, are you ok?” Taylor jumps up and hugs Dan. “Merry Christmas, Dan!” Dan is more surprised than anyone and hugs back, but cautiously. “Dan, go home and spend the time with your family. You don’t need to be here. Go! Go home!” Taylor beats him out of the door and sees Kris Michaels on his way out of the stadium. “Kris! Kris! Are you heading home?” Kris Michaels turns around with a surprising smile. “Yes, care to join?” Kris starts to walk away, already expecting the answer… “Absolutely! I just need to run to the store first! I need to pick something up!” “Err, what? You will? That’s great! No, you don’t need anything! Just bring yourself! That is all we need!” Taylor smiles and puts his arm around Kris and walks out the doors with him into the cold. Kris motions towards his car and they both get in. “Kris, I’m very sorry. I’m sorry I rejected you these past years. I know you were there for me when I needed you, and I forgot, but now I remember, and I want to make it up to you. Please tell me that you can forgive me!” Kris smiles a warm smile before saying.. “Of course Taylor. Of course. Wow, everyone is going to be so happy to see you Taylor.” “Really?” “Trust me, they already have your seat picked out!”
