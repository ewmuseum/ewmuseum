Hidden Desires

13 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/06 There are things in life that define people. Things that slowly mold and shape them into the people they are, and someday will be. To some those points and instances come with welcome arms. They know that someday it'll make them better. Either they'll learn a lesson from something bad or they're revel in the memory of something good. This is the philosophy that some use to get through their lives. But the unfortunate thing is that it's not always that way to all. There are some that see the latest 'failure' as they would put it as a turning point in their life. They see it as something that will keep them from things that they truly want. One of these people is Grayson Brand, ?Gray? to his friends, allies and just about everyone else. He was named for the strange anomaly from his birth. While being a full blooded, for all intents and purposes, he had a strange hair and eye color. This was a foreshadow though. For from his mother he inherited the ability to become something more then human. His mother was from a line of beings that has intermingled with humans many centuries ago, called the Garou by their own kind. Now in his twenty-ninth year, he reflected... why me. All was fine up until about eight years ago. The year he entered into majority was the year he lost his life as he knew it. He had been married for three years already, with a son that had been born from his wife, some surprised to know that she was not a native woman herself. But he was in love. What changed everything for him? His take on his mothers side. When he first assumed the form of the Garou. He was appalled by it. Especially by the rage that built up inside of him. This was a man who had earned his Bachelors in Science, specializing in Biology from the University of Washington, specializing in the biology of ancient cultures. He was supposed to be an intellectual, not some anger driven creature bent on destroying, and eating, what ever was in front of it. This has scared Gray to the point that he pushed... he pushed his wife, the woman he loved and mother of his child away. They were divorced a year later.... That's when he finally followed his brother's suggestions and talked to the Order, whom his mother was a researcher for. Since then he's become a field operative for them. Collecting data on cases and using his biology to a benefit. But his anger and rage have always been his downfall. These have isolated him at the rank of Sergeant for a reason. And then you have the actions of the other night in Osaka. After being freed from the witch's spell, his anger took him over. He changed to the full on form of his 'kind' and attacked what he thought was a danger to their charge, the woman he considers a little sister (and if his brother has his way, that'll be true), Dani Northsky. But it wasn't a threat, and he wasn't thinking, as usual. No, it was her sixth great grandfather, the angel known as Kokabel, The Star of God, or as her people, the Sioux had called him: Morning Star. Without thinking, Gray had engaged him and ended up being tossed, and then forced back to his more 'human' form. He'd been owned, that was for sure. Humility is a strange bedfellow when you least expect it. Gray wasn't an all powerful thing that he sometimes felt himself becoming. Twice now this 'gift' from his mother's side had almost cost them the fight. Gray wanted it to be the last time. But what could he do to get that to be the case? ~A Quiet Place to be Alone~ ~Somewhere outside of the Ryokan that the KB:NAN has been staying in since coming to Japan~ Gray sat there on a large stone, a boulder to be more exact. His legs were bent up in front of him. He wasn't even trying to look dignified about it all. His short, cropped, hair was blowing slightly from a small breeze being carried from the direction of the capital city, Tokyo. In a few days, he and his brother would be getting into the ring with a few others, including two of his own stablemates. To be more exact, not only stablemates, but his current commanding officer and his fianc?e. Any other time he would have thought this was a good thing, the rules of the Order were simple: When you face a member of the Order, you fight with honor. But recently while trapped under that witch's spell, he'd seen what both were capable of. Blaise was a killing machine, pure and simple. A mixture of both two ancient cultures bundled in one with the training of a third. Like himself, Blaise was a member of the Garou from one side of his family. The other was something more spiritual. But then you had the graceful and effective Ukyo, also a killing machine. This was something that he worried about. Could he hold his temper against such opponents. He knew that his brother was bad enough when he got frustrated, but Blaise and Ukyo could move in ways that Gray could only dream of. Team Baked would not be a problem. Josh and Gray had already faced and defeated them in their first match in Global. All you needed to do was bring a cookie sheet out for them to take 'snacks' from and they'll get themselves counted out or something. The others? Did they even really rate yet? Gray hadn't paid any attention to them. His thoughts really weren't on the match anymore. They were on his personal life. Once again he'd realized that he is the weakest link. He sat there, looking at the last remnants of his past life, his life before his 'curse' took him over. A couple of pictures of those things he'd loved before... his wife and son. He looked at them intently, her brown hair and delicate features. His sons strong jaw, like his own and steel gray eyes.... but he didn't have the hair. But he couldn't remember the good things. He could only remember the feelings of what 'could happen' if he were to loose control and change around them. He could only think of the day he feared that he could have killed even his own loved ones and not realize it. ?I see your off to brooding again?? Gray startled a bit. He tucked the pictures down into his palm, and got ready to react to the surprise visitor. It was only his brother though, walking out of the forest into the clearing. He was coming from downwind, a tactic that only a true native would know... how to sneak up on a wolf. ?It's all I've got left, so you can kiss off, Little Cub.? Little Cub or Little Wolf, the nickname he had for his brother, denoting him as the younger, smaller and least mature of the two. Something that Joshua never contended to protest either. ?All you've got left? Give me a break.? Gray looked up at his brother with a bit of distaste. Where could he come off like that? He had Dani, he had everything. He still had his humanity with one small exception that was considered a benefit to have. He, like his mother was an empath. The only thing they seemed to have gotten from their father... well he carried just about every recessive gene that he could, in a biological way. He reinforced the two things that would make his sons...different. ?Yeah, it's all I've got left. I'm a monster, if you've not noticed there Josh. I almost fucked the mission up the other night. Why? Because I couldn't control myself. After eight years of this 'curse' I'm still learning just as much as a cub is. I'm a whelp in terms of others of my kind.? ?But you didn't grow up with your kind. You learned from the Order. You were their first Garou they picked up, ever. You're not going to be perfect. Lord knows it's not any of our lots in life. Now let me tell you something you'd tell me on a regular basis: get your head out of your ass.? ?What's that supposed to mean?? Josh gave him as serious a look as he could. ?Get over it. Get over Jeannie, get over the other night. Get over everything and get ready for the match of our careers. I need your head on straight that night.? Gray didn't take kindly to some of those words. ?How dare you say such things!? ?Hey, when life gives you a shit sandwich, fertilize your fields man. Manure makes things better, you know. So you fucked up? Big deal. Eventually you'll get the hang of it. It's what you are.? ?How? I'm not Blaise.? Josh shook his head on that one, his long raven black hair flipping over his shoulders. ?A bit of jealousy there? You finally got to see how good he is, huh?? ?I'm not jealous.? ?What? He's got the girl of his dreams. He's a hunter like you wanted to become but they deemed you too uncontrollable. He's an officer, some say because of his name. But we've seen it's his ability. And his girl can kick ass just as well as he can.? ?I'm not jealous of envious of him. I'm just not happy with how things are going.? ?Get over it. Shit happens.? Gray stood up, making sure he kept the pictures clenched tightly. ?You have no idea what it's like do you?? But that was apparently enough for Josh to be set off... Joshua Bodaway Brand. While his middle name wasn't Saquamish Indian, like he was, it was Sioux, but it met him well: ?Fire?. Yes, his very moniker of ?Fire? Brand was his real name. And as an empath, when confronted with hostility, he was all to apt to return it. His hand went up to his hairline as his forehead to pull it all back out of his face as he looked at his bigger, both physically and chronologically, brother. ?I don't understand?? Gray said nothing, he really didn't get a chance to. ?I don't understand, exactly what, Grayson? What it's like to be different? What it's like to be the odd man out?? Gray just nodded. ?I'll tell you what, you're an amateur when it comes to those feelings. You know why I say that?? ?Why?? ?Because unlike you, who ran and hid, I coped. I dealt with it. You're the one who doesn't know. You don't know what it's like to walk into a new class room and as soon as they see you, with your dark skin, and larger nose... pure unadulterated hatred like a wall. Not see it, smell it or anything like that, but FEEL it. You don't know what it's like to be able to feel what everyone thinks of you. To know what their true inner feelings are. To know what would amount to their innermost secrets and not be able to react when someone is hurt, because it would give it away. Or to know when they're not something that should be around you, yet no one else listens to you.? ?Josh...? ?And you don't know how I feel right now...? Gray stopped. He couldn't help it. ?Once again, I'm the odd man out. Look at you. You're Dani's assigned Guardian by the Order. You're the one charged with her protection. You're the one she has to rely on, not me.? ?You did well enough against Jaymes.? ?Yeah, well the other night... I was even more useless then you. The only way I could have messed it up would have been by reacting. I'm not capable of doing the things that any of you can. I'm the odd man out yet again! Why? Because other then one mental trick, I'm the odd man out. I'm normal! You're a Garou. Blaise is whatever he is, Ukyo is some kick ass whirling death. And we're all here because of Dani. So tell me, Brother, where do you get off telling me that I don't know what it's like to be different?? Gray backed down a bit. He knew his brother had grown up and had faced things that he'd only recently faced. His brother had been brought up in the metaphysical world due to his mother working with the Order. ?You don't know what I'd give to be just like the rest of you right now.? Gray couldn't believe that statement. ?What? You're nuts.? ?No I'm not.? ?Why?? ?Think about it Grayson. Think about it.? Gray's facial expression showed confusion. Josh let him off the hook. ?I thought you were the scientist. Think about it! In sixty years I'll be pushing up daises. I'll be worm food. And the woman I love will look as good as she does now. You forced yours away... because of the uncertainty of how she'd take your change. I have to think about the fact that after I'm gone, who else will have her?? Gray reached out to take his brother's shoulder. But Josh batted it away. ?I don't need pity. I need you ready to get into the ring this week. Nothing less, nothing more. So get your head out of your ass and be ready. If there is one thing in my life I need to count on, it's my big brother. Don't let me down.? Gray wanted to ask him how he could say that. How he could claim to count on him for anything. But he didn't. Instead his face went long. His brother had put away the rage, the monster for a while at least. In it, he'd learned the biggest fear and desire of his sibling at the same time. But he couldn't answer him. ?I won't. I promise.? ?Good. Finish up here, and say goodbye.? At that, Josh started walking away, back towards the Ryokan Inn. Gray looked down at the pictures in his hand one more time. He fanned them out so as to take them in all at once. One a picture of his wife, one a picture of his son at the age of three, a picture of both of them together.... and a fourth picture... A woman who at the time had dyed black hair with other colors in it. She's sitting at a table, a plate of food in front of her and a few small drinks, coffee and some sort of soda. She's half smiling, the resolution looks like it was taken with a camera phone. A woman who's now in GCW with them. A woman who at any time could end up on the side of the enemy of ally.. a woman who has shown that she could handle his side of things and is not afraid of him. A woman who at least gives him the time of day when they speak... Her first name is Dawn, you can take it from there. ?Sorry, Josh. But 'goodbye' is forever. And I can't do that, especially when in some cases I've not even really been able to say hello, properly.? He slid the pictures back into his pocket and started off after his brother.
