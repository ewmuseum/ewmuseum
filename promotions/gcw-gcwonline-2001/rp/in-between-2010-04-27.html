In-Between

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/27/10 "WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?" The voice of Dirk Dickwood boomed around the locker room. Phil Atken had just returned from his Rampage interviewer and his own agent was seething. The awkwardness left lingering in the room left many around the room making quick exits, Phil Atken included. Dirk however cut Phil off before he could reach the door. Dickwood: So you're not going to explain yourself? Atken: I've got nothing to explain. Dickwood: Nothing to explain? You just removed yourself from contention of a bloody tournament that could result in you actually meaning something in this company and you have nothing to explain? Bullshit! Atken: It's not bullshit, everything I said out there I meant. I'm not going to stand there and let another Andy Murray happen at my expense. Dickwood: That is such a ball of bullshit, the ball is rolling down a hill of bullshit, gathering more bullshit at a rapid pace, reaching it's final destination as a giant ball of bullshit. A bullshit katamari if you will. Atken: A katawhat? Dickwood: Katamari. Atken: Sure, repeating it will clear my confusion right up. Dickwood: The point is you didn't even come and talk to me about this. The double point is that it's fucking idiotic to avoid entering Rampage. Atken: I didn't talk to you about it because I knew you would try and force my hand, by being passive aggressive I get to do what I want instead. Dickwood: And what exactly is it you want to do? Wallow around in a giant void of no career for the foreseeable future? Instead of answering Dirk's rather pointed question, Phil tried once again to head for the door but due to Mr Dickwood's rather rotund nature, he managed to continue his door blockade. Dickwood: You're not going to explain yourself? In all the time I've known you, you've been a loudmouth, a braggart, an idiot, an occasional misogynist but I've never once seen you actually refuse a chance to prove yourselves as one of the best this industry has to offer. I've never once seen you run away from a chance at glory... Atken: What's glorious about entering Rampage? Another opportunity to notch up another loss on the ole GCW belt? I really feel like re-living 2008 again, I really want to sit there and watch guys who came to this company after me skyrocket to success while I'm looking to even get booked on a show. Rampage 2008 marked the true point where I went from actually meaning something in this industry to becoming completely expendable. It marked the point where I felt so worthless that I didn't even put up a fight when my mother demanded I return home and take care of her. I'm not in a rush to relive that moment. Dickwood: Then you try and enter the Rampage tournament and redeem your name. Atken: Or I don't and avoid becoming a punchline again, I avoid looking at whoever this tournaments Chris Bagwells and Andy Murrays are while that awful thought pings at the back of my mind: that could have been me. I can't and won't let that happen again and the safest way to avoid that is doing exactly what I just did. Dickwood: What ARE you going to do then? Atken: I have a few things in mind. Dickwood: You don't think your terrible radio segment is going to give you any weight in the company do you? Atken: Hey, the people value my advice. At least one person has approached me to say he listened to the show. Dickwood: Did he like it? Atken: He said he listened to it, I don't think I can make that any more clear. Dickwood: You need more than that. That's not going to change to way people view you as a talent and you know as well as I do that you are a talented wrestler. I don't know what went wrong a few years back when you decided you'd rather be a joke and pick up a quick buck. I don't know why I allowed that to happen. I don't know why I allowed you to go to NFW and hang around with a midget dressed as me and a woman dressed as a viking, I don't know why I played along with your HILARIOUS shenanigans in this very company, I don't know why I let you get out of shape and become a joke in the ring. Hell, I don't even know why I'm still your agent. Atken: You're thinking about this too much, we don't need Rampage to be successful in GCW, I'll find a way and all I'm asking from you is to trust me. You know how much it means to me to return to wrestling, I'm not going let all this go to waist and all I need from you is a little trust that I know what I'm doing. I... we don't need the hassles of that tournament to succeed here. As I said, I've got something else in mind. Now, can you let me out? I'm done for the night. Dickwood: I still think this is a huge gaffe but have it your way. Dirk moved away from the door, letting Phil and his travel case make their exit from the arena for the night. Dirk sat down in the empty locker room, rubbing his temples, frustrated at what he viewed as Phil's lack of interest in his own career. He knew there was only one way to solve this problem and he knew exactly how to do it. Dirk jumped up out of his seat, ran to the door and peeked out the corridor. Safe in the knowledge that Phil Atken had left the building, he grabbed his mobile phone. Dickwood: Dirk Dickwood here, could you possibly send an interviewer over to the locker room, I wish to make a statement on behalf of my client. ... Dickwood: Oh I know about Johnny's policy for this show but I promise it'll only take a minute. ... Dickwood: Don't worry about it I've already cleared it. ... Dickwood: Five minutes? Great.
