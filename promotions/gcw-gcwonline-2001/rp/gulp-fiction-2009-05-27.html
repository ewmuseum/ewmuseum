*Gulp* Fiction

27 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27/2009 A ray of sunlight peeks through the blinds of my hotel room, landing directly on my face, waking me from a dreamless sleep. I squint my eyes, letting them adjust to the morning. Stretching, my arm touches skin next to me in the bed, startling me a bit. Looking over, I see a blonde, still fast asleep. I chuckle to myself, remembering the previous evening. I look to the other side of the bed, where a brunette lies, also asleep. Both of them are nude, the blonde's tit resting casually on her arm. It's good to be on top. I slide out of bed carefully, so as not to wake the sleeping beauties, and head to the window. Staring out from the 32nd floor down onto the Vegas Strip, my stomach rumbles. I’m damn hungry. I turn and walk over to the desk where the room service menu lies and start browsing through it. After a few moments I decid on the waffles with strawberries and whipped cream, eggs over easy and whole wheat toast. They told me oit was going to be about twenty minutes, so I banked on a half hour so I wouldn't get frustrated. I'm easily irritable when I'm hungry, even when I wake up in a good mood. I started thinking over the events of the previous day, trying to piece things together. Tigers, briefcases full of money, girls, alcohol -- it was quite a bit to handle. And then there was Mark Jackman, the guy who ran into us on the Strip. He told us about our crazy night. None of it was as elaborate as he had made it sound when we first met him -- all pretty normal stuff for Fish and I. Girls, alcohol, crazy antics. The part that had me baffled was this briefcase full of money that he kept mentioning we had. If we had it, where was it now? My phone buzzes. A text. "where u at?" Fisher, while relatively well spoken in person, had gotten on the text lingo train along with every other 13 year old girl in the world. I hate that he doesn't use punctuation. It really isn't that difficult. I text back "The hotel room" and put my phone back in my pocket. Walking back over to the bedroom, the girls have begun to stir a little bit, probably realizing my body wasn't resting between them, and they were now spooning each other. I'm sure they'd done it before with other chicks, but to wake up that way expecting this rock hard body -- well, you understand. The blond stretched as she sat up, then noticed me in the doorway watching them. "What are you doing, Paul?" I told them I was Paul Walker. How else was I supposed to get them BOTH back to my room? "Just talking to my agent, darling. Had some business to attend to." "Oh! Were you talking to him about our parts?" she said as I sat down on the bed beside her. Oh. Right. I also told them I'd get them a role in Fast and Furious Part 10. So I lied, sue me. "Yeah, babe. He said he's going to talk to his people and see what we can get worked out." I brush her hair back behind her ear. My phone buzzes in my pocket again, and I pull it out and press the text button. I realize I'd connected a call. I fucking hate that. I go to look at a text, but it's a phone call, so I answer the phone without even knowing it, and most of the time I don't even want to talk on the phone. Thankfully for me, it was Fisher. He’s already talking by the time the phone reaches my ear. I motion to the blonde that I had to take the call and left the room. "...briefcase isn't full of money, man. We're in some serious shit here." I caught the last half of the sentence. "Woah, woah, woah. Run that by me one more time?” "Get your damn dick out of your ear and pay attention you stupid fuck,” he says, obviously annoyed. "I said, I found the briefcase, and it's not full of fuckin’ money. You need to look at this." I roll my eyes. "Dude, seriously, I've got waffles with strawberries coming and two really hot chicks ready for morning-after sex. Can't you just tell me what's in it?" There was a long pause on the phone before he finally responds. That meant he was really mad at me. He only does this when he wants to spare my feelings from the verbal assault he wants to unleash. Such a great friend. "Dice?" "Yeah, Fish?" "I'll be up in fifteen." Click. *~* Knock. Knock. Knock. I unlock the door to let Fisher in, but no sooner does the door click open, does Fisher come flying into the room holding a silver briefcase. He sets it down on a glass table in the front room. "Dude, what the hell's wrong wit--" He holds up his hand, cutting me off. "Where are the girls?" "In bed still, but I'm still not sure what the he--" Again, my sentence was cut off as he walked back into the bedroom, me hot on his heels. "Ok girls, playtime is over, time for you to go,” he says, aggressively yanking the sheet off them. "Dude, what the hell?!" I say. "Shut. Up. Tyson. I'll explain in a minute." The blond turns to me, her face changing from fear to irritation. "Tyson?" I drop my head. "Fuck." Fisher squints his eyes, confused at what the problem is. Then, a light goes off in his head and he turns to the girls. "Paul Walker? Fast and Furious 10?" They both nod. I back away slowly. "Yeah. That's his usual play." "Ugh!" "Great, thanks Fish." Both girls storm out of the room, slamming the door behind them. Fisher walks back into the front room, sits down and grabs the plate with the waffles on it. He shoves a big bite into his mouth. "Dude, I haven’t even gotten to eat any yet!" I walk in front of him, grabbing the plate out of his hands and throw it on the table. "You just cost me two really hot girls and now you want to eat my breakfast?" I flip him off. "Fuck you. Tell me what's going on." He nods, grabs the briefcase and snaps it open. Then he slides it around so I can see what's inside. Instantly my demeanor changes. Talk about a paradigm shift. "What the fuck..." I mutter under my breath. I've never seen anything like it before. "Well, now that I have your attention," he says. WHACK. I wince, my head snapping forward in reaction to Fisher's slap. "You ever fuckin’ doubt me when I'm serious again, I won't slap next time," he says. "Will you spank?" "Not now, dude." "All right, geez, just trying to lighten up the mood a bit." I chuckle half-heartedly, my eyes moving back to the briefcase. The two of us sit in silence for a few moments, taking in the contents of the briefcase. "So, do we know whose it is?" I say. "Not a clue. But whoever owns that" -- he points to the briefcase -- "isn't exactly someone I'm dyin’ to meet." Something catches my eye right at that moment, and I instinctively move into action, diving towards Fisher and tackling him to the ground just as the window next to us expodes into a million pieces. I land on top of him and he looks up at me. There's a brief moment of awkwardness before I leap back away from him, still staying hidden behind the couch. "What the fuck was that?!" he says. "All I saw was a little red dot on you, and you know me..." Fisher finishes the sentence for me. "Always assuming the worst. Shit, man. I'm glad you did that time." "Think he's still out there?" "Probably. He knows he missed." "Who the fuck is trying to kill us man?" I won't lie. I'm a bit scared at this point. A fuckin' really big bullet just came flying through a plate-glass window, shattering it. Who wouldn't be scared? "Fuck if I know dude." Thankfully the door is only a few feet away and mostly blocked by the couch we were behind at the moment. I grab the briefcase off the table and we both make a mad dash for it. A moment later, we’re both outside the room. Not safe, but at least outside. "I think it's time to get the hell out of here," Fisher says. "Um, Fish?" He turns. "Yeah?" "What's Mezzonote’s first name?" He looks at me blankly. "Jason, why?" I point at the bottom of the briefcase, where the letters J.M. are inscribed neatly in a frilly script font. Fisher closes his eyes. "Fuck." "I'm guessing this isn't a good thing?" I ask, sarcastically. "We need to get hidden. Like yesterday." Fuck my life.
