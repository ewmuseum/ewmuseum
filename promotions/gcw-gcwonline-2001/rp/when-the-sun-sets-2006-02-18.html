When The Sun Sets

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 Feb 06 The story I am about to tell you is as tragic as it is exciting. It is full of greed, love, lust, hatred, and most importantly REVENGE. It’s a story that makes you look deep down inside of yourself and question what you are truly made of. When you lose everything, your very purpose for being do you continue to fight? Or do you give in to the temptation to end it? These are the questions that our Main Character had to ask himself. These questions haunted him for most of his life. His story began a long, long time ago. July 14th 1863 A courthouse Obviously not even remotely close to the ones that one would think of in our own time. The place wasn’t well kept to out standards but for the time it was nice enough. The courtroom did not appear to be able to hold that many people, and on normal days it certainly would not. Today was no ordinary day. On this day the conviction of the World’s most notorious Outlaw, Martin Smalls, would be passed down. The judge sat high on his pedestal staring down at the people In front of him. The entire room was quiet, a hair falling and crashing to the ground could be heard. The Judge’s eyes narrowed at the “defendant” and his smug look. It was the look that rarely seemed to change on his face. “Martin Smalls you are found Guilty of the crimes of Armed Robbery, first and second degree murder, kidnapping, rape, and blackmail. Today this court sentences you to death, to be carried out tomorrow at dawn.” The gavel slams down on the wooden desk in front of him and the people in the courthouse begin to cheer. That look on Martin Smalls? face doesn’t change even as he is led out of the courthouse. Families and friends of some of the victims that Martin had gotten his hands on were the ones in attendance, searching for and believing they finally received justice. The judge slides down from his seat and moves amongst the crowd. He makes a straight line for a certain man in particular. A man wearing a badge, a cowboy hat, and two pistols strapped to each side. The sheriff, Roberto Abitua, also the arresting officer in the Smalls case. Even at first glance it was very easy to see why he was chosen as Sheriff. Standing 6’6? and more than likely near 300 lbs. he was a mountain of a man, intimidating to many. His bright blue eyes seemed to glow as the judge approached and in a show of respect, Sheriff Abitua pulls off his hat showing off his short black hair with sweat making it appear to shine. “Good day Judge Caldera.” The Sheriff says with a smile. “It’s a great day Roberto, because of you this world is a far safer place now.” “I was just doing my job Judge, a job I will continue to do as long as there is breath in these lungs.” Roberto winks. “For that we will always be thankful.” The two shake hands. “Now go have yourself a few drinks. You are a hero tonight.” “Thank you Caldera.” Roberto pats Caldera’s shoulder. “See you later.” Sheriff Abitua was well respected and admired. He fought for the people because he knew that they would do the same for him. If someone needed help Roberto had no problems being that man. Tonight though it was his night. For a very long time Martin Smalls had made people’s lives a living hell. There was never anyone to stand up and face him, until Roberto stepped into the Sheriff’s position. He just could not stand by and watch as innocent people were murdered, young girls were raped, children were kidnapped, and families were robbed of everything they held dear. Martin Smalls preyed on the fearful, and that disgusted Roberto. He was searching for Martin for close to three years. Finally all of his hard work came to fruition. Roberto knew it was going to be dangerous, even knew that there was a chance that he would not come back. Martin after all was a very powerful man. But he couldn’t stop, he wouldn’t stop. The thought of one day it being his wife being raped or his baby girl being kidnapped drove him to go on. His family was his life and he would not simply wait until their number was called. Roberto stood in the middle of the street, dust and dirt blowing all around and watched the sun set behind the horizon. It was always a beautiful thing to behold in his eyes. It signified the end but also a whole new beginning. Darkness engulfed the small town of “Knight Fall” for only a moment before the lights all flickered on. As was normal with the old west, the towns were full of gambling spots, Saloons, bars, etc. People flocked to these night spots and on this night Roberto would join them. As soon as he stepped inside he was greeted by numerous hugs and handshakes. From behind the wall of people came a man that even made Roberto look small. David Sandy or as he preferred to go by, RaZ. He was Roberto’s childhood friend and also served as deputy. The two men embrace, with David quickly handing Roberto a cigar and a glass of scotch. They walk towards the bar and take a seat. “A Hero’s welcome.” RaZ said with a wink. “I guess so.” Roberto takes a drink. “There was no way I could allow that man to stay free. He hurt a lot of people.” “I know.” RaZ offers a light, which Roberto gladly accepts. “But you are far too modest. This is a great accomplishment.” “The town deserves peace. We all deserve peace.” “I’d prefer to get drunk first.” The two share a laugh, a good hearty laugh. Roberto takes a puff of his cigar and lets a cloud of smoke out and mix in with the rest of the smoke lingering in the air. He shakes his head at the comment. “You never were good with serious talk.” “And you never learned how to lighten up. But don’t worry amigo I’ll teach you someday.” “Is that a threat?” “Here,” RaZ lifts his glass. “To a great man, to peace, and to happiness.” “I’ll drink to that.” They both down their glasses and slam them down on the bar top. They continue to talk, their laughing, and their jokes. It is borderline amazing that in a time with so much violence, blood shed, and crime that they could find happiness in most anything. We take life for granted even now but back then you were just one wrong look away from being shot in a public place, and in most cases there would be no arrest. There would be no conviction. There were no Heroes and there was no law. Sheriff Roberto Abitua tried being all that. He slammed down another drink with his best friend. Before another drink could be ordered though Sheriff Abitua declined. “Sorry bud I got a family to go see. A wife to ? well ? that’s none of your business.” Roberto said with a wink. “Aww come one just one more?” “No thanks, but you have a good night.” They give each other a quick hug. “I will see you bright and early tomorrow morning.” Roberto begins making his way through the Saloon, being stopped by random people who give their appreciation for the day’s events. It’s a breath of fresh air for Roberto as he steps outside. A cool breeze moves through the town and the sky is as clear as ever. He looks up into the sky and admires the beauty of the stars. To him life couldn’t get much better. He loved his job, he had a beautiful wife, and an incredible young daughter, his people loved him, and his town was on the verge of complete peace. It was like a dream, the most wonderful dream. Therein laid the problem however. Because you see dreams always find a way to end, sometimes they are very abrupt. As with every sunset, something must end. On this night a man’s most wonderful dream would turn into his most feared nightmare. Sure the sun would come back up from beyond the horizon again but in this man’s eyes, darkness would forever cloud his world. On this night a Hero would die. Giving birth to someone else? Something else.
