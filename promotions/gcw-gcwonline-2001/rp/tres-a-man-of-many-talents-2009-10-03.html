Tres - A Man Of Many Talents

3 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 3rd 2009 The first pay-per-view appearance for the man from Mexico hadn’t gone exactly to plan. Sure, he’d looked good in the match and managed to show off the variety of his arsenal and sure, he’d dominated the closing stages of the match. The problem was that the final 3 seconds he was involved in led to his shoulders being pinned to the canvas and not those of one Cade Carson. All-in-all, it was an impressive showing despite the loss, even more so when we consider that it was only has second match in GCW. With Dangerous Games fast approaching, the best and most sensible course of action would be to take the loss in stride and plan for the monster battle royale at that show, particularly as the winner of that match grabs a guaranteed shot at the world title. Unfortunately, Jorge Ramos has never been known as a calm and collected kind of guy and after the seeing his protégé fall at Warpath, he was certain that the fates were against them and that M3 would quickly would be ejected from the GCW ranks, leaving all three of their motley crew stranded in America with no jobs, no money and no prospects. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Back at the ranch, M3 and Super Monito were surprisingly unruffled by the dire proclamations that were being spouted by Jorge. They trained as they usually did, watched daytime TV as usual and spent the same inordinate amount of money on Oreos. On this particular occasion, Jorge was busy scanning the job pages while his compatriots were interested in only one thing - Saved By The Bell. Even with her early-90s hairdo, Tiffani Thiessen was smoking hot and for two people that had never seen ‘Screeched’, Dustin Diamond was still a goofy comedy figure. With every word Zack Morris spoke, Jorge’s blood pressure rose a little. Eventually he could take no more. ‘Alright! Tha’s it! Es enough!’ The newspaper was sent flying, as were the two bottles of Corona that the Unknown Luchador and his simian sidekick had been enjoying. They didn’t react, other than glancing briefly up at Jorge, before quickly returning their eyes to the screen. ‘No! Joo are gonna listen joo lazy perros!’ Again, a quick glance up, then back to the screen. ‘Joo don’t wanna make me do this…’ Still no response, leaving Jorge with only one option left… The instant the screen goes black, he has their undivided attention. ‘Bueno.’ As he smoothes his clothes down he regains a little of his composure and the little vein that had been popping out of his forehead slowly starts to recede. ‘Es hard situación now. We no have TV belt, we no have next tiro. We fight contra Señor Labor – es esencial to win.’ M3 gives one of his classic shoulder shrugs, this one conveying the message ‘Yeah, we know that’, while Super Monito tries to will the TV back on through the power of his mind. ‘Es muy importante to win for our hope but no just for that…’ Sighing, Jorge shares with them what he has known ever since the win bonus failed to materialise at Warpath. ‘…We need the dinero. We are broke!’ *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ And so, Jorge was forced to enter one of the most inhospitable environments known to man, a place where many have been cut down in their prime, chewed up and spat out like tobacco. A place where even the most assured, confident and educated have been brought to tears. Yes, that’s right. Jorge was going to get a job. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘Do you have any prior experience?’ ‘Si.’ The interviewer looks up from his pad inquisitively. ‘Yes? Okay then, where was that.’ ‘The Ambassador Hotel, in Mexico City. I work there, 3 years. Junior Chef, very big queso in city!’ The look on the woman’s face says it all. ‘The Ambassador Hotel, sounds impressive. I assume we can get references if needs be?’ Maybe it was the language barrier that caused Jorge to pause, the extra few seconds needed to translate his words into English only serving to heighten the bad news. ‘Oh no, es very bad. The hotel, it go on fire, fire in kitchen. It not there no more. Es very bad.’ ‘Hmmm…’ And that was that interview. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘Everything looks very good here Mr Ramos, just a couple more things to clear up.’ Jorge nods eagerly. ‘Are you looking for full or part-time hours?’ ‘Not all time.’ ‘…So part-time then?’ ‘Si.’ ‘…And can you work weekends?’ ‘No. No weekend, no evening. No Friday, Monday or Thursday también.’ There went another one. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ ‘It says here you’ve worked in sales before, why don’t you tell me a little about that?’ ‘Of course, I sell lots of things in the past, very successful, servicio number one!’ ‘…Okay, but what did you sell?’ ‘Oh, sorry, I no understand first time! I sell many things – marijuana, heroin, cocaine. All very bueno!’ It was a good thing the interviewer was sitting down. *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Three interviews down and no closer to gaining paid employment, Jorge was getting despondent. It was particularly tough, as his only failings had been his honesty. Well, that and his total lack of interview technique. Making small-talk by commenting on the roundness of the interviewer’s breasts isn’t seen as endearing after all, apparently. Still, if there was one thing Jorge had learned from the gods of television and cinema, it was that America is the land of opportunity, where anyone can get a job doing anything with just a little commitment. Even in the current climate, where jobs are about as plentiful as straight men in the fashion industry, Jorge was keeping the faith, cold-calling shops, bars, restaurants, animal shelters, cab firms, slaughterhouses and every other place of business within a 10 mile radius, trying to find some opening that would bring in a little money and keep his ragtag group’s heads above the water. It was ironic, then, that it was during a short break from this constant cycle that inspiration hit him… *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Whatever had happened in the last few days, it had dramatically altered Jorge’s mood. Gone was the nervous, panicked, stressed man who had been dominating the apartment with his declarations of doom and back was the easy-going Jorge, loving life north of the border and feeling positive about M3’s chances at WorldWide this week. So much so that he’d decided to treat them to a day at Universal Studios. To say that Super Monito was excited would be an understatement. From the second they had walked through the gates, he’d been behaving like an ADHD sufferer on a cocktail of Red Bull and NoDoz tablets. M3 was his ever enigmatic self, though if you were to watch closely, you’d see his mask moving slightly as his mouth dropped in awe. Jorge, oddly, wasn’t with them at the minute but would be joining them later after his ‘super importante meeting’. It was a shame he couldn’t be with them now, for even in a place as surreal as a theme park, the sight of a man in a mask, accompanied by a midget in a monkey suit raised some eyebrows. They were accosted more than once by overzealous children who thought that they were characters from some as-yet-unknown movie or television show. These minor nuisances, however, weren’t detracting from their experience and as they made their way towards the brand new ride based on ‘The Simpsons’, M3 was grinning from ear-to-ear under his spandex face covering. Milling around outside the entrance to the ride were all manner of characters from the show. From the core cast members to the incidental denizens of Springfield – Cletus, Karl and Lenny, Rainier Wolfcastle – they were all there. For Super Monito, it was all too much. The sight of so many costumed characters seemed to him like nothing except a battle royale. So he went crazy. Within seconds, Maggie was in a headlock and Apu was in a headscissors position. The rest of the cast followed their security training and made quickly for the backstage area as uncostumed ride attendants moved in to separate the monkey from the performers. M3 was stood stock still, not knowing what to do and deciding that doing nothing was probably the best outcome. He didn’t want them both to get thrown out – they hadn’t even been on Jaws yet. If only Jorge was here, he could fix this. What he didn’t realise was that Jorge was there, and not just in spirit. No, Jorge was standing just 10 feet away, head in hands, contemplating whether or not to get involved, hoping that it would end soon without injury to anyone. It was at this stage that Super Monito pulled out a move that would result in an instant DQ in any wrestling ring – the ball claw. Jorge had no choice, he had to stop the madness. This led to the bizarre site of Bumblebee man running towards a monkey, an Asian shopkeeper and a baby, his shouts muffled by the costume as he reaches them and grabs the monkey, throwing him over one shoulder and pointing towards the exit. ‘Es time to go amigo!’ Then, grabbing a stunned M3 by the arm, the bizarre trio take off, leaving a crowd of onlookers wondering what the hell had just happened.
