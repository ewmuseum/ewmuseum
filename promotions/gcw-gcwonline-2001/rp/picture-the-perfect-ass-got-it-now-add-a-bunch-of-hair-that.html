Picture the perfect ass. Got it? Now add a bunch of hair. That's mine.

Date: Aug 27/05 I take a long drag of my cigarette. It's all starting to make sense now. I cough, not a smoker you see, but when the chips are down, people do some out of character things. I blow the smoke out, it hits the mirror and cascades in all directions, obscuring the image before me. That twisted visage, that... that horrible reminder. I wish I could forget the straight nose, the high cheek bones and proud jaw line. It's so handsom. So malevolent when those lips curl into the mockery I call a grin. If only I could forget for just one night. But I can't. I never could, for years I've wanted to, to just put it all behind me and start fresh. But it won't let me. It never will, those blue eyes will always... It happens like this everytime, subtle at first, a slip here, a mistake there. I start to doubt. To wonder if this is truly my calling. The ring, I pledged myself to it, I asked for it's hand in marriage so to speak, and then I celebrated... I celebrated without even waiting to hear the answer. I can see why people smoke, the way it claws at the back of my throat, burning it's way into my lunges. The taste. It reminds me that I'm alive, that I am human after all... Because I am human after all. Sometimes I forget, sometimes I get confused, inside that ring they all start to look a little like the devil. And I sometimes start to think I can do things I just shouldn't be doing. But I just get so jealous, so over come, all these men and women trying to take what's mine, trying to take my ring, to call it their own. Another drag, another night, another week, another match... Will I actually keep my focus this time? Without the drugs, I'm so much weaker than I was before. The pain is starting to hurt a little more every time I go out there. The crowd starts getting to me and I lose myself, I want them to roar so loud it rattles the chains on heaven's closed front gate. But they wont... not for me. Not for Johnny Lexicon. Who is Johnny Lexicon? You ask the right people and they'll tell you. Oh they'll tell you all they know about me and take care to implant as much venom in each word as I deserve. I'm the bastard that rode their asses to the top, whipping their backs raw and then using them as a crash pad the entire way down, screaming and crashing into oblivion... Oblivion. The smoke burns my eyes. I'm still alive. I can still breath, the smoke still burns my throat. When I jacked that match out from Savige I was on fire. I had focus. I could have tangled with him and Hardy both... But what's the point? The people won't cheer a masked vigilante, screwing up bouts and putting his boot in the wrong ass-holes ass-hole. I needed to make it a statement. Putting out two tired and beaten warriors is like a porn flik without the sex scene. It just doesn't make sense. Another drag. Another reminder. But putting The Machine down when he's a little rested and hungry for vengeance. The fans will love that. That's all that's left. Glory, greatness, what do these things matter if the fans won't love you. How great is Lestatt? How great is man who's name is booed, how great is Lestatt indeed. I was this close to my greatness once. So fucking close. And then that bitch had to fold, she had to leave and give me a useless strap with a fancy fucking name. I cough again and drop the cigarette into the toilet with a neat and tidy 'kerplunk'. I've been lost ever since that night, I loved her, Zodiac, I loved her fire for the fight, the competitive spirit. She had everything, I never met a woman who could throw me across the ring. I would have loved her, but she wouldn't let me. She wouldn't let me get close, always pushing me away, always keeping an ally an enemy. She wouldn't let me get my moment either... 'Zeta?' Her voice warbles from the next room. 'Zeta?' She called again, her voice teasing, alluring... But it's not Zodiac. It's not even Eva.... Old habits die hard, with or without a mask I'm still a sucker for a nice pair of legs. Always I keep reminding myself. It's always about what you don't have, what you can't get, what you want so bad you'd beat the shit out of Jesus himself if the fucker stood in your way and said bring it on. But that's the problem, I can fight my way through the best, I just don't suck cock nearly half as good. Politics. It's always been about the politics, behind the scenes, who's doing what with who and where they're gonna take it, and who they're taking it there for. See I'm in nice and tight, with Trey and Eva doing so well, I could suckle this pig for another pay per view or two. But what then? Is this what all my hard work is going to amount to? A lackluster low card match, a poser... They all want me to be like I was in the old days. I was so good at being the bad guy, but the bad guy never gets the love, never gets the respect. I hold up my new mask, white with blue trim around the eyes and a single M on the forehead. They all want me to play the game. Well. Who am I to tell them no? I'm just the guy inside the mask...
