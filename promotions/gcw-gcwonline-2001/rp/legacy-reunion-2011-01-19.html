19 January 2011

Date: 01/19/11 “I’m letting you know right now I’m not going to be at our father’s house for Christmas. Not after what happened last year.” That the words echo off the walls of the meat storage facility turned wrestling training gym (aptly named “The “Icebox”) makes them sound almost ominous. Alexis nods, not surprised by Colin’s decision. “Where are you going to be then?” Colin won’t meet Alexis’ eyes. “Around. Don’t worry about me.” Alexis frowns at Colin’s behavior. She wanted to see her brother for Christmas and she was not going to Vancouver if he wasn’t going to be there. To hell with her drunken excuse of a father and to hell with a mother who quite obviously wanted to live vicariously through her. She was going to Chicago and going to stay there until something better came along, or her mother did, whichever came first. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Colin had been wrong. Alexis grinned at the thought. She wasn’t spiteful like that, but in this case she was glad he had been wrong. She got to see him for Christmas after all, and it was even better like this, no parental temper tantrums to dampen the holiday mood. A miniature Christmas tree sat on a table in the hotel suite, fully decorated. Mostly by her, but Alexis didn’t mind. Garland and decorations hung from every available space, making it seem much more like a home than either of their “real” ones had been. It even felt more like a home. Alexis looked around, nodding in satisfaction. The moment is broken with a click of a lighter. She turns and pointedly coughs at Colin who is topping off his own view of the room by enjoying a cigarette. Colin just as pointedly ignores the blatant overacting, lazily taking in the smoke then blowing it back out into the room. Alexis looks over to the room’s other occupant, but Micah looks to be amused by the whole thing rather than sympathetic. Making a face, Alexis looks down at the drink in her hand, a bottle of Vanilla Coke. She could have gotten away with something stronger, but she had no intention of that, ever. Her father had seen to that. A quick glance around the room proved she hadn’t been the only one. There was nothing alcoholic in the room at all. Having no ashtray Colin was tapping his cigarette into an empty bottle that had recently held The room lapses into silence, Alexis thoughtfully staring at her bottle, not really seeing it. “I wonder if dad misses us?” “That bastard has his head up his ass.” Alexis glances over at Colin, slightly unnerved by the sharpness of his voice. The room lapses into another, more awkward silence. After a moment Colin himself breaks it. “By the way, how is your training going?” Alexis scoffs, and now it’s Colin’s turn to give an odd glance. “I’m not, not anymore. Not under mom, and I’m done with that anyway.” Colin glances over at Micah, who shrugs. He’s heard this before. “What happened?” Alexis’ chuckle is full of irony. “Chalk it up to... ascetic differences. She didn’t exactly like my idea of wrestling outfit, I didn’t like her trying to push hers on me.” Colin watches Alexis, waiting for her to continue, but she simply glares right back at him, not wanting to discuss it further. Their eyes meet and in them is an argument, a battle of wills which Micah, unmoving, watches with great interest. After a fierce, but subtle debate involving little more obvious than slight changes in facial expression, Colin sighs, letting it go, leaving Alexis looking visibly relieved. After a moment Colin glances at his sister again. “So where are you staying anyway?” Alexis suddenly finds great interest in her bottle. “With a friend, don’t know if you guys have met... Alex Edwards?” “Edwards eh? That’s a very familiar name.” Alexis nods, playing with the bottle and straight up refusing to look up. A slight blush crosses her face and Colin’s eyes widen. His eyes move to Micah just in time to see him notice as well and lean forward, an odd expression crossing his face. Colin shifts his eyes back to Alexis, a slight smile playing on his face. “Oh, now I remember. He’s the one you said you had a crush on.” The bottle falls to the floor, the liquid within fizzing with carbonation, the lid on tight enough that it goes no farther. Alexis’ head snaps up, her face coloring further. “I do not!” She meets Micah’s eyes nervously. “I never said that!” Micah smirks, almost playfully. “You didn’t have to.” Alexis recoils, a drift of smoke crossing her face from Colin’s cigarette, a perfect distraction. She masks her face into dirty look aimed at her brother. It isn’t a hard expression to change into. “Will you please put that out!” Colin wears a rather Cheshire grin now. “No.” Noticing his sister still glaring at him, he mouths deliberately, making sure she sees Payback ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Alexis turns her camera around backwards, a practiced move. She knows exactly how to hold it so that her entire face will appear in it’s screen. She reaches around to flick a switch and is rewarded with a small red light that appears on the camera. She nods, smirking at her thoughts for a moment before she starts to speak into the camera. “Happy Birthday mom. See, I didn’t forget. I went out, on my own, got a contract with a company that I didn’t need you for, won my first match...” She talks slowly deliberately, driving every word home. “... and already got myself not only a title shot, but a World Title shot!” She lets that sink it, unable to help her smile of pride. No one of the previous generation had done what the Saints had already. “And I know what you’re going to say, that I slept with someone for it or at the very least put on a nice little show. But, guess what? I never had even a little chance to try the theory yet at all! So, yet another thing you’re wrong about, mom. But happy birthday anyway. Dunno if you’re ever going to bother seeing this. God knows you don’t like seeing me like this!” She stretches her arms out, letting the camera take in every part of the skin-tight wrestling outfit she wears. “Funny how by my doing what you’ve told me not to I’ve gotten more success in two weeks than you had in years! And not only that, check this out.” Alexis swings the camera around to show off the large, comfortable looking locker room she’s standing in. “See this! I guess this place hasn’t had a stable in a while, if ever, so we’re kind of a big deal around here. We are important to this company, and we’ve found one that doesn’t treat us like doormats! Hell, even if it was. I’ll bet we would have had the sense to leave. She swings the camera back around so the lens faces her again, opening her eyes wide and staring pointedly into it. “Just maybe we’ve learned from your mistakes, all of yours, not just you mom, but from every parent of every Saint. We will not repeat your sins.” Alexis sighs, the anger leaked out of her. She looks almost apologetic. “Well, here, to show you there’s nothing to worry about, I’ll show you around.” She swings the camera around again, walking with it this time, and making sure not to miss a thing, first of the room, then out into the hallway. She’s concentrating so much on every little detail that she forgets one thing, to watch where she’s going. As a result she walks right into a young man with dark hair that bears a passing resemblance to CM Punk. “Sorry, sorry.” Alexis glances up, and almost drops the camera. “Alex... hi!”
