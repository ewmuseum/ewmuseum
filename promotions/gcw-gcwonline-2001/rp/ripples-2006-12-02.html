Date: 12/02/06 Ha ha ha Am I going to die? Probably not. Haven't been punished yet. I stumble across it and I'm drunk on confusion, buzzing rum and twitching uncontrollably. It is . Except, it's not. There's a CGI mock-up of Johnny Borealis at the bottom. I remember, there used to be a photo of him. This. This is very funny. Nothing new, I figure. Nothing I wasn't told about. It's just, well. Hearing and experiencing aren't exactly synonyms. The bars on the cage just look so different from the outside, is all. So I'll dig. I'm a member, I mean, it seems like a decent place to start. (What the fuck am I starting here? A clinical dissection of my world trapped in the clam of a petri dish? A journey deep into realizing (again) that we're all just someone's bad idea? I don't know. Call it compulsion.) Interviews. 'A Long String of Totally Unconnected Events That Don't End But So Well “Jordan White “GCW Interview #822 “Date: Let's say the 24th, and ignore that today's the 23rd “Location: Places in NC, yeah “This was one of those easy days. “I mean, I can't remember when this was one of those easy days’it's been a very long time. I woke up, I woke up with thunder and full of life and glowing with it, I woke up a free man without any of those lame concerns that tie down contemporaries in my field. Without success, without fame, without glory, and without needing any of those things. Also, without much on other than this pretty comfortable pair of boxers I've had for a while. “I'm not sure, but I think there was even a smile thrown in somewhere.' It's weird. My life in black and green and nothing. But. Everyone's life in black and green and nothing; just words waiting to be plucked and stories waiting to be learned. I am a god without any responsibility. Oh, Jordan White. Who first? The only way to decide anything is by arbitrary clicking. Bryan Mayhem. Time travels! Shoots his older self! Not bad. A death story for a man still alive, it's a pretty cool concept. Further back, though. This isn't about anyone, this is conjecture or psychoses or the final proclamation of just how far us little wrestler bastards are from anything like free will. I think I've had enough of that for a minute. Who, though? Knight sounds like a start. Zombies? And he dies too. I'm starting to see a picture here. Themes. Like, like a gimmick. Some crazy, totally impossible shit from everybody (or, well, two right now. But there's a pattern, I tells ya.) Now, what's the point of all this shit? Even back into something kind of like reality: vampires, shapeshifters, the inhumanly charismatic and the waytoohumanly wrong. Even me, fucking, especially me. My head looks so weird in words. Spaces and punctuation and accent marks hanging exactly to those little scars on my face that only I remember this well, the spider tattoo that slips between and around the words, that severs them in clean obvious lines and paints this almost perfect picture in abstraction. My eyes twitch in the words, shift 'do' or 'be' from here to there and back again without ever changing the meaning. I bleed pixels until my eyes bleed, until trails of crimson hang down from those noun pupils, dropping vertical through the columns and betraying everything about me. Fuck. Very weird. My future's here, too. A funeral that just doesn't interest me at a date I'm going to forget. Everyone's curious how they'll look in that deadman suit, but, well. It's not worth knowing. It's not worth wasting time on the old flesh (long live the new flesh) when there's so much else to be done. I'm selfabsorbed, definitely. Enough of the other people, enough of those bizarre facts that just aren't as worldshattering as they should be: we're all freaks, or eternal wizards from the land of Lonyore, or ageless thought able to take any form in a liquid world. It's just that maybe there's something here that I didn't catch. Like Trey getting Casey out of the asylum, or how fucked Casey's head was anyway. I just. It doesn't compel me. I don't feel anyone's breath: not mine, not theirs. I feel stagnation and a creeping black fungal growth sporing itself deep in my lungs and festering as it takes hold. Transferring carbon dioxide to oxygen in my lungs, slipping it into my blood without my chest ever expanding, ever contracting. Still like corpses are still. A rotting sunrise is the most hideous thing in the world. I see us all slip into apathy. Each of us, ugly and smoothed, shining plastic; sometimes spackled with dirt, sometimes not, but always with a ceramic sheen. We hold plasticine secrets not worth knowing and we know it; we hold them on outstretched hands just waiting for someone to care enough to take them. The only truth hidden in this whole muck and mire and existence isn't worth fighting for, isn't worth dying for. It means that we can't fight and that we can't die; it means that we're all just puppets and we can't even choose. Would anyone even care? No. Probably not. And it falls on me to wear the fundamental restriction of this universe? Dig, I guess. Don't have any other choice in the matter. And it falls on me to see the cobweb strands that run throughout our lives, the convoluted and ugly plotlines that force us around and on against logic and against happiness and against enjoyment, these silk selfportraits of us and of people greater than us in every way? And it falls on me to understand how limitless my potential really is? I work with the undead and the unliving. What's to stop me from severing some deepdown respect for realism and turn myself into a God in every respect? What if I want an army of unstoppable robots? What if I. No. I like metaphors and symbols and themes too much to sacrifice their power in the name of not fitting in. Because let's be serious here: all vampires do is drain. I could watch GCW bled dry. Or I could do something. Like, laugh as I watch it strangled to death by this sick plaque of rehashed unique. Not out of any hostility to GCW, I mean, just the sheer joy found in any death other than my own. I survive, it fades; the leeches and lepers move on to spread their disease. I hang around in the confusion storm, I make my own little calm center by turning madness into common sense and I build my home there and I wile away my life there. A cat not bound to any of the typical patterns of life (mainly death) leaps to my chest and waits for me to fall back so it can have a good place to rest. 'More to freedom than being able to leave the cage, righ' M?' He nuzzles against my chin. It's no answer. He could talk right now, if someone wanted him to. He could completely clarify the line between chaos and actualized freedom, or he could at least share the recipe for some delicious cake. It won't happen, I mean, some rules matter to me. Are fuzzy things really man's only salvation? 'You're a fucking prick, y'know. I'd kill you if it came up.' There's no way he's believing me (unless he's learned english recently), but he's taking off. Guess I'm just no fuckin' fun these days. Shit, it's winter. Or, well, or it's close enough to winter. Wholly unaware here’and this is because seasons matter, of course, in a world with walls this paperthin and shifting. I can hear them yelling at each other and watching TV and fighting and fucking and being. I could just move. Find somewhere warm with quiet neighbors that have good taste. Assuming such a magical wonderland exists, I will ride my lovely pink unicorn on the rainbow there. So much more waits, and so much more screams. Secrets schism into fullon disconnects and yaws. I'm Jordan Xavier White. I'm not much, I'm less real than anyone that might see this. And that's fucked. No. I'm a labrat; I'm bred and born for the job. No other purposes to serve here, just legs moving down a maze that doesn't even branch, that just tricks the eye with sharp turns and tight passages in blisterblack and never even the scent of cheese. Some primal urge to do as commanded. I'm just words; I'm an open head surgery with a complex paranoia complex dancing around my neurons like comets ablaze in mindless natural fury with nowhere to go but straight down into the green places. Where things live, where things live happily and where things live unafraid and. I'm a wrestler. In a world of the surreal and the unreal and’maybe’the sidereal, I'm a scrawny junkie with a job and a large bank account from fucking over a close friend. That job is to wrestle. Not to do well at it, not to even feel like I should do well at it. Unlike a lot, yeah, I accept. No I'm just Jordan, and all I know is what all of them could/should/would know, if they'd just open their eyes. Shifting stories, mismatched collage histories. Suits. We're all suits stitched together from rags. Our world ripples from their world, your world. Fuckers. It's not if you enjoy or if you hate or anything; it's that we have no privacy. It's that we exist in sudden flashes and fade away. We come back and we're aware of what happened in the absence, and we move on those beats and notes until we've got nothing left to move with and then we're less than dust. We're the dust of an idea, scrawled in sand minutes before the storm. Jokes. We're the puns of existence. We're scars. Scars of scraped scabs; regrettable wastes and. I'm almost a man. These words indicate emotions, these words indicate actions. These words suck. Oh. I don't know if I'm something to say or something to mean or nothing of either’just that I'm as well off as anyone else in this place. Well. Except that burden of feeling like it's on me to keep everything together just because it's looking like I'm the only one here that's figured it out. I owe nothing to this universe, it owes nothing to me. It's a vaseline realm, a slide through and never concern yourself hole in some dying society's bloodletting orifices. We're not even to the point of dreams. We're not even to the point of flashes of insight. We don't even exist. I can grab and touch and steal, I can break my life down into sentence diagrams that tell me my day was noun noun noun verb verb adjective noun adjective verb, interspersed with pronouns and adverbs just to hide how bland this shit really looks to be. It's not being. No. I'm Jordan Xavier White. I'm not much, I'm less real than anyone that might see this. And that's fucked. So, should I succeed? It wouldn't be hard. I'm an idea. I could take over if I wanted to. I know all of their secrets. I can't walk between worlds, but I can be aware enough to know everything that's wrong, to pick at every little flaw and fault and failing. I could be a universal quality control, I could become the karma cannon and He Who Knows and He Who Judges. If there were any fucking compulsion at all. At the start of all this, if someone would've sat down and given enough rules and limits to keep us sane’maybe. No one cared. No one wanted verisimilitude. Some dick somewhere just wanted the chance to jack off, and took it without thinking about the damage. 'Are we goin' up or in circles? Or up in flames, maybe.' A cat is not bound to peoplely courtesies. And, to be fair, I'm not particularly up for 'em. I guess we can't ever expect to do much other than what we were created for’I guess we can't be better than our creators in most ways. I guess there are rules they have to play by, too. I really don't like hitting chicks. Really. 'Shit happens,' or 'the chaotic winds of nothing fate blow heavy.' Something I can hold on to if I keep it in my head, a concrete dream of victory in a battle I don't know name, date, or location for. The sun rises. I just lose. I lose my ethics and I lose my moral code and I lose my consistency and I'm left’as what? A gibbering heap, piled high and still piling. And, for some reason, through all of this, I'm still a wrestler. It can't be my problem, I can't willingly make choices like this. No, no’Sobais signed me up. I'm just a pawn and, in a world of a thousand highly prolific wrestling federations with contracts tenuous as promise rings, I can't quit. Addictions without a good rush are the worst kind to justify. Mainly, well, mainly because it just can't make any sense. I guess that means I like this job. I guess that means I like these people, or these fans. Or fame. No. It isn't about immortality or immorality or powerbombs or track marks or reconstructed nostrils or SUVs or flamboyant tights or pyro that'll singe the hair from your face or sentient sentences that read back or gold belts or shit talking or masturbation or glass menagerie lives or. Or it's about all of those things or any of those things or whatever glorious whatever has to be appended into something for it to be worthwhile, for the fix to be worth the fiending. We're all slowburners, every you, every me, and every we that I can think of right now. We have the time. It's gotta be spent somehow, right? Like, if you turn your head to the side, the whole world become a frequency, a heartbeat. Not like time's but so precious. Most of the time I feel like I haven't aged a day, except for those searing standout pains that only exist in bursts and flashes and strobes. Except for my head. I'm quieter lately. I don't care about those things that used to want to stab deep in my ribs and never let go. I work with things that can't exist in a logical world’dig. I tried to have sex with a chick that had recorded the ebb and flow of get and give all 327 years she'd been alive’cool. I saved either the President's life or his clone's life. With a jet’awesome. I. I kind of understand. It's more fun than blue slacks or mortgages or armoires. It's more fun than being a lounge singer or a pilot or a fuckin' rockstar, really. It's got more color than being real, it cuts out all the gray time and just leaves you with this vague idea that things were boring or things were cool, or what the fuck ever. I haven't lived, really, but if you ignore that then it's been a decent life. Doesn't corrupt me, I mean. It's smooth. And if you tilt your head enough, you can see the black and white of ripples reversed. At distances it's going to blur, at distance's everything's going to equal out. When that much prophesied 'one day' finally hits, I mean, or at fifty paces through space or time. Even then, people have to live moment to moment. They can't shift from where they are to where they were with the ease of changing their mind. They're locked into something. They're trapped in the bodies they start with, they're barred by the moral codes of their formal years, they're restricted by the laws of society and the laws of nature. There's freedom, yeah, but all it allows is doing things done before. It's pretty hollow. Or I'd guess it is. So, real people have the freedom to do nothing new. Us fake fuckers, we have no freedom but we're not limited by rationality or logic. We can bend our world as far away from the legit one as possible’and we don't have to worry about the cost. We're all jokes, pricks and pigs and fuckers and’seriously, now’it's no wonder we're relegated to the dark places. All of that allowed us and the best we can do is rehash old ideas, retread beaten ground. Can I really blame us wrestler cats though? We weren't given choices in this stupid shit, we're not given choices in it now. We are drones, robots, automatons’far more disturbing than the otherworldly or the inhuman. A lot of us, a lot of us are just obvious symptoms of some great disease in our gods, in our renderers and in our creators. But some of us? Fucking shit. Some of us are so perfect I can feel their fucking heartbeats rising out of these pages less than blank. Weird. And it's not me that has to save it. I'm just a symptom. My heart clicks, hollow and pathetic. Desiccated. Tick. Tick. I'm not very fun lately, I guess. Between the assaults and the batteries and all the fucking drama bombs bursting, just hasn't been the time to fuck around like there used to be. Could be the seasons changing. Could be me finding out how much shit there really is around here. And how much gold. I'm Jordan. I'm not crazy. I'm almost concerned with something that isn't me. Oh, Jordan White. What now? I dunno. Spent enough time in the dirty room lately, enough time wallowing in the joy of not having to care about a fucking thing. The joy of walking on clothes, the sweet crunching under layers of junk. It's cool. 'The atmosphere is against me. A hundred thousand tons of pressure working against my system. A victim of gravity itself. Or something. Maybe it's an infection; some open wound of the psyche that leads me straight past paranoia and deep into the primordial loathing. I'll mutate gills through some psuedoscientific instantevolution or I'll drown in the mire. Whichever comes first. But fuck. If I can survive it, I'll be a very angry motherfucker. Instead of just roasting on a slow twist pyre, matchbook in hand.' A very long time, y'know, suffocating in myself’just couldn't claw to the top. Could've just turned around, if it'd crossed my mind. Well. Whatever. Dem's de breaks. Time, time's not quite as scarce as it used to be. Open schedules, wacky hijinks in the land of very well awesome. Really, the fuckin' life. And simultaneous with an international tour’which is practically justified hedonism? This is seriously the fucking life, without a doubt. Which just leaves one thing. Why do I wrestle? Unexplained phenomena. What else could a guy want? Magic? It's all smoke and mirrors anyway. We're just living comics. Except, well. Fuck it. We're alive. This place is real. And maybe this place is real because we're alive. It just can't matter that much, really. Like, what's the worst that could happen: Am I going to die? No, no, no.
