Focus

4 December 2005

Date: 4 Dec 05 “It’s not over amigo, not by a long shot.” RaZiel’s words weren’t really needed, we both knew that I was far from ready to quit now. He was being a good friend though and it was something that I appreciated more than he may ever know. “Trust me RaZ I know. It hurt losing to that son of a bitch but life goes on. Quitting is something that cowards do and I think we both can agree that I am no coward.” “No,” RaZiel smirks “That you are not, never really have been.” “I’ve been close amigo.” It’s true that I had been close to quitting and I do not believe anyone would have argued my case. Losing so much as I had at such a young age can usually do very sick things to one’s mind. Not to say that my mind is 100% clean but things could be far worse. “I don’t think so,” RaZ says with a slight shake of his head “What I think is that you were misguided. You just needed a way to release that anger inside of you.” “Was a lot of fucking anger to release.” We share a laugh now, a genuine laugh. It was strange to me that I could laugh just days after losing one of the more important matches of my career. Or at least that was what some of the magazines and newspapers have been saying. I have a hard time believing this one was bigger than my World Title match with Alex back in DWA or even my beating of Dread for the GCW World Title. Now for Stevens? Yes it was the biggest of HIS career but not mine. I think its why I can find reasons to smile now ? even if inside it still is slowly eating away at me. “Well Rob, I think we have work to do. You may not be the champ but its not time to feel sorry for yourself.” “You’re right ? its time to feel sorry for the GCW for having to witness a Michael Stevens title reign.” Another chuckle came from my friend’s throat. I give him a devious smirk but deep down I knew that Stevens would probably carry that title well. I did not want to believe it in the slightest though. I hope that son of a bitch falls flat on his face, making the people realize exactly what they are missing without me at the front of the pack. After facing a man four times in a few months you learn a thing or two about him though. If there is one thing that I have learned the last few months about Michael Stevens is that he may be the only person who I would even feel remotely close to satisfied with holding that title. Not for his command of the crowd or his way with words or his “mike” work, hell not even his technical prowess. His heart. I beat that mother fucker over and over again, gave him everything I had and yet he would keep coming back. Not only did he take the beatings I’d give but he managed to give me quite the run even in the losses. No one else has been capable of almost snapping my arm in half and hopefully nobody does again. Stevens won’t drop the ball and for that he deserves even MY respect. Too bad he’ll never hear that from me. I slam my locker door closed as does Raz. Figured we had a little less than a week before WorldWide and I wanted to start up my new workout routine. Things were in desperate need of altering and it all started with the preparation for a match. I had become complacent and I could not afford another mishap like what happened at NC-17. It was time to go back to the days of 100 fans in the crowd, one large locker room for everyone, and barely any money after the show. Both of us move out of the locker room. ??So,” RaZiel says without turning his head “Whats next” Do you start finding a way to get your number one contendership back?” “No.” I could feel his eyes burn into me with confusion. “Don’t look at me like that.” I say without looking at him. “I said no.” “Stevens kept weaseling his way into matches with you for god knows how long and he manages to squeak out ONE win in four tries and you don’t wish to go and get your belt back” You have proven you could beat him on any given night.” “Look Dave all things happen for a reason. I think this is the perfect opportunity for me to take myself to that next level.” “There’s a next level for you?” “You can always get better.” “Roberto ? I normally wouldn’t say this, fearing how big your head could get but ? I’ve never seen anyone as good as you.” I stop walking. I go silent. I think about what has happened in GCW since February. Hardy NBK U.S. Title Trey Dread World Title Mayhem Stevens Stevens Stevens NO TITLE I look back up at RaZiel “Obviously not good enough.” My voice flat. “I have a lot of work to do. I blew a perfect chance at NC-17. Sure I have won many matches since coming back here but what does it matter if I lose the match on the biggest stage?” “You can have that back you just need to fight for ?” “NO!” I shout “Before I can worry about Michael Stevens or the World Title I have a certain match at World Wide to prepare for. No more overlooking opponents Dave. I am going to treat every match as if it were for the World Title. I will aim to once again wipe out every and any challenger that may arise from the ashes. Let us pretend that this is February all over again shall we?” “As you wish.” We begin to walk again going towards the main gym where the boxing ring was set up. His words did sink in though and some of them were true. Michael Stevens did get unlimited chances to go after me, beginning with our battle for the United States title, even if it did have Luke Hanson in it as well. The funny thing is that if I demanded a rematch I would most likely be denied. Guarantee they would give me some lame excuse such as it beginning to get stale or the people want to see us move on. I have already prepared for such heinous answers. This is why I know I must go about this in another way. I have to take it a match at a time. I will go through the ranks and decimate and dismantle as required. I don’t care if I have to win the TV, US, or Tag Titles to make my way back to the top. I do not care if there is some tournament that is needed, a gauntlet, a battle royal, a ladder match, a cell, a cage or any other kinds of concoctions that can be thought of. We reach the ring and climb inside. “Tell me Roberto, how did it feel when you heard that three count?” “It burned.” I said quickly “It felt like someone had taken a torch and lit up my insides. It was as if someone had stripped me of any ounce of meaning I had.” “How do you feel now?” “Focused.” It was true for the first time in a long time everything was crystal clear. I was focused on the tasks at hand. I was ready to take life on as it came ? the good, the bad, and the ugly of it. “You don’t honestly believe you can beat Almasy do you?” I crack my neck from side to side. “You know that he will embarrass you in front of the entire world.” I place a mouth piece in “You will fail at World Wide just as you failed at NC-17.” I tighten my hands into fists “You will become nothing ? no hall of fame, no award show, no nothing. You will be rotting next to your buddy Tristain Mayhem, remember him don’t you?” I say nothing “GTT5” You might as well just hand in your resignation now. Clyde Walkins, retarded and all will stomp you into the ground.” I slowly make my way towards him “Then Mary is going to go and find a real man ? someone like Michael Stevens.” I swing my right hand Let the refocusing begin.
