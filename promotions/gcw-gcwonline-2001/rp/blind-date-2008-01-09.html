Date: 1/9/08 START 3:00 pm It is another surprisingly sunny day in the lovely city of Dallas, Texas. The streets are bustling, people are scattered across the sidewalk, and our hero, Clyde Fox, is on his way to meet up with a girl he met online. Dressed in some preppy American Eagle acid washed jeans, and his signature blue tinged Squirtle shirt, Clyde struts his stuff as he nears the mystery girls apartment. He arrives at an eight level, peach colored building. The mammoth marquee that says “Le Residance” projects from the building like a sore thumb. Fox: Ain’t this a little diamond in the rough? Looking up at the building, Clyde scratches his head wondering what to expect from today’s upcoming adventure. He walks up to the revolving doors and pauses. Fox: Ummmm. Analyzing the angles, Clyde steps back and lets a few people pass in and out to observe the mechanism. Once confident, Clyde slips through the revolving door, holding his breath for the entire time.
