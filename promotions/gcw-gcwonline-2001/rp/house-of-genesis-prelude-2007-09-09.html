House of Genesis: Prelude

9 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 09, 2007 Looking back on his career, did Scott Drake accomplish everything he had set out for? He was certainly a success; it would be hard to argue otherwise. He had a loving family that just introduced a new edition in a precious six-pound daughter, who had the most absolutely beautiful blue eyes he had ever seen. His fianc’e loved him with all her heart. It certainly did not hurt that Tessa Windsor, even after child birth, still had a body that made others jealous. He was rich; several extended contracts in televised promotions made sure of that. He was successful; currently enjoying a lengthy title reign as Global Championship Wrestling’s tag team champions with his best friend in the business, Markus Stone. So why did he feel like he just missed out on the lottery? That he was one number away from the big prize? Scott Drake, better known to the wrestling world as Chris Storm, was simply that kind of person. It made him who he was. Friends said it was his biggest weakness, outsiders said it was his best attribute. He was never satisfied with what he had. He always wanted more; be it a longer title reign, a more pristine championship, or to improve his own talents. It waivered on the verge of obsession, and at times crossed over. Several times it threatened to destroy everything he had, while others it helped him rebuild. It was a cycle. Happiness led to hunger. Hunger led to obsession. Obsession led to destruction. Only through his sheer stubborn determination did he manage to regain that happiness through the same hunger that led to his destruction. If he could not break the cycle; it would cost him his family, his friends, and his career. Sooner or later it would cost him his own life (one could argue that at times that nearly came true). Sitting at the dining room table, staring at his newest addition to the family, Aislinn Elizabeth Drake (though it would have been Aislinn Moon Unit Drake if Scott had his way, but NO! Apparently Frank Zappa is just a crazy man), Scott had a rare smile on his face. His lovely fianc’e, Tessa Windsor, walked into the room with a cordless phone in her hand. She had a puzzled look on her face, “Scott, phone for you.” Turning his attention from his daughter, Scott faced Tessa “who is it?” he wondered. Tessa just shrugged her shoulders and handed him the phone, “he wouldn’t say but he was insistent that he talk to you.” “Probably another crazy fan that somehow got my number,” Drake muttered as he took the phone. To give him some privacy on the phone (and because Tessa knew that if it was a fan, Scott would curse him out until the moon turned blue), Tessa took their daughter out of her bouncer and carried her into the living room. “Hello?” There was a pause as Scott waited for the voice on the other end. As soon as the voice spoke, all the color from his face washed away. “Why are you calling me?” On the other end of the phone was a voice he never thought he would hear again. Not many people in the wrestle world phased Scott Drake. He never had a problem going toe to toe with the likes of Bryan Mayhem, Shaman, Hessian, or any other monster of a man. But this man had a way that unnerved Scott. He didn’t like him and he didn’t want to be around him; even if it was just his voice over the phone. “You want me to meet you?” he asked, sounding equally surprised and confused. To him, Jerrod Darson, otherwise known as Genesis, was never a man for business over coffee. The man trained Scott to wrestle, putting him through one of the most hellish periods of his life. As soon as he could, Scott cut ties with Darson and never looked back. Yet here he was talking to him on the phone, asking to meet face to face. Was it retribution he sought from all those years back when Scott left him lying in a pool of his own blood? Was he looking for forgiveness for that intense training regiment he made Drake go through? Or was it something else? Scott shook all those thoughts from his head, “look, I’m busy. Tessa and I just had our first child together and some crazy shit has recently gone down. So I can’t meet up with you now.” While most of that was indeed true, it was not why Scott Drake could not meet his old mentor. Scott simply did not want to be in the man’s presences. “Maybe we can meet up later. I’ve got a show with GCW over in Cincinnati. If you are able to make it out there, we can talk. I’m defending my Tag Team titles with Markus Stone. You remember him right” He was Zero in WIW.” Inside his head Scott begged repeatedly for Darson to tell him it wasn’t that important and to hang up. “Right now” What do you mean “what am I doing right now?”” Scott had to take a moment to look around; making sure that Darson wasn’t somehow watching him at the moment. “I’m with my daughter, Aislinn, at the moment” he told him, continuing to check to make sure he wasn’t being watched. Moving toward the window, he did his best to hide against the wall as he peered out. “Tessa is out getting baby supplies so she left me to sit.” There was a pause as he listened to the reply on the other end of the line before Scott exclaimed “what do you mean I’m a piss poor liar?” Listening him out, Drake nearly fell over himself, “I am not a shitty babysitter. And for the record, Tessa would leave me with the children.” Hearing her name called out, Tessa walked back into the kitchen to see if Scott needed her for something, “you called?” Scott slapped himself on the forehead, realizing that the person on the other end of the line most likely heard that. “Look, I’m busy man. Ok” I’m busy.” There seemed to be some discussion going on with the other end of the line as Scott tapped his fingers on the table, annoyed. “Ok, fine” he said, “if it is so important, I’ll meet you tomorrow. You can tell me this important business then since you won’t seem to tell me this over the phone.” Pressing the end button on the phone, Scott sank into the chair he was sitting in, letting out a deep sigh. Tessa looked on, concern stretched across her face. “What’s the matter, sweetie?” she asked, wrapped a loving arm around her man’s shoulders. Scott allowed another sigh to escape from his lips, looking into her eyes for an answer. “I think I just made a meeting with the devil.” Though he didn’t give her a name, Tessa knew who her soon-to-be husband meant. A frown instantly hit her face. “I don’t want you to do it” she said point blank. The directness of her words took Drake back for a moment. “I don’t want you to see him” she again said, reaffirming her statement. “I already told him I would” Scott told her, letting his shoulders slump down. He wanted to tell her it was her fault anyways; she was the one who exposed his lie. But he knew it wasn’t her fault. He was the one who lied in the first place. “We’re meeting tomorrow at the gym.” This time Tessa was the one to sigh. If anyone knew Scott, which not many people did, it was her. And she knew that there would be no talking him out of this regardless of how she felt about Jerrod Darson. Personally she couldn’t stand the man. She didn’t know a single person in her life with less of a moral compass. She had told Scott that countless times after he had told her all the horror stories of his training. She was the one who had to deal with all the mental scarring it did to Drake. But regardless of what she said to him, he was going to stick to his word. He was just that kind of person. “I’m sorry” he told her, able to tell that the news had bothered her. Tessa waved it off with a simple motion of her hand, “you know I hate those words.” “I’m sor?” Scott had to catch himself before he parroted himself. Tessa gave him a stern look that said she caught that. “I promise to make it as quick as possible. You know I don’t want to be around him any more than I have to.” “That’s just it, though!” shouted Tessa, waving her arms, “why did you even agree in the first place?” Sitting in the chair, Drake stared down at the floor at his feet. He didn’t really know how to express it. The words just ran amuck in his head, eluding a complete thought. “I don’t know” he muttered, managing to barely get it above a whisper. “You don’t know” came a reply that was dipped in both confusion and sarcasm. Scott came up to his feet, moving to the same window he had tried to observe earlier. Resting his arm up against the wall, he leaned into the window and peered out into the world spread out before him. Though his back was turned to her, he could still feel her eyes locked onto him, waiting for his response. “He said it was important, something business related he wanted to discuss with me.” His eyes looking over his shoulder, Scott stole a glance at Tessa, “I think he meant it. This might really be something important. I just had this feeling like I should listen him out. I owe him at least that much.” Tessa stomped her foot down, “you don’t owe him anything!” she barked. The statement stumped Scott, who had never really given much thought of the positives Darson had did for Scott. The most obvious was getting him into the business and training him. But even that seemed more like a negative at times. But that realization that without Jerrod he might not even be in the business still stuck to him as his only ammo. “He trained me. He got my started in this business” he said with half the confidence he meant to use. Tessa dropped her arms to her side, her shoulders slumped forward. “You’ve already made up your mind, huh?” she asked, giving out a sigh, “it doesn’t matter what I say at this point. Not like you would listen anyways.” The last few words stabbed him in the chest like a fiery hot knife. “I’m sorry” was all Scott could manage to mutter. Tessa turned away from him, “never mind” slipped from her soft pink lips before she left him alone in the kitchen. Moving back to the table to have a seat, Scott stared down at the wood, wondering if it really needed to be that big of a fight. It was a meeting, nothing more. Why did it have to be such a big deal? He would go, talk, and then leave. It wasn’t even like he was going to see another woman. All that still didn’t stop him from feeling like a complete jackass, however. Slamming his fist down in frustration, Scott let out a “damnit”. “Whatever Darson wanted, it had better be important”, Scott thought, otherwise he would be sorely pissed. Well’more so than he already was. From the window behind him, the cityscape started to shift. From the west storm clouds came rolling in, an impending sign of the rain heading their way. Then, from miles away but feeling like only inches, a blast of thunder ripped through the air. Scott turned his head to check out the weather, noting in surprise “hmm, that’s strange. The news said it would be sunny today.” Grabbing his gym back, he would do what he normally did when he needed to think; he went to the gym. If nothing else, at least it would be good to get some extra practice in for his match against Jay Terror and Tyson DeBough. While Banned & Exiled~! may have been the favorite going into the match, the last thing Scott wanted was to pull a Michigan in the match. “Gah, those guys suck this year” Drake laughed to himself as he headed out of the door. As he went he whistled “Geaux Tigers” to himself.
