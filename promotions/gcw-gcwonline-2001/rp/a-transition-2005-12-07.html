A Transition

7 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/07/2005 December 15th, 2002. 4:00 p.m. ???If the night was made for sleeping, last night was an utter waste. Images of his slain friend lined the insides of Skylar McGovern's eyelids, so they remained open. They stayed open until the waking hours of the morning, when the hotel ceiling became dull, and his eyelids weighed like sandbags. ???It was his part of his job to deal with this sort of thing. This was completely different, though. Arriving at a murder scene and dealing with a complete stranger's body was one thing. Skylar could deal with that. Being flown two hundred miles with no idea what was waiting at the end of the flight, however, was a different can of worms. Especially, upon arrival, he is brought face to face with an old friend, dead, bloody, and buried in the snow. ???'It's a shame,' thought Skylar, 'that the last thing I ever saw on this job was my dead best friend.' Sky tried laughing at the irony, but the glare he got from the next seat over was anything but funny. He sighed, and told himself to just be thankful for the window seat. Looking out the window, he wondered which city was forty thousand feet below him. He also wondered how, later in the day, would he explain to his boss that he was leaving the agency. ???Despite all his accomplishments in his stint with the company, all his counterparts wouldn't think twice before calling him a wash-up. Skylar laughed at himself, 'You're twenty-one years old. How could you possibly be washed up already?' So they wouldn't call him a wash-up. They'd go back to calling him rookie, pussy, amongst things much more vulgar. It wouldn't bother him though. Luke Glaze's face was nothing compared to what they'd look like if they so much as uttered a word. ???Upon seeing the words 'Starring Keanu Reeves' as the in-flight movie started, Skylar decided that it would be a good time to catch up on the sleep he did not get the night before. January 27th 2004. 4:32 a.m. ???Skylar drove his left into the battered punching bag. He liked to tell himself that it was he, who was doing the battering, but the truth be told it was like that when he bought it at a garage sale a year ago. He brought his right hand into the punching bag, reopening knuckle wounds that had closed only the night before. Those cuts would remain there as long as this nightly ritual continued. ???The disturbance caused by the death of Luke Glaze caused Skylar's sleep schedule to invert. He slept uneasily through the day, and went about his business as darkness crept in. Essentially, he was an owl. ???A few months after quitting his job, Skylar began to notice hints of violence in his personality. People who demonstrated disrespect or insensitivity towards the deceased made the normally placant McGovern's blood boil. Skylar visited a psychiatrist, in search of a cure for these violent streaks. Dr. Johnathan Best's solution was a physically stressful activity that didn't require any training. His personal suggestion was a home gym. ???Dr. Best was probably referring to a Bowflex or some other right-hand product, but Skylar was still bringing in heaps of money from the Antonio Bernini case. Skylar went right on and had a full-scale gym built in the basement of his Baltimore area home. ???Not being able to sleep at night, he took to barricading himself in his gym until the waking hours of the morning. He became obsessed with physical tension, holding onto every word the psychiatrist had said. When the strain of chin-ups, push-ups, sit-ups, bench-press and squat were not enough, and the violent streak made it's appearance, Skylar turned his attention to his punching bag. August 27th 2005. 7:35 p.m ???Skylar sat at his desk, having just awoken from his daily slumber. He stared at the telephone, anxiously tapping his fingers. Would he make this phone call, or would it stand against everything he believed in since Luke's death? Was it violence, if it was his job? Or was it just entertainment. ???Sky glanced across the room at the punching bag. He looked at the grime that had accumulated on it over the years. He looked past the grime. He saw the sweat and blood that had become one with the leather. He saw the tears. ???Besides, it was about time he started putting some money back in his bank account. Not that he had anything to worry about. He silently thanked Antonio Bernini for being a clumsy thief, and he thanked FOX for forking out millions for the exclusive rights to an interview with McGovern after the case. ???Skylar McGovern had made a decision. Looking at a piece of paper, he picked up the phone, and dialed. ???'Steven Caldera.' December 07th 2005. 12:41 a.m ???Skylar gave the punching bag one last blow. Good. Both of his hands were now bloodied. ???He was nervous. This punching bag was his to decimate as he pleased. In two days, however, it would be Jaden Li. He did not want to. But he knew he would.
