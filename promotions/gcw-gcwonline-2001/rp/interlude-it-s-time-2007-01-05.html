Interlude: It's Time

5 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/05/07 ~London, UK~ ~Sheraton Park Lane Hotel~ Alexis looked at the food that had been delivered and she felt the sour taste come up in her mouth. It had been a bad last few weeks for her. She didn't appear to be getting any better. But she had to get finished packing before heading to Barcelona, Spain for a few days. Virgil and Lia had sent specifically for her for this week's festivities, and she could not let them down. Plus it was a great time to be with them after the separation over the last few months. Although she couldn't really say she would have had that separation any other way. She had joined the Order of the Hammer, got to interrogate her former teacher who had turned out to be a major asshole, found a boyfriend, and started to actually... live her life. But she had been with Lia and Virgil for a few years, and had become good friends, and this, she wouldn't miss for the world... Even if she had a raging stomach flu and hadn't been able to keep much down for almost two weeks now, and it had Gray worried almost as sick as she was. She was lucky that Lia and Virgil weren't susceptible to the common viruses that humans were, otherwise she'd have to say no to seeing them at this time. She put one last thing into her bag and zipped it up. ?Damnit, I can't resist...? Quickly she reached down and grabbed one of the hot Chinese sticky buns filled with a pork paste from the dim sum she'd ordered, and was still waiting for Gray to come back up for one last lunch before she flew out... It was something that she had for the first time a few weeks ago, but couldn't for the life of her stop wanting. She ate one down, almost wolfing it.. heh, funny when her boyfriend was a freakin' werewolf to say that. She licked the stickiness off of her fingers, savoring the taste of the candied pork filling. But it was a mistake... She rushed, almost tripping over the rug on the floor, sliding into the bathroom to empty the contents of her stomach. It only took a couple of heaves and the delicacy was expelled. She wiped the sweat off her brow. ?what is wrong with me?? ~Downstairs~ Gray walked into the hotel, his hand resting on the package in his right from jeans pocket. He'd done something he never thought he'd do. Now, he just had to show that he had the guts to actually do it. It had been a few months since he and Alexis had taken a budding friendship and made it more, hiding it behind people's backs, other then his brother and sister-in-law who knew all too well. ?Hey, where are you heading?? Speaking of his brother. ?Heading up to get Alexis. The cab is here to take us to the airport for her flight. Then I'm coming back and putting you through your paces to get ready for this match.? Josh smiled at him, but couldn't beat the feeling of nervousness coming off of his brother's emotional state. It was sometimes rough being an empath. ?Dude, you aren't worried about this match are you? They're chicks.? ?I'll tell Dani you said that.? ?Okay, you got me. But still...they didn't even really beat Blaise and Ukyo. They had help.? Gray put his hands up. ?Look, dude. I know they're Order. I know they're good. But we're the best. We're the champions of not only GCW but also PTC. They're good, yes. But they're NOT that good.? Gray reached out and smacked his brother upside the head. ?Don't get a big head here. They're former number one contenders in 4WW and now they're the contenders here. Don't underestimate them. You do that, you're on the your way to being pinned in that ring. Got it?? Josh nodded his head to him. And they stood there looking at each other, silent for a few short moments. Suddenly Josh blurted out something. ?You didn't?? Gray looked surprised. ?Didn't what?? ?Your front pocket. You've got your mom's ring out.? ?No I don't.? Josh smirked. ?You can't hide it dude. Why would you have your mom's ring out? You aren't thinking of...? Gray sighed loudly. ?Yes, I'm thinking. But I don't know yet. There was a jeweler down the street who offered to resize it. My mother wasn't a small woman. However, Alexis rings fit on my pinky finger.? Josh's smirk changed to a smile. ?So you got it resized. You still don't have the balls I do.? ?Dude it took you months to pop the question.? Josh smiled. ?Yeah, it'll take you even more. Why? Cause you're scared. Well you've got to get stuff done, I'm off man. I'll talk to you later.? Josh left his brother behind, heading out the large revolving door. Gray shook his head, felt the lump in his jean pocket and entered the elevator. ~A few moments later~ Gray entered the hotel room to find Alexis once again in the bathroom. She was obviously not feeling well as she was brushing her teeth, something she did after every time she had thrown up recently while she had this bug. ?You okay, baby?? She looked over at him, holding her toothbrush in her mouth and shrugged at him. She appeared to quickly hurry up and spit the toothpaste and water out of her mouth. Some quick swishing of water and she was able to then talk again without looking like some rabid animal. ?I guess. I just wish it would stop and let me finally eat something more then broth and the occasional potato chip.? He walked over and wrapped his arms around her tiny waist. ?Especially one of those dim sum buns, huh?? He could see the left open container and the one that was missing. ?Yeah. You caught me. But it looked and at first, tasted so good. I really need to get rid of this. I guess you're lucky you've got that super duper immune system. Only one thing can effect you.? ?Yeah. You ready to go see Lia and Virgil for a few days?? She reached back, over her shoulders and tried to wrap her hands around his neck, as best she could. He as a foot taller then her and much more around then her small frame. ?Of course. It's been a while. And what I've been asked to do, is going to be an honor. I hear Mr. Childress is also going to be there?? ?That's what I heard, too. Should make it interesting.? She nodded and then pulled away from him. ?He'll be immune to whatever I have, right?? Gray nodded in reply. She tilted her head and looked at him. ?You're being quiet. You don't want to see me go away for a few days, do you?? His mouth formed something similar to a scowl. He answered truthfully. ?No. We've been together for months now, never separated. I think I've become addicted. These are going to be a long four days for me. It's been so long since I've let someone get so close.? She slinked up next to him and wrapped her arms around him, putting her ear next to his chest, right above his heart. ?It's only a few days. But I know what you mean. Part of me wants to stay, but what I've been asked to do I can't pass up. If you didn't have this match to get ready for, I'd want you there.? He shook his head. ?No, they need their privacy. Lia and I have never been very close. They don't need a Garou snooping around anyway. There are still things best left not done when it comes to that one. Maybe one day we'll be able to get along a lot better. Blaise isn't even going, it's a small private ceremony. You've known her for years. Go and be with your friends.? Alexis smiled. ?Well I'm stealing a shirt and a pair of your sweats, so I have something that smells like you. Just too bad I can't steal something like a teddy bear or a trinket.? A trinket? Gray listened back to the words of his brother just a few short moments ago downstairs. ?Alex, can I ask you a personal question?? ?Yes, and you know the answer for most of them you've asked me. Yes, I'll try anything with you at least once.? She smiled as her hand went up to her hair to brush it back, looking innocent and naive. ?Not that type of question. Something a bit more personal then that. Tell me, Alex. What would you call your perfect man? The man you'd want to be your mate? Your husband?? Her smile drifted away. ?What do you mean?? ?Just what I asked. What are you truly looking for in a man to spend the rest of your life with?? She pulled her lower lip in and chewed on it a bit. ?No answer?? She stopped chewing her lip and answered him. ?If you must have an answer, I would want someone who's understanding of what I need. And of course, tall, dark, handsome and got a good set of tattoos. I've found I'm rather attracted to those.? His visage didn't change. ?You know I've taken a big leap in everything.? ?Yeah, I know.? She started to look nervous. ?What are you getting at?? Gray shook his head as he did when he felt the mental fuzz going about. He walked over and put his hands upon her arms. ?Alexis, before you go, there is something I really need to do here. I've not let anyone get this close for a long time. You know what I am and you're not only fearless of me, but you have loved me. You have become something I find I need in my life at a time when everyone else was pulling away.? He looked her in the eyes. She felt her nerves increase, not knowing what he was going to do. ?Alex, there is a trinket you could take with you. A piece of jewelry that I hold very dear as it has sentimental family value to it.? ?Really?? ?Yes, it was owned by my late mother, given to her by my father.? ?I'd love to have the honor of being given something like that.? His smile returned. ?Well you'd have to answer a question and I'll let you have it.? ?A question? What would that be?? He reached down into his front jean pocket and pulled out the case. He held it in his hand and and then looked her in the eyes. With his other hand he opened it up to reveal the now resized diamond ring that his father had given to his mother when he had proposed. He saw as her eyes went large looking at the rather large stone and golden band. ?Holy... it's beautiful. So what's the question that I would need to answer?? She barely had it out of her mouth when he dropped down onto one knee in front of her. ?Know that we don't need to have a concrete date set yet, we can plan things out, especially when not many others know. But in the end, it doens't change what I'm feeling here.? She couldn't take her eyes off the ring, not saying anything. ?You ready?? She didn't answer. ?Alex... you ready?? ?What?? ?The question...? ?Yeah.? He proceeded to ask the question. ?Alexis Deanna Key... would you do me the honor of becoming my wife?? ?What?? He gave a few moments and then asked the question the more traditional manner. ?Alexis... will you marry me?? It then hit her what this ring was. Both of the Brand Brothers had been given their respective mother's engagement rings to use when they carried on the family. In the case of Gray's mother, she had died while Gray was young, ?Alex?? She hadn't answered yet. She felt lost as to where she was in the sparkle coming off the ring. ?Alex? We don't' have to set a date yet or anything like that. I just want to know that you're going to continue to be there for me. So again, will you marry me?? She snapped out of her daze and looked him in the eyes. ?Yes, Gray. Yes.. I'd love to be married to you.? She dove at him, wrapping her arms around his neck. He positioned her to sit upon his stabilizing knee and pulled the ring out of it's case to put it upon her finger. ~To Be Continued~
