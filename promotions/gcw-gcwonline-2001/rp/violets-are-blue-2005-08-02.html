Violets are blue...

2 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/2/2005 Halfway across the world his twin would be sleeping and here he was stepping off the 747 to walk down the ramp that led into the busy Tokyo airport. Erik VanLorne was tired and irritable. Running agitated fingers through his hair the tall blond wall of muscle headed to pick up his bag before moving to the custom's line. Thankfully this time it was a short line. Within a few minutes he was plopping his bag on the counter for the custom's agent to rifle through as she asked him his business in Tokyo. ' I am visiting an old friend.' It wasn't technically a lie. He was here to find Kanwazawa san's estate and get those diamonds back. Damn Japanese and their stubborn natures. The little Oriental woman looked him up and down a moment before plucking up her stamp and slapping it on his passport. Passing it back to him she smiled politely and spoke in her broken English. ' Enjoy your stay in Japan Mistah VanLorne.' A simple nod was given as he collected his bag and passport heading through the metal detector and out into the main section of the bustling airport. As always, he received hundreds of awe inspired looks from the local people as he stood a good foot over the rest of them. He was a giant here, a spectacle of unusual proportions, which also made him a target. Flipping his bag onto his broad back dangling the thing from his tapered digits he headed towards the front doors of the terminal he was and stepped out into the bright sunlight. Immediately his eyes snapped shut a grimace etching on those rugged features as he fished for his shades and brought them onto his face. ' Master Kanwazawa is expecting you Erik sama.' Immediately Erik stiffened slowly turning to peer at the smaller frame of one Hirohoto, his friend's number one right hand man. Masking his face with neutrality Erik allowed a small grin onto his face and nodded. ' Well then, let's not keep the man waiting eh?' Hiro nodded and gestured to a small car just a few paces down the sidewalk urging Erik to precede him. Inwardly Erik was cursing up a storm. He should have known the Yakuza leader would have kept tabs on his activity after inquiring about the diamonds. He only hoped this meeting would not go as badly as his gut was signaling it might be. As the door was opened to the backseat by another one of the tattooed freaks he nodded and handed off his bag to be placed in the trunk as he slipped inside with Hiro climbing in the other side beside him. The trip to Kanwazawa's home was a silent one. He didn't mind it; silence was golden to him as a man of few words. Those icy orbs would stare at the house as they pulled up the long drive to the predetermined destination. He would have been here either way, but he had hoped to come here of his own volition...and preferably in the dead of night. In like a shadow, out like a ghost, that was his motto. When the car stopped his door was opened from the outside allowing him to exit the vehicle and stand turning to face the rather traditional looking home. On the wooden porch there he stood, the leader of the Kanwazawa family Hirozuko. The German Adonis moved around the car and brought his hands together before him as he bent at the waist in formal and proper greeting. ' It is good to see you again Hirozuko san.' The elderly Japanese man bowed in return with a small but cold smile on his lips. ' A pleasure Erik san. Come let us have tea and discuss things.' Nodding Erik walked up the porch stairs and slipped off his shoes before entering the confines of his old sensei’s home. Quietly he followed the man to the tearoom and took his seat where gestured to sit kneeling formally with his feet crossed at the ankles. Fists would be placed on his thighs as he watched and waited whilst Hirozuko performed the formalities of the tea ceremony. Once offered the bowl of oolong tea Erik would turn the bowl twice as was tradition and raise it to his friend bowing his head in respect before sipping of the bittersweet tea. Wiping the rim clean he turned it twice more and handed it back to the man. With the ceremony officially over Hirozuko leveled a stern gaze on his former student. ' You have come to attempt to steal the diamonds back from me Erik san. This displeases me greatly that you would lose your honor for such a thing.' And there it was, the lecture he knew would be coming and likely followed by a brutal beating if he did not turn the situation around in a hurry. Lowering his gaze a moment he took a breath and looked back up pinning his old teacher's gaze as he spoke. ' You have left me no choice Sensei sama. I would not wish to lose my honor by taking what was given, but in this I must do as I can.' ' What is so important that you wish to steal from a friend?? The answer was simple here. ' My sister's life.' The Yakuza leader's face darkened at those words for only a moment before he nodded in contemplation. ' A family member's life is worth much indeed. You will tell me everything Erik san. Leave out no detail. Is this understood?' Erik nodded and began to speak of the events of the recent weeks... Some few hours later, in Boston, Eva would startle awake as the phone rang next to her bed. Leaning over she stretched her arm across the expanse to pull the phone off its cradle. Dragging it to her ear she rolled to her back and mumbled sleepily. 'Erik it is late.' 'This is not Erik Ms. VanLorne.' Eva's brow creased as her eyes popped open. 'Who is this?' 'Never mind who I am. I have a message for you.' She frowned as the next words slipped free. 'Roses are red, violets are blue, soon Erik will be dead, and so will you.' Click. The line went dead with the buzzing tone in her ear. Eva lay there stunned a moment as she let the phone slip from numb fingers. After a moment the diva snapped out of her stunned silence and lurched from beneath her covers. Feet swung to the floor as she slammed the buttons down on the cradle and released them. Those fingers were dialing Erik's room. Ring ring.........ring ring ..........ring ring.........ring ring...ring ring. Her heart stopped, stifled with fear. Where was Erik? God let him be all right!!! Fighting down the panic she tossed the hotel room phone on its cradle and launched off her bed heading for her purse. Rummaging through the bag she produced her cell phone and dialed his cell. ' Please pick up Erik.... please!' Erik jerked as his phone vibrated in his pocket. Mumbling an apology to Hirozuko he reached into his jeans and produced the phone. It was Eva. Flipping it open he lifted it to his ear. ' Da?' ' Oh thank god you are okay Erik.' He frowned. ' Why wouldn't I be?' ' I just got a call Erik. A death threat. They said you were dead and I would be soon.' Erik's eyes shot to his former sensei, as his mind scrambled to think of something to say to ease his sister's mind. ' Eva is it likely some crazed fan. I will have the hotel security keep an eye on things. Okay? Go back to sleep, nothing it going to happen to you.' ' Okay Erik. ' After a few more words of reassurance he hung up and looked at his friend. ' Things just got worse.'
