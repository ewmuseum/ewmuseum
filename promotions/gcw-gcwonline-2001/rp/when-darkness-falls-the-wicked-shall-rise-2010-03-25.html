When Darkness Falls, The Wicked Shall Rise

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/25/10 “Our deepest fear is not that we are inadequate. Our deepest fear is that we are powerful beyond measure. It is our light, not our darkness, that most frightens us. We ask ourselves, who am I to be brilliant, gorgeous, talented, and fabulous? Actually, who are you not to be? You are a child of God. Your playing small doesn't serve the world. There's nothing enlightened about shrinking so that other people won't feel insecure around you. We are all meant to shine, as children do. We are born to make manifest the glory of God that is within us. It's not just in some of us, it's in everyone. And as we let our own light shine, we unconsciously give other people permission to do the same. As we are liberated from our own fear, our presence automatically liberates others.” Marianne Williamson The camera comes on backstage just after Bishop’s 10 minute Ironman Match for the Hardcore Title. As the camera is filming Bishop is in the locker room getting checked by medical staff of his injuries sustained in the match. Bishop: Watch what you are doing asshole. I mean can’t you see I’m in a little pain dumbass. Doctor: Sorry Mr. Steele, but I have to press on certain areas to see if you have sustained serious injuries that could possibly keep you out of action. Bishop smirks and chuckles at the doctor’s response…. Bishop: You really think I’m going to stop wrestling because you tell me too? Doctor: Well I would hope you would value my opinion, being that I’m a doctor with renowned credibility. Bishop: Look doc this is my career and my profession. When I don’t wrestle I don’t get paid and with me having a newborn son, that is not an option. Got it. Doctor: Yeah I got it, but you have sustained some broken ribs and a little internal bleeding. I suggest you take some time off and rest before your next match, but ultimately you will make your own decision, so my job here is done. Bishop: Thanks doc I really appreciate it. The doctor nods in acknowledgement and packs up and heads out. Meanwhile Bishop slowly stands up clinching his ribs. “Man this hurts like a bitch.” Bishop mumbles as he makes his way to the shower. He turns on the shower and waits for it to warm up to a warm temperature, before getting in. While in the shower soaking his body under the steam and misty warm water, his phone rings. Lil Wayne’s Steady Mobbin plays as the phone continues to ring and ring, before going to voicemail. About 10 minutes later, Bishop walks out of the shower in his towel still clinching his ribs. He walks over and sits down on the bench next to his bag and phone and sees that he has a missed call and voicemail. He turns his phone on speaker and listens to his voicemail. Voicemail: You have 1 new message. First message “Hey baby, it’s me I was just wondering how everything went tonight in your match. I hope you did well and gained that confidence and swagger back that you had all this time. Jesus is good, he really looks like you sweetie. Anyways call me when you get this. I love and I’ll talk to you later. Bye.” End of message, to delete this message press 1. Bishop hangs up from the voicemail and dials his wife’s number. He waits for an answer. Eventually Alexis answers… Alexis: Hello Bishop Bishop: Hey baby how are you doing? Alexis: I’m doing better still in a little pain from labor but nothing that I can’t handle. How are you honey? Bishop: Well honestly I’ve been better, but I’m still kickin though. Just a few broken ribs are all. Alexis: What broken ribs? What the hell happened? Bishop: I had a Hardcore style match, which you know is not my cup of tea, but I tried and didn’t succeed. Instead I got injured by a 6’8 monster named Bryan Mayhem and trusted me it wasn’t fun at all, but now I have to face to newbie’s as I would call them, but more importantly I have some unfinished business to attend in the name of Donny Diamond. Other than that I have been good. Alexis: Well I think you should come home and rest and save that for another time. Bishop: I promise that after WW128 I will come home and rest, but this is something I have to do. Alexis: But what if you get hurt worse? Bishop: I promise I won’t get hurt worse. You have to trust me. If I run away from this now, then I will be letting my fear get the best of me. This is a true test for me of my will and strength to endure pain and suffering. If I want to be a champion this is what it takes to reach that goal. Alexis: Just be careful. The last thing I want is my son to grow up and see his father in a wheelchair or something. Bishop chuckles….. Bishop: That won’t happen, but I got to go and catch my flight to Utah. I love you and I will call you when I have landed. Alexis: I love you and come home safe. Bishop: I will, I will… Bishop hangs up the phone and gets dressed. He grabs his bags and walks out of his locker room still grimacing in pain as he heads down the hallway. He arrives at the garage and opens the trunks of his car and places his bags in the trunk. He slams the trunk and gets into his car and starts the engine. He puts it into drive and speeds out of the garage heading towards the airport. The camera fades away……. Journal Entry #257 When Darkness Falls, The Wicked Shall Rise…. You know once again many have doubted me and my ability. My fans have questioned my ability to win and survive. Hell I have even questioned my ability to be the best. I mean I call myself True Perfection and lately I have been everything but Perfection, but like my father always taught me, never let the people dictate who you are and what you can become. If you know in your mind and heart that you are a champion and survivor then there is nothing that can stop you from being the best. I guess what it boils down to be is Fear. I never knew how powerful and overwhelming Fear could be, until recently. My next true test is to overcome that fear and doubt and not only prove to myself but prove to fans that they can count on me to be perfect. That they can count on me to be great. And my fans can expect the wicked to rise again. Slash and T.A. Giles, I don’t know what you are expecting the outcome of this match to be and frankly I don’t give a shit. All I know is bring everything you got and just hope that what you bring is enough to make my fear a reality…….
