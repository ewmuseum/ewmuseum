Metroids, Demonoids, MMA, and Script Botches, Oh My!

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/2009 12/11/2009 The House of von Doom Afternoon… As I’m watching a DVD to get prepared for my huge match on GCW World Wide against Kris Michaels the MMA wanna-be superstar of intense pain as my buddies Rappin’ Rick Malloy and my sports agent William Smith come in from their lunch date or whatever the hell it is they were doing. I could care less. I’ve got a big match this week that I’m going to win. After entering the living room, both of them look at me funny for some reason after seeing what is on the television. “James,” Smith says to me. “What the hell are you doing?” “I’m getting ready for my match,” I reply back to him. “This loser I’m facing is an MMA fighter or at least thinks he is. I’m telling you this is going to be a cakewalk. I’m just going to walk in there, throw him around the ring, and then pin him?” Smith then motions to the television for some reason. “But what the hell are you watching?” he says in an exasperated tone. “That’s sick!” I point to the television as I reply back to him. “Oh, that’s MMA. Like the stuff Michaels is into. He’s a member of the Men’s Mudwrestling Association.” Malloy starts laughing in the background because he knows what’s really going on. Smith plays it up well. The script I wrote for this skit is working perfectly so far. This is better than if Dennis Miller was the host of my promo. Anyway, we keep going with it. “Uhhhh…James,” Smith says. “Yeah, buddy,” I say as I turn in his direction. “What is it?” “You do realize that MMA stands for Mixed Martial Arts, not Men’s Mudwrestling Association.” I raise an eyebrow looking like I have no idea what the hell he’s talking about. “What the hell is Mixed Martial Arts?” I ask him while looking very confused about what he is talking about. “Is that like when a Chinese guy gets married to a black chick or something?” In the background I can hear Rick Malloy laughing like a hyena over that one. “No,” he responds. “It’s stuff like the UFC. You know, the Ultimate Fighting Championship.” “Yeah, I know it,” I tell him. “That’s what he’s into,” Smith continues. “Not this homosexual mud wrestling or whatever it is. Speaking of which, how the hell did you find this video anyway?” This brings an evil grin to my face. “I had one of my minions steal it from AXEL Action’s DVD collection,” I tell him. “Oh…” he says. “Well that explains a lot.” After a short awkward silence, I clear my throat and get us back on topic. “Ok, since this isn’t the MMA that my so-called opponent participates in,” I say. “I do have a back-up plan.” I get up and walk over to the entertainment center. After digging around, I find the video game I’m looking for and hold it up for them to see. “UFC Unleashed for the Xbox 360. I can play this for ten hours and learn every hold and counter hold that he will be using. I’m going to walk over this guy like he’s a doormat. By just doing something as simple as playing a video game, I will be able to know his strategy and everything that he will do. This is almost going to be too easy.” “James, don’t you think that you’re being overconfident?” he asks. After this I over act my response on purpose as Malloy has to cover his mouth in order to stay in character. Of course the idiot drops his script. Yeah, this is going about as well as an Ed Wood picture. No retakes either…damn. “Nah, of course not. Me, overconfident? PREPOSTEROUS! This guy is nothing but a pushover. I’m going to be making this guy look like the jobber that he is. I’m going to beat this guy with a side headlock with both of his shoulders on the mat. Hell, this guy is so pathetic, I bet he’ll give up during instructions.” Smith throws his arms up in disgust. “OH PLEASE STOP!” he exclaims. I turn and look at him strangely. “But why?” I ask. Smith starts freaking out like he’s auditioning to play Archie Bunker in an upcoming movie remake or something. “Because you’re making a mockery of wrestling!” he exclaims. “This is a sport that you used to love but now all you’re doing is making it into a total joke! You’re walking around the show with a fake title around your waist while you’re hanging out with a vampire! And you almost got into a fight with a pole column last week! You’re losing your mind, James. You really are.” I no sell him much to his dismay. In the background, Malloy is still covering his mouth to keep from laughing out loud. He looks like a bearded version of Bryan Danielson from the infamous Hybrid Dolphins ‘Pod promo.’ “People have been saying that for years,” I reply with a grin. “I’m still as sane as I ever was.” Smith rolls his eyes as I say this, not believing a word of what I’m saying. “In other words, instead of taking this seriously we’re just going to sit around and play video games all night?” he asks. “EXACTLY!” I exclaim loudly as Malloy is now laughing hysterically in the background. Smith and I look over as we can only shake our heads in disgust. “I should’ve hired actors,” I tell Smith who nods his head in agreement. “Yep,” he replies. * * * 05/15/2009 Inside of the dusty, cobweb covered remnants of the Church of the Unholy’s main chapel in Rome, Italy, Count Von Macabre has been fixing up the place for a few hours. The boredom of being the only member of the congregation has severely hampered his ability to going on nightly hunts to kill humans and drink their blood. As he’s cleaning, someone walks into the Church. He stands up and turns to the man. The man seems vaguely familiar for some reason. “I’ve been here for weeks,” the Count says. “And still no one shows up…until you. Who are you, anyway?” “My name is von Doom,” the man tells him with a grin. “I’m one of the main people of this organization. Or, judging by the state of the Church, I may be the only one left. Hell, the two of us might even be the last two members of the Church of the Unholy left walking the face of the earth.” The Count nods his head in agreement. “You may be right about that,” he replies von Doom. “This place has been empty for far too long. Our organization has been around for over a thousand years. And it’ll be around for a thousand more if we do this right.” Von Doom seems very happy to hear that from the Count. “You’ve got that right,” Von Doom says. “Somebody sold us down the river and we need to find whomever it is before they do it again. I don’t want them to get away with it. I’ve spent the past few months trying to figure out who it was but to no avail.” “Got any suspicions?” the Count asks. Von Doom nods his head. “Oh yeah,” he responds. “I have some leads but nothing’s panned out.” “So what are we going to do?” the Count asks. You can almost see the wheels turning in that skull of von Doom’s at this point. He’s had a plan for awhile and now he has a partner to pull it off. “I think we need to get you some wrestling training,” Von Doom answers before grinning deviously. “Why?” the Count asks, a bit perplexed by this idea. “Because I think GCW needs some unholiness,” Von Doom responds. The Count seems to catch on to what von Doom is saying and nods his head in agreement. He remembers hearing about a Church sponsored wrestling tag team that was creating chaos. Now he’s met one of the two men from that team. The other, AXEL Action, is no longer a Church member. Until von Doom tries to suck him back into this madness, that is… * * * 12/15/2009 Las Vegas, Nevada Back at my place, I’ve called forth my various minions and associates for a team meeting. Count Von Macabre is here along with coke snorting rap wrestler Rapmaster Rick Malloy and my sports agent William Smith. They’re all seated on the couch while I am in the lounge chair. It’s at this point that I tell them my brilliant scheme for Kris Michaels this week on World Wide. “Ok, so this is what we’re going to do,” I tell them. “We’re going to get me ready for my match this week by watching some related material so I know exactly what I’m getting into.” Smith looks at me incredulously. “What are you talking about?” he asks. “I rented Fight Club, Mortal Kombat, Street Fighter, and Brokeback Mountain on DVD from Blockbuster,” I tell him before holding up the DVDs for all to see. Smith looks like he’s about blow a gasket. “STOP!” he yells at me. “JUST STOP!” I throw my arms up incredulously, acting like I don’t know what he’s so upset about. “What?!” Smith’s face is bright red now as he starts yelling in my direction. “YOU ARE CLEARLY NOT TAKING THIS MATCH SERIOSULY!” he exclaims loudly. “Pfft…why should I?” I say to him, shrugging off his paranoia like it‘s nothing. “First off we have B. Brian Blair and Jumpin’ Jim Brunzell on the roster now and they’re probably going to dress like the Colony. Then I’m facing a guy who’s in the wrong sport. This is going to be easy. I can beat this guy a million different ways. When he sees me standing across the ring from him, he’ll probably give up during instructions.” He looks even more pissed now. Malloy is trying to keep a straight face throughout all of this but has to cover his mouth to keep from laughing. The Count looks on like it’s no big deal. “YOU STILL DON’T GET IT, DO YOU?!” he yells out loudly. “He is the most dangerous opponent you’ve faced since coming back to GCW. This guy can punish you and injure you. An injury is the last thing you need. You need to start taking this match seriously, otherwise you’re going to be in a world of hurt.” I act like I’m shocked at his yelling but I’m really faking it, which makes me seem even more insincere. “You’re right,” I reply. “I’m sorry I’m not taking this seriously but when you’re as talented as I am, you can afford to do that.” The Count then raises his head to interject. “Hey James,” he asks. “What are we going to do about System-X? They stole the actual GCW Tag Team titles from Caldera’s office and now are running around with them.” “I’ll come up with a plan for them,” I reply with a grin. “They’re mentally incompetent. There’s no issue there. We can outsmart them. Mortifera and the Schizophrenic Demonoids are the real threats.” The Count nods his head in agreement. “Mortifera kidnapped me and tried to scare me,” he says. “The idiots didn’t finish the job so we can easily take care of them.” “What they did was both pointless and stupid,” I respond. “They used heel tactics against heels. Stupid move. That shows that those two big slow moving dinosaurs are nothing but cowards just like I’ve been saying for weeks.” Malloy looks at me funny for some reason after hearing this. “James,” Rick says to me. “Yeah, Rick,” I say as I turn in his direction. “What is it?” “What the hell is a Demonoid?” he asks me. Good question. “I think it’s related to either a hemorrhoid or a Metroid,” I reply to him as he starts laughing. “Or a steroid because taking ‘roids is the only way those two idiots can beat us in the ring.” “What’s Metroid?” the Count asks with a puzzled look on his face. I turn and give him a look. “You don’t know what Metroid is?” I ask him. He shakes his head. “No,” he replies. “Oh my GOD!” I exclaim loudly. “Buddy, we need to get you acquainted with Samus Aran and the space pirate infested Metroid universe.” “Great,” Smith mutters as I go over to set up the Nintendo Wii on big flat screen television so that we can play. “So much for training…” Malloy and the Count wait patiently for me to get the gaming system set up as Smith gets up and goes to the kitchen to get himself some alcohol. What a wonderful way to get ready to crush Michaels like the bug he is. I’ll be ready for him. And if my moves don’t work, I’ll sick some Metroids on him and then pin his blood drained corpse.
