Memories and Destiny

13 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/13/09 Standing and leaning against a large bay window looking down at the people below. His eyes wander from the buildings below to other buildings around them. He looks at the cars and then the people quickly bustling through the streets and sidewalks. He turns his head slightly looking over at Samuelsson. “Just like ants, Always busy busy busy and not paying attention to what is right before them.” Samuelsson shakes his head and turns and throws a few papers onto a table near Shaman. “You better take a look at this.” reaching down Shaman lifts the papers and reads them. He raises an eyebrow and smirks.” They aren't serious are they?” Shaman turns and walks towards the sofa and sits down reaching to the side table and grabs a cell phone. He dials a number and brings the cell phone to his ear. “Yeah Kawanaha get me the old WWA files.” a few seconds pass as Kawanaha responds. “Yep those are the ones. I need a few phone numbers off that.” Shaman leans back and clicks the phone off. “So GCW brass has decided to pit me in a match against Surreal. The guy and myself go back several years. In fact you were there when he was. Of course you and he weren't that great of friends.” Samuelsson chuckles as he opens a beer. “I wasn't friends with anyone. I only respected you because you were the only guy to comeback from injury and come after me.” Shaman nods as he turns his head looking out the window. "You see Jorge Surreal and i go back a bit. He and i have talked in PTC and in the WWA. We been through alot of similar situations. Now GCW is pitting friend against friend. Be it a strong fight or a weak fight there is one thing GCW doesn't realize. Surreal and myself are going to go out of our ways and fight harder than before. WE will not give each other a quarter." Chuckling Shaman leans back on the couch. Samuelsson grabs a chair and sits in it looking over at Shaman. "You know we had alot of conflicts over the years in PTC with the tournaments. Big matches big shows and even more shocking results where people did not expect us to excel very far." Shaman looks over at Samuelsson. "Good point, but i met some good friends there as well. One of them was Lia Ambrosi, Another was Bryan Mayhem who i formed Oblivion here with. Bryan is gone and Oblivion is dead, but does not mean that Mayhem and myself are no9t still brothers in arms." Samuelsson looks at Shaman who places each hand together finger tip to finger tip. "You know Jorge since i came to GCW from the WWA i have been Television Champion, US Champion twice, and Tag Team Champion. I have also gotten a shot at the Heavyweight Championship. Lost the match, but it was a close nail biter. Problem is Jorge that there is more i want." Shaman purses his lips tightly before taking a deep breath. "What that is is bothering me. I am at the top of my game and we have a wave of new people coming and going. I am dangerous and people are forgetting this. Surreal knows i am dangerous. He and i have a grudging respect for one another as friends and combatants. I am sure he is not going to under estimate me as an opponent." Shaman leans forward and slowly stands up and looks at Samuelsson. "We are going to have to go see my Grandmother at some point." Samuelsson looks at Shaman. "I'll pass that woman freaks me out. She is like some devilishly huge demon packed into a tiny old woman's body. She is freakishly unnerving." Shaman chuckles and laughs deeply. "That woman is a trip regardless." Shaman pulls out an envelope and opens it. "You know a fan of mine wrote some Poetry on a poetry website awhile back and i wanted to share it with you. Has alot of meaning." Shaman opens the envelope and begins reading the poetry... ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- A Bar Fly's Patterns. The early mists rise above the eyes. As my mind reels at the thoughts of the weeks gone by. Is it the death that bothers me or is it the heart of the matter. The pained chords that are being pulled constantly. As the darkness and hatred of being deceived consume me. You ask yourself why does this happen to me. Then you realize you have no control over it at this time. The room begins to spin as you realize your mistakes. Then raise another round of things as you stir. The mirrored glasses and myriad colors begin to blur. Then suddenly you ask yourself was it really me in there. Then you begin to understand as liquids flow there course. You feel reprieved of all emotions. You trudge onwards and then order another round. Andrew Eugene O'Brien ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Shaman closes the paper and walks to the refrigerator and opens it pulling out a beer of his own. "You see in a way it describes our own circles and patterns. It is like we are on a path that is decaying and nobody sees this, but us. As we are the gardeners to a fate for only those who are meant to be plowed under." Shaman stands clenching a fist. "Like weeds they need to be plucked. Snuffed out of existence once they are within or grasp or upon our path." Samuelsson stands and looks at Shaman. "We are the Saints of Pain, We are the Lords of Agony. We are Mortifera the masters of our own destiny. There are none stronger than us or more powerful than us if we set our paths before ourselves. We take what we want." Shaman raises his hand at Samuelsson. "Right now this coming match is about me and only me. Like that joke Wolfeinator was against you. One punch really?' laughing Shaman lifts the beer to his lips and drinks it. Shaman turns and walks across the room and lifts his electric guitar and plugs it in and grins as he turns the knobs on the amp up all the way. He rips out a few heavy riffs. Grinning as he drinks some more beer. "Come on Jorge i know you play base guitar and you like to jam." Samuelsson shrugs and reaches behind the couch never leaving his chair pulling the bass guitar out as he practices a few slap chords. He nods towards Shaman. "I was practicing before you came back. We have to have other hobbies that ease our minds." Shaman leans back and begins to play a few chords and stops "Surreal is a friend and finally looks to have found the path. Jorge i don't want any interference from you unless Lucas Quinton comes. If he does you have my permission to disembowel him. He will not cross my path nor will he interfere in my match." Shaman narrows his gaze as he drags the pick across the strings creating an disturbing sounding chord. His fingers seem to play the melodramatic chords followed by the disturbing riffs. Samuelsson's powerful bass riffs echo through the room seemingly complementing the harsh sounds from Shaman's guitar play. Shaman slowly stops as he looks at Samuelsson. "Some say we are born into the grave. Do you believe we are born into the rgave or are we born into our destined paths to grasp ahold of?" Shaman looks down at the guitar listening. Samuelsson looks at Shaman. " We are warriors and masters of our own domain. We control that which we choose to do. If we wish to conquer the weak and cause the strong to fear us that is which we will do." Shaman nods and drinks some more of his beer. "Guess there is only one thing elft to do. That is to send a message to all those who are watching." Shaman runs the beer bottle down the strigns causing a strained whine from the guitar. "We make them understand that which they have released upon the world. Sleep well, but not too soundly for pain has found you. The Physical terrorist has come for your nightmares." Shaman laughs deeply as the camera fades to black. Note: The poem included belongs to Shaman's handler Andrew E O'Brien
