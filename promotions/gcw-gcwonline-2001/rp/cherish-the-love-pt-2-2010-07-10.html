Cherish The Love Pt. 2

10 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/10/10 One week ago. "So, what do we do from here?" "We don’t think." Sliding up behind his wife, Chris wrapped his arms around the waist of wife, pressing his cold lips to the back of her head. He whispered as if his directions were one big secret. "And we don’t tell anybody." Dolled up in a black and white form fitting sundress with floral patterns, Tessa Windsor wandered away from him aimlessly through the spacious lobby of the Jade Palace hotel. Even during their hours journey back to the hotel, the pint sized diva had yet to figure out just what her husband had up his sleeve. Chris, always the mastermind, had mentioned something about sneaking in undetected, catching the kids off guard, and laughing hysterically. It actually sounded a bit cruel to her, but what did she know? She had only ever been the companion in his devious little plots. "You know, I kind of can’t tell anybody if you don’t even tell me." In his nonchalant ways that had been missing since the days of Banned & Exiled~!, Chris humored only himself, "That’s why we don’t think about it." Tessa blinked, as if that were a completely foreign concept to her. "You’ll have to teach me that trick one day." She pivoted around to face the man behind her, completely befuddled. "So what do we really do?" Chris shrugged, "Say surprise?" Truly he had no clue of the answer she was looking for. She of all people should have known he never thought things out, particularly when it came to parenting. He figured all they had to do is walk through the door and that would be good enough, at least from a father’s perspective. "That’s it?" "Sounds good enough to me." She shuddered. Tessa always preferred things to go into things with a plan. Playing impromptu with Chris never worked out well for her. Somehow, despite her maturity and his lack thereof, she always seemed to be the one that walked out looking like a fool. "Are we inviting Johnny along too?" Chris stopped dead in his path, his blue eyes glazing over in anger. "Why the fuck would we invite that piece of shit along? He’s purposely throwing us against each other at Rampage?" Tessa’s own eyes started to roll. Somehow, she ended up being the one responsible for watching after the kid that Chris had brought along overseas to train as a so called favor. Not only was the one having to provide the poor boy with hospitality, it was also her that to remember all the little conversational facts. "So you brought him overseas for nothing?" "Oh." Chris sighed, his memory jogged by the threatening glare of a scorned woman locked straight on him, "The Kid, yeah… him… he can come too I guess." By that point she had to pace back a good six feet to get back to his side. "Gee, don’t act so excited." It managed to squeeze a grin out of him. He squealed back with his bottom lip puffed out. "Buuuutttt!" "Way to keep things secrets." She pressed her finger to her lips as she turned back his way. "You might want to hush if you want to actually want to surprise them." His brow perked, "That the plan?" Her eyes rolled again. Now he was just being obnoxiously mean for the sake of his own humor. Instead of slapping his arm as she should have, Tessa yanked her oversized purse open in pursuit of their room key. By this point they were within throwing distance of their room, there was no sense in trying to plan any kind of elaborate surprise for their kids for the evening. Coming back far earlier than planned seemed good enough, from there they would play it by ear. Dashing past Tess to the door, he whispered loud enough for the entire hall to hear, "You ready for this?" Her head nodded ever so slightly as she tossed the set of plastic cards in Chris’ direction. He seemed more than ready to lead the charge into the room, almost suspiciously so. If his intentions were to surprise Tessa instead he was doing an incredibly awful job of being inconspicuous about it. He rallied one last time, "Let’s go!" Sliding the card in, he flung the door open the best he could given that it would eventually come back in his direction. Tessa relished in the short lived silence, well aware of the momentary chaos about to ensue once the kids processed what was going on. Still, as enthralling as the silence was, she could not wait to see excitement wash over their faces once they realized they were actually going to be able to go somewhere interesting. "Surprise!" While it was only Chris that shouted it was enough to elicit a grand response. As expected both boys launched off the couch and shrieked out in delightful surprise as they bolted towards the door. Foreign television was far too boring to their active minds, and talking with Johnny was no where ever near as cool as the conversations they had with their dearly missed Uncle Markie, who walked out of their lives without any sort of explanation. "You’re back! You’re back!" The boy’s chorus echoed throughout the suite, pulling fifteen year old Christa’s attention away from the cell phone conversation she was having, no doubt long distance gossip with her best friend back at home. The teen either had no concept of just what the term roaming meant to her parents wallet, or she simply did not care. Instead of joining in, she plugged her opposite ear and walked as far away as she could. Turning back towards Tessa, who had perched herself in the doorway, Chris was sure to rub in his sentiments like any good father would, tongue out and all. "Told you it’d be worth it." She lightly smiled, embracing each of the boys as she worked her way in through the chaos. Of course it was going to be worth it. She could not imagine why he would dare suggest she thought otherwise. Regardless, she pecked both sons on the cheek, and proposed the question of the night. "So what do you guys want to do?" The bickering that ensued between was to be expected. Rarely did they ever consent with the others suggestions. Instead of trying to referee it, Tessa turned her attention towards Johnny, who sat back on the couch completely clueless as to what he should do from there. "Chris tell you anything ‘bout tonight?" In her head she had already predicted the shaking of his head side to side that followed. Of course Chris wouldn’t tell him. He never told anyone else aside from Markus any of his plans. Her head turned back her husband’s way in search of any form of courteous invite. Instead of looking back their way, Chris was far more content joining in on the boys little debate. She was sure if he had his way the boys would end up wrestling for a winner on one of the twin beds with Chris coaching them along the way. She vehemently loathed just the thought of it. The last thing she wanted for any one of their kids was to end up like she had at the hands of Jay Terror just a few weeks back. She looked back Johnny’s way with a faint grin. "You sure you want to end up like him?" Johnny chuckled lightly, a bit hesitant to answer. He knew Tessa was kidding, but it did little to ease his nerves. His was still in utter shell shock that Chris had even offered to train him, let alone let him travel along Tessa and the rest of their family. Chris chimed right in with his own bout of humor just as Tessa figured he would. "Why would anyone want to be like me? I mean, seriously, who wants to make money hand over fist and raise incredible children with a breathtakingly beautiful wife?" "You’re horrible at sucking up." Chris grinned, turning his attention towards his pupil. "Can you believe her?" Johnny chuckled, doing his best to fit in. "She’s right." Chris shook his head side to side, never once losing his smug little grin. His attention turned back towards his wife who had plopped herself down on the end of one of the boys twin mattress. "Where’s the munchkin at? I haven’t seen her yet." Tessa shrugged, leaving Johnny to answer. "Up in the loft." He answered anxiously shuffled from side to side. Johnny hated the whole babysitting thing, hated it. But if it earned him the right to train under Chris at no cost to him he would do it until he dropped dead. "She okay?" Tessa questioned, her brow instinctively peaking. Johnny played it off naturally. "Oh yeah." He paused, assumedly to sound like he actually knew what he was saying. "She wouldn’t stop crying about a half hour ago so I figured she just needed some sleep." "Probably that cold she’s been fighting off. Sorry." "Eh, it’s cool. No big deal." "Thanks. I’ll go get her." Just as fast as she had sat down, she was up again. It was obvious how much Tessa had grown to loathe sitting around not doing anything after spending two weeks in a hotel room having to recoup her knee. It certainly increased her appreciation for her career. Course it probably was not the smartest idea for her to be charging up the stairs fast as she could. "Alright little Linni, time to wake up!" The pinging pain in her knee following her ascent served as a rousing reminder to Tessa as to why her husband insisted on her taking it easy before her world title shot at Rampage. She pushed it to the back of her mind. Her kids were far more important than any sort of pain. "Come on Linni." Curling up beside her youngest, Tessa gently brushed away the stray raven colored bangs that concealed her youngest pale complexion. Admittedly she felt a bit guilty having to drag her youngest along when she knew she her Aislinn was not feeling well. "Come on Linni. We’re going on a trip." It still failed to rally the toddler to her feet. "Aislinn?" Silence. Tessa sighed. The girl could be as hard headed as her father, especially when she was not feeling well. "Aislinn, lets go. Daddy’s home too." Still nothing. Tessa surrendered with an exasperated sigh, wrapping her arms around the tot. Suddenly it was Tessa that forgot how to breathe. "Oh god!" How she even managed to squeeze the panicked whisper out of her vocal cords was beyond Tessa. She prodded her cold lifeless tot again. "Aislinn, please? Please!" Shallow breaths were the only thing the Tessa managed to elicit; she cracked. "Chris!" It was the only word she could manage to squeeze out before her emotions manage to render her just as helpless as her spawn. By now she had Aislinn clutched tightly against her chest, her eyes darting around the room for some sort of resolution; that is if she could managed to keep her mind settled for more than five seconds. "Please hurry!" A hysterical mess, she took in the deepest breath she could manage in between sobs. Embracing Aislinn tighter than she had the day her youngest was born; Tessa closed her eyes and prayed. Forget the world title. Tessa needed her one miracle now. ---- "You okay?" It seemed to be the only thing her husband ever wanted to ask her lately. Tight lipped as she could be, the only thing Chris ever managed to get out of Tessa was a nod. She hated to talk about, hated to hear about it. Hell, she could hardly remember any of it. "You sure?" She nodded again, refusing to look up her husband’s way. He already knew the real answer. There was no reason for her to visually confirm it. It would only make thing harder on her. ---- "Please hurry Chris! Please!" Even with Chris within her vantage point at the top of the stairs, Tessa found it impossible to disengage her mind from repeat mode. "Please Chris!" Each plea more desperate than the next, Chris had to fight against his own fight or flight system to get any kind of answer out of his wife as to what she needed him to do, or to what was even going on. "PLEASE CHRIS!" It took Tessa choking on her own tears for him to finally manage to squeeze in some form of a response. "Tess, calm down. What’s wrong?" "She won’t wake up, Chris!" Her motherly panic rendered her just as helpless as her motionless offspring. "I don’t know what to do!" "I need you to calm down Tess." Placidness was far from Chris’ strong suit. If it had been anyone else thoughtlessly screaming at him, Chris Storm would have exploded by now. The only thing worse than that was desperation. He was horribly awkward with consoling. Right now he had no choice but to though as his wife fell apart before his very eyes. "What do we do? I don’t know what to do!" "She need help Tess. I need you to let go." His words fell on deaf ears, Tessa only clutching tighter to their spawn. "I don’t know what to do!" This time he commanded. "I’m going to get help, Tess. Let her go." Plucking the tear stained tot from Tessa’s arms, his heart was miles ahead of his mind. He needed Tessa calm. He needed Aislinn to be okay. He said the only thing a husband could at a time like this. "She’s going to be okay. " ---- "You want to talk about it?" No matter how Chris Storm phrased it, his wife never seemed to concede. "What’s to talk about?" Her attention always returned to her task at hand. It didn’t matter what time of day he caught her at. She always seemed to be busy making the bed, scheduling travel arrangements, reading a book, or trying to fall asleep. "We’re going to have to talk about it eventually." "Not tonight." The light turned off, Chris left alone in the dark. ---- "I need a doctor!" After tonight Tessa swore she would never view the cliché television drama line the same. She kept on her husband’s heels as he rushed through the empty hotel hallways, praying that someone understood their desperation. "Anyone, please!" Though he would never admit it, Tessa knew her husband was nearing his own breaking point. A man could only do battle with his emotions for so long before they charged back, violently stabbing him in the heart. "We need a hospital! An emergency number! Something please!" A door crept open. "Please help!"Chris plead. Tessa prayed father time would be on their side just for this once. ---- Tessa Windsor had always done the best she could to dodge reporters. They had a way of fabricating everything she said. This week she had no choice but to at least acknowledge their persistent knocking at her door, it seemed to be her only key to redemption. "Ms. Windsor?" "What?" "Can I get a few words with you about your daughter?" "Not a chance." The door slammed shut. At least there was some way to express her frustrations. ---- "Is she going to be okay?" Chris looked to the doctor; the doctor looked down at Tessa who had fallen asleep in Chris’ embrace. Neither seemed ready to answer first. Chris bit the bullet. His hand trailed across Tessa’s cheeks, wishing he had that power in some way, shape, or form, keep her subconsciously comforted in her slumber. It might save her from the emotional breakdown he knew she was on the verge of yet again. "Hard to say." Chris looked away from wife to save himself from crashing just as hard. "Shit, she blew out her knee and didn’t shed a tear… I’ve seen her stare down some of meanest fuckers on the planet and not break a sweat… tonight though? She’s scared and I don’t know what the fuck to do... she’s not supposed to be like this… not now. Not when she’s so damn close to reaching the top. She deserves to be happy...she deserves a damn break. Not this though, anything but fucking this." Even the doctor, an older Asian fellow whose years of experience by the composure he maintained seemed taken aback. Here was a six foot five mass of muscles melting down before his very eyes. He looked down at his charts, swallowed, then concurred. "Let her sleep then. We’ll talk in the morning." Chris nodded. It might have been the smartest decision he made in his life. "Wait." He muffled Tessa’s ears. "Is my daughter okay?" "We’ll know in the morning. Wait til then." Wait. The one word Chris could not fucking stand. ---- "Tessa." "What?" "We need to talk. Now. It’s important." Chris hardly seemed to be keeping it together himself. There was no way she was going to escape it this time. "Please, can we wait?" Her lips quivered before she could turn head away this time. "I promise we’ll talk. I promise!" "I can’t wait." His gentle embrace overtook her. "I need my wife back now." ---- Optimists like to believe that each new day marks a new beginning. The each day begins with a blank slate, clear of any error. The moment Tessa Windsor woke up she demanded one. She cried. She begged. She cursed. Most of all she prayed. "Ms. Windsor?" Markus Stone and Jay Terror could have simultaneously bashed her in the head a couple dozen more times than he already had and it would have hurt less than the doctor’s hushed prompt. She ignored it the best she could, until her husband firm grasp yanked her along by the waist. "Come on." "I hate you." Truthfully she hated herself. Chris halted, stopping short of the room Aislinn occupied. The woman he knew and loved was slowly slipping from his grasp yet again. He hated how helpless he felt. He inhaled deeply before looking back her way. "You okay?" "Should I be?" He wondered the same though he would never admit it. "Guess we’ll find out, yeah?" "Guess so." ---- Chris Storm hated crying. He especially hated when he felt responsible for it. "Look, we’ll fix this, okay? Just focus on Rampage and then we’ll have one big meltdown together afterwards. You’re not in this alone." As much as he wanted her to believe it would be okay, he had no clue what he could actually do to keep his wife’s sanity in tack. Rampage was his only wager. It failed hard. "My fuck up? You’ll fix my fuck up, Chris?" Even worse than the tears was watching his wife tear herself apart. She was her own worst enemy anytime anything went wrong. "Our fuckup Tessa, our fuckup." She might as well have not heard him. "Sorry. But this is MY fuckup. And nothing can ever change that." "You’re not trying." "Forget trying. You see where trying has got me, Chris? It’s got me freaking here. HERE!" "So you’re content with staying here, with nothing, coasting by in your life?" She refused to answer. ---- "Ms. Windsor." Tessa’s head slowly prompted up. "We found pills in her system. Pain pills." Then it fell back down again. From that point Tessa tuned everything else out. Anything that followed was sure to kill her. Chris dove in for the save. "Will she be alright?" He paused. Not the kind of response Chris was looking for. "She ingested some pretty potent medication." By then Chris was losing his own nerves. "Damnit, is she okay? Just answer me!" It helped the situation none that even the doctor seemed uncomfortable to answer that. He stepped forth, seemingly practicing his own deep breathing exercises. "She’s fine." A sigh of relief escaped, quickly replaced by Chris’ urge to strangle the dude. If there was one thing not to mess with, it was Chris’ emotions when it came to his family. He might not have been the perfect father, but he had enough sense to know where his priorities should lay. Chris’ temper was cut short by the doctor cutting back in. "But I’m really concerned about how she managed to get a hold of such strong pills." Chris walked across eggshells to his daughter’s bedside. He hated strangers getting involved in his family life, but the last thing he needed was Tessa having another meltdown. "Look. She’s okay. That’s all that matters, right?" "She’ll be fine. But Mr. Storm, I need to know where she managed to get a hold of such pills." Chris might have been able to keep himself from tears, but anger? It was not happening. Anger was his strong suit. He finally burst. "Like I know!" You would have thought the doctor was accusing him of murder. "Do you really think I fucking fed them to her?" "I just need to know where she got them from." The doctor seemingly prayed for the unlikely peaceful interlude where the mass of muscles standing nearby regained any bit of common sense. He wondered how long it would take for security to get to the room if Chris pounced, given his next question. "You’re a wrestler right? Are you taking any kind of pain meds?" Surprisingly Chris only barked back. He was far more concerned with his daughter. "No. I’m not. You fucking happy?" No answer came. Instead the doctor adjusted his lab coat and proposed and proposed a far more demeaning question. "Can I ask how your daughter might have gained access to these prescription pain pills then? Considering your daughter could have died, I think you would want to figure that out too." Tessa tuned back in at just the wrong time, her realization sinking in far too fast. "Oh my god." She gasped, her eyes forming a new layer of tears. Suddenly she knew exactly what had happened. "No! No, no, no!" Both set of eyes turned Tessa’s way just in time to watch her collapse down into the nearest chair in despair. "Those were mine." Her face fell straight into her palms. ---- Present You can’t ignore me forever Tess." Chris slipped his fingers under her chin, trying to force her face upwards. She only buried it further into his his chest. "I’m not trying to." "Then let it go." He sighed, wrapping his arm firmly around her shoulders. "Talk to me. Tell me what’s wrong. You know I believe you, right?" "About what?" "About not having a problem." He should have just stabbed her in the gut with that one. ‘A problem’. He wanted her to open up yet he refused to put even his own thoughts to word. He refused to acknowledge the thought that she had some kind of addiction, the thought that she was as flawed as the man she tried to save in Markus Stone, because that was not his wife. His wife defied the odds. His wife refused to back down. His wife would never resort to pills to get through the day. He knew that. She knew that. It was getting the world to believe that which presented the problem. "Look. Just forget about it, please?" As strong as she tried to be, hiding the tears was absolutely impossible to do when it felt like everything she had was slipping completely out of her grasp. "I’ll be fine. I threw them away." "Your pills?" Chris seemed none too relieved by any of that. His eyes whizzed around the room the room quickly for any sign of a misconception. He failed to find a single one. "Yes." She reinforced the reason why by looking up at him with pleading eyes." I’m not an addict. You’ve got to believe me." He exhaled. It was hard enough to believe that they were even having this conversation in the first place, let alone that his wife had any sort of problem. Hell if anything she had a problem of not taking them enough. Unfortunately he was not the best at keeping his composure. His arms flailed up in complete disbelief. Her lack of faith was slowly smothering his soul. "For the love of god Tessa, I believe you!" "Then let me be! Please? I’m fine!" "You’ve got the biggest match of your life coming up. Don’t tell me you’re ‘just fine’. I know you Tessa. You’re freaking out. And you’re hurt. That’s not a good combination. Not when you’re been working so damn hard for this moment." She bit back with sarcasm. "Fine. I’m a mess. How bout you? Are you psychologically intact knowing that you’re going to have to face your crippled wife in a match? And your daughter? How are you recouping from that? Is that going to affect your performance?" If she wanted him to self destruct there she was doing a damn good job at lighting the fuse. "Tess. Please." "Just let me go Chris? Please. I’ll be fine! I swear. I’ve beaten Terror how many times?" "You’re in pain. You almost lost your daughter. You know we’re probably going to have to square off. Don’t tell me you’re fine." "I’m going to win regardless of all that. That better?" "Tess. Please. Just be honest with me." He tried desperately to keep his eyes locked with hers. "I need you to be safe. I can’t stand worrying about you. Do you think it’s fair to do this to me before all this garbage we’ve got to go through at Rampage?" She gazed towards the ground, pulling away from his grasp. "We’ll talk after Rampage. Please? I can’t handle your concern right now. I’ve got too much other crap to worry about." It seemed far from promising but he bit. At least then he could save her if she lost at Rampage. And if she won? Well then maybe it would fix itself. They would have the big celebration he had promised her for years. It only seemed right that she finally get her chance to shine after all the times she had been there to celebrate with him and Markus. Maybe then he could finally stop worrying. Maybe then they could finally have the life she deserved. "Deal." He stopped himself short. "But promise me this?" "Yeah?" "You’re going to win." She tried her best to smile. "I need to win if I ever want to be something. So yeah, I’ll win." "Good." A knock on the door interrupted both their thoughts. They both knew exactly who it was. "They never stop with the questions, do they?" A pat on the back assured Tessa she could do this. Chris knew better than anyone that she needed to conquer her demons before Rampage. Facing the press was about the only way she could. "Good luck with them." "I can handle them." She smiled back the best she could. "Good luck with Rampage." "You too. I love you. Please be careful though?" "Course. I can take them. And you. And Terror and Fox." "Go get ‘em then champ." He forced his own smile as she walked out the door. He wanted to believe in her. He really did. But he knew better than anyone that the media was capable of eating anyone alive. And that is exactly what he feared would happen.
