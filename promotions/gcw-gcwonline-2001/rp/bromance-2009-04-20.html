Bromance

Date: 4-20-09 Flying first class was pretty nice, I decided, as a rather sexy stewardess walked by with a champagne bottle, checking with us to see if we wanted refills. We were on our way back from Hawaii, where I'd won the American Dream and Fisher had retained his Television title. Both of us had decided we needed to celebrate a bit, so rather than head straight home, it was time for a repeat trip to Vegas. Fisher was in the seat next to me with one of those complimentary pillows behind his head and a sleeping mask that read "American Airlines" on it. Who knew if he was actually sleeping or not ? I think he just liked using all the stuff. I nudged his elbow and he stirred a bit. "Dude, what the fuck do you want?" Oops. Apparently he was sleeping. "Champagne?" I asked, holding my own glass out to the stewardess ? her nametag said Jenny Lee - for her to refill yet again. "Does it look like I want champagne?" Fisher said snootily. Obviously rhetorical, so I left him alone, turning my attention to Jenny Lee. Blond, blue eyes, rockin' tight body that didn't even look like it was fake baked and a smile that could knock any man out cold. She was the kind of girl that if you saw her walking down the street, or anywhere for that matter, you'd triple take. "Thank you, Jenny." I said, flashing her my best smile. "You're welcome, sir." "Please, call me Tyson." She giggled, which was a good start to a flirtatious flight. "Okay...Tyson." Fisher sputtered next to me. I just rolled my eyes. "So, Jenny, how long you been a flight attendant?" I asked, shifting my weight in my chair a bit. "About three months now." "Oh, so you're pretty new to all of this?" "Very much so." She giggled. Fisher took off his mask and sat up, visibly irritated. "All right, really? Jenny ? look, Tyson here wants to fuck you. Tyson ? Jenny here wants to fuck you. I don’t know why, but she does. So why don't you just cut the small talk bullshit and get in the lavatory already and stay there for a couple orgasms so I don’t have to hear this rather pathetic attempt at airplane courtship and get some shut-eye, kay? "TIME OUT!" "That's right, I just pulled a Saved by the Bell. I froze everything, in order to give some sort of cheesy monologue based on the scenario." Fisher stares up at his companion in disbelief. "Uh, dude?" "It's alright Brian, I'll unfreeze you in a minute." Fisher closes his eyes and shakes his head. "Wow." "Normally I'd be pretty pissed off at Fisher, but we actually had this worked out before hand. We knew either of us could get Jenny in bed with us, but I called dibs first." A very disgusted look crosses Jenny's face. "Excuse me?" "Brian wasn't even sleeping, he was faking it so it'd look like he was cranky and just stating the obvious, that way Jenny would just admit she wanted to fuck me, and we'd go back to a private area and I'd nail her like there was no tomorrow." Jenny's eyes open wide and she stomps off towards the cockpit. "Anyways, let's see how this turns out for me." "TIME IN!" "You're a fucking idiot,” Fisher says. I look down at Fisher, then up towards a man with a badge on his lapel. It reads "Air Marshal." "Fuck." *.~.* We landed in Vegas a few hours later and I was escorted off the plane to the terminal where I finally got released. Punishment? Banned and Exiled from American Airlines, all for " sexual harassment" towards Jenny. I don't really know how she heard me, considering I'd frozen everything. We left the airport and immediately headed to Caesar's Palace to check into our room. Planet Hollywood was Fisher's domain, but this was my party. I knew a few people so we got a pretty sweet room, complete with an open bar for the few days we were there. I walked into the room first, followed by Fisher, who tossed his bags down in the corner of the room. It was nine at night ? still to early to go clubbin?, so we decided to start with a few drinks in the room. "Drink?" I asked Fisher. "Rum and Coke - hold the Coke." I grabbed two glasses, filled one with rum on some ice and handed it to him. I turned back to the bar and started mixing myself a vodka tonic. "You're an idiot, man,” Fisher said. “Why the fuck would you think you could freeze everything just by putting your hands into a tee and saying time out?" "Uh, it worked for Zack Morris?" "Yeah, but see, Zack Morris was cool.” "Whatever, man. How was I supposed to know it wouldn't work?" "Uh, because we’re not on TV?" “Yeah, we are.” “Well, we are, yeah, but not right this moment. I mean, if we were on TV, I could freeze everything because I’m the champion of television. But you can’t just go callin” timeouts on an airplane like you’re Bobby Knight.” I rolled my eyes. "Lucky for you, Caldera was able to save your ass,” Fisher added. "Fuck off man. Like you've never done anything stupid?" Fisher snorted. “I was high for like six years, dude. What do you think?” "Yea...well’I was high..." "No, you weren't." I turned back holding my drink and took a sip from the two small straws I'd put into it. Fisher looked at me in disbelief. "Dude, seriously?" I pursed my lips. "What?" "Fuckin' straws?" "Whatever man, I'll drink my drink how I do, and you drink yours how you do." "I'm not clubbin? with you if you drink like that. People will think we're gay." I shrugged. "That could help us get bitches, right?" Fisher threw an ice cube at me and I laughed at him. "You silly ass!" He charged me and tackled me onto the bed, playfully slapping at my face. I tickled him and flipped him underneath me, both of us laughing the entire time. Brian was such a great friend to me, I really was lucky to have him in my life. We always seemed to have such a great time togeth- WHACK! Fisher slapped me in the head. "Dude. What. The. Fuck?" "What?" "You just drifted off into la-la land with some sort of gay smirk on your face." "Oh...uh, yeah. Right. My bad." "Seriously though. Don't ever do that again. That was creepier than a Shaman promo." I nodded soberly. "Anyways. Bitches. Tonight." He emphasized the last word. "Yeah, I know a few places. You want it really easy? Or you wanna head out to a club and see what we can score?" He just looked at me. "Right. Easy." I pulled out my phone and started flipping through contacts to find us some hot spots to go. "So, I don't wanna be a buzzkill - nice work at Shockwave and all - but you know you have a match coming up this week, right?" I turned and looked at him. "Say what?" "Didn't you look at the site?" "Fuck no, I assumed Steve would just give me the week off seeing as I won at Shockwave." "Heh, nope. Rampage tournament. You face some newb." "Who?" Fisher shrugged. "Fuck if I know. Only guys I know around there are that dude with the drinkin? problem and the dude with the hot girlfriend who’s now dead. So, really, just the dude with the drinkin? problem." I took the straws out of my drink and killed it in two swallows. "Fuck." I went to my bag and pulled out my laptop, flipped it on and waited for it to load the desktop. "Man, you win one match, and suddenly you fight every week." "Dude, it's two weeks in a row you pussy." I clicked on the FireFox logo, and went to the website to check the card. "Friar?" Both Fisher and I looked at each other. "Who the hell is that?" I opened up his bio page. "One half of Holy Hell...great. He's like Taylor junior. All hellbent on religion and shit." "You know what you should do?" Fisher asked. "What's that?" "Forget about him ? you'll trash him. Let's go anally violate some 18-year-olds bored on family vacay." "Fuck yes." “If we’re lucky, maybe they’ll be 17.” *.~.* Fish and I walked into Rain, the music thumping, lights flashing and fog blowing all over the club. We were escorted to our table, where there was already a chilled bottle of champagne and a few glasses waiting for us. Also waiting: four women, two blondes and two brunettes, all in slinky outfits, practically overflowing out of their tops. I grinned at Fisher. "You said you wanted easy, right?" Fisher nodded. "Well, there they are. Ready and waiting. Partially drunk as well. I had them liquored up before we got here." "You're too good to me, man." "Eh, you'd do the same." Pause. "Why is it that we always seem to get into these horribly bromantic situations lately?" Fisher nods. "Seriously. We need to stop that." "Hug on it?" Fisher just eyes me. "Oh, right." He shakes his head and turns his attention to the girls. “So, please tell me you’re 17,” he said. I sighed.
