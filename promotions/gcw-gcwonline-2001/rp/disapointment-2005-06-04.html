Disapointment

4 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/04/05 [ The scene opens as we see Bryan Marshall “s name on a locker room door. The door slowly opens and Bryan Marshall can be seen sitting in a chair facing the wall. Marshall. Marshall has his hands on his head. Scratching, wondering what went wrong. Reviewing the night in his mind Marshall slowly begins to bang on the wall. Marshall hits the wall a little harder, and a little bit harder until he finally hits the wall as hard as he can. He gives the wall a head butt and lets out a scream of anger and pain. Marshall turns to the camera as blood drips from the middle of his head.] Bryan Marshall: WHY!” WHY!” I must be a bigger fool then I once thought. I should have never made that with the devil himself. I always stick by my guns and that is to trust no one but yourself because everyone else will let you down. Ryan Stephens, when you went back on your word, that was solely the biggest mistake of your professional wrestling career! Right now you could be the Television Champion! But no, you don’t play Bryan Marshall for a fool and get away with it! You have hell to pay Ryan. HELL TO PAY! DO YOU HEAR ME? [ Marshall gets up from the chair and flips it over as he puts his hands on his hips and walks around in a circle. The camera zooms in as his hair is covering his face. Marshall’s head hangs low and his hair is stained from blood. Marshall begins to talk again in a raspy tone.] Bryan Marshall: Stephens, I came to the GCW and had a golden opportunity that you stole from me. I don’t take kindly to people stealing from me. I believe in the rule an eye for an eye. Which is why I had to do whatever it took to make sure you did not win the television title. Ryan, you should be thanking me, ha ha, thanks to me you got closer to the TV title then you could have ever imagined. You won’t be that close again. You can try to sting me with your words, you call me a walking contradiction. I have eaten my words before only because I was young and foolish. I felt that I was smarter. But you proved me wrong. I still am foolish and not quite as young. I should have never allowed you to talk me into that arrangement. I underestimated Borealis. [ Marshall flips his hair back and wipes the blood from his head.] Bryan Marshall: My words are real Stephens, there is no act. When the bell rings I lose control. I was the first man in that battle royal and it if wasn’t for you I would have been the last! But its okay, it’s okay. My day to shine in the GCW will come soon. Soon people will realize just why I am here. Which is why I am going to make an example out of you Stephens. Next Thursday at Worldwide, you and I will go one on one to this problem we have come upon. [ Marshall spits on the ground and then smears it with his foot.] Bryan Marshall: I still have that sour taste in my mouth that Stephens left. He thinks he is something. With his pretty Armani suit, good looks, proper speech and beautiful wi’I mean ex wife. You know it’s a shame, Ryan seems to have everything he wants accept for his wife. Money can’t buy you happiness. That’s the thing about me, I don’t care for love. I have been stabbed in the back one two many times. Women as snakes, just like you are Stephens. You can not be trusted. Now everyone in the GCW knows this. It came at my own expense but now your on your own. You can never look for help again. No one will be willing to lend you a hand. You want to talk about being contradicting. What about yourself? Why couldn’t you have just went into that rumble with no plan and won it. You needed my help. Do me that favor and admit that to me. That you needed my help. Without me you would not have made it as far as you did. [ There is a knock at the door. Marshall yells out that he wants to be left alone but the knocking continues. Marshall opens the door and standing there is Kevin Jacobs. Marshall knows this man is well respected so he tells him to come in.] Bryan Marshall: Whatever you want to say to me just save it. I know your going to come in here and ask me questions like what went wrong and what do I plan to do to defeat Stephens at Worldwide. Well I don’t wish to be talking to anyone but myself right now! Kevin Jacobs: There will be plenty of time for that after I get a few words with you. Your first match in the GCW was not quite successful but you did earn the respect of fans. Bryan Marshall: Just what is the respect of fans going to get me? Is it going to help me win on Thursday against Stephens? Is it going to get me the TV title? No, I think not. The respect of the fans means shit to me. There was once a time when I cared for the fans and I wanted nothing but to be lived and respected by them. Fans will jump on different bandwagons in a matter of seconds. I only wrestle for two men. Me and the voices inside my head that are continuously telling me what to do. Kevin Jacobs: What are those “voices” telling you now? Bryan Marshall: Don’t give me that’s shit. Saying it like they don’t exist. I know they exist. I can hear them! And right now they are telling me to get ready and prepare to make Ryan Stephens next match a walk through hell. Ryan Stephens thinks he knows what I am all about. He thinks he has me figured out. Well then I will go back to being the unpredictable son of a bitch I know that I can be. This week Worldwide Is going to open with a bang! Because I am a ticking time bomb! Kevin Jacobs: This is your first singles match with the GCW and I wish you the best of luck. Bryan Marshall: Luck. I would take luck everyday if I could. There is one thing that is for sure. It is better to be lucky then good. If you don’t believe that then maybe you’re the crazy one. [ Kevin Jacobs leaves the locker room as Marshall goes back to sitting down facing the corner.]
