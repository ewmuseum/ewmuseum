Bar Flys

1 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/01/11 Whenever you visit Nova Scotia you need to prepare yourself for a good time, especially if you’re in the Halifax area. As the season of spring gradually turns into summer the atmosphere has an enormous positive affect on everyone’s mood. People begin to take advantage of the hot weather by getting outside in the fresh air in their summer clothes. The smell of the Atlantic Ocean has such a distinct smell this time of year that you know lobster season is near by for the community. This was Daniel Cruz's favorite time of the year. He was in the Halifax area visiting the downtown boardwalk. The whole area was filled with people taking advantage of the beautiful spring day. Love birds were walking around the boardwalk with their ice cream cones in hand. Old men sat on a bench discussing the engines and designs of the ships and boats that gently rocked in the harbor. Soft music played in the background as people strummed their guitars looking for tips as people passed on by. It was a typical sunny day on the waterfront. Daniel Cruz walked by these surroundings as he scrolled through his blackberry reading his messages. As the strong sunlight shined down, Cruz slid down his Oakley sunglasses to the bridge of his nose to help him read the screen on his phone. The phone was displaying that a new text message has been received. Cruz opened the message to read… "Do me a favor? Please don't tell anyone I ever trained you. You got your ass kicked!! LOL. J/K man. Keep your chin up. EG" Daniel Cruz tried to conceal his smirk as he read the message. He was waiting to get some kind of sarcastic message from his old friend Eddie Gilbert after his match at World Wide 148 and it finally happened. "...Asshole!" Cruz quietly muttered to himself as he slid his sunglasses back up to his face and placed his Blackberry back into his holster. Daniel Cruz continued his leisurely walk around the boardwalk as the thoughts of his last match ran through his head. Needless to say he was disappointed in losing his match.... his debut match at that. Inside he knew he was better, much better. But in spite of what he knew or felt, he was still pinned in the ring. He knew he had to pick himself up and dust himself off. Cruz was letting these thoughts race through his mind as he approached his favorite spot in Halifax, "The Lower Deck". It was a pub style bar that entertained customers with local bands in the night time. Since it was mid-afternoon on a warm sunny day, Cruz decided to stop in and pass the time for a little while. Daniel Cruz walked into the entrance of the Lower Deck and headed straight to the bar. As he folded his sunglasses up and hung them on the collar of his blue polo shirt, he kept his eyes peeled on the plasma screen television hanging above the bar. Might as well have a cold beer and catch some of the hockey game, it was playoffs after all! As Cruz stood up against the bar watching the game, the bartender made his way over to Daniel Cruz. "What'll it be?" asked the bartender as he nodded his head at Cruz. "Bud Light pal..." replied Daniel Cruz as he reached into his pocket for some cash. The bartender headed towards the beer fridge to grab the ice cold Bud Light as Cruz tossed a twenty dollar bill on the bar. As Cruz continued to gaze at the television watching every move in the hockey game, a loud obnoxious voice yelled at him from down the end of the bar. "HEY!! I..I.. I know YOU" Daniel Cruz looked towards the end of the bar to see a man (who obviously had a couple of drinks already) sliding his way past the bar stools heading in his direction while holding a rum and coke in his hand. "Your that rassler guy from around here aren't yah?" said the man as he lifted himself up onto the stool right beside Daniel Cruz. "Ughh..Yeah.. Hi" said Cruz as he smiled courteously. Daniel Cruz didn’t mind interacting with any of his fans outside of the arena, but today was different for him. Losing his comeback match was still a huge crush for him. Although he knew he had to keep moving on harder and stronger, Daniel Cruz wanted this day to himself. He wanted to unwind and be a regular so he could collect his thoughts and get his mind mentally prepared for Worldwide 149. The last thing he wanted to deal with was a drunken wrestling fan. The bartender placed the bottle of beer in front of Daniel Cruz and slid his change back towards him. Cruz took the beer but waved his hand back at the bartender signaling for him to keep the change. "Thanks bud!" smiled the bartender as he slid the nice tip back into his pants pocket. Daniel Cruz focused himself back on the hockey game but the drunken patron beside him didn’t have any interest at all in the game at this point. He stared at Daniel Cruz with a big smile across his face. "I can't believe it... I use to watch you all the time on television." laughed the drunken man. "Thanks, I appreciate it.." replied Cruz softly. "The guys aren't going to believe this! I need to get them down here" said the man as he reached into his jeans pocket fiddling around for his cell phone. Cruz quickly turned toward the man and placed his hand down on the man's arm preventing him from hauling out his phone. "Please don't" said Cruz as he locked eyes with the drunk man. "I'm sorry... I don't mean to be cranky. But all I really want to do is relax here, enjoy a beer and catch up on the hockey game. I appreciate you knowing who I’ am and your friends being excited to meet me... but I'm just not in the mood today for a bar full of wrestling fans." stated Daniel Cruz. "...Ohh... ok... Well my name is Phil.." said the man as he extended his hand towards Daniel Cruz. "Nice to meet you Phil... and thanks for understanding" replied Daniel Cruz as both men shared a hand shake. "So... what are you doing now?" asked Phil as he finished up his glass of rum and coke. Taking a deep breath Daniel Cruz let out a soft sigh. He thought he was done of this idle chit chat and could watch the playoffs in peace and solitude, but obviously Phil was not going to let that happen. Daniel Cruz took a big drink of his beer and calmly responded to Phil. "I just got back into wrestling. I signed on with Global Championship Wrestling. I was off for awhile but decided to make my return to the ring." "Wow!" said Phil eagerly. "That is cool. I haven’t been in tune with rasslin for awhile now, so I never knew you were coming back. When is your big comeback match?" "I already had it..." answered Daniel Cruz "....And?? How did it go?!" asked Phil. Daniel Cruz sighed to himself again. He clearly wanted to move on from his past match and gear towards the future but that obviously wasn’t going to happen with Phil asking 101 questions. Cruz finished off the rest of his beer and placed the empty bottle onto the bar. "I lost. Tough kid and it was a good match. But he caught me off guard and gained the victory... what can I say." stated Cruz quickly. "Ohh... that must suck then" said Phil. Daniel Cruz rapidly rolled his eyes and just nodded his head. As the hockey game had five minutes left in the third period, Cruz was now hoping this conversation would end so he could watch the game in silence. The bartender then comes along and takes both men's empty drinks off of the bar. "Another one pal?" asked the bartender. "Sure..." said Daniel Cruz as he reached back into his pants to bring out some cash. Daniel Cruz looked over at Phil who had no drink in front of him. Cruz had an apparent feeling that Phil was short on cash. Phil sat on the stool staring at The Canadian Icon with his blood shot eyes. As much as Daniel Cruz wanted the conversation to end with Phil, he didn't have the heart to refuse the man a drink. "And another one for this guy here too..." said Daniel Cruz. The bartender made an agitated face as he looked over at Phil. It was noticeable to Cruz that Phil was quite the regular in this bar and had a tendency to aggravate the staff by hanging around the bar with his drinking habit. "What are you going to have now Phil?" asked the bartender sternly. "errr... whiskey and.... NO.... Vodka and oran...NO. Ughhh...." Daniel Cruz chuckled to himself as the bartender became more agitated with Phil. "Come on Phil... pick something..." said the bartender. "....double black rum and coke..." Daniel Cruz just nodded to the bartender approving the drink as Phil flashed another big smile across his face. The bartender began pouring the drink as Phil leaned over towards Cruz with his body staggering from the alcohol. Phil took his trembling hand and patted Daniel Cruz on his back. "Thhhanks ole buddy! I appreciate that." said Phil. "No problem, just take it easy" responded Daniel Cruz. The bartender then returned with the two drinks for both men. Daniel Cruz passed the bartender another twenty dollar bill as Phil raised his glass in the air. "Cheers to you buddy. Good luck on your next match too. You might have taken an ass whopping in your last match. But like my mother told my father, you can't win them all!" Daniel Cruz chuckled and shook his head as both men tapped their drinks together in cheers. Phil began to start rambling on with a story but Cruz completely turned his ears off to the drunken man. Cruz sipped on his beer as he watched the television hanging from above. This time his mind was no longer on Phil nor the hockey game. His mind was thinking about his next match with Brad Cruz. Daniel Cruz found it amusing how people within GCW tried to figure out if there was some kind of personal relationship between Brad and himself. But in actual fact it was just purely coincidence that both men shared the same last name. Daniel Cruz didn’t really know much of Brad Cruz. Daniel had watched many of Brad's matches in the past and did admire the kid's technique. Besides being a solid wrestler with a random hot temper, there really wasn’t much more that Daniel Cruz knew about his upcoming opponent. In Cruz's own mind, he was just dealing with another Californian beach boy who just happened to share the same last name as him. However Daniel Cruz knew he could not take this match carelessly. He did the same thing in his last match with Dynasty and we all now know how that turned out for him. The Canadian Icon wanted to pick apart every weakness that Brad presented. Worldwide 149 was soon approaching and Daniel Cruz did not want to go down for a second time... especially in Flair Country. "So that’s when I said, that isn't no catfish! Its winter time... were catching smelt..." Daniel Cruz snapped out of his train of thought right at the end of Phil's drunken ramble. Daniel Cruz gave Phil a peculiar look and then glanced over at the bartender. The bartender simply rolled his eyes and shrugged his shoulders; apparently he wasn’t listening to Phil’s story either. At this point Daniel Cruz realized he was in this bar for long enough. It was time to get out, pack up and head back to the United States for Worldwide 149. Cruz grabbed his bottle and finished off the remainder of his beer. "And to this day buddy... they won't let me back in that shopping mall. How the heck am I suppose to buy a Canoe now?" rambled Phil "I don't know man. But look I got to go. Good luck with….ugh… all that stuff." said Daniel Cruz as he gave Phil a quick pat on the back. Daniel Cruz turned around and started walk out of the bar. "Thanks bud" said Cruz as he nodded towards the bartender. "No problem, thank you very much" responded the bartender. As Daniel Cruz exited the bar, Phil sat on the bar stool swaying back and forth. He then shouted to Cruz as he held his drink up in the air. "See you later pal. Thanks for the...aaa... drink! Good luck in your match too.... bust some heads!" Phil then took a big sip out of his drink as he sat alone staring at the bartender. As the bartender began to wipe the top bar with a damp cloth; Phil looked at him with a drunken expression on his face. "I can't believe it…" mumbles Phil. "I just met one of The Fabulous Rougeau Brothers".
