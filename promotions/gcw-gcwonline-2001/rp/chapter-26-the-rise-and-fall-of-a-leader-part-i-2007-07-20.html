Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader- (Part I)

20 July 2007

Date: July 20, 2007 __Start Often, a person's life has been related to a book. Naturally, the prologue symbolizes the birth of the main character. Upon the end of that, chapter one emerges and begins to unravel the scene and supporting characters. Typically, chapter two expresses a common problem and from there the action picks up steam until the epilogue. That's where you die. For Jay Terror, he has made it through the prologue, figured out who the supporting cast was, and lived through torment and despair. In any great novel, it would seem that it was time for Jay to overcome the obstacles at hand and take a stand for himself. Unfortunately though, the author can't simply write that he prevails. There must be build up. And so from an all time low to a great high, Jay Terror is ready to begin the next chapter of his life. Chapter 26: The Rise and Fall of a Leader Part I It was normal to feel like shit, right? In fact, it's almost inhuman to not feel the least bit like a loser after feeling so high on oneself. Right? It really didn't matter, he felt the emotions sink in deeper than ever before and once again things seemed to fall back into that same dreadful schedule. Wake up, train, eat, train, eat, train, ponder dying, and then train again. It was just how Daniel Franco liked to work. 'You know why Tsunami Oni beat you, right?' His voice was crisp from the yelling he had done thus far in the day and floated to Jay's ears with a slight strut. 'I'm serious you know; why did you lose to Oni?' His broad frame found itself hung up on the top rope of the ring. 'It doesn't matter.' 'It doesn't?' This was where he had fun. That signature smile crept onto his lips, with a heavy emphasis on crept, sending a reflection of light into Jay's eyes. 'Tell me I'm wrong, which would be wrong in itself, but weren't you the one who said you could beat him on your own? Weren't you the one who claimed that you were stronger without me?' Jay squinted his right eye a tad and shook his head. 'Let's get back to training fucktard.' 'Ah right, training. With me...right?' His smile became more and more devious with the passing seconds. 'Not alone or anything?' He had driven his point far enough for Jay to relish in. 'Yeah.' 'It's good to know I'm wanted.' It was clear in his eyes that he was proud of who he was. Everything that he had ever done to deceive Jay or anyone else all came together in full and accumulated in one hearty stare. Daniel Franco loved Daniel Franco. 'Come on, let's work on what Oni showed us you can't handle.' He wasn't listening. __________ What is life? What is death? What is anything? It's all a fuckin' lie anyways. You're born and told how cute you are and how you can do anything. When it comes time to do that anything though what happens? You screw up. It's a simple yet complex fact of life and no matter who you are you deal with it. It doesn't matter how rich you are or how powerful you are, everyone tries to do the one thing they taste and smell in the dreams and end up picking their depressed frame off the floor. It's life. So you may ask why bother? Why would you want to go through torture and depression when there’s a bullet sitting in the pistol within your desk drawer? I mean, suicide is the answer to anything you can't handle, right? If it's too tough, load the barrel and hope you can't be revived. __________ The smoke was mesmerizing. It climbed in its elegance and found its way throughout the room filling it with the sweet scent of tar and cancer. To Jay, there was nothing better. The lights of the small apartment had a nasty twitch to them and they flickered on and off in an offbeat way. He tried to count the times they flashed at first and how long it took between each blink but that went horribly wrong. It started well enough. One blink, three seconds, another blink, five seconds, another blink, three seconds, a double blink, chaos. Yes, he was that bored. 'Soooo.....' He hated when people said that. She was absolutely awful at making conversation and her blatant attempts weren’t going to do anything if she kept it up. 'What do you want to do?' For a fourth time she asked him the same question. He wanted to shout obscenities and slap her across the face but that wasn't exactly in the playbook so to speak. 'How about we get some paper bags and go and rob a store.' 'No...again.' It was worth a shot he figured. Every drag he took from the cigarette seemed to be his best effort to stay alive in her presence. Sure, she had a great body and had a reputation for sucking a guy’s dick on the first date but he couldn’t see anything past that. Talking to her was like talking into a hollow log where your voice peeked out the other end and echoed back to your ears. It was dreadful. 'Then we're out of options. Unless you want to go on that balcony of yours and piss on your neighbors I'm all dried up on ideas.' And then she shot that look she had proven to be her favorite of the evening. It was hard to explain but if someone had to they would probably say something about the way her eyebrows tucked in and turned into a long uni-brow. They'd most likely make mention to the way her nostrils bent inwards and the way she grinned...or grimaced. 'Well this is quite a first date, huh?' 'I'll remember it forever.' He sucked off the remnants of the cigarette and tossed it under his shoe on the carpet. 'What are you doing?' 'Burning shit.' Stating the obvious, 'This is my apartment.' He looked left and he looked right and between the two glances he chuckled. 'I'm not sure I'd own up to that one darling.' 'Fuck you.' 'That'd be fun.' He grinned. It was almost too obvious that she pondered the idea of them doing the horizontal boogie. It was something with her mouth. Typically people reply right away after an answer like Jay offered. Jillian, the girl, took a minute though and opened her mouth in a very false gasp. She could certainly fit him inside. 'You're a pig.' 'Does that mean you'll do it?' She shook her head and walked out of the living room and past the kitchen. Her bedroom was all the apartment had left and what do you know, she walked inside it. It took a second but before she vanished completely she poked her head out from behind the drywall, 'You coming or not?' Sweet victory. __________ But what happens when victory comes. A person can’t fail forever can they? Sure it may not be what they really wanted in life but the main part of humanity is settling for the next best thing. It's not exactly losing, although it's not winning either, and normally makes people feel rather good about themselves. Who doesn't like triumph? And so you take what you're handed and run with it. Little things turn to bigger things and before you know it, that pistol is gathering more and more dust. Again; it's life. Soon everything begins to fall into place, things become good and a person is able to walk the streets smiling. Hell, some people skip. Whatever they do to celebrate though, it's made sure that everyone around them knows that they've accomplished something worth praise. __________ It was probably the worst hour and nine minutes of his life. He'd had better and quite frankly, he wasn't a fan of love. Especially not on a first date. Love was a thing that he had never heard enough of as a child. People like his father and mother seemed to care less and less for him as he aged and found little room to tell him anything close to love. He was stupid. He was never going to make it. He was going to die before he hit twenty one. Jay had heard it all before. Except for love, of course....until five minutes before they finished. 'So what do you want to do?' If she asked one more time he decided that he would snap her neck. He'd grab her by the head and yank it backwards until he heard it break free from her spine. Jay took a long drag on his fifth cigarette of the evening and nodded. 'We could always go find some masks and rob a bank.' He winked. 'If you're not having fun just leave.' 'I'm smoking a cigarette and sitting on your living room floor in my boxers, how could I not be having fun?' 'Oh shut up.' He laughed. Jay figured she didn't like it when he laughed like that but who was she anyways? If it wasn't for Devin from the bar, he wouldn't have known Jillian was a real person. He could have passed her on the street and asked her for a cigarette perhaps but that didn't require a full introduction. 'You want to go for a walk?' For the first time after something he'd said she smiled. There was no crazy crack head look or no grunting, she genuinely smiled. 'I'd love to.' Jay found his pants from her bedroom floor and threw the condom on her bed sheets along with the wrapper. He found Jillian checking her hair in the hall mirror and walked past her down the stairs. At the bottom he could hear her feet smashing the wood steps behind him and he opened the front door to the outdoors. 'It's nice out.' She said as she caught up to him. It was true. There was a great breeze passing by and it made sure it gave Jay a quick hug before passing on across the street. 'Yeah.' His cigarette came to an end again and he spit it out onto the concrete. 'It's not too bad.' She nodded and that awkward silence returned. She really was horrible at making conversation. 'So you're a professional wrestler?' 'Yeah.' He was surprised to hear it. By now Jay had figured she didn't know anything about who he was or what he did, only how big his penis was. 'I do a little wrestling.' 'Are you any good?' And that's where she lost the points. 'I like to think so.' Awkward silence. The two made their way down Ridge Street and at one point Jillian reached for his hand. Jay pretended he had an itch on his nose and Jillian slapped his ass instead. She was real classy. For another five minutes they walked, quietly, passing very few people. They saw one guy itching his face like he had a leech stuck to it and another with a hook for a hand but other than that, no one. 'Do you like me?' Her questions continued. Jay looked her way and arced his right eyebrow, 'You're too quiet.' 'Me? I haven't heard you talk unless I asked you something' Jay shook his head, 'Would you rather me ignore you?' And now she shook her head, 'You're impossible.' Awkward silence. They walked for another two minutes and this time saw three guys. They were all together and none of the men had a shirt on. Their bodies were painted in brilliant tattooed patterns and their heads held a different hairstyle for each. As they approached Jay and Jillian, something weird happened. They all pulled bandanas up from off their necks and around their mouths...just like Jay. Before he could say something blood slipped out of his nostril and coated the object he had been punched with. In a second, Jay's vision blurred and he felt as if he was going to fall over. Jillian's face was the last thing he remembered as it began to bleed as well. Then there was darkness. __________ And right when plan B seems like it has made you truly happy, someone comes into your life and decides you're not allowed to be happy. Someone sees you skipping and decides they would rather saw your legs off and watch you roll around in a wheel chair for the rest of your life. And that’s when you're back to nothing. You find the pistol in the office drawer and your fingers grip the handle taking the dust with them. Once again, suicide calls your name and the reaper watches you point the pistol at your head. He places his bony fingers on your shoulder and plays the role of your conscience letting you know things will be ok when they're over. So you do it. __________ As he woke up he felt as if he was back in Jillian's apartment. The light over his head blinked in no pattern whatsoever and laughed at Jay every time he tried to count the seconds before it turned off again. When trying to move he figured out that he couldn't possibly be in the apartment due to the simple fact that Jillian didn't have any handcuffs. Of course he checked. 'Took you long enough.' The voice was one belonging to someone he didn't know. It was deep and resonated well on the walls but Jay could tell it was an act. There was something about it screaming fake. 'If you hadn't hit me so hard I'm sure I could have revived quicker for you.' Footsteps. 'You sure haven't lost that comedic edge Jay.' The faceless voice knew his name. Considering Jay didn't carry a wallet there was no way he could have looked it up while he was unconscious. He was someone Jay knew. 'Eh, shit happens.' 'It's been a while my friend.' The voice fell out of it's tough guy disguise and turned into a more mellow one. 'A long while.' 'Probably.' The footsteps moved to the other side of Jay while still remaining out of his line of sight. 'How have you been?' This was something Jay didn't understand. He had bludgeoned him in the face with some object and now he wanted to talk to him like they were brothers or something. Weird. 'Look, if you're going to touch me inappropriately all I ask is you hit me with that thing again. I'd rather not be awake when you have your way.' 'What?' 'I'm not into that kinda stuff anymore and I think it'd just be best for everyone if you hit me again. Maybe I'll have amnesia and then we can have a good conversation about how I've been. Cross your fingers.' A new voice took over the scene and by the way it rang in the room, this person wasn't pretending to be someone else. 'Shut up Jay, just fuckin' listen.' He paused and during the reprieve Jay could hear a lighter spark up. Like Spiderman sensing someone was in danger, he poked his head up and sniffed the air for the sweet aroma of nicotine. 'We're not going to touch you.' 'Give me a cigarette and I'll let you.' 'Shut up!' A bare hand slapped the side of his head and made him quiet down. The original mystery voice came back, 'Jay, you're quite the man to track down. I mean, you'd think it'd be easy with your traveling schedule but shit dude, you're tough.' 'It's my thing I guess.' His mind raced trying to figure out who the voice belonged to and why he wasn't allowed to see them. 'Do you have any idea who we are?' 'Let's see,' he wanted to say so many things but he figured it would probably end up with him having a broken jaw. His head hurt enough from the slap he took. 'I'm going to have to go with no.' 'Good.' The footsteps stopped and Jay could feel the man's breath in his hair. It was warm. 'We've been watching you Jay. We've seen you in the ring, we've seen you in your home, and we've seen you looking the same in both places. You're miserable no matter where the hell we spot you.' Jay only nodded. 'It saddens us to see you this way. Honestly, we hate it.' He couldn't resist, 'A cigarette would really make me feel better.' The man ignored it though and continued, 'And that's why we want to help you. What would you say if we told you that we could bring back the Jamison Talley you liked. You wouldn't have to rely on the name Jay Terror to sound fierce anymore, instead you'd look like you did seven years ago.' And that's when it clicked. Everything fell into place and the mystery voice turned into the sounds of Art Jankey. 'Wait,' He started to speak again but the footsteps returned and a slim man stepped in front of him. His face, shadowed by a white and blue bandana, came into focus and the long scar across his eye told Jay all he needed to know. 'What's going on.' 'What you don't know Jay is that after you left, The Bandstand Bandits weren't finished. We became bigger, stronger, and more reckless. Everything that you taught us excelled to a new level and brought us to where we are today.' 'You mean...' 'Yes, we need our leader back. It's time that the man who created us took his place at the top.' 'That's not what I was going to say but it works.' Obvious signs of contemplation filled his eyes. Jay didn’t think about things too hard on a normal basis but something as odd and good as this didn't come around normally. 'What do you want me to do?' 'Just wait. You'll know when it's time to take your place.' He left. Jankey said nothing in relation to goodbye, he just walked away. So Jay sat. He sat alone, handcuffed to a chair, thinking about life. Where was Jillian? Would this really be his chance to make his mark on the world? Who knew. __________ And as you hear the hammer strike the firing pin, you wait for your memories to become ash and your skull to split in half. Two seconds go by and your eyes are still shut. Nothing happened. Another three seconds pass and you open your eyes. Fingers, head, thoughts, everything...it's all still there. You're as real as you were before you killed yourself. That's when it takes over. There isn’t a name for it but it's kind of like a decree from god. In some odd way, you decide that it is his doing that you have failed and that with this failure you must resume your life. That's where you find a new beginning. This time, that dream you wanted so bad is now inches from your face. __End
