The Monster that once was(is).

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 The room is illuminated by a reading lamp off in the corner. It’s black, both arms fulled extended, both bulbs lit. The shadows are hiding the dust and debris scattered throughout the room. The kingsize bed sits below an open window. The wind is whipping through it, tossing the curtains back and forth. The bed is littered with bottles of liquor and pills. The white sheets are an off white in the light. The dresser’s drawers are opened, articles of clothing hanging out them. More pill bottles are claiming residence on top of the tall dresser. Sitting in a chair entirely too small for his bulking frame is Bryan “Mayhem” O’Neil. He sits in front of a tall mirror attached to a closet door. He puts a handful of pills into his mouth and chugs them down with some Wild Turkey. He closes his eyes as the pills make their way to his stomach followed by the warmth of the whiskey. He moves his left arm in a circular fashion, wincing as he does it. Staring into the mirror he begins to consider the condition of his body, and the hell he has put it through to make him better. Mayhem use to welcome pain with open arms, but after 10 years of abuse it has started to take a toll on the veteran. With each chair shot, each body slam, every punch, every gash he feels himself begin to weaken. How much more is he going to be able to take? How much more hell is he going to put himself through? “Keep swallowing those pills, you’ll end up the same way those did that came before you.” Mayhem freezes as the voice that sent a shiver down his spine. He looks into the mirror and begins to squint. The image on the other side is himself, but not really. The image on the other side is not mimicking his every move like it’s suppose to. Instead it appears that it has taken a life of its own. The eyes blacked themselves out, and the tongue licked out between the lips was forked, and machine gun like. “Don’t be afraid, I am just you, nothing more and of course nothing less.” Mayhem quickly turns around, begins to take inventory of the room around him than turns back towards the mirror. The image did not disappear. Mayhem looks at the bottle of pills in one hand and the bottle of whiskey in the next, confused at what is going on around him. “Very good Bryan, blame something else for your sins. Always the O’Neil footing the bill elsewhere I see. I am not here because you are so drunk on pain pills and booze. I’ve been here long before that.” Bryan rubs his eyes, than runs his hands through his hair. His hair hurts, and so do his knuckles. The pain ignited a yelp from him that caused him to grab the pills and bottle again. He pours out 5 more, but stops, just holding them. “You know what instinct is Bryan?” Mayhem looks at the image if himself in the mirror, the iris’ of his eyes still pitch black and filled with a void. “All creatures on earth use some sort of instinct to survive. A skunk will spray a rival when it fears for its own life. Cats hiss when scared, opossums play dead, some animals run away. Instinct is the strongest force in the animal kingdom. And look what it has done to you.” Mayhem stares into the mirror, his hands are balled into fists crushing the pills in the process. “It has become an instinct to grab for the bottles and litter your body with them. Everytime the pain gets to you you run and hide behind a bottle of pills. What the hell has happened to you?” Mayhem opens his hand and looks at the crushed up pills in his hands. He begins to the think the exact same thing. Impervious to pain? Not so much anymore. The King of Hardcore? High doubtful. A pill freak. YES!!! “What would the world think once they found out? Bryan Mayhem drowns his pain in pills and booze? You understand that they are finally starting to accept you now? Night in and night out they are cheering you on, day in and day out they are wanting you to win. 10 years ago they hated you. What’s going to happen to your legacy when one of these mornings they knock on your hotel room door only to find you dead on your bead, a pile of shit in your pants and foam at your mouth?” Mayhem rolls the pill pieces in his palm with his fingers, still staring at the image of himself. “I can see it in you Bryan. The monster you became was not the monster you once were. With my help…..” Mayhem shoves the pills and their residue into his mouth and began to swig on the whiskey. The image in the mirror screamed at the top of its lungs. Blood started to leak from his eyes, nose, and mouth. The scream was deafening. The blood started to roll down the mirror. Mayhem stands up looks at the image of himself melting into a bloody mess. The blood begins to roll down the mirror onto the carpeting in front of it. The image that was once trying to control Mayhem is now being contained. Mayhem turns around, tosses the empty bottle of Turkey onto the bed, clinches his fist turns around and punches the mirror. Thousands of little pieces shatter all over the room. Mayhem winces and holds his fist. Blood was drawn, but the demon was casted away….for now.
