The NeMEsis Killer Pt. 1

undated

Date: Days of Futures Past “Detective, come here.” the young cop says as he kneels down before the broken and battered remains of a young woman that they cops were brought in to take a look at. “What do you have for us, officer?” the detective asked as he walked up. “Well, I’ll be damned.” “What is it, sir?” “Her name is Constance Black. She was reported missing a couple of months ago. Her ex-boyfriend thought that something was wrong when she didn’t come to pick up their child together. Detective Colt, you want to inform Mr. Jacobs that we found the woman that he was looking for.” “Sure thing, Detective Dorian.” “Officer, do you see anything unusual about this body?” “No sir.” “You’re young. Looks like her wrists were broken, same with her knees. This may be the handiwork of the NeMEsis Killer.” - - Later that day, Detective Ryan Dorian was taken into the city morgue where Medical Examiner Scabbia was looking over the body of Ms. Black. “Ryan, I think you’re right about this being the NeMEsis Killer. When I turned her over, I noticed that there was some scarring to her back. Three letters.” “NME?” “That is correct. She’s probably only been dead a day, maybe two.” “Bastard.” Detective Dorian looked down on the dark hair of the young woman that was now on this slab. “Cause of death?” “Well, it wasn’t the beating that he gave her, which is surprising. But, it fits with the last few murders that you brought in. A poison derived from the dragon lotus.” “How much of her beating was post-mortem?” “None. All of it came when she was alive. The fractures were actually starting to heal. If she wouldn’t have died, she would have had to have her wrists re-broken to set them properly.” “Any clue on the animal that did this to her?” Detective Wyatt Colt asked as he came in with a manila folder. “Because Mr. Jacobs is here to identify the body. I would like to tell him that we have an idea on who could have done this.” “None right now, Detective Colt.” the ME answered. “I can tell you that this does follow the modus operandi of the NeMEsis Killer. So you two were right on with that assessment.” “Wyatt, let’s let Ms. Scabbia do her job.” The two detectives found themselves in the Old Warehouse district of town, where Detective Dorian’s brother has taken up shop as a private investigator. They walk into the office and are surprised with the clippings that they see strewn about the office. “Detective Colt, welcome. Ryan, I’m pretty sure I know what you’re here about.” “Yeah, the NeMEsis killings. I see you’ve been following them.” “Kinda hard not to, it’s all that there’s been on the news. Three victims. Kristin Zenith, Viv Valentine and now Constance Black. All three women had the letters NME carved into their body when you found them. If I were you, I’d find out who’s leaking that information to the press, because I’m afraid that we’re going to start seeing a series of copycat crimes.” “What do you think?” “That you’ve missed something.” Ryan and Wyatt looked at Jason like there was a question formulating between the two of them. “We missed something?” “Yeah, well, at least with the first two. Both of them were employed with Garces, Caldera and Windsor Worldwide Media. I’m sure that if you check it out, Ms. Black might have worked there as well. The talking head over there is a man by the name of Shane Skwarek. You might want to talk to him about it.” “Thanks, Jason. If there’s anything that I can do for you..” “Tell that captain of yours to keep his nose out of my business.” “No promises.” - - With that information that even the police didn’t ascertain, Detectives Dorian and Colt found themselves in the Downtown office of Shane Skwarek, officially the spokesman of GCW Worldwide Media, but unofficially, he was the person that ran things in that office. “Mr. Skwarek, thanks for seeing us on short notice.” “Yes, anything to find the monster that killed Ms. Valentine and Ms. Zenith, we are more than happy to help out with. They still are missed around here.” “Well, we think that there was another victim that may have worked here, Ms. Constance Black.” The look on Mr. Skwarek’s face was that of disdain. He knew who they were talking about. “Well, that one I’m not sorry is no longer with us. She was only here a short time, but she made waves. There was more than one executive that wanted to terminate her.” “Terminate?” “End her employment with the company. You see, she was hired only because she’s a minority. Her birth parents were both Costa Rican. As soon as we hired her, she started to cause problems for everyone. When we finally terminated her employment, we thought that would be the last we would hear of her.” “So, she wasn’t what we would call a model employee.” “It wasn’t really her, so to speak. It was that Connor Jacobs, her ex-boyfriend. He would come and harass everyone that worked here. He was very possessive of her. ? “Are you saying that you think that he was the one that did this?” “If it was just her, without a doubt. But if you think that it’s tied into Kristen and Viv’s deaths as well, then I wouldn’t think so. As close as Viv and Kristin were, they never met Constance.” “Anyone that you know of that would have known all three of them?” Detective Colt asked, allowing his partner to get a break from the interview. “We here at GCW are a global company. We not only have this office here, but we also have offices in Columbus and Houston. I’m pretty sure that there are going to be a few people that have worked for this company that had dealings with all three of them. Not many, because of how short of a time Ms. Black was employed here.” “Mr. Skwarek, we would appreciate the names of everyone that would have had contact with all three of them. We would like to keep our options open on whom to talk to.” “Well, I can tell you that the first person you might want to talk to, the only person that I can assure you would have had dealings with is Alex Marino. Everyone here calls him Toad for some reason.” Both Ryan and Wyatt stand up and shake hands with Shane. “We would like to thank you for your time, Mr. Skwarek. We’ll go talk to Mr. Marino. Where would we find him?” “At this time of day, you’d probably find him in the executive gym. Trust me when I say that you would be hard pressed not to notice him.” - - The two detectives next found themselves in the executive gym. There were a few older executives down there, but everyone had gathered around a single bench as one man was pushing an immense amount of weight. “You can do it, two more.” the spotter said as he watched on. “You and your competitive ass.” retorted the man that was lifting the weight. He is able to get the bar up one time. “One more. You know you can do it.” The man lifting the weight struggles to lift the weight up that last time, but is able to lock his elbows and place the bar back on the stand. “I told you that you could do it.” “Fuck off. My shoulder is killing me.” The two detectives walk up to the man sitting there, and notice the sweat that was now soaking his shirt. “Alex Marino. I’m Detective Colt, and this is Detective Dorian. We have a couple questions to ask you.” “Pleasure to meet you.” the spotter responds. The two detectives look up at the over seven footer. “I’m Alex Marino. This is Christopher Bagwell.” “Pleasure to meet you two.” Christopher says as he strains to hold out his hand. “How can I help you gentlemen?” Alex asks as the Detectives shake hands with both men. “We wanted to talk to you about a couple of murders that we were investigating. Mr. Skwarek told me that you had contact with all three victims.” “As head of security at this building, I come in contact with everyone from one time or another. If you want to talk, we can head to my office. I’m sure that the two of you would be more comfortable there.” - - The detectives followed Alex up to his office. “What did you want to ask me about?” Alex asked as they got to his office door. “You have an interesting look, there, Mr. Marino. I’m guessing as least part Mexican?” Wyatt postulated. “Close. My father is Italian, my mother was Costa Rican.” “Just like Ms. Black?” “You could say that.” “Well, I don’t think that you were an Affirmative Action hire, you seem to be pretty high on the totem for that.” “This company doesn’t hire based on Affirmative Action.” “Mr. Skwarek told us that she was.” “I highly doubt that. She didn’t even know she was Costa Rican until a month after she was hired in. All she knew was that she was adopted and raised in Toronto.” “How do you know this?” “A year ago, Constance came to me and asked if I could help her find her birth parents. Her adopted parents had just passed and she wanted to know that she wasn’t alone in the world. Being the head of security for a company like this, you have access to some information that even cops have a hard time tracking.” “Why was she terminated then?” “If I tell you, I can get into trouble. Lots of trouble.” “We promise that we won’t tell your boss.” “OK. About four months ago, Constance came across a document that linked Kristin Zenith and Shane Skwarek with a business deal that went sour with a man by the name of Jared Sykes. ? “So” Business deals go south all the time. What makes this one different?” “Jared Sykes works for one of GCW’s rivals.” “Why haven’t you taken that up with GCW management?” “Because, the more I know, the more power I have here, if you catch my drift. “We do, thank you for your time, Mr. Marino.” To Be Continued?
