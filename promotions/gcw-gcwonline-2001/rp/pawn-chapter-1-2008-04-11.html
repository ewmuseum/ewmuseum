Pawn :: Chapter 1

11 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11, 2008 Originally Written: November 13, 2007 The day was Friday, November Sixteenth, Two Thousand Seven, a day in which years of one man's time, money, research, planning, and deception started to bring those he's influenced together into one sick minded experiment. The three individuals being studied in this were unaware of anything, despite their whole lives being nothing more than "scripted" and torn apart in the name of research. There are, however, those involved that knew exactly what was going on, those closest to the three being "monitored", but a lump sum of money and a promise to not open their mouths kept this whole ordeal going. These three families, caught in the crossfire of one man's obsession, will be ironically brought together through a course of unforeseen events. But, will those who know of the situation tell those who don't, or will those who are left in the dark find out about the reason to their lives, and the unfortunate situations they've been forced into? For the sake of being able to move on with their lives, one can only hope that the evil master mind behind everything that has gone awry with these individuals, these pawns, will be stopped. One can only hope for the expected happy ending, but, it is unknown at this point whether those involved will be able to write that happily ever after ending for you or not. ******* The events that began another bad chain reaction in this young man's life started at 4:02 in the afternoon on November Sixteenth, Two Thousand Seven. The phone rang throughout his Lansing, Michigan home, and his living-with-him girlfriend, Kyra, was the one to answer. "Hello?" She asked with the phone receiver held to her right ear. Coming through the large windows in the living room, where the young woman was sitting, was the late afternoon sun that shined off of her bleached blond hair, making the dark roots of her natural hair color more apparent. Her evergreen eyes widened as the person on the other end of the phone answered back to her. She shook her head, despite this person not being there to see it, before she replied. "No, he's not here right now, but he should be back soon, I think." She finished as she looked around, as if to see if he was indeed home yet or not. She paused for some time after her response, nodding her head, once again fully knowing this person was not right there to see her agreement. The person on the other end, though, must have had some important information for the man that was not available at the time, and was relaying this information through Kyra to tell him later. "Would you rather have me tell him to call you back?" She asked, before adding a quick bit of information that may lead the one on the other end to agree. "I'm not sure I caught all that, and he might understand it better than me." She said with a giggle. There was once again a moderate amount of silence from her end as she listened to the person on the other end again, this time, however, she stood up from her seat and grabbed a pad of paper and a writing utensil from off of a nearby coffee table. Jotting a few things down, she smiled, and ended the conversation with the one on the other end. "Alright Justin, I'll have Kaden call you back when he gets here. I'm sure he'll be glad to hear from you." She said, before walking back to her seat, and hanging the cordless phone up, sitting it down on the table next to the chair she is seated in. Just a few short seconds later, a door can be heard opening from another part of the home, and moments later a young, spiky, blonde haired man walks into the living room, and his bright blue eyes look at Kyra, who just finished the phone conversation. "Who was it?" He asked, and she looks in surprise, as if she hadn't realized he was there the whole time. Standing from her seat, she carries the pad of paper over, and hands it to him. "I didn't even know you were here Kaden." She admits, and he looks at what is written on the paper. "You just missed Justin calling you, sounded pretty important." Kyra finished. Looking wide eyed at her now, Kaden does a double take, looking at what is written on the paper and Kyra herself back and fourth. "Why didn't you keep him on the phone and wait for me to get out of the shower?" Kaden asks. Kyra's cheeks start to turn a light shade of pink as she looks at the ground momentarily before looking back at Kaden. "I thought you left." She said to him. "I completely spaced that you were taking a shower, I'm sorry." She confesses and apologizes to him, and he smiles and shakes his head. "Well did he say what he wanted? What did he call for?" Kaden asks. "I haven't heard from him in months, since that whole incident actually. So it's kind of strange that he would just call out of the blue like this." He admits, glancing around the room as the two of them walk back into the living room and sit down in adjacent recliners. Thinking for a few moments, Kyra tries to recall exactly what Justin had said to her, but not recalling. "I don't really remember a whole lot of what he said. He seemed reluctant to say most of what he did, and was talking kind of fast at that." She said, situating herself in the chair she is sitting in. Kaden began to look around the room, thinking of any reason to Justin's call. "There are a few things I'd like to think he called for. Apologizing to me would be a good reason, but I highly doubt he'd be calling just to say sorry." He pondered aloud. "Maybe he called to bug me more about that Havoc Entertainment contract he's been trying to get me back into so he's not just wasting money on me with an unofficial deal." Shaking his head, he throws that idea aside as well. "You don't remember ANYTHING he said?" He asked Kyra, trying to emphasize enough to get her to remember. Shaking her head, she answers his question without speaking, but still thinking about it at the same time. In a brick hitting a wall moment, though, something hit her, and she piped up. "Wait, he did say something about wanting me to tell you that he called with good news." She said, beginning to remember at least one thing during the phone conversation. "Also, something about him wanting to help you, and that he found someone he wants to bring by next Friday that you have to meet." She finished with a smile on her face. Closing his eyes and rubbing his forehead with his right hand, Kaden let out a heavy sigh. "My parents are supposed to be coming by next Friday. As if things weren't fucked up enough between the three of us, Justin and this other person are going to be here too?" He asks himself out loud. "It better be important, because I've been waiting on these so called answers that Dad promised to give me for a long time now!" ******* Inside of a hotel room, a single lamp lit the interior, and was enough light to give the middle aged couple, sitting up in bed together, what was needed for their activities. They seemed to be a bit nervous about something, the female more than the male, but yet they both sat, twiddling their thumbs. "Do you think he knows, Alec?" The female, or apparent wife to the male, asked. Turning his head to the left to look at his wife, the man answered her question. "I don't know Mary, but I assume that he does. He wouldn't have sat all this up if he didn't." Alec answers his wife's question. "I just don't know what we did to tip him off. Did we stumble somewhere? Did we leave a trail? Is he THAT pathetic that he has nothing better to do than monitor us while he works on his shit for nothing projects!" He blurts aloud, obviously upset over something. "Calm down Alec." Mary says to him, trying to get him to settle down. "We must have done something that gave away our intentions. No matter what we did, or how he found out, what we are here to do shouldn't change." She admits her feelings to him. "We are here to right something that we have wronged for twenty-six years, and nothing that happens should change that." She says to Alec, who nods his head in agreement. Almost as if on cue, the phone inside of their relatively small hotel room rings, the little red light on the top of it flashes with every ring. Alec, hesitant to pick up, lets it ring a couple more times before finally answering. "This is Mr. Calix." He says, and he nods his head as he hears the person on the other end begin to talk. Rolling his eyes a few times, more than likely at comments from the other end he finds to be ridiculous, his face straightens quickly. "What do you mean we've defied you? We haven't done..." He is cut off, and is forced to listen some more. Mary, beginning to look more scared as the phone conversation continues, especially at the fact that Alec has done little to no talking, begins to nibble on the tips of her manicured fingernails. "No Dami..." Cut off once more, he nods his head and rolls his eyes before continuing. "I'm sorry. Dr. Sorrow. We had no intentions of breaking our agreement; I don't know how you ever got that idea out of us being in America right now." He admitted, though obviously lying. And yet again, more silence from that end of the phone conversation, and beads of sweat began to form on the brow of Alec's forehead. He sat the phone in his lap while letting out a heavy sigh and shaking his head before putting the receiver back to his ear, and Mary looked as if she was about to have a panic attack. "I understand." Alec said before hanging up the phone. "What did he say? Good lord Alec what did he say, and was it bad?" Mary asks frantically as she latches onto Alec's left arm, waiting for his answer. Shaking his head, Alec looks into Mary's eyes. "He said we were going to be punished for some sort of conspiracy to end his research." He said as he let his head fall to his chest, letting out another heavy sigh at the same time. Before Mary was able to ask more questions, though, he answered them for her. "He didn't say what was going to happen to us, but he did say we would find out next Friday." Her mouth dropped open, but no sound came out for several moments. It wasn't until Alec looked back into her eyes that she had anything to say. "But... That's when we are supposed to meet with Kaden."
