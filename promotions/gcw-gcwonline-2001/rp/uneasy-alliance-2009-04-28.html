Uneasy Alliance

28 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/09 James Stevens sat with his eyes glued to the computer monitor as he typed his latest report on their latest acquisition: Darren Slade. New Wave Entertainment, America’s next best hype machine, was going to put its stamp of approval on a man unproven and, in his opinion, unworthy of that distinction. But he wasn’t making the final decisions on things these days, not since his involvement with his grand scheme to bring back that purple dinosaur…that DAMN purple dinosaur… Stevens rubbed his eyes tiredly as the song continued to ring in his ears…”I love you, you love me….?!!?” His newest project was nicknamed “Dr Extreme” and the “King of Kaos.” He snickered to himself at the ridiculous monikers but shrugged. Wrestling was as much about the spectacle as it was the sport. As long as he brings in the money why should I care? Stevens thought to himself. Projections showed he would be wildly popular, but he wasn’t sure why. Was it his good looks? I’m better looking than this schmuck, Stevens sneered. It definitely couldn’t be his personality because the man was as about as warm as a popsicle stick. He could be somewhat charming but hell even Terrell Owens has good days. The big shots upstairs wanted to know why Slade hasn’t been brought into the fold at GCW sooner. It was Stevens’ fault, they surmised. Stevens mumbled a few curse words as he backspaced after misspelling “Dr Extreme” for what seemed like the hundredth time. What was he a doctor of anyways? He brushed off thoughts of insubordination and went back to his report. He had to make up for his last failure in wrestling, but he was going to make amends one way or another. Knock Knock. His latest protégé was here. Hope he’s better than the last… “Come in.” * * * * My hands shook as I walked alongside the attractive secretary bringing me to Mr. Stevens’ office. She was about 5’7’’, with long blonde hair, blue eyes and legs told me that I was not even worth her time. Didn’t stop me from looking though. “Go ahead and knock on the door, he’s expecting you Mr. Slade,” She said with a sensuality that was more genetic than rehearsed. “T..h..a..n..k..s..,” was all I could get out as she walked away from me and my dreams all in one instant. Ouch. To add insult to injury, I was still reeling from my drunken escapade in the city jail last night. My solo celebration and jubilation of being in GCW got the best of me. Luckily I swept it all under the rug and got my fines paid without anyone from New Wave finding out. The last thing I needed now was heat from the suits. I knocked on the door and heard a very business-like voice return. “Come in.” I opened the door and saw James Stevens sitting behind a large maple desk with a laptop facing him and hawk-like eyes staring down on me. I contemplating urinating on myself but thankfully I didn’t have any juice this morning. 'Mr. Slade, I’ve been expecting you,' Stevens said, motioning to the leather chair in front of him. 'Have a seat.' “Thanks for seeing me,” I replied, checking my zipper to make sure all was well down under before sitting. “I’ll be your liaison with New Wave while you’re under contract. I’m in charge of handling public affairs, your endorsements, meetings, publicity events…” My mind swirled as he went off his checklist of things for me to do. Dinners, movie premieres, charity events…and something about a purple dinosaur? I stopped him mid-sentence. “Mr. Stevens, I appreciate everything New Wave has done for me, I really do. But I’m a wrestler. I don’t know anything about…” “You see, that’s the problem,” He shot back. Stevens got up from his seat and straightened his suit all in one motion. He was holding back pent up frustration, and it made me wonder if he had urinary retention. He seemed like the controlling type, someone who liked having his fingers in everything, but someone else was probably pulling the strings. “I used to represent a wrestler before. A very accomplished athlete, but he lacked the skills to make him an even bigger star than he was. Now I have you…” He looked me up and down and needless to say he wasn’t asking me out to dinner after what he observed. “…I realize I have my work cut out for me. You haven’t even done half the things in your career that he did, and he flamed out faster than the New York Yankees pitching staff.” To say that I was insulted would be an understatement. “Look, I might not have done everything the guy before me did, but I’m a pretty damn good wrestler myself. I’ll prove it to you.” Stevens stopped and suddenly became a much different man. He was professional and a business man, but he was also a jerk. I could tell he was biting at the lip to say what he was thinking. 'I'm a business man, Slade. If you can't handle your business in the ring, you better tell me now, because I'm not here to represent a mid carder...' “What did you say” “Did I stutter?” He replied with a questioning look. I got up from my chair and stuck my finger in his face. From the way he stared back at me, he was ready to bite it off. “You do what you have to do, Stevens, but I’ll take care of business in the ring. I promise you that.” 'Then prove it.' I saw the challenge in his eyes and knew that Stevens wanted me to prove that him that I was worth all the time and money being invested in me. “Tune into Worldwide this week, and you’ll see that I’m going to have an INSTANT impact.” 'Talk is cheap. Just do your job...' Stevens turned back to his computer, typing furiously in a way that almost spoke to me to get the hell out. But before I could leave he got me again. “Oh, and Slade?” I stopped in my tracks and felt like punching him in the nose but thought better of it. “What?” “Keep the booze in the fridge. I’m not going to bail you out again. Do it one more time and you'll be snuggling up to some guy booked in there for sodomy, you understand?” I walked out of the office disgusted, pissed off, but most of all a little nervous. I would be looking over my shoulder the rest of the day. * * * * Stevens watched Slade walk out the door and breathed a deep sigh. “Arrogant and pig headed,” Stevens said out loud as he lit up a cigarette. “Not too different from myself,” A voice replied, entering from a side door. Stevens briefly glanced at the man entering the room with one part disdain and another part relief. “That’s part of the problem.” The man shrugged. “From the business point of view, but not the wrestling. You have to have a certain arrogance if you’re going to rise to the top of this sport. He’s got the instinct.” “Instinct, maybe. But I need him to win matches otherwise he won’t get a deal endorsing Cheerios let alone be the face of this company.” “I think I can help with that,” The man replied. He grabbed the cigarette from Stevens’ hand and put it out. “And don’t you know these things will kill you?” “Just do your part to make sure he rises to the top of GCW,” Stevens countered. “I don’t want any missteps with Slade. We’ve both got too much riding on this.” The man nodded slowly. “I’ll be at his side at all times, whether he likes it or not. Believe me, it’ll be nice to be out there again.” “You’ve been out of the ring for a long time,” Stevens said, reaching for yet another cigarette. “You really think anyone out there is going to remember you?” He stood there reminiscing about the past. “It doesn’t matter, but you know, they did used to call me the LEGEND.” Stevens snickered. “They did? Or you did? There’s a difference you know? This bozo calls himself ‘Dr Extreme’ for Christ’s sake.” “By the time I’m through with him, the entire GCW will be calling him just one thing…the next GCW champion.” And this time, Stevens didn’t dare disagree.
