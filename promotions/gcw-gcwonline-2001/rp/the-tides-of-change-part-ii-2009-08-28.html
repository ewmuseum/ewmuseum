The Tides of Change [part II]

28 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28th, 2009 “You’re up in 2mins SurReal.” “Okay thanks.” It had been quite some time since SurReal had been asked to take part on a talk show. When the call came from Larry King, the man upon who’s show he had appeared many times in years gone by, he simply couldn’t refuse. Remarkably for a man who had performed in sold-out arenas across the globe and who had been in this situation plenty of times before, he still felt the tightness of nerves in his stomach. Perhaps understandably so, after all, it had been over half a decade since he had last been on live television outside of the comfortable familiarity of a wrestling ring. Larry King: “So folks, how about we get our next guest out here?” The question gets the expected cheers from the live audience and prompts the veteran host to introduce the Irishman. Larry King: “Then folks it is my great please to welcome back to Larry King Live, an old friend of the show, now making a name for himself in Global Championship Wrestling, The Tribal War Machine, SURREAL!” Chromium's 'Ex Amino', SurReal’s entrance theme, plays a short blast as the Irish veteran makes his way out, playing up to the live audience a little as he does so. It’s unusual to see him dressed smartly in a rather expensive looking three-piece suit, his normally scruffy long hair tied back and for once, no face paint, no doubt there are a few GCW fans who wouldn’t have recognised him at first glance. With Larry now out of his seat, the two men shake hands as the host motions for his new guest to take a seat. Larry King: “Long time no see my friend!” SurReal: “How are you Larry, yeah I guess it’s been a good few years since I’ve been sitting in this seat.” Larry King: “It’s good to have you back on. Let’s get down to business, you signed for Global Championship Wrestling just over two months ago is that right?” SurReal: “Yeah, around end of May, start of June. The writing was on the wall really for Ringside Wrestling and I was lucky enough to get a call from Christian Zenith, you’ve had him on the show before I believe? Long story short, he knew the situation with Ringside and he set-up a meeting between Steven Caldera and myself. And that as they say, was that!” Larry King: “So you debuted at a pay-per-view, Rampage? Where you were unfortunate to pick up a knock?” SurReal: “Yeah, debuted in a six man tag match at Rampage, and to be honest, we are probably best not getting into the injury. Lets just say that Lucas Quinton would be advised to watch his back! Because there is going to be a time when he is going to have to face me one-on-one, and when that time comes, I’ll be doing everything in my power to show the arrogant little prick that it is unwise to intentionally attempt to injure a fourteen year veteran!” Larry King: “Sounds like there is a little bit of bad blood there SurReal?” SurReal: “Oh absolutely Larry, I’ve been in this business for a long time now and if I had a dollar for every time I’ve seen a rookie like Quinton burst onto the scene I’d be a very rich man! These punks arrive on the scene, they’ve got the build for the business, some even have the look, some of them have the charisma to connect with the crowd and get themselves over. But then they think that the hard work is done? And they forget that a wrestler should ALWAYS be looking to add experience, to master the basics of wrestling and look to add new styles and skills to their arsenal. The biggest difference between young men coming into the sport now and when I was breaking into the business is respect for your peers. That was the ULTIMATE whenever I was making a name for myself, you have to show the respect that is due to those that have the experience, show a willingness to work with everyone and learn from each and every encounter. Listen to what the veterans have to say and take it on board. Nowadays, these kids have no interest in taking on board advice and suggestions from the guys who have put their bodies on the line for years on end. They come in, gain a few early victories and next thing you know they are demanding title opportunities, and main event opportunities, they feel like they should be a hell of a lot higher on the card than they really should be! This Quinton kid is just another one of these punks!” Larry King: “Some choice words for Lucas Quinton there! So the injury that you clearly blame Quinton for put things on a backburner for you, but you made your comeback last week against your old friend Shaman, and managed to walk away with the victory, so you must be happy with that?” SurReal: “Honestly Larry, yes and no! I can’t underline how important it was to get the victory, but at the same time, there were a few mistake in the match, and you know me well enough to know that I don’t take mistakes lightly. I certainly wasn’t happy with the finish of the match, I think it’s very unlike me to misjudge a move like that, and I would imagine that Shaman probably wasn’t too happy about feeling the impact of my knee crashing down on his face. But I think we need to put that down to trying a little bit too hard, I think Shaman knows me well enough to know that it was a mistake and that there was certainly no intent to do something that would seriously risk causing him injury!” Larry King: “Well I guess you will find out if there is any bad feeling pretty soon. As I understand it you are fighting him again on this week’s show?” SurReal: “Yeah that’s right, I will be teaming up with a guy called Cade Carson to take on Shaman and Jorge Samuelsson.” Larry King: “And I hear both of those guys are monsters, over seven foot tall each, big big guys!” SurReal: “Absolutely, they are huge men, no doubt about that, but hopefully Carson is capable of holding his end, if he can, I see no reason why we can’t walk out of the arena with our arms held high in victory.” Larry King: “And is he? What do you know about Mr. Carson?” SurReal: “Truth be told, I don’t know a thing about Cade Carson. I have to admit that my mind has been pretty much focused on trying to get Quinton in the ring, and of course, Shaman. I’ve not had a chance to clue up on Carson, but I’ll be keeping an eye on him when we are in the ring, gotta try and get as much information on him as I can in the next few days because after this week he will go from partner to nemesis!” Larry King: “You refer to Global Championship Wrestling’s upcoming pay-per-view extravaganza.. WARPATH! So what exactly is your match for that night?” SurReal: “At Warpath, it’s going to be myself, Cade Carson, Cyren and the Mysterious Masked Mexican fighting it out for an opportunity to challenge for the GCW Television Title.” Larry King: “And you have a little extra motivation to win that match I believe?” SurReal: “I see you’ve done your homework Larry. You’re absolutely right. Also at Warpath, Quinton takes on Bishop Steele for the Television Title..” Larry King: “Meaning of course..” SurReal: “Meaning, If both myself and Lucas Quinton walk away from Warpath victorious, he has nowhere to hide. I have been trying to get my hands on him for a few weeks now and haven’t yet been able to get him in the ring, but if he wins the strap and I become the number one contender, then its written in stone and I can finally get my hands on the little rat!” Larry King: “SurReal, as always it has been a pleasure to have you on the show, good luck this week and beyond. Ladies and Gentleman, SurReal!” Taking his cue to leave, SurReal makes his way back to the green room with a rousing reception from the audience. Whilst he was well behaved on the show, as he plays the interview back over in his head he knows himself that he came across as very bitter. But thinking about it, so what? He IS bitter at Quinton, and keeping that to himself won’t help him achieve his goal of getting his chance to exact some revenge. The more people that know he is gunning for Quinton the more likely he is to get his man. As for things with Shaman after last week, who knows? Perhaps his mistake in the ring will be held against him, perhaps it wont, either way.. he will find out at Worldwide!
