A New Life

1 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-1-07 The smell of tobacco and cheap alcohol greeted him like an old friend, an odor he had become all too familiar with. He stood at the door, as water dripped from his coat onto the floor. ?Well look what the damn cat dragged in.? Ray Holland lifted his head to see the only man that has really been there for him in the last year. Ray had never spent more than a few months in any one town, he jumped around from city to city doing odd jobs until there was nothing left to do. That is, until he met Sam Morrow. Sam just made him feel comfortable from the moment the two shared words, and for someone who doesn't necessarily get too attached with anyone, Ray decided to spend a little extra time in the small Arizona town. ?Hey there, Sam.? Ray removed his coat and shook it a few times before he hung it up on the coat rack beside the door. ?You miss me?? ?I'd miss you more if you'd stay gone for more than two days,? Sam said with a laugh. ?Still have work that needs to be done,? Ray replied with a chuckle of his own. ?Besides, who's gonna make sure all hell doesn't break loose in here if I'm gone?? ?I made do before you came in here begging for free beers, and I'll do just fine when you're out making some real money on TV.? Ray just shrugged his shoulders as he slid onto a stool in front of the bar. ?It don't pay much more than I was making doing lube jobs at Jake's shop, but the damn benefits are a lot nicer.? ?I didn't know Jake had benefits,? Sam said with a puzzled look on his face. ?He don't,? Ray laughed out loud, ?that's what makes them better.? The two share a laugh, as Sam pats Ray on the shoulder. ?So,? Sam began, ?you want the usual?? ?Nah,? Ray answered with a shake of his head. ?How about you open up a bottle of the good stuff you keep in the back.? Sam pretended not to know what Ray was talking about for a moment, then took a quick glance at the other patrons before he leaned over the bar a bit. ?Keep that shit down.? ?These drunk old fools barely even understand English, Sam,? Ray said with a grin. ?Just go grab us a bottle and pour us a few, we got some celebrating to do.? Sam nodded his head then headed to the back, while Ray glanced around the rest of the bar and thought about all the shit that had happened to him in the last year. Funny how his future as a wrestler started off as the ramblings of a drunk bastard wallowing in self pity at this bar, possibly the very same seat he was on. ~{ o o }~ ?Here he is, Mr. Caldera.? Steven Caldera lifted his head from a mound of paperwork, his best used car salesman grin flashed like a neon sign in Vegas as he stood up and extended a hand to his guest. ?Hello,? he said with a slight pause. ?I'm sorry, but I don't have the pleasure of knowing your name.? ?Name's Ray,? Holland replied as he stared down at Caldera's hand, then back up to the man that acted like the two had been friends for quite some time. ?Well, Ray,? Caldera tried his best to keep Holland from noticing that he was a bit perturbed by the lack of a cordial greeting from his guest, but the rage could be seen as it built up in his eyes. ?I'm glad you accepted my offer to meet with me.? Ray glanced over his shoulder at Toad and smacked his tongue against his teeth. ?It's not like I had much of a choice. It was either this or go to jail for hitting some punk kid. I'm sure you can figure out why I chose this option.? ?Nevertheless,? Steven rested his elbows atop his desk and interlocked his fingers, ?You're here and that is all that matters.? ?Cut the bullshit,? Ray snapped. ?Just tell me what you got on your mind so I can be on my way.? ?Not much for small talk I see.? ?It's been a long night,? Ray answered the GCW owner as he took a seat across from the man. ?Not to mention that the drive home is gonna take a few hours, so spare me the friendly conversation and get on with it, because we ain't friends.? ?Very well, Ray,? Caldera sat up straight and wiped the grin off of his face, then replaced it with the cold calculating statuesque face reserved for the likes of Vivica Valentine and Lia Ambrosi. ?I thought we could be civil about the whole thing, but if you want to get right on down to the brass tacks of the situation, then by all means, let's.? Ray leaned back in his chair, and kicked his feet onto the desk, as Caldera continued his spiel. ?You assaulted one of my employees,? Caldera began in a tone cold enough to freeze the Ohio River. ?Now, not only could he press charges on you, but I could tie you up in so much litigation it would make your ass pucker up every time my name was mentioned.? ?What the hell are you gonna take from me,? Ray joked out loud. ?It's not like I own anything worth any money.? ?Exactly,? Caldera grinned like a Cheshire Cat. ?You don't have the finances to contend with my legal team, and I guarantee that you will do time to compensate me for the loss revenue that Jayce Bradley would've brought in.? ?The guy's a jo-? ?But he's my joke, Ray.? Holland finally understood what this was all about, and he knew that he was not going to walk out of here without giving Caldera what he wanted. ?As much of a slump that Bradley has been in, he's always had the ability to be what I envisioned him when I first signed him. Of course, that was before he got knocked out with one punch by a relative unknown. I can't have that,? Caldera stood up and made his way around his desk, closer to Ray. ?In order for him to have any credibility when he comes back, I need you to be more than just some guy with a lucky punch. I need you to be a force to be reckoned with.? ?You offering me a job,? Ray asked, a bit confused at the whole situation. ?I've made people into household names, Ray. I've created legacies that will forever be talked about whenever the GCW is mentioned.? Cue the grin from Caldera once more. ?Maybe, once I'm done with you, I'll forget everything I saw in Jayce... Maybe you'll be all I need.? ~{ o o }~ ?You thinking about how you're gonna spend all that money?? Ray snapped back into reality and glanced over at his buddy Sam. ?Nah, just trying to figure out who you're gonna have tend the bar when you go see my debut.? ?What?? ?Front row seats, pal,? Ray said, as he pulled an envelope out of his pocket and laid it on the bar. ?No bullshit this time.? ?This is great man,? Sam said with a slight tear in his eye. ?You're gonna be great, you know.? ?I'm out of shape,? Ray replied. ?I got this gut that gets in the way every time I try to do a crunch, and love handles you could lift a keg with.? ?Trust me, Ray,? Sam said as he poured some bourbon into two shot glasses. ?You're gonna do just fine.? ?I hope you're right, Sam,? Ray responded as he lifted up his shot glass. ?I hope you're right.? ?To a new life.? The two toasted each other, then downed the drinks. ?A new life,? Ray thought to himself. ?Anything's better than this one.?
