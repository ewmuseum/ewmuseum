The Big Stage

16 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/16/10 I. Dominic hadn’t been fully exposed to the Texas High School football scene before that evening. His Freshman year at St. Francis High School and he was already given the starting job at Runningback. The pressure had been mounting ever since he had been chosen for the position. He didn’t even have anything left inside of his stomach after puking numerous times. “Listen, Son,” Head Coach Scott Arend yelled out over the noisy crowd. “You’ve been great tonight but we need you again. We need a fucking TD! Go with the one back set, halfback draw. Go get it, Son.” Dominic nodded his head and rushed towards the huddle. It was true, Dominic’s 167 yards and 2 Touchdowns were very impressive for a freshman Runningback. In most circumstances he would have felt completely fulfilled by what he had accomplished already. Problem was that there was more to do, the job had been left unfinished. Dominic reached his teammates in the huddle and gave the play to the Quarterback, Jason Gaffney. Jason smirked upon hearing the call and slapped Dominic on the back. Jason, or One-Squared as Dominic named him, called out the play to the rest of the huddle, ending it with a proclamation … “On GO and we are going to fucking score! Ready, BREAK!” The team clapped their hands in unison, the universal sign for breaking a Football huddle and heading to the line of scrimmage. They lined up, Quarterback under the Center, Dominic behind the Quarterback. “DOOOOOWN!” In one motion the team dropped to their stances. “SET!” Everyone in attendance froze. Their St. Francis Knights were down by 4 points, enough time for one play … possibly two if things went right. The St. Francis Knights were thought to be a young and up and coming team. Their opponents however, The Libbey Cowboys, were the big dogs. State Champions the year before and looking to repeat again. Dominic was in a position to change the fortunes of many but especially his own. He could build a legacy, he could become his own man. All he had to do was shine brighter than everyone else at a time when he was expected to wilt. So, he had to do the unexpected. His hands shook slightly as his mind accelerated to 1,000 miles per hour. Fear crept in. So did doubt. He would have shook his head to knock loose those cobwebs but he couldn’t, the rules of Football prevented that. Instead he gritted his teeth and tightened his muscles. He whispered to himself. “I’m not afraid.” He swallowed. “I’m the starting Runningback of the mother fuckin’ Knights.” He balled his hands up into tight fists. “I’m Dominic Wall!” He closed his eyes and let everything melt away. He knew he could do it, he knew he would do it. Everything he ever wanted had been right in front of him. It begged him to take it. Dominic had every intention of doing just that. He opened his eyes. “Let’s dance.” II. They order their food quickly, Roberto getting a coffee and some French Toast and Dominic choosing a pancake stack with some chocolate milk. Roberto chuckles at his young pupil’s choice of drink. When the waitress walks off with the order, Dominic grins. “What? I like chocolate milk.” “You make me feel old, that’s all.” Roberto shakes his head. “My son loves the stuff too.” They share a quick, quiet laugh. “So, tell me,” Roberto begins. “You think you’re ready for this week’s challenge?” “Yeah, sure I am.” Roberto’s disappointment in the answer wasn’t well hidden, if he intended to hide it at all. “I’m not sure you even believe what you just said. How can you really expect me to?” Dominic groans and fights off the urge to roll his eyes. “Sorry Boss, I guess I’m just nervous. This thing kind of came out of nowhere.” “I agree.” Roberto adjusts in his seat. “You can’t be at a disadvantage before you even walk through the ropes though.” Dominic shrugs “I guess our Commish sees something in me and that is a good thing and all …” his voice drifts off, obviously unsure if he should finish the thought. “But?” “Well,” Dominic bites his bottom lip for a second before letting out a sigh. “I just wonder if I’m only being given this opportunity because of you. I’m LeStatt Knight’s lackey, what if they feel like they owe you something?” The two lean back in their seats when their waitress comes back with their drinks and places them down in front of them. Roberto thanks her, Dominic smiles. The waitress blushes. As she walks away, Dominic’s eyes linger her way a little longer. He refocuses on Roberto who kick starts the conversation again. “They probably do owe me something,” He smirks. “But that isn’t what this is about. Your boss isn’t the same boss I had. Johnny probably doesn’t even remember me anymore with how loopy that son of a bitch is. This week’s Main Event was more than likely made for one of two reasons. One, he pulled names out of a hat or two, he sees potential in you. I say we go with the latter and move on from there.” “Can I ask you something?” “Sure, Dom.” “Do YOU think I’m ready?” Roberto doesn’t answer right away. Instead he picks up his coffee mug and takes a slow drink, careful to not burn his lips. Just as slowly he puts the mug back down on the table. Dominic figures he is stalling. Roberto would probably agree. “Do I think you’re ready to beat Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm? No. Sorry. The odds are stacked against you Mr. Wall. Not only are you going after former champions in GCW, you are doing it with a partner who hasn’t wrestled in years. At least not on the stage that you will at WorldWide. Tessa and Storm will have chemistry on their side as well. You and Borealis will not.” Dominic feels like an apple has magically formed in his throat, blocking his airway. He tries to swallow to force it down but it does no good. He slightly drops his head obviously not at all pleased with what he heard. “Don’t you dare fucking feel sorry for yourself.” Roberto pounds the top of the table which startles Dominic and forces his eyes to meet Roberto’s. His eyes were intense. Dominic always felt intimidated when Roberto glared at him like that. “I said I don’t think you’re ready. I did not say you couldn’t win. Most of what I threw your way was just fluff. Outside influences. Johnny Borealis is not your permanent tag team partner, you don’t need chemistry. You need to focus on you and you alone.” “You think I can beat Tessa? Storm? Even with my little experience?” “You can.” Roberto nods. “I know Tessa well. She will not be at her best that night. She will still be stinging from losing the title. You need to jump on that, use it to your advantage. Don’t let her breath if you’re in the ring with her.” Dominic doesn’t respond right away, he tries digesting the words Roberto had been saying. He wants to believe that he can pull it off in spite of everything stacked against him. Typically Dominic has been able to put up a wall of invincibility. Inside he may have been terrified of the thought of losing but on the outside? He is usually a rock with no signs of weakness. There is something about this sport, about this man that can see right through it. Dominic starts to speak but is interrupted by the waitress again and she is holding the food that they ordered. They all exchange the customary pleasantries as she puts the plates of food down on the table. They thank her and tell her nothing else is needed before she turns and leaves their area again. Dominic’s gaze lingers again and he can tell she is putting a little more sway in her walk now. He doesn’t mind. “How do I-” Dominic is cut off. “Shhh” “What?” “Food is here. Eat then talk.” Although Dominic has a lot to ask, he does as he is told. III. “GO!” The world seemed to slow down around Dominic. It was as if God had pressed the slow-mo button on his remote. The Quarterback takes the snap and immediately begins to fade back, closer and closer to him with each step. Dominic’s eyes focus on the ball but in that moment he felt as if his vision went from full screen to wide screen. Everything was crystal clear to him. The blitzing Outside Linebackers. The Middle Linebacker dropping back in coverage. The Defensive Tackle being swept to the right to clear a path. Dominic exhaled, his moment had finally come. He wanted to make a great first impression. He wanted to let everyone, the fans, coaches and teammates that he was the right guy for the job. Dominic wanted them to trust him, to have faith in his abilities. He didn’t want to wilt under the pressure and the bright lights. It was his stage, his moment to shine. He took the handoff and was immediately met by the outstretched arm of one of the blitzing Linebackers. He shook it off easily with a quick turn of his shoulder. He hesitates for a split second to choose the path of least resistance. He found it. He ran. Adrenaline had been pumping through his body all through the night before the game. Dominic hardly was able to sleep. He vomited multiple times before even arriving at school that morning. It was all behind him then. The puke, the anxiety, the fear of failing, all of it had been shoved aside so that Dominic could focus on one thing and one thing only … scoring a Touchdown. Anything less than that would’ve be considered a failure in his eyes. Dominic squeezed through two of his rather large offensive linemen, felt fingers scrape at his arms. He caught the Middle Linebacker rushing at him but Dominic was too nimble and he spun to his right, leaving the would-be tackler hugging air and crashing to the grassy field. He hop stepped to the right to get his momentum going again. The home crowd was full of excitement and anticipation. They’d all heard about the new stud player on the team but up until that point it was nothing more than hype. Dominic hadn’t proven anything. Dominic was just another helmet, jersey and pair of cleats. Things were about to change though. The crowd could feel it. Dominic could feel it. Everyone involved was ready to explode. He rushed towards the sideline and his eyes grew incredibly large once he noticed that the only thing standing between him and seven points was a Safety. A quick glance at the opposing player told Dominic all he needed to know. His eyes were wide but from fear, not anticipation. The kid didn’t bend his knees, didn’t move his feet. Dominic knew he could run through him or around him. The Safety wanted no part of him. Dominic was like a Shark smelling blood in the Ocean waters. It was time to go for the kill. IV. Dominic pokes at the remaining, syrup soaked piece of pancake on his plate with his fork. He watches as he smashes the middle and the syrup gushes out. Roberto has long been done with his early morning breakfast but he was still working on his third coffee and reading the sports section. Dominic failed in numerous attempts to pull his mentor away from his newspaper and caffeine and he was beginning to grow annoyed. He wants to talk about the coming WorldWide. He wants to ask Roberto more about what it is like stepping into the ring with Tessa and Storm. Dominic would love to learn more about Johnny Borealis. He wants Roberto to concentrate on what is important here … HIM. Just when Dominic thought he’d go nuts with the waiting, Roberto folded up the paper and set it down in the seat next to him. Roberto folded his hands together and leaned forward on the table. “You do understand that I know what you are going through, right?” Dominic doesn’t answer the question. “I do.” Roberto raises an eyebrow “You’re nervous. You are afraid of being the weak link. By far you are in the back of the pack when it comes to experience. You don’t have much of a pedigree.” “You’re not helping make me think any other way.” “I’m not here for that, Dom.” “Then what are you here for?” “To make sure that you make yourself think it.” “I’m confident.” “You’re cautiously optimistic, at best. You have to be more than that. This doesn’t happen over night though, I get that. I wasn’t always what I became. I’ve failed plenty of times, failure happens. People don’t always like to discuss the times they aren’t the best. I say you have to embrace it and let it fuel you to be better. People who are afraid to admit their mistakes are the people who will continue to make them. You’ve already shown your ability to adapt once. You lose to Rediker but you changed and you won at High Stakes.” “But that was Donny Diamond,” stress in Dominic’s voice is evident. “Not Tessa Windsor.” Roberto brings a finger up and wags it back and forth. “Didn’t I tell you not to worry about the things you can’t control? Tessa, Storm, Shaman, Diamond or even Andy Murray … doesn’t matter. You have to just do everything to make your game better. Don’t let the pressure get to you. Put it all on the line and you’ll succeed many more times than you’ll fail. Don’t be afraid to fail and be confident that you won’t.” Dominic opens his mouth to talk again but is stopped when Roberto abruptly stands up and throws money down on the table. “Wait, we’re leaving?” “I am.” Roberto nods towards their waitress. “You aren’t.” Dominic turns to look and catches her staring at him. Now it is his turn to blush. He quickly looks up at Roberto. “You crazy?” “Go, have some fun. Clear your mind. I’ve got a plane to catch anyways.” “You’re not staying for WorldWide?” “Shit, I’ve got a family to take care of.” Roberto points at the stack of bills on the table. “Some of that is for you. Go and enjoy yourself.” Roberto turns and walks away and smiles at their waitress as he heads for the door. Dominic stands up and calls out to him. “Wait,” Roberto stops and looks back. “How’d your first shot on the big stage turn out?” Roberto smiles. “Not as well as yours will.” Roberto nods towards the waitress again. “See you later, kid.” “Bye.” Dominic sits down moments after Roberto leaves. It only takes a few seconds to pass before the waitress shows up and sits down in the seat that Robert was in. Dominic reads her name tag and smiles. “Nichole. My name’s Dominic.” She smiles a bright, wide smile. “Can I ask you something, Dominic?” Confidence grows inside of him. Maybe he can win. Maybe he can beat former World Champions. Perhaps he can be his own man and not just be ‘LeStatt Knight’s protégé’. Maybe … “Was that LeStatt Knight !?” … Not V. Dominic smiled as he sprinted towards the last line of defense. One more time he does a shoulder shake, he faked to the sideline and cut back to the inside. The Safety bit and almost fell over while trying to change directions as fast as Dominic had. For good measure Dominic placed the ball in his left arm and used his right arm to push down on his opposition’s helmet, assisting him in the completion of his descent to the grass at their feet. The crowd roared in approval. They could taste the victory. They were ready to fully believe in Dominic Wall, hell, Dominic Wall was prepared to believe in himself. The smile grew wider as the fanatic howls from the crowd near the end zone grew louder with each step. Dominic’s mind wandered. He thought about the best way to celebrate the game winning score. The image of Samantha, the head cheerleader that never wore underwear and already had breast implants, underneath him in the backseat of his car. Then he thought about what her best friend would look like in that same spot an hour later. The glory of being the town hero was coming to him fast and furious. But so was the other Safety. Fast and Furious. Dominic didn’t see it coming. The Safety launched himself at Dominic’s chest, colliding viciously and sent Dominic off his feet. The cheers turned to gasps of horror. The World turned upside down for Dominic. Upon impact the air was driven from his lungs and it caused Wall to clutch at his chest with both hands as he coughed and wheezed. It didn’t take long for Dominic to realize that he had forgotten one very important thing. The ball. Dominic rolled over in time to get a look at the ball being scooped up by the same kid who laid him out. He wanted to get up and chase him down, take the ball back and maybe his pride as well. The problem was, he couldn’t because his body wouldn’t allow it. He could only watch as the Safety ran out of bounds and right into being mobbed by his teammates. The crowd couldn’t muster the energy to boo. It remained in shock for a long while after the play. Dominic felt alone and it didn’t help that most of his teammates walked slowly towards the sidelines, heads hung low. They didn’t bother helping Wall up or even looking in his direction to see if he was alright. He failed and he had no one else to blame but himself. Tears formed in the corner of his eyes but he refused to let them fall. He took in a deep breath and shook his head before he pushed himself back up to his feet. The walk back to his own sideline felt like it lasted an eternity. He could sense the glaring and accusing eyes of everyone in the crowd burning a hole into him. The walls felt like they were closing in. Dominic hated himself. He couldn’t allow this to happen again. Losing was bad enough but letting his team down? Letting the fans down? It was too much. Turn the spotlight on again … Dominic wouldn’t fail Dominic couldn’t fail Could he?
