When there are no more pages to turn...

25 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 Nov 05 * This is a joint effort by myself and the infinitely talented Jer ... hope everyone enjoys this. * It had started out as a typical day for LeStatt Knight.” He awoke early in the morning next to the love of his life, Mary.” He worked out a bit to knock the sleep from his body and then had himself a bite to eat.” Shower, shit, shave ... the usual events for a man. The day breezed by without so much as a hiccup.” In his mind all there was to worry about was his World Title defense come NC-17 which was sure to test his resolve. Something strange happened though, something that he could not explain.” The sun had just fallen behind the horizon and the sky was full of color.” Knight moved from his bed and towards the window, looking out at the magnificent scene in front of him.” Slowly but surely the colors began to fade and darkness engulfed the world. It was then that something stirred inside of him.” He couldn't explain it but something wasn't quite right.” He knew this feeling but it had been a very long time since it caught up in his throat as it did now.” Sweat beads formed on his forehead and he could feel the beat of his heart become more erratic. Another heart attack? That could not have been it and he knew it.” So what else could he do?” LeStatt moved through his bedroom and got his wallet, keys, and threw his coat over his shoulders.” He kissed Mary goodbye, telling her he'd be back a little while later and headed out of the door. Answers were needed and he knew exactly where to find them.” How he knew wasn't of concern, just another unsolved mystery.” Normally this man wasn't one to question the feelings he would get and tonight was no different.” He kept his eyes on the road and moved around so fast that he probably would have earned a $1,000 ticket for speeding.” For some reason there were no policemen out on this night.” Fate? Destiny?” A 24 hour Dunkin Donuts?” One would never know. It took close to two hours for Knight to reach his destination.” He turned off the engine to his Diablo and stepped out.” His eyes stared at the black bars that made the entrance gate to a cemetery.” To most a place like this would give them the creeps, but to LeStatt Knight a cemetery was a peaceful place.” Although if where his feeling were leading him were right on ... peace could be the last thing known tonight. He moved forward and pushed the gate open.” Fate and Destiny were funny things sometimes.” The clouds played and danced in the sky smothering the moons light in one moment and setting it free the next, Knight walked towards his destination with a purpose, reasons and rational unexplainable, some mysteries didn't need to be explained, they were better off just remaining that....Mysteries, to the top of the hill and towards the back of the cemetery he went to a quiet little corner where only one small stone stood surrounded by the giant oaks that stood Silent sentry around the Graveyard Squinting ahead as the clouds denied the moon it's light again Knight realized he hadn't been the first to arrive, but this wasn't entirely unexpected, the man stood with his arms folded and his head bowed, standing so still he could very well have been one of the stones themselves, a monument to one of Knights worst fears personified, as Knight stopped a few paces away the figure spoke. 'I knew you'd come...' LeStatt placed his hands behind his back and moved another step closer.” He was careful to not get too close though, at least not yet. 'Ghosts are tough to ignore.' LeStatt said. He said this with a slight chuckle.” It wasn't a chuckle that would ruin what was a very intense moment but more of? chuckle out of nerves.” LeStatt Knight and this man had been through years of hell, mostly from their own doing.” But the man was suppose to be out of his life, out of his thoughts, hell the man was suppose to be dead. 'They are...' he said with a touch of uncharacteristic sadness in his voice 'But i'm solid enough Roberto' As the figure turned to face knight he confirmed any remaining doubts that Knight might have had, though the features were older, weary, it was the eyes that drew Knight to him, it always had been the eyes, so alive, so full of life and anger....the face...the eyes of Alex Grey the man known as Payne, Alex took two steps forward coming face to face with Knight he reached out and laid his hand on Knights shoulder 'Does that feel solid enough Roberto?' Knight felt a shiver go up his spine at that moment.” His body jerked slightly and he swallowed hard.” He could never explain what it was about Alex, Payne or whatever it was they decided to call each other as it was all interchangeable. Since the day they met a burning hatred had formed.” A hatred that would burn stronger than any wild forest fire could imagine.” Not only did that hatred fill them but it spread to those that they each cared about the most, or at times those who they only pretended to care about. Yet when their eyes looked at each other an unfamiliar feeling crept inside.” They didn't seem too anxious to throw fists, shards of glass, steel chairs, wood, fire or any other assortments of objects at each other.” Hell if LeStatt did not know any better he could swear that he almost felt at ease around this man. 'It's always been real with you Alex,' Knight moves passed Alex, his hand falling from Knight's shoulder.” LeStatt moves closer to the headstone that Payne stood at, turning his back to his once great enemy. 'Where have you been?' 'In hell' Payne's voice offered as he moved to stand next to the man who'd once been his mortal enemy 'No truer words have ever been spoken Roberto...I've been in hell...with only ghosts to keep me company...her ghost was always there you understand' he said gesturing to the grave 'She was my biggest failing...I couldn't save her from Jason...how long i lamented that Roberto i can't quite remember...which made it even more painful when i came to find...that all she ever had been....was a god damned lie' 'A man's past always has a way of finding him.'? Knight said almost in agreement. 'There are things that you will never forget, its impossible to do so.” The trick is realizing that sometimes the things that have went wrong were never your fault.'? He forces himself to look over at Payne. 'Would you believe it if I told you I never really believed that you were dead?' 'That's just it Roberto....I never existed in the first place....I was a lie...she was a lie...an actor hired by the devil himself...you were always the wild card Roberto...the unexplained variable in my life...I hated you....I feared you...you were the only real genuine thing in my life....which was why I gravitated towards you, I was obsessed with you...I hurt you...you hurt me...through blood sweat and tears we were united...which is why i wanted to meet you one last time...to tell you the truth and give you something? LeStatt lifted an eyebrow at these words.” He turned his body to almost completely face Payne.” He tilted his head up slightly.” 'Truth? Alex I have never gotten anything but truth from you.” You are the only person to ever put genuine fear into my heart.” The power you possess is infinite ... if that life is a lie then I fear for what your truth is.'? Knight brushes some hair from his face. 'What is it that you have Alex?' 'I had a family once Roberto...a mother and father who loved me very much...then the devil came...he took me away from my family, caused me pain and anguish, he made me know true suffering, at the end of his twisted experiments he convinced me that I was Alex Grey, I'd forgotten my family, the devil gave me a new family, a family that hated me...except for my sister' he said gesturing to the grave 'She was the actor who's part it was to love me....for whatever reason the devil didn't like her...he killed her....I went mad and the rest was a blur until I met you Roberto...that part of the story you know as well as I' he smiled 'After DWA...after we went our separate ways, I rested up from my back injury...the devil came back into my life as he had so many times before, he hurt me again...because you see his experiments were starting to wear off....I was starting to remember who i had been before the madness began...the devil sunk his claws into me again but this time i escaped...I came here to let you know these things...my parents had another son after i was gone...you've met him...talked with him...you've met the devil too and it's all making sense now I can see it in your eyes....you know their names' “Martin ? Draven.” Knight didn’t say this in question, somehow he knew. 'Yes....Martin' Payne said with a shake of his head 'Martin is my cousin...he stole me away when i was a young boy...he always appeared young but he's pushing his late forties by now Roberto...he may come after you now, you always made him angry because you couldn't be controlled...Martin's cousin of course is Draven Masters....Draven is the child my parents had after i was stolen from them' Knight turned again, this time kneeling down at the grave.” His hand went inside his jacket and pulled out a white rose.” He tossed it down on the grave.”” Knight smirked slightly, remembering his hellacious nightmare and the significance of a white rose according to the demon form of one Alex Grey.”” 'Alex Masters huh?” Well it doesn't have the ring to it that PAYNE does but it'll do.' Knight remains kneeling but looks up at Alex. 'Martin is a fucking coward.” He has not dared to come near me since the last time he stuck his nose in my business.” I am not completely the same out of control animal that you may remember, but it would only take a certain set of actions to bring it back if only for awhile.” I have always thought that Mr. Smalls deserved to be buried under a pile of dirt and worms.” I would almost dare him to come back for me.' Knight smiles a genuine smile now. 'If only for old times sake.' 'I'm going away Roberto...after all this i'm going some place warm and tropical and i don't think i'm going to come back...but i wanted to give you this' he said handing him a box 'It has some history to it....but i can't think of a more fitting gift to give to you...and if he does come after you i want you to put that in his mouth and put a fucking bullet in his brain Roberto' the box Knight knew contained the gun....the gun Payne had once shot his 'Brother' Jason O'neil with....the gun Payne had almost killed Knight with...it was as cursed a weapon as one could ever come Knight raised to his feet, his eyes fixed on the box.” Soon he lifted his gaze to the man who stood before him.” He seemed to stand there, searching for the best words in such a situation. 'He will get his Alex.' Knight straightens his posture. 'There is a certain truth that you should know at this point.” The truth that for the first time since we met on that night, backstage at a GCW show and I (he chuckles) sucker punched you, I can stand here and look you in the eyes without wanting to rip your head off.' “TO most men this would probably be an insult.” Strangely for these two it was far from that.” For these two it was a show of something other than hatred ... and that in itself was amazing. 'At this point I cannot say that there is a hatred that burns in my heart when I look upon your face or into your eyes.” We've been through everything two people can.” Death, broken bones, broken hearts, Wylie, RaZiel, Michelle, Jeff Wylde, Martin ... yet if I had to put my life in anyone's hands it'd be yours.” Most would find that to be on the brink of insanity.' Knight shook his head slightly 'Without Payne there never would have been a LeStatt Knight.” In a way you are the closest thing I've had to a..' Knight's voice melted in with the sounds of the night and he dropped his head slowly.” Even now it was tough to say such a thing to this man in front of him.” They both knew though. Payne nodded 'No one has pushed me like you Roberto, no one has even come close, not Jason, Not Loki, not even Jeff Wylde, yet i can leave that in the past, we got our answer in DWA and i can live with that, knowing that....' he trailed off then raised his eyes and fixed Knight with a fiery stare 'Knowing that you are the better man' Payne took a step back 'Once I extended my hand to you at this very same spot...but then it was business' He extended his hand again to Knight 'This time...for closure' Knight doesn't hesitate and takes the hand, shaking it firmly.”” 'And Alex...' Knight grins 'If you ever do decide to come back, GCW will be waiting ... I will be waiting.” I think I can honestly say that a spot right next to me will be open.” Together we can do amazing things.” Think about it.' Payne laughed as he stepped back and shook his head 'I’m too old for this shit Roberto' with that he turned away and walked into the night his voice drifting after him 'Take care of your heart you stubborn bastard! The likes of you and I aren't meant for such endings, if you die of a heart attack....I'll take it personally!' and with that Payne was gone Knight turns back towards the grave and looked down at the white rose.” His boot came down on top of it.” Alex had been through alot and so did Roberto and perhaps thats why they understood each other to the extent that they did.” Those two had a respect for each other unmatched by anyone.” For years they wrote the book on violence and hatred.”” 'All stories have an ending...' Knight chuckled and walked away as well.” That part of his life was closed.” He hoped he did in fact see Alex again one day and not at his funeral.” Perhaps at his wedding.” LeStatt Knight and Payne.”” 'What a pair we make.' And finally we come to ? THE END
