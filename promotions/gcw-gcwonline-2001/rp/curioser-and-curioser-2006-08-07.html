Curioser and Curioser

7 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-07-06 Sliding her long legs out of the passenger side door, Dawn cast a skeptical look at the woman who came out of the drivers side. “I still can't believe I let you talk me into this.” It would have been obvious to anyone who knew Dawn what she was talking about. The Steel rose was clad in an “outfit” that was terribly out of character for her. Knee high black boots laced up to her knees, leaving a long expanse of pale thigh that ran up into a scandalously short skirt that was slit up both sides. A belly shirt with a sweeping v-neck and long sleeves covered very little of her torso, and a familiar black leather collar with silver plating completed the look. Dressed similarly, Jane Lancaster grinned slyly over the roof of the taxi at Dawn. “I told you that it was necessary. My contact is going to react much more favorably to you if you're dressed like that.” Looking down at herself, Dawn shook her head slowly. “I'm not sure that's a good thing anymore.” Objections aside, Dawn let herself be pulled through the front doors and into the heavily thumping music of Washington's “Arcology” night club. Heading immediately to the bar she plopped down onto the stool, signaling the heavily tatoo'd bartender. “What'll it be toots?” “Water please.” When he looked at her skeptically she shrugged, “I'm on the wagon.” Reaching below the bar he produced a bottle of water without enthusiasm and thumped it onto the table. Breaking the seal, Dawn took a pull from it before turning to Jane. “So how long are we going to have to wait for this contact of yours?” “Not long at all.” Jane was smiling ear to ear as she pushed away from the bar. “I see him now.” Jake walked into the club, looking around for the woman he hadn’t seen in years. The hair catches his attention first, long red hair that seemed to shimmer in waves as it ran down past her shoulders. Even now it could cause him to pause as he took in her slender form and sharp emerald eyes. She saw him too, the same powerful stature she had known from years before. His own cool blue eyes weren’t as inviting anymore, as though time itself had hardened him even more. Jake walked up slowly, the black dress clinging to her body like an subtle invitation. They embraced, a gentle, familiar kiss on the cheek as she held him a moment too long. Jane stepped back with a smile, only a hair less dazzling then it had been moments ago. She had felt the tenseness in his frame, felt the distance in his embrace. Laying her fingers on one arm she tugged him lightly towards the bar. 'It's been too long Jake...' She paused in the familiar greeting, wondering if he'd rather she get down to business. '...I've brought someone I think you should meet.: Jake nodded without smiling, 'Life got hectic, what can I say? You said this was a matter of some urgency Jane. I've had’well, I've had a tough week. Who is it you're introducing me to?' 'Well, she came to me via an old contact of mine with a rather...unique problem. I thought of you first thing and so I brought her as soon as I could.' She pulled him gently to the bar by one arm, gesturing to a lengthy expanse of pale leg that vanished up into a skirt that was almost a glorified belt. The woman in the skirt turned, black-blonde hair flailing in a wave past her shoulders. Emerald green eyes widened in surprise and disbelief, the same expression mirrored across her pretty face. 'Dawn McFarlane, this is Jake...' '...Hix.' Dawn finished the sentence for her, still staring in disbelief. Jane looked back and forth between the two of them. 'You know each other?' Jake’s icy demeanor was broken when he couldn’t resist playing with these two lovely women. “We’ve met in many different ways actually Jane. I didn’t know you were keeping such lovely company these days.” Stepping forward, he extended his massive hand to her, it had been over eight months since Japan. “How have you been Dawn?” Shaking her head to clear the shock, Dawn placed one hand in his, squeezing it gently. 'I've been better, but hopefully things will improve.' She turned to Jane, 'He doesn't know me in the way he'd like you think he does.' Jake was disappointed she had ruined the joke on Jane, but he understood why. He smiled, his face finally exposing some little joy. He looked over at Jane, “We’ve been in the octagon together as opponents. She’s a professional you know.” He let the idea hang there for a moment as payback for his ruined joke, “I heard she was even champion for a while. We ran into each other in Japan last year, and enjoyed a lovely dinner though.” Dawn looked sour, like she'd bitten into something that didn't appeal to her. 'Yeah well I wasn't a champion for any meaningful amount of time. Dinner was nice though...wanna sit?' She gestured to the stool next to her, getting tired of half shouting over the pounding music. He took a quick look at the stool, satisfied by the steel construction that it could support his massive frame. He sat down, unbuttoning his suit jacket as he did. He looked across, giving Dawn a look up and down that was lingering in a way he probably shouldn’t have been. Once, he had wondered what it might be like to nestle up against her form, but that had easily become a dead end the more he got to know her, but hope as always springs eternal. His voice was more comfortable now that he knew with whom he would be meeting, “Any amount of time as champion is still an accomplishment to be proud of. Besides, I’ve always been a fan Dawn. I was happy for you from afar.” “Will you be joining us?” Jake looked over at Jane then back to Dawn. “Or is this little problem best kept between just the two of us Dawn?” 'Jane already knows all of the details that I know, so I don't see any problem with her staying.' She shrugged her muscular shoulders slightly. Jane grinned, settling in on the other side of Hix. 'You just want to be between two women Jake...' She purred happily, calling for drinks. “Circumstances dictate form for preferred function.” Jake looked over his shoulder at Jane. “Can you get me a beer, your choice.” She nodded as she went about getting the drinks. Jake stroked his goatee for a moment before speaking, “So, I was surprised to be called her for this type of affair in relation to you Dawn, but considering the company you keep I shouldn’t have been.” Noticing the storm clouds in her eyes he continued, “I just meant you aren’t new to this world, but somehow I never think of you as being within it. I am intrigued by the outfit though. Not what I remember you preferring.” At that she flushed, a bright crimson creeping up her neck into her cheeks. 'It's not, I didn't even own clothes like this before tonight. Jane assured me that it would smooth the way with her 'Contact' this evening if I dressed a little provocatively.' “Not what I’ve come to expect from you.” Jake reached his hand out toward her neck, “Except maybe the collar.” She swatted his hand deftly, leaning slightly away at the same time. 'It's not something I'd normally wear. If I'd known it was you I'd have dressed more like myself.' She smiled to take any potential sting out of her words. “Are you saying you wouldn’t dress up for me?” He looked almost hurt as he made a sad face. “I’m disappointed by that.” She made a face, half sticking her tongue out. 'I don't dress up for anyone outside of Lia Jake, I'm sure you know that.' Rebuked but undeterred, he looked out over the crowd around the club. The location was busy, lots of foot traffic and plenty of noise to prevent anyone from overhearing them. Of course, it was lucky for him that it turned out to be Dawn because anyone paying attention might just think this was him meeting an old friend. They wouldn’t know about the real reasons for the meeting. “So Jane, this problem isn’t something you can handle?” Jake didn’t look at her as he spoke; rather he carefully studied Dawn’s reactions. “It must be something very special then. I guess that fits Dawn to a tee.” Dawn made another sour face, turning away to signal the bartender for a drink. 'It's not something I've ever encountered before.' Jane admitted, turning her beer over in her hands, 'She was referred to me by someone a little closer to her. She's ah...well...' Jane leaned in conspiratorially, not wanting either Dawn or any passersby to hear. 'I think she's a half-breed.' Jake maintained the intimate presence with Jane, enjoying the intoxicating scent of her perfume, “That covers a lot of territory in our little corner of the world Jane. Is it speculation, is there evidence, and if so do we know what breed we could be dealing with?” He reached out with the power inside of him evaluating her carefully. There was something there, but he couldn’t identify it right away. 'That's part of the problem.' She continued to whisper, nestling in against him. 'I'm not certain and neither was my contact. The good news is I don't think it's anything that's going to be a *huge* problem. No Elder gods or anything like that. The bad news? I think it's Daemonic.' He picked his beer up from the bar. Jake took a sip, reaching out again with his inner senses while looking like a guy trying to pick up two women to everyone else in the bar. Realizing the provocative dress of his two friends, he might be appearing to negotiating the price. There was something there under the surface, but it kept getting obscured somehow. Whatever was happening here, it had changed since their last meeting. He hadn’t been trying to figure her out as much then, but there were changes underway even now. “I can’t sense that, and I would know.” Jake said plainly with confidence. “There is something happening to right now though Dawn, even as we sit here.” Jane continued touching him, “How could you tell that?” “I’m not the same anymore either Jane.” Jake took a long pull from the bottle. 'What do you mean? You seem the same old 'Big Dawg' to me...' She trailed one finger down his left bicep. 'Besides, she claims to have met her mother, and if the description holds up then she was definitely not human.' He enjoyed the lingering touch. He had denied himself more often than not because anyone he embraced had a nasty habit of being murdered lately. He kept his eyes glued on Dawn, Looks can be deceiving. You should know that better than anyone Jane. Meeting your mother and thinking she’s something unusual isn’t new. Knowing whether she’s from the divine or the damned is trickier because even the divine have been cast out many times.” “I need to know things Dawn.” Jakes leaned in, looking her straight in the eyes without flinching like he always did. The purposefulness could be overwhelming, “Tell me about her, your mother.” Startled at being suddenly included after having been so obviously left out of the conversation, it took Dawn a moment to collect herself. 'Ah she was...tall, taller then I am. Very, very long black down to her waist I think. Pale skin...' She was starting on the minor details, building up to the ones that her mind still had problems coping with. '...and wings. Giant, leathery wings, like a bat or something...and a tail I think.' She blanched at the thought, tossing back a pull of her drink. “What color were her nails or claws” Did she have a human mouth or something else? What about her ears, if she had them.” Jake didn’t turn away. “Tell me about the circumstances, the smell of her, the clothes she might have worn, anything she said or didn’t say. I need to know everything about that moment.” Jake reached out, resting his hand over hers as she held onto her drink, “I know how tough this can be, but the details provide more than you might realize. We have to look through the veil of time and legend to find what we’re looking for Dawn.” Her eyes flicked to his hand and then back to his face. For a moment she waited as though expecting him to remove it...and when he didn't she sighed. 'Red, her nails were cherry red. She looked totally human aside from what I told you, mouth, hair, ears everything. She didn't smell of anything in particular and she was wearing a modern corset and a long dress...' She paused as though remembering something that had been flickering in the back of her mind. 'She was...hot. Not beautiful, not the kind of woman you want to marry and have white picket fences with. The kind of woman you want to pull you into the supply closet at work and fuck you senseless.' He slowly removed his hand, “Was that a sense you had about her, or was that what you felt like doing when you met her?” Taking a more controlled sip of her drink, water as it turned out, she pursed her lips as she mulled it over. 'It was less what I wanted to do and more like what I wanted her to do to me. Like just her standing there was one of the sexiest things I've ever seen...' 'Didn't you say she was your mom?!' Jane asked in disbelief, drawing a sour look. 'I didn't say it was a pleasant feeling.' “Of course you didn’t say that Dawn.” Jake assured her. He finished his beer, and motioned for another from the bartender. He thought about what he knew, and the biggest gaps were what he didn’t know. “What were the circumstance of your meeting her?” 'That was one of the weirdest things about it actually...' She mused, turning the now empty glass over and over in her hands. '...I woke up out of a dead sound sleep with just this...feeling. Like I was being watched but wasn't being watched at the same time, and that I had to go to the back of the house. When I got to the kitchen she was just there, standing on the deck.' Jake took the information all in. He had an idea, the seductive element of her mother being one of the keys from one of his own lessons with Robert. He wouldn’t mention it now, since he couldn’t sense that for certain within her. There were too many unknowns to speculate, never knowing what such speculation could do to her. The second beer arrived, and Jake picked it up. He slowly sipped the beer as he wondered what else could be in play here. Jane had reached out to him because they had been friends, and knew they could rely on each other. Then again, having Dawn dress in this manner was another part of her game she always seemed to play with him. That was why they could never be as close as she wanted, and never for any extended period of time. She enjoyed her games too much. Her mother was only the first half of the equation though, “Dawn, what do you know about your father?” She stopped at that, placing the glass carefully down on the table and turning in her seat so she could look at him. 'I know that he was a great man. One of the best wrestlers this world has ever seen, and the best man I've ever known.' She said it fiercely, as though daring him to challenge her on it. “What’s your first memory of your father?” 'Ah...' She paused, thinking hard. 'I...think it was when he taught me how to ride my bike. That's the first really strong memory I have I think, of how close we were at that point.' “Really?” Jake took another sip of the beer. “I remember when I learned how to ride a bike too. I was probably four or five. What about before then, do you remember anything else?” 'Just...images really, feelings.' She shrugged, looking uncomfortable. 'I can remember the way his mustache tickled my face, or how big I thought his hands were. The color of his eyes and how bright his smile seemed.' Frowning, she turned the glass upside down. 'Not much other then that, but I was too young to remember much of anything.' “Our view of the past can become clouded by time.” Jake leaned back, letting Jane move her arm over his shoulder. “So, what else can you tell me about your father Dawn” Just in general, tell me about him.” 'You knew him too didn't you? You told me that you were in the same venue's sometimes.' She watched him carefully, her expression guarded. 'Why all the questions?' “If you asked me about my brother, I could easily say you’ve been in an arena with him Dawn. You’ve seen him the back, you’ve talked to him and maybe even you’ve had a bite to eat at the same table in back of a show.” Jake explained. “I could say all of that, and I haven’t told you anything about my brother. Of course, if I’m a private person I don’t want to tell you anything. I use double talk, speak without answering questions and turn the question back around on the questioner. Those tactics are simple, time honored and here they don’t do anything for anyone.” Jake looked over his shoulder at Jane, “I could tell you things about Jane, intimate things that might bring the color of her face to match the color of her hair. I could give details that should make all three of us blush, but what I’m telling you is the story of a man who has laid down with her. I’m telling you the truth from a lover’s perspective, not from a sibling’s, a child’s or a father’s point of view.” “The eyes that look upon a thing always judge that thing. Conscious or not, prosecutors hate eye witnesses because we can convince ourselves of almost anything, and then be easily persuaded of just the opposite. Even so, I need to witness your father through your eyes. Through the eyes of a loving daughter.” For a long moment she said nothing, staring through him with eyes the color of liquid jade. When she finally focused on him the skin around those eyes had tightened, as though straining against something. 'He was a great man Jake, kind and loving and loyal. Once you earned my father’s love or his respect you had a friend and ally that wouldn't abandon you no matter how rough the storm. To his enemies he was a terror, a giant bear that could break you in half just by thinking really hard about it. He loved to wrestle, it brought out some of the best in him and the whole world knew it, especially when he was champion.' She turned her head away, hair falling to hide her face. 'I've been to the bottom of the barrel Jake. I've used and abused, I've been...raped in my own home, I've been used up and tossed aside like an old dishrag.' She looked back, eyes bright with tears that threatened to spill. 'But there were all pin pricks compared to the pain I felt the day my father died. I loved my father more then anything in this world or the next Jake, that's what kind of man he was.' “I meant no offense Dawn, and I don’t understand why you would take my simple question so’sharply.” The puzzle wasn’t even close to being figured out, but whatever this was he had hit a nerve. “Why are you lashing back at me for my question” If you think this is tough, then finding out the truth may not be what you really want to do Dawn. The truth can be much more dangerous than anything else.” 'What do you want me to say?!' Tears spilled down her cheeks now and she scrubbed frustratedly at them. 'I don't understand what you're asking me. My father was a great man and a good father. He wasn't perfect and he spent a lot of time on the road, but after my mother left he was all my sister and I had.' “I didn’t know you had a sister.” Jake’s mind exploded with the information. A sister opened up all new doorways and possibilities. If Dawn were a half-breed, then the sister could be one too. “Is she older or younger?” 'Younger. She and I haven’t spoken for a couple of years, we don't see eye to eye on a lot of things...' “You’ve met my family, that one doesn’t surprise me at all Dawn.” Jake laughed. “Family doesn’t always seem eye to eye. What’s her name’and is she as lovely as you are?” She had the grace to blush again. 'Katelyn, and she looks very much like I do. More red to her hair and a little shorter, but otherwise very much like myself.' “Lovely I’m sure.” Jake set his beer down, “Where did you spend your childhood” I need to know to see if I can find any trace of your mother.” 'Mostly around The Estate and Toronto, although we traveled sometimes when my father was on the road. I think we might have started out in Suburbia in Toronto, but we moved to the estate before too long.' “After your mother left?” 'We traveled with my Father a lot after that. Downtime was spent at the Estate, but I wanted to be a wrestler like him so we traveled a lot.' She frowned idly, picking at the cherry stem in her drink. “He kept you close by after she left, didn’t he” Even when traveling, you were very well cared for I bet.” She nodded, twirling the cherry about between her thumb and finger. 'Yeah, he took a lot of time off wrestling to be with us.' He felt Jane’s nails against the sensitive flesh of his neck, but he ignored it. “Well, with such a drastic change in your life I’m sure your father wanted to protect you emotionally from any other harm. Why do you think you spend so much time with Lia?” She blinked at him for a moment, obviously not entirely understanding the question. 'Because I love her.' 'Love develops over time, what I mean is why were you attracted to her? What drew you in to spend all the time with her?' Jake realized how sensitive this could be, so he backed up a little. 'I'm not judging you, Lia or your lifestyle. I mean, I'd be the male version of a slut in many people's eyes, so the fact you're in a stable long-term relationship is great.' He hoped that might calm the waters, 'But the imprints of our experiences are always revealed in our choices. So I'm just trying to understand something of where you were when you met Lia. None of this is easy, but the signposts of our past are all over our present. Sounds hinky, right? But trust me, there's all sorts of things to be revealed.' Nodding in understanding, Dawn pursed her lips and mulled it over, working the thought over in her mind. 'Well...when I first met Lia we were rivals, basically hated each other. In fact she...replaced me when Corvus left me out in the cold like a bad dog. When I came to Global I guess you could say I was sort of damaged goods, I'd been through a lot of bad things with men and I didn't want anything to do with anybody. Lia was just...there I guess. Once I shed the mask and joined Hunting Grounds she came to take care of me, to look over me and make sure I wasn't pushing too hard. I don't really know when the exact moment was I fell in love...I just kind of did.' 'That's the way love should be.' Jake was connecting dots in his mind's eye, seeing the embedded patterns like familiar chords of a song. 'So, you don't have any idea of what else could be happening? What made you seek out help?' 'Something must be occurring that's sent you out into the cold night, and since you're at a club with me talking about this, and not in a warm bed with Lia then there's something scaring you.' He saw more emotions play across her lovely face, 'And I bet that's something you don't want Lia to know about. What brought on the sudden concern Dawn?' She broke eye contact at that, looking to the side, at her feet, anywhere but Jake's piercing eyes. 'When I was in Japan I met someone claiming to be Lillith...' She looked back briefly, eyes sliding past his face. 'Yes that Lillith. She...she said I was hers, that I was just like her that. That Lia doesn't really love me for me, but she loves me because I can't help it, that it's my nature that makes people want me. She told me that someday...I would betray my friends, my family and the woman I love and there was nothing I could do to stop it.' Dawn pressed her hands to her face, nails digging into her forehead. 'Lia and my mot...Roshael know what she said, and they both said she's basically full of it...but they both got very quiet when I mentioned the part about people wanting me because of my nature, not because of me.' 'You met Lillith? Well, there's all sorts of the cast out still walking the earth, so I learned several years ago not to dismiss anything.' Jake thought about how this played into the sequence of events. 'So they both decided not to elaborate on anything when you mentioned it?' 'Lia just looked kind of thoughtful and Roshael didn't really say anything at all.' She sighed, scrubbing at her face with her hands. 'So I have to know. Not just to try and prevent anything like that, but...to just know! I have to know who, or what, I am.' She looked back up, eyes pleading. 'And you can't go to them for the answers.' Jake picked his beer up once again and drained it dry. 'We can help, at least as much as we can. I have suspicions, but suspicions without facts yet. I need to do some research and get back to you. I will also need something from you, something that may seem a little strange, but I'm sure it's not something you haven't been asked for by some others.' Dawn cocked her head to one side, her hair cascading down past her shoulder. 'What do you need from me?' 'As it turns out, I need your blood.' She nodded, as though the request didn't seem out of place at all. 'I kind of figured that might happen sooner or later. I don't have any syringes or anything with me though, or a container or anything like that.' 'I have something in the car with me, Boy Scout and all.' Jake smiled. 'Perhaps we can do that and then go our separate ways for the moment. I know this can't have been comfortable for you, and I don't want to take any unnecessary risks since we could both be recognized. Rumors could be started from a meeting as simple as this.' I parked with the valet, we can take a short ride down the road to someplace more private.' 'Alright sure.' Dropping a bill on the table Dawn followed both of them out into the summer air, studiously ignoring any lingering looks at her leggy display. They walked out into the cool evening air. Jake handed his ticket to the valet, and he waited with a woman on each arm for the car to be brought around. The rental agency had provided a 760Li Sedan for the evening. He took asked them to open the trunk, and took a black duffel bag out of the back before joining the ladies inside the plush interior. They were quiet as they drove down the road, finding a secluded spot at the uppermost level of an almost empty parking garage. Jake looked all around before pulling out the syringe and vial. 'I haven't done this in a long time, but I'm thankful you seem to be a skinny thing with prominent veins.' He smiled, trying to relieve some of the tension. Jake had learned to do this after a fight many years before when a horrible medic in Mexico was screwing up Xavier's arm. Finding the vein, he put syringe in his hand and looked at her, 'Are you ready?' Nodding she turned her head to one side, looking away from her arm. He pulled it out, and did it once again until he had two full vials. 'All done, no real harm.' Jake smiled pleasantly, 'Now, where can I drop you ladies off at, or maybe where can I drop you off Dawn? Jane and I have some’catching up to do.' Something passed across Dawn's face, a moment of temptation perhaps...although it seemed to be something closer to need. 'Ah...just my hotel is fine. I'm sure everyone's wondering where I am by now.' 'As always, whatever you might want Dawn, anything I can do just call.' Jake saw the hesitation, and enjoyed it. 'Thanks Jake...really, thank you.' She leaned back against the seat. 'Jane's got my cellphone number, you'll contact me when you know something right?' 'You'll be the first.” ******************************************************** Opening the door a crack, Dawn was relieved to find that all the lights in the penthouse Dies Irae had rented were off, meaning everyone else was either asleep or out. Slipping through as little space as possible she eased the door shut behind her, leaning against it to take a quick breath before bending down to start on the laces of her new (and terribly impractical) boots. Of course, there was one person who was probably guaranteed to be awake until everyone was safely back. Her voice piped up from the darkness. 'Dawn? Is that you?' Of course, it was a moot question, because she knew it was Dawn, could scent that it was Dawn, but it was a conversation starter in any case. Dawn froze in the dark, squeezing her eyes shut and mouthing a foul curse. '...yeah it's me Lia...sorry if I disturbed you.' There was a soft sigh from the darkness. 'No, I've been awake. Just trying to make sure everyone got in okay.' Dawn straightened up, peering into the dark for any visual cue of her leader and lover. 'Sorry I'm late, I didn't mean to worry you.' Truth was she had been hoping not to get caught, but it was a moot point now. Shadow flipped her hair over her shoulders and stood up from the couch, stretching. 'I wasn't worried, I'm just not taking any chances.' There was something more about Dawn tonight too, something slightly heady. A strange, intoxicating scent that wafted along on the freon-scented air conditioned wind. 'Have a nice night?' she asked Dawn casually, trying to maintain as guarded a tone as possible. If Dawn didn't want to tell her about it, she wasn't going to press for information. Shaking her head with a sigh, Dawn took a few hesitant steps forward, trying to use the sound of Shadow's voice as her guide. 'Not really. I...needed to get out, but I think it might end up being a waste of time.' 'Hm... why's that?' Her voice was soft, and sounded a little far away. Perhaps she was a bit more tired than she was letting on, she thought, and stifled a yawn. Dawn mulled over her answer as she picked her way through the dark, biting off a stifled curse when she banged her shin on a table. 'I'm just not sure I found what I was looking for.' It was cryptic she knew, but there was a twinge inside her whenever she considered bald faced lying to Lia. 'No it's not something I think I could ever get used to wearing either...not in public anyway.' She winked slyly. The wryness turned to a look of amusement before it played off of her features entirely. Something was slightly different about Dawn tonight, and she wasn't quite sure she could put her finger on what it was. She was nothing if not doggedly determined, however. It wasn't that she didn't trust Lia, and it wasn't that she didn't want to tell her...it was just there was this nagging underlay of fear. 'I'm...okay.
