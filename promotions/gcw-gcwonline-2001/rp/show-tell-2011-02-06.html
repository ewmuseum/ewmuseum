Date: 02/06/11 [The scene fades up to a man hitting a heavy bag. He is sweating like a beast from all the hitting against the heavy bag. He has white boxing shorts on and black boxing gloves and white tape on his wrists and black shoes on his feet. In fact, it is Danny Francisco, known to the wrestling world only as Dynasty or Buffalo’s Bad Boy or the Prodigy or New York’s Finest. Danny is killing the bag. He hits the bag with a monster right, then another, and another, and another, and another, and then another. The bag is imprinted with glove marks when he is done killing it. Dynasty stops and takes a breather. He is winded from destroying the bag. He looks at the camera. Kevin Jacobs walks in the room. Danny looks from the camera, over to Jacobs, back at the camera, then at Jacobs, then at the heavy bag, then at the camera, then over to Jacobs. He has two chicks in bikinis there to untape his gloves. He sits in a fold out chair, and grabs a towel, and a bottle of water. Kevin Jacobs walks over to the young upstart, and sits in a chair opposite him. He smiles at Danny. Danny's pecks are sticking out as usual, and so are his 22 inch biceps. He is still catching his breath. Danny looks up at Jacobs before Jacobs looks at him.] Jacobs: “Ready for the interview, Dynasty?” [Dynasty stands up, and gets his gloves redone by the chicks who are now naked. Dynasty walks over to the ring after they grind on him like two crazy sluts. He gets in the ring. Just beyond the ropes and canvas, a spotlight turns on overhead the classic legends Survivor as they begin to play “Eye of the Tiger”. It is loud. A giant man steps in the ring, and he towers over Dynasty, who is not afraid. Dynasty begins to dance around, and a bell rings, but he keeps dancing and doing the Shopping Cart. The two men tap gloves. Jacobs gets up, and walks over to the ring. He looks at the match that is beginning before his eyes and he is afraid. He watches as the two men exchange blows before Dynasty begins to rattle away at the man's mid section. Dynasty knocks the wind out of the man, and then hits him with another right to the stomach. The man is limp, being beaten by Dynasty. The man is nearly unconscious. Dynasty does not stop. He beats the man with his boxing gloves. He insists on not stopping and just continues to rattle away on the man. The man is unconscious. Dynasty will not stop. Right, right, right, right to the midsection. The man falls on Dynasty. Dynasty pushes him back. Buffalo’s Bad Boy climbs the ropes, and gives the 'DynaSwing DDT’' to the man. He puts his foot on the bottom rope and points at Jacobs with his boxing glove.] Dynasty: "Jacobs, you saw it yourself, and you've got it on tape. I just destroyed this dumb s[BEEEEEEEEEP]t… [Dynasty points back at the man]...within ten minutes. He is twice the size of Fujita and Wolfenden. I know he's not as bad as them, but it just shows how damn good I am. I will walk into the Boardwalk Hall, and tear it up. I am unstoppable. I am the king in the ring. I am the beast which has no boundary. I know it. You know it Jacobs. I will show you again if you want me to. I can win. I will win. I must win. No one has a clue of the power I bring when it matters this much, but after 145 they will. Everyone will." Jacobs: "Well Dynasty, I’ve got to get going… so many interviews to do. I need to - " Dynasty: "Go interview this guy’s mom and let her know that her son has been put into a coma and then tell Fujita and Karina, too! Let them all know. Tell them that I put bigger people in comas than them!" [Dynasty points to the door. Jacobs walks out. Trainers rush into the ring as Dynasty turns around. The scene fades to black.]
