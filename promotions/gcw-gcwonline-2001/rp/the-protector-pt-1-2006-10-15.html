The Protector Pt 1

15 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 15 2006 “Take a deep breath in?” Farida Okiko had been born the middle child of five other siblings. With the attention of her parents split between four other needy children she quickly realized that if she wanted anything, it would be something she, herself, worked to get. She graduated two years early from High School and when her parents divorced, tossing her brothers and sisters into a pissing contest for Mommy’s and Daddy’s affection, she immediately did all she could to bury herself in her work through Medical School. She received her degree, top of her class, and caught the first plain out of New York; heading as far away as she could. . “Ok, now let it out” She practiced medicine at the Saint Augustine Medical Center; one of the biggest hospitals this side of Arizona. It wasn’t the most exciting of jobs, in a not-so-exciting part of the United States. But, she enjoyed the stability of her life. She wasn’t vying for attention, wasn’t caught in a battle for her affection between her parents, and could finally start working on something that would benefit her, instead of just something that would help to get her away. “One more time, please” She asked as her thoughts snapped back to her patient. She held the tip of her stethoscope firmly against his bare back, listening for any abnormalities in his breathing. Her eyes trailed back down to a few faded bruises on the side of his ribs, visible from the side of the hospital gown, as she gently placed the palm of her hand over them. “HOLY JESUS!” bellowed the frantic voice of her patient The chart said his name Julio De Hoyas; Obviously of Hispanic origin. Illegal? Probably not, as he had a valid insurance card and spoke English better than most of her interns. It was evident to Farida that Julio had taken good care of himself, as she marveled at his incredibly healthy physique. Safe for the minor bruises to his side and a bump on the back of his head, his complexion was virtually flawless. He had a chiseled appearance, but not too bulky. The skin tone was one of a dark brown, bordering on the color of a very light caramel, while the few tattoos on his body were well placed, and had meaning. “I’m sorry” did I hurt you?” She snatched her hand away as quickly as her reflexes would allow as her deep brown eye stayed locked on the faded bruise adorning Julio’s side. She looked over to his shorter fatter friend, Brack, whose head had already shot up from his magazine; suddenly interested in the exam. “No, your hands” they’re freezing!” Julio whimpered to Dr. Okiko’s amusement. Brack settled down, burying his head back into his magazine as Farida rubbed her hands together trying to create some warm friction between them. “I’m sorry, Mr. De Hoyas,” she softly snickered with a flirtatious tone in her voice, “but I need to know how well your ribs are healing.” Julio nodded, raising his arms. “Well, there’s nothing seriously broken and from what I can tell, this being your first visit, it’s healing quite nicely” she said taking a second to run her fingertips across the lining of his ribs. ? I assume that you have been taking it easy for the past few weeks?” “Not really, I just change out between the back of my neck and my rib area on a regular basis.” “Is that so” She raised a thin well shaped eyebrow and scribbled something into Julio’s file. “What is it exactly that you do, Mr. De Hoyas?” “Oh, you know, a little of this” a little of that? and a whole lot of the in between.” He joked doing his best to be a discrete about his work as possible. “You mean you haven’t seen him on television?” Brack blurted out. His words were met with a stiff glare from Julio; evidently an indication that he should immediately cease and desist all conversation as he quickly dropped is nose back into his magazine. “It might not be my business but to properly treat you for any kind of wounds it helps if I at least now how they originated.” She pressed. “I fell down a few flights of stairs.” He smirked turning his head to greet her, only to realize she wasn’t buying his excuse. “I’m not much for elevators.” Farida was no stranger to injuries sustained in professional wrestling. With the size of her hospital and a wrestling promotion and arena just a few short mile from the hospital, she was quickly able to peace two and two together. “It’s a shame”, she thought softly to herself as her lower lip pushed out into a pout. She had, had such high hopes for this one. He was cute! But, he stood against everything she did. She had devoted herself to helping people, while he had devoted himself to injuring them; as well as himself in the process. Plus, she didn’t believe that there was a future in his kind of industry. Just forty-something year olds with failing health. “Well even with how well you are healing up to this point I would recommend that you steer clear of any sets of dangerous stairs for a while” Another scribble entered my file before she closed it, sliding her pen into a pocket in the breast pocket of her lab coat. “Though, I suspect that any recommendation put forth by me will enter in one ear and out the other.” “So I have a doctor’s clearance?” He wiped some hair from his face as he looked longingly at his cloths in a corner of the room. “And I can put my cloths back on?” “Yes, I still have a few more notes to put in your file but you can collect it from Renee at the front desk.” She made her way for the door, glancing back once more. “Just do yourself a favor and take it easy for a while.” Julio staid silent as the good Doctor made her exit from the room. “What was that about?” Brack inquired, closing the magazine and tossing it aside. “Pardon?” ?”You’re hands are cold?!”? he mocked with an ear to ear grin pasted on his face, collecting Julio’s things from the table. “There are subtle ways of being flirtatious” and that was not one of them!” “It was nothing.” Julio shrugged his shoulders as he glanced out the window. “It isn’t going to go anywhere.” “I know. You started something and then gave her the cold shoulder until she vacated the room.” “I don’t do relationships, Brack.” “Says who?” “Says me!” Brack tossed his superstar his cloths, eying Julio’s wallet as it hit the ground. “Well, some time off wouldn’t kill you, you know?” He shook his head and picked up the wallet with a sigh. “We’ve been busting hump sinces we got to GCW, you have a pretty decent cult-like following, and you placed extremely well in Dangerous Games. I just think it’s about time we took a breather.” “I’ve just gotten my feet in the ground here, Brack.” His eyes shifted as he took a moment to pull his pants up his thighs. “I don’t want to loose whatever steam I have behind me right now.” “You might loose all your steam if you get yourself hurt.” Brack muttered just under his superstar’s range of hearing. With a sigh Brack bent down retrieving Julio’s wallet from the ground. A picture slid down from his wallet as a sudden sadness fills the manager’s eyes. It was a family photo; Julio, his brother, his father, and his mother. An all but broken family in Julio’s eyes. “How long has it been, Julio; ten or nine years, right?” Brack handed the picture to Julio as the young superstar peered into his past. It was a happy family. Something he thought would never end. But, if life has taught the young man anything, it’s that time changes everything, and nothing lasts forever. “You are my family, Brack.” He pulled his shirt up over his body as he added, “Carlos made that clear when he gave me up.” “You father wasn’t the bad guy you think he was, kid.” Brack’s eyes grew livid as he peered up into Julio’s eyes. “I just wish you could understand what happened.” “It’s hard to understand something that you know so little about, Brack” he grabbed his wallet meeting Brack chest to chest. “How about you try letting me the whole story.” “You know I can’t, kid.” “Then keep your nose out of my personal life. I’ll say when I have had enough.” “Just don’t get yourself hurt, kid. You’re in their crosshairs now.” He stood silent as Julio grabbed his coat and left the room with Brack alone to ponder on his words. There was so much that Julio didn’t know about his past. So much that Brack needed a way to tell Julio... But how?
