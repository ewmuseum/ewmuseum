Ashes To Ashes

Date: One Afternoon ---Wyoming--- The wind was picking up at Jake Ennis? Wyoming Ranch. He sits on the front porch, looking out towards the land that he was no longer able to work on. His body, still broken from his final wrestling match, doesn’t allow him to even live a somewhat normal life. He looks on as a black pickup truck pulls onto his property. It’s been two months since he’s seen her. Every time she reached out for him, he pushed her away. He stopped taking calls from her two weeks ago. Jake Ennis didn’t want a reminder of what his life used to be. She will come to him, and he will push her away. Jake doesn’t want this life anymore. He’s become bitter and angry at the world. The truck pulls in, and the fiery Latino-Canadian opens the driver’s side door. “You best be turning around, Ara.” He doesn’t want this anymore. He doesn’t want her anymore. He wants his life to be back to how it used to be. Before wrestling. “Jake, I can’t let you do thi?” She doesn’t get all of her words out before he pulls the shotgun out from next to him. “Jake, I want to help you.” “Who said I need YOUR help?” “Jake, why are you pushing me away?” “Because, ever since I met you, I have felt nothing but pain. Ever since you came into my life, I have been someone other than myself. I want you off my property, and I want you off my property now. I will send you your things.” Araceli turns, the words hurt. She starts to cry as she walks back to the truck. ---Calgary--- As Araceli found herself losing the man she loves, Wyatt Colton was finally coming home. It’s been 2 weeks since he had seen Cammy. He wasn’t supposed to be back until after WorldWide, he wanted to surprise her. He was going to take her out to dinner and spend the night with her before flying back out the next morning. In his arm was a teddy bear. He places his suitcase on the ground and goes to open the door. He jiggles the knob, but the door is locked. Strange, he saw Cammy’s car in the parking garage. He pulls out his keys and unlocks the door. He opens it, walks in and looks around. Looks the same as when he last was here. Strange that Cammy would have locked the door if she was there. He walks into the bedroom and slowly opens the door. There she was, lying there in a tattered t-shirt and shorts, her hair sloppily done up. She must have been painting up on the roof. Wyatt quietly walks into the room, and gently lays his body next to hers. His arm wraps around her as he kisses just under the tattoo she has on the back of her neck. “You’re home early.” “I wanted to surprise you.” “I’m glad you’re here.” “Hopefully I don’t have to be gone this long again.” ---Minneapolis--- Dorian sleeps on the beat up sofa that was in the office. He was pulling in extra work as Wyatt went back home. Andrea had left to go get some lunch, this was the only chance that has to get a few minutes of sleep. The Pit is dark with the exception of the Calgary Flames neon sign that was illuminating the “Motor Pool”. Dorian likes it this way. Harder for him to sleep with the light. No Mercy Enterprises was starting to do well. Araceli and Andrea would take a look at more people to work on the muscle side of things so Dorian and Wyatt could worry bout the PR and getting the name out there. Dorian still looks forward to the days that he gets to go out and do some NME business. By bringing more people in, he knows that he’s going to see those days less and less. He knows though that he has to keep his investors happy. Otherwise, he will find himself like his brother. Being in the wrestling industry longer than he really wants to. This might just be his way out of the industry that spawned Dorian Ryan as a man that is able to put his name behind this company. The phone starts to ring. Dorian doesn’t want to wake up. He figures that eventually the phone will stop ringing and he can continue his nap. The sleep was good. The sleep was great. The phone does stop ringing. But that doesn’t mean that he’s going to get a respite, as Stone Sour’s “Come What(ever) May” starts to fill the office. Dorian rolls over, takes his cell phone off the coffee table, and hits ignore. He didn’t care to look to see who was on the other line. He just went back to sleep. ---Calgary--- Wyatt and Cammy cuddle together as they both sleep. She’s still in her t-shirt and shorts, Wyatt is now in a pair of sweats. The calm is interrupted as “All I Wanna Do” by Sugarland starts to play. Wyatt reaches his arm from around Cammy, rolls over and hits the end key on his cell phone. He was in a good place. He was in a happy place. He was with the woman that he loves. He figured all was right with the world.
