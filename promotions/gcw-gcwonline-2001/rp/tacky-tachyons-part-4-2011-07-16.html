Tacky-Tachyons Part 4

16 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/16/11 Last time on Tacky-Tachyons With a quick gesture ‘M’ lifts Bradley off his feet levitating him three feet off the ground. Brad resists but with other quick motion ‘M’ peels Bradley’s arms and head back, holding him in a pose that resembles a scarecrow being hung on a fence. Brad can no longer struggle against the Mysterious Being’s hold. M: "I’m excited; it’s like peeling back the pages of a much anticipated novel. I can’t wait just submerge myself into its contents. Reading away drawing closer to its climatic conclusion, all while fighting the urge to cheat and skip to the end to see what happens, but I assure you, I plan to take my time as I dissect your past and mind, so with that being said- ...let’s start with the beginning." Never being one for the unknown Bradley shuts his eyes as tightly as he can, in preparation of what is coming next, so tightly in fact that it felt as though his eyelids would fuse together like dough needing into itself. He just waits almost accepting his impending fate… He waits… and waits… and waits some more… and nothing happens… "Something should have happened by now, leave to some inconsiderate omnipresent bag of nothing to keep me waiting." Brad thinks to himself. Bradley waits some more but its getting to the point that he really just wants to get this over with. "You know, I’ve got half a mind to call the whole thing off, Pocket dimension and all. My feelings aren’t given any regard and there’s no consideration given to my precious time. Is that really so much to ask? I mean, after all I was the catalyst for this dimension’s creation, I should carry a lot more sway around these parts. This place literally wouldn’t exist without me. I’m like its god, its big bang, or maybe it’s Stan Lee & Jack Kirby all wrapped up into one. I’m important, far too important to be kept waiting. In fact I should say something, I mean it’s one thing to be pulled part and destroyed mentally, but it’s quite another thing (and rude I might add) to be kept waiting." Brad: "Hey, Doctor Manhattan, what’s taking so-" Bradley opens his eyes and choked on the rest of statement rendering him speechless. He rubs his eyes in hopes of correcting what he sees… nothing… nothing but pure unbridled darkness. He looks to his hands and doesn’t see anything. He begins to panic. Brad: "Am-Am I blind?" Brad’s question is met with a chuckle. Brad: "M? …is that you?" M:" Ha ha, of course, Sorry for the laugh, but I find it amusing how dependent you people have become to your basic senses. Disable just one and it completely compromises your ability navigate through the rest of your existence. A critical flaw if ever there was one." Brad: "Answer the question! Am I blind!?" M simply chuckles. M: "No, your eyes work just fine. There’s just nothing to see." Bradley breathes a sigh of relieve. Brad: "You could have given me some sort of warning." M: "I suppose I could have, but you can learn a lot based solely on panicked reactions alone." Bradley clenches his fist. Brad: "You’re just lucky I can’t see you." M: "Calm yourself, this all just a part of the process. In order to understand you I must know everything." Brad: "Why? So you can steal my body? Why do you want my body anyways and what’s stopping you? You’re this magical floating invisible indictment of atheism, so why go through all this? You could just take my damn body. Why do you even care what I’m like?" M: "I care because I’m curious. You’re a- dare I say –majestic species, and although in the grand scale of the universe you aren’t very big or threatening. Yet you stand up straight as an arrow defiant to all, even the natural laws of physics that are constantly pulling you back down, how noble. As for the rest… you’ll know soon enough." Suddenly with a loud "CLICK & CLACK" a bright yellowish light cuts through the darkness like a knife, actually rendering Bradley blind for a moment. He shields his eyes with his hand as his vision slowly adjusts. The blurriness fades and the images become sharper. He studies his surroundings. The source of the light is a simple bare bulb lamp with a downward facing shade that Brad lightly bats with his hand. It swings back and forth as it hangs a few inches in front and above him. The heat from the lamp can be felt heavily. Bradley looks down in front of him seeing a large wooden table as he realizes he’s now actually sitting... and in a chair. As soon as he takes notice of the chair the sensations the body would normally associate with sitting in a chair fade in slowly similar to way your senses return after waking from a sleeperhold. He seems to be in some sort of interrogation room. Bradley looks around trying to see walls, doors, or windows of some kind, but he sees nothing. Brad: "M, where are you?" The sound of a door opening and closing can be heard followed by the sound of footsteps. "CLOP-CLOP-CLOP-CLOP-CLOP" M finally steps his way into the light wearing his a pristine Italian cut white suit with a cavalier smile on his face. M: "You know Bradley, you act as though you don’t like me, and yet every time I give the illusion I’ve left you… you call out for me. Which makes me think JUST MAYBE you actually do like me… but I you're too shy to admit it… well maybe if I go first you’ll muster enough courage to do so yourself, so here we go. I like y-" Bradley suddenly lunges out of his seat jumping and sliding over the table landing on the other side of the table grabbing M by the collar of his suit. Brad pulls M close and with a twist Bradley’s hips slams M forcibly pining him down on the heavy wood table. M: "I’m not… eer …entirely sure… eer …but I think… eer …I’m experiencing that thing called pain." Bradley grips M’s suit harder as pulls him up slightly. Brad: "Yeah, it can be a bitch, so you better give me the Tachyon Emitter, or the pain shooting through you back is just the beginning." M: "Eer… you do know you could have just asked for it. I wouldn’t have given it to you, of course, but maybe my opinion of you wouldn’t have dropped so dramatically. Now unhand me before I…" Brad: "Before you what? Hit me? I’d like to see you try. I picked a prissy pampered Star Trek actor for this very reason. Physically I hold all the cards." M: "Clever boy, not only can this body not compete with you physically, but you also made me vow not to lay hand on you." Bradley cracks his trademark grin. Brad: "I’d be more than happy to void that verbal contract, if you like." M simply shakes his head with Bradley still gripping his collar. M: "No, an agreement is an agreement. I stand by my promises, but unfortunately… HE made no such agreement." M points slightly to the left of Brad, who refuses to fall for such trickery. Brad: "You really think I’m dumb enough to fall fo-" KA-POW! a large fist backed by tattooed freight train like arm completely blindsides Brad knocking him to the ground hard like a building crumbling to a wreaking ball. Brad grabs his head in pain and tries to compose himself. He checks is nose for blood, but is interrupted via large tattooed arms pulling him up. His attacker holds him up straight and Brad gets a good look at him seeing long weathered dreadlocks over lapping porcelain white painted face and a black drawn on smile. Brad is stunned. Brad: "Mayhem… Bryan?" M: "Yes, Bryan Mayhem… in the flesh more or less." Brad: "How in the hell? He can’t be real?" M gets to his feet. M: "Oh he’s as real as you made him. He’s a figment… and a burn seared into the mind, because at the end of the day some things are only as real as you make them." M walks to the other side of the table grabbing his back. M: "Speaking of real, I still really hurt… oof. That table is rough… Bryan, would you mind giving our friend here a little taste?" Bryan suddenly grabs Brad’s head driving into the wooden table bouncing him of the surface. Bradley lays there a moment dazed till he is pulled up by the 7ft nearly 400lb monster that has little to no trouble pulling Brad back to his feet. Brad stands there dazed. M: "I let my guard down just once. I allowed myself to be venerable briefly and you took advantage. That really hurts my feelings. But that’s your kind all over. Dog eat dog, so regrettable." Brad: "Heh, just a minute ago we were a noble breed. Honeymoon over already?" M simply gives a sharp nod and Bryan drives Brad’s head into the table a second time pulling him up just as quickly. M pleased with himself grins. M: "While your kind has had marvelous developments in its evolutionary progress in the past and still has so much potential for the future… yet, it has become stagnate and stifled. As whole you’ve become content with your current selves halting all biological development… clinging to remnants of biological dead ends such as the appendix, and a tribal need for similarity at the root of all prejudice." Brad: "Makes one wonder why you want to become one us so badly … if we’re so terrible." M shoots a look to Bryan who once again promptly drives Brad’s head into the table. Bryan pulls him again, but Brad is slumping slightly forward. Brad: "The way you described your people... was a endless sea of networked minds, emotions, and understanding. My people are individuals. Each man is an island. Separate thoughts, feelings, and even how we understand individual things. It’s all subjective. What you have with your kind sounds great… utopian even. Why would you reject that?" Bryan dives Brad’s head into the table once again. This Bradley defiant pulls himself up. Brad: "Did you hate them?" M: "What did you say?" Brad: "The rest of your kind… did you hate them?" Bryan drives Brad's head down again. Brad again defiantly lifts himself up once more. Brad: "Was it the lack of physical form? Was it knowing that you were just some random piece of nothingness just like the rest?" Bryan once again drives Brad's head into the table. Brad isn’t able to pull himself up but settles for tilting his head up to look M in the eyes. Brad: "… or maybe I have it all wrong. Maybe it’s not you rejecting them. Maybe they rejected you." M stares back at Brad sternly. Bryan pulls Brad to feet and drives fist across his face. Brad stumbles but catches and clings to the table to steady himself. Blood trickles down his nose. Bradley pulls himself up. Brad: "Is that what happened? Because I think that’s what happened. Especially if they knew you like I know you" M: "Hit him, AGAIN!" Bryan decks Bradley spinning him around slumping over the wooden table. Brad musters everything he has to pull himself up Brad: "I know you… I know your type… your just some sad excuse for an enlightened omnipotent being. You’re the equivalent of mistaking the wind for someone calling your name: a brief laughable annoyance that we quickly forget. …and that's not all you’re-" M is clearly heard enough wearing anger on his sleeve. M: "SHUT UP!" Mayhem spins Brad around to face him and drives his fist into Brad’s midsection doubling him over. He painfully is unable to draw another breath for a moment. After a while Brad is able to gasp for air. Brad: "You’re an opportunistic know it all inter-dimensional asshole jag-off with no fashion sense *chuckle* what kinda tool bag wears an all white suit, honestly?" Bryan drives his boot into Bradley’s ribs. He rolls around in pain until finally settling in the fetal position grasping his midsection. Mayhem pulls Brad to his feet by his hair. M just stares daggers at Brad as Bradley manages to mumble out. Brad: "Who could fucking want you?" M: "You think this all about my kind not wanting me? You think they rejected me? They loved me and I loved them… despite what your underdeveloped mind thinks. The reason I want to be human is because I’m the last of my kind, and it’s been that way for what seems like eons. My kind isn’t made to be alone. Not for long, but your kind is, and like you said "each man is an island"." M looks sad as for a moment and glances at the seemingly near lifeless Brad. M: "I’ll give you credit. You manage to hurt me, in more ways than one. I was going slow burn all this… talk you into accepting my plan, and have you come to terms and make peace with surrendering your body, but you’re too stubborn. This was meant to be painless and humane… but clearly you've shown your comfort deserves no consideration. Bryan if you would, please." Bryan tucks Brad's head between his legs and lifts high over seven feet in the air at its apex and drives Bradley down into the wooden table with a power-bomb that splinters into pieces. Bradley lays their slipping in and out of consciousness. Brad manages to crack open his now nearly swelled-over eye. He sees M and the figment of Mayhem standing over him. M kneels down closer to Brad with a smile from ear to ear. M: "You know what Bryan?" M looks back up to Bryan. M: "I think we have over did it, ha ha!" M looks back down at Brad. M: "Did the Prophet fall down? Bradley, Hello Bradley- Brad’s eyes close everything goes black. "Bradley… Bradley… Bradley… Bradley…" "MR. CRUZ!" A 13 year old boy with a brown head of hair shoots straight up to attention. He manages to pry his eyelids open revealing his sapphire blue eyes. He looks around seeing a class room full of children who giggle at his expense, a dry erase board with the words "Facts about California" and an attractive brunette teacher with that conservative Librarian look masking her true hotness. Bradley: "Yes, Miss Shultz?" Ms. Shultz: "Do you have the answer to the question?" Bradley looks down into the pages of his California History book, but instead sees the pages of the comic book"Bone" nestled in his textbook. Mrs. Shultz re-asks the question. Ms. Shultz: "Name at least one major California export." Bradley doesn’t know… he was asleep… and wasn’t paying attention. His mind races trying every avenue in his brain, but only finds dead ends… till he just finally blurts out. Bradley: "M-m-Movies?" Ms. Shultz nods approvingly. Ms. Shultz: "Hollywood is the world leader in Film and Entertainment, and in a close second in the world is Dollywood in India." Bradley suddenly feels lightheaded and dizzy. Little Bradley cups the back of his head. His teacher noticing expression slowly approaches Bradley. Ms. Shultz: "You don’t look so well." Little Brad teases back. Bradley: "That’s kinda rude…" She cups her hand over his forehead. Which Brad didn’t hate. Ms. Shultz: "You’re running a small fever. Maybe you should pack your things and head to the nurse." Bradley: "Yes, ma’am." Ms. Shultz gives him a reassuring smile. That puts butterflies Bradley’s stomach, or was that just the dizziness? She quickly writes him a note and hands him a homework sheet. Bradley packs his bag and waves goodbye to the class and his teacher as he walks out closing the door behind him. He walks down the hall slowly feeling even dizzier. As we walks passed a maintenance worker he stumbles and nearly falls but the maintenance man manages catch him. Maintenance man: "Whoa, there kid… you okay?" Bradley: "Yeah, I think so… I’m sorry…" Maintenance man: "That’s okay kid… where you headed?" Bradley: "Nurse, teacher sent me home." Bradley shows Maintenance Man his note. He looks it over seeing the boys name and the teachers signature and hands it back to the boy. Maintenance Man: "Well Bradley Cruz, your teacher must really care about you because she made a good call, you should head straight to the nurse." Bradley: "Yes, Mister Maintenance Man, sir." The Maintenance Man chuckles Maintenance Man: "I’m Maintenance Man Mortimer Moore…" Bradley: "That’s a lot of Ms" Maintenance Man: "Well that’s why they call me M" Bradly: "Do you ever watch Star Trek: The Next Generation? You sorta look like-" Mortimer Moore: "I don’t mean to be rude, but I think you should probably be headed to the nurse." Bradley nods and begins to walk down the hall headed for the nurse but as he leaves he shouts back. Bradley: "Bye Mr. Moore!" M: "No need to be so formal… call me Mr. M!" To be continued…
