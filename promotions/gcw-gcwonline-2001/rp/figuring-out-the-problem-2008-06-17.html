Figuring out the Problem

17 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/17/08 As I sat on my couch morosely staring at my TV, I prayed that Erika would choose to stay in her apartment for the day and allow me to mope about my disappointing loss in the Battle Royal in peace. I had resigned myself to a relationship with her, but she refused to accept some of my personality quirks. In fact, not only did she refuse to accept them she actively tried to change them. I was under no delusions as to why she had decided to enter into a relationship with me. She had a kid on the way, and wanted a second income as well as a father figure to be there. But the question in my mind was why I chose to stay in a relationship that had quickly proved to be more trouble than it was worth. I cringed as the phone rang, and I tried to ignore its demand to answer. I had a very simple plan for my day, I was going to watch bad reality television whilst eating substances that could barely pass for food, and hell itself would not be able to stop me. “Dan pick up the phone,” Erika’s voice rang out from the middle of the telephone. I visibly winced and foolishly tried to hide myself from the answering machine’s sight. “Dan I know you’re there, come on sulking by yourself is just not healthy.” "Yes it is," I thought to myself crossly, "at the very least my ears were not bleeding until I head your voice." “Well I am not telling you spend your entire day crying over losing one silly match,” she continued, and I began to clench my fist so hard that my knuckles turned white. How could I even begin to explain that “losing one silly match” may have sealed the rest of my career? That as far as I could see I was cursed to be nothing more than some hired muscle, and in twenty years I would probably known as the guy that Bagwell ordered around. “and I am coming over right now!” I heard Erika finish her tirade, and my face went pale. I simply did not feel the inclination to face her right now, so I chose the only option available to me. I immediately left my apartment without any idea of where to go. I walked the streets of New York for a few hours without looking where I was going, which was admittedly not the best idea. I was partially looking for a miracle, and partially hoping to be run over by a car and taken out of my misery. In hindsight, I would say that a mix of the two happened. I could only see one person who could even begin to provide me with any sort of comfort and understanding. I have looked up to him ever since I was a kid, his name is Sean and he is my brother. At one in the afternoon I boarded a train to New Jersey, eager to see my brother face to face and hear his wisdom. Sean’s House “First of all stop thinking with your dick,” my brother informed me bluntly as I contentedly sipped hot cocoa in his living. My brother lived in a fairly spacious three floor house, which house him, his wife, and their son Benjamin. Luckily his wife was taking my nephew out for some sort of school event so I did not have to talk with them. Do not misunderstand me, I happen to love Benjamin. The kid is already growing like a weed, and I have complete faith that if the kid decided to go into wrestling he could take on anyone in GCW. Unfortunately I am considerably less enthusiastic about his mother’though if anybody asks I completely see her as part of the family and not the source of uncomfortable silences. “Let’s look at the facts here Danny,” he continued, “you hadn’t gotten laid in at least a year if not more. Then out of the blue comes this chick who you say is incredibly hot, and within five minutes she’s giving you a blow job. Now I don’t blame you for sleeping with her, because I am sure that feeling something other than your hand on your lower brain melted your upper brain, and you forgot that she was pregnant. But in the morning she should have been kicked out of the door. Fuck Dan, you can’t take care of a kid!” I sat silently for some time unable to make a logical defense to his accusations. I wanted to say that it was not in my nature to kick a pregnant woman out on the curb, but I had a sinking feeling that such a defense would be quickly knocked down. To buy some time I sipped my drink while I desperately tried to think of something logical to say. With a sigh my brother finally relented and sat down next to me. “Alright” he said as he threw a comforting arm around my shoulder, “we’ll deal with that mess later. Let’s focus on something slightly easier to fix. That being that as a wrestler you suck.” I sputtered in outrage. If there was one thing I was sure of, it was that as a wrestler I was at the very least above average. I opened my mouth to list all of my in-ring accomplishments but my brother raised a hand indicating that I should shut up and listen to him. My mouth closed with a snap. “Save it Dan,” he said tiredly as he sank back into the couch. “Face it, you’ve always relied on your strength and conditioning to pull you through. You know enough basic techniques to be able to pound you’re opponents to a pulp, but the second you run into something you can’t power out of you’re done. That’s why a momentary distraction made you lose against Clyde Fox, and that’s why a woman who is half your size was able to throw you over the top rope.” “So what are you saying,” I responded angrily, “that I need to go to wrestling summer camp?” “No,” he replied calmly, “I am saying that you need to learn that your overly developed muscles aren’t always the solution to whatever problem you’re facing.” Angry, frustrated, and just a bit scared that he was right I stood up and went back home without a word. Back home I returned at eight at night to find Erika reading a magazine on my bed. “Where the hell have you been?” she screeched as she angrily stood up and advanced towards me. “I’ve been here since ten, and I know that you were still in the apartment when I called you. Really Dan, what in the hell were you thinking making me wait here” I stopped hearing her as a red haze invaded my vision. It would be so easy to shut her up by simply planting a fist into her screaming face. I felt my hands become fists, felt the muscles in my arms clench, and then I abruptly turned around and walked out of my home. I made it all the way down the hall before I lost control and began to punch the walls with as much force that I could muster. The only thing that saved me from a broken fist was the fact that the walls were padded due to some recent construction, but by the time my anger had drained my knuckles were already bruised. As I stared at them in consternation my brothers voice went through my mind. "You need to learn that your overly developed muscles aren’t always the solution to whatever problem you’re facing." I knew that if I had an ounce of less self control I would have hit, possibly killed Erika. It was clear to me that I had to learn to think, to act in a way that was not centered on the idea that I could just force my way through every problem. Unfortunately I had no idea where to start changing that particular mindset. I shrugged as I stood up and went to buy some ice for my battered hands. I didn’t have the solution, but for once I knew exactly what the problem was. That was a drastic improvement.
