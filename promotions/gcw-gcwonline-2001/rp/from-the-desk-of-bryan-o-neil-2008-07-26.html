From the Desk of Bryan O'Neil

26 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: july 26, 2008 What is hell? Is hell some sort of place of fire and brimstone that man made up to make sure we follow all of God’s rules and commandments? You fall and burn eternally at the hands of The Serpent King, where the pain is excruciating and the damnation is forever. In man’s version of hell there is no joy, there is no salvation, only fire, rot and decay. Man’s version we all rest with no hope of laying in a cloud and playing a harp with other cherubs. There is only pain. A pain so intense you ask God for forgiveness for all the evil deeds and mortal sins you have committed. In man’s version of hell, there is no redemption. Just sit back and imagine that, imagine that if the Hell man created really did exist. A wife and two children. You work for a mortgage company is Hermosa Beach, California. You have nothing to worry about except for the aggravation of a few rampant punk rockers doing what punk rockers do. Your oldest child is in 5th grade, showing signs of genius. High honors, and one hell of a Volleyball player. Your youngest knows she can never match the wits and athletics of her older sister, but she does not care. She’s a diva, she changes her clothes 3 times a day and often sneaks into her mother’s purse and makes a valiant attempt at trying on some of her makeup. She’s going to be the next Hannah Montana, and you and your wife know it. Anyways, you go out with the guys tonight to celebrate a friend’s promotion. You hit some bars and the booze begins to flow. You just keep downing them left and right. You are having the time of your life, there’s nothing better’well except for the warm feeling of being inside a beautiful woman, but right now you are the king. You and your friends discuss marriage, sex, the economy, sports, and anything else that rears its head into the life that is the party. It’s getting late, you have to be up early tomorrow. This may be a party, but life still exists at 6AM when that annoying buzzer wakes you up. You tell your friend congratulations one final time and you head for the door. You know for a fact that you should not be driving, but the last thing you want to do is call your wife and tell her your are drunk and come pick you up. If you do that, the pussy stops, and a week of sleeping on the couch begins. Besides, you only live 15 minutes away, what could possibly happen? A truck is what happens. You swerve into it’s lane and blast it head on, killing the driver instantly. Thou Shalt Not Kill. You made a cardinal sin. According the Good Book, you are doomed to an afterlife of hell. Did you really deserve it? You lived a great life, you never raised your hand at another. You just made a dumb mistake. You did not kill that person for the bloodlust. You were dumb. Welcome to Hell, feel free to burn wherever you may wish. Pretty fucking stupid huh? Let’s be real, that Hell does NOT exist. Man made it up to scare the shit out of you, and well it’s worked hasn’t it? How many men have killed? Not as many as those that have lived. Ok, what the fuck is Hell? Hell is earth? How many times have you heard that? How many times have you heard that someone’s life is a living hell. They are broke, ugly as fuck, and their mother just died. Life is a living hell. Now let’s think about that. Maybe there is a heaven and a hell. Maybe we are living in hell to prove our worth to this God we are all suppose to love and follow. Every 30 seconds a woman on earth is raped. Every 15 seconds a person is murdered. Every 75 seconds a child is molested. Wouldn’t you call that hell? The leader of the free world is sending kids overseas to die, and for what? Oil? Yeah, this very well might be hell. I think the eternity of burning and decay sounds much more appealing than living in fear everyday of your life, but that could just be me. As I write this, and most likely as you read this our world is crumbling in front of us, and your children, not mine, for I have none, will have to clean up this mess. If there is a God, let’s hope he saves them. “. “.. “.. “.. Those dot’s signify periods of rest for me. Why do I put my rest spots in this journal? Rest is still time, and time goes on. I have no other logical reason for those but to symbolize the time that has passed from my previous Psychobabble. What did I do when I was away from my log? I began to contemplate this theory of hell we began to discuss, and I have come to the conclusion that I am pretty positive I know what hell is’You ready? Hell is looking an opponent in the eye and not seeing the fear you should. Hell is breathing in and tasting the bloodlust of thousands of people booing you. Hell is a fucking wheel that is spinning to determine your fate. Hell is 6’9? 300lbs of pissed off Indian staring you directly in the eye, thinking, tasting, smelling, and hearing the same fear you are. As I look over my shoulder I see mountains of paper and notebooks with all my scribbles. They sent some man here not to long ago to write the story of me and my family’or as he has corrected me time and time again’My family and I. This senseless rant of hell and fear is only for me, myself, and the voices in my head. Well, you may end up reading them, depends how things turn out in a few days. I would be lying to you if I said I was not scared of a man I once called my brother. He is not the only brother I have lost. But now we must compete, compete to the death practically for what? A fucking paycheck. Yes, welcome to Hell, let’s take a walk, and have a tour of this place you will call home. Hard working men and women, frying in the heat to build a new wing to a building. Hell in a construction site’15-20-25 feet above the ground. You fall’well’Welcome to Hell, there’s plenty of room’Explosions and flesh eating wire’Welcome to Hell? “.. “. “.. ? ? ? Sorry I did it again, but I had to clear my head of what my future brings. I am now sitting here staring at a blank screen typing these exact words to you because I don’t know how to end this. Will my ending be a happy one? Or will the hero(villain?) be vanquished? Walk with me in hell, and let’s find out together. Welcome to Oblivion Shaman, for we meet one final time, the final epic battle where only the strong survive. July 26, 2008 Bryan O’Neil Bryan Mayhem to the world. Bryan S. O’Neil
