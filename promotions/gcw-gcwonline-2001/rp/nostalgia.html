Date: 5/205 Another long week after another shitty loss... Though I have gotten to go around Europe on the guys' tab...so it's not all bad. Still, dropping one to Borealis-much less setting myself up for it-that hurts. Jhub provides consolation, though...he's been dead the last few weeks. I'll be half-amazed if he even shows Sunday. I'll be even more amazed if he has a career left after it's all said and done. But it's only Wednesday, which means there is probably some work to be done tonight, so- Luke Hanson has slowly been walking through the backstage area of the arena at which WorldWide is to be held. He's casually wearing jeans, flip flops, and a polo. Upon seeing a piece of paper tacked up and marked card, he stops cold turkey and stares. Well I'll be damned... Hanson eyes are glued to the bottom of the card as he sees four names, three of which particularly matter to him: Toxic, Jhub, and his own. Kevin Jacobs can be seen slowly trudging towards Hanson, keen on getting his first interview of the day. Meanwhile, Hanson's face has gone from a look of confusion, to one of thoughtfulness, but finally settles on one of smug arrogance. He glances in Jacobs's direction and begins walking, allowing everyone's favorite reporter to catch up and get out what he wants to say. 'Mr. Hanson...Mr. Hanson, what do you think about tonight? Any of the GCW faithful-' 'Easy, Kev. I know where you're going, and I don't much care to hear you talk, so let me say what I have to say and then you can run along. See, few years back, I recall being in the midst of about a four month rivalry with the other gigantic youngster of GCW. That man, as some folks will remember, was Toxic. Now, I'm not going to lie. I don't remember too much of what went on back then. We beat the crap out of each other, we talked a lot of shit...I think at one point I got him arrested. And as much as I'd like to say what's done is done... Well, Tox, for some reason, feud or no feud, there's always been something about you that I utterly despise. I don't know what it is. I'm sure it's some complex mixture of attitude, aspirations, and ability. Whatever the reason, I really just don't like you. I mean, if I had to pick the random guy to punch in the middle of the street on look alone, having never met him, I can guarantee it would be you. I still don't know why, but I just hate your f*cking guts. So tonight. Tonight the powers that be have decided to reignite one of the most volatile feuds to have ever run its course in GCW. Maybe only for one night, maybe just for a fun opening gimmick, maybe because Jhub sucked someone off to get into the spotlightj. But whatever the reason, I'm genuinely looking forward to it. You and I have both talked about wanting that coveted second shot here to do something, and have both been fairly unable to do anything in regards of making something of that second shot. Now, as far as Sunday goes, beating the Jhub to death? That's a lock. I know I'm going to come out on top. But tonight...what better way to prove myself than to beat the second biggest man in the company? The man who crippled the president, the protege, and the pill popper. That's you, Tox, in case you didn't catch on. Now, sure, the refereeing tonight will probably be a bit off. Twizzy will be bouncing off the walls, Jhub will be throwing any cheap shot he can. We both know this. But when it comes down to it, I guarantee I'll pin you before the night is over. Oh, and Jhub? I'd like to make some kind of threat about what I'm going to do to you if you screw with me tonight, but I think it would be an excercise in futility. If I get the chance, I'll break you in half tonight, too. And if you screw with me, I promise I'll make it even worse. See you guys tonight.' Having arrived at Hanson's dressing room, he enters and closes the door behind him, and Jacobs shuffles off in hopes of finding another interview. Fade.
