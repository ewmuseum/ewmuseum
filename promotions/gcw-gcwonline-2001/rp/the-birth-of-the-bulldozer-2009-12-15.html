The birth of the bulldozer

15 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/09 “You want me to do what?” I demand in shock. Bill smirks at my obvious distress. “Like I said,” he replies as he leans back in his chair, “I’ve pulled a few strings and had you booked for your first prowrestling match two weeks from this Friday. It’s just an indie circuit match, but it’ll definitely give you some experience.” My mouth struggles to work as I experience a strange mix of excitement, and fear. This is certainly not what I had signed up for when I joined the Rutger’s University professional wrestling club. It’s true that Bill has been telling me that he had every intention of getting me into real matches, but I didn’t expect him to sign me up with only a few months of training! “Bill, we’ve only been doing this for a few months,” I point out somewhat urgently. “I’m nowhere near ready for this, hell you’re the one who told me that people will train for years before they have a match.” Bill doesn’t respond to my complaints with a single word. Instead he stares at me, and I stare right back locking the two of us into a battle of wills punctuated by a stubborn silence. This reminds me of all the times I sat in the coach’s office during my four years of amateur wrestling in high school, except we are in a starbucks instead of an office. “Alright, alright, I’ll do it,” I finally give in recognizing that Bill is not going to back down on this. “But you’re buying me beer afterwards whether I win or lose.” “You’re thinking about alcohol,” Bill actually laughs at that, a full belly laugh that temporarily wipes years from his face. “Kid, don’t you know how many women are willing to sleep with a man who wins a wrestling match, even in the Indie circuit?” I stare at him with an obviously unconvinced expression on my face. He shrugs his shoulder in a universal gesture of incomprehension. “I don’t pretend to know why it turns them on kid, I just know from experience that it does.” If nothing else Bill definitely knows the best way to motivate me. Two weeks is all I have to get myself ready for my professional wrestling debut. The only problem is that I have no idea what is involved in getting me ready for that debut. I know that physically I’m as ready as I can be, but there’s a whole mental aspect to the whole thing that Bill keeps talking about. Oh fuck it, I’ll go into the ring, I’ll pound on my opponent for awhile, and then go home. *Two weeks before the match* “Dude, I swear to God, if this is another story about some girl that you managed to sleep with during a frat party I am go to shove my fist down your throat,” I snarl as I stare at the nearly edible products that the Rutgers dining hall expects me to eat for lunch. Kevin smirks at me as he shovels in yet another heaping mouthful. “Did I touch a sore spot,” he taunts me as he chews what we were told was chicken, “because of the dry spell you’ve been going through?” “I am NOT going through a dry spell!” I whisper fiercely, though in the loud dining hall it would take a shout for anyone away from the table to hear me. “It hasn’t been long enough for you to be able to call it a dry spell! I’ve just had some fucking bad luck that’s all!” “A month is a dry spell,” Kevin asserts leaning back in his chair contentedly. I stare at him, amazed at how much such a skinny man could put away, and the fact that he seemed to be completely oblivious to the less than scrumptious taste of the meal. “I mean look at us dude. You’re completely jacked, I’m a skinny guy with glasses, and you should be getting twice the ass I’m getting. You know why you’re not?” “Because I’m Jewish and Jesus hates me?” “That’s partly it. But you’re just not willing to work hard enough for it. I mean, I got out to a party I make sure that my clothes just right for the occasion, I spend at least twenty minutes choosing the cologne, I make my hair just right, all that good stuff. If you put in half the effort into your appearance that I do you’d be golden.” “I don’t have enough estrogen to put in half the effort that you do,” I answer dryly. But unfortunately, I can’t deny that he’s right about the fact that I haven’t gotten a great deal of action for quite awhile. Now I know that some might think that this should be the last of my concerns considering the wrestling match that I have coming up, along with the finals that I have to study for but come on, I’m a freshman in college. I’m more of a giant walking penis than an actual person. “Well why aren’t you sleeping with Halloween girl then?” Kevin asks sourly referring to the last girl that I had slept with. “From what you told me she’d be more than happy to have sex with ya pretty much every night, but you’ve only slept with her once.” “Dude, I already told you,” I answer him tiredly as we begin to head out of the dining hall. “That girl was a heaping pile of crazy tied up in a huge ribbon of drama. I can deal with crazy, cause let’s face it all girls are crazy. But I’ve officially made my life a drama free zone, and she wasn’t good enough in bed to make me consider dealing with all her issues.” “Anything is better than nothing,” Kevin points out to my great annoyance, “and- well speak of the devil. “Hey Dan, “Colleen’s high pitched voice permeates the air behind me and I struggle to put a pleasant expression on my face. On the surface of it Colleen was a great girl, decent looking, great body, and really, really horny. But after we had sex on Halloween she chased me so much and dropped so many heavy-handed hints about a relationship that I lost any and all interest in her. Now she’s kind of like an average case of constipation, usually harmless but always annoying. “Hey Colleen, what’s up,” I ask in a tone that I manage to keep nominally pleasant. I don’t quite have the heart to tell her to leave me the hell alone, so my strategy in talking with her is mostly to find reasons to keep the conversations short and superficial. “I heard that you’re gonna have your first wrestling match soon,” she squeals in excitement causing me to barely hold back a wince. “Ye-wait a minute how do you know about that?” I ask in horror. The last thing I need is for Colleen to show up and obnoxiously cheer me on. “Bill told me,” she answers cheerfully, “and I’ve been telling everyone about it! I’m gonna get as many people as I can to come and cheer you on!” Oh dear God! It’s bad enough that Colleen will find a way to be there, but at least I don’t want to sleep with her anymore. But if a group of people from Rutgers show up….and they’ll definitely post the fight on youtube….without a word I set off at a run. “Where the fuck are you going?” Kevin calls after me no doubt laughing over the whole situation. “I gotta go watch some tapes,” I holler back with a definite note of panic in my voice. You know how I said two weeks wasn’t enough time for me to become anymore prepared than I already am? Fuck that. I am going to win that match if it kills me. *The day of the match* “Alright kid, what’s your name?” “Dan Black,” my voice comes out much higher than I intended, resulting from a mixture of nervousness and anxiety. I am less than an hour away from debuting on the professional wrestling stage, and I’ve gone through the need to vomit, shit, piss, and call my mother all in the last five minutes. Normally it wouldn’t be that bad, but Bill has yet to arrive. Bill, my wrestling coach, the man who was supposed to be my rock of moral support is nowhere to be found. The man talking to me right now is the one in charge of this particular indie show. He is a bald, fat, man in an old black suit and a pair of unstylish sunglasses. “No,” he replies in a manner that makes me suspect he is rolling his eyes, “I mean what is your stage name. We can go with your real name, but frankly, it needs a little something extra.” Ah crap. Not only do I have to worry about kicking someone’s ass but I also have to have an imagination tonight? If I wanted to be creative I would be in the arts school or something. I’m not one of those picture drawing, music singing, story writing nerds! “Um,” I answer trying to think of something, “Dan deliver the pain Black?” The man lets out an audible and obviously pained sigh. “Oh forget it, I’ll just get the announcer to come up with something. Anyway, you’re up now.” My heart skips a beat when I hear that. “But I’m not supposed to be on for an hour!” “Nah, we changed the booking up a bit. You’re up against a veteran so you’ll really make a statement if you manage to beat him tonight. You’re bigger and stronger than he is, so you might have a shot. Now get to the ramp.” I mechanically follow him as he leads me to the entrance to the ramp. I know that he doesn’t really think I have a shot, hell I don’t even think I have a shot against an actual veteran. I thought that I was going to face someone like me, someone relatively new to this entire thing. This new guy has probably been wrestling for years; I’ve been training for a few months! Maybe I can just make a run for it…but no I’ll never be able to live down running away from my first wrestling match. Ok, new plan, my goal is not to win, just not to be completely humiliated during the match itself. But wait losers don’t get laid….goddamnit Bill. But it’s too late for me to try to think of a plan because it’s time for me to make the long walk to the ring. “Introducing first, hailing from Rutgers University, DAN THE BULLDOZER BLACK!” Seriously, that’s the best they can do? The Bulldozer has got to be the worst fucking nickname that I have ever heard of. If I live through this I am going to kill that announcer. I numbly make my way towards the ring. I know intellectually that the crowd is small and somewhat pathetic by the standards of the major wrestling organizations, but they’re cheers still threaten to overwhelm my senses. A small but noticeable group in the back starts a chant of “RUTGERS, RUTGERS” and I can’t keep a wince from crossing my face. I get into the middle of the ring and stand there awkwardly, uncomfortably aware that everyone’s eyes are directly on me. “And his challenger, hailing from Paris, France…Jacques Legrand!” That bastard didn’t get a nickname. And he’s from France. If I lose to a Frenchman I might as well take a vow of celibacy. He stalks his way up to the ring, obviously comfortable with the stares and jeers of the crowd. He’s not as big as me, but I have a strange sense that his size is not a good indicator of his strength. Most striking is the contrast in our appearances. This guy is dressed in nothing but bright white boots and skin tight shorts that are obviously two sizes too small. His hair is bleached and spiked completely up, he’s an obvious showman that loves to pander to the crowd. I on the other hand am dressed in a pair of jeans and sneakers. There is a predatory gleam in the man’s eyes, I’ve a feeling he knows that this is the first time I’ve ever been in a ring. The bell rings, and in an instant Jacques is attacking me. Two quick strikes to the head leave me dazed, and before I know it he’s hoisting me through the hair and dumping right onto my back. Having been toughened by the little training that I received I instinctively begin force myself to my knees. I manage to get one leg up before another blow to my head knocks me back down. I am completely and totally fucked. I barely know where I am, much less how I am supposed to fight back against this guy. He’s not even giving me time to breathe much less the time to figure out what I want to do. Speaking of which, why isn’t he attacking me yet? I force myself to refocus, and realize that Jacques is busy playing to the crowd. He’s particularly mocking the Rutgers crowd which has fallen silent, and their quiet disappointment is like a physical blow in of itself. Wait a fucking minute…the dumb, cocky, bastard turned his back on me. I have one chance to get him but if it doesn’t work…fuck it less thinking more doing. I gather my legs under me, and get into position. The second Jacques turn back to me I run towards him at full speed. It’s not fancy, it’s not technical, it’s not even smart. If he had the presence of mind to side-step I would have run right past him and straight into the ropes. But he’s too surprised, and as a result my entire bulk smashes straight into him. Every move that I had carefully drilled with Bill has gone completely out of my mind. The only thing I can think of to do is just stand up and stomp the shit out of this guy. I'm getting a surprising number of blows in, but Jacques is forcing his way to his feet through my barrage of stomps, and it’s evident that soon he’ll be standing back up. Even if I’m no longer to numb to move and defend myself properly, I don’t dare try to fight a long match with this guy. It’s become painfully obvious to me that my skill set is far too limited and I’ve been pushed into this match for too early. Desperate and scared I do the only thing I can think of. Before he can get completely up, I grab Jacques, and using a strength that has been increased by my fear I literally through him out of the ring. Now the thing about this particular indie organization…it’s cheap. For instance the area outside the ring is not nice and padded, instead its quite hard, as hard as you would expect a cement floor to be. Jacques’ head makes a small noise when it lands on the ground. The referee begins to count, and I stare at Jacques willing him to remain motionless. I don’t dare pull him into the ring and try to pin him, there’s just too much that might go wrong. 1…. 2…. 3… Ok he’s staying down, good so far. 4…. 5… Ah fuck he’s starting to stir. Come on, he must feel like he has the world’s worst hangover right now. 6… 7… 8… Great he’s stumbling towards the ring. But I'm not sure how to stop him from getting in without putting myself in danger. 9…. 10….. Jacques managed to reach a rope right as the bell rings signifying me the winner. Wait..I won? Holy shit I Won! The Rutgers crowd erupts in a roar of cheers that I can’t help but bask in. It doesn’t matter that I won mostly through luck and admittedly cowardly tactics. One of their own has taken home the win, and that makes me the hero in their eyes. THe feeling of elation within me multiplies when the ref lifts my hand in the air. I have a feeling of invincibility right now…and it’s a powerful one. Tomorrow, I’ll deal with Bill. I’ll find out why he wasn’t here when I needed him, why he put me in a position to get humiliated, and I’ll even try to figure out what it means that I was able to beat this supposed veteran in what was a really short amount of time for a wrestling match. Personally I suspect that Jacques is a veteran of a great deal of losses, and no one will be too surprised by my victory. It doesn’t matter, because one of Bill’s promises does seem to be coming true. I lock eyes with one of the girl’s in the Rutgers crowd…I don’t think she’s licking her lips because her tongue is dry. I know I’m going to have a good night, and tomorrow I’ll have a whole new list of reasons why this wrestling idea may not be as crazy as I thought it was. But I am not keeping the nickname the Bulldozer. It's just stupid.
