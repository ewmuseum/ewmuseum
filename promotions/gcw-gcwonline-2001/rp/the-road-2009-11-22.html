The Road

22 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/22/09 "Nights dark beyond darkness and the days more gray each one than what had gone before. Like the onset of some cold glaucoma dimming away the world." -Cormac McCarthy-The Road Bouncing back and forth in a rhythmic walk, a hooded figure makes his way down a dusty tombstone gray hallway towards a bright window. The figure struggles on, letting out faint gasps for air and moans which are absorbed by the decaying walls. Within five feet of the window, the light begins to fade. He reaches out his fair skinned arm towards the window, yearning for its light but he falls. He begins his desperate crawl for the window where he grabs a hold of the sill. He pulls himself up. Every ounce of energy is expended trying to pull himself up, but he makes it. He looks out into a culdesac as bright as the window, but it too is fading. Beyond the road and trees in an impenetrable darkness moving in. Buildings in the distance, trees, cars, roads...all are consumed until no more. A deafening groan crescendos as the darkness gets closer to the window. The hooded figure pushes up against the window trying to make sense of the darkness, but all is being lost. Through the trees and into his street. The sound is all that can be heard. He struggles to move backward, but he is being pulled toward it. His arms and legs flail against the window until suddenly, the whole wall breaks free from the house and plunges into the oncoming darkness. All he can see is a fading road and the approaching darkness and right before he hits... Zacharia Taylor lunges out of bed and smashes against the ground below. He struggles to regain his breath and holds onto this now healed arm as it gleams with pain. He looks around wildly but it takes only moments to figure out he was only dreaming. Taylor looks up towards one of the windows in the room to see that it is still in the twilight hours of the morning. He pushes himself off the ground and begins to walk downstairs. Everything is the same, but so different. There is a sense of relief and liberation, but the idea is maddening. Christian may have been absent since that night at the church three days prior, but there is a lingering feeling that someone is watching him. Just like a knife cannot stop a headache, no bullet can stop Christian... ...or can it? Taylor flips on the light switch in the kitchen and stares at the counter where Christian would always sit. He peaks around the corner towards the back door, but nobody is there. Even though he would nearly always show up to instill fear and doubt in Taylor's mind, and he is gone, Taylor feels more afraid now than ever. A slave kills his master and flees the scene for a new life, but always must look over his shoulder for some sort of retaliation at the cost of his freedom. Slave equals Taylor. Taylor leans over the sink and splashes water in his face before cupping his hands to take a drink. When he looks up, his is startled when lightning strikes and the tree near the window is cast in shadow. Frustrated, Taylor rips the towel off the counter and wipes his face dry and heads back upstairs... ********* "What I don't understand is what the hell he does all the time cooped up in his house. I rarely see him around town, but it's usually to pick up food and go..." "Yeah, I worry bout the kid..." The two old men in front of the local CVS continue to talk about the town's citizens and then their health issues that have come with their respectable ages. "Hell no! I refuse to let them stick a vacuum up my ass crack! Now way!" "You're on the wrong side of sixty and you still didn't get one? Lucky your colon didn't fall through!" "If I survived Nam at my age, I can survive a little shit clogging me up..." A cab pulls up in front of the CVS, silencing the men for the moment while they try to see through the tinted windows as to who is about to get out. The door flings open and a cautious Zacharia steps out. He looks up at the prying eyes of the old men and they look away immediately. As Taylor walks into the store they both share curious looks and go back to their medical problems. Taylor goes down the isle for over the counter medicines and gets to a basket that has inserts for shoes. He looks down at his foot and then back at the sizes on the boxes. Timidly, he looks up and down the isle and sees that nobody is looking and then pulls off his shoe and checks the shoe size. Ten and a half. He grabs an insert and then heads over to the pharmacist's counter. A middle aged man comes up to him with an irritable look and nods at Zacharia instead of speaking. "I was wondering if you could help me?" "With what?" "Well, I possibly need some kind of pill." "For?" Taylor looks around again to make sure nobody is in earshot and then leans into the window for more privacy. "Well, I have these bad dreams and when I'm awake I have anxiety and paranoia..." The pharmacist looks up at him, raising an eyebrow. "...and I don't feel right about it. I've never been to a doctor or anything, so I don't know what I should be taking." Taylor desperately looks up at the pharmacist who is just staring back at him in a very curious way. After about thirty seconds of dull staring, Taylor clears his throat and begins to talk again. "Is there anything..." "...I can't just give you something. You need to talk to a doctor, and in your case I would see a psychiatrist about your issues and see what they say..." Taylor looks at him in desperation for a remedy, but the pharmacist just walks away, leaving him in ruin. A therapist? The only time he ever really heard mention of a therapist was when Bagwell went to see that woman and all he did was talk to her and have sex with her daughter. Taylor doesn't seem where that will help him. Taylor walks back down the isle where he came from and a bottle catches his eye. The bottle is a deep shade of green with a yellow label. The tag line is "Sleep Aid". Taylor picks up the bottle and reads no more. He brings it up to the counter and pays for it. If anything can help him sleep and possibly not have these bad dreams, he may not have to see a doctor after all. Taylor walks out of the store, still staring at the bottle and makes his way towards the taxi when someone speaks up behind him. "I'd have issues sleeping too, if I were Zacharia Taylor..." Taylor quickly turns around and all he sees is the two old men staring back at him with the same curious looks. Taylor turns back towards the cab and is about to get back in when... "So tell me, how fucked up is your head really?" Taylor quickly turns to his left to see a devilish grin worn by none other than his Scottish friend, Andy Hugh. Taylor smiles faintly and walks over to the Scot and shakes his hand sloppily. "Feelin a little beat up over the other night's showing, aye? Bloody good match it was against those tossers, but you sure did get tossed around a bit, huh?" "Yeah, well one was the World Champ after all but..." "No fuckin excuses. The guy's a cunt, and you remember that. You! You are the next bloke to turn up on top, just keep with it! What the fuck are the pills for? Trouble sleeping?" Taylor takes the bottle out of hiding and reluctantly shows them to Andy. He laughs and pulls out an orange bottle with a white lid and shakes it, rattling the pills inside. "Everyone has their vices I suppose. Lower back pain, eases it up a bit, but the fucker gets real bad this time of year. Anyway, where ya headed?" "Back to the house, need to get some training in and relax a bit before..." "Can I come along for the ride? Not trying to intrude or anything, but ya stood your buddy up the other night at Murray's so ya fuckin owe me!" Taylor squints his eyes in thought and remembers he was supposed to meet Andy at Murray's but everything is such a blur before the match at WW. Taylor motions towards the cab and Andy jumps in. Taylor climbs in after and closes the door. The ride over to his house is a short one, only about five minutes with hitting the few lights on Main Street. When they pull up, Andy gets out immediately, nearly before the car comes to a stop. "This is it?" Andy makes a face of disgust and points over his shoulder at the house at the top of the street with the lake behind it. Taylor gets out and looks at the house and then back at Andy. "Well, fuck, I just expected something bigger! Got neighbors and everything, I just expected something a little more exclusive..." "I own the other two houses too..." Taylor begins to walk up to the house and pulls out his keys. Andy's eyebrows raise and he he cocks his head with a look of surprise and follows in Taylor's shadow. Taylor opens the door and continues to walk to the back of the house where the kitchen is. Andy follows and makes his way to the back and stares out at the dock and lake. "Nice fuckin' lake!" Andy stands matter of factly and then looks around the kitchen as if in search of something. "You want something to eat or drink?" Taylor opens up the fridge and reveals nothing but Gatorade, water, eggs, chicken, and some veggies. Andy bends down and looks at the variety. "Looks like my fridge back when I wrestled in high school. Fuck, I miss that shit. Now all I drink is beer and I'll eat anything as long as it has a carb! Fuckin shit..." He leans in and grabs a bottle of water and cracks the top which falls to the ground. He bends down and picks it up but remains crouching for a moment staring under the cabinet. Taylor bends down slightly to see what he's staring at and then a very cold chill passes over his spine. "What in the fuck is..." Andy reaches between two cabinets and lifts up a potato peeler covered in dried blood with a small piece of skin still between the blades. Andy holds it up between himself and Taylor and stares at it with complete and utter disgust and then looks at Taylor. Taylor unwillingly looks down at his own arm and Andy follows. His eyes light up and he takes a deep breath. "You? You did...you took...your arm...FUCK!" Andy flings the potato peeler over his shoulder into the sink and wipes his hand on his shirt. He stutters without a word and his face twitches and contorts into different degrees of emotion before finally settling in on surprise. "Fuck man! Please tell me you didn't slice your own arm! Fuck, you did, didn't you? Fucking shit man! That was..." Andy leans turns around and looks in the sink at the peeler. He leans closer and closer before his nose nearly touches it. "Look, Andy I can explain. It's..." Andy picks it back up and turns around towards Taylor. He puts his face extremely close to it, clearly staring at the piece of skin. He is now fascinated by the discovery. "Is that skin?" "I don't know how to put it, but..." "Ha, it still has a piece of hair sticking out of it!" "Huh?" Taylor is perplexed by the reaction by Andy. Andy is finding the whole thing amusing like any kid would find a bug in their sandbox amusing. He is trying to dissect it with precision. He goes to touch the piece of skin but he pulls his hand back and squirms slightly smiling at his own disgust. "You are one sick fuck, but I like your fuckin style!" Taylor begins to feel uncomfortable and desperately wants to take the peeler and throw it out of site. He feels exposed and his scarred arm feels as if a breeze has gone over it. "Andy, look. I don't know if I feel comfortable..." "Look, Zach. We're all fucked up. We all do dumb shit to find ourselves, right? Fuck, I slit my wrist when I was younger cause me and my fuckin retarded football cunt thought it would feel good. We all have a story..." Taylor rubs his arm which feels like it is burning and walks past Andy. He hesitates at the back door, but then opens it and walks out towards the lake. An electric current builds up in his stomach, making him tighten it. Behind his eyes a pressure begins to build and they become painful. Everything begins to tremble. Taylor sits down next to the tree right before the dock and takes deep breaths to calm himself. Is this the realization that Christian was talking about? The reality that is finally settling in? Christian may be gone, but Taylor is right here, suffering the aftermath of identity. Footsteps grow louder behind him and Taylor rights himself against the tree, waiting for Andy to come around. Much to his surprise, when the footsteps stop and he turns around to meet the source, nobody is there. He looks over both shoulders and even stands up, but nobody is standing outside with him. Taylor steps to the side and looks around to the street to see an empty road. He struggles with himself, thinking back to the footsteps and shakes the sound out of his head. He walks over to the house once more and looks inside the kitchen and sees at the end of the hall Andy sitting on the couch by the front door. Taylor fights his pride and goes back inside the house. Andy watches him sit down on the couch next to him an doesn't say a word, letting Taylor talk at his own pace. "You slit your wrist?" Andy smiles, "Not to commit suicide. I needed to feel something real. Getting pissed wore off after a while, Football wasn't a release anymore. I needed to know..." Taylor just stares ahead and phases out. He pictures all the times in Clifton where he struggled for the very same thing. "Sometimes, sometimes you just need to take life by the fuckin horns and fuck it in the ass if ya know what I mean. All celebrities..." "I'm no celebrity..." Andy holds up his hand to accept the retort. "right, I mean...well..." "Look, I never told anyone this before, but..." Andy holds on in anticipation but Taylor can't find the strength to say it. Taylor puts his head down and pressure once again begins to build behind his eyes. "If you don't want..." "I...see...things..." Andy raises one eyebrow and shifts uncomfortably in his seat, but none of this catches the attention of Taylor for he is staring holes into the ground, envisioning everything as he talks about it for the first time openly... "I've always done things to get rid of this rage inside of me. I was never able to control it, but I found that inflicting pain was the only way. Not pain to myself, but to others." "By others," Andy tries to understand but is very hesitant to ask questions. "Do you mean in wrestling?" "No, God no. I, I can still see them. I can still see all of them. Their eyes. Every single one of them used to haunt me in my dreams, until I met a different kind of pain, something that used to conceal my sorrow. That is when I started to see him..." Taylor begins to rock back and forth. Andy becomes nervous, but there is no way out of this. He bit off more than he can chew, but he is suck. "Who do you mean?" "Ch...Ch..." Taylor can't say his name out loud. His arm begins to burn and his hands begin to tremble. The windows seem to bulge out and the sunlight comes in like shadows. He tries to focus but his mind is pulled in a million different directions. The walls come in closer. "You don't have to if...." Andy's voice gets absorbed by the darkness around him. The room falls absolutely silent and his head begins to spin so he closes his eyes and begins to push his palms into the sides of his head. Something inside his head breaks the silence. A mush of words that comes out in whispers and slurs. The noise becomes more rapid. Faster, and faster, and faster. More cluttered and then finally clear... "...Christian..." Taylor opens his eyes to the newly found silence and looks around the room but finds everything empty. Andy looks at him timidly. "Christian? Who's Christian? The him? Is that who you found?" "Found?" Nearly disgusted, Taylor looks up at Andy. "I didn't find anything. He found me. I mean..." Taylor shakes his head and everything begins to fall in line in his thoughts. Every second that passes he finds it easier to be in his own skin. The visions begin to fade from his sight... "I saw Christian. He called himself Christian Thomas. I imagined him..." Andy struggles to understand what he is saying, but he doesn't seem nearly as uncomfortable as you would imagine. "Wherever I went, whenever I would open my eyes, he was there. I felt like I was learning from him, finding things out I never knew, but I knew everything. I was him and he was me. Anything he said, I knew already, but I put it out of my mind..." "An external conscious?" "In ways, but it was so..." "Real?" "I would interact with him like any person off the street. I truly felt it was someone else, but it was me. I imagined it all. He had this, this control over me. He would convince me of...to do things. Look here, say this, do that. He made that peeler look like the only thing to get him out of my head..." Andy shifts again in his seat, but this time he stands up and sits in a chair directly in front of Taylor. "I saw him everywhere. I still look over my shoulder, hoping he's not staring at me. I'd even see him at my matches, for christ sake..." "Zach, where did you go after the bar? Tank said he saw you acting, odd. Not pissed, but odd. He said you disappeared into the woods." Taylor looks away from the floor and at Andy. His eyes show genuine concern. "I saw Christian and he hit me, next thing I know we...He tried...He wanted me to kill myself." Andy sits up straight, not expecting that answer. He brushes his hand through his hair and is about to say something but then... "I had a gun...your gun..." "What?" Andy nearly falls out of his chair. "My gun? Zach," Taylor looks up looking horrified by what he is about to say. "I don't have a bloody gun." Taylor struggles to think back to the church. Everything is a blur. Everything seems different than what he thought ten minutes ago. He sees Christian there, then he isn't. He sees the other two men, but then it is just Taylor pacing back and forth madly talking to himself. Then the gun is gone, replaced by Taylor's hand. He points it at Christian, and when the trigger is pulled, Christian slyly smiles and disappears. "No, no, no, NO! I SHOT HIM, NO! NO!" "Taylor, please fuckin calm..." "No, you don't understand! Christian! Oh, no... Christian's still here!"
