The Tides of Change [part I]

14 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14th, 2009 “Midway in our life's journey, I went astray from the straight road and woke to find myself alone in a dark wood” - Dante Alighieri Throughout his short tenure in Ringside Wrestling, SurReal had been embroiled in a constant conflict with a former acquaintance by the name of Lori Equinox. At first, Equinox’s motives behind his unrelenting harassment of the Tribal War Machine were unclear. Gradually more and more of his reasons came to light. When Equinox first broke into the industry in 2002 he was signed to one of professional wrestling’s premier promotions; the Dominant Wrestling Organisation, at the time, SurReal was one of the promotion’s prominent talents, renown for his brutality and underhanded approach to achieve victory by whatever means necessary. Likewise when the two men crossed paths a few years later whilst employed by the World Wrestling Alliance, SurReal’s reputation for manipulation and disregard for others was still at full force. Something about SurReal had stuck with Equinox, he felt a kinship with the Irishman with whom he clearly shared a great deal in common. Fast forward five years, to January 2009, five years in which SurReal had been unable to compete in his chosen profession. Doubts had long circled as to whether or not he would ever be able to lace up his boots and wrestle again, living in that doubt and uncertainty for half a decade had changed the man to his very core. The malicious, uncaring bastard that had been Seán William O'Neill had learnt to appreciate all aspects of life, not in the least any opportunity that came his way to find his way back between the ropes of the squared circle. When Equinox surfaced once more he made no secret of his distaste for the newly ‘reformed’ SurReal. He set about trying to beat the resistance out of him, to attempt to stir up some of that old attitude from SurReal. He had failed… …or had he? “You wouldn’t believe how common it is Seán, nine people out of every ten do the same, they push that little bit too hard in their recovery and end up setting themselves back for a while longer.” “But it’s ok now Doc?” “Should be, just take it easy out there alright?” “Take it easy? Doc I am a PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER! There is no such thing as ‘taking it easy’ for me, I go out there night after night and leave it all in the ring. I have to, I don’t know any other way than to compete with every morsel of energy in my body!” “Perhaps you should rest up for a few more weeks then Seán.” “FUCK RESTING UP! I’M FUCKING TIRED OF ‘RESTING UP’ ITS ALL I’VE DONE THIS LAST MONTH AND A FUCKING HALF! I’M WRESTLING THIS WEEK AND THAT’S THE FUCKING END OF IT!” The doctor, clearly far from impressed with the barrage of abuse he’s just received, closes over the file he was holding. The file containing the test results on SurReal’s most recent injury, after quickly gathering his things he gets to his feet and begins to make his way towards the exit. “That’s it?” “Seán, it is quite clear that my advice and recommendations have very little impact on your decisions. It is also quite clear that you intend to wrestle this week against my better judgement, so be it. I can’t stop you. I suggest you seek alternative medical advice in future, in all my years as a health care professional I’ve never once been spoken to like that, and I certainly will not be spoken to like that again. Goodbye Mr. O’Neill.” With that the doctor makes his way out of the apartment. Leaving behind him a rather frustrated looking Irishman. Hunched over the chair he’s been sitting in for the best part of an hour, he lets go a deep sigh as he pulls himself to his feet and heads over towards the large window looking out at the Albany skyline. “…Fuck…” Muttering under his breath, a moment of regret sweeps over his mind. Well that was fucking stupid, he was a good doctor, he didn’t deserve that! It’s not like the injury was his fault, but it’s ok, before long Lucas fucking Quinton will know exactly who’s fault it was! Even at the best of times, SurReal carried a real dislike for rookies with egos. He had spent too many years honing his craft across the continents, lost too much blood and sweat building up experience enough to make a career from professional wrestling, to take any pleasure in watching time and again a physically impressive young specimen win a contract only to assume they’ve made it. He had earned his spot, paid his dues, and most importantly, shown respect to his peers. He had done things ‘the right way’ and frankly, he had little time for those who had not! Quinton has all the hallmarks of another one of these upstart brats that hasn’t yet earned his spot, yet there he is, steadily climbing the card, making a name for himself… stealing my spotlight! Reckless in the ring, sure the kid can chain-wrestle a bit, but he needs to learn more than the fucking basics! If he’d not been so goddamn stiff at Rampage then all this could have been avoided. But I’ll show him how the vets play stiff! His cell phone’s vibrations, humming on his coffee table interrupted his train of thought. He was so caught up with the thought of teaching Lucas Quinton a little payback, that at first he was unsure of what was making the noise. After a few seconds it all clicked into place, it was his phone… THE BOOKERS! Suddenly the only thing that mattered in the world was answering that call. Unbeknown to Doctor Smith, SurReal had made his mind up long before speaking to him, he WAS returning to action this week, and he had already let the company know this. This was going to be the call he’d been waiting for, confirmation that he was back in business, he was finally going to get his hands on Quinton! He lunged across the room at a blistering place, grabbing the phone and flicking it open before he’d even brought it to his ear, this was one call he was not going to miss! As he got the phone to his ear he just caught the end of the first sentence of the pre-recorded message. …is as follows. The opening bout is Lucas Quinton… …yesss… …versus Bishop Steele. A feeling of absolute frustration overwhelmed him as his concentration slipped away from the message, it had not occurred to him that this was a possibility. How could the booking team not have seen that Quinton was responsible? How could they not grant his request for a one-on-one match with him? We’re they protecting their precious little ‘rising star’? They MUST be! The bastards! …versus SurReal. “Shit! Now I’m going to have to listen to the bloody thing all over again!” Allowing the message to tail off, he patiently waited for the push-button option to replay the message, all the while his frustration and disappointment not to be taking on Quinton was in the ascendancy. He listened intently to the message this time around, long enough to discover that his opponent was in fact an old acquaintance, Shaman, with whom he had worked before. From a positive viewpoint, at least he had a match, so at least they had got the message that he was ready to make his comeback. So, it looks like the Shaman is just going to have to take the brunt of my anger for now. He’s a big boy, I’m sure he’ll put up enough of a fight. I know Shaman well enough to trust that he will give me a fair fight… I do wonder if his big Viking friend will have some part to play though. The two of them might be a tall order! Ha, I crack myself up! Shaman carries an advantage in both height and weight over SurReal, but not enough of an advantage for the Irishman to lose any confidence in his ability to take the larger man down for a pinfall. However his Norwegian accomplice is taller and heavier again, if the two decided to double-team him, then his odds of victory would be slim at best. And so he had to hold to the hope that Shaman still considers him a friend. It was not the match he had hoped for by any stretch of the imagination, but ultimately it wasn’t a bad comeback match, for one thing, Shaman is no rookie! And regardless of whether Shaman takes him on alone, or calls Samuelsson to his aid, at least he will be in the ring with someone who knows how to wrestle without putting their opponent on the treatment table. His issues with Lucas Quinton would have to wait for another day, but he assured himself that his opportunity to exact some form of revenge would not be far off. Next time Quinton… next time!
