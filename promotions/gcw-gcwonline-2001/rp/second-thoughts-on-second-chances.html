Second Thoughts on Second Chances

Date: Town called Desperation When I look at this mask I look at my failure. When last this mask was worn it was on the head of an injured man who every week put himself one step closer to death, one step closer to a fate I'd signed him into. I failed Jordan as the friend I should have been. I encourage him to continue wrestling when I knew he was beyond the breaking point, I lied to keep him going, gave him the hope of one more. One more season, one more title run. I failed him as the leader I could have been. I was callous and obsessed with my own selfish ambitions of being world champion. I see it now because in the eyes of this mask I see who I truly am. I see the hollowness left inside by my actions, by my eagerness to achieve something great, to leave a legacy branded in my name. I did alot of bad things to alot of people, but this I regret doing the most. Not because it backfired, but because of all the careers I've ended and all the names I've destroyed, Jordan is the only one who didn't deserve it. When I asked him to join my stable, to help me take down Lestatt, he helped me. I payed him, but I don't think he did it for the money. He was actually in it for the cause, it was something that now, looking back, I can admire. He had the passion, when he was in that ring he was incredible, given the chance to heal he could still be in that ring doing things I might never even come close to. But it was me who changed all that. And now I have a chance to change it again. Sobias has everything hooked up, all I have to do is decide. Johnny Lexicon is fucked, if I ever step foot in the states and get caught by law I'm screwed, if I get caught by Jordan I'm screwed. I'm cornered and offered the chance at redemption, the golden hand of opportunity has stretched to rap its divine knuckles at my door. But this mask, the name it comes with. To be The Marauder is to give up the last shred of what held me as me. To put this on is to assume a role. A role of purity. A role of redemption. My sacrifices are at an end, I have sacrificed my family, I have sacrificed my friends, my hobbies, my love, my money, my name, and now finally my very identity. There's nothing left to lose, I have been stripped bare of everything but the one single thing that can never be taken. My talent. I was a bad bussiness man. I was a bad leader, a bad friend. I was a bad person. But I am a damn good wrestler. And now, now that's all I am. Now it's all that I need. The Marauder is a wrestler, a warrior, and nothing more. In my moments of self righteous egotism I convinced myself I was just that, a pure wrestler. But under this mask I am seeing through new eyes and I see my mistakes. Everything I did before, I have to do differently. Johnny Lexicon squandered his talent on politics, allowed himself to think he was owed something. But now I can do it over again. Dangerous Games is my chance to do it. Dangerous Games is another of those golden opportunities, but it also made time a dangerous restriction. It looms close and I stand forgotten far from it's shadow. Jordan White is my only chance, I need him to sign me and get me to the show in time, that's all I need. It's funny, it's as if the last month has somehow been leading me into this inevitable situation, with this choice, preparing me to take everything I knew, use everything I know, and do exactly what it is I'm giving it all up for. I'm doing this for this ring, the competition, the drive to be the best, you name it there's a hundred cliche terms but I tell you I'm as addicted to all of them as ever. But where once I had the means I couldn't see the end, and now that it's passed I know where it's been. The last month has taught me alot, in the ring every night with luchadores and thai fighters, I'm more than ready to get back into GCW. I'm eager. Twenty nine other men and women stand a chance at taking a shot at the world champion. And if what Sobias told me is right, then Lestatt is ready to take that world title. Lestatt has done what he's done before, without the proper competition he gets out of control. I respect the guy because I know what he's capable of, but it's because I know him so well that makes me the only man capable of stopping him. Nobody knows it though, nobody important enough that the important people care anyways. Lestatt is a monster that inside the ring understands only two things. Fear. Pain. The fear he causes feuls the pain he inflicts, it drives him. Take away that fear, and he's just another wrestler with a temper problem and a few tricks. But on the same token, he fears me because I do not fear him, and knows I can dish out enough pain for the both of us. He knew this when I first showed up, he knew we had unfinished business and he avoided it. Wouldn't be surprised if he went up top and asked the big boys to keep us apart specifically, the bastard. And this is what makes Dangerous Games so much more important, if I could just get in there and win, I could beat Lestatt. He won't be able to hide, he can't turn away from me forever. But to do anything, I had to get Jordan back on my side, I needed his help but he'd never help Johnny, not after everything. But maybe he'd help the Marauder, maybe he'd give The Marauder a chance. The phone rings and Johnny puts the mask aside to answer it. 'Hello?' 'Jordan's in Mexico, he's looking to kill you, I'm setting something up, meet me at the warehous. 'I'll be there.' Johnny rises from the dingy bed in the dingy hotel and leaves without a second thought. Before long he had ran his way through winding back alleys and long dirty stretches of road, through mazes of a thousand squat bungalos. He obviously knew the route because he lept fences and ducked clothes lines without a break in pace. Finally the houses thinned and long clusters of warehouse's assumed their place, cars passed by in a long line heading in the same direction. Johnny stopped outside a tall building and wiped the sweat from his forehead as a heavy set mexican with black hair greased and pulled back smoothly walked up to him with a mosey in his swagger. 'Glad you could make it, have you made your choice?' There was no polite 'How's it going's or 'good to see ya's just cold business. But that could be expected, these two men weren't friends, before a few days ago they weren't even aquaintances, but Sobias openened Johnny's mind to retribution. He explained the problems faced to him in a legal aspect, he showed Johnny the loophole. Producing the mask Johnny waits, a last fleeting moment of hesitation perhaps? The leather mask rises reverently to his recently shaved head and slips over smoothly, the eye sockets fit perfect and the chin strap tightly, the zipper descends quietly and much too quickly, there was no going back now. Johnny Lexicon died, lost in the desert, it simply took him a month to realize it. Sobias simply stands and watches with a neatly restrained impatience behind his sweaty features, not nearly as affected by the drama of the situation. 'It's a good look for you.' He produces as The Marauder runs his hands across the mask's smooth surface again and again. 'It was made for me, it feels real good, fits perfect. Hand. Glove.' 'You ready to do this then?'
