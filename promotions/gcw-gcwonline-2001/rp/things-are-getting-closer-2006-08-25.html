Things are getting closer....

25 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/25/06 ~Atlantis Place Bed and Breakfast~ ~Vancouver Room~ ~Portland, Oregon~ Everyone had come to Portland for the event. Blaise and Ukyo had a few days here alone ahead of time. Time to spend with each other, and Blaise's daughters before they took the trip back to Vegas with their nanny, Kaylee. Dangerous Games was going to be in Blaise's home town, but also where the entire group had taken up residence in their houses. But for the next few days they were in Portland, Oregon. A place that Blaise found boring and unpronounced in anything that it did. Yes, they loved their art and other things, but when it came down to it, it was a poor man's Seattle. And Seattle didn't cost much. And it was nothing compared to the hustle and bustle of Las Vegas. And right now, Blaise could go for a little bit of that. It would be an escape from what can only be the calm before a storm. **Twang** ?Fuck...? Blaise looked down at the now broken 'D' string on his guitar. Right in the middle, he didn't like that. Was a pain in the ass to fix them. In a not common display of anger, Blaise tossed his ESP AX 400 series guitar at the amplifier that he had his headphones plugged into so that he'd not wake anyone. The resulting commotion did wake someone, the person who was laying in the bed next to the chair he was currently sitting in. Ukyo woke up and looked directly at him. She knew what was going on as his hands went to his temples right away. The look on his face showed that he was in pain. Quickly she got up and headed to the bathroom, in there she grabbed a few of the pain killers that Blaise had prescribed for his knee, even though he'd not really needed them since he'd accepted the mantle of Turren. She poured two of the large pills out of the bottle and grabbed one of the provided cups to put some water in. She stepped out and walked over where Blaise was sitting, handing both the pills and the water to him. ?Thank you.? He took them both from her, popped the pills into his mouth and then chugged the water down. Ukyo sat down onto the bed, crossing her legs and sliding her hands down to keep things decent, even though he'd seen her not only in her panties but in less before, they had been lovers for going on two years now. ?They've come back haven't they?? He could only nod to her as he waited for the pills to take their effect. It was something he didn't like, and they only served to dull the throbbing in his head. ?So it was the sword that was keeping it at bay, wasn't it?? ?Apparently so.? What was wrong with him? It was a conflict on the inside. On one side, the side from his father he was of the Tautha De Dannan, the people of faery found in what is called modern day Ireland. On his mother's side, he was a garou, a werewolf for those who don't know their other terms. The equivalent of what their more tribal practices would call a Galliard or Bard, born under a gibbous moon. Both should have been in sync. But they weren't. To the humans who find themselves to be able to bear the mantle of a faery, their spirit is merged with the essence of a 'mantle' of one of the immortals. When they die, cause while age can not take them, other things can, that essence is collected and turned over to the next person to meet the requirements for that mantle. As soon as the mantle has infused with the body, that person becomes one of the Tautha, and the bearer of that mantle. Most of them learn to access the memories of their past, but in the recent times that has become a feat that requires a lot of concentration. As the society of the world has changed, it's become harder and harder to get that connection back into the past. But Blaise's other half isn't human, and the adaptation of the mantle of Turren is taking longer and is harder then it would normally be as the mantle adapts to what it needs to interface with. Back over a year ago, Blaise came into possession of something that in it's presence and link to him, seemed to bring that pain down to nothing. The Sword of the Archangel Zadikiel, whom Blaise killed on this mortal plane. But just a few weeks ago, Blaise lost that sword when, in accordance with Zadikiel's prophecy to him, he'd loose it to an angel who didn't have one of their weapons yet. Zadikiel was the angel of prophecy and destiny... Blaise had no reason to argue with him. But in the end, the swords heavenly and healing presence had kept him feeling better. He'd planted it near the tree that is bonded with him as a member of the race of tree faery normally referred to as Dryads, but not limited in sex to only be female. That allowed him to remain linked to the sword and with the help of other trees, bring it to him when he needed it. But it was not his anymore. The link was broken, and the pain inside his head came back sharply. It took meditation to stop it for a few hours all together, or the pain killers the human doctors had given him that made him a bit loopy and, didn't kill the pain, only made it so he didn't care it was there. ?Would there be a way to get the sword back? It's not like Dani even wants it.? Blaise shook his head to the negative. ?No, it's hers now. It bonded to her. It reacted to her. I'll just have to live with it. They've gotten better over the years. So eventually it'll get better, and less painful.? ?Any idea when that will be?? He just shook his head to that. **Knock, Knock, Knock** Blaise sighed. His outburst had awoken someone else. He looked over to the clock, 2 AM in the morning... Maybe not woken, maybe they were still awake. Ukyo got up and opened the bedroom door, and standing there, with his drawing pad was Joshua Brand. ?Hey, everything Okay in here?? ?Yeah, he broke a string. And threw the guitar.? ?Which one?? ?The ESP AX.? Josh almost pushed Ukyo aside and stepped into the room. Blaise straighted up in the chair, to at least hide the physical discomfort he was in. Although the sudden dawning on him... Josh would know or soon get a hint as to what was going on. The empath that he was becoming as he got stronger and stronger, was going to figure it out. ?Man, you better not have hurt that guitar. I'd have to have to do something drastic.? ?It'll be fine. Sorry if I woke you up. Lately I've been a little on edge, and the broken string was a bit much right now.? Josh looked at him with a quite blatant ?no shit? look, Josh having been on the receiving end of that 'edge' just a few short weeks ago up on Mount Rainer. ?Actually I'm glad you're up. I need to speak to you.? Blaise hit the answer that Josh was partially expecting. ?Okay, what trouble are you planing now, Josh?? Josh folded his arms and stared hard at Blaise. ?Not planing anything, yet. But have something, I guess, to ask you.? Blaise stood looking at him slightly amused. But Josh didn't return the amused look his was more serous. ?Do to a lead that Terra gave us, I need to take Danielle to New Mexico before she runs out a window and goes flying to him. Either that you need to tell him to go back to Texas.? Blaise raised his eyebrow at Josh. He looked over to Ukyo, who was almost just as surprised. They knew who he was speaking about, but they were not ready for him to speak like that about their boss. ?Who?? ?Mr. Childress. He's floating around the shows, he's poking around and snooping. And he's only a few hours north of us on a regular basis in that tower in Seattle.? ?What makes you think she would do that? I haven't noticed anything lately.? Blaise was to the point, from the corner of his eye he noticed Grayson coming into the room also. Apparently the commotion had been noticed by a few. Or everyone was more of a night owl here in boring land then they should be. Before he can asking anymore, Josh pulls out a familiar book. The hand made, leather bound journal that Dani is always writing in. Josh opens the book and tosses it on the table all over the two exposed pages are the symbol of Suriel, the fallen arch angel that shares a body with Ian Childress. ?I just found this while doing some of my own snooping. There has to be six or seven more pages like that. Oh, accept for the last page. Which has been made in the last week or so.? Josh flips to the last page that she had written on one side was the two symbols of Suriel and Baraqiel's. Baraqiel as they had found out, was the angel that Dani had killed with what had been Blaise's sword on Mount Rainer. The angel that had betrayed her grandfather, Kokabel. Blaise looked though at the second page on the two. In the middle of the second page was the two symbols combined. Gray and Blaise both looked at each other then him. ?Baraqiel's. and Suriel's symbols combined, had to look the other one up when I didn't know that it was. But we heard her say his name on the mountain. She's mentioned him a few times this last week or so when I finally got her to talk about it. I gave you my report on that.? Gray, spoke up. ?When did you find this? Or when do you think she made this?? ?She was drawing it in her sleep last night, I managed to get her back to bed but this isn't the first night she was like this. I don't think she even knows what she is doing she hasn't opened this since the attack. Sometimes she'll get up, go to the bathroom, and then on the way back, stop and write something in her journal. Come to find out, it's not journal entries, but drawing these... glyphs. I confronted her with them tonight. ?Why didn't you report this yesterday?? ?Figured it was just her mind playing tricks with her, you have to understand no mater what she says she can't stop wanting to get near Suriel. She usually doodles his symbol when she is just thinking or letting her mind wander. But tonight it was more, it was something much more. She isn't trying to run she is just dreaming.? Blaise picked up the book and looked at the two pages then he walked over to the combined printer and fax machine he carries for his work over the Order's satellite network. Laying the book face down, he punches some numbers on the keypad. As the machine comes to life he he turns and looks at josh. ?You know I had to report this. It's going to Annie.? ?Why do you think I showed you ? She's been drawing the same symbol for months now. But this was something new, something different and I couldn't hide it from you.? Blaise watched the machine as it worked to transmit the images of the drawings to wherever Annie was working out of right now. ?I just don't know about allowing you two to take off to someplace that you are not even sure where you are going yet.? Josh pulled out his drawing pad and put it down, a few loose pages fell out if it as he flipped pages. ?Terra has marked the place on a map, for us now we just need to get there. I drew this from what I'd seen in Dani's dream. And this is a page I printed up from the internet that Terra confirmed was this Fallen Mesa place near Alamogordo, New Mexico.? Blaise looks at the printed papers, and Josh's drawing. He was prepared. But there were other questions that needed to be answered. ?And what happens if you two get attacked again? Or even she decided to run like she has before?? ?I think I can handle her. She needs this to put some pieces together. Shit. we need this to put pieces together. You have to admit you are in the dark also with some of this as she is. She's had these dreams before, and until she did or went to what it was, they continue.? ?Alright, what happens if she happens to manifest again? She's had dreams of angels fighting in this area, and the legends as this states, say that a Wakan Takan fell there. Something bout a winged skeleton of a humanoid was found there. This is some stuff that could cause her too..? Josh shook his head on that question. ?Blaise... for that we will be in the middle of B.F.E. Who will care if she does or doesn't. The old lady that lives on the property is pretty much a recluse and she doesn't window watch from what Terra told me about her. She's a widow or something, and doens't bother people.? Gray sat there quietly listening to his little brother's argument on why this should be allowed, not really seeing any holes in the whole argument either. Josh was right they all needed this if anything to maybe answer some answers to many of questions. Josh continued. ?You know it needs to happen. This prophecy says that this destroyer she's supposed to be was killed in the southern states. New Mexico fits this properly. I think we're going to have a break through if we do this.? Blaise had one last question. ?Why should I let you go alone?? Josh had an answer for it. ?Why not? A small group is harder to track. Just her and me. We go, we take a look, we record what happens, and then that's that. We come back, and call things done on this front. We're hot on information, we need to act before the trail is cold.? Blaise couldn't argue. ?Let me take this up with Annie tomorrow.? Josh gave a hard look at Blaise, time was the enemy of them right the Evening star knew she would still be near where the fight happened. He knew she was near, and he could strike at any time. However, while the guys were speaking together, outside, someone was taking in the cool night air. Alexis Day, their newest friend, and the one that Grayson was the assigned guardian of right now. A woman who was going through a few internal conflicts right now. Conflicts of faith. She had been raised a devout, very religious person. She had joined the convent at the right age. Also, she had become a vampire hunter for that convent, for that religious order. The group had been known as the Den. But then she had left them, and had been saved by a couple of those whom she at one time hunted and wanted to kill. Now they were her best friends, Lia and Virgil, two vampires. She'd come to hang with the KB:NAN to help them. Lately though, this woman's internal conflicts were coming from an attraction. An attraction to a man who would probably never return it. Her guardian, Grayson Brand. While the two brothers had gone to Blaise to speak to him about this trip that Josh thought Dani needed to do, she'd decided to take a break away from it all and head outside for a few breathes. She walked outside the gray and white painted house that served as the Bed and Breakfast style inn and headed for Gray's truck to finish looking for a few of her things that has been mis-packed when they had left the hot springs. She opened the back door to Gray's Suburban truck and the light popped on, blinding her in the late evening-early morning darkness. She turned her head and let her eyes adjust with the light coming from the side. And then she saw it. Sitting in a car across the street was something, no someone. It looked like a woman, black hair and an electronic device pointed up at the windows. Alexis quickly triangulated the positioning, it was aimed at Blaise's window. ?What the hell?? She decided to take matters into her own hands, always having been a girl to do things herself if she could. She walked over to the car, approaching from the back. ?Excuse me, but reporters aren't allowed around here. Especially if they're breaking eavesdropping laws. Even the government can't do that anymore.? The woman in the car put the shotgun microphone down a bit and turned to face her. Alexis saw her face. That face that was so familiar, but at the same time so different. The woman looked like she could be the sister or cousin of her good friend, Dawn McFarlane. This woman, her looks, and her black hair could only mean one person... Layla the Angel of the Night and Conception as they had come to learn to identify her after the events of the last few weeks. Alexis moved quickly and tried to grab the shotgun microphone from her. She grabbed at it and pulled, hoping to pull the headphones that were on the woman's head too. ?Oh no you don't!? Alexis miscalculated though, the wires went to a small recording device first. She pulled it out of the car, and the wires separated from their respective ports, dumping the device on the ground but not harming the woman in any way. Alexis bent down to pick up the recorder, but the woman had other ideas. ?Bitch!? She opened the card door, slamming it into Alexis' head and shoulder, and sending her sprawling onto the ground. The woman then got out, reached down and grabbed the recorder. She started to run down the road, but had to dump her hooker heels a few yards from where she'd put Alexis down. Alexis was a tough one though, she shot back up and started after her. She wasn't going as fast as she pulled out her cell phone and hit a quick dial button... it was calling Gray's phone. Back up in the room, Gray's phone went off, playing a tune it sounded like something soft, but still rock like. Blaise and Gray recognized it as ?Silent Lucidity? by Queensryche. Gray quickly flipped the small phone open and took it to his ear. ?Grayson...? What came back to him shocked him. ?Gray, Alexis. That bitch from the mountain was down here in a car recording you guys talking. I'm after her on foot. I'd advise you guys to get down here! I'm small and quick, but I think she could outlast me in the long run.? Gray walked over to the window and looked out, seeing the fire engine red head chasing after the woman down the street. ?Be right down.? He flipped the phone closed and put it down on the table. ?We got company outside, Alex is in hot pursuit.? Blaise perked up. ?What?? ?The succubus, she was outside with a device to record us. Alexis spotted her and she's running after.? With that, both Gray and Blaise were off and out the door. Ukyo looked at Josh. ?I'll stay with Dani. You're the faster runner.? Josh nodded and looked the other way. He headed out the sliding glass door to the porch that came over the drive way. He then vaulted over the railing and landed on top of the large truck his brother drove. Another hop and he was on the ground and running after them, ahead of the other two who had to come out of the house. Josh had lettered in track in high school and he was quick to gain ground on the two women, but he could only barely make them out by this time. Layla on the other hand, had other designs of her own. She cut down an alley way, Alexis hot on her tail. But then, as soon as she was gone from the view of the others, Layla turned to face her pursuer. Alexis thought she had the upper hand finally. ?Alright, just hand over the recorder and we'll be good to go, alright?? The dark angel sneered at her. ?So, the warrior nun.? ?Vampire Hunter, semi-retired.? ?Yes, one of Gaius' workers. That old Roman is so out of whack with the world today, it's almost funny. But I've got news for you little girl. Although you did more down going to Ishram's band and leaving his as far as the rest of us are concerned.? Alexis moved in though. She brought a strike around, aiming for the temple at the side of the head of what appeared to be human, but she knew was much more. Layla was thousands of years old though, she'd been around the block a few times. She managed to avoid the blow and then bring up her hand and touch Alexis on the face. It wasn't a strike, but it was enough. Layla made a sound like she would be telling a child to be quiet and go to sleep, almost a hiss as it escaped her lips. Alexis fell to the ground. Layla gloated a little bit. ?Well, as a nun, I'd expect you to be like any other repressed and pent up woman. Let's see if we can have some fun.? Layla touched her forehead and then smiled wide. ?You have a thing for the big lupine? Well, let's give you what you want for right now. Sweet Dreams...? At that, Alexis started to moan slightly, and slightly move on the ground. Layla heard voices coming. ?Hmm, no time to play the cat and mouse game. Guess I'll just have to kick this up to high speed.? Layla leaned over and gently kissed Alexis on the cheek. ?This game could have been very fun. But I'll be seeing you around, nun.? She then bolted away. Josh was ahead of the others, but not by much. He hit the mouth to the alley that he'd seen Alexis duck into and she immediately came into the range of his casual feeling and empathy. And it hit him like a brickbat. Alexis was laying there on the ground, almost writhing. Her signals coming off were as if she was sleeping. And she was having... well let's just say she was enjoying herself from the soft sounds that came from her. Gray and Blaise came up right behind him. Gray say Alex down on the ground, writhing in what could be pain and he immediately jumped to her side. ?Alex? You Okay?? He pulled her upper body up by her shoulders, her arms still free and tried to wake her. Josh took a few steps forward and it hit him. He had to grab his head. She was dreaming, strong and well... exotic? Erotic? She'd been hit with one of the spells of a succubus. It's not like you could expect anything less from a creature that was associated with conception in ancient cultures. Just like he could do with Dani at times, Josh was fully engulfed in seeing the dream. He shook his head at the site... his brother and Alexis from her head. Not what he wanted to see right now. Gray continued to shake her, Blaise was using his other senses, his vision that came from his father's side to try to find the succubus while they checked on Alex. Suddenly Alex partially woke up, but only partially. The dream broke from her head, but the feelings were still there. Alex looked up only to see Gray's face over hers. She couldn't control it in her still half dream state. She leaned up, wrapping her arms around his head and pulled him into a rather intense embrace and laid her lips to his. Gray hesitated. He hesitated for a good twenty seconds before he pushed her away. ?You Okay?? Alexis looked up in shock as she finally came to. She instantly went to a bright red color in her face. ?Yes, I'm fine. Go after her.? ?You need to be taken care of.? Josh stepped in. ?You guys shift, you're four times as fast as me. She's got a head start. I'll take care of Alex, get her back and make sure she's Okay. Ukyo can help me. You two go after belfry.? Gray looked over to Blaise. ?Coast is clear based on what I can see. A few drunk homeless dudes, but other then that. Clear.? Josh broke in. ?I'll make sure we got clothes waiting for you.? Both nodded at that, they weren't wearing their combat uniforms of the Order. These clothes were toast. Both started running as their forms shifted and molded into that of four legged beasts... large wolves. Gray a fine gray color. Blaise start white. They headed out at their fasted and quickly, both had her scent and were tracking their prey. Alexis stood up. ?I don't believe this.? Josh smiled. ?I won't tell if you won't.? She looked at him strangely. ?What?? ?You got hit by what would be a classic ability for a succubus. You were put into a sleep, and a... uhm... wet dream.? She blushed again. ?How do you know?? Josh smiled. ?I saw it.? She blushed even more. ?Again, I won't tell if you won't. Let's get you back and make sure you're good to go.? They started to walk back, slowly. Alexis had to ask. ?So you saw it?? ?Yeah.? ?You know who was in it?? ?I've known, no surprise to an empath. You almost eyerape him every chance you get as it is. Dani's even mentioned a few things about it. My mother thought you two were a cute couple until she found out it wasn't like that. By the way, I think he'll remember that kiss for a while.? She looked down at the ground. ?Yeah. For a moment, I almost though...? ?He returned it?? She looked over at Josh. ?Yeah.? Josh smiled wide with pearly white teeth. ?He did.? Alexis looked confused as they got back to the area near the Inn. Josh chalked it up to them having to have a little talk when the time was right about his brother. The alleys in Portland are pretty old school design. There are places where they actually twist and turn like they do in the old movies. And there are places where they come to a dead end. Layla had found one of those dead ends. She slowed a bit as she approached it. She wondered if she had time to turn and find another direction. The force of a large lupine slamming into her back told her no. She fell to the ground, the recorder in her hand fell and slid across the asphalt to be stopped by a white paw of a large worg (prehistoric wolves that were larger then modern day ones). She watched as the wolves shifted form and became a cross between a man and a wolf, but much bigger then the should have been. ?Fucking lupines! If that little nun hadn't have spotted me... never the mind though.? The white one, Blaise growled in a low rumble that let her know the fight was about to begin. If she let it. ?I don't think so, I'm a lover, not a fighter.? Her eyes flared up in a flash that caused both of the crinos formed garou to look away for a moment. When they had adjusted and looked back she had manifested the soft bat like wings that came from her back. It didn't take her long and she was flying away. The one route the two garou would not follow her. Both let out a growl that would only translate to a few select four letter words in the English language and shifted back to their fully wolf like forms. Blaise took the recorder in his maw and they started to run back towards the Inn. Blaise could only think of one thing... Josh was right. Things needed to happen and happen soon. Josh and Dani would need to take that trip. He had to agree now.
