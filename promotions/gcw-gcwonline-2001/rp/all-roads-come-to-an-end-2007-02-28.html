All roads come to an end.

28 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/07 The warm breeze blows across the desert highway that looms like a large snake across the landscape. A single motorcycle rides upon this mighty snake back a Harley Davidson. The sound defined by the roar of the motor. A large man is seen riding upon the Harley as it continues to speed down the road as the waves of heat flow across the road as if the air currents were that of a mighty ocean. The wind blows through his hair as he rides closer. He seems to slow the motorcycle down as an old abandoned gas station comes into view. Slowly he comes to a stop and kicks the kickstand down as the camera pans in close to his boots as he walks outwards from the motorcycle. He looks about as a cell phone rings and the camera rises up to his face as he speaks. The man is Shaman current GCW TV Champion. He lifts the cell phone to his ear as he speaks. “Yeah what is it?” Listening carefully he nods as he walks under the old awning of the gas station and he stands there in the shade. He shakes his head with a disgusted look on his face. “He should have realized I am no walk in the park.” Looking through the old dusty windows of the gas station he smiles as he sees some old items sitting on the shelves and smiles as the door is still open. He pushes the door open and walks inside and looks about. “You know if Thunder wants to think it is an easy walk down the road then that is his choice. Personally I am going to enjoy shutting him up in the ring. The man may be 6’8” 390 lbs, but I have beaten bigger men.” Shaman looks around as he reaches down and picks up an old matchbox car and smiles. He holds the car in his hand and rolls it between his fingers. “You know it is days like this I wonder why the hell they put idiots on this planet.” Shaman hangs up the phone fully. Folding it shut he places it in his pocket. Turning he walks towards the door leading to the garage area. He opens the door peering inside he notices an old 1964 Chevrolet pick up truck. He smiles and walks around it admiring the truck. “Now this is a classic piece of automotive history.” Looking at it he turns and exits the gas station standing outside he pulls his cell phone back out of his pocket and leans against the wall of the gas station. Dialing a number he raises the phone to his ear. “Kawanaha yeah I need to purchase the deed to this gas station on route 66 south eastern California. The town was Kingman check for me I think I’d like to do something with this piece of property. Yeah I know Kawanaha you can lecture me about it later.” Looking around he smiles as he likes what he sees. “Yeah I know there is nothing here, but it is an old classic area and I think some people should revive it.” Shaman nods and responds slightly a few times and then makes his way back to the front of the gas station. “Ok Kawanaha I get the gist of your argument ok. This purchase may not be wise, but give me that opportunity without a lecture. I am using my own funds here. I just want a project. That is fine Kawanaha your advice is noted. We will discuss this later. Just be sure to lock this property and we will look at its potential.” Hanging up the phone Shaman shakes his head as he walks around the gas station. Stopping he sees and old poster in the window as he walks over and wipes the dust from the window showing an old wrestling advertisement in the window, stating wrestling show in Flagstaff November 18th. He smiles as it was obviously old. Looking at the names on the list and shakes his head recognizing one specific name, the name of the man that trained him as a wrestler. Mean Cowboy Jason Lang. Sitting down he remembers the man fondly as he remembers some of the lessons he taught him about wrestling and life. “Old man Lang you taught me so much about wrestling when I was younger. You were done with the business, but I remember you telling me that the business was never done with you.” Getting up he walks back into the gas station and heads over to the window and grabs the poster and gently rolls it up and looks about and smiles. He walks over to his Harley and places the rolled poster into one of his saddle bags. He turns and looks back at the gas station. “You have stood the test of time and perhaps I will stand all tests that lay before me. I am going to make your path into history a grand one old timer.” Reaching up he pats the side of the building and walks over to his Harley and sits down on it. Admiring the gas station and its architecture, He grins looking at each arch and corner. He then straightens his gaze to a single post as it reminds him of something. More so it triggers a memory more than reminding him of something. “Lia Ambrosi aka Shadow I have not forgotten you or the past. You see you easily forget things when you are on top. I am like that undying memory that continues to nag at your conscious.” Looking out at the desert as black storm clouds roll in the distance. His gaze concentrates upon the unknown beyond those clouds. His eyes light oddly as the sun and darkness combine there. ? This a land of confusion Lia. I have brought Mayhem out from under your world. He is a free being and my brother in Oblivion. There is a path of destiny Lia. That path has brought you before the brothers. You shall be judged for many months to come. The GCW will be judged by us and in the end there will be many things for you to not understand, but do and obey. You are not the upper leader you had thought you once were. Your control falters Lia.” Shaman continues to stare off at the chaos that is the storm. As if he sees a future that only he can see. Concentrating on those paths of destiny and the ones he had left behind obviously. He reaches up and rubs his chin as he watches the clouds roll in the distance. Sitting on his Harley Davidson he reaches down and grabs a branch lying upon the ground. He lifts it up carving a sharpened end upon it. He continues to sharpen it with the pocket knife he had pulled from his pocket. Stopping he admires the jagged end and looks at it holding it arms length away. “Lia do you know how they used to stop Vampiric beings in the old days?” dropping the stick down into the opened saddlebag he closes it securely. Folding the pocket knife and places it back into his pocket. “That was the old days Lia. You have come face to face with two beings that belong to Oblivion. We do not play end games Lia. We are the endgame.” Shaman reaches down and starts the Harley Davidson and grins as he looks out over the Horizon. He reaches down into a small pocket on the side of the saddle bag and pulls a pair of sunglasses out and places them on.” There is no road through the light or the darkness. In the end there is always Oblivion Lia. Jimmy Thunder will discover this same road and in the end it will take more than him or his pills to defeat me.” Shaman narrows his gaze as he lowers the sun glasses. “Yes Jimmy I know all about your pills incident. Word travels quickly in the world of wrestling. The question is are you pure. There is no doubt you’re a big strong man Jimmy, but remember I am as big and as strong. I may not have the same bulk you have on your bones, but trust me I am as mean as they come. You want to test your strength and mettle against me then do it. Do not assume to look past me idiot, because I will take you to the last of your limits. The world turns Jimmy and when hell freezes over you will defeat me.” Shaman pushes the sunglasses back up on his nose as he smiles looking around once again. He starts up the Harley and begins to ride down the road slowly as he looks about watching the wind blow across the desert as it blows up tumbleweeds as they roll across the road. Slowing the bike to a stop he tilts his head sideways and looks to the sky as the sun passing behind the clouds glints off of his sunglasses. “It is always ironic when the sun shines down upon destiny isn’t it. After all you reign at the top and then someone comes along and ruins your day. Maybe you should have read your horoscopes a little closer.” Shaman turns and looks at the lightning flash in the far distance. “Maybe I will try a few for you. Your signs read that you are following a path of painful reverie. Destiny shows blood and pain in your path. Your enemies bear watching. Welcome to Oblivion Lia Ambrosi and Jimmy Thunder.” Shaman revs his Harley and then looks forward once again. “Oh yes Virgil you are not forgotten. The gift for you waits in the ring. Pain waits all who cross Mayhem and Shaman. Maybe you should heed the warning and prepare with caution, because the path has been shown and we all walk it now.” Shaman twists his hand as the bike kicks up dirt and gravel as he roars down the road as he hums Ghost Riders in the sky as he drives down the road.
