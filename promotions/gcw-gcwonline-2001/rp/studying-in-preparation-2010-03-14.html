Studying in preparation!

14 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/10 Before you you see a pile of books and the top of a head moving back and forth. Occasionally a book is placed onto the top of the stack. A young man mid 20's walks over with another cart full of books rolls it up next to the huge table. He turns and grabs an empty cart and pushes it away slowly. Suddenly a head popped up holding a paperback book. Shaman is seen looking at the young man curiously. "You for real? Greys Anatomy? Are you shitting me? Where did you go to school TV trivia college?" Shaman flings the book at the guy as the Library guy smirks. Shaman drops his head back down to the book. His eyes scanning over the shoulder and chest area. He traces his fingers along an area where tendons and ligaments connect muscle to the bone. Shaman turns the page and studies a bit more as he picks up a book that reads amateur wrestling 101. He smiles as he thumbs through the book. Suddenly he stops and looks up narrows his eyes as the Old lady Librarian looks over at him and waves while scowling. Shaman shakes his head as he goes back to studying. "Damn old broad has gotta be what 200 years old? She smells like mothballs and stale potato chips. Just sad that they must make that perfume nowadays. Hell i don't think they had perfume in that day." Smirking he goes back to his studying. He lifts another anatomy book as he begins thumbing through it comparing notes and pictures from the two already open. "Never in my life tapped out to an opponent. Won't ever happen again. I am going to have counter for counter hold for hold." "You are just getting old and slow." Shaman snaps his head up looking at the Librarian guy again. "What is your name?" Shaman asks quickly. The Guy smirks thinking eh got the better of Shaman and in his cocky exuberance speaks. "Well my name is Jason Stein." Shaman leans over at Jason and speaks. "Jason what do you want people to read at your funeral?" Jason quickly steps back away from Shaman with speed. He looks at Shaman as he speaks again not realizing he is digging a deep hole. "Yeah well you're going to lose to Tessa Windsor. You don't stand a damn chance against someone quicker and more agile than yourself." Shaman smiles and flings a book with rather unique speed. Jason staggers a bit being hit square in the chest with the book. He looks down in shock as he looks back up at the now standing Shaman. Shaman steps around and looks at Jason and steps by him. "You go tell the old Librarian Mrs. Girsham is it that you may be needing an ambulance if you keep your crap up." Shaman turns and looks around. "Speaking of crap where the hell is the bathroom in here?" Jason points down to a series of tall old bookshelves. "Down corridor B past the National Geographic archives. You should see the Bathrooms." Shaman turns and walks down past the many rows of books. Stopping at corridor B and begins walking down it. He stops and looks at the National Geographic section and admires the huge collection. "Now that is a lot of damn books." Stopping he turns and notices a old door and walks over to it. Reaching down he jiggles the door handle and shrugs as he goes to pull it open the door handle falls off in his hand. "You have got to be kidding me." Shaman leans over and then shrugs dropping to one knee. He puts the handle back in the door turning it as he pulls gently and the door instead pushes open rather than pulls open. He stands up shaking his head. "Oh this is going to be a good day." Shaman walks into the stall prepares do handle his business as he does what he needs to. Sitting there he ponders over several matters. One is that fact that he was beaten by Bryan Mayhem whom he had always beaten in the ring. Not in just anyway, No in the way he had never been beaten before. He had once before gotten in that situation before and passed out before he submitted. This time he tapped. Not something he would have ever thought he would do, but he did and not know why. He begins to ponder several things as he looks around as he finishes. He turns and looks over at the toilet paper roll and notices it is empty with several little strands on it. "Oh what in the hell." He turns and looks around noticing the screws on the wall to the next stall. He reaches under and feels his hands upwards and finds the other roll of toilet paper and slowly pulls some down and off. " When was the last time they put toilet paper in this damn place 1872?" Shaman frowns as he finishes business and flushes the toilet as it clogs up and the water begins to flow towards the rim. "What the fuck now?". Shaman quickly steps away from the toilet as it makes a weird gurgling sound then woosh the toilet flushes just as it reaches the top of the bowl. Shaman shakes his head as he walks out of the bathroom and back to the table as Mrs. Girsham walks up to him and speaks. "Mr. Whitecloud we do not take kindly to threatening of our staff members. What do you have to say for yourself?" Shaman looks at her and shakes his head smiling. "What do i...Wait a damn minute here you old bats in the belfry nut job broad! When was the last time you put toilet paper in the bathroom or had the plumbing fixed?' Shaman looked at her as she seemed taken aback by the sudden shift in authority position. "Well i never!" Mrs. Girsham said as Shaman responds just as quickly. "That part is obvious with the way the plumbing and toiletries are kept supplied." Mrs; Girsham squinted her eyes pushed her glasses up upon her face. "Well if you must know the budget only allows three rolls to that particular bathroom a day!" Shaman smacks his forehead. "look lady that bathroom looks like it was built when this building was built. How old is that damn plumbing?" Mrs. Girsham looks at Shaman in shock. "Might i remind you that this building is fully functional. That plumbing was redone in the 1920's by the finest maintenance people on this fine campus. The current Maintenance people have stated that this facility is well beyond its need for replacement." Shaman looks at her in a bit of disbelief. "Ok wait one second here did you and i just hear the same thing here?" Mrs. Girsham looks at him and nods. "Yes this facility is not in need of repair. It is well within city code." Shaman rubs his temple as he walks back over to his table just ignoring the Librarian. "I just walked into an episode of Twilight Zone meets The Farside." Shaman sits back down and picks up the anatomy book. Looking to the right he notices a wrestling magazine and puts the Anatomy book back down. He reaches lifting the wrestling magazine which has Tessa Windsor on the cover. "Well Tessa we both are going to be at each other again. We had been friends for awhile at least with mutual respect. I trust you know that i don't give a quarter effort to anyone. Hell i bet you are all going to be wondering why i am at a library studying anatomy books." Smiling sadistically he looks around. "Truth is i am seeking ways for repentance. I am going to make people respect the man and not just the capabilities." Shaman holds up a Ju Jitsu book as well. He grins as he looks around as if someone would have been listening. "Been taking Ju Jitsu and learning mixed martial arts submission moves for a few months now and i think it is time i begin adding them to my arsenal of dangerous moves. After all everyone forgot that i was a technical wrestler and playing the big bad monster has left a sour taste in my mouth. I am back in the ring as a singles wrestler against you. I think it is high time i make sure you are reminded of what i am capable of." Shaman stands and walks towards the window as he grabs an anatomy book. He opens it up and smiles. "Imagine the forces and strength needed to tear a tendon from the bone where it is mounted. How about how much fore it will take to pop a shoulder or hip out of joint. Imagine the sound of it. A painful muffled pop or slow painful stretch then free dangling sensation." Shaman smiles as he reaches up rubbing his chin. He flips the page smirking as he stops and runs his finger down the page. His eyes browse over a few sections then stops at treatments. "Did you know they say that it takes more effort to put a dislocated shoulder back into place then it was to pop it out of place? Dislocated shoulders or dislocations are most of the time worse than breaks. Weeks of physical therapy and restrengthening are needed." Shaman grins as he turns from the window and sits back down at his table. Shaman leans forward as he blows some dust off a couple of books. "Don't think for one minute i had forgotten about you Bryan Mayhem. You think you are standing tall and invincible right now. I am going to knock you off that high horse in a bloody mess. Once that is started the beatings shall be something that you will remember. Our previous matches will be nothing to the pain, carnage and destruction that will follow. Your own nightmares will pale in comparison to what i will bring. You know why Bryan?" Shaman shoves a book forward out of his way as he leans forward. "The simple truth is easy. Who knows you as well as your own brother? That is right Bryan i do and i know what makes you tick. I know what weaknesses you have. In fact we know just about everything about each other. They know about me, but they don't know about you. Perhaps i will reveal to them everything or i may simply watch you squirm." Shaman looks around as Jason walks up again gathering books. Jason leers at Shaman who stands up quickly. He grabs Jason and smiles as he simply twists Jason and pulls with one arm up underneath Jason's armpit and the other over his right shoulder crossing his chest. Jason opens his eyes wide in shock as a sickening squishing sound is heard as Jason lets out a silent scream as tears roll down his face. Shaman releases the hold as he looks at Jason who is white as a ghost drop into a nearby chair sitting holding his shoulder. "Wow not much force at all to dislocate the shoulder. Your collarbone looks even a bit more out of place as well." Shaman grins as he moves some books around and opens another up. "Yup can fix that right here." Shaman reads down a paragraph and walks over to Jason grabbing the dislocated left arm twists slightly pulls lining up the collarbone again. he yanks forward then backwards forcing the joint back into place with a muffled pop. Jason's face contorts in a painful expression as his eyes roll back into his head as he passes out. Shaman looks down at him and shrugs leaning Jason over laying him face first on the desk draping an arm up over the top of his head laying across a book. "Looks like a sleeping babe." Shaman looks around as he turns and walks down a side aisle heading towards the side door carrying a few anatomy books. "That worked a lot better than i expected it too. In fact fixing the problem can be just as painful as causing it. Time to go see a Butcher and a few Nurses next." Shaman whistles as he steps out the side door disappearing as the door shuts closed. Mrs. Grisham walks up to the table to see Jason laying face down in a book with an arm draped over a book near his head laying on the table. She shakes her head and walks away. "All night wild parties makes these young people so tired nowadays. I guess i will just let him sleep for awhile. Now where did that big fellow Mr. Whitecloud go?" She walks off humming something to herself as the sun streams through the library windows lighting the many rows of books.
