Drops Of Jupiter (Part Two: Homecoming)

10 January 2011

Date: 01/10/11 Last night, I had that dream again. It was the homecoming dance my senior year in high school. Everything went exactly the same way, like a movie projector playing back my own memories... "How ya'll feeling tonight?" A large round of applause filled the gymnasium as a woman crossed her arms up against the wall. Taking a deep sigh, her eyes darted back and forth through the crowd of humanity that stood in front of her. Multi-colored lights danced across the corroded white walls. The voice was so loud, she couldn't even her own teeth grinding behind her lips. "Lets get this party jumpin‘!" Soon the beginning tones of "Jumpin' Jumpin'" by Destiny's Child brought smiles to the faces of all around her. As she pushed herself off of the wall she completely ignored party atmosphere that surrounded her and instead made her way over towards the concessions table. Staring blankly down at the ground she watched herself walk across the same gymnasium floor she played basketball on earlier that day. Her ankles bent and buckled above four inch heels, one foot at a time. Just another hour or so, and it would all be over, surely it couldn't be THAT bad... "Amanda!" She heard a scream over the music to her left. With a bit of hesitation, she looked over and saw a short blonde girl wearing a beautiful pink dress. Surrounded by other women bouncing up and down with their high heels in hand, the girl in pink couldn't force the smile off of her face. "C'MON! Have some fun!" A phony smile crept across her cosmetically rendered face while she looked at her friends. They looked so happy. That kind of happiness just wasn't meant for her. Not tonight. A simple shake of the head followed by a fake chuckle seemed to please the girl, who waved Amanda away. Seconds seemed like minutes and minutes seemed like hours as she examined the concessions table. A not-so-worthy escape from the terrible music and overly bubble personalities that stood in line behind her. Their lips moved but the sounds were lost into the music as she awkwardly looked over her shoulder. They didn't seem pleased. "My bad!" Amanda desperately tried to scream over the music, but it too was lost. Her attempt was met with a roll of the eyes just before the Destiny's Child came to a close. "Alright..." The DJ's voice broke the dead air, trying to get something over the speakers to let today's homecoming guests know they weren't forgotten. Amanda however stood out of the way, examining her midnight blue dress as the teenagers around her talked amongst themselves. Their words, private conversations that they thought were between them, whispered into her ears as she looked away towards the stage. "That girl is so weird, doesn't she have a boyfriend?" "He goes to a different school." "Yeah, right... and I have a million dollars and a Ferrari." "Maybe she got stood up? You don't know that." "I know I'd never date something that crazy." Being a beautiful girl in high school always brought envy from others, but being quiet and introverted just made their whispers louder. Sure, being in the marching band and running microphones for the school plays didn't scream "most popular girl in school"... but what did? Blonde hair? Blue eyes? Big boobs? "It's time to announce our class of 2000 Homecoming King and Queen!" The words quickly forced the masses to drop what they were doing and head towards the stage. Amanda watched as they all flocked like sheep to find out who was the most popular kids in school. From a distance, she scoffed at the predictability of her classmates, always quick to point out their flaws and stereotypical reactions to mundane tasks. A drumroll sounded as the DJ held an envelope in his hand. "Tonight's Homecoming King is... Jake McGuffey!" From a distance she watched the anxiety, happiness, and disappointment of all as it swept through the crowd. Jake took the stage with a grin from ear to ear. Grabbing his crowd, he held it high above his head for the entire world to see. A mumbled escaped her lips as the drumroll played once again. "What a jackass." "And your Homecoming Queen is..." From a distance her eyes fixated on the envelope. Her heart skipped a beat and all the air deflated out of her lungs. Lips once pressed tightly together opened in anticipation because in just a few short moments... BUZZZZZZZ BUZZZZZZZ BUZZZZZZZ BUZZZZZZZ BUZZZZZZZ BUZZZZZZZ Amanda Harper would wake up. ---- Drops Of Jupiter, Part Two: Homecoming ---- The city of Las Vegas was a desert oasis, a jewel in the barren crown of the state of Nevada. At night it glimmered across the desert like a diamond, but outside of the city things weren't quite so luxurious. Looking out across the barren desert, this was just the type of dive that she could get behind. Just close enough to the city where it wasn't a trek, but far enough away where she felt like she could get away from it all. Staring at her own reflection, the spider bite piercings and lime-green hair looked back at her through emerald green eyes. The textbook Suicide Girl appearance hid a chaotic and emotional mind behind the beauty. The truth was a constant burden on her psyche. Every day since the night she met Vee she'd had to look herself in the mirror, ashamed of what she had done to try and impress the young woman. Maybe she was scared that Vee wouldn't accept her as who she was... or maybe she just wanted the attention. Either way it was a mistake that couldn't be corrected fast enough. How would Vee react? Would she still want to be friends? Could anyone really care about her the way she was? "Jupiter?" The familiar voice of a friend startled her as she daydreamed staring out into the desert. Looking into the eyes of her friend brought a smile to her face. Pushing herself up out of her seat she greeted Vee with a big hug, leaving her victim to wince a bit uncomfortably at first but she accepted it... patting the woman she knew as Jupiter Skye on the back before she released her grip. "Oh my God, Vee... how have you been?" Jupiter smiled behind black lipstick, her face lighting up like a Christmas tree. "It feels like it's been forever." "Yeah, a lifetime." The woman known to most of the world as Vivica J. Valentine responded, taking a seat while glancing around nervously. She was sure she would be noticed. It was only a matter of time. "I've been busy. You know... living the dream." "I called you here because I need to get something off my chest, I need to just throw this out there." Jupiter blurted out before taking a deep breath and smiling. "Yeah?" Vivica responded nervously, crossing her fingers under the table, praying to God that Jupiter didn't find out the truth before she could tell her. Her front teeth came down upon her pink painted bottom lip just as Jupiter spoke. "I'm not who you think I am." Jupiter tries to put her chaotic thoughts into an organized and rational sentence, "My name isn't Jupiter Skye. I mean it is... but is isn't. I go by that, but my name is Amanda Harper. I'm not a rock star, but I'm in a band. Well, I WAS in a band... I guess I'm not in a band anymore but I feel so terrible about everything and not being able to get a hold of you and talk things through has been driving me nuts because..." "It's o..." "No, no, let me finish." Amanda cut her off, "Because you seemed like a big deal or something, and I didn't want you to like feel as if I had different intentions than I did? People were staying away from you for the most part and you just looked like you needed a friend.. aaaaand I was a bit drunk and you know how that goes right? I feel like such a bitch..." "No, no, slow down..." Vivica breathes a sigh of relief as she uncrosses her fingers, "It's fine." "No it's not fine, I've been a total fucking bitch and a liar..." "No! No, I knew." Vivica smirked a bit, "It doesn't bother me any." "What? You fucking knew?" Amanda quickly changed gears, "How?! What?" "I think I've got a pretty good read on people." The Fearless Phenom smirked, the on-edge feeling slowly melting away as she sinks into her seat, "In my line of work, you learn quick. You have to." "In a record store?" Suddenly the entire world came to a halt as those words echoed over and over in Vivica's mind. She'd forgotten all about the record store lie for just a moment. As they both sat in awkward silence Vivica's brain screamed out for an answer that just wouldn't come... ---- One Week Earlier... "You've been quiet since Dangerous Games, ya know." Some would call Ayake Sonoda's voice overly-bubbly, but to those who know her, the sound of her voice the way it had just sounded would be music to their ears. It was a tone that people hadn't heard from her since before AXEL Action stuck his nose in Vivica J. Valentine's business. The Man In Black revelation was a burden off of her shoulders. "Hellooo?" Ayake questioned as she stood behind the former GCW United States Champion, sitting down on the bench in her locker room spinning tape around her wrist. Pulling the roll of tape up near her mouth, she uses her teeth to rip off the final strand before flattening it out to look like the rest. "I'm just going to leave the..." "When was it you?" Vivica asked, never turning around. Never sharing a glance with one of her former best friends. "Who what now?" Ayake scrambled to figure out what The Fearless Phenom was asking her. "The Man In Black." Vivica looked over her shoulder back at Aki-San, "When was it you?" "I... " Stumbling over her own words, she was a bit frightened to fess up, "I shattered the mirror. While you were getting out of the shower. I was the only one with access to the women's locker room." "And you were okay with it?" "I wasn't okay with the way you acting. What was I supposed to do? Kick your ass?" Ayake responded immediately, "I'm not a violent person, Viv." "I am." "Yeah..." Aki-San responded sternly, "I know. Twice." "Sorry about all that, and whatever." Vivica stood up after doing what was possibly the closest thing to an apology that Ayake Sonoda was ever going to get out of her. If there was one word that could be used to describe The Fearless Phenom, Ayake would've used the word 'Prideful.' She'll make mistakes, but swallowing that bitter pill to Vivica was infinitely more difficult than asking her to beat anyone inside of that ring. It was that same bullheadedness that drove Valentine to success though. She was never willing to give up on anything. "I know you are. It's okay." Ayake responded just in time to not get cut off by the ringing of her friends' cell phone. Pulling the usual sounds of Paramore out of her pocket, The Fearless Phenom just glanced down at the name before shoving it back down into her pocket. "Who was that?" "No one." "Your face says otherwise." "It's personal." "Want to talk about it?" "No." "I've got time. You've got time..." "It's not really a big deal." "Then why not just talk about it? You want to make amends, right? You want to make this work again?" Ayake went on, "Then you can't just go around acting like you don't care about anything." "It's just some girl I met, keeps calling me." "You aren't..." "No." Vivica cut her off before she could finish, "Not like that." "Just making sure... we haven't talked in a while." "Her name is Jupiter." Vivica eased into the conversation with as much grace as a tank. "She's in a rock band, met her while we were in Vegas. She keeps calling and wanting to hang out but, you know... I'm not in Vegas more than what, twice a year? If that?" "She doesn't understand that?" "No." Vivica quickly responded, "She doesn't KNOW that." "You lied to her?" "I didn't mean to it just kind of... came out." "Why?" "I just answered that." "But that answer doesn't make any sense. I want the real answer." "I guess..." Vivica took a minute to think about it, it was something she honestly hadn't put much thought into for a long time, "I didn't want her to see the things I did. The people I've hurt. She wanted to be my friend. No one else was exactly begging for the job at the moment." "So you lied to her? Because that's how you make real friends?" "I didn't think it'd get so complicated. I mean you meet someone, you hit it off, you have a few phone calls and they drift off into obscurity." "Unless, you know, they REALLY want to be your friend." "Yeah, but lets be honest Aki-San, how many people REALLY want to be my friend?" Vivica answers bluntly, "I know, believe it or not, that I'm not exactly easy to get along with most of the time... and most of the people who try are people who just want to know someone famous or want something from me. Being who I am complicates things more than they need to be. I just want a pure, honest, NORMAL friendship with somebody. For once." "And lying about everything you are is so much less complicated." "I didn't say I was right." Vivica defended herself, "Just saying why I did it." "When were you planning on telling her?" "Never. With any luck." The Fearless Phenom looked back at her former friend, "Look, I do dumb things from time to time. We've established this." "You know just as well as I do that you're going to have to tell her someday." Ayake responded, "Better now than later, you know?" "Yeah..." Vivica spoke to herself, "If it were only that easy." ---- "Did I ask too quickly?" Amanda breaks the silence herself, "I do that." "No... no, just didn't expect it?" She responded between her teeth with a nervous smile, "Just you know, we're in a bad part of town. Lots of shoplifters and con-artists and the like. You have to be on your toes or else they'll rip you off. Times are tough." "Yeah, you're fucking telling me. After my band broke up I had to get a job to keep my apartment, so I had to take this job at Blockbuster. Now they're closing it and putting a fucking Redbox out front. I don't know how I'm going to make rent in two weeks. I've been looking all over, nothing. Nothing at all." Amanda shakes her head in frustration, "Where do you work at? You guys hiring? I'm kind of in a tough spot Vee, I'll be honest with you." It felt like every pair of eyes in the diner were now squarely staring at her. Vivica felt a bead of sweat rolling down her forehead as she looked around, just to make sure the world didn't stop for a moment. Every second that passed in this conversation, the truth became harder and harder to admit. She was digging herself a deeper whole, hoping to make it to China. "You know just as well as I do that you're going to have to tell her someday. Better now than later, you know?" Ayake's words of wisdom played through her skull like an air raid siren, telling her it was time to give up on this facade and make things right. It was so hard to do. It would complicate things. It would ruin everything... "Actually..." Vivica run her right hand through her hair, "I don't exactly work there." "They fired you, too?! The fuck is wrong with this town?" "Well I didn't get fired or laid off or anything..." "How are you going to pay rent, Vee? You going to be okay?" Amanda's rage quickly turned to compassion as she looked across the table at her friend, "If you need help just ask." "No, no... nothing like that. I'll be fine." "Doesn't sound fine." Amanda tries to talk things though, "That sounded like a lie to me, and I'm a pretty good lie detector myself. You need some help, I'll help you out, really it's no problem. Did you need cash or anything like that?" "No, I'm serious Jupiter." "Call me Amanda, please. That makes me feel like a bitch." "I'm sorry, Amanda... but no. I don't need anything. I'm good. I mean look I got here okay. I have a car and..." Vivica paused for a moment, letting reality sink in for a moment. "...things will work out." "You don't have a place, do you?" Amanda asks the million dollar question, "I can't let that happen." "It's not that I'm homeless or anything like that!" Vivica quickly went on the defensive, "I have places to go, I'm just getting things back in order and that takes a while. I'm fantastic. Living the dream." A forced smile doesn't do a bit to knock Amanda off-course. "Listen, Vee..." Amanda responds, "I feel really, REALLY bad about what I've done and even though it was never a big deal to you because you knew, it's a really big deal to me. It's been eating me up inside and I feel like I need to make it up to you." "But you don't." "I do!" Amanda insists, "I need to clear my conscience and the only way I'm going to do that is if you come stay at my place tonight. I mean look, it's going to get dark soon and you don't want to be out here... and we both know for damn sure you don't want to be out on the streets in Vegas. Even if it's just for the night, I have an extra room. I won't even notice you're there, I have work in the morning." Resting her forehead in her own hands, Vivica feels herself circling the drain... her own lies sending her spiraling out of control until she hits the bottom. The smart thing to do would to just tell her the truth. That she's rich beyond any wildest dreams that Amanda has ever had and she doesn't have a home because she's too stubborn to buy a new one because she just wants her old one back. That she stays in hotel suites every night for free because of what she does for a living and there was never really a rush to get a place to lay her head down for the night because most of the time she's never home anyway. That everything Amanda has ever known about her, including the entire conversation they've had here today where she's poured her heart out about her own lies, has been a lie to her face. Should be easy enough. "Yeah." She closed her eyes and answered with a wince. "Yes? You'll stay?" "Yeah... I'll stay." Vivica sighed as Amanda grinned from ear to ear across the table from her. Looking at her expression, Viv couldn't help but smile with her. One day she was probably going to break the girl's heart, but at least for this moment... she'd just made her day. ---- "And your Junior Homecoming Queen is..." From the stage she simply closed her eyes as it seemed like the entire world was watching her. With her heart banging out of her chest, she repeated the same words to herself over and over again. "Please don't pick me. Don't pick me. Don't pick me. Don't pick me..." "Miss Vivica Valentine!" The crowd cheered as she cupped her hands over her nose and mouth. While most people thought it was to hold back the illusion of shock and the tears of victory, the reality is that she didn't want anyone from her graduating class to read her lips as she screamed "Fuck!" in response to the spotlight beaming down upon her from the rafters. Pulling her hands away from her mouth, she forced an uneasy plastic smile as her fellow student presented her with her sash and tiara. After taking two awkward steps forward, the shaking hands of the girl who'd grow up to become the GCW World Heavyweight Champion grabbed a bouquet of flowers on her way to the microphone. What to her felt like a softball began to rise up in her throat. Anxiety began to set in. She wasn't used to being the center of attention, not to mention public speaking... a far cry from the woman who'd be thrown into the fire of the public eye through her future relationship with Christian Zenith. The music cut off. Now it was all about her, she had to choke out something... Anything. "Uhhhhh...." A suffocating pause came next, plummeting the Highland Park auditorium into awkward silence as she froze in partial fear. Everything locked up. It was impossible to move. "Thank you, so much... I really didn't plan for this... at all." Everyone's eyes were locked on to her like the laser targeting system from Predator. In her mind, that was all she could think about. Being inside that triangle. She needed to get to the chopper. "Go Giants?" An applause erupted from the auditorium as she backed away from the microphone turning her head from the crowd and leaving the stage. The Highland Park fight song played her exit, as she was hoping that the sound of the marching band would cleanse her thoughts. "Stupid. Stupid. Stupid." She spoke to herself as she passed by all her classmates attempting phony congratulatory speeches, "Go Giants? Really? That's all you could think of Viv? You're a fucking idiot..." In that short moment she just got her first taste of the spotlight, and she quite possibly never wanted to do it again. Being charismatic was always the easy part for Vivica, her very existence seemed to bring out the best and worst of high school politics no matter how much she never wanted to be a part of them. She just wanted to be part of the crowd. A far cry from the camera-hogging socialite her mother was. Yet the spotlight ran through her blood, and was as obvious to everyone who knew her as a shining beacon in the night sky. Resistance was futile. ---- …I always just wake up wondering when I was going to get my day in the sun. "Amanda." The booming voice of a middle aged man barged through her sub-conscience, but it wasn't enough to make her react. "Amanda!" Snapping back into reality, the tattooed vixen jumped before spinning around in a panic. Looking into the eyes of a balding madman in a blue polo. This wasn't just any balding madman however, it was Keith Young, manager of Blockbuster Video. Also known as: her boss. "I'm sorry, I just... haven't been getting much sleep lately and..." "No excuses. We pay you to work, not to slack off. Just because they're shutting us down in a month doesn't mean you can take the month off, because if you want to get out of here a month early I can arrange that." Keith meant what he said, she'd seen it happen to other unfortunate souls before her. He was the slave driver. The man who made all the employees scatter in fear when he walked through the door. "If you have time to lean, you have time to clean." "Yes, sir." She answered, biting her tongue knowing damn well that if she says what she really wants to say, she won't make rent this month. "Anything in-particular that you'd like cleaned, then?" In her mind, that last line was a moral victory. "No, as a matter of fact I don't, but I do have a whole bin of new releases that should've been out hours ago. This whole place is falling apart!" "I'm sorry, I'll get right on that." "And tell Dan to take care of overnight returns when he comes in. Hold all my calls, I have work to do." "Yes, sir." And with the slam of the door, a weight lifted off of her shoulders. Everyone at Blockbuster knew that Keith only went into his office to take conference calls, and this one was especially important. This one might let him know what store he's being moved to after they close this one down so they can put a rental machine outside. He was going to be on a call for a while, and that mean that she could work in peace. Finally, freedom to work at her own pace. Six bins sat on the counter, sorted by category. Action, Sci-Fi, Horror, Drama, Comedy, and Sports... in that order. Walking up to the last bin, she pulled it off the chest-high counter and carried it over towards the sports section. Everyone started with the sports section because it was the lightest and the DVDs were easier to fit onto the shelf. While every other section has dozens of movies brought into the store every week, Sports has just a few. Three. Maybe four. Today there were five. MMA Fights. Documentaries. Wrestling Pay-Per-Views. Nothing of any real interest to a girl who was as much in touch with the sports scene as she was with the current pop music landscape. She was an independent woman through and though. She liked her movies and her music just like she liked her water and whole organic foods, untouched by the scourges of their respective industries and allowed to flourish the way they were intended. A very far cry from the over-priced and over-produced garbage she found herself force feeding the masses on a regular basis. As she dropped the bin to the ground with a thud, she was curious on what type of wretched thing she was going to pull out of that bin today. Maybe a NASCAR documentary about the rise of the sport? The people in Las Vegas sure seemed to love their ultra-conservative, all left-turn, redneck racing cars. Or how about an NFL Films feature on the Arizona Cardinals: A Study In Mediocrity? Whatever was inside, she certainly wasn't looking forward to trying to act like she cared about whatever topic the next customer was going to try and start a conversation about. If she could just get back into a band, she thought to herself, she could finally escape retail hell and do maybe the only job she'd ever love for the rest of her life. Instead she was stuck at Blockbuster Video, reaching into the bin of a thousand terrible movies to pull out the next clunker titled... Global Championship Wrestling Presents: DANGEROUS GAMES. Great, speaking of redneck agendas... let's start off the day with ultra-sexist masochism and dick measuring contests featuring the likes of GCW "Superstars" Andy Murray, Jay Terror, and... And... "No way..." She said out-loud to herself as a pair of piercing blue eyes looked back at her from the DVD case from beneath long blonde and black hair. "...That can't be, can it?"
