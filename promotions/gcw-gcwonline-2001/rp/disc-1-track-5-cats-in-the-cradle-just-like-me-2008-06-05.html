I don't know when, but we'll get together then You know we'll have a good time then ~x~ January 13th, 2004 2:15 AM Amber Lynn Ryan was a sophomore in high school; she had soft brown hair that hung just past her shoulders. When she left the party she forgot her jacket, but if there was ever one thing she didn’t want to do, it was going back there. Her boyfriend Tony was back there. She really liked Tony and all, but like most boys their age his only concern was what was in her pants. So now instead of hanging out with her friends, she found herself walking home at two o’clock in the fucking morning, shivering. A sudden urge overcame her and quickly she glanced behind her. Whether it had been teenage hormonal paranoia or what she had the uncanny sensation that she was being followed. Glancing around behind her all she found was the buildings she had passed, a succession of streetlights and a lot of darkness. With the sudden panic withdrawn, her thoughts returned to Tony or more so what he said. “It’s obvious I don’t want you for your body, but what the fuck after two years I figured we could finally fuck.” It wasn’t enough that Tony had tried to pressure her, but he knew she was self-conscious about her body. When all the other girls had physically grown up, she still passed off as an eleven year old. A noise grabs her attention, and as Amber turns she sees a black Monte Carlo approaching. The car pulls to a halt beside her, and in the darkness a voice speaks. “Hey, you need a ride somewhere?” “I’m okay.” Amber replied quietly. “I’m just on my way home.” “Well the streets are no place for a girl your age at this time of night.” The man in the car responds. “I’m not a little kid.” Amber exclaims. “I didn’t say you were.” The man begins. “I figure your about sixteen years old, which would make you old enough to know how dangerous it is in LA at two in the morning.” After a moment’s hesitation and against better judgment Amber approaches the car. “Well, I suppose it would be nice not having to walk.” The man reaches across the front seat and opens the door, Amber smiles as she meets the man’s deep black gaze. “My name’s Roy, what’s yours?” “Amber.” She replies slipping into the car. “My name is Amber.” “Nice to meet you Amber.” Roy comments, smiling to himself. “Really nice to meet you.” ~x~ My son turned ten just the other day He said, "Thanks for the ball, Dad, come on let's play Can you teach me to throw", I said "Not today I got a lot to do", he said, "That's ok" And he walked away but his smile never dimmed And said, "I'm gonna be like him, yeah You know I'm gonna be like him" And the cat's in the cradle and the silver spoon Little boy blue and the man on the moon When you comin' home dad? Keegan however seems entranced somewhere in a memory, she had never seen a patient as enraptured in their past as Keegan was. On paper he looked like a typical depression patient, but in the flesh he was so much more. His eyes spoke of an inherited sadness, as though the weight of the world rested upon his muscled shoulders. Dr. Copeland’s eyes slowly look over the man before her. As soon as she realizes what she is doing, she forces herself to stop and returns her gaze to her legal pad, making a note to herself; Patient struggles with a set of morals he does not understand. “I’m sorry.” Keegan comments, catching a startled Dr. Copeland off guard. “For what?” “For wasting your time like this every week.” Keegan slouches back in his chair, “I’m sure there are plenty of other things you’d rather be doing then talking to a head case at four o’clock in the afternoon.” “Well it is my job.” She replies with a smile. “And besides, I think we’re really making progress here, I haven’t upset you yet and you haven’t yelled at me yet.” Her comment forces a smile on Keegan’s face. “I guess that’s true.” “So in the interest of keeping things civil today, why don’t you just tell me what’s on your mind.” Dr. Copeland explains, “Last time we talked a little about your sister Carson, but you never did tell me about the rest of your family.” “There’s not much to tell.” “Well what about your father” What is he like?” She probes. “I’m just like him.” Is the only answer she gets in return. “You mean he’s just like you?” “No, I mean I’m just like him.” ~x~ Well, he came home from college just the other day So much like a man I just had to say "Son, I'm proud of you, can you sit for a while?" Every morning of every day, Caleb would awaken at 7:30 AM, shower, shave and dress for the day that lay ahead; meanwhile Katarina would prepare a big breakfast for the entire family. Keegan sat in the living room, doing what he did best, and that was nothing. On the television was the morning news. As his father walked down the stairs last night’s sports scores were just finishing their recap. Caleb stopped and took a seat to finish out the news with his son as his wife finished off breakfast. “This morning Amber Lynn Ryan, 15 of Los Angeles was found raped and murdered. Ryan, was last seen at two o’clock in the morning after leaving a party with her friends. At this time, there are no suspects in the case, but the police remain optimistic.” Keegan and Caleb exchange glances, Caleb is the first to comment. “What is a girl doing out by herself at two o’clock in the morning?” Caleb shakes his head in disgust. “And people wonder why kids are always showing up dead, why if the girl showed any responsibility at all she’d probably still be a live.” “Everything that happened to her was her own fault.” Keegan replies his father nods in agreement. Their verbal tirade on the dead girl comes to a conclusion as Carson comes down the stairs, and Katarina pops her head in to tell them that breakfast is ready. Carson makes her way into the kitchen, Caleb and Keegan follow suit. ~x~ I've long since retired, my son's moved away I called him up just the other day I said, "I'd like to see you if you don't mind" He said, "I'd love to, Dad, if I can find the time You see my new job's a hassle and kids have the flu But it's sure nice talking to you, Dad It's been sure nice talking to you" And as I hung up the phone it occurred to me He'd grown up just like me My boy was just like me ~x~ Present Day After a long day of therapy Keegan did what he always did. He came home, grabbed some left overs from the night prior and sat himself on the couch in the living room of his apartment. This afternoon he was chowing on some day old Dominos, when his phone began to ring. Keegan brought himself to flip off the television as he rose to get the phone. Crossing the living room he halts near the kitchen answering the phone on the third ring. “Hello?” “Keegan, it’s your mother.” The warm voice of his mom brought a smile to his face. “Hey ma, how’s it going?” “It’s going.” She replied. “So when will we get to see our grandson?” “I don’t know yet.” Was all he could say to her. “Your father and I miss you Keeg, we haven’t seen you in awhile. We wanted to make sure you were okay.” Her voice held a certain sadness, there was something more she was not saying to him and Keegan knew it. “I’ve been really busy is all, I’ll be by as soon as I can though.” “Well as long as you’re okay.” “Yeah, I’m fine.” “I’m sure your father would like to talk to you, do you want me to get him?” “Actually mom?” Keegan began, his voice was thick with hesitation. “I gotta go, but I’ll call you back, and I promise I’ll be by soon to see you.” “Oh” okay.” Her voice was thick with sadness, but she held back most of her emotions. “I suppose I’ll talk to you later then, I love you Keegan.” “Love you too mom, bye.” “Goodbye.” Katarina replies ganging up the phone. She peaks her head around from the kitchen table and peers into the living room where Caleb sits on the couch watching the evening news. “Keegan is doing well, he says he’ll be by soon to see us.” Caleb nods, “That’s good hun.” “Would you like some coffee?” She asks, while she is already out there. “Nah, I’m fine.” Her husband replies. “I think I’m going to head upstairs for a nap, I feel awfully tired right now and I don’t know why.” “I’ll come get you when dinner is ready.” Caleb nods again as he rises from the couch. He stops to give his wife a kiss before heading upstairs. Katarina sighs to herself, thinking back to her conversation with her son. It had been a very long time since Keegan had come home, and in all honesty he probably wasn’t going to be anytime soon. But, she could hope after all. ~x~ And the cat's in the cradle and the silver spoon Little boy blue and the man on the moon When you comin' home son?
