Confessions of a Demon

26 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 26th, 2008 No matter what kind of car you have, or how good it runs, or how fast it is, there are still a few things that need to be done routinely to make sure that it stays in top condition. Oil changes, checking fluids, replacing windshield wipers, and last but certainly not least, rotating your tires. A simple procedure of just switching the tires so that the tread doesn’t wear down on one side, it saves the life of the tires tenfold. It takes about 15 minutes once the car is up on the jack, and the mechanic is working on it. Sometimes less if you get to a smaller place where two guys to work at it. It’s the waiting at the bigger dealerships, and tire places that makes this routine bit of maintenance worth procrastinating. Which is what I did. Well, got forced to do. There’s this girl, Cami, who I’ve been sort of dating for the past month or so. She set an appointment for me, and never told me what it was until today, when she said "Ok honey, go get your tires rotated." So, having nothing else to do because I’d left that block of time open for whatever "appointment" she had set for me (hoping it was sex, obviously), off I went to the tire shop. The thing that I forgot to realize about a woman setting an appointment for a tire rotation, is she’s going to go to the biggest AD in the phone book, because obviously bigger means better. In the female psyche anyways. So, here I sit. In the waiting room of a Big O Tire shop, with my laptop and Ipod. I’d been there for about a half hour, with another two and a half to go, or so they told me. Could be more, could be less. In the room with me were three other individuals. A family it looked like. The mother and daughter both sat on the same side of the room as I did, and the father across from us all. All of them obviously had an obesity problem, and they were apparently mormon. How did I know this you ask? Here. Listen. "Did you happen to talk to Brother Sinclair about class on Sunday?" the mother said. "Yep. He agreed that we need to discuss the Savior as pertaining to the Book of Mormon more." "Daddy, what was it like on your mission?" And that’s about all I can tolerate before I tune them out. I can’t stand preachy mormon’s. And I especially can’t stand fat, preachy HICK mormons. So, I continued to work on my laptop. Warpath was coming up, and I had to face off against Clyde Fox, yet again. Thankfully, I’d taken care of his will, and drive to fight a couple weeks back. Burning ones possessions seems to have a way of completely stripping someone of all their emotions, and making them a void of nothingness. Especially when that person happens to have the mind of a six year old. What six year old do you know who would be ok with losing ALL of his toys?” Exactly. "That a MacBook?" The voice snapped me out of my own thoughts, yet again, and I looked up. "Excuse me?" I asked to the fat man. "That’s a Mac right??" I blinked twice. "Um, yea. It is." "Those wouldn’t be compatible with our church family history library software, but they do look ver’nice." He said, scratching his large, pudgy nose. I really didn’t care, and I wanted to tell him that, but I also didn’t feel like starting in on him. "Yea, it works for my needs." "I like the color. It’s pretty." The daughter chimed in. We’ll call her baby Shamoo. Now, the laptop, if anyone knows anything about MacBooks, is white. Plain, white. So, apparently a white laptop was very appealing to baby Shamoo. "I agree Darcy, it is very pretty. So much cleaner than most of the other laptops out there right now." Momma Shamoo chimed in. That was just brilliant, baby Shamoo could no longer be named that, because now she had a real name. Darcy. "Do you keep your scriptures on there at all Mister?" Darcy asked. I’d had enough of the banter, so I decided to attempt to end it, and fast. "So, do you still have your food storage, or did you eat through that already?" The family looked at me, a bit shocked. And then, to my surprise: "Actually, we have a wonderful food storage built up. We’re prepared for just about anything," replied the father. What the hell? He had completely disregarded my insult. You’ve got to be kidding me! I opened my laptop back up, and proceeded to dive back into my own thoughts. Clyde was in for a real surprise at Warpath. Sources had told me that he was seen at a Toys R? Us within the past few days, trying to pick up some kids to molest. That’s SICK Clyde, I don’t care if you think you actually are six, you still look like a twenty six year old? "So have you ever heard of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day?" the mother started to say, but I cut her off before she could finish. "Yes. The Mormons. I’ve heard of you. I’m not interested." Short, to the point, shut them up. Fox was a broken man now. And all that was left to do now was to destroy him in the ring at the PPV. Then, on to bigger and better things. And that wouldn’t be that hard, because Fox seemed to lack any sort of energy to want to fight. "I don’t think you should be talking to my wife like that." I looked up again, and closed my laptop, setting it on the ground. "Look. I’m not here to make friends. I’m not here to learn about your fucking religion. And I’m certainly not here to be your weight loss instructor, no matter how badly all three of you need it. I am merely here to get my tires rotated, and leave. So if you would be so kind so as to SHUT THE HELL UP, then we could all be in this room in peace." There was a brief silence in the room. After a few moments had passed, I figured they had heard me clearly, and leaned over to pick up my laptop. "Our father in heaven?" Oh you have got to be kidding me. "We ask thee at this time to please bless our friend, that his heart may be softened?" It was baby Shamoo. She was praying for me. "We want him to know how much we love him, for he is our brother?" This is priceless. I continued leaning forward, but didn’t grab my laptop. Instead, I continued forward until I fell over, and started to convulse on the floor, drooling out of the side of my mouth (for effect obviously) and screaming profanities. "OH FUCK, FUCK SHIT COCK-GOBBLER. OH RAMMALAMMADINGDONG SHLOMAFRAMASAPHINE COCAINE, I LOVE THE COCAINE!" I continued to spasm, until, just as I had predicted, the father got down beside me and proceeded to lay his hands on my head. By this time, I’d earned the attention of the desk guys, and a couple of the mechanics who had stopped to come and see what was going on. "OOOOOOHSATANHEARMENOOOOOOWWWWWWW!!!!" I continued to howl at the top of my lungs, trying not to break character. The father started to "cast demons" out of me. I lunged at him, grabbing him by the throat. "IJUSTWANTABODY!" Then, in a much deeper voice. "I JUST WANT A BODY!!!!!" I dropped to my knees, and stiffened my entire body, eyes opened wide. In a calm voice I said, "I am the one that dwells within, LUCIFER." I hissed the last part for dramatic effect. "DEAR JESUS, IN THE NAME OF THY HOLY POWER WE ASK THE TO CAST THESE DEMONS OUT OF THIS MAN!" screamed the father. I cocked my head at him, eyes still wide as saucers, and blinked. I stood, and walked straight up to him, and got about four inches from his face. "I. Am. Satan." And then I spit in his face. I proceeded to thrash about, again. By this time, the mother and daughter are starting to show a little bit of fear, and the father is still trying to lay his hands on my head. Finally, when I couldn't take it much longer, I allowed my body to relax, and started laughing hysterically. "Haha! You should have seen the looks on your faces! Hahaha!!" .~. The door slammed behind me as I walked into my condo. "Honey? I found a great way to get my tires done faster!!" Watch out Clyde. I’m insane. Fucking, insane.
