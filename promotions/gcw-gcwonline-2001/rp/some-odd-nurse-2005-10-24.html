Some Odd Nurse

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/24/05 Outside of the window is a beautiful day, and Coyle is fixed over that. Here’s a man being forced to lay on a bed in a hospital, instead of being allowed to be in the comfort of his own home. He’s healed from all of his injuries, but the battle-scars are still ever-so intimidating. His assigned nurse has rest assured that he stays in the hospital, because she grew infatuated with him. She didn’t want him to go out and embarrass himself. For once, he was hideous-looking. After a long day-dream through the endless stare into the window, Coyle sighed. He wanted to be back out there, back on the run, back to working out, back to getting ready for another match; he didn’t want to be restrained from everything anymore. His nurse walked into the room, and announced her salutation. Already use to the repetitive entrance of this nurse, however, Coyle just muttered a “hey”, not putting much meaning or thought into it. And she would proceed forward to help him every time. The nurse was a bit awkward, it even frightened Coyle to a certain extent. But what was he supposed to say? It wasn’t like he could just come out and say, “hey, you, you’re weird” nevertheless, it did cross his mind a few times. There was something about her though, something particularly odd. Coyle’s cell-phone rang. Normally, patient’s weren’t allowed to have cell-phones in this hospital, but his nurse allowed it. He answered it, struggling to say hello because of his terrible cough, but eventually managed to give a good greeting. On the other line was his agent, Marky Rahl. But, Marky had no service. Coyle kept trying to repeat himself so Marky would hear him, but there was just no prevail. His nurse was in the corner trying to listen, as she pretended to fold his blankets. Coyle, “I give up.” He said it in an angry fashion, chucking his phone across the room. There was no crack or break, but there was a jump and a stutter from the nurse. Nurse, “You alright, Mr. O’Conner?” Coyle looked over to the young miss by just moving his eyes, not his head. He huffed a long huff, and proceeded to tell her that everything was alright. Nurse, “Oh, alright sir, well if there is anything you need me to do, just call for me?” Coyle, “Yeah, will do.” The nurse walked out of the room while she shook her hips. Coyle couldn’t help but watch her do this, it was appealing. She turned the corner and left his sight, and it made him think. Coyle, “Nooo?” He chuckled to himself. Forgetting about that though, he rolled back over to look out of the window. Instantly, he became lost again, like last time, just day-dreaming. What exactly he was day-dreaming of now, not even he knew, but he was getting caught-up into it. Then, again the nurse walked in. This time, when she walked in, she stopped at the door and just looked at him. In her right hand was the handles of a paper bag, with the word “Costume Bazaar” streaming on the sides of it. Coyle looked down at the bag, then back up to her face, then back down again, and back up again. Coyle, “Umm, can I help you?” She approached him slowly as a seductive look appeared on her face. Coyle, “Miss, you alright?” She shook her head, still making her way over to him. He didn’t panic, but he started to wonder what the hell was going on. He couldn’t figure it out. Eventually, she made it over to him, and climbed on top of him. What was going on? He had no idea. She did. The nurse proceeded to get comfortable, and ripped off her hat. Her long hair fell slowly and angelically, capturing Coyle’s attention significantly. He was stuck on it all, he knew what she wanted now. She lowered herself to him, and didn’t hesitate to start. [ The following morning? ] Coyle woke up in his hospital bed, and looked around. He was by himself, much like yesterday, but with a paper-bag titled “Costume Bazaar” next to his bed. One eye decided to quirk itself, making him wonder if what happened was a dream or if it really happened. Coyle, “It had to of, the bag is here.” Then he looked around. The suspicious nurse was gone; no odd appearances, no nothing, she was simply gone. He sat up and rest his feet on the floor, hunching over as he braced himself with his knees and his elbows. Repeatedly, he would look over to that mysterious bag, and would consider checking it out. He never actually stood up to go grab it, but he considered it a great deal. What the hell did the nurse get him? What the hell happened last night? When the hell could Coyle go home so things like this won’t happen anymore? Enough was enough, Coyle needed to solve at least one problem in his head. So, with a little hesitation, he stood up, and walked over to the bag. He bent over to pick it up, and saw what was an “S” emblem on the inside. Coyle scratched his head. Coyle, “Is that a Superman shirt?” He grabbed the shirt, and shook it out. The shirt ended up folding out to a costume, and it puzzled Coyle. Coyle, “A Superman costume” What’s this for?” Resting next to his foot was an index card. He was able to read it, barely, but it said ? for this week at WorldWide, become the GCW’s superman.” Coyle, “What!” Fuck this, I need to get outta? here?” He packed up his things, quickly, and walked out of his room. He didn’t check out, he just walked out. Coyle, “I need to get to Denver.”
