Like the contrast of the stark, cold snow falling in the night around them compared to the fine artistic flair of the Kaiseki, her mind was awash with conflicting wants and desires. The legacy of her father loomed large, made that much more achievable by a chance to truly live up to his name...contrasting the doubt and uncertainty she had over facing Shadow once again. Aimlessly she stared into the falling snow, each snowflake like it's own possibility in her mind...
