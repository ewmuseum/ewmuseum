Strange Vacation

11 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/09 The breeze flows across the open grass freely. Each wave of grass rocks back and forth with ease. The mountains in the distance and the clouds slowly moving over them. Across the fields walk two men wearing jackets. The air is seemingly a bit crisp as a deer or two go running by the men as if they weren't even there. One of the men stop and looks at the deer running by as the larger of the two pay no second thoughts to the deer. The first man turns and begins walking again as they seem to be heading towards a cabin. Both men walk up to the cabin entrance as voices are heard from the inside. Two men known in the wrestling circuits as Darkbringer and Michael Stryfe sit on a huge couch watching a football game. They both look over their should back at the two men. "Bout time you guys made it back." Says Stryfe. Shaman removes his coat first and then is followed by Jorge Samuelsson. Samuelsson looks at the screen and then at Michael Stryfe. "You could have at least watched a real mans sport. Thats not football, football is what you Americans call soccer. Stryfe stands up and looks Samuelsson eye to eye. "Wanna make something of it tiny?" Both men seeth as Shaman steps forward and pushes them apart as he walks through them. "Shut it both of you!" Shaman groans in agitation. "This is a vacation not some sort of amateur our at the bar room brawl." Both men shrug and turn sitting down. Samuelsson pulls a chair up for himself to sit in. Shaman leans back in the recliner he had chosen clasping both hands. "So i am to face the Masked Mexican or otherwise known as 3M. Many contemplations have come across for me in this match up. Speed and Agility versus my abilities of Power and Technical prowess. Then again there is one thing he may not have thought of." Shaman smiles as he looks over at everyone else who seems to be listening."My sadistic side." Darkbringer looks at Shaman and shakes his head. "3M kind of sounds like a bad M&M commercial." Shaman looks at Darkbringer smirking and shakes his head. "No this guy should be taken very seriously. He has put together quite an impressive streak." Shaman reaches up and rubs his chin thinking very carefully. "Who have i faced in the past that i could compare this guys abilities with. After all he has gone up against Cheap Labor recently. So watching back on all of these videos of this guy and ranging back on my own experiences there are some similarities." Samuelsson simply yawns and leans back as he and Stryfe shrug as Darkbringer looks about for something. He finds a magazine and lifts it up and looks at it and then throws it at Shaman. "Damn man get with the show you stated it was supposed to be a vacation." Shaman raises his hand and nods. "Ok ok what were the ball game scores?' Stryfe turns and looks at a laptop as he speaks." Alabama wins and becomes #2 over Texas who has dropped one. Florida gets a squeaker over LSU. Boston, Minnesota, and St.Louis eliminated in baseball. Too many games for the pros to mention. Alot of surprise wins." Shaman stands up from the recliner and walks to the window. He places an arm against the wall and leans as he stares out of it looking out into the fields near the cabin. On the other side of the cabins is thick forested woods. He smiles slightly looking at it. "Strange this was my victory against the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse. This cabin, these lands, and the future. I joined them at Darkens urging. Where are they now? Gone and yet here i still remain forging my roads and future. It is strange to think back on things a bit." His eyes focus on the edge of the treeline as he remembers. "Thinking back coming to GCW i wasn't sure what to expect. Alot of the guys here were tiers above the guys i went up against in all of my previous homes. Now i see them as equals guys i go to the back locker rooms with and have wars with in the ring. Whether we like each other or not in GCW we all in one form or another give each other grudging respect. Still Dangerous games is coming and to wrap my hands around it and grasp some sort of victory is the final goal. Finding the grounds to become better than what i am and what we are as Mortifera is the ultimate goal above all else. Fighting this useless uphill battle is growing tiresome it is time to make a statement." Shaman takes a deep breath as he and Samuelsson look at each other and nod. Samuelsson stands up and walks to the refrigerator and opens it grabbing four beers. He turns and walks back to everybody and hands them all beers and raises one. "To each other and our futures for whatever they may bring." Shaman opens his and takes a slow drink. He smiles slightly as he turns to the group. "Yes the future where GCW is put on its ear by what is to come. Do we fear anyone as Mortifera? No we do not. Should we ever be underestimated? No we should not. Are we always underestimated? No we are not anymore. Shaman we begin making it known our intentions? Yes we shall make them known forever more." Shaman turns and walks away from the group and he places his hand on top of his beer as he places it down and speaks. "There was a time when i was a child of a man, Now there is a time when i am an adult of a man. The strength in these battle hardened bones whisper that there is anger and rage rooted deep inside. Shall i release it upon the world and realize that it is ever more in my power to make these changes." Samuelsson looks at Shaman after he finishes. "Do you ever shut up with the poetry? It is giving me a headache. I swear I'd rather deal with a case of the Swine Flu than keep hearing that." Chuckling Michael Stryfe looks at them. "You think that was bad should have listened to the guy named Troy Walcott." Darkbringer laughs as he looks at them both. "No matter the choices you will do fine. Win or Lose you are both Mortifera and nothing will change that will it." Darkbringer turns and sits down as the Fourth Quarter of the Patriots-Broncos game begins. Camera fades to static as Shaman continues to stare out the window.
