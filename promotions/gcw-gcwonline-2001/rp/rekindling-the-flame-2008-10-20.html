Rekindling the Flame

20 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 20th, 2008 Her blouse barely touched her knees standing and rode even higher in the chair behind the desk. With lips pursed and eyes sunken, she answered, 'There's really nothing I can do for you Jay. You don't seem to want to change.' Jay eyed up Anita Baker, took in her enhanced red lips and stunning blue eyes, 'I don't need to change though. Why change?' 'Why?' She paused, thought for a second on an answer she already knew, 'Because you want to be with her still. Don't you get it? She doesn't want the guy you are. Not Jay Terror. She wants Jamison back.' Her eyes shut through frustration, a trait that a psychiatrist was rarely supposed to express. 'I mean -' 'Remember though, remember? We buried that phase of my life. We moved on. I can't just - no. I can't.' 'Sacrifices Jay.' 'Sacrifices? I sacrificed though. I always sacrifice. Haven't you seen? I'm the champion now. The big champion. Burying Jamison was the best thing I've ever done. How can anyone expect me to bring him back? I mean, I have to fight Jordan now. Jordan White. He hates me. He wants me. Jamison can't be the guy he meets in the ring. No.' 'See, I can't help you Jay. This is on you.' 'This is your fuckin' job Anita. Come -' She cut in, stood from her desk, and adjusted the khaki blouse. Her eyes tightened and her lips opened wide with fire sitting on her tongue. 'Get out of my office Jay. You wanted my help, I tried to help. It's not my job to fix you anymore.' 'Anita -' 'No Jay, get out. I tried, really. Maybe one day you'll get it.' That was that. Jay stood, half understanding of the happenings and the other half disgruntled. He heard what she had said, made an effort to understand but something still stood in the way of comprehending her. With one last look and a half nod, he turned and walked out of her office. Anita sat back in the chair, visibly shaken by the incident, and grabbed at her phone at once. She dialed. Two rings before 'Steven, hey. He came to me.' A pause, one for listening, 'Yeah, it was about her. Of course.' She set the phone down and sighed. She could move lands with the wind exiting her lungs. __________ Marble floors and winding staircases were typical. The chandeliers, diamond as usual, were becoming customary. The people, he still didn't know them. There were those that whispered, those that drank, those that ate. Beyond that, they were just people reaping the benefits of knowing a powerful man. That man, Steven Caldera, stood in the center of the floor calling the herd to order. He raised his glass, grinned devilishly, and opened his lips. 'Don't worry; I'm just looking for a second of your time tonight.' He paused and looked confidently at Jay. Jay simply nodded. 'I want to thank you all for coming out tonight. It seems that this is becoming a traditional thing as of late. Parties for an important person I mean. I'm glad of it too. Folks, I'd like you all to recognize the reason for tonight's festivities.' A long pause now, Jay knew where he was going. 'A certain individual has risen to a level that many below him wish they could attain. He's fended off some of the greatest names in the wrestling industry and smiled the entire way up the ladder of success. He's an Outlaw but most of you know him as "The Established" Jay Terror.' Applause begins from the back of the room and dominoes to the front men and women. They face Jay with mouthfuls of self respect, respect that Jay sees straight through. He raises his glass of Jack Daniels anyways. 'Enjoy your night and remember why we're here. Remember what he's done and what he's going to do at Dangerous Games to Jordan White.' Applause for Caldera now. Immediately, he finds questioning people. Jay disappears in the crowd and finds the wall. A chair welcomes him. With his knife, a man slices the silence, 'Congratulations Jay. You've been quite impressive.' A swig of Jack, 'Thanks.' 'You know, I'd like to do a story on you sometime. For the paper. Nothing too complex, just something for the world to see.' He poked his head up, looked at his young features, muscle shirt in particular, and rolled his eyes. 'Maybe. I'll let you know.' 'It's for the Times. Everyone'd read it.' 'Exactly. I'll get back to you.' 'I don't get you Jay. You're in the big time, a huge name these days. A story like this would make you immortal. It'd make you known by people who don't even pay attention to the ring. Don't you want fame? Fortune? Ladies?' 'I got them. It's cool.' He cocked his brow and fixed his fancy sweater, 'You think so. See that girl over there.' He pointed; she was skinny and blonde, half falling out of her seat. 'Girls like that. She's mine tonight but I bet you could get ten of them in a night with the right exposure.' 'I guess I'm missing out.' Another roll of the eyes and a sip of Jack. 'Here's my card Jay. Just give me a call. Something. We'll set it up, it'll be great.' As he extended his arm, the hand of Steven Caldera grabbed onto his shoulder. The man in the muscular sweater looked behind him with a slight grin. 'Steven, hey.' 'George, poaching on talent tonight?' 'Just meeting the man of the month.' 'More than a month George, this guy's bound to be man of the year at the rate he's going.' Jay watched their back and forth, he took a sip bigger than a swig. George was next. 'I was just letting him know about the good things a well written story can do for you. He seemed interested.' 'I'm sure. If you don't mind -' 'Of course.' George caught the hint and shook both hands. He was gone before they knew it. Caldera's eyes met the back of Terror's head; he was tearing the business card. 'Don't get caught up with guys like him, bad news.' 'I figured.' 'You doing alright Jay? Levelheaded?' Stirring the white shreds of paper, 'You know me, always levelheaded.' 'Jay.' His head came up, his eyes were stern and furious, 'You know me Steve. You know me. Shit's legit.' 'Jay, I got a call. I heard something about you and Caroline, about how you were still trying to work things out or something. Was that right?' His eyes became more furious, everything in his body was tense, 'You got a call?' 'Jay, is it true?' 'The truth is a fucked up way of telling you something you don't want to hear.' Caldera shook his head, 'I told you that we need to focus. We need to get everything in line so it works in our favor. You know that.' 'Everything's in order, it’s fine. You know I hate Jordan. You know what I'm going to do to him.' 'I don't think you want to do it though. I know what you're going to do; I have seen what you can do. But somewhere throughout this whole thing I think you lost your focus, lost that disgust. I mean, he's done shit to you and I think that focusing on getting Caroline under your wing again has made you forget that he's the reason for her leaving.' Terror considered but ignored, 'Things are done in that area. My focus is on Jordan and that's all there is to it.' 'Jay, you can't just say that. I need to see that.' 'You will, Dangerous Games is safe.' 'Jay -' 'I'm gonna' take a smoke, be back in ten. Okay with you?' 'Jay, not now. We're trying to talk.' 'It's like you said, this is my night. Talking can wait another ten minutes.' Jay stood, drew his Marlboro's and walked off. Caldera was used to the erratic behavior and simply allowed him to escape. Stress was one thing that he didn't want plaguing his mind. Jay shoved open the main set of double doors and stepped into the chilly October air. The cigarette lit beside the palm of his hand and he inhaled. His cell phone came loose from his pocket and he hit the speed dial. Caroline picked up. 'You around?' She answered, her voice was full of sleep. 'Around, sure. What now?' 'Just wanted to talk, that's all. For a minute.' He took a drag and found a seat on the pavement. 'About what?' 'How are you?' 'No offense but I'm not trying to piss around and pretend we're great. We passed that stage. Unless you got something in particular spinning in that mind of yours then we might as well forget it.' Jay sat silent, she wasn't holding back for sure. Still, he didn't back down. 'Makes sense. You going to watch Dangerous Games?' 'Jay.' 'C'mon.' A sigh, 'I doubt it. Not like I care for you or Jordan now. Not worth the money to check it out.' His eyebrow lifted with another sip of his drink. More smoking. 'Not even Jordan?' 'Jordan helped me figure a few things out about life. He helped me with you but it's not like he was looking for something with me. Neither of us were. I'm on my own, I accepted it. Not like he was going to stick around and solve all my problems forever. It's whatever.' 'You're upset. I know when you're upset with things Caroline. He hurt you.' 'Hurt me? No Jay, he didn't.' Jay's eyes found that ferocious theme once again as he stared across the parking lot. He smoked a longer heavier drag, coughed even. 'Caroline, I know how you are. I know what's going on. He hurt you and you're upset over it. What'd he do?' 'I'm going back to sleep Jay. I'll talk to you another time.' 'No Caroline.' His voice was insistent and forceful, 'Not now. Tell me what happened. I need to know.' 'Jay, he did nothing. Good bye.' 'Caroline.' She had no time for him and did as she promised. He heard the click on the other side and immediately hurled his glass across the lot at a white Mercedes. The alarm sounded and he dropped his cigarette on his pant leg. A hole opened in his dress pants and he cursed under his breath. Standing, he knew that Caldera had been right. He had lost the drive to truly hurt Jordan. All it took was a false interpretation to figure out what had to be done. __________ One strike on the bag for Jordan. One for the guy in the front row with a "Down with the Establishment" shirt. One for the woman yelling drunken insults. One for the person who burned down his apartment and belongings. One more for Jordan. One for Andy Murray liking Jordan. One for Octavian and DeBough not finishing the job he sent them to do. One for LeStatt Knight ruining Andy Murray and Jordan's match. One last one for Jordan. He hit the bag over and over, nothing but hate rested in his eyes. Jordan White was the sole target, sure, but the energy he used when striking came from all corners. It didn't matter how little they did to him, they contributed their bit in each punch. One for the face, one in the arm, a few in the gut, his striking increased as the time went on. Body shots became as strong as bullets. He felt like he could pierce skin with the force behind them. Still, the bag taunted him to push harder. To kill Jordan. He tried. Over and over, he punched, kicked, elbowed, and head butted the bag until he fell over with exhaustion. Water became his best friend and as he sat up he looked at his knuckles. Bloody and battered, he knew they were ready to withstand anything. They were ready to strike bone, ready to break limbs. A smile crawled onto his lips as he thought of Jordan. He knew that he had hurt Caroline and for that fact he was ready to repay the favor. __________ A call came to Jay's phone at three o'clock on a Saturday and he picked up. 'Hello?' It was the Philadelphia Police captain. 'Jamison Talley, this is Deputy Shawn Starkley, I was calling you about the unfortunate happenings that occurred last week with your residence.' 'Right. What about it?' 'I wanted to inform you that we have apprehended a suspect. It turns out that upon leaving the scene, she dropped a clue.' 'She?' 'Yes. Do you know a Caroline Connors?' Silence sat on the line for long enough to beckon a question from deputy Starkley, 'Jamison, are you still there?' He didn't care who was on the other side, he closed his phone and leaned back in his chair on the balcony. He felt sick to his stomach. He wanted to puke, to fight, to yell, to kill. He wanted to do anything in his power. Instead though, he trained his mind to believe one thing. Jordan White was the reason for it all. He had to be.
