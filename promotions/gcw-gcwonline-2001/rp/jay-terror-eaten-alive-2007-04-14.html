Jay Terror-Eaten Alive?

14 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14, 2007 __Start 'You do know you're committing suicide, don't you?' Jamison Tally, better known to the professional world as Jay Terror, sucked off the end of his cigarette with a gleeful expression. His eyes squinted in the way a man's would at climax and his lips puckered around the soft paper. 'Call it what you want, it feels damn good Danny.' Daniel Franco leans back against the balcony rail feeling the gentle Philadelphian breeze pressing against his sternum. An ash leaps off the tip of his colleague’s cigarette but falls short of his face as it dives to the roughly paved base below him. 'And in the ring-how's that going to taste when you're thrown against the ropes and that bit of tar escapes your stomach? Or is that pleasure for the almighty Jay Terror?' The young man pushes back on his spring-loaded chair and bounces forward and back. His lips caress the end of the cancer stick once again and help suck the fumes into his lungs. 'Almighty? I like it. Is it better than the outlaw?' 'The Outlaw makes you come off as a cowboy to be frank. Almighty, though, would just be a lie.' Terror returns a devilish smirk and nods his head showing his lack of care for Daniel. 'Danny boy, you know how this business works. It's all about who can make the other look like a complete fool. So tell me, would you be more embarrassed if "The Outlaw" beat you or "The Almighty"?' Franco shakes his head disgustedly at his pupil. 'What it's about is not being so caught up in how you are perceived.' 'Stop two stepping around the bitch. Which is more fierce?' He slams his fist over the glass table to his left and flicks the butt of his cigarette at Daniel's face. He dodges it easily and watches Jay choke on his laugh. 'Jamison, I think it's time you go. Obviously you're not going to listen to me. You know the deal, when you're ready I'll be here.' Just like that Franco thrusts his body forward and steps toward the glass sliding door. He opens a crack wide enough for his torso and glides through partially before being stopped. 'What am I supposed to do? I'm not going to sit here all night!' The elder man of the two nods knowingly, 'I sure hope not. I'd hate to have the police provide you refuge as you prepare for Jayce Bradley.' 'But...' The door slams with a thud and Jay Terror is left alone on the porch. As it would be, his cigarettes were on the kitchen table. Fancy that. __________ There was something to be said of the hygiene of Philadelphia's streets. If the mounds of trash bags weren't enough to make a man vomit on his morning jog, the dirty diapers and rotting Chinese food would surely do the trick. Even the rats had grown smart enough to avoid the mess that became known as daily life to a select few on West Second Street. Lucky for Jamison Talley, he would be eligible to join the ranks of those very men and women for the evening. The rebellious young man watched over his possible evening home with a twisted sort of expression. From the sidewalk opposing the alley, all he could see was the thick dark shade. For all he knew there could be unspeakable terrors hidden within the cloak of night. Unfortunately for Jay, monsters or any other macabre images would have to do. He stepped across the street casually ignoring the both cars coming on each side of him. He almost made it too obvious he was a high school dropout. Nevertheless, Terror stepped up onto the sidewalk he had seen from a distance seconds ago and found the black bandana dangling around his neck. He drew the fabric up and over his nose trying to look as conspicuous and fear invoking as he possibly could. Too bad the accessory was associated with emo kids and brokeback cowboys. With his eyes squinted and hands ready he went on though, inching closer and closer to the entrance. Jay paused before crossing into the unknown. With a heavy sigh and dash of hope he planted his foot hard onto the concrete. Without warning an arm stretched out from the dark cloud and wrapped around his waist. At first touch he jumped as if he had seen a rat. After a moment of thought he figured he didn't have much to fear as the arm's lack of muscle hugged his side. After that, he dreaded his miscalculation and found the broken box on his head to be rather uncomfortable. Hell, the entire drag was uncomfortable. Rocks, nails, and his personal favorite, glass shards, grabbed onto his flesh with their claws and burrowed down as far as they could. Talley thought of screaming but knew it wouldn't do any good. Instead, he brought his arms up over the back of his head and used them a cushion. Two minutes passed before the individual dropped his legs on the ground. With an uncertain and shadowed face, Jay looked around. 'Hello.' His voice was timid to say the least. In an instant, a blinding light beamed down directly in front of his face temporarily blinding him. In the sporadic scene a grouping of faceless silhouettes could be seen dancing around the fallen Jay Terror in a ritual sort of manner. Their arms beat an invisible drum which serves as their only voice for the time being. The dancing ends abruptly and the things around him bend over closer. 'What is your purpose?' It had a thunderous tone escalating from the damp ground all the way up to the roof of the building adjacent to the bunch. The owner was invisible to him just as everyone surrounding Jay was. All he had was a direction. He spun behind to look up and found blinding heads where faces were supposed to be. 'Shelter’what the hell are you things doing?' Noises traveled around the circle and heads turned to their neighbor in a giant game of whisper down the lane. Like normal, Jay was the outcast and left out of the big secret. 'We are performing our ancient ritual before staking you to the floor and devouring you from head to toe.' 'You're cannibals?' 'We'd eat a dog if it came inside the darkness such as you.' 'Let me go you sick fucks, I know people that would tell people what you’ve done!' The banter returns and once more the secret travels around the men and women around him. 'And we will eat them too. Each and every one of them.' Talley sits up in a defying manner and watches the group bend forward in a pouncing position. For his first smart move of the day, he lowers himself to his back again and closes his eyes. 'I certainly picked the wrong alley...' he mumbles under his breath. The talking figure leans over Jay's face allowing his breath to dig into his nostrils and climb down his esophagus. 'Have you any last words foul mouthed human?' 'Choke on my bones you filthy beasts!' He spits in the speaker's face and shuts his eyes once more. Jamison Talley braces himself and tries to grab the floor below him. A click sounds through the darkness signifying, what Jay believes to be, his end. He waits impatiently trying to figure out what it felt like to have your arms gnawed off. After a good minute and a half passes he gently unrolls his eyelids and looks around him. A brilliant display of lights and signs decorate a once frightening alley. He sits up, obviously confused. 'Is this hell?' The men and women around him fall back in laughter noticing the ridiculous bandana around the lower half of his face. From behind him, the man whose voice had scared him enough into believing the ruse walks. 'Hell to some, life to others. Welcome to our humble abode!' He grins showing three teeth missing from the front of his mouth. Terror guessed they got lost in his graying beard. 'I thought you were going to eat me.' 'You're not the first. Just ask Martha beside you, she wet herself the first night we found her. Can't you smell it?' Jamison cringed. 'Mother fuckers.' 'There are children around, please.' Jay looked around and saw a short boy wrapped in a floral patterned table cloth. His face resembled a dirty toilet bowl and his figure reminded him of Nicole Ricci in a bikini. He could tell the young one tried to smile but his lips quivered and were too weak to flip from the frown he wore. 'You have any cigarettes?' He didn't bother turning back to the man. 'You think we can afford those things?' He grabbed at his discolored corduroys with a rip down the crotch. 'Plus they give you cancer.' 'Bah,' he answered much like he had toward Daniel. 'That's not a proven fact.' The man shrugs, 'Let me show you to your box.' He separates from the group and makes sure the visitor follows closely. While moving, he points side to side. 'Over here we have the bathrooms,' A god-awful smell rushes from the corner taking on the entity of dead fish and month old milk. 'To the left is the barber shop, Jacobi is quite skilled with the shears. Over here we have Caroline's garden,' He points to a bed of nails painted with who knows what. 'Right here is Hugo's box; he found probably the best one of the community just last week down by Fifth Street.' Jay shakes his head warily, 'That could fit my legs, where's the rest of him go?' 'Luckily Hugo has two stubs for legs, he doesn't have too much of a problem.' The man moves forward another few steps and stops fast. 'This, my new friend is yours for however long you need it.' Jamison examines the television box, easily one of the newer additions to the "neighborhood", and shakes his head dismissively. 'Thanks...' 'Of course, it's nothing my friend. If you need anything more, I am Sakaya....' He is stopped by a stout man trotting his way rapidly. He stops beside the odd named host and brings his ear down to his level. Sakaya listens intently and nods accordingly creating disbelieving faces. He stands upright again and looks to Jay. 'For now I must be going. It has come to my attention that a member of our community has been eaten alive by rats.' He smiles, 'Enjoy your stay.' His jaw drops, 'I hate you Daniel Franco.' For that evening he stays but finds little sleep at all. Between Caroline's singing and the sound of rats scurrying past his face, he couldn't find his dreams. __________ 'You're ready to fight now?' The familiar voice of Daniel Franco spills through the holes of the speaker system as Jay looks up at his home. Jamison Talley sighs, 'After a cigarette, I'm ready for butt sex if I have to.' The door opens slowly and Franco peeks his eyes around the corner of the wood. 'Not with me you aren't. Unless that's how you plan on defeating Jayce Bradley, you're going to live out there for another night.' Jay grabs the door and steps onto the hardwood floor inside. He gets a heavy whiff of the scented candles, vanilla to be exact, and smiles to himself. 'Maybe not Jayce, but that Vivica J sure is a lovely catch.' He ignores the sentiment, 'Where have you been?' He twists his nose sideways with the stench. 'My home away from home.' 'Well get into your real home before some little boy mistakes you for his lost dog.' Jay grins and lifts the bandana back over his nose mockingly. The door shuts behind him and the scene fades out. __End
