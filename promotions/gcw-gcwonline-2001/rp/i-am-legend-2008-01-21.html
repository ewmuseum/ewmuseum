I Am Legend

21 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21 Jan 08 What am I going to be remembered for? It’s the question that every man asks themselves at one point in their lives isn’t it? Normally it’s when they are near their end that this question creeps into the back of their minds, nagging and clawing until it makes its way to the front. Not the end of their lives necessarily but the end of something, typically something very important to them. There is not one soul on this Earth who really wants to be a nobody, that doesn’t want their name to mean something when people look back. I have never tried to hide the fact that what I crave the most is glory. With my own two hands I have made my way to the top of whatever I do, specifically my profession ? wrestling. Since the very beginning I have only cared about one goal, to be the greatest. I wanted to build a legacy that no one else could touch in GCW’s history. The plan may have started out rocky because of over confidence, a lack of a good work ethic and just flat out failure. Funny thins about failing is that you can either rise above it or crumble beneath it. I decided on the former. The story has been told time and time again, LeStatt Knight ascended GCW’s ranks quickly and effectively. I was nothing short of a black tidal wave that would engulf the entire industry that I take part in. It was glorious and brutal ? It was pure domination. Over the last three years I have done things that no one thought possible. Two World Championships, a record setting United States Title run, I have retired people, I have embarrassed people and I have enjoyed it. I have seen new people come into GCW and be considered “the next big thing” only to fizzle out and leave me remaining. I am what GCW is all about, I am a living legend. So what comes next? When you achieve such high standards how does a person continue to build on that? Also if you cannot figure out a way to get better then do you even stay in the game? I ask myself the question often, “How much do I have left?” and the answer is never easy. I’ve climbed the mountain I’ve claimed my spot in history So what now? Is the old saying true? What comes up must come down. __________________________________________ The war was coming to an end, a man’s legacy hung in the balance. No one had ever seen a warrior quite like the one that had surfaced. He called himself, LeStatt Knight but he never gave a place of origin. Some that battled the mysterious Warlord claimed that he wasn’t a man at all but something bred in the bowels of hell, sent to this world to do nothing but kill. LeStatt himself probably would not have refuted this story. On the night of the final battle, the warrior sat alone in his tent, dark and cold, only the dim flickering light from a candle’s flame kept him company. His mind was focused, determined. Before battle the warrior thought of only one thing ? his sword. When out of that battle field a man’s greatest ally is the weapon that he holds close. For LeStatt Knight the weapon was a sword, black blade, silver hilt decorated with bright blue stones that matched his icy blue eyes. Covering the blade was the drawing of a serpent which wrapped around the entire thing. Knight sat Indian style, the blade of his sword driven into the ground between his legs, his gloved hands wrapped around the hilt, his eyes closed and his head bowed. Although many considered this man to be heartless or even a demon in human form, it was all fairy tale bullshit. The warrior could feel fear, he felt the pain of a blade piercing his flesh and he believed in God. However unlike normal men he used fear to his advantage. LeStatt knew that if he feared something then his adversaries would feel the exact same. Putting his fears away and using it to inflict greater damage was perhaps his greatest strength. The candle finally flickered out Knight’s eyes shot open, the blues inside of them glow. A scratching sound was made as the sword was pulled from the cold dirt and gravel. His eyes inspected the blade briefly before he sheathed the weapon and made his way outside of the tent. Still he was alone. Yes there was going to be a great battle, one that would be talked about throughout the ages ? but to settle the conflict it was going to come down to two men. Knight looked around at his snow covered surroundings and then up at the full moon. The moon was unusually large and gave off a red glow. Knight imagined the moon being drenched in his enemy’s blood and a smile crept onto his face. “Tonight I make my mark.” He whispered into the winter sky. “On this night the World will forever recall the name, LeStatt Knight.” He stood and admired the moon for just a few moments. A deep breath flooded his lungs and a feeling of anxiety washed over his body as he blew the air back out. It was his one chance and he refused to let it slip out of his hands. He would make sure that the man who would stand in his way would pay the price, that he would make him scream and cry. LeStatt Knight would not stop until he heard a grown man beg for mercy. He marched towards his destiny, a destiny that he forged for himself. Only fools sat around and waited for greatness to find them, the truly deserving force their will on others. The truly amazing put fear into the hearts of all that oppose them. The Legendary make sure that no one will ever forget them ? LeStatt Knight’s lonely figure marched ? His Legacy waited in the distance. ________________________________________________ GCW has been my life since the day I resigned in early February 2005. Since that time I have been married to my dear Mary and we even had our first child together, little Joaquin. I finally have a family to call my own and yet where do I find myself for most of the time? On the road for GCW, away from those that I hold closest. It pains me that I am away so much when at times all I want is to hold them in my arms. So of course the question must be, why the hell do I continue to be away from them? I have the accolades, the prestige and yes even the fans. Is there not a thing such as quitting while you’re ahead? Only other wrestlers can fully understand why we do the things that we do. It’s the rush of it all, it’s our drug really. Things can be going wrong in our lives, I’ve seen people dealing with death, divorce, sickness, lawsuits and all other assortments of ridiculous circumstances and yet when that music hits and the crowd gets to their feet ? it all washes away. The adrenaline flows through the body and it brings you to life. And how about the feeling of winning that first GCW World Heavyweight Championship? It’s honestly a feeling that I could never put into words. I can still feel the bumps, bruises and brutality of that night at Dangerous Games 2005. On that night I became the man that has terrorized GCW for three damn years. I remember the look in Jason’s eyes as I held the flame dangerously close to his face. I can still smell the aroma of gasoline flooding the arena. I can still sense the fear that radiated from every bone in Jason Dread’s body as he wondered if I would really set him on fire. His ass better have questioned it because for the opportunity to be etched in history I’d have burned him and any close relatives that may have been in attendance. I never gave a damn about him, it was always about making a name for myself, about building my legacy into a wall that nobody would ever be able to climb over again. Once he said those two magical words, I Quit, it felt incredible. It was like an NFL player winning the super bowl, the Red Sox taking out the Yankees in game 7 after being down 3-0 back in 04 or the Pistons toppling the Mighty Lakers in the NBA Finals in 2003. It was like the greatest orgasm with the woman of your dreams. It was fucking bliss! I want that feeling again and I will not stop until I’ve had it. People say I have nothing left to prove in GCW But those people are fucking stupid because you always have something else to prove. Even GCW’s Greatest World Champion ever. The ones in GCW now weren’t around for my glory; instead it is something of a fable to them. It’s a bedtime story and I fear that they all believe that my best days are behind me. This new blood, people like Jordan White and Jay Terror are coming to claim MY throne as if I am some old King that has worn out his welcome. Well fuck them. This is my world! And I’m not ready to let it go. ________________________________________ Blood flowed from a gash on Knight’s head, falling into his face. His bones ached, his body bruised but yet his will was as strong as ever. The battle had been fierce but finally victory was in his grasp. Weaponless and with a broken spirit Knight’s adversary fell to his knees. LeStatt brought the tip of his sword to his prey’s chest. “It’s over Dread.” He growled “It is time to surrender!” Slowly Jason Dread’s eyes found their way to LeStatt’s. Knight looked into his soul and saw nothing more than a frightened child. This man, who once stood in front of him with a look of determination, now is nothing but a fragment of what he was. And yet ? “Go to hell!” Dread spat out LeStatt’s teeth clenched together inside of his mouth. “Your defiance will get you nowhere but death!” Knight moves the sword closer, now the tip of the blade touches Dread’s chest. “YIELD!” “I will never yield to you.” He snapped. Knight drove his free hand out and wrapped it around the throat of Dread. He used his superior strength to bring Dread to his feet and then off of them. Jason’s eyes went wide with a mix of fear and anger. LeStatt’s icy blue eyes burned into Dread’s and they even appeared to give off a slight red glow ? just like that blood red moon. “I will snap your neck in half! You can end this war by simply admitting defeat! Death will not have to come if you just say the words!” Knight pulled Dread closer to his face. “This is your last chance before I send you straight to hell!” Knight releases Dread who falls at Knight’s feet on all fours. LeStatt adjusted his sword so that it lay right on the back of Dread’s neck. “Make your decision.” Jason Dread was a proud warrior, a man who had been through many wars and won many battles. He was already a legend all over the Globe and wanted nothing to tarnish that. Yielding to LeStatt could very well do just that but would death be worth the price? Not in his mind. “You win LeStatt.” Dread whispered. “I quit.” From behind the mask of blood, sweat and tears LeStatt Knight smiled an evil smile. A rush of energy surged through him and the sweet taste of victory invaded his taste buds. He closed his eyes and tilted his head back, taking in the entire moment. Destiny has finally found him. There was only one thing left to do. Knight’s eyes opened His sword came up above his head With all of his strength Knight drove the sword down into Dread’s back! The force of the thrust brought Knight to his knees. Blood stained the white snow. LeStatt remained holding the sword’s hilt tightly as he listened to Dread’s gurgling and felt his body twitching in agony. His eyes wandered down to the turned head of Dread ? and Knight smiled as the life in Dread’s eyes vanished. Knight leans his head back and laughs into the night sky. A LEGEND WAS BORN ________________________________________ Jay Terror Just thinking the name makes my blood boil. This punk kid comes in and is handed everything on a silver platter. He doesn’t know the meaning of hard work and adversity. A few months ago he was battling some kid named Tyson DeBough for the TV Title and NOW he is being mentioned in the same breath as a Hall of Famer? Give me a fucking break! The kid wants badly to defeat me, to prove that he belongs. And there’s that word again. PROVE. Everyone has something to prove don’t they? Jay wants to prove that he is World Title material. Ambrosi wants to prove that she could handle the fierce competition for the World Title; Valentine wants to prove that she isn’t some flash in the pan. What is it that I aim to prove? It’s very simple really. I will prove that I am still the best in GCW. That I am not some pathetic old timer who is holding on to some glimpse of a successful past all the while the future is nothing like it. Much like Rich Rollins as a matter of fact. This new Round Robin pits us GCW originals against the new blood that was brought in to replace us. Sometimes the new model isn’t better than the one it replaced. GCW thinks it has seen the best that I can offer and to that I say that you haven’t seen shit yet. This is the rebirth of LeStatt Knight, the second coming of that black tidal wave that caused so much devastation the first time around. However this time the setting is much larger, the violence will be far greater and the fear that I will instill will be like nothing ever seen or felt before. I am waging war It’s me against the World. I hope everyone is prepared for the casualties that follow. Jay Terror, that son of a bitch is the first. Count on it. ____________________________________ The Great Warrior came to his feet, his eyes still fixed on the carnage he had caused. Knight places a boot down on Dread’s back as leverage to force his sword out of his kill’s back. LeStatt admires the blood soaked blade and smiled when he watched the blood drip further down. BOOM! BOOM! The ground begun to shake and it was too controlled to be an Earthquake. Knight headed to his right where a snow covered hill rested. Although his body was tired and weary, he begun to climb up the hill to get a better view. BOOM! BOOM! He almost slid down but he drove his sword into the snow to keep him steady. BOOM! BOOM! He muscled himself upwards, ascending his objective. BOOM! BOOM! He crawled onto the hilltop and slowly stood up straight, his blood and snow drenched sword remained by his side. BOOM! BOOM! The wind blew, whipping the long black hair from his eyes; it helped give Knight a clear view of everything below. BOOM! BOOM! It was a sea of steel blades, bows and arrows, armor and horses, men and women. BOOM! BOOM! An Army marched his way. Thousands upon thousands of people who would love nothing more than to see his head on a stick. The ground quaked underneath Knight with his step they took. Fear settled in. Anger Anxiousness! Knight realized then that no Legacy is ever complete. There will always be battles to fight. Again he smiled.
