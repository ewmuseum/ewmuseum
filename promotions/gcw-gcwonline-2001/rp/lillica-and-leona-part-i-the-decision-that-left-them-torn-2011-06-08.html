Lillica and Leona Part I: The Decision That Left Them Torn

8 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/11 Whenever I am at Tears of Blood I always get frequently asked about one person, Nightshade. Where did she come from? What’s her real name? All I tell them is; she’s now working for me, that’s all I know which in fact was the truth because hell I’m not one to pry she does her job as my secretary and that’s all I ask of her, ever. I’ve never once dared to even try to ask about her past because I knew it was something that she didn’t want to reveal, even to me, perhaps maybe there was some incident that caused her to take a vow of silence on the matter or maybe it was just all too painful to remember which is usually the issue with those who wish to forget about their past period. However when a strange letter with the words "Lillica Stacy" written on the envelope comes to your door, you get the feeling that everything will be revealed, the pent up feelings, the aguish and suffering and turmoil that comes with having a hidden past locked away for years only to finally rear its ugly head once again and remind her why she chose to forget it. -Tears of Blood club: 1:25 pm- [The scene fades in on Nightshade who begins her daily duties as Khaos’s secretary such as gathering the mail at the door and checking for messages on the phone possibly left by bands interested in playing gigs at Tears of Blood. From the door she heads towards the elevator pressing the second floor button closing the doors in front of her as the elevator heads up she glances at the mail to see what came in today looks to be mostly junk mail followed by a few bills and a letter with the words Lillica Stacy on the envelope Nightshade looks at letter with disbelief looking like she saw a ghost because she knew the handwriting of her name.] Nightshade: This can’t be how did she know where I was and how I could be found? No, I shouldn’t be surprised; after all, we are the same. [She arrives on the second floor hoping no one would be around and thankfully no one was for Khaos nor Cory has not yet been in the office yet, she sets the rest of the mail down on the desk and opens up the letter addressed to her and begins to read it.] To my sister Lillica At last I have found you, how long did you think you could run from me? How long did you think you could hide from your past? I told you ten years ago that I would hunt you down make you pay for abandoning The Order and me as well as the mission given to you and tonight is the night I take my vengeance upon you because thanks to your selfless act I was thrown out of The Order, cast aside like trash! So tonight heed my challenge to you and only you meet me at the Western Docks at 8pm sharp and come alone. Don’t even think of bringing that man you call Khaos because this is between you and me so keep him out of it. Your sister Leona Stacy She began to think about The Order and the life she left behind, the life of an assassin a cold dark reality that only an assassin could live, living from kill to kill never seeing the light, always living in darkness and everyday being a trial on life itself. Nightshade knew that someday the shadows of the assassin world would find her but when was hers or anyone’s guess simply put she was found by the one person she left for dead, her own flesh and blood her twin sister, Leona. Back then they were a team a two man cell comprised of herself and Leona; they were code named, The Gemini Killers. These twin killers were taken from their families as children only to watch their parents being slaughtered right in front of them by the same assassins that raised them to be the perfect killers. From there on their life was meant to drenched in blood and killing became an everyday thing they weren’t given a chance to live as anything else but assassins and every mission given to them they accepted without hesitation completing them with the greatest of ease wither it was eliminating a military figure or even a corrupt politician they left no trace of their being and thus rose through the ranks of the assassin world with ease because of their perfect record once again making The Gemini Killers the perfect killers. However during one mission ten years ago everything changed, both women’s lives would never be same again… -May 2001 somewhere in the Czech Republic- The snow falls lightly like cotton on the ground as two shadows race though a white forest heading for an unsuspecting village on a cold dark night it was none other than The Gemini Killers Lillica and Leona on a mission to assassinate the village leader Harold Kozlov who leads a rebel faction against the Czech Republic, he is suspected of having vast knowledge on military secrets and sharing them with other countries especially with the United States which didn’t bold too well with the military or with the Czech Republic so they hired The Order to do the dirty work of silencing one Harold Kozlov. As they arrived upon the village outskirts they find that the house where Harold Kozlov is staying at was heavily guarded by men with machine guns but this threat posed no problem for The Gemini Killers for they had the experience and tools of the trade to easily dispose of such hazards. Like snakes they slither in stalking the guardsmen from behind and one after another they slit their throats quickly and without hesitation rendering them helpless as the two set their sights on the bigger prize who is unaware that his guards are no longer among the living and at the mercy of The Gemini Killers. They infiltrate the house with ease blending in with the shadows past the security cameras and though the laser detectors and finally to Harold Kozlov who was enjoying a cigar while watching television unaware that he was about to meet his maker….or was he? [Lillica untwines a thin string of wire without making a sound to the unsuspecting Harold Kozlov and just as she was about to wrap it around his neck he turns around pulling a gun out to her face at point blank range.] Harold: Well, well, well what the fuck do we have here? An assassin?! Yeah I knew that someday the government would do some cheap ass stunt like this sending an assassin to do their dirty work in trying to shut me up but it looks like I got the drop on you bitch and now I’m going to send you to hell! Lillica: You’re the one that will be sent to hell. Harold: Right. [As he was about the pull the trigger Leona swoops from the ceiling with a huge knife (Were talking about nothing much really about six, seven inches here so yeah pretty big folks) and with pinpoint quickness and accuracy stabs him in the back of the neck and right though his throat.] (You can just see this in slow motion) Harold: Ugh guh h-how was t-there t-t-two of y-you…. [Harold tried to pull the trigger of the gun at Lillica but he was losing too much blood from the wound as it was gushing out of his neck trickling down on his clothing as Leona pulled the knife out of his neck letting him drop to the ground and suffer a grizzly fate.] It’s important to note that when facing The Gemini Killers it’s like well facing Velociraptors you stare one in the eye long enough and BAM the other comes from out of nowhere and well I think you get it after all what you just saw, it’s a perfect example of how deadly these two were. Lillica: Mission Complete. Leona: Yes. Let’s head out before anyone notices… [But as the two were about to head out the sounds of small feet could be heard coming down the stairs both Lillica and Leona were prepared to fight a possible guard when it wasn’t a guard at all, it was a little girl who looked to be at the tender age of only three had just walked in on a murder scene.] Lillica: It’s just a little girl nothing to worry about. [Leona looks at Lillica with concern in her eyes.] Leona: Just a little girl!? Lillica, we have just been spotted we are supposed to be unseen, unheard we are shadows of the night! We have to eliminate her. "Eliminate her" those words ringed in Lillica’s mind over and over again was she going to comply with Leona’s wish? Or would she risk it all and stop Leona from making the single biggest mistake, after all it was only a mere little girl, harmless, scared and wondering what happened to her daddy. Thoughts of what happened to her as a child began to surface for the first time in years, yes her parents being slaughtered right in front of her by the same assassins that raised her to kill she thought to herself that this was very identical to that same moment Lillica began to ponder wither she had been on the right path or not would she save this little girl and risk life on the run or just simply erase her like her sister Leona intends to do and go on being an assassin forever never seeing the light and always being in darkness. Lillica: You’re right Leona; she seen what must never be seen…we have to eliminate her. [Out of nowhere Lillica kicks Leona square in the face sending her reeling backwards and on top of the fallen body of Harold Kozlov. Leona is in shock as to how Lillica could betray her like this.] Leona: What, what are you doing?! Do you realize what you have done?! You’re betraying The Order and me your own sister just to save a mere child!? Lillica: Yes, I’m putting it all on the line, my life, everything. Leona: Why… why now. Why this change of heart all of sudden. Lillica: It’s because of our parents deaths Leona I finally realized how fruitless all this killing is, it’s just one big endless circle of blood and death. Leona: Were assassins Lillica, it’s what we do. You can’t just turn your back on the whole thing just because one moment in our childhood. Now let’s end this discussion and get rid of the child, right now. [As Leona gets to her feet she unsheathes her knife with the intention of slewing the little girl once and for all but Lillica steps in front of her with the intent of stopping her in her tracks.] Leona: So you do intend to betray us and me. Lillica: I told you, I’m putting everything on the line, even my life. So yes as of right now I am fighting for my freedom and if I have to go though you to achieve that…then so be it prepare yourself! Leona: Such petty beliefs dear sister but if that’s the way you want to play this game then fine, let’s play. So it was on Lillica vs. Leona and the only one to witness it was a three year old little girl scared in the corner wondering why these two are about fight amazingly enough she hasn’t cried nor made a sound after all that’s happened here and who could blame her after she didn’t know any better this wasn’t by choice perhaps it was fate that brought her here to bear witness to such clash in the first place. [The two sisters circle each other neither one taking their eyes off one another as they get ready to duel to the death Leona lunges in with her knife but Lillica avoids it catching her arm in the process and follows up with a punch of her own to the gut of Leona forcing her to drop the knife as Lillica bashes Leona in the face with a couple of knee strikes sending Leona reeling back and forced to come up with a different strategy.] Lillica: Give up, Leona, you know as well I as I do that I was quicker study of the two of us, your moves are sloppy and straight forward, you can’t win in this state. Leona: Never. I’ll show you that I am the more dominate sister here and I’ll show you why I’ve been carrying your ass all this time! Lillica: (sighs) Fine, have it your way, but it was me that was carrying your ass all this time. Leona: Hmph! We’ll see who is carrying who after pummel you into the ground! [Leona leaps into the air to deliver a devastating kick towards Lillica but she telegraphs the move once again and avoids it, flustered Leona tries to land a barrage of punches and kicks but none of them can seem to land as Lillica moves effortlessly to avoid all contact.] Leona: Huff puff why can’t I land a single shot on you, just tell me! Lillica: You obviously weren’t paying attention to anything I said earlier so I’ll spell it out for you. You. Can’t. Win. Leona: That’s impossible, were twins, we should be equals! Lillica: It’s funny how even twin sisters can be different too Leona we don’t always think the same thoughts as you might think and this fight proves it and you know it and that’s why I say to you Leona, give it up, you’ll never beat me. Leona: Never huh. [Leona pulls out a gun and points directly at Lillica.] It was the same gun that Harold Kozlov used on her earlier before he met his untimely demise, Lillica suspects that she must have taken it when she kicked her back onto his body when she was going to strike down the little girl. Lillica has never seen such a act of cowardice in her life but she wasn’t surprised, Leona was desperate for victory and would do anything to seize the moment of when she would could say, I defeated my sister Lillica by my own hands. Lillica: You’ve fallen Leona; I didn’t think you would stoop this low as to use a gun to settle this. Leona: I’m tired of these games Lillica, it ends now, first I’ll get rid of you, then this pesky little girl and then I’ll be on my way. Lillica: It won’t be that simple, more guards will come once they hear the gun shot go off, this is just like you Leona, you never think things though, always rushing in and- Leona: Silence! I’ve had enough of your lectures! I win, and you lose dear sister! Goodbye forever! Once again Lillica has found herself on deaths door facing the same gun again as this time there was for sure no way out of this one Lillica knew her time had come and never thought that it could end like this at the hands of her own flesh and blood as if fate had dealt her the joker and freedom would be only granted in death. However by a strange twist of fate a small miracle happened… Little girl: D-daddy…sob [The little girl begins to cry for her fallen father as Leona turns her attention away from Lillica just for a split second Lillica realizing this could be her only shot at stopping Leona lunges forward and tackles her down to the ground as Lillica tries to steer the gun away from her as the little girl watches as the two sisters fight for control of it when out of nowhere a shot was fired.] -BANG- One could only think what could have happened next for which sister did fate smile upon and which sister did fate smite upon, for what the little girl witnessed would be two sisters torn by conflict over personal beliefs and the will to say I am me and this is my choice…this is my decision…
