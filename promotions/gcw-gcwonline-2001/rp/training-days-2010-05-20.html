Training Days

20 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/10 Takes place before the oversea tour Scott Drake stood in the middle of the ring and waited. He hated waiting. That was a given, however, as Scott hated most things. Far away the sound of a door opening and shutting banged through the empty gym. It took a few seconds but a lean looking shadow walking through the corridor and over toward the apron. "You Johnny?" The kid, who looked to be in his early twenties shook his head. "You’re late." He rubbed the back of his head, realizing he was being called out on being twenty minutes late. "Yeah, sorry about that, I got held up in traffic." Scott just rubbed his nose, "still late, regardless of what excuse you have." He walked over and leaned against the ropes nearest to Johnny. "Now you going to get in so we can get started, or are you just going to stand there all day?" "Yeah sure, let me just go get changed" Johnny said as he turned away. "Hey, hey, hey!" The Kid turned his head to look back at the ring and saw Scott shaking his head. "You’re already late, that’s the price you gotta pay. Now get in the ring." Like a deer in the headlights of an incoming freight trailer, the Kid couldn’t even muster the thought to blink. When the words finally registered with him all he could do was throw his arms up in protest, "but I’m still in my street clothes!" "If you want my training, you’ll get in this ring street clothes or not." The look on Scott’s face told Johnny he had better get in the ring quickly. He still tossed his shirt aside along with his cell phone and wallet, but rolled in with his blue jeans and sneakers under the bottom rope. Popping to his feet, he suddenly had Scott right in his face. Drake shoves him into the ropes, and Johnny has to hastening get into a defensive position. "So what happens when you show up to the arena late and don’t have time to make it to your locker room before your match? You think they’ll just hold up the show for you?" He stared right at Johnny, but the Kid could barely make eye contact back. "You need to learn to wrestle no matter what. You could be wearing a fucking space suit, you still better be ready to wrestle." Johnny stares blankly at Scott. "Why would I be a space suit?" "That’s not the fucking point!" "Is this one of those Banned & Exiled~! pranks?" Scott forces his eyes shut tightly, doing what he can from losing his temper so early. "Damnit, forget about the space suit and give me fifty pushups." "For what?" Johnny ask, caught off guard. "As punishment for showing up late" he replies, crossing his arms. Johnny pointed toward his jeans, "I thought having to practice in my regular clothes was for." Opening his eyes slowly, Scott shook his head. "That was a lesson you need to learn. This is the punishment. So get to it, they won’t do themselves." Letting out a deep sigh, Johnny knows there is no way for him to get out of it. He dropped down to the canvas. "One!" To his surprise the Kid looked to his left and saw Scott doing pushups as well. "Two!" Scott comes up from his second pushup to see that Johnny hadn’t followed suit. "Three!" Still in shock, Johnny fell two pushups behind Scott. Scott came up from the pushup and looked at the Kid. "Forget how to count past one or something?" Johnny shook his head and resumed his pushups and together the two of them knocked out fifty each. Johnny sat up, wiping the sweat from his brow. Scott was already back on his feet, looking just as fresh as when the Kid first walked into the gym. "Now that that’s over with, get up so we can start the training" Scott told him, letting himself lean into the turnbuckle. "Show me what you know so I know where to start." The Kid smiled for the first time since he entered the gym. Suddenly he found renewed vigor and flip kipped to his feet. Scott stepped out of the turnbuckle and toward the middle of the ring. Johnny moved in quickly to tie up with him but the much larger and stronger Scott was easily able to shove him away. The force was great enough to nearly knock him off his feet, but he was able to stumble back into the ropes to keep his balance. Like a head-strong child Johnny rushed right back in for a second tie up. "I thought you were going to show me what you knew. You that much of a blank page?" Even during a normal practice session, Scott seemed to have to engage in trash talk. Johnny has a grin on his face, even though he knew a tie up again would only result in the same end. "I’ve got more than one trick in my bag." He shoved into Scott, looking for that little bit of resistance force. Unfortunately all he got was Scott locking onto his bicep and pulling him further in for a huge Belly to Belly Suplex. The impact could be heard through the building and undoubtedly clear in the lower back of Johnny. Scott sat upright in the ring and looked over to see the Kid squirming on the mat in pain. "Kid, I was using that trick since I was in the bush leagues." Onto his back, Johnny opened his eyes and looked up at the lights hanging from the ceiling. A hand suddenly appeared over his head. Reaching up he took hold of Scott’s hand and used it to pull himself back to his feet. Scott gave the Kid a light and playful slap to the back of the head and with a smile told him "now give me another fifty pushups." "WHAT?" "Did I knock an eardrum loose? Trying a rook move like that nets you fifty more pushups." Johnny threw his arms up in the air, "but I am a rookie!" His protest was in vain, as Scott was already down on the canvas. "One!" He looked up at Johnny as he came up from the pushup. The Kid sighed again and got back down onto the mat. "Two!" On and on it went like this for three hours. Scott would push Johnny around the ring and any time he felt he made a mistake the two of them would do fifty pushups at a time. By the time it was noon the Kid could barely stand. He had managed to drag himself over to the apron and allowed his body to hang over the bottom rope and dangle over the ground. Scott, still on his feet, walked over to the ropes and looked down on his young trainee. "I guess we can take a lunch break." Johnny rolled over onto his back, either unable to feel the rope against his shoulder blades or simply not caring, and managed to muster a smile. "Thank goodness." Using the ropes, he pulled himself back to his knees and slid through to the outside. Collecting his things he tossed on his shirt, "I’m dying for some food. I saw a Mc-a-Dee’s like a block away. You want anything?" Scott climbed through the ropes and to the outside where he walked over to a bench. On the bench was a black duffle bag, which Scott reached into and pulled out his wallet. "Good idea" he said as he grabbed a twenty from it. "Go get two of those grilled chicken sandwiches. Tell them no bread though, and apples for the side." He handed the money over to Johnny, "oh and two waters." "You going to eat two of them? I hear that, I could probably down two Big Macs right now." Johnny asked as he pocketed the twenty. "What, no? That’s for both of us" Scott told him. "If you are going to be training, you need to eat healthy." Johnny’s jaw just about hit the floor. First the practicing in street clothes, then the pushups, and now this. "Man," he muttered in a tone that cried out how defeated he felt, "you trying to kill me or something?" Scott walked over to him and firmly pressed his index finger into Johnny’s chest. "I’m trying to turn you into a wrestler. I’ve been in the business for over ten years now, if you want to be trained by me, you are going to have to do things my way. Got it?" Hershel had told his nephew that Scott was intense about his training, he had no idea he meant this. Still, Johnny had made a promise to himself not to quit, and he certainly wasn’t on the first day. So, as much as he hated it, he sucked it up. "Fine, whatever, I’ll go get the food." When he had returned Johnny was in near awe at the sight he had seen. Despite the three hours of training and all the pushups they had been doing, Scott was in the center of the ring doing squats. "Is this man a machine" Johnny wondered to himself as he dropped the bag off on the bench. He walked over toward the ring and held up the two bottles of water for Scott to see. "Hey, foods here!" With two more to make an even one-hundred, Scott exited the ring and took the bottle from Johnny. The two sat side by side on the bench and took out their lunches. "So…" Scott said as he took out his chicken, "you’re Hershel’s nephew, huh?" The awkwardness in his voice gave away just how bad Scott was at small talk. Johnny seemed more interested in his lack of food. Even his stomach growled out a disapproval. "Eh, yeah," he replied "my mom’s brother." He looked around the gym for a moment. It was nothing impressive, if anything it looked worn and old. But nothing seemed in bad condition. "So…" Johnny said, almost mimicking Scott, "when did you first start coming to uncle’s gym?" Neither seemed very good at the small talk, despite their best efforts. Scott tilted his head back and looked up at the lights as he thought, as if somehow the answered had been wrote on the ceiling. "I guess it would be around ‘98" he said. He lowered his head and took another bite from his chicken. "Yeah, that was the year I started wrestling professionally, so I’ve been coming here ever since ’98." Johnny silently chewed on his food, processing the information in his head. ‘Twelve years’ he kept thinking to himself. It seemed like such a long time to someone his age. It was over half his life, Johnny wondered if maybe he could have a career like that. As they sat and ate, making small talk, Scott’s expression became more serious. "Let me ask you something," Johnny stopped chewing and looked over at Scott, "what made you want to become a wrestler? It’s not exactly a normal profession." The Kid’s eyes went glazed over as he sank deep into thought. "The roar" he said, in a barely audible whisper. It was almost as if the answer had slipped out between his lips on its own. It certainly was not the answer Scott had been expecting. "The roar?" he echoed. Johnny turned his head to face Scott, his face was visibly redder than before. "Yeah, the roar of the crowd. There is nothing like it." With a crinkle in his nose, Scott shoved the last of his food into his mouth and stood up. "Hurry up and finish eating, we have to get back to training." The Kid quickly finished what food he had and tosses his shirt and stuff off to the side. He slid in under the bottom rope, failing to notice Scott had stopped on the apron. The man they called the Last Great Technical Wrestler hooked both legs onto the top rope and hung himself upside down. He lifted his body up for a sit-up. "One!" "What are you doing?" Johnny asked, part amazing and part horrified at it. He crossed his fingers behind his back so that Scott wouldn’t see, but it proved to be worthless. "One hundred sit-ups, and I expect the same from you. It’s the best way to burn off the calories we just ate." By his estimate, he had likely already did four hundred pushups on top of the hours of practice. Now, after feeling like he would starve with what little food he was allowed to eat, he had to come up with one hundred sit-ups hanging upside down like a bat? Scott though, had already gotten through five while Johnny just sat back and watched. He pulled himself up for his sixth and looked over at the Kid. "We’re not training again until I see you do one-hundred." Johnny sighed for what had seemed like the tenth time and what he knew wouldn’t be the last. He climbed through the ropes and did his best to climb into the pose Scott had. With all his body weight falling down, he could feel the intense pressure on his knees. He pulled his body up, just as Scott did. "One!" Sixty-six more followed that one, but as Johnny tried to pull his body up for the sixty-eighth sit-up, he could feel all the muscles in his abdomen screaming with pain. His body fell back and by only sheer luck did his legs not betray him and let loose of the ropes. Sweat dripped from his bangs and crashed onto the apron. Scott looked over at him, "you giving up so soon? You still owe me thirty-three more." There was no reply, only the heavy breathing of the Kid. Even if he had wanted to attempt another sit-up, he body would refuse and demand rest. Scott, though, seemed unable to apprehend what rest was and continued with his own sit-ups. He was quickly reaching one hundred while Johnny still could not force his body to even give him one more. "You want to quit? There isn’t any crowds here, no roar, only an empty gym." Those words, as harsh and cruel as they were, served to push Johnny. He would not give in until he had seventeen more done. At the count of eighty-four his body went limp again. "Come on now!" Scott hollered, standing the middle of the ring now, "this is where you earn the right to hear them. Nothing going to be given to you and nothing’ll come easy. You want it, you put in the work." His words were softer, more encouraging now. It was a tone he hadn’t used before in front of the Kid. When his uncle had told him that Scott’s training was intense, he never imagined anything like this. His entire body burned. Yet, somehow, he knew he had to finish. If this was what it took, then there would be no shortcuts. He hooked his ankles together to make sure he had a firm grip and crossed his arms over his chest. It took a long time, most likely to such a degree that Scott would be highly annoyed, but Johnny did all of them. When he was finally finished he unhooked his legs and allowed his body to slump down onto the apron in a puddle of muscles. He had never in his entire life felt so spent. His body rolled over onto his back and when he looked up at the lights he saw a water bottle hanging over his head. "Here," Scott said, "we’ll take a short break before we continue." Johnny was surprised he could even reach up to take it, but gladly gulped down the remaining water from the bottle. After finishing off the last drop, he pulled himself up with the ropes and sat down on the apron. Scott climbed through the ropes and joined him. The two sat in silence as Johnny just allowed his head to roll back and rest on the middle rope. His hair was matted to his forehead, his eyes were closed as he enjoyed the rest and the satisfaction. "How long do you normally train?" asked Scott, who seemed almost surprised that such a workout could take such out of a person. Without opening his eyes or even tilting his head, Johnny told him "about two or three times a week. Typically whenever I can get days off from work." Scott’s eyes went wide. He could never imagine only working out that rarely. "What do you do?" "Construction, well electrician’s assistant" Johnny said. "Really I’m just a gofer, but it pays decent enough that I can still wrestle on the weekends." Standing up, Scott climbed back into the ring. Johnny allowed his head to roll over the middle rope and looked into the ring at Scott. "Alright, enough of a break. Back to training." Johnny groaned with displeasure, but still pulled his body up and climbed into the ring. The Kid did his best to lift his arms up, but they both felt like lead weights. Scott held up his arm to stop him for a moment. With the Kid’s attention, he held up three fingers."We’re going to train for three more hours before we call it a day. I want to see you make it through the entire training." He seemed to be able to read the dejected look on Johnny’s face, "you able to do that?" He nodded back to Scott silently. The truth was he had no idea how he could survive another three hours. He normally only trained for two hours or so and never this rigorous. But he had came this far, there was no sense in turning back. He lifted his arms back into their defensive positioning. Scott again held up his arm to pause him. "You do that, and when we go on our Oceania tour I’ll bring you along as my assistant, all expenses paid." If the training wasn’t already enough to knock him over, Scott’s words almost did the trick. "Are you serious?" "Yep, I’ll match what you make and pay for your expenses" he said as he nodded, "but don’t think this is some kind of vacation. I want you to absorb everything," Johnny’s grin grew wider and wider with each word, "and we’ll be training every single day." And there it went. Almost. The idea of training like this for the next two months seemed near unbearable. But for a chance like that? To be a part of something that rare, Johnny felt like he could walk across water if that was what it took. Scott held out his hand. Johnny tipped his head and shook his hand, sealing the deal. Then, just like that, got sucked in and hoisted into the air for a Belly to Belly Suplex. Scott laughed as he came back to his feet, "good, now let’s resume that training." Johnny looked up at the lights hanging above, wondering just what he had gotten himself into. If he was to survive, he decided, it would require a miracle. Thankfully Scott’s entire life seemed like one giant miracle.
