Date: 06/25/10 The pain in her arms and back seem to have subsided. Her head is throbbing, and there is a burning sensation in her sides and stomach. She opens her eyelids and a clear film covers whatever it is she’s looking at.. She blinks her eyes rapidly a few times to wipe away the film. The second a small light beams into her pupils her head begins to spin and a headache takes residence. She moans, and instinctively puts her hand to her head. Instead of feeling hair and skin she feels something rough. When her arm extends she feels a pinch and a pull at the top of her wrist. Where the hell, or what the hell? She attempts to open her eyes, this time more slowly. She allows herself to breathe deeply, and her eyes begin to focus. The walls are white, ceiling white, and directly ahead of her is some kind of painting. It almost resembles a boy and his dog. She moves her head slowly to the left and notices cabinets, some signage. She moves slowly back to her right, looks at the painting again than moves slowly further. She see’s what looks like a television elevated into a post in the corner, followed by a bunch of computers. The beeping sound that she now realizes comes to and fro every 2-3 seconds. “Ahh, finally up. Good for you,” says a loud voice coming from the left. Ellen turns her head and her eyes fix on this larger than most man. He is balding, deep forehead and chin. He’s wearing enormous glasses that make his eyes leap at you. He has a white coat on with a few pens in the upper left pocket. “How are you feeling today?” Ellen opens her mouth, but her throat and lips are so dry all that is heard is a wheeze and a crack. The man quickly grabs a small Dixie cup and puts it to her lips. She sucks down the liquid, its rather warm but its wet and that’s all that matters. “Where…where am I?” “Belmont Medical Center. You took a nasty fall. You fractured your skull, broke 3 ribs, your shoulder, dislocated your wrist, and broke your leg. Plus not to mention you have numerous cuts and scrapes. It’s a good thing your boyfriend brought you here, otherwise you might be dead.” A swirl of events begins to storm through Ellen’s brain. The house, her friends, the murder, the blood, the guts, the cage, the fall. Ellen sits up quickly and looks at the doctor. “How long have I been here?” “Three days. You don’t remember much do you?” Remember much? What is he talking about? Of course she remembers everything. Wait, boyfriend? “You said my boyfriend brought me here?” Ellen’s head is starting to pound and the room is beginning to spin. “Yeah, big guy. He had long black hair. He said you guys broke into that house on the hill, you climbed up something and fell.” Ellen’s heart begins to race. Big guy with long black hair? Her limbs begin to go numb. “You know, that place is condemned. You are lucky you didn’t get killed in there. Plus there’s the breaking and entering crime we are also talking about. [i]Robyn’s body twitches as the knife is plunged so deep her in that she can feel the giant’s hand halfway in the opening made by the knife. She shakes the hair and blows the rest away and staring right at her his the blue and slumped face of Robyn. A smile comes across Ellen’s face, that is until she looks down and see’s an enormous hole in her friend’s stomach and chest. The hole is so large she can see Robyn’s spine. Ellen screams and begins to twist and contort herself. With every twist she hears a rattle. Where the hell is that sound coming from. She moves her stiff neck towards the sky and see’s her arms are bound with chains to some kind of cage. She starts to shake again, a desperate attempt to remove the chains. She feels something slither from her shoulder, lands on the bottom of the cage with a wet smack, followed by a slurp than another hollow wet slap as it falls further. Ellen moves her head and looks at her nude body and notices there is something wrapped around her. She uses her chin to move what’s on her other shoulder and watches as a long tube falls to the bottom of the cage, slips through and lands far below. She realizes two things. She’s caged high above the ground and two, well when she looks at the hole in Robyn again she realizes she is wearing the inner guts of her former friend. Ellen finally screams and begins to shake the cage violently.[/i] “….so we will be keeping you only another day or two until we know the swelling as went down than we will call your boyfriend and he can come” Ellen blurts out , “My friends. They are dead! They were butchered by some fucking maniac!” The man, whom Ellen has figured out now is a doctor stops dead in his tracks. His face gets pale and his mouth is agape. He is in shock by the loud outburst. He turns around, grabs his collar and whispers something into it. “What are you talking about sweetheart?” “My friends and I. We broke into that house.” Ellen’s head is spinning. Her emotions are about to explode. She knows what she’s about to say is far from believable. “We were just going in there for bragging rights because it’s suppose to me haunted you know.” Doctor nods. “When we got in there a giant man with a painted face killed all of them! He killed them and cut them up!!” “So let me get this straight. He killed ALL of your friends, and let you live?” Ellen’s breathing was starting to get heavy. She wanted to rip this bandage from her head because it was tight and made her head throb. “Yes! I don’t know why!” Ellen begins to sob uncontrollably. Her body shakes with each breathe. The doctor once again mumbles something into his collar. He looks at her chart briefly as Ellen cries. He flips through a few pages and scratches his head. “Ellen. Can I call you that?” Ellen just nods as she tries to stop herself from crying. It’s just making her head hurt more and her eyes burns. “As much as I’d love to help you, there are a few flaws in your story. Ellen that house has been abandoned for over 20 years now.” “NO! The giant painted man! He was there. He killed all of them! Shit what was his name?” The doctor sighs, as two nurses make their way in. They place a few needles on a tray next to her table. “Ellen. Was his name Bryan O’Neil?” “Yes!!! He told me to tell the world about him!” The nurses look at the doctor as he holds a hand up in front of them. He gets closer to the bed and leans over and gets within a few feet from Ellen. “Ellen, you suffered a very bad bump from your fall. I am fearing it is worst than I thought.” Ellen pulls her head back deeper into the pillow. The smell of coffee on his breathe is almost unbearable. “What do you mean?” The doctor erects himself. “Well I think you are delusional. The swelling of your brain is causing you to have visions of something horrible. I have some medication here for you. It’s going to knock you out but hopefully will cause the swelling to subside and these visions to vanish.” Ellen sits up straight and fast. She ignores the pain in her back and arms and head. “You don’t believe me?” The doctor sighs again as he flicks the top of the syringe holding the medication the doctor just told her about. “I’d really like to believe you Ellen. There’s just one problem. Bryan O’Neil was executed 12 years ago.” Ellen sits back in dismay. Executed? That can’t be. The only thing she could do was watch the doctor stick the syringe into her IV and feel her eyelids get heavier, and heavier, and heavier until she fades to black. Her eyes are still foggy but she can tell she’s lying on her back still staring straight into the brightest light ever. The murmurs are from people huddled around her. She starts to move her head and see’s a handful of doctors. There is a large blanket/sheet tented over her chest. She can see nothing and quite honestly feel nothing. 6 or 7 people were beyond this sheet partition. The one booming voice kept asking for different things, terms she’s never heard before in her life. “Wait, Doctor, if we cut that she could be paralyzed!” HUH? What on earth is going on down there. Ellen moans and shifts her head and shoulders as much as she could in hopes of letting them know that she is awake. “Doctor, patient is awake!” Ellen hears a sinister laugh. Appearing from over the partition was the giant man with the painted face. A surgical mask is covering his lips, but she can tell those evil black eyes anywhere. Ellen begins to scream as the scalpel quickly slashes along her throat. Ellen can feel the hot black puddle swim around her shoulders. She can’t speak nor scream as she watches the painted man, Bryan O’Neil, slice her throat open. Ellen opens her eyes and realizes it was all just a dream. Her heartbeat begins to slow down and the sweat from her forehead starts to dry up. She realizes she must get out of here, no one here believes her. The man said his name was Bryan O’Neil, and yet she comes to find out that he was executed 12 years ago? She hears some scuffling across the floor of her room, her nerves still on edge she sits up quickly only to see her doctor. A rush of emotion sets her at ease as she see’s the man here to help her. He is carrying another syringe. “Doctor, what’s that?” “Ohhh, it’s just something that will take the edge off for a bit.” She sits back as the syringe goes into her IV. She can feel the warm rush go through her veins. Her eyelids become heavy as he heart begins to palpitate under her chest which feels as if its being crushed. The doctor rubs his fingers through Ellen’s hair and whispers into her ear, “That name will never be spoken again in this town. Ever.” Ellen feels herself slip away. She’s become mute, her vision is fading, and her breathing is coming and going. She realizes now that the edge this medication took off was her life. Ellen slips into blackness, as the doctor turns off her heart monitor and watches her lifeless body go peacefully….
