The Passenger

15 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/10 I see the bright and hollow sky, Over the city's ripped backsides. And everything looks good tonight. - Iggy Pop In the end, it was easy. You simply… let go. Let go of sanity, let go of humanity, let go of emotion. To suppress Rasa was to endure a thousand migraines a second as your brain superheated and steamed in its own skull. To succumb to Rasa was to bathe in warm honey. You treaded heavy, sticky water beneath your own skin; unable to reach the surface, unable to sink into oblivion. La On a funeral pyre of burned amber leaf litter, its final seconds of life passed it by. Threadbare wings flapped aimlessly, sinking further into the quicksand of damp, composting soil. Its final, haunting song echoed from tree to tree, amplified by gnarled bark and softened by sodden moss. Over the crow, a shadow fell, eclipsing the withering afternoon sun and dulling the shine of its glassy black marble eyes. Maddened by the infections which festered deep within its wounds, and feathers shorn by the stony soil; the young boy stood over it was blinkered to the bird’s former majesty. Once, it had soared above him, its feathers a glossy, tar black and its song carrying for miles on the wind’s currents. Elvir Belial saw only its gasping, rotting throes. He saw the maggots which would soon infest its gas-bloated, decaying corpse and the scavengers which would gnaw the diseased flesh from its bones. He saw the fungi spores riddling the bird’s pitted bones as they were pulled under nature’s damp, leafy tide. A gust was caught by the clearing, the autumnal confetti swirling around the boy’s muddy boots. Then, as Elvir mercifully brought the brick down upon the crow, terracotta and rich crimson joined the amber leaves at his feet. La La Your fingers on the wheel felt prickly-numb, pins and needles scrambling the nervous system. From behind glazed, weary eyes, marbled with bloodshot red, you watched the rain streak the cracked windshield. Orbs of blinding amber dazzled you, while just the faint echo of frantic car horns trickled down your ear to the dungeon in which Rasa had imprisoned you. Threadbare tires screeched no longer as the last of their rubber was peeled away by the asphalt. The detuned radio vomited out disorientating static. Beside your bleeding left ear, the driver’s side unbuckled seatbelt hung. Through the city you were driven, waves of flashing neon washed down your eyes by the torrents of grimy rain which flushed the choking impurities from the carbon-rich night air. Strapped in a straightjacket of your own tissue and muscle fibers, an iron maiden of your own sharpened nerve endings, you were Rasa’s passenger. And all of it is yours and mine. It sung in your head, a voice of equal parts honey and strychnine. So let's ride and ride and ride and ride… A pull from the pit of your fearful stomach began to drag you down in as Rasa stamped your foot down on the accelerator. The gears groaned and wailed, Rasa reveling in the sound of mechanical anarchy. The blaring horns melded into one continuous drone. Your bones shuddered. Suddenly the amber glow above disappeared over the rain-swept horizon. Banana peels, fast-food wrappers and torn plastic carrier backs restrained the squeaky wiper blades. Singing, la la la la la-la la-la… The world around you, flushed of its vibrant color, came to a halt in a cloud of choking, oily smoke. La la la la la-la la-la… You’d arrived. La La La The tattered, charcoal hooded sweatshirt masked his war-torn face in anonymous shadow. Carried on the bustling tide of impatient humanity flowing along the sidewalk, he avoided the sparks of frustrated static and eruptions of temper which flared as human bodies scuffed and buffeted one another. A peppering of icy rain propelled on the piercing wind hooked his hood, peeling it back. Head bowing, he let himself be pulled deeper under the crashing waves of revelers, sinking to depths outside of your peripheral vision. He had made a promise to keep you tethered to reality, to peel back the black, toxic tendrils of Rasa if they were to consume you. You absolving him of such a duty back in Anaheim barely chipped his vow, let alone broke it. Within a thick brush of unkempt stubble, thin, nervous lips pursed. “What the fuck have you gotten yourself into…” A narrowed eye, heavy with suspicion, trailed ten meters behind you where a hunched, sneering goblin of a man slithered through the oncoming human traffic, your decaying odor hooking his hairy nostrils. Elvir Belial saw only the darkness with which you were infested. He saw the short-circuiting of your self-preservation instincts and the blown fuses of your nerve endings. He saw the decaying ruins of a diseased body crumble to dust in the rain. He saw himself mercifully free the last putrid breaths from Rasa’s black lungs. La La La La Generic hip-hop pumped out of the speakers like nerve gas, deadening the brain. Their modesty protected only by the blinking of pink and lilac strobes, a pair of dancers awkwardly twitched their bodies along to the demanding baseline. Faces sculpted by fake lashes, botox and collagen browsed the stage-side stools; lip-licking, clammy-handed stares ground dirt into their perma-tanned pores. Crumpled dollar bills were rubbed between fingertips, releasing their green, heady scent. Its dark hooks anchored in your eyeballs, Rasa dragged your gaze upon the dirty blonde of the pair. Perilously prancing on skyscraper heels, she did her best to mimic her fellow dancer, but every thrust was subconscious and every stroke apologetic. Swathes of dark eyeshadow and an airbrushing of blusher did little to age her face beyond borderline pubescent, while the narrowest of hips limply swayed atop foal’s legs. Your knees swung apart as you flopped a wad of bills down on the lip of the stage, a crack of the whip to bring her to heel. Rasa’s arousal fuelled a heartbeat which hammered on the walls of your fleshy prison like an angry neighbor. Its thoughts, vile and inhumane, bubbled bile up around you. On all fours, she shuffled towards you, oiled knees grinding a sludge out of the stage’s detritus. Unnaturally blue eyes subconsciously slipped towards the scar carved into your right cheek. As the strobes stroked her face, you saw the galaxy of freckles which swirled either side of her nose, radiated by the harsh pink light. “I don’t normally…” Trembling fingers drummed on the wad of bank notes. Her eyes struggled for somewhere to focus; too fragile to match your leery glare, hers slithered between your eyes to the split skin at the bridge of your nose, then the fresh wound parting your eyebrow where a piercing had been torn out. Rasa thoughts violated what scraps of your shredded soul it had yet to infect. They branded grotesque images onto the back of your retinas, painting over what you saw through the glassy windows. No inhibitions, no failsafes, no value assigned a perishable sacks of flesh and bone. Grabbing a handful of straw-like, dirty blonde hair, you - no - Rasa dragged her from the stage. With her jeweled nails scratching at and stabbing into your face, Rasa hissed into her ear all that it had in store for her. Every sickening act, every degrading intention, every orifice; each moistened word dripped with depraved lust. …and you’re going to fucking love every second of it… Her twiggy limbs flailed as she emitted a glass-shattering scream which rung through your skull, its piercing echo slashing into your brain. You fought Rasa with all of your will, muscle fibers fraying and splitting in the mental tug-of-war. Clumps of dry, burnt-out hair were tugged out at the root as a pair of doormen peeled you off of the terrified girl, her eyes weeping black mascara down her freckled cheeks. La La La La La As Belial’s crooked finger tenderly caressed the dagger’s blade, the pitter-patter of rain on its steel sung to him; an ominous, mournful chorus. It sung for you. La La La La La-La Your tongue prodded a tooth which hung from its last, bloody root. Grit ground under your nails as you clawed at the alleyway’s damp, mossy carpet. As your senses realigned, a tightly-bound corset of bruised ribs restricted lungs to shallow, wheezing breaths. Again, Rasa goaded, a lash of its tongue painting your lips with rich, phlegmy crimson. Slowly, it turned your head up towards the doorman‘s raised boot, an eerie, toothy grin greeting its treads. And put some fucking heart into it this time! “You be one fuckin’ crazy cunt, y’know that?” He accepted the invitation of your cheek’s swollen and split skin, stamping down with his heavy boot and bouncing your skull off of the grimy concrete. “Now, stay the fuck down.” Rasa’s reveling in the destruction of your body manifested itself in a shiver which pricked up and down your spine. Your skeleton, flesh and skin was just a meaty rag doll for it to hurl around and rip apart at the stitches. I only grabbed the ugly fucking skank to get closer to you, big boy… Rasa snaked across the alley floor and latched onto the doorman‘s ankle, sinking your teeth into his calf. Loosened teeth and bruised jaw lacked the bite needed to puncture the denim, and he kicked out, flinging you into the wall. A shower of brick dust and chips of mortar fell upon your dull aching head as it landed in a putrid puddle, gritty brown water washing the blood from your teeth. The impact disconnected you from your body, cutting you adrift underneath your skin. Peering out though unfocussed eyes, you saw the doorman’s face loom large, his hand clasping around your throat. A row of gnarled brass knuckles ploughed into your cheekbone. This… Rasa gasped. Is… Another metallic punch chipped away at your faltering bone structure. …So… Again. …Much… Again. …Fun! The doorman recoiled his fist for another sculpting blow, only to see dead eyes roll up into your skull as your head fell limply back. Regardless, he drove his fist down one last time, mangling the bolt of scar tissue which held together your right cheek. “You ain’t fuckin’ wrong, bitch.” He wrenched up a morsel of phlegm and spat down upon your bloodied, flayed face, then watched as his viscous spittle sank down your crimson-blushed nostrils. La La La La La-La La A gust was ricocheted by the alley, the sodden, autumnal confetti swirling around the man’s muddy boots. Then, as Belial mercifully held the blade of his dagger down to your throat, it caught the pale glow of the low, voyeuristic moon. “This frees us both,” he chewed between sharpened, goblin’s teeth. Christian Zenith had instructed him to watch over the host Rasa had infected, and now Elvir Belial would stand over the tide of black, toxic bile which was about to erupt from your swollen, bruise-mottled body. It was as close to an act of human kindness as he was capable of. A black veil was cast across the looming moon’s pitted face. A scarred arm, poking out from the rolled-up sleeves of a charcoal grey hooded sweatshirt, lashed around Elvir Belial’s throat, tugging him back into the shadows. In the darkness, Belial’s blade danced between slithers of light, carving the tattoos from the forearm which barricaded his brain from its blood supply. His fingers tingled, carbon dioxide-flushed tissue turning acidic beneath their prints. The dagger tumbled from his grip, its sub-atomically sharp blade slicing open the refuse sacks he was wrestled on to. Belial’s tongue rolled over his bottom lip, flicking to beckon air into his twitching, asphyxiating body. Before him he saw a moleskin coat stretched around a barrel chest, and a pale, ghostly hand reaching out towards him. “Henry…” A stream of sickly brown liquid trickled out of the punctured refuse bags, a rotting River Styx down which Henry Moloch would row him. La La La La La-La La-La Hazy consciousness peeled your eyelids back. The sounds of the city sounded tinny in your ear. You tried to heave in a deep breath to purge the heavy, dead air from your lungs, only for the cilice woven from your body’s muscle fibers and misfiring nerves to agonizingly ration your intake. The stars above burned through the city’s smog to tickle your optic nerve and blink the black cloud from your eyes. Knelt over you, a man pulled back his charcoal hood. The scant light painted a blurry portrait of his face’s right hemisphere and the awkward, vulnerable kindness in his eyes shone through. His look of pity was a look in the mirror. He was the last friend you had left in the world; a loyal companion who would dive into Rasa’s murky depths to breath life back into your choking, tar-filled lungs. A man bound by a promise, no matter how many times you tried to unravel the ropes of burden from his wrists. Deep in your memory this was ingrained; but like an old, derelict building, your brain had cut off its power and water, taking it off of the grid. Neural pathways crackled around it, none so curious as to stop and peer into the mysterious, vacant darkness they circled. “Who are you?”
