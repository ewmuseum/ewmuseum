Tacky–Tachyons Part 8

14 March 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/12 Dirt-A-Mania.com 2003 Posted by PowellFan47 Article: A Change in the Weather "New Comer "Hurricane" looks hell bent on making his voice heard in MOW:RTW as he shoots his mouth off stirring up all sorts of trouble…" ReekMarksWrestling.com 2005 Posted by XainX10 Article: When an Open Door Policy Hits You in the Ass! "Fresh off his departure from MOW:RTW the so called ‘Natural Disaster Formerly Known as the Hurricane’ Brad Cruz, made his debut in POW, Powers of Wrestling. The brazen young talent is already making enemies…" noholdsbarred.org 2007 Posted by ZettaBites777 Article: America Goes Hollywood "Last night ‘The SoCal Prophet’ Brad Cruz has officially won his first singles championship by winning the POW North American Title. ‘This is a first of many titles to come.’ Bradley said boastfully after his championship win. It does make one wonder are these empty promises by the cocky young gun or a written in stone prophecy of a SoCal Prophet? Either way it begs the question what’s next for Brad Cruz." "What do you mean I don’t have a job!?" September 5th 2008</> Brad stands over a table in the middle of a dimly lit restaurant. His manger Crystal seated at the table tries to calm her client. Crystal: "Bradley could you please calm yourself. You’re causing a scene." Bradley shakes his head in frustration and sits back down at the table. Brad: "It doesn’t make any sense." Crystal: "POW has completely folded. It’s over. CSN pulled their backing from POW and the company just caved in on itself." Brad places his palm over his face. Brad: "Why does this stuff always happen to me?" Crystal: "It isn’t just you. The entire roster and staff is out of a job. I heard executives were jumping out of windows." Bradley looks up raising and eyebrow in disbelief. Crystal: "Well, maybe it was just one executive, and he might of jumped out a first story window, but it’s still pretty messed up." Bradley picks at his salad but gives up in frustration. Brad: "What am I going to do? You have got to have some legit bookings lined up for me." Crystal: "People have caught wind of how difficult you can be." Brad: "I have do something for money. I like pretty things." Crystal: "No one wants to pay your rates, at least not in the states." Brad: "Not in the…? Oh no, not…" Crystal: "Mexico and Japan are throwing you some serious offers." Brad shakes his head no. Brad: "Last time I worked in Mexico they pelted me with batteries. Those little 8 Volts, and they hurt." Crystal: "What about Japan?" Brad mulls it over. Brad: "The food is alright and the people are nice enough, but you walk up to a wrong vending machine or hotel room and you’re in for a rude awakening. I’ve seen things Crystal, I’ve seen things." Brad shudders. Crystal: "Soiled panty vending machines and love hotels aside, it’s your best option, unless you want to work Puerto Rico." Brad shakes his head no. Brad: "I’m not getting knifed in no shower! What about GCW?" Crystal reaches across the table taking Brad’s hand into her own. Crystal: "Bradley, you’ve built somewhat of a bad reputation, and we’re in full on image rehab mode." Bradley shrugs. Brad: "It was never that bad." Crystal: "Oh really, because you had several complaints of harassment from several women in POW." Brad: "It’s not like it was sexual harassment, it was just good old fashioned harassment, fueled by contempt." Crystal: "You beat a man with a frozen bottle of water." Brad: "If you didn’t want me to weaponize bottled water, you shouldn’t be buying me ‘Arrowhead’. It sends a mixed message." Crystal: "You made fun of a wrestler until he up and quit." Brad: "I’m not going to lie, I’m kind of proud of that one. Serious you should have seen him cr-" Crystal squeezes Bradley’s hand. Crystal: "Stop." Brad falls silent and nods. Crystal: "If we can shake this rep I can get you to GCW. I’m not going to lie. It’s going to be a long tiresome road of toil but it leads to the ‘Promise Land’." Brad: "Forty years worth of toil?" Crystal: "Two maybe three tops." Brad nods his head. Brad: "… and I’ll be able to cover my expenses?" Crystal looks down at the table. Crystal: "If we tighten our belts, which means we’ll have finally have to do what we discussed." Bradley shakes his head no. Brad: "That’s not going to happen." Crystal tries to reassure her client with a squeeze of his hand, but he quickly pulls away. Brad: "It’s all I have of him! I give that up and I have nothing." Crystal: "Bradley the property is dilapidated. It’s nothing more than a pile of splintered" Brad: "It was my grandfather’s house, Crystal. It’s the sentiment. My grandfather raised my father in that house, and-" Crystal: "-and you can’t afford the tax on the property, much less build it back up. Your Grandfather may have left the property to when he died, but I doubt his intent was for it to be a burden. Its pulling your head below water." Brad's head slumps forward as he gives a reluctant and disappointed sigh. Brad: "Fine, sell the property. It’s just a patch of land." Crystal nods. She excuses herself from the table as she stands. Crystal: "I’ll get on it now, if you’ll excuse me." She walks over to Bradley placing her hand on his shoulder as she passes by. Crystal: "It’s for the best." She walks away as Brad slumps back down like a child reluctantly accepting his fate. Brad glances up watching the entrance of the restaurant as people enter and leave not really taking any real note of anyone. They’re all just walking hazy blurs. His eyes drift looking over to the hostess and seeing a man standing there waiting to be seated in a tacky all white three piece suit. The moment that shock of white enters his view he suddenly becomes overwhelmed by a dizziness and a flashing image in his head, of man in a field wearing the same suit. Bradley quickly shakes it off. This isn’t the first time that image flashed through his mind. It bothers Bradley until he glances to his left and sees a man grinning at him from a neighboring table. Stranger: "Good evening sir." Brad replies with an unsure tone. Brad: "Evening…" Stranger: "Quite the spitfire you have on your hands there." The Stranger points to Crystal who stands talking on the phone near the entrance of the restaurant. Stranger: "I bet that little spoon full of sugar can get you swallow anything, no matter how bitter the pill." Brad raises an eyebrow, unsure of this man’s angle. Brad: "What do you want, Stranger?" Stranger: "To offer my services. Your manager may be adequate for the small time dog and pony shows, but I’m on another level." Brad: "I have representation." Stranger: "Yes and she’s precious b-" Brad: "I have representation!" Stranger: "Alright, alright, fine. If she’s what you want, God bless." The stranger rises to his feet and slides a small business card across the Brad’s table. Stranger: "I’ll keep in touch." The Stranger slowly walks away exiting through the front of the restaurant. Bradley looks down on the table looking at the small business card. He picked it up and looks it once over on both sides. There’s no phone number, no email address, not even a name, just a black card with a white beveled M prominently centered on a field of black.
