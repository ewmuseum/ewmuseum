Smuggler's run Part 1

Date: Timeless Ahh, the Northern sector of the galaxy. So nice, so quiet, so serene. Too bad no one told the asshole on my tail about that. It's amazing what a bounty on your head will do. Apparently whoever it was that originally put the ten thousand credit bounty on my head, no longer wanted me brought in alive. They wanted me dead or alive, and upped the bounty to fifty thousand. That brought every damn wanna be bounty hunter out of the woodwork. Including this idiot on my tail. He'd been tracking me since I left the planet Valis the equivalent of three days ago. I kept loosing him and he kept finding me. I finally figured out why. It was Meatskin T-Wreck. A true cyborg in every sense of the word. Living tissue over a metal endoskeleton. He was more computer than I was and had decided to become a bounty hunter to constantly get himself upgraded...I gotta find out who's putting him together. WHOAH! That shot was a little too close for comfort. I hit a switch on my control panel and my new and improved shield generator kicked up the protection I needed as Meatskin fired at me again. 'I gotta find out who put this bounty on my head so I can get this shit over with.' I said out loud, 'Maybe I can pay him off and get every one with a ship and a laser cannon off my ass.' Off to my left I could see the Fallout Zone. Only the bravest pilots in the galaxy dared to fly through it. Rumors had it that of the hundreds of pilots that dared it, less than a third of them survived. The Fallout Zone was actually four zones that separated the Southern sector of the galaxy from the Northern. Zone one was was a radiation zone. The clouds of radiation that lit up the skies were the result of the five indusrial planets in the sector. The planets, on their own were harmless, but when the particles of the different chemicals were bleched into space and mingled they formed a radioactive chemical compound that was deadly. The results were varied. It all depended on how much contact you had, but they all ended the same. Sometimes you got a skin rash that ended with your skin peeling off in layers. Other signs were large blisters which ended with your skin melting off. The lucky ones died instantly, usually within seconds of exposure. However, with no wind in space the radioactive cloud had formed itself into a maze, one wrong turn and you flew into the cloud and nasty things happened. The next zone was equally dangerous. An asteroid field. The result of two planets that had collided during a war a hundred years earlier. At least that's what I've been told. The worst part about an asteroid field is that it's constantly moving so you've got no way of safely getting through it, then there was the size of the asteroids. Some no bigger than a man's fist, others the size of a mid-size transport ship, others the size of a small city. One wrong turn in an asteroid field and you were space dust. Which leads to zone three. The wreckage of the ships that didn't make it. Oh yeah, and the zombies. Yeah, space zombies, those that survived the radiation poisioning, but not dying from the crashes. The worst part was the radiation somehow bonded them to the ships, so the result was disgustingly mutated zombies with parts of ships melded with their bodies. Ship plates sicking out of arms, legs chests, and even their faces. Some still had working lasers and thrusters. Those were the worst. If you were still alive after all that there was zone four. A series of six planets that were far too close for comfort, so close in fact that bolts of lightning danced between the planets. The planets were too big to fly around, so most tried to go through them. As I stated earlier not many made it. 'You wanna play? Let's play!' I said turning sharply towards the radiation maze. I turned everything over to my heads up display, and let it guide me through, I turned left and right and went up and down, and around and in and out and over and under and through. At one point it felt like I was going in circles, eventually the maze ended and I shot out the other end none the worse for wear. Too bad Meatskin couldn't say the same. He must have flown into the cloud at some point. He popped up on my viewscreen trying to threaten me with imminent defeat, but most of his human tissue, and a large chunk of his metal endoskeleton were missing. One of his eyes was hanging out by a wire. His jaw was hanging on my a mere bit of skin and one hinge. 'Ahhh ganna gat ya!' he said. 'Sorry pal, you're breaking up.' I said switching the viewscreen off. 'Litearaly I hope.' I then plunged into the asteroid feild, again I let my HUD guide me through the constanly moving field of deadly rocks. I swerved and doged and even flew through some of the larger ones, my HUD even guided me through some close calls when two asteroids collided. I lost a bit of my sheilds by reacting a second too slow a couple of times, but I came out for the most part unscathed on the other side. This time Meatskin managed to avoid damage. He popped up on my viewscreen again, having managed to fix himself somewhat. He did that while flying through an asteroid field? Impressive. 'You're not getting away.' He said, his voicebox fixed, as was his jaw. He was still missing a good chunk of his head, but wires from the ship were plugged into where the missing circuts were. So that's what he did, he was running off his own ships power until he could get himself repaired. We plunged into zone three and came under fire. The zombie ships fired on us and my shields took a pounding so I started firing back. Most of the ships dissolved in a spray of sparks, and blood. The zombies flash frozen when killed again, it wasn't as bad as everyone made it out to be, as most of the zombies couldn't see and were aiming where they sensed the ships flying. However, they had no defense and were easy targets. Meatskin fired on them too and we both came out on the other side, the six planets looming ahead of us. We dove in and flew through the crisscrossing lightning bolts narrowly avoiding getting hit. I would however need to upgrade my sheild generator again soon. I moved in close to one of the planets, close enough to skim the atmosphere, and then flew in a wide arc away from the planet. Meatskin stayed right on my tail, I spun away as a lightning bolt narrowly missed me, but not him. It hit him full on. I watched as his ship exploded silently behind me, and a quick fireball was extingushed by lack of oxygen. I came out on the other side, alive, but my ship would need some serious repairs. I headed into the Northern sector and towards the nearest planet where I hoped they had a repair shop, maybe with a new shield generator. What I didn't know until much later is that there was a tracking device on my ship and Lex the Marauder took the long way around the Fallout Zone, allowing me a false sense of security. Bastard.
