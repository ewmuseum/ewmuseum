The Lost Years Pt 2 - Goin To California

30 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/30/11 “Dorian, We Need To Talk” “Dorian?” I was drenched from my workout. But that didn’t stop me from turning and seeing Jason’s wife Kelly standing there. She was wearing a loose t-shirt and had her hair pulled back in a pony tail. I started to towel myself off. “Yeah.” Kelly looked back down the hall leading into the room that I had originally called my office, but have lately made into a small workout room. “You have a visitor.” It wasn’t something that I had often since I moved in. I respected Jason and Kelly enough that I wasn’t going to bring anyone home, especially not with them having a young son. But I was even more surprised when Araceli walked into the doorway. “Araceli, how are you doing?” I had been almost a month and a half since we had last seen each other. We talked on the phone from time to time, but this visit was a shock to me. She walked into the room and placed her hand on my chest. “Dorian, we need to talk. You should probably sit down for this.” I was starting to feel confused. I wasn’t sure if it was because of why she was there, or maybe it was a side effect of the PSD that had knocked me out of the ring. I walked over to the chair that I had left in the room from when it was my office. “OK, what’s going on?” I didn’t know what was coming next. I had a bad feeling about what she was going to say. I had a feeling that she was going to say the two words that no single man, especially not one that was less than a year removed from getting divorced and was currently unemployed. “Dorian, uh….how do I say this?” Kelly walked over to Araceli and placed a hand on her shoulder. “Dear, I think he’s already figured it out.” “Mine?” It was one night. Every man thinks that they can’t become a father because of one night. “Yeah, it’s yours. But I just want you to know that you aren’t going to have do anything. I’m ready to…” “Bullshit. Bull-fucking-shit. You don’t get to make that decision for me.” “Dorian, I just don’t want you to feel obligated to do anything.” Araceli kneeled before me and took me by the hands as she was saying this. “It’s too late for that. I’m going to be a father. I AM obligated.” I looked up and I saw a smile on Kelly’s face when she heard that. She first met me back when I was nothing more than some snot faced little kid that idolized his older brother. She’s watched me grow and mature, but until that moment she always looked at me as if I was still a kid. Now she realized what kind of man that I had become. “If you ever decide to not want this, I understand.” “I’m not going to pull that card. It isn’t fair to you, and it isn’t fair to our child.” “Don’t say something like that if you don’t mean it. I can do this on my own, Dorian. I’m not asking for your help.” “Well, whether or not you are asking, you’re going to have it. You aren’t going to go through this alone. I promise.” “Dorian, there’s a problem” The only problem with Jason picking me up from the airport is that there wasn’t enough room in the escape to completely stretch my legs out in. At the time, I thought that I would eventually become a front office worker for Durant-Ryan Athletics, get a chance to fly from Detroit to Toronto in that beautiful plane that is parked in a hangar in Detroit. Instead, I was helping Jason unload his and Samantha’s luggage after getting back from Toronto myself. They were heading back to Arizona so Samantha could be with her mother and older brother. Jason was going to spend a day there and then fly back. They were going to leave the day before, but Jason was able to convince Serena to let him have one more day with his daughter. After all, this was the longest that he had her since the divorce. I could see that it was killing him, not being able to spend all the time that he wanted with her. That was one thing that SCCW was allowing him, a much reduced schedule, so that when he had her, he could spend more time with her. I knew that in a little over 36 hours, I would be back here, picking Jason up and heading back to the house. Kelly was going to be home from a business trip in a few hours, but she had a car service to take care of that. So I was going to go back to the house, and just relax. And that’s exactly what I did for the next 36 hours. I just sat back, and I relaxed. I watched a couple of movies and I fell asleep on my couch. Dorian, are you there sweetie? When I heard that voice, it jarred me awake. To this day, I couldn’t tell you if it was something that was on the TV that I left playing or if it was the remnants of a dream, but I could have sworn that I heard Keiko’s voice. I had looked over at the clock on my cable box. It was 4 in the morning. I looked at my phone, there was a text. It was from Araceli, thanking me for being there for her and for our child. She wasn’t going to go through this alone. I was tempted to text her back, but it was late. I fell back to sleep on that couch. The next day, I was sitting at Jason and Kelly’s breakfast table. I was enjoying what was left of the box of Cocoa Puffs that they had picked up for Samantha. Kelly was reading the paper and eating an egg white omelet. “So, Dorian, there was something I wanted to discuss with you. Since Jason isn’t here to try and talk both of us out of it, I think that this would be the perfect time.” I was worried that maybe I had overstayed my welcome in their guest house. “Listen, just give me a few weeks to find a new apartment….” Kelly lowered the paper and shot me that look. Guys, you know the look that I’m talking about. It’s the ‘are you that fucking stupid?’ look that has emasculated many men in the past. “Dorian, you’re family. You’re free to stay here as long as you want. No, I was talking about a different part of your future, especially since you’re now going to be a dad.” I looked at her with a little bit of confusion. It wasn’t because of the concussion, but rather, I had no clue what she was talking about. “OK, I give.” I still didn’t have a clue what she was talking about. But then again, I never claimed to be able to read minds either. “I want to offer you a job. Within Durant-Ryan.” I looked at her like I wasn’t hearing what I thought I was hearing. “You want to offer me…” “Honey, I know that money’s been tight for you the last month, with you’re going to Toronto. On top of that, you don’t have insurance. This way, you don’t have to worry about what’s going to happen when your child comes into this world.” She didn’t know that we were planning on our child being born in Toronto, that way, we wouldn’t have to worry about the health care. “Kelly, you know that Jason wouldn’t want you to…” “Dorian, you’re a little less than a year removed from suffering a severe concussion. You’re still suffering from PCD, and you’re barely able to afford your visits to the neurologist.” She was blunt, but she was right. “And I know that you would eventually like to get out of our guest house and into your own place. Right now, you can’t afford that.” Again, she was right. After all, I did lose my ass in the divorce. That’s something us Ryan Men need to do, find better attorneys. “What kind of job are you thinking of?” “Spokesman for the company, to help usher in a new direction. Damage Inc has been losing money over the last couple of years, and I think that it has to do with the fact that we are paying a boatload to Metallica over the licensing of the name.” Kelly didn’t want me to know that it was part of the reason that Jason had gone back on the road, it was his way of coping with the fact that a business that he helped to create, that he sacrificed the little time that he had back during his MSW days was now on the verge of collapse. “What do you need me to do?” “Go to shows and conventions, kind of like what you are doing now.” I had never worn gear made by Damage Inc, and now she wants me to talk about a new brand that she and Jason are going to be introducing. “I want you to try and line up fighters to wear the gear, a stable of sorts.” “Focusing on MMA?” I knew nothing about that world. That was Jason’s thing. I was hoping that she wasn’t going to try and get me to talk to guys that think that the form of combat entertainment that I came from was beneath them. “Somewhat. Any type of martial arts really. We’re going to try and compete on a larger stage.” “Turning wrestling away?” I knew Jason didn’t like this. That’s why he’s not being asked to do it. As much as he tried to stay out of the business, he couldn’t. It was in his blood, and he wasn’t going to turn his back on the fraternity of the mat. “No, just looking into new directions.” “I’ll have to think about it.” I wasn’t sure that this was the route that I wanted to go in my life, but Kelly did have some valid points. I was about to bring a child into the world, and I didn’t know when I was going to be able to get back into the ring. I had finished my breakfast and spent the day catching up on the happenings in GCW. I didn’t tell Jason or Kelly, but they had contacted me for an appearance while I was in Toronto. I ended up turning down the offer, because I didn’t want to return there without being able to wrestle. By the time Jason had gotten home from his trip, he was tired and wanted to just relax. He invited me to watch the Diamondbacks and Padres game. First time we watched a game together in a long time. When I was in the NWCPac, I had lived just outside of San Diego and became a fan of the Padres. He was a season ticket holder for the Diamondbacks in a former life. I could tell that Jason’s mind was on something else. It had been less than a day and he already missed his little Samantha. Made me think about what it would be like when my child was born. “So, kid, you think that you’re going to be hardened against it, but there is going to be a time after you hold your son or daughter for the very first time that your heart melts. It happens to most of us. It’s part of our DNA. Dad was the same way when you were born. It killed him every time he lost one of us. Sammy and me when we moved to Denver. You when you went to live with Sammy.” As he said this, I looked at the medallion that he had hanging from his neck. Sammy had gotten them for the three of us when we last got together as brothers and sister. Jason and I were heading to London to wrestle for a promotion there. It simply read “Ryan Family, Today and Forever.” I couldn’t think too much about it when my phone started to ring. I looked at the caller ID and recognized the area code as being from the State of Northern California. I only knew a few people there. The Callions……and Andrea. Of course, I did what any normal person getting a call at almost midnight would do. I didn’t answer. I figured that if it was important, they would leave a voice mail. Of course, I didn’t know that the call was so important that the person that doesn’t have a good sense of time decided to send me a text. ‘Call me, Dorian. There’s a problem. A’ Fuck me with a broomstick. Problems for her only meant one thing. I huge problem for me. “I Hated Flying” Last night, I was sitting comfortably in my brother’s theater room, watching as the Padres lost to the Diamondbacks. Today, I find myself somewhat lucky. Kelly was able to score me a business class ticket on this flight to the Bay Area. I hated flying commercial. Stale peanuts, SkyMall catalog, and boring conversation. At least the stewardess……sorry, I mean flight attendant……was not only attractive, but also very courteous. I lucked out and the gentleman that I was seated next to had apparently taken a sleeping pill before we had taken off, so he was out like a light before we were even in the air. I wondered what the problem that Andrea needed me to fly out here for was. I called Araceli to let her know that I was going to be out of touch a little bit, but that I would call her as soon as I was back on terra firma. I just hoped that nothing would happen to her, or the baby. “Excuse me, miss. Can I get a bottle of water?” I asked the flight attendant. It was funny. I have flown enough that I could take a round trip flight to Japan on the airline’s dime, but being in an airplane was a nerve wracking experience. It wasn’t that I didn’t like flying, it’s that I hated it. I never got used to the idea of travelling in a giant projectile that I had no control over. The water was to try and calm my nerves. The music on my iPod was to try and keep me from hearing the sounds of the engine. I hated flying. Especially since I couldn’t find a direct flight from Detroit to San Francisco. “Now, Please, Be Reasonable” Finally, after two planes, the decision not to take an in-flight sedative, and finally landing, I powered my phone back on. There was a text from Araceli. She was thinking about me, and hoped that I got to San Francisco safe. There was also a text from Jason asking me to bring back some stuff that the Callion brother’s set aside for him from their dad’s old wrestling school. I had a few hours to kill before I had to be in Cupertino anyway. I flipped through the contact list on my phone, and found a number that I hadn’t called in a while. “Derek, it’s Dorian. Jason wanted me to come by and get some stuff that apparently your dad wanted him to have.” I listened to Derek talk as I walked towards the shuttle to take me to where I could pick up my rental car. Talking about how some of the kids that he and his brother, Josh were training looked like they could make the jump to the So Cal indy scene. Personally, I didn’t care, as I didn’t like Josh and Derek all that much. I was doing this as a favor to my brother that was all. I had lived in San Francisco for a while when I returned to wrestling after the NWC closed down, and I never called these two guys, never hung out with them, and managed to avoid them when I went to visit their father. Derek is now talking about how he was now looking at making the wrestlers that train there into pure athletes. I wanted to throw up. When I was at that school, Derek was trying to do that to his dad’s dismay. Finally, after a session, I looked at Derek and told him that you can’t make a pure athlete. They are born, not bred. He never understood that. After about an hour of hearing him talk, dealing with the clerk at the car rental place and finding my way to the 101, I was finally on my way to pick some stuff that my brother wanted me to pick up while I was here. I was just hoping that these idiots had the forethought of whatever it was in containers proper for shipping. After another 45 minutes, I arrived at the Silicon Valley Combat Sports Academy. I looked at the sign, and just had to laugh. These guys knew less about mixed martial arts than I knew about how to repair a plane. I walked into the gym. I looked around at the young people getting worked out. Derek was busy showing some kids how to bump. Looks like he’s mellowed out a little bit, not trying to break the kids that have come here to learn how to wrestle. “Dorian, how are you doing?” an older woman asked as I walked in. “Elizabeth, what are you doing here?” I responded as I gave her a giant hug. “I thought I would bring the kids some home cooking, like I did when James was still here.” Elizabeth was the Callion’s next door neighbor. She watched the kids after their mother left them and their father. For many of the young men and women that trained at this gym, she became a motherly figure. “Still making those amazing sandwiches?” I stayed with her when I was training here. She did it as a favor to my brother. I remembered every night, I would get home from a hard training session, and she left for me a sandwich made from whatever was dinner that night. “Yes, and if you wanna stop by, I can throw together some leftover brisket for you.” “Sounds grea…..” “NOW? PLEASE, BE REASONABLE.” Everyone in the gym turned to the office, where Josh was on the phone. He was trying to get his license to promote Mixed Martial Arts, but the State Athletic Committee had blocked him from getting it due to his finances. “I know you think that I’m not going to be able to pay the fighters for the card. But I can assure you that as soon as the financing comes in I won’t have that as a worry….Fine.” He hung up the phone and walked out of the office. “Hey, Dorian.” Josh was a little less of a pain in the ass, but he was still a weasel. When I was training here, there was a concern that one of the employees was skimming from the school’s finances. After a few weeks of investigating, it turned out that Josh was funneling some of the money from the school into an investment that he wanted to make. The investment ended up going nowhere and it nearly cost his father the school. Jason bailed the school out with the understanding that Josh would no longer have access to its finances. Now, Josh was trying to get his foot into promoting, but that black mark from the past keeps coming back to haunt him. “What’s up, Josh? Problems?” “State is trying to keep me from running a show, that’s all. I have almost all of the financing for it.” At this point, I was glad that he didn’t know that I had become involved in the Durant-Ryan. Otherwise, I know he would ask for me to try and get them money from Jason and Kelly. “You think your brother might be able to help out?” “I don’t know, after what happened with the school….” Josh looked me dead in the eye. “That was years ago. I’ve changed. I’m not asking for a lot, just enough to make sure that I can cover the fight purse.” I shook my head. I couldn’t believe the audacity of this guy. “Was this why you called him to pick something up?” Josh was surprised. “No. In fact, I don’t want you to take things the wrong way. I do have some stuff for your brother. Stuff that belonged to dad that I thought that he would like.” “If you want him to sponsor you, write up a proposal and fax it to him. I don’t have time to talk about it right now.” “Well, it’s back at the house. I won’t be able to get back there till later on tonight.” I took a look at my watch. “Well, I have to head to Cupertino, I’ll call you when I’m on my way back through to pick the stuff up. Elizabeth, if you want, I’ll stop by your place later on as well.” Unfortunately, events that would take place later that night would make me have to cancel both of those. To be continued....
