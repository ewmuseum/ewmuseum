Beautiful Day

5 February 2007

It might be the most amazing I've felt my entire life. It was like all the doors in the world opened in front of me, everything that you ever dreamed about getting was yours. You had the world in your hands and no one could ever take it away from you. Adrenaline and joy in its purest form. A moment that I'll never forget for the rest of my life. Sitting in the middle of that ring just staring down at it. It was real and it was mine. I spent all night looking at wrestling sites, and seeing how happy people were for me really brought a tear to my eye. While I wasn't crying like a little baby, I was laying there in my bed. Staring at the ceiling, but I never got a bit of sleep. My life was too euphoric, on a natural high of the likes I've never been on before. Life was a very vibrant and beautiful thing. Even today when I wake up in the morning I run to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship just to see that it's there. To see that this life is real. To see that this wasn't just some unattainable pipe dream. But I can't just stop here. I understand that with my new dream come true comes a great responsibility. Something that you never really understand unless you've been there. To those kids and those fans... you become something more than just another wrestler in the fray trying to make a name for yourself. You arrive. You become a hero. A role model. Because they say with great power comes great responsibility. And responsibility is something I've never really handled well... ---- Chapter One: Easier To Run ---- Summer 2005... High Noon. Texas. The land of cowboys and outlaws. Remember the Alamo. The Lone Star State. There was a lot of history in this state, and all of it had something in common. It was hot. Very hot. Overhead the sun beat down on this hot summer day. Temperatures were on the rise and you could already see heat distorting the vision of the environment, creating little tiny waves along the ivy that grew along the side of the clay colored decrepit apartment. Growing in and out of the building due to years of neglect. Outside the apartment an older man wearing jean shorts and a tank top rocked back and forth, waving the day's newspaper like a fan to cool himself off. A box fan rattled in the window next to him, blasting the searing hot air into the lobby of the project housing. He took a drag of his cigarette, his hearing obscured by the fan as a black Chrysler Crossfire pulled up around the corner. Closing his eyes he blew the black smoke out of his mouth in a long stream. Trying to think only of the sweet tobacco between his lips and not the thriving heat beating down upon his leather-like aged skin. "Excuse me." The voice of a woman made him jump up out of his seat making his cherry red burning cigarette fall out of his mouth and onto the floor. Angrily he stamps his foot down upon it. "Gawddamnit woman!" The man screamed now pacing back and forth, almost like he's trying to avoid having a heart attack, "Ya can't jus sneak up ona ole man like dat! Couldn ya see I was tryin ta take a nap here ya lil bitch?!" She didn't say a word at first, she couldn't help but feel a bit amused at the over-reaction of the cranky old man. Her cerulean blue eyes watched him move back and forth behind a pair of Oakley sunglasses, trying desperately to hold back a snicker. "Have you seen Markus today, Webster?" Finally the words escaped her lotus pink lips. "I dun giveadamn about no Markus." The man muttered angrily before sitting back down in his oak rocking hair, giving a menacing glare to the golden blonde haired woman, "Now get along. Let an ole man getem some sleep Dammit." "Well that's no fucking help..." She muttered under her lips as she walked past the senile old man into the lobby of apartment. Luckily not only was the man a senile fool, he was also far too deaf to hear a word she said. Wearing a pink tube top and a pair of khaki capris she was certainly dressed for the weather, although wearing an outfit a bit out of character. There was no doorman, nor was there anyone in the lobby. Just the sound of an old rattling box fan accompanied her journey inside the Colony Trace Apartment Complex. Opening the wooden door to the staircase it creaked out in response. A sea of heat hit her face almost like entering a sauna as she took a step. A pale yellow light was the only light source in the aging staircase, dimly illuminating her journey up to the third floor. It was almost like a horror movie entering the building. There were definitely things alive and living in here, but who in the hell would want to? No air conditioning, fans were practically useless. How could a person live like this? Perhaps growing up in the rich suburbs of Chicago, and living the past few years in the Estate of one of the richest men in the world jaded her perspective a little. She of course had a choice, but the people here did not. It's the only thing in the world they had. The projects of Dallas, Texas. The hospitality of Christian Zenith is second to none of course. When he gives someone their freedom, he hooks them up with the biggest shit hole money can buy. What a classy guy. The rusted metal doorknob was hot to the touch as she grabbed it only long enough to turn before pushing the door open with her shoulder. Her destination was right across the hall. Apartment Number 11C. Reaching her hand down into her pocket she pulled out a set of keys before sliding one into the lock. Putting her ear up to the door she didn't hear a thing. No music. No television. No nothing. The usual sound of Kenneth Anger movies were non-existent. A bit worried, she by-passed her usual step of knocking on the door and shouting out his name and turned the key. Once again pushing the door open with her shoulder she quickly jumped inside. All the windows were open, and newspaper looked to be scattered around the apartment by the little breeze that seemed to make it through. "Markus?" A hint of paranoia accompanied her voice as she took the Oakley's off her face and placed them onto her shirt. Entering the hallway she opened the bathroom door. No one. Backing up she quickly spun around and pushed the bedroom door open. Vacant. Running her hands through her hair she looked around at the abandoned room. A couple pizza boxes scattered across the floor and a single light left on in the kitchenette. "...Fuck." She said aloud as she thought about her next step. Worse case scenario would be Christia... RING! "FUCK!" She screamed as she reached down into her pocked pulling out her cell phone. The ID read "CHRIS". Everything that could go wrong was. Peering down at the phone she hesitated for a moment before flipping it open. She knew she had to. If she didn't the police would be there in no-time. Maybe she could talk her way out of this. Maybe she could convince him everything would be okay. "Hello?" She spoke sheepishly after flipping open the phone. "Hey Vivica, how's everything going?" The familiar voice of Christian Zenith rang in her ear. Freak-out mode had began to set in her mind. This didn't look good. "Fine... fine!" She said pacing back and forth in the middle of the apartment. One hand holding the phone up to her ear, the other one in her pocket. Trying to think of excuses, and come up with answers for every question he might ask. "Good." Christian said, "How's Markus holding up in his own place?" "Yeah, he's fine too." Vivica answered almost immediately. "Can I talk to him?" Christian asked. Of course, she hadn't thought of a really good comeback for that one. Her heartbeat picked up as she took quick breaths. Come on Vivica. Think girl, think! "Viv?" He questioned. "He... uh..." "What's wrong? Where's Markus?" Christian asked. "I... uh...." "VIVICA." Zenith raised his voice, obviously sensing now that something was terrible wrong, "Where. Is. Markus?" "I... err..." She paused for a moment, trying desperately not to lose control of herself and be able to process her thoughts and emotions, "... I'm... not sure." "Dammit Vivica." Christian went from worried to pissed, it didn't take much. Pissed was always just a half of a second away. "I give you one fucking job. I sent you to Texas, and I tell you to do ONE. FUCKING. THING. Keep an eye on Markus, take progress reports. You already practically killed the guy, apparently keeping TABS on him is far too much to ask." "He was here last night! We talking a little bit about wrestling and..." "But he's not there now. Is he Vivica?" "Not exactly, but I can find him! I'm sure!" "You know what... don't bother." Christian spoke harshly, "You've already fucked up this project enough don't you think? I'll find someone more competent to take over. Go home and do whatever it is that you're fucking good at, because I sure as hell can't figure it out." "I can do this!" "Goodbye." CLICK. "SON OF A BITCH!" Vivica J. Valentine threw the cell phone across the room before kicking a pizza box and over turning a table. No matter how hard she tried and no matter what she did, every little thing that was asked of her seemed to backfire and the most horrible and humiliatingly way possible. Falling down on her knees she clinched her fists and took deep, trembling breaths trying to center herself. Why did this always happen to her? Why is it that whenever someone, anyone, counted on her she dropped the ball. The United States Gymnastics Team wants her to compete in the Olympics, she runs away from home. Christian wants her to get a job, she almost kills a man. Christian wants her to make sure Markus doesn't run away, she goes and finds the apartment empty. She knew she wasn't cut out for this, but what was she cut out for? Holding back the tears that were attempting to well up in her eyes, she saw a yellow flyer that stuck out amongst the ransacked newspaper all over the floor. TOTAL J CUP 2005. In big bold letters across the top of the flyer. Next to it were note scribbled down. An entry number, contacts, what appeared to be an address for a hotel in Mexico City. She only had one chance to avenge herself and this was it. Folding up the paper she put it into her pocket, making sure whatever bastard Christian sent to pick up the pieces would never find it. She'd do it herself. That would show them she was competent. That would show them she could handle it. She'd show them all what she was made of. ---- Chapter Two: The Burden We Bare ---- TOTAL J CUP 2005. That's where her dream started. Her first wrestling match on her own, far far away from Christian Zenith. It had been a little over two years but it seemed almost like an eternity. Professional wrestling was nothing more than a pipe dream, and every move she made and every word she said was run through the fine-tooth comb of Zenith Corporation. She lived an entirely different life, she was an entirely different person. A person not of her own free will, a person trying desperately to adapt to her surroundings to please the ones who she needed to please. Now she was free. Free as a bird. Living the girlhood dream of being a GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Going from city to city to city, trying desperately to keep up with the media blitz that her title win had caused. They were having a field day with the pretty little firecracker blonde being the World Champion. Between all of that though, Vivica was forced to face the responsibility of her own actions she took along the way. She'd stopped caring about the ones she loved. Over the past few months on the way to achieving destiny, she'd made some mistakes. She'd be the first to admit that. Maybe she was a little too hard on Lia especially. Lia was the only constant in her life since Total J Cup. The driving force behind what she accomplished. Lia Ambrosi truly was an amazing woman. A woman, that even though she might've treated a little badly, was someone who she felt like she could only HOPE to be like someday. Once the ecstasy of the big win had ended, she now found herself in a hotel room the night before her first world title defense on WorldWide against Chris Bagwell. Looking at her own reflection in the title belt laying in her arms... she saw a champion. But she'd also seen someone who'd lost a little of what made her... well... herself. With all the media dates she hadn't even hardly seen Lia Ambrosi outside of the game show and the tag match. Not even close to enough time to sit down with her and tell her how she really felt. To tell her that she was sorry for not being able to handle the pressure Caldera and The Establishment had put on her. Sorry that she didn't trust Lia's judgement after she'd admitted to making a mistake. Sorry that she'd put her friendship in jeopardy over the World Championship. They say that's what gold does to people. She had no idea until now. Suddenly the lock on the door to her hotel room clicked and she looked surprised at who could possibly have an extra keycard to her room, even more so at 2:00AM. Slowly the door opened as Vivica grasped the World Championship tight, ready to use it as a weapon if she had to. Luckily for her, it was a familiar face that walked through the door. "You ears must've been burning." It was the only thing Vivica could manage to say at that point in time as Lia Ambrosi closed the door behind her. In actuality she had a million things she wanted to say, but in her own stubborn way she couldn't quite manage to find a way to say them. She was too proud to admit that she was wrong. One could say that Lia was much the same way. Perhaps it was why they understood each other so well, and shared such a strong bond. "Pardon?" Lia responded as she approached the bed. "I was just thinking about you..." Vivica said looking at Lia and then looking back at the World Heavyweight Championship in her hands. "It looks good on you." Lia said, trying to force a smile in the awkward situation that had arose between the two. "Thanks." Vivica also forced a smile before it quickly disappeared off of her face. "I..." The Fearless Phenom paused, almost being able to get her thoughts out into word before they shot straight back down her throat. "You're sorry?" Lia completed her thought for her. Their minds were on the same wavelength. It was on her mind too, that's why she made her way to the room. "Yeah... I'm sorry." Vivica stated somewhat less confidant than every other word she'd ever spoken before in her life, "I'm sorry things happened the way they did and..." "It's okay." Lia said, somewhat understated herself, "It happens. We all make mistakes right?" "I suppose." Vivica said before looking back down at the title. It was almost like she wanted the championship to speak for her through this surprisingly uncomfortable moment. She couldn't make eye contact. She couldn't just act like nothing happened anymore. It'd been eating away at her too much. "That's much more than just a belt Vivica." Lia finally chirps in trying to break the uneasy silence, "With that belt comes A LOT of responsibility. A lot of what you say and what you do will be put under a microscope. Every move you ever make will be put next to every single other person who has ever held that belt in their hands just like you are now." Vivica looked up at Lia from under her blonde hair. "Not just by management either." Lia continued, "By fans, by everyone whose ever worked for or been associated with GCW. That belt gives you power. A power that whenever you say something people listen. To the wrestlers you're a target. You have what they want, they listen because they have to. To the fans, you're like a super hero. Living out their dreams for them. Doing things that some of them will never even have the opportunity to do." "Like a super hero..." Vivica smirked a little bit looking into the belt, trying to think of some corny name to go along with it, ripping off Daredevil was the first thing she could think of, "...The Woman Without Fear." "You'll be all over magazines, television, radio... tenfold to what you're used to." Lia continued, "Reaching out to people that otherwise would have absolutely not clue who you are. Not just wrestling fans either Viv. Late Night talk shows, NETWORK TELEVISION. A single appearance can give someone an impression of you for the rest of their lives Vivica. With that title, you can change people's lives." Vivica again just sat back and listened to Lia continue to talk. "That's why I was so hard on you Vivica. You can't be like Steven Caldera and The Establishment, or like Bryan Mayhem and Shaman." Lia spoke, "You have to keep it away from those people. It's more important than pride. It's about being a role model. It's about doing things the right way. Like all those people did for you when you were a little girl. Without Hulk Hogan beating up the bad guys, and showing that you can do it... why would anyone even bother?" "I thought getting here was tough..." Vivica smirked a little looking back at Lia. "Getting here is just the beginning." Lia spoke, "What you do from this point forward is the true measure of what you're made of Vivica. This is it. This is your chance. Your time to prove that you're the best ever. But... I guess you already know all of this. You don't need my help anymore." Lia Ambrosi taps the World Title that used to be hers sitting on the lap of Vivica J. Valentine next to her. It looked so strange to her with Vivica's name on it instead of her own. "You're the champion After all right? You did something right." Lia stated. "But sometimes Lia..." Vivica stated, "As much as we may not like to admit it. We all need a little help from time to time." Vivica grabbed Lia's hand which was sitting on the title. "Thank you..." Vivica said, "Thank you for everything you've done for me. I can never, EVER pay you back for all the shit I've put you through over the years." They both got up off of the bed and stood up before embracing again, just like they did in the middle of the ring at NC-17. Patting Vivica on the back Lia smirks as she backs away. Heading towards the door she says one last thing. "You've come a long way since Total J Cup Viv," Lia smirked, "Good luck against Chris Bagwell tomorrow. I'll be rooting for you." ---- Chapter Three: Living The Dream ---- The sound of "Paralyzer" by Finger Eleven plays as 105.7 THE POINT Alternative Rock comes back onto the air to announce the commercials are over. It doesn't take long for local DJ who goes by the name Cornbread to begin to speak. "Welcome back to 105.7 THE POINT... this is Cornbread here and I'm here with, a very cute young lady here named Vivica J. Valentine. SHE IS... the GCW World Heavyweight Champion and she'll be wrestling here in St. Louis tonight at GCW WorldWide! Welcome to the show Vivica. It's okay if I call you cute right? Don't beat my ass." "Yes it's fine, and thank you. I won't beat your ass... THIS time." Vivica smiled a little bit leaning into the microphone and adjusting the headset over her ears as she watches the local DJ go on. "That's good. I don't think that big badass dude over there would let me fight you anyway, who is that guy anyway?" Cornbread asks. "I really shouldn't talk about that guy." Vivica says rolling her eyes before looking over her shoulder at two GCW security guards, examining her every word and every action making sure it's to the liking of one Mister Steven Caldera. Even with the championship perks comes a price. "Alright then we won't talk about that guy. So how did a girl like you get into wrestling anyway? Don't take this the wrong way but you are TINY. Just looking at you I know there are a couple guys who told me they think they could take you, but they want to wrestle you for an entirely different reason I'm sure?" "How'd I get into wrestling?" Vivica thought back to Total J Cup 2005 again, this time with fond memories of how far she's come since that day, "I used to watch it growing up, and it's something I'd ALWAYS wanted to do. You know my friends wanted to be a princesses or teachers, and I wanted to uppercut punkasses and get paid for it. One day nothing I was trying to do for the rest of my life was working so I thought.... Why not? Go for it. So here I am." "And quite the name you've made for yourself. You're defending your title for the first time tonight against some dude named Chris Bagwell. Tell me a little about Chris Bagwell." "Oh Chris... he's just some rookie jackass. Some girl must've pissed in his cornflakes in High School." Cornbread and his staff starts laughing. "Always going around degrading women, saying inappropriate things. I suggested for management to let him into the ring with me so I could teach him a thing or two about how to treat a lady." Vivica smiled back at Cornbread. "Want me to rough him up for you?" Cornbread said, "I've been in the ring before myself." "Really?" Vivica put her hand over her mouth so the mic couldn't hear her giggle, "Nah, I think I can handle it. I've taken on much bigger and much stronger than him. He's nothing. A girl should stand up for herself you know?" "That's good to hear. So I hear you're a big rock and alternative fan yourself?" Cornbread asked, "What kind of bands are you into? Any particular favorites?" "Rise Against stands out immediately as my favorite. I like just about everything you play on here, I was listening in the other room. Three Days Grace, Chevelle, Finger Eleven, Hurt... it's all good stuff." "It is good stuff. Well good luck tonight! Vivica J .Valentine with Global Championship Wrestling TONIGHT here in St. Louis with WorldWide! Maybe after the show you can show me a couple moves, right?" "Haha, keep dreaming Cornbread." "Well we're going to start a new block of POINT hits starting with Vivica J. Valentine's entrance theme 'Image Of The Invisible' by Thrice. Starting, right now." Cornbread says before hitting a button and starting the song that's familiar to Vivica's ears as she takes off the headset and lifts the GCW World Championship over her shoulder. "Thanks for coming out, good interview, and good luck in your match tonight. This schedule I heard is brutal. You're been doing this big media tour. I'm sure it's killing you, running around everyday like this." Cornbread says reaching across the table and shaking hands with The Fearless Phenom. "No... it's okay really." Vivica said as she finished shaking hands and backed away, "Every day that I have this title it's another beautiful day. At the end of the night even when you can hardly move because your body hurt so much you have to put everything in perspective. I'm living my dream. How many people can say that?"
