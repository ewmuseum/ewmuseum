Date: 01/01/2008 - 01/02/2008 Part I January 1, 2008 [In the dark twilight hours the moon peers down upon the evil church. Hidden in an unsuspecting place, just a mere mile from the Vatican City border wall in the city of Rome is this church of evil that hosts an unholy horror within. Only the animals know its true power. Deep inside the building hiding in the basement is a power of unimaginable evil. It has been resting there for quite sometime. But now a group has taken over the cause of this evil power. They call themselves the Church of the Unholy. Downstairs they are plotting their next move. Under the candlelight the main players make their plans. Two Americans wearing Japanese style clothing are down there and seem to the be the main talkers of the group. They are AXEL Action and VON DOOM. There's also a crazy guy holding a barbed wire wrapped Kendo stick named Captain Carnage. Two aliens are there as well as a demon named Belial, a guy with tassles on his arm and facepaint who has powers of darkness named Grave Digger, a guy in flashy clothes jabbering on his cellphone named Ace Action, a Satanic Nun, and a whole bunch of guards for them. The they are standing around a large canister filled with green slime in front of which is a sacrificial altar.] VON DOOM: Our dark master will be pleased with all the work we have done. [What some people don't realize is that VON DOOM himself is an evil demon. The black glove on his right hand covers the scars from one of his escapes from Hell.] VON DOOM: When he comes back through the portal once we have all the unholy power crystals to charge it, then we shall be able to bring him to our world and unleash Hell upon this Earth! AXEL: Our almighty Lord and Master, Tioneb shall soon be unleashed upon this filthy, rat-infested shithole that these people call a planet. When that time comes, they will ALL know the error of their ways. They will bow at his feet, because he is laer4...! Ace: Yeah, yeah. Laer4 and all that good shit. Listen, AXEL, you may be the only one of us Action brothers who ever got anywhere in this business, but I'm still your older brother. And you HAVE to listen to me when I tell you that joining a cult dedicated to an evil entity that could possibly turn the entire world upside down on its axis is NOT a good idea. Do you even have a clue how bad for your image this is? And besides, what would mom and dad think? AXEL: You will bow before Tioneb! [AXEL simply waves his hand in front of Ace's face, and he falls to the floor unconscious. Yautja and Xenomorph, the two aliens, look pleased with what is going on.] Yautja: *clicking noises* Xenomorph: *grunt* [The Satanic Nun starts chanting in an ancient form of Latin as the others look on, very pleased at what is going on. Suddenly Captain Carnage starts talking to himself.] Carnage: You talking to me you fucking giant rabbit? [VON DOOM raises an eyebrow, not knowing that Carnage has visions of things in his head and thinks they are real and not imaginary.] VON DOOM: What the hell are you talking about? Carnage: Why are you smoking pot in my face? STOP BLOWING SMOKE TOWARD ME! AXEL: I am AXEL Action, I have no need to smoke this... pot... that you are speaking of. Carnage: MOTHER FUCKER! [Carnage goes to swing the Singapore cane but one of the Unholy Knights who are guarding the canister of slime grabs the cane and shakes his head.] Knight: No canings before the sacrifice. [The Satanic Nun finishes chanting as the Unholy Power Rangers bring a screaming woman down to the basement. The Church members look over and smile over this.] Mancubus Unholy Ranger: We have the virgin here for sacrifice. Belial: Excellent! [He motions for them to bring her over.] Belial: Bring her to the altar for the sacrifice. [The woman is screaming even louder now as the five Unholy Power Rangers strap the woman to the altar and take a step back. Belial laughs.] Belial: I am very pleased to be the one who gets to do the virgin sacrifice. [The woman starts screaming again.] Woman: What are you going to do?! Don't hurt me! [Belial cackles evilly as the others leave the basement.] Belial: I'm not going to hurt you...I'm just going to fuck you, virgin. [She starts screaming as the doors are shut behind them and the rest of the Church goes upstairs to look at the Hell Portal that is located behind the altar of the old Gothic style church.] Satanic Nun: In order to complete our plans, we are going to need to find the crystals of Hell that are scattered throughout the world in order to power it up and bring our "God" upon this Earth. AXEL: Hail Tioneb! VON DOOM: Hail Tioneb! Grave Digger: Soon Tioneb shall walk this Earth once more and then the power of the darkness will fuel the spaceships back to the dark realm of the Anti-God and then the blood will spill across the crimson sky leaving only bodies...and death! [The Cyber Ninjas walk into the room as everyone looks at Digger like he's crazy. Ace turns and greets the Cyber Ninjas.] Ace: Welcome, Cyber Ninjas. There is something you must do for us. You must go to the Vatican City and retrieve the Hand of Satan. It is a most powerful artifact that will aid us in our quest to resurrect the Almighty Tioneb. Cyber Ninja Red: But how will we get it? Ace: By any means necessary. I don't care if you have to rape, pillage and plunder the entire city to get to it. You'll find it in the secret vault underneath the Vatican Library. Do you think you can handle that? Cyber Ninja Black: Yes. And anyone who tries to stop us shall end up dead and spend the rest of their days in the fiery pits of deepest, darkest hell. [Cyber Ninjas turn and leave to go on their mission as the rest of the Church tries to figure out what to do about the portal.] VON DOOM: Now we need all of the crystals of Hell in order to pull this off. After the Cyber Ninjas complete their mission, then we can go for the first crystal. AXEL: Yes, and once we collect all of the crystals of Hell, we can resurrect our Almighty Lord Tioneb, and he can once again rule this earth like he did in life. Grave Digger: Then, after Tioneb comes back from Hell, then the darkness will cover the lands and it will be like my home realm! A world of perpetual darkness and despair! [VON DOOM and AXEL cackle over the very thought of this as Belial comes back upstairs to join them with a big grin on his demon face.] VON DOOM: So, how was it? Belial: I made her scream. I let the bitch go after I came all over her. Another bastard demon child will be born to wreak havoc. [The Satanic Nun wipes a crocodile tear.] The Satanic Nun: Another baby of Hell. What every woman strives to have. [VON DOOM and AXEL seem very happy.] VON DOOM: Yeah, that is so true. I want to knock up a female demon and have like fifty demon kids running around! AXEL: Me too. But she has to have big tits. And not to mention that MY kids will have tails. VON DOOM: Yeah, but mine will have bigger tails that yours. AXEL: It's possible, but my kids will be bigger where it counts the most. [AXEL motions toward his crotch, specifying where he was talking about.] VON DOOM: That may be true but...oh well. Who cares? Our kids will be demon spawn, right AXEL? AXEL: Fucking right, man! Demon spawn all around! [Just then Captain Carnage starts talking to an invisible creature again.] Carnage: Why are you humping that tree little mouse? [The Yautja and Xenomorph look at him like he's crazy.] Yautja: *clicks* [Carnage growls at him.] Carnage: You talking to me, mouse? You talking to me? [Yautja turns to Xenomorph and points at Carnage.] Yautja: *clicks* Xenomorph: *growls* [They look at each other and then laugh as Yautja slaps Carnage across the head. Carnage then snaps back into reality as the "predator" Yautja motions around.] Yautja: *clicks* [Carnage holds his head in pain after the hard slap from the much larger Yautja.] Carnage: Oh...sorry dude. I guess I was out of it. [Belial laughs maniacally and points to the portal.] Belial: It is midnight now. And soon the other creatures of darkness will be upon us. [Some Unholy Demon Guardians pass by them as they make their nightly rounds. They are the sentries of this Church. Unholy Ninjas come over and bow before Belial.] Unholy Ninja: Belial, the Mechas have begun their attack on Tokyo just as you have ordered! [Belial seems very pleased.] Belial: Good. Let us watch the destruction on the television. [The Ninjas go over to the corner of the Church's steeple and pull out a television on a rolling cart. They then get it to where everyone can see and turn it on. When the news comes on the television, two giant robotic monsters can be seen attacking Tokyo! One is Mecha Streisand and the other is Mecha Stein, as in Ben Stein. He uses his Laser Clear Eyes blast of death on some buildings! The Church of the Unholy's fellowship seems very pleased with what is going on.] Belial: First Tokyo shall feel our wrath. Then the church itself. Too long has the church held the power. Now, soon, it will be the Age of the Unholy! [They all start cackling as they continue to watch the chaos unfold on the television set. Today is the day that the Unholy has been unleashed. Only time will tell if they are able to create Hell on Earth...] ------------ Part II [Inside the Vatican Library, the Cyber Ninjas are sneaking around in the darkness trying to steal the Hand of Satan from it's resting spot within the holy building. The Cyber Ninjas sneak past some Vatican Holy Guards and then reach the Vault of Impurities. The Hand of Satan is located in this wing of the library. They sneak over to the two guards watching the entrance to the vault and knock them down. The Cyber Ninjas then go into the vault and look around at the many 'unholy' and 'impure' items being kept there. They then do some searching only to quickly find what they are looking for which is a red clawed hand in a glass case on one of the many shelves in the room. Satax, leader of the Cyber Ninjas who wears red armor, is very happy.] Satax: Lord Tioneb will be very pleased with this. [Cygnus, the blue Cyber Ninjas, is very happy as well.] Cygnus: As will Prince Belial. Satax: That's right. Tioneb be praised! We have done it! [Satax reaches up and grabs the glass case with the Hand in it. Just then, they hear a noise. They turn around and see the Vatin Holy Defenders in full knight's gear staring them down.] Holy Defender #1: Unholy fiends! Holy Defender #2: Put that impure relic back in its place or else! [Satax looks over at him and starts cackling maniacally.] Satax: Foolish children of God. We're going to take it and there's nothing you Monty Python wanna-bes can do to stop us. [The Holy Defenders pull out their swords and challenge the Cyber Ninjas to a duel. The Cyber Ninjas look at each other and then turn to the Defenders.] Satax: Morons. [The Cyber Ninjas then shoot out detonators out of their chests and they explode in front of the Defenders. The Defenders are either unconcious or dead, the Cyber Ninjas can't tell because of their knight's armor. They seem pleased as they walk over the fallen defenders and sneak back to where they came in at only this time they are confronted by someone holding a giant golden cross.] Person: Fear thy power of the lord! [The Cyber Ninjas stop and look at the person.] Satax: And who might you be? [The person steps into the light and it is Pope Benedict XVI!] Pope: Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death I will fear no evil that Satan sends upon this earth. [Satax gets a good chuckle over this but then the Pope holds out the giant golden holy cross and the Cyber Ninjas cover their faces in terror and then run down the hallway to the left toward a giant stained glass window that they jump through to eacape with the Hand of Satan before the Pope can melt them with his giant cross of holiness that will most surely kill them all.] *** An hour later back at the Church... [Inside the Church the church members are talking about Lord Tioneb with their two newest members. One is a funny looking skinny guy in a devil costume named Dan Devil Dan who plays pranks on people. The other is one of Tioneb's best friends back when he first walked this Earth. His name is C.G. Murder and he is telling the Church congregation stories about Lord Tioneb.] Murder: I first met him in a murder salon. AXEL: Chav... I mean, Murder, what's a murder salon? VON DOOM: Yeah, what's that? What is a murdering salon anyway? Is that like where people get their hair done at? Meaning like chicks with beehives like Amy Winehouse? [Murder shakes his head.] Murder: No. It's a place where murderers go to be themselves. VON DOOM: Like a gay bar? [Murder startes angrily at VON DOOM.] Murder: Don't make me kill you. [VON DOOM smiles at him.] VON DOOM: I was only kidding. Take a chill pill, dude. Murder: I'm a murderer. Chill pills are not something I take. I'm a cold blooded bastard. AXEL: Yeah, he feels intense RAGE at all times. VON DOOM: So does that mean he wants to kill me? AXEL: No. I thought he wanted to kill me...? [VON DOOM looks at AXEL strangely.] VON DOOM: So what the hell are we talking about? [AXEL scratches his head, confused.] AXEL: I'm not exactly sure... Murder: I thought we were talking about a murdering salon. VON DOOM: Oh, right. [VON DOOM thinks to himself for a moment.] VON DOOM: Are we going to one or something? AXEL: I hope like Hell we are! It sounds like fun. And besides, what's better than murdering someone? Or even an entire family? [VON DOOM nods his head in agreement.] VON DOOM: Hell fucking yeah! I want to go to one! [Murder smiles and is very happy over this.] Murder: I love it. It'll be just like the good old days when me and my friends would just go around and fucking murdering and murdering some more. It's going to be great to go around killing people. [Devil Dan starts cackling devilishly.] Devil Dan: Can I throw mud on people and drop pieces of dog shit all over the ground? VON DOOM: Dude, what the hell are you talking about? Devil Dan: Can I cannibalize a small animal in front of vegetarians? AXEL: Are you smoking the... pot... that Captain Carnage keeps referring to? VON DOOM: Uh, guys. What the hell are we talking about? [AXEL struggles with his shirt, but ultimately ends up jobbing to it.] AXEL: I DON'T KNOOOOOWWWWW! Devil Dan: Can I go around throwing mud on people's clean cars after they come out of the car wash? Murder: So what are we gonna do? Are we still going to the murdering salon or what? [VON DOOM still looks confused.] VON DOOM: I hope so. AXEL: I don't see why we're not. In fact, I'm beginning to feel the RAGE!!! Murder: Good. Now let's go murder some people. AXEL: Can I rape them first? Or maybe tie them to a chair, slice off their flesh with a razor, and pour salt on their wounds. VON DOOM: Can I use a meat cleaver? Or maybe an uzi! AXEL: I could be like Al Capone! Just think of it, me walking around, decked out in a pinstriped suit, with a Tommy Gun in my hands. VON DOOM: Nah. I want to be Al Capone. AXEL: Fine. But I get to be Arnold Schwarzenegger. VON DOOM: Yeah, and then we can recreate Conan and the Terminator at the same time... AXEL: Then we can blast and cut people to pieces as we are showered with their lifeblood! Yesssss... Indeed... [All four men head out of the Church of the Unholy as downstairs Belial, demon prince of Northern Hell, is standing in front of the Altar of Sacrifice as the Unholy Cyber Ninjas come downstairs with the Hand of Satan. Satax hands it over to the smiling Belial.] Belial: You did very good tonight. Now we have the power of the Hand of Satan! [All of the Unholy minions downstairs look pleased. Some humanoid subcreatures hiding in the darkness are very happy as well as the that guard the church who are watching from the shadows as well.] Belial: And now all we need to make the power of the Hand work is the Deformed Wing of the Bloody Dragon! [Satax seems a bit surprised.] Satax: How are we going to get that? [An evil smirk comes across Belial's face.] Belial: It's simple. Yautja and Xenomorph have already been sent to find this relic. It's at a murdering salon nearby. Satax: But VON DOOM and AXEL Action just left for there as well with those two new guys we've brought in. [Belial's sick smile grows even wider.] Belial: Then we have a better chance of acquiring it than I first realized. [He starts cackling evilly as the night grows even darker outside the church.] (To be continued...) ----------------- Rome, Italy [Every town has a dive like this. The Sword of the Dead Gladiator is a murdering salon, a place where murderers go to hang out and kill people. This is where the VON DOOM, AXEL Action, and Devil Dan go with C.G. Murder, the best friend of Tioneb back when he was alive. They go inside the murdering salon not knowing that their allies in the Church of the Unholy "The Predator" Yautja and "The Alien" Xenomorph are lurking around somewhere nearby looking for the Deformed Wing of the Bloody Dragon that is located inside the Sword of the Dead Gladiator murdering salon somewhere. The lights inside the salon are dimmed and the setting is very eerie. Black walls with pictures of famous murderers on them don't cheer up the place too much either. The five of them go over to one of the tables in the back and sit down. They quickly start looking around for people to kill.] VON DOOM: I want to kill that fatass at the bar. He looks like he weighs four hundred pounds! AXEL: Yeah, I'd like to shave a few pounds off of his fat ass... Literally. But then, there's that skank in the corner whom I'd love to fornicate, then kill... [VON DOOM starts laughing.] VON DOOM: Yeah, that'd be a good choice too. AXEL: Why don't we just fuck up everyone in this place? [Murder smiles deviously.] Murder: It's not that easy to pick someone to kill. You have to really hate the son of a bitch. Like when I killed my family. Those bastards had it coming. All the whining over toys and new dresses. They had it coming. [VON DOOM gets really excited now over the prospects of killing someone tonight.] VON DOOM: Yeah! They had it coming! Just like Al Qaida! AXEL: But I'd actually kind of like to choke my chicken... [VON DOOM starts laughing.] VON DOOM: Thats a good idea too. I want to kill a chicken too instead of choking it. That gets boring after awhile. AXEL: But it feels better to CHOKE the chicken. I mean, when it dies, it spits. Sometimes it spits for quite a distance, and it makes a mess of everything. [Murder shakes his head.] Murder: Guys, you don't seem to be getting the point about murdering people. VON DOOM: There's a point to something other than my dick when I have a hard on? AXEL: Dude.. That's sick. [Murder sighs and then pulls out a knife, which makes Devil Dan giggle.] Murder: Murdering has a point. And the point is... [Murder smiles from ear to ear.] Murder: It's fun as hell. VON DOOM: So it does have a point... AXEL: I'm all for having fun. Let's do it! Murder: Murder is supposed to be a joyous time between a man and his weapon of choice. Men and their weapons bond like family. VON DOOM: How many people have you killed? Murder: Many people. Me and Tioneb and my brother used to kill people all the time. We would go around and murder people all the time. AXEL: What about raping and pillaging? Murder: Well that is something fun I like to do as well when I go out murdering. VON DOOM: I like to have sex after I commit murder. AXEL: Like... with the dead bodies? [VON DOOM shakes his head.] VON DOOM: Nah. Just regular sex. I like to save the sex with big breasted female demons for special occasions, like Easter. AXEL: What about Valentine's Day? Or even Veteran's Day? I mean, sex is good anytime. And besides, what does sex have to do with killing? Killing is about bloodshed and death! Murder: But killing is more than just about that. When me and my friends used to go around killing people, first we would pop roids before going out. Then we would kill people. Me and my number one bro. We would have such a good time. [VON DOOM and AXEL seem really happy.] VON DOOM: This guy rules all. AXEL: Indeed he does. [The three men the look over and see Devil Dan raising his hand.] Murder: Yes? Say something before I kill you. Devil: How about we prank people? [VON DOOM and AXEL look amused. Murder not so much. In fact, he looks like he wants to kill him.] AXEL: Maybe we could go up to random people and yell, "RRRRAAAAAAAAAAAGGGGGGGEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!" VON DOOM: Yeah, how so? [Devil Dan gets up and pulls out a piece of paper with the word "Twat" on it and tape on the top of it. He giggles as he goes over and puts it on the back of some murderer sitting at the bar. The murderer gets pissed and turns around with a cleaver in hand.] Murderer: I'm going to cut off your tail and shove it down your throat devil boy. [Devil Dan cowers in fear only to be saved when another murderer hits the murderer who's trying to kill him in the head with a beer bottle! The whole salon turns into chaos! Murder holds out his knife and smiles.] Murder: It's murdering time! [Just before the salon turns into a murder frenzy, the loud screams of someone being killed outside echo throughout the salon. Everyone stops in their tracks as the growls of Xenomorph and Yautja walk in through the salon entrance doors. VON DOOM and AXEL look very happy as do Devil Dan and C.G. Murder.] VON DOOM: This is going to rule all! AXEL: This ALREADY rules all. This rules the world, the galaxy, the universe, and all planes of existence. Xenomorph: *growl* Yautja: *snarl* [Yautja starts targeting people with weapons using his technological space weapons as Xenomorph climbs up the wall to blend in with the black colored ceiling. Yautja and Xenomorph then quickly take out all the murderers in the salon within a span of thirty seconds. Once finished, they look over at VON DOOM, AXEL, Devil Dan, and Murder.] Murder: Those guys know how to murder... VON DOOM: Damn right they do. They are members of the Church. Murder: Glad I joined then. These are my kind of guys. [Yautja and Xenomorph then walk past them and head down the back hallway of the salon toward the bathroom. They then come to a janitor's closet. Yautja kicks it open and then walks in to grab the Deformed Wing of the Bloody Dragon that is hidden behind some cleaning products on a shelf. Once in his possession, Yautja and Xenomorph walk past the other Church members and head out the door. Murder, Devil Dan, VON DOOM, and AXEL look at each other.] Murder: I'm following them. They know how to murder with the best of them. [Murder starts following them out the door.] Devil Dan: They are tons of fun! And they really know how to party! [Devil Dan then follows them out the door as well. VON DOOM and AXEL look at each other and then look around the salon.] VON DOOM: I think we should get the hell out of here before someone comes in and murders us. AXEL: Yeah, because if we get murdered, then how can we murder others? [They quickly run after the rest of the Church members and head out the door into the streets of Rome. Only time will tell how much more Unholy the Church will get.] The End.
