Dastardly planning or simple screwed up egotism?

15 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/15/11 Sitting quietly watching the sunset on the desert. His thoughts deep in the moment as he reaches down and grabs a handful of sand and lets it slip between his fingers slowly. The dust lit by the setting sun as it drifts away out into the cooling air. His eyes glint in the low fading light, as if they are small burning flames surrounding by darkness. “I always enjoy the sunsets, It is like the chaotic end of days to only be reborn again in the morning.” Shaman smirks as he leans back against a rocky face. He opens a bag of beef jerky and pulls a piece out tearing it as he eats it slowly. Chewing slowly he cracks his neck from side to side. He ponders for a few moments as he remembers something, kind of a quick flash that almost escaped his memory. “ Here is a little definition of something I like so well. Here it is a part of legal threatening.” His eyes narrow as he looks forward as the sun slowly dips beneath the horizon. “Intimidating a party into acquiescing to the demand, whether or not there is a legal basis for it, out of fear of litigation expense, negative publicity, loss of entitlement (e.g. losing a business license), or other negative consequence.” he smirks as he rubs his fingers together. Raising an ear piece up to his ear he pushes it in. Popping his head up and down listening to something. Closing his eyes he begins to listen to the music as if in a trance. “It is all about the money baby and what I can do, look around yourselves you fucking peons, your slaves to the beast. Grinding and grinding, you may have defeated me, but where has it gotten you? Still the slave, myself I am free no matter which way you look at it, win or lose.” A deep laughter is heard from him as he rolls his head side like a charmed snake. He begins to bang his head forward as his posture begins to take an aggressive take. His veins begin to pulse into action as he flexes his muscles in his forearms and rolling his shoulders. His aggressive fashion begins to permeate his whole being. Suddenly he opens his eyes and stands up staring upwards towards the sky. “Underneath it we are watched constantly, man looks at his own feet forgetting that when you look up you achieve the unachievable.” Waving his hand downwards to the desert. “Forgetting that which can kill you makes you blind to reality. Reality is me, the man who stands before you. Am I rambling? Perhaps, but there is something to be taken away from these ramblings.” Turning he walks away his head held higher than usual as he grins. Stopping at the edge he looks down at the desert far below the plateau. “Here is a little excerpt from a poet I tend to like.” Holding out his hands he unrolls a piece of paper as he begins to read it. “Arise to birth with me, my brother. Give me your hand out of the depths sown by your sorrows. You will not return from these stone fastnesses. You will not emerge from subterranean time.” Taking a breath he looks towards the darkening horizon as his eyes narrow. “Your rasping voice will not come back, nor your pierced eyes rise from their sockets.” Smiling as he continues knowing he has hit upon some nerves of many. “Look at me from the depths of the earth, tiller of fields, weaver, reticent shepherd, groom of totemic guanacos, mason high on your treacherous scaffolding, iceman of Andean tears, jeweler with crushed fingers, farmer anxious among his seedlings, potter wasted among his clays-- bring to the cup of this new life your ancient buried sorrows. Show me your blood and your furrow; say to me: here I was scourged because a gem was dull or because the earth failed to give up in time its tithe of corn or stone. Point out to me the rock on which you stumbled, the wood they used to crucify your body. Strike the old flints to kindle ancient lamps, light up the whips glued to your wounds throughout the centuries and light the axes gleaming with your blood.” Raising his hand he touches his lip softly as he makes the hush motion with his fingers. “I come to speak for your dead mouths.” His gaze remains unfocused on a fixed point in the skies above the horizon. “Throughout the earth let dead lips congregate, out of the depths spin this long night to me as if I rode at anchor here with you.” He raises a fist as he cracks his neck to the side, staring empowering himself for much much more. “And tell me everything, tell chain by chain, and link by link, and step by step; sharpen the knives you kept hidden away, thrust them into my breast, into my hands, like a torrent of sunbursts, an Amazon of buried jaguars, and leave me cry: hours, days and years, blind ages, stellar centuries.” Opening a bottle of water now he whispers into it as he drinks it slowly. “And give me silence, give me water, hope.” Waving his hand over the night sky as he grins and then points at the Rocky Mountains in the distance. “Give me the struggle, the iron, the volcanoes.” Then he brings his fists together as he stares simply at his battle scarred hands. “Let bodies cling like magnets to my body.” Raising his hands he grasps at nothing that is the sky far above himself. “Come quickly to my veins and to my mouth.” Closing his eyes now he slowly lowers his head looking downwards once again. “Speak through my speech, and through my blood.” Dropping the paper off the side of the large plateau, his eyes follow the papers path as it drifts in the wind down towards the ground far below. “That was Pablo Naruda, pretty popular poet in my eyes, his words strike a chord deep within me and now I think the follies of many peoples ways is bound to disrupt things forever more.” Quietly he watches the darkness envelope him upon the plateau as the last vestiges of the light disappear from the night sky leaving the stars brighter than usual.
