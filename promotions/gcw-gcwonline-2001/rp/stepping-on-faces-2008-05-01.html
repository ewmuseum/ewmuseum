Stepping on Faces

1 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1, 2008 Even while his eyes were blinded by the lights of the ceiling, he could still hear the voice pounding out orders. Stand up. Block this. Stop falling. An entourage of cat calls followed one another in an assembly line creating the final product in a fed-up, discontent Jay Terror. With all of his chords plucked and the last of his patience trod on, he sat up from the mat and rolled out of the ring, away from Jack, grabbing a cigarette from his pant pocket. 'Where do you think you're going?' 'Out for a smoke. Wanna' join?' 'I thought you understood - the smoking is done with. No more.' 'Funny thing. No. I still got some sticks sitting around in my pocket and it'd be the greatest shame to let them deteriorate, ya' know? It's only fair that I smoke their heads off.' Jack shook his head and dropped to the outside mats as Jay had. 'It'd be a shame if Steven Caldera found out that his "golden boy" was goofing off and avoiding his distinct wishes.' 'He'll deal. Not like he's going to ruin a good thing.' 'And tell me, since when were you a good thing?' Jay smirked as he turned to face Jack. His eyes tightened and his lips pursed before saying, 'Since the day I learned to walk.' 'Smug Terror. Real smug.' 'I'll be back to listen to you some more in ten.' 'You fuckin' prick.' Jay leaned against the wall behind him and took liberty in lighting the cigarette resting between his lips. He puffed at the filter and let a ring float from his mouth. 'Sometimes you have to be a prick to be the best. Not like you'll have to fix the faces you mess up when you're above them looking down.' 'Jay Terror,' he paused creating a dramatic moment, 'if it wasn't for the man I work for, I would deck you halfway to Sunday and stomp on you afterward.' 'He's not holding you back. You know better than that. It's your bullshit macho attitude Jack. You think that you'll mess me up too bad because you're trained. Fuck, wouldn't want to do that to your career, huh?' 'My career?' 'I said it.' He puffed on the end again, taking a longer drag than before. 'You mess up a rising star and your credibility will be shot down. How will you make money when everyone's afraid you'll kick their ass instead of train them?' 'Fuckin' prick.' 'Like I said. Not like I'll have to fix your face when I walk up it to the top.' With a grin as wide as the day is long, Jay turned his back and took his cigarette on a walk out of the wrestling area and back through the gym. A trail of smoke collected behind him in a narrow line and scented the air with an odor Jack would not soon forget. |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Here I am, writing in this stupid book again. Figures, every time some prick messes up my day I end up coming back to it. Whatever. Fought this week. Kicked Andy Murray's ass even. Damn did that feel good. It was like some kinda' wonderful was traveling through my body when I was running the ropes. Hitting moves, playing off the fans, and being better than the others is getting so much easier. Shit. I bet Viv was going ape shit after she watched us in there. I told her though. Told her I'd book a match better than her lesbian touch fest. Speaking of lesbians, I think Caroline might be turning into one. She doesn't seem to be quite right in bed anymore and seems like she's uninterested in the whole deal. Maybe she found someone else. Maybe she's into the vag as much as me. Who knows. I still fancy her though. We'll see how that works out. Ok, fuck life, I hate this book. |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Her long strands of auburn hair streaked down her bent back following the curves of her spine until they met the middle of the chair. In her hand, a mug of coffee burned away allowing steam to spill from the mouth and into the air. 'You know what we haven't done in a while?' 'What's that?' 'See a movie.' Jay licked his gums and smiled, 'Yeah. I guess so. Just lost time I guess.' 'You wanna' go and see one? I hear that movie Made of Honor is kind of funny.' “I don’t know about that one.” He paused and passed thoughts through his mind, “Ironman came out you know?” “I don’t know about that one.” “You hungry?” “Not really. Haven’t been all day.” Jay approached her from behind and rested his hands on the blades of her shoulders. His fingers pressed into the pockets between her bones and massaged her skin gently. She shut her eyes and accepted the gesture. “I’m sure we could work up an appetite.” She denied emotion from spreading across her lips and swayed with the movement of his fingers, “I don’t know if I have the energy. I mean, it’s been an hour.” “Yeah.” The pressure decreased to a meaningful rub dropping the speed with it. “You want to do anything?” “I don’t know.” Unbeknownst to Caroline, Jay puckered his lips and squinted his eyes. A look of disappointment captured his face as he spoke again, “Well I think I’m going to get a drink. You want to?” “Not really. No.” He pulled his hands to his side and walked around the chair she sat in. She watched him gather a black hoody from the floor of the hotel room and slide it over his bare chest. In another pile, he found his blue jeans and tightened them to his frame. “You sure?” “Yeah.” Jay nodded for a second, looked at her blank face, and found the keys to the rental car. In a swift turn, he found the door handle and exited leaving behind Caroline. As the oak door slammed back into place, locking her inside, a slight tear began to form. It rolled down her cheekbone and crashed onto the table in front of her with an inaudible impact that she somehow could hear. “Fuck you,” she spat. Another tear came into focus and before it could fall she found the phone on the table. Lifting it to her ear, she dialed a number and held it close. Two rings later, she spoke. “Hey.” |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Jay had been to California once before but he certainly hadn’t been to any of the bars. Fortunately, they followed the same routine as others he had visited in neighboring states. “Whiskey if you have it.” “Jack Daniels?” “Is there any other?” he mused. The bartender walked down the line and found the bottle leaving Jay alone in his barstool. A slight depression had started to creep into his system leaving his expressions to hang low. Keeping his feelings hidden, he encased them with the edges of his black hood. For a second, he sat and stared at the damaged wood of the counter. It had seen dismal nights with bleak men and women from all over the world hoping that a quick sip of the devil’s juice would spice up their life and add the missing ingredient. For Jay, he was still stumped on how to finish his stew. Thankfully, his glass of Jack Daniels returned with its condensation-covered edges. “Anything else?” “Nah. Thanks.” He walked down the line and found another customer to serve allowing Jay to sip the drink on his own. As he took the initial swig, he bit his tongue hoping he could counteract the bite he received. That’s when he heard the neighboring voice. He listened close after the mention of a familiar name: Jay Terror. “He’s a traitor. You can’t tell me he was doing anyone good by joining up with that snake.” “That snake is the most powerful man in the world. You telling me that Jay isn’t going to be the next world champion?” “I’m telling you that just because a guy gets a shot doesn’t mean he can follow through with it. Look at the way he’s choked in the past. Tyson DeBough took his TV title, LeStatt Knight took him down at NC-17, and I’m sure that Jordan White would have taken him down if it wasn’t for his established peers.” “Oh come on ? those are weak examples. He beat Knight in round two, he ousted Rich Rollins in a ladder match, and he beat Jordan White with his wit and quickness. He’s smart out there and he’s smart taking sides with such a force as Steven Caldera.” “Just wait and see. He’s going to get tossed into the ring with Valentine and she’ll make sure he finds his place. I don’t care that she’s a girl; she has beaten some pretty big dudes. Look at the way she beat Shaman. He’s fuckin” massive.” “It’s all about heart man. You don’t get it. She’s already the champion. She’s fought for the unified title. What’s she fighting to prove” Jay though, that’s a different story. The guy joined the Establishment because he WANTED to become huge. He WANTED to be the champion. That’s heart if I ever saw it.” An awkward silence crossed the barroom and Jay took the minute to take a second swig from his glass. As he set it down, the voice of the man opposing Jay returned. “I just don’t see it happening.” “Tell me this: What’d you think of him when he was sided with White?” “He was cool enough. A snake still.” “He hasn’t changed. All he did was surround himself with the right people. Tell me again how you all of a sudden decided that he is the devil.” The silence came back and Jay took a long gulp of his drink. With a smirk, he left the last bit of whiskey in his glass and walked off from the counter. Passing through the entrance doors, he pulled his hood down and let his grin shine bright in the overhanging lights. |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Maybe I like controversy. I mean, it seems to be following me like a tail would. Everywhere I go, I somehow find myself facing the ugly face of controversy. There’s Caroline, god do I write about her a lot. I just don’t know. Since when do you have no energy to fuck? Since when do you sit in a chair for an hour looking at a wall? Something’s not right. And really, I do want to fix it all. I like spending time with her and I like being around her. But god damn, there’s only so much drama a person like me can take. Over and over she says she wants me to change and she questions my actions. Yet I see how she reacts when I bring home a paycheck. Her eyes nearly double. Maybe things just aren’t meant to be. No. Not that. I doubt that. I don’t want to talk about that. How about the Choosers. Yeah, the Choosers. They’re something. For sure. But this is the chance. Vivica will be in the ring standing across from me for the first time since me and Jordan tagged against her. It feels good too. Like, I’m not even in the ring with her yet and I already have the chills. This is why I did all of this. This is why I joined up with Steve. It’s the feeling of the main event and world title that have driven me to the one extreme people don’t understand. Only if they could get these goose bumps. Then they’d know why. Then they’d cheer. But who needs to hear the cheers. It’s a lot easier to dance to boos than cheers. Less smiling involved. |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Caroline hung up the phone and made herself comfortable in the hotel bed. She pulled the covers over her bare body and shut her eyes slowly. The tick of the wall clock paced her thoughts allowing them to pass through in a rhythmic succession. Thoughts of Jay, thoughts of life, thoughts of her family, thoughts of her past lovers, thoughts of why, thoughts of everything passed through her head making her uneasy. She turned to her side and as she did the door swung open. The scent of Marlboro found her nostrils. “Caroline.” She didn’t answer. Jay nodded to himself and shut the door. His hoody came loose, his belt buckle came undone, and he climbed under the covers beside the girl he had grown fond of on the road. With a sigh, he shut his eyes and tried to sleep.
