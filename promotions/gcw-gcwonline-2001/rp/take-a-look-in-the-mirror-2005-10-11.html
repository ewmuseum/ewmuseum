Take a look in the mirror

11 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-11-2005 Have you ever had a day when you needed to do some reflecting? Ever have a day when shit goes so damn wrong that you could just scream? Ever go look for a friend to talk to but the reality is that sometimes there isn’t a kind-hearted soul to talk to? Ok got that’good! Its been about 2 weeks since Joshua Curtis and Ami Mizuno won the tag team titles at GCW’s “Fallout” and they have been doing their fair share of appearances all over New York and elsewhere considering the media that they received since winning the belts due to a lot of things. What kind of things you ask? Well let’s see first directly related family members to win a tag title since Larry “The Ax” and Curt Henning, first tag team to win the tag titles that supported “Alternative Lifestyles” and how about one of the young tag teams in the history of Pro Wrestling! Joshua is only 26 and Ami is 25 so they still have their best years ahead of them right? It gets even better? Joshua and Ami received a phone call over the past couple of days to return home to Tokyo, Japan and specifically the Egg Dome to promote a card that they were appearing on regardless of whether they retained the tag team titles at the show upcoming against Ricky Thunder and his friend for the newly won GCW Tag Titles that they(Joshua Curtis and Mizuno Ami) now possess. Its now 7:45 A.M. and Joshua and Ami were staying in very familiar territory for them. A friend of Ami’s girlfriend Michiru named Ten’ou Haruka was letting Joshua and Ami stay at her place and they had been there for the weekend. Joshua, being the considerate guest, had protested against staying only because he didn’t want to intrude on Haruka or take advantage of her kindness but Haruka was steadfast in insisting that they stay because since Michiru, whom respectfully declined the invitation to go back, moved in with Ami at her brother’s place in the states she’s been kind of lonely. Ok I can see you scratching your heads on what this has to do with ANYTHING right? Well when Joshua returned to Japan after being told by the federation in England that he needed to train there, he(Joshua) went and met up with a young lady that was quite the speedster as a runner and offered to train Joshua to help him get his quickness up. Joshua agreed and when he heard the name of the young lady was Ten’ou Haruka he was quite surprised with the fact that she was willing to train him. He had heard that Haruka was kind of snobbish and didn’t like being around people because it got in the way of training and Haruka saw this as a way to prove everyone wrong and succeeded in doing so quite handedly. Now while Joshua and Ami stayed at the house Josh made breakfast for everyone and much to everyone’s surprise Joshua was a helluva cook! Hmm hidden talents OTHER than music? Who knew?! So Joshua made breakfast and of course due to the fact that he lived in America for as long as he did he learned to make a lot of dishes that Haruka wasn’t accustomed to like blueberry waffles, bacon(PORK FAT RULES!), and of course some good old fashion coffee! “So Joshua” Haruka started “how is Zack doing” I miss him coming around the garage and helping me with my car.” Haruka finished and Joshua couldn’t help but smile at the mention of his beloved helping his good friend with both of her cars. See it turns out that Zack is somewhat of a “grease monkey” and he loves the roar of an engine! Whenever they aren’t in the ring it turns out that Zack is watching the “Chase for the Nextel Cup” with Ami and Joshua’well Joshua got hooked on it with the two of them. Is that a bad thing? Hell no! Joshua loves to learn things about people that he didn’t know before and the fact that Zack shared that with him was something that he would treasure forever! Ok so back to the story? “Zack is doing wonderful Ruka-chan. He’s been asking when you would come for a visit and I told him that you’d been busy with other things or you probably would come down.” Joshua told his good friend and she nodded softly not wanting to show any sign of hurt but then again she DID want to talk to Ami about Michiru’its just would it come to blows or not? “Ami” Haruka started and the blue-haired wonder looked up almost shock still as she KNEW what was going to come “first before I begin I want you to know that I’m not mad at you for winning Michiru’s heart. I fucked up when I went after her one night and Joshua was there to witness it. He told me that if I didn’t get my head straight and seek some kind of attention that I would lose that kind woman and I didn’t believe it and now here I am?” Haruka trailed off and no one knew where this was coming from but Ami did the most noble thing she could at that point in time? Ami is a kind-hearted soul. Period, exclamation point and signed on the dotted line. Ami doesn’t want to see anyone hurt, physically OR emotionally because well while physical scars go away in time emotional scarring is a little more complex. Its layers upon layers that only goes away if it STAYS away and Ami knew that by holding the crying young lady she was doing all that she could to help keep the emotional scars away, just as she had done for Michiru many times prior when they laid in bed together cuddling, THAT is how important this was for Ami to do for Haruka. People say sometimes that we need to take a good long look in the mirror when it comes to how we treat others and that is what Haruka had done?.THIS was her consequence and there was NOTHING more important on this day than making sure that she was ok, that she was safe and that the hurt that was in her body got cried out. Some of you may read this and wonder where this comes from and all I’ll say is take a look in the mirror at yourself when you try to be hard to others and perceive yourself to be a badass. The reality is that we all have our times when need help and we just don’t know how to ask for it’on this day Haruka asked for help in her own way and its something that she was commeneded for and when Joshua had to call and cancel the interview that day the people understood, for they took a look in the mirror a long time ago and realized that some things were more important than the ring?
