Personal Purgatory

28 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/05 'Fallen angels, Johnny-boy. They're everywhere. They're in sight and they're invisible. They're in every civilization's stories and just about every religion believes in them. Anywhere that has something good has something bad, and there come with that 'something bad' demigods that plague this world and seemingly just like to cause mischief. They're under rocks and in our schools and on TV and in churches and everywhere and nowhere. Demons. Fiends. Imps. But angels, nonetheless.' It's hard to ever tell what Don is trying to get at. Then again, I don't see why it would be easy to understand the devil. The devil incarnate. He who commands the hordes of Hell. The original fallen angel, out of the countless ones out there. But he's trying to get at something...I think. 'Why did you come here, Don?' 'Some of them are angry. Some of them are spoiled and want attention. Some of them get a real kick out of fucking with humans. And some of them just want to mind their own business. Just going through their...lives, if you will. For as unbelievably different from humans as they are, they're really so much the same. In fact, there are only two real, factual differences. One is that they're not made of matierial...they're simply spiritual. The other is that their decision has been made. Their fate sealed. They denied God their love and attention, they turned down eternal bliss. And there is no going back to it. Because if there was a chance that they could go back to Him, it would already be taken and they wouldn't be down here any more. No, Johnny-boy. They're stuck here.' 'Don. Why are you here?' The devil incarnate throws down his cigarette. To my surprise, he actually doesn't light up another one. Something has been different about him today. He's not his usual sarcastic, snide self. To try to put it to words, he's been quiet. Simplistic. Honest. Everyone always talks about how the devil is the prince of lies. And yet, Don has never lied to me. Everything he's said has come true. In truth, I knew even before he came here that I was going to have to deal with his pull soon enough. The fiend has been at my elbow, tempting me, saying to me, 'Fitzwillis. Johnathan Fitzwillis. Good Johnathan,' or, 'Good Fitzwillis,' or, 'Good Johnathan Fitzwillis...do what you know you have to do. Batter her! Bruise her! Make. Her. Suffer!' And the angel tugs at the neck of my heart. And it says to me, 'No. Take heed, honest Johnathan. Take heed, honest Fitzwillis,' or as I just said, 'Honest Johnathan Fitzwillis. How much pain is needed? How much pain needs to be caused and given out to satisfy you? And the fiend says, 'There is never enough pain given.' 'I can see them all the time, too. I suppose it's a kind of gift...considering what I am. They swarm around people. Sometimes they touch a person, and temptation saturates them. That person will do things that normally they wouldn’t. Nuns will renounce God. Murderers will embrace salvation. And foolish humans everywhere call it ?free choice.? If only.? 'Don! Why did you show up here?' I can't hold this back anymore. Don knocks on my happy door to my happy house that me and the woman I love and our daughter live in. There are a select few people that I want to keep away from my daughter. And Kate, who once dated this devil, completely agrees. I doubt that she knows exactly what he is, but she's more than aware of what he thinks. What he believes. What he is out to accomplish at points in life. Immediately upon opening the door, I can feel Sarah take a gasp. My little angel, the most innocent of innocent beings, sees the devil himself. And she doesn't even know what he is. Don turns to me and looks me in the eye. For the first time ever. And that's the first time that I realized that he and I have never looked at each others' eyes before. They're brown. Deep brown. And tired, and lost, and lonely, and proud. But, at the same time, his mischievious smile creeps up and it's like he's laughing about something that he's dead tired of and he's only laughing because he absolutely has to laugh because it's his job. 'It's my time of the year, Johnny-boy. It's time for that one day where everybody, even the smallest of children, celebrate...me. It's kind of like the Saint Patrick's day for Satanists. Everybody embraces evil once per year.' 'Not everybody. Kate and I agreed that Sarah won't be celebrating Halloween. At least not yet.' 'Oh, come on, Johnny-boy. The girl's five years old. Give her a taste of evil. Give her a taste of the real world.' 'Kiss my ass.' He knew that would be my response. A grissled laugh from smoke-crusted lungs shows an amusement at the situation. He knows what I would do for Sarah. This girl that I just met, not two months ago, that I hadn't known for her entire life. But he knows that her entire life spans a period of time where my self torment, mallace, and brewing on bitterness cannot be matched. He knows what she means to me. He knows that I would give it all up in an instant to save her. I would die for her. But he's not here for that. In fact, I'd imagine quite the opposite. 'There are angels and demons everywhere, Johnny-boy. A old woman spills her groceries. Some boy scout walks up, sees her with her spilled groceries, and walks on by. A police officer comes over, sees her struggling with them, and walks on by. And finally, a street thug feels sorry for her. He walks over, picks up her stuff, gives it to her, and hopes she has a great day. That kid's an angel. Later that night, the same kid wants to get into a club. But he can't meet the cover charge. He decides that the best way to get that cover charge is to mug somebody, harmlessly of course, just to get in. He goes to mug someone, and the guy pulls a knife of his own. In the struggle, the thug kills the person being mugged and makes off with his money. That kid's a demon. So tell me, my friend...what makes a demon a demon and an angel an angel?' I want to say absolutism. I want to say that everything has its place and is set in stone as 'good' or 'bad' from day one. 'The choices they make at the time.' 'Exactly. Angels embrace 'the good,' being that which helps others, not taking into account your own standing. Demons embrace 'evil,' being that which helps yourself, not taking into account the standings of others. Who is to say that too much of one is healthy, man? Why is it that any amount of selfishness is wrong and that you must give yourself out constantly? You've been there, Johnny-boy. You've seen that road. It leads to failure. Sorrow. Being forgotten. Dying.' Some people say that the greatest trick the devil played was to convince us that he wasn't there at all. I disagree. The greatest trick he ever played was to convince me to feel pity on him, even with me knowing who and what he is. I stand here...and I pity him. I want his suffering to end. I want him to find eternal bliss and peace because I can't even imagine how much it would drive someone mad to chase after something for so long. Kind of like Eva and myself. I chased after her for oh, so long. And had I not found Kate again, had I not returned to the love of my life, I would be continuing that chase. I wouldn't have capitalized on Trey, and I wouldn't be US Champ. And even if I was, I wouldn't have found the balls that I need and have to hold onto what's mine for so long. And now Eva finds herself in much the same position. With the same belt on the line. She has become obsessed with Johnny, the roles switched from what they were before. I used to dream of being with her, and now that is the farthest from my mind. And now she dreams of wrapping her legs around me...whether for the attempt at a pinfall or for the ol' Saturday Night Ride. Or a little of both. All switched because of choices we each made. I chose to end what we had. And she chose to continue it. He turns back to me. 'I came here because you have another choice to make right now, Johnny. At My Night, Devil's Night. It's no mistake that you face Eva at the event named after me. It's no mistake that you happen to have a crises that your soul rests on at it.' 'My soul? Isn't that a bit much?' 'It's quite simple, Johnny. Continue on the road of revenge and re-revenge and striking someone back for when they struck you for when you struck them and you're going to find yourself at the bottom of a spiral of fighting. A battle that is infinitly uphill that you'll never win. Choice two. Finish this. And no matter what happens, it's over. It's as simple as that, Johnny. You always say how Eva 'acts' mature and you are 'seen' as juvenile. Now's your chance to show the world what Johnny Borealis is made of. End it. Finito. And move on.' I see that he is right. I swore to myself from myself that I would leave Eva be and leave her out of my life. A long time ago. Back when Kate and I got together. Since then, Kate and I have been absolutely nothing short of blissful. And yet, the brooding idea has always hung over my head. What if? What if Eva teamed with me? What if I gave her one more chance? What if I turned on her sooner, to spare myself the pain. What if Eva Van Lorne and Johnathan Fitzwillis? A quick shudder makes me realize the choice that I have to have. Give it enough time, and your hate consumes you totally. It eats away everything that you are until you are a being with no past, no present, no recollection of anything save for the present time which is being used to punish the person or persons in front of you. And so what do I do? What is there left to do but beat her? Again. What is there left to do but walk away from her? Again. What is there left to do? '...bury her...' 'What was that, Johnny-boy?' I turn to Don automatically. Still no cigarette. His face is a bad actor's expression for 'What are you thinking?' He knows what I'm thinking...he's the fucking devil. 'To stop between me and Eva...to guarantee for my good that she is out of my life, that my mind isn't infected with her anymore, to get rid of the worry of imagining I'm with her while Kate and I sleep...to remove her very stain on my soul, I have to bury her at Devils' Night. Not literally. But in every other way. I have to bury all feelings. I have to bury her chance of coming up again. I have to bury her want to try to bother me for revenge ever again. No, Don...this has to end now! She goes no further than Devil's Night. And be I a demon for such a thought, or be I an angel for deciding not to draw out the misery of both of us, my decision stands. Bury her.' His smile tightens. Every now and then, even my very friend hopes I make the wrong choice. 'Bury her. That solves everything, doesn't it?' Don finally reaches down to get a cigarette. Poor guy must being dying. I mean-...poor devil must be dying. But I don't know what strikes me...I don't know what feeling from above or below or inside erupts inside of me, but for the first time, with looking at his eyes and all, the devil hiself...Lucifer...Satan... ...looks human. I know I'm going to regret this. 'Don. Why don't you come in? You can get a drink, say hi to Kate, and meet my daughter...' Just remember, kiddo. Bury her. Your conscience is but a kind of hard conscience to offer such council. That changes nothing. Bury her.
