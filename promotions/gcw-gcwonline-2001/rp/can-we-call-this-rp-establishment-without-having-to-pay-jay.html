Can we call this RP "Establishment" without having to pay Jay Terror $5?

Date: The day it was written Ja Gi Kyung-Moon grimaced as the cocky white boy with the slicked-back bleached blond hair extended a hand and a smile. "Hi, I'm Dolph Ziggler." This gesture was met with disgust, as the Korean man threw up his hands and declared, "I'm done!" But seriously, Amelia Greatbridge had an understated classical beauty to her. Not in the "Sexy librarian" sort of way but just in that kind of vibe that a woman gives off when she's completely serious and professional but you can tell underneath the red blazer and skirt, behind the reading glasses and earth tone lipstick, the neutral facial expression and close-cropped hair, that there was a good fun woman to be discovered. She greeted Ja Gi in the lobby of the Bank of American Financial Suites on Northwest 36th Avenue and Mainline Road. The abundant foliage gave off a first impression more along the lines of "Welcome to Jurassic Park" than "Thank you for conducting business with us today," but it worked. "The Committee will see you now," she informed the martial artist, who arrived for the first official scheduled meeting of the Aristocrats tag team fully dressed in a three piece suit. The Andrew Fezza number was cream colored with a copper vest over a white button-down shirt. The tie by Calvin Klein carried a pyramid design; along with the slip-on shoes from Rockport, everything matched superbly. Add to that the special-ordered black iridium Oakley Square-Wire 3.0 shades and JAGI would have looked more at home sitting beside Usher at the Grammys than to attend a business meeting. The Missouri-born brunette guided Ja past the security desk (with requisite fat desk guard who couldn't protect a playground sandbox) toward the private executive elevator. An electronic keycard granted them access to the private elevator leading to the 14th floor office, the entire level of the building utilized by the near-clandestine Rosewood Committee. JAGI found it odd that the mediterranean blue walls didn't have any photos, plaques, or inspirational posters. The entire office was bare, save for the one meeting room into which JAGI was guided, wherein he was offered to take a seat at the long black rectangular table. On one end, Rufio was already seated. On the other side, representatives of the Rosewood Committee. Amelia Greatbridge sat at the wrestler's end of the table. Legal ledger in hand from Levenger, she seemed to be there to take notes. JAGI probably didn't have a clue what was going on, the office, the committee, and most of all, the guy sitting at the end of the table. Rufio Rosewood more than anything looked disinterested the most out of all of them. The former GCW/Global star hadn't been seen in a few years, yet he didn't look to be any worse for wear. He was still fit, though he looked a bit scruffier than before, a 5o'clock shadow tinting his jawline. Wearing a white tee with a the Mario Bros 1up mushroom on the front, a white studded belt and blue jeans with black Converse Chucks. The color of his Chucks were apparent as he had his feet kicked up on the table, one leg crossed over the other. "So yeah..." he exhaled, leaning back in his chair as he folded his arms across his chest. "Apparently we're here for some sort of reason... I don't know what that may be, but fuck it, if it gets me a job then that's fine." But for a person who hasn't had a legal job in the past few years or so, he should've been grateful to have a steady check coming in again. Not only that, but he was doing the one thing he was qualified to do... and that was kick ass. Now, many are wondering just what the entire deal is with the board room and the so called Rosewood Committee. Rufio didn't even know what that entailed. As far as he knew, this was something that GCW had put together just for the sake of marketing Rufio again to the masses. After all... if there was anyone that could save GCW from it's boring, mediocre entertainers and programming... it was Rufio. Oh... and probably the Korean dude across from him. At the end of the table, the trio of old ass men had their arms resting on the table, fingers interlocked. The oldest, assiest of the men did not introduce himself, simply got down to business. Ninja Toadie: "The reason that the two of you are here is that the Rosewood Committee has secured employment for you. Rufio, you have previously worked in a handful of wrestling companies, the last of which being Global Championship Wrestling in 2006. You will be going back to this organization, and you will be working in a tag team with Ja Gi Kyung Moon. It is the Committee's belief and decision that your habitual... loss of interest in working environments is in large part a matter of having no compatible personalities with whom to work. While the Committee can secure employment for you, it cannot afford to expend the energy to keep advocating for you before you are able to return on investment. JAGI here will serve as your backup. Do whatever you need to in order to make your work manageable, even fun... that is not our concern. However, to retain your contract with the Rosewood Committee, we will need at least 8 months of solid work out of you. "JAGI, consider this as your tryout. You are tasked as Rufio's tag team partner, however, if you do well, we may discuss agent options to increase your exposure as a singles competitor. For now, your job is to back up Rufio in all professional matters. Make him look good, make your team look good. Be clear that you are the subordinate within the unit. Is that understood?" Kyung-Moon rolled his eyes, but nodded and remained quiet. He knew how to play the game with suits. Narrowing his eyes, Rufio stared the old fart down as he spoke. The whole time he explained the situation, it was apparent that there was something waiting on Rufio's mind. So when the old man finished, Rufio pulled one arm out from his chest and pointed it at him. "Who gave you authorization to coin a committee using MY last name? Furthermore, who the fuck's paying for all this?" Standing up, Rufio placed both palms down flat on the table, leaning down to look at them all in the eye. "I don't know what the hell's going on here... or anything you just said, grandpa... but rest assured knowing that I'm going to beat the shit out of everyone that gets in the way of any answers I seek. Got it?" Saying his peace, he began to walk out of the room. As he neared JAGI's seat, he stopped and looked down at him, glaring before looking back at the old man that spoke. "And I don't need Mr. JIGGI here babysitting me, you fuckin' douche." And he stormed out, slamming the door behind him.
