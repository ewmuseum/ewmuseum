Black curtains

7 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/7/2005 'How could anyone do such a horrible thing, even in the name of spite?' That male voice was the first thing I'd heard ever since I was put out no telling how long ago now. I feel myself lying somewhat comfortably, but cannot move a single inch. I hear everything around me, but can't see a thing. I really don't think I am dead, at least I hope not. At this moment, I can't even fully remember what has happened to me. 'How serious is it, Dr. Jacobs?.' A feminine voice this time. I wonder who it is...probably not anyone that I know. I try to remember everything again, but the pain! That pain shoots from the back of my head and sets fire to every nerve from my neck down. Thank god...at least I know I'm not paralyzed...but where to go from here...I try and fit the pieces of the puzzle together for what I know. Doctor...I'm in a hospital. Then again, thinking about that makes me want to smack myself if I could. Of course I'm in a hospital, dipshit, why else would I be in this state? 'I still need to take another CAT scan to confirm the battery of tests which we've performed yesterday. The x-rays don't look extremely promising, though.' It hurts so much to try and remember! It's like my body is torturing itself to protect me from something. I stop thinking again to listen. 'Are these the x-rays, Doctor?' 'Yes they are, Ellen.' 'May I have a look?' 'Certainly.' Ellen, that name doesn't ring a bell. I definitely don't know either of these people. I struggle to listen to soft sounds of papers moving, followed by the sound of a heavy sheet of material, presumably the x-ray aforementioned. I hear the woman gasp at the sight, and can guess her expression matched her tone undoubtedly well. This time I'm not as accepting. Now I'm genuinely frightened. 'And you said he WALKED in here?!' 'Apparently someone who knew him drove him up here, and he walked in on his own with no one else at his side. When he got to the counter he just collapsed and he was rushed into emergency care. That's the story I heard.' Slowly it starts coming back. Bits and pieces, but slowly I start to gather what happened... 'But Adam...!' 'I know...he shouldn't have walked it. He shouldn't be ABLE to walk...look here. You see what happened? Apparently he was with some wrestling promotion that was in-town last night as someone who apparently didn't care for him too much did this to him.' 'My god...' That mother fucker Octavian...that's who did it!!! My body screams out at once as everything comes rushing back to me in one wave. I had just beaten Mr. Danger and Octavian pulls out another one of his sneak attacks...but this one was different. He had it calculated; he had done it so well. I was weakened by emotion and he outsmarted me...something that should have never happened. Then the despair overcomes me. I can't physically express it in my current state, but that dead feeling inside of me rises. He'd gotten the better of my in just about every way. Octavian has taken my home, all of my money, and took away from me the one man whom I'd depended on to get through anything I needed to overcome. Has he finally exploited all my weaknesses? Has Octavian finally won? 'From what I can tell currently he narrowly avoided a fractured skull, has suffered multiple contusions to his head and face...and well, you see his neck.' What about my neck?! I remember falling out of the car and refusing help inside...I was too proud for that, and I remember stumbling in to the front lobby. I don't remember having trouble walking; it was just that damn pain in my skull that wouldn't go away. I think that's what made me finally give up as soon as I was in the safety of that lobby, the pain coupled with the knowing that I would eventually be alright. But am I? All the signs point otherwise. What the hell has Octavian done to me?! The man's voice resounds again through my curtain of black. 'But all his vital signs seem normal. Seth, as his name is, in extremely in shape...that may be what very well has saved his life. He's a very lucky man, I hope he realizes that some day.' If you only knew... 'Doctor ? do you think he can hear us?' 'No, why do you ask?' 'I just think...no, it's nothing. You're probably right. By any means though, I don't think there is anything else we can do for him at this moment. We'll just have to wait until he has awakened.' The sounds of footsteps...I presume they've left me by now. The only thing I hear after soft footsteps is silence. It is normally comforting to me, but at this moment it's too much to bear. I try to keep my sanity with thoughts and remembrances, but I always dwell upon the bad aspects of my situation. Why has no one come to my aid? Will my physical condition inhibit me from ever wrestling again...but even more importantly...revenge? I scream out, but no sound comes out. I truly am a helpless creature...completely worthless to anyone and everything. I need to sink into the abyss which calls for me...I can almost see it. Without fighting, I feel myself slide deeper and deeper into the blackness. Does the legacy die here? Is this the end? ... NO! It can't end like this...I can't let Octavian win without a fight. This is not over...not by a fucking long shot! I fight now ? I feel myself pulling away from the hole I was sinking into. I strain every part of my being to fight my way from the end. In the distance I see a light, faint at first. As I struggle more and more I draw nearer to it. After what feels like forever with my body screaming to end the strain, I take one final lunge at that light. The nurse turns towards the bed one more time before walking out the door. Her eyes open wide as she screams out: 'Doctor Jacobs, he's awake! Get back here quick!!!.' Is this all going to be worth it?
