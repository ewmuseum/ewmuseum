Date: 20-Oct-2006 So more know what's going on with Dani's story right now. **************************** Danielle was a happy woman. Even though she had recently lost the GCW Television Title in a match where she was not pinned. The new and current Champion, Jordan White had pinned a third man in the match. She was happy. Yes, she had come in fourth in GCW's Dangerous Games Rumble Match. Word had it that the top four were destined for some sort of reward, she just had to find out what it was now. But it meant that she was in the ballpark in GCW as the likes of Jacob McKail, Brandon Youngblood and her friend, Lia whom most knew as Shadow. But why was she happy? A few days before Dangerous Games, she had a big change in her life. A change that should only happen once to most people. Danielle Lynn Northsky and Joshua Bodaway Brand became husband and wife at the Wedding Chapel in the Aladdin Resort and Casino in Las Vegas. For all intents and purposes she was now Danielle Brand, but for her entertainment contracts and that, she and Josh had decided that they would keep her Northsky name for them. Oh, some may ask, who is this Joshua Brand guy? Well he's one half of the GCW Tag Team Champions and the PTC Tag Team Champions with his brother, Grayson. Together they are known as UNITE. And all around, they are members of the strongest faction in all of PTC, the Knightmare Brigade: Dies Irae. An elite group that contains the likes of Shadow, Virgil Ambrosi, Dawn McFarlane, Blaise McClancy and others. So while she's had one downside, her career still got a boost the following week by going so long, almost the entire match, at Dangerous Games. And her now married life is great. Well, not everything can make her happy though. She and Josh did elope. Josh is not a Sicangu Lakota or Upper Brul? Sioux like her. He was of the Saquamish People over in the Puget Sound region of Washington. To top it off, his mother was a French immigrant who had been adopted by the nation when she'd married his late father. While she had permission from her grandfather to marry Josh, the rest of the family wasn't too keen on him. Dangerous Games was over, and She was getting ready to head out for the PrimeTime Central event known as GTT6. Her first round match up was against a man known as SSL or 'Sinister' Steven Lee. She'd seen the man around PTC events for the last two years at least a couple of times, but she knew not much about him. But that didn't matter to someone like her. An opponent was an opponent. You had to think on your feet in the ring, be ready for anything. She'd learned a lot in the years she trained at the Order's West Wind Academy in Chicago under not only her now brother in law, but the likes of Sean McClancy and Draven Lox. But one thing sat on her mind for this event. Last year, GTT5 she had lost in the first round to her new ally in the KB:DI, Vivica Valentine. Right now all she needed to do was get through today, and then get through this first round match. First things first though, it was time to announce to the family that the Northsky/Matoskah and the Brands had joined together at a junction made by her and Josh. ~A Few Days After~ ~GCW's Dangerous Games Event~ ~Rosebud Sioux Reservation~ ~Black Hills Region~ ~South Dakota~ It was time. Danielle found herself with her new husband going to go face her clan and family. The last time she was there Josh had gotten into it with her cousin, Steven. Steven was a member of the AIM and other groups who actually made the KKK and others look like the 'Loyal Order of Water Buffaloes' on the Flintstones Cartoon. This time Josh promised to be on his best behavior, but Terra had told Danielle that her cousin was in a foul mood as of late. She'd found out that her Grandfather was stepping down from the tribal counsel of the reservation, and that Steven was making a bid to take over the soon to be vacant seat. However, Steven was not very popular and so it appeared that it was not going well for him. For Danielle, it didn't surprise her one bit. It seems that the likes of him didn't need to be there, if it was up to Steven he would be more into keeping them in the time of their forefathers and taking scalps and other things from the 'white invaders'. He wanted nothing to do with the white man and half breeds. Especially with the type of half breeds that the her own order dealt with. As they drove down the road in the rented Jeep, they both tried to get ready. They were heading for her family's cabin out here in the rural parts of the reservation. It wasn't a small cabin, and in the last two years most of the family had moved out after she had left. Only Steven, Terra, Grandpa Soaring Eagle and the occasional visitor were ever there anymore. Even the stable was down from the fifteen horses that she remembered, to only two now. Danielle looked at her ring on her hand as Josh was driving. She couldn't believe that they had actually did it; no cameras, no reporters. Only Lia and Virgil there with them as impromptu witnesses. She felt a little guilty she hadn't asked Blaise, Ukyo or even Gray or Alexis to stand there with them. But, she was afraid that Josh would have chickened out or figured a way to worm his way out of the wedding if they had been around. Plus she knew that the mental sounds would of been above and beyond what Josh could of handled. He was an empath, able to sense others feelings and emotions, a trait he had inherited from his mother. There was enough from her and the other two in the room, adding so many more at that time might have made it hard for him to think. As it was, he had a headache for a few hours after the ceremony, but then his worries had gone to his brother. The Demons of his past almost bit him on the ass again. Danielle reached over and grabbed a hold of Josh's hand, running her long fingers over the back of his hand before grasping into it. He just threw a smile her way then turned his eyes back to the road. They only had a few days before she had to be in the ring again but they had promised themselves once this was all over they would go on a nice honeymoon. Blaise had promised to pay for the honeymoon as it was, and in talking with even his boss, Ian Childress, they had actually be recommended to stay in Singapore after the event this coming week, and maybe take in some of the sights there. They were also contemplating a hop down to Darwin, Australia as it was so close. As they got closer to the home she'd spent her last eight years of her time growing up living in, she was getting more and more nervous. Finally the car slowly pulled up to the old homestead. He gave her hand a comforting squeeze and then looked and smiled at her, this was going to be the hardest thing for her since their elopement a only a little more then a week ago.. Danielle knew this was going to be a touchy subject, while he had supported it before, one word from her grandfather and all would be ruined no matter what the law said. Although by tribal law, they had been married for over a year now, it was now also under the white man's law. One word from him and she'd have no choice but to either leave him or to leave the tribal people she embraced so much. But Josh knew of one more problem: Steven. Danielle hardened herself ready to speak up for the man she'd chosen if that became an issue at all. As Josh parked the car Danielle looked up towards the house. On the porch was a weathered and leathery looking old man. His name was Soaring Eagle, and he was the father of her mother, Lynn Matoskah as she had been known before she married Daniel Northsky. The old man started to try to walk at a brisk pace to meet the Jeep, hobbling on his cane and not moving much faster then Dani could walk. She barely waited for the car to come to a full stop before she jumped out and ran to meet her Grandfather. 'Tunkaschila, lotancila' She greeted him in the native Lakota tongue. Old Man Soaring Eagle give her a kiss on her forehead and embraced his granddaughter lovingly before he addressed her. 'Now here stands a married woman, no longer a little girl before me. We must celebrate and you will dance for us Sirius Star, and you will have Chikala Shunkaha dance with you.' She smiled wide for the words, in this short time of only a few sentences he'd blessed her marriage. He used her 'Native' name in it as it was. He even addressed Josh by the nickname his family had for him. His brother, Gray was 'Big Wolf' and he was 'Little Wolf' for reasons that only those who actually knew them intimately would understand. 'I would be honored to, but will have to see what dresses I have here still I did not bring any of mine with me. We're flying out in a few days to Seattle to see his mother for a day, and then it's off to Singapore for the wrestling event.' As she spoke another figure stepped out from inside the house leaning against a post that was holding up the roof to the porch. It was Steven dressed in black jeans, a tight t-shirt and a black hand beaded leather coat, his long hair was pulled back out of his face. The shirt was one of his 'we hate the white man' rap bands and it showed what type of mode he would be in for today quite well. She knew him, he'd chosen it on purpose. He spoke but one word to her as he nodded. 'Tahunsa ' Danielle looked up and nodded back. He'd only called her 'cousin', he didn't address her as 'Dani' or even 'Danielle'. She tried to be cordial back, but returned the same sentiment back to him. 'Tahunsa , how are you doing this day?' 'Could be better' Steven used his shoulder to push off from the post and took the few steps to get close to his cousin. He didn't hide the fact that he was looking over her shoulder at Josh. He stopped and then walked towards Josh who was messing with the luggage from the back of the car and trying to balance that and close the side swinging door at the same time. 'Tahunsa, how are you today?' Steven seemed to give a voice of disdain as that word come out of his mouth speaking to josh. Josh could feel the hate from Steven's mind. It was almost like hitting a physical wall. One thing that Steven always failed to remember was that Josh and Grayson had both learned Lakota and Dakota from when they lived in this part of the country when they were training to become wrestlers. Josh tried to keep his promise to Dani, though. 'Very well, thank you for asking cousin-in-law' Josh shifted the balance of the packages in his arms, as Steven lifted a few of them from Josh. With a smile and a reminder to himself that he would be polite to Steven, Josh gave them up. 'Thanks. She insisted on bringing a few things back for the kids at the school and orphanage.' The orphanage in question was a white thing. It was a Catholic run orphanage that was handled by the local nuns. For the first ten years of Dani's life, she'd lived there. Steven did not like them one bit, for more obvious reasons. 'Why she keeps going back there, I will never understand. After all they did to her. And here, she brings them gifts. And sends them money.' 'You don't pay much attention do you? They protected her from harm. Kokabel? Morning Star himself has told her this. Her time in the orphanage was planned. If you don't understand that then you need to really talk to Soaring Eagle. Plus this stuff is all stuff from the Wrestlers of GCW they all kinda just tossed stuff in the boxes for her when she mentioned going to visit orphans. You know even white men have good hearts. And the children are underprivileged, and sometimes just getting something, even something with a Brandon Youngblood signature will help them get through the hard time of not having any parents around.' Josh shoved past him and then set the boxes down on the porch. He could feel the hatred raise in Steven's mind as his eyes bore a hole in the back of Josh's head. Danielle met up with him as he smiled at her, her titled her head and nodded towards her cousin. She spoke in a whisper to him. 'Whats up with him? Same old same old.' 'Of course. He thinks I'm half white devil.' Dani could only shake her head. 'Tahunsa, you have heard of the changes coming to the reservation soon? Yes?' Dani turned around. She knew what he was after here. He was trying to get her goat and get a rise out of Josh. Dani turned around to look at him while he went to gloat about his bid for the seat on the Tribal Counsel. 'I've heard rumors as we were coming in. We had to stop at the gas station for a rest break before we got too far up the road.' Steven nodded to her and then started walking around like some die hard politician. 'Well, as you may have heard, Tunkaschila is retiring his seat on the counsel. And with this we have opened up the campaigns for who would replace him.' 'What do you mean he's retiring it? All elders are allowed to stay on until either forced out of they choose to.' Her grandfather spoke up. 'I am getting to be an old man now. I have seen and served in two world wars, and I would like some time to not worry about anything else before we have a third. I will reach my ninetieth year soon, and I'd like to not have to worry about things that I feel I'm forgetting every day at that time.' Dani could understand that, she took in the wise words as he continued to speak. 'And, I've got too many telling me that I'm just an old man who's only good for telling stories on the porch of Wounded Knee and other things. Although since that is an activity I enjoy, I would like to just move to doing that for my last days.' The way Old Man Soaring Eagle looked, moving his eyes to bring Steven into his peripheral vision in a glance when he said that last statement, Dani knew whom he meant was calling him too old to be of any use. 'Grandfather, you are full of wisdom and experience. You'd let the counsel loose that for some hotshot wannabe politician?' She caught herself after she said it. She knew Steven would assume she was speaking about him. 'I'm nothing of the sort. I am the only candidate to replace him that has the best interest of this nation at heart!' She looked at him with a glare that went right down her nose. 'You and your friends do not have the best of this nation in your hearts unless you're wanting to repeat Little Big Horn and Wounded Knee. You would have us seal our borders and live as foragers and hunters, knocking us back to the stone age.' He took the bait, even if she had not intended to lay it out. 'I want what is best for us. We should be our own people. We should have our land, and we should be free of the influence of people like HIM!' He pointed at Josh. And it was on. 'What's that supposed to mean?' Josh dropped what he had left and took a few steps forward. 'What do you think it means? Look at you? You may have our skin and our hair, but your true blood shines through with your green eyes. Those are not the eyes of one of us.' And the expected return came from Josh's mouth. 'I'm sorry Hiawatha, but I'm not even Sioux. I'm Saquamish. My father's native tongue was Lootshoot and Salish. Both dialects I speak quite well.' 'Your people will allow anyone to join for a few thousand dollars. They can not call themselves American Indians anymore.' That was low. Very Low. Josh's family line could actually be traced back on his father's side to Chief Noah Sealth, also known as Chief Seattle. His native heritage could not be questioned. Dani tried to grab Josh by his arm before this went more then verbal. 'Fucking excuse me, but if not for someone from MY People you'd have been wiped out years ago. Seattle was the diplomat that stopped the Indian wars you fucktard! That's my family, that's my people, and that's my part of the nation. But he spoke for all when he spoke.' Steven wanted the conflict. 'He laid down to the White man like a red dog!' **POW** Steven didn't even see it coming. Many years of training in the arts of Escrima and Kali from the Filipino district of Seattle where Josh had lived for a good portion of his life let him snap out with a well aimed left hook that dropped Steven like a sack of potatoes. As he dropped, Josh was right there on him, bringing his left hand back and then firing it in again and again at his new cousin in law's head and upper torse. Dani could see Steven try to cover his head and face, the man wasn't built for fighting as it was, and Josh was a wrestling champion. It was quite possibly a bigger mismatch then Almasy vs. Kimbusa was on the tale of the tape. As curse words in English, his mother's native French, and Lootshoot/Salish came from Josh's mouth, Dani dove in to pull her new husband off of her cousin. 'Josh, no? not right now, please!' She hooked his left arm under his shoulder and tried to pull him off. She succeeded as soon as he realized who had grabbed him. Unfortunately, Steven had other ideas. The smaller man in height, but not weight, lunged at Josh, knocking both him and Dani to the ground. Dani had enough of it. Apparently so did her grandfather. **whack** They heard the cane come down onto Steven's back. But he didn't stop trying to grab at Josh's throat. **whack** And the cane came down again. That time a slight clunk could be heard as it connected with Steven's skull. He stopped and grabbed the back of his head, allowing Dani to bring up a foot and push him off as he'd pinned Josh down on top of her. Josh helped him to the side of them and then just let him go to drop down to the ground as Dani heard a stern voice from the elder of her family. 'Takoja! My granddaughter, and her husband are guests in what is still my house! You will not bring conflict while they are here!' Steven worked his way up to his feet, still holding the back of his head. 'Why are they here? She hangs out with people not of our kind anymore.' That was again low. As Dani stood she had to respond. 'What? What's up your ass?' Steven glared at her. 'You've forsaken us for people like this McClancy guy, and the others in your entertainment industry. You've posed almost nude for white man's magazines so they can see some sort of Pocahontas. You marry one of their own. The only pure blood you have in your little group is the one who doesn't even look like one of us. His hair is white.' She couldn't control herself. 'Excuse me. But first off, his name is Grayson, and he answers to Gray. His hair is Gray, which is how he got his name. And for your information, he's not as much of an uptight asshole as you, his girlfriend happens to be one of the whitest but sweetest women I know. Second, Blaise McClancy is a good friend, and he's of the Moapa Paiute Tribe, reservation born and raised. Just because his father was an immigrant as Josh's mother was, doesn't make him any less human?' He cut her off. 'But he's dating something even worse then a white woman?' She returned in kind. 'Oh, fuck off, Steven. You don't even have a native first name! Hell, you look at me. My father was a Ogala Sioux, and my mother was a Sicangu Sioux. But you know what else I am, the whole family knows. So somewhere in my father's past, in fact since I'm the seventh generation from Morning Star? something else was mixed in with our people's blood! So if you're going to insult people for being non-pure, I guess you can include me into that.' This was something not normally spoken about but in a position of honor. Dani's line, the Northsky line of which she was the last, meant that she was the inheritor of the position reserved for the descendants of a Wakan Takan, something she had discovered were the very Angels of Heaven, a notion that would undermine the churches claims against her people from centuries past. Her family line started with Morning Star, the angel named Kokebel or 'Star of God' who had fallen for a mortal woman in her tribe. Their child, Fallen Star, was a hero to her kind that most considered a myth if you lived outside of the Sioux Nations. Dani was more then just the inheritor in name. For she had herself shown that she was, by being the seventh generation from Morning Star was Wakan Takan in more then just blood. He didn't have an answer for her outburst though. Steven just looked at her dumbfounded. She didn't stop though. 'You know, Steven, if I didn't have other commitments, you could end up with a non-pure challenge to your bid for Grandfather's seat. And I'm sure someone who could bring forth the wings of a Wakan Takan on her back would hold up a lot better then your RACIST and IGNORANT views would.' He snapped back at her. 'Why would they vote for you? You left us to go live in the other world. To make money and show your body off to anyone who would see it.' She walked up and got into his face. 'Because in this chest beats the heart of a real Sioux. One that is connected to the world around her, even other people who would learn to respect us and our ways more if we showed them the same respect. You, you're just pathetic. Josh, get our luggage, we'll find a hotel for tonight.' She turned to her grandfather. 'Tunkaschila, I'm sorry. Maybe we can take you out for dinner with us? We would love to have you around to speak to before we travel again.' It was obvious that her grandfather had had more then enough of this conflict. But he was going to show a side that her arrogant cousin wasn't expecting. 'Steven?' The man he had addressed turned to him. 'Go call Terra and tell her that you need a place to sleep for the next two nights. Maybe some time in her bed will get that stick out of your ass!' Dani double took the view of the situation. Her grandfather had basically just told Steven to get out, and go get laid by his fianc? for the next two days and not to come back until Dani and Josh had left. 'But, I live here!' Old Man Soaring Eagle took a few steps towards his home, using his cane for support. 'You live here out of the graces of an old man. I'm sure that with my granddaughter from the daughter I lost, and her new husband, I will manage for two days. Josh is actually a good cook from what I've heard. And before I leave you to your woman. And I'll remind you of two things, Takoja. You're woman is a member of the same organization that these two serve outside of this reservation. And I'll also remind you that I lost my only daughter to white men who share your views of the world.' With that, Old Man Soaring Eagle climbed up onto the porch and stepped inside. Dani started to pick up some of the packages and such that were on the porch when she heard Josh take one last dig at her cousin. 'Hey?' Steven turned to look at him. 'Need a cellphone to call Terra?' Steven glared at him as Dani moved to stop a repeat from happening. 'Josh, stop it, and come help take these in.' Josh smiled as Steven almost snarled back at him. Dani opened the porch door and reached in to grab the wireless phone off the wall mount and throw it to Steven. As he dialed the two newlyweds began to pull their stuff inside. Josh spoke in a whisper to her. 'I hope he doesn't do anything like slash the Jeep's tires. I don't know if their roadside assistance can come out this far.' She rolled her eyes as this part of her tale ends. ~End?~ Codex of Lakota Words Used: tunkaschila: Grandfather lotancila: I love you chikala: Little shunkaha: Wolf tahunsa: Cousin takoja : grandchild
