Date: Same Spitty Time, (Yeah this should’ve been before the last Worldwide. But well life kinda got in the way, and considering I have a commitment to the greater story, here it is.) Same caf?, same night. They’d been sitting across the table from one another almost half an hour now. Not a single word spoken, since Sheena had demanded they talk about a few things. The silence hung thick between them palpable. Arms crossed over his chest Stevens was sullen, uncomfortable, and fidgety. It was in those moments all he could have asked for in the world was a glass of Scotch. Just something to take the edge off, this conversation was going to be tough. Unfortunately you can’t always get what you want, and this was definitely one of those times. Oh well, life goes on right? Lighting another cigarette, and inhaling deeply, he finally decided to break the silence. “So” You’re the one who wanted to talk, say something.” “Ok, lets face it Michael, you owe me, big time.” “Owe, you” For what?” “Lets see, where do you want me to start” The mind games, treating me like a piece of dirt, always putting anything and everything before me or us.” “Hey, I admitted I was an asshole, what more do you want from me” Besides you knew exactly what you getting yourself into when we started going out anyway. Thusly I don’t owe you a damn thing/? “You can’t going around treating people like you do and expect to get away with it forever. Seriously, Michael, have you ever even once considered how you sound and act half of the time” Don’t you realize how often you hurt people?” “Honestly, I don’t give a crap.” “That’s such bullshit. You care, I know you do, if you didn’t I wouldn’t get drunk phone calls from you in the middle of the night, begging me to take you back. It’s all finally catching up with you Mike, the years of being an asshole have finally started eating you up inside, we all knew it would come eventually, me, Bruce, Alex, hell even your family knew it.” It sucks when someone flat out tells you something, that you’ve been trying to deny for weeks. Actually it a lot more than sucks, it just hurts. Everything you’ve been pushing down, everyday just boils up to the surface, and all of a sudden you’re at a complete loss for words. Your mind goes blank, your mouth dries up, and your palms start to sweat. You feel the stinging irritation in the corners of your eyes that threatens to turn into tears if it goes unchecked. Swallowing deeply, trying to push it all back, he searched for something intelligent to say. Some wry remark to either change the course of the conversation, or at very least blow off the accusations. The only problem is there’s absolutely nothing there, so in desperation you go with the only thing you can think of. “Fuck that.” Immediately, you’re kicking yourself, and you wish you hadn’t said it. At best you sound like a complete and total fool, at worst you’re sounding like a sullen child that’s just been scolded by his parent. Now the desperation really starts to kick in, and you’re again searching for something to say, something better to make you sound less like an idiot. Again you say the first thing you can think of, and you’re still sounding like an idiot. “Seriously, fuck that.” “Oh yeah, that’s great Michael. Fuck that, such a beautiful philosophy to live your life by, you’ll never go wrong with that attitude. Yes, siree bob, Mike, that’s spectacular, you’re brilliant.” Jesus, she could’ve at least not been that sarcastic. Seriously, how in the hell had things turned so completely. He was supposed to be the one with the rapier like wit, instead he was being talked down to like a child, by some chick he used to think was the dumbest woman on the planet. And now all of a sudden, here she was making cutting him to ribbons with her words, it was just plain embarrassing. “Ok, so maybe that wasn’t the best response on the planet, but seriously thanks for trying to make me feel like dirt, it was lovely, I appreciate it. Listen, Sheena, you might think you know exactly what’s going on in my head or whatever, but really you’ve got no idea. Sure, I’ve head a rough couple weeks, and yeah that sucks but I’ll get over it, hell it’s not that hard to get back on the horse you know. And for the record, I never begged. It’s true I had a little bit too much to drink one night, and I gave you a call, I guess I was desperate.” “That’s just great Mike. Fire back at me all you want, but you’re still not going to change what’s going on in that head of yours. You’ve been a complete jackass your entire life, and it’s we can all tell it’s been eating you up inside. And now, there’s almost nothing left, you’ve got this big dark empty hole, that you’ve been trying to fill up with liquor, and cheap women, anything you can. But it’s not working is it Mike, you’re still just as empty as you ever were, and it’s got to stop.” “Ok, fine whatever. I’mma jackass, we both know that. So really what do you want from me?” “I want you to stop and think Michael. I want you to actually start focusing on the things in your life that are actually important. You’ve got your wrestling, and that’s fine, when you’re there you can do whatever the hell you want, but in life you’ve got to start acting differently or you’re going to destroy yourself. For the life of me I don’t really understand why, but I still actually care about you, or I wouldn’t be here, and I can’t keep caring about you as long as you stay the man you are right now.” “I guess you’re shit outta luck then.” Maybe that wasn’t the smartest thing in the world to say, in fact you regret it the moment you say it. You start feeling like an asshole, because you know everything she’s saying is right. Besides which you know you’ve still got some feelings for her that you don’t want to admit to yourself, so as you watch her fume about to storm away from the table you do the only thing you can, back pedal, and quickly. “Look, I’m sorry, I didn’t mean that. You were just hitting a little close to the mark that’s all. I can’t just be someone else immediately. I know you’re just trying to help me out, I get that, I really do, but maybe I’m just not the best at accepting what may or may not be flaws in my personality. Look, it’s getting really late, and I need to get out of here. If you want meet me at Worldwide tomorrow night, and we can talk some more. I’ll make sure security will know who you are. Again, sorry.” “I dunno Mike, I’ll think about it.” “Alright.” Laying a twenty on the table he slid out of the booth, and stood. Hanging his head slightly he started heading out the door, glancing towards the waitress he shook his head a bit. “It’s on the table.” “OK, sweetie, goodnight, try to stay dry.” “Yeah, sure whatever. Night.” ~fin~
