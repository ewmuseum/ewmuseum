LOST

Date: 06/09/11 I. Its eyes flicker to life, fluttering a few times before actually opening even a little bit. It feels like Its eyes have been closed for years and It could swear that it heard the sound of groaning wooden floorboards as Its eyelids forced themselves halfway open, the widest they were getting at the current time. It feels like it is just waking from a long slumber, maybe even a coma. Its mind is a jumbled mess, Its muscles unresponsive and Its energy sapped. It tries to move anyways. It doesn’t budge. It shuts Its eyes again and for a split second It contemplates falling back into the deep sleep it obviously was in before. The thought is enticing enough that a small grin forms on Its lips and a pleasant groan escapes Its throat. Its eyes reopen. Its mind focuses. It DID smile. It DID make a sound. Which means, It is capable of moving. Of thinking. It is capable of doing all sorts of wonderful things. With newfound determination It tries to focus and It knows it is best to start with one thing at a time. It decides to take a look at Its surroundings. Blackness. Pitch blackness. It can hardly see anything but It doesn’t know if this is because of It actually being surrounded in darkness or just because Its eyes have yet to adjust. It must focus harder on seeing, on orienting itself with Its surroundings. It takes in a few deep breaths. This helps. Slowly things begin to take shape. Just shadowy figures but It knows that It must start somewhere. After a bit of time It comes to the conclusion that It must be engulfed in darkness because the shadows don’t transform into discernable objects. It realizes that It is thinking. It smiles a weak smile. Next, It realizes that It must move. If It has been asleep for as long as It feels then It knows that moving will and should get things moving along nicely. It would love to get its mind back under control but a certain thought pops into Its brain … “All in due time.” It tries to move Its entire body first, head, arms, torso, legs … all of it. It fails miserably and drains more energy in the process. It decides to narrow Its focus. Concentrate on one movement, on one hand, one finger, one muscle. More deep breaths. This helps, again. It focuses on Its right index finger on Its right hand. It tries to recall all of the things It use to do with Its right hand. Did It pleasure women? A specific woman? Did It write stories or poetry? Did It fire a gun? Did It unscrew the caps of water bottles? None of these thoughts brought anything to the surface and this slightly annoyed It but more importantly, It felt something else … something darker, something much more primal. It felt anger. This brought forth new questions. Did It hurt people? Did It ball it up into a fist and strike people? Did It wrap Its hand around other’s throats and squeeze? Did IT kill? A momentary euphoria surged through Its body … And Its right index finger on Its right hand twitched! It smiled again and It thought … Now we’re getting somewhere II. Some things never change. For Roberto Abitua, on this day, he is like he has always been. A predator. He lurks, he hides, he prepares to swoop in and go after his prey. His skills are sharp, he has been practicing for a long time. Inside his old sanctuary, the GCW ring, he got plenty of practice in the old game of cat and mouse. He grins a knowing grin. His prey, although knowing that someone or something is after them, cannot figure out which direction the attack is going to come from. This prey cautiously looks around even spinning completely in the process. Roberto’s position is safe. Of course it is. He is a master hunter. Roberto’s icy blue eyes lock onto their target and when his prey’s back is facing him, he decides it is time pounce. Showing his full form out in the open for the first time, Roberto charges. The only warning his prey receives is something of a war cry just as Roberto is towering over it. The helpless victim can do nothing but stumble back with its eyes bulged out in complete … Uh … No. It falls in a fit of laughter. Because Roberto, the mighty stalker of the GCW ring, doesn’t attack. He tickles. Some things never change … others obviously do. Roberto wraps his hands around the torso of young Joaquin Abitua, his young son, and he lets his fingers go wild, tickling the boy relentlessly. The only torture to little Joaquin is the trouble he has breathing after being tickled for too long but this is when Papi Roberto makes sure to stop his attack. Most people would struggle to recognize this new version of such a horrible man. Domesticated LeStatt? The people would howl Blasphemy! Roberto wouldn’t blame them, of course. He never saw himself being able to truly live happily ever after without the occasional power bomb or suplex to keep his mind clear. Perhaps more importantly, he wasn’t sure if he’d like becoming a fat house cat after being the King of the Jungle for so long. He would’ve bet anything when he quit GCW, after toppling Vivica Valentine, that he wouldn’t be gone long. A funny thing happened to him when he returned home. He got to see his son grow up. He got to make love with his beautiful but somewhat loony wife, Mary … and when he didn’t get to make love to her they sure as hell did what could best be described as wild animal lusting. Roberto had filled himself with so much anger, so much violence that he had completely hidden himself away from how wonderful everyday life could be. How exquisite RAISING a life could be. Life was more than black shadows, purple bruises and red blood. Every time he holds his son he sees the light that was missing in his life. So he does it again now, lifting the boy above his head and allowing the young laughter to echo through the hallway and into the kitchen where Mary was working diligently on something that Roberto was sure would taste wonderful even if it looked like something out of a Dr. Seuss book. “I hungry, Papi!” Roberto’s smile is wide and true. “Then let us go eat, lil nino.” Roberto puts his boy down on the floor again. Joaquin immediately reaches up with one small, fragile hand and grasps his father’s pinky finger. Together they walk down the hall to join the other piece of the Abitua puzzle. Everything is as it should be. III. After what seemed like an eternity, It finally managed to regain control of all of Its muscles. Some still remained very stiff, obviously still fighting off the long and unwanted slumber that they had been forced into but for the most part the mission was accomplished. With this task out of the way It had to focus Its mind onto something else … that being Its mind, itself. It was growing anxious and angry and frustrated that It still couldn’t remember where It was or how It had gotten there. It became down right pissed off when It realized that It didn’t even know WHAT It was or WHY It existed. It did know that something awful must have happened but that didn’t help It regain Its composure of become less angry with itself because that was an easy thought to have … memories don’t just leave you for no reason at all. It sits up, pressing Its back up against the cool metal bars behind It. Its smacks the back of Its head on those same bars since Its neck was still tight and control wasn’t absolute just yet. It growls as the pain shoots through Its body. It wants to grab the metal and tear it apart. It wants to rip the throat out of whoever put It in this cage … Cage. Caged. Its in a cage. It feels dumb for not realizing that It had realized this until now. Disorientation is a wild thing. Again this new revelation did nothing to soothe It. In fact, It just grew more enraged. It was locked up for some crimes that It must’ve committed … now what? How does It get out? How does It figure out why Its here? It slams the back of Its head back up against the bars, partly to try to shake loose any memories and partly to punish It for being so fucking useless. It grinds Its teeth together. It forms fists with Its hands. It takes in a deep breath. This doesn’t help this time. It closes Its eyes tight, again hoping for some miracle or any other form of divine intervention to kick in and just give It all of the answers that It seeks. It just brought more questions. Is there a God to seek miracles from? Does It believe in God? Does It pray? Does It deserve to be saved? Just like everything else, It can’t say. It begins to fear that It may never be able to say ever again. Funny thing about fear. It can bring desperation out unlike anything else. It was not immune to this. It fears for Its survival even if It doesn’t know what exactly It is. This seems like a very small dilemma to It in the grand scheme of things. Save Its life first then ask about the details later. Problem is that It has no tools. No light. No key. Its mind is a shattered and jumbled mess. It has but the most primal and basic of information at Its disposal. The one thing that It can think to try could be Its more successful. Violence. It reaches up with both hands and latches onto the cage bars near Its head and then brings Its legs closer to its chest … What the hell, It has nothing to lose. Using the bars as leverage, It kicks straight out and slams Its feet up against the bars. The entire cage shakes and the loud crash echoes through the darkness. It stops to listen. What if whoever put It there was still around? What if he heard It trying to escape? It listens intently for a little longer. Nothing. It breaths deep and kicks again with more force. The result is the same. Shaky cage, echoing sound. It stops to listen again but doesn’t waste as much time. It kicks again. Again. Again. Again. Again. Again. A sound crawls out from Its throat … Again. Again. Again. Again. Something new. Something foreign. Again. Again. Again. It comes out another time. Again. Again. It recognizes the sound. Again! LAUGHTER. Maniacal. AGAIN! Its confidence grows. It WILL get out. AGAIN! AGAIN! AGAIN! IV. Dinner was delicious … even if Roberto still isn’t sure what it was. Watching the original Ninja Turtles movie for the (approximately) 4,000,0000th time was fine too. At least Joaquin seemed to seriously enjoy it. After the movie, Joaquin and Roberto did a bit of live Ninja Turtle role playing with Joaquin’s Leonardo vanquishing the evil Shredder once and for all! Or at least until the next time the DVD is put into the DVD player. They had a snack together. Ice cream, cookies and cream to be exact. Roberto marveled at how his son turned into a vacuum cleaner when the ice cream was put in front of him and was gone in record time. At least Roberto assumed it would’ve been record time if anyone from The Guinness Book of World Records would have bothered showing up to the historic event. Bed time came for little Joaquin and he whined and complained. He gave Mom the puppy dog eyes and Dad got the pouty lip. Neither worked but Mom and Dad were fun enough to make him believe it would, even if only for a split second. Mom tucked him in and Dad got the great honor of having Joaquin’s favorite bed time story read to him. Yes, Joaquin reads his own stories. When it was finished Joaquin tried to con Roberto into another but he refused. After a very one sided argument, Joaquin backed off. Roberto kissed his son on the forehead, turned off the light in the boy’s room and let him drift off to sleep. When Roberto made his way to the bedroom Mary was waiting for him wearing nothing but the attempted seductive smile that was on her face. She laughed before Roberto could. Mary is a very sexual creature but the sensual, seductive stuff? Its nothing but a game for her, one she wasn’t very good at. Roberto just shook his as he approached his Ebony Goddess and they shared a quick chuckle before their lips touched for the first time. Soft and sweet at first but very quickly it became hungry and passionate. Mary worked her petite fingers vigorously to strip Roberto as quickly as he could ever remember … probably another World Record. When he was striped as bare as she was, he lifted her off her feet and pinned her up against the wall. From there? Well they found a splendid mix of making love and animal lusting. Now he lies in the dark, Mary’s head resting on his chest. Roberto’s fingers gently run up and down her smooth flesh. He listens to her low snoring, watches her head slowly heave up and down with the movement of his own chest. For many years Roberto had wondered if he could find real, absolute happiness and peace. For a long time he thought it was only inside of a ring that he could get that sort of peace of mind and calm in his life. Now he knows different. Now he knows what life should and can be. And as he drifts to sleep he cannot help but think of how lucky he truly is. V. AGAIN! AGAIN! AGAAAAAAAAAAIN! CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK! Its eyes light up in the darkness. It thought that It would never break free! But here It is, staring straight out at an open cage door. Now that It can be free It hesitates. Fear creeps in again but this time the feeling does not seem to empower It. Instead It just feels a very pitiful and very real humanoid sense of being scared out of Its wits. What shall It do when It is out? Will It find what It seeks? Will It know where to go when It does get out? What if It gets lost? What if It dies? The thoughts are almost too much for It to bear. It realizes though that It has to act. Better to die trying than to die waiting. It nods and takes a cautious move towards the door but halts right before actually taking a step outside. Its chest heaves up and down. It steps out of the cage. Almost immediately It falls to the floor, Its muscles again becoming something like JELLO. After a momentary panic It begins to pull itself across the cold, hard floor. It doesn’t know how It knows where to pull to but It does. It crawls until it reaches stairs. Its so dark that It cannot see how far the steps travel upwards but It won’t be stopped now. It begins the ascent. Slow. Steady. Careful. It moves up and up and up until It reaches a door. It pushes the door open and crawls through the threshold. Its tired but It doesn’t stop. Now It seems to slither across the floor. It begins to pick up scents. Familiar scents but still It is unable to pinpoint what they are. The slithering continues. The scents grow stronger. It begins to feel hungry and excited. Its breathing becomes erratic. It slithers to a room and is hit with two very powerful feelings. Its head flicks from left to right and from right to left. Stairs on the right. A door on the left. The smells are so distinct, so overwhelming that for a moment It feels as if It may pass out. It fights the urge to do so. Instead it focuses to identify these smells. It looks to the stairs first. Its senses scream at it, telling It that this is a very familiar smell. A smell that could bring him clarity and safety. The path WILL give him answers although the answers may not be at all what It is expecting. To the door It is stung with something different. Something foreign and fresh. It is struck with the undeniable scent of FREEDOM. It would be alone and free to make Its own choices. It is intoxicated by it! But Its head snaps back to the stairs as the scent beckons It. Then the door yanks harder. The known! The unknown! Answers! More questions! The game of tug of war continues until It feels as if Its head is going to explode! It growls a very animalistic growl and pounds Its fists on the ground! The pulling lowers to a low rumble. Its head clears for just a moment. And with one last look up the stairs It scurries out the door and into the unknown. VI. Roberto’s eyes fire open as if they were blinds pulled open. He can’t breath properly. Sweat pours down his body, soaking the sheets that he is covered up with. He is in a panicked state but he doesn’t know why. He sits up straight but it is all too quickly and the throbbing headache that he hadn’t fully known he has kicked up 100 notches and stopped him in his tracks. He rips at his temples with both of his hands. The confused man turns his body to let his feet dangle off the side of the bed for a moment before crashing down on the bedroom floor. He can’t slow his heart rate, he cannot calm his breathing. He moves to stand up but his headache stops him dead in his tracks like a car slamming into a brick wall. Roberto falls back into the seated position, clutching his head. “Whatsa matter Knightie?” Typically Roberto loves the sound of Mary’s voice but not now, not on this night. Tonight it just sounded like rusty blades screeching against a chalk board. He just shakes his head once as to not have to endure too much extra pain than he needs to. Mary starts a motion to get up and assist but he’ll have none of it and with a wave of his hand to signal to his wife that he’ll take care of it, Roberto gets up, although wobbly, to his feet. Getting his legs settled proved to be a bit of a challenge but it happened. Once Roberto was confident he could move without tumbling and breaking a lot of expensive stuff in the bedroom he lumbered towards the couple’s personal bathroom. He flips the switch to turn the light on first but hisses at it when the sudden brightness makes everything a 100 times worse. The switch is flipped again and Roberto is plunged into darkness. Even in this state, Roberto knows where his sink is and he finds it with minimal bumping into stuff that hurts. He turns the lever for the cold water and dips his had down to drink straight from the faucet. The cool water helps some and when the help was sufficient enough to get all of his brain cells functioning at an appropriate level he lifts his head up and leans forward on the sink, starting into his dark reflection in the mirror. A terrifying thought comes to him almost immediately. He’s FREE. But it is more than that. Much more. Somehow he knew it too. He isn’t just FREE … He… IT… Is GONE …
