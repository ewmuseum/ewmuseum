Date: 9 Feb 06 ** Yeah this mentions LeSTatt ZERO times and really has nothing to do with GCW. HAHA but its something I wrote and so read it or not! ** I could feel my heart pounding inside of my chest, seemingly wanting to burst out at any moment. Beads of sweat rested at the top of my forehead, only a few tricking down the side of my face. I kept clenching my hands into fists, then releasing the tension, my breathing was uneven, and my mind was racing. Nerves had kicked in big time, but that was expected at a time like this. I looked over to my left and was greeted by a teasing smile from the four guys closest to me. They seemed to know what was going through my mind, and that made me grin back at them. Victor took a step towards me, and placed a hand on my shoulder. I looked up at him, with a slight grin and he leaned down to whisper something into my ear. ? It’s alright Robert, nerves are expected ? suck it up “soldier”.” “I know ? things are just going to be so different after this.” “It’s what you’ve been waiting for, for a few years now though ? different isn’t bad.” Before responding back I took a moment to take in the entire scene in front of me. Prior to now I had been letting everything get to me that I hadn’t gotten a chance to fully enjoy the moment. All eyes were on me I knew that for sure, but it was comforting knowing that they were all loving looks. The people who came today, were only there to share in my happiness, or at least for the party afterwards. In the front were my mother and father, my mom already letting tears flow down her cheeks as she clenched my dad’s hand as if life depended on it. My dad probably would be crying if he wasn’t so good at holding that kind of stuff in, but his smile told his entire story. My sisters were all trying their best to enjoy everything when they weren’t enforcing the “rules” of the occasion on all of their kids, whom like normal kids were wanting to talk loudly, move around, and play. Then I looked back to my left, again to the guys who either were my brothers or might as well have been. Victor, my brother-in-law, Alex, my cousin, Paul my bestfriend, whom found himself a comfortable spot in the Abitua family over the years, and of course my brother Jesse. There were flowers scattered throughout the area, of all colors and kinds. The decoration department really wasn’t where I made my voice known, so whatever was up and out was just fine by me. Everyone was so well dressed and clean cut that I almost didn’t recognize some of the people in attendance, hell even I finally had on pants and a shirt that actually FIT to my body, which indeed is a miracle in itself. After taking in everything, a genuine smile comes to my face as I look back up to Victor. “Yes Vic, I have wanted this for a very long time.” Victor laughed out loud, then gave me a quick one armed hug and took his spot back at my side. I took in a deep breath, and let it all out as I got myself prepared to make this next step of my life. I adjusted my tie a bit, and fixed my top button on my jacket, obviously just looking for anything to do, until things really kicked off. That’s when that very familiar music began playing throughout the area, forcing everyone’s attention to the back. My entire body stiffened, as my eyes traveled to a large doorway all the way in the back. Almost like a wave, the people in attendance came to their feet, in honor of the beauty they were to witness. When SHE finally took her first step through that entrance way, it was almost as if time itself had come to a crawl. I had years of emotions and thoughts all caught up in my mind, body, and soul. It is said that at different times in your life, at very traumatic times or even very happy times, memories of your life can just flood your mind. Now she didn’t have more than 40 or 40 feet to travel down that aisle but to me it felt like an eternity. It seemed that just yesterday that I had just met the person I’d want to be with forever ? Summer of 1999 “Alright Ronnie, game point ? you’re about to lose again.” “Just play Robert.” Okay basketball wasn’t my game, but that didn’t stop me from usually beating “Mr. Basketball” himself, R.Jay Stang, an old friend of mine, since first grade actually. We were fourteen at the time, definetly not the coolest kids around but hey we had fun doing whatever it is we chose to do. The game of the day today, was basketball. R.Jay checked the ball to me and I dribbled to my right, since at that time that was the only direction I could go. He stayed on my pretty good, but his facial expressions always gave me a good laugh, especially when he was trying real hard. He reached out a few times to get the ball but to no avail. Then I dribbled the ball down on an uneven crack in the cement, and lost the ball, causing me to lose my dribble, so now I was stuck. “Well ? here goes nothing.” Then I just jumped up and threw up a prayer, which somehow bounced around the rim a bit, before dropping in, giving me yet another win over my good pal. I began laughing, not really sure how the hell I pulled that one off, and R.jay as usual just kinda rolled his eyes and mumbled some curses my way. “Best out of three Robert.” “No Thanks buddy.” “Why you scared Robert !”” I just laughed at him, as I took a seat on some steps that led to his back sliding door. He argued his case a little while longer, before hanging it up and sitting next to me. Sometimes I wondered how me and R.Jay became friends, so easily especially. We were so different, yet usually got along so well, well at the time I had no idea ? but in a matter of minutes I would meet the best thing R.Jay ever “gave” me in our friendship. We heard the sliding door open a little, and his little sister told him, “Mom” wanted him. We both got up from our seats and walked our way into the house to look for Mama. She was in the bathroom curling her hair, getting ready to go out. I’ve always considered Gayle, my “other” mom, because she always treated me well. I always joked with her, telling her I was the older son she should’ve had, only to get at R.Jay though. “R.Jay I don’t want you and your friends making too much noise tonight, because your cousin Kari is coming to stay the night so that she can go to take her test in the morning.” “Oh brother, mom why does she have to come here?” “Don’t worry about that, just make sure you let her sleep, she has to be up early. She’ll be here any minute so behave tonight.” R.Jay quickly walked downstairs, and I shortly followed him down to the basement. While he may have seemed annoyed about this turn of events I, being a 14 year old male was quite interested, to say the least. “What’s your problem Ronald?” “Frickin” Kari, that girl’s mean.” Our conversation didn’t get any further in as we heard the door open upstairs, and an unfamiliar voice came from upstairs. I waited for R.Jay to make a move upstairs, cause I wanted to see what this girl looked like, but of course he just flipped on his TV and started watching basketball. I looked around a bit, for a reason to go upstairs ? “I’m going to get something to drink, you want something?” “Mountain Dew.” I jogged up the stairs which led directly to the kitchen, and there was an opening that led directly to the living room from there, but of course I couldn’t make it obvious that I was looking at her, so I used everything in my body to not turn my head and just go straight to the fridge. I pulled out a couple of sodas and closed the door. I walked towards the basement again, when Valoree, R.Jay’s little sister called my name. I stopped and finally had a reason to look directly at this, Kari. “That’s Robert, R.Jay’s friend ? he’s always over here!” I barely heard Valoree’s introduction of me, as I stood frozen in that doorway, looking at Kari. She was quite beautiful, there was no doubt about it. After a few moments of nothing, I kinda shook my head, and regained my self. “Uh” sorry, Hi.” I gave an awkward smile, and normally this is where I’d get a “Wow you’re an idiot” look, but instead she smiled back, and it looked a lot like the same awkward smile I flashed to her. Something clicked inside me then, like I just felt a lot more comfortable, like maybe I could actually talk to this girl. “Um ? so Valoree where can I put my stuff?” We waved to each other as Valoree led Kari upstairs to her room, and I slowly walked back downstairs to the basement with a dumb grin on my face, that I had to force off before I got to R.Jay. I tossed him his soda, and cracked open mine before flopping down at the computer desk, to find something other than basketball ? and Kari to think about. It wasn’t until later on that I actually got to talk to Kari, as we were all downstairs in the basement. After a period of time where Kari and R.Jay ended up wrestling, and after a few very humorous minutes, she sent him away with a headache and a bad elbow. I of course now found myself alone with Kari, and the weird thing was it wasn’t awkward nor was I THAT nervous. We seemed to hit it off quickly, and although it really didn’t dawn on me until much later, there was a whole lot of flirting going on from both sides ? tickling, laughing, or just finding any excuse to do some innocent touching. Although of that day the most memorable thing was our goodbye. Kari had her bags, and was thanking Gayle for letting her stay, and was just about out the door when I walked upstairs, and Valoree decided to play match maker. “Wait Kari, aren’t you going to say bye to Robert.” Kari and myself made eye contact and just kind of laughed. “Bye Kari.” “Bye” That obviously wasn’t good enough for Val though, as she pressed on. “No aren’t you going to give him a hug “bye ?!” Slowly we made our way to each other, and gave each other a hug, not very long or anything, just a simple hug. She said good bye again, then walked out of the door? That was the highlight of my young life! After that day Kari and myself stayed in contact, and would randomly see each other at festivals and such, but nothing big. I never wanted to call her too much and get her to just get tired of me, so I never pressed anything. I knew how she could be with people, and although we hadn’t gotten into anything remotely close to an argument, I prefer’d to keep it that way. It wasn’t until two years after that first time of meeting her, and always hoping that one day I’d have a shot at her that something developed? NOVEMBER 2001 “Happy Birthday You Son of a bitch!” I yelled out this lovely greeting, as I stepped inside R.Jay’s house and handed him his gift. As I recall I wasn’t originally invited to this little get together at their house, but see I called just finding out what was going on, and it wasn’t when he told me about the “party”, it was when he told me Kari was there as well, that I told him I was going to come in an invade. Hey, how could you blame me ? It was the perfect opening. I mingled for a very little bit, before snaking through the people to get to the basement where Kari was. Things were always different with her, that’s for sure. It didn’t really matter how often we spoke, or if I dated anyone, whenever I saw her, she took precedence. This night was the same way, as we talked and laughed, and again flirted, a lot like the first night that we had met. When things settled down, and everyone left, including Kari I had that goofy grin on my face again. I knew how I felt about her, but would I ever really have a chance with someone like her ? Well I never thought so, until a little bit later that night. I was sitting at R.Jay’s computer, signed onto AOL and checked my E-Mail, and strangely I already had an E_mail from Kari. Not quite sure what it’d be about, I eagerly opened it up, what I found was almost too good to believe. See I had a crush on her for so long, that reading statements from her about how “cute” she thought I looked that night, and how she didn’t know exactly what was making her say all of this, made me feel like I was on top of the world ? but I also had to keep myself grounded, because for some reason I was in denial, saying that maybe it was just somebody joking around with me, maybe her brother or something. One week later I was invited to her house for her own birthday party, and that was when things became interesting. She had plenty of people come and go, and there was a little dancing, which was interesting trying to show some of her friends how to exactly. Of course I know plenty more about that night than I did then, like her telling all of her friends that I was “the guy she likes”, and what not. It was certainly a good time, but again the good bye was the most memorable. “Yeah it’s late I know, so I’ll get out of here before your dad kills me. Although I believe there is something that you promised to tell me is there not?” I could see she was nervous/embarrassed by my putting her on the spot, but I had fun with it. “Yeah I know, but I ? can I tell you later?” “C’mon now Kari, you’ve put it off for awhile haven’t you?” After a few more moments of badgering like I do, she, reluctantly told me three letters. “I.Y.Q.” Now at first this seemed very unusual, and the look on my face probably was priceless. I tried saying something a few times, but she helped me out. “Say it together, and think about it.” It hit me then ? it was her little way of telling me she liked me, “I Like you”, but I guess everyone has a bit of child in them, not that it’s bad ? it was very cute. “Haha, well Kari, I think we know that the feeling is mutual.” We ended that night with a hug, and a promise to figure things out better later, and I don’t think I could’ve felt better in my life, well at that time anyways. Although things almost completely fell apart after that, with some unexpected developments ? things did come back full circle, even if it did take another almost two years to do so. SUMMER 2003 It’s funny how things happen sometimes indeed, as at one point it seemed that Kari and myself wouldn’t ever regain that feeling again completely. I can’t put my finger on exactly how we began speaking again, but I do know that I was very grateful for it. By the end of July, things had finally come to the point that I had been desperately wishing for, since we met. One night at my family’s apartment, we were alone in my room laying around and joking like we had been doing. Our time together lately had grown more and more to the point that we were seeing each other almost every day. Her phone rang and interrupted our conversation. “Hello” ? Yeah what’s up ? ? Oh I’m at my boyfriend’s house.” Those words shocked me as my head turned quickly to look at her, and she greeted me with a devilish grin, as she finished off her phone call and put her cell phone away. “Your boyfriend huh ?” “Hey mister, I can call you whatever I want to.” Of course who was I to argue with her ? It was something I’d longed to hear for almost 6 years. At this point, surprisingly we hadn’t even kissed yet, of course I am still getting there. Later that night we were laying, in the dark, holding each other. It was one of those moments you only see in a movie. We were laughing one moment then, caught each other in our stare, then the moment got tense, as we both wanted it, and both knew it would happen ? hell I think we were both surprised it lasted this long. I finally got the courage to lean in, and we had our first kiss. Her hands ran up and around my neck, and I grabbed onto to her waist. It was something that I will always remember, and always cherish ? but there was also something strange. We both pulled away a bit and looked over, only to see that my nephew Emilio had snuck in and was staring at us. We both began laughing, and knew that only our first kiss could end that way. Over the next few weeks we had a lot to deal with, with my choice to join the Army. Yes it seems the one thing we’ve gotten good at are the good-bye’s of our relationship. Right before I left though, we both knew there was something we had to say to each other, something that was a long time in the making. It took us holding each other, crying. I tilted her chin up, and softly kissed her lips, then wiped her tears before looking into her eyes. “I Love you.” We’ve spent the last few years apart, but very much in love. Now bringing back to today ? My heart still beating incredibly, as she walks down the aisle. Sure she only walked for a few moments, but our entire relationship flashed through my eyes as she did, the good and the bad. Her beauty shined through even under the veil, as I could see her bright smile. I couldn’t help but smile back at her. I reached out and took her hands into mine, and softly caressed them with my fingers. With all the feelings, all the emotions, the nerves, the worries, and the anxiety that has gone into this relationship, and waiting for this day, one thing I know for certain? I Love this woman. From the moment that we first met I knew that I wanted to be with her, and now after these next few moments we will be together forever. Through heartache, goodbyes, Alaska, Basic Training, and everything else, my feelings have stayed the same. Every kiss, every hug, every utter of the words “I Love you”, feel like it is the first time all over again. With each passing moment and each passing day my love for her grows stronger ? “I now pronounce you husband and wife ? you may kiss the bride.” I lifted her veil, and kissed her, a kiss that seemed to last a lifetime, a kiss that I wanted to last a lifetime. Tears were being shed, pictures were being taken, and emotions were running wild. I knew this day would come, and it couldn’t have come any sooner. I know that forever is a long time, but if there was one word I’d use to describe my love for Kari Jean Sipert it would be just that ? FOREVER?
