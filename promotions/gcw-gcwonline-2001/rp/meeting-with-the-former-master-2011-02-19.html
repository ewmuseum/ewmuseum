Meeting With The Former Master

19 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/19/11 Newfoundland is sitting at a brown table looking down at an empty plate between his arms. “I cannot believe that it took you this long to reconnect this link between us. The saying goes that once you surrender to the Dark Lord you can never escape his grip until you died.” KJ slowly raises his head and stares across the table, “I wanted to ask you one question since that day I left your shadow and stood in the light that gave me new beginning.” KJ’s ex-master, Shredder is sitting across from him. Shredder is dressed in a grey robe with the hood up covering his face and the only visible skin is his gnarled hands that are lying on the table. When he speaks it like thunder crashing In front of you and sends a pulse wave through your body. Shredder says with a hint of anger, “You’re the not one that is going be asking the question here, minion. Am I upset that you stole all those souls from me when you left? Yes. But, I am not here to reclaim all those souls that rightfully belong to me. This meeting is for another important topic that needs to be addressed.” As Shredder was talking, KJ was trying to move his body, but he wasn’t going anywhere. “How about this I answer all of you fucking question and you answer the one that has burn a hold in my soul for the-“ Suddenly Shredder booms into the laughter as KJ mention he has a soul. “You honestly think you have soul, minion? You may believe you have soul, but do computer have emotion? No, they have programs to tell them pain or happiness. That’s what you are, minion. You feel nothing unless you’re told to. Those souls you collected for me were for you to learn feelings from.” “So, you were glad that learn emotion?” KJ asks in confusion “I was until you failed your last mission.” Shredder pulls his nails back on table surface as continued, “You didn’t collect her soul to complete your training!” Inside KJ was trying the best he could to move so, he could lung across the table and punch Shredder over and over in the face. The soul he was talking about was his mother’s. “Why would want to my mother’s soul?” KJ asks finally “Because…” KJ sits up in his bed blinking and wiping his face as Kodiak says as he lowers a cup that was once filled with water, “I heard you mumble Shredder and I had to wake you up somehow. I tried loud noises and even slapping you to wake you. Nothing worked until I throw cold water onto your face.” As Kody mention that he slapped KJ to attempt to wake him, his face suddenly begins to hurt and he lets out a yell, “How hard did you hit me?” “A running start?” says Kodiak as he shook his left hand, “You have a hard head by the way. Are you sure that Shredder didn’t replace your jaw with a metal one?” For the first time after being rudely awaken he looked around the room and to his disgust it wasn’t his bedroom or any room he knows. Newfoundland says hoarsely, “Where am I and how did I get here? Better question, how did you locate me?” Gordert helps KJ up to his feet, “Well, I found you by the taint that Shredder used to remove you-“ His words were cut off as KJ begins to crushing his hand, “What taint?” Kodiak says as he slowly goes down to one knee, “Hand is getting crushed, KJ.” KJ lets go of Kodiak’s hand as he stares at Kodiak as he begins to explain. He says, “I am not quite sure what kind science that he used to remove the true taint, but he did say that if he completely removed it you would die. He replaces it with a lesser taint that allowed you to begin to learn. When he first found you after the death of you girlfriend, you were insane with blood lust.” KJ says as he looks around the crappy room that he fall asleep in, “He said that I really don’t know any feelings, but I was told to feel them. Is this true, Kodiak?” Kodiak shook his head, “You wouldn’t understand because even I don’t understand it. He says something just make you think bad thoughts and get flustered.” Newfoundland rips off a dirty sheet off a window to reveal that they are locate next to a lake. “Why I am here?” “Because I want you stay away from that stupid sword.” Kodiak says spitting at the ground, “Yes, I did technically kidnap you.” “Where is The Requite, Kodiak” KJ asks, “If you misplaced it anywhere you know that it could make more trouble than good?” Gordert waves his hand, “That sword is fine, but I am more afraid of your opponent for this show.” “Who am I going against at Worldwide 146?” KJ asks “You’re facing a woman and her name is Carmella Ignito.” Gordert says with a sigh, “Personally I hate facing girls because you don’t want to lose, but you also don’t want to beat a girl. So, its damn you do or damn you don’t problem.” “Doesn’t she think she is something grand?” KJ asks, “I think I heard complaining about the food backstage at the last show.” Kodiak shrugs his shoulders “Anyways,” KJ continues, “Carmella, just because your parents were okay wrestlers doesn’t mean you have the same in ring ability as they do. Do I enjoy going against a female? Hello no! It is waste of my time and the fans time to watch some new chick trying to prove that she can do anything in this male sport. Do I really care if females are wrestling? Nope. All I care is that I am making sure that I am doing my job and cleaning out the trash that is clanging to the entrance door of GCW.” “I do not know anything else besides what was told to me by my interns. Shoot, I don’t even know what you look like, Carmella. I don’t know if you ugly or pretty, skinny or fat, troll or goblin. But, I have a funny feeling if I truly want to see a picture of you I would have to go to a porn site. Those kinds of sites are disgusting and sick. Why would you want to look at naked women or men over the internet? Are you going trust those sites that those people on them actually gave their real age? Most of those people are from broken homes. So, do you enjoy looking at someone who is broken and is trying expressing their feeling by selling their bodies?” KJ slowly ran his hand through his hair, “Why would people even want to expose themselves for the world like that? I can never wrap my mind around that concept of that business.” Kodiak asks, “Are you done? If you are, we need to relocate because someone just pulled up to this cabin.” “Who is it?” KJ asks as he heads to back door “It looks like that cop lady,” Kodiak looks over at KJ, “Did you tell her to meet you here?” KJ shook his head in shock, “No, I don’t even know why she is here!”
