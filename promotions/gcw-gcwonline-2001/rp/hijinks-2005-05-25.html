Hijinks

25 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 25th, 2005 Trey came in early--late in my eyes--stumbling and reeking of enough good liquor that it smelled like the wretched shit. He took a look at me, tightly hunched over his hardcore title and snorting hard through a little straw, and he took a look at Tulz, who'd wrapped herself in nothing but a sheet to try and fight the heat, and then he fell face first into the leather chair. I finally realized that I wasn't in my room, so I took the chick and the dope and fled to the suite that I'd paid for. Tulz had never tried coke, obviously, and she was enjoying it more than she'd seemed to enjoy anything outside of the mainstay of pot. But all that, that's ancillary to any real point I'm going for. Hell, I haven't even quite hit the climax yet. 'What the fuck'd you do to my belt?' 'Which one?' 'The title one.' 'Ohh, that belt. Nothing.' I look it over and yeah, I did something to it. It's covered in corrosion, scratches, and stains like a Denny's counter. The varnish is faded and decayed in spots across the surface, leaving it with a shine that looks like it had at least tried to be inspiring. 'Nothin'? C'mon, man.' 'We've gotta get out of here, Trey. There's work to do.' I grab the belt to examine it and, having come to conclusion that nothing can be done, fling it to Tulz behind me. She catches it and I'm gone, rushing Trey into the hallway with me and making it into the elevator before he finally gets the chance to ask me what the hell it is I'm doing. 'I hadn't told you this yet because I wasn't sure if you could handle it, or if it even mattered, but it turns out that it does. Turns out, GCW's run by an international terrorist group, and you happen to be the next person up to do their dirty work. Now, don't look worried, they've got everything covered. It's not a big deal anyway, just some piss-ass in the Italian supreme court that's been causing trouble to GCW's network.' He stares in silence as we pass through the lobby and outside, and I keep hushing him until we're in the car--the 'tiny little rental' Porsche--and out of the parking lot. 'You serious, man?' I almost tell him no, but I see a game to be played in this. 'Completely. It's the pay-off for Blaq and Toxic taking the dives for you, and a little insurance against Octavian.' 'I earned my shit, and you know it.' I could tell him about the key words, but I think he knows. There's no break in personality now anyway--just a quick change in thought process that makes wholesale megadeath sound like a great idea. He's earned it through all the shit he's been put through to get there, maybe, if you don't consider that it's all my fault, really. So I pat him on the back and keep up with my new ruse. 'Whatever helps you sleep at night. That doesn't explain away the sniper rifle in the trunk or the reservations for Mr. Metzger at the hotel across from where the ambassador's staying.' 'Didn't you say he's on the Supreme Court?' 'No, you're fucking insane. Now tell me, are you in or do I have to call up Caldera and let him know that you're not interested in that world title?' 'This is some heavy shit, man. I'm gonna need a minute.' 'Take a few. We've got all day.' 'Good.' 'Okay, we've got a couple of hours. Really, there's just going to be time to eat--you haven't gotten lunch yet, have you?' 'Yeah, I ate back at the hotel.' 'Damn, I'm hungry. Open up the glove box.' He listens, so I don't have to start hitting him, and hands me the beaten and almost empty Newport pack. I pull a joint out and toss it to him. 'Smoke.' 'Tryin' to think about this, man.' 'Yeah, and eating will help that. Smoke so you'll want food.' 'Cool, cool.' I pull around town awhile, alternating between trying to find something tolerable on the radio and trying to find a place that looks appetizing to my long-dead brain. I try to scratch it but the skull gets in my way, sharp stubble stabbing my fingertips and leaving my brain to itch. True to my real goal, I stop at the first place I see after the joint goes out, and I hope it's worth whatever it'll cost to get a gigantic plate of cheese and pasta, or whatever the local breed eats. There's wine as soon as we sit down and menus I long to read, one beautiful day. Trey and I try to translate, deciding what means pizza, what means lasagna, and what those other things mean. Nothing. I turn and ask the woman sitting behind me if she knows English, but she just raises her eyes and says something in Italian. I turn back to the table, defeated. 'No luck?' 'Nada.' Maybe I shouldn't have said gratzi when I got the menus. Hell, maybe I should've asked then. 'I'll just ask the next waiter I see.' And yeah, this all passes. We eat expensive meals that we pretty much had to design from the ground up, and they're worth every penny. Trey's taking it as some last meal before the storm comes to test his soul. 'Why didn't Caldera ask me?' 'Caldera's just a front, a puppet, a toy. Just because he runs GCW doesn't mean he that he matters, y'know? He's out there so the charade can go on and the money can keep coming in. He does what the higher-ups tell him, and uses his spot to pick out the fittest bastards for the little jobs that come down from the higher-ups. I probably shouldn't be talking about this in public, though.' He nods. 'Why me? Ain't like Eva or Borealis ever paid their dues like this.' 'Who says? There are many things in heaven and earth, Trey.' 'Huh?' 'I don't know, it's some quote that I'm forgetting half of. Look, just because you don't know what's going on doesn't mean it's not happening. There's some shit that you've just gotta work around in life, and this is one of those shits. All you can do is decide how far you wanna go with this little wrestling thing.' 'I want Dread's title.' 'It can be done.' 'I want Knight's head on a spike.' 'Might be a little harder.' 'Private jet?' 'That's all part of the lifestyle you've got waiting for you on the other side. Just one little click away.' 'You been there?' 'Where?' 'The big life.' 'Yeah. It's nice, even if it's not quite Roman Polanksi big yet.' 'Who?' 'Nevermind. Just trust me, it's a good life and it's worth killing for--if you've got to. Sometimes, you've got to. A life for a life.' He slops a fork's stab worth of the greasy goop into his mouth and chews it, turning the thoughts over and over in his head before he swallows to begin digesting them. 'What'd he do?' 'He's the only ambassador to Cuba not under their thumb, a real nationalistic bastard. Communist scum.' 'I can't kill a person for that.' 'He raped and murdered hundreds under the regime of Mussolini.' 'So he's old?' 'As shit.' Why would Italy's ambassador to Cuba be in Italy? For a meeting with surviving Nazis that he's helping escape from the continent, if it comes up. A very rare chance indeed. 'Fuck, man.' 'What, you have an answer?' 'I think I'm in.' Now, on to phase two of my plan, wherein I figure out what phase two's gonna be.
