Stitched Up.

2 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/02/07 The last time we ventured into the world of Jimmy Thunder, he was caught up at a security check point at Cincinnati Airport where a security guard found two tubes of an unknown substance. Jimmy's luck in his personal life had previously been disastrous, but with the security guard holding the two tubes, Jimmy suddenly realised that his luck had taken a serious nose dive. It's not bad enough he's been caught with something he had no idea was in his possession. The worst part about all this is that standing right behind him in the security queue is Perry Stanton. Normally Jimmy wouldn't care about this guy because he seemed like an ordinary member of the public but what Jimmy failed to realise during these crucial seconds was that Perry Stanton was a young reporter who was on a journalistic internship with a small news company called CNN. Perry couldn't believe his luck. A second ago he was standing in a security check point queue, minding his own business when a potential sporting scandal just blew up, right in his face. Once Jimmy had been taken away by the security guards, Perry couldn't grab his cell phone out of his pocket fast enough. His hands started shaking as he searched through his cell phone's address book and called a senior reporter. After revealing to the senior reporter that he had been standing right behind Jimmy when he got busted with the unknown substances, the instructions Perry received was that under no circumstances was he to leave Cincinnati unless he had an exclusive scoop on his hands. Meanwhile, Jimmy has been placed in a private room and is seated by himself while the authorities discussed how they would tackle the situation. With nothing to occupy Jimmy except his thoughts, all he can think of that being caught with something he has no knowledge of was something he needed like a huge hole in his head. This is definitely not the way to prepare for his first shot at a GCW title. Thinking that he was taken away with very little attention brought his way. Knowing that, Jimmy could now calm down, sit back and think how these items could have come into his possession. All he knows is that before he packed his bag he emptied it out so he could re-pack it all neatly, making sure that all his belongings would fit snugly. That would be the only time Jimmy could think that something was slipped into his bag because he left his room unattended while he was in the rumble, other than that his bag was always with him. It only seemed like a brief moment before the security officers returned, followed by a middle aged male dressed in a suit with his black hair slicked back. The suited man, Joe Alvarez, showed Jimmy, his credentials as an FBI agent. He paces the the room for a few moments as he tries to work out how he'll begin questioning Jimmy, who on the other hand, is bracing himself for what he expects to be a torrid time. Knowing in his mind that he is innocent, Jimmy's main goal now is to try and clear his name in time to catch his flight to Boise. If he misses that flight there's no telling when the next flight to Boise would leave, and the last thing Jimmy wants to endure is another night at Cincinnati. "Mr Thunder, before we start proceedings here I would like to remind you of your right to remain quiet, but if you chose to answer my questions, you need to know that anything you say can and will be used in evidence against you. Do you understand this?" Joe asks confidently as Jimmy nods his head. "Mr Thunder." Joe says sternly. "The airport security staff have found two tubes containing a substance, in your belongings. Would you like to tell us what is inside the two tubes?" He asks. "I have no idea what's in those tubes or where they came from." Jimmy says as his voice starts to crack from his nervousness. "That's fine Mr Thunder, we're running tests on the items now and I'll find out in less than an hour what it is. I'm guessing what's in those tubes is what gives you those big muscles Mr Thunder, am I right?" Joe says sarcastically as Jimmy keeps his head slightly bowed but raising his eyes while his anger is starting to brew inside him. "I told you, I have no idea how they got into my bag." Jimmy retorts with slightly more conviction in his tone. "Did the test tubes grow legs Mr Thunder and walk into your bag on their own?" Joe says as he starts to raise his voice. Equally as loud, Jimmy replies with "How many times do I have to tell you that...." "You have no idea how they got into your bag." Joe interrupts with a sarcastic smile. "Mr Thunder, you're in a world of trouble here, may I suggest to you that you start making life easier for yourself and co-operate with us." He finishes with before leaving the room. Jimmy can't for the life of him understand where the tubes came from. The bag was emptied before he re-packed it and he was the only one to hold it since leaving the arena until the security guard took it from after screening it. The only way Jimmy could think that the tubes could get in is if someone tossed them into his bag from a distance, which is highly impossible. At the moment, Jimmy doesn't know how the tubes came to be in his possession, but the one thing he does know for certain that this is a stitch job and he's been stitched up royally. There is no-one in Jimmy's life right now that would go to these extremes to get even with him. He's only crossed paths with Sero Blake since joining GCW but he really doesn't have that deviousness in him to pull something like that off. Apart from him, Jimmy's been retired now for years and if anyone from his past has waited this long to get even with him, then this person has some serious issues with holding grudges. Just as Jimmy started to think there was no way out of this, then it all suddenly dawned on him. He had totally forgotten about something which really excited him. Jumping to his feet Jimmy opens the door and asks the security guard to call Joe Alverez back into the room. It suddenly became so clear to Jimmy, like the black clouds that hovered over him have now disappeared. After what seemed like an eternity, Joe Alverez finally arrives, confidently strutting into the room where he takes a seat opposite Jimmy and crosses his legs. "OK Mr Thunder, are you ready to co-operate?" He asks in a demeaning manner. Jimmy leans forward and looks Joe in the eyes and says, "I spent last night here in the airport. While I was here, I fell asleep with my bag between my feet, as I was waiting for my flight. When I woke up, my bag was under my chair and I thought I had moved it while I was sleeping but now when I think about it, someone could have planted the tubes in my bag then." "Not a bad story there Mr. Thunder. Actually not a bad one at all." Joe says while nodding and frowning his mouth. "But I'll accommodate your little story here, because this entire airport is fitted with the latest state of the art surveillance systems, which picks up a cockroach crawling up any walls you want. So if these were planted like you say they were, we'll find it." Joe says before leaning forward close to Jimmy's face and whispers, "If you're lying, so help me God, I'll throw the book at you." Joe backs up to the door and walks out, leaving Jimmy with nothing but his hopes and prayers that this is the break through that he was looking for. Meanwhile, standing outside like a kitten circling a bird, is the cadet journalist, Perry Stanton. With his flight well on truly on it's way to it's destination, Perry's efforts to get a ground breaking scoop on Jimmy Thunder's dilemma with the law has come to a sudden halt. A story of this magnitude would certainly send young Perry Stanton on his way to bigger and better prospects in his career. With his flight nearing it's boarding time, Jimmy is beginning to feel rather anxious about resolving all this, just so that he could not only clear his name but also board an aircraft that will take him home and away from this nightmare. He watches the seconds click over on the plain wall clock, like they were hours. Suddenly, the door knob opening catches Jimmy's attention like a Playboy Playmate running towards him totally naked. This would have to be the only time he'd rather see a male, namely Joe Alverez, rather than a Playboy Playmate. Joe takes a seat and places a folder in between himself and Jimmy. "We took a look at the video surveillance tapes and it appears Mr Thunder, you were right, we found a person putting something in your bag." Joe announced, making Jimmy smile as his head drops with relief. Joe opens the folder and pushes some screen shots under Jimmy's nose. "Have you seen this guy before?" Joe asks. All Jimmy could see was blurred image but he could distantly see a tattoo on the man's upper forearm. The tattoo is a tribal symbol which is mostly unknown to both Joe and Jimmy leaving both men clueless as to who the perpetrator is. Before allowing Jimmy to leave, Joe takes down his contact details, just in case he needs to question him on anything that should happen to arise. Once he gives Jimmy the green light to leave, Jimmy springs to his feet like a young school kid that hears the last home time bell before the start of summer vacation. Swiftly, Jimmy grabs his bags and heads out the door where Perry Stanton wastes no time in rushing up to him. "Hi there Jimmy, I'm Perry Stanton from the CNN news room." Perry says as he tries to introduce himself while walking at a fast pace next to Jimmy, who is trying to get to the boarding area in time to make his flight. Jimmy, mostly ignoring Perry, begins to run as he is more determined to get on this flight than to speak to anyone associated with the media. Perry on the other hand, undeterred by Jimmy's lack of interest in responding, runs alongside of Jimmy. "You were taken away after the security guards found something in your bag, would you like to make a comment Jimmy?" Perry asks while still running. Finally making it to the boarding area, Jimmy stops running and as he passes his boarding pass to the flight attendant, he turns to Perry and says "I have to board this flight, I really have no comment to make." Before Perry could ask Jimmy another question, Jimmy rushes off down towards the aircraft, leaving Perry somewhat dejected, especially after waiting over an hour for Jimmy to emerge and missing his own flight just to get Jimmy to tell him "no comment." The only thing Perry could do now was to call the senior reporter and let him know what had happened. Once Jimmy's flight landed in Boise, it doesn't take Jimmy too long to disembark from the aircraft and walking past the baggage carousel. Knowing that Emma is somewhere in the airport to pick him up, Jimmy reaches into his pocket and takes out his cell phone to turn it on. With in a minute of him turning the phone on, it's starts ringing. Expecting the call to come from Emma, Jimmy is caught off guard when the cell phone display shows that Steven Caldera is trying to get in contact with him. Wondering why Steven is trying to call him, Jimmy quickly answers. "Hello Steven." Jimmy says curiously. "Hey there Jimmy, how you doin there big man?" Steven asks with a chirpy tone in his voice. "I've had better mornings." Replied Jimmy, who is really starting to wonder why Steven called. "So I hear." Steven quickly says. "Now Jimmy, I heard something about you through the grape vine that rang my alarm bells." Steven said as Jimmy remains quiet. "I need you to be totally honest with me hear Jimmy, because if I find out you've been lying to me, your ass is out on the streets, do you understand me?" Steven says as his tone has turned from chirpy to serious in a matter of seconds. "What did you hear?" Jimmy asks knowing full well that Steven Caldera had mysteriously found out about Jimmy's dilemma in Cincinnati. With a stern voice, Steven asks, "Were you detained at Cincinnati Airport this morning?" To which Jimmy replied, "Yes, I was." Wanting to know exactly what had happened, Steven continued with "Would you care to explain to me what happened, Jimmy?" Jimmy takes a deep breath before saying, "I fell asleep at the airport last night and I went through a boarding area terminal, the security guards found two tubes which looked like steroids. They questioned me for nearly an hour and when I explained to them that I had fallen asleep there, they checked their security tapes and saw that someone had planted the test tubes in my bag." "Is that it?" Steven asks surprisingly. "They got me to identify who tried to set me up but we couldn't make out who it was. Shortly after that they said I was free to go." Jimmy finished with. "And that's all?" Steven asked just to confirm what he had heard from Jimmy. "That's it mate, I'm totally innocent." Announced Jimmy. "OK then, I believe you, but you have to understand that in a business like ours, any scandals, especially from my employees, could potentially bring this entire company down and I've worked too hard and too long to allow anyone to bring down my empire. Are we clear on this, Jimmy?" Said Steven. "I totally understand, mate." Jimmy said while walking outside before asking, "What do we do from here now?" "I'll have a think about it, someone will get in contact with you, but in the mean time, keep your nose clean, I've got so much on my plate right now and something like this was the last thing I needed." Steven said as before the two ended the call. The scene fades....
