Date: 2.20.06 “Out of the way, loser.” Ehren mutters as he pushes his strong hand against the back of a late-teen boy who stands in his way. Ehren carries his large frame down the semi crowded walkway of a shopping mall. To his right is a ginormous college athletics wear store named “Steve and Barry’s”. To his left is a brand new version of the ever popular “Sam Goody” music store. Moore walks cockily forward with a smug grin on his face. His attire is his typical dark tan Khaki cargo pants that are a little baggy. Accompanying his pants is a semi dressy brown shirt with yellow pin stripes stretching horizontally across his chest. Though walking with a very arrogant aura about him, nearly every single girl that he passes turns to check him out. Ehren notices this, allowing his ego to grow even bigger. He continues his strut down the walkway, approaching “Aeropastle.” He smiles as he turns and walks into the store. The sounds of Fallout Boy echo throughout the clothing store. Ehren is greeted by the guy and girl standing at the first two tables folding clothes. “How’s it going?” The guy asks. Ehren shoots a look towards the teenager that could pierce right through him. Moore just continues walking past the young man to the shirts hanging on a wall-rack. He looks through them, one by one, searching for his size. After a few, he takes one down and holds it up to him, sizing it up. Obviously it’s not good enough, because he tosses it onto the rack of jeans next to him. Nobody catches this. Ehren continues to the next rack to the left and files through looking for his size again. He jerks one off of the rack and looks at it. Again unsatisfied, he tosses it to the same table. The hanger makes a small clank this time, causing the teenager folding shirts at the front of the store. He rolls his eyes and walks over, disgusted at Ehren’s actions. Over the teenager’s shoulder, a man in a traditional tan trench coat walks into the store. His face is unable to be seen, covered by the up-righted collar of the trench coat, a pair of dark sunglasses and a large brimmed hat. He walks in and begins sorting through the shirts right by the entrance. Ehren doesn’t pay much attention as he steps forward towards the cargo pants and begins inspecting them. He unfolds a pair halfway and nods in approval, draping them over his shoulder. Seeing enough, Ehren steps up to the cash register, cutting off a mother and her teen daughter. Ehren doesn’t think once, let alone twice about not letting the two ladies proceed ahead of him. He just tosses the pants down onto the counter and smiles at the blonde behind the counter. “Hey baby. How’s it going?” He says with a “suggestive” tone. She fakes a smile and scans the pants, avoiding eye contact at almost all costs. After a few seconds of no response, Ehren sighs and leans closer. “I asked you how it wa?” Moore is cut off as the man in the trench coat walks past, bumping into Ehren’s extended bum. The stranger holds his hand up as if to apologize and take fault, but that’s not good enough for Ehren. “Stupid son of a bitch. Watch where you’re walking. I ought to kick the shit out of you.” Ehren shakes his head as the man walks a few feet past the counter and then turns to walk out. He swiftly walks past Ehren and out the door. Moore turns back to the young lady who waits for Ehren’s payment. He takes out a wad of cash from his pocket and takes two twenty dollar bills from the top, handing them to the girl. “So” Are you going to answer me?” “I was doing great. Until you walked up here.” The blonde says with a smile. Ehren laughs, thinking it’s her sarcastic, flirtatious ways. She stops smiling suddenly, and widens her eyes to assure him she’s not kidding before handing him his change and a bag with the pants in it. “I’m serious. You’re ignorant. Get away from me.” “Yeah. Whatever. You couldn’t handle me anyway, woman.” Moore says as he turns; a little upset by being put down by a twenty year old. He walks briskly towards the door. His bag hits a stack of t-shirts on a table near the door, causing half of the pile to fall to the ground. “Oops.” He says with a laugh. Ehren steps back out into the walkway of the mall and looks around at the surrounding stores. He spots a Gloria Jean’s coffee stand and smiles, beginning forward progress to it. He pushes past a few slower moving people, not trying to step around them, simply bumping past them. He approaches the stand where an older redhead stands conversing with the teenaged brunette; both clad in black aprons. “Can I help you, sir?” The young girl asks as she steps up to the counter. He smiles a cocky smile as he drops his bag to the ground and looks to the menu. He thinks about what he wants as he reaches into his pocket and pulls his big wad of cash out again. “A large White Caramel Oreo and how about your number, baby.” He says as he fumbles through the cash and grabs a ten dollar bill. He glances up and spots the disgusted look on the young girl’s face. Ehren flashes back a look of his own as the girl turns to prepare his order. “I’m only nineteen. And what are you” Like, twenty-seven?” She says as she begins to make the drink. She scoops some crushed up Oreo cookies into a plastic cup before pouring some kind of brown liquid into the cup. Ehren doesn’t know what it is and really doesn’t care; as long as he gets his Gloria Jeans. “Jesus. Chill out, girl. I just want to give you a call.” He says, not quite taking the hint. This is a girl’s worst nightmare. Not only an arrogant guy, but a persistent arrogant guy. Ehren still looks at her, smiling. The older co-worker shoots him a disgusted look as he looks the younger girl up and down. He stops at her butt and eyeballs it until she turns around and comes towards him, setting his drink onto the counter. She takes the ten from his extended hand and punches a few buttons on the cash register, popping it open. She counts out his change and extends her hand to give it to Ehren. She sets the bills into his hand and quickly drops the change. It falls right off his hand and drops to the counter and floor below. “Oops. I’m so sorry.” She says facetiously with a giggle. Ehren looks around to see if anyone has spotted his humility before bending over to pick up the change. All he spots is a few older couples slowly walking by. Who he doesn’t see is the man in the trench coat from Aeropastle sitting at the small caf?-like tables to his right. He snatches the change up and drops it into his pocket, coming back to an erect position with his bag in hand. He grabs his drink from the counter and walks to his right. “Bitch.” Ehren mutters as he gets a few steps away from the counter. Nobody turns down Ehren Moore. He’s officially pissed off now. Moore continues walking forward, spotting American Eagle and making his way there. Just two stores before his destination is Radio Shack. The first window he passes is filled with stacked TV sets, just like the old street shops. On the screen is some wrestling action, obviously hyping a promotion of some sort. Just as Ehren passes, the GCW logo jumps onto the screen and Ehren nods in approval. He slows his pace down for a second to see the logo and then begins to walk normally again. Just as he does, the TV sets buzz and suddenly change to a picture of the mean look of GCW United States Champion Sam Wolack. The picture soon becomes filled with motion; Wolack mouth moving but no sound is audible. Ehren glances, but doesn’t catch who it is. Just then, the disguised man walks up behind Moore and nods at the TV screens. Moore takes a double take and realizes who sits on the screens in front of him, causing him to stop dead in his tracks and come back to the window. “Oh lookie here. Big bad Sam Wolack is on TV.” Ehren says mockingly. “That little son of a bitch. I cannot wait to get my hands on him man to man. I still haven’t forgiven that little fuckshit for costing me my TV Title. The next time I see him, I’m going kill that sumbitch.” As Ehren talks smack to the defenseless video playing on the TV screen, the man in the trench coat casually struts up behind him and stands merely six or seven inches from Ehren. Moore laughs at his comments as he brings his drink to his mouth and sucks some of it through his straw. He continues to suck on the straw for a few seconds, still talking smack. “Yeah. You tell that TV screen, Ehren.” The man says sternly. Ehren’s face turns to that of sheer terror as he freezes any action he was performing. The liquid slowly slides back down the straw and into the cup. “I’m sure you could kick its ass.” This last comment is enough to render Ehren paralyzed with fear. His cup drops from his hand, falling towards the floor and splattering in all directions upon impact. Ehren slowly turns around to see the man in the trench coat standing almost nose to nose with him. Ehren doesn’t speak, only looks forward as the man takes his hat off and drops it to his right side. He swiftly takes the dark sunglasses from his face as well and drops them to the side as well. He unbuttons his coat next and pushes it off of his arms, allowing it to drop to the floor behind him. Ehren stumbles back a few steps, right into his beloved Gloria Jeans as he sees the man now standing in front of him. “Sam”..Wo-Wolack.” He struggles out. Sam smiles and takes a step forward, sending Ehren one more step back. Wolack advances another step and Ehren retreats another. Sam smiles again and advances one final step. Ehren tries to take another step back, but realizes he is butted up against the wall. “You still want to kick my ass” You were being pretty tough to that Joe Schmoe over in Aeropastle.” Sam asks as he leans closer to Ehren. Ehren takes a deep breathe and opens his mouth to talk, but only air escapes. “And that TV screen. Man. You showed it. It might not get HBO tonight, you scared it so bad.” Moore finally gets a hold of his senses and straightens up like a man. He gulps down the saliva that has built in his mouth and changes his terrified look to one of toughness. Wolack chuckles at this action. “I could take you out right here, Ehren. One punch is all I’d need.” “Yeah” Then do it.” Nothing. Neither man moves. They remain face to face. A few seconds pass and Wolack makes a sudden little thrust forward. Ehren falls for it and scrambles for dear life. His feet go out from under him, slipping on the wet tile from his coffee. After a few seconds of cartoon-like slipping, he falls straight onto his ass, landing in the center of the mess on the floor. Wolack smiles and backs off, retrieving his things from the floor and turning back to Ehren. “See you on WorldWide.”
