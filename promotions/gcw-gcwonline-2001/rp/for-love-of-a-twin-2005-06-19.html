For love of a Twin

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/19/2005 ' Momma it has been two days!' Eva opened her mouth to talk into the receiver again only to clamp it shut as her words were cut off by a thick German accent on the other end. ' But-!' A derisive sigh released from her lungs as she slumped on the couch looking more like a child than the grown adult she truly was. 'Da.......da. Okay. ' Pursing her lips with a bit of irritation she tossed the phone on the other end of the couch and stared it at is if it were the bane of God. A hefty sigh left her nostrils once more as she curled up tightly and rested her chin on her knees in ponderment. Erik had not spoken to her in two days. It felt as if he would never speak to her again. Eva had tried to get him to speak to her on several occasions, but it just seemed to be of no use. Muttering in German she grimaced and shot off the couch to pace the floors in circular motion while gnawing on the side of one her perfectly manicured nails. She loved her brother, but how could she tell him whom she had been with at the time he was been beaten and crucified? The tears came again as she heard a small groan of pain coming from the hallway. Whirling the blond goddess would spy her brother leaning with one hand perched on the wall to keep him upright as he tried to make his way towards the den. A soft cry of concern would fall from her lips as she hurried to his side intending to help him. 'Erik, you should not be moving around so much. Your ribs are bruised! You heard what the Doc-' As she tried to assist he would merely push a hand against her with a small growl. The force at which he had pushed her actually had her tumble back into the wall on the opposite end. ' I don't need ~your~ help. You helped enough already.' She stared at him stunned, that mouth working but nothing coming out as she shook her head in helplessness and finally just released a loud choked sob and rushed past him running to her room. ~ BAM! ~ Erik sighed at the sound of the slamming door, feeling badly now for snapping at her when all she wanted to do was help. He could not help himself though. Slowly his head would turn to peer at the still vibrating door with a creased brow. He was angry with his sister for not telling him where she had been, but more than that...he was angry with himself. He should have covered his tracks better and none of this would be happening. DAMN Wrestling! DAMN IT ALL TO HELL! If it had not been for wrestling they may never have found the pair, but as always, he had done as his little sister wished, if only to keep her happy. It was all he ever did when it came to Eva. The night of that beating was still fresh in his mind.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ A knock sounded on the door. Erik came out of the back room and pulled it open only to find himself staring at a gun in his face as a thick Russian accent grated across his ears. ' You know Mr. VanLorne, when one steals from me they are better off being dead....' ' Petre....' ' I see you remember my name.' The gun pressed against his forward forcing him to back up a few steps as the three men entered his locker room. Hands rose in surrender. What else could he have done? ' What can do for you Petre?' ' Where are my diamonds?' ' They were not your--' ~ CRACK! ~ The pistol came hurtling across his cheek leaving a nasty cut in its wake. Erik grunted as he collapsed to one knee and touched his cheek to look at the blood with a sardonic smile. The Russian sighed as he removed his gloves and looked at Erik with a malicious gleam in his eye. He meant business and Erik knew it. ' I will ask you one more time Mr. VanLorne...Where are ~my~ diamonds?' Erik canted his head up to peer at the man with a sidelong glance of semi defiance and amusement. ' How is your brother?' ~ WHACK! ~ Again he felt the sting of a pistol whip from one of the Russian's goons. Erik was seeing stars for moment. He shook his head to clear it. ' My brother is dead...as you well know. Now tell me where the diamonds are or I shall be forced to go to drastic measures to get the information out of you...' Erik looked up stoically as the shadows crowded around him. Several pairs of hands reached for him hauling the rather large man to his feet.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Shaking his head, Erik forced the memory to skip the brutality that had followed. Try as he might, though, he could not shake the final words of the Russian mafia boss before they left him suspended in a crucifixion. ' You have two weeks to find them and return them to me...or my men will deal with your sister next....' The mere memory of that threat had his fist clenching in rage. ' Shiesse....' The curse was muttered beneath his breath as he pushed off the wall and turned once again to stare at his sister's door. He would not tell her of this. He did not want her to live in the same fear he felt even now, but...he also could not stand to see her upset any longer despite his own anger at her keeping a secret. Roughly running fingers through his white blond hair he sighed and slowly made way to her door. There was no knock. Eva blinked and lifted her head from her pillow to peer at her entering twin with red puffy eyes. Glowering at him she rolled away sharply to present him with her back. ' Go away Erik. ' ' No.' ' I have had enough of your abuse ...get out.' ' I came to say I am sorry Eva.' All motion ceased from the pale beauty. Finally she rolled over to look at him with suspicion. ' Really?' ' Yes...really.' Eva scrambled off her bed then and rushed at him, slender arms wrapping around his waist as she collided with him momentarily forgetting about his ribs in her elation. ' EVA!' Shocked back to reality she released him quickly and stepped back stuttering an apology. ' Oh...Erik..I...I am sorry..I forgot.' He sighed lightly and pulled her to him again kissing the crown of her head as she wrapped her arms around loosely this time as not to harm his injuries. ' I am sorry I have been so harsh Eva. I should not have yelled at you for trying to help me.' ' It is okay Erik. ' So they stood there for long moments in a heavy silence, each thinking the same thing on the opposite spectrum. Then finally they both asked the same question at the same time. ' Who was it?' ' Who was it?' Immediately Eva stiffened, as did Erik as they dropped the sibling embrace and stared at each other hard. Neither would answer the other. Erik frowned and stepped to the side walking past her to stare out her window. She just sighed and threw up her hands in surrender before moving to her bed and flopping down that face burying in pillow. ' It was Lestatt wasn't it?' Eva's head shot up to stare at him aghast. ' I beg your pardon?' Erik turned to stare at her with a hardened and angry gaze. ' It was wasn't it?' Her jaw clenched with outrage as she stared daggers at her brother. ' NO! It was not him.' ' I don’t believe you.' They were back at square one once again. Eva growled as she crawled off her bed and stormed towards her door and held it open wide. ' Get out....' ' Tell me...' ' GET OUT ERIK!' Erik clamped his mouth shut, those lips thinning in his anger. Growling he moved from his perch and slowly marched to stand next to her at the door. Icy blues met with icy blues in a suspended moment of high tension. ' I will find out sooner or later Eva...you know this...' ' Just...go Erik.' He nodded silently as he walked out the door. Eva slammed it shut. It looked as if their stubborn German pride would stall the much-needed reconciliation. Back to square one indeed...
