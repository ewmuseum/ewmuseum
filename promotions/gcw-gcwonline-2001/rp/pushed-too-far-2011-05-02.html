Pushed Too Far?

2 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/11 Sitting on his back porch during a clear and comfortable afternoon, with the sun setting over the Owyhee Mountain ranges, Jimmy Thunder’s thoughts have him further away than Owyhee Mountains. The talk he had with Tessa Windsor at World Wide has opened his eyes to something he never thought about before. For several days, the win over Idol has taken a back seat to the train of thought planted into his head by what Tessa said to him. Emma quietly walks up behind her seated husband and puts her hand on his shoulder before asking, "Are you here with us or are you a million miles away?" "I’m sorry, I was deep in thought." Replied Jimmy as he smiles before putting his hand on her hand. "What’s on your mind babe?" Emma curiously asked. "I was having a talk with Tessa Windsor and she said something to me that rang true, it hit me like Mack truck when I looked at things from her point of view." Jimmy said. "What did she say?" Emma asked. "I opened the show last week and she reminded me of how long I had been there and that I deserved to be treated better than to be put on as the opening match of the show." Said Jimmy. Emma smiles before saying, "They just wanted to start the show off with the best. "It’s not that. I've never looked at things from the angle that Tessa pointed out to me. I’ve paid my dues. I’ve spilled blood, sweat and tears for this company way back when most of this roster was in high school. Hell, I bet if you ask, some of them have got a Jimmy Thunder autograph somewhere. I’m not saying I should be main eventing or holding any titles, all I’m saying is that I deserve more than being an opener? Ranted Jimmy. "Baby, maybe you should talk to Garbage Bag Johnny about this?" Emma suggested. "Talk to Garbage Bag Johnny? There’s another problem I have. The guy hasn’t spoken two words to me since I returned. There’s no way in hell I’ll be asking him for anything." Jimmy vehemently replied. "There’s no use getting angry about it all now. You’re home with us, you really need to switch off from all of that." Said Emma. "How do I switch off now that I know I’m being treated like I’m some kind of fucking idiot! I’ve forgotten more about this business than what Garbage Bag Johnny knows about it." Jimmy claims as his anger starts to get the better of him. A shocked Emma pauses for a moment before saying, "You’ve never been angry about anything that has to do with GCW. Does it really bother you that much?" "Now when I think about it, yes, yes it does!" Replied Jimmy. Emma stands behind Jimmy and lowers herself down, wraps her arms around Jimmy’s chest and says, "Feel like having some fun?" "What kind of fun?" Jimmy asks. "How about tomorrow, you take Chloe and me to the carnival that’s in Murphy. We’ll play some games, go on some rides and just have some good old fashioned fun." Asked Emma. Instantly, a smile comes on Jimmy’s face before he says, "As long as I get my own cotton candy." Emma starts laughing as she says "You got a deal." Jimmy stands to his feet and is about to kiss Emma when he hears a car driving up the long, dry, dusty driveway. He looks around the side of his house and can see Sheriff Crandall in his police SUV. They both walk down the stairs to meet Sheriff Crandall. "Hi Jimmy, Emma, how are you both?" Sheriff Crandall asked. Jimmy nods as he says, "We’re ok." He then asks "What brings you out here Sheriff?" "I have to release Deas in two days." Said the Sheriff. "Why?" Asked Emma. "He only committed a misdemeanor Emma, I can only hold him for a period of time, legally, without a trial." The Sheriff explained. "When do you have to let him out?" Jimmy asked. "In less than forty eight hours." Replied Sheriff Crandall. "I thought that I’d give you guys the courtesy and let you know, personally, what’s happening." He added. "We appreciate that, Sheriff." Said Jimmy. "What are our options now?" He asked. Sheriff Crandall pauses, looks to the ground then looks at Jimmy and says, "You or I really can’t do anything Jimmy. He hasn’t committed any major offenses that I know of. Legally he’s free to walk away. When he gets released, I’ll escort him to the state lines and make sure he moves on. That’s about as much as I can do." Explained the Sheriff. "Will you let me know when he’s gone?" Jimmy asked. "You bet I will." Replied Sheriff Crandall. "I appreciate it Sheriff and everything you’ve done for us." Said Jimmy. "It’s my pleasure." Sheriff Crandall said as he tips his hat to Jimmy. He then turns to Emma, tips his hat and says, "Enjoy the rest of your evening guys." "You too Sheriff." Said Emma. Sheriff Crandall climbs back into his SUV and waves before he drives off. Emma gives Jimmy a tight hug and says, "Why cant those creeps just leave us alone?" "They’re miserable in their own lives and want us to be at their level, I guess." Replied Jimmy. "Don’t worry about it anymore. It’ll all be over soon." He said. The next day, after eating a nicely prepared lunch that Emma took the time to make, they headed off to the carnival as planned. Emma, Jimmy and Chloe take the five minute drive into Murphy to enjoy the entertainment that a carnival provides. They spend several hours getting on rides, playing games and generally enjoying each others company. They walk over to a tent where you throw a baseball at three ten pin bowling pins to win a prize. As Emma watches Jimmy throw the baseballs, Chloe notices a little monkey running around behind the tent and decides to run after it. Unbeknownst to her parents, Chloe has managed to rush off after the monkey and not realize that she has lost them. Meanwhile, Jimmy has finished throwing his baseballs at the bottles and claims his prize that he gives to Emma. The two turn around and begin to walk away from the tent when they realize that Chloe is not near them. "Where’s Chloe?" Asked Emma. Jimmy’s smile disappears instantly and looks around while saying, "I don’t know I thought she was with you." The two search through the crowd frantically as their hearts start racing. "Where the hell is she, Jimmy?" Emma asked frantically. Jimmy keeps quiet as he looks around as he begins to panic now. It’s bad enough that his little girl is missing but the fact that he’s worried that Chloe might have been kidnapped by Ian Cross, Anthony Taylor and Christopher Stryfe is just adding fuel to the fire. "I’m going to try and find a security guard or the police." Emma said still panicking. Meanwhile Jimmy keeps an eye out hoping he’ll spot Chloe in the crowd somewhere. He scours the crowd and eliminates the large amount of 5 year old girls that he sees one by one. The longer he stands in one spot without success the longer he knows deep down that she’s been taken. He turns his attention to behind the tents and finds her Minnie Mouse doll. He suddenly feels sick to his stomach as he knows deep down that she has been taken. There was no way in the world Chloe would leave that doll behind for anything. "Have you seen a small girl with brown curly hair in a pink dress?" A frenzied Jimmy asks a carnival employee walking a horse. The employee just shakes his head and continues along the path he set out on. Jimmy starts moving faster, weaving in and out between the ropes holding up the tents, all in hope of finding Chloe somewhere. He manages to find himself out in the middle of the carnival amongst all the crowd and still no sight of Chloe. At that moment Emma finds Jimmy and rushes up to him with a Sheriff’s Deputy that she found. "You still haven’t found her?" Emma asks in hope. Jimmy raises his arm and shows Emma the Minnie Mouse doll that he just found. Emma falls into Jimmy’s arms and starts crying uncontrollably. "Hi Jimmy, what’s going on here?" The confused policeman asks. "Our daughter, she’s been taken." Jimmy said with a blank stare on his face while he tries to hold a distraught Emma up. The deputy puts his communication device to his hand and says, "OC 15" "Go ahead OC15" came the response. "I have a possible kidnapping at the Owyhee County Fair, requesting immediate assistance." Said the Deputy. Jimmy manages to find a chair and places Emma on it as she continues to cry over her daughter’s disappearance. "They took her." Said Jimmy to the Deputy. "Who took her?" Asked the deputy. "Sheriff Crandall knows." Responded Jimmy while still sporting a distant look on his face. The Deputy reaches for his cell and calls Sheriff Crandall. He explains to his boss that he’s with Jimmy and that his daughter has possibly been kidnapped. Sheriff Crandall tells the deputy he’s on his way. While waiting for the Sheriff and the assistance he just called in, the Deputy tries to get a description of Chloe from a stunned father and an inconsolable mother. With all the hysteria happening around him, all Jimmy could think about is seeing his daughter’s beautiful smile and hearing her cute little laugh again. He had taken everything that is precious about her for granted, and now, she’s been taken from him. As her father, Jimmy made himself responsible for protecting the apple of his eye. His failure to perform the task he expected himself to do has almost made him physically ill. A female Deputy takes over with comforting Emma while Jimmy stands, alone, helpless and feeling broken, worse than any GCW match he has endured in the past. Suddenly, breaking his thoughts, Jimmy feels a hand on his shoulder. He turns around and sees Sheriff Crandall standing before him. "Don’t worry Jimmy, we’ll get her back." Said Sheriff Crandall. "They took her, I know it." Jimmy replied. "Come on Jimmy, you have to stay positive now." Explained Sheriff Crandall. "Stay positive? My baby girl has been taken by idiots with no sense and you’re asking me to stay positive like it’s as easy as drinking water." Said Jimmy. Sheriff Crandall looks Jimmy in the eye and says, "I’ll bring her back to you Jimmy, I give you my word." Jimmy looks down as Sheriff Crandall walks away to orchestrate his army of deputies, meanwhile, Jimmy is left to now pray for his daughter’s safe return. Feeling helpless and destitute, the only thing Jimmy can do now is walk away from where he is situated and begin his own search. He begins his search around the parameter of the carnival and only sees a large amount of police presence trying to find his little girl. Not having much luck close by the carnival, Jimmy decides to expand his search and walks in the opposite direction to where the police are concentrating their search. Following his hunches, Jimmy heads further and further away from the carnival, in the direction of a large hill that plateau’s at it’s peak. He walks the steep incline till he reaches the hill’s summit. Using this great vantage point to scour the area, all Jimmy can see is a large amount of police vehicles with their blue and red lights flashing, a road block from both sides of the carnival, as well as desert and sage brushes as far as the eye can see. With his hand shielding the sun from his eyes, Jimmy searches every square inch of the vast, open, desert in hope of finding something. As his head turns slowly from left to right, Jimmy notices a pink image. Stunned at what he had seen, the possibilities gets his heart racing as he takes a breath before sliding down the side of the hill at pace. He races through the sage bushes at a rapid speed and as he gets closer, the image becomes more evident. Once he makes visual contact and verifies that it is indeed Chloe, he sprints as fast as his old, weary legs can take him. "Chloe!" Jimmy yells to get her attention. Chloe just sits there and cries as she sees her father racing towards her like his life depended on it. He slides to where she is sitting and holds her close to him as her tears flow down her cheeks. "I’m sorry daddy, I wanted to play with the monkey then I lost my Minnie Mouse and when I tried to look for her I got lost." Chloe said while crying at the same time. "It’s alright baby, daddy’s here now." Replied Jimmy. "Are you ok?" He asks. "I lost my Minnie doll" She replied while crying even louder now. "It’s alright baby, mummy has her." Explained Jimmy as he picks her up. He carries her for the long journey back to the carnival, feeling re-born after being the one who found his daughter. Inside, the emotional roller coaster Jimmy rode on was enough to make him cry but the pride he has gained in being the one who rescued his little girl far outweighs any other emotion he could be feeling. A Deputy sees Jimmy carrying his daughter and rushes up to them. He takes his coat off and covers Chloe while her dad walks with long strides towards where Emma is seated. He maneuvers around some tents until he appears. Seeing Jimmy holding Chloe in his arms, Emma jumps to her feet and runs towards them. She wraps her arms around Jimmy and her daughter and cries with relief that her daughter is safely back. Once Jimmy gets his wife and daughter safely home, he sits on his back porch and ponders on what just happened this afternoon. Even though it’s now evident that Cross, Taylor and Stryfe had nothing to do with Chloe’s disappearance, the constant worry in the back of his head on when they will strike is frustrating the hell out of him. The unknown on what is around the corner is really playing on Jimmy’s mind. The only way he knows how to squash all that is to face Deas before he is released. Without letting Emma know where he is going, Jimmy gets in his SUV and takes the short trip to Owyhee County Sheriff’s Department and walks into the front foyer where he is met by Sheriff Crandall who happened to be walking by. "Excuse me Sheriff, have you got a moment?" Jimmy asked. "Noticing it is Jimmy trying to get his attention, Sheriff Crandall walks over to Jimmy and shakes his hand before saying, "Hi Jimmy, how’s the little one?" "She’s fast asleep, she’s had a huge day." Replied Jimmy. "What brings you here?" A curious Sheriff Crandall asks. "I was hoping I could see Deas before you release him?" Jimmy asked. "Visiting hours finished a long time ago." Said Sheriff Crandall. "I know but I really need to get some things off my chest." Explained Jimmy. The Sheriff pauses for a moment while he thinks. "Do you really think this a good idea, Jimmy?" He asks. "I do. I need to know what he’s thinking and hopefully find out what their problem is." Replied Jimmy. "I doubt you’ll get much out of him but it’s worth a shot. You got five minutes with him, Jimmy." Said the Sheriff. "Follow me." The two men walk down to the visiting area where the Sheriff tells his Deputy to get Deas. After a short wait, Jimmy is lead into a room where he sees Bryan Deas already seated. Jimmy walks over to where Deas is seated and takes a seat opposite his old work colleague. Jimmy is met with a disturbing smile on Deas’ face and a thick clear panel to separate them from each other. Jimmy picks up the handset on the clear panel, as does Deas. "The great Jimmy Thunder comes and visits me in jail." Deas says with a thick southern drawl and a smile on his face. Getting straight to the point, Jimmy asks, "Why did you come here for Deas?" Deas keeps smiling and says, "What, no how have you been the last ten years or so?" "Deas I’m not in the mood to play games with you, I want to know why you came here?" Jimmy asked while staring Deas in the eye "If you really must know, I came hunting here." Explained Deas. "Don’t give me that crap Deas, you could have gone hunting in a million different other places." Said Jimmy. "Cut the crap and just tell me." He adds. "I don’t know what you’re talking about Jimmy?" Said a smiling Deas. "Listen, I know you’ve got something planned with Cross, Taylor and Stryfe. If you guys want a piece of me, I’ll fight you guys any time, any place. Name it and I’ll be there." Jimmy said. "HA HA HA!!!! Jimmy Thunder, don’t flatter yourself, we don’t want to fight you." Replied Deas. "I promise you, if you guys try and cheap shot me, it’ll be the last thing you guys do." Declared Jimmy. "Don’t worry, we’re not going to cheap shot you either." Said Deas while still smiling. Jimmy realizes that he isn’t getting anywhere with Deas. He nods his head but knows that Deas is lying through his teeth. He knows that Cross, Taylor, Stryfe and Deas are going to jump him but the big question is when. He slowly moves the handset from his ear while staring at Deas who doesn’t lose his smile. "Jimmy." Jimmy hears Deas say which makes him bring the handset back to his ear. "I just wanted to say that you have a very beautiful family." Jimmy’s eyes open widely as Deas starts laughing now. "You leave my family alone. Do you hear me Deas?" Said Jimmy. He starts smashing the handset on the clear screen and continues to say, "LEAVE MY FAMILY ALONE YOU FUCKING PIECE OF SHIT!!! He drops the handset now and starts bashing clenched hands on the thick screen while Deas enjoys Jimmy’s wild, angry outburst. Three Deputies come rushing into the room and try and pull Jimmy back. "LEAVE MY FAMILY ALONE DEAS OR I’LL FUCKING KILL YOU!!!!" Jimmy screams out. "YOU HEAR ME DEAS" I’LL KILL ALL OF YOU!!!" Jimmy continues to scream out as the Deputies try and wrestle a wild and crazed Jimmy Thunder. The Deputies manage to push Thunder out of the visiting room as the Sheriff walks into Deas’ side of the screen and asks, "What the hell happened here?" The Sheriff asks. "I don’t know Sheriff, Thunder went nuts when I thanked him for coming to see we." Deas explained. Not believing a word, Sheriff calls for a Deputy to escort him back to his cell. Sheriff Crandall can still hear Jimmy going wild in the visiting waiting are. Sheriff Crandall stands with his hands on his hips and is now wondering if he’s about to have all out war breaking out in his county. To be continued…….
