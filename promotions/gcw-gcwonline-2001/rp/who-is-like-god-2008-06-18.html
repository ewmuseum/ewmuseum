"Who is like God."

18 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 18th, 2008 A cold wind blows fiercly over the water, causing the ocean to toss and turn violently. A small boat, with a crew of fisherman sit terrified directly in the center of the raging storm, fearing for their lives. The frantically try and keep the boat afloat, using all of their strength, and fixing the damages as they continue to mount. In the midst of all of it, is a man near the back of the boat, sleeping. “Master!” One of the frightened men calls. There is no response from the sleeping man at the back of the boat. Another fisherman goes over and shakes him, “Master, wake up! We’re going to die!” The sleeping man awakens slowly. “Fear not my disciples.” He rolls over, and stands to his feet, his face blocked for some odd reason still by a broken piece of the boat. He raises his hands high into the air, and in a powerful voice commands, “Peace, be still.” The fisherman all turn to watch their master command the seas to be calm, and the storm to be at peace. Nothing happens. The storm continues to rage. The Master then points to the opposite side of the boat, signaling the rest of his disciples to see what he sees. They all turn, and stare hard in the direction their lord has just pointed. They see nothing, but surely there must be something there, otherwise why would he have pointed? SPLASH The men whirl around to see their “master” plunge into the sea, wearing a scuba suit he had stowed away on the boat for emergency purposes. Suddenly, a gigantic rogue wave appears out of no where, coming straight at them. As the wave closes in on their boat, all of them have the exact same thought. “Fucking bastard.” And then, silence. .~. So for those of you who are now completely, one hundred and twenty percent lost, let’s take it back a little while. In fact, let’s take this back about a year ago. Back to Las Vegas, the city of Sin. The lights, the action, the women, the money. A perfect place for a screw up to go mess his life up even more. It was the city that was so hollow, and disconnected from emotion, that it didn’t give a fuck how low you were, it’d pull you lower. Now. Who wants to know where Tyson was the entire time he was MIA? Let me show you. .~. Wide angle shot. The strip. The Bellagio, Caesars Palace, The Venitian. It’s the lights, the pizzaz, and the money. The glitz and glamour of the Las Vegas night scene. It’s beautiful from a far, but let’s get up a little closer. Zoom in. Even from a closer it looks like a grand place. A place where dreams come true. Men winning large amounts of money, surrounded by beautiful women. Women shopping their lives away at the most expensive designer lines known to them. People changing their names, and forgetting about “real life” for a time, even if it only lasts a few days. Zoom in further. A man with a scruffy beard, tattered clothing, and a cardboard sign that reads “Please Help, War Veteran”, sits in front of the Fremont Street Experience entrance. For some reason, he looks vaguely familiar. People pass him by like he’s not even there, a few toss coins at him here and there, but most of them don’t even glance in his direction. Who is this broken man? He can’t be anyone that anyone knows, can he? He can’t be anyone people would actually care about, can he? You would think not. But alas, it’s our dear Tyson. After losing the TV Title to Clyde Fox, he began a downward spiral involving drugs, alcohol, and hookers that has led him to where he now sits, hopelessly begging for money. I’ve chosen this specific day to show you for a reason. In just a few moments, something will happen, that will lead Tyson onto a journey that will forever change his life. “Excuse me sir?” a man in a crisp pinstripe suit stops in front of Tyson, addressing him. Tyson looks up, his eyes showing no emotion. “Here.” The man hands him an envelope, and walks off. Inside it is ten crisp 100 dollar bills, and a note. Tyson of course does what any bum with a drug problem would do. Shoves the envelope into his pocket, and heads to his dealer. For the first time, there’s a glimmer of happiness in his eyes. Or perhaps that was just the knowing grin of a coke addict. As he’s rounding the corner to his dealers apartment complex, he’s suddenly surrounded by three black men, who jump him, and beat the living piss out of him. You see, Tyson isn’t the fighter he once was. The take everything he has, including the thousand dollars he’d just received as a gift from the stranger. Bloodied, and quite possibly harboring a couple broken ribs now, he struggles to pull himself up. Instinctively reaching into his pocket to see if he has anything left at all, he feels the envelope. Pulling it out, he realizes that the note is still inside of it. Grabbing at it frantically, hoping that it would somehow magically contain the man’s home address, or phone number, he opens it. “Therfore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things become new.” “What the fuck?” He crumples the paper and tosses it behind him in despair. He’d just had a thousand bucks to do with whatever he pleased, and now it was gone. And in it’s place, a lousy note with some sort of Christian value passage on it. As if his life couldn’t get any worse. He began his walk home. And I use the word “home” very loosely, because Tyson is homeless. Home is wherever he decided to lay down for the night. He had a few bucks left, so he stopped into a liquor store to buy a bottle of the cheapest whiskey he could find, and downed the entire bottle in two gulps. In his new drunken stupor, things seemed much less painful, and he crossed the street, headed towards one of his sleeping spots. Then everything went black. .~. Tyson slowly opened his eyes, squinting slightly to allow his eyes to adjust to the light in the room. It took him a moment to realize where he was, but as soon as he did, he shot up. Or tried to at least. His back didn’t let him get very far. He was in a hospital room, with various I.V.?s and tubes going into him. He laid back down, fully alert now, and looking around the room. How had he gotten here? Who found him, and cared enough to take a bum who got drilled by a car to the hospital? No matter. Whoever it was would have his blessing. After all, he now knew he was God. .~. Ok. Allow me to explain a few things. Yes, Tyson does indeed think that he is God. Yes, he did just say he would grant his blessing upon the person who brought him to the hospital, which in all actuality, was just the local paramedics who came to the scene of the accident, and saw that he was mangled by a fucking car. Now. Why? While Tyson was unconscious, he had a dream. In that dream, he was told that he was in fact, the God of this world, and that he needed to clean up his life, so that he could lead his disciples in the chosen path. It’s insane, I know, but that’s what he believed, and he felt like he had a life changing experience, and so he made the decision to follow exactly what the dream had told him. So, Tyson was God, at least in his own mind. For another few months, Tyson went about studying “his own” teachings from the Bible, and gathering a following (which essentially consisted of twelve other crazy homeless people who for whatever reason of their own believed him) and taught them “his” ways. And that, is how we ended up on the boat. Yes. You guessed it. Tyson, is the man who was asleep on the boat, and he is also the man that tried to command the seas to “be at peace.” Sound familiar? And somewhat blasphemous? It was at this moment in time, that Tyson had a self revelation. An epiphany that would start the next chain of events, leading him back to the man that you know today. I’ll let Tyson explain in his own words. .~. “Fuck. I’m not God.” .~. That about sums it up. Now, let’s flash forward to about three months ago, just before Tyson made his initial comeback to the GCW. .~. He stood in front of the GCW headquarters, wearing jeans, a turtleneck sweater, and a jacket. He was again clean shaven, and looked much better than the last time that we saw him, laying in a street covered in blood. Tyson took his lit cigarette, raised it to his lips, took a final drag and tossed it to the side. Exhaling, he thought to himself. “Well, here goes nothin”.” He walked inside the large building, and headed straight for President Caldera’s office. Caldera and he had never been the best of friends, but he also knew that if anything was going to happen, it would be Stevie’s doing, and there was nothing he could do to change that. He was the President of the federation. He called the shots. He reached the large wooden door with the gold nameplate on it, and knocked. Not something he would have done previously, but hey, that was before he was hit by a car, thought he was God, and then got caught in a storm with a bunch of homeless guys who thought they were fishermen. “Come in!” Tyson opened the door, and stepped through. Caldera’s chair was turned away from him, facing out of his back window. “What can I do for you?” Caldera asked, never turning to see who was in his office. “I want my job back.” Caldera’s chair spun around slowly with a look of confusion at first, then a sly grin. “Tyson DeBough.” “Yea, it’s me.” He responded. “Where the hell have you been” You just dropped off the face of the planet!” Caldera exclaimed. “There will be plenty of time to catch up later, right now, I need a job.” DeBough’s eyes were fixed on Caldera’s, fully intent on accomplishing what he had come for. Caldera raised his hand to his mouth, and stroked his chin. “And why should I give you your job back?” “Because I guarantee you I can boost your ratings, and make you more money.” Tyson replied. “I see’and why should I believe that” As soon as you lost your title, you bailed on me. Who’s to say you won’t do that again?” Caldera inquired. “Fair enough. I don’t show up once, and you fire me.” DeBough fired back. Caldera thought for a minute. “Done.” “Good.” Tyson turned and began to walk back out the door. “Tyson?” Caldera called after him. DeBough turned back to his boss. “Don’t fuck up.” Tyson just grinned. He’d fucked up more than anyone could possibly imagine over the past year of his life. Failure wasn’t an option anymore. He turned, still grinning, and closed the door to President Caldera’s office behind him.
