Date: 9/4/95 8 Years Old Memorable Memory: Grade 3 2:30 pm RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNGGGGGG! The last thing in the world that I wanted to hear? the bell. The day might have concluded but the pain was just about to start. If it was one thing that I learned in my first day of school, it was to learn fast. And for a slow poke, that was going to be a challenge. I was going to get it all right. One day and I already had a reputation. I mean seriously, how could you screw up your image in just one day of school? I surely wasn’t ready for what was about to take place. I got out of my chair, swung my backpack over one shoulder and walked the proverbial plank to the door. I could feel the sweat funneling out of my pours and down my face. When I walked out of the room, I stood there in complete and utter shock? 6 Hours and 30 Minutes earlier. MMMMMUAH! "Eww Mommy, gross!" I spat as my Mom slapped a big wet one on my cheek. I rubbed at it vigorously until I got the imaginary lipstick off. There I stood? my own little man in my own little world. Ready to take any challenge head on. I turn and looked up at the grimacing building. The sign just above the cathedral doors read; Myles Elementary School. It was my firstest day in a school ever. Up to this point, my parents had me home schooled because they thought it’d be better for me. But I know the real reason. I overheard them talking one time and they said I was developmentally slow and a possible danger in the classroom. But after two years of proving myself, my parents could no longer hold me back. I made it to public school. I was excited? all I heard were stories about recess football games, epic marble matches, and yes? even Pog tournaments! I was super pants-excite to say the least. It helped that it was a moderately warm morning too. You could tell that the school year was going to start something special. "Now be good okay Clyde," Mom said as she knelt down behind me. Kids kept fluttering by as they waved goodbye to their parents like they have many times before. I looked around and was immediately overcome with emotions. I wanted that feeling. I had missed that feeling for two years prior to this. I wasn’t even sure if I’d like it or fit in, yet somehow I wanted to experience it. "Remember about everything we’ve talked about sweetie," She said softly as she kissed the back of my mushroom cut hair, "Mommy is going to work now but I gave you the number if for any reason you need to contact me. Now go on Mister Third Grader? have a good first day of school. Pick you up at two thirty." And with that, my guardian angel walked off to her car. For one of the rare times, I was finally alone. By myself. No one else. No one by my side. I was too overcome by my surroundings to even notice. A man could have offered me candy and I would have accepted it. I was in awe. Clyde Evan Fox was attending school like a normal boy. That’s all I ever wanted to be in life. Otay Clyde. Just take it easy. One step at a time and you’ll be through the day, back at home, sucking down a huge Yoo-Hoo before you know it! I could taste the Yoo-Hoo. Mmmmmm, chocolate. Anyways? I approached the concrete steps and stood there dumbfounded. Did I forget how to walk up stairs? Like really Clyde, c’mon. All the kids around you were sprinting up them and you can’t even place one foot up? Suddenly? BUMP! "Hey! Watch it kid!" A loud voice rang by my ear as I stood back up. I turned around after being bumped from behind and I saw three boys. A rather large, Cartman looking kid stood there. He fixed his cap as his friend on the left, a rather tall and skinny kid, picked up his backpack. And on his right stood a physical mixture of the two. The presumed leader stepped forward as he dusted himself off and got right in my face. "What were ya doing standing right in front of the stairs like that kid?" He meanly portrayed. "Ummm... I dunno. There is still plenty of room to get around me," I said terrified. The pudgy one backed up and turned to his friends. "You hear that guys? He said there’s plenty of room. He clearly hasn’t seen how big I am now has he?" The bully joked, "Listen kid. I run this school. I’m in Grade Five. Everyone knows me and everyone fears me. So I suggest that you stay out of my way!" The tall, skinny kid on the left pipes up. "Ya! You tell em boss!" He shrieked. The one on the right follows suit. "I haven’t seen this kid around before boss. Think we should introduce ourselves," He stated. I really didn’t know what to make of it. Did they like rehearse this whole routine? Did they pick me out of a crowd as someone they didn’t recognize, but wanted to pick on? Regardless, I was in for a long day of school. Bullying 101 was now in session. "Otay," The pudgy one started, "Listen up chump. We are the Fivers. We are all best buds and the baddest dudes this school has. We can’t function without one member, so we always stick together see? I’m Parker, the leader." Parker was your prototypical elementary school bully. He wore baggy blue jeans and a puffy sleeveless jacket. His black ball cap ruffled his hair, as it made him look that much more intimidating. And slim over there is Timothy." The tall, slender Timothy stepped forward. He was wearing a blue trimmed school jacket along with a pair of wicked shades. His blonde hair was jelled up as if he put his finger in a light socket. "Nice to make your acquaintance there, I’m the athletic one," Timothy introduced. "That’s right," Parker resumed, "Timothy used to be a good boy and play on the volleyball team but that was before he got his first detention in Grade Four. Ever since, I recruited him to join the Fivers. And on my right, your left, we have Derek." Derek stepped forward. His green tipped Mohawk immediately screamed for attention. He wore nothing but black clothes and pants. He even had one of those wallet chains hanging out? bad ass for sure. "Yo, what it is!" Derek screams, "I’m the punk one!" "That’s correct," Parker confirms, "Derek loves wearing black clothes and spiking his hair in a Mohawk. You want Advil; Derek is your hook up. And as for me, I’m the fighter. If I hear of any shenanigans taking place on my playground, then you better believe I’ll be there next recess to straighten it out... by any means necessary. Together we are..." "THE FIVERS!" They all shouted as they display five fingers in front of my face. It was a rather lackluster routine, but still, they packed more of a punch than trash talk. To say I was scared... well, my level of trembling had yet to increase mainly due to the fact that I had yet to see them in action. "Soooooo. What happens when you guys are in Grade Six?" I smugly remarked. They turned to each other and began whispering. "Ummm. We’re not sure yet. And hey! That’s none of your business," Parker pushed back, "What’s your name kid?" "Clyde," I replied. Parker folded his arms as if he was not impressed. I don’t know why, but whenever I looked at him he seemed to remind me of a big chubby baby. One that hadn’t been brought up with the same values and morals as me. "Clyde. Ha! Lame. Ain’t it lame guys?" Parker swings back only to disallow a reply from his team, "Clyde. I’ve got my eye on you! Fivers... move out!" And just like that the clan of older kids shoved their way by me. I still was unsure what to make of it. I just thought it might have been the way I looked... maybe it was too obvious that I was a new kid. I tried to shrug it off as I slowly stumbled up the stairs. Then all of a sudden I heard this strange noise... RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNGGGGGGGGG! Little did I know, but that was the bell signifying that classes were starting. I stood there confused as all the kids rushed the door. I managed to spin out of the way as I saw a typhoon of pupils scurry by. I was about to join them but then I saw a stray book on the ground. F. Scott Fitzgerald’s "The Great Gatsby" lay at the base of my light up sneakers. I dug down and picked it up and began to scan the crowd for anyone who looked like they lost something. Suddenly... "Hi," A soft voice melted my ears from behind. I spun around quickly and then I saw her. It felt like my breath had been taken out of my lungs and I was thrown underwater. She was stunning. She had a little smirk on her face and was just standing there. I couldn’t believe it. A little girl who actually wasn’t repulsed by my deer-in-the-headlights look. "Thanks for picking my book up," She stated, "I must’ve dropped it on the way in." My jaw opened... but nothing came out. I could feel the saliva drying up on my tongue as she continued to giggle like a piggy. "Could I have my book back please?" She asked so sweetly. As if I were Parker, I practically punched her with the book as I trembled with nerves. I also managed to butcher the few words I spat out. "Here go. Happy," I stuttered. "Hehe... thanks!" She said, taking no offence to my unwieldy arms, "My name is Lulu. What’s yours?" Her eyes sparkled in the sunlight. It was all that I could notice even though she was dolled up in a yellow dress with a white doily trim. "Clyde," I choked out somehow. She held her book tight to her body and chuckled again. "How cute! Well are you new here, because I haven’t seen you before," She asked. I finally put my arms down and started to get my heart rate under control. "Yes. I’m new. I’m in Grade Three and I have no idea what’s going on right now," I explained. "Well that’s no problem. I’m in Grade Three as well. Come with me, I’ll show you where everything is," She said so kindly. Lulu. Luscious, lusty, ladylike, Lulu. I didn’t know school could be up and down... so fun and yet such a scary place. -- First class. I made it. And it was all thanks to the lovely Lulu. I sat alone on the right side of the classroom as I remember Lulu being called over by her friends. My first teacher was Mrs. Lang. She was an older lady but man did she ever know her way around equations. From all the hype, I was surprised at how boring class actually was. We sat there in silence and listened. Then we wrote down notes. Then we sat in silence and listened some more. It was brutal! My attention span was definitely not ready for this. But I managed through it and made it to recess. I stayed quiet because I was shy. Once I got out of class I headed to the cafeteria to get some food. I got in the rather short line and stood there patiently until? Tap, tap, tap. I turned around only to see Parker and the Fivers standing behind me. "Oh hey there Smyde!" Parker lured, "How was your first class?" "Forgettable. I don’t even know what we talked about. How was your class guys?" I asked politely. "We skipped!" Timothy interjected from behind. They went skipping? Maybe these guys aren’t that bad after all. Heck, I wanna go skipping. Maybe hanging around them would be a good idea. After all, I want to impress Lulu. Maybe I could get her to skip with us!” "Skipping huh?" I played dumb, "That doesn’t sound that cool to me. But? would you guys mind if I skipped with you? Do you guys use the double rope trick?" Parker scratches his forehead in confusion. "What the hell are you talking about? Skipping? we skipped class? as in missed it on purpose," He directed, "You know what Smyde? I hate people who get smart with us. Don’t get smart with us or you’ll be sorry! You’ve been warned!" The Fivers readjusted their shirts and or sunglasses and left me alone. I stood there for a brief moment before Lulu found me. "Hey," She said, "Were those guys bothering you?" "Not really," I replied, "You know them?" "Who doesn’t?" She started, "They are the Fivers. They think are cool and stuff but yeah? I don’t buy into them anymore." ANYMORE? ? "Sooo. I’ll see you at lunch then," Lulu said as she ran off. -- I pushed the door open and exited the caf?. Man was that snack satisfying. All I wanted to do was go to bed but it was only around ten in the morning. I kept wondering how long this whole school thing would last. The bell rang and everyone headed back to class? everyone except me that is. You see I was on my way to the room right before I heard someone yelling from the washroom. I made a mad dash down the hall and towards the little boys room. I flung the door open and was confronted by Timothy and Derek. They were standing over the trash bin as if something or someone was in it. They turned quickly and warded me off. "Hey! Look who it is! Smyde! What great timing! We can toss him in here too!" Timothy boasted. Derek disengaged the trash bin and came up to me. "I’m not afraid of you Fifth graders. Not one bit," I said confidently. "Oh yeah?" Derek teased, "Well you can be not scared in the trash bin!" Derek latched onto my skull as he dragged me over to the bin. I tried to wriggle away but it was no use? he was just too strong. It wasn’t until I managed a junk shot did he let go. I hit him pretty hard too. Derek doubled over in pain ad Timothy tended to his fallen comrade. "Damn you kid! You’re a feisty one," Timothy noted, "Don’t think that you can just get rid of us so easy! We have our eyes on you!" And with that, Timothy took Derek and they stormed out of the washroom. I stood there with a proud smile on my rather hurting head. I started to walk to the exit before I heard a voice? "Wa’wait!" A mysterious voice echoed from the trash bin. I walked over and peered into the smelly pit. Down at the bottom was a boy not too much older than me. I reached in and pulled him out. He wiped off some tissue that had pasted itself to him but couldn’t help retaining a raunchy stench. "Thanks guy!" The presumed schoolmate said to me, "Those guys just won’t stop bullying me." Trying to keep my composure, I gathered more interest. "Those guys are bad news huh?" I asked. "Yeah? big time," The scattered clothed student said. His hair was long and messy. He wore a brown shirt? naturally brown shirt, along with some cut up jeans. He seemed like a nice guy that didn’t deserve any flack from the Fivers, that’s for sure. "Sorry that you had to see that," He said, "They’ve been bugging me ever since I got here last year." "Really? You’ve only been here one year?" I asked. "Yep. I came last year as an exchange student from Chicago. I’m only supposed to spend Grade Four and Five here? but I’ve applied to go back home because of those bullies. They constantly make fun of me because of how I look? then they keep throwing me into the trash bin," He explained, "I just want to go back home." I kept forming all the incoming information about these Fivers. It was interesting to note that they bullied kids in their own grade as well. "I’m Clyde? nice to meet you," I said. "Hi? I’m Johnny," He said as we shook hands, "Oh and please don’t call me what they do?" "And that is?" I said puzzled. "They call me Trash Bin Johnny because I smell like trash to them," Johnny cried, "It’s not my fault-" I cut him off quickly before he could explain. "Listen Johnny. We can get those guys. I’m new here too and I’ll be around much longer than you? I don’t want to be bullied for eternity. Why don’t we give those Fivers a dose of their own medicine!" I preached. "But how?" Johnny asked. "Meet me at lunch time by the tree," I said. -- "And that’s what we should do," I finished up as I looked around the circle of kids, including Lulu and Trash Bin Johnny. It was just the start of lunchtime and we had rallied every kid in the school how had a grudge against the Fivers together. We were going to strike back, and strike hard. The Fivers wouldn’t know what him “em. "Otay, so let’s go over the plan again," I said, "Lulu? you distract them with your charm? drop your books or something. Then Johnny and I will come up from behind and pants Timothy and Derek, leaving them immobile. Lastly, Parker will be vulnerable to and all out noogie attack from all of us! Otay! Let’s do it? BIG TIME!" I encouraged. The plan went into action. We spread out and sent Lulu running towards them, she "tripped" and lost all of her books. Timothy and Derek fell for it as they sat there laughing hysterically unaware of who was crawling up behind them. BAM! The pants had been dropped! Johnny and I were a great team. We high fived each other as we joined the rest of the gang and rushed Parker. He was over in the corner of the yard hustling the marble players when all of a sudden he turned and saw a half dozen kids coming to terrorize him! He tried to run away but his pudgy-ness did not allow for it. We trampled him so hard that we he was crying for his Mommy! Johnny was so joyous during the final moments? I can remember the look on his face. But our time was about to run short. A huge shadow was cast over us as we eventually stopped and turned. A much bigger kid than even Parker appeared before us. "What’s going on?" The kid spoke loudly. "They’re beating me up brother!" Parker squealed to his alleged brother. The kid looked like a teenager. I stood there in awe. "Who is the leader of this group?" The teen asked. Immediately everyone but Lulu and Johnny pointed at me. The big kid got down to my level and looked me straight in the eye. "You have a date with an eighth grader AFTER SCHOOL! Say your prayers kid! Percy is coming for ya!" He screamed in my face. I swallowed hard. Yup. I did it all right. I picked a fight with the toughest eighth grader in the school? I was in for it. -- 2:30 pm I had been trembling the rest of the day when? RIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNGGGGGG! I pinched my cheek to see if it was real. Oh, it was! What happened? I thought I was going to get the snot punched out of me! But someone saved me! I can’t be-weave it! As a crowd filtered around the two trash bins, I got pulled under the stairs by a familiar face. It was Johnny. "Hey! Did you do that?" I asked. Johnny nodded up and down, "You betcha. I’m not the only one with eighth grade connections. Let’s see how they like the trash bins now? ha? it’s kind of become my trademark hasn’t it?" "Nah? garbage bags seem to be more of your thing?" I joked, "But? this doesn’t solve the problem does it?" "Yeah it does Clyde. I got them both expelled for bullying me. I tattled on them but it was the only way. Besides, it doesn’t matt now? I’m got my transfer back home," He said excited. "Thanks Johnny! You have no idea how much that means to me! Wish you luck bud," I said grateful.
