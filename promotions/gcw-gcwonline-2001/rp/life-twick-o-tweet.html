Life: Twick o' Tweet

Date: 10/31/91 START 4 Years Old 7:00 pm Fondest Memory: Halloween I was finally old enough. About dang time too! My firstest Halloween. Correction. My firstest Halloween that I was allowed to go out for. Mommy and Daddy weren't real Halloween flag wavers if you catch my drift... so it was kind of a big deal. The three previous years they had me, they kept debating on whether or not to take me out. Ultimately they were scared of how I would react to all the costumes and chaos. Little did they know that Halloween would fastly become my holiday of preference... it also gave me heavy wood to work with. Gosh I love Halloween porn. Anyways, it was around seven o'clock on Halloween day, 1991 when it all started. "Are you sure about this Zane?" My Mother asked Daddy. "Listen, hunny. I think hes ready. I know what you're going to say... 'what about the doctors orders'... quite frankly hunny, we can't shield Clyde from everything in life. So what... he might have a few defects... the key term is might my dear. This is the right thing. I already experienced three Halloweens when I was his age. Hunny, let it go," My Dad convinced. He was a good convincer. My Mommy knew he was right. I was quietly huddled in my room playing with some Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle action figures. Come to think of it... my parents spoiled me with toys... probably so I wouldn't be so interested in the outside world. I liked Raf. He was by far my favourite. He also wore red... red is cool. That's where I got my costume idea from. Nothing would beat being a Ninja Turtle for Halloween. But my p units had other ideas. "Okay. You're right," My Mom agreed, "Wait here, let me get Clyde." She walked out of the living room and down the hallway to my penthouse suite aka the dungeon of doom. She tapped lightly on the door and entered. I was sitting there on the edge of my bed, wide eyed and expecting to be told that I wasn't allowed to go. "Your Father and I talked it through... we're bringing you out this year," My Mom said. Bringing me out? Out of what? The closet? I didn't plan to do that until I was 32 and living out of the house with my boyfriend Fredrico! Oh wait... you mean Halloween! Yay! I get to go Twick o' Tweeting... BIG TIME! I can't wait to go as Rafael! He has knifey things that hurt the bad guys! Hiya! Boo yeah! "But..." My Mom stated. I looked up at her with big puppy dog eyes. I knew something was up... it was just a matter of what. I knew she'd spoil it. Damn doctors and their restrictions... why can't I be a normal special kid? Isn't every kid suppose to feel special anyways? What a crock... especially when the special kids grow up one day to realize that they aren't especially special. What would you rather have... a kid who has culture shock or a kid who knows his place? Thanks societally accepted "morals!" "You can only go out wearing the costume I picked out for you," My Mom delegated. Oh gosh. You have got to be kidding right!? RIGHT!? I mean... no way. There's no way I'm doing that. You'd have to be a four year old douche bag who was found in a snowbank to wear that! Oh... If you only knew how I felt. Pretend you just got a date with the hottest chick at your work. The build up is there, you even exchange textual messages via mobile phone device. Then the day comes only to be asked if her two friends can come along for the ride... and they are guys. That's the equivalent to this. Instead of going as an awesomely radical awesome Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle, I, Clyde Fox was going as... A Bumble Bee. Yuck. Yeyyow? I had to wear Yeyyow? Bwack and Yeyyow. Eff that mate... I did end up wearing it of course. I wasn't about to miss this Halloween for the life of me. My Mom dressed me up in a yeyyow sweater, a bwack toque, bwack gloves and pants... and to top it off, I had a huge stinger made out of paper mache latched onto my buzzing behind. Oh, did I mention the face paint too? Yeah... ghetto. I pounced out of my room to let my Dad take it all in. "Well..." He said as he rubbed the back of his neck, "Now I have two hunnies." I bit my lip and frowned like the child I was. "Okay sweetie, don't forget your pale," My Mom directed. I grabbed my pumpkin pale and waltzed towards the door. My Dad waved goodbye as my Mom followed me to the sidewalk. "Okay," My Mom started, "I figure we can start at the Lancers house okay?" Ughhhh The Lancers!? But they are so rude! Their son Billy is mean to me most days! Two days ago he even bit my foot clan action figure! You gotta understand... my neighbours, The Lancers. They were the complete opposites from us. They were mean, and unkind. Why would I want my first Halloween experience there? But my parents always insisted on being nice to everyone. They had one son around my age. Billy. He would always play outside in his yard... in the dirt. Yuck. He always got dirt under his fingernails and he'd try to touch me. Fitting that as we walked up their front stoop, there was Mr. Lancer and Billy... who was dressed as a construction worker. "Claire... how nice of you to drop by," Mr. Lancer stated. "Hi Hugh, and hi Billy. Happy Halloween," My Mom replied. I was left with Billy as the elders conversed. He seemed to be better than me at everything. His costume was custom made, he was bigger, stronger, faster, and he could also talk. "Ewwww lookie who it is," Billy bullied, "If it ain't Smyde Cox. The douchiest douche douche known to the douche-conomy. What are you dressed up as? A bumble bee? Yeah... that is so you... you'd take it in the bum bum. Just don't sting me... I'm not your type douche!" See that pile of nothing on the ground... ? Yeah... those are my feelings. You destrodered them. "C'mon douchey douche. Talk back. I'm tired of your lame ass being so dang mute," Billy pushed, "Come on... Smyde!" That word kept ringing in my ear... Smyde... my gosh darn name wasn't Smyde! And I thought I made a pretty decent bumble bee! "Well, do you have any candy for us?" Mr. Lancer asked my Mom. How rude... we came to their door and yet they asked us for candy. See, this is what I'vd been talking about all along. "Well actually we came..." My Mother was cutoff. "No candy huh... that's fine. Come on Billy, we have houses to hit up," Mr. Lancer exclaimed as the split inbetween us and headed off. Like Father, like son. I wasn't a very happy bee. I remember Billy stopping and turning to me though... that's when it all went down. "See you around... Smyde," Billy laughed as he blew kisses. "CLYDE! My name is Clyde! TWICK O' TWEET, BITCH!" I screamed as ran up and speared him off of the front stoop. I swear, I even made a buzzing sound when I did it. My Mom immediately swooped down the steps and split the two of us up. Mr. Lancer was quick to get in there too... however, he was only concerned with his kid. I managed to give Billy Lancer, the neighbourhood terrorisor, a black eye amidst it all. I was proud. "Get your kid off my kid!" Mr. Lancer yelped. My Mom ripped me away and shielded me behind her back as we retreated back to our house. "That dang kid of yours has issues Claire! Real bad issues!" Mr. Lancer said. "No he doesn't Hugh. It was just an accident. Come on now," Mom said as she then turned her attention to me, "You okay sweetie? You want to go back to the house?" I nodded yes. I had my fill for my first Halloween. I glared back at Billy. He was holding his head... I stuck my tongue out at him before walking back with my Mom. "Did I hear you say your first words Clyde?" My Mom asked. I shook my head no and smiled. I was amazed she didn't hear. But I did buzz a few times on the way back... guess I made a pretty killer bee after all.
