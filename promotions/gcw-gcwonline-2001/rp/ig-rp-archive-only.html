Date: Today, Wait, stop me if you’ve heard this one before. Boy meets girl, boy loses girl. Sounds pretty clich? doesn’t it? In fact it’s probably a story you’ve heard a hundred times before, but you’ve never heard this particular version. When it happens in the movies, a convenient plot twist will come around at just the right moment to turn things around. They get back together, embrace, and share that perfect kiss as the sun sets and the music reaches a crescendo. It’s all very beautiful, absolutely perfect, and all very fake. The pure and simple fact is, that in real life things rarely end perfectly. Those convenient plot twists and changes of heart are a lot fewer and far between. In most cases when boy loses girl, she’s gone for good. Now two things can come of this, either boy uses the pain to make himself into something stronger, or he lets it destroy him. Which do you think is more common? If you couldn’t tell this is going to be a story about the latter scenario. Our protagonist goes by the name Michael Stevens, known to some as the Spitfire. I call him Mike, and by the end of the story you’re going to know him well enough to be on a first name basis as well. Although, you might not want to be, that’s for you to decide. Just look at the guy, he’s a mess. Sitting in the same bar, drinking his drinks, night after night, trying to forget. Sure the name of the bar changes every night, and it’s always in a different town, but if you look really closely you’ll see the people are the same. At least if you’re looking through the right set of eyes. It’s not hard to see that when he looks around, he’s only seeing two faces, hers and her brother’s. What’s that? You want to know why? That’s a pretty long story, but I’ll give you the short version if this is your first time. See a few months ago Mike did something really bad. He took things a bit too far and the brother wound up six feet under. From that it should be pretty easy to tell why he lost her. For brevities sake I won’t really go into more of the details. You want to know the story, ask him yourself. Just don’t expect the answer to come easily. You’ll probably have to buy him at least a couple drinks. Not that he needs more to drink understand, but he’ll just keep drinking until they kick him out. So yeah, sit down next to him, offer him a drink, he might take it. Although I’m guessing he’s not one for company right now. I haven’t even approached him, yet, and I’ve been watching the guy for nearly a month now. But hey it’s worth the shot right? Anyway back to the story at hand. Mike’s a mess, don’t believe me, take a look for yourself. See how his hand shakes as he lifts the glass up to his lips? Look how he keeps his head down, trying not to see anyone around him. These aren’t the signs of an individual with good mental health. He’s scared, and he’s trying to hide in a bottle. Apparently he’s doing a pretty good job of it, most nights by the time he walks out of the bar he seems happy enough. At least he does when he can convince someone to go with him. You know what I said earlier, about getting him to tell you his story? Well, don’t expect to get a second glance from him unless you’ve got a nice set of tits. Sorry, but with Mike, that’s going to be about your best way in. He’s a little bit of a man whore, but then again, maybe he’s just trying to find a connection. Aren’t we all? The world can be a tough place, everyone in a rush, people pass by without seeing. They’re too busy too rushed, more importantly they’ve trained themselves not to see anything going on around them. It’s too difficult, everyone’s got their own problems to deal with, they’re just too busy to see the urchins in the street. Too rushed to stop for a moment and lend the lush at the end of the bar a hand. Blind to the world around them, they continue on forward, without ever realizing that there’s something missing in themselves. A little hold that they could fill, if only they knew how. But they don’t, and the hole remains a little pocket of darkness in even the most happy of people. It’s the reason dance clubs, and singles bars have become so popular. The place to hook up, the place to find someone, the place to not be alone if only for one night. It doesn’t matter that in the morning they’ll still be alone. Doesn’t matter that in the morning in the glaring light of the sun they’ll still be an empty husk. A robot performing it’s programmed tasks, never deviating. Work, hook up, sleep repeat. For many this is life, and they have no regard for where it will eventually take them, because no one looks towards the future. I’m here to tell you, and you might not like the sound of it, but continue on your course of depravity, your foolhardy searching, and you’ll become this man, Michael Stevens. Here sitting before you, is your destiny, and it’s only a few short drinks away, aren’t you the lucky one? But, enough generalities for now, first and foremost we’re here to discuss Mike. A participant in more than his share of late night bar room pick-ups, his entire life he’s been searching for that connection. Night after endless he, he’d find a new honey, a new baby doll. A new face, a new nubile body to satisfy every carnal desire. Those were the days, he seemed happy enough, you’d never catch him without a smile on his face. Of course you’d never see a sign of life in his eyes either. For so long he has a man dead inside. Ever heard that old saying, “the lights are on, but nobody’s home?” Yeah, that’s a pretty good description of Mike back in the day. He walked, he talked, he made love and war. But he wasn’t human, and if you’re one of the people that needs to be hearing all this, neither are you. If this all sounds a bit too familiar, a bit too much like home, you’re just the type I’m talking to, and you know who you are. It was around that time he found her, a vision right out of every Julia Roberts movie you’ve ever seen. She was intriguing, beautiful, everything he never knew he wanted. And just like those hackneyed cookie cutter Hollywood productions, the music swelled you just knew they were supposed to be together. Of course this wasn’t your typical story, and the characters in this great romance were anything but perfect people. Her name was Sheena Ramone, hair the color of cotton candy, dressed like a hooker attempting to pull off the school girl look. Their eyes met and she smiled, a bottle of whiskey shared, a bed nearly shattered by acrobatics. Just like that they were inseparable. And by that I mean to say she wouldn’t leave him alone. He’d seen her as another conquest, another notch in his already whittled down bed post, but there was something about her that he couldn’t shake. They became lovers, something he never truly accepted. To say their relationship was always a rocky one, would be something of an understatement. Unless they were between the sheets, they couldn’t stand each other. She was an idiot, he was an asshole. The only thing they could ever agree on was the next person to share their bed. Both their sexual appetites leaning more towards multiple partners. Thinking back, it’s a wonder neither of them ended up diseased, but I suppose they say god has a special place in his heart for children and fools. They were both. As time passed, Mike would come to view her as something less than a person, just another employee. If nothing else he saw her as something of his private whore. He allowed her to exist in his world provided she offered herself up to him willingly whenever he so desired. Sufficed, to say, that didn’t end up working out all that well. Actually that’s something of a misnomer, she never really complained. I think she must have just been happy to be with him. Perhaps she was honestly and truly in love, poor girl. For her Mike was like something out of a money, he was rich, powerful, good looking, strong. Everything a little girl could ever dream of in a man. If you were to see the two of them back in those days you’d just have to shake your head and sigh. She followed him everywhere like a puppy, completely blind to his abuse. Sure he never hit her, but just listening to the way he spoke, the things he called her, there wasn’t anything else to call it but abuse. Periodically back in those days, he’d have a rapid mood swing, demand she pack her things and leave, that he didn’t need or want her around anymore. She’d go, hurt and afraid, slinking off into some quiet little corner to find peace, along with the other people he’d throw away. And without question weeks would pass maybe even months, and he’d call and tell her to come back, and just like that, with barely less effort than the snap of a finger, she’d come back. But he never said he was sorry. In those days Stevens was the type who was blinded by his own glory. He saw himself as something completely infallible, almost something more than human. He called himself perfect, humanity in it’s highest most evolved form. No, he could never apologize, that would’ve meant he’d made a mistake, and back then that just wasn’t possible. So the cycle continued, and you could almost call them a happy couple when they were together. Despite all the mental torment, I think she stayed because of the quiet times. Those cold nights where they’d curl up together, and be peaceful. Yeah, even as I speak I become more firmly convinced she really did love him, for those times if nothing else. Too bad that made her blind to every other way he mistreated her. Of course no one can be expected to take that level of abuse forever. I remember hearing about the night it finally happened. I wasn’t there, but I wish I could have seen it, I truly do. Mike had gotten in one of his moods and demanded that she leave him be, just go off somewhere and wait for a call. I don’t know what caused it, how this time was so much different, but Sheena snapped. Finally she yelled back, instead of getting hurt she got angry. When she finally did pack up her things, she swore she’d never speak to him again, that it was over for good. If only she’d made good on that promise, none of this would’ve ever happened. Maybe, just maybe if she’d stayed away, both of them would be in a far better position these days, but that’s just conjecture, who really knows what would have ended up happening. After the fight, Mike went back to what he knew best. The parties, the women, the booze, it all flowed freely. For the first time in a long time Stevens had a smile back on his face constantly. To anyone that would listen he’d loudly proclaim how much better off he was now that Sheena was gone from his life for good. Not that he talked about her too much you understand, that might betray too much of what was really going on inside his head. Mostly he just smiled, just drunk, just fucked. That was his life, and by all outside appearances he was really enjoying himself. With every excess his smile grew wider, with every conquest he felt a little more human. Sometimes self destruction can truly be a beautiful thing, and lead to self discovery. Stevens made self destruction an art form, it was only a matter of time before he finally saw it for what it was. He was in Rome at the time. The Pope had just died, and the world mourned. More specifically the fine Irish Catholic Stevens family mourned. At the time he first heard the news he shrugged it off, it wasn’t like he knew the man, and it’d been years since he stepped foot into a mass. Sure his family was probably upset, but it wasn’t exactly like he was on the best of terms with them either. In fact back then he couldn’t even remember the last time he’d spoken with them. It was the phone call from his father that really started him thinking, about all the things he’d done wrong in life. The old man was, as always disappointed in him, but that was to be expected. He’d never really let things like that bother him, at least he never showed that they did anyway. Yet this time it was a little different, there was too much else going on. The phone call hurt, and a lot worse normal. At the time he could only wish for someone to talk to, wish for someone to help him ease the pain. Instead of finding a person, he found a bottle. Enlightenment in the easy to handle liquid form. The next weeks he’d travel all over Europe, stewed to the gills. Yet this was a different kind of drunk than before, it wasn’t about partying and happiness. No, now Mike drank to forget, drank to kill the steadily building pain just behind his eyes. Drank to take the edge off the knife that plunged into his heart. They call it rock bottom, and at that moment, he swore he couldn’t fall any further, be buried any deeper. Miserable, lonely, pickled, he began his journey back to the states. Demanding a drink the moment he stepped foot on the plane, he attempted to settle in for an uneventful flight. And yet, it was here in those dark moment suspended over the ocean, hurtling towards the future, that it came to him. There was one person he could call, one person he could talk to, one person who would always come running back whenever he wanted. That person was of course Sheena Ramone. Yet she was different this time, their conversation was brief, and sharp. She didn’t want to hear from him at first, nearly hung up the phone upon realizing who was calling. Using every scrap of charm he’d developed in his life as nothing more than a glorified conman, eventually he convinced her to agree to a meeting. Although he never said it, she could tell it was his way of apologizing. In that moment, it was enough. Of course he never went to see her. In the light of day, with the sun shining bright, his life didn’t seem so bad. And calling on Sheena would be to admit defeat, admit that he wasn’t perfect and had made a mistake. Apparently he hadn’t quite hit rock bottom after all. Having dismissed the call as nothing more than a bit of drunk dialing depression he went back to habit. Drinking away the lonely hours. At least until she found him. She came to him in a storm, demanding an explanation. While he’d been floundering, she had thrived. Come into her own as a person, when she arrived at his door step, for the first time in her life she could call herself a complete person. She wouldn’t simply let him cast her away again, she was going to dedicate herself to helping him, even if he wouldn’t have the decency to help himself. I suppose it was all her way of paying him back. In the end all the torment she’d suffered thanks to him had been enough to turn her life around. To make her whole, and while it had been a rough experience, she’d come out far superior on the back end. Maybe she still loved him even if she wouldn’t admit it yet, but certainly she still felt she owed him a measure of thanks. It began something again what it had been in the beginning a business relationship, but now on more of an equal footing. Finally, the drinking and the excess became too much and Mike finally broke down. In that moment he laid everyone on the table, and he finally said it. Those two little words that Michael Stevens had never even considered saying. I’m sorry, and just like that they came back together, the music swelled, and the would have a happy ending after all, Hollywood would be proud. At least until he made the mistake, until he snapped, and did THAT. Worse yet he’d lied about it, covered his tracks to make sure no one would ever truly find out. In the old days it wouldn’t have bothered him, just reaffirmed his constant belief that he was indeed untouchable. But this was a different Michael Stevens, this was at last a whole person, a person with a soul. The guilt tore at him, until he confessed, and this time she ended it. Walked out of his life, and with a sick certainly Mike knew that this time, she’d never come back, Hollywood ending be damned. Which leads us back to today, back to this bar, same bar as yesterday, same bar as tomorrow. He’s back at rock bottom, the climb out of the pit of despair had proven to be too great a challenge. Today he drinks to forget all he could have had, drinks to forget his sins. The confession did nothing for his guilt, sometimes he wonders if he made the right choice. If he hadn’t told, at least she would still be with him, at least he wouldn’t have to face it alone. But in the end he knows he couldn’t have done that, he’d found true love, and couldn’t lie to the woman that had brought that light into his life. Wait, you’ve got to see this, it’s the part of the night where I come in. He’s just been cut off, and he’s being told to leave. In the old days he might have caused a scene, demanded another drink, started a bar fight. These days he just doesn’t have the strength. He collects his dignity, and maybe shouts a rude phrase at the bar on his way out. His little revenge. More often than not, a couple guys will start to follow him out, it’s not hard to see the guy’s got money. And a guy with money that drunk is a pretty tempting target. So silently I rise, and shake my head. I’m not the biggest guy in the world, but they can see in my eyes that I mean business. “Touch him, and die.” That’s usually enough for them to back down, I’ve only once had to actually get physical while shadowing Mike. I came out of it without a scratch on me, I do feel sorry for the other guys though. I drop some cash on my table, and glide outside, staying a few steps behind him. He never sees me, and even if he does I’m sure I’m dismissed as another ghost haunting him. Sometimes he stumbles, passed out before he can make it back to his hotel. Those nights are the roughest, carrying him inside, paying off the night to keep his mouth shut. He’s not ready to know yet that he’s got himself a genuine grade A approved guardian angel. I’m just waiting for the right moment. He stops to rest a moment against the brick fa’ade of some building. He lights up a cigarette, slowly turning his head from side to side, as if searching for someone. Maybe he’s trying to find her, hell I don’t know. He staggers forward towards the road now, instead of back towards his hotel. This is different, and suddenly I’m intrigued, this may just be my moment, and I quietly creep up behind him. Cigarette dangling from his lips I see he’s waiting for something, and as a set of headlights begin to approach, I know just what he’s waiting for. “Goodbye” He whispers the word as he stretches each arm to the side, letting himself fall forward into the road, and under the approaching car. At least he would if I hadn’t seized a handful of his hair. Dragging him away from the edge I toss him against the building. He slumps down nearly weeping, raising his head to see me, his savior. His eyes search my face, I’m not certain he recognizes me, but then again, it’s dark, and he’s drunk. “What the hell are you doing?” “Saving your life Mike, what else?” “Why?” “Because I can.” He probably doesn’t like the answer, but it’s all he’s going to get, at least for now anyway. I wait, and watch him, as through eyes glazed with unshed tears he again searches my face, shadowed though it may be. Squinting he leans forwards. “Do I know you?” “Maybe, maybe not.” He’ll learn soon enough. I think this is going to be fun. ~fin~
