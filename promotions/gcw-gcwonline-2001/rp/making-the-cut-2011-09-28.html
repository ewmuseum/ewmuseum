Making the Cut

28 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/11 The sounds around his head were all jumbled, the noise was loud however no sound was more distinct then any other. However the one thing that was obvious to him was the pain shooting in his back and legs. He tried to come to his sense and over power the pain however he was unable to. His last breath of air and in his only chance of not slipping into a coma was for him to use his last bit of strength to tap. All of a sudden one sound was louder then all others, the ringing of the bell. Laying in the middle of the ring, beaten he had never felt so beaten. Wanting to raise his body and get away from surrounding sounds, get away from the pain that he is currently feeling. The sweat woke him up, it was dripping off his face starting to getting into his eyes. “Damn It!” Cameron laid in the bed at his hotel in Nevada. It had only been three hours since he had been defeated by William Curr. The pain and agony was still in his body but that wasn’t what was bothering him. He had been beaten in his only opportunity with GCW. He had studied his opponent and he thought he had what he needed to be beat him. Obviously he was wrong. Cameron sat up in his bed wiping the sweat from his forehead with his forearm. Never had he thought he would have lost in his first match. He had been.preparing for a long time to get his opportunity in professional ring. He blew it and he knew. "GOD DAMN IT" Garret hastily slammed his fist on the bed before getting up and making his way over to the mini bar. Reaching in he grabbed the last cold water. After ripping the cal off Cameron gulped down half the ice cold water down instantly. Feeling the relief of the cold water in his veins he once again wipes away the remaining sweat with the front of his forearm. Cameron makes his way back to bed slowly sipping the water before laying back down. Placing his head back on to the pillow Cameron hoped to get some rest before the morning came. The clock already read after midnight and he knew all the Internet hounds were starting their buzz about his first match. He could only hope that Steven caldera would make his decision before he could read the reviews. with that Cameron shut his eyes, sleeping into a deeper sleep before hoping, praying for the opportunity he always wanted think how he may have just blown the chance. He wouldn't know til the morning for now he just wanted the memories of earlier tonight to disappear. Fade to Darkness.... The sun was just starting to peak into the window of his hotel room. He had hoped to get up earlier in the morning but the bumps and bruises he had just taken the night before made it difficult for him to get out of bed. Wiping his eyes he slowly rolled over and took a look at the alarm clock sitting on the night stand beside him, 8:30 am.... Cameron slowly made his way out of the bed, not moving the pace he usually would. Bumps and bruises were apparent on his body. It looked like he had been in a street fight the night before. However he knew that this was only the beginning, being able to do it week over week, or hell even day over day would be the real test of time. However he needed to last that long in GCW to even have to worry about such a thing. Cameron made his way over to the mini bar, a slight memory of what had happened the night before when he made the same exact trip only hours ago, only not really happy with the reason why he had been awoken. Opening the fridge he grabbed the first thing he saw, a carton of orange juice. After swigging it like a champion he placed it on the counter and turned on the coffee pot. Slyly looking over his shoulder he glanced at the phone and lap top that was sitting on the little table by the window. “Ah hell, now is as good of time as any....” Cameron was referring to not only seeing what the critiques had written about him on the blog sites, but also the hope that Steven Caldera may have reached out to him and asked him to stay despite his loss. Cameron allowed the coffee to brew as he made his way to the table. Looking at the laptop he thought to start there, however he knew how long it was going to take for it to boot up. After starting that process he went right to his smart phone. Quickly he scanned for missed calls, for emails, for text messages, before making the big leap to the world wide web. Wishing, hell some might even consider it dreaming that someone may have given him a glimmer of insight of what he was about to read. Nothing, nothing at all no one had texted him, no voicemails, not even an email saying what people he thought about him. Slowly he sat down in the chair, breathed a sigh of relief. It was only shortly lived however, Cameron knew he would have to do some research see what people had thought. He opened the first website, the first critique of the past evenings events. The first words shocked him, for some reason in his stomach he knew what he was about to read however in his head he was not quite sure how to handle it. “GCW new superstar lackluster at first performance.” Cameron knew the heat he would be getting from the media, he knew it was part of chasing his dream to become a professional. However he was not expecting the first thing to be said was how bad he was. He tried not to let it bother him, so he continued on reading. “It is no surprise that Tough Enough did not sign this guy.” He wanted to not be reading this, he knew he had to. However those last words hurt, he worked hard. Harder then anyone else in the his territory and to hear such words actually burned him inside. Clenching his fist he wanted to put it thru the table, what was that going to accomplish? Nothing and he knew that, however for a brief second he thought that it may help with the pain. Knowing better he looked down and then the buzzer went off for the coffee maker. If he slammed his fist thru the table he would have to pay for the table along with the use of the mini bar and he knew that with comments like this he may not have a job tomorrow. Black with sugar, was the way he liked it but today he didn’t have the time to look for sugar. Gulping it down even thought it was hot didn’t matter to him, he needed to see what the next person had to say about his performance last evening. Refilling his cup he goes back to his spot at the desk looking for more information. The next headline Cameron read he glanced over it quickly, it wasn’t until the third website he went to did he think he found a glimmer of hope for career. It read, ‘Garret’s performance wasn’t the best but it was solid.” Cameron was starting to smile a little bit, he found the happiness he needed to hope for his career. It still was not the biggest ray of hope, however it was a ray. Sipping his coffee he understood none of anything he was reading actually mattered, it all came down to Steven Caldera and the front office. Moving from subject matter to subject matter they all said the same, some hated his performance, some thought it wasn’t the worse performance they have seen, hell even a small group, a small group may it be liked his performance. Quickly he changed his sources and went to Facebook, not all reliable but he needed to atleast keep his mind of something else. Reading the feeds was mindless, it didn’t take long for everyone to start their bitching about the recent changes Zuckerberg made. All Cameron could think about was get over it, it is a stupid website. Going back to sipping his coffee, hoping any moment he would be able let his mind go to rest, he wanted an answer. How did the people of the front office think, we’re they going to keep him, or discard him. It didn’t take him long to get the answer he wanted. “You’ve got Mail!” No one really uses the AOL anymore but that is the easiest way to get your point across. It opened in the bottom of the screen. The subject line read, “Preliminary World Wide 154 Card.” Before reading Cameron knew two things, either he was still apart of the wrong distribution list or he made the cut. Not getting to ahead of himself he started to read down the card. It didn’t take long, the second match of the evening. His name was there, he had made the cut. Letting out a shout of enjoyment Cameron was excited seeing his name. He didn’t know for how long it would last but he atleast knew that he would be on World Wide 154, giving himself another shot at making the cut. Not even getting passed his match his mind was racing, he already wanted to start doing his homework on his next opponent, Brad Cruz. It didn’t matter who they placed him against he knew it was time. He knew he was going to make his name this week. Standing up from the excitement Cameron stretched his arms up, grabbed his coffee and stared off into the skyline of his window. He took another sip and wanted to start setting up his game plan for Mr. Cruz. However he didn’t want to waste any time he started packing for Utah. He was moving on to the next city, with Global Championship Wrestling.
