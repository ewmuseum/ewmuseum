A Man's Worth [updated]

Date: August 2008 "The dash between death and birth, could never explain one man’s worth." I am tired. I have pushed myself for months now. I have no energy left, not a drop of energy to push my feet forward one after another. My muscles are cramping on me, and my veins are hardening. My very heart pumps irregularly, causing pain in my chest that is barely recorded into my mind from the exhaustion. My fingers are there, but I can’t feel them, I only feel the stiffness in the immoveable lump that I once called my hand. I need to push myself, push myself into motion. Zacharia Taylor sits up on of the bench that he calls his home and faces down into his hands. His calf muscle is pulsating from pain of cramping. He veins in his arms and hands are borderline ripping out of his skin. His body is in an exhausted, rare form. Lack of food and shelter in addition to constant mobility has taken a hard toll on the man of faith. State to state, country to country, plane to bus. His ventures never end in relaxation, only frustration and more mobility. The Lord looks down upon greed and falsities, taking much pride in abstinence, servitude, and privation. A man’s worth is not how much money he has in his bank account or the car he drives, it is the man’s soul. Morality; code of ethics. Salvation comes to those who wait, not those whom indulge in the "necessities" of the materialistic culture. There are two pure forms of worth; birth and death. What happens in between is the dash on the tombstone. The dash is straight and rigid. The goal of life is to get from birth to death in the straightest, quickest way to stay as pure as possible. At least, that is what the dash shows. Purity from birth to death. Taylor knows that the Lord does not agree with this, for Taylor would not have to be the mercenary of the Almighty if this were true. Taylor practices these vital foundations to his religion. He uses his money for survival. Money goes into food, clothing, and transportation, but is starting to feel the pain of not having shelter. His body is slowly dying from the conditions that he is living in. His feet are never rooted into one place anymore, yet he still can muster the strength to do his job. His prayers fall short of being answered, but being in the service of the Lord, he knows that prayers from the ones in servitude are less acknowledged. His head becomes heavy in his hands. His eyes become focused. The money in the bag becomes vibrant. Not having a realistic identity in the world, Taylor does not have a bank account, nor does he have a form of identification besides a birth certificate with Zacharia Taylor in typeface across it without any true meaning. Taylor is his real last name, but Zacharia is a variation on Zachary, the name his parents gave him. With a surreal identity, it makes it hard to establish a bank account or get a driver’s license in the state of New Jersey. All the money that Taylor has accumulated over the last seven months at GCW is sitting in his gym bag, untouched except for food and travel. The crisp hundreds and fifties make up a bulk of the cash, yet the side pouch of his bag still bulges with the valuable mint. Greed. Is it greed to use the money for basic necessities of the world? Shelter, transportation, communication. Chris Bagwell indulges in spending the massive amounts of money that he makes. Without much of a track record in wrestling, or anything of any caliber in the work force, Taylor was given a small sum of money for his enrollment into GCW. When presented his first check, Taylor only took half, not knowing what to do with the money, he donated most of it to the church, and kept the rest for himself for food and transportation to and from matches. Once he became Television Champion of the federation, Caldera refused to let him take less than his worth. In the eyes of the President, that was a lot more than half of starting rate. Taylor stands up, his right knee giving out as he stands, sending him into the edge of the bench with his other knee. The bench slightly moves, but is pulled back into place by the rusted bolts pulling the bench to the cracked cement platform. The frill of the torn jeans start to turn a deep crimson red from the new gash across his knee. Looking up to the stars, Taylor mutters a few words and then stands with the support of the bench. Once erect, he grabs his bag and heads out of the park. The Clifton rush hour traffic has left it’s impact on the enraged drives of the city. Taylor simply side steps cars that would do better if they were just parked in the center of the street, and makes his way into the center city. The world opens up to a center of shops and venders. Taylor hesitates, but then walks right into the shopping center. The tables are littered with a mixture of junk and specialty items such as pocket books and kitchen cutlery. The possibility of fraud and stolen items litters Taylor’s mind, but he knows that he is not looking for pleasures, but necessities. "The new line of Coach available right here. This is the authentic brand for the dealer’s price." "Armani! The smell you have been dying to have." "Sony’s new line of HD TV’s! Don’t pay $2500 when you can get it for $1800!" Taylor takes interest to the phone booth that is standing next to the nearest alley. As he starts to walk over, he looks to his right and sees a booth of cell phones. A large sign on the wall behind the vender flaunts the words "Pre-Paid" and "Sale" in white letters against a red backdrop. Taylor hesitates, but then walks over to the table. Every company imaginable was laid out on the table, all with different plans and features. "Hey there my man, what can I get for you?" "Eh, well, I don’t have any address or anything to sign up for..." "Not necessary my man, these pre-paid deals are a pay and clear plan. You buy whatever model you like, what accessaries you want, and how many minutes you want. When you are done, you can either buy more minutes, or throw the damn thing away." Taylor looks at the phones with new light. He never imagined that he could own a phone without having a stable residence. He looks at the phones on the left side of the table, but immediately looks away after seeing all the gadgets they came with. When he adverted his attention to the overall picture, he was baffled and completely ignorant to what he needed. "Need some help? Trouble selecting a phone?" "To be honest with you, sir, I have never purchased a phone, nor have I ever used a cell phone. I’m not sure..." "So you are skeptical to what you need? I can help you with that." The salesman picks up a phone off the right side of the table and lifted it up as if his hand is a pedestal. "This is the lower end model for my customers that have no idea what they are doing. This baby is the basic model of a cell phone. No bells and whistles on this one. It is strictly for incoming and outgoing calls. The plan is also very simple. We give you the starters plan where it is only 100 minutes, but it is a discounted ungradable price after your minutes run out. I can give all this to you for only $40. So what do yo say." Taylor pans his head around the stand, seeing a ton of people walking past and looking in his general direction. Feeling uncomfortable and vulnerable, he closes his bag with his free hand. "I will take it, I just have to, take out some money." "No problem my man, I will be right here for you!" Taylor walks past the booth towards the alley that is next to the phone booth. He walks into the alley, looking all around him before taking out his money. He tries to conceal it, but it makes it difficult to see what he has. He opens up his hands and pushes hundreds and fifties past his hands until he comes across a couple twenties. He takes three out and puts them into his pocket. He puts the money back into the bag and heads out of the alley. As he exits, three guys stare at him as he walks through the crowd once again. Taking notice to the men, he adjusts his eyes, and faces the booth in a way in which he can see them. "I will take it, how much will it cost with tax?" "No tax my friend." "But isn’t there..." "We build tax into our prices. It makes it easier on the street venders." "Ok, well, here is the forty [hands over two of the three twenties] Now how do I use this?" The salesman turns over the package and shows him the activation instructions. After pointing out the script, he immediately walks over to another customer with the same greeting he gave him just minutes ago. Taylor starts to walk while reading the instructions to himself. After reading through the simplistic procedure, he raises his head to be met with a fist. Taylor walked his way back into the alley while being immersed in the instructions of the phone. The three men that were watching him while at the booth waited for Taylor to get away from the view of the crowd and jumped him. They saw him pull out the money before, but they didn’t realize who they ambushed. After stumbling back after being hit, he sets his feet and retaliates. With a smart right hook, the assailant is decimated and falls instantly to the floor. The other two run in, attacking with a barrage of unguided strikes from their hands and feet. After taking the abuse far too long, Taylor pushes the man on the right against the wall and strikes him with an elbow across the jaw while the other man is still striking him. He feels a ripping feeling in his knee as the man in front of him kicks forward out of desperation. The exhaustion sets into his joints, combining with the crippling pain, forcing Taylor to the ground. As blows start to crash down on his back and head, a rage builds up inside of him. An uncontrollable sensation of power. The pain starts to become numb, and the vision of the night where he received the scar across his chest comes into his mind. The immense pain of his chest blows up into his fists, thrusting his arm up into the attackers face. His nose explodes with blood and his eyes roll into the back of his head. The assailant’s legs become insignificant and he falls limp. Taylor stands to his feet, and turns around. The third of the attackers takes out brass knuckles and puts them around his hands. "You mother fucker. It’s motha fuckin time now nigga!" The assailant punches wildly at Taylor, but his fist is caught by Taylor’s hand. The second fist immediately comes striking into view, but Taylor is far too quick for that as well. Now with both hands in his possession, and the lack of mobility due to the brass around his hands, Taylor slowly twists the man’s arms until there is a pop in the wrists and elbows. Screaming in agonizing pain, the man starts to plead. "I’m sorry. Have merc.." Before the man can even finish his words, Taylor sends his foot into the man’s jaw, rendering him unconscious or dead. He walks over to his bag and newly bought phone and walks down the alley. His adrenaline pumping so fast, he feels completely unhindered by exhaustion. This natural medicine always heals his wounds, making him oblivious to the real condition his body is in. "Hmph, mercy..." Taylor walks over to a bench and opens up his phone. He holds in the end button to power the machine. After he goes through the activation sequence a women comes on the phone and says, "You now have 99 minutes of phone operation available. 3 minutes will be deducted from your allotted time every time a number is dialed. Press #2 at any time for your available minutes. Thank you for choosing Nextel as your pre-paid service provider." Taylor pulls out his leather book and flips to the back to look at phone numbers. Bagwell. Clyde. Father Emanuel. Andy Murray. The list is nowhere near extensive, but it is all he needs. Already knowing the number, he dials Bagwell’s. * "Please enjoy the music while your party is reached... “BREATH... (pounding of bass and metal guitar)? "Who’s this?" "Hi Bagwell, it’s Taylor." "Where the hell are you calling me from?" "I bought one of those pre-paid phones today." "Wait, you bought something? Look at you, living the luxury of the times." "Yeah, look, I wanted to call you about Warpath." "I don’t think there’s much to talk about. Bagwell is going to steal the show and Murray is going to be left unconscious, if he’s lucky, in the middle of the ring." "I wanted to talk to you about that. What you did last week was wrong. The match was over, the fight was over, and you had to..." "Had to what? Look Taylor, we don’t see eye to eye anymore. We used to have the understanding that when things needed to get done, we did them. We don’t have time to sit there and contemplate every little action. You got soft with all that God shit after you left the Movement. You used to be one scary mother fucker, but now you are weak, just like the rest of them. Just like your so called “allies” that think they are on top of the world." "You know that you were wrong Bagwell. You could have killed Murray." "And he would have done just the same. He was going to put my face right into the concrete and you know that, or did you miss that? Andy Murray is no saint. You need to open your God damn eyes Taylor. Taylor sits there on the bench, staring at the cracked concrete before his feet. He visions Murray putting his mentor through the concrete in the parking lot and then snaps out of it. "You take it too far every time! By God, you don’t care. [Bagwell starts to laugh] You need to learn when to end it. The fight should have stayed in the ring, in the arena, but you had to bring it into the streets. You brought Kincaid and Action into it when you shouldn’t have. If you only had Black things would have been worse." "Let me ask you this Taylor, how’s your head?" Bagwell starts to laugh uncontrollably. Taylor tries to settle his breathing, but his heart starts to race. He tries to calm his anger, but rage erupts. His adrenaline is so fierce now that his veins harden to the point of bursting, and he raises to his feet. The grip on the phone tightens to a vice, making the cheaply made plastic shift and click. "Bagwell, God help me, put me in the position and your reign at GCW would be over. Fortunately for you, we will not cross paths for some time. With that aside, your Movement will not be at my mercy. Your cheap tricks and manipulations have gone too far. Whatever yo have instilled in the mind of Black from the start, he is becoming more and more like you. He nearly killed me with that chair in my match against Ngozi, and then after the match was over, he attacked me once again with that God damn chair. This is where it ends." "What, Taylor? What ends? You have been wining by luck! You have nothing here. You are the Television Champion of GCW! Big fucking deal! You are not on my level, you will never be! You will always be at least one step below me. You remember who trained you, you remember who helped bring you up in the streets when you couldn’t fend for yourself. I never told him to go after you with the chair, that was all improv on his part, mighty fine script if you ask me. "You are sick, Chris..." "The world would not be the same without Chris Bagwell." "I’m coming after you, Chris. It may not be soon, but it will happen." "You will fall just like Murray will at Warpath. You are nowhere near my level, Taylor. You will never be near my level." "The hell with you..." Taylor hangs up on Bagwell and looks at the screen. "33:10" About 60 minutes left on the phone. Clenched in his fist, the cell phone starts to click and clack at the grip of Taylor. He throws the bag inside of his bag and pushes his hood down off his head. Closing his eyes for a brief second, he looks up and sees Chris Bagwell's face intruding into his mind. His grim smirk and smarting stare. It is over, Bagwell is no longer one of the company of Taylor. The fallout of the Movement kept a friendship, but now it has gone way too far. An innocent man's life was on the line, and only for glory and fame he acted. These are the selfish entities that Father Emanuel tries to prevent Taylor from falling into. Taylor looks back into the bag and sees his leather bound book resting next to the phone. It seems like a good opportunity to tie up some loose ends while there is downtime. He picks up the book and flips to the end to look at the phone numbers. The very site of Bagwell's name infuriates Taylor, but he looks at Clyde Fox's number and dials it into his phone. "For international calls, an additional minute will be taken off for every minute in use. Thank you for choosing Nextel." Taylor shakes his head and looks down at his watch. "Heto?" "Clyde, it's Zacharia.." "Taylor? You called! I'm sow happy you called! Do you want to go play lazer tag or play hopscotch or..." "No Clyde, no. I wanted to call you to let you know that no matter what happens, I am still in your corner. I just got off the phone with Chris Bagw..." "Baggy Tits? Tis he causin twuble again?" "I called him, Clyde. I wanted to talk about the match at the last World Wide, with Andy and everything. I figured I could maybe talk some sense into him, but it didn't work." "Baggy Tits is gone Zachy. You need to wet go of him and know what side your are on." There is a long pause by Taylor while he is thinking about what Clyde is saying. "Zachy? That's mine right? Zachy? What I meant was that you were on mine. Zachybutyoucantleaveme!YouaremyonlyfriendhereinGCW!Idon'twanttogobacktothewaythatwewerebeforeintheMovement!" Clyde almost runs out of breath as he speaks so fast that most of the words seem to be one word drawn out. "No Clyde, I was thinking about what you said. I'm on your side. Don't worry. You know that you and Andy are the only real friends that I have." "Maury got pwetty messed up, huh?" "Yeah...we got there a little too late. I know that Murray is no saint, just like Chris said, but he would never nearly kill someone just for money and fame.... Would he? Maybe I don't know him anymore..." "But Zachy, you gotta understand! You're not with him anymore, you're not like him. Zachy, you don't need to support him anymore." Taylor sits back on the bench and reflects on what Clyde is saying. He can't separate the fact that they have known each other so long and that Chris helped train him and bring him up when he needed help. Bagwell is the second closest thing to having a father, Father Emanuel being the first. When a man is left to fend for himself when he is left alone by death, who does he turn to? Where does he find the strength to decide between two mentors, two father figures? "Clyde, you are right. But something I wanted to ask you." "Shoot Zachy!" "I'm getting tired. I don't know how much more of this constant contention and fighting I can do anymore. My body is killing me, and my mind isn't doing any better. Black really took it out of me with that shot to the head. I need you pal." "What are you tryin to say?" "I'm trying to say that come Warpath, I don't know if I will be able to retain the title... I need you there. I need you to help me... help me lose it." "WHAT?!”!" "I need you to chant for Ngozi, not me. I want you to get the crowd together..." "I wont do it Zachy." "...so I lose the title to Ngozi. It can't fall into the Movement's hands." "Why do you want to lose?" "I can't take this anymore. My body is killing me Clyde. I don't know how much of this constant work I can take." "I refuse! I won't do it." There is an awkward silence on the phone. Out of respect of Clyde's loyalty, Taylor can't help but be happy at the answer and the reaction that Clyde has given, but on the other hand, it hurts to hear someone fight against him. Maybe it is confusion, maybe just exhaustion talking, but the title needs to be lost, it is just a matter of time and to who. The line in the stone is etched ever so fine, but where do I go? "I am sorry Clyde. Sorry for putting you into this position. But please, please be there for me." "I'll be dare Zachy, I'll be dare..." Clyde starts to get choked up. "I have to go Clyde, I will see you at Warpath..." There is a click on the other end of the phone before any other words are spoken. Taylor bows his head into his lap and starts to hit his head with the phone in hand. His body stiffens up and his hands become tight and sore. He drops his hands and starts to squeeze them to try and release pressure. Nothing ever works, but he relentlessly tries. He looks down at his phone and looks at the call history. "13:01 minutes" Doubled. Fifteen minutes left on the phone. Fifteen more minutes left to talk. Fifteen more minutes to talk himself into more and more confusion. Taylor pushes the leather bound book off his lap, watching it crash to the ground, and he puts his head back into his hands. Visions of the ring come into his mind. An empty ring. There are no fans, no announcers, no wrestlers. An empty arena, something that is seen as a bad sign, or a nightmare to any manager or CEO. The vision is not as peaceful as he thought it would be. The silence digs into his head, making him feel more and more hurt by the site until he can't bare it anymore and throws his eyes open. His eyes are blurred from tears, taking his eyes a minute to push out the salty water of imaginary salvation and refocus. The book lays open on the concrete ground, opened up to the page of numbers. "Father Emanuel". The name flashes out at him. He looks at the number, back tot he phone, and then back to the number. About a half hour left on the phone. That is plenty of time. "Hello?" "Father Emanuel, it is Zacharia." "My son! How pleasant it is to hear your voice and to hear that you are alright. I have been praying for the day in which you would pay me another visit. A phone call is much appreciated, my son!" "I am sorry Father, and I wish you to forgive me. I have not been myself the last couple months, and I want to apologize. I pushed away the very person that I needed guidance from, and I sought it out through someone that turned out to be a mistrusted and misguided soul. I have called for guidance." "I am more than just your priest, Zacharia, I am also your friend. Why don't you come by the church tonight and we will discuss this over some tea or coffee. I am also sorry for what I have said." "That in neither here nor there, Father. I will head over to the church now, I would enjoy a session of guidance and forgiveness." "Alright my son, just hea.... What was that?" "I didn't hear anything?" "There was a loud bang near the lobby of the church. I am walking up now." "Was it maybe something that fell?" "No, it was far too loud for that. Almost like a, a..." [BANG] "Father, that was a gunshot!...Father?...Father?" Taylor yells into the phone, attracting the attention of pedestrians walking by. His screams drown out the noise of the traffic and banter from all around him. He scoops up his bag and book with a swift motion, and starts to run down the street, still screaming into the phone. He pulls the phone away from his ear and notices that the screen says "Call ended" and hits redial. After five rings the phone is picked up. "Father?" [BREATHING] "Father is everything ok? I am heading over now! Father?" [BROKEN BREATHING] "I will be there in a minute Father!" Taylor hangs up the phone and runs around the corner of the street, the park coming into view. The sun starts to set, casting a red/orange tint to everything in the park. The world looks like it is burning. The trees wave in an engulfed way, burning with every change of wind. He runs through the park with amazing speed, running into Central Ave, and up to the church. The Church door is open. Taylor runs to the side of the church to look in the window, and the illuminated church paints a devastating canvas of violence and blood. Father Emanuel lays still in the middle of the isle. Turning his head left and right vigorously, he fails to find anyone in the church. Not even the priest's quarters are open. With the intention that the intruder has already left, he enters the church through the back, entering into the basement. He reaches the spiral staircase that leads from the basement into the back hall of the church, connecting the priest's quarters and the office. Creeping slowly up the stairs, still, nobody is found. The church is as silent as he can always remember it being. There are no signs of destruction or invasion, everything is as quaint as he always remembers. He runs into the church itself, and spots Father Emanuel laying in the isle; no assailant visible. Taylor jumps down the steps from the alter and reaches the priest laying in a bath of blood. He grabs the priest's hand, which stirs the man. "Zacharia, I knew you would come." "Father, you will be alright. Where did the man..." "It is too late for that, he is gone. He took the donations box and left out the front..." "Save your strength Father, I will get help." "It is too late, Zacharia. It is..." "No, I can save you." "Pray for me. That is all that can be done." Baffled by the answer of his mentor, he sits in shock, but then closes his eyes. Tears immediately start to spill over onto his face and onto his cloak. Nothing comes to mind as he sits there in the moment of distress. His mind can only focus on the bleeding Father in front of him. "In Nomeni Patri Et Fili Spiritus Sancti. Lord, if you can touch down with your healing graces and tend to the relentless wounds of your son, Emanuel, in this time of need, I would be very grateful. I do not know where I would be without his guidance and his life. Maybe it is selfish for me to ask to keep someone that you want by your side, but please, God, don't take him. I need him so bad at this time. I need his wisdom. I have been a servant of the Almighty most of my life. I only ask this one favor. Please, hear my prayer. Hear my prayer. Hear my prayer...." The echo of "Hear my prayer" resonates through the church. Father Emanuel takes his last breath and dies in Taylor's arms. Rage and remorse fills his heart. Taylor bows his head in silent prayer and remembrance. A click echoes off the walls behind Taylor. His head becomes clear and focused. His eyes become clean and focused. His breathing slows to a confident pace. His body tenses up to deliver a quick and decisive move. Ready for what is about to happen.... Taylor flings his arm back as quick as possible... [BANG]
