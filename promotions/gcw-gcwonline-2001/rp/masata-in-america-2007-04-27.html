Masata in America!!

27 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27 2007 Friday Morning LAX 6:30 AM. Masata Diaries I am Inaka Masata. I have been invited to compete in GCW as an international exchange. The plane has just landed from a long flight from Tokyo , Japan. I live in the Shibuya district. Nice place for people my age. Very busy and popular for most young people between the ages of 18 and 26. My life revolves around wrestling and the Sengakuji temple. Did you realize that the 47 Ronin are all buried there? It is a place of honour for me. It is a place where I am to become one with the ancestors and heroes of the past. As I sit here writing this I watch as the jetliner pulls to the terminal, is this the first of my American visits? I always ask myself these questions when I come somewhere else. I wonder what this new wrestler named Kyle James is going to be to me. Will he be a challenge or simply someone who gets in the ring and brags? I have been told he is very talented by the GCW officials. He also has been trained by Shaman. He is well known here in Japan for his tours here when he was a free agent. I am not sure if I should be worried because of the gaijin group called Oblivion. I am a champion in my country. I am undefeated in 3 years of wrestling service and as a good will gesture Steven Caldera invited me to come here as a good showing of Japanese talent. In hoping this will be a good tour of honour and talent. Kyle James should know and understand we battle differently in Japan. I should be making more notes of this match, but truthfully these Americans always think Japanese wrestlers are always rice and noodles. I am not a Sumo and I am not some stupid looking Geisha. Rest assured that I will show why I am a Masata. The name is honourable and will be remembered here. LAX 6:55 am. Interviews “Hello GCW faithful I am Cammie Williams awaiting the arrival of Japanese import Inaki Masata in the Cultural exchange wrestling program. Inaki Masata has been the hottest new rising star in Japan. Ok folks I think Inaki Masata is getting ready come up the terminal.” Several Japanese reporters and new media folks rush up to the terminal as Masata walks through them. Security personnel seem to push through the crowd as he makes his way to a podium set up. He steps up and smiles for the camera. “Konichiwa Los Angeles America.” Someone leans over and whispers into his ear and he nods. “My apology I meant to say United States. Customs are different to me. I must work on this.” He looks at Cammie Williams and nods to her. “Cammie Williams from GCW Internet you shall go first.” Ms. Williams steps forward and smiles. “Mr. Masata what has brought you to America” It isn’t everyday a cultural exchange of sorts is so easily done. You are a huge star in Japan and coming here for something like this is a big jump.” The translator leans in and whispers in his ear again as Masata nods and raises his head. “The challenge is great here. I come to test my abilities against American talent so one day they will come and test us. In Japan we usually get the older washed up wrestlers. They are still talented, but they do not have the same drive as the younger and hungrier ones.” The translator leans in once again and whispers into Masata’s ear and Masata looks back and whispers something and then nods his head. “The United States is like the Ryogoku of the world. Ryogoku is the center of the Sumo society of wrestlers in Japan. Just so some of you will understand.” Masata steps away from the podium as he bows to the crowd. He is ushered off towards the left as Steven Caldera is standing clapping his hands together as Masata bows to him and Caldera bows back to Masata. Caldera motions for Masata to walk towards a side door as the cameras are flashing and Masata’s personal translator accompanies him and the door shuts. “This is Cammie Williams GCW internet reporter sounding on the arrival of Inaki Masata The Robotic Freak as he is better known in Japan. More news will be coming as anything arises. We are waiting word from Kyle James about his current match up with Inaki Masata.” Camera fades to black as the GCW logo appears.
