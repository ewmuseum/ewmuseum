Knowing True Terror

26 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 26, 2007 __Start He didn't truly understand the reasoning behind flying back to Philadelphia after Worldwide sixty five. Sure, it was both Daniel and Jay's home but it didn't make much sense to take the flights they did. It would be a few days before they'd have to jet off across the states to their next location. There they'd stay for a few hours and again they'd be on their way home. Whatever Franco's reason for the hassle was, he was quite adamant on keeping it to himself. On the plane, both of the men sat as if they had never seen one another. Although they were seated beside each other, neither of the two turned to their friend to speak. Jay knew exactly why. In some way he had let down his trainer. Daniel Franco was a man of business. In his current occupation he was supposed to aid his client in any way possible to ensure he succeeds in the ring. After Terror's performance against Bradley however, Daniel knew he wasn't performing as great as he should be. Instead of blaming himself for neglecting his pupil he decided it was easier to place any blame on Jay. To say the least, he wasn't too happy to hear how he'd never excel higher in GCW. This brings us to now. As he had last week, the young wrestler stands among his friends in the alley. According to Daniel, he had this week to clean his act up. If he wasn't proficient in his match against Damon Aldair he was supposedly going to be dumped and disowned. With one box of Marlboro 27s and a freshly laundered shirt he casually strolls through the alleyway. The different spots of stench seem to have gathered together to form an odor designed to fry the arm hairs of a man a mile away. Jay could already feel his skin absorbing the stench and as he looked closer he swore he observed his black hairs turn white. 'Well aren't you a catch.' The voice was that of a woman. Her frail words limped toward Talley. For his sake he wished she was looking somewhere behind him. 'Come over here sweetie.' He hadn't noticed how close he was to the woman until her cane tapped his right kneecap. 'Ow bitch! I'm trying to walk here.' He glanced up for a second and noticed it was Caroline standing outside of her alley-famous garden of nails. 'You're quite the feisty one today.' She tapped at his knee again with the wooden rod. Jay looked closer at her lips and saw the set of rotting teeth planted in her fading pink gums. 'I'm just in a hurry.' 'Could you spare a second to help me with something sugar?' 'It depends, will I have to touch you?' 'No, you just...' He interrupts, 'Be anywhere near you?' 'Not exactly, I just need...' Again he cuts her off as he figures out the small details. 'I guess I can help, what do you need help with.' He flashes a false grin her way. After a heavy sigh she picks up again with her shaky words, 'I need you to change the light above my home. It's been flickerin' all week and it's bound to die anytime now.' Jay's eyes scale the wall crawling higher and higher up until he sees the single light bulb tied at the end with a thin wire. He follows the wire up now and finds that it connects on the high end to the power line above. 'You mean that thing up there?' He asks pointing. 'Yeah, that one. I found a new light yesterday on West Third. I reckon someone dropped it.' He ignores her as he tries to figure out how he was going to get up there. His best guess came from the sight of a half broken box and an old rusted waterspout. Both items were about as safe as a drunk on a balcony. On any other day he would have most likely left and continued on his walk. Today, or better yet, this week Terror felt as if he had something to prove. Not just to Daniel but also to himself. If he could replace this bulb he could beat Aldair and keep Franco in his corner. 'Where's the light?' She walks to her fed-ex box-home and rummages around until she comes up with a tiny bulb. She hands it to him and he sets off to play Spiderman. 'I'll catch you if you fall.' Jay looks back at her with a scared look in his eyes as he tucks his nose in and squints his brows. She giggles and he turns back to the wall. Most of the community had their eyes on him now. He places his first foot on the box and feels it sway below him. Quickly he grabs onto the rusty fountain and boosts his other foot up to it. The light bulb sticks tightly to his palm even with the gallons of sweat growing in it. Terror now reaches up for the wire and bulb but finds he isn’t as close as he suspected he'd be. Even on his toes he is short of the diminishing light. 'Don't worry sugar, I got you.' As if the words didn't terrify Jay enough, the stick penetrating his asshole did the trick. He could feel the wooden cane poking up into the back of his pants and taunting his derriere subtly. In the confusion and overall detest he manages to loose footing and finds out that he is higher than he thought. The damp box does little to catch him and unlike she had promised, Caroline was out of sight when he hits the concrete. As his eyes begin to shut he looks up to the light and sees his father. 'You failed again.' He mouthed. Jay Terror closes his eyelids and loses himself in his nightmares. __March of 2002 'Can you do anything right?' Dave Talley spat at his nineteen year old son. Even though he had the power to move out and do as he pleased, there was something keeping Jamison Talley under his father's control. Sometimes he thought it was because he wanted to be a professional wrestler and under his dad's training, he'd be able to achieve that. Other days he knew he wouldn't make it very far on his own. Today was one of the days that he fell between the lines. 'Get up and try it again!' He exclaimed angrily. Jamison fixed his mother's tights that he had been forced to wear and pried his body from the mat. Dave watched over his son like Hitler himself. If he had one more mistake, he was sure the Nazi general would send him to the oven until the skin melted from his bones. 'The top rope?' 'That is what you were trying to do, right?' He says sarcastically, 'Come on; use your head for something other than a hat rack kid!' The young Talley climbed the turnbuckle pads and perched on the top for a moment. It was higher than many people had figured it was when you were up there. Jamison was used to the heights by now however. He sucked in as much air as he could and with his lungs full he leapt forward with a semi-flip. George watched him come toward him and saw he hadn't completed the entire rotation of the flip. He moved to the left and watched his son twist his leg in a rather sick manner. 'AHHHH!' He yelped in pain and squirmed left and right holding onto the limb. His father walked over to him and placed his foot onto Jamison's torso. 'You failed again.' His voice was softer this time. It was a tone his son had come to know very well. The last time he spoke in that way Jamison had been delivered to his front door in a police car. Spray paint covered his shirt telling his father all he needed to know. 'I,' He struggled, '...tried.' 'Not hard enough.' He lifted his foot and walked away disappointed. Jamison felt the pain of his leg lift into his heart where it went on to crush the fragile organ for yet another time in his life. __Present His eyes began to flicker with the introduction of the smell of feet and sewage placed in his face. The blue orbs in the center of his eyes found a pea green gym sock hanging overhead laughing at the face he was making. 'Get it away!' 'Ah good, you're awake!' Jay popped his head up from the ground and saw Sakaya flashing a wide toothless grin. 'That could wake Snow White hombre, ick!' He scraped at his tongue with his fingernails and wiped the fungus onto Caroline's box. 'Are you feeling any better?' 'I would be if I had a cigarette,' He reaches into his jeans pocket and finds that the box of Marlboro's has gone missing. '...what did you do with them?' Sakaya smiles heavier, 'I told you those things would kill you. So...I kinda sold them for this shirt.' He points to a large yellow smiley face on the white background of his shirt. Underneath the cheerful visage are the words "Don't worry be happy." 'No...you couldn't have!' Terror reaches up and grabs Sakaya around his neck. The other's of the community rush to his aid and help their leader out of the chokehold. 'Kill me.' 'If I gave you the cigarettes I would have been doing just that. You should be thanking me you know.' 'I should be stuffing that musty sock down your throat.' He bangs his head on the cardboard. Sakaya looks at his hand and notices he's still holding the item of clothing. 'Maybe later. I'm not in the mood for any greens at the moment. You're ok though?' 'Just go away...' He shuts his eyes and listens to footsteps walking away from him. With that, he tries to find a way to get a steak with potatoes and gravy for the evening. __End
