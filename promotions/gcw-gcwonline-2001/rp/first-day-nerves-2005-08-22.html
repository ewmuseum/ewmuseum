First Day Nerves

22 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd August Today was my first day at GCW. I walked down the long corridors wearing my black sports vest, black jogging bottoms and white trainers, whilst carrying a sports bag containing my wrestling gear. I stopped for second to look at some pictures hanging on the wall, they were past GCW World Champions: The Jhub, Octavian and Jason Dread were amongst them. Some had now gone from GCW, but most were still around. I carried on walking with a tingling in my stomach as I was eager to prove myself in the shadow of those people. It was a huge day for me, as I was just about to make my debut here tonight in front of not only the many fans in the arena, but also the fans all over the world. I stopped outside a door with “Angel” on the front. I pushed the door open and stepped inside the locker room. The room was quite small, but more than enough room to meet my needs. I unzipped my sports bag and took out my wrestling gear. After a few minutes, I was ready to wrestle. The black knee-high wrestling boots were shined up so much, he could see his reflection, and they were laced up. The black knee pads and elbow pads were securely fastened on, and his black and white trunks, with “Angel” on the front and a pair of angel wings on the back, were on. I had already done his warm-ups so my muscles were all stretched out to avoid pulling a muscle. I glanced over at my watch and saw that I had time to spare, so I sat backwards on the fold-up chair with my hands on top of the back part and my head resting on top. That’s when several thoughts rushed into my head at once. I had to prove to the rest of the world that I was the best, and I had to go out there and devour my opponent. Steven Caldera recognised my obvious talent, and now, the rest of the world would. It’ll only be a short matter of time, before I had the GCW World Championship around my waist. The crowd would be chanting my name: “Angel ? Angel ? Angel” Wait a minute, that last “Angel” wasn’t the crowd, it was one of the crew men’s voice through the door. There was another knock. Crew Man: “Angel, you’re up next.” Angel (smiling): “Yeah, I’m coming.” I got up from the chair and headed outside. I couldn’t help smiling, this would be the moment everyone will realise how great I truly am.
