Smuggler's paradise

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A long time ago If you wanted to get away, this was the place to do it. A backwater planet on the edge of the galaxy. The planet offically had no name, and so did most of the people that came here. Well they had names, but the names they used weren't the ones they were born (or hatched or spawned) with. This was the kind of place where you didn't ask questions, unless you wanted to get vaporized or kicked out of the airlock. That was another thing about this planet, it was so far away from it's main star that it was always night, and the surface was colder than anything you can imagine. I've seen the results of what can happen when you get rowdy enough to get thrown out. You get flash frozen and explode, usually both at the same time. The planet was a smugglers paradise and there were no discernable cities. It was a planetwide flea market almost. If you wanted something illegal or even slightly illegal you could get it here. If they didn't have it here, then chances were it didn't exist. It was a series of large domes on the surface interconnected by a series of underground tunnels. You didn't make eye contact here either no matter how many eyes you had. That would get the business end of a blaster aimed at your skull (or wherever your brains were located). After my run in with the Maruder and Eva a while back, I'd been staying one step ahead of them. Now I was here, if I could get away from him anywhere it would be here. He wouldn't show his no-face here, unless he wanted even less of a face. I sat in a booth in a smoky bar. No one looked at me and I didn't look at them. At least not for too long, I kept scanning the room looking for my contact. He was supposed to be here fifteen minutes ago. Hopefully the idiot didn't get himself killed. Nope. There he is. I waved him over. I sipped at my drink and winced as it burned it's way down my throat. Damn this stuff was good. He eased his large bulky hairy body into the booth sitting opposite me. He barked something at a passing watress, she nodded and headed back to the bar. He barked at me. 'You know I can't speak your language. Speak english.' I said. He laughed. 'Fine. Did you get it?' I nodded, 'Damn near cost me my life Wild. If you weren't paying me 20 grand for it I woulda told you to get it your damn self.' He nodded his large hairy head. 'Yeah, but you know I can't stand that planet, who in their right mind wants to live on a planet with three suns?' 'You'd be surprised.' I said having been there and seen the beings that called it home. 'Anyway, my money.' He chuckled, the waitress returned with something brown and fizzing, placed it in front of Wild and looked at me, I killed the last of my drink and ordered another one. As soon as she was gone he produced a flat, thin square box, four inches long on each side. He pulled a stylus from the bottom of it and started pressing icons on the touch screen. He turned it to me, and I in turn used the stylus to press a few icons. I watched as the 20,000 was transfered to an account of mine under an assumed name. 'Now my merchandise.' he said tucking the data pad away. I paused as the waitress came back with my drink. As soon as she was gone I reached under the table and pulled out a metal lock box, and pressed my thumb to the lock. It hummed and clicked open. He reached inside and pulled out a smaller box. I tucked the larger one back under the table. He opened the second box and grinned. It was a very unsettling sight, kind of like seeing your grandmother naked. 'Nice.' he said, 'You sure this is authentic?' 'I nearly got my ass blasted off on that damn hot planet and you have the nerve to ask me if it's authentic. I killed six people to get my hands on that! You're damn right it's authentic.' 'Calm down,' he said, 'I wouldn't have hired you if I didn't think you could get the job done.' 'It wasn't easy.' I said, 'Not just getting away from those Duners, but I got a Marauder on my tail.' His eyes widened and if he had eyebrows I suspect they would have gone up. 'A Marauder?' 'Not just any Marauder. Lex.' 'You sure?' 'How many Maruaders do you know that are bounty hunters and have StarJammer class ships?' I asked, looking around just to make sure the no-faced bastard hadn't followed me. 'Just one.' he said, 'What's he on your ass for?' I pulled the medallion from my tunic. 'This.' He whistled. An impressive feat for a being that didn't have lips. 'Where'd you get that? I haven't seen that in years.' he said. 'How much you want for it?' 'You can't afford it Wild.' I said tucking it back. 'I took it from Eva VL. She wasn't too happy about it, but them's the breaks. No one's getting this from me unless they pry it from my cold dead hands.' 'Well if you've got Lex on your tail, that very well could happen.' 'Marauders are bounty hunters, not killers. You want someone brought in alive, you hire a Marauder. You want them dead, you hire a Dark One. You want them dead or alive, you hire any one else. Someone wants me alive, they sent him. He almost got me not too long ago.' 'But you escaped.' 'I didn't get these cybernetic implants because they were cool.' 'You should get those updated.' 'Updated? Are you kidding me? You have any idea how hard it was to find someone to give me these implants in the first place. I don't exactly run in the high and mighty social circles. I'm a smuggler for fuck's sake. I was lucky I could find a doctor on any planet in this galaxy that was willing to implant the fucking things in me after I stole them.' I said. 'So where you off too now?' he asked finally taking a sip of his drink which had stopped fizzing. 'I don't know.' I said, also paying attention to my own drink, taking a sip before speaking again. 'I hear the Northern sector is nice this time of year.' 'Yeah, just watch out for the fallout. I hear it's a bitch to get through.' 'I've been through worse.' I said. He polished off his drink in three huge gulps, dropped some credits on the table and stood. 'I'll see you later Mike.' He was one of the few beings in the galaxy who knew my real name. 'Later Wild. Call me if you need any other rare items stolen.' He chuckled again, 'You know I will.' He left and I saw him exchange an angry glance with a Dark One in the corner. I shook my head and went back to my drink. I was planning a visit to the Northern sector of the galaxy, there were some nice planets over there. Most of them tropical. But he was right, the fallout zone you had to fly through was a bitch. If you didn't have nerves (or implants) of steel you could get seriously fucked up flying through it. But not me. I was half machine after all.
