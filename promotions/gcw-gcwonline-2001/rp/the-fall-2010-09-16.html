The Fall

16 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/16/10 “Oh god it hurts!” Laid sprawled out across a makeshift bed of tissue paper and an ugly orange pad, her knee swollen beyond recognition, Tessa Windsor was certainly not a sight for sore eyes. “I’m going to KILL that fucking bandana wearing piece of shit!” Or her husband’s eyes either for that matter. Under most pretenses Tessa would have took in a deep breath, told her husband to relax, and then followed through her own advice as she exhaled. Tonight was a whole new ballgame though. So new that she hardly knew her husband was even there. “Don’t touch it, please!” Her teeth clenched together tightly as she caught the smallest of the nurses hovered around her reaching back out towards her leg. Tessa might not have been able to move her knee but her hands still had plenty of power behind them. They were the only means of protection Tessa had left. She swatted helplessly at the only nurse gutsy enough to get within arm’s reach. “Please don’t!” Tessa plead again. The nurse ignored all of it, even the tears. “It hurts! SHIT, STOP!” It was too much for Chris Storm to take. He groaned, knowing Tessa hated a scene, even more so when he lost his temper. To him having to stand around helplessly and watch all of this play out was the equivalent of asking a group of five year olds to stand silently in line for hours on end. It simply was not happening is this lifetime. “What the fuck is wrong with you? Don’t you hear her?” Intuitively his fists balled up as if he were taking on the entire roster on his own. Instead he chased back a trio of women hardly any bigger in stature than his bite sized wife.”Don’t touch it.” “Sir.” “I said leave her.” “She’s…” “….in pain? No shit! You think you prodding her is going to make that any better?” “Mr. Storm.” Trying to cut back in was futile, especially when it came to Tessa. “The names Scott.” “Mr-” “No, it’s just Scott. And I’ll call you when she’s ready.” “But…” He reaffirmed even more boldly. “When she’s ready, I’ll call. Got it?” “She needs…” “…time to herself.” He cut back in firmly without any form of regard for their profession.”I can assure you that out of everyone here right now, I’m going to be the one acting on her best interest and I’m sure as hell not going to put her anywhere near harm’s way.” “Your wife…”She stopped mid sentence, heeding the plea made by Scott’s index finger. Under most circumstances, the many known to the GCW as Chris Storm would have lost his temper by then. He hated standing around helpless, absolutely hated it. But after everything his wife had been through tonight, he figured the least he could do was spare her the little pride she had left. She would have done the same for him without question. “Twenty minutes.”Scott bit down on his lip. He might not have ever admitted it, but right about now he was nothing more than a desperate man merely looking to save his ailing wife. “That’s all I ask, twenty minutes.” “Fine then.” Even as Scott towered over her by nearly a foot, the brunette woman standing before him showed little intimidation. Her opinion on the matter was plenty clear, the scowl on her face help tell it all. “Twenty minutes.” “Twenty minutes.” He nodded, assuring with a nod and the slightest of smiles. “Not a second more.” His head turned back Tessa’s way the moment the nurse rounded the corner. It took but only one look at the tear drenched bangs that caked her face for him to realize what he was up against. Twenty minutes would not be even remotely enough to save his wife from the emotional battle about to be waged within her heart. ***** Once upon a time, I imagined what it would be like to be on top of the world. There were masses of fans standing from their seats to cheering me on; the consistent flashing of camera’s illuminating the arena, pure adrenaline pumping through my body, and most importantly the love and support of my peers, the ones who helped me through this incredible journey. Once upon a time, I lived through all that. I walked out with my head held high. I survived. And then, as I took my first steps, I learned what happens when fate decides none of that meant to be. Once upon a time I fell so hard I swore I’d never get back up. I failed. It was the end. ….or so I thought. ***** “Thanks.” It was a sentiment he heard so little in the context of wrestling that Scott Drake had to make sure he was hearing that right. “What’d you say?” “Thanks.” His wife meekly answered, not really sure what to make of the situation herself. “For chasing them off. I needed that.” He shrugged. He really had not a clue what to say. Usually she loathed the confrontations and commotion the followed her husband around. While she hated the press, she dealt with them. The medical office? Certainly not her favorite place, but she always tried to tough it out. Tonight she was beside herself though. Tonight, she needed privacy. She needed tranquility. She needed someone who could clear her mind of everything that just transpired before her very eyes, and she knew Scott Drake could do that. Tonight, she welcomed his defense. She knew it would be the only thing that would help get her through the rest of the night. “You okay?” “Would you really believe me if I said yes?” “No.” He grinned as he looked back her way. “So spare yourself straining yourself to say it.” She smiled for the first time since she had been wheeled to the back. His charm always had a way of winning her over even in the worst of times. She peered down at her knee, swollen, bruised, and beaten to the point where she swore it looked like a whole new body part had grown on attached to it. “Sh-“ She stopped herself short, “I’m screwed, aren’t I?” “Would you believe me if I said no?” He heckled. This time her smile dissipated. It probably would have been a lot more charming if Tessa had some kind of evidence she would make it out of this okay. “It’s never hurt this bad, ever.” Her eyes remained glued to her knee as if she remained transfixed on it long enough it would suddenly heal itself. Her pessimism made that all the more unlikely. “I’m so screwed.” Scott propped himself down on the table beside her head. His fingers traced up her cheek, stopping over her eyes. He might not have been able to clear her mind, but he hoped he could at least deter it back to him, or something a little less depressing. “You’ll be fine, I promise.” Cliché? Yes. True? Probably not. But it worked. “I know.” It sounded far from reassuring. Tessa always had been a horrid liar. Scott knew it, but he was not going to waste his time calling her out on it. Tonight had already been grueling enough on her. “You’ll make sure of it.” “You just need to promise me you’ll take it easy for a bit.” Scott was sure to chime in. He swore up and down that one day his wife’s reluctance to miss even a beat was going to kill him. For all he knew, this could be it. Her lips curled in. Saying yes would be like signing her life away. Instead she shrugged. “We’ll see.” “Please?” Before he could get an answer, a voice from behind the door chimed in. “Alright, times up.” Any second now Scott knew they would be barraged by the medical staff who would more than likely chase him out of the room for sake of their own concentration. He tried again, “Please Tess?” She waved him off. Scott Drake was far from a religious man, but tonight the only thing he wanted to do was pray. His wife needed it. He needed it. ***** I watched as the person I love the most climbed for years and years. Dedication, sacrifice, and commitment were daily struggles for her. And then, after so many years, she finally got to the top of her mountain. I could not have been any more proud of her. I knew the feeling, though I know I never appreciated as much as she did. And then, just like that, she fell. My perfect little angel had bent wings. It broke my heart. The cost it took to get to the top seemed to crush her and I never noticed. I covered my eyes, turned the other way. Maybe I was just as much to blame for her downfall as anyone. But I wasn’t going to let it be the end. Not if I had any power over it.
