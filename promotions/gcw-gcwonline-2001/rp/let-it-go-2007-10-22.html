Let it go

22 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 Oct 07 “I’m not exactly sure why you are letting this get to you so much.” “Because I fucking am okay Alex?” “Look I know it’s not an easy pill to swallow but?” “Listen I’m not in the mood for any of your bullshit pep talks.” The words escaped my throat before I could even think about bringing them back. The words were also so cold that I feared I might have frost bite to my esophagus. I don’t mean to snap at Alex but I’m also incredibly irritated. I feel bad slightly ? but I’d never tell him that. Instead I keep my gaze away from him and on the DVD in my right hand and the rolled up magazine in the other. I didn’t have to look at him to realize he wasn’t happy with my attitude. “Well Roberto I don’t really give a damn what you want to hear.” He moves closer to me, knowing full well he had a great chance of pushing me further over the edge. “Sometimes you NEED to have some reason. You take every loss way too tough and you’ve allowed this ignorant fool of a columnist get under your skin. Let it go!” So I do. By smashing the DVD on the floor then smashing it beneath my boot. I turn and glare at Alex. “There I’ve let it go.” Alex shakes his head and folds his arms over his chest. “Listen my friend and listen good. You got tossed out of a ring by a fluke chain of events. I mean I don’t even know how it is physically possible to do what Vivica did to eliminate you. Yes you lost your shot at the World Title at NC-17 but it is now up to you to earn another shot.” I take a deep breath, attempting to relax and release all of the tension in my body. I’ve a chance at the greatest prize in this business to Vivica fucking Valentine. Again. I think that’s what is pissing me off the most about this whole ordeal. First that little bitch rolls me up and squeaks out a win at Warpath, before promptly going and showing she didn’t belong against Jordan White in the Finals. Now THIS ? a one armed woman against the greatest force Global Championship Wrestling has ever seen. How the hell did she win? How did I allow it to happen? “I can already see her gloating at WorldWide Alex and it makes my stomach churn. I have watched her since she arrived and she has never once done anything too impressive. Holding onto the United States title for a record time, in a watered down pool of contenders is hardly proof of her excellence. Now she benefits from coming into the battle royal late and stealing a victory right from under more deserving people’s grasp.” I can feel my face contorting into a look of pure disgust. “Fuck Vivica Valentine!” “Stop feeling sorry for yourself and make it right!” Alex spits back. “It wasn’t THAT long ago that you were NOTHING Roberto. Have you really come so far that you think you are unbeatable or that you are owed a victory every time you step into the ring?” “No Alex you know that.” “Then stop pretending like it is the end of the world every time you drop one. Maybe if you took the time to figure out why you lost instead of bitching about it and,” He leans down, picking up some of the piece of shattered DVD. “Get to learning what happened instead of destroying the evidence as if it never happened; you won’t have to suffer bullshit losses. You won’t have to worry about seeing someone like Valentine stand victorious.” God Damn Alex. No matter how pissed off I ever am or how low I believe I’ve sunk he’s there to help place things back into perspective. I smile a little inwardly but never let it show on the outside. “You know Alex?” Then it hits me. Yeah I appreciate Alex’s advice and his ability to keep things in perspective but why do I even need it in the first place? Once upon a time I didn’t rely on anyone but myself. Now I rely on far too many people and let far too many others dictate where my life is heading. Mary Alex Caldera Valentine Jordan In one way or another all of them have chosen a path for me instead of ME making my own. “What Roberto?” Alex’s voice brings me back to the conversation. I blink a few times before really snapping back. “Things just have to change.” I glide by Alex and grab my coat before heading to the door. “Where the hell are you going?” He asks. I stop and turn to my friend. “There’s a gnat that needs to be brushed away.” I throw the magazine I was holding down at the feet of Alex. He looks down briefly then looks back up at me and smirks. I don’t smile back. There’s no room for smiling anymore. ______________________________________________________ “Fuck.” Yeah it’s about all I can get out really. After sitting in front of a computer screen for a couple of hours and having nothing to show for it, there’s really not much TO say actually. I feel around to my right for my bottle of Corona but surprise, surprise it’s empty. “Fuck.” Yeah it’s appropriate in this case too. I look at the blank page of Microsoft Word and with each passing moment it seems it’s mocking me more and more. The steady blinking of the cursor appears to gain a life of its own and I can almost hear the laughter coming from inside. If I could reach in and strangle it I would ? but that’d make me crazy wouldn’t it? “Alex?” The voice from behind me startles me and I spin around quickly, my face probably showing more of the frightened boy inside of me than I really want it to. When I realize its only Sarah, my editor I relax. “Damn Sarah you scared me.” She chuckles softly as she moves closer to my desk. “Sorry, I didn’t even know anyone was still here.” “Neither did I,” I finally let a smile out. “Having trouble with this week’s article.” “Yeah I can see that,” She nods in the direction of my computer. “Not even a title yet. Deadline’s tomorrow.” “Yeah I know.” I grumble. “Nothing jumps out at me.” “They just had a huge show Alex, Dangerous Games was a success. How can you not have something?” “That’s just it. That’s what everyone would be doing right now. A review of the big show but to me that’s overdone. We all know it was a good show so why exactly should I go over it again” Besides,” I smirk. “It’s not controversial enough.” She smiles again and shrugs her shoulders. “Well Alex you can always do what I do when in doubt.” “Which is?” “Poke holes in the credibility of an established star or stars and wait for the e-mails to come pouring in. That’ll give you articles for weeks. The readers aren’t stupid, well most of them aren’t. They can fuel you like nobody else really can. I’m sure you got plenty of praise and hate mail from that LeStatt piece you wrote.” “You aint kiddin”!” I say a bit TOO loud. “You’d be amazed at how many people still love that guy even if he’s one of the biggest pricks in GCW.” “Sounds like you have your article then.” I nod. “Maybe I do.” “Alright well make sure to lock up when you’re finished, I’m outta here. Good luck with that article.” “Alright, thanks Sarah.” “No problem.” I watch as she walks out of the office and disappears around the corner. Damn she has a tight ass. I’d fuck her. “Concentrate Al.” I tell myself. I turn my attention back to the screen and begin to type. “Heroes and Villains: Is there really a difference?” BY Alejandro Fonseca AKA Sero Blake The world of wrestling has seen many faces, gimmicks, men, women, and personalities come and go over the years. For the most part however we break these people up into two categories: Heels and Faces or to break it down further ? Heroes and Villains. Once upon a time the line between the two was bold and black, no mistaking who was who and what was what. Today things are different however. We are in a world where fans can boo a man when he walks down the aisle but respect and perhaps even admire him when the cameras are not rolling. Take last week’s piece I wrote about LeStatt Knight. You’d think that the majority of the feedback from that article would be AGAINST Knight. No one is as arrogant, as vicious or as cold as that man ? people would LOVE to see him fail yes? The fans want to see his downfall? Well in the case of the bastardly LeStatt Knight, the answer is a resounding “Forget something?” I say outloud. My writing is interrupted by the sound of the door behind me swinging back open. The footsteps quickly move towards me but Sarah doesn’t answer my words. I swing around quickly. “What the?” “Hello Mr. Blake, it has been awhile.” Slowly I stand back up. “Mr. Caldera ? wha-what are you doing here?” “I’ve come to make you an offer son.” I catch him as he looks down at my article, briefly reading it over. Then back up to me. “An offer you cannot refuse.” “Yeah?” He smiles. The devil’s smile. “Yeah ? Let me welcome you back to GCW.” “GCW” I can’t wrestle anymore Mr. Caldera.” “Oh not as a wrestler, I want you to do exactly what you are doing here. Just for more money and strictly for GCW ? more specifically me.” “So there’s a catch.” He laughs. The devil’s laugh. “Isn’t there always?” There is indeed but do I really want to make a deal with the devil? Again? “Fuck.” I whisper to myself. Again it’s appropriate here too.
