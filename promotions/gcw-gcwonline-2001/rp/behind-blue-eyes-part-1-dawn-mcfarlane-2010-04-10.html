I'm reminded every time I walk outside and reveal myself to the world, feeling naked and exposed. Allowing anyone with a soapbox to take their shots on me, but I'm supposed to be the better person and take it on the chin without saying a word. Like any fighter I can only take so many shots to the chin before I'm knocked out, and like fighters before me I feel the best defense is a good offense. When you start landing offense, it's amazing how fast the world turns on you. Suddenly at all of five foot, four inches tall I'm a bully pushing around men twice my size. Paying no attention to the mental and verbal abuse that I've endured over the past few months, people refuse to point the fingers at themselves. The real world doesn't accept me anymore, and it again leaves me feeling much like I did as a teenager. Alone. Emotional. Confused. Yet the iconic words of The Who ring louder to me personally than any other time in my life. I've found myself doing what I did as a teenager, submerging myself into a world full of music. A world where people understand me and speak to me on a level I can understand. Music has always been the biggest part of my life outside of professional wrestling. Songs can move someone, change their opinions, bring tears to their eyes, or even clinch their fists in anger. Music makes you feel alive. Inside of a mosh pit, I'm no longer Vivica J. Valentine... a fallen phenom martyred by the masses for her "new found outlook on life." I'm just a girl who wants to feel moved. A girl who wants to feel alive. I'm anonymous in a crowd of people just like me. I feel like I belong. I'm at peace. I'm home. That's what brings me here to some hole in the wall club on the outskirts of Hollywood, California. Waiting in a dark and crowded room full of people I've never met before for a band I've never heard of. The musk of humanity wafted through my nose and sweat dripped from my brow. It was enough to make a girl want to gag, but this kind of uncomfortable feeling at its roots is what music is all about. Taking a chance on something new, hoping to share an experience with a hand full of people you've never met for the rest of your life. These are the things your most cherished memories are made of. As the lights came on, the thunderous applause from the audience kicked my heart into high gear. The bile I felt rising up the back of my neck suddenly sub-sided as he took the stage from the left. On my tip toes I managed to catch a glimpse over the sasquatch that stood in front of me. His hair was a dulling red, dressed in a black leather jacket over a sweat-glistened yet pasty white chest. The bristles of his goatee swept across the microphone as he brought it up to his lips... "Thank you. We are Jesse Torrent and The Pack, and we're happy to be here tonight all the way from British Columbia way up north." His voice was that of a leader, he bought a natural charisma and stage presence with him to the masses. People felt compelled to follow as the rest of the band took their places behind him, going mostly unnoticed to everyone but myself. Someone else on stage had grabbed my attention in a startling fashion. I made eye-contact with a girl near the back of the set, her green eyes almost stopped the entire world around me. Those are eyes that I'd recognize from anywhere. Jade and judgmental, beneath bright orange hair dyed blue they couldn't hide from me. It was the piercing gaze of a Steel Rose. Those peepers belonged to Dawn McFarlane. "AN-I-MAL! AN-I-MAL! AN-I-MAL!" The chant was just a background noise to my tunnel-visioned stare at my former stablemate. Things between Dawn and I were always rocky at best, but at the end of the day she was supposed to be my friend. She was supposed to have my back. Yet like everyone else outside of Lia Ambrosi when the time came she abandoned me. Dies Irae came and went, and I'd never seen or heard from her again. "I see some shirts out in the audience that a certain lady on stage will appreciate, just for you guys tonight we're going to play a little song you might recognize." Sometimes fate has a funny way of bringing people together when they least expect it. A smirk crossed my face as it seemed like the drumsticks of the shirtless man next to her raised up and struck the canvas drum tops in slow motion. Perfect Dark was looking squarely back at me like she'd seen a ghost as I forced my way to the front of the crowd, pissing off a behemoth along the way. Just in time to hear the opening lyric of her old entrance theme, I wondered to myself... Nothing was there. As her heart-rate began to slow she noticed a white curtain lofting in and out with the breeze. Breaths returned to normal. Eyes became fixated in the darkness with the assistance of a faint moonglow. She could feel the wind against her neck as she pushed the blankets aside to air herself out. Sweat dripped down her brow as she now sat on the side of the bed wearing nothing but a pink cami and a pair of black fleece pajama bottoms. The silence was a welcome one. A break from the constant hustle and bustle of Zenith Manor and the dreadful GCW road schedule was just what she needed to get a good night's sleep and finally focus on her wrestling career. Becoming a wrestler was her first priority. No matter how much the fans cheered her throwing her body randomly at various opponents hoping not to die, she knew that it was no way to make a living. One day she was going to miss. One day she was going crash and burn. Then just like that, all her dreams... her destiny would be gone like sand through her fingertips. After all, that is what woke her up tonight. Sheer acute pain. Her body spasmed so hard that it almost felt like someone reached out and grabbed her. She just needed to get her mind off of it. Maybe a little walk to loosen up the joints would do the trick. "Fuck..." She blurted out as she forced her rapidly aging body off of the mattress. They call them the 'every day aches and pains' in the business. Yet to her the pain was almost intolerable. Wincing with each step she could feel every bone in her body crack in unison. Muscles throbbed in places that she didn't even know she had muscles, and on top of that there were certain parts of her body that no longer moved in the direction they were supposed to. Evidence of this was her near inability to turn her own doorknob to leave her room. She had to use her left hand to turn her right wrist while her other hand was shaking the doorknob uncontrollably. With a loud screech the door did swing open, showing the age of the place she now called home. One by one, each thudding limp made the maple hard wood crack underneath her. Walking across floors older than her grandparents, she often wondered how this place managed not to collapse under itself. Up ahead in the distance, the image before her cerulean blue eyes practically answered the question for her. "Alexis?" She groaned out a sound that at least resembled her voice. Eyes barely open behind matted locks of baby blue and blonde. Even without the makeup and style, she managed to keep a girlish charm about her. A natural attractiveness you might say, even if she walked like Igor from the Frankenstein movies. "Miss Valentine." Alexis responded immediately, chirpy as usual. Even at 4:30am she managed to act like the inevitable grasp of exhaustion didn't phase her. "What are you doing awake at this hour?" "Pain." She replied, "How do they all deal with this?" "You mean Dawn or the others?" "All of them." Vivica simply shook her head thinking about it, "They always seem so invincible." "Well Dawn obviously isn't like the others, they're a bit special." Alexis smirked, "Dawn has her moments, I'll just say I've seen chinks in the armor." "Could've fooled me." The sarcasm in her voice was very evident. Alexis looked off to the side, almost as if she didn't even acknowledge the sarcasm. She knew though. Everyone knew. Outside of Shadow she was quite the unwelcome guest in her own home. "I thought she was perfect." "No, no..." Alexis managed to maintain a smile, "Not at all." With those words Alexis kept moving forward, going about her business without a second a spare. Her job might be the hardest in the household. Anything and everything that happened in the Estate was by her design and with her direction. The job paid well, but not nearly enough for the hours upon hours she'd put in. There were no sick days or vacations for her. She'd had to move in years earlier just to keep up. Away from a life that she might not have been so willing to leave. "Alexis." Vivica looked over her shoulder, grimacing as she turned her body to lock eyes with one of the only two people in the house she may consider a friend. "Yes Miss Valentine?" "Where is Dawn, anyway?" The question was one Alexis was already instructed to answer very specifically. "Dawn is very busy." Alexis repeated, "She can't be disturbed." "Why?" That response, Alexis didn't expect so much. "Why... what?" Confused, the keeper of the household took a few steps forward towards The Black Mariah. "Why is she always so busy? Why can't she ever be disturbed? Why are things the way they are instead of the way they're not?" "Well, she has a lot going on..." "So do you." Vivica answered, "So do I. So does everyone else. She makes time for Shadow, she makes time for Virgil... hell she even makes time for Markus. But never for us." "When you do what I do for a living, Miss Valentine... you come to expect it." Alexis' answer was simple. "It's a thankless job." "Well thank you, Alexis." Vivica spoke, "Thank you for not making faces when I showed up at the doorstep. Thank you for not begrudgingly showing me around the place, not even attempting to cover up the fact that you didn't want me here." "You're welcome, Miss Valentine." A smile crossed her face, but not the plastic one she wore twenty-four hours a day. A real one. A smile as genuine as the expensive artwork that lined the hallways. "I know how hard it is feeling like a stranger in someone else’s home. I hope someday that you'll feel like it is your own." Those words brought the slightest bit of hope to the soul of a girl who'd later be dubbed The Fearless Phenom. In the eyes of her stablemates all she'd ever wanted was to fit in and be respected. Grow up to be something more than the valet who used to follow Christian Zenith around, and develop her own legacy. With time the legacy would come, but being part of the team never would. ---- The shock back to reality was always a harsh one after a good concert, and while the faint scent of Mary Jane still lingered in the air I too had to come to terms with the fact that my experience was now over. Screeching guitars and melodies still played through my head, lovingly as I looked around at my surroundings trashed and scattered by the masses. How fitting that after everything that's transpired in the past few months I find myself standing here alone. Well, mostly. The so-called staff were walking around picking up water bottles, beer bottles, and whatever filth the fans left behind before they trampled outside to go on with their lives. Maybe I was still here because I wasn't willing to let go. I wasn't willing to step outside and face the fact that my life... my dreams have become a movie in which everyone is a critic. Constantly jumping on their soapboxes to give a more emphatic thumbs down than the last. Thought provoking stuff, right? The truth was that I couldn't get those eyes out of my mind. Those condescending corneas that peered back at me, if just for a second. I know she saw me. I know she had something she wanted to say. I could read it on her face. After all these years, I guess I finally found out what happened to Dawn McFarlane. She became successful, just like she was at everything else... doing the exact same thing I would probably be doing with my life if my wrestling career was over. But I'm not like her, I would never just simply walk away. Even with the things I've been forced to go through, I've learned more than ever that this was really my only option in life. No family, no friends, no lovers... just me and the squared circle. My career, no, my legacy is the only thing left that matters to me. When I lace up those boots and walk out to the music I know without a shadow of a doubt that this is what I was born to do. The ring is all I have. I'd never quit. That's what makes me better than her. Walking up to the stage, the roadies were wrapping up wires and turning off equipment. Quickly running around like a dance, weaving in and out of each other in order to pick up in the quickest fashion possible. They lived on the road, just like me. Some of them may have families, friends, maybe even children but when you live your life on the road... the unfortunate truth was that all of those things were second. That's why the people would never really understand me. No matter how I explain it they'll never be able to feel like I do about what I do. Even Andy Murray of all people should understand that he's second best. My love for a man will forever come second to my drive to be a champion. It's a cold and bitter truth that someday everyone will have to learn for themselves. Murray too will be forced to stand in that ring one day and have to choose. He'll make the same decision. The people will prove their ignorance. They'll go up and arms. I'll be backstage to tell him I told him so. "Do you need something ma'am?" A deep voice broke me away from my daydream, completely zoned out from my surroundings. Blinking a few times I looked back at him, wearing a jean jacket and sporting a bandana. I don't think he could be more stereotypical. "I..." With hesitation I was almost afraid to say what I've been thinking all along. Why is closure so important to me? It'll just end up in another fight. It wasn't worth my time... "I need to speak with Dawn." "McFarlane?" The man answered me with an assholish smirk, "You're joking, right?" "No, I'm serious." I replied, wanting to smack that dick right across the face. Why the fuck would I be joking? "She's busy." He answered getting up, "It's best you move along. We got shit to do and we can't be bothered right no..." "Vivica?" A voice interrupted Scruffy McRedneck before he could even finish. Looking past his 80's style super mullet I saw the same eyes I'd looked into so many times before. I guess I got my wish, now the question is... what do I even have to say? ---- A drink of water and then it's off to bed. That was the plan anyway, because the more she looked around the more frustrated she'd become. Pictures of Dawn McFarlane and her many awards lined the halls side-by-side with hand-crafted artwork made by the finest artists in the world. A fitting tribute to herself, one day Vivica hoped to create the same with a tad of humility. That word was severely lacking within the McFarlane Estate. It surely needed a dose of it. With each step the boards under her creaked announcing her arrival as she drew near the bathroom door. Her steps became easier and easier as she fought through the agonizing pain to stretch out her body. Plodding thuds became graceful squeaks, and then a squawk as she forced her body to a stop. A bright light shone from under the bathroom door, slightly burning her pupils now adjusted to the dark and brooding hallways. Reaching out for the doorknob, she went to grasp the cold brass before suddenly there was nothing there but an eruption of light. "Fuck that's bright..." Vivica exclaimed, shielding her eyes with her right arm as a silhouette of a woman appeared before the light went away with a click. Blinking a few times, she tried to adjust back to the darkness to see who it was exactly standing before her... "You get used to it." The woman spoke, using a voice instantly recognizable for Vivica. It belonged to Dawn McFarlane. “Speaking seeing the light, it's about time you finally left that crapbird Christian and came to your senses like I did back in Global." 'He wasn't always the way he is now...' Vivica responded, making excuses for her actions. Trying to vindicate herself in the eyes of the Mistress Of The Mat wasn't an easy feat, but she was sure as hell trying. "I'm sure he has Viv, you've just been too blinded by power and wealth. I used to think like you, I used to think Christian was a brilliant man. The more I was around him, the more I realized how much of a joke he really was. Nothing more than a paranoid, insecure man compensating for it with lots of money." Dawn's lecture never seemed to end, 'I've been trying to tell you for over a year now, you just wouldn't listen to me would you?" There was no response to her question, just an irritated glare from the eyes of The Black Mariah. It seemed like every conversation she tried to have with Dawn turned into a petty display of one-upsmanship. The condescending expression across the face of the Steel Rose told the story as Vivica sealed her lips, thinking it best to say nothing at all. "Got nothing to say then?" Dawn said with a bit of a smile before she walked away, "Maybe you should open your ears a little more often. You'd be surprised what you can learn if you closed your mouth long enough to do it." "Just get out of my way..." Vivica said before marching past the head of the household and into the bathroom. Like oil and water, the likes of Vivica J. Valentine and Dawn McFarlane never quite seemed to mix. Valentine in-particular had spent nearly her entire career standing next to the woman now returning to her business with a triumphant swagger down the hallway, but the rift had always been there. It was more than just two girls being catty, in their life absolutely everything turned into a competition, and Vivica would lose every single time. Flipping the light switch back on, The Black Mariah shook her head looking at her reflection in the mirror before spinning the water on. If it wasn't for Lia, she wouldn't hesitate for a second to cock back and blast that bitch right in the face with a right hook... but she knew what that'd do to her best friend. A fight in the house would be devastating and go against everything Dies Irae was trying to accomplish. Until then Vivica just had to do what she'd been doing... Eat her crow and move on with her life, hoping that through some miracle of God one day Dawn McFarlane and herself would see eye to eye. Unfortunately that day wasn't now, and tomorrow didn't look good. ---- I felt almost like a child on the first day of school as Dawn walked across the stage towards me with water bottle in hand. Her blue hair reminded me a lot of the hair style that I used to have, during my first United States Title reign. Come to think of it that was the last time I'd seen her. Funny how history has a way of repeating itself, no matter how hard you try and change it. I couldn't help but smile as she jumped down to a sitting position on the side of the stage, looking back at me. I could almost immediately tell that something was very.... different about her. "I've got it...Trevor." It took Dawn a moment to remember his name, he wasn't her regular crew. The same green eyes that had stuck so firmly in my mind flicked over the Fearless Phenom with interest, picking out details on a girl Dawn hadn't seen in...she didn't even know. Years. "Hey kiddo." She smiled, offering the water bottle she was holding. "How's tricks?" "They're.... fine." Vivica tried to make conversation without tipping too much of her hand. For years the only think she ever wanted was for this woman to give her the time of day, for this woman to respect her and here she was. Years in the making, this was quite possibly her last chance. "Been busy, not sure if you've kept up or not since Lia left." "I've been pretty much solidly out of the business for years, except for some teaching down at Cloud Nine. You still with that place?" To say Dawn had hard feelings about her time in GCW would have been something of an understatement. "I am. It has it's ups and downs... but it's home." Vivica was acting a bit out of character, showing some restraint as she chose her words wisely, "I've put in too much time to turn back now, you know? I've left my mark, I have to seal the deal. Without GCW my life would be..." It was like talking to herself in the mirror again. Wasn't there a time when she'd felt the same way? Never give up? Never walk away? It didn't stop a little snark from creeping in though. "...much better?" She suggested, with a wry grin. "Doubt it." Vivica answered, peering back at her former stablemate a bit defensively, "I've got to travel the world, see places I've only dreamed of. It's not really in my personality to up and quit." "Not even when it was in your best interests." Dawn added, nodding and taking a sip of her water. It was a strong memory of Viv, always determined to bull right ahead and never give up. It was as endearing as it was maddening. "How's it working out for you then?" "I'm the United States Champion." A smile crossed the face of The Bulletproof Blonde as she finally found her opening to gloat, purposely avoiding telling Dawn about her humiliating defeat at the hands of Andy Murray, "Won the World Title off of Lia a few years back, broke her longest title reign. It's been good. Finally got that monkey off of my back." Vivica went to reposition herself and winced as she tweaked her wrist the wrong way. She tried to play if off, hoping Dawn wouldn't notice. Dawn couldn't help an awed whistle, smiling at her former stablemate. "Wow, now that's something to be proud of. You've come a long way since we first met shorty." It was genuine, the feeling of being impressed, but there was a small pressure about Viv beating Shadow that brought Dawn's hackles up. It was second nature. Viv's reaction was quite different, however. She answered Dawn's remark with a glare. The woman formerly known as the Steel Rose just simply looked back at her, smile slowly erased from her face. "What?" "Why do you act like this for?" Vivica answered, with a bit of attitude in her voice. "Act like what for?" Dawn didn't care for the sudden attitude, especially not now of all times in her life. She matched Viv's glare with an emerald one of her own. "Something wrong with me complementing you on a win?" "You've always been like this." The Bulletproof Blonde explained, "I always tell you something and you blow it off, whistle like it's no big deal. I've busted my ass for years to get where I am and you act like what I do is like a joke to you. Are you jealous that I beat Lia? Is that what it is?" "Jealous? Of you?" It came out before Dawn could stop it, but now there was no going back on it. "What part of what I said made you think it was no big deal? You won the fucking World Championship, from Lia of all people! What do you want me to do, fall on my knees and praise you?!" "It'd certainly be a better reaction than treating me like a child, like ALWAYS." Valentine's aggravated demeanor turned into a rant, "You aren't any fucking better than me Dawn. On your way out the door of GCW I beat you, and don't even act like you didn't care. I saw the look on your face. You were in shock. You could've been something big too, you know. You had what it took, if you just dropped the cynical, holier than thou attitude and did your God damn job. You couldn't so you QUIT, but in your mind you're still better than the world! Posting all your shit on the walls, constantly reminding everyone you know about how amazing you are. Are you really so full of yourself that you don't even see it?" "Posting all my shit?" Dawn hopped from the stage, tossing the bottle aside to get into Viv's face. "You're the one who was always desperately seeking everyone else’s approval for everything you do. Are you such a fucking robot that you can't take any pride in your own accomplishments? You've got to come all the way out here and tell me off after years of waiting? Yeah, I couldn't hack in GCW and that's why I left and I never tried to pretend it was for any other fucking reasons. What the hell else are you expecting me to say? GCW was the end for me, I quit the fucking business because I wasn't up to it. Now you're going to come here and rub it in my face because you are up to it? Which one of us is posting her shit on the walls and trying to make everyone see how great she is?!" "It's because I am great, Dawn." Valentine snarled back, "I fucking deserve it and all everyone gives me is a bunch of bullshit!" Pushing herself down off the stage Vivica picked up a half-full beer bottle off the ground and hurled it at the wall. A nearby staff member saw it at the last minute and leapt out of the way. With a smack that echoed across the venue the hard plastic cracked against the wall, leaving beer to gush out from every direction as it fell down to the ground in two separate pieces. Taking a deep breath, Viv's cerulean blue eyes peered back at Dawn again. "I never wanted a mother, I already have one that I can't stand." Viv's frustration masked the tears that were trying to force their way out, "All I ever wanted was a friend who'd treat me like they were my equal. Like they fucking cared. Alexis, Lia, and Tessa were all I had. One by one I lost every one of them and years later you're still looking down at me. Don't you know what it's like to be alone?" Dawn stiffened in response, but her voice softened slightly. "Of course I know. The only parent I ever knew died when I was a teen Viv, you know that. I'd give anything in the world to have my Dad back, to not have to go through life without him. But you push people away Viv, you always have. I don't look down on you, I don't think I'm a better person than you, but everything I do or say comes across to an insult to you and there comes a point where there's nothing I can do about it anymore. You won the World Title from Lia and that's fucking amazing, but what else do you want me to say?" "It's too late." Vivica responded back stepping through the garbage ridden floors, "You had your chance years ago." "Is tha..." "Fuck off, Dawn." Valentine glared back at The Steel Rose with tears running down her cheeks, no longer able to control the raw emotion that had run a rampage across her life in the past few months. Her emotions had become her, unable to rationalize the honest words of a changed woman trying to make things right. She'd become an animal, "It's just like Dies Irae, Dawn..." She sniffled a bit and then snarled her final words. Dawn McFarlane had no choice but to watch helplessly as Vivica once again continued straight down her own untamed path: "I'm better off alone." "No one knows what it's like to feel these feelings, like I do, and I blame you."
