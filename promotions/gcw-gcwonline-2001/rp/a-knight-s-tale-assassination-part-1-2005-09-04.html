A Knight's Tale: Assassination (part 1)

4 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 Sept 05 I remained as quiet as I could possibly be as I walked down the empty sidewalk. The cement was wet from the rain that had poured on the city throughout the day, this also brought a chill in the air, one that was enough to make me shiver more than once. My trench coat had collected so much water in the long walk that it felt like it weighed about two hundred pounds, but I kept it on no need to lose it now, I might need it for further events that would happen soon. I reach into my jacket pocket and pull out a cigarette and a lighter. I place the cigarette at the edge of my lips and flick the lighter on, firing up the end. Smoke swirled into the sky, my eyes followed its life span until it disappeared into the night air. This thought brought a slight grin to my face, just as that smoke vanished so would this man that I was hired to destroy. I don’t know the man really, don’t have to. I have killed many in my lifetime and I would kill plenty more before my time was up. This Michael Stevens was just another job, another face, another paycheck. Some people in my line of work get bothered by it eventually, their stomachs can no longer handle the thought of taking a woman’s husband from her or stealing a child’s father ? I don’t have that same problem. I don’t kill because it is all I can do, I don’t kill because I have some emptiness in my soul that needs filled. The only reason I kill is because its what I love to do. Consider it a hobby of mine, pain, suffering ? death. Once upon a time death was all mankind knew, and not only did they know it but they embraced it. Mankind has grown week I am afraid, they fear death as if it was all a bad thing. If God allows me to kill the ones I do then it must be their time. If it was not their time then God would strike me down for taking on his role in the circle of life. I am still standing, so obviously I am doing him a favor. I lift my watch up to check the time, 9:30 PM, I was scheduled to complete the mission in fifteen more minutes. I never arrived late to a job and I never slipped up on a target. This is usually when I begin going over the different ways that I could kill the man I was after, as well as all of the things that could go wrong. What if he wasn’t alone? What he heard or saw me as I approached? What if the police are tipped off before I arrive? What if my pistol decides to malfunction? At one point or another these problems have arisen and I take care of them quickly. I don’t much like to use guns anyways except for a last resort but the one who hired me for the job made it clear that that’s how he wanted this job done. Who am I to argue? I could always say things got a little out of hand and I had to resort to breaking his neck with my bare hands, as is the case with most of my targets. Time check, 9:37 PM. I had merely eight minutes until it was time to collect my pay check. I flicked what was left of my cigarette to the curb and blew the smoke out and into the sky. My eyes traveled upwards to that black sky, and I caught its beauty. I was a night person no doubt about it. The day time had to many people, too many sets of eyes, too many ignorant bastards that probably didn’t even deserve to share the same air as me. The night time is much more peaceful, relaxing, even if death is right around the corner this feels like any other night. I didn’t even realize that I still had my sunglasses on so I pulled them off, letting my icy blue eyes look at my surroundings cleanly. I stuff the glasses in my inside coat pocket before stopping my walk and looking around at where I was exactly. I took in a deep breath, slowly letting it out right before sliding the trench coat from my shoulders and letting it land on the grass next to me. I was cloaked in black, as per usual. A black sleeveless shirt that seemed to be drawn to my body and a pair of black slacks was the extent of my outfit. I adjusted the two holsters that I carried two black pistols in on my body, making them easily accessible. I pull out a pair of black gloves and slide them on, they fit snug, probably the same kind that OJ wore. Just the thought of this brought a slight chuckle from deep down inside. I shake my head, getting all of these silly thoughts out of my head ? I had to focus, not being focused could get me killed. The last thing I do is tie my long hair back, making sure it stays in place in a pony tail. 9:45 I crack my neck back and forth before walking forward again, creeping towards a house aligned with the rest in his neighborhood. Sneaking around back I don’t make a sound, careful not even to allow the grass to make sounds underneath my feet. With one leap I clear a fence that blocks the front to the back yard, heading to the back door which I was told was always left open. It was unlocked and I slowly pushed it open, leading to a small room. A set of stairs led to the basement and another three or four steps up took me right to a door that would lead to the kitchen. With my right hand I pull out a pistol and take it off “safe”. Using the tip of the pistol I push the door open and peer inside, nothing. All of the lights in the house were off and all was quiet, in a way I felt like the Grinch who was coming to steal Christmas. I take some steps inside, careful to not move anything or step too hard, as everyone knows its when you don’t wish to make any sound that everything does. An entrance way into what appears to be the dining room lies ahead. I move past the stove, microwave, etc. and cross the entrance way quickly swinging my body to the right, pointing my weapon to where the room extends, leading to the bathroom and the sink. I immediately turn my attention forward to the rest of the house, swiftly moving through it. I move through the living room and an empty bedroom downstairs before realizing he is probably exactly where I didn’t want him to be, upstairs. The staircase that leads the upstairs bedrooms was very narrow. I reach in with my left hand and pull out the other pistol just in case. For some reason things didn’t seem right to me. I had been doing this for years and something just felt off about this entire situation. I put my foot up on the first step, then bringing my weight up onto it. The steps do make a slight noise which makes me stop and tighten up, nervous that it was heard. Again my foot comes up, this time I skip a step to quicken the process. The steps make slight noises but nothing too noticeable as I get to the top. It breaks off to the left and to the right now with a door on each side probably leading to a different bedroom. “FUCK!” is what I thought to myself, I had to make a decision and hopefully It was the right one. I move to my left going towards the door. As I get closer I can hear something escaping from the behind the bedroom door. As I get closer I can tell the door is open a crack. I put my ear to the door and listen inside. When I get this close it is very obvious what is going on. Heavy breathing, moans of pleasure, the sound of flesh smacking against flesh ? someone is getting their brains fucked out. Just as I did with the back door, I slowly moved it open with the end of my gun until the door came halfway open, enough for me to slide in. The couple didn’t hear me open the door, I am guessing they were a bit too busy to do so. I moved inside and slowly came up from behind them. The man held his lover’s legs in the air as he pleasured her. He had his full concentration on her, and she was laying there, eyes closed, taking in the great pleasure. He only stopped when I pressed the barrel of my pistol to the back of his head. Him stopping his lustful assault made her eyes open up. “Scream and I will pull this trigger and blow your boyfriend’s brains against that wall in front of you.” I whispered. She wanted to scream, they all did. Neither of them moved neither, not even so he could pull himself from inside of her. I slightly looked up and saw his hands were clutching her thighs tight, so tight that her legs were shaking because of his own shaking. I pulled the second pistol up and aimed it at a spot on the back of his head right next to my other weapon. “Nothing personal Michael ? business.” “Well you have the wrong guy.” A voice from behind me, I don’t even have time to react before the barrel of a gun is now pressed up against my head. I sigh, letting out steam. I was so careful, how the fuck did this happen? I could feel the anger building up inside of me. How did he know I was here? Was I set up? “Michael Stevens?” “That would be me.” The voice behind me proclaimed. Death was always around the corner, I was surprised it took so long to catch up to me. “Well Michael how about we jus?” The butt of his gun crashed into the back of my head, causing me to fall flat on my face. Everything began going dark ? Fuck! Well we all have to go sometime.
