Self doubts Pt. 2

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/11 These damn dreams keep repeating themselves. I can smell the flesh searing itself from the muscles. Cooked meat and burnt hair. Beasts non human scream in agony and above all of this I look down upon the plains. Still this monster, perhaps a balrog chases me, I am no longer afraid of it, but seeking a higher ground for combat. Kicking my feet into the dry red soil I watch it float up into the dry arrid atmosphere. It is coming I can feel it, what am I going to do with it. As I watch I feel a hand grasp my shoulder as I look I see... “Mr. Whitecloud you seem to be lost in a day dream again, Would you please elaborate on these struggles again?” The psychiatrist looks at Shaman waiting for his response. Turning his head slightly while laying on the couch. He lets out a deep breath as he speaks. “Repeating myself tends to annoy me Doc.” “This exercise isn't about what annoys you or what you dislike, it is about getting to the root of the issues.” Sitting up he places both feet on the floor as he turns. “Well Doc if I am paying you my hard earned money you need stick to what I have asked you to stick too, but this once I will amuse myself with your request.” Leaning back once again he thinks for a few moments and begins speaking describing certain events. Of course he is not revealing everything in fear of incriminating himself or making himself seem weaker than he really is. Squinting my eyes I see the beast on the horizon, the hand on my shoulder is a younger version of myself. He shows himself to me once again as a solid anchor to who I am it seems, but he has acted before on my behalf in this dream. He has always been the more aggressive personally driven version of myself. I had always asked myself why at that point in time I was so driven to prove myself to everyone. Now I only want to prove things to myself, but this balrog will take everything within myself past present and future to defeat. “Shaman or should I call you Andrew?” “Shaman is fine Doc, I am not here for you to personalize yourself with me, I am here to address some simple things as requested by certain people of authority.” “Have you considered that maybe this balrog is perhaps yourself at some point in your past?” Rubbing his chin for a moment he shakes his head in disagreement. “No that beast is not anything close to what I had released upon my opponents or what I was willing to do in my past.” “Look around you, this is not the place to be keeping secrets.” The Psychiatrist waves his hand around the spartan styled room. Closing his eyes as he remembers the dream as he responds. “What dark secrets I have inside me will make this dream seem like a Pokemon kids cartoon.” Weapons is what I need, yeah two men against one giant beast, odds are truthfully way out of my favor here. Where are the other two, the vicious and violent version of myself. Hell I'd even settle for the The thinking and devious version right now. The fractured parts of my psyche that make up my allies in this dream, are everywhere when they are not needed and now are nowhere to be found facing this most ancient of creatures. Reaching down I grasp the largest object I have at my disposal. Strangely enough it is a sledge hammer. Not necessarily my choice of weapons here, but can inflict grievous amounts of damage if wielded correctly. Turning my head I finally see some more versions of myself. The childhood version disturbs me the most, It is almost as if they are wanting me to bring them all together for war finally. Then suddenly it dawns on me, I turn and observe the landscape once again, The strange familiarity of it all. “What do you see? What is familiar about this to you?” Opening one eye he looks at the man, this small man whom he could easily crush. “Look you need to either shut up and let me finish or give me a refund for all of these sessions.” “Please carry on.” Groaning a bit “Just what I thought no chance in a refund.” Placing the mighty weapon of choice on my shoulder. i begin walking towards the balrog, the creature is as gruesome and foreboding as legends would suggest. As I walk the landscape seems to shimmer and change as I walk. Behind me walks my 12 different versions of my past. As I stop at the feet of the balrog something funny hits me, he is actually wearing the green viser of a poker dealer. He speaks deeply as I can feel the heat from his breath. It is almost as if he has a blast furnace as an inner core. “Sit and play your hand accordingly” he says to me eying me carefully. His eyes seem to drill deeply into my inner being. My soul seems to shudder with revulsion as I take a seat grabbing my cards. Once the hand begins I notice something as we continuously play the card game, neither one of us is winning. The heat seems to get more intense as the game goes on, as my past shadows observe the game intently. “Trust me Doc, this was the weird twist in the dream, it was as if I was stuck somewhere and couldn't progress any further.” Tapping his fingers on the couch he ponders what the dream could mean. Writing something on his scratch pad the shrink looks at him as he places a fore finger to his glasses and pushes them up on his face as he speaks. “Sounds to me you have reached the proverbial crossroads literally.” “It was a dream Doc, Nothing more nothing less.” He sits up placing his forearms on his thighs as he looks around. Thinking to himself he wonders if this is part of his own reality. Knowing he might be at a crossroads in his career, some have said he has just been coasting along the last year or two. Looking up at the Psychiatrist he frowns as he turns his own gaze back to his hands. “Ok maybe there is something more bothering me, It is this whole crossroads thing. You know the whole direction I am going currently. I don't know where I want to go, For the first time I have everything, yet I am not satisfied with where I am and who I am.” Standing up the Psychiatrist walks to the window and looks out it. He taps the glass as he speaks. “Sometimes dreams are a window into our souls, I believe you see your own frustration at what is happening around you. You are physically and financially powerful, but yet you have seen the rug yanked out from under yourself and you believe it can happen again. Your strength lies in your bindings to your family and chosen careers.” Standing up Shaman walks to the window and reaches down and grabs a chair. He slings the chair through the glass as it shatters, smiling he looks at the psychiatrist. “Look all cured, the glass ceiling is shattered and I can move on. Session over and so are our future meetings.” Turning on his heel he quickly exits the office. As he steps out onto the street he begins walking as he places his hands into his pocket shuffling his feet a bit. His mind just reverts right back to his sudden outburst and change of subject in the psychiatrists office. What was he really afraid of? As the sidewalk seems to go on and on forever as his mind wanders over many things, then he suddenly stops and looks inside a store window. A book shop where they have wrestling magazines and on the cover of one of them is himself. Holding up various titles over the years. Holding seemingly every title he could ever have gotten his hands on. Now he holds The Vanguard title with GCW. With the opportunity to once and for all snag something and make it all his own. Is this the crossroad of my career? Will this be the defining moment or will it be just another fleeting moment of glory that has always plagued me throughout his wrestling shelf life? Reaching into his pocket he pulls out his cell phone. He dials a number and raises it to his ear as he looks about. “Hello yeah Doc I think after all we will need a few more sessions.” Nodding as he listens to the Psychiatrist on the other end. “Yeah I came to that conclusion as I was walking. I had ended up with more questions rather than a clear head and a single thought.” He holds the phone for a moment and then lets out a deep breath. “See you on Friday.” Hanging up the cell he looks about the are and notices a small bench nearby as he meanders over to it and sits down. He watches the sunset slowly over the water of the bay. The sunblazing and burning reminds him of the eyes of the balrog in his dreams. The deep burning eyes that seem to judge him no matter what he does. The fans are like legions of demons and starving hordes, they always want more out of me, in the end they always get what they want like ravenous little beasts. Hungry for flesh and blood. My violent nature is always willing to give it to them. Is this why I am seeing this shrink? I wonder why they always come out in the end as the ones I begin to dislike, I have never done anything just for myself. My career is like a who's who of wrestling red carpet, I overcame every obstacle they said I could not overtake. Many had said I was washed up and only reason I was still going was because people felt guilty for me and had pity on my career. Yet where the hell do they get off saying I have not achieved anything? Turning his head he gazes at an old woman and a child just looking. His anger grows as the rage within himself begins to boil over. His upper lip begins to twitch as he just simply looks at them. “What do you want you fucking vultures? You have had your pound of flesh, now its time for me to take it back.” The old woman gently shoves the young man away as they both look back wondering if he has gone off the deep end. He looks down at his hands seething with anger, rage, and many more sorts of conflicting emotions. He wonders to himself if he may have finally caught the 101 train to nutsville. Looking at his palms carefully he speaks to everyone and yet nobody. “Brad Cruz you are the hardcore champion for a reason, now understand that being hardcore will not save you from the many ways I intend on breaking you. Sleep well but not too soundly for I am going to share with you my dreams” He simply closes his eyes and drifts off into the land for which he has known lately better than reality.
