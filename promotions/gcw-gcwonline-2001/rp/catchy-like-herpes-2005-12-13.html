Catchy...like herpes.

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13 Jack and Jack just sitting in a dark corner of the bar. Jack and his good friend Jack, a good friend that's always been there for him. Always stood by him and known him, inside and out, through and through. Inseparable. Spiraling with each other, chasing each other down the void in life where pain meets pleasure and from pleasure pain is derived. On the brink of darkness and being awake, Jack and Jack are eternal friends. Some people prefer to hold the bottle by the neck. There's something about the feeling of the weight working against you, only to give and rush toward your waiting gullet as it passes the apex. Then, holding the bottle by the neck, you choke down what you can, spilling what you can't force down, until vision blurs into a sweet euphoria. And you can forget that the world exists. But me? No. Me...I grab it by the bottom. The widest part. Jack's hand on Jack... eternal friends. So many bottles, but it's the same person, the same friend going down my throat with its amazing metal taste. Like I can already taste the blood boiling and the incredible bouquet of pride being swelled in my stomach. And after each swig, the bottle of Jack Daniel's sits on the table, waiting for Jack Fitzwillis, Johnny Borealis, to pick it up again. Story of my life. 'You just don't have it together. And that stinging... that's you knowing it. Knowing everything. I know everything, don't I? I know I know more than you. That's for damn sure.' Eva VanLorne? Fuck her. Mike Savige? Fuck him. Fuck 'em all, man. I'll take 'em all. I'll down 'em all. I'll- ... I'll take some more of Johnny's cough medicine. Ease the pain. Kill the pain. Better than any little white pill, my old friend Jack kills the pill and heals the ills and slides down my throat, torching everything on its way. Total destruction. Scorched Earth policy... that's what Jack Daniel's is good for. Burns away everything that's wrong until you're fresh... reborn. Like straight outta the womb and all. And you stand up and you look in the mirror and you recognize yourself for the first time in years. That's why I drink. Not to get drunk. But to remember what it's all about. And it's all about-... ... ... 'I forgot.' 'You forgot a lot of things, darlin.' Jordan. Fucking. White. The Hate Machine. If there was anybody I'd be expecting to see in this bar, it wouldn't be him. Self centered asshole who calls himself some sort of wise man. I call him too wise for himself. Too many ideas floating around in his head and not one of them good enough for him to concentrate on. Sitting there with a cigarette burning away, burning away his life. Take a swig of Jack and he starts to blend in to the background. Just like he did in real life. just like when he gave up being a wrestler and started just simply managing Trey Tremendous. And now what? Trey's probably dead in a ditch and Jordan's looking for somebody to leech off of. 'What's your deal, Hate Machine? What are you up to?' 'Eh. Trey's probably dead in a ditch and I was just looking for a place to get a drink. But I see that good ol' Johnny-boy Borealis has pitched tent here and calls this back corner his own while he downs himself a bottle of whiskey.' 'Yeah, well, if yer liver don't hurt, it ain't dessert.' 'True spoken words of a man to define men.' He's got an arrogant smile on his face. His being feces-...fesh, fesh, fetish... facetious? Yeah, that's it. His being facetious about my abilities is one of the bigger insults I've had lately. Well, that and losing to two people that I know damn well I can beat. 'A man to define men? You're God damn right that's me, and don't even pretend it's not. I'll have you know, Jordan fucking White, that I cross all the rules and break all the lines!' Break rules? Cross lines? Cross rules. Break lines. Line break. 'Hey kiddo, don't get mad at me. After all, what the fuck am I gonna do to you? And what do you care? I haven't seen a ring in a long... long time. The doctors say my body's so full of drugs and holes that the drugs went into that you could pretty much shine a strobe light in my face and I'll die of shock.' 'We can try that.' A cough/laugh. That means he coughs when he laughs. But I won't rule out that he might just sound like he's laughing when he coughs. Eat shit. 'I don't get what the hostility's for, Johnny-boy. Has your recent losing streak got you terribly, terribly sad?' Take a swig of my best friend. The only friend that understands. Take your time, Jack. Arr-tick-yule-ate slow-lee. 'I am not on a streak, Jordan. Not a streak of losing. Not a streak of sucking. Not even on a streak of not giving a shit. Two losses. Two fucking losses, Jordan... they do not constitute a streak.' 'I see. It's just that you have two losses in a row. That you claim not to deserve. And what is it, pray tell, that we say that that constitutes?' '... go fuck yourself, Jordan.' He laughs silently. Amused, as he should be. Stupid prick. Meanwhile, Jack's got the ability to cure what ails his friend, Jack. A full swig swirls around, like so many memories, until Jack enters Jack's bloodstream, enters the life of Johnny Borealis. Things getting a little more hazy. Things getting a little more shakey. 'You know, you remind me more of me every day, Johnny-boy. What with the attitude change and the substance abuse, and now the bitterness to ever admit that you're wrong. It's kind of flattering. Kind of look at you and see myself a few years back. I see a young Hate Machine, a young Jordan White. Someone who's really going to grow up to be all he can be, all he lets himself be. You know? What was it that you said? Crossing the rules. Breaking the lines. You could be incredible someday. Someday you could honestly grow up to be just like Uncle Jordan. The epitome of man. The true spirit of Christmas. The Ayatollah of Rum and Cola. And you can know that you'll make me proud of the fact that you were enough of a man to do it. That you... Johnny Borealis... were enough of a man to say, 'Fuck potential! Fuck what could be! I'm going to take all this skill, all this talent, hell, my entire life. And I'm going to flush it completely down the fucking toilet. That's what I'm going to do.' His words stir me. Tired, bloodshot eyes creak open and look Jordan White over. For a moment, he fools me. For a moment he doesn't look like the Hate Machine I know and love. The arrogant prick that hates everything that is and knows more than any other man will be given credit for. I don't see the man that wakes up, opens his eyes, and hates the world around him. For the briefest of seconds, I catch it, even in my inebriated state. And he can't hide it any longer. I see a man that is constantly awake... and who hates himself for living the life he lives. I sit in silence with the person I used to call my friend for a little too long. A little longer than I thought it'd be. Not, like, that I notice it's long. No. But rather, I sit there... and I just exist for a few seconds. And he just exists for a few seconds. And it's hard for either of us to actually place ourselves doing anything. I'm not drinking Jack. I'm not sitting in a chair. I'm not even in a bar. I'm just... existing. And he's not scratching the riddled holes on his arm that are rest shelters for heroine needles. And he's not coughing up black viscous liquids that nobody should ever see. And he's not hating anything. He's just... existing. We sit here. And the world turns. And the alcohol ferments in our souls and the band plays and Eva is the US champ and Trey is probably dead in a ditch somewhere and Dave Matthews won't stop making music and the Blood Elves are being added to the Horde and people protest this and that because they don't agree and there's a bar with two people that are doing nothing. And everything, everywhere... is merely existing. Simply continuing. I don't even notice I've been staring at Jordan, thinking he may be right, until he turns and looks me in the eye. I guess I had to have been staring at him for him to turn right to me and look me in the eye, as that is the only way that my eyes would be looking where his eyes would be at the time that- 'It all kinda makes me wonder... back when I was Johnny Borealis, why couldn't there have been a Hate Machine to come up to me? Back when I was a dreamer. An idealist. Hell, back when I was still a person.' Jordan's warnings couldn't be clearer. His backwards message might get lost on the wind with somebody else. But I guess Jack Fitzwillis wakes up way too late in the day to miss a show like his. And in a half second, it's gone. Jordan White, the Hate Machine, turns back to the band that's playing and he pretends he cares about them. About life. But I can tell that he hopes, somewhere inside of him, that maybe I heard his words. And that maybe his life's waste won't happen again. That perhaps he can make things right by keeping me from fucking myself the way he fucked himself. Or maybe the man just wants to listen to the music. And smoke his cigarette. And keep on existing. So I won't ask him. And I won't share my thoughts with him or anyone else. Just you, Jack. And just you, Jack. Jack and Jack. The best of friends.
