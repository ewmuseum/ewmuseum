The Quest, Pt. 2: Facilitation

16 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/2005 We fade up on a restaurant somewhere in Scottsdale, Arizona. Looks to be a Chinese restaurant. Sitting at a table, eating some Kung Pao chicken is a man dressed in a rather sharp looking suit. You could tell that he was out of place not only by the suit, and the briefcase sitting next to his chair, but for the fact that he was eating with a fork and drinking some sort of cola. Business Suit Guy: Where is he? Walking into the restaurant, wearing a 'Who's Your Papi?' T-Shirt, black and red motorcycle pants and leather jacket is Sam Wolack. He walks up to the table and sets his motorcycle helmet on the chair next to the one he decided to sit in. A waitress comes up with a menu. Wolack: Don't need it. Give me what he's having, only with tea and chopstick. Waitress: Yes sir. Business Suit Guy: Well, Samuel Wolack. Ms. Durant told me a lot about you. Wolack: Did she? She also mention that she ripped my brother's heart out? Probably not. Business Suit Guy: Well, I didn't come here for idle chit-chat, I came to do business. Wolack: Well, Mr. Espinoza, I think that you found the man that you want for your new line of boots. Mr. Espinoza: As you know, since your brother and Ms. Durant divorced the second time, she went away from the wrestling industry and decided to focus more on the extreme sports market. Wolack: No kidding. Mr. Espinoza: She heard that you just pulled off a televised stunt in Detroit by.....am I reading this right? Wolack: Yes, I BASE jumped off of the Ambassador Bridge. Add into that El Cap, Petronas Tower and some radio antenna in South America. Mr. Espinoza: Didn't you get arrested. Wolack: Yep. Paid my fine and got out of there. Wasn't bad actually. Mr. Espinoza: Well, Ms.... Wolack: You can call her Kelly. I've known her for many more years than you have. Mr. Espinoza: Well, Kelly, would like to have you take part in our new ad campaign. I'm guessing she wants you to BASE jump off of a cliff or something. Wolack: She also informed you that the shoot is going to take place in Thailand, right? Mr. Espinoza: That's right. I don't know how you got her to agree to it, but that's where it's going to happen. Wolack: Good. Scene fades to a little later in the evening. Wolack is riding his motorcycle down a road that is regularly populated by college students looking to hit one of the night clubs. He pulls in behind a black Cadillac Eldorado. The door on the Eldorado opens and out comes the same man that gave Sam the envelope outside his brother's wrestling school. Man: You have the cash? Wolack takes his helmet off and opens up his jacket, pulling out an envelope. Wolack: Yeah, I do. Wolack hands the envelope to the man. Just then, ten undercover FBI agents pull their guns on the man. One of the agents takes the cash from the man. FBI Agent: Thank you, Mr. Dooley. We've been tracking you for almost a year. Mr. Dooley: What did I do? FBI Agent: Well, we can add extortion to your list of evil deeds. You see, Mr. Wolack here was gracious enough to tell us about this meeting tonight. Mr. Dooley: Sam? Buddy? Pal? Wolack: I'm not your friend. You see, I had the FBI check out what you said about Thailand, and when I saw your picture, I figured that there would be a good reward for your capture. I'm sure that you can give these boys what they want to keep your ass out of prison. Mr. Dooley: But he'll kill me? Wolack: That isn't my problem, you son of a bitch. You knew the situation that I was in and you thought that you would profit from it. FBI Agent: Mr. Wolack, we do ask that you stay stateside for the time being. Wolack: No, no, no. I already have my flight to Thailand booked. I have a photoshoot to attend. FBI Agent: Fine, but we don't want you to get involved in the investigation. Wolack: You don't have to worry about me, agent. FBI Agent: Good. Mr. Dooley, if you don't mind, we have a plane bound for Washington. Wolack: Bye, Dooley. As the FBI Agents put Dooley in one of their cars, Wolack goes into Dooley's car and grabs an envelope. Wolack: Hopefully he remembers to give me a call after he makes his deal. FBI Agent: What are you doing? Wolack quickly puts the envelope in his jacket and zips it up. Wolack: Seeing if he left anything of any importance in the car. He didn't. Wolack get's back on his motorcycle and starts it. The FBI Agent grabs his arm. FBI Agent: Aren't we forgetting something? The FBI Agent hands the envelope of cash back to Wolack. Wolack: Thanks. FBI Agent: You sure he's going to talk? Wolack: He isn't the type that could stand for prison very long. Very jittery. FBI Agent: OK. Well, you better be on your way then. Wolack: No worries, agent. I have all the faith in the world that you'll find Andrea. FBI Agent: Don't worry, we will. Wolack puts his helmet on and then squeals his back tire before taking off. Wolack(VO): Me, trust those idiots? They still haven't found that girl in Aruba, I highly doubt that they're going to find Andrea. Thanks to Dooley's need to get a new life, I find myself one step closer. First I have to wrestle 'Johnny Heroic', then I'm off to Thailand, where I will find her. Scene fades to black.
