Secrets and Negotiations

3 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/06 ~Venetian Hotel Suites~ ~Las Vegas Nevada~ Blaise was wary about all of this. It wasn't often that he was called to come alone and then have Ukyo bring someone else to a meeting with Ian. It was cool that he had been told to show up casual and not get into his uniform. But being told that both Ian and Fey wanted to talk to him didn't set well. And to put it bluntly, neither did the feel of the hallway here in the Venetian as he walked up. Something was up. He took a few steps out of the elevator and looked around. Everything appeared normal... in the normal. Quickly he looked around and made sure that no one else was there. He saw no one in the physical. A slight shift of his vision though, and his eyes almost seemed to go from emerald green to a luminescent green almost glowing and then he saw them. There were guards... Two guards, both on either side of the door. They were not normal guards either. Their gear allowed them to be partially in the astral and in the physical and to shift back into the physical when they were needed. The markings on their gear showed that they were visitors. Visitors from the Courts of Tir Nan Nog the Faery Courts of the old world. In fact, their markings put them as members of the most elite fighting force the Courts had. The Wild Hunt. In fact, the antlers coming off of their helmet brows showed them to be ones that served Herne himself, a man who was the biological brother to Annie Leanan. The two turned their head, they had sense him using his powers to detect them. ?Prince Turren, they are expecting you. You may pass.? He just nodded to them. They were here on official business and he didn't recognize them at all. But Blaise's contacts with the old world courts was limited. His hand hit the handle and the door swung open. Standing in the large living area was Annie Leanan, in her usual sharp business suit. Today's was a deep velvet in blood red. She smiled wide at Blaise as he stepped in. ?Ah, good t' see ye 'ere. Ukyo will be bringin' Alexis by later, riogh?? He shook his head at the whole situation. ?Yeah, although why you wanted me early, I don't know.? He saw Ian step into the room from the other side, coming from the bed room area as he was drinking from a bottle. ?We have some things to catch you up on, boy. I think you should take a seat for it. Fey will be out here in a moment after she's done.? Blaise looked around. ?What's up?? He then heard a noise from the bathroom area. It didn't sound too good. Annie answered him. ?Fey is just na' feelin' well. She's been 'aving some trouble keepin' food down lately.? Blaise looked at her without blinking. ?She's sick? But she's a faery princess. She doens't get sick.? Annie smiled. ?There are a few reasons we can get sick.? Blaise looked a bit dumbfounded but smiled. His mind had made a conclusion. Annie signaled to one of the servants that were standing in the back of the room. Quickly the young girl... another of the Sihde as Blaise could tell, went off to get whatever it was that the princess had asked for. Blaise took a seat in a large recliner. Annie took another chair, and Ian came over to the couch and took a spot on that. Shortly thereafter the servant girl showed up with a tray of various finger sandwiches and a pot of herbal tea with enough cups for everyone. The young girl poured each a cup, and an extra cup for Fey when she would no longer be indisposed. As she did, Ian decided to start. ?I guess she'll be a bit longer. We needed to speak to you alone for some reasons. It deals with things that only you should be privy to, and a few others. I'm sure you've become aware of a few power struggles going around?? Blaise took a sip of the tea to be cordial, it wasn't his normal drink. But this was something that seemed to warrant it. ?Such as you and Malik? Although I'm a bit lost in that one.? Ian nodded. ?Well, that's the big one for the Order side of things. Although I don't know what's up his ass, other then my position on our current biggest case. And a few other things that I need to finally inform you of.? Blaise stopped drinking and leaned back in his chair. ?Fill me in.? Ian grinned at him. ?I've made an offer to Lia Vlassy to take her on as a student, and she accepted.? Blaise's face went to disbelief. ?Okay, what wind is she under?? ?All four.? ?What?? ?There is very little one of the starter academies could teach a twelve hundred year old vampire who is a former special forces sniper for the Soviet Russian Army during World War II. She's seen more combat then I have. However, she's also forgotten more about such then I have. You can consider her being put right up to the Four Winds level. We've done it before with a few others. Do you have an issue with this?? Blaise didn't show any emotions. ?I personally have no issues with it. She's one of the most honorable and talented acquaintances we've had. I dare say I could one day call her friend. Virgil too, even if he's got that hard armored shell around him. But what do the other students think?? ?They don't know. It's a private thing. You can consider it more an exchange of services. I show her some things, including how to keep her eyes on her opponent in the ring.? Blaise sighs out loud. ?Yeah...? Ian continues. ?And she will take care of something utilizing some of the talents I'll be showing her to ensure that I get a thorn removed from my side.? Ian gave Blaise a knowing look at that one, and all Ian could do is nod before he addressed further. ?I'd imagine this has your uncle Korwyn all kinds of pissed off.? ?You could say that. He still holds grudges. He fails to realize that I'm doing things with the open check that Lord Ishram has given me. One of our enemies are the Fallen Ones. But we've had some of them join our ranks. We would have no issues if Lia decided to side with us in our fight. She's not with the Camarilla or the Sabat, she's a free agent as far as we're concerned. And one that has powerful allies.? ?Laszlo. But he serves Cain.? ?Yes, apparently part of the reasons he decided to come forward while I was around was the fact that Cain has taken exception to the movements of the other two paragons. While Ishram and Cain have been squabbling over these last few thousand years, the other two have amassed an army and they appear to now be working together.? ?Both the Dark Lady and the Roman?? Ian nodded. ?That fucking sucks. All we need now is for them to get in touch with Ass-To...? Ian cut him off. ?I get your attempt at a joke. But that's a potential issue. It seems that the succubus who attacked Alex and Josh on different occasions is the daughter of the Dark Lady and the Light Bearer, himself.? Holy FUCK! That's all Blaise could think when that one hit him. He'd read that Astaroth was the brother of the fallen one known as Sammael or Ibilis. But the father of Layla from Lilith? ?So, you're bolstering the forces?? ?I've not had a chance to speak to The Hand of Cain, himself. But he did not come out swinging. I know he and Korwyn have a long standing rivalry, but right now he's appearing to be at least an informant. I'd assume he'd be asking to see Lord Ishram soon, and he knows that he'd not be able to take on a paragon almost as old as his own sire. I think this is going to come down to blood being thicker then water if two sides form.? Blaise shook his head. ?You think that wouldn't work? The Roman is an impostor to any real power. And the Dark Lady is scattered on her forces.? Ian shook his head. ?This is not true anymore. You have even run into the Roman. He's oganized more then we originally though. This is why I wanted to speak to Ms. Key.? ?Ms. Key? Who's that?? Ian blinked at him. ?You're friend, Alexis.? ?Alexis Day...? Blaise smiled. ?No, her real name according to Lia is Alexis Day. She's a former member of a small group of Vampire Hunters from Ohio called The Den. They're an offshoot of one of the Roman's groups.? Blaise seemed to be a bit surprised by that. ?Okay, is this one reason why you've taken Lia as a studen?? Ian put his cup of tea down. ?I took her as a student because that's what I had to offer her to get what I needed. And she realized it was something she needed too. A mutual relationship of barter. I know things she doesn't, but has the tools to use.? ?The Mark.? Ian nodded, Blaise continued. ?If I didn't know better, you were trying to get your own 'Hand'.? Ian smiled wide. ?The relationship between a sponsor and a 'Hand' is something more. Much more. The bond between Ishram and Korwyn is strong, even though strained at times. If Ishram orders it, Korwyn is bound by his own life to do it. Same between Lazslo Vlassy and his sire. Speaking of Korwyn though...? Ian reached down and grabbed the remote control and hit a button on it. The TV and DVD player for the suite came to life, Ian continued. ?I'm sending a copy of this as well as a field ops pack to Lia today. But you need to see what has been released as to what happened in Spain when Josh and Dani got there.? Blaise started looking at the screen, but he had a question in his mind right away. ?Can you tell me how they got there?? ?Yes, when some of the others showed up, Dani... teleported like her grandfather does.? Blaise wasn't too surprised. He'd seen a few of the creatures called Angels us that trick before. As that sunk in though, he watched on the screen as the man he knew as Lord Ishram issued an order and then Korwyn Malik, Captain of the Order's Hunters went to attack Dani. They were in Ishram's sanctum in Spain. He watched as Korwyn tried using the powers that the Mark of Ishram/Cain gave him, the ability to warp the reality around him. Korwyn had always been a fighter, so he used it for that benefit and purpose. And then he watched as Dani, fully manifested as the Angel of Virtue that she was, did something that he'd never seen before. As lightning arced across her wing, and then leaped to hit Korwyn, repeatedly. ?What the Fu....? Blaise leaned forward in his chair, as Ian hit the pause button. ?They spoke with her and tested her afterwards in some ways. Annie was there. It would appear that after destroying the angel known as Baraquiel she now has his abilities as the 'Lightning of God'? Blaise could only say one word. ?How?? Ian's answer didn't help. ?You tell me? However, I'm told that my uncle has been even more of an asshole since she whooped his ass. The mark will not protect him against something that comes from that realm. It's the realm that put the mark on them.? Blaise went to comment, but the sound of a door opening and closing caught everyone's attention. Stepping into the room, looking paler then she usually did was Ian's wife and Blaise's cousin, Fey McClancy-Childress. Her red hair was pulled back but still loose heading down her back to her buttocks. She was wearing a slightly long dress that showed a regal air but was still quite plain. Blaise expected to see the choker around her neck, but she was wearing something else right now. Her necklace that held a seed from her tree back in the homeland inside a vial. It was something she had given Ian when they married. For a Dryad it contained the link to her soul that allowed her to be far away from her tree. Since she and Ian were never to be too far apart, she had given it to him to hold for her. She normally didn't wear it. Wild Hunt acting as guards and now that? She looked at him sharply with her 'you just put your foot in your mouth' look. ?Me Condition? What bloody condition would ye be talkin' abou'?? She's na' pregnant, at least na' riogh' now.? ?Well, what is it then?? Annie almost choked while saying it. It was almost appearing to be funny. She then took the moment to reply to him. ?Because it's 'ow th' courts are run. She leaned back, her arms going to her abdomen. But still looking okay for someone who has been throwing up for what looked like days. ?Well Oberon 'as recognized that there are Tautha, Faery an' Wee folk over 'ere. Some, like yerself are princes an ye're physical form was born over 'ere. Yer tree is'na even an oak. It's a Yucca, the indigenous tree o' the Upper Mojave Desert.? ?Yeah...? ?So, 'e 'as proclaimed th' formation of a new regional kingdom.? Blaise's brow folded over as he tried to understand that. ?Like Mag Mell in Scotland, Mag Mor in Germany an' Karnach in' Brittany. They'll be pullin' int' Charleston in a few days, an' then it's goin' via truck t' it's new plantin'.? Blaise sighed some relief. ?An' then there is th' reason why we 'ad to put a guard on Patti, t'. Crown Prince Bres... Sean McClancy... 'as decided 'ed take part in th' move t'.? That meant it was pretty harsh in the courts right now. ?So, what is this new kingdom going to be called?? He made sure he was making a statement. ?I can imagine what type of shit storm this is about to become.? Annie could only nod. Fey spoke up though. ?I am to be Queen Maeb of Tir Tairngire, Blaise. Me 'usband is na' of the Tautha or any other Faery folk. This leads t' one complication.? ?Hit me.? ?Bryan an' Brittany are na' old enough t' be considered in line fer me new crown.?
