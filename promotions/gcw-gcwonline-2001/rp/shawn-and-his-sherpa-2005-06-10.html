Shawn and his Sherpa

10 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/10/05 - a brainchild of Michael Jay Akers. A man of complex thought. Born April 5th, 1982. He wants to be a comic writer. ....and with out further adue, the man of the hour. A man whom none of you will probably remember from back in the day. As you will see as you read along, it's canalizing this man remembers anyone at all. As you read, you will be introduced to the lovable and laughable, Shawn Johnson, a former superstar of sorts from the olden days of wrestling, a man who has spent his retirement doing the things he has always loved. Things such as building treehouses, traveling to odd places and collecting Japanese Interns. It was along the way of his long fabled travels to Micronesia, Shawn came along a man who would forever change his laugh. A mysterious man this was. A man from Micronesia, I know, I too wondered the same thing. Is Micronesia even a real country? For the sake of arguments, let's assume it is. Anywhoo, this man, this man of knowledge was Lopsang, Shawn's sherpa. For those who don't know what a Sherpa is, it's a mountain Guide. Lopsang guided the mountains, for now....he will guide Shawn Johnson. A minor hurdle in this relationship is that only God Himself knows what language Lopsang speaks. Who knows is Shawn really understands his Zen like comments. Only time will tell to see the true unraveling of the newest superhero. A man named Shawn. A mountain guide named Lopsang....Oh shit, what am I thinking with this...Oh well, here goes nothing] . .a commentary with Shawn Johnson accompanied by the one and only Lopsang, the mysterious Zen Guide! Location: A cave outside Tampa, Florida. You know, one of the many caves in Florida. Time: Dusk Reason: A introduction to Shawn Johnson. [Caves....The perfect place for two people to get to know eachother. Cave drawings, fire, language barriers...these all add to the fun. Tonight, we have two men, one looking for answers and guidence, and one looking for someone who speaks his language. Lopsang, in a way only to be described as zen-like sits on a rock. Only a true master could sit on a rock like him. To the left of him sits Shawn, sitting on a lawnchair. A lawnchair from where you say? That doesn't matter. I don't even remember if it was a lawn chair come to think of it, but nonetheless. Let me shut up so we can all meet our new favorite people. Ever. Next to Journey that is.] Shawn Johnson: [Looking around the cave] Ahhhh. I tell you what Lopsang, there is one word I hear this cave shouting at me. You know what that is? [Lopsang stares at Shawn with a blank stare] Shawn: Your right brother. Cozy. You hear it? Chooooo-zzzzzzy. It's like the breeze across my ear. Aren't you glad I took us here? It's a perfect place to unwind, and bond like men. [Lopsang brushed the dirt around with a stick. People, Im serious, this is a man of infinite wisdom, glowing with the auroa of freshing picked knowledge.] Shawn: If it wasn't for my ongoing physical and spiritual training, I don't know if I would be able to handle taking such a beast of a man, this Minitour of Doom, this exalted Crown Prince of everything French, this Twisted son of a... [Shawn points to Lopsang as if he was waiting for the 'Shut yo Mouth'. However, I don't think Lopsang has looked up from the dirt once in the past 15 minutes] Shawn Johnson: It's a...unforunate that my first fight back has to be against this man. What's even worse as I have heard people around here refer to him as humorous. [Shawn pauses and looks at the dirt pushed about by Lopsang and ponders. Then out of nowhere, his lets out a big 'Ahjhhhhh'] Shawn: Lopsang, your knowledge reminds me of the pagan god, Zeus. Albeit a very much smaller, hindu version. The dirt you push around is a simple metaphor. Twisted is the dirt, I am the stick. Pure Genius. [Lopsang then takes the stick and then inserts it into his mouth. I know dude, I thought the same thing...This nigga is crazy as hell. He's the only reason I'm still reading this crap. OK, so let me some things up. We got a cave, dirt, a stick thats now in some dudes mouth, a metaphor, another embarassing white-nonwhite moment (the whole 'Shut yo Mouth' thing) and even a Zeus reference. Although it seems the little person running my idea department in the ol' noggin is out to lunch, I am going to make an effort to continue] Shawn: The biggest difference between me and Twisted, you ask? My determination, my heart, my love for the sport. Do I know what I'm doing right now? No..Do I remember who I am fighting next week? Not really..But you know what, I am going to go out there and give all I got. I bring it. Lopsang, you remember that time we got jumped by the bandits in Tibet? I don't know if we would have took them if it wasn't for your skills in the bo staff. [Lopsang goat stands up, eats Shawn's cowboy hat, then walks out of the cave, Lopsang right behind it.] Shawn Johnson: Son of a gun..I don't even know what goats are for. What purpose to they serve, and why in the world do they have a penchant for cowboy hats? [And with that, this segment from the mind of Michael Jay Akers of Los Angeles, California has ended. I believe this segment was definatly off the wall. I dont know where most of it came from and I dont really remember what I even wrote. But hopefully, the experience is something you enjoyed. And will remember. Let me tell you a little about myself: I came back into the game of e wrestling for the fun and the competitiveness, I look foward to setting up some neato storylines, I think we could thing of some awesome ones. As you can see, my writing style is a little different, but I like to think that is imaginative and fun. When you read Shawn Johnson segments think of someone along the lines of a Owen Wilson. Then I believe you might see the short work a little better. By the way, if anyone is even still reading this, or bothered to read this far, I am thinking about changing Shawn's name to simply Han. But go figure...and feedback would be great. Send me a PM or whatever you feel is necessary to appease yourself. Thank you. Oh, P.S. Don't expect super long roleplays from me,. I like keeping them short. I feel ones around this size are eeasier to read and can captivate an audience, and yes I know there are two 'ee's on easier 15 or so words back. Thanks again - a brainchild of Michael Jay Akers. A man of complex thought. Born April 5th, 1982. He wants to be a comic writer.
