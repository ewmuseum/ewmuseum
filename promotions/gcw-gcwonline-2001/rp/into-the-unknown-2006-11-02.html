Into the unknown

2 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2/06 The darkness is cold and empty as nothing envelopes your visual senses. Darkness and nothing is what resides here in this place as you ponder where it is you are at. Nothing is all that touches your senses. A soft buzzing sound is heard in the distance. Through the darkness a whisper is heard. Kind of muffled. Very realistic and very determined as the voice is muffled not much is known as to who or what it is. The voice whispers raspy and muffled. “The world is black and devoid of life. This world is my world at the moment. I perfect the path of spiritual understanding. Do I fear death no I do not for it is my destiny. You can run from it, but it will only catch up to you eventually.” A sound of footsteps can be heard as his breathing shallows as he listens. “They come for me. Isn’t it surprising as to what your world is when you cannot see or feel your surroundings?” Listening he lets out a deep breath as a tearing sound can be heard. “It is time for me to become one with my future and show that it is time for me to awaken the rage and sadistic path I have refused for a few years now.” The darkness grows still as no breathing and words can any longer be heard. Something erratic and dangerous comes from the darkness silence. The footsteps grow louder and then the darkness fades to light. ((Somewhere else)) Two men walk into the morgue pushing a cart of a dead body. They are seemingly talking about the patient and then one changes the subject suddenly. “Would you believe that this patient used to be a professional tennis player?” The other man chuckles and looks at the dead patient. “Yeah age really catches up to ol thunder thighs here.” Laughing they both push the cart into the room and lock the wheels and look around. “Yo Jason did you believe that some wrestler guy requested to be locked in the body closet?” The other man looks over and shrugs and pushes a cadaver into the closet nearby and opens the door and pulls out another one. Looking about he shakes his head. “Yeah it is not like this place is disturbing enough. We work at low minimal pay and some high priced wrestler wants to be locked in a closet. Idiot plain and simple.” The other man looks at him again. As he begins to open another locker he looks around. “You know the day after Halloween. Working here this place is creepy as hell.” The other man laughs as they here something and they both stop dead in their tracks and turns around looking at the closet that they had opened previously. They turn at each other and then look over at the corpse on the tray. “Did you hear something there?” The other man nods and looks around. “Nobody is here but us!” Turning both me observe the body bag sitting up as they both scatter and run headlong into the wall and stop and turn around as the body bag begins to move and the zipper slowly begins to open. The huge form of Shaman is slowly revealed with an emotionless stare at both men as he watches them intently. He slowly extracts himself from the body bag and cracks his neck from side to side. “You two must be the morons who shit themselves when they are confronted with the unknown. I’ll tell you what it was easier crawling in here than it will be to hurt you idiots. The emphasis is on the word idiot.” Shaman swings his legs off the sliding table and stands up and looks at the two men. “Ok who has a car I need to go somewhere else and one or both of you volunteer. Doesn’t matter to me, but rest assured it is going to get done now.” Shaman stares at both of the men as they motion towards the exit door and they both lead him out to a small sedan barely big enough for a short fat man. “You have got to be kidding me.” Shaking his head he climbs into the car with his knees in his chest as the other two get in and they drive as Shaman hands them a piece of paper. “Take me here now.” The men look at Shaman like he has been on some kind of drugs. “Look boys don’t give me any crap just do it.” Shaman stares bullet holes through the men as they drive. A few minutes later they come to a graveyard as Shaman gets out of the car and they begin to look at each other as Shaman walks away from them and walks off into the cemetery. “Rejects plain and simple. You see Mr. Mayhem I am working on understand everything about the match you have prepared for us. I mean I am tired of watching people claim to be the best the nastiest people in the game. The damn list goes on and on. Look at yourself there Mayhem. Look in the mirror your not playing different game than what I have already seen. I have been there and done that Mayhem.” Walking to an open and newly dug grave. He reaches over and lifts a control from behind a grave as a casket slowly raises from the newly dug grave as he grins and watches it rise to the surface. He smiles and opens the casket and grins. “Creature comforts of home. Warm and soft as well as basically the last place you will ever know. I am enjoying searching and understanding the darker side of myself. I mean I am a rich and powerful casino owner so why can’t I indulge my darker half. Show the world that there is something very disturbing about me that even they don’t know.” Shaman climbs up into the casket and sits there and then lays back looking up as he speaks. “Bryan Mayhem you and I at Devils Night are going to enjoy one bloody fight. What am I going to do” I will let you find out first hand in a brutal fight. One you have never ever been in. I heard what a fight you and Lestatt Knight had had a while ago. That will pale in comparison to what I have planned and you better come fighting harder than any man has ever fought.” Shaman sits up and looks at both of his hands and then looks up at the clear starry sky above. His eyes narrow as he watches. He turns his gaze to the graves near him. “Let’s face facts Mayhem. You have never met a man like me. I have been known by many names in the past. The most Sadistic S.O.B in the business, The Native American Bad Ass, The extreme hardcore icon the list is filled aplenty. In fact one name is not so well known though. It is one of the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse. Yes I used to be a member of that infamous stable. In fact I was a driving force in its latter configuration. Ye I hide the rage and strived to control it and bury it. Later years I stepped away from my sadistic nature and tried to confine myself within the rules. I came to GCW and realized I needed to follow no rules. I needed to be myself and through that realization I figure to be a danger to anyone and everyone who opens the door for my challenge or my passing of them on my way to the top.” Turning his head towards the camera his eyes are black as coal as they seem lifeless and unmoving as he stares intently at the nothingness beyond that seems to be the future of unknowns to all but him. “Mayhem what are your limits” Do I fear being buried? These are questions you begin to ask questions. Who or what is my opponent? Truth is I will answer that one for you Mayhem. I am a force of nature. I am rediscovering the need to bleed. I am rediscovering the hunger I thought lost long ago. I don’t care about gold and tine plated leather. I am looking for a fight plain and simple and when you’re the opponent I don’t get any bigger a fight than that.” Shaman grins and lays back and stares at the sky. “You won’t find any heroes here Mayhem. You’ll find one sadistic individual who is going to fight you more ferociously than you will ever have gathered anyone to ever be. So you ask yourself a simple question now Mayhem.” Laughing deeply as he begins to close the casket. “How do you handle a snake who enjoys being buried. The man who has no fears. In fact the man who thinks Fear is never boring. The extreme Devils dance on Devils Night is coming. Are you prepared for the end that justifies the means?” The casket closes as the darkness has a voice now. The voice is Shaman’s. “I have come to bring you pain and blood. I am going to make you bleed Mayhem. I am going to make you bleed and hurt more than any man has ever done in the past. Will you be prepared or will you fall like the rest who have sought my rage. The test has begun better be prepared for the future has come to Devil’s Night and in the end it doesn’t matter who is victorious all that will matter is who was buried and who was not. It is time to suffer the reality of life Mayhem. Death comes for all and eventually everyone is worm food!” The casket slowly begins to be lowered into the ground as a creepy old man begins to toss shovel fulls of dirt down on top of the casket as he cackles madly. You begin to wonder truly if being buried alive is such a bad thing after all. Is it the darkness of being buried or the fact that you seek the embrace of the darkness and silence of the cool damp earth? The ground from which everything comes into existence, the building blocks of the basic of elements. The test of wills has begun and will it be the sadistic or the rage. Who will rise above the earth and look down upon the ground with disdain.
