A Garbage Bag Placed Over the Prophet’s Head

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/11 Just prior to World Wide #144 <------------ Not a typo! ^_^ The scene opens with the normal preshow hustle and bustle; Brad Cruz and Slider make their way through a cavalcade of wrestlers, referees, and roadies that haunt the backstage of an arena before the show. They break free from the small mob pressing forward toward their destination. Cruz wearing his street clothes and a pair sunglasses, his face has a stoic and "all business look" about it. Slider on the other hand also dressed in his normal clothing with his trademark bandanna and dreadlocks has a worried look radiating from his face. Slider: "Cuz, I don’t like this… I don’t like this one bit." They continue walking as Cruz seemingly ignores him. Slider: "Honestly Cuz, first thing you do in GCW is completely alienate Sally Ford, and then call out GCW’s champions, which lead to you being broken in half by Bryan Mayhem. Then you decide that Jimmy Thunder and Hot Mask needed to be told a thing or two and WHAM! you’re in a triple threat taking a pin, and now your big plan is to go and give the GCW commissioner an ear full? If you ask me that sounds a bit rash." Brad stops and turns toward Slider. He removes his sunglasses tucking them in the collar of his shirt. Cruz places his hand on the shoulder of his younger cousin and smiles right before he grabs a hold of a fist full of Slider’s dreadlocks pulling his head back. Slider yelps in pain. Brad: "Where do you come off telling what I’ve done and what I was unable to do? Yes, I lost to Mayhem… but he didn’t walk out champion and that’s because of me! Shaman had it easy … he just picked up the pieces." Brad continues to walk dragging Slider who is still in the grips. Slider: "Ow-Ow-Ow- but- Shaman’s really tough… *sniffle*" Brad: "Screw that guy, looks like someone went double Subzero on both his eyes. Plus I hear he sits in the middle of the forest alone fondling his mystic orbs." Slider: "*Sniffle* …double Sub Zero?" Brad: "His face paint, it looks like Sub Zero’s scar from Mortal Ko- never mind. As for the Hot Mask and Jimmy fiasco it came down to who would be lucky enough to get the unbroken pinfall. Hot Mask had the luck of the draw… so congrats to him, he won the match… only to later fail at his opportunity for the U.S. title. …BUT! he does look like something that would live under a child’s bed feasting on their fears… so he’s got that going for him. Brad continues to drag Slider behind him until finally arriving at the office door of GCW commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny. Cruz releases his hold on Slider who quickly returns to a vertical base rubbing his scalp in pain. Slider’s face is teary eyed and pouty reminiscent of a freshly punished child. Brad: "Are you going to learn to keep your opinions to yourself, Sly?" Slider: "*Sniffle* Yes Cuz…" Brad: "You’re not going to spout off with every stupid idiotic thing that pops in your head?" Slider: "No Cuz… *sniffle*" Brad: "…because that would make you Toga… is that what you want, to be Toga? Sly, if that’s what you want, we can put you in a skirt and find a nice homely girl named like candy to follow you around. Is that what you want? A nice homely girl?" Slider thinks a moment. Slider: "I guess not, Cuz." Brad: "Good, now let us give this so called figure of authority a rude awakening." Cruz turns to the door reaching for the handle. Slider: "Okay Cuz, but let’s be tactful and subtle." Cruz turns back to Slider with a cocky smile. Brad: "Sly, look who you’re talking to… subtlety and tact are my specialty. Don’t believe me?" Brad turns to the door; he looks as though he’s about to knock politely and then just kicks with authority causing the door to fly open! Cruz jumps into the room hands on his hips like a bleach blond Superman. A young lady at the desk is startled from noise and seeks refuge from under her desk. Brad: "I’m here Johnny, you "Captain Caveman" looking son of a bitch!" Slider: "I thought you could be subtle and tactful!" Brad: "I could, just like I could also be an organ donor, but I’m too-umm, what was the word Crystal used?" Slider: "…inconsiderate?" Brad: "Actually I think it was "irredeemable" but I like yours better… implies there’s still hope." Slider shakes his head walking passed Brad and over to the desk now housing a startled young lady. Slider: "Umm hello Miss." Darla: "Please don’t hurt me! I don’t know what shady back alley dealings my employer has reneged on, but I assure you I had nothing to do with it!" Slider: "We aren’t here to hurt anyone." The young lady pokes her head out revealing a head of mid length silky red hair, a milky complexion, and a pair of understated librarian glasses. Slider’s heart jumps into his throat. Darla: "Are you with the police then? I knew this day would come. I mean Mr. Johnny is really nice, but I always suspected it was because he was so heavily dosed …oh god, he won’t do well in jail." Slider chocked with love can’t respond, so Cruz interrupts. Brad: "We aren’t from the police. We’re under Garbage Bag’s so called employ and we need to speak to him now…" Brad looks down at the name on the desk. Brad: "…Shelia." Darla: "Oh I’m not Mr. Johnny’s Secretary Shelia. I’m one of the interns, I’m Darla. I’m just here taking messages and answering the phones. Both Shelia and Mr. Johnny are out at the moment." Brad: "When will they be back?" Darla: "They didn’t say… I wasn’t officially briefed why, but I can take a message for you, and I can make sure they get it." Darla pulls out a legalpad and a pen with a little flower on top. Darla: "Okay first things first, name?" Bradley sighs Brad: "Brad Cruz." Darla looks up. Darla: "The Brad Cruz?" Brad gives a debonair smile as he answers. Brad: "You’ve heard of me?" Darla: "Well yes, and also the one thing they said was that you might stop by and ask why you weren’t included in the main event for Jay Terror’s world title, and if you did to give you this folder that contains a detailed explanation." Darla hands Cruz the file which he promptly opens. Slider has since recovered and chimes in talking to the pretty young redhead… he now plans to spend the rest of his life with. Slider: "I’m Sylvester Derek Montoya, but they call me Slider." Darla: "Why?" Slider: "Huh?" Darla: "Why do they call you Slider?" Slider: "My names is Sylvester, Sly like Sylvester Stallone and Der from Derek. Slider… It makes sense…" Darla smiles, Slider can feel his heart plunging back and forth. Darla: "Oh cute." Brad: "Okay what gives? All that’s in this folder is a piece of paper with the message "No one likes you!" written in sharpie and like ten filled out complaint forms." Darla: "One from Keith in PR and nine from Sally Ford." Brad: "Keith knows I was kidding." Darla: "Keith is now too afraid to go to bathroom alone for fear of a second abduction." Brad: "Big Baby. Why does Sally have so many? I haven’t harassed her nine times... three tops." Darla: "Well they’re stapled in groups of three, so I assume she wasn’t filing complaints for nine separate occasions, but rather for three lengthy occasions and she needed all the extra space." Brad: "So this is what it comes down to? A crudely concocted message and ten pieces of bureaucratic tattling kept me from a shot for the World Title? It figures!" Darla leans back feeling as though Cruz was ranting at her. Slider noticing turns his attention to Cruz taking the heat off Darla. Slider: "It hasn’t been all bad, Cuz" Bradley’s head slumps down below his shoulders as he lets out a sigh. Slider: "You did get a title shot on your debut." Brad lifts his head as he replies. Brad: "Yeah, for a hardcore title. Do I look like one of those glass chewing lowlifes? Besides, the whole hardcore thing went out with attitude, purple barbwire logos, and kayfabe. I’ve come too far to change my job description from wrestler to furniture mover, and If people were still really into tables, chairs, and weaponized knickknacks they’d be massing in groves to the parking lot of their local IKEA rather then selling out our arenas!" With another sigh Cruz slumps over again. Brad: "I’m just so tired." Cruz slowly walks out the room while closing the door behind him. Slider sits on the corner of the desk occupied by Darla giving her his full attention. Darla just stares at the door with some concern for Bradley. Slider: "So Darla, tell me about yourself." Darla: "Oh, I’m not all that special… is he okay?" Slider: "Things haven’t gone the way he planned, so naturally he’s a little rattled." Darla: "That can be really hard to deal with. You think he’ll be able get over it alone?" Slider: "Any second now…" Darla: "Any sec-?" Suddenly a loud crack echoes through the room as the door flies open once again with the help of Brad’s Italian Leather adorned foot. He walks in with a new found purpose. Darla and Slider jump to their feet looking at Cruz who just stands there breathing hard with anger and intensity, each breath forcing his shoulders up with every inhale and then a sharp drop with every exhale. Darla: "Mr. Cruz?" Slider: "Cuz, you alright?" Ignoring the inquiry Cruz points to Darla with purpose. Brad: "YOU!" " Darla: "Me?" Brad: "I want to leave a message. Take dictation, and keep your mind out the gutter… " Darla quickly grabs her legalpad and flowered pen and quickly prepares for Cruz’s next words. As the words pour out Darla scribbles away, her little flowered pen swaying sporadically in every direction as if caught in a storm. Brad: "Date Jan 21 2011 Anno Cruzini, in the year of our Cruz. Dear Commissioner Johnny In regards to the world title and Champion Jay Terror, please inform him that while at the moment I am nowhere near the title scene… where I will remain ( due to your short sightedness and incompetence clearly cultivated by your freebasing lifestyle, Johnny ) until I am able to rise above this oppression with my life anew, restored, and purged of this GCW poison like the phoenix… or Jesus… or Aslan the Lion! … and in time I will possess that Championship. In fact while you’re at it you can pass the message to Borealis, and Wolfmother, and when you’re done with that GBJ, do the rest of GCW the favor of taking whatever bag it is you associate yourself with (whether it’s a garbage bag, plastic bag, paper bag, or hefty bag) and pull it over your own head and affixiate yourself. Yours in Jesus, Brad Cruz "The SoCal Prophet" P.S. I still think you look like Captain Caveman " Cruz pulls a picture form his pocket handing it Darla. Brad: "…and Attach this to the bottom. Let’s go Sly." Darla quickly staples the image to bottom as Cruz begins to walk out. Slider: "Give me a sec, I just want to say goodbye." Brad: "Fine, but hurry up." Cruz walks out leaving Darla and Slider alone. Slider turns to Darla and places his hands on her desk and stares straight down at the wood finish nervously. Slider: "Darla, I-I’m no good at this kind of stuff. I’m not the most confident person in the world… Clearly that gene was hogged by a certain member of my family." Darla gives a light giggle as Slider nervously chuckles. Slider trying to capitalize on her interest in him just blurts out the first half thought on his mind. Slider: "See, I-I like your… face… and I would like to talk to it… later." Darla grabs and tears a piece of paper from her legal pad and scribbles something down, and tears a even smaller peace, and hands it to Slider with a smile. Darla: "My face looks forward to hearing from you." He nearly leaps for joy as he looks down seeing her number, but plays it cool and gestures if could borrow the remaining piece of paper and her flowery pen. She agrees handing them to Slider who begins to do some scribbling of his own. Slider: "Here’s my number, my email, my Facebook, my AIM, and my Twitter! Well on second thought..." Slider scribbles out the Twitter. Slider: " I forgot Twitter rubs some people around here the wrong way." Slider hands the paper and the two politely shake hands. Slider: "Thank you I’ll call you, but I gotta go or my cousin will kill me if I keep him any longer." Darla smiles as she waves while Slider rushes out the door backwards waving goodbye. Slider steps outside as the door closes behind and he leaps for joy. He rushes down the hall a short distance to a waiting Brad whom Slider precedes to glomp onto his back in excitement. Brad manages to shrug him off. Brad: What the hell is wrong with you! Slider: "I got her number!" Brad: "The redhead's?" Slider: "Her name is Darla and yes!" Brad: "Wow, I’m not totally unimpressed. Good job, Cuz." Slider stops in his tracks and his jaw nearly drops. Slider: "Did you call me Cuz?" Brad: "Yeah…" Slider wipes the tears from his eyes. Slider: "It's been so long since you called me that." Brad: "I suppose it has." Slider: "Got a cute girls number and you called me Cuz…" Slider wipes more tears away. Slider: "Best day ever!" Cruz shakes his head trying to mask a smile. Brad: "Come on, let’s go celebrate. I got a burden off my chest and you got a chica's number." They begin walking down the hall like two cowboys riding off into the sunset. Slider: "Cuz…" Brad: "What Sly?" Slider: "How long do I wait to call her?" Brad: "I usually wait three days… but if she likes you she’s probably into those needy wimpy guys with no self-esteem, so for you, tonight." Slider: "Sweet!"
