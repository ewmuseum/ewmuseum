Betrayal of a Contract!

13 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/05 Betrayal of a Contract! Introduction: Yet again the federation I called home as closed on me and left me in the dark to seek refuge. The federation however would not close before the entire roster learned what the name Donny Diamond really meant. My name is to be remembered by everyone as it signifies greatness and promises everyone to expect me to become their Champion. Not only does my name promise me to become Champion but it also promises that everyone will learn to fear the man behind the name! Now I find myself in a strange environment where no one knows who I am. The only reason I find myself in this federation known as GCW is because of one man, because of one foe. Just like me he is also trying to make a name for himself. This week the two of us both will be making our debut here in GCW, and for the both of us it is against two people we know absolutely nothing about. However the only difference between us is I don’t mind dwelling into the unknown. My first opponent in the ranks of GCW will be against this Poe Dunn. Poe I hope understands what it means to come and get into the ring with a legend, I hope he understands what fear truly means. Do not underestimate the being you’re getting into the ring with Poe. Donny Diamond will not be the type of opponent you are used to. When paired with me as your foe inside of a ring I’m like a thick barrier of fog. You can fight and fight but I will never go down and as time passes you will be completely engulfed by me. Then when it becomes too much for you to handle I will suffocate you in the middle of the ring and then you will remain on your back hearing the referee slam his hand down onto the canvas for the final count. Take this little message as a small taste as to what Donny Diamond is all about. I’m not the type that will talk trash about you in ring; instead I let my skills do the talking for me. Poe Dunn the defeat of you will serve a great purpose as you shall become the one selected to let the world know of Donny Diamond. When our match is concluded Poe Dunn will be the new spokesman that lets GCW understand the true meaning of fear. Poe I want you to make it clear to the entire roster that Donny Diamond will raise above all odds and defeat all comers. Make it known that a new wave of power is coming into this federation and I will only get stronger every step of the way. The Living Nightmare is going to be coming for you, very soon. Say your prayers to God himself, and for you Poe Dunn I shall take them to him personally. A cemetery in Los Angeles, CA. Early Afternoon. [The outside atmosphere is of a normal day for Los Angeles, from the sky beams down rays of sunlight with a few clouds here and there to present us with a pleasant breeze. Out in the distance you can see me standing in the same attire I always wear. I walk towards the crowd of people to hear the ceremony for the now departed Ron Shaffer. The area is filled with people who believe themselves to have been a major part of his life, but I know if Ron was still here he would be wondering as to who all of these people really are.] [I wait for the service to come to an end and watch on as everyone begins to head to their vehicles to leave. When the area is empty I move out from my spot under the tall oak tree and kneel down at the edge of his grave. Removing my sunglasses from my face, I look down at the marble box where his corpse will remain for the rest of time.] Donny Diamond: You know Ron I should be the one inside of that box awaiting the return of my soul to the pits of Hell. On this headstone they scribed your name as being Ron Shaffer, while I am the only person left who can remember you as Ron Diamond. The reason your there is because of me entirely, if anyone should be blamed for your death brother it is me. [Kneeling there I remember how I traveled into the pits of Hell to meet the Lord of Darkness himself. I remember how he wanted my head severed from my neck and placed as a trophy on his mantle. No one was capable of doing such a task and he knew the only way for this to happen was for me to do it for him.] Donny Diamond: He wanted more souls for his army Ron, but he wanted one so strong that it could command his entire fleet. I knew the soul he was searching for was the soul of Vince Jones, and I was the only one strong enough to deliver him into the pits. All I ever wanted was to see you freed from the mistakes I have made, because you never deserved to be punished for my actions. Donny Diamond: The brother you loved so much was nothing more than a common murderer with the true secrets of God and the devil himself. Can you understand why I made the deal to deliver Vince Jones into Hell? He promised me your freedom if Vince was to be delivered, and if I was to fall to defeat then my soul would do his bidding for eternity but still you would be freed. I could not bring Vince to him, but nor could Vince take me down. For some reason he set your soul free of darkness anyhow and your heart was left with nothing more than pureness. You saved another for the price of your own life brother, but you would still be breathing air had I been able to deliver Vince Jones. [Letting the black sunglasses fall from my grasp they drop down into his grave. Rain begins to pour down from the skies, but never do I move from my place. Pain is felt deep within my heart as I remain at the grave of the last soul to carry the same name as me. I begin to cry a bit as I know the reason my brother is there is because of the choices I made in my life.] Donny Diamond: My only wish from the day I sold my soul to him was to become better than our dad ever was. I never knew the truth as to what our father really was until I became so great and so praised my life was taken. My heart is curious to know who really gave me a second chance at life, because I am deserving of only one thing and that is eternal punishment. But now I know the truth as to my purpose on this planet brother. Donny Diamond: I was never promised abilities to become a great wrestler, I was blessed with that at birth and he led me to believe it was from him. Winning everything I have won was because of me, not him. I made a promise to our mother that I would never let anything happen to you brother and I have let her down. But I will give my word on a new promise and that is I will make things right for our family. When my final hour comes we shall all meet together in the gates of Heaven and evil shall be perished for good. When you see dad, give him back his sunglasses and tell him I’m sorry? [Getting up from my spot on the side of my brothers grave I wipe the water off from my face. Turning around I’m somewhat startled by what I see before me, while at the same time I was expecting it. Before me dressed in her normal hood and her head tilted down is the half demon that was in love with my brother, Dark Specter. She stares me down from my position before her as she begins to speak in her light voice.] Dark Specter: It would be so easy for me to simply take your life right now Donny. But what fun would it be for me to do such a thing, when you’re so weak and filled with guilt. Donny Diamond: I dare you to even try your chances of defeating me. You come to talk about me being weak, when the death of my brother as much of your fault as it is mine. Being as your half demon you have connections to know of the happenings from your master’s kingdom. Yet you remained by his side and did nothing as he was killed! Dark Specter: You dare speak down to me as if you’re somehow better? Because of you he forgot that I even existed! You took from me the only person who has ever loved me and you try to blame me for his death! If I had ever known his life was to be put in danger I would of done everything possible to make sure he was safe. So tell me Donny, what is next for you? Donny Diamond: I just signed on with a new federation so I’m probably going to just wrestle there for a bit till I get my head cleared. Vince Jones is in this federation as well so I’m going to finish what I started with him, but before I get my hands on him I got my debut match against Poe Dunn this week. One thing Ron really loved was wrestling and I think he would be happy to know I won’t stop. Dark Specter: Good to see you haven’t given completely up on things. And I do agree with you thinking Ron would be happy to know you are still doing what you love. I’ll be seeing you Donny, because we now have something in common. [From before my eyes Dark Specter vanishes and I go back to the old oak tree. I take a seat beneath it to shelter myself from the rain as I stare at my brother’s grave. As I think about his death I become more and more focused on my match against Poe Dunn who I know I will defeat in my debut!]
