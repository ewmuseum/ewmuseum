Staring into his own eyes, fiery passionate eyes, he recognized the man he was. Powerful. Omnipotent. He was a deity sitting in a world of mortality. His shirtless top, the part Leanna found most appealing, glistened with post-coital sweat and a cigarette hung low from his teeth. He could look for days but the faint squeak known as Leanna's voice spilled into the air and split his concentration. 'Come lay with me.' He tried to shake it off, act as if he hadn't heard it. Staring still, he looked to get lost. To his dismay, a guide came to break him out. Leanna's impatient arms wrapped around Jay's heavy shoulders holding him tighter than he ever wished to be held. A kiss on the neck. Another on his ear. She wanted more but Jay pretended he didn't realize it. 'Yeah?' 'Come play. Come on.' 'I have things to do. You know?' 'Like what?' 'Like things. An explosion of words spill from her mouth, 'I'm falling for you.' 'We've fucked twice. How can you be -' Barging in, showing Jay who owned the house and room, 'It just happened. I don't know. 'If it's not what you're wearing,' eyeing up her pointed tits, 'Than I'm not sure I'm interested.' 'Fuck you.' 'You already did.' A shake of the head and another strong drag. Soft sobs were suffocated by the cloth. To the scene, Jay pulled loose another cigarette and lit it in the doorway. Always people to see. The events, the limousines, the hired help. Living the life of a superstar had taken some getting used to but once you fall into place and understand the scene you must fit, it becomes logical. It becomes easy. Standing in the center of the Banquet hall, Jay was finally feeling comfortable in his black Armani suit. He was finally feeling comfortable with a cigar in place of a 27. Everything seemed easy. That's when he felt the hand of fellow Establishment member, Octavian, on his shoulder. He had a strong grip, one that caused a quick head jerk to make sure he wasn't in danger. 'Getting used to it yet?' Jay sucked off the cigar and blew smoke over his head. 'Mostly, yes.' Pausing a second, he added, 'What's this for anyways?' 'Steve. Something of a way of paying homage to him while he's in the hospital. More of an excuse to drink fine wine and smoke Dominican cigars I guess.' 'Really?' 'Yeah, kinda' wild.' Jay looked at his own cigar before taking another draw, 'Who's hosting? I mean, it seems a little weird really. An event for someone who isn't even here.' 'Honest?' 'Yeah.' 'Steve is. It's a way of keeping his friends close I guess. He shows them to a good time, lets them enjoy themselves without even having to say hello.' 'Bet he gets enough "get well soon" cards too, huh?' 'Too many.' They each laughed, short and to the point. 'You enjoy these?' 'It's an acquired taste. I remember the first one. Not so much. As time goes on though, you meet people. You recognize faces. Something about them just clicks and you feel like you are someone. You know, someone special.' 'And what if I am someone special already?' 'Then you have to show everyone just how special you are. You can't just sit back and hope people know.' He takes another drag, a good drag. The smoke poured through the gaps in his teeth. 'How do you do that?' 'I don't know. I guess winning is a good start. But not everyone cares about our profession. Most people seem to ignore the things we do out there.' He sighs a second and continues, 'So for them, look confident. You had it for the wee-' 'No,' he cuts in, 'I mean actually having it. Like owning it. What's that like?' 'You ever play sports when you were young?' 'No, never.' 'Ok.' He searches for another example, something relatable to Jay. 'You know that feeling you get after winning a match? The relief that it's over and you just added a check to your win column?' 'Yeah. That's a decent feeli-' Octavian cuts in now, 'Take that and multiply it by ten. Then take that feeling and wake up with it. Work out with it. Watch TV with it. Shower with it. It's with you all the time, it gives you a sorta' swagger when you walk.' 'It's what makes people know you're something special.' 'Exactly.' Jay grins, takes another draw from the cigar, and dances around in his leather shoes. 'You think I got it in me?' 'If Steve didn't think so, you wouldn't be at this banquet and you wouldn't be talking to me. He sees something special in you. He sees a future. It's hard not to see really. So yes, you have it. You just have to go for it. You can't say "I'll do it next time". There may not be a next time.' 'Yeah.' 'You nervous?' 'I was, yes. Something got taken away from me though. Not sure what really. I just feel lighter, like I can fly even.' 'Well you better fly. And don't think about coming down. You hang up there until you're ready to come down yourself.' 'Thanks man. I appreciate it.'
