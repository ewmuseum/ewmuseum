Back on the Road

4 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/06 I’m going out on the road for the last time and I’ll be constantly travelling around the country, hell, around the world, in order to follow my dreams. To pursue my career to it’s logical conclusion. To keep the promise I made to my wife. The cost of this to me is meaningless and I plan to succeed no matter what. My seemingly petty ambitions are the last thing on my mind now. Then... “Breakfast’s ready, hun,” Mary called from the kitchen area in the next room along. Jacob could already smell the cooked bacon and fresh coffee wafting in from the kitchen in the next room over and instantly it made his. His wife’s cooking was to die for and every time he had to go back out onto the road he insisted that Mary cook him breakfast beforehand. Mary was happy to oblige he knew; she believed it would be a suitable deterrent should his attention ever wander to any other girl. He considered the notion and found it to be completely preposterous. Not the deterrent part; if it made Mary feel better about him being away so often then that was fine by him. No, he had a problem with whole cheating part. As if I would cheat on her, he thought to himself, his faced contorted at the very idea. Jacob packed the last of his neatly folded shirts into his bag and then zipped it up securely. Realising he couldn’t exactly go to breakfast wearing nothing but his boxer shorts, he pulled on a pair of grey sweat pants he’d found lying on the floor and the blue vest next to it, the one he’d gone running in the night before, then headed out of the bedroom door. He grimaced upon entering the claustrophobic hallway, he was too wide to walk down the corridor normally, instead he had to walk sideways like some kind of two-legged crab with herpes, in order to gain entry to the next room. Mary had never complained about the place to him, she just put a positive spin on things every time Jacob had complained about something - which, to be fair, was as regular as clockwork - but he wanted more for his wife. He wanted more for his possible future family. Upon entering the kitchen he was greeted by a warm, but oddly arousing, smile from Mary who was just putting the plate stacked with bacon on their make-shift dining table. “Morning,” Jacob said, drawing closer a the kiss. Mary wrapped both arms around his neck and kissed him. Jacob lifted her up by the waist, her flowery dress wafting upward, teasing him with a glimpse of her pink lace thong. Damn, he absently thought, She really doesn’t want me to go this time. Mary hated it when he had to go on the road and although she understood that his wrestling was their main source of income - and his passion - she constantly did things subconsciously that always made Jacob think twice about leaving the apartment at all. Just after the Christmas break was the worst; she’d woke up early and wandered around the apartment naked. That time, he had to admit, it’d almost worked. Later, once he’d finished my breakfast and changed into a pair of light blue jeans and a plain navy blue hooded sweatshirt, Jacob kissed her goodbye. Jacob hauled his bag out of the bedroom and set down by the door, as he reached for his heavy winter coat. That’s when he noticed the doctor’s appointment card on counter by the door and eyed it warily. Jacob turned to Mary, his left eyebrow raised speculatively. “Going to the doctor’s today?” Mary developed a nervous expression Jacob recognised immediately - and could do from a mile away - and then grinned. “Just a check up.” “Are you sure?” He asked her, feeling rather stupid just having to. Mary placed her hands on her hips defensively. “Jacob, of course I’m sure. Just drop it will ya?” Jacob rolled his eyes, sighed and picked up his bag ready to leave. “Whatever. See ya Tuesday.” Mary kissed him on the cheek and then replied. “Yeah, later.” Now... The smell ruminating within Jacob’s apartment now was far less appetising than a freshly cooked breakfast by his late wife. The air was stale, its aroma consisting of an unhealthy mix of sweat and feet. He sat up on the bare mattress and grimaced upon the sight of the bare room surrounding him. The place summoned up memories of Mary, memories more painful than anything he’d ever experienced before. He just couldn’t stand it anymore. Mary’s stuff was gone now; distributed amongst her family and friends. The majority of his stuff was also absent; sold for what money he could get. This is Jacob’s last day in the apartment; he’d promised to give the keys back to the landlord at midday, but he had no intention of staying that long. He wanted to get out of the place as soon as humanly possible. Jacob got out of bed and threw on the same pair of sweat pants and t-shirt he’d worn for the past three days and hauled out his bag from the main wardrobe. Jacob crammed his screwed up t-shirts and pants into the bag without regard of placement or neatness. There’d be nobody to consciously nor subconsciously tempt Jacob to stay here now. Today, he’d be leaving this place and going on the road for good, keeping the promise he’d made to Mary on her deathbed. Upon leaving the apartment minutes later, he slammed the door behind him purposefully. He’d be able to leave the place behind him with minimal ease, but the memories would follow him for the years to come. He just hoped he’d be strong enough carry them. End
