Two Time Loser

14 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/14/2005 ( The Events that led up to the discovery of Eva after the show) She couldn't remember the last time she felt this sore. Rollins had been a tough advesary and in the end he had one. That thought gnawed at her pride like a virus to a computer. Scowling she slammed open the door to the back entrance of the arena. Rollins had beaten her...not again though, no, she would have her rematch and when it happened, it would be a whole new ball game. It was time to do some research on old Mr. Rollins. Slinging her gym bag over a throbbing slender shoulder she stalked into the private parking area and fished for her keys to the rented car for these past two weeks. Bone weary, angry, and exhausted she dumped her bag on the ground and shoved the key to the door in giving it a quick twist. As her hand came to connect with the handle the sound of a bottle being kicked across cement startled her. Jerking her head up those icy eyes cast their sight about looking through narrowed vision for whomever it may have been lurking about in the shadows. ' Hello?' No answer. Frowning she mumbled under her breath. It was most likely some reproter wanting to get a scoop. It was a part of the gig as a professional wrestler, or in this case, a diva who had rapidly become a favorite. Heaving a breath she turned to her door once more and jerked it open. Releasing it she stepped to her bag and bent to scoop it up only to freeze at the sound of her door slamming shut once more. Slowly she rose to turn only to have her eyes widen in shock the split second before the butt of a pistol connected with the side of her side sending her into oblvion's arms. ' Take her into the van and work her over good. Make sure to dump her behind the dumpster over there. Do not mess this up comrades.' The last thing she remembered was the sound of footsteps walking away, but that voice...she knew that voice all too well... -- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Eva woke up to her mouth feeling dry only a few moments later, groggy and completely disoriented. Chilled eyes worked overtime trying to focus as she groaned from the throbbing pain in her temple. What had happened? She remembered getting to the car, hearing a noise, opening the door and then.... Those eyes widened as she realized she couldnt shut her jaw and move her hands. ' Wakie wakie Freulien.' Shifting her head to look above her prone form despite the pain Eva's heart sank to her toes. There at her head loomed the face of a man she had met in the past on a few occasions. The smile on his face was sadistic, the promises behind those curling lips the kind of ones worst nightmares. The jostling of her prison cause that body to shift, head turning to the opening of the back door of the van as another member joined the private little party. ' Well?' ' He is gone to meet with the other one and collect the diamonds. Apparently he came through.' Blinking Eva rolled to her side trying to get up only to find herself shoved back down. ' Well, does that mean we let her go?' The moment of silence was thick in the air with tension. Eva's eyes shifted from one face to another silently praying that they just let her go. ' No. He said to finish the job as a reminder never to steal from him again.' ' Excellent.' No sooner had the word left his mouth than Eva was already kicking wildly at the one at her feet. The heel of her boot connected solidly with the face of one of her would be assailants with a loud crack followed by the spewing of blood from the front of his broken face. ' BITCH!' She screamed behind the gag to no avail as he launched himself over her form and brought back a fist striking her hard enough to rocked her whole body to the side with a muffled cry of agony. She was seeing stars almost literally as rough hands rolled her back to her back and sent another back hand to the other side of her face. Tears stung her eyes in bitter rage and pain as once again she tried to fight back despite the uselessness of the endeavor. Pain exploded in her side as beefy fists flailed away at her ribs severely bruising them as the three fought for dominion. In the end they would win this first round. She was finally subdued, one holding her arms above her head the other straddling her legs. All of them were breathing with labored breath. ' Fiesty whore isn't she?' ' Indeed. Petre had said she was. I can not help but wonder if she is as good as he said she was in other things as well.' Eva growled and struggled for a moment only to go deathly still at the next words. ' I don't know...perhaps we should find out?' At this point complete panic settled into her very bones as she screamed against her makeshift gag and back to thrash around wildly. The lioness in her had awakened full force once again as she twisted her boody those hands curling into claws as nails savagely gouged into the wrist of one of her assailants. The van was rocking heavily as the trio struggled for control. A sharp fist across her temple soon had her seeing stars again as she valiantly fought for self preservation. Another fist to that temple had her completely sense, only the warmth of her own blood telling her they had opened a wound. She was loosing this battle. A ragged cry escape past her gag as she felt herself subdued once more. The feel of hands roughly tearing at her clothing would be the last memory she had other than the words which would burn in her memory for a long time to come. ' Compliments of Petre..bitch.' Darkness ensued ...... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Eva slowly regained consciousness. The pain that came with consciousness was like being punched in the gut. It stole her ability to breath. Bitter tears stung at the back of her eyes as she whimpered against that gag still in place. Agonizing against it all she began to move heavly arms shifting as she struggled against the last of her binding managing to pull swollen and bruised wrists free only to tear that gag away with a strangled cry of pain. She didn't have to look to know what had happened. Everything about how she felt was enough to signal the tragedy that had befallen her. Curling into a tight ball on her side she wept bitterly not having the will to drag herself from this place she had been dumped. Broken glass shards dug viciously into her hips and arms as she trembled and sobbed in shock. It felt like forever she had been here when she heard the sounds of approaching feet and a familiar voice. Lestatt! Eyes popped open wide as she finally gained a momentary burst of willpower. Pulling herself up to her hands and knees she began to crawl, dragging that groggy and aching body from behind the huge dumpsters at the arena. She could only pray she had the strength to let him know she was here....
