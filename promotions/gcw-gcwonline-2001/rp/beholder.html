Date: Timeless They ask me why I’m evil now. They wonder why I jumped ship to Dies Irae, away from everything they believed in. Away from Eva. Away from Tessa. Away from LeStatt. Away from those three letters that everyone seems to love so much. GCW. I am no more evil than everyone else in Dies Irae. No more evil than most in GCW, for that matter. For evil is in the eye of the beholder. In the eye of he who declares something evil. It is natural, then, that Eva and Tessa and LeStatt declare us evil. In declaring us evil, they declare themselves good. More importantly, they explain their own inferiority. People are fickle creatures, so confident in their own abilities. No one can be better than them. They are monarchs of kingdoms of their own creation, the kingdom of Global Championship Wrestling. For months, Eva and LeStatt ruled because there were none better. And then, I arrived. And then, Dies Irae arrived. They couldn’t beat me. Couldn’t beat Dies Irae. And so, Irae became “evil”. A group seeking to rid corruption has been unfairly pilloried as evil. And why? Because Shadow likes to dress in black? Because she and Virgil are vampires? Because Dawn McFarlane, Vivica, and our fearless leader make Eva look like a joke by comparison in both appearance and skill? Yes. Emphatically so. All of the above. You see, Knight’s not stupid. Nor is Eva. They know that with us around, they will never again rise to the heights that they once occupied. So they raise rebellion against us, to secure their own spots. GCW is their home. It’s supposed to be safe and secure. A place they can dominate effortlessly. But we make that impossible. In the brackish pond that was GCW, we came in as alpha predators, a pack of hunters that swallowed all the smaller fish. And now, there are but two groups left: The eaters, and those to be eaten. Perhaps nobly, the smaller fish band together. THIS is the nature of the Asenscion. A group of prey banding together to avoid the predators. But THINK, everyone! Think of what happens if we’re gone! All that happens is that Eva and LeStatt go back to consuming you. They’d turn on you in a heartbeat if it suited their purpose. So really, what is the difference between Dies Irae and Eva’s little clique? The fact that Dies Irae cares about its own. The fact that I care what happens to each and every member of our group. We have come together for a common purpose that is far beyond this sport, far beyond mere wrestling. Eva and Knight ally to cling to their pathetic dreams of glory. To grasp at the glorious straw of being World Champion again, a goal that is out of reach so long as Dawn McFarlane holds the World Championship. And, indirectly, this brings me to Nacosto Nerezza. A man smart enough to not fall for Eva’s tricks. A man--nay, a vampire who dances to the beat of his own drummer. Who does what he wants. When he wants. How he wants. Truth be told, Nerezza, I know not why we are set to meet in the field of pitched combat. Perhaps it was due to our conversation a few weeks ago over Miss Windsor. Whatever the reasoning, it is my first time in the ring in some time. My return to battle, against a capable opponent. Against a man who has held tag team championships all over the world. I look forward to this contest, Nerezza. For, in spite of what you may think. It isn’t personal. It is strictly business, strictly in promotion of Dies Irae. Only by bringing GCW to its knees will it flourish. Only by inflicting what appears to be cataclysmic damage can it be revived. In the process, the unrighteous will fall away. In the process, the best will thrive. GCW, ask yourself. Are you against us because we’re evil, or are you against us because we’re better than you?
