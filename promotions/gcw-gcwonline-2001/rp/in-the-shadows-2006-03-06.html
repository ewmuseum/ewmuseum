In The Shadows

6 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 6, 2006 ---- Prelude: Cast Under Shadow No sleep, No sleep until I am done with finding the answer Won't stop, Won't stop before I find a cure for this cancer Sometimes, I feel like going down and so disconnected Somehow, I know that I am haunted to be wanted ---- I need a little room to breathe. That was the thought going through the mind of hundreds of journalists around the world, trying to get a good glimpse of the stage that lie in front of them. Many of them were reminded of their younger days, moshing in the pits at their favorite rock concert. Reaching out desperately to touch those in which they idolize. Others felt like sheep to slaughter, tightly packed into an arena conference room... waiting for their time to die. Reguardless of their ethnic backgrounds or genetic makeup, each had the same thing in common. They wanted the big story, they wanted to be the journalist everyone talked about the next day. Each and every member of the Rampage tournment had a similar dream in mind for the up coming Pay-Per-View. The winner would walk back into the United States of America as the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. The loser? Back to mediocrity and the every day obscurity in which they lived their everyday lives. The hushed whispers of the room turned into muffled small talk as GCW Play-By-Play commentator James Bryan made his way up to the podium. The flashbulbs from media photographers began to flicker, putting almost a strobe light effect on JB as he stopped with a smile on his face in front of the microphone. He raised his hand the silence the applause of the media, who eagerly brought out their tape recorders, microphones, notebooks, and pens to get the big one. 'Welcome everyone to the Global Championship Wrestling News Conference, I am James Bryan... Play-By-Play Commentator of GCW. In today's press conference we will be talking with the stars of GCW's RAMPAGE, sure to be the biggest wrestling event of all time here in Japan.' JB paused, waiting a little for the journalists to catch up, 'The first person I will be introducing to you will be one of GCW's newest members. This superstar captured the GCW United States Championship two weeks ago to become only the second woman to do so in GCW History. No woman has ever won the GCW World Heavyweight Championship, and this lady will have the opportunity to be the first at Rampage. Ladies and gentlemen I give you, 'The Black Mariah' Vivica J. Valentine.' 'Bleeding Mascara' by Atreyu plays on the portable audio equipment set up in the conference room for the big media event. The photographers push each other out of the way and fight to get the best shot they can of the beautiful Miss Valentine. As she makes her way on stage she looks out at the masses, almost instantly blinded by the eruption of lights. With the GCW United States Championship around her waist and an arrogant smile from ear to ear, she approached the microphone. After her self-celebration on WorldWide, everyone knew that this questioning session was going to be a good one. 'Hello? My name is Vivica J. Valentine... THE one and only Champion of the United States. My first round opponet for Rampage is going to be Dawn McFarlane. The floor is now open to any and all questions about myself, or course, and the Rampage tournament.' Vivica said, trying hard to put forth a 'professional' image. It was quite hard, mainly because the lack of respect of the media made her want to kick them all in the face. This was mainly due to past experiences though, and she was trying her best to put the past behind her. No matter how uncharacteristic it might be. 'This is Dave Needney, of the Pro Wrestling Inquierer. Vivica as a close friend of both Dawn McFarlane and Shadow, both of the number one seeds of this tournament, how deserving do you think they are of those positions. How well do you see them doing in a GCW tournament... contrary to their brilliant Master of the Mat showings?' A man asked from the audience. It wasn't a question asked about her, but... it was close enough. 'Well obviously I think they're very qualified. Shadow was the Global International Champion forever, has a guarenteed Global Title shot at Rich Rollins, and only lost one time in her career in GW. Her one loss was to Dawn McFarlane, who has made a name for herself in PTC through CW1 and winning serveral Global Championships, including Master of the Mat. I don't think them being qualified is even a serious question. The question should be, is everyone else in the tournament?' Vivica answered, 'Next question... preferribly one about myself in Rampage.' 'Vivica this is Joe Marcellus, of the Wrestling Post. What is Christian Zenith up to these days? Do you think he's going to come back to the ring? If so would you say a return to Global Wrestling is out of the question... or do you think he'd join GCW to be a little closer to you?' Another man asked, obviously aggrevating Vivica. 'That wasn't exactly what I asked but Christian is very busy running his company and I don't see a return to the ring happening any time soon. Next question... my name is VIVICA, I'M IN THE RAMPAGE TOURNAMENT.' She said surveying the crowd for the next victim... journalist. 'Mark Russel, Wrestling Entertainment Weekly. Miss Valentine, you had some experience in Global Wrestling in the ring against Danielle Northsky. How surprised were you when she lost her spot in the tournament to Jayce Bradley?' A man asked, leaving The Black Mariah almost completely dumbfounded. 'Does anyone have a single question about myself?' Vivica looked around the crowd, where no one immediately shot up their hands. It was as if they were all pondering what they could possibly ask GCW's newest champion. 'Alright, fuck you guys. I'm leaving.' Vivica J. Valentine said before turning around and walking back off stage in frusteration. The crowd was flabbergasted by her language and antics as she marched backstage where a staff member began to scream at her. She didn't know who it was, nor was she paying attention to a word they said to her. Why couldn't anyone ask her about her expectations, dreams, or recent changes after winning a championship like everyone else? Why was it so difficult to give her the respect she deserved? Was it because she was a girl? Was it because she was the smallest person on the GCW roster? No matter what she did it was a fluke. She was a side show attraction, when she worked just as hard if not harder than every other member of the roster. As she boarded her limo, slamming the door shut in the face of the anonymous GCW staff member, one thought crossed her mind. Why was it so tough being beautiful? ---- First Passage: The Past I've been watching, I've been waiting In the shadows for my time I've been searching, I've been living For tomorrows all my life ---- From the back of a limo everything seemed to be so peaceful. The world moved by so fast around you as you sat in the lap of luxury. Surrounded by anything you could possibly want, on a much smaller portable scale. A private jet trip back gave her a little bit of time to cool down after the assinine questions she had to listen to at the RAMPAGE Press Conference. Just another reminder of why she had always hated the media. Sure she was probably going to be fined for breaking a contractual obligation, but since when has money been something she is in short demand of? Did they actually believe she was going to sit there and take that bullshit? The travel time had given her a lot to think about while her temper mellowed out. It had given her time to realize what exactly was at stake here. The winner got a shot at the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Their name would go down in the record books as being the best there was in GCW at that point in time in history. Could someone possibly over look something that big? If she pulled this off she couldn't be ignored. Everyone had to stand up and take notice, they didn't have a choice anymore. Yet in order to pull this off, three people stood in her way. The first of which was Dawn McFarlane... the same person who stood in Christian Zenith's way of becoming Master of the Mat and winning back-to-back tournaments in Global. Banging her hand on the mat, she watched helplessly as Dawn McFarlane drilled Christian Zenith across the chin with a European Uppercut. Christian Zenith stumbled back, and Dawn McFarlane took advantage with a hurricanrana. Vivica, wondering what she can do to help jumps up on the apron to get a better view of the match. Quickly through, she's confronted by the Global Wrestling Referee. 'You know you can't be up here!' He yells back at her. 'Like the hell I can't, I'm just trying to see what in the hell is going on.' Vivica sneers in retaliation. 'You don't get down I'll make sure you don't see this match at all.' The Black Mariah gives the GW Official the finger before seeing Dawn McFarlane go for a moonsault and land face first on the canvas. Zenith rolls out of the way and gets up to one knee, trying to regain his focus. Unwillingly, Valentine jumps down from the apron down to the floor... where at her vertically challenged height it becomes much more difficult to see the events in the ring. Suddenly Zenith locks McFarlane up in a leg grapevine, and she screams out in agony before The Midnight Messiah drops an elbow on her. 'Come On Christian!' Vivica screams as Christian Zenith whips Dawn McFarlane into the corner. Zenith follows and attempts a Lou Thesz Press, but finds a dropkick to the jaw instead sending him down to the mat in pain. Vivica J. Valentine grimaces as Dawn regains control of the match. Zenith reaches a verticle base at the same time as McFarlane, but Dawn gets the upper hand and attempts to irish whip Zenith. Unsuccess, Zenith counters with a whip of his own but suddenly Dawn turns it into a handstand on his shoulders. 'What in the hell...' Vivica said aloud from outside the ring, watching almost in slow motion as Dawn McFarlane came down from the handstand drilling Christian with a High Impact DDT. Everything began to move slowly, almost as if it was in a movie as the referee counted the pinfall. One... Two... Three. 'Miss Valentine...' A familiar voice startled Vivica as she jumped a bit and looked towards the door where Marques smiled back at her. 'We've arrived at the manor Miss Valentine.' 'Thank You Marques...' Vivica replied with a sigh. She must've nodded off for a bit. Although she was now awake, the feeling inside of her was very much the same as it was when it happened. She just wished there was something she could do, anything she could've changed to make that match come out differently. Yet the past is the past, and you can't do anything about it. Still groggy from her power nap, she made her way out of the limo and looked up at the massive Zenith Manor North... isolated on a large plot of land just outside the city limits of Chicago, Illinois. The cold breeze blew her long blonde hair in front of her face, quickly making her grasp her black leather bomber jacket close to her body to warm up. Yup, she definitely was home again... and Old Man Winter was there to welcome her home. Although she was still trying to wake up, she couldn't shake the memories she'd seen away. As little as she wanted to think about having to face her own teammate, it was the only thing on her mind. Maybe there was something more than the obvious going on here. They say those who do not learn from the past are doomed to repeat it. It was a line Vivica often heard from her bitchy teachers whenever she began to fail history class from total apathy. Perhaps her old history teacher was right. She couldn't do anything to change the past, but she could change the future. A victory over the same person who last eliminated Christian Zenith from a tournament. What would that mean for her career? Would that mean that she was just as good as the person everyone had compared her to her entire career thus far? A smile crossed her face as she walked up the concrete stairway towards entrance to her home. It was just what she needed... a victory over Dawn McFarlane was just what the doctor ordered. It was about much more than avenging some silly old grudge, it wasn't that at all. It was about respect... not just from the media, but from her friends. Maybe if she won, Dawn would talk to her again like a person. Maybe if she won, Virgil wouldn't be all pissy with her anymore. Maybe if she won, she could make Lia proud of her. ---- Second Passage: The Present They say, That I must learn to kill before I can feel safe But I, I'd rather kill myself than turn into their slave Sometimes, I feel that I should go and play with the thunder Somehow, I just don't wanna stay and wait for a wonder ---- A flash of light split the skies, which quickly returned to a dull shade of gray. The clouds absorbed nearly all the light from the sun, making it seem much later than it really was to the citizens who inhabited the city Below the sound of thunder rumbled through the streets. Although a drop of water was yet to touch the earth, the scent of the coming story could be smelled in the air. A downpour was rolling in. While some people hurried to make it home after one last trip to escape the show, others stood at their windows waiting to behold mother nature and her beauty. One person in particular had no destination at all. All she wanted to do was drive, and never stop. Driving helped her think, she often came up with some of her best ideas while driving... although she didn't drive very often. No billion-dollar vixen ever did. She had a lot on her mind, and didn't quite know what to productively do with it. Working out just didn't do the job anymore, she'd began to do it so regularly it was like brushing her teeth or washing her hair. Suddenly the sound of her cellphone ring 'Bleeding Mascara' by Atreyu could be heard in the car. Without hesitation, she pulled over to the side of the round and flipped open her cell phone. Tucking her blonde hair with baby tips under her ear she place the phone against it... 'Hello?' She said, not even taking the time to look at the number which was calling. 'Hey Vivica...' A familiar voice brought a smile to her face, the stress of the past week and the magnitude of everything going was starting to drive her insane. It was nice to have a friend to talk to every now and again. 'I need to talk to you about something.' 'What about?' Vivica J. Valentine answered, looking up at the Zenith Corporation building she just happened to park in front of. What are the odds? She'd been driving laps around the building for more than a few minutes. Perhaps it wasn't that coincidental. 'Virgil is a little... upset with you.' Lia spoke, pausing a bit before going on, 'That whole cowbell incident last week on WorldWide.' 'Ah...' Vivica sighed on the phone, with her beautiful teal colored eyes looking up into the matte sky. 'I guess it did sort of backfire a bit... but Virgil advanced, and that's all that should matter really. I'm sure it's not going to be his only shot at the Television Championship. Besides, now Chrissy-Poo don't have to be on television every week. He can go back to... I don't know, subscribing to online sex sites or whatever it is he was doing for three weeks.' 'I understand that you get a little riled up sometimes, but you have to work on that Viv. You have to make a conscious effort to not do things like that to your teammates.' Shadow said before going on, 'No matter how funny it might've been.' 'It's just so frusterating... I don't know what he wants from me.' Vivica explained, 'I go out there and pin a guy to win our match. What happens? Nothing. I win the United States Championship, not even a 'good job', I get a dirty look and Nothing. US Title defense? Nothing. The guy isn't even giving me a chance, I don't know what to do anymore...' Vivica released onto Shadow, closing her eyes and taking a deep breath. 'Give him a little bit of time, he isn't like Dawn. He's different Vivica... he's not even human. Things don't work for us the same it works for you. You just have to give him a little bit of time, I'm sure he'll come around.' Shadow explained, 'So are you holding up well? Having to face Dawn and all?' 'Yeah... I'm fine.' Vivica said, 'I've come to terms with it, anyway. I just need some time to get my head straight I think. Everything is coming at me so fast, I'm not used to this life yet. It's crazy.' 'Yeah... it can be hard.' Shadow expressed, 'Hang in there though, you're doing a great job. Good luck in the tournament by the way. Those media bastards might not care, but I know I'm going to be rooting for you.' 'Thanks.' Vivica responded with a smile on her face, 'I better get going, I see something that I havn't seen in a long time.' With that said Vivica J. Valentine exited the silver BMW, and locked the door behind her with a remote lock. Slowly she walked towards an old run down tennis court that lied next to the Zenith Corporation building. She stopped in front of the large steel mesh and ran her hand across it. Remembering the first time she'd ever met Miss Lia Vlazy... inside this very place. As she opened the door and walked inside, almost the entire scene came back to her. It was a day just like today... the rain was pouring and Shadow came out of nowhere. Their fight was seemingly in slow motion, jumping all over the place. Although it was only a matter of minuts it seemed like hours of going back and forth. To this day Vivica still remembered it as one of the toughest things she'd ever done. Yet it was that day that she earned Lia's respect. With that respect came a very valued and strong friendship that held true even today. 'Give him a little bit of time, he isn't like Dawn. He's different Vivica... he's not even human. Things don't work for us the same it works for you.' She remembered the line Lia had just told her a few seconds ago. She was right... Shadow never really liked her until the night she was forced to earn her respect in this tennis court under Christian's supervision. Perhaps the same thing had to be done with Virgil. Vivica certainly had learned a lot and became a much more accomplished fighter than that day against Lia. Shadow was so quick she couldn't even locate her half the time. Almost like clockwork, the skies broke open and a downpour followed. It was exactly like that day, and possibly a day to come. Eventually the day would come where she would have to cross that bridge, whether she really wanted to or not. Yet today there was something more on her mind. She couldn't think about Virgil right now... she had her own problems afterall. A reputation was at stake. ---- Epilogue: The Future? Lately I been walking walking in circles, watching waiting for something Feel me touch me heal me, come take me higher I've been watching, I've been waiting In the shadows all my time I've been watching, I've been waiting I've been searching, I've been living for tomorrows In the shadows, I've been waiting... ---- Bump bump... Bump bump... Bump bump... The emotion from the crowd flowed all over the arena. They waiting patiently for the next match... Round One of the RAMPAGE tournament. Dawn McFarlane vs Vivica J. Valentine. It was the first time Vivica had ever faced one of her own teammates, and it was obvious as she paced back and forth behind the curtain waiting for her cue to come out for the match. Bump bump... Bump bump... Bump bump... It was eating her up inside, it was hard to contain the excitement that began to flow throughout her body. Her heartbeat was ringing in her ears and her stomach was in knots. As uncomfortable as it was she wouldn't give up that feeling for the world. Just a few steps away from breaking out. Just a few days away from coming into her own. Bump bump... Bump bump.. Bump bump.. 'This is it Viv...' she said out loud to herself before bouncing up and down a little bit. Adrenaline had already taken over and she wasn't even out there yet. The Black Mariah couldn't even imagine what she'd feel like when she run out onto that stage. People work their entire lives to get a shot like this, and 90 percent of them never make it, 'You can do this.' It was time to write her own history. It was time to become something more than a media sideshow. It was time to be more than Christian Zenith's valet to the general public. It was time to succeed every expectation that had ever been placed on her since she was a little girl. Vivica couldn't turn back now, it was her shot, maybe the only one she'd ever get. She'd waited in the shadows of her teammates for her time. Her time is now. Music Lyrics: 'In The Shadows' by The Rasmus
