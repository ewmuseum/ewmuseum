Pawn :: Chapter 4

24 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24, 2008 As they sat inside of their rental car, a 2008 Pebble Beach gold Nissan Maxima, with the windows rolled down, the forty-something married couple pleaded their case through an intercom system. The black heavy metal gate that blocked the drive through the large stone wall that surround Southwind Gardens prevented them from going any further, without proper authorization. It was mostly cloudy, but yet a very humid day, nothing unusual for Kansas in late April, and the tiny beads of sweat that rest upon the foreheads of the two could have been contributed by the weather itself. They waiting impatiently inside of the car, just outside the gates to what looked like a very reasonable institution, for a response from the person, or people, on the other end of the intercom. "This is pointless Mary!" Tapping his fingers vigorously on the steering wheel, the man said to his wife. "We've wasted our time coming here!" He continued. "I told you that son of a bitch wouldn't even let us onto the property!" Starting to lean out of the driver side window, he finished exclaiming. The man's wife, Mary, looked very displeased at him, and with a roll of her eyes and a shake of her head, she retorted. "Alec Calix, you should be ashamed of yourself!" Glaring holes through him, she waited for eye contact from her husband before she would continue. "Hello?" Trying to ignore the fact that he knew Mary was now extremely upset with him, he questioned to the intercom, hoping for a response. There were a few moments of silence before a broken up voice replied to Alec, but it was merely a quick answer, asking for the couple to hold on just a bit longer while they waited for the approval of clearance for the couple to visit their son, Kaden Calix. With that, Alec had no choice but to turn his attention back to Mary, and made the dreaded eye contact. She continued to glare at Alec, who could barely look back at her, as if her blue eyes had turned to fire. "We are the ones responsible for Christopher being in there, so I could give a good God damn less what you said earlier this week!" With no remorse for who might have heard, she bellowed. "We are going to do whatever it takes to get him out, and settle all of this once and for all!" Keeping to himself after that, Alec didn't say another word, and hoped Mary cool off quick like should usually did after short bursts of anger. Before Mary could say anything else, the voice on the other end of the intercom system came back through, loud and clear. "You said your name was Alec Calix, correct?" The question came from the same female as before, sounding much younger than Mary and Alec, she was probably in her mid to late twenties. Alec answered back with a short, loud grunt to confirm the receptionist's question. "Well Mr. Calix, I'm sorry, but due to new security measures, Southwind Gardens only allows visitors once a month." With a not so convincing voice, she admitted. "I don't believe a single fucking word you just said, and even if I did, why didn't you just tell us that right off?" Alec questioned sternly, confused at the whole situation. About forty-five seconds to a minute went by with nothing before the young woman had finally thought of an excuse. "Sorry sir, but I'm new here, and..." Still not believing the story, it was Mary who cut the receptionist off. "Are you kidding me?" Not giving any time for an answer, Mary questioned. "You are the same prissy little bitch I talked to on the phone almost three months ago, and now you are trying to say you are new?" She continued to question and rant. "So you let us in, because there is someone in there that doesn't belong, and we are here to take him home!" The desperation could be heard in Mary's voice as she finished yelling at the intercom. There were a few more minutes of silence once more before the young woman replied once more, and for the last time. "Have a lovely day." In confusion, Mary and Alec looked at each other as if they asked each other what that was supposed to have meant. Their question was answered as quick as they would have asked it, though, as red and blue lights from a Kansas State Trooper vehicle flashed in their rear views as it pulled up right behind their rental, and parked. ******* Kaden sat in an uncomfortable chair inside of a well lit room, a room that looked too much like a happier interrogation room from some cheap cop movie. Besides the brighter than white walls, a solitary barred window, and Kaden himself, the only other objects in the room were the table he sat at, and the empty chair on the opposite end of the table. It had been some time since Dr. Sorrow left the room, right before their first "session" was to begin, with some "important business" that he had to tend to first. So as Kaden sat there by himself, he turned to the only form of entertainment he had since he was tossed into Southwind, talking to himself. "You know, I could really go for a grilled cheeseburger right now." He said, licking his chops, thinking about real food. "Actually, I think I'd rather have some company more than food." He continued to chat with himself. "Yeah, what I wouldn't do for some bedroom company with Kyra right about..." He stopped what he was doing as soon as the sound of the door to the room opening echoed in the nearly empty room. In stepped Damian with a huge smile on his face. 'Now, where were we?" Walking over to the table, and sitting down in the other uncomfortable looking chair, he asked. "Ah, yes, we hadn't even started." Remembering before Kaden could even open his mouth; Dr. Sorrow pulled a clipboard out of the briefcase that he carried in with him. "So, how was your week?" He asked. "Seriously?" Kaden questioned with a raised brow. "This is how we are starting this?" He shook his head and rolled his eyes, but with a shrug of his shoulders, he decided to play along. "Well, Dr. Sorrow, I must say, being locked up in a ten by ten box nearly every day of the week is such a thrill!" Full of sarcasm, Kaden begins to answer the question. "Oh, but I'll be damned if you guys didn't surprise me with that whole Global Championship Wrestling contract last week." His sarcasm continued. "There was nothing better than getting the opportunity to be around all my peers again, in an environment that I am comfortable in, being babysat the whole fucking time by the steroid twins." He finished and looked over to the door, noticing the twins looking in through the tiny window in the door. Damian shook his head, not losing eye contact with Kaden the whole time. "Can we be serious for a moment Christopher?" Damian asked. "I understand we kept you in your room for longer than we should have, and I understand that being escorted by Franklin and Robert to your events each week for GCW are going to be annoying, but it is the only way we can let you outside of these walls until we decide if you are fit to be out on your own again." Writing a few notes down on his clipboard, he finished explaining himself. For a second time, Kaden shook his head and rolled his blue eyes into the back of his head. He opened his mouth, as if he wanted to say something, but stopped. He ran his hands over the top of his head and through his blond hair before letting out a heavy sigh. "I don't even fucking see why I'm still here!" Scooting his chair back and standing up, he exclaimed. "I'm not trying to gouge my eyes out, or claw at my own face, nor letting tiny voices inside of my 'special' little head tell me what to do!" He continued. "Oh, and you haven't had to restrain me, throw me in some kind of padded room, or once attacked anyone in attempt to escape this fucking place!" It was if he couldn't stop rambling once Damian got him started, but he continued to do so as he paced back and fourth. "So I say once again, I don't see why you've still got me locked up like some kind of sick science project!" It was as if Dr. Sorrow had his thoughts stolen from him, because he couldn't think of anything to say back to Kaden right away. He continued to jot things down on his clipboard, trying to act as if he was speechless, or clueless on how to justify Kaden's admittance to Southwind Gardens. "You know Christopher, we havve got rules and regulations that I am trying to follow, and if it were up to me..." "Fuck you Damian!" Pounding both fists down onto the table, Kaden roared. "If it were up to you, you would have kept me locked in that God damn room forever; am I right?" He asked, but continued without an answer. "No, if it weren't for lawyers, ethics, and people in the world who actually care about me, I would have ended up pounding my head on the wall inside of my room until death!" Kaden finished. Damian threw his clipboard back into his briefcase, and stood up. "I think we are done here for today." He said as he started to walk towards the door, which opened, allowing him outside, but the institute assistants, the steroid twins, were standing in the way, not allowing Kaden outside. "By the way, Christopher, your parents are here." Turning around and looking at Kaden with an evil eye, he admitted. Kaden straightened up, as if there was hope after all, and he started to pay attention to what Dr. Sorrow had to say. "Yeah, but unfortunately our policy doesn't let stupid little fuck's friends and family visit; so they were escorted off the premises by the police." The door was then shut, and Damian walked away with a sick grin on his face, leaving Kaden in "Session Room #1" to expel all of his anger, which involved throwing anything he could get his hands on at the door and window, not to mention quite a few things he shouted at the top of his lungs that he wanted to get off his chest. ******* A short two and a half hour drive to the northeast of Wichita, Kansas, where Southwind Gardens is located, just outside of Kansas City, Kansas, sits one of the largest homes in Kansas occupied by less than five people. Michaels' Manor, a home fit for a king, is one built by Justin Michaels' great grandfather in the late eighteen hundreds, and willed down through the generations; though Justin himself had to fight the state of Kansas in order to bring it back to the Michaels family Now two people spend their lives inside the walls of that great manor, but Justin's fianc?, Lindsay, who had moved in with him over a year ago, up and walked out on him a day or two previously. Justin had been away on business, and hadn't been home thirty minutes before he realized something was wrong. It was then after reading a note left by Lindsay on a small table by the front doors to the manor, that Justin knew something was not right. He tore off his black suit jacket, and loosened his orange, plaid print, silk tie, before removing his cell phone from the right pocket of his black suit pants. He pressed a few buttons on the keypad and then stuck the phone up to his right ear. It obviously did nothing but ring several times as the disgusted look on his face suggested, and he gave a few more seconds before the voicemail of the individual he was trying to reach kicked in. "God damn it!" The first words out of his mouth for his message obviously explained his frustration. "Something has happened, and it's my fault, and I never expected this, and something has to be done about it immediately!" Justin continued to ramble on. "I though I was doing him a favor, and I think that, maybe I was the one who didn't know better!" Pacing back and fourth, Justin continued to talk to the recording on the other end. "It doesn't really matter anymore, the only thing that matters is how we are going to fix this, and give that guy, someone I hope will still call me a friend in the future, his life back." He stopped in his tracks, and paused, as if he were done recording, but he still had one last thing to say. "So Damian, call me back as soon as you get this, because we have to work something out!" Stressing the fact that something had to be done, he closed his phone before tossing it onto the same table that Lindsay's note to him sat on. Without though, Justin balled up his right fist, and punched the nearest thing to him, the solid oak doors leading to the outside world of Michaels' Manor. "Son of a bitch!"
