Around the Horn!

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/19/08 Quietly the sun sets over the town of Salem Massachusetts. The buildings take on a calm demeanor as the tourists speed up their departures from the closing gift shops. All tend to disappear other than the die hard ghost hunters or just plain disturbing ones who enjoy the occult. Even amongst these individuals there is one single solitary lone man standing amongst the shadows. A large man who is out of place here as he would be anywhere. Shaman watches the town square grow quiet as he stares at an old church. That is where he will be heading. He walks across the street looking at the church. "May it be known that i have business here. I have business here every year about this time, but there are several aspects that have me glued here this year. One aspect in the arrival of Dangerous Games." Shaman walks to the door of the old church and opens it as he walks in. The rafter are spooky as the sun seems to cast beams through the shadows. Shaman grins as he walks forward and someone in robes is ahead of him. Obviously by the build it is a female. She lifts her arm and there is a bag being held out to Shaman. "As requested every year you have come through. I have much need for this at Dangerous Games. Thank you for your time Rose." She nods and turns around as she kneels before a Pegan Pentagram. Shaman turns and walks out of the church not looking back. He walks to the center of the town as he grins looking about. "Hard to believe atrocities against people for believing they were witches happened here. Now there are covenants everywhere throughout Salem and many many practicing witches and witchcraft. Hard to believe what the Puritans fought so hard against has taken over the town. Use of positive energy from a negative effect. I guess that's why they call it Karma." "Let me taker you on a tour i guess it is the least i can do. I mean after all it does have meaning for me and into Dangerous games." Shaman climbs up in his Chevrolet Tahoe and drives off as he slows down at a corner. "The Witch House." Shaman grins as he puts the truck in park and looks at the house. "Lemme see here if i remember this right." Shaman taps his temple a second. "In February, 1692, three accused women were examined by Magistrates Jonathan Corwin and John Hathorne. Corwin's home, known as the Witch House, still stands at the corner of North and Essex Streets in Salem, providing guided tours and tales of the first witchcraft trials. John Hathorne, an ancestor of author Nathaniel Hawthorne, is buried in the Charter Street Old Burying Point. " Shaman scratched his forehead as he thought for a few more seconds. "By the time the hysteria had spent itself, 24 people had died. Nineteen were hanged on Gallows Hill in Salem Town, but some died in prison. Giles Corey at first pleaded not guilty to charges of witchcraft, but subsequently refused to stand trial. This refusal meant he could not be convicted legally. However, his examiners chose to subject him to interrogation by the placing of stone weights on his body. He survived this brutal torture for two days before dying." Shaman looks around and shrugs. "Sounds kind of familiar doesn't it? Torture, segregation, murder, and last but not least death. It always seems to me that alot of people would rather kill what they don't understand rather than leave it be and allow it to be in the natural order of things. They say this place has over 552 recorded historical documents of each trial and incident. You know they even have the torture devices and remains of some of the victims around town most notably the Town Clerks officer where the fingers of Joseph Bishop.Creepy that they'd disrespect the dead by keeping their remains for personal display." Shaman shakes his head as he drives around looking for something. Something only he seems to be looking for. He stops at the Witch Museum. "This is the Salem Witch Museum. It was made from an old church and turned into something that will force people to remember the deeds of the past. Kind of morbid in a sense, But if we do not remember our past we will be doomed to repeat will we not?" Shaman drives around the block and grins as he turns the corner heading onto the highway out of town. "You know there is something about a guy called the Headless Horseman. Folk tale or true story, but here is the true version of it. Didn't happen in Sleepy Hollow actually was originally in a town called Tarrytown." Shaman turns his head checking his mirrors as he begins to speak. "One cold winter night, early in the New Year, a certain Dutchman left the tavern in Tarrytown and started walking to his home in the hollow nearby. His path led next to the old Sleepy Hollow cemetery where a headless Hessian soldier was buried. At midnight, the Dutchman came within site of the graveyard. The weather had warmed up during the week, and the snow was almost gone from the road. It was a dark night with no moon, and the only light came from his lantern." Shaman clears his throat and looks over at the side of the road as the road grows a little darker at night. "The Dutchman was nervous about passing the graveyard, remembering the rumors of a galloping ghost that he had heard at the tavern. He stumbled along, humming to himself to keep up his courage. Suddenly, his eye was caught by a light rising from the ground in the cemetery. He stopped, his heart pounding in fear. Before his startled eyes, a white mist burst forth from an unmarked grave and formed into a large horse carrying a headless rider." Shaman passes a car and drives on down the highway as he looks about his mirrors once again. "The Dutchman let out a terrible scream as the horse leapt toward him at a full gallop. He took to his heels, running as fast as he could, making for the bridge since he knew that ghosts and evil spirits did not care to cross running water. He stumbled suddenly and fell, rolling off the road into a melting patch of snow. The headless rider thundered past him, and the man got a second look at the headless ghost. It was wearing a Hessian commander's uniform." Shaman slams his hand on the steering wheel and slows down a bit looking over his shoulder. "The Dutchman waited a good hour after the ghost disappeared before crawling out of the bushes and making his way home. After fortifying himself with schnapps, the Dutchman told his wife about the ghost. By noon of the next day, the story was all over Tarrytown. The good Dutch folk were divided in their opinions. Some thought that the ghost must be roaming the roads at night in search of its head. Others claimed that the Hessian soldier rose from the grave to lead the Hessian soldiers in a charge up nearby Chatterton Hill, not knowing that the hill had already been taken by the British." Shaman looking for something slowed and turned on his blinker as he turned down a small road. "Whatever the reason, the Headless Horseman continues to roam the roads near Tarrytown on dark nights from that day to this." Shaman slowed to a stop as he flipped the Tahoe into park as he got out and walked up onto a porch and knocked on the door. An older woman came to the door and smiled seeing Shaman. "Andrew so glad you came this time of year. I was worried you wouldn't come." Shaman smiled and kissed the woman on the forehead. "Thanks Grams glad you have the same place. I wouldn't miss it for anything in the world. You do know i am in a huge wrestling match for GCW?" The older woman smiled and looked at him patting his cheek. "You are big and will win it. I am sure of it. If not they don't know what they are missing." Shaman smiled as his grandmother was always positive no matter what. She looked at him and grinned and walked into the kitchen as she yelled back. "Go get cleaned up and wash for dinner. I got something special made that you will like." Shaman knew it best to not argue with his grandmother and made his way to the bathroom and washed up. He then returned to the dining room table and sat down as she brought out a huge plate full of steak and potatoes and green beans. He smiled. "Been awhile since i had a home cooked meal." His Grandmother poked him in the arm. "Looking a little lean there better eat at least two plates." Shaman looked up to say something, but something in her eyes made him retreat. "Yes Ma'am." She walked back into the kitchen as Shaman looked into the camera. "If anyone says anything about this i will hunt you and your family down. You won't like the results." Shaman ate the food as he talked while his Grandmother was in the kitchen. "Dangerous Games is a place where you will make yourselves known. Create some achievements, make the battles even bigger and epic. Take a shot at a title you deserve. Concentration strategic teamwork, working with people you normally wouldn't spit on if they were on fire." Shaman turns his head as his Grandmother comes walking out with another full plate of food and a Blueberry pie. "I think this is going to be like Thanksgiving." His Grandmother kisses him on the cheek and pats his shoulder as she walks back into the kitchen. "I swear to everything i hold sacred you say something about this i will maim you for life!" Shaman continues to eat the food as he stops and looks around. "Of all the Bagwells, Murrays, Knights, there are more people wanting to prove themselves even more. I want something out of Dangerous games and that is to win. Will i win i don't know truthfully. I am one half of the Tag team champions of GCW. Bryan Mayhem and i are a dangerous pair. You would think that it may be a bad idea that you think he and i will not fair well. People look at me as an underdog. I like that because underestimating me is a curse everybody has suffered through in the past." Biting another mouthful of food he continues to eat. His Grandmother comes back out and looks at him. "Where is that Bryan fellow friend of yours? He should have been here this time. He came last time and this time you come alone. I should whip his ass for standing up a dinner invitation!" Almost choking on the food he smirks. "Grams no worries there, Bryan had a business delay. He got caught up somewhere in Britain for a thing. I am not sure of the details." She she shrugs and looks over her shoulder as she heads back to the kitchen. "Your brother said he is coming tomorrow. I know you won't be here so i will let him know you came and said hello." Shaman shakes his head. "Nothing scarier than that woman. She puts her foot down you look to see if it is on your toes. She is like a Nuclear bomb wrapped up in a firecracker form." Shaman drinks some iced tea and looks at his hands. "Biggest question is who will step up at Dangerous games and grab ahold of the shot at the titles or simply be the Champion. Working like a mad man and getting yourself at the top alone is the biggest deal anyone could ever wish for. Problem is what if your coming in to simply hurt someone?" Shaman laughs as he leans back against the back of the chair. "Happy days oh so happy days." Shaman laughs as he leans forward again and eats his food.
