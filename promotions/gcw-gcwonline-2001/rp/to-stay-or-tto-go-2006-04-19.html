To Stay or tto Go?

19 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 April 06 I should have just fucking quit. I almost got through saying it all too. I have other things that need to be attended to, things that are far more important than anything GCW can give to me. Instead that son of a bitch had to push me. Instead I allowed myself to be sucked into his little game of cat and mouse. I could be on vacation with Mary right now, making sure she didn’t do anything to jeopardize our baby’s life. I could have her in my arms and enjoying a sunset on some beach. Instead? Bryan Mayhem. Not only that but this week I get to be in Florida, a place I do love but this week it is a place that I get to embarrass myself at. American Gladiators? Did I suddenly get pulled into the fucking 1990’s? I don’t know exactly what the hell Caldera was thinking allowing such lunacy to happen on live television but he must have smoked something with Team Baked to let them talk him into this abomination of a WorldWide. The only good thing that I can even remotely pull out of this is the chance to bash Seymour Almasy’s head in. After Rampage I have been waiting for that opportunity that’s for damn sure. Yet therein lies the problem for me. Do I really want this anymore? What real reason do I have to stay? GCW has basically been turned upside down and inside out. Gone are Jason Dread, Johnny Borealis, Mike Hardy, Octavian, The Jhub, Toxic, Twizzy, Rei Tiu, Johnny Lexicon, and most importantly? Michael Stevens. Sure he and I went to war like perhaps no two people ever have in this company’s history but because of the lengths he went and limits he pushed me to I can only feel one thing towards him. Respect. In my eyes Dawn McFarlane doesn’t even deserve to hold that title in her hands or wear it around her waist. That title is Michael Stevens. After the hell that I sent him to, he fought back and took it fair and square. Dawn? Sure what she did was impressive but quite frankly I don’t think she has what it takes to beat Stevens on his best day. I obviously couldn’t. Then again I haven’t been at my best since NC-17. Which is what really makes me question my desire for GCW and wrestling period. I haven’t felt that fire burning like it did last year in months. Last year I was determined to prove to the world that LeStatt Knight was the ONE in GCW. Not only that but to prove to everyone who doubted me in the early years that they couldn’t hold a candle to my greatness. But not I feel that I have accomplished everything that I could have ever hoped for. So what could possibly be next? Another drink would be next I think. It’s a hot night in Florida, as is the case with most nights. A dip in the hotel pool sounds refreshing but a shot of tequila? Now that’s heavenly. It’s also most likely the reason that my mind is all over the place tonight. It’s also more than likely the reason I wish Mary was here so I could fuck her until the sun came back up. But that’s neither here nor there. I walk, or stumble if you prefer over to the window and stare out at the dark waters that spread out endlessly. Sweat beads dripped down my face and my chest. What to do? Do I just walk away from it all and live happily ever after? I haven’t felt the warmth and love of a family in so long that the very thought of it seems so foreign now. I was so young when Cindy and Starr died ? Their warmth might feel foreign but the emptiness I felt the day they were put under ground still eats away at me. It defined me for so long. It made me into what I am, that I know for sure. I have been an evil man for so long now because of everything that has happened in my life. I revert back to it only because I know that getting close to people ends in pain and suffering. Even your most loved companions can leave you. My parents Cindy Starr Wylie Mi Tio y Tia RaZ ? That fucking prick. I don’t think I can afford having Mary leave my life. I don’t know if I could handle losing another family. So perhaps I should leave GCW behind and give Mary all of the love that she deserves. What if she grows tired of me being away so much? What if I miss birthdays, holidays, and random events like our baby’s first steps because of GCW? I would never forgive myself if she left because I neglected them for something that’s not all that fucking important anyways. It’s not like the paychecks mean anything to me. Hell for the past year all of my GCW checks have been going straight to funds to help the Hurricane Katrina victims. Point being is there really is nothing keeping me tied to GCW. It’s all new people besides a handful like Eva and ? Bryan Mayhem. Mayhem is like a damn gnat at a barbecue. No matter how many times you swat it away it just keeps coming back. Sure this is an unusually large gnat but it’s a pest regardless. I have beaten that man numerous times and during none of them has he ever come close to beating me. Yet now he seems to want to play these mind games and for what? To get me into the ring one more time only to be humiliated? If it wasn’t for him jumping me from behind each and every chance he gets he’d never stand a chance. I don’t understand what exactly his infatuation with me is. So lost in my thoughts I didn’t even realize that someone was banging at my front door. I snap out of it and place my glass down. I can only let out a small smirk when I remember the advice that a friend of mine was gave me. “Good ole Jose” Cuervo ? the only friend that will always be there when you need him.” I walk ? er ? stumble towards the door. Normally I probably would ask who it is or check through the peep hole. Jose? makes you a bit less cautious than normal. Instead I just swing it open. “Que Paso?” “Package for you Mr. Knight.” “Isn’t it a bit late for packages?” “Sorry sir but somebody just dropped it off at the front desk and said to bring it up to you immediately.” “Whatever give it to me.” “Here you go sir so?” I didn’t care to hear anymore and slamming the door in a person’s face usually is a good way to get rid of them. Will he consider me an asshole? Yeah I figured as much when I heard the word mumbled on the other side of the door. The package was large black shoe box, probably once held some combat boots or something. It has been spray painted black so couldn’t be for sure where it came from. On the lid it has an envelope taped to it. “Fucking groupies.” I toss the box on the bed and slide back over to my window only to pick up my glass and fill it back up. I sit down on the bed and look down at my glass as I begin to slide it back and forth between my hands. I take a large swig before turning my attention back at the box sitting on my bed. I have gotten a lot of bullshit like this from random fans, mostly women who wanted to sleep with me before. Naked pictures, marriage proposals, keys to the room they are staying in, etc. If nothing else they were always good for a laugh. So I tear the enveloped off the box and open it up. It contained only a small index card with a short note on it. The message was very short butt with the mixture of hair, sweat, darkness of the room, and many glasses of tequila I couldn’t make out what it said right away. I focused as best as I can and let the words out as they come. “Family memories can last an eternity.” I place the card down on the bed and pick up the shoe box, placing it in my lap. I rest my glass of tequila in my left hand and with my right slowly pull off the lid. Instantly my eyes burned and a lump forms in my throat. I try to get a grip, perhaps convince myself that I just passed out from drinking too much and this is a hallucination. The thud of the glass and the spray of the liquid inside of it reminded me that it wasn’t. Like a punch to the gut or a chair to the face it slammed into me. I couldn’t stop the tears from coming down. The feelings of helplessness ? that feeling of emptiness swept me up. All from a small, dirty teddy bear. A bear that belonged to my daughter. A bear that she was buried with. I fall to my knees, the bear clutched in my arms. Then it hits me ? THIS is why I can never leave it all behind. Then everything goes black.
