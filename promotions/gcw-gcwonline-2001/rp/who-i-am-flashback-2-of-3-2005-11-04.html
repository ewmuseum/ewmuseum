Who I am (Flashback 2 of 3)

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/04/05 The room is dark. There is no sounds emitting. No signs of life whatsoever. The camera pans around room, but little can be seen. The only hint of movement is a small sliver of light reflecting off of the chrome surfaces as the camera continues to pan. Suddenly the door to the lifeless room swings open, and the light comes on. “Remember Ricky, nobody is to blame. Just try to understand. This is all a part of the cycle of life.” A voice speaks to him. “I know all about the cycle of life mom” Ricky replies brushing a tear away from his cheek. Ricky approaches the bed where his brother lies motionless, and limp. He stares silently as tears continue to flow. A voice can be heard from behind him, however Ricky hears nothing but murmured chatter, unable to make out the words being spoken. Ricky reaches out to touch his brothers hand and speaks one sentence. “You lied to me” Ricky turns away from the bed and starts to head out of the room when his parent intercept him. Ricky stops briefly looking up at them as hurt fills their eyes. He just shakes his head slowly and pushed past them. His mother starts to follow, but is cut short my his father. “Let him go hun. He needs time to himself.” Ricky enters the hallways tears again filling his eyes. He makes his way towards the elevator and stands there leaning against the wall, forehead pressed against the sheetrock, when the familiar “ding” of the elevator is heard. Ricky doesn’t move as the door slides open. A strong booming voice is heard from behind him. “Ricky I’m sorry. I know how much you two..” “You know shit.” “Ricky please understand. I couldn’t tell you. I made a promise to?” “Whatever. You never cared about him, you never cared about us. You simply cared that he carried your ass to the tag team titles time and time again.” “Ricky it’s not like that?” “Whatever Sean. Or should I refer to you as SuMa” That’s the name everyone knew you by right?” Ricky stares at the giant in front of him. The legend of CCW. The man who had every title, the man who lifted his brother into stardom, and the man who had adopted Ricky as his own little bother. The man who betrayed him by not telling him of his brother’s condition. “I’m leaving. Go say goodbye to Shane, it’s the least you can do” Ricky hits the button the elevator again this time is opens right up. He enters without speaking a word and stares through SuMa as the door slides shut. Ricky slumps against the corner of the elevator as the whir of the engines provides a slight comfort, when suddenly a loud clunk is heard, and the elevator jerks to a stop. Ricky stares at the ceiling and just mutters 3 words before collapsing to the floor in a virtual darkness form the days events. “I hate you ?” ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Ricky blinks twice realizing things still appear dark. He strains his eyes realizing that he is no longer in the elevator. He crawls from the bed, and to the bathroom door. As he opens the door he takes a step in then notices he is staring into a closet. “What the hell”.where am I?” Ricky looks around for another door, and slowly pulls it open peeking out. He tiptoes down the hallway, taking in the unfamiliar settings. Suddenly an unfamiliar voice is heard from the kitchen as panic strikes Ricky. He thinks through the events leading into now and can not recall where he is or who else may be there. “Ricky, you’re up.” Comes the voice from behind him causing him to leap into the air. “Yah’I am Sean. Why am I here?” “You collapsed in the elevator when it broke down. Found you lying in the corner when the doors opened back up.” “Yah, I guess that on top of Shane was just too much for me, so quickly. Thanks for looking after me. I assumed you called my mom to let her know where I was?” The demeanor on the face of the big man is grim. He puts an arm around Ricky’s shoulder and leads him into the kitchen where Sean’s mom is there hanging up the phone a tear falling from her eye. “Ricky, I haven’t seen you in so long you have grown well” she speaks. “I guess. Can I use your phone” I need to call home? “Ricky” Sean starts? Ricky whips around at him as he notices the aura in the room “Ricky, its your parents. They were broadsided by a drunk driver on the way hom from the hospital.” “What!” Your lying to me! Where are they!” I want to talk to them! Are they ok?” Sean’s mother wraps her arms around the outraged young man in her kitchen hugging him tightly. “I’m sorry Ricky, they didn’t make it.” “NO! This is too much’I cant take it anymore” Ricky lunges for the door screaming out in mental anguish as he is restrained by Sean. Sean has a huge arms wrapped around Ricky both of them shedding tears. Sean’s mom joins the fray as the all sit silently, aside from Ricky who screaming out the words “I hate you” over and over.
