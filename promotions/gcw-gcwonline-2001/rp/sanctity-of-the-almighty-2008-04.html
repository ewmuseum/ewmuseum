Sanctity of the Almighty

April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April "The most powerful thing one possesses is his mind. If one is in indecision, the mind will work itself out." [Fade into a distorted shadowy image in a mystified alleyway] “I want the pleasure of killing you with my bare hands, not this gun.” The shadowy figure throws down his pistol and slides his hand over his head, as if pushing back hair. He takes a step closer to Zacharia, but he doesn’t move an inch. Is this confidence or is this shock and fear? He tries to reach for the cross around his neck, but it’s not there. How could his cross not be around his neck? He never takes it off! He frantically feels under his sweatshirt and in his pockets, but there is nothing there. The shadowy figure stops. Detail in his face is nonexistent, but his body language shows he is smirking. The figure reaches into his pocket and pulls out a chain which was followed by a cross. Zacharia’s cross. The detail in the cross was explicit, showing every grain and scratch in the cross, but furthermore, Zacharia can see himself in the cross. He stares at the cross for a few seconds, he is not himself. He is bloodied, he is scared, he is frail looking. He does not have the impact of appearance that his muscular figure usually possesses. For the first time, his feet uproot from the ground and take a stammered step backwards. He catches his balance but is entranced by the cross swinging back and forth in this shadow’s hand. He begins to raise the cross up and steps closer to Zacharia, backing him up against the edge of the alleyway. Zacharia follows the cross as if he is hypnotized by it and then sees something else when the cross swings to his left. It is another shadowy figure. He looks behind him but nothing is there, but it is ever so real in the cross. He stares at the cross with the intent to figure out the image’s identity when suddenly the cross freezes in midair. The shadowy image holding it is also frozen, but the image behind him is more animated than before. It walks towards him, putting a hand on his shoulder. “So this is what you do when you venture out into the streets at night. You are pathetic. To ever think that you were capable of acting out the will of the Almighty. Zacharia, you are a disgrace to the church and are unworthy to follow through with the will of our Lord. Sacrifice yourself right now to the Lord in the presence of his human speaker and you will be forgiven for your sins.” Father Emanuel, who has now taken off his hood, revealing his face holds out a knife to Zacharia. He has a very serious look on his face, the look that Zacharia has noticed is present whenever he is talking about his religious views. He is stuck. In time, space, and the ever so real confusion that he feels day in and day out. “Father, but why must I sacrifice when I can change for the better” I can conform to your ideas better if you would only tell me my flaws. I could change my ideals and carry out what you think is...” “Is right Zacharia” It is far beyond what I feel is right. These are the demands of our God. Have you learned nothing in over a decade of this divine education?” Zacharia hesitates, “Yes, father. I believe that I have learned and practiced it and carried out His will until the end. This end. I feel that I have done it right. I do not understand what you expect...” “Stop right there in your speech Zacharia. Expect” Expect? Have you not listened to me? It is not what I expect, but what He expects. Who ever told you to feel? You have a distinct purpose in this world, and that is being a mercenary of the Almighty. It speaks of your role very distinctly in the Modern Testament. You were to follow it. You were not to believe or feel certain things. It tells you what to feel. It tells you what to believe.” Zacharia does not know what to say. He stands there with his mouth open, getting dryer the longer he waits to speak. Should he speak? He can’t recall any time Father Emanuel has ever talked to him in this manner. This is not reality, it can’t be. Time has stopped. The only one who has the power to do so is God Himself. Did he stop time? Is this the message that he really wants to convey through Father Emanuel? “No, my Lord would not ask for my life when there is so much more to do. Father, I do not know what you are possessed by, but this is not the Lord’s will.” Father Emanuel hands Zacharia the knife and turns his back to him. Zacharia turns the knife over in his hand repeatedly until he reached his decision. He reaches out, now feeling a purpose, and empowered, and grabs the cross from the image still stuck in time before him. He puts the cross around his neck and slashes the image in the throat. Father Emanuel turns around at the sound of tearing flesh. “THIS is His will Father. To rid the world of evil, not to rid the world of who you decide. You cannot play God. We are all conduits to His will, and I BELIEVE and I FEEL that what I have decided is right in His eyes. If it is not, where is the mercanary to take me down” Where are those employed by the Unknown to take me to this Hell? No Father, I am done with this game.” Zacharia throws the knife down and blesses himself holding his cross. Suddenly, Father Emanuel runs towards him holding another knife and runs him through. All life and power slowly fades from his body. The last thing that he sees is his cross dangling from Father Emanuel’s bloodied hand. [Fade out to black] “Who ever told you to feel” You have a distinct purpose in this world, and that is being a mercenary of the Almighty. It speaks of your role very distinctly in the Modern Testament. You were to follow it. You were not to believe or feel certain things. It tells you what to feel. It tells you what to believe.” That is what I need to do, I need to go as I feel, as I believe is right. I must take in the knowledge of the Almighty, not of the book written by Father Emanuel. I have been carrying out His will for long enough to know what is in His interest. I will not fail again. I was carrying out the will of Father Emanuel, that is why I was being punished. That is why I have been beaten twice over. I must break away and follow my heart, the true connection with the Lord. I must follow my heart, do my will. Not the father’s. ****** Waking up against the wall of the confession booth, Zacharia feels a new passion, a new sense of determination. “Were those the true ideals of Father Emanuel” Has that been a vision of God? Did I experience this event last night as an answer to my prayers? Does it matter? God does not have control over any being, otherwise He would not need amercenary to cleanse the world of evil, would He not? He obviously sent me a sign when I was young to go to the church of Father Emanuel, and He has now sent me a sign to take a leave of the church and use my knowledge and experience to my will. I believe that our wills are one.” The light pouring through the windows of the church give an illumination to the stations of the cross in the stained glass. The one right in front of him was of Jesus carrying the cross. This was his burden. The cross was the burdens of the human race, the sins, and he was bringing those sins with him to die. He was being sacrificed. He is the lamb of God, and the lamb’s soul purpose is to die, to be the sacrificial lamb of human kind. “Jesus did not want to die, but it was God’s will, the purpose that he put into the young baby Jesus Christ when he was born to Mary. His purpose is to die, my purpose is to cleanse and kill. I want to fulfill this purpose. I want to kill those that are evil, I want to punish those that have wronged. If death is the only way to cleanse and deter them from their ways, so be it. They chose their path long ago...” As he stood up, he looked at the basin of holy water meant for the baptisms in the church. Every morning possible, Zacharia dunks his head into the basin to cleans himself of the sins of the night before. This might be the last time in a while that Zacharia is in this church. He needs to clear his head and fulfill his own will for the time being. He walked over to the basin and said his morning prayer. “Forgive me Father, for I have sinned. I have confessed to Father Emanuel the night prior, but I stand here to wash away any remainder sin that still lingers in my body or mind. With this blessed water in Your name, please cleanse me in body and spirit so I can fulfill my purpose on this plane. If you see fit, take my life with my head submerged in Your holiest of water. In Nomeni Patri et Fili Spiritus Sancti.” He submerges his head into the water. After a few seconds the water starts to turn red. The basin now seems as if it is filled with crimson blood, boiling around the head of Zacharia Taylor. After about a minute’s time, he raises his head and gasps for breath as the remaining water rolls off of his face. He opens his eyes and stares into the basin of water that was once as clear as air, but now is deeply colored of blood. He reaches up to his face and feels cuts around his eyes and his once broken nose. He is bewildered as the pain is reanimated into his mind. He has forgotten a couple nights before his loss to Murray and Mathews, but what truly angered him was the taunt and humiliation by Fox. He was so caught up in the loss mentally and religiously that he forgot the impact it had on his body. The dream, he was bloodied. God wanted him to see the damage to his face, wanted to show how he thought of himself frail, bloodied, and scared. This was a loss of more than body and mind, but of religion. “I now know that my question of religion has been answered. Now that I am on the right course and the right path, now knowing the will that I must follow, my own, I am protected by His power. I do not know what he has planned for me, but I know what I must do. I look into the basin of blood and think this is a sign. This must be a sign. It is the sin that is being burned off from my flesh. It was my purgatory, it was the cleansing of my own flesh. I now know my purpose. I know what I must do.” Clyde Fox The name rings throughout his mind. He cannot get it out of his mind. Clyde Fox The name does all but extinguish pain in his heart. A man of true evil. Someone that must be dealt with. There is no reason for him to be standing in front of any audience. There is NO reason for anyone to be susceptible to his harmful ways. He is not only evil, but he has gone mad. He must be silenced, he must be punished. It will take more than just holy water to cleans his soul, he is too far gone. “I must deal with Clyde first. The others will get their time, they will be safe for the time being. But I must get Clyde Fox.” With thundering, determined steps Zacharia walks out the door. The sun never seemed so dull before. The heat never seamed so distant. It was as if this new purpose separated him from the world. His blood burned with anger, his sweat boiled on under his hood. He turned to the left, needing to get to the pay phone. He looked every direction, not afraid to stare a passerby in the eyes until they looked away, trying to find something in each person. There was someone in the phone booth, of all times, why now. Zacharia sat down on the bench beside the booth and started to look at his battered hands. If water were to flow onto his hands like a river, there were plenty of cracks and crevices for the water to flow into. Scars littered his hands, and fresh cuts stood out. He has never analyzed his hands as he did now. He never noticed a birthmark on his left wrist, the way hair didn’t grow evenly on the inside of his wrist, or how badly his hand was scared from the cross in the remains of his house so many years ago. He closes his eyes, trying to think of the day his parents died, but all he could see in his mind was Clyde Fox. With more anger he opened his eyes and stood up. He could not bare to just sit and do nothing. Sitting was not getting him anywhere. Sitting would not get him closer to his mark. If the man inside the booth would just hurry up. There are far more important things to talk about than a birthday cake for his boss. Finally, he could not take it anymore. He walked up to the booth and pressed his scarred hand onto the glass window pain. “I need to use the phone, sir.” The man looked at him in a strange way. His eyes went over his face before answering. “I will be off the phone in a moment. Not long, I promise.” *What was he looking at? Why did he look at me like that* Zacharia hit the glass again. “Really sir, I need to use the phone. It is important!” The man looked at him again, and then stared at his hand against the glass. He said “I will have to call you back Louise” and hung up the phone. He hurried out of the booth and sat on the bench. Zacharia took up the phone and looked over at the man that did not dare stare into the booth anymore. In the moment of realization, Zacharia’s reflection came into focus. He now knew why the man stared at him in such a way. His gashes were worse than he expected, and his nose still had dried blood above his lip. Blood was stained onto his skin and stuck in his scruff. He looked as worse as he felt. Clyde... “Bagwell! Bagwell, I need your help.” “Zacharia, what’s up man” I’m sorry about your loss. I don’t know what happen, man, it was just not your time I guess. What ya need?” Bagwell’s voice seemed up beat. His voice was being drowned out slightly by wind gusting against the phone. “About that, I figured some stuff out, I know what I need to do. I have a new purpose... I’m not going back to the church for a while. Father Emanuel...” “Is outside his mind. I told you this for how long Zacharia” You are so thick with your religion, you just don’t see though some things. Finally, I finally have you in then?” “In” Yeah, I’m in, but I need a favor first. I need you to do something for me?” “Yeah my man, what can I do” You need me to pick you up?” Bagwell seemed even more excited, but a slight hint of worry was in his voice. Zacharia never asked him for anything. “I need to get to Ottawa, Ontario...” “You need to get where” Ontario, Canada? Why? Man, I don’t know. You have to fight... Oh, right! Clyde. Kincaid is, well, he is busy taking care of some stuff. I will swing buy. I will get you where you need to go. I can set you up with a bus ticket and whatever you need. Will you be ready for the fight?” Zacharia hesitates for a moment, grimly smiling. “Oh yeah, I am getting ready. That is why I need to get to Ontario...” “My man, I like how you think. Where do you want to meet...” *** Stepping off the bus never felt so great before. The air was different up here compared to New Jersey. The night sky has never been so brightly lit with stars before, at least never seen by his eyes. Ottawa. Clyde Fox. The two have been streaming through his mind for the last day. His face felt a lot better, the stinging was going away, but the cool crisp wind gliding over his face was a sweet deterrent of this pain. His body was throbbing, but now with a new direction, a new location, there was no time for thought of pain, only of purpose. A phone booth lit up to his eyes across the street. What were the chances of Fox being listed in the phone book? This was his only chance at finding him. Even under the protection of God and night, it would be foolish to go up to people on the street asking where Clyde Fox resides. The phone book was similar to the one he used back at home, and flipping to the “F” section was just as tedious. There was only one Fox, Clyde in the book, and the address was right next to it. There was a map of Ottawa inside the phone booth. He realized that he was only a matter of three blocks from the address. He started to walk on. The night air turned to a chill the closer he got to the house, the later at night it became. He was not used to the thin air and cold chill. Clifton never got so cold. A bus went by with an advertisement for a television show. He recognized it straight away, it was Pokemon. God damn that show. God damn Clyde Fox. Was this a sign? Isn’t everything a sign? One more block. Around the corner. A bus pulled up to a bus stop twenty feet from the corner that he turned. People were getting off, people were getting on. He attracted a couple odd stares as he walked by, but his eyes opened when a tall, goofy looking man walked off the second exit to the bus with a blue shirt on and a grimly sewn stuffed animal on his shoulder. What are the chances? What are the chances that there is another person like Clyde? No, there is no chance. The air suddenly became warmer, his blood started to flow faster. He started to follow Clyde. He was carrying shopping bags in his hands. One of them said KB Toys and the other said Toys R Us. Even the way that he was walking was aggravating Zacharia. The way that he was prancing up the street, the way that he was swinging the bags, the way that he had the stuffed animal on his shoulder. He followed him up the street and up to his house. He stayed outside of his side window, watching Clyde sitting on his sofa watching television. He steadily starts to flip through channels until it fell onto the religion channel. He panicked at the site of the cross and threw the remote at the screen and yelled, running into the other room. Zacharia slipped into the shadows and found a place to sit and watch, looking for a weakness in his rival. He was looking for something to hold against him, something to push into his face and scare him... What could he use... The cross. He ran from the cross. Zacharia looks down at his chest and it was as if his cross was glowing. That was it, that is what he can use against Clyde. The cross... the cross...
