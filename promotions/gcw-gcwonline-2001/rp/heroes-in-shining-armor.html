Heroes in Shining Armor

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless It's 6 in the afternoon in the northlands of Missouri. Some people are headed home form work, others are on their way in for second shift. But if you're anyone who's anyone in the Northen Mo. geek community, there's only one place to be. Things for Thinkers on SE 10th St. in Bethany. It's in an old strip mall right next to the Joe'n'Go (which by the way has the greatest crullers this side of the Mississippi). It used to be a discount furniture store, so it's pretty huge. 3500 square feet of total geekdom. Racks and racks of game books and comics. Three glass cases full of collectible card games and the like. A storehouse of limited edition figurines and replica swords. Anime, comic, game convention, and Kevin Smith movie posters plastered on the front glass; eliminating any possiblity of natural light creeping in and protecting the denizen of dorkdom from the ridicule of random 'cool' passersby (Most of whom are either going or returning from some dead-end job pumping gas or wrangling a register. Yeah, they're so cool.) You will never find a more retched hive of bad hair and geekery. We must be careful. The sign on the door says 'closed', but the door is open. If you belong here, you know it. A little thing like business hours won't stand in your way. There's two long tables of laptops plugged into the free hub, engaging in sweet, sweet, PvP melee on the Breakbone server. There's another table of card throwers. YuGiOh duelers and the few remaining Magic players flip cards and swear at each other and their bad luck. A cloister of pasty-faced high school freshmen are circled around Michelle, the quasi-goth Dungeon Master whom holds court as geek-queen in her fishnets, B-cups, and black lipstick. She's not a bad DM, but there's a reason that her only regular players are the high-school frosh's. Every one of her campaigns is the same. Slay a bunch of Orcs and Vampires on your way to confronting the mighty Succubus of Doom. Yeah, it's cliche. But if you're a pasty-faced geek with little hope of getting any attention from a girl, Michelle is always in need of new followers. But it's next to the register is where the real action takes place. That's where local heros Dave 'The Rogue Paladin' Montgomery and Jim 'Meatshield Trainwrecker' Brandt pontificate on all things geek with long-time store appointee and fount of knowledge Max 'Greedo' Wallace. (It's worth noting that Greedo isn't an employee. Some of the younger people think he's the owner, but they're wrong The truth is, Max used to hang out by the counter all the time back in the day. Eventually, the fat-man that owns the place started letting him watch the register when he went on Twinkie runs. Eventually, Greedo started watching the counter all the time, and the Fat-man went into hiding. That was like 10 years ago. Nobody even knows if Greedo gets paid, but that's one of those things you just don't talk about.) Greedo picked up his name five years ago when he started talking trash to Meat about SCA combat drills. Meat invited him out to a heavy fighter practice one time. Max was so busy talking trash that he hadn't even swung his 'sword' when Meat clubbed him over the head with a mace and knock him out cold. The joke was he didn't eve get a shot off, just like Greedo the bounty hunter. (Dammit Lucas, hear me now. Greedo didn't shoot first! Han waxed him in cold blood, cause that's what a bad ass smuggler does. Don't edit in weakness just so that Luke 'the crybaby' Skywalker doesn't look like a total bitch in comparison. You have the power to make it right Lucas. Do it, do it now!!) And so, that's where GCW's d20 duo are planning their assault on the Tag Team titles, believing the best ideas come when one is on completely familar ground. The two of them are absentmindedly sparring with a pair of Force FX lightsabers (the cool glowing ones with build in sound effects) while Greedo berates them with questions. Greedo: Come on!!! Do it for me!! Meat: For the last time, NO! Greedo: Pleeeeeeeeese! Meat stops in the midst of his lightsaber duel with TRP to stare in credulously at Greedo. A lapse which allows TRP to wack him lightly on the top of his head. Meat: OW! Seriously, I'm not going to feel up Eva during the match just so you can say you know a guy who knew a guy who groped someone famous. TRP: Besides, she's cool. She actually talks to us, which is weird. Because she's like, way hot. Meat: Dude. TRP: Super hot.. Meat: DUDE! That's SO two weeks ago. TRP: Right, sorry.. Meat: Besides, I'm not going to demean one of the few people who'll actually stand up for what's right. Meat gave Greedo a little extra look, just to insure that the subject was closed. With his sensabilities satisfied, Meat again reached out with the Force.........FX saber toward TRP, whom blocked his thrust easily. Greedo: So what ARE you planning to do during the match? The Guild continued to spar as they thought and answered. Somewhere off on the other side of the store one of Michelle's mind-slave frosh's watched the lightsaber duel in awe, much to Michelle's chagrin. TRP: Well, we've already fought Ambrosi and Almasy to a standstill. I think now that we know what to expect, we'll be able to go the extra step. Parry, parry, slash and duck. Meat: And we're even up with Team Baked, 1 and 1. TRP: Lost the first time because we underestimated them. We have to respect their willingess to go balls out till they get knocked out. Meat: But we totally beat them in 17 syllables the last time we fought. TRP: We've offered them a case of Cheetos not to spit the bong water in our faces this time, so we'll see what happens. The Rogue Paladin began a whirling attack using Form V saber technique, which Meat was able to block only because his arms are way longer than TRP's. After several blocked attacks and the requisite saber clash and push, Meat took a knee in Jedi meditation as TRP prowled around the counter in classic Darth Maul fashion. TRP: And that leaves the Brand's. Meat: Don't say 'The Brand's'. Say it like that and I think Tom and Terry Brands. The All-American dudes from Iowa. TRP: I thought that was The Foundation's schtick. Meat: It is, so call them Unite. Or call them Josh and Gray, I don't care. Don't make them sound like NCAA champions, cause we'd be SO screwed. TRP: Agreed.. And with that, TRP and Meat flung themselves at each other again, this time both whirling in a semi-reconstruction of Anakin and Obi-wan on the catwalk. Greedo however had stopped watching entirely. Something had piqued his interest and he was now typing furiously at the keyboard of his laptop, searching for something. Meat climbed up on a chair, which groaned under his weight but somehow held firm. Meat: Give up, I have the high ground. TRP was undaunted as he lept to the top of the glass counter, then turned to swing down at Meat. unfortunately he was met with a tap of Meat's saber to his belly. TRP fell to the floor in mock agony as Meat took The Paladin's saber and wailed in falsetto Meat: But you were the chosen one! Most of the card throwers applauded, the WoW'ers raised their liters bottles of Dew in salute, and Queen Michelle found herself getting a little bit moist in spite of herself. (This is the part where the story would get REALLY interesting, had fate not intervened) Greedo: Guys, I think you really need to see this. (Yup, there it is.) Greedo put his laptop on the counter and spun it so that TRP and Meat could see. Pictured were Josh and Gray Brand, the header of the page read 'Were-kin'. TRP: What the hell is that supposed to mean? Greedo: It mean, they're were-wolves. Meat let out a deep laugh at the mention of it. Meat: Right, and I'm Dark Lord of the Sith. Greedo: Dude, just read the article. And TRP and Meat did read the article, which took 5.6 seconds. Not because the article was short, but because all good geeks are power readers. TRP: How in the hell did you find this? Greedo: I did a search for topless vampire nuns for a forums skin I was building and ran across it. It didn't register until you called them Josh and Gray. TRP: Normally I'd ask what you were doing looking for naked vampire nuns, but seriously, what website is this. Greedo: conspiracy-seed.com Meat: There's links to half of Dies Irae here. TRP: Greedo, are you serious about this crap? Greedo: There's more to most of the stuff on here than there is to the grassy knoll theory. TRP ran a hand along the side of his face in frustration and disbelief. TRP: Greedo, this is a bunch of fanboy crap. Greedo: So there's nothing out there in this world that you couldn't explain? The possiblity exists. TRP: Hypothetically, yes. Greedo: So? TRP: So I think you need to get some sun. Take a vacation. Even Meat and I have to get outside once in a while. Meat looked up from the screen, a somewhat worried look on his face. Meat: Dave, some of this stuff is pretty indepth. I mean, it could explain a few things. TRP: Like what? Like the fact that they've got to beat someone in the ring on Thursday. So what if they spend their time pretending to be werewolves or whatever? We wander around sometimes in armor wacking people with rattan sticks. Kettle and pot dude. Meat: Still, it might be worth reading. TRP: And hypothetically speaking, even if they were, it's not like they're going to 'wolf out' in the ring and slash our throats with big nasty pointy teeth. They still have to beat us in the ring, which they haven't done. It all comes down to who wants it more, and I know I didn't invoke the Silmarillion for nothing. So let's quit sweating the details and throw down on Thursday like only we can. Meat thought for a moment, before smiling wide in a toothy grin at the prospect of actually combat. After all, werewolve stories all ended the same way. They got taken down by heroes in shining armor.
