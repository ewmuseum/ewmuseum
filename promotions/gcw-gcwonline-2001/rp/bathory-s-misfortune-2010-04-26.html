Bathory's misFortune

26 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/26/10 1963 The basement is cold and balmy, and looks almost like not a single soul has been down here in years. The giant gravity furnace that manages to heat the entire house is humming an annoying drone type sound. In the wine cellar a low whistle sound is heard. There are so many holes and cracks in this building that when the wind blows through a crack you can strike up a tune with a whistle. Anton, young and drunken, covered in sweat and black, is digging coal into the furnace. It’s a painstakingly annoying job that must be done 3-4 times a time, sometimes more depending how cold it is. Anton just moved in this huge mansion on top of Belmont Hill and is already starting to regret it. It’s paid for, which makes life easier as a farmer, and sure there’s the small inheritance bestowed upon him, but there’s just too much work involved in keeping up with this old rickety thing. The mouth of the furnace is wide open, asking for more food. Anton figures if he shovels a bigger bundle in now, it may save him a trip later on. He makes haste and shovels a huge bundle into the waiting inferno. All he’s thinking about is that next sip of alcohol. The sooner this task is done, the sooner he can sit upstairs in the main room and drink himself into oblivion. The last shovel is heaped into the furnace, and he makes his move to close the mouth when a crackle is heard followed by the furnace vomiting out a bright red ember that lands on the forearm of Anton. The shock hits first, followed by the instinct. He looks down and watches as the ember burns a hole into his arm. He shakes his arm violently, shaking the ember from his flesh. Anton grits his teeth and screams through them. In an act of anger, he picks up the shovel and swings it at the wall. One would expect to hear a loud ding or clunk sound, but instead the shovel goes right through the makeshift brick. He looks at the spade of the shovel buried deep into the wall, cocks his head left, ignores the pain in his forearm and violently pulls the shovel from the wall. A large chunk comes with the shovel showing a huge hole. Anton walks towards the hole, peers inside but can’t see a thing. He starts to pull the wall away with his hands revealing a five foot high deep dark cavern. It’s about 3 feet off the ground, and is about 6 feet wide. Anton pats his chest than pockets, than looks around. Towards the back of the furnace room on a shelf is a flashlight. He rushes towards it, grabs it, turns it on and shakes it to make the beam stronger. He comes back to the hole he excavated and flashes the light into the hole and begins to investigate. He can see nothing. The beam illuminates only a dark cave. Human nature being what it is, this hole has intrigued him. He hikes up his pants and pulls himself into the hole. Anton is an average size man, but still has to squat down to make it through the hole. He begins to move slowly, his heart pounding in anticipation of where this hole may lead. He keeps moving, sweeping his flashlight back and forth to catch a glimpse of something interesting. He turns around and see’s his basement, the hole is a good 50 yards behind him now. He starts to pick up his pace, heart still pounding. He’s heard a lot of things about old houses like this. The older inhabitants use to bury money, especially during economic crunches, gold sometimes jewelry hidden away. He continues his pace until the bottom falls out from him. He lands with a thud, and a jolt runs through his knee. He yelps out in pain, followed by a few select cuss words. His fall was only a few feet, but when you are not anticipating it, the fall could feel like a mile. The smell becomes stronger, and a heavy mist seems to succumb him. He looks around the blackness. He dropped his flashlight and it must have turned off when it hit the ground. He begins to grope the ground around his general vicinity and he finally gets a hold of his light. He turns it on, shakes it for a brighter light, and what he views in the beam causes him to slam backwards against the wall. He stood in a large cavern teeming with the bones of Belmont’s lost souls. Atleast a hundred skeletons most still intact, their clothes having long since disintegrated in the cold storage of the tomb. The beam flashes on one curled up in a fetal position in a corner. Another lay besides a small boulder, it’s skull cracked, pieces still lying strewn about. A pair of skeletons lay along the wall, their humerus and radius bones intertwined. They were holding each other in their dying arms. One was sitting up, a bottle of centuries old whiskey sitting propped against the pelvic bone. Next to that soul was a skeleton with long black hair, showed both her horror and death pose at once. On the wide plates of her browned pelvic bone lay minute bones, and a skull the size of an apple. Anton vomited, the bones of a mother and her unborn fetus. There’s a large pile of assorted bones about 2 feet high. They almost appear to be put there on purpose, as if to hide something. Anton wipes his mouth, stands up slowly and realizes he can extend his legs. He walks to this bone pile slowly, afraid to disrupt the remains of those in this small room. He tip toes almost as if he’s sneaking up on someone. He reaches the pile of bones and quietly moves them away one at a time. He shivers and holds back bile as he moves each bone. With each bone, he begins to wonder who was the owner, what they looked like, what they did to deserve dying down here, and even more gruesome how they died. It seems like hours, but it’s only a matter of minutes when he comes to the chest that’s hiding under the human wreckage. Anton was expecting this chest to be locked, but to his surprise it was not. It’s ingredients were also a surprise….. 1562 It was the year of the Soul Capture. The year according to prophecy when Satan and his demons were to come to the earth, take on human form and take who he wants to hell. In the Bible there’s a version written about this called The Rapture. The rapture has yet to happen, yet most believe in The Soul Capture. Europe was going through war, fighting in the name of God, yet most believed the fight was lost. Elizabeth Bathory was believe to have taken form of a beautiful woman to lure those into her chambers and commence with The Soul Capture. She resided during the summer in what’s now called Belmont. Her castle, the largest most palatial thing on the countryside can be seen from the harbor during the summer. The story has been told before. She would chose those pure and capture them. They would than be held in a chamber handmade by her servants until she felt necessary to use them. Not all would make it to the torture chamber where she would shower in their blood to become as pure as them. Others would starve to death and wither away. Those unlucky enough to live through the starvation would often sit in the urine and feces of the deceased. They would be held hostage in this tomb, some for months, with a large chest filled with 50 gold bars. She would torture them with the gold, knowing that they would never be able to enjoy it. Elizabeth Bathory was executed with this little secret passageway. Upon her arrest she ordered it to be sealed shut, never to be come upon again, not until 400 years later that is. 1980 The O’Neil boys were in the field, just south of the hill from which their home rests. Jason was picking apples from orchard while Bryan, a young teenager, sat under a tree listening to music. His hulking form blended against the large trunk. He paid little to no attention to his brother. Jason’s wounds were still healing from the previous night’s beating, but he showed no emotion to them. A loud explosion was heard north of them. Both boys jolted as a billow of smoke followed by a loud drunken cheer filled the day sky. Birds and rodents alike ran for cover. Coming from an enormous hole in the hill below their house is their drunken father. He is laughing up a storm, jumping up and down like a maniac. His boys smile at each other. Whatever it is, they see a smile on his face, and anything is better than what he normally shows. “I did it boys!!” Bryan pulls his headphones down and looks at his father. “What?” “Well I just made a secret passageway from our house to right here!” He stands up tall holding his hands high in celebration. Jason looks at his brother and shrugs as he goes back to picking his apples. He keeps thinking to himself why on earth did father make a secret passageway? Anton runs over to Bryan grabs and gives him the biggest hug in the world. Jason see’s this and is just as shocked as Bryan. This is a man that has never shown any form of emotion or love towards his children, and now he is hugging them. Bryan lets a smirk leak from his face. “Father, why do we need a secret passage?” Bryan asked. Scratching his head, “Well..S-Since our house is so old..we…uh might need to sneak out just in case in catches on fire or something. Yeah.” Anton turned his head and started to stare at the hole. Bryan immediately sniffed the a different motive. There was something his father was not telling him. Later that evening Bryan tip-toed down the steps, each making more noise than the previous. His father lay on the couch, bottle of Wild Turkey empty under his arm. His mother stripped naked, whip marks on her back, her face buried deep in Anton’s groin. Bryan saw the powder residue on the glass table. Both will be out for a very long time. Bryan flips on the light at the bottom of the steps and illuminates the basement. The gravity furnace hissing and humming as it warms the entire house. Drips are heard from leaky pipes and the whistling of wind through the cracks. Bryan walks around slowly, investigating his surroundings, he notices he is still on his tip toes. He stops and calms himself. There’s a fear and adrenaline running through his body at the same time. He is not afraid of the basement, but he is afraid of getting caught snooping around. He’s also excited about unveiling his father’s secret. He continue his search but to no avail. He starts to become frustrated. He has searched the wine cellar, the laundry room, and even his father’s work room and he has found nothing. Where the fuck is this secret passage? He starts to give up when he notices a curtain drying on a line by the furnace swaying back and forth. He approaches slowly trying to catch where the gust is coming from. He comes to the curtain and stares at it. He raises his hand and waves it back and forth slowly. There is no gust coming from this side, but perhaps maybe the other… Bryan pulls the curtain down and his adrenaline turns into outright joy. He see’s the hole carved into the wall. He puts his hand into the wall and reaches back as far as he can, but feels nothing. He looks around and begins to ponder whether or not to crawl inside. He runs back towards the steps and strains his ears for any signs of life from upstairs. Once he’s convinced that he is in the clear he rushes back towards the hole and pulls himself in. His massive form has to crawl on his hands and knees through the dark tunnel. He’s halfway through the hole when he realizes he forgot the flashlight. He curses himself and starts to scoot backwards out of the hole. He keeps turning his head to look behind him so he does not miss the drop. He gropes with each foot before committing to each step and that’s when something on the other side grabs his leg and yanks on it. He yelps in fear and tries to scramble forward but the person on the other side has a vice grip and won’t let go. Bryan’s yelps turn into whimpers than followed by tears and sobs. He can’t escape the grasp. He’s finally pulled from the hole, his body landing with a thud knocking the wind out of him. He rolls over on his back, grasping for breath when he see’s his father, eyes bloodshot, bags forming under them, a white foam at his mouth peering down at him. He definitely looks drunk, but angry at all. “Being nosey boy?” “No father, I just wanted to see the secret passage. I was just curious.” Anton hands him the flashlight and motions him forward. Bryan lies there, curious as to why his father has been so nice to him. He was expecting the beating of a lifetime, but instead is being encouraged to go into the tunnel. “Grab the fucking light boy. You want to see, go right ahead. I’ll stand here and wait until you get out.” Bryan stands up, dusts off his pajamas and grabs the light. He turns it on and heads towards the hole. He lifts one leg to climb in and looks back at his drunken father. Anton waves his hand forward and mouths hurry up. He starts to crawl again, this time guided by the light. The crawl felt like it took forever. Small pebbles and dirt are digging into his knees and his palms. Every time he bumps against something dirt gets in his eyes and nose. He pauses, sneezes, and than continues on. He reaches the end and waves his light around like a lighthouse on the ocean. Bryan see’s nothing but a large room, followed by another tunnel. At the beginning of the other tunnel stands a chest. All the bones and fragments were removed by Anton years ago and fed into the furnace. He spent years and years digging the other tunnel. Bryan climbs down and walks towards the chest. He runs his hand across the splintery wood. The metal plating is in relatively good shape, very little rust and a relatively smooth finish. He pulls open the chest and falls backwards. The gold shines and twinkles in the beam of his flashlight. Bryan scoots backwards in a crablike walk and bumps into something. He jolts, and stands up quickly staring into the eyes of his father. “Father! Is that real?” Anton nods and walks towards the chest, shuts it and motions his son towards the hole. “Wait Father hold on. Where did it come from?” “I don’t know, but it’s now ours. Through that tunnel on the other side was hole I blew out. I used the sled to push that enormous boulder by the river in front of it. I cemented the remaining holes and left enough C4 at the end to blow a hole in that boulder the size of the moon. Just remember if you ever have to use it, light the shit and fucking run.” “Does mom and Jason know?” Shaking his head, “No it’s our secret son. You tell a soul I will slit your fucking throat. Just remember to guard this fortune with your life, no matter what.” 2010 Both men are staring at the boulder. It’s about the size of a small house, and there’s a layer of cement surrounding it. They are trying to figure out a way to move it and get inside. “Bathory’s fortune has to be in there. Her house once stood 500 feet from it. Since they’ve demolished the place there’s no way to tell where the tomb is now.” Wiping spittle from his chin, “Well Gary, I think we excavate the land right above, maybe like 200 foot from the boulder we might hit the tomb.” Gary puts his hand to his forehead to shade his eyes from the sun. “Well, let’s get that backhoe in here and start.” Ron smacks his hands together, lets out a loud whoot and runs towards the machine. 2 hours later Both men cheered loudly as they hit a blank patch. There was nearly 10-15 feet of dirt to dig through when they came to an opening. Gary jumps inside and realizes they are 4 different passages. He stands in the very center of the room that was once the burial place for over 100 souls. He runs his hands across his throat up to Ron telling him to kill the machine. “Get down here man, we have some more digging to do.” Ron jumps from backhoe and shimmys down the pile of dirt. He begins to look around and see’s the passages as well. “Which one Gare?” Gary circles, looks up and points to the one behind him. “Well, it’s not that one, that one leads to where the house use to be, and with the destruction of the place we would have surely heard about the fortune being found. Let’s split up. You see anything get me on the walkie and vice versa. You do that one, and I’ll check this one. If we end up empty we will both check the final. I’m on channel three.” Ron turns his walkie to channel three and investigates his passage. He turns his light on his hard hat on and walks into his hole. Gary does the same. Ron’s hole leads to nothing but a small tomb. He see’s nothing but remains of what looks like animals. Perhaps family pets. Gary’s hole on the other hand expands open to this gigantic room. The ceiling must be 15 feet high. This room is so big you can probably place a small house right inside it. Right in the center of the room lies an old splintered wooden chest. Gary squeezes the button on his walkie and screams, “RON GET OVER HERE WE HIT THE MUTHAHUMPING JACKPOT!!” Gary gets to his knees, opens the chest and let’s out a scream as he lays eyes on the gold bars resting inside. It was the last thing he ever saw. The last thing he ever heard was a cracking sound, preceded by the last thing he ever felt, two giant hands on the side of his head. Ron rushes into the room to see Gary slumped over something. Ron is confused at the first because he can see Gary’s face and backpack at the same time. It was too late before he realized Gary’s head was turned a clean 180 degrees. A large hand grabbed Ron’s throat from the side. Seconds later blood was gushing from where the hand was all over the ground. The blood was coming out pitch black. Ron put his hands towards his throat and realized there was a hole. He falls to his knees, than his side and he see’s giant thing with long black hair and a painted face holding his Adam’s Apple. Ron gasps for breath only to have a giant boot come crushing down on his face, shattering the faceplate sending bone fragments into his brain killing him. Bryan O’Neil grabs the legs of both men and drags them from the tomb. The only thing going through his brain was the words of his father, “Just remember to guard this fortune with your life, no matter what.”
