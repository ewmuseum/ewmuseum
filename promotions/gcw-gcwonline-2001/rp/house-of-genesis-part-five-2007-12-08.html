House of Genesis: Part Five

8 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 8th, 2007 “You know” he said, “you didn’t have to come along.” Tessa shrugged her shoulders, tossing a lock of hair over her shoulder. “I wanted to see what all the fuss was about,” she told him. Stepping out of his 1977 Chevy Camaro, he grabbed his gym back out of the back seat and tossed it from his side to over his shoulder. Scott lowered his head and reached into his pocket. Though it seemed pointless since Tessa was beside him, Scott still slipped on a pair of shades to try and hide himself somewhat. His efforts were futile though, the flock of women that hung around the gym where he practiced still noticed him and still came hurrying toward him, despite the presence of Tessa there at his side. Like vultures they swooped down one by one, picking at him with their compliments and come-ons. But each was met with the same response; a deaf ear and a cold shoulder. He did as he always did, he ignored them and politely pushed his way into the gym; each time cursing, hoping that the owner would just put in a back or side entrance for him. Cooing, Tessa chimed in “This must be why you are late coming home so often.” Scott looked back at her, not half-so smiling. One of the women there among the crowd took a long look at Tessa, who stood beside Scott and asked loud enough for all to hear “How dare she walk next to him after her deal with King, I bet she already fucked him too; just like she does with all the wrestlers in the locker room.” The words manage to find their way to Tessa’s ears, who sharply turned toward the woman and focused her eyes squarely on her. “Why you dirty?” she never finished her sentence though, as Scott wrapped his arms around her slender waist, preventing Tessa from lunging at her. “What the hell are you thinking?” he snapped, yanking her toward the building to try and create more space between her and the woman. “You hit a fan and not only will your career be dead, but you will be looking at heavy fines or even jail time.” Despite his words of warning, Tessa still clawed and kicked, trying to break free from their fianc’e’s grip. But Scott held fast around her waist, keeping her body pressed against his. “Don’t worry about them,” he told her, “no matter what they say, you know I would never leave you for any of them.” Once the two were in the gym and finally in the back where they could relax, Tessa looked to Scott with a scowl as nasty as any wicked witch of the west could give, but she kept her lips sealed as they headed through the door and toward the ring. Standing in the ring, waiting as usual in full gear, was Cleveland Paige. At the moment he kept himself amused as he waited by bouncing off the ropes and high stepping across to the other side. Coming off the far ropes he faced the door and noticed the pair walk into the room. He stopped with his high steps and leaned against the near ropes. “Woah, I would totally hit that.” Neither said a word, but Scott does roll into the ring after just dumping his bag off at the apron. He looked highly agitated but kept it within himself, leaving Paige free for now. Genesis could only smirk upon hearing Cleveland’s assessment of Tessa though, “you wouldn’t want to mess with that Paige, she’s one hell of a crazy bitch.” Already still fuming about the woman outside, Tessa could take no more. Despite the outstanding size difference between her and the near seven-foot man, Tessa had no problem getting in his face and letting him know “if it was up to me, Scott wouldn’t even be helping your sorry and ungrateful ass!” In the ring, Scott took advantage of the distraction to take Cleveland Paige off his feet with a sweeping Single Leg takedown. The young prospect shot back up to his feet, demanding that that one didn’t count. “You should be focusing on what’s in the ring, not out of it” Scott told him, “that is how people lose matches.” To add to his point, Scott did what he always did, he took Paige down again while the two argued in the ring. “Man,” Tessa said with a laugh as she waited, “this is the guy you’re supposed to be training to take your place” The only reputation he’ll uphold is the one about you always jobbing.” Paige, in his hot-headed ways, instantly whipped his attention to her to shout back a few curse words he had learned on the streets growing up; but instead found the back of his head hitting the canvas once again. The sound echoed across the stone walls, signaling the third and final takedown. Another chance came, another chance failed. Rolling out of the ring, Scott Drake grabbed his stuff without saying a word to anyone. He had a stoic, chin-high expression on his face as he headed out of the door. Without even saying a word to Tessa, he passed by her and made her wonder just what was going on. The new mother found herself following him despite being clueless as to the situation. “Hey,” she called out, reaching for him, “where are you going?” She caught herself looking back at the ring, “you don’t have to leave” she told him, feeling like she had ruined his practice and that he would be mad at her for that. Under no other circumstances could she ever see herself expressing him to stay around Darson any longer than he had to. Scott turned on his heels as he was about to reach the doorway and looked her in the eyes, “this is just how the practices are.” He went on to explain just how the practices worked, telling her how the rules is if he took Paige down three times than practice would automatically be over. He expected her to understand, to maybe say something than to be on their way. What he got instead was a fist full of rage as Tessa popped him one in the shoulder. “Ass!” she exclaimed, “if your practices are this short, than how come you come home so late?” Reminding her that he still has to get in his own training did little to ease her anger. She still looked at him assumingly with those sharp and piercing blue eyes, “this has something to do with those groupies, huh?” “How can you say such a thing” the pitch in his voice rose now, nearly crackling with anger. “How can you even think such a thing?” again he repeated. Looking at him with those big pouty eyes, Tessa seemed to just lose it all then and there. “It’s because I’m fat!” she shouted out. “I’m fat and I’m a cow now that I’ve given birth.” Her breath became shallow between sobs, “and I can understand if you wouldn’t want to be with someone ugly like me when you have all the young and beautiful women hanging all over you.” Embracing her in his arms, Scott gently guided her out of the room and to some place where they could have a bit more privacy. Holding her there in his arms, he looked into her eyes with all the love that was in his heart. “You aren’t a fat cow, you aren’t ugly, and no matter what you or anyone else says; you will always be the one for me.” A sly smile couldn’t help but creep onto his face as a dirty thought entered his mind, “you know” he told her as he wrapped an arm around her, “I wouldn’t mind a three-way though.” She pushed away from him, turning her head and making her leave. Scott laughed for a bit but soon gave chase to follow her. He laughed again, letting her know he was only joking with her, but it did naught to fix her mood. Turning on her heels ever so hastily, she looked him over as if she knew not what to make of him. “Can’t you ever be serious” she asked, poking him in the chest with her index finger. “I’m going home” was the last words out of her small pink lips before he opened the door and exited. Scott slung his gym bag over his shoulder, disappointed. The owner of the gym couldn’t help but overhear the exchange, leaning out of his office to get a look. “You ok, Scott?” “Yeah, Mac” Scott replied, letting a sigh escape from him, “I think I’m just going to skip practice today.” He allowed the words to linger for a moment before taking his next step toward the door, “but I’ll see you tomorrow.” “Alright, Scott, take care.”
