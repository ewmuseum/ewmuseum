Date: 04/01/11 Take One The ship rocked under the force of an explosion, sending the entire bridge into chaos and causing several officers to fall to the floor. Books and reports toppled over as several conduits blew apart in a shower of sparks. Through it all, Captain Rick Fistric remained stalwart, composed, and grounded. He gripped the railing with his calloused, war-hardened hands and used sheer force of will to keep his body perfectly straight as he sought an explanation from his scared crew. What should have been his proudest moment had quickly turned into a nightmare. "Status report," Fistric barked without a glimmer of surprise or indignation in his voice. Always the perfect soldier, he knew that his crew needed a stable rock to cling to if they were to weather this catastrophe. "We've got a coolant leak in sector thirty-two," stated his second in command, Commander Dean Corrinthe, the only one of his crew that was not knocked out of his seat. Corrinthe's voice was louder, more steady, and somehow more authoritative than his commanding officer's as he continued, "Weapons systems offline...auxiliary power is not responding." For a split second Fistric was agitated at Dean's tone, but he immediately chalked that up to preparation. Yes, the commander's confidence was due to Rick's grooming and example, and he should be proud that Dean was emulating him. He had trained his crew to act without thinking, and they were already working their way back to their stations. In order to survive a crisis in space, a star cruiser's staff had to act like cells in an organism. They all had their duty, and each person knew that the time to deal with the fear of death was after the fact. Once the alarm ceased and the status was back to GREEN they could cry or drink or pray, but not before then. Reports from all decks flew by with restrained order, like the whirring traffic of a metropolis, and Captain Fistric picked out the necessary kernels of vital information. "We weren't fired upon, so someone tell me what caused that explosion," the Captain directed his crew where and how to swim amidst the ebb and flow of panic and duty, "And repair communication relays. I want to speak with the captain of the Antrian ship. Maybe they know something." "Captain!" The first officer was out of his seat and grappling with one of the other crew members before the shout even left his lungs. Corrinthe gained leverage on a young ensign who had just pulled a pistol from its holster, forcing his arms up and back and unable to aim the firearm. Dean seemed to gain the upper hand and worked leverage on the would-be assassin, looking to lift him up, when Fistric realized where the two men were located. "No! You'll damage the gravity generator!" Fistric barked. Time froze as Dean went to lift and slam his opponent and every person on the bridge sought a handle with which to hold down their soon-to-be floating body. Dean Corrinthe paused and breathed heavily for a couple of seconds before responding with the detached coolness of a spectator rather than one who was in the heart of chaos and destruction. "Do you really have to say that?" the commander's authority and clarity evaporated, replaced by a casual tone unbecoming a leader. The entire bridge crew stopped and stared, their bodies still nestled comfortably by the ship's gravity systems. A few officers couldn't believe what Corrinthe said, a few pretended not to hear it, and Captain Fistric's confusion cracked his disciplined visage. A ripple in the very fabric of space/time seemed to crash reality all around them. Doom was no longer impending, replaced instead by a hushed awkwardness. "...what?" "It just seems really clunky and expositional," the first officer elaborated, his grappling opponent opening his eyes quizzically and sitting up, "I mean everybody's going to be floating in a couple of seconds. I think the audience is going to figure out why." It took a couple of seconds for the steam engine of the crew to grind to a halt, their collective acting ability going from full speed to zero, complete with a meltdown from off stage. "Are you kidding me? CUT!" proclaimed the voice of God. The world shattered. ---- I always figured a director would be more surreal, like the ones you see in cartoons. The cheap console crumbles beneath the light weight of the small man, and he lets out a little "ow" because yeah...this business hurts. Welcome to wrestling, you prick. For a moment the dust dances in the air, everyone stares in disbelief, and I realize I've made my choice. I've doomed my acting career. From this point onward the only roles I will ever be offered will deal with being a wrestler in a wrestler's role, because that's who I am and that's what I do. I tried to branch out, I tried to see what was there, and the truth is it's not worth the effort. It's not worth the bullshit and the self-loathing that I would bring about. Every bit of my nature told me not to slam Ensign Reyes because it would doom me forever to being cast for what I do in the ring, and I pushed past it for the simple victory of getting this day over with. I can safely say I will never be able to stomach doing that again. "Cut! Perfect! Absolutely perfect!" the director shouts, and suddenly he is everybody's friend. He loves me and looks like he is about to give me a hug. I'm ready for all the apologies that have been building up as he busts out his iPhone and shatters the world yet again. "Alright, five minutes to reset and we'll run it again! I like having two perfect takes, people!" I'm gonna go throw up. ---- ---- Take Me Away My body is used to punishment in the ring and pampering outside of it. I work out, I eat well, I get massages and acupuncture, and I do ten million things to remain in tip-top shape. People think I'm gifted with good looks and the body of a man ten years younger, but the truth is I work hard every minute of every day to fight the devil known as "aging." Right now I've spent all day living off of coffee and a snack cart, and I'm sorry if it sounds like I'm whining, but my system isn't dealing with it well. "Fifty-two takes? My God, Johnny. That's awful." Her voice is ambrosia, her breath is rejuvenating even through the phone. "I had to scoop slam somebody in a situation where I would never, ever scoop slam somebody. I did it nine times. I've officially sold whatever soul I had left." "Oh, like there was all that much left." Her lighthearted joke is too on-target. I'd claim I'd sold my soul for youth and fame a while ago, but I don't know who I gave it to. "I threw up after every single time I slammed him. Every part of me wanted to punch or wrist lock or...I don't know! But I couldn't win that fight, the fight for what to do. That one little win...if that prick would've just let me do something that made more sense." "Oh my God, my poor baby." Somehow she feels genuine concern. I'm amazed, because even I can't help but scoff at how pathetic that is. Really? I'm bitching about slamming a guy. What about the poor bastard that was getting slammed by the guy that wasn't even mentally there most of the time? By the fifth time I put him through that damn console, I'd thrown up all the nutrients in my system and I felt like I was going to drop him any second. "Whatever. I did the damn scene and I'm only in a couple more for this episode. And the director has reduced my part a bit to fit budget concerns." Keep telling yourself that was the reason, Johnny-boy. "Oh...well, when are you going to be home? I was thinking of making some special plans, if you know what I mean." A tiny puppy whine escapes from my lungs. There are few places I'd rather be, but sometimes life is just plain cruel. "I'm flying to Baltimore first thing in the morning, hun. Tomorrow's WorldWide. And right after my match I've gotta jet back out here to finish shooting." I can hear her heart break, the pieces hanging in the air like wind chimes. All she wants to do is see me for five minutes, put her head in my chest and pretend that we might actually be normal for one night. No Johnny Borealis, no Dean Carrinthe, no bullshit. "It's okay, Johnny. You know what they say. Nothing worth having in this world comes easy." I swear to God if one more fucking person tells me that- "That's what they keep telling me." "Have a little faith, Johnny-boy. You know that saying? Absence makes the heart grow fonder, and so on and so on. When I do get to see you it'll be worth every moment you're out there working so hard." I let it out in one long exhale. Every bit of exhaustion and frustration, every wretch and worry. In that one moment, I just want all the walls to come down, and I can feel myself at peace. I feel like I can finally say what I want. A tiny orb of horror bursts deep within and before I can stop myself, I do just that. I say what I want. "I love you, Kate." Silence. "..." "..." Oh. Fuck. I dare not breathe as the phone comes away from my ear and my eyes look at the screen. All that stuff about karma and things coming around from going around? Maybe I ought to just keep my thoughts to myself. Because nothing has come around quite like looking at that screen and seeing Sarah D'Mateo's name in bold, black letters. They might as well be the letters on a tombstone. Every text message from earlier, every moment she's been sitting at home just aching to hear my voice, and I repay her like this. Sarah's got two rules in our little open relationship. If I sleep with someone I tell her who it is, and don't ever sleep with Kate. Because I don't love Kate, I love Sarah. Kate and I are long-since over with. Right? "Sarah, I-" The line goes dead. On the other side of the country, a woman is sitting in bed with her heart ripped out and laying on the floor. I can blame it on a long day or the vomiting or the lack of food, but in the end I can only truly blame it on myself. "Cut. Let's do that again, get another take, and this time we'll get it right." The sick joke doesn't even echo, my voice falling on dead air. I spent all day bitching, scoffing at the idea of doing multiple takes because of a principle, because in real life we only get one chance. Of all the times to prove myself right...
