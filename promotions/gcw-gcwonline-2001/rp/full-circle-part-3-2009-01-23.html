Full Circle (Part 3)

Date: 1/23/09 2 weeks ago It all started off with a common scene at little shot gun gym located on the outskirts of Boston. Inside of the squared circle, in one corner, stood GCW’s most notable icon, LeStatt Knight. Opposite him in the other corner at a notable height distance stood a woman who many considered to be an enigma, Tessa Windsor. The imaginary bell inside both their heads rang and the two dashed for the middle of the ring and locked up. Of course Knight, having the obvious size advantage, forced Tessa down into a less dominant position. Never one to play the submissive type, Tessa put her brain to quick work and swung up at Knight’s midsection with her knee. As expected this brought her back up to equal playing field. Like a pro (at least she ought to have been after six years in the business), Tessa charged for the ropes. LeStatt looked up just in time to see Tessa fall face first into the mat after having tripped over her own damn foot. “Damn it!” She shouted aloud. Her tiny fists pounded the mat in frustration before lifting herself back up to her feet. Knights obvious little smirk could not be hidden He had been around her long enough to understand how pitiful little things like this frustrated her to no end. “Way to go Killer,” rather than respond back to Knight’s comment Tessa marched her way back to the middle of the ring, head still held high, pride still emanating from her face. Knight obliged to her obvious attempt to ask for a redo stating, “let’s try this again.” This time Knight let Tessa have the obvious upper hand in their lockup. Again she swung her knee up then tucked his head in between her arms and chest to bring him down with a DDT. She stood up then ran for the ropes once more. This time however, she came back at him at a much slower pace. Knight, having more than enough time to recover, stood up and let her run straight into his brick wall of a chest. Once again frustration spread straight across Tessa’s face. She realized how bad she was looking right about now. “I really hope this isn’t the kind of fight you’re planning on bringing to Clyde Fox at NC-17..” Her head shook sadly from side to side, her head no doubt running through a list of a million excuses. “I’m sorry.” She apologized, resuming her spot in the middle of the ring. “One last time.” Knight commanded, taking his own spot opposite her. Again he reached out and locked up with her. Much to his dismay there was absolutely no struggle coming from her side. Hell, there was not any kind of power coming from her side at all. It was like she stopped trying. “Is there a problem Ms. Windsor?” He asked, this time much more agitated than the previous times. “No,” She started up, pausing only to gain back her lost train of thought, “just let me take a break, I think that’s all I need.” Although frustrated from the obvious lack of motivation coming from Windsor’s end, Knight complied and followed her out of the ring. Tessa’s pace to the back was much quicker than her pace inside of the ring, the reason for that being obvious once Tessa laid her eyes on her. Although her mind seemed to be trashed today, that seemed to have little effect on the focus Tessa had on her one year old daughter, Aislinn. Her eyes widened in amazement as she watched her daughter take a handful of her first steps towards LeStatt’s wife Mary. Suddenly of all LeStatt’s frustration with Tessa seemed to sink away. Being a parent himself he could understand how damn confused and frustrated Tessa must have felt about spending so much time away from her kin. In fact, Lestatt’s own eyes trailed over to his own son who was sitting in Mary’s lap, cheering Aislinn on. “They grow up so quickly.” He remarked in the unusual soft side that only those close to him got to see. “Yeah, no kidding,” was all Tessa remarked back with at first, then another thought came to mind, “it’s not a wonder Chris is pushing to spend much more time with her.” LeStatt, none too fond of Chris, curled his bottom lip up in disgust, “I think that’s more of the fact that he wants to see more of you. Pity he can’t move on.” This time her answer was much softer, “Yeah.” LeStatt picked up the obvious signal being sent out by her whether she was aware of it or not, “Don’t cave in on me now, you’re almost there. You’re so damn close to what you really want. He’ll just make you give all that back up. He’s in this business for himself Tessa. The only reason he even watches over Markus is because he knows if that knee of his blows out again, any chance Chris has of glory is completely out the window.” Tessa, too caught up in her thoughts to actually reflect back on what LeStatt had said, simply nodded her head. She assumed that whatever he was saying had to at least have some truth to it. After all, it takes two to tango and her and Chris most certainly tangoed, be it for better or worse. -- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- December 30, 2006 5:00pm Chaotic was the word of the day for Tessa Windsor. Between an autograph session, multiple errands, and an extremely important visit to her, the day felt as if it would never end. Never was Tessa ever so relieved to pull into the driveway of her oversized home on the located beachside in Chatham, MA. “Mom! Mom! Mom!” Before she could manage to turn off the headlights of her 2004 Lexus GX470, her two adopted sons were out in the bitter cold in just their PJ’s, dying to greet her. It was the absolute best thing to come home to. “Hey guys!” She greeted them after cutting off the engine then hopping out of the vehicle, “I missed you two so much.” She would have had much more to say to them had they given her the opportunity to speak. Instead, the wrapped their arms around her waist and waited for the obligatory kiss on the forehead from their mother which was delivered shortly after her greeting. “Where have you been?” The youngest boy, little Chris, questioned. Already several steps ahead of her and his brother Evan, Chris stopped in his tracks and added on to his question, “Are you going to stay home for awhile now?” “We’ll talk about it in a bit.” She answered, taking his hand once she finally caught up to him, then leading him the rest of the way to the door. “But I want to know now!” He pouted, though his overwhelmingly excited facial expression changed none to fit his tone of voice. “I’ve got a surprise for you, just wait.” Bribing always worked well. He released his mothers hand and scampered his way back into the house, straight to his room upstairs. “Hey beautiful.” Tessa’s head turned away from her youngest son to her fianc’e Chris Storm, whose arms were wide open, waiting to engulf her. She leapt forth into his arms and wrapped her own arms around his broad chest. Considering the broad mass of muscle he was, her arms barely fit. But that mattered none considering how tightly he was holding on to her. “God, I missed you.” He whispered into her ears, stamping the top of her head with a kiss. “I missed you too.” She replied back, her arms returning to her side. It was moments like this she wondered if anyone on this earth loved her more than Chris Storm. “How’d things go?” He questioned, keeping the gap between them to a very bare minimum. Which each step she took, he took two, as if he expected her to bolt at any minute. She plopped her purse down on one of the granite counter closest to her inside the kitchen, “Well it depends on how you define good and bad.” His left eyebrow perked up in question, “What do you mean?” Her hands rummaged through large pile of mostly unimportant stuff inside her genuine leather coach purse and dug out a piece of paper that had been folded over several times, “Well it all depends on how you feel about the matter at hand.” “Let me see!” His tone of voice was much like that of the boys when they had seen their mother pull in. Tessa excitement level was much on the opposite end of the scale. But knowing that her fianc’e would not be held back any longer, she handed the paper over to him and quickly made a dash for the living room. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ “You know, I seriously hate you.” It was a piece of Tessa’s mind that completely caught LeStatt Knight off guard. He blinked not once, or twice, but four times as he contemplated what Tessa could mean by this. Did she seriously hate him? “Why’s that?” He finally got around to questioning, his eyes returning back to his son who was now up and attempting to call Aislinn in his direction. “You don’t even have to try and things work out for you.” Her own gaze shifted towards Javier and she can not help but at least smile despite the fact that internally she was struggling to make sense of how she would ever get her life on the right track, “You’ve got it all, LeStatt.” In some perspective he did, but there were plenty of obstacles he had to overcome to get there that Tessa was clearly overlooking. Really, there wasn’t any need to diverge into those kind of details though. Instead, he did his best to be a good mentor and friends, simply assuring Tessa, “You’ll have it all one day too Tessa, you just think too much.” “Yeah, I do.” She admitted, but even his words could not pull her mind away from the moment that continued to play in her mind. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “Oh my god!” The extremely large mixture of emotions contained inside Chris Storms voice made it extremely difficult for Tessa to predict just what was going through his mind as he tightly clutched on to the little sheet of paper inside his hand. Tessa, whose day had already been far too long, buried her head into one of the pillows lying on the couch and closed her eyes, hoping for the best but expecting the worst. Tossing the little sheet of paper down on to the glass coffee table Chris plopped down on the couch beside Tessa, letting his head hang over the back of the couch. His hands moved up towards his face and he let out a heavy sigh. “I can’t believe this.” He shouted in that same mixture of emotions. Tessa’s heart felt as if it were about to rip through her chest and explode. This was not going to end well, she just had a feeling. Her head finally lifted up, only to fall back down on Chris chest, “I’m sorry Chris, I’m so sorry, I am sorry. I don’t”. I really don’t know?. Just?. Really?. I’m sorry. I don’t know what happened.” By now she was in tears, desperately trying to catch her breath. Chris himself was out of breath and absolutely speechless, “You don’t understand Tessa.” “I do though!” Her voice was much louder than she had intended, “I messed everything up for us! My wedding, our careers, this isn’t good!” “Shhhh!” His lips pressed up against hers, knowing it’d be the only way to get through to her right about now, “Just calm down.” “I’m sorry.” She continued to sob, albeit much more softly now. “Tessa,” he whispered in her ears. It did not help to ease her nerves much, but he had plenty of hope inside him that these next batch of words would, “Everything’s going to be fine. I’m not mad at all, I’m ecstatic.” “But”.” “I’m going to be a father, Tessa!” He shouted, his excitement finally starting to shine through,” I mean, I already am, but you don’t understand how much this means to me Tessa, you really don’t. This is the best thing that’s ever happened in my life.” His smile could have lit up the entire room, but instead it only made Tessa cry more, only this time they were tears of joy. Maybe things would not be as bad as she feared. She breather a sigh of relief and looked into his eyes, her smile as wide as his, “You’re the best.” “You’re better.” ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- “What did I tell you about that thinking?” “Huh?” Tessa’s daze was interrupted once more by LeStatt softly shoving the back of her shoulder to get her attention. Immediately she straightened up her shoulders and pretended as if she had no clue what he was talking about. Her defense was absolutely weak, “I’m not thinking, I’m watching Aislinn. Hopefully she’ll turn out to be a lot smarter than myself. That and she won’t attract the mentally incapable, I pray. Oh god do I pray.” As always LeStatt was straight forward with his response. People would like to think he was easier on Tessa because of everything she had put herself through just to gain his respect, but in all honesty, he expected much higher expectations of her than he had of anyone else on the GCW roster. It would take those strict expectations to make a star out of Tessa, “Beating yourself up isn’t going to gain you any pity from me, Tess. It’s not about what you lack; it’s about what you are.” He got the same old response he had from her most of the day, “I know.” “I think it’s time we call it a day.” That finally caught Tessa’s full attention. Her eyes widened as she quickly ran through that prior list she had of excuses, “Obviously your heads not in the game, so there’s no sense in me wasting either of our time.” “Wait!” “What?” She grabbed his arm and begged for him to stay, if only for a few seconds, “Just let me ask you a question.” “What?” He questioned once again, only this time a bit more impatiently. “How do you do it, LeStatt” You’ve got Mary, you’ve got Javier, and if I know you, when you walk out of NC-17 you’ll have the world title. How do manage to have it all? Everything is so perfect for you.” His response differed none from the one she figured he’d give her, “Stop thinking Tessa, you’re only hurting yourself.” This time, she finally surrendered her thoughts. Her meek response showed that she was none too happy with his lack of an answer though, “Okay.” “I’ll see you later Tess.” “Bye.” She softly replied, scooping Aislinn up in her arms as Knight helped his wife up off the ground. Her head turned to the side, knowing that if she watched any longer those thoughts that Knight was trying to extinguish would quickly return. “I guess it’s just me and you today, Aislinn.” Tessa sighed. Out of the corner of her eyes she could not help but to notice how happy Knight, Mary, and Javier were together. It truly was something Tessa could only wish for. God, she would have killed to been in Mary’s shoes. The whole happy helping housewife role seemed much more appealing right about now than the miserable single mother who was struggling to reach any sort of success in her career bit. “God I hope LeStatt’s right.” She thought aloud, as she glanced down at Aislinn who faithfully laid her head on her mother’s shoulders as if she were trying to cheer her mother up. Even when the going got rough, Aislinn was always able to squeeze a smile out of her lamenting mother, “Come on kiddo, let’s head home. I think we both need a nap.” ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ August 27, 2007 7:30am “Rise and shine sleeping beauty.” “Huh?” Clad in an extremely unflattering hospital gown, Tessa rolled over as much as she possibly could to see who’d be crazy enough to be calling her beauty. Of course, having been in bed for nearly two days, fatigued after having just given birth to a healthy seven pound infant the day before, Tessa looked much more to fit the role of the insane person. “It’s time to wake up.” “Huh?” Tessa questioned once again, squeezing her eyes tightly shut in some awkward attempt to force herself to back to reality. Sitting next to her bed with a glass of water waiting for her was her fianc?, Chris Storm. Though he wore a smile on his face, his day had hardly been one to smile about. But the sight of Tessa laying there was too hard for him to resist. “Do you want to see her?” “No.” She giggled, her eyes rolling up and around, “I don’t. She’s not mine.” Chris rolled his eyes, “oh ok, that’s fine because the police took her in as evidence anyways.” Grabbing the only thing she could, a small paper cup on the tray beside her bed, she flung it at Chris? head, “oh they did not!” “Why would I lie to you?” Again she giggled, “Because, it’s the only way you can manage to keep me around.” As arrogant as one can be, Chris teased back, “You ought to be glad I keep you around. Do you know where I could be right now?” “Wrestling half naked men?” “Well yes” he answered, “but it would purely be in the most heterosexual way possible.” She wrinkled her nose at him, giggling still, “is there a way to heterosexually wrestle half naked men?” “Oh you.” He said as he stood up from his chair and reached over to tickle her. “Okay okay!” She surrendered, squeezing in a request between her much more intense giggling, “You win! Just bring me my baby already!” “Your baby?” He released her, only to fold his arms over his chest in an incredibly feign pout, “What about my efforts” Do you know how damn hard I had to work?” “Shut up!” The giant grin plastered across her face made her words ten times less intense, “Go get our daughter already.” He moved away from her, raising his arms up in surrender. “Fine” he said, “I’ll go get her for you, but only because I can totally see some side boob in that hospital gown.” Tessa looked down at her chest, realizing that in her tussle with Chris, half of her breast was in fact showing now. She reached for her cup, but quickly noted she had already thrown it at him earlier. “Just go!” “Fine fine. Just do me one favor?” “What’s that?” “Cover yourself up already! We don’t want a damn crowd forming around here.” He quickly ran out the door before she found something else to throw at him. “Men,” her eyes rolled around in a full circle once again. As much as she hated to admit it, his antics always managed to make her smile; regardless of crazy they were at times. Sitting in her bed, forbade from moving, Tessa could do little while she waited for Chris to return. As her thoughts drifted here and there, Chris Storm was able to sneak back into the room unnoticed with their newly born daughter. Slowly, not yet realizing they were there, Tessa turned her head until she finally spotted the two out of the corner of her eye. Automatically her breathing ceased as she laid eyes on the tiny bundle of joy she had managed to bring onto this earth not even a day earlier. How she ever managed to regain her breath Tessa would probably never be able to figure out. Only one thought came to her mind, “She looks just like you.” Indeed Aislinn did show some resemblance to her father. While she only had a small bit of hair, it was black just as her fathers was naturally. Her eyes were the same color as her mothers, but her face most certainly matched up with her fathers. There really was no way Chris would ever be able to deny that baby being his. “I just can’t believe we did this.” Pure excitement poured from her heart. He shared her sentiments, glued keeping his eyes glued on two of his most precious possessions, “It really is a miracle.” “A miracle indeed,” Her head nodded as she laid back in the bed, Aislinn snuggled up close to her, “A miracle I’ll never regret.” ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Never was much too strong of a word. But it seemed to be the highlight of everything that embodied Tessa Windsor. Never forgetting. Never giving up. Never reaching her goals. Never reaching the top. Never dating Clyde Fox. Never getting in over her head. She was taught time and time again to never to say never, but never seemed to be the only word that surfaced to her mind every time she put it to work. It was so convenient, so fitting, so everything. So before heading home, Tessa had been sure to remind her daughter, as if she cared or even understood, “I’ll never catch a break.” The going had gotten past tough, so why not always say never? It made plenty of logical sense to the now overly negative Windsor, so fuck, she was going to stick to it. Maybe if she were lucky it’d get her a win over Clyde Fox and teach him what never meant while in the process, just maybe.
