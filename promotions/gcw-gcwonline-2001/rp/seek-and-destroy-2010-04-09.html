Seek and Destroy

9 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/09/10 He is a man of routine, though you would not call that one of his virtues. Truth is; he does not have many virtues. He is selfish, mean, arrogant, and cold. He is a man of organization, though that is neither a virtue either. He must be organized; it is a part of his routine. Or rather; it is better to say that his routine is to be organized. Scott Drake does not have many virtues. If one had to list a virtue of his, it would have to be his stubbornness. It is said that he is so stubborn that he should run through a brick wall if such an idea was to get stuck in his head. People sometimes mistake his stubbornness with loyalty. Do not make that mistake. Scott is selfish. His stubbornness is what makes him loyal. His day starts like all the others; at the gym. He has gone to the same gym every day since he first broke into the wrestling business. He knows the owner, and likewise the owner knows him and his routine. This, undoubtedly, is why he continues to go to the same gym; because the owner never interrupts his routine. Though as she may have tried, Tessa Windsor never could mold him into the father he should have been. Scott was not, and likely never would be, a good father. A good father does not do the things that Scott Drake does. That is not to say she has not made improvements on. The sheer fact that he actually has learned how to flush a toilet is an improvement (though she is still working on the whole “putting the seat down” bit). So, when he could be spending time with his children, Scott instead finds himself lying on his back as he forces himself through only stubbornness to lift the bar up for just one more rep. He had to get to fifty reps. By the 49th, however, the 225 pounds that sat on the bar felt more like 2,500 pounds. Grunts escaped from his lips involuntarily. He knew he was inches away from getting that final rep. That fact alone pushed him to keep going until he got it. “….AND……FIFTY!” he screamed out as the bar hit the rack and dropped in, his arms finally able to drop down to the sides like worn out pieces of leather. They laid there at his sides, numb to the rest of the world, as he stared up at the ceiling above him. As he did each day, he allowed his body to rest while he stared up at the lights. The fluorescent lights shined down on him like a yellow sun, dousing him with energy. That nurturing source was covered, however, by a hovering shadow, casting Scott into darkness. The shadow spoke to him “man, that’s pretty impressive, I don’t know if I could do that many if I had to!” “What…” Scott said as he sat up, “do you want?” The shadow was a well-built kid likely in his early twenties. He was wearing a Banned & Exiled~! shirt. Scott always hated when people talked to him while wearing one of those, it meant they were a fan. It was either the look Scott was giving him, or the question he had posed to him earlier; but the Kid seemed unsettled now. It was almost as if he no longer could remember why he had approached Scott in the first place. In a vain attempt to try and keep things moving away from the awkward silence that hung over them like a dark cloud, the Kid chuckled lightly “I guess it’s pretty cool, we go to the same gym.” Scott looked around, confirming that they were, in fact, in the same gym. He hated stupid statements like that. There was no intelligent way to respond to a statement like that, only more meaningless chatter or sarcasm. Thankfully for Scott, he liked him some sarcasm. “Yeah, being in the same place as someone else is pretty cool. Keeps that whole ‘social’ thing working.” The Kid laughs even more nervously now, telling he is crashing and burning in front of one of his favorite wrestlers. His eyes dart back and forth, trying to find anything to save him. Scott just lets out a sigh audible enough to draw back the Kid’s attention. “Look, you want an autograph?” Scott bends over, reaching into the gym bag beside him. Producing a black permanent marker, he signs his name into the white B&E~! logo on the black shirt. The pen returns to the bag and Scott does his best to give the Kid a smile, one that almost expresses how much he really just wants to be left alone. Looking down at his shirt, the Kid still cannot find any words. He manages to mutter out a ‘thanks’ before finally turning away and leaving. Scott again gives out a sigh, though this one in a much more needed relief. To close out his workout, Scott ends it with 15 more reps before reaching into his bag and grabbing his towel. Wiping the sweat from his brow, he tosses the bag over his shoulder and heads toward the counter near the door, where the owner of the gym, Hershel, resides. “I don’t care if you have to post signs or what, but I don’t come to this gym to do autograph signings.” He tells Hershel, pointing an accusing finger at him. The old man just waves his off, offering Scott a smile. “Ah come on now, Johnny didn’t mean any harm. He’s a good kid, works real hard.” Scott Drake is still too annoyed to relax, however, “well just fuckin’ great. Maybe Garbage Bag will give him a gold star as well.” “Wants to become a wrestler too.” Hershel replies, choosing to ignore Scott’s last comment. “I’ve seen him a few times in the ring, he looks pretty good. Raw, but he looks like he could be right with some training.” He stops, almost to see if Scott would say anything. After a short moment of silence, he continues on in no real direction. “If I hear right, he signed up over at Bruce’s for some classes.” Scott snickers at the idea. “And did you tell him Bruce’s is the worst?” “Yep” “And?” “Was all he could afford” the owner tells him with a sad drop of his head. “Real shame.” Tossing the towel back into his back, Scott shakes his head. “When the hell did you become Oprah?” “Just saying,” Hershel tells him with a shrug of his shoulders. Like he always does, Scott gets agitated at the small talk and cuts straight to the bone. “Look, I’ve gone here a long time, and I know you’re not the kind of guy to worry about what training other guys get outside your gym.” He looks at Scott out of the corner of his eye, most likely making the discussion to stop beating around the bush. “You have been coming here a long time, and I know that look in your eyes. Whatever you got swimming around in your head, talk it over with the Misses first. I’ve never seen anything good come when you’ve gotten that look, and goodness knows she’s the more level-headed of you two.” “I don’t have any look in my eyes” Scott answers bitingly, “I just know what I need to do. Ain’t no sense in trying to deny it. I don’t like it, others won’t like it, I know sure as hell Tessa won’t like it; but I gotta do it. I gotta end this.” Hershel looks Scott up and down. He could tell when Scott Drake wished he was more like Chris Storm. Scott had doubts, Chris had determination. Whatever idea he had cooped up in his head, he most likely realizes he can’t act on it. Chris can, though. Chris can carry any plan out with cold and unforgiving direction. Hershel reaches across the counter and places his hand on Scott’s shoulder, calming him down. “Go home, go talk to Tessa.” Scott nods his head and removes Hershel’s hand from his shoulder. Turning away, he heads toward the exit. Hershel sighs and rubs his temple with his wrinkled fingers. “Just don’t do anything you’ll regret” he mutters under his breath, “…again.” -~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~- Scott stands in the doorway for a moment, allowing time to gather himself. “Scott, is that you?” He lets out a soft sigh and walks through the threshold, putting on a smile. Sitting on the couch in the living room is Tessa. She has their two year old, Aislinn, wrapped up in her arms as she twist her neck around to try and get a look at the entrance to the living room. “Yeah, just getting in from the gym” he tells her, tossing his bag on the floor near the couch and allowing himself to slide in beside the two. He gives her a soft kiss on the lips but Tessa instantly pulls back from him, looking into his eyes with a great sense of worry. “What’s the matter?” Scott is taken back, “what do you mean?” She just shakes her head at him, “I know that look. You can’t force a smile to save your life, and when you try it only means something it bothering you.” ‘This,’ Scott thought in his head, ‘is a clear sign you’ve been with a person for WAY too long.’ He sucks it up, absorbing her comment and tries even harder to make the smile stick. “I’m fine, really. You just worry too much.” “It’s Markus, isn’t it?” Try as he can, that smile is wiped from his face as if a sharp slap had just struck him across the cheek. Her head drops down; she clinches Aislinn closer to her. “Just forget about it,” her eyes dart up to meet him, “isn’t that what you told me to do?” He takes her hand, cupping it in his own, “Don’t worry” he tries to assure her, “I know what I’m doing. I know what I have to do.” She pulls her hand from his, covering Aislinn’s head. “And just what do you ‘have to do’?” Scott lifts himself off the couch and stands defensively. “I’m just going to let him know what his options are.” “And what is that?” He pauses. His eyes fall down onto the carpet. He seems too nervous, or perhaps scared, to answer her. “And? What are his options?” she again asks. Taking a deep breath, Scott looks up at her. “He can either quit,” he spits out like venom, “or I will make him quit.” He drops to one knee in front of Tessa and Aislinn, “I promise he won’t bother us again. I promise.” Despite his best attempt at comforting words, Tessa’s expression only grows more worrisome. She places her hand onto his head. “Don’t.” “Don’t what?” “Whatever you are planning” she tells him. “I know that look in your eyes, Scott. You are planning to do something you will regret.” At his side his fist clinches into a ball of rage and frustration. His teeth grind together “the only thing I will regret is if something happens to any of you.” Tessa exhales, standing from the couch, her hand still on the top of his head. “I know I can’t talk you out of whatever it is you are planning in your head.” She shakes her head disappointedly, “I never could. But please…” their eyes meet, “…don’t act like any of this is for us. You are doing this for yourself.” Scott lifts himself up, her hand now falling from his head to his cheek, “what?” “I could see it in you for weeks now, ever since NC-17. It is killing you not being out there” she tells him as her fingers gently brush the outline of his jaw bone. “I wanted to pretend, to ignore it, and think that you could be happy being a stay-at-home dad. But it’s killing you.” “No, it’s just…” Her finger moves from his cheek to his lips. “It’s fine. You are a good man, a good father. I shouldn’t be expecting you to be someone you are not. As much as I hate to say it; you are a wrestler. You are Chris Storm.” She moves in, removing her finger and gently kisses him in the lips. Tessa steps away from him, heading out of the living room. Despite her back to him, she continues to talk. “I love you. But, don’t act like you are doing this for us. This is just your excuse to get back into the ring.” Her head turns until her chin is resting just above her shoulder. “Whatever you are planning, don’t do it.” Scott Drake is alone now, no one to argue back with. His body slumps onto the couch, feeling it could no longer support him. Even his head cannot stay upright, having to catch it with both hands. No one wants him to do what he’s planning, as if they all got together and knew exactly what he was going to do. They act like he will regret it. They don’t know him at all; he will never regret protecting his family. And that is exactly what he is going to do, protect his family by destroying Markus Stone.
