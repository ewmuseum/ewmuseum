Who am I? (Flashback - pt.1)

12 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/05 “Ricky I’m going to be fine. You just need to be strong ok?” “Be strong” You just told me you were dying Shane! How can I be strong? How am I supposed to react to that? What did you think I would say? I cant be strong, I am only a kid! I am 10 years old Shane, and my world is crashing down.” “Ricky, I just said I have cancer. I never said I was dying. It is treatable in most cases little brother. Just hang tight, ok?” “How bad is it, and how long have you known?” Ricky asks him. “I’ve known for quite some time?” “Who else knows” Ricky interrupts. “Nobody. You are the first I have told. I take that back, I told Sean. He needed to know. His career depended on it.” “So why are we here now if you are doing just fine?” Ricky takes note of his surroundings. They are in a hospital room, with Shane sitting on the bed. He is not in hospital attire but rather his normal street clothes. The room is empty aside from the two of them when there is a knock at the door. “Come in” Shane speaks out. Entering the room is Ricky’s parents. They stand there silent staring at Ricky then at Shane, not knowing wy they are there. They are confused which of their children seem to be hurt enough to be admitted to the hospital. “Are you two ok?” “Yeah dad we’re fine.” Shane replies. “No I’m fine, ask Shane why we’re here?” Ricky screams out. “Shane, what’s going on?” His mother asks. “Mom, Dad’I need you to understand firstly that things are going to be fine.” “Shane what is going on?” Their mom looks to the nearest chair and stumbles to it and situates herself into it, when suddenly the door opens and the doctor is standing there. “I’m sorry” he speaks “I didn’t realize you had visitors, I can come back.” “No” Shane answers “Stay. This is my family. My little brother here knows what’s going on, but I haven’t had a chance to tell my parents yet. Maybe you can tell them doc.” “If that is what you would prefer Shane. Mr. & Mrs. Thunder firstly let me introduce myself. My name is Dr. Jonas Whinst. I have been working with Shane here for the past 4 months. Shane has been diagnosed with cancer.” His mother breaks down into tears, clutching at her nearby husband. Shane gets to his feet and heads to where his mom is sitting and kneels on the floor in front of her, whispering words of comfort to her. She wraps an arm around his neck, still crying uncontrollably. Meanwhile his dad stands tall and stares at the doctor with hurt in his eyes. “How bad is it?” he asks. “Well that is why we are here tonight actually. Shane had an appointment scheduled after several treatments to see if there has been any improvement in his condition. I am sorry to say, that the cancer is terminal. It has since spread into his brain, and we are unable to contain or operate any further. I am truly sorry” Ricky jumps to attention tears streaming down his face, and begins screaming at the doctor “NO! You fix him! You make him al better! That’s what you assholes get paid for! YOU FIX HIM!” “Ricky” Shane is interrupted? “NO SHANE! You will be better! You will be! You have to be!” With tears streaming down his cheek their father approaches the doctor and in a whispering voice speaks “How long does he have?” The room falls silent and all eyes, including Shane fall on the doctor. “I’m so sorry?” the Doctor starts. “How long?” Shane asks standing up “After analysis from my staff and myself, we have determined that the probability of you surviving more then another 10 days is unlikely.” “YOU LIE!” Ricky rushes at the doctor but is intercepted by his brother who wraps his arms around him hugging him tightly. Tears flow freely from the eyes of Ricky as he hugs his brother there on the floor. “He’s a liar Shane?” “Hey little brother, it’s going to be ok. I need you to be strong for me. For mom, for dad.” Their parents are holding each other behind the brothers, their mom still in the chair. The tears flow from all in the room. Their parents don’t speak, they know that no matter what is said there is nothing they can do to change the diagnosis. Dr. Whinst takes a step towards the door bowing his head. “I’m sorry again Shane. Would you like me to send in the chaplain” He may be able to answer some questions or soothe some?” “GET OUT YOU BASTARD!” Ricky yells. The doctor closes the door as the Thunder family is left in disarray in the hospital room.
