The past is the Future!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Unknown Quietly a camera comes into view in an empty hospital room with a single wooden chair sitting in the center. Wires hang from the walls and a huge single light hangs from the ceiling. The place is old and the paint is chipping in certain areas. Upon the wall there are several small paintings and sketched pictures. A huge shadow enters the room and the chair is pulled to the side as Shaman sits down. Looking at the room carefully he then turns and looks at the camera. "It is funny how this facility used to be called the top of the charts in cutting edge technology to only be ten years later it is closed for progress in the field. Truth is that they were bought up by corporate America and left to rot. This facility was bought up by one of the larger hospital conglomerates. Not able to hold the money they were getting to keep the doctors and patients as well as the technology advancements. I stayed in this very room ten years ago after a very brutal match. I was beaten down and beaten up, But i wasn't defeated. No i gained even more strength from that match. Now here i am looking at the possibility of gaining the larger prizes in GCW. Another title placed within my hands with one of the best partners i have ever had in my wrestling career. He and i are best friends and without this there would be no other. We are brothers in every sense of the word." Looking around he waves his arms outwards and stands up looming high above the camera looking down at it. "Looking down upon the world below you realize that you have forgotten soemthing. Something that is as dangerous as discovering some new dangerous disease. You realize that you left a pair of beasts locked up in a cage. These beast are ready to break loose. They want to envelope and destroy everything that comes before you. There is a world of dangerous aspects crowding around the world that you feel safe in. There are wolves at your door and they are waiting for the one chance to charge in and tear you to shreds." Shaman turns and kicks the door open and steps through walks down the corridor as the dusts rises from his foot falls with old doctors charts and half destroyed hospital beds in the hall. The lights above him flicker as if waiting for the next emergency to come. Shaman stops and looks left at a nurses station as he narrows his gaze. "The changes come and they never are easy on the eyes. Destroying the historical and cultural changes that came before it. It cares little for anything other than the fact that things will be the way they are after it has come and gone. History means nothing if you are just staring at the future. Bring the noises and the ultimate destruction. Bring the guns and the bombs and destroy a race of people. Bring your warriors and kings. Come and face Oblivion for it shall take your breath and hold it within its grasp as you turn blue and pass out." Shaman turns and enters the nursing station and sits down in a chair and picks up a phone and grins as he hits the page button as the overhead intercom system sparks to life. "Paging paging the Doctor, emergency surgery is needed for those facing Oblivion." Shaman laughs loudly over the crackling intercom system as he throws the phone down and rolls the chair around the nurses station. "Oh what fun shall we have in GCW. Many people will forget that there are things out there that are as dangerous as they come. I will enjoy destroying the beliefs of everyone. I will enjoy crushing the dreams of those around us who forget their places. It is paranoia of those seeking greatness yet ever failing to identifying the threats who loom before them." Shaman stands up and begins to walk down the hall as he looks about angry as he stares at the abandoned hospital around him and he then slowly calms. "These places plain and simply suck. You get treated literally like a piece of meat so they can gather and collect all of your money. Then when the suck you dry they ship you off to another facility hoping to get the last remnants of your self esteem. You look around yourselves and see what i see. Pathetic nothingness and the hopelessness of corporate crap and how they continue to give you the bullshit excuse of it being in your best interests." Shaman walks to the stairwell door and enters as he walks down the stairs he looks down and concentrates on his slow deliberate walk down the stairwell. "You forget that the corporations are the ones who destroyed the United States economy recently along with the governments blind eye turning to the corruption and degradation of American society it brings. Now you draw your eyes to GCW. Where everyone is forgetting the reality of everything. You are not safe and someone will come and take away what you hold dear. Come into Oblivion where the darkness will embrace you and destroy everything you are." Shaman opens the door to the parking lot outside as a crew walks in beside him and on the side of the van it reads. Hospital Reclamation project. Shaman grins and looks at everything else before he speaks again. "There is a future for everything GCW and in the end Oblivion is waiting, but there is a ray of sunshine of that other side of Oblivion. You get to walk away eventually after to heal in the hospital." Shaman laughs deeply as he walks off across the parking lot disappearing between the construction vehicles.
