Horrible, Wretched Monster (Part 1)

Date: 06/11/11 A horrible, wretched monster was Red Gordon, father of three and husband of none. A vile beast, the sort of man that has those cliched, "dangerous eyes." That was to say, they were clouded with animosity. His hatred, though seemingly unspecific in nature, could be directed at anything in anytime, and was the one lucid quality which defined him as a human being. His scalp was red, burned only by the sun and the refusal of sunscreen. Shirtless in his yard in Memphis, a small brick house surrounded by small brick houses behind him. A bag of fat hung over the waistline of his jeans the same way a bag of sand would look if draped over a fence. Zip Gordon was Red’s only boy, and though he was only 11, he was already showing signs of pussyness. And Red, of all the things he didn’t intend for his son, didn’t intend on him being a pussy more than anything else. His daddy didn’t let him be no pussy, and he damn sure wasn’t gonna let his son be no pussy either. So he stood shirtless, in the cold winter, just as he was making his son. The major difference in their situation was that Zip was being doused in water, in a hose held by Red. Watering his boy in the same fashion you might a rose bush, he stood champion of the lawn. Drunk as hell, but regal in his own sense. His chin was up, his smile was proud. He owned that piece of property, that god damn little brick house, a place where handles of liquor came to die. He owned that boy, too. He damn sure, damn sure owned the water hose. Besides all of that, it was his American right, by the glory of God almighty, hallelujah, to whoop this boy’s ass anyway he saw fit to choose. If he wanted to raise his boy like a pansy, he’d ship his sissy ass off to Canada, or England. "You wanna sip tea in England, you little queer?" He asked the boy before him, tucking his free hand in his pants to scratch his cold, although sweaty privates. The hose continued to blast water while he held his thumb over the end, pelting his son with the burst. His son. His only begotten son. Zip had the same brown hair that Red himself used to have and the same god damn eyes, as well. Too bad for him, he was a wimpy kid. A little queerbait, Red would say sometimes. "No," his only son answered the rhetorical insult, refusing to allow his voice to quiver. He did not want to sip tea in England, god damnit all. He was getting stronger, mentally, Red realized. This was good. Evidence that he was a good father under the grace of the lord. Thy Heavenly Father. Hallelujah. "Bullshit!" Red cursed and slung the hose back as a cowboy does a lasso, swirling it around once or twice. Zip didn’t have time to dodge. His eyes only widened as the copper edge came to strike the side of his face. "NO!" Zip screamed, and stood back up from the knee the strike to the face had caused him to take. Though cut on his cheekbones, set high enough to surely be a handsome adult later in life, he didn’t even wipe the blood away. He only grit his teeth and clinched his fists, legs spread apart and looking up at his father from his brow. Red was surprised, shocked you could say. He felt his loins recoil in horror, threatened by the confident way in which his son was dealing with his lesson in control. He couldn’t allow Zip for a moment to think he could gain it. No way, not in his damn house. Doing so would be allowing his own son to call the shots. He threw that hose out once more and struck Zip in the side of the head. He fell to the ground, soaked and blacked out before he even touched it. A little girl screamed. Red turned to the window, his two little girls crying and screaming, "Daddy!" Even though it was Daddy that was beating their beloved brother half to death before their young eyes. Even though it was Daddy that was breaking their hearts in half. "Oh, hell," Red said while he rolled his eyes. He dropped the hose and pointed at them as he walked toward the window. "Getcher asses in your damn room before I go in there and make you damn do it!" The girls scattered from the window long before he finished his sentence to fulfill his order. Though everyone knew it was never the girls who got punished physically, they also knew the girls wouldn’t stick around and find out. He turned back to his kid, laying there on the ground, face up and eyes closed. His cheek still bled in slender drips. His chest, it rose and fell softly. Red walked over to the hose, now laying in a muddy pool of its own water, and lifted it from the brown pond and into the air once more. "Wake your ass up, little man." Red rained water down on Zip’s bloody cheek. ^*^*^*^*^*^ A horrible, wretched monster was Zip Gordon, lover of no one and handicapper of one. It had been the sort of night every young amateur professional wrestler dreams of. Tennessee Valley Wrestling, the circuit Zip worked regularly, was being visited by talent scouts from the big boys in Georgia. He’d fucked everything up. Like a child, like a fucking idiot, he dropped Red Phillips on his head doing a piledriver. Straight on his fucking skull. Last he heard, the kid couldn’t move his arms or legs, and couldn’t feel it even if you punched him right in the balls. Fuck you, God. He’d have liked to say it, no, scream it, at the top of his god damn lungs. How could he have fucked up that big of a deal? How could he have done it? Already 35, he’d probably have to wait until he was fifty to get a shot at the big time after making that big of a mistake. Christ. Zip vowed right then and there that if he was still wrestling in the independent circuits when he was fifty, he’d shoot himself right in the brain with a bazooka. He vowed it on the line of cocaine laid before him, next to three other lines just like it, rocky enough even yet. A rolled up one permanently placed up his nose, the lines bedecking the hotel room table’s edge delicately, contrasting brilliantly against the fake-stained redwood. He leaned over in his green cushioned stool and inhaled. When his eyes closed he only saw "Mad Dog" Red Phillips, his neck wrapped up in that medical gear, the paramedics (the real ones, not the show ones) lifting him up on a stretcher. The two hundred or so people in the arena applauded. "Well-Known" Zip Gordon, however, did not. Offering his condolences instead, he pushed his way through the crowd of referees, ring side assistants, fake paramedics and real ones to the stretcher with tears welled up in his eyes. Though he felt exactly like the pussy his dad said he was, he was crying and was unashamed to admit his shame and sorrow for what he’d done. He grasped Phillips’ hand and sobbing said, "I’m so, so, so, so, SO fuckin’ sorry man. Jesus Christ am I sorry." Red refused to cry at all. He looked at Zip with intensely angry eyes. "I’m trying so hard to give you the middle finger right now," he said as they carried him off. Just as he’d be carried the rest of his life, in some form or another. It wasn’t the first time he’d paralyzed a man named Red. At this god damn rate, it wouldn’t be his last. "Honey child," a hooker on the single queen bed with the hokey, thin, flowery bedspread over the top of her. "If you want more, you’re either gonna have to give me some of that or give me some money. You owe my ass anyway." Her ebon skin was a perfect, milky cream. Her eyes screamed of generosity and kindness. "Sorry," he shrugged and finished the last of those gorgeous, fish scale lines. He lifted up and snorted in a few times. Then, nearly choking up, Zip rolled his eyes in the back of his head and said euphorically, "There’s the drip." "Whatchyu mean, ‘sorry?’" He sat quietly for a moment, eyes closed, facing the ceiling. He ran a hand through his receding brown hair, cut military style, as she asked her question two more times. "It means," he finally said, opening his eyes and staring at her like she just stabbed his dog in the heart as opposed to interrupted his high. "’Sorry, I don’t pay niggers for pussy.’" She was absolutely beside herself. "What the fuck did you just say?" Of course. He should’ve known this bitch was going to make World War Three out of this. Ever since that damn Martin Luther King. His daddy had been right about a lot of things before trying to beat Zip’s ass when he was a grown man. Now his daddy wasn’t right about anything. He didn’t talk anymore. ^*^*^*^*^*^ The flash of green across a monitor, accompanying beeps. His father with a tube in his mouth. A needle protruding from Red Gordon’s arm. He was hugging his two sisters when the nurse had asked, "Are you sure?" "Sure as the god damned Sun," he’d answered. His sisters cried harder as the nurse left. When she closed the door, he let his sisters from his embrace. They looked up to him so much as children, he was everything they wanted to be. It seemed that was no longer the case. If anything at all, they merely looked up at him, for he was a good half foot taller than either of them. "Say goodbye if you want," he said as he walked towards the door. "But I wouldn’t waste my breath." Sarah, his youngest, prettiest, blondest sister looked at him with an angry squint, burying her eyes beneath her brows. Her nose crinkled up, her lower lip pouted and her upper curved like Elvis. "What the fuck is wrong with you these past few years, damnit!?" Zip didn’t answer. He just opened the door and closed it behind him, gently. The girls found it to be rude and hurtful to ignore the question that had been on their mind a near half-decade. In truth, Zip just didn’t know what to say. ^*^*^*^*^*^ "Trayvon’s gonna kill your fuckin’ white ass," she grinned something sinister, throwing on her clothes. "You’re not even paying me? Fuck that you fucking bitch. Fuck you!" She ran at Zip wrecklessly, braless and pantless but in her skimpy yellow shirt and stockings, intending to pound him into oblivion. She beat on him with her skinny little arms and bony knuckles, beat on him for invoking the slur against her people. Beat on him for doing drugs right in front of him and not sharing. Beat on him for not paying. But especially, especially for the n-word. Zip defended himself laughing, blocking her fists with his arms, unaware to the fact that he was near his breaking point. Everyman’s got a breaking point. His father had told him that. And you’re really pushing me to the rocky edge of mine, kid. Give me a little nudge and see what happens. She slapped Zip Gordon right across his mouth. Give me a little nudge and see what happens. In a motion so swift that the woman didn’t know it happened until it was far too late to defend herself, Zip stood up and ripped the golden wig off of her head. He noticed she had a rather pretty shape, for a noggin anyway, pretty enough indeed as he slammed it against the table. "You want some fucking powder you stupid bitch?" He slammed her face into that table again, and again, and again, stopping only after hearing the crunch of her nose and the shriek of her voice. Only after hearing the ejection of blood, spewing like ketchup from a packet after twisting it too much. He dumped the rest of the bag of cocaine (which couldn’t have been more than a quarter gram) on top of her fallen body. She cried quietly. Zip respected that enough not to touch her again. He knelt on one knee and spoke in a quiet, soothing voice neither of them knew of until that moment. "Is Trayvon your pimp?" She said nothing, just whimpered quietly. "Come on," Zip said as though he didn’t have time for all this shit (that he created). "Is he or isn’t he your pimp? Don’t make me do that to you again." "H-H-…He’s my pimp. He-he’s my pimp." "You’re gonna tell him some old nigger man did this, aintcha Candy? May have even been your grandpa. Aint that right?" She refused to answer him. To use that word in her presence was a slap in the face. And since she did not have the strength to fight him physically, she used the strength she had not to answer him out loud. To do so would’ve been to unleash a fury of adjectives and nouns and verbs suggesting what to do with those very nouns that was so harsh and suggestive he may have killed her outright to do it. If Zip had known of the strength it took from Candy not to say a word, not to say anything at all, he’d have more respect for her than he did anyone else. Though, he may have had more already. "AINT THAT RIGHT?" He asked her. She nodded, so she did. And she felt ashamed in doing so. Zip smiled a sneer and stood up. He threw on his tracksuit quickly, effortlessly. He could dress in twenty seconds flat without even trying, usually, as everything he wore was slip on all the way down to the hemp sandals on his feet. "I’m glad we’ve reached an understanding," he grinned again as he spoke those horrible words and slipped out the door. But Yolanda Harris, or Candy depending on who she was speaking to, didn’t reach an understanding. In fact, you could go so far as to say there wasn’t a drop of understanding left in the marrow of her bones. She was gonna tell Trayvon exactly what happened, word for word. And Trayvon was going to kill that white motherfucker. ^*^*^*^*^*^ Sarah and Jessica Gordon were crying once more, for the same old reasons they always were. Zip was taking his beating like usual, and it was becoming a bit irritating. Bent over the front porch railing, so the whole neighborhood could see, Red was beating the hell out of Zip’s fifteen-year-old naked rear end with a chain. Red knew that fifteen was a prime year for back talk and sassing your folks. He was fifteen once, damnit. And just like Red, Zip would have to suffer the consequences of his smartass little words. "Stop being mean!" Jessica, the dark haired blonde of the two, cried shrilly. Her voice was undeveloped yet and when she screamed, it pierced the ear better than any lady in any earring shop. Red turned to her and offered a wicked stare, both girls turned and ran without the slightest delay. Red turned towards his boy, angrier than ever. "Turning my girls against me, are you boy? You little son of a bitch." He said and lifted that chain in the air again. "WAIT!" Zip yelled, panting nervously. "What is it? Keep in mind, aint near nothin’ you can say to take away this whoopin’. You brought it on yourself. And if you waste my time with foolishness, it’s gonna get even worse. So?" Zip smiled at his father, knowing full well the possible implications of his stupid actions. "Fuck you, dad. Fuck you." Flabbergast! Shock! Horror! Dear Lord Jesus God, if you could just give me the strength not to murder this boy outright in my own yard, father Jesus Lord, I’d be forever grateful. In Jesus name. In Jesus’ sweet, holy name. Amen. Hallelujah. Zip Gordon smiled as his father’s face darkened. Zip felt as though he was looking at the devil himself, which was exactly who he wanted. With any luck, his father would kill him. It could save his sisters any further pain, for old Red to get his due on death row. Yes sir. Yes it would. They’d go to foster care, maybe get adopted, maybe go to college, maybe not become whores with daddy issues like Zip had read about. "What the fuck did you just tell me?" Red asked like a bull questioning whether or not a matador really just showed him that red, bloody cape. "I said, ‘Fuck you.’ And I hope you die from cancer, you horrible, wretche--" That chain came down quickly, with the force of thunder it seemed, and struck Zip Gordon right across the back of the head. This time, even though Zip was breathing, once Red watered him with the hose Zip wouldn’t wake up. This time, Red Gordon really fucked up.
