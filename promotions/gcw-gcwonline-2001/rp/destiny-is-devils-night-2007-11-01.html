Destiny is Devils Night

1 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/1/07 Shaman is seen sitting in the middle of a wrestling ring. His eyes are focused on several magazines before him. One shows the pictures of him and Jonathan Winters in the WWA together. He reaches down and lifts the magazine up and examines it with his eyes and then runs his hands across the surface. His eyes notice the date and he frowns a bit and whispers something. "The great screw job is yet to come. Both of us got the blunt end of the deal." Flipping the magazine to the side he angrily grabbed another one that showed Shaman walking away from the WWA offices. He ran his hand across his chin angrily as he breathed a deep breath. Shaking his head he threw that one down as well. Finally he picks up another magazine that read "Shaman and Jonathan Winters thrive outside of the WWA!!" Shaman grins and leans back in the chair and opens the magazine and begins to read it. "Ironic isn't it that i was told i would never be anything outside of the WWA and here i am the GCW with three different title reigns under my belt. Bunch of ignorant jack asses thought they knew what it would take for ratings. Truth is they wanted to save money and get rid of someone who spoke his mind and did not kiss their collective asses. Winters yeah and and i go back a ways and have a mutual respect. We both know what each other can do and that is why we have a match against each other. was there any respect there? Always has been and we remained friends. I actually thought the WWA was giving him a screw job as well sounds alot like the Bret Hart situation doesn't it. New talent comes in old guy returns and gets the push because he screams about being a better ratings getter. Sound familiar yeah thought it did. Winters and i were better off moving. The WWA front offices had no room for us with there heads stuffed so far up there paycheck asses." Shaman simply flips the magazine away as he looks over at the titles hanging on his wall. He then looks down at the one in his lap. He grins and lifts it up holding it towards the camera. "Lets talk titles, I have had every title in the WWA and then gained the TV Title in GCW as well as the US Title twice. I hold the US Title here in GCW and believe me i feel like i have fought tougher opponents in GCW than i ever had anywhere else. Am i filled with hatred and dislike? Nah not worth my time. In this business wrestlers come and go and the feds think they know better than the fans who pay for our services to entertain them. Through the blood, the sweat, and the tears we still go out night after night and perform." He grins and drapes the title over his shoulder and stands up. "What to expect at Devils Night is simple. Expect Winters and i to go out and give it our all. Blood for blood, tear for tear, pain for pain. There is no give between the two of us. We are going into this with one purpose and that is to win and show each other that we can beat each other. Only one person to prove anything to nowadays and that is ourselves. We have done it all now and there isn't much left other than good ol competition." Shaman walks to the ropes of the ring and grips them leaning back slightly as he drops against them testing the flex. "Kind of brings me to another point here. Lia Ambrosi and Vivica Valentine. Time and time again Caldera has pitted them against us hoping we'd take each other out for his own personal amusement. Valentine has some major issues with my partner Mayhem who has suddenly holed himself up somewhere with no contact. If i have to try and win the match myself i will. I have bigger fish to fry this Devils Night. Lia should realize by now how many times we all end up in matches against each other and everytime how much we put out against each other and in the end somehow Caldera gets some sick little satisfaction out of it. I am tired of him getting the upperhand and we suffer because he doesn't get his little payback. Time to move the chess pieces and play the game of chess the way it is meant to be played." Shaman turns around and grabs the chair spinning it around as he sits resting his forearms across the back rest as he looks around the ring and all of his achievements through out the gym. He turns and gazes at the Famine mask hanging on the wall. He then turns his gaze to a barbed wire wrapped baseball bat. Afterwards he smiles and lifts a magazine from the ring apron again. This one showing his face bloodied and weathered as he grins sadistically. "When a man bleeds it brings out the strength in his actions. Shows his grit and determination and at the very least his stubbornness to not give up. I have never submitted in my life to any challenge and i will climb even further up the ladder of pain to achieve what i wish to achieve. Have i hidden behind anything ever in my career. Not in the slightest fashion would i even consider it. I fight the good fight and always enjoy the war." Shaman smiles and turns the magazine over showing the cover. It reads "PRIMES Winters versus GCW's Shaman. Two friends in a drawn out war for superiority" He grins as he opens it to GCW's page section and holds it towards the camera pointing at the picture. "Oblivion vs Choosers of the Slain." Shaman laughs deeply as he eyes the pictures and then taps his finger across the name. "Choosers of the Slain are Valkyries pure warrior maidens who come to escort the souls of the warriors to Valhalla the great Hall of the viking dead and Asgardian gods to sleep until the Horn is blown and they are needed for battle again. Kind of putting yourselves up on the pedestal there Lia and Vivica. I would have preferred Sisters of Battle there. You two do like a good fight over anything else. None the matter anyways. Truth be told i am actually going to come and speak with you Lia before all is said and done and the same with you Vivica. I am actually going to do you both a favor that we all will enjoy before Devils Night is over." Shaman stands up and folds the chair and lifts it and walks towards the ropes and looks all around him. "This is the first ring i ever wrestled in. I bought it as soon as i could. I own rights to my name and alot of the past i was involved in. I built a franchise name into a huge organization. I built a casino out of my own hard earned money and proper investments made. I have held shows in my Casino and made even more money with it. In the end i am a self made man who will do what is necessary to achieve what i deem a goal. I have a sorted past and yet i rose above the clutter to become one of the household names in the wrestling industry. I have come to fight for everything i am going to get ow and if anyone steps in my way i will crush them to dust." Shaman leans the chair against the wall and shuts out the lights and walks out into a hall way and walks as the lights shimmer from low voltage or possible shorts in the wiring. He opens the door to see Kawanaha leaning against the Suburban he owns as he smiles at her. She looks at him and shakes her head. "What are you smiling about we are late!" Shaman grins and walks past her and shakes his head. "Always business with you Kawanaha." She looks over at him and frowns. "You are always slacking and taking nothing serious." Shaman slips in behind the steering wheel and looks over at Kawanaha. "You have helped me alot and i appreciate it very much. You have no worries either way. I called the investors and told them that i moved the meeting up two hours. We are still well within our time frame. I explained to them i had personal wrestling business and since they are both big wrestlign fans i also promised tickets to the next casino show." Kawanaha smirked as she sat down and shut the door behind her. "Not to shabby from someone whom i view as a slacker." Shaman chuckles and starts the truck. "Yeah i'd expect that from you. Either way any news from Bryan Mayhem yet?" Kawanaha shook her head. "No we left messages everywhere where he said you could find him." Shaman frowned as he turned on the headlights. "He better be there i do not wish to have to deal with him brooding all night in some screwed up fashion. He is my friend and partner, but damn that guy needs to work on communication." Shaman accelerates kicking up dirt and dust as he drives off down the road into the distance as the tail lights disappear in the darkness.
