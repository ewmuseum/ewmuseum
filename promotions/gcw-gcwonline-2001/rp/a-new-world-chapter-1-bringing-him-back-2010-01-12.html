A New World - Chapter 1 - Bringing Him Back

12 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/12/10 [...Five years ago...] We are inside a run down building - It has been a long time since it was seen for its beauty - The paint is peeling from the walls, dust floating in the air and the only light comes from the moon through small holes in the ceiling. In the corner sits a man with medium length black hair, and dressed completely in black clothing. "All of my memories bring me so much pain..." The man sitting in the corner and speaking of his memories is The Living Nightmare himself. He is speaking of the life he has lived up to this very moment, and of all the pain he has had to suffer the entire time. Donny Diamond - The man who sold his soul to Satan himself in order to seek revenge against all of those who have wronged him. Only to be plagued with guilt and finding himself fighting his own living nightmare to get back what he should have never lost to begin with. "When you look into the eye's of the devil and see no sign of God, you know there is no hope..." In the ninth circle of Hell he wasn't prepared for the battle he would be forced to endure. After all when you think of Hell the only person that comes to mind is Satan himself, you would never think of yourself as the true enemy. However, that was his final trial, Satan was defeated and it was a battle against himself to see who would become the true ruler of Hell. "I learned that the words of God are the words of truth. Without evil you can never have good." He speaks the truth after all, just take a look at the world today. A war being fought over in the Middle East that marked the death of their ruler only to have another country salvage their land. A city washed away by a hurricane only to have the mayor say it was justice so that a deprived race could revive a sunken city. And now a new time for America where the President only cares to make changes for the people who can afford it. No matter how much good people believe they are doing, they cannot see the evil and pain they are creating. "That is why this must be done... In order for the world to see the truth, evil must return." Those words come from the man who wanted to make the world a better place, but has learned that there is only one way to make that happen. With the edge of a razor he slides it acorss his wrist and lets out a sigh of relief as blood begins to flow. Beside him the pool of blood grows bigger and bigger with each drop and it doesnt take long before the demons within the pits of Hell can be heard cheering as the final breath of Donny Diamond is released. §Aside me I stand and I see you running, When the piston fired my final breath screamed like thunder, Wrap me in your arms as I bleed away, Be happy for me and please don’t cry, Smile as I die for I am happy today, Let it drip, (My crimson wine)§ There is no lights but there is endless darkness and the only sound comes from a raspy voice not far off. Unknown: A visitor? No one ever comes here, so tell me why dear? With a flick of his fingers the darkness fades away and everything can be seen once again. The voice comes from an old demon, with green glowing eyes and dressed in cloak that has been drenched in countless amounts of blood. His old fingers crack as he opens a dusty old book and slowly turns the pages. You however are dressed in the strick black clothing you were known for always wearing, and your arms are stretched out and held in place by chains of flames. Unknown: Where, oh where are my manner's... It is I who controls this fleet and sir my name is Zeik. In your life someone has planted a nasty seed, but in my book I see a kind little deed. On the line sign your name and away shall go my bracelets in flame. But before you sign make sure your mind is kind, for once your loose anyone can choose. Diamond: Little demon that rhymes, where exactly am I? Zeik: Mr. Diamond that is longer shiney, your now in a world far from being smiley. Your in a world where no light will ever be shed, but your neither living nor dead. Diamond: Then tell me of this deed you speak of. Zeik: Your free to wonder without any fear, only until your name is spoken clear. Then to Earth you must return, and a new master for you to learn. Do his bidding until he tires, or remember from Hell and all the fires. A demon of others sin you will become but no longer are you his son, do the bidding for as long as the plea and then your soul once more will become so very free. Diamond: If that is the case, then you already know my answer. As those final words are spoken the flames around his arms are no more, and to his knees he falls. In a spiral of black smoke Zeik soon vanishes, slowly Diamond rises from his knees and walks off into the mist. [December 2009] Unknown: So all I do is follow the instructions here in this book and the deal is done? Unknown #2: Correct sir. Unknown: Then I guess all that's left is to summon him... A small conversation is on-going between two people, one dressed in normal business wear while the other hides himself in the shadows. The two of them shake hands and a small book is exchanged between them. Business Guy: Thanks so much for doing business with me sir. Unknown: No sir, it is you who needs to be thanked. This little book is not only going to be the answer to all of my problems, but it will help a lot more people than you can ever imagine. Once the business transaction is completed the business man is shown the door. Our inheriter of the book follows the hallways, of what seems to be his home, and heads down a flight of stairs. While making sure his idenity is never revealed he opens the door and walks into the basement. The walls are made of pure concrete and bricks while the door is bolted shut behind him, the area is free of anything that would make the room look unused. Unknown: Demónio do pecado que eu chamo para seu renascimento! Eu dou boas-vindas a lhe com um sacrifício da pureza, e à morte da carne! (Demon of Sin I call for your rebirth! I welcome you with a sacrifice of purity, and the death of flesh!) In Portuguese he speaks as his eyes read from the book he holds. Before he braced to a table is a small child, male, who is trembling in fear. As if trying to comfort the boy he calmly passes his hand aside his face. Imagine a horror movie and you will get the image before you, other than being strapped to this table, the floor around him has a small carving of an inverted pentagram. Needles line the tables, but only time will tell what they are to be used for. Unknown: Seus serviços pertencer-me-ão, Dagda! Seja renascido fora da raiva, da dor, e da vingança. Tome de mim esta alma pura e abrace a escuridão uma vez mais! (Your services will belong to me, Dagda! Be reborn out of anger, pain, and revenge. Take from me this pure soul and embrace the darkness once more!) After those words are spoken screams can be heard as the man, now to be called Dagda, takes one of the needles and pierces it through the eye socket of the small boy. The screams become undying as the same is done to his other eye, only to be followed by the slicing of the ankles and wrists upon the helpless child. From the table the crimson wine drips down into the pentagram on the floor, once it is completely outlined, flames erupt. Dagda: Não há nenhum salvador, e nenhuma punição do pecado. Torna-se o demónio que você foi criado para ser. Você viveu após a morte, e terminou sua própria alma. O Now é o momento para que o diamante de Donny seja renascido! (There is no savior, and no punishment from Sin. Become the demon you were created to be. You have lived past death, and ended your own soul. Now is the time for Donny Diamond to be reborn!) It doesn't take long before the flames engulf the boy, the smell of his burning flesh fills the room. Soon his cries fall silent and pure darkness consumes him, but suddenly from the darkness stands a man. The corpse of the child is ripped apart, as he kneels before Dagda from atop the table. His body is covered in scars from the battles he has endured over the years, his eyes stare deep into yours with a glow of red, covering him is a nice shirt and pants both matching the color of his eyes. Diamond: Dagda do you understand what you have done, by summoning my me from my final resting place? Dagda: I know exactly what I have done, and that is why I know you will be living off my every word. Because I know "people" like you, and I know exactly what craves deep down inside of them. You have lived the life of a Champion, but your hunger was never satisfied, but it will be. Diamond: You will never understand what it's like to be me. I have battled to destroy humanity and have battled to save it. There is nothing I lust for, nothing I need, nothing that you have could ever tempt me. All you have is a worthless book! Dagda: You could not be more wrong, but I will first show you that I am not here to control you, but to work with you instead. I have had people set up some things for me, but in order for it to be complete I need you as the final piece of the puzzle... Over five years ago a scar was left on your face to make sure you would ever forget! At the very mentioning of the scar Diamond passes his hand across his face. Under his right eye is where the shard of glass slashed him, the pain was unimaginable, but so was the taste of defeat. Dagda: There is a lot of money to be made for me, if I'm able to make a big impact in this federation called GCW. Me a wrestler? No, that would not happen - ever - but to bring back to this world someone who is capable of perfection is my answer. Diamond: How can you call me back for such greed? Dagda; Greed? No... No... I have brought you back for something much better than greed, I have called you forth for revenge! Inside the walls of GCW is the man that gave you that scare so many years ago. Anger rages from deep inside of him at the thought of the person that gave him the scar. He attempts to lunge forward to get more answers from Dagda, but the circle of flames act as a cage containing him. Dagda: That's right, you know exactly who I am speaking of, SurReal. In fact as we speak I have some people setting the stage for your return. No one will see you coming... SurReal will be so blinded that he will think his rival Equinox is behind it, but Equinox will just be our pawn in this plan and SurReal will get exactly what he deserves. Diamond: SurReal will taste his own blood I will make sure of it. The warrior of Sin will do battle for you, and many will pay during my rearrival... Just make sure SurReal is mine! Dagda: At Worldwide 122 is when Equinox will face SurReal in a last man standing match, and that is when you will take him down. Take SurReal down and bring me the the fame I'm after and I promise I will find many more souls for you feast upon. [Present] Rain falls from the sky as clouds move back and forth always keeping the sun hidden in the back. Its a lazy day where you would just cuddle under a blanket and take five minute naps to pass some time. Sitting on top of a building in the city of Austin, Texas all the while letting the rain pour down on him is Donny.. It has been a little over a month since his arrival to GCW where he destroyed SurReal and Equinox in the center off the ring, but now a new target has been selected. Diamond: Sparkly Goddess... No Sin is as satisfying as Lust... On a GCW Worldwide card where nearly all the matches make sense coming off the Christmas Chaos pay-per-view. Equinox has a chance for revenge as he takes on the German bitch Ngozi, SurReal is faced with the opportunity for gold and revenge as he takes on Cheap Labor, but Donny Diamond is selected to face the returning Tempest. Diamond: A legend in the circle of OSW, but I am the reason legends go missing. Just upon thinking of her, he can smell of all her sins. But this is different from a battle he would of had five years ago. No longer is he fighting to bring souls to Hell; the souls he defeats now is for himself and for the promise he made to Dagda. One seeks fame, while the other seeks complete dominince over anyone he comes into contact with, but really the goal for the two of them is the same. Diamond: Sparkly Goddess I cannot wait for the moment when my hand comes in contact with your flesh. Flesh so sensual and soft, I will cherish the moment I pull it apart. Do not worry over pain, because I promise it will be so pleasurable. From the roof of the building he falls with his crimson red coat flying along the updraft. Walking down the street he doesn't really keep Tempest on his mind for much longer, but back to SurReal his thoughts go. When the two of them finally get to do battle, how will it end... The Tribal Warrior lost all of his friends in the battles they had with The Living Night, and he is the only one left. The battles were fought in the name of someone else, much like his fight with Tempest will be, but his fight with SurReal will be for him. Diamond: Let the tears from Heaven fall because there will be no savior for him... He speaks softly about SurReal as walks in the rain, letting his crimson red clothing get soaked. Years ago he burned his father alive in an alley, much like the one he just passed. Would it come to that with SurReal? NC-17, GCW's premiere event, is only a few weeks away and everyone wants to be a part of it to take their claim for fame. Is this what Dagda is gunning for with the resummoning of the Emotionless Bastard? Diamond: Rest my darling and bask in the glory of your past days. Together we will make love with violence, and I will leave you on your back begging for more... The clouds begin to move faster as the wind picks up twisting the rain around. Lightning begins striking across the sky and one bolts strikes down on Donny. As the smoke fades away he is no longer there, instead is someone dressed in complete white with a long sword drawn out in front of him. He slices it across the cement causing a few sparks before walking off into the darkness.
