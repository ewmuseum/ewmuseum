Masks within Masks

3 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/03/2006 It was sort of amusing to see how other people in the world responded to winter changes in temperature. For Dawn, a day at seven degrees Celsius was on the cool side of pleasant, light coat and sweater day. For the people in Stockholm that meant it was time to drag out winter coats and hats, thick gloves and even long scarves to protect against the coming winter. Shaking her head with a smile, Dawn dug her hands further into the pockets of her black duster and breathed deeply of the cool air. Winter was definatly coming, but as far as she was concerned it wouldn't be here officially until the temperature dropped well below zero and snow started to fall. Until then it was a pleasure to enjoy the cool breeze on her face and the strange looks she got from passersby. Adjusting the strap of her gym bag once more, vainly seeking that perfectly comfortable way to rest it on her shoulder, she reflected glumly that she was out of the Dies Irae penthouse for business and not to enjoy the Autmn air. She'd received a rude surprise in regards to her most recent hurdle, one that had prompted the Steel Rose to leave the comfort of her hotel room and seek out the nearest gym. ********************************************************************************* Earlier that day... There was an annoying chirping at the back of Dawn's skull, the kind of noise that hinted there was something she should be doing that had managed to somehow slip her mind. It chirped and chirped and chirped, scraping the back of her brain and making it impossible to sink back into the heady slumber she'd been enjoying just moments before. Slowly, agonizingly, emerald green eyes opened to hateful slits, glaring balefully at the object that had dragged her so forcibly from the comfortable numbness of a few moments prior. Dawn hated cellular phones. If she wanted someone to be able to get in touch with her at all hour of the day then maybe she would have gotten one, but being a woman who inherently enjoyed her privacy that wasn't something that appealed to her in the slightest. Fumbling with one arm she flailed at the chirping phone, hoping to be able to strike it with a stray blow and either turn it off or fling it far enough away that she could return to sleep. In her current sleep addled state, however, there was little chance of accomplishing her objective. In retrospect, she supposed that was why Shadow had made her buy a small one. Pushing herself up on her forearms, the Steel Rose pushed and pulled herself groggily over to the edge of the bed, snatching the phone off the night stand before collapsing back into the tangled mess of pillows and covers. Snapping the phone open she held it up to one ear, eyes closed and voice pitched threateningly low. ?This had better be fucking good.? ?Awww c'mon now senorita, is that any way to greet an old friend?? The voice on the other end was heavily accented, the words flowing off his tongue with a delightful Spanish backdrop. ?Luis!? Dawn sat up suddenly and winced, one hand going to her backside as she shifted her weight over onto one hip instead. ?Er, I mean Master Sehra, I hadn't expected to hear from you.? ?Oh you know how it is, I've got to check up on my most attractive student from time to time don't you think? Besides, you never call, you never write, I was beginning to think that you didn't like a certain old man anymore.? Dawn could practically picture him in her mind, sitting on his front porch with his feet up on the railing, smiling like the cat that just swallowed the canary. Well two could play at this game. ?You're hardly an old man Master Sehra, besides, I've been SO busy spending time with Lia I'm surprised that I have time for anything else.? She shifted again, wincing at the unexpected pressure on her sensitive behind. There was a wry chuckle from the other end of the phone. ?Please Kaiden (Note: Japanese for Little Dragon) I'm too old and lecherous for such a ploy to work on me. I can go inside and download that kind of thing from my cable for less then I'm paying for my phone bill.? Flushing a bright red, Dawn self consciously straightened her sheets even though he couldn't see her. ?Fine, so you win that round again...what do I owe the pleasure of your call to Master? You could have just called me collect if you wanted to talk, I don't mind.? ?Well there's more to it then that, but even I did I can afford my own phone bill thank you very much.? He added with an amused note to his voice. ?More to it?? She sat up straighter, some of the fog clearing from her mind. ?It's about that young man you're facing this week, the one who likes to see you in the shower.? His voice was still amused, but there was a harder edge to it. ?Julio?? Pushing a handful of tousled hair out of her face, Dawn leaned her head back against the wall. ?On a first name basis with him now?? Luis couldn't resist the tease. ?I just do it because it irritates him.? She fired back, frowning. ?So why are you doing it while you're talking to me? You're not going to irritate me by saying his first name you know.? His tone was still light, but Dawn ground her teeth in frustration, he was always like this. ?Didn't you say there was something you wanted to tell me about him?? ?Yes there was. I've been doing some looking into him since I found out you were going to be facing him, and I found some things that are a little disturbing to say the least.? ?Would you quit being dramatic and just tell me already? I swear you should have been in theatre not wrestling.? ?Isn't it the same thing?? He fired back, ?Besides, I'll have you know my Macbeth was highly regarded when I went to university.? ?AGH! You're worse then Lia for giving me a straight answer!? She banged her head against the wall in aggravation. ?His father is Carlos Dehoyas.? Dawn froze, time seeming to slow down in the room around her. She was no longer aware of the cars racing by outside, or the steady ticking of the various appliances about the Knightmare Bridgade's penthouse suite. The only sounds were the beating of her own heart, and the steady breathing of her mentor on the other side of the phone. ?Dawn? Are you still there?? ?Y-yeah.? She replied shakily, ?I'm here.? ?Are you alright?? He was concerned, the accent slipping a little. ?You were quiet for a long time.? ?I thought Dehoyas' son died in a match several years ago.? ?He did...apparently he had two sons. I don't know the details exactly, but for whatever reason the second son was exiled from Mexico for some kind of grievous family slight. Carlos wouldn't meet with me to talk about it, but whatever happened between them it was huge.? ?And you're sure it's Julio?? She asked. ?I'm one hundred percent sure Kaiden, it can be no other.? ?Carlos Dehoyas...son of a bitch.? ?Actually his mother is a very attractive woman, I've met her once or twice over the course of my career.? It was forced, but the joviality helped to lessen some of the sudden strain Dawn was feeling. ?So...was he trained by Dehoyas? He was one of the best Luis, one of the best Mexico has ever seen.? Okay, maybe not that much of a lessening. ?You don't have to remind me Kaiden, I've been on the receiving end of some of that brilliant offense more then once. I don't think I ever put one over on the old man, and the one time I was graced to tag with him we completely cleaned house.? ?So...did he train Julio?? Anxious. She chewed frantically on her thumbnail, betraying the sudden anxiety gnawing at her guts. ?At least the basics. I don't know if he taught him everything he knew, but at the very least he helped to lay the foundation for the kind of wrestler La Sombra is now.? Luis sounded grim. ?He's like me.? Her voice was quiet, small. ?What?? ?Julio, he's like me. He's wrestling to get back into his fathers good graces isn't he? He's trying to erase that family incident that happened between himself and his father.? Silence. ?That's it isn't it? That's what you called to tell me, that he's wrestling for his father...just like I'm wrestling for mine.? ?Dawn...you cannot look at him that way and you know it. This man was trained, at least in basics, by one of the greatest Luchadores this world has ever seen. If you let your guard down for even a minute, if you let sympathy or empathy thaw your heart to him...he will put you down, and he will put you down hard.? More silence, but for the sound of her thumbnail slowly giving way beneath the gnawing of her teeth. ?That's why he's so offended by everything that I do. We're the same, he and I, and he doesn't like the way I'm doing things.? ?I don't think it's that. You just found out about his father, chances are he doesn't know about yours. Just because you share the same last name doesn't mean that people automatically connect the pair of you.? His voice was low and serious. ?Dawn I didn't call to tell you what you had in common emotionally with La Sombra.? ?Emotionally?...We can't have much in common physically Luis.? She adjusted uncomfortably, rubbing lightly at her bruised posterior. ?No...but you have similar training.? ?What?!? She sat up suddenly, ?That can't be right, I never trained under Dehoyas, I trained under you...? Her voice trailed off, the obvious suddenly placing itself directly in front of her. On the other end of the line, Luis Sehra chuckled softly and crossed one booted foot over the other.?...and I trained under Dehoyas. A great deal of what I taught you, I learned from him. So if I passed that onto you...? ?...then he may have passed it onto Julio.? She shook her head, ?But I studied under Master Mifune as well.? ?Si, and that's your major advantage here. You can fall back on the things he taught you, and try not to use the things I taught you. He'll know the moves I taught you, and know the counters for them. You'll know the same things about him, but you'll be able to fall back to other training.? Dawn smacked one hand off her thigh, voice rising in excitement. ?So if I stick to one thing, I can have the advantage over him.? ?Si, now you're understanding why I called. Still...? ?Still?? She sounded perplexed. ?He's probably been training night and day for you...and you're still in bed!? He put some of the trainers bark into his voice and she jumped, looking sheepish. ?Okay okay, I get it I get it.? Kicking both feet over the bed she stood with a wince, ?Thank you Master, thank you for everything.? ?I have to look out for my students hey? If not me, who else? You take care Kaiden, put on a good show.? ?I will. Take care Master.? ****************************************************************************** Stopping in front of the Gym, Dawn unconsciously flexed her right fist, the crimson bindings around it creaking from the pressure. ?Like two sides of the same coin,? She murmered. ?Both fighting for the same thing.? Shaking her head as if to clear it she pushed open the front doors and walked inside, duster billowing behind her. La Sombra was her enemy plain and simple, no matter the similar upbringing they shared. Fate had thrown them together, and fate would help see the victor through to the end. ?I don't believe in fate.? She mouthed to herself, heading into the women's locker room.
