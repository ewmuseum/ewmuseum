The depths of hell

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless This looks like a good place. Quiet and desolate. Maybe no one will recognize me and I can drink a beer in peace. (walks inside dive bar) Good, just a few rednecks. Kind of difficult going out when you're almost 7 feet tall and a major name in the Wrestling World. These fuckers barely look conscious enough to wipe their own asses. 'What will it be big guy?' I'll take a Rogue. 'Sure thing. Coming up right away.' He heads to the back to fetch my beer. I begin to scan the bar. Pool tables, dart boards, a college basketball game on the Big screen. Jukebox playing some awful country music. Couple guys playing pool, with their fat mamas encouraging them on. Some weird looking old guy sits three stools down from me. He keeps looking at me. One eye winked shut, the other glosses over. He's barely shaven, and I can smell him from here. I'll pay him no mind. (bartender sets down beer) Ahh, now that's refreshing. Our of the corner of my eye that old fucker is still staring at me. Calm down Bryan, don't make a scene, you'll end up killing him. 'I once knew your father and whore for a mother.' I look around, me? Is he talking to me? 'Yes big fella, I am.' Did I just say that out loud? 'No you didn't.' I turn towards the old man. One eye still winked shut, he begins to give a dreadful smile. Missing teeth, there's only a few visible, and those are beginning to rot away. His breath smelled of the something wicked. Part of his tongue was missing, lips chapped and cracked. Green mucus formed at the corner of his lips. He starts to lick his lips with his half severed tongue. 'I know what you're thinking their big boy. I can read your mind.' I turn away once again, and sipped my beer. Who the fuck... '...is this guy?' I turned again and stared at him, deep into his bloodshot eye. I turn my nose upward, to almost look down on him, as if it wasn't hard. 'You may scare the children of Belmont there big boy, but to me you're just a pussy. Your mother and father are rotting in hell, because of you.' I continue my stare, not knowing where my mind will wander next, almost afraid of my own psyche. 'You want to snap my neck, I see that. Go ahead fucker, give it a shot. And when you're done, I'll fuck your whore of a mother in hell. In hell you hear me motherfucker.' I stand up and walk closer to this poor excuse for a human being. I'm inches from him. I'm a towering presence over him. He simply looks right up at me and starts to laugh. It's a wheezing death sound of a laugh. 'You're a violent man, this we all know. But you ain't shit to me boy. I raped your mother, robbed your father, beat your younger more famous brother, but you want to know the best thing I've done?' My teeth begin to grind. I clench my fists, I see red, I see blood. 'You want my blood bitch? Come on, hit me, but I guarantee you won't make it out of here alive Bryan. Come on, or would you rather see what I did?' That hideous laugh again, that sick disgusting almost inhuman laugh. He points to the big screen and the image of the basketball game metamorphsizes into a picture of a famaliar red barn. Jeffery's barn. My youngest brother. Dsfigured and metally retarted at birth. My father made get rid of him when he saw what an atrocity he would be to the O'neil name. 'You watching?' I don't remove my eyes from the screen. BOOOOOOOM! I almost jump, and thats when I realize the barn is ablazed, with my younger brother still in it. I look back at the old man, and completely snap. I grab him by his neck and I begin to squeeze with all my might. His other eye opens, and they seem to turn red right before me. His severed tongue is forked, his skin is scaly, his breath is of death. I losen my grip. 'Ever been to hell? Well, come with me.' I stare into his eyes, and they suck me into them. It feels like I'm traveling into some other dark realm, some realm of fear, emptiness, and despair. I let go of his neck and begin to feel a burning in my eyes. I close them to shut out the burn and when I open them, I'm higher than everyone. I Stare down from what is some sort of loft and notice my mother. My god, thats my fucking mother. She's staring at me, sobbing. I attempt to grab her, but for some reason my hands are restrained. I look and notice the horror. I'm nailed to a fucking cross. The world stares at me from my perch, watches blood stream from the nail wounds. The old man is down below, holding my mother, staring at me with that sadistic smile. He holds her tight. She buries her face into his chest. Once again he stares at me, and flicks that forked tongue in my direction. He nods. I try to scream, but nothing. Not a single whimper. My mother lifts her head, looks at me, than him. His old scaly hands grab her by her face, tilts her head upward, and digs his teeth into her throat. Her screams fill my head. He rips his face away with such force, most of her throat comes with it. He spits it out as she falls to the ground. I struggle, but only hurt my cause. I feel the nails rip through my skin as my struggle continues and intensifies. I scream, and look towards the 'heavens' and thats when I feel it. A sharp sting, and ripping sensation. He stuck the spiteful spear into my side and begins to thrust it violently. 'Ever been to hell?' I look again towards the heavens, and let out what I think is a blood curdling scream that most likely falls upon deaf ears in this world I'm in. I feel a warm pattering wet dripping on my nose, and look up. I'm in my bed, here in Belmont, staring into the ceiling. The dripping was blood. My ceiling is covered in the black blood that erupts once a person dies and loses oxygen. It's a shiny black blood, you can see and image in it. My eyes adjust, little more. I'm staring into it's eyes, waging war here on earth. On earth, where mankind rules all, and hell is just a puddle of blood away. I close my eyes, and continue my slumber, this time undisturbed.
