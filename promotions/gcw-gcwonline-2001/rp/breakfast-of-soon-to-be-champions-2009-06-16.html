Breakfast of (soon to be) Champions

16 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16, 2009 “I done tooooold you!” Uncle Truck stands feet away from the counter, peering over his shoulder at his partner and the commotion nearby. With his back to the bigger half of Holy Hell, Friar looks forward with a submissive slump in his shoulders. The crinkles in his mask resemble humbleness. The crinkles on the face of the woman opposite him… resemble pissed. “It’s SHArice, muthafucka! And the last thing I need in my mo’nin’ is some no-shirt mask-wurrin’ freakshow, comin’ in hurr with they’ big-ass buddy, and scurrin’ tha shit outta the whole goddamn place!” Truck turns full circle and steps to the counter. Friar folds his hands together and searches his soul for another way to say it…but finds nothing. A large hand wraps over his shoulder like a boa made of World Strongest Man arm. “Excuse me, Sharice?” The woman looks over Friar’s shoulder. He eyes slowly scan up twelve inches and her eyes grow in size. He looks up at a surprisingly charming smile beaming off the grill that was his massive partner. A short glance between the two before Friar catches the quickest wink he’s ever been winked. “I couldn’t help but overhear you two talkin’. And, well…I’m sorry to hear that you’re strugglin’ on this beautiful, beautiful Tuesday mornin’ but… could I ask you a favor?” “Truck…I thought we agreed I’d do the talking to the people and you stick to moving mountains?” A slight pat on Friar’s back comes from the mighty paw that is Uncle Truck’s right appendage. He leans into Friar’s ear, whispering: “Relax buddy. I’m talkin’ to Sharice, here. Plus, I think she digs the tats. Back me up, bro.” Friar looks back to Sharice’s cold gaze at the two of them. Her arms fold over her massive bosom and her eyes lock onto the ones beneath the mask. Friar forces a smile to her in politeness. Uncle Truck leans closer in. “Do you think…just maybe…you’d find it in your heart to sneak us in? We’ve got a huge meeting at two. Plus…I hear your waffles are to die for.” A glare that could demolish a double-reinforced diamond windmill comes from the lady working at the luxurious eatery in midtown Manhattan. Uncle Truck matches it with a smile. Which draws one from her in return. “Oh how could I say no to that pretty face? What did ya say yuh name was sugah? I got a table that juss’openin’ up.” Truck looks to the gaping hole that is Friar’s mouth. He lifts the jaw upward and smiles at his partner with a cunning grin. “Zestwich. Bill Zestwich.” “Alright, Mista Zestwich. Follow me, please. And feel free to enjo’ tha view -- aheheheheeehe.” Swaying her hips from side to side, Sharice steps from around the podium and leads the duo down the aisle of this restaurant. Each table seats the finest suits and nicest jewels from the world’s inventory. Friar looks at Truck. “Are you sure we’re supposed to be here? This doesn’t look like our type of place.” Friar scans his surroundings. "Oh, and nice alias, Bill." “Can it, bro,” Truck says, smiling and nodding to a pair of barely legal blondes as he passes by. “We’ve got a lot of pull these days, with the wrestlin’ gig and all. These people look up to us now. They will really kiss our asses after we put on the clinic against December Revolution.” Friar chuckles and looks up at his giraffe-like partner. “Look up to us? To you maybe. And that’s only because you remind people of the Twin Towers. These people don't follow wrestling.” The woman stops and spins, catching Truck’s eyes on her voluptuous hips and rear. Another gut-wrenching smile comes toward the pair of professional fighters. “Here ya go, boys,” Sharice casually says, pulling out a chair for the bigger member and leans in to whisper in his ear. “If you lookin’ for desert you know whurr I be sittin’. Right ova thurr…” She points to the chair next to the door. It is directly in his line of view. A sick look comes over Truck’s face while Friar watches on in agony. The big man then claps his hands together and look to Sharice like a stoned little school boy. “Will do, momma!” Truck smiles, watching Sharice walk past. She also gets a large hand from the big man across the right side of her ass. She jumps, smilin’ back all sexy like. “That’s Grade A right there, brother! I can’t wait t’take a bite outta that.” Friar let’s out a sigh of annoyance, “My brother have you learned nothing? Sins of the flesh have no stake in my quest. I follow the Light. A proper and pure way of life that--“ “Proper?” Truck interjects. “Pure? Come on now, Mask Man. Don’t feed me those lines. We do one thing and we do it well -- hurt people.” Truck pulls out a cigarette. Staring around him, he notices a few wandering eyes lock onto his cancer stick. With the flicking open of his Zippo he draws a few more stares He lights it an exhales a plume of smoke into the air over their table. Lifting the coffee mug, he turns it into an ashtray. Friar’s anxiety peaks at the point Truck’s big boot lands on the table, making enough noise to draw all eyes in the house. “Jesus, Trucker!” Friar says, shooting out of his seat and to his feet. “Take a seat, bub,” says the big man. “You know Jesus ain’t gonna save me.” A waiter comes by and taps Truck on the shoulder. “Excuse me sir…there’s no smoking allowed in here. You’ll have to put that out.” Truck shoots the man a steely gaze that could cool the room by ten degrees. Shortly after, a plume of smoke enters the wait staffer’s face. “Relax, bub,” Truck starts, “I been smokin’ Reds longer than you been servin’ salads in the men’s room.” In frustration, the waiter runs off amongst a crowd of angry patrons. Truck chuckles at the sight. “So, that’s it?” Friar pleads with his partner. “You sweet talk that woman to get us in here for a healthy meal before this match and you’re going to just smoke our way out of here? You really know how to make yourself heard.” Truck grins again. “No brother. That ain’t it. This is just the beginning! I mean, think about it. We’re here in GCW’s bill and we’re in New York City. The Big Apple!” “Yeah well,” Friar scans around the restaurant. “The Big Apple ain’t exactly the Poland Springs of watering holes. Rampage isn’t exactly going to be a walk in the park.” “Yeah, you’re right…” Truck says pulling on the cigarette once again. “It’s gonna be easier! Seriously, bro. Just enjoy it. Anyways, the guys we take on are still wet-behind-the-ears and getting’ a feel for the ring. We’ve got the jump on ‘em already!” Friar glares at Truck. The big man notices and slaps his partner’s knee once for composure. “Plus their tallest guy is as tall as you!” Truck’s head falls back and his mouth opens. “Hah! I love being a giant.” Truck puts the cigarette out in the mug and drops some water in to extinguish the embers. He looks around and snaps his fingers. Friar clutches his wrist, drawing it down. “Partner,” Friar starts, “you and I have a lot to do. This place is a breeding ground for insolence and sin. You know as well as I do that our past has a lot of dark spots but this is our chance to right where we have gone wrong. Do not let the temptation of fame and riches corrupt whatever that is you call a cerebral muscle and blur the lines we’ve drawn in the sand. Do you get me?” The waiter comes back over, this time standing behind a man wearing all black and a manager tag. Friar facepalms his mask as he catches a curious eye from the authority figure. “Excuse me, gentlemen. I was told there is an issue. Could you come with me?” He waits patiently and holds a false smile over his lips. Truck stands up after glancing to Friar and soon is the center of attention for the restaurant. “Aww shit,” Truck begins as he makes an attempt to read the nametag. “I was just talkin’ to my boss…Steven Caldera…and he was-“ “Wait…” the managing figure stops the big man, holding his hands up. “You…wrestle for GCW?” Truck grins back to Friar. “Sure do, fella. But don’t think that for one minute I work with that scum-suckin’ two-faced pigeon-holed jerk. Nope. Not me. Me and my partner, Friar, wrestle in GCW. You might have heard Rampage is at the Garden this week and…” A rumbling comes from behind the big man and he stops for a few seconds. He notices the buzz going around the place and begins to speak to the whole body of people in his vicinity. “Of course, of course! I am so sorry,” Claude, the manager, says apologetically. He ushers over a fresh tray of fruit for the guests. “We had no idea! I’m sure it was all a simple misunderstanding and you-“ “Actually,” Truck says as the smile fades away from his face. “It was a test. A test to the good people, here, at Filipetti’s fine dining. A test to see if you were the type of people that our cause stands to help – the good people of Earth.” A small applause comes from the masses. Friar stands and the people stop clapping. His mask is a bit unsettling to many. “Good children of the world,” he says with an odd pitch in his voice. “Thank you for making us feel welcome in your wonderful city.” An applause brought on by mostly confusion brings a smile to Friar’s face. “It is a long journey to the top, as many of you know, and we intend to make that long haul into the darkness in order to preserve the Light. This time, the battlefield chosen by Caldera the Corrupt crosses our paths with yours…at Madison Square Garden.” Truck pats his partner’s back as the roar of a restaurant and its’ patrons slowly drowns out anything they say to each other. The two lock eyes and both have a bright smile over their face. “See buddy?” Truck says to Friar. “We’ve got a lot of pull these days. You think guys like fives or Winters get treated like this?” Friar rolls his eyes. “Let’s keep it that way.”
