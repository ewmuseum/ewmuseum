X's Oddity

Date: Sometime [Scene fades up] A room. Just like any other. Except that this room really isn't like any other. All along the walls are masks. From African Tribal masks to traditional lucha libre masks, masks from all around the world. No rhyme, no reason, just, well, odd. This is the world that X lives in. A world that isn't all on the up and up. The mask that many people look at as a fashion statement, is really a part of X's world. Whether it's to keep up with the enygma of his persona or the tradition that follows the mask into the world of professional wrestling, one will never know. All that there is to know about X is everything that I will ever tell you. You see, I'm X's voice. I'm the Storyteller. I'm the one that those like the Guild should fear. Because you don't know what control I have over X. The Enygmatic One. Born of this world, but not of it. When it comes down to it, X's reality will crash down on GCW. Maybe, just maybe this room of masks will give a clue on who he is. [Camera pans around to show dead center X's mask.] But then again, that clue............could lead to just another mystery. Guild, are you ready for what X is going to bring to you. I think not. I think that you don't have the capacity to understand all that there is to understand about X. The complexity of this man's psyche is not easily understood. Members of the Guild, get ready for it. Get ready for when it's all said and done, they mystery will consume you, too.
