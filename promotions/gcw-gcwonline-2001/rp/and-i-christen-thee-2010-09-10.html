And I Christen Thee..

10 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/10 "Everyone get on the cursed BUS!!" As sixteen random folks shuffle awkwardly onto a massive, jet-black RV, a salt and pepper-haired individual stares at them coldly through dark shades. His thick head of hair wafts slowly in the breeze in unison with the loose parts of his slightly ridiculous leather robe. The robe resembles that of a Christian virg-MONK, only made of leather and a slick black shade. His face is taut and cold with sharp cheekbones protruding from beneath his eyes and a long nose splitting his eyes. His lips purse in vehement contempt as he watches the last of the folks disappear into the RV, their picket signs clacking against cold steel. He joins them shortly after, slamming the heavy door behind him as the huge RV roars to life. As the man steps into the RV, he takes his place between two large couches, standing at the head of the mini-congregation. Nervous, anxious stares meet his cold features as he looks from one group to the other as though he were surveying future prey. He whips both arms out and faces his open palms to the group with the speed of a viper, eliciting a gasp from the groups. His raises his head to the ceiling and begins to SHOUT. "CHILDREN OF THE GODDESS… our lives have PURPOSE! Your worthless existence persists to serve Her in the eternal struggle to FREE Her from Her prison!" The man’s gravely, booming voice rises in emphasis over certain words, but never leaves a metal-ringing volume. Everyone in the RV looks on in awe and fright as though the man’s voice could explode their dumpy heads at any moment. "LISTEN, and feel my words swell within your weak, addled minds!! WE HAVE WAITED for our time, and our time has COME! En’Flarim’s rage burns for the blasphemers, the Zionists, the children of a modern day MOTHER GOOSE! The calamities that have befallen this world are HER RAGE MADE MANIFEST! The very sun BURNS and FLAYS our flesh, shuffling MANY from this mortal coil every waking hour!" His gaze snaps down from the ceiling and stares straight ahead, emotionless behind the dark shades. His voice continues to roar over the engine of the RV as the giant vehicle lumbers down the road. "WE, Her blessed followers, have felt the COMING of the ENNNDDD! For too long she has been locked within her FIERY PRISON, driven to incarceration by the retarded populous of this misbegotten rock! I watch in utter delight as-" One from the congregation suddenly speaks, cutting the man off. Her voice shakes with fear as she raises a finger as though to silence the man. "Most Grand Archon, blessed leader, what are we going to do?" The man’s mouth slowly curls into a sneer, revealing a line of pearly white teeth. His gaze begins to turn and fixes itself upon this hapless woman who continues to shake in her very shoes. Oh how mighty his wrath will be! What punishment will he bring upon the ant, that whose purpose is to the carry the weight of Her message upon it’s tubby back?? His voice falls to a more manageable, yet still very sinister tone… "Nameless cretin, you do not speak before spoken to. En’Flarim hates you for interrupting her Bringer of Light, and I feel her rage building. Pray for your life to end suddenly." Before the woman can react, the man snatches a picket sign from the floor, rears back, and smashes the woman straight across the face with it! Wood meets flesh with a sickening slap that sends her head bouncing off the space-age plastic like a damned wig-wearing tennis ball. She slumps forwards and cradles her face, bursting into tears as blood seeps through her hands. The man clears his throat, drops the sign, and continues his maniacal rant. "CURSED are the unbelieving! DEAD are the uncaring! THIS IS YOUR FATE! I have SEEN Her wrath, and she has blessed me as her warrior! For so LONG I carried Her heart, her WRATH, her HATE into battle with miscreants! PCW was my playground, and the unbelieving were fat sloppy children upon which Her HATE was cast upon like giant flaming DODGEBALLS! I have injured, beaten, mangled, mauled, and ROASTED enough men, women, homosexuals, retards, beast-lovers, and cunts named Gadget to fill a thousand LIFETIMES! And I…" He pauses to soak in their wide stares. They look like deer about to meet their steel, horsepower-driven fates. The woman continues to sob quietly, yet she nods at everything he says. "… I have HEARD her calling again! No LONGER will we sit idly by as the blaspheming bastards and bitches continue to INSULT Her with their continued existence! She hates this WORLD for being a cesspool of faggotry and lying! And we shall bring her message anew to a new herd of drooling MANCHILDREN!" The congregation nods solemnly and lowers their heads, mouthing words to themselves in prayer. The man smiles to himself and raises his arms as though embracing the sky. BOOM The RV goes smashing over a speedbump, startling the entire group and causing them to gasp and grip whatever or whoever is near. The man, however, goes rocketing into the ceiling of the RV like a leather-coated bottle rocket. His head collides with the ceiling with a nasty thud before he comes tumbling to Earth. The man lands on his feet and lets out a string of profanities that could curdle fresh breast milk. He goes rushing into cockpit, coat flapping behind him wildly. A moment later, all the congregation can hear is the sound of his voice followed by what sounds like a wet towel smacking across flesh. "YOU STUPID…" SMACK! "…IGNORANT…" SMACK! "…FAT…" SMACK! "…INCOMPETANT…" SMACK! "…BLIND…" SMACK! "…BOY-LOVING…" SMACK! "…RETARD!" With each smack the RV swerves, sending the hapless folks within spiraling against each side. The man suddenly bursts forth from the cockpit and runs his hand through his sweaty, mangled head of hair. He winces and flexes his hand in and out, looking at the deep red mark that replaced his tanned flesh. He grabs his wrist and looks up at the frightened, speechless group of onlookers. Shooting them a look of contempt, he brushes past them quickly and enters a room in the back of RV, slamming and locking the door behind him. They all breathe a sigh of relief, some shaking their heads while others wring their hands together. A man in his 30s looks around at all of them and gives a nervous chuckle. "Just another day aboard Starship Tracer…" Fin
