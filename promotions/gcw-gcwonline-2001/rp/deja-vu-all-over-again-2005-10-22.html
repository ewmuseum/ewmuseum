Deja vu all over again

22 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/22/05 'Dan Rivero. Dan Fucking Rivero. I can't fucking believe it.' Those were the words of my business manager/agent Jeff Wylde. Former GCW superstar and former holder of the TV title, the title that now graced my waist. 'Remind me again of what he did.' I said, sitting down on the couch next to Cindy and sipping at the beer I had retrived from the mini bar in my room, I had yet to leave San Diego since I wasn't booked on the next card. I'd be there, just no rush if the booking comitte was giving me the night off. 'He wrecked my car.' Jeff said, 'A rare vintage 1964 Mustsang, totally fucked it up. Trashed it from one end to the other, it took me nearly nine thousand dollars to fully restore it, and I had to look all over hell and creation before I found someone who could actually do it.' I whistled, I had seen the car in question and the restorer did a hell of a job, he hadn't told me at the time that the car had once been the victim of an assault. Up until now I had no idea. 'I was in the Revolution at the time with Jason O'Neil, Bryan Mayhem and Alex Grey. Not too long after that Jason left the Revolution to join the Establishment, and I challenged Rivero to a three stages of hell match. The first fall was a Parking Lot Brawl which I won, we dragged ourselves to the ring and had a Finishers Only match, which was the Wylde Ride versus the Rivero Elbow, and he won that one hitting me right in the sternum with that damn elbow, and then the final fall was a Scaffold Match, I won that one, hitting him with a Wylde Night and making him fall off. Then Bryan chokelsammed me off effectivley ending the Revolution.' I took another pull off my beer and tried (without much success) to belch quietly. 'You don't say.' Wylde said, and even over the phone I could tell he was smiling at me. 'I just did.' I said, also smiling, 'And now he's back.' 'And he took it to Bryan Mayhem, though Bryan looked like he was enjoying it. For as long as I've known that man, he welcomes pain. He probably wanted Rivero and his cronies to hit him again.' Jeff said, 'Of course after what he did to Jake Summers, Byran also enjoys dishing out pain as much as he enjoys getting it, in fact I think it's even moreso that he enjoys hurting people.' 'He's a sick bastard there's no doubt about that.' I said, 'So what can you tell me about this Rivero character?' 'That like LeStatt Knight he was once considered a joke around GCW. Backstage it was like the punchline to a joke. 'Why did the chicken cross the road?' 'Dan Rivero!' But if LeStatt's drastic turnaround isn't proof enough that people can change, then you'd better believe that Rivero's changed, and from what I saw it looks like it was a change for the worse.' 'And he's got friends with him as well.' I said remembering the man in the Steven Caldera mask and the smaller masked man, both who held Mayhem down while Rivero made his comeback speech. 'Yeah, and there was something about them...something familiar.' there was a pause on the other end of the line, 'Nah...it couldn't be.' 'What?' 'Nothing.' I knew he was lying but I didn't push the issue, instead I did the wise thing. I changed the subject. 'So why am I not booked this week? The GCW run out of people to feed to me?' 'Probably that and the fact that you've been going nonstop since you walked in the door. You took the GCW by storm and quickly snatched up that TV title, and you've outdone my reign I think.' Jeff said, 'And while that title is nice, I think the world title would look even better around your waist. If anything you need to find LeStatt Knight and get yourself into the main event at Devil's Night.' 'I was thinking about that myself,' I said after taking another sip of my beer. 'The way I see it, I've got an umblemished record, undefeated since I set foot in the company, the TV champ, and the best TV champ since you held the title.' 'And according to the latest polls both on line and in the magazines, the third most hated wrestler in GCW.' 'Just the third?' I asked, arching an eyebrow. 'The third what?' Cindy asked me. 'Third most hated wrestler in GCW.' 'Just the third?' she asked. 'That's what I said. Who's over me?' 'LeStatt Knight and Rich Rollins.' 'Well that makes sense I suppose.' I said. 'What makes sense?' Cindy asked. I told her. She nodded 'Makes sense.' 'That's what I said.' 'You still gonna go to next weeks show?' Jeff asked me. 'Yeah, I might as well, I may not be booked but I wanna be where the action is. Never know what can happen. Plus I do need to talk to your buddy Lestatt.' 'Don't call him my buddy. Not even joking. The shit I've been through with him I could write an entire book on.' 'You dedicated a whole chapter to him in your autobiography.' I reminded him. 'Yeah, but that's not enough. Trust me, if I went into every little detail of all the shit I endured with him from XCW to DWA to 3W, it'd take a book thicker than fucking Webster's Dictionary. The undabriged version. The point is, if LeStatt's made a complete turnaround from the GCW jobber to the GCW Champion, then chances are Rivero's made a 360 as well. Be careful around him.' 'Jeff, you're telling me to watch my back.' I said almost laughing. 'You're talking to a seven time world champion. You think I don't know that by now? Come on man, this is me you're talking to. If anybody can watch their back it's me. Even without allies I can take care of myself.' 'Just concerned for you, both as my friend and an investment.' 'I'm glad you said friend first.' 'Well the money you're making is nice, but you're my friend first.' Jeff said, 'You know as well as I do even if you quit wrestling tomorrow I could still make a shitload of money. But at this point it's not about the money, not with all the shit going on in GCW. You never know who's going to be standing behind you with a knife in their hand.' 'I thought it was the ones in front you you had to watch out for.' I said, remembering something he once told me about friends and enemies, 'An friend will stab you in the back while an enemy will stab you in the chest.' 'Indeed,' he said, and I could hear the low bass grumble that he passed off as a chuckle come through my cell phone, 'But in a place like GCW you need to be looking in both directions. Especially with guys like LeStatt and Mayhem running around. They're likley to stab you from both directions at the same time.' 'I can handle myself, J, you know that.' I said, taking another sip of my beer. I cracked my neck. 'Don't worry about me, I'll be fine.' 'I know,' he said, taking me at my word, 'I got the Wylde Tyme Corporate jet on standby so you can head to Denver whenever you're ready.' 'We'll probably leave tomorrow.' I said, 'We're gonna meet Brian for dinner tonight. Promised him the other night after I whipped football boy.' 'Alright, later man.' 'Peace.' We hung up. 'So what next?' Cindy asked me as I snapped my phone shut. 'Well I need to talk to LeStatt Knight about getting in the main event at Devil's Night. As good as a champion as LeStatt's been, there comes a time in every wrestler's career where he needs to pass the torch and let someone else stand in the spotlight.' 'And you're that man?' 'Damn straight. I'm not a seven time world champion for nothing. I can handle the pressure of being at the top of the mountain and having a target on my back. I did it before I can do it again. It's about time for LeStatt Knight to pass the torch and I wanna be the one to do what Michael Steven's couldn't.' I said grinning and finishing off my beer. 'I'm going to become the GCW World Heavyweight Champion.'
