CWMC First Round RP: The Way of Mortals

4 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4, 2007 __Start Rule number one for mortals: Hold your ground. __________ The connived Cluelessness. Astonishment. Acknowledgement. Celebration. Greed. It was a cycle that most men in the profession went through. For Jay Terror, it was his first. He could hear the roar of the audience and he remembered standing up hand in hand with the referee. His body ached and he wanted more than ever to lie down and get sleep. As the referee congratulated him he honestly couldn't figure out what he had done to achieve such merit. Jay could remember walking backstage and receiving pats on his back and words of encouragement. The reason, though, was still unclear. It wasn't until he woke the next morning that he realized the bruises were still there. His slumber didn’t wash away the pain nor the marks like he figured it would. Everything was real. Jay rubbed his eyes and sat up from the body mold he had created in the sheets. His vision came back slowly and as it did he looked around noticing a rather unfamiliar atmosphere. It was a room. It had a sliding door. It had one bed. It had a bottle of water with a tag on it. With all the clues pieced together he imagined he had rented a hotel room for the evening. After looking left he knew how it happened. The bottle of Jack Daniels created a lopsided rainbow on the far wall using the light seeping through the sliding door. It was empty. He followed the line along its path and found that it stopped in the corner just above another table. There was another empty bottle of Jack looking back at him. He shook his head and spun to get up from the bed when the phone rang on the night stand. He let it buzz three times before realizing it was his and once more before picking it up and answering it. 'Hello?' He mouthed into the speaker. 'I hope I didn't wake you sir, but we have a guest at the front desk for you. Would you like me to send him up?' The voice belonged to a middle aged man, according to the tone, and held a certain edge to it. Jay wrinkled his eyebrows and laid back down on the bed. 'Who is it?' 'He has introduced himself as Daniel Franco.' Terror remained silent. 'Would you like me to send him up?' He held his silence for another moment while shutting his eyes. Jay had hoped Franco's name wouldn’t come up after severing his ties with him the week before. He had even warned GCW officials that he wanted Daniel to know nothing about his current location. Unless someone slipped up, Daniel was either very sneaky or someone involved in the Chris Williams tournament had given him information. 'Is he alone?' A pause. 'Yes sir, just Mr. Franco.' 'If he comes up here with a woman you can be sure that you won't have a job in this hotel any longer.' He would have mentioned the type of hotel but he was still unsure where his drunk self had brought him. The man from the front desk relayed the information and returned, 'He is with no women. Should I take that as a yes to send him up.' Jay figured he was going to regret his decision but once he said it he had no choice, 'In five minutes.' __________ The Conniver He had practiced his smile in private for well over four years. It seemed that every mirror he passed he found a second to flash his teeth once in preparation for the right time. As he walked through the hallway and into the elevator, Daniel Franco found one last moment to test his work. It was show time. He was surprised to hear Jay had stayed at a hotel. It had become customary for him to return home to Philadelphia in between matches in order to prepare properly. He knew where to find help and he knew what he had to accomplish. Nevertheless, Jay didn't return home from Flagstaff and, instead, took homage within the Marriott. Knowing the circumstances, Daniel understood. He pressed the button for the fifth floor on the panel and watched the door close in front of him. Through the crack he noticed an elderly woman waddling toward the elevator but felt no pity for her slow pace. The doors shut and the small room began to ascend through the building. It was time to win back his fame. __________ The connived He pulled his legs through a pair of tattered jeans and sat back down on the bed. His hands burrowed into the pockets and his eyes shut as he thought about what he would do without Daniel. From the moment he had taken him under his wing, Jay had found something he disliked. Whether it was personality, style, or his lack of care for Jay's psyche he always had a way of bringing out the man people knew as Jay Terror. Without the mocking and drive Franco offered, he was only Jamison Talley: "The Great" Dave Talley's son. That man wasn't meant to be a wrestler. Or was he? All at once, he could remember the night before. Phil Atken had become another number in Jay's record book. What neither man knew at the time, though, was that Phil Atken would become the first man to fall victim to a new Jay Terror. The free Jay Terror. He made his way through the first round of the Chris Williams memorial tournament on his own and there would be no one stopping him from doing the same in the second round. That was when he decided that Daniel Franco would not make his way back into his life. The knock on the door came exactly five minutes after Jay had hung up the phone. Daniel always was one to keep time. With a deep breath and a heavy groan he stood from the bed and made his way over to the door. The knock resonated again and Jay looked through the peephole. He watched Daniel's hand sink in for a third knock but before it could reach he answered, 'Hold on.' The door came loose easily and after freeing it from the locking mechanism he was face to face with the most devilish man he had ever met. 'Can I come in?' Franco's voice was shaky and seemed fragile, even, to the ear. Living with him for as long as he had, Jay knew that he would have to keep strong if he wanted to finish things quick. 'Yeah.' Terror stepped back and found a seat on his bed once again. Daniel, on the other hand, took the liberty to look around and get a feel for the walls around him. 'Nice place you have.' 'You don't need to butter me up Danny, god knows I'm not trying to keep you here.' Franco looked hurt by the remark. In all honestly he figured Jay had some intentions of listening. He looked at his grim stare though and found another realm of thought. 'Right. Jay there's a lot I could say to you right now but most of it, I'm sure, you wouldn't bother digesting. I just wanted to come in and reconcile our differences before they were too far from saving.' 'Reconcile our differences? You do understand that we wouldn't have differences if it wasn't for you, right?' 'Not entirely.' He paused. 'You're not open to much of anything I say.' Jay turned around and showed Franco his bear back. He felt around and found a dark bruise which had become raised in the center. 'Do you really wonder why?' Franco placed his hand in his hairline. 'Jay...you were acting mad. Heather had said you were calling her Daniel.' He turned back and shook his head keenly. 'Who's Heather to say anything? As far as I'm concerned, the both of you created the lie when you first met and were simply waiting for the right time to strike.' 'It's not like that.' He lied in a way the accused would to avoid jail time. It was how Franco worked. What he knew about Heather and how he knew her was a complete secret to Jay. 'Heather was yours. She liked you and some guy like me wasn't going to hook her in to a fantastical campaign in order to take you down.' 'And that's why I hate you.' He had a questioning look on his face. 'You've said some crazy shit before but this is ridiculous. You want to take me down?' He let the idea sink in with Daniel a moment before continuing. 'I'm the reason for you making it in this business. I do the fighting, I take the punishment, I do it all Daniel. Why would you destroy your only ladder to success?' 'Destroy?' He looked dumbfounded by the word. 'Jay, I could crush you in an instant if I truly had that in mind.' 'Go for it.' 'There's no use.' 'Oh come on.' 'What you fail to realize is that talent like you needs to shine.' It was awkward to hear at first. For four years he had never heard any sort of praise come from Franco's lips. On limited occasions he had received encouragement but he had never been referred to as "talent". 'Then why have you been scheming?' 'Scheming? I don't know what you mean.' Again, he lied almost too well. 'You think I'm blind? I've seen you and Heather in the park. I've listened through the walls when you two thought you were alone. So please, tell me Daniel, what's the punch line you have been planning?' Like a failed bluff, Daniel had lost the hand. From his corner he had two choices. On one side he could come clean with his originally intended plan. Opposite from that he could create another lie and find his way out of the situation. 'Look,' he started, 'there is no plan. All I wanted was for you to excel higher than you have. I wanted you to win more than the Television title. I wanted you to surpass the national heroes in the Chris Williams tournament and become an icon. I swear to you that all I wanted was for you to become immortal.' 'No man is above mortality.' Daniel could feel the shift. It was subtle at first but slowly the cards were beginning to fall in his favor again. 'Although true in life it has no meaning in wrestling. Jay, you're a star. I know I haven't sold you on it before but I couldn't risk you becoming too cocky.' 'That's the most ridiculous shit I've ever heard.' 'Is it? Tell me this then, if I would have told you that you were the best wrestler alive, would you be the GCW television champion today? Would you have fought as hard as you did if you knew you were good enough to slack off?' Daniel could feel it more and more with every word. Jay's face began to fall into an agreeing sort of look as if he were finally listening. 'Would you?' He placed his face in his hands and groaned slightly. 'You tranquilized me with a needle. You fed a woman facts so I'd trust her and be with her. How can I trust anything you're saying?' Franco was persistent though. 'Would you? Honestly.' 'No.' He slumped back and laid down across the bed in a defeated manner. He had given in to the man he said he would overcome due to a few rhetorical phrases. Honestly, he felt sick with himself. 'I'll expect you back in Philadelphia then for training. We have work to do if we're going to reach immortality my friend.' Daniel began to walk away with his million dollar grin spread across his face. As he did, Jay sat back up and looked at his manager. 'Get me a damn cigarette.' 'You can't keep smoking them if you want to get to the top.' He spoke with his eyes and Daniel understood. He found the box of Marlboros on Jay's bag by the door and tossed him the box with his Zippo. 'I'll give you this match with Oni to show us where you stand on your own. After that, be ready to listen again.' 'Get out already.' Jay simply wasn't happy with himself. __________ The Conniver Daniel walked down the hall nodding as he stepped. If anyone felt immortal it was him. Pressing the button to the elevator he knew that once again he had defeated Jay Terror. The victory, although tiresome, was a victory nonetheless and would serve as his greatest accomplishment. He had finally realized the true power he had over his pupil. Franco stepped into the elevator the same way he had stepped out of it and found a resting spot on the corner handrails. Unlike before, no one came to the doors rushing to get in. For this reason he was in no hurry to shut them. When they did close he reached out for the panel and pressed the square for the ground floor. Immediately the room plunged downward. 5. 4. 3. It stopped. Just as he was getting ready to exit the hotel and return home the elevator made an abrupt stop. He watched the doors slide open and on the other side a woman appeared. Her face, shielded behind a pair of golden sunglasses, showed a certain happiness in its features and made Daniel cringe. She stepped in and walked straight for him. 'I thought I told you to wait in the lobby.' She placed her body against his and nuzzled close to Franco's ear. 'I felt like exploring.' 'Did you now?' She moved in to kiss him but Daniel wanted nothing of the sort. In one fluent motion he grabbed an object from his beltline and jabbed it into her sternum. 'That's not too smart now is it?' She slumped into his arms and opened her mouth as if she were ready to shriek. Franco held her out though and in a second swing stabbed the object through her heart. She was dead. 'He was on to us dear. I hope you can understand.' The elevator came to a stop on the main floor and Daniel grabbed her body. He propped her against the wall and stepped into the lobby. Luckily, no one was in the area of the elevators. He dropped the object into his pocket again and found his cell phone in the other. Speed dial number three activated and in a second, a voice kicked in through the line. Daniel answered, 'It's Franco. I need a little favor.' __________ Rule number two for mortals: When faced with no choice, begin damage control. __End
