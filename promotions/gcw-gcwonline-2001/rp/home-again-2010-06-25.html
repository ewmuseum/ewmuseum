Home Again...

25 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/25/10 Los Angelas, CA. [Sitting in the seat of the taxi I look out of the window as the sun beams down past a few clouds to light up some of the paleness resting on my face. The envelope I'm holding in my hand no longer holds any cash, since that's how this taxi is being paid, all that remains is a key. Looking into the rear view mirror I can see the driver is a little nervous, although I would be too if I was driving someone that looked the way I do. As the drive continues I toss around images of the last Worldwide event in my head as I finally ended such a one-sided feud with Bishop Steele, and after two wins it would seem I was back on my game.] [Finally the taxi comes to a stop and I nod to the driver as I open the door and step out to some fresh air. Quickly the driver speeds off as my eyes shift forward to the home I haven't entered in so many years. Last time I was here Ron was still alive and I was a man feared by so many people, since then I have been met with the death of my brother and placed at the bottom of the card. Looking at my home, you wouldn't think five years have passed, because the upkeep has been amazing. Walking forward I extend my hand towards the doorknob, but before I can it opens and I'm greeted by a hug from an older man.] ?: DONNY! You've finally found your way home after so long! Man I've been keeping tabs on you from the television and you have changed so much. Donny: I'm sorry... I can't seem to recall exactly who you are. I just got out of the hospital a few weeks ago and my head is all boggled still. ?: I'm Larry! Come on you have to remember me kiddo, I showed you how to wrestle. Back when you made it big in the PTC I was your go-to guy that helped you win the PTC Global Title and DWO Heavyweight Title! We spent hours at my gym, training for your match to make sure you were at the top of your game. Come on, come inside, we can talk more there. [I follow this Larry person inside and he leads me to the main quarters where all the workers welcome me back home. My home was truly a marvelous place as I look around at all of the Championships hanging on the walls, and all the pictures from the matches I've had over the years. Shaking my head for a moment I force myself to stop trying to remember everything I see here so that I can focus on Larry. Taking a seat on a nice black leather chair I prop my feet up on the glass table as Larry takes a seat on the matching couch across from me] Donny: Excuse me Larry, but how exactly am I still paying for this place and the people keeping it up? I mean, I don't exactly make the big bills fighting guys who barely have a job with the company. Larry: Well a few years ago when you hit it big Callisto Bane didn't have much of a choice except to sign you to a big contract. But I can't say I haven't been worried about you losing it all, I mean you disappeared from the face of the Earth for five years and then I hear from some pal's that you're on television working this GCW company. Donny: Yeah... GCW was kind enough to let me work for them, but it hasn't been easy there. Larry: No joke kiddo, I been watching. You lost a few steps over the years, back in the day you wouldn't lose a match like you did to a tramp like Tempest, or to that Irish pussy SurReal. The Donny Diamond I knew would have no trouble making those two fall on their heads for an easy three! Donny: It's not that easy Larry... It's just not the same anymore. [Like a scene from a movie Larry flies up off the couch and slaps me hard across the face. I hold my jaw looking at him as he stands over me, anger flowing in his eyes.] Larry: Not that easy? Not the same anymore? What the hell is wrong with you boy?! You've been hanging around those damn emo kids for to long, because you would never speak nonsense like this! Tomorrow morning you are coming with me over to the gym and we're going to get your ass back into gear you hear me! Donny: ...Okay. Larry: Stephanie show this boy to his room before I start kicking his ass all the way to it. [Larry backs off slowly, the anger still very noticeable as he motions for Stephanie. I see her coming towards me, and I assume she's one of the maid's here, based on how she's dressed. After getting up from my seat I follow her, all the while I can still hear Larry openly bitching to himself over the remarks I made.] The next day... [It's not such a beautiful day today, as the sun is mostly hidden behind the clouds and thunder can be heard roaring in the distance. Looking above the door before me the wording reads “Larry's Total Package”, I laugh inside at such a silly name for a gym. Opening the door I walk in where Larry is just inside waiting for me, with another guy standing beside him.] Larry: Donny, this here is Ben and while we been waiting for your lazy ass to arrive he has been going over some of your recent matches. Ben, allow me to introduce to you the once great Donny Diamond. The two of us are going to get you back in shape and back on your game. Ben: Nice to meet you in person Big D. Donny: Uh yeah, same here. Listen Larry, I been thinking... I over heard some people in the locker rooms talking about my name Donny Diamond, and how it's a joke, because no one can be scared of a punk dressed in black named “Donny Diamond”. Maybe we need a name change? Larry: Look kid, you dress like you want and act like you want, because neither of us here care about your gimmick. And changing your name isn't going to make people start taking you serious. Your name can be Donny Diamond, or Kimbusa for all I care, but it won't mean a damn thing until you start giving people a reason to fear you! Now get your ass in that ring! [No matter what I seemed to say to Larry all it did was make him even angrier. Walking past him I roll into the ring where Ben is waiting for me, but before I can get back onto my feet he drops a leg across the back of my neck. He leans down in front of me with a smirk on his face, as I hold my neck in pain.] Ben: Your first mistake is making sure your opponent doesn't have an opening to attack you no matter where you are in the match. Like Larry said I been watching your tapes and I know all about you liking to work the submissions on your opponent. I know all about how you like to wait for your opponent to make a mistake so you can capitalize. But how are you going to do that when I just leg dropped you before the match can even start? [I push up on all four trying to regain myself and Ben knocks me right back down with some stiff stomps, grabbing me by the arm, and locking his arms around my jaw for a cross-face. I pound my fists down onto the mat in pain.] Larry: How are you going to win your match this week when Ben has already basically beaten you with that cross-face? You think T.A. Giles is going to just hand you an opening so that the match can go as you planned it? You think Lori Equinox is just going to wait for you to get your knees up before using his Poison Frog? No! Ben: And don't even think I'm going to let this hold go if you reach the ropes. You need to learn to fight past the pain, you need to remember that sometimes the only opening you will find in an offensive is the one you make! Now come on and escape this hold, because I'm not seeing a former champion, all I'm seeing is a wanna-be wrestler about to tap to a cross-face. [Everything that the two of them say is the truth, but the pain is so intense that tapping out just seems like an easier answer. But I know neither of them will let me go even if I do tap, so I do as I'm told and try fighting past the pain. Soon I'm on my elbow and knees, and I quickly roll over to push Ben into a pin where he releases the hold.] Ben: You have to ready for anything when you are about to step inside the ring. Giles may not know what to expect from you during the match, but you need to remember that Equinox has already faced you once. He knows how you work and it's going to be your job to make sure neither of them knows what to expect when its time for the match. Larry: Wrestling is the game of human chess and its your job to make sure you are always one step ahead of your opponent! And you have to remember that even though you think the match is won, it's never over until that final count hits the canvas. [My eyes are focused on Larry as I climb onto my feet holding the back of my neck. I quickly look over to the opposite side where I see Ben running towards me, I duck his clothesline attempt and lift him up onto my shoulders for a fireman's carry. This is the setup for my finishing move the Sinner's End and I know Ben has no chance for escape. I twirl Ben forward to catch him in tombstone position, and unexpectedly he pulls me down into a roll up.] Larry: And T.A. Giles has just won the match because you got caught being cocky! I just got done telling you that a match is never over until you hear that bell ring. [After Ben releases the roll-up I stand on my knees in shock at what just happened. Over the years I became so good, but as time passed while I was away it seems I have forgotten more about wrestling than I was able to remember.] Larry: Look kid, I know you want to be champion again. Hell, if you didn't I'd be asking you what the Hell is wrong with you. Being a champion is the reason we're all in this business, but it takes time. You can't just wake up one morning and go “Hey I want to be World Champion!” It takes work to get there, and your first step back to glory wasn't defeating Bishop Steele. No, your first step will be defeating Giles and Equinox! Donny: And what about Shaman? Ben: Unless you have a match against him, which you don't, you need to focus on your opponents only. Sure Shaman is going to pose a threat for you in GCW, but until the two of you face, don't give him another thought. Larry: Now stop with the jibber-jabber and get back to work. Start learning the moves in-and-out again so that you will be able to win this match. We are going to be sending an entirely different Donny Diamond back to GCW! [Just as those words come out of Larry's mouth, Ben hooks me around the waist and slings me back down to the mat with a German suplex. Dazed for a moment I stare up at the ceiling as I know I must get myself back to the top of my game in order to defeat my opponents at the upcoming Worldwide event, and eventually Shaman as well.] - If you think about it, this is really a life lesson. Because no matter what we want out of life, we have to work hard for it and eventually reach the top of the “ladder”. Sure some times we may get knocked a few steps back, but you take the knock-back in stride and move back forward with double the effort. The only person that can stop you from achieving your goal is you.
