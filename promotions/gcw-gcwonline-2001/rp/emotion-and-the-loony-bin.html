Emotion and the Loony Bin

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: WW 111 Full-bodied crimson bullets drip down onto the black and white tiled floor of the shower. The crimson trails through the the grout with the push of the water behind it into the stainless steel drain. The source, dangling two and a half feet above remains still with veinlike streams of water rushing over the arm. Zacharia stands, propped up in position by his other arm straight out in front of him against the wall, his head held low. His knuckles look as though they were torn away, leaving gaping holes where skin should be. The blood starts to slow, but then Zacharia makes a fist, pushing out another stream. He slaps his free hand against the tile on the wall and then runs it through his hair. His eyes remain closed, but his face twitches in either pain or anger, both of which have followed him through Bagwell's gym since the start of his training. "Zach, I'm closin' up!" Zacharia opens his eyes and slowly turns towards the voice. His eye is also grotesquely sliced open and swollen, along with a blown up nose. He closes his eyes and straightens up in the raining city water, on Bagwell's expense. He takes a deep breath as the voice raises in volume. "Zach, I'm fuckin' closing! Get dried and get the fuck out!" Zacharia turns off the water and creeps over to the bench where a worn towel rest. He leans over for it and winces in pain, holding his ribs. Pulling on his boxers and shorts, his stomach tightens, revealing every fiber in his sides, his ribs conforming to his abs and ribs. He holds his side as he pivots on his toes and sits down with a slight struggle. He leans forward very slowly and raises his hands to cover his face... ********** Taylor dances on his toes across the ring, ducking under a rope that is across the center of the ring, punching an imaginary opponent as he comes up. The young Taylor is sweating, his chest and back glistening in the overhead lights, but he is barely fatigued. After a few rounds, he drops to the mat and begins a series of varied pushups. He just finishes up with raised push ups against the ropes when Chris Bagwell steps into the ring. "At least you're getting stronger..." Bagwell keeps a stern face staring at the rising Taylor, "...but your style and attitude is still shit!" "Well, I'm trying, you have to give me some credit, Chris." "No I don't, you know why? Because those scumbags out there won't respect your hard work and that you're trying. You want to follow this crazy ass religion, and you need to fulfill your destiny." Taylor starts to breath heavy, attempting to calm himself, but Bagwell continues on his rant. "If you want to train, I want to see that you give a shit, not that you are going through the motions. Any monkey off the fuckin' street can do a push up or duck a rope. A REAL man can land the punch and..." Just before Bagwell can finish, Taylor lunges at his trainer, but Bagwell was ready for it and swats him away towards the mat. Taylor jumps up and goes to attack once more, but he stops, eyeing down Bagwell. "You know that talking about the Lord..." "Zach, I know. I did it on purpose. You need to learn how to control your emotions. You are far too quick to punch. You need to control..." "How am I supposed to control that. All I know is my religion, and I have the utmost respect for Father Emanuel and what he crafted..." "Stop! All you talk about is the religion. I know, I know you don't need to talk about it everyday. I know what your world is like, and God help me if I ever get into that shit. Look, you need to be able to take whatever your opponent throws at you, even if it is only words...especially if its only words. I don't think there is anything written in your Book that awards those that fight fists with words." Taylor looks to the ground, trying to calm himself. A man in a brown hoodie steps out from behind Bagwell and stares at Taylor. He quickly closes his eyes and shakes his head only to open them to see a confused Bagwell standing in front of him. "Look kid, I know that you have some issues that you're trying to work out, but there's no reason you can't do this shit. You got it in you." Bagwell reaches into the corner next to him and pulls out two guerrilla pads and puts them on. "I want you to focus." Zacharia takes a deep breath and raises his loosely taped hands to his stance. Bagwell walks over to him, hands at his sides and kicks Zacharia's left foot to the inside and then adjusts his body to turn a little more straight. Satisfied, he takes two steps back and raises his guerrilla pads. Bagwell moves around his hands, signaling for different types of punches with his movements. Zacharia follows strictly, matching everything perfectly, until Bagwell changes up the routine. He starts to throw sweeping punches at Zacharia to evade, which he is able to do, but just barely. After about a minute's time, Bagwell plants his feet as if he were in his own stance, and throws harder, faster punches at Taylor, some landing, knocking Taylor off beat. It isn't until Bagwell takes a step forward, causing Taylor to fall back, does he start to become frustrated. "Move you're damn feet!" Complying, Taylor nervously steps into an incoming punch, knocking him to the ground. Bagwell stands over him, shaking his head. "Get up," Bagwell says. Taylor punches the ground before getting to his feet. Another punch is thrown, and he evades it by taking a step backwards, but now, his back is up against the rope cutting the ring in half. Taylor, looking petrified, bobs and weaves out of the way of the incoming punches, landing a couple of his own on the pads in between evasive maneuvers. Bagwell throws a fake left and then comes in with a right forearm to his face, which connects right above his eye, instantly turning the surrounding area read. "What are you doing, Chris, I thought we were sparing." Taylor looks panicked as his back is semi-arched over the rope. "You don't like someone breaking the rules, yet you do it all the time. Focus! Picture you are in a fight out there and do what you can to evade. Remember, keep focused, don't let your emotions get the best of you!" Bagwell takes a step back but then quick steps forward and throws a jab with his pads, but Taylor evades. Taylor lands a couple jabs, but then doesn't expect a sweeping cross from Bagwell's pad. The flat part nails him hard, forcing him to fall to the ground. Taylor yells and gets up. Bagwell hits him again, harder, sending him to the ground again, but this time, Taylor sweep kicks Bagwell, but misses. As a repercussion, Bagwell thrusts his knee upwards, hitting Taylor once more in the eye, but this time, a stream of hot blood wisps through the air. That is all he can take before his limit is reached, he loses it. Zacharia charges Bagwell, lifting him up and slamming him into the corner. "TAYLOR!" He punches wildly at Bagwell, but the pads keep him safe, until a wild haymaker smacks him in the jaw, stunning him for a couple seconds. "TAYLOR STOP!" Taylor ignores the commands of his trainer and continues the rage. Bagwell throws down his pads and grabs onto his shoulders. He begins to knee Taylor in the side repeatedly until he recedes enough for Bagwell to throw a strong hook to the same vulnerable spot. Upon impact, Taylor's ribs snap. "NO MORE! CONTROL YOURSELF!" Bagwell pleads with him, but Taylor is in a raging stupor, and comes in for another attack. Bagwell throws a retreating jab square into Taylor's nose, two droplets of blood smack the floor from his right nostril. Taylor yells and goes for another takedown, but Bagwell finishes it with a very strong downward haymaker to Taylor's busted eye. The impact sends blood flying, and he hits the mat with amazing force. He is mildly confused at the moment as everything is spinning and white hot pain rushes through his eye. Bagwell takes a deep breath, tends to his hand, and gets out of the ring. "I told you, Zach, you need to control your emotions. Look at you! Just fuckin' look at you! You made me bust my hand too, fuckin' kid. Get fuckin' washed up and get the fuck out of my gym. If you don't want to listen, you can go out there on your own. Only, the God damned scumbags you're up against won't stop because you smack the floor. I was tryin' to help, but you fucked that up. Get out of my fuckin' gym!" Bagwell walks to the furthest door and slams it upon his descent. Taylor rolls over and uses one hand to cover his profusely bleeding eye and the other to hold his ribs. Later, Bagwell will find a blood trail leading into the showers, but right now, Taylor is trying to collect his thoughts, but the pain is overbearing. Eventually, Taylor trudges to the showers, after taking in everything that just happen. He knows that Bagwell doesn't mean forever, well, hopes at least. He lost it, and couldn't find himself to stop, once again. Out of frustration, he punches the tiled wall of the locker room, only splitting his knuckles wider. Now cleaned, cuts starting to clot, he sits on the bench, hands in his face. "Taylor, I said get the fuck out! I'm gonna drag you out by your fuckin' hair if you aren't out of here in five minutes." Footsteps start loud and then fade away. Taylor musters the strength to stand, and get dressed. After a struggle of getting all his clothes on, he walks out the back door before Bagwell can even come back in his five minute absence. It was very late for him to be walking home, and it was for certain that he wasn't going to get a ride home from Bagwell. He set himself walking, hoping a bus comes by soon, but he didn't want to stand in the same still. He makes his way through broad street and then turns onto grove, where hundreds of bus stops are, and every scumbag in Newark hangs out at night. He ignores the stares from the white eyes lingering in the allies and on the porches. He just tightens his grip on the bag and continues on his way down. Before a bus comes down the street, a group of black men walk up to the young Taylor, and start harassing him, but Taylor does not stop walking, nor does he make eye contact. His heart begins to beat in rapid succession, as if it were the bass line for a bad rap spanish rap song. A bigger group of guys surround him, causing him to stop. "Where ya goin', white meat? Ya need somethin'? I got more shit den the motha fuckin' CVS Pharmacy down the street. You need it, I gots it." Taylor slowly looks up into the speakers eyes, piercing him through. "You got fucked up, white boy. I got some shit dat with knock ya on your motha fuckin' ass quicker then you can say 'I fucked dat nigga up' but shit, I don't know if ya can handle that!" The guys all start to laugh, now eyeing the bag over his shoulder. Taylor tries to push his way through the crowd, but there is no luck, so he drops the bag to the ground. "Move." Everyone starts to laugh, but Taylor stares at the speaker with a fierce gaze, much like the one he gave Bagwell. His blood starts to to go into every muscle in his body, and adrenaline speeds his heart up to the point that the clots in his hands and eye can no longer hold, and blood pushes through. "I think it is in your best interest to move, sir..." Everyone laughs again, and the speaker again sizes up Taylor. He isn't too much bigger than Taylor, but he is certainly not in as good of shape as him. He isn't wearing a shirt, but his pants are near his mid thigh and his calven clines are showing to his stomach. He starts to wave his arms and begins to pump blood through his arms by way of making a fist. The crowd responds by making an open circle around the two men. Taylor runs a hand over his ribs, and he winces even from the slightest touch. Taylor wouldn't be able to make it out of this fight on his own, not even if he was in the shape to fight. He would need a divine miracle, or.... "What cha doin' here boys?" A massive black guy comes into the crowd, holding a baton out in his hand, and his radio in the other. The police officer continues once the crowd starts to scatter. "What you doin' T? Pickin on the local white kids? You're better than that! Shit, I would expect you to be big time by now! Slingin' shit left and right to all the big playas, but shit, you got nothin', so why don't you run along to mom and dad, hm?" T shakes his head and steps back holding his thumb to his neck as the cop laughs. The police officer turns his attention to Taylor. "What the hell you doin' here kid? You lost or trying to get... What the fuck happen to your face?" The police officer tightens his grip on the baton, "Did these mother fuckers do this?" "No, sir. I was training, and thing got out of hand. I'm just trying to go back to the church, but I didn't want to stand and wait for a bus." "Don't blame ya, kid but you know where you are? This is no place for a white kid to be wondering around. And why the hell are you going to a church this late at night?" "Sir, no disrespect, but God does everything for a reason, and I'm not so sure that you would understand..." "God? Church? You fuckin' crazy white boy, I will give you that, but I need to take you home to your parents before..." "I don't have any sir, but I need to get back to the church. I live there, with Father Emanuel. He will be expecting me in, if you would please. I want to get back." The cop eyes him up and then walks over to the police car. He opens the door and allows Taylor to sit down. The cop gets into the front seat and pulls onto the street, flying. After a moment, he makes a turn off of grove. "Wait! The church is in Clifton! We are going the wrong way, officer! Please!" "I'm bringin you back to the station first. We need to get a couple things in order. Then, I will bring you home." "But, that is my home, sir. I live there! My parents died years ago, and Father Emanuel took me in...It was God's will, sir!" The police officer stares at Taylor in the rearview mirror and shakes his head. "See, its that God shit that worries me. We will take care of you at the station, promise!" **********
