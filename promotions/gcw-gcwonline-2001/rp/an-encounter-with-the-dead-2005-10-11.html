An Encounter With The Dead

11 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11th 2005 The deed has been done, the prophecy fulfilled, the first step in creating pandemonium has rose. The story we last left off with involved cruelty and several near death experiences. Now though, I must find out about the woman who brought me towards the light, she brought me the names of men who killed like Michael. The woman was Georgia Mason. I woke in a cold sweat as the recurring nightmare continued again. The same sort of nightmare that when you wake up you believe something has truly happened. The same sort of nightmare that makes you believe that you have fallen into the world of the dead. The same sort of nightmare that makes you fear for your life as you wearily look up into the darkness around you. You get the idea, its something that strikes you with fear and wonder. The same darkness trapped me in; I stared into the darkness for a moment creeping from my comforter that was pulled up around my face. I turned to the clock, rubbed my eyes so that I could see it clearly and stared at it blankly. 4:23 A.M. I rolled out of the bed and shuddered softly from the cold that engulfed the room. “Damn, it’s so cold.” I whispered to myself softly as I walked into the bathroom. The bathroom mirror shone back the image of me, as I stared at myself in it. The bathroom had beige tile flooring with marble counters and a large shower and bath. I ran the water from the facet and began rubbing my face with it. “That dream, it’s horrible.” I whispered to myself again. I pulled down my hands from my face and looked into the mirror with horror. Georgia Mason stood there as plain as day behind me. She stared into the mirror, into my eyes with a grimace on her face as though she were rearing to kill. “The’Book”.Will’Find?.You Aiden.” She said softly, but without a moment passing she disappeared as I blinked. “What the hell?” I said to myself as I was distraught by what had just happened. Then there are also those dreams, within dreams where you believe that you are in reality but you are actually dreaming. Truly this time, I woke up in a cold sweat, searching for Georgia Mason around the large Jackson Estate. Could this have been some sort of subliminal message within my dream? I looked over at the clock just as I had in my dream and it read. 4:23 A.M. I repeated what I did in the dream but no Georgia Mason showed. “If you go there, she will come.” A strange voice uttered to me as though whispering in my ear. I, typically thought of the saying “If You Build It, They Will Come”, a quote that remains in my mind from the movie Field of Dreams. The words of a higher being had overcome my mind as I searched the bathroom for a person. My mind went into a spell of chaos as I believed that someone was there with me. The strange whisper followed me throughout the day but I wanted the answers. Where was I supposed to go? Who was talking to me? Why was I supposed to go there? Who is she, was it Georgia Mason that will come? Ring Ring Ring The ringing of the phone began just as I walked by it; I looked at it for a moment, recognized the number and picked it up quickly. Although I recognized the number, I wasn’t sure why exactly the call was being made to my house. “Hello” “Hey Aiden, I need you to come into town tonight.” The distinguished voice said through the phone “Alright Jess, I’ll be there. Is there some sort of problem?” I said to him. “Nah, I just wanted to see you. It has been a while since we’ve met considering you’ve been gone almost every week wrestling.” He said to me softly. “Alright, I’ll be there. Hooters in Union Station sound good?” I replied. “Yup, see you later bro.” He said to me as I heard a click of the phone. [fade] The music pumped through the car as I gazed at the skyline of St.Louis. I was growing weary of actually walking through St.Louis on this night but it was a necessity that I see my brother. He saved me and this is the least that I owe him. Nearly 5 minutes later, I arrived at the destination. Union Station was a large mall-type building with several stores and restaurants inside. The night was young but the stars peered out through the clouds of the sky, the moon was engulfed by a large cloud. I walked through the dark parking lot looking around me, passing several people with my head down; I finally looked up only to see Georgia Mason standing there. I instantly ran towards her yelling “Georgia.”, as I gained a closer look at the woman I realized she was not Georgia, the woman looked at me strangely then walked away quickly. After the mix-up, I walked into the building and looked around at all of the people. To the right was a large fudge shop in which the workers had currently been singing and to the left of me was a large elevator, exactly where I was supposed to be going. I walked up to the elevator, walked in and pressed the button arriving at the top at Hooters. “Aiden!” the voice of my brother called to me as I exited the elevator. My brother sat there alone with a beer in hand waving at me. His face was glowing with a smile across it; he looked like a small child who had just received exactly what they wanted for Christmas. I walked over to the table and sat down. “Aiden, it’s been too long nearly a month now. I’ve watched every one of your matches. You did great in the tournament and destroyed that kid Rei Tiu.” He said to me in excitement. “Rei is a tough man, and it was quite a match.” I replied. “Aiden, did you see who you are facing this week?” He said to me. “Nope, didn’t catch the commercial yet.” I said back to him calmly. A small girl walked up beside us, dressed in the Hooter’s attire, she interrupted the conversation by just walking up. We both looked at her for a moment until she spoke to us. “What can I get ya” boys?” She said with a sweet serenity in her voice. “I’d like a beer, and we’d like a large order of wings.” I said back to her. “How would you like those wings?” She said back. “Mild should be fine. Thank you much beautiful.” I said. The young girl smiled softly then disappeared quickly. I looked up at my brother who was looking at me grinning. “A sweet talker, are you?” He said to me as he teased. I laughed softly as he said this. “Anyways, who am I facing?” I said to him attempting to change the subject. “Seymour Almasy.” He replied. For a moment I was overcome with a state of shock. Seymour Almasy, the former PTC Global Champion, the man who has made his name so present in GCW. The name that I never thought I would hear next to mine. “Se..Seymour?” I replied. “Yeah, for the cruiserweight title.” He said in repliance. I sat there for a moment sulking in the realization that I was facing one of the biggest athletes to hit any federation. Seymour’s history was a vast one; he has battled in many feds holding various World Titles. My brother began to laugh at me as I gazed into my glass. “He’s gonna kick your ass isn’t he?” He said chuckling. “Did Rei Tiu kick my ass?” I said to him. After uttering these words to him, the smile from his face drew to a look of uncertainty. My brother realized how serious I was about this match and that the result could not be a loss, and if it was a loss, it wouldn’t be a loss that came easily to the man known as Seymour Almasy. “Anyways, a lot of the wrestling websites say that you’re some kind of hype that is going to die out after a month or so.” He said to me. “You know they’re never right, a month of my career in GCW is already coming up quickly and there are bigger plans for the future fo...” I said to him. Instantly, I was stunned. Everything began to move in slow motion; finally a tunnel of darkness engulfed me as I feel into a dream. The same dream or nightmare you could call it that always was in my head. “If you go there, she will come.” The voice spoke to me once more. This event was something different from the usual dream that had occurred before. The same darkness trapped me in; I stared into the darkness for a moment creeping from my comforter that was pulled up around my face. I turned to the clock, rubbed my eyes so that I could see it clearly and stared at it blankly. 4:23 A.M. I rolled out of the bed and shuddered softly from the cold that engulfed the room. “Damn, it’s so cold.” I whispered to myself softly as I walked into the bathroom. The bathroom mirror shone back the image of me, as I stared at myself in it. The bathroom had beige tile flooring with marble counters and a large shower and bath. I ran the water from the facet and began rubbing my face with it. “That dream, it’s horrible.” I whispered to myself again. I pulled down my hands from my face and looked into the mirror with horror. Georgia Mason stood there as plain as day behind me. She stared into the mirror, into my eyes with a grimace on her face as though she were rearing to kill. “The’Book”.Will’Find?.You Aiden.” She said softly, but without a moment passing she disappeared as I blinked. “What the hell?” I said to myself as I was distraught by what had just happened. This dream though, it was different. I walked from the bathroom, no I didn’t walk I nearly ran to the desk that sits in my house. I opened the drawer to find a bloody picture of Georgia Mason, under it sat a large wooden box. “Aiden, are you okay!”” My brother said as the scene around me poured back in, there were several people standing above me. “I’m fine but what happened?” I said slowly. “You blacked out or something, you took a hard hit on the floor. Are you sure you are okay?” He said to me, I could tell he was worried at this point. “Can you take me home?” I said to him quickly remembering the vision, or dream that I had just had. [fade] Arriving at my house, my brother assured me that he would stay for the night to make sure that I was okay. The virtue of friendship that was past mentioned in the story before was quite apparent to me; my brother truly was my best friend in the rough times and the good times. I knew that deep down inside my brother missed Shane and our parents more than I did. As I entered the large wooden door, I ran to the desk and opened the drawer, finding a normal picture of Georgia sitting there, and beneath it the box just as it had been in the dream. I pulled the box out and put it on top of the coffee table. “Jess, have you seen this before?” I said to him softly as I examined the box carefully. “No, Why” What is it?” He said to me as his focus snapped to the box sitting on the table. “Okay, there’s a pretty big story behind this. You know how I just blacked out at Hooters” Well, I had a vision about this desk, and the box that was in it but I never found out exactly what was in it. But I think I have an idea of what it is. A voice has been telling me If you go there, She will come. I guess the place that I had to go to was Hooters because she came to me in the dream once more but she sort of gave me the answers this time. I kept having this recurring dream of her coming to me though and telling me that I would find the book. The diary that we have been searching for, Jess, I have come to believe that the diary is the contents of the box.” I said to him. “Wow, that’s quite a story. Well open it.” He said to me. “Do you believe me?” I said to him. “Sure, I do. What reason would you have to lie to me?” He said in reply “None, but this thing is locked can you go grab a hammer from the garage.” I said to him. He returned with a hammer in hand and a grin on his face. He knew the contents of this diary contained exactly everything that X needed in order to put many criminals away. We both had the same insurmountable joy inside knowing that we would find the others who murdered our parents. I smashed off the lock with excitement, pushing up the lid of the small chest. The contents of the box were exactly as I had guessed; this torn and tattered book was somewhat like gold to me. I opened the book instantly, searching for the page that contained the names of the many men involved in the killings of the Jackson parents. The murder that left 4 boys parentless, to be raised by their butler who would pass away nearly 3 years later. The boys then would be raised by their eldest brother. Entry 1 19 October 1970 Dear Diary, I hid from my father as he came home in a tyrant of rage, he had murdered once again I could tell. I heard him speak of it to my mother and I listened through the door. He caught me at the door and hit me hard with a Wooden stick. I bled a lot; I wish I could get away from him. He got us more money to eat but he killed another innocent person it makes me sad inside sometimes. I hope he never finds this or he’ll murder me I know it because if someone else finds it beside him then they’ll know the bad things that my daddy has done to all those people. Sincerely, Michael Mason I flipped through the pages skimming each of the entries carefully noticing all of the bolded and underlined letters. I wasn’t sure exactly what this mean but many of the pages were torn and tarnished several missing which wasn’t a good thing for me and Jesse. “Do you see something in the writing Aiden?” He said to me skimming over it next to me. “No, it’s baffling with all these bolded letters and stuff I can’t quite find out what they mean.” I replied to him. “I’m thinking the same thing, but maybe this means something?” He said as he read from a stamp in the back of the book on the hardcover. “A moment passed is a moment wasted, the words are construed, and the words are distorted. The day will arise that a man reads this book; he will witness the accounts of a child, a battered child, a child with his childhood robbed from him with abuse and having to work at such a young age. The words you find will be found from within your heart, because the heart tells everything of a man. The words contorted in the series of sentences will give you the answers.” He read straight from the stamp. “Maybe the words that are messed up mean something to us.” “Possibly.” My brother said in reply. “Listen Aiden, as always you know that I have connections. We are going to send this to a professional on hand writing and other things. He may be able to decode what we are trying to find. I truly think that you need to get some sleep tonight.” He said to me as he got up leaving the house. “Read between the lines.” The ever so familiar voice spoke to me once again. My eyes grew with weight as I lay on the couch staring at the television screen. Eventually falling into the darkness, a swirl of dreams rode through my mind. I was laying there over Seymour having his hand raised in victory. The dream then transferred to Georgia Mason standing there once again, she spoke softly. “God has given you a give young Aiden.” She said to me softly. Waking from the darkness once again, I thought of the now passed away Georgia Mason, my brother Shane Carbon who had passed away, and my parents. I thought of the closure that would soon follow with all of their deaths. When Georgia gave me the answers she would find her closure within. I still had yet to find my closure with my parents or Shane; the time would come soon enough though. All of these deaths were on my to-do list, and they would be checked off one by one. [fade] This week, I will walk into Worldwide with the same glory that Seymour Almasy holds. I will walk into Worldwide a champion, in his own respect he is a champion. Seymour remains undefeated in GCW as do I. Seymour will test my courage, my resilience, my will to be a champion. One man will walk out of Worldwide with a loss in their column and the gold. When the stakes grow higher, the will to win increases, the amount of pain you are willing to endure increases, the yearning for victory is always in your reaches but you have to push further to achieve it. “A man once said it is impossible to win the race unless you venture to run, impossible to win the victory unless you dare to battle. And my friends my shield is on, my weapon is in hand and my armor is on. All you have to do is bring that battle so I can reign victorious.” I leave you not on this day with a quote from the bible, this quote here speaks from my heart and shows the true tests of will that I will endure, the true tests of strength that I will foresee. The test has been handed out and the time has come for me to take the test on, and the test will not beat me in the end.
