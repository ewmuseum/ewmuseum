Freezing ... dying...

23 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 Aug 05 It all seemed almost like a dream now. The rain splattered on top of my head and shoulders, soaking my entire body. A light breeze was constant which had my teeth slightly chattering as well. I would probably wake up with some kind of nasty cold but at this point that was the least of my problems. The only warmth that came was on my side where the blood was seeping out from beneath the bandages and dripping down my ribs. The blood that was on my hands had almost completely rinsed away, but for some reason not all of it would fucking go away. It could have taken me an hour or a day to reach my destination, but it felt like I simply took one step and was there. My mind raced through everything that had been going on in my life, and I still couldn’t figure the answers out. Now if its because I’m freezing, bleeding, or going insane I can’t quite figure out yet either, but I am sure the answer will hit me eventually. The water made my clothes feel as if it were armor resting on me, making me hundreds of pounds heavier. I didn’t care though, my interest was only with learning the truth about what was going on inside of me. I trudge through the mud on the outside of this hospital just doing my best to not fall down and to find her room. Not that seeing her face was going to change THAT much, because that image of her hovering above me, with the heat of that fire digging into me was plastered in my mind. Suddenly that sharp pain ran up and down my side, I knew I left the hospital too early but damn it I couldn’t risk Mary finding me and questioning me about what happened, I was too weak at that moment. FUCK how could she have beaten me? I do NOT get broken down like this, it just does not happen. I fall to a knee and clutch at the bandages again, feeling the blood oozing out but at the same time washing away. I could be dying right here and right now but for some reason that fact seemed trivial and not just because I have looked death in the eye before .. This is unexplainable. I used everything in my body to pull myself from out of the thick grass and mud and engulfed the area. My hand reached upwards and grabbed at the concrete building, trying to get a grip on something, finally finding the window ledge. I kept my hands as steady as possible so that I could find the strength, and slowly but surely I was on my two feet, albeit wobbly. I looked into the window that I stood in and my eyes grew wide, my stomach flipped, and my heart sunk. Even through the rain, the cloak of dark hair that almost completely covered my sight, and the pain that coursed through me, I recognized who I was looking at. I swallowed hard as I tried to focus in on just her ? but it wasn’t just her. That fucking bodyguard of hers was there as well, what was his name? Ariel? Arael? Aries? Ah something like that anyways. I watched as they smiled together, as Mary grabbed his hand and put it to her face , then they talked together. I could not make out what they were saying, not that I cared anyways ? the fact that she touched his hand made me sick to my stomach. Wait ? FUCK NO! I can’t be jealous can I? JEALOUSY? ME? Son of a bitch what is wrong with me? I lowered my gaze and dug my face into my hands. I couldn’t believe what was happening to me. Every time I seem to think things are going right and it is going to be just fine, this MARY comes in and throws a monkey wrench into my whole operation. Back in 3W Roberto was weak and ripe for the picking. Sure he had that Medea broad but she was nothing to him and I knew that I could intervene there. Besides that bitch was crazy and so was that entire clan of people she called a family. Then Mary came back into his life. As our ? did I say our? God Damn It! HIS love for her grew I lost my grip on his soul and he pulled away victoriously. Her love is what stops me from being what I know is possible. Her love is what keeps me grounded. Yet ? No don’t even think it LeStatt! She is going to ruin you! You face Michael Stevens in less than ONE week for that GCW Title. I’ve got to keep it together here, at least until I bury Stevens into the ground. Plus all this time I am worried about Mary, RaZiel could be close to dead by now. I know what I would do if in Steven’s position and if he is even remotely similar to me then RaZiel is going to need every ounce of that pain threshold. Stevens would get his though, maybe not at World Wide but when he steps into that ring with me on Sunday, Annihilation will be putting it lightly. His games will be at an end, and if I have my way ? so will his career. From the corner of my eye I could see shuffling inside of the room so I lifted my head to look. The bodyguard smiled slightly before leaving Mary alone inside of the hospital room. The urge built up inside of me to climb through the window and ? Well a whole number of things could happen. I could go in there and strangle the life from her body, although that wouldn’t be happening. Perhaps I could go in there and find out if she really did write that letter, because if she did not then SOMEONE is out to make my life a living hell. Or maybe, climb through this window, crawl up beside her and fuck her until the sun came up and she couldn’t walk straight anymore. Even after what we went through I know she’d let me inside of her, making her moan with great pleasure, and feeding that lustful craving that she has built up inside, just as I do. Or? Mary’s body shifted and she began staring right out of the window that I now stood at. At first there was little doubt in my mind that she saw me, but she didn’t even look at me. She stared into the dark sky behind me, being just as confused as I was. Her smile that she gave to the bodyguard was no longer on her beautiful face, but it was replaced by sadness and sorrow. Then it happened. I watched as tears formed in her eyes. Something inside of my turned and flipped at this sight. She was hurting inside, it cut her deep. A single tear drop fell from her eye and began to tumble down her cheek. I followed it with my gaze. It almost seemed to move in slow motion. I didn’t want to let that tear drop fall and splatter just like one of these rain drops. I wanted to rush in there and catch it before it hit the ground and let her know that it would be alright. My body remained frozen though. I could only watch as the tear drop fell and then disappeared into just a small puddle on her bed. I watched her now, as tears streamed down her cheeks, each one falling off just like the one before it. I reached up with my right hand and touched the glass, I know this is as close as I can get to touching her for now. I stood there until I saw her eyelids begin to flutter closed, until eventually she was sleeping soundly. It was funny, even though I wasn’t in the room with her, I felt as if we were together, but now that she was asleep ? loneliness kicked in. The pain rushed back, all of it did. The blood continued to drip out, the rain continued to beat down on my head, and my mind continued to race. I turn my back on the window and slowly slithered down the hospital building untiL I was sitting in that cold mix of dirt and rain. I shiver violently and uncontrollably. Things were so unclear and yet even with all these questions left to be answered I wouldn’t care if I died right here. My vision became fuzzy and soon my eyes shut ? If this is my last night I am fine knowing it was “with” her ? Darkness.
