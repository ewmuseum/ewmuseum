Conducting Business!

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/06 Walking through his Casino Shaman seems to be wandering aimlessly as he thinks of several things. Including his match with Peter Vetra. Walking casually he stares at the patrons spending money for him. Walking he makes his way to the security elevator he had installed as he steps forward and scans a card as the door opens and he steps in pressing the button that reads office. Quietly he stands in the elevator which is playing Blackened by Metallica. Grinning he was glad he never went with the traditional elevator music. The elevator comes to a stop and the doors open to a huge office where there is a woman and a camera crew waiting for him. He walks in and walks to the huge window that overlooks the whole casino floor as he keeps his arms folded behind his back as he observes. Lifting the radio at his side he speaks. “Jerry Zone 3” Watching as the security guards quickly rush through the casino over to the man who turns and runs right into the huge form of Jerry the security man. Shaman nods and turns around pulling his chair out od his desk and sits down looking at the woman. This woman is Suzy his on again off again lover in his life. She began her career as his camera woman and now she is his balance in the world sometimes. Never involving her in his wrestling business any longer. He turns and looks at her and smiles gently not wanting to put her on edge. “Suzy what brings you here to my office” You said the other day you did not wish to speak to me ever after what happened a few months back?” Suzy looks at him and shakes her head smiling as she points to the camera man and she leans back revealing a bit of leg. As the camera man looks at her and winks and grins looking at her from the legs upwards. Shaman observes this as his knuckles turn white. She may have not given him time of day in months, but his blood is boiling at the brazen display from the camera man in front of him. Suzy leans forward grabbing a pen and pad and begins asking questions. “So what do you think of your arrival in GCW and how are you preparing to face Vetra?” Shaman narrows his gaze at Suzy and places both hands palm down upon the desk as he breaths deeply. “A new place a new home. I am preparing for a fight that is what I am doing. Vetra is a talented person and I want to win regardless of what happens. I am going to keep training hard. I have been a champion of many titles; I have been a hardcore legend. Hell I have even been put through the living hells of life. Yet where am i” I am here in GCW starting anew. I want this challenge and I welcome it.” Waving his hand around at the casino he grins a bit. “I worked for years and I invested wisely. I actually footed the bill for all of this myself. I worked hard Suzy and I work even harder in the ring. My drive is unequaled by anyone that I foresee in my own eyes. I grew up through the crap and junk of reservations. I dealt with my father and uncles drinking and my mother dying of cancer. I tried to give back to my people and to only realize that sometimes people don’t want to be helped. They look at you as a pariah a person who acts above all else. Yet where had it brought me” It brought me to the depths of depression, the place where I buried my rage and anger in the place that I have embraced. I fought everything I believed and tried to turn away from it. Now I embrace who I am and what I have become. The high road is not the easy road. It is the road I jumped off so I can cut my own path.” Suzy looks at Shaman with a bit of concern and gets up and walks over to him and gently kisses him on the cheek and grins and sits down on his lap and snuggles up close to him. “What is all of this for?” Suzy looks into his eyes her forehead against his. “For being true to yourself and staying loyal to me no matter what. Your loyalties and faith are going to be tested very soon and you will know I am always here regardless of when we get back together.” Shaman looks at Suzy then turns and stares bullet holes through the camera man. “You know that you are playing with fire regardless. So I will advise you only this once camera dude. I will break every damn bone in your body and make sure your breathing through a tube for the rest of your damn life got it!” The camera man shakes as the screen visibly shakes as he has never seen this side of Shaman. The violent serious side that was merely rumored to be part of his beginning career as a professional wrestler. Suzy smiles as she wraps her arms around him. She speaks to him. “What about Dorian Ryan?” Shaman looks at Suzy and grins. “Mr. Ryan I will be waiting for you at the Arena. I won’t be hiding we should have a talking session man to man. I don’t give a damn who you thought you were or are. You know the old phrase an eye for an eye. You hit me and ruined what I had to say and then I ruined something you were shooting for. Your shot at the top. You just don’t get it. Your attack on me was petty and useless. We could have simply passed each other by and moved on. Instead you chose to attack me. Dangerous to say the very least. Either we drop the issue come the show or it escalates and I leave you to the vultures to pick your bones clean.” Shaman gently runs his hand through Suzy’s hair as he gazes at the casino below. “You see Dorian the world below is a rat race. We are men well above that. We are warriors who survive and fight scratching and clawing our way through the battlefield. Yet you and I also aren’t afraid to bleed and do what is needed. By whatever means Necessary.” Shaman looks around and picks up a magazine on his desk. It is a wrestling magazine and he holds it up and reads the headline which reads. “Shaman signs with GCW. The veteran is here again!”” Shaman sets the magazine down and thumbs through it with his freehand. “GCW the worlds best talent and they had invited me to join and got me a lucrative contract. I am looking forward to the heavy and excellent competition. I have Peter Vetra as my first opponent and it means a lot that I beat him. I think it is high time I get back to business in the ring as well as here.” Shaman grins and motions for Suzy to get up. Suzy stands and grins as she slips the pen behind her ear as she looks at the pad and smiles. “Business first we need to choose this weekend entertainment. We have several choices.” Shaman listens to Suzy speak as his mind wanders as he thinks of the coming years in his career. What will his achievements be. Will he return to the pinnacle of being a champion. There is no fear ever in his mind. Suzy gives the list of entertainers. He grins and says three names that are on the list. “Kenny Rogers for the country bar, Disturbed for the concert, and hell get me Iggy Pop!” Shaman grins as he leans back and plays the radio in his office as Suzy grins and looks at him. “Shaman gotta play some Kiss Calling Dr. Love. Life is too short to not enjoy the little things in life.” Suzy speaks up as he looks at her. “I have always preferred Crazy Night by Kiss. Either way we can book them for Saturday in the Ballroom.” Shaman thumbs his fingers across the edge of the desk lightly. Leaning forward he grins. “Book them we will have the best damn show by having the hottest band in the world KISS!” Shaman leans back and looks at Suzy. “Be sure to make sure that the GCW wrestlers and Staff have discounts in my Casino and hotel. I want them to be comfortable and know I am a businessman regardless of what happens. The guarantee will be enforced and promised as I have never backed down from my word.” Shaman stands and looks out onto the casino floor and grins once again. “Peter Vetra is going to have a fight that he ahs never expected. I am going to bring him the fight like he ahs never faced before and in the end I am going to triumph one way or another. He wishes to cheat I will dig deeper and do what is needed. Dorian Ryan and I should not interfere in one another’s matches. I guarantee that I will not interfere in his. He will have to win everything on his own and trust me he and I will chat and settle things or start a war.” Shaman stands crossing his arms across his chest as he watches the casino floor. Suzy walks up next to him after she sets the pad down on the desk and Shaman looks over at the camera man. “Now would be a good time to leave.” His words are sharp with edge as the camera man quickly departs dropping the camera as it fades to static viewing only Shaman’s feet.
