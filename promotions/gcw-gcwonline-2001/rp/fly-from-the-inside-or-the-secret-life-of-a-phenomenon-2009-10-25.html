Fly From The Inside (Or The Secret Life Of A Phenomenon)

25 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 25, 2009 A lot of people wonder what happened to that girl... "A changing of the guard is happening in Global Championship Wrestling with Dangerous Games coming around the corner. Gone are the days of LeStatt Knight's dominance, Jordan White's apathy, Jay Terror's betrayal, and Lia Ambrosi's invincibility. The last remnant of a dynasty stands between the likes of Clyde Fox, Zacharia Taylor, Dan Black, and Dangerous Games victory. Vivica J. Valentine." The one who won Dangerous Games with one arm tied behind her back... "But obviously The Fearless Phenom isn't what she used to be. Overshadowed by her habitual flirting partner Andy Murray, Vivica has become somewhat of a sympathy act. Her underdog tactics have long run its course, becoming stale and predictable. She doesn't have the same fire, the same flare, the same passion that was once synonymous with her name." The one who stood up and said no, when everyone else was saying yes... "Standing alone, defiantly in the face of The Establishment is simply a thing of her past. Now even Steven Caldera realized her braces on Tessa Windsor, Lia Ambrosi, and Lindsay Troy is what kept her in the fight. Now alone she seems lost in the shuffle, waging yet another war of self-preservation against Digital Mortality... who has successfully pointed out her every flaw." The one who beat the unbeatable... "But you can't blame Digi for doing the obvious. His former association with Valentine would surely end in his demise. Just like the three previously mentioned ladies, Digital Mortality would be another victim of The Establishment. Instead of becoming a statistic he blazed his own path, taking the opportunity to shoot at the target Vivica J. Valentine has worn on her back for nearly a year." The one who brought together the heartbeat of a nation... "If you want to get to the top of Global Championship Wrestling, you have to rip out its heart for the world to see. While a new heart is being groomed in the form of The Scottish King Of Cool, the old one is faulty and must be disposed of. Vivica had a great run, and has assured her spot in the GCW Hall of Fame and stories of lure. But like every high flyer and female wrestler, her career has run its course painfully early. She's put into the fruition the dreams of every fan of Global Championship Wrestling. We've laughed with her, we've cried with her, we've asked her to do it all... and until now she's done it all and more. But alas we find ourselves at Dangerous Games 2009, where I'm sadden to announce the death of Vivica J. Valentine among GCW's elite, it really couldn't end any other way because let's be honest..." What ever happened to The Phenomenon? "...that's a lot of weight to ask someone to carry on their shoulders." ---- Here's the weight of the world on my shoulders Here's the weight of the world on my shoulders On my shoulders All alone I pierce the chain And all in all the sting remains And dieing eyes consume me now The voice inside screams out loud I am focused on what I am after The key to the next open chapter Cause I found a way to steal the sun from the sky Long live that day that I decided to fly from the inside Every day a new deception Pick your scene and take direction And all in all I search to connect But I don't wear a mask and I have no regrets I am focused on what I am after The key to the next open chapter I can't escape the pain I can't control the rage Sometimes I think that I'm gonna go insane I'm not against what's right I'm not for what's wrong I'm just making my way and I'm gone Here's the weight of the world on my shoulders Cause I found a way to steal the sun from the sky Long live that day that I decided to fly from the inside -- "Fly From The Inside" by Shinedown ---- Chapter One: Forsaken ---- "I'd like to welcome everyone to the Dangerous Games 2009 Press Conference here in Miami, Florida." A well-dressed James Bryan, better known to the Global Championship Wrestling audience by his initials JB, seems to be the master of ceremonies for today's events, "Steven Caldera is away on business so I'll be taking care of the hosting duties for today." A colorful cast of characters line a teal and black clothed table, all with name plates and microphones in front of them. Although a more varied group of individuals probably couldn't be assembled before the masses, the individuals on stage were all too familiar with each other. For the past two years they've been on the road together. Through thick and thin, better or worse, till death do they part. While the gathering may not be each other's biggest fans, living the business brings a common bond between all of them. They learn each other's mannerisms, nervous twitches, and triggers. People may think that preparing for a match such as Dangerous Games involves watching a lot of tape, but in reality it's the ones without tape you have to be scared of. The four people on stage, although never involved in severe altercations with each other, could read one another like a book. While one of them had arguably the biggest challenge of his career in front of him, the other three knew if they wanted to be a winner... they had to go through each other. "To my far right is the Rampage 2009 winner. He's a favorite to win our main event Dangerous Games match. Ladies and gentlemen, Clyde Fox." With a grin from ear to ear, Clyde Fox raised his hand to acknowledge the cheers from the crowd before joining them to clap for himself. He loved clapping. It made him feel more comfortable in front of the hundreds of people staring holes into him. "To his left and to my right, we have another unarguable favorite to win our main event. Ladies and gentlemen, lets give a round of applause for Zacharia Taylor." Taylor acknowledged the cheers and applause from the media with a simple nod. Certainly not as open and innocent as Clyde Fox sitting next to him, Taylor was confident in his place. The applause of the crowd didn't much phase him. "To my left we have a familiar face to anyone who has covered Global Championship Wrestling over the past three years. Tomorrow she'll be facing Digital Mortality in a Falls Count Anywhere affair as well as competing in our main event. Vivica J. Valentine, ladies and gentlemen." The Fearless Phenom greeted the round of applause with a victorious smirk before raising her trademark flying V logo up on her right hand. She looked over at the man to her left with a smile as the crowd calmed down for the next announcement. "To my far left we have our GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Tomorrow night he'll be facing Hall Of Famer Jason O'Neill in a defense of his title. Ladies and gentlemen, 'The Scottish King Of Cool' Andy Murray." The applause for Murray was undeniably the loudest of the bunch. Dozens of fans lined up behind the media got up from their seats to give Andy Murray a standing ovation. Seeming shocked, Murray looked over at Valentine with a smile across his face, shrugging as if he didn't know where it came from. Vivica just blew off his bragging as the conference continued on. "Now that you know the names and situations of four of our top competitors, the floor is yours to ask whatever questions you may desire to four of the worlds greatest athletes." James Bryan announces before he takes a seat. Quickly the media reacts, hands shooting up from all around the crowd. JB does his best to keep a running order in his head but the calamity of the event makes it near impossible. Pointing to the person closest to him, James Bryan gives the first reporter the floor. "My name is Bryan Mitchell from Pro Wrestling Report, and my question is for Vivica J. Valentine." "Shoot." Vivica responded immediately with a anxious smirk. She couldn't help but show her spark. These were the situations in which The Fearless Phenom thrived. While the limelight may be a little new to the two individuals to her right, to her it felt like home. "There was a piece in the latest edition of our publication that detailed the decline of your GCW career. The reporter came to the conclusion that you're the last of a dying breed of the old guard here in Global Championship Wrestling. Do you have a response to that article?" The question was right to the point. Valentine was a bit shocked from the question, she wasn't one to much listen to the internet wrestling community or read wrestling related magazines. She lived the business. She breathed the business. She bled the business. You often don't need to read the events in which you've lived. "Seriously?" Vivica responded, unsure of what to say, "Wow, I hadn't heard a story like that had come out. What do I think? I think it's absolutely ridiculous." The reporter felt as if his answer was short changed as Valentine contemplated the situation. Someone had wrote a piece about her being past her prime? She was turning twenty seven next week. Nothing had crossed her mind more in the past few days than another shot at the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Not that she was looking past Digital Mortality, but Dangerous Games is like Christmas to the whole GCW Roster. Everyone has a list, and it all reads the same thing. I want to win Dangerous Games and main event NC-17. "Next Question." JB sounded as another reporter stood up. "Yes, my name is Nicholas Young from Wrestling Today." He spoke eagerly, like a kid in a candy store. The expression on his face is one Clyde Fox was certainly familiar with, "I have a question for Clyde Fox." She tried to pay attention but she just couldn't do it. She was last member of the old guard of Global Championship Wrestling? What about LeStatt Knight? As that thought crossed her mind, she soon remembered his complete lack of presence on the cards. How could she forget something as huge as LeStatt not being around. If there was a silver lining since losing the tag titles at all, it had to be the lack of LeStatt Knight. Surely there had to be someone. Lia... no. Ah, what about Jord... oh. Jay Terror? Tessa Windsor? Bryan Mayh... One by one she went through the motions of naming every major player in her Global Championship Wrestling career up to the point. Yet one by one she checked them off. Fired. Retired. Disappeared. Fired. Retired. "Shit..." She said under her breath before looking over at Andy Murray, "Surely you don't think that..." "My question is for Andy Murray." A reporter quickly interrupted her mumblings. She almost forgot the press conference was going on at all. "Yessir?" Murr responded, not as much as having a chance to make eye contact with his good friend. A once green as grass rookie breaking into the industry, Global Championship Wrestling was her training grounds. She'd taken her beatings on her way to the top. She'd been embarrassed on national television. She'd been booed out of arenas. She'd showered in the adoration of millions. On her way to the top she forced her way into the limelight and achieved her destiny. Now at 27 years old she found herself among wrestlers the same age or older than herself. Yet they were the new blood in GCW, and she was labeled a thing of the past. "Vivica." Jolted from her day-dreaming she almost jumped a bit before looking around, but it wasn’t' a reporter who caught her attention... it was Andy Murray. "Did you need something?" "No..." Vivica shook her head making an odd look on her face, trying her hardest to hide her insecurity, "Nothing at all." Murr responded with a strange look, obvious confusion as he looked at the girl next to him and simply shook his head. Now Valentine put her game-face on. She'd been in GCW longer than anyone around her minus James Bryan. A Dangerous Games win resided near the top of her resume, surely someone has a question about that. Wanting to know how it feels to be on top, what her plans were to get back there again, what kind of knowledge her new-found veteran status could pass on to the up-and-comers next to her. Andy Murray. Zacharia Taylor. Andy Murray. Clyde Fox. Zacharia Taylor. Clyde Fox. Andy Murray. Andy Murray. Clyde Fox. Zacharia Taylor. One by one the questions rolled in, not a single one directed towards her. Were there really no questions for her? Maybe the guy writing the article was right. Maybe she was already being replaced. Anxiously she waited for a question to come her direction. Anything at all to settle the mixture of disbelief and self-doubt swelling up inside of her from her heart. The same heart that was once claimed to be the biggest in GCW. Caring too much was a double-edged sword in the end, unfortunately. "Thank you everyone and that'll conclude our press conference for today. Don't forget to check out Dangerous Games on Pay-Per-View and see which one of the very talented athletes before you succeed on one of the biggest stages in our business. Thank you, Miami!" James Bryan ended the ceremonies to the applause of the crowd and media alike. As all the wrestlers got up from their seats, the media flocked towards the sides of the stage as the usually do. It was their one change to get a one-on-one interview with GCW's greatest. An exclusive only to be published in their own publications usually brought out the worst in the wrestling media. Ruthlessness broke out between them as they push and shoved each other, jockeying for position as the subjects of their attention made their way towards them. It was obvious that Vivica J. Valentine was in no mood for an interview. For the first time since the beginning of her career she felt outcasted, almost like she didn't belong in the same group of peers she just minutes before were reading like open books. Not making eye contact with the media, she kept her head down hiding an obvious scowl while she marched off stage. "What's wrong V?" Murr tried to reach out and grab her shoulder. Hoping to catch her and find out what was eating her up inside. To react this way to adversity wasn't common practice for Vivica J. Valentine. Reading her wasn't as easy as an open book in this circumstance. She'd had her feelings hurt. Her pride had taken a huge blow as she pressed past the reporters, not even acknowledging their existence. "V!" Andy again tried to stop her but it was too late. As she pressed past the reporters, it didn't matter anyway. Not a single one tried to stop her as they flocked to Andy Murray, GCW World Heavyweight Champion, blocking any attempt her could possibly make to be with his friend. He watched helplessly as Valentine disappeared behind the curtains backstage, only quickly being able to catch a quick glimpse of the tears running down her cheeks. For the past three years she'd lived in the warm glow of the limelight. Today, it'd finally burned out. ---- Chapter Two: Riptide ---- HEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAA! The scream resonated through the empty gymnasium. With the lights half turned out, the wood freshly mopped wood-paneled floors gave the obvious impressions of a closed facility. Tonight was a special case though. Tonight, no matter how hard he'd tried, the closing manager just couldn't get rid of her. With every warning she explained that she needed to work harder. She wasn't able to give up now. HEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAA! If one word could be used to describe Vivica Julian Valentine, most of the people close to her would use 'stubborn' without hesitation. Her endless heart and fighting spirit appeared in more than just the middle of the squared circle. It drastically effected her personal life. The more times she'd been told no, the more she wants it... and the less willing she is to give it up. Unfortunately suffering the brunt end of her stubbornness tonight was the closing gym manager, leaned up against his desk twirling his keys impatiently as GCW's Fearless Phenom showed no signs of ending her workout any time soon. HEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAA! With another scream came another vicious shot to the hanging punching bag. This time a sickening side kick, in which the move itself made Vivica take a step back and favor her back. A slight grunt escapes her lips as she falls down to one knee momentarily, before getting right back up to a vertical base. Wanting to close him gym, the supervisor watched helplessly... wondering if the kids had been put to bed back home yet. Surely, he'd make it home in time for a bedtime story. He prayed. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! A not-so-subtle bang on the glass door quickly caught his attention as Vivica continued to work out in the background. Looking over his shoulder, a large man appeared in the doorway knocking on the glass. His words were silenced by the door between them but it was obviously he wanted to come inside. Walking over towards the door, the supervisor pointed towards the closed sign... clearly marked for over an hour ago. As he mouthed the word 'Sorry.' to the other man, he watched as his wannabe patron points towards the back of the gym. "Her?" The supervisor stepped aside, giving his guest a clear view of the woman in the back. Staying very close to the door a conversation, albeit muffled and spoken loudly, was somewhat possible. "I need to talk to her." His words were accented, but audible as they came through the glass door. Hesitating the manager held onto his keys, looking back at the girl, and then back at his guest. While it was certainly against gym policy to let someone in this late after hours, at this point he was willing to do just about anything to get rid of her. His family was waiting on him. After eyeing him suspiciously, he finally pushed the key into the lock on the door and opened it for hopefully the last time. Once inside the man was even bigger than he looked from the outside, obviously he'd spent his share of time inside of a gym. "Make it quick, I was supposed to close over an hour ago." The manager said as he closed the door behind him, "I can't leave until she does." "I'll do my best." The man answered, "No promises. She's stubborn as a mule." "I bet." The manager said before taking a seat back behind the counter, going back to twirling his keys and waiting. Letting out another scream, the man watching her as she obviously strained herself. Coming closer and closer, he began to notice even more. She wasn't standing normally, favoring her right leg as she took a couple quick jabs to the bag. Taking a step back she arched her back oddly. It was almost like she was falling apart right in front of his eyes. How long had she been at this? "Hey V?" The familiar voice of a Scot chirped in through her ears, pausing her in her tracks. What was he doing here? Who told him where she was? He couldn't see her like this. She was a mess. What would he think if he knew how insecure you really were about things? A cocky smile and an heir of confidence can get you far in this business, but this was about much more than business. "What's up with you?" Andy Murray's voice rang loud as she felt like a deer in headlights, "You're not looking so good." Almost as if on cue, she turned around gingerly to look him in the eye. He had no idea how right he really was. Her eyes were pink and puffy, the remnants of the dried tears the lined her cheeks. The Fearless Phenom wore no make-up, instead she looked like she'd been up all night working her ass off. Sure, success doesn't come easy but at what price? "I don't want to talk about it..." She answered quickly, turning her back to the current GCW World Heavyweight Champion once again. He wasn't meant to see her like this, no one was. "Did the press conference really shake you up that bad?" Murr chimed in as Vivica took another swing at the punching bag, "Shit, you should hear some of the things they're saying about me out there. Monsters they are." "I don't think you get it..." Vivica mumbled with her back to The Scottish King Of Cool, "You don't understand what it's like to be me. Someday your time is going to come, and you're going to lose your championship. Then maybe you'll understand what I'm going through." "You have the same chance as anyone else of winning that thing." Murr interrupted, "If you ask me I'd say you have a better chance than anyone else." "You say that now but you don't know Steven Caldera like I know Steven Caldera..." Vivica's voice broke up as she continued on, obviously in a bit of pain straining her words, "Once that championship is gone he'll do anything he can to bury you, to break your spirit. You'll succeed at first. Be on top of the world... you'll feel unstoppable but then the world stops playing fair." Valentine winced as she turned around to look Andy Murray in the eyes. "He'll take away anything that makes you happy, he'll make your life a living hell unless you're one of his precious Establishment members." Vivica continued, "Maybe I'm too stubborn to play his game or maybe I just have some sort of moral code that no one else in this fucking place has, but it feels like your drowning. Trying to go up for air only to have your head held down against your will." "But this is your chance Viv..." "I know, but there are 29 other wrestlers who are just like me out there Murr. Every year the names and faces change but the objective remains the same. 29 people fighting for one breath of fresh air, one opportunity to make a difference not only in their own lives but in GCW." Vivica frustrated clinches her fists, "You know a girl can only hold her breath for so long before she drowns. Maybe Digital Mortality and those media guys are right. Maybe it's just time for me to accept that these guys are just better than I am... and I'm just wasting my goddamn time staying up until the wee hours of the morning busting my ass for a fight I just can't fucking win." "It's not tru..." "It's hard to wake up and look at yourself in the mirror in the morning, knowing what you used to be and wondering if you'll ever get it back again." The tears began to stream down, "You push and push and push... and you don't even get any fucking results. I go out there every night and damn near kill myself just to hear that I don't got it anymore. At first the memories are fresh in your mind and you know you could do it again, but as time goes on..." "You don't have to kill yourself over this, Viv." Murr explains, "If you'd just..." "Not kill myself over this?" Vivica explains, "This is what I live for Murr. I eat, sleep, dream, and bleed this business. Without this life and this company I'm nothing. I don't have a family, I hardly have a home. I don't even know the names of my neighbors. We live our lives out of suitcases and where some people have boyfriends, kids, parents... I don't have any of that. I have Global Championship Wrestling." "If you'd just accept some help every once in a while, and stop being so stubborn about everything things would be a lot better you know." Murr interrupted, "We all need a little help every once in a while V. Even you." "Thanks, but no thanks." Vivica answered, "I already think I've talked too much." "Just take my god damn help, will ya?!" Murr raised his voice, tired of his statements and opinions being brushed aside in favor of some personal manifesto in Valentine's brain. "Things don't..." "Quit dodging me and just take my fucking help!" "I can't!" Vivica snarled back, "You don't get it do you?" "Apparently I don't. Why can't you just say yes?" "If I say yes you'll end up just like everyone else Caldera fucking stole from me. I can't let that happen! Not to you." "I don't care V! It's something that I've accepted can happen, why don't you quit playing martyr and just let me help you. I'm a grown man, I can make my own decisions. I don't need you to make my decisions for me." "I'm not going to let you." "Why the bloody hell not?!" "Because I fucking love you, alright?!" As the words escaped her lips she even shocked herself. Not to mention the look on Andy Murray's face, which quickly became slack jawed. An eerie silence crept in over the gym as Vivica looked down at the ground, she couldn't even make eye contact with him. He had so many questions, but none of them seemed to come out when he tried to formulate them into words. Just silence stood between them as Vivica contemplated the consequences of what she'd just admitted. Picking up her gear bag off the floor, a shower never even crossed her mind as she slung it over her shoulder. "I..." She stuttered as she moved away from Murray, still frozen in shock, "I have to get out of here." With those words, she simply left. A relieved sigh escaped the lips of the manager as she made her exit while Andy Murray still stood in shock. He didn't know how to feel or what to think about what he'd just heard, only one thing was painfully clear... "Hey man," The manager spoke, "You have to get out of here." ---- Chapter Three: Welcome To The Show ---- Some say she spent so long overachieving that she worked until she had nothing left. Others will tell you that nothing happened to her, and she'd been here the entire time. The only thing that was really for certain is that somewhere, right now... Vivica J. Valentine was working her ass off against the odds to make a difference. You can't run away from your problems, you have to face them head on or they'll eat you alive. Because nothing in life has ever come easy for me. It's hard to be just barely over 5' and tell a complete stranger that you wrestle for a living and you're damn good at it. But for whatever reason the morning drive to the next event always brings a calm over me. Through all the aches and pains, and emotional roller coasters over the past few days when I step foot into the arena I cease to be Vivica J. Valentine anymore. When I get under those lights and in front of the thousands of screaming fans, I turn into The Fearless Phenom. The Aerial Artisan. The Image Of The Invisible. The Woman Without Fear. But in the end though, maybe Digi was right about me. Maybe I had been missing something. Maybe I did need a fire lit under my ass. Whatever his reasons may be it's going to be the biggest mistake of his entire life. I refuse to bust my ass for no reason. I refuse to accept defeat under any circumstances anymore. I won't let my head be held under the water by anyone. I've come too far to drown tonight. Tonight I'm walking into Miami, Florida an underdog at my own pay-per-view. I never thought I'd see the day. They don't call me Miss Dangerous Games for nothing. I am the girl who was set on fire and then won the battle royal with one arm. People may call me stubborn, but I'm strong in my convictions. I'm not leaving that ring tonight unless it's by my own will. Either I'm walking of Miami as the #1 Contender to the GCW World Championship, or I'm not walking at all. After tonight there will be no more doubts. No more questions. No more will people ask "What happened to that girl?" They'll be saying "Do you remember that time Vivica J. Valentine showed up in Miami and won Dangerous Games... twice?" Welcome to my comeback party ladies and gentlemen, I hope you enjoy the show.
