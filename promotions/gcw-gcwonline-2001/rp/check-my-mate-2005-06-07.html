Check my mate

7 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/7/05 'I don't know...did you ever just feel like a walking bullseye?' 'You realize who you're asking this question to, right? The king of all scapegoats.' The man's got a point. 'Then you know what it's like to wear a crown and have the crowds all want to prove themselves to you.' 'God damn, do you do anything except bitch, bitch, bitch? I've met only a few people who win something, and all they can see in it is a bad thing. You're one of those people that could take the jackpot in the lottery and see that more people are going to try to rob you. You win a new car, you're paranoid that people are going to scratch it.' 'I'm far from paranoid. I'm just saying that I feel like I'm being sized up now.' Don takes a long drag on his cigarette. It disappears underneath not only its own fire, but his. Cold, tired eyes stare at the smouldering end of it as he throws it to the floor. Starts on his third pack of the day. 'Sized up, eh?' 'Yeah. Like it's '49 and there's a gold rush right to the belt around my waist. I mean, I kinda get the idea that people with titles couldn't give a shit, but that's a given. But still, it's like everyone else is hungry...wanting it, wanting not only the title and the belt but also just to put their foot on my chest and not let me get up.' 'So you're saying that they've become...you.' 'Hardly.' Never saw myself as a wolf among men. Conquest was never nearly as important as the fight to get there. The end of the match, whether it a win or a loss, was always heartbreaking and a real downer. The adrenaline stopped flowing immediately, whether I got my hand raised or not, because I knew that was one off my limit. One match closer to never fighting again, one match closer to death. And during it, you can do anything and everything. One man can triumph over every obstacle, and one man can either cause an entire arena to erupt in extacy or break every heart that watches. But when it's over, it's over. 'I think you've got a severe ego problem, Jack.' The devil himself takes a chug from his can of Gennessee. I always found it ri-God-damn-diculous that he actually imports that piss-water into Canada so he can drink his favorite lousy beer in his home up north. Then again, there aren't many things that Don does that aren't ridiculous. Like defying the omnipotent. Or making The Crusades happen. Or causing Tom Goes to the Mayor to replace The Brak Show on Adult Swim. ...I still haven't forgiven him for that one. 'You're telling me that I have an ego problem!' 'Jack, you're sitting there going on and on about how everybody wants a piece of you.' 'Everybody does, in some way or another.' Compare and contrast. Erik and Eva VanLorne each want a piece of me. But in opposite ways. Who's got two thumbs and is ungodly gorgeous? This guy! 'Jesus, you're like I always used to say, 'I'm itching like a man in a fuzzy tree.' You climb to where you want to be, and you look around for everybody to come up and get you, and between the rush of being up there and the fear of getting brought down you shake so much that you're about to fall off.' '...you just quoted Elvis.' Don looks up from his beer, surprised that I'd know that. 'You just quoted Elvis...and said it was one of your own. You lying sack of shit.' 'Ahhh...but I did both at the same time. I quoted Elvis and quoted myself.' What? Don points his index finger to his head and smiles a nasty, grimy smile. His five o'clock shadow doesn't hide his little smirk, and the cigarette in his hand is almost gone already. And it takes me a minute to comprehend what he's trying to say. 'You're trying to tell me that you were Elvis?' 'Give the boy a prize.' Bullshit. 'Bullshit. I'll believe when you say you were the priest that caused The Crusades. I'll even believe that you were against the Holocaust. But there's no way that you can convince me that you, in front of me, were The King.' 'I am The King.' Throws down his nth cigarette and lights up another. Addiction, addiction. 'It's true what they all say. Elvis didn't die, he just went home.' 'So you're trying to tell me that Elvis- ...you... - didn't die, but rather just descended into Hell?' 'Catholics believe that Mary didn't die, but just went up to Heaven.' 'Good for them.' I've already had enough of this conversation. Trust the devil to lie. Well...if you can...trust him to...do that...cuz he...always...lies. God dammit. 'If you didn't know that Blue Suede Shoes alluded to the fall of one Donald Angelicus Lucifer, you're more ignorant than I imagined.' '... That was the worst name I ever heard.' With a shrug, the devil himself takes a sip of beer and watches some more TV. We're big fans of MacGyver, the two of us. The man's a freaking genius, and in general there's a lot of nostalgia in the show. Reminding us both of better times, though I'm sure that some of Don's better times are from a bit farther back than the '80's. A couple minutes of silence drive me nuts. So's this itching. Scratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratchscratch- 'Dude, what are you, a dog? Chill.' 'Sorry. I just...itch...in a very sensitive spot.' '...you got crabs, kid?' 'No, smartass. I shaved.' He stares at me for a second. 'You...shaved.' 'Well, I shaved...a place of...never have...for...shammashamma...-ness.' 'You didn't actually say anything just now, Johnny-boy. You made sound. But that was neither a statement, a question, or an exclamation. You just strung a bunch of words and quasi-coherent sounds together to cover up what I was asking and hope that I wouldn't pay attention.' 'I shaved in an awkward place is all!' Don't eyebrows shoot up momentarily. The devil himself may not be surprised by much anymore, but the man isn't heartless. He knows my pain. 'Oh. You shaved...there. Why the hell would you go and do that?' 'I figured you haven't lived until you do.' 'Well, I've lived for quite a while, and I never have.' Stop. Back that train up. 'You? You who are the source of temptation. You who personify evil and corruption. You who slaughtered the innocent, caused angels to fall, took on God, salt the Earth, plot continuously, are the center of sin, and made sexual deviancy have never shaved your crotch? I find that really, extremely surprising.' 'Yeah, well, I got my dignity.' Hmm... It makes sense. The devil himself got thrown out of paradise because of his pride. Why wouldn't he refuse to shave his balls for eons for the same reason? 'So why'd you do something stupid like that, Jack?' I want to lie and say, 'Because I felt like it.' But the guy sees through that anyway, so I'll just save us both time. 'Well...I wanted to clean up...certain parts of me. You know, for some people I wouldn't mind...being cleaned up for.' 'You want to be all pretty and shaved when Eva VanLorne shoots you down, eh?' 'First off, no. Second, she wouldn't shoot me down.' 'Like the shuttle Columbia.' I give that son of a bitch the nastiest look. 'What? Too soon? Fine. But anyway, that's one hornet's nest you should just back away from and let be. There's no sense stirring up an absolute ton of trouble and putting forth an endless amount of effort only to get burned and burned badly.' 'I don't get burned. History shows I do the burning.' 'Yeah, well, she's a little different than all the little fangirls that fall head over heels for pretty boy Johnny Borealis.' 'I'm sure I can bring something that she hasn't seen before to the plate myself.' A slight change in facial expression, a slight stillness in my face shows Don that the mood has changed. From relaxing to serious, it's almost too smooth of a transition for anyone to tell where one started and the other faded slowly into nothingness. And now Don and I aren't two idiot friends sitting on a couch, one drinking shitty beer and the other drinking good ol' imported Guinness, watching MacGyver and talking about shaving their pubes. He's the devil incarnate. I'm Johnathan Borealis. All. There is. To it. And he's thinking of tempting me, and I know it. He's thinking right now of offering me Eva on a silver plate because I've got a little juvenile crush and he thinks I'll just throw it all away to get a hand on her. But he knows damn well that he's wrong. He knows that if I want something, I want to get it and I don't want help. He knows how I'm not the kind of guy that chases after something that he doesn't have even a hope of attaining. It's simple. I want something I can't get anywhere, anytime. And I think she wants the same thing. I don't doubt in the slightest that she can get any man that she wants in an instant, and that they'll kiss her ass from day one. And she'll take them, she'll be completely in control, and she'll make every decision. Eva's a powerful woman, and she loves more power. And she's got this little thing for Mr Tall Dark and Tortured Soul, LeStatt. 'Fucking hate that douchebag.' 'What was that?' 'Eh-...nothing.' Whoops. Let the internal monologue slip there. So why do I know she can't get me out of her head? If I'm pretty much opposite of the guy that she's shown an interest in, and you don't get much more opposite of that pretentious fuck than me, then why in the hell is Eva giving me a second thought? That's the fun in the game. You don't let them know. The entire fun in the chase, in the hunt, is making them wonder why they can't forget you. Cheesy pickup lines? Flowers? Those aren't the true goings on of what I'm doing here. It's all something she's used to; a game of chess. Moving pieces to distract tactically and precisely, first confusing your opponent and catching them off guard. And then we dance a dance for a time, and with one so lovely and with a wit so sharp as Eva's the dance is that much longer, that much more enjoyable, that much more exhilarating. She approaches, and I counter. I go on the offensive, she counters. And we dance the game of chess until one takes that final initiative and both are the better for it. ...but then it's over. No more chase. No more hunt. No more chess or dancing or any other illusions or symbolism. A man and a woman together, and that's it. Isn't it? And if you're at all interested in a little blatant Borealis fact that you won't see posted by anyone in any signatures (hopefully), I'll fill you in. I, Johnathan Borealis, hate when that's it and you're done. Part of me wants to never attain what I strive for. Ever. Because it's not the win, it's never the win. It's the fight. It's not the outcome, but the hard-pressed labor that goes into everything that is the only reason for getting up and going on. Great job. You won a title. Now, stop celebrating and go defend it. I love you, you love me. Now that that's out of the way, let's start hating each other slowly. Death's coming, death's coming, death's coming. Oops, you're dead. No more anticipation for that greatest adventure, cuz it's over. 'By the way, if you're going to be thinking a lot about Eva, you might want to be on your toes. I invited Kate over for dinner.' My jaw drops. I look over and Don is smiling. The son of a bitch knows what this does to me. Especially now. 'We're having oysters, red wine, and pasta.' I know they're coming. I know the words are coming. But I can't stop them or hide from them. They just come. 'We're dating.' He smiles again. He's had this since the beginning and hasn't said anything. He's been dancing that dance. He's been playing the game. And now he's got his checkmate, and what've I got?
