The Morning After
Toxic
Global Championship Wrestling Roleplays #12
Date: 12/19/2002
Location: Orlando, FL

For months he’d heard that one sound, that one song. It was there in the back of his mind when he went to the supermarket, the Movie Theater and, well, even with his shrink. Eight months. Eight months since he had last heard the song outside of his own head. Then, the song had been muffled by a frantic crowd. But the song is still as clear as a crystal ball in his mind.

Even now, echoing through his head was “Life’s Sweet Drug,” the heavy rock song that had signaled his defeat. The song that had signaled two years wasted of blood, sweat and tears. For it was this song that proclaimed Twisted the GCW World Champion after a grueling battle royal. To him the song symbolized what he lost, no, what was stolen from him.

Revenge. He thought. It had been on his mind for months. He’d had some measure of it the day after being defeated. The champion had been left a bloody mess at his hands with himself standing over the champ. But that hadn’t been enough to quench his thirst for revenge.

Toxic looked at himself in the mirror. He hadn’t shaved in weeks. The man in the mirror still looked young but now he looked somewhat different. Toxic could put his finger on how he looked different but he knew something had changed.

Toxic turned away from the mirror and looked around. His room was a mess. The $400,000 condo had turned into a barn overnight thanks to his twenty-first birthday party. There was broken glass and some sort of fluid on the carpet. He could only hope the liquid came from the broken bottle. The walls had numerous holes in them and one of his lamps had been kicked over and broken. His birthday, like most of his parties, had been a blast but the excessive partying was starting to get too much. He was still in shape but he needed to hit the gym. Ever since the phone call he’d put it off but now there would be no more procrastination.

Revenge. The thought came to him again. It’ll be a long and tough road but when I get there he won’t know what hit him. No man shall steal from me and expect forgiveness. There is no forgiveness. He is no more of a man than any other of them. They are still and always will be less than I am.

Toxic turned to look in the mirror again. ‘Soon Twisted. Very soon,’ he muttered to the face in the mirror. He grinned.

He walked over to the phone on the other side of the room. The cord was unplugged, probably due to some drunken mishap last night. He plugged it back in, picked up the receiver and dialed.

‘Yes could I speak to Kevin Jacobs please?’ he asked.

(Pause)

‘Hey chubs get to the studio tomorrow. Be there at five.’ Toxic said.

(Pause)

‘I don’t care if you got an interview with the pope just get your fat, Twinkie-eating ass to the studio.’ He demanded.

Toxic hung up the phone. He looked in the mirror again.

‘The countdown begins.’
