Maybe I shouldn't have eaten that many? No, no; it was essential like: I live, I die, and at this moment I'm tripping this much (also; this much less than I was tripping in any preceding moment, and so on and so on until the questioned eating) and letting my head run allegories and metaphors whenever I can get my head up. Two compound eyes are still just two eyes at the end of the day; but we're on into the night now, aren't we? Too dark for whatever gods want to exist to be watching; too solitary for them to mark the place. We are disconnected. Eternally young. I feel this is a big moment. How's that stand? Like trees against hurricanes, like sand against the surf. Great crashing waves of ether rock the planet wild; scream palindromic in my ears. They're not bleeding, they're understanding.
