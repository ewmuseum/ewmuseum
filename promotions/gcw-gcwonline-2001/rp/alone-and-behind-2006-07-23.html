Alone and behind

23 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/23/06 There are things that aren't for most to see on camera. There are things that are the personal lives of people as they pass through this mortal world on to the next realm. And sometimes people's paths run into each other and it's nothing short of a collision. Sometimes you get this collision and the world falls apart around them. Then, sometimes they run into each other and it was more a work of fate, of desires that they just don't understand. They say that the plants that grow in adversity are those that grow the strongest... What they say is true. ~Las Vegas, Nevada~ ~Brand & Northsky Home~ ~Queens Ridge Subdivision~ ?OH MY GOD! YOU'RE KIDDING ME!? Alexis couldn't believe what she was hearing over her cell phone as she was still unpacking her multitude of things that the Order had provided to her since coming in onto this assignment. As she pulled clothes out from her dresser in the guest room she would be using for a few weeks and put them into her bag to travel with Gray up to Canada for GCW's next show. It wasn't Dawn's estate up in Canada, but it would do. It was a seven bedroom monstrosity in this heat infested town. As for what she was talking about? She was on the phone with her friend Lia Vlassy, or Sterescu would be proper with the news she just got. Now that they've taken the time to settle things down, she's finally getting caught up with the whirlwind of the last few weeks. ?So you have had a total recall or most of it?? What's this? Well to bring you up to speed, Alexis was asked to work with Grayson Brand to help come up with a means of finding out why the two vampire friends of theirs, Lia ?Shadow? Vlassy and Virgil Ambrosi couldn't remember their past live(s). There were blocks that they should have known. For Virgil, his wife and family from when he was human. For Lia, her husband and family. Interesting that they had found each other so many centuries later, yeah centuries, they're vampires remember? But it seems that while they had found themselves to be lovers in this day and age, the parts of their pasts that they were missing... were each other. Soul mates that had literally traveled through time again to find each other. A truly romantic notion that Alexis was happy to hear about. Although that meant that her assignment to work with the KB, particularly Gray might be coming to an end. Alexis stopped pulling clothes out and walked over to the window. She pulled the shade out a bit, letting the blaring sun flash into her room. The room overlooked the large swimming pool with a fake wall of rocks that a waterfall cascaded down behind it, blocking the view of the neighbors to the pool. But she wasn't looking so much at the pool, but more of what was in it. She'd learned that Gray took his regular workouts with a bit of zeal and dedication. Right now he was just finishing up after a few hours in the gym in the area that was technically almost under the pool on the third, underground level. He usually finished it all off in a swim and then he was ready for the rest of the day. She didn't know how he did it though, cause of how hot it was outside and how much sleep he appeared to not get on a regular basis. ?Yeah, well we'll have to get together and talk about it when I get up to Vancouver. Gray and I are leaving later on tonight when it cools down. We had to make sure the lab and that was all secured here, as well as take care of a few small things for him in this area. So we're about a day and a half behind the others.? She nodded to the answer she got in the phone, and then looked out again. Gray was climbing out of the pool in what would only be classified as a speedo for covering what needed to be covered for common decency. Although she knew that at night, Dani and Josh sometimes didn't worry about even that. ?Yeah, I'll let you go then. I know you don't like to be up too much during the day. Keep safe and all that. I'll be seeing you in a little under two days. We'll have to go do dinner all together or something. Maybe up on the Space Needle's restaurant.? She hung up her phone and set it down. Quickly she closed the drawer she'd been working on and then opened another. She started to move a few select pieces of clothing over again, and then a sound overtook her attention. On the second story of the house that was above ground, the six bedrooms up here actually shared one large bathroom. It had a separate shower and large jacuzzi bathtub, in fact it was a mirror of the one downstairs for the master bedroom's occupants to use. The sound, was that of running water. Obviously Gray had taken to having a shower to cool off after the over one hundred degree temperature swim to finish his workout. Her guest room was right next to the bathroom, chosen by her for this reason. Gray's room was at the other end of the hallway, technically being the first room that you'd have to go by to get to anything up here. A trait she'd already picked up on in his role as either hers, or previously Dani's guardian. He always chose to sleep in the first spot, living room or otherwise to be the first thing that someone or something would encounter if they came in. He was always on guard, no matter what. She reached out and slid her door open a bit more then the crack it had before. She could see the door to the bathroom was also slightly ajar. Tempation was something she was raised to avoid. But in this case, if it was a test of her faith in that training, she had just failed. Slowly she opened her door and slide along the wall, actually finding herself tip-toeing as she got over by the door to the bathroom. She leaned against it and peered into the room. Immediately the color in her cheeks rose to almost match the bright dye job of her own hair. Standing in the glass doored shower was the heavily tattooed man who was her partner in the lab and her guardian outside of the McFarlane Estate. She stood looking at him, as the water cascaded down his chest and back, the main parts she could see from her vantage point. ?Alexis, you really shouldn't be doing this? She whispered to herself as she leaned in a bit more. Suddenly he reached up and turned the water off. He opened the swinging glass door and stepped out of the shower and grabbed his towel. First thing he went for was his long, gray colored, hair that gave him his namesake. He'd been named for the off color of his hair and eyes from birth. Needless to say, from this new vantage point, Alexis found herself... staring. He was muscular, tall, and she'd found that she had a thing for body ink after her 'rescue' at the hands of Shadow and Virgil. And to be blunt, some of the arguments that he'd had with his brother as to who was the more manly, he'd just proven his side of things to her, at least. He stopped drying his hair and then moved to dry the rest of himself off. As he turned, Alexis panicked and almost dove for her room, away from the door to the bathroom. As she entered her room though, she stepped wrong and fell towards the bed. As she came down, she managed to hook the handle of her suitcase and pull it down with her, dropping everything it it down to the floor and managing to make a bit of a ruckus. She didn't have time to get back up to her feet when her door was swung open. ?Alexis? You okay?? She looked over her shoulder to see him standing there, his towel wrapped around his waist now. ?Yeah, I'm fine. Just tripped and caught my suitcase. Now I have to refold and repack everything.? She tried not to look at him as she started to work on picking her stuff back up and getting it repacked. ?Alright. Well we're going to head out about seven. Easier to drive at night around here until we get to the northern California or Oregon lines. Then the temperatures will drop pretty good and at least be comfortable.? She just nodded and kept putting her clothing back into the suitcase. He took the hint though and walked off to finish getting ready on his own part. As soon as he was gone from her room though, she let out a long sigh as images were still racing through her head. ~Almost Two Days Later~ ~Stevens Pass, Washington State~ Everything had been going rather well for the trip. Although Alexis seemed to be quite a bit distracted. Gray noticed this, but wrote it off to their pending meeting with Shadow. Their breaking of the hold on their memories had lifted far too many implications. Who would would have wanted to meddle with the memories of these two particular vampires so that they would forget that they had been a happily wedded couple when they had been human? But it obviously wasn't some genetic or medical thing. It was sorcery, and some powerful shit too to last what was looking to be over a millenia based on their ages since they had been embraced into the world of the Vampire. More and more shit just getting piled on, all the time. Not to mention, Blaise's letting him know that he thought something was up between Dani and Colonel Childress on a spiritual level. Well not with Mr. Childress himself, but more the co-habitor, the co-pilot of his body if you will. Most of the Order knew of the being that was only referred to by his common name ?The Wrath?. All in all, though, Gray's specialty was Xenobiology. That was a physical science. When you started going to the veil and the spirit world, his expertise started to dwindle down to almost nothing. They were using his Suburban, and it felt good to be driving this truck instead of that mobile lab for a change. Yeah, it had to stop every few hours for a gas up, but it was a big 4x4 capable truck and could do just about anything he wanted, especially climb these steep climbs on the passes in the Cascades as they were rapidly approaching the Seattle-Tacoma area. From there, it's two ferry rides to Vancouver and then a few ferry rides back for the next show. And for Gray... he was coming home. He was born, raised and claimed a small town over on the Kitsap Peninsula, about fifteen minutes from the Bainbridge Island Ferry. It had been a long while since he got a few weeks to spend some time here. He already made sure he had his fishing gear on hand. This time of year was prime time for Coho and King Salmon, and it was time to get some much needed rest to think everything else through. What else could someone toss onto the fire now? Wait... The road here was narrow and the Suburban wasn't small by any means. Wait... Was that another car on the road coming at them? Wait... In the middle? Gray had enough time to look over to Alexis who had dozed off in the passenger's seat. ?HOLD ON!? The car didn't move, and Gray had to react now. To the right was a rock face, he had to go left and head for the grass and trees! He pulled the wheel just enough to turn without spinning everything, the tires still squealed a bit as they changed direction quickly. ?DAMNIT!? Alexis was now awake, but she didn't make a noise, she just held onto the 'oh shit' handle at the top and braced for the worst. The other truck kept coming, and went by them as they headed to the left hand shoulder on the road as your facing west. Gray didn't have time to try to get back onto to the road though, as the truck's momentum carried them right into a small pacific pine tree. Well it was small compared to some of the others, but big enough that the front of his truck didn't look like it was in that great of shape anymore. Gray grabbed his cellphone off of it's dashboard holder and threw the door open with a force. Alexis quickly looked into the side mirror and saw that the other truck has stopped and pulled over onto the shoulder. ?Gray, they stopped!? Gray said something under his breath as he looked at the now steaming radiator and other broken pieces in the front of his truck. He turned and looked at the now stopped vehicle a few hundred feet back and shook his head. Sliding his cellphone into his pocket he started to talk towards them. ?I'll be right back. They couldn't fucking drive right, maybe they could take us back to Leavenworth and we could call for the others to come get us and get a tow truck out here for this piece of shit.? Alexis had never heard that type of anger in Gray's voice before. But then again, they'd just wrecked his own personal truck on a tree in the mountains of Washington State. She undid her seat belt and then stepped out of the the truck. She looked back in time to see three people get out of the other vehicle as Gray was approaching them. She heard a woman cry out to him. ?Is everyone alright back there?? Gray answered. ?Yeah, except for my truck. We got bounced around a bit, but it's not going anywhere else.? Then Alexis' hair stood almost on end. ?Good? She saw the pistol being pulled out of the woman's jacket. It was small, a .38 or similar caliber. A silencer was on the barrel for good measure. Alexis watched as three flashes went from the gun's barrel, and three times, Gray's body rocked, and fluid flew from it. He dropped to his knees as one of the men spoke out. ?Take care of him. Make sure he's down for the count. He's paranormal, so be careful. I'll take care of Ms. Day.? Ms. Day? The Society, the Den, her old bosses. They'd run into them in Washington DC. But now they were out here. They had just run them off the road! They had just shot Gray! She turned to bolt away, head off into the woods. Over by the other truck, the woman and another man looked down at Gray who was only on his knees, looking down at the three holes in his chest. The man was holding a tire iron in his hands. ?Hmm, look like you got some car trouble. Maybe I have the tools to help you right out.? He pulled back to swing... Alexis was running, but the dirt was loose and slippery in her sandals. She hit a rock and fell down to the ground hard. The man came up right behind her. She turned and looked at him. It was late, almost 9:30 PM, but up there it was just getting to sunset. She instantly recognized the man who had been on the receiving end of Gray's warning at the mall in D.C. ?So, Ms. Day. Looks like it's time for you to get your final absolution. Are you prepared to be judged in the eyes of your Lord?? She didn't answer him. She started instead looking around for natural weapons to use. She saw him reach into his coat... he'd have a gun like the one woman had used on Gray. ?Now, you know my dear, the deepest and darkest, and coldest circle of hell is reserved for traitors such as yourself. I hope you're looking forward to it.? He pulled out the gun. Another small one with the silencer. She continued to look around for something to use. But she knew he had the upper hand. She felt like a damsel in distress instead of a trained killer, just like him. ?Yeah, and you get that from a fantasy book printed thousands of years after Jesus was teaching. It holds as much relevance as Dr. Suess does.? A sound from the other direction... something animalistic? The man turned his head, just what she needed. She lashed out with her foot and caught him in the wrist. He almost dropped his gun. Her other foot went for the sweep and caught him just right to put him on his ass. That time he dropped the gun. She then got up and bolted, lunged actually, for a large fallen tree branch. If he wanted a fight he was going to get one. He stood, the gun back in his hand though. Alexis kicked out, sending her shoes at him, the sandal flats were not good on this ground. He ducked them easily, and then brought the gun up to try to aim at her. And then something landed in between them... The body of the woman who had been with him. She was bloody all over, and it looked like she'd met up with something that had decided that her entrails needed a good inspection from the inside out. Her mouth opened as she then reached for the man who was obviously in charge, but only a gurgling noise came from it. ?What the hell?? The man said, loudly as he turned to look in the direction the woman's body had come from. Alexis dropped the branch as she saw it. From the direction of Gray's truck walked... it was walking, yes... a large beast. The snout was long, like a dogs with large eyes of a steel gray color. The fur was of a similar gray color. It's body was tall, powerful. A good nine to ten feet tall, she calculated. It's muzzle and claws were bloodied. She looked over in the distance and saw that the other man was laying near the truck, in a similar state, only not moving. The man pulled the gun up and opened fire... a blotch of blood formed on the beast's body, but it kept coming at him, slowly. He fired again, and again and again. Still it came slowly in his direction. ?Gray?? Alexis addressed it. She knew that he was paranormal. She'd tested a sample of his blood from a cut he'd had when he'd refused the normal baseline stuff for the lab's work. He was right, he wasn't human or vampire, so it wouldn't have done her much good to have his blood. Now she saw what her guardian really was. So did the man who now had an empty clip. He threw the gun at the humanoid animal and then ran in the direction of the two vehicles. Alexis could only shake her head as she thought about this man's idiocy in thinking he'd outrun such an animal. And then what she saw told her that he was about to face the same fate as his two colleagues. As quickly as he'd started running, the animal shifted form in mid step. It went from a large, almost ten foot tall monstrous wolf man, to a not so large, and very sleek four legged wolf. The beast used that form to catch up to the man by the time they were back by Gray's truck and then it leaped at him. In mid air the beast shifted form again, taking on that huge powerful form again... and slammed the man into the busted and bent up hood of Gray's Suburban. Alexis heard his rib cage collapse under the weight and impact of the blow. As the man then started to slide down, the beast took on a much more soothing form for her, as Grayson Stiqayu Brand once again took his natural born shape, that of the large man who Alexis knows as her partner and guardian at this time. Although for the second time in as many days, he was as naked as a jay bird in front of her. Gray just looked at the man as blood came up from his lungs and erupted through his mouth. ?I shouldn't have let you off easy the last time, asshole.? Alexis didn't care at the moment, she ran over to him. ?Gray, are you okay? They... they shot you?? Gray turned around as she came over. ?Yeah, they used the wrong fucking kind of bullets. Good thing they three aren't going to get any reports out either. Cause that would just be majorly messed up.? He was angry, it showed. He held out his hand to her and deposited a handful of small caliber bullets. He then walked away, towards where he'd been attacked before. He reached down and pulled what was left of his pants up from the ground and retrieved his cell phone. He didn't bother to put it to his ears as he was telling the phone to dial. ?Dial Annie!? The speaker phone made the audible noises associated with dialing a digital phone, for aesthetic purposes only, of course. It was answered quickly enough. ?Gray? Is tha' ye? Why are ye callin' this late?? He pulled the back door to his Suburban open and started pulling out their travel bags. ?Got a situation Annie. You have the location of this phone?? ?Yes, why?? ?I've got a busted truck, and three dead assholes here. The Den just tried to pull a hit on Alexis by running us off the road.? Annie didn't wait. ?Is Alexis alrigh'?? ?Yeah. Head asshole was the one I ran into in DC and gave the warning too. He's been neutralized as well as two others. Male and female for those. Their vehicle looks like a rental. I'm going to need to get it off the road some.? ?Alrigh', were they any trouble?? He shook his head, even though she couldn't see it. ?No, but how quickly you think you can have a sweeper team here?? ?Yer close t' Seattle. I could dispatch them as a rescue unit.? ?I need a tow truck too. My truck is toast.? ?Give me a few hours. Try t' clean up so tha' yer safe in th' dark. Don't go anywhere though. Yer in rural area an' after dark it starts t' really get rare fer cars t' come by. That'd be why they 'it there.? ?Not planning on it.? He shut his phone up and handed it to Alexis. ?What's this for?? ?Help me get this stuff out. I don't trust a car with an engine this broken to stay safe. Might be a leak or something. Their truck isn't driveable right now.? She looked over at it, and saw what he was saying. The rear window had been smashed in, from the blood pattern on it, it looked like one of the other bodies had impacted with it. However it would advertise for a police officer that something had gone on. ?I see that. Are you okay?? He looked at her, almost questioning why she'd ask that. ?I'm fine. Shifting like that allows my wounds to get healed up unless they're...? ?Silver.? He nodded. ?You're a werewolf?? ?That's one way to put it. My people call them Skinwalkers. According to Blaise's grandfather, who is one also, as a people they call themselves the Garou.? She did a double take. ?Blaise's grandfather is a... Garou, too?? Gray smiled. ?Yeah, so is Blaise.? Her jaw dropped with that. Gray was working quickly and professionally. He kept wet wipes in his truck for a reason. He was using them to clean the blood off of his body before getting to far. As he did that, Alexis caught herself... leering. ?Take this stuff over there and wait for the sweeper team. There is some blankets and that you can use. I'm going to move their truck off the road and take care of some other stuff. That phone has GPS in it. So they'll know where you are.? ?What about you?? ?I always expect at least two strike teams out of the Order, and expect the same from anyone else unless their idiots. I'm going to go on perimeter guard until they get here.? ?But...? ?No buts, you're my charge and they just tried to get to you. Keep your eyes and ears open. I will to.? At that, he started to walk away and then shifted to the full on lupine form so he'd have a smaller frame and faster build to get around the place. He headed into the woods as he did so. Alexis, silently, listened to him. She took the stuff and headed off towards where she had run to before the killing had started. She spread out the blanket and settled down. The night was silent and no cars pulled by at all. She felt herself doze off a few times, while they waited. Each time she woke up to a pair of steel gray eyes staring at her in the darkness, the gray fur of the lupine made Grayson look like a ghost in the night. Quite a bit later, Gray's cell phone rang to let them know that the tow truck was almost there. At that time she signaled out so that Gray would know and headed back up near the road. Gray, from his vantage point just shook his muzzled head. ?Yeah, what else can they throw into this fire?? ~To Be Continued~
