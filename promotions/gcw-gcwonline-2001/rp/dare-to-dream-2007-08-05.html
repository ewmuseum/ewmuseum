Dare To Dream

5 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/05/07 After several years as a professional wrestler, finally Jimmy Thunder has the chance to compete in his home town of Sydney, New South Wales. Not only is Jimmy competing in the pay-per-view Tsunami show, he is also getting his first ever crack at the GCW United States title. Recognising the enormity of the occasion, the New South Wales Government have spared no expense to mark the occasion, starting of with an official ceremony where thousands of Sydneysiders have assembled at Town Hall, in the heart of the city. Standing on the official dais is New South Wales Premier, Morris Iemma, Sydney Lord Mayor, Clover Moore, Jimmy Thunder as well as his girlfriend Emma and his son Jimmy Junior. Dressed in a stylish Italian suit and wearing sunglasses, Jimmy laps up the warm Sydney sunshine while taking in the electricity from the thousands of fans that have converged to Sydney's Town Hall, to help celebrate Jimmy's career thus far. To signal the start of the ceremony, a band plays Jimmy's entrance theme "Your Disease" by Saliva as Jimmy Junior and Emma make passing jokes to each other which catches Jimmy's attention, who then turns to them with an angry look which instantly stops their smirking. While enjoying the band's performance, Jimmy looks around at the sea of fans that surround the stage and can't help but feel a little humbled by the turn out. When Jimmy left Sydney to start a new life in the United States, nobody knew or cared about him. Several years later all of Sydney knows him and now they're here to show their appreciation for him. Thinking along those lines has put a lump in Jimmy's throat and an immense amount of pride, beating in his heart. The music fades and the fans start cheering and chanting Jimmy's name as the New South Wales Premier, Morris Iemma approaches the podium and raises his hands to try and get the fans to stop chanting so he can talk. After a few moments they go quiet as Morris looks down at his notes. "Time and time again, the state of New South Wales produces world class athletes, exceptional humanitarians and everyday citizens who excel at giving their heart to their fellow man during their time of need. It is not everyday we get the opportunity to recognise the special achievements from our people in their chosen fields, especially when one of our very own makes the rare trip home. On behalf of the State Government, we welcome home one of Sydney's favourite sons and as Premier of New South Wales, please join in with me as I present him with the key to the city, to the and only, Jimmy Thunder!" The crowd erupts into a loud roaring cheer as Jimmy steps forward and shakes Premier Iemma's hand and accepts the key to his hometown, Sydney Australia. Looking at the key, Jimmy is awestruck at it's beauty and charisma. Instantly Jimmy feels a connection between himself and the key and values it just as much as his World Championship belts. He raises it up to his lips and quickly kisses it as the fans continue their cheering while Jimmy takes a quick glance at Emma and Jimmy Junior who are eagerly applauding as well. Jimmy stands at the podium for a while and smiles at all the adulation's from the fans until it eventually dies down. "Growing up in this beautiful city is a privilege that not many people have the chance to experience. After travelling and competing in cities from around the world, you start to realise how true it is that Sydney is the best city in the world. When I was kid running around the streets of Sydney, I felt comfortable because I knew I was home and when I came back here yesterday, I was overwhelmed by the same feeling, it's something you only get when you hold something close to your heart. In my eyes, it doesn't matter where I live, I will never experience the warmth and the safety of city like what Sydney gives to me. I'd like to thank some people who have made this proud moment possible, firstly I'd like to thank Premier Morris Iemma and Sydney Lord Mayor Clover Moore, who have made this special event possible, my girlfriend and son who made the trip out here to be with me during this proud moment and most importantly, I'd like to thank you guys, the fans. Not just for coming out here today but for being there for me my whole life, the way you guys open your arms to me is something I'll cherish forever. Thank you" The crowd starts cheering again as Jimmy lifts the key up in the air as both Emma and Jimmy Junior step forward and give Jimmy a hug. He then shakes hands with Morris Iemma and Clover Moore to thank them for organising the ceremony. Instead of walking off the stage towards the back area into a secured section of Town Hall, he jumps off the front of the stage and heads to the fence where the fans are and starts shaking hands and hugging them as well as signing autographs for what seems like an eternity for the security, who are on hand to ensure pandemonium doesn't break out. The Day of the Tsunami Show It is several hours before Jimmy is scheduled to leave his Sydney hotel, Jimmy is sitting in his hotel room trying to eat his lunch before a pre-organised live interview takes place inside his hotel room. Jimmy plays with his food by using his fork to move it around the plate as his mental preparation for his first shot at the GCW United States title are in motion. Having been in countless World Title matches, Jimmy has been in more high pressure situations that he dare poke a stick at but he can't remember being under as much pressure as he is now. The pressure he feels isn't from any outside influences or media hype, quite frankly, Jimmy has surprisingly been able to keep his head out of all the local printed and televised news, which was by no accident. Realising that public expectations from his hometown crowd would be the hardest form of pressure to to withstand, Jimmy has deliberately avoided all news media contact and has rarely spoken to any public figures about his chances at the Tsunami show. Apart from making several appearances for charity and a variety show hosted one Rove McManus, one of Australia's funniest talk show hosts, Jimmy has mainly stayed out of the public eye. He'd use the gym that is inside the hotel and swim countless laps of the hotel pool to keep his fitness level up. At several stages during the day and night, Jimmy would sneak out of his hotel with Emma and Jimmy Junior to show them all around Sydney, mainly including famous landmarks are and where he grew up on the streets. The interview from Channel 9's main television journalist/personality, Eddie McGuire, Jimmy will get his first taste at what all the hype surrounding his Tsunami United States title match with Shaman is like. Jimmy hears a knock on the door and opens it to find Eddie standing there with his camera crew with him. After being deep in thought for what seemed like only several moments, it was then that he realised that he had lost more time that what he initially realised and his preparations for his match later tonight had well and truly begun. Once all the introductions had finished the camera crew went into full swing to set their equipment up as Eddie gives Jimmy a basic rundown on what kind of questions he will be directing towards Jimmy. While the camera crew are setting up, Jimmy seizes the opportunity to freshen up and change his clothes so that he can prepare himself mentally for what shapes up to be one of his toughest interviers ever. Wearing his own Jimmy Thunder t-shirt, Jimmy walks out of his room and takes a seat opposite Eddie and tries to relax for the interview. The Interview That Stopped A Nation Eddie: Hello everyone and welcome to a very special exclusively live Channel 9 production, I'm sitting here with the man who has the dreams of an entire nation riding on his shoulders, Jimmy Thunder, thank you for taking time out for what can be one of the biggest nights in Australian history. Jimmy: Thanks mate, hopefully the result goes the way everyone wants. Eddie: What's it like being back home in Sydney and competing at Telstra Stadium that will be jam packed to the rafters? Jimmy: It is overwhelming to be honest, I'm getting goose bumps now knowing that I'll be competing in the same stadium where other Aussie athletes have won world titles in their chosen sport, gold medals during the 2000 Olympics and where our soccer team beat Uruguay to secure their spot in last year's Soccer World Cup. Eddie: Do feel any added pressure from the fans who are expecting you to win? Jimmy: No I dont Eddie, I've tried my hardest to keep away from media hype and speculation but knowing that it's not everyday that I get to come here and face someone like Shaman in a pay-per-view event for one of the most famous titles in professional wrestling history then the only pressure you get is from yourself and containing that pressure is something that I'm dealing with at the moment. Eddie: I bet the fans waiting outside and along the streets wanting to wish you well doesn't add to the pressure as well. Jimmy: Holy smoke! I knew there were fans outside but I didn't know they were lined up along the streets too. This is just insane. I'm totally awestruck right now. Eddie: Do you know why you've become such a huge fan favourite here in Australia? Jimmy: I have no idea, traditionally, Australians usually rally to support our own but the support I'm getting right now is something I had never imagined in my wildest dreams. Eddie: Tonight when you get into the ring with Shaman, which Jimmy Thunder are we going to see? Jimmy: Right now my adrenaline is running on high emotions and I have to contain that before I climb into the ring with Shaman. It's easier said than done but if I can bottle it up and climb into the ring as the Jimmy Thunder I was before I came home, then I'll be in with a fighting chance. Eddie: Jimmy, I know you're just about to start preparing for the match, what thoughts will you take with you into the ring? Jimmy: I'm going to go there with thoughts of winning, not just for myself and all my hometown fans that have taken the time to show their affections towards me, but I'll be going there with my mind set to win for every Aussie kid who dares to dream. Eddie: In case you haven't realised, the entire nation will come to a stand still and rise as one when you walk into Telstra Stadium and walk towards the ring and do battle with Shaman for the GCW United States title. Thank you Jimmy and good luck. Jimmy: I just want all of Australia to know that I'll be giving them my best and I would personally like to thank everyone who has cheered for me and let them know that their love and support is something I will cherish till the day I die.
