AXEL in Time- Chapter IV: Love In An Iron Maiden(a.k.a. A Love That Transcends Time)

1 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1, 2009 "A glooming peace, this morning with it brings. The sun, for sorrow, will not show his head. Go hence, to have more talk of these sad things; Some shall be pardoned, and some punished; For never was a story of more woe than this of Juliet and her Romeo." ? Excerpt from William Shakespeare’s Romeo and Juliet. -- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- London, England: 1600 A.D., the era of such noteworthy people as William Shakespeare, Christopher Marlowe, Francis Drake, John Hawkins, Walter Raleigh and last but not least, Queen Elizabeth I, herself. It was a time of uprising for the Protestant faith as people not only tried to break away from the Church of England, but from England itself. This time period was known as the Protestant Reformation, and it is thought to have begun in 1517 A.D. with Martin Luther’s Ninety-Five Theses but is also considered to have ended in 1648 A.D. with the Peace of Westphalia. The movement started as an attempt to reform the Catholic Church, because many Catholics showed great distaste in what they thought were false doctrines and malpractices within the church, such as the teaching and sale of indulgences. The churches were also practicing the sale of positions inside the ranks, which is also known as simony. Well, that’s what the history books will tell you, but what the history books don’t know is that history has become subject to change. Having been stuck in Elizabethan England for several weeks, the man known as The Unholy One had become quite accustomed to the ways of the people in Britain; dressing and talking like them, even sharing some of their mannerisms. Deciding it was best that he not don his face paint in this era for fear of being considered a witch or heretic of some sort, he actually blended in much better than would be expected of someone with a personality such as his. Always the type to harbor feelings of intense hatred and murderous intent towards his fellow man, his heart has never had room for such trivial emotions as happiness or love. He’s never shown camaraderie towards any man or woman, with only two exceptions’his older brother, Ace, and his best friend, James von Doom. However, due to his defiant nature and lust for competition, he has found himself in the midst of uprising, of revolution; of the Protestant Reformation! Because of his drive and dedication to the cause, the revolutionaries even adopted AXEL as their leader. The truth of the matter is that AXEL really couldn’t care less about the “cause”. For the first time in his life, he was in love, and as sick as it makes his stomach feel, he kind of enjoys the feeling. Alysea, one of the Queen’s chamber maidens has caught his eye and captured his heart. She’s the fairest lady in all of England with her chestnut brown hair and eyes so blue that they almost look like a couple of crystals embedded in her skull. Not to mention the fact that she has a body that is built for sin, and sin, she does when it comes to AXEL. They don’t call AXEL “The Deflowerer” for nothing. He’s quite well known for taking the innocence of virgins without even the slightest hint of a second thought about it. Though with Alysea, or Aly, as he so affectionately calls her, it’s different. That’s why he’s taken the responsibility of leading the uprising’to impress his precious Alysea. The Queen, whom created the Church of England wasn’t happy with this uprising at all, and had decreed that any and all members of it would be executed swiftly and justly. This made things quite a bit difficult for the blossoming romance between AXEL and the lovely Alysea. Luckily for the two of them, The Unholy One doesn’t fear death. Besides, what’s the Queen going to do? Behead him? If the Queen knew that AXEL rather stealthily sneaks into Alysea’s bedroom each night, the consequences would be dire. Their plan is always the same: When the hour strikes Midnight, AXEL sneaks past the guards and scales the wall leading to Alysea’s bedroom. On one particular night, things don’t go as planned. Everything is fine at first, with AXEL sneaking around the castle courtyard, hiding behind and in the shrubbery. At one point, he even pretends to be a fountain fixture, hoping that the patrolling guards won’t notice him and figure him out. Finally, he makes it up the wall leading to her window and into her room. By the time he gets inside, she’s already asleep, so he peels the sheets back and slides into bed with her and places a soft kiss on her forehead. "Sleep well, my sweet?", he says softly, curling up next to her before drifting off to sleep, himself. All is well until a knock comes at the door. The two lovebirds awaken and begin scrambling to find someplace for AXEL to hide. Finally deciding on the standalone closet in the corner, AXEL opens the door and hops in, pulling it closed behind him. There’s another knock on the door, this on much louder than the one previous. "Wait a moment! I’m getting dressed!" "Alright, Lady Alysea; do try and be quick, though. The Queen needs you." "Why would she need my assistance at this time of the night? She’s usually asleep by now." AXEL can overhear the entire conversation from within the closet, and he knows that making an escape is going to be difficult. Whenever one of the Queen’s attendants is being summoned for, there are always guards, en masse, with them. Suddenly, AXEL feels a tickling sensation within his sinus cavity; the first sign of a sneezing fit. He takes a deep breath, placing his finger underneath his nose and pressing up to try and relieve the annoying tickling feeling, but to no avail. "Ah?!" "What was that?!" The guard looks accusingly at Alysea who simply giggles and shrugs her shoulders. "That was me. I thought I was going to sneeze, but it was a false alarm." AXEL breathes a sigh of relief as she takes the blame for the sudden noise. He knows that if the guard thinks it was her, then he won’t attempt to investigate. Then, all he’ll have to do is wait until the coast is clear and make a break for it. His nose begins to tingle again, and this time, he breathes in deep, the breaths escaping in short, audible gasps. "Ah?! Ah?! Aaaaah-CHOO!!!" As he sneezes, he stumbles forward, falling flat of his face on the floor. The sound of the thud when his chin makes contact with the hard, stone floor of the bedroom alerts the guard. "What was that?!" "I-It was nothing! I promise!" AXEL rolls on his side, trying to get under the bed and out of view as the Alysea stretches her body in an attempt to block the guard’s view. The guard simply moves her aside, only to spot AXEL who is trying to squeeze himself into the small space beneath Aly’s bed. It’s times like these that he curses himself for ever picking up a barbell. "Run away, AXEL! If he catches you, he’ll take you to the Queen and she’ll have you killed!" By now, AXEL’s already halfway under the bed and is pretty much stuck, unable to get himself free. It doesn’t stop him from struggling to get out from his rather compromising position. By the time he manages to get himself unstuck, it’s already too late because when he turns around, he’s got the sharp tip of a spear pointing right between his eyes. Eyes widen and jaws drop as hands go up above his head as if showing he has no intentions of doing anything rash. "Whoa, easy does it, big man. I only came here to see her.", says AXEL pointing over at Alysea. "We’re in love, after all. You wouldn’t ruin that for us, would you?" The guard lowers his spear, stroking his chin as if he’s thinking. It appears, for the moment, that AXEL’s words have gotten through to the man. Considering his options, the guard raises his spear again, shaking his head and laughing. "It’s either you or me, boy, and if I were to let you go and the Queen found out that I let a member of the uprising go free, she’d have MY head." AXEL’s eyes shift from left to right like he’s looking for something, and he does the only thing anyone in their right mind would do. He makes a mad dash for Alysea’s balcony and in a feat of pure athleticism; he steps onto the stone railing and leaps, falling into the moat below. He surfaces, spluttering and coughing water up out of his lungs, only to look up, sighing in disappointment and rolling his eyes as five different spears are pointed at him. "Goddamn it!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Coming to his senses a few hours later, he finds himself chained to a wall inside a prison cell in the castle’s dungeon; head throbbing, vision blurred. The guards weren’t real friendly with AXEL as they were hauling him into custody. Then again, he DID struggle and manage to disarm one of the guards. He would have gotten away easily if he’d only been more careful and watched his back. One wouldn’t think so, but the shafts of those spears pack a hell of a wallop. "Ungh? Where am I?" "You’re in the castle’s dungeon, lad." Shaking his head to clear the cobwebs, he finally gets a good look at his surroundings. Dank, dusty and dark; the dungeon wasn’t a very welcoming place for visitors, much less prisoners. The smell of rotting flesh assaulted AXEL’s nose, causing his lips to curl in disgust as his eyes scan the room for the source of the scent. Over in the far corner rests a pile of decomposing human remains which upon sight, causes The Unholy One to shudder. "My God! What have they done?!" Faced with a slew of new emotions that he isn’t used to feeling; emotions such as sympathy, guilt and overall distaste for the horrid acts that had been committed within that dungeon, he can’t help but burst into tears. As his past misdeeds replay in his mind, he tries to stifle those emotions only to fail miserably. Overwhelmed by his feelings, he begins to sob loudly. "How could they do this? What gives them the right?!" "They’re in charge, my boy. They can do as they wish. That poor fellow over there was tortured to death right here in this cell for stealing a loaf of bread from the market. I knew him rather well, and he didn’t have the gold to pay for it with. He was only doing what he needed to do to feed his family." AXEL’s eyes finally manage to affix themselves to the man who addressed him. His clothes were ratty and torn, beard and hair matted with blood and pus from infected wounds. His body is dirty, bruised and battered; ribs showing from lack of sustenance. His skin holds no pigmentation, completely pale from being locked in the cold, dark dungeon for what appears to have been months. AXEL’s heart aches with sympathy for this man, and his curiosity gets the better of him. "Why are you in here? What did you do that was so bad to cause them to treat you like nothing more than a common street rat?" "Well, my boy; I was the leader of the Protestant uprising here in London. My family and I were on our way to the theater to see William Shakespeare’s ?Romeo and Juliet? when I was accosted and my family was murdered by the Queen’s guards right before my very eyes. Apparently, one of my "comrades" took a bribe from the Queen and told her where I was going to be. That was six months ago. I’ve been in this rat infested shithole ever since. What about you? What crime did you commit?" AXEL manages to pull himself together, though his cheeks are still stained with tears. All he can think about at this moment is Alysea and what could happen to her if the Queen were to find out about them. "The only thing I’m guilty of is being in love. If being in love is a crime, then I’ll gladly face the axe of the executioner. My dear Alysea? The queen’s going to kill her if I don’t find a way out of here. You see, I’m the man who took over as leader of the insurrection once you were thrown in here. They found me in her bedroom, where she and I had been sleeping. I was hidden in the closet until I sneezed and fell out. I made an attempt to escape using the moat, but they caught me, and here I am." The other man simply shakes his head, dropping his gaze to the floor. AXEL starts to get the feeling that the man knows something he doesn’t. "I’m afraid it may already be too late, my boy. The Queen doesn’t waste any time having those who cross her executed. I’m sorry." "N-no! She can’t b?" That’s all AXEL can manage to utter before he is cut off by the sound of the dungeon’s door swinging open and slamming into the wall. In walks a big, burly, shirtless male. His physique is rather doughy, and he appears as if he could do well with a shaving of his chest and back. Over his head is a piece of black, opaque cloth eyeholes cut in it for visibility and a hole for his nose so that he can breathe. As he makes his way into the dungeon, he makes a beeline straight for AXEL. He draws back and catches The Unholy One flush on the jaw with a right hook. "It’s time to go, heretic. I’m going to rid London of every single one of your kind if it’s the last thing I do." The executioner unfastens AXEL’s chains, causing him to slump to his knees on the floor. Being a little addled and woozy from the shot to his jaw, AXEL is having trouble gathering his wits about him. "Come on, now! Don’t make me drag you. It won’t be pleasant for you, I assure!" The hooded man wraps his fingers in AXEL’s hair and snatches him to his feet rather unceremoniously. Sliding a coil of rope down off of his shoulder, he uses it to tie AXEL’s hands behind his back. Grabbing hold of the rope and pulling up on it, he takes full control of the body of the Unholy One and begins leading him to the place of his public execution. Deciding that it is best that he doesn’t struggle, AXEL simply does as he’s told, taking a moment to look over his shoulder and give one last glance at the poor, mistreated fellow behind him. He opens his mouth to say goodbye, but isn’t able to due to being shoved out of the door. "Hey! There’s no need to shove me. I wasn’t trying to struggle or escape." The executioner doesn’t say a word, but instead prods at AXEL and sends him onwards. Trekking down the castle’s long main corridor, the duo exits the front gates of the castle. From there, armed guards escort him up onto the execution platform and force him onto his knees, body resting on the chopping block so that his head hung off the edge over a wicker basket below. With his hands tied behind his back, there isn’t much he can do to shield himself from the angry townspeople who are shouting obscenities and throwing items such as eggs and rotten vegetables at him. "HERETIC!" "Go back to Hell where you belong, Lucifer!" AXEL grunts in agony as a potato collides with the side of his head, but he doesn’t cry out, for fear of showing weakness. He takes the same approach with them as he would with a bully. In other words, just like with bullies, if AXEL shows any signs of weakness, the angry mob of people gathered in the town square to watch him die will eat him alive. The chant of, "LUCIFER! LUCIFER! LUCIFER!", begins to ring out into the air as the executioner, axe in hand, climbs the steps of the platform and approaches the chopping block. "AXEL Action, you have been charged with the crimes of holding unauthorized assemblies and heresy; you have been sentenced to immediate execution. Have you any final words before your sentence is carried out?" AXEL closes his eyes and takes a deep breath, thinking back on all those passionate nights he shared with his sweet Alysea. He can’t help but smile happily as he reflects on everything they had done together out of their love for each other. Snapping out of his trancelike state, AXEL shifts his gaze up to look out over the crowd of people, then to the executioner himself. "Charged with crimes of unauthorized assemblies and heresy? Do you honestly think I give a damn about the “cause” or about this uprising? The only reason I ever became a part of it is because I thought it would impress the woman that I love. If I’m guilty of anything, it’s being in love. If being in love with the lovely Lady Alysea is a crime, then go ahead and kill me. I can promise you one thing, though. You’ll be killing a man who hasn’t truly committed any crime." A hush falls over the crowd of angry people as they glance around at each other, the executioner and at AXEL. The people mutter and mumble, arguing amongst themselves as to whether or not he is who he’s being accused of. From out of nowhere, a brick flies at AXEL’s head and just barely misses. "KILL THE HERETIC!" This gets the crowd riled up once more, and the executioner lifts a hand to silence them all. Once the crowd quiets down, the executioner lifts his axe above his head, preparing to swing. "By the power vested in me by the Queen of England, I will now carry out your sent?" "NO! STOP IT RIGHT NOW!" Alysea manages to make her way past the guards and onto the platform where the execution is to take place. She throws herself on top of AXEL and cradles him into her arms, holding his head against her chest. He struggles against her, knowing that if she interferes like she’s doing now, things won’t turn out well for either of them. "Aly, what are you doing? If you don’t leave, they’ll have you killed, too. There’s no sense in both of us getting killed over this. Besides, I don’t belong here, anyway. I? Well, it’s difficult to explain. Let’s just say I’m not from England." "You’d better listen to your little lover, girly. I’ve got no problem taking your pretty little head, too.", says the executioner, sliding the tips of his fingers over the edge of his axe, the razor-sharp blade splitting the flesh, spilling his blood. Alysea shakes her head, clutching AXEL tighter to her body, practically refusing to let this go on any longer. "I don’t care where you’re from, AXEL. All that matters is that you’re here, and I refuse to let them murder you like this when you’ve done nothing wrong. Besides, there’s something I need to tell you." AXEL’s eyes grow wide as those last few words echo inside of his mind. What could she possibly have to tell him at an inappropriate time such as this? He shifts his gaze upwards, tilting his head a bit curiously as he awaits her words. "I’m pregnant." AXEL’s jaw drops, his eyes still wide with amazement. The executioner lowers his axe, seemingly stunned by the announcement that was just made. The crowd continues to yell and cry out for the death of the “heretic”. The loving couple ignores the shouts of the crowd and focuses only on each other. The executioner drops his axe and leaves the platform, shaking his head. "AXEL?" "I’m sorry; you caught me off-guard for a moment. I can almost swear that you said you’re pregnant. Is that what you said?" Alysea nods, smiling as she unties the rope that binds AXEL’s arms. However, before she can finish untying him, a gaping black vortex rips a hole in the sky. Gazing upwards, AXEL notices the portal and sighs sadly. He presses an affectionate kiss to the lips of his love before getting to his feet. "I have to go. I can’t stay here any longer. If I do, your life will be in constant danger. Why don't you to come with me? There’s no knowing for sure where we’ll end up, but sooner or later we’ll end up back in my time. We’ll both be safe there." "I can't leave, can I? I mean, the Queen needs me. Though without you, I'd be completely miserable. I just don't know if I could live if I were to lose you." AXEL stalls for as long as he can before the portal begins to close. He takes Alysea’s hand in his own and gives it a little squeeze as he backs towards the rift. With each step he makes, Alysea takes a step as well as if following him in. "Please, Aly, come with me. We'll make a life for ourselves in my time; just you, me and our child. Apart from that, after everything we've been through, it'd just be a shame for our love to end because of a difference in time periods. Please, don't make me go back alone." Having lulled him into a false sense of security, she takes one final look around and nods before stepping up to the rift. Thinking everything is fine and that she's going to be right behind him, AXEL steps inside. Once he's in, Alysea makes the ULTIMATE sacrifice by letting go of his hand. She watches, in tears, as the portal seals itself shut behind him, separating her from the man she loves for the rest of eternity. As much as it hurts her to be without him, she knows in her heart that she did the right thing; that what she did was for his safety and well-being. "Farewell, my dearest AXEL. I'm sorry..." By the time AXEL realizes she isn't with him, it's far too late to go back for her. Heartbroken and in tears over the fact that the only woman he's ever loved betrayed him (or so he thinks), he continues his journey through time, knowing that he has to press onward no matter what happens. Just as the love between the two star-crossed lovers, Romeo and Juliet, was immeasurable when compared to the love of others; so too, is the love of AXEL and his precious Alysea. The love he feels for Alysea will never die, nor will he forget the time that they spent together; but recent events have brought him face to face with one simple truth: Time waits for no one.
