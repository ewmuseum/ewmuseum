Best laid plans

1 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01 Sept 2005 Is it like a breach of confidence to have your best friends boyfriend professionally executed? Well hell if he deserved it then no, I say no it isn’t. Although it might put a strain on your friendship, perhaps even end it, perhaps even cost you a few nights sleep but hey, you can’t make an omelette without cracking a few eggs. At least that’s what Mary’s best friend Skye had told herself over and over again ever since seeing Mary in hospital after her and LeStatt’s lil fall out. Skye couldn’t help being protective of her friend, you would to if you’d shared what they had shared for the best part of their teens. Mary had been having just as many sleepless nights as Skye ever since her run in with Shina back in Mexico, not to mention worrying about the future for her and Roberto. The nightmares were back, nightmares about her Uncle and what he’d done to her. What he was still doing was sometimes worse. It’s a lot harder to fight a ghost than it is a real live person. A lot harder. Mary was just finishing up her workout at the gym while Arael stood guard as always. He hadn’t left her side since the whole Roberto-burning building affair. Somewhere deep inside he seemed to take part responsibility and he stood sentry, in silence by the door. Mary wound down with a tumbling part-martial arts routine then free weights and a session on the kick bag before stretching out. Possibly excessive but she knew zodiac was no push over. The woman was damn near untouchable back in 3wa and Mary hadn’t forgotten, not at all. Mary sat now panting and stretching out while glancing over at Arael whoes gaze was firmly set on the door. Mary raised and eyebrow. “You quiet Eddy. Summing wrong” Mary stank?” Arael didn’t move apart from shaking his head and keeping his gaze on the door. Mary got up and walked over to him. “Hey stone groove, you been all quiet Tyson for a while now, Mary notices ya know. Are you mad at her?” Arael’s broad shoulders moved up and down in exhalation. His shaded eyes looked down at her but his arms stayed folded across his chest. “No Chica, I’m not mad at you.” “Then why ain’t ya talky McTalk anymore. You getting to be as boring as Levi lately Mister.” Arael almost smiled. “That’s not such a bad thing Mary. Leviath gets the job done, he never loses a client and he never gets distracted. I would do well to act more like him.” Mary’s brow furrowed. “Well fine then you be like Levi and Mary be like Mary and we all be one big happy freaking none talking family, how bout that?” She pouted and went to collect her gym bag from across the room. Arael felt bad. “Don’t be like that Mary. I’m just trying to do my job. I can’t risk losing another client and after Mexico and Roberto, I’ve been coming a little too close for my own liking. I’ve been lacks with you, I’ve allowed myself to be distracted to break rules and boundaries that could get us both killed. I wont have your blood on my hands too Mary, I can’t have that.” Mary turned and looked at him sympathetically. Of course he couldn’t see the expression on her face but he could hear it in her voice just as well. “That what this is about” You working twice as hard cause ya thinks ya gonna get Mary hurt?” Arael shrugged in a perhaps kinda way. “Well listen up busta, Mary ain’t going nowhere and she can damn near take care of herself mister. Don’t you start beating yaself up over shiznit you cant change. We’ll be just fine. Besides, Mary’s kinda gotta prollem with watching you die too. You’re like familia now, and Mary don’t got much of that going on so she ain’t letting go once she finds it.” Arael looked away from her and back at the door. “Maybe that was my biggest fear Chica. This was, is, a business arrangement and now, now I’m so worried about looking after you that my judgement is clouded. I cant afford to have any of my other senses dampened after losing my sight.” Mary walked over to him and reached out to touch his hand before pulling back. “Mary unnerstands. Listen if you need to be a lil distanty then Mary won’t complain anymore okies?” “Obrigado Mary.” “back at ya eddy.” That instant Mary’s cell phone rang playing Sean Michael’s theme tune. She sang along for the first few bars then answered the phone. “Holaa?” Her cousin Kagerro was on the other line. “Mary” Shit are you ok, you didn’t call when you got back home and shit and when I called ya phone didn’t pick up. I’ve been freaking out here?” Kagerro switched into Japanese without even noticing and then slipped between Japanese and Chinese. She most of the time only did that when she was overly stressed. Mary tried to decipher what she was saying but her Japanese wasn’t nearly good enough to keep up. “Jigga huh” Slow down Missy, Mary can’t keep up.” “Sorry, look are you knocked up or what?” “No Mary ain’t gotta pie in her oven.” Kagerro audibly exhaled on the other end of the line. “Thank god. I know how fucked up that would be if you were Ty. Oh I almost forgot. Harvester and me, we gotta leave Mexico like next week at the latest. Maybe this weekend. HSW died on us but this federation AW seem to dig us so we got contracts there. Only thing is, it’s in Canada but at least I get to put some distance between me and the Six for a little while.” Mary remained silent and took all the information in before speaking. “Woah’err’okies. But eww Canadia” You’ll get stampeded by a freaking mounted bear. Mary seen gentle ben damnit!” “Firstly, I doubt mounties in Canada ride bears, although I might like Canada more if they did. Secondly, I’m not even sure who gentle ben is. I don’t think we got that back home. Thirdly, I’ll be fine. It’s gonna be a lil chilly and shit but apart from that we’re good to go. The Six have been real quiet lately so I think it’s a good time to leave. I’ll be in touch in a when we get there and give you the address and stuff. Cool?” Mary nodded. “Sure thing dingaling.” “Right I gotta go. Oh and PS, If Knight touches you like that again. He’s a dead man!” The line went dead, no goodbye, no anything just a threat and an empty line. Mary put her phone down too. “That was Kagerro, she says hey. Well she dint say hey, she said she might be killing Statty if they get into it like they did again but apart from that she’s peachy.” Arael resisted the urge to say “join the line.” Instead they headed home, it was getting late and Mary had her first match in GCW the next day. It would be no walkover, they both knew it. They got back to Mary’s apartment which is really just a converted fire station. Skye was waiting by her car out front. Once inside Mary went to have a shower which left Skye and Arael alone. Skye had her arms folded and turned to Arael. “Well?” “Well what Miss Skye.” “Don’t play nao intelegente con mim Eduardo. You know exactly what I mean.” Arael eased his back in a standing subtle stretch. “I am at your disposal Miss Skye, you and your father.” Skye looked at him quizzically. “But you still think I’m wrong?” “I’m not paid to analyse your behaviour Miss Skye.” Skye was becommign frustrated. She knew what he was doing. When he acted like a friend, like someone with an opinion, something to say, she busted him back down to employee. Instead of risking insobordination or worse, Arael had decided to stay where he had been put. “Ok Eddie I get it. Quit being difficult, I want to know what you think. No repercussions.” Arael raised an eyebrow from behind his dark glasses. “Non?” Skye shook her head and folded her arms, “Sim, non.” “Very well. I think you’re being rash. I think that perhaps your protectiveness over Mary is clouding your judgement. I believe that Miss Mary knows what she is doing here, and I know she is too strong to let any man abuse her ever again. What happened between her and Senor Abitua was, is unfortunate but I’ve seen men driven to worse by less. He seems to be going through some adjustments of late and only Mary will really truly understand what or why this is.” “You don’t think he should pay for what he’s done” You don’t think that Mary has had enough turmoil from bitchass men in her life to last her an eternity? You don’t think seeing her lying there bloody and beating warrants his fucking life Eddie. You honestly didn’t want to crush him while he lay unconscious on that grass? You didn’t want to throw him back into the fire and save Mary anymore hurt?” Skye was livid now but Arael was still calm. “Sim Miss Skye I wished to do all of those things but this was before I spoke to Mary and to be truthful, I believe that if Senor Abitua dies, you will be punishing Mary, not him.” Skye was silenced by Mary’s footsteps skipping down the hall upstairs and her voice yelling that she’ll be in her trailer if anyone needs her.. Skye and Arael were like statues. Skye leaned over to him. “He will be punished and Mary will survive Arael. I want him gone. Tonight!” “I told you before Miss Skye, I need the bosses say so.” Skye pulled her cell phone out of her purse. “And you will have it.” She dialled a number and waited. “Hola pai” conversa a Arael.” She handed Arael the phone. “Sim Senhor” Sim eu posso. Hoje ? noite? Sim naturalmente. Muito bem. Sim, ser? feito.” Arael handed the phone back. “Very well Miss Skye. It will be taken care of but who will guard Mary?” “I’ll stay with her. Wait a few hours, I want her asleep when you leave. Don’t take too long. I bought your tools with me, they’re in the car.” Arael nodded while Skye turned and went upstairs. He really didn’t want to have to do this but an order was an order and he was an assassin first and bodyguard second. He switched into work mode and just like that, Roberto Abitua aka LeStatt Knight became just another faceless target. It was getting close to 1am and Mary was no asleep. Skye had stayed with her until she fell asleep and then given Arael the nod to leave. He did so with a black metal case in his right hand. He had a driver come pick him up and take him to where he needed to be. If it meant planes, trains or automobiles, he had it at his disposal, wherever LeStatt was he could find him within a few hours. Arael climbed the fire escape of a prestigious building across the street from the hotel that LeStatt Knight was renting a room in. He reached the top in record time without breaking a sweat. Skye sat watching Mary sleep. She’d pulled on of LeStatt’s old over sized dress shirts and snuggled down into it like a life line. Arael sat by the edge of the ledge of the building on near by discarded wooden box and opened his case. He took out a pair of infra red binoculars, and 5 pieces of a sniper rifle complete with silencer. He attached a hands free device to his cell phone and put the ear piece on. Meanwhile Skye watched Mary sleep on. She lay down beside her and Mary snuggled into her spooning. Skye stroked her hair and watched a smile play over Mary’s lips. No prizes for guessing who she was dreaming about. Mary murmured in her sleep, something about Statty and babies and fighting. It was all pretty disconnected but there was a common thread to the train of thought. Roberto and not giving up on him. It reminded her of the many times she had wanted to give up on Mary but never did and in the end, it was worth it because what she was left with far out weighed the price it cost to get there. Arael rested the rifle on its stand and aimed across the street at LeStatt’s balcony. The door was open and the lights were on dimmed. He attached an heat sensitive, high intensity lens to the rifle and took off the binoculars. His blindness wasn’t complete, it was partial, he was clinically blind but he could still see light and dark, shape and shadow and over the years it had been all he needed to be the best at his job. Well Leviath was the best but he was a close second. Skye was crying now and she didn’t know why. Mary had pulled Skye’s arms around her and pulled on of her hands up to her face to snuggle like a pillow. Arael eased his shoulders and then sank in against the rifle like it was part of him. He put his finger on the trigger and zoned out all surrounding noises that he didn’t need to hear. His phone was set to vibrate and he had to figures in his sites. One was a big bright light, broad. The other was tall also but slender. They both moved about back and forth. Arael let out a controlled breath and focussed down the sight, through the heat sensitive magnifying lens and waited for his chance. Skye kissed Mary on the cheek whispering. “Just a few more hours bebe and you’ll be free again. No more hurt, no more pain.” But Arael’s words came into her head again. Who was she really punishing. More to the point, who was she really trying to make feel better. Mary or herself? She looked down at Mary and wondered. Arael caught the tall white blur standing still. He had made all the prior arrangements to make sure this was the right room, and the right hotel. He had connections in the city help make sure that everything had been covered, everything had been thought out. That no one but his target would be harmed. He breathed controlled breaths and slowly squeezed the trigger while the figure stood still by the balcony window. He squeezed slowly harder and harder until he felt a buzzing and his finger stopped just before the trigger jarred and the rifle went off. “Yes?” He didn’t have to do anything but answer as his cell phone was on voice activated hands free. “Eduardo?” “Sim Miss Skye.” “Is it done?” He started to press the trigger again. “In 1 second Miss Skye.” “Don’t!” “Don’t?” “Sim, don’t. I’ve changed my mind. Come back home. Leave it.” “As you wish.” Without any word, he packed up his things and left the roof top. Back home Skye crawled back into bed beside Mary. “I’m sorry O Anjo. I just want to keep you safe.” She barely whispered the words. Mary snuggled in closer to her and continued sleeping and Skye cried until Arael got back.
