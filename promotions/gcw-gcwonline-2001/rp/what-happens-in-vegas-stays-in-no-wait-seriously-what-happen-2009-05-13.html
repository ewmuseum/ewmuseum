What happens in Vegas, stays in...no, wait, seriously. What happened?

13 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 13th, 2009 "What the fuck?" It's eight in the morning outside The Little Chapel of Love in Las Vegas. The air smells like morning, with a hint of shame and bad decisions. In the alley next to the chapel lies an old man, homeless no doubt, fast asleep against the wall of the small church, if that’s what you want to call it. His hands are wrapped in dirty, bloody bandages, and his face is severely sunburned and looks like the inside of a baseball mitt. "Dude, wake up." I shove Fisher off my lap, waking up to the odd situation. Fisher is curled up next to me and I'm propped up against the front of the building with my legs stretched in front of me. Fisher squints his eyes, allowing them to focus in the early morning sun. "What the hell happened?" "I dunno dude, but why the fuck are you wearing a dress?" Getting to his feet, he gives himself a once-over. "No clue. But I look hot." I shake my head. "Do you remember what happened last night?" Fisher raises an eyebrow at me. "Did you not hear me ask what the hell happened like thirty seconds ago?" "No." "Ugh. You never pay attention to me." He whines, mockingly. "Why did I marry you?" "Uh, what?" "Wedding dress," and he holds up his left hand, "wedding ring." I stood up, looking at my own left hand. Sure enough there was a wedding ring on my hand as well. All I could do is hope it was a drunken shenanigan that wasn't actually real. I didn't think gay marriage was even an option in Las Vegas, but I've been proven wrong about the Sin City many a time. I started walking down the road, back towards the main portion of the Strip. The old man stirred as I walked past him but didn't wake up. Fisher quickly got up quickly and caught up to my side. "I need to change, man. I'm not walking around the Strip in a wedding dress." He paused. "Especially not next to you." I rolled my eyes. "Fuck off dude. Where do you think I'm headed?" "Uhm. Where?" "We can find a clothing shop along the Strip somewhere and get you a temporary clothing fix." "Awe! You thought of me!" "No," I quickly responded, "I thought of me. I don't want to be seen walking around with some homo in a wedding dress." "Touche." The duo continued walking down the Strip in silence, neither one of them really wanting to be seen associating with the other for fear of the "gay" stare. You know, the one where girls look at you like they want to be your best friend, and guys are trying to figure out how exactly "it" works? After a mile or so, they come up on one of those Vegas shops. Shot glasses, t-shirts, etc. Lucky for FishBough, they had shorts as well. "You're joking, right?" Fisher says. "You have a better idea?" "I'd rather be walkin’ around in a damn wedding dress than dressin’ up in a fuckin’ ‘I love Vegas’ tee-shirt." He stops out front of the store, refusing to go in, and places his hands on his hips. A brief chuckle escapes my lips. "Dude, you seriously look really bad in that position." "Hey sweetheart!" Fisher whirls around, seeing two larger, burly looking men walking their way. The one on the right was the cat-caller. The other one whistles. "Damn bitch, you look fiiiine!" says the other. Fisher looks at them, then at me, then back at them. "I do like fine,” he says. We enter the store. Fifteen minutes later, we're back on the Strip again, walking back to our hotel room. Fisher is now decked out with a "What happens in Vegas, stays in Vegas" tee-shirt, flip flops and a pair of black shorts that have dice on the leg. "You know, it's rather odd to have you this close to my balls." I eye him. "Um, what?" He points to the dice on his pants. I roll my eyes again. "Hey! Dude! It's you guys!" Both of us whirl, partially protecting our asses this time around. A man approaches us, casually, but well-dressed. He raises his hand to Fisher for a high five. Fisher mock-enthusiastically obliges. "Um, do I know you?" Fisher asks. "Ha ha ha, whatever man." He shrugs off the question and turns to me. "Dude, you were a GOD last night! How'd you do all that?!" Fisher and I exchange quizzical glances. "Do what?" "Whadya mean do what!?" he says. "The girls, and the drinks, the tiger, the briefcase full of money, Elvis, the girls, the partying, the drugs..." I cut him off. "Wait, briefcase with money?" "FULL of money dude!" "Did you say something about bitches?" Fisher interjects. I palm my forehead. "Really?" He shrugs. "What, can't blame a guy for havin' a penis!" The stranger and Fisher high five again, this time Fisher with some real enthusiasm. "So wait...you two really don't remember anything about last night?" the stranger asks, his face painfully contorted in distress and confusion. I felt like I was in a really bad movie about waking up the next morning and not remembering what happened the night before, but everyone else seems to know about it. I'll pause to let that sink in. "I honestly have no idea what you're talking about," I say. "Well shit. You need to see this man." He pulls out a small video camera from his shoulder bag and opens it, cuing up a video. "Check it." Fisher and I crowd around the small screen as the video starts. It's the Siegfried and Roy show, and the tiger is up on its hind legs doing a trick. Suddenly, the tiger breaks loose and makes a beeline for the audience. A man jumps into the front of the pack headed for the door screaming. As the figure gets closer, they both notice the running, screaming man is actually me. "FISH! HELP ME! SERIOUSLY! I CAN'T BE DEAD TO WIN RAMPAGE! "I still don't know what you were talking about with all that shit,” says the stranger. Fisher and I exchange glances again. The tiger is gaining on the crowd, and me, but ends up getting tranquilized just before it reaches him. "See! There!" He points to Fisher's left hand. In it, sure enough, is a black briefcase. The man puts the camera away. "What the fuck!?" I say in disbelief. "I got attacked by a fucking tiger?!" "Where did that briefcase come from?" Fisher asks, almost to himself. "And more importantly, where the hell is it?" I throw my hands in the air. "A TIGER MAN! FOR FUCKS SAKE A TIGER ALMOST KILLED ME!" "You're alive right?" I hesitate a bit. "Yeah?" "Then you're not dead, so quit yelling like a loon out in public." I cross my arms over my chest, defeated and irritated. "So, were you with us the whole night?" Fisher asks the man. "At least 'till about three, yeah." "Hmm. What's your name?" "Mark. Mark Jackman." "I'm Brian, and this whiny little fairy is Tyson." I nod, still not speaking. "Nice to meet you guys.” He pauses. “Again." “So, what exactly happened last night?" Fisher asks. Little did we know the answer to that question would mean our lives would never be the same.
