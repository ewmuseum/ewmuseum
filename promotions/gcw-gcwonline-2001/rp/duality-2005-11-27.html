Duality

27 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27 Who am I? Isn't that what everything we ever do boils down to? Isn't that what life is about? Finding out who we are. Answering that question. Who is Johnathan Fitzwillis? "No, John. I know we're not married. Or dating. Or even technically seeing each other. I know exactly what this is. But I need to know that your daughter is your priority. And hearing about your latest sexual escapades every week isn't exactly instilling that confidence in me." Kate's mad. Kate's cute when she's mad. "It's an act, hun. A game." The look. "Okay, nix that. But I'm telling you, Kate, I didn't sleep with any of them. A couple forced their lips on me, a couple had wandering, wandering hands. And a few flashed some breasts. That's honestly the extent of it. No more." This is a conversation I've been dreading for a while now. But I've known it's been coming. In a sense, it's kind of like punching a brick wall. You know it's going to hurt, but you go through and do it anyway. Kate takes a deep breath and looks up to the right corner of her eye and I know that there's a speech coming. "It's just that fidelity is something I'd want Sarah to learn. Especially at a young age. I don't want her growing up wondering who her father is, even if she knows his name. I don't want her asking me who those women you have hanging off of you are. I don't want her to grow up hating you for being infatuated with sex and hating me for not taking her out of your life sooner..." Her words drown away, like waking up from a dream. They echo into the sounds of The City as I stand there, facing the last of the Conclave of Justice. Fraulein. The White Knight is technically still alive, but he's off. Doing bigger and better things than fighting me. But Fraulein stands there, untouched by the chaos that I've sown to the rest of the world. I'm with only my two favorite pistols, the ones I call Michael and Loki, the twin angels of battle. One of justice, one of death. Both shine in the night, laying still at my side for the time being, their mouths not preaching just yet. Fraulein speaks and shatters silence with her words. "You've killed them, Heroic! You've killed every last one of them, you bastard! The Masked Avenger, MVP, Christening...all of them! You call yourself a hero, but you killed all of them!" Who is Johnny Heroic? Rage fills her eyes and her fists clench. Her powers of observance and persuasion won't hold much on me. I have two clips and two guns. And that's, frankly, all I need. "I killed them...Fraulein...because they got in my way of being a hero. They all...got in my way..." I've got wounds gushing sweet red life out of me faster than my body can plug them, and I got a head that feels like it's been hit by a train with so many cars that I can't see the end of it. Bodies surround us on the desolate landscape. Bodies that I can barely identify anymore. Can't tell where one member of the Conclave of Justice starts and another one ends. Just one big happy family, so to speak, all meshed together. It's what they would have wanted. She shrieks like a banshee. That of a ghoul haunting the night out of a lust for revenge and triumph. It consumes her. Fraulein is no longer beautiful. She is terrible in every way. My fingers cannot depress the triggers fast enough to sooth me. But, the wail fades into the ringing of a cell phone. Kate stops speaking for a moment to let the world begin turning again, and an answer brings the devil into my life. 'Hello?' 'Johnny-boy. Long time, no talkie.' Kate hears Don's voice. She can't fucking stand him. 'I'm leaving, John. Tell me when you want to discuss our future.' She walks away. The world melts away. And I hear a voice boom through, louder than any I've heard since...since I heard his. 'Hello, Johnny Heroic.' '...Destine.' My arch nemesis. The one whose bodyguards and best friends I first killed when I lost my powers. My powers of the best luck in the universe. Which, might I point out, since I have no powers per se, I still retain the best of luck. Didn't he- 'I thought you killed yourself.' 'You think a lot of things, Johnny-boy. I'm still up here in Canada, just kicking it.' Don't voice on the cell phone is more smoke stained than usual. Gritty and flegm-filled. Tired and ready to quit on life. 'So, now that you've chased away the woman I love, Don...what is it exactly that the Devil has call for now?' Destine takes a moment and looks down at Fraulein's body. He knew I'd kill her. He knew I'd kill the entire Conclave as he threw them at me. But it's the fact that I actually went through with it and did it that amazes him the most. So I was wrong about him killing himself. So now what? Is he here to kill me for that fuck up? 'You've done better than expected with my charge, Heroic.' 'Your charge?' 'Oh come now. Did you think that you were the one to put this puzzle together? Did you think that you were the one to drop your little 'holier than thou' act and start really getting down to what counts. Not letting anyone stand in your way. Not allowing anyone to fuck up what you deserve.' 'What do I deserve, Don?' I can't believe what this guy's saying. He's taking the credit for my work? For my realizations? The Devil may be the master of lies and the prince of evil, but I am my own man. The choices I make are mine. And I'll be damned if I listen to him go on about- ...well, I'll be damned if I listen to him in the first place. 'Oh give me a fucking break, Johnny-boy. Your little mean streak has given you what? More fame, more power, more control over your life than anything else you used to do. And why? Because it allows you to stand up and tell the world what it can do with itself. Sorry that I'm the one that planted the idea in your head.' To be brief, now Destine's really starting to piss me off. 'How do you claim to be the one in control of what Johnny Heroic's been doing for the past few months, when you're the one that was supposedly dead, Destine?' 'It's quite simple, Heroic. I am the one that took away your powers.' The shock hits me like a tidal wave. There's an explanation for everything. And everything has an explanation. 'How did you manage to do that?' 'It's actually simple, Johnny-boy. I arranged the match of you versus Trey versus Rich Rollins. I had a little say in the fact that you were to be fighting Rollins for the TV Title a couple weeks before when you weren't exactly feeling top game. Then, it was a simple matter of planting the seeds in your head that made you want to hurt him. Made you want to hurt him bad. Bad to the point where you didn't just take back the TV Title. Oh, no...you went for the big one. And, in doing so, you were forced to turn your back on Trey. And the rest...is history.' Destine stares at me with a smile on his face. He knows that I've almost completely failed at what I am. I look at the scene around us. How many have died because of him? Because of me. One in the same we possibly are, dancing eternally in a chess game of lives and he's been pulling the strings attached to my fingers attached to the trigger of my guns, Michael and Loki...the entire time. So, what does he want now? 'I'm so glad you decided to come out to the open with this. But what's your reason? You never just come out and say what it is you want.' Silence from Don. A smile from Destine. He wants me to end him. He wants me to be the one to kill him. Don wants me to hate him. Destine wants me to shoot him. With the half clip I have left. Two guns raise up and I almost shout my loudest curse that I know into the phone to tell my arch nemesis, and oldest friend, to go to Hell. When she comes back. Tears fill her eyes. She chokes a bit, but the words come out. '...I'm sorry, John... I love you.' My arms embrace Kate before she can say anything else. 'I'm sorry...Heroic... It wasn't...your fault...' Kneeling down, I take Fraulein's head in my hand. She didn't deserve this. And I can still hear him trying to reach me. 'Johnny-boy. Hey! Talk to me, man.' Desperate. He wants it. He needs release. I reach the phone up to my ear again and have the sights set on him and my trigger on the finger as I eye the woman in front of me. But her tears tell me not to. Her tears just tell me to let go. Who the fuck are you? What have you turned in to? Johnathan Fitzwillis. Johnny Heroic. Johnny Borealis... 'Let him go, John. Let him go. You've beaten everybody that you need to, anyway.' Her words are clear. Clear as chrystal swords that cut chains that hold me to the past. The past that is Don, the Devil incarnate. Kate used to be my past. Don the future. But somewhere, those roles switched. Somewhere, that which caused me to rot rejuvinated me and that which brought me new life only stole what it once gave. ...Fraulein's words cut deeper than any sword can cut any chain. 'You beat him, Heroic. ...he wants you to fail...completely. But...you won't. Heh...it's too bad...that all you had to do...was kill us all...' Her eyes shut. Her spirit rests in my arms. I cut my nemesis, my friend, off so that I no longer hear what he says. But she's wrong, isn't she? Kate's wrong. I haven't beaten everybody. Fraulein's wrong. I haven't killed all of us. 'No...there's-... The barrel, both proverbial and literal, is at my own temple. My own head. And there's only one way to finally let it all rest. The end of Johnny Heroic. A new beginning for Johnathan Fitzwillis. '-...there's one more to go.' Who the hell am I? Who is Johnny Borealis? I am where fantasy meets reality. I am where dreaming meets waking up. There is the tension of the real. There is the relaxation of escape. I am where the two meet in the middle and dance the night away. Bang
