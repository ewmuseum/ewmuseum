The Gatekeeper Returns

10 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/08 A tall dark figure loomed in the distance; a small smile curved the full ruby red lips of Cleopatra. She slowly increased the speed of her stride closing the distance between her and the figure. Daniel followed behind her, his head hung low, his steps slow and deliberate. The figure starts to become clearer, it is the Gatekeeper, Vassago. Cleo runs to him and wraps her arms around him tightly, pressing her breast against his chest. Vassago mindlessly puts an arm around her shoulders. ?I am so glad you have finally decided to join us.? Cleo purred in her lovers ear. ?Who is he?? Vassago asked narrowing his eyes with enmity. ?Vassago, this is my son Daniel.? Cleo placed a hand lovingly against Daniel's cheek. Daniel pushed Cleo's hand away from him and ran off into the night. ?What a charming young man Cleo. Didn't you teach him any manners?? Vassago glanced in the direction that Daniel ran. His eyes filled with contempt. ?He is having trouble accepting what he is,? Cleo's lips twisted in a mischievous smile, ?I couldn't stand my son being a mortal.? Vassago scanned Cleopatra with his eyes, part of him filled with lust and a bigger part of him filled with detest. He didn't even know the boy but he detested him and his presence. He was going to ruin his plan. He needed to cut Daniel out of the picture. He just needed to find an opportunity to do so. ?Delylah... Delylah,? Nebt-Het shook her daughter gently, ?Del honey come on wake up we are at the bridal boutique.? Delylah slowly let her eyes flutter open as her mother's loving face came in to focus. Icicles shot up and down her spine, the Gatekeeper was here. He was the last person she wanted to see again. The way he looked at her made her feel impure, violated. She tried to manage a smile for her mother's benefit but Nebt-Het could sense her daughter was ill at ease. ?What's wrong Del?? Nebt-Het's eyes became as dark as storm clouds, ?Did you have another dream?? Delylah shook her head, ?I am fine mom, just a little nervous about the wedding thing,? Delylah covered her lie by beaming a huge smile, ?I have never been real feminine mom and I am totally lost as to what I'm doing.? Nebt-Het smiled lovingly at her daughter, ?Are you sure you're alright?? She nodded when Delylah shifted her eyes away from her as they started toward the boutique. ?I am over what happened Delylah. You don't have to treat me as if I were a child,? Nebt-Het grabbed her by the arm and spun her around so they could be eye to eye, ?I am a part of all this whether you like it or not. She is my daughter too.? Tears filled Nebt-Het's eyes as she choked out her words through her sobs. ?Mother, I am just trying to protect you. I don't know what I would do if something happened to you,? Delylah's eyes filled with tears, ?I can't risk you mom.? Nebt- Het looked at her daughter in a new light. She knew that Delylah loved her but since the day Amun was killed everything was different. Neither of them acted the same or even felt the same as they did before that fateful night. Nebt-Het reached up and gently wiped away her daughter's tears, looking at her like it was the first time she saw her. ?Come my darling and lets get you fitted for your wedding dress. I want you to look like the Egyptian Goddess that you are.? Delylah slowly slipped her arms through the satin straps as her mother slowly zipped the back of the dress. She turned around to face the wall of mirrors that she had avoided since she walked into the place. The lights made the sequins on the gown sparkle like diamonds. Nebt-Het sucked in her breath as she beheld the beauty that was her daughter. She grinned broadly as she set her eyes on the headpiece. Nebt-Het placed the silver tiara upon her daughter's head. Delylah's ebony hair set the small stones to shimmering. ?I... I...,? Delylah stammered at her own reflection, ?I never knew how beautiful I truly was until now. I look so much like you mother.? Nebt-Het's dark eyes twinkled, ?You truly are a goddess my dear. You compliment the dress, the dress don't compliment you.? The shopkeeper slowly walked around taking measurements, tucking in the loose parts and pinning them. ?I vill make zis dress fit you like a glove.? The stout shopkeeper said with a glint in his eyes. Delylah stood there as if in a trance, delighted by her own reflection. She simply nodded to the shopkeeper and slowly slid off the dress when he was finished with his alterations. ?We will be back in a week to pick up the dress.? Delylah told him, her voice cold and hard. The shopkeeper swallowed hard and nodded. Delylah turned on her heels and left the shop with her mother in tow. ?Why were you so short with that man Del? He seemed like a very nice fellow.? Nebt-Het asked as she screwed up her face in confusion. ?I don't know mom. Sometimes I just don't feel like myself.? Delylah's voice filled with apprehension. ?Maybe you just need some rest dear.? Nebt-Het offered. ?You might be right mom. Take me back to the hotel so I can try and rest.? Delylah asked in a defeated voice. Nebt-Het loaded her daughter into the car and sped through the streets toward the hotel. She hoped that Paul was still there. She needed him to be there, that was the only way she felt her daughter would be safe. When they reached the hotel they immediately went up to their rooms. When they entered Delylah's suite it was empty. A note was left on the nightstand. Del, I am going for a walk. I need to work off some of my excess nervous energy. I will be back real soon. I love you. Please don't worry about me. I will be fine. Paul Delylah sighed and fell backwards onto the bed and stared up at the ceiling. How she wished that Paul were here to hold and comfort her. She knew something was horribly wrong. She knew The Gatekeeper was here in Paris. That would bring nothing but trouble to all of them. Now they had to worry about two of them instead of just Cleo. ?I am going to shower dear. You rest and I will sit in here with you.? Nebt-Het said softly. ?I will be fine mom. You don't need to watch me sleep.? Delylah said sarcastically. Nebt-Het's eyes filled with agony as she slowly crossed the room headed toward the bathroom. Delylah saw the pain in her eyes. ?Mom, you can sit here with me. I guess it's better if we all stick together anyway.? Her voice filled with regret. The sound of the shower lulled Delylah into a deep sleep. A deeper sleep that she had never wanted. A sleep that was dangerous to all that she loved. Daniel's eyes filled with anger, fear and hatred as he watched his mother with Vassago. There was something about that man that he just didn't like. He was slowly honing his vampiric talents to work to his advantage. He never led Cleo to believe that he had a clue as to just what they could do. He wanted her to think him weak. He didn't want her to know that he planned on killing her one day. He had heard through the grapevine that if he or someone were to kill the one that made him he would become mortal again. He wasn't sure if this was true or not but it was worth a shot. Anything was worth a shot if he could become normal again. ?There you are Daniel you had me worried sick.? Cleo crooned as she closed the distance between them. She seemed to float now that she had her friend back. Her emerald eyes twinkled as if they truly were the precious gemstones they were colored after. Everything about her seemed to change, make her more alluring to her male prey. Every time Vassago whispered in her ear she giggled like a schoolgirl. ?I am going out to feed darling. You must come with us.? Cleo smiled knowing how Daniel felt about hunting. ?I have already fed mother.? Daniel spat. Vassago narrowed his eyes as he contemplated Daniel's response. ?Suit yourself. See if I care if you die anymore. I don't need you know that he is here.? Cleo retorted pointing her dagger like finger tip at Vassago. She looked at Vassago but her words had no effect on him, ?Are you coming along Vassago?? Vassago pinched his chin between his thumb and forefinger as he eyeballed Daniel, ?I think I will stay here with junior. Just to make sure he don't try anything funny.? Cleo pouted and stalked off into the night to fulfill her need to drink. Daniel stared at Vassago wondering how he knew what he had planned. He could feel it in the pit of his stomach. He tried to tap into his mind but it was no use. His thoughts were locked up tighter than Fort Knox. ?I think I am a little hungry,? Daniel lied, ?You may stay here and await my mother's arrival if you wish.? Daniel secretly hoped that he would just go away and never return. ?I have other things in mind but don't you worry junior I will be back. I don't plan on going anywhere until my mission is complete.? Vassago's voice was completely void of any emotion. Vassago's indifference frightened Daniel so he quickly fled the lair putting as much distance between him and that creature as possible. Daniel wondered what mission he was talking about. Why he came to Paris? How did he know where to find Cleo? There was something strange about him and Daniel would make it his business to find out just what was going on. Delylah's eyes slowly fluttered open, nigh had already blanketed the sky in darkness. She looked around the room and Paul was stretched out on the much to small sofa watching television. She looked over to him, relieved that he was alright. ?Paul... he is here. The Gatekeeper has come to help her finish things.? Tears filled her eyes. Paul rushed to her and wrapped her safely in his strong arms. ?Everything will be fine Del. I promise it will be alright.? Paul told her earnestly. Delylah hoped that he was right. He needed to be right or everything that they had hoped and dreamed this trip would bring would be shot down in a blaze of glory. Delylah vowed that she would not go down without a fight. She would risk her own life to save the ones she loved. She had a feeling that she was the one that The Gatekeeper truly wanted.
