Conquering Fears

14 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/05 “Ricky its supposed to rain today?” comes the voice from the other end of the line. “Aww come on, it’s only supposed to sprinkle for an hour or two. The park will still be open, and I want to go!” “Is a park really that big of a deal?” “You don’t understand! They have one of THE best rides in America!” “Well I think I am gonna pass, but you can go ahead if you want?” the voice still brushing off the idea. “Fine, I’ll go alone” Ricky hangs up the phone and gives it no more thought, as he slips his cell phone into his pocket and walks out of his small Orlando Apartment. Ricky pulls the door shut and stares out across the graying skies, and hops in his car parked in the lot. He fires up the cherry red, Prelude and kicks a few stray rocks up as he tears out of the lot. Ricky reached down and turns on the radio, and pops in a cd and starts singing along with Linkin Park. The traffic on the road isn’t too bad as he moseys along, skimming through yellow lights, not wanting to stop and delay his fun for the day. Ricky glances at the clock on the dashboard that reads 7:08 P.M. “I should be there as soon as they open! No line for my coaster!” Ricky exclaims; to absolutely nobody. Ricky continues down the road as a slow drizzle starts to fall. He stares out at the sky leaning near the steering wheel, trying to get a better view. He looks side to side repeatedly but continues onward. He arrives at the theme park within minutes. He pulls into the lot and is directed by several teenagers with orange vests and parking flags in which direction to go for parking. He finds the designated spot and hops out of his car, locking the doors and jogging to the entrance. He gets to the front of the park and locates the ticket booth and saunters over. “One adult please!” Ricky beams. “128.99” “WHAT”!”! Are you kidding me?!” “No sir, the prices are posted right there.” “Hmm can I get a senior or kiddy ticket then?” “Good try. Would you like to purchase the ticket?” “Damn” 130 bucks’Is it worth it?” Ricky asks the young attendant “Well I like it, but I don’t pay to get in. We get free passes for working here.” “Well how bout family” Boyfriend? Friends? Do they think its worth it?” “Honestly they have never paid to get in. I get 14 visitor passes a season so they use those” the girl giggles a bit as she says this. “Damn, I need a friend like you?” Ricky pulls a credit card from his wallet and slides in under the glass window. The girl swipes the card and slides it back to Ricky. They wait a few minutes and she then slides him a slip of paper to sign. Upon signing it, he slips his copy and the card in wallet and enters the turnstiles with ticket in hand. Upon having his ticket taken he is granted access into the park, and straight away has a camera in his face. “Would you like souvenir photos?” they ask. “No thanks” Ricky answers shortly, and heads on his merry way towards the beast he came to conquer. Ricky is one of the few people at the park at opening times. He rushes to the roller coaster and jams through the cow ropes. He weaves through until he reaches the coaster where nobody else has made it there yet. He hops in the queue for the front seat and waits. A young couple gets in queue beside him for the second seat and sparks up some small talk. “You ever rode this before?” The young man asks “Nope, I have heard so much about it, and I have been dying to.” Is Ricky’s reply. “He loves these things but didn’t want to go alone. They scare the piss out of me.” His girlfriend adds. “Babe its not gonna be THAT bad.” “It’s only a coaster right” Not like its gonna fly off the track or anything.” Ricky adds, trying to soothe her but obviously failing miserably. Finally after several minutes the crowd appears and they slowly start to fill the seats on the coaster. The first seat still belongs to Ricky. As the coaster reaches capacity, an attendant walks through and checks the straps ensuring everyone is strapped in securely. With a thumbs up signaling they are god to go, a buzz is heard and the coaster whips out of the starting gate and immediately starts the climb for the massive descent. Ricky is smiling from ear to ear as he hangs on every click of the coasters climb. Then suddenly it stops. “What the?” Ricky notices they aren’t at the top yet, and the coaster has stopped. “Babe what’s goin on?” comes the female voice from behind. “Just give it a minute, it’ll go” He replies. 2 Hours Later “Get me outta here!” the girl shrieks. At this point Ricky is laughing uncontrollably at the fiasco taking place behind him. “What are you laughing at you asshole”!” the young man yelps. “Please get us out of here!!!” the girls desperate cries come answered in the form of a maintenance man and a release instrument. He slips it under Ricky’s seat but just as he does a voice comes over the intercom stating the ride is functional again. “You all can either ride or come with me” the repairman states. “I came to ride, I waited this long” Ricky laughs as behind him the girl can’t get off the ride fast enough. The ride starts up with a whirr as it continues the climb up the rails. Ricky’s anticipation has turned to excitement and maybe a bit of fear as he hangs over the edge, staring down the 90 degree drop. Whoosh~ The coaster drops, whipping through several turns and rolls, and corkscrew after corkscrew. After a short 45 seconds, the ride comes to a halt in the bay, and when the seats locks release, Ricky realizes he was the only one who had stayed for the ride through after the mishap. Suddenly a man approaches him from a booth. “Mr Thunder, I would like to offer you a free T-Shirt, for your trouble today. I was going to hand em out to everyone, but you seem to be the only one to survive it, so to speak. This is the first time we have had any trouble with “The Violence”. Ricky laughs as he takes his t-shirt. He holds it up and reads aloud “I conquered The Violence”
