Story Progression? Character introduction? Mein gott!

20 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/20/07 'Mike?' 'Yo?' 'Haha, man, you are never gonna believe this shit.' 'Frank? Fuck are you calling me about?' Frank fidgets in the phone booth, keeping his cellphone clamped to his face with the payphone's handset. 'I got a job, dude.' 'Fuck are you calling me about? That's like five this month, an' I never pretended to give a fuck. C'mon. Dude. Sleep.' 'Y'know those were just all on some bullshit, this is the real deal.' 'I dig, I dig. Kinda like Alyssa was the real deal.' 'Man, she was on 'at same bullshit tip. Fuck alla that, this is real like the motherfuckin', like the fuckin' Whopper's real.' 'Word?' 'That is motherfucking word. You at yours?' 'Yeah, yeah. You need somethin'?' 'Place to sit down an' some o' that girl, yeah.' 'Fuck it. Gimme ten minutes.' 'It's a fuckin' thirty minute walk from here.' 'Then take a taxi, Moneybags. Don't mean a fuck to me.' 'Haha, alrigh', alrigh'. I can tell you rest about this work when I get there.' 'Whatever.' 'Whatever's fuckin' solid. Later.' 'Peace.' He hangs up his cell and lets the payphone swing into a crash against the glass. Five paces from it, he makes a fullcircle just slow enough to see it not crack as it isn't busy shattering--good enough. Bunkering down into a thick black coat, strings of brown hair bounding from his nose to his cheek as the long line of breath rises up behind him, he moves in bursts around the block with mobile anxiety that twitches his whole torso. He looks over his shoulder to no one and digs his hands deep into lined pockets against a storefront to a pizza place that hasn't opened yet. Staring at a faded sky, flipping open his cellphone to check the time, sucking teeth to pass what's not missing yet. He keeps the phone open and looks just perfectly busy running up through a list of numbers past their prime. Four minutes until Mike says he's ready, Frank's starting to throw his hand up at passing taxis. Blues and yellows a green live up to their introduction as passing taxis; it's a red one that betrays the narration to let him in. He gives an address and starts counting how late he is--two minutes past perfect efficiency already. The cabbie, of the old white type, doesn't pester the impenetrable or the unreachable. He judges Frank one of them and just drives. Frank plays passenger well, tapping out text messages inbetween sudden bouts of a sort of preemptive nostalgia to sidewalk trees gone pale in the early morning shimmer and stone faced buildings that won't let anything slip. Downtown mirrors his head, mirrors his cab by sections. Light traffic like time alone. They both drift in and out until they stop, decisive and sharp. Disbelief's spouted, money changes hands and Frank's free to step out in front of a brick apartment building that's maybe caked to the pores in city grime and city sin. Buzzing up--'Mike, s'Frank. Lemme in'--and leaning in until the doors click and he gets to climb three flights of dingy steps under antiseptic lights just because an elevator's been dead since the dawn of mankind. Down a green hall, he knocks. Mike opens with a yawn, bald and fat enough to be an intimidating kind of huge in spite of a height disadvantage. He clenches a paw to his gut, stained white T stretched drumtight. 'Wait out here a minute, dude, I gotta get dressed,' shuts the door. Frank, idle, waits until it opens again. 'Thought I might of gave you long enough.' 'Shit, coulda been later.' 'Fuck it, dude, I tried.' Frank steps in and pulls the door shut behind him, keeping cadence to the nearest couch and collapsing. New York hip-hop, circa 1985, plays as T.O. drops a pass for the hundredth time in slow motion replay. A middle aged man with a face pulled by some sort of gravity well in his overbitten chin has something to say about it. 'What you need?' 'Shit. Gimme an 8.' 'Got what you owe?' 'Yeah, with a little interest thrown in. Good relations, y'know.' 'i don't give a fuck what if it is if I'm gettin' money I ain't earn. Good relations, whatever.' He leans over beside the entertainment center and pulls out a sock with a bag in it. Stacked like Russian dolls, he pulls out another bag and tosses an even smaller bag to Frank for a bobbled reception. Frank eyes the bag and nods. 'That's word, man. You got your suitcase around?' 'Jyeah, but I gotta be at work in a hour.' 'I feel you, ain't gonna take that long.' 'What I'm sayin's maybe you can stick around if you hand me alla that money now.' 'Haha, I got you.' A wallet in his pocket becomes a wallet in his hand; more money changes hands and he goes back to fucking with the bag. 'So yeah, about this job.' 'Yeah, I noticed you actually had a full wallet.' 'S'an advance. You ever watch that shit, GCW?' Mike shrugs. 'Little bit. Why?' 'Well, I'm at that show last night for ANC, right, and I go out and I run my match and that's all good.. I lose, I mean, but it's all just a show. Match don't mean shit to what I'm talkin' about.' 'Then why you talkin' about it?' 'I'm not.' He dumps a waxy white rock onto a CD case and takes a small black bag from Mike. 'It's after this show, this dude comes up to me in a parking lot. I didn't recognize him at first 'cuz his hair was kinda shaggy, but while he's introducin' himself an' shit I see this spider tattoo on his forehead and I'm like, 'I recognize this. This cat's Jordan White.' An' like, right as I'm thinkin' it he comes out with it.' 'Shit.' 'Yeah. An' like, fuck is he doing at this little nothing gig?' A razorblade takes a chunk and starts to powder it down. 'Turns out, he don't live too far away or nothin', but it's like, he's doesn't want his job no more and he's looking for a replacement.' 'Sounds like a smart dude.' 'Motherfuckin', you don't even know. Like, cat was wearing a furcoat and drivin' a straight pimped out Impala from way back. Says the money ain't good enough to keep him around anymore, so he's got this offer.' 'Jyeah, replacement. How you gonna replace a dude?' 'Just, like, replace him, the way he put it. Basically, see, he's gonna shoot me his paychecks if I go out there and do his job. Still had to run it by the place an' all, but, I mean, he shot me a nice fuckin' upfront to think about it.' 'You thought about it?' 'Haha, man, I am fuckin' there. Six figures, free travel, fame?' The tacktacktack of razor on plastic metronomes for him. 'How's a dude like me gonna let something like that just slip on by? This is the big fuckin' break, y'know? Just gotta give up my name, and fuck is a name anyways?' 'What all's he mean with replace though?' 'Like, I gotta change my name and he's gonna drop me in, let me know whose side we're on, where we're standin', alla that. It's like, like he's gonna sorta be managing me. Keeping his name clean, y'know, while mine gets to rest. I'm figurin' like, fuck it. People are gonna notice, I can stir up a little noise for when I finally go back to being Frank Black. I'll already be on level.' 'Shit. Nice deal.' 'Yeah, shit yeah. Oh yeah, dude, I had a question for you and I'd forgot that shit for like three weeks. Shit, like, how do you tell when one's gonna let you get away with smackin' her around?' 'What?' Mike stops like tanks stop turning. 'Your bitch, y'know. How the fuck do you know one that'll let you hit her an' shit?' 'The hell you talkin' about? Bondage and shit?' 'Nah, man, not sexual shit. Just, like.' 'Shut up while you're ahead, Frank.' 'I was just wonderin'.' 'Then find somewhere that it's cool to wonder that shit. I don't give a fuck.' 'Whatever, man.' 'Fuck a whatever, boy. That shit ain't cool.' 'Fuck.' The guillotine quits clacking. 'You got a smoke?' 'I thought you quit.' 'This is me quitting.' 'So you quit paying for 'em.' 'Means I smoke less.' 'Or bum more.' 'Same difference.' 'Frank. Man.' And the blade's dropping again. 'Yeah?' 'Nevermind.' 'Nah, what was it?' 'I said nevermind. Shit.' 'But you were gonna say something.' 'And you shouldn't pay any nevermind to that, didn't you hear that?' 'I was just curious.' 'Don't be. You're a buggy little bitch for a prowrestler, y'know.' 'Whatever, man.' The edge of the razor focuses on a corner, cascading white boulders into fine snow that hangs beneath the crest in intermittent piles. Chunks shatter to dust, dust to crystals, crystals to atoms in a blink of God's eye. 'Whatever.' 'That shit's gonna kill you.' 'Then why the fuck do you sell it to me?' 'I don't care, isn't my problem. You're a big boy, take your pick.' 'I choose,' pitches him forwards, pulls a McDonalds straw from his pocket, breaks a section clean across the razor and shoves the smaller piece in his nostril. He doesn't breath, he sizes up that corner and pulls it straight into a line from inches away. The straw dangles; he works quickly. Snort, and. And this is how that God cat felt when he dug his hands in the mud and sculpted that first mountain range to worship from. He--Frank--reverses his pitch forwards almost perfectly to crash him into the couch--as much as a person can crash into almost a feet of soft padding. He snorts again, again to clear the traces clinging to hairs and send them on that great plunge with their sisters. The sorority mingles in the back of his throat, drawn tight as he stares at the ceiling. Waiting. His jaw slacks, his eyes pinch tight and his nose starts to quiver in anticipation. He breathes; he teaches his lungs to snort and it's just enough to pull down the drip. The drip is cotton candy in the soul. The drip is the electric jolt of omnipotence on a creep down the back of the throat that leaves a spreading glow of brilliant numb on lightspeed routes through the central nervous system. It is 'charge,' and it is 'zap,' and it is rock and motherfucking roll salvation on loan from perdition with no real pressure or expectations that imply the necessity of a return--experience, on the other hand, is screaming its throat out about 'this too shall pass,' but there's a brain too in love to listen to objections that she's not smart enough, or hot enough, or clever enough or prudent or pretty or proud enough to keep anyway. It has to end--it should end--and no one wants that when things look like they're going alright if one takes judgment of heart based on subtle changes in facial musculature. His eyes open. 'Jordan White. Haha, fuck, it's not a bad name. You don't remember back when he was in 3W, do you?' Mike shakes his head. 'Oh, shit. Man, you are missing the fuck out. Back then, like, I don't know, three years ago, he had like the most fucking amazing match with, oh, goddamnit. I think it was Draven Masters or whatever his name his and Risk, that biglikeenforcertype dude, handicapped match and all, you know, hardcore rules to it.' Mike nods. 'So yeah, that old fuck Draven and Risk are just standing in the ring waiting, and Jordan's music's rolling and it keeps rolling--like, I think it was Alice then, maybe Hate to Feel--and these two just decide to go backstage, looking like it's just a straight noshow outta Jordan. But, see, as soon as they opened the fucking curtains to go back, Jordan started tossing smoke bombs down on them and dropped in with a motherfucking whatsitcalled, a staff, like, a bo, y'know, what Leonardo had.' 'Leo had swords, Don had the bo.' 'Word, word. But yeah, he's just in this fucking heavy cloud beating both of their asses until the smoke clears and this motherfucker is decked out like the goddamn Karate Kid and shit, nunchuks and shit all tucked in his belt. So, I mean, yeah. He tore the fuck outta both of them before that shit was over, I mean, like, it was fucking beautiful shit. It's a good name, y'know, Jordan White. I think I could dig it.' 'So this is like, what, some faggot wetdream of yours?' 'Nah, nah, nothin' like that.' He puts his palms to his eyes, to his forehead and shakes either arms or head. Either way, it's a seismic violence that ends when he slaps his palms to his knees. 'Haha, y'know. He was just cool, back inna day. One of those dudes sorta made me think about wrestling.' 'Dude sounds like a dweeb.' 'Shit, maybe, I dunno. I just, me personally, I just think he's a cool motherfucker and I don't mind workin' for him, takin' his name. Shit. Frank Black, fuck kinda name is that?' 'Was yours. Fuck it, do I just have to start calling you Jordan now?' 'Don't know that I'll be in town much anymore, y'know, but yeah, I mean, I should probably start getting used to it.' 'Alright, then, Jordan. You wanna step out on the balcony and blaze this joint with me before I gotta go in?' 'You know I don't fuck with that, man.' 'Oh yeah, you're all high and mighty. I remember.' 'Dude.' 'Hah, oh shit, not 'dude.' I'm fuckin' scared now, Jordan.' 'Chill, man.' 'Just trying out your new name, that's all.' And things just get more character developmenty and boring from here. We all get the idea, so let's see how things go for the original Jordan White--currently busy rambling in an incredibly white bathroom. 'Sage Black.' Draws a razor along the contour between chin and neck, rolls his eyes until those muscles at the back strain and grumble. 'That's a ridiculous name.' 'And Jordan Xavier White isn't?' 'Can't you just let the kid wrestle as you?' 'I don't see how that's gonna establish me as a franchise. Jordan White needs to be a title, y'know, not just a gimmick.' 'Then find a scientist, or a nobel laureate, or, I don't know. Someone that's not a wrestler.' 'Gotta start small, test the market.' 'I hear this is how Bill Gates got his break.' 'Now you're just being a bitch.' 'Yeah. So, do you have to wrestle this week?' 'I, uh. I don't exactly know. I mean, I'd hate to go out there and lose to someone I called a total waste on national TV, but. y'know. I can just not wrestle. I don't want this kid sucking and ruining everything before it starts. Other hand, goth chicks smell kinda funny when they sweat and maybe I will let him go and fuck up my good name.' 'You stink when you sweat.' 'But it's a good, manly stink of toxins fighting to the surface.' 'So this is how you people piss your money away.' 'So this is how you people mooch.' The razor drops, he drops the razor, to the ribcage tattooed across his chest and starts to drag laps along the ink. He tilts his head, leans forwards and drops the razor into the sink to pop a zit on the bridge of his nose. This is US title contender Jordan White, for a little while. What's in a name? Letters, sometime hyphens or apostrophes. Period if there's a middle initial. A lot of little scratches or a few little scratches; nothing more. What they hell do they sign contracts in, anyways?
