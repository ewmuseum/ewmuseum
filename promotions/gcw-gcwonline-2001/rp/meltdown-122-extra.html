Date: Immediately after Worldwide Kevin Jacobs hustles through the backstage area, clearly out of breath, his head darting back and forth, looking for someone. His eyes light up as he finds his target wheeling a suitcase behind him. Jacobs: JASON! Jason O'Neil stops and turns around, a smile on his face. O'Neil: Kevin? It's been too long. How's the wife? Jacobs: She's... *huff puff* ...well. O'Neil: Catch your breath Kevin. I know what you're looking for. Why would I beat AXEL down, when Jay Terror was right there? The answer is simple: Sure, I could beat Terror within an inch of his life and then take a foot, but, why bother when messing with his mind is so fun? I'm an agent of chaos, and sometimes, you've gotta go the unpredictable route. AXEL caught what rightfully should have gone to Jay Terror, only I want Jay Terror to be in pristine condition when I take that foot and end his career at NC-17. I don't want any excuses about nagging injuries. I'll go in bloody, battered and bruised, and still be better than Jay Terror. Week in and week out, I'm going to be a sort of guardian angel for Terror, but, more importantly, I'm going to be a guardian angel for me and my paycheck. Wherever Terror wrestles, you'll find me, making sure that my payday and ultimate embarrassment of Steven Caldera is fulfilled, making sure that Jay Terror walks into NC-17 at one hundred percent. That's why I beat down AXEL. Take umbrage though, AXEL, that each punch to the top of your thick skull was made with Jay Terror in mind. I was punching you, but, by proxy I was punching Terror in his face. Someday when you've got some misplaced aggression to take out, you look up Jason O'Neil, and while I'm beating your ass again, you can pretend you're me, and we're beating on someone else. Kevin: What about next week when Terror takes on your brother as part of a fatal four way? O'Neil shrugs and turns around starting to walk away. O'Neil: He'll have to understand, then. Jacobs looks at the camera and nods before the feed cuts to snow.
