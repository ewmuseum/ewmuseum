Date: !!-8-2005 It had been a few days past since the attack by the group of thugs at the University and yes I said “Thugs” cos that’s all they were and anyone calling them anything other than that would be considered an idiot to be kind! Sorry had to get that out of my system now I can get on with the story? The attack had made the news like wildfire in Shinoku and elsewhere due to the popularity of the person that was attacked. See Ranmaru isn’t just some person that just Japan knew about, everywhere the young man went people knew him from the clippings and the pictures and he was arguably one of the most recognizable people in the world and needless to say he’s more popular than the President in this country. Why? Well he’s got a nice personality, smart, handsome, as ass to die for and? Oops sorry’had to catch myself there for a moment in time but you get the idea right? The point is that because of the attack on the man it made its rounds in the newspapers and the odd thing was its not like he was beat up as bad as he could have been! The attack WAS stopped before any physical damage was done to him so why would people try to make it any more than that? Cos they needed to sell papers that’s why! For the days after the incident it was hard to deal with the reporters and the interviewers as they were constantly trying to get a scoop or a story from anyone at the apartment when finally Curtis had lost his patience and literally called the stations and newspapers and told them everything that he had known about the incident and that they should just leave well enough alone cos it was traumatic enough for his friend and he didn’t want to revisit it anymore! Surprisingly they listened and stopped calling the apartment or visiting to get a story but they did say that they were sorry to hear about it at all? Now’s where it gets interesting?. Ok so you think that’s it right? That’s the end of the story and we can go home now right? WRONG! If it were that easy there’d be no point to me telling you this story right? Turns out that that whore of a fed head in Steven Caldera had his secretary track down where he was staying and fax him where he had to wrestle next. *No good piece of garbage! Why can’t he leave me well enough alone to enjoy my life huh?* Joshua thought to himself as he read the fax and a surprised look came on his face as his mind began to wonder again *CW title match. This is going to be an interesting situation all things considered with Jackson, Rei Tiu and this Varisty fellow. I wonder what Steven is trying to prove by booking me in a match like this? Does he really want me to become a champion so quickly or does this asshole have other plans for me? Well whatever is going on in his head I’ll be sure to make him regret trying to f*** my life up’believe that!* Joshua thought to himself as he ripped up the fax and threw it in the garbage before heading off to his bedroom to get ready for work at the University again and he just KNEW that something was going to happen there again and today he really wasn’t in the mood! When he got to Koei University Ran was already there as evident by the door being unlocked and Ran’s smiling face awaiting him at the door to get to work and he didn’t seem one bit upset hat Josh was later but then again it was only by a minute so? “Good Morning Joshua” Ran started “how’s everything going this morning for you?” Rand asked and the look Joshua had on his face was one that made Ran somewhat jump but it told him that it was best to leave well enough alone for the moment and he did so for the rest of the day. Later in the day Joshua apologized for being in a foul mood but he also explained to Ran that his bastard of an owner had tracked him down to his home country and gave him orders to come back to wrestle this week. Ran frowned when he heard that because he knew it meant that Joshua would be leaving again but he smiled when Joshua told him that the minute he took care of his business in GCW he was coming back home. “Ran remember one thing hun. Steven Caldera owns only a piece of paper that has my name on it and that can easily be torn up and thrown out but friends are a lot harder to come by. Steven could buy friends but that doesn’t make them “true” friends’not like what I have with you, Kei, Kai and my friends back in the states. I came home to be with those whom cared enough to have my back when I came out to my mother and for that Ran there is no end to the amount of thanks I can give you or no end to the debt that I have to repay to you Mr. Samejima.” Joshua said and Ran only laughed when Joshua used that formality on him. “You know you never have to be that formal with me Joshua. I appreciate it but I never expect you to be that formal with me ever. I just want you to be happy and I’m glad that you came back to your roots and that you aren’t leaving us for good. I also want you to know that the Dean came to me after the incident a few days ago and said that if you wanted to come back to work full time here you are always welcome to do so because he knows ain’t no one around here dumb enough to come after you with how you were a few days ago.” Ran explained and Josh couldn’t help but laugh at the comment made. “The worse part is that that wasn’t nearly as bad as I could have been. My boss pisses me off worse by telling me I HAVE to be at every show for some dumb reason or another! Don’t get me wrong I love the fans and that’s one of the few reasons I go but other than that I’d rather tell my boss to kiss my ass!” Joshua said as he finished his section and was on his way to helping Ran with his. “If its that bad then why do it” You seem much happier when you are here so couldn’t you just tell Mr. Caldera to go bug someone else? I know its bugs you that he makes you do things that you really don’t want to and I also know if it was your choice you’d take a kendo stick upside his head for putting you through this right?” Ran asked and Josh nodded before speaking again? “Its not just that though. Steven has proven that when he’s lost interest in his toys he gets rid of them like a bad habit. He did it to a guy named Michael Hardy, did it publicly as a matter of fact, so he’s shown he can’t be trusted and that’s why I know I can’t trust him’hell this one dude named Seymour Almasy can’t stand him and if that man can’t stand you then you KNOW your in trouble!” Joshua told Ran as he finished with the last of the files before speaking again “Well enough talk about my asshole boss in GCW’wanna go get lunch” I’m buying.” Joshua said and Ran nodded before speaking . “Ok but about your situation Josh how about this week I go in place of Zack” I’m dying to meet the fans of this place and it’ll be a fun experience besides.” Ran said and Joshua nodded before they walked out of the room and Joshua just knew that things were going to be interesting from now on!
