The Lessons

10 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/11 We are taken to a street. The camera is standing across the street and focusing on a large square building. It looks like it's seen better days. Above the rusty, metal, double-door entrance are some shoddy letters: Michael Reese's Pro-Wrestling School The street is clear, so the cameraman proceeds to cross over to the training gym. The doors slowly open up to show that the inside is in slightly better condition that the out, but still nothing special. There is various training equipment laying around, with a wrestling ring in the center of the massive room. Inside, the protagonist of the story (Toga) is learning from one of the few greats to have never stepped foot in a Global Championship Wrestling ring, "The Submission Master" Michael Reese (See "The Moment" for details). The camera approaches and can soon hear their conversation as Reese is trying to explain to the smaller man. Reese: "... it's very important!" Toga: "I know. I'm trying!" It's not quite clear what Michael is talking about, but there is a bit of obvious frustration dripping down his face along with his sweat. Reese: "We've been here for several days, and we only have tomorrow left. After that, you must go and face the biggest challenge of your life." Toga: "I know!" A massive man walks out from the locker rooms. He has a blonde ponytail, a tight red t-shirt, forest green khaki pants, and a hockey mask over his long face. He approaches the ring, unbeknown to the men in the ring. He grabs a folding chair along the way. Reese: "So, what is it that you do want to learn from me, since nothing seems to be sticking with you..." Toga: "I'm trying!" Reese: "I know... but tell me something that interests you the most." The skirt-clad champion ponders for a moment. Toga: "Teach me your crossface!" Mickey raises and eyebrow and attempts to keep a chuckle at bay. Reese: "My "Infinitum's Requiem?" You've got to be kidding me?!" Toga: "No! That move has given you countless victories in your career! It's your go-to move when you truly need to hurt someone!" Reese: "It's a simple move... but it's not just knowing how to use that move that has given me so much success. It's all in the execution of it and the strength and determination attached to it...." Toga: "I'm strong!" Once again, the larger man tries his best to keep a straight face. The hockey-masked man slides into the ring with a chair. He sets it up in the corner and sits on it. Neither man seems to pay much attention to him. Reese: "Well, I'm sorry. But I can't teach you to use the move effectively." Toga: "Why not?!" Reese: "Two reasons, you aren't strong enough... and secondly... well..... just try it out on me..." Thomas nods before he runs over to Mick. The GCW Hardcore Champion grabs the long, tribal-like-tattooed arm of his teacher. He tries to pull him down to the mat. Mickey doesn't budge. Toga stops. He ponders for a second... then quickly jumps onto the back of Reese. He puts his hooks his fingers in front of Reese's face, trying to pull him backwards... Michael takes one step back to better balance himself, but otherwise, still won't budge. Toga reluctantly releases the hold and takes a deep breath. His tiny mind is racing around trying to figure out how to get the bigger man to the ground to get the hold on. He glances to the giant masked man in the corner. He wanders over, politely asks for the chair. The massive man stands up and steps aside. "Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto" takes the chair, folds it up and bolts back over to Reese, going for a quick, deadly strike! Mickey deftly catches the chair, pulls it out of Toga's hands, and immediately plants the smaller man with said-weapon.... Reese: "Fail." Tommy is staring up at the lights... or the stars... or the tweety-birds.. Reese: "First off, you aren't large enough to effectively use it against the opponents you have at NC-17, but beyond that, when you are in a no-rules setting such as you will be, and when you have this many opponents, it's unwise to even try a submission hold that makes you so vulnerable. No matter how skilled you are with the move." Toga slowly rolls over to his stomach. He tries to get back up. He gets up to his knees, but his face is still on the mat. He very slowly manages to pull his head off the mat, and sit up on his knees. He holds his head, barely able to stay straight up. Reese: "I have been teaching you some wrestling while you've been here, but in reality, almost none of that is going to help you at NC-17. You know that. Mostly, you need to learn how to defend yourself effectively against strikes and whatever crazy-ass weapons might be lying around." Michael turns and nods to the even-larger man standing in the corner. The man exits the ring, lifts up the apron, opposite the camera, and starts digging around. Reese: "Let's take a look at your opponents... first off, we have Bryan Mayhem. This is HIS fight. This is his HOME. He is going to be your biggest threat... but he suffers from the same thing as all of your opponents... he's trying WAY too hard to be "The Man."" Milenko finally pulls out a Singapore Cane that has been wrapped in double-sided tape and rolled around in broken glass. The masked-man steps back up onto the apron, then over the top rope. Reese: "Just look at his appearance, for starters. Who the hell paints their face black and white, and tattoos "666" on their chest? He's obviously delusional... though he would have to be, wouldn't he? But that's not necessarily a good thing. It might make him unpredictable and pretty much willing to do whatever it takes... but it means he's completely unstable... something else your opponents all have in common. Being unstable is synonymous with being reckless. Willing to do whatever it takes, but incapable of putting much fore-thought into anything. Basically, he's trying way too hard. He's obviously just as worried to "look" the part, as he is to "play" the part. Mean's he's going to go out of his way to make sure he dominates everyone in the fight. Means his only goal isn't the championship. It's to maim his opponents." Milenko hands the cane to Reese. Reese: "Thanks, Milenko." Michael winds up like he's going to strike Toga, who hasn't really came to his senses from the chair-shot yet. His gaze eventually finds Reese... Tommy almost looks like he's going to crap himself, as he immediately snaps out of it, falls over, and scurries to the farthest corner... Reese: "While it is wise to avoid the weapons whenever possible... the terror on your face means you can't focus on anything but the weapon itself. You have since forgotten who is holding the weapon. Anyone holding this could be a threat, but what I'm saying is, you've lost your focus on the bigger picture. You aren't thinking about how you might be able to use my dependence on this weapon to your advantage, or how you might counter it, as hard as that might be. Your mind is telling you to stay far away from me, which isn't bad. But it's not the only option and you MUST be able to weigh those options instantly." Toga stares at him, wearily. Reese: "As menacing as this weapon is... I can promise you that you are going to find less traditional wrestling weapons in your fight in Belmont. Hell, I wouldn't be surprised to see chainsaws and swords..." The skirt-clad coward raises his eyebrows... then a look of terror falls over him once again. He hadn't even thought of that. Reese: "But back to your opponents, let's take a look at Shaman. He is a bit more likely to be focuses on what should be the goal for everyone in the match... The title. His appearance is simpler. Off his rocker, yes, but not as much as Mayhem. He has made it clear that he plans on proving to you that you don't belong in the Hardcore title scene. He might be your biggest personal threat, but not the biggest threat in the fight. That is, once again, Mayhem. He is suffering from the same delusions as anyone in the match, he's hell-bent on destroying the opposition. Typically that would play out in his favor, but from my understanding, the goal isn't to destroy the others as much as to retrieve the title. His eyes, are most certainly, not on the prize like they should be." Michael sets the tip of the cane on the mat and leans against it a bit. Reese: "Next we have Dorn Tracer.... a dumber name I have never heard. He has almost been a ghost lately. Showing up only when necessary. He likes to think of himself as being a methodical, vicious technician... and he is definitely the weakest link in this match. He has kept to himself almost entirely in the past couple months, which means, like the rest, he's going to be a bit unpredictable... Maybe he's been focusing on this fight this whole time. Which would mean he might be your biggest threat... but I think, most likely, he's just simply given up. He's in this fight. He'll do what he can. But his desire to compete is lacking. He is not going to be focused on the title as much as hurting people, but then again, he might not even be able to focus on that. This man is a lost soul." Toga is still sitting in the corner, listening to Reese, but keeping his eyes on the glass-covered cane in his hands. Reese takes a breath. Reese: "And finally, we have Donny Diamond. He shares something in common with everyone else in this match... he's fucking bat-nuts crazy! Everyone in this match has proven that they can and usually will be sadistic. But this man is possibly a murderer... beyond that, he's part of a cult. Unfortunately, he hasn't drank the kool-aid yet... and he should. Unlike your other opponents in this match, Donny appears to be under full control of himself. But he's shown that he obviously isn't. Outside of the ring, aside from being a cultist, he appears to be able to focus on the big picture. In a match or a fight, he seems to lose that focus. He is the biggest wild card of the match. He is either going to be set on the title, or he's going to be set on destroying everyone else." Michael raises the cane and starts staring at the shards of glass covering it. Reese: "So, what do I think of your chances for this fight?.... Actually, I think they're pretty damn good... if you play it smart, which prior to you winning the title, seemed impossible for you to do. You've become a new man since you won that championship and you've managed to surprise me and everyone else. But you need to pay close attention to your opponents, and you need to keep your eyes on the prize; The GCW Hardcore Championship. Everyone else in this match has proven that they're perfectly fit for this match. They all have the same agenda... to destroy one another. But they seem to be forgetting that this is for a title. There is a greater purpose to this match than to be the craziest, most sadistic mother fucker in GCW. I've met men like these before, but never have I seen so many in the same place. I have no doubt in my mind that if you play this defensively, smartly, and remain focused on the goal, you can win." He points the cane at Toga... Reese: "But first... you need to prove that you can play defensively... so, I would like you to come over here.... and TAKE this cane from my hands." Toga shakes his head violently. Michael glances over to the masked-man, Milenko. Reese: "Fine..... I'll do it!" The purple-haired trainer hands the cane back off to the larger man, Milenko. Reese: "But Toga, I want your FULL attention on this." Toga slowly nods, seemingly scared for Reese's sake. Michael takes a couple steps away from Milenko, then nods to him. The giant man approaches Reese, holding the cane like a one-handed sword. Milenko goes for a high slash, aiming for the chest or neck, Reese ducks under it. A low slash, Reese hops over it. A thrust, Reese leaps backwards, but trips! He falls backwards, and Milenko sees his key opportunity to strike! The masked-man brings the cane down like a hammer but Reese is too fast for him; he rolls backwards over his shoulders, then using his arms helps propel himself back up to his feet. Milenko once again goes for a thrust, with Reese in the corner! Michael barely manages to sidestep out of the way. He goes for a quick right hand towards his attacker, but Milenko tilts his head away. Milenko attempts to regain a suitable stance to strike with his weapon again, but Reese goes for a left punch. Milenko once again, barely manages to tilt his head away, but is now reeling backwards, trying to get his stance back. Mickey sees his opportunity. He quickly slides beside Milenko and goes for a leg sweep. However, Milenko manages to hop over it... Reese was prepared for that. He sits up, quickly, and lays one of his feet flat under the masked-man's. Milenko lands on Reese's foot. Michael takes advantage of this and quickly pulls his foot out from under the larger man. Milenko stumbles back into the corner, as Reese rolls back on his shoulders, then kips-up to his feet. He charges Milenko. The larger man goes for a mid-torso swing, but once again, Reese uses a baseball slide to avoid the swing. He kicks Milenko in the shin, then as soon as he stops sliding, he lays flat on his back and delivers a swift, ultra-cringe worthy kick to the larger man's jewels! He falls to his knees, as Reese rolls away and quickly hops back up to his feet. He takes a half-second to size up his kneeling opponent, then delivers a perfectly-placed mauy thai kick to the giant's skull! He collapses, and Mickey quickly grabs the cane from his fallen opponent. Toga stares at him in awe. Reese: "And that... *deep breath*... is how you unarm an opponent with a weapon too dangerous to grab. Remember, that you don't have to fight honorably if your opponent is trying to end you with a weapon such as this!" Tommy stands up, as Milenko rolls out of the ring, holding himself. Reese: "Are you ready to try?" The klepto shakes his head no, then points outside of the ring. Toga: "Let's do it out of the ring, since I won't be in one for my fight..." Michael smirks a bit, proud that his student has taken something into consideration that he, himself, hadn't. They both climb out of the ring, as Milenko wanders off, slowly, still holding himself. Reese: "I'm not going to hold back... so, you had better be ready and willing to give me everything you've got as well!" Toga nods, then charges Mickey. The teacher quickly sidesteps, tripping his student. He drops the cane towards Toga, but stops it a centimeter from Toga's skin. The skirt-wearing freak doesn't move, knowing that he's already lost. Reese: "Game over." Michael moves his weapon away from the smaller man. Toga slowly rolls over, glancing up. Reese: "What was your biggest mistake?" Toga: "I... I got reckless." Reese: "Well, yeah, but how were you reckless?" Tommy rolls over and sits up. Toga: "I... um.... charged you?" Mick nods. Reese: "Exactly. While sometimes you can catch your weapon-wielding foe off guard by charging him, it's not usually the best approach. They have the weapon, which means instant confidence. You don't have the weapon, which is going to deplete your confidence. Unless you're 100% sure you are better and more agile than your opponent, you DO NOT go to him when he has the weapon." Michael offers his hand to help Toga up. The Hardcore Champion accepts. Mickey pulls him to his feet. Reese: "In most cases, because someone has the weapon and you don't, they are more willing to approach you blindly. They know they have the advantage. They can inflict a lot more damage to you than you can to them with a single strike. But their greatest strength, the weapon, is their biggest weakness too... They typically become reckless as well. Remember how I first dealt with Milenko? How I let him come to me? That's what you do. You let them come to you. If they won't, then you get out of there. You're faster than they are. So, turn and run. Gain higher ground if possible, but mostly, MAKE THEM come to you." Toga returns to his starting position a few paces away from his weapon-wielding foe. Reese: "They have the weapon. And considering how psychopathic your opponents are, they're gonna want to use it. It's not just about the championship to them. It's about eliminating all doubt and opposition. They want to destroy you just as much, if not more, than they want the championship. They will come after you. If they don't, then once again, remember, you are faster than them. It they try to go after the goal, the title, and you aren't in their way, then follow them. Run around them if you can, if not, then take advantage of the fact that their back is probably to you now." Tommy nods, seemingly understanding what his teacher is teaching. Reese: "Now... let's begin again. Remember, they may have the offensive advantage, but you have the defensive one." Toga and Mickey circle each other for a second, before the larger man charges him. Toga stops and waits the split second for Reese to reach him. Michael attempts to clothesline Toga with the cane, just as Milenko did to Reese earlier. Toga manages to duck it, barely. Toga strikes quickly with a swift punch to the ribs of his attacker. It barely stuns Reese, but stuns him it does. Long enough for Toga to get away anyway. Toga slips past him and regains his footing. Mickey turns around and glares at him, playing the role the best he can. Once again, Mick charges his opponent. He goes for the same upper swing as before, but this time, he's faking! Tommy ducks, but the cane doesn't pass overhead. Reese kicks him in the chest, then quickly twirls his cane around and goes for a piercing stab to the chest of his fallen opponent. Toga's eyes widen, knowing that Reese isn't going to stop the cane if the champion can't get out of the way... but he manages to, suffering just very light scratches from the glass on the cane to his back, as he rolls away. Reese quickly turns and goes for a hammer-like strike, but Toga just keeps rolling with it. Michael misses, then chases after him, and tries again, this time, Toga quickly changes directions and rolls INTO Reese's path... but between the path of Mickey and his cane. Toga gets kicked in the ribs, but the larger man ends up tripping! Reese falls on top of the cane, barely missing face-planting it. He rolls over to his back, as he tries to quickly pull the cane from his shoulder area. Toga quickly gets back to his feet, holding his ribs. Michael removes the cane, but not all of the glass, from the wound, then sits up. Shining Wizard knee strike to his face, from his more agile opponent! Reese falls to his back, with the cane beside him, but still in his grip. Toga reaches down to grab the cane, but the slightly-dazed Reese quickly throws his knee up, nailing him in the forehead! Tommy stumbles back and falls onto his ass. Mickey sits up, then rolls to his knees to stand up, with his hand pressing down on the cane on the floor. Toga regains his composure, hurries to his feet, steps on the cane, then kicks Reese in the face with the sole of his boot! The larger man's fingers gets pinned under the cane, as he reels back. He returns upright, dazed.... Toga grabs Reese by the back of his head, then pulls the leg that isn't standing on the cane back. Michael regains his composure just in time to get a direct knee under his chin! His fingers slip out from under, and off, the cane! He falls to his back! Toga quickly bends down, grabs the cane from under his own foot. He stands over his fallen teacher, with the power... Reese opens his eyes to see the cane above his face. Toga touches the tip of the cane to Reese's nose, with a smile on his victorious face! Toga: "Booya!" The GCW Hardcore Champion tosses the cane away, and offers his hand to help pull his teacher to his feet. Mickey stares at him, once again back to his stoic-ness. He takes a deep breath, and reluctantly takes Toga's hand. Using all his weight, Tommy helps pull the larger man back to his feet. Reese nods to him, before he starts picking the glass out of his shoulder. Reese: "Good job. You've managed to best me... which is something I never thought would ever come from my lips. You fought well. You took each opportunity you could to hurt me.... but you still got lucky." The smile on Toga's face slowly fades. Reese: "I'm not trying to be pretentious, but you did not manage to convince me that you could do that a second time. There were too many close calls.... but it's a start." Mickey takes a look at the clock on the wall a couple yards away from him. He takes in a breath. Reese: "We should take a break and bandage our wounds. But in an hour, I want to be back out here. I want you to unarm me again...." Tommy stares at him for a second, before nodding with a confident smirk on his face. Reese: "Kid... you might just be worthy of that championship after all...." The smirk turns into a full-fledged grin, as Reese pats him on the head and walks towards the doorway Milenko came, and left, through. Toga glances back at the cane on the floor, nods in approval of his own skills, then scampers over to catch up with his teacher. In just a couple more days, Toga will be in Belmont for the biggest fight of his life... Will he be ready? Will he be able to walk away with the championship? Will he even be able to walk away to begin with? All this and more will be answered at Global Championship Wrestling's biggest event of the year.... NC-17!!!
