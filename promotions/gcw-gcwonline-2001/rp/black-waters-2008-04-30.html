Black Waters

30 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30 April 08 STATIC “Hello, this is Steven Caldera, CEO of Caldera Enterprises Incorporated. Today, myself and a crew of brave men and women will search the open sea ? in search of a creature that has stood the test of time. Today, we will make history. Let me introduce you to the crew?” STATIC “Has anyone ever even seen this thing?” “There have been documented sightings but no one could quite explain it’s entire look. We only know that it’s vicious and huge.” “A good combination eh?” “Extremely.” STATIC “Is that it!”” “Where!”” “Starboard! Starboard!” The camera falls as someone knocks it over. “Shit get the camera!” STATIC “Good going Octy. You saw a damn whale, how could you mistake that for our monster?” “Guess we’ve been out here too long Sir.” “Next you’ll spot a school of fish and think it’s the Loch Ness Monster.” “Well ? Nessy would be pretty damn cool.” STATIC “What do you got?” “Full house boys!” Everyone around the table slams their cards down. “Yeah fuck you too.” STATIC “Mr. Caldera here again. It is now 9:37 P.M and we have not made contact. I didn’t really think we would yet either but it was worth a try. The sun is just about setting below the horizon now. (He points, the camera follows to see the sun setting, making quite a beautiful scene.) Check that out right there, amazing isn’t it” (The camera moves back to Mr. Caldera) Hopefully day two brings us better luck.” STATIC “This shit stinks.” “Well it is bloody, dead fish.” “So your date from last weekend?” “You should know, it was your wife.” “BURN!” “Ok guys, let’s focus here.” STATIC “Nothing again?” “No sir, just more of the same. Whales mostly. They are large enough to give us false hope once in awhile.” “Well get some sleep ? hopefully tomorrow will be better.” “Very well sir.” STATIC “We’ve just awaken on day three. Getting ready for a quick breakfast before getting to work. I’m getting excited because I don’t believe it should be much longer, most of the sightings have been near this area. Wish us luck!” STATIC The camera zooms in on Steven Caldera sleeping on a lawn chair, hat covering his eyes from the sun. Loud snoring fills the air and the air coming from his nose and mouth flip his hat up with each breath. Laughter comes from beyond the scene. STATIC “Well the tranquilizer weapons are working. Got this one from a pretty good distance.” “Nice ? at least we know what’s for dinner.” “Not sure if it’s a good idea to eat a fish with a shit load of tranq in it’s system.” Silence? “Never did drugs before?” STATIC “Another day, another lack of visual of the beast. We worked a bit longer today as you can see. The Sun set about an hour ago so we’re now surrounded by black waters. It’s a really nice nig-? “SIR!” “What’s wrong?” “There’s something coming.” “Another Whale?” “I highly doubt it.” “Why?” “It’s big ? real big.” Caldera looks back to the camera. “This could be our lucky night.” STATIC “Get the damn fish in the water! He’s here somewhere!” “Everyone grab a rifle! This fucker won’t be easy to take down.” “Where the hell is he!”” “Sir, if the sonar is correct he should be right under us!” The entire boat quakes from being hit, the camera tumbles to it’s side. “He’s here.” STATIC “FIRE! FIRE! FIRE!” Darts fly through the night air, hopefully hitting the target these men are after. “GOD DAMN IT WILL SOMEONE HIT HIM!” “We are sir!” “It’s not affecting him!” The camera slowly moves towards the edge of the boat, in time to see a dark shape underneath the water flash by. The cameraman stumbles backwards, almost falling down. “What the fuck.” The cameraman whispers. “We’re gonna die.” STATIC “What the hell did you get us into Caldera!”” “I’I don’t know.” The boat trembles from a massive hit, everyone on board falling down. It’s but a moment later when the boat is ripped in half from the incredible force of another blow. Screams fill the area. “Oh My god! Oh my god!” STATIC Caldera, Octavian and obviously the cameraman are huddled together on one half of the boat. The camera shakes violently as the man holding it, trembles in fear. “Why the hell are you still holding that”!” Octy asks. “I-I ca-can’t let go.” The camera turns to the other half of the boat in the distance, survivors holding on for dear life. Suddenly their screams become frantic ? Then their boat is pulled down below the surface. The dark mass heads straight for them. STATIC A dorsal fin knifes through the ocean’s surface as the beast speeds towards it’s prey. “Shit the fin must be seven, eight feet high!” “No! No! No!” Just before the fin reaches their portion of the boat, something both spectacular and frightening happens. The great beast leaps out of the water, showing off its massive triangular head and hundreds of rows of teeth, each 8 inches long. “NOOOO!” STATIC BLACKNESS The gasps from the people in the audience help stop the auditorium from being deathly silent. Slowly the lights come back on, the crowd still in shock. Their eyes eventually shift from the scene and back to the only man standing on the stage. Steven Caldera places his hands on each side of the podium, his head lowered, his emotion showing through. The crowd waits for him to continue. “Ladies and gentlemen, what you just witnessed is the account of events that really happened. I lost nine men on that voyage, a voyage to capture a monster that man had never really seen up close and personal. It was all simply hearsay ? not unlike what our friends overseas have in Loch Ness.” Caldera shifts his weight, takes a deep breath. “Some people claimed what they saw from a distance was a giant whale, understandable really. People also had estimated this thing could be anywhere between 50 and 60 feet long. Others thought maybe just a school of fish, enough to play tricks on your mind right?” Caldera sighs. “They were all wrong, dead wrong. What we were dealing with was a creature far more intelligent, fast, strong and large than we could have ever imagined. An 83 foot, 76,000 lbs, Shark ? Or as most would call it, Megalodon.” The crowd does their necessary ooh’s and aah’s. “We were not prepared to attempt to take down a creature of this size. Our miscalculations caused many people to lose their lives ? It was a night that will forever be remembered. Those brave men and women deserve to be remembered as heroes. I will ensure that happens.” The crowd applauds. “Of course as you all know, I am not the only survivor of the attack. My captain, Gaius Octavian also survived, please give him a hand.” Applause ensues once again, a roaring applause actually. Once the crowd dies down, Caldera continues. “Today you are here because of this tragedy, to pay some respect to all of those who did not make it. However you are also here to see history made once again. As everyone is well aware, our high dosage of tranquilizer that was pumped into the great beast DID in fact begin to take effect. We were also aided by AIR support that quickly flew in once communications were lost. I am here to tell you that the deaths of my crew will not be for nothing! We captured the beast! This Megalodon and now we will learn all that we can from it. This is truly an incredible day. You are all here to catch the first glimpse of this monster up close, to marvel at its vicious beauty. Now any questions?” Of course every hand flew up immediately, voices clamoring to be the first chosen. “Mr. Caldera, what kind of structure has been built to contain such an animal?” “Well right outside we have, “The Establishment”. It is what equates to a large fish tank, of course it’s no ordinary fish tank. I will not bore you with all of the details but just know that there is no way that shark will be getting out, ever.” Caldera carefully chooses someone for the next question. “Mister Caldera, has there been a name given to this animal?” “Yes, we call him, “Knight”.” “And why that name?” “Well as you’ll see, he is no ordinary shark. Besides his large size, he also has a unique jet black color and a set of piercing blue eyes. It is truly amazing. So he just reminded most of us of a Black Knight.” Hands rocketed up again ? but Caldera cuts them short. “Sorry, I’m getting word that everything is prepared outside. Any further questions can be answered on a personal level. You all have my contact information. Now if you would please, follow me outside ? the show is set to begin.” Anxiously the crowd moves their way out of the auditorium. Caldera walks off stage and approaches Octavian. “Very good sir.” “Was I convincing?” Steven asks. “I would believe you cared about all that bullshit, especially the couple tears you shed. Nice touch.” “I thought so too. Fuck that, We’re alive and that’s all that matters. Now we can make a fortune off of that enormous killing machine. Time to put on a show.” “As Always.” - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Confusion Hunger Frustration Anger These feelings consumed him, he couldn’t help it ? he’s an animal. A freak of nature, the kind that was never meant to be held captive, to be given restrictions. His captures weren’t prepared to deal with him the first time around, unaware of his agility, strength, violent tendencies. Well they won’t be prepared to handle him this time either. Anger Confusion Hunger Frustration A mix of emotions that would normally make a person more inclined to screw up, make mistakes. However with this beast, mistakes don’t come often. He only has one purpose, to seek and destroy. To devour, to kill ? to be the OMEGA. From beneath the surface, a devil bides his time. He is a force of nature An act of GOD. And no man can hold him captive. Until now no man was silly enough to try. The commotion from above irritates the great beast. His Anger grows? It’s time to get from behind these walls, To do what it is in his nature to do.
