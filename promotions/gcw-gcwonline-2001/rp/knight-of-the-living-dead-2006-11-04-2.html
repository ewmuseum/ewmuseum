Knight of the Living Dead

4 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 Nov 06 “Good match tonight.” As they always are. Another night, another match, another win. Only thing different about this particular night was that my ? friend ? as strange as that is to say at times came along to watch. Alex Grey hadn’t been around a GCW arena for a very long time, as a matter of fact he looked a bit nervous. “Thanks, glad you enjoyed the show.” We were heading towards the parking lot. The night was over so it was time to go out, get drunk and get hit on by intoxicated young women. I was more interested in the drink but sometimes it was amusing to watch the women try to not notice me. What can I say? I’m irresistible. “What kinda place should we go to tonight?” “Fuck if I know Roberto.” Alex shrugs. “Somewhere with beer.” “I second that.” Finally the garage doors. We were merely a short drive away from getting drunk. I figure after winning another match I was authorized a good time tonight. I push the doors open. “NO GET AWAY FROM ME!” Both of our heads turn to find where the voice is coming from. No I don’t know who it was but the voice is most definitely two things. Female and Terrified. A look of concern forms on Alex’s face, probably mirroring my own. The sound of shoes slamming against the concrete fills the air ? problem is there are many more than just two people, that’s obvious. We reach the end and a body flies up to me, startling everyone involved. “Help!” “Tessa?” Yes, now I’m confused. “What’s wro?” “Holy shit.” Alex took the words right out of my mouth. A mob of fans were running straight for us. “RUN!” Not that I really had to give that bit of advice but I do ? and we run as fast as we can towards the entrance to the arena. Alex reaches the doors first, holding them open for Tessa and me to run in and then slamming it shut. No more than a few seconds go by before an incredible thud is heard and the doors fly half open. Alex and I slam into the doors, forcing them shut ? or at least for as long as we can. “Tessa, get something we can lock this fucking thing with!” I use every bit of strength I can muster to hold the door closed. Sweat pours down my face and I can feel the veins popping out of my head. The sounds on the other side of this door are almost inhuman. Tessa returns with a rather heavy metal pole ? quickly we use it as a barricade. The pounding continues but the door will no longer push open. “What the fuck was that?” Alex asks. “Tessa?” “I-I-I’m not sure.” I approach her. I know she’s shaken but explanations were needed. “What happened?” “Ciel and I were leaving. We heard some commotion and there was a group of people huddled around in the parking lot. We didn’t know what was happening or if something was wrong. Then they came after us.” “Wait ? where’s Ciel?” “Dead.” She said flatly. “They ? well?” “They what!”” Alex chimed in. Her eyes found mine. “They caught him and then they began to eat him.” ?””””. ?”” Alex said ?””””” I say “Yes ? you heard me right.” It takes me a moment to really figure out what to say. I mean I don’t think I’ve ever ran into this problem before. Battling cannibals wasn’t covered in my GCW contract or my martial arts training growing up. “Cannibals?” “I think it’s more than that.” “Look guys,” Alex speaks up. “Whatever it is, I think we’d be better served figuring it out somewhere OTHER than right near the door they are trying to rip down. Can we go please?” “Sorry Alex, let’s move.” So we move down the hallways although I’m not 100% sure where it was we were moving to. All I know is that a hiding spot or a clear way out was needed. By the look on my company’s faces they would agree. “HEY!” A voice and a body comes flying from around the corner. My first reaction? Send a punch which connects squarely to the nose, flooring whoever it hit. “OUCH!” “Kevin?” Kevin Jacobs. I don’t feel bad about punching him in the nose. I help him up anyways. “You alright?” “Fine.” “Good now tell us what the fuck is going on here.” “I’m not exactly sure what it is. What I do know is that everyone was filing out of the arena when someone bursts through the doors from the outside, covered in blood. They had been badly wounded by what appeared to be cuts and bite marks. We lost him.” I’m still confused. “How the hell does that cause a riot?” “Well?” Kevin looks nervous. “Well?” “We only thought we lost him. Moments later he got back up but he was different.” “How different?” Tessa questions. “He attacked us. Clawing and biting anyone who tried to help.” “Alright so let me see if I get what it is exactly that you are trying to tell us.” Alex cuts into the conversation. “We’re not facing just an angry mob of fans?” “No.” “Or just some weirdo cult who likes to eat people?” “No.” “Not even deformed, inbred freaks?” “No.” “So that leaves ?” “Looks we don’t know what it is. All I know is that it is spreading and fast.” Kevin says. “Roberto I take back what I said.” “About what Alex?” “About me having a good time here tonight. Now I am having an incredibly bad fucking night.” Even among everything that is happening I can’t help but chuckle. “Well let’s make it out of here to try again alright?” “Let’s go.” I guess sometimes nights don’t go according to plan. Actually sometimes a night goes by that a script couldn’t have even been close to being written. At least not in a realistic manner. Tonight was one of those nights. ________________________________________________ “Do it.” We have seen far too many die on this night for us to hide who we really are. I stand with Blaise and Gray, locked inside of a male bathroom. We had been forced to hide our true nature to fit in with this world that we live in. Our power is great but the fact is, our numbers are not. Tonight we are finding out that there are some things worse than our kind. “Shadow, maybe it’s not wise.” “It is Blaise. Vivica was killed and Mayhem has disappeared. Our numbers our falling. If we don’t act now everyone will die.” “So be it.” First it happens in their eyes. They transform into nothing but black holes in their heads. The veins all throughout their body begin to protrude from their skin. The sounds of bones breaking and in turn changing themselves into something completely different fill the room. The pain is evident by the screams of pain. The screams however change into growls which turn into howls which turn into roars of anger. Each man falls to their knees bringing their hands up to their face. The claws force themselves out from those trembling hands. “Beautiful.” I whisper. The hair forces its way out, growing longer and fuller. Their snouts burst forward causing those roars to become even louder. Their bodies grow larger and more muscular by the second. The last bit of change ? the razor sharp fangs that come down. They are now completely unrecognizable. They are now who they really are. “Now let us go.” I let my own fangs slid down and show them selves. I move toward the door and kick it open, ripping it off its hinges. The hallway is full of these creatures. The creatures that have taken it upon themselves to eat everything in their path. It was now time that they felt the same kind of pain. Blaise attacks first, pouncing on one of them and using his claws to swipe the throat, ripping the head off. Gray runs in the middle of a group of them and begins ripping arms and legs off before leaping onto one and biting its head off. “My turn.” I leap through the air, sending a fist through one face and then swiftly turn, driving a straight kick to the throat of another. The blood flowed like a river. The violence was great. We destroyed disgusting creature after disgusting creature. Yet one thing was becoming incredibly clear. “There’s too many of them!” They kept coming. They kept trying to feed. Blaise tossed two more, head first into a wall, crushing their skulls. Gray wrapped his paws around two more throats, snapping them in two. Then Blaise has one leap onto his back and bite down hard. A groan of pain escapes his throat. Three more rush Gray, knocking him to his side. “NO!” I yell ? but it’s too late. The numbers swarm my two brothers in arms until you see nothing but these monsters, these zombies. I have to get out. _____________________________________________ “MOTHERFUCKER!” The pain surged up my arm from the vicious bite. Those assholes just keep coming. Sitting with my back against the wall is the first break I’ve had since all of this happened. It’s kind of ironic to me. Since I was born I could feel this hatred and this evil lurking inside of me. I’ve done horrible things, however necessary they were. I’ve talked of hell on Earth. I never expected this. I have been through my brother’s murderous intent. The Sick upbringing of my parents. My home of Belmont crashing down on top of me. I now think it was all as a way of getting me ready for this sick day. This is a day that I was made for. No way will I just sit by and give my life to these sons of bitches. Tonight I fight until my last breath has been given. I stand up, extending my huge frame. I walk forward and turn a nearby corner. Then I freeze. Standing before me wasn’t the zombie-like people that have flooded this arena. This was something worse. Chunks of their flesh were torn from their bodies and blood soaked into their fur. They growl at me obviously ready to have another dinner. One thing was for certain. These were no fucking dogs. “Welcome to Hell.” They attack, coming at me fast and furious. One with white (and now red) fur leaps at me. I reach out and catch the beast, wrapping my massive hands around its throat. The force of which it attacks knocks me off my feet and onto my back. The smell of flesh and blood fills my nostrils. From the side the second “dog” comes and bites down onto my shoulder. The pain is incredible ? yet somehow only motivates me more. With one powerful motion I twist my hands and snap the beast’s neck. Its body goes limp and I quickly toss it aside. The second pounces right on top of me, taking its brother’s place. I roll the monster over and take the advantage. A claw comes up and cuts open my face. Blood flows from the wounds and covers my eyes. The creature kicks, tossing me from on top of it. I wipe the blood away just in time to see it leap through the air. Then watch as a metal pipe goes right through its head? Everything went quiet. I look to my left. “Bryan,” Her voice speaks out. “Shadow ? I had him.” “Whatever. Let’s go.” Welcome to Hell indeed. __________________________________________ Kevin comes flying from behind me and blasts what used to be Draven Lox in the head with a “steel” chair. Draven’s head turns from the blow but that’s about it. Slowly his head turns, those white eyes staring into Kevin’s. Jacobs seems to JUST realize what I already knew. Those chairs don’t REALLY hurt. He turns. “Holy fuck.” Draven lunges forward with a growl and sinks his teeth into Kevin’s throat. Blood splatters everywhere. “Move! We have to get to the entrance!” Tessa, Alex, and I begin to run. A mob of Zombies running right behind us. In the distance I see the clearing where the main doors are. Closer? Closer? And! “HOLY ..!” “Who the hell are you?” Alex yells out, obviously scared. “Don’t worry Alex. I know who they are.” Shadow and Bryan. “Where are you all headed?” Shadow asks. “The entrance ? we got to get the hell out of here and find some help.” Tessa answers. No use talking anymore. I know they will follow so I walk towards the entrance. A low growl from behind distracts me. My head snaps back and looks at Shadow. Or the hulking figure behind her. “Bryan?” Forcefully he wraps his arms around shadow and lifts her up. Then bites into her neck. She screams in pain and her blood falls from her neck. But that blood is black not red as it should be. “Alex take Tessa and run for the door ? GO!” He doesn’t hesitate. Out of sight they go, leaving me alone with Bryan Mayhem ? Zombie Bryan Mayhem. “Go to hell Bryan.” He only growls back. The big man moves forward but I dive out of his way. I go up to take out his knee but he swipes his hand and it collides with the side of my head. The force is great and it sends me crashing to the floor. His powerful grip comes onto my body and lifts me up. He bites down. I reach up and grab him by the hair. Using all the strength I have I pull his head away from my shoulder and he brings my flesh with him. I feel the warm blood run down my body. I hold his head back as his teeth chomp down over and over again as Mayhem tries to get another taste. I bring a fist back and drive it forward with every thing I have. The fist drives right into his mouth ? through the back of his head but not quite reaching the skull. His body falls lifeless. I pull my arm from his mouth and stand back up. “Fucking Zombies.” I don’t waste anytime going to the? “NO!” At the doors Tessa and Alex stood. Eyes white Blood dripping from their mouths Chunks of flesh ripped from their bodies. I fall to my knees ? This is it. My breathing becomes shorter My vision becomes blurry And then? And then? Everything goes black. Happy Fucking Halloween.
