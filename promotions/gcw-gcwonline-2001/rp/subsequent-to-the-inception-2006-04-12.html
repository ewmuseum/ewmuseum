Subsequent To The Inception.

12 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-12-06 Nothing rises from my feet of clay, but it's OK Red mist spreads across my fingertips, ardor slips Don't look at me with your mother's eyes Or your killer smile Sing a lullaby ---- We'll never truly understand the concept of what death holds. We'll never fully grasp onto the possibility that we do, in fact, visit The Heavens or if we turn into a box of android cereal, or if we become absolutely nothing and time moves on like it should. Frankly, by common sense, it's officially impossible and only those that experience the occurance will ever be at one with it all. I guess that's the way things should be anyways. Why bother living this life if you already know what's going to happen to you sixty or seventy years down the road? 'Hey Ned.' 'Oh, hey Gregg.' 'I just found out that I'm going to be working the floors at Hell's food hall.' '..Shit, that makes two of us, bro.' Every obstacle that every scientist on the planet had to go through would turn into a wasted event. There would be no reason to discuss, discover, and study if the given fact of our outcome is already in front of us. So, in a slightly humorous way, I think this way is perfect. If I'm gonna turn into 'nothing' then I might as well be 'something' while I have the time. Then again, I wouldn't know. As things should already appear, I had a beginning before it even existed. The way I see it, my life started when I was twenty-two years old. Any history previous of that number is numb and encased in a block of enternally-frozen ice. Though I seem to find myself digging at it with an icepick more and more as time passes by, I honestly don't reccomend it. Like the book, or quote, or whatever says, trap your personal demons and never let them escape and never convince yourself to temporarily release them because, in the end, it's never an innocuous choice. There's so much to get into it, but I figured this would be the best way to delve. I don't want to drift people into space with my stories without knowing absolutely cock about me. That's wrong on a professional basis and an ethical basis! Shit, I remember when my mother would lock me in the basement for talking to strangers while at the bus stop. No, not because I was talking to strangers, but because it was always a discrete 'Hi' that I barely mouthed and only did out of friendliness. No, that was good enough for old mother superior. She wanted me to address everyone who greeted me with a handshake, a full anouncement of my name, and a verbal greeting. Needless to say, Hitler wasn't dead. He's now a tran-sexual retired nun that I call 'mom'. Her beliefs have always slowed me down. I hate to say it, but I hated her for it. I was never given a proper freedom and, to say the absolute least, she treated me like absolute shit. No, not even normal human shit that's dropped into a toilet. I'm talking about a diaper that has taco-shits in it after the baby got into a jar of Newman's mango salsa, that's lit on fire. I don't know what I did to her as a kid, maybe I wasn't smart enough, I don't know but, it wasn't a childhood that I look back at and smile. I look back at that childhood and find myself inches from jumping out of my 8th-floor methhouse apartment building in Sun Valley. She carried along the belief that 'ruler-disciplining' was one of the strongholds in leading your children through a proper life. I don't know. I guess I just don't know what's proper there? Like, am I supposed to spot it on the bang of the gun like all of these religious crazy nutes are doing? But anyway, onto the methhouse! The Methhouse~! The outside was terrible. It was a white house that had orange and blue graphitti on the left side of it. Three of the concrete stairs leading up to the front door are basically demolished from God knows what. The air around the apartment had a smoldering smell that kept most of the children in the neighborhood away. It wasn't that they weren't curious, it was because the parents knew what was going on there. They got the hint from the shady tinted cars that would pull up in front of the house with neon-lights covering the front grills and bass beats booming against the apartment windows. I'm saying all of this in past-tense because they've since been evicted from the apartment. The men that were busted were producers and sellers of the stimulant. No lie, I now have a meth-head living downstairs from me. Believe it or not, I'm not worried about it anymore. At least this guy will only kill himself. The douche bags that previously occupied the first floor could've blown the whole apartment off the map. I've lived here for three years now. I've seen my landlord twice. I mail my rent to him and that's that. He ignores any and every problem that involves the house and it's gotten me agitated. The only good thing is not having to frequently see your landlord. I've never gotten along with them in the first place, and frankly, don't have any plans or interests in starting now. I guess I started it. The first apartment I ever owned, right? I move in and immediately have issues with the people living upstairs and this flamboyant homosexual living in the basement. The flamboyant gayman blasts his technotronic music, I mean he BLASTS it past midnight. I asked him nicely on more than one occasion. So, one of those nights where you're so bored you don't what to do yourself comes along. I can tell I'm in an edgy-mood and the smallest of peeves start to get to me. I'm tired of moping around in hopes to discover something that I can actually 'do', and am dying for entertainment. Unfortunately, I'm kind of ugly so it's not much fun participating in a club atmosphere. Besides, the music is repulsive and half of the crowd is tapped out of their skull on speed and ectasy. I can see myself standing in the darkest corner of the place; my eyes bugged out of my cranium. I've got this burning joint in my right hand down near my hip and I gulp. 'Gods of Rock.. Please save me.' Jesus, what happened to chilling out, smoking a bone, and listening to 'Animals' or, if you're a neo-toker, some Radiohead? Now it's all about being in a race, this high-speed race. You have to move as quick as possible and the bass has to send you into a shockwave. There's this obsession for lights, soapsuds, and earaching microphones that have as much negative feedback as this year's Ozzfest. Give me a sweet guitar groove, a simple bass line, and a drummer that knows how to pull you in, and I'm good. My brother, who's no longer with us, was this kind of partyer, and to this day, it still effects me hardcore that I was never able to get him away from cocaine. So, to bring things back in order, the meth-head living below me seems to resemble my brother in appearence and in personality. I've spoken to the meth-head, whose name is Andy, a dozen times. He's even invited me to lunch at 'MickN'Dails', a diner where most of the hungover strippers and homeless people lay low. Lately, I've been wanting to know more about Andy. It's hard to hate on someone that is inadvertantly pretending to be the second shed o' skin of a sibling. The work was trash until I got 'the call' a couple weeks ago. Don't get me wrong, I love the fact that I've been signed to wrestle for a living. It's great. But, I don't remember ever signing up for it. I suppose it was something I signed up for at one of last summer's festivals while utterly trashed. Until next week, I work at 'The French Fry'. They're part of the 'burger crew', but they also specialize in gourmet french fries that are baked with spices, a homemade cheese sauce, and the crunch is ADDICTING! Crunch! Crunch! Crunch! Oh God, let's be brutally honest here, even though this could be an inadvertant ploy to embarass myself. I've cleaned piss off of the floors in the kitchen. Yes, URINE. How does urine find its way onto the kitchen floor of a fast food restaurant? People literally have hockey games in the refrigerators, using frozen chicken patties as pucks. To be an employee at 'The French Fry', you have to endure a 'condiment bath'. This is where they lay you on one of the stainless steel counters, which already has a layer of mayonaise on it, and pour ketchup, mustard, relish, and whip cream all over you. If that wasn't enough, they light you on fire. Okay. No, they don't light you on fire. They cover you in brown sugar and make you recite the alphabet backwards eight times. Sounds awful right? Well, it was. So, there. Now you know me. Or maybe you know the things you never really wanted to know? Who cares? Avant is coming to GCW with something new in store. No more plain, crusty cliche bullshit. That's right. NMPCCB, bitch.
