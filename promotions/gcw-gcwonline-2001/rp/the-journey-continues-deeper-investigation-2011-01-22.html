The Journey Continues (Deeper Investigation.)

22 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/22/11 Somewhere over Oklahoma..... Looking out the window Shaman ponders in deep thought. He turns and looks at the stewardess and nods as he makes a drinking motion with his hand. She smiles and nods as she walks up the aisle. A few moments later she returns bringing him a beer. He takes it from her and nods as she speaks. “Would you like a glass?” He shakes his head no as he turns reaching for his I phone. He watches some of the GCW promos and shakes his head. Browsing though until he reaches his opponents promo. Chuckling slightly he speaks up. “Kind of ironic if you ask me, You got some skid mark trying to do something too many people have done way before him.” The man sitting next to him looks at him confused. “You talking to me ?” “Nah just kind of speaking out loud, But since you are here I think I will explain.” The man nods listening to Shaman as he begins to speak. Drinking a bit of his beer he looks at the man. “You a wrestling fan? Of course you are if you weren't you'd be some hypocritical hippy pap smear wouldn't ya?” looking at the man sternly the man only nods as he listens. “Damn there is a lot of people nodding today, freaky like we are all bobble heads.” Thinking for a second he clears his mind. “Anyways back to the point, you see I am going against some guy named, wait a second what is his name?” Scrolling through the I Phone screen he finds the name as he speaks again. “ Playboy Johnny Davis...What the hell type of name is that?” Grumbling, he drinks some more of his beer as he taps the corner of his phone on the little drop down table. “You know it is just kind of sad that this kid thinks that he will win. Claiming to be a hardcore icon. Now that is a rich one, I have done and seen more than he has in a lifetime. He claims to have gone the distance with some guy nobody knows or gives a damn about.” Turning his head he looks back out the window. Thinking for a moment as he speaks his eyes gazing out at the clouds below. “Tell ya what Mr. Davis, Go get all of your pretty little insults, bring them to the ring. After you get an ass kickin you can get up and tell me what all the insults and bragging had gotten you.” While watching Shaman talk to himself, the man in the seat next to him raises his arm suddenly. “Uhm stewardess??” Las Vegas, Nevada 3:15 pm, LVPD Crime Lab..... Detective Reischmann strolls down the corridor holding a box in both of his hands. He turns and takes a stairwell downwards into the basement of the building. He places the box against the wall and reaches out grabbing the door handle and pulls the door open. Taking a left he walks to a large double door and bumps it open as he walks into a huge lab. Most likely the crime scene investigation unit lab. Setting the box on the table he turns and notices FBI Agent Cristoff standing there with arms crossed watching some of the computer finger print results. “Water doesn't boil if you watch it Agent Cristoff.” He speaks up as he opens the box. Cristoff turns and looks at Reischmann as he speaks up as well. “Gasoline doesn't ignite unless you apply fire or an excellerant.” Looking over at the box he crosses the floor quickly. “Sp what have we got?” Pulling out files and folders Reischmann begins placing them on the table. “ I took the liberty of pulling up all the files of anything remotely close to this case. As you can see by the dates this has been going on long before The High Spirits Casino was opened.” Running his hand over a file Cristoff notices the time stamp on it. “This one dates back to the 60's, wasn't that when the FBO began its first Mob hits in Las Vegas?” Reischmann stands up straight and nods. “There was a lot going on back then, the truth we wonder about was did they really let go of control of the Las Vegas underground.” He looks at some of the more recent photos as he looks over at the other files. He holds up another folder from the 70's and drops it down near Cristoff. “The frequency of these occurrences seem to almost be exactly ten year intervals. In fact right to the day and hour. Coincidence? Perhaps or perhaps not. There is a connection though.” Reischmann grabs his coat as he looks over at Cristoff. “Lets do a little foot work out in the desert. Let the crime lab geeks handle this.” Both men nod as Cristoff stands up straight and follows Reischmann as they walk out the door. Turning right and heading up the stairwell. Getting to the middle landing near the door Cristoff stops and looks at Reischmann. “You're not doing this to just get a cigarette?” Reischmann smiles as he opens the door to the outside. Cristoff smirks and walks out the door heading over to a Crown Victoria. Both men climb into the vehicle with Reischmann in the drivers seat. Cristoff shuts the door and leans back. “So where we heading?” “Well there are a few old places just outside of town I think we should look into.” Reischmann puts the car into gear and turns her completely around in a sharp turn and quickly heads out of the lot into traffic. 6 hours later Bodie, Nevada... Both men climb out of the vehicle and simply look around. Cristoff looks at the old abandoned ghost town and then looks back over at Reischmann. “Ok why are we here?” Reischmann casually lights himself a cigarette and looks at the buildings. He watches each building as if something is going to happen and he smiles. The wind blows through the town causing the grass to sway lazily back and forth. Reischmann finishes the cigarette, as he does so he lifts his foot slightly and leans over crushing the cigarette out on the sole of it. “I brought you here to show you that because you may not see something doesn't mean it isn't there. Bodie was used by the mob back in the 40's as a small escape place to conduct business in a sense.” He casually points at a building that seems out of place. He motions for them to walk forward towards it. Cristoff listens top Reischmann ramble for a little bit. He then shakes his head as he speaks up. “Ok what the hell does it have to do with this case?” Reischmann casually points his hand to the top of the ridge where a jeep is sitting. “Look familiar?” Cristoff looks up and recognizes the jeep of his dead agent. He turns his head eying Reischmann carefully. He then takes off in a solid march styled pace towards the jeep. His eyes shoot back and forth looking for anything out of the ordinary. He reaches the jeep and notices that something is slightly out of place. Bullet holes actually litter the edge of the jeep, but no blood. He looks around the ground for shell casings as he finds none and very little evidence of anything here other than the grass matted flat. Reischmann reaches the jeep at a casual pace and then points around the area. “Forensics think the jeep was taken here after your agents death. The matted grass was called by hard down force caused by a helicopter. Somebody is going through an awful lot of trouble to steer us away from this investigation.” Cristoff fell silent as he observed his surroundings. He gave some thought to this aspect of the investigation earlier. It was as if every time they thought they found something it turned out to be a wild goose chase. Yet Las Vegas Police new the game better than anyone else. He didn't like being made to look bad, but this was the best course of action and he knew it. “Look Reischmann there seems to be a lot of suspicions going around in all directions including yours. In some cases the knowledge you have or the ways you go about them casts you in a suspicious light.” Looking at Reischmann he doesn't flinch or even draw his gaze elsewhere. Reischmann seemed a bit taken aback by the remarks. The Las Vegas Detective never thought he would have ever been accused of being part of anything. He turned and looked back towards the west. Suddenly both me were startled by two low flying F 15 Eagles from Nellis AFB which wasn't too far away. Observing slightly Reischmann realizes they aren't far off from the target flyzones of practice pilots. They are learning the flight procedures of the fighters. Suddenly something dawns on him as he turns and looks at Cristoff who is still watching the fighters overhead. “Cristoff what if the reason nobody heard the gun shots or saw anything was because they couldn't hear them and were distracted?” Cristoff looks at him and nods his head. Turning he looks at the bullet holes and then to the fighter jets. “He was supposed to go to Nellis, Perhaps this wasn't a mob hit after all. Everything continued to steer us in that direction. Lets head to the base and turn it on ear.” Placing a hand on the jeep he turns and walks down the hill back towards the vehicle they took there. Several hours later back in Las Vegas..... Nellis AFB Las Vegas Nevada.. Cristoff waves his credentials at the gate as they both are allowed to pass. Slowly driving they slow to an official building where the Federal Bureau of Investigations flag is flying lazily underneath the US flag. They both get out of the vehicle. Cristoff turns and heads for the door as Reischmann looks all around him. Cristoff whistles getting Reischmann's attention. Both men turn and head into the building as Reischmann stares at some old photos hanging on the wall. “Been a longtime since I have been here. I was stationed here back in the 80's man. Was an F-4 Phantom pilot. Absolutely loved that aircraft.” Smiling he turns and follows Cristoff down the corridor to a series of offices. Waving his hand across the table in front of him as he looks down at the files. “Drug cases on base here, I don't know why I didn't see it before, There is a collaboration of evidence.” “We aren't exactly Homeland Security where everything is available to us Cristoff. Right now lets figure this out., according to cell phone records your agent was here at this base. It was one of the last times he was seen alive.” Holding the cell phone records forward he gives it to Cristoff. Cristoff thumbs over the records as his attention is drawn to something else. Through the window he notices some low flying helicopters. “I think we need the flight logs from the flight control officer in the tower.” After a few minutes an Airman walks in and hands a sealed folder to the two investigating the case. Cristoff opens the folder and looks at the Airman and waves him off. Reischmann walks over to Cristofof as both men take separate folders and begin thumbing through the flight data recordings on paper. Reischmann looks up at Cristoff. Cristoff shakes his head and then looks at the air field across the way from the window. “Cristoff looks like we have a long night ahead of us. There are over 40 recorded aircraft. We are going to have to speak with the pilots too.” Dropping the folder down onto the table he simply watches the field. Reischmann shakes his head as well closing the file he held. He knew Cristoff was right and this time he wasn't going to dispute it. Turning he looks at Cristoff as he clears his throat. “I'll make the coffee you get the food.” “This is a sad day for the United States Air Force.” Cristoff says as he reaches in and pulls up a folder. His eyes focus quickly as he stares at a few names. He grabs a highlighter and marks something on the page. “Where did you say Andrew Whitecloud was?” “He was flying east for a wrestling show or meeting why?” Reischmann looked over at Cristoff with curiosity. “He used to be in the Air Force as a para rescue operative. Says here he used to be an environmental specialist crew chief. On a C 130 to be exact. Perhaps we can use him after all to find the killers.” Rubbing his chin he thinks about how this would work to their advantage. A former inside man knowing the workings of enlisted men.
