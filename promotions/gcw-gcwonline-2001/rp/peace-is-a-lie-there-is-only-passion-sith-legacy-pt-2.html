Peace Is a Lie, There Is Only Passion (Sith Legacy, pt.2)

undated

Date: A Long, Long Time Ago "Dude, who would win in a fight, a Sith, or Megatron?" Chris Storms asks his best friend, Markus Stone. The thought of his friend's question echoes in Markus Stone's mind. Who would win? It would probably come down to the strength of the Sith Lord's force powers. Of course, this leads to another glaring problem. "What if Megatron ever decides to attack us during a match?" he questions himself, hoping that giving a voice to his thoughts would lead to a solution. Markus looks around himself, hoping for some sort of clue to his problem. Of course, when you spend one week after another going from one hotel to the next, you tend to realize that the hotels don't offer squat when it comes to inspirational material. Plain curtain, plain sheets, plain lampshades. Hell, even the fancier hotels are plain once you start looking past the blankets they give you. The only thing a hotel is really good for is mediation. "That's it!" realization dawns on him. Meditation, isn't that what all force users do when they try to solve a problem? Crossing his legs on the bed, Markus begins to relax. Slowing his breathing, the Master of Disasterness relaxes his muscles, one at a time. Calming thoughts run through his mind with each breath. Death, destruction, dead koalas. Mmmmm, koala meat... Delicious. Slowly, ever so slowly Markus enters a trance-like state, delving further and further into himself as the outside world slips into emptiness. Peace is a lie, there is only passion. Markus feels his emotions filling his mind, warming his blood. With each pumping beat of his heart, Markus can feel the force swelling within him, pushing out against his flesh, seeking a release, seeking to be wielded by the Master. Through passion, I gain strength. As if being injected with life itself, Markus begins to feel rejuvenated, more alive than ever before. Boundaries inside of himself are broken, and the temptation to reach out and dive into the force calls to Markus. Through strength, I gain power. Like a generator, powering itself, Markus feels the power feeding on itself, growing exponentially. Through strength, I gain victory. This, this very moment, is the exact point where Markus always stops. Like an invisible barrier, Markus can feel the force wrap itself around him, begging to be wielded. Calling for him to master it, unify himself with it, and control it. Markus cannot. Failure falls on his shoulders, angering him. He reaches out, as if to grab a hold of a glass. Markus doesn't open his eyes, knowing exactly where the paperweight is atop the television. Stretching the sinews in his arms as far as the can, he begins trembling as he attempts to push as hard as his mind can, struggling to push himself past his body and lift the object. Sweat beads on his forehead as he strains to wield the force, pushing himself to his utmost mental limits to achieve this most basic of force techniques. Markus can feel every particle in the room, every molecule around him. Markus can feel the exact point in space where his furthest atom stretches, and the conditioned air around him begins. Yet he can't push the tendrils of the force out, can't push them to wrap around the paperweight resting five feet in front of him. "Fuck!" he snaps, the rage boiling inside of him. Anger at his own failure weighs down on him, disdain at his own disappointment. The rage builds on itself, feeding the force inside of him. As if he blew open the walls of a dam, the force surges across him. Markus almost falls back, feeling wave after wave wash over him. Though not quite a wave, more like one continuous, never ending stream of pure power. Pure, raw, beckoning power. A sense of nausea stabs him in the stomach, threatening to come rushing past his throat, spilling the air around him with it's deathly aroma. Death. That's what it tastes like. Markus reacts like any normal person would. He doubles over, dry heaving the feeling out of himself. Coughing and hacking he comes back to the reality around him. Feeling no significant change around him, Markus sighs out his failure. "Better luck next time," he muses to himself. Then he notices the paperweight laying on the floor in front of him. "Holy.... Chris! Wait, shit," Markus tries to call his friend, then realizes that he's alone. He quickly reaches into his pocket, and pulls forth his cellular phone. Fumbling with it, he manages to clumsily dial Chris' number. Markus hops out of the bed and begins pacing the room, visibly shaking with excitement as the phone rings. The voicemail picks up. "Dammit Chris," he begins, waiting for the option to run through before the phone signals for him to begin his message with a beep. "Dude, dude, DUDE!! I did it, I fucking did it! I moved a paperweight! The force dude, with the force! We're gonna be Sith Lords!!! Can you believe it? Can you, can you, can you? Oh my god dude, this is amazing! I am so gonna force choke the shit out of everybody at Worldwide this week! Dude, call me back. We have so gotta practice this for the inevitable showdown with Megatron! Later dude!" Markus snaps his phone shut. "Megatron is SO going down." With that, the preparation will begin. The dogs of war shall be released, and the confrontation, avoidable as it may same to be, will commence. Soon, oh so very soon, Megatron shall be handed his defeat at the hands of the two greatest Sith warriors this side of the galaxy. So begins the reign of Darth MarkusMothaFuckStonicus....
