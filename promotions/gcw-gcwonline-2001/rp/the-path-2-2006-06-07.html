The Path ... 2

7 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 June 06 My eyes shot open and darted around the room. I was expecting to see Stevens but he was gone. Instead I was lying on a couch in a locker room. I didn’t move my body much well besides my eyes. I didn’t feel quite as tired anymore but most of all I was confused. Was it all a dream? Did the paramedics find me and pull me into a building after dragging me from the disaster that became Belmont? The fact that I had no bandages or bruises or aching muscles would say otherwise. If that were true then how long was I out? More questions that I needed answers to but wasn’t sure if I’d get them. Then suddenly a knock came at the door. The knock was soft but it made me jump to my feet in anticipation. When I realized it was just a simple knock I calmed down slightly but still moved towards the door warily. “Wh-Who is it?” “Your mother ? who do you think it is?” That voice ? It couldn’t be!” I swung the door open and standing in front of me was the always lovely and equally dangerous Eva VanLorne. She was wearing a set of tight black leather pants and a matching halter top. She let her hair flow freely about her face. She was indeed gorgeous. “Eva ? what is it?” “What do you mean what is it?” She asked as if I were crazy. “We have strategy to talk about remember?” Eva lifted and eyebrow and pushed her way by me moving into my locker room. Strategy? What the hell kind of strategy would I have to talk through with her? Slowly I closed the door and turned to face her. Eva had her back to me and her arms crossed, leaning most of her weight on one leg. “Eva what strategy?” She spun around and looked at Knight as if she were examining him for a mental illness. “You didn’t get dropped on your head too hard last week did you” I’m defending my title tonight! I have get a plan of attack for Shadow.” She moved closer to me and touched my cheek and then my forehead. “You feeling okay?” “Ye-Yeah Sorry.” I forced a smile and shook my head. “Just waking up must be half asleep still. So you won the U.S. Title from Vivica?” “The U.S. Title?” She chuckled. “Okay I know you are in a bit of denial Roberto but I am the World Champion now.” I froze ? I couldn’t help it. Eva had become the World Champion and I didn’t remember? I tried my best to hide the look of utter shock on my face but it probably wasn’t going to help much anyways. I let out a small laugh before nodding my head. “Of course ? I’m just messing with you.” “Alright well get your head into the game amigo. I need to grab my gear down in my room real fast but I’ll be right back ok?” “Si.” “Don’t miss me too much.” Then she disappeared out of the door. I ran my hand through my hair trying to piece everything together. “What the?” “Fuck indeed.” I spun around only to see Michael Stevens standing in front of me. “Don’t do that.” “Becoming a little jittery?” He said with a chuckle. “I’m confused.” “As you should be. But let me help clear the fog for you. After you recovered from Belmont you went back to GCW a brand new man. You openly apologized to Eva and promised to help her take down the threat of Dies Irae. Of course she was reluctant but a few beat downs and saves later ? she took you into her little group. Together you two led the charge and held them back. It all came down to a Four Way for control and for the World Title. Shadow vs. Dawn vs. LeStatt vs. Eva.” “And Eva won obviously.” “True but it’s the way that she won that really shines. Dawn was finished you hit her with a top rope Knight Fall to the outside of the ring. Shadow had just gotten the advantage from Eva and looked to be trying to put on the finishing touches. That’s when you slide in, spin her around and BAM! Flatten that bitch with a Knight fall.” “So Eva took advantage.” I mumbled. “Not exactly amigo. You went to go for the cover but instead you were met by Eva’s bright blue eyes. Instead of taking the title you did the honorable thing ? you picked Shadow up and let Eva finish her off. Then you stared as the referee counted the ONE, TWO, THREE.” “You’ve got to be?” “No I am not kidding you. It was how you gained her ultimate trust back.” I took a seat. It had been a long time since I had done something for someone else that didn’t give me anything in return. I couldn’t quite believe that I would REALLY do something of that nature. I looked up at Michael with a smirk. “So Knight the compassionate?” “The fans loved you for it. Ever since then they have cheered and chanted your name along with Eva’s. Also since then you and Eva have become the best of friends. She has given you that outlet that you have always wanted.” “And Mary?” “Right on schedule. Baby is healthy inside of her and she and Eva even get along to an extent.” I sat in silence for a moment, contemplating what was happening. It was a nice thought, to have that someone I could confide in and speak to. Someone who would listen. Plus I was going to be a father. “So what is my life missing?” “Well that depends.” “On?” “What you really want out of life.” I didn’t understand what he meant. Either life is good or it isn’t. There never seemed to be much of an in between with me. “I just want to be happy; it’s all I’ve ever wanted.” “Are you sure?” Stevens cocked an eyebrow. “Because I can distinctly remember a savage and cold hearted monster that used to terrorize GCW. You broke me down further than anyone ever had. Many days went by where I would contemplate suicide because of where my life had headed because of YOU.” “But that’s not who I aim to be!” I stood up and looked Stevens in the eye. “People DO change.” “Is it that you have changed” Or have you just gone soft Knight?” “I got what I want Michael. My wife, my baby, a friendship, and I am doing what I love.” “No what you have is a spot playing around in Eva’s shadow. Since that night all you have done is become her bodyguard. You have been too busy caring for her career that you have neglected your own.” “Maybe that’s how it’s meant to be!” “You are a diva LeStatt ? you NEED the spotlight.” Then I snapped, I shoved Stevens back with all of my strength sending him flying to his back. “NO!” I snarled at him. He smiled up at me from his back. My breathing was heavy and my body hot from the anger that had built up. Slowly I realized what had just happened and I backed up. Stevens pulled himself up and kept that arrogant smirk on his face. “This happily ever after” It doesn’t suit you. Look at things in the right way Knight.” “And what is the right way?” “Eva stole your soul.” He spat back. “You gave her the world but she has given you nothing back. You wouldn’t be happy in this role for one week let alone the rest of your life.” “What if you underestimate me?” I asked. “What if I don’t?” He was right though. As much as I wanted to believe that I could be the good guy I just wasn’t sure if I could now. My anger got the best of me here so quick. Would I be happy just being one of the typical guys in the locker room? Could I be content with just having the fans cheer me ? not instilling fear? “I thought you were supposed to be convincing me that I should be the good guy?” Stevens stared at me with a devilish grin. Slowly the room around me started to change. The walls began to erode and go black, and Stevens changed as well. He was engulfed in the darkness ? but I could still see his eyes. Eyes that were once bright with life, turned a blood red right before my very eyes. “Good” Now when did I ever say THAT life would be what is best for you?” Suddenly behind him another door slowly pulled open. Sounded like it hadn’t been open for years and looked that way too. Spider webs stretched out as it opened and what looked like bats, fluttered out as well. “The other path?” I whispered. “Yes the other path LeStatt.” His voice has transformed into a growl. This wasn’t Michael Stevens, this was someone else. SomeTHING else. “Where will it take me?” “Freedom! You cannot be second team and you cannot be the American Boy Scout. You were sent to this world for one thing and one thing only. Devastation. You are like a wave that cannot be stopped. You are a god amongst men. TAKE what is yours! Destroy your enemies and cement your place in history.” “But ?” There were no buts, ifs, and what’s though. Whoever this was ? was right. I was not Roberto Abitua, the self conscious guy that wanted people to love him. I AM LESTATT KNIGHT. People needed to learn that again. People needed to FEAR that again. “The path is there for you to take ? GLORY is there for you to take.” Again I begin taking steps towards the doorway. This time there is no fast beating heart or no fear of the unknown. Only confidence. This is who I am, for better or for worse. The world didn’t have to accept me ? but they would fear me and in turn they would RESPECT me. I reached the door way and walked right through. The door slammed shut behind me. And there I was, standing in the rafters. Staring down at the ring. Staring down at Dies Irae. Staring at my destiny.
