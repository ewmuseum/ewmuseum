Barry Allan and a Child's Toy

20 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/11 This ought to shame Sean and Steve into posting something.... “Okay, open ‘em,” she said with an enthusiasm a bit too strong. You’d have to know her like I did. And knowing her like I do, it made me weary to pull back my hand from in front of my face. But I do. She doesn’t look all too happy when my first reaction is to laugh. I’m giggling a bit long, long enough that she gives that weak sister’s punch to my shoulder. That cuts the chuckles for a while, the time I stand up and hug her in my left arm, messing up her hair with my right. “It’s nice, Gracie, really. What’d they fleece you for on this though?” “Don’t bother asking how much and just tell me that you like it.” “I like it. Another dime into Caldera’s… or shit, Frosty’s coffers. What do you think? Do you think it’ll fit?” ‘It’ was the US title. All mine. Cradled in my hand as my head dipped to check it’s reflection in the bright plastic. The black foam and Velcro clasps complete the product, a play thing for aspiring children to fight each other over. A token of rage for mothers who’ll find it all over the house they try to keep presentable, and wrestling in the child’s spirit is making them far from presentable. “This might just be the perfect motivation.” You’d think that maybe being in LA under the bright lights and in front of the loud and claustrophobic masses would be enough to do the trick, but I’d be lying if I hadn’t been feeling all that I used to getting my head beat in for the cheers of children. Shit, with Grady up to his usual scams and Palmer enjoying 3 square meals a day in a correctional facility somewhere, it’s been lonely. I know, suck it up princess, right? But damn it if this ain’t the type of environment that makes monsters. Seeing the smile in my little sister’s blue eyes was great at cutting down on some of that bullshit. “It’s just enough to make me want to get the real thing,” I said. “And I hope you do, but isn’t that kind of out of your hands?” she brought up, confused as much as everyone else. You'd have to be a rocket scientist, or as crazy as Frost himself, to understand the stipulations surrounding the Rampage tournament and the US title contenders or straight up US title triple threat. “I’ve got enough of a say in it, Sis. I’ve got a favour coming to me from Borealis, and even if he doesn’t listen, than I just got to win and I get a shot somewheres down the line.” If I was a bit more paranoid, looking all about the Marriott room I’d rented I might have seen her; the lady in the red hood. Somewhere Baron Von Blackberry is yelling, “RETCON PUNCH!!!”
