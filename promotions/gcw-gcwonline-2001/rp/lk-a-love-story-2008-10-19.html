LK: A Love Story

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 Oct 2008 28 November 2005 7:13 A.M. I had been lying in bed, staring at the clock for hours. The glowing red numbers had started at 4:03 and have since transformed themselves numerous times to get to where I was, 7:13. I tried to sleep, counted sheep, popped a couple of pills, watched some TV but none of it had the effect I needed it to. I thought about grabbing a bite to eat but my stomach was twisted in knots. I just couldn’t shake what had really been on my mind ? I was heart broken. Everyone has heard the old saying, “breaking up is hard to do” and I’m sure most people would just roll their eyes when they hear it again, I know I chuckled before when I heard such a clich? phrase. I don’t laugh anymore, I can’t. I know what it is like to love and then to have that love taken away from you. It hurts, makes things tough to do. I rolled over again, taking my eyes off of the clock and I stared straight up at the ceiling fan. I tried to focus on the spinning of the blades and the light “buzz” sound that it made as it whirled around and around. Again my attempts as clearing my head didn’t work. I shook my head, doing my best to wipe away the thoughts of the day’s events. But instead of clearing my mind, it just began to work in overdrive. I just couldn’t believe it, I didn’t know what I did wrong. I thought I treated her well, as well as any man could in fact. I made sure she looked her finest, I gave her love and affection and I took her everywhere with me. I cherished her and yet, I couldn’t hold onto her, like it wasn’t meant to be. Frustrated and confused and sat up in bed and spun around, letting my feet dangle from the side of the bed until my feet hit the carpeted floor. My body felt tired but my mind was racing so I stood up and headed into the front room of my hotel room and sat down on the couch. I pulled my gym bag up onto my lap from its place on the floor and unzip it. After a few moments of digging I pulled out a photo album. I smiled immediately upon seeing the first picture. It was me and her at Devil’s Night. Side by side on the throne that Caldera had built for us. Together, we were able to look down upon everyone battling that night. That night I was a king and I had everything I could have ever wanted with me. Our relationship was and is funny, maybe not comedy type funny ? but “funny”. I still remember the first time I laid eyes on her, she was with another man. It was love at first sight and that unworthy prick that she was with didn’t fully appreciate her, not the way I could’ve. Problem was that she didn’t even know I existed, not that I could blame her back then, nobody knew who I was. I’d see her off and on in the years following that first time, never really got a chance to REALLY be with her. I was anxious to get to know her intimately but it wasn’t easy, never is in matters of the heart. In 2002 I lost my opportunity to see her at all. At first I was sad but it didn’t hurt too much, I barely knew her. Of course I was excited when a year or so later I saw her again, even had the opportunity to meet her. I was so nervous, I blew my chance to make a good impression. She ended up going home with yet another man that night. It didn’t take long for things to go bad again and I wouldn’t see her for two years after that, even if I thought about her often. Very often. I flip the page in my photo album, the next picture being of me at a press conference for NC-17. Of course she was there with me again, she had quite the glow to her that night. Things just always seemed perfect when we were together, like it was fate. Perhaps it was fate that brought us together in 2005. She was more beautiful than ever in 2005 and this time I was determined to make her mine. I had done a lot of growing up since the early days and I knew, 100% in my heart, that there was no way that she would not be mine. I knew that I was the perfect man for her, nobody else would do the things for her that I would. So of course she ended up with somebody else, just like all women they seem to have this desire to be with all the wrong ones before realizing that the most wonderful was sitting in front of them the entire time. I wouldn’t be discouraged however and I continued to fight for her love ? to be the ONE. I became desperate, I had pined over it for so long that I was willing to do anything to have her. I would lie, cheat, steal ? kill. I made my move the best way I knew how, sneaky. Behind her new man’s back I had started to court her. I didn’t hide how I lusted over her and for months we flirted ? And in October I finally got her! Sometimes, something you’ve wanted so bad doesn’t live up to your expectations. I can happily say that this wasn’t one of those times. It was so beautiful, what we had. Yes I admit that I believe she looked great draped all over me but it was also vice-versa. She helped mold me into the man I could be, that I was suppose to be. We fed off each other, shared in each other’s successes. I gave my all to making her look as great as she ever had. And boy did I have to fight off others that came for her. They were relentless in their pursuit of a love of their own. They just didn’t understand, this one was MINE and I was never going to just hand her over. Of course I knew that they wouldn’t stop, I didn’t stop. I fought them off, tooth and nail, to hold onto what I held most dear to me. The love that kept me fighting, kept me living. I slammed my photo album shut and tossed it to the floor. Anger took over me, it was also mixed with confusion and disbelief with a pinch of denial. How could it be over? I kept asking myself that question, like all love sick people, figuring that it had to have been something that I did. Maybe I didn’t fight for her enough? Maybe I didn’t treat her as she deserved to be treated? Maybe I didn’t let her shine as brightly as she is suppose to? I didn’t know the answers to the questions, maybe never will. Lying in bed, the early morning hours the day after losing her however, I couldn’t think of much else. The anger began to swell up and distort my thinking. Hate would follow, that bitch falling into the hands of a man that I couldn’t fucking stand. What was she thinking!” How dare she leave LeStatt fucking Knight!” I dropped my head and planted my face in my hands. I couldn’t sleep I couldn’t eat Couldn’t breath. Couldn’t fucking LIVE. Does anyone have the time tested method of coping with the loss of the one you hold closest? There is no magic pill or counseling session that can really fix a broken heart. There was only one thing for me to do.. Get her back? I fought hard to claim her as my own and then I knew I had to battle with an even stronger drive and determination to get her back. No one could bring out the best in her the way I could. Her other suitors weren’t ME and that was enough to know that they weren’t right for her. No, fuck them, they didn’t deserve her. I knew I’d hold her close again? Because love cannot be denied. And I love nothing more than being The World Heavyweight Champion. 19 October 2008 3:37 P.M. Roberto finishes his story, his back turned to LeStatt Knight as he admires a picture of himself holding the GCW World Heavyweight Title. Knight sits in a leather chair, his feet propped up on the desk in front of him and a confused look on his face. “No one deserves it like I do.” Roberto says with a sigh as he puts his picture back up on the wall. “Some may call this a very unnatural love.” Knight says back, his grin coming back. “Maybe.” Roberto spins around to face his other self. “But it is for real. I know now what I have to do ? what WE have to do.” Roberto folds his arms across his chest and leans back on the wall, Knight nods his head in agreement. “I know you do, the match with Murray opened your eyes.” “It did,” Roberto brushes some of his hair out from in front of his face. “Murray was tough, one of the best I’ve ever faced but this is about more than that now. This is about reclaiming something that I have been without for far too long.” “The Title.” “No, it’s more than that. I love being the World Champion, not just for the leather and gold strap. It signifies power, glory, domination. It means that you are the one name and face that everyone else looks up to. In certain ways, not even Steven Caldera can touch you. Winning Dangerous Games is the first step in becoming the most powerful man in Wrestling.” “Very good.” Knight takes his feet off of the desk and moves to stand up. He lifts a picture frame from the desk as he approaches where Roberto is standing. “You need to remember what you’re feeling now. This hunger, this lust for victory. It is what will lead us to victory. I can only help so much, at some point you have to let it all out, unleash the beast.” “I will ? you can count on it.” “No,” Knight tosses the picture to Roberto, who almost lets it fall but manages to catch it. “YOU have to count on it. Words are cheap, just go out and fucking win.” Roberto goes to say something but he thinks better of it and goes silent, only nodding his head. Knight smiles and walks towards the door, stopping short of leaving. “Ya might be onto something though.” “What?” “Love ? perhaps it is what will lead you to the title again.” Knight shrugs. “I’d prefer breaking someone’s face but it might work for you.” “It’s a twisted outlook on love.” “That’s the best kind.” Knight winks before disappearing, leaving Roberto alone. Roberto looks down at the picture in his hand and he smiles. The picture shows Knight, standing over Jason Dread’s body right after laying him out at Warpath 2005. He holds the blood stained Title in his hands. Roberto focuses in on his eyes in the picture, full of lust and some kind of animalistic hunger for the title. So he looks up from the picture and stares into his won reflection in another frame hanging in front of him ? He’s happy with what he sees. The hunger is back. His true love would be the next one to follow.
