Midnight Thoughts: Part 3

2 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 2, 2007 This roleplay is NOT for High Stakes. It is a joint rp between Samantha and I. High Stakes roleplay will be up later this week. This cannot be happening, not for another night. It is day three, and it is another restless night. Fate is just teasing me now. An hour ago I was able to fall asleep. My body slipped out of consciousness and into a sweet slumber. But every few minutes that sleep is interrupted and stolen from me. Beside me, turning into me, was the gentle body of Tessa Windsor. Her arms toss and hit me in the face, knocking me out of my wondrous rest. To say I am annoyed is being polite about it. I am actually relieved when she has to get out of the bed and makes a mad dash to the toilet. Serves her right for sending me right back to square one. I’m not bitter towards her, don’t get me wrong. But this lack of sleep can’t be good for my in ring state. With how little sleep I’ve been getting lately, my eyes ought to stay sealed shut for a good twenty four hours once I close them. Unfortunately, life’s got a way of tormenting me. So instead, the opposite occurs, and every little sound is magnified by ten. Naturally, that means I can’t ignore Tessa’s pitiful whining from the bathroom. I’d pretend that I was fast asleep, and never heard her call, if it wouldn’t be so blatantly obvious that I were faking. At least she wasn’t throwing up this time. That phase had finally seemed to settle. Hopefully from here it’d be smooth sailing, not only for her sake, but my own sanity. “Scottttttt,” She calls, knowing full well that I was taking my time getting to the bathroom, which was nothing more than a few feet away from our bed. Call me a prick, but honestly, she ought to be able to handle herself, “hurry up!” Her grimace makes it obvious that I better speed it. Unfortunately for her, three nights of no sleep gives me little sympathy, “What’s wrong?” “I need toilet paper.” ?””..uh? That’s it? Toilet paper. I understand she’s pregnant and all. But seriously, I’ve been sleepless for three nights now. It’s no wonder I never bothered with this relationship stuff earlier on in my life. I wonder if she pestered Dawn the same way when those two lived together. Still, I know I won’t get a moment worth of peace unless I give in and get her some. So, I get it. Then, I return to the bed. My eyes once again close. For once, it seems I might be able to catch some Z’s. For once, my eyes are staying shut, and my mind stands still. The forecast seems perfect for a good nights rest. That is, until Tessa returns. Obviously, my insomnia’s either contagious, or my seeds just as restless as I am. Judging by the groan that she makes, it’s most likely related to the second. “How on earth did I fall asleep earlier?” She wonders aloud to herself. I’d wondered the same, for my own selfish reason. Right about now, I’d do anything to sleep. Unluckily enough, I’d been awake for the whole ordeal of her kicking and rolling around, trying to fall asleep earlier in the evening. “Couldn’t tell you,” I reluctantly answered, as I buried my face into the feathered pillow underneath my head, in hopes that she’d take the message, “if I knew, I’d do it myself.” Not out of friendly conversation, but rather concern, she inquired “What’s keeping you up?” “Just what happened between you and Dawn?” There was stillness. The night echoed in silence. For a minute I thought that perhaps she had fallen asleep. Rather Tessa had just been stunned by the blunt nature of the question. I wasn’t going to let it rest that easily though, not when she had kept me up, and now I wasn’t able to go back to sleep. “What went on between you and Dawn when you two were dating?” This time I could hear Tessa rustling under the sheets. She was stalling for time. Did she really think I would just get bored of waiting and go to sleep? Just even letting the thought of their relationship slip into my mind opened the floodgates and know I knew it would be pointless for me to even try to get a wink of rest. “You can’t possibly tell me that’s what is keeping you up.” She groaned, yet still made an attempt to move in closer towards me and get comfortable. Rolling to my side to face her, I stared directly at her. "It is. I want to know." Her eyes trailed down, in attempts to escape my glare, “It’s nothing you need to worry about. It’s done. Wouldn’t you rather think about what we’ve got right here?” Her eyes never came back up, but she assured that she was still listening to everything I had to say by flopping her arm down on me, and wrapping it around my back. I didn't want to tell her how it's been trolling my mind for days that would seem weak of me. But I needed, wanted, to know what had happened. I needed it to put the entire thing behind me. My mind needed the rest so that my body could finally get the rest it needed. "Just please, Tessa" I asked, trying to say what I wanted to say without actually saying it, if that makes any sense. To prevent her from trying to move or roll away, I wrap my arms around her as well. I wanted to be sure she couldn't escape from me. She sighs heavily, hoping it’ll send some kind of signal. The only kind of signal that offers though is one that scares me. She doesn’t want to hurt me. That’s all it tells me. That means it just has to be bad. “We spent a few nights together. That’s it.” She looked back up at me, only to see if I’m satisfied with that answer. "A few nights?" That didn't sound good. A lot can happen in a few nights, I knew from personal experience, experience that produced a bastard offspring. “Yes.” Her head nodded along with the word, “That’s all there is to know.” I couldn't shake in my head that something was being covered up. Those answers were just too empty to be honest. "So you two just sat around and talked about nothing the entire time you two were together?" Her frustrations with the topic began to show through, “Obviously not, but why are you so worried about it” You weren’t so concerned with my relationship with Seymour, and you hate the guy to death. But when I’m with a woman, it’s a totally different story. All because it’ll give you material to masturbate to. Is that all you want?” Brah! Rawr! Smash! That's what I thought in my head at least. Her comments made me so angry, my toes curled in rage. "So if it's not a big deal, then why is it so secret? Nothing this, nothing that. You wanted to know what the matter was, I told you." My arms were now off her and pulled back. "So don't go pulling Almasy into this." “You just don’t want that image,” She hissed back, convinced that I was some sort of pervert. Granted my past didn’t exactly help that out. But with her, it was different. It had nothing do with sex. It was much more so beating the crap out of anyone who laid hands on her, be it man or woman. “All you need to know is that you hurt me back then, and I took refuge in Dawn. We all do spontaneous things when we’re hurt.” I hated the language she was using. It was such blatant cover words. She didn’t want to budge, with only made me feel she was truly covering something up. Something happened, I know it did. Otherwise she wouldn’t have tried to change the topic to Almasy like that. I have never been in a relationship for an extended period of time that didn’t involve sex of some kind. I can’t possibly see how she could have been in a relationship with Dawn and the two not done something. It’s driving me nuts, I hate it. Tessa knows what happened. Dawn knows what happened. I don’t know what happened. That puts me at a clear disadvantage here. Going into High Stakes, I can’t do anything if I’m not holding any of the cards. I directed my eyes toward Tessa, practically begging with her. “Please, I need to know.” What I hate most about all this, is that I don’t want there to have been anything that happened between the two. Yet no matter how many times she tells me nothing happened, I don’t think I’ll ever be able to believe her. Funny, as much as it is frustrating, that the answer I want, is the answer I can’t accept. “You don’t,” She flat out answered while crawling out of the bed, pulling the blankets along with her, “and if you can’t accept that answer, I’ll catch the first flight back to Boston.” Sitting up in the bed, I watched as she headed toward the door. “Stop” I said, “please don’t be like this.” I dropped my head down, not knowing where to go from here. I wanted to press on, to see if I could get her to crack. But at the same time, I didn’t want to drive her away. “Then stop bringing up the past,” She shouted back, regardless of the fact that I was begging her sincerely, “I’m with you. Not Dawn, not Seymour, or anyone else. I gave up my chance to finally make a damn name for myself in the business for you. Something you wouldn’t do for me. Yes, I had sex with Dawn. Okay” Is that what you want to hear? Go on and tell the damn press, and chalk up a few more points up for yourself.” There are only a small handful of times I have ever cried. It’s a weakness, something a man should never do. I’ve prided myself on rarely ever showing a soft side of myself. But I have to admit, as much as it shamed me, I started to cry at that point. It was slow, just a single tear I could feel rolling down my cheek. But others followed, causing me to need to wipe my face with my wrist. “No,” I told her, still staring down at the bed sheets that remained on the mattress, “it’s not what I wanted to hear.” “Then what do you want?” Staring dead at me, she must have seen my tears and just not cared. I had it coming to me for years now. I wished I had an answer for that. But I couldn’t think of anything to say. That flood gate of thoughts seemingly had run dry, leaving my mind a blank slate. Slowly moving my eyes up toward her, I pleaded, “Just tell me you still love me.” Her eyes darted in the opposite direction, “Why” You wouldn’t believe me anyways. Besides, you got to me first. Isn’t that all you wanted from me?” “Come back to bed with me,” Tessa didn’t budge though, holding onto the blanket in her arms as if it was a life raft. “I’m sorry for bringing it up, I shouldn’t have.” Her head dropped, as she rather unwillingly plopped back down beside me, seemingly ready to cry at the drop of a hat herself, “It doesn’t matter. You would’ve found out anyways, I’m sure.” “No, I shouldn’t have pushed you to tell me when you didn’t want to. I’m sorry.” Giving me another look, Tessa seemed to be debating over if she actually believed my apology or not. Either she was too tired to continue to fight, or she was willing to accept my apology, “It’s fine. Just go to sleep.” I knew it wasn’t fine, but I didn’t want to drag out the fighting any longer than it already had been. Laying my head back down to the pillow, I turned my back toward her and whispered loud enough for her to hear me “love you.” She gave me a weak “love you, too” in reply and curled up to sleep. In the morning, I decided, I would try and talk with her again. But for now I was going to let time try to heal this wound. Man, I need some sleep.
