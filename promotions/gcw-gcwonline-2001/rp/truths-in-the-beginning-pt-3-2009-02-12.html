Truths in the beginning PT.3

12 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/09 A blurred sensation and weird feeling envelopes you. The sensation passes as you stand before a bunch of glowing bluish gray orbs. They seem to float in and out of focus as if they were mere thoughts or just plain figments of your imagination. Looking through the orbs you notice a large figure. Shadowed between them, but hard to make out. The Figure reaches a large hand outwards grasping and orb holding it palm upwards. Bringing the orb towards his face he concentrates upon it. You seem to be pulled forward by an unknown force and are presenbted face to face with Shaman. He turns his gaze towards other orbs as if you are not even there. Quietly Shaman releases the orb he has in his hand as it floats aimlessly away into the white haze. Shaman turns and grasps another and looks deep into it. "Interesting that we come to this place. It is as if i am dreaming and yet remembering everything all the same. Truth is i am here to confront somethings in my past. I buried long ago. They may be hindering my movement forward in any direction. I mean after all things could be worse. I mean can it be that someone thought i would lay down and roll over in defeat?" Shaman twirls the one orb in his hand. His eyes focus on it. The images upon it show the match against B&E where he just lost the GCW Tag Team Championship. He narrows his gaze and then squeezes the orb as it bursts into thousands of little sparkling glittering orbs. "No use dwelling on this. Business has to move forward and i am moving forward. Who is my next opponent. Oh yes some guy named Wood i think. Trouble is he should do more homework and concentrate more on whats in front of him rather than some stupid soap opera at home. Not a concern of mine either way. Wood will understand soon enough where i am going to be coming from. In fact let me give him a little history lesson. Perhaps i will just move around here and find just the right piece of memory. Oh yes Jason Connstable." Reaching forward Shaman grasps another orb as it flashes a bright green and then an image appears as Shaman watches it. He tilts his head to one side as he seems to actually smile. "You know this was like my third or fourth real match. I was big and a bit clumsy. problem to it was that i had skill, but was just nervous and this guy kept playing at it. Taunting and goading me into doing something. I did do something though, as the match progressed i became more and more confident. I began using things i was trained in. I began using the massive amount of technical moves in my arsenal. You know a sweet chin buster here and followed by a swinging neck breaker. I began following move after move with changes in my tactics." Stopping he looks at the orb as if he notices something he recognizes. His eyes narrow as he watches himself work in the ring. His demand and drive is far more than it is now. His motions and actions look alien to himself. He taps a free finger at the edge of the orb. He opens his eyes a bit and purses his lips as he ponders for a moment. "That doesn't look like me anymore. People think i may have become a one trick pony lately. Oblivion this and Oblivion that. truth is i have come to realize i need further training. The match ended with me breaking this guys arm. Yeah i broke it wasn't a proud angered raging Native American moment either. I actually threw him through the ropes and hurt him when i stepped outside the ring. I grabbed the ring bell and broke his arm at the shoulder. Successfully ending his wrestling career for good." Releasing this orb he watches it drift away. He turns and looks at another orb. Not even touching it he just simply watches it as it floats to him. he pokes it with a finger as it shimmers and waves like a water globule in zero gravity. "His name was Madcoil, 7 foot terror from Mongolia. He and i went into the WWA together reigning in each others parade helping one another and then in the end things began to fall apart. he and i fought side by side at all times. Keeping the odds evened as we fought, but something just wasn't working. I mean after all he and i worked to well together. track records aside things like this are doomed to fail in the end. If something goes good eventually it is doomed to fall apart at some point. Back in the WWA he and i had numerous opportunities at the Tag Team Championship. He cost it every time with brash actions and not thinking on the direction we had planned. Some folks recently told me the something was going on within Oblivion. Only action i saw that was a mistake was bringing in Kimbusa." Shaman taps the orb as he leans back on his heals. "Madcoil fell to me in the Himalayan pit. It was done and over finally. I moved on to better things and he last i had heard had moved on wrestling in Japan and Europe. He is a mere after thought of the past. Then i realized i had to do something further. i fought harder and harder to gain my own reputation. Not some damn link to others. Then he came and all the hell that had begun was something i knew needed to be changed." An image of a man in robes flashes before all. Shaman narrowed his gaze and stared at the man. "His name is the Magician. He and i fought wars, not just the little ones. Our conflicts ended up bloody and brutal. He came at me with a vengeance and i returned the favor. I wanted him dead and no man alive was gonna stop me. he and i went through fire, glass, you name it to just get at each other." Shaman takes a deep breath as he stared at the orb. "The bastard even dropped me inside a Port A Toilet. That is one thing that i will never forget. It changed me and allowed me to choose who i have become now. The Magician disappeared for a few years and came back talking to me. He brought Darken with him as well and asked if i'd become Famine the Fourth Horseman of Apocalypse. I accepted and he was chosen as Pestilence. He and i formed the WWA Tag team of The Horsemen. Once there we made our appearance felt against the dangerous team of Havok. Deimos and Phobos were a dangerous duo and had slaughtered and conquered everyone before them, but they never faced myself and the Magician. We came at them with everything we had in a hard fought match. It lead to my first Tag Titles in my career. Unfortunately the Horsemen fell to Havok later and Magician and i had a falling out. He and i faced off for one more match and that was the end. No more Magician and no more Horsemen." Shaman simply shoves this orb to the side as he leans backwards as a chair appears and he sits down upon it. "There are plenty of bad choices and more bad decisions in my career. One example would be The Militia. A group i wanted to form to be a do gooder and fight all the bad stuff going on. Give the fans heroes and people to look up to. Truth was it was a joke, we were a laughing stock. Too blind to see it and realize that what we wanted had turned bad before it even started. I was the linch pin to the whole deal. I was the meat on the bone that carried it to its end. It should have ended much much sooner, but i refused to give up on it. I fought many a battle and many of those battles were won at my hands alone. Here let me find a specific one and show you what happened." Shaman begins pushing and shoving orbs out of the way as he looks around. Each one flutters and bumps into one another as he is searching for a specific one. Finally he seizes one and grins as he grabs it and holds it forward. "My first taste as A World Heavyweight Champion. It was the WWA's, but it was tainted. I fought hard step by step, but just at moments felt i was falling short. Then somebody nails Tracer Bullet with a chair. What did i do at that moment? I simply pinned him in a cheap fashion and became WWA Heavyweight Champion! Yeah it was kind of cheap, but i knew that i needed the opportunity." Waving his hand he lets the orb go as one simply floats to him as he looks up at it. "I regained the Championship a few years later beating the current Champion Grunge in the ring as well as Kash. I forget the fourth member perhaps he wasn't worth mentioning. 2 months later i get screwed by Kash and the Corporate lack jobs. Then after that it goes downhill. The history is there you can go read it if ya want. I came to GCW and that's where i have been until i recently returned to the WWA to work out some old aggressions and solve the issues i had before. I have a goal in my life now and that goal will be reached." Shaman stands up and taps his head. Then reaches both hands up and acts like he is removing something and then suddenly a background is seen with Shaman dressed in a black and yellow suit that in a weird way resembles Bruce Lee's suit in Enter the Dragon. Shaman holds a headset of sorts with attachments and wires. he looks at a technician and nods. "Great way to illustrate what i wanted to get across to folks Leroy. Some of that media i thought was dead and gone. I wonder how you had such vast resources to stuff like that?" Shaman turns and steps out of the projection area and walks to a door. "You know Woods you and i are going to have to fight. You are not going to like how things will be turning out in that. I will knock you around and you are going to have to earn every damn bit of ground you gain. Right when you think you have gained a foothold i am going to knock your ass down. Once you attempt to stand up i am going to drive that thick skull of yours back into the mat." Looking around Shaman smirks. "Guess you are going to have to pay attention to the history lessons to understand who you are facing." Shaman holds up a magazine and points at the cover depicting him and Mayhem on it. "You see Mayhem and i have fought countless times and every time i had ended up on top. Mayhem is a bad ass, but he will tell you himself that i am as dangerous as i talk. He and i have fought wars. Not these little things you see we literally went out of our ways to try and end it by extreme measures. You get your shot at e and in the end it is your mistake by accepting. I took his TV Title at that time. He and i have fought many more times after wards, but he and i are brothers in arms now. Even if we have doubts on whats going on around us there is one focus that is constant. I will do what it takes whenever it takes me." Shaman tosses the head piece down upon the ground. He turns and stares at the camera. "So where do you think you stand in this career of unknowns. You don't stand much of a chance when i am aiming for your throat. Time has come and you will get a dose of reality what its like to come into GCW. Time to get punished for making a stupid mistake. Welcome to GCW rook you are going to need every piece of luck you can get now." Smiling a sadistic smile Shaman begins to recite a demented nursery rhyme. "Hush little baby don't you cry, Shaman is gonna show you some ways to die, When this happens you'll know why, Your efforts here aren't worth a try, Bring back the pain and the rain, I will watch all of your blood drain." Shaman laughs deeply as he exits the room as the technician looks around definitely freaked out. Camera fades to black focusing on the empty head piece on the floor.
