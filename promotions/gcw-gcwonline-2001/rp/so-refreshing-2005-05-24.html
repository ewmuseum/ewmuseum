So refreshing...

24 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24 May 05 The water from the ice pack had already began melting forcing cool droplets down my neck and back ? that was fucking annoying. My head had still been spinning after that damn chair shot from, well that sorry son of a bitch who stuck his nose in my business! I threw the pack at the wall in my hotel room and watched as it broke open, Sending water all over the wall and carpet. This was not the best night of my GCW tenure that was for damn sure. This night has made me begin to rethink a lot of what I have been doing here in GCW since its opening. Sure the fans cheer me, the critics are raving about me, and I have been thee U.S. champ for what must seem like forever, but it’s not enough. As respected as many claim that I am, I just don’t feel it. NEVER would I have allowed such a thing to happen like it did at World Wide back in my 3WA or early DWA days. Eva Van Lorne? Who the fuck is she? It is a total mockery of my United States Title reign that she was even allowed to step foot in the same ring as myself. What am I becoming? Am I growing so weak that I am allowing pathetic creatures like Eva and her mentally challenged brother to get the one up on me? I fell right into their trap and allowed them to make an example of me. I understand their logic that’s for damn sure though, I mean if you wish to make a lasting impression you take out the big fish and there is no mistaking who the big fish in GCW is. Forget Octavian, Jason Dread, Mike Hardy, Trey Tremendous, Johnny Lexicon, Toxic or any of these others who are trying their best to prove something ? at this point in time they are no match for me. It is here that my dilemma lies. Can I be contempt with merely the United States championship? SHOULD I sit back and watch as my “pal” Jason Dread waltzes in the spotlight perhaps give him his glory if you will? What would the world think of me if I went to take what is rightfully mine in the first place? Would the fans hate me? Would they despise me the way they despise Octavian now? Perhaps they’d boo Dread and cheer me as they do every time I walk through those curtains. Is it possible that I’d be called a backstabber, manipulator, or my favorite ? an EVIL man? Knowing the fans of the wrestling world they’d hate me with a passion that can only be rivaled by Octavian’s passion for power. Is that the path that I wish to take though? Can I live with myself knowing that I do not need to become the monster that consumed me like it did in my war with Alex? I know that side of me still lurks deep down somewhere maybe not as deep down as I think. No matter how many times that I believe that I have burned that sick fuck out of me, I can always feel his presence. I hear that word, EVIL, being thrown out left and right like it is some kind of infection. Toxic’s recent actions, Octavian’s quest for glory, Caldera’s power trips, and Luke Hanson’s turn on Jhub were all considered evil acts ? but are they really? Does the GCW even know what pure evil really is? If they don’t then I certainly do, I do because I have this dark corner of my mind where it lays dormant. With all of the bullshit that has been stockpiling in my life lately, professionally and personally I have been wondering if it’s even worth fighting anymore. Mary is the only one I care about so as long as she isn’t caught in the crossfire I am not worried of the things that I could do. Maybe I NEED to let everything out, say fuck the fans, the hell with so called “allies” and most importantly no more worrying about some reputation as a “good guy”. One thing I have learned in my life is that to be the top dog you must be ruthless in your journey. My journey will only end when I have obtained immortality, not in the literal sense but I will not rest until the name LeStatt Knight is a part of history. I have tried to be the nice guy ? some kind of hero but all it has gotten me is the adoration of the people, the same people who would spit on me just as soon as they would shake my hand. One wrong move in those people’s eyes and they will try to eat me up and spit me out. It is well documented that fans and media love to build someone up, but perhaps even more love to tear them down. Well I am one person that will not give THEM the chance to tear me down. I do things my own way and I shall continue to do them in my own way. The next step in this journey is Warpath and a meeting with Alex Blaq. That poor fool has no idea what he is getting himself into. He dares to ask me if I know how it feels to lose EVERYTHING? He dares to test me by putting an indirect threat on Mary’s life!” Well I have a rude awakening for Blaq come Sunday night, not only will I walk away still the GCW United States Champion, but I will give him the sweet taste of his own blood. I will give him the sick pleasure of choosing the order in which his bones are shattered, and when I choke the life from his body, that last gasping breath of his will be like music to my ears. This Thursday at World Wide I get a chance to right the wrongs that happened this past week. Borealis is simply a stepping stone, people like Eva and her brother, Alex Blaq, Octavian and the rest of the GCW universe is going to be in for a hell of a ride. It’s about time to put an end to these silly references to evil forces at work. Starting this week I will prove that these pitiful fools know NOTHING about evil. Warpath is such a fitting name... This will not be the end of this path to war though, no for my friends in GCW it will be a horrific beginning.
