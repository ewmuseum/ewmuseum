Don't do anything stupid, okay?

12 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/06 Rampage was a night that will forever shine in the history of the GCW this year. Yes, many things happened. One of those was something that most have overlooked. 4 Teams came in, two teams went out. You can't discount either team of the KnightMare Brigade now. In a dual pin... on each other's team. Yes, a dual pin on each other. The other teams didn't want it. They didn't even really try. But what did you see in the KB? The same thing you always see when Brigadiers have to have a contest against each other. You saw the best and most honorable fight of the night. While the other teams, the Foundation and Team Baked were doing something else but wrestling in the ring to win a match, both Unite and FutureShock were trying to win. Winning IS everything when it's the way that success is measured. Those who can't cut it don't win, and thus they're not successful in wrestling. It was almost like the other teams didn't even show up. But now the rule is there. For the fourth time in their career the team known as Unite are number one contenders for a federations tag team championship. However, each time previous to this they failed to get a shot at the tag team titles. CCWA: The federation closed shortly after their debut match where they beat the number one contenders. CORE: They win the contendership and the federation then decides to kill their tag team division. Global: Same thing, they win the contendership, then the existing champions decide that they're just going to vacate and leave. However the Global brass decided that meant that the tag team division was done for. Now, Global Championship Wrestling, GCW... has two sets of number one contenders and a division that's beginning to get started. They have just crowned tag team champions. A controversial team of Seymour Almasy, Virgil Ambrosi or Eva Van Lorne. Two of them defend, with Seymour being the required one to defend them at all times. But now, now they are looking at two teams coming down upon them. Two teams that serve the same faction. Two teams that are not afraid to put their brotherhood aside to fight a match. Two teams that want to succeed. But which team is more hungry? Well only time can show that. One of the two teams are two time tag team champions. With one of it's members actually a four time tag team champion in total. The other team? Three times a bride's maid, never the bride... They're not hungry, they're starving. A tag team isn't a tag team until they can put a title reign in their resume. This time it's so close they can taste it. But first, the Foundation? A team they faced at Rampage? One of the teams that shouldn't have even bothered to show up? Yeah, them... Harvey and Clark LeClere From Iowa City, Iowa. Who the hell names their kid Harvey? Or Clark for that matter? Obviously someone from Iowa City. Obviously someone who has sampled more herb then Team Baked have. We see it. Due to the main event, Almasy, Eva and Virgil aren't available. So tell you what, we'll take the Foundation to school again. Of course we've already beaten Team Baked on their own. Now we take out the Foundation and then it's that simple: No Excuses. Once that's done, go ahead. Give us the champs. We're ready. Small errand first? Good to go. In the meantime, let's get back to our story of the Brothers Brand. ~Miami, Florida~ ~The Shore Club~ ~Triplex Penthouse~ Grayson Brand, a man who's caught in a conflict right now... he's in a tight spot. He's the one taking this dual contendership very hard. You see, the two teams that won, as you should know, are in the same faction. The same stable. Josh got the pin for his team, on the smaller woman known as Ukyo. Ukyo is Blaise McClancy's girlfriend, no Fianc?, and tag partner. Blaise pinned Gray, and Blaise is smaller then Gray. However there is no secret that Blaise is the more experienced of the two. There was a reason why the fight was taken to Gray there. Now, Gray was expecting it to happen that way. Due to both Blaise and Gray being a bit more then human. In fact they're almost the same species. Blaise has something else tossed in there, but nothing that changes or alters his ability to physically take a fight to someone. Gray and Blaise didn't hold back on each other. One thing with being a member of the Order who has paranormal abilities, and keeping with the sanctions of the Order to not show your other side to mortals is that it's harder. You have to pull every punch, every kick. You have to control yourself to keep from everyone finding out the truth about you. And you have to ensure you don't break some poor sap in the ring. Blaise let Gray have everything. The two leaders of this faction of the KB, and Blaise showed Gray that he's in charge. If it was an alpha challenge for leader of the pack between them, Blaise showed his dominance. Josh though, had taken the fight to Ukyo. A woman he knows could kill him if she wanted to. She'd shown her hand in a fight out side in Japan. But Josh managed to pull it out in the end and get a pin on her. What would Gray have to worry about? Well Ukyo was just recently cleared to wrestle after an injury that took her out of the picture for a few months. Did Josh hurt her? Will he and his brother have to take their shot at the same time as FutureShock, or is GCW planning on keeping things going for a bit. ?Are you sure that she's okay?? Gray had a sense of urgency in his voice. ?Yeah, she's cool. Although she's not used to being on the receiving end of a rana. She's the smaller one. She's used to being the one doing it. She'll be fine. Just a bit of a wakeup call. If she was going to be pinned by someone though, it's good that it was him or you, and not one of the other guys.? Gray stood on the porch to the penthouse's private pool deck. Standing next to him was not his brother, but the fiery red haired man he called Lieutenant, Blaise McClancy. ?So what's the plan?? Gray was a sergeant in the order. He wasn't used to making things like plans. He let his officers do most of the thinking when it came to field work. Other then that, he was a scientist. That's where he was allowed to get creative. ?Well this week, you've got the Foundation. A team that was busy playing hide the sausage with Team Baked in Tokyo. They spouted off about how we didn't deserve to be in the ring with them before Rampage. We both proved them wrong. But right now, they're probably thinking that they could have won if they'd not gotten into that little game of tiddlywinks outside the ring with the Baked Boys. So right now, you need to put them down. Other then that, who knows. I'm pissed off that we're not getting a shot right off the bat as it is. Either of us. Instead they book some stupid match to showcase four sets of boobs, tossing Virgil into what he's more loyal with. His love for Lia or his holding the tag belts with Seymour and Eva.? Gray nodded, then came back. ?Four sets of boobs?? ?Yeah, Shadow, Valentine, Van Lourne and Almasy.? ?But Almasy doesn't have...? ?He doesn't have to have them to be them. This is a fluff match. It's not like we don't know who Virgil will side with if it comes down to it. Would he really take Seymour and Van Lourne over some shiny trinket, instead of the woman who is his lover? Or even the other woman who is his stablemate?? Gray sighed. ?Well I'm not worried about it. The Foundation spoke a good fight, but then they dropped the ball worse then the Seahawks did. So it's not a big deal for us. They'll be good practice. What about you two?? ?I'm patient. So is Ukyo. But, enough on business. Time for business. What's up with Josh?? Gray would have stepped back if he could have. But his position against the stucco covered wall prevented it. ?I don't know if I'm at liberty to say. It's a personal matter.? Blaise's facial mask changed. He openly showed his disappointment in that statement. ?Personal issues and matters cause problems that will effect both of you in the ring, and effect us if something happens like in Vegas or in Japan. I need my people at one hundred percent. If I'm to help them, I'm going to need to know things.? Gray shook his head, he understood but the issue was a bit more then even Blaise could handle. ?I just don't know, Blaise.? ?Look, it's time for me to let you in on something. Your laptop has been used quite a bit lately accessing the library database...? ?What?? ?The first incident I saw was when I went to Okinawa. We know who was using it at that time. It was Dani. But recently someone else took to using it when you've left it alone.? ?Josh, actually I let him use it. He's allowed access.? ?To the research library? That's not his specialty. You're the scientist.? Gray sighed. ?What has he been looking up?? ?Different things, it looks like he's doing research on Vampires mostly right now. We're currently in a loose alliance with two of them. A few others have come into GCW. As we know already, they like to congregate. However, Shadow and Virgil are a bit different then the others. Creatures like Nezzra and the others coming in mean that soon there will be a fight to see who is the biggest and baddest of the Vampires though. We've seen it before. Is he planning on starting a war or something?? Gray looked surprised at the revelation of his brother's studies. ?I don't know. That's not what I was expecting.? ?Well he's done some other lookups, about different immortals, stopping on areas where it details how they got their gifts. I'm half wondering if he's helping Dani on her research, except their so far off each other's areas.? Gray got inquisitive. ?What has Dani been looking up?? ?She's been trying to solve the riddle given to her by her Grandfather in his visit in Japan.? ?So what have you guys gleaned from her research?? Blaise turned his head slightly as he answered. Gray picked up he wasn't going to fully tell him the truth. ?Nothing much. We're hoping that she's finding what she needs though. They're still trying to decipher the recording I made of their conversation too. Bloody problem is that we have no one who speaks Angelic on the payroll.? Gray could catch that Blaise had shifted to not giving the full truth there. But there wasn't much he could do about it. ?Is that the official statement?? Blaise's face dropped. He knew Gray has been with the Order for quite a few years. He knew how things worked. ?Yeah, that's the official statement. Of course if you want more, I'd have to direct you to Ms. Leanan.? Gray gave up the fight on that one. He knew that Annie wouldn't let too much out until she had cleared it. And that would take weeks if she wanted it to. ?Well, Josh has a lot on his mind. Including a slight dilemma that's come up to him. But I can assure you, it won't impair him in the ring. Or in a fight. He's still got too much to worry about. It's something he'll have to solve for himself, just like Dani. And they both need to do this to figure out their future together. So that makes it very personal.? Blaise nodded. ?Well, I'm going to go check up on Dani and Ukyo in the salon. Just do me a favor...? Gray interrupted him. ?Don't worry, I won't let him do something stupid.? Blaise smiled as he turned to walk away. Gray just watched as he headed out of the topmost floor of this three floor penthouse they'd all gotten to stay in. All five of them, to keep each other close. As Blaise disappeared, Gray then decided to turn and go find his brother. At the end of the large private swimming pool was a small deck overlooking the beach. A table was there, with an umbrella bent at an odd angle to protect from the most harmful rays of the Florida sun. Seated there was his brother, Joshua. On the table were a collection of books that he'd apparently taken from the local bookstore or library. Also there was a small device he could use to put his Ipod in, and have it play music through speakers for him. The hotel provided a couple of them for their use. ?Little Wolf, what are you getting into? Don't do anything stupid, okay?? Almost as if on cue, Josh puts the book down that he's reading, placing it on the table next to the Ipod's dock. He then gets up and walks away, heading for the kitchen and drink bar inside. As we look at the book, laid out in a way that is keeping it open to the page he has a marker on, we can see that it's a book on Vampires. Very fitting since they've openly supported Shadow and her stable. Especially the current GCW World Champion, Dawn MacFarlane. It's the chapter and page that it's open to that leads to the most interesting though... ?How does one become a vampire?? That is the question in bold at the top of the page. Just what is going on? Only time will truly tell.
