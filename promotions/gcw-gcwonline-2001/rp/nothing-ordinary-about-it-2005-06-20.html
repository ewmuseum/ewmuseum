Nothing ordinary about it ...

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 June 05 Sometimes I wonder what it would be like to have led an ordinary life. I will lay in bed, just like tonight and stare out into the night sky and dream, dream about what things could have been like. Lord knows that “ordinary” is certainly not the way to describe the events that have transpired in my lifetime. Life in itself is a gift, a gift that you must take and shape and form. Life is like a bright summer day, no clouds, no worries, just a cool, relaxing breeze. It’s enjoyable, or at least it is suppose to be. You aren’t suppose to question why or how, but just know that it’s there for your enjoyment. My life on the other hand has always had a grey sky and black clouds. No matter where I go, who I meet, or what my intentions are, that storm follows me. Sometimes it clears up enough to catch a glimpse of what that blue sky should look like ? but that’s when I usually find out that’s just God pointing his finger and laughing in my direction. My light was always a bit dimmer than the others. I wasn’t born in happiness, cigars, hand shakes, and congratulations. Instead I was born in pain, death, suffering and hatred. No matter what people told me over the years I, unknowingly murdered my mother. I killed her when she gave birth to me and my brother Damen. It is something that I have had to live with since I was young. They tell you, doctors, loved ones, that it wasn’t your fault, that there was nothing you could do, and indeed that may be true but does that stop it from happening? If a family man is driving down a street and a child runs out from behind a parked car and the man hits that child and kills him is it alright? Sure he had never committed a crime nor had he done wrong in his life, but he is still a murderer now just the same. God certainly does have a sense of humor I know that one for sure. After moving with my Uncle Juanito, God waited to make me suffer for killing my mom. I grew up with everything a child could ever want, toys, friends, clothes, money, love, and affection. Sandra, my Tia was as loving to me as she would have been to a son of her own. He let me find love, and even have a child. It was when my life was at it’s happiest that God chose to even the score. It was then that everything in my life fell apart around me. I watched as the ground seemed to break, the walls became closer and closer together, and the heavens rained blood ? my family’s blood. I had never committed a crime to anyone, I was polite, caring, funny, and a good father. Sure I was young but I knew that it was exactly how my life should have been, but did that stop God from making an example out of me? NO! He took Cindy and Starr from me and then poured their blood on my hands. Like the fall of the Roman Empire my life went into shambles, hated by my Uncle and disregarded by my Tia Sandra, they banished me ? “You know whining about this shit isn’t going to make it better.” The voice startled me as I sat up in bed and looked at my bedroom doorway. Standing there was who I figured it would be, and in a lot of ways hoped it wouldn’t be. I sighed and pushed my hair back from my face. “LeStatt you sure as hell pick the wrong fucking times.” He smiled at me from behind his long hair, a smile that would probably haunt my dreams ? if I wasn’t already dreaming. He folded his arms and leaned his body on the wall next to him before speaking anymore. “Look Roberto this is the only time, you know and I know that we have some things that need to be discussed. First things first though, shall I pat you on the back for the job we did at World Wide beating those sorry pieces of shit.” “It was nothing just a small games ? it’s not like I accomplished exactly what I wanted to.” “But we did Roberto ? you only gave Dread enough to keep him satisfied for now. We couldn’t have allowed him to go one on one with us for THAT long or else it would kill the suspense and anger building inside of him for Dangerous Games. Quick tags and even allowing Stevens to pin Dread was pure genius.” “Perhaps?” I now spun my body to let my feet hang off the side of the bed and touch the floor. I cocked my head to the side to look at Knight after a long yawn. “But there is something else or you wouldn’t be here now.” “True Roberto, there are a few things that I have in my head ? first being Eva VanLorne.” My head turned as if it were on a swivel to look at Knight completely. “What about her LeStatt” She is nothing in the grand scheme of things, you know I want nothing to do with her. It’s you that seem to be growing more and more infatuated with her as the time goes on.” “Oh come now Roberto you can’t tell me that your hormones don’t start kicking in when you see her, when she looks at you ? when you stare into those blue eyes of hers.” “Shes a beautiful woman yes, but that doesn’t change the fact that I have no interest in her. I love Mary and that is who I am with, Eva VanLorne is nothing to me and she better remain nothing more than a fantasy to you.” He whips his hair back ala The Undertaker, and moves a few steps forward a bit frustrated, perhaps mock frustration. “Roberto! Be a fucking man! That woman is just asking for m’US to set her straight. She wants us inside of her, showing her the things that no other man in her life ever will be able to.” “No LeStatt! Mary takes care of all I need.” “THEN let me do it! Give me control for awhile Roberto, let me do what must be done! She can be invaluable to us, with her cunning, her looks, and her money ?” I cut him off. “I have enough money LeStatt.” “True but she can get us places we would never dream of. Do you see how many of these slobbering dogs from the roster come to lay at her feet each and every week” Yet she makes fools out of them all, all except us. She wants us Roberto and I can see it in her eyes. I can read deep into her soul, she has that front because she has been hurt but that’s a wall she is willing to let fall for US! Think about the possibilities!” “NO!” I screamed this at him, the sound of my voice even caught him by surprise it seemed as his black eyes widened for a second. The look I was giving him was probably enough to burn a hole through him ? if he were some regular creature. “You will not get full control here LeStatt and I will not allow you to ruin the love I have for Mary. Eva is to remain at a distance, do you hear me !”” His eyes now burned into me as well, I could see it, he wanted to rush me ? to take control on his own, but I was ready this time. This time was different, he needed me just as much as I needed him for us to pull off the over running of GCW. After a long intense stare down, LeStatt smiled again. “Very well Roberto, but just know that she will not be able to stay away from us ? or in this case, ME. You can not stop fate Roberto, you of all people should know that. She will find us, that I have no doubt.” “We’ll burn that bridge when we come to it. NOW is there any other PRESSING needs to be discussed?” “Dangerous Games.” “What about it?” His smile widened and his eyes flashed with a crimson red. “Dread must be punished Roberto, we can’t allow that pathetic fool to go into Dangerous Games thinking it will be a simple match that he is walking into. We need something more extreme, something brutal, but yet something that can fuck with someone’s mind.” “Last Man Standing?” “No not that one.” “No holds barred?” “Fuck you.” “Ladder match?” “Next?” “Barbed Wire Rope match?” “For fucks sake Roberto I said this had to mess with his mind! Think back for a second will you, to 3WA. There was one match during your time there that defined what you did. It was a match so intense that the entire federation was talking about it afterwards. You and your opponent gave it your all ? leaving nothing to chance. It was a match that after you lost made you rethink how good you were ? it made you first begin to think that you FINALLY needed me?” “Lexicon.” “That’s right Roberto, after that match your entire mind went flying out of the fucking reality box. You questioned your desire, your heart, your intensity, your talent. THIS is what we need and want from Dread. He needs to fear you, and to rethink his entire being. Only ONE match can break a man into that kind of psychological fuck up, only one match can make a man fall to his knees and beg for you to stop ? only one match can eat at a man so much that he must bow to your power!” “I Quit Match.” My eyes had fallen to the ground now but I could feel LeStatt smiling at me ? or perhaps smiling at the fact that he could see right inside of my mind and know that I agreed with him. I hated him knowing what I was thinking, because he could always use it against me. My submission to Lexicon was the one thing that could get to me, and HE knew that. I had NEVER quit before and I never will again? “YES ROBERTO! Use that fear you have, that anger ? that complete hatred. Johnny Lexicon is no longer the threat but put his head on Dread’s body, and then proceed to rip it the fuck off!” I sat there with the anger building up inside of my body. I tried to fight it but I just couldn’t, it was boiling up and was about to spill over. Tapping out to lexicon would haunt me for the rest of my days ? my one and only TRUE failure as a man. NEVER would it happen again. “Yes Le?” He was gone, he knew he had done what he set out to do and the bastard did it well. He knew how to get to me ? “Sweet Dreams.”
