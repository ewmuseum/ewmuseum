A Prelude to the Unholy- Part III

23 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/23/08 "Can I help you, sir?" Those were the words that the stewardess spoke to me as I sat in seat number three of row five in the Coach section of the plane. I thought to myself, "Yes. With your help, I'd like to join the Mile High Club." I wouldn't, however, actually say that to her, as that would have definitely meant jailtime for me. The Japanese don't take too kindly to sexual harassment and sexual misconduct, after all. "Yes, I'll take a bottle of water, please?" That was all I responded with as the woman nodded and went on her way. As I sat there, I took in the sights around me. Disappointed over my lack of a window seat, I resorted to watching the in-flight movie. Well, I attempted to watch, anyway. It was some crappy Jane Fonda flick, and quite honestly, it wasn't grabbing my attention. Upon deciding that the movie wasn't for me, I reached up into my luggage compartment and pulled out an issue of PWW; or Pro Wrestling Weekly, for the more literal-minded out there. On the front cover was Icon, one of the many rivals and enemies I had gained since stepping into the ring for the first time a few years prior. He had been nominated for Wrestler of the Year. Wrestler of the Year, my ass. Either way, I flipped to the 'Rankings' section of the magazine, hoping to see if I'd gone up any. After all, I had just completed a nearly year-long reign as UWF TV Champion, and I was the current I3C Champion. I had taken on all challengers, and defended both championships successfully each time, but when I got to the page I was looking for, I was at the bottom, as usual. Disheartened and disappointed, I did the one thing I knew how to do-- I retreated into the safety of sleep. At least when I was asleep, things like that wouldn't and couldn't bother me. _______________________________________________________ Almost as if right on cue, I was taken back to that horrible day as soon as I drifted off into Neverland. Suddenly, it was like I was at the scene of the accident that took my parents eleven years prior. I could see the flashing lights of the ambulances and police cruisers, and I could see the paramedics trying to cut my parents' car open to get to them. I could see blood trickling from underneath the car door, pooling on the pavement below it. "Hurry up, or they aren't going to make it!" I walked right up to the wreckage, heartbroken, as they tried to save my parents. Obviously, since it was just a dream, no one could see or hear me, but that didn't keep me from yelling at them to hurry up and do their jobs. "Please! Don't let my parents die! I need them..." They eventually managed to make their way into the pile of twisted steel and broken fiberglass, only to pull the mangled corpses of my parents out. They had finished just a few seconds too late, because my parents had already breathed their final breaths. __________________________________________________________ "Mom, Dad, no!!!" I awoke with a start, back in the confines of the airplane. I looked around only to see the other passengers staring at me wide-eyed and gap-jawed. I couldn't help but be a little embarrassed, seeing as how I'd just had quite an outburst in the middle of a bunch of people whom I didn't know. I was sweating bullets, and my face was stained with tears, as apparently, I'd been crying in my sleep. "Are you okay, sir?" I looked over to my left and noticed the stewardess with my bottle of water in hand. However, this wasn't the same woman that had taken my order a few minutes prior. This stewardess was a petite, rather shapely Japanese woman with large breasts and a nice, round butt. "Holy shit, she's a bombshell. I'd love to slip her my egg roll." , were my thoughts as I took my water from her and watched her walk away. I could already tell that I was going to love being in Japan, and we hadn't even arrived in the country yet. I sipped at my water, simply contemplating what I was going to do once I arrived in Japan, and that's when I realized that I hadn't a clue as to how to speak Japanese. "Fuck. I'm going to need a translator."
