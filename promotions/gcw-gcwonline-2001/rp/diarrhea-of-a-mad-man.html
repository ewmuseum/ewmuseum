Date: Now “Chicks go whack for action packed.” chortles “the unholy one”. I don’t know if he’s trying to be John Hughes or Clive Barker. He’s a child. One of God’s one little children. He’s a star, he’s a rising star. He shines bright like opiate tar. I’m a relic of the past, I’m a broken soul who cannot last in the new hip scene. I’m a dinosaur drag queen. I’m a faggot sucking loving fucking the American dream. I’m an iceman, a sideshow waiting to be thawed. I’m a pagan God waiting to be adored. Adorn me with your applause and layman’s spit. I’m entertaining suicide with Apollo’s prick. Who’ll be the first to taste Kenny Mercury’s rainbow. I’m the magi who’ll tear down your whole stable. Coyotes and feral dogs howl at a false moon. I’m the rabid christ baboon. Enemies are a novel subject, their ego; they can’t protect without assaulting those of another? touch yourself and rape your brother. Oh, great profane gods, your words fill me with such hollow power. Coffee black and caffeine showers the spirit and all cower before me. I can use a dirty word and skewer a nipple just to be absurd, but I can never really mean anything. I don’t deserve to win, I will always die. I’m aching for a slice of Axel’s precious pie. Give it to me on the canvass, your most private obsessions aped to the masses. Touch my sweat and draw my blood. Your name will rise above. For years I was hailed as a king. A lord of pain and acid dream. You were a boy whose wasted youth was engulfed by barking with a fevered tooth. Soothsayer, say a sooth to soothe my arthritic limbs. Stimulate my passion and start again. The circle of power spins dibble-dabble. Piss stained woof-woof ribble-rabble. There’s nothing more for me. Screamed the meteorite, Kenny Mercury.
