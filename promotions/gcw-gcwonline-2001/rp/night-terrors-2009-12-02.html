Night Terrors

2 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/2/09 "No, I just...." Taylor struggles against his own mind. Nothing seems clear anymore. If it isn't a blur or patchy, nothing can jog his memory of the episode. Episode. A word that is used in clinical psychology as well as television. His show would be a montage of images of laughing faces and the creepy flashes you see in moves like "The Ring" or "The Grudge", but in the eyes of a psychologist he would be in with the likes of those at Arkham Asylum. "Please just....alone...." His hands crawl up his head like inchworms and begin to pull on his hair. Veins begin to pop across his hands and bulge in his forearms. He wretches up and down, struggling against an unseen strength. "I want to... left alone..." He opens his eyes but the tears act like walls of ice. Every shadow, every outline of furniture and appliance looks decrepit and nomadic. He scratches at his eyes, shedding more tears until his back goes entirely upright and he blinks hard but less frequent. He pans the room without moving his head and then slowly begins to relax, using the wall as his support. He slides down until he is completely relaxed. Taylor takes a few deep breaths before wiping his brow with the back of his hand. The perspiration drips from his hand. He takes another deep breath before sitting up in bed, then takes off his sweatshirt drenched in what the name implies. His skin shows every fiber and vein in his body. His skin is paper thin. ...he hasn't eaten in days... "Now now, my psychotic friend, lay back down and relax. That a boy..." The sweatshirt soars through the air and crumples in a ball as it hits the ground. Taylor closes his eyes once again and slowly begins to drift off into a deep sleep. **** "I wish everything was more simple. I want to live like those in novels." Taylor pivots on the pew and faces Father Antonio. "How so, my son?" "Well..." Zacharia Taylor has never really read many novels. For the most part, it has been The Modern Testament written by Father Emanuel up until about six months ago. Since then, Taylor has read two books, one a farce on Religion and the other a romance novel that still has two hundred pages left to be read. What Taylor is referring to is the stories he used to hear as a child... "Father, I've known pain, suffering, sin, and death my whole life. If I wasn't told to do one thing, I was ordered to do another. I am destined to live in agony until the young age He takes me..." Father Antonio blesses himself as he usually does when talking to Zacharia Taylor. He is nothing like anything Salem, NJ has had to offer in his twenty long years as the local priest. He empathizes with Taylor, but rarely sympathizes, or so he says. "Zacharia, the world was given you by the Lord to make a difference in this sinful reality. Life is full of sin and temptation, and until you find the light in all the dark, you will feel nothing but those feelings you mention. You need to find the light, my son." "I have fought in the name of our Lord since the age of sixteen. I've told you this, yet you still tell me the tribulations I face are due to my nearsided look on life? That is where you are wrong, Father." Father Antonio blesses himself once more and goes on to speak, but then stops himself. He passes two more beads from the Rosary through his fingers and then stands up. "I am nothing more than a preacher, so take it for all it is worth. If you would like to join me in silent prayer at the alter, please, do follow." Father Antonio adjusts his Priest Jacket and takes a very slow and graceful walk to the alter. Zacharia contemplates standing, obviously torn between two possibilities and then he finally stands. "I rather not." Praying was never one of his options... The priest acts as if he heard nothing and continues his stole. Zacharia pushes open the doors and exits into the rainy Salem streets. He walks towards the center of town where the life of the town should be, but is not on a Tuesday night. "Come to me, my friend." Taylor turns off of the main road and heads into a parking lot for Murray's Bar. The bar is just starting to become a haven for those caught in the stormy weather, but very few are drinking at 4:30 this afternoon. It doesn't matter what time it is for Andy though, in Scotland, a beer is just another beverage in the fridge. "Aye, there he is. Tank, get this cunt a fuckin' beer." He takes another swig and then looks over at Zacharia once more as he takes off his hood. "Bloody shit, mate. What the fuck is wrong with you? You look like you just got caught fuckin' a nun." Zacharia looks at him with a feeble smirk and nods at Tank, signaling for his special... A bottle of water. "Fuck that shit! No lager?" "Bad couple days..." "After the shit you were tellin' me, I fuckin' imagine..." Zacharia thinks Andy knows what he knows, but in all reality, and I really mean reality, Andy doesn't know half of what Zacharia thinks he knows. "Think I could get some chicken with that gravy, Tank?" Tank writes it down and walks out of site behind the wall to the kitchen. Andy looks over at Taylor with a goofy stare. "What?" "You want his gravy?" The Scot laughs heartily and then focuses back to his lager. Zach looks over his shoulder to the parking lot but shrugs it off as if he was looking at who just came in the door. One of the waitresses from the local diner smiles at him as she takes off her jacket and hangs it up, and Taylor returns something of the like. Tanks comes back and gets a couple drink orders at the end of the bar and disappears once again. "Aye, what do you suppose is on the agenda for tonight? More maddening..." "What do you mean?" Taylor jumps at it quickly, making Andy shutter before answering. "Er, training, Zachy Boy. Fuckin' maddening training for your next match." Taylor shakes his head in frustration while Andy goes back to drinking his lager. The waitress takes off her hairband and starts to swish her hair around and then begins to fix it. Andy, transfixed on her chest, nudges Taylor to look, but he has no interest and goes back to burning a hole in his hands. Tank comes back with a steaming plate of grilled chicken with some kind of brown gravy over the top. He puts it down with silverware and turns around to get something else. Zacharia perks up at the smell of the food, but his stomach turns and his face instantly drops. "I think I see a fuckin' pube! Aye, what the fuck you playin at over here, Tank?" Andy laughs but the comment is unheard by Tank and Taylor shrugs off the comment. Andy drinks to the bottom of his lager and then taps the glass down on the bar and then slides it in the direction of Tank. "The problem with you Americans," he belches getting the attention of everyone in the bar, "is that you don't know how to make a fuckin' beer! Not to mention this small fuckin' can bullshit!" Tanks breaks open a bottle and pours it into the mug and then slides it back. Taylor continues to pick at the food, moving the ingredients of the gravy around the plate and slicing up the chicken, but he doesn't eat a thing. Andy eyes him up and his playful grin turns into an inquisitive one. Something isn't right with Taylor. He knows this, but doesn't know why, yet Taylor thinks he should know why... Taylor looks over his shoulder once more and looks at the front door and freezes, tightening every muscle in his body before very slowly turning back to his food. He sits there, staring, but doesn't move an inch. Andy turns towards the door and then pans the bar before looking back at Taylor perplexed. "Didn't think you'd make it back..." Andy stares at Taylor, now moving after he spoke. Andy looks next to him on both sides before returning to Taylor. "Back from where mate? I didn't go..." "Stay out of this Andy..." The vibe that Andy gets is nothing like he's ever experienced in the three short weeks he's known Taylor. He's seen some real lows, and some highs, but never this. Andy takes another cautious swig before looking around once more. Andy and Taylor are the only ones on that side of the bar. Even Tank is elsewhere.... "For someone spending time in a swamp, you don't look to bad..." "Mate, what in the bloody hell are you..." Taylor looks up at Andy with a stone cold expression and stares him down, putting him in his place. Andy lifts himself off of the barstool and readjusts himself, wanting to move away, but can't seem to find the strength to do so. He looks at Taylor very closely. He sees that he is very rigid and his eye is twitching, but everyone's eyes do that under stress at times, right? He takes another swig and then looks over again, noticing that his right hand is crunching up into a fist and then flexing out again over and over. "You need to stop..." Andy looks at his hand holding the beer and then looks around. "What?" "You need to stop..." Andy, growing nervous and frustrated slams down his beer and begins to raise his voice. "WHAT?" "YOU NEED TO STOP!" Taylor is now out of his seat and standing tall with all the town looking on at him. His eyes look as if they are passing through Andy's. Andy, now also out of his seat stands defensively but trembles. "WHAT? WHAT? What in the bloody hell are you talking about?" Taylor's eyes readjus to Andy. "Not you!" "What the fuck are you talking about, Zach? You havin' a fuckin' mad episode or something?" These words spark off something in Taylor that hasn't happen in quite some time, well before this moment in Salem. Taylor lunges forward and grabs Andy around the throat and pins him against the bar. Everyone looks on in fear, not moving a muscle. "Mad? What the fuck did you say to me? You have no idea what you're talking about, Andy!" "What...goin....with..." "You have no idea how hard it is to walk in my shoes. The shit I have to deal with! You have no idea..." TAylor begins to get teary eyed and red in the face. "No idea...NONE!" He stares into Andy's fading eyes and then something inside him releases and takes control once more. His hand slackens with some resistance and then he takes two quick steps back seeing Andy coughing. Taylor cracks his knuckles in his right hand and then quickly runs his hand through his hair. The sudden change in Taylor sparks talking in the back of the bar. Andy slowly recovers. "Andy, Andy, I'm so sorry. I didn't mean...Andy..." Andy pushes Taylor away and clears his throat. His voice sounds hoarse. He takes a quick swig from his lager and looks up at Taylor. "Just go the fuck home, Zach. Fuck off.." He goes to plead with him but he looks at the astonishment of the crowd, putting in perspective of what just happen. Emotions building up inside him, he breaks free from his corner of the bar and runs out the front door. The cold December rain crashes down into his face and saturates his clothes. Images of a terrified and fading Scot flash between reality and a dream state. He loses focus in the rain and struggles to see while running full sprint down the main road of Salem, when suddenly... man clearing his throat Taylor quickly jumps to the side and falls over his own two feet in the quick transition. He falls and rolls in the large accumulating puddles in the road before righting himself holding onto his knee. He looks down at his knee to check for bleeding, which it is not and then two feet stop right in front of him. Zacharia very slowly looks up at the man in a suit jacket and pants. "Ah, Zacharia, still desperately trying to run from your problems?" The man tosses a gun at his feet. The barrel is covered in mud...
