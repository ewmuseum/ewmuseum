Before Now... (II)

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/25/10 Dickwood: It's done. We find Dirk Dickwood, "Agent to the Stars and More" and Phil Atken, "David Hasselhoff Lookalike Regional Finalist 1998" sitting in Dirk's fancy dancy shiny office based out of sunny London, England (for he deals with celebrities, of the British variety). Dirk is sitting in front of a large oak desk and Phil sitting on the opposite end, feet up on the table for he is an uncooth gentlemen who should take a long hard look at himself. Dirk pushes a pile of paperwork in front of Phil with one hand, forcing a pen upon him with the other. Atken: Calm down Dirkypoo, I need my lawyer to check over this first. Dickwood: I am your lawyer. Atken: You're an agent and lawyer? Dickwood: No, just an agent. Atken: But you're my lawyer? Dickwood: Yes. Atken: But you're not a lawyer? Dickwood: I'm your lawyer. Atken: Are you really my agent, I thought I fired you from that? Dickwood: I'm both. You signed back up with me after you spent that night sobbing over accidentally smashing Mr. Tibbles to death with a hammer. Atken: Okay, so to try and get this straight... Dirk holds his hand up in the air, motioning for Phil to stop. As Phil is in many ways, a trained pet, he obeys his master's command and stops in his tracks. Dickwood: Sorry but if I didn't stop you we'd be stuck in an endless loop for hours. Atken: I still don't understand. Dickwood: I am both your agent and your personal lawyer. It's not that confusing. Atken: Are you a trained lawyer. Dickwood: Let me answer that question with a question... Atken: No need, I'm convinced. Dickwood: Well in that case... Dirk continues to attempt to force both the pen and pile of papers in Phil's general direction, however Phil continues to resist in some form of paper and pen tug of war. Atken: Look, I'm just coming off a surprising tragic loss and I'm not sure if I'm ready to make a life altering decision like this without giving this some thought. Dickwood: She was 87 and you hated her. Atken: I was talking about that puppy I ran over. Dickwood: That was giant pitbull and it didn't belong to you. Atken: Really? A bulldog? Dirk nods his head as Phil lets them said words sink in. Atken: Maybe I should visit an optician. Dickwood: That's what I keep telling you. Atken: No, you keep telling me "if you keep doing that, you'll go blind". Which isn't even true. I done did some research. Dirk looks back at Phil in horror. Last time Phil attempted to do some research it almost lead to two seperate counts of public indecency and he's only bought less pants since then. Dickwood: Where? Atken: The internet. Dickwood: Oh god, who let you on the internet again? Atken: Well you see suddenly last week I became filthy rich. Dickwood: I know that, I just thought that the internet had some kind of restraining order against you. Atken: Turns out the internet is not one giant singular body. Dickwood: So who's been getting the e-mails I've been addressing "Dear Internet,"? Atken: I'm pretty sure it's some drunk in Ireland with a fetish for shiny hats and pancakes. Dickwood: Wow, I have a lot of "unique" videos but I don't think I've seen one cover that kind of problem. Both Dirk and Phil take a moment to think about the said fetish. Both men immediately shudder and Phil attempts to erase the memory by jamming a pen in his ear. He later claimed that this was the most direct entry to the mind brain. Then again, he also once saught medical advice about getting his brain drilled to erase memories. I'm not sure if he ever attended biology... or common sense classes. Atken: Why do you want me to sign this so quickly anyway? Dickwood: Let's just say the GCW contract is time sensitive. Atken: I don't follow. Dickwood: Well you know Caldera... Atken: My close personal friend Stevey Boy Calders? Dickwood: That'd be the one. Well, this comes from his office... Atken: Annnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnd? Dickwood: And it may not be valid for very long, he's stepping down within the week and we may not be looking at a replacement who'd be favourable to us. Look, I've looked it over, it's pretty much the same as your last deal. Atken: Pretty much? Dickwood: Give or take a penalty or two. If Phil had water in his mouth, he would have spat it all over the joint. It would have been incredibly messy I can tell you that much. Thankfully he did not have water so instead he just yelled... Atken: PENALTIES? Dickwood: Appearance fees owed, that kind of thing. Atken: But I spent that money on... Dickwood: I know what you spent it on but as you signed and did not fulfill the whole contract you still owe your buddy Stevey... Atken: Only I call him Stevey. Dickwood: Regardless, you still owe Caldera money. Atken: It's the principle of the thing. Dickwood: I would have thought the principle thing would be to show up for your entire contract. Atken: How much are we talking about here? Dickwood: A few thousand. Remember that comment about the water? Yeah, that again. Atken: A FEW THOUSAND? Dickwood: It'll be worth it in the long run. Atken: So, are you saying there's going to be a long run? Dickwood: Well, I'd say that's very much up to you. Phil sits back and ponders this statement for a few moments, stroking his chin as he does show. The memories of the week long benders, the missed shows, the drunken shows, that one show he set the locker room on fire by accident intentionally. They come flowing back one after the other as he contemplates whether he plans to be in it for the long run, the long run, the long run... Dickwood: PHIL! Phil's head snaps back towards Dirk's direction. Atken: What? Dickwood: You've been repeating the words "the long run" for the past ten minutes. Atken: The long run... Dickwood: STOP THAT! Atken: I'm just concerned Dirk, I don't think I'm ready. Dickwood: You were the one who asked for me to do it. Atken: I thought I had time... Dickwood: Well you don't. You and only you have to make this call. Sign it, and you return to America. You return to wrestling. You return to the one thing you actually care about. Don't and you get to sit and mope about Catpiss Manor... Atken: I always said that was a bad name... Dickwood: ...and you're stuck there probably for the rest of your life. Fading away into obscurity, perhaps becoming a one of those "What happened to..." questions on some pointless radio show hosted by a loud mouthed jackass. Atken: That sounds like every radio show. Dickwood: Yet you want to go back to America... Atken: Look, I want this, I really do. I've been living in close proximity to hell for over a year now. I'm ready to get my life back in shape. Dickwood: But? Atken: BUT LOOK AT THIS FLAB DIRK! LOOK AT IT! IT'S UNSIGHTLY! As Phil yells at Dirk, he lifts he shirt to display his belly, practically rubbing it in Dirk's face in the process. Dickwood: PUT IT AWAY. Phil pulls his shirt back down and returns to his seat. Atken: Told you it was unsightly. Dickwood: Look, we can worry about you getting in shape later but we need to get this signed now. We can't risk this negotiation being turned over to unfavourable hands and that's what's about to happen. Atken: I get the time sensative nature, no really I do. So, if you'll give me the contract, my lawyer will review it as quick as he can and everything will be hunky dorey for a return to Americatown. Dickwood: Oh for Christ's sake!
