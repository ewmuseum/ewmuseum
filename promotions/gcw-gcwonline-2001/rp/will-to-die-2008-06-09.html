Will to Die

9 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/9/2008 I always assumed that if went through my entire life and never went to jail that I wouldn't be missing anything. I was wrong. It's the middle of the night and here I sit, less than 24 hours after the events of High Stakes, alone in a cell... and my eyes are open. I didn't expect jail to be like this, to be so... liberating. On any given day I spend a lot of time alone and I had mistaken that time for solitude, but I now see how wrong I was. There was a drunk in this cell when they brought me in, but it took little more than an hour for them to rouse him and set him back out into the world. Since then its just been me and me alone. Every so often you hear something, someone in another cell or even an officer moving about, but otherwise it's silence. This isn't the sort of 'peace and quiet' silence everyone seems to strive for, no, this is an unnerving deadness, a lack of anything and of everything. I can't hear the cars out on the streets, or even the familiar hum of an HVAC quietly sterilizing ever breath I take. It's the sort of audio black out you might imagine the deaf living with on a daily basis. It forces a man inside himself, setting him loose inside a maze of his own thoughts, fears, and memories. I don't pretend to have it bad, the fact is that I've been here less than a day and I probably won't be here come tomorrow, but the silence makes no exceptions. The silence could careless if I'm in for a lifetime or in for an hour, it breaks me down all the same. Perhaps I could resist it... but I don't. Hell, how often does a man really get to sit alone with nothing for company but himself and his thoughts? I drift in and out of sleep, dreaming even when I'm half awake. I see a snake coiled around the tree of my life, this is the beast which tempts good men to do bad things. He whispers to me, he beckons me, he tells me things that change my vision of myself. This snake is no reptile, he is a man. He is my brother. ---- Emptiness again Left alone again Left to question all that's happened No one cares No one tries All that's left is the will to die ---- Family is a funny thing; the less you have the more you appreciate it. After my father died I was left with only one living relative, my brother Travis. I can't really say that I appreciated Travis any more than I had because what I felt in regards to him was more like an impending doom. My brother is a full 15 years my senior and that gap, an age difference we once used to name our now defunct tag team, meant that he would surely die years before me. I wasn't sure that I could take that... Emptiness again Not after the pain of losing my father... Left alone again Especially without ever really setting things right with dad... Left to question all that's happened So for better or worse Travis was all I had... No one cares Who else would be there for me... No one tries And once he's gone what will I have... All that's left is the will to die After dad died Travis changed. A false sense of entitlement descended upon him, ?Our father was wrestling royalty, this business was his legacy, inheriting it is our destiny? became Travis' mantra. I didn't buy it, I couldn't. That wasn't the man my father taught me to be... but every so slowly Travis wrapped his coils tighter around me. Finally, after realizing he couldn't break me by force he did the one thing I couldn't stand fast against... he abandoned me. I'm convinced now that he knew the effect it would have on me, to walk away as he did, making it a far more calculated move than it at first seemed. It took only a few weeks before I fell in line and bent my will to his own. I said and I did things in those days that I feel ashamed to admit now. Someone once told me that they were glad my father didn't live to see those days... and so am I. After a while the snake's coils loosened and he slithered away. Injured and retreating his hold over me was broken, and now I was not only alone but I was also a fractured man. I had only one option... ---- Try... Try to hold on to what's left inside Empty (and this emptiness I'm left with) Searching (has me reaching for a reason) Lonely (this loneliness consuming) Reaching (I'm searching for the answers) Will you do the same? Try... Try to find peace ---- I spiraled downward, and the bottom came much faster than I had expected. It's an odd thing to find yourself where I was, in your own personal moral basement. I hadn't arrived there because I had killed anyone, or broken any law for that matter, no... I was there because I betrayed who I was. I had betrayed the man that my father had made me and for what? To keep from being alone in this world, to keep as a companion my only blood, a brother who was nothing like me or the man who had raised us. My brother may have corrupted me, but he could not, in six months, undo what my father had spent a lifetime creating. Could he? I knew there was good in me yet... Try to hold on to what's left inside For now I was alone... Empty (and this emptiness I'm left with) I had to find my center... Searching (has me reaching for a reason) Leave my blood behind... Lonely (this loneliness consuming) And move on... Reaching (I'm searching for the answers) My beautiful silence is suddenly shattered and my journey brought to a screeching halt as the heavy iron door of the hold cell slides open. The metal-on-metal scrape of the door wrenches me out of the depths of my psyche and back to reality. ?Bail's posted kid.? The portly guard says as he opens the door the rest of the way. I glance up at him, almost sad to have to go. It's light now, no telling how much time has passed. My little ordeal has been nothing, twenty-six hours in jail and now I'm back on the street. I know how lucky I am to be one of the privileged and, despite my brief soul search, the experience has been painless. ?Shaman didn't want to press charges.? Says some talking head lawyer GCW sent to retrieve me. ?How could he after what he did to his own damn casino? Am I right?? I try to act interested. ?Yeah, tell me about it.? ?So listen...? he's doing his best to turn on his authoritative lawyer voice. ?Mr. Caldera hasn't been real happy about this whole situation.? I have to interrupt, ?How's McKail?? I really do want to know. ?Ah, Jacob... well he's fine, got out of the hospital yesterday. It took about a dozen stitches to close the gash on his head.? He pauses for a moment and then gives me some advice which I suppose he thinks will be news to me, ?I'd uh... probably stay away from him if I were you.? I nod, and pretend he didn't just state the single most obvious thing in my little world right now. We approach a rental car and climb inside, its off to the airport as I apparently have a match next week. Happy or not Caldera certainly isn't hesitating to use me. ?Yeah, they put you against this new guy Lester. He's apparently a big deal, some actor or something.? Then he says the smartest thing he'll probably ever say, ?I'll leave the strategy to you, what do I know about wrestling, I'm a lawyer. Am I right?? You are indeed correct sir. I join him in a fake chuckle and then I turn to face the window, I'll never be able to stand his inane chatter for the entire ride. The first thing I notice after turning to my side is that the noise has returned, the white murmur that we all live our daily lives to and yet somehow don't notice. My mind can't focus, at least not on the things I had been thinking about, so I think about McKail. What am I doing with him? Whether or not he and I had business in the past has now been made irrelevant. I fucked up, accident or not, and eliminated him from that battle royal. Now, more than ever, I really have something to apologize for. Only, I can't apologize to him... I can't even get near him. I can already imagine McKail flipping out at the mere sight of me, and that'd be before I try to ask his forgiveness. I don't know what to do, how to fix this. I miss the fans cheering, I miss the feeling of pride I once had in stepping into the ring. I've got to get that back but I fear that McKail can no longer be the one to give it back to me. I don't know what to do, I honestly don't. For now I can only look inside myself, point my eyes to Timmy Lester, and try to find what remains... All that's left is the Will to Die Lyrics from ?Will to Die? by Strife
