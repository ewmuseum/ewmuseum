Date: 10/19/05 “Fuck!” Chrissy-Poo slammed his fists onto a snack table backstage, he had just lost to Mike Savige? Meaning? TWO WEEKS AGO? “I cannot fricking believe that” I lost? Wow? I’m? simply astounded?” Chrissy plopped down in a chair. “Bullshit” complete bullshit? Why does this happen to me? WHY? I mean? man? I’m so much better than that? I KNOW I am? I KNOW it?” “Yeah” I say that now? it’s easy to say that I could’ve beaten him? coulda shoulda woulda? Fuck? Maybe I’m not good enough?” “What the hell am I doing here” I want to go home?” PRESENT DAY? Chrissy-Poo settles down in his Phoenix, Arizona hotel room. His bags rest in the corner as Chrissy collapses onto the bed. “Ahhh” the sweet smell of the cum of a thousand strangers? I just love hotel beds?” Chrissy flicks on the television. He takes a deep breath as he soaks in the mindless dribble of late night T.V? the road has been taking its toll on him. Chrissy had been second guessing himself? dragging his feet along the highways that connect the GCW’s tour stops? His loss to Mike Savige for the GCW Television title had hit him hard. “It’s not like I haven’t lost a match before” But why does it seem like I can’t win when I really need to? I mean? sure I beat The Demensioner? but he’s a God damn tag team wrestler?” “If I’m going to make an impact” I’m going to have to act like an impact player?” “Jesus Christ” what am I saying? Look at me? I’m a sideshow attraction? I’m nothing? the cheap pops have worn off already? if they were even existent? Angel has been fired? I’d find it hard to believe that I’m not soon behind him? The dick? I hope to God I’m not stuck sitting next to him in the unemployment line? he smells like cabbage?” “And now what am I going to do” I have no program? What the hell do I bring to the table that no else does?” “Jesus” I’m so fucked?” “Sue Johansen is a dirty old woman?” Chrissy turned the T.V. off and crossed his arms behind his head with a sigh. “This week I take on Macavity Frost” La-de-friggin?-da? Frost Ass Cavity? some new jack-hole just droolin? to kick me when I’m down? Fuck it? I don’t even care anymore?” “This match” I’m not even going to try to win? I’m just going to kick this guy’s ass? Because I’m pissed... and his name is even worse than mine?” Chrissy-Poo rolled over and tucked himself under the security of the cum-splattered blankets? Chrissy had been being jazzed on his entire life? This was no different. The End.
