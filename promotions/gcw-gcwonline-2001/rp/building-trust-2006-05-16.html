Building Trust

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/16/06 Atlanta, Georgia A few hours after Meltdown went off the air. Virginia Highland Bed & Breakfast Ukyo walked out of the bathroom, straightening her skirt out. Her lavender dyed hair was done up into a style that showed it's length and fullness. Makeup, something she normally didn't wear, adorned her face. Her clothing? A blouse, black and silken over a vinyl skirt. Her legs showed a darker hue that her normal tanned skin couldn't show as it looked like she was actually dressed up to go out. Blaise was in the room as she entered. He was dressed in a pair of black slacks and a silken shirt that matched hers. Blaise hung up his cell phone as soon as he saw his fianc? was finally ready to go out. “How are my little girls doing?” She asked him as she finished up a few small things, ensuring that the contents of her purse were ready. “Kaylee says they're asleep already. They got the letter of acceptance today, for their coming schooling. So guess I'll be dishing out some cash in the upcoming years. Annie has granted Kaylee as a full born nanny though. So we're good for the travel.” Blaise slid the phone home into it's holster that he kept on his belt. Ukyo slung the small purse over her shoulder. “You're looking to be full of thought. What's going on?” Blaise smiled at her. She could read him though, their connection had grown stronger over the last two years since they had begun to date and move up to the position they were in now, soon to be married. “An update came about from the court case against Becca. Looks like they're going to tack on some more charges.” Ukyo was already at his side, touching his face as soon as he mentioned the mother of his twin daughters. They had dated for years, and she had been intended to be his wife. She had his two children. But then she had fallen from grace in the relationship, after he had been injured while wrestling and had to take some time off. As soon as the income was gone, so was she. “At least the girls have you, though.” That was the major concern on his mind, she knew it. The girls weren't hers. She hadn't birthed them. Although the desires to do so were there, she wasn't the one who'd been with Blaise at that time. But she had promised not only him, but his mother, that she'd do her best to become a mother for them. His comment made her smile though. “Come on, let's get out of here. I don't want to be here when Josh starts celebrating his first ever title win.” “Why do you say that?” Ukyo smiled wide. “Because I don't want to be here when the cops are called due to the noise he and Dani will make.” “Don't say things like that, she's already moping around enough after being arrested in Jacksonville for 4 hours.” Ukyo nodded her head. “Which is why I don't want to be here when it happens. I think she'd kill someone.” Blaise made a noise similar to a bull snorting. “You may be right. But as it is, they deserve some time alone also. Gray is already out, he phoned me a few minutes ago. He's going to go take in a movie. I think he's feeling pretty down as it is.” “He's feeling lonely. Problem there is, the girl he's hung up on, isn't exactly very approachable.” “No, he's afraid to ask. Even after the show, in the ring, they talked. He was one to really congratulate her. But he's afraid to ask. He's still got some emotional hangups over being what he is... but enough about shop. It's time for you and I to get some time to ourselves. If the other two aren't going out, then dammit, we will. Let's go find someplace we can enjoy ourselves at.” Blaise held out his arm to the lovely Japanese lady who had fallen for him. She hooked his arm with hers and they both headed towards the door and then the small parking area for the Inn they were staying at. There they both climbed into Blaise's custom Chevy Avalanche truck, currently setup for it's SUV mode to protect from the elements. They drove off, leaving Dani and Josh alone for the night. About an Hour Later Still at the Inn Outside Two figures sat across the street from the inn, about half a block down. They were both on matching Ducati motorcycles, helmets covering their heads. However, as one spoke to the other, their identities were revealed to those who would know, Virgil Ambrosi and the woman known as Shadow. “Are you sure you don't want to just call him” It's not like it wouldn't take much to setup a meeting between you and him?” Shadow shook her head. “No, not for this. By setting up a meeting via phone, we run the risk of at least one Brand knowing. The one in question. And something tells me that the other one knows of what his Brother is after. I need to make sure that I meet with Blaise McClancy without the others.” Virgil didn't say anything. He knew she was set on this. They had delayed their usual trip up north for this. But, he knew why also. “If we're going to work with these people, it's imperative that we establish a setting that evokes communication and trust. Just like you said. You're the one who talked me into it.” Virgil smiled a bit at that. He had dropped the seed. And while he didn't find the situation perfect, he had enjoyed seeing the announcers eyes and hearing them when the two factions came out together. First they took Seymour Almasy from them, and then they bolstered their forces with the KB:NAN. Now GCW was playing catchup. They couldn't compete with this force at the level they were at. “It didn't take much to talk you into it though.” “No, you had a good argument. Although it's going to be rough at the start. A few of them have enough tension between them to start their own fires if we're not ready for them.” “Yes.” That was the only answer she got. They both knew that Vivica and Dani weren't on great terms. But that was stemmed from the words of Vivica's boyfriend. And then there was Dawn and Gray... Lia didn't want to think about that one right now. Not to mention the Brand she came to speak about, Josh, didn't get along with anyone, really. That's what confused her right now: She was doing this out of concern for him. He thoughts were broken with the familiar sound of a bird's call. Both looked up as Virgil's 'pet' crow. More like his familiar, Mobius, landed on the tree next to them. Virgil looked up to it to meet it's gaze. “From his description, Dani and Josh are the only ones here. And they're not going to take to being interrupted too lightly. Gray apparently hasn't shown up yet. And Blaise and Ukyo went out. He found his truck sitting in the parking lot for a club called The Masquerade downtown.” Lia almost chocked upon hearing the name of the club. “I guess it's a good thing we usually dress to impress. ? She flipped down the face shield for her helmet and then started her bike with a jump. Virgil followed suit and both sped off down the road together. The Masquerade Industrial and Metal Nightclub Downtown Atlanta Well, normally the club was used to cater to the Industrial-Alternative scene, with a healthy dose of metal thrown in. Just the type of club that Blaise is used to. Something that Ukyo had grown more used to after locking him down. Then again, one of Blaise's other jobs is that of a metal speed guitar player. However, this night was not a normal night for the Masquerade. It was a night that catered to the more Gothic and fetishist community in Atlanta. And that didn't both Blaise or Ukyo either. When you work with the dredges that live on both sides of the Veil, you get used to it. Leather and Vinyl seemed to be the clothing of choice. With a strong amount of fishnets tossed in for good measure. There were old school 'Lost Boys' and Siouxie and the Banshee's Goths, and there were no age Marylin Manson Goths with their face paint and all in one building. If there is one thing this genre of culture was all into, it was accepting the different factions that it housed within it. There was no 'coastal' rivalry between them. This wasn't gansta hip hop/rap or anything like that. It was just people getting together to have a good time, enjoy some music and maybe afterwards do what people do when they feel good. For the moment, that's exactly what Blaise and Ukyo were doing. As the only song that Blaise has ever found suitable by Hoobastank was playing from the DJ's machine, the song about hooking up with a woman called “Inside of You”. The two were on the floor, dancing with each other, as close as they could get without breaking local ordinances. In the front of the club though, the familiar pair that was looking for them strolled in. Both looked about with a smile on their face. As one would guess, this was just their type of place too. They looked around and Shadow smirked at the arrogant display of debauchery. “De Sade would be proud, huh?” The words rolled off her tongue with her Eastern European accent. “A few others too. Not what I'd expect from Blaise and his prudish woman.” Shadow looked over and saw the two dancing on the floor. Ukyo's arms were around his neck, and his around her waist. With Ukyo being only an inch shorter then Blaise, the two are looking into each other's eyes. Lia's eyebrow raises at the sight of the two dressed a bit more outgoing then she'd become used to. “Well, she's not looking the prude right now. She dolls up pretty nice, don't you think?” At that she points over to them, and Virgil notices what she had spied. They start to work their way over towards them as the song comes to an end. Over on the other side of the room though, as the song ends, Blaise and Ukyo break their embrace. “Want a drink?” Ukyo just nodded. “Be right back, find a table.” Ukyo found herself a table pretty quick. It was one that needed stools to sit at, set up for a small group to have a very intimate evening. Soon after that, Blaise showed up with two bottles and two glasses. The bottles had a design on them that she was somewhat familiar with: A gargoyle holding up a mug of ale. It was the brand of ale that Blaise's cousin was a spokesperson for... Arrogant Bastard Ale. Hell, his cousin was the living avatar of what they promoted. “You didn't...” He smiled wide to her, pearly white teeth in contrast to his flame red hair. “This stuff is nasty you know that...” He smiled wider. “Only for the first glass. Then it gets better after that.” He poured two glasses, one from each bottle and handed her one. “If your cousin wasn't the one pushing this crap, you'd not touch it.” “No, if it didn't reduce our inhibitions for later on, after we get back, I'd not drink it.” She smiled wide at him. “Why sir, are you trying to get me drunk to take advantage of me?” His answer was a wink and then bringing the glass up to his mouth to take a large gulp. She follows suit, her nose wrinkling as the strong ale slid down. She almost coughed it up as she put the glass back down. She wanted to laugh, but that changed as they were interrupted. She heard the stool next to them being pulled out, as a woman's voice came to her ears. “Je tenhle sedadlo volny'? The accent and language was unmistakable. The woman's voice also. Blaise looked around and then answered her. “No, it's not. And neither is that one for him.” Blaise nodded in the direction of where Virgil was now coming from the bar carrying two wine flutes. “Mr. McClancy, you speak Czech?” He grinned towards the woman he knew by her real name, Lia Vlassy. But he only knew that due to the records that his parent organization kept for the last few centuries. “I don't.” “How did you know...”” “Tone, inflexion, the act of pulling out the stool and pointing to it as you spoke. I'm half Irish, raised by an Irish born father. I know the routine. There are things that language doesn't always have to convey.” For some reason, that made her settle down a bit. At least until Blaise's next question. “So is this social or business?” He said it with a smirk on his face as he then pulled his glass of the dark colored beverage up to drink. Virgil was now close enough to put one of the glasses of wine in front of Shadow, and take the seat that Ukyo had now left for him so that he could be next to Shadow, in a better position for the talks to come. “Business, I'm afraid.” As soon as she said that, Virgil noticed that Blaise's demeanor changed. His hand went down to his belt ad he touched something. Ukyo and he both looked around, that far away look coming upon both of them. Shadow knew this to be them shifting their senses over to the other side of the veil to look for potential issues that could come up. “You see anything?” Blaise chuckled. “Nothing except for that guy over there.” Blaise pointed over at a man who was dressed in his Marylin Manson finest with some blonde with too much mascara sitting on his lap. Virgil sneered when he looked at him. “Damn, his kind always give us a bad name. So, now that you see him here, you going to go kill him?” Blaise shook his head. “No.” Virgil took him in full view when he addressed him now. “Why not” You're one of the Order's hunters, right? You kill vampires.” Blaise again shook his head. “No, the Order is not anything like the Society of Leopold, of their zealots in the Den. I hunt what shows it's true form to the world with no other means of explanation other then they are what they just appeared to be. If he were to take her right now, bite her neck, and try to feed in front of all of these people, I'd have no choice. As long as he abides by the rules though, he has every right to feed... after he leaves here. And she has every right to be his prey if she's going to be stupid enough to fall for such clich’d pickup lines.” Shadow was almost lost in his explanation, until she realized one thing. “You know of the Den?” Blaise rolled his eyes. “Unfortunately. Damn amateurs. I've had a few run ins with their parent organization.” “The Society?” “No, worse. They're leader when it comes down to it. When he went to actually kill a known Demon who knew the whereabouts of a few things I was tracking once. His name is Gaius Cassius Longinus. Sainted in the fourth century.” Shadow didn't react to it, she was busy reading Blaise's face. Virgil though, he recognized the name. “The Immortal Roman?” Blaise nodded. “Yeah, the man who killed the most famous man in the world as he hung from a bunch of wood in punishment for a made up charge. Longinus' punishment, to become as immortal as the fathers of both races that feed on humans are. One of them, the father of your race, Cain. The other the father of a smaller race, but in charge of the Order of the Hammer. But, Longinus, his goals are far different. He's closely aligned with the church, but few know of his existence.” Then Blaise dropped that part of the conversation. “But they're not why you're here...” Shadow exhaled the best her undead lungs could. “I'm here to speak to you about one of your own. In the last few weeks he's engaged in a behavior that's got me worried.” Blaise answered after taking a few seconds to figure out the formula. She said “he” and she was concerned, worried to be exact. It had to be Gray and his closeness to Dawn. “Well, when it comes to Grayson, you can be assured that unless Dawn makes the first move, nothing will ever...” Shadow's eyes narrowed, not what she wanted to speak about. “It's not about Gray and his obvious attraction to Dawn. While that would have me concerned on a personal level, as you can well guess, I don't fear nor worry about that. He seems to be very much the stereotypical scientific nerd who can't bring himself to act on his impulses.” Blaise made a movement with his finger that ended with it pointing to the middle of his forehead. “Bullseye...” Shadow nodded to him. They both knew how much tension there appeared to be there. But with neither Dawn nor Gray willing to make a move, it would be that way for a long, long time. “Well if it's not Gray, then... Josh?” Shadow almost blushed, if she really could. Most knew Josh as a brash, arrogant, impulsive asshole who didn't get along with anyone for any length of time, other then Dani. “Yes, as strange as it sounds, I'm worried about Joshua.” It looked like Shadow was trying to force the words, they sounded strange even to her. Blaise was intrigued. “Can I ask why?” She sighed again. “Some of his behavior lately has got me on edge. I know he's prone to making rash, uneducated decisions. And what he approached me about, I'm almost sorry I had to turn him down.” Ukyo's eyebrow rose up. “No, he didn't come to be for anything that would impair his relationship with Northsky. More he thinks it would help him in a very long term relationship.” Now Blaise sighed. “He asked you to embrace him as a Vampire?” Shadow didn't know why, but Blaise knew the answer. It was almost as if he'd known for a long time. “How?” “Lady Aine Leanan, our representative on this case in our Research department has been monitoring Gray's laptop as the field scientist on this. We've been following Dani's research on what her great grandfather told us in Japan. But occasionally, there has been some searches on the subject of how to make a mortal... immortal. He probably figured you'd be the closest option for a quick fix to that issue.” She shook her head this time. “So I'm just confirming what you suspected.” “Yes. But I do have to thank you for it. Takes a lot for someone to stick up for Josh. He's got that rough personality.” Virgil spoke up this time, as Shadow was a bit taken for words apparently. “Rough personality isn't really the way to put it. However, you must realize that when Lia turned his request down...” Blaise just nodded in his direction. Virgil continued on though. “If he's set on following that path, he'll find some way eventually. And whatever way he finds, if it doesn't come from an ally or friend, then he's bound to get in trouble someway. You know this.” Ukyo spoke up. “Ambrosi-san, you may be correct. But there are some things that we can not change. As far as we know, there are very few ways to become immortal outside of being born one such as Blaise is. You two were not born that way, but it came as a result of the vampiric embrace your sires gave you.” That seemed to get Shadow's attention. “While you're right, there are a few ways that have been documented over the years. Many a mortal has gone on the wild goose chase, and either been eaten by cannibals or fallen some how on their quest. And if Mr. Brand messes up, it'll run the chance of messing up not only anything you have going, but me and my crew.” Blaise broke in. “I don't think he'll be that quick...” Shadow didn't wait for him though. “He seemed pretty desperate to me. It's obvious that he and Northsky have something major. He's probably trying to get up the balls to ask her to marry him. But she's obviously more then him. I know he's an empath, you've told me as much. But for him to be looking for immortality and not for both of them, he's trying to catch up because he's behind. That would mean that Blaise is not the only immortal in your little faction. I'm going to assume Ms. Northsky is one also.” Ukyo looked at Blaise. It was their main assignment. But there had to be some openess here. “You're right.” Blaise conceded her inquiry. “Okay, that's got us talking. Now, can I know what I'm working with here. We have extended the truce to a full blown alliance. When were were backstage you said that you're just a lieutenant, and I'm the general. But you know your men better then I do. I grant you that, but I need to know my resources.” Blaise smiled wide at her. “What?” “You're time from the Russian military is showing through. You've a tactical mind afterall. You just need to break through. Many years of memories, I'm sure the human mind can only hold so much for rapid recall.” “How do you know about my time in the military?” He chuckled. “First things first, otherwise you'll claim I tried to change the subject on you, and it'll only hurt the trust we're trying to build.” That actually shocked Shadow. Usually people would go down the path, and let it be. Blaise continued. “Dani is something that's unique. Something that's never happened in this world before.” Shadow leaned in a bit, taking a sip from her wine glass as he finished. “She's not kidding when she says she's six generations from the great Sioux hero, Fallen Star.
