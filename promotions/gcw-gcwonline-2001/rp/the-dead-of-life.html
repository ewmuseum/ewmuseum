The Dead of Life

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Decades Ago Some people learn how to recover from the traumatic events that occur in their lives. It’s almost impossible to learn how a mother can recover from a stillborn child, or how a person can overcome the guilt of accidentally killing a friend. These events are absolute torment for the people and their loved one’s involved. Although my story does not involve the death of a child or a friend, it involves my own death. I am telling this story because I am dead. I have been dead for sometime now. When you are dead, days, weeks, months, and years don’t exist. You can’t tell yesterday from 3 weeks ago, it’s just one big long fucking hell of a day. Before I start the story, let me explain death. You feel nothing the second your last breathe is extinguished. There is no white light of an all loving God beckoning you to come to him. There is no serpent of that light enticing you with goods to come with him. You die, and that’s it. You come back to this earth as a spirit that wanders seeking a new refuge. When a woman becomes pregnant, and wandering soul, sometimes called Bodachs, has found a new home. I wander aimlessly looking for my chance to return. Death is easier than life. There is no pain, there is no suffering, and there are no goddamn rules to follow. You can communicate with the living, but only those with that niche can actually talk to you. That Sandra Brown, and John Edwards don’t have that niche, they only claim they do for fame and fortune. I only know of one person in the limelight with that niche to communicate, and that person is myself. You see I am dead, but I also walk the earth amongst the living. I have the same body, just a different soul. Let me tell you the story of the death of Bryan O’Neil. I refuse to start at the beginning because you all know the tortured past of Bryan O’Neil and his younger brother Jason. I will not beat a dead horse, because like the Civil War, you can tell its story without telling the story of The American Revolution, or as where I’m from The War with the Colonies. I only tend to tell you the story of my actual death, and rebirth..so to speak. I left the abusive home at the young age of 17 before I later returned there to slay my father of his abuse. I was a vagabond, a nomad. I roamed Europe looking for some sort of resolution to my life. I was almost 7 feet tall, I had this nasty pitch black hair. I was a freak of nature, a beast that belonged in the circus. I wanted more than that, so I roamed. I held a few petty jobs. I was a longshoremen, worked in a slaughterhouse, and even got a well paying job as an undertaker. I believe they gave me that job because of my sheer freakish size. I talked very little, and when I did I mumbled. The cemetery director once told me “Son, everyone can see your big ass, but we can’t hear a fucking thing you say. A man must be seen and heard, otherwise you’re just some statue that everyone stares at, and than forgets in 5 minutes.” The job as the undertaker paid me well. I lived in the cemetery. Now I’m sure you are saying “oooh this freak also lived in the cemetery, what a stupid gimmick.” I lived there Because I could not find residence anywhere else. It was quiet, and it allowed me to read and learn. I learned a lot about the human body, death, and the decay process that summer. Oh before I go on, your hair and fingernails do not grow while you die, that’s a crock of shit. All those movies that show zombies with long hair are also full of shit. Dealing with death got me grossly interested in the macabre. I was already prone to violence, but now you add my greed for blood and guts made me a dangerous figure. I started with small animals (yes this is starting to sound like a serial killer story I’m aware) than I moved onto to larger animals. That wasn’t enough for me however. Now even though they never found the body of that boy that was beating my younger brother, I never once took my lust for blood out on humans. I only did so on the dead. I became enthralled with the thrill of the “hunt” as I call it. I would exhume bodies myself, than probe through them to see how everything works. I repeat, I never once took a life at this point in my life, never once. I began to talk with the dead, I would often hold philosophical debates with more than one corpse at a time. My favorite was Descartes debate on even though we see this desk, does it really exist as a desk. I would argue my point that Descartes was overthinking this whole desk issue until all hours. When the time came I sent the bodies back into the earth, and would hope to discuss these issues again with them at a later time. How did I get away with this? Well let me explain to you that in Europe, bodies are buried only 4 feet deep instead of 6. If they are buried 6 it is because the plot above them was bought for this person’s loved one. Dirt in England is also very loose because of the salt content of the water surrounding them. Cemetaries in England at that time often looked like mole hills. It’s not uncommon at that time to see a pile of dirt ride where the body was laid to rest. Erosion and how to stop it was not perfected until the next decade. It was Halloween (yes I know this really is starting to sound gimmicky but let me finish, and you’ll understand). Now Halloween doesn’t have its luster in England that it does in the states. Only the sick and twisted take Halloween to the level of sickness. In Norway Satanists often burn down churches on this day. We get some of that in England, but like I said, only the sick and twisted rape society with those deeds. I don’t know the entire story, but apparently a Satanic cult known as The Agents of Oblivion were making an attempt to contact Aleister Crowley. The leader of this cult was named Mayhem. They all had some sort of dark moniker for a name. I know there was Mayhem, Hellhammer, Maniac, and Necrobutcher were some of the few. I came into contact with this NecroButcher freak months prior. I caught him doing the exact same thing I did on a daily basis. He was exhuming bodies, but when I caught him, he was digging his teeth into them. I think my size scared him away, but before he left he screamed “Shemhamforesh” which is Enochian for “Hail Satan.” Some of the details are cloudy to me because I don’t remember what I did to the half eaten body. I coulda swore I reburied it and that’s it. But I was smited by my boss the next day for a missing body. Keep in mind, this is a little shit town in England, so there were no police records of this incident. Anyways back to this Mayhem gentleman. During this attempted summoning of the Father of Satanism, he made his own sacrifice. He offered his own blood to the fire of Tibet. Well he knifed his wrist so deep he died from massive blood loss. It is said that his fellow Agents of Oblivion members drank their leaders blood to receive his life force. After that they left him for dead. Like me, this man was a beast of a human. He looked almost as tall and freakish as me. His body was wheeled into the morgue. His legs were dangling over the base of the gurney, he was that tall. The cemetery director pulled the bloodied sheet off and revealed a man with long black hair, and a painted face. They called this Corpse paint. He had a whitewash background to create the corpse feeling. Than he would paint almost decorate the rest of his face with a black. He had black lips, and his black tongue began to loll out of his mouth. His eyes were painted like a raccoon, but were extended at the point. Coming from the center of his forehead was an upside down cross. Extending down from the shaft of the cross 666 was painted. This man was truly into his craft. His body was covered in Satanic and Cult like tattoos. He had the serpent tattooed on his chest. His arms covered in Beelzebub and Satan himself. His nipples were pierced. Now keep in mind, body piercings were not common during this time. I examined the gash in his wrist. Right now it looked like a slab of meat. Have you ever been cut so deep that you see little bubble like things exit the wound? Those are fat deposits. I saw where his arteries were severed. The human wrist has no veins, only arteries. Arteries take blood away from the heart, while veins bring blood to it for its cleansing. If he was going to bleed himself for a religious reason he should have cut his palm. They bleed heavy, but clot thick. He chose the wrist, for some reason, maybe he thought his suicide would bring him closer to his “god.” “I Don’t think we need an autopsy here O’Neil. It’s dead bang, he died from massive blood loss. Put him in the cooler for the night, than we can bury him tomorrow. He has no next of kin, so send him to the back with the rest of the unknowns.” The director left me with that task, and I did as he asked. This sonofabitch was heavy. I wheeled the gurney and grunted as I pushed it over the small indent in the floor. I came to our “cooler” as it’s called. It looks more like an oven. We have all these small little doors in the wall, and they open inward like an oven. They still use these types to this very day. I lifted the wheels on the gurney ever so slightly so I can push it into the opening. Now I don’t know why I did this, but I did it. Before putting him into the cooler I looked him over one more time. I rested my head on his cold chest, maybe hoping for a heartbeat, or maybe for the sick pleasure of the dead flesh on my skin. I moved my head inches from his face. His eyes now closed, were a quarter inch from nose at the most. I was breathing heavy on him. My hand rubbed the side of his cold face. This is almost motherly on how I was touching him. Than for some reason I had this feeling of anger rush through me. I yanked his hair, lifted his head, and smashed it back down onto the gurney. I simply lost it. I did it over and over and over and over until I heard the crack. I busted his head open. If he would have been alive blood and brains would seep out of the crack I made. I did this with such a fury that I began to sweat. It was barely 30 degrees Farenheit in there, and I was dripping with sweat. I reached my hand over his head and felt the crack I made. I began to finger the hole, and began to push my finger deep in. I felt a pop, and than mush. I busted open the membrane and now his brains began to leak out all over my hand. I felt guilty, and excited all at the same time. I looked back into the face of this Mayhem character and whispered “See you in hell.” I hoisted him into the cooler, slammed the door, which echoed hollowly throughout, and made my exit. My mind was content and thoughtless as I walked back into my quarters. I never even bothered to wash my hands. I just lied down, closed my eyes, and drifted off into sleep. A dreamless sleep, my final sleep, my silent sleep of death. I was jolted awake by a thunderous crash. I sat up, and panned the room with my sleep filled eyes. I saw a hand reach through the broken glass, and unlatch my door. I sat there, motionless, scared, shocked, and a plethora of other adjectives I’m sure. You often see in movies when boulders are about to fall into a person and all they do is look without moving. I’m sure all of us scream for the dumbfuck to move, and he doesn’t, he only becomes a crushed mess. Well that’s because of shock. You body ceases all functions and your brain’s logic drowns itself. That’s how I was. I saw this hand unlock my door, and yet I sat there. As the door opened, a large hulking figure walked in. I could not see who it was, only his silhouette. As he got closer to me certain things started to become evident. He was shirtless, than got closer, he was fully nude. He took one more step, and that’s when I saw the painted face. Mayhem? No way, he’s fucking?. The last thing to go through my head was the curse word. It is my understanding that he drove his fist into my chest, and crushed my heart with his barehand. Why did I tell you this? Well I am thoroughly convinced that this beast took my soul with him. Why do I think this? Well I’m dead, and the dead doesn’t have visions of life. I do. I’ve had visions of this man causing harm to many, and I’ve felt and heard the cracking of other’s bones. I can now see the face of a beautiful woman. Everyone calls her Lia. I see this giant man crushing her weak body. I see her blood. I’ve come to learn that Lia is David to Mayhem’s Goliath. These two are suppose to meet in an epic battle of good vs evil, life vs death. These two forces are suppose to clash. The fate of the world lies within the heart of this underdog known as Lia. It is my understanding that this Mayhem, whom I can read his thoughts, has plans to end this Lia woman once and for all?.On more than one occaision I have screamed “Lia move!” only for her skull to be crushed. This Lia keeps coming back, like a rabid dog, more convinced that it’s her destiny to end this reign of terror. I often doubt her ambition and will, because I feel, in the end, death succumbs us all. Good Luck Lia, please free me from this hell?.
