Date: 10-XX-2005 We fade up on an apartment. Sitting in a leather chair drinking a lead crystal goblet filled with White Zinfandel is a man who's face has been superimposed with the symbol that has become his identity, the letter X. He's just sitting there looking through a photo album. The sound of the tumblers in a lock clicking as a key is inserted can be heard. Then the sound of the key turning and unlocking a door is heard. Through this sound, X keeps looking through the photo album, taking another sip of the wine in his crystal goblet. The door opens and you hear the sound of keys hitting a glass side table. X: [Censored by the network] A young lady walks into the room. She has a stunned look on her face. Young lady: How in the hell did you get in here? X tosses the young lady a key with the name Kara on it. Young lady: At least you didn't take my name off of it. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: You couldn't? Then why haven't you called in the last six months? You couldn't write? Christ, [Censored by the network] could have simply e-mailed? You up and disappear. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: Don't take that tone of voice with me. I called your cell phone. I e-mailed you. I tried to write to you. But you decided to do what it is that you do. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: Don't say that. Don't you dare say that. Don't tell me that you did it for us. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: No, you didn't. You did it for the same selfish reason that caused you to leave the last time, I know it. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: I don't believe you. X: [Censored by the network] Kara: Shut up! X: [Censored by the network] Kara walks towards X. Kara: I said shut up. X: [Censored by the network] X stands up as Kara gets toe to toe with him. Kara: [Censored by the network] Kara punches X a few times in the chest before hugging him and crying. X: [Censored by the network] Scene fades to later that evening. You can hear a cell phone ring. A woman's hand grabs it and answers. Woman: Hello, this is Andrea. Jesus, it's three in the morning, can't it wait until later. Fine, Mr. Darcy, I'll be there in 30 minutes. There's a knock at the door. Ms. Scabbia: At least give me enough time to get dressed. Call the driver and tell him that I'll be 5 minutes. Scene fades to the office of Mr. Darcy. It's 3:15 am. Mr. Darcy is drinking coffee from one of those cliched 'World's Greatest Dad' coffee mugs. He's wearing khaki pants, a black shirt and a gray power tie. Ms. Scabbia enters the office drinking a Dunkin' Donuts coffee wearing a pair of sweats and a Missouri Tigers T-Shirt. Mr. Darcy: Andrea, sit down. Ms Scabbia pulls up a chair and sits in it. Ms Scabbia: Mr. Darcy, it's quarter after three. What do you want? Mr. Darcy: Do you know where your wrestler is? Ms. Scabbia: What kind of question is that? Mr. Darcy: The kind of question that you need to answer. Because while you aren't taking the care to know where X is, he's out. You need to know everything that he's doing. You need to know everyone that he's doing. Ms. Scabbia: What are you talking about? Mr. Darcy hands a folder to Ms. Scabbia. In it are rather explicit pictures. Mr. Darcy: Her name is Kara Jennings. Six months ago, her and X were an item. Robert Larabee had already hired [Censored by the network] for this project. He was told that he wasn't to return to her. These pictures were taken tonight. Ms. Scabbia: You had X followed? Mr. Darcy: Yes. We can't afford anymore surprises. You're to go to his apartment at 10 and let him know that this was not acceptable. This is against the terms of his employment with us. Ms. Scabbia: Jack, come on. [Censored by the network] needs to have a life outside of this. We've taken his individuality away from him, next we're going make him resent the fact that he even signed on to do this. Obviously, he cares for her, or else he wouldn't risk everything to go back to her. Give him this. Mr. Darcy: I'll tell you what. You talk to him later. You tell him that we're going to reconsider this part of his contract. Let him know that we know about this tryst. Make it clear to him that he needs to beat Aiden Jackson. He needs to walk out of that match as the GCW Cruiserweight Champion. He does that, he can continue his relationship with Ms. Jennings. Ms. Scabbia: Fine. Ms. Scabbia stands and exits the office. As soon as the door closes Mr. Darcy picks up the phone and hits a number on his speed dial. Mr. Darcy: Hi, Kara. You've done a good job honey. I'll see you when I get home. Scene fades to black.
