Full Circle (Part 2)

11 December 2008

Date: 12/11/08 “You are absolutely crazy!” It was not the first time Tessa Windsor had heard those words spoken, and it most certainly probably would not be the last, especially coming from her thirteen year old niece turned daughter. But this time, Christa Windsor had absolutely meant everything she had just said. “It was my house, was it not, Christa?” “You’re still crazy.” Standing outside the main entrance of Chris Storms New York City condo, Christa could only hope that her adoptive mother would come back to her senses or at least have the realization that breaking into places certainly was not on Tessa’s list of fortes. They had already been outside the door for a good five minutes now, and it felt like it’d probably be another five excruciatingly slow minutes before her mother would finally cave in and give up. One could only imagine how embarrassing it was for Christa to watch her mother shuffle through numerous sets of keys trying to find the correct one, and growl extremely loudly when she was wrong in her selection. On top of that, her younger brothers were running the hallway, pulling the same antics you’d expect from their father. Needless to say thirteen year old Christa had both sets of fingers crossed that none of the neighbors her age were around to see all this. “There is no way in hell he changed the lock.” Tessa desperately persisted, paying no attention to any of her four children “That dumbass wants me to come back.” “And yet here you are. He must have known.” As angry as Tessa should have been with Christa’s words, she was far too focused on the task at hand. In the key went. The final session of twisting and turning the doorknob had begun, hopefully to end much more successfully than the small handful of previous attempts. “Fu-? Christa broke out in hysterics as Tessa did what she could to catch her tongue mid curse. The eldest Windsor child assumed it had something to do with her three younger siblings also being tow, but this time she was wrong in her assumptions. Instead, her mother was caught up in joy as the door finally swung open, “YES!” “Wow, did you ever luck out.” Tessa was far too absorbed in other matters to turn around and shush her niece. Instead, poor Christa had gotten delegated to watch her siblings as Tessa went in on her mission to get her stuff back. “Keep Aislinn quiet.” Tessa commanded as she turned the corner towards the master bedroom, “I’ll only be a few minutes.” Of course Christa rolled her eyes the minute Tessa turned her back. Why the hell did she have to play mother to Aislinn so that Tessa could play James Bond? It never made sense. Tessa, on the other hand, was far more worried about the kind of condition her stuff would be in. Surprisingly, despite her near five month absence from the condo, everything still seemed to be intact, including all her possessions. “Wow.?Her mouth gaped wide open upon further inspection of the bedroom. Nothing had changed at all. The family portrait taken six months after Aislinn’s birth still sat front and center on the nightstand beside the bed. Inside the walk in closet, her clothes were still neatly hung, and every single pair of heels Tessa had brought along with her to New York were still there. Same with her shirts, her undergarments, her skirts, not a thing was missing. It was almost like Tessa had entered the twilight zone. There was aboslutely no way at all she had expected things to be like this. Feeling like complete and utter shit was most certainly an understatement for the way Tessa felt right now. She’d been dropped on her head hundreds of times in her career, had elbows dropped straight into her ribs, landed funny, had the sense knocked out of her more times than she cared to count, yet none of that even combined could compare to what she felt right now. Talk about a bitch smack to the face, it certainly felt like just that. It was almost as if Chris were expecting her to drop by. “Mom?” The questioning of Evan ripped Tessa straight out of her thoughts and back on alert. Tessa peered down at her adopted ten year old son,” What’s wrong?” Evan shrugged. Was something supposed to be wrong? “I just wanted to know if me and Chris could play with my Playstation while we wait for you” We’re bored.” Talk about something Tessa did not want to be bothered with while she was in her current state of mind. “Go ahead.” She sighed, hoping it would keep them occupied while she took what she needed, “Just keep it down.” Evan nodded, leaving Tessa by herself once again. Out in the living room, Chris? 52 inch Sony Bravia had reached its full volume capacity. Without doubt it was the younger Chris, Storm’s biological son from another woman, who had pulled such an antic. Sure enough, Christa had begun to fuss at him for such an act. It was all far too much for Tessa to have to deal with. Rather than do the responsible thing and intervene, Tessa closed the bedroom door behind her and went to work. After all, it would give the kids a chance to work on their problem solving skills. Her thoughts returned back to the sight of the untouched room she stood in. It had stolen her breath quite a few times since she’d walked in. How was it that it was Chris who had been stabbed in the back and yet he was the one able to continue on as if it never happened? “Nothing’s changed at all.” Tessa reiterated to herself, forcing her mind to admit that this was indeed reality. Her eyes were wide as could be, particularly when they returned back to the family photo on the nightstand. Maybe he was simply too heartbroken to part with it? People always seem to cling on to past when they are heartbroken, and that’s exactly what Tessa chalked it up to. He was simply refusing to let go. “Wow, look what the cat dragged in.” After her heart skipped a beat, Tessa turned around to face an even bigger reality, the explanation she’d have to provide for all this. Particularly the explanation needed for the man who was standing in front of her, shaking his head quite disappointedly, Chris Storm. Even a deer in headlights could not match the anxiety Tessa had surging through her body right now. In the back of her mind, she cursed her two little boys out to no end for having the volume up so loud that she failed to hear Chris sneak in. Fighting to keep her glance from diverting to the side, Tessa put on a brave face and acted as if she belonged there right now, “I’m doing you a favor and getting my stuff out of your way.” Her words were met with silence. His hands folded up against his chest as his large frame propped itself against the doorframe, watching her every move. His actions were indeed speaking much louder than any word he could have said would. “Fine, don’t say anything.” Tessa huffed, diving into the closet to escape his intimidating glance. Things only grew worse for her there. Now he had the chance to corner her and get the answers he had seeking for months. “So, let me ask you. Did stabbing me in the back make you any better of a person?” With Chris blocking the way out of the closet, there was little way for her to escape without an answer. “Please,” she pleaded while loading her arms up with as many shoeboxes as she could carry, “don’t do this to me. You already know my answer. Why make it any harder on yourself?” He shrugged his massive shoulders, keeping an eye on his ex fianc’e the entire time, “It doesn’t hurt me any. I’ve come to live with the fact that you’re about to ruin your life. It sucks to watch, but hey, what do I care” You’re happy, aren’t you? Coming in 4th place in DG and all? Isn’t that like being fourth best? And now you’re tormenting a guy who hardly knows what planet he’s on. You’re getting real far Tess, real far. I bet you’re really proud of yourself. You’ve really done SO much better without me. Your eye is really on that prize. So let me ask, you think Lestatt will share that title shot of his with you since you guys are best buds and all now? I mean he did pick you out of everyone because you were oh so worthy and GCW’s next big thing.” Not amused with how sarcasm drenched that last sentence was, Tessa turned around only to offer a very bitter filled response, “Fuck you.” “Been there, done that.” Chris had finally pushed past his limits. Without thinking, Tessa dropped her armful of boxes and lunged forth with a right that was easily caught by her much larger counterpart. Chris grinned from cheek to cheek as he stared down her V-cut blouse that hugged her cleavage quite tightly. “I thought only virgins were supposed to wear white.” She did not bother to respond this time, knowing that he had her cornered there. Instead, she headed back into the closet and attended to the pile of shoes and boxes that now lay on the ground. Chris looked down at her pitiful self, and couldn’t help but to feel bad for his ex-fiancee. In the back of his mind, he felt like maybe he was somewhat responsible for the Tessa standing before him right now. Maybe he was really was responsible for what was transpiring in front of him right now. It was not often that Chris preferred to be a gentleman over a jackass, but when it came to Tessa, his soft spot was well known, “Look,” dropping down beside his petite princess, his sudden change is mood was evident by the gentle nature that emanated from his face, “I’m sorry. I just can’t stand to see you acting like this. It’s not right. The Tessa I know would have been flattered by someone like Clyde having a crush on her. She would have thought it was absolutely adorable and done what she could to make him smile but at the same time let him know she was not interested.” Her eyes never once lifted up to catch glance of his sincerity, “I’m not coming back.” “You don’t have to,” Of course he was disappointed, but he did his best to disguise it, “but at least have a change of heart. At least see the woman that you’re becoming.” “Independent?” She snapped back rhetorically. “Just”.” Before Chris could finish with his plea, both his and Tessa’s attention were ripped away by the wailing of Aislinn, their one year old daughter. Like a true mother, Tessa immediately acted upon her motherly intuition and sprung into action, “Excuse me. I have to go take care of MY daughter.” That right she had swung towards Chris earlier now felt as if it had connected with his chest. While he may have been far from father of the year material, did she really have to dig that low to make her point? Now not only did it feel like he had lost his soul mate, it felt as if his children were slipping out of his grasp as well. Ever so gracefully, Tessa slid through the gap between Chris and doorframe and made a mad dash for the oversized living room. Inside, Tessa’s three eldest were circled around the baby of the family, trying desperately to comfort her. “What’s wrong?” The panic in Tessa’s voice was highly evident, despite her mental attempt to stay calm cool and collected, especially around Chris. Not a single one of them answered Tessa’s question. Instead the three of them tended to their little sister who continued to writhe and squirm in Christa’s arms. If Tessa was not already feeling like a piece of shit for the way things were going down with Chris, then she would certainly start feeling like one now. “What’s wrong?” Tessa questioned even louder, this time pushing the boys aside to clear the pathway to her baby. Instinctively she scanned Aislinn from head to toe for bumps and bruises, but found absolutely nothing. Agitated by her lack of knowledge and any sort of clue as to what was going on, Tessa once again questioned her kids, only this time in much harsher tone, “Why is she crying?” Still no answers. Frustrated, Tessa yanked Aislinn from the arms of her eldest child and plopped down on the leather recliner in the corner of the room. Her arms wrapped tightly across what could easily be considered her most prized possession. As heartless as some considered Tessa to be, it was quite the contrary when she was with Aislinn. It killed her to have her daughter wail like this and not understand what was bothering her. Not only was she failing to reach the cusp of greatness, failing to be a good friend, now she was failing to be a good mother. “Shhhhh.” Tessa whispered, gently rocking the recliner back and forth. While her efforts were full hearted, they fell short of their intentions. Aislinn’s wailing only increased in its decibel level. “What’s wrong?” This time the question came from Chris, who was lagging way behind the rest of the group, still trying to recover from the verbal lashing Tessa had given him back in the bedroom. Obviously that was a question that still had yet to be answered. Rather than at least attempt to provide Chris with an answer, Tessa turned her nose up and cradled her daughter as close as she possibly could without suffocating the poor girl. Many watchful eyes waited patiently for Tessa to put a stop to this incessant wailing. It helped absolutely none. Finally, though not the smartest of moves, Chris moved in for the save and scooped Aislinn away from Tessa’s arms. Quite magically the wailing stopped within milliseconds. The envy Tessa was feeling was ever so obvious, but she never so much as exchanged a glance with Chris. Talk about karma, things certainly felt as if they were coming back to bite Tessa in the ass. After all the sleep and moments she had missed out on to take care of Aislinn, this is what she got in return. To put it in blunt terms, it fucking sucked. Sure enough the moment Tessa finally caved in and looked over in Chris? direction, she found that her Aislinn had her wrapped her arms around her fathers neck and clung on desperately as if she would never see him again. Her eyes were sealed shut and she breathed what was no doubt a sigh of relief after seeing her father for the first time in weeks. Chris, who was just as relieved to finally get to hold his daughter after weeks of her being withheld from him, sank into the loveseat and vowed to himself to treasure these few seconds. Chances were with how irrational Tessa had become, it would be the last time he would see his daughter for another few weeks. Even Tessa, who had seemingly lost every bit of remorse for her fianc?, felt her heart sink when she watched Chris show his gentle fatherly side. That tremendous soft spot of his was what had always drawn Tessa to him. It was not often that Chris got attached, but if he did, he would put his life on the line against the worst of things to protect those close to him. “It’s not worth all the shit he put you through to stay here with him.” It was that sole thought that kept her from caving in. It seemed pathetic to have to keep repeating such a damn negative thought in her head, but it was the one thing that kept her new attitude intact. She was already this far in, there was no way she could turn back now. And even if she did, there was no sort of guarantee he would take her back. Time seemed to crawl as Tessa sat there, feeling like a heinous beast for being partially responsible for what was transpiring in front of her. While she was only a year old, Aislinn seemed to have enough common sense in her young mind to know that if she let go, she might not see her father for weeks. Finally, Tessa’s mind broke away from the self loathing. She pulled herself up out of the recliner and yanked Aislinn back into her arms. Of course this set off Aislinn’s wailing once more. “Why do you have to do this to me?” While the question was no doubt probably more aimed towards Aislinn who was screaming at the top of her lungs, it was Chris who would have to answer. After all, it was who Tessa’s frightening gaze was directed towards. “Do what?” He questioned, a look of complete puzzlement washing across his face. “That!” She shouted aloud. This only puzzled him more. He shrugged, which furthered the cycle the two seemed to be trapped in. Of course, Tessa once again reacted with infuriation, “You’re toying with my mind, trying to make me feel guilty!” Chris, who had done absolutely nothing out of the norm, continued the cycle yet again with another shrug which would no doubt infuse Tessa with even more infuriation. Yeah, things were really complicated between these two, “Maybe you’re making yourself feel guilty.” “See!” She shouted out as if she had reached some kind of revelation, “You’re trying to screw with my mind!” As fucking nuts as he thought his ex fianc? was acting at the moment, he never once thought to himself that it was because she truly was psychotic. Instead, in the back of his mind Chris cursed out LeStatt Knight for allowing his girlfriend to be completely brainwashed by his bullshit. This was not Tessa. This was a monster created by that bottom feeding prick. “Look, calm down.” He tried to reason with her, “I’ll take the kids for the night and you can go out and have some down time. I think you could really benefit from some. You’re probably just overwhelmed by all that crap LeStatt has been feeding you.” Of course it did not work the way Chris had wanted it to. Instead it all was turned around and shoved back into his face, “Stop trying to blame him for your mistakes. I hardly get to speak with him now, he’s got more important things to worry about than having to lug my ass around, and yet you blame him for everything I do.” Talk about something coming way out of right field. Here he was trying to help and everything was still his fault. Now it was time to resort to some reverse psychology. “Look, just have a night to yourself. I know it’s been a long time since you’ve gotten to go out and enjoy yourself because I haven’t quite fulfilled my duties as a father and spent some time with the kids. I’m sure we could all benefit from it.” “It’s still a no.” Tessa clenched tightly on to Aislinn who threw all her weight backwards to try and escape her mother’s grasp. It was quite obvious whose choice she’d go along with. For his little girl’s sake, Chris gave it one last shot, “I’m not going to take them hostage or anything. Come on Tessa, he had to at least leave your reasonability intact.” Much firmer than last time, she gave the same answer, “No!” This time she turned her back and began to march out of the living room, hollering back to her eldest, “Come on Christa, let’s go.” To avoid another completely embarrassing moment, Christa obediently followed through her aunt turned mother’s command, albeit keeping a large gap between the two of them. “What about us?” The question coming from ten year old Evan caught Tessa off guard. At the very least it got her to stop on her way out the door. Contrary to popular belief, motherhood does not help you become anymore prepared for difficult questions such as the one presented before her at that moment. The entire time, Evan never budged his eyes away from his mother’s glance. It was almost as if she were saying he and little Chris were not valuable to her because they had not a single drop of Windsor blood. Of course that was not the case, but chances are it would be perceived that way anyways, so why try and fight the inevitable? “It’s better if you two stay here.” The elder Chris stood there with his oversized arms folded across his perfectly sculpted chest. His head shook slightly to and fro, clearly disappointed in what the woman he would have given up his world for had become. “But why?” This time it was the younger Chris who was questioning her motives. Tessa’s head turned around from the trio of glares to provide one hell of a clich? copout answer, “You’ll understand why when you get older.” Of course given that he was a carbon copy of his father both physically and mentally, younger Chris had to question the woman who he had looked up to as a mother, given that his actual biological mother had bailed out on him and the elder Chris in much the same manner, “You’re not coming back, are you?” “He’s your dad, Chris. You belong with him.” “Don’t do this to them, Tessa. You have more sense than that.” The elder Chris pleaded, staring her dead in the eye. His plead fell on deaf ears. Wisely Tessa immediately pulled her glance out of his eyes view. She walked over to each of the boys, planting a kiss on both their foreheads before heading out the door. “I love you guys.” She assured, already halfway out the door. “I love you”.” Before the two boys could finish the door closes, cutting them off from the rest of their sentence. Chris let out a heavy sigh as he fell back into same seat he had occupied earlier with Aislinn. The only difference between now and then being that now he felt like half of a man. Now, he was left with nothing left but his boys and his thoughts. Sadly, the only thing he could think about then is how absolute nothing was worse than watching the woman you love shatter into pieces. It was the absolute worst feeling in the world only made worse by the fact that there seemed to be nothing he could do to stop her descent. It really did suck.
