Broken Machine

14 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/14/2008 .::Disclaimer- May Contain Strong Language and Violence::. .::Viewer Discretion Advised::. ---- "Show no emotion, and it can destroy your soul." ---- Mechanical Rhetoric Something I have never understood, just why I feel this compulsion to fill that empty ache. Coke. Booze. Sex. Whatever hits that whim. Broken beyond repair, this Machine needs an overhaul. So fucking tired, I ache all the time. Even now, as I sit here, cold and alone, I can feel it. Emptiness. I know they say when you stare into the abyss it also looks back into you. I am that abyss. I am the darkness now. Yet, some last shred of humanity remains inside this shell. A soul, still tethered to this empty, deceased body. I suck in the darkness; hold it close like I'm drawing sustenance from the silence. Cannot rip out my own soul. Try as I might. What a sobering thought indeed. It felt familiar, this line of reasoning, like maybe I'd been here before. Maybe I had? Hard to say, really. Everything fades into this twisted ecumenical blur when I'm on the road. Dead man, lying sprawled in these sheets, lacking the energy to move. Fear running wild in my mind, feeding the doubts. Close quarters, hot bodies. Her hand touches my bare arm, and it's like ice on my skin. Things start to look the same when the color bleeds out of your world. I'd done the same thing for days. Vegas, baby. Eat. Sleep. Fuck. Drink. Sleep. Drink. Sleep. Sleep. Depression had its own bizarre order and recently I've slept more than any man rightly should. Fucking endless mindfuck shit. Where the fuck is my thanks, Caldera? Mind telling me just who the hell this Jacob McKail asshole is? Is this my message? Have I been reduced to trash? Throw me a match now and then, just to keep me on the string? It's insulting, man. Fucking insulting. All or nothing, that's the way it goes. You don't play this shit with me. Sitting here, sipping water, trying like hell to keep the shit down. Got this headache that just won't quit, wrapped around my head like a vise, squeezing. Crash and burn, let myself have a couple hours rest to regroup before I go at it again. Relentless. Nausea, and the twitching nerves are keeping me company right now. I'm listening to the room around me, hearing that void echo against my ears with absolute silence. How many times must I repeat myself before you get it? How many times do I have to stand before you, stripped of ego, with my head bowed before you get what I'm saying? How many arenas do I have to break myself in for your endless amusement before you understand? Tell me. A hundred? A thousand? When does it end? Watching the sunlight creep across the floor gives me perspective, and an image that fits with what I'm trying to say to you. You think I'm some untouchable wannabe champion, even though I haven't done shit here. You see- it's easy to see the whole picture when you're a million miles above it all. Look down at my hands, the scars on my knuckles, the ragged nails. Callused and twisted, these are the hands of a workingman. Not the hands of a well-paid asshole like you. These are hands that always seemed to fall short when reaching for fame and fortune. Oh, you call can me a legend, like that's any sort of accolade. It's not, because I am not legendary. I'm not even going to try and lie myself into that status. They won't remember me a year after I retire- and I really don't expect them to. They don't remember the guy that everyone was afraid of, that guy who overshadowed them all simply by circumstance. They remember the stars that burned bright, the ones who dazzled with intensity. They remember the flash in the pans, and the absurd. They remember the likes of Vivica Valentine, Andy Murray, and Thom Lestrange. Not me. Look down at the floor; wiggle my toes against the carpet. I feel alive, barely in these groggy moments. I can hear myself breathing, the sound deafening in the silence. Thinking more about this nameless, faceless asshole, and the hype surrounding this match. I think about her. I think about this business, and the concept of erosion. Every night I leave a piece of myself in that ring, each time in a different place. Every night, I chip away something of myself. Fragments of heart and soul, shreds of my psyche. This business- it changes you. For better, or worse, it does. Maybe you don't see it until it's too late, but it happens. Maybe you're still riding the waves, high on the wins you've achieved here, but you'll understand soon enough. You'll see when the fame and fortune and everything that goes with it perverts your reality. When you become nothing more than a hollow, plastic shell, smiling for cameras. That's when you know. You've become soulless, the walking, living dead. Fucking sellout bullshit. Don't trade your integrity for gold. Don't trade that part of yourself that you keep secret for their cheers. It's not worth it. Time isn't on my side today, and the sunlight gets closer across the floor. Mid afternoon now, and I am already dead tired. I don't look at the clock. Time doesn't matter right now. My hands don't matter. My heart doesn't matter. I don't matter. Pay no attention to these empty, broken thoughts as I get up, startling her from her reverie. Exchange a look that says it all, and she knows I'm ready. So many things I want to say right now, but I can't string together a coherent thought to save my life. My tongue will tangle. I can't get past her eyes. Always her eyes. She looks at me, and she sees so much. It hurts to stay silent, but never with her. It's easy to not say what you think. It's easy to not do what you want. Sometimes it's easy to make it hard- to make it hurt. All makes sense then. Grasping at straws, but when it's all I got, I'll take it. ---- Saturday, June 14, 2008 1604 hours, local time Phoenix, AZ "One last fighting chance, babe. That's the least I can give." Exhaustion carved his features from granite, accentuating the hollows under his eyes, and the lines around his mouth. A grim smile curved his lips as his fist slammed into the heavy bag. "Is the shitting thing rolling?" "Yea," came her muffled reply, from off camera. Jackson nodded to himself, raking a hand through his sweat-soaked hair, making the unruly strands stick up in spikes. "Good," he punctuated the single word with another impact against the bag, sending up a puff of dust. The grim smile twisted into a sardonic smirk, and his voice dropped low, as though mimicking someone. "Buck up and shut the fuck up? that's what my father told me. Yeah, he told me a lot of shit like that. It was either the deep south rhetoric, or that patriotic bullshit- Hiroshima, Vietnam and Praise Jesus. Dear old Dad was all over that shit like gangbusters. Do one more tour for your country, die on some foreign soil for a concept that changes depending on what douche is in office. Drilling this hate into my head with his fists. He'd tell me that niggers, kikes, japs, gooks and wetbacks were lower lifeforms while pounding me into hamburger. Tell me that Jesus doesn't love me because I'm a fuck up who never listens. Shit didn't change. I still don't listen." Another one-two shot slammed into the bag, and Jackson hit it again before it could stop swinging, his lips skinned back from his teeth in an animalistic snarl. He paused then, letting the emotions flicker through his dark eyes, all negative. Anger tightened his jaw, making a muscle twitch there as he slammed another series of combos into the faded crimson leather. "Haven't?" left hook, "heard anything," right cross, "new from you, Jacob." He bounced back on the balls of his bare feet, sidestepping as though the bag was his opponent. "I'm waiting for your revelation, man. You didn't say shit. Fuck that respect bullshit, if that's gonna be your tactic; you and I both know this isn't about how much we like each other. I don't want you to hand me that copout. I respect you, you're a monster, but I think I can take you. What the fuck is that? So goddamned hypocritical I could puke. Do you know how many times I've heard that? I mean? seriously? I swear to GOD, every last guy in this locker room is a drooling RETARD!" Another barrage of fists slammed against the bag, leaving smears of moisture on the leather surface. Sweat. Blood. Perhaps a mixture of both. Sweat dripped down his arms, he was soaked, and his chest and neck were red from exertion as he ducked and weaved again. He breathed hard through his mouth, the vibration of bass in his voice betraying the current of fury running beneath the surface. "I had?" rabbit punches sent the chain rattling as the bag jittered back, "high hopes for you, kid. I really did. I thought maybe, just fucking maybe Caldera booked me against you as a little surprise. You know, a little 'sorry I fucked you over at High Stakes' kinda deal. And then you put me to sleep with your pedantic bullshit. You don't get it, do you?" He snickered, casting a look at the camera, "this is your ticket to the big time. Serious shit, man. Beating The Machine? Fuck YEAH! Everyone, and I mean, EVERY ONE of those jerkoffs in the locker room are just chomping at the bit to get a piece of me. Here's your chance! And you can't even be bothered to say boo to me about this." He rolled his shoulders, tilting his head from side to side, working the tension from his neck before moving away from the bag, reaching for a bottle of water on the floor. This he uncapped, and poured over his face, letting some slip into his open mouth as he tilted his head back. "Fucking sad, you know that?" He hunkered down, his lips thinning into a white line. The scars on his back were visible in the sunlight that streamed through the casement windows, visible as he moved his shoulders, like hieroglyphs in some forgotten language of pain. Search & Destroy tattooed there, above the mangled scar tissue, as though reminding others of the strength he possessed. "Everything I am is right here in front of you, man. No fucking secrets. Nothing hidden. Take a look. What do you see? Do you see this monster you think I am? Do you see this egotist who's so focused on the win that he's already written you off? I'm curious, what do you see? Do you see what I really am? A challenge, an equal perhaps? No, you likely don't, because none of you hear a goddamn word I say. Ever. This will slide through the cracks, and you will shake your head, and wonder what you just watched. Invent your own meaning to this. Call me a bastard. Call me undeserving. Call me your victim. Call me whatever you want, it doesn't define me, and it doesn't change reality." He tilted his head back, looking up at the rafters that spanned the ancient ceiling. "I try to reason with you, as though you have any hope of getting this. You don't. I'll cut you some slack, my friend. I know you're new to this. Guys like me; we try to eat you up. We get inside your head, under your skin, and we fuck with you in ways you don't get, long after that match is over. I could tell you that you won't win, because you're not good enough, but you're smart enough to know that's just hot air. They will try to break you out there. Destroy you. I can't be broken, because I've been there already. I am broken. I am that sick, twisted place you go in the dark, when you spiral into depression and despair. I am sickness. " Humorless laughter passed his lips as his head dropped back down as though it weighed a ton. He looked down at the plastic bottle, warped in his hand as he squeezed it. Blood oozed through the wrappings on his hands, drying to black between his fingers. "They come at you, try to pull you down to their level. That's what they want, you see. They will try to destroy you, at all times, on all levels. Mentally. Physically. Emotionally. You've gotta be strong, and hard. You need to be cold, without remorse, without weakness. Show that quiver, any minute flicker, and they'll tear into you like wild dogs. The things that go bump in the night will twist your balls and send you crashing to the floor in unimaginable pain. Are you ready for that? Listen to the talk, and hear beyond those words. I'm no Chris Bagwell. No Clyde Fox. I am no jackass- no half-ass joke. No piece of fluff mid card filler. Maybe not a fucking legend, but I've been here long enough to teach you a thing or two about pain. Every sound, every breath and every motion wants a piece of you. Can you cope with that? Can you fucking feel my groove, kid?" He rocked forward; moving back to his feet so that he could extricate the sweat soaked pack of Camels from his back pocket. He slipped one bent, sodden cigarette between his lips, lighting it without the usual flair, pure business without a wasted motion. He felt slightly odd, in fact, he felt lightheaded as he drew in more of the sharp smoke, feeling the rush go straight to his head. Had he eaten today? He really couldn't remember. At this point, he wasn't really even sure what day it was anymore. "Fuck, you probably think I'm lying. Prob'ly think I'm shaking in my boots, right now, just thinking about getting in the ring with the awesome Jacob McKail. Who the fuck are you again? Are you undefeated? Are you a champion? Are you something special? Great? good for you, man. Yeah, I'm not so lucky on that, am I? I know you're gonna stereotype me, pigeonhole me into that 'overrated asshole' category. Figured I'd beat you to the punch, why the fuck not, right? I know what you're going to say, because EVERYONE says the same fucking thing, every time. Tell me I'm going down, like some cheap hooker on a well paying John. Not even remotely, but let me ask you, does it make you feel good to say that? Do you sleep better thinking that you've got my ticket already punched?" He took a long drag off the cigarette, exhaling slowly as though trying to center himself. Anger snapped in his eyes, narrowing them as he looked off into the distance. "I'm not going to pussyfoot around this, guy. I want to be honest with you. I love the pain. I love this shit. That's why I'm here. It's the only thing that reminds me I'm alive. I don't hide behind bravado, what you see is what you get. I don't pull punches. I don't tiptoe around the truth. I'm so sick of these pissing contests I'll give it to you straight every time. You know what? My gimmick is all about acting like the tough as nails asshole. I don't talk good; I just get the job done. Yeah, you caught me in that lie, you really did. I'm no thug. I'm not Tony Soprano. I have a good education. I can speak half a dozen languages fluently. I can fire a handgun with deadly accuracy. Does that mean shit in this business? No. Hell no. Whoever tells you any different is just blowing hot air up your ass. You want to humiliate me; you want to beat me until I cry? You want to steal away my opportunity to get ahead here? Go for it; hope you're ready to waste eternity on a hopeless cause. You see, my father was good for one thing, really. He beat me every day, and each time it took a little longer for me to break down. Eventually, I didn't cry at all, didn't show any reaction. I swallowed the pain, and used it for fuel; it sparked my anger. I took all he had, and left him exhausted. That was my best revenge? so you tell me. What can you do to me that hasn't already been done? What do you bring to this little dance of ours? Integrity? Tenacity? Or are you just the joke that never quite realized that he was a laughingstock? Think about it, man. Look into yourself and tell me what you see there. Don't be afraid to face reality. I do, every time I look in that fucking mirror. Every time I see the scars on my hands, on my face, on my back. Every time I feel that twinge in my knees. Every time I listen to the tired old shit from assholes echoing what my father told me. I'll never be good enough. Never be strong enough. I was never meant to succeed. Fuck that. Fuck you." His voice dropped to a pained whisper, and he dropped the cigarette to the floor, stomping it out under his bare heel. He peeled back the sweat-soaked wrap, revealing the raw knuckles. His hands were numb from going at the speed bag for more than an hour before starting in on the heavy, and stopping to cut this promo. "Don't make me repeat myself, kid. You think I'm sitting over here with a smile on my face, and a fucking spring in my step because I know I'm gonna win. No, instead I'm trying to convince myself that I can even make do out there. That's my reality. Every week it gets harder. What do you expect me to do? We all lie to ourselves. Look at me, here right now, lying to myself, trying to convince with hollow words of encouragement that I've got a shot. I'm willing to accept a reality where I walk away the loser. That's the thing, win or lose, I'll still walk away under my own power. Can you say the same? I don't think so, not for a moment. For you this is everything. EVERYTHING." The word was a low growl, and Jackson turned, slamming his fist into the bag. It connected off center, his knuckles mashing on impact, and sliding off the bag, knocking him off balance. His wrist collided with the bag as it came back, and he felt something give, a bone snapping. He shook his hand, already feeling the numbness. Flexing his fingers, his features were pinched with the pain. "Look around this place, Jacob. Survival is what you need to learn. Animal instinct. You need to disown, disavow, discard. Break the lies over your knee, and throw them to the curb. You don't need that shit. Dislocate, tear it all apart. Disconnect. Distance yourself from this shit. Transcend. Ascend. Learn the truth, and break out of the lies. Look around at the animals, and realize they're all looking back at you. Hungry. Are you ready for that? Are you ready to be broken and spilled out? Are you ready to learn who you really are between those ropes? with me?" He broke off, trying to remain focused. His nerves were jangling now, pain screaming from his wrist as his fingers buzzed and tingled. "I'll wait, and see? look into your eyes? what will I see in that ring? Fear, masquerading as confidence? Look into my eyes, friend. You won't see either. I don't have faith in my ability to win this thing, not anymore. I won't call myself champion. I know precisely what I am? who I am. I am Brad Jackson. Nothing more. Nothing less." ---- Mechanical Rhetoric Shit! Shit’shit’fucking shit. Dancing in the inferno. Dancing in the dark.
