Date: 03/11/11 At the United Nations headquarters in New York city, the delegates of the world are flustered. A dark, menacing force has threatened their way of life. Chaos looms ahead. Who is this nefarious villain? The Cold Bastard! From his secret underground lair, he holds the nations of the world at his mercy. But what does this evildoer have in store? And who, if anyone, can stop him? "Gentleman, your time is up." The voice of Cold Bastard boomed through the room, as all eyes of the United Nations delegates focused on the large screen in the room. And on screen stood the Cold Bastard. Light and dark blues zig-zagged in abstract patters over a jet-black costume. Blue crystallized mounds sat upon his shoulders, short spikes protruding every which way. Along his forearms, jagged icicles ran down in a tight row, and upon his head the visage of a demon straight out of Dante's Inferno. The room stayed silent for a moment. It was the delegate from the United States, the finely groomed Mr. Jacobs, that took charge. "You know we do not negotiate with evildoers." Cold Bastard laughed. "Negotiate?" He shook his head from side to side. "This is no negotiation. You were e-mailed my list. You get me what is on the list, or you suffer the consequences." Mr. Gireaux, the delegate from France, burst forward, list in hand. "But your demands..." Cold Bastard crossed his arms and leaned forward on the screen. "What of them?" "They're...they're..." Mr. Gireaux was flustered beyond belief. "They're obscene!" Cold Bastard leaned back, arms still crossed. His voice slowed. "How so?" Mr. Jacobs ripped the list out of Mr. Gireaux's hands. "Gimme that, Frenchie." "Hey," Mr. Gireaux gasped, a gasp that was simply ignored. Mr. Jacobs gazed down at the list. "I mean, look at this stuff." He pointed at each item exaggeratedly as he spouted it off. "A locket of hair from a virgin elf? The dildo that Thomas Jefferson used..." Mr. Jacobs eyes squinted as he read. "...on Martha Washington..." The inflection of his voice spiked. "...that one time?" Mr. Jacobs looks around at his fellow constituents, who offered no solace for the item. And all Cold Bastard did was nod as if pleased with himself. Mr. Jacobs shook it off and continued. "A mix tape with 'I Wish' by Skee-Lo recorded thirty times in a row? A signed photo of Babe Ruth with two hookers with the personalized inscription, 'nothing but bitches and booze?' " "An' " Cold Bastard replied. Mr. Jacobs looked up from the list. "Huh?" "A-n-apostrophe," Cold Bastard responded. "An' booze." "And some of this stuff doesn't even exist! A flux capacitor?" Mr. Jacobs grew indignant. "Really?" Cold Bastard shrugged. "It's on the list. I want it." "I mean..." Mr. Jacobs couldn't help but chuckle as he crumpled up the list and threw it to the ground. "Are you some asshole, or are you just plain stupid?" Cold Bastard growled. "So that's how it's gonna be, huh?" Mr. Jacobs sighed, and rubbed his face. "Good lord." "Fine." Cold Bastard raised his right hand, where a remote control device with red buttons rested. "This remote control..." Cold Bastard grabbed the camera and pointed it behind him, where a large mechanical-skeletoned ray gun pointed towards the sky. "Controls that freeze ray." Suddenly, the delegates of the UN's disposition shifts, from a group of flabbergasted leaders to worried men and women. "Wait," Mr. Jacobs shouted out. "You need to give us more time!" "Your time is up! Paris is now doomed!" Cold Bastard held the remote high in the air, as Mr. Gireaux gasped. "Prepare for eternal winter!" Cold Bastard pressed the red button down, and... ... Nothing. Cold Bastard pressed it again. And again. He brought it back down and jiggled it furiously. "C'mon, you goddamned piece of shit." A murmuring encompassed the UN room, and Cold Bastard held his finger up. "Just..." His confident voice eroded, and he tapped his extended finger against his chin a few times. "Just gimme a second, alright?" He turned his back to the camera feed, attacking the remote. In New York, however, the delegates groaned. Mr. Gireaux uneasily rubbed the back of his neck. Mr. Jacobs checked his watch, before finally deciding he's had enough. "Look," Mr. Jacobs called out. "If you wanna continue this later--" "No!" Cold Bastard barked, causing some delegates to jump. He grew fidgety. "This is happening right now, okay? I just..." He shook it furiously, now screaming. "Come on, you fuckin'..." He tilted his head up. "Lexi! A little help, please?" He got no answer. A few delegates chuckled, as Cold Bastard could take no more. He turned to the feed and began typing under it. "I'll be back in five minutes. Don't go anywhere or..." He typed even more furiously. "Freeze ray!" And the feed went dead. Alone in his underground lair, Cold Bastard took off his helmet to reveal himself as debonair teenaged philanthropist Triumph Frost. And yet he was anything but pleased. "LEXI!!!" Again, no answer. Taking it upon himself to discover the issue, he tossed his helmet down and stormed past the fancy computer setups, the Avalanche, his personal all-terrain mobile tank, and the ColdSnap, his personal attack jet, and made the trek up the long winding stairs. He pushed through the secret cutaway bookcase in the library room of his Pacific Heights mansion overlooking the San Franciscan bay, when he heard a "wah-wah-wah" noise from off in the distance. As he past room after room, the "wah-wah-wah" grew in noise. Closer and closer, past the gym and lounge room, it got louder. When he passed the game room, the "wah-wah-wah" echoed off the walls. The rumble ensued, and when he hit the kitchen, there was his personal assistant-slash-maid-slash-Alfred-slash-confidant Alexis Thorpe, the type of young buxom blonde beauty straight out of the mind of horny comic book artists, manning a floor buffer with large headphones over her ears. Oblivious to his screams of her name, when she finally spun the buffer around, he spouted off and gestured wildly. As one in her situation might do, she simply pointed to her ears and mouthed "I can't hear you." Triumph continued to scream, and when she finally turned off the machine and took off the headphones, she let out a loud "WHAT?!?" "I said," Triumph said with authority. "What the hell happened to my freeze ray remote?" Alexis put her hands on her hips. "Did you remember to put in the batteries?" "Yes," Triumph coyly spouted off, as he opened up the port to reveal no batteries. "No," he coyly said again. Alexis brushed her blonde hair back and smiled, grabbing the remote from him. She reached into her pocket and pulled out two batteries, placed them in the remote and handed it back to him. "Now, you remember that you have the charity dinner at eight tomorrow night?" Triumph laughed, remote in hand. "What do I look like, an idiot?" FWOOOSH!!!! Triumph's eyes became saucers, as he bolted through the kitchen to the outside patio. "Oh shit," he repeated over and over again, as Alexis rushed closely behind him. They made it outside fast enough to watch the trail of a blue beam float into the night sky. Triumph gazed up at the laser as it dissipated into the atmosphere. He could do nothing but sigh. "I bet I get blamed for this," he muttered. ****** Across town that same night, career criminals Chuck and Marty hung around outside a jewelry store, trying to look as inconspicuous as possible. For the Leader of Apes, Andrew Murray Jr. had taken control. Control of the Foundation of Evil. Control of the villainy. Control of his terrorizing monster. And as the MechaMurr humped away at the Transamerica Pyramid, and a fearful public watched on in confusion, and with his own watchful eye upon the top of the mechanical beast he had constructed... Murr Jr. could do nothing but cackle away, proudest of his greatest accomplishment.
