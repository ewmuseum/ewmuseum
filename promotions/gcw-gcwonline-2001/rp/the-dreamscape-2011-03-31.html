The Dreamscape

31 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/31/11 THE NIGHT FOLLOWING: "Bullshit!" The camera flickers to life, looking out of a hospital room into the hallway. Lollie paces in front of the doorway, with a cell phone to her ear. The camera pans around to get a feel for the setting. It looks left; a counter with a few Get Well balloons and a couple wrapped presents. 180 degrees from it's original position, the camera is staring out a window. Judging by the architecture outside of the window, this is a UK hospital. The camera continues to pan around until we see Toga lying in a hospital bed, hooked up to some machines, seemingly unconscious. Lollie: "That 'match' was utter bullshit!" The former Hardcore Champion's manager can be heard yelling into her phone in the hallway. The camera remains focused on Toga, who is motionless. Lollie: "It was completely stacked!" The camera slowly pans back over to the doorway. Lollie is now standing in the doorway, leaning against the door frame, staring at her client in the bed. Lollie: "Fine. We will talk about this when we get back into the country. But I want you to watch that 'match' again, because every man in that 'match' got cheated!" She quickly flips her phone shut and shoves it into her pants pocket. She continues staring at Toga, still motionless. Lollie's eyes show anger, fury, fear, and sadness all at once. She takes in a deep breath, then approaches her friend's bedside. The gal in green sits in the chair beside Toga. She takes his hand in hers. Lollie: "I don't know how I let you go through with that, last night. We knew this would be the outcome..." The air is still except for the beeping from the medical equipment that Toga is hooked up to. Lollie wraps both of her hands around Toga's and raises it up to her chin. Lollie: "Tommy... you fought more valiantly than I've ever seen. You proved to all of the naysayers that you can hold your own against anyone in any situation! Despite having almost no knowledge of the 'match' or the area prior. And despite the fact that Mayhem stacked the deck against every one of his opponents." She rests her forehead down on his hand, still held inside of her own hand. She takes in another deep breath. Lollie: "The doctor said you might not wake up anytime soon. But that can't be. We both know that you're just resting. After you fought your heart out, you deserve a little bit of rest." Her eyes are closed. Her heart is racing. Her voice is soft. Lollie: "You are such a wonderful man. No one else sees the man I see. They don't even see a man. They see a boy. But I know you're a man. The man for me. We've both gone through so much together and that's not about to end any time soon. We are made for each other." The machines continue to beep as they are meant to. Her heartbeat is in complete sync with the beeps coming from the machines. Lollie: "So, Tommy, you get your rest. I'll be here when you wake up. Then we'll go back home. We'll go home and you'll take that title back from Mayhem. Because I know you. And I know you can do it." She lowers his hand back to the bed, then leans forward the rest of the way and rests her forehead on his forearm. Lollie wraps her arms around Tommy's arm and proceeds to fall asleep. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ INSIDE OF A DREAMSCAPE: There are no cameras. No beeping machines. No doctors. No hospitals. Tommy is lying inside of his own bed. Inside of his own home. Inside of his own head. His eyes flutter open, as a yawn rushes its way out of his chest. Tommy rubs his eyes and sits up. He looks around at his own room. It looks like the room of an eight-year-old boy. There at figurines and posters decorating each of the four walls. There's a toy plane hanging from a string next to the a replica of our solar system, which still sports Pluto. There are posters for "Brave Little Toaster" and "My Little Pony" tacked up on the walls. A toaster sits beside his bed much like an alarm clock would. "Chutes and Ladders" is sprawled out on the floor. Tommy rubs his eyes again, before slowly spinning his legs around so they hang off the "Angry Beavers" bedspread. He takes in a deep breath, which triggers another yawn, before he pulls himself up off of the bed. His body, which is covered in "Rocko's Modern Life" pajamas, appears stiff and off balance. He starts walking towards the door, stepping around the board game on the floor. He places his hand on the doorknob, but hesitates before opening it. He glances over his shoulder to his desk, which is decorating with He-Man figures, among others. Beside the Buzz-Off figure is a picture frame, which is angled away from the door; toward the bed. Tommy releases the knob and walks back over his "Chutes and Ladders." He approaches the desk and picks up the picture frame. The picture itself is static, like it's a television set on an unavailable channel. Tommy puts the frame back down on the desk. He looks over his shoulder again. Across the room is his Brave Little Toaster poster. Tommy makes his way over to the poster so he can examine it closer. He places his palm on Toaster, almost as if he doesn't recognize it. His eyes search the poster for something, but it obviously isn't found. Maybe a reminder? Tommy removes his hand from the poster and glances down at the board game at his feet. He kneels down beside it. The young man looks it over, before place his index finger inside of one of the chutes. He traces it across the board, showing no emotion or familiarity towards it. With his hand on his knees, he pushes himself back to an upright position, before spotting a TeeVee in the corner, with a badly damaged VCR in the TV stand beneath it. Approaching it, he steps over a Sonic the Hedgehog plushie. Tommy leans down and pushes the POWER button on the television. It flickers to life. The same static that sat inside of his picture frame is now bouncing around his TeeVee screen. He places his hand on the monitor, and the sound of thousands of small static charges can faintly be heard. The young man looks down at the broken VeeCeeaR. The cord isn't coming out of the back of the television stand like it normally would be. It is coming out the front, beside the VCR. It's unplugged and also visibly damaged. He takes the cord between his fingers until the end is in his hand. He stares at it for a second, before reaching out and pushing the VCR POWER button with his free hand. Magically, the TeeVee screen changes to a still-photo of Lollie smiling. He drops the cord, and once again places his fingers onto the screen. The sight of Lollie would certainly make Toga smile... but Toga isn't here right now. Tommy stands back up. Tommy looks back over to the picture frame. The static is gone. It looks just like an empty picture frame. The young man steps back over the stuffed animal and the board game towards the desk. He lifts the frame once again. Behind him, the teley is still on, but the picture is no longer a still-photo. Lollie is pounding against the glass as if trapped inside the TV. Tommy doesn't notice. He sets the frame back down next to Buzz-Off, and once again walks over to the door. Lollie, from inside the TeeVee, watches him, anxiously pounding and seemingly screaming to get his attention. Tommy places his hand on the doorknob again and twists it. He slowly pulls it open and is quickly engulfed in a white light... ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ THE NEXT MORNING: Lollie is sitting up on the back of the chair, with her shoes on the cushion, staring out the window. Toga is still lying completely motionless in the bed, just a few feet from her. There are distinguished sleep-deprived bags forming under Lollie's eyes. Her stare is almost as far away as her mind is. Whatever it is that her eyes have rested on from the outside world, she doesn't see it. She's deep within her own thoughts. Footsteps are heard behind the camera. It pans around to show a nurse has entered the room to check on her patient. She fiddles with the beeping machines a bit before looking Toga over with her own eyes. Lollie reluctantly pulls herself from the depths of her own mind. She watches the nurse finish up her routine, before walking out. The gal in green hops down from the back of the chair and walks over to Toga. Lollie: "I hope your dreams are better than the ones I haven't been able to have..." She brushes his hair back, leans down, and kisses his forehead, before walking back to the chair and returning to her perched position beside the window. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ RETURN TO THE DREAMSCAPE: The white light begins to dim a bit. Slowly, the setting begins to become clear, as if emerging from fog. It's a GCW WorldWide ring, but the arena is empty. There are no cameras. No roaring audience. No referees. No show. Tommy is standing inside of the ring. Inside of his Rocko pajamas. Inside of his own head. He rubs his eyes, as the white light switches with the arena lights. The young man looks around at all the empty seats that surround the ring that surrounds him. He sees the turnbuckles. He sees the ropes. He sees the commentating table. He sees the announcing chairs. He sees the ring bell. He sees the Globaltron. It is static. Tommy approaches the ropes closest to the entrance way. He places his hands on the ropes. Grips them tight. He continues to scan over the empty arena around him. The young man slides his hands over the ropes as he approaches a near turnbuckle. On the padding is the GCW logo. Tommy places his hand on the logo. A faint smirk pokes out for a second, before fading. He releases the ropes and removes his hand from the turnbuckle pad. Stepping through the ropes, Tommy slips on the apron. He grabs the middle rope as his feet dangle over the abyss below. There are no mats. No floor. Just darkness. Tommy fights to hold his grip as a loud clank of a metal folding chair forcibly folding is heard, followed by another, and another, and another. The sounds repeats itself over and over. The silence between the clanks grows shorter. The floor seating is folding itself up, from the outside in, in a spiral motion. Once a chair is folded, it vanishes into thin air. The arena seating is already gone, as are the walls. Darkness has replaced them. The sound of the chairs folding is deafening, as Tommy continues to fight to get a better grip on the ropes. The chairs finish folding and vanishing, and are quickly replaced by the sound of the barricades falling back onto the audience-floor and then disappearing as well. The commentator's table falls apart, folds itself, up and flies off into the darkness as if it was a bird. The announcing chairs poof into thin air. The ring aprons roll themselves up, starting opposite Tommy, then fall into the abyss below him. Tommy slowly starts to pull himself up. He places his foot on the apron and pushes himself up high enough so he can hook his elbow around the middle rope. Suddenly, the canvas and ring-floor vanish below his foot, leaving him dangling once again! Tommy glances down at the darkness beneath him, attempting to grip the ropes even tighter, but not having the energy to do it. The turnbuckles shift down a couple inches, accompanied by what sounds like a shotgun cocking, which startles Tommy a bit. He holds the wrist of the arm that is hooked on the rope with his other hand, effectively preventing him from slipping. Suddenly, the turnbuckles are blasted into the black sky, like they were just shot by a cannon! The ropes wave in the air, but stay connected to the turnbuckles. Tommy stays connected to the ropes. He disappears into the darkness above, as the Globaltron lights up with a shot of Lollie pounding at the screen once again... ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ THAT AFTERNOON: There is a McDonald's Happy Meal box sitting on the table, with Lollie and Katsy Kitsuna sitting around it. There's a Young Justice Aqualad figure sitting off to the side. They are nibbling at their hamburgers, but their small bites indicate that they aren't eating because of their hunger nagging at them, but rather because they feel they should be hungry. Lollie: "You think he'll like his toy?" Katsy glances over at the Aqualad figure, that Lollie is now holding in her hand. Lollie's focus is on it, but her mind is far, far away. Katsy glances down at her Littlest Pet Shop puppy she got with hers. Katsy: "He might like mine more." A very faint smile comes over Lollie's face as she looks over to the puppy. Lollie: "Probably.... We should name it Daisy." Katsy hands the toy puppy over to Lollie who sets it next to the Aqualad one. She raises her hamburger to her lips, but doesn't bite down. She doesn't even blink. Her mind is still deep in thought. Katsy sighs. Katsy: "Are you okay, Lollie?" The gal in green slowly takes a nibble. Katsy watches her, with a concerned look in her eyes, as Lollie finishes chewing her tiny bite and swallows it. Lollie wets her lips. Lollie: "Yeah..." She opens her mouth to take another nibble, but Katsy lightly grasps her wrist and lowers her hands. She stares Lollie deep into her eyes. Katsy: "Honestly?" The two women stare at each other for a few seconds, without blinking, before Lollie quickly closes her eyes tight. Lollie: "... no...." She drops her hamburger onto its wrapper before covering her face with her hands. Katsy glances back to Toga in the bed, motionless, then sighs, not knowing what to do or say. Lollie: "... I... I just... want Tommy back..." Katsy places her small hand on Lollie's shoulder. The machines continue beeping. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ RETURN TO BELMONT: The sun is rising in the distance as the turnbuckles rocket up from the channel of water below the bridge. They fly over the hill where everyone had parked their cars, then disintegrates! Tommy falls flat on his face in the dirt. He slowly pushes himself up to his hands and knees, breathing heavily. His pajamas are now covered in dirt and perspiration. The young man gets to a kneel, before he starts observing his new environment. His blue eyes seem to study every detail of the area, as if it's completely foreign to him. He sees the few damaged buildings across the old bridge. Then turns around to where the cars had parked before the gruesome, life-altering fight had occurred. They are all gone. Except for one. It's not Toga's. There is a man leaning up against the car, staring off into the sunrise, with his back to Tommy. The young man slowly pushes himself to an upright position. There's a big red mark on the inside of his elbow where the ring rope was just moments ago. He dusts his PJs off a bit before he approaches the man. Every step seems to be harder and heavier than the last, but after a few seconds that feel like eternity, Tommy is just a couple feet from the man. "You were wrong." Tommy isn't sure what the man is talking about. He opens his mouth to reply but nothing comes out. Tommy grasps his throat in surprise. "You thought you could handle the brutality." The man's back is still towards Tommy, who once again tries to respond. Nothing. "You are all so stupid to put your lives at risk for a piece of metal." Tommy gives up on trying to speak and just stares down at the dirt beneath his feet. "Out of everyone that has stepped on this ground, I was the only smart one." The young dreamer looks up, unsure of what this man is talking about. The man slowly turns around to reveal himself as Dorn Tracer. "You should've followed me, you foolish child." Tommy cocks his head to the side. The man before him looks like Dorn. Sounds like Dorn. But he isn't Dorn. In fact, aside from the sight and sound, he has nothing in common with him. Having never actually observed Dorn in real life, this is just Toga's idea of what the man might be like. Or just Toga's conscience taking form. "Now look at you. You're so damned broken, you can barely stand!" Suddenly, TOGA falls to his knees. He is in the same attire he was for his Belmont fight. He is covered in dirt, sweat, and blood. The marks from the rope that Mayhem used to choke Toga are stretched all the way across Toga's thin neck. He grasps at his throat once again, realizing why it is that he can't speak. The wound on his head, from the gravity furnace, begins seeping blood once again. Toga's eyes begin to flutter shut, as he starts to lose consciousness. Dorn walks around the car and kneels beside him. "I'm sorry, kid. But this is the end." Each time Toga closes his eyes, the world around him gets veiled in darkness, as if the "viewer's" eyes were tied directly to Toga's. Dorn places his hand on Toga's chest, with a disappointed look in his eyes. Toga's eyes flutter shut. They reopen to see Bryan Mayhem kneeling exactly where Dorn was. His hand exactly where Dorn's was. A sinister smirk stretches over Mayhem's evil clown face. The Hardcore Champion slides his hand up from Toga's chest to his throat, and slowly clenches his fingers around it. Toga begins gasping for air, as Mayhem's grip gets tighter. Flutter. Flutter... Flutter..... Darkness. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ THAT NIGHT: Miss Kitsuna is gone. Lollie is once again sitting at Toga's bedside. Her head resting on Toga's shoulder, with her fingers intertwined with his. Exhaustion appears to have caught up with her. They both remain motionless for a few seconds, before Toga gasps for air, opening his eyes! His eyes dart around the room without moving his head, before they his eyelids shut as suddenly as they opened. They remain closed. Lollie groggily opens her own eyes. She slowly pulls her head off of his shoulder, making it look like her head weighs a ton and takes everything she has just to lift it. She sits upright for an entire second, before slouching back in the chair. She turns her head to look at Toga, who doesn't appear to be awake anymore. She sighs. Lollie: "I wish there was something I could do for you..." The gal in green pulls herself back to an upright position, once again appearing to need all of her strength to do so. She musters up a bit more and stands up. She places the palm of her hand on Thomas' cheek, then leans down and kisses him on the lips, lightly. Lollie brushes his hair back with her hand a few times, before sitting back down. She pulls her legs up into the chair and curls up into the smallest ball she can. Her eyes flutter shut again. ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ RETURN TO THE MOMENT: Flutter. Flutter... Flutter..... Light. Thomas' eyes flutter open. He is walking along the street, in his new fancy suit, beside Michael Reese once again. Katsy and Lollie are several yards ahead of them. Thomas looks around, trying to take in his surroundings that seem to puzzle him a bit. Even the suit covering his body seems completely foreign in his eyes. Mickey suddenly stops. He quickly reaches over and grabs a handful of Thomas' shirt. He throws him up against the nearest wall, and stares him straight in the eye. The girls hear the commotion and turn around. Reese: "Shut... the... hell.... up." Just as it really happened, Katsy starts screaming at Michael to put him down, as the girls run back. Their emotions are definitely troubled. And they do appear to be screaming, but the screams are completely muffled. Almost no sounds come from them whatsoever. Mickey starts saying something also, but just the same as the girls, his voice is muffled. Thomas' face shows fear and confusion. To him, this moment has never happened. He has no idea what's going on. Miss Kitsuna and Lollie start trying to pull at Reese's arm, trying to get him to release Thomas. The larger man refuses. Thomas begins to cough as the marks from the Mayhem's chain slowly appear around his throat, even though this moment took place before the Belmont fight. His suit begins to tear randomly. Dirt-stains appear as well. Suddenly, the sharply dressed young man looks like a bum. Michael throws Thomas to the ground, just as he did before. Lollie rushes over to Thomas. Mickey starts barking orders at the young, formerly, well-dressed man, but only the volume and intensity increase. The words are still as muffled and incomprehensible as they were a few moments ago. Suddenly, the sound is clear. Reese: "One week. That's all the time you have left.... So, tell me, Thomas... what are you going to do with your final days?" Tommy snaps his focus off of Reese, and slowly glances over to Lollie. She stares deep into his blue eyes, extremely concerned. He opens his mouth to speak, but due to his throat injuries from Belmont, he can't. An echo of his voice is heard from out of nowhere. Thomas' Echo: "I.... I......" The GCW Hardcore Championship belt suddenly appears beside him. He glances to it, then back to Reese, who looks like he could snap at any moment. He returns his attention back to Lollie. Thomas closes his eyes.... Thomas' Echo: "I.... I....... Lollie, I..." With his eyes still closed. His heart racing a million beats per second. His new suit ruined. His GCW Hardcore Championship likely lost in a week. And Reese's words echoing through his head......... Thomas reaches down, grabs the GCW Hardcore Championship, stumbles to his feet, then runs. He runs. And runs. And runs. Mickey, Katsy, and Lollie all become distant figures before they fade into darkness. Tears stream down his bruised face, dropping off his cheek and skipping his scarred throat. Thomas squeezes the GCW Hardcore Championship close to his chest with a death grip. The city he was in has been completely covered by darkness. There is nothing left in Thomas' world except him and his GCW Hardcore Championship. Without warning, a very quick, very bright light flashes past Thomas, blinding him. He finally stops running, as his hands fall to his side. The GCW Hardcore Championship still in his hand. He seems stunned, as Lollie is standing in front of him in her green dress. Lollie: "Tommy..." The moment is covered in sadness. The moment itself never happened. Lollie: "This isn't how it happened..." Thomas stares at her, as the tears on his face continue. Her eyes are wide, with her own tears forming behind them. Lollie: "That title isn't yours anymore..." He glances down at the GCW Hardcore Championship in his hand as it fades. Thomas tries to pull it back to his chest to hug it as tightly as he can, but as soon as his hands reach his chest, they are empty. Lollie: "You fought valiantly. But we both know that you don't belong there. That title was never meant to be yours. You had to return it." Thomas stares at his empty palms. Lollie: "I am sorry, but you can't stay here anymore either. You need to come with me." She extends her hand out to him. He watches her, unsure of what to do. He looks around at the darkness around him, then back to her. He reluctantly reaches for her hand. Lollie: "I am here for you. I always will be. If you just take my hand, we can be together again. If you just take my hand..." Thomas is breathing heavily, as he slowly and softly places his hand in her's. Lollie: "... it's time to wake up, love." Lollie begins to glow. Suddenly, a light emerges from her and once again envelopes the darkness, blinding Thomas. Everything remains completely white for several moments before slowly fading away. The faint sound of a machine beeping can be heard.... ~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~-=-~ The white light disappears to reveal Toga lying in the hospital bed. Lollie is once again slouching in her chair beside him. Her hand in his. Lollie's eyes slowly open. A second later, Toga's eyes do the same. She glances over to him, groggily, as his eyes slowly take in the scene. A moment passes before she realizes what she's seeing: He's awake! She leaps to her feet and throws her arms around him. Toga slowly places one hand on her back. Lollie: "Tommy! Omigawd, you're awake!" The former GCW Hardcore Champion lightly taps her on the back. She sits on the edge of the bed, releasing her hug. With a big smile covering her tired, unflattering face, she stares deep into his baby blues. Lollie: "How are you feeling, hun?" Toga slowly raises his hand and makes a so-so motion with it. He then places it on his throat, which is still covered in marks from Mayhem's chain. Lollie: "I suppose I should call a nurse in, shouldn't I?" She flashes a quick smile, before she gets off the bed and walks out the door. Toga slowly sits up. He rotates his body so his feet are hanging off the edge. He starts unhooking himself from the machines which gets the machines quite angry. They start beeping louder, except for his heart monitor. It flatlines. The young man in the hospital gown slowly pushes himself off the bed, and steps onto the cold floor. He stumbles a bit, but manages to stay upright. He places his hand on his bandaged head. A nurse rushes into the room with Lollie following her. She tries to convince Toga to lay back down, but he doesn't listen. He just stares at the worry on Lollie's face. He lightly pushes the nurse away, and stumbles into Lollie's arms. The nurse reluctantly let's her take him as she goes to turn off the annoying machines. Lollie: "Tommy, you should lay back down. You aren't well enough to-" Toga puts his finger over her lips, shushing her. They stare into each others eyes, deeply. Toga leans in... Lollie closes her eyes... Tommy rests his head on her shoulder, leaving her lips alone.... Her eyes slowly open. Lollie: "Tommy... you should at least sit back down. I haven't slept well, and I can't hold you up much longer..." Tommy pulls his head off of her shoulder and stumbles back over to the bed. He sits down on the edge. The nurse begins looking him over. Lollie: "The doctor said that it's possible that you could have suffered some amnesia. So, he told me to ask you some questions when you woke up. Are you up for it?" He slowly nods. Lollie: "What year is it?" Tommy tries to speak, but can't. He holds up two fingers, then makes an "O" with them, then holds his index finger up, then puts it down and raises it again. Lollie nods. Lollie: "Do you know your own name?" Toga slowly nods again. He grips his throat, unable to tell her. She slowly nods. Lollie: "I'll take your word for it, Tommy. Do you remember my name?" He stares at her for a few seconds. Her heart begins to race. Tommy's face is blank. He shakes his head no. Lollie's face is covered in fear and sadness. She closes her eyes tight. Possibly trying to fight the tears back. Nurse: "His memory loss is most likely just temporary. He seems to trust you, so you just need to be there for him and be patient. Eventually, his memories of you will most likely come back." Lollie nods, still refusing to open her eyes. Tommy appears uncomfortable with the thought that he's obviously causing her pain, but isn't sure why. Lollie: "Thomas. Do you remember GCW?" She opens her eyes to see him once again nodding yes. Lollie: "Garbage Bag Johnny?" He nods. She takes a deep breath, swallows her tears down, then continues. Lollie: "He... he... he scheduled you to a match. I didn't think to tell him that you wouldn't be medically cleared. I suppose I should call him now..." Toga shakes his head no. Lollie stares at him, unsure. Lollie: "What do you mean 'no?' You can barely stand!" He looks over to the nurse who looks back to Lollie. Nurse: "We need to run a few more tests, but miraculously, I can't find much wrong with him except for bruises on his skin. I can't explain it." With that, Toga stands back up. He doesn't stumble. His balance seems fine. He opens his mouth and manages to fight out some words. Toga: "Let's..... go....." The former champion walks past Lollie, out the door. She stares at him in shock as the Nurse tries to tell him to come back. Lollie quickly grabs his clothes off of the nearby table and follows him out. Lollie: "Sorry, Nurse. I'll try to get him to come back!" She chases after him down the hall, trying to convince him to come back, but he ignores her.....
