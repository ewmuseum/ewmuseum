Mortifera Revealed Pt. 2

12 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 12th Prologue.... Shaman slowly steps forward before the history and past of Mortifera is revealed. He stands before the camera simply crossing his arms looking on. His gaze staring straight out seemingly at nothing and everything at the same time. He begins to speak slow and clear. “Jason O'Neil, Mayhem's brother ironic that you and I face one another next World Wide. I know of your brother Jason, but you I will have to learn a lot about. Perhaps it would be best to make a few phone calls to England.” Shaman stops and looks around as he clears his throat. “Church of Unholy we haven't forgotten about you. I know you and Mortifera have unfinished business. I am not about comedy and jokes. That embarrassing match will go down as the fuel for the fire.” Shaman turns and looks around. “von Doom you talk about chaos and destruction, Obviously you haven't decided to take a look at the careers of the men you face. In fact lets look back at some past of GTT tournaments. You lost to Samuelsson. Was it that hard to forget?” Shaman grins as he cracks his knuckles. “The war is merely begun. You can play the game, but can you play it better than the men who will make you bleed?” Shaman grins and rubs his hands together. “Let the games begin von Doom for The Church of the Unholy will not ever know what is going to hit them.” Shaman rubs his chin and walks in a semi circle. “I shall be a man of few words this evening. I want to thank all those in Dangerous Games. I enjoyed cracking skulls and eliminating people. Soon I will be returning to the pinnacle. Soon Mortifera will arrive at the pinnacle. People like Jason O'Neal will remember to never overlook the people you are going to be facing.” Shaman turns and walks away as he waves his hand away from himself. The camera goes static and then aset of words pop up on the screen. Mortifera Revealed. Pt. 2 A man sits in the shadows as he clasps his hands before himself. To his left a bookcase and he seems to be sitting in a rather comfortable looking leather chair. The man clears his throat and smiles as his lower portion of his face is seen. “Well welcome this evening to the second portion of Mortifera revealed. This time we are going to bring you back on Shaman and his early wrestling career.” The man reaches to the side and lifts a book and opens it. “So let us think back here on the time period. 12 years ago no that was the WWA. How about a little further back to 1994. Where Shaman was into a strong solid year of training and finally he was going to have his big match. The first big match of his career. How did he prepare and what were the thoughts of everyone else around him.” Turning the pages of the book the narrator looks at each page carefully. Seemingly looking for possibly missed details. “We all know about the Wrestling titles and achievements. His current career and his more recent pasts. His dedications to the places he goes too. The fact that he was a man of great ambition for whom the fans used to love. Now we will find out what makes him tick. What paths he had chosen to get to where he is now. Come with me as we bring you to the beginning of the career of the man named Shaman.” The Narrator sits back in the chair and begins reading the pages as he slides his finger through the sentences. August 13th 1994.. High School Gymnasium in upstate New Hampshire. Crowd of around a thousand people. Not far from Dartmouth college. People are anticipating a great show with legends and new upcoming wrestlers on the show. Back behind the curtains the promoter watches as a man named Tyson McCauley walks up to him and speaks. “Look Gabriel I am not sure about this Shaman guy. What type of ropes has he earned in the business to get a shot at my title?” He jams his thumb over to the left as he motions over at Shaman who looks a little nervous and worse in the sickness department. “Kids barely keeping his lunch down. Truth is I don't want to be hurt by some rookie who doesn't know anything.” Gabriel the promoter looks over at Shaman and smiles. “Look Tyson that kid could probably break you in half and word has it from the promoter I paid says the kid is top choice out there.” Gabriel grins and waves over at Shaman who is pretty pale looking somewhat sick. Tyson groans and looks at Gabriel. “Damn kid gets a shot at my title without earning it.” Tyson looks at Shaman head back into the locker rooms as Tyson looks at Gabriel. “It will be a match he will never forget.” Tyson smirks and walks down into the locker rooms. As he passes Shaman he manages to shove him into a few other wrestlers who back off quickly looking at the immense size of Shaman. Shaman raises his gaze towards Tyson and stays silent. Gabriel slowly follows down into the locker room. Gabriel looks at Shaman and nods and smiles respectively. He hands Shaman and envelope looking about. “Here kid here is your take for the evening. Do well in your match ok?” Gabriel looks at Shaman as Shaman nods , but remains silent as he sits down and begins changing. Gabriel looks down at Shaman as he begins changing thinking this will be the last time he sees him unhurt. Knowing Tyson has been with him in the fed a longtime he tended to understand the man pretty well. There wasn't anyone that could stop him from doing what he wanted. Walking away he almost felt bad for the new kid. Someone hurriedly runs through the locker room and walks over to Shaman. “Hey kid you're almost up. Better get up to the stage. Any choice for music?” Shaman reaches into his bag and pulls out a cd and hands it to the man. “Use this I tend to like the sound of the music and it gets me pumped up and works well with the crowd.” Shaman grins as the man runs away. He turns and begins lacing up his boots as Tyson McCauley comes over to him and pushes him in the shoulder as Shaman barely moves. Shaman raises his head and looks at Tyson. Tyson smirks and looks down at him. “Look here kid. I am the man on top here have been for six years. There is nothing you can do that will not keep me as champion.” Tyson shoves Shaman again as Shaman stands up and looks around at all the other guys smiling. Shaking his head Shaman walks towards the stage as he paces back and forth behind the curtains taking a deep breath. Breathing in deep trying to not get sick. He paces back and forth as he waits. He turns his gaze and watches two men pass him and hurries through the curtain. He can tell that these guys are some sort of run in for the previous match. Shaman returns to his pacing as he stops and whispers to himself. “The ace is in the hole.” He returns to the curtains as the crowd roars and the current attackers run from the ring as the fan favorite deters and beats the men away from himself. They both come through the curtain and looks at him. “Good luck kid you are gonna need it.” The first one smirks as he steps through and Shaman looks at them taking a deep breath as he responds looking at them both. “Luck is for the weak.” Both men shrug as they turn and walk on. Shaman knows this match is going to be very harsh and very stiff. He is the good guy in this match like he has always been. He wasn't afraid of bleeding though. He found he actually kind of liked pain and bleeding. Clearing his mind though he turns and looks out the crack in the curtain as the last man leaves the ring and comes back through the back. Shaman turns and steps backwards as he waits. He stretches his arms across his chest and clenches his fists. Suddenly his music hits. Triumph of Steel by Manowar blasts over the make shift PA system as it echoes around the gym. Shaman slowly steps through the ropes. Walking to the ring taking smooth deep breaths. This is what he was waiting for watching the fans scream and boo. Some cheering him on makes for a very unique blend of things happen. His nervousness and his adrenaline seeming becoming one as he steps closer to the edge of the ring. It is as if he is his own machine. The closer he gets the warmer the gears get and the machines oil lubes all the joints. This match is going to be a hostile one he knows this. Will it be a clean match he remembers the mans hostility and thinks otherwise. Knowing he will do the match as cleanly as possible, but this guy crosses a line he will get hurt. The guy is already out to hurt him. Shaman reaches up and grabs the rope pulling himself up onto the ring apron. Slowly he steps over the top rope with ease. Knowing it is just an intimidation point to the crowd. He stepped into the ring and towered over the referee and the crowd. The ring seemed a bit small to him. Like he was a giant in a far away land. A stranger in a very unwelcome place and he was about to face the king of their world. Shaman turns his head as the music hits. Thunderstruck by AC/DC blasts over the speakers. Tyson McCauley World Champion was coming through the curtains. The crowd just adored this man. The man that was bound to hurt his opponent because he was new. This man Shaman had already begun to dislike. Clearing his mind Shaman ran through his first set of moves. What he was going to do and counter. He walked back and forth across the ring as Tyson began throwing chairs and anything he can grab into the ring. He grabbed a mic as he was tossing things into the ring. "Hey kid can't have a real match without obstacles. Time for your welcoming party to the east coast." Shaman groaned as he knew this match had taken a different twist. Something he did not expect and this would be his first real taste of how things can go horribly wrong as he knew it. preparing for the worse Shaman quickly took stock at what was around him. He noticed an older wooden steel folding chair. The ones that didn't give and made note of it as Tyson rolled into the ring as the crowd cheered him on. The one thing Tyson did not realize was that Shaman spent three months in Japan before coming to the east coast of the United States. Shaman was hoping it could be used as an edge in this very hostile and violent environment. Tyson smirked as he looked over at Shaman who was contemplating. "Don't think too hard!!" Slamming Shaman in the back of the head with a Garbage can sending him staggering forward. Shaman reached back and touched his head, he was bleeding. This was unexpected as he turned slowly to regain his bearings. Suddenly he was struck in the head again, but from the front this time. Why didn't the referee ring the bell he thought as he dropped to one knee. Tyson McCauley circled for the kill. In his mind he was going to teach this kid a lesson for coming in and stealing his fire. Not feeling guilty he slammed the kid in the back a few more times. He then raised his arms in victory turning away from his opponent. Shaman notices this and grabs the chair. He is angry as hell and doesn't care what happens now. This was survival as far as he was concerned. He stood slowly pulling the chair up with him above his head as he yelled at Tyson. "Big fucking mistake asshole!" Tyson turned at the last second to be nailed by Shaman. A crushing blow indeed as the chair crashed down onto Tyson's skull breaking the chair in the process his head goes through the wooden center of the chair. Breaking Tyson's nose with what remains of the chair. Shaman watches as Tyson crumples in the center of the ring as Shaman slowly releases the chair. The chair slides from his grip at the shock of what he has just done sets in. The slide of the smooth metal in his fingertips only ignored through the silence and shock of the crowd around him. The motion was slower than slow motion replay. He watched every detail as he looked at his own hands. The first time he had ever struck a man in anger and rage in the ring. This time none of it was controlled. He felt good as if he had to turn on the valve for some sort of energy reviving steam. He turned and slowly stepped through the ropes. Hopping off the ring apron he was barely noticing any of the happenings in the ring as a few EMT crew ran past him to the ring. He didn't stop or turn around he just continued to the back. As he entered the locker room alot of guys moved and gave him a wide berth. Sitting down Shaman grabbed a towel and placed it on the back of his head. he groaned at the sting of an open wound. "What the hell were you thinking out there?" Gabriel grabbed Shaman's arm as Shaman calmly raised his head and looked at Gabriel. "I did what i had to do!" Shaman turned his gaze back down towards the floor as he began to unlace his boots. Gabriel looked at him and turned and looked at the locker room. "Well you are fired Shaman. To no longer work here or on the ...." Gabriel was cut off buy the guy who owned his competing federation in the area. The man stood at the door and grinned as he looked over at Shaman. "You are Shaman? Heard alot about you kid. Just wanted you to know i saw the whole thing and have it on video if you ever need a lawyer. Gabriel he has a contract offer from me which means if he wishes he will be on the show for Thursday." Shaman looks up and looks at the new man. "Not the best moment to be fighting over my services wouldn't you think?" Shaman sounded a bit annoyed as a Police officer walked into the back and spoke up. "I am looking for Shaman, Oh sorry son didn't see you there." He looked at Shaman who held a bloody towel to his head. "Son several fans said Tyson attacked you. Do you wish to press charges?" Shaman looked at the Police officer. "No sir, I think he is going to suffer enough for several weeks." The police officer nodded and left the area. Gabriel looked around and down at Shaman as he huffed. He turned and walked out the door as several of the other wrestlers looked over at him. The other promoter walked over and placed a hand on Shaman's shoulder. "Names Gary Heimricht ever heard of a company called CWC?" Shaman looked up and shook his head. "No well it is this huge dream several investors and Fed heads have had. I think you would fit right in with our Florida unit." Gary just began to ramble on as Shaman listened. Back to Present..... The man turns another page as he grins as he sits up slowly. Closing the book he narrows his shadowed gaze towards the camera. "It is funny how time and small events change a man. First time being forced to bleed. First time letting anger and rage take control of yourself and learning you liked it." Tapping his fingers lightly on the chair he looks at the closed book. "The CWC was an umbrella federation where Shaman came through. A place where he was discovered and found his road through the NCW later to be changed to the named WWA. From which he came to GCW." Placing the book down he leans over and looks around the room. "Many things have come and gone through this mans career. Those of you know for sure you cannot understand his methods nor his madness. Yet could it merely be the way the man known as Shaman wants them to be?" Bringing his hands together he smiles slightly. "My pleasure is bringing you this history of events. Understanding these men can be most unnerving, but knowing them both well and knowing their past makes me a most unique individual in this circumstance. More to come more to come i will assure you. Time is on my side my friends and soon the discoveries of each will be implemented in each phase as i present with you the truths and building blocks of who these two mysterious men of Mortifera are." The man laughs a raspy laugh as the camera fades to black. The end......not even close.
