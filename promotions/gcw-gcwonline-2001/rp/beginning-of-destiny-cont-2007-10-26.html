Beginning of Destiny Cont...

26 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-26-07 Standing before the stone Shaman patiently waited for the sun to shine down upon it. He knew the great spirit would be waiting for him once the sun shined high in the early morning sky. The task before him he knew not of its origins. Perhaps it had to do with his own inner turmoils of late. His drive fr something further. He just knew that things were not of the same as those of his past. At great moments in his past he had always consulted the spirits and they tested him and his loyalties. Reaching down he lifted the brown and golden leaves. The signals of the coming winter of cold and death. Sleeping and slumbering giants of the world creaked and rocked in the cold winds. These hills would be covered in snow and frozen death. The struggle for survival within nature would rage as he stood looking at the beginning stages of it. Like an angel of death before ones passing. Crushing the leaves within his hands he released them as a breeze scattered them about before the forest floor. He turned and a great bear stood before him as the sun shown brightly upon the stone that as before him. The great bear stood and spread its huge massive paws outwards. "You see the land before you and its cycle of life. You see it for what it is and not what it could be. Open your eyes to the world that comes. It is an ever turning wheel, much like your trials and tribulations of your past. You stood and you fought for everything you believed in. The battle did not give you what you sought. You realized you were fighting a dead cause. You found a brother in arms in your destiny now. You are two pebbles building the same mighty wall. Two dangerous pebbles which could destroy the wall if removed." Looking at the great bear Shaman pondered a moment and his gaze hardened as he looked at the bear and spoke. "My teaming, aren't why you are here." A deep laughter rose and echoed through the forest and the bear dropped down to all fours. He began walking away and stopped as he looked back at Shaman. "Come i have much to show you." Quietly he stopped and looked at the Great Bear. He raised his hand and motioned for him to stop. "There isn't a path i haven't gone down in this life. I have been a champion in all aspects. I have held positions as an owner a business man and everything under the sun. I have a beautiful friendship and relationship with a woman named Kawanaha. Yet each and every time you spirits want to interrogate me and send me on these journeys of destiny you keep forgetting i am not the same man i was before." Shaman steps forward walking with the bear. The ear turns its head and looks at Shaman as he walks. "You Destiny is entwined with your past, present, and future. Have you not ever pondered the reactions of these futures and pasts. You have achieved everything physically achievable for a man in your position. You have wealth, You have power, You have everything, but one." An image appears before Shaman he stares at the image a minute and then looks back at the great bear. "You call this destiny? I call it a sham just for more useless trinkets." The great bear lets out a deep throaty laugh and walks on as Shaman follows. "Even trinkets and items have there places in destiny. Remember King Arthur and Excalibur, Many thought of the sword as a trinket, but in truth it was a symbol of what was to come and how it was to end." The great Bear walked as he talked. He had a very interesting toothy grin Shaman realized as they walked. The bear was wise in his methods and temperament. He had always pondered this thought over the previous meetings. The bear began to speak in a poem type of speech. " Man has a destiny. The destiny of man is not set in stone. Yet the destiny of man is to return to the stone. The path tot he stone is set with trivial accomplishments. Beginning the path through the unknown creates illusions. Creatures and beings of renown and fantasy exist here. From these beings man uses his achievements to create. Imagination is a path many men take. Science is a path for the weak minded. Too many answers cannot be answered. Many happenings cannot be merely explained. The sun is the only constant in mans destiny. It is followed by its sister the moon. Destiny is easily defined. Define it by making your mark. Show the path to others." Shaman looks at th bear as he quit speaking. Shaman smirks as he speaks. "Then i have one for you bear." Shaman walks as the bear turns his massive head and looks at Shaman. "Destiny is enveloped in Darkness. The blood of the weak willed fills the road. Opening the path to mass injections of rage and anger. Open your arms wide and accept your destiny. Accept the destiny of Oblivion." The bear walks forward and then stops on the edge of a cliff as Shaman comes to a stop beside the great bear and looks out of the landscape as the clouds are growing darker and a wind has become a bit brisk. They both look out as if anything and everything is being explained and answered right before their very eyes. Shaman reaches his hand out and makes a wave motion across the landscape in the distance. "My people walked these lands for centuries before the Europeans came and began conquering and killing them in the name of progress and religion. They called us barbarians and they killed us with guns and weapons my ancestors did not have. Did they crawl into a hole and disappear? No my ancestors fought hard and valiantly made a place out of the chaos that had become there future and destiny. Can i say the same for others that live today? Not really some hide in the bottle of fire water or hide behind some lame excuse that the white man owes them something. Myself i believe i make my own destiny and own path in the world. You can show me a hundred easy paths and i will grind out my own." Shaman turns and looks at the great bear. Shaking his head he turns away and walks off from the bear as the bear speaks. "Good the first steps of your destiny have finally begun with you relying on yourself." Shaman ignored the bear as he walked watching the grey clouds roll over head slowly. He knew there would be rain soon. He just wondered when and how much. He turned and looked at the mountains ahead as his phone rang. "Hello?" He seemed to listen intently and nodded his head a few times. "Winters yes i know him." Shaman looked at the phone a second as he pulled it away and then brought it back to his ear. "He what? Look i am returning to the Casino now to review this message left for me. Make sure everything is in place." Shaman shuts the cellphone off and puts it in his pocket as he slowly walks away thinking and pondering. "Great a US Title defense and an announcement for GCW's World Wide. What else can happen while i am trying to trace my path of destiny." Shaman disappears over a ridge. To be Continued....
