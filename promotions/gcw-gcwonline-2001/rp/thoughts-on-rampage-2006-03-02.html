Thoughts on Rampage

2 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 02, 2006 In many ways, change is good. It can bring about a whole new process of thought, and even a more efficient way at that. When it comes to politics, change can be good. Economics, change can be good. Fashion, change has been great. Watching something take shape, change, and form its own identity is what change is all about. This is how the Mona Lisa came to be, starting form a Carte Blanc, or blank slate. DaVinci saw something, and put it into motion. He did something about it. Plenty of times, we’ve witnessed change go wrong, though. Some tried to change what worked, with a marvelous chance, and they fell short. What has this brought us? Lessons on what not to do. Although, it has shown us that if we dare to change, and succeed, the benefits could be great. But the consequences could be greater. Kind of like that dare that your friend will insist you do, and you know you could do it. You know that if you did that dare, that every single person in school would say, “Yeah, that kid’s the BALLS!” But if you did it and for some reason messed up, you’re not goin? to the ballpark on Saturday afternoons for a while. Maybe never again. Get my drift? Global Championship Wrestling was experiencing much change. A whole new roster shift was occurring. Kimbusa was signed just two weeks ago, obviously increasing the excitement in the Heavyweight division. Now, Shadow and three other newcomers were prepared to advance toward the World Title in the Rampage Tournament. Things were movin?. But what many did not know is how more change was to come. Change from a much quieter division. Two men were about to make their mark on an industry they had second-hand experience in, but First Class skill was going to be their propaganda for this campaign. These men, Harvey and Clark LeClere, or better known as “The Foundation” in GCW, were poised and prepared to make their mark not just on GCW, not just on the fans, the LeClere’s had a bigger idea? Taped hours before GCW’s Rampage. “The Foundation will undermine everything you think you THOUGHT you knew about Tag Team Wrestling.” Clark LeClere chimed in. He was wearing his black and white varsity jacket, with a pair of black Oakley M-frames. He was a proud man, chess puffed out and arms crossed over it. “Meet Harvey.” He nudged his head to his right. “Hi.” This was Harvey LeClere. The stare on his face said it all. He had his arms crossed, and black and white varsity jacket, also. “We’re the Foundation.” These two men stood there, and then the camera panned back. Then, Clarke spoke: “Tonight, tag team wrestling in Global Championship Wrestling will be changed. The number one contender will be determined, and the hunt will begin. Now, with all fairness to the Guild and Team Baked?” Clarke then looked to Harvey. “Actually, with no respect to The Guild, and an embarrassing amount of disrespect to Team Baked, because everybody knows that neither of those putrid duos deserves to have any chance at tag gold, whatsoever. Now, as far as UNITE, or Futureshock, all’s there is to spill out of my mouth is?” “Pathetic?” Harvey butted in. “Hmm” That is a good guess, but we were looking for “No Chance”. C’mon, Harv. You don’t think that these guys have anything on us, do you?” Clarke tapped Harvey’s shoulder, and then Harvey broke his pose. “No, man. No.” He adjusted his posture. “What I don’t get is why these chumps are even involved in this deal” I don’t get what qualifies UNITE or Futureshock to compete against us. I mean, it’s simply going to be a massacre when those saps step into the ring with us. We’re going to ? ? “Hey, hey. It’s all good, mon. We’re hiiigh, remember” Don’t you want some munchers, maaan? Aaahhahaha!” Clarke was now wearing a Rastafarian type beanie with dreadlocks and dark aviator sunglasses. He3 swayed from side to side with a huge grin on his face. Harvey stared at him and then pushed his brother back and out of view. “What I’m trying to get at is simple. It won’t matter who we beat,” Harvey counted off on his fingers. “Where we beat them.” He counted again. “Or When. Because to be straight to the point about this,” Harvey’s hand pointed straight in the camera. “We will be the GCW Tag Team Champions. We will dominate this company with ease. And lastly, we will, most definitely, show all of you uneducated people that wrestling is not a sport.” Both LeClere’s leaned in. “It’s a way of life.” Both men slowly turned around, and the scene faded to the image of, in bold print. In black and white: “The Foundation”.
