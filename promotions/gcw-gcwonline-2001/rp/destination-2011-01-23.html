Destination

23 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/11 Sheep. Every one of them. One at a time, they waited until the GCW staff member waived their finger, giving them the okay to walk out into the airport. Every member of the GCW roster had to get their face time. Their adoring audience was waiting. Time and time again, the airports were almost exactly like the shows. Wrestler walks out. Fans acknowledge wrestler. Wrestler reacts to fans reaction, then continues on their way. It was the life of a professional wrestler. One day you're a celebrity, the next you're a joke... it just depends on where you are and who you're with. With her backpack slung sloppily over her right shoulder and wearing a pair of giant headphones, Vivica J. Valentine didn't even hear the Columbus, Ohio go crazy as Andy Murray headed down the escalator... but she knew they did. She could see it in the eyes of the people around her. Alexis O'Ryan. Micah St. Clair. Alexander Edwards. They all looked around nervously, sharing a glance with one another wondering if some day that would be one of them. The star of the show. The biggest reaction. "That was me once." Vivica thought to herself over the screeching guitars of Avenged Sevenfold in her headphones. 2007 seemed like a lifetime ago, when the spotlight was firmly heating her shoulders and the crowd would hang on every word that she said. Things were much simpler then. It was an easier time. Somewhere, life just seemed to get more complicated. Looking over her shoulder, she locked eyes with Jared Sykes. A nod is all he exchanged as he readjusted his bags. In just a few days they would be stepping into the ring against each other for the second time. Last time, neither of them came out on top when Tchu became the last PrimeTime Central Elite Champion. Now, Jared Sykes was a member of the Global Championship Wrestling team. His SCCW run surely must have seemed like a lifetime ago. When the fans were lifting him up on their shoulders and making him the star of the show. Even though neither of them said a word, they shared a mutual sentiment: Starting over is a bitch. Standing just ahead, Josh Briggs waved on an eager Toga, who was just in front of her in line. As he took a step forward, a disappointing sigh left the mouths of the masses who gathered down below. They holding signs supporting their favorite superstars. They tightly gripped onto printed 8 x 10s and autograph books. Some were looking to make a memory they'll remember for the rest of their lives, others ready to make a quick buck. She watched Josh's lips move, but doesn't hear a word over "Gunslinger." It was probably better off that way, as she pulled the headphone off from around her ears and left them to hang on her neck. "What?" She answered with the slightest hint of aggravation in her voice. She'd done this a million times and it was her favorite part of the song. "I like your haircut." "Fuck off." Valentine snapped back instantaneously. "No, I'm serious. I think it looks good short." "Then thanks?" She attempted to backtrack while maintaining her pride, "I liked it better before." "I know. I was there." Josh spoke just before he looked over his shoulder and then back at the GCW mainstay, "It's your turn. Knock ‘em dead, sunshine." The joke brought a smirk to her face. A loving tap on the shoulder accompanied her first step onto the escalator, and down below she watched the crowd push and shove their way to the front in order to get the first glimpse. Just like every single show, she couldn't help but hold her breath, and all she could hear was the music still played out of her headphones: "But with all that we've been through, after all this time I'm coming home to you..." "Hey! Viivicaaaaaaa!!" A man screamed from the front row right before a round of applause came forth through the crowd. Word spread through the masses like a wildfire, causing the cheers to grow louder with each passing second. As she reached the bottom, she looked on as airport security held back fans and eBay merchants alike trying to get an autograph or just a simple wave. While she didn't remember the faces, she did remember the reaction last time she was in Ohio. It wasn't pleasant, so she must be doing something right. "Please Viv, just a quick signature." "I love you Vivica!" "You're still a bitch!" Of course, mandatory smark had to chime in with his opinion as she continued on. While the cheers were certainly nice and brought a smile to her face, it was still different than it used to be. It wasn't Andy Murray's cheers. It wasn't Beatlemania. There was still work to be done. You can't account for all of your sins over night. Sometimes, it's better if you fight for it. "Miss Vivica?" She felt a small tug on her jeans while reached out for her bag in luggage claim. Taken by surprise, she quickly spun around to look into the big brown eyes of a little girl holding on to her latest issue of GCW magazine. "Can you sign this for me please?" Airport security quickly rushed to the scene, previously distracted by the bigger fans in the audience. Looking out into the crowd, Vivica could see the little girl's mother. She was screaming over the fans... "Kelsey, get back here!" The fear of losing her child was worn on her face, "KELSEY!" As airport security surrounded her, Vivica put up her hand to stop them for just a second. Getting down on one knee, she looked into the big brown eyes of the little girl and grabbed the magazine out of her hands. As she pulled a sharpie from her jean pocket, she heard the little girl say... "Someday I'm going to be a wrestler just like you." Those words stopped Vivica in mid-autograph. It hit close to home, bringing back fond memories of her own childhood... sneaking over to friend's houses to watch wrestling on television because she wasn't allowed at home. She never got to see any of her own heroes. Never got to watch any shows live. "Kelsey, is it?" The girl froze in fear as her idol acknowledge her presence. "Can you do me a big favor?" While she didn't get an answer, she knew the little girl was listening intently. "When you grow up and you become a wrestler, like you say you want to do..." Vivica paused for a moment, finishing her signature on the magazine, "I want you to be better than me." "O... okay." A huge tooth grin crossed the face of little Kelsey as security gently led her back in the direction of her mother. Vivica's suitcase came by on a second pass, and she pulled it off of luggage claim and glanced at the escalator as Dynasty made his way down towards the masses. She knew that she'd made her share of mistakes, and her history is something that could never be rewritten, but if she owned her mistakes, learned her lesson, and kept someone else from falling into the same traps that she did... She would always be a step better than Karina Wolfenden, and that is what it was to be a real role model.
