Crashing Down

12 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/06 Changes seem to be the one thing that is for sure. Everything is changing around us. Everything is in constant motion. Change keeps things from being boring. Change keeps things interesting. Change leads to challenges, and challenges are what we thrive on. But too much change can overwhelm someone. Too much change can run you over, chew you up, and spit you out as something less then you were. But that's for the weak people. For others, things that challenge you, they must be taken down and destroyed before they do that to you. This is what GCW is facing right now. We're here to induct change upon this place. And we have. The combined alliance of Dies Irae and the KnightMare Brigade have taken all. They've taken it and held it and they're calling the shots. While Caldera writes our paychecks and plays around in the bush leagues, we are your lords... ladies... masters and mistresses. GCW, you have to adapt and overcome, right? Why? Why fight change? Change is inevitable. If not us, then it would be someone else sometime later. You might as well get used to us being here. Especially people like Jayce Bradley. I am change, and I'm coming for you. Nut up or shut up. Stand up or get the hell out of my way. I don't care which. Just make up your mind and get it over with. ~A Few Days Ago~ ~Kentucky~ Lia had gotten up and walked with Annie without too much of a protest or a fight. Blaise knew where they were going, he'd talked to Annie, protesting for the last two hours. But she had promised Blaise that no harm would come to Lia. He wasn't going to do anything to get in the way of this truce or alliance. However, Virgil wasn't too sure. ?How can you allow this?? Blaise looked Virgil in the eyes. He knew that was the only way to show the man you meant business now. He'd picked it up. Virgil ate fear or uncertainty. ?Annie gave you her word.? ?And how do I know the word of your Order is...? ?Annie is more then just in the Order. She's a princess of the Unseelie Courts of Tir Na Nog. Nothing will happen to Lia.? Virgil looked only slightly convinced. ?I know he won't do anything, okay?? Virgil really wasn't too convinced there. So Blaise pulled out something from one of the pockets on the inside of his jacket. It was a knife like Virgil had seen Blaise hold before. But instead of the gleaming silver that Blaise used, it was jet black. ?What's that?? ?That's his spirit blade. I'm the custodian of it while he's in there. Annie wouldn't allow him to go in armed.? Virgil shook his head. ?You're lucky Lia trusts you. Korwyn Malik doesn't need his blades to do bad things. He just prefers it.? Blaise smirked at him. ?Speaking from experience there, Virgil?? Virgil smiled at the challenge. ?Actually I think I am. I seem to remember maybe meeting him at least once in Spain. When he was with the Templars. It's fuzzy, hazy. But the name is familiar to me. And the atrocities he did.? Blaise nodded. ?Another war, another time.? Virgil nodded to him. ?How goes the research with Alexis?? Blaise walked over to the bar in the room, and poured himself a drink. ?They've gone through all of the samples the Order had. And Alexis is smart. She found an issue with all of our sample collection and data. The spirit blades used by the hunters taint the samples. Don't know why, but they're doing things at the genetic level. So we're trying to get them some untainted samples.? Virgil nodded again. Blaise poured him a glass of wine and walked over to him, holding it out. Virgil took the glass and sniffed its bouquet a bit. ?So, you're the leader of this little pack. Tell me something, how are you ensuring Alexis' safety?? Blaise took another drink. ?Well I've uprooted the routine of my people for it. We know the Den has moved. We know the Society knows that she's left the estate. They seem to leave her alone up there. I wonder why that would be?? Blaise gave him a sly smile. ?That would be because of me. Alexis is our friend, and has proven her loyalty to us. It's my duty...? Blaise smiled wide to him. ?You still hold the old code of the knights to heart, don't you?? Virgil nodded. ?Good, that's respectable. I'll have you know Virgil, I've assigned her a personal Guardian. You know of the Hunters, do you also know of our Guardians?? Virgil nodded his affirmative again. ?I've heard stories.? ?Well I've got two of them under me. She's been assigned one of them.? Virgil sipped the wine and asked the obvious. ?Who?? ?Sergeant Grayson Brand.? Virgil didn't look happy. ?What? Why? What are his credentials to do that? I know he's a scientist, but what makes him a bodyguard.? Blaise put his glass down. ?Grayson is a fighting instructor. He was best in his class in our West Wind Academy and was picked up as a teacher. He's also been a Guardian for about eight years. He's never lost a charge.? Virgil didn't look any happier. ?I pulled him off of Dani's guardianship and put a green-horn, Josh, on her. Dani is my main job here. But I wanted to make sure Alexis was safe. I wanted to make sure she felt safe, and Lia was happy with the arrangement.? Virgil was almost trying to stare him down. ?And for the record, Gray was the best pick. You know what he is. Anyone tried anything, he'd be a big fucking surprise for them at least the first time. After that, we'd all be there. Gray was almost made a hunter. He's good.? Virgil's composure changed slightly. ?What kept him from becoming a hunter?? Blaise smirked. ?You said you know Malik. That means you know how he works. You tell me?? Virgil could only shake his head. ?Gray didn't have full control of his feral side. I've seen his temper go off. Malik likes cold blooded and calculating killers.? Blaise raised his drink glass. ?Bingo. But, remember, Gray is also a scientist. He's a logical thinker. And he's also the most gentleman guardian I've ever met. Alexis' virtue, how ever much she has, will remain intact in his hands.? Virgil didn't' have a chance to say anything. At this time Lia and Annie both walked out of the conference room. They were followed closely behind by the man that had killed more vampires through the centuries then anyone else. An enemy of old. Now an ally. This was a change that needed to be tackled, and wrestled to the ground. ~A few days later~ ~Washington, D.C.~ Don't things ever stop? Don't they ever give a day's rest? No... Blaise has woken up this morning to an emergency phone call by Annie. And it had been on his cell, not the Order's communications network. It had been private. That was enough to raise his suspicions and get things moving this morning. After hanging up with Annie, he'd leaned over and kissed Ukyo right on her lips. She'd awoken slowly at that, and by the time her eyes were fully open, he was already getting dressed. ?What's up?? He threw a t-shirt on, and then moved to lacing up his moccasin boots. ?Annie, she just called on my cell. Said it was important and I needed to come talk to her. She's downstairs.? ?Okay, well I'll watch over Dani then. She wants to try to see if Lia can come see her and her grandfather today.? Blaise nodded in agreement as he then got up and headed out the door. He didn't wait around. As soon as he was into the common area, it was just a stop by the refrigerator to grab a Sobe No Fear, and then it was out the doors. Gray wasn't even sleeping on the couch, so he and Alexis were already in the lab. Things were working like clock work. Annie could only ruin that today. He drank the No Fear down in gulps as the elevator went down to the first floor. Once the elevator was at the bottom, he moved quickly towards the small meeting room she'd prepared. He walked right in, and saw only Annie standing there. ?What's up? Who died or is going to kill us today?? Annie shook her head. ?It's na' tha' Blaise. This is more outside business. Real world stuff.? ?Oh?? Annie didn't look too happy. She pulled out an envelope and handed it to Blaise. ?What's this?? He looked at the envelope, and noted the return address for it... Four Winds Entertainment Seattle, Washington He opened the envelope with a vigor and pulled out the letter and a small separate piece of paper. ?What the hell?? Annie adjusted her skirt a bit, showing how uncomfortable she was. ?What is this? What's going on here?? ?Tha', Mr. Blaise McClancy is yer share o' th' now liquidated stocks an' property o' Four Winds Entertainment. That's a check for jus' under fifty million dollars.? He shook his head in disbelief. ?But I didn't own that much stock. And I know the materials wouldn't have sold for that much.? Annie pulled out the next piece of paperwork for him. ?Actually, it's na' quite yours yet. That check is pending a signature from you.? She handed him a folder, a sheet of paper was inside of it. ?Wait, this transfers Sean's shares to me? Can you fill me in here, without all the cloak and dagger? Why did he close 4WW? If it was in trouble, all he'd have to do is call me.? She answered him quickly. ?It wasn'na in trouble, per se.? ?Okay?? ?Na, bu' there 'as been a situation. We 'ave 'ad to pull Sean and Patti from th' public eyes. If they're in public, they're a target.? ?A target? The Order will take care of it.? ?This isn'na an Order situation. The Order is na allowed to get involved in th' Politics of the Seelie Court.? Blaise didn't have to say anything. The look on his face showed her that he wasn't believing anything. ?Look, Blaise. This 'as 'appened so quickly. So some o' us are still tryin' to get th' world to stop spinning. But let me tell ye, as a representative of the Courts, we've 'ad to pull Sean, the bearer of th' Mantle of Crown Prince Bres MacElatha and 'is wife from the public's view. Fer Patti's sake.? ?What about Patti? And what does this have to do with me? Other then being Sean's cousin, I'm really not his next of kin or anything.? ?No, but ye're the one who can use th' money publicly. Ye're also the sole owner o' th' house in Vegas now. As well as the condo building in Seattle, an' a few other things. 4 Winds Entertainment is no more, and ye're owning all o' wha' is left o' it.? ?Why?? That got Annie to smile. ?Cause Sean finally figured ou' 'ow t' stop all the fighting. Out of the four crown princes, yer father and 'is don'na want to be named heir. Sean... Bres and Nauda want it. But Nauda is a gobshite, ye know this. And Sean finally ga' something over him. But we know 'ow Nauda's current bearer works.? Blaise smiled. ?Patti's knocked up, huh?? Annie's smile diminished. She didn't' confirm that. Blaise knew why it was a touchy subject. Years ago, it would have been her looking at the title of Crown Princess, and not Patti. ?I know, Annie. But try not to be to stuck up on it. They're happy.? ?She's na jus' pregnant, Blaise. They've checked 'er. It's a traditional birth fer a McClancy. She's carrying more then one. It's still too early to tell, fully. But there is more then one life force in 'er womb. In nine months, if Patti Anderson-McClancy gives birth t' a male heir, Sean will be named Oberon's Heir for this next cycle.? Blaise nodded, as he grabbed a pen from the table. He uncapped it and then signed his name to the documents. ?Well, as the executor of the company's assets, I guess you've done your job. I'm now rich again. Guess I have to take the boys and girls out for dinner tonight or something. Not like a bank will cash this right away.? Blaise had a nervous silence after that. ?Ye okay, Blaise?? ?Yeah, but there is something else you're hiding from me here. I got the money and what they couldn't liquidate, but what about the side of the Order this does affect?? ?Wha' abou' it?? Blaise started to get a bit irritated. ?It's simple. I'm only a lieutenant. And I've only got that by accepting your little project here. Sean was the Brigade's captain. Who's taking the Brigade over? Who's my new boss?? Annie smiled, wider then she'd done on many things. ?Tha' is a good question there.? ?Oh, stop it. We both know that Sean and Patti don't hold me in very high regard. Sean's leaving on good terms. That means he picks his replacement. Who the hell is he making the new Captain, so I can know who is going to expect me to kiss ass.? Annie reached into the pocket of her blazer jacket and pulled out what looks a piece of paper with a few pins stuck into it. She tossed it across the table to Blaise. ?Ye can keep talkin' crap like tha', but I' ga' a few things fer yet. Jus' so ye know, it was Sean who recommended you fer me mission. An' when I asked 'im th' same thing ye just asked me... who is now in charge o' the KnightMare Brigade, 'e took those off 'is lapels 'imself.? Blaise looked down at the insignia that was used on the Order's uniforms to denote a captain of a unit. He reached down and picked them up. ?Congratulations, Captain McClancy. Not only is th' money all yers... But the KnightMare Brigade is yers t'. All factions now answer t' ye. An' ye were 'and picked by someone we' 'ope will also be th' next King o' Tir Na Nog.? It took Blaise a moment to absorb what was being said here... ?Now, Mr. McClancy, there is a few things we need t' finish. An' I ga' news fer ye. The bank will cash that check. Bu' ye'll need me there t' do it. I'd like t' get this done t'day. Can you come with me?? He held the new insignia in his hands.... ?Yeah. Let's go.? ~~~~~~~~~~~ Changes.... Challenges... I'm ready, are you? ~To Be Continued~
