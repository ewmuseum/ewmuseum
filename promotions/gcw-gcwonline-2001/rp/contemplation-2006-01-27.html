Contemplation...

27 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 Jan 06 I find myself alone once again. It is a feeling that I am more than used to by now but this time it is not because I MUST but because it is what I choose. The holidays are over and supposedly work is going to resume as usual for me, GCW and all. Something is fighting it though. I think about going back into the bright lights and the crowds of ingrates that hover in those stands and it just gives me a headache. For awhile now I have just felt unmotivated, for lack of a better word. I have been wondering about why this came about for the last few weeks. I have sat alone in run down motels and empty bars just so I would not have my thoughts polluted with whatever kinds of filth other people could swarm me with. I have much on my mind ? some of it I really could not even begin to explain to the fullest. Since GCW reopened I have felt this calling to show up the world. I felt as if I had this chip on my block, a chip large enough to house a third world country really. I was disrespected and tossed aside in the old days of GCW and that is something that has never set well with me. So as most everyone in the world noticed, I took out my anger and frustration on any and everyone who stepped in my path. It began with the likes of Mike Hardy and Natural Born Killer and it has ended with the likes of Michael Stevens. Dominating the United States Title scene was incredibly worthwhile but that Epic World Title reign was something of legend! Yet now ? what am I fighting for? I no longer am just looked past or through because the GCW and its inhabitants know what I can and will do to the ones who doubt me. I have set records, won titles ? including the grandest of them all. It is almost a god damn certainty that the name LeStatt Knight will rest in the pages of the GCW Hall of Fame one day. So I sit here in this dark and beat down motel room where I can guess that many a prostitutes have been used in unimaginable ways and think. Strange as it sounds its places like this that one can feel most comfortable at times. The room smells of dust and old sheets, of sex and cigarettes, and in my case of an expensive bottle of ? well actually I am not even really sure what the hell it is I have been drinking. What I do know is I have drunk a lot of it. Back on topic ? what do I have to fight for any longer? It is a question that I have desperately tried to answer but for some reason have yet to find one. Do I want to allow myself to be swallowed up by the mouth of mediocrity? Do I dare to let my name be drug through the mud if my performance goes way down simply because my mind is not “into it?” Honestly I do not know what it is exactly that I want or need right now. Perhaps I should simply quit now and keep the image of who I am in their minds. I could put the wedding together of Mary’s greatest dreams. Take her on the honeymoon she wants, make love to her over and over again. We could buy a new home, far away from all of our problems, have children, live happily ever after. Perhaps I could even put more work into My Uncle’s company. It is still thriving but my touch in all that happens isn’t very strong as of now. I think this ? but I know deep down that it may never work out that way. It is in my blood to seek and destroy in the way that only Wrestling can provide. If I quit would I end up coming back numerous times, only to look worse and worse each time? If I quit could I live with that decision knowing it is one of the things that has consistently made me happy over some of my most trying times? Wrestling has been an outlet for me, an outlet for the aggression, the anger, and the hatred that I have bottled up inside. Each time my fist connects with another man’s face and you know the pain is unbearable ? a piece of that anger in unleashed. Every single time that my head is smashed by a chair a bit of that aggression is soothed in that instant. Every time I pin my opponent’s shoulders to the mat and win a match ? that HATRED I feel for the world calms for just a brief second. It’s like the feeling a heroin addict gets when that needle first pierces their skin. It is total euphoria but yet moments later they want, crave, and NEED more. Wrestling has been my drug for this long. Yet the question still remains if I NEED it anymore? When a person’s life cycle has reached it’s end they die. When a movie’s play time is over, the movie ends. So what happens when you have accomplished the only number of things that you have ever wanted to? I have gained the respect of my peers. I have fought my way from the bottom to the top, winning the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. I found love in the form of Typhoid Mary, when I never thought it would be possible again. I found a friend in David Sandy, or RaZiel. I buried a hatred for a man which at one time I LIVED to hate. I have found happiness? So is this chapter of my life closed? Do I turn the final pages and end this reign of “terror” as it has been referred to as? I wish the answer was sitting right here in front of me, but sadly it is not. As a matter of fact I must step into the ring in a matter of days with the most unlikely partner I may have ever had. Bryan Mayhem? All I know is that even with my doubts I cannot let my game slip, not one bit. If people see it then they will try to expose my weakness. I will give it my all ? as if it were my last. Perhaps it will be. Sure it is not the grand send off that I would like but any match after NC-17 would fail to compare. So perhaps it is my destiny to simply ride off into the sunset, leaving the GCW Kingdom for Michael Stevens to lead. If anyone has earned that right it is him, even if it kills me to admit ? even if it is just to myself. Regardless of what I choose I know what I have meant to this business and what this business has meant to me. Regardless if the fans loved me or hated me I gave them what they wanted to see and deep down they all know that as well. So tonight I will drink, I will drink until the world goes black and my memory melts away. I will take in the smells of this motel, the taste of the alcohol, and sounds of the ever moving city outside. Tonight I will let everything come to me. I will enjoy this for what its worth. Because tomorrow could be the end. Sweet Dreams.
