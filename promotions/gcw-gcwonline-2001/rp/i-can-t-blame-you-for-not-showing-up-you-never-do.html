I Can't Blame You For Not Showing Up. You Never Do.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Will you show? A question I've asked myself for a week. Will Steven Caldera show is face at the arena, and enter the ring with me? Will he prove that all of this 'retirement' talk was just that: talk. I know Steven Caldera. I've wrestled in his company long enough to know when he's lying. There's still a desire in his heart to get into the ring, and get himself torn apart. And I'm just the man to do it. Face it, Steven. You've been ducking me for years. Every time I show up somewhere, you leave. Every time I come to find you, you conveniently 'retire', only to come back six months later. You're worse than a cockroach, Steve. You're not as honorable, and not nearly as clean. The moment I left GCW, you had it in for me. You wanted to see me splattered across the mat, and you stopped at nothing to acheive that goal. You hired Kimbusa, and held his wife and family over his head until the job was complete. And when he jumped onto my chest, I've got to admit, I thought it was over. I never thought I'd walk again, much less enter the ring. I never thought I'd be able to wrestle at the level I did as The Dark Messiah. The Scarecrow would be the death of me. But, I did. I came back, stronger, and with a purpose. It's just a shame that FSW management didn't care for you, and shipped you out before I could return. It's a shame that Kimbusa ran to OSW before I could snap his fat little shoulder off of his torso. You showed up in RPW, just long enough to run away as soon as you heard I was hired, and coming for you. This time, it was different. I figured with you in a nostalgic mood, pushing jobbers to the moon with titles they earned in the backroom, rather than in the ring, you'd hire Dan Rivero back. Rivero, was to come in with John Reed, and the two were going to tag together. But, Rivero, the genius he pretended to be, had a better idea. He told you a week before their debut, that he had a line on the location of former GCW Superstar, Badger Benandanti. He told you 'Stevie, baby, I got the Badger, and he wants back in. Here's my idea...' And you approved it. Never knowing that I was the one under the Caldera Mask, you went along with every single part, even shaking my hand backstage after my first Worldwide. I'll bet it sickens you knowing now that I was the one under the mask, not Badger, huh? I'll bet you've got the Ivory soap, and you're scrubbing your hands, getting the feel of O'Neil off of your skin. I won't lie, Steve. I had to take a long, long shower at the hotel that night, to get the disgust off of my skin. And so, two weeks ago, there I was. In the middle of the ring, the mask came off, and shock was heard around the world. Jason O'Neil, the prodigal son of GCW had returned once more, this time, coming not for the GCW Championship, which should have been his, long, long before, but, for the head of Steven Caldera. And where are you now? Running scared. It's a catch-22, isn't it Steve? The biggest event of the year, with the biggest matches of the year, and yet, you can't show up, for fear of getting beaten down by the man you've been running from for three years now. Rumor has it, you're not even going to bother. You're just going to sit in the hotel room, watching on TV, hoping and praying that I don't show up. It's a shame I'm already here. Before the ring, before the fans show up, and before the arena even opens, I'm standing here, at Madison Square Garden, with nothing else to do but sit, and think about how I'm going to hurt you. I'll tell you exactly how I'm going to hurt you: Slowly and methodically. I'm not going to simply jump you from behind, and wait for the blood to flow. I'm going to make sure you see me coming. I'm going to tell you exactly what I'm going to do to you, before I even do it. I'm going to break your nose, your cheek, your arm, your leg. I'm going to tear your shoulder, your pectoral, your trapesius, your abdomen, your quadricep, and your calves. And finally, when you can't move anymore, I'm going to show GCW exactly what's been missing the last three years. It's a long, long drop to Penn Plaza, Steven. Think about it, and I won't blame you for not showing up.
