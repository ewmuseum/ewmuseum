666: The Story of Bryan Mayhem. Chapter III

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ? The year Bryan O’Neil entered preschool was the year that not only the O’Neils, but all of Belmont were dealing with a very special person. Special as in violently special. Yes Bryan was abnormally smart for a child his age, and his constant thirst for knowledge often initiated violent tantrums. Before we go on, you may have realized I just skipped about four years in little Bryan’s life. Well, I will not go and tell of every single day in this boy’s life because this book will be as long as the Oxford Dictionary. What is there to say about a little boy? He learned to walk, learned to talk, and was evening doing weird little calculations in his head despite having a mother that drank and hooted everything in site, and a father that would give beatings for wrong doings, and would entertain women nightly, women that were not Laure. Bryan kissed his mother goodbye, and she stumbled away as Bryan went to a seat that had a blue star and his name. The teacher and the teacher’s assistant looked at Laure, dressed in a nightgown, hair a mess, missing teeth, and smelling of booze and frowned. They looked at each and both made mental notes to keep a close eye on Bryan. “It’s been so long, but that is something I can never forget. This large boy coming in with his decrepit and decayed mother. He didn’t know better, he must have thought all mommy’s look like that. He kissed her and smiled. We thought she was going to be the issue, not him,” says Mrs. Dockler, Bryan’s preschool teacher. “He came in everyday is clothes that smelled of booze and urine, and they were always too small. I was teaching for about 5-6 years at that time, and I have never seen anything like this. What was I to do?” Could Dockler be one of the reasons why Bryan became so distant and later violent? Had she nipped this all in the bud in the beginning chances are things could have been a lot better. England at that time did have a Family Services that present day United States has now. They instead had Directional Parenting Committees. The DPC would never take children from their parents before having meetings and counseling with the parents. “I didn’t call the DPC because Bryan was very smart, just very quiet. He didn’t seem to notice or mind that he was totally different from the other children. He went on with his everyday activities. Sure the thought that he lived in that castle on the hill scared me a little, but who was I to tell people where to live. I kept my eye on him until that day when I realized that I was dealing with the devil.” Bryan made no friends. He talked to no one, and did not participate in class. He would daze off into some other world during story time. He made his t’s upside down. Most children his age write letters backwards and upside down because their brains are still learning formation techniques. That’s why every one in a while a child’s writing assignment would look something like a dyslexic’s nightmare. Bryan never made any mistakes with his lettering and numbers except for the T. No one said anything, and no one expected anything to be wrong except to continue working on this with Bryan. He would nod his head in understanding and continue to go back to making them upside. “There was an assignment I had the students do in class and I went to check everyone’s work. Bryan’s was spot on perfect so I gave him a sticker with a really big red star. He got mad at me and said his first words in months. He yelled that the star was the wrong way and demanded it be changed. I told him that no it’s right, and he threw a temper tantrum.” It’s said that Bryan stood up and began to destroy the room. The play kitchen was thrown about and his punched a hole in the lockers. The other kids watched in fear as the big scary kid finally snapped. “Mrs. Morgan my aide and myself made an attempt to control him, but his freakish strength for such a young child was amazing. We could not handle him. He would lash out at us and throw punches.” Kathy Morgan’s nose was broken. All this took place in front of 15 crying and screaming 4 year olds. Ben Archer the Vice Principal of the school heard the commotion and came down the hall and saw the destruction and blood. He ran in, and out of desperation and fear landed an open hand slap to the face of Bryan. Bryan stopped his tirade and looked straight into the face of Archer. “I don’t condone the physical discipline of young children, but you should have seem the chaos and blood. It was the last thing I could think of. However when I hit him and he stared me down it looked as if this child was possessed by something stronger than us.” Replies Archer. Archer ordered Dockler to get the other children out, call the O’Neil’s and call for help for Mrs. Morgan. Archer knew he had to be strong and show no fear, even though he was shitless. The thought that the child is only four ran through his brain and he wondered why he would be scared of such a little boy. He could not explain it, nor could he explain why he had his fists in the air ready for a full contact battle to the death. “He told me I was dead, and he would one day prove it to me. He than sat down and put his head down and started to ask for his mommy. Yes this child, after all this destruction and entropy asked for his mommy. It’s like he was possessed and the real person came back. I have never been so scared in all my life.” The school tried to call his parents for about an hour. It turns out that Laure was passed out drunk and Anton was entertaining three different whores. When he was done he finally decided to walk over and answer the phone. Ms. Hanrahan the school secretary had the honor(?) of calling his parents. “The father told me to just beat him until he passes out and he will be fine. I was flabbergasted at the very notion, and than informed him that it’s imperative that he comes to get his son. He cursed loudly than hung up. We weren’t sure if that meant he was coming or not, and we had no other contact person.” Archer retorted, “After that day we made it a school rule that all children have atleast three emergency contact numbers.” Bryan kept his head in the desk, and barely seemed to move. Around 11:30AM a disgusting filthy man came into the room. He was wearing urine stained boxers, food and who knows what else stained wife beater. He wore galoshes on the wrong feet was smoking a cigar. He walked in, said nothing to anyone, grabbed his son by his hair and landed a closed fist punch to the face of Bryan. Blood erupted everywhere, and Bryan’s eyes rolled back. “I was in awe, I can’t believe his father did that. But, to be honest, I am glad it was all over. Only two of us saw the incident. Myself and Mrs. Dockler. This man and this boy scared the hell out of us, and we wanted this all to end. We nodded at Mr. O’Neil as he grabbed his bloodied son and swung him over his shoulders and left. On his way out he said to us if we run to the law he will kill us and the boy.” At this point in my interview Ben Archer started to shake, and tears began to fall down his face. I asked him why they refused to tell the authorities, and he had no comment. He knew he was guilty of child abuse by not reporting it, and I think the very fact that this boy became a monster weighs heavy on his heart. He did say one thing to me before leaving the interview. “What does it matter anymore. He’s a world known millionaire. Let’s just let him have his life he loves and leave the boy alone.” I wasn’t floored by the word boy until I came back to my hotel and read over my notes. He called his him, when Bryan is now a middle aged grown man practically. I made an attempt to call Ben Archer and ask him the reasoning for the boy comment, but he never accepted or returned my calls. Why is he still calling Bryan a boy after thirty some years. I may be looking into it too deep though. For all I know, Bryan was still a boy to him. Ben Archer after all is nearing 70 years old. Bryan claims that he spent nearly a week in his bed after a few days of abuse at the hands of his father. Anton would go downstairs, have some drinks, come upstairs and whale on Laure for creating such a devilish misbehaved child, than come in the room and beat on Bryan until his hand was sore. Anton would pass out in front of the television with a beer, and an ice pack over his hand. This could very well be one of the reasons why Anton’s hand in his old age became decrepit and arthritic. Bryan never did return to preschool. As a matter of fact he never returned to school at all. The school board felt it was in Bryan’s as well as the faculty, staff, and other student’s best interest to be home schooled. Home schooling at the time was a radical concept. Today its so common that you never think twice when someone says they are home schooled. Bryan’s form of home schooling was different however. They had laws in England at this time stating that every child must attend either parochial, private, or government funded educational institutions. In order to abide by this Parliamentary Law the school had to figure out a way around it. “We decided that we would still keep Bryan enrolled, but not attending sessions during the week. His mother would come pick up the assignments and be his instructor than return all this assignments on Friday. Unfortunately, this only lasted a few weeks before Mrs. O’Neil stopped coming all together.” Hanrahan would later take over as Principal upon the dismissal of Ben Archer for atrocities to the school and country. Now those of you that know this Bryan “Mayhem” O’Neil character most likely already have realized that Bryan is far more intelligent than one that never spent a day in school. Bryan claims he was taught how to read, than after his mother stopped helping with his home studies he decided to educate himself. As you will read later on, Bryan spent a good majority of his teen years fighting, worshipping a dark lord, and reading. Hard to believe this is only the beginning of a cursed life.
