The Day the Dead Walked

31 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/31/11 Smoke filled the air, the bass bumped, lights flashed a plethora of colors. Saturday night at Lacy’s was the busiest night of the week. Married men and single men alike filled the room to live a sexual dream that will only succumb to a wet spot in their pants. The patrons range from rich executives trying to buy themselves a blowjob to the absolute gutter slime of the streets. The main stage was filled with the two headliners simulating lesbian sex while a group of men were whistling and hooting at them, throwing money on the stage like it was confetti at a birthday party. There was the champagne room where only the richest can afford. Rumor has it there is a lot more going on back there than a simple lap dance. Everyone notices the giant man walking in the front door. The bouncers look at him funny and talk to each other. The dancers and patrons alike all look in his general direction. He sets off an internal alarm that something is not right. He does not look like anyone that’s ever been in here before. Most of Lacy’s are “regulars” every once in a while with a few stragglers making their way in for a quick fix. This man was enormous. He had black stringy dreadlocks that went to the middle of his back. He was wearing a long black trench coat with a black shirt under and black jeans. This man was the epitome of depressed Goths everywhere. He slowly walks in, heads towards the bar, orders a beer and finds a table to sit(uncomfortably) at. The crowd around him starts to go back to their normal routine, still peering at him out of the corners of their eyes, waiting for something to happen. He goes into his back pocket and pulls out a money clip atleast an inch thick and throws it on the daily. The girls smile, the guests smile, and all is well back at Lacy’s again. Bunny, a tall lanky, very thin dancer made her way towards this giant. She looks at the money clip than sits down next to this beast of a man. She puts her left leg over his and starts to rub his shoulder. “I have never seen you around here sweetie. You must be new.” The giant lets out a smirk and simply just nods his head in approval. Bunny stands up, blows him a kiss and moves the table from him. She opens his legs and gets in between them. She starts to move her hips from side to side. As she gets more into her routine, the giant moves his head back. He closes his eyes, let’s out a shutter that causes Bunny to smile. Destiny and Ginger are sitting down in between sets having a drink. They are watching Bunny with the new guy and shaking their heads. Destiny leans over and talks into Ginger’s ear. “ Anyone with money she’s on. She does have that crack issue to deal with.” Ginger starts to laugh almost spitting up her drink. “Those are just rumors.” “You sure about that? She probably weighs what 95 pounds?” Ginger giggles again. They both hate Bunny because for some reason men flock to her. It may be the long legs, or the blonde hair, but all the dancers just wish the world would know what Bunny is truly about. “She’s going for her finish Ginger.” Both girls watch as Bunny straddles the giant and begins a sex simulation. She has made men finish more than once so Bunny jokes that it’s her finishing move. What happens next seemed like a dream to those that saw it. Erupting from Bunny’s lower back was a blade and a bloodied hand. She yelps in pain, falls over and dies. The giant pulls the blade and his hand from the hole in her stomach and slides her off. The place goes crazy. Girls screaming and running as the giant turns around to catch a bouncer in the throat with the knife. The bouncer’s throat is ripped open and blood pours from the opening. The bartender jumps over the bar and jumps on the giant’s back. This giant uses his left arm, reaches over his shoulder, grabs the bartender by his shirt and rips him over his shoulder onto the hard ground. He plunges the blade repeatedy into the bartender splattering the tender’s blood all over his face. A dancer rushes by this giant in an attempt to escape but he quickly snags her by her hair, brings her close to his body, grabs her head and twists it so violently that her head does an entire 180 degree turn. He turns just in time to catch a swinging bat. He drops his blade, grabs into the bat with his other hand and rips it from the bouncer. The bouncer puts his hands up in a futile attempt at surrender, but is met with a crushing blow to his face. His faceplate is shattered, nose crushed, and his teeth fall from his mouth as fragments of his skull go into his brain killing. Destiny and Ginger, the first two to witness the bloodshed, crawled under the table they were sitting at. Both are crying heavy sobs, mewling like an injured animal. They hold onto each other for support. They both see the giant boot stop right in front of their faces. Both girls instinctively try to crawl away until the table is picked up and tossed aside violently. The giant man grabs both girls by their hair lifts them up and in one quick powerful motion collides both of their skulls against each other. He drops their bodies to the floor. What once made them all their money, has now taken their life. Their faces were crushed so bad the coroner ID’d them by their remaining teeth fragments. The giant scoped the room looking for any other victims, but everyone has left the place to seek cover. The front door comes blasting open again as law enforcement came in guns blazing putting well over 20 bullets into the chest and stomach of this giant. He dropped to the ground, surrounded by the blood, gore, and carnage he caused. Lt. Inks was the first to check out the giant. His gun was still poised, his eyes not leaving this gian at all. Inks bent down, put his fingers on the giants neck and felt for the pulse. There was nothing. He waved over the remaining officers and asked for an ambulance on his radio. 9 years on the force, he has never once seen anything so sadistic and brutal in all his life. The motive was never determined. A year later Lt. Inks sits at his desk and still looks over that report. He has seen some of the worst slime on earth, but he has never ever dealt with what he did that day. The look of that giant, who was later ID’d as Bryan O’Neil, still haunts him to this very day. Inks has often woken up in the middle of the night in a sweat. O’Neil would often chase him through the woods, or dark alleys, most of the time getting within a fingertip from him until he wakes up, unable to fall asleep again. “Hey Inks, we gotta call man. Some grave robber or some shit. You might be interested in this.” He exhales, runs his hands through his hair, grabs his gun, coat and stands up, putting the file back under the desktop calendar. “Grave robber? What did they take?” Inks partner Wilson was always excited about getting out of the office. Being a detective wasn’t actually a good time to him. Sure the pay was great but there’s no action. Wilson was a short stout man with thinning red hair. His moustache and goatee had signs of wear and tear. He was halfway out the door before he heard what Inks said. “The body.” Inks stood there in shock. He wasn’t sure if the shock was from the body being stolen, or the matter of fact way his partner said it. Police work can do things to you. Makes you numb to some of the most vile acts the world has ever seen. Inks and Wilson barely spoke in the car on the way there. Inks had a plethora of thoughts racing through his head, and Wilson get excited to be out of the office. Wilson is in his 40’s but the way he acts towards cases and what not one would think he’s a teenager. As they pull up to the morgue, Inks grabs Wilson’s arm before he gets out of the car. “You said I would be interested in this case. Why would you say that?” Wilson sits back and looks over at his partner. “You remember the massacre at that strip club last year? That sick freak of a man that went in and nearly killed everyone in the building.” How could he forget. Inks instead jus nods. “The body missing is Bryan O’Neil.” With that Wilson climbed out of the car, shut the door, and walked towards the front door of the masoleum. Inks sat in the car and collected his thoughts. The only thing that kept running through his head is there’s something strange about this that all this comes back one year after that strip club massacre. Inks gets out of the car and let’s the cold air hit his face. He is feeling flush, as if he’s coming down with something. His hairline begins to hurt, his eyes are heavy, and there’s a gurgling going on in his stomach. He reminds himself to take something once he gets back to the office. When he steps inside he shows his badge and is immediately let to the back where all the embalming of the dead is done. The room is spotless and shiny. One would never figure a room of this type would be as clean, well because after all they are already dead. This is city morgue where John Doe’s and prisoners that have just rode the lightning go when they are perished. Wilson walks up to Dr. Wilkins and shakes his hand. Inks follows suit looking over Wilkins with a fine tooth comb. Wedding ring, silver hair, glasses, some wrinkles. Man is obviously a happy family man but is also scarred by the likes of his line of work. “Dr Wilkins, I am Detective Wilson this is my partner Lt Inks.” “Thank you for coming so fast gentleman.” “Not a problem. What exactly happened?” Right before Wilkins goes into his story Wilson pulls out his little black notebook and licks the tip of his pen, an awful habit that is gross and annoying. “Well got here at 5am like I always do, walked around doing my normal routine. Today is the 15th so we go outside and remove all flowers and gifts for the decease and when I got near the back with the John Doe’s and No Kins I noticed a disruption..” Inks retorts, “What kind of disruption?” “Well the grass was not rolled back to its fitting place and there was dirt tossed aside wildly. I immediately ran inside and called the police.” “Ok go on.” “I got a hold of the judge and asked for a permit to exhume the grave. Regardless of the situation I need approval..” “We are aware doc.” Wilkins obviously still flustered about what has happened rolls his eyes and continues. “I waited maybe an hour before I got the approval and we started the exhuming process.” “What does that consist of doc?” Inks starts to walk around the room taking note of everything around. As Dr. Wilkins explains to Wilson what is done while exhuming a body. Ink is positive that there is something missing and that the doctor is hiding something, something very important. “….so that’s when we pulled up the coffin, extracted any remaining air from the ducts and opened the lid. The body of Bryan O’Neil was missing…” Inks yells back, “Missing? How does a 400 pound man that’s about 7 feet tall get misplaced?” Wilkins looks back at Inks, shakes his head, “Lieutenant, did I ever say anything about misplaced?” Wilson puts his fingers to his lips telling Inks to quiet down. “Sorry he’s just getting over the flu, he’s not feeling to well. Anything else we should know about?” Wilkins put his hand to his forehead, than covers his mouth. “Uh…well yes there is.” Inks and Wilson both just stare at Wilkins, waiting for a reply. “When we opened the coffin, my assistant was inside. He was brutally beaten…….” Wilson and Inks are sitting in the car eating chili dogs and drinking Coke. Wilson is flipping through the pages of his notes he took from the visit today. Inks is staring out the window trying to piece everything together. “Ok Inks, let’s see what we got. The Unsub breaks into the morgue. But for what? Anyways he kills the overnight assistant in a pretty brutal fashion, so he must be relatively strong, maybe a former athlete. But why does he dig up O’Neil? Do you think there’s a connection?” Inks stared out the window fearing the worst, hoping for the best. “What gets me is why remove the body of O’Neil, replace it with the assistant, than go through all the work of trying to cover it. Doesn’t make any sense.” Inks, still looking outside, “Maybe no one dug up O’Neil. Maybe no one stole the body.” “What are you trying to say?” “I don’t know man. All we know there is someone violent out there, and we have nothing but an dug up grave and a missing body. I just don’t know.” Inks thought to himself, ‘this is going to be the longest day of my life.’ How prophetic….
