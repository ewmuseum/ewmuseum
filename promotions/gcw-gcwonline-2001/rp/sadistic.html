Date: Timeless -=Mayhem has never been in such a place before. Here he stands, inches from the pulpit in the house of God. The very God he was raised to hate. He's not here seeking the help of the Lord, he'd never stoop that far. He's here to think, and vent.=- Mayhem:'Just a few feet from me a man of god stands above his sheep and tells them that this invisible man loves them, and that if they do what they are told by a book, a mere work of fiction, they will die and ascend to this place where they live eternally. However, if they act against this god they will descend into the depths of hell and burn at the hand of Satan.' -=Painted face and all Mayhem walks across the altar and sits in the chair of the priest. People praying quietly sit in the pews and look at Mayhem disturbingly.=- Mayhem:'So maybe I should act as the priestand tell the head sheep, a man who calls himself Enygmatic, and simply goes by the moniker of X, that what he's doing is a sin in the House of Mayhem. Written in the Chronicles, there is a commandment, the first one to be exact, that states, 'Thou shalt not piss Mayhem off'' -=Elderly women paying their respects to god gasp at the Blaspheme vomited out by the mad man in the face paint.=- Mayhem:'That's twice you've interfered with me, and twice you've cost me. Now it's time for us to finally meet. This Sunday, yes the Sabbath oddly enough, me and you, UFC rules. This match does not favor you at all. Techincal wrestler you may be, but you can't make a man submit, when that man enjoys pain. I'm not going to put you in armbars, and sharpshooters. No X, I'm going to knock you out, and end your sinful ways. I bleed the blood of the dead X, and you'll be the very first one to experience my anger and wrath. The last person to interfere the way you did was a man by the name of Jason O'Neil, the Dark Messiah. Yes my very own brother, and yes I fed on his black mucus infested soul. X, you are trying to make an impact, an impact it may be, but an impact of negative repercussions. You'll be known as the man that smeared the mat red with his own blood. Lestatt Knight and Michael Stevens will be wrestling for the title later on that night in a pool of your blood. A blood I yearn to taste, and take from you. The world thought I was sick and twisted before, the worst has just begun. Welcome to Oblivion X.' -=Mayhem stands up, stares at the altar and closes his eyes. When they open the white cloth draped over the altar sets ablaze, causing alarm and panic amongst the worshippers.=- Mayhem:'And better yet, Welcome to Hell.'
