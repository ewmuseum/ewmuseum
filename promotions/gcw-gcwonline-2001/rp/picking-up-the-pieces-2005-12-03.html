Picking up the pieces...

3 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 Dec 05 They say that all good things must come to an end. Why? They say that change is inevitable? Why? GCW NC-17 has come and gone and as advertised, the very face of the company had been altered. It was a night where good triumphed over evil, light withstood the darkness. The fans all around the world got to stand and cheer their beloved heroes and while the so called “bad guys” were left to sit and wonder “what next?” Well what the fuck is next? I lost The words had yet to really sink in yet. I never imagined that it would come to this, never in a million years. I was on top and what’s more, it didn’t appear as if things were ready to change. LeStatt Knight was on his way to putting one dog out of his misery for the very last time. Michael Stevens could not withstand the beating; it was like every other time we had met in that ring. While the actual journey was different each time, the ending always seemed to be the same. I can only imagine it being like some kind of sick maze that you just keep hitting dead ends at. Then it happened. The mouse found the end of the maze and he conquered his greatest challenge. So here I am alone and trying to put it all back into perspective. The moment I heard that hand come crashing down to the mat for the third time it was almost as if my life had been shattered into pieces with a sledgehammer. I tried to kick out once again but my body was far from cooperative. In that moment my entire run as United States and World Champion flashed before my very eyes. Every victory from Mike Hardy and Natural Born Killer in the tournament for the US strap to Eva VanLorne and even my loss to Trey for the US Title. Dangerous Games came to mind when I scared Jason Dread enough to quit and I became World Champion. The smell of Bryan Mayhem’s flesh after I slammed a wall of fire in his face seemed fresh in my nostrils, the victories against Michael Stevens, watching Devil’s Night from up top and even the fear that shot through my body as Stevens and I played Russian Roulette. I could hear the voices calling me the most dominate champion ever, the praise and the fear all rolled up in everyone’s voice who ever mentioned my name. Only one thought came to my mind when it was all done. Will anyone care? Now that I have been exposed and the title belongs to another will anyone remember what LeStatt Knight did? Will I simply get my name in a record book for being the champion just as others like John Reed, Tristain Mayhem and Shane Lane? Or will the world look back at what I have accomplished and marvel at the greatness of it all? Will they be able to look past all of the hatred they feel for me and realize that there was NO ONE else who could have carried the torch higher and farther than I did for these past ten months. Not Jason Dread Not Octavian Not Michael Stevens Not The Jhub Not Johnny Borealis Not Eva VanLorne NO ONE. I was it and Steven Caldera understood that but for some reason he also seemed to fear it. Deep down I think he feared the day I became champion because of what I would do. Destroy everyone I did just that too and with great execution might I add. I realize what my problem was though, as a matter of fact it is painfully obvious now. I did the one thing that I have always scoffed at, I believed my own hype. I let the commentators fill my head with talk of how unbeatable I was and it eventually led to my downfall. A broken arm and four Inferno DDT’s later was when I realized this. By then it was a case of too little too late. The pieces of my life could have been found all around that arena, some pieces probably will never be recovered. I sat in my hotel room for hours just staring out the window. I did not wish to see or talk to anyone, including Mary. I had to sort out what the hell had to happen next. I had quite the decision to make. You see GCW has been on quite a trend since its reopening. Now there have been three GCW World Champions but that’s not the trend. First Jason Dread embarrassed Octavian. Octavian disappeared. Then I destroyed Jason Dread Dread disappeared. Michael Stevens broke LeStatt Knight. Does Knight disappear? Part of me wants to that is for sure. Part of me is so disgusted and broken that I would rather hide forever than to face a crowd that will point and laugh. God help me if I run into Michael Stevens backstage or something to that sort. Part of me thinks that maybe it is time to end this thing once and for all. Pick Mary up and ride into the sunset and live our fairy tale ending and the hell with wrestling. Part of me thinks that maybe my mind would not be haunted by all of the images of my past if I could just get the anger out of my life, anger that begins and ends with GCW. Luckily that part of me is the weaker part. The other side of me cannot wait to climb back into that ring, which is already a signed and sealed deal. December 8th I will begin to piece it all back together. No I have no desire to go for Michael Stevens head already, there is no need to do so just yet. To fully piece back the shattered pieces I must rebuild from the ground up. It will be almost as if this is the relaunch of GCW once more. I must prove to myself that I can climb back through the entire roster from the most ridiculous imbecile to the most highly respected member. I will not decline nor run from any challenge be it from Rich Rollins or Chrissy-Poo. No one is safe. I will not run and hide like the cowards from the past, it is not in me to hide. I know that I am beatable now and obviously there is nothing super human about any of us. I will fight with a brand new passion and FIRE. That fire burns for one reason and one reason only. To be the best. Michael Stevens will have his fun. He will prance around and perform for the people, they will cheer, stomp and cry. Sadly in the end it will all be for nothing. Soon they will all realize that the title doesn’t belong around his waist ? it is MINE. I will rip through this roster once and for all until I have proven once more that I deserve my shot at that top spot. No more taking opponents for granted No more underestimation No more ego Just business. It begins with the perfect babyface. A man that much like me had a streak ruined at NC-17, even if it was hardly what mine was. This man gets cheered by the fans, respected by those in the back, and loved by the people who run this ship. He is the epitome of everything I hate in this profession of ours. He is Seymour Almasy. December 8th 2005 marks a day that few will forget, especially Almasy. I have heard his claims in the past about needing just one shot to take my world title. His arrogance makes this all the more fun. I know of all his accomplishments, PTC this and PTC that ? what the fuck ever. This is GCW and when it comes to GCW the ball is in MY court. My long road back to the top starts immediately. Fuck Michael Stevens Fuck the fans Fuck Steven Caldera And most of all, coming into World Wide Fuck Seymour Almasy When one chapter ends? A new begins.
