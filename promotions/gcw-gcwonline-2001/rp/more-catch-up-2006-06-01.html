More Catch Up

1 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/06 ~A Little Over a Week Ago~ ~Off the road in South Carolina~ Josh's head pounded with all the emotions going on. Ukyo, Gray, Blaise... stress. Distress at that. Mostly coming from Blaise right now as he carried Dani back to the car over his shoulders. Gray moved to intercept him and help load Dani back into her Highlander. ?What's going on?? ?Someone was out there. Wings, White hair, Dani knew his name. Whatever the hell is going on, we've been found out. Let's get moving and fast. Closer we get to a town or city, the less likely they are to do anything major.? Gray nodded and helped him load Dani into the car. Josh climbed into the back from the other side and helped make it a bit easier. He buckled up and decided to stay back there with her. Gray took one of Dani's traveling blankets and wrapped it around the now windowless door and then slammed it shut. He then climbed into the drivers seat and got the truck moving again, Basie's Avalanche close behind it. Josh looked down at Dani, sleeping now, heavy. Her body showing the cuts from the window and from running blindly through the woods. In her mind was something that Josh could just make out. A name. A face. A mystery at this time. There were things that would need to be answered, and soon. ~A few days later~ Josh had helped them get Dani cleaned up, and was playing babysitter. She would be stepping into the ring to become the new GCW TV Champion in a few days. He needed to keep her comfortable until then. Her body healed quickly, and not many knew about the incident out in the woods. He'd already taken her truck to a dealer and got the window fixed with a crappy story about some boys throwing rocks around. Everyone else was feeling the stress though. Blaise had tried to interrogate her about this Adam and everything else. But she wasn't speaking to him. Unknown to her though, Josh was able to get some information out of her while she slept in a deep sleep next to him. It was a mean trick, but Josh knew this was going to get them all in a world of shit, and he needed to move to help them out. Which is why he'd moved to do something else too, and now he was going to take that to Blaise and try to help her out. Right now it didn't matter if she wanted to be helped or not, she needed it. **knock, knock** Josh knocked on the door to the room that Blaise and Ukyo were using. He could hear the muted sounds of Blaise's guitar playing in the distance. More then likely he had his amplifier set really low, but the musical sound still traveled. ?Yeah... I'm here, come on in.? Josh opened the door and saw exactly what he was expecting. There sat Blaise, wearing only a pair of jeans and his moccasin boots. Slung across his chest was his stratocaster guitar, hooked to a small portable amplifier. The headphones were laying to the side, and he was just tooling around with it all. ?Josh, what can I help you with?? Blaise moved to put the guitar down into it's bracket to hold it for him. ?It's not that you can help me, I'm going to help you.? ?Really?? Josh sat down in the chair across from him, pulling it about three feet as he did so. He then pulled his drawing tablet out from under his arm and started flipping through the pages. ?The other night, when Dani had that attack. I could sense something.? ?Yeah, you're an...? ?No, not just her emotions. More then that. It was if I saw a snapshot of what we going on in her head. In the dream she had. I saw something...? Blaise nodded as Josh turned the drawing tablet around. On the paper, drawn in a fine pencil, was a portrait of a young Native American Boy. His facial features resembled that of Dani quite a bit, but there were definitive ?Meet Adam Chaska Northsky. His middle name means 'First Born Son'. Very common for the Lakhota to use it.? Blaise reached out and took the tablet from him. ?So, this is Dani's older brother? Do we know this for sure?? Josh handed him some more stuff. One of them a computer printed copy of a newspaper clipping from July 5th, 1985 for the Todd County Tribune out of Mission, South Dakota. The closest town to the Rosebud Reservation that Dani was born on. The story detailed how a 'hate crime' had been committed over the weekend. Coroners put the deaths of Daniel, Lynn and Adam Northsky as happening late evening on the 3rd. They had all been shot once to the head and then hung from a tree with anti-native slogans drawn on their bodies in some sort of marker. ?They tried to cover up what it was, and who had done it. Pinned it on the local KKK and white supremacists groups.? ?Yup, but it was apparently this white haired angel, and our old buddy Jaymes. But look at the listing. Their daughter was not found at the scene. There is a small newspaper article that made it a few years later, she was eight, giving Dani's name as playing on the orphanage schools soccer team. But nothing else. Then a few years after that, her grandpa finally came and picked her up.? Blaise nodded. ?She doesn't want to really talk about it. But I've cheated a bit on getting the information. She was very young. Don't know how she recalled it so perfectly. But apparently the truck that crashed into her parents car was driver-less. Just let roll down the hill with no lights on. They then pulled her parents and Adam out, and did the deed to them. It's pretty rough, but she remembered them saying the baby wouldn't last long anyway. Then while they were finishing, something pulled her out of the other side of the car and took her away.? Blaise nodded again as he read more of the paper clippings Josh had found. ?They thought Adam was the Angel to be born.? ?They both might have been. Both from this magical seventh generation. We could have had two angels, but they got one.? Blaise had an impressed look on his face. ?That's true. But they were thinking it was only this six year old boy as the first born. But Kokabel knew differently. I'd lay odds that many times great grandfather was the savior that night. And then he dropped her off at that Orphanage a hundred miles away. After a while, no one cared about some little Indian girl. And these guys got arrogant.? Josh nodded as he turned a page in his drawing book. It was one more thing that Dani had dancing around in her head. The angelic symbol for one Suriel. ?And can you tell me what this is?? Blaise smiled. ?That is something she drew in her journal that night too. And the reason why this all happened.? ?What?? ?That is the symbol for an archangel who is no more. Name of Suriel. Currently he's a spirit embedded in the body and head of one Ian Childress.? Josh's jaw dropped. ?The Wrath?? ?Yeah, that's what his name means. The Wrath of God. The angel of guidance and punishment of other angels who strayed. He's the one who called Dani's manifestation forth in the lodge. I don't doubt Dani's memories, even though she was very young. What gets me is the timing. I think Suriel called her memory forth because of who was following us.? ?What's up with white hair?? Blaise got up and headed over to the stack of books they had recently been given. He opened one up to a purple book mark, more of a Post-It note that was jammed in between some pages. He put it down onto the table next to Josh. ?Don't say any names. But ?Bearded Man? is close to what it means. He's one of the Evening Star's assistants. A treasure hunter who can find any hidden treasure, specially those hidden by enchantments of magicians. He's also able to speak with animals and a few other things.? Josh starts reading the book entry. ?And this is who you saw?? Blaise nodded to him. ?She said his name loud and clear. I think her total recall of that night was to let us know who's following, and how close they were. Not only that, but a total recall of that night in particular, will have her pissed. The Sioux tradition of revenge is pretty intense.? Josh shuddered. ?Thanks for your research though. This means that some of the questions we had are answered without disturbing her.? Josh nodded and got up to walk out. As he reached the door, he turned around. Blaise just looked up. ?Yes?? ?We're going to be able to take these guys on and keep Dani safe, right?? Blaise just looked up at him. ?I'm not a soothsayer, Josh. Never have been. But I can tell you this much. These renegades, they'll have to kill me to get to her. I promise you that. And I've killed one of their own before. So I know what I'm doing.? Blaise looked over at the sword that sat, wrapped up in a silk cloth, on the table. Josh smiled and nodded in his direction as he walked through the door. ~A little more recent~ ~The City of the Show~ ~Hotel Parking Lot~ The van was a big one, moving van quality, a twenty-four-footer. It read that it was for some moving company on the side. But the play on words, Avalon Moving, showed that it wasn't a real moving company and was linked to some fantasy name that the Order would have registered it under. Gray stood looking at it as the driver stepped out. He was a small man, wearing a uniform for the supposed moving company. He didn't hang around though. He moved towards the large black luxury car that sat there and handed the keys to the pale skinned, raven haired lady that stood there. ?Thank ye.? He climbed into the back of the luxury car and she started to slink her way over to where Gray and his new 'assistant', Alexis Day, stood. ?So, what's this you called me out here for, Annie?? Yes, their benefactor, Aine ?Annie? Leanan. She was the person who had brought them their truck to use. But what was this all about. When she had called, she had only asked about Gray and Alexis. It was strange, because Alexis wasn't even part of the Order. She was working with him on a situation dealing with the Vampire Lia ?Shadow? Vlassy, nothing based on the Order. But, Gray knew that Annie knew about that also. She walked up and held the keys out to him. ?These are yers. All I need ye t' do is sign this piece of paper.? ?What am I signing?? Annie smiled. ?Well the researchers are gettin' tired o' ye sendin' us stuff t' test fer ye. Yer a field scientist fer us. So time fer ye t' be able t' do work in the field.? Annie smiled at both of them, taking in the look on Alexis' face as she then walked over and opened up the side door on the truck. Inside was a veritable mobile laboratory. ?Holy...? Alexis couldn't hold it back. She'd not seen anything like this in years. And then it was in a classroom. She stepped up short stint of stairs that folded out and walked in. There were some storage bins, that were designed to hold living things if need be. The largest was about the size to hold a person if it needed to. A couple of tables, one big enough to serve for dissections of large creatures. The others were smaller and around the edges. Equipment for taking DNA snapshots, centrifuges, everything... it was there. ?Where did all of this come from?? ?Corporate environments owned by the Order of the TaleSmith companies. It's mobile, hooked up to our satellite network, and has most of the equipment for very common things you should encounter. You can do the work I've sanctioned Gray to help with, and he can keep up with our work and our subject.? ?Well, I've got the blood samples from Lia and Virgil in my bags still. I can get them in here and start working on them. All I'll need is some work with some un-tainted vampire samples and I should be good to go. Thank you.? Annie smiled at her. ?As I said, it's on the network. We have quite a few things from dealings with vampires throughout the years that may already be of use to you there. On the clipboard is an envelope. I've given you field scientist access to the network for the time being. You are working under a warrant by me. Gray is your host, he has final say on things. I'm putting the lab in his name because he is a member of my research team.? Alexis was still looking around, like a child in a candy store. ?I understand. And while I'm working here, I'll try my best to also help Gray and his work. I know he's been sending in samples collected from the Northsky girl over the last week or so. I take it you want to start doing those locally, and not send them in.? Annie smiled at the girl. She was smart, nothing got by her. She could see why the Den had taken her in so long ago. But didn't quite know why they had turned on her, she would be hoping that either she or Lia would be willing to fill her in sometime. Alexis turned around and looked at both Gray and Annie. ?So, no crap. Why are you doing this?? Annie smiled wide at her with her perfect white teeth. ?Alexis, dear. I keep a promise to people. One day, Lia will owe me, and I see 'er as a person o' 'er word. I'm an Unseelie Faery. She knows this, she knows tha' I will call the dept. Don'na know on what, but if I call it... she'll pay be back. Then again, maybe she already paid me fer th' debt. Having a former member of the Den 'ere, is quite a bit fer me. If you're willing to give some infermation.? Alexis' face sunk a bit at that. ?I've no problems with that, except for what they'll do if they ever found out.? Annie smiled even wider to try to comfort her. ?Ms. Day, if ye were t' ever 'ave any issues fer wha' ye do fer us? Yer Den would find itself bearin' th' same issues as the other groups tha' 'ave tried to stand up to us. I meself would see to it tha' th' offenders would be meetin' our 'unters. An' in th' meantime, ye do know abou' some o' our units we keep riogh'?? Alexis nodded. ?Yes, I've heard stories of both the hunters and the guardians. I've made out the tattoos on both Ukyo's and Blaise's wrists. They're both hunters.? Annie nodded. ?And yer sharin' a lab with a guardian. Now, I'll let ye start getting' used t' the lab. Gray, can I speak t' ye really quick?? Gray nodded and walked over towards the car with her. ?What do you need?? ?Jus' a question answered. She's a sweet girl an' all. Bu' can I ask wha' brought 'er 'ere?? Gray answered pretty quickly. ?She came down with Lia and Virgil when we gave her the information on Laszlo. She ran pretty quickly. She had brought Alexis along. She explained Alexis' quest for her, and we offered to take her while Lia ran. Why?? ?Nothin'. Bu' doesn' Lia ride a motorcycle?? ?Yeah.? ?So wha' did this girl bring with 'er?? ?A backpack with a couple days worth of clothes. She's called back up to Dawn's estate a few times, but hasn't really asked for anything to be sent down from what I've seen? Why?? ?Comfort. She's goin' t' be a boon t' us while she's with us. I want ye t' know. I'm pullin' ye off yer guardianship o' Dani. She's ga' Josh all t' willin' t' take that spot.? ?Josh isn't ready...? ?Yer na' 'is trainer. Th' actions th' other nigh' show tha' we're abou' t' get int' a fokin' war. Yer assigned t' Alexis now. I want 'er kept safe. I've spoken t' Lia an' I promised 'er tha' much at least. Even if it's just show duty fer a month or so, yer it.? Gray didn't look too happy with that, but he gave the only answer he could. ?Yes, Ma'am.? With that he turned and walked towards the mobile, lab, leaving Annie standing there. ~A Week Later~ ~In The Lab~ Gray walked in and looked over at the woman with the striking mixture of both dyed fire engine red and naturally blond hair. She was looking over a bunch of printouts as Gray was running a few things with the bandages from Dani's night a little over a week ago. ?What do you have?? ?Other then a headache in knowing that the Order really needs to stop using Spirit Knives on things it's studying.? ?Oh, really?? ?Yeah, oh, by the way. I need to draw some blood from you for a human baseline. I've got some of my own, but I need two. I need to isolate what is human and Vampire to help break down what could be doing this.? Gray double took for a moment, then gave his answer to that request. ?If you're wanting a human baseline, I'm not the one to give it.? ?Oh? I'm sorry. I didn't know. What about the stuff you're doing with Ms. Northsky's stuff.? ?Heh...? He pulled out a piece of 'cell' that looked similar to an x-ray, except it had lines across it from a DNA snapshot. ?Well, she's not a good pick either. See, here is her in her normal state.? He slid the cell up onto the light board. ?That looks pretty normal.? ?To a point. But this is her when she's not feeling quite human.? He put the other one up there and the number of bars on it, denoting different genetic code, tripled at least. ?What is that?? ?That is the most perfect DNA ever done like this. That is the DNA of an Angel, a Virtue to be exact.? Alexis shook her head. ?A what?? ?I'll explain it later. But she's a good pick. Not to mention, you try to get a needle near her, and she'll break your jaw. If it's not got ink going into her skin, and it's taking things out, she won't have it. My brother, Josh is the most normal of the bunch.? Alexis raised an eyebrow at that remark. He chuckled. ?Yeah, I said it okay?? ?Nothing special about him?? ?He's an empath with a showing of possible telepathy. Other then that, the only special he's ever had was riding that short bus to school.? She laughed. ?Now, what's your issues here?? She produced similar things, the first two were labeled as ?Lia Vlassy? and ?Virgil Ambrosi?. ?This is Lia and Virgil. As you can see, different types of Vampires. But, there are some things similar that makes them vampires. They have the regular human DNA pairs, but then there is more added.? Gray nodded. ?But the Order has all of these recorded ones over the last decade or so. Take a look at this.? She fans out the ones she's printed on the high resolution laser printer in the lab. Gray looks them over. ?Those spots. They're common on each one. So those are common vampire genes?? ?No, they're not. Look at Lia and Virgil's.? He did so and then nodded. ?So ghosts on the printouts?? ?No, I printed other things too. And they're not on there. But they're on all of these genetic profiles. I can only find one common thing.? ?What's that?? ?All of these guys were dispatched by hunters. Hunters usually use those spirit knife things that Blaise uses. Nothing else would be the same on these guys. States apart, sometimes countries. Different clans, they should be almost fully different.? ?Got ya. So, that's hokey. So what do we need?? She sighed as she just put everything down on the table. ?Well, I need some untainted vampire blood. Preferably from the same clans their from.? ?Well, guess we'll make plans to go dispatch a few vampires. In the meantime, I need to get some stuff done.? Gray walks over and starts to mess with a set of slides. It looks like he's extracted some of the blood from Dani's bandages that are being kept in a refrigerator. He takes a small eye dropper and pulls a bit of a solution out of a small jar. He drops a few drops and then puts the other slide down on top. He repeats this two more times. Alexis watched him as he almost fumbled with it all. He then picked up a single slide sandwich and tried to put it into the microscope. At least he did until he cracked it right in half. ?DAMNIT! That's the third time. I figured a short break would stop that!? He was bleeding from the cut he got on his fingers, and he moved to get a paper towel to blot it. Alexis stepped up. She'd seen him do it twice before. For some reason his larger fingers were really getting in the way. ?Here, let me help you with that. Some of this equipment looks like it was made for midgets.? As he put the pressure on the cut, Alexis moved the second slide under the microscope's lens. As she did, she found herself taking a deep breathe of the air about him. Cologne... Drakar Noir. She'd gotten used to it lingering over the last week. Gray tosses the paper towel into the waste basket and starts to look through the lens. Alexis takes a look towards his hand and notices that his cut is already closing up. Just as she's going to say something, the door on the side opens. ?Hey!? It's Josh, his brother. Alexis moves back to her side and sits down. ?Mail call! It's for you from Annie.? He hands Gray an envelope. It's labeled that it's for his eyes only. ?Thanks, I'll take a look outside. In the meantime, Alexis wants to poke you with with a needle in your arm. Something about needing another sample of a pure human.? Josh looked at her nervously. ?Hey, little bro. You've got ink. Can't tell me you're a wimp and all that. Give the lady some blood.? Gray got up and put his hand on his brother's shoulder. He forced him to sit in the chair he'd been sitting in. He got up and walked out of the trailer and then stood there as he opened the letter. But inside of it was nothing but a letter, and one of the Order's, well TaleSmith's corporate credit cards with his name on it. On the letter was just a few lines of writing, the last one of which was: ?It's been a week, get that girl to a store and get her some clothes. Make sure she's comfortable.? He sighed, and looked back up to the truck's door. To Be Continued
