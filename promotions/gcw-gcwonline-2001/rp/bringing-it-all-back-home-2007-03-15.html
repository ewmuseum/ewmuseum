Bringing It All Back Home

15 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/07 I think we all have gods. I know mine personally, or I think mine personally, or whatever. Reality isn't hard. Reality's exactly like I see it, and exactly like anyone else sees it. Deviations that cannot coexist do, have to. I find it hard to communicate things in the spiderweb way I understand them. I jut off at intersections; I take bad angles and lose the original thread over a silk horizon. But I do know my gods, the one that made me and the ones that watched. I know that I exist in a very tenuous fiction, and I know that people I know might not. Reconciling paradoxes, again. Nothin' special. Just the forwards motion of existence doin' it's thing: existing. I know that I don't breathe as deeply when I wake up anymore. I guess that means something. I know I fixate on shit. And I know that drugs are bad for me, but I just don't know it. Y'know? I'm thinking about god again, not some capital G affair’an all caps one if anything. GOD. The rationalization of an abstract concept. The subversion of something clean. Whatever. Not God like "sentient being in control of universe from Airship Heaven," not god like "the person that writes me," (and I only believe in the one I've met, and I'm not sure I believe I met him): GOD like an operating system for the universe and everything in it, like some fundamental set of laws that are only beyond comprehension because of a slightly less fundamental restriction on memory capacity. Nothin' fancy, I'm just co-opting someone else's word because it make sense. See: soul used as a measure of humanity and not spirituality. Okay, maybe we can't dig the rules because we're too smart for them. We outthink simple things and we still die slower than we reproduce, so maybe it's a good thing that we're not idiot apes perfectly in tune with nature. It's just, I don't know. It's unattainable. It's intangible. We don't get it; big motherfuckin' period. We get little flashes that feel like something that Big and they drive us insane. We hold onto crazy theories like this because there's a lot of us that wanna think there's something Big. Whatever. If I'm not real, it can't be’but it can be just as real as me, and that scares me. See: 13 and 14, or whatever they're wearing today. But I do think we've all got something like that, and I think all that shit has something to it. No, shit's not breaking grounds on the nature of reality; shit's not explaining how the strings knot; shit's not saying why we exist. It just has a lot to say about us, and most of it gets scary. I know what I look like as a god. I know that I picked it from some childhood vision of pure power, and I don't know what fostered that shit inna first place, and I know that nothing scares me more than that; that my brain defaulted to red as what kind of god I'd be. I used to think I was a revolutionary type. Not a revolutionary, no, but the kinda guy that could inspire people to maybe stand up and maybe do something. Blue. But no. I'm red. I've got a lot of time to myself. Danielle and me don't go out much. We're desert bone pale, and it doesn't suit either of us. I remember when we were gold. I remember when colors didn't mean so much. I've grown or I've shrunk; all I know is I've changed. Shit, a month ago I was Jordan White. Now: Sage Black'll do. It's not a certain thing. It's not a comfortableinmyskin thing. It just works. I know the new Jordan White, and I'm trying to make him in my image so I can leave it behind. A handmedown because I love myself enough to consider making multiple mes as a positive thing. Oh fuck, I haven't really lost it this time. I'm just. Misaligned. I think I might be getting more like my god everyday, and maybe that means that crimson in Jordan can be buried in someone else's grave. All I know is that it's not my name anymore, and I gave it up in order to respect that. I do so many things for so many reasons that I'm never sure of a purpose; there's something nice about every action to a single goal. Something just gets lost when it's all a minor step in every direction. Some of us have Gilgamesh's blood, some of us have Enkidu's. I like to think they remain close but never blend. I like to take this as my origin story. I do this because it sounds cool, but that's enough to make it valid. My head's cracking, is all. I'm thinking about the world that has underwater lakes and crystal caverns and holes in the ocean dropping miles and miles and miles down, and I'm thinking about things that could only exist in those places. It's all bullshit, but it's very warm. Okay, so I'm calling existence as a placebo effect. Okay, so I'm coming up with dolphin grace. Okay, so reality's very, very liquid. I've locked all the doors, all the windows. I'm not creating a world; I'm not populating it. I'm hiding from it in the sparse and empty. Danielle, she's here. Somewhere, I don't think she's scared or hiding or paralyzed but she's not right here. She's just here, in a general sense. I think I'm looking for her. I think she killed the power, at least to the lights. A couple fuseboxes. Fuck. I'm not very sure why, right now. I've been needing to smoke to concentrate lately’it's not some image thing, some tepid bullshit thing that has no meaning beyond image, no substance beyond fake style. It's me slipping into gray. I'm bleeding. It doesn't feel bad. It itches, stings when I scratch it. My arm's not numb. I don't hear it dripping. I don't think I'm going to die. Something pops on, something electronic. "Saaaaaaaage," it's saying. The PA. Her voice. I know she's here. If I pull hard enough on my smoke to light up maybe two square feet of wall up close, I'm going to cough and I'm not going to see anything. I'm just smoking to think. Does this engagement have rules? Any sort of laws to abide in times of skirmish? Is this a battle of cunning or attrition? Can I just hide behind a couch and wait for her to sober up? No more than I can just hide behind a couch and wait for me to sober up and consider this a victory, no. And the PA laughs, or she laughs. Killing the power's as good as closing your eyes now, babe. Cutting more ties to a reality you can clutch to in the name of survival, jumping blind into the hallucinations. I fucked up. Can you hear me? Fuck. I have to talk. There's gotta be one of those voice sender things somewhere around here. It's a fuckin' wall. Come on. Click. "Danielle?" "Danielle?" comes the speakers. Fuck, that's me? Fuck, that's me. A tenth of a second delayed, maybe. Reality vibrates on the same pattern. "You can't fi-hi-ind meeeee." "Is this this a ga game?" Fuck. "Is this a game?" I'm fightin' the reverb, trying to ignore echo and forge through sentences. "Uh huh." "Who's winning?" "I dunno." "I'm blood. Ied. Bloodied." "That's not good." She cackles and cuts off, blank. "No. No. So. This is sport?" "Oooh. I like that. I'm game." "You're prey?" "Who prays here? Heee." "Danielle?" Nothing. Dead air. Stale, stagnant, suffocating. Silent. Serene? I'm breathing dinosaur air and I don't know if I should be hunting or hiding. Both fit. This place seems so much bigger in the dark. It might be tight footsteps, it might be lingering along walls to not get lost in worlds of glass and steel. It might be me moving in circles, my own voice clouding my head. It's telling me that there's a mummy around the corner, the angered jerky remains of an ancient king whose dominion was the sun and whose rule was razorblade just. I won't see anything but a glowing eye, it tells me, lunging’not lumbering’with a modest gurgle. He could bring the sun itself down, that big bright motherfucker, and bathe me in his golden power as I cook on an atomic level; he won't. He'll use his remaining tooth, he'll dig at me with claws-n’e-fingers, he'll drink my blood and my waste and my entrails even as it slops out the half of his skull lost to his assassination. It's my voice; it's not me. It's that me that exists in a world of terrifying and terrified, that cowers from the call of monsters and plays at one when he gets a chance to relax in a place of only people, homo sapiens, Us. He knows very well what kind of thing hate is, he just doesn't care. He doesn't speak for the whole of the populace. But I can understand the fear. Nestled in quiet animal corners of the soul; there is a new Jordan White because I am not the kind of person that is good at famous. I am electric ecstatic, illuminating sidewise instead of on the scale of progression and regression. I'm fucked up, and my fucked up wife has something that cuts and is somewhere I don't know, other than that I won't be able to see her when I find it. More frightening than a goddamned mummy or a vampire’maybe not a Frankensteinian monster, but still. I am more afraid of what lurks in the pit of my soul than in the pit of my imagination; dig? "You're not finding me-hee-heeeeeee, I've got allll of the keys." She's locked herself, somewhere, I Meanwhile, Jordan White! He's not doing much, actually. Crosslegged on a bed in the small bedroom of a loud apartment, neck splayed back and eyes rolling. It's his city, it's not his apartment; he thinks he knows the guy that owns it, but he's really just aware of a guy who's friends with the owner. It's close enough for him. Another party on another weekend in another scene. Outside of his door, a white man with half of his dreadlocks hidden under a dirt yellow knit cap talks about the relevance of hip-hop to society at large to a transient group of anywhere from two to twenty while a scratched vinyl plays Guided By Voices over a series of rigged up loudspeakers. There's a rush of noise and light and being’but that is outside of the door. Inside, a jug of red wine sits open at the foot of the bed, cradling a thirty-something woman with purple hair and a government grant and the third highest selling nonfiction book of the party. She mutters politics under her breath, in halftones and broken notes to a very captive audience of sleeping pills and sedatives whirling around the brains of vessels. Jordan keeps quieter, talking into the neck of a freckled blonde girl that says she was born in Nigeria and has a stretched black tattoo around her ankle that doesn't look to be anything much. She smokes a joint and he’s asking: "y'think we're safe?" "Here." "Nah, anywhere." "No, here. Take the joint." "I don't smoke." "An' I don't shack up with random guys at parties. Smoke." He takes it, he smokes. "Oh god, we're too safe. Mmm, you know? People say we're not, but we are, here, now. We're safe, our children are safe, our streets are safe. We don't even know what dangerous means." "What's so bad 'bout that?" He takes small hits, not coughing; he passes. Rhythm is established. (Fast fact: The man currently named Jordan White was a stoner in high school, despite his current “fuck marijuana” stance!) "Complacency doesn't breed change. Things get so fucking boring. That's why we do stupid shit. Risks are fun, not, you know, safe." "What about people live dangerous lives?" "Oh, like what, soldiers? Racecar drivers? Alligator hunters?" "People whose lives are at risk daily, yeah." "What's scary about that? It gets tense, but you can always quit. No, no; you can't understand fear in America. It's not pervasive, it's not everywhere." "I say there's plenty of fear in America, shit. I mean, fuck, shit might not be like death at every corner, but there's definitely a fuckin' fear to the place. We ain't like, we ain't chill for sure. S'paranoia, s'xenophobia, s'racism." "Maybe my standards for fear are higher than yours." "May be. S'just, you can't look at the average person here'nd say they ain't fuckin' scared, y'know? Y'gotta be shittin' me." "There's bigger fear in other places. That's all." "Y'think? I mean, chilled to the core's chilled to the core, no matter what's causin' it. Fuck, what is it, relativity?" "Heh, relativism." "Yeah, yeah. That." "What did you say your career was?" "Haha, oh fuck. I'm a professional wrestler." "No shit?" "No shit." "Don't they drug test?" "Nah. I dunno. I don't think so." "That might be good. I didn't catch your name either." "I wrestle as Jordan White." "But it's not your name?" "Nah, Frank Black." "Oh my god. Do you know Alyssa Milner?" "Yeah." "Oh my god. I know. Or, like. I know of you." "Yeah?" "Yeah. You're a fucking prick. Be glad I'm comfortable." "I am, I am." "No, seriously. You are fucked up." "I. I don't wanna hear about it." "And I don't want to talk about it. Pretend you're someone else." "Just call me Jordan White." "There. I like that. And I never even heard your side of the story, so." "Shit, y’know? Assume the best outta me, move on." "Exactly, boy. Oh god, it is a small world." "Nah. Big world, big city, small scene. Lotta overlap, 'n' shit, y'know?" "Groups clinging with other groups for solidarity? A mob of mobs." "Yeah." Outside, someone mutters along: sister, I need wine for color in my skin and darkness in my eyes. He mumbles the rest, catching the right word or the right idea at random. Others join in; no one corrects anyone. The guy that actually lives here retaliates, turns the speakers up and switches the album out for a hazy jazz of instruments that lose their path/forge their own path through shotgun smoke and the ghosts of the old, the loved and the loathed. The blur of nostalgia, the anesthesia of memory. Jordan stares at the joint when it's on him. Rolls it between his fingers, just a coned nub that doesn't hold a light anymore for the resin. At his feet, or beyond his feet, someone stammers out an explanation for a justification formula; like the amount of dead (real) over the amount of dead (possible), multiplied by the intangibility of freedom. I'm holding my breath and hoping I'm wrong. I really, really, really thought she'd be locked up in the control room, control box, psychovault, whatever it is. That industrial sealed hole that controls my home. I just, I don't think I was right. She had a knife last time. I'd maybe prefer it was a gun, like I'd maybe prefer I had thermal vision. Oh shit, and invisibility, shoulder mounted laser cannon, long dreads, alla that. She's breathing. I know it's her; I wheeze sometimes, she never does. "D?" If she's concentrating, I can feel safe.
