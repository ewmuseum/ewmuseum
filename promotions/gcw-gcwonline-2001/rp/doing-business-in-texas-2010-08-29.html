Doing business in Texas.

29 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/29/10 The music plays loud in this not so smoke filled room. The patrons dance around the dance floor doing a two step to some old George Jones song. Nimbly the waitress slips between the drunken cowboys to the lone large man sitting at a table. She places a couple shots of whiskey down on the table and smiles as she walks away. Looking up he looks around the room shaking his head. “This has got to be the reverse of Little Big Horn.” looking around Shaman is a bit annoyed. He grabs one of the shots as he drinks it down quickly. He turns as some of the Texans in this place are eying him carefully. He looks at them and smirks as he takes another shot of whiskey. If only these fucking redneck retards knew what I could do to them. Take this guys dancing with his ugly woman. I'd kick his last tooth in and smile all the while. Cheers you bunch of fucking dumb asses. He motions for the waitress to come over to him. She smiles as she steps near him. “What can I help ya with sugar?” He points at the table top and makes a circle. She smiles and nods as he looks the place over. It has a rustic look similar to an old west feel. The dance floor is hardwood, he would hazard a guess of cedar perhaps. Not much difference in his mind then a lot of the hardwood floors near Louisiana. Then he turns slowly looking at the caged in band area. “Now I remember why I came here, I hate evaluating places I own.” Lifting up another shot he takes a drink of it. Holding the glass up his eyes focus on his own fingerprints as they reflect the light through the glass. Spinning the glass between his fingers he slowly places it top down on the table. This is not what I want to be doing this evening. I should be training and preparing for High Stakes. I do not know who I am going to get as an opponent. Last time I held this event at my Casino things got way way out of hand. Yet again the flow of money was good for both sides of the table, of course my take was much more than expected. All the fans aren't just going to watch a show, they are going to gamble. As the waitress comes back with his drinks he motions for her to sit down. “So what is your name?” She sits down nervously as she slowly responds. “My name is Flo, short for Florence.” Nodding he runs his fingers around the top of one of the shot glasses. He grins as he looks up dipping his pinky in the whiskey. He looks at the whiskey on his finger and licks it off in a disturbing fashion. His eyes turn to Flo with a seriously dangerous look. “So Flo how long have you worked here?” No emotion on his face as he awaits an answer. She looks at him as she fidgets a bit at the uncomfortable questioning. “I have worked here for nearly 6 years now.” Rubbing his chin for a few moments, he then reaches forward grabbing a shot glass and drinking it down slowly savoring the burning sensation. “Do you have a clue as to who I am Flo?” She nods her head in response, her obvious answer is one Shaman had expected since he did not announce his arrival. He turns his gaze to the bartender. His eyes notices the Bartender slipping a few bills from the register into his pocket. His eyes narrow as he observes closer. He watches some of the bouncers flirt with the women. Shaking his head he turns back to Flo. He studies her for a moment as he decides on something. “Flo what would you do with this place if you could make some changes?” She smiles as she leans forward confidently. “First I'd fire the Manager, He kept all of the dirty bastards that were here before.” She points at the bartender. “For example he has raped three women using some date rape drug, as well as steal from the till.” She makes a disgusted expression. She shakes her head as she thumbs near her face towards the bouncers. “All they do is wanna fight, then exploit the waitresses and women patrons. It is just disgusting that we cannot work in a clean and respectable establishment.” She sighs as she rubs her temple. Shaman looks over at the others she pointed at. He himself brings his hand up rubbing his temples. Oh why did I buy this place from bastion back in 2000. I knew this was the wrong location, but it did make a good income at the time. I didn't like the place then and don't really like it now. Flo seems to be one of the few honest ones in here. I wonder if the other waitresses work as hard as flow and are honest. I better ask more questions before I make a decision on what to do with most of the employees. “Flo do you really not know who I am?” She smiles as she leans forward and speaks to him. He notices the smell of her perfume, strong at first yet not displeasing. She looks back and forth with her eyes as she speaks. “I didn't want to say anything, but yer one of those wrasslers aintcha? Shaman I think, right?” She sits back seemingly pleased with herself. Shaman genuinely smiles for once as he eyes her. “You can call me Andrew Flo. So tell me do all of the other employees act the way of the bouncers and bartender here?” She nods her head no as she looks at him. “They aren't all bad, Just mostly those I mentioned and the manager.” Looking at Flo he hands her a fifty dollar bill. “Thanks for your time Flo.” She smiles leaving him to his thoughts. Strumming his fingers on the table he eyes the room. Suddenly he sees his opportunity as he grins purposely checking out one of the cowboys girls. He checks her up and down and whistles at her. I kind of miss doing this, Been a longtime not training this way. Guess it will make me feel alive again. Guess it will be another way to get rid of these bouncers who are about fucking useless. The cowboy turns and looks at Shaman eying his woman. Pushing her to the side he walks to the table placing both hands on it staring down at Shaman. “Hey boy you lookin at mah woman?” Shaman looks at the guy not moving as he grins. “So what if I am?” The cowboy swipes an arm across the table spilling all of the shots onto the floor. He looks at Shaman with a cocky grin. “What ya gonna do now Boy, You gonna cry?” He laughs as he looks around at everyone else in the bar laughing as well. Shaman slowly stands up while the cowboy isn't looking. Suddenly the cowboy turns as the bar quietens down. His face becomes flush as he realizes he may have bitten off more than he can chew. Being too stubborn to back down the cowboy bucks up as Shaman responds calmly. “I was thinking you and your Texas ways may be gentleman like and go to the bar and kindly replace my drinks.” jerking a thumb over his shoulder he notices the bouncers aren't moving as they seem to be chuckling and watching the scene unfold. He turns the gaze back to the cowboy who seems to be mulling over the decision. Seems to me these fuck offs for bouncers, are in this for the fight after all. Who am I to deny them their spotlight in the sun. The cowboy looks up at Shaman and gives him the finger. Shaman reaches out as the finger is mere inches from his face and grabs ahold of it and twists while bending it sideways. CRACK!! Feeling the mans finger snap, he lets go, Grabbing the mans shirt pulling him towards himself, as the man comes forward he drives a fist into the mans face a few times over and over again. He simply releases the man as his girl jumps on Shaman's back. Grabbing her from over his shoulder he literally slams her down onto the table as the table breaks. He turns as the three bouncers stand up. He looks at the three of them as he speaks. “Fucking crazy broad.” The bouncers step forward surrounding him. He eyes the three of them as one of them speak loudly. “We don't like your kind here Tonto, the reservation is to the west about 58 miles.” As they surround him, he eyes the situation as he thinks quickly. So my guess here this is a very dangerous situation....for them. “Well fellas I think you may want to think this through before you make a couple big mistakes.” He pokes the big one in the chest as he speaks, Daring him to take action. He smiles as he looks around. “It isn't everyday that a couple of inbreeding beef fed Texas hick rednecks get stupid enough o face me three on one. This can only end badly for you.” The first bouncer puts a hand on his shoulder trying to restrain him as Shaman literally ducks underneath the arm, wrapping his huge arms around the mans chest hoisting the man up and rams him into the solid steel post behind him. He turns and kicks a table knocking one of the other bouncers off his feet. The third and last Bouncer charges, as Shaman reaches down grabbing a chair and slams the man over the back with it. Holding the splinters of whats left of the chair in his hand Shaman looks around the room. “Does someone want to put two and two together now or should I go out of my way and explain it further?” The bouncer who was tripped up by the table gets up and looks at Shaman. He gets stupid as he charges Shaman, who this time side steps him grabbing him by his pants and shirt running him headlong into the metal post. Come on these guys can't be this fucking stupid. He thinks to himself as he turns looking at the bartender. Walking over he steps behind the bar grabbing the bartender slamming him face first down upon the bar. Busting the bartenders lip and nose he pulls the bartender back in an upright position as he begins to bleed. Reaching into the mans pocket he pulls out the large wad of money. He turns and drops it onto the register. He hoists the bartender up and tosses him over the top of the bar and out onto the bar room floor. From the back a skinny man comes running out with a baseball bat yelling and screaming. He slams it down on the bar top looking at Shaman. Turning slowly he narrows his eyes looking at the manager. The manager waves the bat pointing it at him. “Who the fuck do you think you are? No no who gives a shit get the fuck out of my bar if you know whats good for ya.” Chuckling the larger Shaman walks towards the smaller man as the local Sheriff walks through the front door and people scatter and step to the side. The manager stays unmoving as he gets a little more confidence with the Sherriff's arrival. “Barry get this fucking Indian out of my establishment, Get him for drunk and disorderly conduct.” He looks over at the Sherriff as he nods. “Throw in assault as well.” Barry looks over at Shaman and shakes his head. “Hell Mr. Whitecloud I should have known you would be coming to town. What has it been about 7 years?” The manager drops the bat in shock as he turns looking at Shaman and then to the Sherriff. He then looks back at Shaman as he speaks. “Who the hell are you?” Looking at the larger man he begins to get really nervous. This is the part I love the most, going to let these prejudice hicks that they have been put in their damn places. Slowly he leans against the bar and observes everyone once again. “I own the place.” The Sherriff nods as he looks at the bouncers and bartender. Reaching over Shaman snatches the bat from the managers hand as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out a nextel phone. He presses the button and speaks clearly as the manager watches on in awe as he never really knew who the owner was. “Tyrone, you Jimmy, and Michael you guys get over here to the bar. You are going to work tonight.” He puts it back in his pocket. He turns and grabs the manager by the shirt dragging him over to the Sherriff shoving the manager so hard he falls to the floor. “Guess this is as good as anytime to tell you all you are fired!” He kicks the manager in the ass as he falls down again. “get the fuck out of my place and I don't want to hear you were ever back in this place. If Flo tells me you had showed your faces here you will be regretting every damned minute of it for the rest of your life.” He looks over at Flo smiling. He turns to her handing her the baseball bat. “Your the new Manager Flo make those changes you want to make.” The Bouncers and Manager get up to their feet and scramble out the door. The Bartender slowly attempts to get to his feet as Shaman slams a big foot down hard into the center of his back. Knocking the man flat back onto the floor. “If there is one thing I hate more than a liar, it is a thief. Barry this one I want charges of robbery, laundering, and what the hell ever he an be brought up on involving theft.” The Sherriff nods as he kneels putting his knee between the shoulder blades of the Bartender as he cuffs him. Dragging him to his feet the Sherriff leaves the bar back through the main exit. Turning Shaman walks to the phone and picks it up. He dials a number as his fingers turn the old rotary styled phone numbers. He waits for a moment as someone on the opposite end answer. “Yes this is Andrew Whitecloud, I am disappointed with your managerial service with my bar here in Texas and I wish to advise you that your services are no longer required and terminated.” Waiting as someone seems to be yelling on the other side of the phone. “What grounds do I have for this? It is simple breech of contract, Unsavory practices, and least of all not managing it the way I had set out for you.” Shaking his head Shaman turns and hands Flo the phone. Flo takes the phone s she listens for a moment as she speaks calmly and politely. “Mr. Whitecloud has decided that you shall hear from his lawyers and shall recover restitution for damages and lost income. Thank you and good day.” She hangs up the phone as she looks at Shaman. “I hope I didn't over step my bounds there?” Shaman pats her on the shoulder re-assuredly. He turns and pulls a chair out as he looks over at Flo. “You did fine in fact exactly what I would expect one of my managers to do.” Pulling a leather covered notepad out of his pocket he sits down as he begins to think and write a few things down. “Flo we have a lot of work to do to make this place respectable again. I hope you are up for the task because simply I am a slave driver.” He chuckles as he turns to write. Before he can start writing the Sherriff walks in and speaks up. "What are we going to do about the Cowboy and his woman? Kind of seems to me that even though they were at fault they could get you for excessive force of sorts." Just great i don't need this right now. After all of the happenings i was just hoping to clear my head. Thinking on this a moment Shaman sighs as he scratches his head. "Yeah guess you are right there, Don't press any charges and send me the bill for anything medical." Slapping Shaman on the back the Sherriff smiles. "Hope you got that out of your system kid, with all that's been going on in your life lately you sure needed it." The Sherriff adjusts his belt as he heads towards the door as Shaman simply watches as his emotional demeanor has changed completely. That felt relieving and weird at the same time. Natural for me to want to unload on those guys further, but I can only do so much within the boundaries of the law. I wanted to scream out and lash out at these guys, but I have so much on my plate as of late things have been bugging me. Look at my matches in GCW and my other wrestling ventures, hell for the first time ever I have pushed myself into the PTC top 5. That is a new place for me since I had never been there before. Reaching over the bar stretching slightly. He slides the cooler door to the side and reaches in pulling out a beer. Opening it he feels the sharp edges of the cap between his fingers. He looks up at the mirror noticing a lot of people had left the bar now. Usually was the case with most bar room brawls of this type. Looking back down at the pad he begins to write again. What have I really done here today? Did I do this for the right reasons or did I come here to simply throw out a power reminder of who is in charge? Has the money changed me a bit? Am I thinking about money more than I am about myself lately? All i achieved was beating the crap out of people that i could and not get the one true target i want. Why did I let Donny Diamond get the best of me at the last show I was at with GCW. Will I be able to handle the situation at hand. Why did he have that letter in hand. He looks over at Flo who is putting the wad of money into the register. She turns and looks at Shaman as she speaks. “Mr. Whitecloud I think we should just close up for the evening.” looking at his watch he nods in agreement. “Guess you are correct Flo, go ahead and pay everyone for the evening and give them each a hundred extra.” She nods as she turns taking the register drawer into the back office. I feel bipolar lately, every time I lash out with rage, I am followed up with depression. It is like I am fighting a shadow and not even getting the better of it. Maybe this time I should consider just hanging up the boots and just running my businesses. Maybe once in my life learn to turn on the light. Standing up he heads towards the door and exits out into the hot dry Texas air. Just a few thoughts on his mind and one simple one. What is next?
