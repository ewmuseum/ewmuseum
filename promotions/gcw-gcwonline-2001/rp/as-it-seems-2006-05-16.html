As it seems ...

16 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 May 06 Sometimes things aren’t always as they seem? I could barely breathe let alone stand or talk. The taste of blood and sweat formed a very bitter mix inside of my mouth. It had been a war, not that I didn’t expect it to be though. I battled this seven foot monster all throughout HIS home turf. I battered and bruised his body and in turn he bloodied and wounded mine. His hunger to rip me apart was strong that there was no doubt. Perhaps I never would understand his reasoning for coming at me the way he had, then again I didn’t really have to. All I understood was that this beast threatened the only part of my life that meant a damn to me anymore. He hurt the one person in this world that I could trust and love unconditionally. For that he had to pay. It didn’t matter what Bryan Mayhem threw my way, I was going to walk out victorious. I HAD to. I was driven to do things to this man, if one could even call him such, that I had never done before. Not even Jason Dread held a place in my being like this Mayhem. So anxious to do battle with him was I that I agreed to step into “oblivion” as he liked to call it. Many called me foolish but I knew better. I knew that things weren’t always as they seemed. I was prepared to take him on, the very best he could throw at me. I was willing to handle flames, blades, and glass. I was hoping to taste blood, tears, and dust. I was yearning to sense the fear and agony that would ooze out of his every movement when I was finished. So into Belmont I stepped, heart pounding and my pulse racing. It was like nothing I had ever seen before that was for certain. Dark, Gloomy, Vile. It was a place that most people could only think up in their worst nightmares. From the outside the giant shadow of the building seemed to laugh and point. It almost dared you to walk in and even HOPE to actually walk out in one piece. Of course things aren’t always as they seem. So the battle raged on. Two men who wanted nothing more but to rip the other’s heart out and feed it to a stray dog. It was far from pretty and in all reality I had never been in that kind of physical pain before. Emotionally I was charged, no way was I going down to this whack job. Physically though my body began to break down and fall apart. Not wood, glass, splinters, or fire would keep me down. I wouldn’t allow it. Each time I was sent crashing down I pulled the motivation from somewhere. I thought of Mary’s smiling face. I imagined holding her hand as she gave birth to our child. I pictured Starr’s bright green eyes staring at me in awe of the world around her. Each time I stood back up to my feet. Each time I glared at the beast of a man facing me and smirked. Then I pulled out a different type of motivation. The motivation to rip him in two. I imagined the look of those flames that burned the head stone of my daughter. I could see the pieces of the headstone shattering over and over again in my head. I could taste the blood that trickled down Mary’s chin as if it were my own. My anger grew and grew, and with it the punishment I dished out became even more fierce and vengeful. Then that final horn went off. “I’ve said this once, and I’ll say it again you can never beat me. I’m the greatest this to ever happen to GCW. Your days of torment are ov?” It was about that time that I probably should have stopped talking and gotten out of that retched house. I should have turned my back on that bastard and been named victorious. If nothing else the explosion heard upstairs should have told me that would have been the smart thing to do. Two things stopped me from doing so. One would be that my body was aching so much that I could barely move anymore. TWO? Well arrogance and anger is a son of a bitch and I thought I’d have more time. Things aren’t always as they seem. “Your days of torment are over. I shall walk up those stairs and destroy this monster in the very match that he created, ending careers on the way. O’Neil ? tell me what Oblivion is like.” He didn’t even attempt to speak, but he didn’t have to. He figured this was the end, he knew that house better than anyone. I should have known. I should have understood, yet I didn’t. So when I turned to leave ? CRAAAAAAAAASH! The side of the house collapsed locking me with the beast. I didn’t need to look at him to know that he was smiling. Perhaps not on the outside but deep down, Bryan was grinning ear to ear. He had lost to me, I had destroyed him in a way that he never thought possible. Yet somehow I wasn’t going to exactly be the big winner either. “There has to be another way out!” He chose his words carefully. “Welcome ? to hell.” I dropped to a knee knowing there was nothing left for me. Again I thought of everyone that I have left in my life. Mary, Our baby, Starr ? A tear rolled down my cheek. The thought of not seeing them again was very real. The fear of never breathing again was very real. But the anger I felt towards Bryan? For some reason it wasn’t there anymore. I couldn’t explain it in the least. My face went expressionless and my body motionless. What had I become? Who had I become? Where was the path I was heading down really going? All good questions ? Unfortunately answers were going to be hard to come by. The heat from the flames found us in that basement and began spreading quickly. Pieces of stone, dirt, and wood began tumbling from the old ceiling. The world was literally falling down around me and yet I remained very still. What else was there to do really? Cry? Beg? Pray? None of it would help. This life was lost ? LeStatt Knight was dead. Then again ? Things aren’t always as they seem. Sometimes? They are much worse.
