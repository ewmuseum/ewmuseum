US Championship?

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 12th, 2005 (I stand with my arms crossed over my upper torso as I speak to Steven. Steven, sitting at his desk with his hands laced together resting on his stack of documents is attired in a fine fitted beige Armani suit. Him, looking as comfortable as could be, my presentation of myself to him must display tastelessness and homeliness. Slits on my knees of my fading wrangler jeans, and a black polyester shirt, I was probably more comfortable than he was. I was obviously dressed to chat with the man.) ?””?United States Championship match huh?” Caldera- “Hey Mike you know, this is a beginning to a long road.” “I agree Steven, but if I beat Johnny B. and become United State Champion, it will throw me away from being Heavyweight Champion.” Caldera- “Look at it this way Mike. Knight was able to break a record no one thought possible to be broken. He held this belt for a record amount of time. He was able to go in the record books as possibly the best US Champion GCW has seen. And what was to come next” Look at him now. He is the World Heavyweight Champion.” “You have a point. I could use this to my advantage. Soften Knight up a bit. Set my sights on breaking his record. Then set my sights on him. But you sure this is the right path?” Caldera- “Mike, it is the right path. The US title isn’t just forfeiture to throw you away from your dreams. It is a path you can follow to your dreams. If you defeat Borealis, you can use the US title to your advantage. Anyone and everyone will want to face you. Try and dethrone my prot’g”. By doing this, you will experience more challenges, face harder opponents, and be wearing the temporary gold until your dream is fulfilled.” “You know what”.I’ll do it. You’re right Steven, this title could be what I need. And I could be what this title needs. It could be my building block, shaping me into a true heavyweight. This was a great idea. Was it your idea?” Caldera- “Come on Mike as if you had to ask.” (I let out a genuine chuckle as I shake Steven’s hand.) “I’ll see to it that your idea doesn’t fall to waste.” Caldera- “I’m sure you won’t.” (I release from his grasp as I turn to exit his office.) Caldera- “Oh and Mike”.” (Turning back to him, I see his pride of how he has changed me glow in his face, like a proud parent, a true mentor.) Caldera- “Play it smart.” (I nod my head showing my approval and my gratitude towards the man who has pulled me from my mid-career crisis. All I have left to do is empress him. Show him I am no longer a failure. This man is giving me a huge opportunity, and I am going to take it.) (I walk out of his office, closing the door behind me, as I march on down the hallway, the walk towards my dressing room feels like hours. There are millions of things running through my mind. My past, the present, my future. I reach my locker room door as the thought process ceases. I turn the knob and say softly to myself?..) “The time is now Mike, the time is now”..” (I let out a soft sigh of excitement as I enter my dressing room and close the door behind me.)
