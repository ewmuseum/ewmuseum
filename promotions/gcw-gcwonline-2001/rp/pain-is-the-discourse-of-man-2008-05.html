Pain is the Discourse of Man

May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May “A storm shall arrive over earth and through sky as the angels from heaven begin to cry? The twinkling of the Spring stars in the sky dance in Zacharia Taylor’s eyes as he lays down on the park bench in Brookdale Community Park. The rustling of the wind and the occasional hoot of an owl acts as a sweet ambiance in the dark park, enticing Zacharia’s eyes to close and submit to the dreams that God wills him to see. The Church. The Modern Testament. Father Emanuel. It has been a month since he has left the church, yet he can’t rid his teachings and all that he has known his whole life in a matter of weeks. No, the Church is forever branded into his mind. An owl turns his head around and down to look at the passing rodent on the ground. Zacharia follows the rodent and owl with his faltering mind. The owl stealthily swooped down and stuck out its claws to grab its prey and then swooped off behind a tree. It is funny how nature always puts out metaphors for life challenges. When Father Emanuel tried to explain what he must do when he was just a boy, they went out into this very park and sat on this very bench to watch nature take its course. Zacharia can’t recall ever feeling sympathy for the rodent in the night. Father Emanuel told him that he was the owl and his prey were the offenders of the world. Zacharia must swoop down and take out his prey with the swift hand of God. Practical and visual lessons are hard to forget... He fights off the falling grains of sand in the Sandman’s hourglass, but can’t stop them. His eyes slowly close, submitting himself to dreams. [slightly cut off on both sides] “Hey buddy. Come on! You can’t sleep here.” Zacharia stirs from his dreams and looks up into a flashlight from the Clifton Police Officer. The overweight cop was sent on foot patrol to go over the park to make sure no homeless took shelter in the public park. Public, meaning for everyone, yet there are always exceptions. “Come on man, I’m sorry but you have to go home or go to a home. What’s your name?” “I’m going home officer.” The police officer watches Taylor as he stands up, the beam of light falling over his bare chest. The light reflects brighter off of the smooth scar across his chest, coming to the attention of the officer. “That’s a, a mighty fine scar ya got there, son. Haven’t seen one like that in a long while. How did ya get that?” Zacharia gives the officer a cold stare, turns around, and then turns his head slightly. “Sin.” Zacharia pulls the sweatshirt that was his pillow over his shoulders and slightly zips it up. He runs his fingers over his scar and grits his teeth. Pulling his hood over his head, he makes his way out of the park. All that he can think about was questions. “Is Father Emanuel really that dogmatic with his views of the Modern Testament” Why is that all I see is Jesus Christ? I never said those things to Father Emanuel, yet I feel so strong about it. I don’t want to be the sacrificial lamb as Jesus Christ was. I will not die for the views of one man, only my God. “If my God sees fault in my views, would he not send someone after my life” Am I the only mercenary of God on this plane? Why must I suffer under the will of Father Emanuel? I need answers, not questions...” Zacharia turns the corner of the gate of the park and heads down towards the center of the city. He looks down at his hand that is brutally scared by the cross when he was a child. Hours could have passed, but time had stopped into a limbo of reality in Zacharia’s head. He lifts the cross from his chest with his open and hand puts it in the outline of the scar and stops. The glint from the cross in the moonlight casts a shadow onto his index finger. He has the sudden urge to look up in the direction of the shadow. The Church stands tall with the contrasting shadows cast by the moonlight. Dropping the cross back to his chest, he walked up to the church and stood at the door. The eagle head knockers on the door stand at waist level. He reaches out with his scarred hands and follows the grooves of the “feathers” and the beak of the sacred bird. Power. Freedom. Peace. The eagle has more symbolism than the innocent bird could ever imagine. An inanimate object has superiority over all of us. Funny how even in inanimacy, nature is the guardian of all things. The knocker pulled forward to allow entrance. Zacharia steps into the church and into the lobby. Nothing has changed. The same candles are lit as before, the pews are illuminated by the candles on the sides and the low, dim lights from the ceiling. He steps forward into the church hall, every footstep echoing off the empty walls. As he passes by the basin of holy water he dips his hands inside and cleanses them of sin. He raises his right hand to his hand and does the sign of the cross while lipping the stations as he moves his hands. As he reaches the alter, he kneels down on the steps and bows his head. “Forgive me, my Lord, for I have been away from your house for a long period of time. I have never broken contact with my Lord nor have I questioned You, but I need guidance. I look at Your Son, Jesus Christ and I take pity on him. Is this the path that I am following” Is this the fate that I must succumb to? I do not question You, my Lord, but I do seek some wisdom to my knowledge.” As Zacharia Taylor stands up, a door opens up from behind the alter. Falling back to his knees, Zacharia stares up from a bowed head at the location of the noise. Pretending to pray further, he watches the figure of Father Emanuel come out and pass by the cross. After kissing the Alter, the priest goes to light the alter’s candles when he looks down and sees Zacharia praying. “My son, Zacharia! You have returned to the church. Praise God for your safe return.” Zacharia drops his hands from prayer and looks Father Emanuel in the eyes with a very cold stare. “I have not returned, father. I am here to seek wisdom in my confusion.” “I can help you with whatever you need, my son. After all, who better to turn to than your mentor?” Zacharia stands in frustration. He puts up his hood once more and turns his back. “I have come to talk to God, not his disciple.” “But, Zacharia, is a month’s disloyalty to the Church not enough..” “Disloyalty” Disloyalty not to the Church, but to your teachings Father. I have not turned my back on my Lord, nor have I on His House. You should make no gesture to disloyalty, Father.” Father Emanuel straightens up. Anger and remorse fill his eyes. He makes a fist, but then turns it into a soothing palm. “My son, I know nothing of disloyalty. I follow the vision and the knowledge that the Lord has bestowed upon me. There is no disloyalty where I lie. God wills that I preach and you practice. We both spread the word of God in similar but different ways my child.” “Very different ways. My hands are my instruments of God, where your mouth is yours. I suffer damage to my skin while...” “Zacharia, like I have told you before. Damage to your skin is no more than the damage of a spade...” “...digging into the earth. I know.” Father Emanuel is taken back by the angst in Zacharia’s voice. The lack of patience and the tension can be seen in his body. Father Emanuel has never seen such hostility. “How did you know that I was..” “Maybe you are not the only one that God gives visions to, Father. I know the frustration you face without having a practitioner to carry out your bidding, but I am in too much question of your “wisdom” to fall back into this Church.” Zacharia turns around and walks towards the doors of the Church. “I do not need you to hold my hand through this Hell on earth. God may have willed me to practice, but you may be lost in your own teachings.” “But, my son. Please, if you would just talk to me and let me know what bothers you.” “Not now, Father. I still give you the respect that you deserve for your standing in the Church, but I still see confusion in your eyes.” “But, where are you going” What have you been doing? If you are not betraying God, are you still carrying out his bidding?” Fath Emanuel reaches out to Zacharia as he walks up the isle of the pews. His gesture and words go unnoticed to the back of Zacharia. Zacharia reached the basin of Holy Water and cups it into his hands. He looks down into the shadowy reflection of himself and drops the liquid back into the basin. He blesses himself and then pushes on the tall wooden doors to let himself out. The day breaks upon the earth with a dreamy state of grey. The sun’s rays are dispersed into the grey clouds of the sky. Zacharia heads out to the left in search of his ever popular phone booth. Once he reaches the booth, he looks around and enters. After dialing the seven regurgitated digits, he waits. “Hello?” “Bagwell, it’s Zacharia.” “Look man, the Movement isn’t so happy with what you are doing. I just want to let you know that I am doing this as a friend.” “I know. I appreciate it Chris. I am going to keep it short.” “I’m not sayin that I’m not gonna talk to ya. I’m just sayin that even though some may say one thing, you know that I care for ya, kid.” “I’m sorry for making it harder on you. Black, he just...” “Black is doing what he does best. Keep your cool and your eyes in line. The Movement is goin to miss ya man, but let’s get to why you called. Keegan O’Conner. That’s who your up against.” Zacharia reaches in his back pocket and takes out a leather wrapped book. “O’Conner... He’s new right?” “Yeah, but that doesn’t mean that he’s a pushover. He was a heavyweight champ, tag too. They call him...” “Chaos.” Zacharia reads from his book. “He’s a big guy, huh” 6'6 and 285? A little impractical don’t you think? Started as a boxer in golden gloves, but ended his career with a cheap shot to the ref. Went into wrestling.” “Typical California hot shot. Drugs got the best of him. Drugs, alcohol, steroids. Ya know he’s got the small ball syndrome?” Zacharia takes the phone away from his ear and grits his teeth. He hesitated before putting the phone back to his ear. “You are too much Bagwell. I just want the facts before getting ready for my match. He’s not as good as he may think. Hitting an innocent ref, taking up drugs and everything. He bounced in and out of rehab, three different organizations in wrestling.” “Yeah, you can classify him as a BAMF by your religion bullshit. I mean, he’s not as face as everyone may think. You gonna take him?” “Yeah, I like where this is going.” “So go pray and do your thing. I will see you at World Wide. Just, don’t go following me into the locker room or anything. I did this as a favor, as a friend. The business end of it is different.” “I know. Thanks for the help Chris. Think you can do...” The phone beeps and then clicks back off. Bagwell hung up before he had a chance to finish. Maybe it was just a favor and it was time to get back to business. He does have a Movement to lead. Zacharia hangs up the phone and puts the book back in his pocket. He steps out fo the booth and walks down the sidewalk, heading towards the bus station. A bang echoed out of the alley to the left of the hardware store down the street. “I have more important issues at hand” He turns the other way and goes to avoid the alley when a clasp of thunder stops him in his tracks. “I understand...” He puts up his hood and quickens his pace to the alley. The sight of a man slapping a bleeding women stuns him for a moment. He takes out his cross and looks up to the heavens. He mumbles a prayer to himself and then takes off his hood and sweatshirt. He runs over to the man and punches him across the face, knocking him into the dumpster before taking the man’s head and slamming it into the wall. The man falls unconscious to the ground. "Feel the wrath of the Almighty. Seek salvation and repent for your sins." He turns around and sees the women on the ground. He helps her up and she falls against the wall. Her left heel is broken, her skirt torn, and the cloth that she considers a shirt is bloody and torn. Taylor turns around and grabs his sweatshirt. He puts it on and zips up the front before speaking: “Are you ok?” “Yeah, I’m fine. Fuckin scumbag tried to get more than what he paid. Then he tried to take the money back after everything was done and over with. Thanks for...” “Paid for what?” “You know baby, for some services. Interested?” Zacharia looks around in shock and tenses up. "Come on baby. Why so tense?" She reaches out and slides her hand from his chest up onto his shoulder as she walks around him to his side. "You need some r&r if you ask me. But what do I know?" He makes a fist, and grabs her hand. He pulls her back in front of him, slightly tripping her up on her broken heel. Her smile turned into a look of fear. She takes a step back but becomes pinned between the wall and Zacharia. “You’re not his girlfriend, wife, friend?” “No, no. I’m just doin my job, baby.” “Get down on your knees.” "So you want some r&r? Alright baby, it won't cost you a thang. This is payment for..." "GET ON YOUR KNEES!" Zacharia stared fiercely at her until she got down slowly onto her knees. She pushes up against the wall and pulls her half opened shirt closed. “You will kneel before me and accept the prayer of guidance. You are not an evil person, but you have chosen ways that are not accepted under His will THE GUIDANCE OF THE LORD COMMANDS THEE TO LIVE BY THE RULES OF THE COMMANDMENTS BUT ALSO OF THREE SPECIFIC GUIDELINES. DO NOT LIE, CHEAT, OR STEAL. DO NOT LIE FOR SELF GAIN OR FOR DECEPTION TO HURT SOMEONE ELSE. DO NOT CHEAT YOUR BODY AND LIVE A HEALTHY AND STABLE LIFE BY THE GUIDANCE OF THE LORD. DO NOT STEAL MATERIAL OR MENTAL CAPACITY. AND NEVER STEAL ONE’S LIFE. LIVE BY THESE PRINCIPLES AND YOU SHALL LIVE LIFE FREE AND CLEAN Now rise and recognize Jesus as your Savior and God as your Lord.” The women rises and looks more scared than ever. She looks around as for someone to help her, but then looks back into the eyes of Zacharia Taylor. She closes her eyes for a brief second before spitting into Zacharia’s face. “Crazy ass motha fucka. You got another thing comin if ya think that shit’s gonna work.” She runs off into the street. Visions of her getting hit by a car come across his mind. He makes a fist but then stops himself. He looks up into the cloudy sky for guidance, and closes his eyes as he leans up against the wall.
