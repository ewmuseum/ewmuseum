Through the Looking Glass( Infinite Gaunlet RP)

3 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/2005 ( This was for my IG rp. I just wanted to acrhive here.) She was getting sleepy. Pale blue eyes drooped as head lolled back for a moment before she snapped awake and caught her falling book. The book was good, in fact, it was a great read and probably part of the reason she hasn't gone to sleep as of yet. Turning to eye the clock Eva grimaced. Three am. A small sigh wafted between pouty lips as she dog-eared the page and closed the fantasy novel to place it on the nightstand next to her rented bed. Stretching out she flipped the light switch and turned to curl up comfortably beneath the sheets. It was time to sleep...and dream. And dream she did, of a far away place in another dimension. It was a fantasy of her own that if one thought about it was conceived through her wrestling career and one awesome book... The sunbeams danced gaily over the lush green trees as laughter flew upon the gentle breeze fluttering like a joyful spirit for all who dwelled near to hear. It was a time of celebration. The new king and his consort were to be crowned and the kingdom would once again be whole. Smiles were upon every face as the two walked the red-carpeted aisle towards the thrones. It was a grand ceremony, vows spoken and people cheering as that crown was laid upon Roberto's head. His consort followed, a dainty tiara now resting upon hair of pale satin. Amongst the people stood the great bard of the realm, his face etched with a smile as he stood forward to toast the new monarchy. ' My lords, my ladies!! I beg thy attention!! ' He voice rang over the crowds hushing them almost at once. He stepped upon the lowest step and turned to the crowd for a moment as he continued, ' I feel a toast is in order, in celebration of our new king, and his lovely consort. May they see nothing but peace during their long-lived reign. May the lands flourish under their rule, and may all the people love them, as they love their people. Long live the King and his Consort!!' The crowd went wild with cheers mimicking his words. ' LONG LIVE THE KING!! LONG LIVE HIS LADY! Hip Hip HUZZAH!' Through out it all the woman who sat quietly next to the king smiled sweetly, her heart pounding as she looked towards the bard, a secret look passing between to the two. She wanted to celebrate. She wanted to enjoy this new station, but knew she could not. There were dark forces in play, and she felt like a pawn in a living game of chess. As the glasses were raised she lifted her own and greeted her people with a brilliant smile, that wasn’t heart felt and called out in her soft voice to the crowd, ' I promise you that this will be a reign not soon forgotten!!' Again cheers went through the nation as they celebrated her words, unaware of the true meaning behind them. She settled back into her seat and slid a glance to Roberto who smiled at her as he leaned to the side and whispered in her ear' I am glad that you were chosen as my consort. Together we shall reshape the nation and become strong once again.' Her lips twitched as she bestowed on him a semblance of a smile and purred out in a whispered tone, ' Indeed, we will.' The celebration lasted long into the night, and even the next day, as the people played games of physical prowess, the bards sang songs of heroes past, present and future. Gifts were showered upon Roberto and Eva as they walked amongst the people. Through all this there were heavy thoughts burdening her mind. Each time she looked to the Master bard, Lestatt, he would lift his glass to her with a black-hearted smile, causing her to shudder with repulsion. A pawn. She finally decided she needed to rest and headed to her chambers for the night. She swept into her private chambers, swallowed by the darkness for only a moment before there came a light. A single candle glimmered on the table near the center of her room, and next to it was Lestatt. She felt his cool stare as she stalked across the room, pointedly ignoring him for a moment whilst she made ready for bed. Nimble fingers tugged at the strings that kept her dress tightly laced to that slender form, and yet they would not give way. Frustrated, and angry she tried again trembling with both fear and loathing. Warm breath on her neck caused her to freeze, her skin prickling along the sinewy cords and down her softly curved back and arms. The brush of calloused fingers caused her to flinch, as she felt the lace give way finally with his pull. Idly she thought how appropriate it was that the strings bent to his will like so many others, yielding and without thought to his true motives. No words were spoken as she stepped away from him, shrugging the finely crafted material from slender shoulders while she passed behind the privacy blind. She wanted to stay hidden behind that thin wall, hoping that maybe he would give up his wait and leave. With jerky movements the dress finally fell away as she donned her dressing robe the gold clasps linking together at the waist. Silently praying for strength, she took a deep breath and stepped out once more to face him. Head up high she moved to the table, her gaze never wavering from his. She felt as if his presence would swallow her courage. Silently she sat in the chair opposite him, small hands folding demurely in her lap before finally the deafening silence was broken. ' For a moment there I thought you would give away the game Eva.' His voice rang out softly, the tones almost threatening in each spoken syllable. He leaned back his hands clasping at the fingers and resting idly against his chest, whilst he pursed his lips a moment and spoke again.' I hope I need not remind you what would happen should you try to rebel against me.' Glacier colored eyes flashed at him, fury seething in the glittering pools as she responded with tightly reined anger. ' Nay Lestatt I know well what would happen should I try to betray you. You take glee in driving the point in as much as you take glee in twisting people to your will, or impaling a wayward man upon your sword.' She fell silent a moment, her heart a thunderous roar in her sensitive ears. With a heavy sigh she closed her eyes, then opened them again to peer at him as she asked' What is it you want?' The smile that etched along his lips sent a cold chill up her spine. It was dark, sadistic, and cruel in every way as his eyes lit up with his well hidden malice whilst he leaned forward and murmured in dark tones.' It is time we begin the next phase of this plan. Roberto must be well loved by all his people, but his lady even more so. I will help you to win them all. When we have them, it will be then, that Roberto's fall from grace shall happen. Inevitably ending in his demise, and my stepping up as the new king by their cherished queen’s side.' Disgust caused her stomach to twist into a knot sorely wishing to empty its contents, yet she squashed the sensation. Her eyes fell away, staring into the candle's flame, as if entranced before she whispered in a soft heart felt plea. ' Please do not make me do this. Roberto is a good man.' As the plea was swallowed by the silence that followed she squeezed her eyes shut in attempt to dam the tears that threatened to spill. She could hear the rustling of his coat as he made motion. Her face flinched back as her head turned, at the feel of his wretched touch. Her eyes came up to look up at him mutinously, as he grasped her chin between strong fingers forcing her gaze to his as he droned. ' Ah but my love, he is not the man for this kingdom. I know this is difficult for you understand.' His statement continued in softer, seducing sounds as his other hand came up to caress her cheek, and strum through her rich platinum locks. ' I promise you once this is done. Your life will be easier.' She fairly choked on the tears that were held at bay as she fought the urge to run. Her voice trembled, with sorrow as she lowered her gaze in shame. ' You are not the man I once knew. The man I loved...' She wanted her skin to crawl, she wanted to continue to hate him, but like so many, she felt herself fall prey to his charisma, and haunting charm. She relaxed; her cheek actually pressing into his hand for more of the false affection like a cat seeking the attention of it's human. Her mind screamed the warnings, over and over again, but her body was a traitorous vessel, arching into his tainted touch. A single tear fell free, his thumb lifting and brushing it aside as he came forward and laid a surreal kiss upon her quivering lips. A small sound of anguished surrender was lost in the kiss as she gave herself to him yet again, like so many times in the past, unable to break the spell he had woven about her being.... Pale cerulean eyes jerked open as Eva arched upwards to sit in her bed. She could still feel the pressure of his touch on her skin. His kiss upon her lips. Shuddering violently the daunted diva reached out and flipped on her light. Slender palms would rub briskly along her face as she took a heaved breath trying in vain to shake it off. Already she knew what this dream was about. Biting back the urge to scream she slowly pulled aside her bed covers and allowed graceful feet to touch upon the floor. A robe was pulled over that nearly bare form and tied shut as she left the confines of her room and headed for the elevator. Pressing the down button she anxiously watched the numbers rise until they had reached her floor at last. Stepping inside the barefooted goddess spun and compressed the first floor option. A yawn would emit from her lips, hand rising in effort to stifle it as the compartment met the first floor. Without regard for the strange looks she was receiving due to her rather odd attire, Eva marched over to the front desk and rang the service bell. ' Yes ma'am?' ' I need some stationary and an envelope if you would be so kind.' ' Absolutely Ms. VanLorne.' The desk clerk disappeared and Eva turned to lean back against the desk elbows perched as she watched those who were busy watching her. A voice clearing behind her caused that platinum crowned head to turn over shoulder and gaze at the offered supplies. 'Will this suffice?' ' Yes that will do fine. Thank you.' Eva took the offered paper and headed back upstairs to her room. Once inside the privacy of her 'chambers' the robe would be shed and that woman would sit at the small desk provided to begin penning a letter. ' Dear Mary...' It felt like the letter took forever to write. Turning to peer at her clock she growled lowly. It was going on seven am already. It had been at least an hour. Frowning she put down the pen and read her words to Lestatt's woman. Satisfied, she nodded and signed her name. She was doing the right thing wasn't she? Who knew.... all she knew is that she wouldn't play second best. If she couldn't have all of him, then she would give all him back to Mary and move on. It was a first in history. Eva didn’t like to lose.
