Squeaky Clean

20 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/20/11 The scene opens up with Toga sitting on a couch watching a teley-visi in what appears to be a local hotel. On the screen is his debut match from WorldWide 145. There is the sound of running water coming from the bathroom. Tommy keeps pausing it as his squeaky hammer strikes a foe, then rewinds it, plays it, pauses again, then rinses and repeats. He seems to be studying it. Maybe, just maybe, he'll be able to wrap his tiny lil' brain around why his squeaky hammer might not have worked.... He seems completely stumped. The running water stops. Toga looks down at the coffee table directly in front of him, where the squeaky hammer that he stol-.... borr-......... bought and paid for... lays before him. He picks it up and eyes it like it's the world's finest, tastiest gummy bear, and while you want to eat it, you don't want to ruin the amazing craftsmanship of the bear shape.... or some other random analogy. The bathroom door opens up and Lollie walks out with two towels. One around her hair, and one around her unmentionables that were just subsequently mentioned. She glances down at her client, who pays his geekily-attractive female cohort no attention whatsoever. Lollie shifts her eyes to the paused monitor, then back to Tommy, with the hammer in his hands still. She sighs. Lollie: "Forget it, Tommy. You're never gonna figure out why that hammer didn't work on them.... just as I'll never know why it DID work on you..." Toga slowly sets the hammer back on the table, so as to not break the table.... yeah.... his mind works that way. He reluctantly draws his focus from the hammer to Lollie. Toga: "But if Theo won't teach me the secrets of the Super Poke, then I must learn the secrets of the Squeaky Hammer!" Lollie starts rubbing her hair with the upper towel. Toga picks up the remote to the TeeVee again, and resumes his futile search for nonsensical secrets. Lollie: "You have bigger things to be focusing on this week... You have a potential chance to win the Hardcore championship!" She drapes the towel over the back of an armchair. She adjusts the towel around her torso, then walks directly in front of Toga to the dresser. She pulls out a drawer and starts rummaging through it. She pulls out various articles of clothing, then scampers to the kitchen, where she hides from the camera, behind the bar. If Toga chose to, he could get a full view, but the cameraman remains a gentleman, and the asexual amoeba on the couch remains an... asexual amoeba... She pokes her head over the counter a couple times, to make sure the camera's not peaking. Unfortunately, it's not. Lollie: "Tommy, if you manage to pin Mayhem, you'll not only earn your first win, but you'll win a championship with it! Things really couldn't be much better for you, considering you are Oh-and-One." Tommy slowly glances over... Ooooooooooooooo..... but Lollie is finished. She walks out of the kitchen wearing green, Yoshi pajama pants and a white tanktop. She sits next to him. Toga: ".... who is Mayhem?" The gal in green slaps her forehead. Lollie: "I'm obviously not doing my job right if you don't even know who the champions are..." Toga: "I like championships. They're shiny!" She nods then takes the remote from him. She points it at the teley, and fast forwards to the next match. She points at the screen. Lollie: "That is Mayhem." Tommy stares at it for a bit, before a smirk stretches across his thin face. Toga: "He's got a funny nose!" Lollie: "Mmhmm. Though I think he looks funny altogether...." Toga: "What's "Tug Life?"" She looks at Toga blankly, then back to the TV, then back to Toga. Lollie: "That's not Mayhem... That's Hot Mask." Toga: "Does he have a sidekick named Hot Pockets?" Lollie: "Oh... if only....... Wait.... No! Stop paying attention to him. Pay attention to the clown with dreadlocks!" Tommy starts to watch the match from WW 145. His ADHD starts to kick in, as his nose starts to twitch. He slowly pulls his attention from the TV to Lollie. She glances at him through her peripheral view. Toga: "You smell like watermelons. Big... juicy... ripe... watermelons...." The gal in green quickly stands up. Her speech is fast and obviously uncomfortable. Lollie: "Oooookay... well, I'm tired. Goodnight, Tommy...." She rushes into the bedroom, covering her chest. Toga: "But what about the watermelons?!!" The bedroom door slams, as Tommy sighs and reluctantly goes back to watching the circus on TeeVee...
