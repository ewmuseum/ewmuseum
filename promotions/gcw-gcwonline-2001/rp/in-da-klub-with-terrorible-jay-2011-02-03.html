In Da Klub With Terrorible Jay

3 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/03/11 (The scene fades from a black screen to one with lots of flashy lights. There are people dancing on poles, people dancing on tables, people dancing on people, and between them all is the one and only Jay Terror. He has a cigar in his mouth and he’s smoking it to the face like a boss. On each knee is a fine ass bitch. Both the bitches have a bottle of Patron and they’re pouring it into the world champion’s mouth and making out with him.) ~**! Man with AWESOME ED HARDY Shirt On !**~ Yo Jay, this VIP shit is sweet bro. ~**! Man with NO Shirt On !**~ Yeah bro montana, It’s like I’m the situation dude. Just drinking all night and getting laid in the club yo. (Jay Terror smokes his cigar. The girls fight to kiss him again but Jay ignores it as he sees the cameraman coming his way. Jay Terror pushes the bitches off his lap and wipes the Patron off his v-neck shirt.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Where the hell you been, man?! I thought I told you to be here a half hour ago. Don’t you know who I am? ~**! Cameraman !**~ I’m sorry Mr. Terror. There was traffic and... ~**! Jay Terror !**~ That’s because you’re not Jay Terror. Ain’t no traffic when I’m cruising the streets. You getting my face right? How’s the lighting? ~**! Cameraman !**~ It’s colorful. ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Like good colorful or like this shit’s gonna’ make me look whack? I can’t be looking whack in a promo broseph. ~**! Cameraman !**~ My name is Pete, not broseph. ~**! Jay Terror !**~ It doesn’t matter what your name is! (Jay Terror grabs the camera lens and pulls it close to his face.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Let’s get this shit done with. It’s all about the bottles n’ models and you’re messing with my swag bromeo. (He shoves the cameraman back and takes a big gulp from the bottle of Patron. After that, he fixes his designer sunglasses and shrugs a few times to warm up for his interview.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ What’s up Jersey?! Yeah, I’m in the nation’s armpit with the Jersey Bois. If you’ll remember, they were one of the greatest stables to never wrestle a match in GCW. Now they’re dancing it up VIP style with the one and only Jay Terror. You know why? Because I’m the world heavyweight champion and I get whoever I want in wherever I want. That’s just how we roll. (He nods because he’s confident.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ The fact is, I’m just a bad mother fucker and these tan shits know that no matter how many days they go to the gym, they’ll be doing their laundry again and again after messing with me. Because I scare the shit out of people. Take for instance my match last week. Don’t matter how drunk I get because I’ll still crack skulls and take names. It’s what I’m all about and if you don’t like it then you can get the fuck out. Turns out that Garbage Bag Johnny wants to make my life hard though and he’s booking me in a match this week against one of the no name idiots that I beat last week. Last week I wiped the floor with his pathetic ass and Johnny wants to stick him in the ring with me again? Haha, that unfortunate little bitch. (The Jersey Bois slap high fives in the background as they grind up on some fine ass hoes.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ You see, I’ve done my research on Alex whatever his name is. Turns out that his family is one of the most famous ones in the history of our sport. Hell, they’ve been in this business since it first started it sounds like. I’d like to make one thing clear though, their legacy and your background doesn’t give you any advantages. I realize that you are trying to make your own name in this game and you’re trying to prove that you can do things without dad, but let’s be honest: it was the name that got you in the door. No matter how hard you try to be different, you’ll always be their kid and you’ll always get your chances based on their history. ~**! Dan “The Dude” Mazzerati !**~ Daddy’s boy! ~**! Andy “The Party” De Luca !**~ Kid’s gon’ get smashed by my boy! ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Of course, you could make your big break through this week though by beating me. I mean, a win against Jay Terror is something that your mom and dad never did, mostly because they couldn’t. I don’t want to get your hopes up though. The problem with facing me is that I simply don’t care whether you stay or go. No matter how noble your cause is and how badly you want to make it, I’m not going to let you strut your shit if it’s going to make me look weak. I have a reputation to uphold and if you didn’t know, I’ve created it through shear brutality and dominance. I’ve broken arms, legs, faces, spirits, and anything else you can think of. I’ve ended careers and don’t think that I’m above finishing yours before it can get started. (Jay Terror grins like an evil genius.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Currently you’re holding onto a defeated record after losing to me last week. While I started on the same foot, there’s a difference between me and you. I had a true will to win and I understood what it took to do so. I didn’t care if I had to sell my soul to go places and you..........well I don’t think you have that same passion. Unfortunately, it’s not something that you can fix either. You see, after years of learning from your parents and hearing about their accolades, you’re set in your ways. Change is just an idea, not something you can grab and run with. Trust me, I’ve been around the block enough. Still, I’m feeling like a generous guy tonight so how about I give you some pointers on saving your career. (Jay Terror takes another drink of Patron.) ~**! Shane “The Muscles” Rossi !**~ Don’t be a bitch. That’s all you gotta’ know, kid! ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Don’t fuck with me he meant. After this match, I hope to never see your face again. If you survive, stay away and you’ll already be on your way up the ladder. Next, don’t tell us how you’re different than your family. No one cares and no one believes you. Instead, do something special and prove to people that you’re more than that schmuck dad of yours. I don’t mean fighting me, I mean take it to the guys like Andy Murray, Vivica Valentine, and anyone else the crowd likes and destroy them. That’s how you turn heads. Finally, don’t pass up a deal with the devil. That’s all I gotta’ say, hermano. ~**! Cameraman !**~ That’s it? ~**! Jay Terror !**~ Did I say you could talk, you dumb shit?! ~**! Cameraman !**~ You said finally. ~**! Jay Terror !**~ I’ll be done when I say I’m done, chump. ~**! Ben “The Tits” Moretti !**~ He being a problem, Jay? I’ll take his whack ass out, duders. ~**! Josh “Gin and Juice” Rizzo !**~ We’ll give him the shakedown if he’s gonna’ disrespect you, broseidon. ~**! Jay Terror !**~ I think he’ll shut up bois. Keep up your dancing. (The Jersey Bois don’t need to be told what to do and never stopped smacking ass or chugging bottles.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ You see Alexander, the key to the game is commanding the people around you. With power comes respect and with respect comes dominance. I want you to remember all of this because after I finish you off, I want you to take it all, roll it up, and smoke it to the face until it sinks in. If you do, I can promise that you’ll make it somewhere and maybe after I retire as champion, you could even take over where I leave off. Maybe. (Jay Terror stops and waves for his bitches to come back. They’re making out against the railing but break it up at his call. They take to his knees and Jay kisses both of them.) ~**! Cameraman !**~ That it? (Jay Terror parts the women and flashes him an evil stare.) ~**! Jay Terror !**~ That’s a god damn wrap Broseph Stalin. Get the hell out of here before I have to get my Jersey Bois after you. (The Jersey Bois start to fist pump and collect in the background as Deadmau5 starts to get louder and louder. The cameraman doesn’t wait to find out what they have in mind for him and the scene fades to black.)
