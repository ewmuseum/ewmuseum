Pawn :: Chapter 3

16 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 16, 2008 If you can imagine one of the loneliest and depressing places on Earth, then just for a moment, put yourself in that place, you might come close to understanding what Kaden has been through for the last four months. Despite the fact that he was angry to no ends, even Kaden couldn't imagine being locked up inside of a tiny box of a room to be as bad as it was. There were no daily doctor visits, no medication delivered in tiny cups, and the only time the door to his "room" had even been open was when they tossed his ass inside back in December. The only way he's even been able to keep track of the days are by the regular intervals in which the little slot on his door opens, and his meals are given to him; if not for that, he surely could have easily given up his grip on reality, and let go of what little bit of sanity he had left. He is stuck in a situation that would make anyone go crazy, let alone the fact that he is already considered being mentally unstable. Needless to say, going from a life of daily exercise, three healthy meals, and the company of those he loves, or at one point did love, to the situation he finds himself in now, there are more than just a few things wrong with him since the last time everyone who cares has seen him. But today, well today is going to be the first glance at "change" that Kaden will get to be apart of in over four months. What might go wrong during the first conversation he gets to have with anyone since December? The question that should be asked, and more easily answered, is, "What could possibly go right?" ******* As the lights came on inside of his room, much like they did every morning around six thirty, Kaden was already sitting up in bed, waiting for the little slot in his door to open, and a tray of less than desirable food to be slid through for breakfast. This was not the case, however, as a knock came to his door, startling him, a familiar voice called to him from the other side, but for him, the familiarity of that voice was not welcomed. "Good morning Seven Twenty-Seven." Shaking his head, Kaden ignored the voice coming through his "food hole", while still noticing the slightly wrinkled face of one Dr. Damian Sorrow. "Come a little closer here, so we can talk." There was no movement from Kaden as he continued to sit up in bed, that of which he had already made on that morning, his arms crossed and his eyes now fixated on the wall. "I know you must be upset Seven Twenty-Seven, and I know we put off starting your treatment too long. But you see, we are very busy people around here." Not that the words meant anything to Kaden, but something about Dr. Sorrow's higher pitched voice got under Kaden's skin. You could see it on his face, more so as he started to bite his tongue, trying not to open his mouth. "If you do not work with me, then we cannot treat you properly." Damian continued. "I have some news that you just might enjoy Seven Twenty-Se..." There was no hesitation from Kaden at this point as he launched himself off his bed, and quickly got to the door on his knees. Damian had no time to react as Kaden's hand grabbed his collar through the slot in the door. The slot itself was not big enough for his whole arm, but Damian was close enough that Kaden could grab him without even having to get half of his forearm through. "I have a name!" With fiery eyes, Kaden exclaimed. "I suggest you start using it, or this just isn't going to work for either of us." Letting go of Damian's collar, and getting to his feet, Kaden walked over and grabbed the lone chair in his room, and carried it over by the door before setting it down, and sitting on it. "Now I don't know where you get off, or what other crazy bastards you got running around, but I was given a name when I was born, and I prefer to hear that when someone is talking to me." He finished. Silence was deafening for nearly a minute or two, but the "good" doctor finally gulped down the mouthful of saliva that had built up while he was shocked into silence. "OK, Christopher..." Once again, Damian was interrupted. Throwing the chair backwards, Kaden kneeled down to get face to face with Dr. Sorrow through the slot in the door. "You can call me Kaden." He stated. "And for fucks sake, can I get out of this damn room for once?" The desperation in voice could be heard with this question, and even a bit of a concerned look on his face. "I'm tired of being locked up like a lab rat." Kaden said, standing up and starting to pace back and fourth. "I need exercise, I need something!" Felling Kaden had paused long enough for him to actually speak this time, Dr. Sorrow tried to continue. "Well, then you will be happy to hear what I have to tell..." As if he was just waiting for Damian to speak to interrupt him, Kaden did it again. Not looking too happy about it, Damian stopped what he was saying without trying to forcefully finish his statement. "Do you realize that you just took me out of obligations that I've signed a contract over?" Obviously asking him about GLOBAL, Kaden's voice is now full of concern. "I hit a slump there before you pricks took me away from it, but damn it, you have to let me at least continue wrestling if anything!" Kaden exclaimed. Rubbing his eyes, as if to prolong the news he's waited months to tell Kaden, Dr. Sorrow repositions the thing framed spectacles on his face. "Kaden, that is part of the news you are not going to like." He admitted. "GLOBAL closed down just weeks after you were admitted here." Before he could even finish that last sentence, Kaden had already began a rampage, hitting and throwing anything he could find inside of the small space he'd been confined to. "You mean you couldn't have told me this months ago?!" Asking the question at an incredible volume, Dr. Sorrow scrunched his face slightly, startled just slightly by Kaden's tone. "So what the Hell are you going to do about this?" Kaden continued to question. "I mean, you lock me up here without any kind of evidence to my behavior than word of mouth, and then don't even bother to examine me to find out if I belong here!" Taking a deep breath, Kaden continues. "Now you tell me my job is gone four months later?!" He finished. As Kaden glanced back through the "food hole", he noticed a smile plastered on Damian's face. "Now now Kaden, you must realize that I still have the good news to give you." Dr. Sorrow got up from the floor where he was sitting to look through the sliding door, and he starts to pace back and fourth, Kaden was the one now on the floor looking through the hole. "You see, I think I have found a perfect way to start your treatment and examine you." Damian began. "I am all for experimental studies, and I feel that, in your unique predicament, the only way to examine you properly would be to put you back into your natural everyday life." Pausing for a moment to let some of the information soak in, Damian continued. "Of course, you will continue to stay here, but once a week we are going to supervise you in the place you feel most comfortable, the wrestling ring." This news should have come as good news for Kaden; however, he did not treat it as such, flying off the handle. "How do you expect o be able to do that when GLOBAL is now closed, you son of a bitch?" Kaden asked, moving back to his bed. "I can't necessarily just find somewhere else to wrestle when you've got me locked up in this Hell hole!" Staring back at the wall, Kaden exclaimed. Damian, still pacing back and fourth outside of the door, only had one thing to comment on. "Well Kaden, then you should be so lucky that Steven Caldera is a generous man." Without hesitation, Damian walked off, leaving Kaden to think about what just happened with a surprised look on his face.
