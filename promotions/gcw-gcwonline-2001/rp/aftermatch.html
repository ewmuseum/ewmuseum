Date: A while later The night was cool, a stark contrast to the earlier heat of the day. A western wind blew, bringing with it the salt scent of the ocean. He sat on the ground with the outer wall of the hospital pressed against his back, and his legs stretched out in front of him. A cigarette hung between his lips, as he stared upwards towards the night sky. The lights of the city blotted out most of the starlight, but if he tried really hard, he could see a few scattered across the air. He felt lower than he had in ages, everything had just boiled over in those horrible moments and he’d completely lost control. The thought of what he’d done made him sick, he could still feel bones cracking under his feet. No matter how hard he tried the memory wouldn’t go away, and instead kept floating to the surface. A cruel reminder of what he was capable of when pushed too far. Jeff, Cindy’s husband had taken immediate control of the situation after having checked on Lance. That was a blessing because Stevens had basically shut down. It took Alex and Jeff working together just to get Michael to his feet and into the car. They’d sped the entire way to the hospital, thankfully they hadn’t been stopped. Jeff had said every second was going to count. After they’d gotten him to the hospital, Alex had driven Sheena home, while Cindy, Jeff, and Stevens had stayed behind for any word on Lance. The initial prognosis was hopeful, he was unconscious but there was a good chance he’d wake up. The biggest complication to matters where the drugs in his system, they retarded his body’s ability to cope with the savagery of the beating. The doctor had questioned them as to what had happened, Stevens had been ready to answer. He needed to confess, hopefully the doctor would call the police, and he’d end up in jail, that’s nothing but what he deserved. He’d never gotten the chance though, Jeff stepped up with a story about how they’d all gone inside to get the table while Lance had stayed outside to finish his cigarette. After he didn’t come inside for quite a while, his sister had gone to check on him, and found him a bloody mess in the parking lot. They’d all come out to rush him to the hospital. She was understandably shaken, and was taken home. A shadow fell over where he sat, looking up he saw Cindy, her face a mask of concern as she looked down at him. Clearly she was exhausted, dark circles already forming underneath her eyes. “Are you ok Mike?” It seemed like the most absurd question under the night sky. How could he possibly be ok, after what he’d done? Besides what did his condition matter anyway, he wasn’t lying in a hospital bed, after being beaten to a bloody pulp. Still she was obviously concerned, and he just didn’t have anything left to be angry at her for the question. He was too drained, almost empty. “I’m numb. I don’t know what the fuck happened Cindy. I just wanted to talk to him, and I did that.” “Yes, yes you did.” “Is he going to be alright” Any news? I can’t stop thinking about it, he was crying Cindy, begging me to stop, and I just kept kicking him. I’m a fucking monster.” “No, you’re not a monster Michael. You were just pushed too far, lost control. Jeff, actually wants to talk to you about that, he thinks you may need some counseling.” “Fuck that, I can’t stand shrinks. But Lance, is there any news?” He hung his head again looking down at the concrete between his knees. The movement caused the ash of his cigarette to fall free down to his chest. It was almost completely burned away, he didn’t even notice the cherry was dangerously close to his lips. Under normal circumstances he’d feel the pain, but tonight he couldn’t feel anything. Cindy plucked the butt from his mouth and tossed it away, before putting one arm around his shoulder and squeezing. “We don’t know much of anything yet. He’s got some broken ribs, and at very least a concussion. The doctors are saying he could wake up in a few hours, or a few days. We really just don’t know anything at all right now. But they’re hopeful.” “And if he doesn’t wake up that means I killed the woman I love’s brother. What the fuck am I supposed to tell her Cindy” How do you say you’re sorry for stomping the life out of someone’s brother? I just don’t know how to deal with any of this. I’ve hurt people before, that’s part of my job, we all know the risks, and we’re professionals. I’ve never done anything like this, and I just don’t know how to handle it.” “Well, you’ll tell Sheena, exactly what we told the doctor. I doubt she’ll remember much of anything that happened tonight anyway. Jeff, and I agreed we’re going to try and protect you on this Michael. Alex will be on board too, you know he’ll do anything for you.” That was just too much. He’d nearly killed a man, and he had people lining up to keep the blame off him. He couldn’t understand why they’d do something like that for him. Jeff was putting his career on the line, Cindy pretty much the same, they could both be risking jail time for trying to cover this up, and neither seemed to care. Why were they protecting him, he was the last person in the world that deserved to be protected? Bringing a hand up to his forehead, he felt hot tears stinging at his eyes. Twice in one night he was going to cry again. He felt wretched, nothing he could imagine was worse than feeling like this. “Why” You shouldn’t be helping me, I don’t deserve?” He could hardly speak the words came out as gasping breaths. Cindy gathered him more in her arms and held him, hand stroking the back of his hair. She cooed soft noises into his ear trying to calm him. “Because you’re family, and I love you. You’re a good person Michael you just made a mistake. And we’re going to help you get through it. No one’s going to look too deeply into this anyway. The kid’s a junkie, and violence is just a part of that world.” He couldn’t remember a time when he’d heard her speak like this. Cindy would usually think almost exclusively with her heart, and now she seemed to be coldly dispassionate. It was almost frightening. What was worse is he knew that for all her dispassion in speaking about Lance, she did what she was doing for love of him. He supposed those family bonds went deeper than he’d ever thought they could. Pushing away from her he slowly stood. Turning his back he began to pace, for the first time trying to look at everything logically. Much of what she said rang true, but he wasn’t sure if he could follow through with it. He’d always been a good liar, but the weight of that had constantly prayed on him. To do what she was suggesting he’d have to lie to the one person he cared about most in the world. How could he look her in the eye after lying to her about something this big? “But how am I supposed to look at her after that, Cindy” How can I face her knowing that not only am I responsible for her brother being in the hospital, but that I lied to her about it?” “I don’t know Michael, maybe you should think a little more about the counseling. Jeff said he’d be willing to talk to you whenever you want to.” “I don’t know, I’ll think about it. I’ve got a lot to think about right now.” “I know, I’m sorry Michael.” “Yeah, me too.” ~Fin~
