Bouncin' Back (Part 6)

22 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/22/2005 [The camera fades into the patio of a small open air caf? in downtown New York City. The sun is shining brightly in the sky. The citizens of New York can be seen walking about the streets enthralled in the usual hustle and bustle of their daily lives. All is well on another typical day in the city. The camera pans around the scene and catches sight of all the customers of the caf? sitting at their individual tables enjoying their lunches. The camera continues to pan around the scene and finally stops and zooms in as it catches sight of Reno Banks, The Agent of the Stars, sipping on a cup of coffee and reading a newspaper. He finally sets the newspaper down, reaches under the table and whips out his briefcase.] Reno Banks: Alright. Now let's take a look at the paperwork. [Reno shakes his head in disappointment.] Reno Banks: Its a shame Mr. Jones came up short against that Ricky Thunder. I told him he's gotta be on top of his game; but, who listens to me? I'm just the lowly Agent of the Stars. [Reno Banks opens the briefcase, whips out a small envelope, rips it open, and quickly begins looking over the flier that is inside.] Reno Banks: GCW WorldWide coming from Calgary, Alberta, Canada this week. How quaint. [He turns up his nose with an expression of disgust at the thought.] Reno Banks: Couldn't they pick a better venue than Canada? It might be frigid up there. Mr. Jones and Miss Jasmine aren't gonna be too thrilled with the trip. Oh well. Now what does WorldWide have in store for Mr. Jones this week? Chrissy-Poo versus Dimensioner. Eh...who are these people? [chuckles to himself and shake his head] Chrissy-Poo?? Come on. [Reno slowly regains his composure and goes back to scouring the card listing.] Reno Banks: Okay. Next match on the card. Ummmm... Donny Diamond versus Coyle O'Conner. [frowns at the thought of Diamond and shakes his head disapprovingly] That damn Donny Diamond. What's after that match? Angel vs. Ricky Thunder. Hmph! That's of no importance. Next...Rei Tiu versus Zodiac, never been a big fan of the cruiserweights. [Reno Banks starts to appear a bit worried as he continues reading on.] Reno Banks: Well, maybe GCW has realized my clients value and decided to make him a headliner. Alright. We have Mike Savige versus Meatshield Trainwrecker in a TV Title match. [Reno raises an eyebrow at the crazy name and continues reading.] Reno Banks: Okay...next match is...Seymour Almasy vs. Trey Tremendous. [Reno's anxiety continues to build as he scours the card up and down frantically.] Reno Banks: Submission Showdown between Michael Stevens and Jason. Wait a second. LeStatt Knight versus Phyl?? Hold on! Something has to be wrong here. [Reno continues to scan the card listing in horror at the absence of Vince Jones' name.] Reno Banks: Okay...we have a problem here. [Reno Banks smiles nervously as he sets the bulletin down to the side. He rests his ringed hand on the table and begins pecking on it with his finger in fear. Thoughts of his fear begin to flood through his mind like a tidal wave.] 'Alright, Reno. What are we going to do now? I mean we could call Vince right now and let him know what's going on. Yeah. We could do that...' [Reno starts smiling and nodding his head in approval of his proposal.] '...but then there's always the possibility that Mr. Jones just might flip out. I mean we're talking about 'Mr. Temper Mental' himself here...' [The smile slowly fades away from his face to be replaced by a worried one.] '...Yeah. Mr. Jones does tend to blow things out of proportion. There's no telling what he might do when he finds out the bad news. Well, I guess I can't keep this a secret forever. He'll find out sooner or later...' [Reno lowers his head and shakes it back and forth in shame. He suddenly lifts his head up and smiles.] Reno Banks: Ahhh...but I can postpone the news for a more fitting time. He can find out, just not right now. [Reno tries to regain his composure as he gets back to working on his cup of coffee and newspaper.] Meanwhile in another part of the city? [The camera fades in and catches sight of the front door of an apartment. The sound of a key being inserted into the doorknob and unlocking it from the outside can be heard. The doorknob turns and slowly opens revealing Jasmine, The Crown of Jewel of Wrestling, stepping inside with a smile on her face. Her smile slowly vanishes from her face as she catches sight of the apartment. She spots chairs in the den flipped on their sides. She sees the shattered glass of the big screen t.v scattered all about the floor as if it had been smashed in by force. Ripped up papers and magazines can be seen littered all around the vicinity. A broken lamp can be seen laying on the floor as well. Jasmine steps inside and closes the door behind herself in shock at the messy state of the apartment.] Jasmine: V, are you here? Are you home, V? [Jasmine tiptoes her way across the debris that has been scattered all about the room and finally reaches the hallway. She nearly trips over the lamp and continues on her way down the hallway. She finally turns towards what appears to be the room of Vince Jones. The blinds are barely cracked and small streaks of light dance across the walls and bathe Vince in a dim light. He sits there with his head down as he just stares at a small photograph that he holds in his hand. A single tear appears to trickle down his cheek as he sits there in silence.] Jasmine: V, are you alright? [Vince Jones quickly awakens from his trance and raises his head spotting Jasmine in the doorway looking back at him. He quickly rises to his feet, tosses the picture down to his side, and rushes the door in fury.] Vince Jones: Get the hell away from here!!! [He quickly slams the door shut and locks it. Jasmine takes a step back and looks at the closed door in confusion. She stands there in silence for a moment and then knocks on the door softly.] Vince Jones: What the hell do you want? Jasmine: Are you alright in there? Is something the matter? Vince Jones: V needs time to himself! Aiight? Jasmine: Do you need someone to talk to? Vince Jones: No!!! There’s nothin? to talk about! Now leave me the hell alone! [Jasmine takes a step back from the door in defeat. She turns back around and goes back down the hallway to the den. She walks over to the couch, brushes aside some of the torn and scattered papers and sits there in silence. She then decides to pick up the phone and call Reno Banks.] Back at the Cafe... [Reno can be seen sipping on his coffee and peacefully reading the newspaper. All of a sudden his cellphone begins ringing, startling him a bit.] Reno Banks: Uh-oh! Who's this? [Reno whips out his cellphone and sees the name 'Vince Jones' on the screen. Reno takes a deep breath and finally answers the phone.] Reno Banks: Uh....hello?? [Reno pulls the phone away fro his ear and lets out a sigh of relief.] Reno Banks: Its just Jasmine. Thank God! [Reno puts the phone back up to his ear.] Reno Banks: So, Miss Jasmine, how are you? [The camera switches to a split view of the cafe where Reno is and Vince Jones' apartment where Jasmine is.] Jasmine: I'm doing fine. Reno Banks: And what about, Mr. Jones? [Jasmine turns towards the hallway leading to Vince's room.] Jasmine: Well...that's what I why wanted to talk to you, Reno. See, V hasn't been taking that loss to Ricky Thunder it seems. He's been extremely moody. The only thing that I can think of that might get him going again would be a win. He needs a chance to redeem himself in the ring. So, I was kinda wondering if you by chance knew who he'd be facing this week at WorldWide because he really needs something to keep him occupied at the moment before he goes off the deep end. [Reno smiles nervously.] Reno Banks: I see... Jasmine: So, do you have anything for us? [Reno begins scratching his head a bit nervously and looking around hoping for an idea to hit him.] Reno Banks: Well...Uh...is Mr. Jones anywhere nearby? [Jasmine looks around and shakes her head.] Jasmine: No. Reno Banks: Alright. Here's the deal. It turns out that Mr. Jones hasn't been booked for this week's show. [Jasmine's jaw drops in surprise.] Jasmine: V doesn't have a match this week?? [Reno's eyes pop open wide in horror.] Reno Banks: Not so loud! Not so loud, Miss Jasmine! I don't wanna get him upset. Vince Jones: WHAT??? [Jasmine whirls around in surprise as a loud bang can be heard down the hallway as Vince whips the door open in fury.] Vince Jones: V. Jones ain't got a match this week?? Jasmine: [to Reno] Toooooooo late, Reno. [Vince comes storming down the hallway towards Jasmine.] Vince Jones: V ain't got a match?? [Jasmine shakes her head 'no' and Vince rolls his eyes.] Vince Jones: Give me that damn phone! V gotta holla at the b*tch, Reno, for a sec here! Someone obviously ain't takin' care of V's sh*t here! [Vince snatches the phone out of Jasmine's hand and slaps it up beside his ear.] Vince Jones: RENO!!!! [The view switches to the cafe where Reno can be seen holding the phone away from his ear as Vince can be heard ranting and raving like a madman on the other end. The noise finally ceases and Reno puts the phone back up to his ear.] Reno Banks: Well, if it makes you feel any better, Mr. Jones. I'm sorry. I tried my best to help, but I just couldn't make any progress this week. [The camera switches to Vince's apartment where he can be seen pacing back and forth as Jasmine watches from a distance. Vince stops in his tracks and laughs to himself.] Vince Jones: Oh, so you sorry. You sorry. So, you think that tellin' V that sh*t's gonna suddenly make everything better? Is that it, Reno? You think that if you apologize V's gonna suddenly be like 'Okay. It's all good?' Well, let V tell ya what he thinks of your damn apologizin' ass... [The camera switches back to the cafe scene where Reno once again pulls the phone away from his ear as quickly as possible as Vince flips out and begins ranting and raving once again. He slowly brings the phone closer to his ear as Vince continues yelling on the other end.] Reno: Mr. Jones...Mr. Jones calm down for a second. I have an idea. Mr. Jones, take a deep breath in...and out. Mr. Jones follow me now breath in...and out. [The camera switches to a split screen view once again as Vince can be seen breathing in and out. As he does so he slowly begins to calm down and regain his composure.] Reno Banks: Alright. Are you feeling a little better now? Vince Jones: [nodding] Aiight. V cool. Reno Banks: Now listen. Closely, Mr. Jones. Yes, you may have lost to Ricky Thunder... [Vince rolls his eyes and begins clenching the phone a little bit tighter.] Vince Jones: He slid by! [Reno nods in agreement.] Reno Banks: Yes. He slid by. That's correct, my error. Anyways, I think it would be a good idea to see this as an opportunity, Vince. So, you're not wresting this week. Big deal. I see this as an opportunity to scan the GCW scene. Its an opportunity to check everything out from a different perspective. You could attend GCW WorldWide this week and observe... [Vince Jones shakes his head.] Vince Jones: V ain't followin' ya there, Reno! V ain't a spectata! He a fighta! Reno Banks: I understand that, Mr. Jones. You're a fighter and you will always be a fighter, but sometimes even a fighter has to take a step back and take in the view. Sometimes you gotta be patient to make it in this business, Mr. Jones. Look, I'll book you a flight. If you're willing to go you'll be heading out for Calgary... Vince Jones: Calgary? Reno Banks: Yes, Calgary, Mr. Jones. Vince Jones: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Calgary,as in Calgary, Alberta, Canada? Reno Banks: [nodding] Yes, that Calgary. [Vince Jones snarls at the thought of the trip.] Vince Jones: V hates Calgary! Reno Banks: Yeah. I figured you wouldn't be too thrilled with the trip, but I think the trip would be very beneficial for you. Vince Jones: If V heads all the way out to Calgary someone's gettin' their skull smashed in for sure. Reno Banks: Mr. Jones, no! Don't cause any disturbances, just lay low. That's all I ask. I want you to go there, lay low, and just observe. Patience can take you a lot further than you think, Mr. Jones. [Vince Jones begins breathing in and out heavily in frustration.] Reno Banks: I know this will be hard on you. You want some action and you want it now, but trust me. If you just remain patient and watch the scene you just might find your meal ticket out there somewhere. Get back to me first thing in the morning and let me know if you're willing to go and keep your cool. If so, I'll have someone pick you up and carry you to the airport. Ciao! [Reno hangs up the phone and the camera switches back to a regular view of Vince's apartment where he sets the phone back down on the hook and just shakes his head. Jasmine looks at him in confusion.] Jasmine: So, what did he say, V? Vince Jones: V may be goin' on a lil trip in the morning. Jasmine: [in surprise] A trip? Vince Jones: Yeah...a trip. [The camera slowly fades to black.]
