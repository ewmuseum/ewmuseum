60 Minutes: Violence in the Square Circle

10 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: march 10, 2001 'The world of professional wrestling has always excited and awed fans young and old. If you recall last week we did a segment on the popularity of backyard wrestling. Explosions, weapons, broken bones, and a lot of blood. Well this week we examine the level of professional wrestling as a backyard aspect. Bryan Mayhem, nicknamed by his fellow wrestlers as 'The Lunatic' has become a household name amongst many like these kids.' 'He's insane. He takes more pain than anyone I've ever seen.' 'He brought Satanism out of the underground.' 'Intense. He really tries to hurt people. Scares the hell out of me.' 'One word. BELMONT!' 'What the last child was just referring to was a match inside the oldest mansion in England. It was the original home of the world's very first female cannibal Elizabeth Bathory, known as 'The Blood Countess'. This home has been in Mayhem's family for years, and Bryan decided to bring his opponents into this very house for a match from hell.' -=The Grim Reaper, slowly and quietly walks through the foyer. This old house smells of death and decay. Where is he? He can sense him. Mayhem is near. The Reaper enters the kitchen and sets off some kind of trigger. A loud beep followed by an explosion. The freezer door stationed to his left blows open smashing The Reaper in the face dropping him to the ground. Blood begins to leak heavily from behind the mask....=- 'That was only a sample of what happened in that match. Once again the children watching the match live on PPV.' 'GREATEST MATCH I'VE EVER SEEN!!' 'I've never seen two people bleed so much in my entire life.' 'Mayhem spelling his name on the kitchen floor with the Reaper's blood was disgusting.' 'Needless to say Bryan Mayhem was the winner, but those who have entered this dark match have suffered many consequences The Grim Reaper (Russel Tory) wrestled his final match in that house. He is still dealing with complications due to the injuries. Syko (Matt Chapman) ended his career in this match. He suffered head trauma, and broken bones. He was not available for questioning. Reed (Paul Barker) also wrestled his final match here. He was paralyzed from the waist down after breaking his back being bodyslammed through the floor into the basement. Having to sleep with a breathing machine Mr. Barker regrets every second.' 'I have never experienced pain like I did in that match. The President of the Federation I was in at the time told me this match will put me on the map, especially if I win. Yes it put me on the map, but left me in this chair.' 'Do you feel Mr. O'Neil (Bryan Mayhem's legal name is Bryan O'Neil) felt any sorry or regret for what he did to you during this match.' 'None. None at all.' 'Why?' 'The look in his eyes. He should not be allowed to perform in the professional wrestling world. He's sick.' 'Why is it a man like this is allowed to perform in front of millions and millions of people each and every week. He's a practicing Satanist, and is out to inflict pain. Mr. Alex Walnut the former President of EHWF, a former employer of O'Neil, refused to speak to us. However, we did get some words from Bryan's younger brother and fellow wrestler Jason O'Neil who wrestles under the name JNightmare.' 'I can't speak for my brother. We do not speak at all.' 'Why is that?' 'Because I hate him. He's sick and insane. F**k him.' 'Jason walked off the set and would not return our calls. We decided to head to the source. We hear all this publicity about what the world calls 'a monster' and yet we haven't heard his side of the story. Is he misunderstood? Or by some weird reason are all the matches and careers ended just accidents? We'll see.' 'Bryan did sit down with us, and we did not realize his size. He was the largest man I have ever come across. The painted face did not help the cause either. I was intimidated, and scared. I've heard stories of him snapping, and becoming enraged and violent. What if I was to ask the wrong question and it sets him off? I don't think the security here is strong enough to hold this man from snapping my neck. But than I thought, wait he's not real, he's and entertainer. Isn't he?' 'Bryan, why this. Why not perform as a Hulk Hogan like man, fan favorite, and a do-gooder.' 'This is not a performance for me. This is real. I grew up being beaten and tortured by a sick man I called my father. This is my revenge.' 'But why this profession? Not that I'm condoning this, but don't most people with abusive parents become serial killers?' I was hoping this would change the mood. It didn't. 'Murder is illegal. Otherwise that's what I'd be doing.' 'Do you understand that children around the world watch you each and every week, they watch your actions, they might even decide to imitate your actions. Don't you feel you need to set a better example for these children.?' 'No, parents need to set examples, not people they see on TV. And it's ok, parents force into their children's skulls that what I'm doing is fake. It's a TV show.' 'Well, isn't it?' 'No. Ask Russel Tory(Grim Reaper) He'll tell you everytime he visits the rehab facility, if it's fake.' 'You enjoy that don't you?' 'Yes, I do.' This really made me nervous and deicided that this was the very end of everything I can take. I looked at my life and decided that what I'm doing right now is just a job, a job I love, but I can't comprehend getting myself injured for this job just to get a story out of it. So I asked him one more question, and his response sent a chill up my spine. 'Bryan, where do you see yourself in five years?' He started right at me, and without a change of emotion he said 'Dead.' I Never did an interview like this before. Leaving I couldn't understand anything that came out of it. I went in to interview a wrestler, an entertainer, but leaving I felt like I was interviewing a murderer. Does this man really think he'll be dead? And does he fear the idea of his own death? It's something we may never know, and something we might have to wait five years to find out. Is all wrestling like this? I've come to the conclusion that the answer is no. I feel that if the company Bryan is in fails he'll never find work again. He's the product of an extreme owner trying to make a quick buck, and what better than a Lunatic to dish out pain, and bring in the bucks. After this interview I learned of two things. Two rather disturbing things. 1)Bryan's merchandise, which contains Satanic Innuendos such as 666 and inverted crosses is the largest selling in the wrestling world, bringing a net gross of nearly 1 billion dollars last year. I never knew that the T-Shirts people were wearing that say 'Mayhem: The Devil's Soldier' was a wrestlers shirt. I assumed it was some hard rock band's shirt. 2) Weeks following the interview Bryan was fired from PCW and up and coming popular wrestling federation for abducting the girlfriend of another wrestler to force him into a match. He was fired, but left with his $48 million guaranteed in his 6 year contract. Yesterday, one day before, the airing of this footage I received a thank you card from Bryan. He said, 'Just like everyone else you made me more famous than I already was before. I will be back, and just wait. Sincerely, Mayhem.' It was signed in blood.
