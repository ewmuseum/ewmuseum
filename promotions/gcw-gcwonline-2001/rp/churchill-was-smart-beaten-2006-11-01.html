Churchill Was Smart (Beaten)

1 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/01/06 'Success is the ability to go from one failure to another with no loss of enthusiasm.' - Sir Winston Churchill - 'FIGHT!' Punch. 'FIGHT!' Kick. 'FIGHT!' Punch. Kick. 'FIGHT!' Kick. Kick. Punch. 'FIGHT!' Punch. Kick. Kick. Punch. Elbow. Kick. Punch. A loss, and a flight out of Texas has led him here. One move, and a three count has put Aaron Rain on a plane to go half way around the world. He'd made the hastey arrangements when he woke the next morning with a headache, feeling quite the worse for wear. Being dropped on your head can make you do some crazy things. He'd had to pay an arm and a leg to get a last minute flight so close to the holiday season, but he'd deemed it worth it to get here. To go through this. The screams of 'FIGHT!' stop, and Rain leans on the red punching bag in front of him. His blond hair is dripping with sweat, and the tape that he'd wrapped around his hands and wrist has gone useless. He turns his head slightly, just so that he can stare at the man standing next to him. About 6'4', and 300 lbs, the man that Rain calls 'Z' stares down at him. He looks to be at least ten years older than the 26 year old Rain, and obviously has quite the size advantage. Z had been the 'big thing' (no pun intended) on the English independents when Rain had moved over there to train, and so always commanded respect, especially after the two men had become friends. Rain had never, and still doesn't understand the name 'Z'. The best explanation that he's heard so far is that the man actually has an embarrassing family name. Z was the main reason that Rain moved to Mexico, only about a year after training. It was there that Rain met a man by the name of Chris Michaels, who happens to be the owner of the very dojo that he's training at right now, though the man himself spent most of his time in the Japanese branch. 'I thought you wanted to train. I thought you lost.' Z glares at him. 'I do, and I did.' 'Then why aren't you trying?' 'The hell do you mean? We've been at this for the last six hours.. I had a red eye flight... I have jet lag..' 'Why'd you bother coming, if you're just going to make excuses?' '...' 'Stand up straight.' Rain stands away from the punching bag, all the while giving Z a dirty look. The bigger man just shakes his head. 'Chris would be embarrassed... You've got an hour break, then we move into the ring.' The look on Rain's face grows impossibly dirtier. While the two men are, in fact, friends... Their views and beliefs tend to clash at times. Rain turns away, and begins to pull the tape away from his hands. Slowly, the two fairly new tattoos on the back of each hand come into site.. A pair of bold, black X's. He begins to walk, grabbing a large bottle of water off of a table as he goes. He stops, and takes a drink. 'My hero in this business...' He frowns. 'Forget that. My hero in life is a man that used to go by the name of the 'Mystery Man' in England, Japan, and Mexico. My hero is a man that has broken his neck, dislocated both shoulders, lost his family, and has never recieved the gratitude that his work deserves. My hero is a man by the name of Chris Michaels.' 'Chris Michaels motivated me. He inspired me to be a better person. Everything that he did pushed me to be the person that I am today.. Because I watched the way that he used to live life. I saw first hand what happens when a man abuses medication, only so that he can walk, let alone wrestle. I saw what happened when a man went out to the bar, just so that he could avoid thinking about the family that he's left behind to pursue his dream.' He takes a drink. 'At Chris Michaels expense, I learned the dos and don'ts of life itself. Chris Michaels missed his family when he was away... I know that. He's told me that. But he made the mistake of thinking that going out to parties would somehow ease that pain. He made the mistake of thinking that sleeping with random women would somehow help his marriage.' 'Because of Chris Michaels, I've made a pledge to myself. I will never give myself the chance to make those mistakes. I will never run away from the pain that wrestling causes, but I will, in turn, accept it as a natural consequence of doing something that I love.' 'Because of Chris Michaels... I've sworn to be the best that I can be. That means that I won't drink. I won't smoke. I won't inject, or snort, or spread, or do any of the other stupid shit that people do to get high.' 'But despite all of my attempts... There's on thing that I just can't refuse to do.' 'That's because it's out of my hands.' 'I cannot make the moral decision to not lose.' 'Draven Lox, you beat me. You dropped me on my head, and you pinned me. I've watched it over, and over, and over since arriving at Michaels' dojo... And there's no way around it.' 'I lost, you won.' Another drink. He takes a moment to brush some of the hair away that had been stuck to the side of his face. 'And as much as I want to accept that... I can't.' 'So I'm here.' 'You see, where Chris Michaels would go out and take a bottle of pills. Where Chris Michaels would go out and pick up a woman to fuck.. I find myself here. I TOOK A RED EYE FLIGHT TO GET HERE, BECAUSE YOU BEAT ME. BECAUSE OF THE COMMITMENT THAT I'VE MADE TO MYSELF TO BE THE VERY BEST.. I'M HERE. I'm here getting yelled at... I'm here training. I'm here to be better than everyone else.' 'That's the way that I am.' 'I'm not here to fucking entertain people... I'm not here to make anyone laugh. I don't give a damn if people like me, because I just can't change the way that I am to suit the interests of higher ups that can't lace my boots.' 'I've come to GCW for one thing: recognition. I'm here to be regarded as the best in the world, because I believe that I'm the best... Because nobody can match my drive.' 'Let's get one thing clear...' 'I may have lost, but I will never be beaten.' 'Dorian Ryan... A nap isn't going to help you.. I need to be known as the best.' '... The supercard that's coming upon us... Is now referred to as Devil's Night. Year's down the line... That name will no longer exist. Come November 4th... Saint's Night is born.' 'I will not be denied... One way or another, no matter how many times I lose in this promotion, I WILL rise up the ranks. Like Winston Churchill once said... History will be kind to me, for I intend to write it.' He takes one final gulp of water before throwing the bottle away. He lowers his head and brushes by the camera, presumably leaving to find another bottle before continuing his training.
