Self doubts and temptation.

28 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/28/11 “Well why the hell does it always come out this way?” Walking through flames and fire as his feet sink into soft gray ash. Burning embers flow up into the air as the air is bitter hot and almost not breathable. It is as if you are struggling in the fiery pits of hell with no way to escape. Struggling along in the barren ash land of sorts you try to get your bearings as a forlorn howl is heard far off in the distance. Like something long forgotten has become a focus once again as you become its prey. “Good good continue allow me to see what you see fully.” Hesitant a voice returns a response. “Uhm yeah sure...anyways.” Something black moving like a wolf in the shadows, always just out of sight behind the fire, moving like a breeze through the smoke. Seemingly just like the smoke, small glimpses just out of eyesight. Never being seen fully, but always making its presence felt. Then before looms a large foreboding construct of mother natures design or even perhaps your own design in your mind, but bigger than life. “What is this construct?” Annoyance heard in the mans voice as he responds. “If I can finish speaking I'll gladly tell you.” “Please continue.” A dark and massive granite cliff, rough at all of its edges and barely able to be climbed. Yet the climb must be done. So to escape this beast that is stalking you climb upwards. Fingers begins to bleed from cuts caused by the jagged edges of the granite outcroppings. Struggling to get distance between yourself and what ever manner of being that is stalking you. As you climb the sounds of the howling seem to fade and become distant. You feel a bit of relief as you get to the plateau of the cliff. Slowly you begin to pull yourself up onto it and then you stand. Suddenly a blast of heat and swirling embers within ash float around you as if the land below is defiantly reaching out for you. “So no escape and feeling of being pulled back in?” A low growl is heard in response. “Who is paying you?” “You are please continue then.” As you stand and slowly turn you begin to see someone standing there. It is as if you are looking at a darker mirrored version of yourself, A memory fleeting and yet right there in front of you. Something is shaken awake just in the back of your mind as you look on. A twinge at the base of your neck that was slowly creeping up your spine and then settling somewhere. You feel an uneasiness begin to gnaw in the pit of your stomach as you spin and turn seeing the creature that had been hunting you in its full glory. Stepping forward you grasp at your darker self who simply holds out a bow and arrow. You grasp them as they look fragile and old within your hands, suddenly spinning you notch the arrow and spin barely aiming and let fly. Suddenly a cool breeze flows from behind you as your shot rings true and hits the targeted beast in the chest. It explodes in a swirling mass of shadows and ash. You feel a pain and twinge in your chest and then you suddenly wake looking at the ceiling sweaty and breathing hard. Scared and not scared at the same time. “How does this make you feel after you have had these dreams?” A few deep breathes are heard as a simple slow response is heard. “Defeated.” Sitting up quietly he wipes his hands across his own forehead as he looks over at the psychiatrist. His eyes gaze over he attractiveness. He business like demeanor hides a well kept woman who is in great shape. He tactfully rubs his face with his hands while looking her over carefully. He then quietly speaks up again. “What do you think of these dreams?” She turns placing the pad down as she looks him in the eyes. “What I think is that you haven't faced your struggles of the past yet, you seem to be struggling within your own bonds. Each defeat you take, you realize you are falling deeper and deeper into your own self made trap.” Closing his eyes for a few moments he listens to her speak. His eyes shut, he remembers the darker shadowed image of himself. He wonders where he has seen this image before. The shadow of who he was maybe or something less dramatic than that. “Uhm look doc if you cannot help me more than this, I really think it is a waste of both of our times.” Slowly he goes to stand up as a soft feminine hand grabs his arm holding him still for a moment. He turns and looks at her his eyes meet hers. For the briefest of moments he is almost captivated by them. He then gently grabs her hand and removes it from his arm. “I have many things to attend to and staying here isn't on that list of priorities.” Removing her glasses slowly as she observes him. She takes the measure of him quickly. “Are you sure there is no convincing of you to do otherwise?” Turning he walks to the door he shakes his head. “Yes I am positive and thank you for this session, I may or may not call again. I do appreciate you helping me open my eyes to several things once again.” Turning and walking away his mind goes back to the dreams, very in depth and dangerous, but yet something still is nagging him, what could it be? What direction is he taking to cause perhaps his conscious to bother him? Is there meaning to all of this or is it something brought on by a bad stomach ache or gas? In the end it looks like he is the only one with an answer to any of this, could it simply be that he is unsure as to the end result of his current course. Only simple thing to do for one self doubt is drive away and continue again down this road again for yourself. Closing all out behind him he walks away wondering what it all was meant to be, what is the possible outcome for him and his destination. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Yourself doubt by Grace Someday I told you once then I told you twice this game we play it just isn’t right well whatever a haters gonna hate a players gonna play it will always be this way don’t take it personal don’t be delusional we’ll get it through it soon just ignore the people around us we can be anything don’t you know that by now your damn self doubt is really getting to me why don’t you see the person I know you to be
