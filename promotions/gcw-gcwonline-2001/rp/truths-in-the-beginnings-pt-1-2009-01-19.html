Truths in the beginnings PT. 1

19 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/09 A man , a person of interest, a being who has literally done it all in the business of wrestling. Yet he comes from the shadows of nowhere and everywhere at the same time. He comes before you from the sands of time. Much like the Rock of Gibraltar he has withstood the time and beatings of nature and man alike. The war ravages on as he begins his ascent to the unknown. Stepping from the darkness Shaman is seen looking around. He flicks a zippo lighter and grins as he lifts a torch and lights it. He then bends down low and lights a huge bonfire as it erupts into flames. Before you images and shadows flow past the screen in deep and meaningful patterns. Each pattern seems to reveal pieces and portions of his past. He grins as he looks up at the camera. "Ladies and Gentlemen of the world. I am here to reveal a lesson of times. Upon the ending of these lessons, you will understand the past so that you can better understand the future that is yet to be revealed." Casually tossing the torch into the fire he reaches down and lifts a handful of sand and begins releasing the sand into the wind as the images continue to swirl and fly around him as if waiting to be plucked from the air. Releasing the rest of the sand he slaps his hands together cleaning the sandy residue from his hands is a wispy cloud of dust. As if cleaning the sand from his hand removes the taint of the past he looks forward. "The lesson today that will be taught, Will be for you to understand the man before he was the man and how he came to be. The strengths and trials. The moments and in the end the final building of who he will become may come clearer than anything you have ever known. Understanding is the true path to knowledge. You will know what makes the man tick as you begin to face him. You may understand why it is futile to ever think the man defeated or ever counted out. From this day forward may you begin to understand the man before you named Shaman." Shaman waves an arm as the images begins to swirl as suddenly he disappears and a boy of 6 or 7 years of age sits before a Television screen watching his favorite wrestlers of the 1980's square off for the grandest of titles. The Giants and Immortal legends of the day. The Nature boys and Four Horsemen of the past who galloped through the imagination and causing you to want to be the greatest of them. Dreaming of one day making your own star amongst their pages of legend. Wanting to be the greatest before all the world. Shaman steps in and walks around the images and scene kneeling and looking into the eyes of the boy. "Star Struck i was at this age. I dreamed of being one of them, but had no ways of understanding how to get there. Shadowy images of static on the television. At times i couldn't see anything other than hearing the voices of the announcers move by move description and commentary. Great wrestlers of old by gone eras announcing other great wrestlers of an era that would be soon to end as i became a teenager. Yet look at me then?" Shaman looks around the room as he watches the boy who is excited and energetic. An energy never lost it seems. An energy always revealed in each and everyone of his matches, just given in a different way. The excitement of each move the cringing looks of a hard hit or a submission hold announced. The wild eyed look of wonder when a hero went down and another came to save him. The anger on the face of a villain downing his boyhood heroes. The patience to wait for what is coming next. The impatience of not wanting to wait for the next show or event. "Youthful exuberance always filled the air when i was young. Mom had always told me that things would come for me if i worked hard for them. Dad always showed me the way to get there. Then i dreamed many a countless dream of being where i am now. I wonder what i would say to myself if i saw me know through those eyes." Shaman stands as the room swirls back to life flashing by revealing images of a truck in the darkness hitting a man. A grave stone followed y a woman lying in a bed with two boys and one girl watching. Then followed by another gravestone with a 13 year old boy crying over it. Slowing down again the flashing images slow to come and reveal a 16 year old boy sweating and swinging at a punching bag hitting it for all he is worth. A man stands behind him pointing at the bag speaking and yelling words of encouragement. At each instruction the teenager dodges and weaves striking the bag as he was told. Shaman reappears again watching as the Teenager who's features seem to be shadowing the adult standing near him watching him go through his paces. "This is a strange vision. Seeing the face so full of anger and concentration. Looking at each and everything that is invisible and just out of my grasp. Angry at the world for taking what was mine from me. Taking my mother and father was a strength i had never knew until i used it as an fuel from my anger. No excuses just the building of the strengths and using them on proper avenues of aggression." Stopping he turns and points at the man that is training him as his teenage self. "That man he focused my rage and aggression. He provided the oath for me to grow and build. He showed me how to be prideful of who i was. Then the true focus of being loyal to who i was and who i was going to be." Waving his hand through the image he stands beside a ring as it waves itself back into focus. The images of the screaming fans in the gymnasium. All looking violent and blood thirst like no fan you will ever see at a huge event. The uniqueness of these fans who feed on every move and action of these wrestlers who have nothing to lose and everything to prove. Outcomes walks Shaman in his original garb over five years before his long debut within the WWA or even GCW. Before he held any titles or was even a name on the wrestling circuits. he was just a man going out and wrestling to entertain and show the world he would be somebody someday. "Guess those of you who are forgetting what its like to earn the positions you are at are wondering what i am talking about. Truth is you need to take what is yours and grab it fully and not let it go. There is something i want and soon you all will see it as it grows and grows further. The closer we get to Birthday Bash you will understand this. This portion is nowhere even close to being finished. I just decided for now i need to rest and wait to show you more." Shaman turns and disappears into a wispy mist and the camera fades as Shaman stands in the ring looking about as the man we see may be just beginning his journey leaving more mysteries than answers. What is in store for those wanting more of the truths being presented today. To Be Continued.....
