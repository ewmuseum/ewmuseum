Starbucks Sinners!

20 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20, 2008 News of a boycott didn't typically stir Jay Terror but the fact that the church was promoting it made the established one think twice. With his hands stuffed down his pockets and his hair catching the passing wind current, he glanced up at the brown beacon with a wide grin on his lips. There it was, the one sign that had brought a quasi apocalypse to the Christian faith, a disaster large enough to push back all other happenings and daily rituals on a Sunday due to its evident symbolism. Nudging Caroline, he spoke - 'You know, I might just agree.' She looked over the spread-legged mermaid through squinted eyes and a crooked face examining her half exposed breasts plastered for the world to see. 'I can see it too. Yeah. Can't say I'm offended though.' 'You see,' he paused and gawked at the undersized cups on her chest, 'I agree. It's hardly worthy of all the attention. Ya' know?' 'I'd say. What kind of news is there to report on a Sunday though?' 'True. But you'd think that the Christians would be in church instead of here. I mean, look at them.' His finger lead Caroline's eyes across the strip mall parking lot to a nearby gathering of men and women. Signs extended from their palms waving high in the air reading "Is it worth the horror?" and "Try Saxby’s!". Jay shook his head fast and looked back toward the white font on the logo. 'You thirsty?' She was slow to respond due to the overwhelming sight near the rental car. She wasn't worried about a break-in or any damage, Steven Caldera would cover such costs. Instead she felt uneasy having the sadistic amount of holy men and women in its general vicinity. There was something about them that tended to turn her sour. Nevertheless, she shook off the fear and turned slowly back to Jay. 'What was that?' 'I'm getting a drink. You too?' 'That's why we came, yeah?' 'That and to see the sign. One down and one to go, right?' Caroline nodded and stepped inward to grab Jay's hand. Their fingers interlocked and their strides matched as they crossed a no parking zone onto the sidewalk. As they approached the glass doors of the establishment though, voices called from the crowd behind them shouting threats. 'Don't do it unless you believe the devil lives!' 'Do you have no heart?' 'Where's your mind? Haven't you any care for the children?' Jay and Caroline each wanted to ignore the calls but something tempting sat with the gibes. It wasn't often that they could respond to people with their own opinions and ideas. Especially on a subject as ridiculous as the one at hand. Turning, Jay opened his mouth first allowing it to echo across the lot toward the gathering - 'I have no children sir.' Petty and hardly fulfilling, the comment drifted back to the crowd and into the beckoner's ears. A man with a furry gray beard and long white hair stepped to the forefront of the people and gritted his teeth, 'That is because God doesn't bless sinners with such miracles.' They each shared a laugh and slapped at their knees. Caroline joined in on the fun saying, 'The miracle is avoiding having them actually. Not sure where you get your ideas.' 'Sinners!' The man with the beard nodded to the crowd's speedy response and spoke again, 'Go ahead, say what you feel. I understand that there are those that do not believe in the almighty on his green earth. I am aware of your kind. I too am aware that god's divine power warrants myself and others to verbally and physically voice our opinions to whomever we choose.' 'A fight you say?' called Jay. 'Fighting is the wrong word, sinner. Settling does the lord more justice.' Terror fought back another laugh and stepped forward raising his chin, 'Let's see how well you can settle then.' The bearded man chewed over the challenge and after a minute and some pressure from other followers, he began his trek across the lot. With ten steps he was close enough to whisper to Jay if the need arose. 'Do you doubt that I will settle you?' 'I doubt you will be successful. Yes.' 'Jay,' Caroline spoke up, 'don't -' 'It's ok.' He looked at his girlfriend with friendly eyes, eyes that she couldn't easily deny, and then back at the old man. 'So what will it be?' 'Tell me, what do you have to prove with this? The lord will forgive me and banish you forever. You'll be just like Adam, stuck in the wasteland for eternity. What makes that so appealing?' 'The thought in the back of my mind showing me that this is all happening over the fact that Starbucks has a naked mermaid promoting them. Reason enough?' He looked frustrated by his diversion and therefore crossed his arms. As his toes tapped on the ground, the old man raised his chin like Jay's. 'You're not worth my time.' 'You're serious?' 'Very.' 'The fuck?' 'See!' he announced with an enthused undertone. 'Words like that prove that you are less than me on the totem pole in life and therefore proves that I have no need to vanquish you.' Caroline laughed at him. 'You Christians.' 'Us Christians?' 'Yeah. All of you. Off your damn rockers.' The old man tried to add a last sentiment but before he could, both Jay and Caroline turned around and pushed open the glass door to coffee heaven. A tiny line, unordinary but mainly driven by the growing controversy outside, stood behind the counter. Back outside, the elder man raised his hands in triumph as he reported to the other followers that he had conquered the great sinner. 'You know what you want?' 'Yeah. You want the usual?' Caroline asked. 'Of course. Change isn't my thing.' 'Not so sure about that.' 'Huh?' 'You have money?' she questioned. 'Yeah but -' She put her finger to his lips and watched him grab his wallet from his back pocket. Slapping a fresh five in her palm, she walked to the line leaving Jay to look for a place to sit. Before his quest, he casually glanced outside to check on the fanatics. He could see them sucking the life out of an unexpecting family as they approached the Starbucks doors. He shrugged and found a booth for two a few inches away. To his left, Jay made notes on a man and woman hacking away at separate laptops across from one another. Immediately he envisioned an instant messaging conversation sprouting between the two, with virtual words taking the place of an actual conversation. The man, sipping from his needle-thin stirrer, hardly looked away from the screen making Jay chuckle to himself. He shifted his attention back to the counter where Caroline stood. Her hands pressed on the countertop and her hips touched the side setting her body in a sinuous shape. He watched her lips move and the way the man behind the counter listened. Before he could read her lips though, a pair of coffees came into sight on the bar and she lifted them into her hands and turned toward Jay. Her walk was slow and her loose sweatpants swayed as she moved until she reached the chair across from Jay. “He thought the whole thing was kind of funny.” “What’s that?” She sat and passed Jay his coffee, “The Christians outside. He thought that they were acting a little ridiculous too. Said he only worked part time and it didn’t affect his wages.” “Makes sense.” Jay took a swig from the coffee and fanned his mouth soon after due to the heat. “Kinda” hot.” “It isn’t iced coffee you know.” “I know. Just pointing out that it’s hot.” Caroline nodded and motioned Jay to look at the laptop duelers beside them, “Funny how things are becoming these days. “That’s what I was saying. It’s bound to happen though. A silence overtook the air as both sipped through the hole in the lid. Coffee passed through their systems simultaneously warming both their hearts. Caroline decided to speak. “You wrestle this week” Shocked to hear her talk about wrestling, Jay sat still. “Yeah. A big one too. “Yeah” “Yeah. A whole mess of people are supposed to be in the ring. One of those matches where everyone hates someone on the other team.” “Who do you hate?” “Viv.” “You hate her.” “That’s a loaded question.” Caroline leaned forward and put on her interrogative face, “How so?” “I mean, I don’t hate her for the most part. It just makes sense. Last week she wound up calling Jordan as her challenger for the belt and not me. It’s my shot that I won and she picked the guy I beat. Ya” know. It’s kinda? messed up on her part.” “And you hate her because of that?” “Yeah. I’d say so.” She nodded and took another sweltering sip of coffee, “I wanted to apologize for what’s been going on lately Jay. I’ve been kinda” out of it.” He knew what she meant, “What do you mean?” “Come on,” she playfully paused, “I’ve rolled over in the bed and deprived you of me long enough for you to make some connection. I was upset, that’s all.” “It’s cool.” “I thought you were supposed to be training with that guy Steven hired.” “I was. I don’t really like him though so I took the day off.” “You did what?” Her inquisitive face switched over to her confused one, “Jay, you know that you can’t just do that.” “Yeah. But I mean ?” he stopped before he lead the conversation any closer to controversy. Instead, he sipped at the coffee and leaned back in his chair. “I just don’t think it will help.” “Why’s that?” “I’m already at the top. It’s not by chance.” “Jay,” the confused face transformed into an upset one, “come on. Be real. You know that things wouldn’t have been so easy if Octavian hadn’t showed up.” “I’ll give Jordan credit, yeah. It doesn’t mean things would be different though. I’ve grown since my time with Daniel and I’m pretty sure training isn’t what I need now. It’s peace. Anger management even. I swear Viv is going to drive me insane if she pulls off any more stunts like this.” “All I’m suggesting is give things a chance. I know that you don’t go for things that you don’t believe in but maybe, for once, you can make an exception. I’ve heard how bad you want this and I’m understanding how much it means to you. If you want to get it done though, you’ll have to conform.” “I’ll see what I can do.” With a sigh, she stirred her coffee around and allowed the silence to return. Its caped self overtook the air masking the table they sat around with a cloud of solitude. Jay and Caroline meant to speak, they didn’t have a problem making conversation, yet they remained silent. Perhaps it was the overwhelming feeling of acceptance? Maybe it was the music playing overhead. Either way, the silence remained and continued on through the rest of their drinking. __________ When you think you’ve figured a person out, it seems that they find a way to prove you otherwise. I thought I knew who Caroline was becoming, I thought I knew what would happen to us. Now look at things. She’s back to herself. She’s alive and full featured again. Was it something I said? Did she finally figure out what I’ve been trying to explain? I just don’t understand. Yes, I do like how things have turned out. But something seems strange. Maybe I’m just paranoid. I can’t think about this. Not now. I’m at a point in my career where distractions cannot arise. I can’t stop and deal with petty differences when the gold is hanging there for me to grab. God damn do I want that title. I’m going to get that title. No matter what.
