She’s just crazy. She’s been like that ever since she popped that kid out.” Markus shrugged as they finally stumbled across the station there baggage was expected to arrive at, “alright man. If that’s what you want.” Firmly Chris assured, “it is.” -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- With the trance Tessa was in for a moment, one would have sworn she was seeing things. Her big blue eyes glassed over, it didn’t take much of a look to realize she was being plagued by the past. To an outsider, it might not be as obvious what was going through Tessa’s mind. To those like Markus Stone who knew her past, and where she wanted the future to go, what was floating around in her simple mind was transparent. Assuming his role as a surrogate big brother to Tessa, Markus did little to let Tessa down gently. It was so much easier for him to dive into the harsh reality of it all, especially given his own current state of mind, “Don’t give him another chance to mess with your mind Tess. The moment he gets what he wants he’ll leave you to drown.” Even with his harsh reminders, Tessa seemed keen to stick with her ideas of what could come from all this mess, “he’s changed Markus. You made him realize what he’s doing wrong with us, the kids, everyone really.” Much more skeptical, Markus packed another punch filled with reality, “how many times has he said that to you? To me? To anyone he wants something from for that matter? The difference between you and everyone else is all he’s gotta do is curl you up into his arms, look into your eyes, and shed a single fucking tear and you’ll do his bidding. Can you say the same?” She avoided the topic the best she could by serving Stone his own dose of cold hard reality, “Maybe not. But at least I can get another job without needing someone else to vouch for me. Can you say the same?” She had him right there, but she did not end with just that, “you’re a liability Markus. I knew that when I vouched for you to Steven Caldera two years ago, and I know it still stands true today. If you’re not off the wagon already, you’re probably really damn close. Yet here I am still trying to support you regardless of your bad habits, why can’t you do the same for me?” The answer was simple, “because your bad habit drives me to mine.” It still did not make the fact anymore accepted than what he figured it’d be, “That’s crap Markus!” “Really?” “Really!” “Do you know how many times I’ve been left for dead because of your ass? And I mean that quite literally.” Tessa looked him in the eye as he spoke, wishing if nothing else she could burn a hole straight through him at this certain period of time, “Chris doesn’t care about me, he doesn’t care about you. You’re nothing more than a damn trophy to him. If he couldn’t sleep with you whenever he wanted to, he’d have absolutely nothing to do with you, or the kids for that matter. It’s the same way he treated me anytime we lost. You’re nothing more than a notch.” “Bull.” The pair of eyes that suddenly appeared from behind Tessa seemed to concede with her thoughts, only in a much deeper voice. Who the voice booming behind her belonged to was ever so obvious, there was no need for Stone to take a second glance in Tessa’s direction. The moment Markus chose to acknowledge his presence it would be on.
