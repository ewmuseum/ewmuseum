Skeletons ... TWO

30 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30 Oct 05 25 OCT 1998 “Damen why are we here?” “Business.” “What kind of business could you possibly have out here?” “Look Roberto if all you are going to do is ask stupid questions then you can start walking home now.” A silence fell in the car now. Roberto Abitua was very uncomfortable in the situation he had found himself in. He worried for his brother’s safety, which is the only reason he agreed to even come on this trip. Damen had never actually told him where the money came from that he always carried around but it wasn’t that hard to figure out. Roberto turned his head away from his brother and stared out into the night sky. Something didn’t feel right to him but he couldn’t quite place what it was. He went to speak but abruptly stopped himself and instead began chewing on his finger nails. This young man’s life had been completely unpredictable up to this point. It was barely a year ago that his Wife and child were killed in an auto accident in their home town in Spain. Roberto loved his family so much that it hurt him every time their names were brought up now. If it wasn’t for finding a twin brother that he did not even know existed, he may have ended his life. There’s no doubt that the guilt he felt could have easily led him to do it. Not a day went by that it wasn’t a consideration to him. The pain that just stayed in his body sometimes felt unbearable. Damen saved him. He was never told about the other side of his family until he was thrown out by his Uncle Juanito. Slowly but surely Roberto became a man again, he had a family again. He had his brother, his abuela Francis who gladly let him into their home, and even was introduced to a man named David Sandy. David was almost like a father figure to the young boys. He recognized what they needed, and gave them an outlet for their anger. Wrestling. Roberto had just begun training for this wrestling stuff but it was something he enjoyed. It was a way to get away from the pressures of the real world, not to mention that he was learning fast and had unlimited potential. Things were forming for Roberto, which is why he was so worried about his brother’s “business ventures” that he spoke of. He felt that Damen simply had no clue what kind of crowd he was really getting himself into. Damen was from Toledo, OH ? not a very SMALL city but not a large one either. He had a lot of respect where he came from, but it meant nothing here. They called it Mexican Town in Detroit, MI. The neighborhood was far from ideal, neither were the bad people that lurked inside of it. Damen and Roberto may have been the same age, but Roberto had seen much more in his lifetime. He knew the type of his person his brother was, acted like a bad ass but inside was incredibly gifted and could do whatever he really wanted to with his life, but at the same time was too stubborn to work for it. Roberto had been in his ear since he came to Toledo about concentrating on school and even wrestling to keep him out of trouble. It was always the same though, he didn’t want to give up the life style he led, the money, the girls, etc. “There they are.” Damen spoke softly while pointing towards an incoming vehicle. Damen looks over at Roberto now. “Wait here this won’t take long.” “Damen!” “Trust me, I’ll be right back.” Roberto decided not to argue and let his brother walk off leaving the car running. Roberto crossed his arms and leaned back in the seat. He watched his brother approach a group of about 5 or 6 men, it was hard to get an exact number in the dark. They all shook hands and shared a few words for a moment before moving towards the building in front of them, that appeared to be abandoned. His stomach turned inside of his body almost as if it were going to burst out. He kept tell himself. “I Should be in there.” Roberto whispers to himself. He fights the nerves the best way he can. He tells himself that he can trust Damen’s judgement, that everything will be okay and in a matter of moments Damen will step from that door a smile on his face, his money in his pocket and they could go back on that hour long drive back to Toledo and joke about how nervous Roberto was. Damen had been doing this for a long time now hasn’t he? Of course he has! So there was nothing to worry about at all. Roberto laid his head back and closed his eyes. A slight smirk spreads on his face kind of chuckling off the way he had worried about his twin brother. They’d be gone soon. The problem was the minutes flew by and they weren’t gone. Roberto opened his eyes again and noticed that fifteen minutes had passed. His body tightened up as the thoughts of what could be happening flooded his mind. Roberto tried keeping the worries to a minimum, but it didn’t work quite like he hoped. He swung the door open and stepped outside, his feet making the small stones under his feet crackle. He looked up into the sky and saw the full moon shining brightly, but it was the color that bothered him. The moon seemed to have a red tint to it. The Moon of Blood. Slowly Roberto took step after step towards the door leading to maybe his worst fear. His heart pounded harder and faster with each step forward. Fear consumed him but it was something he had to do. Roberto reached the door but instead of just barging in he kneels and looks in since it was left cracked open. He sees Damen, being held by two rather large black men. Blood trickled down his mouth and bruises covered his face. A large Hispanic man stepped up into view, perhaps the main man of this operation. “Who the fuck did you think you were dealing with Damen” This isn’t Toledo anymore you pathetic fuck. You wanted to step into the real world ? into MY WORLD and you pull this? Who the hell are you to be making demands?” The man shouted before punching Damen again. Roberto cupped his hand over his mouth. He wanted to do something but his body wouldn’t move. His eyes were wide as he stared into the room. He watched as Damen lifted his head just enough to spit blood all over the leader of the group’s white suit. The man smirked and then nodded to his two henchmen. They bashed the back of Damen’s head causing him to fall on his face. His assailants now place their boots into the back of his neck holding him down. “You are brave you little shit I’ll give you that. The problem with brave people is that they don’t know when they are beaten. You are stubborn and foolish Damen, but I am going to fix that problem in a hurry.” Roberto couldn’t close his eyes even if he wanted to. Not even when A pistol was pulled out and aimed directly at the back of his brother’s head. It was as if Roberto was watching this on TV, it couldn’t be real could it? Something like this was only in movies, one person’s life couldn’t be filled with so much pain and heartache. Then it happened. Damen’s eyes caught Roberto’s for only a brief second. A tear streamed down Damen’s eye as they made contact. Roberto just wanted to reach out and grab Damen by the hand, pull him from everything and run ? run away as fast as they could and be safe. It was a fantasy though, a dream that was never to come true. The blast didn’t even make Roberto’s eyes blink. He was in a trance. The bullets both found their mark, digging deep into Damen’s head. Those eyes that were once full of life became black and lifeless. The tears were replaced by blood and his body went limp. Roberto couldn’t move, couldn’t breath, couldn’t think. Life had finally began to mean something to him again. Then it was all stripped away from him once again in the blink of an eye. His eyes shut closed hard ? “You saw him die?” The Doctor’s voice forced my eyes open. I didn’t respond right away as a single tear dripped down the side of my face. I swallow hard before turning and looking at the doctor who also seemed slightly moved by the story. I nod slightly before saying anything. “Yes ? watched as his life was stripped from him like it was nothing. These men had no feeling or no remorse. There probably would be no justice either. Damen was a gang banger and the police would probably just let it go.” “Did they ever find him” The one who killed your brother I mean.” “No.” I turn my head again. “The story isn’t over though.” “What else could be left?” “The most important part.” “Which would be what?” “Revenge.”
