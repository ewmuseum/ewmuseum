EndWar: Child's Play

14 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/14/10 I. The room is ordinary, four walls, a ceiling and a wooden floor. The ceiling looks to be crumbling, the walls appear to be mostly stripped of its paint and the floor is dusty and rotted. This room is also very dark, only the dim light of a candle showing any signs of light. The candle rests in the center of a small wooden table, round shaped. This table has seen better days, there are scratches, chips taken from it, writing from black markers. The table sits in the center of this dark room, a set of black chairs keeping it company with a chair on each side. On one of the chairs rests a man, or at least something like a man. LeStatt Knight, a creature that craves success and demands excellence. For years he has been at war, struggling to gain the one thing that he has never had … control; complete control anyways. Since the day that he was born he has been shackled by the fact that the life that he has, wasn’t given in such a natural way. In fact most people would liken LeStatt’s birth to the monster of Frankenstein. A man, Roberto Abitua, tired of failure and longing for a feeling of importance and power set forth to create something or someone that could bring it to him. He had tried to be the person that he thought people wanted but forced into a place of uncertainty and bleakness, Roberto created the being that the world NEEDED. What he did not know at the time however was that this would also be the person that the world HATED. Out of desperation, LeStatt Knight was born. Like any animal or person, LeStatt was excited about all of the possibilities that stood in front of him. He was created to be powerful, cunning, intelligent and very, very good at making people feel fear. From day one he enjoyed using the tools that were given to him and he cherished the moments he had with, as he called them, victims. Every fractured bone, every bleeding cut and every scream full of pain … It makes LeStatt feel more and more alive. Carnage feeds the beast and the beast never seems to be satisfied. And yet … There is always something, a feeling, that he has not been capable of shaking away. It is as if no matter how hard he fights that he is being limited in what he is allowed to do. Like a genie, he has the power to do most anything but the power comes at a price and that price is that he is a slave. Roberto, his creator, is nowhere near the man that LeStatt is and yet it is Roberto who holds LeStatt back from being a completely unstoppable force. LeStatt Knight has grown to hate his creator. That hatred intensifies with each passing day and now it has gotten to the point where he can no longer hold it inside. LeStatt’s body feels as if it has this energy running through it that is anxious to get out. He is always in pain, feeling as if his skin could explode at any moment, destroying him and Roberto at the same time. LeStatt has no intention of dying, what he wants is his freedom. Knight’s eyes shift up towards the door as it slowly is pushed open. Starting as a sliver but continuing to grow as the door opens all the way, light invades the dark room. LeStatt sees the dark figure in the middle of the light and although he cannot make out any definitive features he recognizes him. He can smell him, that fear that always resides in him. It makes Knight sick for a moment, weakness it something that he hates almost as much as his creator. “Took you long enough.” Knight’s words are low and deep. “Welcome. Shut the door and sit down.” Even slower than when the door opened it is pushed closed. Roberto hesitates, taking in a deep breath before walking towards the table that Knight sits at. Knight momentarily closes his black eyes and listens closely, the beating of Roberto’s heart gets faster as he gets closer. Roberto stops when he reaches the chair set out for him. “What do you want LeStatt?” “I want you to fucking sit down.” Knight spits these words out with enough venom to kill a village. “Please.” Knight’s eyes open up. Roberto swallows, his bright blue eyes searching Knight’s black, lifeless ones for answers. When those answers do not come Roberto sits down but never taking his eyes off of the monster that he created many years ago. Here they are, monster and creator, sitting face to face. “What’s this about?” Roberto asks. “This?” Knight’s eyes break the stare, he moves them to the small flame that dances above the candle in the center of the room. “Actually…” Knight looks back at Roberto again … “Freedom.” II. He takes a few tentative steps forward ensuring that he doesn’t make a sound. Slowly and carefully he pokes his head around the corner to check and see if the coast is clear. His eyes dart right and then left then back right again. When he believes things are safe he walks completely from behind his cover. One more very careful step is taken before he takes off, pumping his legs as fast as he can towards an open door at the end of the hall. He inches closer, a feeling of relief coming over him as he realizes he’s almost to the safe zone. Then a hulking figure appears, seemingly out of nowhere. He looks up at the towering figure which appears to be 20 feet tall by his estimation. He shrieks and does his best to turn and run away but the Giant is focused and follows closely behind. He can feel the ground quiver with each powerful step taken from the coming Giant. He shrieks again as the Giant’s powerful hands wrap around his waist and lifts his body into the air. The Giant tosses him into the air, making his body turn while in mid-air so that when the Giant catches him again they are staring face to face. The Giant smiles a great big smile and brings his face in … maybe to eat him! …or… To give him a kiss? This makes him laugh and squirm. “Daddy! Daddy! Stop!” He calls out. “Pleeeease!” “Stop? Stop what? THIS??” His father continues to kiss and tickle him until the boy just latches on, pulling himself as close to his father as he can get. Both of them laugh until their faces and their stomachs hurt. After a few minutes the boy pulls himself away from his father so they can look at each other again. “You scared me Daddy!” “Aww I’m sorry Joaquin,” The father responds back. The father is GCW’s own LeStatt Knight. On this day he spends quality time with his son, time that he does not get nearly enough of. Joaquin Abitua is one of a very select people. Most people who know LeStatt Knight only see one side of him. Either they see the brutal persona he portrays on television or they know Roberto, the kind man that loves his family and loves his work. Of course Joaquin is only going on four years old so he does not fully grasp the significance of this split personality. He does see the change and in some ways even understands how quickly that switch can flip. To him it’s almost as if when this LeStatt character comes to the front, it isn’t his father anymore. When Knight is in the front seat, Joaquin feels true fear. Not the unconditional love that he feels for Roberto. Lately Joaquin has recognized his real father very rarely and even when he does there seems to be some struggle from him. As if he’s had to try too hard to be that man. This is not an issue today, at least not right now. At this moment his father loves him, the fear is not in his little stomach. There is no voice in his mind telling him not to come out of his bedroom when his father calls. “So, Niño,” Roberto asks with a smile. “What would you like to eat?” “Ummm …. Cookies!” “Cookies?” Joaquin nods his head vigorously “Your mother probably won’t be very happy with that decision.” “Cookies!” Roberto smiles. “Cookies it is.” He gives Joaquin another quick kiss before walking with him towards the Kitchen. “I have to leave tomorrow morning anyways, let us enjoy this right?” Joaquin nods again. Father and son walk through the house, a large house in fact. Joaquin feels safe in his father’s arms so he leans his head on Roberto’s chest. He listens intently to his father’s beating heart. THUMP-THUMP… THUMP-THUMP… THUMP-THUMP… It’s relaxing to the boy. It makes his eyes heavy, the fact that he hasn’t had a nap today kicking in. THUMP-THUMP… THUMP-THUMP… He didn’t want Daddy to leave. THUMP-THUMP… He falls asleep. III. “Don’t believe for a second that it’s going to happen.” Roberto shakes his head. “We’ve been through this time and time again.” “No.” LeStatt’s voice remains even as he tries to keep himself under control. “You’ve been through this. I’m a fucking prisoner here, always have been.” “You are damn right you are a prisoner LeStatt. That is what happens to people who bring violence and death. You can’t be allowed to roam free, you WILL not be allowed to. This place, this world … this is your home.” “It’s not a home, it’s a hell.” “Label it as you wish, the fact still remains that you will not be getting out of here.” LeStatt’s hands, spread out on his side of the table, form closed fists. His jaw tightens, that hatred for Roberto preparing to boil over. “It’s not my fault that I am this way. I did not create myself. YOU did this Roberto. You made me this way.” Roberto’s stare falters, a thousand images running through his head in a matter of seconds. He knows LeStatt speaks the truth. He brought this pain on, the suffering of many people rest in his hands. It makes him feel weak. Which is exactly what LeStatt wants. This thought snaps him back into the moment. His blue eyes meet LeStatt’s again, his posture straightens. “That’s why I’m doing THIS too.” LeStatt leans back into his chair, the tension failing to leave his body. “I don’t get you Roberto, I really don’t. You claim I’m so bad, that I cannot be trusted and yet without me? Where exactly would you be right now? Who would you be?” “I’d be content with the knowledge that I’ve never caused anyone pain.” “Have you forgotten about Cindy?” Roberto hasn’t forgotten about Cindy of course, or Starr. Who could forget their first wife and child, especially a wife and child that died because of a man that they loved and trusted. A lump forms in Roberto’s throat. Although he knows that these comments are just LeStatt’s method of trying to break him down so that he can take over, they still hurt. Roberto’s silence causes Knight to smirk. “I wasn’t around then was I? I wasn’t around when your bastard of a brother was shot in the fucking head either. I wasn’t apart of your life when Victor burned to death either!” “BUT YOU WERE!” Roberto’s voice comes out surprisingly boastful. It catches LeStatt off guard. “You were always around. That darkness that fills you, your life force. It was always here.” Like a shark that smells blood in the water, Knight refuses to relent. “You are a coward Roberto. You refuse to accept responsibility for your own actions! You fuck things up, you put people through pain and to cope? You create me. Someone that you can pass along your guilt to. I carry your burdens so that you can attempt to live this fantasy that you have of what a perfect life is. You’ve used me to get fame and fortune. You’ve used me to become a legend in your profession. You use me to attack people that you are afraid to and when my skills are no longer needed, you box me up and try to forget about me.” LeStatt’s face twists to a snarl. “You’ve used me for the last time.” “Do you know what’s sad about this LeStatt?” “What?” “That you actually believe that you even have a choice.” Roberto smiles. “You don’t. You never did. I hold the other end of your leash and I control how long it is at any given time. If your use is no longer required? Then I’ll just bury you until the day that we die.” Knight’s fists slam on the table top. A growl escapes his throat as the bones in his hands smash into the wood. “I’M TIRED OF THIS!” “Calm down.” “Fuck you! Do you know how tired I am of having to listen to you? Of having to see you or SMELL you? You are this scared child that cannot do anything for himself. Your scent is repulsive. Just sitting here now makes me want to vomit. You are weak! You are pathetic! You-” “I own your ass!” Roberto is the one who leans forward this time. “You are NEVER going free.” The Monster’s hands shake. The creator’s heart pounds in his chest. A resolution is coming … IV. THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP… The heart beat’s faster rate wakes Joaquin. His eyes open and he lifts his head up to see what caused the disturbance. At first he is quite groggy so everything is distorted, dream like even still. THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP-THUMP… It takes a few moments for Joaquin to get the grogginess out of his eyes. He looks to his right first to see his Mother standing directly in front of him. He knows her looks too … she is not very happy. So his head turns back to his father. Once he looks at his father’s eyes, that fear runs through his body like a winter chill. Joaquin motions to be let down which his ‘father’ complies to. He knows better, that isn’t his real father. His real father is losing a fight that he cannot afford to lose. As soon as Joaquin’s feet touch the floor he runs off to hide around the corner. Again his head pokes around the corner to spy on his mother … and the man that is suppose to be his father but isn’t anymore. He feels warm tears forming in his eyes. He doesn’t like being afraid, he doesn’t like his Mom being sad and he already misses his father. He also hates to hear them argue … Transformation complete. The voices grow louder and louder as Joaquin’s mother and “Father” argue longer and longer. He cannot make out what it is that they argue about and even the words he can readily understand do not make enough sense for him to piece it together. He knows that he hates this man, this impostor that comes to find them so often. The shouting becomes even louder and louder until Joaquin hears powerful footsteps marching through the house followed by the slamming of the front door. He listens for his mother and he receives the soft sound of sobbing in return. Like a tornado, this man that was supposed to be Joaquin’s father leaves nothing but sadness and destruction behind. Joaquin walks to the sound of his mother crying. Perhaps he doesn’t fully understand what’s going on. Maybe he is nothing more than a young child but he can see things that most other people cannot. He knows both LeStatt and Roberto. And he hates LeStatt. V. “You are right about one thing.” “Yeah? And what is that exactly?” “We can’t keep this going.” Knight leans back again, Roberto’s words doing more to annoy him than to help him become more at ease. “I know,” LeStatt let’s a low noise out. It is something similar to a laugh but probably best described as a grunt. “I should just kill you.” “Please,” Roberto shakes his head. “We both know you can’t. If that were at all possible you’d have done it by now.” It is a true statement. Every day LeStatt has thought about taking Roberto’s life. He’s pictured it in great detail. It’s typically the highlight of his existence. They are so vivid, so graphic that he can taste Roberto’s blood swishing around inside of his mouth. The problem is that there is no destroying Roberto because … well … what then? He wants freedom but without a vessel to control what good is freedom? There is only one way for LeStatt to obtain the only thing that he is missing. Roberto has to give in of his own free will. “I could just push until you go crazy.” “You’ve tried that LeStatt. You’ve made me see things that most people could only imagine in their worst nightmares. Because of you I’ve had to relive Cindy’s car accident. I’ve had to live a life where Mary dies, killing me in the process. Your sick games have brought so much heartache … But I’m still standing. So bring it Knight, bring your worst. Drop me into a world where I never met Mary. Place me in a setting that my Uncle Juanito abused me and Sandra. Do what you believe to be necessary. The final result will remain the same. You’re damned to be a prisoner.” LeStatt reaches out and runs an open hand over the flame from the candle, allowing the heat to run through his hand. Pain is something he can relate to. “Perhaps,” Knight smiles. “But perhaps there is another way.” “No, there isn’t.” “Just listen to me. There is a way that you can be rid of me. You can have everything you’ve ever wanted without my interference.” “And how is that LeStatt?” “Just beat me. That is all you have to do.” Roberto’s head cocks to the side as he examines his own dark passenger. “And what is the challenge?” LeStatt takes his right hand and slides it across the table towards his body. His hand comes off the table and moves to his side, where he digs something out. Although it could’ve been anything, Roberto doesn’t move to defend himself. Just like Knight he is tired of this war. “This.” LeStatt’s hand slams down on the table and then slides away. Beneath his hand was an object, large and silver. The dancing light from the flame made the object glisten. Roberto’s eyes are drawn to it … “You’re serious?” Roberto asks without his eyes ever moving from the object. “Deadly.” Knight’s eyes narrow. “So what do you say? Will you put it all on the line? Are you willing to sacrifice everything to be rid of me?” Roberto’s blue eyes admire it still. It’s almost as if it glows in the dark. While staring, it feels like it’s calling to him. Like a beacon of light in an otherwise dark world. It’s an image of FREEDOM for both men. Roberto’s eyes raise, again meeting Knight’s. “Who goes first?” Knight smiles. “Let’s find out.” LeStatt reaches out and grabs their beacon of hope, spinning the .357 Magnum revolver to decide who gets to tempt fate first …
