Project Exiled

25 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 25th, 2009 “The first rule of Project Exiled is?” Before Chris storm could finish his sentence he was cut off. “Not to talk about Project Exiled?” “What” No! Then how would we get people to join? Tell everyone about this shit. Only lie, and make it sound cooler than it is.” Chris looked around the room and saw a pretty pathetic sight. There was his close buddy Markus Stone (who made up the other half of the uber-awesome tag team of Banned & Exiled~!), Rikki Roxx, and Arizona. The last two sat under a large banner that read “Project Exiled” and looked as if a group of third graders made it during recess. As Arizona looked around Rikki Roxx’s apartment, he raised his hand. “So, like, what is the point of Project Exiled again?” Markus Stone took this question. “Project Exiled is a secret project we’re establishing to fuck with the establishment.” Again, Arizona just had to ask a question (don’t you hate people like that?). “If it’s supposed to be secret, then why are we going to tell everyone about it?” “Because we’re not going to be able to fuck with the Establishment if we’re relying on you two” Stone answered as he pointed to Arizona and Rikki Roxx. “We need some like, Navy Seals or some shit, people with experience in explosives and other cool stuff.” For the third time, and hopefully the last, Arizona raised his hand. “Now are we talking about the Establishment as in the government, or Establishment as in Caldera.” Chris and Markus exchanged looks. “Caldera” Stone replied. “Yeah, we’re not messing with Obama. That nigga fuck some shit up.” Stone turned to Chris, “not cool.” “What” It’s true.” This time it is Rikki Roxx with the question. “So do we have a plan?” The master minds behind the greatest tag team in the known multi-verse do what they always do when someone asked one of them a question they didn’t know the answer to, they huddled. After a few seconds of deliberating, they break and face the group. “We plan to blow up their main offices while no one is there, then before they get back we convince Starbucks to build a new location on top of where their building used to be.” “It’s brilliant” Chris said proudly smiling. Arizona and Rikki Roxx slowly turn to each other, almost to ask if they were in the right room. “Does Starbucks know about this yet?” “We may have mentioned something to one of the cashiers there” Chris chimed in, “but knowing how aggressive their growth strategy is, they would most likely jump on the spot once they notice the last flickering ash burn out.” Nervously Arizona looked around his surroundings, almost as if he expected highly trained operatives to burst into the room at any second. “Isn’t that’like terrorism and shit?” “Nonsense!” Stone objected. “We’re only planning on wrecking massive damage unexpectedly in hopes of creating fear so that Caldera gives into our demands.” “To put one of those sweet Wolfgang Puck griddles in our locker room so we can make pancakes and maybe some Jaeger Schnitzel if we get banned to our locker room for the night.” Chris said, already dreaming of the German style Veal cutlets. Rarely the voice of reason within the group, even Rikki Roxx had to speak up on that plan. “Actually, that is the definition of terrorism. Using terror to get what you want.” Smiling, Chris taps his head where his brain would be if it hadn’t already left after the stupidity started. “Exactly, that is why we’re using fear instead of terror. So we’re really more of fearist. Fearism” Yeah.” Arizona shook his head, “same thing. Fear and terror mean the same thing.” As per usual when the two get confused, Chris and Markus look to each other to bail the other out. Thankfully Markus Stone is the smarter of the two and knew how the handle this. “Nuh uh, if they were the same thing then why would they use different words” Answer that smart ass.” “He’s got you there” chimes Storm, glad he won’t get shipped off to some secret prison and most likely violated in ways unspeakable even in a Banned & Exiled~! Roleplay. “Do you guys have a thesaurus” I can show you.” “The’sore us?” “The saucer?” “Dude, are you talking about like a dinosaur or something” Cause if we had one of those around here, we wouldn’t hang out with you guys.” Rikki Roxx walked over to his book case, looking past all the 80’s Metal cassettes he’s collected over the years, until at the bottom he found a few books. Going through them, he found the one he had been searching for and brought it over to the dynamic duo of awesomeness. “This” he said, “is a thesaurus.” Chris took the book from the God of Rock and looked at it, examining the various pages inside of it with an expression of great sadness upon his face. “What’s the matter?” He gazed up at Rikki, tears nearly forming in his eyes, “I was hoping for a dinosaur.” Markus instantly snatched the book from his partner and went to the F section. He scanned over the pages before slamming it shut. “Hot damn!” he yelled, “It seems we are terrorist.” The two looked at each other, realizing what they were getting themselves into. “But I don’t wanna be a terrorist! I just want my Wolfgang Puck!” cried Chris, who for no good reason grabbed the thesaurus and chunked it across the room, as if that would somehow solve all their problems. Just then, there came a knocking on the door. Chris and Markus jumped nearly ten feet in the air, a remarkable feet considering the degree of whiteness they both possessed. “Oh shit, its homeland security! They’ve found us!” Hearing Markus? assumption, Chris promptly dove behind the couch. “Tell them I’m not here.” Rikki Roxx went to open the door but before he could the door was suddenly kicked in. Wood went flying throughout the room and Markus joined Chris behind the couch. A barrage of men came storming (no relation) into the room wearing heavy body armor and dark clothes. “Get on the ground!” they all yelled in sync. When a group of men crash into your apartment (or perhaps the apartment of a friend) and said group of men are burnishing automatic weapons, it is always in your best interest to do what they say. That is exactly what Banned & Exiled~! did. They got the fuck on the ground. Before they knew it, they were handcuffed and being taken out of the apartment. To where? Only the ones who took them knew for sure?. ?””””””. It was Guantanamo Bay. “Shit, we’re in Guantanamo Bay!” See. The members of Banned & Exiled~! were loaded off the plane, their burnt orange one pieces stood off against the sunset. Markus Stone looked down at what they made him wear and felt as if he would lose his lunch. “Did it have to be burnt orange?” he asked. One of the guards looked at Stone and tossed him the “Hook’em Horns” sign with his right hand. “This place is just as bad as advertised. I feel like I’m in hell.” Chris gave his friend a glance, “really” I feel like I’m in Austin.” Markus shrugged his shoulders, “same thing.” The four men walked off the plane, stepping through the barbed wire gate that surrounded the Cuban prison. No sooner do they get five steps inside the prison that a whistle blew. Everyone stopped what they were doing and looked toward the guard towers. A man wearing a fine looking suit walked out of the main building. “Alright, we’re closed. Let everyone go” he ordered, waving a group of papers in his hand. The guards looked at each other and then just dropped their guns. “WOOOO!!!” Both members of Banned & Exiled~! cried as they threw their arms up in the air in celebration. Making their way around the prison, the guards begun to uncuff everyone. Once their hands were free, Chris and Markus turned to each other. HIGH FIVE~! “So, like, do we just get back on the plane then?” Someone was getting back on the plane, but it wasn’t the dynamic duo of awesomeness. All of the guards and the man who held onto the papers loaded onto the plane that Banned & Exiled~! had just exited off of. “Alright, that’s cool, we’ll just’uh’catch the next one I guess” Markus said as the engine kicked on, drowning him out. “Dude, I think they left us.” Chris said; worry starting to creep onto his face. Markus shrugged it off, “good thing we’re rich.” He pulled out a cell phone and started to dial a number. “Yeah, hey, we could use a ride. Yeah, we’re in Guantanamo Bay. Long story. Thanks, I owe you one.” Hanging up the phone, he turned back to his bestest buddy, “we got a ride.” Chris scratched his head in confusion, “dude, we’re you hide that thing?” “Don’t ask.” And so after a few hours of waiting with the nicest Middle Eastern people you’ll meet in a prison, Banned & Exiled~! saw their plane touch down. In a matter of hours they were back in New York and hanging out at Rikki Roxx’s apartment again. Sitting on the couch, Arizona looked over at the two as they came in from the kitchen, having just grabbed themselves nice cold Cherry Cokes. “So,” Arizona asked, “what did we learn today?” “That we’re morons?” Chris answered, unsure. Markus jabbed him in the ribs, “No you idiot,” he said, “we learned that terrorism can sometimes be funny.” “No! We learned that maybe blowing shit up isn’t always the answer to your problems.” Arizona answered his own question. “Pssst” Chris rolled his eyes, “I dunno about you, but blowing up shit is always the answer to my problems.” This time it was Arizona that smacked Chris. “No!” he yelled, “blowing shit up gets you sent to Guantanamo Bay.” “Which Obama closed down” replied Stone. “Yeah, I told you not to mess with Obama. That nigga fuck some shit up.” Rikki Roxx stood up in the room, everyone turned and looked at him. “Maybe that,” he said, “is what we learned. Don’t mess with Obama because he will fuck some shit up.” “I agree” said Markus and everyone laughed until the credits started to roll, or some shit.
