A sobering reality. An idol is gone.

25 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 25 2007 Fenway park. The oldest park in Major League Baseball a lot would assume and understand. Wrigley field with the Ivy would be consider the oldest as well. Yet Fenway park is the oldest bar none. It has the history. A lot of players to back that history up, the late great and deceased Ted Williams, How about Carl Yaztremski, or even the enigmatic Jim Rice. The park has a lot of history and today that history is even more curious since the wrestling world has come to Fenway park. The Boston Red Sox have invited him down to Fenway for a meet and greet signing. His autograph sessions were interesting to say the least. Sitting in the box seats he is watching the perennial Red Sox slugger at the plate. The itch slides in low and inside. Ortiz swings and sends the ball well over the fence in right field as Shaman simply watches and smiles shaking his head. “I would not have sent that pitch into that area. Ortiz has the ability to turn massive towering homeruns ? Shaman leans back and looks over at the gentleman next to him and he frowns. “Go ahead and ask the questions I can see you want too.” The man nods and speaks.” Sorry bout these, but the fans of GCW wish to know where you and Mayhem have been the last few weeks. You have a demanding presence and you have been silent.” Looking over at the man Shaman rubs his hands together and reaches up rubbing his temples. “There are a lot of things people want to know about Oblivion. The truth is there is a lot they are not going to know. In fact I have a flight out of here this evening to Australia.” The guy looks over at Shaman and shakes his head. “Cutting it kind of close there aren’t ya?” Shaman laughs deeply as he looks at the pitcher throw a strike. “You know a player does not give his best secrets during the play of the game. He doesn’t even reveal his game plan till he has retired.” Shaman looks at a woman as she enters and hands him a piece of paper as Shaman looks at it in disbelief. “You sure this is correct?” She nods as Shaman slumps back in his chair. “Chris Benoit has just passed away with his whole family.” The other gentleman looks at Shaman in shocked disbelief. Shaman looks at Fenway Park as he watches the ball game. His eyes looking at everything the history of the place and simply stands up. He turns and walks from the booth. Shaman exits as he gently runs his hand down the wall like a child would. The touch being registered deep in his mind. The man quickly follows behind Shaman as Shaman looks at him. “I want to remember where I was at this moment. The moment I heard that one of the people I looked up to passed away in our respected sport.” Several children are running around and stop in awe as they stare at the huge shadowed figure of Shaman coming through the corridor. They smile and wave and hold up paper for Shaman’s autograph. Shaman smiles softly and signs the autographs. He pats each child on their heads and ruffles their hair. The autograph says “Follow every dream till the very end.” Followed by his signature. Walking out of the park he heads for a Limo nearby and climbs in and sits there as he reaches forward and grabs a drink. He sips the drink as he stares at the glass. He speaks to himself. “You know I remember the first time I had seen him wrestle. He was wearing those stupid frilly gold laced white tights, wrestling against some no namer. He won obviously. Time flies and then they are gone. Makes you think of your own mortality. Leaning back he lifts his laptop from the seat as he punches up information. He looks at the window as the man who was in the box seats with him knocks on the window. “Shaman are you ok” You seem floored with the news?” Shaman looks at the man and sighs. “I am going to go ahead and leave for Australia. I have several things to think about on my way there. See you in Australia.” Shaman closes the window as the limo drives off leaving the man stunned, but Shaman’s demeanor. Camera fades to black with white letters closing the screen with these words. “In Respected Memory, May the Wolverine live on forever in our heart and minds.”
