Drops Of Jupiter (Part Three: Revelations)

11 March 2011

Date: 03/11/11 "Hey Vee, you're never going to guess what happened at work today." A swinging door slammed shut wildly behind her as she waded through piles of laundry. Across the living room she trekked, almost tripping on nearly forgotten band equipment with flailing white plastic bag firmly clinched in hand. With a final leap she cleared the mess and found herself in the hallway before banging on her spare room door, a room once occupied by a former bandmate turned lover, now only opened for the once-in-a-year visit by a random friend. Turning the doorknob and throwing her body weight into the aging hardwood, she continued her sentence. "I found this wrestling DVD, and there's this chick on here who..." Absolute silence. The bed was made. The luggage was gone. It was almost like no one had been there at all. Curiously, Amanda Harper walked into the room and tossed her plastic bag filled with a stack of DVD's onto the bed. An act that had become second nature to the green-haired clerk, judging by the condition of the rest of her apartment. It was then she could've swore she heard a noise coming from behind her, perhaps it was just her imagination but... "Vee?" She questioned turning around, glaring back towards the hallway through emerald eyes, "You in the bathroom?" No response. Yet she still had to open the bathroom door to see for herself. She already knew the answer, but the visual confirmation of her theory made her heart sink just a bit deeper into her chest. Honestly, she'd looked forward to sharing the story with Vee, but even more so spending some more time with her. The night before was spent watching Inception and ordering pizza, followed by telling stories of their childhood and the things they'd been in trouble for as children. Turns out they both had a soft spot for soldiers and they both grew up going to fairly wealthy neighborhood high schools, although Vee's family was rich and Amanda had merely lied and said she lived at a relative's house. Even though Vee was dead to the world that morning, she'd discussed sticking around for the day before she went to bed. With no job and no home, why in the world would she be in such a hurry to get up and leave? Amanda understood her not wanting to intrude, but she was certainly in no hurry to kick her out... and she knew that. Yet as she began to walk out of the bathroom, snapping out of a deep thought, something glistening under the yellow tinted light caught her attention. Something that wasn't there before. Turning around, she looked down at the counter to figure out what it was. Perhaps Vee left a single trace of her presence after all. A laminated rectangular white paper attached to a silver clip was what laid in front of her. Picking it up to take a closer look, she turned it over to reveal a picture of Vee staring right back at her. Next the picture it read: "Global Championship Wrestling Backstage Pass" "Performer: Vivica J. Valentine" The word shock didn't even begin to describe the sensation that overwhelmed her. Suddenly, everything made sense... except how she felt. In the face of betrayal, Amanda Harper looked up at her own reflection in the mirror, and ran her hands through locks of green and black. There was nothing else to say but to ask herself aloud: "What the fuck do I do now?" ---- Drops Of Jupiter, Part 3: Revelations ---- "We invite you to take part in our newest segment... Come On Jon Stewart!" An embarrassed Jon Stewart looks at the bottom of the television screen just seconds before a black t-shirt lands on top of the television, blocking the view and muffling the laughter of the crowd. Soon it's replaced by frantic rustling and tossing, followed by grunts of frustration. Turning around, Vivica J. Valentine punches the corner of the wall in frustration. The result causing minimal damage to the wall, but busting open her middle knuckle as she screams out... "SON OF A BITCH!" followed by the flailing of her right hand. Looking down at the blood beginning to trickle, she puts the knuckle into her mouth biting down on it slightly to deal with the pain and bleeding as she tramples over a month's worth of laundry to swing around the corner to the bathroom. With one hand she goes to battle with a travel bag, ending with her dumping the entire thing onto the counter in search of a bandage. Mission accomplished. You can't tell me to feel! The truth never set me free So, I did it myself... "Dammit, not now Amanda..." she said to herself as she went back out to her hotel room bed, which on top laid a suitcase with nearly nothing in it. Mounds of laundry were piled up on everything else surrounding the area. The television, two lamps, and night stand now all laid claim to an article of clothing or two. You can't be too careful anymore When all that is waiting for you Won't come any closer You've got to reach a little more Vivica didn't even acknowledge the call going to voicemail as she reached the bottom of her suitcase to find nothing there. All that work. All that mess. All the hassle for nothing. She could just scream at the top of her lungs if it wasn't already almost two in the morning. That, however, didn't stop profanities from spilling out between her lips like BP in the Gulf. "I just had that fucking thing in my hands, I knew I did! Where the FUCK did it go?" DO DO DOOOOOO "NOT FUCKING NOW AMANDA!" She screamed in response to her text tone, pacing back and forth trying to recount her steps from the morning. She woke up. She packed her clothes. She made her bed. She went to the bathroom to get ready. DO DO DOOOOOO It would go off until she answered it, it was better to shut it up now before it drove her insane to the point where it would go flying off the 3rd floor balcony. With a frustrated growl Vivica stormed over to the nightstand and tossed the bra off of her cell phone against the wall. Flipping the phone open, she had no plans of actually reading the message but when it opened it seemed short enough. "From: Jupiter Skye To: You I know the truth. We need to talk." Then, the world stopped. Taking a seat on the bed, she pushed the scattered mess aside, her eyes remaining fixated on the screen. It was funny how two sentences can suddenly put your entire life into perspective, and subsequently turn what you thought was a nightmare into a situation that you wish you could have back. A situation where petty things such as a silly, replaceable GCW personnel pass get you upset and the thought of the on-going lie that you burdened for months being revealed and biting you square in the ass never crossed your mind. "Fuck my life." You can't tell me to feel! The truth never set me fre... Still slightly frozen, Vivica answered the phone before hesitantly moving it up to her ear. "H... Hello?" "Vivica? Is that what Vee is short for?" "Yeah, but..." "I found your pass on the bathroom counter." FUCK. There's where she left it. Closing her eyes she pounded the palm of her left hand against her forehead in frustration. "Why didn't you tell me, Vee?" "Look, it... it's complicated. I didn't want things to..." "It's complicated? Vee, I did the same thing. It KILLED me to keep lying to you like I did to the point where I had to tell you. I gave you the opportunity to do the same and you lied to my face?" "I didn't 'lie' per say." "You fucking lied." "Alright, I lied, I'm sorry it's just... you know. Fuck." Vivica could hear Amanda's breath on the other side of the phone as she tried to collect her thoughts, explaining her way out of a day that she'd hoped would never come. "Being who I am, it's hard to go out and make real friends. Having face-to-face contact with somebody who is talking to you as a person and not talking with you trying to get something. Tickets. Interviews. Autographs..." Vivica paused for a second, "I was just happy you didn't recognize me, honestly." "So because you have trouble trusting people, you lie to them?" "No, that's not it see... look." Vivica's thoughts were a chaotic storm of emotion and self-analyzation, "I've done some things recently in my life I'm not entirely proud of. Life got a bit out of control for me and I lost my mind for a bit. I didn't want you to think that's who I was. I didn't want you to judge me by the things I've done, but instead for the person I'm trying to be." "But how can I trust that you're that person?" "Because you already know me, Jupe." The fact that her name was Amanda temporarily slipped Vivica's mind, "...I mean, Amanda." It was a mix-up that hit close to home for Amanda Harper, who took a few minutes to collect her own thoughts. "Look, I can relate. It's not like I was up front and honest about the whole thing." Amanda spoke, "You seemed cool and I just wanted you to think I was important, I'm sure you had your reasons." "I did." "You gave me the benefit of the doubt. I'll do the same for you." A huge sigh of relief blew through Vivica's pink lips, and with it a burden lifted off of her shoulders. Suddenly, it was as if all the reasons she had for lying all-along were gone. Leaving only a smile to cross her lips as she stood back up in her hotel room. "But listen, can you do me a favor, Amanda?" "What's that?" "Wrestling isn't really your thing, is it?" "Not particularly. It all seems kind of silly, if you don't mind me saying so." "Good." Vivica spoke, "Can you not watch then?" "Why?" "Celebrity changes people." She answered, "I've seen it happen, it'll change things." "Vee, I'm a big girl, I think I can handle..." "I'm serious." Vivica interrupted, "For me?" An extended silence on the other side of the phone certainly didn't put Vivica's mind at ease. "Yeah." Amanda finally answered, "It's all kind of silly anyway. I wouldn't want to think any less of you." Vivica could almost see Amanda's smirk on the other end of the phone. "I have to get going Amanda, I have to get a new pass tomorrow." "One last question?" "Yeah?" "You don't wear those hideous spandex do you?" "No." "Oh, thank Christ!" "Goodnight, Amanda. I'm sorry." CLICK. ~*~ "Goodnight, Vee." CLICK. "It's alright..." Amanda Harper said to herself as she looked down at her cell phone, now back on the main menu screen. Its florescent glow beamed softly off her pierced face before she placed it on the nightstand. Alone in the dark, she ran her hands through her hair and closed her eyes. It was so hard to let her frustrations go. Even though she'd done the exact same thing, she was having trouble coming to terms with the fact that Vivica had lied to her face. It was nothing new for her, though. Lying was something that she'd dealt with her entire life. Growing up bouncing from foster home to foster home, it wasn't until high school that she'd finally found a place to call home. That place was never with her mother, who called her twice a year her entire life to let her know she'd bought her presents for her birthday and Christmas only to find out at the next holiday that they'd somehow been "lost in the mail." Why did she expect Vee to be any different? Why did she expect anyone to be honest anymore? The reason the guilt hurt so bad was because she'd realized that she was doing the exact same thing to Vee that had been done to her for her entire life. Just the thought of turning into her showgirl-turned-stripper-turned-prostitute mother made her stomach turn. She didn't even know who her father was... he was just another buck to her mother. Perhaps he was high profile lawyer, or a movie star, or a famous musician. Honestly the thought about who her father was didn't cross her mind nearly as much as it did when she was younger. She grew up and became an adult, and mom and dad would forever be Dave and Cindy who took her in as a teenager and made sure that she got her high school education. Their reward for making sure she got her education was zero interest in college and their daughter being a lifetime employee for Blockbuster with pipe dreams of someday being a famous rock star... but they supported her in everything that she did. They paid for her spider bite piercings when she was 17. Her first guitar. Her first amp. Her first tattoo. What more could a teenager ever ask for? Besides a friend, of course. A wave of exhaustion overcame her, forcing her eyes shut and her mouth wide open in a yawn. She had to be back at work in the morning. Another day closer to the countdown where she lost her job. Things over the horizon didn't look so great either. Nobody was hiring. The economy was shit. She couldn't even scrap a band together for a buck here or there. Certainly it was only a matter of time until she was forced to go crawling back to mom and dad... again. No matter how much work she put in, she could never seem to get ahead. When was karma going to award her for her hard work? Instead she got the last couple of dollars in her wallet completely wasted on six GCW DVDs that she couldn't even watch. Of course Vee would tell her not to watch, how else could her shitty day be complete? There was the plastic bag at the end of the guest room bed, taunting her like a childhood bully. It was 11pm and there was nothing on non-cable television, perhaps she could just watch the DVDs until Vee's match and then just skip it. She'd made it clear that she just didn't want Amanda to watch her and the things she's done. They mention Vivica Valentine and she'd hit the next button. It couldn't be THAT difficult. Plus, like she'd told Vee over the phone, she was a big girl. The fact that she was who she was didn't bother her at all. She was still the same Vee, whether other people thought she was famous or not. After all, being a professional wrestler hardly makes you famous. It isn't the late 90's anymore. If she were a Mixed Martial Artist, that'd be a different story entirely. With those thoughts she crawled across the bed, digging out Dangerous Games 2010 via cell phone light, and placing it into the DVD player. The sound of "Dangerous Games" by Alcatrazz almost immediately made her jump back up out of bed to turn the DVD back off, but the roar of the crowd and flashing lights kept her attention. It all played out like a rock and roll concert, and the wrestlers... they were the rock stars. To be able to captivate and command a crowd like that, no matter how silly the whole premise was, must be amazing. She watched with interest as a wide array of characters crossed her screen. Garbage Bag Johnny, Christian Zenith, Johnny Borealis, Dominic Wall, Triumph Frost, Bryan Mayhem, Chris Bagwell, and her personal favorite so far Clyde Fox all brought something unique and different to the table. She'd almost completely forgotten her promise to herself when the face of Vivica J. Valentine graced the television camera with her presence. Picking up the DVD remote, Amanda couldn't help but smirk at the sight of her friend on the television. With her thumb placed over the "NEXT" button, she held it out in front of her before... "Easy." her friend's voice echoed across the room. "I'm guessing I've got about 90 seconds - tops - before Christian arrives, so I'm only going to tell you this once..." Alright Amanda, that's enough. "I know who you are, and I know you already know that. Yeah, you’ve hidden your face behind that mask and strapped yourself inside that suit again now you’re here, but you were stood just a few feet away from me at Zenith Manor and I saw the real you… and it wasn't what I expected to see. I would've never guessed it was you. And It hurt worse than the hardest punch you could have thrown." Just hit the button now... "But I’m not Christian. I’m not standing her holding what I know over your head. Nobody else knows who it was who felt the need to retreat behind that mask and buckle themselves into that suit. You? You could strap anyone else into that suit, have them hump Zenith’s leg while you walk around with nobody any the wiser. Me? I created this hell for myself, and I’m paying for it now with everything I do here. I’m hated, and most will likely hate me until my body can’t do this anymore… some even after that. But it doesn’t have to be like that for you. You can still make this right, because we both know you deserve better than Christian Zenith has to offer." That thing she was talking to, it didn't even have eyes. It didn't even look... human. "What Zenith is making you do is not worth your name, and you know that. He’s used you to end careers, punish his enemies… hell, he used you when he put a hit on the Commissioner. What do you get? You get to remain hidden beneath that mask, and he’ll make you think it’s easier that way. Then, when he’s done with you, or you’re too wild for him to control, he’ll find someone to break you down. I would know, it's been me before." How could she be ashamed of this? She was... "You’ve choked Frost out how many times now? 2? 3? Yet he’s walking around, Hardcore title dragging behind him. Borealis’ hand probably still stings from when he tapped out to you, and he’s walking around with the TV title around his waist. And tonight, what’s Christian going to tell you? To go and win yourself a belt as a reward for your obedience? Or is he going to tell you to serve Tempest up to me on a silver platter?" She was... "Tell me that little touch didn’t feel good. Tell me that isn't what you live for." She was just trying to save it. Amanda's hand slowly dropped and laid the remote down on the nightstand and watched as the show unfolded in front of her. ~*~ "Viv?" With no answer the doorknob turned, and peeking his head inside Christian Zenith looks around the room to find his fiancé with her eyes glued firmly on the television. "Are you alive?" "Yep." It was tough to even catch a blink from her eyes as she looked on at the match in front of her. "I have to twist your arm to get you to show up for the GLOBAL tapings." "I watched it when I was a kid." "I'm sure you did, but why are you watching it now?" "Her." Vivica picked up the remote and paused it right on a picture of tall woman with colored streaks in her hair. She had been flipping all over the ring in the previous few minutes and a bit harder to capture. Especially with this giant mechanism made of glass surrounding it. "Who is she?" "Karina Wolfenden." Christian answered, "Why are you going through my tapes?" "I was bored." "Well, I came by to see if you wanted to go grab lunch..." "Why do you have tapes of her? Are you facing her?" Vivica said, completely ignoring the comment that Christian had just made. "Seven of the Best is coming up, and she'll be representing PRIME. "Then answer the question." "Have you even had breakfast yet?" Christian questioned as Vivica picked the DVD remote back up and pressed play once again. Mesmerized by the woman on the screen in front of her, right then and there she'd fallen back in love with professional wrestling again. The sight of a girl just like her holding her own is all she needed to see. She was so much different than Lia and Dawn, it was hard to make a connection but Karina... "Hello? Viv?" He'd lost her interested, and she'd lost her chance at lunch as Christian simply turned around and left the room. Leaving her to her own devices, like he so often did. Laying in bed, minutes turned to hours and hours turned to days. She'd stopped showing up to her internship at Zenith Corporation and really only left the mansion to go to GLOBAL shows, hoping on a whim that she'd see her there... that she'd get the chance to meet this Karina Wolfenden. Now, she often wished she'd never met her at all. ~*~ The sun peeked over the curtains, shining into her emerald eyes as she cupped her hand above them, preventing the sun from blocking her view of the television. Her eyes were red and her eyelids sagged. The phone on the nightstand next to her showed six missed calls, all from the Blockbuster Video that was set to fire her in just a few weeks. DVD cases once inside of a plastic bag now laid on a stack on top of the DVD player. The plastic bad now lies on the floor. Next to her lies a bag of Bugles unopened, but there if she ever needed them. "No. No, no, no, no. After what happened, Viv, I don't think I'll ever be able to call you an acquaintance again, let alone something like... that. You haven't thought this through. You haven't thought about the ramifications of what could happen. This is the same kind of snap decision you make every time something big happens to you, and it's the exact same type of decision that's landed you in every spot of bother you've been in throughout your career. I'm doing fine: I just won Dangerous Games and I'm the number one contender. I'm back where I belong, my home-life is good, and I'm happy. I don't need this dark cloud over me again. If you're genuinely trying to turn over a new leaf and get on with your life then this is a terrible idea. I got over us a long time ago. If you ever want to be happy again then you need to do the same." Adjusting, she sat up straighter. "I don't want a relationship, I just want my friend back. Can't you see that? And if you don't want to believe the words I say, that's fine. If you don't want to be my friend, that fucking sucks, but that's fine too. But if you think the person who stabbed you in the back last year was who I really am... then you're even more fucked up than I am. They say actions speak louder than words, Andy, and if you want to find out who I really am I suggest you take your head out of your ass and pay close attention to what happens on that television screen. I'm the person you thought I used to be, not the person you think I am now. You'll see." A lump began to rise up in the back of her throat. "And congrats on winning Dangerous Games... but winning that match was the easy part. The hard part comes now, and that's living up to expectations. Not buckling under the pressure of what that means. If anyone would know, it's me. Good luck, you're going to need it." Her eyes began to shut against her will, but she forced them back open again. "If you think you can find a way to "show me" you're trustworthy then, by all means, go for it. But hey, save the smugness. I don't need your advice. I've been walking this Earth a damn sight longer than you have, Viv. Don't think you know something I don't. If you're really trying to make amends, well, you haven't exactly made the best start." Her jaw slacked and the breath is taken out of her lungs like a sucker punch to the stomach. "It isn't how you start, it's how you finish. I'll see you around, Andy. Merry Christmas." Her eyes narrowed as she watched Vivica J. Valentine hand her gift to Andy Murray and then exit the room. Murray looks down at the gift for just a split-second before tossing it across the room and directly into the trash. A snarl crossed the lips of Amanda Harper, and her fingers clinched into fists.
