Date: 5/13/01 13 Years Old Women May 13th, 2001 2:30 pm Life is an experience. You ride the hell out of it and then die. Of course, there are more complex, philosophical takes on life but that’s Clyde Fox’s version. A very basic, simple approach to things. And to be honest, it’s how he prefers it no matter how fantastical it might seem. Of course, we all know that there are endless nuances in life. But for Clyde, he really only had one. Bitches. Sure, he was one of the dim lucky ones that weren’t affected by everyday worries. But he always had trouble with women for some strange reason. Being ignorant to other worries was looked at as a gift he had. But with the good came the bad. And the bad is his inability of love. “Kay eye bee bee!” Clyde shouted. Sunday. Sun. Hot. Heat. Bright. Sweat. Yuck. Spring. “KIBB?” His friend replied back. KIBB… keep it basic bud. Yeah, I made that one all by myself! No help at all! The tree provided ample shade for the two school chums who were melting away an ideal Sunday. Two bright-- two minds collected under one branch. Two very young minds indeed. So young that they had not yet been exposed to the acne annihilator. Just baby smooth cheeks with an awfully young looking face. Innocent yet deviant in one feral swoop. “You know, if you look at your life and take away all of the unnecessary garbage, and leave yourself with the zeroes and ones of it all, you’ve got what’s in my head,” Clyde said. “What? Nothing?” His friend replied jokingly. “No, not nothing silly face! Zeroes and ones! The most basic elementary roots to anything. The ways we live our lives are decided by these very codes you know!” Clyde professed. Zeroes and ones. Basic. If only life were that easy. If only we could all see what Clyde sees. This wasn’t the turn of the century talking… it was his roots. His being. His whole embodiment. Like the most basic of computers, Clyde Fox had one button; the ON button. His button would surely be tested today though. The very basic foundations was about to experience a very basic feeling; love. It’s all part of growing up and for Clyde’s life up to this point; it was already a mixed bag. Check in the column of ‘Infusing Teenage Hood’ here. “Whatever you say man,” His friend remarked. Their heads turn in opposite directions. Clyde looks right, as his friend looks left. “Whoa… hey… Clyde. Look, it’s…” His friend commented. “I know, I know. I can sense it man,” Clyde confirmed as he turns his head. Enter her. It was a completely different ball game now. Forget everything that you know, or think you know about Clyde Fox and women. Sit back, relax and take it all in baby. Ohhhhh yeah. Love the smell of hot teen poon in the noon. This girl’s name is irrelevant. It doesn’t matter. So for all intensive, and immersive purposes, slap on the name of your choosing. Preferably a woman you hate. A real thorn in your side. A real butt plug in, well, your butt. A girl that no matter what, has always been a bitch in your life. No doubt, this girl was she. One of those stiffies back in the elementary school days. She’d eventually blossom into one of the crazy party people. Dark brown hair of course. I mean sure, blondes get that trashy reputation but in all reality, it’s just one big cover up by the brunettes. They are the true psycho bags. And the blondes were stupid enough to allow them to be falsely labeled. Stupid enough… or dare we say smart enough? Pure genius. Even though they are all psycho. The dark hair just contains their evil thoughts deep within their minds. Blondes can’t hold them in and therefore can be read like a book. Note to user: Always take a blonde up on her challenge to play poker. This girl in particular could represent hundreds of women. The epitomization of what a woman is on the outside, but also what is evolving on the inside. Looks have everything, yet nothing to do with it. Club guys will smack anything that walks including ugly girls who are willing. A girl this good looking though could be just as big a whore as the next bitch. Her walk said innocent but her lips said seductive. Either one couldn’t be trusted. Sure, she had a nice smile and was relatively approachable, just like most of the women on this planet, but it’s one thing know this girl, and another to actually know her. There’s no point in getting attached. Clyde could see it as light as day. He could play the whole damn relationship in his mind in a sequence that would take her two real steps. The point where he goes up and talks to her with nerves flying around in his stomach like a bad slice of cold pizza eaten the night before. All those feelings for nothing. He already knows she isn’t worth it, yet no matter what guy knew that, they’d still be nervous. It would only create a false hope in both their hearts that something genuine was going to come of this. She’d play along because who is she to deny herself the attention right? That’s really what all girls want. They want to meet a ton of people, slim down the group into their closest and most trusted friends, but always have someone giving them a ton of attention. Clyde, or any other guy for that matter would assume it’s because they actually have interest. A talk could turn into a coffee or heck, even a date. The date at a restaurant could work only if you realize she’s there to get free food and nothing more than a few looks from other guys at surrounding tables. Yes, she might only be a teenager, but at this point she’s just learning how to be a seductive, manipulating, secretive little bitch. It’s really the tip of the iceberg and that’s sad for such young kids because they’ll have to go through so many years of garbage before finding an adequate person just to date. If that even exists in this world. She may laugh, smile, and flirt but deep down she’s horny for a scoop of ice cream and a night of Simpson reruns. Wait… what are we saying here? She’d more like be horny to meet up with her infected gal pals, hitchhike over to the nearest college dorm party and corral herself some fresh college meat. No way would she be caught dead with you. She’d rather hump a wooden stick in hopes of getting splinters in her make-fun area than sleep with you. After dinner comes the walk home. She’ll chuckle and still tell you that you’re a fun, stand up guy but she isn’t looking for that bro. A flirt-faked kiss goodnight seals the emotions in a nice little present marked ‘I’m a douche bag, use me tender’ from Clyde Fox. You’ll talk on the phone when it’s convenient for her or when she has nothing to do but that’s never the case. This is the type of broad who is always doing something. It’s like they have their schedule posted in the most public places for people to walk by and choose the most convenient time slot to hang. Or you’ll make it to a party and she’ll be there, just on top of some other guy. Lies of how she feels or really wanted to test how much she likes you would be given just in hopes that she doesn’t lose her little tag along. This is the type of girl who, ten years from now will have had no ‘boyfriends’ and just fuck-latchskies. Every guy wanting her, but she only wants to party. No time for commitment or serious relationships. This bitch is on a mission to grind up on any guy she finds in the bar. But sadly, this is where our story turns. Your emotions aren’t supposed to get all hurt and shit. She thought she made it clear that she didn’t like you that way. It just doesn’t matter to her. Man up dude, man up. You could take comfort in the fact that you did nothing wrong and that she’s just a homoerotic bitch who will probably realize her own self-destruction one morning after. She’ll realize that she could have been cared for, loved, looked after, and everything. But instead she threw it away for a life of mediocre partying and ultimate emptiness. You can create it in your head that she’s really missing you deep down inside somewhere or face reality which is that she is probably ecstatic with her decision and she’s having a great time on her back each night. You know it’s true. Especially considering other guys are slime balls… you should know if you are one. Of course, not every guy is a slime ball… but yes, every girl is a bitch. You might have thought this wouldn’t become a one sided affair… but it is. From the eyes of Clyde and beyond. At least you can avoid one thing. That annoying conversation that she really does like you, but just wants to be your friend. That is sure to drive any guy up the wall. But what does it all mean? A girl who likes you, shows they like you, keeps you around… only to throw you by the wayside!? It’s not like you did anything even remotely questionable to make her stray. What does she think she’ll find out there? Something better? In truth, she might, but she never even explored what you could give her. Yeah, okay… I’m going to not see where this potentially great relationship will go for a life of drugs and alcohol and the chance I might never be happy again. It really is up to chance, and if the bitch is willing to go with percentages, they’ll be fighting a losing battle. Exit her. “Why didn’t you say anything man?” Clyde’s friend asked. Clyde playfully pats his friend on his back. “Not worth it man. I already know what happens,” Clyde replies. Trying to piece it all together, his friend leans forward. After a moment, he turns back with his reply. “Can’t understand normal things, eh bro?” His friend asks. Clyde sits back and places his hand behind in head in relaxation. “Yup. That about sums it up,” Clyde smirks. Words: 1,800 approx.
