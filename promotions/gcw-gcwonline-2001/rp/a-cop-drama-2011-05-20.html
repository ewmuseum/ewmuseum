A Cop Drama

20 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/11 January, 2011 Since falling upon particularly hard times at the hands of some bad financial moves and a double-talking agent, Amy Campbell had been staying for free with her best friend, a professional photographer and artist who'd moved to Oakland to be closer to LA. It was her way of returning the favor from when she first moved there and Amy had convinced her then-husband to let her crash for a few months. There hadn't been an incident yet, and Amy's life outside of those walls was finally calming down. The bartender from Amy's favorite haunt also lived in the building, and was always a pleasure to bump into. He was a great guy, but also a pushover, and had been pretty quick to give up their address when pressed by another bar patron he'd grown to like. That's what led Ryan to that doorstep, looking like a grinning idiot with those flowers clutched tightly in his hands. He'd been through enough not to give a fuck what kind of reaction waited behind the door when he knocked, though the girl who answered wasn't the one he was so fascinated by. She was taller than Amy by a good few inches - most people were - and wore dark hair in a sort of boy's cut, short and swooped to one side. The woman was pretty, and dressed a lot like her friend, in a fitted t-shirt and skinny jeans, but lacked any visible tattoos. She also didn't look particularly impressed by having a handsome man on her welcome mat. ''Uhh. Hello.'' ''I...'' It was strange, he hadn't been the least bit nervous on his way up. He had to clear his throat, licking suddenly dry lips. ''I was wondering if...'' The lanky woman rolled her eyes at first, but changed her expression to offer up a slight grin. She pitied him, clearly. ''If Amy's here, is what I'm guessing.'' ''You must be Claire.'' ''She been talking shit?'' Ryan laughed. ''Wow. That's gotta run in your circle.'' ''Excuse me?'' ''The whole 'What's it to you?' tilt to everything you guys say,'' he raised a hand in apology. ''Sorry, I don't mean to sound like a dick saying that, just...'' Her hand was on her hip. ''Well... you're not Alex Pierce.'' He nodded. She opened the door wider to make room for him. ''And because of that, I'll give you a pro tip: Amy's laying on the couch because she doesn't want you to see she's actually here.'' ''Damnit, Claire!'' ''Oh, don't even start with that.'' Her hand was still on her hip, though she'd turned to scold the back of the sofa. ''The kid brought flowers.'' Part of him wanted to defend his manhood - and the Wolverine t-shirt he wore - by speaking up. The part of him that Amy had already walked away from twice knew better. He was silent until he saw the back of the redhead as she rose slowly from the couch, and it almost looked like she was hesitant to face him right away, hurriedly sweeping hair away from her face. Was she actually worried about how she looked? When she finally faced him, there was nothing to worry about. His grin got wide, and in the corner of his eye he saw Claire roll her eyes again, flailing in mock disgust. As her best friend flounced off into the other room, Campbell folded her arms and stared straight at him. She seemed to be trying to hide the smile, but it was a damn tough thing to contain with a face like that. ''You brought flowers.'' ''Don't tell me flowers are on that long-ass list of things that get you pissed at me too.'' When she bit her lip, it drew a smile. ''You're kind of an ass.'' ''I've been called worse,'' he said. ''Probably by your roomie there.'' ''From what I told her, she thinks you're too self-assured and overcompensating. Not that you hated me - I already gathered that part - but that you were somehow even more pissed about something I might have said. So...'' He raised the flowers again. ''I brought back-up.'' She didn't reach for them, even when he gestured to urge her to, but she did smile. Genuinely. ''They're actually nice, and that's thoughtful, just...'' ''Just... you can't take flowers from a nice man who feels a little like a stalker right now?'' ''I w--how DID you figure out where I was, anyway?'' ''Jeremy at the bar. He mentioned you were staying with someone in his building.'' Pierce leaned back against the steering wheel, her fingers lingering on the other woman's jaw. She took a deep, shuddering breath. ''It's okay.'' ''I miss--'' She was cut off by a sniffle and a bell that brought Alexandra back to earth. The bell meant school was out, and Quinn wouldn't be far behind. Whichever.'' ''I'm sure one of us will figure it out.'' Perhaps it was guilt, but Amy miserably watched every step Alexandra took, and when her mismatched eyes drifted away from the woman walking to the car behind her and back to her phone, the sickness set in. She slid her finger across the touch screen and sighed as she thumbed the message. 'I have to see you tonight.' She winced as she highlighted his name and hit Send.
