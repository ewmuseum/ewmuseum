All my pasts remembered Part 2

31 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/31/05 I could barely see due to the blood in my eyes, I wiped them clean and looked around. I saw Hacksaw barreling at me, his face also soaked in blood. I ducked and grabbed the front of his tights and threw him forward, slamming his face into the cage yet again. He staggered back and fell flat on his back. I pulled myself to my feet using the ropes and looked for the cage door. Where was it? There! To my right, holding the ropes and risking a glance at Hacksaw I moved. He started to move to, I willed myself to move faster, he lunged at my foot, grabbed it! Shit! I pulled free, his blood slick hands not able to get a hold of my foot. The referee swung the door open and I stepped through the ropes and then took one step. My foot was out. I held on to the door. Two steps, both feet were out. Three steps. Four steps. No more steps. My feet were on the floor, the roar was deafening. I fell down. 'Here is your winner and the NEW IWO World Heavyweight Champion...MIKE SAVIGE!' I wasn't the Machine yet. Ten pounds of gold and leather were pressed into my hands and I realized I had done it. The dreams of an eight year old boy were realized in front of 18,000 people. Fast forward to Boston, Mass. I was now the Machine, I was still the IWO Champion going on month number eight of my reign. I had just finished a show at the Boston Garden. I accepted a ride back to the hotel with another wrestler by the name of Alex Marshall. It was a rainy night, the roads were wet, the other driver was drunk, and my career almost ended. Alex swerved as the car barreled into our lane, but the other car swerved in the same direction. Alex swerved again, and the tree was already there. I felt wetness on my face, was it blood or rain? I saw red lights...I think, everything was so blurry. Why can't I move? Oh God, I'm dead. HEY! Ease up with the fuckin' light in my eyes. Voices. 'His pupils are dilated...', 'the other one?...', 'concious...', 'this one...?' Again with the fuckin' light? Jesus! I can see, cut that shit out! '...thready...oxygen...' A mask on my face, hey, I can breathe now. My leg hurts, it really fuckin' hurts. 'Let's move him.' This looks like an amublance. And that looks like a nice black void that I'll fall into now, thank you very much.... White. At least it's not black. Antiseptic smell. My leg itches. WHAT THE FUCK?! Is that a cast? On my leg? SHIT! I looked around and found the call button. A nurse, a doctor, John Steele, Alex Marshall and Scott Mercer bustled into the room. John Steele was the president of the IWO (and yes, that is his real name), Alex was the wrestler I was riding with, and Scott Mercer was my agent. Alex had a cast on his arm. He was the lucky one. I had a shattered kneecap. It took six hours of surgery to get all the fragments out of my leg, another three to put everything back together again. I would need at least three to four months for it to heal properly. I wanted to die. My career was over. Everyone wished me well, but I ushered them out. I watched on TV as the accident was turned into an angle when Alex appeared on TV with a broken arm. The cards and letters from fans flooded in. Day after day after day, for two months I got cards and flowers, and candies...and some rather provocative pictures from some female fans. I was determined to get back on my feet. But first I had to drop the title. I agreed with Steele to film a promo from my hospital bed. 'As you can see, I'm a little worse off than Alex Marhsall is.' I pointed to my leg, 'The doctors say, it will be at least three months before I can even walk again, due to the fact that my knee was shattered. They say I may never wrestle again.' I paused knowing the boos that would get. 'But I promise you, I will walk again, and I will wrestle again. Before you know it the Machine will be back! But for now, I must regretably surrender the IWO World Heavyweight title. But a word to whoever wins this tournament Steele sets up. That title is still mine, you're just keeping it warm for me!' Fast forward six months. Upon my return to the ring after three months of rehab and working out getting back into shape, I turned heel. Because it was during my time in the hospital I met Cindy. She had just debuted with a tag team known as Damage Inc, a couple of big bruisers named Jeff Wylde and Roadblock. She had been planning an angle with Steele to add me to her stable before my knee fell apart. I liked the idea of turning heel, and I readilly agreed. We were in a six man tag team match with myself and the Dynamite Brothers (Tommy and Johnny) versus Damage, Inc and David Washington. During the match Sinnamon (as Cindy was known), kept jumping up on the apron distracting the ref. After I nailed Washington with the Bombs Away (my finsisher at the time, which was a diving headbutt from the top rope), Sinnamon jumped in my face and started screaming and yelling and gesturing wildly. The Dynamite Brothers looked confused. I turned and told them I would handle things. Then I clocked Tommy. Then Johnny. Then Roadblock and Wylde joined in and we beat the fan favorites down in the middle of the ring. I grabbed a mic and laid down my first heel promo, basically telling the fans that I didn't need them anymore and they could all go to hell. Something Hulk Hogan would do years later when he started the nWo. Fast forward 3 months. Machine Gun Smith had won the title in a sixteen man tournament. I wanted the title back, Steele agreed to put it back on me. I agreed to job in two matches to Smith, and he agreed to do the job in two matches to me, split up over the four weeks leading up to the Merciless PPV. Then the night of the match, we put on a 20 minute spectacular. I won when Wylde ran in and laid out Smith with the title belt. I can still hear commentator Tony Michaels screaming, 'NO DAMMIT NOT LIKE THIS! NO!' Yes, Tony, just like that. Fast forward to now, and my TV title match with Eva VanLorne tomorrow night. Was I ready? Is the sky blue? Is water wet? Is the Pope Catholic? Does a bear shit in the woods? Was I ready? Was Eva VanLorne ready was the question. I'm the fuckin' Machine baby. I've been ready since I was eight years old.
