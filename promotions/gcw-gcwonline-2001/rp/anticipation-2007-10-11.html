Anticipation

11 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/07 If there anything in the world Tessa Windsor hated with a heated passion, it was anticipation. Anticipation made title matches nothing more than stressful. Anticipation made the cab ride home from the airport seem that much longer. Anticipation had caused more than a fair share of problems inside her relationship And most of all, anticipation always, ALWAYS, made her second guess her self, regardless of the situation. It was nothing more than a miracle that nine months worth of anticipation hadn’t totally eradicated the petite in pink. She’d spent many a night scared to death that things would go wrong for her and her fianc’e. That she were eating the wrong products, or laying the wrong way, or being too active, when in reality, one humid afternoon in August would prove that everything was just fine. The afternoon that she’d brought upon this earth a beautiful baby girl, that’d shared her eye color, and smiled just like her father. Now, the only thing she had left to anticipate was her return to the ring. “Just one more week,” She thought aloud, as she scrolled her index finger across the week of the seventh, “Then I’ll hit the gym.” On cue, her reminder of just how difficult it’d be to fulfill that promise to herself chirped, or more specifically, cried, which in return led Tessa to let out a heavy sigh, “If I can ever coax Scott to watch Aislinn.” It was back to mother mode for the woman who’d in recent months been reduced down to nothing more than a manger for her fianc’e’s tag team. While she loved her fianc’e to death, being able to do nothing but lecture had grown to be quite a bore. On the other hand, she certainly had her hands more than full now between playing mother, fianc’e, and faithful ally to Vivica Valentine along with trying to fit sleep and getting back into shape in this entire equation. It seemed absolutely impossible. Not that it ever stopped Tessa from trying. “Where is he?” Scooping her one month old, Aislinn Elizabeth, into her arms, she darted straight for the bed and collapsed ass first onto the biggest one in the suite her and Scott had rented out in Cleveland. She’d met up with Scott back at five this afternoon, to assure that he’d be coming home soon. And of course, in typical Scott Drake, or more so Chris Storm fashion, he promised he’d be home shortly. After five years worth, she was well aware that Scott’s definition of shortly, was always two or three hours from the time. But shortly was starting to press into tardiness. Tessa, as well as the children, were starting to grow weary waiting for him. “I can watch Aislinn for you.” Christa, Tessa’s niece, offered her adoptive mother. She was well aware that her mother was about to burst, with both anger and concern, “If you need me to.” “No,” Tessa was quick to retort back, “It’s not your job dear.” She would have loved to argue otherwise, given that her big sister instincts had kicked in, but now wasn’t the time. “He better get in soon, or else?” “Or else”, would have to wait for another time. Not that she would have ever followed through with it anyways. Right on cue the hallway door clicked to confirm that Scott had finally returned from his jaunt. It also confirmed that whatever was left of Tessa’s concern, was about to transform into full fledged anger with a tinge of disappointment. Before he could drop his gym bag to the floor, she was on the attack, “What happened to coming here soon?” Automatically his hands lifted up in front of his chest, poised in a defensive position, “I had things I needed to do.” “Like what?” She questioned, barely taking time to catch a breath. “I needed to speak with my new prot’g”, and then catch a bite to eat.” He lowered his hands slowly to his side, hoping that his explanation would ease her nerves, “I’ve been gone all day. Haven’t had a chance to eat.” It wasn’t good enough. Not when she had things of her own she’d wanted to accomplish. “You could’ve come here to eat.” She thrust her head back into the pile of pillow at the head of the bed, “Hell, you could have brought your kids out with you to eat. You told me you were coming home soon.” His answer didn’t change any, “I was hungry. Sorry. I didn’t think you’d want to go out.” “I”.” She stopped to look down at Aislinn in her arms, then over at Christa, who was disappointed in the two of them, then over to the two boys, who’d passed out on the full sized bed just a few feet away. Her attitude was a disappointment to them all, “I just wanted to go to the gym, that’s all.” Scott’s eyes and ears both perked up. This had the potential to benefit both him and her, “I could train you.” “I know how to wrestle, Scott!” She shouted at the top of her lungs. He backed down instantly, his hands back up to his chest, “Relax, I’m just trying to help. I figure you could show this new guy how it’s done.” “I’m your fianc’e, not your damn pupil.” She hissed, laying Aislinn down on the bed beside her. He looked down at his fianc’e, who’d cuddled up beside their daughter. Something just wasn’t right. It wasn’t like her to be this offensive. “What is going on with you?” In a daring move, he plopped himself down by her feet, “You’ve been in bad mood ever since last weeks show.” As much as she would’ve liked to, she couldn’t look her fianc? in the eyes. She was far too disappointed in him, and even more so in herself for not being able to stand up to him, “I just need some time to myself, Scott. I need to get to the gym.” Taking the opposite side of Tessa, he wrapped his arms around his fianc’e’s slender waist, which she’d been nothing short of lucky to get back, “You need to relax.” He kissed the top of her head, hoping to chase away the anxiety, he had no doubt brought into her life. In return, she turned to face him, with an answer that would not ease any of his worried, “I need to get into the ring Scott.” “Not yet.” He begged, brushing a stray stand of hair out of her face, “I know the doctor said you’d be okay, but please, just wait a few more weeks.” Unintentionally he had just rekindled the war between them, “I was just fine last week.” “You were lucky.” “I was able.” She stared into his eyes, daring him to say otherwise, “Vivi needs some help out there. You and Markus need some help.” He wanted so desperately to argue otherwise, but he didn’t dare crush her spirit. Instead, he cupped her chin and let his eyes do the talking, “We can all handle ourselves. It’s not worth putting yourself on the line like that.” “Please’just let me?” She pleaded, avoiding his eyes like the plague. “We’ll see.” He responded, turning his eyes to his youngest love, who had somehow managed to ignore all the bickering between her parents, and fall into a peaceful slumber, “Just think about her as well. She needs you around. Don’t forget that.” He certainly knew how to tug her heartstrings. “I won’t,” She looked over to their daughter as well, overwhelmed by that fact that she’d managed to endure nine months of hell, to bring this precious gem onto the earth, “I won’t.”
