Love Lost In A Hale Of Gunfire Bleeding Through ---- Intermission: Keep Your Advice ---- A long time ago a dominant member of the GCW roster gave me a bit of advice on my first WorldWide that I held dear through most of my career. The words of a future Hall Of Famer. Words of advice that should be priceless to anyone aspiring to be great from someone who knows what it takes. Those words were: "You need to stop giving a damn about these fans, or you'll never accomplish anything around here." Lestatt Knight... go fuck yourself. I took your advice and I took it to heart. I turned my back to the people who fuel this business and let me live my dream. The people who keep a roof over my head. The people who let me look at myself in the morning and say I love my life. I may have broken your record for the longest reigning United States Champion... but I lost a little bit of myself. I was living a lie, hoping one day I'd be a hall of famer just like you. You saw where it got me. Bitter and broken... on a losing streak that lasted well over 3 months. My allies turned their backs on me, I couldn't look the fans in the eye. I had NOTHING. It was a lonely night when I realized that I didn't need your help. I got back to being myself again. I got back to enjoying the fact that I was alive. I blame 3 of the worst months of my life on you. First I lost my title to Brandon Youngblood. Then I lost my match against you. I'll take the World Title back... but first I'm showing you that not only do I not need your fucking advice. I'm better than you. It's sad to see what you've turned into. A member of the Ascension, once adored by the fans for sticking up against the big bad bullies in Dies Irae. Defending the honor of the federation you loved so much. Maybe you've been drinking a little bit too much of your own Kool-Aid. Now look what you've become. Sucking Caldera's cock for another title shot. It'll be my honor... no, my privilege to shatter your little power play right in the middle of the ring for the entire world to see. The legend of Lestatt Knight is just another pawn on my way to destiny. A washed up has-been... trying desperately to cling onto the spotlight. Pathetic. It's my time. Move the fuck out of my way... or I'll knock you the fuck out. ---- Chapter Eight: Revelations ---- The icy glare sent chills down Markus' spine. This was a man that he'd seen many times before, and was the only man who he thought could be more of a monster than himself. Christian Zenith... the manipulative bastard owner of Zenith Corporation. Leave it to a business executive to make a vampire feel innocent about his existence by comparison. "I don't belong to anyone." Markus grunted as he tried to regain himself after the electric shock that would've killed a moral. Unluckily for Markus... he was no mortal. In fact, immortal would be a better description. "I beg to disagree..." Christian pointed down at Markus and had the four men dressed in riot gear sit him up. As they touched him Markus glared at them and went to make a move, but the sight of the remote of Zenith's hand gave him second thoughts. Collared like a dog. Maybe this is what he deserved for what he had done... but not from the likes of Zenith. "I specifically remember you signing a contract for me. A contract that listed you as an experimental patient. I nor my medical team have checked you out. Do not think that because you ran away from me that our little project is over. In fact... it's just beginning. I own you... and I'll do whatever I please. You were the one who signed your soul away, and I intend to keep my business deals until the end." "I have no soul for you." Markus sneered back, "You took what little I had a long time ago." Christian smirked a little and began to pace a little as his partners had Markus' arms restrained behind his back and cuffed. Blair tried to squirm out of the cuffs but it was of no use. Like always Christian had done his homework. "You're right about that... you have no soul." Christian gazed into the eyes of his former patient, "It takes a soul-less monster like you to do the things you did. Not only are you a cold-blooded murderer, you've also made a knack of completely ruining the lives of everyone you meet along the way." "First... you wasted millions and millions of dollars. MY millions of dollars to be nothing more than a 'sensitive' war machine." Christian continued, "You have it all. You're immortal, you're intelligent. You're the perfect solider, you're a childe of destiny. Yet you choose to live a peaceful life. Staying indoors all day watching television hooked up to your IV. With me, you could be something. With Vivica? You're nothing more than her next shoulder to lean on." "You leave Vivica fucking out of this!" Markus screamed and Christian instantly squeezed the remote sending a shock through Markus' body once again. Squirming and shaking screamed helplessly as Christian looked on with a sadistic smile. "Don't interrupt me coward." Christian spoke out, "Then you go and steal my love. The love of my life Vivica J. Valentine. Right out from under my nose. To run away with her... thinking you're going to spend the rest of your life with her and live happily ever after. Let me tell you something pal. No one can love a monster like you. She was the first person to see your blood stained murder. You think she could love an uncontrollable animal? She's using you." "Liar!" Markus screamed. "Whatever helps you sleep at night..." Christian sarcastically responded, "but you know it's the truth. She'll do the same thing to you she did to me. Leach off of you everything she can, and leave you high and dry when she's done. Hell, she's probably already moved on to the next victim. Using him for everything he's worth why you're on your knees at my feet. Defending the poor whore's honor." "I'll kil..." Markus started to speak but was quickly interrupted by Christian's boot smacking across his temple knocking him onto his side with an electric collar around his neck and his hands chained together behind his back. With a couple of quick stomps to his ribs, Zenith made a point to show his superiority to Markus in his weakened state. "So why don't you tell me what it's like fool?" Christian got down on his knees and into Markus' face as the guards watched, almost like statues in a circle around them, "Why don't you tell me what it's like to lose the love of your life?" "She'll come for me." "She thinks you're dead." Christian spitted in his face before getting back up to his feet, "As far as she's concerned you are dead. Hell... maybe she's better off. Lest you kill her like you did the last person you loved. I don't know which one of you I feel more sorry for. I should've just sat back and waited to see which one of you stabbed the other in the back first. But I was never the one to sit back and watch. I'm the leader of the pack. The head hunter. I live for the thrill of the chase, I live to watch you squirm and let you know the kind of man I am. A dominant man. The alpha male in your sea of pathetic wolves." "What do you want from me?" Markus said, bleeding from the side of his mouth from the earlier kick... still on his side looking up at Christian Zenith. "I want you to suffer. I want to watch you break." Christian said, "I want to watch you pay for your sins against me." "I'll never break to a coward like you." Markus tried to pull himself up to his feet but is quickly stricken with another electric shock. "You stay on the ground like an animal. Like you belong." Christian smirked as he backed away towards the door he and his guards entered from. "I'll give you a little bit of time to think about what you've done childe. Trust me kid... you'll break. It's just a matter of when." Those were his last words as they left the room and the door shut behind them. Markus still laid on his side, a small puddle of his own blood on the tile floor next to his lip. As he looked across the room in a blur, only one thing still remained in Markus' mind. It wasn't revenge... it was Vivica's face. Smiling. "Hang in there Vivica..." he said to himself in a slightly hallucinating state aloud, "I'll come for you. He'll never destroy me. He'll never destroy our love." ---- Chapter Nine: A Change Of Destiny? ---- The hustle and bustle of the airport was business as usual for the many travelers. Some of them were on their way to see loved ones from far away. Others with their loved ones to consummate their marriage. Everything seemed to be moving in fast forward. Everything except one person. A woman sat in a chair waiting for her flight almost as if she was stuck in time, as life passed by all around her. A pair of cerulean blue eyes focused on a photograph... not blinking. A second away felt like an eternity for The Fearless Phenom. Restless nights left her eyes watery and tired. A bit of makeup make rings around her eyes almost invisible to the public. Yet looking in the mirror she knew. She could see the toll his death had taken on her. Taking a deep breath she watched as everyone boarded the flight to Miami, it was her flight. The last one she could take in order to make it to Warpath on time. She knew she had to get on that plane, but the first step was the hardest. It was a step that she didn't know if she could take. That one step would be the hardest step in her life. Looking down at the photograph again, she didn't know if she could do it. To just move on with life like nothing happened? What sort of memory would that be? No funeral, no nothing? It was these thoughts that kept Vivica J. Valentine from falling asleep at night. The images were nightmares in her mind. Daydreams of flames flickering the Nevada sky made her sick in the pit of her stomach. Closing her eyes she leaned back in the chair. Not looking at the picture she tried to make the first move. Nothing. Not even a flinch. Maybe it was all useless. What would one more trip to the site hurt? After all... he deserved it. "Vivica!" Suddenly a familiar voice broke her pity party that had almost let her give up. Opening her eyes slowly she saw quite possibly the last person she expected to see in front of her. The face of Tessa Windsor. Closest of friends in The Hunting Grounds. Bitterest of enemies during the wars of Dies Irae and The Ascension. She had always had a soft spot in her heart for The Teeny Tiny Terror. Vivica always saw a little bit of herself in Tessa. "Aren't you supposed to be watching the kids?" Vivica responded, obviously not in the greatest mood of her life. As much as she liked Tessa... now wasn't quite the time for chit-chat. The crossroads of life was upon The Black Mariah, and she'd stalled for far too long. "They're right here." Tessa said, stepping aside to reveal the faces of two darling little boys. They were 9 and 6 years old, respectively. The younger one looked just like Mr. Chris Storm himself... a future member of Banned & Exiled~! for sure. Poor kid. The other seemed quite a bit more bashful, but had a big smile on his face. "Miss Valentine..." The older one spoke as Vivica looked back at him. "Yes darling?" Vivica answered. The child looked down a little, obviously a bit awe struck from meeting The Fearless Phenom for the first time. "Could you sign my shirt?" He said holding out a silver colored sharpie. It was the first time Vivica noticed his shirt. It was black cotton... with a baby blue tribal V on it with the word FEARLESS in all caps underneath. It was Vivica's shirt. The sight brought a smile to her face, the first time she'd used those muscles in what seemed like years. "Of course hun." She said before reaching out and signing the shirt right on his left shoulder. "Me too! Me too!" The younger one butted in immediately afterwards handing her a Vivica J. Valentine trading card. Pulling it out of his hand, she quickly signed 'With Love, Vivica J. Valentine.' With love, the word lately had made the heart of The Fearless Phenom skip a beat. This time was different though. Maybe love meant something more than the way a woman loved a man. Love was never a word she'd used to describe her life. Love was never a word she'd used to describe the way she felt about herself. At this very moment though, she thought to herself... this is what she'd worked for her entire life. "The boys had been dying to meet you..." Tessa interjected with a grin from ear to ear, "they love watching you wrestle as much as mommy. That's what they said anyway." "Yeah!" The oldest one chirped up, "You're fighting Lestatt Knight tomorrow aren't you?!" Vivica glanced down at the picture, not answering right away. "He's a jerk." The boys statement made Vivica chuckle a little, "I hope you beat him! You're going to win the whole thing aren't you?" Vivica looked into the child's excited eyes. She couldn't help but smile. "I'm going to kick his ass..." She stated, "just for you." "LAST CALL FOR FLIGHT 709 TO MIAMI." An announcement was made over the loudspeaker as Vivica J. Valentine looked up at Tessa Windsor who just nodded. She knew what was going on with The Fearless Phenom. She had to. Who told her though? "It looks like you have a plane to catch Viv." Tessa stated gathering up the boys were still gawking at one of their favorite wrestlers, "it's time for us to go. Maybe we'll meet up with Miss Vivica after her big matches okay?" "Can we?" The younger one said, looking up at his role model. "It sounds great." Vivica smirked a bit before getting up out of her seat. It's the farthest she'd made it to getting on to that plane yet. Looking into the eyes of Tessa Windsor, she knew she had to say something. "Good luck... champ." Tessa said putting her arm on Vivica's shoulder. "Thank you, again." Vivica responded before walking towards the airplane terminal. "BYE MISS VIVICA!" The boys yelled as Vivica took her first step. It was the first step into the rest of her life. Deep down inside, even though he wasn't there... she could feel Markus watching her and smiling. He'd be proud. A right turn towards the destiny that she'd knew she'd always wanted since she was a little girl. It just took a little reminder from a couple of special little kids who reminded her so much of herself when she was that age. Who was she to deny them of their role model. Now boarding flight 709 to Miami. Next stop: destiny. After all, she did tell Lia Ambrosi she'd see her at the end. She had a promise to keep. ---- Chapter Ten: The Other Side Of The Curtain ---- The sound of "I Dare You" by Shinedown fills the arena as Vivica J. Valentine stands behind the black curtain separating herself from the ring. Wearing a black baby tee with a purple tribal V logo on it and a pair of black and violet Tripp style pants. She can see the eyes of thousands looking up at the screen, admiring a montage of everything she's gone through her in her GCW career. At the same time, similar thoughts are going through her mind. She was at the end. If there was a time to make a move... the time was now. A fury burned in her cerulean blue eyes as she went into her own world. Vivica was tired of people like Lestatt Knight and Jordan White getting the easy life. Getting handed things that she worked hard for. Tired of watching everything she's ever wanted ripped away from her. Everything from love to glory. This was the accumulation of every step she's taken in her life. This one wasn't going to get away. Lestatt Knight was the epitome of everyone who told her she what she could and couldn't do this night. Her parents who told her she couldn't be a professional wrestler. Christian Zenith who told her that her dream was nothing more than a silly game. The media who said that she'd be nothing more than a glorified jobber for the rest of her life. Brandon Youngblood who told her that she'd never win the big one. Virgil Ambrosi who told her that she didn't belong in Dies Irae. Lestatt Knight who told her than she'd never get anything accomplished giving a damn about the fans. Every single one of the were fucking wrong. Looking down at a picture of Markus for the last time and gracing it with a kiss, she placed it down into her pocket as "Image Of The Invisible" by Thrice began to play over the arena. Her eyes burned with a fiery passion. Global Championship Wrestling better watch out. This is her love. This will be murderous.
