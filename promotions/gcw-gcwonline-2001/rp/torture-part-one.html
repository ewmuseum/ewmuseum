Torture Part One

Date: Today Torture: Function: noun Etymology: Middle French, from Old French, from Late Latin tortura, from Latin tortus, past participle of torquEre to twist; probably akin to Old High German drAhsil turner, Greek atraktos spindle 1 a : anguish of body or mind : AGONY b : something that causes agony or pain 2 : the infliction of intense pain (as from burning, crushing, or wounding) to punish, coerce, or afford sadistic pleasure 3 : distortion or overrefinement of a meaning or an argument : Function: transitive verb 1: to cause intense suffering to: TORMENT 2: to punish or coerce by inflicting excruciating pain 3: to twist or wrench out of shape The camera pans open to a funhouse like setting where we see Keiko “The Dragon” Yoshimoto sitting on a bar stool in the middle of the room. Behind her is a something we cannot see with our naked eyes as whatever is behind her is covered up in a very thick black curtain. “Greeting loyal fans and some haters out there,” she smiles into the camera, “Last week was an emotional rollercoaster for the team of Fiery Passion. All last week we heard that we were going to lose to the team of Ciel and La Sombra. They were better than us, they had more experience and they would be the ones to face Future Shock to see who the number one contenders are for the tag team titles. Guess, that didn’t happen didn’t it.” From behind Keiko’s a voice pipes up, “Hell, no it didn’t happen.” Keiko rolls her eyes and gets off the stool. She opens the curtain to where she can see inside it and whispers, “Damn Hope, can’t you wait until it’s your time for this.” “I don’t want to do this,” Hope responds back, “I’m starting to have second thoughts here.” “Well, I’m not going to do it! I don’t trust your aim!” “And what makes you think, I’m going to trust yours.” “If you don’t shut up so we can film this promo here, you’re going to have your tongue ripped out by me personally.” “Fine! Keiko returns back to her stool where the camera pans down to see that there is a bag that is open at her feet. In that bag, there is an assortment of knives sticking out of them. Tied around one of the handles appears to be a handkerchief or a bandana. “I deeply apologize for that,” Keiko sighs, “However that slight little interruption is the point my partner Hope and myself is trying to make here. You see everyone thought with that match we would be the torturies and not the torturers. Truth be known is that Ciel got to see first hand the kinds of torture that Fiery Passion and the Knightmare Brigade: Dries Irae can dish out. Our kind of torture is not one you want to be on the receiving end of, for you will have to leave the ring with some form of aide.” Keiko walks back over to the curtain all the while she is talking to the camera. “It’s rather funny though, as earlier in the night, the grand leader of the Ascension at the time, you know Eva, was all big and bold about the Ascension taking over and trying to one up the KB. Who was left standing tall in dominance that night?” “It was us,” Hope shouted through the curtain. Keiko punches the outside of the curtain omitting a slight squeal from the hidden Hope. “Yes it was us,” the Asian laughs, “Even the leader of the Ascension got to know that first hand as she was betrayed once again that night and left shattered at the announce table. For all we know, they will be picking splinters out of her for a few weeks now.” “Then their is the announcement after Hope and I win our match, which we’re not going to get Future Shock right away. We’re kind of cool with that, as Future Shock is our comrades, and before any of you nay Sayers start with your brand of bashing, we want to tell you this. No, there isn’t any bad doings in the KB. Anyone who has followed us knows when it’s time to fight each other; we do it honorably, unlike the retches of the Ascension. That, I can promise you will happen. Torture is saved for those who try to oppose us. So Sereniti, I ask you do you oppose us, or are you going to fight with honor?” Keiko now reveals what is behind the curtain. Hope is revealed on a black and white polka dot bikini and her long blonde hair is hung loose. However she’s not standing upright. She is strapped to a large black circle by her hands and feet. The Dragon then smiles pressing a button on the table beside her causing Hope to spin around in a circle. “When I get off this, I’m kicking your ass,” Hope yells. “Just shut the hell up and we’ll get through this,” Keiko says taking out the knives from her bag. It’s revealed that the one object was indeed a black blindfold. She then places the blindfold over her eyes while Hope cringes in her bounds. “As you can see, most people think that our team turns a blind eye to our competition,” Keiko begins, “These rumors are actually what makes us stronger.” Keiko then grabs one of the knives and tosses it as hard as she can at the wheel. The knife lands just mere inches from Hope’s right hand causing her to scream from the shock. Keiko seems unphased and continues her speech. “These are the times where we may miss a few blows have a few setbacks, but we do finally hit our mark.” Again Keiko throws a knife, this time it slashes through the left bra strap of Hope’s bikini. A small trickle of blood falls from her shoulder blade. “Watch it, bitch,” Hope screams. Keiko ignores her, “You see there’s an almost seven foot giant awaiting us. A very challenging task to get through, yet one we’ve faced before in our other home. Two men that is bigger than us in weight and height.” Keiko throws two more knives one lands way above the head and the other lands just under her left foot. Keiko then removes the blindfold and turns away from her blonde friend. She then holds up a mirror and still is talking about Sereniti. “It takes quite a bit of balls to take on an actual Samurai and her student. This is an excellent way to debut in this league. Too bad, your debut will be your first loss. Don’t take it to hard though, Fiery Passion’s debut was also a loss, but look at us know, a running for the tag titles and we’re moving up faster in the ranks than Eva’s fall through the table.” Suddenly the doors burst open and Keiko screams as she sees two people come storming through the door. It was a bad enough of a scare that she tosses the two knives behind her back without aiming with the mirror. Hope in turn screams, not because of the interruption but because the two knives landed dangerously close to each side of her. “K-K-,” Keiko begins to say. Her hand presses down on the table where the button activates the wheel that Hope is strapped to. Hope comes to a stop right where she started to begin with. Although she is slightly dizzy, she too sees why Keiko is suddenly stricken with fear. “Kawanaha,” she screams, “Kit! It’s a pleasure to see you.” “Oh really,” Kawanaha Kuno says seductively, “Is that why you two bitches couldn’t be bothered to come home after the Dangerous Games Pay-Per-View.” “Do you have any idea what you’ve put my sister through,” Kit Kuno screams, “Every night she was worried you were dead or something worse. Let alone Christopher couldn’t even tell us where you were hiding!” “Then how did you find us,” Keiko questions. “Simple we used the Dragon.” Kit smiles, “After all, we know how to summon her thanks to you, and once we told her you two were in trouble. She came right to you.” “I can’t believe you two,” Kawanaha yells, “What in the bloody hell were you thinking!” “We thought you would punish us anyway for us losing at the pay-per-view,” Hope suddenly cries. “And you would have been right!” “But we won this past week! That would make up for it, would it not?” Kit interrupts her sister this time, “It does lighten the load of your punishment. We can’t physically hurt you badly because you are in the run for the gold now. That is the only thing saving you right now. However you still need to be punished. This time it won’t be Kawanaha dishing out your torture! This time it will be me!” Hope was thankful she was still strapped to the wheel, for she would have passed out like Keiko did after that revelation. Kit grabs a knife from the wheel and cuts the leather bandages loose. Hope screams as the Hawaiian dominatrix makes sure to jam the blade into her arms and legs as she does so. Finally Hope collapses to the floor in a blooded heap. “Grab that Asian bitch, and take the flying beast back to Hawaii, you’re punishments will be given to you both and that bastard child Christopher when we get home!” “What the hell has Christopher done this time,” Hope screams with thoughts of killing the Hawaiian where she stands. “What does it matter to you,” she spits, “After all, you want to see him dead more than I do. I figure you would take pleasure in it.” “I would if he had done something wrong!” “Watch your tongue you whore! I’m the one dealing with you personally. Every word you speak in hatred towards me will add to Master Clarkson’s fate. You don’t want his blood on your hands.” Kawanaha and Kit walk out of the room with Hope seething on the floor. She wraps her arms around Keiko and hoists her up to carry her out of the room. “I swear to God, one day I’m going to kill her for the honor of Christopher and myself. The hell with Bushido tradition, that bitch is mine!” Time: The Next Day Twenty-four hours later, Keiko and Hope along with Christopher Clarkson was sitting in the large living area of Kawanaha’s mansion. The three of them were handcuffed and dressed in what best could be described as rags. Kit and Kawanaha were also there, and they looked like their usual elegant self. Kawanaha is in her blue bikini with matching sarong, while Kit was in a traditional dominatrix outfit. Kit’s outfit was far more reveling than that of her sister’s for it was cut low and very clingy to the skin. “It took me a while to decide your punishments,” Kit said to break the silence, “I can’t beat either one of you physically to much. It’s a shame for me, but you two are still useful into finally getting some gold in this family. First with you, Christopher! Where the hell have you been lately” You are supposed to be out there managing these two bitches!” Christopher doesn’t even glance at her, “Because they don’t want me at ringside! And I respect their decision!” “Had you been there they wouldn’t have lost at the pay-per-view for fear of what you would do to them.” “I’ve never been afraid of this man,” Keiko shouts, “He’s nothing but a gutless pig who deserves any punishment you see fit to give him.” “I’m so glad you said that,” Kit replies, “For you see you two will be spending a lot of time with him this week. The two of you will pleasure each other in anyway possible!” “What,” all three scream. “I didn’t stutter.” “But she will kill him,” Hope cries, “I won’t allow them to happen!” “I’ve asked you once and I’ll ask you again! Why should you care?” “Because I love him you blind whore!” The entire group went silent. Hope sits there seething and her eyes glow red. Kit stands there unphased, but it’s Kawanaha that speaks up this time. “You love him,” she whispers. “Yes I do, Mistress.” “How could you love him” He’s evil!” “It doesn’t matter why! It’s the fact that I do! If you must know we’ve been together since the day I recorded the CD demo!” “You’ve kept this secret from me!” Kawanaha in her anger slaps Hope hard across the face. Not just once but many times knocking her to the floor. Hope tries to cover herself despite her handcuffs, but Kawanaha won’t let up. “You lying little cunt,” the Mistress screams at her blonde slave, “How could you do this to me!” “Dammit Kawanaha, stop this,” Christopher shouts, “If anything you should be beating me. You wanted her to like what I do to her, and she now does.” Keiko who had remained silent this whole time turns her head to Christopher, “I’m going to kill you. I don’t know what you did to her, but I swear to God I will kill you!” “I don’t care what you do to him now,” Kawanaha says walking out of the room leaving Hope crying on the floor. She looks up at Christopher with tears in her eyes. “I’m sorry,” she says to him. “I still love you,” he whispers, “She was going to find out sooner or later anyway.” Kit grabs Hope by the hair dragging her out of the house. “The key to you twos cuffs are in the cushion beside you,” she says, “Your punishments start now! This bitch is coming with me!” To be continued?.
