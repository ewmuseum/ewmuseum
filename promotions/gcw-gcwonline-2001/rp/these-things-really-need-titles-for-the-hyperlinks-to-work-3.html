Date: Timeless You know the disclaimer by now so I’m not going to repeat it. If you can’t handle this you know ehre the “back” button is on your browser! Morning came once again in the city of Shinoku and Joshua was up really early to get his breakfast and everything and at about 6 in the morning the two lovebirds in Ranmaru Samejima and Kei Enjyoji got up to an “American style” breakfast that Joshua had prepared and the two were surprised at the culinary masterpiece that lay before them. Joshua had made blueberry pancakes with his own whipped cream, bacon that wasn’t burned to hell and a couple of platefuls of donuts that could feed an army! “Morning guys” Joshua started as he was smiling at the two that stood before him wide-eyed at what they saw “this was my way of saying that I appreciate the fact that you let me stay with you for a couple of days and I wanted to make sure that you knew that it was being repayed in kind because I always repay my debts to people that help me.” Joshua told them and they both smiled sweetly at him as they took their places at the table. “Your generosity was always second to none Joshua and its what we loved about you even when you were younger. I wish you had stayed on the path to working full time at the University though becaue the Dean really misses you there.” Ranmaru told Joshua and the young man formerly from Tokyo, Japan and now from New York City for the first time in a whole literally had to fight back tears from streaking down his face as he remembers the friends that he had to leave when he had the money to go to wrestling school. Joshua loved everyone there equally and while he wouldn’t admit it to many people Ran was his first real crush. Ran made Joshua feel special and it didn’t bother him so much that Kei was his man now because he knew that he had Zack and that made him happy. As breakfast came and went Joshua went to the bedroom to go get changed into one of his suits that he had packed for the trip and when he came out both Ran and Kei were pleasantly surprised by what they saw: A man dressed in a tailor made black business suit with a white shirt and tie, a nice pair of black penny loafers and a pair of glasses not unlike Ran’s. The reaction that came from Ranmaru was one that Joshua never expected in a million years! “Oh my GOD Joshua you look absolutely gorgeous! What’s the occasion?” Ran asked and Joshua could only smile before he gave his answer to his best friend and ultimate supporter. “There was one thing I did forget to tell you last night Ran. While I am here I am going to be helping out at the University and specifically you my dear friend.” Joshua told Ran and the former Kendo star stood up and gave Joshua another really big hug! “You don’t know what that means to me right now Joshua. I never thought that I would get to work with you again at the University and now, now I get to work with you and you get to have a part of your life back that should have never left. Welcome home Joshua L. Curtis.” Ran said as tears streaked down his face and Joshua couldn’t help but smile when he heard that. “Its ok Ran I’m glad to be home. I don’t know why I don’t come back more often than I do to be honest when you deal with the bastards that I deal with in the front office at where they employed me. They don’t get it that when I’m happy I’m more focused on my work than when they screw me around. I just want to be happy and now I am Ran.” Joshua said as he placed a smallkiss on kiss on his cheek before walking out the door and getting in his car to go to work early to get the door unlocked for Ran when he came in later that day. As the day went on Joshua was happily taking care of business in the archives when some “roughnecks” in the school were heard outside the door harrassing someone on the staff and that didn’t fly with him, not in the least. If someone wanted to “play rough” with Curtis it was the wrong guy to pick a fight with and he intended to make sure that the perp paid for what he did! When Joshua walked out to the scene that was there he saw that a gang of about 4 to 5 kids shoving someone up against a wall and screaming slurs at them. “Why the fuck did you call me “hun” huh? I’m not interested in your kind or your lifestyle and I’d be happy if you left me the fuck alone!!” The kid screamed and Joshua had enough of his behavior by grabbing the student by the back of his shirt collar and throwing him across the hall into another wall. “Let’s get something straight sunshine! YOU do NOT lay a hand on student’s for any reason! You let them be and they will let you be ok” I’m sure the person meant nothing by hte comment and was just being nice to you but YOU took it to a point that makes me want to report you to the Dean. Hate Crimes will NOT be tolerated as long as I’m here and sunshine that’s going to be a while do you understand me?!” Joshua said to the student and the now terrified student just nodded and Josh let his grip go before the student scampered away from the crazed man. When Joshua went to go see who the person was that the student went after he was surprised to see Ranmaru a mess against the locker shaking visibly and trying to find his glasses. Ran? Ran its ok the student’s gone now you can stop haking.” Joshau told the visibly saken Ran before kneeling down, picking up Ran’s glasses and holding the man in his arms while he cried. The rest of the students that were gathered in the hallway cleared out and let them be but at the same time they wanted to get after the students responsible for the attack on Ran’s person and well being themeselves even though they knew that Joshua had everything under control. “I-I’m sorry Josh. When I got here some of the students were telling me that some kids started here and were harrassing people that were “different” from them. I went and tried to make peace with the kids and that is when they came after me and slammed me into the locker.” Ranmaru said and one student was walking by when Josh was with Ran and he made it a point to speak up to Joshua. “I saw what happened Josh. Those punks deserve to be beaten and beaten badly for hurting Ran and if I have to do it myself I will but I think you want to get after them as bad as I do correct?” The student asked and Joshua nodded but was curious as to the name of the student. “That’s very nice of you that you want to teach the kids a lesson but would you mind telling me your name” I’m not sure you and I have ever met and I’d like to know who I’d be thanking.” Joshua explained and the student very politely nodded and did as asked by Joshua. “The name is Kai Sagano. I’m the person whom is ranked #1 in the World inthe art of Kendo and I’m a big fan of Ran’s. I’m glad to see that you are here to help him and don’t worry I’m not mad at you for helping because Ran is a sweetheart and someone that needs people like you.” Kai said and Joshua took that as a compliment. “Thank you Mr. Sagano and believe me when I catch those kids they won’t like who they meet...believe that!” Joshua said as he took Ran to the Archive room and gave a silent “thanks” to Kai as he shut the door as he knew what would come within the next couple of days...
