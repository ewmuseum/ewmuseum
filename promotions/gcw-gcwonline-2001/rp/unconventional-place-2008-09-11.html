Unconventional place!

11 September 2008

Date: 9/11/08 A musing in the mind.... Quietly the world spins round and round as i sit here looking over at myself in the mirror. What have i become? Am i scared then i realize no i am not afraid of who i am. What was i in the past? Did i become more than what i was have i pulled my strengths together and built upon the foundations i fought so hard to place before me. Each stones throw into the pond ripples more and more until you lose yourself in those ripples. Sooner or later you will build enough stones to walk across that pond and become the one who has conquered it. Where do we go from here? Morning in Nevada... The sun slowly rises over the plateaus and mesas in Southern Nevada. The mountains in the distant background are as dark as anything ever seen. Shaman rolls over on his sleeper out under the stars as he grins and sits up looking at the dark sky turning blue as the sun rises in the distant. He turns and looks out towards the east. "Soon the sun will be rising over those mountains. Soon i will be moving in a powerful and strong direction. Soon Mayhem and i will discuss business once again." Shaman rolls to the side and uncovers himself as he looks at the world coming to life around him. "A new day brings new opportunities. Mayhap the new opportunity will be with Bryan coming to his damn senses. I am tired of everyone assuming they know what we are thinking so it is time to go straight for the throat and get to the bottom of the pile. No more fleet footing around the garden. I am going to stomp through all the damn tulips and get the straight answer." Shaman stands up and stretches as he turns and walks towards the edge of his deck. "Undisturbed land out here. Yeah i own the majority of it, Unfortunately here comes the distraction part." Suddenly two F-16's blast over head at high speeds heading out towards the southern part of Nevada. "I am still fascinated by fighter jets and World War 2 battleships, But then again this is fine by me. You know my favorite fighter jet is the F-4 Phantom thing is a flying tank. I'd like to fly in a Crusader those things are awesome." Shaman reaches down and grabs a pillow and blanket on his lounger on the deck and walks inside and heads over to his bed. He flops down throwing the pillow under his head. "Man i am still tired and i have a damn tag team match with Bryan to ...who was it again we face?" Shaman groans and rolls over. "Bah hell with it who cares business is business and needs to be handled first. Bryan and i need to get things on the same page and finished. Too many things i want to gather and gain within GCW." Shaman rolls over and pulls the blanket up over his shoulder. "Shaman is tired am too tired to deal right now." Shaman groans as the curtains automatically open on their own and shine sunlight in on him. "It hates sun, sun must go away!!" Shaman groans and sits up looking at the shades. "I should have painted the windows black or just left a wall there. Well who cares, down to business as needed." Shaman rolls over and grabs his cell phone and unplugs it from his charger. He then dials a number and waits patiently. "Hey Tyrone whats the number count for last night?" Shaman nods his head seemingly happy. "Damn that is some good figures. A ten percent increase from last week. Keep me informed on today's outputs and income. I am going to be flying to Chicago this afternoon to catch a Cubs baseball game at Wrigley, but anything nutty comes up contact Kawanaha and get it squared away." Shaman turns and grins as he hangs up the phone. "Good day gonna be a damned good week." Shaman stands up humming as he disappears walking out of the room.
