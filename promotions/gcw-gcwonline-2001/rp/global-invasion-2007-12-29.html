Global Invasion

29 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 29th 2007 Splat! Splat! Splat! Splat! “Hey Chris, why do we do this?” The ever wise-in-his-ways Storm turns to his partner in crime, Markus Stone, giving him a “whatcha talkin” bout Willis?” look. “Do what?” Splat! Grabbing another egg from the carton, Markus looked to Chris. “All of this.” To emphasis his point, he held up the egg in his hand before he cocked back and let it fly. Splat! Chris stopped what he was doing, letting the egg in his hand roll between his fingers. “I dunno, I never really thought about it.” With his free hand he took a moment to stroke his chin to give himself a bit of time to ponder the issue. “I would image we are living in a bubble of carelessness brought on by a lack of empathy to our positions within the company based upon our current market worth.” He winded back, aiming his shot. “Plus we’ve both been doing this long enough to be fucking rich out our asses. That never hurts.” And he let loose. Splat! A beautiful solid buckwood door with an elegant design carved into it became the next victim to the egg barrage. GCW Owner Steven Caldera appeared from behind the door, furious to discover the source of the sound, discovering his majestic Columbus home covered in what would soon become rotten eggs. “You damn kids! Just wait until I find you! I’ll have the cops come down on your asses so hard it will make a prison porn look like soft-core shit!” He had plenty more on his mind but Markus Stone already had an egg ready and nailed him in his red Ohio State robe before he could even take two steps. He looked around in the darkness of nightfall to see who had thrown the object running down and ruining his favorite robe, but the pitch black hid any criminal minds from sight. The two ran in any direction opposite of where Caldera faced, moving in top speed before any security guards could be alerted. Pranks for them were always much more fun when they didn’t get caught. A block away from the house, parked around on the curb, was a stretch black limousine in idle. The two dart into the limousine, a chuffer holding the door open for them before he moves around to the driver’s side and enters the vehicle. “I trust you two had a pleasant evening, gentlemen” the driver asked in a dead-on servant tone. Chris allowed himself to be absorbed into the plush leather interior of the limo, cracking his head toward the pane of glass that separating the riders from the driver. “It was great Bostwhich, we’ll have to have Kuff come around next time.” “Very nice” he told them as he pulled away the limousine and started down the street at a nice and slow pace. Reaching down, Markus grabbed a black aluminum bat off the ground and rolled down the window on his side, which faced the passenger’s side of the vehicle. Before he stuck his head out of the window, he looked to his partner, “I dunno Chris, I mean haven’t you ever thought about going singles?” “What you serious?” Storm asked; a mixture of hurt and confusion across his cut face. The two had been Tag Team Champions in GCW for over two-hundred and fifty five days, longer than any other tag team in company history. Why would anyone want to break that up? Especially when they still had the titles at the moment. Hanging out of the window, Markus Stone unloaded with a big swing and absolutely smashed the passing mailbox. Bits of wood and plastic fill in every direction with the mail as Markus ducked back into the safety of the limousine. “Well I was just saying, I mean it’s not like I was planning to break the band up. Just thinking.” The two switched places with Storm taking up the baseball bat. “Nah man, I mean we’ve both had enormous singles careers. Shit dude, we’re both Hall of Famers. What more?” SMASH! Pulling back in, Chris grabbed a letter that had gotten snagged on his shirt and tossed it back out the window. He offered the bat over to Markus but the Man Formerly Known as Zero shook his head and declined the bat. “Well I know that,” he said as he leaned back on the plush leather seating, “I was just thinking that we’ve been steamrolling all the other tag teams in GCW. I was just thinking it might be nice to get a change of pace.” With his feet kicked up, Markus Stone grabbed himself a small bottle from the mini-bar and kicked it back. It seemed Chris had been ready for that, lighting up better than a discount Christmas tree. Lifting up, Chris reached into his back pocket. “Gotcha covered on that!” he proclaimed. “That is why I signed us up for the Tag Team Battle Royal Open by Global.” “You what?” Markus spitted out, his drink spilling onto his shirt. Chris nodded proudly, showing him the contract. Sure enough, at the very bottom, was the name “BANNED & EXILED~!” Markus Stone had to hurry and finish his drink before he could even think of responding to what his partner had did. In fact, he befuddled him so much that he just had to ask “and what made you think that was a good idea.” “DUDE!” Storm shouted, “Think about it, what happens if we win the Global Tag Team titles?” He paused, allowing for his partner to think on it before he finished his sentence, “duel champions.” At that proposition Markus Stone had to rub his chin, giving it considerable consideration, “Duel champions you say.” “Think of it, the legacy of the greatest tag team ever will be solidified if we manage to win Tag Team titles in two different promotions” Chris told him, continuing to sell his partner hard on the idea. From the look of it, it seemed to be working on his partner. “With the PTC Tag Team titles retired, this might be one of our only chances to make history.” Markus laughed, slapping the hard-selling Storm over the shoulder, “you had me at hello.” Storm stopped, looking at his friend, “uh, dude, I never said hello.” “That was a joke, man” Markus had to inform him, to which Chris nodded his head. He sat up from his lounging position and took hold of the aluminum bat from the floor of the car, “and to celebrate.” The window rolled down, he leaned out of the window, and? SMASH! Sitting back into the vehicle, Markus looked to his partner and held out his fist. Chris nodded and tapped fist with him. “Global is about to get Banned & Exiled~!”
