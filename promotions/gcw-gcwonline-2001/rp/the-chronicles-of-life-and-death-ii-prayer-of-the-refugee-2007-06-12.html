The Chronicles of Life and Death II: Prayer of the Refugee

12 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-07 You’re alone, no friends, no help, and not even the belief in a higher being could save you now. What exactly can you do? You’re alone, because you’ve been abandoned by the people who were supposed to love you unconditionally for the duration of your life. You have no friends because you’re afraid to open yourself up to people. You have no help, because with every waking breath there is a strict drill sergeant barking orders at you. And you have no belief in a higher being because would a God of any kind make a child suffer the way you have? Is this the life a child should be made to live? Shouldn’t a child be able to live free in a home of loving parents and relatives? Those were obvious not the options and the only thing left lurking in your mind is the fact that all of this was your own fault. What is there left to do? Your heart grows hard and you feel nothing. The pain that you begin to endure on a daily basis has become that of mere repetition. The punishments for disobedience would only horrify normal individuals; but you they don’t bother anymore. Being locked inside of that dark, dank, dismal hole has become home for you. It has become a place of solace and refuge that you desire to be; if only to escape the world around you for a split second. The voices inside your head have become you’re only friends. The peers that surround you are nothing more than mindless simpering pukes. The kiss asses of the world in training, but you however, will not fall into that labeling. You’re far too strong for that to happen; you’re mind isn’t fragile like their minds. August 12th, 1986 Virginia Juvenile Military Boot Camp Richmond, Virginia It’s cold. So very cold, and my eyes are finally beginning to adjust to the darkness that surrounds me in my confines. I have to crouch in my pit, hugging at my knees, while my head scrapes across to the top of a square piece of metal that keeps me securely locked within. The only way out is to wait; wait until the door is lifted and the blinding light protruding from the sky above strikes your eyes. Then it’s back to work once again and with the first mistake made upon returning, it’s back to the hole. No matter how hard you try, eventually everyone will end up here at some point in their stay. Major Alexander doesn’t take kindly to those that are weak willed, and those that are will be punished accordingly. By the words that have been spoken in my direction I seem to have the least amount of any potential within my peer group. The hole has begun to slowly become my home, and the voices within my head are more a friend than the individuals I share most of my day with. I would much rather be locked within my dank, dirty hole as opposed to having to spend my time with the mouth breathers that encompass my surroundings. “No, I don’t think I’m the one who is losing their mind.” The young Jesse Ramey said as he rocked as far back and forth as he could within in home, “You must have me mistaken with yourself, because the last time I checked you were the one going crazy.” Jesse paused as he continued rocking, “You must have been watching someone else, last week I did not cry. There wasn’t a tear shed; you must have been watching one of the other kids here. I haven’t shed a tear since arriving here. Why should I even care what anyone thinks, or if anyone misses me” They sent me here, so obviously they don’t love me. I shouldn’t have to worry myself by crying over their mistakes. I’ve done nothing wrong.” “Spoiled?” Jesse questioned himself, “How was I spoiled” Were you there? No, of course you weren’t. I won’t even dignify that with a comment from you. All you want to do is cause problems for me, why are you even here?” Jesse pauses, “My only friend” You’re not my friend. I don’t need friends, and if I wanted one you would be the last I would even consider befriending. You need to just leave, because quite honestly there isn’t enough room in here for the two of us.” “You’re a part of me” I don’t think so, that’s the most ridiculous thing I’ve ever heard. You’re a psychopath! You could never be a part of me.” Jesse pauses once more, “Keep talking all you want, you’re never going to get me to believe a word that you say.” “How can you be here” How can you be talking to me? That’s a simple question; you just are. Nothing else can explain it.” Jesse stops rocking and lowers his head between his knees, “I’m not crying! I am not a baby! I am not spoiled! Leave me alone! What did I ever do to deserve to be stuck here” Why won’t you leave me alone? You remind me of the people I left behind, the people I don’t need anymore, the people who didn’t need me! All I want is my freedom, to live my life alone! Why can’t you people understand that? Just leave me to myself and I won’t ask anything of you.” “Always ask for something?” Jesse snapped his head back up, “I’ve never asked you for anything.” “That’s ridiculous!” Jesse barked, “You’ve got me confused with someone else. You must be thinking of someone else. I’ve done nothing to you and I’ve asked nothing of you. Please just go away and leave me alone. I don’t need you to try and bring me down. I’m strong, I’m strong minded. My heart keeps beating and as long as I wake up in the morning all I can do is try harder to get out of this place.” “I will,” Jesse continued after a pause, “I will get out of this place. Stop saying that! This isn’t permanent; this was only temporary! I heard it myself, with my own two ears; I heard what was said. I will go back home, I won’t have to endure this life forever. People want me home, just not the way I was. I’ve got to change; I’ve got to make things different before I go back. I need them to love me; I need them to want me. It was my entire fault to begin with; I should have been a better person. But I can change that; show them that I’m a different person and that I can be what they expect of me.” “How would you know?” Jesse continued to question himself, “All I have to do is put forth the effort and I’ll make it. This is something I want and as long as I put my mind to it I can achieve it.” “You don’t know anything,” Jesse scoffed, “you don’t know me. You know nothing of the person that I am inside. Have you ever taken the time to get to know me?” Jesse pauses once again awaiting his reply, “You’ve been there all along” That’s a little hard to believe, why would you just all of a sudden make your presence known if you were there all along. That makes no sense to me, and I’m not going to acknowledge it. I’m not going to listen to you anymore. That’s a choice I can make; I can turn you off anytime I want.” A long pause ensues as Jesse tucks his head back between his knees and begins to sob, before jolting his head back up, “Just shut up! You know nothing! You’re no help to me! Just go away; just leave me alone!” A slow unlatching sound is heard coming from outside of the metal door and then a creaking as the door begins to open. The light begins to shine forth within the hole and Jesse’s eyes are slow to adjust to the light. A blurry image looms overhead, but the voice coming from the other end is evident. “Ramey!” The shouting voice of Major Charles Alexander the III is heard booming across the grounds, “Who in the bloody hell are you talking to in there?” Jesse wipes the tears from his eyes and looks upward, “No one, sir.” Jesse barks out in a very military manner. “Dinner is done, Ramey! Out of the hole and to your duties!” Major Alexander places his hands behind his back and exits. Jesse slowly stands up from within the hole as the voices slowly begin to subside. The laughing, crackling voice continues in his mind, but begins to numb the further he makes it from the hole. He turns back one last time to look at the place he had come from. This won’t be the last time; and it won’t be the last conversation that he will continue to have with ensuing battle within.
