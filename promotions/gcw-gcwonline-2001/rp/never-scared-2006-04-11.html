Never Scared

11 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/11/2006 Never Scared (Part One) Journal Entry One Silly me. I thought this was something that could keep me out of the 'game'. How wrong was I. After five years of hustlin' and dealin' with the cops. Payin' em off, cuttin' deals with em' and so forth...you just get tired of it. Even though I don't really know if I could actually give up the game entirely, I really need to find a way to slide from this stereotypical profession for a young, well-dressed African-American black man before I get put away. Yea, yea, I know. That was wrong to say but it was the truth. Every time I were walking down the street dressed with nice urban clothing and my pants saggin' a lil bit, a cop would pull up like there were a problem. Pissed me off. First rule, never act scared. Date: April 8th, 2006 Time: 8:30 p.m. Location: Local gym in Flint, Michigan The night air breeze begins to water the eyes of those who have sensitive eyes. As it slightly drizzles outside, Orin whales on his favorite 'enemy', as he calls it at the local gym down the street from his house while a fight goes on in the ring behind him. Everyone focused on the fight while Orin continues his normal workout. The place was slightly run down, having been there for twenty years. Wallpaper tears off the building's walls as most of the equipment needs to be replaced. Except for Orin's special punching bag that he has personalized and put up every time he's there and taken down when he leaves. They respect him a lot there since his father helped open up the place. Most of the lights were out so it was moderate light in the building when it got dark outside, except for over the ring. The owner's name is Charlie. Orin calls him Chuck, which he hates it. He's there most of the time. He's aged in the face but his 33 years of living doesn't really do anything for him for his look. He almost never misses a day unless it has to do with his kids, Ryan and Phil. Orin hardly pays attention to fights, unless someone he knows is involved and even then...he turns and continues with what he's doin after the first round. Orin lays into the punching bag as Charlie turns away from the fight and walks towards Orin. Orin sighs as Charlie approaches. Charlie: OJ, OJ, O to the J! How ya doin today son? Orin: Son? Charlie: Sorry...kind of forgot. Orin: Yea, I bet. Charlie: What's wrong with you? Orin: Nothin'. Are you okay today Chuck? Charlie (irritated): You really need to learn how to respect your elders boy and stop callin' me Chuck for the last time damn it! Orin (shocked): Really? Or what? Charlie raises his hand to put his finger in Orin's but Orin smacks his hand down. Orin: Don't you ever...ever put your hand in my face...son. Charlie: I'm sick of your little attitude problem that you have when you come here. All I tried.... Orin (interrupting Charlie):...And I'm sick of you coming over here to talk to me, as if I give two shits about you what you're saying, and interrupting me with my work out. Now, if you'd excuse my back. Orin turns towards his punching bag and starts to punch it again. Charlie walks away with an evil look on his face and walks towards the ring. The crowd around the fight begin to draw off as Xavier King, a semi-professional boxer stands above the fallen challenger for the local title. Sad. Xavier seems to win all of his fights, except one... ---- Date: April 23rd, 2001 Time: 5:00 p.m. Location: Local Gym in Flint, Michigan The lights are bright and the audience anxious as the two contenders get loose in the ring. Orin Jacobs and Xavier King, arch enemies since grade school set to settle this right here in this ring on this lovely spring afternoon. Xavier was favored almost twenty to one. Orin coming in this fight with it being his first and Xavier hasn't lost since he began boxing, which was three years ago. Orin in green and black and Xavier in red and white. Xavier comes to the middle of the ring and bites down on his mouth piece as the ref calls Orin to the middle of the ring. Ref: Now, I don't want no low blows. I want a good, clean fight. When I say break, break. When I say to your corners, go to your corners. Now...shake hands. Orin sticks his hand out and Xavier rudely hits his hand down with force. Orin stares King as he puts his mouth piece in and heads towards his corner. Ref: Alright, lets fight. Ring the bell. The bell rings as King heads over towards Orin. Orin turns around to catch a hard right to the face and a left to the gut. As he goes for another right to the face, Orin ducks and lays a nice right, left, right combo to the face of King. Xavier catches his balance after stumbling back a couple of steps. He picks up his hands and throws a hard left jab at Orin that misses and Orin catches King in the gut with a nice right, left combo. King, seeming not phases by the body blows, catches Orin in the face with a hard right as Orin stumbles and hits the corner of the ring. Xavier rushes him in the corner and starts whaling on the lower back area of Orin, which is faced away from the ref. Audience: HEY! What is the fuck is that? REF! The ref rushes over to the corner and pulls Xavier away as Orin holds his lower back. Ref: Watch it King. One more time and that’s a disqualification. Ya hear me? Orin brushes off the cheap shot and rushes at Xavier. King swings with a right, Orin ducks and lays a left, right, left combo connecting with Xavier's face. Xavier stumbles back but Orin is up on him with another three hit combo to his face. King throws a desperate right at Orin but he leans back and comes forward with a right haymaker which sends Xavier to the ground. The ref counts... Ref: One...Two...Three...Four...Five...Six... The crowd around the fight are trying to get King to get up but the ref keeps counting. Ref: ...Seven...Eight... Xavier shows movement as he gets to his knees but he falls back down as the ref continues to count. Ref: ... Nine...TEN! Ring the bell. The bell rings as Orin leaves the ring with no celebration. The ref turns around to raise Orin's arm but to his surprise he's not in the ring so he attends to Xavier. ---- Xavier looks on as Orin continues his work out. Charlie walks to his office and gets on the phone. A few seconds past as Xavier walks over to Orin. Orin looks over and stands straight up out of frustration. Xavier: So... Orin (frustrated): What do you want, damn! Xavier: What's with all the hostility? I just came over to... Orin (interrupting Xavier): ...What? Wish me a happy new year? Xavier: No...I just wanted to inform you that Charlie wants me to whip you ass. Orin (attentive): Is that right? Xavier attempts to get closer to Orin but Orin steps towards him first. Xavier: Yea...he told me to come over here and not say anything to you, just kind of give it to you. Orin: Is that right? So what happened? Xavier: Well, I knew you couldn't fight so I thought it'd be wrong to just come over here to kick yo ass like that. Especially since I hear you started wrestling instead of boxing...along with your little business you got goin' on still. Hmm... Orin (confused): So, what you tryin' to say? Orin gets even closer to Xavier as Xavier throws off his gloves. Xavier: I'm sayin' that you ain't shit and you won't do shit even if I was gonna do somethin'. Orin: Hmph...Okay. Orin turns away but quickly turns around and lays one to Xavier's face as he falls to the ground. Charlie drops the phone and jumps out of his office and runs to where they are. Orin (turning to Charlie): What?! Orin jumps at Charlie and Charlie falls back and hits his head on the ground. Orin: Next time I come in here, since you all big and bad, me and you...Orin verses Xavier, part two. Lets see who's the one whipping who’s ass. The scene fades out and away as Orin takes down his punching bag and heads to the locker room.
