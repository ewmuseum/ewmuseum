Just another day

7 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7.7.08 Quietly in a locker room no sounds are heard as silence reigns supreme. A large man sits quietly untaping his hands from a recent battle royal he did not intend to win. A gleam is seen in his eyes as he looks around. Looking at both taped hands as he continues to untape them. His eyes focused on a simple matter at hand. The future of what is to come and how he will tame that future. Pulling the last of the tape from his left hand he then begins untaping the right as he chuckles and then continues. A cell phone rings and he stops untaping his right hand and reaches down into the bag and pulls it out. He answers the phone nonchalantly as if it isn't anything important. "What do you want?" He nods his head as he is listening. His expression turns to interested questioning as he begins to speak again. "So that is where he had been holed up all this time? No no Andy Murray this week........ Yes i was there and that was me returning....... Why did i not do better? Who cares my goal has merely just begun.... yeah yeah i hear ya see you on Monday!" Hanging up the phone he smiles and leans back finishing untaping his right hand. Shaman seems rather interested in what he is doing currently. Is there answers in his taped hands or is there a plan forming in his head? Who knows what he is thinking, sometimes it even baffles my mind and i am the narrator. Shaman casually stands and walks to a locker and pulls out a business suit and grins as he hangs it on the hook by the door. "Wonder why i am going to be wearing a suit? It is an easy answer ya bunch of jack asses. I am a businessman and own 2 Casino's and several Bars and clubs. Yet again i have to catch a flight to go out to GCW land again. I have to face Andy Murray this week. Chris Bagwell's nemesis and the man who pulled a cheap win over me last week. Am i bitter not at all truthfully i can thank the plebeian for giving me a bit of fuel for my fire. I'll tell ya what follow me in about fifteen to twenty minutes and i'll give you a little history lesson of past events that will lead to things to come." Shaman goes back to getting dressed as the camera fades to black. On an jetliner 7.7.08 20 minutes later As people scuffle about in between the aisles on the jetliner Shaman is stowing his carry on luggage above the seating assignment. he turns and sits down. Kind of wishing he had the window seating assignment. He gets bumped by and older woman's purse as he groans and rubs his temple. He looks at his cellphone and then looks around watching the crowd of people on the jetliner. He turns and looks over at a boy and grins and then leans towards the camera. "Let us have a little lesson here in past history. That little boy over there. He is outgoing and from his accent i would consider him to probably be from Scotland. Curious enough he reminds me of a Young Andy Murray innocent flashing a smile little full of himself as an adult. Yet with no concept of the real events going on around him. That child's innocence will be a boon and a curse as he grows older. Let us hope he doesn't turn out like Murray obsessed with Bagwell. Kind of makes you wonder if Murray is stalking or looking to Marry Bagwell. San Francisco has paperwork for that now Murray." Shaman leans back a bit as the pilot announces the plane is preparing to take off. Shaman buckles his seat belt as he looks at the stewardess and she smiles. "Can i help you with your seat belt sir?" Shaman raises an eyebrow and shakes his head. "No, but you can be a good little girl and get me a beer and some tequila when we are ready to be served." The stewardess looks at Shaman and frowns and huffs as she walks away. The guy next to Shaman smirks and laughs. "Sure told her." Shaman turns his gaze over at the man then smiles and narrows his gaze. "If i wanted to here from an ass i'd fart." The guy looked at Shaman kind of shocked, but went back to his business and simply shut up. Shaman grins and then looks at the camera. "Here is another example of GCW bull. Take this chump here sitting next to me. He reminds me of Chris Bagwell opening his damn mouth when nobody wants to hear his crap. Then again there is only one thing that works in all this aspect. Bust someones chops and laugh a bit. Then when you look back at everything you realize there are bigger jack asses than him. GCW Prez Caldera he is about begin a jack ass and enjoys it. The Establishment they used to be like the airlines once were. Strong and powerful until they started falling out of the sky and bringing in cheaper labor and parts. What do i mean simple economics. You see The Establishment had very intimidating people in the past. Including champions who were worth their weight in gold. What do we have in the Establishment now. That is right folks Chump lights.Nobody worth even mentioning other than being somebodies lackey. Truly pathetic in all senses of the word." Shaman leans back as the seat belt light goes off and he grins as he places his arm down upon the arm rest as the stewardess comes by and slams him in the elbow. Shaman grabs his elbow staring bullets through the stewardess. "Damn it i should have flown first class rather than coach." Shaman rubs his elbow grabbing is beer and then turns and takes the shot of tequila and drinks it down quickly. Another stewardess walks forward and smiles as she hands Shaman a closed envelope. he looks up and nods and stands up. "About damn time." Shaman stands up and reaches in the top compartment and grabs his carry on bag and walks to the front of the jetliner to the first class section. "See ya losers." Shaman grins and disappears through the curtains to the front of the plane. Chicago O'Hare 7.7.08 4 hours later Shaman stands on the people mover. he stands still as he watches the people walk by as the people conveyor keeps going he looks at all the posters and adds go by slowly. He stops and stares at a GCW advertisement and walks against the direction of the pathway. He chuckles and stops then turns and walks with the people conveyor. "Advertisements everywhere, i kind of feel like that damn caveman in the stupid gecko commercials. Stare as they go buy walk back to look at it. In all instances i tend to Wonder why the hell we become super stars and heroes to the masses." Shaman steps off the conveyor and walks to another terminal where he stops and notices a bar and walks in. he decides it is time to get a drink and relax before his next flight in two hours. Walking to the bar Shaman sits on one of the stools placing his carry on bag down next to it as he looks at the bartender. "Amberbach draft and a shot of tequila. Oh yeah give me some lemon and salt too." Shaman grins as he leans forward onto his forearms as the bartender looks at him. "Been awhile sine i have seen you stop in." Shaman nods and looks about as he drinks the shot down fast. "yeah about 4 years since i came through here last. Lately before all the crazy gas prices went haywire i drove around alot." The bartender nods and begins to wipe glasses and puts them away. Shaman drinks the beer slowly enjoying the gold taste and thick darkness of it. Shaman places the draft beer down and looks up at the tv screen and watches the local sports games. "Nice the Red Sox won against Minnesota in a close one. Too bad they split the series with the Yankmees." The Bartender smiled and waved his hand up at all the Cubs and White Sox memorabilia. "Our teams seem to be doing ok as well." The bartender smiles and leans against the bar and speaks. "Ok what are your expectations against Andy Murray?" Shaman looks up at the bartender. "Expectations none at the moment. I am in a match with him and my concentration is on Mayhem come Warpath." Nodding the bartender smiles and looks at him. "Well noticed you went back to the other place you spent so long at. Is it all for nothing you seemed to look like you were on the short end of the stick there." Shaman looked at the bartender and shook his head. "It was about the money plain and simple. They contacted me and brought me in for the money to return during its close ten year anniversary." Shaman simply brushed it off and returned to the beer. He looked up as the bartender began wiping the glasses again. "It always amazes me how under estimated i am or under used in all of the years. I gain things and not appreciated for it. I fight against the biggest and the baddest. Compete against the best and then win championships and titles. The worst part is i get angry everyday when i stop and look at the things that have been achieved and then suddenly you realize that once again you get looked over or someone in corporate decides you are going to be screwed." Shaman stands up finishing the beer placing it down on the bar top. "What do i owe ya?" The bartender waves him off. "Nothing man on the house." Shaman nods and turns and walks away after he picks up his carry on bag. Shaman turns and walks away down the corridor towards the terminal. He seems to be pondering something as his calm demeanor seems to begin to fade. "Something something what is it that is on the path. I know there is something missing a small clue and piece to the larger puzzle. I need to locate it and define it as i begin to rebuild the plans. I hate this crap of them forgetting who i am. Destiny is foretold by what you do." Shaman smirks and walks towards the terminal as he begins to laugh. "Maybe is Murray is lucky i will begin to show him what i mean." Shaman hands the ticket to the lady at the booth and he then takes the stub and then walks down the ramp towards the plane as he disappears entering the plane.
