A self conversation.

28 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/06 Shaman is seen walking through the Casino as he smiles and hakes hands with some of the patrons. He walks into the bar area as he takes a seat at the bar and raises his hand and a bottle of tequila is slid over to him as he sits there looking at it. Rubbing his chin he then reaches for the bottle and pours himself a glass of tequila and drinks it down. Not sure as to why he is sitting at the bar he turns his gaze up towards the TV hanging above his head. “Jeff Harris a new test for me. The caliber of opponents is tough maybe I should make a lesson out of Mr. Harris and take care of all issues especially Ryan. Why am I just casually sitting here watching life go by though?” Slowly he drinks and then grins. “Now I remember it is really simple. Because I can and nobody has anything to say or can do about it. I mean hell who is there really out there that is going to stop me. I mean look around you folks. I am the same extreme dangerous individual that I always have been. I have just been quiet basically.” Shaman looks at his glass and pours himself another drink. “Not everyone in GCW has to be melodramatic or even serious about everything. I don’t care who I piss off or even face in the ring. I have a job to do when I get there and that’s kick ass and take names later.” Shaman drinks the glass quickly and then leans back. Looks at the glass and bottle. “You know come to think of it it isn’t everyday you get a shot at a title when you come into the fed as a new person. I mean after all it is a shot at something prestigious. I mean a piece of leather tin plated gold around your waist is at least a start towards the top. I have already been there, but I do have the hunger again. It is the best feeling in the world to go after your opponent and beat them stupid to get what you want.” Shaman smiles and pulls several casino chips out of his pocket and holds them up and twirls one in his hand as he looks at the camera. “You know what this is” It is a symbol of what I hold for power in the real world. I can influence anyone and everyone with just a drop of one of these. It is consider money and the way of life in Vegas. Yet I am not out to do that here. I am looking at how I am helping create a need and a way to help my people. In the ring I am setting an example and showing people that even I can set my standards high and become something I wasn’t. It gives people hope and faith in a possibility a dream can come true.” Setting the chip on the bar he then pulls some others out and slides them to the barkeep. “Leo go ahead and give these to random customers. Show them that the Casino and Hotel appreciates their patronage.” Shaman stands up and takes the bottle of tequila with him. Walking through the Casino he looks at the wrestling museum area that he had created. He walks in and stops and looks at a life size replica of him when he first entered wrestling, with all the war paint and wrestling gear on. He smiles as he looks at his garb. “That was many years ago. I came into the wrestling world and wanted to make an impact for my people’s heritage. The situation failed miserably and yet I succeeded through sheer guts and guile. Yet I fought harder than any man ever should have to get what I wanted, While some got things handed to them on a platter.” Shaman walks on into the museum leaving his past self behind as he walks through looking at everything around himself. He stops at the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse display. He stares at the masks and reaches down and lifts the mask of Death. “One day these things will be prized possessions to a collector. Right now they are a symbol of power and history of my career. I was a member of the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse. Yet something was missing and the group disbanded as nothing would ever come of it. Yet we did destroy several portions of who stood against us. Never again will I wait as long as I did to make a point well known.” Turning he walks away to an area that says future sight of GCW memorabilia and matches. He looks to the left as an area reads WWA. “The past is always good to remember so you won’t be doomed to repeat it, but to live in that past is doomed to failure. I am not going to hold onto the past. It is not healthy for me anyways. I am in GCW and that is the here and now.” Shaman turns and walks towards the exit of the museum. “The path is set right now. Jeff Harris is my target as of the moment. I am going to hurt him and that will be it plain and simple. Your days are number Mr. Harris. It is time for you to open your eyes and realize that Shaman has come to take care of business and you are simply in my way plain and simple.” Shaman smiles and stops. “Jeff Harris will be made an example so Dorian Ryan can pay attention to what is coming.” Shaman stops and makes his way out into the crowd and disappears.
