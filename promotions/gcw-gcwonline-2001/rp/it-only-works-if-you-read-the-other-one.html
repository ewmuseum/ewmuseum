It only works if you read the other one.

Date: Like, kinda late. 'What are you thinking about?' She asks. 'If I told you... It would ruin everything.' 'Ruin what?' The Marauder is silent, the white of his mask nearly fading into the white of the pillow under his head. A dim lamp sending shadows dancing across the entwined forms of man and woman, sheets stretched to cover and shifting with the movements of a lovers groping. 'Some things are better left as they are.' Taking her hand in his own, The Marauder lifts it to his lips quietly. 'It only works if you let yourself believe.' 'You're confusing me Zeta.' Her head pulls away from his broad, hairless, chest. His lips curl into a grin. 'What are you thinking?' He asks. She sighs in defeat and lowers her head back into the crook of Marauder's armpit. 'Actually I'm wondering something.' She says softly. 'I'm wondering what you look like under that mask.' Her head turns to peer up at him, bedroom green eyes tracing the lines of his jaw and nose. The Marauder fails to speak, but before she can persist a growling erupts from behind the closed door. The Marauder reacts instantly, pulling his arm clear and slipping out from under the sheets with a powerful grace. The light bouncing off his bareback he creeps to the door and crouches close to the wall. Shadows dancing beneath the crack, sporadic and quick, the dog senses something. Alert in bed the woman coils bedding around her and draws the linen up to her chin silently. A creak. The dog howls and barks. Footsteps, a yelp, more barks. A pair of feet approach the door, the knob begines to turn, The Marauder tenses, cautious, the door starts to open... Marauder grabs it and throws it open quickly, catching an outstretched arm he flips the invader over his shoulder and to the floor. Putting a knee against the man's throat before he can react. Eyes squeezed shut tightly lest they bear witness to the manhood dangling but a foot from his forehead. Marauder twists his body as the two men from the other room move closer and hesitate. With a click The Marauder presses the invaders own pistol against his forehead, dead cold eyes glaring at the twin barrels aimed in his direction. A not quite Mexican stand-off with a naked not quite Mexican Luchador. 'Oh my god, David!' The woman screams and covers her face. 'Don't make any sudden movements and you might live through this.' The first gun weilder says through thick lips. 'Marauder stop!' The woman screams again, rushing from the bed with a flutter of fabric and descending upon the prone and breathless David. His face red with desperation, but powerless to free himself from the powerful olympian of a warrior. 'Claire!' David manages to yell. Marauder gives him enough for a breath but doesn't move the barrel even as Claire fusses over the scene anxiously. 'Put down your weapon and get on the floor, hands on the back of your head!' The black man says again. 'I don't see a badge.' Marauder says in a hushed tone. 'As far as we're concerned, this is a home invasion, and this is self defense.' Moving his weight, Marauder muscles David to his feet and puts the man between himself and the two in the living room. The dog retreats into a far corner, still bawling in confusion. 'This, David, is where you get to make your choice.' The Marauder speaks into the man's ear, the gun pressed firmly against his spine. 'When you get angry, you like to fight with your wife, you avoid her face so that people won't see. Do you know why you're afraid people will see?' David frowns, his eyes moving about but it's clear nobody but him can hear The Marauder's words. 'It's because you're ashamed of yourself. And now, tonight, when you found my note, you became so filled with jealously you were ready to come here and kill us both.' 'They'll never find your body.' David mutters with false bravado. 'You still okay in here baby doll?' A feminine voice, lilting and exotic, floats into the tension and draws a barrel towards the front door. 'Always were my favorite babe!' Marauder replies. Two guns point through the door and the gaudily dressed hooker in the center is relaxed enough to hold her own pistol at ease. The two cops were now outnumbered, flanked, and dealing with a hostage situation. 'Marauder stop it!' Claire calls from her spot in the corner. 'Just gimme a minute.' Marauder says loudly, pressing his lips close to Davids ear and he starts to whisper again. 'You see David, this is not your lucky day. First your wife, now this, what next? Your job?' A grin. 'Didn't you see the sticker that mentioned video surveillance on my door while you picked the lock?' A chuckle. 'See, luck as it happens to be is a lady, and a very fickle one at that. You treat her right and you might come out tall, treat her bad... Well. Hell's Fury. Woman scorned. Or so they say.' 'Marauder!' Claire calls again. The dog continues to bark, the cops in the living room try to reason with the hookers holding guns, David sweats, and Marauder nods slowly. 'You take your wife back home, you tell her you're sorry for driving her away. You treat her right and you save yourself from ever having to go through that pain in your gut ever again, that twisted betrayel that tastes like vomit in your mouth and burns holes in your heart. Because that's what she felt every time you hurt her, everytime you raged...' With a shove Marauder sends David sprawling. Claire reacts instantly and falls ontop of him, tears pouring from her eyes as she sobs. David rolls over and looks up at her. 'I'm sorry.' He mutters quietly. The Marauder backs off a step as the two in the living room lower weapons and the hookers take the advantage. 'C'mon boys, let's just get you back in your car and forget you ever come here.' Marauder's favorite says, her heels clicking against the wooden floor boards as she shifts towards the bedroom. 'Nice doll.' A finger traces down Marauder's chest, who looks at his nakedness unabashedly. The room filters quietly under the pretense of drawn weapons. David stops at the front door as Claire turns to The Marauder one last time. Without a word she takes his hand for a long moment and then turns away. David's arm falls over her shoulder and they walk out into the hall. Marauder looks at the little silver cross in his hand before closing his fingers around it reverently. 'You leaving again sugar?' The hooker asks. The Marauder slips his legs into a pair of button fly jeans and shrugs as he fits the belt buckle into place. 'I got some big things coming up, gotta catch my bus.' 'That whole wrestling thing?' 'Something like that' He replies to her skepticism. 'When you coming back?' 'When it's needed.' Her lips purse at his response. The Marauder pulls a shirt over his head and starts shouldering his way into an ankle length trench coat. 'How did you get her here anyway?' She asks before he can leave the room. He stops with his back to her, the mask lowering and turning as he speaks over his shoulder. 'Some things are better left as that are.' He lifts his sports bag and pats his dog's neck. Convinced now it's a cross between a lab and some sort of terrier. 'It only works if you can let yourself believe.'
