Double Vision

23 June 2007

Date: June 23, 2007 __Start He knew it was wrong. The way she cooed. The way she pleaded. Everything she said came out in such a pleasing manner that there was no possible way she could be truthful. 'I'm glad I met you.' She struggled with the words and Jay took his finger to her lips. She went quiet again and stared deep into his eyes. He couldn't see though. His vision was lost to Daniel Franco at this moment. No matter what he thought about, it somehow ended up running back to his conniving manager. His face was the one he saw when he looked down at Heather. Those thick black rimmed glasses looked up at the outlaw accompanying that devilish smile he had created over the years. 'Oh baby,' she spoke again but Daniel's lips moved in unison with the feminine voice. Jay jumped from his place and fell to the floor beside the bed. 'Is everything ok?' He looked startled as she moved to the edge of the bed. In Jay's head, she was still a he and that he wasn't someone he wanted creeping toward him. 'Get away.' 'What did I do?' Her lips pursed in a mock kiss fashion and Jay quivered at the sight. 'Keep the fuck back.' 'Come back up here baby.' Her long smooth fingers were replaced by hairy knuckles and bitten fingernails. They tapped over and over on the bedspread taunting Jay to come back and lay down again. He shook his head. 'Stop!' He tried to shake the thoughts loose from his mind but they had no intent in leaving. 'Fuck!' 'Baby...' Her voice was empty now. The tone shift made it obvious that she knew something was wrong. Nevertheless, she continued to stare at Jay while he laid spread out on the carpet below. His eyes were shut and his mouth was hanging open in a dumbstruck fashion. The door to the room opened abruptly and to their surprise, Daniel stepped through. His eyes met Heather's and then traveled to Jay. 'What's going on?' 'Just shut up!' Terror shot without looking. Daniel took a seat beside Heather and made sure his eyes were kept to the floor as she put her clothes back on. 'What happened?' 'He just started freaking out...I'm so confused.' 'Jay, come on now. It's Daniel.' He twitched on the floor and rolled over to his stomach covering his ears. 'I'm not listening.' Franco turned toward Heather and spoke quietly, 'Do you think he knows?' She hooked the last loop on the back of her bra and shook her head unknowingly. 'I couldn't tell. He just fell to the floor and told me to fuck off.' 'Watch him, I'll be right back.' 'But...' She started to speak but he was on his feet and then gone all in a second. She turned her line of sight onto Jay's back and listened to him making random noise. His body was bare and showed scratch marks running deep into his skin. She smiled at the sight and shook her head playfully. 'You know, I wish we didn't have to do this. You aren't that bad of a guy Jay. It's just...I can't disobey.' Daniel glided back through the open doorway with a squirting syringe in hand. The needle flashed in the light of the room and Franco jumped down to the floor beside Jay. 'What are you going to do?' She asked. 'He needs to be sedated. My guess is that he's falling even deeper into the Jay Terror character than he needs to, especially at home.' 'You mean you're going to tranquilize him?' 'It's for his own good.' The two exchanged glances for a good moment. Daniel was the first to look away and as he did so he took the needle and dug it into his lower back. Jay jumped at the initial prick and tried to grab at the pain. Daniel ripped the syringe out and Jay rolled onto his back again. 'Fuck yo...' He was out. Daniel stood up and grabbed Heather's hand. He kissed it softly and she smiled playfully. 'Let's let him be. He needs his rest.' __________ Often times people wonder what life is like from a different perspective. For fourteen year old Tommy Goldbaum, he had the lucky experience of learning about Jamison Talley from that perspective. Being his neighbor, Tommy had yet to receive any perks. He gathered that Jay was a wrestler. He also knew that Daniel was a mean man who never waved when Tommy waved to him. He had learned that they had a girl that liked to come over a lot and he had seen her tits almost every time she was over. Jay and Daniel normally took turns from what he saw. With knowing so much and seeing just as much, he'd hope for something more than just being neighbors. Perhaps a shirt. A wave. Anything would do so long as it showed they recognized his existence. As he looked out from his window he poked his telescope lens outside and pointed it across the street like normal. The scene was regular to him. The girl who came over to the house a lot had her shirt off and her face was pinched in with her eyes closed shut. She looked very angry and kept bouncing like she was excited. It was really hard for Tommy to figure out anything that was going on. Uncaring, he continued to watch. Jay had no shirt on and Tommy watched him flip the girl under him. He saw his lower private part and knew very well what that was. Having one himself, he was aware what it was used for. Tommy wasn't getting why Jay would go to the bathroom in the bed with a girl around though. He watched closer and saw Jay fall to the ground. This was where he zoomed in and paid closer attention. Daniel walked into the room and soon after ran out. He came back with a big shiny thing and stabbed it into Jay's back. 'Huh?' Jay stopped moving and Daniel and the lady walked away hand in hand. 'They killed him.' He pushed away from the telescope and turned around facing the door of his room. 'Mom,' he shouted aimlessly, 'Jay is dead!' She questioned him and he yelled back, 'Jay from across the street. He's dead mom!' She warned him to stop spying on the neighbors and decided to ignore everything else he said. Tommy turned back to the telescope hoping Jay had moved but he hadn't. He lay on the floor, naked, in a fallen heap. Tommy wasn't exactly the brightest kid in the world but he knew a thing or two about life. The fourteen year old crossed his room and found the phone on his dresser. He picked it up and dialed three digits in. He drew the receiver next to his ear and waited. A voice kicked in and Tommy cut it off. 'Someone killed Jay. They stabbed him!' The man on the other line questioned him but Tommy ignored it. 'Jay Terror. Daniel killed him with his lady friend.' They argued to the point where Tommy screamed in a high pitched voice and slammed the phone down on the charger again. He dashed across the room and focused the telescope onto the street where Daniel and the lady were walking outside. 'I need to save Jay.' __________ He sat for two hours before Jay stirred. At first it was only a twitch. It started in his hand and ran up his arm and across his upper back where it mimicked into the second arm. 'Uhhhh.' He said through gritted teeth. Tommy watched his eyes flicker from half open to fully closed and then back to half open. They never did open all the way. 'Hi.' He said in the simplest and least perky way he possibly could. Jay tried to look but he was still blind. His limbs wouldn't do what they wanted and his mouth wouldn't form the sounds they were supposed to. Instead he moaned and smacked his lips together in an undecipherable manner. 'I thought you were killed.' 'Ugh.' He could feel his fingers moving again and his arm started to gain some sense of navigation. All the while he kept smacking his lips trying to form words. 'I brought you a Band-Aid and some lemonade. When I get hurt mom gives me lemonade and I feel a lot better.' He holds the Spiderman Band-Aid in his left hand and a small cup of lemonade in the other. Jay begins to find more and more movement as the seconds pass until he is able to sit up entirely once again. 'What happened?' He shakes his head off and the words come out clear enough to be understood. 'Daniel stabbed you and I thought you died. But now you're sitting and not dead.' Jay stared blankly for a minute and tried to move his arms. They lifted off the carpet slowly and his hands gripped around the cup. His first sip was rather unsuccessful seeing that the yellow lemonade dripped down the front of his chin and into his unclothed lap. 'Stab me?' 'Yeah, he had this big needle and he brought it over his head and he stabbed it in you. And then you stopped moving and you were dead looking.' 'I feel dead looking.' 'Was that a joke?' 'Did you find it funny?' 'I don't know what funny is.' 'Right...' He tried for a second time with the drink and found it much easier to handle. The liquid was refreshing as it spilled down his throat and wet his insides. 'Where did Daniel go?' 'He went away with the lady friend you were bouncing on.' Jay wanted to question so many things about the boy but decided not to. He had more important issues to deal with before finding out how he broke into the house and why he was watching him in his room. He tried to stand and as soon as he got to his knees he felt the pressure on his lower back. It was obvious something had injured him with the pain seeping deeper into his body. 'I need to get out of here.' 'And go where?' 'Anywhere.' 'You can live with me. Mom could make you lemonade and give you more Band-Aids if you need them. We have superman and trains too.' Jay arched his brow. 'Yeah...not so sure about that. I don't even know your name.' 'I'm Tommy Goldbaum from across the street. My mom is Charlotte Goldbaum and she is across the street too.' There was something odd about Tommy that he couldn't quite put his finger on. He looked, in Jay's eyes, to be at least fifteen or so yet he acted like he was ten. The way he spoke and the things he said didn't seem to fit the typical teen his age. He set the questions aside like the rest of them though and extended his hand for the boy. 'I'm Jay. Thanks for the help.' He shook his hand back and as he did so Jay felt something he hadn't felt in a long while. He wasn't accustomed to shaking hands or thanking people. Doing it with Tommy though made him almost smile. It was odd. 'You should get home though.' 'Mom doesn't know I'm here and she wouldn't want me outside this late.' 'Then sneak back into the house.' 'I don't know how.' 'Come on, I'll take you home.' He found a way to stand and after stretching, him and Tommy walked out of the room and downstairs. They stepped outside and made their way across the street. __________ Daniel liked the feeling he got when Heather held his hand. Part of him knew that she did it out of fear but a small portion figured she might genuinely like him. As they strolled through the park he smiled when he looked over at her. 'What?' She questioned. 'Nothing. Just smiling.' 'Uh huh.' She didn't bother to find a reason behind it. Instead she accepted it for what it was and smiled back his way. They rounded another bend and she looked off into the distance. 'It's nice out tonight.' 'Gorgeous.' His eyes weren't on the night sky. Instead he focused on her tight shirt and short skirt. 'When should we get back home? I mean, he'll be waking up soon, right?' 'Possibly. Jay's not that strong so I'm sure we have time still.' 'Will he know what happened?' 'He'll have a big headache and probably be unable to move and talk for a while. Other then that, he'll think he fell over and hit his head.' 'I like that.' He wrapped his arm around her shoulder and drew her closer to his body. She allowed herself to get cozy against his jacket and placed her head on his shoulder. 'I like it too.' __________ He had never talked to any of the neighbors. He'd seen a few of them getting in and leaving their homes but never said a word. For the most part they didn't enjoy the fact that he smoked and wrestled. The bulk of the people surrounding him had kids and both of those hobbies were ones they didn't wish for their children to participate in. 'Mom is nice, don't worry.' The kid's face was so reassuring that he couldn't help but believe him. 'If you say so.' He twisted the knob and walked into the front room of the house. The TV was the first thing that came to their ears after shutting the door behind them. Tommy led the way up the mini flight of stairs and Jay followed as he surveyed the room around him. The overall layout was very much like the one in his and Daniel's home. On the wall across from the front door, though, was a large picture. At first it was hard to make out what exactly was being portrayed in the paint but after squinting his eyes and tilting his head to the side he found a male with his head opened up. His brain was split into small particles and hung above the skull as if it were showing off his knowledge. It was...different. Jay found his way into the next room and looked in at two different faces. One was Tommy’s; his teeth were shown in full. The other was his mother's, or so he presumed. She was young by her looks yet elder by the way she received him. 'You're not dead I see.' 'Not the least bit.' She glanced Tommy's way and he kept his smile going. 'Tommy says that you are going to stay here?' 'I wasn't planning on it.' The boy blurted out, 'But you can't go home.' 'I wasn't planning on that either.' 'Where were you going to go then?' His mother questioned. Jay hesitated and looked around the room for something that could spark an answer of some sort. 'A friend's place.' 'We have a guest room, you can stay for tonight until things are back to being normal for you.' 'I swear I'll be fi...' 'No,' she interjected, 'You stay here tonight. Tommy will bring you to your room.' Jay shook his head and gave in. He watched Tommy stand proudly and give his mother a kiss He led Jay away from his mother and into his room. Something told him life was about to change. __End
