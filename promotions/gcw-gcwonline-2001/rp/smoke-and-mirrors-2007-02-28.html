Smoke and Mirrors

28 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 28th, 2007 Key's rattled in the door, the harsh jangling of metal on metal disturbing the previously silent state of the apartment within. The soft fluorescent lighting of the hallway quickly seeped in as the door opened, quickly engulfed and obliterated by the warm sunny glow already suffusing the rooms inside. A swift and sudden kick opened the door the rest of the way, dislodging packed snow from the tall black boot that had struck the door and propelled it into the copper doorstop on the adjacent wall. An armful of Safeway bags followed the kick, proceeding their bearer by almost a foot before she managed to struggle her way into the apartment and kick the door shut again with a grunt of annoyance. ?You'd think I'd never been grocery shopping alone before.? The bags owner muttered, kicking off her boots and padding down the linoleum floor on sock feet before turning onto the tiled floor of the kitchen. Setting the bags down on top of the chest style freezer, the woman known to the wrestling world as The Steel Rose leaned back with her hands pressed against her lower back, groaning as she eased out the kinks from carrying her groceries up. ?There's not even that many of them.? She groused, eyeing the bags balefully. Although numerous, the bags themselves contained only the the very basics for someone suddenly found living on their own. Milk and orange juice went into the fridge, followed quickly by sliced meat and a jar of pre-sliced pickles for sandwiches. Bread and bagels went into the pantry next to bags of rotini noodles and boxes of white craft dinner, while extras went into the freezer with frozen waffles and a long box of pizza pockets. Dusting her hands off, Dawn leaned against the freezer, folding her arms under her breasts and casting a critical eye over the sparse kitchen accoutrement's. Slowly, unwillingly, her eyes drifted to the one thing posted on the door of her fridge, held in place by a KB: DI flexible magnet. Predominantly white in colour, roughly three inches high and five inches long, with simple black lettering stretched across the front of it. Depente's Gym 740 Elgin Street West Vancouver, BC [detail withheld] Ian had given her the card before she left Ontario, when she'd come to his office practically begging for instruction. The refusal still stung more then a little, a nettle of pride that had wormed under her skin and was busily making as much of a mess as possible...but the truth of the matter was that he'd been right to refuse her. Claiming he wasn't the right teacher for her, he'd handed her the business card currently adorning her fridge, informing her that ?Joseph Depente is one of the best outside of the Order. Maybe he can help you find what you're looking for.? That was almost a month ago, and it had taken her three weeks to gather up the nerve to call the number on the card and ask for an appointment...to an answering machine. The cold, businesslike, tones of a woman obviously getting on in years had informed her that no operators were available to take her call and if she would please leave a message then someone would get back to her. That was a week ago. Now she stood against her new freezer, in her new kitchen, in her new apartment and wondered for what seemed like the hundredth time what she was doing here in the first place. ?Not like anyone's left back home anyway.? The words had come without her bidding, slipping from her lips to hand in the air like a leaden choker around her neck before being carried away. The Estate was all but empty, everyone who had once sheltered within it's walls having moved on to their own futures elsewhere, leaving Dawn without a clear sense of purpose. Lia called at least once a week, and they saw each at WorldWide, but she was worlds away it seemed when night fell and there was only a cold bed to crawl into. To her surprise she even found herself longing for the sarcastic bite of Virgil's humour, the sardonic wit that earned him the ire of many and the respect of those willing to see the man behind it. Everyone she cared for had left the Estate, even Vivica had moved on entirely, leaving it a shallow shell of the place it had once been. Yet out here on her own, kilometers away from anyone she knew, it seemed like the most reasonable of responses to flee back to the safety of it's stone walls, cower in her old bedroom and pretend that nothing had changed. But everything had changed. Everything but her. And so she'd come out to British Columbia on Ian's advice, chasing... ?Chasing what?? She snarled, turning on her heel and stalking out of the kitchen. ?Some self absorbed gym instructor who can't be bothered to call me back? A place of my own that doesn't feel like a fucking tomb?? Sock feet sunk into the dark carpet of the living room, her black-blonde hair whirling out behind her from the violence of her sudden movement, long legged strides carrying her to the wall unit that predominated the living room...or more appropriately the large collection of pictures that dominated the top most surface. Pausing before the forest of frames and glass panels the look of frustration eased from her face, replaced with a look approaching wistful or nostalgic. Emerald eyes roamed over each picture on the unit; Many pictures of her and Lia, some taken with Virgil or Vivica and even a rare backstage picture with the elusive Bryan Mayhem and herself, still flushed with victory over La Sombra. ?So why...? She murmured, head coming forward to rest against the cool wood of the wall unit. ?...does this place still feel like it's eight million meters wide and I'm the only one in it?? It's not like the room wasn't well furnished, or was spartan but for a few things. She was leaning against her new wall unit, across the room for the couch that Lia had bought her when she'd talked about getting her own place. The southeast corner was entirely dominated by a corner piece desk and computer, so far untouched beyond being plugged in and set up. Even the concrete porch outside her sliding glass window had a tatami practice mat and a simple lawn chair...but the entire place felt empty all the same, just as bad as the echoing halls of the estate. ?Hell, nobody even calls aside from Lia.? She snorted in amusement...just before the phone rang. Starting in surprise she stared at the cordless wall unit for a moment before dashing across the room to snatch it off the cradle before her answering service picked up. ?Hello?? ?May I speak to Mrs. McFarlane please?? The woman on the other end spoke in clipped, businesslike tones, and sounded altogether very familiar. ?It's Miss, and you're speaking to her.? There was a slight sound of disapproval from the other end, as though her caller resented being corrected. ?Yes. I'm calling from Depente Gyms, regarding the message you left us earlier.? ?You mean a week ago?? She couldn't keep the annoyance out of her voice, teeth half bared. This woman was treading on her last nerve. ?Indeed. Mr. Depente is a busy man you know...but as a result of your message he believes we can fit you in today at two thirty.? A quick glance at the clock showed it to be five minutes past two, and Dawn couldn't help but quirk an eyebrow. ?Twenty five minutes? That's hardly notice.? ?If you'd prefer we can reschedule...I believe he has something free in...June.? Dawn was certain the gagging noise she made resulted in a smug smile on the other end, but she ground her frustration down. ?Two thirty it is then.? ?Excellent. Please be prompt, he doesn't like to be kept waiting.? Click. Running her tongue along the inside of her mouth in frustration, Dawn set the phone back in its cradle with exaggerated gentleness, as though she were afraid even the slightest pressure would rip it from it's moorings. ?Prompt indeed.? She grunted, stalking towards her bedroom for her biking leathers. ******************************************************************** Although not ideal for winter travel, Dawn's custom Ninja could maneuver the back streets and crowded traffic ways of Vancouver's downtown core better then her corvette coup. The sleek black and red machine howled through the busy streets, it's mechanical scream announcing it's presence long before it arrived and long after it had passed by. Heeling the bike into a sharp turn she slipped between an angrily honking cab and a guardrail, easing her bike into the smallish parking lot next to a large squat building. Depente's gym had started life as a product warehouse, intended to store farming supplies and equipment that never materialized when it's parent company went bankrupt. The building sat dejected and abandoned for almost a decade before a young entrepreneur by the name of Thomas Corbec purchased it and had it renovated, reselling it for almost twice what he paid to one Joseph Depente. The outside of the building had been painted a steely blue-grey, and the opaque windows of the past had been replaced with larger windows to allow for better lighting and air flow. Large glass doors with security bars had been installed in the front, and it was through these transparent gates that Dawn stepped, kicking snow from her riding boots. Not far from the door stood a tall man in a dark blue suit, his surfer blonde hair cropped close to his head and spiked up with styling gel. As she watched he looked down at his wrist watch before looking back up at her, the cut of his sleeve unable to hide the gleam of gold. An air of annoyance hung about his shoulders like a cloak, reflected in his eyes as he pulled down his designer shades to look over the rim. ?Miss McFarlane?? He asked, taking in her form fitting biking suit. ?That's right. You must be Joseph Depente.? She stepped forward, offering one hand. ?Please, call me Mr. Depente.? He said with complete sincerity, taking her hand in an overly firm grip. Gritting her teeth she squeezed back, not enough to hurt, but enough to show him that she wasn't going to be muscled around by a suit and an eighty dollar pair of sunglasses. He released her hand and smiled as though it hadn't happened, slipping the offending hand into one of his pockets. ?I'm afraid I don't have much time, would you like a tour while we talk?? She couldn't keep the frown from her face, her eyes narrowing into emerald slits. Didn't have much time? He was the one who'd called her down here in such a hurry and now HE didn't have enough time? Without waiting for her answer he turned towards the gym proper, waving with one hand to encompass the entire building. ?As you can see I've got quite an operation going on here. Depente's has a long history of offering quality training for athletes such as yourself, so we're always busy.? Much to Dawn's dismay, the reverse seemed to be true. The gym was painted the same grey as the outside, although it seemed a little livelier without constant exposure to the elements. A small set of offices sat in one corner, and the rest of the building was taken up with practice matting and exercise equipment...all of which had certainly seen better days. The mats were cracked and peeling with age, most of the machines showing corrosion and in some places overt rust on their previously sensitive mechanisms. A group of lean looking men and woman seemed to be milling around, all of them dressed in the same collared shirt and black shorts. At a gesture from Depente they started towards the pair, their loping prowl setting off alarms at the back of Dawn's head. ?This is my staff. They've all got years of experience in their respective fields, and they've all served Depente Gyms well during their terms of employment.? Judging from the looks on their faces and the drab uniforms, those terms had been a lot longer then any of them were comfortable with. They moved to slowly surround the pair of them, a large man with a name tag that said ?Robert? moving out of sight to her left and a ropey woman named ?Anne? slipping out to her right. Dawn let her helmet hit the floor, her restrictive armoured jacket following quickly, the noise causing Depente to turn around with an amused smile. ?Oh don't be alarmed, they're just eager to welcome you to the Depente Family.? ?Oh yeah...they look real eager.? She returned, loosening her hands and checking over each shoulder slightly, locating Robert and Anne. ?I bet we're all going to be one big, happy family.? A darker man, his name tag marked Alex, made the first move, feinting forward and trying to push her back towards the two behind her. Taking an aggressive step forward she slipped inside his reach and plowed an elbow into his sternum, wrapping one arm around his head and felling him with a sharp knee lift to the head. Instinctively she dropped into a low crouch, lashing out out with one leg and catching the other woman, Aya, as she advanced with a sweeping kick. The wind whooshed out of her would be attackers lungs and she spiked a heel kick down into her solar plexus, driving any remaining wind out and curling her into a fetal ball. Rolling sharply she slid to her feet, having dropped the field to three, Travis, Robert and Anne. The former of whom both advanced in tandem, backing her up towards the nearest wall, arms wide to keep her from darting away again. Cold concrete touched her back and she shot forward, tucking her body in against Roberts and stamping one heel down on his foot. A howl of pain answered her attacked and as she ducked, Travis' punch whistled over her head and caught his companion high on the cheek, dropping him to the floor like a marionette with it's strings cut. Not one to pull punches, Dawn leaned her weight back onto her palms and snapped a sharp kick up between his legs, feeling the heavy buckles of her boot jar against his pelvic bone. Travis pitched over with a groan, his hands going between his legs as the colour drained out of his face. Dawn was fast to her feet again, but not fast enough to avoid a stinging kick that she caught on her right bicep, a tingling sensation spreading down her arm and numbing her fingers. Warily she circled Anne, sure to keep her eyes on where Depente stood with his hands shoved into his pockets, watching with apparent disinterest. ?Not bad.? Dawn commented, trying to draw her opponent out. ?Tae Kwon Do?? ?Wing Chung. Sweeping Hawk style.? Came the gruff reply. Nodding, Dawn stepped in close and Anne stepped back...or tried to, finding that her lead foot had been trapped under Dawn's, who followed in with an open finger strike to the windpipe that sent Anne choking to the floor. Wheeling around she advanced on Depente, eyes narrowed as he held his hands up in surrender. ?Whoa whoa, there's no need for that...whoa now. It was all just a test okay? A test, I'm not really Joe.? The stopped her, words spat out between clenched teeth. ?What?? ?I'm not Joe, this is just a test. It's standard for certain people, and you're one of those people...although you did better then most, man.? He said sheepishly, looking past her at the gasping and moaning men and woman on the floor. ?A test?? ?Yeah, he wants you to meet him outside the gym.? Reaching into a breast pocket he pulled out a folded piece of paper, holding it to her with one hand. ?This is the address.? Reaching out she snatched it from his hand, eyes still rooted on his. ?Give me one good reason I shouldn't leave you like your friends back there.? Grinning widely, Not-Depente shrugged, adjusting his tie. ?I havn't done anything to you.? ?Fine.? Reaching out she snatched the sunglasses from his faces, noting with satisfaction the way he flinched away from her hand. One sharp movement and she crushed the expensive eye wear in one gloved hand and let it fall to the floor before turning on one heel and stalking towards the door. ?You'll have to pay for that.? He called after her, earning a middle finger over her shoulder. ?Call the cops, I fucking dare you.? ****************************************************************** It wasn't hard to find the address on the piece of paper, a trendy downtown coffee shop that called itself ?Arcadian Dreams? and seemed to cater to a crowd of artists and poets, writers and actors...and one older African American man in a black shirt and green cargoes, with a newspaper on the table and a smile on his face. As she approached he rose from his chair, holding out one huge hand. ?You must be Dawn, I'm Joe Depente, pleased to meet you.?
