The streets are calling...

4 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-4-05 For Jayce Bradley, the streets of San Antonio are not a happy place. Not in the least. They are at best a concrete jungle, playing habitat to the most despicable animals to grace the dirt ball we call earth. Drug dealers, hookers, pimps, crackheads, and rapists. Animals that prey on their fellow humans out of no other reason then to satiate the human desire for self destruction. It's a desire inherent in all men, the cause for wars, for murders, for abuse. It all comes down to that small burning instinct that man contains to destroy oneself and those around him. However, he walked these streets with confidence. Jayce was no stranger to self destruction, and the path that such a hunger leads one down. After the death of his brother, heroin addiction laid claim to most of his teen years. Before that, it was rummaging in dumpsters for food or clothes. In those times he had seen things that no one young or old should have to ever be subjected to. Murders, overdoses, gang related shoot outs and beat downs. Oddly enough you start to become numb to the shock of seeing a total stranger face down in a pool of his own blood, or your best friend covered in vomit; a heroin needle still stuck in the small crook of flesh on the inside of the elbow. That; however, all seemed distant now, there wasn’t the white-hot sting of heroin withdrawals racking his system. The black jeans, boots, and t-shirt that now covered his thin, muscular frame, paled the rags he once clothed himself in. Under it all; however, the clothes, the overcoming of addiction, these streets and alleys winding through the south side of the city still haunted him. You can't leave the past behind, and when you try it tends to walk up and smack you in the face until it all comes flooding back like so many bad dreams all at once. Such an instance brings us to the here and now, on these miserable streets that house the American depravity, amidst these ramshackle project buildings and paraphernalia riddled alleyways. It comes calling again like a banshee. A long mourning howl of the past revisited in shocking detail, as the camera seems to find itself in the wrong situation at the right time. 'YOU FUCKING BITCH! YOU FUCKED HIM DIDN'T YOU!' The voice came rolling out of the blackened corridor of an alleyway, carrying enough venom to poison the air around the sound waves that comprised it. 'I..I..promise I didn't....I woul...' The second voice, a female, broke in before trailing off. 'DON'T YOU LIE TO ME!' The sound that followed carried louder than the voice itself. The sound of flesh on flesh--reminiscent of a pistol blast--shot against the night air, followed by the pitiful whimpers. 'That’s alright...I'ma make it so don't no one touch you again girl.' Jayce turned the corner just in time to see the flash of cold stainless steel flicker beneath the slivers of moonlight that managed to break through the blanket of smog that permeated the air of his hometown. However, he was a second too late to prevent what happened next, as the blade lashed out. For a brief moment, he could see the fear in those beautiful baby blues of the woman flicker across the blade, before it etched its thin crimson line from the corner of her left eye to her chin. He moved quickly as was his trademark, but his target was a step ahead and gone as Jayce's boots carried him to a skidding stop before the hysterical babbling woman. Her face had an inherent beauty, almost a regal bearing beneath the river of blood and tears that had stained her fair skin a crimson hue. Thick raven tresses clung to the trickle of blood flowing from the wound--her lips swollen, split, and trembling. She looked upward, her eyes meeting his for a brief second, a brilliant pale blue--almost white--meeting cold steel, before rolling to nothing but white as her tiny slim body slumped forward. Bradley stooped quickly to catch her, before she face planted the concrete. He stooped low, bracing himself before lifting her lithe frame away from the grime covered floor of the alley; holding her close to him as he stood. He cast a glance over his shoulder briefly, before turning from the alley toward the street. It was the people that did things such as this that proved just how bad things can get in those forgotten places that dot the country. Places where even the most atrocious acts are viewed as nothing more than commonplace. As Jayce slowly walked the street, unnoticed by those few cars that passed, and catching nothing more than a sidelong glance from the two people he passed, the memories swirled about his head. They coiled like snakes nestling against the inner folds of time gone by. As he walked, his shirt started sticking to his chest; quickly becoming matted with the blood that flowed from the girls wound. He saw himself playing back through his mind's eye. He saw a young boy, no more than 16, laying in alleyway, his cheek resting in a puddle of muddied stagnant water. A bit of vomit leaking from the corner of his mouth. The needle still hung from the vein, choked with a belt, the plunger only half way through the dirty brown fluid that filled its small glass chamber. He again saw the boy, younger this time, beaten, bruised, and crying. His knees hugged to his chest as he hid in a small corner of a cramped apartment much like the one he now rented. He paused a moment, his eyes closed as he cradled the girl in his arms, pushing the memories back before proceeding on the few hundred yards that lead him to the hap hazard building that housed his apartment. Nearly an hour later, Bradley sat on a milk crate adjacent to the cot that served as a bed; his eyes focused on the girl before him. His elbows rested on his knees, his hands holding what at one point and time had been a white towel, though now seemed more crimson. He let those steely blue orbs dance over her features--soaking them in. She was petite, no more than a bit over 5 feet tall. Her build held a tight, compact, well muscled physique similar to a dancer's. Her face was what held his attention the most, soft features coupled with ivory hued skin, the angle of her cheekbones was gentle, but strong enough to hold one's attention. Her nose held a slight upturned angle, though not enough to take away from her overall beauty. Her right cheek was bandaged, covering the smooth incision that had carved its way across her visage. Gently he rung the towel out as he examined her face a bit closer, letting the towel push away the remaining bit of dirt and grime that clung to her cheek and forehead. He sighed as he withdrew the towel, gently tracing her features with his fingertips. Obviously this slicing wasn't the first abuse the young woman had suffered. Her jaw line held small lumps just below the surface of the skin, where bone had meshed unaligned with bone--healing in knots. His fingertips slid gently along her hairline, feeling the goose egg just past her forehead--hidden under those soft ebony locks. He sighed a bit, lowering his eyes enough to take notice of the crisscrossing scars that zig zagged there way cross her inner wrists. He almost felt a kinship with her seeing those scars. Its a bit of a reassurance to know that pain isn't a solitary thing. Jayce moved quietly away from the girl, standing as he tossed the towel in a corner, retrieving a second from the gym bag he left situated by the door. He soaked it in a pot of warm water, before ringing it out and placing it across her forehead. His thin arms crossed his chest as he stared down toward her, a soft sigh passing over his lips. Kneeling down, he gently slid a blanket, though it looked more like a big rag, over her. Nestling it under her chin before stepping back, the camera sliding around to take in just his upper torso and head. His face slightly hidden behind stray locks of dirty blonde hair, as they fall like a curtain over his features. Another soft sigh emanates from his lips, before the scene begins to drift off into silence. 'Who the fuck are you?!' The voice came quickly, defensively as the girl sat bolt upright. Her eyes held the mixture of shock and confusion of someone waking from a bad dream, only to find themselves safe in their own bed. The camera took in her visage, as well as the look of surprise on Jayce's face before it slowly faded out.
