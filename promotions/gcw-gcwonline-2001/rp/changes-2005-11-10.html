Date: 11-10-05 “The worlds going to freakin hell in a handbasket.” Mary switched her TV off. What with the Tsunami’s, hurricanes, tornadoes, earthquakes and bombings that seemed to frequent the news, she was just about done with needing to hear anymore about any of them for a while. Of course she couldn’t get away from all of those disasters. She had the results of one living in her house for the best part of indefinitely. The three children she had agreed to look after while their teenage mother was ill in hospital after being made homeless by Rita, were out visiting her with a charity liaison. The house seemed unusually quiet. Mary’d gotten used to having the lil rugrats around. She tried not to think about what it would be like to say goodbye to her adopted family. Some consequences aren’t worth considering until the last minute. She could hear a pin drop just before she stepped on a stuffed rabbit. It belonged to the youngest of the kids, little 4yr old Kanye. Mary picked it up and took it upstairs to the spare room he shared with his sister Alize and his older brother Jack. On her way back from the spare room she went into her room, her bathroom to be exact. She opened the bathroom cabinet and took down a small pill box. Inside the box there were no pills though, only three flat foil rectangles. She stared at the un-used razor blades like they were some kind of puzzle. Then she spoke as if she was speaking to them. “Dunno why Mary keeps buying you things. She promised people, she wasn’t gonna hurt herself no more.” She gazed back up at her reflection in the mirror and looked sad. “Damien made her stop hurting herself, he made her promise. He made her so she didn’t have to, so she didn’t want to and then he turned on her and made it so that she wished she could keep doing it until she wasn’t here no more. She wanted to be gone forever, to just curl up and sleep and never ever wake up.” She sighed and touched fingertips to her reflection gently. She looked apologetic. “Mary’s sorry Marianne. She dint mean to hurt us again. Just sometimes things get so hard and Skye and Mary said they wasn’t gonna get messed up in the drugs again. Not like they was so this is all Mary has if she cant have the rest to help her get through.” Her reflection smiled back sweetly, innocently. “It’s ok Mary. I understand. You do it to keep in control, so you can protect us like you promised you would. Forever and ever.” Mary nodded back at the reflection. “Sure thing Marianne. Mary’s gonna look after us. She’ll always look after us. Mary loves us.” Marianne smiled back and nodded, “I love you too Mary. We don’t need those things anymore, not now.” Mary nodded back, “Nope, we don’t need them, not now.” She was smiling again and this time the reflection smiled back with just her reflection. “Things are gonna be different now. People tried to hurt Mary, tried to take Mary’s happy ever after away, tried to make her cry, tried to break her.” She pouted, “But not this time Jimmy. Mary gotta brand new bag and she’s packing it full of ass whip.” She looked slightly confused but then smiled impishly, “then later, some creamy whip. It’s time to take back Sunday damnit!” She skipped out of the bathroom just as the doorbell rang. Arael who was downstairs by the door, got to it before Mary and let in Skye, his boss’s daughter and Mary’s long time, best friend. Skye burst in more than walked in and slapped a piece of paper down on Mary’s coffee table. “I take it you’ve seen this shit?” Mary looked confused. She picked up the piece of paper. It was a print out. She read the headline. It was a print out of a transcript of the latest GCW card and its results. “What’s up Skye Blue, Mary dint know you watched the wrestling that hard.” She forced a nervous smile knowing Skye wasn’t buying any of it. “Seems like I don’t watch the shit enough, either way I never saw this coming. Turns out lover boy is even more of an asshole than I first thought Mary. You need to stay away from him. I don’t know why you helped him out the other week but look at this shit. He ditches you for that Eva and then shits on her too. The man has serious fucking issues. I shoulda ended him when I had the fucking chance.” Skye was all of a sudden heaving with anger. Mary on the other hand was confused and slightly uneasy at the same time. She put the piece of paper down at looked at Skye, really looked at her and this almost caused Skye to look away. Arael kept quietly to the side. “When you had the chance?” Her head did that involuntary tilt to one side that she did when she couldn’t quite grasp the concept of something she was seeing or hearing. “What” It doesn’t mean any?.” Mary cut her off. She stepped closer to Skye, almost in a stalking manner. “que voc” f’z?” Mary asked her in Skye’s native tongue. Mary only ever went bi-lingual on Skye when she was really happy or really pissed. Skye was betting on the latter. She racked her brain for a cover story but came up nil. She was actually fighting the urge to scream the truth. Mary asked her again, “que voc” f’z?” Skye didn’t reply. She glanced over to Arael and Mary raged inside. “DON’T LOOK AT HIM, QUE VOCE FEZ SKYE!””!!” She lowered her voice back to normal with some effort. She took a deep breath. “Mary’s gonna ask you one last time Skye Blue. What did you do?” Skye’s gaze dropped quickly to the foil packet in Mary’s hands. She’d forgotten to dispose of them before she greeted Skye at the door. She was on her way, she just forgot. This time Skye’s eyes were the ones to hold the beginnings of a fiery anger. She snatched the razors from Mary’s hands. “You wanna know what the fuck I did Mary” I tried to save you from shit like this.” She held up the packets. “Shit like Roberto fucking Abitua or Lestatt Knight or whatever fucking personality he decides to show at the time! He’s a fucking cancer Mary! From what you’ve told me and from what I’ve seen, everything and everyone he touches breaks. He’s pain, he’s death and I should have had him fucking assassinado when I had the goddamn chance!” Mary couldn’t breathe for a minute. She was just silent. Even Arael asked if she was alright but she didn’t respond, she barely acknowledged him at first. Then she stepped closer to him. Her voice was so low they could barely hear it. “Get her out Eddie.” Arael looked at Mary then at Skye. Skye gave him a warning glance that he didn’t need to see to feel. “Stay where you are Eduardo or it’s your ass!” He stood still. “Get her out Eddie please. Mary don’t want her here no more.” Her voice was dangerously calm. Arael shook his head. “Can you both not just?” Mary didn’t let him finish. With lightning speed she whipped one of his guns from the holster on her nearest side and had it aimed, two handed grip, straight at Skye’s face. “GET OUT BEFORE MARY GETS YOU OUT!!” Skye took half a second to be shocked before anger overrode it. “Voc” ousa amea’ar-me? After all I’ve fucking done for you. AFTER ALL THE FUCKING PIECES I’VE PICKED UP AFTER YOU!! And you point a gun, at me, Skye?” “YOU TRIED TO KILL HIM!!” Mary forced her voice lower while Arael contemplated how to get the gun back. “Give me the gun Chica, you don’t want to do this.” Mary ignored him. All her attention was for Skye. Nothing else existed right now. “I tried to protect you from any more pain Ty, don’t you see” Look at what he’s doing to you. Look at what he did. He nearly killed you in that fight at his mansion. You join the federation to be closer to him and he spits in your face by dating Eva behind your back. He disrespects you on live TV in that ring and backstage and you still go running back. You’re hurting yourself again and you don’t even see a fucking problem here?” Mary’s arms were starting to shake as if the weight of the gun were getting to her. “He would never hurt Mary on purpose. Statty loves Mary, like she loves Statty and you tried to KILL HIM!” Her breathing was ragged. Arael leaned over, “Please Chica, please give me the gun.” Mary shook her head viciously. “Then please just lower it. You can keep it, I wont try to take it from you but please aim down. You don’t want to hurt Skye by accident. I know you, I know you’re angry but it would destroy some part of you if she were harmed by your own hand.” “But she was gonna do the same to Statty, Eddie, she was gonna do that,” “I know Chica, I know. Please just lower it.” Mary thought a moment and then lowered the gun to her side slowly but didn’t let it go. Her eyes were back on Skye. “Como?” Skye looked puzzled. “How what?” Mary tapped the gun at her hip. “Como Skye, how, how were you going to kill him huh” Ask Papa for a favour? Have him send one of his best? Have him send someone to sneak up on Mary’s Statty and put a fucking BULLET IN HIS HEAD?” She was wide eyed and wild. “Bets you had the best. Bets Daddy let you take his?” Mary trailed off. She couldn’t breathe again. She didn’t want the answer to the question she was about to ask. She feared she already knew it. “Who you get” Who you get to do it?” Skye remained silent. “WHO YOU GET?” Mary aimed the gun at her again. “It doesn’t matter who it was.” “TELL MARY, QUEM?” Mary’s finger was dangerously close to squeezing the trigger. Arael could hear the metal moving in the dead silence. He exhaled and knew his next step was a very bad idea. “It was I Chica. I was ordered to make the hit.” Mary swung round enraged, eyes even wilder. She was hurting and she had a gun in her hand, it wasn’t a good combination. She lunged at Arael. She took the wind out of him. She actually took him to the ground as he wasn’t ready for that kind of impact. She screamed at him. She screamed how could you and why? She sailed a fist across his jaw. It was the fist with the gun in it and it bust him open. He instinctively grabbed her wrist when she went for a second blow. He managed to whip the gun out of her fist without endangering himself and he slid it across the floor out of reach then he held her arms down and against all his training, once again he held her and she cried. She cried because she felt betrayed but she cried more because she was so angry, so enraged and if it couldn’t come out in violence it would find another outlet. He stroked her hair and whispered apology after apology while she sat in his lap like a child. Skye stayed where she was standing. Mary turned her tear stained face to her. “Mary knows you was only doing what your heart told ya Skye Blue. She knows you love her and she knows you’d do anything for her and is the same back, but she loves Statty. She loves him like a burning inside her and you was gonna take that away, just like that.” Skye lowered to her knees but didn’t come any closer to Mary. “O Anjo, it was killing me to see what he was doing to you. After you nearly died getting over Damien, I couldn’t watch you go through that shit again.” “He ain’t Damien Skye,” “Isn’t he Mary” He switched on you just like that, just like Damien did. Look at what he did to Eva. Pretended to care for her then beat her like Damien did to you. You can’t still love this man. He left you and now he’s left Eva and now your fucking harming yourself to deal with this shit. I don’t understand how you can even still love him after seeing that.” “You don’t understand Skye.” Skye grew frustrated. “He never loved you Mary! He said he loved Wylie and look what he ended up doing to her. He said he loved You and he put you in the hospital over a stupid fucking misunderstanding. He said he loved Eva and hmm, Wylie number two much” He does the same to her that he did to Wylie. At least Eva wasn’t pregnant at the time. That would have been too damn perfect.” “SHUT UP! You don’t get it Skye. You never listen.” Skye exhaled and shook her head. “Then help me understand Anjo.” Mary slid out of Arael’s lap and he let her. “Mary knew.” “Knew what?” “Mary knew all the time, Mary knew what was gonna happen. Statty has his reasons and Mary has hers. All that matters is that they together and they happy.” “And fuck off to everyone else huh?” “It ain’t like that Skye, you know that.” Skye got up. “I love you Ty, you know I do but I don’t know how much more of your life I can stand by and watch bebe. It’s like watching you driving with your eyes closed and praying to god you don’t hit anything. How you can be with a man with a mind like his, I don’t know. It would scare the hell out of me. He’s a monster Mary.” “That’s what you don’t get Skye. He’s Mary’s monster. She seen the scary, she’s seen the nice in him and she loves it all. She digs it all, she wants it all and everyone he hurts, deserves it, all of them. Wylie was a skanky ho who slept with his big bad enemy and Eva, well Mary dunno the history there but she needed to be takey down a peggy or two damnit.” Skye walked to the door then turned back at the last minute. “What’s happening to you Ty” You say Eva deserved that? The woman’s been through a hell of a lot from the rumours I’ve heard. Some shit you should be able to sympathise with and then she gets her heart stamped on by Lestatt and you stand by cheering him on? Shit I’m not gonna lie, I’m scared Ty, I’m scared you’re your fucking soul bebe.” She put her hand on the door handle, “But hell, it’s about time you stopped being the whipping boy and started doing the whipping.” “So what you saying Skye?” “I’m saying just be careful ok?” Mary nodded and then Skye was gone. Mary looked over at Arael nursing his now bleeding lip then back up at the door. Her face slowly spread into a grin. 'Excitin times Eddie.'
