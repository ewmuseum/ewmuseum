We Are Who We Choose To Be

10 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/10 ------------- PCW Turmoil 123, April 30 2003 Dorn and Gargano get to the feet and once again exchange punches, weakening each other even further. Gargano lifts up Dorn in a Body Press Slam but Dorn wiggles his way out of the move. Dorn hits a Big Bang kick to the nuts of Gargano! Gargano stands, holding his member as Dorn lifts him up for a reverse tombstone piledriver! Dorn connects, making Gargano's neck crash helplessly against the ring mat. Dave: Dorn hit the Zodiac! Dorn hit the Zodiac! Jeff: That move should be banned. Gargano could be hurt! Dave: Quit your whining! Dorn goes for the pin on a lifeless Gargano! 1….. 2….. 3…..!!! 'Innervision' by System of a down blares over the PA system as Dorn Tracer gets to his feet after winning the match. Over the music, Clay Claybourne makes the announcement… ------------- PCW Turmoil 124, May 6 2003 VJ pulls Tommy up and gets behind him hitting the Osaka Send-off (VJ grabs the victim by both sides of the head and falls to a sitting position, driving the back of their head into the mat). Dorn doesn’t notice as he locks a figure-four onto "The Druid" who is screaming out in pain as he reaches for the ropes. It doesn’t do him any good, as he isn’t a part of this match. Pharaoh is just getting to his feet as VJ is picking Tommy back up. VJ holds Tommy’s arms behind his back…letting Pharaoh deliver punch after punch to Tommy’s face. Dave: It looks like the Power Stable is handling matters here now. Dorn has "The Druid" tied up and now Pharaoh and VJ are having their way with Tommy… ------------- PCW Supremacy 2003, May 25, 2003 Violence Jack and The Pharaoh hold on tight as Dorn grabs the top of the hood and rips it backwards, pulling the entire thing off and revealing the Druid's identity to the world... a gasp bursts forth from the crowd in unison, as well as from the announcers. Dave: WHAT THE?! Jeff: THAT'S the Druid?! Teresa: RIGHT ON! The camera focuses in on the Druid's face... and is revealed to be... ...Brianna Washington... Tracer just stares for a moment, completely shocked. The mic plummets from his hand and slams into the mat, sending out a wave of screeching static. Pharaoh and VJ exchange looks then stare down at Brianna Washington, who scowls at Dorn Tracer. They toss her down to the mat and stand beside the Astronomer, gawking at his estranged stalker. Dorn picks the mic up off the mat and gives Brianna a funny look. Dave: IT'S BRIANNA WASHINGTON! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! Jeff: That bitch! What business does she have with Dorn, anyway?! Teresa: Why don't you just shut up and find out, numb nuts?! The Astronomer ever so slowly raises the mic to his lips as the crowd quiets down once more. Tracer: ...why? Washington, mocking him: Why WHAT?! Tracer: Why? What the FUCK did I do to you?!?! Brianna scowls at him again and SPITS at Dorn, and though it falls short and lands on the tip of his foot, it draws an enraged look from the Astronomer. Washington: I'M SICK AND TIRED OF YOU AND YOUR FANATIC BUDDIES HURTING INNOCENT PEOPLE! You beat and maim those that doesn't deserve just because you're PARANOID that they're all against you! I'M TIRED OF IT! The masses erupt in cheers for Washington's courage to take on the fanatical trio. Dorn raises the mic once more, but Gadget cuts him off! Washington: I'm not done yet!!! Jeff: THERE'S MORE?! She's making them look like criminals! Teresa: Well no shit! Dave: They deserve every bit! Washington: Sandy Wong... another innocent person. Sweet, kind, loved... girlfriend of Skylar Green... and SHE WAS MURDERED BY THAT MAN! Brianna Washington throws an enraged pointer finger right at Violence Jack. The Baron of Brutality sneers and just looks at her, not phased by her claim. The crowd sends heated jeers at the murderer, but he just continues grinning. Washington: In the worst act anyone could perpetrate, it happened to someone it shouldn't have! AND YOU'RE HIS PARTNER, DORN! YOU STAND FOR EVERYTHING HE DOES! I CAN'T ALLOW SOME THAT ASSOCIATES WITH A MURDERER TO RUN FREE AND DO THE SAME THINGS!! … ------------- "We’ve come a long way since those days…" There’s nary a sound except the gravely, familiar voice of The Archon. He speaks hushed, as if lost in some faraway memory. Strands of black and gray hair hang over his dark blue eyes as he gazes right through the wooden table before him. The room is his cramped "office" aboard Starship Tracer, the divine vehicle which he and his followers use to travel from one destination to the next. The room is little more than a bedroom with the beds and dressers removed, instead replaced with a cheap desk and chair with a table sitting in the middle of the room. Dorn taps the whiskey glass in his right hand against the table, rattling the ice and brown liquid within. He takes a slow sip from it, smacks his lips, and raises his eyes to the figure sitting across from him. "I was a fool then. Misguided and lost amongst the sea of morons, miscreants, and unbelievers." Dorn takes another drag from the glass as the man seated opposite of him speaks, "I don’t understand. You were a champion… a warrior. They feared you." The man’s voice is rigid and pronounced with each word oozing smug ego. This man is well-dressed in a dark expensive suit with a pressed white undershirt and a dark blue tie to accompany the outside. His light brown hair is cropped and neat. The man would almost be attractive if it wasn’t for his absurdly creepy green eyes peering from the rectangular lens of his glasses. "Pray tell, old friend. How exactly were you ‘lost?’" Dorn clears his throat and takes a moment to reflect on the man’s words. The memories come rushing back, flooding the Archon’s mind with feelings of triumph, agony, and rage. His days in PCW were the pinnacle of his career, truly, but also represent a dark time in his life. He was the Goddess’s warrior and champion, searing Her name into the minds of all his opposition. Opponents came and went, Her rage was the constant. The fixed variable. That was a different time; Dorn was young, gullible, trusting, and most of all: empty. "I was lost without Her light; shuffling from one blaspheming backslider to the next. I crushed hopes, dreams, spines, skulls, crotches, and careers in Her service, but I felt a emptiness… a lack of Her divine presence, if you will." Dorn clears his throat again and gives a short sigh. "I could feel Her anger as I placed my fate and trust unto others. En"Flarim hates this world and all of its rotten bastard children. They hail sandaled sand-rats, calamari monsters, fat naked men, and any other Mother Goose butt-fucking creation comes to their addled simple minds. I sided with such miscreants to further my own goals and it angered Her. I lost championships, sustained injuries, lost matches, lost FAITH!" Dorn’s voice begins to rise as his hand grips the glass tighter. "PCW was the land of the lying DAMNED! I slept amongst beasts and tramps, wallowing in their lies and discrimination." The Archon takes one last swig of the drink and slams the glass down. The man sitting opposite doesn’t budge nor react; he merely smiles as Dorn continues speaking. "Seven weary years have passed since those days and She has since fit to take me back. Prophets, liars, their children, their entire fucking lives are DOOMED when this world burns. I’m merely the messenger. The thousands upon thousands that lie through their teeth, preaching love, peace, and understanding will fucking roast like Thanksgiving turkeys when She’s free again. You’ve felt it, Lucius. The sun grows hotter every year…" The man, revealed as Lucius, nods and places his hands together, listening intently. "That’s Her, Lucius! She’s come back to us, to wrench us forth from this bullshit hive of butt-fucking ants. En’Flarim hates Earth, and the calamities that befall this place are testament to Her rage. She wants me to carry the banner once more unto the breach and pierce the hearts of thousands. And Lucius…" Dorn stands up and learns against the table, staring deep into Lucius’ eyes. The man neither moves nor speaks, he just continues to sit rigidly and listen. "…it will be glorious. War without end. Harlots, charlatans, liars, false prophets: They’ll turn or be bathed in solar fire. The idiot poindexters of this world foolishly believe the end will be some billion years away… HA!" Dorn’s voice grows more intense as the volume lowers. "We’ll see where that gets them when their flesh melts from their bones as they cry for their fucking mommies. Too bad it’ll be too late for any of them!!" Dorn removes his hands from the table and stands tall, folding his arms across his chest. Words goes unspoken from either man for what feels like hours before the suited man known as Lucius clears his throat and cocks his head slightly, still staring into Dorn’s cold, almost-lifeless eyes. "As was spoken, old friend. This is all old news to us. We protest, wreck, and picket functions of all kinds across this entire damned nation. We’ve brought more tears to simple-minded folk than McDonald’s has delivered children to diabetes. But I’m curious…" The man removes his glasses, breathes into the lens, and wipes them clean on his pressed suit before placing them back on his nose. "How do we ‘up the ante’ from here, so to speak? You’ve got us heading to Dallas when we’ve already caused enough trouble there to fill a lifetime. Was there something we missed?" The Archon scoffs at Lucius’ words and shakes his head. "Dallas, Texas… land of the brother-loving horse men. I’m thankful everyday when She wipes their stink clean from this place with fires, hurricanes, and other forms of destruction that are far too merciful to their kind. However, I’m regretful to say we still have business there. I believe I’ve found a new pedestal for our message. Another company…" "Another federation?" Lucius inquires, already knowing the answer. Dorn nods. "Of course. Another dismal box of mutants and shit-stains. One cannot deny, however, their exposure to the rest of the world. It will be a pleasant trip down memory lane as I sweep them off this world like the retarded raccoons they are. The company is inhabited by a sordid sort, including…" Dorn’s eyes begin to narrow, his voice pouring venom at his next words. "… Tessa Windsor. How that slut wasn’t beaten within an inch of her life by her own family out of mercy, I’ll never know. En’Flarim works in ways I as a mortal cannot fully understand, so I’ll assume She lets Tessa stay on the world to torture her in various ways. May we all be thankful for that!" Lucius nods as a rumble envelops the room. Both men look around for a second as the room jerks forward. Lucius looks back at the Archon and smiles, "It would appear the Enclave is ready to move on. Shall we?" Dorn takes his seat and waves his hand dismissingly. "Yes… it’s time. We have a destiny, you and I. I pray you last to see it come to fruition." The Archon’s words trail off a bit as he turns and stares out the window silently. Lucius’ eyes fall to the table and to the glass of liquid that has now spilled across the word. A faint smell of whiskey begins to fill the room, but neither man seems to notice. Lucius just continues to the eye the glass… lost in his thoughts… Fin.
