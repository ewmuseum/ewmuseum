Back to business

11 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/10 A hard boiled egg sits on a table. Nothing special about it. Just a simple white hard boiled egg. A hand reaches from the right and lifts and rotates it. The room you see before you is a bright white room. Sitting in the chair to the left of the table is Shaman in a white pair of pants and a white shirt unbuttoned to about four buttons from the neck. Smiling, he begins to speak. “A simple hard boiled egg. This egg has many unique features, much like the men I have to face for the Hardcore title.” Holding the eggs within his fingertips he looks at it. He rotates the egg slightly as he smiles. “The comparison for this is easy.” Holding the egg up, he reaches forward past it and taps something as two images pop up.  “Each man I must face has a hard exterior that must be cracked.” Standing up he waves his hand as the two images become life-size. Both images are of Donny Diamond and Bryan Mayhem. “Isn't easy when it comes to these two men is it.” Nodding his head at both the images. “Mayhem and I are going to be like the typical feuding Hatfields and McCoys. Neither one of us are going to give up and neither of us will ever back down. Must be why the people that be want us in the ring, Figuring they can reign in our chaos.” Shaman holds the egg up looking at it comparing to Mayhem's head. “Nope, he doesn't have an egghead shape even if he is painted like Frankenmime. No, the truth is I am kind of tired of seeing your face all the time Bryan.” Shaman shakes his head and walks by the image. “You see Bryan, I moved past you during the tournament. Proved to everyone that your loss was going to happen to come from a better man.” Rolling the egg in his hand back and forth Shaman grins. “After all who else is going to beat you at your own game for your precious little title?” Running his fingers across the egg motioning as if he was going to drop it. “Precious little egg is like a  precious little ego, isn't it, Donny boy. “ Holding the egg up he twists it back and forth. “It is funny, you know. You kept trying to tell me I was Bishop Steele all over again, and yet it was I who came out and ended that little feud you had with him. It was pathetic and useless.” Laughing loudly, Shaman grins as he flexes his muscles in his forearms a bit. “Funny thing was you always accused me of coming at you from behind. Problem is after I kicked your ass you never knew if you were coming or going, hell you didn't even know if you were conscious.” “Here. I have a little history for you.” Shaman taps his finger on something as the images of himself pop up. The first image he is wearing Native American garb as he points. “See, when I first came into the business I wore this get up. Being taken as a joke, I began to change.” Running his hand through the image it disappears. “Next I was the hardcore icon that people have come to know.” The image shows Shaman wearing Bluejeans, combat boots, and a t-shirt that reads 'Got Blood!?'. “That was my beginning to change. If ya notice I still wear the face paint at this time.” Shaman looks at the picture again for a moment. “Get the fuck out of here.” Waving his hand through the image again it disappears. He seems to be disgusted as he looks at the other images. “Screw this, in fact no need to do a history lesson for you two. You're both guys who are going to have to get your asses kicked in facing me.” Stopping, Shaman looks around as he holds the egg. Seemingly he had gotten off track as he turns back and walks to the chair and sits down. “Ok, ok. No more ranting. Back to the point at hand.” Holding up the egg as he points at it. “Like an egg, your opponents have layers to them. Once in the battle, you begin to peel away, layer after layer. You begin to break them down.” Taking his finger he pops the egg cracking it as he peels back a piece of the shell afterwords. “Until you begin to reveal the soft underbelly of their weaknesses.” Grinning sadistically, he continues to peel the egg back a bit. He points a finger and circles it through the air as the images of Bryan Mayhem and Donny Diamond stands before him. “Sometimes I don't make sense or lose focus before I get into the match. Hell, I will even ramble on sometimes when I am trying to find the weakest point on my target.” Peeling more of the eggshell loose, he stops looking at the half peeled egg. “Sometimes showing what is inside also reveals what makes them tick. In Diamond's case, it is obvious what makes him tick. What makes those little squeaking wheels in his head go round.” Tapping his temple as he leans forward a bit looking at the eggshells. “Your ego is bruised Donny boy, and in the end you will make a bigger mistake than you had before.” Pushing on of the eggshells around with his finger he simply smiles as he observes it. “Perhaps it is you that was turning into Bishop Steele and you are scared that you will become nothing. Just like him.” Smiling he continues to push around some of the broken eggshells. “Much like your own psyche, isn't it, Bryan? You are broken, and lost and wandering around in your own little dreams.” Waving his hand slightly all of the eggshells seem to float and return to the table. “You always seem to forget that I know you as well as anybody in GCW, if not better. Bryan, you have weaknesses, and I will gladly reveal them to everyone.” Holding his hand up the egg vanishes from it and seems to recompose upon the table whole. “You once told me I was broken and was a former shadow of myself. Yet lately you have seemed to become the shadow.” Nodding his head over his shoulder an image of Mayhem showing sadistic glee upon his face in his dominant moods, then showing his less emotional states recently. Rubbing his hands together, he simply looks over at the egg. “Are you scared of what will be done, Bryan? You bringing me nothing to the ring. Have you become so exposed this time around your dominance needs to be refueled?” Placing his hand upon the table he runs it across the surface slowly. “In this room there is nothing real, The real world is that ring from which I will raise my hand in victory one way or another.” Looking over at the egg which has become whole once again. It slowly begins to crack and hatches. Looking at it, an image of Shaman emerges. “Then again, growth and rebirth come hand in hand, do they not, gentlemen? Diamond you have begun to see sides of me almost forgotten. Mayhem as seen sides of me that which are gone. Or should I say, mutated.” Chuckling a bit Shaman raises his hand. “Shut it down Charlie, I am coming out.” The images and whole room shimmer and disappear as Shaman is seen removing a helmet. Slowly he removes gloves as he watches Charlie shut down the computer system. “This is the real world. No pretty pictures or cozy fires.” Stretching a bit he turns and looks around the room. Reaching down he picks up hard drive. Charlie, the operator of the virtual machine, reaches over and takes it. Pushing up his glasses, he stares at Shaman with bulbous looking eyes caused by his glasses. “That is sensitive material, good sir.” Waving his hand holding the hard drive grumbling and mumbling about equipment and the treatment of them. “On that note.” Walking away Shaman brings his hands together as he walks. “I am not underestimating either one of you, I know what both are capable of.” Cracking his knuckles he stretches his arms forward. “Don't worry Donny boy. I don't expect either you or Mayhem to lay down faster than a two dollar whore.” Smiling, Shaman walks down the corridor slowly. His arms stretched out tracing the walls with his finger tips. He begins humming as he walks, stopping he talks again. “A day comes when everyone wakes up and forgets the man next door. This man watches all of his neighbors closely. He knows they are plotting against him.” He strums his fingers on the walls. “He knows they are coming to take away everything that he is, but he refuses to give in and fights for what he has.” Walking to a door, he opens it and steps out into a board room with cardboard cut outs of every GCW superstar. Looking at each one around the room, he sneers slightly. “I had put everyone of you on notice, yet you ignored everyone as you went about your own business.” Shaman walks to the closet door and opens it. Someone is tied in the closet with a hood over their head. “Sometimes drastic measures brings things to extremes.” Smiling, he waves his hand over the person's head. “I bet you are wondering who this is? The truth is it could be anyone dressed as a man. Could be a woman, teenager, or even a corporate big wig from GCW.” Grinning, he walks around tapping the person on the shoulders and head as the person flinches. Leaning down, Shaman whispers into their ear. “Scream a little and they will listen, scream a lot and they will cover their ears.” Smiling sadistically, he walks around the person as they are breathing quickly and kind of jerking in the chair. “You see, not everyone has the gift to cause mental pain. But...Everyone has the gift to cause physical pain.” Grinning, he reaches over and grabs two books. He walks up behind the person and slams the two books together causing a scream to emanate from the person bound in the chair. The person is visibly shaking as he turns and looks around. “Mayhem and Diamond, what did you think I was going to do? Some dramatic way of belittling you? No. I am going to make an example out of you. I have already won the mental game. Now I have to win the physical game against you two.” Grabbing the chair, Shaman drags he person back into the closet. Reaching over, Shaman pulls the mask off the person, revealing Jacob, his original trainer. “This is what happens when you push someone too far. Jacob here wanted to try and get me and his daughter split up. In fact, he tried to muscle in on my finances at the casino. Ringing up a substantial debt. Falling out of favor in my eyes.” Smirking a dangerous sadistic look comes across Shaman's face. “Come Jacob we have some business to attend too.” Chuckling Shaman opens another door in the closet leading to another room. Shutting the outer door Jacob screams loud through his covered mouth with a look of fear on his face. His hands stretching towards the door, pleading for someone who is never going to come.
