All Joking Aside...

6 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/6/05 NC-17 brought about some of the most memorable matches in GCW history? The Mount Gagazet Deathmatch between Rich Rollins and Seymour Almasy? and the World Championship war, where Michael “Spitfire” Stevens finally got the best of LeStatt Knight. But there was one match in particular that meant the most for our hero, Chrissy-Poo? One match in particular he will never forget. The Television Title. Chrissy-Poo staggers out of the hospital and ducks into his car. He turns the ignition and holds his head. It aches? as does his legs? and his arms? and the rest of his body. Chrissy drives off into the night. “Chris! Man, it’s Wally! I saw your match” and I just wanted to congratulate you on the win, man! You were awesome! Alright? I’ll talk to you later?” *BEEP*? You have no more messages? Chrissy stands over his telephone and answering machine. He stares blankly for a few moments before stumbling over to a nearby couch and collapsing into its soft embrace. “I did it?” Chrissy whispered to himself? and he cracked a smile. He had done it. He confronted the monster, Bryan O’Neil, face to face? and he had won. Chrissy drove himself to the very limits of his being? perhaps even beyond? and in the end, it paid off. Chrissy was now the owner of a shiny new GCW Television title belt? and something even more important? “I can’t believe this” I’m? a? champion?” Chrissy turned his head, catching a look at the Television title belt slung over the back of a chair. He would have NEVER imagined even getting this far. He weighed 160 pounds and wrestled under the name CHRISSY-POO, damn it! This is NOT supposed to happen. “This cannot be happening?” Yeah, that’s what I said? Chrissy thought back to everything that had led to this? from watching Sting and Sid Justice on television? to his Jimmy “Superfly” Snuka trading card? and his “Hacksaw” Jim Duggan action figure? to when he first saw a wrestling show... watching WWF’s Monday Night RAW in Milwaukee with his cousins? watching X-Pac and Kane in the main event? Then Chrissy recalled his high school wrestling team? he joined a whim senior year and ended up becoming a state champion. His love of professional wrestling only grew from there? and flirtation became obsession. He worked as a ring monkey for the Universal Wrestling Federation in Chicago? setting up the ring and doing other odd jobs? Suddenly there was a job opening for color commentator on the federation’s B show, Wednesday Night Underground? Chris tried out and got the position? His commentary team with Ed Johnson is still talked about? one of the best comedic combinations the federation had ever seen. Even more important was the connections he made with the other wrestlers? he worked side by side many of the greatest wrestlers alive including GCW’ers, but then UWF’ers, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins, Moraless, and Alex Grey. After Underground’s cancellation, Chrissy went to the independent federation, the SCW to fine tune his in ring skills. Soon he got a call from his friend from UWF? Rich Rollins? and he was offered a try out with the GCW? and amazing? he was offered a contract. “This has to be some kind of sick joke?” Well? that’s what it was SUPPOSED to be. The character of Chrissy-Poo? the whole act? it was all supposed to be a joke? imagine some scrawny wrestler getting the shit pounded out of him? he was supposed to be a joke? a comic relief? a jobber? a TOOL of the federation? but now? “The Television title?” And that’s where it came to Chrissy. He wasn’t a joke anymore? he wasn’t a pushover? he was a champion? he wasn’t a joke, no one was laughing anymore? except him. “Haha” HA! Those motherfuckers? I fucking SCHOOLED their asses! I’m a GOD DAMN GCW CHAMPION! HAHAHAHAHA!” Chrissy rolls over onto his stomach, his laughter muffled by a pillow. Yes? he was a champion? but for how long?
