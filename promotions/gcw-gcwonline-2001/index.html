Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Global Championship Wrestling opened in February 2001 and ran in two incarnations: from that month until the summer of 2002, and again from February 2005 to March 2012, with two and a half years of silence between them. Its own history page traces its line back through the Cyber Wrestling Union and, before it, the American Championship Wrestling Federation to its president, Steven Caldera, who came up through both and stood at its head throughout.

The first incarnation was built on Meltdown, a weekly that survives in fifty-four cards, and on pay-per-views named NC-17, Warpath, Shockwave and Dangerous Games. The second was built on WorldWide, which its own FAQ puts on its X:TV network every Monday night and which was numbered past 150, with the same big nights above it. Dangerous Games, a thirty-man match whose winner took a title shot, has eight winners on the Record Book, from 2001 to 2010. NC-17 VIII, in March 2011, was staged as the company's tenth birthday.

Eight championships passed through 206 reigns: the World Heavyweight, United States, Television, Tag Team, Hardcore, Vanguard, Cruiserweight and Women's. Its own Record Book gives the longest reign to Banned & Exiled~!, 428 days as tag champions, and lists its annual awards for 2001 and for 2005 to 2010, the 2009 winners revealed in four parts at Christmas Chaos. Thirteen wrestlers were inducted into its Hall of Fame, the last four announced in the weeks after its farewell.

Its last surviving card is Dangerous Games of 17 March 2012. On 26 May 2012 a farewell post signed “SC” said the time had come to say goodbye and promised a final show, a last Hall of Fame class and a few farewell moments; the four inductions followed in posts from 29 May to 2 July 2012, and nothing later survives. Whether the final show was ever staged isn't in the surviving record.
