Attracted to Heat?

20 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shawn Porter Finally pulls in a victory match at the latest World Wide. After the match Porter was receiving plenty of negative heat from the fans. The question is do getting booed every week on World Wide a great pleasure and is he worth the fans heat? We caught up with the enthusiastic Shawn Porter to hear his words just about an half-hour after his match with Kyle James. ?After talking with Paul my personal trainer and friend about my latest actions in ring. I manage to pull out a victory that we both planned on getting. Kyle James was just number one on the list of falling foes in GCW. I made a mistake at Warpath by not defeating Tyson ?whats-his-name? but thats in the past and I'm looking forward to the future because I'm the future of this business and working with everybody will get me up the ranks as a better person and a better athlete. As for the fans booing me after i out smarted that moron Kyle James, they have the right to do what they please but that still doesn't bother me at all. Is the negative heat worth it? I'll say yes and I'll take as much as these people can give. Because I'm Ballin' and Shot Callin'. So everyone can bank one that.? We asked him does he plan on making it to Brisbane. He replied... ?Yeah, I'm on my way to the Prez office now. I'll try to get something going because i feel that I'm hot and I'll show everyone why they're not!?
