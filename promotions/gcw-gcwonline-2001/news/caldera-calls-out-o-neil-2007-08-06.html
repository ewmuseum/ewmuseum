Caldera Calls Out O'Neil

6 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a story breaking from Primetime Central tonight, GCW President Steven Caldera has called out former GCW star Jason O'Neil. O'Neil had an impressive and lengthy GCW career but is perhaps best known for his storied on- and off-camera rivalry with Caldera. The two, at one point allies in The Establishment, had a falling out that led to O'Neil becoming perhaps Caldera's biggest rival. Caldera and O'Neil took their rivalry across several promotions and through several tournaments. O'Neil recently reemerged onto the scene in the Chris Williams Tournament, in which GCW contracted wrestler Kimbusa is also entered. O'Neil, however, withdrew from the tournament last week, which reportedly angered Caldera greatly as he had hoped for a chance to eliminate O'Neil. Caldera now is publicly putting on a media blitz to get O'Neil back in a GCW ring. Caldera is pushing O'Neil to appear next month at Dangerous Games in a match against the President himself. The hope on Caldera's part is to entice O'Neil into one final match in GCW. Thus continues the odd trend now developing of Caldera bringing back his enemies to end their careers in GCW, with O'Neil now following Rich Rollins. GCW has also been in recent negotiations with former stars Luke Hanson and Jason Dread, also former Caldera rivals. Privately some in GCW believe that Caldera is hoping to use the current powerful incarnation of The Establishment to eliminate all his greatest rivals once and for all. But as far as O'Neil goes, Caldera has assured the world that he will keep The Establishment backstage for their match. No word has been released from O'Neil. For now, GCW is expecting to book Caldera vs. O'Neil for Dangerous Games, but there's no indication that O'Neil will accept the challenge.
