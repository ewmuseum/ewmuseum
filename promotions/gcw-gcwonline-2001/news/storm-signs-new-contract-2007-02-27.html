Storm Signs New Contract

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Storm signed a new contract with Global Championship Wrestling today. After appearing in controversial fashion on WW61, Chris Storm has reportedly signed a pay-per-appearance deal with the Global Championship Wrestling. Originally scheduled just to be a substitute for his pregnant fianc?e, Tessa Windsor, Steven Caldera was quick to reaffirm his commitment to the fans by signing another high profile talent to the company. "I bring my fans what they want, and Mr. Storm was smart enough to sign with us, expressing his commitment to our brand." Mr. Caldera was quoted as saying shortly after signing. Chris Storm is probably most famous for his controversial Global title reign in Primetime Central, where he defeated Seymour Almasy in a match many people say was fixed. After the closure of Universal Wrestling, many thought that Chris Storm’s days in PTC were done when he signed a long term contract to the Legacy of Champions. "May no mistake about it, I’m still one-hundred percent loyal to Legacy and my signing here by no means is any indication that I plan to start skipping out on dates." Chris Storm stated in a press conference, "I’m in GCW for one reason, to make Banned & Exiled~! the greatest shit since sliced bread. With Markus covering my back, I know we can burn a trail like GCW has never seen." Those very words were felt on the latest edition of WorldWide when the duo dismantled former Tag Team Champions UNITED and rising star Katie O’Clare. The two have announced they have big plans for WW62, and the fans will have to check in to see what they have up their sleeves. As of right now there is no word how many appearances are mentioned in the contract, or for how much money, but by all indications it seems like the contract won’t expire any time soon. "I’ve got unfinished business in GCW that many casual fans don’t know about. But with the GCW, and this contract, I plan to finish that business." Ominous words for the Rock Superstar. Tune in to WorldWide to find out just what Chris Storm and Banned & Exiled~! have planned.
