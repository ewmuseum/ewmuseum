Imperium Rides Again? (replay)

3 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We have just got our hands on a clip of an interview from Peter Vetra and Hoyt Williams on the last edition of REVolution before they hit the stage at Devil's Night/ GAN.... We go to the tape to show you. Nick: At great American Nightmare former PRIME stable Imperium will implode as two former members face off as Hoyt Williams and Peter Vetra step in the ring against each other. Richard: Arguably the biggest, baddest, and best stable EVER in the history of PRIME. To see these two men go at it bring tears to my eyes. Nick: We have Hoyt Williams standing by, along with Peter Vetra on the phone but before we get to that lets take a trip down history lane and jump all the way back to Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan on March 9th, of 2005. It was Revelation 56!! ***The tape plays??*** "Los Diablo along with their manager stand side by side with Peter Vetra, and Hoyt Williams. Hoyt Williams: Shout like the abrasive lusting rubes that you are. You can shout till your throats turn red and your voice vanishes like the ambitions you never had. Shout till the wrath of God strikes down in anger to silence your stupidity. Shout until Satan sticks his spear of torment straight up your ass. It doesn't matter anymore. Tonight I will sleep in the presidential suite of the best hotel Michigan has to offer. I can because I am God's champion and your angel of death. I'm going to eat in the best restaurant with my brothers here; brothers not bonded by the message of God, but rather by the promise of power; while you people eat at the McDonalds or some other preprocessed garbage you bought from the Wal-mart. Good for you, not for me. But do please enjoy it as your day of reckoning is here. Richard: AMEN! Nick: Shut up. Hoyt Williams: Last week I went to hell. Nick: I wish he would have stayed there. Hoyt Williams: I looked into the devils eyes and prayed to my father in Satan's church while Lucifer torched me. God spoke to me and he told me to seek out the other three horsemen and bring them together to ride again. Bring them together to carry out the sick and twisted work of a vengeful God. He told me they are not going to be bible men, although two do know the words. He told me they will walk with the corruption of booze and sins but at heart they are righteous men. He told me not to change them, or preach to them, as that could damage them but to simply let the mercenaries be themselves as warriors and riders. Hoyt walks over and stands next to the two masked Mexicans which is quite a site to witness three muscle clad men all standing over 6'6" in height. Hoyt Williams: I present to you six foot six inches of "The Beast" Angelus. He is a man of faith who is ready to take you down and wipe out your existence with bane. Hoyt walks over and places his hand on the giant shoulder of Baltasar. Hoyt Williams: This man is six foot nine and is known as the "Red Devil". Just as a brutal and mean as Lucifer only he's on my side. He will take you to the doorway of hell my friends then push you through. He will rape your soul with a sick smile on his face. Hoyt gives Los Diablo five as he walks over to Peter Vetra. Hoyt Williams: Don't let the size fool you because it's misleading. "The Devine Assassin" is so fast and so stealth he'll kill you before you even know you’re going to die. He brings the skill and speed to this foursome that put us over the edge of dangerous and into the category of deadly. Again, a less than positive reaction from the fans. Hoyt Williams: Then there is me... the Son of God, Your Personal Jesus, PRIMEs Pontiff, The Angel Of Death, at six foot six and the strongest man in this federation. Let it be known from here on out that if you mess with any one member of this brotherhood you mess with all of us, including God. Nick: Quite a group they have there... and worryingly so. Richard: Oh praise the lord I'm glad I'm on their side. Hoyt Williams: We are the fatherhood of god... the brotherhood of man... we are the Imperium!! **QUICK EDIT** "Baltasar hands the mic over to Peter Vetra as the crowd rains boos down on the Red Devil for his remarks toward the crowd favorite Ellie. Peter Vetra: Many people would look at me and wonder why would I join sides with these three men? What has gotten into Peter Vetra? Well the truth is I now have the satisfaction of having my back covered and not having to worry about what might happen." BACK TO NICK AND RICHARD LIVE Richard: AHHH THE memories!! Nick: Something’s are best forgotten. Now let’s jump forward two years and take a look at GCW’s Deadly Games that happened a few weeks ago. This footage is courtesy of GCW. ***The tape plays?*** "Bryan: That was Hoyt Williams working with Peter Vetra to combine for that elimination there? Vetra smiles approvingly at Williams and extends his hand in friendship, pointing out to the rest of the field. Bryan: “and it looks like Peter Vetra is attempting to form an alliance here with Williams! Yale: That turncoat! Bryan: Still, it’s not a bad strategy, Dave? Williams nods and accepts Vetra’s handshake to the boos of the crowd. Vetra turns to go to work, but Hoyt suddenly pulls him back and delivers a massive clothesline! Vetra flies head over heels and bounces off the apron, landing in a heap next to Fox on the floor. The crowd boos even harder as Hoyt smiles at his handiwork. PETER VETRA ELIMINATED Bryan: NO! Vetra just got double-crossed! Yale: That’s what he gets for dealing with PRIME! Serves him right! But now someone’s got to put Williams in his place." ****QUICK EDIT***** "Bryan: So we’re nearing the halfway point of the match and we’ve got nine men in the ring. Jay Terror is still trying to eliminate Tyson DeBough, Chris Storm is tenaciously trying to toss LeStatt, Phil Atken’s getting worn out by a fresh Christian St. Pierre, Markus Stone is hurting and Ray Holland is getting the tables turned on him by Hoyt Williams! Hoyt takes over on Holland and drops him to the mat with a right hand. He then turns and delivers a kick to the back of Chris Storm’s knee, causing Storm to drop to the mat and allow Knight to get back to his feet. Williams lunges at Knight and clubs him in the face with a forearm, then pulls him to the middle of the ropes. Williams attempts to whip Knight, but Knight reverses. Williams hits the ropes and bounces back to duck a lariat from LeStatt. Williams hits the ropes again, but this time finds himself falling headfirst to the floor! Peter Vetra hangs on the top rope, having pulled it down as Williams hit the ropes, and Williams hits the floor beside him. HOYT WILLIAMS ELIMINATED Bryan: THERE GOES HOYT! And it’s Peter Vetra exacting revenge! Yale: He never did leave, JB, and as soon as he saw Hoyt heading for the ropes he low-bridged him! Hoyt is gone, and GCW holds serve again! Holding his head in a daze, Williams slowly stands up to find Vetra yelling at him from above. Williams tries to rush him but the officials restrain both men, and a pair of security guards rush in from the stands to separate the two. Bryan: Wow, Hoyt wants at Vetra here but the GCW ring crew isn’t letting this escalate any further. Yale: Williams brought that on himself, JB, and he just didn’t notice that Peter Vetra hadn’t left the ring area!" The camera returns to the live here and now and the broadcast table of Nick and Richard. Nick: Now here we are today, and at the GREAT AMERICAN NIGHTMARE our HOYT WILLIAMS will take on his former stablemate GCW star PETER VETRA!! LIVE AND ONLY ON PPV!!!! There is still time to call your cable provider and sign up to watch a PPV that will be sure to go down in PRIME and wrestling history. Richard: Hoyt Williams and Peter Vetra were supposed to be here tonight but Hoyt had to back out due to an injury. Nick: Standing by at his home in Chicago, IL is Hoyt Williams. Hoyt are you there? The screen splits from the broadcast table to Hoyt Williams sitting at a desk in a suit with the American flag behind him. Hoyt Williams: Yes I am here. Hello to all the fine American voters out there who will help me get to the White House. After last weeks match I was asked not to be on the show tonight because apparently, I upstaged most of the roster. Now I understand that, as none of them are the son of God! Being the fair down to earth guy that I am, I decided to take a week off. Plus it will give me some time to heal my back. Richard: Dare God! What is wrong with your back? Hoyt Williams: It’s a long term injury from all the years I carried those other guys on my back while they were in Imperium. Also all the years I carried the wrestling world and PRIME. Now I’m ready to carry it again but I need a week off to rest up my back. Plus running for President has been hard on me. Also it’s Saw weekend and I had to get my ticket early. Nick: You just watched the videos we just watched. What are your thoughts on your relationship with Peter Vetra?” Hoyt Williams: Who? Nick: Peter Vetra. Hoyt Williams: No idea. Who is that? Nick: The guy in the video. Hoyt Williams: Hummmm the Mexican guys or the short one? Nick: The short one. Hoyt Williams: Is that Peter Vetra? Nick: YES! Hoyt Williams: Oh, because I signed this match and had no idea who he was. I know he’s in that other federation that’s sooooooooo amateur. God told me he tuned into that show and it was so bad it drove him to start drinking. Crazy isn’t it? So anywho I went on their little web site to see if I could find a picture of this Vetra guy to remember if he was the tough Mexicans or the weasely errand boy, and guess what? They don’t even list him!! This guy sucks so bad, a half ass federation is embarrassed to put him on their roster’and you know what, I don’t blame them. Nick: Now you did take a cheap shot at him in the ring at Deadly Games. Hoyt Williams: He shook my hand and gave me a smile I thought he was one of those homos. Now God hates the gays and even as a candidate for the Republican nomination I had to do what was best for me and the other guys in the ring. Wrestlers and GOD hate the gays. It’s true it’s in the bible. Nick: Well how did it feel when he pulled the top rope tossing you out of the ring? Hoyt Williams: It really felt good as it cracked my back in just the right place. Where is Richard, because that’s such a stupid question I can’t answer it with the intelligence and brilliance that I normally do. You’re not talking to a dim witted dame like Lindsey Troy. Richard: Sorry Hoyt! What he meant to ask was how horrible was it when Peter Vetra robbed you by breaking the rules set up by management. Hoyt Williams: Worse then planes flying into towers!! I can’t stand people who break rules that govern what we do. I think the referees should be stricter and punish people who can’t follow the basics of man. Maybe that’s why he’s not listed on the roster they are punishing him by keeping him off the shows. Brilliant if you ask me! I know the people who didn’t believe in my brother were treated bad in the holy-cost thingy in Germany but come on what Hitler Vetra did to me will not and can not go unpunished. AS a candidate for President I will work to punish those who can’t follow the letter of the law starting with this evil sinner. Hoyt pounds his desk with anger. Richard: You have my support for sure. Nick: Are you saying that being knocked out of a rumble was worse then 9-11 because that’s disgusting and wrong. Hoyt Williams: Can we cut off his mic? Nick tries to talk but only his mouth moves no sound comes out. Richard: How great are you? Hoyt Williams: Better then getting a blow job on a rollercoaster in an amusement park in heaven. Nick: Is this thing back on?”” Ok, on the phone is Peter Vetra. The screen splits three ways as we now see the broadcast booth, Hoyt Williams, and Peter Vetra from GCW headquarters. Hoyt Williams: Who? Nick: Peter are you there? Hoyt Williams: HELLO? Peter Vetra: Yes I’m here. Hoyt Williams: Who is that?” Peter Vetra: You fucking know who this is! Hoyt Williams: Yes Richard I hear you. Peter Vetra: Hoyt why did you turn on me? Hoyt Williams: I thought they shut Nick’s microphone off. Peter Vetra: THIS IS PETER VETRA!! Hoyt Williams: Oh, hi how are you? Listen kid lets make this like old times, I need a BLT with no lettuce. An Arnie Palmer, and a naked salad. Now leave me alone I’m talking about fighting some other loser from my past at this joke of a pay-per-view. Peter Vetra: The master of comedy tries to come thru once again. But as usual fails miserably. See the problem Hoyt, is you were to busy thinking of your little man servant instead of thinking of your priorities. Hoyt Williams: No need to call me a master, "Your Savior" is fine. Peter Vetra: Williams, when you ran Imperium you thought you ran all of us. IN all actuality you thought we were all your pawns, but we weren't we were the furthest thing from that?. Hoyt Williams: Then why am I in the hall of fame and your not? Why did I go on to big things and you didn’t? Why am I even talking to you with out my BLT and Arnie Plamer? Peter Vetra: All of those things were actual useless questions... all of which you expected me to come back with a witty response, set you up for your next punch line. Hoyt Williams, I have figured you out. You will win the war of words, but once we step into the ring that’s when the game really matters. Hoyt Williams: I am a fairly witty guy, thank you. But I have to say you got me. Saying that I’m going to beat you in talk but lose to you in the ring is genius. I mean that’s the kind of bulletin board material that locker rooms are made of. You my friend are a walking talking genius. How could they have over looked you for the Hall of Fame? I mean Olsig got in! It’s a shame Prime over looked you for the hall of fame, GCW over looked you when doing their roster page, and God overlooked you when he was giving out personality and talent. I feel for you sinner. Nick: Peter do you have a final thought? Peter Vetra: You can talk the talk but can you walk the walk? Hoyt your time has coming whether you want to call it you’re second coming or just a personal relevance for yourself. The truth is this is the last time Peter Vetra will step into the ring or be in the same ring as a PRIME "superstar". The time will come and this is my time. Nick: Hoyt, final words? Hoyt Williams: Somebody has been listening to too much of the Chambers Brothers and, "Time Has Come Today". My final word is this, vote for me and if you don’t love Hoyt Williams go to hell! The camera cuts back to the broadcast booth! Richard: The time has come and that time is live and only on Pay-Per-View at Great American Nightmare!!
