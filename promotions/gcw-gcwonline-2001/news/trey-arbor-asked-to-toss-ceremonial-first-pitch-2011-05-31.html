Trey Arbor asked to toss Ceremonial First Pitch

31 May 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seattle’s Trey Arbor flies home to receive the honor of throwing the first pitch in front of his hometown Seattle Faithful. The Seattle Mariners welcome the currently struggling AL East Baltimore Orioles to Safeco Field to square off in game two of their three game series tonight. Later this evening, Seattle’s once hopeful Cy Young candidate, will face up against his old team in which he looked promising to win the award with. Erik Bedard, posting an astonishing one earned run in the last 19 innings, walking 5 and whiffing 22 in that span, has been tossing the cheese as well as he has ever, leaving his opponent, Jeremy Guthrie with a tough opponent to seek his 3rd win of the season against. Guthrie has been just as hot posting 4 earned in his last 15 innings of work, walking simply one and sitting down 8 by way of the K. Trey Anderson, Seattle native better known as Trey Arbor of the GCW (Global Championship Wrestling federation), will take the mound at 10 PM Eastern Standard Time to hurl the ceremonial first pitch. Miguel Olivo will likely be asked to suit up and receive the pitch, just in case Arbor proves to chuck his patent 94 MPH fastball that once had him scouted for his rightful chance at playing America’s Favorite Past Time.
