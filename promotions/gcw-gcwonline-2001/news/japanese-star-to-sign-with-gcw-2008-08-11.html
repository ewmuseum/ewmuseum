Japanese star to sign with GCW?

11 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sources within Global Championship Wrestling management tell GCW Online that Steven Caldera's talent search is not over. With coups such as Garbage Bag Johnny under Mr. Caldera's belt, the boss met with another talent as recently as yesterday, with the intent of bringing this man into the GCW fold. The talent is rumored to be Kenjiro Ito, formerly of Jolt Wrestling and PRIME. He is best known for his long Ambassador Championship reign in the Jolt promotion, as well as his obscene, profanity-laced tirade interview style. Ito competes with a hard-hitting, heavily Japanese-influenced style. Inquiries to Mr. Caldera's office were met with a non-committal response from his secretary, who said only that Caldera was meeting with a variety of talent from around the world in hopes of convincing them to sign with GCW. Other sources, though, say that the deal is all but done, and that Ito could make his debut as soon as WorldWide. Stay tuned to GCW programming for future developments.
