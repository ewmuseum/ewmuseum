Friends No More

16 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Oblivion is dead! When most of us were expecting a reunion of Oblivion into a three man stable, the tables were quickly turned on all of us. Mortfiera vs Bryan Mayhem has gotten bloody and personal. After the airing of WorldWide this week Bryan Mayhem and his former friend and ally Shaman were seen backstage beating the holy hell out of each other. There was a point where it seemed Mayhem had the edge as he began choking Shaman with cable wire. Many on hand said Shaman's face turned blue and Mayhem was talking to himself during the entire attack. Shaman was spared by his partner. Jorge took a pallet and broke it over the back of Bryan Mayhem. It took 25 police, security guards, and GCW officials to break up the fight. All three men were badly beaten and bloodied. In what seemed to be a rather calm interaction the past few weeks between Mayhem and Mortifera has turned into an all out war. As of this time we have no information on what started this brawl backstage, but according to reports all three men have been fined significantly. The dollar value has not been released but the rumblings backstage say all three men must cover the expense damages done to GCW property and medical bills of the 4 hospitalized onlookers. We will keep you posted as this story develops.
