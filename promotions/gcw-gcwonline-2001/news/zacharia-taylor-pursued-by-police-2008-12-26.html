Zacharia Taylor Pursued by Police?

26 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Police exhume a body in relation to a suspected kidnapping and murder of an unidentifiable man in his thirties. Police exhumed the body on the morning of the 25th of December, Christmas Morning, at a church in Clifton, New Jersey. Authorities say that an unnamed woman walked through the graveyard in the courtyard of the church when she saw blood next to a broken headstone. When the report was filed, the coronary office dispatched their team. The blood proved to be at least a month old. When the area was dug up in front of the headstone, which was unmarked, the body showed to be beaten to death, with a possible blunt stab to the neck/chest region. The body was mostly decomposed, which made it more difficult to identify, but DNA tests were sent out. Nobody at the church was available for questioning. People of the community say that GCW's own Zacharia Taylor was left in charge of the church recently upon the death of the main pastor, Father Emanuel. Zacharia Taylor is the main suspect after a man came forward saying about a month ago there was a disturbance in the church and then Zacharia was seen running through the park, looking like he was running away. Zacharia was also seen breaking into a car that same day in front of his church. Police put a warrant out for his arrest, upon suspicion of the crime, but the warrant is just to track him down for questioning. Police have been in touch with GCW President Caldera, but he hasn't been in contact with his employee either. "Mr. Taylor is a hard man to find, not having a phone or mailing address," President Caldera says. "I was unaware of his employment by the church, even though I know of his strong religious beliefs. He is one of our rising stars and I could not see him being involved with such a case." Police will be looking for Zacharia Taylor wherever the next show is held in the GCW company. Even though he is a man of the church, people are advised to call police before approaching him.
