GCW Tag Titles Possibly Defended at WorldWide

12 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It looks as if the GCW Tag Team titles will get defended a little earlier than expected. In recent weeks as confrontations between the champions Banned & Exiled~! and the team of Vivica Valentine and Lindsay Troy have escalated, rumored had been swirling that the two teams would meet at Rampage for the titles. But at a recent interview, Banned & Exiled~! made a challenge to the duo of ladies for the tag team titles to take place at WorldWide this week. But the record-breaking tag team wasn’t ready to strap on their wrestling boots just yet, no rather for the first time in GCW history, the title will be defended in…A DEBATE? As odd as it seems, it seems nothing is too odd for Banned & Exiled~! The two announced that they are ready and have even gotten “the Ragin’ Cajun” himself, Mr. James Carville to be the moderator for the debate. Storm: Oh yeah, he’s a huge fan of our work. Completely loves us, we’ve had dinner with his family and signed some autographs for his kids. He’s a great guy. Stone: We feel he can be a fair and unbiased moderator for the debate. Storm: Absolutely. When reached for comment, both Vivica J. Valentine and Lindsay Troy denied comment. No one at the GCW home office could be reached for comment due to moving reasons, so it is yet to see if they will officially allow the match. But given the close relationship between the Owner Steven Caldera and his members of the Establishment, it would not surprise anyone to see the match happen.
