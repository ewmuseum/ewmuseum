Hall of Famer O'Neil to Return to GCW?

4 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a surprising ceremony at NC-17, Global Championship Wrestling inducted Jason O'Neil into the Hall of Fame. Only a select few superstars make it to this level, and seeing O'Neil there comes as something of a surprise. O'Neil certain has the talent to be there, and he most definitely made an impact on GCW. But O'Neil is without a doubt the biggest thorn ever wedged into GCW President Steven Caldera's side, and to learn that Caldera signed off on the induction makes things doubly confusing. During his later years with GCW, O'Neil, who is often credited as one of the principle founding fathers of the company, actively tried to shut it down. In fact, the first closing of GCW is often attributed to O'Neil. Though Caldera has never spoken publicly about the incident, it is said that Caldera has never forgiven O'Neil for his actions. Yet when the moment came, Caldera gave his blessing, and O'Neil is now enshrined next to the legends of GCW. And now, less than a day later, reports are flying in that O'Neil may be making a return to the company. While it is highly unlikely that O'Neil would ever sign a long-term contract with GCW, it is looking possible that he may be hoping to make another appearance on GCW Television. It is assumed that O'Neil is simply hoping to address his induction, but with the Dark Messiah, anything is possible. Perhaps greasing the tracks is the presence of Commissioner Christian Zenith, who has been acting as an intermediary between Caldera and some of his less friendly contacts. It is doubtful that Caldera and O'Neil would meet face to face, but it is possible that Zenith could be meeting with O'Neil this week to negotiate an appearance. This information is largely speculation from sources inside Caldera Enterprises. The company is still on tour on the west coast, and Caldera remains sequestered inside the new CEi office in New York. It is being reported that O'Neil contacted the company late last night after the NC-17 broadcast, but this is far from confirmed at this point. Stay tuned to GCW in recent weeks. A Jason O'Neil return to the GCW locker room could mean serious trouble.
