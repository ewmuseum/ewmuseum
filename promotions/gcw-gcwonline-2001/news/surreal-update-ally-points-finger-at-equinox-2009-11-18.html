SurReal update, ally points finger at Equinox

18 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCWonline.net has just received an update on the condition of SurReal. The Irishman was mowed over by his own motor vehicle just before the main event on Worldwide. Fans and staff alike were in shock to see this atrocious attack, whilst GCW prides itself on its competitiveness, sometimes boundaries can be overstepped, and this attack on the Tribal War Machine serves as a reminder of that. Remarkably SurReal managed to avoid any serious injuries, though he is suffering from a mild concussion and is quite badly bruised. Many had feared the worst at the time of the accident and the unfortunate loss of the camera that caught the event added to the concerns for the Irishman’s health. But happily he will live to fight another day. Meanwhile other members of the GCW roster have had little doubt as to who was behind the attack. We managed a quick word with SurReal’s long time friend Shaman who was quick to suggest the culprit. "It is as obvious as the day is long! Ever since Lori Equinox came to GCW bad things have been happening to SurReal. There is no point in him denying responsibility, he has to hold his hands up and suffer the consequences because when SurReal comes back, he will be coming for Equinox!" Shaman voicing the general consensus within GCW that the bitter newcomer is behind not only the horrific assault at Worldwide, but also the tricks and mindgames that have plagued SurReal for several weeks now. The word from GCW HQ is that Equinox will be appearing on this week’s Worldwide in an interview with James Bryan to face these allegations. Don’t forget to tune in to see what Lori Equinox has to say for himself.
