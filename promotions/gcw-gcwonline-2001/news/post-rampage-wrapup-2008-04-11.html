Post-Rampage Wrapup

11 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, Kevin Jacobs back with you once again to wrap up an exciting tour of Japan! The whole crew is back home in the United States now, enjoying some well-earned R&R. Japan's too far away to commute home between shows, so, save a few exceptions, everyone's been on the other side of the Pacific for over a month. But we're back after a wildly successful and profitable tour of a beautiful country, and I've got all your news, notes and tidbits from the tour, Rampage, and the coming months! -Rampage did impressive buyrates. The final numbers haven't been released, but GCW is anticipating that they will exceed expectations. The Japanese fans were tremendous hosts, and it was really nice to see how courteous and intelligent they are when it comes to wrestling. The Tokyo Dome was rocking for Rampage, and the buyrates were no doubt boosted by the increased attention in the East. -Thankfully there were no major injuries to report. World Champion Vivica J. Valentine spent an extra night in Tokyo in a hospital for observation; she suffered some pretty major blows to the head thanks to challenger Shaman. Valentine is said to have suffered a concussion, but it's nothing major. Valentine won't be in action when WorldWide returns in Seattle, but she should be fine. Jordan White suffered a pretty major laceration from the chair shot delivered by Jay Terror, and took seven stitches in his forehead. Beyond that, nothing more than the usual bumps and bruises to report! -Tyson DeBough returned at Rampage, and targeted Clyde Fox. For those of you who may have forgotten (since Clyde held the title for so long!), Clyde Fox defeated DeBough to win the title back in January. DeBough had been on injury leave since then, but apparently never forgot that Clyde had taken his belt. Expect DeBough to be on WorldWide, and expect Fox to be back on his feet. -Speaking of returns, I spotted Brad Jackson backstage at Rampage. He didn't have much to say to anyone, but his name appeared on this week's WorldWide lineup. Looks like Jackson, who also has been recovering from injuries, is back and ready to go. Jackson, like DeBough, was a fast-rising star in GCW before an injury derailed his ascent, so expect both men to quickly pick up where they left off. -GCW has signed a number of new Tag Teams. With Banned and Exiled~! now on year two of their historic title reign, expect the competition to heat up! We've also got some impressive new singles talent coming in. With GCW topping the ratings pretty routinely, it's pretty clear that all the top free agents are hoping to earn a contract from the top promotion in the world. -At Rampage, GCW announced its next major Pay-Per-View event, Warpath. But what wasn't announced is the return of several other PPV dates to the schedule. While GCW's "supershows" were highly popular, GCW decided the increasing popularity of the company demanded that these events return to PPV. So our next Pay-Per-View offering will feature the return of the highly successful High Stakes! The show did huge numbers last year, and the roster is extremely pleased to see the event return to the lineup. Last year the event was hosted by Shaman in his casino, but rumor has it that the relationship between Shaman and President Caldera has soured, and in response Caldera is looking at several sites in Las Vegas for the show. -Speaking of Caldera, the new-look Establishment will be at WorldWide to address the crowd. New Number One Contender Jay Terror will definitely have some explaining to do after his shocking turn. -Rumor has it Lindsay Troy has been granted permission to attend GCW's WorldWide broadcast next week. While Troy is a well-documented rival of our esteemed owner, Caldera is reportedly anticipating huge ratings from the massive Troy/Valentine matchup scheduled two weeks from now. Troy, the PRIME Universal Champion and the PTC Unified Champion, will meet GCW Champion Vivica J. Valentine in an historic one-on-one contest. PTC officials are reportedly flying to Columbus this week to meet with Caldera to go over the ground rules, but if I know our President, it'll be Caldera doing all the talking. -Chris Bagwell was uncharacteristically soft spoken on his GCW Radio appearance this week. Seems the Rampage Tournament runner-up is at a loss for words after his henchman Kiljaden Kincaid miscalculated and cost him the match. The vast majority of the company sees referee Hal Jenkins's decision to let the match go as a good call, and the company officially upheld the match earlier this week. Andy Murray's talent cannot be denied, but there are still some within the company who are taking Bagwell's side in the matter. Murray will go down in history as the winner of the 2008 Rampage Tournament, but something tells me we haven't heard the last of Mr. Bagwell on the subject. -Clyde Fox was said to take the loss of his Television Title pretty hard. Fox has earned a tremendous following, and there's little doubt that he's earned the opportunity to challenge for more gold in the near future. In the meantime, Bagwell's accomplice Zachariah Taylor has really established himself and should make an excellent Television Champion. -Octavian got a big win at Rampage over Bryan Mayhem, but I witnessed first hand the aftermath of the matchup. Mayhem gave chase to Octavian, who managed to elude the big man and escape in an Establishment limousine-- only to sneak back into the arena later that night. Still, the crowd is startlingly behind Mayhem lately, and the big man will no doubt be seeking to get himself back into the world title picture. -Nobody's heard from Shaman since he came up short in the main event. Shaman reportedly boarded a plane back home immediately and has stayed out of public since then. He did send one of our road agents a text message assuring us that he'd be at WorldWide. This strikes me as the calm before the storm; expect Shaman to have plenty to say this week. -Big win for Digital Mortality over Seymour Almasy at Rampage. Thanks to the victory, Digi took the United States Title belt for himself after Almasy stole it from the rightful champion, Tessa Windsor. But there's no indication of what Digital Mortality intends to do with the belt. It's common knowledge now that Digi wants the title, but he's got to get himself a match with Windsor in order to make the belt his own. Tune in this week to find out just what Digi intends to do with the belt. -Expect a comment this week from GCW regarding the LeStatt Knight/Lia Ambrosi match that didn't air at Rampage. I don't have all the details, but it seems there's some controversy brewing there that caused the match to be delayed. That's it from GCW for this week! Tune in to WorldWide for all the Rampage fallout, and don't forget the huge Valentine/Troy match in two weeks!
