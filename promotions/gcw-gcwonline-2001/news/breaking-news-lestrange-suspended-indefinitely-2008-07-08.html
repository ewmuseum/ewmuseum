Breaking News: LeStrange suspended indefinitely.

8 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In an official press conference yesterday, it was revealed that Englishman Thom LeStrange has been suspended from GCW until further notice... Internet rumour has been rife with speculation as to the current circumstances of GCW wrestler Thom LeStrange. Recent GCW events and television shows have been marked by the Englishman's absence, fuelling debate over both his relationship with the company and the state of his private life. It was announced today by GCW President Steven Caldera that Thom LeStrange has been suspended from GCW until further notice. Once a decidedly (some would argue almost obnoxiously) public figure, LeStrange has become something of a recluse of late. GCW faithful had become accustomed to the exquisitely-dressed, erudite and confident figure that LeStrange cut during his first few months with GCW. However, since the well-documented passing of his parents, eye-witnesses report that LeStrange is a shell of his former self. Those who have seen him on the rare occasions he has ventured into the public sphere have noted a dishevelled appearance, and an apparent disregard for his own wellbeing. One member of the public, upon spotting LeStrange at a gas station described him as "wearing dark sunglasses, walking in an ungainly fashion," and as having "horribly pale skin and a sickly cough." The majority of the infrequent sightings of the Englishman have been in bars and nightclubs, and, unfortunately, in varying states of inebriation and intoxication. Unofficial and unconfirmed reports of drug and alcohol abuse are beginning to emerge as the LeStrange story gathers momentum. One club-goer is believed to have reported to have seen LeStrange quote "With pupils the size of dinnerplates, grinding his teeth like a madman." Global Championship Wrestling have been distinctly evasive on the subject, but in a short press conference, Steven Caldera had this to say: "GCW regrets to announce the suspension until further notice of Thomas LeStrange." The official reason cited was a violation of the GCW drugs policy, but Caldera noted that the suspension was "As much an intervention as a punitive measure," and that he felt the suspension was necessary "For Thom's own wellbeing as well as rule enforcement," and stated the company had "a genuine concern" for the Englishman's health, "both physical and mental." It is reported that LeStrange will be mandated to undergo a combination of rehabilitation for his alcohol and drug abuse, and grief counselling for the loss of his parents, and will only be permitted to return to a GCW ring following psychiatric approval. Whilst we do not condone LeStrange's behaviour, we wish him well with his recovery, and hope that he may cast his demons aside and return to the promising form he displayed at the beginning of his GCW tenure.
