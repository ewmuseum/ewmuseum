GCW Signs Japanese Independent Wrestler

7 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW announces a new signing! GCW officials announced today the signing of another talent to continue fleshing out a GCW roster that has seen departures in recent weeks and months. The lucky recipient of a GCW contract this time is Japanese wrestler Ryuji Kamigawa. Standing 6'5", and weighing 300 pounds, Kamigawa brings a power game to GCW rings, but also brings a flash and flair that men his size often do not possess. GCW officials were particularly impressed watching him execute a manuever he calls "Ten Kara" (From Heaven, in Japanese), a picture-perfect, high hangtime moonsault off the top rope. "He's as well-rounded as any prospect of his size that we've seen in quite some time," one GCW scout said. "He's a world of potential once he gets some experience." Kamigawa was made known to GCW officials by a perhaps shocking source: GCW pariah (and, admittedly, former PTC cornerstone) Seymour Almasy. Almasy is, perhaps not coincidentally, the man who trained Ryuji. When asked about taking advice from a man so unpopular within GCW, that same talent scout was short and to the point. "Almasy and GCW may not like one another," he said, "but each side at least accepts one thing. Almasy would never dispute that GCW knows how to make money, and we would never dispute that, when he's focused, Seymour is both an incredible talent and an incredible judge of talent." While Kamigawa is still in the process of receiving a work visa, he will be making his debut at Shockwave 2011 in the Rampage Royale. Due to the fortunate circumstance of the show taking place on the moon, he will be able to compete even if his American work visa is not fully processed by showtime. More news about Ryuji Kamigawa and his debut will come as we receive it. Kamigawa has already received a roster page on GCW Online, for any curious fans who wish to read about the newest newcomer to the company.
