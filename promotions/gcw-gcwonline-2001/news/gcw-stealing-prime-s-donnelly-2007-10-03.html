GCW Stealing PRIME's Donnelly?

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With a joint event with rival promotion PRIME looming a month away, the competition has never been heavier... in the ring or in the boardroom. Rumor from GCW Headquarters today says that GCW officials, including a personal appearance from GCW President Steven Caldera, met with suspended PRIME wrestler Nitz Donnelly. Donnelly caught PRIME's eye on the independent circuit as a sensational high-flying talent, but due to his rather rough demeanor he earned a suspension from the company a short time ago. Donnelly, reportedly angered by this suspension, did receive the largest signing bonus in PRIME history, but with Caldera's notoriously deep pockets in the room GCW could top PRIME by offering him an even higher number. Donnelly is noted for his problems with authority, problems that earned him his suspension with PRIME, making his interview with Caldera rather strange. Still, it is common knowledge that Caldera would be more than happy to put up with the headache if it meant trotting out Donnelly as a GCW-contracted superstar at Devil's Night/Great American Nightmare. Donnelly was interviewed for this article, but GCW editors have declined to print it here due to the graphic nature of its content. Still, GCW has been aggressively pursuing new talent, and it now appears that with the fourth wall breached, GCW has no problems reaching into PRIME's talent pool in search of new names.
