The Conchairto Heard 'Round the World

18 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That sound you heard at WorldWide this week? It was the sound of the silence of a shocked capacity crowd. It was the sound of a staggering betrayal. It was the sound of a drastically shifting landscape. It was the sound of the steel chairs of Chris Storm and Markus Stone colliding with the skull of Vivica J. Valentine. It was the sound of Banned and Exiled~! joining The Establishment. In a scene no one thought possible, the GCW Tag Team Champions joined The Establishment in dismantling Valentine and her tag partner Tessa Windsor. Just days ago it seemed the legendary group had fractured beyond repair. But Brad Jackson, Tyson DeBough and Brian Fisher, quarterbacked by Jay Terror, concocted a fiendish scheme to reclaim the spotlight in a big way. The match was set: Valentine was to take on Fisher and DeBough, a match presumably handed down from the office of President Steven Caldera. Tessa Windsor underwent an unexpected change of heart, casting aside some misplaced loyalties to stand in her friend Valentine's corner once more. Unfortunately for Windsor, The Establishment has vastly superior numbers thanks to their two newest members. Windsor and Valentine felt the brunt of the rejuvenated Establishment, which may be the greatest collection of talent the group has ever seen. Even with alumni including Jason Dread, Octavian, Michael Stevens and Toxic, The Establishment has never been this fearsome from top to bottom. The result is a group that suddenly looks poised to dominate Global Championship Wrestling, even in the absence of their spiritual leader Caldera. After recovering from her considerable injuries, Valentine reportedly caused quite a scene backstage. The irate former World Champion went looking for the traitorous Tag Champions, but The Establishment had already departed for the evening. Valentine isn't likely to cool down before next week. For her part, Windsor retreated quietly from San Diego and hasn't appeared publicly. However, indications from backstage suggest Windsor isn't any happier about the situation than Valentine, especially given her existing relationship with Chris Storm. GCW fans should expect a response from the two talented female superstars next week. Windsor will be in the ring with Jay Terror in a fatal fourway main event, and Valentine will undoubtedly be seeking revenge. Even if the odds are once again stacked against her.
