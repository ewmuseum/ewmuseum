Warpath Underway; PRIME Invades GCW!

26 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Raleigh-Durham, NC- In one of the most anticipated matches of the year, PRIME star Lindsay Troy wrestled in the GCW ring against her most bitter GCW rival; President Steven Caldera. And, as expected, Caldera did everything in his power to make sure things went GCW's way. To Caldera's credit, he ordered a restart to the match after Lindsay Troy made use of a steel chair. But in the end, when the chips were down and there was nowhere else to turn, The Establishment made an appearance. The match ended in Troy's favor, but the real story came after the bell. PRIME stars Killean Sirrajin and Tchu hit the ring, backing up Troy and battling The Establishment to a fierce stalemate. After the show, Caldera was enraged that Tchu and Sirrajin had involved themselves in the match. Lindsay Troy, meanwhile, has been bragging that she was fully prepared for Caldera's antics. "Poor predictable Stevie," Troy said after the match. "You really thought I wasn't going to be ready for that?" Caldera has made it clear that the issue is not over, and he intends to strike back at all three PRIME stars. In other action, the Warpath Tournament is underway! Four exciting matches took place this week, and four talented wrestlers are moving on. The Establishment's LeStatt Knight and Jordan White advanced after solid victories over Kyle James and Draven Lox, respectively. Jayce Bradley won a surprisingly hard fought battle over talented newcomer Jay Terror, and Twizzy won a quick battle with Damon Aldair. The Tournament continues next week with the rest of the first round matches. Also this week, The Takahashi Brothers won a good opening Tag Team bout with Pleasure and Pain, making an impressive debut with the company. The GCW Tag Division continues to experience a resurgence, and GCW is excited about the presence of both these teams on the roster. Conspicuous by their absence was former champion Lia Ambrosi. Ambrosi was reportedly seen backstage, but did not make an appearance on camera. The rumors have already begun that the loss to Mayhem at High Stakes has really devastated Ambrosi mentally, and she is taking time to recover her wits before trying to get back into the ring. Ambrosi is expected to be back on the air next week, though it remains to be seen what capacity she'll be in. Don't miss WorldWide next week as the Warpath Tournament continues, and Bryan Mayhem puts his World Title on the line for the first time! Mayhem faces Twizzy, who is still embroiled in the Warpath tournament. This has led to much anger backstage that Twizzy has bypassed the tournament and received a title shot, but those who know President Caldera and his current issues with Twizzy speculate that this may be less of a match... and more of a beating.
