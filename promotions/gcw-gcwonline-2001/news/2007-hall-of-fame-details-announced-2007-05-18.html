2007 Hall of Fame Details Announced

18 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW officials officially announced details for the 2007 Hall of Fame induction to be held at Warpath. Each year at Warpath, the anniversary of GCW's first Pay-Per-View program, Global Championship Wrestling honors one of its greats by enshrining them in the hallowed Hall of Fame. This year, GCW will be adding a new member to the Hall, as well as honoring those who have already received this great honor. Though this year's honoree won't be named until Warpath, the word at GCW headquarters is that this year's Hall of Famer is an active member of the current GCW roster. While there are several names that could be considered for this spot, it is widely held that Twizzy, who has been lobbying hard for a spot in the Hall, will get his wish and be inducted at Warpath. Twizzy, one of GCW's most beloved wrestlers, is a one-time GCW World Champion and a two-time Tag Team Champion. Twizzy returned after an extended hiatus to attempt to bolster his impressive resume and remind the voters of why he deserves to be remembered as one of GCW's all time best. While his induction is by no means official, it seems quite likely at this point that the longtime veteran and fan favorite will get his wish. In addition to the new member, GCW expressed its desire to honor those who have already been enshrined. Octavian, Rich Rollins, The Jhub and Jason Dread currently hold places in the Hall of Fame, but of the foursome only Octavian received a true induction ceremony. While there have been constant rumors surrounding all four legends, none are expected to be present at Warpath. Still, in an effort to re-introduce GCW's history to a new generation of fans, GCW will release a series of "Best Of..." DVDs. The DVDs will be sold individually and as a set, and will feature the best moments in the careers of these Hall of Famers. Rumored to be up first are "The Best of Rich Rollins" and "The Best of Jason Dread." Sources say that GCW has been in contact with Rollins and Dread in hopes that they might play host to their own highlights. Compilations featuring The Jhub and Octavian are rumored to be in the works as well, for release this fall. GCW has not announced further details, but the DVDs will be available here at gcwonline.net. Expected retail price is $19.99. The first two in the series are expected to be released just before Warpath later this month.
