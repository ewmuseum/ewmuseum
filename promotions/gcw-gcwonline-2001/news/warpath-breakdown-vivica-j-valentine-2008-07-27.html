Warpath Breakdown: Vivica J. Valentine

27 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Warpath just days away, James Bryan joins us to break down the exciting Main Event match between Vivica J. Valentine, Jay Terror and Jordan White. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship is on the line, and this is shaping up to be one of the most competitive GCW Title matches in history. JB will be breaking down each competitor's strengths, weaknesses and motivations, and we'll cap it off with the staff's prediction. Today: Vivica J. Valentine Introduction: What exactly do you make of Vivica J. Valentine? As Champion she has inspired a wide range of responses. While the fan response has been monumental, critics continue to have their doubts. Valentine is no doubt a worthy champion, but is she worthy of the mantle of "Greatest Champion Ever?" It's one of the most hallowed unofficial titles in GCW culture. For years LeStatt Knight has been the undisputed best of all time, but now his status is in doubt. Valentine has set the mark as longest reigning champion in GCW history, but some say she has yet to have a truly defining moment. LeStatt Knight defeated Michael Stevens twice before finally falling; Valentine defeated Shaman, but has yet to have another significant defense. Biggest Strengths: Speed, speed, speed. Valentine is an absolute blur in the ring, and can contort her body in ways no human should be able to. As a result, her lack of size is negated by the fact that her opponents can rarely get their hands on her. Valentine can run circles around Jordan White, and should be able to keep Terror off balance as well. She's also got a significant edge in big-match experience and success. Biggest Weaknesses: Size, size, size. Valentine just doesn't have any. Though she's proven to be anything but fragile, she still can't match up shot to shot with most wrestlers, much less two who hit as hard as Terror and White. If she gets caught in too many high-impact moves, she'll be in trouble. Biggest Career Victory: Defeating Lia Ambrosi at NC-17 2007 to become GCW World Champion. Valentine has had lots of big wins; she won Dangerous Games in 2007 and defeated LeStatt Knight in last year's Warpath tournament. But nothing can top her World Title victory over her friend and mentor Ambrosi. What She Needs To Do To Win: Stick and move. Valentine does not want to trade blows with Terror or White. She wants to keep the match fast paced and high flying; in the air and on the move she's got a distinct advantage. Let's not overstate the issue: Valentine's tough and has the chin to take a punch. But in a match this significant, the longer things go the more they favor the champion. But she's got a huge handicap in that she does not need to be involved in the fall to lose the title. Valentine will need to stay extra vigilant and make sure she's never too far from the action, lest the match end without her being present. How She Matches Up With Terror: This will be their first true meeting in the ring. Valentine's speed edge isn't as significant here; Terror can move pretty well and should be able to manage. Still, Terror's improvisational skills compliment Valentine. The Champion can react quickly and won't be taken by surprise. Valentine will want to keep things moving quickly, however, to keep Terror off his game and on his heels. How She Matches Up With White: White has managed to get the best of Valentine on several occasions, and defeated her at Warpath last year. There's no doubt this will weigh on Valentine's mind during the match. But while White is still trying to evolve, Valentine's game has been taken up a notch since last year's meeting. Valentine and White engaged in a violent match at High Stakes where she evenly traded heavy moves with White, but she doesn't want to do that again. The match left Valentine hurting, and while she walked out with the belt, this time she's got Jay Terror to worry about, too. Valentine will need to avoid the big exchanges and try to bide her time. Motivation: The word for Valentine's role in this match is greatness. How much of it does she have? How great a champion will she be? If Valentine loses this match it won't take away from her legendary reign. Yet the critics will only begin to speak louder. The stars are aligning for Jay Terror and most are picking him to become Champion. There's little doubt that this has left a chip on the Champion's shoulder. Valentine's ascension culminated in a huge title win at NC-17. Terror's own rise is strikingly similar, though Valentine did it without Establishment assistance. Still, Valentine is all too familiar with the story. Now she is seeing it from her old mentor's eyes, as the champion hoping to retain against the fast riser everyone thinks is destined to win. Valentine knows about destiny, and she is fighting for her own. A victory in this match, with the odds so firmly against her, would certainly be the defining victory Valentine needs to cement her status as the greatest champion of all time. Valentine is deeply aware of this; she has done everything she could do to prove herself, yet she keeps hearing the whispers. This match is Valentine's opportunity to bring the ascension of Jay Terror to a sudden halt, and to show Jordan White what it really takes to be champion. But the odds have never been this tall. Valentine has had a knack for fighting her way out of corners before, and has performed brilliantly under the biggest of spotlights. So why is everyone picking against her? Because all great things must ultimately come to an end. Next Up: Staff Predictions: Tomorrow
