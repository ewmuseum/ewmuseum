Murray "shocked" by Bagwell death

11 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the rest of the world struggling to come to terms with the sudden death of one of professional wrestling's more notorious characters, a somewhat surprising voice has spoken up. Speaking candidly from outside Global Championship Wrestling's Houston offices this afternoon, Andy Murray has expressed his ?shock and astonishment? at the abrupt passing of Chris Bagwell. ?I was as blown away by the news as everyone else. I woke up yesterday morning with about forty messages waiting on my phone to tell me that a guy I was standing backstage with just twelve hours prior had died. I am completely and utterly shellshocked.? Murray ? who is currently recuperating after a fatal cannibalisation at Shockwave left him stuck in Scott Stevens' digestive tract ? has a storied history with Bagwell and their now notorious 2008 Feud of the Year has set the bar in GCW for midcard feuds. The two waged an all-out brutal war for the best part of half a year and their bloody wars need no introduction. The fact that Andy Murray's name is down as the first GCW roster member to comment on Bagwell's death may cause some surprise among the GCW faithful, but the ever-classy Scot has responded with respect and diplomacy. ?I worked very closely with Chris for a massive chunk of 2008 and, for better or worse, I think I came the closest out of anyone else in the company to figuring out what made the man tick. Chris Bagwell was a big part of defining everything that I've since accomplished in GCW and the company is definitely a lesser place without him. Our television characters were like chalk and cheese, but never have I come across a man who worked so hard backstage. Chris Bagwell was a breathtakingly talented entertainer and I am deeply saddened by this loss.? It is likely that Murray's words will incur the wrath of Steven Caldera, who is said to have imposed a blanket media ban for GCW wrestlers in times like these following the death of Rich Rollins in 2008.
