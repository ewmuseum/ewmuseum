Chris Storm Rejects New Contract

19 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christian Zenith Continues to Try and Impress as NC-17 approaches Word around the water cooler is that only days before NC-17 Chris Storm has rejected a new contract that would have made the former record-breaking tag team champion available for the PPV. The contract, drawn up by Christian Zenith, is apparently the second one this month Storm has rejected. Sources are saying that Zenith personally talked to Storm about the new contract in hopes of continuing to impress Steven Caldera. GCW has been trying to get Storm to sign a new contract ever since his two-year agreement ended in June of last year. Storm wrestled for just under a month with a per-appearance deal after that but has been out of the GCW ring ever since. He has repeatedly said that he has redirected himself to spend more time with his family and to support Tessa Windsor behind the scenes. An agent for Storm said he will be making an appearance at FanFest, but only to sign autographs and to support Windsor. Signing Storm to a new contract would have been a big feather in the cap for Big Z, as GCW could really use a tag team specialist to help shore up the division. Possible new partners included Rikki Roxx, his old pal Arizona, and even possibly reuniting former two time PCW Tag Team Champions, the Schmoopies for a run at the titles. But at the moment, Tessa Windsor, the other half of the Schmoopies, seems to be more likely to make a run at the World title, if she can get past Markus Stone at NC-17 in what has got to be one of the hottest matches of the show. Storm was also one of the most popular wrestlers with the fans as a part of Banned & Exiled~! And there’s no doubt the company would enjoy the merchandise sales increase it would likely see if he was to re-sign. The company typically likes to try and surprise fans with big names returning at NC-17, but as of right now it doesn’t look like Chris Storm will be one of them. -Jimmy Brazzo Lead Writer, Between the Dirtsheets
