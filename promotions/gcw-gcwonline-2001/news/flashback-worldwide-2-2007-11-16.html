Flashback: WorldWide 2

16 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The second edition of WorldWide took place from the American Airlines Center in Dallas, Texas. Called by James Bryan and President Steven Caldera, the event kept the momentum going for recovering GCW. As only the second show since the company arose after a nearly two-year long hiatus, WorldWide 2 still shows the company trying to find its identity. Still, the event was of very high quality and featured much of the talent that made GCW the best. You can view the show here. The event kicked off with Octavian introducing his Imperial Roman Championship belt. As GCW's only three-time World Title winner, Octavian had become obsessed with perpetually holding gold. With the World Title vacant until ACWF, GCW's first Pay-Per-View since the rebirth, Octavian decided he simply couldn't wait. He awarded himself the title that he would hold indefinitely, but Jason Dread appeared to reignite their intense rivalry. Niklas Bauer, the massive German, got the first of several Hardcore title shots against the inimitable Hardcore Legend Toad, but Toad was too much for him and retained his belt. In other action, the legend of LeStatt Knight perhaps began on this show as Knight devastated the once-dominant Khan. Later, Jason Dread took on Toxic, but after an attack from behind by Octavian, Dread fell to the former teenage phenom. Peter Maxwell, "The Dark Messiah," made an appearance on this event, assaulting Caldera's protoge Mike Hardy. It would touch of a short but brutal feud between the two. In the evening's main event, The Jhub took on the returning and always controversial former World Champion Tristain Mayhem. Mayhem stole a win from The Jhub, but the night wasn't over. President Caldera revealed a tag team match to decide the first entrant into the World Title match at ACWF, and revealed that Mayhem would be forced to team with his greatest rival, Octavian, the following week. WorldWide 2 continued to shape the company that was trying to re-establish itself as a powerhouse. The event was only one hour long, but it provided the building blocks for the future.
