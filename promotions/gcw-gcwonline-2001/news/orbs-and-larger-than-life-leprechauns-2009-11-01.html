Orbs and Larger Than Life Leprechauns

1 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last night’s festivities were a little more than what the world could handle. Reports have come in from all over the world of public appearances of some of GCW’s very own. From glowing orbs to police reports of scary larger than life leprechauns, GCW stars made the best of Halloween! Reports first came into GCW Headquarters from Ireland and Scotland. In Ireland, video was taken from inside a bar of a reported “larger than life leprechaun” stormed in from the bathroom and began taking pints for everyone and smashing the glasses into tables. Sources state that GCW’s own SurReal then proceeded to t-bag a man screaming “I hate you Andy Murray!”. Police picked him up several minutes later. Moments later, reports came in that Andy Murray was arrested for being….Andy Murray…. The second reports came in from the east coast. The press notified GCW’s President Caldera of their custody of former Television Champion, Dan Black for public disturbance and assault. President Caldera made a few calls and Black was released. Black was repeatedly in a fist fight after he entered a bar and came in contact with a man dressed as Hitler and another as a member of the KKK (later reported as Casper the friendly Ghost). Black confronted both men declaring he was Jewish, and after the man dressed as Hitler laughed at him, Black hit him until police arrived. The second report from the east coast came from Newark, NJ where sources say two men baited kids and stole their candy. Two different reports came about the same two men. One acted as a local priest, baiting in the children as the other man robbed them blind. The other was one man baited MILFS while the other robbed the unsupervised children. Zacharia Taylor was caught while one of the kids claimed he was touched by the priest and Chris Bagwell when one of the women he was baiting was a man… Finally, a report came in very late last night from Arizona when police were in a stand off with a freakishly big Native American. The standoff took place in a Viking Museum where a reported Jorge Samuelsson was being put on exhibit later in the week. The large Native American made his last stand, throwing glowing orbs at the doors and large exhibits to barricade himself in. He was let free after two hours in negotiations, one term being allowed a shot at the World Title after the next PPV. Caldera was reached for comment after the last report. “What has happen tonight is a disgrace to GCW and I will deal with all parties individually. The only one still in custody is Chris Bagwell…and no, Shaman will not have a Title Shot.”
