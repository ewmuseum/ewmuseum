The Onslaught

31 January 2010

The onslaught continues as reports are coming in that Jay Terror and Jason O'Neil have met again at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. The onslaught continues as reports are coming in that Jay Terror and Jason O'Neil have met again at the Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. Terror, coming off a Just Wrestling loss in Croyden was met by O'Neil who used his suitcase as a battering ram. Terror reportedly exited the tunnel and was met with a stiff shot to the back of the head amidst waiting family members. O'Neil continued his attack on Terror until airport security was able to jump in and defuse the situation. O'Neil was arrested on charges of assault and battery while Terror suffered a broken nose from O'Neil's punches. "O'Neil's a dead man," was one of the few sentiments Terror had to offer. "He made a mistake doing this so close to NC-17." Over the last few weeks we've seen Jason O'Neil and Jay Terror taking this rivalry outside of the ring and into the public on a few occasions. As it stands, both men will face each other at the biggest show of the year and on the line is more than just an addition to their win loss record. Terror is led by Steven Caldera and this win is said to be the one that proves Terror's worth to not only Caldera but GCW as a leader. For O'Neil, it's the sweet victory he's been looking for since his earlier days in the company. Past that however, it seems that this has escalated to a completely personal level in itself. Both men are fighting for their lives with passion as their true weapon. O'Neil, who will likely post bail, will be at NC-17, we've heard, and will be ready for possibly one of the bloodiest matches in GCW history. Terror, as many remember, was involved in a brutal ACWF match at Dangerous Games 2008 against Jordan White and he showed that he can fight through pain. Although he lost, he showed heart that is rarely matched. However, critics can't help but vote against Terror due to his abysmal record at NC-17. In 2007, Terror was bested by LeStatt Knight in the match of the year and in 2008 Terror was defeated by the Scottish King of Cool, Andy Murray. Could this be Terror's turning point in his career? Still, O'Neil has fought his wars against the likes of Badger Benandanti, Steven Caldera, and most recently Andy Murray. Although he has been absent from television, O'Neil has a mindset where winning is the only option and a loss to Terror will inevitably end things as he knows it. Whoever comes out with the victory, it's certain that there will be blood shed and it will get graphic. It's the nature of these men and they wouldn't have it any other way.
