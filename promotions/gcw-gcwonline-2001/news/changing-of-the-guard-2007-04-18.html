Changing of the Guard

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At High Stakes in Las Vegas this week, Global Championship Wrestling underwent a significant makeover. Three titles changed hands, and an old power rose again to threaten the very foundation of the company. While President Steven Caldera's resurrection of his famed Establishment stable sent shockwaves through the roster, perhaps no development was as significant as the changing of the guard at the very top. Bryan Mayhem, who for five years waited for a chance to climb to the top of the world, finally got his shot at High Stakes. Though Mayhem had a massive size advantage over the undersized Lia Ambrosi, many felt that Ambrosi's pure skill and tenacity would be enough to retain the title. Though she gave him an extremely competitive match, Mayhem's size won out. Ambrosi fell via pinfall, and the GCW World Title changed hands. The title now rests around the massive waist of the biggest man in GCW history, leaving only one question; can Mayhem be beaten? In other action, Twizzy made his return to the GCW ring after a three year layoff. He quickly shook off the rust, however, giving Jordan White quite a scare as he came close to winning the United States title. In the end, however, it was the involvement of President Caldera and returning legend LeStatt Knight that turned the tide. In a complete shocker, President Caldera took charge of his company once more, reforming The Establishment with Knight and White and costing Twizzy his shot at the belt. What Caldera's vendetta against Twizzy is remains to be seen, but it seems clear that Twizzy's bid for the Hall of Fame may be in jeopardy. Also, Banned and Exiled~! rose to the top of the tag team ranks, defeating Fiery Passion for the tag belts. B&E continues to take GCW by storm, and now seeks to make an even bigger impact by competing as singles in the upcoming Warpath tournament. Jimmy Thunder defeated Kyo Kushinaga in a brutal steel cage match, but following the bout experienced a rather odd event at ringside. The name Hydra appeared on the MegaTron, and Thunder now finds himself facing this enigmatic superstar in the first round of the Warpath tournament. In addition, Katie O'Clare captured the Vanguard Title, though she has attempted to return the title to GCW officials. In the night's opening bout, Draven Lox returned to the ring and defeated Kyle James in a Hardcore bout. And in an extremely personal match, Jayce Bradley won via disqualification, but suffered a tremendous beating at the hands of the owner of the venue, Shaman. GCW now turns to the Warpath tournament, which kicks off this week on WorldWide with four first round matches. Warpath is just five weeks away, and with the power shifting in GCW, the next month should prove to define Global Championship Wrestling for years to come.
