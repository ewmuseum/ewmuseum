GCW — Column

Unique Critique #5

5 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Lia Ambrosi- Sadly we haven’t seen much new stuff from the World Champion. In the course of losing theWorld title and gaining it back, it almost flew like a loop. There wasn’t any real growth and we find ourselves back where we started before Mayhem entered the picture. Would have been nice to see a new roleplay since then as well. Phil Atken- Not much to go with here, one segment and one roleplay. Both were funny, and both did a solid job of selling Atken. He is luckily to have entered GCW when he did as he has countless options opened up to him to explore. He can either go gunning for the Television title with Shawn Porter, Tyson DeBough, and Jay Terror or he can feud against another few comer. Chris Cross- I was disappointed with a lack of segment presence. It is usually not a good sign when a new talent doesn’t submit a segment for his debut. But at least he roleplayed and it looks like it could be the start of a series, so hopefully we see more from Cross. The fact that he also posted a news story helps get him 2 stars. Tyson DeBough- Tyson nearly already got a four star rating with the great work he’s been doing. What kept him from getting the other half a star was a lack of a roleplay. This is a roleplay fed and roleplays are the best way to build characters. You’ve sold me on a great card presence and story, but now I need a reason to care more about Tyson as a person. Hydra- A disappearing act keeps Hydra down but it looks like he may return. Hopefully he will return in full force as he is someone who shows he could have talent. A good feud partner match would be Tabloid. One masked wrestler trying to uncover another. Brad Jackson- Easily the strongest newcomer from the newest bunch, he joins the ranks of Jay Terror and Jordan White as people who made a quick impact. What he lacks though is more of a defining care presence. He could be a serious contender for Dangerous Games if he can just sell himself to the larger GCW audience. LeStatt Knight- Being sporadic hurts Knight’s score but the quality of his work when he does appear helps keep him about two stars. He’s managed to keep himself floating about the mid card talent but with his feud against Twizzy seemingly dwindling down, he needs to find something new and quick. Bryan Mayhem- What keeps him from being the first five star here is that Mayhem has a problem of either being a great addition to the show or completely forgettable on the show. He doesn’t seem to be selling the feud against Vivica as well as he could, but the feud is still one of the best in GCW at a moment. If he could get a fire lit under him, he could not only be a strong five star wrestler, but a multi-time World Champion as well. Paddy O'Shea- Real life problems keep Paddy down. When he’s here, he’s great. But it’s a matter of real life allowing him to be here. Hopefully things fix themselves and we see a great addition return to the Television title division. Shawn Porter- The gimmick change felt it came too sudden and two quickly, but it could help Porter get over his hump. The strong field of newcomers he’s entered with has thinned some, but the new wave seems to keep Porter from really advancing. He needs a solid feud and to keep building. Jesse Ramey- He fell off the mat. He would have gotten less, but he did have a few recent appearances and have been used to help the Television title picture. Jose Rodriguez- Jose has yet to debut, hence the low score. But at least his bio looks nice. That’s worth half a star right there. Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins- Ok so Rich isn’t being handled by Andrew that hurts the score as it takes away any expectations for the character. If the handler had been there then it would have added a new dynamic for the character and made things a bit more unpredictable. Rikki Roxx- Completely forgotten about. Banned & Exiled~!- Banned & Exiled~! continue to build and build through the shows and have quickly became the top of an imaginary tag division. A lack of a real threat keeps them from getting five stars. Shaman- Shaman is like Mayhem in that he’s either there and a nice addition to the card or he’s completely forgettable by the end of the card. Hopefully a returning Kyle James can change that and give Shaman a memorable feud. 'The Outlaw' Jay Terror- What was once the best of the newcomers is now the best of the midcard. He’s done an amazing job with the Television title, so much so that you hardly want to see him ever drop the belt. But after breaking the longest title reign record, it may be time for him to shoot for bigger fish. One has to wonder if he’ll give a repeated performance if given the US title. Jimmy Thunder- Jimmy came out of the Australian tour looking the best and needs to keep the momentum going. He has plenty of options open to him, he just needs to explore them and really develop a card presence that makes him prime to be a main eventer. Tabloid- Could have been a higher score if he only showed a card presence. He seems to be getting by just on the gimmick in the form of roleplays but needs to learn how to translate that gimmick into the shows. Twizzy- Twizzy lost a lot of steam coming out of Tsunami and needs to build it back up. Hopefully he can pick it back up and either respark the lost interest in his feud with LeStatt Knight or find a new target to go after. The latter looks to be a much harder road. Vivica J. Valentine- It seems Vivica has yet to lose a step, continuing to build toward a cake walk win at Dangerous Games. Right now Vivica is he person to chase, despite not even having a championship belt. It won’t be long until she’s in the World title picture as the front runner. Jordan White- Jordan is having a slow build toward being the top heel in GCW. His program over Rich Rollins establishes him as a serious World title contender but he can still build more. Right now he looks like he’s set to be the next World champion.
