Ranking The Champions

9 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Vivica J. Valentine pinned Jordan White at Warpath last week, the world was stunned. Elated, overjoyed, but stunned. Going in, Jay Terror was the hands-on favorite to walk out the champion. Nearly all the wrestling pundits had Valentine's reign ending. And why not? The odds were certainly against her. She was up against Terror, The Establishment's hand-picked champion, and Jordan White, who many consider the most talented wrestler in the world. Making matters worse, she didn't need to be pinned to lose the belt. Yet Valentine stepped in and denied Terror his destiny. Terror has been silent since the match, and Jordan White is said to be highly disappointed. It's that sort of reaction that denotes a great champion. The challenger walks in confident in his chances, and walks out devastated courtesy of the retaining champion. The storyline going into the match was that Valentine needed a defining moment as champion to earn a spot next to the greatest of all time. It's a debate that has raged on for nearly a decade in GCW: Who is the greatest World Champion in history? It's the highest level a wrestler can attain in this company. There are champions, then there are great champions, and then there is the elite few, the greatest of all time. There can be no denying that Valentine has now joined that elite group. But is she the best of the best? Does her successful defense at Warpath launch her above the greats like Ambrosi, Octavian and Knight? It's been a hot topic in GCW this week, and it's a debate that will no doubt continue for months. It is clear, however, that Valentine has now firmly entrenched herself in the conversation. For years the consensus has been that LeStatt Knight was the greatest champion of all time. While it is true that Octavian holds the record for most title reigns (3) and Ambrosi broke Knight's record for longest reign (which has since been broken by Valentine), Knight blazed a trail that was unprecedented in his time. In 2005 the sort of dominance Knight displayed had never been seen before in this company. Knight defeated Dangerous Games winner Michael Stevens twice, and took on every challenger the company had to offer. Nobody could pry the belt away from him. In terms of quality of opponents and sheer number of successful defenses, Knight remains tops. Ambrosi dominated the World Title picture for all of 2007. She won the title from Knight at NC-17 in 2006 and held it until NC-17 2007, the only break in her reign coming during a two-month period when Bryan Mayhem was terrorizing GCW. Octavian holds one of the most hallowed records in GCW, one that is not likely to be broken any time soon. As GCW's only three-time World Champion, Octavian has long been regarded as GCW's original "great" champion. Octavian was the first wrestler to successfully defend his title at a Pay-Per-View event, at a time when competition was so fierce that champions did not last long. However, he has not found the same level of success in the latter part of the decade, a decline that has some questioning the long-time Establishment member's credentials. Valentine has broken into this elite group with one of the most astonishing title reigns of all time. She surpassed Ambrosi's record for longest reign weeks ago, and each day the record grows. She successfully fended off Shaman at Rampage and then survived Warpath against White and Terror. Oh, and that's not to mention who she defeated to win the title in the first place: Lia Ambrosi. Valentine has proven herself as one of GCW's all-time great big-match wrestlers, thriving under the spotlight that comes with the GCW World Title. Whatever is to come for Valentine as her title reign continues, this much is certain: Vivica J. Valentine is now marked for the Hall of Fame. GCW is a company that prides itself on its rich history, a history built on the shoulders of the legendary talent that has competed between its ropes. Enjoy watching Vivica J. Valentine wrestle, fans. One day you'll look back and thank the wrestling gods that you had a chance to see this legend in action.
