World Wide Takes Place!

30 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The trip to High Stakes is about to come to the end.... Here we have the final show before High Stakes in GCW's own Shaman's casino. Tension will be high, will Shaman beat Lia Amrbosi taking her down a notch before she faces Bryan Mayhem for the World title next week? The Vanguard is on the defense in a three way with Peter Vetra being pit up against Jayce Bradley and Vivica J Valentine... Plus much much more... be sure to catch the transcript on the latest World Wide Link. Along with the previous two matches that were mentioned we have the opener of Kyo Kushingana vs. Draven Lox, followed by the Television Title match up for Kyle James vs champion Jimmy Thunder. And right before our Vanguard championship we have newly (as of a month or so ago) acquired tag team Banned & Exiled ~! facing off against Katie O'Clare and Dawn McFarlene will their common hatred for B&E be enough to unite them for a victory... Come and find out... only at WorldWide 64 from Philadelphia!
