Caldera Comments on Lawsuit

25 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW President Steven Caldera, currently the target of a lawsuit by Banned and Exiled~!, released a comment today directed towards the embattled stars. "There will be no settlement," said Caldera, who holds a degree in Law from Harvard. "The entire GCW legal team is going to band together to make an example of Stone and Storm." "GCW superstars are held to the highest of standards," Caldera's statement continued. "Markus Stone and Chris Storm seem to think that their suspension relieved them of their contractual obligations. As long as they are GCW superstars, and they will be for a long time, they will be required to behave according to GCW mandate." The statement was brief, but it seems clear that GCW and President Caldera will not be capitulating. Be sure to tune in to WorldWide this week, as this situation is nearing a critical state.
