Terror Lands, Is He Prepared?

12 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been confirmed that Jay Terror has finally landed in Tokyo and is ready to fight. It's been confirmed that Jay Terror has finally landed in Tokyo and is ready to fight. Reports circulated last week that The Established one returned to his Philadelphia home in lieu of his loss to Chris Bagwell. During his time, many have overheard that he met with Cary Kolat, former Olympic wrestler, for training sessions. What could this mean for his double jeopardy cage match? Perhaps we'll see a more advanced style from the freestyle fighter and a new strategy that could send him to the top of the rankings. Then again, many wonder if the travelling will be too much before such a large match. When asked for an interview in Tokyo, Terror declined stating, "Just got off my plane. Don't need to be doing this." Will he be ready to take on Tessa Windsor, Clyde Fox, and Chris Storm? Only time will tell. For now, we sit and wonder whether he's become the technical wrestler that he needs to be in order to win.
