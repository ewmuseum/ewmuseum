Dangerous Games: Major Storylines

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Dangerous Games now just days away, Kevin Jacobs and the GCWonline.net staff takes a look at some of the major storylines in this week's Dangerous Games match. Each year, the Dangerous Games match offers one wrestler the opportunity of a lifetime: the chance to headline the biggest show of the year. But to many of GCW's wrestlers, this year's Dangerous Games match holds even greater significance. Here are some of the major storylines to follow during the show: Vivica J. Valentine: Can She Repeat? The 2007 Dangerous Games winner is considered by many to be the favorite to win in 2008. A Dangerous Games repeat would be unprecedented; not only has no wrestler ever won two Dangerous Games matches, nobody has even come close. Twizzy, Michael Stevens, Lia Ambrosi; none of them were in contention the following year. But Valentine's riding high and desperately wants her title back. The biggest obstacle? If someone can eliminate Valentine it will be an accomplishment in and of itself, so Valentine will enter the match with a huge bullseye on her back. LeStatt Knight: Can He Add to His Legacy? LeStatt Knight has done it all in GCW. He's a two-time World Champion. He's beaten nearly everyone he's ever faced. He's in the Hall of Fame. But he's never won Dangerous Games. Last year's second place finish to Valentine left a bad taste in Knight's mouth, and he's hellbent on victory. A win would get Knight the World Title shot he's been thirsting for, but he's got the same problem as Valentine. Knight's a threat to win, which means everyone will be targeting him. Andy Murray: Is His Time Now? Andy Murray debuted at NC-17 2007. If he wins Dangerous Games, he'll be headlining NC-17 2008. That's quite a first year for the coolest man in GCW. Murray is coming off an emotional and controversial win over Knight that some think has affected Murray more than he cares to reveal. But Murray is focused on winning this year's Dangerous Games match. Murray has won every big match he's ever been in; will that streak continue and land him the title shot most think he's destined to receive? Garbage Bag Johnny: How Far Does the Streak Go? Garbage Bag Johnny, the American Dream holder, extended his unbeaten streak by pinning the number one contender Jordan White at WorldWide 100. Johnny's got to get by Tyson DeBough, where his American Dream will be on the line. But if he can survive the Establishment's hitman, will his streak extend to Dangerous Games? If he doesn't win the unbeaten streak will be intact, but a victory would certainly add to his rapidly growing legend. Chris Bagwell: Put Up or Shut Up? There's no denying that Chris Bagwell is great. But is he as great as he's been claiming? He debuted at the same time as Andy Murray and has been in second place ever since. For the boastful Bagwell, there's a lot riding on his performance in this match. A victory would overturn a year of near-misses; seeing Murray walk out the winner would only drive Bagwell further into his shadow. Digital Mortality: Out of the Shadows? Digital Mortality has been one of the quietest, most dominant United States Champions in recent memory. Digi's mystique keeps him under the radar, but the field would be wise to pay attention. Digital Mortality must defend his title in a triple threat match earlier in the night, but he's certainly looking to take a huge leap out of the shadows and into the spotlight. Tessa Windsor: Ready for Showtime? Tessa Windsor is focused on Vivica J. Valentine. She wants to defeat her former friend and mentor and finally emerge from Valentine's considerable shadow. Windsor has always been immensely talented but feels her career has suffered because of Valentine's legendary status. But Windsor could completely trump Valentine by winning Dangerous Games and thrusting herself into the NC-17 title picture. The Field: The Year of the Unknown? Each year the Dangerous Games winner has emerged from a group of favorites. Could this be the year a sleeper rises up and steals the match? Most within the company believe this is the most talented Dangerous Games field in the history of the event. With such big targets on the backs of the top stars, this could be the year someone sneaks through and shocks the world.
