Blockbuster Match Announced

9 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, Ohio- As Global Championship Wrestling returns home after a long voyage across the Pacific, news has broken today confirming a match that has been rumored for weeks: the PTC Unified Title is coming to GCW. With the Unified Title, a title spanning all three of the major promotions, now on tour between GCW, PRIME and SCCW, GCW now has its opportunity to host the next defense and send a GCW superstar to attempt to claim it. And the match on tap could be one of the biggest in GCW and PTC history. Lindsay Troy successfully defended the Unified Title against Jonathan Rhine of SCCW last week. Troy, who is also PRIME's Universal Champion, now brings the Unified Title belt and puts it on the line against GCW's biggest star: GCW World Champion Vivica J. Valentine. The match will headline WorldWide #88, which will emanate from Portland, Oregon. There can be little doubt that this appears to be a match of epic proportions; for the first time ever, the top champions of GCW and PRIME will meet one on one in a GCW ring, with the biggest title in PTC on the line. Troy, well known to GCW fans for her long-standing rivalry with GCW President Steven Caldera, has risen to superstardom in professional wrestling after capturing two of the biggest titles in existence simultaneously. Troy defeated fellow PRIME wrestler Tchu to capture the Unified Title, then defeated Rhine on SCCW programming last week to set up this match. Valentine, who defeated Shaman at Rampage this week, comes into the contest on an incredible hot streak. As GCW Champion and arguably the company's biggest star, Valentine now faces an even larger spotlight. The eyes of all PTC will fall upon the GCW ring at WorldWide in two weeks as the top stars in professional wrestling collide. Lindsay Troy, though popular among GCW fans for her constant harassment of Caldera, stands to walk into a highly hostile environment at WorldWide. Valentine secured her place among the top stars in GCW history with her defeat of Shaman at Rampage, and no wrestler is more beloved within the company. Troy and Valentine have had occasion to interact in the past, and their relationship is far from sunny. Still, the two are said to have a tremendous amount of respect for one another, a bond perhaps forged from their mutual hatred of the company's President. For his part, President Caldera is said to be extremely excited to be bringing the match and the title to GCW. GCW last hosted a Unified Title Match back at WorldWide #83, where Tchu defeated Clinton Sage to capture the title. The match was a tremendous success for GCW, especially considering the hatred GCW fans bear for Clinton Sage. Though Caldera is a noted rival of both Valentine and Troy, there's little doubt where Caldera's loyalties lie. "GCW is extremely pleased to be given the opportunity to host the PTC Unified Title once again," Caldera said in a statement this morning as he returned to Columbus from Tokyo. "And while Valentine is not long for the GCW World Title, everyone in the company, including myself, will be rooting for her to bring the Unified Title back to GCW where it belongs." No GCW wrestler has ever held the Unified Title, but GCW has laid claim to the title's precursor, the Global Title. GCW wrestlers Lia Ambrosi, Chris Storm, Seymour Almasy and Kimbusa have all held the title at one point or another. But now all PTC gold has been consolidated, and GCW will be pulling for Valentine to become the first GCW wrestler to win the belt. Don't miss WorldWide 88 two weeks from today, April 23rd. The event will be headlined by Lindsay Troy vs. Vivica J. Valentine for the PTC Unified Title in a battle of the GCW and PRIME Champions! And be sure to follow GCW and PRIME programming over the next two weeks, and rumors have begun to swirl that Valentine and Troy are prepared to take the battle to each others' doorsteps.
