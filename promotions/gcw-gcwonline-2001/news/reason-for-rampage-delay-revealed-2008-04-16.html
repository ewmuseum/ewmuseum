Reason for Rampage Delay Revealed

16 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After an intense investigation, the reasons behind the delay of Rampage have finally been made public. Problems from the production truck caused the broadcast to experience irregularities while trying to send it overseas to the United States from Japan. In the show before Rampage, the production truck GCW generally uses for broadcast and transmissions was unexpectedly damaged by wrestler Thom LeStrange in one of his encounters with fellow wrestler Andy Murray. The event, which took place on the 86th edition of WorldWide, saw Thom LeStrange send the production truck, with Andy Murray inside, crashing into a wall. Much of the equipment inside was damaged and some of the personal inside were taken to a nearby hospital for minor injuries. No word yet on if any punishment is expected against LeStrange, but when reached for comment Murray was more than willing to share words: “I’d like to apologize to all the GCW fans across the globe on behalf of that wretched little run Thom LeStrange for what has happened here. It seems that the only skill Thommy’s ever developed is the fine art of being first class chowder-head, and if it weren’t for him sticking his oar in again the show would surely be on the air by now. I hope Stevie C. gives the disease ridden abortion-survivor his comeuppance before I have to, otherwise I may do something that I might later regret!” Because of the incident, a replacement production truck had to be found with short notice, including a number of new workers to sit in for any still on injury leave. This is believe to be the cause of the problem, as many of the workers said they were unfamiliar with the equipment being used. In his defense Thom LeStrange defended himself and issued an apology to the fans of GCW, “I take issue with the belief that the troubles regarding the broadcasting of the Rampage pay-per-view were entirely attributable to myself on a number of counts. Firstly, I find it hard to believe that a company as large, well-funded and well-equipped as GCW should ground to a halt with the damaging of one measly production truck. Secondly, that my actions were in no way "excessive and unnecessary", and in fact were whole-heartedly justified measures of revenge against a cantankerous and aggressive thug. And thirdly, I would even argue that if anything, my actions brought a greater degree of publicity to the Rampage event." When reached for comment, GCW Owner Steven Caldera simply stated “no comment” and said the situation is still under review at the moment. At the moment punishment does not seem immediate as Thom LeStrange is scheduled to face Digital Mortality on the next edition of WorldWide. Andy Murray is not scheduled to wrestle, but is listed as an appearance for the show.
