Chris Cross & Geraldo Rivera

14 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(AP) Chicago, IL. “Today, professional wrestler, and self-proclaimed "savior of professional wrestling"; Chris Cross, gave Geraldo Riviera exclusive access to his professional establishment in downtown Chicago. There is still no answers to just what it will be, but Chris gives the world a bit of insight in this interview; The sounds of a busy night in Chicago are present below, as the scene before me began to unfold quickly. Directly in front me was an elevator, a grand elevator, of gold and red velvet, and odd design for an elevator I thought. As I stepped into the elevator I saw a man sitting on a wooden stool, upon a cushion of red velvet, a smile upon his face. "Top Flood; Executive Offices" the man says, as he motioned me with an open hand toward the door, which slides open with ease. Before me was what seems to be emptiness. Bland, gray walls, atoned with earth colors surround you, nothing affixed to the walls except what appears to be a spray painted "x", as to mark something? "Ah, come in, he’s expecting you" I heard a softened voice say as I turned around, spotting an older gentleman, adorned in a tuxedo, the coattails following behind him, and white gloves upon his hands, which are extended to his right, motioning to the opened door. I proceeded into the room, which contrast greatly to what preceded it. Endless color flows through the room, the walls painted a dark orange, framed art sits upon the walls, shelves hold memorabilia of all kinds, and a stereo sits on the shelf, humming as music flows from the speakers, a CD case sits propped up against the speaker of the small machine. The cover of the CD case reads "Reel Big Fish". As I gazed upon the scene in front of me, the lights suddenly dim to darkness. The music I could once hear was now silence, quickly changing to a low, pulsing beat. As the beat grows louder and louder, I began to clench, expecting the worse, suddenly I heard it; "Jump...Jump!"?it begins to grow louder "Chris Cross will make you’Jump?.Jump!" At the low, wooden desk directly in front of me, I flinched as something begins to take motion’a chair begins to turn. Before me was a smiling man, his brightly white colored teeth almost glow in the darkness, a smile fixed on his face. "Ah," he begins, crossing his feet over his ankles as he places them on the desk before him. "Not everyone can put a show on like me, then again, not one has a song for themselves. You know, many people ask me why I don’t come to the ring with that song, I tell them’you know, I’m getting ahead of myself. First let me get done with the introductions; I’m Chris Cross, and yes; I will make you jump, jump." The man laughs at his own joke as the lights in the office slowly rise again. "Helps with the atmosphere, don’t you think?" the man asks as he notices the lights. The older gentleman that I first noticed outside of the office approaches the man behind the desk, a small tray in his hand. "Anything sir?" the older man asks. "Anything for you?" the man behind the desks asked me pleasantly. I declined, still extremely confused. "No thank you" the man behind the desks says quickly, turning back toward me. "I trust you are confused, and a bit upset that I have taken you all this way from your normal activities just sit and talk, you must want to get on your way, and I do not want to stand between you and your schedule, so I will make this as quick as humanly possible. These are dark times in the history of wrestling, our business is slowly disintegrating. Full of young children, only mere teenagers, some not even old enough to drink alcohol, yet these men think they can win a world title! Today is the first step in changing the face of professional wrestling forever! I am Chris Cross; former World Champion, which would explain the back wall quite nicely, don’t you think?" The man that referred to himself as "Chris Cross" says, pointing to a wall I had looked over as I first entered the room; a wall covered in championship titles behind glass. "Former," Chris begins again "World Champion, currently seeking employment in the wrestling business once again. In a single year, I have captured more titles than many superstars can claim years of life! If you were to count every championship belt in that cabinet, it would reach uppers of 25; 27 to be exact. Slowly but surly, everywhere I have gone has seen the end; the One World Coalition; Golden Championship Wrestling, in the words of Nine Inch Nails; “they all go away’in the end”. The establishment of Professional Wrestling has done nothing to help itself either! Scouts are offering more and more money, larger contracts each day, for a wrestler!" Chris stops, and reaches into an open desk draw to his right, lifting out a pack of cigarettes and a lighter, slipping a cigarette into his mouth and lighting, inhaling slowly as he turns back toward the camera. "It’s as though Professional Wrestling is trying to destroy itself; not being proactive about such situations are as helpful as lying down and painting an “X” on your chest! The problem goes deeper than wrestling as a society, it reaches to the core of wrestling; the promotions themselves. Promoters hand top-grade titles to their wrestlers in hopes of silencing their voices; keeping down the nucleus of the business!" Slowly Cross stops and exhales the smoke through his nostrils, turning his head toward the camera, raising his left eyebrow. "So you’re curious as to why I brought you all the way to Chicago, and all I’m doing is preaching wrestling ethics, which are probably boring you now. But the reason you’re here is not to hear my endless babbling as you might consider it," Cross stops to laugh at his own joke, once again, "the reason you are here today, is to pay witness to an announcement that will SHAKE the very foundations which professional wrestling stands upon at this moment’and that announcement will take place in no more than three minutes." Chris Cross suddenly pauses and turns away from the camera, turning his chair, his back now to the camera, as his head stretches upward to look up at the clock affixed to his wall. "Twelve O’clock" he says, looking at the digital clock that reads 11:58am. Just as the Florissant red numbers change to; "12:00", and the clock springs to life announcing; "the time is now twelve o’clock in the afternoon; July of 2007", Chris turns his chair back around and places his left hand over the telephone receiver that rests on his desk, his right hand resting on top of his desk. "Just seconds now’ah, here it is" Chris says as he answer the ringing phone, as though he expected it, we can only hear parts of his conversation; "make it good news’awesome, who took us up’yeah, I’ve heard of them, they’re big, but not HUGE at this moment, the perfect stage’yeah, just finishing up’alright thanks, later." We hear his broken conversation end with a "click" on the other line, as Chris hangs up the phone. "Ahem, anyway, where was I? Oh yeah, the announcement that will shake the Earth or whatever, you know I really do better when this crap isn’t scripted for me" Chris says laughing. Chris stands and pushes his chair away from himself, reaching into his drawer and taking out another cigarette, this time not making a big production of it, just smoking it and moving on. "You see, I’m not always this big shot with the nice car and the nice suit, sometimes I’m just me, you know? But what I say behind that desk, what I do in the ring, it reflects who I am, and I don’t like what’s going on lately, that’s what I came up with this whole deal, that’s why I wrote out this speech on note cards the night before. Now I’m all shaken up, for a moment there, it looked like it was back to the little leagues, but it worked out!" Cross is visibly shaken but begins to breath deeply for a moment, and crushes his cigarette, which he has smoked almost to the filter, in his ashtray and takes a seat in his chair again, interlacing his hands and smiling at you from across his desk; he’s ready. "Today, you will bare witness to an announcement that will shake the very core of wrestling as we know it, that will change professional wrestling forever; Chris Cross is returning to primetime television, and to professional wrestling on an international scale; Global Championship Wrestling! That’s right; a new era in wrestling will truly be born from the blood, sweat, and tears of Chris Cross in the Global Championship Wrestling! No one can silence me now as I go around the world, destroying the false images of professional wrestling as it’s known today, and destroying those professional wrestlers that help build that image, by embodying that image, by promoting that image! It will be a dark day for those that have fallen off the radar of professional wrestling, that have accepted bribes in the form of championships to shut your mouths, a dark day for those that oppose Chris Cross!" Chris smiles and looks up toward the camera, his pupils growing large; "and when professional wrestling comes to shut me up, when they come for me, I’ll be sitting at my desk, with a gun in my hand, wearing a bulletproof vest, and I promise there won’t only be one bullet in that chamber, and I won’t be wasting a single shot on myself. If the truth in professional wrestling is going to die; then Chris Cross is going with it! Superstars can make jokes; no I do not wear my wrestling attire backwards, no I do not rap, but if it were not for me, professional wrestling as these gentlemen know it would be different, a lesser product than it is now!" Chris Cross stands, and motioned for me to follow him as he heads toward the door of his office, opening it, and pointing toward the outside; the unfinished waiting area. "What you see before you is the future, what you just witnessed was the present, and the future! Chris Cross is here, Chris Cross has arrived, but Chris Cross is not going anywhere! Chris Cross will be here long after everyone else has gone, to ride off into the sunset’even if it’s by himself! Before you is what will soon become “Cross, Inc.” an ingenious tool I have created to inform the world of the issues that face professional wrestling on so many medias! We have Chris Cross DVDs, Chris Cross t-shirts, Chris Cross hats, sweat pants, you name it, “Cross, Inc.” has put my picture or logo on, and now it’s out there to help acknowledge to the world that there is a problem in wrestling, and they must help support the solution’by wearing my merchandise!" Cross pauses and opens his suit jacket, he’s wearing a black t-shirt, a white image of Cross? face, "Chris" above the image, "Cross" below the image. "Only fifteen dollars!" Chris exclaims happily as he smiles and points toward his t-shirt. He smiles broadly for a moment more before heading back into his office and closing the door behind me as I settled down in the chair across from him. "I thank you for joining me today, and please feel free to place orders at Chris Cross dot com, to help the cause!" Cross said as he turned his chair and pressed a small red button on a small control panel in front of him "security" he said, "please see Mr. Riviera out." -END
