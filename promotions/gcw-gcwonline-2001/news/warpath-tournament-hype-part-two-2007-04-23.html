Warpath Tournament Hype Part TWO

23 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The second set of interviews! Hello GCW! Kevin Jacobs back again with the second set of interviews regarding the Warpath Tournament along with a few other recent topics. I won't withhold this information any longer! KYLE JAMES KEVIN JACOBS: How do you feel about your chances of winning your first round match? KYLE JAMES: Honestly I feel good about winning my first round match up. Sure he's got all the ackolades in the world under his belt but that doesn't mean anything in the ring. I don't care who he is or what he's done in the past. When he gets in the ring with me he'll have to bring everything he's got and then some to stop me from beating his ass on every single inch of that ring KEVIN JACOBS: How much do you know about LK? KYLE JAMES: I don't know much about him besides the fact he is a former world champion. He is just coming out of retirement and he got the un-friendly task of having to face me in the first round KEVIN JACOBS: As you probably know, most in GCW feel you are a MAJOR underdog in this matchup. What will your strategy be to beat a man of this caliber? KYLE JAMES: Honestly let them think what they want. I'll do what I normally do in the ring and I will take him out on my terms. Let the guys in the back say and think what they want. They all know they don't have the balls or the talent to take him on in the ring. KEVIN JACOBS: Very well, if not YOU then who do you feel has the best shot at taking the win at Warpath? KYLE JAMES: It will be me... no questions asked. If by some miracle of god that it isn't me then I'm willing to put money on Shaman KEVIN JACOBS: Tell the people one reason why you are going to win the GCW Warpath Tournament. KYLE JAMES: For the shear fact that no one and I mean no one in the GCW today is better then I am. I don't give a damn who you are, you won't stand in my way. I will win this tournament and I will get my shot at the GCW world title. It's as simple as that. KEVIN JACOBS: Well Mr. James thank you for your time and good luck - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - VIVICA VALENTINE KEVIN JACOBS: Tell the people how you like your chances of winning your first round match up. VIVICA VALENTINE: I feel my chances are great Kevin. Everything I've worked for in my GCW career has led to this. I'll be damned if I let myself get knocked out in Round 1. KEVIN JACOBS: Your first round opponent is Markus Stone, what do you feel about him personally? VIVICA VALENTINE: About him personally? I can't say I've really paid much attention to his and Chris Storm's antics. That's just what they are... little kids trying to get attention. I have more important things to do than to be assed to pay attention to a man with the mentality of a kindergardener. All I know about him is that his name is Markus Stone, he acts like a child, and he's losing to me in Round 1 of the Warpath Tournament. KEVIN JACOBS: If you had to choose one person in the entire tournament that you consider the greatest threat whom would it be? VIVICA VALENTINE: It has to be Lestatt Knight. A future GCW Hall Of Famer and he has complete and partial backing from the owner of the company. What more could you ask for? I'm surprised Caldera hasn't just put him in the finals yet. KEVIN JACOBS: According to many in the backstage area Caldera has basically given him a free ride to the Final Four at least. Nobody gives Kyle James a shot at beating Knight, do you share that opinion? VIVICA VALENTINE: As much as I'd love to see someone like Kyle James knock the cocky-ass attitude off of Lestatt Knight... this isn't his nor Caldera's first rodeo. He'll get to the second round, no matter what. The only advice I have for Kyle is to keep his eyes open. Plus... I want to be the one who knocks his ass out of the tournament. It's something I've been wanting to do for a long time. KEVIN JACOBS: The two of you have tangled for the World Title once before, very entertaining match-up I must say. You have been around GCW quite sometime, even shattering one of Knight's great records (The US Title reign). If you could give me one reason why you will win this tournament, what would it be? VIVICA VALENTINE: I want it the most. GCW has thrown me down over and over again. I keep getting back up and brushing myself off. NOTHING can keep me down. Not this time. Ever since I was a little girl this is all I've ever wanted to do. I made a mistake last year at Rampage. It won't happen again. I won't be denied of my dreams. KEVIN JACOBS: I will say that you have fought, tooth and nail to get where you are today in GCW. It is stars like you that GCW should be leaning on for years to come. Vivica it was a pleasure and thank you for your time. I now give the floor to you to give any last words to your fans. VIVICA VALENTINE: Thank you for the support you guys have given me through all the ups and downs. At Warpath, it'll all pay off. I'll take my shot from Caldera's cold and crippled hands, and then I'll take the GCW World Championship. I guarantee it. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - LESTATT KNIGHT KEVIN JACOBS: Not that I really need to ask you this, but how do you feel about your chances of advancing to round two? LESTATT KNIGHT: Ha are you serious? Kevin how long have you known me? Six years now? When have I ever NOT been confident? I AM moving on ? simple as that. KEVIN JACOBS: Kyle James has been heard claiming that he WILL defeat you, any responses? LESTATT KNIGHT: Look, that asshole can say anything he wants to but fact is he has no idea who I am. I haven’t been around in awhile but my finger prints are all over the GCW, hell I AM GCW. This is all I need to know about what’s his name “and no I won’t use his name ? He has made NO impact on GCW what so ever. What did he even do at High Stakes” Oh yeah he got beat by Draven Lox. Draven Lox? What a joke. This is going to be like taking candy from a baby. KEVIN JACOBS: Is there anyone in this tournament that you feel will give you a lot of trouble if you have to meet them? LESTATT KNIGHT: Like who Kevin? Not my first round opponent that’s for damn sure. Maybe Vivica Valentine is who you are referring to? The one I wiped the mat with when we battled for the World Title? Shaman? Jay Terror? TWIZZY for god’s sake? Please. The only two I can honestly say may do anything are Jayce Bradley and of course Jordan. I actually have never met Bradley in the ring so I don’t know anything about him ? but he’ll still fall like the rest. KEVIN JACOBS: What if you and Jordan do meet in the finale? LESTATT KNIGHT: Then so be it. Look I don’t even really know Jordan White so I have no problems stepping into the ring with him. No matter if you are brothers, sisters, cousins, father and son or just fucking each other IF you are in this game you are in it for one reason. The World Title. I’m a competitor, it’s why I have done this for so long. If Jordan and I meet in the finals then I will treat him like I’m going to treat everyone else. KEVIN JACOBS: Knight now here is the million dollar question, Why the Establishment? LESTATT KNIGHT: Next question. KEVIN JACOBS: What? LESTATT KNIGHT: Next question! You will get your answers soon enough. KEVIN JACOBS: Well I am going to open up the floor to you, any words you’d like to give to Kyle James or GCW in general? LESTATT KNIGHT: Prepare yourselves ? This isn’t going to be pretty. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - STEVEN CALDERA KEVIN JACOBS: Mr. Caldera, with Warpath coming up very soon how confident are you that someone in The Establishment will take home the victory? STEVEN CALDERA: Why should there be any doubt in my mind? There shouldn't be any doubt in yours. The Establishment stands for one thing; control. Absolute control. But you know what? Even if The Establishment weren't a part of the equation, the two greatest wrestlers in the world today are LeStatt Knight and Jordan White. I think the surprise in this tournament won't be that The Establishment wins, but rather that The Establishment wins based on pure, unadulterated talent. KEVIN JACOBS: Most of the GCW is still in shock that LeStatt Knight would join forces with you. He has always claimed he would do things on his own, can you shed any light on why he chose this path? STEVEN CALDERA: I won't speak for him, he's earned the right to speak for himself. But personally, I'd say he did it because he shares my vision. My vision for a group that isn't just bent on control through manipulation. It's a vision for the evolution of The Establishment; a group now bent on more than just manipulation, but total physical domination. Nobody exemplifies domination like LeStatt Knight. You're going to see not only the next level of myself and The Establishment, but LeStatt Knight like you've never seen him before. KEVIN JACOBS: While I may not agree with some of his antics I can say with confidence that, that is NOT a good thing for the rest of GCW. How do you feel this Establishment measures up to the ones of years past? STEVEN CALDERA: This will be the best ever, Kevin Jacobs, and I'll tell you why. Octavian and Jason Dread and Michael Stevens were all fine leaders, and are all legends. But LeStatt Knight is something even more than that; he's on a level of his own. LeStatt Knight is going to take his career to a level never before seen in professional wrestling, and he will be succeeded by our protoge Jordan White. The Establishment has always had people capable of great gang warfare, but these guys are the best of the best at what they do... including one man who is the best of all time, and another who aspires to become the same. KEVIN JACOBS: Well let's take a short turn from Warpath and move onto WorldWide this week. Two questions really. The first being what advice do you have for Kyle James who has openly dismissed your leader, LeStatt Knight? STEVEN CALDERA: Pray to whatever god you believe in that your career doesn't end this week. KEVIN JACOBS: And the second question, what are your plans for Ms. Troy this week? STEVEN CALDERA: Refer to the previous answer. KEVIN JACOBS: Well I appreciate your time Mr. Caldera and good luck to you and The Establishment. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Well as you can see everyone involved is ready to go. I'd like to thank each person who participated for their time. Remember to tune into WorldWide this week as the Warpath Tournament starts up! Kevin Jacobs
