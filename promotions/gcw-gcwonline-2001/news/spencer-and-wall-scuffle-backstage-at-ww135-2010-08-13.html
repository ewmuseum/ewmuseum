Spencer and Wall Scuffle Backstage at WW135

13 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After seeming very on edge in his match earlier in the night, Spencer goes even more off the chain, running into newcomer Dominic Wall in the process. The last few weeks have not been going very well for GCW wrestler, David Spencer. After starting off strong in the Rampage tournament, Spencer has dropped his last four matches. As if that wasn’t bad enough, it seems that the man formerly known as Face has begun to resort to his old antics. In his match with Shaman, he was much less technical and methodical than he has been since his return, using eye pokes and low blows to try and gain an advantage over the much bigger man. In the end, though, it was not enough. What viewers didn’t see, however, was what happened backstage at WorldWide 135, as the main event was going on. - A pair of doors slam against the walls to their sides as an irate David Spencer comes rushing through them, startling some backstage crew who happened to be standing nearby. Spencer pays no attention to them as he continues to storm through the hallway, kicking over chairs and boxes along the sides of the hallway as he does. As the backstage crew looks on, Spencer finally slows down and stops, putting two clenched fists over his head and leaning them and his scarred forearms up against the wall. Noticing that the crew members are staring at him, Spencer turns his head to face them, harshly yelling in their direction. Spencer: What are you looking at!? Hmmmm? Haven’t you people done enough already!? David lets out a series of horrible coughs, getting off the wall to put his hands on his throat. The crew members are clearly uncomfortable with the situation. However, the masked man’s words attract someone else to the scene. That someone is none other than GCW newcomer, Dominic Wall, who peaks his head around the corner as he is removing head phones from his ears to see what the racket was. Dominic: Yo, chill out amigo. Shit can't be that bad. As Dominic says this, Spencer is able to overcome his coughing fit. When he regains what could be called composure he directly his attention straight at the newcomer. Without much of a warning, he walks right up to Dominic Wall, grabbing straight at his shirt, just below the neck, getting right in his face. Spencer: Chill out!? Do you have any idea what I have to go through on a daily basis!? Hmmm? DO YOU!? Dominic smirks and let's out an uncomfortable laugh. Spencer does not appear to be pleased at this reaction. Spencer: Oh, so it’s funny now... Spencer nods rapidly, reaching into his pocket. Spencer: Well, we’ll see how much you can laugh when your throat is as scarred as mine! Without hesitation, Spencer pulls out his lighter and attempts to ignite Dominic’s shirt with it. Dominic’s demeanour suddenly shifts to a very serious one and he quickly reacts, knocking David’s arms away and quickly leaping back into a fighting stance. At this point, Spencer’s not looking for any more formalities and he follows Dominic just as quickly, attempting to tackle him down to the ground. Dominic sprawls in response, holding the takedown off, but Spencer is quick to swing his momentum in the opposite direction, throwing his legs up for his signature triangle choke! Dominic is able to see it coming, however, and reacts accordingly - intentionally throwing his legs forward to escape Spencer’s submission attempt and then yanking his arm away as he rolls backwards to his feet. At this point, the ruckus has brought the attention of GCW security, who flood in to separate the two before any more can transpire. Security Officer: Alright boys, save it for the ring! Wall seems to keep his cool, simply looking a little bit put off from the attack as he adjusts his shirt. Spencer, on the other hand, needs to be restrained by several men. Seeing that his struggle is in vain, David calls out to Dominic, who is already heading back to the exit he was about to leave through earlier. Spencer: You’ll see soon enough! Without turning around, Dominic gives a parting remark. Dominic: Bring it, bitch. I'll be here every damn week! With that, the GCW newcomer heads out of the doors, leaving the security to deal with the riled up David Spencer.
