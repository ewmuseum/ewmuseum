A Blog of Champions, Part II

30 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I want to thank everyone for the feedback on this little project of mine. First of all, anyone who hasn’t done it yet, go and watch the repeat of WorldWide 98. As we get even closer to the 100th show and eventually Dangerous Games, things start to get very interesting in every level of the card. Not one feud or match should be overlooked. Now, most of you that have known me for my stint here in GCW or elsewhere know that I’m not a man of many words. I like to do my talking inside the ring and this is just something I threw up on a whim. What I find hilarious is that I see some snot faced jobber who just arrived in the face calling me out. Now, I don’t mind him doing whatever he wants to do, but I want you, my dear readers to consider the irony of him calling me out for writing a blog in his own rambling blog. Moving on to things that matter though, I got a couple of emails I want to get through for this edition of Blog of Champions because there are some very interesting questions in here. Let’s go to the first one? Dear Digital Mortality, You noted that Garbage Bag Johnny was the American Dream winner, but you didn't speculate as to when you think he will cash in his pot. I'm sure you must have thought about it at some point. What are your thoughts? Do you think he'll cash in at the conclusion of one of your matches? Or will he let you know before hand? Do you think it'll be right away or something that will happen sometime later down the line? Your Fan, Harry Hepperstein Thanks for the email Harry and the answer to your first point is “yes”. I have thought about it a lot. A very important part of this business happens before the contestants get into the squared circle. I have speculated about when GBJ will cash in on his American Dream prize but the honest truth is that I have no clue. It is very hard to try and read the mind of someone who doesn’t seem to be all there. Since he probably does not think the way a rational person should, it is almost impossible to speculate. I do not think he will spring it on me at the end of a grueling match though. For all his absentmindedness and his goofy shenanigans, I think he is someone who doesn’t want to taint his legacy by cheating himself into a title. It will be interesting and unnerving to wait for the moment he cashes in. If you want a prediction, it would make a nice splash to do it at Dangerous Games. Dear Digital Mobility.... my cell phone still won't get any reception... what do you suggest I do? Love, Clyde Fox XOXOXOXOXOXOX Erm? I? Yeah. Dear Digital Mortality, You've wrestled all around this crazy universe in feds like Global, UWF and DWO. How does your GCW experience (thus far) compare with your stints in these other tenured promotions? What are the biggest differences and similarities, if any? - Jobby McBagwell I have to say that I’ve enjoyed my time in GCW a lot. It isn’t to say that my time in the other feds I’ve been has been a waste or anything because they all had their things that made them special. I regards to the UWF, it was hard for me when the place closed because that is where I got my first shot and where I made it big. It was my home and it will always have a special place in my heart. The biggest difference between GCW and the UWF and DWO is mostly that GCW is a little bit more level headed (if that makes sense). You would see stuff that you would never be able to imagine in the ring on those two places. If you don’t believe me, a chimp was UWF champ for a while. Yeah. A chimp. I would like to make an analogy here. UWF was more like a hooker you pick up at 3 in the morning on the street. The nervousness and excitement that everything can happen makes it thrilling. DWO was like a nice S&M bondage session. A lot of whips and leather (who expects any different with Chryste and Callisto Bane in there). Global was like sex with your wife. It's ok but sometimes unexciting. GCW though is like a high end escort service. GCW is a lot more professional and the characters that reside in it? except for a few, seem very human to me. While the UWF was very much a place where we ran the asylum, Steven Caldera (whatever you think of him) is a man that has a passion for the business and runs a well-oiled machine that entertains millions of people. I am really proud of the work I am doing here in GCW and of the work I have yet to accomplish. Dear Digital, Who is the most attractive female in GCW and why? Part 2... who is the most attractive male in GCW and why? Sincerely, Flip Coitus Hahaha. I’m not sure if I should answer this, seeing as most of the females in the roster can kick my ass, but I have to say Lia Ambrosi (even though she was fired). There is something about a Slavic raven-haired beauty that can kick my ass that makes her special. Attractive males? I wouldn’t know. I’ll have a friend of mine answer though. Hey everyone! This is Dee (Digi- Death? yeah, the Death) and my vote for most attractive male in the roster probably goes to Bryan Mayhem. Tattoos, dreads, a painted face and a penchant for spreading pain and suffering around gets me going! This is Digital again and I would just like to say that the views expressed by Dee are definitely NOT my own. Ok? moving on! Dear Digital, Who do you think is the new up-and-commer in each division? How do you see the divisions changing from here till Dangerous Games? Your Loyal Fan, Mike Bagweener If I go by title scene, the guy to watch in the TV title division is probably Kenny Mercury. If you call me biased, I would accept the criticism. The thing is, I have been in the ring with Mercury and been around the guy for many years and when he is determined to make it, he is golden. He can get very full of himself, but he is skilled enough to pull it off. Wouldn’t surprise me at all to see him holding the TV title very soon. In the US title division is a little bit harder to choose a person since it is a very stacked division but I really do believe that Zacharia Taylor is destined for great things and we might just see him shoot to the top of the division by WW 100 or Dangerous Games. He is really looking to prove that his TV title reign wasn’t a fluke. This division won’t be as easy for him, but I expect him to overcome it. In the World title division I want to throw out two names: the first one is The Scottish King of Cool Andy Murray and the second is GCW’s very own enigma, Tessa Windsor. Ever since he got here, Murray has really been on top of his game. Also, ever since losing the US title, Tessa has really shot herself up the ranks and into the top group of the main event. I don’t see a lot of change happening from here until Dangerous Games since there’s very few shows left. It will be interesting to see what happens AFTER Dangerous Games. Dear Dig, How can you compare your times with Legends in other feds to other guys who are legends in their own rights in the GCW? Mikey "Sweaty Elephant sack" Bagsmell To tell you the truth, legends don’t concern me that much. Sure, there is a certain level of respect that you have as a new guy coming in towards the accomplishments of those before you, but you can’t let them get into your heads. In the end of the day, they have to step into the ring with you and at that moment you are equal. Of course, I’ve known and wrestled UWF, DWO and Global legends in my days in the business as well as in the tournaments I have participated and it is definitely a rush. Being able to defeat someone who everyone claims will probably take you out is thrilling and really helps not only your reputation but how you view yourself against the field. Whenever you defeat a legend you get people talking. I remember during GTT5, I was put against two of what were considered at the time the best of PRIME, Hoyt Williams and Killean Sirrajin (I think both are PRIME Hall of Famers). Nobody expected me to beat these two back to back, but I did. It never really mattered to me to get people talking about it but it matter that I thought I could beat anyone, no matter how the odds seemed. I did face LeStatt Knight once and was able to defeat him but so far in my stay here, he is the only one that is considered a legend that I have faced. I definitely look forward to battle some of the other big names in GCW. Well, thanks you all for your questions and stay tuned for the next installment. I’m going to do some predictions for the Dangerous Games match. Take care readers, and keep watching.
