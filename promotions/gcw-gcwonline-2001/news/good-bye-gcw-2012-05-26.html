Good Bye, GCW

26 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hardest part is letting go. The hardest part is letting go. While I make no excuses, I think it’s a testament to GCW’s ubiquitousness that we began to take its existence for granted. By the end, it was easy to let it slip away because we assumed it would always be here. Finally the time has come to say goodbye and put things to rest. I must say that my biggest fear in life is departing this world without having left behind something of value. My aspirations lie elsewhere now, as have many of yours, which is why this time has come. But I think we can safely say that, within the confines of this game as we’ve defined it for ourselves, we accomplished something special together. Global Championship Wrestling started in February of 2001. While we did depart once before, GCW ran for the majority of the past eleven years. During that time we had over two hundred members, who produced over two hundred and fifty shows, about five thousand roleplays, nearly a hundred Poser images, dozens of radio shows, ten websites, and well over a million words of content. Overall I’d say that is a phenomenal, well-rounded body of work from a unique and talented group of people. While we were sometimes derided as silly, or our work dismissed as meaningless fantasy, or occasionally met with bouts of self-loathing, we survived to a point that is unprecedented in our activity. While our longevity has been matched, I don’t believe our quality has. The response to our farewell has been very positive. I deeply appreciate all of you who have voiced enthusiasm about helping to send GCW out in style. It would be a very great shame to let our final moments come and go in an apathetic silence. I have learned a great deal from having worked with you. My writing and storytelling skills have evolved, my understanding of coding and graphics is improved, and I learned a few hard lessons about leadership as well. I’d like to thank each and every one of you for sharing with me your skills, your minds, and your passion. It is because of you that GCW came to be and fulfilled its promise. My promise to you is that I will take the lessons learned here into whatever may come next for me, and I truly hope you do the same. And should we not meet again, I hope the memory of our brief collaboration is enough to inspire you to chase your passions and succeed in them. I am truly grateful to all of you. In the coming weeks I hope to show my appreciation when I can by capping GCW with its final show, its final worthy Hall of Fame inductions, and a few farewell moments befitting GCW’s history. I hope you all join me to cap your stories in a manner best suited to your taste. Thanks for everything. -SC
