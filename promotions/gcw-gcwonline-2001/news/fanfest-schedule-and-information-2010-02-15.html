FanFest Schedule and Information

15 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The NC-17 VII FanFest schedule has been released, and we've got all the details for the big weekend in Dallas. With NC-17 now less than a week away, the excitement level has started to crescendo. GCW started off the week of festivities by releasing the full FanFest schedule this morning, and we've got an exclusive first look for you. Spanning two days and spread over seven stages and event centers, FanFest will bring GCW to the fans from the very stadium that will host NC-17 on Sunday. This Friday and Saturday at Cowboys Stadium, all your favorite GCW stars will be appearing in person to meet fans, sign autographs, and address their coming matches. Outside the stadium fans are invited to tailgate, and there will be ample food and drink for everyone. Inside, the Cowboys Stadium floor will be packed with events, games, food and other activities, in addition to the seven main attractions. The Budweiser In-Ring Event Center will feature the headlining names of FanFest. See your favorite wrestlers, listen to interviews with GCW backstage staff, and watch out for special guests. Plus, get your picture taken in the very ring that will be used at the Pay-Per-View! The Coca-Cola Stage will feature wrestler interviews, autograph and photo sessions, and much more! Plus, get an exclusive preview taste of Coke’s newest product, Coke Minus-One! It’s the new Coke sensation featuring negative-one calorie: Lose Weight by Doing Coke! The Cialis Stage will host top names for autographs, meet-and-greets and more. Drop by for exclusive behind-the-scenes looks at the construction of NC-17. Get a look at what it takes to run the lights, entrance themes and videos, and special effects of NC-17 VII. The Snickers Kid’s Fun Center is a children-friendly attraction for younger viewers. Your kids can meet their favorite GCW wrestlers, and enjoy fun and games in a GCW-themes environment. Win prizes and purchase the latest GCW action figures and playsets! The Doritos Cool Ranch Chill Center is your spot to mingle with GCW staff and crew, in a friendly face-to-face meet and greet. Enjoy snacks, drinks and more while conversing with the people that keep GCW running. Also stop by for special appearances from GCW stars! The Dallas Cowboys Stage features appearances from GCW wrestlers, as well as the top names responsible for putting on this tremendous event. Check out special drop-ins from Commissioner Zenith, Cowboys Owner Jerry Jones, and President Steven Caldera! The 5-Hour Energy Jam Center boasts free samples of your favorite energy drink to power you as you enjoy the latest in GCW media. Play the latest GCW Video Games on XBox and Playstation against GCW wrestlers, and watch classic NC-17 footage on the big screen.
