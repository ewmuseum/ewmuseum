Quinton May not Make Rampage?

4 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a controversial decision, the United States immigration offices have officially put Lucas Quinton's H-1B status visa on hold. Lucas Quinton is set to compete in the opening bout of Rampage 2009 with partners AXEL Action and Mark in the Dark, they are scheduled to face off against SuRReal, Machida, and Face in a six-man tag team match, and it looks like Quinton might not make the event. Sources inside the government suggest there was a clerical error in regarding of his previous application supplied by employer Global Championship Wrestling (tm). Other sources are saying that because of his recent family controversies that he is being denied re-entry into the United States. Sources also say that even if the matter is resolved, Quinton might still miss the event. Further inquires to the Department of State has so far been declined. Quinton, in a telephone message, had to say about this transgression: "You know, it's sad to think that because of some sort of wanker's error that I am stuck back here in England until this is resolved. No matter what happens, I will be there, even if I have to swim across the pond myself, I will be there. Don't count me out just yet..." More as it develops.
