Unique Critique #11

10 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Judgment without accountability. GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Disclaimer: Many different people have come and gone over the last several months, so this Unique Critique is going to go over only those in which are "Role Play Active". There are several on the roster at the moment that have yet to RP yet have done one segment or so, but they will not be mentioned. Thee will likely be another Unique Critique by myself or someone else in the coming months or so. If you did not make this cut, if you are still around for the next one, you will be mentioned. This Unique Critique will be divided up into two sections for each character. One for one card presence and another for Role Play content. AXEL Action: Matt brought in a character as a tag partner, but he has come a long way since. AXEL was a commanding force in the TV division, always on the heels of the TV Champion, but was never given the chance at a shot until he took it at NC-17. AXEL isn't seen often on card unless he's in a match, but this rarely seen prodigy tends to turn heads when he is in the ring. It would do wonders for his character to be in more segs on the card, especially when he is in a match later that night. AXEL is usually a tough and commanding guy in and out of the ring, but he usually comes off surprising in segs because you don't get to see him very often... Matt has come a long way in his writing since the days of the Church of the Unholy. At first, you never really knew what to take from the role plays, often they never had a linking theme, and if they did, is sometimes felt like a struggle to read though the past of AXEL, because it didn't flow right. Now, Matt knows how to link themes together, add detail, and construct an interesting story, well written enough to deem him worthy of being in the US title. The last couple rp's are very well written and read very well (I personally like the diary format of the newest one). I'd like to see a really compelling story put together by Matt to draw a reader in to follow the rp's. I would also like to see him rp more often to really prove that his force in the ring can be backed up by his writing. Matt is a hell of a guy and is willing to do what is necessary to get things done. He is willing to match write, which is important, and wants to be here. He needs to be a little more active on card and really put an image of AXEL in our heads for us to fear AXEL. 3.5/5 Lantlas Anduril: Alex brought in GCW's Resident Elf with a very high expectation due to his World Title run elsewhere. Lantlas came in and raised a lot of eyebrows, not b/c of his ring presence, but because of the fantasy element he brought in. His awkward way of speaking, his seemingly quiet demeanor and lack of feud really put Lantlas off to a slow start. Just now, in the days of Warpath did Anduril finally get a much needed win over Dan Black after a huge feud. The Elf is going to really head into GCW's roster head first now that everyone is giving him the respect that he damn well deserves. Black, former TV Champion, was not an easy person to feud against because of his ego, but Lantlas kept the segs rolling and really showed up on GCW's shows consistently. I would like to see Anduril in more matches and find another good feud to keep him on the card after Warpath. Alex came in, as mentioned before, with very high expectations, and GCW can really kill your ego in your first rp based match. Alex has a very specific way to write, and the first rp was cryptic to many, but after the third, those who read the rp understood it a lot better. Alex likes to write like a philosopher, and those rp's are really good, but when he tries to just write a story, it comes out clean and strong. Philosophy type writing can really put off some readers, but if you change it up and mix in a good story, like Alex does, it can come off really strong. His latest rp was really mostly dialog, but it draws emotion and concern while giving a strong message throughout. Dialog is something many struggle with, but with Alex's style, he can write novels... Alex is finally on a roll with writing and I hope he can keep it, but it is very easy to get lost in the shuffle like he has before. He just got off a strong feud, but what happens after Warpath will really define his character and show us if he is to be feared or not. 3/5 Chris Bagwell: Reading the last several UC's, it seems that it is mostly dick sucking to Shane. Although I'm not going to get on my knees for him, I may jerk him standing up. Shane is an amazing asset to this fed, hands down. Bagwell is on every card, nearly in between every match, and is mentioned on every forum, every match, every seg, etc. Nobody could possibly have more mention than the man that everyone loves to hate! If he is in a seg, you know there is going to be some controversy. He always has one of the top feuds every cycle, and now as head of the Establishment, he can do nearly whatever he wants. JUST WIN A TITLE MAN! This fed wouldn't be standing strong without Shane. He has written over 14 matches in the last several months, writes numerous segs which are all great, and occasionally writes a rp which are always really, really well written. The stories could use some work sometimes, but very solid writing. Just like several people on the roster, you need to drop the Love shit in the rp's and write really deep stuff, like your last two rp's, and you will win over more fans. his writing has earned him numerous awards and a solid spot in the World Title division. We will just have to see what happens after Warpath is posted to see who won... Shane, keep doing what you are doing, and there can be no fault at all. You are the only one getting 5 stars because of how much you do behind the scenes as well as on card! 5/5 Dan Black: Big D came in under the regime of The Movement, and has come a very long way since. One of the longest recorded reigns in the TV division, a number of defenses, and how many people has this guy taken out in the ring to end their careers? Big D is a very strong presence in the TV and US division and to treat him any different is foolish. Even though he lost to Anduril, this doesn't mean he is getting soft. Big D needs to keep up the aggressiveness and stay heel, because the face stuff just didn't work in his favor. We need to see more consistency in on card presence in segs and matches. Big D will be here for a very long time, and time will only prove that his ego is as commanding as his wrestling. Dan has written quite a bit when it comes to rp's and he has progressed possibly more than anyone in the TV or US divisions. His writing back in the day was mediocre and had many, many flaws. It took a while for him to settle into a style, but he got there. Ever since he started the first person rp's, he has really excelled. His writing is really getting good. He needs to decide how he wants Big D to talk and needs to really dwell into his stories. Sometimes Big D sounds like a Harvard grad, other times just an angry, forced individual. This is easy to clear up...drop the big words. The stories are a little extreme at time, but many don't come off as deep enough to really keep the interest of everyone. I'm still on the fence with the High School thing, but we will see where he goes with it. Dan knows he has to work, but doesn't always push forward like many in his "class" that came in over a year ago. Over the last many months Dan hasn't written many segs, has written a couple matches, but only a couple, and tends to struggle with writing at times. More writing and more on card presence will definitely help! 3.5/5 Cade Carson: Cade Carson is someone that I' not too sure anyone really knows about. Only being on card three times in total, one seg and two matches, it is hard to really say too much about him on card. Him and Surreal beat Mortifera at 117 together, only to be beaten by Surreal after the match. I think that Cade can be a very interesting person in GCW, but too little is known about him. I can say that I really didn't dig the seg. It was very mysterious and all, but after the seg, all that I got out of it was it was very similar to what Face used to write; this mysterious person being/giving an interview. Religion always stirs some people and drives others to do interesting things, so we will see what happens. Ryan did really well in the rp's. I do like the active life going on behind the scenes and all. I did feel that some of it was a recap of the show a little too much, like the latest "Pandora's Box" with the newspaper. I think it would have been more effective if Cade was described as being on the couch with some guy staring at him while every once in a while Cade yells out a quote from the paper. The writing is solid enough and has great potential for GCW! Little is known about Cade Carson but the rp is unfolding, and the presence on card will eventually rise. I'm intrigued to see what happens here...2/5 Tyson DeBough: Mike has been in GCW for a very long time, and he has every right to be at the prestige that is his at, or does he? Tyson DeBough was relatively unknown to everyone except as the Establishment goon that was told to take people out. It wasn't until Fisher came in that Tyson was a threat. On card, if you saw Tyson, Fisher was close behind or Tyson was running to catch up to Fisher. These two were inseparable until recent. Tyson stopped showing up so much on the shows the last couple shows and he is usually in the background. Tyson was great right up though Rampage, but he is falling out of sight, and slowly out of mind. FishBough was great, but Tyson just isn't that much of a threat since he got the US division... Mike's rp's were never great, but they were hysterically funny. The writing was simple, and the stories were nothing more than a prolonged joke. I know some were wondering if he was writing them or if Fisher was because their styles are so damn similar. It has been since Rampage that he has written anything, and I know that he hasn't been writing segs or matches either. This is truly sad to see him falling back to where he was prior to Fishbough because he truly does have a lot going for him. We need to see more on card presence, a feud, a US title defense...SOMETHING! If this was written around Rampage the rating would be much higher... 3/5 Brian Fisher: This guy is responsible for putting GCW on their knees with laughter. His smart ass comments in every show, his many trips to the announcing table, and his segs that make you want to fall over in laughter, Fisher has it all. Fisher was on the card so many times in matches and segs that you just wanted him to host his own show, that was until recently. Like DeBough, Fisher hasn't really been around in anything since Rampage. It was such a big show and FishBough were in the new Establishment, but other than a couple rants and segs following around Murray, these guys aren't what they used to be. Fisher is seen on card, and does a lot more talking than DeBough, which has usually been the case, but Fisher is not the happy go lucky guy he used to be. Fisher has a very particular style of writing, and it was unmatched in the TV Division. Everything thought that he would make it to the finals and win at Rampage, but he couldn't beat Fox, but in all reality, it was all based on what style you liked more, and the judge seemed to go for Ross. Fisher is a writer at heart, and a reporter, and you can tell both in his segs and rp's. He is a very funny, but serious guy that can out-write many on card. I would like to see more of him on card and come back to where he was, but he is just simply amazing at what he does. Again, if this was written a month ago, the rating would be higher. 3.5/5 Clyde Fox: Few are on the roll that Ross is right now. Clyde Fox has come a very long way, and he is starting to really turn heads. Momentum is on Clyde's side right now with him flying up the ranks. He is all over the cards, mostly along the terms of Bagwell, Murray, Taylor, etc. Clyde is really funny, and is quirky in many ways, but he is everyone's favorite, no matter who you are. He is on the fence between the US and World division, and is a prime candidate for DG. His Rampage win was huge over Taylor, and he is starting to top the charts in high ranking players he has beaten. Ross' writing has come along a lot. He has come a long way since the Pokemon days and his "Life" stories were amazing. The new series is just as good, but I don't think that everyone understands the gravity of his writing. He can make you laugh, cry, and really wonder about yourself all in the same line. His comedy trumps a lot of the deep stories we read in other top tier writers in GCW. Ross is also consistent in writing segs and matches. He is just under Shane with over 11 matches in the last couple months. I would like to see more segs (even though he is mentioned in a lot) and I would like to see another really good story like the "Life" series. Clyde is on a roll and it will be hard to keep him down from here on till DG. There is very little else he can do at this point besides going for the titles... 4.5/5 Garbage Bag Johnny: Josh brought GBJ in to change GCW forever. GBJ is one commanding force in GCW in every show he's in. If he isn't pulling jokes into the most serious situations or impersonating another character, he is sitting in the office of Zenith and getting yelled at for his actions. He has been active in the new Establishment and with everyone involved, but he hasn't been as active in matches lately. His last big match was Rampage or maybe even against Taylor a little over a month ago, but it has been his segs that are killing it. Josh is an amazing writer in segs, matches, and rp's. Even though he hasn't been writing many in the last couple months, one has to remember he was writing two rp's for every one match because he is the World Champ in Prime. Josh has only been beaten by a few, Taylor ending his record, but he is still feared in an rp battle. He is in the top five writers of GCW without a doubt. I would like to see him in more matches and more active here and there, but personal issues in real life are holding him back. 4/5 Andy Murray+.25: The World Champion and everyone's favorite smart ass! Murr kicks ass on the card all the time. He loves to destroy people in the ring and then tear them apart in the many segs he is part of. He is in more segs than Bagwell himself, and that is not easy to do. Murr's presence on the shows is just commanding for lack of better words. He is a show stopper and he knows it. Murr is cocky, but he has every right to be. Now that he is a face again, he is better than ever, and his segs with Bagwell are priceless. I, for one, am very happy to see him as the World Champ of GCW. It will be interesting to see the results of Warpath... Murray loves to go off how he is so damn efficient at kicking ass when it comes to RPs. He really knows how to write something epic. He doesn't write very often, but that comes with the territory of being World Champion. His style is unmatched but his story sometimes lacks depth because of the romance type stuff he writes about ALL the time. He is much like Bagwell in that respect, but his style is just amazing. He is also among the top five writers in GCW, possibly even the top. He writes a match here and there when needed, and will write segs out the ass, so we get a lot of his writing even if not in the form of an rp. I would like to see more matches, but I know that is hard being the World Champion. Little else to say about this GCW great! 4.75/5 M3: The Mysterious Masked Mexican has only been around for two shows, but has written two rps and a seg to warrant a review. M3 reminds me a lot of Fisher in a lot of ways, but in his own way. I did really enjoy the seg, the funny way of introducing him, being as small as he is and playing on Sally Ford the way that he did. I think that on card, he comes off as really funny, even though he may not be the funny type guy, it works really well for him. His debut match was a success as well, which gives him a great start in GCW. M3's writing is very clean and fun because of the simple details, but also the comical way of writing the dialog. Because he is Mexican, there is a funny language barrier that can be enjoyed in the first rp. The story attacks something simple, and makes it funny, mostly through dialog, but the descriptions can earn a chuckle here and there. It is too early to judge what is going to happen with M3, but I think there is something really special here. 2/5 Man in Black: Who are you? He comes in randomly through the shows and causes chaos, which is fine, but you never know what is going to happen mostly just because he is the resident troublemaker that interferes with matches, but it has been going on loosely for what seems like years now, and we know nothing at all about him. Writing? Well, there is the one rp, but it is only one written a while back for 113 for a filler match, but there was actually an rp. The rp was decently written in a diary format, but that is the only writing that you get from him. There is one seg, two interferences and one match all the way back to NC-17 of 2008, but he was around before that as well. Frustrating with the inconsistency of The Man in Black being around and no unveiling of who he is. 1/5 Nacho Grande: This character was introduced with the Thrillion Man March which was great, but he's really never on the card, at all. We get little segs here and there, but nothing really at all worth mentioning. He is a great gag character that we can't feel for because he's never around. He had a good run in segs for a while dating back to the early 100 matches, but nothing in recent that is sticking out besides maybe the mess of a ME at 115. Nacho is controlled by Alex (Anduril). I would like to see more Nacho and his Thrillian Man March causing trouble like B&E~! used to.. 2/5 Lucas Quinton: Andrew came in here with a passion to write, and he has really impressed. Quinton comes in with a very similar gimmick as Steele and they both explode. In the previous 8 shows, Quinton has been in the last 7. He comes in and starts to seg like crazy and is in several matches. He comes in with the English accent and is cocky as hell. Every seg is exciting to see the progression of the two personalities of Steele and himself. Might not have the best record, but his impact in the TV division is felt, and he is possibly the next TV champion! His writing is good, but can use work. You have to give props to a guy that comes in here and rp's just because he wants character development to happen with his character, which you have to respect. His writing is simple, but it feels like it is lacking depth of something. I'm not sure if reading it I want to see more character, because it is written in first person, or if it is just a longer, detailed, more in depth story. His segs are really well written, and he has even written two matches, which will get you far in this fed. I don't know what it is about his rps, but I can see with him motivation, he will get really far and possibly hold onto the TV championship for a while. I'd like to see more polished writing, but his motivation is killing it right now. Keep up the great segs and we will see what will happen after Warpath! 3/5 Bishop Steele: Nobody better to follow up Quinton with than Steele! Although not on the card as much as Quinton, Steele still commands a presence. His similar gimick, but also differences from Quinton make these two stand out. His whole line of True Perfection is great because the cockiness really projects well and you can look at him on the card and see a little Chris Bagwell brewing. He is in several segs and matches since he came in, and you can tell that he wants to be here bad, and for just coming in and having a possible shot at the title, is amazing. Steele's rps might be short, but they are good nonetheless. Most of his rp is dialog, which reads similar to a screenplay, but it is the dialog that drives the plot, not the details. His cocky attitude is great and it comes through in the dialog really well. I would like to see more detail, just a little setting and everything to get a feel for where and what he is doing, instead of just straight dialog. These kind of rps will work for the TV division, but once you get into the title or further, these kind of dialogs won't work. You need a depth that is lacking form real story telling. I can see where this style can be easily adapted into something larger. The hard part is done, which is the dialog, and I really would like to see more seg and match writing because the ability is there. The no show at Warpath is a minor set back, but great things will come out of the feud with Quinton. 2.5/5 Markus Stone: Stone has been around for a very long time, and he knows how to show up on a card. Half of the insanely fun B&E~! he has parted his tag and went into the singles. His history is there, but Markus Stone is very spotty on the card. When he is there, he is great, but since he went singles, there was a lot of drama and it wasn't fun like we miss from B&E~!, Stone is a champ in his own right, but the lack of on card presence since the split has made him fade into the background with all the new talent. Stone can write. Gered can write! He can make things funny, he can make things serious... the only problem is, he's not around enough. We get these great forum posts, but we don't get segs, matches, or rp's like we used to in the days of B&E~!. We are missing out on something great without him here, but there are plenty of people stepping up where we can forget to miss him. We need more presence! 2.5/5 Shaman (Mortifera): Shaman has been here for ages, going in and out of tag and singles, and he is still doing his thing. Shaman is on nearly every show, whether it be in singles or tag, and he is often looked over, which is a shame. Shaman knows wrestling, and he knows who he is in wrestling. He comes off as too aggressive or possibly too prestigious and a lot of the new people have no idea who he really is. Shaman bounces in between too many cycles without a feud where we just see him as a filler here and there, but with the new Tag building, maybe it will be his time to shine ever since Mayhem left. Andy has a lot of trouble putting his segs and rps together, but his matches are pretty good. He has trouble with editing, and it is very obvious in the segs/rps. Sometimes the errors come off as comical, sometimes they are disruptive to the work, but you can't deny his ethic. Andy works his ass off, doing matches and segs and rp's on a constant basis. You may joke about the very hard to follow stories or the confusing presence on the card, but he is a work horse. He has been through GCW's good and bad, and he has tried to keep his head up through the whole thing. Andy, honestly, you need to edit your stuff to keep it in the running against everyone on the card. It is all there, you just need to edit to keep it as a strong piece. You get this rating because of ethic. 3.5/5 SurReal: SurReal has only been here for a little while, yet I feel like he's been here forever. He has fit into everything really well in GCW, but if you looked on the card, you couldn't really tell. SurReal was trumped at Rampage, but has yet to lose since, which is great, but with only one very short seg establishing that he's friends with Shaman...we have no idea who he is. We know that he is a tough Irishman but that is about it. We need to see more of him on the card in segs to know who he is. All we have is a mean looking Irishman staring at us in match promos. The boomster is all over the forums. He is a little bit of a troublemaker who loves to have fun. I feel like he's been in GCW for a long time, but only because of the forums. He has one seg that he wrote, which was pretty good, but very short, but then we have his rps. One is a character development piece, which I give him props for writing (makes up for the Rampage no show?) His newest "Tides of Change" is mostly dialog, which seems to be the new thing for the newer guys in GCW. It was pretty good, but I needed depth to the scene. I get who SurReal is in the dialog, but I don't get to see what he is like. Also, the 500+ paragraph of dialog from SurReal wasn't very fitting imo because nobody talks like that, only Bagwell.... With some polishing up in writing, and more card presence, SurReal can be a real threat, but until then, keep up the forum posts! 2.5/5 Zacharia Taylor: Few have accomplished as much as Chris. Taylor has been in several ME, Held the TV Title for one of the longest logged periods, held the US title twice, and is one of the few to take down GBJ. Taylor seems to go in spurts on the card, especially after PPVs with matches and segs, but the last couple months, Taylor has been everywhere from into the Establishment's matters to FishBoughs, etc. The loss at Rampage was a big blow, but he recovered nicely. He has faced the elite GCW has to offer, and has mostly come out on top. His presence isn't always felt on the card, but he is a threat in the ring more than anything. Chris writes novels, not rps. Have you ever tried to read one of his rps? It is a shame that he has been no showed against so much in the last month or so, but he still writes regardless. His rps are amazing, but they are so damn long that it is tough to sit in one session to read. He is right up there in matchwriting with Shane and Ross, writing over 13 matches in the last several months. His segs are hit or miss, and so are his feuds. The biggest problem is being paired off with people who have been dropping out or whatnot. He is a threat in writing, but needs to really buckle down on determining who Taylor should be on card and where he should stand among the greats in GCW. It will be exciting to see what happens at DG. A workhorse on and off the card, but needs to really decide who Taylor needs to be on the card. 4/5 Vivica J. Valentine: Billy has crafted a Phenom. Vivica is a force to be reckoned with, and you need to watch out when in the ring, or same hall as her. She is always on the card, involved with something. She is usually seen in the top tier with Bagwell, Murray, Fox, GBJ, etc. Her on card is amazing and she is often in a fight with Zenith or Caldera himself. The last female of the upper division, she has a lot to command, but she does. Billy is definitely one of the top writers in GCW, but we get so few rps from him. His rps are usually really good. The writing is nearly flawless, it is just the story sometimes is a bit reminiscent of everything in the matches and segs which can sometimes be a bore if you read the shows. The stories could be so much more, but he gets the job done when needed, and has been on top too many times to count. Matches come here and there out of Billy, and a ton of segs fill in the writing gaps. I really don't know what I would like to see? Maybe another feud to epic scale? Without it, Viv falls into the background, even though she is all over the show. 4/5 ______________________________ There are many honorable mentions of those that left or are on a break. For those that are new, I will list them.... Jordan White-Former World Champ and one of the most amazing writers... Lestatt Knight- Former..well...everything. Most recent former World champion...he will be back. Jay Terror- Former World champ. Great personality and poser work. Brad Jackson- Former Establishment member. Face- Former GCW creep with a mask. Jake Reed- Amazing writer, coms here n there. Tessa Windsor- Former US champion. Great writer. Chris Storm- Other half of B&E~! Danger- Creator of Dangerous Games.
