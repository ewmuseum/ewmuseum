A special GCW report by Website Reporter Evil James (Von Doom) was back at it again today. For the second time this week, he has made the claim that the Church of the Unholy are the uncrowned GCW World Tag Team Champions and he also put out a challenge to the roster of GCW. During a Church sponsored death metal concert featuring their newest band Deformed Fetus, Evil James addressed his "fans" or, to be more precise, the five nerdy looking guys wearing James von Doom retro NWC T-shirts. This is what he had to say. "Today I am here to inform you all that myself and AXEL Action, the Church of the Unholy, are the future damnation of professional wrestling. I sent one of my stooges...I mean associates, to the dressing room of Banned & Exiled~! last week to challenge them on behalf of the REAL World's Tag Team Champions and what did they do? They slammed the door in his face without hesitation." "Well, let me tell you something. If it had been me and AXEL and if it had been us at their locker room door, then we would've DRAGGED them to the ring and made them surrender the titles over to us." The reporters that were there, including me, got a good chuckle over this until Evil James gave us 'the look of death' and we stopped. "But, of course, there is one thing to note. If they would happen to lose the titles beforehand, then we would GLADLY take the titles as an offering, a TRIBUTE to Tioneb from the future champion Kimbusa's buttcrack." "So we will have to see who we end up facing in the future match. In fact, we are SO confident in our abilities that we are having our master slaves...I mean, master blacksmiths back at our Church complex dungeon are working on making us our very own Tag Team Championship belts." At this point in the conference everyone got confused, especially the reporters. The Church congregation behind us raised lighters to tribute this announcement for some reason. "We will soon be the unsanctioned UNHOLY World Tag Team Champions. All factions of the Holy will cower in the wake of our false idol. They will then bow before our feet before make them kiss our feet in tribute to our greatness." "But now we have the BIG Rampage pay-per-view coming up as well. A lot of great matches will take place on that show. One will feature a piece of crap I took against a toilet flusher. The flusher will flush and it will go down the drain, so we already know who will win that one." This brings an evil, demented grin to Evil James' face. "Then there's the AWESOME match pitting Almasy's left hand against his penis. I'll bet you a hundred bucks that a Emperor's Club hooker will run interference and pull a Monica Lewinsky on him before doing a double swerve and pulling a Loraina Bobbit on him to end the match via penile decapitation." "And lest we forget the battle between Vivica and Shaman. All she'll have to do is flash him and she'll win via KO. Easy pick there. So what else is left? The tournament finals. Yeah, a tournament that we, the Church of the Unholy, were not able to take part in." Evil James shook his head over this. "This is my compromise. We are challenging ANYONE in GCW to a Japanese style deathmatch. We want to make some people bleed. We want to give the people what they pay to see and that is violence." Evil James smiles deviously. "So, my brethren of GCW, bring it on. Because Lord Tioneb as sent us on a mission to show all of his GLORY to the masses. And for him we shall succeed.." Evil James then turned to leave when one of my fellow reporters asked him a question. "Where's AXEL Action?" he asked. Evil James simply smiled over this. "He's busy on church related business at the moment." "What's he doing?" the reporter asked as a follow-up. "He's at your house fucking your mom." Evil James then turned around and left the other reporter dumbfounded and the rest of us in the press area giggling like school children who just heard their first curse word in a public setting.
