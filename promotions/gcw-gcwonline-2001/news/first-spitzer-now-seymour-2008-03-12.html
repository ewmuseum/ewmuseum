First Spitzer, Now Seymour

12 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW superstar Seymour Almasy will admit tomorrow to using the Emperor's Club to hire a high-priced prostitute. This is the same group that NY Governor Eliot Spitzer used. New York, NY -- With a government investigation having uncovered evidence that New York governor Eliot Spitzer engaged in sexual relations with a high-priced prostitute, many are wondering if any other high-profile figures will be revealed in the course of the probe. According to sources close to Seymour Almasy, let the wondering end. It is strongly probably that Almasy will admit tomorrow to having hired the services of one of the Emperor's Club's high-priced prostitutes. Seymour's assistant Jonathan Calver has been notably tight lipped regarding the situation, saying only that Almasy would deliver his statement on the issue tomorrow. What GCW has learned, however, is that Almasy's name appears nowhere in New York's papers. Rather, Almasy is coming clean to preempt what would appear to be inevitable media discovery of his actions. Since breaking up with his then-wife Laura Winters in 2004. Almasy has seemingly bounced from love interest to love interest. Seymour himself has admitted in the past to having enjoyed the services of a woman named Aya on a Universal Wrestling Federation tour of Japan. While Governor Spitzer may face several felony charges, GCW's legal team has told GCW Online that Almasy will only face a potential charge of patronizing a prostitute in the fourth degree, a class B misdemeanor in the state of New York. "From what we've been told," our source stated, "Seymour hired the woman in New York for a meeting in New York. The governor's gravest legal problem comes from the fact that he paid to transfer his female accompaniment across state lines, which is a felony." No other details are available at this time. Almasy has scheduled a press conference for 1:00 PM EST for tomorrow. More news will be reported as we get it.
