Backstage Report #4

19 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, Kevin Jacobs here off the heels of High Stakes with another Backstage Report! High Stakes was a great show, and it drew great ratings. GCW brass is very pleased with the direction of the company, and revenues are at a two-year high. On that note, let's get started with some backstage news. -Though everyone is pleased with the attention High Stakes garnered, most backstage are nervous about the return of The Establishment. Though most of the current GCW roster was not around the last time The Establishment existed, the group is a thing of legend in GCW and something you hear about fairly quickly. This may also be the most purely talented group yet, with legend LeStatt Knight and the US Champion Jordan White involved, as well as the crafty Sage Black. President Caldera will address the company this week on WorldWide. -The hype has already begun on the Warpath tournament. Early returns from the fan polling has LeStatt Knight winning the whole thing. It's pretty easy to assume that Knight is going to be given every opportunity to come out of the tournament the winner, as President Caldera has a history of finding a favored champion and doing all he can to see that the belt ends up around their waist. Still, the field is immensely talented. In addition, Head of GCW Security Toad, once a staunch rival of The Establishment, has privately assured me that his Security Team will be on hand to make sure the tournament goes down relatively fairly. -Fan polling will begin in about three weeks with regards to the new Hall of Fame inductee to be announced at Warpath. As of now, the ballot is expected to include Luke Hanson, Mike Hardy, Toxic, Demented, and current GCW star Twizzy. Twizzy has to be the hands-on favorite, as many backstage have gone out of their way to welcome Twizzy back and offer their support for his Hall of Fame bid. Twizzy wants to be enshrined very badly, to leave his mark on his beloved promotion. Though President Caldera seems bent on holding Twizzy down, it seems that Twizzy's already got enough momentum to make his dream come true. -There's been some backstage banter already between LeStatt Knight and Kyle James with regards to their upcoming Warpath tournament match. James has been boasting to people that he will defeat Knight "with ease" next week on WorldWide. Knight has yet to comment, seeming to pay little mind to the brash newcomer. James has yet to make a significant mark on GCW in the ring, but is regarded as a highly promising prospect. Obviously, a win over Knight would be a career-making accomplishment, but Knight won't go down easily. James had better not underestimate the most dominant GCW champion of all time, or things could end badly. Still, most backstage are rooting hard for James to make good on his boast and oust the sellout Knight. -Jimmy Thunder is, despite his objections, being escorted around by GCW Security this week. Thunder received a bit of a scare when the lights went out and a heavy rain began to fall on him inside the arena at High Stakes. Thunder has not commented but appears to be unworried. Still, GCW is not taking any chances with one of its top commodities, and is making sure this "Hydra" doesn't try anything until his debut next week. -GCW has released Fiery Passion and Kyo Kushinaga from their GCW contracts, and we wish them all the best in their future endeavors. While all three were great athletes, a statement by President Caldera said that they simply did not fit in with the GCW blueprint for success. GCW reportedly gave each of them ample severance packages for their excellent service. -To fill the spots, GCW has been recruiting hard. The company has signed two new tag teams, several bring young singles wrestlers, and is in negotiations with more. There has been a great deal of controversy within the PTC ranks regarding talent that might be departing from GCW rival WWA. While President Caldera has said he's had preliminary contact with a few WWA stars, he has assured us that no actual negotiations will take place with anyone under contract with a rival promotion. GCW appears to be attempting to avoid controversy, but Caldera is known as an aggressive recruiter, and is likely to get whomever he desires. -Former GCW star "The Violence" Vince Jones was backstage at High Stakes. Reports say that he's negotiated a return, but nothing has been made official. Jones is reportedly on his way to Raleigh-Durham, where he's expected to attend WorldWide. -We've been getting lots of get-well wishes from fans for former champion Dawn McFarlane, who has taken a leave of absence. While she cited physical problems in her request, most believe that she was also experiencing mental fatigue. McFarlane is one of the hardest working wrestlers in GCW, noted for her relentless commitment to physical training. The road can be a tiresome place for professional wrestlers, and Dawn probably just needed a rest. We're sure we'll see her back in a GCW ring soon. -Watch for the debuts of Damon Aldair and Jay Terror, as well as Total Domination this week on WorldWide. GCW is very excited to have these top prospects on the roster. That's it from me this week. Be sure to tune in to WorldWide on X:TV this Tuesday!
