The Messiah Irate With Poor Employee Management

20 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a brutal assault on Worldwide 136, The Magnificent Messiah seeks retribution - in one form or another! PHOENIX, ARIZONA - A day removed from WorldWide Episode 136 and a week prior to the always hot High Stakes pay-per-view in the Mojave Desert, things are already starting to heat up. After a brutal beating at the hands of 'The Hardcore Legend' Bryan Mayhem, rumors are beginning to circulate that The Magnificent Messiah has had just about enough of the lack of respect he's been shown. "It's pathetic," said the GCW superstar to reporters while exiting the arena last night. "The executive staff of Global Championship Wrestling needs to start showing more concern for the welfare of their superstars." It's unclear whether or not The Messiah's comments were directed solely towards the attack by Mayhem. Over recent weeks, The Magnificent Messiah has been on the short-end of a series of negative gestures by both the Commissioner and Acting Commissioner. Not to mention, the entire locker room has all but dismissed his presence. When asked for his thoughts on The Magnificent Messiah, long-time GCW employee Shaman responded, "Yes, my casino is Magnificent, isn't it?" Needless to say, the reporter who tried to explain that TMM was actually a superstar and not a compliment to the casino spent the night in the hospital. So what's next for the underrated, undervalued Messiah? "There's only one solution when you want something accomplished," exclaimed the returning Dan Black. "You take it. You got out there and take it. That's what The Magnificent Messiah needs to do. He needs to take the respect he wants." Black was very open in his ambitions for The Messiah. In what seems to have been a string of attempted recruitments, Black approached The Messiah last night to deliver his standard issue speech. Initially, The Messiah's reception to the pitch was weak. However after the assault by Mayhem, the offer seemed to look increasing lucrative. Will The Magnificent Messiah ultimately accept the offer from The Golden Child? Or will he release his frustrations elsewhere - say, on the Hardcore Champion Trumph Frost - at High Stakes? There's only one way to find out...
