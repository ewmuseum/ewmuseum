Hall of Fame: Bryan Mayhem

2 July 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW will be slowly releasing the final Hall of Fame inductions. Inductee number four is the biggest, baddest, and longest tenured of the list. After a brief hiatus, GCW returned to announce another entry to the Hall of Fame this week. This one is sure to inspire quite a bit of discussion, as there was intense debate about whether this person would be enshrined. Bryan Mayhem, who joined GCW in 2001, making him the latest of the GCW Originals to join the Hall of Fame. Rich Rollins, Badger Benandanti, The Jhub and Octavian were all alumni of GCW's predecessor, the Cyber Wrestling Union. Mayhem joined the original iteration of GCW over a decade ago, having been recruited by his brother, fellow Hall of Famer Jason O'Neil. Mayhem doesn't have the resume of the other GCW originals, but his contributions to the company are hard to deny. Mayhem has been one of GCW's most consistent performers since his debut; while the other names mentioned came and went, Mayhem never took a break and never ventured away from the GCW ring. It's hard to paint such a vicious, inhuman man as loyal, but Mayhem must be given credit for his tenure. Longevity, however, is not the reason Mayhem has been inducted. Mayhem has established himself as an iconic GCW personality, the undisputed biggest monster in company history. His unmistakable appearance and brutal, powerful style made him one of GCW's most recognizable stars, and his skill in the ring was sometimes masked by his straight-forward approach. A multiple-time Tag Team Champion, Hardcore Champion and Television Champion, Mayhem made his mark across the ranks of GCW. In 2007, Mayhem finally won the big one, defeating Lia Ambrosi to became GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Mayhem's reign was dominant and cemented his status as a main event player. Also known for his horrifying and unparalleled Belmont Match, Mayhem made hardcore, no-holds-barred wrestling his own personal trademark. While Toad is known as GCW's Hardcore Legend, Mayhem redefined the genre when it came to no disqualification combat. Mayhem is truly in a class of his own, an original in every sense of the word, and this has led him to be honored with a spot in the GCW Hall of Fame.
