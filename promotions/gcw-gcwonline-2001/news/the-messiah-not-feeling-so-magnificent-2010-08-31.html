The Messiah: Not Feeling So Magnificent

31 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the beating from Mayhem at WW136, what shape will The Magnificent Messiah be in for his Luck of the Draw match at High Stakes? Phoenix, Arizona -- Mere days before the upcoming High Stakes event, rumors are leaking from sources inside the Magnificent Messiah camp, saying that the injuries sustained from the brutal beating at the hands of Bryan Mayhem are worse than originally thought. No specifics on the just how severe these injuries are were given. "He's not feeling well," our source told us, refusing to indulge our curiosities further. "He is still, at the moment, planning to appear as intended at High Stakes." He is slated to be part of the Luck of the Draw series, which could result in either a Hardcore or TV Championship shot against reigning double-champion, Triumph Frost. The Messiah was left bloodied and barely conscious at WorldWide 136, following a run-in with the man who calls himself The Lunatic. He did not appear, at first, to have sustained any serious damage and has since given an interview regarding his supreme dislike with the lack of respect he's been shown since debuting this past March. What shape will the Messiah be in to compete at High Stakes? Will his condition be the difference between victory and defeat? Will he even feel strength enough to compete, or will he simply no-show the Pay-Per-View event? At this point in time, no one knows for sure.
