Woods medical report

28 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today we received an envelope containing the medical report of the Innovator of Impact, CJ Woods. Last week, on WorldWide, the GCW loyal watched on as the Admiral of Awesome, Andy Murray, destroyed CJ Woods with not only a steel chair but a SLEDGEHAMMER! Woods has been brutalised time and time again lately, all starting off with a steel chair beating by the recently deceased Chris Bagwell. Since then we've seen Woods brutalised by LeStatt Knight, Dan Black and an array of stars at Shockwave but none have done as much damage as the man attempting to get a title shot at the GCW Champion, Andy Murray. At WorldWide 109, we saw LeStatt Knight pitting Murray against his ally, CJ Woods, in a match to grant him a shot at Rampage. But just before proceedings got underway, Knight through a curveball at the duo calling it a Last Man Standing match. Murray WAS in fact the last man standing, but CJ did not go down without a fight. Rising to his feet on numerous occassions, even to the shock of GCW fans. Woods' resilience was known to GCW, but as to how far his resilience went, it's regardless to say that the world was shocked as time and time again, CJ got to his feet. Despite the brutalisation Murray put on him, with the assistance of a steel chair. The devil himself, LeStatt Knight, was urging Murray to hurt and wound Woods, handing him the steel chair and eventually handing him the sledgehammer that shocked Woods' to his foundations and the GCW fans to boot. Woods was stretchered out of the arena and admitted to the general hospital where doctor's stitched up his forehead, giving the GCW rookie seven stitches. Speculation over the past few weeks have been made in regards to the strength of CJ's ribs, which have been forced to accept the brunt of many attacks over the past few weeks. Without doubts they have to be weakening and according to the report we've got, CJ has a massive four fractured ribs running along the right-hand side of his body. CT scans showed there was some minor bruising as well. As to whether or not CJ has been cleared by the GCW medical team, it's unknown but the fans can bet their lives that despite what the doctor's say, CJ Woods will be fighting to get into the wrestling ring against Brock Alyas to progress in the GCW Rampage tournament. Hopeful to garner himself a shot at the United States Championship title, which he missed out on at Shockwave. Fingers crossed CJ can remain in tact between now and Rampage as the only thing we can see standing in his way is his health.
