Roster Movement

18 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, OH- The night before WorldWide comes to Phoenix, Global Championship Wrestling officials announced some major roster moves. In what has become a trend, GCW has released several superstars to make room for more. President Caldera and other GCW heads have made it clear that GCW roster spots are going to be highly coveted and highly competitive, and as a result a few wrestlers who failed to produce have been released. Phil Atken, Caden Kalix and Patrick Vander were all released from GCW late last night. Atken, popular in his few appearances, struggled to remain healthy. Vander made his debut a few weeks ago but never found a niche. The release of Calix came as a bit of a surprise, as he had racked up an impressive unblemished singles record. There was talk of giving Calix a raise, but Calix reportedly surprised GCW by requesting his release earlier this week. However, as usual, GCW has left the door open for each of these stars to return some time in the future. To fill the spots left open by these releases, GCW has announced the signing of Garbage Bag Johnny, "Lunar" Sonny Davidson and the re-signing of Paddy O'Shea. O'Shea and Johnny, both former AWC wrestlers, have not officially inked their deals, but their arrival is expected in the coming weeks. Davidson, a brawler who made a name for himself on the independent circuit lately, is also expected to ink his deal in the immediate future. GCW has received oral commitments from all three stars. GCW also reportedly has a standing offer on the table to Jay Phoenix, though Phoenix does not appear ready to sign any time soon. Phoenix met with Caldera several weeks ago, but contract talks fizzled after a hot start. Tune in to WorldWide this week to catch a glimpse of some of these new stars!
