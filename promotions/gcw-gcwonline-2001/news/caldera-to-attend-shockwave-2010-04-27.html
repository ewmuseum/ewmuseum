Caldera to Attend Shockwave?

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW President Steven Caldera Has Flown to Hawaii Despite his pledge to remain off the tour, GCW President Steven Caldera has flown to Hawaii and may be in attendance at Shockwave. Caldera has not spoken publicly since NC-17, where in a tremendous sendoff he handed operations of the company to Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny. It is no secret, however, that Caldera views the retirement of LeStatt Knight as a major turning point in GCW history, and simply could not stay away for his final match. Caldera himself continues to refuse to speak, but his office did release a statement: "President Caldera is a man of his word and intends to continue to hold to the terms of his agreement with Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny. However, GCW remains Caldera's company and he reserves the right to attend GCW events at his pleasure. The retirement of LeStatt Knight is a significant event and should President Caldera choose to attend, it will be to witness this historic moment first-hand and not to disrupt the operations of GCW." This would mark the first time President Caldera has appeared backstage during Commissioner Bag's tenure, but it is likely that Caldera would merely watch Knight-Valentine from a private box and avoid interfering with the event. Still, Caldera's presence only seems to add weight to what it already regarded to be one of the biggest showdowns in GCW history.
