O'Neil-Terror War Escalating

25 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The battle between Jason O'Neil and Jay Terror is growing more intense, and the law has become involved to keep the two apart. Conspicuous by his absence from Worldwide the last few weeks, Jason O'Neil was thought to be nursing a calf injury and off the road. Word comes in from our backstage spies that O'Neil's been behind Jay Terror's every move -- literally. An anonymous GCW employee has pictures of Jason O'Neil casually following Jay Terror around backstage at a safe distance. The photographs do show O'Neil peeking out of the corner of his eye to see where Terror is at all times, even going somewhat undercover to hid his identity. "It's freaky. Real freaky," the source revealed. O'Neil -- who was a victim of an attack at the hands of Terror after Worldwide 123 -- admitted his intentions weren't for good. "Yeah, I admit it," O'Neil said after Worldwide. "I've been stalking Jay Terror. I'm following him, waiting for the perfect moments to punch him in his face. Hey, Terror, that's one, prick." When pressed further, O'Neil put his hands in the air and shook his head. "Look, there's too much talking going on, not enough action. Caldera was talking, I was talking, Terror was talking, Bryan was talking, Axel's going to be talking. I'm done talking. It's time for action. Now, if you'll excuse me, I'm going to Terror's hotel room." BREAKING UPDATE: Jason O'Neil and Jay Terror were arrested early this morning for aggravated assault and criminal trespass as they destroyed the second floor of the hotel each was staying in. Details as they come available. UPDATE 2: Details are coming in regarding the fight Terror and O'Neil were involved in early this morning. Jason O'Neil was taken to a local hospital with what's being described to be serious injuries. Jay Terror was bailed out by Steven Caldera, who was said to be smiling as he lead the superstar away from the scene. A witness to the action described the fight as "surreal -- as though Peter Griffin and that Chicken came to life and destroyed the second floor." Jason O'Neil threw Jay Terror through a soda machine and broke a vase over his head while Terror threw O'Neil through two hotel doors and out a second story window. O'Neil's injuries are described as lacerations and a possible concussion and are not nearly as serious as first speculated. UPDATE 3: Jason O'Neil has been released from the hospital, and has been processed in and out of jail, bonded out by Bryan "Mayhem" O'Neil. The two left without comment.
