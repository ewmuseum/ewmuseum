Tempest hospitalized briefly after Dual Halo match

22 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest wows the crowd, but suffers the consequences of the notoriously high risk match. After putting forth a valiant effort on behalf of GCW in the Dual Halo match Sunday night, Tempest was admitted to Northwick Park Hospital suffering from severe pain in her back and side, which was thought to be from possible internal injuries. While it was determined that she had simply gotten a bruised kidney during the match, she was kept overnight for observation due to the danger of possible kidney failure. Luckily Garbage Bag Johnny's experience with the Dual Halo gave him reason not to book Tempest this week and a wise decision that turned out to be. Not only did Tempest pound her former GCW rival James von Doom in the tunnel between the Halos for ten minutes and endure the return beating, but she also stunned the sold out Wembley Stadium crowd with a 16 foot Lollipop Drop from the upper level of the Halo to the mat below on SCCW’s Alexandra Pierce, stopping all action at the time. Tempest was eager to speak to reporters the next morning and said, “I’m fine! No really, it was just a hard match and I managed to get kicked, punched and dropped all on that one side, so everyone got a little worried when it wouldn’t stop hurting. But I’m fine and you can expect me back on GCW television making as much trouble as ever soon enough!” Whether Tempest will appear this week remains to be seen considering both her injury and the fact that she is still reeling from the loss of her television title just days before the grueling Halo match. Has the Sparkly one finally bitten off more than she can chew?
