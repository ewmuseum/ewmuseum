New-Look Establishment Rising

2 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Dangerous Games, it appeared as if The Establishment was dead and buried. Jay Terror lost the World Title. Tyson DeBough lost twice, in his match with Garbage Bag Johnny and the Dangerous Games match. And President Steven Caldera mysteriously and abruptly disappeared without so much as a word. But even from afar, you can never count The Establishment out. This time, however, Caldera can't take the credit for masterminding another Establishment resurgence. This revival belongs to the former World Champion Jay Terror. At WorldWide this week, Brad Jackson left the ranks of the Movement in favor of The Establishment. In doing so, Jackson made himself a major player in GCW, taking out the Hall of Famer Jason Dread and aiding Terror in a major victory. Sources say it was Terror who personally engineered Jackson's defection, a major coup for a group that was in desperate need of a boost. Mission accomplished. The new-look group of Terror, Jackson and DeBough suddenly threatens to take control of GCW once again. And the group only stands to grow, as it's well known that DeBough is looking for a new Tag Team partner. With Caldera gone and the title around the waist of Jordan White, many assumed the group would be left for dead and that Terror would suffer the fate of so many other former Establishment champions. But Caldera has apparently shown faith in "The Established" Jay Terror, leaving him the keys to the group. And Terror has responded with a move that has saved the group from an unthinkable period of irrelevance. This week's victory for Terror and rebirth for The Establishment is a sign that Terror is still growing into his role. He was an excellent champion who ran into a difficult position at Dangerous Games. Caldera has made a habit of eliminating those who fail him, but Terror got a stay of execution and the move paid off. Terror responded with a big night and a big addition, and now The Establishment is a threat once again.
