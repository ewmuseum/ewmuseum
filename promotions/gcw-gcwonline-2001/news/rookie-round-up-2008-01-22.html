Rookie Round Up

22 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Bagwell Debut: NC-17 Matches: vs. Thom LeStrange (lost) Segment Count: 5 Roleplay Count: 4 IC: Bagwell has a set gimmick that is easy to recognize and picks up quick heel heat. He’s tossed himself into the mix with the other newcomers and helped set himself apart. Getting into a feud would give Bagwell more material to work with and keep him from getting too repetitive. OOC: Thus far he has been a great contribution to the fed. He’s been roleplaying and willing to post something out of the box. He seems like once he gets a style down, he can really roll. I don’t think anyone of the newcomers can say they have made as much of an impact OOC as Bagwell has. He’s started to integrate himself into the GCW culture, which has proven to be a key to success. Depraved Debut: WorldWide 81 Matches: vs. Phil Atken (won) Segment Count: 0 Roleplay Count: 2 IC: Not much to go on as far as IC goes within any real form of a show presences. The gimmick and everything seems like it could be there, but he hasn’t stepped up yet to separate himself from others. It doesn’t appear like he’s scheduled any appearances for 82 as well. OOC: The roleplays are good and I realize he will be leaving soon because of military duties. So best of luck to him with that. Digital Mortality Debut: N/A Matches: N/A Segment Count: N/A Roleplay Count: 1 IC: Normally it would be hard to rate a person when they haven’t even made a single appearance on a show yet. But most people aren’t Digital Mortality. He is the PRIME-killer, he is the J Cup Winner, he is the UWF Hall of Fame member. With Digi, you know the talent and commitment he brings. By the next PPV/Supershow he will have set the bar for the other newcomers. It will be up to them if they want to pass him or watch him pass. OOC: The Unknown Ninja in our chats, Digi is an easy to approach guy and fun to talk to. He should be just fine in the GCW and only add to the already deep roster. Docta Z Debut: WorldWide 82 Matches: vs. Clyde Fox (TV Title) Segment Count: N/A Roleplay Count: 3 IC: I can’t judge him in character because he hasn’t made a formal debut yet. Through his introduction roleplays the reader is able to get an idea of where Docta Z comes from, but it is still unseen of where he will go. OOC: I like the fact that he’s roleplaying for just the purpose of roleplaying, rather than waiting to be booked and just putting out one piece. He’s showing a commitment we don’t usually see from newcomers. Hopefully he’ll be able to keep the momentum going, as it could build to a very successful career. Kiljaden Kincaid Debut: NC-17 Matches: vs. Mark Matthews & Depraved Segment Count: 1 Roleplay Count: 0 IC: Dark gimmick, will need to do a lot of separate himself from a crowded gimmick. I have to say that trying to go for a dark gimmick is most likely one of the hardest gimmicks now in e-feds because it has been done so many times and done so badly so many times. Many people are just tired of it. OOC: I’m ready to chalk Kincaid up to a drop seeing as he already no-showed his first match. People who do that typically drop by their third appearance. Thom LeStrange Debut: NC-17 Matches: vs. Chris Bagwell (win) Segment Count: 6 Roleplay Count: 2 IC: Out of all the newcomers, this one shines the brightest. No digs to Docta Z, but if any one of the newcomers could have gotten a title shot at Clyde, it is this man. He’s got a great gimmick that is well executed. He’s jumped right into a well organized feud with Andy Murray and been a very high note on each card he has appeared on. If he can carry the momentum, him and Murray will be the rising stars of “08. OOC: He’s been active on the boards and made sure he’s contributed so major kudos to him. He even mentioned being a possible match writer in the future, which is always a huge plus for any one. Andy Murray Debut: NC-17 Matches: vs. Mark Matthews (win) Segment Count: 7 Roleplay Count: 2 IC: I’m a bit surprised that Murray has been hit or miss at times with his segments. But he certainly knows his character and is comfortable writing him, that shows clear in each segment. When he does hit, he hits hard with solid work. His feud with Thom LeStrange has been another great undercard feud for GCW. Murray can easily be a bit hit in GCW if he just keeps his momentum going. OOC: The nice thing about Murray is that he’s a proven commodity. He was great in AWC and he was great in FUSE. I’m sure Hyde and Dean would both highly recommend him to any fedhead, but luckily we landed him and hopefully he doesn’t leave anytime soon. Mark in the Dark Debut: N/A Matches: N/A Segment Count: N/A Roleplay Count: N/A IC: N/A OOC: I’m only rating him because he came highly paraded by Ben. Thus far what I have seen from him is 2 IG roleplays for PTC and nothing for GCW. What happened? This is why newcomers (even if they are old guys returning) shouldn’t be hyped until they prove it. Too often they disappoint. At this point we’re more likely to see Hydra return before he makes his debut. Mark Matthews Debut: NC-17 Matches: vs. Andy Murray (lost) Segment Count: 2 Roleplay Count: 2 IC: Sadly Matthews has not set himself apart from the thick pack of newcomers that have entered GCW. A lack of real presence on the previous card makes him easy to forget when others are putting up great work. OOC: It’s good to see he’s roleplayed for both matches and isn’t no showing on us. Hopefully he can get himself a feud and start to build an on-screen presence because with the depth of talent on the roster at the moment, it is easy to get lost in the shuffle. Divisions are in need of filling and now is a great time for newcomers to step up and decide where on the card they want to be placed. Luckily for Matthews, he still has plenty of time to impress with the Rampage tournament.
