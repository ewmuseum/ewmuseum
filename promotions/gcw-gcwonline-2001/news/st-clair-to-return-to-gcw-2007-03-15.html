St. Clair to Return to GCW?

15 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Major news came down from one of GCW's rival promotions today, as former GCW head office honcho and current head man at PRIME, Peter St. Clair, has stepped down from his duties. Sources are still trying to determine if St. Clair is parting ways with the promotion entirely, or merely stepping into a different role. St. Clair was widely known as President Caldera's right-hand-man and gained great respect in the wrestling community through his work in GCW. St. Clair was active chairman of GCW as recently as last summer, but he parted ways with GCW when he was offered the top job at PRIME. While Caldera and St. Clair haven't always seen eye to eye, people within Caldera Enterprises say Caldera has always had a great deal of respect for St. Clair's abilities. However, those same sources have been consistently reporting that Caldera is taking on more and more personal responsibility, and the likelihood of another high-level management body being hired is slim. Further, Caldera seems to be happy with the job St. Clair's replacement, Ian Childress III, has done since coming on board. However, there has been some speculation that Childress's job might be in jeopardy. While there has been absolutely no displeasure whatsoever with his work thus far in GCW, some believe that Caldera is intent on reversing the delegation of powers he initiated last year, with the aim of taking total control of the direction of the promotion. While some believe that the firing of Childress could open the door for St. Clair to return, it is highly unlikely that St. Clair would ever accept a lower-end job in GCW, nor would Caldera make the lateral move of replacing Childress with St. Clair when his ultimate objective is to control things personally. Still, there has been no concrete evidence to suggest that Caldera is seriously considering terminating the contract of Ian Childress. In fact, sources say that Childress is in line for a promotion to Chief Operations Officer in the next few weeks. Despite these reports, Caldera has been his usual guarded self and has not provided any direct hints about what he might be planning for the front office of GCW. St. Clair only released a brief statement today revealing his departure from his post; he has not made further comment regarding his future in the business. It seems only natural that his sudden availability would lead many to speculate that Caldera would make him a lucrative offer to return, given their history together. However, it seems to this reporter that the more likely result will be the further restructuring of Caldera Enterprises to allow Caldera more hands-on access to the company. -Kevin Jacobs, reporting from Columbus
