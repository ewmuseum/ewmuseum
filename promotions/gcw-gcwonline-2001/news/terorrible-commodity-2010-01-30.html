Terorrible Commodity

30 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW Website Hacked and Terror's Profile to Blame. GCW’s website recently suffered an outage that caused much consternation among it’s millions of fans and had them scratching their heads. “What, GCW can’t pay its bills on time?” columnist Jason Reed wrote in an internet blog that pounced upon the outage almost as soon as it occurred. But the truth was much stranger than any of the fiction imagined by internet smarks trying to take pot shots at the Global federation. The website was shut down for a violation of its terms of service for posting “lewd and inappropriate images and text.” Apparently someone hacked Jay Terror’s bio page and rewrote it to include things like Accomplishments: Biggest Jerkface of 2009, Winner of the First Inaugural Waikiki Banana Eating Contest, and Littleton Arts Society Painting Fair Honorary Award. This last apparently referred to another frightening entry which listed that in his spare time he liked to “Paint petunias while wearing women’s underwear.” The images that caused the site to be yanked were said to be related as well. “Yeah, um… someone out there can really Photoshop,” said Vance Whitter of Global Communications Network’s cyber crime division, of the apparent hacking incident. “I mean the thought of Jay Terror in women’s underwear is bad enough, but did this cyber-terrorist really have to go that far?” Whitter assured us that the rest of the site was clean and new security measures were already in place to protect sensitive information. Asked who might be responsible Whitter merely said, “Oh I have no idea. We’re not going to be able to trace what they did. GCW people might be better placed to find out.” Will they? And what further risks does GCW and in particular, the Terrorible one’s reputation face at the hands of this unknown cyber miscreant?
