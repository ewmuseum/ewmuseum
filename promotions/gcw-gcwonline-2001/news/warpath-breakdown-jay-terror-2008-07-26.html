Warpath Breakdown: Jay Terror

26 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Warpath just days away, James Bryan joins us to break down the exciting Main Event match between Vivica J. Valentine, Jay Terror and Jordan White. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship is on the line, and this is shaping up to be one of the most competitive GCW Title matches in history. JB will be breaking down each competitor's strengths, weaknesses and motivations, and we'll cap it off with the staff's prediction. Today: Jay Terror Introduction: Once one of the most beloved up-and-comers in GCW, Jay Terror's career has taken a sad turn. As his fame grew so did his ego, making him an easy target for GCW President Steven Caldera. Caldera told Terror what he wanted to hear, tempting him to the side of The Establishment with the promise of the fame and fortune he deserved. Say what you will about Terror's personal choices; he remains one of the most talented wrestlers in the world, and things seem to be shaping up in Terror's favor. President Caldera won't be there to help him this time, but he might not need it. Terror is in top form and has been receiving intensive training from the best trainers in the world. Terror will come into this match in top shape and with loads of confidence, and to further his edge, he doesn't need to pin Valentine to win the title. Terror's ascension is almost complete; he needs only to win the title to finish off his journey to the top. Biggest Strengths: Terror is fast becoming one of the most well-rounded wrestlers in GCW. Though he made it to this point on the strength of his ability to improvise, Caldera's training has also shown him the virtues of adhering to a strong gameplan. Look for Terror to come into this match with a great strategy (no doubt formulated by Caldera), and he'll be able to improvise if things don't go according to plan. This makes Terror very unpredictable, and very dangerous. Biggest Weaknesses: While there is no doubt Terror is a championship-level talent, he is not very tested at the top. Terror's only top-tier matches came against Jordan White at Rampage and LeStatt Knight at NC-17. Terror lost to Knight and had help against White. Still, Terror's confidence should help negate this. He can't match Valentine's speed or White's strength, though Terror has a knack for wild offense that tends to be difficult to counter. Biggest Career Victory: Defeating Jordan White at Rampage to become Number One Contender. This was a tremendous match where Terror's overall Round Robin victory was complete. But here lies the biggest knock on Terror once again: he had help. Terror has never won a top-level match without the aid of The Establishment. The Establishment won't be able to help him at Warpath. What He Needs To Do To Win: Keep his head. The critics are shouting loudly about Terror's lack of big victories, but Terror doesn't seem to be aware of it. He's oozing with confidence and there doesn't seem to be a doubt in his mind that he is a few short days away from becoming GCW Champion. But he needs to come in with the appropriate level of respect for his opponents, otherwise he'll be in for a surprise. This should be an incredibly intense war, and Terror needs to be ready for the competition. How He Matches Up With White: Terror always seems to be one step ahead of Jordan White, perhaps because he knows White better than anyone else. Their short friendship during the start of 2008 may have given Terror some special insight that he continues to exploit to his advantage. Physically they are very similar. White has the edge in size, but Terror has an edge in quickness and agility. Both are gifted brawlers, but Terror's recent training might give him an edge in other facets of wrestling. How He Matches Up With Valentine: Terror's exceptionally fast, but Valentine has world-class speed. Terror will be able to manage Valentine's speed, but he can't match it. Valentine's speed advantage won't be so significant here, but Terror definitely wants to slow the pace when he's matched up with the Champion. Valentine's proven on the big stage, and she definitely won't be lacking confidence. Terror will need to be mentally prepared for Valentine, because she's on top of her game. Motivation: Jay Terror's story is about to come full circle, and the word for his story is ascension. While Jordan White is still trying to find himself, Terror found himself in The Establishment and the results have been stunning. There's no denying Terror's talents, but some remains skeptical about his legitimacy in the main event. Terror will need to win this match and capture the title to silence all the doubters. But Terror doesn't doubt himself one bit. That much has become clear. So deeply does Terror believe in his destiny that he turned on his friend Jordan White at Rampage in order to assure his title shot. Valentine may have foiled Terror by adding White to this match, but make no doubt about it: the spotlight is on Jay Terror in this match, and he wants it that way. There's been tremendous buzz around the company lately that the stars seem to be aligning in Terror's favor. That's a difficult notion to argue with; Terror's personality has become so brash and so massive that it has dominated GCW as of late. He really has become larger than life, a quality no doubt inherited from his current mentor. But Terror is his own man, not merely another Caldera clone. Terror arrived in GCW last year and quickly won the Television Title. He parlayed that into a share of the NC-17 main event against LeStatt Knight, a match that won the 2007 Match of the Year award. Terror has been on a steady and inevitable climb to the top ever since his debut, and now his moment has come. At Warpath he will finally have the opportunity to complete his climb and reach the peak. The ramifications are deep: if Terror wins, not only will he have completed his ascension, but he will have consolidated all of GCW's power into The Establishment. There is a lot at stake for Jay Terror and for GCW in this match. But it remains to be seen if Terror can deliver. Next Up: Vivica J. Valentine: Tomorrow Staff Predictions: Monday
