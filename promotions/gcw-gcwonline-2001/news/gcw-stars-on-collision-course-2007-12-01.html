GCW Stars on Collision Course?

1 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the World Title picture set for the remainder of 2007 with Vivica J. Valentine and Lia Ambrosi scheduled to meet at NC-17, some have turned an eye to the new year. And what they've seen is a future in which the World Title will be more hotly contested than ever. In particular, three GCW stars appear to be headed on a collision course to settle who among them will challenge for the title in the new year. Jordan White, Jay Terror and LeStatt Knight are all working to stake their claims, but it would appear as though only one will earn the right to challenge either Ambrosi or Valentine in the new year. White recently had his first World Title opportunity against Lia Ambrosi, but fell just short. However, since White abandoned The Establishment, he has raised his game to a new level. White has been bristling with confidence and is said to be more active than ever, indicating that the loss has ignited a new fire in the controversial star. White had his rematch clause revoked upon leaving The Establishment, but at the rate he is improving he might earn another shot sooner than later. Terror is on a similar path. Though he lost the Television Title to Tyson DeBough at Dangerous Games, it is readily apparent that Jay Terror is the next big star in GCW. Terror is wildly popular both in the crowd and backstage, and his in-ring skills are also rapidly improving. However, though he is undoubtedly nearing the top ranks of GCW, he must first get through LeStatt Knight. Terror and Knight are expected to face off at NC-17 in a battle that could go a long way to settling this debate. But where Terror has risen out of obscurity, Knight has been working hard to reclaim his past glory. And so far, so good for the former two-time champion. Knight was shut out of the World Title picture in 2007 during Lia Ambrosi's amazing run, and those close to the situation say that Knight has never been more motivated to reach the top. If Terror and Knight do, in fact, meet at NC-17, it would represent one of the biggest bouts of the year. And that's not to mention White's ACWF match against two-time champion Rich Rollins. Whatever happens, the World Title scene in 2008 stands to be a highly contested one. Whoever walks out of NC-17 with the belt might face the toughest challenges of any GCW World Champion in the history of the company. But which of these three stars emerges will largely be decided at NC-17 and in the first weeks of 2008. And, of course, there is always the possibility that someone entirely new rises to push them all aside...
