Taylor Bent On Making An Impact

8 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW rookie Zachariah Taylor arrived on the scene with almost zero experience, hired on potential alone and, some say, on the insistence of Chris Bagwell. Just a few months later, Taylor is poised to make a major impact on GCW. Taylor's in-ring improvement has been visibly torrid. He's learned at an exponential rate, dumping former friend Bagwell to continue his rise untethered. Taylor's rise came to a head at Dangerous Games, when he defeated Digital Mortality and Jacob McKail in a triple threat match to claim the United States Title. His reign lasted roughly two hours. Garbage Bag Johnny, the American Dream holder at the time, cashed in his prize during the Dangerous Games match and stole the title away from Taylor. Since then, Taylor has had his sights set on regaining the gold and exacting revenge on Garbage Bag Johnny. This week on WorldWide, Taylor ratcheted things up another notch. The current American Dream holder, Jacob McKail, informed Garbage Bag Johnny that he would be cashing in and taking his US Title shot that very night. Taylor, the defacto number one contender to the US Title, will be getting his shot at NC-17 regardless of who the champion is. But Taylor had no interest in seeing McKail win the title. Rather than let McKail cash in, Taylor assaulted Johnny, rendering him incapable of defending his belt. For Taylor, the match with Garbage Bag Johnny, now official, represents more than a shot at the US Title. It's an opportunity to pay Johnny back by ending his streak, a feat that no doubt would catapult Taylor to even greater heights in GCW. Garbage Bag Johnny's streak is one of the most remarkable accomplishments GCW has seen in some time. Even Andy Murray, who arguably has had the most dominant year of anyone, has not accomplished the kind of winning streak Johnny has. And the streak has come against quality opposition. The crown jewel in the win streak? A WorldWide 100 victory over now World Champion Jordan White. But with the streak still expanding, Johnny finds himself the center of great attention. And he's never faced an opponent as motivated as Zachariah Taylor. Taylor wants the US Title and he wants to put the first mark in Garbage Bag Johnny's loss column. Taylor has a huge challenge ahead of him at NC-17. But if he can accomplish what he seems so bent on doing, he will have made himself a major star in GCW, in a very short period of time. Garbage Bag Johnny has worked hard to make himself a superstar. But Taylor can vault him if he can pin him on Pay-Per-View.
