Banned & Exiled~! Make History

5 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling’s latest PPV, NC-17, was an amazing event. However, not only was it an amazing night, it was also a record-breaking night. In what was one of the most unusual and certainly controversial matches in GCW history; the huge fan favorite team of Banned & Exiled~! accomplished what no other tag team in GCW has ever done. While the duo is already the longest reigning tag team champions (and longest reign for any title at 430 days), but now it seems they can also add another record to their list. By defeating Oblivion and reclaiming the tag team championships belts, Banned & Exiled~! has became the first team in GCW history to win the World Tag Team Championship belts twice. As surprising as it may seem, no tag team in GCW’s history has ever reclaimed the belts after losing them, that is, until NC-17. This will actually be Markus Stone’s second time as tag team champion, but the third time for his partner. On account of a technicality, this is actually Chris Storm’s third reign as tag team champion. While at the time it was not mentioned, after LeStatt Knight had ended Banned & Exiled~! first record breaking title reign, he was forced into picking a partner by President Steven Caldera. At WorldWide 91, on June 18th, 2008, LeStatt Knight named Chris Storm his partner. Under GCW rules, this constituted as his second reign as World Tag Team champion since he had previously lost the belts. By amassing his third reign as champion, Chris Storm passes Jason O’Neil, who had just been inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame, for the most World Tag Team championships by a single wrestler. Whether their second reign as champions is as long as their first remains to be seen, but what is sure is that regardless of how long they hold onto the belts this time around, their status as GCW’s Greatest Tag Team ever appears to be as strong as ever.
