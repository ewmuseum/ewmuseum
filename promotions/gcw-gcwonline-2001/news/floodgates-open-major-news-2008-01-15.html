Floodgates Open: Major News

15 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW has made its 2008 debut, and WorldWide hit Dallas with a bang. And Vivica J. Valentine never saw it coming. The new World Champion addressed the crowd to celebrate her tremendous victory at NC-17, but there was little time for boasting. Before long, everyone with even the slimmest claim to challenge for the belt had arrived to make their case, and the result was a massive tag team main event. While Rich Rollins and LeStatt Knight of The Establishment were victorious at NC-17, Jay Terror, Jordan White and former champion Lia Ambrosi all made an attempt to position themselves for the first title shot of 2008. But by the end of the show it was an unexpected figure stepping up and making the most compelling case of all. United States Champion Shaman entered the ring and assaulted Valentine, signaling his intention to chase the biggest prize in GCW. Valentine has yet to respond, but is expected to do so next week on WorldWide. Needless to say, Valentine, who won the tag match to begin the year on a high note, will not let Shaman's actions go unanswered. Meanwhile, GCW made a major announcement following the conclusion of the night's broadcast. In an effort to resolve the crowded line forming behind Valentine, a Round Robin will begin immediately between Knight, Rollins, Terror, White and Ambrosi. The Round Robin will take place over five shows, with two matches per episode. Each week one wrestler will have a bye. The wrestler who finishes the Round Robin with the most victories will become the Number One Contender to Valentine's title. The schedule has been set and will look like this: WorldWide 82 Ambrosi vs. White Knight vs. Terror Bye: Rollins WorldWide 83 Rollins vs. Ambrosi Knight vs. White Bye: Terror WorldWide 84 Rollins vs. Terror Ambrosi vs. Knight Bye: White WorldWide 85 Rollins vs. White Ambrosi vs. Terror Bye: Knight WorldWide 86 White vs. Terror Rollins vs. Knight Bye: Ambrosi Rampage will follow WorldWide 86 at the conclusion of the Round Robin. This means WorldWide next week will feature rematches from Dangerous Games and NC-17. Ambrosi and White fought an extremely close match for the World Title at Dangerous Games, and Knight and Terror went at it in a match later dubbed the Match of the Year. With such huge matches every week, WorldWide stands to do record ratings over the next two months. Also next week, details of the Rampage Tournament will be revealed, and the tournament is expected to begin within two weeks! Stay tuned for much, much more as these exciting situations continue to develop. And don't miss WorldWide next week as this amazing Round Robin kicks off with two massive matchups!
