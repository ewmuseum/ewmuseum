GCW To Host PTC Title Match

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling announced today that it will be hosting the next PTC Unified Title Match on WorldWide 83 in St. Louis. GCW officials had been in negotiations with PTC leaders for the past several weeks to bring the big match to WorldWide, and those negotiations appear to have been successful. The title, previously held by GCW names Seymour Almasy, Rich Rollins, Lia Ambrosi and Kimbusa, is currently held by Clinton Sage, formerly of FUSE. And after Sage's last appearance in GCW, he shouldn't expect a warm welcome. Sage last appeared in GCW at Dangerous Games last year, where he made his feelings towards the company quite clear. Despite the ire Sage drew from the GCW crowd, his free agent status nonetheless has led to speculation that President Caldera may be chasing the controversial star. Of course, that appears to be idle speculation only. Sage's departure from FUSE was followed by a relatively quiet period from the usually verbose wrestler, and this title defense will mark his first venture into the ring of 2007. Sage will take on PRIME wrestler Tchu in what should be a very closely contested battle. The PTC Unified Title, the merger of the former Global and Extreme championships, is a highly esteemed belt that is contested between GCW, PRIME and FUSE. GCW is hoping that by hosting this event, it will bring attention back upon the promotion and allow the company to receive a title shot for itself. Still, the final WorldWide before GCW leaves for Japan is shaping up to be a tremendous sendoff. With two Round Robin matches, a World Title defense and the exciting matchup between Tchu and Sage for the Unified Title, WorldWide in St. Louis should prove to be a major event.
