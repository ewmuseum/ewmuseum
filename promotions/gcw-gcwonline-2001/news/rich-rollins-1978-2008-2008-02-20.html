Rich Rollins, 1978-2008

20 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CHICAGO (CNN) -- Professional wrestler Richard Rollins was found dead Thursday morning of a possible drug overdose in a South Chicago apartment, the Chicago Police Department said. Rollins was unresponsive when a housekeeper found him in bed, surrounded by "an odd collection of sleeping pills, pain killers and other prescription medication," she said. Both Olsen twins were reportedly found sleeping beside him, oblivious to the deceased superstar?s condition. The award-winning athlete was 29. Rollins was found naked and unresponsive, facedown on the floor at the foot of his bed by a housekeeper trying to wake him for an appointment with a psychiatrist, said police spokesman Samuel Singer. "Pills were found in the vicinity of the bed," he told CNN. "This is being looked at as a possible overdose, but that is not confirmed yet." He stressed police have made no determination of the cause of Rollins? death -- that would be done by the medical examiner. He said the pills were not "scattered about." No note was found and there was no indication of foul play. Rollins was found at about 3 p.m., and was pronounced dead at the scene by emergency personnel about 3:30 p.m. The Olsons have since been questioned, but their testimony was nullified after they stormed out of the station, presumably because the investigating detective kept collectively calling them "Michelle." When asked for a comment, Rollins? employer and owner of Global Championship Wrestling Steven Caldera was unavailable. Speculation has already begun over the true cause of Rollins? untimely demise. Co-workers had begun noticing "a severe lack of awareness" in his recent outings in the squared circle, noting that he "seemed distracted, uncooperative." He had been getting very little sleep due to the extreme travel schedule his position in the company required, and as such began taking a number of prescribed medications to compensate. In addition, mounting litigation over his assault of a Westboro Baptist preacher seemed to weigh heavily on Rollins? mind in recent weeks. Pastor Phelps and his congregation plan to picket the dead wrestler’s funeral. In what seems to be another chapter in the recent string of high-profile deaths of young performers, Rich Rollins has made his statement clear. He died in the very manner in which he lived: quick, over-the-top, and mired in controversy. More on this story as it happens.
