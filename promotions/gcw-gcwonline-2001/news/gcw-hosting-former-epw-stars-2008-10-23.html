GCW Hosting Former EPW Stars?

23 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the news that GCW rival EPW has closed, it seems President Caldera and GCW are swooping in to add more premier talent to the GCW roster. Already confirmed (but not official) is the acquisition of Alex Grey. Grey last competed in GCW in 2002, where he was considered one of the company's top rising stars. Grey has since gone on to carve out a very successful career, and returns to GCW to finish what he started. But GCW is not likely to stop there. The latest rumors say that GCW is hosting several former EPW stars at this week's Dangerous Games Pay-Per-View. While it is not known if any of these stars will sign in the near future, it seems likely that the event is intended to woo them towards GCW contracts. GCW has been diligent in protecting the names of the invited stars. Also rumored to be near a contract is the tag team Rollin Large. Those hoping for some new faces during the Dangerous Games match shouldn't hold their breath, however. GCW is suggesting that the Dangerous Games lineup is set and there aren't likely to be any last-minute additions or subtractions. Still, tune in to GCW in the coming weeks as it is likely several new wrestlers will debut; President Caldera usually lands his targeted talent.
