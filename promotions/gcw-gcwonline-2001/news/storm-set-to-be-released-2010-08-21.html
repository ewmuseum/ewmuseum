Storm Set to be Released

21 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW ready to release troubled superstar as soon as High Stakes It looks like Chris Storm may finally get his wish. GCW headquarters has released a statement following Chris' latest antics stating that it will void out his contract if he wishes. GCW Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny, one of the people most upset with what Chris did, even went as far as to say he will personally hand Chris Storm his contract and rip it up with him. Chris Storm is expected to make a statement at High Stakes regarding his plans for his future in GCW. Will it be the last time people see the former Tag Team Champion in a GCW ring? Known for his antics inside and out of the ring, Chris Storm has seemingly taken it to a whole new level these past weeks as he attempts to get out of his contract. He skipped out on his World title match, tried to destroy the Commissioner's office, has viciously attacked the Head of Security, Toad, has cut from shows early, and threw matches by attacking his fellow competitors with a steel chair. But this looks like the final straw for one of GCW's most controversial wrestlers.
