Shipley's Business in Tatters: Bankruptcy Declared, Assets Seized

6 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just Wrestling's future is in doubt after Jody Monroe flees mounting debts. In a shocking turn of events this past week, the independent touring promotion owned by Tim Shipley (and previously frequented by GCW's Cheap Labor and "Normal" John Johnson, among many other wrestling luminaries), Just Wrestling, found its future hanging by a thread after its Canadian Tour collapsed into chaos and disrepute. Shipley's number two, Jody Monroe, has been single-handedly running the promotion for some months now, but recent pressures, such as her struggle to attract star names and the unprecedented $100,000 bond paid to take then-champion The Back Alley Brawler out of jail for the tour, clearly took their toll on the experienced businesswoman. Monroe disappeared before the second show of the Canadian Tour and is believed to be in hiding. Given the strong links between GCW and Just, it is a great shame to see the credit crunch claim another victim. We can only hope Just will recover, but Shipley's flight from America with the Just Wrestling Championship belt in tow, as seen on WorldWide 130, will only further antagonise investigators who are looking to seize any and all assets to repay Just's debts. With Shipley, who is the top seed for the ongoing Rampage Tournament, presently embroiled in an unfolding war with former GCW World Heavyweight Champion Andy Murray, it will be interesting to see how this week's events influence the Englishman's state of mind at WorldWide 131 on Monday. In the main event, he forms an unlikely team with bitter foe Murray, who returns from a rib injury sustained at the hands of Shipley and his hired hand Cheap Labor. They face the familiar duo of Jay Terror and Clyde Fox, two of GCW's Gold Star contenders who will fight for the World Heavyweight Title at Rampage.
