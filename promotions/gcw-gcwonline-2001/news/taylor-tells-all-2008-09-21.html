Taylor Tells All

21 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a big night for the Former Television Champion, Zacharia Taylor sat down with Exclusive GCW Reporter Sally Ford for his first sit-down interview since he came into GCW in January. He was still fired up from the night, making him a lot more talkative than the strong silent one he is known to be. Sally Ford: So, Zacharia Taylor. It has been quite some time since I have been able to talk to you one-on-one. A lot has happened, and you have created some name for yourself. How do you look at yourself now as opposed to when you first came into GCW? Zacharia Taylor: Well, Sally, I came into the wrestling scene as a way to control myself, or my aggression for that matter. I needed a way to get rid of it, and Chris Bagwell told me that this was the way to do it. I have learned to control myself a lot, being a lot more passive than aggressive. SF: You say passive, but everyone knows you are nothing even remotely passive in the ring. Not to mention when you get fired up, with The Movement or even tonight with McKail, you seem to turn into your old self. ZT: There are always exceptions to the case of any person. I try not to lose control and keep myself cool. Sometimes my emotions get the best of me. SF: Going back to Chris Bagwell, what happen with the two of you? ZT: Chris takes things too far. He does not now when it is time to end things. I realized this not only after I stepped away from the movement, but when he continually attacked Andy Murray in the parking lot that one WorldWide, nearly killing him. I realized that he was bringing me down and I needed to end it. SF: What are your thoughts on Bagwell and Clyde? They are always together anymore and it would seem that he has a terrorizing control on Clyde, much like he did on you. ZT: I would have to disagree with part of that statement, Sally. He could never make me do things like he is trying to get Clyde to do. I would never ruin a match by introducing weapons, brass knuckles, into a fair match! Nor would I ever follow him relentlessly no matter what he wanted me to do. No, I was not like this. SF: I'm sorry, Zacharia, my mistake. What are your thoughts on the alliance though? ZT: It is not an alliance, by any means. If it were, Clyde would have ended our match tonight the way Chris wanted. If I had lost, I would have been right there after Chris, but circumstances changed. It will not last long, I will make sure of it. SF: I hope so, for Clyde's sake. Let me ask you, how do you feel about what is going on in the US Division right now? Pretty heated! ZT: [laughs] Yes, I would have to agree with that. We have a much more active and close division than anywhere else in GCW right now. In the Television Division, I knew my place and I knew I would win. I would see someone as a challenger, and then break them down to nothing again. Here, I can't do that. We have Digital Mortality as our champion, even though I lost to him last week, we have a mutual respect for one another, and know that our match at WorldWide 100 can go either way. We then have Clyde Fox, who we all know is a great contender and adds variety to every situation. Jacob McKail who has been hit or miss, but you also have to remember that the division we are in is very tight. SF: I would agree that it is pretty stacked, but what Garbage Bag... ZT: Johnny. See, I am not sure if I could say that he is in our division. I mean, where else would you place him, if not in the US? I see him as in the same league with Andy Murray and Chris Bagwell, they are not really in any division, but they are Upper-Card wrestlers. Yes, he won the American Dream Match, but I don't see him cashing it in right now. I don't think he is looking at he US Belt right now. Think about it, if he is running around with the likes of Andy and Chris, the US Belt will hold him back. SF: Hold him back, yet you have your eyes all over it. ZT: This is different Sally. I have my eyes on it because I am nowhere near he level of the World Title. SF: Some may argue that you are ready... ZT: Whatever they may argue, I know that I am not ready. I am still very new to this, where many of these guys have a decade more experience than I do. I see it this way, I set the record for the Television Champion as the longest holding champ, then it was obviously time for me to lose it, and I did, at Warpath. Now, I have my eyes on the US Belt, but look Sally, I just got into this division and they threw me up against LeStatt Knight and Digital Mortality, and came close. I am ready for this, and I want to make it the most exciting division, just like I tried with the Television Division. SF: A little confident, maybe? ZT: Yes, very. If I almost beat Digital Mortality when the belt was not on the line, I think I can beat him when it is on the line. Then my challenge is Digital, McKail, and Fox, where I would have beat all them before. Unless Johnny gets back into this division as a main contender, I will be set. SF: You have been through a lot, always making yourself present in GCW. Whether it was the new guy from the Movement under Bagwell, wining the TV Belt, fighting against the Movement with Andy and Clyde, or hanging with the likes of Digital Mortality and LeStatt Knight. Do you have any plans for after Dangerous Games? ZT: I have ideas more than plans. Everything will change based on if I win the belt, or how far I go in Dangerous Games. If I beat Digital at 100, then what is there to offer at DG for me as a contender? If I go far in DG, who knows. The sky is the limit. SF: I have never seen you this hyped up before, Zacharia. Your position in GCW has you feeling good, and I am glad to see that. Before you get going, let me ask you what you think will be the final four at DG... ZT: Well, I think that it will be close. Fourth, third, and second will be close between Andy Murray, Chris Bagwell, and Vivica Valentine. Who will place what, I am not sure, but I think it is obvious that the winner will be Knight. He has done everything that you can imagine in GCW, I think he needs this, and he will do anything to get it. SF: Well, thank you very much Zacharia, it was a pleasure. ZT: The pleasure is all mine.
