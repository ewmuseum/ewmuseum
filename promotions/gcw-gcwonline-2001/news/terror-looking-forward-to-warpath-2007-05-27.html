Terror Looking Forward to Warpath

27 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After losing to Jayce Bradley in his first match on GCW TV, it seems that the resident smoker has had a flame lit underneath him. For three matches he has gone and pushed out his best efforts in order to attain the Televsion title and respect from others backstage. Coming from the small town of Bow New Hampshire, the young star and Daniel Franco have left a slight distaste in the mouths of Jimmy Thunder, Damon Aldair, and most recently Brock Sheperd. 'His confidence level just keeps getting higher,' Daniel Franco said with us at an interview after Jay's win at Worldwide 68. 'He is realizing that he has the talent needed to come up with some strong victories in the sport.' Franco, the biggest push Jay has, has made sure that he puts forth all of his effort in his matches in the GCW ring. So far, it has payed off. This week at Warpath The Outlaw is scheduled to fight Katie O'Claire for her Vangaurd title and his Television title. From what we understand, Terror is very excited to be competing at the level he is at such an early stage in his career. 'I think if he has his head on straight and has a little extra push from me, he'll be the one to unify the vanguard and tv titles and lead the new faces of GCW up the ranks.' Daniel added. Be sure to check out Jay Terror in action at Warpath and see if Franco's confidence will prevail.
