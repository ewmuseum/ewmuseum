Tempest Suffers a Broken Neck

25 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Dangerous Games on the horizon, is the Sparkly One gambling with her life? Whether it was by Triumph Frost, Rasa, or even Vivica Valentine that was the straw that broke the camel’s back (or neck in this case), word on the low down is that Tempest’s neck is in fact broken. While the girl who glitters is notoriously cat like, private in all things, but especially guarded about revealing anything about her health, ole Jimmy has his sources. And those sources said at least a vertebra or two is fractured. Those sources, who were more willing to talk after a few margaritas and some really rough karaoke, indicated that x-rays they had seen showed evidence of hyperextension, the fractures and an enlarged disk. Apparently there’s some evidence that she’s already been treated to some degree although it’s hard to guess what. Tempest has always been pretty on top of her health, kind of a nut really. She employs chiropractors, massage therapists, acupuncturists, herbalists and all sorts of whackos to help her stay ring ready. So who knows what she’s been trying to do to deal with this or for how long? While the extent of the damage may not be clear, two things are: It’s worse than she’s letting anyone know and Tempest’s days in wrestling may be numbered if she doesn’t take a break, or at least stop getting into wars she can’t handle. And no amount of Oolong tea and mud baths will make that better. Finding further evidence has been tricky though. Things got real quiet after Jimmy made this little discovery and apparently her sweetheart, one Troy Rivers, is part of the carpenters union who seems to be chummy with the garbage collectors union and voila – trash taken right out their door to the truck and crushed. It’s getting so an honest muckraker can’t make a living anymore. But enough of my problems. What does the future hold for our sparkly little Cyberpunk Goddess? Will she be able to reap revenge on Rasa, Frost, Zenith, Valentine? Will she call in the damn 82nd Airborne like a normal person? Time will tell, but with any luck I’ll find out first. Between the Dirtsheets Jimmy Brazzo
