Dangerous Games: First Look

27 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Dangerous Games 2008 right around the corner, the GCW staff is taking an early look at the big event. While the actual list of qualifying entrants has not been released, a number of names are surfacing as early favorites. Much can change in the next three weeks, but for now we've asked our crew to glance ahead and handicap the match. Sally Ford's Favorites Bryan Mayhem 8/1: The biggest man in the match is in the best shape of his life and may be unstoppable. Andy Murray 6/1: The 2008 Rampage Winner wins every big match he's in. This could be more of the same. Chris Bagwell 6/1: Murray may have beaten out Bagwell one on one, but this is a different situation and Bagwell is hungry. Sally Ford's Sleeper Mr. Danger 20/1: President Caldera's former boss has looked impressive despite his age. Nobody wants it more than him, and he could surprise. Sally Ford's Winner LeStatt Knight 2/1: Knight's almost even-money to win. He looks to be in the best shape of his career and is hellbent on winning this match. It's going to be tough to deny him this year. Barry Goldstein's Favorites Tyson DeBough 10/1: If you don't think the Establishment member will have an advantage, you don't know wrestling. LeStatt Knight 4/1: He wants it bad and he's the best in the business. What else needs to be said? Vivica J. Valentine 3/1: It pains me to do it, but I've got to give Valentine a chance to become the first ever two-time winner. Barry Goldstein's Sleeper Brad Jackson 15/1: Jackson's been on the brink of greatness but injuries keep derailing him. He could shock some people and win this match. Barry Goldstein's Winner Bryan Mayhem 3/1: He's big, he's nasty, he's desperate to get back to the World Title level. Mayhem's got all the Dangerous Games records; now he gets his win. Kevin Jacobs' Favorites Andy Murray 7/1: Does this guy ever lose? No. Tough to pick against him in big matches. Clyde Fox 9/1: Laugh if you want, but despite his recent emotional issues he's developed into a subtly superb wrestler. Tessa Windsor 6/1: The former US Champion is taking her game to the next level, and this could be her night to streak into the spotlight. Kevin Jacobs' Sleeper Zachariah Taylor 15/1: He's got a US Title shot at WW100, and the DG winner usually comes from the US Title scene. That bodes well for the fast-rising Taylor. Kevin Jacobs' Winner Vivica J. Valentine 2/1: I'm putting my money on Valentine. She's won it before and she desperately wants to win it again. Never ever bet against the Fearless Phenom. David Yale's Favorites Shaman 10/1: Everyone's raving about Mayhem, but Shaman beat his Oblivion teammate at Warpath. Tessa Windsor 6/1: They say little women can't compete in these sorts of matches, but Ambrosi and Valentine are the last two winners. LeStatt Knight 3/1: What, you think I wasn't going to mention the greatest champion of all time? David Yale's Sleeper Jacob McKail 25/1: This guy has been nothing short of excellent since his return, and everyone's overlooking him. Watch out for McKail. David Yale's Winner Chris Bagwell 1/1: That's right, I'm guaranteeing it! Nobody else should even bother showing up, because Bagwell's going to headline NC-17 this year. Period. James Bryan's Favorites Lia Ambrosi 12/1: She's been quiet lately. Too quiet. That should make people afraid. Vivica J. Valentine 5/1: Yes, she's won it before and should be favored, but everyone will be targeting her. She'll have a tough time repeating. LeStatt Knight 4/1: People can target Knight all they want, but I don't see him going down easily. Could be his year. James Bryan's Sleeper Garbage Bag Johnny 12/1: He's never lost in GCW, yet nobody's looking at him. His amazing reign could continue with a shocking Dangerous Games victory early in his tenure. James Bryan's Winner Andy Murray 3/1: Murray's had one of the most amazing years I've ever seen. He debuted at NC-17 2007; he'll be headlining NC-17 2008 with a Dangerous Games victory. Stay tuned for much, much more from the GCW staff as Dangerous Games approaches. We'll be back to handicap the entire field, get predictions from some friends outside of the company, and take a look at the rest of the card as soon as it's announced.
