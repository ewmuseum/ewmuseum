Dangerous Games Preview

6 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With GCW's most highly anticipated event on the horizon, GCW has asked some of its long-time employees to preview the event. This week, long-time GCW play-by-play man James Bryan handicaps the field and makes his pick as this year's winner. Hello, everyone, James Bryan here with a look at how the GCW roster will fare in the 2007 Dangerous Games. Naturally these aren't very scientific; this match is more of a crapshoot than anything and it, without a doubt, the hardest match to win in professional wrestling. That said, we can make a pretty good guess. So here we go: Phil Atken 24/1 - Atken is a supremely talented wrestler and everyone backstage is really high on him. That said, he's just getting started here in GCW and the inexperience usually means trouble. Chris Cross 50/1 - Cross has had a hard time getting on camera. Doubtful he's even on the roster by then. Tyson DeBough 15/1 - Tyson DeBough has looked great lately, and he's been running circles around the real TV champion Jay Terror. He could be a sleeper to make noise, but his odds still aren't real high given his inexperience. Hydra 50/1 - You just never know when this guy is going to show up. Unfortunately, he usually doesn't. Brad Jackson 15/1 - Here's another one of my potential sleeper picks. Jackson has looked phenomenal since debuting and could shock some people. I wouldn't be totally surprised to see him make the final four. Still, with all the other talent in the ring it'll be tough for Jackson to go all the way. LeStatt Knight 3/1 - How can you pick against LeStatt Knight? The greatest world champion of all time is at his best in big-match situations. He's never been in a Dangerous Games match before (because he's usually wrestling for the world title), so inexperience will be a factor, but this is LeStatt Knight we're talking about. Definitely a favorite. Caiden Lazarra 30/1 - We recently signed Lazarra and I was really impressed by his workout. But once again, to see someone so new to the GCW environment win Dangerous Games would be remarkable and totally unprecedented. Bryan Mayhem 7/2 - This monster finally won the big one, and now he wants it back. Mayhem dominated the field last year, destroying everyone in his path en route to setting a new record for eliminations by a single wrestler. He was eventually toppled by future-rival Lia Ambrosi, but this year Lia's not there to foil him. If someone else doesn't step up I could very easily see Mayhem get back on top. Paddy O'Shea 20/1 - Paddy is one of my personal favorites, but also a guy with some personal issues. He's been off the road a bit to work things out, and we're still real high on him. If he ever got it together he could be there at the end. But I'm not sure he's there yet. Jack O'Toole 50/1 - I don't like either of the Blue Collar Boys' chances, as they're still really raw. Tommy O'Toole 50/1 - See above. Shawn Porter 20/1 - I actually really like Porter. He's an underrated talent with great potential to improve. But potential doesn't win this match; just ask Toxic. Jesse Ramey 50/1 - Ramey's been hurt and is actually not actively touring with the company. Some say he might be back in time for the contest, but it's not looking likely. Jose Rodriguez 30/1 - Rodriguez is another very promising young talent that needs some seasoning before he's really got a chance. Rikki Roxx 1000000/1 - If Rikki Roxx wins this match I'll wear a dress on camera for a year. Tiara Bell Russell 50/1 - Pleasure and Pain is a good team with good talent, but tag teams rarely fare well in this match. You'd think they'd have a better advantage, but it's just too tough an environment for anyone who isn't used to fending for themselves. Wayne Russell 50/1 - See above. Chris Storm 5/1 - Now here's an intriguing name. The Tag Team Champions have been absolutely dominant, and both members look to be in top form. However, where Markus Stone has yet to distinguish himself in singles competition, Storm has. He's a former PTC Global Champion, a high honor, and he holds a singles victory over LeStatt Knight. I don't know if Storm will be singularly focused enough to do what it takes to win this match, but if he ever got the drive he could very easily skyrocket to GCW stardom. Markus Stone 10/1 - Stone is a superior talent and has loads of potential. I was very high on him before Chris Storm arrived to form Banned and Exiled~!. But Stone isn't as accomplished as his teammate, so while there's a chance, I wouldn't say it's a great one. Shaman 5/1 - The former United States Champion is still one of the top names in this company. This guy is just so big and strong and good that it scares me sometimes. Still, Shaman has work to do and lots of room for improvement in the ring. If he steps up his game I'd say he's got a reasonable shot at winning this match. Jay Terror 4/1 - Yes, that's right. I'm going with the Television Champion to make some noise. Terror is quite possibly the single most promising athlete on the roster and a guy that could very easily make a huge name for himself at Dangerous Games. There's no doubt that President Caldera will do what he can to ensure the rebellious Terror doesn't make it out of this match with the title shot, but Terror is definitely my underdog pick to win it. Jimmy Thunder 7/1 - The current United States Champion is pound for pound the strongest guy I have ever seen. And, to set the record straight, there's nothing artificial about his gifts; Jimmy Thunder is a natural freak of nature. His power will give him a real shot at winning the match, as he'll be real tough to eliminate. Still, Thunder strikes me as a guy who is one step shy of greatness and would need to find that step before he could win it all. Tabloid 20/1 - Tabloid is a rookie who GCW scouts snatched up at the very onset of his wrestling career. In him we see endless potential, but he's going to be in the ring with 29 seasoned vets. Not good odds. Twizzy 2/1 - Twizzy is the odds-on favorite to win this match. And how could it be otherwise? Sure, he lost to Knight at Tsunami, but the fact remains that Twizzy is the only wrestler in this match to have won it before. That's a massive advantage. Twizzy still wants another shot at the World Title before he's done, and this may be his last chance to get it. Expect to see a massively motivated Twizzy in the match, a man who could become the first two-time winner. Vivica J. Valentine 4/1 - Some might be surprised that Valentine isn't higher. After all, she fell just a hair shy at Warpath of earning a World Title match at Dangerous Games. But she's got two things working against her; size and Bryan Mayhem. Though Mayhem seems to have moved on, Valentine wants Mayhem again at Dangerous Games. If Valentine comes into this match having already wrestled Bryan Mayhem, and she's already at such a disadvantage in weighing so little, I just don't like her chances. But she still has to be ranked up there because there isn't anyone more focused and driven in GCW today, and that counts for a lot. Other 15/1 - That's twenty-five names as of now. That leaves five spots open. GCW always fills them from various sources, so there's no telling who might show up. And with rumors flying that GCW is trying to reacquire former stars and guys like Rich Rollins still technically under contract, someone could definitely sneak into the match and steal it. But that said, the names under contract right now are all totally focused on this contest and won't let it go without a fight. Anyone who comes in cold will have a really tough time eliminating a field of people who have bled for months to get this opportunity. Anything can happen in this match, though, so don't assume the winner of this match is on this list. Final Four- LeStatt Knight, Twizzy, Bryan Mayhem, Jay Terror My Prediction- I'm picking Terror to be a surprise part of the Final Four, but that's as far as I'll go. He's amazing, but he's not at the level of those other three guys. Twizzy has the experience of knowing how to win one of these matches, but the truth is that he won a scaled-down version including only 20 people. I don't see it happening. That leaves Mayhem and Knight alone in the ring for the first time. And in the end, I see Knight prevailing. Mayhem's solidified himself as a sure-fire future Hall of Famer, but LeStatt Knight is in a league of his own. He rocketed out of his slump at Tsunami and served notice that he's back on track. If Knight continues to progress and gets back to his level of greatness, nobody can touch the man. That's why my pick to win the 2007 Dangerous Games and compete in the main event of NC-17 (yet again) is LeStatt Knight. As for his opponent, I'll give everyone a little bonus and breakdown the main event for the upcoming Pay-Per-View. Lia Ambrosi versus Jordan White: Mat Wrestling Lia has altered her strategy to make her attack more ground-based, and she can operate at full capacity against the smallish White. White is a total amateur on the mat and will need to keep the match hard-hitting. Edge: Ambrosi Striking Both wrestlers excel at straight brawling. Lia uses precise martial arts strikes while White basically prefers to wail on his opponent. While Lia is superb in this area, this is where White will focus his attack. Edge: White Speed White is quick and able to attack at light speed. Lia's also extremely fast and agile and can move as well as anyone in the ring. But where Ambrosi will be methodical, I predict White will take a very frenetic approach to the match, giving him a slight advantage. Edge: White High-Flying Lia has abandoned most of her aerial attacks, but she hasn't forgotten how to use them. Jordan White has a lucha background and will likely try to utilize that to his advantage. Edge: Push Strength Lia is really powerful for a woman her size, but White still has her by about a hundred pounds. Edge: White Smarts No contest here. White is sharp but still needs a lot of guidance. He's still learning the game, while Lia is one of the best ever. Edge: Ambrosi Experience Same as above. White has a lot to learn and has never been in a Pay-Per-View main event. Lia has and usually wins. Edge: Ambrosi Intangibles Lia has that experience edge and knows her way around the ring. She'll be totally prepared for this match. But Jordan White has two things going for him: momentum and Steven Caldera. With a win over Rich Rollins under his belt and President Caldera helping out along the way, White has the intangibles to negate Lia's advantages. Edge: White Pick These two match up really well and it should be a great match. But I'm picking Lia to retain. Though White has momentum, there are still big questions about his heart. Does he really love wrestling and does he really want the title as much as Lia does? I don't think so. He may have proven he deserves this match, but I think Lia will prove that he doesn't deserve that title.
