Two Steps Until The Big Show!

16 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With only two shows left to go until the 100th episode of Worldwide, one can only wonder what the superstars of GCW have in store. Rivalries will heat up and the stakes will be elevated! Match 1: Mr. Danger vs. Face Just a week ago, both of these superstars led their in-ring debuts in the GCW. Unfortunately for both men, their debuts ended sourly as Danger fell victim to Axel Action and Face to Dan Black. But another common factor in both matches was the attacks each man led on the other after the match. Despite being new to the active roster, these two seem to have a boiling history! Match 2: Joaquin Pierre & A Mystery Partner vs. The SoCal Stretching Crew The Movement shocked the world last week when they introduced their newest member, Joaquin Pierre from GLOBAL fame. The background between Pierre and Black is unknown, but it seems as though the future is looking bright for the newly reformed stable. Black, however, promised another shocker for this week when he teams Pierre up with ANOTHER new Movement member. Will the new member be an active member of the current GCW roster, or will we be seeing another shocking debut? Match 3: Keegen O'Connor vs. Dan "The Bulldozer" Black vs. Phil Atken vs. Axel Action Since winning the TV Title, Keegen O'Connor has remained in the shadows and keeping his belt warm. But the predators have finally found him and are looking to sink their teeth into some gold. The returning Phil Atken hopes to make up for his loss last week to Chris Bagwell while both Movement members, Dan Black and Axel Action, are looking to finally bring a championship back into the group. One has to wonder, however, if Axel and Black will work together -- or be pit against one another! Match 4: Samson & Delylah vs. Oblivion Since capturing the Tag Team Titles, the team of Samson & Delylah has overcome all challengers for their title belts. However, nobody expected the untimely reuniting of Bryan Mayhem and Shaman to re-form the dangerous Oblivion stable. Despite losing to the team of Andy Murray and LeStatt Knight only a week ago, this duo is still roaring with a head of steam. Could this be the end of the S&D era? Match 5: Clyde Fox vs. Zacharia Taylor vs. Jacob McKail In what is predicted to be the biggest draw of the night, three familiar superstars will once again be matched up against each other in a battle for a chance to face Digital Mortality at the Worldwide 100 Supershow. Will a Chris Bagwell-led Clyde Fox catapult himself to another shot at US title belt, or will Zacharia Taylor get the rematch from his new-found mentor? Or perhaps Jaco McKail will shock the federation and defeat both GCW veterens. Match 6: Andy Murray vs. Tessa Windsor Andy Murray has been a man on a mission as of late, coming off a gigantic win over Chris Bagwell at Warpath and then managing to coexist with LeStatt Knight just last week to overcome the returning Oblivion. However, Andy is now being faced with a close friend of LeStatt and argueably one of the greatest US Champions to date, Tessa Windsor. Tessa's recent battles with former World Champion, Vivica J. Valentine leave one to wonder -- is the Scottish King of Cool finally biting off something more than he can chew? Match 7: Chris Bagwell vs. Garbage Bag Johnny Garbage Bag Johnny has remained undefeated thus far in his short GCW career. Some have even went so far as to name him the fastest rising star that the company has seen in a while. Perhaps its his interesting sense of humor or maybe even his strange odor that's keeping him on top. Nevertheless, GBJ gets his first chance at the upper card as he goes up against the ruthless Chris Bagwell. Can Johnny keep his streak rolling or will Bagwell's wrath put an end to the newcomers coveted streak? Main Event: Jordan White vs. Jay Terror's Gauntlet, II Last week, Jordan White was able to successfully overcome the first round in Jay Terror's gauntlet by defeated fellow Establishment member, Tyson DeBough. But this week, Jay Terror promises to have a couple extra tricks up his sleeves (or under his bandana). Will White's luck continue or will "The Established" get what he wants and ruin White's chances of EVER getting another title shot... again?
