LeStatt Knight Hospitalized?

19 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There are numerous reports coming out of the Phoenix area that GCW Superstar, LeStatt Knight collapsed late Saturday night while working out in a nearby gym. Sources say he was taken to the hospital immediately by an employee of the gym which he was working out at in preparation for his big match against Jordan White. When asked about this news and what it could mean for the Main Event at NC-17, a CEi representative had this to say. "We're learning all of this just as quickly as you all are. Apparently Mr. Knight did have some complications but has already left the hospital. We have gotten no further details on why he went in or what his status is as of this moment for NC-17. Once we find you, we'll be sure to issue a statement." But when pressed the representative gave a more direct response. "As of now I'd bank on seeing LeStatt Knight at NC-17." As many know, LeStatt Knight has had a history of injuries ranging from a shattered ankle to an actual heart attack just days before a title defense against Michael Stevens back in 2005. With LeStatt already leaving the hospital it would appear this isn't quite as serious but once there's a concrete status we here at GCWonline.net will keep the fan base informed.
