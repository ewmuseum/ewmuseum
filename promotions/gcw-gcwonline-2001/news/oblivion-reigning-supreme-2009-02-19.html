Oblivion reigning supreme?

19 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the title says folks it seems Oblivion isn't quite as dead as first thought. More to the point of just Oblivion is going to be coming to World Wide 106. Two men Shaman and Kimbusa the last two or should we say most recent members of Oblivion are at odds. Kimbusa brought the fight to Shaman by demanding a match against him at 106. Shaman was seen at GCW headquarters over the weekend meeting with GCW officials. Oblivion's departure and sudden disbanding leaves GCW with a contract unfulfilled and has requested Shaman and Kimbusa uphold that contract. Shaman made a point to be at GCW headquarters to handle all odds personally. After much deliberation GCW has announced that Shaman and Kimbusa meet in the ring and settle the difference. Kimbusa was not available for comment, but Shaman left a letter explaining the decision and the process that brought this about. "GCW Officials requested i stand with my contractual agreement. There was a little clause in that contract though. Oblivion is dead if Bryan Mayhem and Shaman choose to dissolve it. Oblivion is gone and never coming back!! GCW's joke of putting Lard ass with Oblivion hastened the decision. Mayhem is gone and i am here. At World Wide 106 Kimbusa will get what he deserves for screwing Oblivion out of a title. No in fact more importantly i will finish business once and for all. He has dodged me for several years now and he gets me right in the face." No responses have been received from either camp. Shaman has shut off all communication ties with GCW as his staff states that he is training and wishes to not be disturbed. One inside source at GCW states that this is going to be a match to remember between two Legends of the sport in their own rights.
