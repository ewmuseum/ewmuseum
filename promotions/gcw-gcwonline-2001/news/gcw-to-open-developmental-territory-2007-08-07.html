GCW To Open Developmental Territory?

7 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reviving a story that has been reported for several years now, GCW is reportedly renewing interest in opening a developmental territory. With a plethora of raw talent floating around the independent circuit and the high rate of turnover recently experienced by GCW, the company is once again exploring the merits of creating a place to home-grow new talent and expand its horizons a bit. While this possibility has been extensively explored in the past, and while GCW did in fact open such a territory for a brief time in 2002, a new twist is reportedly being considered. GCW officials are rumored to be in negotiations with representatives at rival promotion PRIME to create a joint developmental territory. The concept, as it is being reported, would be to open a territory funded and operated jointly between promotions, and featuring independent talent that would be contracted with either GCW or PRIME. Both promotions would be able to send talent to the territory to grow and learn, and could then be called up to their respective parent company when deemed ready. While there has been no indication of interest from PRIME, GCW seems quite serious about creating such a promotion. With the collapse of Primetime Central and the decline of wrestling in general, a large number of wrestlers have been left without major contracts and lack the opportunity necessary to break into the remaining big promotions. A developmental territory, GCW is reasoning, would be a new opportunity to bring in fresh new talent and adapt them to the rough demands of wrestling for a promotion like GCW or PRIME. Stay tuned as more news is expected to be officially announced in the near future.
