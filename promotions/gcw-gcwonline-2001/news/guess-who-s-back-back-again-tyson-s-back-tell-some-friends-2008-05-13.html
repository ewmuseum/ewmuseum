Guess who's back? Back again? Tyson's back. Tell some friends.

13 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been month's since anyone has heard even a whisper from the normally loud mouthed Tyson DeBough. The former GCW Television Champion seemingly fell off the face of the planet. No one knew where he went, until just the past few weeks, when he showed up once, taking out Clyde Fox, and then really blasted back onto the main scene, taking out the current World Champion, Vivica J. Valentine. Now an official member of "The Establishment" he's made quite a ruckus, along side his former sworn enemy, Jay Terror and also Mr. Caldera. If you assumed that having Vivica J. Valentine as a World Champion was a defining moment for GCW, then you were wrong. If you think that currently having Banned & Exiled as the longest reigning tag team champions in the GCW is unique, you were also, wrong. And, if you think Lia Ambrosi has any shot of being anything else besides a has been, you're so far off, you may as well just bury your head in the sand. *ZING!* Of course everyone is aware of the return of Tyson DeBough by now. If you aren't, then you obviously don't pay attention to the entire show that we make you pay money to watch. He was right back in the limelight again. What better way to "establish" that you're back in the game again, by laying out the World Champion, and then hooking up with your old worst enemy to form one of the most dangerous teams in the industry? This, of course, is why Tyson is a genius. The question still remains though. Where has he been exactly? No one seems to have any real answers as to his whereabouts for the last few months. But, does that really matter? I'm sure that all will reveal itself in its own due time. After all, Tyson is the smartest person in the GCW to date. He obviously has a reason for his rhyming. Word also has it around the rumor mill that Caldera will be providing DeBough with his own security detail for the upcoming WorldWide, so as to keep anything from happening. We all know he's a marked man now. Stay tuned. More to come.
