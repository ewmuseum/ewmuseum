Where does he go from here?

19 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After suffering another defeat does the mean the end for Donny Diamond? Coming off some inside sources from GCW, there is a rumor circulating the locker room about one Donny Diamond. Following his Hardcore Title loss to GCW Superstar Bryan Mayhem, no one has seen Donny Diamond. Although we know Donny Diamond is not one to be to social with the others, he is normally seen backstage after a match. Though after promising a new era to start in GCW for Donny Diamond in an interview on WorldWide, things don't seem to be going his way. Would the Oblivion Bomb that was heard throughout the arena last week be the end for Donny Diamond? The answer is yes, or at least that's what everyone else is saying!
