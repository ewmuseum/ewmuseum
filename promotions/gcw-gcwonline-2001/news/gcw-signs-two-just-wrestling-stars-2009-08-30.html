GCW Signs Two Just Wrestling Stars

30 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From Just Wrestling: GCW has signed two major Just Wrestling stars. ALBANY, NY - Global Championship Wrestling, the worldwide televised promotion whose World Heavyweight Champion, Andy Murray, starred on Just Wrestling's Long Hot Summer tour, have this weekend confirmed the signings of Just Wrestling stars Cheap Labor and two-time Just Wrestling Champion Aaron Nothings. Nothings, who has not appeared on television since his time with Wrestling Midwest, is set to reignite a budding war with Murray that has seen him defeat the Scottish King of Cool twice on Just Wrestling shows in the last month. However, GCW will be a significantly different challenge for a competitor who is optimistically described as rough around the edges. Much has been made of how Murray, a showman at heart, struggles in the touring hothouses whose hostile atmosphere Nothings thrives upon. GCW's big open arenas will be precisely the opposite and Nothings will have to prove himself in the undercard, possibly first in dark matches as GCW look to assess whether such an unconventional performer can survive in the aestheticised world of televised wrestling. For Cheap Labor, this move represents an ironic first shot at the big time for a man who just parted ways with his longtime agent, Dave Goodenough, who has been striving so hard to get Labor a written contract with a televised promotion. Cheap looks set to be positioned as a loveable underdog in the GCW midcard, and will have to curb an inconsistent temperament to succeed - he memorably "out-Nothingsed" Aaron Nothings on the Long Hot Summer tour, not only defeating the now-JWC but looking a ragged shadow of his previously clean-cut self. Just Wrestling booker Tim Shipley, who himself has just signed to smaller promotion High Octane Wrestling, welcomed the moves. "It's great not just for Aaron and Cheap but for everyone here in Just Wrestling. It shows that with hard work and determination you can get yourself to where you want to be. I've always told the guys that people pay attention to us, much more so than other independents. Aaron and Cheap both have a heap of talent. I think Andy [Murray] hooked them up with the GCW guys and they took it from there. They can go a long way fast. Aaron holds two wins over Andy and Andy's their Champion." Asked if he would sanction a hypothetical match between the respective Champions with both belts on the line, Shipley was forthcoming. "I'd be all for that. I think it would be great to see. I don't know if it would be a pay-per-view match, because they will have plans for Andy for the coming months. But to see that as a WorldWide main event, Andy Murray and Aaron Nothings for both belts? I think that is a fantastic idea." Shipley was not concerned that the pair's jumping of ships could portray Just Wrestling as merely a feeder promotion. "I don't think that's right. We can be a stepping stone for people, sure, but Aaron and Cheap will be back, if not for the Homecoming tour then the one after, and maybe intermittent dates here and there when their schedules allow it. I think you have to have a certain strength of character to succeed in Just, and Aaron and Cheap definitely have that, and they recognise that Just put them on the map. They'll be back and it'll be good for us. It's a great deal for them because they now have what they always wanted. But it's great for us too. With the exposure Just is getting from Cheap and Aaron going to GCW, and to a certain extent from what I'm doing with HOW, we're getting a lot of publicity and it could sell more seats when we go back to New York. And that's got to be a good thing." Indeed, Shipley was philosophical about Just's status viz-a-viz the televised promotions. "That's not what we're aiming to be. We're fine just where we are, that's how I like it. I like touring and I like us not having the pressures of weekly TV, stories to fabricate. I like that we can be there for guys who need us. Josh Marquez, he was in AWC a few years back, I invited him into Just expressly as a showcase, to get himself a deal. He did a good job and SCCW took him. I like to be able to provide that for people." You can see Nothings and Cheap Labor in action on GCW WorldWide this coming week. -FROM JUST WRESTLING
