Tempest Responds to Frenzied Internet Speculation

9 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the rumor mill on overdrive, the Cyberpunk Goddess speaks on intentions, challenges and the future of Rampage. In the twenty four hours since Worldwide 131 hit the airwaves, the GCW fan forums have lit up with a firestorm of discussion, arguments, criticism, counter criticism, and more wild suggestions than even David Yale could dream up. Following the Draw ruling by referee Hal Jenkins in last night’s first round Rampage matchup between SurReal and “Normal” John Johnson after the match broke down into complete chaos, both commentary and speculation have been rampant. Everyone seems to have an opinion. There’s enough criticism to go around from those who blame the entire mess on the official for letting things get out of hand, to SurReal for bringing Christian Zenith to the ring in the first place, and indeed into his camp. Many suggest that Zenith is equally or even more responsible and is manipulating the entire scene to his own designs. A small number blame “Normal” John Johnson, but they seem to be the fans with the least spelling ability or grasp of the facts in the match. But the widest range and largest amount of commentary surrounds GCW Television Champion Tempest and her role in the chaos. Even after discarding the usual number of posts stating that Tempest only has a job because of her looks, has slept her way to the top and the rest of the usual sub-Neanderthal material, a great deal of speculation about Tempest’s motives persists. Commentary ranges from her being a completely innocent bystander to having intended to cause the whole thing, as David Yale suggested at the time. This last accusation is especially meaningful as it pertains to her having to face the winner in the second round of the Rampage tournament. I asked Tempest to comment on the matter and was surprised when she did, as she is usually somewhat aloof from fan commentary. “Well, I usually don’t feel like I have to answer for everything I do; that’s true,” she said in a phone interview. “Honestly I have no apologies to make for last night. But I think people are confused and starting to read into my actions stuff that isn’t there. I went out there to do commentary and entertain the fans, and to keep an eye on Christian Zenith. Anybody who’s been paying attention has seen that he will get into a match and swing things in SurReal’s favor. So anyone who wants to question my motives, I’d suggest they take a good hard look at the facts and question Zenith’s first.” With some trepidation I asked about the rumor that she might have intended to influence the outcome of the match, considering it was her opponent to be decided, and indeed that now she might not have one. “Just like I said last night, I don’t care who I face. I’ll face Godzilla. I’ll take ‘em both in a triple threat. I’ll take on Zenith beforehand for the right to have him locked in the back during the tournament match. Any of this crap about me trying to throw things off means I’d be afraid of these schmucks, and I’m not. At. All.” So while the TV champion may remain a mystery and a controversial figure, she has at least spoken clearly. Now all that remains to be seen is what the consequences will be and indeed, who she will face on the road to Rampage. submitted by Ayake Sonada
