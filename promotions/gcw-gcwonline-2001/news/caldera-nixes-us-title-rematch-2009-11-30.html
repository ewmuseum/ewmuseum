Caldera Nixes US Title Rematch

30 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dan Black and Axel Action have agreed on a US Title rematch, but President Caldera isn't quite ready to see it yet. This week on WorldWide, US Champion Dan Black agreed to give the former champion, Axel Action, a shot to reclaim the title. The two were prepared to meet as soon as this week at WorldWide 122, but GCW President Steven Caldera apparently had other plans. Instead of being granted his rematch this week, Axel finds himself scheduled to face off against Jay Terror in a singles bout in the main event. While Axel's first main event appearance might seem to be a fair consolation, the situation is cause for some concern. Terror, who returned two weeks ago and won the 2009 Dangerous Games, appears to be in the best shape of his career and is on his own quest to regain the GCW World Championship. With Caldera pulling Terror's strings once again, Axel would seem to have an extremely dangerous obstacle to overcome. With Black given the week off, if the US Title match occurs as expected at WorldWide 123, Axel will find himself coming off a hellish battle with Terror going into his highly anticipated bout with Black. There is much speculation about why this situation has played out as it has. Some believe that this is the doing of Dan Black's camp, which is growing increasingly influential in GCW as Black's star rises. Others believe Caldera is hoping Terror can knock Axel down a peg, as the former US Champion has seen his own career take off to new heights recently. In any event, it is becoming clear that someone is conspiring to derail the rise of Axel Action in GCW. And while it may come as little comfort to Axel, he may take some solace in the knowledge that if they're shooting at you, you must be doing something right. Axel is reportedly more than willing to accept the challenge of facing Terror in this week's main event, and has shown no reticence to take on all comers. While the odds are certainly stacked against him, Axel finds himself with the unique opportunity to defeat a Dangerous Games winner and the US Champion in consecutive weeks, which would no doubt turn his Cinderella tale into a rocket ride to the top.
