Knight to Name Number One Contender

5 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LeStatt Knight has released a statement: this week on WorldWide he will be naming his next challenger. At once the speculation has centered on the presumptive challenger-in-waiting, Andy Murray. After winning last year's Rampage tournament and finishing as the runner-up in the 2008 Dangerous Games, then defeating Jay Terror at NC-17, Murray has all the momentum in the world. The spotlight has fallen on him, and with the recent history between the two, it seems they are on the road to another showdown. Knight is expected to put the World Title on the line in this week's main event. As GCW champion, Knight wields tremendous influence. As a Hall of Fame member and a three-time champion, Knight's power as champion is unmatched. As a result, he is flexing his muscles by proving just how much stroke he has. Few champions have such freedom to hand-pick their challengers. Knight isn't immune to the influence of the company, however. Knight has reportedly been under pressure to name a challenger with Shockwave right around the corner. Knight has eagerly obliged, further fueling speculation that he has had a challenger in mind all along, and that the challenger is Andy Murray. This is far from official, and Knight isn't beyond throwing a curveball. But with Murray holding a singles victory over Knight from WorldWide 100 last year, Knight is likely to be looking for revenge. For Murray's part, the Scottish King of Cool has been relatively quiet on the subject, though he is clearly aware of his runaway momentum. Murray has dropped a few not-so-subtle hints on GCW television recently, and it is clear that he is ready to take his shot at the biggest prize in professional wrestling. This week's WorldWide should set the stage for Knight's third title reign. The legendary champion is hellbent on making this historic reign the biggest in GCW history. Knight's most legendary run came against Michael "Spitfire" Stevens, a world-class wrestler with whom Knight was able to produce some of the greatest matches of all time. Since then Knight has been waiting for another chance to defend the GCW World Title against someone who can match his legendary credentials and create the kind of history Knight is pursuing. Which bodes well for Andy Murray.
