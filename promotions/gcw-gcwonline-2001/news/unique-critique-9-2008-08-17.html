GCW — Column

Unique Critique #9

17 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Author’s Introduction: This week in Unique Critique, I will be rating the characters much like the last UC. AXEL Action While the Action character at first seemed like James von Doom-lite, he has really been making an impact within the shows. The feud with James von Doom and the eventual victory over his tag partner turned enemy at Warpath made him stand out even more for the recent cycle. His recent singles win really goes a long way at establishing him as a threat. With a change in his overall writing style, Matt definitely seems to be coming into his own with his recent work. It will be interesting to see what is next for Action. Lia Ambrosi Lia Ambrosi was sort of absent for a while, not because she was not there, but because there was really no impact to what she was doing. Her work with Bryan Mayhem was short but sweet at the same time. There was something in the air that gave the feeling that something was going on. Her victory over Almasy at Warpath was just the tip of the iceberg as it was announced that Christian Zenith would be the new GCW Commissioner. Their history together promises some sort of interaction between the two that will surely make Lia’s stock rise once more. I really haven’t seen Kat that much at the forums lately but hopefully she will be back soon to take GCW by storm once more. Seymour Almasy Seymour Almasy saw a great transformation since his arrival. With a new look and some heelish antics, he was surely on his way to the top of GCW. Something happened along the way though that created this sort of setback for Almasy. It’s been a while since his last role-play and while he is still a constant presence on the shows the recent lack of activity definitely hurts the character. I really would like to see Sean put more heel into Almasy as his work with Tessa was fantastic. Besides all that, Sean is one of those guys that always has a million ideas going through his head. Great guy to work with. Chris Bagwell What can be said about Chris Bagwell that hasn’t been said already? From the moment he made his presence felt as a GCW rookie, he has dared people inside GCW and outside to care about who Bagwell is. He has challenged some of the top talent in GCW, after probably insulting them first, and basically he has positioned himself at the top of the pile. While he may not have been able to best his nemesis Andy Murray, he came out of the feud as one of the company’s biggest heels. There is no show where you will not see Bagwell plotting to take over. Outside of the in-show presence, his interviews on the radio show are always the highlight of the night and entertaining as hell. Out of character, Shane took it upon himself to stand up when the fed hit a rough patch and lead the way. He is loyal and doesn’t mind voicing his opinion as to what is best for GCW over what he may accomplish for Bagwell and himself outside of GCW. This is one of those guys that owners would kill to have on their roster. Dan Black Dan Black is one of those guys that have been greatly helped by associating himself with Chris Bagwell. He just needed a little time to get used to the GCW way of working and now he will probably get another shot at the TV Title that Ngozi holds. His work has greatly improved since the first time he got here and at that rate, he will be a major player soon. As to out of character, I haven’t really had a chance to talk to him and I don’t think I see him that much on the forums but I would definitely like to see him around more. Digital Mortality With his recent victory DigiMon has been living up to the hype he got when he arrived. His feud with Tessa Windsor was great and really put both over in a good way. With the U.S. title over his waist, he will have to face a great challenge in the form of the American Dream. He will need to be at the top of his game every time because if he isn’t, his title reign will be a short one. Loz is very active on the boards and is always open to help anyone who asks with advice, proofreading or whatever. Tyson DeBough I’m glad that we are seeing more and more of Tyson each week. I had heard about him for a while but there was a stretch of time where he was not really active. Now, though, DeBough is all over the shows. His involvement with The Establishment really puts him in a position that not everyone can attain. He wasn’t able to win the American Dream match, but he will surely be a presence in the division in the coming months as we draw nearer to Dangerous Games. Mike is one of the few guys that I haven’t really gotten to know that much. Clyde Fox Without a doubt one of my favorite characters in the whole fed. Clyde Fox to me is the perennial underdog character that always seems to find a way to defy the odds. His presence on the shows is always funny and his work is always inspired and quite frankly hilarious. I really like that in his recent work, he is delving a little deeper into what makes Clyde Fox be Clyde Fox. Ross is a great guy to talk to. He is always willing to write a match or do whatever to help the fed out. Consistent guys like Ross are what a fed needs to be great. Garbage Bag Johnny With his undefeated streak and the American Dream match under his belt, GBJ is a threat to anyone. His hilarious antics during the shows and in his role-plays always make me laugh and mark for the greatness that is GBJ. Now that he has the chance of challenging for the U.S. title whenever he wants, he can definitely play some mind games with the champion, Digital Mortality and eventually take the title from him. The US title division really got interesting with this cycle. Josh is another of those guys that I really haven’t had a chance to interact that much with and it’s a shame because if he is as funny as his character, he must be a riot. Kiljaden Kincaid While Kiljaden Kincaid always seems to be in the shows, I’m not sure if the character is being used as an NPC or what. There are no recent role-plays or anything to signal his level of activity within the fed. This is a guy that could use the fact that he is working with Bagwell and use it to his advantage. Hopefully he will do it soon. Lestatt Knight Really, I defy anyone to tell me if there is anyone more legendary in this current GCW roster than Knight. He is one of those guys that no matter what happens he will always be close or at the top of the pile. The feud with Chris Storm and his involvement with Tessa Windsor were great and definitely pushed him to be one of the top guys at this years Dangerous Games event. His recent encounter with Andy Murray makes me really excited about what might happen down the line between the two. Rob is a really great guy to work with and a great leader in the locker-room so to speak. He will give advice, help with match writing, and will come up with ideas that will help the guys come together and work with each other. Timmy Lester Timmy is definitely a character I’d like to see more of. He has an interesting concept for his character that I would like to see fleshed out more. Also, even though he is always booked in matches, he hardly ever appears in segments to hype himself which definitely does hurt him. A little more involvement in the shows and in the forums and we might see Lester getting title shots soon. Andy Murray This another of my favorite characters in GCW. The work he has put up is constantly top notch. Whether it is his interactions with Thom LeStrange, his battles with Chris Bagwell and his recent encounter with Lestatt Knight, Murray is constantly at the top of his game. I think he will very soon be challenging for GCW’s top title and will have a great showing at Dangerous Games, maybe even winning the whole thing. Out of character, Andy is great to talk to. He even stays awake till very early in the morning just to make an appearance at radio shows. That’s what I call commitment? or having nothing better to do! Bryan Mayhem Besides having a freaking scary looking poser, Mayhem is still relevant in his post-World title universe. His feud with Shaman was pretty brutal and that last match between the two at Warpath should have been a fitting end to their feud but I guess there is some unfinished business between the two according to WW95. While he may have lost that last match at Warpath, Mayhem’s stock is on the rise and as a former World champ, people should watch out for The Lunatic to aim for the top. Jacob McKail McKail has really shown how good he is during the last few months overtaking his enemy at Warpath and going on to greater things thanks to that impressive victory. He did lose to GBJ at the American Dream match, but much like all the other participants in that match, it doesn’t mean that is out of contention. He is still very much a threat to the rest of that division. I foresee McKail making huge waves this cycle leading to a great showing at Dangerous Games. Daria Ngozi The TV champ has been extremely impressive since her debut in GCW. She has been in TV title contention since her debut at High Stakes and was finally able to get the gold at Warpath with a victory over the very impressive Zacharia Taylor. This is another person I expect will make her presence even more known which will lead to success in one form or another at DG. Ryan is a great guy. He will give feedback and do whatever is needed in order to keep the fed working. Keegan O’Conner I have always liked the O’Conner character. I think many things can be done with the character. His short feud with Markus Stone was kind of lackluster. There hasn’t been a lot of activity promo wise and it’s a shame. I know that Josh could take this character to the very top if he wanted to (or had the time). Something is missing though. Hopefully Josh can come in this cycle and tear it up. Paddy O’Shea Paddy has been doing very well since he came back and started teaming with Cary Turkington. They were unable to win the tag titles at Warpath but I’m sure that won’t keep Paddy down for too long. His work is very hilarious and when he makes appearances during shows with GBJ or Cary Turkington, they steal the show. I hope to see more of singles O’Shea as I think Mick has the talent to be a top player. Rikki Roxx Because of someone? Rikki lost the streak for consecutive 5 stars. Now it begins again! Chris Storm It’s been a sort of quiet few weeks for Chris Storm. After that amazing record setting tag title reign, it is to be expected that there is a bit of a downtime. Storm was unable to defeat Knight at Warpath and we are yet to see what the future holds for a singles Chris Storm but hopefully we can see him come alive because I know that Bobby when he sets his mind on something, he doesn’t stop until he gets there. Bobby is one of those guys that are perfect for this game. His mind is constantly rolling on how the shows can be improved, how match writing can be improved and his advice is always welcomed. He is also working on the Magazine which I really hope to see soon. Markus Stone Sadly, Stone is the lesser known in the Banned and Exiled~! tandem. Ever since they started working singles, Stone has really taken a bit of a step back while Storm moved up. I’m not sure if Bobby is still handling him as a singles guy or if there is another handler that works as Stone (I heard something like that somewhere.) or how the thing works but Stone might end up with the short end of the stick while Storm basks on the glory. Shaman Much like his nemesis, Bryan Mayhem, Shaman is one of those guys who will always been in contention of some sort. His win over Mayhem at Warpath certainly makes him a viable challenger. One of the things I’ve always liked about Shaman is that he is old-school as old-school can be. Andy is one of those guys that are very passionate about the work he puts up as one should be. He doesn’t beat around the bush and gives his opinion on whatever he feels like. He is also loyal as all hell. I have a lot of respect for him. Jay Terror Jay Terror is pretty awesome. I’ve always been a fan of his work and now he was finally able to dethrone Vivica for the biggest strap the GCW has to offer. It was definitely a surprise at WW95 to see Jay win the title, not because I didn’t think he could do it but because I just didn’t expect it to be so soon. There is something about the characters that makes people gravitate towards him. After his heel turn, it took Dan a little bit to get used to the heelness of the Establishment, but in recent weeks, Jay Terror has really established (pun intended) himself as the top wrestling heel in the company. It will be interesting to see what happens next as we roll closer to WW100 and then Dangerous Games. Will Jay be able to hold onto his title? Cary Turkington I think that after Clyde Fox and GBJ, Cary is definitely one of the funniest characters GCW has. His shenanigans around the show always bring a smile to my face. Although he wasn’t able to defeat the reigning tag team champions, I can see Cary finding success as a singles competitor. I’m interested to see where Sam goes with his character next, but it will surely be hilarious. Zacharia Taylor When I heard that Taylor’s handler had never been in this game before, I was completely surprised. Sure his Docta Z days were kind of generic, but since then, Chris has really found his niche. The top is not far away from Taylor and I predict that we will be seeing him in the main event sooner than everyone thinks. Vivica J. Valentine What can be said about Vivica? The former GCW World champ is basically at the top of GCW. She surprisingly lost the title to Jay Terror after a fantastic defense of the title at Warpath. It was definitely a huge surprise. I am definitely interested to see if she goes back to challenge for the World title or if she begins to feud with Tessa Windsor. Billy is a really cool guy who really takes his legacy seriously and wanted Vivica’s reign as important as anyone that came before her. I think he was able to accomplish that. Jordan White Jordan White is a very interesting character. There is something about him that makes him matter. Maybe it’s the way he is written by Jayce and the certain mystique that the character has attained during his stay. I do think that his apathetic stand has sort of held him back a little bit. Jayce is probably the best pure writer here in GCW. There is something about his writing that just makes everyone want more. I guess, in a way, that is what this hobby is about. Tessa Windsor Tessa is another of my favorite characters in GCW. She seamlessly works different programs with different people without losing a step and almost always coming out on top. She quietly held the US title for a long time and fought off most of her challengers. Sure, she lost to Digital Mortality at Warpath, but losing the US title was for Tessa her ticket to the top. I am a fan of her work as she always goes for quality over quantity. She doesn’t need a novel to be able to portray the level of emotion that she does in her work. Sammie is great. She is easy to talk to, she will help out whenever is needed by the fed or any handler and I think this is her chance to reach the top of the game. NME The NME is a team that I know very little about and would definitely like to know more. They recently haven’t been on the winning end of their matches and I’m not sure that JJ has been able to fully get his attention on the NME. Samson & Delylah The current Tag Team champs have very big shoes to fill after the reign that B&E~! had. They are definitely up for it though. It’s very interesting to see this intergender duo as it is something that is not usually done. Angel’s work is very good and I predicted they would get the tag titles not too long ago. Now the important thing for her and her team is to keep the Tag titles in the spotlight.
