GCW Undergoing Aggressive Expansion

23 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The growth of the GCW roster reveals unexpected ambition by Commissioner GBJ and cooperation with President Caldera. As GCW prepares for WorldWide 128, fans have taken note of the rapid expansion of the company's active roster. This has given rise to a number of questions about who in the company's new power structure is responsible for the explosion. The surprising answer is that Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny and President Steven Caldera appear to be on the same page. Caldera, like his protege Jay Terror, was said to be furious that Johnny's approach to WorldWide last week was not exactly as he promised when he was handed the company. But business being business, Caldera is apparently cooperating with Johnny's plans to expand the GCW roster significantly. With the company running hot and gaining new fans thanks to its stellar NC-17 Pay-Per-View, which was followed by one of the most highly rated episodes of WorldWide last week in GBJ's debut as Commissioner, it seems that Johnny is working fast to expand the company. Though he is undoubtedly unorthodox, his business sense appears to be a bit more highly honed that originally expected. The notoriously ambitious Caldera, who has always dreamed of a day when GCW was the only wrestling promotion standing, was not about to stand in GBJ's way as he began opening up the purse strings. With the company flooded with cash following a highly profitable beginning to the year, Johnny is expanding WorldWide and featuring a bigger cast of characters than has been in GCW for several years. While Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny has hiring and firing prerogatives, it is still Caldera who ultimately signs the checks. It seems Caldera is holding true to his word to stay out of GBJ's way, and has authorized whatever new contracts GBJ is able to ink. Many in the company credit Garbage Bag Johnny's tremendous in-ring resume for allowing him to expand the roster so quickly. A GTT7 finalist, former United States Champion and a highly respected wrestler, GBJ wields a tremendous amount of respect among the wrestling community. While President Caldera is no less respected, GBJ is better liked on a personal level, and it seems the excitement caused by his turn at the helm has the premier talent in the wrestling world once again flocking to GCW. We will keep you posted as GCW continues to aggressively expand its borders. It appears as though the hiring will slow for the time being, but GCW has certainly grown significantly and appears to be gearing up for what it hopes will be a dominant run atop the wrestling world.
