Zacharia Taylor's Here and Now

11 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The fast rising star of 2008, Zacharia Taylor has pushed his way into the hearts of many for his righteousness and commanding talent in the ring. Starting back in January of 2008, Taylor was unknown, but the trash talking king, Chris Bagwell vouched for the talent of his apprentice. President Caldera made him sit on hold through December, and signed him in at the start of the new year. Being one of the original henchmen of The Movement, one would go as far to say that he was a founding father, under the dictatorship of Bagwell. Taylor came in angry and explosive. His first match was against the lovable Clyde Fox, Television Champion at the time. Fox, being in the game longer than the first-timer Taylor, really showed Taylor what GCW was all about. Taylor rebounded and beat Clyde Fox at the start of the Rampage tournament. His winning streak did not start here, losing to none other than the new Andy Murray, who is not new to the wrestling world by any means. After several interferences on Clyde Fox and others that Bagwell wanted to bring down, Taylor was given the opportunity to go up against his now rival, Clyde Fox for the Television Championship Belt at Rampage 2008. Taylor accepted and ran away with the win. With the sudden realization from Shaman, Taylor saw Bagwell's true colors and Taylor left The Movement to Bagwell and his henchmen, Dan Black and Kiljaden Kincaid. The Movement missed Taylor, but moved onto taking in Axel Action soon after. The long lasting feud with The Movement was compromised by smart associations with Andy Murray (whom was the only loss since their match for the rising star for quite some time) and Clyde Fox, who was once a rival, but turned great friend. Taylor started to take out the whole Television Division, while turning Face with the cheers from the crowd. Dan Black as always on his heels, but it wasn't until a certain female came into the ring did Taylor have to step it up. Daria Ngozi swept into GCW and started to take names. The loss to Taylor was expected, but it was more of a fight than he even knew. The longest TV title reign in GCW's history ended with the Warpath Television Title threeway with Dan Black, Daria Ngozi, and Zacharia Taylor. Comments after the match showed Taylor was looking to get rid of the belt, and he finally found someone worthy to lose it to. After a small investigation, it was proven the match wasn't thrown. The Television Divison suffered after the loss of Taylor, going from a competitive and passion driven division, to a slow moving lump of newcomers. Due to personal reasons, Daria Ngozi did not turn out to be the champion everyone hoped, and the title sat in her hands for some time without any competition. Finally, Dan Black was able to step up and challenge where Daria could not refuse, and lost it, but he title still lays without much challenged competition. Around the same time as the move to a different division, Taylor challenged The Movement and all those in it. Chris Bagwell became a mark, but nothing ever developed out of it except the few attacks from the Clyde/Chris duo. Tensions become high when these two are in the room together, yet Taylor's self control has been more than we can say for Bagwell. Zacharia Taylor saw this move into the United States Division as something that would change his career forever, and he was right. Taylor suffered losses to big names, but was taunted by the likes of Jacob McKail that he could not handle the new division. Taylor then set his sites on the champion, Digital Mortality. The first showing was epic, putting Digital on his toes, but still pulling off the win. Taylor did not stop there, he attempted to go after McKail backstage, but was eluded before his second match against Digital which ended in a draw after a devastating powerbomb through a table off of the barrier. Taylor was ready for his rematch at Dangerous Games, which he was promised. The Movement's change in power took tension off of the feud with the Movement from Taylor and Black, but nothing has developed since. With The Movement taking in new members, feuds can rise at any moment. The Dangerous Games match had a lot of hype, calling for Taylor to be one of the sleepers of the match. Taylor prepared for his match against Digital Mortality for the US title, as well as the epic Battle Royal match with all of GCW. Nobody expected what would come from that night. Only a week from the night, Taylor is feeling the aftereffects, and so are others in the Federation. Taylor entered into the US match determined, and he took the title from Digital Mortality in a three way match with Jacob McKail. He was hailed champion, just coming out of the TV division and only facing Digital from the divison, yet he was still champion. At the Dangerous Games final match, Taylor entered in and made an impact but didn't get any eliminations. It wasn't until he was knocked out did things go disastrous for the young Champion. Garbage Bag Johnny compromised on the opportunity he was given and pinned Taylor, cashing in his American Dream opportunity, and took the title when the young champion only held the belt for a matter of hours. When Taylor came to, he flipped out and attacked Johnny. He knocked him out of the ring and continued on a fury to the point where security had to take him away. Johnny was treated for minor injuries, but is ok. It seemed as if Taylor became the Taylor everyone thought he grew out of. The fire in his eyes was not like he was of old, but definitely not what we are used to. It will be interesting to see how he reacts when he gets back in the arena after the heartbreaking loss of the title that he worked so hard to attain. Zacharia is still one of the fastest rising stars in GCW, so it will be interesting to see how he bounces back after the loss, and how he will react when he sees Garbage Bag Johnny for the first time since he was pulled away, leaving him bleeding on the steps.
