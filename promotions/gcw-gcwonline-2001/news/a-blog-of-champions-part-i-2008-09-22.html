A Blog of Champions, Part I

22 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Blog of Champions: Part I A few days ago, I was training for my match with Zacharia Taylor when someone told me it would be interesting to write a blog for GCWonline.net. Having been in the business for a long time and seeing it evolve over time, it was inevitable that it would come to blogs. Normally, though, you would get a low level employee or someone who has never really stepped into the ring. That is why I decided to give you the real thing, the current defending GCW United States Champion, Digital Mortality. You may be asking yourself what the purpose of this blog is, but deep down you know. As a fan of wrestling myself, hearing the opinions of the people I look up to about the business, the other guys in the locker room, and anything at all is fun and interesting. When I went to Steven Caldera with this idea, he was all for it, but he was adamant of seeing shots being taken at GCW and his boys. I have a big problem with that because it not only ruins the integrity of the idea and my own. I have been in this game too long to give up my integrity so easily. He still has his reservations, but he had to agree to my demands. So, first of all, I would like to announce that I am opening the comments for your questions. I will try to answer them all so take advantage of that. Let’s get this thing cracking then. First of all, check out replay of WorldWide 97. As the 100th edition of WorldWide looms ever closer, things are really starting to heat up. Everyone is really aiming to get a top spot and be the winner of the Dangerous Games match. Besides my own match against Zacharia Taylor, you get to see two up and coming names in the ever popular and slightly deranged Garbage Bag Johnny and Daria Ngozi going head to head. Also, other big names like Chris Bagwell, Andy Murray, Jordan White and the usual group of guys and gals? plus someone I know very well who made their debut? sort of. I have to give props to Taylor. I had been hearing all about this kid for the past months but my years in this business have made me sort of cynical about some of the highly touted young guys. Taylor is the real deal. I wouldn’t mind stepping into the ring with him again and putting my title on the line. In fact, I want to give props to the whole US title division. When I got the call from GCW officials and Caldera, the first thing they told me was that they needed talent to make this division rise up to the level it is supposed to be at. I like to believe that the work me and Tessa did made the belt matter a bit more and now with these tremendously talented group of guys, I think I will need to watch my back carefully. Especially after Garbage Bag Johnny won the American Dream match. I wanted to talk about the American Dream match for a little while and give my thoughts on it. I understand why it was done from an entertainment purpose and the whole shaking things up the higher ups like to do all the time, but the whole idea behind it goes against everything I’m for. If someone truly deserves a title shot, he has to prove it in the ring. Winning some match and having the power to challenge the champion whenever the winner wants is not only cowardly but unfair. If someone truly deserves the title, they will step right in front of me and have a match that was announced and they won’t wait until I have had a grueling match and come and steal the belt from me. What is done is done though and Garbage Bag Johnny is the person that can decide to steal my belt whenever he wishes. He might not be completely right in the head and know what is going on exactly (as you might have seen with the questioning I had to go through at WW97) but he is still one hell of a wrestler. I wouldn’t mind losing the belt to him or anyone else for that matter, but only if they can prove themselves in this sport we all love. Anyways, keep checking in for the next edition of A Blog of Champions and send me your questions, if any, about anything. Take care readers and keep watching.
