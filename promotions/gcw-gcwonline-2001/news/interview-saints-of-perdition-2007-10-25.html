Interview: Saints of Perdition

25 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, everyone, Kevin Jacobs here with an exclusive, online-only interview with the latest tag team to arrive here in GCW. Hunter and Gunnar Saint, collectively known as the Saints of Periditon are here with me today, and I hope to get a little more information an enigmatic duo. Jacobs: First off, gentlemen, thank you for joining me. Gunnar: No problem. Hunter: Beats sitting home watching tv... Jacobs: Well, I suppose we'll start off with the obvious question. We know that the two of you are cousins and were picked up off the local circuit and were highly touted by the scouts, but we know very little of your origins. How did you two come to wrestle together, and where did you get your start? Gunnar: We grew up cousins closer than brothers. Our uncle was a small time promoter in the south and we were sent to live with him after getting in some trouble with the authorities. Uncle James thought teaching us some wrestling and making us work in his promotion, as ring crew guys, would give us a little structure. Hunter: Ah the good ol' days. Gunnar: We got into a fight with on of his top teams who were causing trouble backstage. Let's just say we stomped a mudhole and walked it dry. Uncle James said he saw dollar signs after that and started training us hardcore. Jacobs: You proved yourselves in a fight, but combat in a wrestling ring can be an entirely different environment. How were you able to make the transition? Hunter: There's a difference? Gunnar: As cueball just pointed out, we didn't really change out approach much. We just had to remember to rest our fists at the count of four so we don't get DQ'ed. Hunter: Took a few times. Gunnar: That and we're not always allowed to use weapons. We were always good as working as a teams. My mom was a deaf mute, so we both learned American Sign Language and learned to read each other's body language, so many time with just a glance he knows exactly what's running through the ol' noggin. Jacobs: So you two learned to brawl and had great chemistry... With that, what were you able to accomplish early in your careers that caught GCW's eye? Hunter: We won a shit load of tag titles throughout the southern indy scene. Gold talks. Gunnar: Cueball is right about that. When you back up what you say and carry more gold than Fort Knox, people tend to notice you. Jacobs: So you've experienced nothing but success in your careers. But now you're undoubtedly on the biggest stage you've ever encountered. How has life changed since joining GCW? Gunnar: Things were rough to start, but we have since found our path. As for GCW, we had to get used to all the travelling. My '68 Camaro SS isn't a big fan of these long trips and we're both not huge fans of leaving our uncle and Hunter's little sister. Hunter: You can say that again. Though the bitches are much hotter up here in the big leagues. Speaking of which... Lia, I love you! Gunnar: Give it up. She doesn't know you exist. Jesus fuckin' christ... Jacobs: You received a Tag Team Title opportunity relatively early in your tenure at Dangerous Games, but failed to come through. How do you plan to bounce back? Gunnar: Systematically destroying B&E! physically and mentally. I'm not Dr. fuckin' Evil. I'm not going to sit here and spill my guts, telling you what we have planned. We will get what we want. Jacobs: That's great, but in case you guys didn't know it, Banned and Exiled~! has routinely turned away every challenger to face them. Most agree that they are the greatest tag team ever to step into a ring. How do you plan to rise to that level of greatness so fast? Gunnar: We don't need to rise to any level. We are equal with them. Every lame ass fuckin' team they have fought has tried to play Banned and Exiled~!'s game. We are not playing into that funny ha ha bullshit. They will play our game, the pain game. They want to pull practical jokes? Fine. We will counter by breaking their backs. Let's see who wins that game. Jacobs: But some would say they're not all fun and games. Storm and Stone are both very highly qualified wrestlers. You've talked at length about your attitude, but tell me exactly how you plan to match up with them in the ring, should you meet again. Hunter: Well, I got that munchkin Stone. I'm gonna throw his ass all around the ring and put my fist through his face. That's that. Gunnar: That's how he fights just about everyone. He's simple, but you gotta love him. Anyhow, you spoke of these other teams that matched up against Banned and Exiled~! and there is one thing setting us apart from them. We are a tag team. It is a lost art in wrestling in this day and age. Using the most basic tag team strategies is enough. You cut off the weak link and keep the dangerous one on the outside. It doesn't get much simpler than that. Jacobs: Well, then, aside from your obvious championship aspirations, what are the Saints of Perdition's long-term goals in GCW? What do you want your legacy to be? Gunnar: The same as everyone else. We want to be remembered as the most feared and dominant team in wrestling history. The team everyone else is compared to. Hunter: And do the horizontal lambada with Lia. Gunnar: I'm gonna hit you so hard you're gonna starve to death rollin', cueball... Jacobs: Well, gentlemen, this certainly has been enlightening. Is there anything else you'd like to say to the GCW audience? Hunter: Ladies, I'm single. Gunnar: He also sleeps with a pink unicorn. Hunter: Fucker...
