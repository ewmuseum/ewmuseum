The Best Ever?

29 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A little talked about subplot in the upcoming World Title match between Lia Ambrosi and Jordan White is the greatness of Lia Ambrosi's World Title reign. Anyone who has managed to fight the long and arduous fight and attain the GCW World Title is deserving of our recognition (except, perhaps, Shane Lane), but Lia is arguably fighting for something greater, something that perhaps only Rich Rollins, Octavian and LeStatt Knight can claim: to have transcended that greatness and reached an even higher level. But elsewhere on the GCW roster, the same search is taking place on another plateau. Chris Storm and Markus Stone, collectively known as Banned and Exiled~!, have been GCW Tag Team Champions for almost six months now. While they've got a long way to go before they sniff the impressive title reign record set last year by UNITE, the consensus among those in the business is that B&E has already vastly surpassed their reign. The dominance Banned & Exiled~! has displayed in the Tag Team division is, perhaps, beyond comparison. LeStatt Knight's domination of the World Title in 2005 certainly stands out at the measuring stick, but have Storm and Stone surpassed even that historic run? Part of the reason the dynamic duo has failed to garner more attention is because of their somewhat mischievous nature. This reporter is even guilty of having overlooked their considerable talent due to the overwhelming aura of sneakiness that surrounds them. But despite their somewhat quirky disposition they remain two of the top wrestlers in the world: Chris Storm is a former PTC Global Champion, and Markus Stone was a highly touted singles wrestler here in GCW before teaming up with Storm. The Tag Team talent in GCW is superb. You can't even get a tryout or get a scout to come see you work unless you've already earned the reputation for being a stellar tandem. But Storm and Stone have essentially run circles around them all. There really hasn't been a team in GCW that could threaten their title reign, leaving little doubt that they'll break UNITE's record. Of course, Oblivion represents an unexplored and perhaps insurmountable challenge, and the Saints of Perdition could end up giving them a run for their money. But with the level Banned & Exiled~! is wrestling at, it's simply difficult to see them losing their titles any time soon. Banned & Exiled are in the midst of a truly special run in GCW. Perhaps due to their irreverent nature or the fact that they've crossed President Caldera one too many time they've failed to garner appropriate praise for their work. But when you watch Banned & Exiled~! next, take a moment to appreciate the fact that you're getting to see the greatest tag team in GCW history.
