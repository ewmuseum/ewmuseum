Bryan Mayhem to Join the Circus?

21 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bryan Mayhem Offered a Contract By The Ringling Brothers? With Dangerous Games looming around the corner, many tend to wonder what special guests will be stopping into the GCW for a one night stand. But what many are overlooking is the possibility of certain impact players that might not be participating in this year's blockbuster event. According to the official Ringling Brothers Barnem & Bailey Circus website (http://www.ringling.com), the company has recently signed GCW Superstar Bryan Mayhem to appear in a series of circus shows over the next several months. Coincidentally, the first show scheduled on the tour falls on the same date as Dangerous Games. Apparently, the company famous for their wild circus acts has been looking around for a replacement to fill the void of a sick bearded lady. They say the twisted child-molesting look of Bryan Mayhem would work perfect in her (or his, as some may argue) spot, especially with Bryan's steroid induced man-boobs. Other rumors have circulated that Mayhem would not, in fact, be replacing the Bearded Lady but instead being the feature attraction of a new live version of 'Pin The Tail on The Jackass' (a more explicit version of the popular childhood donkey game). The corporate news story has since been removed from the Ringling.com website, probably due to legal contractual matters. Nevertheless, it will be interesting to see if the man who held the most eliminations at this very event last year decides to skip out to pursue a much more rewarding job opportunity in having tons of little children jab pins into his ass! Bryan's agent wasn't available for comments.
