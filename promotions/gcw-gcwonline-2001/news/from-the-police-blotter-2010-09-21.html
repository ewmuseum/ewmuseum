From the Police Blotter

21 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In which Barry Goldstein uncovers some new information about what went on after High Stakes. Record ID 3517 Incident Date September 9, 2010 Time 5:56:00 AM Shift I Drunk and Disorderly Location Caesar's Palace Summary On the above date and time LVPD officers responded to a call that a "mighty big Native American looking type" and "the best damn dressed homeless man I'd ever seen" were terrorizing the casino floor after the former gentleman started a brawl with his blackjack table and surrounding blackjack tables. Damages to the casino totaled $12,488, which the latter man agreed to pay for in exchange for a resolution without arrest, insistent that he was "getting his game on this one gold bitch" in reference to a statue that was part of the casino's decor. The two agreed to leave peacefully. Adults Arrested ----------------------------------------------------- Record ID 3518 Incident Date September 9, 2010 Time 6:06:00 AM Shift I Disturbing The Peace Location Caesar's Palace Summary On the above date and time LVPD officers responded to a call that there was a ruckus about in the hallways of the eighth floor of Caesar's Palace. Officers on the scene found the source of the ruckus, the same Native American gentleman and well-dressed homeless looking man from the previous report, who claimed they were lost in a maze. The larger gentleman resisted help, claiming he was stuck to the floor between bouts of laughter. The other gentleman ran for the window and threatened "the most grandiose escape you'll ever see" before attempting to jump from the window into the pool below. He was apprehended after punching through the window with his fist. Both persons were arrested for disorderly conduct but released the following afternoon. Adults Arrested Johnathan Gumphries, Andrew Whitecloud
