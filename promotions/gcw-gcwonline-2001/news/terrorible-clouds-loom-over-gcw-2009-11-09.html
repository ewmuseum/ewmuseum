Terrorible Clouds Loom Over GCW

9 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Dangerous Games, President Steven Caldera made a promise that his hand-chosen number thirty would walk into the Dangerous Games match and win it all. As the anticipation mounted and the world saw returns from countless former superstars such as LeStatt Knight, Tessa Windsor, and even Bryan Mayhem, some began to wonder just who would be the masked man Caldera believed so heavily in. As the final countdown came to one though and 'Who Shot Ya' by the Notorious BIG came over the PA system, people realized just who he was talking about. In triumphant fashion, or in an ultimate act of loyalty, the former 'Outlaw' Jay Terror showed up from behind the curtains to the dismay of not only the fans but the inhabitants inside the ring. The most shocked was none other than Vivica J. Valentine who's last meeting with 'The Established' was the one where he took the World Title off her hands. As he marched to the ring, it was obvious that no one was happy to see him back in GCW and everyone took steps to ensure that he would not be the one to leave Dangerous Games with the golden ticket in hand. Ultimately though, in what has been named one of the most controversial moves in Dangerous Games history, Jay Terror snuck up behind a defenseless Vivica Valentine and eliminated her fulfilling President Caldera's promise. Although shocking that he did win, the bigger shock was the fact that he was still in the match after a devastating dropkick outside the ring through an announce table by Clyde Fox. Still, 'The Established' has done something that Steven Caldera desperately needed to resume credibility of his name. The ultimate shock is not where he's going but why he's back in the first place. The last time Terror was seen X:TV was at Rampage where he lost to Tessa Windsor in what seemed like his last chance to prove his worth to The Establishment and Caldera. Later that very same night Caldera sent FishBough after Terror and would deliver the hardest message he's ever had to to another superstar. He was rejected. After the vicious fallout, Terror disappeared from the spotlight for months apparently going over and over the idea that Caldera didn't want him to lead his reveled Establishment any longer. This brings the question - why would he be willing to come back and work under Caldera's sinister grip? As of now, this is unknown. What is speculated are a few things. For one, a lot of people believe that Terror's short stint on the JUST Wrestling tour peaked his enthusiasm for being in the ring. Seeing as Tim Shipley's federation was low budget however, perhaps Jay craved the attention he was receiving and the large paychecks he was promised. Other believe that he is stuck in a cycle of hate. They speculate that he is unable to break free from Caldera's hands mainly due to the fact that he was propelled so high up the rankings the last time he was offered help. Finally, others believe it was President Steven Caldera who begged and pleaded with Jay to come back. A lot of people have made mention that Jay resembles Caldera as a wrestler between the ropes. Since his devastating injury in the ring, Steven Caldera has perhaps been trying to live his life through Jay and he is trying to use him to do things he was unable to do. If so, this could be the darkest of all the omens looming over GCW considering his spite towards most of the roster. Especially that of Vivica J. Valentine and Andy Murray. This leaves everyone to wonder what does the Champion and his new found love interest have to look forward to? Will Terror be the same despicable man he was before parting with GCW or will he be worse and base all actions on the sick plans of Steven Caldera? As of now, only time can tell. What can be seen however is that Steven Caldera holds some of the most powerful puppet strings currently and will do his damndest to make Jay Terror dance all over the GCW stage once again.
