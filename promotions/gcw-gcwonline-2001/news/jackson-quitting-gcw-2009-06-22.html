Jackson Quitting GCW?

22 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Excerpt from The Wrestling Truth (http://www.wrestlingtruth.com) posted June 22nd, 2009 by Alex Collins The rumor mills are abuzz tonight, surrounding well-known Establishment badboy Brad Jackson, and his departure from GCW. Some speculate that Jackson's grudge match with Garbage Bag Johnny at Rampage will serve to be the final match in his nearly two year stint with the company. Others seem convinced that Jackson will remain until he retires once and for all. Jackson, however, has been ducking the questions for more than a month since he first made his feelings known on the subject. Is he leaving in disgust? Is he staying as Caldera's lackey? The GCW Public Relations offices have nothing to say on the matter. Jackson's website has been curiously dormant for the last month and a half. And then, last Wednesday, Jackson posts a two line blurb on his Twitter that blows all of those ideas out of the water in one fell swoop: Closing a chapter in my personal universe. Two years was long enough. Goodbye GCW. 12:44 AM Jun 17th from TwitterFox Misdirection? Perhaps, Jackson has been known to deliberately toy with the media in the past, in a vain attempt to keep his personal affairs out of the limelight. I'll keep you posted on what happens, as it happens, but for now stay tuned to Jackson's site, and make sure to order the broadcast of Rampage on PPV this week!
