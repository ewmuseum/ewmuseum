Rollins/White Showdown Looms

7 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Tsunami this year, Global Championship Wrestling will change forever. What began as a minor event billed mostly as a stop on the way to the highly anticipated Dangerous Games has developed into what could be one of GCW's biggest events of all time. And at the top of this huge event is one of the biggest matches to cross GCW in a long time: Rich Rollins returns to the ring to take on The Establishment and Jordan White. The Number One Contender has shown no fear when discussing Rollins this week. White's critics chalk this confidence up to a lack of appreciation for the situation he finds himself in. After all, nobody has ever beaten Rich Rollins in an Iron Man Match, and the list of those who tried and failed reads like a who's-who of professional wrestling. Vince Reed, Octavian, Khan, Mike Hardy and so many others have met Rollins in his signature match, but the end result is always the same: Rollins wins. White put himself on the main event radar when he defeated Twizzy and Vivica J. Valentine in one night at Warpath to earn a shot at the GCW World Champion. Now he's got a chance to prove his worth; should he manage to defeat Rollins and make history at Tsunami, there will be no denying the validity of his oft-criticized position as number one contender. Not to be forgotten in this equation is GCW World Champion Lia Ambrosi. The Champion has been relatively quiet since the announcement of the match, though she is expected to be in Sydney to witness the bout first hand. Those close to Ambrosi have hinted that she has had her doubts about White's ability to compete at a world championship level, and this contest will no doubt reveal White for what he truly is. GCW President and Establishment head Steven Caldera was visibly apprehensive about White's acceptance of the challenge issued by Rollins. After all, Caldera has no desire to see his chosen challenger fall to Rollins in a match that Caldera is all too familiar with. The last time a Caldera prot?g? met Rollins in an Iron Man Match was two years ago at Dangerous Games, where Mike Hardy engaged in the best performance of his career, only to fall short to the Renegade. The defeat derailed the fast ascent of Hardy, and his career never truly recovered. There is talk that Caldera is privately worried that a loss to Rollins would do the same to White; White could certainly no longer be considered the undisputed number one contender to Ambrosi's title if he falls to a twice-retired Rollins at Tsunami. For the Champion's part, Ambrosi has reserved comment. No doubt she would hesitate to defend her title against White at Dangerous Games if White is unable to defeat Rollins this week. And with a victory over Rollins as unlikely as it is, White seems to have put The Establishment in a difficult position. Should White lose, does Rollins then become the number one contender? Does he get added to the main event of Dangerous Games? The questions are valid, but it can be said for certain that not even President Caldera could justify giving White the title shot he earned at Warpath if he cannot defeat Rich Rollins. So if White fails like so many before him, the landscape of the GCW World Title scene will change dramatically. But what if White finds a way to win? What if White can make history and defeat Rollins in his trademark match? Most who cover GCW have said repeatedly that beating Rollins in an Iron Man Match is "impossible." But if White pulls off the miracle, a different but equally dramatic change will take place. A win over Rich Rollins in this environment would instantly pencil Jordan White's name in alongside the greatest wrestlers to ever step into a GCW ring. Such a win would remove any last shreds of doubt regarding his legitimacy as number one contender, and perhaps more importantly would legitimize this incarnation of The Establishment. Caldera, White and LeStatt Knight have claimed repeatedly to be seeking dominance of GCW through pure in-ring talent rather than Caldera's trademark manipulation. There is no denying the greatness of LeStatt Knight, a man so highly acclaimed that it is unlikely anyone will ever surpass him. Despite the heavy and well-deserved criticisms aimed at Caldera, his status in the wrestling world remains a lofty one. The weak link, therefore, is the inexperienced and untested White. This week at Tsunami White can raise himself to the same status of his Establishment-mates. Beating Rich Rollins in an Iron Man Match would be that great an accomplishment, great enough to put him on the same level as a Hall of Famer and the founder of the company. But beating Rich Rollins in an Iron Man Match is something that's never been done before, has never come close to happening before. The pressure is on you, Jordan White, to do the impossible and prove yourself to the world. Because if White falls short, everything will unravel for The Establishment and Lia Ambrosi will be left with no choice but to defend her title against Rich Rollins at Dangerous Games.
