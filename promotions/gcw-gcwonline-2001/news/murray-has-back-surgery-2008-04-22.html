Murray Has Back Surgery?

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to sources close to Andy Murray, the Scotsman secretly went under the surgeon’s knife just thirty-six hours after Rampage 2008 went off the air. The alleged procedure was said to have been to relieve compartmental syndrome ? a painful condition that reduces muscular mobility and strength ? in the lower-back area. Murray, who has hit the ground running since signing for GCW in December, is well-known for having a problematic back. Recurrences of an injury originally sustained in 2003 have plagued him since; most recently keeping him out of action from February 2007 to December of that year. It is estimated that such injuries have cost him somewhere between two and three years of his career. “While he hasn’t really experienced any severe pain since signing for GCW, he just wanted to take a precaution,” an anonymous source stated. “This minor surgery should help prolong his career by another couple of years at least.” While Andy Murray himself was unavailable for comment, the rumour does make sense given the fact that Murray’s omission from the WorldWide 87 card. Andy is also currently not scheduled for a match at WorldWide 88, but is highly likely to be in attendance.
