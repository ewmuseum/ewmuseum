"Baseless Assault"

20 December 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A telephone conversation with Grady Patrick sheds light on the chaos that ended WW 155. World Wide 155 ended in a melee, started by a ‘baseless assault,’ says Grady Patrick. Midway through the main event tag match Quinn Gregory fell. Our cameras were unable to pick up much more than that. Patrick maintains his innocence in the matter. ‘I simply showed concern for the girl; I myself was frightened by the sight of a collapsing seventeen year old. It wasn’t long before I had to worry for myself, on the brunt end of an unprovoked assault by Amy Campbell.’ And while our cameras might not have seen what happened, Patrick claims he has found fan-filmed footage to prove his claims of innocence. We asked to see the video, but were told, ‘I am going to Las Vegas, where I will show it for all to see. I am fully expecting a formal apology from Ms. Campbell.’
