Tribal War Machine to miss Worldwide

1 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News has reached us at GCWonline.net that SurReal will be absent from the upcoming Worldwide broadcast. Christian Zenith has given SurReal, who turned 35 at the weekend, the week off. After going through a glass table along with Donny Diamond last week at the hands of Lori Equinox, it can't have been a particularly happy birthday for the Irishman. Both SurReal and Diamond spent the night in hospital after Worldwide but we're cleared to leave the next morning. Sources suggest that SurReal will be travelling home to Belfast this week to compete for JUST Wrestling, the independent touring promotion headed by GCW newcomer Tim Shipley. It will be the first time SurReal has wrestled in his homeland for over a decade, he is scheduled to face the New Frontier Wrestling talent: Impulse. There has been some surprise that GCW talent be given time off at the moment, especially when considering that the upcoming broadcast will be the company’s last before NC-17. However in light of his recent bad luck (having lost against Cheap Labor and having been involved in the brawl with Equinox and Diamond last week) perhaps not appearing on Worldwide will do the Tribal War Machine a world of good.
