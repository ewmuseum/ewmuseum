Commissioner GBJ Addresses GCW on GCW Radio!

8 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny makes shocking WorldWide 127 announcements on GCW Radio with Eddie and Andy. In his first true address as GCW Commissioner, unlikely choice, Garbage Bag Johnny, made some claims about WW127 that promise to make the first WorldWide after NC-17 a spectacular event. Garbage Bag Johnny did so by announcing that every title will be on the line at 127, which airs live from the Pepsi Center in Denver, Colorado on March 16, 2010. Newly crowned GCW Television Champion Tempest will be defending her TV Title against one of the oddest wrestlers to disgrace a GCW ring, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS. After her win over Cheap Labor and her increasing presence on GCW TV, Tempest is predicted to have a very successful TV Title run in a division that plans to be more aggressive than it has been in some time. To make those predictions come true, she'll have to face her first challenge in a wrestler so unorthodox it defies words. Retaining GCW United States Champion Dan Black isn't even given the benefit of knowing his opponent, but with Digital Mortality back on the lineup for WorldWide, Big D is going to have to really be watching his back. When asked for comment after the show, Commissioner Garbage Bag John implied that bad things wouldn't happen to Dan Black if he wasn't such a bad Jew. Perhaps Dan will keep that in mind next time he brags on sleeping through synagogue on high holidays. We all know that the new Commissioner has the utmost respect for "high holidays." Main eventing the stellar card, Andy Murray, the longest reigning GCW champion in history clashes head on with GCW Legend, LeStatt Knight. The two know each other very well, and all of their battles so far have been absolutely epic. This match promises to be no different with the biggest prize in the game on the line. Both men are coming off huge wins at NC-17, and many are predicting that Murray's illustrious reign will come to an end just a week after he fought so hard to protect that belt at the PPV. Most shocking, however, Garbage Bag Johnny stripped Bryan Mayhem of both tag team belts he won from Mortifera at the PPV with a surprising victory in a handicap match. Inside sources say that Mayhem is none too pleased about the new commissioner's decision, but Commissioner GBJ has promised that he has something else in store for Bryan Mayhem. He better, if he wants to stay in one piece. However, GBJ might want to consider an even greater threat than Bryan Mayhem in Steven Caldera. Caldera enjoyed his send-off at NC-17, but if he's still got an eye on GCW, Garbage Bag Johnny might have already started overstepping the role that Caldera wanted him to have. If GBJ continues to try to shake things up, will Caldera trust Jay Terror enough to let Terror deal with GBJ's outlandishness, or will he come after GBJ himself if the new commissioner doesn't behave as he was instructed.
