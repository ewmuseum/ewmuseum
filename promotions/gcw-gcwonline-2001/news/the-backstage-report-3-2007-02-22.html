The Backstage Report #3

22 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Jacobs here with the return of the GCW Backstage Report! I'll be coming to you periodically to bring you the latest in backstage happenings around Global Championship Wrestling. -I've got the inside scoop on President Caldera's new title division to arrive this week! While I've been ordered to leave most of the details for Caldera himself to reveal this week on WorldWide, there are a few things I can clear up. First of all, yes, the Hardcore and Cruiserweight titles are merging into one. Peter Vetra's double title reign didn't last long! He'll be trading in the two now deactivated belts for one new one this week. While I can't divulge the name or details of the new division, I do know that the rules will be unlike anything ever seen before in GCW, and it will create an entirely new type of wrestling environment when the title is on the line. Peter Vetra is also expected to defend this new title in the near future. President Caldera did say that the Hardcore and Cruiserweight Titles could some day make a comeback, but don't expect to see them back any time soon. -WorldWide's rating was way up this week, reflecting a really great show. The Title vs. Title match, the Battle Royal and the debut of several new faces really pushed things up to a new level. I know GCW is going to be working very hard this week to keep the momentum going. -Chris Storm debuted this week in place of Tessa Windsor in her tag match. Windsor is on maternity leave now, but many within GCW are skeptical that she'll ever return. She seems content to stay home with the new baby when it is delivered, and GCW may be working to arrange terms of a release. It will be an amicable parting, however, and GCW is well known for taking good care of their stars when they leave on terms such as these. Best of luck to Tessa with the baby. -Lia Ambrosi was overhead this week expressing frustration that she hasn't defended her World Title since she defeated Dawn McFarlane a few weeks ago. Lia promised to be a fighting champion after winning the title at NC-17 this year, but hasn't had the opportunity to do so. President Caldera is on record saying that he prefers to save World Title matches for larger events to increase revenue. Lia has been rather vocal lately about wanting to put her title on the line, so we'll see who gives. -GCW brass is very impressed with new stars Markus Stone and Jimmy Thunder. Thunder, the massive powerhouse who body slammed 450 pound Bryan Mayhem this week, is expected to get a Television Title shot next week. Markus Stone has a budding partnership with Chris Storm, and those two look like they could turn into a formidable tandem. Look for big things from those three in the coming weeks. -The rumors surrounding LeStatt Knight's absence seem to be true; Knight is expected to file for retirement very soon. Knight's job description is essentially going to change to "Consultant" while his contract will remain the same. However, "Consultant" is just a euphemism for "Retired." Knight has such high standing in GCW that he's essentially being paid to do nothing out of respect for all he gave to the company. GCW will miss Knight greatly if this does indeed to be true. -James Bryan and his wife are expecting their third child next month. JB isn't expected to miss any time. -I spoke with Luke Hanson recently at Caldera Enterprises Headquarters. The big man looks like he's in great shape, but claims he was just there to catch up with President Caldera and, of course, make use of CEi's world-class gym. Still, buzz has it he's still pondering a return after he graduates college. -Hall of Famer Jason Dread was also spotted backstage recently, but he was only there to say hello. Dread is enjoying retirement and won't be back in the ring. -The duo of number one contender Bryan Mayhem and Shaman is causing some nervousness backstage. With both Mayhem and Vivica J. Valentine leaving Dies Irae this week, the status of the most dominant group in GCW is up in the air. Still, I've spoken to Dawn McFarlane, Virgil and Lia Ambrosi, and the members of KB:NAN and it seems to me that the group isn't in any trouble. They're going through a bit of a transition right now, and things appear to be changing for them. I expect them to regroup and end up better than ever at the end of the day, and perhaps come out of it a bit wiser. -To answer the multitude of emails I've received about Jordan White and his new manager, Sage Black... No, I have no idea what the hell is up with them. "White" was recently inked to a GCW contract, and we'll have to wait and see exactly what those two are up to. Still, White hung on to the very end of the battle royal this week, nearly getting a World Title shot. So keep an eye on this interesting and confusing situation as it continues to develop. That's it from backstage in GCW! I'll be back with more next week! Until then: -KJ
