Bagwell to Host New Radio Show?

13 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Over the last six months, GCW Radio has returned to the airwaves to a tremendous response. Though the show is on temporary hiatus while the company negotiates with satellite radio providers, the buzz continues to be high. GCW Radio has become so popular that the company is considering the possibility of adding a second show to the lineup. And while David Yale is the early favorite to return to the airwaves as host, another name has popped up: Chris Bagwell. The brash superstar, whose radio appearances are regularly the highest rated segments, has gained quite a loyal following. Though few sympathize with his attitude, his ability to entertain is unquestioned. Fan response has been so high that GCW officials are reportedly negotiating with Bagwell to add a GCW Radio broadcast to his job description. So far, Bagwell is said to be open to the idea, but little is known about the direction or success of the negotiations so far. It is said, however, that the show would largely be scripted by Bagwell himself, meaning the format is uncertain. Still, with the fans, even those that hate him, crying out for more Bagwell, it seems the company is prepared to give them what they want. However, with contract talks with Sirius and XM Radio slowing, it may be some time before GCW Radio returns. When it does, however, it is looking more and more likely that a second broadcast will be added to the lineup, and its host will be Chris Bagwell. -Source: Sally Ford
