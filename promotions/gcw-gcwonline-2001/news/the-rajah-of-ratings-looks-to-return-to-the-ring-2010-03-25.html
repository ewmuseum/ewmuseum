The Rajah of Ratings Looks to Return to the Ring

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former GCW mainstay Chris Bagwell hopes to capitalize on GBJ's aggressive expansion. It was one hundred ninety eight days ago that we were last graced with the presence of the self-proclaimed 'Rajah of Ratings' in his devastating loss to then-World Heavyweight Champion Andy Murray. According to a reliable source at GCW Headquarters in New York City, Chris Bagwell has recently expressed significant interest in rejoining the very company which rejuvenated his career three years ago. Bagwell parted ways with GCW back in September after losing a well-fought bout with Andy Murray, at Warpath. Bagwell's contract had allegedly expired only a week prior to the pay-per-view and he had been in negotiations with GCW Head Offices leading up to that point. However, being Steven Caldera's new poster boy, his loss crushed any momentum he had in signing the dotted line. Since going off air, Chris Bagwell has not participated in any other organization despite temporarily being signed to a contract with JUST Wrestling (which was later revoked by JUST owner Tim Shipley). "I'm absolutely ecstatic about it," said David Yale, a long time supporter of Chris Bagwell, when asked about Bagwell's rumored return. "Chris adds such a unique dimension to GCW that we've been missing over the last six months. It would be fantastic to see him back in the ring." With new Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny in the driver's seat, a spokesperson for Chris Bagwell noted that he feels "very confident" in the two parties reaching an agreement. Just a few days ago, Commissioner Johnny went on record to note how he's readily expanding the GCW roster to greater depths than it has ever been. Naturally, the addition of a former GCW main-stay couldn't hurt that momentum. But not everyone backstage has the same sentiments as David Yale. "He's a despicable pig and nothing more than a trouble maker," said Ayake Sonoda, whom Chris Bagwell assaulted about a year ago. "The morale in the locker room has been at an all-time high. That's something that Chris Bagwell can only bring down." While Bagwell's talent is certainly undeniable, his relationships within the company have worn thin. Prior to his absence, he sabotaged alliances with long time friends Zacharia Taylor, Dan Black, Axel Action, Jay Terror, and Clyde Fox. Not to mention, he's never quite seen eye-to-eye with superstars such as LeStatt Knight, Vivica J. Valentine, and Andy Murray. With Worldwide 128 only days away, one has to wonder if the 'Human Hype Machine' will be booking a room in Salt Lake City!
