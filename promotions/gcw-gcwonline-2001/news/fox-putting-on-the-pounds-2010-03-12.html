Fox Putting On The Pounds

12 March 2010

Ottawa, ON - Reports have been fluttering in from Clyde Fox’s hometown of Ottawa, Ontario, Canada about his recent disappearance since losing at the hands of the living GCW Legend, LeStatt Knight at NC-17 VII. Although critics gave Fox’s performance rave reviews, word on the street has it that Fox has isolated himself from the rest of society by traveling back home during his off week. No one is certain for sure, but close friends and family hear that he’s doing nothing but binge eating. One local fan has gone as far as perusing around Fox’s home during late night hours, trying to get a picture of Fox. He claims he’s captured a few photos that show Fox has ballooned in size. Rumors have been spreading that Fox has gained upwards of three hundred pounds since falling at the hands of LeStatt Knight. Others believe he’s been over exercising and has lost upwards of one hundred pounds, if possible. Although these rumors seem outrageous, it’s still something that shouldn’t be put past someone with a mental and depressive history like Fox has. There could be any number of reasons why Fox is either trying to bulk up or slim down. After talking with one of his old high school friends, he said that he knows that Fox loves to eat a ton of food and maybe after losing the match, he noticed he has to gain weight in order to compete. Others say that Fox is just too distraught to handle the loss, so he’s turned to food for comfort. While they debate, a select few settle on the fact that Fox thinks he’s not skinny enough. I suggest those people get their heads checked. Whatever the case may be, we were able to confirm Fox’s appearance at the upcoming WorldWide 127. Stay tuned as the truth is revealed next week. Kyle Bold, Canadian Press
