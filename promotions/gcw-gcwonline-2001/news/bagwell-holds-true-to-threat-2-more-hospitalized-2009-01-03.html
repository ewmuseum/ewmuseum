Bagwell Holds True to Threat, 2 More Hospitalized

3 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In what some considered an empty threat, Chris Bagwell began a 'hospitalization spree' in which he promised to put GCW staff and superstars in a hospital bed for every day that passed in which his wishes weren't granted. The assault began with Ayake Sonoda, who was innocently interviewing Bagwell when he just snapped. After an encounter with World Champion Jordan White, many though that the attacks would in fact cease as Bagwell seemed to be appeased. But they were wrong. Although Chris Bagwell hasn't held true to hurting a new staff member every day, reports have been flooding in that he has injured two more, making his streak every GCW event. After a house show event during the week, GCW ring crew members Sven Teeter and Randy Murreigh were brutally assaulted while tending to their normal post-show duties. Authorities say that the two men were extremely frightened and didn't know how to react to the maniacal Bagwell. So much so that reports also indicate a wet spot on Teeter's pants, indicating he may have pissed himself after watching Murreigh beaten down. When asked why he targeted these two men, Chris Bagwell said in a phone conference, "Because they have dumb names and even stupider hobbies." Although the hobbies Chris speaks of are unclear, we were able to gather that the two ring crew members were enthusiasts of some sort of fictional online roleplaying game. Apparently, Bagwell isn't too big a fan of the roleplaying idea. With Bagwell's threats seemingly being fulfilled, many are raising questions as to when the GCW executive board is going to do something. Commissioner Zenith is going to have his plate full this week on Worldwide and some are even speculating that President Caldera may be forced to return to set Bagwell straight. But there's only one way to find out! Tune in this week on Worldwide...
