Valentine Arrested At Airport

22 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Early Monday morning a viral video hit the internet, depicting GCW United States Champion Vivica J. Valentine being detained by autorities at a Hong Kong Airport. The incident occured while several hundred people were boarding a flight towards Beijing, China. A cell phone video clearly shows the GCW United States Champion struggling with authorities before being pinned to the ground and drug away. The grainy video also appears to show GCW Head Of Security Toad in the struggle, but was not clear enough to confirm nor deny. "That might have been me in the video," Toad commented when asked about the situation, "but it might not have. You're not my boss, I don't have to answer to you." An unreleased GCW source issued the following statement on the situation: "Vivica J. Valentine suffered a concussion at the hands of AXEL Action at WorldWide 132. She will not be cleared to wrestle in China and for her own safety we won't allow her to travel with Global Championship Wrestling. We believe in her current state that she is unable to make decisions for the benefit of her own well being, and therefore we will be making these decisions on her behalf. She will remain under the care of doctors in an anonymous location in Hong Kong and will be evaluated to see if her status changes before Rampage." Our sources have gathered that she escaped custody of doctors and GCW security from her location around 6:45am in an attempt to board a plane to China to catch the next episode of WorldWide. With the help of local authorities, they managed to apprehend her just moments before boarding. Rumors are rampant about where this anonymous location could be. Sources are saying anything from the United States Embassy to a location owned by GCW Television Champion Tempest. Valentine is expected to be released later this afternoon and taken to her anonymous location by the Hong Kong Police Department. She will not be charged because no crime was committed. There is currently no timetable on the return of Vivica J. Valentine, although sources close to the situation believe she'll be back in time for Rampage.
