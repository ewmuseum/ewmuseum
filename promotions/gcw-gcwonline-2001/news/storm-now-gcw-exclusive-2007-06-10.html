Storm Now GCW Exclusive

10 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arriving in Melbourne today, one-half of the tag team champions greeted the fans with a surprise interview. Chris Storm was on hand for over an hour, answering questions, and talking about the recent success of the reunited Banned & Exiled~! But Chris Storm surprised when crowd when he made a huge announcement. As of last night, Chris Storm is now an official part of the GCW company, signing a written contract finally. Up until this point, Chris had been working off a pay-per-appearance deal with the company for slightly under four months. But recent events have unfolded over the past few hours that had Chris Storm signing an exclusive contract to GCW. Chris had a few words for his former company: "I am very appreciative of my time in Legacy. They gave me the chance to wrestle a number of great stars I would have never otherwise crossed paths with. I wish them the best with their future endeavors, but for now my focus is on GCW and Banned & Exiled~!" There is still no word on how long or for how much the contract is worth, but it is one of the few exclusive contracts with GCW. What changes will this bring for the Rock Superstar? "For starters, I can finally wrestle a heavier schedule than before. With my commitments to Legacy, I was limited to tag team matches or short singles bouts. Now the fans can finally get 100% Chris Storm. I don't have to worry about other commitments or obligations." And it couldn't have came at a better time. On the 69th edition of WorldWide, Chris Storm finds himself in the main event against possibly his hardest opponent yet; LeStatt Knight. With the Establishment and President Steven Caldera backing him, the odds have never been more stacked against him. But what would Banned & Exiled~! be without a plan or two up their sleeve? "People want to make a big deal out of my match against LeStatt Knight, but you've seen that B&E~! can come out of a tight squeeze. We showed that at Warpath." Indeed they did, surprising opponents and fans when Markus Stone returned from injury, one he oversold to fool the Establishment. Thinking Storm would be partnerless, even the referee was shocked to see Chris Storm pin his own partner to retain their titles. The entire thing had been planned from the word go, once again giving the tag team champions something to smile about. "I have to give Markus credit, that was his idea. When they took him to the hospital and found out his knee wasn't injured, he oversold the injury to the officials to get the Establishment off our backs. Then, when the match was announced, he was quick to notice the loop-hole in the rules that gave us the win. He's a hell of a guy." Refusing any further questions, Chris shuffled off to the arena to get ready for what could be the biggest night of his career.
