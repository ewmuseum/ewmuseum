Banned & Exiled~! Fined

19 April 2007

The news coming from High Stakes continues as both members of Banned & Exiled~! have been fined for their actions at the Supershow. The news coming from High Stakes continues as both members of Banned & Exiled~! have been fined for their actions at the Supershow. Both have been fined $5,000, but no other actions are slated as being taken against the two. The decision, and amount, comes after a long meeting between the casino owner and GCW superstar Shaman and GCW owner Steven Caldera. The subject matter was the multiple arrest both members of the now Tag Team Champions had at the Supershow. As fans who attended the show remember, Banned & Exiled~! was arrested and later released after being caught cheating at the casino games. What they may have missed was the two later getting arrested once again for creating a disturbance and damaging casino property. Steven Caldera is said to be very disappointed in the two, but is not surprised by their actions. He is reportedly planning to take measures to minimize the damage the two do form now on. Banned & Exiled~! could not be reached for comment, but it is expected that the two are still celebrating their victory and new championship.
