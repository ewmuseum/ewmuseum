GCW Mourns Chris Benoit

26 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWE wrestler Chris Benoit was found dead in his home today along with his wife and son, WWE.com is reporting. This is devastating news to the entire wrestling community. Though we here at GCW never had the privilege of working with Chris Benoit, he was undoubtedly one of the absolute best to ever work in a ring. GCW extends its sincere condolences to Benoit's surviving family and friends. Benoit certainly has touched GCW. As one of wrestling's most popular workers, many of the current GCW stars owe their in-ring strategy and skills to Benoit's distinctive style. He is undoubtedly one of the most modeled-after stars in history, and his loss is a crippling one for professional wrestling. GCW is deeply saddened by this loss. While WorldWide will air as planned on Wednesday, the entire GCW staff and crew will be on hand to express their feelings and celebrate the life and career of one of wrestling's all-time greats. This week's WorldWide is dedicated to the memory of Chris Benoit. Please join us this week to honor one of our favorite wrestlers of all time.
