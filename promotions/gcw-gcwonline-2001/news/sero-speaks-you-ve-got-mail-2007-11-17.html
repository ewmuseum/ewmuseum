Sero Speaks: You've Got Mail!!

17 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello fans of GCW! More importantly though hello fans of Sero Blake! I want to start off this edition of “Sero Speaks” by giving a thank you to everyone who dropped me E-Mail comments about how much they enjoyed the first column. I had a lot of fun bringing you the first edition and I look forward to bringing many more in the future. Let me just say that a lot of my predictions for Devil’s Night were correct ? I deserve a gold star or something! Now this brings me to this week’s edition. I’ve thought long and hard about what I’d hit GCW with the second time around and I debated between interviewing The Establishment, reviewing Devil’s Night or maybe doing a wrestler spotlight. All of these ideas sounded wonderful but when I checked my E-Mail I realized that I had plenty of fun, entertaining and even a couple enlightening e-mails since my request to drop me a line a couple of weeks ago. So what does that mean? MAIL TIME! So without further a due! WTF? Johnny Lexicon is coming back to GCW? I thought this guy had been fired by Caldera himself? Sure Johnny Lex was a dominant force back in his heyday but that was what? 4 years ago in 3W? One How the hell did Lexicon weasel his way back into the GCW Ranks? Two how is his past history going to affect his status in GCW? Three does this make GCW look desperate for hiring back people who they’ve fired in the past? I mean who the hell is going to show up next? Tristain Freaking Mayhem? That would most definitely NOT be cool! Jerry Jameson, Atlanta, GA Not only would Tristain Mayhem not be COOL ? he wouldn’t be DOUBLE COOL! Alright now that I got that out of my system I can continue on. Johnny Lexicon has always been an enigma to me to be honest. I have followed his career back for awhile now and notice that as Jerry here states, he was once very dominant. The man has a submission victory over GCW’s All-World Champion LeStatt Knight. Yes LeStatt Knight tapped out to somebody people! Since that time though Johnny has had sporadic success in different feds (FUSE and GCW mostly) but nothing too substantial. How will he do this time around in GCW? Well seeing as how we haven’t heard a peep from him since he showed up at Dangerous Games it’s hard to give a good guess. Unless that fact alone tells us all we need to know. Stay Tuned. Hey Sero, next time you come to Georgia, you got to stop by and chow down at Abdullah's House of Ribs and Chinese Food. It's like an orgasm in your mouth. But, I did want to know, of all the returning faces that came in at Dangerous Games, who do you think will be the first to exit GCW? Dancy Colbert in Peachtree City, Georgia Thanks for the tip Dancy ? you paying? If so I’m all for trying some new stuff! Well GCW sure has had quite the influx of new yet old people haven’t they? To be perfectly honest I haven’t been impressed with any of them so far so they all could be out the door really ? BUT if I had to choose ONE I guess you could just look up to the previous question. Johnny Lexicon COME ON DOOOOOOWN! Hey Sero! I was an Octavian Mark back in the day no doubt! That dude was the most badass World Champion to ever set foot in a GCW ring! So you can imagine how happy I was to hear about his return to GCW! The guys going to sell out PPV’s and Shows with little effort! But I am however concerned about one thing. We’ve all seen what happens when Former Icons try to return and reclaim past Glory, I’m reminded of Hulk Hogan returning to WWE, the guy was a big an Icon as ever but his in ring performance was simply horrible! He was limited to a few basic moves, and while his physical build was impressive his body was limited in what it could do! I don’t want to see this happen to Octavian! What Kind of Physical Shape is he in? Is he mentally prepared for stepping back into a GCW ring? Reassure this paranoid Octavian fan! -Octavians Biggest Mark Well your claim that Octavian is the greatest World Champ ever is highly debatable to me BUT I respect your opinion. Octavian has looked great physically, perhaps the best shape he has ever been in. His problem right now seems to be adjusting to the new wave of talent GCW has. The man was the greatest back in the day but the GCW wasn’t quite as loaded with talent back then now was it? Octy is accustomed to being given everything ? to being the MAN. In today’s GCW he cannot win anything without work and he is NOT the MAN in The Establishment (Rollins and Knight are both more suitable for that title as of now). How will he respond to being third fiddle? (Fourth if you count Caldera) My guess is not kindly. ?! General Tso from parts Unknown And in keeping with the theme of returning superstars! What’s up Sero? I was just wanting to know exactly how many women line up after the shows to be ya’lls next “groupie?” I might get into wrestling if the answer is up to my liking. Jeff from Michigan. Well Jeff I might get fired if I go into too much detail on this subject in such a public forum. All I’ll say is this. It’s worth it. OMFG! Dude it’s like every former GCW star that ever was is like coming back! That’s freaking awesome! And not just any kind of awesome but like fifteen freaking minutes of air guitar solo awesome! But you know what would be freaking more awesome?”” If Tyrant came back! That dude was awesome Persona-freaking-fied! He fired dudes out of a friggin CANNON! That is like the picture perfect definition of AWESOME FREAKING COOL! Dude PLEASE tell me Tyrants coming back! The guy is INSPIRATIONAL! He TOTALLY inspired me to build my OWN Cannon in my parents garage! The thing did backfire though’and it did kind of burn down my parents garage, and I did kind of lose half my pinky finger in the blast? But the AWESOME news is that I get off Probation in like 2 months! And the first thing I’m going to do is TOTALLY buy a GCW Ticket and come to the show, and I REALLY wanna see Tyrant there! So please tell me he’s coming back Sero Dude! -The BIGGEST FRIGGIN AWESOME TYRANT FAN IN THE WHOLE FREAKIN WORLD! And if you think I’m not I’ll Drop Kick you in the friggin FACE! To be honest I’m almost afraid to respond to this E-Mail in fear that if I give an answer that this guy doesn’t like he may come hunt me down and try to blast me from this cannon of his. And I do NOT want to be drop kicked in the face. That stuff hurts. Good work on that first column, GCW really can use something like this! My question is about Vivica Valentine. I was very pleased to see her come out on top at Dangerous Games and is now the number one contender for the GCW World Title. Problem I see with her is that she is such a good underdog will she be an effective champion? Timothy Tee from Los Angeles, California Tim you aren’t the only one worried about this. She has been played up so long as the ultimate underdog that you have to wonder if she’ll lose steam if she manages to topple Lia Ambrosi and capture the World Championship. I honestly wouldn’t be surprised if she was so exhausted after winning it that she’d drop the title not long after ? some people can’t handle success. With NC-17 fast approaching and the World Title being decided between Vivica Valentine and Lia Ambrosi ? who do you see challenging the winner in the early part of 2008? Gary from Indiana Now this is a tough question because I feel there are a number of worthy people. You could make a case for Jordan White, Rich Rollins, Jay Terror and LeStatt Knight right off the top. Then there are a number of bubble type people who’d make interesting challengers like Brad Jackson or Shaman. Also you can’t forget Bryan Mayhem either. Nor whoever the loser is ? they should be entitled to a rematch. Since I’m not down with coping out though I’ll give you an answer ? hell I’ll give you my prediction for the NC-17 Main Event. Lia Ambrosi defends her title against Jay Terror in early 08. Book it readers! AND time for my last question. DRUM ROLL PLEASE! What the hell happened Sero!” I know you are from Columbus just like me brother! F*!$ing Illinois!” Man I hate life right now! We better beat Michigan! GO BUCKS! An Angry Buckeyes fan! This was a devastating blow to my weekend! It was like watching your beautiful girlfriend get drunk and sloppy and end up making out with your best friend. All while you have to watch. If Ohio State doesn’t rip the heart out of those Michigan bastards I’m flying home to Columbus and setting the campus ablaze and that’s all there is to it! Well that’s all for this week my friends. Make sure to continue to drop me E-mail and I’ll be sure to bust out more Mail Bags. Now I’m off to buy a new Buckeyes jersey and harass some lame Michigan fans! GO BUCKS!
