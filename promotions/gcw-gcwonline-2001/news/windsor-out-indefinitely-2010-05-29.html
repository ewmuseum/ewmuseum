Windsor Out Indefinitely

29 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fan favorite Tessa Windsor expected to miss at least two weeks, if not more. It is no secret to GCW fans worldwide that the last two weeks have been rather grueling for fan favorite Tessa Windsor at the hands of Jay Terror. Starting at Shockwave, the pint sized diva took a brutal chair shot at the hands of Jay Terror, barely a month removed from an earlier concussion suffered at the hands of long time friend Markus Stone. While her presence at Worldwide 130 was ill advised by both the GCW medical staff and upper level management following the events at Shockwave, Windsor personally decided that it was in her own best interest to square off against Terror. It appears that may not have been the best decision on Windsor’s behalf. Following her match at Worldwide 130, it was widely evident that Jay Terror had managed to invoke some pretty serious damage to Windsor’s knee. The extent of the damage still has yet to be revealed by either medical staff or upper level personnel. While Windsor has met with several members of the medical team to discuss such matters, none wished to comment citing that recovery time would largely depend on Windsor’s willingness to cooperate with medical personnel in terms of a recovery plan. Since then there have since been several attempts by reporters to get in touch with Windsor regarding her injury, none of which have been successful. It seems that Windsor has every intent on keeping the extent of her injury under tight lip. Rumor is she may be suffering from a hyper-extended knee. When questioned about his fiancé’s condition, Chris Storm assured that, "It was no one else’s business," and that, "Tessa will comment when she’s ready to." In regards to Jay Terror, Storm insisted that his former Establishment colleague would "largely regret what he’s done to Tessa," should Storm ever get a hold of him before Rampage. As far as Rampage is concerned, word is that Windsor is adamant about still competing in the dual cage match set to take place for the vacant world title, injury or not. While it may not be the wisest of decisions for Windsor, one can only imagine that she’ll do everything in her power to make sure she sets foot in what would be her very first PPV main event.
