Hall of Fame: Andy Murray

3 June 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW will be slowly releasing the final Hall of Fame inductions. The second inductee is one of GCW's all-time greats. Global Championship Wrestling is proud to announce the second in its final line of Hall of Fame inductees: Andy Murray. Murray arrived in GCW as part of a staggeringly talented recruiting class and quickly emerged at the best among them. Though he encountered fierce resistance from long-time rival Chris Bagwell, Murray won the 2008 Rampage Tournament to rocket up the ranks. Murray engaged in impressive matches for the remainder of the year, before finally running up against GCW's icon of destruction, LeStatt Knight. The two engaged in an epic back-and-forth battle that saw Murray unable to score a decisive victory. Finally, after defeating Jay Terror at NC-17 that year, Murray landed another title shot at Knight one year after his Rampage tournament win. Murray defeated Knight and kept his honor in the process, becoming the longest-reigning and most beloved champion in history. Nearly a year later, Murray met Knight in a rematch on WorldWide and Knight recaptured the belt. But Murray persevered, never losing his cool demeanor that made him so popular with the crowd. Ultimately Murray met Terror in an NC-17 Main Event rematch, and once again Murray emerged victorious, capturing his second and final World Title. Murray's career was cut short when he was forced to vacate the title due to injury, but his mark had already been made. Murray arrived in GCW on fire and never let up, developing into a larger-than-life superstar of epic proportions. Few have been able to match Murray's combination of charisma and fierce popularity, and his accolades have earned him an obvious seat in the GCW Hall of Fame.
