James Karma Sits down with Alex Grey

16 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alex Grey talks about how it feels to "Come Home" to GCW among other things Alex Masters. Maybe you know him as Alex Grey, former EPW Evolution Champion. Maybe you remember him as Payne, the man who went to war in promotions like DWA, xCw, 3W, and the place he ultimately got his start GCW. From whatever point in his career you may know him from you’ll be hard pressed to deny that he’s never gone out to that ring and given nothing less than One Hundred Percent, a man who’s paid for his fame and infamy with blood sweat and tears. I sat down with Alex to discuss his feelings on EPW closing, his Return to GCW at Dangerous Games, and just what the future holds for him. -- James Karma: Well first off let’s talk about EPW, we were both employed there and I know I got the shock of my life when I got the call telling me not to bother to come into work, where were you when you got the call? And what went through your mind? Alex Grey: I was just stepping off the plane and walking through the terminal in Arizona, I was pumped to be putting my title on the line at Cell Games against Aaron Rain, when I got the call I had to sit down, it was like someone had knocked the wind out of me, after awhile you get complacent, you think your organization is going to be around forever and that you’re going to have a secure paying job, you take for granted all the sacrifice that goes on around you, everything that gets done and all those people that put the show together every week, so I guess when I got the call I was blindsided, I thought “Man I sure didn’t see that coming” James Karma: I think we were on the same page with that train of thought, now you still hold the EPW Evolution Championship correct? Alex Grey: I do James Karma: What do you feel when you look at it? Would you give it back? Is it a positive in your mind? Or a negative as to how things can fall apart? Alex Grey: If Richard Rush asked me to give it back than I would out of respect to the memory of the Company, but no ones asked and I don’t feel inclined to part with it, and to answer your other questions’all of the above, I paid a heavy price week after week to get that Evolution Title, I went through Mental and Physical Hell with people like Shawn Singleton and Upton Osgood, so to go down as the last Evolution Title Holder it’s an Honour it really is, on the other hand I wouldn’t be completely honest if I said I didn’t feel a pang of regret every time I looked at it, I wonder what I could have done, how far I could have climbed on the EPW ladder, but the bottom line is I’ll always be proud to have been EPW’s last Evolution Champion. James Karma: Good, we’ll leave the past where it is then, let’s talk about where you are now, GCW! How did it feel to come back at Dangerous Games? Alex Grey: It felt amazing James, I wasn’t sure what kind of response I’d get from the crowd, if they’d still remember me, and if they did would they remember me for the man I am? Or the violent personality I portrayed? But if felt damn good James. James Karma: Was it coincidence Lestatt Knight was the first person you attacked once you hit the ring? Alex Grey: *Smiles* No it wasn’t James Karma: Care to elaborate? What are your feelings on Lestatt? Is the fire still there between you two? Alex Grey: If you’re asking do I still hate the man? Then no I don’t, Roberto and I reached a mutual understanding years ago, does that mean we wouldn’t beat the hell out of each other if we stepped into the ring against each other? Hell no, See with this mutual respect there’s still a healthy dose of competition involved, but I wont lie, at Dangerous Games I was just itching to get in his face again. James Karma: Do you expect a confrontation in the ring with Lestatt Knight soon? Alex Grey: I don’t, I expect we’ll talk soon but I don’t see a match up for a long time, Roberto’s on a whole different level than me now, he’s quite literally perched at the top right now, and I’m looking up at him from the bottom, and I’m fine with that’for now James Karma: So does this mean that if you were walking down the street and you saw Lestatt Knight running towards you on fire you’d actually put him out? Alex Grey: *Chuckles* Yeah I’d put him out James’but I might watch him burn a little first. James Karma: Alright we’ll leave that particular subject alone then, What made you decide to come back to GCW? This was were you got you first shot at the big time correct? What took you so long to come back? Alex Grey: GCW was my first shot at the big time, a talent scout caught me wrestling in an Indy Fed called IWF, they brought news back to Caldera and he offered me a developmental contract, I was a different person back then, more interested in personal glory and gain, it made me difficult to work with at times, the GCW world was so much bigger, I was performing on a whole new stage, infinitely bigger than that of IWF, if someone hadn’t stepped in and forced me to calm down I probably would have blown my chances and ruined my career by either saying something stupid or inflicting a career ending injury on myself. James Karma: You say if someone “Hadn’t” stepped in? Who stepped in and forced you to re-evaluate yourself? Alex Grey: Jason O’Neil’I owe that man everything James, he didn’t have to step in, he could have watched me spiral down that path to self destruction and not done a thing, but he did intervene, he took me under his wing and helped train me, showed me safer ways to handle myself in the ring, showed me how to unlock the potential in myself, and even though I haven’t seen him in years I still incorporate many of the lessons he taught me into my life even today. James Karma: So with O’Neil more or less a mentor to you how did you come to leave GCW in the first place? Alex Grey: Well even Jason couldn’t keep me from saying stupid shit James, when GCW closed I parted ways under bad terms with Caldera, I said some things that I truly regretted and went off in a completely different direction, truth be told I enjoyed greater measures of success elsewhere than I did in GCW, but what it took me years to learn was that if wasn’t for GCW I wouldn’t have become a name well recognized enough to get into places like DWA and xCw, the day I realized that I was completely humbled. James Karma: So Steven Caldera looked you up the day EPW closed and offered you a spot? Alex Grey: No, Caldera offered me a spot when GCW re-opened years ago, he even cared enough to stay in touch over the years, he told me’and it blows my mind’that there was still a place for me in GCW, he never really let me forget it, at first I turned him down, I wasn’t in any shape to wrestle, but when I started getting back into shape what held me back was the things I’d said all those years ago, ultimately I Debuted in EPW when I could have gone to GCW, but I needed to prove some things to myself first, when EPW closed I was sitting there in Arizona thinking “Ok’Steven wouldn’t have offered me a chance to come back if he didn’t want me’just what the hell am I waiting for?” James Karma: And so you decided to come “Home” Alex Grey: I did James Karma: You’ve already participated in one match, a Four Way Battle for a number one contenders shot at the United States Title, Were you disappointed by your loss? Alex Grey: Not at all James, Winning a title shot on my return would have been icing on the cake, but I was out there to prove that I could compete with those three and that I deserved my position in GCW, there were a couple moments where I almost had the victory but I’d like to think that those couple of moments validated me in the eyes of the fans and hopefully some of the higher ups backstage. James Karma: Well I’ve taken enough of your time, just one more question, you’re a different person now, with years of knowledge under your belt, you’ve learned lessons and things and skills you didn’t have in GCW before, so are we going to see a different Alex Grey now? Or are we going to see the same Alex Grey hell bent on fame and personal gain? Alex Grey: *Smiles* The years have taught me to be patient James, I’m not expecting anything but a “Chance” in GCW, I’ll work hard to live up to the faith that Steven Caldera obviously has in me, but at the same time I do have personal goals, things I “Will” Accomplish here in GCW, I’m just patient enough now to realize that they won’t be accomplished over night. James Karma: Rome wasn’t built in a day’would you care to enlighten me and the readers as to what some of those “Goals” may be? Alex Grey: *Smiles again* Not a chance in hell James
