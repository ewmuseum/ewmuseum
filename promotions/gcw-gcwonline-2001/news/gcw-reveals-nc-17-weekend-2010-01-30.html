GCW Reveals NC-17 Weekend

30 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling has unveiled plans for this year's NC-17 weekend of events. As the biggest event on the GCW calendar, NC-17 tends to span more than just one evening. This year, GCW has announced the biggest buildup yet, focused around the weekend of the blockbuster event itself. With GCW already in the D/FW area after this week's WorldWide in Fort Worth, the company will be setting up shop early. Next week, WorldWide will emanate from the American Airlines Center in downtown Dallas, which is to be preceded by a fan festival at Victory Park outside the arena. The WorldWide festival will include meet-and-greets with major GCW stars, games, activities, and prizes. The prizes include the opportunity to win two ringside seats to NC-17 the following week. The full Fan Fest weekend will begin on Friday morning and conclude Saturday night. The event will be held inside Cowboys Stadium in Arlington, where the full set, stage and ring area will already be built. In place of floor seating, GCW will have numerous booths, activities and games for fans to enjoy, as they mingle with GCW staff and crew. There will be plenty of autograph stations, and every single GCW star will be appearing during the weekend. Each night will conclude with a performance by a major rock group, though the names of the bands have not yet been revealed. NC-17 will then take place on Sunday, February 21st. Doors to Cowboys Stadium will open at noon, and fans will be able to join the party with activities leading right up to the opening bell at 7PM CST. The event is scheduled to last four hours. But that's not all. Following NC-17, GCW is planning a massive WorldWide to launch the 2010 schedule. While details are not likely to be released until after NC-17, it is rumored that the GCW World Title will be on the line in the main event of WorldWide 127, a blockbuster headliner to the start of the new year. Stay tuned as NC-17 7 approaches. We'll be here will full details of GCW Fan Fest Weekend, as well as full reports at the conclusion of each night.
