WorldWide Airs, Dangerous Games Looms

5 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chicago, Illinois- WorldWide aired from Chicago in front of a capacity crowd that knew it was in for a treat; WorldWide followed a few weeks later by one of the most highly anticipated events of the year. Dangerous Games will return to Chicago in three weeks where every member of the GCW roster will compete for a chance at the main event at NC-17. Kevin Jacobs here with a long overdue overview of GCW's goings-on. A lot has happened since Tsunami, which was a tremendous success for the promotion. Tsunami did huge ratings on X-TV, and these great shows are helping the network to expand. Rumor has it that Caldera Enterprises executives are considering offering television spots on X-TV to other wrestling promotions in hopes of making the network a one-stop location for wrestling on television. -As far as cooperation goes, GCW does appear to be interested in expanding its horizons. President Caldera is notoriously ambitious and is always looking for ways to increase GCW's influence in the wrestling world, and the latest rumor involves a joint venture with PRIME. Though PRIME is considered GCW's primary rival, a mutual respect and growing relations between locker rooms seems to have made cooperation possible for the first time. Though by no means confirmed, sources inside Caldera Enterprises say that Caldera recently had a sitdown with PRIME leaders at a Chicago area restaurant to discuss a dual-hosted event. GCW's annual Devil's Night supercard coincides with PRIME's Great American Nightmare, and rather than run competing events GCW has proposed combining the shows and co-hosting them, allowing for the possibility of cross-promotional matchups. There's no word on how PRIME took to the proposal or how serious GCW is about the event, but it isn't beyond the realm of possibility; PRIME Champion Lindsay Troy recently wrestled on WorldWide against President Caldera. It's also rumored that invitations might be extended to the wrestling world abroad to compete in this year's Dangerous Games. -GCW is still buzzing after Jordan White defeated Rich Rollins at Tsunami. Rollins himself was expected to appear at WorldWide this week, as it was hosted in his hometown of Chicago, but Rollins was a no-show. Combined with LeStatt Knight's dominance against Twizzy (who also failed to show for the event), The Establishment certainly seems to be riding high. And with Lia Ambrosi now defending her title every week, it might be a tall mountain to scale for Lia to retain her belt at Dangerous Games. Still, despite White's newfound respect and legitimization, it remains to be seen if he's truly got what it takes in the ring to be World Champion. -I often get emails from fans wondering how Banned and Exiled~! are still GCW employees given all the headaches they cause President Caldera. The answer is simple; Caldera is a sound businessman first and an evil overlord second. Banned and Exiled~! merchandise is far and away the top seller in the GCW store, and there's no way Caldera would give up that revenue so quickly. -While on the subject of the tag team division, we've got another new tag team trying out this week. I'm hearing that GCW has already hammered out a deal with them. I'm not at liberty to reveal their identities, but their tryouts so far have been sensational. -Yes, Jay Terror is still the GCW Television Champion, and the records will continue to indicate as much. Tyson DeBough stole the title, and the only reason he still has it is because President Caldera seems to admire the moxie. Still, you can bet that Terror will get his hands on DeBough eventually, and when he does I have a feeling he'll be taking his title back. But don't forget Paddy O'Shea in the equation. He's been battling injuries, but I talked to him recently backstage and he says he's healing and he still wants the TV belt for himself. -Speaking of injuries, Brad Jackson suffered a sprained ankle at a recent house show that GCW doctors urged him to care for. GCW takes great pride in its vigilance when it comes to our stars' health, and nobody is forced to perform when injured. Still, Jackson is a hell of an athlete and I've never seen anyone more motivated. He's caught a lot of eyes and is arguably the hottest star GCW has seen in a while, so when he returns to the ring look for him to pick up where he left off. -A lot of people in the know also like Phil Atken. I talked to a friend who works for his former employer and he was very high on Atken's in-ring talents. Atken is an extremely gifted wrestler and if he ever gets hot, watch out. -I can confirm the rumors that GCW was in very serious contract negotiations with Jason Dread about a return, but the talks fell through. Dread is in shape and has been dabbling in the independent circuit, and expressed interest in returning full time. However, strained relations between Dread and President Caldera combined with Dread's increasing interests in his entrepreneurial pursuits caused the deal to collapse. Apparently the two sides couldn't agree on how much time would be required of Dread on the road. Still, Dread's a Hall of Famer and, some argue, the greatest pure wrestler in GCW history. You can bet that if Dread wants it bad enough he'll show up. -I can also confirm the rumor that Caldera tried to acquire Primetime Central during its twilight hours, but no deal could be met. -I have a feeling next week's WorldWide is going to be a big one. World Champion Lia Ambrosi will have to face the wrath of President Caldera, whom she really hurt badly in this week's main event. Caldera was originally reported to be out after doctors recommended he stay in Chicago for medical care, but Caldera refused and checked himself out of the hospital. I have no idea how severe his injuries are, but expect him to be in bad shape and in a bad mood next week. Plus all of GCW's biggest stars are in competition, and the World Title will be on the line. No word yet on who the challenger will be, but I heard that Lia's leaning towards someone who recently made Australia very proud... Don't miss the show next week, folks. I'm Kevin Jacobs signing out.
