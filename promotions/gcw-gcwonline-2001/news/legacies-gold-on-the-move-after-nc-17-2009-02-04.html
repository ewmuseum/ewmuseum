Legacies, Gold On The Move After NC-17

4 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PHOENIX, AZ- With a record-breaking crowd of 104,329 jammed into the University of Phoenix Stadium, GCW put on what it hoped would be the biggest Pay-Per-View broadcast in its 9 year existence. Despite astronomical expectations, the consensus appears to be that the company delivered. With twelve matches on the lineup and appearances by several GCW legends, NC-17 provided quite the bang for the viewer's 60 bucks. Despite the higher-than-average cost to order the show, GCW expects record buyrates. Heading in to the event there was much talk of legacy, headlined by the most legendary wrestler of them all, LeStatt Knight. NC-17 is easily the most memorable event of the year in GCW, and a dedicated GCW fan can recite the outcome of nearly any match in the history of the broadcast. It was on this stage that Knight and a host of others fought for the glory that comes with an NC-17 victory. Knight had the most gain--and the most to lose. In his main event contest with Champion Jordan White, Knight had the opportunity to do the one thing he'd never done before: win the GCW Title at NC-17. But White, who many believed to be poised to join the ranks of the all-time elite, was after a legacy-defining victory himself. The result was an epic clash unlike anything either man had ever experienced before, an event already generating match of the year buzz. But Knight wasn't the only man on a mission. Zacharia Taylor, after defeating Digital Mortality for the United States Title at Dangerous Games, had the belt swiped by the undefeated Garbage Bag Johnny. Since then Taylor has been hellbent on paying Johnny back by ending his reign and his unbeaten streak. The incredible showdown proved that both men are worthy of the gold, but only one could take the belt home. In the other two-thirds of the triple main event, Vivica J. Valentine met Chris Bagwell in an Unsanctioned I Quit match, and Andy Murray went toe to toe with Jay Terror. With The Establishment seemingly splintering and leaderless in the absence of President Caldera, it was up to Terror to strike a rare victory for the flailing group by defeating the immensely popular Murray. For his part, Murray came in surrounded by speculation about his future. A victory, it's been said, would put Murray in line for a GCW Title shot. For her part, Valentine was after something more urgent: avoiding the endless months and years of ridicule that would come her way should she lose to Bagwell. Bagwell's agenda was more serious. Intent on taking his career to the next level and being seen as more than a mouthpiece, Bagwell gave Valentine one of the most grueling battles of her life. With the entire wrestling world watching, GCW once again pushed the envelope with an innovative and exciting event. NC-17 is replaying on X:TV all week. Do not miss this historic event, and check back next week as GCW returns to follow up this Pay-Per-View spectacular with WorldWide 105.
