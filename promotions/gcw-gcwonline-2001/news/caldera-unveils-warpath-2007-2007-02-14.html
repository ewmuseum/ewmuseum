Caldera Unveils Warpath: 2007

14 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, Ohio- From Caldera Enterprises in Columbus today, President Steven Caldera unveiled his plans for GCW's next Pay-Per-View event, Warpath. Warpath, which will take place on April 29th, 2007, from American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida. Besides the inevitable GCW World Heavyweight Title defense that will take place, President Caldera unveiled a theme that will give a nod to GCW's roots. "Warpath will be the culmination of a 16-person tournament that will be held in April. Sixteen of the top competitors in GCW will be given spots in a single-elimination tournament, the final two rounds of which will be held at Warpath itself," Caldera said in his press release. GCW's first Pay-Per-View event was Warpath 2001, on which an 8-man tournament was held to crown the first GCW Champion. Octavian defeated The Jhub in the finals of that tournament to capture the title. This announcement was anticipated by many in GCW due to Caldera's decision to forgo the annual Rampage Pay-Per-View event. The Rampage tournament is annually one of the biggest draws in GCW and also a tremendous opportunity for a GCW star to make it to the top. This, Caldera said, will give them that chance, at one of GCW's premier events. The winner of the tournament, as is custom, will receive a GCW World Heavyweight Title shot. The shot, however, will not come easy. "To win the tournament, a wrestler will have to win four matches in three weeks," Caldera said. He promised to populate the tournament with the best GCW has to offer. How will competitors be selected? "When we grow nearer to Warpath, qualifying matches will be held for those 'on the bubble.' However, I myself will personally name most of the field based on overall performance. I'll be watching for people to impress me starting as early as this week, so just about everything that happens on GCW television for the next two and a half months will impact my decisions." With Warpath still ten weeks away, GCW is considering holding one of its "Supercards" to help hype the Pay-Per-View event. Caldera did rule out any Supercard event in February, but suggested that a Spring Break themed event might be held on a Monday in March. "We'll just have to wait and see," Caldera concluded with a coy smile.
