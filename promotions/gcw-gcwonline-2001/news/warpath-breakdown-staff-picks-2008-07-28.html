Warpath Breakdown: Staff Picks

28 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Warpath just days away, James Bryan joins us to break down the exciting Main Event match between Vivica J. Valentine, Jay Terror and Jordan White. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship is on the line, and this is shaping up to be one of the most competitive GCW Title matches in history. JB will be breaking down each competitor's strengths, weaknesses and motivations, and we'll cap it off with the staff's prediction. Today: Staff Predictions GCW has asked each of its contributors to submit their predictions for the Warpath main event match. Here's what they had to say. Barry Goldstein- Jordan White's apathetic, anarchic act is wearing on my nerves. This kid just doesn't seem to get it, and he's fine with that. He doesn't have the passion to win this match, no matter what his apologists say. My instinct says Valentine doesn't have the size, and damnit, Jay Terror's been impressive. I'll give Valentine credit, she's been an impressive champion. But all signs point the other direction. We'll have a new champion. PICK: Jay Terror Sally Ford- Us ladies have got to stick together. I like Jordan White, I think he might be the most talented wrestler in the world. But can he figure out the game in time to win this match? Not with the smarts of Valentine against him. Terror has proven nothing to me; who has he beaten? Until he does something to prove me wrong, I'll remain skeptical about him. I'll be rooting hard for my pick-- PICK: Vivica J. Valentine Kevin Jacobs- In my opinion, Valentine has officially surpassed Knight as the greatest champion this company has ever seen. The result of this match won't change my mind on that. Still, a successful defense wouldn't hurt. Terror's in great form, though. I know Sally doesn't think he's proven anything, but I think he has. This could be his night. But my prediction? Jordan White has finally got this thing figured out, and he's going to prove it by taking home the strap. PICK: Jordan White Toad- I don't care for any of these jackasses. I fall asleep at night dreaming of caving in Jay Terror's skull with my baseball bat, but I wake up in the morning with some wood for Valentine. That Jordan White jackass? He's the only one I can stomach. I don't give a hot damn if he doesn't care, he's the only one that doesn't make my job harder. So I give him my pick, even though I don't give a rat's ass. PICK: Jordan White David Yale- Do I even need to say it? This one's elementary! Okay, so I don't like what he did to my broadcast colleague a couple weeks ago (even though it did give me the chance to take the lead and shine!), but Jay Terror is on the verge of becoming champion. Valentine's been great and all, but her time is up. And Jordan White is in over his head. Bet the rent on this one. PICK: Jay Terror James Bryan- As I appear to be the only impartial member of the panel, I've tried to be objective. I didn't even let my rather strong negative feelings about Jay Terror affect my judgment. Terror is spectacular; I know first hand. White is obscenely talented, and Valentine is the total package of finesse, smarts and experience. Terror's never won a big match like this, and White could go either way. So while I feel it will all come down to execution, I'm picking the champion to keep her belt. PICK: Vivica J. Valentine
