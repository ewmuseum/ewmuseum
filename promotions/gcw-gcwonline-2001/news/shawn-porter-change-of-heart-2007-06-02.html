Shawn Porter: Change of Heart?

2 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Did Porter actually change his heart for the fans? Has the main event's ego calm down? GCW Officials sat down with newcomer Shawn Porter. They believe that he has a change of heart going into Warpath. After hearing his debut interview with the lovely Sally Ford, Shawn stated,"I'm gonna give it one hundred percent every night. And its all for myself and not for these fans, they show no love for me so i show no respect for them". Shawn asked to speak with us about his sudden "change of heart". "Okay everyone i was wrong for judging the people so early in my career. I know the fans mean more than anything in this company. I'm just humble to be apart of GCW and to make my debut come Sunday. This is one of my most difficult challenge I've had in a while. So i know i can't screw it up. I'm willing to give 110% for the fans if they give me credit for what i do in the ring. if your the best why not say your the best. I know my skill is up there with the mid-carders. So why cheat me? All I'm saying is I'm going to put on my show this Sunday at Warpath and everyone can bank on that." We've asked him if he thought about getting a manager. He replied... "Um not really into sharing the spotlight with these managers. If i had to it would be my agent Veronica Andrews. I'll make sure she'll be on the scene in a while." Did Porter actually change his heart for the fans? Also who's this Veronica person? Has the main event calm down? All will be answered at Warpath.
