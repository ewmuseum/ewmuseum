Bantam Books to Release Biography of Bryan Mayhem

12 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Lunatic in black and white. Bantam books has just been granted permission from GCW to release the long awaited biography of the most twisted man in wrestling history. Author Alyssa Nedved spent an entire year with Bryan Mayhem learning his life and what makes him tick. "I didn't realize the task that was at hand until I spent the first few hours with him. Such a tortured troubled soul that has never felt the love of a real family. I think most people will be shocked at the life this now certified legend led." The book is not due out until early 2009, but Ms. Nedved has allowed GCW to print her Prologue on its very own site. "It's like the story of Mick Foley, Charles Manson, and a Hollywood celebrity all rolled into one twisted story." You can pre-order the book today at Barnes and Nobles.com and Amazon.com.
