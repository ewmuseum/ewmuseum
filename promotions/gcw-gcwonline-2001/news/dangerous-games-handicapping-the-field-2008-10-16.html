Dangerous Games: Handicapping the Field

16 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Jacobs here with the first of many Dangerous Games previews. All this week we'll be breaking down each of the exciting matches on the lineup for next week's Pay-Per-View, along with many looks at the Dangerous Games match itself. Today we start with a preview of the entire Dangerous Games field. While GCW does not release the official list of entrants ahead of time, everyone on the roster is eligible for participation. As such, we'll break down each wrestler and predict who has the best (and worst) chances of success. Axel Action - 20/1 AXEL has been on quite a hot streak since striking out on his own. Now with The Movement, AXEL has established his own identity as one of GCW's top up and comers. He's got the raw talent to pull of a massive victory, but does he have the ring savvy? Lia Ambrosi - 8/1/ Lia's won the match before, so how can she not be a favorite? Her doubters point to the fact that she hasn't wrestled in many big time matches recently. I don't buy that. She didn't suddenly forget how to wrestle in the last few months. Phil Atken - 30/1 Atken got a good win at WorldWide 100, even if it was assisted. He's got a good chance of winning the Television Title from Dan Black, but he's made too many enemies to survive this thing. Chris Bagwell - 6/1 Bagwell's one of the favorites to be sure. Bagwell's come up short in some big matches, but his opposition was Andy Murray. I suspect he'll have to get by Murray to win here, but you know Bagwell will be looking to even the score. Dan Black - 15/1 The Television Champion is nearing the same standard Zachariah Taylor set for the belt. Like AXEL, Black has excelled since jumping out of his former ally's shadow. But Black is still relatively inexperienced, and that's a tough hurdle to overcome. Digital Mortality - 8/1 I'm giving the United States Champion a great chance to win this thing. You don't hear much talk about Digi as a favorite, but that's only because he manages to fly under the radar. That's a huge asset in this match, as people will be targeting the flashier stars. Mr. Danger - 25/1 This match is named after Mr. Danger. It was his idea, after all. This will be the first time he's actually competed in his own match. He might want to win it just as much as anyone, but age has slowed him down. Tyson DeBough - 12/1 DeBough gets extra credit simply for being in The Establishment. You don't think Caldera would love to see DeBough win this match? He's got the talent, but does he have the focus? He's always been one big win away from superstardom. Clyde Fox - 10/1 Fox is one of the most wildly popular stars in GCW history. His lack of physical strength is a major deficiency. And does he have the awareness to survive the chaos? Fox does have a lot of friends, especially now that he's broken free of Bagwell, but friends don't go far in this match. Face - 25/1 Face has proven to be a dangerous competitor, and was even approached by Commissioner Christian Zenith with a business proposition. But with the threat he poses, he'll likely draw plenty of attention to himself, which is never a good thing. Garbage Bag Johnny - 10/1 There's no doubting the undefeated GBJ's uncanny in-ring ability. But this match requires him to focus on not one, but many opponents. Can Johnny really hone in that long and survive the anarchy? Brad Jackson - 16/1 Jackson has looked good since returning from injury, but still shows a bit of rust. Jackson is another star that's always been on the precipice of stardom, and he's got the physical skills to do it if he returns to form. LeStatt Knight - 8/1 Why is Knight not favored more heavily? He's perhaps the most physically gifted star in the world. And that's why. Knight's hellbent on winning this match, but he'll have a massive target on his back. Everyone will know that eliminating LeStatt Knight would be instant recognition. Andy Murray - 6/1 Side by side with Bagwell once again as one of the favorites. Murray defeated Knight, sort of, at WorldWide 100 and has never lost in situations like this. He won Rampage, he can win Dangerous Games. But like Knight, there's so much hype around him that the entire field will be out to get him. Bryan Mayhem - 8/1 Mayhem is the all-time record holder for most eliminations in a Dangerous Games match, and he's looked better than ever lately. Holding gold will only sharpen his thirst for more. Jacob McKail - 10/1 I love McKail in this match. He's been under the radar and he's phenomenally talented. For some reason he hasn't been able to put it all together; this is his chance to make a statement, and he knows it. Kenny Mercury - 20/1 Mercury showed up in GCW a few weeks ago looking like a wreck. I can't imagine he's turned it around fast enough to win something so large, though his rivalry with AXEL Action may wake something up. Keegan O'Conner - 30/1 O'Conner has been inactive due to injury, but we're told there's a chance he might be able to compete. Still, winning in his first match back is a long shot. Joaquin Pierre - 20/1 Pierre arrived in GCW and eliminated star-in-the-making Daria Ngozi. Can Pierre assume her position in the company? I think he'll stand out from the pack, but a win isn't likely. Rikki Roxx - 1000/1 I would add more zeroes but it's not worth the time. Chris Storm - 8/1 Storm made the Final Four last year and recently returned to television. If Storm makes the match, he'll be a clear threat to win it. Markus Stone - 16/1 Stone is every bit as talented as his tag team partner, but for whatever reason hasn't fared as well in these situations. Shaman - 10/1 New Tag Team Champion Shaman is looking to break out and get another shot at the GCW Title. He's got the size and the power, but big men historically don't fare well in this match. And can Oblivion work together or will they cancel each other out? Zacharia Taylor - 8/1 I give Taylor an excellent chance of pulling this match out. He's one of the fastest risers in the company, but he's still off the radar. If he keeps a low profile, Taylor's got the talent to announce his arrival on the big stage in a big way. Vivica J. Valentine - 4/1 Four-to-one seems like way too high for anyone in this mess, but Valentine's the only one that deserves it. Valentine won it all last year and has only improved as a wrestler since then. And Valentine's looking for ways to improve her place in GCW further; back-to-back DG wins would certainly fit the bill. Tessa Windsor - 6/1 Another under-the-radar wrestler whose star is rising fast. Not just fast; Tessa seems to be on the fast track to the big time. Armed with her new attitude and now leaping out of Valentine's shadow, Tessa is just one big win away from being the biggest star in the company. Other - 15/1 The field will also include a number of tag teams. It's always assumed that tag teams will have an advantage, but they so seldom luck into being in the ring together that it's not much of an edge. And as always there is likely to be a number of surprise entrants, and that gives them a huge edge. But historically the field has been quick to unite against anyone trying to gain an advantage by not announcing their arrival beforehand.
