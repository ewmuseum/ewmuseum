GCW's Latest Addition

17 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW?s newest superstar, Jake Reed, sat down with me just minutes after his unexpected hiring on WorldWide. Reed showed up, presumably from the ether, and ambushed Jacob McKail begging him for his forgiveness. Forgiveness for what? I pulled Reed aside on his way out of the building after his short signing meeting with GCW President Steven Caldera. I immediately questioned him about his connection to McKail. Reed had this to say? "What’s our connection? In a past federation I let some people who I trusted talk me into some bad choices. As a result of those choices McKail suffered. I’m trying to recover from that, put my life back where it was, and I need his forgiveness in order to do it." Truthfully though Reed has never so much as set foot in the same ring as Jacob McKail. Reed was associated with one of McKail’s greatest rivals and despite limited direct interaction Reed still seems to believe he has somehow wronged Jacob. When I pointed out these facts to Jake Reed he did not see them in the same light. "If I kill your kid in a drunk driving accident I never touched you but yet I have still wronged you. It’s the same way with me and McKail." Flabbergasted that Reed just compared screwing someone over in a wrestling ring to vehicular manslaughter I quickly changed the subject. I asked Jake about what he hopes to accomplish in GCW. "Well, I never came here expecting to get a job. I guess I should view this as a blessing, a second chance. All I really want is to hear the crowd cheer for me again, to have them love me again. If I can get McKail to forgive me then the fans have to embrace me? right?" Jake Reed is clearly a young man who has lost his way. Some may remember Jake as the son of beloved southern wrestling pioneer “Big” Bill Reed and, despite being only 25, he has over 10 years of in-ring experience. Jake is clearly a talented wrestler, and future star, but his current mental state seems to be placing that future in jeopardy. Reed begins his GCW journey this week on WorldWide when he faces Chris Bagwell’s associate, and Movement member, Dan Black.
