Unique Critique #6

29 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Author's Introduction: Let me start off this edition of Unique Critique with the things I took into consideration when coming up with my scores. This isn’t just about what the character does on-screen but also includes some of the contributions OOC and of course how often and how entertaining a person Role Plays (or doesn’t Role Play). I believe this is a fair assessment and if you don’t think so? Well go write your own! Lia Ambrosi Lia is the champion and has been for an impressive amount of time this year 2007. That being said, the feud with Jordan White was far from incredible. I understand that it wasn’t completely the fault of our champion BUT as the champion you have a responsibility to rise above all of that. She’s still doing good things which is why I am giving four stars but I believe there is plenty more that can be done with the character. Phil Atken Interesting character but hardly around enough to really get into it. There aren’t exact dates on the Role Plays but one a month isn’t usually going to cut it when it comes to moving up the ladder. Atken isn’t on the shows enough (in seg form) to warrant any higher a score than the one given either. Tyson DeBough I love this character! I have found his constant hounding of Caldera to join The Establishment entertaining and his feud with Jay Terror for the TV title was awesome BUT I cannot give anymore than the 3 and 1/2 stars here because of the lack of Role Playing. As of this writing it is the 29th of November and the last Tyson DeBough Role Play was October 10th. I have no doubt that this character can explode but the constant effort needs to be there. Clyde Fox The character is fairly new to GCW but, to me anyways, has made a hell of a splash so far. The Role Plays are coming in nicely (and entertaining as all you-know-what!) and he even has a strong seg presence, considering he hasn’t even had the opportunity to be on many shows as of yet. I’d be tempted to go even higher with this score but I think we need to see this kind of output go on for a bit longer. I know I’ll be keeping my eye on what lies ahead. Hydra Zero Stars Huh? Why is he even still on the roster!” (Editor's Note: Because he's a freakin' jobber!) Ray Holland I think the character is on his way and he has a strong on-air presence but hasn’t Role Played in nearly a month, so that hurts his score a little bit. The other thing is that this “feud” with Shaman really isn’t catching my interest like it should be considering Shaman is the United States Champ and I believe that title should be high up on the card. The Nashvillain No segs, One RP. I think the one star here is very warranted don’t you? Brad Jackson Great Role Player that has nothing to really go on right now and isn’t very active. When This character is on it’s something to see but right now it’s just a big disappointment. Branson King The only new member of The Establishment that has put forth effort. He’s had a few matches, gotten involved in a few storylines and has actually Role Played recently. If my sources are correct, he also has quite a good feud coming up which should do nothing but help all parties involved. Elijah Keeling Newbie but was involved in WW 70 in seg form and has produced an entertaining RP. I look forward to being able to add a couple more stars to this guy’s score in the near future. Kimbusa Zero Stars He’s on the roster but we all know what he is really used for. (Editor's Note: Fuck you.) LeStatt Knight 2007 has been a fairly off year for LeStatt Knight because of Roberto being gone for all the summer (and then some). Since coming back though I have seen a lot of activity for the recently anointed greatest World Champion in GCW history. He match writes, segs, Role Plays and now has his own column. The only reason I am not giving him a 5 is because I feel his Role Plays have been lacking a little something recently. Mark in the Dark Zero Stars He’s really in the dark? Foster Nackedy Zero Stars Him too? Octavian Zero Stars A huge disappointment so far. I have heard a lot of good things about the character of Octavian and since the character has been back the handler has been nowhere to be found. No segs or Role Plays? I was expecting a lot more. Pegasus I give a fairly middle of the road score for Pegasus because that’s exactly what we’ve gotten from him so far. He has Role Played this month so that’s a plus but the only seg we’ve seen him in was the first show that he was around for. He’ll need to step up his game to gain more love from this writer. Dorian Ryan Solid overall effort so far from one of our old-but-new guys in GCW. I’d like to see more on-air stuff going on but there’s a decent output of Role Plays but I think Dorian can be much more. Time will tell. Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins I understand that Andrew had to take off for something but this is about grading what’s going on in GCW. The only reason Rollins gets the star and a half that he has is because he has participated in a couple segs and he is in a feud with one of the best GCW has to offer (Jordan White). For the most part though this Renegade isn’t the one the world knew and loved and that’s a sad state of affairs. Rikki Roxx Does Roxx deserve anything less than FIVE!” I think not. Chris Storm One of the best GCW has to offer. From Role Playing to Segging to Match writing the character and handler have it all. What’s keeping me from giving Bobby/Storm a perfect score? The lack of a strong feud right NOW (although one is on the horizon) and the fact that I believe it’s time for Storm to move onto singles action. Christian St. Pierre There’s really nothing to report on this guy. I wish we’d see more activity but we don’t and who knows if we ever really will? Markus Stone M.I.A. ? at least we still have Storm! Shaman Loyal, hard working, dependable. Shaman comes with it every week and Andy is someone you can really count on. The Role Plays and segs leave much to be desired however so I have to knock points off for that. 'The Outlaw' Jay Terror An incredible talent GCW has itself in Jay Terror. His Role Plays are always top notch and it is my belief that he only lost the TV title at Dangerous Games because he is destined for far greater accolades. This feud with LeStatt Knight will really push Terror over the top if it is worked right (which it has been so far). I believe a win over Knight will push Terror to 5 stars and he’ll never look back. Vivica J. Valentine Billy has been on fire all year long and it was needed with GCW having a few down points throughout the past 12 months. Vivica made it to the finals in the Warpath tournament, went through a hellacious feud with Bryan Mayhem and won the DG battle royal to claim a spot in the NC-17 Main Event. If there was an award show at the end of the year then Vivica Valentine would be my vote for Wrestler of the Year. Jordan White Yes my only five star roster member at this point. Yes his feud with Lia Ambrosi, well, sucked but that was a result of lack of segs and not the talent involved. We are seeing now what Jayce can do with Jordan when he decides to Role Play and the results have been incredible. I have been reading nearly every Role Play put out in GCW and the Jordan White Role Plays are the most entertaining hands down. The world title IS in White’s future, I have no doubt about that. Tessa Windsor I’m really enjoying the Tessa character right now and with the separation from Chris Storm and Marcus Stone I am even more intrigued. Tessa has always been a character that was on back burner, never really distinguishing herself from the pack. I think that is sad because there is so much potential there and the fact that Sammie can Role Play and seg with the best of them makes the possibilities endless.
