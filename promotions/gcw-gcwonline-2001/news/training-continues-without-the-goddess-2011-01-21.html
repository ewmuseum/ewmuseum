Training continues without the Goddess...

21 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brecca Xanthus has enrolled in a private training class with one of Canada's most celebrated wrestlers as Alexia deals with family issues. The Colossus of Rhodes has left his paradise to train in a rural part of Canada under the guidance of someone who according to Alexia is "One of the most celebrated Canadian wrestler to live." We do not have the information on who exactly this trainer is but Alexia seemed confident as she spoke. The stunning Greek beauty (servants walking paces behind) spoke a few more words as she rushed through the airport to catch her flight back home. When asked why was she going back she replied "A member of my family is ill and I need to be there for them, Ive told Brecca his training is top priority for him, but for now, I'm needed at home." The self proclaimed "Goddess" spoke one more convincing line to the reporters as she walked to her gate "Its been a slow process, but I'm waiting for the perfect time, everyone thinks they have an idea of when I will unleash him, but they are sorely mistaken, Brecca will rise when I see fit." Our reporters were shoved away from following any further by her many servants. It seems Brecca is taking training rather serious, this could be a problem for the entire GCW locker room.
