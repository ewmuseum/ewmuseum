SurReal looks for Shockwave victory

19 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Irishman speaking to revered wrestling columnist Tom Mason spoke of his delight at finally getting his opportunity for retribution against Tempest. SurReal has never been someone who liked to keep his opinions private, and of late he has voiced his concerns on the direction of the professional wrestling industry. As someone who has dedicated his life to the art of wrestling, the Tribal War Machine has steadily become more and more frustrated with the current era of 'sports entertainment' that now overshadows the wrestling industry. His distaste for these 'entertainers' no more apparent than in his volatile relationship with the current GCW Television Champion, Tempest. "Let me be honest, she is good at what she does. That said, what she does is entertain, she is not a wrestler, nor will she ever be. You look across the industry today and it's rife with these entertainers, it's disgusting to see our great sport reduced to this." At Shockwave, Tempest will defend her title in an I-Quit Match. Something that pleases the Irishman greatly, most of his recent bookings having been somewhat out of his comfort zone. "At NC-17 I had no choice but to go to extreme measures. Donny Diamond had made it quite clear that he wanted to have an opportunity to have a rematch for a victory I scored over him long ago that he obviously never quite got over. And ever since Mayhem reinstated that Hardcore Championship my name has been associated with it, presumably due to my association with the now defunct Dominant Wrestling Organization. Let me take this opportunity to remind folks that I joined the DWO to clean up their act, show them that true scientific wrestling will always win the day." Unfortunately we are unable to print the Irishman’s response to Mr. Mason’s follow-up question which asked "If your technical wrestling always wins the day why is it that you have never held a world championship, yet the same cannot be said of the ‘entertainer’ Tempest?" Try as we might, there was no possible way to translate his response without risking causing offence. He did however have some final words of warning for the Cyberpunk Goddess prior to Shockwave. "It’s as simple as this. In this day and age, wrestling is a dirty word. Whilst you sports entertainers run riot in MY industry, we, the men and women who have dedicated our lives to this great art are pushed to the sidelines, bit-part players. That is going to change, mark my words bitch. At Shockwave, you had better bring everything you’ve got, because I am going to tear you limb from limb, I am going to make you scream for mercy before I decide whether or not to grant it! When all is said and done, the GCW Television Championship will be around my waist when everyone at the Aloha Stadium hears you cry ‘I… QUIT!’" On an unrelated note, SurReal expressed his delight to see Chris Storm return to the active roster in GCW. The Irishman professes a great deal of respect (who would have thought it) for the Rock Superstar. "It’s great to see a true wrestler like Storm back on GCW television. Here is hoping that justice is done and he takes the World Championship for himself. That would be a great day for technical wrestling!"
