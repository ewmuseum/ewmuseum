GCW Announces Two New DVDs

21 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling has revealed the next two videos coming to DVD. Full details inside! GCW has announced the next two special-edition DVDs it plans to release, and they're both expected to be huge. By popular demand, GCW is releasing its much anticipated Dangerous Games compilation, which is expected to be housed on six DVDs. This massive collector's set will feature every Dangerous Games match in their entirety, complete with breakdowns, behind-the-scenes looks and full statistics. The set will also include a number of famous complimentary bouts that took place at the Dangerous Games Pay-Per-View. Already confirmed to be appearing is the legendary Iron Man Match between Rich "The Renegade" Rollins and Mike Hardy at the 2005 event. GCW is hoping to begin promoting its popular Dangerous Games event even earlier this year, perhaps spawned by popular response to former GCW Champion Andy Murray's early announcement that he intends to win the 2010 match. GCW will also offer commentary tracks featuring the winners of each Dangerous Games match, though it is assumed that 2001 winner Shane Lane has not been invited to participate. Also on the docket, in anticipation of his coming retirement, is a Best of LeStatt Knight compilation. Knight himself is said to be collaborating with the GCW video division to compile his list of favorite matches, and he will be recording extensive commentary and new interviews for his career retrospective. This DVD is expected to be packed with in-ring content spanning his entire career, plus many never-before-seen moments that may reveal new truths about the GCW icon. No official release dates have been announced, but the Dangerous Games DVD is expected to be released first. Knight will begin recording his commentary in the weeks following his retirement, and his DVD should be expected by summertime.
