NJJ is JUST so excited

12 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Normal John Johnson surprised the wrestling world appearing on WorldWide 128 last week and is said to be excited by his GCW Television in-ring debut. This week, NJJ, the current Just Wrestling Champion, makes his GCW wrestling debut against T.A. Giles, who left WW128 with a victory against Slash and Bishop Steele. Yet Normal John Johnson is looking forward to going head-to-head with Giles and can't wait to take the next step in his career. "My in-ring debut on television? I'm pretty excited. It's safe to say that I'm certainly excited. I mean, sure, Just Wrestling is really awesome, y'know, being the Champion and all, but a TV debut is a whole heap of different exciting. Right?" Johnson's professional career isn't even six months old and he's captured his first wrestling championship title over in Just, and he hopes to continue that success in PTC's Elite One Shining Moment match which will give the rookie an exciting chance at the Elite Championship, much akin to GCW's own Vivica J. Valentine at SCCW's Temptation (which is set to be filmed in Iraq). "There's a lot of excitement happening in my career at this time. Just... GCW... Elite Championship... hopefully by the end of my first year in wrestling I'll have captured a few belts... not to be greedy, but I've waited a long time for this." Having unsuccessfully completed Joey Malone's wrestling school in Phoenix, Arizona, many moons ago, Johnson shelved his wrestling career to marry his childhood sweetheart and start a family. Johnson's GCW television debut is scheduled for WorldWide 129 against T.A. Giles.
