Mayhem Seeks Revenge?

11 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Octavian's toughest challenge to date? After an embarrassing loss to the Legendary Octavian Bryan Mayhem has sought revenge against the Hall of Famer. After being fined $25,000 by GCW to the destruction of his lockerroom after the loss, Mayhem is seeing red. Octavian has been through wars in his career, but many think he has never been stalked the way he is now. The 3X World Champion has angered a man known for ending careers, and for the first time ever, the crowd is behind The Lunatic. When asked about Octavian Mayhem simple says, "I have a surprise for him. I will challenge him to a match he's never seen before, and a match he can't possibly win..." Mayhem has yet to inform us of what kind of match this is, but knowing Mayhem, it won't be pretty. Stay tuned for more
