Tempest’s Detention by Chinese Authorities Continues

24 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest’s manager holds press conference to update fans on the Television Champions status. After a few days of constant calls and wild rumors circulating about Tempest’s whereabouts and just what the police might have hauled her away from Worldwide 132 for, her manager Miyuki Matsudaira finally held a press conference to answer questions and give the public the story so far. Opening with a prepared statement, she had this to say: "The government of the People’s Republic of China, acting on unsubstantiated information, has chosen to detain one of its most beloved children, despite its lack of authority to do so. Those of us representing her, both personally and with Global Championship Wrestling, have retained international legal council to resolve this situation and hope that we will be able to do so quickly. As it is, her citizenship is a challenge to this process. We remain confident that we can secure her release before Rampage and indeed that China will be satisfied that it has chosen poorly in detaining her at all and we will expect a formal apology for this incident." Questions were asked by members of the press and are summarized here: Darren Stearns, Squared Circle Scene: Where exactly is Tempest and is she all right? With China’s record on human rights, may of her fans and friends are keen to keep her name in the forefront of people’s minds until she’s back on GCW TV where we can see her. Matsudaira: I’m not at liberty to disclose her location, but I have spoken to her in person and can assure you she is well. Despite a hard match with Shaman to secure her place in the semi-finals of Rampage, she’s fine and she has been treated well by the authorities. James Bryan, GCW: What exactly did the Chinese authorities arrest her for? You remarked on her citizenship, but I’m not sure I understand why that should be at issue if she’s re-entering the country of her birth. Matsudaira: I’m not at liberty to give out specifics, and in fact, as I said earlier, it is our position that her detention is entirely unlawful. What complicates matters is… well, Tempest isn’t exactly a citizen of anywhere. She was born in Hong Kong, that much she will admit to, but there is no official record of her birth. Also, that was before it was part of China and she was born to a British citizen and a native of Hong Kong. She has also been in the United States since 1994, and was married to a Canadian citizen. So the matter is complicated. Jimmy Brazzo, Between the Dirtsheets: Wait, whoa, married? Since when? And are you sure this isn’t a giant cover-up for Tempest and Vivica J. Valentine sneaking off to some opium smoke filled love nest to lick each other’s wounds and… Matsudaira: Who let you in here? Honestly, I’m not going to dignify that with an answer. (Brazzo is escorted to the nearest exit by GCW security.) Dean Shute, Mat Matters: Notwithstanding the ridiculous, there have been rumors of some kind of connection between the arrest of Tempest and apparent arrest of Viveca Valentine, including that Valentine may be recuperating from her injuries at a home owned by Tempest. Is there any truth to either rumor? Matsudaira: Tempest does not own any property in Hong Kong. If they have anything in common it would be the ill fated interest of one Christian Zenith, who I learned was the ‘concerned citizen’ who informed certain authorities with an interest in Tempest of her plans to return to Hong Kong. So this is entirely his doing as far as I’m concerned. Tempest’s penchant for trouble seems to have gone too far for even her this time and as she misses Worldwide 133 and doubts linger about whether she will get herself out of it in time for Rampage, one wonders whether a brilliant plot of former GCW commissioner Christian Zenith has come to fruition or if this is just the beginning volley in a war that will escalate even further, starting at Rampage.
