Chris Bagwell Challenges PRIME's Lindsay Troy

21 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's no secret that Chris Bagwell has been stirring up trouble among Globel Championship Wrestling over the past several weeks throughout the locker rooms. It seems like just about everyone is eager to jump on line to give Bagwell's mouth a good whack. With that said, it should come as no surprise to hear that Bagwell's name has slowly leaked out to other members of Primetime Central. His lack of respect for just about every superstar in PRIME and Sin City Championship Wrestling has gotten a lot of people talking. And his lack of acknowledgement for women's rights has gotten under the skin of one, Lindsey Troy. For those that listened in to PRIMETIME CENTRAL RADIO this past week, you may recall the brief spitting match between Chris Bagwell and Lindsey Troy. Bagwell made it a point in commanding Lindsey to "go back into the kitchen." Obviously, the comment did not sit well with the former PRIME Universal and current PTC Unified Champion. She had a number of choice words for the leader of the Bagwell Movement, harping specifically on his recent Rampage loss to Andy Murray and also on his prior loss to GCW World Champion Vivica J. Valentine. "I don't know where this b*tch gets off coming on MY show and talking shit to ME" said Bagwell in his post radio show commentary. "And to top it off, she had the audacity reference my lack of PTC accomplishments. I was winning championships while this girl was trying to figure out that her tampon belongs in her vagina and not her ass!" Bagwell went on to say that he's confident in Vivica's chances against Lindsey Troy at this week's WorldWide, for the Unified Title belt. "This little slut couldn't even hold on to her own hoaky organization's championship belt. You mean to tell me she's going to come in and beat the World Champion of the single greatest company left in existance? Yeah, alright." When asked about Troy's comments regarding her immediate success in PRIME, Bagwell had this to say: "Blowing people gets you far in love. You suck off the boss and you get a title shit. Simple as that. Look at Jay Terror." After having a number of other derogatory words for Lindsey Troy, Bagwell closed his interview with an open challenge to the former PRIME Universal Champion. "Let Lindsey Troy pick the time. Let her pick the date. Let her pick the place. Hell, let her even pick the organization. This is an open challenge to the b*tch. If she's as good as she says she is, she'll stop dancing around the question. But the fact is, she's afraid of what will happen if...when she loses." It's believed that Lindsey will merely just dismiss Bagwell's challenge, but he assures us he'll continue to harass her until she acknowledges it. Will Troy ultimately accept? Her match with Valentine this week could be the deciding factor in that equation.
