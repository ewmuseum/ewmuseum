WW106: Invasion of Troy

1 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just when things seemed most dire for Vivica J. Valentine, she got an assist from the unlikeliest of people. Faced with a rematch against Tyson DeBough and Brian Fisher in which she had no partner, Valentine called the only person left on the planet that could help her. Despite some rocky personal history, Lindsay Troy answered that call and aided Valentine to a massive victory. Valentine now finds herself in a position to challenge Banned and Exiled~! for the GCW Tag Team Titles. Speculation immediately began swirling that Valentine is attempting to keep Troy on as her partner, and GCW may be preparing a short term contract for Troy. It remains to be seen if Troy will sign the deal, but Troy cashing a GCW paycheck would be a major story, especially considering her rocky past with GCW Owner Steven Caldera. Troy, a mega-star in GCW's rival PRIME, has made a successful side-career out of being a thorn in Caldera's side. Troy has done everything from eliminating Caldera from the Dangerous Games match to pinning him one on one in a GCW ring. Caldera added to the personal rivalry by refusing to air Troy's victory over Vivica J. Valentine on a controversial episode of WorldWide last year. Since then Troy has not appeared in GCW, making her return all the more surprising. But with Caldera away from the company and the opportunity to tweak him presenting itself once again, it may have been impossible for Troy to resist. We'll be back soon with a look to next week's event, where the world will be tuning in to find out if Lindsay Troy will return once again. Next week's event is also rumored to feature a number of high profile title defenses, so don't miss WorldWide 107 on X:TV.
