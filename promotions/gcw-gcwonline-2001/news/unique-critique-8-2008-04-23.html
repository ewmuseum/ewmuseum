GCW — Column

Unique Critique #8

23 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Author's Introduction: This week's Unique Critique rates all superstars on a combination of role-play quality, overall in-character GCW involvement, and out-of-character GCW contributions (by the handler). Lia Ambrosi Since losing the World Championship to Vivica Valentine, Lia has essentially fallen under the radar. Who once recently was a superstar that carried the GCW on her back has now, in my eyes, become just another big name on the roster that doesn’t seem to really be doing too much. When she does put out work, Lia is a phenomenal character and a pleasure to follow. However, her recent lack of showing up has really put a damper on her reputation. The flop of a match with LeStatt Knight wasn't much of a help, either. But since her recent decrease in GCW activity is only short lived, it doesn't affect her rating TOO greatly. Phil Atken Not much to say about Phil except that there has constantly been a ton of hype behind this guy and he hasn't done much with it at all. I feel like I've seen more work from guys that have only been here a few weeks than I have from Phil Atken in several months. It’s my feeling that someone should talk to this guy and see what his agenda is. Either he's going to do something with himself or he's going to get off the roster. I did take the time to read through some of the things he's written in his GCW career and none of which is terrible. However, nothing he's done seems to be convincing enough to have him sit on a roster spot that he's not even using. Seymour Almasy After over a year of absence from the GCW, Seymour returns to the picture and immediately makes an impact felt. Since his return, he's made it a point to be seen on every show that the GCW has put out (both in segments and matches). I think I speak for the majority in saying that his return debut was definitely a shocker and something that nobody can really say they saw coming. With his fresh new look and a fresh new era in GCW, I see this guy really making an impact over the next several weeks. As a handler, Sean has certainly wasted no time introducing himself to all those that he was unacquainted with. After talking with a number of people, I've heard nothing but good things about Sean's personality and his eagerness to befriend his fellow role-players. And he's certainly no stranger to contributing to the forums and keeping them alive. There's nothing better than a little camaraderie. Chris Bagwell Here's a guy that came into the GCW and immediately ticked off the right people. He got a World Title shot in his third match, was challenged by LeStatt Knight a week later, and has gotten on just about every guy (or gal)'s nerves in the locker rooms at one time or another. And you know what? You've got to love it! He's a quick witted trash talker that we all just love to hate. Bagwell jumped right into the mix of things and made it seem like he's been here forever, with his frequent PTC Radio call-ins and his constant barrage of stable members challenging everyone who ever even thought of wrestling. Bagwell definitely has all the right tools to make something big of his time in GCW. Although some may view his Rampage loss to Andy Murray as a set-back, I see it more as an opportunity to really explode. I'd like to see the Murray/Bagwell feud continue, ideally. But I wouldn't be opposed to seeing this guy dance around elsewhere. As far as out of character, Shane has proven to be a very solid member to our GCW community. He's responsible for a number of well-designed Poser characters and is frequently surfing the forums. Again I stress -- nothing better than camaraderie! Dan Black & Kaden Calix Both are brand new. No rating yet. Digital Mortality Digi is a guy I wasn't certain about when he first came into the GCW. His name kind of gave me the impression he was going to follow a Mortal Kombat gimmick or something. But to my pleasant surprise, Digi has proven to be an excellent contribution to the roster. I particularly like the way Loz ties together all of his role-plays to the point where if you didn’t read the previous one, you’re essentially screwed out of understanding the new one. I’m sure some shun this idea, but I think it does a good job to promote his writing. He’s essentially forcing people to read ALL of his work, thus building a fan-base. I’d love to see Digi get an opportunity soon to perhaps challenge Tessa for her US Title. Out of character, Loz has one of the most upbeat personalities on the GCW Roster. He’s a friendly guy, always looking to throw in a couple good words of advice. What was that “C” word I kept using? Tyson DeBough I debated on writing anything for Tyson, based on the principal that he hasn’t really been around in recent months. However, I will go out and say that I was very shocked to see his comeback and even more shocked when he cost Clyde the TV title match. I look at Tyson a lot like Phil Atken, which some may disagree with. I think he’s got a hyped name, but I honestly haven’t seen much of him. Hopefully, his return to GCW will ignite a very nice “retribution” feud with the likes of Clyde Fox. Clyde Fox Clyde Fox is hands down one of my favorite characters currently in the GCW. Clyde’s last role-play, for Rampage, was both incredibly unique and incredibly hilarious. My only complaint about the role-play is that it lacked a lot of actual writing and, instead, focused mostly on the video. I think if he were to tie in short clips of the video with more actual writing, he may have actually retained at the PPV. Nevertheless, Clyde is constantly a riot to read and we certainly haven’t seen the last of him as TV Champion. I’d LOVE to see his feud with Zacharia Taylor grow, as the contrast between their characters does wonders for them both. Out of character, Ross has really been doing a great job of keeping me entertained between PTC Radio broadcasts with the Ross Is Right webcasts. Definitely a funny guy! Brad Jackson Brad is another character that I’ve contemplated not writing about, only because he’s essentially been out of action since NC-17. Nevertheless, he is back and definitely has a tremendous amount of potential (as per his previous role-plays). I’d love to see Jackson stir things up with a guy like Thom LeStrange, but I’m a bit worried Jackson will soon be sitting next to Roger Clemens in a Human Growth Hormone scandal. Kiljaden Kincaid There isn’t much to say about Kincaid, being as though he’s a non-playable character in the GCW. I think the character of Kincaid has tremendous potential to really be something big, if he were to be controlled. However, I definitely think he serves his purpose as one of Bagwell’s many cronies. His massive size makes him a major factor in determining whether or not you want to mix yourself up with Bagwell, for fear of his groupies joining the party. LeStatt Knight There are two things that I’ve wanted to see in the GCW for a while. One was Jay Terror turn heel, and the other was LeStatt Knight breaking away from the Establishment. Well, I must’ve been a good boy this year. I tend to think that the Establishment was really holding LeStatt back a lot. I tend to think of The Rock’s days with the Nation of Domination. The stable can only do so much for a guy’s career before he’s obligated to go off on his own. And for LeStatt, the timing couldn’t be better. My only real peeve is that since LeStatt separated himself from the Establishment, we haven’t seen him really pump out any work. I was really hoping for a more dominating showing from a post-Establishment Knight, but I guess we’ll just have to wait around for that. Ideally, I’d love to see Knight jump back into the World Title scene. I think a Knight/Valentine match would be incredible. Hell, I’d love even more to toss Jay Terror in the mix for a three way dance. It doesn’t seem like LeStatt has even been given a legitimate title shot in quite some time, so it would definitely be well deserved. This, of course, is assuming we start seeing a lot more work from LeStatt again. Thom LeStrange Thom is a tremendous writer and his work with Andy Murray has done nothing but prove that. However, despite his skills, he seems to have come up short in all the matches that really mattered. I had my money partially split in LeStrange’s triple threat qualifying match against Bagwell and Dorian Ryan for the Rampage tournament. I felt confident in seeing LeStrange pull a win ? but he didn’t. Then there was the TV Title match at the Rampage PPV, where LeStrange had an AWESOME chance to steal the spotlight ? buutttt he came up short again. I don’t necessarily attribute LeStrange’s big losses to him not writing that well. Rather, I look at it as LeStrange being the kind of guy who brings out the best in other people. I think it’s safe to say that Bagwell and Taylor were both worried about LeStrange pulling a come-from-behind win and so they pushed EXTRA hard for the win. I mean, take a look at Andy Murray. Without taking anything away from Murray, a great deal of his GCW fame is because of his stints with LeStrange. My only advice to Thom LeStrange is to stop letting other people steal the limelight and get the win in the big match! Andy Murray Impact. If there’s one word that can describe Andy Murray’s time in the GCW, it’s IMPACT. And no, I’m in no way referring to the lame show put on weekly by Total Nonstop Action. Andy Murray joined the GCW a few months ago and quickly propelled himself into the high mid-card, climbing over all the other members in his enrollment class and becoming the winner of the Rampage tournament. His work with Thom LeStrange has been absolutely outstanding and his roleplays themselves have been mind-riveting. Murray is a guy who, undoubtedly, should be in the World Title scene VERY soon. As I mentioned earlier, I’d love to see a feud really boil with Bagwell, mostly stemming from the Rampage match. These two guys have a tremendous amount of hatred for each other and I look forward to their radio show antics each and every week. I could go on and on and boast about Murray, but I’ll cut it short and say that this guy is my pick for a future World Champion. Bryan Mayhem I like the character of Bryan Mayhem, with the whole face paint thing and what-not. It seems like every guy in the roster these days is some muscle head, gimmick-less, fighter looking to essentially just whip some ass. Granted, Mayhem still fits two of those three categories, but at least he does it with a neat looking getup! I haven’t seen much of Mayhem’s work, but what I have seen isn’t half bad. Although, I must say, I do expect a lot more from a guy who used to be a World Champion. Perhaps it’s the ring rust, but I just don’t see Mayhem living up his hype either. However, it may just be too early to make that decision. He did JUST make his return debut. My advice to Mayhem is just to prove me wrong and start cranking out stuff to PROVE why you were once a World champ. Keegan O'Conner At the time of this writing, Keegen hasn’t done much in GCW so out of fairness; I will not rate him either. However I do want to go ahead and say that Josh, as a handler, seems to be a fantastic guy. He’s got no problem jumping into the mix of things. I look forward to seeing what Keegen can show us. Octavian I’m again going to reference Phil Atken here. Octavian, to me, is extremely over-hyped and he doesn’t seem to produce the material that his hype builds him up to be. When I hear about Octavian, I instantly think of him as being right up there with the likes of Vivica, Jordan White, or Jay Terror. Yet I feel like the work he puts out makes him a mid-carder at best. Perhaps it’s the fact that he’s a hall of famer and a three time World Champion that I hold him in higher regard while reading. Who knows! All I’ll say is that Octavian really doesn’t live up to the hype at all. But maybe we’ll soon see some change! Dorian Ryan Dorian was, at one point, one of my initial picks to win the Rampage tournament. However, he quickly lost my faith in that pick after no-showing the first and second rounds. His roleplaying has seemingly been on a hiatus, with the exception of like one or two over the last several weeks. As a handler, JJ is a great guy who’s done a bit to help backstage. But as a character, Dorian Ryan is virtually falling under the radar. Rikki Roxx Rikki Roxx is perhaps one of the most lovable characters that the GCW has to offer. His laid back, “cool” retro personality is a ringer for me EVERY time. I pray for the day that Billy puts Vivica on the bench and starts roleplaying as Rikki Roxx. This guy would be a DYNAMITE mid-card personality! And who can ignore the weekly Rikki Roxx? Retro Ring Entrance. Talk about alliteration! Major props to Billy for such a wonderful creation. Blake Scott At the time of this writing, Blake Scott was too new for me to feel comfortable rating him. Banned & Exiled~! Little can be said about this duo that hasn’t been said already. B&E~! is by far one of the best GCW has to offer. Aside from being the tag champs for over a year now, B&E~! have single (or double) handedly held together the GCW tag team division. Their roleplays are always well written and their segments always hilarious. Bobby, as a handler, is among the best in the business. Not to knock anyone else, but I look forward to Bobby’s feedback the MOST after every show. He really put a lot of time into reading through everyone’s material and he gives honest, helpful, constructive criticism. He’s not afraid to tell it like it is and in such a fashion where you WANT to learn from what he says. Bobby is by far one of GCW’s richest assets. Shaman Shaman is one of the few guys left in the game that still roleplays primarily about his opponent. That’s something I can truly respect. Being an old-timer myself, I can remember the days when a match was decided on how quick-witted your character was and how well he got over with people. It seems that a lot of roleplaying these days is centered on telling a story, largely having nothing to do with wrestling. I, myself, am guilty of that. With that said, I give Andy tremendous props for sticking to his guns. My only peeve about Shaman is his latest transition in storylines. A few weeks ago, Shaman was the US champion. He then goes and drops the belt to Tessa Windsor to get a huge push into the World Title picture. Up to this point, I’m liking where its going. However, Shaman ends up losing to Vivica and falls out of the limelight only to follow up his post-Rampage match with another loss. I think Andy is at a point where he’s already done as much as he can with Shaman in the mid-card and now he’s REALLY gotta pump extra hard to boast this character into the main event. I’d like to see Shaman’s presence in the main event last longer than only a single match. And I’d love to see this losing streak take a turn for the better. 'The Outlaw' Jay Terror Is he the Outlaw, or should I start referring to him as “The Established?” That’s the gimmick now, right? In one of the best (and most predictable) moves I’ve seen in a while, Jay Terror sells his soul to Caldera’s clan. And with good reason. Jay Terror just SCREAMS heel when I see his character. I just couldn’t buy the whole babyface thing. Now, with Caldera’s backing, Terror goes on to get his World Title opportunity. All I have to say to that is that I hope Vivica didn’t get too attached to the title belt. There’s not a doubt in my mind that Terror is going to be the one who dethrones our otherwise perfect current world champion. As a handler, Dan is ranked way up there with the best of them. He’s a frequenting poser artist and an all-around nice guy to chat with on the forums. Dan, keep up the good work. You’re definitely one of the hardest working guys in the game today. And you’re still so damn young, too. Zacharia Taylor Here’s a guy that came into the GCW with a questionable gimmick that you’d see in early 1990’s WWF, under the alias “Docta Z.” He was nothing more than a sidekick to Chris Bagwell, living underneath the enormous shadow Bagwell was casting for himself. Next thing you know, Taylor drops the nickname and explodes into the TV Title scene, stealing a victory and the belt. Taylor’s feud with Clyde Fox is absolutely awesome. Those two guys, alone, gave the TV title some well-deserved respect. I’d like to see Taylor continuously break away from Bagwell’s shadow and really make an individual name for himself. He’s been progressing with his writing at tremendous speeds and I wouldn’t be surprised to see him dabbling his nose into the US title scene in the near future. Perhaps a dual title holder? If anyone, I think Taylor has the ability to really pull it off and shock a lot of people. Vivica J. Valentine Without a doubt, Billy is the glue that holds GCW together. When hell freezes over and the GCW starts crumbling, Billy has surfaced and brought the place back together. I can remember one instance, in particular, where Billy stepped up and took complete control over putting together a Worldwide show because Steven Caldera had run into a few personal problems. I think we owe a little bit of gratitude to Billy for his ongoing devotion. Aside from being the genius behind Rikki Roxx, Billy is also the genius behind being a guy in a woman’s world. Vivica has proven to be an excellent GCW Champion and is constantly producing mind blowing roleplays to erase any doubt that she should in fact be on the top. I’m EXTREMELY eager to see the showdown between Vivica and Lindsay Troy and I’m confident in saying that Billy will walk out the winner of that match. Nothing against Lindsay, but I just think Billy wants it more than anyone else in the PTC right now. If he’s not being considered already, Billy will undoubtedly end up in the GCW Hall of Fame with the paths he’s taking. Jordan White I’m going to rank Jordan White in my top 3 favorite characters in GCW. I’m also going to rank him as the top 1 most underrated character on the roster. While the Round Robin was still going on, every other participant was predicted to win the whole thing by somebody. Not a single person paid any attention to Jordan White, yet he was the only guy who had went undefeated through the whole thing. It absolutely sucks to see White coming up on the short end of the stick against Jay Terror, but I guess that’s how it goes sometimes. I really hope to see White fight his way back into the title picture, and maybe rally Terror for a rematch. Jordan White has what it takes to carry the GCW on his back. He now just needs the opportunity to shine. Tessa Windsor If Jordan White was #1 most underrated character, Tessa Windsor is #2. What makes Tessa second to Jordan White is only the fact that her talent IS, in fact, recognized. Tessa’s sudden jump into US Championship Gold was well-deserved, but at the same time restricting. I think that when she’s challenged, Tessa puts out World Title-level material. It’s just unfortunate that not everyone can be gunning for the top spot at the same time! Nevertheless, Tessa is going to ultimately make the United States title just as coveted as the World Title, and perhaps even more competitive. There’s no person I’d rather see as US champ right now than Tessa Windsor. I also wouldn’t mind seeing a match against Digital Mortality or Thom LeStrange. As I mentioned before, Thom seems to be able to bring out the best in everyone he faces and that may only further my argument that Tessa belongs in the World Title picture. Church of the Unholy There are so many of these guys that I’m not even sure who handles who, or which guy is which. Just I’m going to direct most of my comments to the voice of the Church, James von Doom. Von Doom is without a doubt one of the funniest, strangest, creepiest, and nicest guys on the roster ? all in one. He’s got a ton of ideas jumbled up inside his head and just loves to throw them out there. However, that seems to be hurting him. I think what von Doom lacks is a bit of focus. This topic popped up on the forums recently, and I can’t help but agree. James von Doom is a force to be reckoned with, when he’s concentrating on one thing. I’d like to see the COTU drop about 998 of their other characters and concentrate solely on writing for Axel Action and James von Doom. I also agree that some of the really far-fetched segments and such should be cut down. I’m not saying to get rid of all the craziness all together, because that’s what makes the COTU so much fun to read. I’m just saying to try and keep it a bit more realistic and toned down, where it’s actually semi-believable. And even then, the possibilities are ENDLESS! Cowboys from Hell There hasn’t really been much released by the Cowboys from Hell yet, but I do like where the gimmick can go. Once again, JJ has kind of been falling under the radar a bit in the GCW and I may even credit that to writers block. In my opinion, he’d be in his best interest to maybe put the Dorian Ryan character on hiatus and really build up the Cowboys from Hell. With the tag division finally growing, I think there’s a lot more opportunity for JJ to make a statement here than there is in the singles division. Rebel Express and Samson & Delylah Both teams are too new for me to want to rate. But I will say that I’m glad to finally see some sparking interest in the tag team division!
