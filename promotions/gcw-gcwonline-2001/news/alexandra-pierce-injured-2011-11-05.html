Alexandra Pierce Injured?

5 November 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After two grueling matches at the Rampage pay-per-view, sources say the GTT7 Champion is injured -- but how badly? It appears that the Queen of Lies was telling the truth. After suffering what appeared to be some form of knee injury in her match with Alexander Redding at WorldWide 154, questions circled the controversial superstar. Was she injured, or was she engaged in another of her infamous charades, exaggerating the extent of the injury? Could the Spider be trusted, or was she simply spinning more webs. Independent sources have told GCWOnline.net that Alexandra Pierce was checked into the California Hospital Medical Center following her loss to Johnny Borealis in the 2011 Rampage Finals. Reports are that the missed moonsault kneedrop she attempted against Redding resulted in a Grade 2 sprain to her left posterior cruciate ligament (PCL). The injury was exacerbated in her matches against Borealis and especially Aaron Fujita, but is said not to require surgery at this time. Doctors have instructed Pierce to avoid physical activity for the next few weeks and she was seen walking out of the CHMC on crutches following treatment. Pierce, who had not suffered a pinfall or submission loss since the 2010 King of Kings event against Violence Jack, has been performing for both GCW and PRIME since March's NC-17 event, an ambitious schedule that racked up almost 15,000 miles of travel during PRIME's worldwide. However, PRIME has not put on an event since the end of July, so demands on her injured leg will be much less intense over the next couple of weeks. However, reports are circulating that Alexandra will prominently placed on the card for the 155th edition of WorldWide (lineup to be announced shortly). It remains to be seen if the Spider is intending to compete against doctor's orders. More updates as we receive them.
