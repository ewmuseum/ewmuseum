Peter Vetra set to retire?

19 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After his performance at High Stakes, Peter Vetra is heavily thinking about retiring. As it was seen to all the watched on television Peter was carried out on a stretcher and it appears that he has a broken his leg. With that he is thinking about retiring. After having a successful run as the first ever Vanguard Champion, Peter went into High Stakes on a good note defeating one of his opponent and Jayce Bradley the week before. However in a match of this caliber Peter could just not get the win and actually tapped out to the Viceroy. Early reports are that Peter Vetra has broken his leg and will be out for a long period of time, which is why he is thinking of actually retiring. If you tuned in you would see that Peter would not need to worry about money as he placed a beat on the Monarch Butterfly before the evening as if he knew she was going to win. Quick numbers say he won somewhere close to 250,000 thousand dollars on the bet. GCW members are expecting to hear from Peter at this weeks World Wide 65 to comment on his injuries, on his winnings, the vacancy of the belt, and of course his FUTURE~! Tune in this week
