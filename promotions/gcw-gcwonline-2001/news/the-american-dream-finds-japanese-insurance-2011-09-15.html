The American Dream Finds Japanese Insurance

15 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aaron Fujita was recently spotted discussing important business with one of GCW's Japanese stars. Which one is it? READ ON TO FIND OUT! While Aaron Fujita and Karina Wolfenden have exchanged words over GCW's website in recent weeks, our sources tell us that the holder of the American Dream may be seeking to take more affirmative action towards using his banked shot to take down the vaunted and dangerous K-Wolf. In the days leading up to WorldWide 153, Fujita was seen at a Phoenix-area steakhouse, taking in a meal with GCW newcomer Ryuji Kamigawa. While GCW attempted to send a camera crew to capture this conversation, the crew arrived too late to record any footage, leaving us with little to rely on about the crux of this meeting save for eyewitness accounts, and a blurry cameraphone photograph that proves the meeting happened. "They were seated at a table in the back," GCW fan Michael Williams reported, once our camera crew arrived on the scene. "Fujita covered the tab. It was hard to overhear exactly what they were saying, but the name "Karina Wolfenden" was mentioned numerous times." Another fan in attendance, Ryan Ratcliffe, paints a more sinister picture. "I was a table away from them," he told GCW reporters. "Fujita spent most of the meal trying to hire Kamigawa to take out the K-Wolf so he could cash in his shot. Kamigawa didn't talk much. He really just sort of listened, until the end, when he asked if Fujita could pay his rate. Fujita nodded, and told him to stay on call, just in case." Neither Aaron Fujita nor Karina Wolfenden could be reached for comment on this story, however, GCW Online was able to get in touch with Ryuji Kamigawa, who had the following to say: "What Mr. Fujita and I were discussing is our business, and our business only. I will, however, say that he wishes to hire me to take care of some business on his behalf. I do not find this unusual, indeed; if you ask anyone backstage in Global Championship Wrestling, they will all tell you have I have an open and standing offer for such things, provided one can compensate me properly." More on this developing news story as we receive it, but at this time, it seems clear that Aaron Fujita is tired of waiting for Karina Wolfenden to be vulnerable, and would rather create an opportunity for himself to take the second most prestigious championship in all of professional wrestling.
