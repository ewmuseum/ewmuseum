Bagwell Bails on Worldwide

16 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The career of Chris Bagwell has thrived over the last year on the superstar’s ability to run his mouth and always turn the tables on every situation thrown at him. However, after his defeat by former World Champion Vivica J. Valentine at NC-17, some speculate that Chris Bagwell may have finally been silenced. The world was watching this past week on Worldwide to see what Bagwell’s reaction would be to what some call a humiliating defeat at the hands of the one hundred thirty pound female prodigy. Would he point the blame finger on someone else? Would he snap and continue his backstage rampage? Or would he dismiss the situation entirely? But Bagwell never showed. Worldwide 105 ran its course and without a single showing of the self proclaimed Rajah of Ratings. According to sources backstage, Bagwell never even entered the building that night, leading some to believe his shame must be astronomical. Yet could there be something more to the story? It’s been Bagwell’s trademark to make his opinion very clear on-air. Others are speculating there is more to the story than we’re aware. Chris Bagwell’s recent reunion with long-time girlfriend Rebecca has apparently had him in question of his career. Nevertheless, it was assumed that Bagwell’s pride would keep him in the business for a long time. No real explanation could be provided for Bagwell’s absence and Bagwell couldn’t be reached for any comments. The question now remains ? have we seen the last of the “2008 Heel of the Year” in the GCW locker room?
