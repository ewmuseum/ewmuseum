Twisted Flesh World Tour 2008

9 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Church of the Unholy is proud to announce it's Twisted Flesh World Tour 2008. This rumor was proved to be true by Evil James von Doom earlier today. Over the past week, rumors of some sort of 'World Tour' involving the Church of the Unholy have been rampant in the underground for weeks. There have been rumors about them participating in barbed wire matches, deathmatches, inferno matches, flaming cage matches, exploding cage matches, and other types of violent, brutal matches. Evil James did not comment on this. However, he did say that "a pint of blood for Lord Tioneb will surely be a good omen for the future." Evil James did not comment on this. However, he did say that "a pint of blood for Lord Tioneb will surely be a good omen for the future." Evil James also had some sort of comments about GCW and the role of the Church there. "This is the best place for us," he said with a smile when asked about it. "GCW is the perfect place for people of our persuasion. However, it is sickening to me that they don't seem to see the true power and influence that we hold in the wrestling world." "Instead of talking about us and the things we are doing, all I'm hearing about is Seymour Almasy and Chris Bagwell. Well folks, that is about to change. If we have to decapitate people in the ring, we will. The top people may be talked about all over the wrestling world, but deep in the underground, they are talking about us." "James von Doom, AXEL Action. I've had a cult following for years in this business and now that AXEL has joined the fray, we can only go up from here. For years I have been on the cusp of greatness and so has AXEL. We shall have that glory very soon." When asked what he meant, Evil James had a devious smile on his face. "I am going to make a Nostradamus-esque prediction right now," he said with a smile. "I am predicting that we will END the Age of Banned and Exiled~! and take the GCW World Tag Team Championship belts from them." "I know people are going to read this and say that I'm just being 'insane' like they usually do but this time we will prove them wrong. The end of the world is coming soon. I have forseen it. The Rebellion against the Antichrist will be formed in the coming years and soon you will see that I was right and I was the good guy, not the villain. The same can be said for AXEL." "But that's another story for another time. GCW, PTC, TEAM, everyone who comes across this. I am predicting that 2008 will be the year of the Church of the Unholy. And soon you will call us the Saviors of the Wrestling World. Our influence has already been felt in the PTC and in the presidential race with Senator Johnathan Richard Mabus being endorsed by us. We are the future of wrestling and we have no fear of death. The Dark Age is upon us. Let the darkness seep into your souls and join us on ther Dark Side before it is too late..." Evil James then left the interview abruptly and headed back to the Church of the Unholy's headquarters in Rome, Italy to meet with AXEL Action and other Church leaders to discuss the World Tour.
