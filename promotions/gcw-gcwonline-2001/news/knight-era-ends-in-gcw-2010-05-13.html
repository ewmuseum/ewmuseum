Knight Era Ends in GCW

13 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another major era has ended in Global Championship Wrestling, as LeStatt Knight departed with a win over Vivica J. Valentine at Shockwave. It's over. In an epic war that will no doubt live on for another decade to come, LeStatt Knight's ten year career ended at Shockwave when he pinned Vivica J. Valentine in the center of the ring. This week GCW will be bringing you a number of retrospectives on the career of LeStatt Knight, culminating in the Best of LeStatt Knight DVD to be released in early June. But for now, we can only pause to weigh the size of the changes that have swept GCW this year. First, at NC-17, GCW Founder, Owner and President Steven Caldera stepped aside and handed control of his company to Garbage Bag Johnny. At Shockwave now, GCW's biggest star stepped aside. Who he is handing his spot to remains to be seen. With Caldera and Knight now gone, Bryan Mayhem remains the only remnant of the original GCW. A significant void has opened up at the top. While Garbage Bag Johnny has done a spectacular job of filling GCW's power void, the gap created by LeStatt Knight is not so easily filled. Not only did his retirement cost GCW its true icon, but it also cost the company its reigning World Champion. With the Rampage Tournament coming up and a number of new stars hoping to move in on Knight's spot, the only thing we know for sure is that the void won't be empty for long. Stay tuned all week as GCW looks back over the career of LeStatt Knight, and looks forward to a future filled with promising new stars who are aspiring to the same kind of greatness Knight achieved in Global Championship Wrestling.
