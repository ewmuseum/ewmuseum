Evil James Claims Conspiracy!

10 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As he was leaving WorldWide this week, I caught up with Evil James and he was none too pleased with what went down in the Church of the Unholy's World Tag Team Championship match this week against Banned & Exiled~! When asked about what happened this is what he had to say As he was leaving WorldWide this week, I caught up with Evil James and he was none too pleased with what went down in the Church of the Unholy's World Tag Team Championship match this week against Banned & Exiled~! When asked about what happened this is what he had to say. "It's a complete conspiracy against us," he growled with a snarl. "As far as I'm concerned, we are the GCW World Tag Team Champions right now. The tapes show it. We had Stone pinned and the ref counted the three. Storm made the save AFTER the three count. The ref's hand struck down for the three count before Storm made the save. Look at the tapes and see for yourself. We are the uncrowned champions. Those belts should be with us now!" At this point he slammed his suitcase against the wall and turned back to me with a crazed look on his face. "And then why in the hell are the Rebel Express allowed out there? See, like I said, it is a complete conspiracy! People don't want us to win or succeed because we're controversial. We're racy. We don't implore the good public image that most wrestlers are asked to present. Instead we kill small animals, have sex with hookers, and we create chaos. We try to be " I asked him what he meant by 'good public image' and this is what he said. "Not us. We're not good role models" And that is all he had to say about that. I then asked him about the Church's plans for this week after their loss and he had this to say. "We have no plans at this time," he said firmly. "We honestly could care less about anything right now except for High Stakes. See, we don't have anyone to hurt on this show yet. We'd like to bloody up the Southern Rebels, but that would be too easy." "See, they spend all of their time in the back cowering with fear of us. They're afraid of us, that's why they didn't do jack shit when they were out there at ringside this week. They are scared of us and therefore are too inferior to face us at High Stakes." When I asked him what he meant by that, he smirked deviously and responded with his usual bit of sarcasm. "What I mean is this. When we come out to the ring and have to wait for them to come out, you know what'll happen? We'll have to go to the back, break open their locker room door, and then we would have to DRAG them to the ring just so they would show up for the match. That's how afraid of us they are." "And let's not forget about the Cowboys from Hell. They are on our hit list too. As is anybody who our buddy Chris Bagwell and the Movement need taken out. We are going to make an impact on WorldWide this coming week and we will hurt somebody. That is a guarantee." He then snatched up his suitcase and stormed out of the building.
