Jay Terror: Aftermath

19 September 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

After last night’s vicious onslaught on Markus Stone, Jay Terror mysteriously collapsed in the center of the ring only to come back to life to flip off the world. People have become used to the antics of The Outlaw for his entire career, but something felt different about last night’s send-off. As it turns out, there was something different about it. Jay Terror may never return to a GCW ring. In a closed interview after the show, Jay Terror revealed that for eight months he has been battling Small Cell Lung Cancer, an aggressive form of the disease that requires intensive care. Of course, this would help identify why Jay seemed to be much thinner and much weaker than he has been seen before. When it comes down to it, Jay said that he "could hardly raise that chair at the end of the night." He went on to reason that "if [I] couldn’t swing a chair anymore, what’s the use of going on with things?" For a man who has made a career out of shifty business and shear brutality, Jay Terror proves that his final chapter is nothing different. He will be remembered as an innovator, a champion, and a man who lived every second of his life for the sport. He’s a two time world champion, a television champion, and was one of the top choices for last year’s wrestler of the year. What he leaves behind is certainly a true mountain for other bad guys to climb and we’re sure that he will not be forgotten soon. It is unclear what his plans are next, but we at GCW wish him the best of luck with treatment and hope that someday he can rebuild his strength and return to the ring to tie the loose ends he’s leaving behind. For guys like Rich Rollins, Vivica J. Valentine, and Triumph Frost, I’m sure they agree as well.
