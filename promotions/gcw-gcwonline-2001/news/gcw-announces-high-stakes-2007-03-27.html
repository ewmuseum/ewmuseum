GCW Announces: High Stakes!

27 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, Ohio- Just before WorldWide this week, Global Championship Wrestling announced its next Supercard event; High Stakes. The casino-themed event is expected to be hosted in Las Vegas, and currently appears to be heading for annual status. High Stakes will occur next week on X:TV, and is expected to feature the highly anticipated showdown between GCW World Champion Lia Ambrosi and Bryan Mayhem. High Stakes is also rumored to be heading for the High Spirit Hotel and Casino in Las Vegas, which is owned by GCW superstar Shaman. This has caused quite a buzz backstage, as it seems the tandem of Shaman and Mayhem are growing in influence every week. President Caldera, however, assured the company today that the deal was done businessman to businessman between himself and Shaman, and though Shaman may own the building, Caldera will be the one overseeing the night's activities. "Shaman made a competitive offer and we accepted," Caldera said in his statement. "But when we roll into town, it'll be GCW's arena for the night. Shaman himself will, of course, be permitted to oversee things, but at High Stakes, it's my show." Still, there are some private whispers going around that this move by GCW might stack the odds even further against Lia Ambrosi. With the show being announced for Vegas, oddsmakers have already begun sizing up the card. Currently, Vegas has the World Title Match rated with a 4:1 advantage for the challenger Mayhem. Other rumored matches for the show include a big Vanguard Title Match, Jordan White's first defense of the United States Title, Twizzy's in-ring return, and much more. GCW is hoping to attract fans for an entire weekend of events. The roster will arrive in Vegas early next week, and will be making appearances at the High Spirit Casino in the days leading up to the event. The entire card is expected to be announced immediately following WorldWide this week. Check back for more details.
