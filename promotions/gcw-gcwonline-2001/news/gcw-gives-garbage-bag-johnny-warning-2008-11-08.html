GCW Gives Garbage Bag Johnny Warning

8 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW President Steven Caldera may not be touring with the company this month, but he's still got his eye on things. And today he reminded the company of that when he gave Garbage Bag Johnny a stern warning with regards to the United States Title belt. There's nothing Caldera can do about Johnny's reign, but it's the physical title belt he's concerned about. The current GCW United States Title is the fourth title belt used by the company, all because of employee negligence. The original US Title belt was burned and damaged by its original holder, Thirteen. Thirteen, angered at Caldera, destroyed the belt in protest. Thirteen was promptly fired. A new title belt was developed, but in October of 2001 then-champion Tristain Mayhem gave the belt away to a developmentally disabled child after an episode of Meltdown. The title belt has since been purchased back by the company at an auction, but the belt was out of GCW's possession for nearly four years. In the meantime, a third US title belt was constructed. When GCW closed in 2002, the final title holder, Toad, refused to return the belt. When GCW demanded he relinquish it, Toad responded by melting the belt down into a variety of gold jewelery for himself. That gold earring Toad wears on WorldWide? That used to be part of the GCW US Title belt. The fourth and current belt has managed to survive the last three years, but GCW is now apparently concerned that the old issue might arise again. Garbage Bag Johnny has been informed that if any misfortune should befall the US Title belt strict disciplinary action will follow. This notice follows a rumor that has been making its way through the company that, after winning the belt at Dangerous Games, Johnny tried to score some quick cash by pawning the belt in London. It seems the company is keeping a close eye on Garbage Bag Johnny's treatment of an important piece of GCW's history, and is prepared to act swiftly if he should mistreat it in any way.
