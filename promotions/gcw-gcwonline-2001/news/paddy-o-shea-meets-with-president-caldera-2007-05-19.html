Paddy O'Shea Meets With President Caldera

19 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, OH- Free Agent Paddy O'Shea met with President Steven Caldera at Caldera Enterprises Headquarters in Columbus today. O'Shea is one of the better known names on the market, and is a supremely talented wrestler. O'Shea has wrestled for some of the top promotions in the world, most recently PRIME and AWC. O'Shea recently resurfaced and expressed an interest in returning to full time in-ring action. As always, Caldera was on the ball, immediately arranging a meeting with O'Shea through his agent. O'Shea agreed to the meeting, and President Caldera spent the afternoon wooing him in his office today. While nothing has been announced formally, sources say the meeting went well, and Caldera and O'Shea are in the final stages of contract negotiation. GCW officials expect a deal to be signed by the end of the weekend. O'Shea is perhaps the biggest name to be connected with GCW during this massive influx of new talent, and would join a growing list of phenomenally talented new stars. GCW has been aggressive in adding to its already impressive roster, in an attempt to bolster its position in the wrestling world and produce a superior product. President Caldera has been opening the purse strings, ponying up the large dollar amounts necessary to bring in this caliber of talent. With O'Shea nearing a deal, and the promise of more big names to come, it should prove to be an interesting week as GCW heads into Warpath, where this impressive new roster should begin to take shape.
