Tyson talking Tag??

3 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No, not the body spray company, I'm talking about tag TEAM. Yes ladies and gentleman, the ever popular fan favorite in Tyson DeBough has decided to take his wile, charm, skill and charisma over to the tag team division. And what better way to start out that journey then with a match against the current tag team Champions, Oblivion. We caught up with Tyson briefly: "Well, I figure since Dangerous Games, and my charitable action towards Garbage Bag Johnny, that I would take my love and adoration of this sport to a different division, just to see what could happen. I'm sure I can bring a lot to the table, and show all of the teams how a tag team should function." When we asked about his "charity": "Um, did you not see me hand him the match? GBJ is a great guy, and he and I are pretty much like best buds. The whole thing was just for kicks. I told him before the match I was going to let him win the match before we even started up." He declined further comment. Will this be a permanent switch for everyone's lovable GCW personality? Or is this just a brief step sideways for the ever-aspiring DeBough? Only time will tell, but we expect great things from the prodigy.
