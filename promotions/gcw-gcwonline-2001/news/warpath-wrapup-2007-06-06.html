Warpath Wrapup

6 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Warpath 2007 is in the books, and what an incredible event it was! Global Championship Wrestling rocked Miami, which witnessed the amazing finale of the Warpath Tournament, and a gut-wrenching main event that saw a real changing of the guard. Lia Ambrosi is once again the Heavyweight Champion of the World, having found a way to defeat the monster Bryan Mayhem in the main event. The majority of GCW is elated to see Mayhem fall, but Oblivion was on the rampage after the show. Shaman, who defeated Jayce Bradley earlier in the night, said that he, Mayhem and Kyle James would be stepping up their efforts to dominate GCW and collect as much gold as possible. Speaking of Bradley, we've received word that he's been officially released from Global Championship Wrestling along with Class Act and Brock Shepherd. Katie O'Clare suffered a setback on a prior injury against Jay Terror, and has been taken off the active roster while she recovers. O'Clare remains under GCW contract and we wish her a speedy recovery. Terror defeated O'Clare soundly at Warpath, further solidifying himself as one of GCW's brightest prospects. The crowd is really getting behind this young man, even if President Caldera hasn't taken to his smoking habit. Still, Terror has strengthened the image of the Television Title to unheard of levels, and looks to continue his incredible run when he takes on his next challenger, Tyson DeBough. Back to the Tournament, the event was a huge success for GCW. GCW officials are thrilled with how well the tournament was received, and everyone involved fought very hard for a chance to emerge victorious. Vivica J. Valentine made a huge splash by eliminating LeStatt Knight, and the former two-time World Champion was furious after the show. Knight has been impressive since returning and was hellbent on chasing the World Title again. Still, he's been supportive of his Establishment teammate Jordan White, who defeated Twizzy and then pinned Valentine to become the new Number One Contender. With White now guaranteed a title shot at Lia Ambrosi, there has been some speculation that the United States Title will be vacated. While this is common practice, some believe that President Caldera is considering breaking the trend to allow White to attempt to become the first man in GCW history to simultaneously hold both the World and United States titles at the same time. In one of the more heartbreaking moments in GCW history, President Caldera robbed Twizzy of a spot in the Hall of Fame, passing it on instead to LeStatt Knight. We've confirmed that Twizzy did, in fact, win the fan voting, which ostensibly was to decide the new inductee. But Caldera changed the result personally, placing Knight in the Hall. Knight is unquestionably deserving of the honor, but Twizzy is said to be devastated. Still, Twizzy returned later in the night to get a measure of revenge against Caldera, taking him out of the equation in the Tournament finals. Though Twizzy got a few licks in, be assured that this intense rivalry between Twizzy and Caldera is far from over. GCW heads to Australia for the next month! We'll be touring there up through Shockwave, which should be an exciting show. This is GCW's first ever tour of Australia, and many of the superstars are excited to explore the new territory. Our tours abroad are tough on many stars who are forced to spend long stretches away from their families, but GCW does an outstanding job of providing creature comforts on the road to ease the trip, a testament to the financial success of the company. Don't miss WorldWide when it moves to Wednesdays next week! We'll be live from Melbourne for all the fallout from this fantastic Pay-Per-View. And don't forget, Dangerous Games is on the horizon!
