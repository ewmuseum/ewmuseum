Play By Play

Andy Murray, Vivica J. Valentine and the Commissioner's Chair With the news that Steven Caldera is apparently stepping away from GCW televised tapings for an indefinite amount of time, much debate has risen over whether Tim Shipley or Christian Zenith will be the face of authority through 2010. On WorldWide 126, Jay Terror interviewed both men, but glib words to each after the interviews confirmed that Terror, as usual, had something up his sleeve. While at the moment, we can only speculate Terror and Caldera's motives, I'd venture to guess that come NC-17, the power shift won't be as cut and dry as Caldera handing control over to either Zenith or Shipley. In one corner, Commissioner Zenith has a lot of things already playing in his favor. He's the incumbent, so he's resting on quite a bit of experience with this particular roster. In his time as commissioner, Zenith has stayed out of Caldera's way, which might pay off. If Caldera is looking for someone similar to himself, Zenith fits the bill better than Shipley. Zenith has the benefit of watching how Caldera operates first-hand, and being shrewd and ambitious like Caldera, has already shown he can shape himself into the mold Caldera has set. However, the fact remains that Caldera did bring in Tim Shipley on a wrestler's contract. Given the timing of this signing, marching steadfast towards NC-17, it's hard to believe that Caldera would introduce a name like Shipley into the equation without major plans for him. From a booking perspective, especially given that Shipley is without a match on the NC-17 card, his introduction would only be a distraction before GCW's biggest show of the year if he were just a wrestler. If Caldera were to hand the reins over to Shipley, the timing of his addition to the GCW roster would be no less than calculated. Surely, the entire GCW locker room is fully occupied by the pay per view, and if Caldera were to unleash something that would change the face of the company, now would be better than ever to sneak such a change through. Tim Shipley would be that change. With GCW's recent renegotiations with PTC, GCW stands to benefit from wider exposure to fans and potential new hires alike. While Caldera himself isn't shy about the distance he plans on keeping from PTC dealings, Tim Shipley would excel here. His independent promotion, Just Wrestling, has attracted names from several of the federations in the community, and surely, some of Shipley's contacts could be chomping at the bit for a run in a larger promotion like GCW. Noting that Jay Terror is the current Just Wrestling Champion probably doesn't hurt his case either. Jay Terror, of course, is the X-factor here. With a title shot lined up in GCW, Caldera has been very up front about the fact that he needs to test Terror. Caldera has been grooming Terror for years now, and no wrestler has been able to win Caldera's approval anywhere near the level Terror has. If Jay Terror defeats Jason O'Neil at NC-17, it will be a definite sign that Terror is just one test away from achieving whatever his mentor expects him to achieve. The next step? The GCW Championship. While Caldera feels it's necessary for Terror to complete this final test without him present, Caldera is undoubtedly going to equip his protege with all of the essential tools to help him secure the belt. One of these steps is a commissioner who won't challenge him, and that's where Andy Murray and Vivica J. Valentine come into play. The Scotsman has definite history with Tim Shipley, dating back to AWC and reemerging most significantly with Murray's time in Just Wrestling. As we saw on WorldWide 126, there seems to be a friction between the two, stemming, perhaps, from Murray's recent successes. In AWC, where the two were peers, Murray hadn't yet matured into the incredible force he is today. I sense some jealousy on Shipley's part, most recently with his experiences in HOW, that while he's been incredibly successful on the business side of things, Murray surpassed him quite handily in terms of in-ring notoriety. On the other side of the coin, there's never been any love lost between Vivica J. Valentine and Christian Zenith. Given the rocky history between the two, Zenith has the advantage of knowing how Valentine's mind works much better than Shipley could ever learn. If Vivica Valentine defeats Andy Murray to win the GCW Championship, Zenith would be the man most able to help get the title more quickly around Terror's waist. Watch for it at NC-17. Chances are, the commissioner won't be named until after this match is decided -- if it's going to be one of these two guys. If Caldera has already made a decision, which is likely given the magnitude of this choice, it may not be either of these men -- or it may be both. Splitting the power may be in Caldera's best interests as well. Neither Zenith nor Shipley have any incentive to keep the title on either Andy Murray or Vivica J. Valentine. If both Shipley and Zenith were given some measure of power, Terror would benefit from one man's history with whoever ends up holding the belt. Shipley and Zenith would keep each other in check, and if Caldera were to step back into the picture at some point in the future, Shipley and Zenith would ensure that neither commissioner would gain enough power in Caldera's absence to oppose his return with any serious threat. Then again, there could be other candidates who have yet to be seen. One of them could even be Steven Caldera, pulling a stunt solely to shake out anyone ambitious to ruin his protege's path back to GCW Championship Glory.
