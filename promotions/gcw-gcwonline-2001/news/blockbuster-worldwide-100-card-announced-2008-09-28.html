Blockbuster WorldWide 100 Card Announced

28 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling surprised the wrestling world tonight by releasing the WorldWide 100 lineup several days before schedule, and it's already got people buzzing. GCW and owner Steven Caldera promised a massive lineup to pay tribute to GCW's longest-tenured weekly broadcast, and the company didn't fail to deliver. While a number of heated rivalries will have to wait until Dangerous Games to reach their climax, WorldWide 100 is populated with huge matchups featuring every wrestler in the company... and one major name from the past. Headlining WorldWide 100, as expected, is a World Heavyweight Championship match featuring current champion and Establishment member Jay Terror. His opponent, however, is already causing quite a buzz within the company. GCW has announced that two-time former champion and Hall of Famer Jason Dread has agreed to a one-night contract to compete for the GCW Title. Dread, who last appeared in GCW nearly three years ago at NC-17 2005, has confirmed the agreement on his website and is reportedly on his way to Aberdeen. Whether he will appear on WorldWide this week to promote his World Title match remains to be seen, but Dread's return to the company has been locked down. Dread, once an Establishment member himself in 2002, has not seen eye to eye with Caldera since their falling out shortly before Warpath of that year. But Caldera seemed eager to invite Dread back to take on his current hand-picked champion. "This will give the world a chance to see Jay Terror defeat a true legend, a world-class talent," Caldera said during the release of the lineup. Dread was the first World Champion of GCW's second era, and appeared on the first episode of WorldWide. WorldWide 100 will also feature Hall of Famer LeStatt Knight taking on Andy Murray in singles action. Knight issued the challenge last week and it was accepted by Murray, who many consider to be the favorite to win the Dangerous Games match. Knight himself is also a heavy favorite, however, and the two will do battle in a massive showdown just one week before they both vie to become the number one contender to the GCW World Title. Certainly the winner of this match will have momentum for himself and will have dealt a crippling blow to his opponent. Another major storyline in GCW of late has been the amazing undefeated run of Garbage Bag Johnny. Johnny, who got his biggest win yet over Chris Bagwell this week, now faces his stiffest challenge yet. Garbage Bag Johnny will be in singles action against Jordan White in a true battle of titans! There is no doubt The Establishment has a role in this match; Tyson DeBough has been gunning for Johnny while Jay Terror has been trying to prevent White from obtaining a GCW Title shot. This match is win-win for The Establishment, but represents a colossal showdown between two of GCW's top names. It's the streak against White's World Title hopes, and something's got to give. In the other headlining match of the evening, Tessa Windsor will be forced to team up with Vivica J. Valentine to take on Clyde Fox and Chris Bagwell! The catch? The winning team will face off at Dangerous Games with a special stipulation attached: the winner of that match will enter the Dangerous Games battle royal at number thirty! One of these four stars will seize the opportunity to gain a massive advantage in the biggest match of the year, while settling some serious personal scores in the meantime.
