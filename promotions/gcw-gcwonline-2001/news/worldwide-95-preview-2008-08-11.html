WorldWide #95 Preview

11 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW continues its tour of Canada this week, as WorldWide comes to Montreal for its first broadcast since Warpath. Last week's amazing Pay-Per-View event saw two titles change hands and a wealth of tremendous matches. The battle will intensify now that every member of the roster is looking forward to the opportunity of a lifetime as GCW approaches Dangerous Games. This week GCW looks forward to the most anticipated event of the year, as each wrestler will attempt to separate themselves from the pack. What to Watch For -Vivica J. Valentine shocked her doubters by retaining the GCW World Title at Warpath. Jordan White was badly wounded and Jay Terror has been a mess since then. With reports now coming that Terror was arrested in Philadelphia this week, it seems that Valentine's victory has sent The Establishment into shambles. President Caldera will be back on television, however, and will no doubt have something to say about Valentine's victory. -Chris Storm's status is uncertain after his devastating loss to LeStatt Knight. Knight, on the other hand, has been vocal in declaring himself this year's Dangerous Games favorite. Knight has never won the big event, but after his tremendous victory at Warpath, he might be on track to capture his first Dangerous Games win. Storm is expected to be at WorldWide, but has been uncharacteristically quiet since the Pay-Per-View. -Andy Murray continues his amazing run in GCW, once again at the expense of Chris Bagwell. The two engaged in a hellish match at Warpath that is already generating Match of the Year buzz. Despite his loss, Bagwell also seems to be destined for greatness. With the two rising on parallel paths, it seems inevitable that they will remain in conflict for some time to come. Will their war continue with Dangerous Games on the horizon? -Seymour Almasy is said to be distraught after losing once again to Lia Ambrosi. What is the Final Fantasy's status? -Digital Mortality won a huge match, capturing the United States Title from Tessa Windsor. But DigiMon's title reign already stands to be an adventerous one, as the first ever American Dream match is taking place on WorldWide. Garbage Bag Johnny, Zachariah Taylor, Jacob McKail and Tyson DeBough will compete for the right to challenge the United States Champion any time, any place. Who will win the American Dream, and will Digital Mortality's title reign last long? -And Garbage Bag Johnny is still undefeated in GCW. Can he keep the streak alive? -Daria Ngozi ended the incredible title reign of Zachariah Taylor at Warpath. The new TV Champion will be in action against Keegan O'Conner--but rumor has it Dan Black isn't ready to back off. -Bryan Mayhem and Shaman nearly destroyed one another at Warpath, with Shaman coming out on top. What is next for the two monsters of GCW? Injury Report -Jordan White was hurt at Warpath. While he has not been barred from travel, reports suggest that he won't report to Montreal this week. -Shaman and Bryan Mayhem are both hurt, but it seems unlikely that either will let the pain stop them from showing up. -Vivica J. Valentine reported continued pain in her injured ribs, but won't miss any time. -Chris Bagwell and Andy Murray also suffered damage in their closed cage match at Warpath. Bagwell will be in singles action this week, and Murray has already competed in an Elite Title match at PRIME this week. -As stated, President Caldera has recovered from his surgery and is expected to be back on television this week. His knee is reportedly still in pain, but he won't miss any more time. -Chris Storm may have lost, but LeStatt Knight took the brunt of the punishment in their match at Warpath. Knight's ankle was damaged in the victory, but it won't require surgery. Knight won't wrestle this week. On The Line -The Tag Team Champions, Samson & Delylah, who successfully defended their titles at Warpath, will be in action, defending their belts against the returning SoCal Stretching Crew. -The Television Title will be up for grabs as new champion Daria Ngozi defends against Keegan O'Conner, who defeated Markus Stone at Warpath. Next Week Next week, GCW heads to Winnipeg for WorldWide #96, as the company creeps closer to the WorldWide 100 spectacular in Columbus, Ohio.
