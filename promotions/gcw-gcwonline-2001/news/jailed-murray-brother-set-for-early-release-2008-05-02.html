Jailed Murray Brother set for Early Release?

2 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Several popular professional wrestling websites have today been flooded with rumours concerning Cayle Murray, the incarcerated brother of GCW superstar Andy Murray. It has been speculated that Cayle, who turned 26 last week, could be set for an early release from his prison sentence on grounds of good behaviour. The youngest Murray sibling was jailed in June 2007 for assault and battery, and was not due for release until December of this year. However, it has been said that prison staff have been very impressed with the Cayle’s behaviour, and he could be free as soon as the end of May. Cayle Murray is perhaps best known for his brief stint as part of “The Amazing Technicolor Dream Team” with his brother in FUSE. In a spell lasting no more than two months, the Murray brothers managed to capture the federation’s “Fatal Faction” tag team championships. It is understood, however, that the chances of Cayle making a return to professional wrestling are slim-to-none. The news will surely be of great relief to Andy, who has gone on record to call 2007 “one of the worst years of my life”. As well as the imprisonment of his brother, 2007 saw The Scottish King of Cool divorce his wife and sustain a potentially career-threatening back injury.
