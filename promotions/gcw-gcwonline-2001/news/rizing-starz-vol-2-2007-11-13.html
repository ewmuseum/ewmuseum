Rizing Starz (Vol. 2)

13 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Konichiwa again everybody! Ayake Sonoda here again with the second installment of Rizing Starz! The only column in GCW that gets to know the people behind the athletes you see in the ring. Today's story of course will be no different as we all get reacquainted with a familiar figure from GCW's past. No I'm not talking about LeStatt Knight, Octavian, or Bryan Mayhem. I'm talking about Dorian Ryan. Many of the newer fans out there may think Dorian Ryan is newer member of the GCW roster, but he's been around this federation longer than a lot of your GCW veterans. After a very successful career in England and Japan, Dorian headed to the United States where he became a NWCP Golden Ticket Winner and a finalist in the NWC Hurricane Cup. While in NWC he, although he did find some success, he was cast into the shadow of NWC and future FUSE star Jason "Jay Draven" Ryan. Seeking a spark in his career, he made his way to GCW after an invitation from Steven Caldera to wrestles as a masked wrestler simply known as X. While X started out strong, the character struggled in the later parts of its tenure in GCW. Other wrestlers took their shot from under the mask but never quite captured the same feeling as when it was portayed by Dorian Ryan. Shedding the mask, Dorian Ryan became known as Sam Wolack where he had a very memorable blood feud with Coyle O'Connor. Later he captured the GCW United States Championship from Eva VanLorne, and held it until the invasion of Dies Irae where he lost it to current #1 Contender Vivica J. Valentine. Sam Wolack then changed his name to Dorian Ryan as we know him today where he fought along side LeStatt Knight, Tessa Windsor, and Eva VanLorne in The Ascension. The Ascension stood up for GCW in a fight against the newcomers from Dies Irae, but Dorian Ryan unfortunately had to leave the struggle to take care of his wife who had a nervous breakdown due to a fire that destroyed their home. Their house had many memories and it took a long time to cope but now Dorian Ryan has made his way back to Global Championship Wrestling, where at the next episode of WorldWide he has the opportunity to collect his second GCW Championship in a Television Title match against Tyson DeBough. ---- Name: Dorian Seth Ryan Birthday: May 16, 1982 Birthplace: Detroit, Michigan Current Residence: Minneapolis, Minnesota Height: 6' 1" Weight: 220 lbs. Eyes: Green Hair: Blonde but dyed Brown Blood Type: AB+ ---- Ayake Sonoda: Welcome to Rizing Starz Dorian. It's a pleasure to meet you! I saw you a couple of times when you first started in the business. You were on a couple cards my father headlined in Japan as Prime, I don't think we've ever spoken before though. Dorian Ryan: Actually, I remember your father. He's the person that introduced me to my first wife. AS: (laughs) He was a people person, always looking out for people and trying to play match maker. I can't tell you how many wrestlers he tried to court me to. So how is everything at home since the fire? DR: Getting better, I think. Hopefully, Andrea will come home soon. It will help for her so see that the world didn't end with that fire. AS: Something like that is tough to get over, tell her that your entire family is in my prayers. Onto business, so you've had a long career in GCW but many of our newer fans may not know you. You've gone through a lot of changes in your career, any reason for that? DR: Evolution. When I first signed to GCW, I was still recovering from some injuries, so to get me on TV and exposed, we created this character named "X". Originally, I was going to come back as my original character, "Prime", but obviously that wasn't going to happen because of the comparisons to another wrestling company. AS: Yeah... we've been having problems with that certain company here lately. You did eventually shed the mask to go under the name Sam Wolack. Why Sam Wolack? What was he all about? DR: Originally when I joined Minnesota State Wrestling, Jason and I didn't want everyone to know that I was his brother. So we concocted the name Samuel Francis Wolack based on my sister, Samantha, and her husband, Francis Wolack. AS: An ode to your sister, that's sweet. So you're really into sport bikes right? We were talking about it earlier and you said that's what you were doing in your time away from GCW was building them. DR: Yeah, during my first run in Japan, I got turned onto the custom bike scene. My brother was always into Harleys, so that helped to rub off on me also. But, to clear up one rumor that's gone on throughout my career, my initial knee and elbow injuries were not caused by a motorcycle crash. AS: With all the motorcycle injuries in sports here recently I'm sure that comes up a lot. I've been around the wrestling scene since I was a little girl, and almost everywhere I go I'm surprised by how much an entrance theme means to a wrestler as far as feeling and personality. Vivica touched on that a bit last column, is your theme "Pain Redefined" by Disturbed important to you? Is there a story behind why you come out to that song? DR: Well, the use of "Pain Redefined" came about after some negotiations with a couple of record companies. Initially, I was going to use Metallica's "Don't Tread On Me" for my entrance when I returned, when I used the Gadsden Flag as a trademark. But when that fell through, I decided on Pain Redefined because that's what I wanted to bring to people like Vivica, who had put me on the shelf for six months, as well as Virgil and Lia Ambrosi and Brandon Youngblood. AS: It definitely sets a tone, I think it fits you very well. Speaking of some of the other wrestlers did you have any idols growing up? Anyone who inspired you to join the business? Someone who you wish to be like in the ring? DR: Obviously I idolized my older brother. Former NWC Hall of Famer Tony Pride was another guy that I watched. James Callion Sr, who ended up training me for the ring was another inspiration. One of my moves is named after another one of my idols, and a guy I got to meet in Japan in Liger. But amazingly, my style came about because my sister forced me to watch Olympic Gymnastics. AS: (laughs) Wow I would've never guessed that. So do you think there is any relationship between gymnastics and professional wrestling? DR: If there isn't one already, maybe after this interview there will be one. AS: Dorian Ryan, paving the way for gymnists everywhere to start their professional wrestling careers. So I have a couple quick-hitting questions for you before we wrap this up, you think you can keep up? DR: Careful, I'm not as fast as I used to be. AS: (smiles) I'll try to keep a nice pace for you. Favorite stock sport bike? DR: Stock.....wow, probably Kawasaki '83 750 Turbo. It's the first bike I had in Japan. AS: Favorite restaurant? DR: There was this great steak house in Okinawa that I used to love eating at. The name translated to "The Golden Lotus". AS: Ha! I've been there before! Favorite place to visit? DR: The Kawahara estate just outside of Tokyo. London, England. Thailand. AS: Favorite GCW star besides yourself? DR: Wow. That's a tough one. Even though we've not really talked in the last year, Tessa Windsor. AS: Celebrity crush? DR: For a long time, it was Winona Ryder. AS: See that wasn't so bad was it? DR: Not really. But, if you do this with Lia, tell her that even though we have the same alignment, she's still got a target around her waist. AS: I'm sure she's ready for you. Good luck on WorldWide against Tyson DeBough for the Television Championship! Maybe next time you can show it off to me if you win? I've never actually got to hold any of the belts yet. DR: Thanks for bringing me on here, and tell your father that I said hi, and I'm still waiting for our chess rematch. AS: Will do! ---- That's all we have for this installment of Rizing Starz! Stay tuned for our next installment coming soon... but with who? Who knows! Just know that Rizing Starz (Vol. 3) is on the way. Until next time... Sayonara! - "Aki-San" Ayake Sonoda
