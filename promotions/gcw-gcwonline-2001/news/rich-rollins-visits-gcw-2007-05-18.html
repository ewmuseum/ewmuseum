Rich Rollins Visits GCW

18 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW Legend Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins was spotted at WorldWide this week, leading to the usual speculation that the future Hall of Famer might be returning to the company that made him famous. Rollins, who has been involved with GCW since its start in 2001, has been out of the business for several months now. Rollins is thought to be retired and enjoying his time off with his family. Still, when a wrestler of his stature surfaces, it leads to a great deal of speculation. But those who saw Rollins insist he was just there visiting friends. The former two-time World Heavyweight Champion is one of the most notorious wrestlers in GCW history, and was last seen wrestling at NC-17 last year as a member of rival promotion PRIME. President Steven Caldera took The Renegade's defection very personally, and together with Seymour Almasy fought Rollins and Angelo Deville in an ACWF Match. Caldera defeated Rollins, dropping The Renegade's ACWF career record to 0-2. Sources backstage say that Caldera and Rollins did meet behind closed doors, but there is no word regarding what was discussed. Though their recent history is extremely checkered, Rollins and Caldera have history dating back to the Cyber Wrestling Union, which Caldera ran in 2000. Still, there are no indications that Rollins intends to return to any wrestling ring, much less in GCW. We'll keep an eye on the situation, but for now, don't look for The Renegade in GCW any time soon.
