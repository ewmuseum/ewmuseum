Chris Storm jumping ship?

24 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why is one of GCW's top stars traveling to Russia? According to multiple sources, Chris Storm never checked in with GCW the day after Rampage, causing speculation that he was off on a bender with Markus Stone after losing the Golden Ticket Double Jeopardy Cage Match. But your pal Jimmy Brazzo dug deeper and found that Chris Storm was actually in Moscow the very next day. What's in Moscow? Either he really needed vodka or there is something else is going on. (And believe me, my bar tab has never been bigger trying to find out. Damn, those Russians can pound it down!) But who, at the same time, was in Moscow? None other than rival promotion All-Star Championship Wrestling. Is Chris Storm, who has been very vocal about his desire to leave GCW, about to jump ship? Can the former B&E~! star simply not handle having to give up the spotlight to his wife? Is there trouble in paradise for GCW’s most solid couple? Word from my various inebriated sources inside Russia have claimed that Chris Storm may be getting courted by different members of the ACW roster, though no obvious ties seem to be currently working in GCW. Could GCW really be losing someone who just shortly before was competing for the World title to a rival promotion? And does this have anything to do with the Commissioner on vacation for the week? Where is ole Garbage Bag anyway? Not in his usual hookah smoke filled hangouts; I checked. But you can bet Jimmy will stay on top of this one. Like a fly on… well, you know. Jimmy Brazzo, Editor in Chief, Between the Dirtsheets
