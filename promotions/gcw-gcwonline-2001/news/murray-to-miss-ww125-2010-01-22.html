Murray to miss WW125?

22 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The World Champion could be set to miss his first show in 2 years. Find out more inside. The internet wrestling community is this afternoon rife with speculation that GCW World Heavyweight Champion Andy Murray could be set to miss his first ever GCW broadcast since joining the company in December 2007. Murray, is said to be despondent following the full collapse of his relationship with Vivica J. Valentine at WorldWide 124. Many assumed that the bond formed at Dangerous Games had already imploded when Valentine’s frenzied Repentance step-up enziguri cost Murray the Christmas Chaos main event, but Murray walked into 124 with his fingers crossed, launching one final heartfelt plea to his estranged partner. He didn’t get the result he was looking for. After a night of contemplation and soul-searching, Valentine (who had earlier berated long-time friend Tessa Windsor for showing concern) turned-up after Murray’s successful title defence against Dan Black and AXEL Action. One kick to the head and four chairshots later, the Scottish King of Cool was lying in a puddle of his own blood in the middle of the ring. Cynics may claim that the demise was a long time coming, but nobody could have predicted such an immense shift in Valentine’s personality. Usurped as the company’s most popular worker by her own boyfriend (and possibly Clyde Fox, too), Valentine was already in an insecure position by the time Dangerous Games came around, and losing out to Jay Terror only compounded her misery. Coupled with her growing dissatisfaction at Murray’s over-protectiveness (resulting in a forfeit loss to bitter rival LeStatt Knight three weeks ago), the strain has apparently become too much for Valentine to take. Unable to accept her dwindling spotlight and driven by the one thing that truly motivates her (the GCW World Championship), Vivica J. Valentine has snapped. Murray, who is thought to have suffered a broken nose as a result of 124’s attack, is said to be “devastated” after entering 124 still clutching a glimmer of hope and leaving it in the back of an ambulance by his partner’s hand. The GCW Champion has built his reputation on his impeccable sportsmanship and professionalism, but the chances of him attending 125 are “slim to none,” according to sources close to the Scotsman. Neither Steven Caldera or Christian Zenith were available for comment at the time of writing, but it is understood that a no-show by the company’s highest-grossing asset will not be looked upon too favourably by GCW staff and officials.
