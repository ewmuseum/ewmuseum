Match Change for WorldWide

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a last minute shocker, GCW Owner Steven Caldera has decided to invoke his ability to make changes to the card for the seventy-fifth edition of World Wide. The Tag Team Champions, Banned & Exiled~! are listed to take on rivals, the Blue Collar Boys, will now be a Handcuff match. But not any normal Handcuff match. Rather than the opponents being handcuffed to each other, Chris and Markus will be handcuffed to each other. Their opponents, Jack and Tommy O?Toole, are not required to be handcuffed together. The change in the match appears to actually be the fault of a recent column posted on the GCW website where Banned & Exiled~! were questioned as possibly the best tag team ever in GCW. When asked about the change in match, Mr. Caldera simply issued a statement saying; “if those two want to prove they truly are the best ever, than this match should be no problem for them. I’ve seen a great number of amazing tag teams, each able to possibly came to be the best ever. This is simply a test to see if Banned & Exiled~! do indeed rank up there with them.” With the Tag Team titles still on the line, and the champions still aching from last week’s attack against newly acquired tag team Saints of Perdition; it will be a wonder how competitive the champions will be this week. Banned & Exiled~! could not be reached for comment at the time of writing. Further comment from them is expected.
