GCW stars taste GTT7 success

27 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling may have spent the last few weeks submerged in a Dangerous Games-induced state of hysteria, but elsewhere several of the company's biggest names have been blazing a hot streak right through Primetime Central's returning GTT event. GCW United States champion elect AXEL Action may have had to pull out of his first round clash due to illness, but his absence proved fruitful for newcomer Aaron Nothings as their three-way with Joshua Kosidlo turned into a one-on-one contest, and Just Wrestling mainstay Nothings barely broke a sweat to overcome the former Global competitor. Elsewhere in the Shakespeare bracket, GCW's returning Puppetmaster, James von Doom, literally destroyed Just Wrestling “talent” Skylar Montgomery in a relatively one-sided first round clash. Two of GCW's heaviest hitters in Vivica J. Valentine and 12th seed Andy Murray also tasted success. Valentine overcome the returning “Exceptional” Dave Hurst in the Pynchon bracket, wall PRIME hall-of-famer Tchu fell by the hand of the reigning Global Championship Wrestling in a hotly-contested affair. Murray's victory over Tchu is the latest in a line of wins over PRIME alumnus; he defeated Wade Elliott and Devin Shakur in last year's Elite Title Inaugural Tournament. So successful were GCW wrestlers in GTT7's first round that Lori Equinox was the only active member of the roster not to pick up a victory, as he lost his three-way dance with Nova and Bryan Dawkins after being pinned by NFW's Risen Star. Without including AXEL Action's dropout, GCW's first round GTT7 record stands at an impressive 4-1. With the rest of PTC may spend their time talking about SCCW's dominance on the interfed scene, GCW's quiet impact has gone almost completely unnoticed, and that could definitely work in the favour of Valentine, Murray, von Doom, and Nothings. In the second round, 15th seed von Doom will face Just Wrestling alumni Buck Fledger, while Nothings will tackle 7th seed Violence Jack. Murray and Valentine's matches are still be to drawn.
