GCW Adds <del>Five</del> Six

15 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Columbus, OH-As previously reported, Global Championship Wrestling has added five new wrestlers to its active roster. After an extended lull in hiring, GCW sent out its scouts to scour the nation for new talent to bolster its roster heading into 2008. The resulting talent search led to five new hires, all of whom were at Caldera Enterprises headquarters in Columbus this week to finalize their deals. "Detective" Kraig Carter was the first to finalize a contract and debuted with the company this week on WorldWide. Carter, a former GCW employee, reportedly renewed the previously standing contract he'd had with the company. The other four, Kiljaden Kincaid, Chris Bagwell, Andy Murray and Thom LeStrange represent a diverse range of talent. GCW scouts are reportedly ecstatic about their arrival, and all are expected to become major parts of the company after GCW returns in the new year. This sudden and unexpected hiring spree represents a bold new doctrine for GCW heading in to 2008. President Steven Caldera had been relatively quiet behind the scenes during the final quarter of 2007, which most attribute to his hard work to promote NC-17. Now that NC-17 preparations are nearly over and the show is a little over a week away, Caldera seems ready to begin glancing towards the new year. 2006 represented a significant regression of business for GCW, and Caldera personally suffered heavy losses. Caldera was said to be very distraught over the poor performance of the company and made many drastic changes at the beginning of the year. The first quarter of 2007 started slowly but saw financial improvement, and the company made major strides during the rest of the year, culminating in what is expected to be a record gate for NC-17 to close the year. The Pay-Per-View extravaganza at Reliant Stadium in Houston sold out in less than twenty-four hours. As a result, GCW is expected to continue to be highly aggressive in searching for new talent. In addition to the superb additions just made, speculation is rising again that the company will be looking to add some marquee names to the roster at the start of the new year. While the company has been extremely tight-lipped about the business plan for the new year, there is some speculation that the company could be keeping a close eye on the soon-to-close promotion XUW as a potential talent pool from which to recruit. Caldera was even speculated to have been in talks to purchase FUSE again, but that was never confirmed and if true, talks never got far. However, these new hires are representative of the tremendous success GCW has experienced in 2007, and hopes within the company are that 2008 will be the company's best ever. NOTE: GCW has also reportedly come to terms with "Marvelous" Mark Matthews.
