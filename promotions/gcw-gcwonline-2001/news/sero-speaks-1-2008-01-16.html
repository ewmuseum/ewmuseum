Sero Speaks: #1?

16 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and Salutations my friends! It’s your pal Sero back for another exciting edition of “Sero Speaks”. It’s been some time since I’ve graced you with my presence and I apologize for that BUT with the holidays, NC-17 and other obligations I was forced to take a bit of time off. Yeah I have other obligations too, you think Caldera pays me the big bucks just to write some stuff for your enjoyment? Peanuts my friends, a brotha needs another job. I am back though and will jump back into making this a more consistent deal for 08. So what’s today’s topic? Will I give an NC-17 review? Maybe a recap of GCW in 07? How about an in-depth look at one of GCW’s superstars? Nah, not really! Let me start by taking you back to WorldWide 81 this last week. Vivica J. Valentine, GCW’s brand new World Champ comes out to address her adoring fans. She gives herself a short speech until she is interrupted by none other than LeStatt Knight. Then Jay Terror Then Rich Rollins Then Jordan White Then Lia Ambrosi Then the popcorn vendor in the 5th row Then ? Well you get the idea. Now, all of these superstars feel (besides the popcorn vendor, although I don’t have confirmation on this) that they are the deserving ones to be named the number one contender to the World Title. This of course led to a huge pissing match and subsequent cluster f*!& O? Doom match at the end of the night. Entertaining match I must say, really helps to establish what GCW’s Main Event scene is going to be like for most of the year. So we have LeStatt Knight, Rich Rollins, Lia Ambrosi, Jay Terror and Jordan White all clamoring for a shot at the gold that Vivica owns at this point right? Well is that enough? Of course not! After this 10-man (and woman) tag match is finished yet another person tosses their name into the hat and its one big ass name. GCW United States Champion, Shaman. Now I was quite surprised at this personally. Shaman has been fine in the ring and has been carrying a very weak US Title scene for awhile but is he really World Champion material? Does he deserve a shot? If not him then who? Well rest assured fans because Sero Blake is here to break this down for you all. I am going to rank all of the people involved in GCW’s main event scene from 6 on down to 1 ? we’ll settle who exactly the REAL number one contender is. Let’s get started! 6. JAY TERROR This pick will probably surprise a lot of people because the world is in love with Jay Terror at the moment. I agree there is plenty to like about him but I also am forced to wonder if he is really ready for this kind of spotlight. Look I’m not questioning the guy’s toughness or skill inside of a wrestling ring. At NC-17 this man gave GCW’s greatest World Champion a hell of a run and lost only because he just couldn’t continue anymore. Problem is he still got himself in that position. Jay may not have tapped or said the words “I quit” but what else was there for him to do? He was locked up tight, his ass wasn’t moving from that spot. Facts are facts right? Well Jay Terror went from battling Tyson DeBough for the TV title to Main Eventing NC-17 with LeStatt Knight. Throughout the entire lead up to that match Jay was one upped by the smarter, stronger and more experienced person. I just think he has more to learn before being thrust into the big time ? maybe he’ll prove me wrong. 5. LIA AMBROSI This is not a knock on Lia more than me just being tired of seeing her in the Main Event. Ambrosi had quite a run with the title in 07. I don’t buy that it’s the best in GCW’s history because to me length means nothing; it’s about the quality of your run. Ambrosi’s greatest feat? Beating Bryan Mayhem. Not so great if you ask me. If there was the kind of competition in 07 as there is today? Lia would have held the title for a few months and been overtaken by someone else. The girl is talented though and I will recognize her as worthy of getting an opportunity again down the road. For now though I want to see something fresh. 4. SHAMAN I’ll be perfectly honest; I’m not overly impressed with Shaman. He’s big, he’s slow and he enjoys hanging around Bryan Mayhem. Obviously something is seriously wrong with him. Given all of that though he is incredibly powerful and he is a crafty veteran of the game. Shaman has been around this industry since I was a tiny child and that is something that can always make a man dangerous. He forcefully threw his name into the mix and I have a strong feeling that Vivica will grant him a match because she’s so desperate to prove that she is worthy. In sports there is a thing called a “trap game”, a game that should be a cakewalk but for whatever reasons the inferior opponent comes out firing on all cylinders and catches the “big dog” napping. Vivica, if you’re listening ? beware of the trap game. 3. RICH “THE RENEGADE” ROLLINS I actually want to put the great Renegade lower on this list BUT the fact that he was victorious at NC-17 makes me feel obligated to put him in the top three. Rollins is one of, if not THE most recognized and decorated of GCW’s superstars, past or present. The things I’ve seen this man do inside of a wrestling ring are breath taking. My issue with him? Besides ACWF where has all of that been? Most of the things you hear about from Rollins are past tense, what will the future hold for this Hall of Famer? He has more to prove before being the number one contender in my book. 2. LESTATT KNIGHT Thought he was going to be number one didn’t you? It was a real struggle deciding between Knight and Jordan White for number one here, it really was. I see LeStatt as the unofficial LEADER of The Establishment. Sure Rollins is around and Octavian was great and Branson King is an Icon but none of them have the kind of impact weekly that Knight does. I’ve gone and studied all the versions of Caldera’s Establishment and in most cases the leader was very apparent ? mostly because the said leader AND Mr. Caldera would ALWAYS let you know who it was. This time is very different. Knight has been very low key in his ways but it’s been effective. I believe (save Valentine’s) Knight’s victory at NC-17 was the most impressive considering how he won and in what type of match and even taking it back a bit further ? he destroyed Twizzy twice, was in the final two at Dangerous Games, embarrassed Jay Terror on a weekly basis and then defeated him at the grandest show of the year. What kept him from being number one? His loss to the guy I put at number one on WorldWide a few weeks ago? 1. JORDAN WHITE This may be my favorite GCW star right now. Jordan stood up to Caldera, has beaten Rich Rollins, beaten LeStatt Knight, is wildly entertaining both inside the ring and out AND seems to be only getting better. Also, unlike Terror, he has been battle tested. White was the United States Champion, Won the Warpath tournament (beating the current World Champion in the process) and SHOULD have beaten Lia Ambrosi at Dangerous Games. There’s nothing to dislike about the guy besides his loss at NC-17 to Rich Rollins but I still grade him higher than Rollins because of the situation in which Rollins got his victory. ACWF? Give me a break. That match is made for hardcore fanatics, the World Title is almost always decided where it counts ? inside the ring. White and Rollins are 1-1 but White took Rollins out in an Iron Man Match, much more impressive to me. So there you have it, Sero’s “NUMBER ONE CONTENDER RANKINGS!” I believe you’ll see Steven Caldera’s incredible Round Robin take GCW to new heights. I also believe you’ll see the results come out similar to what my rankings show. Could there be surprises? Of course, Jay Terror could come out firing and win the whole damn thing. Stranger things have happened BUT I wouldn’t count on it yet. I do think this Round Robin will benefit a guy like Terror more than the rest of the field because he IS the only one who hasn’t quite been fully battle tested yet. I cannot wait to see how it all shakes out. Before I go though I want to say a little something to the New Champion, Vivica Valentine. Watch your ass, because you have the greatest talent in GCW’s history coming to kick it. That is all for this week everyone! Be sure to give me feedback, questions, comments or whatever your heart desires.
