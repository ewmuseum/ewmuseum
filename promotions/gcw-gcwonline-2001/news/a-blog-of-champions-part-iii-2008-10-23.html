A Blog of Champions, Part III

23 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The really huge one is upon us. Dangerous Games will be a momentous event for GCW that will change the way the next few months will play out. Nothing like a little competition to get everyone on their game. As you all might know, every title will be on the line plus some grudge matches we’ve all been looking forward to and of course, the Dangerous Games match itself. So, I guess its time to give my predictions for this event of epic proportions. ACWF Match - GCW Title Jay Terror vs. Jordan White This is probably one of the most interesting match ups I have seen in a while. Jay Terror has proven that he can win the coveted World title, but at Dangerous Games, he has to prove that he can defend that title belt. On his way is one of the most impressive guys I have seen in a while. I think everyone knows Jordan White has what it takes to be the man in GCW, but will he be able to not only defeat Terror but The Establishment and Steven Caldera as well. Not only do the contestants make this match exciting, but who has the advantage in the dreaded Asphalt, Chains, Wrenches and Forklifts match. This will be one brutal encounter. In the end, I think Jay Terror will define his title reign with this victory. Vivica J. Valentine vs. Tessa Windsor Ever since she aligned with LeStatt Knight, everyone has seen Tessa Windsor skyrocket up the ranks to the very top. Now she has to face one of the biggest challenges of her long career. She faces not only the former GCW World Champion, the 2007 winner of the Dangeroud Games match but at one time her best friend, Vivica J. Valentine. While I understand that Vivica has an advantage, having fought and won before on this kind of big time matches, I think Tessa has finally found herself and understands that the top is there for her to reach. I don’t think there is anything to stop her from getting to it very soon. Chris Bagwell vs. Clyde Fox As much as everyone dislikes the man, no one can deny the talent that Chris Bagwell has and his knack for the business. While his style of working the business is not my own, I understand that you have to do what you have to do in order to get noticed in this oversaturated game. Clyde Fox is? um? unique. He might be young and na’ve, but behind that exterior there lies a wrestling machine. He can go. I know. I have been in the same ring with him. I think Fox will get the huge upset here. US Title Match Digital Mortality vs. Zachariah Taylor vs. Jacob McKail I have been training to the best of my ability and when I get in that ring, I can guarantee one thing. The fans will come out the winners. While I have never faced Jacob McKail, I know of his accomplishments and I have seen him in the ring since he got in GCW and he is definitely a threat, but I have confidence that if I am on my game, I will be able to overcome him. Zacharia Taylor is one guy that I believe will eventually carry the US Title division to new grounds, but not yet. I still have stories to tell in the squared circle, and until I do, I don’t expect to lose. Good luck to both of you, it will be one hell of a fight. Tyson DeBough vs. Garbage Bag Johnny While Tyson DeBough has the weight of The Establishment behind him, Garbage Bag Johnny has shown that he has what it takes to get to the next level. I think he might be gunning for the US title this night as well, but I can’t really tell. I can’t be thinking that this might be the night GBJ cashes his American Dream in. This will be a tough battle for GBJ, but I see him pulling through. Samson & Delylah vs. Pierre & Jackson Samson and Delylah are not pushovers but Pierre and Jackson aren’t either. This will be one tough match and I expect some real sparks to fly as these two teams clash. I think the experience as a team for Samson and Delylah pull the through this very tough match-up and into title contention once again. TV Title Match Dan Black vs. Phil Atken It will be interesting to see if Atken can actually take down Black who has been doing a great job as TV champ. In the end though, I think Black wants to prove that he belongs and if it takes holding on to that title for a few more weeks, then I see him doing it without much of a problem. AXEL Action vs. Kenny Mercury Is this the UWF all over again? Both of these characters have one thing in common (with me as well), we all at one point or another were part of the UWF. I have been in the ring with Mercury and know what he can do, but he is too much of a mess yet to be able to take down the resurgent Action. Action has looked really good for the last few months and if he continues with this streak, we might see him knocking at my door if I can defend my title. Action will take out Mercury in this one. Mr. Danger vs. Face Mr. Danger is trying to get back in the swing of things and this GCW veteran will probably pull no punches when facing? well, Face. It will be interesting what Face can do against a veteran like Danger. I think youth will prevail in this one and Face takes the win. Dangerous Games Match Predictions: -Final Four: I have the “Scottish King of Cool” Andy Murray, LeStatt Knight, Tessa Windsor and Chris Bagwell as the final four without counting myself, of course. -Most Eliminations: (Predict Who and How Many) I think the raw power of Oblivion combined will make this hell for anyone else. I think they eliminate half the field by themselves. They don’t have another match, so they can concentrate on just eliminating people and winning the thing. -Longest Endurance: (Predict Who and How Long) Tessa Windsor. I think she is small enough and agile enough to remain unnoticed by most of the bigger guys and she will be able to survive for a long time. -Winner: I think Andy Murray has made such an impression on fans and backstage that he will feed off of it and come out of this thing with the victory. Well, those are my predictions for Dangerous Games. I hope you all tune in and enjoy a night of violence and surprises that GCW will bring you. Since this might be my last Blog of Champions, thanks you all for reading? Take care readers and keep watching.
