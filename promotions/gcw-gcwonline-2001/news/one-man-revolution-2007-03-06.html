One Man Revolution?

6 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The Deathscythe Outlaw" Kyo Kushinaga has been announcing Grave Revolution’s awaited return to the wrestling world for months now. However, it appears no one on the GCW roster has step up to join him in his revolution. We managed to get Kushinaga’s comments about this. "I’m not surprise nor am I discouraged about this turn of events. I’m more appalled at the fact that these fighters don’t want to do nothing to revolutionize this place. You all are satisfied on how things are being run here. You all are satisfied with the champions here. You all believe GCW is perfect as it is. I believe it is time to wake up and smell the black roses. This place is long overdue for a change." One has to wonder what kind of a change he has in store for GCW. It’s no secret Grave Revolution’s motive was to annihilate the KnightMare Brigade once and for all. It has become clear the change doesn’t involve this group anymore since the group has become somewhat inactive in the wrestling world. Kushinaga was able to clarify us about this. "I haven’t been here long enough, but, I can see this place is all about what your name is and the connections you have. If you look at all the champions here, most of them either have a big name or they’re in an alliance with some group that’s giving them that easy push to the top. It makes me sick. It’s about time to cure the sickness here. It’s time for it to be learned that it takes more than a name and connections to become the best here. It takes hard work and an unbeatable fighting spirit to rise to the top." "The Deathscythe Outlaw" sounded serious about this. Still, one has to wonder what his first step going to be to start this revolution. On this edition of WorldWide, he’s facing the one man that finished second place in the rumble from last week, Jordan White. Will his revolution official start at WorldWide?
