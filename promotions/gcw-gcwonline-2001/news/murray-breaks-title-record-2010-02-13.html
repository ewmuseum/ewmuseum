Murray Breaks Title Record

13 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The year may not have given him many favours thus far, but finally The King has a reason to be cheerful in 2010. It’s official – as of today (Saturday, 13th February), Andy Murray is the longest serving GCW World Heavyweight Champion of all-time. Today, Murray’s 237-day reign edges out that of his former flame, Vivica J. Valentine, but with his next defence due at NC-17 in seven days’ time, The Scottish King of Cool is guaranteed at least 244 days as the holder of GCW’s top title. Having defeated LeStatt Knight for the strap at last year’s Rampage event (held on June 21st), Murray has ensured his place in GCW history and etched his name into the record books by overcoming stern challenges from the likes of Jordan White, Jason O’Neil, and Chris Bagwell along the way. In total, he has made 7 success defences of his belt, making his reign not only lengthy but relatively competitive as well. For Murray, it is the first glimmer of light in what has been a dismal 2010 thus far. After his girlfriend, Vivica J. Valentine, turned on him at Christmas Chaos, the shell-shocked champion has had to endure a barrage of verbal and finger pointing from his former partner. At times, the usually lively Scot has visibly struggled to come to terms with Valentine’s sudden and violent betrayal. Renowned throughout the industry for his charm, manners, and good nature, Murray now finds himself standing on an emotional battleground the likes of which he has never experienced, although he will surely take some solace knowing that he has bettered Valentine’s cherished record. There is no doubt that Valentine will be left stinging by Murray’s latest accomplishment, even if it has been inevitable for a few weeks. Time and time again, Valentine has expressed her anger at the fact that her now former partner’s star was slowly beginning to eclipse hers, and this latest twist will surely further fan the flames of her discontent. This is not good for Andy Murray. No amount of pep-talks from LeStatt Knight will be able to adequately prepare him for the challenge ahead. At NC-17, he faces easily the biggest threat to his championship reign yet. Valentine is hungry, determined. A woman who says she is willing to die for the GCW World Championship. He violent streak knows no limits, and she will literally do anything and everything it takes to wrestle the belt from Murray’s clutches. This could be a huge problem for Murray, who, despite claiming that he is ready for whatever Vivica may throw at him, is known for his reluctance to unleash violence on even his most hated foes, let alone opponents he may still have lingering feelings for. On paper, the odds don’t look great for him. The GCW World Championship has never been successfully defended at NC-17, and it is unlikely that he will face a more dangerous opponent than Valentine for the rest of his career. However, those loyal to The Scottish King of Cool know better than to bet against Murray. When put on the big stage, this nice guy rarely finishes last.
