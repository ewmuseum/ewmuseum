WorldWide Update

23 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Internet rumors have been whirlwind spiraling around about the delayed WorldWide that has moved back the original date of the Rampage PPV. While the front office insists that these delays will have no effect of future schedules, they have admitted that unforeseeable problems have arisen during the oversea tours. They continue on to say, however, that it is not because of any of the recent scandals to hit members of the roster. Rather, sources say, it is because of travel visa problems that delay the show. A number of backstage staff and even talent were not assigned travel visas for the correct length of time and when they tried to travel to Nagoya they were stopped by security. Of those stopped, the highest caliber appears to be the reigning Tag Team Champions Banned & Exiled~! A representative for the group stated that the front office did not give them the proper information regarding how long the tour would last, thus creating the problem. Those affected by this are Chris Storm, Markus Stone, Rikki Roxx, Arizona, and Alexa Saint (who still is under GCW contract and travels with Stone). Banned & Exiled~! made sure to add; "This is just ol? Stevie trying to get one over on us. He thinks he can keep us from getting proper time to train for our match. But the jokes on him; we hardly ever train for our opponents!" To which Chris Storm added, "yeah we just get lucky and hope they wore themselves out with all that “working out”." Tessa Windsor, who reportedly did not have any visa problems, still is listed as one of the ones that stayed behind. Ms. Windsor apparently filled out her own paper work, ensuring it would be correct. It is believed she stayed behind to help her fianc? and his tag partner correct this situation. One source claims she is very upset with the two, believing they again did not take responsibility for themselves and claims she said "they would be very upset if I [Tessa Windsor] have to miss any appearances because of them." She had more to say regarding the issue, but they can’t be repeated without heavy censorship. Others believed to be affected by passport issues are LeStatt Knight, Seymour Almasy, Chris Bagwell, Lia Ambrosi, and Kiljaden Kincaid. Some sources say even Steven Caldera has had problems with his passport, but that is chalked up more to mischievous acts of Banned & Exiled~! than any legal issues. Unnamed insiders say the two somehow got a hold of his passport and proceeded to draw glasses, a funny mustache, and a giant afro onto the picture with a permanent marker. Earlier reports listed the Church of the Unholy as also having visa problems, but that has been proven to be incorrect. Evil James and Axel Action have been held up by security as well, but because the disembodied head of a Japanese prostitute was found in their bags. She was reportedly a sacrifice to Tioneb. Why they carried her head through security has yet to be discovered. It is believed the two may have been inhaling paint fumes before stepping into the airport. That or they are very stupid. We’ll keep updates on the story as it develops. But for now it appears WorldWide 86 will be on hold until the issues can be resolved.
