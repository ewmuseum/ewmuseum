Jackson Out Of Establishment?

1 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Excerpt from The Wrestling Truth (http://www.wrestlingtruth.com) posted July 1st, 2009 by Alex Collins Rampage has come and gone, leaving us with a very different impression of the monster known as Brad Jackson. The barbed wire cage match was chosen specifically by the man as punishment for his latest target, the fan favorite Garbage Bag Johnny. Those who have followed his eleven year career know that some of Jackson's most memorable wins have come inside similar structures. To see him denied for the first time seems to have marked a downward spiral in Jackson's GCW career, lending further credence to the rumors floating around the Internet before the Pay-Per-View event hit the airwaves. Representatives from Jackson's camp, as well as the superstar himself have been tight-lipped on the subject of Jackson's departure from GCW, as well as new rumors stating that Jackson may not have a place in the new Establishment. After failing to finish the battle that he started against Garbage Bag Johnny at Steven Caldera's request months ago, it's no wonder to find Jackson on such shaky footing. Although, truth be told, it's not much of a stretch of the imagination. Jackson has floundered within the promotion since gracing their ring more than two years ago, never really managing to break out of the mid card hell that has threatened to bury him more than once. Fizzle and fade like a defective bottle rocket seems to be Jackson's new modus operandi. Will Jackson remain as part of The Establishment? Will he even be on the GCW roster come WorldWide 113? I guess we'll find out soon enough.
