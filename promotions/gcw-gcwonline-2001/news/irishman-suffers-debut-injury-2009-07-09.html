Irishman suffers debut injury

9 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCWonline.net has today learnt that one of it's most recent talent acquisitions; the Irish veteran, SurReal, was the unfortunate victim of a back injury which will derail any thoughts of making an immediate impact within the company. The injury, a Herniated Disc occurred during the six-man tag match at Rampage which pitted SurReal and his partners; Face & Derek Machida against the team of AXEL Action, Mark in the Dark & Lucas Quinton. A herniated disk occurs when the disc is damaged by a number of situations. Some of the most common causes of a disc injury are; A fall or accident, repeated straining of your back, sudden strain on the back from lifting / twisting violently or degeneration of the disc from ageing. SurReal, who spent the majority of the last five years battling back from what was presumed to be a career-ending back injury, is receiving steroid injections and anti-inflammatory medications to alleviate the pain whilst attending ongoing physical therapy. The injury is not thought to be very serious but having taken SurReal’s recent medical history on board the medical team working with GCW has taken the measures as a precaution against further injury. As a result, he has been taken off the active duty list for a minimum of two weeks whilst his progress is closely monitored. The man known as the “Tribal War Machine” is reportedly devastated at the timing of the injury. He felt he was coming off great momentum from his recent run in the now defunct Ringside Wrestling and had hoped that momentum would carry him forward as he looks to continue his career with Global Championship Wrestling. Sources close to SurReal revealed that, he himself is blaming wrestling rookie Lucas Quinton for the accident. Rumours have spread of some genuine dislike developing between the pair, and no-doubt watching Quinton’s rapid rise in stock in the wrestling world during his time away is bound to rile the Irishman further. As always when we hear of any developments we will let you know!
