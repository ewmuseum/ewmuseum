Warpath Breakdown: Jordan White

25 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Warpath just days away, James Bryan joins us to break down the exciting Main Event match between Vivica J. Valentine, Jay Terror and Jordan White. The GCW World Heavyweight Championship is on the line, and this is shaping up to be one of the most competitive GCW Title matches in history. JB will be breaking down each competitor's strengths, weaknesses and motivations, and we'll cap it off with the staff's prediction. Today: Jordan White Introduction: Jordan White is one of the most enigmatic wrestlers in GCW history. A man with few close friends, it's difficult to know what he's thinking at any given time. White has gained a cult following over the past year and a half, having proven himself to be one of the most extraordinary talents this company has ever seen. His actions, however, are erratic and it is difficult to pinpoint just what, exactly, Jordan White stands for. With Jay Terror fast ascending and Vivica J. Valentine making a case for being the best GCW Champion of all time, White's detractors say he has not evolved to a level that will allow him to compete with his opponents at Warpath. However, on pure talent alone, White might have an edge over both of them. His weaknesses, however, are pronounced, and he'll have to overcome the ring savvy of Valentine and the improvisational skills of Terror to win. Biggest Strengths: White's biggest advantage in this match will be his explosiveness. White can strike quickly and with a great deal of force. If he can take an opponent by surprise, he could manage to steal the victory. Remember, Valentine does not have to be involved in the decision to lose the title, giving both challengers an edge. White has proven that he's deceptively strong and tough. He's not used to having a size advantage, but he will be the biggest wrestler in the match. Biggest Weaknesses: White has struggled in big-time matches lately. In 2006, he defeated Valentine at Warpath and then defeated Rich Rollins at Tsunami. Since then, however, his Pay-Per-View record is not good. He lost to Lia Ambrosi at Dangerous Games, lost in a rematch with Rollins at NC-17, and fell to Terror at Rampage this year. White's exceptionally talented, but for whatever reason he's yet to break through and win the big one. If White has the confidence required to win, he'll be fine. But his head might prevent his body from doing what's necessary. Biggest Career Victory: Defeating Rollins at Tsunami. Rich Rollins, the Iron Man, made a career out of his unbeaten record in Iron Man matches. White and Rollins locked up in an Iron Man bout at Tsunami 2006, and White took Rollins down. This appeared to be the precursor to a title win, but once again White's mental issues kept him from winning the title. What He Needs To Do To Win: Hit hard and hit fast. White's got the size advantage in this match, so he needs to adapt his game to utilize that. Both of his opponents hit hard, but White will have the edge in a slugfest. White needs to make this match a brawl and keep it at ground level. How He Matches Up With Terror: White has never managed to get an edge on Terror, but this will be the first time they will be in the ring together with the playing field level. Terror had Caldera at his back at Rampage; the President isn't expected to make it back in time for Warpath. Terror will have a slight speed and quickness edge, but these two have similar techniques. White demonstrated at WorldWide last week that he might have retained more technical knowledge from his time with The Establishment than he's been leading on. How He Matches Up With Valentine: White's quick, but Valentine can run circles around the best of them. White will need to keep the champion grounded, forcing her to match up blow for blow. White defeated Valentine at Warpath last year, and has routinely edged her in their meetings since then. White has always managed to use his explosiveness to keep Valentine off balance, but the Champion's game has been taken to another level since winning the title. Motivation: White is about as talented as they come, but that has never translated into a GCW World Title win. White claims he is only in the business for the paycheck, but his actions suggest there might be more to him than he reveals. Even after Jay Terror interfered in the High Stakes main event, White took advantage and tried to steal the title from Valentine. This hasn't made White all that popular in the eyes of the fans, but most seem to understand that White secretly harbors a deep lust for the gold. The story of this match for Jordan White is evolution. Can he evolve into a World Championship-caliber wrestler? Can he win on the big stage with the odds against him? It has been clear in the last month that Valentine and Terror are savvy in the ways of professional wrestling. White has always resisted playing the game, preferring to do things his own way. However, Valentine has been pushing White to break free of whatever barriers he has and become the wrestler he's always had the potential to be. If White is prepared to come out of his shell and engage the business on all its fronts, he could get what some say he wants more than anything. There is a battle raging within Jordan White; it seems he wants the World Title but might not be willing to compromise his apathy long enough to get it. Will he do as Valentine and Terror suggest and become more like them? Will he refuse and fall short? Or will his unorthodox approach to these matches finally land him the big prize? Whatever the case, it is impossible to know what Jordan White's true motives are. He keeps himself well guarded and meticulously prevents anyone from gaining insight to his mind. This much remains true, however: Jordan White's legacy could be defined by this match. He can erase a year of near-misses with a huge title victory at Warpath, or he can add to the long list of failures. White remains immensely popular and continues to have the potential to become one of the most dominant wrestlers in the world. But he's going to have to evolve and become something greater if he wants to accomplish that. If not, he'll continue to be a tremendously talented wrestler that merely shows up to collect his paycheck. Next Up: Jay Terror: Tomorrow Vivica J. Valentine: Sunday Staff Predictions: Monday
