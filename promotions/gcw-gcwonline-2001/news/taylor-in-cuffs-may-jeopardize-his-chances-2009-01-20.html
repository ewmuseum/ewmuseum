Taylor in cuffs, may jeopardize his chances...

20 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the spectacle Zacharia Taylor and Garbage Bag Johnny put on at World Wide 104, we now have confirmed reports that Zacharia Taylor is in custody in New Jersey in suspicions of theft and the murder of an identified male, Michael Brewster. Intentions are still unknown, and due to resisting arrest, the police chief of Clifton said, no bail will be posted. Zacharia Taylor, the fast rising star of GCW in 2008 will have to face justice right before the biggest match of his career. He tried to evade police at the World Wide show, and used Garbage Bag Johnny as a hostage all the way up to the ring right before the main event where Johnny took out Taylor, leaving him for the police. GBJ was nowhere for comment after the arrest, but several wrestlers who looked on at the top of the ramp as Taylor was hauled away came to life after the show: Andy Murray on top of the ramp talking to a crowd of reporters: Murray: "Zachy T cleaved a fool? Man, what a stupid mother sucker. You know, I thought that dude had came to his senses when he ran away from Bagwell and the boys, but I guess there was something loose inside that head of his all along. And on the eve of his biggest ever match? Stupid little bastard." AXEL Action from his locker room: AXEL: "It's a real shame what happened to Zachariah Taylor. Getting arrested and all, you know? But it's what happens when you try to make deals with a man who is so burnt out that he can't even remember to bathe himself. With Taylor gone, that just means there's one less person I have to step over on my way up the ranks." Newcomer Lantlas and his manager Devon react after the show: Devon: “How Exactly are we supposed to feel anything about some guy we don’t even know yet” We just freaking got here, man! But come on, if he can’t handle himself in jail, he best not be in the business. Why, I’ve got a record a mile long and you don’t see me bitchin?, do ya? Why, just the other day? Lantlas reaches out and covers her mouth. Devon: Oh come on! Lantlas whispers something to Devon. Devon: What do you mean it's best not to piss off anyone yet? What, are you gonna make me fight the match? Lantlas stares at Devon. Devon: Fine, you always ruin my fun! The Thrillion Man March sat down for an interview before they left the arena: Beef Supreme: Who did he say? Quesa Dilla: Jeremiah Taylor? Nacho Grande: No no, Zachariah Taylor. Quesa Dilla: Dude, any name with a Z in it can't be good. Beef Supreme: Seriously, man. That name alone sounds like he was up to some mischief. What's the charge, anyway? Quesa Dilla: He knocked over your Taco Bell and left Wendy's 3conomics ads there! Beef Supreme: That son of a bitch, I'll kill him! Beef Supreme runs down the hall, and Quesa runs after him, screaming it was just a joke. Nacho Grande stares after the two. Nacho Grande: I apologize on behalf of... Well, anyone who saw that... But hey, if the cat did what he did, he got what he had comin', but if he didn't, there's always a superhero he can call to save the day! NEW KIDS OUT! Nacho hums the chorus line to "Hangin' Tough" as he dances away. Dan Black had a much different take on it: "I can't say I'm surprised. Its like I said before, Taylor only managed to hold the television title for as long as he did because he kept ducking his competition. He won't become the United States Champion because he just doesn't have what it takes to be a real champion’and getting arrested is his way out of getting destroyed by Garbage Bag Johnny" Then Brian Fisher elegantly responded: Fisher: “Uh, Who the fuck is Zacharia Taylor?” Chris Bagwell, long time friend of Taylor’s commented after his match with Jordan White; Bagwell: "I saw him taken away, but arrested?"? Bagwell responded promptly with a sound of shock, as if he hadn't heard a thing about it.” "For murder?” I don't know what kind of fucked up gimmick that sonuvabitch Stevie-boy has Zacharia doing now, but he sure as hell picked the wrong guy to try and pass off as some murderer.” Taylor wouldn't kill a dead ant!" The big question? Will Zacharia Taylor make it to NC-17? Will he miss the biggest opportunity of his career to cement himself into GCW history as truly the fastest rising star? Garbage Bag Johnny is undefeated in the company, and Steven Caldera has already said that Taylor can do it. So will we have a match? Nobody knows.
