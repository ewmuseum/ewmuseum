Year-Ending Hiring Spree?

14 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As previously reported, "Detective" Kraig Carter debuted on WorldWide this week after completing his contract negotiations with GCW. Carter represented the first new hire after a period in which the company brought in no new talent. That certainly seems to be coming to an end. GCW is reportedly in contract negotiations with Kiljaden Kincaid, Thom LeStrange and former AWC wrestler Andy Murray. All three were recently spotted at GCW Headquarters and are reportedly negotiating contracts. GCW's fourth quarter financial report is due out shortly after the new year, and it is expected to be the highest quarter of earnings in company history. To capitalize on this momentum it is being said that GCW is attempting to bring in more and more top talent to bolster its already impressive roster and continue its push for supremacy. Though the year is coming to a close and GCW will close its operations for the holidays following NC-17, it seems the company will be opening 2008 with a healthy compliment of new talent.
