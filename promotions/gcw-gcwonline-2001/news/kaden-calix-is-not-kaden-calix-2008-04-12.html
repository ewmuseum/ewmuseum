Kaden Calix is NOT Kaden Calix?

12 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As many around the industry know, Christopher Calix, better known as Kaden, has not seen the light of day outside the stone walls of Southwind Gardens, located somewhere in central Kansas, since late 2007. So how did he sign with GCW? Lately there has been word about that it's just a hoax, and the young superstar has not made his way to Global Championship Wrestling after the fallout of GLOBAL late last year. Some fans have even been quoted in the matter of saying, "It's just GCW's cheap way of grabbing more of an audience, hoping to get the attention of disappointed fans from the now defunct GLOBAL." and also, "This is a joke right? GCW surely wouldn't use him like that would they? Feeding off his growing popularity with another federation to get more attention? If it is all true, shame on GCW!" The only other explanation would be from the less than a handful of people who noticed an unknown man, clearly in his sixties or older, roam the halls outside of Caldera's office six to eight weeks prior to the "signing" of the up and coming superstar, Kaden Calix. Unfortunately, with Calix being held "hostage" within the walls of the mental institute, by the request of a man he thought was his friend, billionaire professional wrestler and business man, Justin Michaels, Kaden is not available for any comment to put rest to these rumors. When contacted about Kaden, Michaels only had this to say: "Kaden is my friend, but the torture that he endured at the hands of his very own parents drove him over the edge. He became very ill, and I did what I thought was best." He did not comment on the suspected signing of his "friend" to GCW, so until we can see for ourselves, this one will not be put to rest.
