Aaron Fujita Remains Focused on the American Dream

10 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aaron Fujita responds to the bold comments made by K-Wolf. With Well-Known Zip Gordon looming ahead of him in the first round of the Rampage Tournament, Aaron Fujita’s concentration could be elsewhere; such as his shot at the United States Title. Fujita was asked about his state of mind this past weekend at an autograph signing at the Gamestop in his hometown of Plainfield, Illinois. “Of course that’s something that’s in the back of my mind day in and day out.” Fujita said. “But that’s where you weed out the strong minds versus the week minds. No matter how much my shot at the United States title weighs on my mind, my focus shifts to the next match hand. Beating Zip Gordon and advancing in the Rampage tournament is what I’m focused on right now.” When asked about Karina Wolfenden’s comments earlier this week, Fujita laughed and had this to say. “She can say whatever she wants about me not having any balls, but it’s all a façade. You can bet your ass I’m going to cash my shot in when she’s worn down. She can put this show on and say that she’ll be ready whenever I show up, but the reality is that it is just that; a show. I can count the competitors in this business that could be one hundred percent on top of their game in that situation on one hand. Nowhere on that list does the name Karina Wolfenden come up. The fact of the matter is that when I do cash in my shot, she’s going to be worn down, unprepared and most importantly very, very beatable. I’ll give her credit to being one of the best I’ve ever seen, but when the clock strikes midnight, the little Cinderella story she’s got playing in her head is going to come to an end. It won’t be a happy one either.” One has to wonder when exactly Fujita plans to use his shot at the US Title. This week both he and K-Wolf are in first round action of the 2011 Rampage tournament.
