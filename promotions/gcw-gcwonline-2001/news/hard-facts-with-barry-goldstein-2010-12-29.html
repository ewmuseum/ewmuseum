Hard Facts with Barry Goldstein

29 December 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Local favorite Barry Goldstein gets the REAL inside scoop through his secret, ninja-like strategic camouflaging techniques. Hi Folks, Barry Goldstein here with the latest batch of backstage gossip. "How does Barry do it?" you ask. "How does Barry get these juicy secrets?" Well, a true magician never reveals the secret to his tricks! Anyhow, we saw a lot of new faces pop up in the Dangerous Games match, and a lot of interesting names appear on the GCW roster page as of late. Who's here to stay? Who's already gone? * FishBough might not be a permanent addition to the roster. Their appearance at Dangerous Games was surely unexpected, but since then, neither has committed to any specific bookings with GCW. However, it is believed that the Tag Team Titles might re-enter the picture, and FishBough may be repurposed as the purveyors of this initiative. * The addition of Aimz and Terrence Kingsley to the GCW Roster mark the signings of two highly renowned names. It has been theorized that GBJ has been letting loose the purse strings in response to PRIME's Jewel in the Crown tournament, which netted PRIME some huge free agents in The Sentinel, Violence Jack, and former GCW star Jacob McKail as well as double agents like Desade. Get it? The jury is still out on whether or not Aimz is a lesbian, but I've got my eye on that. ;) * Sources close to the Thunder camp have revealed that with his bad run of form and heavy pressure from his family, Jimmy Thunder will be at World Wide this week to announce his retirement. The source revealed that Jimmy endured immense pressure from his family to stand down for the last time and concentrate on his family rather than risk injury. * Former SCCW Champion Jared Sykes is no stranger to GCW Television. With two GCW matches under his belt, he's had some exposure to the fans, namely in a PTC Elite Title Tournament victory over Andy Murray. We might not see Jared in action until next after NC-17, however, but I've heard rumblings about a Murray-Sykes feud depending on the outcome of the World Title bout between Andy Murray and champion Jay Terror. * Keep an eye on El Moreno and Dynasty. Insiders have bookmarked this duo as a pair to watch moving towards NC-17. With Moreno's 3-0 start over some high profile challengers, we might see a memorable streak. Either way, the two are set for a major push depending on their workrates this cycle, and despite Moreno's hot start, I've heard that Dynasty might be primed to be in world title contention by this time next year. * Local favorite Barry Goldstein has been under investigation for spying on the GCW women's locker room through a drillhole that mysteriously appears in every arena. He faces similar accusations for the GCW men's locker room. Despite what conclusions may arise in the near future, I've heard from a very good source that these rumors are untrue. Well, there you have it, folks. Remember...you didn't hear any of this from me!
