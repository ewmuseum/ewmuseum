Mr. Action! Signs Deal with GCW

8 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What was thought to be merely a rumor has now been deemed as a true fact. Global Championship Wrestling has in fact come to terms with the signing of wrestling veteran and current Legacy of Champions grappler, Jesse Ramey. The announcement came earlier today during a press conference held by Jesse Ramey himself. Jesse Ramey is a veteran of the sport and has been wrestling for over sixteen years and has worked for various global promotions such as Old School Wrestling, Five Star Wrestling, PURE, Action! Wrestling, the Squared Circle, and is a current member of the Legacy of Champions. "I’ve been looking for quite some time to extend my reach on the professional wrestling community," Jesse stated during his press conference. "The Legacy of Champions wasn’t enough for me; I needed to branch out and build off of more competition. As many people know I’ve never been able to settle myself into one place and be happy with beating every single person that is thrown in my path. So, when I was approached with a contract to wrestle for Global Championship Wrestling I wasn’t hesitant in the least." "GCW will soon know the reason I’ve gained the moniker of Bad Attitude, Mr. Action!, and the Total Package of Professional Wrestling. Today marks a corner stone for the GCW community and I guarantee ratings and buy rates in the coming months will increase with each show that I am featured on." It is unknown when the ever confident Ramey will make his wrestling debut in Global Championship Wrestling. But let us hope that a man who can definitely talk the talk will be able to back up his words.
