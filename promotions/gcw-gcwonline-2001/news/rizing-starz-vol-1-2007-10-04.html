Rizing Starz (Vol. 1)

4 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Konichiwa everyone! My name is Ayake Sonoda and I'm here to report to you the very first installment of Rizing Starz. This will be a regular column where I sit down with various members of the Global Championship Wrestling roster and get to know them as... regular people. Find out what they like to do on their time away from the spotlight. I couldn't have asked for a better person to start with than my first participant Vivica J. Valentine! A former GCW United States Champion and PrimeTime Central Infinite Gauntlet Champion... The Fearless Phenom has been a mainstay in GCW for almost two years now. She's faced everyone from Brandon Youngblood to Tessa Windsor, Sam Wolack to Lestatt Knight, and everyone inbetween. Joining GCW as a member of a stable known as Dies Irae along side current World Heavyweight Champion Lia Ambrosi, Vivica wasn't originally accepted by GCW fans. Though through hard work and determination she's easily become one of GCW's biggest stars. At Dangerous Games, the one they call The Fearless Phenom faces what many call her biggest challenge to date. Facing GCW Legend Bryan Mayhem in an Inferno Match where to become the winner you must set your opponet on fire. After this match, she's currently slated to participate in the Dangerous Games Battle Royal itself to earn a shot at the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Leading up to the big night, I caught up to Vivica to learn more about the woman behind blue eyes. ---- Name: Vivica Julian Valentine Birthday: October 31, 1982 Birthplace: Highland Park, Illinois Current Residence: Highland Park, Illinois Height: 5' 4" Weight: 131 lbs. Eyes: Blue Hair: Blonde Blood Type: O+ ---- Ayake Sonoda: Welcome to Rizing Starz Vivica! How does it feel to be the first victim... er... guest? Vivica J Valentine: (laughs) It's a little scary now that you said victim! I'm just kidding, it's exciting did I win a lotto or something? AS: Dart board. VJV: Ah... should've known. You can call me Viv. It's what I'm used to. AS: Okay then Viv (smiles). A big part of your life seems to be music. I know personally just from chatting to you just a bit before this interview that you're really into rock music and metal. It's not something you usually expect from a girl who grew up in a rather wealthy suburban place like Highland Park. How were you exposed to this music? VJV: I grew up like most girls in my generation listening to Britney Spears and Christina Aguilera but I really got into No Doubt. I had friends who listened to No Doubt that listened to other bands like Smashing Pumpkins, Weezer, and Garbage. Once I dove into the whole rock scene I was hooked. It's like a drug for me, I absolutely love it and still catch live shows whenever I get the chance. AS: Who is your favorite band? VJV: Rise Against. Definitely. They're from Chicago like me and their music is just so honest and passionate. It speaks to me I think since we kind of had the same upbringing. Great lyrics, unique sound, absolutely love those guys. AS: But your entrance music is a song by Thrice called "Image Of The Invisible". Which is also the name of your unofficial fanclub. Why that song? VJV: It kind of speaks about how I felt about my career right after I lost the United States Championship, and how a lot of people out in the crowd felt as well. I felt kind of like a payroll number, a non-important member of this company. No one paid attention to me as a person, I was kind of lost in the shuffle. Not to mention a majority of the people in the back didn't even really seem to see the crowd as much more than dollar signs, instead of individual people. I was in that crowd growing up going to wrestling shows and such... I kind of feel like I can represent them. It was time to rise up and let everyone know "Hey, we're here, we're important, and there is nothing you can do about it." AS: That's kind of cool. I like that. Speaking of rising up, you've never really had a great relationship with GCW staff... namely Steven Caldera and Toad. Not to mention you seem to only have a few very close friends and shut yourself off to everyone else. Why is that? VJV: I grew up in a rich high school full of snobby girls? (laughs) But really... I haven't really had the best relationships with people through life. I've been stabbed in the back, a lot. It gets tough to open up and just trust people. You have to earn it. AS: Makes sense to me. Growing up in the wrestling business I can kind of relate, my father was kind of the same way. VJV: You're Yuji Sonoda's daughter aren't you? AS: Yeah. (smiles) VJV: I saw some tapes of your father growing up, he was very good. Tell him I said hello! AS: He appreciates it I assure you, I'll let him know. You grew up watching wrestling, who were some of your favorites? VJV: Ooooh... Randy Savage was my favorite. "Superfly" Jimmy Snuka, Shawn Michaels, Owen Hart, Dynamite Kid. AS: Can't go wrong with a list like that. I have a couple quick questions for you before I let you go. VJV: Fire away! AS: Favorite color? VJV: Black. I know... it's not really a color. I love it anyway. AS: Favorite food? VJV: Chinese. AS: Favorite place to wrestle? VJV: Oh damn... United Center in Chicago. I'm a homer. AS: Favorite GCW star who isn't yourself? VJV: Lia Ambrosi. She taught me so much. AS: Hottest man on television? VJV: (laughs) No comment. AS: Alright that's all I have for you today. Good luck at Dangerous Games Viv, maybe next time we talk you'll be #1 Contendor? VJV: That's the plan, thanks for having me Ayake. ---- We'll catch you next time fans somtime after Dangerous Games with Rizing Starz Vol. 2! Until then... Sayonara! - "Aki-San" Ayake Sonoda
