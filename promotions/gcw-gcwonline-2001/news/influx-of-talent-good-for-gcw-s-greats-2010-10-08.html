Influx of Talent Good for GCW's Greats

8 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fresh faces flesh out industry's most impressive, celebrated roster with just weeks left before Dangerous Games. Who will rise? Who will fall? Within the past few months, GCW has brought in young talent to help keep the industry leader's ratings at the top of the charts. Names like Bagwell, Storm, LeStatt Knight, and Jay Terror can only continue to be considered the best if they are put up against the best. In the past few weeks, Global Championship Wrestling has put together a hiring drive to showcase some fresh faces for their future. GCW made it apparent that they have an eye for exceptional talent, signing such talent such as Donny Diamond and Triumph Frost, who quickly gained contender status a few simple months after stepping into the GCW spotlight. Other notable newcomers are Dominic Wall, who has reached the Main Event just three matches into his career. Fresh signings Sylus Creed, Dorn Tracer, and Alexander Redding squared off at WW 138 in a great first impression on the GCW faithful. Another new signing will debut at WorldWide 139, GCW officials report. The newcomer, Dynasty, is a fresh product from the dark and dreary Mexican wrestling circuit with "all the potential to push GCW top talent." More information on this signing will be released next week but as for now, the only details we have on this wrestler is that he has no professional wrestling experience, reportedly playing professional baseball for two years then turning to Mexico for refuge from athletics. With Dangerous Games just around the corner, we have to wonder how the rookies and the proven stars of GCW will clash in the weeks leading to DG - culminating in the massive thirty-man melee - a match that will shape the GCW's turn into 2011 the minute the bell rings.
