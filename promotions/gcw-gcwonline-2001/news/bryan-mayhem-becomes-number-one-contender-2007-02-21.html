Bryan Mayhem Becomes Number One Contender

21 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At WorldWide this week, Bryan Mayhem finally scaled the mountain and became the number one contender to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. By winning the ten-man battle royal in Cincinnati, Mayhem earned his first ever GCW World Heavyweight Title shot. The longest tenured member of GCW's active roster now has one final hurdle to clear in order to rise to the top for the first time; World Heavyweight Champion Lia Ambrosi. Mayhem, whose name has risen to newfound prominence in recent weeks due to his new alliance with Shaman, seems poised to capture the title for the first time. One of the most physically dominating wrestlers in GCW history, Mayhem has always fallen just short of what has always been his ultimate goal. However, after six years of destruction, Mayhem has finally found himself in a position to capture the highest title in the company. World Champion Lia Ambrosi, however, showed no fear on WorldWide after seeing Mayhem earn the title shot. Mayhem violently split from Dies Irae earlier in the evening, and the night ended when he dropped Jordan White over the top rope to earn his title shot. Mayhem then rose his arms and summoned a ring of fire around champion Lia Ambrosi, a chilling sign of what is to come for the champion. Ambrosi, however, is facing this new challenge with a renewed sense of vigor. The Champion is at a distinct size disadvantage, giving up nearly three hundred pounds to the massive Mayhem. Ambrosi, however, has proven her ability to stay in the ring with the biggest and most powerful of GCW stars. Having beaten legend LeStatt Knight for the title, Ambrosi has shown that she has a mastery of leverage and deceptive strength that allows her to compete with wrestlers many times her size. While no date has been set for the title match, President Steven Caldera has said that the details of Mayhem's title shot will be finalized in the next two weeks. When asked if Mayhem/Ambrosi could headline Warpath this week, Caldera said, "I certainly think so. Mayhem won't have to wait that long to get his shot, but if the situation permits I could see a rematch happening at Warpath. It all depends on what happens in this first bout."
