WorldWide 95: A Bad Night for Valentine

16 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MONTREAL, QUEBEC- Coming in to WorldWide this week, World Champion Vivica J. Valentine was flying as high as any wrestler could. She had defeated Jay Terror and Jordan White to retain her title at Warpath, and in doing so had essentially sent The Establishment into a tailspin. But in Montreal, the empire struck back. WorldWide 95 opened not with a much-deserved championship celebration, but with the returning President Steven Caldera. Caldera declared his intention to "set the house in order," and his first act was to make a one-on-one rematch for the GCW Title between Valentine and Terror. The main event was another furious battle between the two, and Valentine proved once again that she has the biggest heart of any wrestler in the world today. But still weakened after her last defense and facing an incredibly determined opponent, Valentine found herself up against her toughest defense yet... But the bad night didn't stop there. After the match during Valentine's standing ovation, one of her closest friends and colleagues Tessa Windsor made her way to the ring. Valentine had to be hoping for a reconciliation, but after Windsor's actions it is clear she'll be getting the exact opposite. Elsewhere, President Caldera was making it clear to fellow Establishment member Tyson DeBough that Garbage Bag Johnny's incredible streak was not to continue in the American Dream match. And with Jacob McKail and Zachariah Taylor also vying for the prize, the odds would be too long for Johnny to overcome... right? DeBough may not have finished the job during the match, but Garbage Bag Johnny wouldn't have long to celebrate... After the match an angry McKail assaulted Taylor outside the ring, where the two were separated by GCW Security. Rumor has it that McKail confronted Taylor again backstage, but Security was on top of the situation and a physical altercation was avoided. The Tag Team Champions Samson and Delylah are still rolling, taking out the SoCal Stretching Crew to retain their titles. They've got a long way to go to catch the record set by Banned and Exiled~!, but they're already drawing some comparisons. Cary Turkington was all over WorldWide this week, getting under the skin of The Establishment's Octavian. With Caldera making it clear that he wants Cary, Paddy and Johnny kept out of the spotlight, Octavian is hellbent on reigning Cary in. But was he successful? Guess. Later, Chris Bagwell got back on track with a win over Clyde Fox. But with Fox coming off two straight wins over Tyson DeBough, both of these men are being talked about as Dangerous Games favorites. Clyde Fox and Chris Bagwell are about as opposite as opposite gets, and this rivalry is likely to continue as we draw nearer to the big event. This week's WorldWide was a tremendous event that turned GCW on its head. The company was shocked by this unexpected turn of events, and now it seems clear that The Establishment is once again in firm control of GCW. With Caldera's infamous group pulling all the strings once again, the question heading into next week's event: Who in GCW will step up to fight The Establishment now?
