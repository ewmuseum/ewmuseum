Bagwell To Rally Superstars in Inter-fed Battle

21 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the sudden announcement of the TEAM Interfed Battle between PRIME's future stars and GCW's future stars, one would imagine that it'd be only a matter of time before someone decided it was time to voice their opinion. And who better to get things riled up then the 'mouth' of the GCW himself, Chris Bagwell... "PRIME Wrestling? They couldn't come up with a better little anecdote than PRIME for their organization?" questioned Bagwell when asked of his thoughts about PRIME Wrestling. "PRIME Wrestling is a group of guys who are trying to live off the since faded success of Primetime Championship Wrestling and Primetime Central. They're named in hopes that some dyslexic dickhead gets all confused and mistakes one for the other." The match was announced Tuesday, pitting PRIME's Simply Beautiful, Jason Natas, Union Jack and Jimmy Bonafide up against GCW's very own Chris Bagwell, Zacharia Taylor, Thom LeStrange, and Brad Jackson. The event is set to take its course on May 31st from the Philips Arena in Atlanta, Georgia. When asked about his opponents, Chris had this to say: "I made a challenge to Lindsey Troy a few weeks back. She had the audacity to go ahead and call me out, claiming how she'd beat my ass and blah, blah. A ton of shit talk. After not only IGNORING my request and not so much as acknowledging it, Troy decides to send over a bunch of nameless rookies to fight me? Give me a break." Bagwell's confidence seemed greater than it has ever been before, which as we all know is hard to top. He assured us that we'd be hearing a ton more on the subject of the match come the next installment of GCW radio. We closed our interview with Chris Bagwell with a question about whether he's the slightest bit worried about PRIME pulling a victory over GCW, even if it meant one of his teammates giving up the pinfall. He had this to say: "So as long as we don't have the same screwjob referee officiating this match that was officiating the Valentine/Troy match, I've got not the slightest bit of worry in my partners' ability. Zacharia Taylor marched into the GCW and made a very profound statement by taking the TV Title from Clyde Fox. Thom LeStrange has made his presence felt in the United States title division, battling with the best. And Brad Jackson... just look at the guy. He gives little kids nightmares. The GCW crew are newcomers that made an impact. The PRIME crew are newcomers who are still waiting to get their first taste of TV time. But as I said .. lets just hope our 'officials' aren't shooting quayludes before the match" With that said, Bagwell joined the rest of his Movement on the tour bus to High Stakes in Las Vegas. Be sure not to miss the huge interfed war on May 31st!
