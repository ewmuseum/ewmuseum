More than two thirds of the members of the Church of the Unholy were arrested and detained after trying to break into a Chinese Military Weapons Depot late tonight. After being caught on a mission to raid weapons for an attack against the Cult of Obama, the Church of the Unholy lost almost all of it's membership after the failed weapons raid. Over a hundred people were arrested during the clash with the Chinese Military.
