DeBough Celebrates Championship Win

13 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the very controversial stunt pulled by Tyson DeBough at Tsunami, what with him stealing the GCW Television Championship and running off with it, questions have been flying around the roster area. Even more astounding, is President Steven Caldera, who refused to acknowledge the wrong in this, and has even sanctioned DeBough's first title defense on the next World Wide! GCW Press got ahold of DeBough via phone lines earlier today, just long enough to ask him a few questions. GCW: So, why did you steal the title?? DeBough: Steal? I just took what was rightfully mine. I earned the right to wear this belt. GCW: But Terror never lost to you, or O'Shea. He is the rightful champion. DeBough: If I was the champion, I would make sure my belt was no where around to be "stolen" by anyone. In my opinion, I am the rightful owner of this belt now. Finders keepers. GCW: And your defense? DeBough: Mark my words, from this point on I will be the longest reigning champion...ever. GCW: Ever? DeBough: World, Tag Team, United States, whatever championship you want. I will become the longest title holder in history. GCW: Inevitably this will lead to confrontations between you and Terror, and O'Shea. How do you plan on handling that?? DeBough: It's already been handled. And then he hung up. What looms for Terror, and O'Shea? And who really is the rightful champion? Tune in to the next World Wide to see.
