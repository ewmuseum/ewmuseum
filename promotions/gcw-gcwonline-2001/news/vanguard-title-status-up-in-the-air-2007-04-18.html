Vanguard Title Status up in the air?

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VANGUARD TITLE STATUS UP IN THE AIR? GCW News has just broken a shocking story not privy to the fans watching High Stakes live from the Casino. Shortly after the supercard was over, a package was found placed at the doorstep of President Steven Caldera's office waiting for him. The package contained the belt for the Vanguard Championship neatly folded up and placed inside. Taped to the centerpiece was a handwritten note that read as follows: Attention Mr. Caldera, Sure and I thank ye for trusting me to handle a match of this caliber. Winning the four-way Vanguard match has been me greatest achievement to date. What everyone still fails to realize, however, is that I never have been, and am still not, interested in this championship. Sure and Katie O'Clare hails from the old school of wrestling, pitting flesh against flesh, bone against bone, will against will. To fight with steel chairs and wooden tables and kendo sticks is an abomination to everything I believe in and have been trained to believe since I first entered wrestling school. Sure and a title defended under so-called Hardcore rules has nay meaning, since it takes little skill and desire, but only brute savagery, to wield weapons. Long have I been a foremost proponent of facing an opponent and defeating them with me bare hands. Code of the gladiator, blood and guts and honor. Ye have me gratitude for pushing me and me abilities for a four-way contest with wrestlers of the caliber of Peter Vetra, Rufio, and Vivica Valentine. However, a piece of leather that is defended with steel chairs and kendo sticks is of no use to me. Accordingly, I am returning it to do with as ye see fit. It interests me, and profits me, naught at all, and I am better off without it, that I can fight me next challenge with but me own body as me weapon. Flesh against flesh, bone against bone, and will against will. -Katie O'Clare, The Monarch Butterfly Repeated requests for comment by Mr. Caldera, on the letter written by Ms. O'Clare, and her apparent abandonment of the Vanguard Title, have not been answered. GCW News attempted to contact Ms. O'Clare but her agent has yet to return any calls. Updates will be provided as they are obtained, but for now, it appears that nobody is talking.
