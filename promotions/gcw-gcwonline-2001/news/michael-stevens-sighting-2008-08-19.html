Michael Stevens Sighting?

19 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's being reported today that former GCW World Champion Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens was spotted at Caldera Enterprises headquarters in Columbus. These reports are thus far unconfirmed, but Stevens has been known to appear at CEi from time to time. Of course, Stevens has been out of the company for two years ever since suffering an injury at the hands of Johnny Borealis in Januray 2006. Stevens has repeatedly stated his intent to return as soon as his recovery is complete, but both Stevens and the company have been incredibly tight-lipped about his status. While any Stevens sighting would of course spark speculation of a possible impending return, some inside sources believe that Stevens' next GCW appearance is likely to be in a non-wrestling capacity. Nobody within the company has been able to confirm that GCW is anywhere close to reaching a deal with Stevens, further strengthening the notion that Stevens won't be back in the ring any time soon. There is, however, long-standing speculation that Stevens is the next GCW wrestler in line for a Hall of Fame induction. The Hall typically adds a new name every December in correlation with NC-17, and it is possible that Stevens is in Columbus to discuss a possible induction ceremony appearance at this year's Pay-Per-View spectacular. Still, many are holding out hope that Stevens may be nearing a return. He has been extremely quiet and is not seen in public often, so it is difficult to gauge his progress. If Stevens is in Columbus, it is probable that the company will keep his presence there under wraps.
