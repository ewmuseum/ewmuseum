Protect you from Mayhem

20 January 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW Legend set to film Allstate Commercial We have seen them for over a year now, and those in the wrestling world were wondering when this would finally happen. GCW Legend Bryan Mayhem is set to air his first ever commercial, for Allstate Insurance of all companies. Dean Winters who's known for his "Mayhem" character on the commercials will finally get to meet the real Mayhem. Rumor has it the ad will air during the Super Bowl where a large group of friends are seen partying inside of a house watching the Big Game. Bryan Mayhem will than bust through the floor, and destroy the house. Yes he will be wearing his known face paint but whether or not he has any lines is yet to be seen. "He's a big man, but he's someone I enjoyed watching. I will be more than happy to let him take over the Mayhem roll, just for this commercial. We may have to fight inside of Belmont for future rolls however", quipped Winters in an interview. The internet has been buzzing about this, and for the most part it's a positive reaction, we will just have to see how well The Lunatic of Belmont does in a part he's not use to doing...a controlled atmosphere.
