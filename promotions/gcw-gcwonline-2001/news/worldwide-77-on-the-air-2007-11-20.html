WorldWide 77 On the Air

20 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WorldWide came to the nation's capital, and as forseen by President Caldera, The Establishment left a little bit bigger. And if the events that took place in the night's main event are any indication, it might be getting even better in the near future. In addition to the bizarre events at the end of the night, the US Title picture heated up, Tyson DeBough lobbied to join The Establishment, and Tessa Windsor made a stunning decision. All the action can be seen here. The show kicked off with Rich Rollins finally explaining his shocking decision to join The Establishment after years of fighting them. Rollins revealed that Jordan White's victory over him at Tsunami left a lasting impression, and now Rollins is obsessed with revenge. White accepted Rollins' challenge for NC-17, which turned out to be an invitation to the most brutal environment in wrestling: an ACWF Match! Tyson DeBough seems to be pushing hard for a chance to join The Establishment, and Caldera appears to be content to let him dream. What Caldera's true feelings are regarding the Television Champion remain to be seen, but the open spot in The Establishment didn't go to DeBough later in the night. DeBough is said to be crushed that he didn't get the invitation, but isn't about to give up hope! A game of one-upsmanship was played between Jay Terror and LeStatt Knight, with unfortunate consequences for one young ring hand. Terror had staked out the locker rooms in hopes of ambushing Knight, but Knight was two steps ahead. Terror didn't exactly hit his target, and found out that it takes a little more than just hiding in a locker room to take out the Hall of Famer. Shaman defended his United States Title against Phil Atken, but Atken wasn't alone in wanting to get his hands on the belt. Brad Jackson and Ray Holland both had the same idea, inserting themselves into the match and making their intentions known. Shaman may be facing a huge obstacle with three challengers seeking the US belt. But can any of them overcome the others long enough to get it? Later, Vivica J. Valentine defeated Clyde Fox in singles action. Fox made an impressive showing against a top-tier opponent, but his biggest letdown of the night was losing his beloved Squirtle doll... to his older brother. Banned and Exiled~! teamed up with manager Tessa Windsor to take on The Establishment team of Rollins, Knight and Octavian, and the numbers seem to finally be taking their toll. Branson King, self-proclaimed greatest tag wrestler in GCW history, joined The Establishment at the same time Tessa Windsor grew sick of the champions' antics. Windsor left and the numbers overwhelmed Storm and Stone. Now with King on board and seeking the titles, will Banned and Exiled~!'s legendary reign finally come to an end? And in the main event, Lia Ambrosi took on Kimbusa in a huge Lumberjack match... with The Establishment at ringside as the jumberjacks. With the title on the line Ambrosi fought valiantly, but the numbers and the sheer size of her opponent were too much. Ambrosi seemed to be on the brink of losing her title, but managed to escape certain doom once again. Vivica J. Valentine came in, presumably to help her friend. But the end was one of the most shocking events in recent WorldWide memory, leaving more questions than answers. Has Vivica J. Valentine gone over to the dark side? Will The Establishment add another member next week? And has the friendship between Valentine and Ambrosi been irrevocably damaged just weeks before their huge showdown at NC-17? Tune in next week to find out, as LeStatt Knight has been handed the keys to the show and will book every match himself!
