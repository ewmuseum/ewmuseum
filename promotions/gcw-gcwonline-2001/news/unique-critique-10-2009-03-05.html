GCW — Column

Unique Critique #10

5 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Author’s Introduction: Wrestlers and handlers will be rated on their all-round contribution to Global Championship Wrestling, both on-air and behind the scenes, much like the previous edition of UC. In addition to this I will attempt to outline what kind of things I expect in 2009 from each wrestler. If the critique seems a little harsher than usual and you've picked up a lower star rating than expected then please don't feel too disappointed; I hold everyone in this fed to very high standards and I consider myself to be a harsh critic anyway. Besides, I love everyone equally anyway. AXEL ACTION He came into GCW very much in the shadow of the "notorious" James von Doom and during his earlier Church of the Unholy days it seemed very much like Axel Action was taking the backseat to his louder partner. After coming out on top in that particular feud, though, Axel's looked consistantly strong and pulled off some decent victories. Matt's nice guy and an improving writer and it looks like he's enjoyed being given the chance to shine on his own. I think he'll continue GCW's recent tradition of strong Television Champions with a strong reign. On-card I think I'd like to see a little more from him though, and a big feud certainly wouldn't go amiss. LANTLAS ANDURIL N/A Far too early for me to pass comment on this guy, but I do know that he is a very good writer and I'm sure he'll prove to be a welcome addition to the GCW roster. I do have some slight reservations about an elf running around on WorldWide but the handler's definitely a strong enough writer to make it work. I'm sure he'll be a big success. LIA AMBROSI Look, I think that Lia is one of the best characters in recent GCW history. Her World Title run was nothing short of stellar and her feud with Vivica J. Valentine in 2007 was glorious, but it's no secret that she's quietly sunk into the background throughout the course of 2008. A recent spurt of activity and a half-decent feud with Face showed that there perhaps is some life left in the character but it's still a shame to see such a fantastic character taking a peripheral role. I'd love to see Lia Ambrosi alive and kicking in 2009, but unfortunately I get the impression that this could prove to be another transitional year for the former champion. CHRIS BAGWELL As much as it pains me to feed the guy's ego even further, there is no way that anyone can deny that Shane deserves anything less than the full five stars. Chris Bagwell is one of the most gloriously obnoxious heels I've seen in a long time and it's thanks to the handler's constant dedication to getting his name out there that Bagwell was 2008's Heel of the Year. He's been involved in some great feuds with the likes of Andy Murray and Vivica J. Valentine and has always been great on the radio shows. Backstage is where Shane comes into his own, though; the guy is, quite simply, a workhorse. He has contributed enough OOC work to warrant a five star rating alone, and the fact that he is one of the easiest guys in the fed to get along with/make fun of certainly helps. As a writer he still has a little bit of work to do, but I would be surprised if we didn't see the World Title around Chris Bagwell's waist in '09. DAN BLACK I put him on a level playing field with Axel in that he came into the company under the wing of another superstar (Bagwell in this case) and has done a solid job of improving himself. He gets marginally better with every roleplay and I'm sure that by the end of this year he is going to be a genuine force to contend with in the upper divisions. He doesn't get himself involved backstage very much and I think I'd like to see a little more from Dan in that department as I don't think he's integrated himself into the fibre of the fed as well as a guy like Matt has, but he is doing a very good job and should hold his head high after a good reign as TV Champion. DIGITAL MORTALITY Everyone and their dog knows that Loz is one of the best writers in the game today. He's been very good for a very long period of time and I am absolutely delighted to be able to tell people I'm in the same fed as a writer of this calibre. Digi, the character, offers something a little bit different to your standard efedder. Here's a wrestler that doesn't need to be in four segments every week to make people care about him, and by keeping a low profile Loz's creation has a genuine mystique about him. I dropped one star because I don't think we've seen the best of Digital Mortality in GCW yet and recently he seems to have dropped off the radar a little bit, but I wouldn't bet against Digi being a strong force in the WT scene by the year's end. MR. DANGER I'm not sure how to compare Mr. Danger to the others given that it doesn't seem like he's much of an active competitor these days. I was a little bamboozled by his return and I think his importance to the company could've been sold a little better during the early stages as I genuinely had no idea who he was. That said, he definitely fills a niche on the roster. Here's a guy who's time has been and gone; he's struggling to prove to the current GCW crop that he's still relevant and he's bitter at the fact that the wrestling industry has moved on in his absence. The potential for this character is enormous and I think Mr. Danger as a character is far too important to be relegated to the roll of Dan Black's lapdog. TYSON DEBOUGH I love this character, seriously. What a fantastic heel. DeBough is one of the most loathsome individuals on the roster and it's great to see a renewed burst of consistancy from Mikey. His tag team with The Fish is showing great promise and I'm sure the two of them will be tearing it up with Banned & Exiled in no time at all. I always thought that this was a great character being held back by the handler's work ethic and I'm hopefully that we'll continue to see more from DeBough over the coming months. BRIAN FISHER I haven't seen a massive amount of Fisher so far but what I've seen I've definitely liked. He's put out a couple of solid pieces of writing and the handler is doing a good job of getting involved behind the scenes. If he can continue the good work then there is no reason why DeBough and Fisher can't be massive in the tag division. CLYDE FOX Look, I've got to be honest; Clyde Fox doesn't make me laugh anymore. Maybe I'm getting increasingly grouchy in my old age but I definitely do not find the character as funny as I used to. That's not to say he isn't entertaining though. There is a good reason why Clyde Fox is the most popular character on the roster and Ross has done a fantastic job of creating an ejoyable, loveable character who is entirely unique. OOC Ross is a solid guy and a hard worker who writes good matches and is always involved in decent angles. The one concern I do have about Clyde Fox is that he's seemingly been stuck in the exact same position on the card since losing the TV Title to Zacharia Taylor, but that could all change after his victory over Tessa Windsor at NC-17. FACE Alex's development in GCW has been slow and quite often unpretty, but he's definitely beginning to make some serious progress. He's improving as a writer and adapting to the style well he's coming off a MASSIVE win over Lia Ambrosi at NC-17, so the guy has a lot of momentum at the moment. Face, the character, is great for GCW and fills up the fed's "batshit crazy" quota nicely. Another decent, ever-improving writer who can only get stronger in 2009 if he applies himself in the right way. GARBAGE BAG JOHNNY Oh boy. We all knew he was good before he joined GCW, but the guy's dominance since arriving has been second to none. Quite simply, Josh is possibly the best writer in the game today. Everything that this guy puts out is must read material; he's a fantastic roleplayer and I always get a Hunter S. Thompson vibe from his pieces. He steamrolled every opponent pitted against him up until his unfortuante loss to Taylor at NC-17 so it'll be very interesting to see where the character goes from here but I'm certain that it'll be awesome whatever he does. Really, I'm finding it very hard to pick faults in the effort Josh has put forth since joining; his writing has always been of the highest standard, he's pitched in with match-writing and he's been involved in some great angle work. Wrestler of the Year 2009 contender. BRAD JACKSON Where are you? Jackson has been in and out of GCW for the past 16 months without ever really breaking away from the pack. It's a massive shame as Lisa is a titan of a writer and Jackson is definitely one of the best heels in GCW on his day. Every time Jackson comes back he promises big things only to sink away into the shadows again with sporadic bursts of activity in-between and if applied a little better this character could be a MASSIVE force in GCW. I'd like to see a lot more from one of my favourite GCW characters in 2009. JAGI He's been kinda quiet since he got here and his partner bailing on him certainly hasn't helps, but I know that the handler is a very talented guy and hopefully he can pick up the pieces of The Aristocrats. There isn't much more to say than that. LESTATT KNIGHT There are no words I can type that will cover just how important LeStatt Knight is to GCW. He is, without question, the most successful character in GCW history and the first name everyone remembers when they think about the fed. 2008 saw him add a Dangerous Games victory and a third World Title victory to an already legendary legacy and Rob has done an excellent job of keeping a true GCW original fresh, up-to-date and relevant. He started off the year as very much another face in the Establishment's crowd and has gone from strength to strength following a series of stunning angles with the likes of Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor and Jordan White. Because Rob's a real leader too and always contributes as much behind the scenes work as his busy schedule allows him. With the exception of maybe Vivica J. Valentine no star shone brighter than LeStatt's in 2008, and I expect him to have another stellar year in 2009. KILJADEN KINCAID Why is Kincaid still on the roster? I understand the fed might have a need to have an NPC jobber/enhancement talent around, but how many times has Kincaid actually appeared on GCW television recently? He serves absolutely no purpose whatsoever. THOM LESTRANGE This is a tricky one because I genuinely feel that Thom is one of the finest writers in the fed. Jimmy has created a really rotten character here and LeStrange is one of the best heels I've seen since Angelo Deville and Sonny Silver. And what a writer, too. His segments are second to none and his work with Murray at the start of the year was great. Unfortunately, though, since then Jimmy has phased in and out of GCW and never really captured the same spark as he did with Murray. A feud with Brad Jackson fizzed out before it ever really got started and his return at Dangerous Games failed to gather any real momentum for the Englishman. Here's hoping his latest appearance isn't another false dawn as I love this character and think that if Jimmy had Shane's work-rate then Thom LeStrange could've been 2008's Heel of the Year. ANDY MURRAY The all-conquering Scotsman has had one of the most memorable debut years in recent memory. Quite simply, Murray has steam-rolled everyone and aside from finishing second to LeStatt Knight at Dangerous Games he's been almost completely unstoppable. Some of his angles have been quite hit and miss at times but his feuds with LeStrange, Knight, Bagwell and now Jay Terror have all been fantastic and the fact that the guy's still unbeaten in roleplaying competition tells you all you need to know about his stories. Murray is one of the strongest on-card presences that GCW has and backstage the guy's put a lot of hard graft in. If he can maintain his focus and work ethic over the coming 12 months then there is no reason why he can't make a genuine Wrestler of the Year push. BRYAN MAYHEM I love the character and appreciate his importance to GCW history. A member of Oblivion, Mayhem is a true titan, a former World Champion and a terrifying opponent who nobody in their right mind would want to cross paths with. His GCW history is strong and it's been good seeing him reprise his role as GCW's resident monster after a strong run as a Tag Team champion. The one thing that does concern me about Mayhem, however, is the fact that on-air he constantly seems to come across as Shaman's sidekick as opposed to his tag team partner. Oblivion promos and angles seem to involve a lot of Shaman talking and Mayhem hanging around in the background like a kid who's just been told off by his Mother, and that doesn't suit the charcter at all. I'd like to see a more-involved Bryan Mayhem in 2009, without a doubt. JACOB MCKAIL Kris similar to Loz in that everyone in this fedding circle knows him to be one of the most consistant writers in the game. Like Loz, he's been around for a long time and has always put out top quality writing, arguably without every reaching his full potential. McKail's been great since he came into GCW; his work with Jake Reed was top notch and I'm looking forward to seeing the American Dream holder making Zacharia Taylor's life a living hell over the next few months. Behind the scenes he's not the easiest guy to get hold of and he could maybe do with being a bit more visible, but his character is amazing and I know that there are big things ahead for him. NACHO GRANDE N/A Again I'll decline from commenting until I've seen more from him, but with a name like that how can he be anything but epic? RIKKI ROXX Anyone who gives Rikki Roxx any less than the full five stars is a big lame idiot. CHRIS STORM Bobby is a phenomenal handler; a genuine backstage leader, a workhorse and a man who always puts his point across regardless of how popular it may be. His feedback has been invaluable to a lot of roster members and his pointers have no doubt helped a lot of us improve our work and adapt to the GCW. He just gets GCW better than anybody else, and it would be a terrible day in GCW history if he ever were to leave. On-air, Banned & Exiled~! are rightly two of the most popular characters in the fed and have done very well to come back after a recent hiatus. They ruled the tag team roost for so long and I'm not about to bet against them having another epic reign this time around as the likes of DeBough and Fisher snap at their heels. Storm was also responsible for one of the most engrossing angles of 2008 in the Windsor/Knight/Storm love triangle, which was one of the most believable things I've ever read on an ewrestling show. MARKUS STONE The other half of Banned and Exiled~! is another GCW powerhouse. When he and Bobby are writing Stone and Storm together the result is often electric and the two of them have created a real Edge and Christian kinda vibe with B&E. His military absence(s) make it a lot harder for me to speak on Gerad than his partner but he's a popular presence behind the scenes who always chips in with his fair share of work. I stop just short of giving Gerard the full five simply because I haven't seen him as active in GCW for an extended period of time like his partner, but I'm sure that if you asked me again in a couple of months he'd definitely be hitting the full rating. SHAMAN From a backstage standpoint Andy is a guy that any fedhead would kill to have around. With Shaman you're guaranteed roleplays, segments and hard work and I don't think I've seen a man more dedicated to the cause. He does what he does and makes no apologies for it. Unfortunately I feel that sometimes his writing lets him down. Don't get me wrong, I'm a massive fan of writers sticking their neck out and doing their own thing and it is oddly refreshing to see a guy writing strictly old-school promos in the current ewrestling environment, but I find a lot of his stuff to be pretty derivative. His work-rate and dedication make him a credit to the fed, but I'm not sure he has the flexibility (at the moment) as a writer to ever really progress the Shaman character any further. JAY TERROR What can you say about Jay Terror? He's gone from rookie sensation to the figurehead of the Established and has bounced up the card following top notch feuds with DeBough, Knight and Jordan White. He was excellent as a babyface and after a rough settling-in period I think Dan's really found his voice as a heel and this character has excelled as a result. His roleplays cram so much content into so few words that it really makes me jealous; he can tell a story in 4,000 words that would take many handlers 8,000. The dude can write, basically. As a poser artist Dan's created many fantastic graphical representations of the GCW roster and rightfully picked up the Poser Artist of the Year award, and he continues to operate on-air as one of the must read GCW writers. I drop a half star because he has seemed kinda quiet over the past month or two compared to what we usually expect from Dan but he's still done a heck of a lot more than most of us. A top tier writer, without a shadow of a doubt. ZACHARIA TAYLOR Chris' development in GCW has been interesting to watch. In his first ever e-fed he's gone from writing poetic 500 word roleplays to the thousand word "War and Peace"-esque epic than finally toppled the seemingly invincible Garbage Bag Johnny at NC-17. I think he's a fantastically talented writer and he has more raw talent than just about anybody else in the fed. The thing that sets him apart from most of us is that he is a writer first and an ewrestler second (given his academic choices). Sometimes this means that his roleplays maybe aren't as well-suited to GCW as, say, Rob's, but more often than not it really sets him apart from the rest of the pack. Chris is an enthusiastic guy who's good fun to talk to and he puts a lot of time and effort into helping make GCW the all-conquering powerhouse that it is today. With a little work Zacharia Taylor could well be a genuine contender for 2009's Wrestler of the Year. VIVICA J. VALENTINE Billy is an invaluable component of GCW. He's one of the most clued-in guys backstage and is a great presence to have on the roster. He knows what works and he knows what doesn't, and as one of the most level-headed personalities on the roster he comes across as a very professional operator. Everyone knows that Billy's one of the finest writers in the game, too. His style is simple and uncomplicated yet warm and real; nobody in GCW writes a convincing, lifelike relationship as well as he does. Vivica J. Valentine rightly won 2008's Wrestler of the Year after a stellar twelve months and a near year-long title reign that had to end at the hand of GCW's summer star Jay Terror. I'm not entirely sure what the future holds for Valentine but I cannot wait to see where Billy goes from here. CJ WOODS N/A Again it's probably a little bit too early for me to comment, but Woods has already put up a couple of decent roleplays and his made himself known backstage. I'm really looking forward to seeing how his GCW career pans out. JORDAN WHITE I rate Jayce as one of the finest writers that the game has ever seen. Rollins, Deville, Kannon, Troy, Tchu... honestly, I think you could put Jayce up against any one of these guys and he'd have a winning chance. From the perspective of this hobby his roleplays are works of often Hemingway-esque brilliance and when he hits his stride he's nearly unstoppable. That's not to say his talents are restricted to roleplaying, though; he's one of this fed's best matchwriters and his work with the likes of Valentine, Terror and Knight has been nothing short of outstanding. Outside of his obvious writing talent, Jayce has created one of the most compelling characters in the fed; an enigmatic, apathetic presence at the top of the card who meshes nicely with the psychological mindfuck of LeStatt Knight and the pomp and flair of Andy Murray. He finally picked up a hard-earned World Title reign after toppling Jay Terror and I'm sure there's more gold ahead for him in 2009. TESSA WINDSOR Tessa is one of the most popular characters in GCW and another character who has successfully made the jump from face to heel with relative ease. Whether it be through her earlier antics with Banned and Exiled~! or her uneasy interchanges with LeStatt Knight, she's always been one of the finest characters on the roster and she always finds herself involved in top notch antics. Unfortunately it seems as though Tessa has been hovering the middle ground between the United States and World division for too long and I would definitely like to see that extra push from her in 2009. Tessa is one of the most obvious choices for a World Title angle on the roster if she picks things up a notch and I'd love to see a Windsor title run. Backstage Sammie seems to apply herself very well and she's another vital cog in the GCW machine.
