Sero Speaks: United!

23 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well fans your pal Sero Blake is back. I normally wait a little longer than this to get something new up and running but I got a lot of positive feedback on my last column about the state of the World championship and it’s contenders. In light of that I believe it is time to move on (by request) to the next title in GCW. This won’t be the exact same kind of article because that’d get boring. Instead, I am only giving a brief run down of the current "contender" as shown on gcwonline.net but also give a little booking advice to anyone who might be listening on what I’d do to make the whole thing work. The next title on the list is the United States title. Now let me run down what I think of the US Title in general, not just today’s version but throughout GCW’s history. There has been an assortment of great superstars that have held this title in the past. Names like Khan, The Jhub, Tristain Mayhem (before he went crazy), NBK, Toxic and Toad made up the star studded list from pre 2005 GCW. Since the grand reopening we have seen LeStatt Knight capture it first and he ran with it as far as any man could. Then we have Rich Rollins, Jonathan Borealis, Trey Tremendous, Eva Van Lorne (twice before SHE went crazy), Vivica Valentine and Jordan White. If you were to ask me the two best reigns? I’d say LeStatt Knight and Vivica Valentine but for two completely different reasons. Many people consider Valentine’s record shattering run the most impressive because of the length of time that she held the title. It is very hard for me to disagree with that logic but at the same time I also see why others claim that LeStatt’s reign, while much shorter, was far more complete. It takes major talent and skill to hold any title as long as Valentine did from February 2006 until October 2006 (when she was beaten by Brandon Youngblood, who also went crazy I hear) so I applaud her efforts. The only thing that even gives Knight backers an opening is that 2006 was seen mostly as a failure for the company, even Caldera admits as much whenever I’ve heard him speak of it. Knight on the other hand had many people more into the US Title division when he held it. I have went back and read plenty of old articles from GCW and PTC about this subject and plenty believed that Knight was the real face of the company even then ? BEFORE he won the World Title! That’s impressive right there. I also don’t have the exact numbers right here but it’s believed that Knight may have defended his title close to as many times as Vivica did in half the time because he decided to defend the title every show just to prove a point. So to be honest you can’t go wrong with either as the greatest ? and I refuse to choose. Now here is the real issue with the US title. Throughout the years it has always been just a shade under the World title in prestige, a few times even surpassing it. Now a days it has become more like the United States Cham-Zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz? Now no offense to Shaman or anything who seems to be doing an admirable job of carrying what appears to be a dead division but as we’ve seen he is also looking to move up, probably tired of getting nowhere in a once proud GCW division. The problem of course has been the lack of good competition for the title. GCW has a stacked World Title division (more stacked than it has ever been) and now a booming TV title division. Hell even the tag division is beginning to show signed of life beyond just Chris Storm and Markus Stone. Yet the US title remains floundering, trying to find it’s feet. There just doesn’t seem to be much of a middle ground in GCW right now. It’s top and bottom heavy. I don’t mean "bottom heavy" as an insult either, just the fact that everyone is so new. Check out the contenders on GCW’s website and it is fairly comical. I’ll run them down for you: Tessa Windsor Branson King Dorian Ryan Digital Mortality Andy Murray Now read over that list briefly. Let it sink in. Has it sunk in? Good. Are you excited about the possibilities? Me either! Look I know Digital Mortality was a highly sought after Free Agent after the closing of Global and he is very, very talented BUT he JUST got here! How much credibility can he possibly have with GCW fans? I’d say a guy who is just now debuting has more work to do before being put in just a high spot. Andy Murray is also a newcomer, one that has shown great promise I agree, but at the same time do we know if he will be a keeper? I’ve watched many young studs come and go in GCW (myself included, damn knee injury!) so will this one last? I hope so because he has talent, but he isn’t ready to carry such a great division. Dorian Ryan and Branson King are established but they are too on again, off again. Ryan has actually held the US title before but he has more to prove before we can put him back where he once was. King is just coming off a tag title feud ? this is like putting a guy who is battling for the TV title against the greatest GCW champion of all time within a month? What? It happened!” Let’s move on. So we come to Tessa Windsor. GCW’s resident enigma. As talented as anyone who steps in between the GCW ropes but for some reason just can’t find a way to pull it all together. I believe that she can and to be honest she’s my favorite of all the so-called contenders out there at the moment. The big question is how to fix this mess right? Well here are just a few ideas that I have in this brain of mine! First off the cluster at the top (The World Title) has to be broken. When this round robin is completed I believe you’ll see this happen. If Jordan White finishes with the #1 contendership as I predicted in my last column then I can see a couple of the people in line for the World Title maybe put down a bit. I believe this will really benefit one guy in particular, Jay Terror. I didn’t hide my feelings that I don’t believe Terror is quite ready for the grandest stage yet. The TV title is one thing but going after the big one? There’s no way a guy can comprehend the magnitude of it. When Terror fails to capture the title shot I’d immediately get him involved in the US title scene to give it some life AND to get him more battle tested. Another guy that I believe needs to move up is Chris Storm. Yes he has been all about his tag team and seems to love doing what he does BUT he is WAY past that now. I’ve seen this guy pin the best GCW has to offer and not just in tag matches. Besides could you imagine the ratings of Chris Storm vs. Tessa Windsor for the GCW United States Title? Oh yeah I’m feeling it. Then you’d still have Shaman and by the time you let some of this play out guys like Digital and Murray would have grown some in the fan’s eyes. Let me paint this picture for you alright? Say we’re marching into Warpath 2008 and you look at this line-up: WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH Best 2 out of 3 falls Vivica J Valentine ( c ) vs. Jordan White SINGLES MATCH World Title Number #1 contendership LeStatt Knight vs. Lia Ambrosi UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH “I Quit Match” Tessa Windsor ( c ) vs. Jay Terror vs. Shaman SINGLES MATCH Digital Mortality vs. Dorian Ryan TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH B&E~! ( c ) vs. Rich "The Renegade" Rollins & Kimbusa SINGLES MATCH Andy Murray vs. Thom LeStrange TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH Four Corners Match Clyde Fox ( c ) vs. Chris Bagwell vs. Docta Z vs. Depraved TAG TEAM MATCH #1 contender match Samson & Delylah vs. The Cowboys From Hell Now check this card out from top to bottom. You have a rematch of last year’s Warpath tournament finals as the Main Event. Two of the greatest GCW champions for the top contender spot for the World Title, a triple threat match for the US title with all kinds of talent an intrigue, match between Digi and Ryan to unofficially decide who could get the next shot at the US title, a tag team title match that is just calling to be done ? and a victory would also make Rich Rollins GCW’s first official Grand Slam champion! The rest of the matches aren’t spectacular BUT they would promise to be very entertaining AND on a stage this big you never know who will jump out at you and show off. Plenty could happen between now and Warpath of course, people could be injured, quit or get fired. Others could step their game up quickly and be higher on the card. For what it is though I believe this card would rock. The United States division needs a boost and I believe Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor and Jay Terror are the boost it needs my friends. And that is that, on that! Until next time ? Adios!
