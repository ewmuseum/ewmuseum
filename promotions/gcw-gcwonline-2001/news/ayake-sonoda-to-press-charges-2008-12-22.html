Ayake Sonoda to Press Charges?

22 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since Chris Bagwell's brutal beating on Ayake Sonoda this past week at Worldwide #103, reports have been flying from various sources that Ayake has met with a team of lawyers regarding this matter and are considering pressing charges against the self-proclaimed Rajah of Ratings. "Chris' actions against Ayake were completely uncalled for and extremely unprofessional," said James Bryan, who was on the receiving end of a Bagwell beatdown only a little over a month ago. "Something has to be done and soon. This isn't the first time he's done harm to a staff member and he's beginning to think he can do whatever he wants." Bryan expressed a great deal of anguish after seeing Chris Bagwell attack Ayake and noted that it isn't in their contracts to take this sort of abuse. When asked about a possible punishment, JB said that although he wouldn't want to see Bagwell lose his job over the situation -- he would like to see a heavy fine to set an example to other 'jerks' backstage. We were able to get a word from Chris Bagwell over the phone regarding the incident and here's what he had to say: "I don't feel bad for what I did to that slut. I was making a statement. Nobody had any problems when Vivica J. Valentine was messing around with my career. Nobody said a damn thing. So I took matters into my own hands and I'm going to continue doing whatever the hell it is I want until I'm given what I truly deserve." Chris also added that if we'd asked him these same questions in person, he'd have probably broke our arms. Speculation is now a-float as to whether or not Commissioner Zenith or President Caldera will grant any of Bagwell's requests (one of which would be a match at NC-17 against Vivica J. Valentine and the other being a coveted World Title Shot against Jordan White). Some say that by giving in to Bagwell, you'd merely be egging him on to continue his path of rampage. Regardless of what happens, one thing is for sure. If you're a non-wrestling staff member, keep out of Bagwell's way. He seems rather adamant about hospitalizing a number more.
