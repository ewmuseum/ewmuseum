Relative of GCW Superstar Signs Deal?

3 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Even in her short time spent on the scene in GCW, Mindy Mercer has done what she can to remove herself from the pack of newcomers that have signed with the company in recent months, stringing off a number of early wins during her tenure before a disappointing stint in the Rampage tournament. However, she isn't the only one in her family to have a competitive background. Michael Mercer, while relatively unknown to the GCW faithful, is a New York based wrestler who has made a name for himself in numerous other companies for nearly the past ten years. Most notably, he was the final World Champion and final inducted member into the Hall of Fame in the now defunct SAWF, and also shared some brief success with his cousin Mindy with time spent in Global. Last news on him had revealed he was close to signing a short term contract in Japan, but now that is rumored to be out of the question. According to several anonymous sources, the superstar has been seen back in the States and has openly spoken of no interest in returning to the far east in search of competition. While he's also been quoted saying GCW wouldn't be his first choice out of his current options, another source has a record of him saying that with the possible signing of another superstar to the growing GCW roster, that tagging along with his cousin would not be out of the realm of possibility. "I'm not going to [expletive] around when it comes to where I'm going to end up," he commented. "When I get any news I'll be as open as hooker legs about what my decision is. [Mike] Ryba might be headed to GCW so even though it's not my first choice, if he gets the contract I'm probably not going to be far behind." Ryba is another wrestler based around the east coast, having joined Mercer in several of the same companies, including SAWF where the two enjoyed a lengthy reign as tag team champions. If both competitors sign, it could very well shake up the rejuvenated tag team title picture in GCW as well, not to mention add to the lengthy singles roster.
