Newcomer Prepared to Take it Slow

30 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW Newcomer talks about his past, present and his future inside the GCW ring. North Little Rock, Arkansas – Global Championship Wrestling newcomer El Moreno is a little bit different than most young wrestlers in the business today. The 25 year old from San Diego who grew up in Tijuana, Mexico is getting his first shot at the big times, but the youngster isn’t letting it go to his head. “For some people, it takes a long time to get to the point where I’m at,” Moreno stated during a brief telephone interview. “I’m excited to have the chance in GCW and am looking forward to making my way up the ladder.” When asked if he had any aspirations for championship gold in GCW the California native answered the question with the calm and poise of a veteran of the sport. “GCW is loaded with talent, but of course I think I can succeed. The obvious goal in any competitive sport is to get to the top, to be the number one guy. I don’t have any crazy ideas about winning the World Title within the month. I’m going to take it slow, I want to face the best and I want to beat the best. I’m just happy that GCW has given me the opportunity to do that.” Growing up in Tijuana, Mexico has obviously had a profound effect on the newest member of the GCW roster. He sports a traditional lucha libre mask; an homage to where his love for wrestling began. However, El Moreno may in fact be an innovator in bringing new styles to the world of professional wrestling. He has been candid about his time spent training in the military and his martial arts expertise. “Yeah, I’ve done time in the army. When I was really young it was lucha libre only, I grew up idolizing the luchadores of Mexico. But as I grew up, I also grew to love jiu jitsu and mixed martial arts. I began to train in some of these disciplines during my time in the military, and that is definitely evident in my offense inside the ring.” There was very little video of El Moreno, whose real name is unknown. In fact we managed to find only one video on YouTube of the wrestler and it was from a cell phone camera. However, the brief footage was impressive. The newcomer is a natural striker with crisp boxing and Muay Thai offense. He is a mat wizard and a master submission expert. However, one might be somewhat surprised by his lucha influence, as his offense consisted of very few high flying manoeuvres. “What can I say?” the rookie answered. “I was never any good at flipping or doing any of that stuff. I was always more into the submission fighting than gymnastics.” Although the brief video we saw wasn’t of the highest quality, we did see one thing that stood out. Estrangula; the rookie’s finisher. The chokehold was applied with vice-like grip and took only a few seconds to choke his opponent completely unconscious. We asked the newcomer about his finisher and he was quick to agree with our initial assessment. “If you get into Estrangula, you’re not getting out.” Although there are still a lot of unknowns with the GCW newbie, there is a lot of interest in what the prospect could bring to the table. His dynamic offensive mix of Submissions, Striking and traditional wrestling will surely be a problem for anyone on the GCW roster to handle. And with El Moreno expected to be at this week’s WorldWide 138 in North Little Rock, we could be finding out sooner rather than later, what this rookie is all about.
