GCW Signs Six

26 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some of the greatest wrestlers Global Championship Wrestling has ever seen have joined the promotion in large groups. In 2005, future GCW World Champions Lia Ambrosi, Dawn McFarlane, and Vivica J. Valentine joined in a mass hiring that would shake the foundation of GCW known only as Dies Irae. Later at the end of 2007 another mass group was hired, featuring the likes of Andy Murray, Chris Bagwell, and Thom LeStrange. Today Global Championship Wrestling hires six, who hopes to follow in the footsteps of those two groups before then. Straight from the desk of Christian Zenith's office on behalf of GCW President Steven Caldera, the papers for the hirings of Brock Alyas, Friar, Uncle Truck, Junious "June Bug" Slaugter, Craig "CJ" Slaughter, and Orlando "Big O" Atkins were filed for employment. The group consists of a singles superstar who has began to make a name for himself in New Frontier Wrestling, and two tags teams who hope to bring a big impact to the recently thinning Tag Team Division. Brock Alyas is a former NFW Rookie Of The Midyear, and plans on making a big impact in Global Championship Wrestling. A veteran of the independant scene Brock has struggled to make his mark on the industry due to a few presently unknown prison terms, but has seemed to have got his life back on the right track. Blessed with power and technical skill, Alyas has the tools to make it big in the professional wrestling industry if he manages to conquer his biggest weakness... himself. Character issues have also haunted the team of Junious "June Bug" Slaugter, Craig "CJ" Slaughter, and Orlando "Big O" Atkins in the past... known collectively as a group named Affirmative Action. June Bug's mat skills, CJ's high flying antics, and Big O's raw power make Affirmative Action a well rounded team that is sure to make waves in Global Championship Wrestling. Represented by talent agent Roland Washington, the team is here to bring a bit of "civil rights" to GCW. What that means for the future one can only wonder. Another team ready to make their big time debut on WorldWide in the future is the team of Holy Hell. Getting their big break, Global Championship Wrestling will be the wrestling debut of the duo of Friar and Uncle Truck. Not much is known about this team except the reputation as always being ready for a fight. I'm sure we'll find out more in the coming weeks when these professional wrestlers make their debut on X TV.
