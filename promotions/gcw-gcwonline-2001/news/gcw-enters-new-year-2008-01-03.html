GCW Enters New Year

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2008 has begun in Global Championship Wrestling, and with it comes a wealth of new information on the company's future. NC-17 is on the air, and as predicted the show was an enormous success. Now the company is looking forward to a great new year. Kevin Jacobs here with a massive GCW update for you! I've got news on NC-17, President Caldera's state-of-the-union speech and a whole lot more! Follow the jump for the whole story. NC-17 Rocks Houston, Sets Records -As anticipated, NC-17 turned out to be the biggest event in GCW history. With a final attendance tally of 93,127 the event shattered the previous company record for fans packed into a venue. It also is rumored to have broken the pro wrestling indoor attendance record, but that hasn't been confirmed. In correlation with the attendance record, GCW smashed its one-night gate mark. The company pulled in many millions of dollars simply from live attendance, a number that is not expected to be released. Either way, the wrestlers and crew all took home tidy holiday bonuses, helping to boost the already sky-high morale in the company. -The entire Fan Fest weekend was also a big success. Turnout was great at Reliant Stadium and in the neighboring Astrodome. Nearly all the roster turned out to appear, despite it being optional. The fans had a great time meeting the stars and interacting with real members of the company. GCW is making it a point to become more hands-on with its fans, and the buzz around the company at the moment is tremendous. I had a great time during the entire weekend and I'm already looking forward to December of 2008. -President Caldera addressed the roster and crew after NC-17 before everyone headed home for the holidays. Though his on-air methods aren't the most popular, he remains highly respected as a leader. Why? Because he was generous in sharing the earnings from the event with everyone from the main event wrestlers down to the guys who set up the pyrotechnics. And because he was quite clear in his lofty goals for the company in 2008. I'm often asked why the roster simply doesn't rise up and mutiny, but this is why. Caldera might pull some strings to benefit his chosen few, but he still offers a great work environment and few are willing to give that up. - NC-17 injury report: I'm happy to report that this list is fairly thin. LeStatt Knight suffered some severely sprained ligaments in his left arm near the end of his match with Jay Terror, but thankfully nothing was torn or broken. He should be able to compete right away, though he won't be at 100% for a few weeks. Rich Rollins and Jordan White are both pretty banged up, but that comes with the territory in ACWF. Rollins suffered a severe laceration on his face and took nineteen stitches after the show. White also took some stitches and suffered a bruised rib from his jump into the truck. Still, both guys feel fortunate to have made it out in one piece and shouldn't miss any time. Brad Jackson took a particularly hard Desert Spike from Shaman in his US Title bout. He suffered a concussion and complained of neck pain afterwards. Jackson is scheduled for tests today, though doctors don't expect to find serious damage. It is unknown if Jackson will miss any shows. Beyond that, nothing more than routine bumps and bruises. GCW appears to be in very good health heading into the new year. -The backstage area remained fairly orderly. Credit Toad and his GCW Security guys for keeping things running smoothly. -GCW's newest talent wasted no time in making an impact. Andy Murray, Thom LeStrange, Mark Matthews, Chris Bagwell and Docta Z engaged in a bit of an in-ring riot, refusing to remain backstage during the biggest event of the year. It was an impressive debut for all of them and already they look to be fitting right in to the GCW system. The entire company is extremely excited to have them and are looking forward to seeing what comes next from this curious group of competitors. -And they'll get the chance to prove themselves as Rampage is next on GCW's Pay-Per-View schedule. Details of the event are expected to be released later today, but already it looks like the entire roster is going to be getting a chance to make themselves a star. Check back later for the full details. -The first WorldWide of the new year is expected to be a big one. Ostensibly the show won't look much different than the usual show, but with all the excitement happening in the company it should be momentous. -Congratulations to Twizzy on his Hall of Fame induction. This guy worked his ass off every single day for this company and its fans, and nobody was more universally loved and respected. Though President Caldera and LeStatt Knight managed to block his induction earlier in the year, Twizzy managed to achieve his dream. The Hall of Fame wasn't complete without him. Other names to watch out for possible induction this year are Michael Stevens and Toad. Lia Ambrosi's name often comes up, but with her career still at its peak it's doubtful she'll get consideration until her entire body of work can be seen. In short, she's got a chance to make her inevitable induction even more emphatic. -And we must close with congratulations to Vivica J. Valentine on finally winning the GCW World Title. She took down one of the all-time greats, and in doing so reclaimed a great friendship. Big props to both Valentine and Ambrosi for remembering why they respected each other so much and burying the hatchet. Still, the World Title stands between them and the feelings can't be completely mended already. I've no doubt they're willing to trust each other again, but you can bet Ambrosi will be wanting her title back. In addition, Valentine is looking at a group of potential challengers including LeStatt Knight, Rich Rollins, Jordan White and Jay Terror. That's a pretty intimidating list. Congratulations, Vivica J. Valentine, you're now the most hunted World Champion in history. Valentine has always been an underdog in her career, and winning the World Title might not have changed much. She's etched her name in the annals of GCW history with her win and her rise in 2007 was one of the year's most compelling storylines. But she's going to have to face off against someone out of that list, so her challenges aren't over yet. That said, Valentine has proven herself to be an incredible athlete, so perhaps it's that group that should be afraid of the champion! As for who will end up with the title shot, it is expected that the issue will be resolved in the coming weeks. President Caldera has been mum so far, but one would expect that, with their victories at NC-17, Knight and Rollins would have the upper hand. Still, Terror, White and Ambrosi are deserving and will undoubtedly get their opportunities. The World Title picture stands to be wildly competitive and entertaining in the first part of this year! -Well, everyone, that's it for the first update of the year. Check back all week as we get you ready to start 2008 with Global Championship Wrestling!
