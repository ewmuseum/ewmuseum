Pete the PG Tips Chimp To GCW?!?

20 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As crazy as it may sound, the lovable Chimp just may be appearing on GCW television in the near future! Recent word around the block is that Pete the PG Tips Chimp was being planned to be brought in to work with Jordon White over the United States title before Jordan moved on to face Lia Ambrosi for the World Title at Dangerous Games. Though many don't realize it, Pete the PG Tips Chimp is actually no stranger to GCW competition. During his famous run in GTT5, Pete face off against two ex-GCW members in Blaise "Hazard" McClancy and Hall of Fame member Rich "The Renegade" Rollins, to which Pete the PG Tips Chimp lost. With rumors of Rollins returning to the company, could Pete also be looking for revenge for his GTT lost? Rich Rollins did go on to win the tournament, and later claim the PTC Global Title. Could the Chimp be thinking "what if I had won?" At the moment, everything is speculations, as nothing has been confirmed with anyone in GCW. But word around the internet has been saying that in the next few weeks, we might very well see the most well trained monkey ever in GCW.
