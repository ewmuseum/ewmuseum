K-Wolf Aims to Shatter Dreams

3 August 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
United States champion calls out American Dream holder ahead of WorldWide 152. With Johnny Borealis having set out the path she’ll need to navigate to win the Rampage tournament by selecting her in his Fox bracket, GCW’s United States champion Karina Wolfenden took time out this week to discuss the sword which has been dangling above her head since NC-17; The American Dream of Aaron Fujita. "Pretty sure with the advancements they’ve been making in stem cells that someone can grow [Fujita] the balls to cash in his shot," she told Barry Goldstein during a promotional event for the upcoming Rampage first round proper. "If he steps up to the plate this week, once I’m done with Arbor, then I’ll give him the shot - the match - he earned. But if he thinks he can sneak down to ringside after I’ve been at it with the likes of Borealis, Kingsley or Pierce, then I’m fighting dirty and leaving Deadlights locked on until they have to call the fire department to pry me off." When asked if the threat of a recuperated Aaron Fujita ambush post-match might distract her in her Rampage efforts, the K-Wolf, true to form, wasn’t about to talk her chances down. "The night [Fujita] won his shot, I went 30 minutes with the woman who is now the world champion," she answered. "After 29 minutes and 59 seconds I still had enough in the tank to pin her. So, yeah, if I have to go 15, 20, 25 minutes to win my Rampage matches, then I can still go the few extra seconds it’ll take me to choke Fujita out." Karina Wolfenden faces Trey Arbor in the first round of the Rampage tournament at WorldWide 152, while Aaron Fujita faces "Well Known" Zip Gordon in the opposite half of the draw.
