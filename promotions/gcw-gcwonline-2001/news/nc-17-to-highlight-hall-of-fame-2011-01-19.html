NC-17 To Highlight Hall of Fame

19 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling has extended invitations to its Hall of Fame members to attend NC-17, and most have agreed- with one notable holdout. NC-17 VIII is being cast in an historical light, encased in the monumental arrival of GCW's tenth birthday. And with this year's wrestling spectacular set to take place in one of GCW's most legendary stomping grounds, Madison Square Garden, GCW is putting the event in the context of its greatest stars. Some of the biggest names in GCW history are expected to be in attendance, though the company refuses to release the names of those confirmed. That said, one major name is confirmed as a holdout. Four time World Champion LeStatt Knight. Knight, who retired last year at Shockwave, has not shown his face on GCW television since his final contest against Vivica J. Valentine. Obviously, Knight's name is the biggest and freshest in the minds of GCW's fans, and his presence at the event would lend a tremendous amount of fire to an already huge lineup. GCW officials have stated that it is unlikely that any of the Hall of Famers will be in action at the event, but have not ruled anything out. These appearances are not merely intended to look to the past, but also to cast a brighter light on a new induction. The GCW Hall of Fame will welcome a new member, according to a top-ranking GCW official who declined to be named. Two years ago GCW inducted Jason O'Neil, and since then the Hall of Fame has remained quiet. A number of names have been mentioned as potential Hall inductees, but 2010 came and went without a new wrestler being nominated. Despite his continued absence from GCW television, GCW Owner and President Steven Caldera retains final say on which wrestlers get enshrined. It is rumored, however, that Caldera has ceded some input to Garbage Bag Johnny and that the Commissioner will factor into the newest nomination. Nobody at GCW will comment on the rumor that the latest inductee has already been named and informed of their honor. Regardless of the process, everyone is in agreement that the Hall of Fame will be adding a new member at NC-17. And the current Hall of Fame will be in attendance to welcome their newest inductee. Prepare for more information about NC-17 as GCW's 10 Year Anniversary approaches.
