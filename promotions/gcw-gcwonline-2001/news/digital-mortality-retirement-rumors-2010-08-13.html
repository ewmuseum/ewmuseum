Digital Mortality Retirement Rumors

13 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors of Digital Mortality's retirement have surfaced in the wrestling world, despite the appearance of a Man in Black at WorldWide 135. The United States Title scene has been remarkably quiet since Vivica J. Valentine destroyed AXEL Action's larynx at Rampage, and a subsequent and rare win over Andy Murray probably won't have challengers lining up to face the Fearless Phenom, but Vivica was noticeably shaken when the Man in Black appeared on the MegaTron during the bout with Murray. Signs point to a return from Digital Mortality, who revealed himself as the Man in Black last time the figure graced GCW television. After all, he technically still does hold the American Dream, so it would make sense that he's back to redeem his shot at Vivica. However, after confirming rumors of his retirement, the possibility that it could be someone else has definitely been raised. Of course, as the old saying goes, "Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me." If Digital Mortality WERE to replay the Man in Black card, he'd need to throw prognosticators off his scent. It's too early to tell anything for sure after just one brief MegaTron appearance, but I have a feeling that we'll be seeing more of the Man in Black, whoever he--or she--may be.
