Caldera Speaks on Eve of Rampage

16 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With Rampage now just days away, many of the evening's more subtle storylines are coming under the microscope. While the implications of the Murray/Knight, Bagwell/White and Terror/Windsor matches are obvious, new developments have made it clear that there's more on the line that titles and pride. It may be the uncertain status of GCW President Steven Caldera that has people talking the most. With rumors circulating that Caldera has been preparing to depart the company in favor of other projects within Caldera Enterprises, it has been suggested that the pressure is on Jay Terror to step up and take on a larger leadership role. When Terror joined The Establishment early last year, it seemed that Caldera had merely snared the latest big thing. Terror made the best of Caldera's guidance and his newfound fame by defeating Vivica J. Valentine to become GCW World Champion. But Terror's tenure as Caldera's chosen leader has been far from typical. When Terror lost the GCW Title, many assumed he would be dismissed from the group like every Establishment leader before him. Instead, Caldera stepped aside and charged Terror with regrouping and bringing The Establishment back to the forefront of GCW. Terror got off to a slow start, but has ultimately helped to forge a group that now holds all but the top title in GCW. Still, the task of leading The Establishment is a demanding one, especially with Caldera nearing another possible departure. With victories at Rampage The Establishment can position itself to completely control the company, with Terror at the helm. A win over Tessa Windsor would make Terror an undeniable top contender to the GCW World Title, the only belt the group does not currently control. However, with Windsor gaining momentum, a loss would put Terror on the outside looking in, and many have speculated that Terror could once again be fighting for his career. Hearing these rumors, President Caldera uncharacteristically spoke publicly about the matter. "Jay isn't like his predecessors," Caldera said. "Jay is special. Win or lose, it won't change how I feel about Jay Terror." There is no denying that Terror and Caldera have grown close in the last year. Once bitter rivals, the two have found common ground, and those with insight into Caldera's mind suggest he sees Terror as the second coming of himself. Terror's career has certainly mirrored Caldera's, though Caldera's career was cut short by injuries. In Terror Caldera may have a chance to see his career pan out like he wanted, and in Caldera Terror has the kind of mentor Caldera had in Mr. Danger. So while Caldera insists he will stand by Terror, the stakes still seem to be high. Terror is on the brink of returning to the top tier of GCW, but has one of GCW's fastest rising stars in his way. Regardless of the outcome, Caldera maintains that Terror will remain in good favor. That is, if Caldera is telling the truth.
