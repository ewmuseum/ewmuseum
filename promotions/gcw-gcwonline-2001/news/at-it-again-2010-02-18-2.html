At it again!!

18 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Breaking news from GCW headquarters has a violent and unique twist. Breaking news from GCW headquarters has a violent and unique twist. Bryan Mayhem just a day after attacking Shaman, Shaman had taken the war right back to Bryan Mayhem. Outside a required GCW fan signing Bryan Mayhem was thrown through a plate glass window of a store front as he and Shaman began brawling once again. GCW has no comment on the violent nature of these two men. Calls to Steven Caldera have been returned unanswered. Many GCW backstage officials feel that when these two are on the road to war nobody gets in their way. Throwing in the third factor in Jorge Samuelsson Shaman's tag team partner in Mortifera things look to be very unique and interesting. Samuelsson defeated Mayhem's brother Jason O'Neall and seems unfazed by the violent natures of both Shaman and Mayhem. His specific comments were as quoted. “I have battled Shaman and will tell you he is the meanest SoB you will ever run into when he gets angry. Mayhem and Shaman know each other very well. This isn't a fight many people would want to be in. I am looking forward to it, in fact I just want to have the all out fight here.” After the interview segment Samuelsson received a cell phone call. Departing quickly he left with little to no word of the happenings. GCW Psychiatrist Justin A Plainman spoke about the weird and often confusing friendship between Shaman and Bryan Mayhem. He stated that both men have been very violent and unique in causing pain to one another. Yet they seem to escalate it every time they meet. Neither man has been known to quit or not go overboard. With NC-17 just around the corner the violence is going to get worse. With NC 17 fan fest coming fans and GCW staff are both wondering what is next. Currently the store owner for the GCW Fan appreciation showing is not pressing charges as he states it has boosted his business. He has responded by stating he wants to get permission from GCW, Shaman, and Bryan Mayhem's agents to post the likeliness of them with a GCW logo in the window when it is replaced. GCW has not responded to the remarks or reports.
