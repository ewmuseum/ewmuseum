White Leads List of Releases

3 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW has released a list of post-Rampage firings, most dramatic of which is former GCW World Champion Jordan White. While White has yet to release a statement following his inexplicable self-imposed loss to Chris Bagwell, his departure from GCW was all but ensured by his actions at Rampage. With the upper hand in his loser-leaves-GCW match, White rolled a limp Bagwell on top of himself and accepted the three count, all before leaving without a word. This is undoubtedly one of the biggest storylines affecting GCW coming out of Rampage. With White now officially gone from the company, GCW has lost one of its biggest and most popular stars. And with rumors swirling that Jay Terror is also on his way out, GCW may be in store for some major turnover at the top. Also formally released today was Lindsay Troy, who was fired at Rampage by GCW President Steven Caldera. Furious that the Establishment-aligned Banned and Exiled~! had lost the belts, Caldera kicked the visiting PRIME star off the tour and is refusing to allow her to defend the belts. Valentine will presumably be fighting solo when she puts the belts on the line this week at WorldWide 113. Darek Machida was also released from his GCW contract this week, as were a number of developmental stars. GCW stated at the start of this quarter that it would be cutting costs in response to declining attendance, which is attributed to the economic recession. Recession or not, President Caldera is said to be gearing up for even more firings this week. Even though Caldera unveiled a new, better-than-ever Establishment at Rampage, he is said to be furious following his disposal of one of his best friends in the business, Jay Terror. Tessa Windsor, the sparkplug who upset Terror and catalyzed his violent removal from the Establishment, is expected to bear the brunt of Caldera's wrath this week. But there's no telling who will be next. With The Establishment in a state of flux and the entire company on edge, the notoriously unpredictable Caldera is liable to shake things up in some unexpected ways. Whatever happens, GCW has already been shaken to its core with the loss of White. At WorldWide this week, things might just change even more.
