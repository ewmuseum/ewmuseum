WorldWide Rumor Roundup

15 June 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lineup for the final broadcast of WorldWide should be announced soon. Here's what we're hearing so far. Could the final WorldWide actually become a two-parter? That's what it sounds like from GCW Headquarters in Columbus today. President Steven Caldera has reportedly issued his X:TV staff to, under his direction, compile a list of best-of GCW moments to air this week on WorldWide. That would set up WorldWide in two weeks to be broadcast live to conclude GCW's free television offerings. The final WorldWide has been set for the same place WorldWide began-- Nationwide Arena in Columbus, Ohio. While many within GCW were pushing President Caldera to schedule it in a bigger marker, sources say Caldera has always remained appreciative to the Ohio area crowds that supported GCW's earlier offerings, and this is GCW's opportunity to say thank you. That still leaves GCW with the chance to schedule NC-17 in a larger city, with rumors suggesting New York, Los Angeles and Chicago are on the final shortlist. The cards for the final events are expected to begin taking shape soon, but among the most pressing questions is what will become of the GCW World Championship. President Caldera has stated that whomever holds the belt at GCW's conclusion will be permitted to keep it, so it only remains to be seen who will receive this high honor. It is now looking likely that new GCW World Champion Johnny Borealis will be defending against Dangerous Games winner Brad Jackson at WorldWide. There are also a high number of GCW luminaries expected to be in attendance for GCW's finale, including President Caldera himself. GCW is also reportedly seeking to fly in every member of the still-growing GCW Hall of Fame to participate in NC-17. Stay tuned to GCWOnline.net this week as the official lineups should be announced soon, along with more members of the Hall of Fame.
