Terror Dismal after Dismissal

30 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Rampage, it became quite apparent that Jay Terror had been given his last chance at showing his worth at the helm of The Establishment. Pitted against the Teensy Tiny Terror Tessa Windsor, he hoped to seal the deal and move past her to another future world title run. Tessa however, had other plans for Terror. In a heated battle, she would come up with a victory leaving Terror feeling not only dejected but utterly humiliated by the fans watching and his stablemates alike. For the first time in his career, he felt like a failure which of course didn’t happen often when you lived in the world of Jay Terror. Following the match, it seemed almost as if Steven Caldera was willing to forgive Jay for the loss and give him another chance at being a leader. Jay would not be so lucky. After a heartfelt decision and a brutal beating done by none other than FishBough, Jay would be carried out of the arena due to an injury to his ankle. This ride on the stretcher, we’ve heard, was one of the darkest ones of his GCW tenure. Says the EMTs, Jay seemed heartbroken by the betrayal and confused by his new reality. Was he replaced? Was he traded in like others before him in the Establishment? Of course, one could only see this coming. In the past, The Establishment has had its ups and downs leading to new leaders and new times. With LeStatt Knight, Jordan White came into the picture. After his defection, Jay Terror came into the picture. Knight would later leave after being pushed aside by the so called “Golden Child” leading to the new uprooting. Still, this leaves questions for Jay Terror himself. Reports have come in stating that Jay hasn’t been able to be contacted and has actually flown home to rest in peace instead of continuing touring with GCW. In fact, trainers have told us that he has missed appointments to work out his ankle since the injury. Has Jay Terror up and left GCW as a whole? Does he feel unwelcome? What is known is that he is a new man. Once a boisterous young gun running his mouth about his greatness, Jay made it clear that there was no better. Could the loss to Windsor be the final straw in his confidence? Did the Terror tower topple with the gut wrenching loss? Most believe so. The fact is, if Jay Terror is seen again most see him more down to earth and off his mountain. Instead of believing in himself, most see him falling victim to doubt and second guessing. Only time will tell what will come of Terror. What can be said however is that Steven Caldera has a place in his heart for Jay Terror, a place that Chris Bagwell and the other members of the Establishment will have trouble filling. To Steven, Jay was a glimpse at the past, one he hoped to bring back to life and share with the world when the time was right.
