GCW Planning International Tour?

15 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW may be scouting sites for its next WorldWide tour. Global Championship Wrestling officials, headed by Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny, are reportedly meeting this week to discuss plans to take the GCW tour international once again. GCW has aggressively expanded worldwide since its rebirth in 2005, and in the past five years has enjoyed highly lucrative tours of Australia, Europe and Asia. GCW even held NC-17 2007 in London, which was a major windfall for the company. While the company reportedly considered a South American tour to benefit the victims of the Chile earthquakes, it was deemed that the situation was not yet suitable for a full schedule. Instead, GCW will be sending wrestlers to Chile for appearances and will be donating heavily to relief efforts. It seems now that GCW is headed to the Pacific. The hot rumors suggest that the 2010 Rampage Tournament will be held either in Australia or Japan. This rumor has spawned from the soon-to-be-closed deal bringing Shockwave to Hawaii. Hawaii tends to be GCW's jumping off point to its Pacific neighbors, and is also a traditional landing ground for the start of the warmer months in the northern hemisphere. If the tour heads to Australia, Sydney will no doubt land the Rampage Pay-Per-View event. If Japan gets the nod, Tokyo will once again play host to a major GCW event. It has also been suggested that GCW is attempting to negotiate terms with China for a date in Beijing, which could earn record revenue. However, at this time the company is having difficulty negotiating an acceptable broadcast deal with the Communist government. Still, GCW appears set to embark on yet another exciting tour of the world, following the conclusion of its remaining continental US dates this month.
