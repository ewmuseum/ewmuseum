Best of Rich Rollins DVD In The Works

10 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a little over a year ago, the wrestling world took a tremendous blow when "The Renegade" Rich Rollins was found dead, naked, in his home. Rollins' death came as a shock to friends and foes alike, as they viewed the GCW legend to still be in spectacular shape. Earlier this morning, GCW's sub-Headquarters in Houston, Texas announced a partnership with Texas-based Sventeater Entertainment. This new alliance would allow Global Championship Wrestling to continue releasing new, interactive DVDs and other media content that have since been lost over the last several years. With the recent passing of the anniversary of Rollins' death, Ben Smaldera of Sventeater Entertainment noted that "there just really wasn't any other option. What better way to launch a new partnership between brands than to have Rich Rollins as the poster boy product." Smaldera spoke about his uncanny love for Rollins as a person and a superstar, despite some of his lows. "Rollins was just magical. Every time I saw him compete, I just had this amazing feeling rush through my body," he said. "It was bliss. A natural ecstasy." Although the official release date of the "Best of Rich Rollins" DVD hasn't been announced, we're told that more details should follow in the coming weeks of Worldwide!
