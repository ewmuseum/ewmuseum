Sero Speaks: Devil's Night

3 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW Didn’t think I’d be back here so soon but hey I can’t complain too much now can I? Especially not with the amount of money that Steven Caldera is paying me (again). For those of you who don’t know me, my name is Alejandro Fonseca BUT you can just call me Sero Blake. About a year ago I was a member of GCW in a wrestling capacity, however it was very short lived since I blew out a knee during a house show. Things looked grim for awhile but I quickly got hooked up with some Indy feds doing odds and ends such as talent relations, booking and website work. Now I’m a columnist for GCW. I can honestly say it is very good to be back. I had admired GCW for so long before I got my chance that it was deflating to have to quit so soon. When Steven Caldera came to me a few weeks ago about a job I couldn’t refuse. So now I am here preparing to do my best to entertain you all on a weekly basis. Tough task I know! But enough about me! I am sure you are all wondering exactly what I am going to bring to the table right? Of course you are! Will it be a ranking system? Maybe my own version of the “Unique Critique?” What about in depth interviews with the stars? Or how about even show reviews and predictions? The answer? All of the above AND more. I didn’t come here to be pigeonholed. I have seen far too many generic columnists out there who do the same crap week in and week out. Eventually that kind of work gets tedious and not only do the fans get bored BUT so does the writer and they are more than likely going to fall off the face of the wrestling map. I refuse to do that my friends. GCW is a proud company and a proud company needs a distinct voice, I am here to become that voice. Of course I know that GCW has Mr. Caldera leading the charge and talented stars like Lia Ambrosi, Rich Rollins, Jay Terror, and Bryan Mayhem. I am also fully aware that Kevin Jacobs does an incredible job with the backstage work he does. The wrestlers respect him because of all he has done and I do as well but to be frank, I’m here to be the best at what I do. I won’t hold back the tough questions, I will not back down from any person in that locker room, and I will not be censored. I promise you that you’ll get everything uncut and untamed. It’s in my contract! Now I know what you are probably thinking right now. That I’m too arrogant, that I have proved nothing and you can’t wait for one of the superstars to get annoyed with me and punch me in my nose. I say bring it on. If you get under their skin in such a manner you are doing your side incredibly well. I do not expect everyone to respect me right off the bat, I will earn your respect through hard hitting reporting and that’s the bottom line. Now with all of that out of the way it’s time to get into the REAL meat of my first column for GCW. It is only fitting that with Devil’s Night fast approaching I give some in depth analysis on all of the GCW related matches. So let’s get a quick rundown of the GCW side of this event: GCW CARD: Buried Alive Match Choosers of the Slain vs. Oblivion The Establishment vs. Jay Terror & Jordan White GCW Tag Team Titles Banned & Exiled~! vs. Saints of Perdition Tessa Windsor & Ray Holland vs. Tyson DeBough & Brad Jackson vs. Christian St. Pierre & Phil Atken Clyde Fox & Pegasus & Dorian Ryan vs. Branson King & The Nashvillain & Elijah Keeling INTERFED MATCHES: Vivica J. Valentine vs. Tony Gamble Kimbusa vs. The Illustrious Face Eater Peter Vetra vs. Hoyt Williams Shaman vs. Jonathan Winters Dorian Ryan vs. Nitz Donnelly Right off the bat I can tell you that there are a few matches here that I could care less about. They are here for filler and nothing more. Dorian Ryan vs. Nitz Donnelly? Who!” What? Others have an intriguing duo (Tyson DeBough and Brad Jackson) but they aren’t matched up against anyone really worth mentioning unless you count a returning Tessa Windsor who really never amounted to much in her first run. Yes she’s pretty to look at and she’s got that whole underdog thing going for her but ? what else? She tried leading the Ascension back when GCW was “under attack” from Dies Irae but talk about a massive failure. So this leaves us with the meat and potatoes of this event and I’ll discuss them each in the order of interest to me. SHAMAN vs. JONATHAN WINTERS GCW’s United States Champion taking on PRIME’s 5-Star Champion. This would appear to be one hell of a matchup wouldn’t it? Two big time names at two of the grandest wrestling institutions known to man. Champion vs. Champion! Bragging rights galore go to the victor. Time to cheer on our GCW brethren in this showdown of “awesomeness”! Then why am I more interested in seeing Winters? I’ve compared these two, watched tape, listened to people talk about each and I’ve come to only one possible conclusion. Shaman is lucky that he’s got such a huge size advantage. Look I respect Shaman for what he has done for this sport, I really do. That big bastard has been around for a very, very long time and accomplished a hell of a lot more than I ever could’ve dreamed of. Problem is his days are numbered. Guys like him and his tag team partner, Bryan Mayhem, are becoming dinosaurs ? and they are almost extinct. Being huge and strong can only take you so far before true wrestling ability takes over. Jonathon Winters is going to embarrass Shaman and prove to the world the one thing that I already suspected. Shaman is a fraud as the U.S. Champion. It’s a shame where that title has gone BUT I will not start on that (look for that column soon!). Sorry GCW, Winters outclasses our man here. GCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH I will keep this short and sweet. There are only TWO reasons why I am interested in this match. 1) Marcus Stone and Chris Storm are hilarious. 2) It may be even more comical (sad?) watching these two hold on to a dead division. PETER VETRA vs. HOYT WILLIAMS And down goes GCW again! This may seem like it’s getting one sided (2 wins to 0 tends to do that) but what can I say? I mean if you want to we can compare the credentials of these two superstars and it still won’t really add up! Hoyt is a former PRIME Universal Champion and a 5-Star Champion. Vetra’s time in GCW? Eh? All you need to know about him is that GCW hasn’t even thought enough of him to add a roster page. PRIME- 2 GCW ? 0 KIMBUSA vs. IFE Now I know I said size only gets you so far in wrestling when discussing the Shaman/Winters match right? Well in this case I’ll make an exception. IFE is all of what? 97 lbs? Give me Kimbusa with the win along with taking his victim home and making him into soup for dinner. GCW is on the board! CHOOSERS OF THE SLAIN vs. OBLIVION Size against speed. Skill against clumsy. Breasts against .. well you know where I’m going with this. The most intriguing aspect of this match is the teaming up of the current World Champion and #1 contender. Vivica and Lia have the skill in this matchup and for the most part they have owned Bryan Mayhem. If this match were in a wrestling ring and the two women weren’t getting ready to battle for the World Title at NC-17 I’d give them the victory. Problem is that the match is NOT in a ring and the title WILL come into play. It always does. (Jason Dread/LeStatt Knight anyone?) The buried alive match is the only match in which Mayhem and Shaman can really be called favorites (Belmont too) so the two big lugs win here, taking GCW’s “Main Event”. VIVICA VALENTINE vs. TONY “THE GRIN” GAMBLE Surprisingly this match is the very first match on the card but it is also what I like to call a “potential show stealer”. Pound for pound these are two of the most talented people in this entire industry and that’s saying something. In saying that though I believe Vivica is on a roll and will not be upstaged here. Now she better not make me look bad! AND FINALLY! The match that quite frankly blow them all out of the water? THE ESTABLISHMENT vs. JAY TERROR and JORDAN WHITE From a pure talent standpoint you may not be able to get better than Terror and White. Terror has been, well, a terror since arriving in GCW. The kid is improving with each match and he looks to be ready to step up into the big lights of the Main Event. Jordan White proved he is already there by giving Lia Ambrosi the fight of her GCW life. Many people I’ve talked to think that given another shot, White could topple Ambrosi ? I believe he will get the chance sooner rather than later. So you’d think I’m taking them to win right? Until you look on the other side and see that they are facing two of the most respected, feared, glamorous and accomplished wrestlers to ever step into a ring. Not just a GCW ring but a wrestling ring PERIOD. LeStatt Knight and Rich “The Renegade” Rollins are a pairing of the likes that GCW has never seen. Knight has become what many call the most dominate World Champion in GCW’s history while Rollins has become a superstar on every level possible. Then if you add in the fact that things are getting real personal between these four with Knight and Terror starting 1 and 2 at Dangerous Games (with Terror being dumped by Knight late ? then confronting him at WorldWide) and Jordan getting a super kick to the face from Rollins this past week (as revenge for his loss at Tsunami). None of this taking into account that White WAS part of the Establishment just weeks ago. Needless to say this match will be explosive. I don’t care what the card reads, this IS GCW’s Main Event. So I hope the world watches Devil’s Night this weekend to see my predictions come true. I also hope you’ve enjoyed this initial edition of Sero Speaks. Be sure to drop me feedback @ [detail withheld] as I will be answering e-mails from time to time. Adios Amigos!
