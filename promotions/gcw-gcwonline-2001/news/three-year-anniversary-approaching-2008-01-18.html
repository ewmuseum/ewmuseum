Three Year Anniversary Approaching

18 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW experienced unprecedented success in both economics and ratings in 2002. The company ran for a little over a year non-stop during that first run, and inevitably every GCW product will be compared to that era. But few can debate that GCW has topped itself. After reopening on February 9th, 2005, with the first edition of WorldWide, the company is nearing the three-year mark of non-stop operation. This tremendous achievement is to be marked by a very special edition of WorldWide. GCW's new flagship promotion has now surpassed its predecessor, Meltdown, with 81 episodes in the books. Because GCW does not have its usual supercard event scheduled before Rampage takes place on Pay-Per-View, the three-year anniversary episode of WorldWide is expected to act as a huge jumping-off point. WorldWide: Three Years of Domination will air on February 9th and will mark the first date of GCW's tour of Japan. The event will take place in Yokohama at Yokohama Arena, and tickets go on sale tomorrow. Nothing has been released with regards to matches, but it is expected that the Rampage Tournament will begin at this event, and it will also see the continuation of the exciting Round Robin competition to decide a new number one contender. The episode will air live in Japan and on X:TV, with a taped version re-airing on X:TV during its usual primetime time slot in the continental US.
