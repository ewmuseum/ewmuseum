GCW — Column

Unique Critique #1

22 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Lia Ambrosi The champ's been a bit scarce lately. Has been all over WorldWide, but hasn't roleplayed in some time. Still, she's the champ and is on top of GCW because of her workload and sheer talent. Her feud with Bryan Mayhem looks like it could be a good one, but she'll need to pick up the pace just a bit. Virgil Ambrosi Hasn't posted a roleplay in almost two months. Hasn't really started anything on the show. Not much to review. Jayce Bradley Bradley is really talented. His writing is very good, but he's been a bit abbreviated lately. He's clearly one of the most talented guys in GCW, but he still needs to pick up the pace. He might be one of the most talented, but there are lots of people in GCW that will outwork him at this pace. Had a good seg this week and has something brewing with Shaman, so if he steps it up and shows he really wants it, I could see him at the top of GCW soon. Sero Blake Who? Kyle James Still waiting for his debut. Didn't know he was booked this week, so we'll wait to see what he's got. Kyo Kushinaga Kayin's doing a good job. He had a good showing this week on WorldWide. The relevance police might come for him for his RPs, though. I think he creates a very cool vision in his writing; he's got a very clear environment painted, and his writing doesn't have many weaknesses. He just doesn't talk about wrestling very much. Hopefully he gets a feud going so we can see what he can do in that sort of environment. Draven Lox Hopefully he gets back soon. In the brief glimpses of him we've seen he's been good, but hasn't been around long enough to make an impact. Blaise McClancy RJ's got a good excuse. Bryan Mayhem Bryan has stepped up his game. He's always been around and never complains, and has quietly crept up to the top of the card. To my knowledge this will be his first World Title shot, so it should be fun to see what he and Kat come up with. However, the output hasn't been great... I gather he's been sick. Hopefully with this big win he'll start turning out the RPs again so we can see what he's got. Dawn McFarlane A no-show this week means a low ranking. James needs to get back to championship form. I hear that he might have something new coming up, so I'm sure he'll get back to work and climb right back towards the top of the card. Danielle Northsky Another no-show. Kimi gets a pass due to her personal situation, and the fact that she wasn't booked. However, Northsky as US champion hasn't done anything yet, and she needs to take the initiative and get herself on the show. Scott Nielson Nielson just needs to help us know him better. There isn't much to go on, just one roleplay. Again the relevance police show up; the RP had nothing to do with anything, and was too short to give us any clue what was going on. Looked like he had to cut it off in the middle of writing it. His writing isn't bad, but it clearly lacks direction. The coming weeks will be revealing. Katie O'Clare Alan is a terrific writer, really underrated. But he's never really been involved in anything that makes him jump out. His most recent RP was very good; again, another talented GCW writer who paints a very good picture. The interaction with the native Americans was interesting. The relevance police might take a look at this one, but Katie studying with Native Americans due to her upcoming match with the Native Americans of UNITE was actually a nice idea. Looking forward to see what Alan can do when placed in a position to create a good feud. Rufio Marq hasn't really been around. He hasn't posted many roleplays in the last few months, but is slowly getting himself established on camera. He's a good writer, but needs to get into something new. If he gets something interesting rolling, Rufio could be a good character. Chris Storm Gets four stars for writing two matches before he even debuted. Now we'll see what he does for himself. Markus Stone Stone's looked good since his debut. His debut roleplay showed that he has the talent to be in GCW. It wasn't mind-blowing, but it was good enough to show that he's got promise. He's also got an interesting team now with Chris Storm, so he's getting off to a very good start. This new prospect could be a solid part of GCW for quite a while. Shaman Andy's one of the best workers in GCW. He's transitioned from the old script style of RPing to the new novel style, and his RPs already look better. His writing, however, is still a bit stilted. He needs to work on his sentence structure a bit; perhaps try reading the RPs out loud and listen to how they sound. They just come off as a bit herky-jerky sometimes. However, he's clearly showing good promise and is moving up the rankings. Team with Mayhem looks like it'll bring about some good moments. Jimmy Thunder Thunder is another good looking prospect. He's come in and posted some good stuff; he's clearly talented enough to go far in GCW. Seems like a friendly guy, which is always a plus. Need to see more from him on a weekly basis to get a good reading, but he's got the look of a big player. Peter Vetra Adam apparently has been working very hard. He has been posting the shows, appears on the forums often to help organize things, and still finds time to do posers, write his RPs and write matches. His writing is good, though he does miss some punctuation here and there that gets distracting. Like many people, just needs to proofread! Overall, though, a vital part of GCW. Vivica J. Valentine Hasn't done much lately. Did write a match this week which helps, and was all over WorldWide. Her departure from DI should bring about a good fresh start for Billy, so hopefully he'll take that and run with it into something that will elevate him and get him off this losing streak. Jordan White White has been one of GCW's best kept secrets for a while now. If you haven't read his stuff, treat yourself and do so. He's definitely a bit... odd. But his writing is exceptional, and it's very unique. However, he needs to get something going on TV. If he gets into a good storyline that we can get behind, he could be special. Fiery Passion Well, he's clearly a good writer, but... the explicit sex thing always turns me off. Sex isn't inherently a part of wrestling, and though I could definitely imagine a scenario in which it could be an important part of a storyline, it just doesn't work here. Frankly, I don't think an e-wrestler could have deep enough insight into sex to turn it into a relevant subject. It just seems like porn to me, and that doesn't really do it for me. Still, he's been a reliable worker, so that means something. I just hope he ditches the explicit sex and gets back to wrestling.
