GCW Radio Breaks New Barriers

19 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I AM THE WOLFENATOR!" Those four words rung loud and clear as GCW Radio Episode 5 came to a close tonight when superstars from around the globe tuned in. "It was an absolutely ground-breaking event," said host Eddie Fowler after the show. "We set all sorts of listener records and had an unmatched amount of callers." In what was supposed to be a very thin, low-key show; April 19th's episode of GCW Radio set a new record with a high of 27,121 listeners on air at one time. The show stretched to new boundaries as it welcomed in superstars from rival PRIME and various other organizations around the country. In the slew of callers were normal GCW favorites like Garbage Bag Johnny, Lindsey Troy, and "Big D" Dan Black and novelties such as Jeff from Northern Ohio. We also had GCW superstar firsts when we heard from Zoey and the GCW wrecking machine, Shaman! Although we missed segments such as Clyde Fox and Bobby's Taco Stand; nobody could've predicted the pandemonium that would've been caused after getting new callers such as Norm from Teaterville, Ohio, the Wolfenator, The Terminator, or Joey H. Apparently, superstars Wolfenator and Terminator from independent organizations outside of Detroit, Michigan just happened to stumble into GCW Radio and liked what they heard. They ended up calling into the show and things picked up from there, with challenges being sent back and forth. It was Shaman that silenced the opposing organizations when challenging them to a handicapped match. But Joey H, their alleged promoter, insisted on making it a tag bout. It was then that Shaman resurrected Chris Bagwell from the dead and we heard a live call-in from Bagwell's ghost!! "If you missed the show, you have to stop what you're doing right now and go listen to it," Eddie went on to say. "Global Championship Wrestling is really ready to take itself to the next level in radio entertainment and this is the way it can be done." For full access to archived podcasts, check out the official homepage of GCW Radio by going to www.nowlive.com/gcwradio.
