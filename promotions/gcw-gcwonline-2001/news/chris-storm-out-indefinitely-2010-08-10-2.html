Chris Storm Out Indefinitely

10 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jay Terror puts record-breaking champion on medical leave. Only twenty-four hours after WorldWide went off the air, GCW is getting information from Sharp Memorial Hospital that Chris Storm has suffered a fractured orbital bone from his encounter with Jay Terror. Chris Storm, a former three time Tag Team Champion, will have to wear a protective mask, but has been medically cleared to return to action. A number of different athletes who have had similar injures have had to undergo similar measures to prevent further incident, including NBA star Hedo Turkoglu. Despite being cleared, however, Global Championship Wrestling has released a statement saying it will not clear Storm for active competition and will place him a medical leave. The move comes as a bit of a surprise, given that Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny has repeatedly refused to suspend Storm for past behavioral issues. Could this finally be the GCW front office having enough of Chris Storm and his antics? Was trying to walk out on the main event for the World Heavyweight title the final straw? While no one in GCW will comment on these speculations, one person did not seem upset by the move. “He’s one of the most selfish men on the roster. I’m glad the front office is finally doing something to discipline him.” James Bryan, play-by-play man for GCW. “I’m not saying he deserved what happened to him. What Jay Terror did went over the line. But he deserves to be punished for trying to ruin WorldWide, and for his antics backstage.” Chris Storm has yet to be reached for comment, but one can assume the mood he’ll be in when he hears about being unable to compete. If they thought he was angry before, they likely have not seen anything yet.
