Chris Storm Fined for Protest

11 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former member of Banned & Exiled~! fined maximum for protest on WorldWide. Despite no longer being a part of the notorious duo known as Banned & Exiled~!, Chris Storm still seems unable to keep himself out of trouble. The former record breaking Tag Team Champion has been fined the GCW maximum allowed, $50,000, for leaving WorldWide before final bell. Under normal circumstances an early exit does not merit such a large fine, but given the repeat offender status Storm has, GCW authorities decided to impose the largest possible fine. Chris Storm has gone on record as saying the reason he left early was out of protest, a direct shot at current Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny. Ever since Garbage Bag Johnny has taken in his new position of authority, he seems to have clashed with the anti-authority figure of Storm. The two were seen at the very beginning of WorldWide arguing over the injury that caused his wife, Tessa Windsor, to miss the show. Tessa Windsor, for the record, will not be fined for missing WorldWide, citing the absence as medical. It is unlikely this fine will do anything to help the relationship between the two and GCW officials are unlikely to suspend Storm even if he pulls another similar stunt this week. Officials have apparently already protected themselves incase Chris Storm decides not to show but not booking him for the second straight week. In fact neither Storm nor Windsor are booked for the show despite both competing at Rampage in the Double Jeopardy Cage match for the World Heavyweight title. It is unsure if either star will even appear at the One Hundred and Thirty-Second edition of WorldWide. Fellow World title contenders Jay Terror and Clyde Fox were also given the night off and are unconfirmed for WorldWide, although it does not seem to relate into the protest or fine against Chris Storm. Neither Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny nor Chris Storm were available for comment.
