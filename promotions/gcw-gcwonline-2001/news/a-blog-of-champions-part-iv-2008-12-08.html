A Blog Of Champions, Part IV

8 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, apparently, now that I won the silver plate thing, they thought it would be a good idea to do something called a weblog. I don't much know what that is, and it sounds kind of silly to me, but they were pretty adamant that I come up with some kind of idea to write down for the GCW website. I got to thinking, cause I tend to do that sometimes when I'm on drugs and there isn't any crazy shit coming after me, and I thought that a lot of people probably wonder what it would be like to hang out with a champion for a day. Now, there are too many people out there to coordinate that they all get to hang out with a champion, so I did the next best thing. I decided to find a champion and hang out with him for a day and then write down what happened. Well ain't coincidence a sneaky son of a bitch, because I knew just the champion. Myself! Of course, there were a bunch of contingencies and dilemmas and whatnot, but I had a ways around most of them. Thing was, how the hell would I hang out with myself when there's only one of me? I seen on the television before that a man would walk into a box and it'd double him up, and there'd be two of him that would walk out, only one of them was a dweeby scientist, and the other guy was still him, but he wasn't such a poindexter. The science was there, but unfortunately, I didn't have the time nor the resources to invest in finding, using or developing such a machine. I came up with the next best plan, you see, and that's often the best plan because it's the most feasible, even if there is a better plan out there. I'd buy a tape recorder and narrate my day into the recorder, and then I'd find a regular guy to carry the tape around. And every time I asked him a question, I'd have him press play on the tape player, and I'd write down the responses to my questions. It was failsafe and foolproof. I learned a lot about being champion from hanging out with the guy I tasked with pressing play on the tape recorder I had recorded all of my thoughts into the day prior. For one, I learned that being champion is about more than wearing gaudy jewels around your waste and winning fights or losing them via disqualification or count out. No, a champion has to go above and beyond to represent the company he is championing, but what would a champion like Garbage Bag Johnny do to bolster his image and by extension GCW's? That wasn't the question. The question was what COULD a champion like Garbage Bag Johnny do to fulfill his contractual obligations while expending the least amount of effort possible? The answer was a blog. But what good was a blog without an idea, and what kind of idea but a good idea could make a blog any good? What was a blog? What was an idea? These were good questions, albeit questions with no answers. For the only way to measure ideas is to test them, and blogs are like assholes. They each let waste pass through a tube bookmarked by expanding and constricting circles called sphincters. At least that is my understanding of how the internet works. Back to the matter at hand, I learned I was not only a man of great physical prowess but of rivaling mental acumen. You see, I had this idea to post a blog about what it would be like to spend a day with a champion, which was dictated into the tape machine. In it, I expounded upon the methods which I would tape myself as I was apparently doing at the time, and I was amazed as I continued to divulge my thought processes. I would then hire a man to play the tape in response to questions I would later ask him as he surrogated as myself. He was a fine substitute, and I made sure to have him dress as was the fashion indicated by the man on the tape: Bearded, ravaged and consumed with the inescapable duty that packaged itself with championship.
