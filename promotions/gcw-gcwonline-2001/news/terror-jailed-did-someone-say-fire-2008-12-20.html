Terror Jailed: Did someone say Fire?

20 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Oklahoma City police have apprehended and jailed young Establishment leader Jay Terror after allegedly setting a woman on fire with a lit cigarette. Little is known about the actual truth to the story but what can be surmised is that Jay Terror will be spending time in the county jail mainly due to his past record and the severity of the crime. "He lit another individual on fire for reasons unknown to us. For all we know, he could have intentions to hunt her down and kill her," said police Chief Jack Hannigan. "He will be held until we are able to sort out the details and after that, bail will be placed." The victim, a young local news reporter, had been in Terror's room for the evening before he turned violent. "He was a perfect gentleman for most of the evening. At the mention of his ex-girlfriend though he went crazy and tried to kill me." Many wonder if the burning of his home from last month has to do with the recent crime. Others speculate that Andy Murray is finally getting to Terror and driving him to a place that he hasn't been since dealing with Jordan White a little while back. Either way, Jay Terror is expected to be at Worldwide this week where we will hopefully know more about the circumstances surrounding his recent arrest. Neither Terror nor Steven Caldera had any word on the situation.
