Bagwell Confirmed Out For PPV

14 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW has announced that superstar Chris Bagwell will not be participating in next week's Dangerous Games event. Chris Bagwell, whose star seemed to rise at Warpath despite coming up short against rival Andy Murray, is a surprise scratch from Dangerous Games 2009. Though Bagwell had not been seen the past two weeks, it was assumed that he would be present to fight for another opportunity at the GCW World Title. But a press release from GCW today made it clear that Bagwell would not be at the event, and that the announcement was not simply a misdirection ploy. The company has given no reason behind Bagwell's status, but speculation has already begun that this is punishment from President Caldera for losing to Murray. However, Caldera is usually quick to publicly abandon his former Establishment leaders, and doing so privately would be out of character for the President of the company. Moreover, Bagwell's own silence is uncharacteristic of a wrestler who has made his career by demanding the spotlight. Dangerous Games represents a tremendous opportunity for each wrestler in the company, and Bagwell would no doubt have been a favorite to win the match this year. He performed well last year, finishing in the Final Four, and no doubt would have come close again. With such a huge opportunity passing him by, it is difficult to imagine anything but serious injury keeping him out of action. There is a third possibility. Bagwell's pride may have been irreparably damaged by the loss to Murray. Bagwell's rivalry with Murray is well documented, as the two have followed similar career paths to reach their current heights. Yet Murray has been consistently out in front of Bagwell, and this latest setback may have sent Bagwell back to the drawing board. Still, speculation will continue until Bagwell or GCW releases some facts. For now, Bagwell's status will remain a mystery, but it is clear that he will not be among the 30 participants in next week's Dangerous Games match.
