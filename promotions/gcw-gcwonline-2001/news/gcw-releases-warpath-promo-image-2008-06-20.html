GCW Releases Warpath Promo Image

20 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
COLUMBUS, OH - Global Championship Wrestling released the official promotional image for Warpath 2008 this afternoon. The image features the three combatants to compete in the main event for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship: Champion Vivica J. Valentine, Jordan White, and Jay Terror. Terror, who won the number one contendership by beating Jordan White at Rampage, has finally received his shot. But he's going to have to share the spotlight once again. Terror has been so overcome with jealousy that he has set out to destroy his former friend White. Terror cost White the title at High Stakes when it appeared White had the match won, and inflicted further damage by sending the 500 pounds Kimbusa to attack White this week on WorldWide. The plan seems to have backfired, however, as the Champion has inserted White back into the title scene, adding him to the Warpath main event. The result is one of the most anticipated matches in GCW history. Valentine, whose title reign today surpasses the hallowed mark of LeStatt Knight's 2005 streak, will defend against two of the top stars in the world. White and Terror will no doubt be anxious to step back into the ring with one another, but will have to remember to stay focused on the champion to come home with the ultimate prize. Warpath will take place in Toronto this year, the start of a brief Canadian tour for the company. The event will be broadcast on Pay-Per-View for $49.95.
