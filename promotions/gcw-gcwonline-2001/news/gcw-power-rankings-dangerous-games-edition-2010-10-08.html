GCW Power Rankings: Dangerous Games Edition

8 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
How do the GCW Superstars stack up against each other in the lead up to one of GCW's most popular events, Dangerous Games? In the spirit of every sports site doing power rankings for Football season, here is a top 10 of GCW Superstars in the lead up to Dangerous Games. I have no set criteria, just my thoughts and my observations while checking out all of the editions of World Wide. If you don’t agree? Tough shit, go make your own list! If you do agree? You’re awesome. Let’s dive right in! JUST MISSING THE CUT: Bryan Mayhem -Losing streak not helping, looking forward to seeing him go back after his Hardcore Title Chris Bagwell -Blew a chance to get his hands on GCW gold again, lost to a rookie Donny Diamond -Has not been on the winning side much lately, could get a huge boost by toppling Shaman in the coming weeks Johnny Borealis -Fresh face, couple big tag wins but he has not done much individually yet. Could move up the charts with a bullet very soon. THE GCW POWER RANKINGS 10. SHAMAN The Big Man is one of the hardest working and intimidating people in GCW. Sometimes he seems to get overlooked but he continues to simply chug along and keep working. He is shining right now while being involved in a hellacious, blood feud with Donny Diamond. He also has a couple of nice wins in a row after beating Chris Storm at World Wide 133 and then two consecutive wins over David Spencer. I look forward to a huge blow out to finish off this battle with Diamond and as usual, look for Shaman to make his presence felt in the Dangerous Games battle royal. 9. TEMPEST The former Television Champion has had a rough go of it in the last few weeks. She has lost her title, failed in getting it back, had a mini anger control issue and most recently gotten pummeled by our United States Champion, Vivica Valentine. Even she has admitted that things are not going swimmingly for her. I believe she took steps in the right direction at World Wide 138 however and I believe that she will build upon it. She may be biting off more than she can chew with Rasa, Zenith and now Valentine but if anyone is up to the task, it is Tempest. I don’t see her as a favorite for Dangerous Games but I do see her making a little noise. 8. CLYDE FOX Sadly, his campaigning days are over now that Andy Murray has put a stop to it this week. It should end up being the best thing for everyone’s favorite hero. Clyde can now focus on what is truly important, his own Dangerous Games exploits. The kid can do damage at the event and just watch him tossing out LeStatt Knight last year for evidence. Clyde has not been as active on World Wide as he normally is but as always, when he does show up he brings life and energy to the building. 7. DOMINIC WALL Although an early bump in the road had some doubters creeping in early (losing to Rediker in his debut), he has done plenty to erase any doubt since. He has beaten Donny Diamond and then went on to team up with Johnny Borealis to topple GCW vets like Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor, Bryan Mayhem and Chris Bagwell, even getting the pin fall in both of the tag matches. Of course, the World saw him stop the runaway train that has been Triumph Frost and become the GCW Television Champion in the process. He is quickly gaining a head of steam and his confidence has to be sky rocketing now. Don’t be surprised if he is higher on this list come Dangerous Games. He could already be considered a very Dark Horse in that race. 6. TESSA WINDSOR Another young woman who isn’t having the best go of things right now. She and her husband fell victim to Dominic Wall and Johnny Borealis just days ago and, more importantly, she lost the World Title to Jay Terror. That said, she WAS the Champ and that carries some weight in my eyes. It will only take one big win for her to shoot back up to the top and I get the feeling that she may get another crack at Terror very soon (Like maybe Dangerous Games soon?) and will have a great opportunity to redeem herself and possibly hit the top of this list. 5. ANDY MURRAY The Scottish King of cool hit a bit of a cold spell recently, losing to Vivica Valentine being the icing on that cake, before toppling Tim Shipley at High Stakes in a physically taxing match. Coming out of High Stakes I thought his momentum would propel him to the top of GCW yet again but injuries suffered during that epic match with Shipley has forced the longest reigning World Champion in GCW’s history out of the ring and on the sidelines. The people still love him, he still has all of the talent in the World and he REALLY wants to make a statement at Dangerous Games. Watch out for The Murrman ladies and gents, he is for real. 4. VIVICA J. VALENTINE She just cannot stay out of the sights of Men In Black can she? At least this time she seems to have some reinforcements on her side. Teaming up with Zenith and Rasa makes for a very interesting dynamic. I don’t think anyone involved trusts the other that much and the likelihood of it exploding is probably in the 99% range. Still, these are three dangerous characters. Vivica has continued to make an impact on GCW television although she has not been in a GCW ring, taking care of GCW business, since World Wide 135 when she got a huge win over her former lover, Andy Murray. She might be higher if she had defended her United States Title since Rampage. Perhaps Tempest can light a fire under the U.S. Champ? 3. RASA Rasa, to put it bluntly, has rocked. In one on one encounters, he/she/it has destroyed almost all challengers. The one loss it does have, it made up for by toppling Tempest a couple of weeks ago. Rasa has turned into a hitman of sorts for Christian Zenith and that will continue to only equal bad things for the rest of GCW. Rasa’s in-ring talents are undeniable and I could see them taking it to a Dangerous Games victory and beyond … like a World Title win at NC-17. 2. JAY TERROR He’s the Champ. Period. Terror also came out and made quite the proclamation at World Wide 138, promising to wield his power as World Champion unlike anyone before him. Knowing who his mentor is, I believe we would all be foolish to doubt him. Terror has beaten a who’s who list of opponents while in GCW. He won Dangerous Games last year in his return match, showing no ring rust at all. Now he has conquered a person, in Tessa Windsor, that had previously had his number. The Terror Era could be epic … but let’s see him defend that title once or twice before really crowning him. 1. TRIUMPH FROST An upset special maybe? Perhaps to the casual fan but not to anyone who has been watching the same shows as I have. Yes, Frost just dropped his Television Title to GCW rookie, Dominic Wall but he has still been on fire. The man doesn’t lose often and has taken on all challengers while holding onto the TV and Hardcore Championships. He also is a very brash and loud character that loves to get under people’s skin. There probably isn’t one person in the locker room that he has not pissed off yet. For my money, he is the favorite to win Dangerous Games and as of now I don’t believe it is close. Three weeks can be a life time in GCW however and anything can happen.
