McKail to be at World Wide?

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to reports emanating from the office of President Caldera, it is rumoured that Jacob McKail and Global Championship Wrestling have in fact come to a verbal agreement and it is said that the former GCW Television Champion may even be in Portland, Oregon this week in order to attend GCW World Wide 88. Whether McKail will have some involvement in this event is yet to be seen, but it is widely thought that he will soon sign to a permanent contract for an initial one year period. Stay tuned for more news as it comes.
