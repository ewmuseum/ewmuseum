GCW Announces Rampage, Japan Tour

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With NC-17 in the books, GCW is looking ahead to its next Pay-Per-View offering, Rampage. The highly anticipated event was canceled in 2007, and to make up for it the company in planning a big event for 2008. Rampage will be held in the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan, which GCW has announced will be the conclusion of a tour of Japan to begin late this month. Also of note is the announcement of this year's Rampage Tournament. The first tournament was won by Jonny Montano, last defeating Michael Stevens. The second was won by Dawn McFarlane, last defeating Lia Ambrosi. This year's tournament will not offer an opportunity at the World Title, merely tremendous bragging rights for the winner. The tournament is expected to begin in the next two weeks, with the company rumored to be preparing brackets to reveal this week on WorldWide. GCW has not released specifics on the Japanese tour, but has revealed that it will present four shows from overseas, including Rampage. The three shows preceding the Pay-Per-View event will be held in Yokohama, Osaka and Nagoya. The company previously toured Japan in 2002 and met with tremendous financial success. GCW remains highly popular in Japan and the company hopes to reinforce that popularity with its visit. GCW will produce a few more domestic dates before heading overseas, depending on the final travel schedule and the final date for Rampage.
