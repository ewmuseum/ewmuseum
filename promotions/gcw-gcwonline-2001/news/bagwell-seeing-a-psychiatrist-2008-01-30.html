Bagwell Seeing a Psychiatrist?

30 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For those that are familiar with the new GCW star, Chris Bagwell, it would come as no surprise to learn that there have been recent reports that the controversial superstar would be seen exiting a psychiatric office in southern New Jersey. Chris came to GCW and immediately spilled his numerous chauvinistic thoughts on the former World Champion, Lia Ambrosi, as well as the current champion, Vivica J. Valentine. His comments weren't taken lightly and he quickly came under criticism by a number of his peers. So are the allegations of Bagwell's psychiatric visits legitimate? According to a source close to Chris, "He [Chris] has had a lot of very dramatizing events go down in his life that he's often dealt with on his own. It would only be natural for him to finally have collapsed and went for medical help. It would be smart of him." When asked what to expect from Chris in his upcoming World Title bout, the source responded, "If I know anything of Chris Bagwell's past and how he reacts when he's dealing with stems of his past -- I'd hate to be in Vivica Valentine's shoes. You can certainly expect to see him make an impact unlike any he's made to date." Its no secret that Bagwell has suffered two close losses in his debut match-ups, but he's obviously pressing the right buttons somewhere. He recently accepted a match against LeStatt Knight and now is going on to square off in a World Championship Title match. Could we potentially see the same side of Chris Bagwell that led him to win a WrestleGod Tournament and earn a spot into the semi-finals of both a Diamond in the Rough and Golden Turnbuckle Tournaments? We very well may.
