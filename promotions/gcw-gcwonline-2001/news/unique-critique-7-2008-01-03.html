Unique Critique #7

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class Author's Introduction: No, it's not anonymous this week. This is your immutable fedhead writing, and this time I'm rating everyone based on their entire 2007 body of work. True, some came in late in the year, so they might garner higher ratings simply based on a handful of appearances. So each rating should be taken on its own and not compared to others with different scales of time. Only current roster members are being rated. Lia Ambrosi Four stars for Kat after she was World Champion nearly all year? Yeah, that's how I feel. Kat was great this year, but not transcendent. She's absolutely deserving of the accolades she'll get from 12 months of main eventing. That's damn near impossible to do. Most of the time she did an amazing job with it. However, there were times I felt Kat did not do much with the World Title, and for a time that hurt its stature. So Kat gets four stars because she was amazing most of the year, and closed out the year in tremendous fashion, with a few holes in between. Phil Atken Two and a half is about right for Colin. There's very little to go on here because Colin's been in and out. Still, I like the character and the handler, so I'm hoping we get to see more of him in 2008. Chris Bagwell This same post will appear for all the new guys. I haven't had the chance to get to know them very well yet, but I'm already very impressed. Without any prompting or guidance they organized and executed a match for NC-17. That's amazing stuff from people unfamiliar with their surroundings. If these guys stick they're going to make an immediate impact and I'm excited to throw them into the fire. Tyson DeBough A Tale of Two Tysons. Mike was amazing for his feud with Terror, then kinda dropped off the face of the earth after winning the TV Title. He gets three stars for participating in one of the best feuds of the year, but loses points for not following it up. If the good Mike shows up in 2008, watch out. Here's hoping he does. Clyde Fox I really like this character and this concept. Three stars reflect the fact that Fox has been a great, consistent part of GCW shows and has shrugged off initial mockery to become quite entertaining. Now we need to see Fox involved in a storyline with another member to move up. Still, great addition to GCW. Brad Jackson Lisa has been a tremendous writer and was, for a time, looking like a surefire main eventer. But at the end of the year she vanished. If Lisa makes a quick comeback and works hard she'll be up there. If not, she'll be gone. I hope for the former. Branson King He really came in and threw himself into GCW. Until his unfortunate forced departure, he had become one of Bobby's only legit tag opponents. The writing wasn't mind-blowing but it was good. I hope all is well with him and he'll make a great member when he returns. LeStatt Knight Rob really recaptured his form in the latter part of 2007. He had some blips at the start of the year that were out of his control, but when he finally got back in full force he was awesome. You're seeing the real LeStatt Knight here. He's one of the hardest workers I've ever seen and he's really creative and easy to work with. His work with Dan was great and he'll be at the top of the fed as long as he wants to be. If these guys stick they're going to make an immediate impact and I'm excited to throw them into the fire. Octavian Half a star is being generous for one RP. John is a GCW legend but didn't follow through with his comeback. He's got one more chance, let's hope he takes advantage. Dorian Ryan JJ is a good member who hasn't distinguished himself. He's always in contact, is reliable and wants to do well. He'll always be given the chance to do so. But he seems to lack focus and drive and needs to get those things to take the next step. Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins This is a bit distorted since Andrew was only REALLY here for about two weeks this year. The rest of Rollins' stuff was all me. But he came back with little rust and did some good work. He's been reliable and enthusiastic, and also routinely aids me in kicking ass in Halo. If Andrew's heart is in GCW in 2008 you'll see him be a real, true part of GCW's main event. He might even get that elusive third World Title. Rikki Roxx I concur with the previous columnist. Roxx rules you all. Chris Storm Nothing less than five stars for Bobby. He's contributed to every show we had since he arrived. I can't think of one he took off. He routinely steals the show with his wildly entertaining segments, writes great matches and runs amazing storylines with a minimum of help. He's been a steadying force backstage and frontstage and nobody is more passionate about what they do. We're all very fortunate to have Banned and Exiled~! in GCW. Shaman Andy is extremely loyal and an exceptionally nice guy. He works hard and routinely writes matches. His writing hasn't progressed as I'd like; I hope he focuses on improving a bit more this year. And as the US Champion a second time he's been a bust. Still, that's not really his fault; champions are inevitably compared to Bobby in terms of carrying a division, and that's not really fair. Andy's had little help and will be given the chance to show what he can really do with a storyline in 2008. I suspect you'll see a better Shaman at the top of the year with more to do. 'The Outlaw' Jay Terror Here's another five-star guy. Dan has been one of the absolute best backstage people in GCW since day one. As a fedhead you cannot possibly undervalue such positive personalities, because they positively effect the rest of your roster. Dan's easy going, great to work with, wildly creative and a tremendous writer. The crowded World Title scene makes it tough to reward him adequately, so I hope he'll settle for my highest praise, because he was reliable and participated in some of our best storylines this year. Vivica J. Valentine How can you give Billy anything less than five stars? A lesser man would have quit GCW at the end of 2006. He was mired in a terrible losing streak and really had nothing going for him. He had awful luck with bad feud partners that didn't show up and had gone from amazing US Champion to jobber. But what did Billy do? He used the losing streak as motivation for himself and his character. He worked even harder and didn't let his lack of an opponent deter him. He busted his ass and clawed his way right back to the top. He damn near won Warpath and clearly won Dangerous Games. And now as World Champion he'll make an incredible leader for the fed at the start of the year. He's got a tall order with such amazing talent chasing him, but of all of them it's Billy who clearly deserves to be at the top of the mountain. Jordan White Jayce might be the best writer I've ever seen in this game. You give me the name of an all-time e-wrestling great and I'll gladly throw Jayce at them. Unfortunately, for a time, Jayce wasn't much of an on-card presence. He posted an amazing RP for Dangerous Games but didn't come away with the title, a fact that he could have used as an excuse to mail it in. But to his great credit he used it to drive him, and as a result he's now one of our best all-around members. His RPs still lack a sense of mass appeal (though I'm not exactly suggesting he change it, since mass appeal is overrated), but he's transformed himself from great writer to great fedder. Andrew might have edged him at NC-17, but everyone who has ever put their fingers to a keyboard should fear the crap out of this guy. Tessa Windsor Sammie's an enigma to me. She's extremely talented, she's always around helping out, she's always on the shows... yet somehow this hasn't translated to success. She took some time off here and there which interrupted any momentum she might have gained, so that's part of it. She was also quite committed to B&E~! for a while. But now, on her own, Tessa should be a top star in GCW. If Sammie gets a bit more selfish and focuses on her own success, which she should, we'll see Tessa become a much bigger part of what we're doing, and that's a good thing.
