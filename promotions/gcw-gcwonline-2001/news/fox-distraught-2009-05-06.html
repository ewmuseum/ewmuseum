Fox Distraught

6 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Everyone's fun, lovable, candy obsessed, friendly neighborhood LAN Party Hostess Clyde Fox has made his feelings public about the passing of one time friend, Chris Bagwell. When he heard the news, Fox was immediately overcome with emotion. So much that he was unavailable for comment on the situation. Fox finally got back in front of the camera to send a tribute to his pal the following WorldWide. It didn't help as it sent Fox back into his noted depressive state. This carried over to WorldWide 110 where Fox had slipped further into despair. Little did he know, but Chris was actually very much alive. Playing a huge trick on everyone, Bagwell made face time later that night. Word caught like wildfire as the very foundation of GCW was rocked when Bagwell returned. The word reached everyone... or we thought everyone. Still in denial, Fox blames himself for Bagwell's "death." So much, that he held a press conference early Wednesday morning outside of his home. "I am just still so sad about woosing my friend," He spoke in a frail, nervous voice, "I thank you all for coming and making this a very special play date." Locked away in his room, Fox did nothing but write poetry and catch up on Warcraft during his reflective time. He also was adamant about pointing out that he made the little sandwiches for the press conference himself. And that he also moved up from level 25 to 28 with his Warrior Monk thingy. When told at the conference that Bagwell isn't really dead, Fox reply was that of a delusional person... "No... no he isn't my friend," Fox said in a loving voice, "He's very much alive. Alive in all our hearts. Making sweet gum drops made of sour lime. You know... the kind that are coated with sugar. I know somewhere... wherever Bagsy is... he's smiling right now... looking down at us like his children spawned from his naughty place." It was a sad thing to see such a happy person be brought down so much by his own blindness. The sandwiches were good though.
