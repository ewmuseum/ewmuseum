Jacob McKail Negotiating With GCW?

16 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the sighting of Jay Phoenix at Caldera Enterprises headquarters in Columbus, speculation began to rise that President Caldera was once again in the hunt for new talent. Today, the rumor mill is heating up again as former GCW star Jacob McKail was spotted in Seattle, the sight of this week's WorldWide broadcast. McKail has not been near Key Arena, where GCW officials have already set up shop to begin the early preparations for the show, but he did disappear into the hotel where President Caldera is believed to be staying. Caldera always travels under an alias and is difficult to track down, but some are speculating that Caldera and McKail met this afternoon to discuss potential contract terms. McKail left the company under a dark cloud in 2006, jumping ship to rival promotion FUSE. However, he has been somewhat under the radar lately, appearing only to capture the Infinite Gauntlet in PTC competition. McKail recently expressed his desire to return to one of the big three promotions, and some say that Caldera promptly buried the past rather than let the competition take a crack at McKail. With Jacob McKail and Jay Phoenix both meeting with Caldera in recent weeks, it is clear that Caldera is opening the purse strings once again to sign new talent. With the wild success of Rampage, which did record numbers, GCW is flush with money and Caldera has no intentions of hording it. Stay tuned for more on this story. Even if McKail is signed by tomorrow it is unlikely that he will appear at WorldWide, but anything is possible.
