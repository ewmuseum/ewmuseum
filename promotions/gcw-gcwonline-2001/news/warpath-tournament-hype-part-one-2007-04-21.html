Warpath Tournament Hype Part ONE

21 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kevin Jacobs interviews numerous GCW superstars on a few hot topics! Hello GCW! Kevin Jacobs here to give you some more entertaining reading material. I set out to get word from some of the GCW superstars that are involved in the Warpath Tournament. Needless to say each individual had some interesting things to say about other competitors, their own chances of winning as well as some of the hot topics going on in GCW. We attempted to get ahold of Jordan White but could only come in contact with his manager, Sage Black. SAGE BLACK KEVIN JACOBS: What do you think your prot’g??s chances are of advancing in round one? SAGE BLACK: Hah, you think I'd waste money on a cat that couldn't make it past a round one? Nothin' on Draven, I mean, but this Jordan kid's really not bad. KEVIN JACOBS: It has been brought to my attention that You, Mr. Black have known your new stablemate LeStatt Knight for quite sometime ... did that bond have anything to do with your joining the Establishment? SAGE BLACK: I, uh. Wouldn't really call it a bond. It's somethin' like respect, y'know? The situation's like, I'm in a position where I'm trying to market Jordan White as a brand name basically, and the Establishment was a chance to put him on that higher level with someone like Knight. So yeah, you could say it had an influence. I mean, better Knight than some of these other chumps. KEVIN JACOBS: If Jordan and Knight end up meeting in the finals of this tournament, who do you see walking away the victor? SAGE BLACK: Hah, already trying to establish some discontent with the brand? If it comes to that, and I honestly it looks a little inevitable, I think it'd be odd. Knight's a sick competitor, but I've seen people that I didn't expect just blow him out of the water. Maybe Jordan's a Trey Tremendous, you know? And there's no real way of knowing 'til it happens. I really have to pick? Shit. Man, let's say White even if I'm not totally buyin' it myself. Solidarity, yeah. KEVIN JACOBS: Is there anyone in this tournament besides Jordan that you really think is going to be a major roadblock? SAGE BLACK: Off the top of my head, Knight's always gonna be a serious issue in a situation like this, and Shaman's one of those big people that just aren't very easy to beat regardless of how skilled you are, so he's there. Very literal on roadblock, let's hope we just get to dip around him. I've gotta say I see Jayce Bradley surprising a few people, and if he gets momentum behind him, he's gonna be hell on that other bracket I don't have to worry 'bout for awhile. Not sure I'm supposed to say this, but Twizz is gonna be crucial. He's gonna be tough as shit to get by for everyone he's up against. So yeah, it's not so much the easy tournament it could've been a couple months back. KEVIN JACOBS: What words of wisdom would you give to the people who will run into the Establishment during this tournament? SAGE BLACK: Wisdom? You're putting stress on me now. Huh. How about, bet against yourself? It seems to be the trend these days, and it's definitely a good way to make some quick cash. KEVIN JACOBS: The floor is now yours, thanks for your time. SAGE BLACK: I. I didn't have anything planned. Have fun out there, all you crazy kids. Yeah, I'm still working on my Casey Kasem voice. It's gettin' there, I think. Take it easy, Kev and America. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - SHAMAN KEVIN JACOBS: First of all Shaman, good luck in the tournament. What do you think your chances are of advancing in round one? SHAMAN: Chances are for those who make them. Myself i will do what is necessary. KEVIN JACOBS: What will it mean to you to take this tournament and have an opportunity to go against your stablemate, Bryan Mayhem? SHAMAN: Bryan and i have an understanding. He and i area team regardless of what happenes. He and i like to fight and our past ios obvious. If it comes down to he and i then it will be done. Does not effect our future plans for Oblivion. KEVIN JACOBS: Of all the very talented individuals in this tournament whom do you feel has the best shot to make the finals? SHAMAN: Anyone and everyone does KEVIN JACOBS: So there's no one person who catches your eye as the most difficult to overcome? SHAMAN: Nope everyone is a step to the final plateau. If you want to win you will work even harder at it. KEVIN JACOBS: Well let's do a little bit of an overused device here. I am going to give you names of a few of your fellow tournament competitors and you give me a short tidbit on what you think about them, good? SHAMAN: It is business. KEVIN JACOBS: Vivica Valentine SHAMAN: Potential, but squandering away on lesser trivial issues. Has only to prove to herself nobody else. KEVIN JACOBS: Jordan White SHAMAN: Don't know much about the guy other than him being US champion and the newest member of the Establishment. KEVIN JACOBS: Jayce Bradley SHAMAN: Someone who got in my way and is still trying to get in my way. One word and thats "pain" KEVIN JACOBS: Kyle James SHAMAN: Tutelage been teaching him the ins and outs for awhile now. Should be coming along on his own now. KEVIN JACOBS: And one more, LeStatt Knight. SHAMAN: The past should have stayed retired, but got lured out by fame and fortune. His reappearance doesn't change anything. KEVIN JACOBS: Well let's switch gears for a moment. Bryan Mayhem just won his first GCW World Title after many years of service to this federation, how has he felt after accomplishing such a feat? SHAMAN: It is his due plain and simple. KEVIN JACOBS: Alright I am going to give you the floor here, any last words you'd like to say? SHAMAN: No words. I will let my actions in the ring speak for themselves. Stayed tuned and wait for it. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - JIMMY THUNDER KEVIN JACOBS: What do you think of of your first round opponent’s antics? JIMMY THUNDER: In all my years in this sport I've had every antic thrown my way. Each time I've come up with the perfect response. With the help of my friends at The Dollar Store in Boise they have given me something that will help me out the next time this Neville Nobody wants to pull the same stunt again. KEVIN JACOBS: Is there anyone in the tournament that you feel you should maybe pay more attention to? JIMMY THUNDER: Oh yeah definitely, when I look at my half of the draw, after I beat this Hyrdo character then I could potential collide head on with Jordan White. If that's the case, you better pre-warn the GCW front office to have some extra EMT's on stand by because GCW fans will experience first hand what a real war is going to be like. KEVIN JACOBS: What do you think your chances are of winning this tournament, Who would you like to face in the finals, and whom would you prefer to face if you win the tourney, Mayhem or Lia? JIMMY THUNDER: As long as I'm concentrating and on my game it'll take some effort to stop me, at the moment, after beating Kyo, I feel extremely good about myself and I'm in the best shape of my life. I really don't care who I face but if I could pick someone to face how could you go past someone like LeStatt Knight. The guy could make you an instant main eventer. The only thing is for him to do that is to beat him in the middle of the ring. I know if I could manage to do something like that then I could shoot for bigger and better things. Mayhem is a big dosile idiot and Lia is a great athlete, I know who I'd rather face. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - JAY TERROR KEVIN JACOBS: How do you feel about your chances in round one? JAY TERROR: My chances? To some it seems that Bradley is going to be about as hard to overcome as the vietcong in the 1950's. What makes him so special though. He's a normal guy like myself, no special powers that I know of. So for round one, I predict that the rookie will have his chance to show a vetran miracles do happen. KEVIN JACOBS: There are a number of established superstars in this tournament, out of the lot of them which do you feel poses the greatest threat? JAY TERROR: Threat? Come one, threat's are nothing more than a false promise. They all talk the same with their witty remarks and menacing tones but of them, I honestly can't see a single one proving his points. Especially not Lestatt Knight. KEVIN JACOBS: How much can you possibly know about LeStatt? I mean I am by no means a supporter of the former World Champ but to underestimate him has proven to be costly. JAY TERROR: I've heard of his accolades in the ring, of course it seems dire to underestimate such a man. But let me tell ask you this, where has he been? What has he been doing to stay in top form? I'm fresh talent, ready to go like no other. Lestatt on the other hand is what some of us like to call a page in a book. People have read over his chapter and have moved onto the climax of the novel, not the rising action. KEVIN JACOBS: Interesting take. Well out of the two people that seem to be battling for the title, Lia Ambrosi and Bryan Mayhem whom do you prefer to battle if you win the tournament? JAY TERROR: Either would be something I'd enjoy. After High Stakes though, something inside me is wanting to end this little high Mayhem is feeling at this point. He's climbing up and up and sooner or later, someone will have to bring him down. Hopefully me. As for Lia, could you relay her my phone number for after this thing is over? KEVIN JACOBS: HA I can see what I can do. Just another question or two. In your short time in GCW are there any superstars that have stood out to you? JAY TERROR: Stood out, of course. Namely LeStatt..and you know my take on him. Beyond that talk-loving guy, Vivica Valentine is a catch. I mean, a girl like that doing what a man should be doing deserves something. And hell, she's damn good at it. I'd let her pin me down anyday. KEVIN JACOBS: Any last words? JAY TERROR: All I can say is think twice before you go with the people you know. I know the locker room is dead set that I'll be gone very soon. After I beat Jayce in round one though, I hope they can stomach the fact that The Outlaw is for real! - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - RUFIO KEVIN JACOBS: How do you feel about your first round opponent, Shaman? RUFIO: Well you know... everywhere I go I'm hearing people cryin' about 'Oh Rufie-baby! We miss you! We've you been?' And you know... I don't tell them shit, cause it's none of their damn business. I should give you the same answer, but I'm feeling generous at the moment. Now Shaman... every time there's a minority in the house White Corporate America always wants to put them against one another. I mean I'm the last person to talk about Native American pride... that shit went out with UNITE and their career, but I gotta say I don't wanna fight that dude. Every time I get nailed into a contest or some shit like this they always put me against the big son of a bitch... like people enjoy watching me choke out giants. Fighting big guys like him are like fucking fat chicks... it's fun at the start, but in the end you're bored and exhausted from the heavy lifting... and there's no winning. That's not saying I haven't slayed my share of monsters... in the sack and in the ring... but hell, you know how it is with me. If I'm not hung over and I feel motivated, I might beat Shaman's ass. Otherwise it's up in the air, dig it? KEVIN JACOBS: I dig Mr. Rufio, I dig. Now there are many talented people in this tournament from Jordan White to Vivica Valentine to Jayce Bradley to LeStatt Knight to Twizzy. Out of everyone involved who do you find as the greatest threat to your success? RUFIO: ... Who the fuck is Twizzy? KEVIN JACOBS: Former GCW World Champion, recently returning! RUFIO: Geez, this company goes from bad to worse. First they skimp on my paychecks, then all the women here are lezzie bitches, and now I find out a guy named Twizzy was a world champion. Well to answer the dumbass question, no... there's no greatest threat to my success. And if someone could be named for sake of naming someone, then my greatest threat is myself. Unless I'm getting some sort of tournament bonus out of this... like all expenses paid travel or whatever, then I'm probably not gonna be too interested in overexerting myself. But if someone ends up pissing me off I'm probably gonna beat their ass for the hell of it, and if I win, hey,.. score. KEVIN JACOBS: SO winning the right to go for the World Title isn't important to you? RUFIO: Oh so it's a tournament to get a chance at winning the title? That's like paying a whore for the chance to have sex with her, you only feel cheated when you don't bone. I mean, you do all that damn work and just to challenge someone? Hell I can walk up to the champ right now and just challenge him to a duel, and I bet you he'll give me a match like that... and trust me, I know how to shame someone into the ground until they give in. Who's the damn champ anyways? KEVIN JACOBS: Bryan Mayhem RUFIO: STILL? Didn't someone beat him yet? KEVIN JACOBS: He just won the title Mr. Rufio. RUFIO: From who? KEVIN JACOBS: Lia Ambrosi RUFIO: Oh yeaaaaaaaaaaaaah... man that was a blur. Ohhh, nevermind I had Mayhem mixed with Lestatt. Can't blame me for confusing one boring homo with another. Anyways, last time I did the mathematics, being a world wrestling champion only gains you a 12.7% increase in pussy... and that's meager compared to what I'm already getting now based off of outside factors. Yeah, the pay might shoot up a little more, but compared to the amount of work they want you to do night after night, is it really worth it? KEVIN JACOBS: Well Rufio I appreciate your time. Any last words? RUFIO: Yeah watch out for those Bryan Mayhem matches. They turn straight people gay and gays into Mexicans. Everyone goes down a notch. _________________________________________ As you can see, confidence levels are at an all time high!
