WorldWide 68 On The Air

26 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Baltimore, Maryland- Next stop, Warpath. WorldWide #68 aired from Baltimore tonight, and the Warpath tournament is down to its Final Four. In addition, the rivalry between Bryan Mayhem and Lia Ambrosi is at its boiling point, and the Tag Champions are on the ropes. GCW heads to Boston for Warpath this year, where a new Number One Contender will be crowned. The month-long Warpath Tournament is nearing its completion, as LeStatt Knight, Vivica J. Valentine, Jordan White and Twizzy have earned the right to compete on one night for a shot at the GCW Title. That shot will come against either Bryan Mayhem or Lia Ambrosi, who are confirmed to meet next Sunday in the main event. Mayhem is still the odds-on favorite to retain, and many in GCW are already planning perspective matches for Mayhem to take on any of the remaining Warpath competitors. Also announced on WorldWide tonight is a big four-corners Tag Team Title match. Banned and Exiled will defend against Class Act, Pleasure and Pain, and GCW's newest tag team the Blue Collar Boys. Making matters even worse for Banned and Exiled, Markus Stone suffered what is reported to be a severe knee injury at the hands of the Blue Collar Boys, and President Caldera has made it clear that Chris Storm will not be permitted a replacement partner. It looks like the impressive title reign of B&E~! might be over. It was a tremendous show tonight on X:TV, don't miss it. And don't forget Warpath, Sunday, June 3rd, 8PM EST, live on Pay-Per-View ($49.95).
