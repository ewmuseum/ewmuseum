Unique Critique #3

26 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW's trademark column is a comprehensive review of the entire active roster. Written by a different anonymous contributor each week, Unique Critique is the most honest assessment of each member's performance you can find. The commentary ranges from complimentary to mercilessly cruel, so if you can't handle it, don't read on! But if you want to know how each roster member stacks up in the eyes of one writer, Unique Critique offers you the chance. -Mediocre -Average -Good -Great -World Class First before I get to rolling with my breakdowns I want to make a emphasis on what I am looking for when I breakdown the critique here. First I am looking for a story and where you’re trying to go with it. Also I am throwing grammar out the window here for this one. I am concentrating more on the meat of the story telling, So that everyone knows where I am standing when I write this. Too many people lately are focusing on the writing aspects and not as much on the show itself. So with my best unbiased voice and opinion here is the breakdown. Lia Ambrosi: Former Champion after High Stakes. Tells a great story and puts on a good presence within the show. Personally feeling is she should have had more appearances as the champion and should have carried the story into her rp’s more on how it frustrated her character more, would have been nice to see her dealing with it on a more personable level. Rated Bryan Mayhem: Carried a great story with Lia Ambrosi. Needs more umph in the rp, Last one seemed a bit rushed and thrown together. In character storyline and development is great, but we also want to see what he thinks about the current happenings around him. Rated Lestatt Knight: Good start to a return and held the surprise factor. Looking forward to seeing how you carry the story through in the future shows and your reactions in some of your rp. Rated (return factor and not much else low rating) Kyle James: Pretty good showing when first coming in. RP’s are decent could use a little improvement in the story aspect. Build around something and work on the pliable story there. Small tweaks and adjustments to carry the story further would make the character spring more to life. Segments in shows should pick up a little. Use the show and RP to tell your story a bit capture the audience. Rated Banned & Exiled: Getting the juvenile delinquent feel here. Something done before, but you two take a different approach to it. The on show activities and Rp’s have threaded themselves nicely. Would like to see more consequences happening in the rp’s about what you have done in shows. Rated Jimmy Thunder: You tell a story about your character and his happenings. The dialog between two people is good. Looking sometimes more like a simple soap opera set up you should show us more about Thunder a little about who he is and what makes him tick. The current Steroids happening should have an effect on him. Let it show in some of his actions with frustration and even in show. More on show segs may help here as well. TV Champion needs exposure. You are a ranking star with a title. Rated Jay Terror: Your rp seemed to jump a bit. Good story, but I had to go back and read it a couple times. There were some portions that were confusing. Some simple directional shoves and the piece was perfect. Over all I liked where you were coming from with the story. Just make the transitions between pieces easier to follow. Rated Takahashi Brothers: The rp was fun. Has me waiting to see what you have planned for the show. As one of the new guys on the block it is hard to keep up the fun and interesting banner between siblings. The story was about you coming to America and your upbringing and relationship between the siblings. Let us see where you go next with it. Rated Jordan White: Good rp. Only place I saw a bit of lack was in show segments curious if you are going to touch bases on Jordan Whites connection to The Establishment and how it will effect his home life and the people around him. Good rp and good story more in card activity would be awesome. Rated Jayce Bradley: Your feud with Shaman is looking good and you are putting out some good rp’s. Curious question is though where do you go from here? You are still up in the air with the feud with Shaman and things seem very unsettled between the two of you. I like the fact you stay and mention your opponent and the games that are being played. Don’t let the story slide find a new groove with it. Shaman: You have improved a lot from when you first entered GCW. Your story and on show stuff has improved and I see you carrying a bit of a classic wrestling feel in your segments and rp’s, But your hurting yourself over the last few weeks with a lack of show presence and how you are telling the story. Grasp the story and keep it rolling you can do it. Rated Vivica J Valentine: You’re jumping around a bit. You have solid stories, But needing some in card actions that are just more than interactions. You are one of the firecracker women in the sport. What will you do next is how we should e guessing you. Bring the surprise and unknown factor back and you have hit the nail on the head. Rated Rufio: Lack of activity over the last few weeks has me marking you down with a low mark, would like to see you more active since you have a creative and entertaining character. Rated Katie O’Clare: Solid rp and great performance and even better story through basic descript of actions done to you. Brining forth the action and working through adversity in the story and in card segments. The Victory over the three opponents pulled a card not often seen. Rated Twizzy: Your return to GCW is a good feel story about it. Segments and such need a bit more of a push. Maybe another segment added and you will have hit it. Want to see how you respond to the Establishment. Rated Draven Lox: Inactive and a bit slow over the last few weeks, but when you rp you put out a good quality story, would like to see more segs from you in card. Rated Damon Aldair: Been a little quiet on your front, would like to see more. Rated Overall once again everyone did well. I just touched bases on what I think would be more entertaining and easier to follow for people to make themselves more recognized in the bigger picture. GCW is growing in leaps and bounds and is getting its good legs back. Keep up the good work and keep working hard.
