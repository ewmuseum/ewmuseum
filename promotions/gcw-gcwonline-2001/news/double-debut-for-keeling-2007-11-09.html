Double Debut For Keeling

9 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just days after revealing himself as GCW's newest signing, the world's only carbon-neutral wrestler - Elijah Keeling - has made a second debut. While not yet having competed in a match for his new employer, Keeling stepped into the GCW ring to shoot his first music video appearance, appearing in the Oscar-winning Melissa Etheridge's rework of Nickelback's "Rock Star" entitled "(Hey, hey, I wanna be like) Al Gore". In a homage to the original video, the Etheridge version, from which all proceeds will go towards climate change awareness, features a mixture of household names and more obscure celebrities mixed in with everyday people, all mouthing the lyrics in time to the track. Keeling can first be seen lip-synching in a 3 second snippet in the GCW ring, between clips of Sting amidst a rainforest and Natalie Portman draped across the hood of a hybrid car during the passage, 'I'm gonna go one better than Jodie Foster/Win a Nobel prize to go with my Oscar'. He can then be seen for a further 2 seconds between Jake Gylenhaal standing on a polar ice-cap and an un-named elderly woman standing in front of a wind-farm during the passage, 'I'll be like Al - your Superman and your Zorro/When you're swept away on the day after tomorrow'. While some would see rubbing shoulders with celebrities as a distraction, Elijah is said to be itching to make his in-ring debut... and it was announced tonight that he'll get his chance to do just that at the upcoming WorldWide #77 where he'll face a tough test in the form of former GCW United States champion Dorian Ryan.
