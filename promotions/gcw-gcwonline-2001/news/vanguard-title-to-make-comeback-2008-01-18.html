Vanguard Title to Make Comeback?

18 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With a heavy influx of new talent, GCW has reportedly begun discussing the return of one of its dormant titles. Over its seven year history, GCW has also used and retired the Women's, Hardcore, Cruiserweight and Vanguard Championships. Last year, GCW merged the highly popular Hardcore and Cruiserweight titles into the Vanguard division, a unique new concept in wrestling. It appears now that GCW in considering a return of the Vanguard Title. In Vanguard matches, a wrestler could only score a victory in Last Man Standing fashion, when his opponent is unable to answer a five-count. Matches could also end on submission or knockout. However, some in GCW remain loyal to the wildly popular Hardcore title and its 24/7 rule. The title was the usual no-holds-barred championship, but was defended at all times and all locations. One of GCW's most lasting moments was an episode of Meltdown featuring Toad losing and regaining the title four times in rapid succession. Still, with such a fast-growing roster of championship-caliber talent, it has become apparent to most GCW brass that another title belt is needed. Expect a new championship to debut within the next month.
