Cheap Labor: A Pawn In The Chess Game?

29 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to various reports and evidenced by paparazzi photos released earlier this morning, the newly crowned GCW Television Champion has all ready tarnished his young reign. Shortly after the conclusion of Worldwide 121, Cheap Labor was spotted at the Beverly Loan Center (Pawnshop to the Stars) in Akron, OH. He was seen trying to negotiate an acceptable selling price for the Television Title belt. Witnesses report that, shortly after the bartering started, Cheap Labor belligerently accused the clerk of “low-balling” him and became confrontational. Attention was brought to the local authorities and Cheap Labor was escorted from the building but received no further penalties. Cheap Labor was unofficially “stripped” of the official Television Title belt this afternoon, less than twenty four hours after the news had surfaced. The new “belt” is a cardboard cut-out wrapped in tin foil and details have been drawn on in Sharpie marker. The replica was graciously donated by art students at Forest Hill Elementary School in Akron. An agitated Steven Caldera, President of GCW, had this to say of the federation’s rookie titleholder, "GCW diligently protects not only the image of its title belts but the belts themselves. While we recognize Cheap Labor as the Television Champion, the belt remains the property of Global Championship Wrestling and it is not his to sell. As we can no longer trust him with possession of the belt, we've decided that a plastic replica belt is more fitting for a champion of his caliber. He'll carry the replica for the duration of his reign, which will hopefully be very short. Cheap Labor will be monitored very closely by GCW officials." Those close to the Television Title holder say his well-documented quest for finances has become more increasingly frantic over time thus resulting in inappropriate actions. Labor was gladly available for comment on the matter but, per request of GCW officials, those comments cannot be included in this article due to “language detrimental to the enterprise.” Cheap Labor may be seen defending the Television Title on Worldwide 122 against Daria Ngozi and Mysterious Masked Mexican.
