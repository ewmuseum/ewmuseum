New Establishment Member This Week?

15 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Two weeks ago on WorldWide, the wrestling world was shocked when President Caldera seemed to make the unthinkable happen. When The Establishment was resurrected with Jordan White and LeStatt Knight at the helm, the group was threatening but not altogether unprecedented. When Octavian returned to supplement the group, it seemed like Caldera was bent on bringing the best of the best into his legendary stable. But when Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins returned to GCW and replaced Jordan White, thereby betraying his nickname and joining Caldera for the first time, the face of The Establishment changed forever. Take it from me, ladies and gentlemen. If Rich Rollins can join The Establishment, anyone can join The Establishment. Rollins was the last person one would have suspected. Regardless of his attitude for the fans, the one constant in the career of Rich Rollins was his hatred for the establishment, both the figurative and actual groups. But when Rollins pushed all that aside to align with Caldera, Knight and Octavian, it proved that this version of The Establishment might have a draw that few will be able to resist. And Caldera has promised it will "only get better from here." Now, it's entirely possible that this is merely backstage gossip going overboard, but it seems the wrestlers of GCW seem to think it's only a matter of time before someone else turns. And while Caldera himself hasn't come out and said it, it seems his camp is leaking the notion that The Establishment will, in fact, be welcoming a new member this week on WorldWide. Of course, Caldera has done his best to intimate that this new member will be number one contender Vivica J. Valentine, but Valentine hasn't shown the warning signs. But perhaps the only man who knows the truth is Peter Maxwell, who posed as an EMT to eavesdrop on her conversation with Caldera two weeks ago. Tune in to WorldWide to find out if the rumors are true, and if The Establishment will, in fact, improve on its already amazing cast.
