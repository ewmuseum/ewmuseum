30 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coach Belial of the Church of the Unholy has released their weekly injury report for the upcoming Rampage PPV. The Church of the Unholy's Coach Belial has released the Church's weekly injury log for this week of Rampage . This is the weekly injury report that the Church has put on their website. They have said that this will be a weekly thing and will do it to show that they have, and I quote Evil James, "Nothing to hide like those dirty politicians who don't show their tax returns or pay to have sex with hookers. " There also is a rumor that this is just mind games and BS but Coach Belial had this to say. "We don't fool around over injuries. If they want to believe it's fake, then they can. If not, then we'll show them what's real and what isn't. Only time will tell." Coach Belial also expressed concerns with AXEL Action's recent "rough" behavior of sexual nature. "AXEL needs to lay off the rough sex. I mean, come on, Evil James has sex with all kinds of women and yet his penis doesn't get broken every week." In regards to Evil James' nagging shoulder injury, the Coach scoffed at comments that he is merely ripping off Bill Belichick by "claiming" that Evil James has the same injry Tom Brady "supposedly" has every week. "Evil James' shoulder is of great concern. In fact, I almost benched him this week but decided against it considering that it's a pay-per-view and I'm trying to get him a PTC Unified Title shot later in the year." When asked a follow up question about Evil James' wanting a Unified Title shot, he had this to say. "We WILL make the Championship...UNHOLY." Out: Captain Carnage (escaped into the woods) Possessed Homeless People (their tent city was evacuated by the police due to decomposing flesh being found there so we cannot find them so they are out) Questionable: AXEL Action (penis inflated due to looking at porn and too much masturbation) Xenomorph (burned by his own alien acid blood during combat) Probable: Evil James (right shoulder injury) Devil Dan (poop got in his eyes during a prank) Every other Church member is fine and healthy according to this UNHOLY organization.
