Interview: James Bryan

9 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, Kevin Jacobs here with a wrapup of the amazing Devil's Night/Great American Nightmare event! To talk about the historic night, we've asked longtime GCW road agent and play by play man James Bryan to come back and sit down for another interview. Here's the transcript of our conversation. Jacobs: Thanks for being here, JB. Bryan: Always a pleasure. We're all really excited about the big GCW/PRIME show, so I've been looking forward to talking about it. Jacobs: For starters, what were your overall impressions of the show? Bryan: It was a success. I don't think anyone in the front offices of either promotion is disappointed with it. And it did phenomenal buyrates, numbers that we think will far surpass anything GCW and PRIME have ever done individually. I think some fans were disappointed that they didn't see some things they expected to see, but overall it was a lot of fun to be a part of. Jacobs: How do you think GCW's product matched up with PRIME's? Bryan: Honestly I don't know that either promotion was necessarily on top of its game. I think everyone was taken out of their comfort zone a bit, knowing their rivals were just on the other side of the locker room. But that said, I think GCW showed the world what we're about. I thought we put on five great matches, and PRIME fans couldn't have helped but see what makes this company great. Jacobs: Let's talk a bit about individual performances. For starters, Banned and Exiled~! retained their titles again, this time against Octavian and President Caldera himself. Could this be the first tag team ever to be inducted into the Hall of Fame? Bryan: I think if they retired today they'd have to be inducted tomorrow. That's just the bottom line. Not only have they held those titles for a long time, nobody has even come close to beating them. These guys aren't even getting challenged, not even by a couple of legends in Octavian and Caldera. And the scary thing is, they're still going. One knock on them is the lack of great challengers, but I think the fact that they're so rapidly turning challengers away is more a testament to their greatness than anything else. Jacobs: How about Lia Ambrosi? Obviously a big storyline here is The Establishment having the three greatest champions of all time in their ranks. Is Ambrosi the fourth? Bryan: Well, the fourth greatest champion of all time in Jason Dread, and fortunately he hasn't shown any interest in joining The Establishment. So Lia's looking to be the fifth. Is she there yet? It's tough to say. The thing that sets those other four apart is that they did it over a greater period of time. But Lia has held that title for almost the entire calendar year, which is really unprecedented. In all of 2007 nobody but Bryan Mayhem has managed to beat her. I think she's a first-ballot Hall of Famer, but a win at NC-17 over Vivica Valentine would cement her status as all-time great. Jacobs: Speaking of Valentine, she finally silenced the doubters and buried Bryan Mayhem alive at Devil's Night. How do you see that main event coming out? Bryan: That's almost impossible to judge right now, because Valentine's only been on that stage once. She got a World Title shot at Devil's Night last year and lost to LeStatt. Since then she's topped Knight twice and won some big matches, but NC-17 is bigger than anything she's ever been a part of. Ambrosi won in the main event there last year, so she's got a massive advantage. Jacobs: Not gonna wager a guess then? Bryan: I'll make a pick a few weeks from now. Right now there are still a lot of question marks around that situation. Not to mention the seeming distrust breeding between those two best friends, and President Caldera's hint that Valentine might be the next person to turn. Jacobs: Do you think she's ready to join Caldera? Bryan: I can certainly understand the temptation. Standing side by side with Knight, Octavian and Rollins would be a truly amazing thing. But I just don't think Valentine would compromise her integrity by taking a shortcut. She's gotten where she is by herself, and she needs to prove she can win the big one by herself. Jacobs: Let's go back to The Establishment for a moment. Rollins, Octavian, Knight, Caldera. And Caldera says it might get better from there. Is this the best incarnation of The Establishment, and can anyone stop them? Bryan: Yes and yes. Yes, this is by far the best Establishment ever. The version with Dread, Stevens and Toxic is certainly a very, very good one. That's three World Champions right there. But these are three Hall of Fame wrestlers you're talking about. That said, there's greatness elsewhere on the GCW roster. Jordan White may have come up a bit short in winning the World Title, but what you're witnessing now is truly special. Here's a guy that came up short but used that to motivate him, and he's taking his game to the next level. And Jay Terror's doing the same thing. Those are two guys that might become legends some day. So I guess what I'm trying to say is that this is an amazing time to be in GCW, because you're seeing three Hall of Fame wrestlers trying to re-establish themselves against a group of people that could some day reach that same level. Jacobs: GCW is planning Rampage 2008 to start the year as usual. Who do you see winning the tournament? Bryan: Again, it's really too early to tell. But if Jay Terror can prove he can stay in the ring with a guy like LeStatt, and not many can, I think The Outlaw might be able to take it. I think Jay Terror and Jordan White both have the World Title in their futures. Jacobs: Well, JB, thank you for joining me. Bryan: My pleasure. Be sure to tune in to WorldWide this week, we've got a good one for you!
