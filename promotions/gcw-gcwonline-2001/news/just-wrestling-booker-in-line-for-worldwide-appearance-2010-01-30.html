Just Wrestling Booker in Line for WorldWide Appearance?

30 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Former AWC, cW, HOW champion to appear at WorldWide 125? The whereabouts of Tim Shipley, owner and booker of successful independent touring promotion Just Wrestling, have been something of a mystery since his final appearance as High Octane Wrestling LSD Champion two weeks ago. Shipley had his HOW contract terminated due to what High Octane Television called a "career-threatening knee injury" that he sustained before Christmas and aggravated by deceiving doctors into clearing him to continue to compete. He also handed over the reins for the present Just Wrestling tour - the first to leave continental North America, touring up and down Britain - to his Marketing & Public Relations manager Jody Monroe. He remained in the US with pressing "business affairs" to attend to, according to the Just website. So why do rumours this morning suggest he will be appearing at GCW WorldWide 125? Shipley, 24, was once the wrestling world's "next big thing", surging up the ranks in Core Wrestling and then the Atlantic Wrestling Club before a horrific back injury so derailed his progress that he was forced to retire from the game. He returned years later with the quite overt intention of making as much money as he could to pay a friend's medical bills, yet his time in Ringside Wrestling showed that the Englishman had overcome the hurdles of his injuries and could once more aspire towards a career at the top of the wrestling world. But Shipley is an intelligent man and a shrewd entrepreneur, and rather than pursue his own dreams he saw the market for a high-quality independent promotion. Shipley built the Just Wrestling business model on bringing in big names he knew well at cheap prices, who brought in the fans, and subsequently building the place around unknowns like Judas Crippen and Aaron Nothings, allowing him to cut off the likes of the Illustrious Face-Eater and Andy Murray when their monetary demands escalated. Just is now undisputed in its position as the most respected non-televised promotion out there, and Shipley benefited greatly from the exposure accorded himself when he signed a lucrative HOW contract last year. But with GCW President Steven Caldera set to step down at NC-17, Shipley's experience and profile could set him in good stead to take over... We don't know in what capacity Shipley will be at WorldWide tonight, or even if he will appear at all. But we would put good money on the Englishman's intentions stretching further than just to join the active roster. Be sure to tune in and see for yourself.
