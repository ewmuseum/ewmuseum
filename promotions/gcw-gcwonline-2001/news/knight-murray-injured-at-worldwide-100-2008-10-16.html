Knight, Murray Injured at WorldWide 100

16 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following their hellish "I Quit" match at WorldWide 100 this week, both Andy Murray and LeStatt Knight were treated for what GCW officials called "significant but not severe" injuries. The match ended when LeStatt Knight lost consciousness, but had things continued much longer the result might have been different. Murray reportedly blacked out while receiving medical treatment backstage. Murray was said to be "morose and nonresponsive" by the GCW medical team in the moments following the match, but Murray was reportedly acting like his usual self by the following morning. Knight also lost a significant amount of blood; both wrestlers were given transfusions at local hospitals. Knight refused stitches, reportedly telling doctors that he would "do it [himself]." Despite the loss, Knight was said to be in high spirits following the match. Knight and Murray are both considered among a large group of favorites to win the Dangerous Games match next week. While both wrestlers were adamant that they will participate in the battle royal as planned, medical teams have been instructed to keep a close eye on both of them. It is possible that, should their injuries be aggravated during the match, they will be removed from the ring to receive medical attention. Stay tuned for more information during the week, but it is likely that both Knight and Murray will keep low profiles while preparing for the biggest match of the year.
