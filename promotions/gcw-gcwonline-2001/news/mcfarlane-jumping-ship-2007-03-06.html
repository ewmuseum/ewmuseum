McFarlane Jumping Ship?

6 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There has been very little seen from former GCW World Champion Dawn McFarlane since her high profile defeat two shows in a row to current GCW World Champion Lia Ambrosi. Although the two friends and stablemates shared a warm embrace at the culmination of their final match, little has been seen or heard from McFarlane since that time, with the exception of a lackluster showing at the #1 Contender Battle Royal. Sources close to McFarlane say the former Champ seems to have pulled heavily out of the public spotlight, spending an increasing amount of time alone and away from friends and family. Rumour abounds that the expansive McFarlane estate on the outskirts of Toronto was closed recently, with no clear report as to where Miss McFarlane now resides. These reports, amid constant sightings of McFarlane with Global Champion Jake Hix, seem to point towards a potential jumping ship in the superstars near future. Miss McFarlane was not available for comment.
