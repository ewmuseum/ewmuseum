Dismissive Murray Laughs Off Establishment 'Revival'

4 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GCW superstar Andy Murray has this morning launched the latest in a line of vitriolic media outbursts, this time aiming his bile at GCW's mainstay power stable, The Establishment. "The whole thing is a sham and a joke," Murray told reporters in the early hours of the morning. "Seriously, how many times has this group switched personnel recently? They seem to kick somebody out and bring in a new lackey every week. It's pathetic. How is anyone supposed to feel any kind of threat from a group that changes members more regularly than Garbage Bag Johnny changes his underwear?" The outburst follows on from an uncharacteristically vindictive damning of the vultures that have hung over The Scottish King of Cool's head since drawing swords with LeStatt Knight at WorldWide 100. It would appear, however, that this latest statement may have been brought on by his recent encounters with the Establishment's current adopted "leader," Jay Terror. "I find it utterly ridiculous that people are building this up as some kind of shift in momentum. They take a guy in and kick him out four weeks later. How many times has this happened now? Soon enough Brad Jackson will lose his interest in GCW again and slink off into whatever filthy black hole he emerged from. And Tyson DeBough? There's a name that hasn't been worth a dime since last Summer. The Establishment has no stability; they're a mess, all over the place, and until they find that stability, I refuse to even acknowledge them as a 'threat'". With Murray set to take on new Establishment member Brad Jackson in a "throw in the towel" match at WorldWide this evening, one can only assume that the Scotsman's words will only seek to further provoke the ire of Jackson and Establishment sidekick, Jay Terror, who will be at ringside with The Mechnical Animal. Fans are assured of a monstrous battle between two of the most powerful wrestlers in GCW history, that's for certain.
