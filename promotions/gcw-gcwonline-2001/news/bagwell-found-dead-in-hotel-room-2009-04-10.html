Bagwell Found Dead in Hotel Room

10 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier this morning, a phone call was made to the GCW Headquarters in Columbus, OH to report some devastating news - Chris Bagwell is dead. Although no official word as to the cause of death has come in ? many are beginning to speculate the abundance of alcohol consumption could be the underlying factor in this tragedy. "I received a phone call from Mr. Bagwell’s girlfriend, Rebecca, at approximately 7:15 this morning stating that Bagwell was unresponsive when she attempted to wake him this morning for their flight home," said Officer Timothy Philips of the Honolulu Police Department. "By the time we got an ambulance on the scene, it was already too late." Less than twenty four hours prior, Chris Bagwell was leaving the Shockwave pay-per-view event after defeating Jordan White. He was last seen mingling with his girlfriend, Brian Fisher, and two Honolulu prostitutes before exiting the arena. Concession workers at Aloha stadium, who wish to remain nameless, claim they overheard Bagwell talking to Fisher about having a celebration which would entail a tremendous amount of drinking. Fisher was unavailable for comment. Rebecca Paris, Bagwell’s long-term girlfriend, is said to be an emotional wreck. The two were recently reunited after years apart and so her heart seems to be falling from her chest. When asked of the incident, she neglected to comment. With this being the second death of an active wrestler in the last year, rumors are beginning to circulate around backstage that the GCW and President Steven Caldera could be under some major political fire. GCW does not currently have a standard procedure for an active drug policy in place and thus may ultimately get heavily fined, should narcotics come into play in this case. More on this story as it develops.
