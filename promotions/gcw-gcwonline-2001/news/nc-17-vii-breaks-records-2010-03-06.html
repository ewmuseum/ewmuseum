NC-17 VII Breaks Records

6 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NC-17 VII aired last night on Pay-Per-View from Cowboys Stadium, and as expected has shattered GCW records. While statistics are still being compiled, last night's NC-17 event appears to have broken GCW Pay-Per-View records. GCW President Steven Caldera met with Dallas Cowboys Owner Jerry Jones over a year ago, well before Cowboys Stadium was complete, to book NC-17 at the palatial venue. Caldera has his sights set on major records while spending a great deal of money to promote the event, and it seems as though the investment has paid off. The official attendance for the event came in at 106,246, easily breaking the attendance record set last year at NC-17 at University of Phoenix Stadium in Arizona. The incredible gate has reportedly left GCW wrestlers with record paychecks for the event, which has obviously made for very high spirits backstage. Even more impressive are the expected Pay-Per-View buyrates. While GCW was ambitiously hoping to attract 800,000 buys, early indications are that NC-17 sold over a million views. For a professional wrestling event this is extremely impressive, and many are crediting the hype surrounding the venue itself for bringing in casual customers. Of course, the quality of the event itself deserves credit for most of its success. The event so far as garnered extremely positive reviews from a number of media sources that do not normally cover professional wrestling. GCW's media blitz before the event was intended to draw as much coverage as possible, and it delivered with one of GCW's best events yet. Of course, NC-17 also brought some major changes to the company. The GCW crew will be here all week bringing you all the behind the scenes news as GCW begins a profitable but uncertain 2010 schedule.
