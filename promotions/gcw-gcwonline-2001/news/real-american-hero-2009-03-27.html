Real American Hero?

27 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Earlier today, GCW received a local California news bit that was aired on the 6 O'Clock news. The clip depicted Tyson DeBough and Brian Fisher, both dressed all in red white and blue, proclaiming their fierce loyalty to the United States Government, President Obama, and the American way. "We just really love our country." A blue and white faced DeBough commented. "It's the only place I know where you can still hire a prostitute legally, the supreme court will debate about legalizing marijuana -- which I think is a great idea -- and I can get a local news anchor to come and record me with my face painted, in my underwear." DeBough proceeded to pull off his pants, revealing some skin tight, American flag patterned underwear. The two friends also had large flags that they wore as capes, and sparklers which they waved around shouting "We're number one!" Fisher carried about a bag of purple skittles. No one is really sure how he got them all to be purple, but sources assume he has some serious skittle connections. GCW representatives interpreted the stunt to be something to do with Tyson's upcoming American Dream match, which was confirmed by the note that was attached to the video tape. For family viewing purposes, the note has been edited, even though there was a note from Fisher claiming yet again because he was the Television Champion, that he made the rules of TV. "F**k Woods, F**k Action and F**k Face. Ha ha ha - F**k Face." More on these events as they unfold.
