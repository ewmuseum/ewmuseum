Goldy's Locks: NC-17 Edition

4 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who better than Barry Goldstein to give you the inside scoop on who to put your money on for NC-17? Take this to your bookie! Hello Folks, I'm Barry Goldstein. Some of you may know me as GCW's Third Reporter, but ol' Barry's a whiz with the numbers, and after I make my picks, I'd wager that it won't be long before I got myself a shiny new nickname: Number Two! Anyway, NC-17 is a stacked line-up with several matches featuring multiple competitors, so you can make some real money here with lower probability favorites. Of course, there's always a chance that my locks won't pan out exactly as I say they will. You might lose some money, but hey, I'd lose my reputation. So I'm not responsible for any money you lose. In fact, my reputation is priceless, so we better both win, or I'll send you all a bill for your share of the perceived value of the reputation I lost. Now without further ado, let's take a look at these match=ups! Clyde Fox vs. Triumph Frost NC-17 VIII starts off with a tricky one as Clyde Fox and Triumph Frost take each other on in a match with very little build up. This means that the two of them will be facing off with very little to tell us how their very different styles will play against each other. Triumph Frost has looked more and more like a World Championship class wrestler each week despite being one of the most unlikeable bastards on the roster, so it's hard to bet against him, but I can't overlook the wins he has secured over both Vivica J. Valentine and Karina Wolfenden since Dangerous Games. Goldy's Lock: Clyde Fox Aimz vs. Garbage Bag Johnny This match is similar in terms of build-up to Fox vs. Frost, but the strange circumstances surrounding this match make it a bit of an easier pick for me. With the hair vs. hair stipulation, a loss for Aimz would be more embarrassing than a loss for Garbage Bag Johnny. Couple that with the fact that this match is a rain check for a GTT7 bracket final that GBJ won via forfeit, Garbage Bag's lack of time in the ring over the last year, and high expectations for Aimz's debut, and my pick is fairly obvious. It's never wise to count Garbage Bag Johnny out completely, but part of me thinks his eyes just aren't on the prize in this one. If Aimz is favored too heavily in this one, GBJ might be a nice underdog pick, but if the odds are more even (which they should be), or Aimz is the underdog, she's the obvious choice. Goldy's Lock: Aimz Four Square Rampage Qualifier Now, don't put a lot of money on this one unless you're feeling real lucky. This match has too many variables for me to offer a reliable Superfecta. Nobody knows at this point what the rings will look like, but for shirts and goggles, I'll offer four picks, but don't blow too much on them. Goldy's Locks: Phil Atken, Dorian Ryan, Brad Cruz, and Hot Mask. Colin O'Ryan and Jimmy Thunder vs. Theo Palmer and Alexander Redding I think this tag match is pretty easy. Theo Palmer and Alexander Redding are the closest thing to a tag team GCW's seen in a while, even without an active title. Thunder and O'Ryan are both fine wrestlers in their own rights, but Palmer and Redding will utilize their teamwork to pick this one up rather easily. Goldy's Locks: Theo Palmer and Alexander Redding Donny Diamond vs. Bryan Mayhem vs. Shaman vs. Dorn Tracer vs. Toga This match is going to be a lot tougher to predict than I expected. With Bryan Mayhem fighting on his home court, he'd be an easy man to pick, but Diamond and Shaman are equally vested in the Hardcore Title. Dorn Tracer has been a virtual non-factor, and during Anarchy Rules, Shaman and Mayhem were the only two to trade off the title. Toga's a definite wildcard in this one, but unless everyone else kills each other (which is possible), he's going to get creamed. This one's a tough call, but Diamond has both a strong desire to be the champ, and Dorn Tracer might be enough of a factor to help him get there. Goldy's Lock: Donny Diamond Chris Bagwell vs. Johnny Borealis Chris Bagwell is like the Barry Goldstein of the ring. He's been around for a long time and done great work, but he's never fully gotten his due. Bagwell has never won GCW gold, and that'll give him the edge. Borealis and Bagwell are both great showmen, and even though Borealis has been performing as good as we've ever seen him, I think this match means more to Bagwell. Borealis has done some wonderful things with the HDTV Title, but with the momentum he's built up since his return, he knows his run isn't stopping there. Goldy's Lock: Chris Bagwell Dynasty vs. Aaron Fujita vs. El Moreno vs. The Magnificent Messiah Now here's where Barry is going to make some real money. The books already have El Moreno as a favorite in this one, and that's well deserved. If there was nothing on the line, I'd say El Moreno, too. But Dynasty and Aaron Fujita have also looked really good since Dangerous Games, and the hype around Moreno's streak puts the pressure on him and off everyone else. Aaron Fujita's been getting a ton of "heat" with his "I'm Not Mexican" campaign. I'd put my money on him. If I'm right, it's going to be a payday no matter what happens in any of the other matches. Goldy's Lock: Aaron Fujita Vivica J. Valentine vs. Karina Wolfenden A lot of people have been waiting a long time to see this US Title bout, and I think we're in for a hell of a match here. Both of these women have tremendous capability and the potential to be the best in the business at any time. Right now, though? K-Wolf is ALWAYS dangerous, but she's been a bit less consistent since losing the anonymity. Vivica J. Valentine looks stronger now than she has since betraying her true identity around this time last year. Now that the fans are back on her side, she's got the edge in Barry's book on this 50/50. Goldy's Lock: Vivica J. Valentine Andy Murray vs. Jay Terror Finally, the match for all the marbles! I'd say that this one is another toss-up, although I'd give a slight edge to Jay Terror. Andy Murray knows what it takes to be the champion, and there's no man or woman in GCW right now who is more capable of beating Jay Terror. Andy's been giving his all, though, for years, and Dangerous Games might have been the straw that broke the camel's back. After an arduous victory there, Andy's used up a lot of his tank just to get this title shot, and Jay's been using his power and influence to stack things in his favor. If it was anybody else but Andy Murray across the ring from him, Jay's edge would increase exponentially, but I think right now, Jay will do what it takes to retain. Goldy's Lock: Jay Terror
