Vince Jones At High Stakes?

30 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Las Vegas, Nevada- Rumors from Las Vegas this week speculate that former GCW star "The Violence" Vince Jones is pondering an invitation from President Steven Caldera to attend GCW's upcoming Supercard. Jones, recently back on the free agent market, was a highly successful GCW star and was quickly courted by GCW upon resurfacing. President Caldera, currently embroiled in a spat with PRIME star Lindsay Troy, offered Jones a chance to see how GCW handles Troy when she appears on GCW television. Troy and Jones recently had words as well, prompting Caldera to extend his invitation to The Violence. Jones has reportedly been sent a front-row ticket to High Stakes, but it is still unclear if Jones will show. Still, Jones has expressed his interest in returning to Global Championship Wrestling, though his decision is still pending. Caldera has not commented officially on the situation, but seems particularly interested in bringing the volatile star back to the GCW roster. Caldera was notably enamored with Jones's work during his first GCW stint, particularly some excellent work he did with Ricky Thunder at last year's NC-17. Speculation is that Caldera will be throwing a large dollar amount at Jones in an effort to get him to return to the company. Tune in to High Stakes to find out of Jones accepts GCW's invitation to attend.
