Roxie Sykes Released

25 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Santa Barbara stunner leaves GCW for PRIME. On the eve of GCW's Australian tour, the promotion has confirmed the release of Roxie Sykes. Roxie, 28, is best known to GCW fans as manager of Rampage Tournament #1 seed Tim Shipley, but has recently made strides to strike out on her own. Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny initially denied her the chance to try out in the ring, but she finally got her chance at Shockwave, where she impressed in her dominant defeat of Aaron Fujita. It is thought that the display persuaded GBJ to offer Roxie, who has performed non-wrestling roles in Just Wrestling and High Octane Wrestling before GCW but has never had a wrestler's contract, a spot on the roster. But this came too late for Roxie, who had already signed up with PRIME as part of the Born Again tournament. PRIME, one of GCW's chief rivals in the Primetime Central group, has been slipping in the ratings lately and launched Born Again in a bid to revive the promotion's fortunes. Roxie joined the New Era bracket with other relative unknowns such as Hush, Crazy Joe, and GCW's own Bishop Steele, as well as more established names Castor Strife and Shawn Jessica Hart. She won her first three-way match, and is set to contest a second round encounter on this week's ReVolution. Garbage Bag Johnny had hoped to persuade Roxie to stay on with GCW alongside her PRIME role, but with GCW set to tour Australia, the logistics were deemed too difficult. Moreover, Roxie still bears a grudge against GBJ for what she describes as "taking her for granted" when she was employed as Shipley's manager - memorably, the Commissioner once made her water his plants while he talked about more important issues with Shipley, a move which prompted complaints from several feminist organisations. But there is a twist in the tale for Roxie, for Garbage Bag Johnny - himself a former PRIME 5 Star Champion - is also contesting the Born Again tournament. If both win at ReVolution, the two could meet in the main event at Ultraviolence. Perhaps Roxie will have the chance to show GBJ once and for all what she is made of.
