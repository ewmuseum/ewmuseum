Reed Out of Jail, McKail Out of Hospital

11 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the AP Wire... GCW superstar Jake Reed was released from a Las Vegas jail on Tuesday when High Spirits Casino owner Andrew White Cloud, better known under his wrestling persona Shaman, declined to press charges after an incident at the Casino on Sunday during the High Stakes pay-per-view event. Reed, a recent addition addition to the Global Championship Wrestling roster, along with fellow GCW star Jacob McKail incited a near riot during a backstage brawl that spilled onto the casino floor. Local Las Vegas Police arrived on the scene at the High Spirits Casino just in time to see Reed smashing a glass door into the head of a charging Jacob McKail. As a result of the events Reed was taken into custody and McKail was taken to a local hospital. Apparently the two were fighting over the outcome of a wrestling match just moments earlier, it was a fight actually initiated by McKail who, in the end, got the worst of the brawl. Jacob McKail was released from Mountain View Hospital early Monday morning with 12 stitches in his head and an order to take two weeks off. Naturally McKail immediately requested, and was given, a match for this week's WorldWide broadcast. GCW President Steven Caldera could not be reached for comment on this serious flaunting of a physicians orders, but in the dangerous world of professional wrestling this sort of thing is par for the course.
