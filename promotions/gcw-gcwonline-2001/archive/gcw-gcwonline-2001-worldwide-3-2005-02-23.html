WorldWide 3, 23 February 2005

WorldWide 3 Hartford Civic Center Coliseum — Hartford, Connecticut ---- Introduction The GCW logo flashes on screen, before ‘Wake Up’ begins playing amid quick screens of Toxic, Twisted, Jason Dread, Octavian, The Jhub, Steven Caldera, and Mike Hardy. The intro closes with a quick, subtle flash of the evil face of The Dark Messiah, before the shot opens into the Hartford Civic Center, and the stage explodes with fireworks! The crowd goes crazy as the fireworks display erupts and finally finishes, and the cameras begin sailing over the crowd, zooming in on signs reading “The Messiah Has Returned!” and “Jhub 4-20”. The excited voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue, welcoming us to the show. The camera finally rests on the commentator’s booth, where JB sits next to his new partner… Mr. Danger! As their faces appear on the MegaTron, the crowd cheers in excitement for Danger, who turns and smiles out at the fans. Bryan- Welcome to the Hartford Civic Center, GCW fans! With over fifteen thousand fans jam-packed here in Connecticut, we’re ready for another episode of GCW WorldWide! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan, and this week I’m joined at ringside by my new broadcast colleague, Mr. Danger! Danger- Why thank you, JB! I’ve got to say I’m damn proud to be back here in GCW. When my former employee Steven Caldera called me and offered me this job, I just couldn’t refuse. I mean, what could be better? I’m six feet away from the best wrestling in the world, and I’m getting paid to watch it! Bryan- Well, certainly there’s nobody better equipped to analyze the GCW action that Mr. Danger, and he’ll be here each and every week with us at ringside. And tonight, we’ve got a huge matchup, as bitter enemies are forced to team up in tonight’s main event for a chance to compete for the GCW World Title! Danger- That’s right, and you’d better believe there’s a lot on the line tonight. Not just pride, but a chance to get in to the ACWF match. Caldera is making everyone go through hell just to get a shot at the title in the most deadly match there is, and you’d better believe each and every one of those spots is worth the price you pay. Bryan- Jason Dread and The Jhub, who just fought each other two weeks ago, team up to take on Octavian and Tristain Mayhem, and you’d better believe those two are going to have a hard time getting along. But first off, speaking of Mayhem, President Caldera has promised to start this show by confronting his former friend, and I’m going to turn things over to him now! With that, “Established” cues up and the crowd explodes in cheers. President Caldera, clad in one of this fantastic suits and looking well groomed, walks quickly onto the stage and heads for the ring without much fuss. Andrews- Please welcome the President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! Caldera seems entirely focused on his message and pays little attention to the crowd as he steps through the ropes and accepts a microphone from Joey Andrews. With that he signals to cut his music and begins immediately. Caldera- Before I ask that Tristain Mayhem join me in the ring… The fans boo at the mention of Mayhem’s name. Caldera- …I’d like to explain exactly what’s going through my mind right now. You see, when I first met Tristain Mayhem, he was introduced to me by a scout who had watched him beat up a few rowdy drunks as the bouncer of a night club. Right away I could see he was going to be a star… He just had the look. There aren’t many people who have that look, the look of a superstar, but Tristain had it. He’s always carried himself like a star, and there’s nothing wrong with that. I always watched the new talent in the ring to gauge their progress, but I watched Tristain because I simply enjoyed doing it. And so, when he finally got himself in a position to challenge Octavian for the World Title, I knew I had a leader for the next generation of the company. The crowd has grown quiet, listening carefully to Caldera, whose eyes have fallen to the mat. Caldera- But then something happened. I gave Tristain the ball, I have him my support… Most importantly, I have him the time he needed to do what he was destined to do. Most people in GCW don’t get many chances at the World Title. And if you don’t win it the first time, you usually don’t get a second chance. But Mayhem got a second and third chance, and he won it like I knew he could. But when he did, something inside him changed. I don’t know what that was, which is what I intend to ask him, but for some reason he decided the GCW World Title wasn’t worth a thing to him. I invested three months of time, millions of dollars, and countless hours in preparation, tailoring this company to him. If he had kept going in the right direction, if he had listened to me, the entire company would have profited. Instead, GCW started spinning downward, weakening it to a point that allowed The Dark Messiah to kill it, all because he wanted to run off and tag team with his girlfriend. Well, needless to say, I’m a little upset about it, and Tristain, you’d better get your ass out here and explain yourself to my face. Caldera isn’t forced to wait as “My Way” by Sid Vicious hits instantly. The crowd instantly rises up in boos as Tristain Mayhem slowly saunters out onto the stage. Clad in a messy pair of black jeans and a plain white wife beater, Mayhem seems dressed rather casually, even for a wrestling show. Mayhem has a cocky smile on his face as he walks slowly to the ring, making Caldera simmer as he waits. Mayhem pauses before reaching the ring, stopping to admire an attractive young woman in the second row. Mayhem finally shakes his head and slides into the ring, standing directly in front of Caldera. Mayhem pulls a microphone out of his back pocket and begins to speak, just inches from Caldera’s face. Mayhem- So… there’s something you wanted? Caldera smirks, glaring at Mayhem. Caldera- I want an explanation. I want to know what the hell happened to you. Mayhem- What happened to me? What the hell happened to you? You probably don’t realize it, Mr. C, but you’re the one who’s changed, not me. I’m the same Sleazy T I’ve always been. I never once claimed to be the savior of your little company, and I never wanted to be the guy with the ball. Sure, I wanted the world title, but that’s mostly because I knew it would get me laid! The crowd boos and Caldera gives Mayhem a scornful look, cringing at his crudeness. Mayhem- Now, I should say that you were right about the women, they didn’t do anything but drag me down. My policy now is a twenty four hour cap on all my relationships. But that’s about the only thing in my life that’s changed. I started with your company because you gave me good money. I kept going because Sleazy T always likes to be on top. And now I’m back because I felt like I needed a hobby. Caldera sneers at Mayhem, balling his fists in anger. The crowd begins to stir as Caldera seems prepared to attack, but Mayhem doesn’t stop. Mayhem- Frankly, you call me your friend, you call me someone close to you, but I was never any of those things. I’ve always been in this for one person, ME. I really don’t care about you, I don’t care about this company, and I sure as hell don’t care about these fans! The Hartford crowd is on its feet not, screaming obscenities at Mayhem. Mayhem simply smiles, enjoying the intense reaction. Caldera- But you’re forgetting that you wouldn’t even be in the position to say these things without me, Tristain. You’ve got the money now, sure, you don’t need this job. But without the fame and fortune you got from me, this company, and these fans, you would still just be beating up eighteen year old kids with fake IDs at the bar! Mayhem- You’re giving yourself a little too much credit, Steve. I am who I am, and that’s never gonna change. You sure as hell didn’t make me. You didn’t give me anything. Hell, I made you! I made this company what it is today by giving these people a rivalry they could enjoy watching. And then, when I got tired of it, I left to go do things my way. Yeah, I left you high and dry. But frankly, Steve… I don’t give a damn. Caldera shakes his head in amazement, finding himself unable to speak. Mayhem begins backing away towards the ropes, motioning to leave, but continues speaking. Mayhem- Now, as long as we’re having this little soap opera here, I want to clear something up about tonight. Frankly, I don’t give a damn about teaming with Octavian, or ACWF, for that matter. You see, I’ve been watching the tapes of some ACWF matches in my spare time, and it seems they’re kinda violent. Now, one of these days soon I’m gonna get my hands back on that GCW World Title, but ACWF isn’t the way I had in mind. So, with all due respect, Steve, you can just count me out of tonight’s main event, because I don’t want to be in ACWF. Mayhem smirks and steps out of the ropes, turning his back on the boss and leaving. Caldera is furious and approaches the ropes, leaning out at Mayhem as the crowd boos furiously. Caldera- DON’T YOU TURN YOUR BACK ON ME, YOU SON OF A BITCH! Caldera screams the words with startling intensity, prompting the crowd to cheer and Mayhem to freeze. Mayhem spins and stares back at Caldera, surprised and taken aback. Caldera- You’re scheduled to wrestle tonight, and damnit you’re going to wrestle tonight! If you don’t wrestle in the main event tonight, I’ll suspend your ass for all of eternity. You say you don’t need my money? Fine, I don’t need it either. But I have a feeling you’re not going to be happy sitting back collecting those checks while the show goes on happily without you. Second, you’d better be damned sure this isn’t over between us. I’m going to be watching you every step of the way from now on, Tristain, and if you take just one step out of line, JUST ONE, I’ll be there to put you back there… personally. Established hits again as Caldera gives Mayhem an intense stare. The crowd cheers wildly, sneering at Mayhem as he walks by in frustration. His frown finally disappears by the time he reaches the stage and he turns to give Caldera one more instigating grin before heading to the back. Bryan- Wow! What a confrontation! Danger- You said it, JB. I was there every step of the way back then, and no matter what Mayhem wants us to think, he and Caldera were close. And Caldera had high hopes for that kid. Now he’s just flushing it all down the toilet. Bryan- Well, he’s certainly amoral, and he made it quite clear tonight that he’s just out for himself. Whatever happened to change Tristain Mayhem we may never know, but Steven Caldera is sure as hell taking this very personally! ---- Toad vs. NBK Out With the Old hits in the Hartford Civic Center, and out comes the Hardcore Champion, Toad! The crowd erupts in cheers as the Toad emerges onto the stage, holding his Hardcore title in the air for all to see. He gives the crowd an odd glare, catching a few fans at ringside off guard. Toad slides into the ring and raises his Hardcore belt again, which is on the line due to the 24/7 rule! Unforgiven II hits next, and NBK heads out on stage. The crowd gives NBK a mixed reaction as he charges the ring and immediately begins tearing into Toad! Toad drops his belt in the corner and turns to NBK. NBK catches Toad off guard and begins pummeling him into the corner. Toad begins fighting back, and the two come to a stalemate of right hands. Toad breaks it with a blatant kick below the belt, followed quickly by a DDT. Toad covers, but the official refuses to count following the dirty move! Toad stands and threatens the official, allowing NBK to roll him up from behind for a fast two count! Toad kicks out and gets back to his feet, nailing NBK right between the eyes with a hard elbow. Toad whips NBK into the ropes and catches him with an impressive power slam. NBK slowly gets up once again, but Toad delivers a scoop slam back to the mat. Toad hops onto the second turnbuckle and nails NBK with a perfect leg drop, earning a two count. Toad pulls NBK back up and kicks him in the gut before hitting the ropes and attempting a swinging neckbreaker. But NBK powers him back into a back suplex, garnering a two count and an impressed roar from the crowd. NBK sends Toad into the corner and follows it in with a huge clothesline. NBK then immediately hooks Toad up and gives him a belly to belly suplex out of the corner for a two count. Toad staggers back up and NBK tosses him into the ropes. Toad comes back with a big cross body block, but NBK somehow catches him and delivers a big fallaway slam for another near fall! NBK collects Toad and hooks him from behind, going for a reverse suplex, but Toad displays impressive power of his own by lifting NBK up off his feet and tossing him to the mat! NBK quickly springs back up but Toad begins pummeling him with right hands. Toad whips NBK but NBK counters. Toad attempts a clothesline but NBK counters into a huge chokeslam! NBK picks up Toad and sets him on the top rope, climbing to attempt a 38 Special! The crowd cheers along as NBK drops for the move, but Toad holds on to the top rope and NBK falls hard to the mat below. NBK slowly gets to his feet as Toad hops off, and NBK charges back at him. Toad rises back up with his Hardcore title belt in his hands, and nails NBK right in the face! NBK goes down and does not move as Toad stares blankly down at him. The official immediately calls for the bell as Toad slowly begins walking back up the ramp. Winner: By Disqualification, Natural Born Killer ---- Not a Game Toad raises his title belt in the air as a confused crowd boos the outcome. The show fades to the backstage area as NBK slowly gets back to his feet, and his arm is raised. LeStatt Knight- Si Chicita, I'll be home Manana. TeQuiero Mucho Mami, Adios. LeStatt Knight slams his cell phone shut and places it in his coat pocket as he walks through the backstags area. He seems to be moving his way towards his locker room. As he turns a corner and bumps into a man that's just standing there, up against the wall. LeStatt Knight- What the hell are you ... LeStatt's voice trails off as he sees Mike Hardy, his opponent for later on that night, standing in front of him. Mike doesn't even acknowledge Knight, and Knight's expression changes from frustration, to a small grin. LeStatt Knight- Miguel, que paso ? Mike Hardy- (Distraught look on his face)......More than you know Chico..... Mike's eye flinches as he turns his head back into his dazed expression. Knight raises an eyebrow at the very odd demeanor that Mike is carrying himself. The two never had a history, and tonight marks their first match against each other ever, but still Knight knew he was different. LeStatt Knight- You seem a bit out of it amigo, did O'Neil scramble your brains more than we know ? Knight waits for a response that he never gets. His face tightens, as he is already getting irritated with this little game that Mike is playing. LeStatt Knight- Well whatever it is Mikey, you had better snap out of it ... because tonight you face something much worse than some Messiah... Sweet dreams. Mike Hardy- Dreams.....I can't dream.....My fate can't rely on what my frail mind thinks upon.....There's no more time for thinking Esse’....No more time for games..... Mike turns to the wall as if he just shut off all means of conversation with LeStatt. He solemnly makes his way past LeStatt without batting a second glance. As Mike walks away from Knight, LK moves some loose strands of hair from his face. He takes in a deep breath, and chuckles a bit to himself, before thinking a bit out loud. LeStatt Knight- You're right Hardy ... this won't be a game. ---- Ben Hates Tom Brady WorldWide returns and My Own Summer hits in the Hartford Civic Center, sending the crowd to its feet! The fans explode with easily the loudest reaction of the night as The Jhub marches on stage, smiling proudly and sporting a brand new official Tom Brady Patriots jersey. The Connecticut fans scream that much louder in response to The Jhub’s support for his home state, as he slides into the ring and climbs onto the ropes to pose. Andrews- Please welcome to the ring, from Stamford, Connecticut, The Jhub!!! The Jhub smiles at Andrews and snatches the microphone away from him, before enthusiastically addressing the crowd. Jhub- HOW ‘BOUT ‘DEM PATRIOTS?! The crowd goes wild in response, as The Jhub proudly surveys the fans, pointing out a few who are also sporting their Patriots jerseys this evening. Jhub- You know, I was sitting in the back, twiddling my thumbs and waiting for the main event to roll around, when I decided I really belong out here with my hometown crowd! The fans erupt once again, eating up every word. Jhub- Tonight I’m teaming up with one Jason Dread to take on Octavian and Tristain Mayhem. Now, maybe it’s just me, but I don’t see this as being much of a contest. I mean, we’re fighting for a spot in The Jhub’s match, we’re in The Jhub’s state, and we’re facing two of The Jhub’s bitches… Seems like the deck is stacked in my favor! The crowd laughs mightily as The Jhub chuckles to himself. Jhub- Now, it goes without saying that I’ve come here to win, and I guarantee each and every one of you fine Patriot faithful that The Jhub is going to be in ACWF Six as of tonight! Now Jason, I know you’re my partner in all this and you want a shot too, but you’ll get yours next week. I’m simply not a patient man, and I just feel like giving back to my community by honoring the New England Patriots with a Super Bowl win of my own… right here tonight in HARTFORD! The crowd goes wild as My Own Summer hits again and The Jhub hops onto the top turnbuckle to pose for the fans once more. He then steps through the ropes and begins signing autographs for the fans at ringside. Danger- Can he really do that? Just come out here and eat up time whenever he wants? I swear, in my day, we only had one microphone and I was the only one allowed to hold it. Bryan- Well, he loves his home state, Mr. Danger, and these people love him. And The Jhub guaranteed a win here tonight! You like his chances? Danger- Actually, yeah, there’s no way Mayhem and Octavian are going to get along tonight! ---- The Contest, Part I The camera opens to a quiet shot of Steven Caldera’s secretary Janice, a pretty young blonde of about twenty five. She sits at a modest desk outside a large door with the words ‘President Steven Caldera’ inscribed on its face. Janice is studiously going over some paperwork while talking to someone on the phone, obviously quite busy. Too busy, in fact, to notice a large cardboard box sliding into view behind her. From around the corner comes Twisted, pressed tightly against the wall and pushing his box in front of him with his foot. The crowd cheers as they watch Twizzy stealthily sneaking behind Janice, eyeing Caldera’s office door with determination. Janice does not seem to notice him as he reaches out for the handle and swings the door open. Danger- Oh, what is this goof up to now? Bryan- Well, obviously Caldera placed his secretary out there to stop some unwanted visitors, but she’s not doing a very good job of that tonight! Twizzy sneaks the box inside and steps through the door, quietly closing it behind him. The camera switches to the interior of the office, where Twizzy finally relaxes. Twisted- Hey Caldy! Twisted looks around for a moment but can’t see Caldera. Then he looks over at Caldera’s desk and notices someone is sitting the chair with their back turned to him. He sets the box down and takes a step forward. Twisted- You’re not still mad at me about the whole petting zoo in GCW Headquarters idea are you? The figure in the chair still doesn’t turn around. Twisted- Look, I know I said… The chair suddenly swings around and Twizz is now staring at the massive, grinning Toxic. Toxic- Hey buddy. Twisted’s shock quickly turns to disdain. Twisted- One, I’m not your buddy. And two, what the hell did you do with my roommate. Toxic- Hey ‘roid junkie, if your brain was even half functional you’d realize that Caldera always seems to have other matters to deal with. So to answer your question I don’t really know where the hell he is. Twisted- Well it’d be nice to stay and talk, but you’re kind of a jackass. I’m going to find Caldy. Toxic- No you’re not. Toxic stands up out of the chair and rolls his sleeves up. Toxic- We’ve got unfinished business. Twisted raises an eyebrow. Toxic- Well I guess from my point of view it could be finished, seeing as how the last time you saw me it was from a stretcher, looking twenty feet up. Twisted scowls as he thinks back to just over a year and half ago. Twisted- Yeah well, at least I’m not a smelly goat-monger! Oh burn! Toxic- You can joke around all you want but the fact is I wouldn’t bother trying to impress Caldera, because you don’t have what it takes to make it through ACWF anyways. Twisted- At least I can impress people. Toxic takes a step forward and gets right in Twizz’s face. Toxic- I think Caldera would have no choice but to be impressed if I left you in a bloody heap right now. Twisted- I doubt it. What would impress him is if you could sell more T-shirts than me! Toxic looks on dumbfounded as Twizz opens the box he carried into to reveal it’s full of GCW apparel. Twisted- So what’d you say? You up to the challenge? Toxic- Yeah, right. Like I need these grubby fans in here touching me with their greasy hands, while they fumble around in their pockets for money. It’s ridiculous! Caldera- Actually, I think it’s a great idea. Toxic and Twizz turn around to see Caldera standing in the doorway. Toxic- You probably would. You’ve got the business sense of a heroin addict. How long is it going to take you to run GCW into the ground this time? Caldera smirks and steps forward. Caldera- Let me put it to you this way Toxic…if you want to be anywhere near the ring once ACWF VI goes down, I suggest you ask Twizzy here to lend you some shirts. Because frankly, I find this to be a very tough task, and anyone man enough to sell a GCW t-shirt is man enough to compete in ACWF. Now get the hell out of here. Caldera walks away leaving Toxic standing in disbelief. Twizzy begins jumping up and down in joy as Toxic shakes his head, and the camera returns to ringside. ---- Mike Hardy vs. LeStatt Knight Broken Promises cues up, and Mike Hardy emerges onto the stage sporting a much different look from last week. His wrestling tights are gone, replaced now by a tattered pair of jeans. He’s shirtless, showing off his surprisingly well built upper body, and his long black hair is clearly in need of grooming. He slowly walks to the ring to a subdued reaction as the fans observe his odd appearance. Hardy slides into the ring with an emotionless face, waiting patiently for his opponent. O’Fortuna hits next, and the crowd begins to boo LeStatt Knight. Knight gets an unexpectedly loud reaction as he slides into the ring, only to be immediately met with a series of hard kicks to the head from Hardy. Hardy picks Knight up and begins hammering him with careless right hands, before whipping him into the ropes. Hardy immediately turns and springboards off the second rope, catching Knight with a huge twisting senton and sending both men to the mat. The crowd oohs as Hardy gets back to his feet and waits for Knight, nailing him with a huge leaping dropkick, followed by a diving headbutt. Knight rolls out of the ring to regain his bearings, but Hardy takes off with a suicidal leap over the top rope, nailing Knight with a 360 corkscrew splash! As the crowd continues cheering Hardy on, Hardy begins clutching his back in obvious pain. Knight seems stoic but slowed as Hardy rolls him back into the ring. Hardy hops onto the top turnbuckle and takes off with an enormous moonsault, but Knight rolls out of the way and Hardy lands hard on his midsection. Knight gets back to his feet and scoops Hardy up recklessly, driving him down with a double underhook backbreaker. Hardy clutches his back in pain as Knight delivers a side backbreaker for a two count. Knight whips Hardy hard into the turnbuckle, and Hardy drops to the mat. Knight picks Hardy up for a powerbomb, but Hardy counters into a hurricanrana for a one count. Knight gets back up only to get a chinbreaker delivered over Hardy’s head. Hardy pulls himself back to the top rope and dives off with another corkscrew splash, nailing Knight perfectly but causing Hardy to clutch his back further. Knight slowly manages to get up to his feet, and Hardy goes for the Bottom Feeder! Knight counters by tossing Hardy off his shoulders high into the air, but Hardy catches Knight on the way down with a big bulldog, landing hard on his back yet again. Hardy can’t seem to get back to his feet again, and Knight rises first. Knight picks Hardy up and whips him into the ropes. Hardy counters with yet another hurricanrana, but doesn’t have the strength to pull Knight down. Knight stays on his feet and pulls Hardy back onto his shoulders, driving him into the mat with a vicious diving powerbomb! Hardy is clearly out cold, and Knight covers him for the elementary three count! Knight is moving on, and Hardy is out cold in the ring. Winner- Via Pinfall, LeStatt Knight Bryan- Wow! Big win there for LeStatt Knight, who’s moving on to face NBK in the finals for the United States title, but what the hell got into Mike Hardy tonight? Danger- I dunno, JB. He comes out looking like a slob, he wrestles like he’s got a death wish, and ends up beating himself up so bad that Knight put him away with relative ease. Bryan- Well, he showed guts just being in this match after the beating he took last week against The Dark Messiah, but Hardy clearly wasn’t himself here tonight. And we’ve all seen that President Caldera is blaming himself for Hardy’s recent troubles, so we’re gonna have to wait and see how he takes this turn of events. ---- Allied Chaos WorldWide returns and we see Steven Caldera in his office, furiously signing some paperwork. He glances up as his secretary buzzes him on the intercom. He ignores the call at first, but when it rings again he acknowledges, obviously irritated. Caldera- Yes? The secretary's voice responds, slightly muffled by static through the intercom. Secretary- Mr. Niker and Mr. Pearce here to see you sir. Caldera tosses his pen down and leans back in his chair, hands clasping behind his head... Caldera- Send them in. Moments later the door opens and in enter Connor Pearce and Kyle Niker, GCW's newest prospects. Caldera- Ahh, Mr. Niker. And Mr. Pearce. So this is the infamous Allied Chaos. Both men grin widely. Connor sticks his hand out, but when Caldera does not take it, he idly wipes it off on his pant leg. Caldera- Listen guys, let’s make this quick. I approved the company’s verbal agreement with you, but I heard about your request for a contract, and I’m still not sure you’re quite ready for the big time. The collective faces of Allied Chaos register utter shock. Obviously they are not used to handling this type of rejection. Connor looks close to tears, but Kyle manages to stammer out a response. Kyle- B-b-but sir! I think we are exactly what GCW needs. A shot in the arm of pure, unadulterated charisma. A genuine guarantee of increased ratings and- Caldera cuts Niker off with a dismissive wave of his hand. Caldera- Yes, yes, yes. I've heard it all before. That's just it guys. It's not original. In GCW you gotta be on the cutting edge. I mean look at you guys. Look at your clothes. Your hair. Its been done. AC check themselves over quickly, their faces drooping more with every passing moment. This is not the warm welcome they envisioned. Caldera- The one thing you guys had going for you...I don't even see her here. This hot young valet... what's her name...Mallory? Connor- Oh, her. Kyle- She's bringing up our luggage. She should be here pretty soon. Caldera- What? Come on, guys, I don’t have time for this… Kyle and Connor turn on each other. Kyle- I told you this was a bad idea. Connor- You told me!? You were the one who insisted I get this stupid haircut before coming up here. Kyle- Trust me, you needed it, hippy freak! Connor- Fascist fool! Caldera merely closes his eyes and rubs his temples as the two bicker. Finally it is interrupted by the intercom buzzing again. Secretary- Mr. Caldera, there's a Mallory here to see you. Caldera- Sheesh… Send her in. The door opens to admit AC's loyal valet, Mallory. She is obviously regaining composure after having to cart the trio's luggage up to the office floor, but she is still stunning. She brushes her blonde hair out of her eyes and extends her hand to Caldera. Mallory- Mr. Caldera, what a pleasure it is to finally meet you in person. Caldera flashes a wide smile and accepts Mallory's hand... Caldera- And you, my dear. Although I have to admit, your boys are starting to get on my nerves. Kyle releases the chokehold he has on Connor and turns back to his would-be boss. Kyle- Mr. Caldera, please! Just give us one chance. Don't make us beg you! Please! Caldera grows more annoyed by the second. Caldera- Niker if there's one thing I can't stand its a man with no dignity. And by the looks of things you and your partner are a double dose! Now get out of my office before I call security! Caldera's hand reaches for a button, but is stopped by Mallory's hand on his arm. Mallory- Mr. Caldera, is that really necessary? Caldera seems more upset for a moment, but the naturally calming presence Mallory has brings him slowly down. Caldera- Well, I...its just. *Sigh* I'm sorry, Mallory. But lately with this Tristain Mayhem thing, and The Dark Messiah back, I have more important things to worry about than this. That’s why you’ve been talking to my assistants, they’re in charge of new talent. Mallory- Maybe Connor and Kyle could be of some help. Caldera- Those two? How? Mallory- Maybe they could take care of some of that business for you. Caldera, his mind quickly formulating a plan to get rid of his visitors, suddenly smiles, as if a lightbulb has gone off over his head. Caldera- I’ll tell you what, guys, I’ll get you a match next week right here on WorldWide. Would you like that? Kyle- Yeah! Connor- Of course! Caldera- All right. You guys just go sit someone quiet backstage and watch the rest of the show. Someone will give you a call and let you know who you’ll be facing next week. You just have to promise not to bother me for the rest of the night, all right? Mallory- I’ll see to that. Come on, you guys. Connor and Kyle leap high into the air and give each other a big high-five, before receiving a stiff shove from Mallory, ushering them out of the room. Caldera sighs, arcing an eyebrow as he watches Mallory step out of the room.
