WorldWide 136 Arizona Veterans Memorial Arena — Phoenix, Arizona ---- For Every Action... The scene opens in the Commissioner's office, his Segway parked in the corner of the room. He's examining a blackboard as he plans his booking for High Stakes. As it stands, he just has a bunch of question marks facing off opposite more question marks. The commissioner smiles, admiring his work as a booker. GBJ: I think it's missing something... Commissioner Garbage Bag chalks in another match between one set of question marks and another. GBJ: Perfect. Bagwell: Nah, perfect'd be if you had a framed portrait of yours truly hanging square in the center of that board. In walks GCW's Local Celebrity and Neighborhood Superhero unannounced. The Rajah of Ratings peers at Johnny's disaster of a game plan with a puzzled look. Bagwell: What the hell are you even trying to do, Johnny? GBJ: Oh, this? It's my latest brilliant idea. I call these Luck of the Draw matches--fitting for High Stakes, huh? Anyway, Triumph Frost and Tempest will end up being two of these question marks, and I'm going to randomly draw up these matches at High Stakes. I'm thinking we'll get four or five matches out of it, and two lucky competitors will be getting title shots just because of the way the chips land. Garbage Bag fixes one of the question marks that was sloppily drawn. GBJ: What do you think? Before answering, the Human Hype Machine makes it a point to study the chart thoroughly. He nods his head and scratches his chin as if he has even the slightest clue as to what GBJ's drawn. Bagwell: And which one of these question marks would wear a Yarmulke? Pointing to another oddly drawn mark. Bagwell: This one? Garbage Bag Johnny surveys the question marks and shrugs. GBJ: I have some folks in mind, but if you're looking for something a little more certain out of Big D at High Stakes, I can make sure he's not involved, if that sounds good. Bagwell: His involvement doesn't bother me. It's the dreidel up his ass that's got me on edge. You know what all that shit was about the other night?! Garbage Bag shrugs again. GBJ: Beats me, man. I've got bigger problems on my hands than Dan Black...like figuring out who interrupted the WorldWide broadcast last week or finding out who's behind these invoices. I got another one earlier today that was charged to someone named "JB". You know anything about that? Got any cousins or uncles or half-brothers or anything with those initials? Chris scratches his head, oddly clueless Bagwell: JB?! Doesn't ring a bell, buddy. Unless someone's trying to use your other alias, Johnny Bagwell. But I thought we laid that gimmick to rest months ago. He shrugs. Bagwell: I wouldn't worry about it. All that money's coming out of Caldera's pocket anyway. Heaven knows he doesn't need it, either. I heard he just recently made a donation to the Ben E. Teater Memorial Scholarship Fund. GBJ: I guess you're right. It isn't a big deal. At least not compared to someone shutting down the power during a World Title Match. That blackout cost us a lot of money, and it ruined the main event. GBJ scratches his chin. GBJ: Blackout, huh? If you do run into Dan Black today, you think you could maybe see if he knows anything about it? Chris smirks. Bagwell: I told you that little sonuvabitch was up to no good. He's bitching about trying to make a statement around here, so it'd totally be something he'd do. As if losing to Bryan Mayhem wasn't statement enough. Chris chuckles to himself, hoping for a laugh out of Johnny. But the Commissioner is drown in far to deep of thought to even acknowledge the joke. Bagwell: You at least know where that little dick is? GBJ: Still in my trousers. Garbage Bag Johnny catches himself just a moment too late and tugs on his collar. GBJ: Oh, you're talking about Big D? Haven't seen him. But I hear he's putting a band of goons together, so if you're thinking of going after him...just don't estimate the power of goons. They'll do about anything you tell them to, no matter how bad of an idea it is. The Master of Manipulation nods his head. Bagwell: This is a guy who spent the bulk of his career in matches with Axel Action. You really think I'm worried about what of goons he can rally up? I'll make sure Big D pays... Just as the Rajah is about to make as graceful an exit as his entrance, he peers back over at Johnny. Bagwell: A lot of questions on the board, tonight. Don't hurt yourself trying to get the answers. GBJ: Aye aye, cap'n, but don't worry about me. If I get in a pinch, I'll just speed off to safety on my Segway over there. Garbage Bag Johnny points at his vehicle of choice and Chris nods at it, not wanting to offend the commissioner's ride before leaving. After he does, Garbage Bag examines his doorknob, trying to remember if it was always gold. ---- Introduction The lights dim in the Arizona Veteran's Memorial Arena and the Phoenix crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the fans in Phoenix go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue as the cameraman selects some homemade signs to display.
