AXEL Action Biography Real Name AXEL Action Nicknames "The Angel of Vengeance" Hometown Tokyo, Japan by way of Hollywood, Florida Birthday 7/26/81 Height 6'2 Weight 225 pounds Fed Titles GCW Television Championship (x1), GCW United States Championship (x1) Fed Awards The Clyde Fox Most Uber Unbelievable Glob of 2009 Award, GCW Most Improved Wrestler 2009, GCW Rising Star 2009 Biography [Under MAJOR reconstruction.] Other Feds UWF, XUW, OWA, Circle of Pride, INA/I3C Other Fed Titles UWF Television Champion(x2), OWA World Heavyweight Champion, OWA World Tag Team Champion, INA/I3C Champion(x3) Other Fed Awards INA/I3C Hall of Fame(Class of 2009), Will probably be nominated for Asshole of the Year in Circle of Pride(Alongside James von Doom.) Strengths Very proficient with strikes, can TKO his opponent with even the simplest of forearm smashes. Is absolutely deadly with submission holds, his favorites being the STF, his One Winged Angel, and his specialty submission: Judgment Day. Weaknesses Has the tendency to put his own body and life on the line to win his matches, with no care of possibly injuring himself. Will almost always go high risk if there is an opportunity to connect. Wrestling Style Technical/Submission/High-flying/Brawler/Hardcore Finisher Move Total Sacrilege Finisher Description It's a normal, everyday Cutter. Although, it's performed more like the RKO. He usually plants the Cutter right off of the Unholy Trinity. Alhough he has been known to nail it out of nowhere as well. Finisher Setup Move The Unholy Trinity WW Entrance [I won't have a set entrance for a bit. He then backs up, gets a full head of steam and plants a Tiger Wall Flip. Finally, he backs away once more, and runs in with a Corner Yakuza Kick(ala Kawada/MVP) that sends the opponent stumbling out of the corner.
