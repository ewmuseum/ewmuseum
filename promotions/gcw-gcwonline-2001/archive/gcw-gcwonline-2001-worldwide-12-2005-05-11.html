GCW — Card

WorldWide 12, 11 May 2005

11 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The lights dim in the arena, and the Lisbon crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The Portuguese crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “ADDICT” and “Esta Tarde Destino de Hardy” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Lisbon, Portugal” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: Hello, everyone, and welcome to GCW WorldWide! I’m James Bryan coming to you from beautiful Portugal for another exciting GCW event as we continue the Road to Warpath! Tonight we have an enormous show on tabs, and it’s made all that more special by the presence of the man seated here at ringside to fill in on color duty this evening! GCW Hall of Famer Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins has been kind enough to offer his services, and he’ll be adding his views to the show all evening!

Rollins: Thanks, JB, I just couldn’t resist the chance to get back in GCW. I’ve got a lot of lost time to make up for and I just felt this show needed a real Renegade feel, and what better way to get myself on TV all night than to sit right here next to you at ringside?

Bryan: Well, certainly a lot of questions have arisen regarding your agenda after you came in and challenged The Dark Messiah last week, and he is in action here tonight against Mike Hardy!

Rollins: Last time I saw Hardy, I was world champion and he was losing in the opening match every night. I’ve got to say that I’m surprised with the little shrimp, but we’ll see what he’s really made of tonight. Frankly, I don’t see what all the hype is about. You people don’t realize that Steven Caldera can orchestrate whatever events he wants, and Mike Hardy is nothing more than Caldera’s pawn. I think that’s going to come out tonight when the Messiah annihilates Hardy right here in this ring.

Bryan: Well, that remains to be seen… But what’s worse, the GCW World Champion Jason Dread is stuck in a battle royal, pitting him against Alex Blaq, Luke Hanson, Mr. Danger AND Octavian himself! Rich, what are Dread’s odds tonight?

Rollins: Zero. Don’t get me wrong, I’m not that impressed with Octavian or anyone in his little clique, especially that relic Mr. Danger, but it’s four on one here, and Dread’s seriously hurt. I mean, the man’s career is in serious jeopardy. Frankly, I don’t even see Dread surviving to the Coliseum Cage match in two weeks at Warpath.

Bryan: A tremendous challenge for Dread here tonight, plus lots more as GCW continues the Road to Warpath!

He sat alone in the back, somewhere dark, wrapped up alone in his pain and misery. The last couple days had been hell, and he definitely looked worse for wear. His right wrist in a cast, lip still swelled where it’d been split, one eye blackened. At least he’d had a chance to shave and get a shower, thank god for small blessings, right? Simple black tee shirt and jeans, again small favors, no match meant no changing into wrestling gear, no serious physical exertion. Frankly he wasn’t sure his ribs would allow any heavy breathing, not yet anyway.

God he felt stupid, getting into a bar fight all alone with a room full of regulars in a foreign country, yeah he was a damn Einstein. He really didn’t even know why he was actually here, should’ve completely taken the week off, but knowing Steven he probably would have suspended him for failing to appear, even though he didn’t have a match, bastard.

He’d never do something like that to his little pet Hardy. It just didn’t make any damn sense, a guy like Stevens dropped for a shmuck like Hardy. That’s probably the only reason he really showed up tonight, see if he could do something to the kid, make him suffer for trying to steal the spot light. The kid was supposed to be facing The Dark Messiah tonight, that should be fun to watch, Maxwell would kill him, especially if there was some incentive involved.

Yeah, now that was an idea, just a few weeks ago Maxwell proved he could still be the old TDM, the sickest bastard ever to grace a wrestling ring. He just needed something special to forgo all the hip tossing and head locking. Fifty grand would probably do the trick…

Michael Stevens: Ugh.

God it hurt trying to stand up, but you’ve gotta do what you gotta do. He was going to find Maxwell, and make sure the monster would pull out all the sick stops to maim Hardy in every way possible. Yeah the kid was going to pay, and big time.

Walking away, he never noticed the two figures standing in the shadows watching. After he’d disappeared around a corner, Allied Chaos, Kyle Niker, and Conner Pearce, stepped out.

Conner Pearce: Do you think he’ll do it?

Kyle Niker: I dunno buddy, but hell we can ask right?

Conner Pearce: Yeah, it’s worth a shot.

Kyle Niker: I say we wait, impress him in our matches tonight before we ask.

Conner Pearce: Sounds like a plan.

Both: YES!

The Two high five as the scene cuts away.

El Rayo Azul vs. Kyle Niker

Azul is already in the ring, waiting patiently for Allied Chaos to arrive. Their music finally hits and the duo appear on stage, jumping excitedly as they head for the ring. Pearce pats Niker on the back in a show of confidence, and the two execute a perfect leaping hi-five before Niker slides into the ring.

Niker gets to his feet and hops onto the turnbuckle, posing for the crowd. His music fades and he turns to Azul, taken aback by his luchador attire. Niker turns to Pearce on the outside and joins him in laughing at Azul, who simply stands at the ready in his corner. The bell sounds and Niker swaggers towards Azul, laughing and poking fun at him. Niker finally reaches Azul and studies him up and down, snickering at his outlandish attire.

Niker finally rears back and slaps Azul in his masked face, prompting the crowd to boo wildly at the show of disrespect. Azul does not respond, merely remaining at the ready. Niker offers his face to Azul, goading him to retaliate. Azul simply shakes his head, and Niker responds with a hard right hand! But Azul dodges the blow with shocking speed and slips behind Niker.

Surprised, Niker spins around and finds himself hooked in a front facelock. Azul lifts Niker high in the air for a suplex, but backs up a few steps and drops forward, planting Niker on top of his head with a brutal DDT! The crowd cheers with enthusiasm as Azul nimbly hops to the top rope and measures Niker, who lies motionless towards the corner. Azul extends into the air and takes off with a scintillating 450 splash with a twist! He nails the move he calls Torcer Dar Vuelta, and Niker is out! Referee Fellows drops for the elementary count!

1… 2… 3! Azul wins in quick and decisive fashion in his GCW debut!

Winner: El Rayo Azul II

The crowd stands and applauds Azul as he slides back out of the ring and heads for the back without much celebration. Pearce climbs into the ring and examines his fallen buddy, who has yet to move following the quick offense from El Rayo Azul. Pearce shakes his head in disbelief as the show heads backstage once again.

The camera cuts to backstage, where Kevin Jacobs stands in front of a GCW backdrop, microphone in hand. To his right stands a man with a large, golden object sitting on his right shoulder. That object is the GCW World Title, and that man is Jason Dread! The crowd pops huge as Jacobs heads right into his interview.

Kevin Jacobs – Jason Dread, I know you've had a rough past week at the hands of the Praetorian Guard, and especially Gaius Octavian. With the injuries you sustained just a week ago, how are you feeling going into your match tonight?

Jason Dread – Obviously I'm not going to be at one hundred percent, but I'm very confident that I can perform as well as I ever have. Just because of the underhanded booking of Mr. Danger, I'm not going to sit tonight out just because the odds are not in my favor.

Jacobs – Because of Danger you sit in the seat of going against every fighting member of the Praetorian Guard in a Battle Royal tonight...are you the least bit worried about a possible four on one gang-up on you?

Dread pauses for a moment, finding the correct way to word what he says next.

Dread – Of course there's always the possibility of having all four of them go after me at one time, but Kevin, this is hardly the first time in my career to where I've been on the receiving end of having several men after me in one match. I survived ACWF to get the World Title, and I'll sure as hell survive tonight to once again show what "royal" asses Octavian and his usual gang of idiots really are.

Jacobs lets the pop from the crowd sink in and slowly die off before continue, giving a wry smile.

Jacobs – With the upcoming Coliseum Cage match at Warpath, you're faced with the situation of not knowing what exactly you're getting in to before you step into the ring for the match, while Octavian gets to actually design this match. Why exactly did you accept this proposal of Octavian's?

Dread – If you know your GCW history, Kevin, you could see that any time I've ever been in a match with Octavian, I've deftly handled him just as if he was another Dan Rivero.

The crowd gets a good laugh out of the requisite Rivero joke.

Dread – And I figured that now was the time to let Octavian have the advantage for a change. In matches where we've been even, he's always been at my mercy. Everything boils simply down to that I'm better than what he could ever be. The man can have all the money and power he wants, I'll stick to my god-given talent to get me by. As everyone can see...

Dread pats the World Title on his shoulder.

Dread - ...it's obviously worked.

Without another word Dread walks off past Kevin Jacobs. After standing motionless for a few seconds, he turns towards the camera and sends us off to commercial.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The show returns to find Steven Caldera sitting in his deluxe office. The walls are papered and the floor carpeted, rare amenities on the road in a wrestling promotion. Caldera’s desk is obviously portable but nonetheless impressive, and he sits behind it in a large leather chair, comfortably working on his ever-present paperwork. Across the room comes the subtle creaking of a wooden door, followed by a timid voice.

Hardy: Hi.

Mike Hardy steps into the room with his hands in his jean pockets. Caldera, wearing his usual Armani suit, eyes the somewhat less elegant Hardy in blue jeans and a plain white shirt. He sighs and drops his golden pen on the table, pushing himself out from behind the desk to approach Hardy. Hardy’s eyes lock onto Caldera with a firm but clearly unconfident stare, and Caldera stops at the corner of his desk, leaning against it as he studies his former student.

Caldera: Hi there, Mike. Can I do something for you, or did you just come here to tell me about how great you are again?

Hardy: No, nothing like that. I actually… came to apologize. Look, I don’t know what the hell got into me, but you were right. Spitty was just using me, and for whatever reason he decided to attack me rather than help me last week. Well, I see what was going on now, and I want to go back to the way things were. I liked the feeling of being able to do anything, but I guess Stevens was just trying to turn me against you. So I was just hoping that you might help me out with a couple of favors…

Caldera: Favors? You want me to help you after the things you said? Well, frankly, Mike, I’m glad you see Stevens for what he really is, even if he did have to beat some sense into you. But don’t come in here and assume you can just apologize. I learned a lesson with Michael Stevens, too. I tried to build him in my image, and he did that very well. But you have to do it the right way. And I will not help you win the GCW Title if you’re not going to do it the right way. Talk is cheap, Mike, apologies don’t do any good. You have to prove to me that you can do this.

Hardy’s eyes suddenly fill with confidence, and he speaks his following words with much greater conviction.

Hardy: I do deserve this.

Caldera: Fine. Then here’s what you need to do… Beat The Dark Messiah tonight. Don’t back down. Don’t just fight to survive, but fight to win back your honor. He stole your dignity away from you not too long ago, and now he’s the only man in this company right now that’s gotten the better of you. He’s a sick, sadistic, evil monster that will stop at nothing to hurt you. I say Mike Hardy can be the same! Meet him with equal intensity, Mike, don’t just try to stick and move. Don’t listen to them when they say you can’t compete with the elite. You know better than anyone that Mike Hardy is a world-class wrestler. And I know that you can ratchet up the intensity whenever you need to. Well, tonight, you need to. Don’t just beat Maxwell tonight, Mike. Destroy him. Brutalize him. Don’t just pin him, annihilate him. Show him absolutely no mercy. I want you to come back here with his blood on your hands. And when you do, Mike, when you destroy him, when you finish him off, then I’ll know you’re ready to take the next step.

Hardy, chest heaving with intensity as he listens to Caldera speaks, sneers with a sort of sinister pride.

Hardy: I’ll bring you his heart on a damned platter.

Caldera: Tonight, Mike, I want you to remember everything I’ve taught you. And there’s a lesson you should remember from Stevens, too; that you are nobody’s pawn, that you are NOT the lovable loser any more. You’re Mike Hardy, future champion of the world and the greatest damned athlete in the world. You wanted to gain an edge? Tonight you can do it. Go out there and meet the most dominant, evil man in wrestling and destroy him, Mike. Do that, and there’s nothing we can’t do together.

Hardy, without another word and with sudden ferocity, tears the door open and walks out to prepare. But he stops dead in his tracks as the red-haired, ragged looking Michael Stevens stands in his way. With a drugged, spiteful gleam in his eye, Stevens glares at Hardy, who stands at the ready.

Stevens: I just came to say… good luck.

Stevens sneers at Hardy, refusing to move. Hardy simply sneers back, speaking with a bilious tone.

Hardy: Just watch me tonight. What I do to Maxwell will only be a fraction of what I’m going to do to you.

The two continue to stare each other down as the shot fades away from the volatile scene, heading back to ringside.

Jonathan Borealis vs. Connor Pearce

Pearce and Niker emerge on stage for the second time tonight, heading down the ramp with considerably less enthusiasm. Niker holds his head in pain, walking gingerly while feeling the effects of his match with Azul earlier in the night. Pearce slides into the ring and hops up and down lightly, a little embarrassed for his partner and determined to change the team’s luck.

The arena hushes as a guitar can be heard over the PA, starting out loud and distorted but fading after a few seconds. The guitar almost fades out as it hits once, twice ...three more times. The stage explodes in a mix of fireworks, noise, and pure adrenaline as "Rabble Rouser" by KMFDM kicks in full. During the first verse, Jonathan Borealis makes his way down to the ring, stopping along the way to check out some for the better looking girls. As he gets into the ring, Jonathan stands in the middle and the music hits the chorus at which point he crosses his arms in an "X" in front of him, sticks up both middle fingers, and all the lights in the arena cut, leaving the ring doused in pitch black lit only from the background by hundreds of camera flashes.

"COMMANDEER!

OWN YOUR FEAR!

ASSOCIATE AND INTEGRATE!

A RABBLE ROUSER IN YOUR FACE!"

The lights come back up and Jonathan is ready to get the match started.

Pearce charges as the bell sounds, hitting Borealis in the back of the head with a stiff forearm. Borealis spins around but gets clubbed with a few more blows, and Pearce sends him to the ropes. Borealis bounces back with a shoulder block on the thicker, heavier Pearce, who staggers back a step but stays on his feet. Pearce then hits the ropes, but Borealis follows him and nails a cross body block, toppling both men over the top rope!

Borealis lands on the apron and rolls back into the ring with ease, as Pearce lands hard on the floor. Borealis waits patiently as Niker moves over to help Pearce to his feet. Pearce angrily rolls back into the ring and charges Borealis again, but this time Borealis scoops him up into a tilt-a-whirl, dropping him down into a piledriver! The crowd explodes in laughter, knowing the match is over with the execution of the Hecatomb.

Borealis stands with a wide, cocky smile, and arrogantly places a boot on Pearce’s chest. Flexing his arms to the crowd as the official makes the count, Borealis smiles at the fans. 1… 2… 3! Borealis gets another AC member with a quick win!

Winner: Jonathan Borealis

Borealis arrogantly steps away from Pearce and steps onto the turnbuckle, posing for the crowd again. The fans boo Borealis but mostly offer laughter for Pearce’s embarrassing loss. Borealis steps out of the ring and heads for the back, jawing with the fans at ringside as he leaves. Niker shakes his head painfully as he rolls into the ring to check on his fallen compatriot, and the show heads backstage once again.

The scene cuts to the back where LeStatt Knight is sitting in his dressing room, preparing for his match against Twizzy later on tonight. He is shirtless but has on some baggy black sweat pants and black athletic shoes. His hair is tied back neatly, showing off his Icy blue eyes that shimmer in the light above. A knock on the door interrupts his thoughts, and he stands up to answer it. He swings the door open and standing before him is a delivery man.

Knight: Who the hell are you?

Delivery man: Hi I am from FedEx, Mr. Abitua ?

Knight: Yeah what do you want ?

Delivery man: I have a package for you actually. (He pulls out a manila envelope) Here it is.

Knight takes it from his hands and looks at it.

Knight: Who is it from ?

Delivery man: Actually it doesn't say, just was instructed to come bring it to you.

Knight: Thanks.

Delivery man: Well if you'll just sign...

Knight slammed the locker room door in the guy's face before he could finish the rest of his sentence. Knight stares at the envelope all the way back to his seat, obviously a bit intrigued. He opens the envelope up and immediately his eyes grow large. His lower lip begins to tremble some, then his hands begin to shake. He lowers his head as his entire body begins to shake. He then drops the paper work, having it scatter on the floor beneath him. Then he lets out a roar into the air ... almost like some sort of wild animal. He then gets to his feet and rushes out of his locker room. Whatever was in that envelope was very important, and cut LeStatt very deep.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

“Do the Evolution” hits the house speakers and the fans start to boo immediately as Toxic makes his way down the ramp. Toxic steps into the ring and reaches for a mic as the music dies down.

Toxic – Well it seems almost fitting that at GCW’s first big show I took out one washed up wrestler, and at Warpath I’ll be doing the exact same thing.

The crowd boos.

Toxic – And maybe after Caldera quits wasting my time like this, I’ll finally get the title shot I deserve.

The metallic chords of “Life’s Sweet Drug” blast through the arena. The crowd leaps to their feet with cheers as Twisted walks out on stage.

Twisted - What are the ingredients for Toxic? Five parts ego, ten quarts fury, and nineteen gallons of stupidity. At Warpath I'm going to prove to you that I'm still at the top of my game!

Toxic – Cute. And I’m sorry, when was the last time you won a match? I think Dan Rivero has had more frequent winning streaks in his career.

Twizzy gets a little perturbed at the mention of his former Tag Team Championship partner

Twisted - Hey! Hey! Hey... Dan Rivero was a former Superstar in this promotion. He had more credibility, stamina, titles, integrity, one night stands, framed pictures of himself, water balloons, and gambling dept than you'll EVER have!

Toxic smirks. Maybe the old, arrogant side of Toxic is slowly coming back.

Toxic – Either way, after Warpath you’re going to be right back on his level…where you belong.

Twisted - I can promise you that as long as I have this little bottle...

Twizz holds up a bottle of Twistodium.

Twisted - ... I'll be better than you are, Tox. You're not about to crush my confidence, you Goat Monger!

Toxic – I don’t recall that little bottle helping you break your fall from the MegaTron two years ago. Oh and I guess that little bottle made the BIG difference in your ACWF match didn’t it?

Twisted - Here's a life lesson for you, kiddo. Sometimes you just don't take enough pills to get the job done.

The Co-Dependent turns to the audience and preaches.

Twisted - Remember, kids. Always take enough to finish off the dread... or the Octavian, or the Hardy, or the Jhub, et cetera.

The crowd cheers.

Toxic – Well I hope you won’t O.D. on what you’ll need to take me on at Warpath. In fact, I hope you’ve taken some in advance because I’m two seconds away from coming up their and giving you a little Warpath preview.

The MegaTron flickers on and the face of Steven Caldera appears on the screen.

Caldera – Easy now. We’re not going to be having a fight tonight. No I want you two to be perfectly healthy for your match at Warpath. But this needs to be settled, so I’m going to make your match no disqualifications.

The crowd erupts in cheers.

Caldera – But! If either of you lay a hand on each other before Warpath you’ll automatically forfeit your spot in the battle royal at Dangerous Games. Do I make myself clear?

The MegaTron shuts off and Toxic and Twizzy stare each other down.

Toxic – Consider your main event status permanently revoked after Warpath.

Bryan: Well, Renegade, it seems like we’re finally going to get some resolution to this long running feud between Toxic and Twisted in just two weeks at Warpath!

Rollins: It’s about damn time. And frankly, I’m excited to see if Toxic can finally take his rightful place at the top of the company. I’ve known this kid since he was in diapers, JB, and though he’ll never be as good as me, I think it’s his time to step up and embrace greatness.

United States Title Match – LeStatt Knight vs. Twizzy

O’Fortuna cues up and the crowd explodes in cheers! LeStatt Knight appears looking mentally worn as he staggers towards the ring. He gets halfway down the ramp he brushes past an angry Toxic, and the big man actually stops and turns back towards the ring. LeStatt slides under the ropes and drops his belt on the mat, uncharacteristically somber and gloomy.

The bell sounds as Toxic saunters back to ringside, and watches as Twizzy moves in on Knight. Twizzy hammers Knight with hard rights and lefts before sending him to the ropes. Knight bounces back and gets caught with a huge back body drop from Twizzy. Twizzy smiles and reproduces his Twistodium bottle from his pocket. He pops the top and dumps a few in his mouth, before recapping it and placing it in the corner. With a jolt, Twizzy explodes forward and drops Knight with a hard clothesline.

Twizzy quickly picks Knight up and hits a swinging neckbreaker, quickly covering Knight for a one count. Knight gets back up and throws a right, but Twizzy blocks it and nails Knight with a stiff headbutt between the eyes. Unfazed, Knight strikes back with a headbutt of his own. Equally unfazed, Twizzy cups his hands over Knight’s ears and screams out with a Rebel Yell!

Knight staggers back into the corner and Twizzy charges. Knight gets a boot up and kicks Twizzy in the face, staggering the addict. Knight charges out of the corner but gets caught in a powerslam! Twizzy hooks the leg for a two count. Both men power back to their feet, and Knight immediately hooks a side headlock on Twizzy. Twizzy backs into the ropes and shoves Knight to the other side. Knight bounces back and crushes Twizzy with a spinning heel kick, dropping the codependant!

Twizzy staggers back to his feet and Knight sends him through the air with a side belly to belly suplex. Knight pulls Twizzy right back up again and sends him into the turnbuckle. Knight charges in and nails a high leg clothesline, flipping backwards over the top rope! Knight lands on his feet as Twizzy staggers out of the corner and falls to the mat. Knight quickly hops to the top rope and takes off with a suicidal flying headbutt!

Knight nails the move and both men lie hurt. After a few moments they each begin crawling to opposite corners. Knight begins pulling himself up the ropes as Twizzy heads for his bottle of Twistodium! But he reaches the corner and finds his bottle nowhere to be seen. A confused Twizzy staggers to his feet and looks up the ramp, where Toxic is standing on the stage, smiling and shaking the small orange Twistodium bottle. Twizzy’s eyes grow wide and he moves to step out of the ring, but Knight spins him around from behind and plants him with a Knight Fall!

Knight makes the cover and gets the elementary three count! The crowd applauds but shouts in anger at Toxic, who simply sets the bottle down on the steel stage and turns to leave, laughing heartily. Knight is handed his belt but he remains stoic, staring out into the distance and ignoring the official as he attempts to raise his hand…

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WorldWide returns to Portugal, and we find a close-up of The Jhub’s face, cheek bulging with some sort of unknown substance. With a big hand he shoves more of whatever he’s holding into his mouth and continues chewing. The camera zooms out and the crowd explodes, seeing The Jhub seated on Steven Caldera’s desk. Caldera looks disgusted with The Jhub’s chewing, presumably a mouthful of tobacco.

Caldera: Do you have to chew so much at one time?

Jhub: Oh, yeah! It’s the only way to get the best flavor.

Caldera: Honestly, you’re grossing me out. That’s really a disgusting habit.

The Jhub simply smiles and pulls out his pouch to get more. He produces a large packet of Big League Chew, and he shoves another large clump of pink bubble gum into his mouth.

Jhub: So, what’s the deal with our boy Dread tonight? You want me to take a tire iron to someone’s knees before the match?

Caldera: Nothing like that. But pass word on to LeStatt, will you? Dread’s hell-bent on doing this thing himself, but I have no interest in seeing the World Champion have his career ended here tonight. So you and LeStatt should be… vigilant.

Jhub: Vigilant?

Caldera: Vigilant.

Jhub: Gotcha.

The Jhub tilts his head back and pours what remains of his Big League Chew into his mouth, carelessly tossing the empty packet on Caldera’s office floor. The Jhub smiles and heads for the door, exiting without another word. But only a moment later, the door reopens and The Jhub pokes his head back into the room.

Jhub: You know, we could just assume Dread’s gonna die and you could give me the title…

Caldera: Just get out of here…

The Jhub shrugs, satisfied that he tried. The show then heads back to ringside, where the crowd waits in high anticipation for the next match…

Mike Hardy vs. The Dark Messiah

The crowd explodes in boos as The Dark Messiah appears on stage, with no fanfare or music to play him to the ring. His face is intense and his eyes furiously red, but he appears to be entirely focused on the match. Ignoring the shouts from the crowd, TDM enters the ring and takes a spot in the center, chest heaving as he awaits his opponent. The Hand That Feeds hits next and the crowd explodes! The reaction is unanimously cheerful as Mike Hardy appears on stage. Hardy, also looking entirely focused, heads to the ring with a light step and slides under the ropes.

Hardy is immediately attacked by TDM, who nails him with a hard forearm to the back. The bell sounds as Hardy battles his way to his feet, but TDM takes over with his overwhelming strength and powers Hardy back into the turnbuckle. TDM begins laying in sickening closed fists to the face, and after three blows Hardy’s nose begins to drip blood. Official Josh Briggs tries to slide in and push TDM out of the corner, but TDM violently shoves him away and continues assaulting Hardy.

TDM whips Hardy with incredible force to the opposite corner, but Hardy drops and slides, stopping his momentum with a boot on the bottom turnbuckle. Hardy springs back up and charges, but TDM meets him in the center of the ring with a vile clothesline! Hardy flies head over heels and lands in a heap, and TDM lifts him right up off the mat. Hardy’s nose continues to bleed as TDM shoves him back into the corner and continues to wail on Hardy’s face.

TDM brings down four more sharp closed fists, nailing Hardy in the eye. Hardy, sickened, drops to a knee and attempts to wrap TDM up. TDM simply powers Hardy up and throws him overhead with a belly to belly suplex. TDM climbs on top of Hardy and begins laying in more vicious fists to the face, apparently trying to blind Hardy with hard shots to the eyes and nose.

Hardy shoves TDM away and scrambles to his feet, finding a new energy. Hardy meets an approaching TDM with a forearm shot to the face, and begins pummeling TDM to the delight of the crowd! Hardy backs TDM into the ropes and whips him to the other side, but TDM bounces back with a vicious spear tackle! TDM drops Hardy but immediately pulls him back up, unceremoniously tossing him over the top rope to the floor. Briggs again attempts to halt TDM, but TDM shoves him away and follows Hardy to the floor.

TDM pulls Hardy up and sends him face first into the steel guardrail. Hardy bounces back and fires a hard elbow that hits TDM in the throat, staggering the big man. But TDM angrily grabs Hardy by the hair and viciously sends him face first into the steel turnbuckle post! Hardy bounces off hard, blood now dripping from his nose and a cut over his left eyebrow. TDM, eyes glowing with fury, dislodges the top section of the steel steps and hoists them over his head, stalking Hardy as he pulls himself up the announce table.

Hardy gets to a knee, but TDM brings the steps down directly on top of Hardy’s head, dropping him instantly. TDM tosses the steps aside and rolls Hardy onto the announce table, then follows him up. JB scrambles away as TDM pulls Hardy to his feet and hooks him for a DDT! But Hardy slithers out and slides behind TDM, hooking him and driving him through the table with a huge Waterfall! The crowd explodes as TDM and Hardy lie amongst the wreckage, and official Josh Briggs stands in the ring, pacing nervously but not counting.

TDM and Hardy remain motionless for several moments, and it’s Hardy that moves first! Hardy slowly manages to get to his knees and crawl to the apron, rolling under the bottom rope. Briggs instantly begins to count TDM out as he recovers on the floor, but Hardy grabs Briggs by the belt and breaks the count! Briggs shouts at Hardy in disbelief, as TDM rolls back into the ring and gets to his feet.

Hardy, face covered in blood, immediately meets TDM and begins hammering him with closed fists to the face, driving them into TDM’s brow with all his might. TDM staggers back into the corner, and Hardy nails him with three hard European uppercuts. Hardy gives TDM a snapmare out of the corner and hops onto the second turnbuckle, taking off with a dropkick to the back of TDM’s head!

TDM painfully gets to his feet and Hardy stalks towards him, the crowd cheering wildly. Hardy hooks TDM and nails a perfect vertical suplex, but does not go for a pin. Hardy pulls TDM back up and applies a rear waistlock, and he hoists TDM up into a German suplex! But Hardy stops TDM at the top of the move, then flips him over into a face-first powerbomb! The crowd explodes as Hardy nods confidently and gives TDM a kick to turn him over, then heads to the top rope!

Hardy nimbly hops to the top and smiles from beneath his crimson mask, finally taking off with a swanton bomb! Blood flies from his face through the air as he nails TDM with a perfectly executed move, and Hardy makes the cover! 1… 2… TDM KICKS OUT!

The crowd explodes in shock, certain Hardy had done enough for a three count. But Briggs, sweating and nervous, reluctantly informs Hardy that TDM escaped in time. Hardy sighs but then glares back down at TDM, where he scoops him up and throws him over the top rope towards the ramp! Hardy follows TDM out as TDM staggers up the ramp towards the stage. Hardy grabs TDM and swings back around towards the ring, but TDM reverses and whips Hardy hard into the apron. TDM charges and nails Hardy with a knee to the gut, dropping Hardy as the crowd explodes in boos.

TDM hits Hardy with a hard right, before lowering his shoulder into Hardy’s midsection and driving forward, spearing Hardy into the steel steps! The steps explode apart as TDM leaves Hardy in a heap, heading under the apron. TDM reaches under the curtain and produces a ladder! TDM begins pulling the ladder out, and the ladder just keeps coming… until he has produced what must be a twenty foot ladder! TDM slides the massive ladder into the ring and climbs in, setting the ladder up in the corner of the ring.

Hardy slides into the ring on the far side, and TDM moves to meet him. Hardy gets to his knees and fires a hard right to TDM’s gut, but TDM cuts him off with a straight kick to the face! TDM leaves a bloody footprint on the canvas as he drags Hardy towards the ladder! TDM drives Hardy face first into the steel ladder, and Hardy drops onto his back. TDM, smiling sadistically, nudges Hardy in the head with his boot as Hardy struggles to his feet. TDM hoists Hardy back to his feet again and tosses him over his shoulder, turning to the ladder!

With incredible power and balance, TDM begins to climb the ladder with Hardy slumped over his shoulder! The crowd stands and begins crying out wildly, begging Hardy to recover. TDM seems to be laughing evilly as he ascends higher and higher, nearing the top of the twenty foot ladder! TDM finally reaches the top step, and adding his height Hardy is now twenty five feet off the mat! TDM begins to maneuver Hardy, but Hardy slips off and lands with incredible agility on the top step on the opposite side of the ladder!

Hardy begins pummeling TDM with hard rights, landing them with every ounce of strength he has left. TDM begins to waver, losing his grip on the top of the ladder! The crowd explodes, knowing TDM is just inches from falling over twenty feet to the mat! Hardy rears back for one last big shot, but TDM blocks it and clasps a robotic grip over Hardy’s throat! TDM holds the choke on Hardy as Briggs shouts desperately for the two to return to the mat.

TDM pulls Hardy close to his face and begins screaming at him, before hooking him in a front facelock and lifting him off the ladder into a vertical suplex! The crowd screams in terror as TDM holds Hardy high off the ladder, straight in the air and perilously far from the canvas! TDM finally flings Hardy back, but Hardy somehow lands a step below TDM, keeping his balance by grasping TDM around the waist! The crowd goes crazy as Hardy manages to stay alive, but TDM rears back with a vicious elbow to the temple.

Hardy loses his grip on TDM, and TDM turns in place to face Hardy. TDM begins nailing Hardy with sickening fists to the face, splattering Hardy’s blood on impact. Hardy manages to hold onto TDM’s belt, desperately trying to hold on. TDM simply continues hammering Hardy with his bare knuckles, until Hardy finally stops him with a low blow! Hardy wavers for a moment, then with sudden and unexpected power, picks TDM up off his perch on the ladder and holds him horizontally in his arms!

The crowd explodes and cameras begin to flash as Hardy holds TDM in a precarious position! Hardy then suddenly jumps off the ladder, performing a moonsault with TDM still in his arms! Hardy flips backwards and flies down face first, landing with all his weight over the prone body of The Dark Messiah TWENTY FEET DOWN! The crowd explodes as the ring explodes on impact! All four top ropes snap free and one turnbuckle gives way, causing the entire ring to slump downward! Briggs loses his balance and falls to the mat as Hardy lies motionless on top of The Dark Messiah’s broken body!

The crowd continues to cheer raucously as Briggs crawls up the inclined ring where Hardy has the cover on TDM! With the entire arena in total chaos, Briggs makes the count!

TDM KICKS OUT!

The crowd erupts in complete disbelief, as TDM defiantly holds his left arm in the air, his shoulder narrowly rising off the mat. Briggs seems in shock as TDM actually begins to climb to his feet! Hardy, face stoic in utter astonishment, rests on his knees as TDM, face bloody but smiling nonetheless, stands before him like an indestructible monster.

Hardy sneers and gets to his feet, charging with a hard right hand. TDM remains unfazed, and abruptly slaps his hand around Hardy’s throat, driving him into the slanted mat with a vicious chokeslam! TDM quickly slides out of the ring next to the ladder and takes hold of one of the dislodged ropes. He wraps the rope around one of the steps and ties it back around the turnbuckle, securing the ladder in its upright position. He walks around the ring and collects another broken top rope, pulling it free from its remaining turnbuckle with frightening power.

TDM climbs back into the ring with the black rope and methodically wraps it around Hardy’s neck, tying it securely around his throat! TDM smiles an evil smile and heads to the ladder, stepping to the far side with his back to Hardy, and he begins to climb. He tosses the rope over the top of the ladder and begins his ascent, pulling with each step and dragging Hardy closer to the slightly inclined ladder!

TDM gets to the top of the ladder and begins pulling the rope over his shoulder, attempting to hang Hardy from the ladder! But Hardy begins to climb with each tug, trying to reduce the pressure on his neck. Still bleeding badly and gasping for breath, Hardy tries to keep up with TDM as he pulls the rope tighter and tighter, trying to choke the life out of Hardy!

Hardy finally nears the top, and TDM remains with his back to the ring, pulling with all his might. Hardy suddenly bursts upward with sudden speed, leaping to the top step! TDM, confused at the sudden slack, has no time to react as Hardy grabs the hanging rope and tosses it around TDM’s neck! Hardy pulls with all his might, choking TDM with all his might! Hardy turns for better leverage, and the two begin pulling on opposite ends of the rope, choking on another in a deadly test of strength!

Hardy finally jerks forward with astonishing power, flipping TDM over into a Waterfall off the ladder using the rope! The crowd EXPLODES as TDM flies down to the mat, landing with a sickening thud on the ring! Hardy pulls the rope off his neck and gulps in a few breaths of air, before sneering at the broken man below him. Without a second thought, Hardy takes off with an amazing shooting star press from the ladder!

Hardy nails the move perfectly on the inclined canvas, hitting TDM with incredible impact! The crowd leaps in the aisleways in joy, as Briggs drops for another count!

The fans explode as drinks, shirts and signs fly into the air. The Hand That Feeds cues up again as Hardy slowly pulls himself to his feet. Sickened from loss of blood and several incredible impacts on the ring, a dazed Mike Hardy slowly stumbles into the turnbuckle. Finding no top rope to hold him, Hardy slumps back to the mat, as Briggs calls for medical attention. TDM lies in a heap in the center of the destroyed ring, motionless and defeated for the first time in three years.

Briggs raises Hardy’s hand as the Portuguese fans begin chanting his name. Hardy slowly pulls himself up and looks down upon his fallen opponent, slowly coming to terms with what has just happened. Finally, in one last burst of energy, Hardy throws his arms into the air in victory, as the crowd continues screaming in astonished anarchy. Medics reach the ring and rush to Hardy, urging him to lie down. But Hardy shoves past them and climbs out of the ring, walking back up the ramp under his own power.

The crowd continues to cheer as Hardy stumbles to the back, his nose broken and blood still flowing from several lacerations on his head. But Hardy has won again, and this time he’s saved GCW!

Winner: Mike Hardy

It’s amazing how much pain you can forget when the object of your most absolute hatred pulls off a miracle, and survives what should have been a slaughter. Stevens kicks a steel folding chair sending it flying into the wall.

Michael Stevens: GOD DAMNIT!

As he turns looking for more furniture to take out his aggression on, he finds the two members of Allied Chaos right behind him smiling a pair of cheesy smiles.

Michael Stevens: Who the hell are you two?

Kyle Niker: Kyle Niker, and Conner Pearce.

Conner Pearce: Oh he so just got lucky.

Kyle Niker: Sure the way we see it, Caldera screwed with your head, just like he screwed with ours. Obviously you know how things work around here..

Conner Pearce: And you know all the tricks to get things done in the ring.

Kyle Niker: Just what we could use.

Shaking his head sadly, Stevens just doesn’t know what to say for a moment.

Michael Stevens: And what do I get for teaching you all you’ve ever wanted to know about wrestling?

Conner Pearce: Our company?

Kyle Niker: Sparkling personalities?

Michael Stevens: You know what, why not? You to kids want a teacher? Well class is now in session, and the first thing you need to know is, teachers thirsty, so why don’t you to go grab me a beer, and we’ll get to work, after what I saw tonight you two definitely need it.

Allied Chaos high fives, and stands looking at Stevens for a minute.

Michael Stevens: Thirsty, now.

Conner Pearce: Oh yeah.

Kyle Niker: Just a minute boss.

The two turn and start heading down the hall way, while Stevens turns shaking his head as we fade out.

Our view switches over to backstage in the arena. We come into the middle of a conversation between Jason Dread, who is standing and pacing back and forth in front of LeStatt Knight, sitting and taping his wrists on a bench in the middle of the locker room. Knight is looking downwards as he is the first we hear to speak:

LeStatt Knight: Jason, look man, I know you aren't fit enough for tonight. I'm not even sure if you could take on just ONE of the Praetorian guard tonight. You had horrible damage done to your neck, you know that and I know that. Why are you going to push this on yourself?

Dread stops pacing, and stands motionless for a moment. LeStatt turns his head upwards to face Dread.

Knight: I know you're a proud man, but this isn't the time to have your ego outweigh your sensibility. Let me put this in terms which may be easier, Jason...

Jason Dread: Rob...don't.

Knight: ...if you make one wrong move, you could NEVER be able to walk again...

Dread: Stop it...

Knight: ...or even worse, it would be very easy in your condition for you to DIE...

Dread: ...I said stop!

Knight: And you think you could get through four of them at your throat at once?!

Dread screams his next words at an almost deafening volume, surprising us at his tone.

Dread: Shut the hell up, LeStatt!!!

He breathes heavily, his voice turning to a quieter, and slightly more agitated tone. He leans towards LeStatt, menacingly.

Dread: Don't you think that I've thought about all of this?! Can't you understand that there has been very little else on my mind other than with one wrong move I could lose everything?!

Knight: Jason...Jhub and I are on your side. You just don't see it as apparent as you should.

Dread: What are you saying?

LeStatt sighs as Dread completely misses the point.

Knight: I know you're a proud man, Jason, but you need to stop kidding yourself...you're in no condition to be here to fight, and in no way are you going to be able to win tonight on your own. You need to learn when to ask someone for help.

Dread's tone becomes infuriated once again.

Dread: God dammit, Rob! I won this damned title on my own and you bet your ass that I'm not going to let some injury keep me from getting back everything that Octavian has taken away from me!!! Now, if you'll excuse me, I'm going to be on my way to take care of the Praetorian Guard just to prove you and everyone else that doesn't think I can't do this!

Dread storms out of the room, angry at the words which LeStatt Knight just spoke to him. Still sitting on the bench, Knight's face conveys nothing but the slightest bit of concern mixed with disbelief.

Knight: You're not fooling anyone, Jason. Thank your lucky stars Caldera is watching out for you, too...

Knight follows suit slowly out of the room, the scene coming to a close. The shot then returns to ringside, where JB and Rich Rollins sit in anticipation for the main event.

Bryan: Well, Renegade, we’ve seen some amazing stuff tonight, but how about that match with Hardy and Maxwell?! I can NOT believe what we saw in that contest.

Rollins: JB, you ain’t kiddin’. I have to admit, that was the single most shocking thing I’ve ever seen in professional wrestling, and this is coming from a guy that saw the Grim Reaper win the Women’s title. Hardy did exactly what Caldera wanted; he destroyed The Dark Messiah! But does nobody see what’s going on here?

Bryan: Hardy’s establishing himself as a superstar!

Rollins: NO! Hardy’s establishing himself as another name in a long list of Steven Caldera’s pawns! Who is the one man Steven Caldera needed to get rid of more than anyone? The Dark Messiah. And he just used Hardy to do that.

Bryan: But come on, Rich, even you have to admit that was a shocking match. Hardy has never been known for those sorts of performances, but he not only held his own but destroyed The Dark Messiah! Fans, Maxwell was taken out on a stretcher and sent to a local hospital at the commercial break. Mike Hardy didn’t just beat him, he damn near killed him.

Rollins: And I’m telling you that Steven Caldera gets all the credit for that, though it was a damned act of God that this went down this way. I came here because I wanted to do exactly what Hardy just did, I wanted to get rid of the son of a bitch that kept trying to kill GCW. Frankly, I’m pissed off that Mike Hardy has apparently robbed me of that opportunity. JB, I knew there were only two men that could get rid of The Dark Messiah, Steven Caldera and Rich Rollins. Well, Caldera sent Hardy in to do his dirty work and by some miracle he got it done. And you know what, JB? I’m more than a little pissed off that Caldera gave the match tonight to Hardy and not me. I made it clear last week that I wanted Maxwell, and I didn’t get him. That’s not how you treat a hall of fame wrestler, JB.

Bryan: I guess not…

Rollins: You guess not? Damnit, JB, you saw what happened here tonight. I just got robbed of my prey, and I have Steven Caldera and Mike Hardy to blame for it. The Dark Messiah probably won’t be coming back after what we just saw, so it seems I need to set my sights on a new target. And I don’t think I’ll have to look very hard.

Jason Dread vs. Alex Blaq vs. Luke Hanson vs. Mr. Danger vs. Octavian

Might of Rome cues up in the arena, and the Lisbon crowd explodes in boos. The Praetorian Guard emerges on stage, led by Mr. Danger, all looking regal and ready to go. Blaq holds his Hardcore title belt high in the air, Hanson towers over his allies, Danger looks fit and impressive, and Octavian himself is clad in his glamorous gold chain mail, wearing the Imperial Roman Championship belt around his waist. The diamond studded belt glimmers as the foursome walk to the ring and climb inside, ready to destroy Jason Dread.

Just Got Wicked hits next, and the crowd erupts! Dread emerges from the back walking gingerly, sporting the GCW title belt over his shoulder. Dread nails Blaq and drops him into the corner. He drops Danger with a forearm to the skull, and lays Octavian out with a hard right as well! Dread is finally cut down by a huge clubbing blow to the back from Hanson, who places all of his four hundred pounds on Dread’s back while the Praetorians regroup.

All four men begin stomping wildly at Dread’s back and head, before Hanson hoists him up. Hanson holds Dread at bay while Blaq grasps his Hardcore title belt and charges. Dread ducks the blow, and so does Hanson! Blaq’s momentum carries him into the ropes… where he topples over the top to the floor! Blaq gets to his feet but is immediately sent over the guardrail!

Octavian angrily takes hold of his Imperial Roman title belt, which he hadn’t had the time to remove, and takes a swing at Dread! Dread ducks again, but Octavian puts on the brakes. Hanson nails Dread with another hard shot to the head, as Danger simply shouts orders from the corner.

Dread points to the title proudly and shouts at Octavian, who begins fuming with fury. The GCW logo appears on screen as WorldWide fades out, and Jason Dread has easily thwarted Octavian, at least for this week………
