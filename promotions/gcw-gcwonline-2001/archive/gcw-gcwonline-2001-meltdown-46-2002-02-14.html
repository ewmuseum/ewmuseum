Meltdown 46, 14 February 2002

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 46 Teater Bowl — Northern Ohio ---- Meltdown #46 (The lights in the arena dim, and the Canadian crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes for an instant, before an incredible explosion of pyro ignites on stage! The Meltdown music begins to jam into the arena, as the fans go absolutely wild at the start of Meltdown! The pyro finally ends its fantastic display, and the cameras begin to pan over the crowd.) Bryan- Happy Valentine’s Day, everyone! We’re live in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada, continuing our exciting Canadian tour for Meltdown! We’ve got a big night ahead of us, including a bout that has over a year of history behind it, as Khan and the Jhub go one on one! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan, as always here at ringside with David Yale! Yale- And, as tends to be the common trend when the Jhub and Khan lock up, Khan is going to twist the Jhub in ways never thought possible, before destroying him in front of these idiotic Canadian fans! I’ve been waiting all week for this, JB! Bryan- On top of that, Badger Benandanti and Toxic lock up, and the tag team titles are on the line! (The cameras head backstage, and find The Jhub standing in his locker room with Kevin Jacobs. As the Jhub appears on the MegaTron, the crowd goes wild, though Jacobs is staring off to the Jhub’s left, looking rather nervous about whatever he sees. The Jhub smiles over in the same direction before turning to Jacobs.) Jacobs- Uhh… Jhub… You asked me to come in here, but… Jhub- Just hold the microphone, Jacobs, that’s all we pay you for. I only have one simple message tonight. I am going to find out who dropped me from the rafters at Rampage, Kevin. I’ve been bounced around and jerked off for weeks now, and tonight I’m going to do things the hard way. Even if it means capturing President Caldera and tying him up in my locker room. (The Jhub smiles, then turns around once more. The camera zooms out, and the crowd bursts into cheers again as they see a ravaged Steven Caldera, his suit messy and bound to a chair by rope, sitting to the Jhub’s left. He growls at the Jhub from behind the duct tape sealing his mouth, as the Jhub continues to flash him a huge smile. The Jhub finally gives Jacobs a shove, and Jacobs steps aside. The crowd continues to cheer in agreement with the Jhub’s abduction, as the Jhub rips the tape off of Caldera’s mouth.) Jhub- Now, call it instinct, but I just KNOW that you know who was behind this. So you and I are going to sit here all night until you tell me who it was. (Caldera simply glares at the Jhub, perhaps attempting to kill him with his scowl.) Jhub- Fine. You just sit there, Caldy, while I go take care of your Establishment. I’m figuring this out tonight, whether you like it or not. (The crowd cheers once again as the Jhub stands and exits the room, leaving Caldera alone, bound to his chair in the room. The cameras then head back to ringside, where David Yale now sits with his mouth open.) Yale- He… he can’t do that! Bryan- Well, it seems that the Jhub is taking things into his own hands tonight! Yale- He can’t do that, JB! The Jhub is going to pay! Bryan- Easy, tiger. I don’t know about you, but I don’t really care to be involved with that situation! Bryan- And Toxic crushes Badger right away with a clothesline! The bells sounds, and Toxic immediately scoops Badger all the way up, and slams him back to the mat! Keep in mind, if Badger is pinned here, Toxic wins the Hard*core title! Yale- Badger quickly gets back to his feet, but Toxic rushes in and sends him headfirst into the turnbuckle! Badger bounces out of the corner, and Toxic gives him a sharp kick to the spine, sending him down to the mat! Bryan- Man, Toxic sure is going crazy here! Badger quickly pulls himself back up in the ropes, but Toxic moves in and snaps off a big right hand! Badger goes down to a knee, and Toxic continues to pummel the Badman! Yale- Badger managed to roll out to safety on the floor, and begin to escape Toxic! But Toxic slides out and gives chase around the steel steps! But hold on, Badger makes a quick about-face, springboards off the steel steps, and takes Toxic down with a flying clothesline! Bryan- Brilliant, out-of-nowhere move from Badger! Badger manages to pull himself up and slide back into the ring, as Toxic regains his senses on the floor! Toxic slowly sits up and manages to pull himself back onto the apron! Yale- Toxic gets to his feet, but Badger is on the move again! Badger charges and launches himself at Toxic, flying through the air with a ninja-style kick, sending Toxic off the apron and chest first into the steel guardrail! Bryan- Wow! Badger literally flew through the air there with a kick right to Toxic’s face, and I guess that’s one of those Bad-Fu moves he’s been perfecting! Yale- Bad-Fu… Good God. I’ve never heard anything so ridiculous! Bryan- Well, it’s beating your boy Toxic right now! Badger steps back out of the ring to the floor, and lifts Toxic to his feet! Badger moves Toxic over and whips him hard, right into the steel steps! Toxic hits with great impact, sending the top section of those steps to the floor! Yale- Toxic goes manages to begin crawling away from Badger, before getting up to his feet on the other side! Toxic begins hobbling away, but Badger is on the move again! Badger springboards off the last section of the steps, flies at Toxic, and brings him down with a bulldog right to the floor! GOOD GOD! Bryan- What an amazing move by Badger! Badger took flight again, this time hooking Toxic up in midair and driving him down to the floor, face first with a bulldog! Incredible!
