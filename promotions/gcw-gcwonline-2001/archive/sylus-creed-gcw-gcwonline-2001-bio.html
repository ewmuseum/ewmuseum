Sylus Creed Biography Real Name Sylus Creed Nicknames The Wolf Hometown Edison, New Jersey Birthday December 15th 1986 Height 6'2" Weight 225lbs Biography Sylus was born in Edison, New Jersey. Nothing was out of the ordinary with his childhood. He grew up, went through school, and was thrust into the real world. There was where he began to see that life isn't simple, and it very well never will be. His life up until this point was filled with twists and turns that would drive anyone to the point of a binge. He struggled to make money, leaping from job to job whether it was being fired or quit. Until he got an opportunity from his most recent job. While working as a janitor at a gym, he began to talk with one of the regulars that worked out. Different conversations started and finished, eventually working to the point where Sylus was asked about his life. The man saw Sylus talk of the struggling he had gone through, still ending up with a piss poor job of cleaning urinals and wiping down equipment. The man told Sylus what he did, and that since Sylus was already scraping the bottom, trying out the business won't hurt him one bit. Sylus began to train, honing his skills so he can work for something that didn't consist of soap and mop. Even though Sylus' life so far has been free of problems, he was told that if he wish to take this way of life, than he will have a big surprise in the future.
