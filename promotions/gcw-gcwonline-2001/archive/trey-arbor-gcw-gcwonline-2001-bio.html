Trey Arbor Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Trey/Arbz/R-Betray Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday November 27th 1985 Height 6'3 Weight 242 Biography Trey grew up in a abusive family, mother constantly being abused by his stepfather, his father moved to Texas at an early age after his mother divorced him over his alcohol abuse. Trey found it within himself at an early age to begin training and getting bigger and stronger to protect people who could be bullied and pushed around. He matured fast and filled into his body earlier than most. He is a sweet submissive person and cowares to the sounds of verbal abuse. (he cant control it he grew up that way. hes not extremely confrontational, he would rather just solve other peoples problems rather than his own, just to veer off from the thoughts of his own life.) But push him to far and you will be on the receiving end of an ass beating. Three Years ago after a normal day at his local gym, Trey receives a call from a hospital in Dallas about his father, Logan. He has cirrhosis of the liver and is currently in bad shape. Trey knows it is only a matter of time before his only father is to pass away so he decides to jump on the next available flight to Dallas international airport. He spends a few days with his father in the hospital asking doctors and nurses how his conditions are doing. Logan seems to be fading as time rolls on so Trey decides to Donate half his liver to save his father. Trey may have never seen his father and never really got to know him, but Trey is a giver. It is just how he turned out seeing all the abuse his mother has gone through. If Kendra (Trey's mother) were only half as strong as Trey she wouldn't be afraid to move on from this highly domestic abuse ridden relationship, yet she fears her well being if making that desicion. Logan's surgery was a huge success and shortly after, Logan is recovered and doing well. Logan is so eternally grateful towards Trey that he asks Trey to live with him, and Trey agrees to move in. Three years later Logan and Trey develope a bond unbreakble to any Father and Son. Trey (25) and Logan (43) train long and hard side by side everyday at the local gym. Finally after 3 years of indy training and total domination of local wrestling cards, Trey receives the call... Other Feds GCW (under Mike Hardy) ACWF, and you know the whole ordeal haha! Strengths Power, Submission and Agility Weaknesses Legs, Gets winded a bit easily ever since the Liver surgery that saved his father's life. Wrestling Style Technical/Submissionist/High Risk(rarely ever) Strategy Trey likes to win by out wrestling. Mostly like a Chris Benoit meets Mic Foley, because he also prides himself on never calling quits. Finisher Move Thorn Driver Finisher Description Reverse bodyslam into sitdown powerslam Finisher Setup Move Usually a ducked clothesline or a hurdle over the opponent as to where the back of the opponent is facing Arbor. Setup Description Read FINISHER SET UP MOVE....Pretty basic haha! Special Moves Pumphandle Slam, Tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, Texas Clover Leaf, Abdominal Stretch (Oldschool Abdominal stretch though, usually consisting of the top rope for leverage) Other Moves Standard technical and submission manuevers. Many times I've been sitting around...around...around...around Ohhhhh I'm feeling fading.....BOOM" There is a huge explosion of forest green pyro technics as Trey stands holding the arch way of under the Titan tron and does 3 or 4 one handed pull ups and drops to the stage below and stands out on the platform for a minute soaking in the buzz from the crowd. He slowly makes his way to the ring with his Oakleys covering his eyes, and his backwards cap covering his soft brown hair. He circles the ring one time and removes his hat from his head. With both hands on the visor of the cap and slowly paces while bending the visor to a nice curved shape and finds a very lucky fan in the audience and places the cap on their head. He slowly reaches for the sleeve of the fans shirt and dusts off the shoulder, smiles and gives them a thumbs up. Trey then turns to the ring behind him, climbs in, and makes way to the turn buckle and uses the ropes to stretch his limbs as he either waits for the bell to sound or awaits his opponent to make way to the ring. He removes his glasses and hands them to the ring assist standing by.
