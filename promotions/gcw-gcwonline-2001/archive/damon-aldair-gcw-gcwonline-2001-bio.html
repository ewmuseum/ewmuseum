Damon Aldair Biography Real Name Damon Aldair Hometown New York Birthday October 30th, 1978 Height 6'1 Weight 215lbs Biography Damon Aldair is not a gimmick. He is a man. Actually, he's more than a man. He's the human representation of the 21st century yuppy culture, of disillusioned millionaires and of the cruelty of wealth. Imagine the life. You grow up with a successful father, a Wall Street trader. You want for no material posessions, your every need is catered for by a vast array of servants, you graduate from college - Harvard Business School, naturally, with a top class degree, but none of it matters. You have become numb to grandeur and riches, it no longer holds any thrill. Imagining it? Good. Because that is the life of Damon Aldair. Society forces him to wear a mask. To play the role of the functional, successful bachelor. Ask anyone who has met him and they'll tell you the same things. That the only thing that matches his good looks is his charm, that he has a deep-rooted sense of morals and ethics and that he is generous with his fortune. These people don't know Damon Aldair. They only know what he shows them. Damon does not seek companionship, nor love, nor friendship. He cares about one man: himself. Don't let the mask fool you, he doesn't care about the war in Iraq, or about the lack of humanitarian aid reaching Sudan. This is merely dinner chat with the rich liberal elite he socialises with. The only thing that drives Damon Aldair is ambition. Ambition to be the best. Be it the richest, or the most stylish, or the most sophisticated - this is his aim in life. If pretending to care about some little starving kid half a world away brings him closer to his goal, then so be it. "In order to be successful, one must project an image of success." - Damon heard this line in the film American Beauty, and quickly adopted this as his personal mantra. There's one thing Damon has wanted more than anything else in his life, though, and that is the ability to do damage. When he was a teenager and in his early twenties, he found doing emotional damage was more than enough to quench his thirst. Those were simpler times - when reducing a girl to tears, seeing the mascara smeared all over her pathetic, quivering face, was enough to content him. Around the age of 22 he started to change, though. Don't misunderstand, Damon will forever enjoy mentally and emotionally scarring others. But it's like watching your favourite movie over and over again (Damon's is "Closer", a critically acclaimed 2004 release) - you will forever enjoy it, but over time it loses some of it's original thrill and appeal. Now Damon wants to inflict an all new type of pain. The type of pain he can see, and admire, and manipulate to his liking. He wants to hear bones break, he wants to see blood ooze from a cut. He wants to hurt people. The worrying thing? Damon can do just that. He doesn't do over 1,000 stomach crunches a week for no reason, nor does he work out six days a week for the thrill of a toned physique. Since the age of 15 he has been trained in freestyle wrestling, muay thai kick boxing and mixed martial arts. It's amazing what money can buy you in New York City. For Damon it happened to buy him private training sessions with the best instructors money can buy. For the last 12 years Damon has been perfecting his craft, learning to channel his anger into more productive ways - why bloody a nose when you can dislocate a shoulder? Make no mistake. Damon has no passion for this business. He has no respect for the rules, nor for tradition. He was never a wrestling fan, nor will he ever be. He doesn't need GCW's ridiculous salary. He competes because he wants to be the best, and to cause damage to others. He seeks only glory, without any sacrifice. And the ironic thing? Damon doesn't care that you know all this about him, or that you know his darkest desires. Because, honestly, to him you're of no importance and you are beneath him. Just like everybody else. Wrestling Style Heavily influenced by martial arts, in particular Muay Thai kickboxing. Also uses multiple submission maneouvers and just about any suplex you can imagine. Finisher Move The System-Breaker Finisher Description A triangle choke, this can locked on out of nowhere, as a counter to a move or as the follow up to the Calf Stomp. Finisher Setup Move Calf Stomp Setup Description Damon stomps down with all his might on the back of his opponent's leg, forcing them to fall to their knees and into position for the System-Breaker. Other Moves TRADEMARK MOVES "The Knee Duet" - After whipping his opponent into the turnbuckle, Damon walks calmly to the other corner, before charging across the ring and hitting his opponent in the temple with a high knee. After his opponent inevitably collapses into a sitting position in the corner, Damon runs the ropes twice to build up speed and momentum, before hitting a running facewash on his opponent with such momentum that it carries both him and his opponent out of the ring through the bottom and middle rope. "Baisuki Knee" - Damon hits the ropes and meets his opponent with a flying knee to the skull. Damon's impact results in both he and his opponent landing on their Double stomp - this can be hit from any position - be it the ground, the top rope, when his opponent is in the tree of woe position. Damon just enjoys driving his weight all the way down into his opponent. From behind the curtain Damon Aldair emerges, with smoke billowing all around the entrance way, giving an eerie, other-worldly feel to these peaceful moments before his brutality is unleashed. He is in no hurry. Standing at the top of the ramp he surveys the crowd through his ice cold blue eyes. These are not the eyes of a man, though. They betray no hidden feelings, show no emotion, hint at no humanity. They show only a machine with a mission - to destroy whomever happens to be staring across the ring at him. Now, as he walks to the ring, his stride starts to quicken ever-so-slightly. His features, already so chiselled, somehow become even more hardened and outlined. He has no need to stop and trade insults with the fans, they are of no significance to him. He is above them. Instead, he is focused on the hunt. As he climbs the ringsteps and walks across the apron he has no doubt that this is a hunt. He is the hunter, his opponent is the prey. The outcome is inevitable. Finally he acknowledges the crowd, standing on the apron and turning towards them. There is no grand gesture, no words spoken, only the traces of a wry smile working it's way across Damon's features. He knows he's better than the fans, and he knows he is about to step into a battle which he will win. As he steps through the ropes and retreats to his corner to ready himself, he has but one thought. Life is good.
