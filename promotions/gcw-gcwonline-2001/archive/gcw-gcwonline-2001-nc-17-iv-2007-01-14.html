And out of her shadow, Lia Ambrosi had FINALLY emerged. Looking up, she punched her fists skyward. Her eyes closed, she let out a victorious yell. Her tired body was week, but the spirit remained. Bryan: I just... I don't even know what to say. Shadow walks away with a hard-fought victory here tonight, and I don't even think a majority of the fans would begrudge her that. Yale: She walked out here a completely different animal, and the strategy paid off- this is GCW's best foot forward going into 2007. Bryan: This was an amazing match, Dave, amazing. I don't know that we've ever seen such a hard-fought World Title match. But it's not quite over yet. The lights dim over the entryway, and a song begins to play. I know your life is empty And you hate to face this world alone So you're searching for an angel Someone who can make you whole I cannot save you; I can't even save myself So just save yourself Bryan: What's going on here? Yale: Well, that's pretty obvious- Ian Childress has something to say here. And it's well that he should, because Shadow is his student, and has just showed how much she's learned from her teacher. Indeed, as the song continues, Ian Childress appears on stage, dressed all in black, as is his custom. His long black hair is pulled back in a sleek ponytail, giving him the appearance that he is decidedly all business this evening. He comes down to ringside and speaks with the timekeeper for a moment. Bryan: He's almost smiling- I think this may be a first. Regardless of whatever reaction the fans may be having at the moment, Mr. Childress proceeds directly up the ringstairs. Draped over his forearm is GCW's pendulous world title, its facets gleaming under the high lights of the arena. He pauses, smiling slightly at the exhausted, sweaty, bloody woman in the ring- only in one business was that the acceptable condition for one's student. Mr. Childress motions for a microphone, and is handed one in short order. Bryan: What could he possibly have to say on this occasion? Yale: Well, I think his student's actions spoke for her, but I can imagine that he might have a few things to get off his chest, considering he's got such a hand in GCW now too. Mr. Childress takes the microphone and gathers his thoughts for a moment, before speaking into it, his voice as serious as always. Childress: I want to say, first of all, as your sensei, I'm proud of you tonight. You came out here and you took the lessons I taught you about your own skills, and put them to work with something else... He pauses, and taps his chest. Childress: Call it whatever you want. Heart, pride, desire- but it came from the right place inside you. You may have come out here wanting to prove something to yourself, but you proved something to every single person here watching, and every person watching this on TV too. Bryan: He's right about that much. Yale: C'mon, he's trying to talk! Childress smiles. Childress: It's for that reason that I wanted to come down here and make it official in front of everybody- I want to present you with something that you earned with every drop of blood and sweat that you poured into that canvas tonight... but I can't. A murmur sweeps through the crowd. Bryan: Now, what's he talking about? Yale: I don't even know... Childress paces the ring, frowning slightly at the way the fans have gotten stirred up. Childress: I've known you for less time than quite a few people- one of them, you just faced in the ring tonight. But I think I know you rather well nonetheless- because you came to me to accept the offer of being my student. And as my student, I've seen you struggle to define yourself as something more than other people said you were. I've seen you go out there and try to form a new identity for yourself and fail against Seymour Almasy. I've seen you dangerously close to giving up everything that you'd ever worked for, just throwing up your hands and disappearing... but you persevered. Again, that small smile came. Childress: And tonight, you got the reward for it. You forged a new identity for yourself by your actions alone. But... Here, he paused, standing right in front of her. Childress: I want you to start things off on the right foot. And in order to truly become the person and the competitor that you've shown yourself to be tonight, you've got to leave behind the things that have held you back. She appeared not to understand him- that perhaps wasn't so surprising, given the long, brutal match she'd just been through, and all the blows to the head. But then again, he wasn't exactly making himself clear immediately, either. Childress: You know, the more I thought about it the less it made sense. You call yourself Shadow- well, let's think first on the fact that there's already a Shadow in XUW. Bank on that, number one, causing a good deal of confusion about which one you are- and since he was here first, you get compared. Not favorably. Shadow, hands on hips, seemed perturbed by this comparison. But it was something that had always perturbed her. The stubborn streak in her had laid just as much claim to the name as he had. Childress: And another thing... What's it symbolic of? You're naming yourself after something that's incorporeal. Something that follows greatness, but when greatness turns around, the shadow disappears. Bryan: I'm not quite sure I understand what he's getting at... Childress: For a long time, you've been letting the old identity control you. You've given a lot to help your faction and your friends become successful, but unfortunately, in doing that, you didn't bother to look out for yourself- and that's the way this business works. It's not that you don't have the talent, it's that maybe before, you didn't understand how cutthroat every battle can get. It's hard to know that sort of thing when the other promotions that you've worked for wouldn't even let you sniff their World Titles for fear of how far you'd go with them. Ian Childress reached out and put his hand on his student's shoulder. She looked up at him, unflinching. Childress: It's time that you stepped out of the shadows of those that came before you and started forging your own path. You proved that you're capable of taking the necessary steps by coming out here and doing what you did tonight. Now, you need to step out of the cocoon. A small smile presses over Shadow's lips, an exhausted smile, but a smile nonetheless. Childress turns to the crowd. Childress: I once considered becoming a man of the cloth... for various reasons, that didn't materialize for me, but because of my training, I've been recognized by my Order as essentially the equivalent of a clergyman. I say this for no reason than to note that a few weeks ago, I presided over a very important moment in the life of this woman and her significant other- their marriage. He pauses for a moment, looks down at the title draped over his arms, and then back at his student. Childress: With that said, I'd like to present to you your NEW GCW World Champion... Some of the crowd seemed pleased- a few scattered cheers were heard. Childress: “The Black Magdalen”... LIA AMBROSI! He placed the belt in her hands. Its weight was satisfying, natural, like it was exactly where it needed to be. Ian Childress raised his student's free arm. In the other, she raised the GCW Title, the end of it clutched in her gloved fist. The fans might not have been dancing for joy, but they could at least respect the titanic effort that one woman had gone through to get to the point of realizing her dreams. Bryan: I have to say that, whatever your personal feelings about Lia Ambrosi, she's the new face of the company, and I think that may be a good thing. Yale: I know it's a good thing. This match was phenomenal on the parts of all three competitors, but she came out here and really shone. As Lia celebrates in the ring with her mentor, “March of Mephisto” begins to play over the speakers again. Lia's face splits into a very natural-looking, but absolutely spent smile. Bryan: Unfortunately, that's about all the time that we have for tonight, so we'll catch you on the next World-Wide- until then, this is James Bryan for David Yale and Global Championship Wrestling… we have a new Heavyweight Champion of the World! NC-17 2006 fades out as its new World Champion stands before a shocked crowd, having finally met her destiny… and ready to forge a new one….
