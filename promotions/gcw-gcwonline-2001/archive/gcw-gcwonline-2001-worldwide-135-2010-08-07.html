WorldWide 135 San Diego Sports Arena — San Diego, CA ---- WW135: In Which Tim Ruins the Beginning of Johnny's Night The sun is on its way down, but San Diego is still scorching. Tim Shipley slumps over a bollard in the car park behind the Sports Arena, mopping at his brow. Shipley: Damn, I thought this was supposed to be a cold open. His white t-shirt clings to his upper body, but although he's otherwise in his ring attire, he's too classy to be caught outside without a top on. He looks up as Garbage Bag Johnny sails in on a Segway, his beard back to its wily wild ways after his brief absence from the Commissioner's office. GBJ: You mind shifting the cones? He motions to the two orange traffic cones in the centre of the space reserved for him right in front of the back door of the arena. Shipley: I think you can squeeze between them, Johnny. GBJ: Yeah, but it's the principle. Grinning, Shipley pulls himself up and grabs the cones with both hands, lifting them up and ushering Johnny's Segway in like he's guiding a plane to the runway. Exhibiting tremendous command over his vehicle, Johnny masterfully parks the Segway and follows it up with just the classiest damned dismount you could imagine. He clicks a button, and the machine beeps, the security alarm engaged. GBJ: Fine automobile, that Segway. What are you driving nowadays, Ship? Shipley: Driving? What am I, a farmer? I came in the stretch. He waves a laconic hand at a gleaming vehicle towards the back of the lot, whose numberplate clearly reads "RAJAH"; but GBJ doesn't notice, too excited about showing Shipley the fingerprint-sensitive starter button. GBJ: To each his own, then. Limos are nice, but as a man of great influence, I think it's important to appear standing whenever possible. Shipley: Hey, why's your Segway got Bermuda plates? GBJ: No tax down there. Can you believe it? Anyway, sorry I wasn't around last week to congratulate you on your Rampage win, but I had some business. I'm sure you understand. Shipley stretches his arms back behind his head, cracking the shoulders. Shipley: You're becoming a real little businessman, aren't you? The whole mustard-in-the-beard thing must really wow them in the boardroom. GBJ: A true businessman has no time for relaxed pace snacking, Tim. And speaking of getting down to business, I trust that since I'm back, shenanigans like trying to upstage our new GCW World Champion, Tessa Windsor, won't happen tonight. Shipley smirks. Shipley: A wet tissue could upstage Tessa Windsor, Johnny. Let's talk as businessmen, she's not exactly the World Champion to get the registers ka-chinging, am I right? So it's tempting, Johnny, to pull out that toothpick you've been missing and cash in my shot right here, right now, tonight. It's tempting. I mean, you did say any belt, any show. GBJ: I did say that. Garbage Bag Johnny swallows hard enough to notice. GBJ: But she's already defending her title against Chris Storm, tonight, and I figure with all this technical wrestling skill you've got going on, you'd want to use it to prove yourself rather than doing something opportunistic and vulture your way into a advantage, like, oh, I don't know, bring back your Ellis-lite sometimes-manager to gain a victory. Shipley reaches out a hand and touches the wall for no reason other than to show that this exchange of dialogue is not taking place on the moon or somewhere ridiculous like that. Shipley: Weasel? Me? Oh, Johnny, what do you think of me. No, I have Rasa tonight and I'm not falling for your whole tactic of getting me to fight several matches in one night again. Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm, I'll just be watching with interest. I mean, so far as I can see, that is. Shipley nods back at GBJ's chosen mode of transportation. Shipley: I suppose I'll know where to find you when I do decide when to cash in. And who to cash in on. GBJ: Good. I'm glad we understand each other. Now, I'd love to stay and chat, but I've got a pretty busy night ahead of me, and you've got that very match with Rasa coming up soon, which reminds me, I've got some packing to do. Shipley: Night flight, Johnny? Shipley grins. Shipley: Try not to freak out when they switch out the lights for take-off. It's standard procedure. The commissioner gives Shipley a strange look. GBJ: Heh...I meant packing the boxes for the Trick or Treat match, and then with "night flight?"
