WorldWide 126 American Airlines Center — Dallas, Texas ---- Introduction The lights dim in the American Airlines Center and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Dallas fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: Welcome, everyone to Dallas, Texas, city that is graciously hosting us for the month of February! And welcome to the final episode of WorldWide before NC-17 number seven! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan alongside David Yale, and Dave, this is it! Yale: The last gasp before the leap, JB! I'm excited as hell to be here in Dallas just days before NC-17. Bryan: Tonight we've got three big matches for you, and a whole lot more. And we've got a bit of an NC-17 preview for you, as Tempest locks up with NC-17 opponent Cheap Labor and Kris Michaels in a handicap match! Yale: Yeah, Tempest got on the wrong side of Commissioner Christian Zenith last week, and this is what she gets! And with Zenith looking to flex his muscles a bit given what's going on at GCW headquarters, Tempest chose the wrong time to irk the Commish. Bryan: It'll be a big test right before her Television Title shot at NC-17, but speaking of Zenith, tonight could be his night to put the lock on control of GCW. President Caldera confirmed this week that he is stepping down at NC-17, and it seems to have come down to a race between Zenith and Just promoter Tim Shipley! Yale: We've been talking about it all week, JB, and things seem to be coming to a head. Next week at NC-17 President Caldera will reveal who he and Jay Terror have chosen to head GCW as we begin 2010! Bryan: This is a decision that will no doubt rock the foundation of GCW. We've also got a chaotic main event tonight, as Bryan Mayhem takes on US Champion Dan Black in a lumberjack match. Yale: This one's got the potential to be explosive, JB, and tempers are running high so close to the big night! Bryan: There's a lot to do tonight, so let's get started right away! ---- Old Friends The backdoor explodes open and coming into the arena to mixed emotions from the crowd without his face paint, dreadlocks back in a ponytail carrying a black gym bag is Bryan Mayhem. He's walking briskly through the backstage passing numerous employees who seem to jump out of his way. Kevin Jacobs runs up to him to get a few words. Jacobs:"Bryan, tonight you face Dan Black in a non-title match in the main event. People are calling this a statement match. What kind of momentum would you have heading into NC-17 with a victory over someone like Black?" Bryan stops in his tracks and turns towards Jacobs who takes a deep breath and swallows. Mayhem stares deep into Jacob's, almost cutting right through him. Mayhem:"Statement Match? I love how I have become the underdog since my return. You are too old Mayhem, to slow, everyone is younger and faster than you. This is a statement match for Dan Black. Just imagine the momentum he would have if HE beat ME. Dan Black is a pebble on a rocky road to the top. I have beaten the likes of Valentine, Shaman, Octavian just to name a few. I have beaten Hall of Famers within an inch of their lives. Don't talk to me like I have something to prove. I have spent years proving to you and everyone that I am the most feared man in the world." Bryan pushed the mic from his face and continues on his way to his lockerroom. He walks at the same pace and is stopped at his door. Standing in front of his lockerroom is Mortifera. Three of the biggest men in GCW history in one location. Mayhem:"You ladies care to move?" Jorge: "We want to talk to you." Mayhem:(pointing at Jorge):"Who's this clown? Jorge quickly gets nose to nose with Mayhem. Jorge:"You think I'm scare of you a has been like you?" Shaman pulls Jorge back as Mayhem barely moves, keeping the same calm look he had seconds ago. Shaman:"Bryan, just because we are friends, does not mean I will take it easy on you. I know what it takes to beat you, and I will stop at nothing to do so." Mayhem: "Friends? Shaman, we were never friends. But I am glad you aren't going to take it easy on me, because I might have felt bad for snapping your fucking neck, and this wannabe's standing with you. Now move." Mortifera look at each other, Shaman looks disturbed, he knows a repeat of last year's Death Match may be looming. ---- And Here My Troubles Begin The camera comes into focus on a locker room door bearing the name “Tempest” in swirly sparkling red letters, just as the door cracks open and the aforementioned GCW star peeks out cautiously. She turns to look one way down the hallway and suddenly a tiny furry face pops into view over her head; a white ferret face, surrounded by a multicolored clown collar. The ferret looks the opposite direction from where Tempest is looking, and then they switch, their heads pivoting to face opposite directions. Then another ferret face appears atop the first, this one darker and wearing shiny pink sunglasses. It proceeds to look up and down just for good measure. Tempest: Ok Emil… Fernando, I think the coast is clear. Maybe I can get out of here unseen. Just keep an eye out. She steps out the door in this evening’s wrestling attire, a shimmering tiger striped body suit in silver and white and the ferrets leap to the ground and fall in behind her looking around as they go. Tempest: I’ve gotta try and stay out of trouble this week. I’ve got enough on my plate with this handicap match and oh… that Christian Zenith… He just makes me so mad! Tempest scowls and pops her fist into her palm before turning to look down at the ferrets. Tempest: Seriously guys, can you imagine anyone getting so upset as to throw me into a handicap match over a little harmless electronic… exploration? The ferrets dutifully shake their little heads as they trot along and Tempest returns to her thoughts, not noticing that a shadow has appeared behind her. It is a much larger shadow and closing fast. The ferrets notice and start scampering over her feet frantically, and tugging at her tights. Tempest: Guys, not now! I’m trying to think… how do I get myself into these things? The ferrets leap as the shadow looms closer, now recognizable as a thoroughly un-amused LeStatt Knight. They scurry up Tempest, spiraling around her body and landing on her shoulders in time to turn her head, and consequently the rest of her, around and face him, just as he reaches her. Tempest: Eep! After the involuntary squeak, Tempest summons up her most winning smile and speaks as bravely as she can manage. Tempest: LeStatt! Hi There! I was just looking for you… Knight puts a hand up to signal that Tempest shouldn't continue with that sentence. Not surprisingly, she is quick to do as Knight wanted her to do. The GCW icon looks her up and down, a deep breath causes his chest to move up and slowly come back down. He lets the tip of his tongue escape his mouth for a moment, long enough for it to run across his bottom lip. Knight: Save it senorita, we both know you had no intention of coming to see me. Not after you found a way to steal $100,000 from me. Let me ask you something ... did you really think you were going to get away with this? Tempest’s mouth opens and shuts a couple of times. Her blue eyes widen in a manner not unlike that of kittens in Humane Society posters. The ferrets curl around her shoulders, hiding their faces beneath her hair. Tempest: Steal? But… but you did say “bill me” when I asked you about contributing to Love for Haiti. I just assumed… Knight: You assumed what? That I gave you permission to take six figures from me? Lady, you've already done your best to make me look like a fool once. Do not make the mistake of insulting me again. Tempest shakes her head vigorously, and the ferrets pop back out and join in. Tempest: Oh no! I would never do that! I just… that is, I’m sorry if I uh… interpreted your words too… optimistically. What can I say? I’m a ‘glass-half-full’ kind of girl. Tempest shrugs as innocently as she can possibly manage and smiles, albeit nervously. LeStatt folds his arms across his chest and looks harder at Tempest, studying her. He takes in another deep breath and even manages to crack a quick smirk. Knight: It's not about the money senorita. I could care less about that, I have plenty. Rest assured that I will not be getting local authorities involved either nor my high priced lawyers. The cause that the money is going to is worth it. I can even appreciate the prank ... cute joke, really it was. Tempest: Really? Something almost like hope sparkles in Tempest’s eyes for a moment. Knight's smile finds its way back. Knight: Really. But it fades away just as fast, his features hardening. Knight: But let me tell you something ... If you EVER have this notion that you can take advantage of me or that I am just some punk that can be walked over? I will come hunt you down and bury you. I have been here for far too long to allow someone like you to think you can just fuck with me. Do not cross me again Tempest ... it wouldn't be wise. Tempest blinks up at Knight and gulps audibly. Tempest: Well... while wisdom has never been my strong suit, I could very well turn over a new leaf here in GCW. In fact, I’m feeling well motivated that way right now. Yup! Highly motivated indeed. Knight: Motivation is good. Now run along senorita ... I'm sure you have things to be doing. Tempest nods and smiles nervously, giving a little wave as she backs away slowly. Knight spins on his heel and stalks away down the hall leaving Tempest with her own thoughts. Tempest: Ooooh yeah, I got things to do. Now… how to do them without getting into any more hot water? ---- Labor Meets The MMA-Hole. We’re brought backstage where Cheap Labor, plainly clothed and wrongly dressed for a wrestling match, is speaking to his partner Kris Michaels. The former MMA star is fighting a ghost -- dodging invisible punches while throwing a series of stiff counter strikes. Cheap Labor: Are you even listening to me, dude? I can’t do it. The Executioner bends sharply backwards to dodge an unseen roundhouse kick and then pulls The Invisible Man down with a sharp leg whip. Cheap Labor: I had a dream last night. I want her to be the bread to my butter. I want her to be the curtain to my rod! The Television Champion snickers at how visually appropriate that last example was. Cheap Labor: There is no way I can do it. There is absolutely no way I can help you out this evening. The former MMA starter continues to romp around the room like a break-dancing Chuck Norris. Cheap Labor: Kris. Michaels. Kris Michaels. Mr. Michaels! THE SON OF MRS. MICHAELS, LISTEN TO ME! The Executioner pauses, his shoulders heaving. There is no way I can be your tag partner tonight. It’s nothing against you, but I can’t do it. Michaels: Why not? Cheap Labor: [changing stories] I’m... uh, not feeling well. I stubbed my toe in my hotel room this morning. Yeah, that’s right. I stubbed my toe and now it’s really sore. I think I just need some time and some Epsom salt and maybe some nail clippers. But there’s no way I can wrestle tonight, though -- no way, no how. You need to quit making excuses for yourself, suck it up, and do your job. We can always “Major Payne” this thing. How about I break one of your fingers to take the pain away from your toe? I bet you’d really cry then. Cheap Labor: How about we don’t and say we did? Michaels: How about you go get your pansy ass dressed and get ready to fight? Cheap Labor: You do realize that you shouldn’t answer a question with a question, right? Michaels: Why not? You just did. Outsmarted again! Labor sees the writing on the wall and realizes that he will not be leaving this exchange with any dignity intact, so he quits trying. Cheap Labor: Please, dude. Please! Just do me a solid. Do me five solids. You can the number one contender for the title if you let me off the hook. I don’t care; just don’t ask me to come out there tonight with you! She’s a girl! I came from a Christian upbringing. You’re not supposed to hit girls! Michaels: This is what she signed up for. She’s a wrestler. That’s what she does -- she wrestles. A fist flies at Labor’s face like a precision guided missile. Michaels: I think you’d better evaluate your options, buddy boy. There is an easy way through tonight and that’s through Tempest. His face is painted with a variety of emotions. He looks angry, upset, downhearted, confused… … and fearful. JB: Kris Michaels has offered his terms! Bryan: Dan Black managed to get a two count on Mayhem with that Spinning Heel Kick, but I can't imagine it did much more than stun the big man. The tide of this match has turned a complete 180 as Dan Black's taken control of things. Yale: See? What'd I tell you, JB? Dan always finds a way to turn lemons into lemonade, figuratively speaking. Mayhem attempts to fight them off, but the odds are overwhelming. Dragging him to his feet, both members of Mortifera roll him back into the ring so that Dan Black can attempt to pick the bones. Yale: Another smart move by Dan Black, JB! He used the lumberjacks to his advantage so that he could plan his next move. Genius! Bryan: I can't really argue that point with you, unfortunately. I never said Dan Black wasn't smart. He's just a vile, underhanded man and his kind always gets what they deserve in the end. Black leans over and wraps his arms around the waist of Mayhem, preparing to hoist him up. Bryan: My God, Dave! Is he really going to do what I think he's going to--? Yale: I think he is, JB!
