Meltdown 61, 1 August 2002

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 61 Gateway Arena — Sioux City, Iowa ---- Meltdown #61 Nearly two years ago, Global Championship Wrestling was founded by President Steven Caldera. Six months ago, it was destroyed. But tonight, the legacy continues. Nearly every great Global Championship Wrestling star has returned to compete in their home ring once again. And new stars have appeared to oppose them in their own rise to the top. Tonight is the rebirth of Global Championship Wrestling. The voice, crackling through Arco Arena, finally fades out along with the house lights. The jam packed crowd rises to its feet, as red flames suddenly erupt from the ring! The flames kiss the rafters above, setting off three huge rockets that streak down and strike the stage with a colorful explosion! The lights finally return to reveal a jammed house, screaming wildly for the return of the company! Cameras fly over the arena, panning over the jubilant fans and their signs. It finally comes to a rest in the center of the ring, where GCW Commentator James Bryan is standing. Bryan- Welcome back to GCW! The crowd greets this statement with a warm explosion of cheers, such to send Bryan reeling for a moment. Bryan- It is my great pleasure to introduce to you the man that started it all… Owner and President of Global Championship Wrestling… STEVEN CALDERA! Caldera’s name is met with an uncertain reaction, as Established cues up overhead. The reaction continues to increase until Caldera himself finally appears on stage, wearing a tremendous grin with his tremendous suit. He quickly struts down towards the ring, where everyone is applauding, even JB, though some are noticeably weary. Caldera reaches the apron and steps inside, opening his arms out to the crowd. After a moment, Bryan humbly surrenders the house mic, and takes his leave, but not before a friendly handshake from Caldera. JB gets a warm parting reaction from the fans, as Caldera begins. Caldera- Well, everyone… Welcome home! A huge round of applause follows, as Caldera continues beaming. Caldera- I wanted to come out here first thing so that I could go ahead and make a few things known. First of all, last time we were all together, I made a promise… A promise I intend to keep. I promised to stay out of what goes on in this ring. I promised to let GCW run itself. And that’s exactly what I intend to do. GCW will not ruled under anarchy, however. I want to bring back an era of wrestling that nobody has seen for a decade. Global Championship Wrestling is now administrated by the officials, as well as the newly organized GCW Security team. The rules will be enforced when needed, the officials will not be pushed around without heavy fines… The way it ought to be. The timid fans in the crowd seem to warm to Caldera now, as the applause grows in volume. Caldera- That said, there will be a few changes around here. First of all, you’ll notice that the announce booth down here at ringside is empty. Perhaps I should address this. James Bryan and David Yale did a hell of a job last time around, but they won’t be calling Meltdown any longer. A slightly disappointed reaction results, but Caldera presses through it. Caldera- JB and Dave both still have jobs here, and you haven’t seen the last of them. But this time around, I was looking for something special in my commentators. So, it gives me great pleasure to introduce to you the first member of the new GCW announce team… Your color commentator… and former ten time GCW Hardcore Champion, TOAD! An explosion of wild cheers follows as Out With the Old hits overhead. Toad himself pops out from backstage, clad in a rather sloppy looking suit, complete with loosened tie. Toad comes bounding down to the ring and slides in under the bottom rope, smiling the whole way. He snatches the microphone from Caldera and addresses his fans. Toad- HEY TOAD FANS! The crowd’s reaction only grows louder, as Toad continues. Toad- First off, Mr. Caldera, I’d just like to thank you for bringing back my old stomping grounds… But that’s enough about you. Back to me. Yours truly seems to have some chips of bone or something like that floating around in my spine, and the doctors tell me I shouldn’t be wrestling… at least not ‘till it’s fixed. This is met with considerable boos. Toad- …So, I told the doctors to go **** themselves… But Mr. Caldera here won’t let me wrestle. More boos, this time at a rather embarrassed Caldera. Toad- But that’s ok. Maybe you’ll see your Hardcore legend back in the ring again someday, but until then, I’ll be down here at ringside. Until next time, Toad fans… Adios! Toad receives another huge ovation as he shoves the mic back into Caldera’s chest, and heads out towards the announce table. Caldera smiles as he watches Toad go, raising his arms to the crowd along the way. Caldera- All right… Now that that’s taken care of… I’d like to introduce your new head of security, the man who will be in charge of keeping things in order here in GCW from now on… Mr. Danger! Another surprised reaction rises up from the crowd, as they begin cheering for Caldera’s former employer. Danger, wearing a tight black shirt reading ‘GCW SECURITY’, takes his time in getting to the ring, smiling at the fans as he goes. Danger finally reaches the ring and carefully steps through the ropes, before being handed the microphone from Caldera. Danger- Well well well… GCW is back again. Now, if anything is constant in this world, it’s man’s continual pursuit of power. I won’t deny that I’ve always been looking for a way to take things over from you, Steven. But let’s face it… All that served to do is injure the company. So when you called me a few weeks ago and offered me this job, well… I couldn’t say no. Hell, look at all the neat toys they gave me… Danger reaches to the utility belt wrapped around his black jeans. From it he pulls a long black rod… A nightstick. Danger- …This baby looks particularly fun. So, if anyone back there wants to test GCW security… I’ll be here all night. As long as I’m around, things are going to run on a tight ship here. So go ahead… make my day. The crowd cheers Danger again, as he tosses the mic back to Caldera, and steps up to the turnbuckle to display his shirt. Caldera smiles as Danger exits the ring and begins heading back up the ramp. Caldera- Well, that about does it. The last thing I have to address is the empty seat next to Mr. Toad down there. Your play-by-play announcer will be… me. Caldera takes a large bow before heading down to the floor. The crowd cheers once more as Caldera takes a seat next to Toad, and the two begin the show! Toad- …Thank God, I was getting bored down here. Caldera- I tried to keep it short, was that not good enough for you? Toad- Hell no. Especially that Danger guy. I thought he would never shut up. Caldera- …Anyway… Thanks for being here, fans! We’re about ready for our first match! Let’s go to ring announcer Joey Andrews! Toad- God, and I hate this guy’s voice even more than yours…
