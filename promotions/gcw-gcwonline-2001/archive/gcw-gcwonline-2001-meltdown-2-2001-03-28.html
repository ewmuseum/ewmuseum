GCW — Card

Meltdown 2, 28 March 2001

28 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 2 1st Mariner Arena — Baltimore, Maryland ---- Meltdown #2 (The camera fades into another packed house, as Meltdown begins. The fans go crazy as the stage lights up with fireworks, as the show begins.) Bryan- We are live in another sold out arena for Meltdown! Hello, everybody, I’m James Bryan alongside David Yale, as we get ready for another exciting GCW event! Yale- I’ve been ready ever since the last show went off the air! What an event the first Meltdown was! This should be an extraordinary night! Bryan- Well, the heat between President Caldera and a few of his employees has already begun, as Caldera helped Octavian get the best of The Renegade last week! Yale- President Caldera sent the message that he is in firm control, although he did suffer the loss of his bodyguard, Khan! Bryan- Khan was taken to the emergency room, fans, and believe it or not, tried to get out of the ambulance on the way! But he has suffered a fractured vertebrae in his neck, and will be out for. . . Who knows how long! Yale- Well, for now, let’s go down to ringside, and get Meltdown number two underway! (Blood Brothers hits the speakers, and the fans begin to cheer. Desert Falcon walks from the back, stopping at the edge of the ramp. He raises his hands, as the fans cheer louder, and he walks to the ring.) Andrews- The following contest, set for one fall, is a special guest referee match! Introducing first, weighing in at 195 pounds. . . Desert Falcon! Bryan- Well, Desert Falcon is in the ring, and is looking for some redemption over that incident last week! (The camera shows a replay of the fast count and payoff by JD Billion to the referee.) Yale- And that’s why this match was made a special referee match! To make sure it’s fair! (It’s All About the Benjamins hits and the crowd boos loudly as JD Billion walks out from the back. He is carrying a microphone, and slows his pace as he heads to the ring.) Billion- Now, Falcon Boy, I know you think that you have a chance in this one, since there isn’t any referee for me to ‘bribe.’ Well, that is true. But that’s because tonight’s referee is the only guy around with as much money as me! (2001 hits, and the fans stand to see who it is. Bran$on King struts out from the back, wearing a referee’s shirt. He walks to the ring, joined by Billion.) Bryan- What the hell is this? Bran$on King? What do these two have in common that they would be pairing up like this? Yale- Don’t jump to conclusions, JB! Just because Billion announced him as the referee doesn’t mean he can’t do the job! Bryan- Oh, give me a break. King rings the bell, and Falcon rushes in on Billion! Falcon welcomes Billion into the ring with a hard series of knife edge chops! Yale- Falcon has him reeling, as he sends Billion into the ropes! Billion bounces back, and Falcon catches him with a high back body drop! Billion lands hard, as Falcon stays on him! Bryan- Well, Bran$on King has stayed neutral so far! Falcon lifts Billion up, and sends him into the ropes again! Billion bounces back, and Falcon hits a dropkick that catches Billion in the jaw! Yale- Billion is down again, as Falcon tries to stay on him! But King has stepped in the way, and is checking on Billion! Bryan- Oh, please. Yale- Falcon tries to shove him aside, but King insists that he check on Billion! Bryan- The crowd is showing its displeasement over this situation, as King finally helps Billion back up! Falcon finally shoves King aside, and gives Billion a right hand shot to the face! Yale- Billion falls back into the turnbuckle, as Falcon hops onto the second rope, and starts pounding away on the exposed face of Billion! Bryan- Falcon going to work, as. . . Wait a minute! Bran$on King from behind pulls Falcon off the ropes! Falcon lands on his feet, and turns to King! King is scolding Falcon! Yale- Wait, Falcon rears back and gives King a shot to the face! King goes down! Falcon turns back to Billion, who kicks him in the gut! He sets him up and hooks the arms! There’s the Debt! Bryan- That’s Billion’s trademark! Billion flips Falcon over and covers him, as King sees it! King drops! ONETWOTHREE! Another fast count does Desert Falcon in! (The bell rings, as 2001 plays. King and Billion stand in triumph, as King raises Billion’s arm.) Andrews- Here is your winner. . . J.D. BILLION! Bryan- That’s a damn shame! Something has got to be done about that! Desert Falcon has been screwed here two weeks in a row! Yale- Oh, come on, JB! Don’t mess with guys like Billion and King! They’re rich! They can do anything they want! Just like President Caldera! Bryan- Yeah, our President, who allowed this to be a special referee match! What part does he have in this alliance of JD Billion and Bran$on King? Yale- Who knows. Well, up next, we’ve got a match that could be the most hellacious one to date, as Toad and Gigas, two of the toughest men in the sport, lock up for the Hardcore title! (Fuel hits, and the crowd stands as Gigas walks to the ring, Kendo stick in hand. He steps through the ropes and mounts the turnbuckle, as the fans cheer.) Andrews- The following contest, set for one fall, is for the GCW Hardcore Title! Introducing first, weighing in at 320 pounds, the challenger. . . Gigas! Bryan- Two really big men are about to hook it up here for the only title there is in GCW as of now! And both want it bad! (Out With the Old hits next, and the fans jump to their feet and cheer. Toad walks to the ring, as fireworks are set of behind him. He smiles widely at the sight of his own pyro, and continues to the ring.) Andrews- And his opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds. . . He is the GCW Hardcore Champion. . . Toad!! Bryan- Toad is set for action here, but seems a bit reluctant to get in the ring with Gigas while he’s holding that kendo stick! Finally Gigas relents and steps out to the floor, as Toad unloads! Yale- There’s the bell, and this match is underway! Toad fires some stiff left jabs, and Gigas drops his Kendo stick! Toad hooks Gigas up and whips him hard into the security railing! Bryan- Toad stays on him, finally taking off his belt! Toad takes Gigas by the hair, and leads him up the entrance ramp! They get to the stage, where Toad hooks Gigas up! Yale- Toad lifts the big man up, and suplexes him onto the steel stage! That’s impressive! Toad seems a bit shaken from it himself, as he lifts Gigas up, and brings him through the curtain to the back! Bryan- They’ve made their way to the backstage area! Toad shoves Gigas forward and trips him to the ground! Gigas gets to his hands and knees, and Toad kicks him in the gut! Yale- Gigas rolls over and sits up, as Toad searches for a weapon! He’s found a fire extinguisher, and is hunting down the escaping Gigas! Bryan- Toad finally has Gigas within range, and he fires! But Gigas reaches out and pulls someone in the way! Toad sprays the poor guy with the fire extinguisher! Yale- Gigas springs back out and takes Toad down with a clothesline! Wait, the smoke has cleared, and it was ‘The Fueler’ Luke Hanson that Gigas pulled in the way! Bryan- Hanson is furious, as he grabs a steel chair! Gigas picks Toad up, as The Fueler cracks him in the back with the chair! Gigas stands up in pain, as Hanson give him another shot to the skull! Yale- Gigas goes down to a knee, as Toad gathers himself! Toad swings a right hand, and catches Luke Hanson in the face! Hanson drops the chair, as Toad grabs Gigas and takes him further into the backstage area! Bryan- Toad whips Gigas hard into a door, and Gigas goes through it! Toad follows him in, and they’re in the boiler room! Toad stomps Gigas in the spine, as Gigas tries to ready himself! Yale- Toad lifts him up, and scoop slams him onto the concrete floor! Toad ctaches his breath, as. . . Wait, there’s someone back there in the shadows! Bryan- Someone has stepped out from the back! He’s got a crowbar, and he’s going after Toad! He sings the crowbar, and lays out Toad with a shot to the face! Yale- That’s. . . That’s Wicked! Wicked is here, and he’s laid out the hardcore champion with Bar-Bar! Bryan- Bar-Bar? The crowbar has a name? Yale- Gigas is back up, as Wicked gives him a shot to the gut with Bar-Bar! Gigas doubles over, as someone bursts through the door into the boiler room! Bryan- Luke Hanson is here again, with his chair still in hand! Hanson swings, and cracks Wicked over the skull with the chair! Wicked drops down, as Hanson jumps on him, and they begin brawling! Yale- Gigas and Toad are both slowly and quietly crawling away from the boiler room! Toad gets out first, followed by Gigas! They both stand, and Gigas gives Toad a forearm shot! Bryan- Toad staggers back, only to be clobbered with a trash can lid out of nowhere! Someone else has interjected himself into this match! That looks like Damien, another new signee! Yale- Everyone wants to make a name for themselves! Damien gives Toad another shot, as Gigas hobbles back out through the curtain to the stage! Bryan- Toad finally reaches up and gives Damien a low blow, putting him down long enough for Toad to follow Gigas back down to ringside! Yale- Gigas is back down to the ring, where the match started! He’s got his kendo stick back, as Toad greets him! Gigas spins around, and cracks Toad over the skull with the kendo stick! Bryan- Toad falls hard, as Gigas covers! ONE! TWO!! TH- Toad kicks out! Toad is really proving his toughness! Gigas is confused, as. . . Wait. . . Wicked is running down the ramp! Yale- What’s he doing out here? Wicked still has Bar-Bar, as he makes his way to Gigas! And now Luke Hanson is coming down the ramp! And Damien! Bryan- Wicked gives Gigas a shot to the knee with the crowbar, as Hanson goes to work on Toad! Damien is here, and begins stomping on Toad as well! Wicked rolls Gigas into the ring, as Damien and Hanson lift up Toad! Yale- Wicked drops Bar-Bar, and hooks up Gigas! He drops him down with a monstrous DDT, that he calls the Sacrifice! Gigas is down and out, as Hanson and Damien rolls Toad into the ring! Bryan- Wait, now Wicked is by the ropes! He hops over, landing on top of Damien and Hanson with a suicide dive! And in the ring, Toad rolls over to cover Gigas! Yale- ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! It’s over! (The bell rings, and the referee hands Toad his belt. Out With the Old plays again, as Hanson, Damien, and Wicked fight on the floor.) Andrews- Here is your winner, and STILL GCW Hardcore Champion. . . Toad! Bryan- Toad is victorious once again in a chaotic hardcore title defense! Yale- We saw three newcomers interject themselves in this match, and I’m sure they will be getting their shot at Toad soon enough! Yale: Well folks, let's get to our next match. A cruiserweight division match-up! Should be exciting. Bryan: Sure as hell should! Andrews: The Following contest is scheduled for one-fall, introducing first, weighing in at 195 pounds, Chameleon! Yale: Well, Chameleon is in the ring waiting for Michael "Hickenbottom" Hardy. Bryan: Doesn't those fans hitting you bother you, David? Yale: Yes, I am gonna take care of this. Hey, would you stop--- It's Hardy! He's sneaking in through the back door! Bryan: He slides into the ring and the bell rings! Yale: Look at Chameleon! He's confused! Hardy turns him around and fires some right hands! Chameleon blocks a right hand! He blocks another! Bryan: Chameleon ducks a forearm shot and kicks Hardy in the gut! He grabs him by the head and drops him down with a DDT! Cover! ONE TWO TH-- No kickout! Yale: Chameleon picks Hardy up and throws him into the turnbuckle. Chameleon follows him in with a clothesline. Chameleon pulls Hardy out of the corner andwhips him into the ropes. Bryan: Chameleon lifts his knee and flips Hardy over it. Chameleon runs into the ropes and comes back with a leg drop. Yale: Where's he going? Chameleon climbing to the top rope! Hardy stands up. Chameleon jumps. He goes for a clothesline but Hardy extends his body and dropkicks Chameleon in mid-air! Bryan: Man, what impact! Chameleon rolls out of the ring and is standing in front of our Spanish friends. Yale: Looks like the Spaniards might get up close and personal with Hardy and Chameleon! Hardy is on the top rope! He jumps, goes for a hurincanranna but it gets reversed into a Powerbomb through the table by Chameleon! Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! {Crowd continues to chant} Yale: Listen to this crowd! They love it! Do it again fellas! Chameleon picks Hardy up and throws him back into the ring. Bryan: Cover! ONE TWO THR-- No! Hardy kicked out! I can't believe it! Chameleon climbs to the top rope and comes down with a headbutt! He picks Hardy up and whips him into the ropes. Hardy runs back, BOOM! Powerslam! He covers again! Yale: ONE TWO It's over! THRE---No, Kickout! Bryan: This has been an exciting match so far. Can't wait to see what these two young competitors have left in them. Yale: Well, this looks like it could take a lot out of them. Hardy reverses Chameleon into the ropes. Chameleon grabs on and stops dead in his tracks! Bryan: Hardy runs towards Chameleon and gets lifted up and over, but he lands on the apron! He turns Chameleon around and shoulders him in the gut. It looks like he is going for a sunset flip but Chameleon fires a right hand at Hardy and shoulders Hardy in the gut! Yale: Chameleon jumps up and over Hardy, doing a front flip in the process! He lands on the floor and grabs Hardy's legs. Boom! Powerbomb to the floor! Oh my god! Bryan: That had to hurt! Chameleon lifts Hardy up and throws him into the barrier. Ref: ONE! Yale: The ref is making the 10 count. Bryan: Chameleon runs towards Hardy but Hardy backdrops Chameleon to the concrete! Hardy jumps over and picks Chameleon up. He lifts him up over his head and drops him across the gaurdrail! Yale: Hardy takes a fans beverage and sips it. Now he is dumping it over Chameleon! Bryan: Hardy tosses Chameleon back to ringside and hops over himself. He tosses hameleon into the ring and Climbs up top. He leaps! Boom! Missle dropkick! Yale: Hardy picks Chameleon up and bounces off the ropes, he goes for a forearm shot but Chameleon ducks and Hardy hits the ref! The ref is out! Chameleon goes for a clothesline, but Hardy ducks and hits a swinging neckbreaker! Bryan: Now where is Hardy going? He is getting a chair from ringside! The crowd is showing their approval! Hardy slides into the ring as Chameleon climbs to his feet. Yale: Hardy swings and connects with Chameleon's lowerback. Hardy positions the chair on Chameleon's chest and motions for the Swanton Bomb! Bryan: If he hits this, it's over! What about what we saw earlier tonight? Bran$on King showing up as the Special Referee in JD Billion's Match and then assisting him for the win. Yale: Hardy is perched on the top turnbuckle! Hey, look who it is! It's JD Billion and Bran$on King! They pull Chameleon out of the ring and pick up the chair. Bryan: Billion's got the chair and King shoves Hardy off the top rope! BAM! Billion swings and connects with Hardy's Skull! Yale: Billion drops the chair and hits the Debt on Hardy right onto the chair! Hardy is busted wide open! Bryan: Chameleon crawls over and covers Hardy. King revives the ref and he begins to count. Yale: ONE TWO THREE! He got him! Andrews: Your winner of the match, Chameleon! Bryan: Now look at these three! They are standing over Hardy's unconscious body with their hands in the air! Yale- So how about this union of Bran$on King, JD Billion, and now Chameleon? I never saw this one coming! Bryan- Well fans, up next, we’ve got a debut match, in which two new GCW superstars will attempt to send a message, in their first televised GCW event. (The lights in the arena drop as the fans pull out their lighters. Unforgiven hits, as an eerie blue light rises over the entrance ramp. A man in a black cloak walks to the ring, and steps through the ropes.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 227 pounds. . . Armageddon! (Armageddon drops his cloak and tosses it outside the ring. Peace Sells hits and red strobe lights flicker on stage. Paladin walks from the back and climbs into the ring.) Andrews- And his opponent, weighing in at 325 pounds. . . Paladin! Bryan- This should be a great match! These two rising stars are very dark personalities, and should make for a great debut bout! Yale- There’s the bell, and we’re underway! Paladin has a distinct size advantage, but Armageddon doesn’t seem too intimidated! Armageddon charges in, catching Paladin with a flying forearm! Bryan- Paladin is staggered, as Armageddon begins firing fists into Paladin’s face! Paladin is staggered, but he reaches out and gives Armageddon a powerful shove, sending him backwards onto the canvass! Yale- Armageddon rolls over and back to his feet, charging back in! He gives Paladin a dropkick to the sternum, sending him back into the turnbuckle! Armageddon hops out to the apron, and climbs the turnbuckle! Bryan- Armageddon stands behind Paladin, hooking his head! Armageddon takes off, dragging Paladin down into a bulldog! He’s got the big man down! Yale- Armageddon gets right back up, and heads up the turnbuckle again! Paladin gets back up, as Armageddon takes off! He catches Paladin and twists him over with a flying head-scissors takedown! Bryan- That’s uncanny leverage by Armageddon! He turns over, and covers Paladin! ONE! TWO!! Paladin powers out! Yale- Paladin gets up, trying to regain his senses, as Armageddon stays on him! He moves in with a series of boots to the knee, as Paladin finally recovers and clotheslines Armageddon right down to the mat! Bryan- Paladin shakes the cobwebs out, as Armageddon slowly gets back up! Paladin slaps on a chokehold, and lifts him up! Chokeslam! Yale- That’s it! There’s a cover! ONE! TWO!! TH- Armageddon kicks out! Armageddon is showing some resilience, as Paladin lifts him up! Bryan- Paladin whips Armageddon into the ropes! He bounces back, and Paladin lifts him into the air with a guerilla press! Paladin drops him onto his shoulder, and sends him straight down with a sidewalk slam! Yale- Ouch! Paladin stares up at the crowd, as the fans start to get into this match! Paladin picks Armageddon up again, and whips him into the ropes! Armageddon holds on, reversing, and sliding back underneath Paladin’s legs! Bryan- Paladin loses sight of him, as Armageddon charges from behind, hitting Paladin with a chop block! Paladin goes down hard, as Armageddon goes up top! Yale- Paladin is still down, as Armageddon takes off! He hits a guillotine leg drop! Armageddon might have hurt his leg, but he’s covering Paladin! ONE! TWO!! NO! Bryan- Paladin gets a shoulder up, though not with as much authority as his earlier kickout. Armageddon lifts Paladin back up, and hops up onto his shoulders! Yale- Armageddon going for a hurricanrana, but is countered into a powerbomb! What a move! Paladin slowly gets back up, as Armageddon, somehow, pulls himself up using the ropes! Bryan- How is he doing that? Paladin sees the rising Armageddon, and grabs him in a front facelock! He hooks the leg, and MY GOD! He turns him over into a ferocious neckbreaker, and into a pinning situation! Yale- That’s called the Last Request! The ref counts! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! This one’s history! (The bell rings and the fans cheer as Paladin’s arm is raised. Peace Sells hits again, as Paladin heads to the back.) Andrews- Here is your winner. . . Paladin! Bryan- What a debut match form these two! Paladin has taken his first victory here, but Armageddon put forth an admirable effort! Yale- How did he get up after that powerbomb? There’s something not quite right about him, JB! Bryan- Well, we’re ready for our main event! And quite frankly, I can’t wait to see this mystery partner of Octavian’s! Yale- I’m telling you, JB, it’s got to be huge, whoever it is. This is President Caldera we’re talking about! Has he ever let us down when it comes to surprises? Bryan- I guess not. Let’s head down to ringside. (My Own Summer hits and the fans go crazy as the Jhub heads down to the ring. He steps through the ropes and cheers the fans on.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 275 pounds. . . The Jhub!!! (The Jhub sits back in the turnbuckle as Born to be Wild hits. The fans cheer monstrously as Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins emerges from the back, and heads to the ring.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, weighing in at 225 pounds. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins!! Yale- I hate these guys! I’m sure President Caldera has something up his sleeve for this match! (Physical plays, and the fans begin booing immediately. President Caldera walks out from the back, and heads to the announce table.) Andrews- Please welcome President Caldera! (Caldera sits at the announce table, and puts on a headset.) Yale- Hey, there, boss! Caldera- Quit brown-nosing, David, I’m not in the mood. Yale- Sorry, Mr. President. Bryan- Mr. Caldera, tell us, who is Octavian’s partner? Caldera- You’ll find out in just a few moments here. I think you’ll like this one. (Might of Rome hits, and the boos continue. Octavian walks out to the ringside area, smiling all the way, as fireworks explode on stage.) Andrews- And their opponents, introducing first, weighing in at 285 pounds. . . Octavian!! (Octavian steps onto the apron, waiting for his partner. Break Stuff hits, and the fans stand in question. They begin to boo once again, as John Reed emerges from the backstage area, and heads to the ring.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, weighing in at 275 pounds. . . John Reed! Bryan- John Reed?!? This is the guy that ended his brother’s career! Yale- Mr. Caldera, you are a genius! Bryan- Reed and Octavian step into the ring, as the bell sounds! The Jhub moves in, going after Reed, as Rollins meets Octavian! Here we go! Yale- I still can’t believe it! John Reed! Bryan- The Jhub powers Reed into the corner, and the ref separates Rollins and Octavian! Reed reverses the Jhub into the corner, and begins firing away with right hands! Yale- He’s got the Jhub reeling! The fans are going crazy! Wait, the Jhub blocks a shot! He fires a right of his own! And another! The Jhub is fighting out of the corner! Bryan- The Jhub rears back, and delivers a sharp right that sends Reed down to the canvass! Reed pops back up, as the Jhub fires another shot! But Reed ducks it, spins the Jhub around, and lifts him into a belly to back suplex! Yale- Reed gets back up, as the Jhub tries to recover! Reed whips the Jhub into the ropes! But Rollins makes a blind tag! Rollins springboards off the top rope, as the Jhub slides behind Reed! Bryan- Rollins hits Reed with a missile dropkick, and the Jhub is there to roll him up! ONE! TWO!! Reed kicks out! Yale- The ref shouldn’t have counted that! The Jhub’s not the legal man! Caldera- He’s fired. Bryan- The Jhub exits the ring, as Rollins lifts Reed up and suplexes him! Rollins floats over, and covers! ONE! TWO!! NO! Octavian moves in and makes the save! Yale- Rollins gets back up, glaring at the Italian Stallion! Wait, Reed gets up and hits a low blow! Rollins is doubled over, as Reed hooks him up and plants a DDT! Bryan- Reed turns over and tags in Octavian! Here come the boos, as Octavian goes to work on Rollins! Octavian drops a quick leg, then locks on a side headlock! The Jhub threatens to break it up, but the ref stops his progress. Yale- Octavian uses the opportunity to prop his legs on the bottom rope to gain leverage! Rollins screams out in pain, as the ref turns and Octavian drops back down to the mat. Bryan- The ref checks in on Rollins, as Octavian wrenches back! But the Renegade won’t give up, so Octavian releases him and drops another leg across his neck! Yale- Octavian covers Rollins! ONE! TWO!! Rollins kicks out! Octavian argues the count, but isn’t getting anywhere! Caldera- That ref is definitely fired. Bryan- Rollins scrambles towards the Jhub, but Octavian grabs him by the leg and pulls him back towards his corner. Octavian tags Reed back in, and holds Renegade’s arm up! Yale- Reed boots Rollins in his exposed ribcage, as Octavian steps back out to the floor. So tell us, President Caldera, where exactly does John Reed fit in to this picture? Caldera- You remember the last time we saw John Reed? He tossed his brother off the top of a cage and ended his career. Do I need any other reason to hire him? Bryan- Reed sends Rollins into the turnbuckle, and follows him in with a back elbow! Rollins staggers out of the corner, and Reed kicks him in the gut! Reed sets him up. . . Piledriver!! Yale- That’s it! Here’s a cover! ONE! TWO!! THR- NO! How on earth did Rollins kick out of that?! Bryan- Reed seems a bit frustrated, as Rollins reaches out in vain for his partner! But Reed stops him with a rear chinlock! Rollins seems to be getting a sudden burst of energy, as he tries to stand! Yale- Rollins is somehow getting to his feet! But Reed is still hanging on above him! Wait, Rollins sits down, and connects with a jaw breaker! Bryan- Great resourcefulness by the Renegade! Caldera- This is not good. Yale- Rollins reaches out for the Jhub again, but Reed has him by the boot! Rollins finally makes one last dive, and makes the tag! The Jhub leaps into the ring, as Octavian joins the fray as well! Bryan- The Jhub takes down Octavian with a clothesline! And there’s a boot to the gut of Reed! The Jhub hooks Reed and drops him in a DDT! Octavian rolls out to the floor, as Jhub covers Reed! Yale- ONE! TWO!! THR- NO! Reed kicks out! Reed is a tough guy, I’ll tell you. The Jhub lifts Reed up and sends him into the ropes! Reed bounces back, and the Jhub hooks him for the downward spiral! Bryan- NO! Reed counters with a knee to the gut, and drops the Jhub in a swinging neckbreaker! The Jhub is down, as Octavian reaches out for a tag and gets it! Yale- Reed wisely tags out, as Octavian hops in to go to work on the Jhub! Octavian stomps the Jhub in the spine, as the Jhub pulls himself up in the turnbuckle! Caldera- Come on, Octy, beat the crap out of him. Shut his big mouth. Bryan- Octavian takes the Jhub by the arm and whips him to the opposite turnbuckle. WAIT!
