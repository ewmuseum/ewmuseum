Kenjiro Ito Biography Real Name Kenjiro Ito Nicknames The Bringer of the Ice Age, the Future of Wrestling, The Cocky Osakan, the Bad Will Ambassador Hometown Osaka, Japan Birthday April 30, 1979 Height 6'4" Weight 256 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Kenjiro Ito has referred to himself as the Future of Wrestling from nearly day one of his career. At times, he has shown that promise, his tenure in Jolt Wrestling showing that he can be utterly dominant. At other times, though, he appears mortal, as his PRIME tenure can attest. Several things about Ito-san, though, are perfectly clear. He is a fuck. More than willing to say or do ANYTHING to get the attention of rivals both in and out of the ring, Ito has the talent to back up the shooting off of his mouth. Abandoning his homeland and its traditional approach to raising rookies, Ito came to the United States and established a reputation out of the gate. He has been out of the limelight for a year or so, licking his wounds, and waiting for the right time and place to return. The time is now. The place is GCW.
