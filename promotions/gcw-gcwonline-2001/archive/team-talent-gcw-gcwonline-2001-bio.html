Team TALENT Biography Biography TEAM HISTORY After being born into money, Noah and Carson went on to be amazingly average students at West Beverly Hills High. This wasn’t too much of a problem as they only ever wanted to do one thing - be actors. Unfortunately for them, their acting abilities didn’t match their dreams, leading them to a series of failed auditions, usually in soap operas. Luckily, the family money was still there and they opened up their own production company Tyler and Law Entertainment - TALENT for short. Camera flashes go off round the arena as the music builds to the vocal. As the vocal hits, Team TALENT’s entrance video plays and they emerge from backstage. They make their way to the ring posing for the crowd and checking themselves out. After climbing into the ring, they each pose on the turnbuckles, removing their shirts as they do so and sending the females in the crowd wild.
