WorldWide 91 US Airways Center — Phoenix, Arizona ---- Where GCW Happens ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Phoenix fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: We're live with a sold out crowd in the US Airways Center in Phoenix, Arizona for WorldWide! Thank you for joining us back on X:TV fans after last week's amazing Pay-Per-View in Las Vegas, and High Stakes was a shocking night from top to bottom! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan alongside David Yale, and Dave, in a night of surprises, what shocked you most? Yale: Thanks, JB, but I don't get surprised! I can see everything coming before it happens thanks to my keen nose and expert sources! But I'll pick the moment that undoubtedly shocked everyone else in the world, and that was the END of the year-long atrocity known as the Tag Title reign of Banned and Exiled~! It's OVER! It's OVER! It's finally over! The air smelled a little sweeter this morning, the sun's shining a little brighter, because LeStatt Knight is the holder of the GCW Tag Team Championships! Bryan: Somehow I think you're the only one that feels that way about it, but it's true, fans, the tremendous Tag Title reign of Banned and Exiled~! was ended when LeStatt Knight nearly killed Markus Stone... and scored the victory with a little help from Tessa Windsor I might add! Yale: What a great night it was all around! Knight is now the first Grand Slam Champion in GCW history, JB! Add that to his Hall of Fame career! And Tessa Windsor has evolved her game to another level, retaining her United States Title and picking up a mentor in LeStatt Knight, the greatest wrestler of all time! My heart's pounding again just thinking about it. Bryan: Well, Digital Mortality was screwed out of his shot at the US Title, and with Tessa's sudden change in attitude, I think we can bank on Digi having a think or two to say about the situation! And in other developments, Chris Bagwell finally put his money where his mouth is and defeated Andy Murray! Yale: Bagwell's really going to extreme lengths to humiliate Murray, JB. Valentine will have to defend her World Title against the number one contender... and she's lucky to still be champion after what Jordan White did! So let's get this thing underway! ---- Hail The Champion(s)! The lights cut out, the only light coming are the ones brought in from the flashing cameras scattered throughout the arena. From behind the curtains walks GCW's most recognizable face, LeStatt Knight, one tag team title wrapped around his waist and the other draped over his shoulder. The boos are instantly heightened. Affixed to it is a temporary nameplate which reads “Thomas J. LeStrange.” It is a reasonably smart sign, fashioned out of metal, with the letters carved in an elegant serif hand, however upon any further inspection, the shadow it casts on the door and the visible gap between the two objects reveal that it has been stuck onto the plywood with poster putty, rather undermining LeStrange's stab towards the grandiose. Yale: This could be bad, bad news for LeStrange… Accompanied by loud cheers, the imposing figure of Andy Murray, tall and broad (several inches and many pounds larger than his English counterpart, LeStrange, who himself was not diminutive) striding towards the camera, his face a curious juxtaposition of anger and calm. His face appears cool, collected, possibly determined. But in the tiny details - the slightly narrowed eyes; the tautness in the corner of his lips from the clenching of his teeth, and the flare of his nostrils, one can see the barely-concealed vitriol crashing through the Scotsman's arteries. With a large intake of breath, Murray pushes the door open without knocking. Yale: Andy Murray does NOT look in the best of moods tonight! Bryan: Can you blame him!? Last week at High Stakes we witnessed Thom LeStrange Albion Drop the hell out of Murray, effectively killing off his chances of defeating Chris Bagwell! Yale: Pah, Bagwell had it won anyway! LeStrange was but a mere coincidence… Murray: English. Bone to pick. Now. As he surveys the room, he is met with an unsettling view. Everything appears almost as he had expected, but yet somehow all is different. The most obvious difference is the yawning chasm on the wall, the vast tract of empty wallpaper where previously a family portrait has been hung. Now it is nowhere to be seen, and the wall seems somehow empty; naked without it. One can only speculate that this is another consequence of the intense denial that the young LeStrange has exhibited of late following the tragic death of his parents: he now wishes to see no reminder of his kin for fear he may have to digest and accept the truth. Instead of the customary selection of fine cheeses and champagne on the low coffee table in the middle of the room, there now sat several bottles of whiskey, all at various degrees of emptiness already. The gramophone player still lies on a table in the corner, at present playing the Overture from Mozart's Magic Flute. The vigour and liveliness of the tune belie the sombre surroundings, almost acting as some horrendous melancholic joke. Next to it lies a familiar stack of well-thumbed books; Apollinaire's Alcools and Nerval's Les Chimeres, two of LeStrange's favourite French poets. However, Thom was rapidly becoming as unstable and as unseated as the mad poets themselves. The camera finally moves to LeStrange himself. As always he sits upon an almost throne-like armchair, made of mahogany and covered with scarlet velvet held in place with golden studs. But unlike the imposing, authoritative regal figure he has previously cut, Thom LeStrange now resembles nothing more than a mad old monarch in the final stages of senility and decrepitude. More slumped than sitting in the chair, his hair is lank and unkempt, his skin sallow and wan, his eyes hollow and glazed. He still wears his expensive, elegant bespoke suit, but the shirt is untucked, the top button undone and the tie knot loose and low. Murray: Jesus H.! You look like shit… Spoken by anyone else, these words may have sounded like genuine worry cloaked in macho nomenclature. Spoken by Andy Murray, it is pure insult.
