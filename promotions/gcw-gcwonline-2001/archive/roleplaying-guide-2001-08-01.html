Roleplaying Guide

Setting- The place the interview takes place in does matter. Instead of always using the ring as your interview site, try mixing it up. Try a setting that enhances your character. If your character is a fun-loving type guy, have him be in a bar, laughing it up with some friends. If he's dark and mysterious, try the boiler room. Be descriptive when talking about your setting, as well. It does make a difference.

Dialogue- What your wrestler says may be a bit overrated in its importance, but it definitely makes up the bulk of the roleplay. Try to stay in character. Don't rip off dialogue from a real wrestler. Stay original. When replying to another wrestler's roleplays, read it carefully. Look for things you can turn around or focus on. Pick it apart and really go after them. Don't be afraid to be mean. However, this does not mean one should use things such as 'I'm not stupid, you're stupid.' Or 'You just got that from the WWF' or things like that. Don't bring up real federations in roleplays.

Closing-Wrapping up a promo is a tough part of it. Coming up with a catchphrase can help, but, again, keep it original. Leave us with something to think about, don't just end it.

My apologies that this is so short. A better version will be up soon.
