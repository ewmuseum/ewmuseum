Wes Sanders Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames 'The Rage' 'The Last Boyscout' Hometown Salt Lake City, Utah Birthday 28th December 1976 Height 6'1 Weight 227 Biography Little to none is known about Wes Sanders before he joined the WWA, some believed he hailed from England, others believe he was born and abandoned as a child. Even his real name is not known. He is recognised as a local amature in the underground martial arts circuit but he was under then name 'Surge'. Mark Harrington obviously had his talent scouts looking more or less everywhere as he signed a WWA Wrestling training contract in November 1999. Harrington saw talent in this youngster training him to be a Lucra Libra fighter but never knew the true nature of this being. He started to fight in the WWA back in Early Febuary 2000 where he became number 1 contender in 1 month 14days the quickest in WWA history. This was mainly due to him winning March Madness 2000 winning over TJ Donaldson, this led to Rage taking on the un-defeatable Nik Seiger in May, Rage lost, and with it he lost something else, Rage then had spells in the WWA back and forth which led up till the date of 14th May 2002 14th May 2002 a lost stranger was found bumming around a diner looking very rough, eyes bloodshot and his breath stunk of booze, Rage had decided to turn to drink for comfort, but this is where his life changed. He met people just like himself in bad spots and with that Wes Sanders was born, a man who wouldn't look back would not quit, and would never be defeated like Rage was in May 2000. He respects all he he is facing and demands that respect back, he gives nothing but 110% in all matches. Now known as the last boyscout, by the late great Ian Tense Wes has moved on from losing his rival and best friend in the WWA. GCW has accepted him, but will he accept them. His long and painful past might come back to haunt him. Like most things Time. Will. Tell.
