Neon Biography Real Name Ryo Kazaki Nicknames The 10,000 Watt Wonder, Deathmatch Kid, Barely Legal, The Stunt Show Hometown Nagasaki, Japan Birthday Novemeber 21, 1988 Height 6'0 Weight 191 Biography At 7 years old he performed his first back flip off the school jungle gym in front of an audience of 40 juveniles. After landing on his feet and soaking in the applause, before being reprimanded by the school officials of course, he knew risk-taking was his calling. So it was only natural when at 15 years old, Ryo Kazaki sought out the great deathmatch icon, Gunner 'Beast' Gojoh and begged to be taught how to wrestle. This is of course where I'd regal you with how Ryo idolized Jushin Liger, Misawa and the like...but this is not one of those stories. Yes, wrestling is fun for the Barely Legal Daredevil...but it's the roar of the crowd and nurturing of showmanship that keeps Neon coming back. This has nothing to do with tradition or proving he's the best, Ryo could already tell you he is. It's all about stealing the show and doing the moves NOBODY else can do. His crash and burn 'spotfest' style is widely hated by traditionalists of puroresu, but Kazaki is the darling of the Japanese youth (due to his good looks and cocky swagger) and a sure-fire legend in the deathmatch scene. It could also be said the hatred for his style, by insiders at least, is so widespread because Ryo has the ability to be more than just a 20 minute stunt show. He just chooses not to evolve to that style. At 18 years old, the bright lights of stardom and all the vices that come with it are his only concern. With the young phenom being responsible for rising attendance at 'garbage fed' independents, especially with young females, many wonder who will be the fed to take a risk and bring the 10,000 Watt Wonder to the big time. Curbing his spotty style could very well reveal the next great lightweight in Japan. But with his cocky attitude and history of backstage headaches, would he truly be worth it? You may very well be in the main event...but it will be Ryo they remember when the last pinfall is counted. Even if not for the right reasons.
