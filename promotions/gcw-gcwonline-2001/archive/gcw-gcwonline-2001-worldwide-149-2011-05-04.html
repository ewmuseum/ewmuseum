WorldWide 149 Charlotte Bobcats Arena — Charlotte, North Carolina ---- WW149: State of the Union Undressed The camera slowly fades in from black, revealing the simple but powerful setup of a wooden desk with a massive American flag hanging as a backdrop. Walking into view, Chief of Staff Rich "The Renegade" Rollins raps his fingers along the smooth mahogany while kicking a large swivel-chair in front of him. He spins the chair once, then slides into it and sits, eyes locking on the camera. More well-dressed than usual, the Renegade sports a finely-tailored gray suit opened down the middle to reveal a blue tee-shirt with his old Era of Anarchy symbol scrawled across the front. Grinning underneath a trademark bandana, Rollins folds his hands and leans forward on the desk. Rollins: Question... What was the last thing to go through Osama bin Laden's head? He pauses for dramatic effect, tilting his chin to the side as he peers just slightly off-camera. In an instant his gaze snaps back. Rollins: Answer... A 5.56 millimeter motherfucking BULLET. Eat shit and die, Al Qaeda. You don't fuck with the red, the white, and the blue. Unclasping his hands, Rich Rollins spreads them to either side, pressing his palms down on the desktop. He smiles. Rollins: Now that I've got that outta my system, I'd like to take this moment to address YOU (he points at the camera) the GCW general public. Since this time last year, ticket sales have seen more profitable bumps than a glass tabletop in Charlie Sheen's living room. Our Television division has gone high-def. Tessa Windsor won a World title. We saw one legend retire, while another one... (he grins) came back from the dead. We watched the Reign of Terror crash to a goddamn halt at NC-17, a campaign that was funded by none other than the wrasslin' war criminal himself, Steven Caldera. The number of nips on our active roster are at an all-time high, while the number of Chris Bagwell title victories have maintained an even zero percent. In short, we're entering a Golden Age of Global Championship Wrestling, and we got one man to thank for it... Garbage Bag Johnny. Offering a respectful nod, the Renegade's face sags ever so slightly. Rollins: Unfortunately, John-boy won't be with us tonight. Thanks to an unfortunate encounter with the windshield of Ecto-1 last week, our beloved Commissioner is holed up in a hospital with more drugs in his system than Lindsey Lohan at a Daft Punk concert. In his absence he has asked me, your Chief of Staff, to maintain some semblance of order until he can return. Leaning back, Rich Rollins kicks his feet up on the desk and lets out a gut-busting laugh. Rollins: Yeah. I know. Watchin' me keep order is about like watchin' Stevie Wonder paint by numbers, so needless to say, tonight is gonna be epic. We've got a Hardcore title match, eight Rampage qualifiers, and two massive tag-team trials to manage this evening. I dunno how Johnny does this from week to week, I really don't. But I ain't Johnny, and tonight, we do things the Rollins Way... which is to say, any which way we damn well please. In addition, yours truly has a pretty goddamn massive announcement to make later tonight, and since ain't nobody here to tell me otherwise, I can do and say whatever the hell I want. Eat your heart out, Steven Caldera. In the immortal words of Rick James, "Fuck yo cou-" Sure enough, as unexpected as ever and out of nowhere, in stumbles Triumph Frost, enjoying a fine Texas brew and acting like he just don't give a fuck. Which he don't, in case you were wondering. He takes one look at the pimped-out nouveau commissioner, bandana and all, and can’t help but laugh. Triumph Frost: Holy shit. Hey, can you get me the directions to 21 Jump Street? As Rollins prepares to say something, Triumph does as he’s wont, and interrupts. Triumph Frost: I gotta know, what were Emilio Estevez and Judd Nelson like in 1988? Rollins’ blood continues to boil, as Triumph’s grin only grows. Triumph Frost: Wait, wait. One more. I assume you gave Richard Grieco pretty good head to get to raid his wardrobe. Triumph continues chuckling to himself, as Rollins is anything but pleased. When Triumph finally composes himself, he lets out a calm comment. Triumph Frost: Right. Continue, please. Swinging his feet off the desk with an incredulous, deliberate sluggishness, the Rene-Chief eyeballs his unwanted guest. Clearly not impressed or amused, he adopts a rigid posture that the ear-marked copy of Managing Dummies for Dummies laying on the corner of his workspace would call "the Power Play." Rollins scowls. Rollins: I'm in the middle of an important Address, you fucking half-bred snatchwaffle. Do you not see the flag? From what I hear, you already got one monkey on your back, Frost. You sure as shit don't want me as a third wheel. What. The fuck. Do you want? Triumph Frost: Nothing. Triumph pauses and pulls out a tiny single-serving bag of Jawbreakers from his pocket. He tosses one in his mouth and talks as it rattles around. Triumph Frost: Well, there is something, but I don’t think a jerk-ass bonebag like yourself can do shit about it for me. The Renegade arches his brow, cocks his head, and leans heavily on his desk. He shoots Triumph an annoyed frown that he follows up with an extremely condescending tsk-tsk. Rollins: You obviously weren't paying attention just now. Garbage Bag John-boy is on the shelf this week. I'm runnin' this joint. Tell me what I can and can't do again, please, I'm all ears. If you got sumthin' to say, spit it out. Triumph bites down on the Jawbreaker, unleashing a large crunching noise, and washes the crushed flecks coated with red dye #40 down with a gulp of the brew. Triumph Frost: Obviously… He lets out a candy-coated smile, and nods. He takes another sip, and just seems to warm up to the whole situation in general. Triumph Frost: Alright, you Hell’s Pussy-looking motherfucker. I’ve an idea. So, umm… Triumph surveys around, as if expecting to spot someone or something out of the ordinary. When he surmises there isn’t anyone in probably fifty feet of the area that isn’t a former double champion, he gets comfortable. Triumph Frost: Ahh… Triumph walks over to Rollins, and leans forward, whispering something into his ear. Rollins’ eyebrows pique in the meanwhile, and when Triumph takes a step back, he’s surely pleased with himself. Triumph Frost: So? A mischievous grin marches across the Rene-Chief's face. Finally, Rollins nods. Rollins: Consider it done, Frostbite McTriumph. Consider it done. Swallowing a mouthful of Jawbreaker, Triumph Frost loads another one and nods his head, pleased with himself. He turns to leave, but Rich Rollins holds up a hand. Rollins: Not so fast. Spinning on one heel, Triumph stops mid-chew and turns back to face the Renegade, his lips pursed with sugary salivation. His beer sloshes a little at the sudden twist, but he compensates like a professional and doesn't spill a drop. Rollins' eyes narrow dangerously. Rollins: If you fuck me on this, it's your ass. And yes, I just used the words "fuck me" and "your ass" in a sentence. Deal with it. Triumph can’t help but toss the last of the Jawbreakers in his mouth and just sheepishly nod in agreement of Rollins’ edict. Triumph Frost: Gotcha, boss. And with a smile, Triumph makes his exit.
