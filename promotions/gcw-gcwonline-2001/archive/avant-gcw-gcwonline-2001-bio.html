Avant Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames 45-Alive Hometown Sun Valley, Idaho Birthday February 12th, 1975 Height 6'3' Weight 227 Fed Titles None yet. Fed Awards None yet. Biography Coming VERY soon. And, unlike a lot of people in the fedding world that are being spoiled with CGI backstage scripting, I truly mean that. Other Feds AWC, PRIME Wrestling Style Hardcore/Loose Finisher Move Paradigm Shift Finisher Description Top rope - Snap Flip while opponent is on the mat - Before impact, situates himself into a sitting position and executes and rediculously dangerous double leg drop. Finisher Setup Move Anything that knocks the bastard down and out. Setup Description Pick a card, any card. Other Moves Avant tends to do things to the absolute extreme. He'll risk 100% of his body to put on an entertaining show. Needless to say, a lot of his moves are very much so involved and are incredible dangerous. Rigormortis Hookjaw - The opponent must be sitting on the top rope for this to work. Avant hooks said opponent's head in between his bent arm and uses his head to form a lodging-effect. Avant must be standing on the bottom rope. He jumps off and lands on his knees, sending said opponent's jaw into Avant's shoulder, crushing it. Poison Vinegar Machine - The outcome is comparable to an enziguri, but the opponent doesn't counter at all. Avant irish whips said opponent into the ropes and stops him in the middle by using his foot like a bumper. The opponent doesn't know what's going on, which gives Avant adequate amounts of time to raise his leg to the opponents shoulder. (he'll jump if he has to) Avant jumps and uses the momentum from the other leg swinging in the opposite direction to spin until the swinging foot makes contact with the back of said opponent's head. The sound is to the effect of a hundred elastic bands hitting the same wooden surface. This, unfortunately, only happens on special occasions now because of an incident that left him with a shattered coccyx. Angst In Animation - Scoop slam that turns into a modified snap Pearl River Plunge, bar the pin at the end. Super DDTs - A lot of high risk DDT's that showcase him flying off the top rope and out of the ring in order to sacrifice as much as possible. Flying Superkick - Usually occurs after a strong whip into the ropes. Avant will bounce himself off the opposite side of the ring and jump, with one foot pointed out significantly, at his opponent and knock him upside his jaw. Sit-Down Powerbomb - Does this frequently. Similar to the 'Pearl River Plunge' that Ahmed Johnson, formerly of the WWF, used throughout his tenure. Quite powerful and can sometimes be the nail in the coffin pending on the opponent. Shoulder-tackle-through-the-ropes-thing - So yeah, if his opponent is dazed and unaware of what's going on, Avant will through him outside of the ring and bounce himself off the ropes afterwards. The outcome? He executes a baseball slide through the middle rope and clocks his opponent, in the face no less, with a shoulder tackle. Greetings with a chinbreaker from the upstairs - Avant holds the arm of his opponent while he's sitting on the top rope. Mind you, Avant's on the middle rope. He catapults himself off, while having his opponent's head hooked around the grip of his arm and lands on his ass, sending his opponent to his knees and getting his jaw crushed. He has a softspot for submission moves to. Here's three of his favorites: Camel Clutch Double-twisted Figure-four lock (This hurts both the opponent AND Avant but is very effective.) Scorpian Lock - Crosses his opponent's legs and winds them back until there touching his/her backside. Avant then proceeds to stand on his opponent's ankles. Avant's Entrance The opening guitar riff of 'Kindred Spirits' by Liquid Tension Experiment crunches and crawls throughout the arena. White searchlights circulate throughout the crowd as the opening snare beat takes hold of the flame. Crimson reds and exotic greens flourish the walls and ring canvas as the video tron showcases the word 'Avant' in numerous different settings. The last setting being a brick wall, which crumbles to the ground and sends sparks shooting from the base of the stage. Avant, in all of his strange glory, comes running out with both hands up high in the air. Screaming to whoever about whatever, a huge grin peels across his face as he immediately slides into the ring. He jumps to one of the turnbuckles and raises his fists towards the crowd before his music slowly dies down. Entrance Music 'Kindred Spirits' by Liquid Tension Experiment
