Daria Ngozi Biography Real Name Daria Emily Ngozi Nicknames The Daring Diva Hometown Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo Birthday unknown Height 5'6 Weight 125 lbs Fed Titles Former GCW TV Champion Fed Awards none Other Feds SCCW/FUSE, Global Other Fed Awards none Strengths Good striker. Very fast and agile. Unorthodox. Weaknesses Small. Bad temper. Stubborn - she will not give up a fight once it has been started and will pass out or cause herself serious injury by remaining in a submission hold because she will not tap out. Wrestling Style Striking oriented high flying, submissions Finisher Move (1) Delirium (2) Genocide Finisher Description Delirium - spinning capoeira kick. Genocide - front flip piledriver. Finisher Setup Move No main setup either move can come from many different situations. Special Moves Beyond This World (corkscrew moonsault) Broken (springboard into a Busaiku knee kick) Chiropractic (straight jacket hold into a back cracker) Other Moves Daria is most definately striking oriented in her approach, and this will always be what she goes to first. She is very elusive due to her training in capoeira, and uses a lot of counters such as cartwheels, rolls, and sweeps. Unorthodox style, a bit rough around the edges but otherwise effective. In addition she has very strong legs, so most of her submission holds will be holds that use the legs to apply pressure. She loves to use combination striking to wear opponents down for her high flying and submission offense.
