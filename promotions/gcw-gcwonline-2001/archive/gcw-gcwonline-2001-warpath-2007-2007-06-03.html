Warpath 2007 Miami, Florida ---- Warpath: A History Global Championship Wrestling burst onto the scene in April 2001 with its first Pay-Per-View blockbuster, Warpath. On that night, eight men stepped into the ring for a one-night tournament to decide who would become the first ever GCW World Heavyweight Champion. On that night, Octavian made history, defeating John Reed, Thirteen and The Jhub on his way to the first of three World Title reigns. In 2007, four men remain in a month-long tournament for a shot at that very same belt. Six years after GCW's first foray into Pay-Per-View programming, four men have battled through the very same tournament for a shot at the very same prize. A chance to compete for the biggest prize in professional wrestling is on the line tonight. LeStatt Knight, Vivica J. Valentine, Jordan White and Twizzy will battle it out, each needing two victories in one night over world-class competition, just for a chance to fight the Heavyweight Champion of the World. Tonight, GCW celebrates its sixth year with the biggest show of 2007. Tonight, every able-bodied member of the GCW roster steps into the ring for a taste of the Pay-Per-View spotlight.
