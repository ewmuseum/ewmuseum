The Guild Biography Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography The ultimate “game geeks', Dave Montgomery (Paladin) & Jim Brandt (Meat) have progressed from table-top gaming, to MMORPG’s, to Live-action Role-play and beyond. Either having conquered or been banned (for taking it way too literal, like challenging the local SCA “Duke' to a blood duel in his front yard on a non-SCA Tuesday afternoon) from their previous paths of adventure, they now quest to become champions in GCW. Personality: The Rogue Paladin: The brains of the duo. He’s the thinker/planner/lead speaker for the team. Lightning fast and technically proficient in the ring, fights honorably until the opponents prove themselves unworthy of that honor. If the situation calls, can be insanely sadistic and hardcore (preferred weapon is a kendo stick) because he’s “dual-class' with a high “weapons proficiency'. Meatshield Trainwrecker: The power of the team. He’s a big, tubby, ox of a man that can absorb impact like none other. He brawls and mauls; and while he has a similar sense of “honor' as TDP, his application is a little less discriminate. There’s little room for niceties in his style of physical melee. Appearance/Apparel: TDP has shoulder length brown hair and brown eyes. To date he has been completely unable to grow a beard, with vexes him to no end. Although his build is slight, his shoulders and forearms are quite defined (from archery and swordplay practice). To the ring he wears a Hunter Green traveling cloak (with gold piping). He wears Hunter Green long tights with a golden templar cross on the thigh. Black boots with a gold +5 on the side (because they’re magical boots that are +5 to hit) and black kneepads and elbow pads. Often he wears a black “The Guild’t-shirt but occasionally when the mood strikes him he’ll wear other shirts (like Thundercats, Green Lantern, Transformers, the Mikey Whipwreck dragon one, etc..) Out of the ring he usually wears cargo shorts and one of those silly anime button downs. If it’s a formal occasion, he has a wide array of tunics, cloaks, and frilly renaissance shirts to choose from. PS: He also has a full set of armor and a sword stored away. Meatshield Trainwrecker can be described in one word. Round. He doesn’t suffer from man-boob syndrome yet, but he’s got a thick coating of blubber (although there is some definition in his arms). He keeps his jet black hair close cropped, except for his shaggy mutton chop/mustache facial feature. His eyes are a piercing hazel, and he normally has a displeased expression on his face (as if he’s pissed because you’re staring at him because he’s fat.). He wears a black cloak to the ring, with a skull and crossed axes on the back. His pants are black, his boots are black, his pads are black. The only thing that isn’t black is the silver skull that acts as his belt buckle. Out of the ring he usually wears jeans and any of a myriad of metal band t-shirts he’s collected.
