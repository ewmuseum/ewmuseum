Shaman Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Shaman Hometown Mojave, Arizona Birthday 5/30/1974 Height 6'9' Weight 330 lbs Fed Titles GCW Tv, 2x GCW US, GCW Tag x2, GCW Hardcore Biography He started his wrestling career surprisingly enough as a boxer. How did this fit in with wrestling. Simple he watched several wrestlers train and made his move to wrestling liking the sport much more. Working hard in his career he started off hard and fast going to GCWF and winning the British title against the likes of Darrian O'Toole. Staying with GCWF for a few years he managed to hod,t he title the longest there. Never challenging for the Heavyweight title was a mystery to even him as he departed and moved on to an umbrella federation called CWC where he joined FSA and SCWF. Both federations had alot to offer and alot of influence in his career, But after winning some titles there he felt the need to move on. He then joined NCW which later became the WWA. In the WWA he had spent the majority of his wrestling career. After winning all the titles available to him he seemingly became bored. He was edgy and verbally attacked the staff and crews. he had developed many stables and joined several in his Tenure. His most memorable one was the Four Horsemen of Apocalypse and The Militia. Winning several self styled matches he became the man best known for the Himalayan Pit match. After his last title reign he seemed to become even more closed off and decided it was time move on after an agreement could not be met with his contractual agreements and a new contract was offered and wasn't up to the standards he wanted. Moving to GCW he hopes to find that all of his years of hard work will give him enough momentum to bring in the fires and passion he had set aside so long ago at feeling his routine was mundane. Shaman came into GCW with a full head of steam hitting Bryan Mayhem head on and capturing the GCW TV Title after several brutal encounters between the two. Shaman later on headed on down the road as he and Mayhem joined forces to create Oblivion one of the most dominant forces in GCW history. Mayhem shortly after disappeared leaving Shaman to his own devices. During this time Shaman once again found some steam and headed into the US Title scene and won it not once, but twice. Reforming Oblivion with Bryan Mayhem after a succession of brutal and grueling encounters between the two had set a precedence between the two men. They ran amok through the GCW ranks once again and shortly there after captured the GCW tag team titles. Unfortunately only to lose them to there annoying rivals Banned & Exiled. What is in store next for Shaman has yet to be written. Other Feds WWA, GCWF, SCWF, and FSA Other Fed Titles WWA Heavy x2, WWA US x1, WWA TV x2, WWA Extreme x7, WWA Tag x1, Fsa South x1, GCWF British x1, SCWF Middle x1. Other Fed Awards WWA heavyweight x2, WWA US Champion x 1, WWA TV champion x2, WWA Tag Team champion x1, WWA Extreme Champion x 7, SCWF West Coast Champ x1, GCWF British Champion x2, FSA Southern Coast champion x1, GCW Television Title x1, GCW US Title x2, GCW Tag Team title x1.
