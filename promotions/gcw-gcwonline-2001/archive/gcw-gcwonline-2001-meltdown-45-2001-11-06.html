GCW — Card

Meltdown 45, 7 February 2002

7 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 45 Bicentennial Center — Salina, Kansas ---- Meltdown #46 (The GCW logo flashes quickly over the screen, before the Meltdown music cues up. The lights in the arena dim, as fireworks quickly begin to explode from the stage! The crowd goes wild with excitement, as the fireworks finally end their fantastic display, and the cameras pan over the fans. James Bryan finally chimes in on cue, as the crowd continues to cheer.) Bryan- GCW continues its tour of Canada, as we come to you live from Winnipeg, Ontario! And tonight, we hope to finally get answers to some questions still remaining from the fallout of Rampage! And of course, the question at the top of that list, is who assaulted the Jhub? Yale- Come on, JB! We already know! Octavian cost the Jhub the United States title! And the Establishment is still riding high! Last week, Michael ‘Spitfire’ Stevens joined the ranks of the Establishment, making it that much stronger! And rumor has it, another person will be joining tonight! Bryan- That remains to be seen, Dave, but I must admit, I am worried about the growing power of the Establishment! And now without a commissioner to fight them, I don’t know who can stop the Establishment! (The cameras take a final glance at the crowd, before heading backstage. We find a full view of the large dress shoes of a rather elegant man. The camera slowly pans upward, moving past the man’s business suit, before revealing the face that belongs to President Steven Caldera. The crowd instantly begins to boo, as Caldera smiles a grim smile. As the camera backs out even more, we find that Michael ‘Spitfire’ Stevens stands with Caldera, earning even more boos from the already angry crowd. Stevens smiles widely, seemingly excited about something. The two stand in the parking garage, in front of a large GCW semi truck. The two continue to glance out at the entrance, obviously waiting for someone to arrive.) Caldera- He must have been held up in customs again. Too bad those Canadian morons don’t know who they’re dealing with. Stevens- Jason will be here soon enough, Steven. Here in a minute, I’m going to run and find NBK. We still need to talk to that guy. (Suddenly, a pair of headlights appears in the entrance way. Caldera and Stevens jump to the alert, as the vehicle pulls into view. However, the limo they were expecting is nowhere in sight. Instead, a small El Camino pulls into the garage, and swings to a clumsy halt in a parking spot. Caldera and Stevens stare in awe of the broken down vehicle, as the door swings open. The crowd begins to cheer as GCW seamstress Pete steps out of the car. The car noticeable rises as Pete’s weight exits the car, and he slams the door shut. With a smile, Pete waddles his way towards an incredulous Caldera, who attempts to ignore Pete by turning to Stevens. However, Pete insists on being acknowledged, and steps in front of Caldera.) Pete- Hey Mr. President! Caldera- Go away, Pete. Pete- Aww, come on! What’s going on here? Super-secret Establishment meeting? Come on, let me in! Caldera- NO! Pete- Please? I want to help be a part of the most powerful group in GCW history! I want to help you guys to dominate wherever you go! Caldera- You want to be powerful? Pete- More than anything! (Caldera, suddenly getting an idea, smiles at Stevens. Stevens gets a questionable look on his face, as Caldera now begins to smile at Pete.) Caldera- Fine. Tell you what. Why don’t you become the new… GCW Commissioner? Pete- Commissioner!? Would I get to be in the Establishment! Caldera- Of course! If you wanted to be powerful, then all you have to do to become Commissioner… Is defeat Michael Stevens here tonight! Pete- Establishment member against Establishment member? Is that good? Caldera- Yes. (Pete considers this, as Stevens catches on to Caldera’s plan. The two men smile at the confused Pete, whose face finally lights up.) Pete- I’ll do it! Caldera- Excellent! Whoever wins that match tonight will be Commissioner! Good luck, Pete. You’d better go get ready! Pete- Thank you, Mr. President! (Pete quickly walks away, hauling his rather large duffle bag over his shoulder. Caldera takes a position next to Stevens, who is now beaming.) Caldera- Want to be commissioner? (Stevens simply smiles widely and nods, as they wave good-bye to Pete. Stevens nods back at Caldera, before leaving the garage and heading back inside. The cameras then return to ringside.) Bryan- Oh, come on! It seems rather obvious to me that Michael Stevens is being set up to become the new commissioner here tonight! Yale- Well of course, JB! The Rampage winner against the seamstress?! Who do you think is going to win!? Bryan- Well, fans, that debacle aside, GCW is preparing for SubZero on February 25th, but just two months from now, on April 28th, 2002, GCW presents their big one-year anniversary Pay-Per-View extravaganza, Warpath! We’re coming to you live from the already sold out Madison Square Garden, but a few more tickets will become available next Monday at ten in the morning! Don’t miss out, because it will be your last chance to see Warpath live! Yale- And you don’t want to miss out! (Once again, the cameras head backstage, and we find NBK, the United States Champion, arriving in his room. He drops his duffle bag onto the floor, and pulls the US title from around his waist, hanging it on the coat rack in the corner. With a sigh he unzips his bag, as the crowd reacts with an undecided chorus of both boos and cheers. With a start, NBK jumps, as a knock echoes through the room. NBK turns to the door and swings it open, revealing Michael Stevens on the other side. NBK sighs and simply turns away, though Stevens enters the room.) Stevens- Hey, Killer, how’s it going? (NBK remains silent, continuing to unload his bag.) Stevens- Listen, that offer is still on the table. I took the step last week, man. I joined the Establishment. This right here is a once in a lifetime offer, NBK. This right here is the step everyone wants to take. And tonight, on this night, you are going to take that step. (NBK, now annoyed with Spitfire’s continued presence, quickly spins around to come face to face with Stevens. Spitfire remains smug as NBK delivers a gruesome glare, staring Stevens down. The crowd begins to buzz, but NBK simply turns back around, finally speaking in an angry tone.) NBK- Leave. (Stevens, taken aback now, subtly begins pulling the United States belt off its hook. NBK continues to unload his gear, as Stevens clutches the US belt. With a sudden burst of movement, Stevens clobbers NBK in the back of the head with the belt, sending NBK to the floor. The crowd boos with anger of Stevens’ assault, as Spitfire drops the belt and delivers a big boot to NBK’s skull. NBK lies reeling, as Stevens exits the room.) Stevens- I want your answer TONIGHT! (Stevens disappears, as we watch NBK struggle to his feet, and the cameras fade to a commercial break.) (Meltdown returns, and we find a replay of Spitfire’s assault. As we watch Spitfire level NBK, the announcers discuss the heinous attack.) Bryan- Fans, just before the break, we witnessed Michael Stevens with an unprovoked assault on NBK! Yale- Unprovoked?! JB, NBK is playing rather hard to get with joining the Establishment, and Stevens was just accelerating his decision! Bryan- Well, Stevens has demanded an answer tonight, but he’s still got to face Pete of all people for the commissionership tonight! (The cameras head to ringside, where Joey Andrews waits in the ring.) Andrews-Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall. (Boogieman hits and LeStatt Knight makes his way to the ring.) Andrews-Now approaching the ring, from Mexico City, Mexico, weighing in at 245 pounds, LeStatt Knight! (Knight rolls under the ropes and mounts a turnbuckle to a mixed reaction before getting down and pacing back and forth.) Yale-It's amazing to see Knight standing after that brutal beating he took at the hands of Payne at Rampage. We haven't seen him since then. Bryan-After taking three of those Lessons in Payne, I'm surprised his neck isn't broken, especially that one he took on the chair. (Burn starts playing as the crowd erupts with boos. Demented appears on the stage as pyros go off around him.) Andrews-And his opponent, representing the Agents of Oblivion, from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing in at 225 pounds, he is Demented! Yale-The man formerly known as Chameleon was turned back into Demented at the hands of The Lunatic Bryan Mayhem. Bryan-And ever since the change we've seen a more violent side of Demented emerge. He took a severe beating last week at the hands of the Hard*core champion Luke Hanson. But he did come close to winning the title before a DeFueler put him down. Yale-And the bell sounds as Demented finally gets in the ring. Bryan-Both men charge in and lock up and Knight gets the early advantage pushing Demented back to the ropes. Yale-And the referee calls for the break, and Knight breaks and then unloads with a right hand! Bryan-Knight is just wailing on Demented now with right hands! He sends him to the ropes, Demented ducks a clothesline and comes off the other side and just speared Knight! Yale-And now here's where Demented needs to stay on Knight. He needs to keep Knight down and punish him if he wants to win. Bryan-That's why I like working with you Dave. You clear up so many things for me. Yale-Thank you. I do what I can. Bryan-Anyway, Demented pulls Knight back to his feet and kicks him in the midsection before dropping him with an implant DDT. He tries for a quick cover but Knight kicked out after one. Yale-Demented is just toying with Knight now. He knew he wasn't going to be able to keep him down. Bryan-Demented twists the arm now and runs to the ropes. Demented jumped over the top rope snapping the arm of Knight as he did! That may have dislocated Knight's shoulder! Yale-Knight writhing in pain on the mat and Demented just yanked Knight out under the bottom rope and slams that arm into the steel steps! He puts Knight into a hammerlock and just slammed him on the floor! Bryan-Smart wrestling here by Demented regardless of if you like him or not. He's taking a body part and working it over. He takes that arm of Knight out, and it's going to limit LeStatt's offense. Yale-And tossing Knight back in the ring, Cham--I mean Demented is going upstairs. He waits for Knight to get back to his feet, and hits a missile dropkick to the right shoulder, continuing to work that arm over. Bryan-Demented grabs the arm now and twists it up into a hammerlock, and starts wailing on the shoulder again, repeatedly slamming his forearm into the shoulder, and you can see the pain on Knight's face. Yale-Demented now, takes Knight over to the corner, wraps the right arm around the top rope and once again starts pounding the shoulder. Bryan-Now he pulls him out of the corner and goes for the chicken wing bulldog and dropped him! He rolls him over for the cover! He's got one, he's got two, but not three! Demented looks frustrated as Knight kicked out! Yale-LeStatt Knight is very much still in this thing. He's down but not out, but Demented is staying right on him, takes him over with a snapmare and slaps on a reverse chinlock. Bryan-Demented trying to slow things down now and he now grabs the arm and twists it around his leg while keeping that chinlock cinched in. Yale-And this may do it for Knight. He's starting to show the wear and tear that Demented's put him through for the last few minutes. Bryan-Wait! Knight struggling back to his feet! He's managed to free his arm and now he's back up! He starts ramming that left arm into the ribs of Demented! Once, twice, let's make it three! Knight is free! Yale-He bounces himself off the ropes and charges Demented, but Demented catches him in an armdrag take over and Knight starts favoring that shoulder again! Bryan-Demented grabs him and takes him down in a tiger driver! And now the man formerly known as Chameleon heads upstairs! Knight isn't moving in the middle of the ring. Demented leaps! Corkscrew moonsault! Yale-No! Knight rolled out of the way at the last moment! Demented hit nothing but mat! And that could shift the advantage to the side of Knight! Bryan-Knight is struggling back to his feet, and he's up! Demented is over by the ropes and pulling himself up. Knight heads over and grabs Demented. Yale-Nails him with a left, and another! Sends him across the ring and he goes charging in! Huge splash in the corner! Amazing how quickly the tide of the match can turn around! Cha--Demented slumps down in the corner, and Knight is now standing on his neck! Bryan-He breaks before the five count, and now whips him into the ropes, and catches him dropping him with a hotshot! Yale-Demented's head snapped back when it hit those ropes, and now he's down in the middle of the ring! Demented is backed into the corner now and Knight unloads with a knife edge chop! (Knight goes on to unload with five more chops with the crowd screaming out Whoo! after each shot.) Bryan-Knight setting Demented up on the top rope! A couple of shots to the jaw, and now he heads up! Superplex! He floats over for the cover! One, two, thre--no! So close. Demented just managed to get his shoulder up! Yale-Knight can't quite believe it! But he stays on Demented! There's a right, and a left, and a right, and a left, a right, left, and Demented is still on his feet! Knight whips him into the ropes and nails a Samoan drop! Bryan-Demented manages to get to his feet yet again and Knight nails him with another right hand! Demented staggers around and here comes Knight from behind! Tries to roll him up! One, two, and no! Demented kicks out, but they were too close to the ropes! Knight just went tumbling out of the ring, right in front of us! Yale-And I think he may have hit his head there JB! And Demented is still down in the ring! Bryan-The referee is now issuing a count to Knight, but Demented comes out and tosses Knight back into the ring. He wants to win this thing in the ring. He slams Knight, and pulls him back up and whips him into the corner. LeStatt hit hard! Yale-And here comes Demented, he's charging in. And Knight with a boot to the face! Demented staggers backwards! Knight goes charging out and nails him with a clothesline! And now Knight going to the top rope! Bryan-We could see the Knight Tyme here Dave! And Knight jumps! He nailed it! Yale-But he's gone back to favoring that arm. I think he may have landed wrong on that right arm! Remember that Demented was beating the hell out of the arm at the beginning of the match! Hitting that Knight Tyme may have been a huge mistake by Knight! Bryan-Well, we've got Knight favoring his arm, and Demented looks to be in la-la land! The referee is starting to count, and unless one of these men gets up this thing is over! Yale-And look at Knight, he's crawling over to Demented with his one good arm. And he drapes his left arm over Demented! One, two, three--no! How in the hell Demented kick out of that? Bryan-I don't know, but by God he did! And both men are whipped. Neither man moving. And the referee starts issuing another count. Knight slowly gets back to his feet, and pulls Demented up with him! Yale- Knight lifts Demented up, but his arm gives out, and Demented lands on his feet behind Knight! Demented quickly grabs Knight’s head, and pulls him down into the Strike! Big move from Demented! Bryan- Demented crawls on top of Knight, and gets the count! ONE! TWO! THREE!! It’s over! (The bell rings, as Burn hits. The crowd begins to boo mightily as Demented warily rises to his feet, looking down at his fallen foe. His arm is raised, as he moves to exit the ring.) Andrews- Here is your winner… Demented! Bryan- Demented comes off with a big win here tonight, and you’d better believe that this Demented is ready for some major trouble! Ever since being kidnapped by the Lunatic Bryan Mayhem, something has snapped in this man’s mind, and quite frankly, I fear what may come of it! (As the crowd continues to boo as Demented returns to the backstage areas, the cameras head to the back. Barry Goldman stands with Twisted, and the crowd reacts quickly to his visage on the MegaTron. Twisted looks to be thinking deeply, almost staring down at the ‘Lost Soul’ shirt he wears.) Goldman- Twisted, it seems like a lot has been going through your head as of late. Now watching your former teammate in the Lost Soul Militia defeat LeStatt Knight, where is Twisted heading? Twisted- You know, the Lost Soul Militia is still alive in me. But ‘The Lunatic’ Bryan Mayhem did something to Demented. He’s different now. The Lost Soul Militia was meant to be a team dedicated to making waves for the underrated stars of GCW. But now Demented seems to have been corrupted by this Mayhem guy, and quite frankly it makes me sick. The Agents of Oblivion are really not the right people to be following. We weren’t about darkness. We were about the spotlight. But now Demented is a bit confused, and I wish to God that I could do something about it. (Twisted looks up with a start, and Goldman does a double take. Demented appears next to Twisted, breathing heavily from his match. Twisted looks on, confused, as Demented pulls Goldman’s microphone in.) Demented- You’re the confused one, Twizz. The Agents of Oblivion showed me what darkness really was. But they also showed me the light. I’ve seen that light, my friend, and it’s made me better. I’m something stronger now. Stronger than I was. Stronger than the Militia. Stronger… than you. (Demented as Twisted share a pair of intimidating glares, before Demented slowly walks away, his eyes not leaving Twisted until the last moment. Twisted, now angry, simply walks away in the opposite direction, leaving Goldman alone as the show fades to a commercial break.) (As Meltdown returns, the show heads to the parking garage once again. Steven Caldera now stands alone, having removed his overcoat to sling it over his shoulder. He stands patiently, casually glancing at the vehicles in the parking lot. Finally, a pair of headlights appears in the entrance, and a car pulls forward. As it approaches, Caldera sighs with relief as he spots Jason Dread’s limo moving towards him. The limo pulls to a halt, as Caldera moves towards the door with a smile.) Caldera- I thought you’d never get here. Hurry up, we have things to do. (Caldera swings open the door, but his smiles quickly fades to an amazed and confused glare. Suddenly, Caldera is sent flying back to the pavement, landing unconscious on the cold floor. The crowd cheers in confusion, before the one and only Jhub steps out of the limo. In his right hand he clutches a sledgehammer, which he has just used to lay out the President. The crowd cheers mightily as the Jhub smiles down at the President, before reaching back into the limo for his bag. He grabs his belongings and slams the door closed, heading inside.) Jhub- Take five, Steven. You look terrible. (The crowd continues to cheer as the cameras zoom in on Caldera, who still lies hurting on the pavement. Suddenly, a car screeches around the corner and pulls into the garage, before coming to an abrupt halt. The engine stops and both doors swing open. As the headlights click off, we see that this late arrival is in fact a yellow taxi cab. Two men exit from the back doors; Khan and Jason Dread. Dread drags his title belt as he and Khan rush to Caldera’s aid, as the President slowly regains his senses.) Dread- What happened? Caldera- That son of a bitch! Dread- What happened? Caldera- The Jhub. The Jhub was in your limo. Why was the Jhub in your limo? Dread- Uhh… He stole it at the airport. Khan and I had to catch a cab. That son of a bitch. Caldera- Khan… take care of that for me, will you? (With a sadistic grin that seems all to familiar on his face, Khan smiles. With that, Khan and Dread help Caldera to his feet, as their cabbie looks on. The show heads back to ringside, as the Establishment members regroup.) Yale- The Jhub is really going to get it now, JB! Bryan- That was a rather clever trick by the Jhub, but I agree, the Jhub is going to have hell to pay, as it seems obvious that Khan now has his sights set on the Jhub! Yale- Like Khan said, Caldera points, Khan destroys! And Caldera just pointed in the direction of the Jhub! Andrews: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the GCW Television Championship! (Evangelist’s music plays.) Andrews: Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 295 pounds…THE EVANGELIST!!! (Just as The Evangelist steps through ropes Born to Be Wild hits. The crowd boos as Jeff Wylde heads to the ring with Roadblock.) Andrews: And his opponent, weighing in at 265 pounds, from Sydney, Australia, being accompanied by Roadblock…JEFF WYLDEMAN WYLDDDDDEEE!!! (Wylde steps in the ring and Evangelist immediately attacks him.) Bryan: And The Evangelist is not wasting anytime. He pounds on Wylde and knocks him to his knees. Evangelist stomps on Wylde’s head. Evangelist sends Wylde off the rope and follows him with a clothesline. Yale: Evangelist is really on fire tonight! Bryan: Wylde and Evangelist are trading blows now. Wylde with a thumb to the eye and then a kick to the stomach…DDT! Wylde picks up Evangelist and delivers a release German Suplex. Wylde drops an elbow and then rolls over for a pin. ONE! TWO! THR- Bryan: Evangelist kicks out! Jeff Wylde has The Evangelist by the hair and he pulls him in…fallaway slam! Wylde is dominating The Evangelist so far here. Wylde with an Irish whip but The Evangelist reverses it! Wylde hits the ropes and there’s big boot from The Evangelist! The Evangelist picks up Wylde and throws him over the top rope! Wylde hits the floor hard. Yale: Hey! They shouldn’t be out here! Bryan: The Evangelist whips Wylde into the steel steps. Now he’s got Wylde by the head and charges towards the ring post but Wylde reverses it and sends The Evangelist face first into that solid, steel ring post! Yale: The Evangelist could be out cold! (Wylde walks over to the timekeeper’s table and grabs a steel chair.) Bryan: Look at this! Wylde’s gonna cheat…again! (Wylde picks up The Evangelist and rolls him into the ring. Wylde follows in behind him with the steel chair.) Bryan: Wylde is gonna bust The Evangelist’s skull wide open! (The Evangelist slowly gets to his feet and Wylde winds up. Wylde goes to hit The Evangelist but the ref gets in his way. Wylde and the ref begin to argue.) Yale: What does that ref think he’s doing? (Wylde winds up to hit the ref but from behind comes The Evangelist. He pulls the chair from Wylde’s hands and places it on the ground. The Evangelist hooks Wylde up and delivers a reverse DDT onto the chair.) Bryan: Reverse DDT onto the chair! Wylde is out cold! Cover! ONE! TWO! THREE! Bryan: NEW CHAMPION!!! (The ref hands The Evangelist his belt and raises his hand.) Andrews: Here is your winner and new GCW Television Champion…THE EVANGELIST!!! (The Evangelist climbs out of the ring and heads to the back. Camera cuts to commercial break.) (Meltdown returns, and heads backstage. Octavian and Toxic sit in a locker room together, each in their own folding chair. Octavian appears to be in deep thought, as the crowd reacts with an undecided roar of volume.) Octavian- I don’t know. I was with the Establishment for so long, but Steven did not come to me with this. I know nobody will believe me, but I really don’t have anything to do with it. Toxic- Come on, man, you can tell me. There really isn’t anything wrong with being in the Establishment as far as I can tell. Octavian- Are you joking? Caldera is a dangerous man to have as an enemy. Quite frankly, I never saw this coming, and the fact that he did not consult me, the founding father of the Establishment, is an insult! Toxic- Seriously, man, give it a rest! The Establishment wouldn’t be such a bad place to go. You should go talk to Steven. Octavian- Why? What, have they gotten to you, too? Honestly, I don’t know what is going on in this place any more.
