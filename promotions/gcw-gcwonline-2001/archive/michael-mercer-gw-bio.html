Michael Mercer Biography Real Name Michael Mercer Nicknames The Man-Ox, Mikey Mayhem, Mister Merciless, The Bronx Bomber Hometown The Bronx, New York Birthday April 20, 1980 Height 6'4 Weight 226 Other Feds PDW, XWL, HCW, GWE, SAWF, Global! Other Fed Titles HCW Intercontinental Champion, GWE US Champion, SAWF National Champion, SAWF TV Champion, SAWF Tag Team Champion, SAWF World Champion Other Fed Awards SAWF Hall of Famer Strengths 10 years wrestling experience; well rounded; never submitted in his career; has enough charisma to supply a third world country. Weaknesses Liable to let emotions overwhelm his judgment; occasional nagging back injuries; sometimes fights stubborn thus throwing technique to the wind. Wrestling Style Fuck Your Shit Up Fu Strategy Typically Michael fights in a kickboxing stance and his strikes are his usual opening moves. When he grapples, he'll use the quickest and most efficient movements to keep his opponent off guard. When he's fighting smart, he's never moving carelessly; every step he takes is calculated and made to expend the least amount of energy as possible while inflicting the most damage in whatever situation he finds himself in. If he finds himself frustrated or feeling like he's been losing a good portion of the match, he'll start to let loose. He especially loves to badmouth his opponents while circling them, taunting them by raising his hands up and out and exposing his chin. Finisher Move Last Breath Finisher Description Anaconda Choke Finisher Setup Move Nothing. Special Moves Absolutely Merciless / Dreamcrusher (Emerald Fusion / Sambo Suplex) Other Moves (Will go further in depth later) Lots of suplexes and submissions. Likes to use trips and sweeps to take the match to the ground and if he's not doing that he's keeping distance with hard punches and kicks. Entrance Music 'The Second Coming' by Juelz Santana Ring Entrances I mean even though we stand alone, we stand together. The familiar sound of Berlioz's 5th Movement Symphonie Fantastique ominously chimes throughout the arena. The lights dim until a lone spotlight shines over the top of the entrance ramp. Once the music kicks in fully, however, Juelz Santana's 'The Second Coming' cues Michael Mercer's appearance in the spotlight. A cocky grin creeps onto his face as he looks out to the crowd as they give him a heavily positive welcome. They say family that prays together stays together And a family that walks apart just falls apart So together we stand, divided we fall United we form Voltron, and take on all (Let's move) Yes, the bird's left the nest I'm all grown up, I gotta fly with the rest Best of the best is what we strive to be A legacy is what we tryin' to leave Calmly Michael saunters down toward the ring, the spotlight on him the whole way. Clad in trademark gold and black trimmed shorts and boots, his gloved hands grasp at the bottom of his black T-shirt to pull it up and over his head before flinging it a few rows into the crowd. Mikey Mayhem stops at ringside for a few seconds before abruptly running to a nearby security barricade and jumping atop of it, standing tall as he plays to the crowd. Let's say goodbye to the past The future's here, at last The second coming, the new beginning The truth is speaking, you should listen So glorious, victorious, we take what we want, we born warriors So glorious, victorious, we take what we want, we born warriors The Man-Ox drops down from the barricade and promptly runs to the ring, sliding in underneath the bottom rope. Quickly Michael heads to a nearby corner and climbs the turnbuckles to get the crowd cheering some more, as the rest of the lights in the arena start to come back on. He crosses his arms in front of his face, representing the Irish and Italian flags tattooed respectively just above the back of his left and right wrists. He hops down from the turnbuckle and turns toward the center of the ring, lounging back against the corner idly in wait for the match to begin.
