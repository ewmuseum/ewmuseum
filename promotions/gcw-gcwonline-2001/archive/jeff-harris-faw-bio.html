Other Moves Finishers - XTF (This move hasn't been used since early XPCW days when Jeff won the hardcore title, what it is, is an STF submission hold but Jeff locks in both legs, very tricky to do. The move now however is used more frequently in DWA.) - Chain Reaction (Jeff first debuted this finisher in WFW, what he does is he takes the opponent down via arm bar, then puts the arm between his legs and pulls back on the opponents neck.) Signature Moves - Hardkore Bomb (This move was once called the 'Jazzy Bomb' What he does is he picks the opponent up for a normal Powerbomb then runs and releases the opponent.) - Fate Of Doom (This move used to be his old finisher called the 'Fucking Face jam' he kicks the opponent in the stomach then runs against the ropes, then hits him with a face jam into the match using his legs.) - Shadow Kick (This move has been used by Jeff for quite a while, now. The Shadow Kick is a run against the ropes then a butterfly super kick to the neck of the opponent.) - Diving Powerbomb (This move has been tested out in some house shows by Jeff Harris but has only used it once in GCW, what he does is he gets the opponent on the top rope, then goes to the top rope himself, he picks them up in a Powerbomb position then jumps off the turnbuckle giving the opponent a top rope Powerbomb and a low blow at the same time.)
