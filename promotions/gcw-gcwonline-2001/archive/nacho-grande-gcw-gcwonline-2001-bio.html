Nacho Grande Biography Biography Straight from your Taco Bell drive-thru window comes Nacho Grande and his friends Beef Supreme and Quesa Dilla. Providing they get the days off for which they requested to get to the shows, they're always ready with a boombox and a New Kids cassette to break it down backstage and have a little fun with the fans. Nacho loves superheroes and roleplaying. Despite his unique guise of being an idiot, he's a very talented wrestler, even though his friends aren't. He doesn't care about money or fame, just wants to have fun. He still keeps his job, cause well, when he signed the contract, he forgot to include his payrate, so it's minimum wage by default.
