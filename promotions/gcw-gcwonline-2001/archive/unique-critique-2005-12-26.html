Unique Critique

26 December 2005

What this is The star-rating column of the roster, graded from none to five on roleplaying ability and activity.
                   
Unique Critique

Each character is rated from no (if anyone deserved no stars, they'd be fired) to five stars (excellent) in terms of roleplaying ability and activity. If your ego is too frail to handle criticism, read no further!

John Reed- So close to the top, he just needs to take that last step. More airtime will help him to gain a more solid character that should allow him to get to the top. ****

Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins- Since his return, Rollins has been impressive as usual. His creative, insightful roleplays and solid reliability have helped him gain his second title reign in GCW. Rollins will be at the top for a while at this rate. ****1/2

The Jhub- The Jhub is another impressive figure. He's got some great character depth and a good, if bizarre, sense of humor. Once he gains a bit more insight into the psychology of the game, he'll be one to beat. ****

Toad- His recent title win is a testament to his versatility. However, a drop of activity has cost him a bit. I sense it's only temporary. . . Toad is a big player. ***1/2

Octavian- Octavian is one of the best. He's come such a long way since I first met him. His role as Commissioner has also helped give him his deserved spot at the top. Octavian will hold gold again, and soon. ****1/4

Toxic- He's doing rather well now for being on the road. Gotta respect this guy given his current situation. ***3/4

Mike Hardy- Very loyal and patient with GCW. His determination had made him one of the best. He also has improved quite a bit over the months, and now is one of the most underrated stars. It will be fun to see how far Hardy can run with his new push. ***1/2

'The Fueler' Luke Hanson- Hanson has proved himself, and is now a solid main-eventer. There is no quesiton of his determination or ability. Hanson is almost to the top. ****1/2

Zippy- He's the TV champ, and he's still got it. A little inactivity hurts him, but he seems to be getting back into the swing of things. His great sense of humor is his biggest asset. ***3/4

Capo- He's a bit inactive, but still fun to watch. I think he's one of the most underrated guys on the roster. ***3/4

Chris Jordan- Same guy as Toxic, done very well for being on the road. Jordan has had a better angle going for him than Toxic, so he's been a bit more active. I'm giving him some slack for his current travels. ***1/2

Shane Lane- I'm disappointed by his inactivity. I won't rate him, only because I know he's good, but hasn't been around.

Dan Rivero- Rivero has done his time, now he's ready to move up. Once he solidifies his character on camera, he'll be up to a whole new level. If it weren't for his inactivity, I'd give him 4 stars. ***1/2

LeStatt Knight- He's really come on strong. His character is a good one, and he's showing great promise. ***3/4

Trash- Trash is still looking good. He's got a good character, and a good personality. He's got himself at a good level, but I think he has the potential for more. ***3/4

Sick Freaks- The Sick Freaks are good. Not quite to the main event level yet, but they're a solid tag team. ***1/2

Impact- Back for another run in GCW. With some more exposure, he could go far. ***1/2

Tristain Mayhem- I am very impressed by this guy. He is the greatest prospect in GCW right now. Given some time to pay his dues, so to speak, he'll get himself into the main event picture with ease. ****1/2

Big Skails- Despite his lack of activity recently, he's still got a decent character. Most of the time I hate the gangster gimmick, but he pulls it off rather well. ***1/4

Kurt Richards- He's impressed me with the effort he's willing to put forth in his work. His impressive work against Octavian has proved his ability and insight. Watch out for this newcomer. ****1/4

Eric Allen- My only complaint about Allen is his lack of use of the shift key. Other than that, he's showing great promise. ***3/4

Icebabe- She's really taken some time to learn how GCW works, and went from joke to dangerous rather quickly. Icebabe may still be a bit hot-tempered, but she's improved greatly. ***1/2

Katanya- Always been good. She's been one of the most impressive women I've seen. She's got a future in the soon to come women's division. ***3/4

Gidget/Emma- I'd like to see some more activity, but I can't blame them for the lack of angles for them. Once the women's division begins, they'll be good. ***

Unique Critique will be updated as needed for each person. Check back often!
