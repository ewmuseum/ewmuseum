Meltdown 6 Alltel Arena — North Little Rock, Arkansas ---- Meltdown #6 (The camera opens into the dimmed arena, and the GCW logo flashes. Suddenly, pyro lights up the stage and the music hits, sending the fans into a frenzy. The camera pans over the crowd, finally resting on the announce table.) Bryan- We are just three days removed from the biggest GCW event ever! Warpath was by far the most amazing night any of us here at GCW have experienced, and we’re glad you are joining us here tonight for Meltdown! Yale- We have a new World Champion, and he is Octavian! We have a new United States Champion, and he is Thirteen! And we have a new cruiserweight champion, and he is Chameleon! What a night that was, JB! Bryan- Three new champions were crowned that night, but in addition, we saw the return of both Rich “The Renegade” Rollins and Khan! Yale- What the hell was Khan thinking? He assaulted the Jhub, Octavian, and even President Caldera! Hopefully, we’ll get some answers from Khan here tonight as well! Bryan- We’ve got a great lineup here tonight, thanks for being here, everybody! (Physical hits, and the crowd’s murmur turns to heavy boos. President Caldera walks to the ring with a microphone.) Andrews- Please welcome the President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! Caldera- On Sunday night. . . (Caldera is interrupted by an “asshole!” chant.) Caldera- I am fully aware that I am an asshole, and I enjoy the acknowledgment. So why don’t you just be quiet and let me talk. (The crowd boos, but quiets.) Caldera- On Sunday night, the Establishment went home with the gold, as I promised it would. Not everything might have gone our way, but I promise you, those who oppose me will be put in their place soon enough. For now, I would like to take the pleasure of introducing YOUR World Heavyweight Champion. . . The Italian Stallion. . . Octavian!! (The crowd boos as Might of Rome hits. Octavian struts to the ring with his belt around his waist, and enters the ring.) Octavian- Perhaps from now on, you American idiots will listen to me when I make a promise! I told you I would walk out of that sh*t-hole New York as the champion, and that’s exactly what I did. Get used to seeing me with this belt, America. I’ll have it for a long, long time. Caldera- This marks the beginning of the Establishment’s domination of GCW. From now on, we rule all, and all will obey me. This is our own imperium, or Empire for those of you that don’t speak Italian. Now is our time. We will not be opposed. (Suddenly, the lights in the arena drop, leaving the entire area in darkness.) Bryan- The lights here have gone out, now, and the President has been interrupted! Could we get someone to put the lights back on? (Debonair hits, and the fans erupt in both cheers and boos. An eerie green light fills the ring, and Caldera and Octavian search the area for Khan. Finally, four figures drop from the ceiling and land on the stage. They are small, lifeless dummies. One is shaped like Caldera, one like Octavian, one like Reed, and one like Toxic. The fans go ballistic as Khan finally emerges from the back, wearing a long, black robe.) Yale- What the hell is this?! Khan is here, and there are effigies of the Establishment on stage! What does he think he’s doing? Bryan- Khan is on the stage with those four dummies, and he’s pulled something out of his pocket! (The crowd goes nuts as Khan flicks a lighter, and touches it to Toxic’s effigy. The dummy erupts in flames, and the crowd’s cheers intensify. Khan raises a microphone to his face.) Khan- One by one, the Establishment will fall. (Next, he touches the flame to John Reed’s doll, which burns as well.) Khan- Until finally. . . (Khan ignites Octavian’s effigy, and the fans go crazy) Khan- Only one is left for me to eliminate. (Khan finally stands in front of the Caldera effigy, and he lights it on fire. The four flaming likenesses light up the stage, and Khan stands amid them.) Khan- This is the future. Be prepared. Your Empire will fall, Caldera. I will not be denied. (Debonair continues playing as Khan heads for the back. Caldera and Octavian talk nervously in the ring as stage hands run out with fire extinguishers and put the effigies out.) Bryan- What a demonstration by Khan! The President is cowering in the ring with his champion! Yale- Oh, please, it’s just simple mind games from Khan, and he can’t touch the boss, JB! Bryan- Well, folks, tonight we’ve got three titles on the line, including Thirteen’s title defense against a mystery opponent! Right now, let’s go backstage to Kevin Jacobs!
