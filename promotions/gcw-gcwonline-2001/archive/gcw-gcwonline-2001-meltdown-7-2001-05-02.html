Meltdown 7 1st Mariner Arena — Baltimore, Maryland ---- Meltdown #7 (The show fades in to a packed house. The lights go down as the GCW logo flashes on the screen. Suddenly, loud fireworks light up the arena, and Meltdown is kicked off. The crowd goes nuts as the camera fans over the arena, resting on the announce table.) Bryan- Welcome, everyone, to another edition of Meltdown! We are coming to you live in front of a sold out crowd for a huge event on Meltdown! Hello, I’m James Bryan alongside David Yale, and folks, we’re ready to go! Yale- This is great! President Caldera has promised us announcement concerning both the United States title, and the next Pay-Per-View event, Shockwave! Bryan- Well, for now, let’s head to ringside for our big debut battle royal! (All About the Flame hits, and the Flame heads out to the ring.) Andrews- The following contest is a battle royal! Men are eliminated when they are thrown over the top rope, and both feet hit the floor. Introducing first, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing in at 279 pounds. . . The Flame! (The Flame enters the ring as Countryboy Can Survive hits. Mike Byrd dances to the ring, slapping hands with the fans on his way.) Andrews- From Durham, North Carolina, weighing in at 230 pounds. . . ‘The Legend’ Mike Byrd! (Break Stuff hits, and the crowd boos. Red Hot heads for the ring.) Andrews- From Long Island, New York, weighing in at 230 pounds. . . Red Hot! (The Game hits, and the crowd boos again. Mikado emerges, and slowly stalks the ring.) Andrews- From parts unknown, weighing in at 232 pounds. . . Mikado! (Freak on a Leash hits, and Shane Lane emerges and heads to the ring.) Andrews- From Beverly Hills, California, weighing in at 210 pounds. . . Shane Lane! (Fearless plays, and Freak heads for the ring.) Andrews- From Dallas, Texas, weighing in at 300 pounds. . . Freak! (Finally, Demons Surround Me hits, and the crowd boos. Capo emerges, and heads for the ring.) Andrews- And from Cleveland, Ohio, weighing in at 257 pounds. . . Capo! Bryan- Well, all seven competitors are in the ring. Remember, you are only eliminated if you are thrown over the top and both feet touch the floor! Yale- There’s the bell, and this thing is underway! Capo and Shane Lane have begun to fight, as have Freak and The Legend! The Flame and Red Hot are going at it, and Mikado is observing from a neutral corner! Bryan- Lane has control of Capo, and is trying to toss him out! But Mikado charges over and hits Lane low with a leg sweep, and both men fall back into the ring! Yale- The Flame has control of Red Hot, and he’s got him scooped up! He’s trying to get him over the top, but Red Hot has the top rope hooked, and he’s not going anywhere! The Flame gives up, and slams Red Hot to the mat. Bryan- Freak and the Legend are still throwing punches, and Freak seems to be taking the upper hand! Wait, Mikado comes up from behind Freak, and dropkicks him in the spine! Freak tumbles over the top rope, but lands safely on the apron! Yale- The Legend now goes to work on Mikado! The Legend continues punching away, as Shane Lane sends Capo into the ropes! Capo bounces back, and Lane sends him high overhead, and over the top rope with a back body drop! But Capo manages to land on the apron! Bryan- Red Hot has taken over on the Flame with a series of right hands, and he has the Flame against the top rope! He back up and charges! But the Flame ducks, and tosses Red Hot over the top to the floor! Red Hot is gone! Yale- One down, six left in the ring! Mike Byrd is pummeling Mikado, until Mikado blocks a shot, and hits Byrd right in the chest with the Final Touch! Byrd goes down to the ancient Tae Kwon Doe move! Bryan- Wow! Freak is back in behind Mikado, and he takes him down with a clothesline form behind! Meanwhile, Shane Lane is being pummeled by Capo! Wait, the Flame comes in behind Capo, lifts him up, and dumps him over the top rope! Yale- Capo lands on the floor, and we’re down to five men left in the ring! One will win it all! The Flame and Lane are going at it, with Lane pummeling Flame with knife-edge chops in the corner! Bryan- Mikado is getting stomped by Freak, as Mike Byrd tries to recover! He gets to his feet, as the Flame takes Lane down with a clothesline! Byrd sees and opening, and heads to the second rope! Yale- He gets up, and takes off! There’s the Honky-Tonk Smash, an elbow from the second rope! Lane is hurting, as the Flame lifts him up, and tries to toss him out! Bryan- Lane hooks the top rope, but now Byrd is there to help! With the added pressure, Lane lets go, and tumbles to the floor! Four men left! Yale- But wait, Lane is furious! He’s back into the ring, where the Flame and Mike Byrd are fighting! And Mikado and Freak are going at it, as well! Lane moves in behind Mikado, and hooks him in an abdominal stretch! Bryan- Lane lifts him up, spins, and drops him on his head! WOW! Superstar Slam! Lane has made his point, as he heads for the back! Yale- Lane ha destroyed Mikado, and Freak is trying to lift him up! But Mikado, with what he has left, has the bottom rope hooked with his feet! He’s not going anywhere! Bryan- Now Byrd is in control of the Flame, and he charges in! He clotheslines Flame against the ropes, and they both tumble over! But the Flame lands on the apron as Byrd tumbles to the ground! Three men left! Yale- The Flame, Freak, and Mikado! The Flame pulls himself back into the ring, and goes after Freak! Freak and the Flame begins battling it out, as Mikado slides underneath them and heads for the turnbuckle! Bryan- Mikado is still suffering the effects of the Superstar Slam, but he’s climbing the turnbuckle! Freak and the Flame are trying to get each other over the top, and they’re all tied up! Yale- Wait, Mikado takes off! There’s a missile dropkick to Freak’s back! Freak falls, and brings the Flame with him! They both tumble to the floor! IT’S OVER! (The crowd boos as the Game plays. By winning this battle royal, he has paved the way to bigger things in GCW! Yale- All seven of these guys gave it their all, though, JB! Don’t count any of them out in GCW! Chris Jordan emerges, and he is pulling Emma along with him. He struggles to break free, but he has a death grip on her hair.) Bryan- What in the hell? Jordan already has Emma! Fans, we really cant condone this match. . . Yale- It was Emma’s idea, anyway! Jordan tosses Emma into the ring, and he goes in after her! She gets up, and Jordan grabs her by the hair again! He shoves her to the mat, and someone tosses him a mike! Jordan- All right, you little ~no audio~, I’ve had enough of you. I should be out there winning titles, but instead I’m here in the ring with you, a pathetic woman! (The crowd boos.) Jordan- So what I’m going to do is this. . . I’m going to turn my back, and give you five seconds to get out of my ring. This is your last chance! (Jordan turns his back, as Emma slowly gets to her feet.) Danger- Everything Caldera obtained, I owned twenty percent. (The crowd begins to cheer as Caldera shakes his head in disbelief.) Danger- Now, I lost most everything when ACWF fell. But Caldera obtained GCW. And that means I own twenty percent of it! (The crowd goes ballistic as Caldera throws a tantrum in the ring. Octavian simply watches in disbelief.)
