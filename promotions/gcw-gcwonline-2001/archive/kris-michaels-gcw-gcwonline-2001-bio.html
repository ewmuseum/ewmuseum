Kris Michaels Biography Real Name Tristan Kristopher Michaels Nicknames Kris "The Executioner" Michaels, "The Executioner", "The submission artist" Hometown Toms River, NJ Birthday July 11, 1985 Height 5'8 Weight 190 Biography Real name, Tristan "Kris" Michaels, grew up in several different places throughout New Jersey before settling with adoptive parents in Toms River, New Jersey at the age of ten. His legal guardian went over to his military father when him and his wife separated only a year after Tristan was adopted into the family. His father was rough on him, but good to him. He taught Tristan how to fight and got him into the local "Y" for formal training in wrestling. Tristan exploded in his class and his coach thought he should enter into the competitive leagues around the Toms River area which was heavily known for wrestling at the time. After winning every division possible up to the age 15, even the nation level, Tristan's father wanted to see him do more, so he pushed him into Mixed Martial Arts. His innate wrestling ability helped him excel in many gyms, but no coach had the time for competition style training, so his father shipped him up to Newark, NJ to "Diamond MMA" where he was trained one-on-one with Master Gomez in Brazilian-Jiu-jitsu and eventually in Muay Tai a year later. At the age of 17, September 10th, 2002 in his senior year, Tristan made his debut into competition MMA at a local competition in Atlantic City, New Jersey where he went up against an undefeated BJJ and Kickboxing artist. Tristan entered in his middle name, Kris, to keep his wrestling conquests out of the ring.... ...He won via armbar in the first round... After graduating high school, Tristan, now known in the MMA world as Kris Michaels attended Ocean County College so he can maintain his strict diet/training schedules. Not being concerned with belts and the politics at his gym, Kris left Diamond MMA and started to train with several different Masters from BJJ, Muay Tai, and wrestling at three different gyms. When his wrestling coach was hurt from a bad toss from Kris, he was shipped up to have once a week sessions with a Professional Wrestler up in Newark, but after about a month, the Wrestler left the gym and pursued personal issues. While at OCC, Kris was picked up by the WEC (World Extreme Cagefighting) and ran with the name Kris Michaels in the middleweight division. Trying very hard to maintain school at the same time, Kris graduated from OCC, but never went further in his education. He took up several different contracts between 2003-2007 with WEC, making enough money to train to fight and diet. 2007, His father passed away, leaving Kris with a house and a car. The military paid for everything, so he was left with a good amount of cash. Dana White called up Kris asking if he wanted a shot on UFC, and Kris accepted. Kris beat Jon Fitch at UFC 75 in the second round and got Submission of the Night with a nasty triangle choke. Kris decided to stay out of the UFC, even though he was offered a contract from Dana White personally. With everything going right, he didn't want to chance it, not to mention he didn't like the spotlight in fighting, he just wanted to fight, get his money, and leave. A couple fill in fights over the next two years with the UFC and an impressive record professionally with 22-2-0, Kris decided he wanted to go into the entertainment aspect of the fighting lifestyle and went into talks with his old wrestling coach, Chris Bagwell. Kris bulked up from a very lean 170 to a still very lean, but solid 190. Chris Bagwell got Kris into GCW. Kris knows little in the realm of wrestling, but he knows fighting, and he knows entertainment. Will this babyface nice guy really make an impact on GCW? Strengths Well versed in submissions, stand up strikes, and counters. Greatly conditioned and well experienced. Weaknesses Although a phenomenal MMA fighter, he doesn't know the ins and outs of Professional Wrestling. Will try to put on a show, and sometimes, takes too big of a risk... Wrestling Style Submission Artist, Mat Technician, Risk Taker Finisher Move The Executioner Finisher Description Usually when opponent is charging, Springboard or jump off turnbuckle at opponent and put into a Guillotine choke and falls to the floor. Out of shock alone, opponent usually taps immediately. Entrance Music "Papercut"-Linkin Park
