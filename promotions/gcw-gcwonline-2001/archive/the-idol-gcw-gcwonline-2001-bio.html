The Idol Biography Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday 25/03/1986 Height 6 ft Weight 230lbs Biography McMirra was born in Los Angeles, CA. His Mother was a stay at home Mom and his Father spent 30 years in the US Army. McMirra was brought up well and was an only child who wanted for nothing. He was the 'Popular' kid within his High School but never a bully (Think Freddy Prince Jr in 'She's All That'). He left School and attained a Business Managagement Degree at the University of California before starting up his own Media business which covered celebrity news. He ran the business for 4 years before selling up for an undisclosed amount of money. A young, good looking, entrepeneour who made his millions before getting into the wrestling business. Originally named 'Pure Excellence' Andy McMirra who was a fan favourite, entertaining fans along with his former manager Lee 'Taxi' Weighman with humour and passion for the business. Taxi was given the boot by McMirra who started to believe his own hype, he was already cocky towards his opponents but began getting cocky and arrogant towards every day people including being booed at when making an appearance on a TV Chat Show. Now under the name of Andy 'The Idol' McMirra he would steal disabled parking spaces from people in wheelchairs because he thinks he can, if you have been to Tenerife then he has been to Twentyerife. The Comedy and humour is still there within his character and comes out regularly but he does not do things for the fans anymore, he is literally a one dimensional, tunnel visioned man who believes he is quite literally the idol of all idols.
