WorldWide 79 Atlanta, Georgia ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Atlanta fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: We're just two short weeks away from NC-17, and another capacity crowd is on hand for what should be a tremendous event! Now within shouting distance of the biggest event of the year, GCW invades Atlanta on a night that promises to deliver on the biggest secret in the company! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan alongside David Yale, and Dave, there are two huge stories we're following here tonight, the biggest of which being the situation with Vivica J. Valentine and Peter Maxwell! Yale: Thanks, JB, and you're absolutely right! After weeks of conveniently being in position to know things the rest of us don't, Peter Maxwell has pledged to reveal it all to us tonight! He's been watching and waiting for weeks now, he knows what the rest of us don't, but he's going to share... and out Valentine once and for all! Bryan: And, of course, the Tag Team Titles are going to play a huge role in this event! Stone and Storm are just one day shy of breaking the hallowed record of longest title reign in GCW history, and if they leave this event with the belts, they'll break it! Yale: But you have to know President Caldera will have something up his sleeve, JB! He's been obsessed with getting the titles away from Banned and Exiled~! before they etch their names in the record books, and Branson King and Octavian are gunning for them! I just can't imagine a scenario where Caldera allows them to leave here with the belts! Bryan: There's a lot more on tap for tonight as well. Shaman defends his United States title against Ray Holland, who has another opportunity to advance up the ranks. Yale: Holland looks good, but I'm told Shaman is on the warpath, JB. This man has suffered enough of these wannabes nipping at his heels, and he's going to put Holland down for good! Bryan: And then there's the huge bout between Jay Terror and Tessa Windsor! We were treated to a massive WorldWide matchup last week between Jordan White and LeStatt Knight, and this week it's Terror and the woman who turned her back on the tag champs, Tessa. Yale: Tessa has finally struck out on her own, JB. She's immensely talented, and tonight could be the night she rises up and announces to the world that she's a force to be reckoned with! Terror won't know what hit him. Bryan: All in all we're in for a tremendous show as we're only days away from NC-17! Let's get this thing started! ---- Geaux Tigers! Chris and Markus turn from Caldera and head back up the ramp to the backstage area, leaving a very annoyed owner still in the ring. Bryan: My what a show we have tonight! Yale: Banned & Exiled~! should have never messed with Caldera. Now it will all come back to haunt them. VO!
