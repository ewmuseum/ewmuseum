The Jhub’s Insider, Vol. 1

1 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A guest column by one of the roster’s comedy characters, speaking his mind in his own voice.
 

  The Poopsider Vol.1       Hey guys, it's me, The Jhub, writing this week's edition of the Insider. I wrote this week's because A.) I wanted to. B.) No one else did. C.) I have a place to speak my mind  and D.) I'm dead sexy. Nah, I just actually have something to say this week so I just wanted to speak my mind.       You see, what most people don't understand is that [detail withheld] But on another subject, Ben comes up with pretty much everything and every feud that's going on right now, with the exception of those who started other feuds. Yours truly does try to put his word in as well. Granted, even though sometimes I don't like what Ben wants to put up, I support him because 1.) He knows what he's doing. and 2.) He's my best friend. Did I like losing my title on my first defense? No. Of course not. But the thing is, I know it's also for the good of GCW. I've been side-by-side with Ben since the ACWF and, not to sound conceded, I know that somewhere down the line, The Jhub will reign again. When will that happen? I dunno. And honestly, I don't care. I know I'll be doing this for a while so I don't care to question. Anyway, I congratulate Shane Lane on winning the belt. I look at him as the Kurt Angle of the GCW. Where as he's gone up the ladder of success very quickly, he still managed to keep heat. That, to me, is impressive. Now, but in logic, if you think about it, would The Jhub vs. Shane Lane at Annihilation really be appealing to you? I didn't think so, because it doesn't to me. Would The Jhub vs. Rich Rollins? Yes. Would The Jhub vs. Khan? Oh hell yes. Will any of those happen soon? Probably not, but hey, you get the gist of what I'm saying.       I do have to say though, I want to give thanks to all those who have supported The Jhub and ranked me as a 4 or above on the Unique Critiques. That seriously does mean a lot to me. One of my favorite guys though, with out a shadow of a doubt is Mike Hardy. I love the guy. I actually look forward to his roleplays, as well as Toad. Toad has always kept me laughing and I don't know how. Chameleon is good too, I personally think he'd do good in the hard*core division. Hmmm.......also, I'd have to say one of my favorites is Khan. Khan and I have had possibly the best feuds ever. The ACWF match was probably the highlight of it.....even though he beat the living shit out of me, but hey, it was fun to read.        If you haven't figured it out by now, I'm pretty much just babbling on. Well anyway, I'll be writing another one of these soon, but when is the question. All I can say is that wish me luck in the GCW, as well as in Poop Wars. :) 'Till next time, adios!     ~The Ayatollah of Poop aka General Jhub~
