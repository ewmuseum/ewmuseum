Jimmy Swindle Biography Real Name James J. Swinn Nicknames "Cheap Shot" Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday May 23rd, 1985 Height 5'10 Weight 225lbs Biography Born in the small desert town of Yucca Valley, California to his parents, Mr. John James Swinn and Cheryl Asonie Swinn. John James Swinn passed away due to a drug overdose when James J. Swinn (Jimmy Swindle) was four years old. Circumstances surrounding his father's death leaves James Swinn conflicted and feelings of guilt consume him, even today. "Cheap Shot" Jimmy Swindle moved to Los Angeles after a lackluster High School career to pursue a dream in acting. Swindle loves being in the spotlight, the center of attention, and hates when the focus is on somebody else. Jimmy Swindle doesn't exactly symbolize your ideal "white knight." He has the tendency to say awfully rude things, which can drive people away from him. He's everything that's unlikeable in a person. He often insults God himself, and also often calls himself God. Aside from being very egocentric and verbally viscous, Jimmy Swindle is also yet very insecure. A horribly jealous husband, doesn't take criticism well, and an element of narcissism plagues him. He's a married, but refuses children, which is a theme that's often argued about amongst him and his wife. Weaknesses: Doesn't quite understand the concept of "hard work" thus always finds an easy way out of a situation Has trouble with impulse control, mostly when it comes to other women and his wife. Anger issues, coupled with alcohol is a horrible - sometimes abusive mix. His anger problems also corrupt his in-ring abilities at times, can cause his judgement to be off and cost him the match. Lack of morals, sometimes a cruel and viscious person which can cause some people to sway away from him. Against veterans, this may haunt him. But Swindle is a young man, and in the ring it shows. He may back talk the crowd, the ref, he's a hothead young kid who thinks he's just the next big thing and there ain't nothin' that can stop him. Sometimes, he bites off more than he can chew. And sometimes, he chokes.. Insecurity issues, which causes him to be an extreme jealous husband and if anyone crtiques him, he doesn't take it very well. Strengths: Won't back down from a fight, however, even if he finds an easy way to win it. Resourceful in the ring, it takes some brains to find slick shortcuts and outsmart your opponent to use them Values strong friendship, if nothing else. Will honor a friend as a man, so long as the friend does the same. Believes in the bonds of brotherhood. Resiliency. Swindle can take a large amount of pain, and still fight back. So much that it's almost desirable to those who watch it. He's too insecure to lose. Other Feds HPW,RHW,HWA,FCW,WARPED Other Fed Titles HPW World Champion x3, HWA World Champion x1, HWA International Champion x2, HWA X-Treme Champion x2, CWC North American Champion. Other Fed Awards HWA Triple Crown Champion Strengths See Bio Weaknesses See Bio Wrestling Style Pure heel. Opportunistic, if he can find a way to cheat - HE WILL DO IT. So that would make him a tactful wrestler, think Ric Flair Strategy Jimmy Swindle's strategy is this: To win. By any means necessary. Finisher Move Smoke and Mirrors Finisher Description A DDT, which Jimmy then keeps the opponents head tucked after the DDT, and folds his legs around the opponent, choking him out into submission or KO WW Entrance "You work hard.. you make money.." The curtains split open as Moses did the sea, and out emerges the perfect contradiction to the analogy. "Cheap Shot" Jimmy Swindle soaks in the lone spotlight on himself, on top of the ramp-way. "Cheap Shot" Jimmy Swindle, wearing his trademark wrestling gear, makes his way down the ramp with half a smile on the radius of his lips. The lone spotlight glistens off his baby-oiled chest as he jumps onto the apron, and parades his body before stepping into the ring. Jimmy Swindle stands in the middle of the ring, arms up as if he's praying to the heavens - the spotlight on him as he awaits his opponent.
