WorldWide 137 Dallas Convention Center Arena — Dallas, Texas ---- WW137: Reclaiming the Crown, Defending the Jewels The door to the Commissioner's office is wide open, inviting any to walk on in. Panning back, the newly returned Johnny Borealis seems to be interested in doing just that. Clad in a snazzy button up shirt, khaki pants, Oakley sunglasses, and million dollar smile, the party crasher at High Stakes walks with a spring in his step right into the open door. Borealis: Johnny! It's your new best friend...Johnny. I am never gonna get sick of...hello? Having walked in blindly, Johnny Borealis hadn't noticed the office is empty. But not quite empty. A large, very large pile of neatly stacked money is on the Commissioner's desk. Borealis stares for a moment, then does a 360 degree turn. Nobody. Borealis: Nobody. Johnny B stares at the money for a moment as a smile slowly creeps up his face. A hand reaches out and looks to touch it, then pauses. His other hand reaches up and takes off his Oakley's and he chews on the pointy end for a second before he looks behind himself one more time and reaches for the money. Borealis: The man guards his cash as well as he guards the rest of his assets. Borealis is still looking back to the doorway as the top couple of stacks of cash are removed, revealing a bit of black hair behind them. Johnny B continues to check behind him as he removes a few more stacks, and Garbage Bag Johnny's glaring face is revealed behind the mountain of cash. Johnny B's pockets are almost full, and he turns toward the desk to see if he can snag any more dollars. His eyes grow to the size of dinner plates as he is face to face with the Commissioner. The more bearded of the Johnnies starts clapping very slowly. GBJ: Mister Borealis! I almost didn't hear you come in here without a punk band and a camel procession and Patrick Stewart to announce your arrival. Here, take a seat after you put that money back. His hand still outstretched, Johnny Borealis freezes. He doesn't move a muscle and neither does the Commissioner. GBJ: You know, I'm not a T-Rex. I can still see if you if don't move. Borealis begins to slowly put the money back, stack by stack, flashing a boyish smile as he does so. Once every bit of money is back on the pile, he peers around the side to his Garbage Bag counterpart. Borealis: That's a...big ol' pile of money to leave sitting on your desk there. GBJ: Yeah. It's one million, three hundred fifty one thousand, seven hundred forty-nine dollars and fifty cents. That's how much your entrance cost, man. As the number sinks into Johnny Borealis' head, Garbage Bag Johnny continues, still light in tone. GBJ: And while I admit, I got some kicks out of you raining all over Chris Storm's pity parade, I am a man who believes that frugality is the cornerstone of any successful business. Borealis: Weren't you and Andy Murray on the moon a while back? Garbage Bag Johnny conveniently ignores the question. GBJ: I can't sweep 1.3 million under a rug, Johnny. Wish I could, but then I'd have to spend even more money on a rug that could fit so much money under it in the first place, and then I'd have to sweep up the money I used to buy the rug somewhere, too. Before you know it, here I am with all these sweet Persian rugs. The bottom line is, you're going to have to pay it back. Borealis: Let me be straight, Johnny to Johnny. I never actually did anything illegal per se. I never forged your name, I signed mine. I never pretended to be anybody I wasn't, I was simply getting the okay on purchases from your very own people. GBJ goes to interrupt but he is cut off. Borealis: Fact is, Johnny...your people around here don't respect you. Oh, they like you well enough...those that don't side with Zenith. They think you're fun, think you're one of the guys. But they all figure you're either too baked off your own supply or too knee deep in the bullshit of the psychos on your roster to question insane purchases! Borealis' feet drop back down the floor, collapsing GBJ's neatly stacked pile in the process, and the baby faced Johnny leans in close to the desk. Borealis: I spent over one million dollars of GCW's money and hijacked your Pay Per View, and not a single person backstage saw it coming. Because they think you've got zero control up here. And none of them think you're gonna be in that seat too long. Garbage Bag Johnny stands up and starts clearing the table of money, shuffling it all hastily back into a large burlap sack with a dollar sign on it. Once he's done, save for a few stray Benjamins, he pulls out three folders and starts shifting them back and forth on his desk like a game of three card monte. GBJ: You make some good points there, number two. I do like fun. Now pick a folder, any folder. Commissioner Johnny stops shuffling folders. They're labeled "A", "B", and "C". Perfect Hair Johnny looks down at the folders, then back up at the Commish, then shakes his head. Borealis: I had more. I was gonna say how I could be your go-to guy and I could- GBJ: Yeah yeah, I know. Jesus you talk a LOT. Pick a damn folder. Johnny Borealis narrows his eyes like an old west gunfighter for a moment, then reaches his head up and brings it down on the folder marked "B." For "Borealis." Garbage Bag opens up the folder revealing a blank sheet of paper. GBJ: You know what this is? Borealis shakes his head slowly, then shrugs. Borealis: Rolling paper? GBJ: This, my friend, is a list of movies you've been in that anyone's heard of. I couldn't even find you on IMDB. But that's not the folder I wanted to show you. You want "A" or "C"? Borealis: A, I wasn't listed as "Johnny Borealis," you dingbat. B, I'm aware of my blacklist in Hollywood thank you very much. And C, A. Garbage Bag Johnny flips open folder A. This folder has writing on it. GBJ reads it over, making a clicking sound with his tongue while he shakes his head. GBJ: I guess "A" is for "attorney." Now, I won't pretend to understand all this legal mumbo jumbo, but I do understand that GCW has a crack team of attorneys, and I have full power to use them to nickel and dime every last cent back. Johnny then flips open the final folder. GBJ: That means "C" is for "contract." I'd suggest you take this one. You won't be making as much as Tom Cruise or anything, but I believe it's a fair contract. You sign it, and I won't dial up my crack team...or my crack team of lawyers. You don't, and you better hope that the executives at Touchstone are huge Tossers fans. Johnny Borealis stares like a stone into Garbage Bag Johnny's eyes for a beat before his perfect pearly whites appear back on his face. Triumphant smile plastered on him, Borealis takes the contract from the Commissioner and starts looking it over as he speaks. Borealis: HA! I knew you couldn't pass up a deal like this, my man. Why the fuck do you think I busted my ass to pull all that off? So I could get five measley minutes to talk down to Chris Storm? Please. This contract is- Pretty Boy Johnny seems to pause for a second as he holds the pen. He looks up at the Commissioner and his smile fades, an almost untraceable amount. Borealis: ...this contract is a joke, right? You forgot a couple of zeros after the salary, boss-man. Garbage Bag Johnny clicks a backup pen across the desk from Johnny Borealis. GBJ: It is what it is, man...except, you'll only be getting half of that until your debt's paid. Johnny and Johnny stare at each other. Perfect hair versus beard in a contest of wills. A tumbleweed rolls by across the desk, causing both Johnny's to look confused for a second before Borealis picks up the pen and points it at the Commissioner. Borealis: I walked out of there last time...because this company couldn't pay me the ridiculous offer they made me. They threw a shit-ton of money at me because the Commish was panicked, scared, and dumb. And it led to four wasted years of my life. Johnny and Johnny stare at the pen for an overly dramatic second. Borealis: If I cared about the money, I wouldn't have sprung for Patrick Stewart or fire dancers. I wouldn't have taken your show by the balls and squeezed the way I did. Looking Garbage Bag Johnny in the eye, Johnny Borealis signs the contract. His autograph has been thrown down on thousands and thousands of pictures and paraphernalia, but this may be the first time it holds meaning. Borealis: I had to know if you were smarter than you look...if you're smart enough to outdo the political bullshit you're currently facing. Now I suggest you don't undervalue that piece of paper I just signed, and what it can do for you. And this company. GBJ: It is done! The Commissioner raises his arms in a manner of rejoicing. GBJ: Now go squeeze some balls out there tonight, kid, but remember whose balls you're squeezing. As long as you keep your mitts off my hairy set, we'll get along just fine. Your Guilty Pleasure stands up from his seat across from GBJ and returns a bit of the luster to his boyish smile, starting to back out of the room. Borealis: I was going to mention that. You don't like to pussyfoot around your main events, do you? Chris Storm and Johnny Borealis have words and BLAMMO! Why not throw them at each other, along with Storm's wife and the prodigy of LeStatt Knight? Johnny Borealis gives the immortal wink and gun to Garbage Bag Johnny. Borealis: I like your style. Valentine: I never said it was you personally... Vivica glares past The Leader Of Men to the monster hovering behind him. Valentine: It’s that thing. Valentine: I’m not interested. Zenith: Oh, you will be interested. Bryan: This is a potential death blow here... Yale: This might be the greatest alliance I’ve ever witnessed in my life. Bryan: This could be very, very bad for GCW.
