News/Notes- 4-3-01- I'm posting Meltdown. The pop-ups got bad reviews, so I switched back. Also, we've dropped pretty low on the FWLnet list, so if everyone would go to the FWL site and try to click us up, it would help. 4-2-01- I switched the banner on the page to be a pop-up now. If everyone would go to the OOC board and let us know what you think that does for the site, I would appreciate it. 4-1-01- I'm sensing that a week in between cards is a bit much. I might add a second card, with some writing help from John Reed. In addition, click on the Warpath link, as I've posted the card so far. We'll be piecing together the tourney brackets soon. 3-31-01- Put a new poll up. It's down at the bottom of the page. I, (the webmaster) will be putting a new poll up every week on Sunday.
