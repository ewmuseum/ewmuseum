Meltdown 14 Reunion Arena, Dallas, TX ---- Introduction The lights in Reunion Arena dim, and the Dallas crowd begins to murmur. Mr. Danger doesn’t give a damn which one of them wins it, but he did leave both of them lying! Yale- Shane has won, but I don’t know if this is good or bad! I mean, what business did John Reed have down here?! Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time! Join us next Wednesday for Meltdown!!! Goodnight! The show ends with Lane slowly recovering, still not aware that he is the champ. Danger walks to the back, satisfied, as the show fades to black. . .
