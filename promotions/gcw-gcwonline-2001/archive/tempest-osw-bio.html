Tempest Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Cyberpunk Goddess Hometown Hong Kong Birthday 4/1/76 Height 5'2 Weight 133.3 Biography Tempest disappeared from the wrestling scene and in fact from public view more than five years ago, after a tumultuous career of both great achievements and terrible defeats. She was a champion is Old School Wrestling and Old School Fighting as well, not to mention a two time cruiserweight champion in fWo before arriving on the Primetime Wrestling scene. But personal tragedies marred her professional success and as she faded into the twilight many were left to wonder what happened. Others simply muttered, "good riddance," but a few have maintained hope that one day that rumble of distant thunder and that flash of far off lighting would once again signal the coming of Tempest. After reappearing in GTT7 and PTC's Infinite Gauntlet that hope was finally rewarded. And now, in GCW, it is being realized fully once again as Tempest re-emerges full force. Other Feds Formerly OSW and fWo Strengths Speed, Agility, Balance, Freakish strength for her size, especially in the core, and the ability to think on her feet Weaknesses Size, relative strength is still much less than many competitors, both her high flying and close quarters submission styles are all or nothing deals, very risky, sometimes has too much fun for her own good Wrestling Style High Flyer/Close Quarters Submission Strategy Tempest's approach to competition is very much like her approach to life. She's in it with everything she's got, for better or for worse. She'll go flying with no regard for the consequences and be more likely to remember all the times that work than the times that don't. Ditto her close quarters submission moves, which even if not applied as finishing moves can wear an opponent down. She has learned to use her strength, which is mostly in her core, to do modified moves that a man would attempt with his upper body, by using her entire body. Of course this is risky because she's right there to be grabbed if it doesn't work. But when it does, it pays off big. She is also very much about the head game. If she can get an opponent to beat himself by running headlong into a ringpost, she's happy with that. She will observe and use weaknesses and react to what she's given rather than formulating a strategy ahead of time. On their feet at the opening strains of "Battle Flag" by the Lo-Fidelity Allstars, the crowd’s volume starts to rise as flashes of lightning and quick shots of a whirling maelstrom of wind and rain illuminate the darkness on the screen accentuating the beats just as the words kick in. Then the bass beat kicks in and the images flash to that of a woman’s cobalt blue eyes flashing out of the darkness and a tumble of jet black hair, her face lit by flashes of light and a smile with a light all its own. These are interspersed with the lightning and the storm. A white hot light flashes from the screen and the real Tempest bounds from beneath the screen to a sparkling shower of blue pyro and a raucous cheer from the crowd. Andrews: Making her way to the ring, from Hong Kong, in the People’s Republic of China, weighing in at 133.3 pounds… TEEEEEEMMMPEEEEESSST!!! Bryan: Yale: Tempest skips and leaps in time to the music’s heavy beat, slapping hands and waving to throngs of fans, spinning happily as the light glints off her sparkly ring attire. As she reaches the ring, she bounds up the steps and doesn’t stop, mounting the ropes and standing atop them, toes on the tops, she raises her arms to a new chorus of cheers. She smiles mischievously and tilts her head to one side. Bryan: Yale: She laughs and leaps into a twirling dismount, before heading to the opposite corner to leap once to the top and salute the fans in the same manner. She leaps again, landing gracefully and laughing as if it were as easy as walking.
