Alexis Frost Biography Real Name Alexis Frost Nicknames The Diamond-Studded Princess Hometown Huntington Beach, California Birthday May 21, 1990 Height 5'6 Weight 106 Biography Alexis Frost came from the upper-middle class. Growing up in Huntington Beach, she always had a vested interest in fashion, even if her hidden passion was in the veterinarian field. When she met Triumph Frost in France one summer, her whole world changed. A whirlwhind romance later, they were married in Australia and living in his San Franciscan mansion. It was Alexis that pulled Triumph out from the underground fighting world and brought him to GCW, hoping to find a safer outlet for his aggression. She always remains by her side for her own reasons, even though everyone else in the world seems to question them at every turn. She's not afraid to mix it up, having stood up to the likes of Bryan Mayhem, a man that may or may not be a sadistic serial killer (spoiler: he is). Alexis has an affinity towards the female wrestlers, noting Tempest, Tessa Windsor, and Vivica J. Valentine amongst the people she most admires, to the point of blabbering Triumph's ear off over their greatness and having him ponder sticking a gun in his mouth to end the incessant noise. She loves to dress in the latest designer clothing and has a mouth that would even rival her man's, as daunting an outcome as that may seem. And while her model-like looks and fluttering eyes may make her look harmless to Triumph's opponents, she is anything but. Management Style In Yo' Face!
