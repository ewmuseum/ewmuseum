WorldWide 154 EnergySolutions Arena — Salt Lake City, Utah ---- WorldWide 154: An Introduction Andrews: SALT LAKE CITY, UTAHHHH.... are you ready for WOOOORRRRRRLDWIIIIIIDEEEEEEEE?! Pyrotechnics explode in hues of blue as the the lights begin to dance and the WorldWide logo appears on the MegaTron. "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine rips through the festival as blue and yellow fireworks criss-cross the stage, travel down the ramp, and ignite the ring in a colorful explosion that finishes with a shower of golden fire. The camera crews look to the fans in the arena, holding their handmade signs in support of their favorite wrestlers: I Feel A High Pressure Front Moving SouthWest Across Azuma KINGSLEY'S GONNA KILL YOU K-Wolf For Triple Crown Is anyone else ready to rock?! Let's get things going here tonight on WORLDWIDE! ---- 'Ms. Pierce', If You're Nasty. Backstage. The chyron reads: "Earlier Tonight". The wrestlers & guest entrance to the EnergySolutions Arena bangs open; the brief glimpse we get of the outside means it's no later than maybe noon or one. While the GCW staff would be able to say straightaway who would be entering just by the ungodly hour (seriously, what do you do for eight hours in an arena before a show?), for those folks at home, we should run through the details. The woman in the middle is a hair ahead of the others, her short, gliding strides simply more purposeful. She's tall, lithe, and scowling, in fitted pants and a "QUEEN OF LIES" tank top. To Alexandra Pierce, this shit is serious business. To the women following her in... not so much. Alex doesn't turn on her daughter, but the words themselves have a harder, harsher tone. Pierce: We're staying because Amy needs our support. Kathryn Shaw trails the pair of Pierces, seemingly in no hurry. The Siren is on the phone, and she's finishing up her call as she comes into mic range. Shaw: Well, bring them to the arena and she can try them on here. Okay, baby... mmmbye. A tap to the "end call" button does what it says on the tin. Shaw: What did I miss? Oh, wait, let me guess. She wants to do something that a normal seventeen-year-old would want to do, and you won't let her because you need someone Amy might actually believe is telling the truth about how she looks in her swimsuit, and isn't just interested in taking it off. The Medusa grunts noncommittally, plowing forward. Her daughter is much less laconic. Quinn: How do you do that? Shaw: Oh, like it's hard. The girl glances off to the side, and her upper lip curls. Quinn: Who's the weatherman hair headed our way? Shaw: Interview, of course. Quinn: Will you predict what he says, too? Shaw: I could. The Weatherman Hair in question would be Barry Goldstein, not yet dressed in his show finery (because let's be honest, Alex is super-duper-mega-mega-king-kong-mega early), but even so, he's got a GCW mic and he's about to make a very common mistake. Goldstein: Alexandra! Alex! We--just wait a second, will ya? She waits a second, and silences him with a snapping stare. Goldstein: Alexandra, just... I just wanted to ask you a question about your actions last week. She says nothing, simply staring. Goldstein: So it's okay if I ask you something? No response. Goldstein: ...all right, then. So, Alex... last week, after a stirring speech about how determined you were to get to the finals at Rampage, you... The microphone droops a little at the unrelenting stare. Barry glances to them, his hands spreading in question. Goldstein: What did I-- Shaw: The name...? She lifts her brows encouragingly. Goldstein: Ms. Pierce? Don't misjudge -- I knew I was going to get thrown around like a rag doll last week, just like I know I'm liable to get twisted up like a pretzel this week. I think the barbed wire under the ring worked out okay, too. Barry's frown is a slight one, but he's clearly working through something. Goldstein: Didn't... okay, didn't Amy say a few weeks back that she was hoping to win people's support? That, uh... that she wants to be worth something to somebody somewhere? Quinn: [quiet] Ooh, good one. Pierce: She did. Goldstein: But you cheated last week. Shaw: It was a hardcore match -- you really can't cheat... Goldstein: You tried to hurt a man. And you already warned Alexander Redding that he's not safe tonight. Pierce: Was there a question in there somewhere? Goldstein: I'm asking... I'm asking if Aimz is okay with this? With your... slide into the darkness, I guess you could call it? It just seems that -- everybody here knows who you were. What you were. Aren't you at risk of becoming that again, just to get rid of one man? Her smile thins, and the space between them evaporates. Her heels put her a smidgen taller than Barry is, looking down the length of her nose. Pierce: If you had ever been required to deal with him, you would not be asking me that, I promise you. That's the only response she gives, spinning away from him to march down the hall. Goldstein: That wasn't an answer. She doesn't turn back. Goldstein: Hey! Hey, that wasn't an-- Kathryn lays a soft hand on Barry's arm. Shaw: Careful there, stud. You... probably don't want to push her. Goldstein: It's my job to push her. It's my job to ask the tough questions. It's my job to-- The soft touch of Kathryn's fingers turns Barry's face away from the stalking Spider and towards her. She's smiling, which is never a good sign. Shaw: And do you know what my job is? Goldstein: Not... really? Shaw: It's my job to make sure that people like you leave her alone. So what do I have to do to make sure that happens? I can assure you, I can be quite accommodating. Barry turns towards the ravishing brunette, a brief smile touching his lips. Goldstein: I'm not going to allow myself to be distracted by offers of... physical comforts. Kathryn's smile becomes a quiet, almost mocking thing, her hand on his shoulder. Shaw: Oh, hon... usually, I'd take care of this myself, but Quinny will be eighteen in just a few months, so I think I should leave that to her. Don't you? Barry looks for the Devil's Daughter, his brows lifting. Goldstein: If she's only seventeen, I don't think she should-- He doesn't find her, because Quinn is standing behind him. She drops to a knee and brings her forearm between the interviewer's legs at no small amount of velocity. Barry crumples as you might expect. Quinn: Learn some manners, you helmet-haired jackass. Shaw offers her goddaughter her arm, and the two women trot off after the Medusa. Cut away. Bryan: That was earlier today, fans... and I think what Quinn did was a little bit uncalled for. Yale: Hey, if everybody knows what Alexandra Pierce used to be, is there really any use for reminding her about it all of the time? Bryan: Terrence Kingsley no doubt hopes to face Pierce in the finals of the tournament, but he'll be facing Ryuji Kamigawa tonight, who already has pinfalls over Bryan Mayhem and Ryan Gallway in his GCW career. Despite Alexandra's polite words, I'm left wondering if she really will be so accommodating if either Kamigawa or the winner of tonight's main event trips up the Pit Viper. Yale: A pissed-off Alex Pierce is a bad thing for a lot of people. Bryan: And one of them is Alexander Redding. Speaking of whom... I think it's time we got this ball rolling. Cut away. ---- Desade vs. Alexander Redding Right from the start, it's clear this isn't going to go the same way that Alexandra Pierce's match last time out at Burning Man did. Sure, the basics are the same -- Grady Patrick leads Alexander Redding out to a little bit of Social D (he's a gangster), and then the EnergySolutions Arena goes dark for the start of Hurt's "Wars". Alexandra's elevator has returned now that we have a proper stage, and this week, it has two passengers -- a tall woman in white with a towel draped over her head and a ravishing brunette in a gleaming midnight blue dress (well, technically, at least), with a headset microphone. Kathryn Shaw runs her mouth -- it's about the third best thing she does with it -- as the music dims, promising to "speak in a language even Al Redding can understand". "Okay, eh, it's aboot to get pretty excitin', don'tcha know, but before we go out for some Timmy's, we figured why not do a little wrasslin'. So lemme introduce you kids there to my bestest pal, eh? She stands one-point-seven-five meters tall and weighed in at sixty-five kilos! Oh, jeez, she's the Queen of Lies, don'tcha know -- Alexandra Pierce! DEEE-SAAAHHHHD!" This would be where the woman at her side throws off the towel. And where it's not Alexandra Pierce, of course, but her daughter, because Desade has bounded over the guardrail and up onto the apron. She takes the top rope in a single bound, running it like a tightrope. Unlike last week, Grady has Red prepared, and when she leaps for the shotgun-style missile dropkick, he catches her in a modified powerbomb, flipping over into the achilles lock as Hal Jenkins calls for the bell. Redding cranks back on the hold, but Pierce is able to scramble for the bottom strand of rope, and the Canadian technician exercises his right to hold on till the count of four, trying to slow the faster Spider down. When he finally breaks the hold, Alex (the female one) tumbles to the floor while Alex (the male one) climbs out to the apron after her. Our wonderful referee makes a big theatrical count as the Willing Villain hits the floor. Redding pulls Pierce up by the hair, and she shoots up with the tug, swatting his hands away. A couple of open-handed shots to the face rock Alexander back, but he ducks the roundhouse kick that follows. It's important to note here that Pierce stops her own momentum around 180° into the spin, both because it allows her to catch Shaw's eye at ringside while she makes some kind of hand sign, and because Redding slides in to deliver the hammerlock into a twisting around clothesline he calls "MEH! SHADDUP,", and she's too dazed to fight back the second time he lifts her. Red goes airborne Bret Hart style as he whips her towards the security barricade, and the force of it send Pierce flying to thud against the steel. Alexander slides into the ring, coming up to one knee with a wide, shit-eating grin in place, his arms spread to drink in the adulation of the masses. The fact that outside of Grady that sounds more like boos is really immaterial, but, later on, it'll be this arrogance that Kathryn Shaw cites as her excuse for getting involved. Shaw climbs up to the apron to bark some unkind things at the Willing Villain, which in turn draws Grady Patrick around the apron to defend his man (not to mention leveling some deprecations of his own). This is just about when Hal Jenkins completely loses control of things, as Kathryn ends up turning to get her bitch on, and Grady stomps up the steps to meet her. She's loud, and he even merits snaps, but she misses (or at least seems to miss) his surreptitious wave to his charge. Redding takes the hint and slides out of the ring to do more damage. He's not at all deterred by the sight of the dark-haired and bespectacled form draped across his would-be victim. In fact, Red simply loops his arms lazily around the waist of the human shield, setting her aside in a manner that can at best be called "demeaning". The trouble is (as those fans sitting nearby might have told him if he weren't such a raging asshole), that wasn't Quinn lying atop her mother, but Pierce on her daughter. A moment of shock is all the Medusa needs, launching herself at Redding. Her flying ace crusher lays him low, and, conveniently, this is about when Shaw cedes the argument, slithering back down to the floor with both hands upraised. By the time poor Hal Jenkins has turned, Pierce has shed her dark wig and glasses, and she rolls Redding into the ring and bounds to the apron. She slings herself over the top in a rolling senton and continues past for a quebrada -- but Red rolls out of the way. She lands on her feet and pivots, this time for a moonsault kneedrop. Redding rolls the other way, and she misses. And the entire arena gasps at her uncharacteristic yowl of pain when her knee crashes into the mat. Pierce scrambles back into a neutral corner, clutching her leg. Her face is contorted in agony. Hal Jenkins physically separates the two Alexes, dropping to a knee to check on the condition of the GTT7 champion. JB and Dave begin speculating on the extent of the injury, anywhere from a dislocated patella to ligament damage. Redding doesn't wait long for Pierce to be examined, brushing past to stomp at her knee. David Yale is quick to point out that there's virtually no one in GCW worse to have as an opponent if you've injured a body part than Redding. He yanks on her foot, and it's about then that Quinn displays the bordering-on-stupidity gumption she simply must have picked up from Aimz, as she clambers to the apron to take a swing at the Willing Villain that must have missed by a good two feet. It's in full view of the official, though, and Jenkins is left with little choice other than to toss the Teenaged Anarchist over the strenuous objections of her and her godmother. While Quinn and Kathryn argue with the ref, Redding slaps on a figure four leglock, earning the derision of JB when Grady Patrick reaches in to add additional leverage. Quinn finally relents, both hands squeezing Shaw's in thanks, and Hal slides to the floor to make sure that the girl is all the way gone. She ends up on the stage, and there might be a slight redhead lurking nervously in the entranceway. Jenkins turns back, but not before Kathryn has had a chance to dig her expensive manicure into Alex Redding's eyes to break the hold. Pierce uses the bottom rope to pull herself into a corner, Kathryn at her side to whisper words of encouragement. Grady Patrick thumps the head of his cane on the mat to get his blinded client's attention, but when he slides it in as a weapon, the sound has also attracted the notice of both Jenkins and Shaw, the latter of whom makes a clumsy grab for it. It's Hal Jenkins that comes away with the weapon, however. He wrests it from both Shaw and Redding, casting it to the floor at Grady Patrick's feet. Kathryn rolls over, and our cameras cut to the crowd with all due haste to avoid a heavy FCC fine. In the instant the shot is away from the ring, there is the wet smack of flesh on flesh and a shouted "HEY!" from David Yale. When it comes back, Shaw on the floor pulling up her top, and Alexander Redding is splayed out in the middle of the ring. Alex puts no weight on her left knee as she crawls atop Redding. Hal Jenkins turns back and has no idea what happened, even though Grady Patrick is shouting. Left with no other choice, Hal counts the one, the two, and the three, making the Queen of Lies the first of the final four advancing to Rampage. Winner: Alexandra Pierce No music comes. The moment the bell rings, Amy Campbell leads the trainers out to look at her lover's knee, and while they look at her, David Yale has the truck queue up some footage. If you watch carefully, Quinn passes something to Kathryn when she gives her the hand squeeze. If you watch carefully, Kathryn Shaw passes it to Alex while Pierce is in the corner. If you look carefully, Shaw adjusts her top during the scuffle for the cane. There's only one camera that caught what happened -- the one up on the stage that usually takes a shot of the crowd. It's a long shot, showing Alexandra Pierce pulling herself up to her feet. She limps over towards Redding, and when he turns away from the scuffle over the cane -- when he does and the cameras jerk spasmodically away from Kathryn's impending nip-slip, Alex catches him with a shot to the jaw. He collapses like a ton of bricks. David Yale grouses about how Redding got robbed by Pierce, and the so-called Best Wrestler In the World didn't do more than a handful of moves. That grumbling is quieted as we come back live and the training staff and Aimz help Pierce out of the ring. She puts no weight on the left leg. As they lead her up the ramp, Alex raises a fist up to the crowd, and Kathryn turns at the head of the ramp to wave to some of the throng -- can't have the kids too worried, right? As she's led through the curtain, Yale references Alex's "Queen of Lies" nickname as gently as he can.
