WorldWide 97 Pacific Coliseum — Vancouver, British Columbia ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Sacramento fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: WELCOME EVERYONE! His voice is nearly completed overshadowed by the roaring ovation from the crowd. Bryan: We are LIVE from the Pacific Coliseum in the very beautiful Vancouver, British Columbia! My name is James Bryan and I'm joined by my colleague, and friend, David Yale. Yale: Let's not get ahead of yourself, JB. Friends?! Bryan: What am I going to do with you, David! Yale: Hah! Well, for starters, you can tell these people about the spectacular night we've got ahead of us. Bryan: You couldn't have used a better adjective, David. Tonight we're seeing the in-ring debut of a new GCW superstar, Face! He'll be up against the new leader of The Movement, Dan Black, in what we anticipate to be a good showing of the Movement's new direction. Yale: You can't forget about the two returning debuts of Mr. Danger and Phil Atken, either! Bryan: That's right! Mr. Danger returns to the GCW for his first match in... well God I don't even remember how long! And Phil Atken looks to create a better name in GCW for himself this time than he did the last when he squares off against Chris Bagwell. Yale: I don't particularly think Bagwell is going to just lay down for Atken, debut or not. Bryan: And what about the reuniting of Oblivion? Yale: Well that is certainly something to see. Shaman and Bryan Mayhem are one hell of a group to mess with. Bryan: Well tonight, they're being pit against the team of LeStatt Knight and Andy Murray. One has to wonder if these two will even be able to last in the ring together! Yale: I don't know, JB, but I'll tell you what I really can't wait for. How about Jay Terror's gauntlet for Jordan White! Bryan: I can't even begin to imagine what the World Champion has in store for his former best friend. Yale: Well, no better way to find out than to get this show rolling! Bryan: Then let's make this official. WELCOME TO GCW WORLDWIDE!! ---- The Big Question The scene cuts to backstage, in the locker room of the Movement. Kevin Jacobs is standing by, looking like a complete nerd, as always. The view pans out to reveal AXEL Action sitting in one of the chairs, adjusting the kick pads on his boots. Jacobs clears his throat and begins to speak. Kevin Jacobs: Good evening, wrestling fans. I'm Kevin Jacobs, here with you on another exciting edition of Worldwide, and I'm hoping to get a few words with the Unholy One, himself, AXEL Action. Upon hearing his name, AXEL shifts his gaze upwards, his painted face having no expression on it, whatsoever. He stands and walks over to Kevin Jacobs, those soulless eyes staring at him, almost as if he were trying to burn a hole through the nerdy announcer. AXEL Action: What do you want, Jacobs? Kevin Jacobs: I was wondering if I could have a couple minutes of your time to ask you some questions? AXEL Action: You've got five minutes, because that's all I can stand to tolerate you for. Jacobs looked at AXEL like the Unholy One had killed his puppy. However, he proceeded to his first question. Kevin Jacobs: Coming off of the heels of a big win against Timmy Lester last week, what are your feelings on your current winning streak? AXEL Action: Well, Kevin, I guess this would have to be one of those 'I told you so' kinds of cases. You see, when I returned to wrestling in America a little less than six months ago, I told everyone that I was going to cut a swath through anyone who stood in the way of what I wanted. All the doubters, all the naysayers, well, they all emerged, as always, and claimed that I wouldn't amount to anything. Thanks to Lord Tioneb, I'm proving them all wrong. Now that I'm out from under the shadow of James von Doom, I'm free to rise up the ranks and eventually take what I and everyone else in this place covet most-- The GCW World Heavyweight Championship. My winning streak is only the first step to achieving that goal. Kevin Jacobs nodded, taking in every word. He seemed to be overall impressed by AXEL's bold words. Kevin Jacobs: Also, what are your thoughts about Mr. Dan... At that point, AXEL snatched the microphone from Kevin Jacobs and pushed him out of the scene. AXEL Action: Mr. Danger, Mr. Danger... Who are you? Honestly, you're supposed to be some kind of 'legend' or something, yet no one seems to know who you are. Honestly, I've asked everyone in the locker room, and none of them seem to know anything about you, other than you were one of the pioneers of the Dangerous Games match. I'm sure you plan to come in here with your big comeback, and just tear right through me, correct? Well, I can guarantee, right now, that you won't. In fact, after tonight's match, there won't even be enough left of you to retire. So, I once again pose this question... Who the Hell is Mr. Danger? At that moment, a member of the GCW ring crew walks by, and having heard AXEL's question, says, "He's a legend! Honest!" AXEL Action: Bullshit... ---- Danger pre-match interview The scene opens up in the backstage area, where we see GCW interviewer Kevin Jacobs standing in front of the WorldWide set with a microphone in hand. Jacobs: Ladies and Gentlemen. Please welcome my guest at this time, MR. DANGER!! The crowd erupts as Danger emerges into the scene. He is dressed to wrestle tonight. He is wearing black wrestling trunks with the word "DANGER" across them. He also is wearing a new Mr. Danger GCW t-shirt, also black with a yellow triangle surrounding an exclamation point that reads, "DANGER, He's Back in GCW". The crowd roar quiets down. Jacobs: Danger, we are moments away from your return match here in GCW. It's been years since you have been a competitor inside the squared circle. Tonight you step into the ring with GCW star Axel Action. Your thoughts?? Danger: Kevin, I couldn't be more ready to compete here tonight! I've been preparing for this moment for months now, and tonight its finally time. Time for me to prove to the entire world, and most importantly to myself, that I can still get things done in the ring! Crowd cheers as Danger pauses. Danger: And Axel Action! I've had my eye on you for awhile now. You have quite the winning streak going, but tonight thats all gonna come to an end my friend! You had better be ready for the biggest match of your career, because I'm gonna take you to school inside that ring tonight!! Crowd erupts again as Danger now has a huge smile on his face. Jacobs: Danger, last week here on WorldWide you made your grand return to GCW, only to be interrupted by GCW newcomer Face. Your thoughts?? Crowd boos at the mention of Face. Danger's smile quickly turns into a face of rage and anger. Danger: David Spencer, Face, whatever you want to call yourself. You made a very bad call by coming down and interrupting my return speech! The only words of advice I have for Mr. Spencer is this. Stay out of my business, and stay out of my match. Because if you stick your nose in my business again.... I'm gonna rip that lighter out of your hand and light that mask of yours on FIRE!!! Crowd erupts in cheers. Danger: But back to the matter at hand... AXEL ACTION!! You had better be ready for a fight, because one way or another, I'm walking out of here tonight with a victory! And after tonight, I'm gonna go into Dangerous Games, walk into the battle royal, and walk out the winner and the new number one contender!!!! Jacobs: Mr. Danger, thank you very much for your time, and good luck in your match this evening. Danger: Thank you Kevin. Danger leaves the set and begins to walk towards the entrance to the arena. The cameras follow him as he walks. He passes by a few GCW backstage workers on his way to the ring. Backstage Hand: Good luck tonight, Danger. Danger: Thank you. Bryan: Fans, we are literally moments away from our opening contest as Danger is making his way towards the arena entrance as we speak! Yale: He had better be ready JB! Axel Action is one tough cookie! Danger continues to walk. He passes a few more doors and hallways as he approaches GCW head of security and former Hardcore Legend Toad! Toad: Good luck Danger. Danger keeps walking as he salutes Toad. He starts to pump his arms and breathe deeper as he is getting mentally ready to compete. He then passes GCW US Champion Digital Mortality, who is standing next to the water cooler. DM: Good luck Danger. Danger keeps walking as Digital Mortality pats him on the shoulder as he walks by. Danger is pumping his arms and twisting his neck. As he gets closer to the arena entrance, he turns a corner and standing directly in his path is none other than GCW President Steven Caldera!! Danger stops immediately, as he almost runs right into him!! The crowd explodes with cheers!! The two make eye contact and Danger just gives Caldera a cold hard stare right into his eyes. Caldera glares back at him. The fans are going nuts! Danger takes in a deep breath and then speaks. Danger: Steven.... Caldera's stare turns into a somewhat evil smile. Caldera: Danger.... I just wanted to say..... good luck.... Danger's eyes never looked away from Caldera's. He just continues to stare at him, as he then takes another deep breath and walks past him not saying a word. Caldera then turns his head as he watches Danger walk towards the curtain. Caldera's smile turns to a slight chuckle as he nods his head. Bryan: WOW! The tension is so thick you could almost cut it with a knife! Yale: There is so much history between Danger and Caldera, I thought the roof was gonna blow off this place! Bryan: Fans, the match is next!!!! ---- Mr. Danger vs. Axel Action Bryan: Up next folks, we have a surprise for the old school GCW audience as Mr. Danger makes his return, right back in to a GCW ring. Yale: Danger once yielded quite a bit of power in this company, it will be interesting to see how he manages against a man really trying to define himself outside of tag action . Will he still have some of that ring rust I hear so much about or will he be able to take out the Axel Action? It's going to be an interesting one to watch. Bryan: Indeed, both men will be trying to continue to establish or re-establish themselves in GCW, it will be interesting to see to what lengths either man will go to achieve their goal here tonight. Yale: Their evenly matched competitors too, both stand at the same height and Danger has a very narrow weight advantage, it should be a good one. Bryan: Let's go over to Andrews to hear the introductions. Andrews: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first... “"Take a Look Around" by Limp Bizkit begins to play as Mr. Danger makes his appearance at the top of the entrance way. Andrews: He stands here tonight at six foot, two inches, he weighs in at two hundred and thirty five pounds. He is MISTERRRRRRRRRRRRRR DANGERRRRRRRRRRR. Danger's pyro hits as he is halfway to the ring on the entrance ramp. He will stops and looks up at the ceiling as he raises both arms into the air then 3 bursts of blue and red pyro will explode behind him. Yale: Danger only back in the company for a cup of coffee, and already trying to show off with a fancy pyro display. Bryan: At least give him a chance to get in to the ring before you start going after him. Danger slaps a few fans hands and shakes a few more as he makes his way down to the ring. He jumps up on the apron and enters the ring, giving a few waves to the fans from inside the ring. Bryan: With Danger in the ring, we're about to get our first glimpse at GCW newcomer Face. With that said, back over to Andrews. Andrews: And introducing the opponent... The opening drum solo of "Perfect Insanity" by Disturbed hits as the outline of a man wearing a hooded cloak emerges to a rather large round of jeers and boos from the crowd. As the first strain of the opening guitar riff begins, he thrusts his arms out to the side and throws his head back in a crucifix pose as pillars of fire explode along the perimeter of the stage. In the light of the fire, the robe is shown to be black in color with intricate red designs with silver linings. He flips the hood back to reveal himself as none other than The Unholy One, himself, Axel Action. Andrews: Currently making his way to the ring, he weights in tonight at two hundred and twenty five pounds, he stands at six foot, two inches. He is... AXELLLLLLL ACTIONNNNNNNNNN! Bryan: And there he is, the man trying to make sure that Mr. Danger's return is not gonna to be pleasant or victorious. Yale: And prove himself viable as a singles wrestler once more. He saunters to the ring with a snarl upon his lips. With a purpose in his step, he climbs the ring steps and turns to face the crowd while standing on the apron, and gives one more crucifix pose to another round of boos, before entering the ring and removing his robe. Bryan: So here we are, the relative rookie, going up against the returning veteran. Yale: My money is on Danger being a broken down, past it, out of it, husk of a wrestler. This one is going to be a cakewalk for Action. Bryan: Very risky to write-off so many years of experience and expertise. Yale: I guess we'll just have to wait and see, won't we? Bryan: We're about to get an answer as this match is now... The bell rings. Bryan: ...underway! Mr. Danger and Axel Action begin to circle each other, sizing each other up. Yale: You know I just realised this match is about Danger vs. Action. It's a true Hollywood match! Bryan: An astute observation for the ages. Action lunges towards Danger but gets caught in a headlock by Danger, who in turn begins a ratchet up the pressure. Danger tries to jump up and pull his legs around Action, aiming to lock in grapevine, but Action uses this moment to flip Danger over into a pinning situation. One! Two! Bryan: Kickout there by Danger. Impressive showing by Action there, especially right out the gate of this match. Yale: The guy is trying to make an impact, like I told you, youth trumps experience in this bout. You just have to realise it's been a while since Danger felt the pressure of the ring. Both men find themselves back up to a vertical base as the circling of the ring begins anew. Each man hoping to spot an opening. Danger launches in with a series of forearms to the face of Axel Action, stunning him momentarily. Taking advantage of the stunned Action, he whips him into the ropes, waiting for him to rebound. As Axel comes back towards Danger, Danger attempts to go for a discus clothesline but Action ducks out the way. Danger spins around the meet Action again and before Action can taking advantage of the situation he's found as the victim of a double leg takedown. Bryan: Action just couldn't deliver on the opportunity of a slightly dazed Danger there... Action is quick to act however, rolling over on Danger and throwing a good few knees into his gut for good measure to go along with it. Yale: I'm sorry, you were saying? Action backs off away from Danger as he pulls himself up after the series of blows from Action. As Danger is getting back up on his feet, Action runs towards him, looking to nail him with a Shining Wizard, however Danger ducks the running knee, Action can't stop the momentum and goes flying into the ropes. As he rebounds back off the ropes, Danger goes for the roll-up. ONE! TWO! Bryan: Another kickout! Yale: If Danger was hoping to sneak in a win here tonight, he's mistaken. It's going to take a lot more than a roll-up to take out Axel Action. Danger looks mildly frustrated at the kick out and goes for a running knee, however Action manages to avoid Danger and Danger's leg gets caught up between the middle and bottom ropes. With Danger's leg entangled, Action goes up to the top rope, hoping to take advantage of the exposed and defenceless Danger, as he's about to dive off on to Danger's leg, the referee jumps in the way and refuses to let him continue. As Action and the referee argue, Danger uses the moment to lean up and shake the top rope, causing Axel to crotch himself. Yale: What is this? A handicap match? Bryan: The referee did the right thing, if Action was allowed to pull of that dive, Mr. Danger's career could have been over before it even began again. Yale: Whether it was right or wrong, that referee just gave Danger an opening that he should not have had, and now he's helping Danger get freed from the ropes. Can you say bias? Bryan: I can say “doing his job”, I hope that's good enough for you because that's exactly what this is. With Danger now free of the ropes cluthes, he hobbles over the the crotched and downed Axel Action, he climbs up and positions Action for a superplex, however Axel begins fighting off Mr. Danger. The back and forth continues until Action knocks Danger flying off of the top rope. Danger rolls through the fall however and goes back towards Action. Bryan: Neither man is letting the other gain the upper hand in this bout. Yale: To give him credit, it seems like Mr. Danger may have successful fought off the ring rust. Danger goes back towards Action, who is still perched on the top rope. However, as he walks over, Axel boots him right in the face. As Danger turns back towards Axel, slightly dazed, Axel positions himself and takes out Mr. Danger with the Total Sacrilege cutter. Yale: Well, a moment is a lifetime in wrestling. Bryan: And now Axel is going for the cover! ONE! TWO! THREE! The bell rings once more. Andrews: Here is your winner, AXELLLLLLLL ACTIONNNNNNNNNN. "Perfect Insanity" by Disturbed hits once more, as Axel Action raises his arms in victory, looking down at Mr. Danger, satisfied in a job well done. He bails out the ring and heads backstage. Yale: I told you, youth would triumph over experience Bryan: You still have to hand it to Mr. Danger, who gave it his all in his return to GCW. He just wasn't quite quick enough tonight. Yale: Well, he's not exactly getting any younger, you have to wonder if he'll ever get any quicker. Bryan: Hold on! I think that’s Face coming out of the crowd and Danger is completely oblivious! Sure enough, when Danger’s back is turned towards him, Face dashes out from the crowd and slides into the ring under the bottom ropes. The crowd gets into a frenzy, but it’s too late as Danger gets caught completely off guard with a chop block to the back of his knee! Danger goes down hard and the crowd boos loudly as Face gets back up to his feet. Yale: Down goes danger! Down goes Danger! Bryan: What is Face doing!? First he interrupts Danger’s comeback speech on the last World Wide. Now he attacks him after his debut match? Yale: Yep. Danger clutches the knee Face hit the chop block on as he rolls to his side in pain. However, this doesn’t stop Face from continuing forward with his attack as he rips the leg away from Danger’s hands and then flips over Danger, pulling Danger’s leg along with him in a hamstring pull. Danger now rolls to his good knee to try and comfort the leg, but Face just throws a hard soccer kick to the hurt knee. Now Danger is rolling on the canvas in pain as Face stares down at him with sightless eyes, letting him writhe for a while. Yale: I wonder if Face knows about Danger’s knee surgeries... Bryan: Gee, what do you think? Of course he knows! And now he’s just letting Danger roll around in pain. This guy’s head isn’t screwed on right! The crowd continues to boo and jeer Face tremendously as the masked man takes the hurt leg of Danger in his hands, then starts throwing rapid, hard kicks to the knee of Danger, who is pretty much defenceless at this point. Face suddenly stops, however, after about ten or so kicks and walks off to the side of the ring. Bryan: Well, it looks like even Face has some decency in him. Yale: I wouldn’t be so sure... Contrary to what Bryan seemed to think, Face was not leaving the ring. Instead, he calls for a microphone from a ring aide, who promptly gives him one. Face then turns back to Danger, who is still visibly in a lot of pain, and begins to stare directly at him, even stooping down a little bit to get closer. Face: Do you see now? Hmmm? You’re not cut out for this, Danger! You’re old and crippled, but your too damn stubborn to admit it and accept your life being one big pile of mediocre crap! The crowd boos louder than ever at Face at that last comment. Bryan: Face is stepping over the line here! First he tries to aggravate Danger’s old knee injury and now he’s verbally harassing and disgracing him when he can’t get up to fight back! Danger’s face is now twisted with a mix of pain and anger. He tries to get to his feet to get back at Face for what he’s said, but his knee gives out under him when he does. Face shakes his head at this and continues to speak directly to Danger’s face. Face: You’re a ticking time bomb, Danger...Except, when the bomb goes off, it won’t hurt any enemies of yours. No; just you and your precious family... That’s the golden word it seems as Danger suddenly lunges forward at Face as soon as Face mentions his family. However, he over calculates his knee’s ability to hold up and Face is able to sidestep Danger and shove him into the ropes. Danger turns, but rebounds off of the ropes right into a rolling knee bar applied on his bad knee! Danger screams out in pain as Face locks in and tightens the hold right away! Bryan: My God! Face is going to tear Danger’s knee apart! Yale: Well, Danger probably should have thought about this happening when he came back to the ring... Bryan: Dave! Yale: JB! The crowd boos loudly, but then suddenly cheers as security and EMTs FINALLY runs down the ramp. At that point, Face lets go of the hold and hurries back out into the crowd, soon disappearing in the masses. The EMTs quickly head into the ring and tend to Danger’s knee. Bryan: Well, Face may have just put Danger right back on the shelf after only one match back. Yale: Yeah, I guess Face might have gone a little far – but Danger really should have thought about this happening with his injuries in the past being well documented. Bryan: I don’t care about that, Dave. Face needs to learn he can’t just mess with careers like that. Yale: Would you want to teach him that, JB? I know I wouldn’t. ---- Dropping Compliments Last week the world saw a side of Tessa Windsor they had hoped never to see. This week, the sight was not much different. With her hair pulled back in a tight ponytail, one was easily able to what little emotion there was on Tessa’s face. She slipped through the hallway not her usually chatty self and stopped at the door of GCW’s second in charge. Basing her relationship with Zenith off his past fondness of her abilities, she assumed they’d be at that same level once again and bust into his office as if it were nothing. Windsor: Now what’s that about me lacking killer instinct, Christian? She plopped herself down in a seat across from his and kept her eyes locked on him with a smirk spread wide across his face. She was dying for his critique, dying for him to admit that she’d finally blossomed into a real threat. Zenith: I... could get used to that. Tessa Windsor seemed oddly satisfied with the answer from her former Hunting Grounds mentor. It was the feeling one would get when their parents told them they did a good job, or a coach said you had a nice. The cold cadet blue eyes of Christian Zentih glared back at Tessa Windsor, as if there was just a little bit more to it. Zenith: Now, if we could just move this new found sense of power a little BEYOND the realm of a deeply personal vendetta... and more into the realm of making a statement. People are going to think Vivica was a personal thing, and that won't happen every night. If you want that World Championship as bad as I think you do... you need to keep your success fresh in their eyes. Tessa nods, absorbing the information like a sponge as he continues. Zenith: Because the average person is a fool. A very 'what have you done for me lately' type of person. If it hasn't happened in the past two weeks you need to do it again. People are fickle, ignorant... and very stuck in their ways. Windsor: I strongly agree with you. However, if I know you, which I know for a fact I do, you’ve probably already got some kind of ace up your sleeve. So tell me Christian, what’s this idea that you have boiling in the front of your mind? She could hardly contain her nerves as she anxiously awaited her next chance to shine. Zenith: I booked a match for later tonight... Tessa Windsor vs. Markus Stone. Regular match. You won't have the weapons to use so you better make it count. Tessa nodded at the proposal from Zenith, with a bit of a smirk. Zenith: Also... don't worry about Valentine. She's no where close to being medically cleared. She won't be causing any problems for you for a long time. You did quite the number on her. There’s an odd silence between the two as even Tessa seems surprised that she was capable of such an act. It is a short lived feeling however as she quickly shrugs it off and goes back to her mean new ways. Windsor: Good, that’s one less obstacle. It’s just a matter of time now. Just wait Christian, even you’ll be surprised. She heads for the doorway but is stopped by a lingering thought. Windsor: And for the record, none of this is because of you. You simply gave me some means to make a statement, so don’t you even think about giving yourself any sort of credit. I know how you are Christian, and I’ll be damned if you think that I’m some kind of pawn in your own grudge with Vivica. As she turned around and left the room a smile crept across the face of the GCW Commissioner, almost laughing at Tessa's last statement. She can deny it all she wanted to but the truth is... she already was. ---- An Unlikely Re-Introduction As the combatants of the last match disappear into the backstage area, a large man suddenly leaps over the guardrail surrounding the ring, emerging from the sea of GCW fans, and swiftly dives under the bottom rope. Within a split second the man has torn the microphone out of Andrews’ hands and shoved the ring announcer aside. Yale: Who in the blue hell is that? JB? Bryan: Uh… It seems as though we have some kind of security breach, an audience member has jumped the guardrail and… um… GCW’s head of security, Toad-- No wait… he was fired two weeks ago… Anyways, someone in the security team, should be arriving very promptly to take care of this interruption, sorry, folks… Yale: He’s got a mic! Can we get that cut or something? No wait… take a look at this character, I want to hear what he has to say before security makes him choke on that stick. The fans are as confused as the commentators… perhaps even more. The man standing in the center of the ring rolls his head along the grooves of his shoulders, then peers out with bloodshot eyes sunken deep within his skull. The man is large… over six feet in height… around 6’ 3” (give or take)… though he hunches forward like a scarecrow, making numerous onlookers uneasy. He wears a ragged grey and brown poncho… rancid with filth from a thousand gutters. He face is covered by a thick scraggily reddish mahogany beard, easily a half foot in length. Atop his crown is a massive wily goldenrod mane that stretches past his mid back… a couple long blonde braids fall off one side of his head. The man runs one of his fingerless glove-dawning dirty mitts through his Rapunzel-in-Rags follicles, and brings the microphone up to his mouth with the other. He speaks deep voice, raspy and wry. Man: Global… Championship… Wrestling… So this is where you’re hiding from me… You thought you could run… you thought that if you left the Universal Wrestling Federation you could escape ME… you thought if you came all the way down ‘ere to this promotion that you’d never have to me… WELL YOU WERE WRONG, RENEGADE! BECAUSE HERE I AM!! Yale: Renegade?! This guy is demented! Bryan: If he is referring to Rich Rollins, GCW Hall of Famer… well… Tragically, the world of professional wrestling lost him earlier this year… God rest his soul… Man: RENEGADE! You rose through ranks in UWF like no one else ever had before… except for one lone soul… the shooting star himself… I’M TALKING ABOUT ME! KENNY MERCURY!!!! Saliva spewing from his lips as he raises his voice in a fit of madness, Kenny Mercury lunges into the ropes and stares menacingly down the entrance ramp, perhaps waiting for a response that could never be delivered. One of his tattered black wrestling boots rests on the middle rope, the other on the canvass. His tights are revealed as the poncho whips over the side. They are black, with a red astrological Mercury symbol embroidered on the side of each leg. Surrounding the head of the symbol like the halo of an icon painting, is a white cog shape. Bryan: I can’t believe it… but that’s Kenny Mercury, formerly of the UWF, the same federation that once housed current GCW superstars like Digital Mortality, Seymour Almasy, Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor, and Axel Action, among others! Yale: Including the late great, “Renegade” Rich Rollins, JB? Bryan: That’s right, this guy was one of their top stars at one time… But-- Yale: Sheesh! That guy’s seen better days! What the hell happened to him?! Bryan: I have no idea, but even still… He doesn’t have a GCW contract of any kind, so he has no right to be in the ring, disrupting the flow of the show! Mercury: So now, Renegade… now that the Manalishi has told me where you were hiding… Kenny’s jaw drops into a deranged open mouthed smile… the kind that makes kids hate clowns… forever. He takes his index finger and brings it up to his temple. He slowly rotates his finger back and forth, as if to grind something unseen into his skull. Yale: Mana--wha? Bryan: I don’t know… Mercury: Now we can finally have the match that we were DESTINED to have… I’ve decimated and crippled every opponent the UWF had to offer… except you… and that time is now! The world of professional wrestling will see the rebirth of the SHOOTING STAR, KENNY MERCURY! … and the burning out of your light, Rollins… once ‘n for all…. Just then, a squad of GCW security team members charge down to the ring from backstage. They quickly surround the ring and begin closing in… Kenny steps up to the top of a turnbuckle. Bryan: Finally… security is here… though a little late… Maybe they just aren’t as efficient without Toad at the helm. Yale: Questioning Commissioner Zenith’s decisions? Bold. Mercury: EEHEHA! I’M A SHOOTING STAR! THE TECHNICAL WIZARD! I’M A SHINING GOLDEN GOD!!! GYAH! Kenny outstretches both arms wide and arches his back, soaking in all the spotlight he can… Just as a security team member grabs him by the poncho, and tears him down into the waiting arms of the GCW security team.
