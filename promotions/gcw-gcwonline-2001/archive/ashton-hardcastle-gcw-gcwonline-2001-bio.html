Ashton Hardcastle Biography Real Name Ashton Hardcastle Nicknames 'Teen Heart Throb' Hometown Beverly Hills, CA Birthday 11/03/84 Height 6'0 Weight 215 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography 'Teen Heartthrob' Ashton Hardcastle is a cocky, and arrogant 20 year old ex-model from Beverly Hills, CA. He was born to rich parents from Beverly Hills and grew up with only the best treatment. At the age of 15, he began working out/modeling for such magazines as 'Teen Beat' and 'Seventeen'. He thought his modeling career would bring him his dream of being famous. However, he was apparently less then stellar in his pictures, despite him thinking that his pictures were amazing (again, the cockiness coming into play as Ashton is a bit of a narcissist). He had always had an interest in professional wrestling, and decided that he had a great look and was very athletic. So he has come to GCW on a journey to reach his ultimate dream... being famous. But will his cockiness and arrogance get in his way in the pro wrestling world? Only time will tell...
