Ash Bombay Biography Real Name Levi Ash Nicknames Violent Messiah, Da Best Thing Since Porn, Da Gift and Da Curse, Angel of Violence Hometown Oklahoma City, Oklahoma Birthday 1-14-1983 Height 5'9 Weight 220 Biography Ash was born in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA. He is a part white, part spanish, and a part Japanese decent. A bit of a mutt, but he was raised in the united states loving the use of American amature wrestling, plus the high flying of lucha libre, and the stiff style of the Japanese pro wrestling scene. Ash began his wrestling career at a tender young age of 12. On his 12th birthday, Ash was like, I'm going to be a wrestler, and there isn't anything you can do about it. He wrestled under the name of "Lightning" Levi Ash. Being a short kid, he was able to get away with being a tall midget, and wrestling other littler wrestlers. He eventually got into a renowned wrestling promotion called CROW. He quickly defeated the Wardancer for the CROW Lightweight Championship. He would later go on to defeat "Sadistic" Shane Alexander for the CROW Hardcore Championship. He helped usher in the use of the hardcore matches into CROW. He made a small name for himself but never claiming the big championships. Once he turned 18, he began a new career of being a porn star. Upon his return from the lifestyle of the porn industry, Ash really developed as a wrestler. He was still making porno flicks, but was also rising the ranks of the wrestling federations he was in. He walked into UDWA and won the World Championship in his first match. Outlasting 30 other UDWAers in a royal rumble to proclaim the vacant championship. The promotion quickly folded upon the controversy of Ash being a porn star, and a wrestler. Everyone was shocked at the venture and quickly jumped ship. Ash then joined a promotion called EPW. He quickly became their first ever US champion, and won their Extreme championship at the same time, as well as their Tag Team champion at the same time. But Ash was willing to give all that up for that EPW World Championship. Which Ash did, and Ash ended up in the ring with his tag team partner T-Bone. The match was an instant classic, but Ash was thrown into the dumpster first, and T-Bone walked out the EPW World Champion. The promotion soon folded after that. Ash Bombay toiled around a few wrestling promotions after that, but nothing really kept his heart like EPW did. Ash continued to do his porno flicks tho. Ash soon came out of his semi retirement in full force in Early 2005 however. He joined XWA. Ash quickly claimed the XWA TV championship as his own. In his first XWA ppv he was apart of the Main Event, defending his XWA TV championship in the infamous Eclipse II match. He went up against such XWA stars such as Streets Disciple, Chris Bomber, Annabelle Lee, Kris Keebler, and others. Ash being brand new to the promotion, and less then a month out of retirement, fared decently as he didn't win the match, but successfully defended his XWA TV championship in the match. Ash then went on to win the XWA Xtreme Stipulations Champion, along with still being the XWA TV Champion. Even though being a double champion, Ash still wasn't getting the respect that he deserved. Ash's two month long XWA TV title reign was then ended by Annabelle Lee's younger sister Charlie Tarasov. A couple of weeks later, Ash's XWA XS one month championship reign was ended by XWA legend The Excitor. After a month and a half of losing all his matches, Ash knew that it was time for a change. He began to reflect his life, and decided it was time to step out of the Porn Star light, and lose his porn star name of Levi Ash, and go to his roots, and use his real name of Ash Bombay. During this time, Ash began getting religious visions that he absolutely could not explain. It's almost like being a stigmata. But he is the Second Coming. Ash Bombay quickly rose to the finals of the Hellfire II tournament. In the finals he went up against XWA Legend, and world renowned Miss Perfect Latrisha Lamarte. Ash went on to upset her and win the Hellfire II tournament. Riding an outstanding winning streak, Ash Bombay then went up against Chris Bomber and his 6 month championship reign in the main event of the Hellfire PPV. In match of the year candidate, Ash Bombay upset Chris Bomber and became the XWA World Champion. Ash was also named XWA's 2005 Wrestler of the Year, Newcomer of the Year, was in the Match of the Year, and headlined the PPV of the Year for XWA. Ash Bombay being on top of the world couldn't have been any happier. However, it didn't last long, as upon much controversy, XWA was put on a hiatus. Now, for months Ash Bombay has been searching for a new home. But then XWA came back and Ash quickly continued where he left off, by winning his second XWA World Heavyweight Championship in less then a month by defeating Kris Keebler and the Excitor. Ash was once again on top of the world with Whitecloud, Maroon, and Corey Rameriez at his side. But history repeated itself again, as once again XWA closed it's doors before Ash had a chance to defend the title. Ash then joined another wrestling promotion back in October of 2006, ICE. He along with Avarice Inc. *Pierce Ward, Tre Crawford, Alexander Fayt, Ash Bombay* shot through the ranks of ICE at a fast rate, defeating everyone in his path, until he met Tsukino Meiou in the semi finals of the Road 2 Glory tournament. He lost in controversial fashion, however it has never been investigated exactly why that was. Ash took that loss bitterly and toiled around ICE for a couple of months even making his hate known for Jake Matthews public, only to have that die as Matthews joined the company, then quickly left before Bombay and Matthews could meet in the ring. Then finally at Clash of the Champions in March of 2007 he wrestled Donovan Midas for the ICE Frontier Championship. Ash went on to win the Frontier Championship that night, and went to wrestle the other champions the promotion had to offer. He had the match in hand until Steven "Tempest" Henri, who Ash injured a month prior at Cyberslam III, made his return, and attacked Ash, allowing Kayleigh Jameson to pin Shane Alexander for the win. Ash Bombay finally broke through with ICE Championship gold, and reformed Avarice Inc. *Ash Bombay, Pierce Ward, El Jefe Bronco, "Profit" Mike Williams, Trigger Happy SMD*. The word around the promotion is it's only a matter of time before Ash Bombay is atop of that mountain reigning as the ICE Universal Champion, only thing holding him back right now is politics, and people protecting certain people. Since that happened, Ash Bombay decided to leave ICE. Ash Bombay, the warrior he is also concurrently wrestling in the promotion PDW at the same time as being in ICE. Bombay started off fast with the company, as he quickly formed an alliance with Kurupt Loc, and Kindred London, and were known was Explicit Content. Ash Bombay lost his first match when he and Kurupt Loc lost to Michael Mercer and Mike Ryba. Things haven't gone right for Ash much since, EC, quickly fizzled out and broke up. Ash Bombay and Magnificent then formed an alliance. Since then Ash Bombay has proven that he can wrestle when he wants to show up or not. He has beaten Jack Mehoff for the PDW Notorious Destruction Championship, and then successfully defended it over his most hated rival, Jake Matthews, which is definitely one of Bombay's biggest win to date. Now with Ash getting a win over Matthews, the sky is the limit for Ash Bombay, as he already was quickly putting a masterminded plan in motion for PDW. However, management and Ash butted heads quite to often and Ash Bombay was screwed harder then a "Hart" in Montral. Now Ash Bombay begins a new chapter, one that brings him to GCW.
