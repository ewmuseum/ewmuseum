Hall of Fame

26 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
                   


Khan Khan is the first inductee to the Hall of Fame. For quite some time, Khan dominated the ranks of every roster he graced, amazing fans with his high flying tactics and brutal offense. One of the greatest athletes of all time, Khan held the United States Title on four occasions throughout his career. Due to nagging injuries resulting from his high-impact style, Khan was forced to retire at a young age. His memory will live forever.


Chameleon/Demented Chameleon survived several character changes in his career. After finally finding something that worked, Chameleon switched between Chameleon and the character Demented. Both were insane. His entertaining material and bizzare personality made him one of the greatest cruiserweights on all time. He held the cruiserweight and tag titles.

The Jhub The Jhub's cynical, sarcastic persona will allow him to go down in history as one of the greatest characters ever. Despite never reaching his full potential, the Jhub will forever remain one of the funniest, most entertaining roleplayers ever seen. He held the United States and World Titles in his career.


Zippy Zippy's unique sense of humor and childish innocence will be his legacy. He never failed to entertain, despite seeming to have the IQ of a three year old. Zippy was an unorthodox wrestler, whose unique style and bizarre persona was one of the best in GCW. Zippy held the TV title twice in his career.
