Micah St. Clair Biography Real Name Micah St. Clair Nicknames The Unfortunate Son, The Heartless Angel Hometown Toronto, Ontario, Canada Birthday October 17th, 1987 Height 6'1 Weight 215 Biography Micah St. Clair is the son of former FWA World Champion Stu St. Clair and FWA Hall of Famer Jenny Ignito. The 'Unfortunate Son' of the two biggest heels in the company's history struggles to deal with the darkness inside him...even with the bright future ahead of him. Will he ever be rid of his family's sins? Other Feds As of right now, I'm also in the FWA...but I've been Rp'ing for...14 years. Wrestling Style Technical High Flyer with a solid base of Catch-As-Catch-Can Wrestling. Finisher Move The Fall From Grace Finisher Description http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Nf61fyVLJU&feature=related WW Entrance An eerie darkness envelops the arena. As it does, multicolored UV lights shimmer from underneath the entrance stage. As the soft feedback from the Deftones’ “Diamond Eyes” plays, we hear the famous line from the movie ‘Hellraiser’… “Welcome…to Oblivion. Amen.” As soon as that line ends, “Diamond Eyes” begins in earnest. The flashing UV lights seem to spotlight on a figure squatting over them, his face covered by his interlocking fingers. The lights seem to have tinges of random colors, each of those colors causing the figure’s fingernails to glow those colors. As the song reaches it refrain, “Time will see us realign…diamonds reign across the sky. Shower me into the same…realm.” The figure stands, revealing Micah St. Clair and his multicolored ‘diamond’ eyes. Ring Announcer: From Toronto, Ontario, Canada...weighing in at 215 pounds...he is YOUR HEARTLESS ANGEL....MICAH ST. CLAIR! He walks out of the UV lights as his name is announced and the house lights come up. As he leaves, his eyes and nails go back to his natural color. As the song continues, Micah looks around at the massive crowds…some cheering for the second generation saints…some booing the attitude of the newcomers. Micah enters the ring and sits on the mat against the far turnbuckle, staring those ‘diamond eyes’ into his opponent… Special Moves See Below...
