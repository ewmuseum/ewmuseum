The Foundation Biography Fed Titles None to Date Fed Awards None to Date Biography The LeClere Boys both have led a life preparing them for a career in wrestling; be it collegiate, Olympic, or professional. Their father was an All-American at Iowa University, and their grandfather was the wrestling captain at Iowa State University. Harvey was the first to show promise, being the older of the two, and took to the mat in fifth grade. His brother, Clark, who was a little bit bigger followed in his brother’s footsteps by entering a wrestling clinic when he was 10. The two excelled in the craft of Greco-Roman, Shoot, and Freestyle wrestling. The two soon became the face of Iowa University’s deep recruit talent, as both were part of a “package signing”; Harvey would graduate and then enroll at Iowa U. right out of high-school, and then Clark would graduate early and be admitted into Iowa U. a year ahead. Their reputation on the mat was spreading as they both entered college; both gaining nicknames from local and regional media reporters, “Slick and Smooth”. Slick, Harvey, was called the best pure talent in a wrestler since Rulon Gardner and his brother was compared to a younger Kurt Angle-like wrestler. People began to think these two boys could come home with gold like the Hazewinkel brothers from 1968, or '72. After dominating upon the top collegians, the LeCleres were staring face to face with a life changing decision. They had become high school legends, college kings, and now were one step away from representing America in the Olympics. Clark was to be the top contender for Greco-Roman, and Harvey would take Freestyle in his sights. As expected, both were nominated to go and compete. After the brothers both wrestled each of their first round qualifiers, they were enjoying some down time as three Olympic committee officers approached the men and arrested them on assault and battery charges being pressed by a fellow teammate, who was not as talented as them. The LeClere Boys were disbarred from Olympic competition for life, and no real investigation was done. This is believed to have been a simple bribe to the committee from the accusing party, Julius Ringold, the well known son of a real estate tycoon and USA Olympic wrestler. The LeClere Boys now look down a new path. A path that many have traveled, but few have dominated. They believe that with their wits, skill, teamwork, and unrelenting passion for excellence, they can become what some call a great tag-team in a generation where tag-team wrestling is dead. The LeClere's hope to be what some call 'legends'. By building their dynasty, they in turn hope to create what the LeClere's call? A legacy.
