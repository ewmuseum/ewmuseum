Dr. John Danner Biography Real Name John Danner Nicknames Dr. Danner; The Doctor; The Shrink Hometown Manchester, England Birthday 10/10/1982 Height 6'8 Weight 297lbs Biography John Danner was born in Manchester, England and began taking an active interest in wrestling during his teens, visiting them gym to bulk his size and refine his wrestling techniques. While studying at university he began wrestling on the British independent scene with life-long friend Jay Jordan and his sister, Missy Jordan. The two wrestled as a tag team for a few years quite successfully - their success attributed to their two different wrestling styles; Danner's power game and, due to having a much smaller in size, Jordan's speed and high flying ability. Once Danner graduated, the three were scouted by Evolution Pro Wrestling and signed a deal to compete in the tag team division with Missy Jordan as their valet. Danner and Jordan moved to the United States and debuted as Drunk Wrestlers Inc. (DWI), known for their comical show appearances and their love for beer and liquor, even handing out beers to the fans from a portable cooler on their way to the ring. Within a couple of months of signing for EPW, DWI won the EPW Tag Team Championship. However, EPW experienced difficulties and closed its doors soon after, leaving DWI as the final EPW Tag Team Champions after a single successful title defence. Now out of work, Danner and Jordan returned to the independent circuit but with little success. After Missy Jordan had returned to the UK, Jay Jordan began drinking heavily, a habit that eventually killed Jordan in a car accident when driving under the influence. Following the death of his friend, Danner decided to retire from wrestling and focus on his education again, becoming a doctor of psychology. During his studying, Danner had begun to suffer from black outs and would come round with bruises and aches. Danner was buying coffee when a young man came up to him and congratulated him on his recent wrestling match, which struck Danner as odd as he hadn't wrestled in some years. It was soon after that Danner received a phone call asking to appear on a local house show due to his recent performances. Danner visited a doctor and explained what had happened and, after some referrals and extensive investigation, Danner was diagnosed with Dissociative Personality Disorder - more commonly referred to as split personalities. Danner decided to take advantage of the success his alter ego had gained and took up wrestling once again. "One more effort and I have my education to fall back on should I fail once more." It was not long before Danner was scouted by Global Championship Wrestling. The extent of Dr. John Danner's dissociative personalty disorder is not known, and it is not known how many personalities he possesses.
