GCW — Card

WorldWide 9, 20 April 2005

20 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The lights dim in the arena, and the German crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on several signs written in English, such as “Hanson = Sellout” and “Hardy 4 PM.” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Berlin, Germany” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan- GCW continues its European invasion as we blaze the trail to Warpath! We’re live for WorldWide here tonight in Berlin, Germany, and we’ve got over twenty thousand here to see Global Championship Wrestling! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan at ringside once again with Mr. Danger! And Danger, last week we were all shocked as Luke Hanson turned his back on his long time friend The Jhub and stood side by side with Octavian!

Danger- Surprising to you, JB, but not to me! Hanson is a smart man, and he’s got the potential to dominate in this business! But he realized he needed the right allies to get it done, and what better ally could he choose than the next GCW World Heavyweight Champion, Octavian?

Bryan- Octavian issued the challenge last week to Jason Dread for a one on one match for the GCW title at Warpath, and Dread didn’t hesitate to accept! Jason Dread and Octavian will be headlining our first Pay-Per-View event at the end of the month, as GCW returns to the states for Warpath in Houston, Texas! But we’re here in Berlin for another edition of WorldWide, and we’ve got a packed lineup.

Danger- You’ve got that right, JB! We’re going to see a huge match in tonight’s main event, as new teammates Luke Hanson and Octavian go up against the Hardcore and World champions, The Jhub and Jason Dread! And, of course, the United States title is on the line as LeStatt Knight takes on hot new prospect Trey Tremendous!

Bryan- Mike Hardy will be back in action as well, but perhaps biggest of all is the return of Toxic! Toxic, who was suspended by President Caldera last week for his interference in ACWF Six, will be back to address the crowd here momentarily! Plus, don’t forget President Caldera himself is back, although in a somewhat… limited capacity.

Danger- I saw him earlier today, JB, and he arrived in a wheelchair! It seems Toxic did quite a bit of damage to Caldera’s knees, and he had surgery last week to repair some torn cartilage! He’s going to be stuck in his office back there tonight, so once again it seems like the only authority in GCW won’t be around to keep things in line!

Bryan- You sound happy about that…

Danger- What can I say? Anarchy equals ratings.

The crowd rise to their feet in surprise and suspense as "O' Fortuna" begins to play in the speaker system filling the ears of the fans in attendance. After a few short moments of the music playing LeStatt Knight, clad in a black suit and the United States Championship over his right shoulder walks from behind the curtains. The fans begins cheering loudly for the superstar who has thus far been perhaps the most formidable superstar in GCW.

Bryan- And we’re going to kick off the show tonight with a few words from the United States Champion! Possibly the most dominant wrestler in professional wrestling today has been a man of few words since winning the US title two months ago!

Danger- Without a doubt, LeStatt Knight is an amazing wrestler and the greatest United States Champion we’ve ever seen! This man has been simply unbeatable since winning that title, but we’re still not sure just what exactly this man is about.

Slowly he walks down the ramp, as always calm and collected. He smiles slightly as he passes the fans but he doesn't reach out to them. He gets to the apron and slides the U.S. Title under the bottom rope before climbing up and into the ring himself. He walks over as the time keeper hands him a microphone. Knight walks to the center of the ring, laying the U.S. Title loosely on his shoulder again. The crowd only takes a few moments to settle back down, obviously interested in this fairly silent superstar. LeStatt brings the microphone to his lips, moistening them with his tongue before speaking.

LeStatt- Fans of Global Championship Wrestling, I stand here tonight for the first time EVER to address things that concern this company. I have seen many things happen in my years as a part of GCW but never have I felt compelled to comment on them. I have sat back and watched others make fools of themselves with their pointless commentary, but tonight it's my turn to lay it all down.

A few weeks ago you all witnessed me defeat Michael Hardy and Natural Born Killer to capture this U.S. Championship. he motions towards the title that hangs from his shoulder It was an incredible night for me as I had been looked over, around, and through for the better part of my stay here, dating back from when I first arrived as a young and ignorant "Khris Krowe" to my U.S. Title bout with Tristain mayhem and all those useless matches with Capo and Dan Rivero. Even when I did capture a title I was stripped of it eleven days later.

Now I promised myself that I would not allow this to continue. When I agreed with Steven Caldera to return I knew that he more than likely thought I was the same man that walked out those years ago. I was out to prove something to the GCW. Not just Caldera, You fans, or people like Octavian, Jhubin, Jason Dread, or Toxic, but to myself as well. I was out to prove that I could reach their status and beyond. So when I won that U.S. Title tournament I knew it was my golden opportunity to prove that Titles in GCW still had prestige.

I vowed that I would be the fightingest champion ever to grace the GCW. I was going to make this Title mean more than it ever had by putting it on the line against any and all challengers. So far I have turned down no one and I don't plan to start now. Week in and week out I have went against the odds and came out victorious.

Hell ONE week after I suffered from a near fatal heart attack, I walked out at ACWF and defeated Luke Hanson and Michael Stevens. It has been put in magazines that regardless of when my reign ends, I should go down as the greatest U.S. Champion in GCW history. While I am flattered by the claim I will leave that decision up to you ... the fans.

What I will say is that I was simply tired of watching these titles mean nothing. I was sick of watching people like Tristain Mayhem, Octavian, hell even the older incarnations of The Jhub and Jason Dread use these Championships as some booster seat to bigger things. They were more like a 'pretty' watch or a new set of earrings than anything else. They weren't champions, they were pretenders.

I thought going into ACWF that the world would have to suffer once again through a petty reign. It looked as if Octavian was going to take it home once again, and that the spoiled punk would be shoved down our throats again. It wasn't until the final hour that fate seemed to step in, and we have finally seemed to get a worthy champion ...

Jason Dread.

The fans erupt at the mention of the name and Knight simply nods in agreement.

If Dread follows through with the promises he made to me last week then yes, he deserves your praise. IF he can become the man that he is fighting to be, then yes he is a deserving champion. IF ... that's the key word here. As I told him and I will say it once more here in front of the world. IF Dread fails to run with the ball, I will come for him and take it from his fingers and take it to where it's never gone befo...

LeStatt's words are cut short as "Might of Rome" begins playing through the arena. Knight's face goes to one of annoyance as he places his hands on his hips and stares at the entrance awaiting the inevitable. The duo of Octavian and the massive Luke Hanson appear on stage, much to the anger of the German crowd. Octavian wears an elegant gray suit, black tie and his Imperial Roman Championship Belt. Hanson is wearing jeans, Docs, a white dress shirt, untucked with the sleeves rolled up, and a black tie. His kendo stick rests on his shoulder, held loosely in his right hand. The two just smile and continue to antagonize the jeering crowd with gestures and insults. Both have mics, and Octavian begins to speak as they get to ringside.

Bryan- Well, you knew it was just a matter of time before these two got involved…

Danger- This is going to get real interesting! What does Octavian want with the US champ?

Octavian- I look into the ring right now and I see the most dominant United States Champion of all time. I see an incredible force, a veritable monster! I see LeStatt Knight, perhaps the greatest champion in GCW today. What do you see, LeStatt, as you look back up at me? You should see a man who stands next to the biggest, meanest, most powerful man in wrestling. You should see a man who carries all the power in GCW. And you should see the only man to have had a greater title reign than yours… The most dominant World Heavyweight Champion of all time!

The crowd boos furiously, as LeStatt looks up the ramp at the duo, a blank expression occupying his features from beneath his cascading black hair.

Octavian- But that’s not to say I don’t respect what you’ve done. The only thing I can’t stand is that you would give your praise to a champion like Jason Dread. He has done nothing to earn your respect, LeStatt. You have accomplished something that has yet to be matched in this new generation of GCW. And that makes you a greater champion than him. And yet you still find yourself playing second fiddle, you still find yourself subordinate to this man. Why is that? Why do you think that is, LeStatt? Well, look up here at the two men on this stage. Standing before you are the most powerful men in wrestling today. It’s because you don’t have friends like us, LeStatt, that you’re destined to wallow in matches against Trey Tremendous and Johnny Lexicon, rather than competing in the main event like me.

More boos come raining down from the crowd, but Octavian ignores them and presses on.

Octavian- I come to you tonight with an offer, LeStatt. An offer that you simply cannot refuse. I’m giving you the chance to, for the first time in your life, make the right allies, to choose a side and take control of your destiny. Tonight, LeStatt, you will see that I have a plan to take GCW by storm, to make my allies stronger and to get back what’s rightfully mine… The GCW World Heavyweight Championship!

The crowd boos as Octavian and Hanson begin to approach the ring. The crowd boos mightily as they head for LeStatt, who takes a protective step backwards.

Bryan- Uh-oh, they’re headed for the ring now, and this thing could get dangerous.

Danger- Why? They’re here to help LeStatt!

The two step into the ring, and Octavian remains in the corner. Hanson smiles and looks right at LeStatt as the crowd buzzes warily.

Hanson- LeStatt, I'll be perfectly honest. As much as I'd like to spend my time and energy telling you what to do, there's something else that needs to be said…

…Jhub.

The crowds reaction immediately shifts from anger to absolute joy. Hanson's face goes from cocky smile to half-sneer, half-contempt, before returning to the smile.

Hanson- Yeah. Yeah, that's right, the epitome of the GCW fan. Cheering what is or is soon to be DEAD. Jason Dread? Gone. LeStatt Knight, here, if he doesn't wise up? Gone. And the Jhub? Old, far passed his prime, and apparently scared shi*less for his lack of presence over the past week?

Hanson leers at the kendo stick in his right hand.

Hanson- Without a doubt, gone.

More boos ensue from the crowd.

Hanson- So where you been, buddy? I threw the gauntlet down. I was sure you'd be chomping at the bit to get a piece after last week. Instead you're off somewhere, what, drinking warm milk, playing shuffleboard, and hitting the sack at 6 PM? I don't know. But by all means, buddy, come on down and give me some kind of an answer...or at least let these simple-minded buffoons get a look at your face so they can feel good about paying half their week's salary and two hours of their life to GCW. Let's see that Botoxed ole mug.

The crowd explodes with boos, as Hanson turns his attention back to the ring.

Hanson- Now, LeStatt, let me tell you how it is, whether you want to hear it or not. We are the force to be reckoned with in GCW. We're extending the olive branch to you to be with us. Let me assure you that it would be in your best interest to do so. We both know that between me and Gaius here, we could...well, use your imagination.

The crowd boos mightily, but they’re cut off when Just Got Wicked cues up! The crowd explodes with cheers as the GCW World Champion emerges on stage! Dread, clad in black jeans and wearing his GCW title belt around his waist, heads towards the ring with a serious looks on his face.

Bryan- Here he comes! The GCW World Heavyweight Champion is here, and this crowd has exploded! And this situation is about to get volatile!

Danger- Oh, man, this is gonna be good!

Dread steps into the ring and walks past Octavian and Hanson, who hold their ground in the front right corner of the ring. Dread is handed a microphone and takes his position across the ring from his foes, with Knight stuck right in the middle.

Jason Dread – LeStatt, what in the hell is wrong with you?! Are you honestly considering some sort of alliance with these two...has beens? It is blatantly obvious that the two of them can't make it well enough on their own, so they have to form this corrosive cohesive unit...why do you need to be a part of it?

Off to the side, Octavian takes a step at Dread, but Hanson stops him mid-stride, holding him back and avoiding the confrontation.

Jason Dread – You, along with everyone else in this arena knows you are much better than that. You've come into your own as of late...why jeopardize that by being associated with these two clowns?!

Hanson, obviously aggravated by the comment, let's go of Octavian. Both stare directly at Dread, whose body is turned towards LeStatt and away from the duo.

Jason Dread – I like to think of you as a man I respect, LeStatt...that's something I don't say about too many people. Not only do I want that to continue, but I want to see you someday standing right across from me in a match which will be heralded as the best GCW has seen to date...

...not to be as washed up as both of these two jackasses-

Octavian instantly charges, hitting Dread with a hard right. Dread fires back as the crowd explodes, and Dread begins hammering Octavian with fists! Octavian manages to pull Dread forward, where Hanson is waiting with a huge boot to the face! Dread goes down and Octavian and Hanson begin stomping him viciously, while Knight simply stands in the center of the ring, watching blankly.

Bryan- Oh, man, a huge boot from Hanson and Dread is in trouble! And look, his ‘friend’ LeStatt Knight isn’t moving an inch to help him!

Danger- Maybe Octavian’s sales pitch worked, JB!

The crowd begins chanting for LeStatt, urging him to help Dread, but Knight remains motionless. Dread reaches out to LeStatt in pain, as Octavian and Hanson continue to assault him. Finally, cheers erupt in the arena as The Jhub appears on stage! The Jhub rushes to the ring and slides under the ropes, going right for Octavian!

Bryan- The Jhub! The Hardcore Champion is here!

The Jhub takes Octavian down with a hard shot to the back, and begins nailing Hanson with hard rights. Hanson tries to fire back but The Jhub is on fire, hitting him with all his might. The Jhub backs Hanson into the corner, right past LeStatt, who seems extremely torn about the situation. The Jhub nails Hanson with hard boots as Dread gets to his feet and goes after Octavian!

Bryan- All hell has broken loose here, folks, and LeStatt Knight hasn’t chosen a side! What’s he thinking?!

Suddenly, a large figure slides into the ring and blows by Knight! Knight watches on in confusion as the new presence in the ring steps up behind The Jhub and spins him around! The crowd grows silent as he kicks The Jhub in the gut, doubles him over, and instantly drops him with a vile piledriver! The Jhub lands hard and instantly reaches for his neck, as the new figure rushes to Dread. Dread sees The Jhub on the mat and charges, but the man steps behind him and drops him to the mat with a double leg takedown! The man instantly slaps on a guillotine-type submission hold, and Dread begins screaming in pain!

Bryan- What the hell?! Who is that?!

Danger- I know exactly who that is, JB!

Octavian and Hanson manage to recover and begin stomping away at The Jhub. The crowd boos monstrously and begins chanting for LeStatt again, though the US champ remains frozen in the corner.

Danger- Ladies and gentlemen, meet Alex Blaq! Octavian’s newest mercenary!

Bryan- Well, whoever this guy is he’s obviously in Octavian’s camp! How the hell do you know so much about this guy?

Danger- I know things, JB!

Octavian breaks off and lets Hanson continue to punish The Jhub, and he drops to a knee next to Dread. Blaq pulls back hard on Dread as Octavian screams in his face, slapping the world champion with contempt. Finally, Octavian nods and Blaq releases the hold. The crowd explodes with boos as Octavian smiles at Blaq, before extending his hand. Blaq shakes it and “Might of Rome” cues up again. The boos intensify as Octavian turns to LeStatt and shouts “Think about it!” before the trio turns to leave.

Bryan- My God, what a heinous assault by Octavian, Hanson and this Alex Blaq on The Jhub and Jason Dread! They meet in tonight’s main event, but the damage may have already been done! And LeStatt Knight just watched the whole thing go down, and didn’t move a muscle!

Danger- Tonight is going to be a historic night, JB! And after this evening, I GUARANTEE you there will be another member in Octavian’s empire!

Khan vs. Niklas Bauer

We return from commercial with Niklas Bauer in the ring, spouting off his German rhetoric, to the annoyance of the fans. Since no one knows what he’s saying, it’s absolutely pointless to translate, but, I think he was ordering a Quarter Pounder with Cheese, a Vanilla Shake, and a Large Fry. But, I’m not entirely sure.

Regardless, Bauer kept talking, and the words “challenge anyone in the back to a fight” came out of his mouth, only in German. At least, that’s what someone in the back thought.

I dont care nothin about it…

I dont care nothin about it…

Debonair.

Dope.

Only means one thing, kids.

Khan was in the hizzy, fo’ rizzy. And the crowd didn’t like it at all.

Somewhere, Jim Ross sat straight up, and like a man possessed screamed “KHAN! KHAN! KHAN! KHAN! HE’S BACK! KHAN! KHAN! KHAN!” Thankfully, Jerry Lawler slapped him across the face.

The platinum haired warrior walked to the ring, and stepped in with reckless abandon. Never mind he was giving up at least 100lbs to Bauer. His face showed no reaction as he entered the ring, and stared across the ring at the German strongman.

Quickly, the bell rang, and Bauer went to work. He darted at Khan, who deftly sidestepped the attack, and put a kick to Bauer’s side. This angered the German, and again, he turned, and went after Khan, earning another kick to the side of the knee.

Bauer fell to one knee, and Khan went ballistic.

Kick to the head.

Kick to the neck.

Kick to the chest.

Kick to the head. Again. And again. And again.

The final kick caught Bauer across the nose, and he screamed out in pain, as the sickening crack was heard through the arena. Khan just shattered his face.

Blood ran from Bauer’s nostrils as the referee looked at the injury. The referee went to call for the bell, but caught a kick to the face from Khan for his troubles. The crowd was bewildered as Khan lifted Bauer off the mat, and pushed his face into the turnbuckle. Screams were muffled as Khan smiled at the misery he was causing.

Khan finally let go, and the turnbuckle was stained red. Bauer fell to the mat, bleeding all over the ring when Khan motioned backstage.

The Dark Messiah walked the ramp at this cue, and the fans booed like crazy. He held a bag in his left hand, as he walked to the ring, a sick smile on his face. He entered the ring, and placed the bag in a corner. Khan lifted Bauer off the mat, and TDM climbed to the top turnbuckle. Khan placed Bauer on his shoulders, and TDM grabbed him by the head, dead lifting him off Khan, and turning him around.

A few referees attempted to intervene, and were chased off by Khan as TDM leaped from the top, with Bauer between his legs, upside down.

That’s right. Fall From Grace. From the top rope.

In other words: OUCH!

Bauer’s head spiked the mat, as his lifeless body fell to the ground, TDM stood up, and looked over the crowd. He pointed at Khan, and then at the bag he’d brought to the ring.

Khan smiled and obeyed, handing the small bag to TDM, who unzipped it, and reached inside.

Once again, TDM pulled out a blowtorch.

Once again, the fans in the arena were sickened.

And once again, GCW cut it’s feed until TDM could be removed from the ring.

Johnathan Borealis is walking in the backstage area, dressed in black jeans, Converse shoes, and a blue tee-shirt. As he walks past a corridor, Jordan White can be seen standing behind him, and he calls out to him.

Jordan - Hey Johnny-boy. Been a while.

Borealis turns and faces Jordan White.

Borealis - Jordan.

Jordan - What are you doing here? You don't have a match tonight. You haven't had a match yet, if I recall.

Borealis - What are you doing here? You're a talentless uncharismatic hack.

Jordan - I'm managing Trey Tremendous.

Borealis - Really? In that case, I don't know which one of you is more screwed.

There's a few seconds of tenseness as Jordan White and Borealis stare at each other, the false smiles having left their faces.

Jordan - I was hoping you'd leave petty rivalries with a federation that died.

Borealis looks as though he's about to say something for a moment, but simply walks past Jordan and off camera.

Borealis - Funny. I was about to say that I had hoped you died.

The two glare at each other before the scene fades into the back where Mike Hardy is doing some pushups in what appears to be his locker room. The sweat pours down off his face, as if he’s been at it for some time. After a moment there’s a knock on the door.

MH: Yeah, it’s open…

The door slowly glides open and Michael Stevens steps into the room, with a big smile plastered across his face. He shuts the door behind him and looks down.

MSS: Mike, how you doin’ buddy?

Hardy gives Stevens a suspicious glance, but responds friendly enough.

MH: I’m alright, stayin’ fresh with my time off.

MSS: Glad to hear it man, glad to hear it, enjoying the Hayabusa?

MK: That bike is the real me…I love it.

MSS: Yeah I thought you would man. So listen Mike, mind if we talk for a second?

MK: I guess so.

Stevens takes a seat on a nearby workout bench, once seated he tosses Hardy a towel to mop off with.

MSS: Listen buddy, I know I told you last week, I wasn’t going to pressure you, and I’m still not planning on it, I just thought I’d give you some free advice. They’ve been talking about Toxic all right Mike, and all he’s doing is going out to the ring to talk for a minute. He’s the guy getting the spotlight while you’re the guy who’s supposed to be the future of this company. How does that make you feel?

MK: Doesn’t bother me all that much….I guess.

MSS: Listen buddy, it shouldn’t make you feel good at all. Toxic’s just gonna head out there and spout off about how great he is, for no other reason than he beat your mentor at ACWF. But you know what Mike, you’ve beaten Toxic, cleanly, twice, that’s something not a lot of people can say. He’s one of the best in the business and twice now, in the center of the ring you’ve beaten him. That says a lot for you Mike, and even if it doesn’t mean anything to you, I’m really proud.

But the thing that gets me Mike, is that you’re not the one out there. It should be you in the spotlight, not Toxic. See Mike, you can’t sit around and wait for the spotlight to find you, you’ve got to reach out and take it whenever you can. It seems to me like your opportunity should be tonight. You’ve got a guy you KNOW you can beat, spouting off about him, trying to make himself famous, and you can stop him. Tonight’s your glory night man, you can go out there look Toxic right in the eye, and tell him NO, he’s not the future of the company, Mike Hardy is the future of the company. Grab the brass ring kid, you know it’s what Steven would want you to do, and since he obviously doesn’t care enough to be here for you right now, I’m just trying to tell you what he would. You’ve got all the tools kid, and if you do this tonight, I promise you, you’ll be a star.

But hey, I’ve gotta go, my match with Twisted is up pretty soon and I gotta get ready. Just think about what I said Mike, you’re almost to that brass ring, you’ve just gotta reach out and grab it.

Stevens stands, pats Hardy on the shoulder, before heading out towards the door, leaving the kid to think. As he’s walking out however we just get a glimpse of his trademark smirk, as he seems to chuckle at some inner joke. Hardy then tosses his towel into the corner of his room and heads to the ring for his match.

The newly crowned GCW World Champion Jason Dread, enters his private locker room, and places his GCW World Championship title on a table. He turns his back on it for just a moment, while opening a small refrigerator, pulling out a bottle of water, and taking a large swallow from it. By the expression on his face, it's easy to tell he is suffering some pain at the hands of the beating he'd received moments before. Dread smacks his lips, and lets out a sigh.

But, Dread wasn't alone.

TDM – Good water, eh?

Dread whips around, and sees Peter Maxwell behind him, smiling.

Dread – Oh. It's just you. For a minute there, I thought I had something to worry about.

TDM smiles at the verbal jab from Dread. The two have been enemies since their entrance into GCW over four years ago, and there's obviously no love lost between the two.

TDM – Come on now, Jason. Is that a way to treat someone who stopped by to offer his congrats?

Dread – I suppose not. But, since when is a visit from you simply for the purpose of giving someone, especially me, a pat on the back?

Dread pauses for a moment, surveying TDM.

Dread - Where's your bitch?

TDM shrugged and smiled again.

TDM – Elsewhere, taking care of an unfortunate incident we had nothing to do with.

Dread – You mean the disappearance of NBK?

TDM – You call it a disappearance. I say NBK's too embarrassed after the beating he took at ACWF to show his face around here ever again. I certainly didn't do anything to him.

Dread – Right. Because you never do anything.

The smile started to fade from TDM's face, but, he hid it well. Contempt grew in his glare at Dread, as he reached down beside him, and picked up Dread's Championship belt.

TDM – Jason Dread, GCW world champion. This is what, the second time?

Dread – It is.

TDM – Wow. It's amazing what can happen. I mean, here you are, carrying fifteen pounds of gold to the ring, and there I am, sadly, sans a belt.

Dread scrunched his face up, and shrugged. A sarcastic tone overtakes his voice.

Dread – I suppose if you decided to actually wrestle, and not worry about trying to shock Caldera into firing your ass, you might actually be considered for a title.

TDM smiled an obviously fake smile.

TDM – Perhaps.

Dread – And yet, there you are. The King of Shock and Awe. You know why no one will get into the ring with you, Maxie?

TDM shrugged.

TDM – I don't really care.

Dread – Get over yourself, Maxie. Seriously. You do care. You know it absolutely kills you that Mike F’ing Hardy of all people is in the main event, and not you. Ninety percent of the fans out there know you're better than that. And yet, there you are, beating up Niklas Bauer. I mean, Niklas Bauer. Let that soak into your head, Maxwell. There isn't a GCW World Champion you've EVER lost to, and you're damn near killing a curtain jerker.

He pauses, then annunciates every syllable for the next two words he speaks, adding emphasis:

Dread - NIK-LAUS BAU-ER

TDM – Shut up.

Dread smiled, and continued his rant.

Dread – Maybe if you gave a shit about more than case, you'd actually get yourself some sort of good match. Do you really think Caldera's going to risk one of his stars against you? You're sloppy in the ring…

TDM – I'm warning you…

Dread's smile never wanes, he continues on with the tirade.

Dread – You're not nearly as over as you think you are…

TDM – Shut your face, Jason…

Dread – And the only reason these fans give you any reaction at all is because they know you can do better. They don't care if you're wrestling or not. They hate you because you don't have what it takes anymore.

Taken enough, TDM charged forward, and grabbed Dread around the neck, pushing him against the wall.

TDM – Listen here, and listen well. I'm not going to repeat myself to you, or anyone else. When I want to take that GCW World title, I will, and there won't be a goddamn thing you, Caldera, or anyone else can or will be able to do about it. Fuck these fans, fuck Mike Hardy, and fuck you, Jason.

He snarled, and pushed Dread's head into the wall, leaving a sizeable dent in the drywall. He picked up Dread's Championship and spit on it, leaving a sizeable loogie on the faceplate, before dropping it on the ground.

TDM – That's what I think of your championship, your title, and your reign. If Octavian doesn't take it off you, Dread…

…I will.

Maxwell turned and walked out of the room, leaving Dread grinning with delight, while putting a hand to the back of his head.

Dread – Good...I wouldn't have that any other way.

Mike Hardy vs. Johnny Lexicon

He was loaded too, and just thought it would be a good idea, and it was. He offered me 50/50 financial backing, and with our agreement, I myself would be in total creative control of the new company, so I couldn't refuse.

I found some new blood too. Rookies to the sport to fill out the roster. You might know some of them. Khan, Mike Hardy, Octavian, The Jhub, and one more very unique superstar.

Danger- Well, I first met Steven at an independent show in Columbus, Ohio. I was doing some recruiting, and this guy caught my eye. He was young, he was quick, and he just had that spark. He had only been in the business for a few months, so I decided to offer him a contract. It wasn't a whole lot of money or anything, but it was better than making 50 bucks a night he said. Steven didn't realize his own potential at the time, but I did. He told me he was about to give up when I signed him. He said he just didn't think this was his cup of tea.

Former COD stablemates battling it out. Well, Caldera couldn't believe I wanted him to start a feud Dero. He was nervous as hell, but he went out there and nailed Dero with a chair, and that started the war.

Danger- The plan was to establish Caldera as a serious contender, but still put over the top guys. Well, the fans were reacting so strongly for Caldera, I had no choice but to start building him more and more. The World Title was to be decided in a one night only single elimination tournament at the Deadly Decisions pay-per-view. The plan was for Caldera to fight and fight, only to put Dero over for the title in the final match.

I couldn't believe the reaction he got when he beat The Jhub in the first round of the tournament. It was actually after that match that I changed the plans, and told Dero that Caldera was going to get the belt.

Danger- There were many others in the back that felt that Caldera didn't deserve the title. I mean think about it, a rookie came in there out of nowhere and beat 10 year veterans for the belt. Many were upset, and they gave their notice. Others didn't like the way we did it. I went against the booking committee's advice and gave Caldera the belt anyway. All hell basically broke loose backstage, so my partner and I decided it wasn't worth sorting it all out, so we closed it down.

It wasn't long before the prez of CWU went AWOL, and Caldera was left to run the entire booking operation. He made out unbelievably well considering his limited experience in the business, but I guess some of us are just born to do what we do. Be great. Anyway, after a few months Caldera had some business problems in CWU and he left, and decided he could create his own promotion, and thus GCW was born.

Danger- Yeah we kept in touch, that's about it. Until he called me one day right after he got GCW going and asked if I would like to be a part of it. Well, the rest is history. How was I going to refuse this offer? Get back into the business again under someone else's payroll? At least if it were to fail I wouldn't be taking the fall this time. Hell yeah I was in. Caldera hired me as the commissioner of GCW in March of 2001 and I had a good run. Caldera and I started an on-air rivalry. I loved it, it was the classic teacher versus student story. That lasted for awhile, and after 6-8 months I just got burned out. I took a long time off and Caldera let me know that I was always welcome in GCW.
