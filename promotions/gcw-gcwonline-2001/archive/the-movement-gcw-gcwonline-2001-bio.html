The Movement Biography Federation Titles None Federation Awards None Biography The Movement began as the start of a new wave of talent hired into GCW. Incoming superstar Chris Bagwell decided he didn't like the way things had been ran in the federation and his response was to create a Movement, of sorts, to make things work the way he had wanted. Of course, by doing so with force. He brought in a long-time friend by the name of Zacharia Taylor as his initial member. Shortly after Taylor joined forces with Bagwell, both superstars were entered into the 2008 Rampage Tournament. After powerhousing through the first round, Bagwell announced that his first round opponent, Kiljaden Kincaid, had succombed to Bagwell's power and thus also joined forces with The Movement. It wasn't long after that Bagwell and Taylor were then able to recruit another Rampage Tournament entrant, Depraved. Bagwell and Taylor soon began rallying to recruit another newcomer, Mark Matthews, but the recruitment never became official before Matthews ended up being released. The Movement began climbing the ranks and gaining notoriety. While Chris Bagwell advanced to the finals of the Rampage Tournament and began a very heated rivalry with Andy Murray, Zacharia Taylor went on to defeat the then-reigned Television Champion, Clyde Fox. Taylor toppled his competition in the TV Title division, quickly being named one of the greatest TV Champs the company had ever seen. The Movement grew again at the introduction of two masked superstars. The two masked individuals would come to the ring and assault various opponents of the Movement, never revealing their identity. At the same time, The Movement had created an alliance with The Church of the Unholy (Axel Action and James von Doom). Many began to speculate that von Doom and Axel were, in fact, the two masked men. Rumors really started to fly when The Church assaulted Movement member, Depraved, at the command of Chris Bagwell. Depraved was released from GCW shortly after his release from The Movement. In Depraved's place, Chris Bagwell introduced the Extreme Champion from Hostility Wrestling, Dan "The Bulldozer" Black. At this time, Zacharia Taylor had been slowly drifting away from Bagwell. The two hadn't been seeing eye to eye on many levels. Zacharia's drift from The Movement hadn't been a sour one. In fact, he and Chris remained very close friends. However, that all began to change when Bagwell brought in Black. Black and Taylor went at each other's throats almost immediately. Taylor finally broke free from The Movement when Black made it very clear he'd been gunning for Zacharia's Television Title! As time passed, people began growing unsure as to whether or not The Church of the Unholy were, indeed, the identities of the two masked men. At High Stakes 2008, the identity of one of the masked individuals was finally revealed. In a high-profile match against Andy Murray, Chris Bagwell had brought out one of the masked men as a "bombshell" that he'd planned to drop on Murray that night. Just before the match, the masked man took off his mask to unveil -- a woman! It was Jane Murray, the ex-wife of Andy. This steam gave Bagwell the ability to capture his first win over his rival, Andy Murray, and gain revenge for the Rampage Tournament finals. The Movement remained stationary for a few weeks, with no major activity. Then, just before Warpath 2008, the second masked man was finally unveiled. It was Axel Action! Axel and von Doom had been on the verge of splitting up and Axel had already made a deal with the devil himself -- Chris Bagwell. Bagwell and Axel had been talking for some time about cutting the dead weight that was James von Doom. Von Doom and Axel battled it out at Warpath when Axel finally became victorious, sending von Doom packing for good. Just recently, Dan "The Bulldozer" Black stood up to Chris Bagwell after he had a major outlash at Kiljaden Kincaid. Black declared himself the new leader of The Movement and made it clear that he, Kincaid, and Axel Action have decided to overthrow the original leader, and founder, of the stable. Black has threatened the rest of the wrestling world that The NEW Movement will be a force to be reckoned with and will not fall to pieces like the Bagwell-led Movement did.
