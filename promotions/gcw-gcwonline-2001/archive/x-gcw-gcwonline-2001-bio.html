X Biography Nicknames The Enygmatic One Height 6'1' Weight 215 Biography X is a mystery. There are many people out there that are trying to figure out the background of this enygma. Many people believe that he's a veteran wrestler that suffered from a major facial injury, and that is why he wears a mask. There are people that note the resemblance to luchadore Miguel Rodriguez. There are other people that claim that his use of the moniker of 'The Enygmatic One' proves that he's really 'The Enygmatic One' Eric Rossi. It is unknown if any of these theories are true or not, as X has done everything he can to keep the truth about who he is hidden.
