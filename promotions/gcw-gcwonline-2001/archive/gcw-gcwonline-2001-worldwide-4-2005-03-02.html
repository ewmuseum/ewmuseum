WorldWide 4, 2 March 2005

2 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WorldWide 4 American Airlines Arena — Miami, Florida ---- WorldWide 4 The lights in the American Airlines Arena dim as “Wake Up” begins building. The GCW logo flashes on screen before a tremendous explosion of fireworks erupts on stage! The pyro display continues for a few moments before the house lights come up, and the cameras begin scanning the crowd for signs. ‘Jhub = ACWF’ and ‘LeStatt Knight- US CHAMP!’ The crisp, clear voice of James Bryan appears in the background, enthusiastically welcoming us to WorldWide! Bryan- Hello everyone and welcome back to WorldWide! We’re in the sold out American Airlines Arena in Miami, Florida, and we can certainly feel the Heat at we approach ACWF! Danger- Mr. Danger here, feeling a distinct wave of déjà vu! Weren’t we just here, JB? Bryan- That was the American Airlines Center, this is the American Airlines Arena. Danger- Whatever, I fly Delta. Bryan- Regardless, we have a huge show once again tonight, folks, as President Caldera has promised he will add two more participants to the ACWF match! Last week we saw Twisted and The Jhub earn spots in the world title match, and Jason Dread, Toad, Octavian, and Allied Chaos all have a chance at getting in right here in Miami! Danger- Well, normally I would say that the two man side of a handicap match has the advantage, but when Octavian is given the chance to seize power, he takes it. I feel sorry for the new guys tonight! Bryan- On top of that we have Mike Hardy taking on Toxic… Danger- … I think I saw them bringing in Hardy’s tombstone earlier… Bryan- …And The Jhub has a shot at the United States Title right here tonight! And there have been rumors that there will be yet another big return right here tonight on WorldWide. Danger- Every week it’s someone new, and there are so many big names still floating around out there, you never know who’s gonna show up. Bryan pauses and appears to listen to something coming in through his headset, as Danger turns and poses for the crowd. The fans continue to go nuts as the image of Kevin Jacobs appears on the MegaTron. Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen, I’m afraid there’s been an accident backstage… Let’s send it over to Kevin Jacobs… The scene fades to the backstage area, where a group of men -- it's easy to tell that they're paramedics and trainers of the sort -- are dashing through the halls, yelling for the random person who happens to be backstage to simply move and get out of the way. In the middle of the group of men, a stretcher is being pushed by one of the balding men while the others all clasp on to it, keeping it steady despite the fact that the stretcher, as of now, remains empty. One of the figures is recognizable; the name to go along with the face is Kevin Jacobs, who runs along a side, making sure to stay in the cameras view. He is a little winded, but that doesn't stop him from being that oh-so-darn-good Interviewer he is. Jacobs- We've just received word that referee Hal Jenkins has been struck by a motor vehicle in the parking lot! The pudgy little fella' lags behind a bit, and little balls of sweat begin rolling down his forehead as he gasps for his air, and manages to keep up with the other running men -- a few more hours on that treadmill collecting dust in his mother's basement surely would have made this situation easier for the poor guy. Jacobs- So far... no word has been said of who could have possibly done this to Hal. However, I’ve received word that a major star will be returning here tonight on WorldWide, and they might have had something to do with this! We'll keep you updated as we get the information... The scene fades out with Kevin growing further behind. So, it seems someone has gone and ran over a referee -- isn't that just dandy? The show then brings us back to JB and Danger at ringside. Bryan- Well, what can I say? I certainly hope Jenkins is all right, and I hope that whoever did this gets what’s coming to him. Danger- Who cares about the referee? You heard Jacobs, someone big is here tonight! Once again the cameras head backstage, where the cameras find a grungy looking Mike Hardy sitting on a speaker case. Hardy’s hair is growing out and he obviously hasn’t shaved, and he’s clad in the drab gear consisting of black jeans, black pads and black boots. His blank stare is finally broken as a new figure enters the scene. President Steven Caldera emerges onto the screen as the fans go wild, and he takes a seat next to Hardy. Caldera unbuttons his fine black Armani jacket and clasps his hands together, his gaze aimed away from Hardy. Caldera- Hi Mike. Hardy manages a twisted grin, suggesting half satisfaction and half disdain. Hardy remains silent but appears to listen to Caldera as he speaks. Caldera- Listen, Mike, I know you’ve had a rather rough start these past few weeks. And I know you spent a lot of time in the hospital these past two weeks. I just wanted to let you know that I’m… concerned about your wrestling style lately. I mean, last week you were just throwing yourself around out there without the slightest thought given to the consequences. LeStatt Knight nearly tore you limb from limb, and he could have if he wanted to. I just wanted you to know that I want to help, and I don’t want to see you get yourself hurt. Hardy suddenly springs to his feet with startling agility, and glares back at Caldera with a burning rage in his eyes. Caldera is so startled that he, too, leaps to his feet, taken aback by Hardy’s intensity. Hardy- NOW you’re concerned! Now, after three weeks of losing, and getting my ass kicked for the world to see? My grandmother has spent two weeks crying her damn eyes out because of what she’s seen. It was you that left me alone with The Dark Messiah, it was you that caused this. You didn’t give a damn about me up until now, and now you’re just here because you’re protecting your damn investment. Well, Mr. President, you can go to hell. The crowd gasps in shock, and Caldera is obviously stunned. Hardy does not leave, but maintains his hardened stare on Caldera. Caldera- Mike, I’m sorry, all right? I apologize. I don’t know why The Dark Messiah chose you to beat on, but I’m sorry I didn’t see it coming. I should have been the one in that ring, not you. I know that. But damnit, Mike, wrestling like you have some sort of death wish is not the answer here! Hardy- Then what the hell is? It’s the only way I can seem to get these people to care about me! It’s the only way I got your attention! Caldera- Listen, Mike, you’ve got this… aura about you now. Ever since you were in the ring with The Dark Messiah you’ve been… darker. I’m not saying it’s a bad thing, but I’ve seen what he can do to people, and I don’t want you to follow their path. I want to see your harness this new intensity, you’ve got a great aggression that you can use to your advantage. But you can’t just throw yourself around without a care about what it’s doing to you. You have to be smart about it. You have to learn when it’s time to take a chance and when it’s time to play the odds. You’ve got to learn to be smart! Hardy- Oh, and who’s gonna teach me, you?! Caldera stops for a moment and drops his gaze to the floor. The crowd begins to buzz as Hardy scoffs and turns to leave. Caldera- Yes, yes I will. Hardy stops in his tracks and turns back to Caldera with a skeptical look on his face. Caldera- Yeah, tonight, when you face Toxic, I’ll be in your corner. Congratulations, Mike, I’m your new manager. Without another word Caldera spins on his heel and walks away, leaving Hardy shaking his head. The crowd explodes in cheers as a thin smile slowly creeps over Hardy’s face, and he walks away with a spring in his step as WorldWide cuts to commercial. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Allied Chaos is seen warming up in the locker room area. Mallory sits on a nearby bench dressed in fairly formal evening wear while the two go through a series of stretches and pep talks... Connor- Oh man, this is too easy. Kyle- Dude, I know. I mean check it out. Us, the two greatest athletes in GCW, no no no, sports-entertainment history, taking on one measly little Euro-trash poser. We can't lose! AC gives one of their trademark high fives, but Mallory cuts the premature celebration short... Mallory- Guys, we can't be too confident. I mean Octavian is a storied champion. You guys haven't even darkened the inside of a gym for months. Connor- Hey, hey, hey, I've been using my gazelle glider everyday. Mallory- Connor, you're using it, alright. Using it for a coat rack. Connor sticks out his finger to protest, but then just nods... Kyle- Mal, the fact is, that there's two of us. TWO! Against ONE! Heh, if there were ever better odds than that I haven't seem 'em. Connor- Yeah, yeah. No one in history has ever won a handicap match when the odds were pitted against them, right? Kyle- Its a statistical impossibility! Connor- We are so in to the GCW! While the two continue to boast, the camera zooms in tight to Mallory's face that looks more than a little apprehensive... Again, we find ourselves backstage with the crew of paramedics, trainers and a tussled-hair, sweaty Kevin Jacobs. The crew now stand before a pair of double steel doors. Above them, an EXIT sign hangs proudly. The question that must instantly come to mind is this- If Hal Jenkins has been assaulted, and ran over by a motor vehicle... then why is everyone standing inside the building? Why hasn't those paramedics gotten out of there, got the downed referee and brought him back to the safety -- why, damnit!? Why!? -- of the arena? Jacobs- It's to our understanding that Hal Jenkins is behind these doors... the problem is, I don't think we can safely reach him. For the last few minutes --- As if on cue a loud screeching of tires is heard screaming beyond those doors, and Jacobs goes silent. The screech is loud, and echoed furiously by the structure of the parking lot. Shortly following the screech -- you guessed it -- a crash resounds from the parking lot. Breaking glass, scraping metals, a car alarm rings sounds off -- don't you hate those? -- and then, the sound that doesn't necessarily fit the scenario- a laugh. A high pitched, twisted, maniacal, devious laugh that only seems as if it could come from the Wicked Witch of the West. The laugh, as cliche' as it sounds, is just one of those laughs that sends chills down your spine, and brings forth the goose-flesh on your arms; it's one of those laughs that twist your stomach in to a bulge, grasps your throat and crumbles it in to that uncomfortable lump you get during nervous situations -- one of those laughs that instantly erects those little hairs on the back of your neck. Yes, it's quite the disturbing laugh, and -- wouldn't you know it? -- Kevin Jacobs seems to be displaying all of the afore mentioned symptoms coupled with a pale face. Words need not be said to explain the situation. Hal Jenkins is laying injured in that parking lot, and a madman has infact ran him over -- and the mad man is still in there, in his car, driving around, crashing -- destroying -- into anything he wants damn well feels like. Another crash echoes beyond those steel doors -- was that a popping sound? -- and more wreckage can be heard grinding against the cement. Another car alarm joins in -- and even another. Then, the laugh. Bryan- My, oh, my, we really need to get some help back there for poor Hal Jenkins. Danger- What the hell is going on back there? Someone needs to go back there and give that coward Jacobs a push. Bryan- Well fans, our first match tonight has a berth in the ACWF match on the line. If Allied Chaos manage to win this one, not only do they get GCW contracts, but they’re in the world title match! But if Octavian wins, he finally gets the shot at the world title he thinks he deserves. Set it Off by POD hits, and the crowd rises to get a look at the newest members of GCW, Allied Chaos. Kyle and Connor appear on stage, followed closely by the lovely Mallory. The crowd greets them with a surprisingly loud boom of cheers, which Danger deduces is directed at Mallory, and not Allied Chaos. The duo hit the ring and raise their arms in the air, obviously thinking the reception is theirs. All attention is quickly diverted to the stage, however, as Might of Rome hits. The boos become deafening as Octavian makes his way to the ring wearing his glamorous entrance attire, and of course the Imperial Roman Championship title belt. Octavian reaches the ring and removes the belt, as both members of Allied Chaos stare at it in awe. Octavian begins removing his attire, but turns to the ramp as Just Got Wicked cues up! The crowd explodes in cheers as Jason Dread makes his way to the ring wearing street clothes, and takes a spot at the announce table. Dread and Octavian share a heated glare, but the sound of the bell turns Octavian’s attention back to the ring. Octavian turns right into a perfectly timed double dropkick by Allied Chaos, sending him reeling into the turnbuckle. The crowd cheers in surprise, but rather than following up, Allied Chaos turns and delivers a perfect high-five. The two finish their celebration and turn back to Octavian, who explodes out of the corner with a double clothesline, flooring both men. Octavian pulls Kyle to his feet and nails him with a big European uppercut, sending him all the way over the top rope to the apron. Connor staggers back to his feet and quickly attempts a springboard dropkick, but Octavian counters into a sit-down powerslam! Rather than attempting the pinfall, Octavian turns his attention to Kyle, who is pulling himself back into the ring. Octavian pulls Kyle back to his feet and tosses him all the way across the ring with a belly to belly suplex. He then quickly mounts the turnbuckle and raises his arms into the air, eliciting a tremendous chorus of boos from the fans. However, Connor appears out of nowhere, mounts the ropes behind Octavian, and nails him with a second rope German suplex to an incredible ovation! Octavian lies reeling in the ring as Dread applauds Allied Chaos. Connor struggles back to his feet and helps Kyle back up. Connor actually appears to be bragging about his move to Kyle, who seems unimpressed. Mallory screams to them from the outside to finish the job, and the two finally get back on the offensive. Octavian has pulled himself back up on the ropes, and the two whip him to the other side. Octavian bounces back and ducks a double clothesline attempt, coming back with a big shoulder block on Connor. Kyle fires off a right hand, but Octavian flips him over in position for the Roman Devastator! Octavian drops Kyle right on his head, then quickly turning to Connor. Octavian hooks Connor’s legs and hooks him in the Roman Rapper. Connor immediately taps out, the bell sounds, and Might of Rome hits again. Winner: Octavian Octavian instantly turns his attention to Dread at ringside, holding his Imperial Roman belt high for him to see. The two stare each other down as Allied Chaos retreats to the back, and the show fades to commercial. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Standing in the backstage area is Kevin Jacobs, in all of his balding glory, microphone in hand. He is wearing a GCW Black polo shirt with red trim, and a pair of khaki pants, which are his on his stomach and tightened with a cheap belt. He has a goofy smile on his face. Kevin- Hello fans of the GCW, it has been a dandy of a night here so far and it will only get better. Right now I have the privilege to get an exclusive interview with the NEW United States Champion, LeStatt Knight! The camera pans out, showing that LeStatt is indeed standing right next to Jacobs. He is wearing a black “A-shirt”, and a pair of baggy black sweat pants. His hair is tied neatly back in a pony tail, and a set of sunglasses rest perfectly on his nose. Kevin- LeStatt Knight, last week on World Wide you went into two matches, against very, very established competitors and shocked the world by win… Knight cuts him off. Knight- Wait a damn minute Kevin, who exactly did I shock ? You ? The Fans ? NBK and Mike Hardy ? or maybe you mean Steven Caldera ? Well if these are the people you claim I ‘shocked’ then I don’t give a damn because the only thing that matters is that I did NOT shock ME. I walked into World Wide KNOWING that I am the best thing that this place has to offer, and I have yet to be proven other wise. Kevin- Excuse me LeStatt, I didn’t mean to offend you. Knight- Listen Kevin, I am used to those kinds of remarks. People have been doubting me since I can remember … especially in this company. It is just fine that people will continue to not see me as a top contender either, because then they will fall that much harder. Kevin- Well ACWF VI is coming up quickly LeStatt, what do you think your chances are, especially after becoming the United States Champion, of being entered into the Main Event ? Knight- ( chuckles a bit before answering ) Do you really think that Caldera is willing to enter ME into that match ? Hell no he isn’t, he has too many investments to protect in that match … and even more to come. He doesn’t want to risk them looking bad on my account. It wouldn’t have mattered WHAT I did, I never would be involved in that match just as I never got a fair shot the last go round. Kevin- You sound almost as if you believe there is a biased towards you, LeStatt. Knight- No there seems to be a biased towards anyone who hasn’t decided to kiss Caldera’s ass at one point or another. I have kept my mouth shut for long enough around here, everyone has … it’s time somebody steps up. As for ACWF, like any fighting champion I will put it on the line. Against who ? Well I see that Luke Hanson has some kind of new found interest in me, as if getting tossed off of a Scaffold wasn’t enough for him years ago. That’s just fine with me … if Hanson wasn’t that shot, he can have it, I say sign me up. Kevin- You heard it folks, Knight has accepted a challenge from Luke Hanson for the U.S. Championship ! Now Knight, let’s concentrate on tonight, you have a U.S. Title defense against a legend around these parts, The Jhub. Knight- ( a smile creeps across the lips of Knight ) Do you think it was by accident that this match was made ? Hardly amigo … I WANTED this match tonight. The Jhub is someone who I’ve watched since 2001, and even then I far from impressed. That man is nothing, and tonight I will prove that he is nothing. He has gotten babied and carried throughout his career, a career that I would gladly bring to an end tonight. I’ve heard him remark about how he has done things on his own merit … I almost laughed myself to death. Since when has being in The Establishment or System-X been your own MERIT ? Since when has needing others to do your dirty work, your OWN merit ? The Jhub doesn’t know the meaning of the phrase “My own merit”. Tonight, I will knock Jhub off his high horse … I will teach him a lesson in humility. Kevin: And what does the future hold for LeStatt Knight ? Knight- Listen and listen well GCW, I am not here for games, for laughs, or for friends … I am here to be THE MAN. I will defend this U.S. Title each and every NIGHT. No one will be shot down an opportunity to step inside the ring with me, because I will not be like people like Octavian and Jhub who claim they have beaten the best that there is, and feel they are owed something. I will do what they never could … actually beat EVERYONE. Now since I know I will be excluded from the festivities of ACWF VI, then I have a small challenge to issue out. Soon all six competitors for the World Title will be announced, and I want a shot at them all. Not for their spots in ACWF VI, they can have that … I want to prove a point. So beginning this week with Jhub, I will BEAT every competitor that is involved in that match. Be it Octavian, Tristain Mayhem, Twisted, Toxic, Jason Dread, The Dark messiah … whoever. So Next week for My United States Title I am offering TWO people who are going to be in the ACWF VI match to step up. A Triple threat match … for the U.S. title, who is it going to be ? With that Knight walks off, leaving Kevin Jacobs alone. The show then heads back to ringside, where JB and Danger sit awaiting the next match. Bryan- Well fans, LeStatt Knight hasn’t been very vocal since arriving in GCW, but he just made a hell of a challenge! I’d say that was rather insightful! Danger- Insightful? Hell, he’s ready to take on the world! Mark my words, JB, this man LeStatt Knight is the future of this company! He’s got the skill in the ring, the attitude and the intensity to do anything. It’s only a matter of time. Bryan- Well, he defends his United States title against The Jhub, but right now we’re ready to decide another spot in the ACWF match! Out With the Old hits, and Toad makes his way to ringside with the Hardcore title belt around his waist. The crowd boos Toad heavily as he taunts them on the way down the ramp and slides into the ring. Just Got Wicked hits next and the crowd explodes again for Jason Dread. Dread charges the ring and slides in, immediately exchanging blows with Toad to begin the match. Toad takes over quickly and fires Dread into the ropes, but Dread counters with a flying forearm to send Toad to the mat. Toad rips his title belt off his waist and takes a hard swing at Dread, but Dread ducks and nails Toad with a dropkick to the back of the head. Toad goes down and drops the belt, which the referee quickly tosses outside. Dread begins hitting Toad with some big right hands, then takes him over with a snapmare and locks on a rear chinlock. Toad struggles to break free and finally gets to his feet, nailing Dread with some elbows to the gut. Toad shoves Dread into the ropes but Dread hangs on tight, dragging Toad back down into a side headlock. Toad pulls Dread over into a pinning predicament, but Dread breaks the hold at one. Both men get back to their feet and Dread nails Toad with three hard knife-edge chops, before dropping Toad with a single leg takedown and turning him into a single leg crab. Toad struggles to find the ropes, but Dread holds him fast in the center of the ring. Toad finally raises up and flips Dread out of the hold, but struggles to get back to his feet. Dread charges Toad but Toad rises up quickly with a hard axe handle to the side of the face, flooring Dread. Toad picks Dread up and hits a stalling vertical suplex, followed by a standing splash for a two count. Toad collects Dread and plants him with a DDT, then heads for the top rope! Toad reaches the top and signals for the Toad Jam, but Dread nips up and charges the turnbuckle, nailing Toad with an amazing high dropkick to the midsection, sending Toad flying off the top all the way through the Spanish announce table!! The crowd explodes in awe as Toad lies in pain in the wreckage, and a dazed Jason Dread tries to regain his senses in the ring. Toad miraculously manages to pull himself together and roll into the ring by a nine count, but Dread is waiting. Dread waits for Toad to get back to his feet, then nails a perfect Tres Flores! Dread hooks the leg! One… Two… The three count never comes. Dread looks around to see that the official has been pulled out of the ring by Octavian! The official begins screaming at Octavian to leave the ring area, but Octavian blows by him and grabs a chair from the timekeeper! Dread gets to his feet and threatens Octavian, but backs away when he sees Octavian coming with the chair. The official immediately snatches the chair away from Octavian and takes it back to the timekeeper, as Dread reaches through the ropes and grabs Octavian by the hair! Octavian instantly rears up with his Imperial Roman title belt in hand, nailing Dread with a diamond-studded shot to the face! Dread staggers back as Toad recovers, hits the ropes and nails him with a Toad Slam! The official orders Octavian away from ringside before seeing Toad making the cover in the ring! Octavian retreats to the stage and watches as the official makes the count! One… Two… Dread kicks out! Octavian is in shock as Toad angrily gets to his feet and pulls Dread up. Dread suddenly rears back and begins nailing Toad with some big right hands, before setting him on the top rope. Dread climbs on and hooks Toad for the top rope DDT, but Toad tosses him off violently with a huge slam. Toad then climbs to his feet and takes off, hitting a monstrous Toad Jam! Toad hooks the leg and the official counts. Dread won’t be kicking out this time. Winner: Toad Octavian nods in satisfaction as Toad recovers his Hardcore belt and raises it in the air. Dread lies in pain in the center of the ring as Toad poses to the furious crowd. Octavian simply smiles as he turns to leave, allowing Toad to enjoy his celebration. Toad is going to ACWF!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ We return to see that our heroes -- a crew of paramedics and a petrified Kevin Jacobs -- have... well, no, they have not progressed at all -- are not a single step closer to saving what could possibly be an already severely wounded -- if not dead -- referee Hal Jenkins. This time, a group of security guards have joined the squad of would-be-heroes. The screeching only continues, and now, numerous car alarms have been activated causing a sound that could drive any sane man insane. Mayhap, this is what keeps our offender going inside that lot? Giving the current circumstances, it probably isn't. In fact, it's not. The thing keeping all the chaos going is simple: The man behind the wheel is enjoying every god-forsaken minute of it. The laugh says it all. The screeching of a hard turn echoes again, and again... and again. JACOBS: Is he... ? Yes, Kevin, the man behind the wheel is doing doughnuts in the middle of the parking lot. It's the only reasonable -- if you could call it that -- explanation for why the screeching of tires hasn't stopped. Another minute passes -- there it is! -- and the crash follows. Now is their chance! The car has just collided with something else, and that means -- if even for a moment -- it isn't moving, and it'd take another second or so to regain momentum. That one second is the window of opportunity -- the chance to save Hal Jenkins -- if he can be saved. By now, the crowd behind the door has grown in considerable size. Fans, other officials, managers, stage-hands -- they've all gathered. After all, that's human nature, as cruel as it is -- Hey, look! The neighbors house is on fire, let's watch! No need to try to help during the entertainment -- and that's why the crowd has gathered. Alright, maybe a few of them are concerned about Hal. The security guards take one glance at each other -- a look that says it all; that says this must be done now as the situation is dire, and damnit, if we fail he could die if he's not dead already! -- and nod. It's time. Now or never. Hero or coward. That one second, long and horribly drawn out by the nature of time and the sudden burst of adrenaline baked in a crisp, golden crust of intense silence... Breaks. One man did what no one had dared do: -- to the surprise of everyone -- spoke. His calm voice sliced through the silence like a knife through a thin layer of skin -- just as it does butter. His easy, light hearted voice comes as surprise, instantly sending everyones hearts into their stomach and causing them to jump at the suspense of what was about to happen. Not to mention, it ruined the chance to save Hal, as the sounds of the car begin again. The unnecessary revving of the engine, the screeching tires and the dragging of metal all return, all because of this one man who boldly stepped forth and asked, "Hey, guys... what's going on?" As if Kevin Jacobs' face couldn't have grown more pale, it does. The little color that was left has been flushed away, down the proverbial toilet -- don't forget to wipe -- and the infamous double-take follows. Jacobs- H-H-Hal!? Hal? Who's Hal? Hal Jenkins? Yes! It's Hal Jenkins! He's come to save the day! Wait a minute? Isn't Hal...? Yes, the infamous double take -- we all fall victim to the comical act -- sweeps through the crowd. Then, the silent question on everyone's mind is spoken by Jacobs. Jacobs- Hal!? Are you alright!? How'd you get out of there? What happened? Alright, it's more along the line of the plural form of question. No matter, Hal is supposed to be in there -- victim of some maniacal driver -- in need of help by the Hero Squad, right? Jenkins- ...What are you talking about? A calm smile follows his words, and Kevin does yet another double take. Jacobs- Weren't you hit by a car earlier tonight? By now, the others have given all their attention to Hal -- the screeching from beyond the doors have faded into the background. Jenkins- I suppose you could say that, hahah, If you wanted to exaggerate it all... Curiosity strikes. Yes, what the hell is going on seems like the appropriate thing to say. Hal freezes for a moment, and begins to laugh -- however, a violent crash brings that to an end...for a second at least. In his mind, Hal begins putting the pieces together leaving the rest of us clueless. The security guards now see their opportunity arising yet again -- the crash! We have time to get the guy and not worry about rescuing someone! -- and don't wait for the explanation. The doors are kicked down, and the security guards pile through... and freeze. There, amongst numerous trash cans, toppled ladders, cardboard boxes, recycling bins, cans, poles, pieces of paper and random pieces of glass that came from who knows where is the source of the screeching tires. Yes. In the middle of the parking lot, amongst the chorus of car alarms, one vehicle -- if you could call it that -- spins in circles. The vehicle is a Jeep Wrangler... well, a Power Wheels Jeep Wrangler. The driver -- whose long dark hair is pulled into two pony-tails, one in the front, and one in the back -- is young, and small... clad in only wrestling boots and pajama pants with two unforgettable faces decorating them: Tom and Jerry. ... It's... Zippy. Zippy looks around at all the people who have now crowded in to the empty parking lot. He smiles, and laughs and extends one arm up only to begin furiously waving it back and forth. Zippy- Hi! ... I didn't do it! It was... was... uh... Gidget! Yeh! It was Gidget! It's her fault! He looks around, seeing blank looks on everyones faces. Zippy- BYE! Happy Hallosevalenteastermas Day everyone! Suddenly, the revving of the engine, and the screeching of the tires isn't too bad. Zippy revs up, and speeds down the parking lot, arm extended as he touches cars that he passes, setting off the car alarms. As Zippy fades from view, Jacobs focuses his attention at Hal. Jacobs- We were told you were hit by a car! Jenkins- Zippy ran over my foot while I was on my way to the building... I told someone who must have blown it into -- The screeching is heard again, and Zippy speeds back into view -- vrrrooooom vroooooom!! He yells -- and passes the crowd of people, carefully navigating into the building. Mayhap it would have been better if it were some maniacal, devious, conspiring, evil lunatic... but it's not. GCW is in for hell, it seems. Zippy is back! The cameras head back to ringside, where Mr. Danger has buried his face in his hands. Bryan- Zippy’s back! One of the most loved wrestlers in GCW history has returned! How about that! Danger- THAT was the big return? Give me a break… Bryan- What do you have against Zippy? Danger- Other than the fact he’s an idiot? Nothing, really. I was just hoping for a big name as promised, you know… Like Badger Benandanti, or Rich Rollins, or Hulk Hogan, or something. Bryan- Well, we’re ready to head back to ringside for a match that might hold huge ACWF implications, Toxic versus Mike Hardy… and Steven Caldera will be Hardy’s manager! Do The Evolution hits and the crowd instantly turns sour. The massive Toxic emerges on stage, pausing to strike a breathtaking pose before heading for the ring. Toxic gives a confident smile towards the ramp as Broken Promises hits next, and Mike Hardy appears on stage, with Steven Caldera right behind! Hardy is dressed in his black ensemble and still seems unconvinced about Caldera, but Caldera walks side by side with him all the way to the ring. The two pause as Caldera gives Hardy some last minute instructions, and Hardy slides into the ring. He instantly pops up onto his feet and hits a diving dropkick on Toxic, sending the big man staggering to the ropes, where he topples over to the floor below! The crowd explodes as the bell rings, and Caldera nods approvingly. Toxic, fuming, slowly climbs back into the ring and threateningly approaches Hardy. Rather than backing down, however, Hardy charges Toxic and begins peppering him with quick right hands! Toxic fires back a response, but Hardy ducks it and nails Toxic with a dropkick to the calf. Toxic falls to a knee and Hardy hits the ropes, flipping over and hitting a facebuster on Toxic! Hardy quickly leaps up to the top rope and dives off with a moonsault, but Toxic gets his knees up, and Hardy lands hard and rolls off in pain. Toxic pulls Hardy back to his feet and lifts him up with a guerilla press, displaying his impressive strength. Hardy manages to counter and slips behind Toxic, but Toxic immediately spins and takes Hardy down with a sadistic clothesline. Toxic lifts Hardy back up and shoves him into the corner, before bealing him all the way across the ring to the other side! Hardy slowly gets back to his feet and Toxic hits a big vertical suplex. Toxic turns to Caldera and mocks him as the crowd boos, but Hardy rolls Toxic up from behind for a near-fall! A shocked Toxic gets back to his feet and swings with another clothesline, but Hardy slips behind him and leaps onto his shoulders, pulling Toxic down into a victory roll for another two count. Toxic kicks out with such force that Hardy is sent flying through the ropes to the floor in front of the commentator’s table. Hardy gets back to his feet and gets a baseball slide from Toxic. Toxic slides out after Hardy and picks him up for a sidewalk slam, but Hardy counters into a flying headscissors! Toxic screams out in pain as his knee collides with the steel steps on the way down, and Hardy slides back into the ring and turns to Caldera! Caldera nods in approval and Hardy hops onto the turnbuckle, waiting for Toxic to get back to his feet. Hardy takes off with a huge flying moonsault, which connects perfectly! Toxic hits hard and Hardy leaps to his feet, throwing his arms in the air as the crowd roars its appreciation. Hardy rolls Toxic back into the ring and charges for a clothesline, but Toxic slips behind Hardy and nails him with a huge T-Bar backbreaker! Hardy writhes in pain as Toxic hobbles back to his feet, favoring his left leg. Hardy gets back to his feet, but Toxic sends him right back down with an earthshaking powerbomb! Hardy appears to be out cold as Caldera shouts towards the ring. Toxic smiles and covers Hardy nonchalantly, but Hardy gets a shoulder up at two! Toxic, in shock, picks Hardy up and sends him hard into the turnbuckle. Hardy bounces out and Toxic picks him up in the fireman’s carry for the Toxic Waste! Toxic swings Hardy around, but Hardy drops behind Toxic, hooking his head from behind. Hardy flips Toxic over with a reverse snapmare right into a HUGE facebuster, and the crowd explodes! Caldera signals Hardy to go for the cover, and Hardy hooks the leg. One… Two… Three! Hardy wins! Winner: Mike Hardy The crowd explodes in disbelief as a somewhat shocked but confident Hardy hops onto the turnbuckle, raising his hands in victory. Caldera steps into the ring and applauds Hardy, who steps down and gives him a surprised smile. Hardy shakes Caldera’s hand appreciatively as the two head out of the ring. Toxic starts to come around and glares furiously up the ramp, as Caldera simply grins back. Toxic begins kicking the ropes and threatens the official, who promptly bails out. Toxic continues his angry rampage as WorldWide fades to a commercial. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Bryan- Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome back to WorldWide, where moments ago we saw something that frankly shocked the hell out of me! A replay of Hardy’s big move to beat Toxic is shown, with the crowd still cheering in excitement. Bryan- This has got to be a major setback in Toxic’s quest to get in to ACWF, and there are only two spots left! Danger- That’s right, Octavian got his spot earlier tonight by beating Allied Chaos, Twisted and The Jhub got their spots last week, and Toad shocked Jason Dread and is going to ACWF himself! Time’s running out for Toxic, and Mike Hardy might have jumped in front of him in the standings with that win! Bryan- Well, it seems Steven Caldera gave him some rather good advice, as he handed Toxic his first defeat in over two years! But you’d better believe Toxic is going to be on the warpath next week. He had to be escorted out of here by security at the break, and he is not a happy man. Danger- Well, coming up now we see LeStatt Knight defend his title against The Jhub! I can’t wait for this one, LeStatt is on a hell of a roll so far! And The Jhub must be a little distracted after Toad turned his back on him and officially ended System-X! Fuel suddenly hits overhead, and the crowd stands with a big ovation. The massive Luke Hanson slowly saunters onto the stage, smiling confidently at the crowd! Hanson begins making his way to the ring, high-fiving the fans along the way! Bryan- Speaking of System-X, here comes another former member in Luke Hanson! Hanson made his return last week, and boy does he look great! Danger- The biggest man in the business, JB, and one hell of an athlete, Hanson’s challenge for the United States title was answered earlier this evening! It’s gonna be Hanson versus Knight at ACWF for the United States title, and that should be a hell of a match! Hanson reaches the announce table and takes a seat, smiling back at the fans as he does so. Hanson- Hey guys, though I’d come down here and get an up close and personal look at my good buddy The Jhub. Bryan- Welcome to WorldWide, Luke! Your challenge was answered earlier tonight, and you’ve got your US title shot at ACWF! Hanson- I’m looking forward to it, JB. That damn belt belongs around the waist of the biggest man in sports today, and that’s where it’s gonna be come ACWF. My Own Summer hits and The Jhub storms out on stage! The crowd erupts in cheers, chanting heavily for The Jhub. He stops halfway down the ramp and shakes the hand of a fan holding a System-X sign, then continues on his way. The Jhub marches to the ring and hops onto the turnbuckle, closing his eyes as he’s bathed in camera light. O’Fortuna hits next, and the cheers fade to a hushed awe as the lights in the arena dim. LeStatt Knight slowly stalks down the ramp, the GCW United States title belt draped over his shoulder proudly. He glares at The Jhub the entire way to the ring, but The Jhub refuses to give ground. Knight finally reaches the ring and tosses his belt aside, immediately assaulting The Jhub at the sound of the bell! Knight takes Jhub down with a big spear tackle, hammering him furiously with big right hands. The Jhub reverses and returns the blows, but Knight tosses him away and both men get back to their feet. They tie up and Knight gains the upper hand, powering The Jhub back into the corner. Knight immediately hits The Jhub with a low knee to the gut, then takes him right out of the corner with a bulldog! The Jhub painfully rolls out of the ring but Knight follows, sending Jhub face first into the steel steps. Knight then whips Jhub hard into the ring apron before rolling him back into the ring. Knight stands Jhub up and sends him into the ropes, but telegraphs a back body drop and gets a hard DDT for a quick one count. The Jhub then hits Knight with a leg drag and immediately steps into the figure four! Knight’s face remains stoic despite the pain, and after a few moments The Jhub gives up on the hold and nails a hard elbow drop to the sternum. Jhub pulls Knight back up and sends him into the turnbuckle, but Knight charges out with a clothesline. The Jhub ducks the clothesline and drops Knight into a reverse armbar! Knight quickly gets his foot on the bottom rope, and The Jhub hops onto the second turnbuckle. The Jhub dives off with another elbow, connecting for a two count. Jhub pulls Knight back up but Knight pushes him off and nails him in the face with a vicious right hand, staggering The Jhub. Knight then hops onto Jhub’s shoulders and takes him down with a perfectly executed hurricanrana, hooking the legs for a near fall. The Jhub powers out and gets back to his feet, only to get a hard clothesline back to the mat. Knight hooks his leg again, getting another two count. Knight pulls Jhub back up and hooks him for a DDT, but Jhub counters it into a northern lights suplex! The Jhub stands and signals for the Sharpshooter, but his attention turns to the ramp as Toad walks on stage. Toad slowly saunters to the middle of the stage to the ire of the crowd. Glaring at The Jhub, Toad turns to the fan with the System-X sign and snatches it away, tearing it up into several pieces and tossing them in The Jhub’s direction. The Jhub curses at Toad, but gets spun around from behind by LeStatt Knight. Knight nails the Knightfall, and covers Jhub for the elementary three count! Winner: And Still GCW United States Champion, LeStatt Knight Toad makes his way to the ring, while LeStatt Knight backs his way to the ropes to take his belt. Toad slides in as The Jhub painfully gets to his feet, glaring angrily at Toad. Toad points an accusing finger at The Jhub, and takes the Hardcore belt from around his waist, threatening The Jhub with it. Bryan- Toad has cost The Jhub a chance at the United States title here tonight, and now he’s looking to do more damage! Luke, I… Luke? JB fades out as he sees Hanson standing on the apron with a steel chair! Hanson winds up and nails LeStatt Knight in the back of the head, dropping Knight like a rock. Hanson steps over the top rope and into the ring, glaring at Toad. The Jhub slowly gets back to his feet and remains wary of both men, as the crowd explodes in cheers at this sudden System-X reunion! Danger- I never thought I’d see these three in the ring together again, JB! Bryan- What’s gonna happen? Toad has his Hardcore belt, Hanson has that chair, and The Jhub is still feeling the effects of his match! System-X is in the ring together again! Hanson, Toad and Jhub stare each other down, with Toad inching towards Jhub, and Hanson warning him off with his chair. Amid the chaos in the ring and in the crowd, nobody notices the lone man sliding into the ring with a steel chair of his own. Bryan- HEY! WAIT A MINUTE! THAT’S…. The man’s red hair catches the corner of Jhub’s eye, but it’s too late. The man nails The Jhub in the skull with a heavy shot, dropping Jhub instantly. Toad never sees the second shot coming, and is sent through the ropes to the floor with a big swing. Hanson turns around with his chair ready, but the newcomer connects one chair with the other, sending Hanson’s own steel right into his face, instantly bloodying his nose! Danger- I don’t believe it, JB! He just took out all three men! The crowd explodes in astonishment as Michael ‘Spitfire’ Stevens drops his chair in satisfaction. With a big grin he looks up at the crowd and nods at his accomplishment. Bryan- Michael Stevens is back in GCW! Danger- There was a big return after all, JB! Bryan- And he’s taken out The Jhub, Toad, and Luke Hanson! What’s he doing?! Stevens turns to the fallen body of LeStatt Knight, and eyes the US title belt. Stevens grins and drapes the belt over his shoulder, then takes a stance directly overtop the fallen body of Luke Hanson. Stevens raises his arms in the air, the US belt over his shoulder, smiling confidently. Bryan- Michael Stevens has the US title belt in hand, and he’s standing over Luke Hanson! Michael Stevens has sent a hell of a message here tonight, Mr. Danger! Danger- No kidding! He’s the only man standing with four guys down in the ring! There’s gonna be hell to pay for Michael Stevens next week! And what does this mean for the United States title match?! Is there another challenger? Bryan- Hopefully we’ll get our answers next for. For Mr. Danger, I’m James Bryan, THANK YOU VERY MUCH! The GCW logo appears on the bottom of the screen as Michael Stevens climbs onto the turnbuckle with the United States title belt. He then tosses the belt carelessly over the fallen body of LeStatt Knight and climbs out of the ring, posing for the crowd as the show fades out………
