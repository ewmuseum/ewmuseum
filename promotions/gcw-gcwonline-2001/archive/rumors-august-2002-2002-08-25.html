Monday, August 05, 2002

Negotiations with Former GCW Superstars?

Former GCW Superstars Alex Grey, and Jeff Wylde were seen leaving GCW headquarters today. Wylde, back in the states after spending off time in Australia, admitted there was an interest, now that TDM was in charge. "Caldera bailed on this place, and I still love it. Fuck, Caldera was the reason we got to be friends in the first place. I'd love to give it another go."

Grey, was not so candid. "I was here. Big deal. Can't a guy come see his brother in law without people thinking he's returning?" When asked bluntly, If he was returning Grey answered. "Not in your lifetime, which could be over in a few minutes if you don't get out of my face."

More as it comes.

Sunday, July 28, 2002

Heatwave Banner Released!

Posted on the GCW splash page, the Heatwave banner has been released.

Featured on the image is Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens, who had an impressive showing in the Dangerous Games battle royal. This is leading to incredible speculation that Stevens will be headlining the show.

However, GCW officials have shot this rumor down, stating that, "All GCW promotional images are made with quality in mind, but do not in any way reflect the events behind the shows they are made for."

However, Michael Stevens was on his way to the top before GCW closed... could he end up on top anyway?

Thursday, July 25, 2002

New Staff Member?

GCW HQ, Columbus, Ohio:

Steven Caldera shocked the world at Dangerous Games, announcing his departure from GCW television forever. With Caldera now off the road and away from the wrestlers themselves, it leaves one to wonder how order will be maintained.

Caldera, in an interview earlier today, alluded to the hiring of a new staff member, whose sole purpose will be the completion and administration of contracts. This employee will be the only disciplinarian present at events, aside from referees and security. As of now, GCW has yet to officially sign any contracts. All deals are to be settled as of Meltdown this Thursday night.

So, with the event booked to go down but nobody on the roster, who will show up to seal some deals for GCW? Or does President Caldera have something else up his sleeve?
