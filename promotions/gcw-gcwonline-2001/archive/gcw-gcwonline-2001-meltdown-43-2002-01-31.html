GCW — Card

Meltdown 43, 31 January 2002

31 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 43 North Charleston Coliseum — North Charleston, South Carolina ---- Meltdown #43 (The lights in the arena dim, and the MegaTron lights up. All eyes turn to the screen, as shots from Rampage begin to play.) Yale- TIGER DRIVER! What a move! But who the hell is this?! (Octavian lies motionless in the ring, as Caldera still remains unconscious. The referee slowly begins to stir, as the cloaked figure reaches for his hood. The crowd’s buzz grows and grows as the figure slowly pulls his hood down…) Bryan- KHAN!!!! I DO NOT BELIEVE THIS! (As the hood slides off, the figure’s face becomes clear. Khan stands in a GCW ring for the first time in six months, glaring down at Octavian’s broken body. The crowd explodes with an incredibly intense reaction of cheers as gasps of disbelief. Khan then swiftly reaches down for the street sign, before heading to the top rope!) Yale- I Don’t believe this is happening! I thought Khan had been fired!! Bryan- Khan is heading for the top rope! What is going on here?! He measures Octavian off, and leaps! ARABIAN FACEBUSTER with the street sign! Octavian is out cold! And the crowd is loving every last minute! Yale- Damnit! This isn’t fair! And the referee is just now waking up! Khan reaches for Caldera, and pulls him on top of Octavian! Here’s the count! (The referee warily begins a painful count, and the crowd shouts each count as loud as they can…) Bryan- ONE!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!! Steven Caldera wins! (We then see scenes from the end of the World Title Match.) (Octavian attempts to get to the apron, but bails out as Badger moves in. The crowd cheers wildly for Badger, as Caldera and Khan watch from the ring. Dread and Mayhem both lie hurting on the mat, as Khan begins climbing the turnbuckle. Badger raises his arms to the cheering crowd, and turns to face his allies. However, as soon as he faces Caldera, Caldera swings the chair, connecting with a vicious shot to Badger’s skull!!) Bryan- WHAT THE HELL?! (The crowd instantly begins to boo, confused and angry.) Yale- What the- Bryan- CALDERA JUST LEVELED BADGER! What the hell did he do that for?! Badger is out cold from Caldera’s chair shot, and Khan is perched on the top rope! And Toxic and Octavian are just watching in total shock! Yale- What is going on here? Caldera turns around and tosses the chair to Khan! Khan stares down at the fallen men in the ring, and takes off! (Khan soars through the air, moving the chair under his legs. Khan then lands perfectly with a leg drop over the chair, hitting Tristain Mayhem clean on the face. The crowd boos in total shock and incredulous hatred, as Khan and Caldera head to Dread.) Bryan- What is this?! Why have President Caldera and Khan destroyed Badger and Mayhem!? Will someone please tell us what the hell is going on!? Yale- Look! Caldera is pulling Dread up to his feet, as the referee is forced to count! (Dread warily looks up at Caldera with a smile. Caldera helps Dread to his feet, as the referee makes the ten count. The crowd boos in anger and Octavian and Toxic watch in shock as the referee makes the final counts… eight…. Nine…. Ten! Mayhem doesn’t move an inch, and the bell rings.) Bryan- DAMNIT! Andrews- Here is your winner… and the NEW Global Championship Wrestling Heavyweight Champion… Jason Dread!!!!! (The crowd in the arena boos the scene, but quickly becomes excited as red and blur pyro erupts from the stage! The pyro runs down the ramp and hits the ring, and the turnbuckles explode with red fire! The display finally calms, as the cameras pan over the wild fans.) Bryan- Just four days ago, the very foundation of GCW was turned upside down, as we witnessed one of the most shocking events in our history! Welcome everyone to Meltdown! I’m James Bryan, as always at ringside with David Yale, and Dave, at Rampage we saw the return of the Establishment! Yale- Yes we did! And I loved every damn minute of it! Finally, President Caldera came to his senses, and together with his returning bodyguard Khan, he helped Jason Dread win the world title! What a great night! Bryan- Well, fans, we’re hoping to get some answers tonight, and in our main event, we’ll see the Jhub and Octavian team up to take on Dread and Khan! (The Establishment theme cues up in the arena, and an enormous chorus of boos rises up from the crowd. President Caldera, Khan, and the new world champion Jason Dread emerge from behind the stage, and confidently walk to the ring. Dread holds his title belt up to be seen, as fans on the ramp flip him off in anger.) Bryan- And we’re being joined by the Establishment right now! In all honesty, Dave, what we saw on Sunday was truly one of the most heinous things I’ve ever seen, and I’m really disgusted with these three men! Yale- Come on, JB! These guys are the most intelligent men in GCW, and there isn’t anyone that can stop them now! (The Establishment enters the ring, and Caldera is handed a microphone. Their music fades, but is replaced by an equally loud ‘Asshole!’ chant. Caldera’s grin turns to a scowl, but he simply continues without acknowledging the crowd.) Caldera- This is rather like deja-vu all over again, isn’t it? Seems like only yesterday I ruled this ring with my Establishment. But then I turned away from all that, and spent my time and energy trying to do what I thought was the right thing, and help Tristain Mayhem win the World Title. But Mayhem turned his back on me. So when all of you call me an asshole, you just remember who it was that turned his back on all of us first! (The crowd boos loudly, but finally quiets.) Caldera- Tristain Mayhem turned his back on me, not the other way around. So don’t hate me for giving the World title to a man who truly does deserve it… JASON DREAD! (The crowd boos yet more, as Dread presents his world title belt for all to see.) Caldera- Now that the Establishment is back, we’re already looking for new members. And it was only a matter of hours before we found one. This man is one of the greatest talents in GCW history, fans, and at Rampage, he went all the way to become the first ever winner of the annual Rampage tournament! So, without further ado, here is the newest member of the Establishment… (The crowd seems shocked, having heard that Montano has joined the Establishment! However, Lust for Life cues up, and the crowd begins to boo in confusion. Michael Stevens emerges onto the stage, smiling broadly. He walks to the ring with a gleam in his eye, as the fans scowl in anger.) Bryan- Hold on a minute, Michael Stevens didn’t win the Rampage tournament! Yale- Sure he did! You saw it, JB! Montano was getting destroyed, and in the end he had to CHEAT to get the three count! But that doesn’t mean a thing! Michael Stevens is the real Rampage winner! (Stevens enters the ring, and Caldera hands him the microphone. His music fades out, as the crowd continues to boo.) Stevens- That’s right! The REAL winner of the Rampage tournament is the newest member of the Establishment! (The crowd boos, as the MegaTron lights up. The closing moments of the Rampage finals are shown, where Montano rolls up Stevens, using a handful of tights for leverage.) Stevens- Look at this! Montano clearly cheated! Montano was getting his ass handed to him, and he had to cheat to get by me! And so President Caldera has informed me that I am now the REAL winner of the Rampage tournament! (More boos from the crowd, as Dread and Caldera applaud. Stevens smiles and hands the microphone back to Caldera, who prepares to speak. However, My Own Summer cues up, and the crowd leaps to their feet! The Jhub emerges from the back and walks to the stage, microphone in hand. His music quiets, but the crowd continues cheering wildly.) Bryan- The Jhub is here! The Jhub was screwed out of the US title at Rampage, but we’re still not sure who it was that assaulted him! Jhub- Never in my life have I seen such an incredibly collection of peon sellouts in one ring! The four of you really make me sick, you know that? Now, as everyone knows, I was dropped twenty feet through the air to the mat at Rampage, costing me my United States championship. Now, I’m not sure who is was that was behind it, but I’d bet a pretty penny that the man behind it is standing in that ring right now! Yale- How dare he accuse the Establishment?! Bryan- Makes sense to me, Dave! (Caldera goes to answer the Jhub, but Dread asks for the microphone. Caldera hands it over, and Dread turns to the Jhub.) Dread- That certainly was a nasty fall, wasn’t it, Jhub? Well, you need to ask yourself one question in your search for your attacker. Who do you trust? Who can the Jhub trust? You saw what your little System-X buddy Luke Hanson did to Toad at Rampage, didn’t you? You can’t trust him. Hell, maybe you can’t even trust Toad himself! Well, Jhub, I know who it was that attacked you. And I’ll give you a hint. The man who attacked you isn’t necessarily in the Establishment just yet… But he will be standing right next to you in tonight’s main event. So be careful who you trust, Jhub. The man who tried to take you out at Rampage is closer than you might think. (Just Got Wicked hits, and the crowd boos. The Jhub, now thinking deeply, suddenly gets a look of realization on his face, and turns to leave. The Establishment slowly exits the ring, as the crowd continues to boo.) Bryan- Wait a minute… is he insinuating that Octavian was the man that assaulted him? Yale- Yes! It was Octavian! It has to be, JB! (The Establishment heads to the back, as Meltdown fades to a commercial break.) (The show returns, and the screen holds a still shot of the Jhub for a few moments, with the text ‘During the Break’ overhead.) Bryan- Fans, during the break, a confrontation between Octavian and the Jhub occurred backstage. (The still shot begins to move, as the Jhub walks through the hallways, spying Octavian talking to a technician. The Jhub spins Octavian around, and begins to shout.) Jhub- I’m on to you, you son of a bitch! Octavian- Jhub, what the hell are you talking about? Jhub- Just so you know, I don’t trust you one damn bit! And if you so much as flinch in that match later tonight, I’ll kick your ass, too! Stay the hell away from me, Octy, don’t for one moment think that I’m stupid! (The Jhub then storms by Octavian, bumping into him as strongly as possible. Octavian, confused and a bit angry, glares back, as the scene cuts to live action at ringside.) In her debut match, the Hunter gives Katanya a wrestling lesson, and scores the victory. Mike Hardy and Zippy team up to take on Kippo and Tobias Fang. Hardy and Zippy take over early with some unorthodox high-flying moves, but Kippo turns a cross body from Zippy into a powerslam, which is enough for the three. Luke Hanson takes on Demented next in a brutal bout. Hanson takes over early following a quick boot to the face, and follows things up with a big guerilla press slam. However, Demented takes over following a great missile dropkick to the skull, and then a chop block to take the big man down. However, Hanson spots Demented preparing for a back body drop, and plants him into the mat with DeFueler for the three. In the back, Jason Dread approaches NBK. Dread smiles, pointing out the Jhub’s nasty fall at Rampage. NBK grins just a bit, but turns away from the World Champion. Dread asks the new United States champion if he would like to join the Establishment, and NBK remains silent. Dread begins to get angry, shouting that NBK would not have won his title without the Establishment. NBK simply glares at Dread, and walks away. Twisted prepared to take on Michael Stevens next. Stevens took over immediately, taking down Twisted with a side headlock, followed by an armbar. Stevens then went for his Reverse Fujiwara Armbar, but Twisted scrambled into the ropes. Twisted managed to come back with a dropkick followed by a big suplex, but only managed a near-fall. Twisted then attempted a superplex, but Stevens managed to pull the turnbuckle cover off, and then hop down, causing Twisted to fall seated on the steel turnbuckle. The official spots the turnbuckle and calls for the bell, disqualifying Stevens. Angrily, Stevens lays out the official, and storms away. Badger Benandanti was found backstage by Kevin Jacobs, who asked Badger what he thought about the events at Rampage. Badger wasn’t happy at all, and at the moment had very little to say. All he could reveal is that he will be getting even, and we’ll just have to wait to see. In a huge match, the Agents of Oblivion dominated a depressed Double Cool. Tristain Mayhem started things off with The Dark Messiah, who leveled Mayhem with a big lariat, before tagging in the Lunatic. The Lunatic went absolutely crazy, pulling Mayhem into the corner and stomping away at his face non-stop for a solid four minutes! The Lunatic then dragged Mayhem to the center of the ring, and dropped a succession of leg drops over Mayhem’s face. The Lunatic then propped Mayhem up in the corner, and began firing away with unmerciful punches to the nose and forehead. Mayhem finally slumped to the ground, and the Lunatic made the cover. The three count was made, and the Agents of Oblivion won the tag titles with little more than brutal basic offense. In the main event, The Jhub started off against Jason Dread. The Jhub warned Octavian to stay back, and refused any help. As the Jhub tended to Octavian, Dread took him down with an axe handle, and began pummeling him. Dread then went for a back body drop, but the Jhub countered with an uppercut to the face, followed by an STO! The Jhub then hit Dread with a big swinging neck breaker, and Octavian asked for a tag. As the Jhub argued with him, Dread tagged in Khan, who assaulted the Jhub from behind. Khan hit the Jhub with a bulldog, followed by a springboard moonsault. Khan then went to the top rope, and connected with a big flying kick to the jaw from the top! Khan then set the Jhub up for a Tiger Driver, but the Jhub pulled Khan down, and sent him flying with a slingshot right into Jason Dread! All three men go down, and the Jhub collapses. The Jhub manages to get to his feet, but he stumbles back, and Octavian tags himself in from behind! Octavian rushes into the ring, assaulting Khan with big boots. The Jhub storms out of the ring and grabs himself a steel chair, before climbing back into the ring to see Octavian take Khan down with a Greco-Roman throw. Dread grabs the referee by the shirt, holding him in the corner, before a confused Jhub approaches Octavian and levels him with a chair! The Jhub then turns to attack Khan, but Khan doubles the Jhub over, and hits a big Tiger Driver! Khan then snatches up the chair, and swiftly makes it to the top rope, where he takes off, landing on the Jhub with an Arabian Facebuster to the Jhub’s face! Dread releases the referee, who makes an elementary three count, as Khan pins the Jhub.
