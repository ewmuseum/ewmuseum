Worldwide 43 - The Aftermath of Rampage - PTC PARTICIPATION WANTED!

22 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT Dawn McFarlane vs. Bryan Mayhem GCW World Championship Match NO DQ. Of all the GCW superstars, Bryan Mayhem has been the most vocal in his displeasure of a newcomer winning the GCW World Championship. Will Mayhem come to the front of the fight against the new blood in GCW who hold nearly all the belts? Can Mayhem lead the charge against the newcomers? Will Dawn have a first successful defense? LeStatt Knight vs. Kraig Carter Some view Knight's actions at Rampage as what may have cost Carter a victory, and a chance to be GCW's TV Champion. Carter requested this match, and Caldera gave his blessing. Will Bryan Mayhem be a factor, after being cost the match as well? Vivica J. Valentine vs. Eva Van Lorne US Title match. After losing her US title, Eva Van Lorne has made no small bones about her desire to get her title back. Vivica Valentine, however, has designs on keeping that belt around her waist for a long, long time. Can Van Lorne recapture what's rightfully hers? Will Valentine be victorious? Jason Warr vs. Chris Kannon Television Championship Match In only his second match in GCW, Jason Warr won the right to face Chris Kannon for the TV Championship, a belt that hasn't been defended much as of late. That all changes as we move on to Warpath! SPECIAL ATTRACTION MATCH - A GOODBYE TO [name withheld] Character of Ross’s Choosing vs. Character of RJ’s choosing. [name withheld], known to GCW as the handler of Moraless is leaving the game for what could be forever. In light of that, there’s one match that he’s always wanted that he’s never gotten: A one on one contest with [name withheld], and one of his characters. So, to say goodbye to Ross, and thank him for his time spent in GCW and PTC, we’ve decided to allow this special attraction, one off matchup, pitting the two against each other. Can Ross raise his hand in victory one final time? Will RJ spoil his goodbye? This is where you come in, folks. Both RJ and Ross will post their RPs here at PTC. Then, it's up to each of you to read said RPs, and pick a winner, sent to me by PM. Deadline will be Wednesday, March 30th. Feel free to predict the outcomes below.
