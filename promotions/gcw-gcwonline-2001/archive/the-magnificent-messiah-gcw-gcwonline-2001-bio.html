The Magnificent Messiah Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Magnificent One, The Harbinger for the Magnificent Era Hometown Las Vegas, NV Birthday October 23, 1977 Height 6'1 Weight 246lbs Biography Standing just six foot one inch tall, the Magnificent Messiah is hardly the most physically imposing professional wrestler, but his wherewithal and in-ring intelligence more than makes up for his size disadvantage. This is not to do disservice to his technical abilities inside the ring. The Magnificent Messiah is among even the soundest of technicians, not to mention his extensive training his Muay-Thai kickboxing. You would be hard pressed to find a fiercer competitor than the man who calls himself the Savior of a Generation. With his ends-justify-the-means attitude, the Messiah isn’t one to turn his nose up at an opportunity to bend the rules in his favour. Other Feds JUST Other Fed Titles N/A Other Fed Awards N/A Strengths Pure ability. Cunning. Ring intelligence. Charisma. Lack of conscience. An 'end-justifies-the-means' mentality Weaknesses Emotions often get the better of him, resulting in DQ defeats (not releasing holds, pushing ref,). Panics when being dominated. Greatly intimidated by much larger opponents. Wrestling Style Technical striker/high flying tendencies Strategy The Magnificent Messiah has great confidence in his ability. He is always trying to wear his opponent down using his technical and striking abilities. Despite his reputation as a man not afraid to bend and work outside the rulebook, the Messiah does have phenomenal skills inside the ring. No matter how the match finishes, he always starts out a fierce competitor. As the match wears on, the Messiah's actions are dictated by one simple fact: is he winning this match? If he is, you can bet he will keep the pace slow and methodical, wearing his opponent down with a wide-array of non-match-ending submissions designed to weaken his foe, suplexes and demeaning tactics such as head swipes or cocky pins. He is like a cat playing with a wounded mouse. He will continue to play with his prey until he can either finish them with high impact moves, the JFT or Righteous Awakening or they make a miraculous comeback. Should that comeback occur, the Magnificent One's strategy turns more desperate. He is always more likely to bend the rules when in this vulnerable state. Rarely will he result to dirty deeds (rake of the eyes, low blows, leveraging a pin with the ropes) if he is in complete control of a match. When on the defensive, the Messiah turns to his quick strikes and arial assault, looking for a quick turn around so he can take the reigns back from his opponent. His most dirty deeds come when he feels he is mere moments from defeat. He will do almost anything in these crucial moments to keep himself alive and give him a surprise win. He also utilizes more surprise pins (small packages, schoolboy, ect.) when at his most desperate in hopes he can steal one. The Magnificent Messiah never goes down without a fight.
