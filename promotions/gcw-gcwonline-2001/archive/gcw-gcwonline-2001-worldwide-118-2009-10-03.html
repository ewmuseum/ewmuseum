WorldWide 118, 3 October 2009

WorldWide 118 Arena at Harbor Yard — Bridgeport, Connecticut ---- Introduction The GCW logo flashes on the screen. As it fades out, it opens to a sold-out Connecticut arena with tens of thousands of screaming fans on their feet. "Wake Up" by Rage Against The Machine kicks up throughout the public announce system as it is complimented by the explosion of bright blue fireworks around the ring. The stage ignites almost immediately after with silver pyrotechnics just as the voice of James Bryan cues in. James Bryan: WELCOME EVERYONE! The camera's pan across the arena, showing off the fans' signs and their enthusiasm. Bryan: I'm James Bryan and I'm joined tonight by the ever-so-clever David Yale and we'd like to welcome you to GCW WORLDWIDE! David Yale: And what better place to be than the beau-ti-ful state of Connecticut, where the women are classy, sophisticated, and smoking hot! Bryan: It's always good to know that your hormones never get the best of you. Yale: Oh enough of you. Let these people know what they've got in store for them. Bryan: Ha-ha! It's been less than twenty four hours since we bore witness to one of the most grueling World Championship matches of all time and it was Andy Murray who was ultimately victorious over the dynamic Chris Bagwell at Warpath! Yale: But Chris Bagwell didn't go down without a fight, JB. It took four Hex Breakers to put the Rajah of Ratings down for good. A lesser man would've given up after one. Bagwell: Give credit where credit is due - Chris Bagwell sure as hell gave Andy Murray the fight of his life. Unfortunately for Chris, it concluded with the Admiral of Awesome completing the full sweep of The Establishment. Yale: It sure wasn't President Caldera's night. Every single member of The Establishment lost in singles competition. Fisher fell to Zacharia Taylor, DeBough lost to Axel Action in a non-title match, and Garbage Bag Johnny was defeated by his son and the 2009 Rampage Tournament Winner, Clyde Fox. Bryan: I quiver at the thought of what will go on tonight. The guy who said there's no wraith like a woman scorn has surely never met President Steven Caldera when he's pissed off. Yale: As they say, JB, I guess the show must go on. We're coming off just an absolutely incredible pay-per-view event and now we're faced with another huge one in only three weeks! Bryan: That's right - Dangerous Games is upon us! The annual GCW 30-man brawl will feature the event and we've seen quite a few stars born at that event. Yale: The field is wide open, JB. Dangerous Games is one of those events where anybody could get lucky and be the winner. Even you! Bryan: I think I'll continue sitting right here, where I'm comfortable. Yale: Well we've surely got a lot to look forward to tonight, regardless. A ton of new superstars have graced the GCW roster and we're going to get a good look at all of them in action. Bryan: That's right, tonight we're going to see the new class take over the show in such form as Barney Green against Nacho Grande, Bishop Steele squaring off with Killswitch, and Mysterious Masked Mexican battling Cheap Labor. Yale: What about the new Television Champion, Lucas Quinton, in his first defense against number one contender Cade Carson. Bryan: Both Quinton and Carson have been making waves in the lower ranks as of late and I'm certain they're going to continue to impress tonight. Yale: Carson has been on a hot streak - that's exactly what Quinton doesn't want to have to face in his first defense. Bryan: And how about the main event! The prolific Zacharia Taylor takes on newcomer and Just Wrestling superstar Aaron Nothings! Yale: I've heard a ton of hype behind this Nothings guy, JB. I hope he doesn't end up like Mark in the Dark or Darek DeAngleo - a complete bust. Bryan: No sense in stalling any longer...ladies and gentlemen, WELCOME TO WORLDWIDE! ---- Thanks "Established" cues up to begin the show, and a massive chorus of boos greets the owner and President of Global Championship Wrestling. Without much fanfare Caldera appears, looking entirely uninspired. He trudges his way toward the ring, and the lack of excitement quiets the crowd. Bryan: And we're kicking things off here with President Caldera himself! After Chris Bagwell fell short last week at Warpath, Caldera is once again without the GCW World Title and, perhaps, without a leader! Yale: Bagwell will be back, mark my words, JB. But this isn't a particularly good night for Caldera. Warpath was a disaster for The Establishment, and Caldera looks like he's having difficulty regrouping here. Caldera soberly climbs through the ropes and enters the ring, microphone in hand. He motions to cut off the music, and the technicians immediately oblige. Caldera: I'm not wasting a whole lot of time tonight. It's pretty clear that I'm not happy, but I'm not going to sit here and rehash the failures of certain Establishment leaders. I'm going to look to the future. I'm going to look toward Dangerous Games. The crowd actually roars at the mention of GCW's most anticipated event. Caldera: But, as is usually the case in this company, one can't really take a step forward without revisiting the past. But... But... Caldera suddenly freezes, his bold words quickly losing their strength. Bryan: What's this? Caldera seems... I don't know, he seems spooked somehow. Yale: Yeah, there's something going on here, JB. Caldera: I... I can't believe this is about to happen... Steven Caldera stands in the ring for a moment, a microphone at his mouth, ready to speak. Just as he opens his mouth, darkness fills the arena. A steady beat of a timpani drum echoes slowly at first, growing with a slight crescendo at each beat. Bryan: New entrance? Yale: I don't know. I don't have anything on my card. Anyone know what's going on? A single spotlight shines into the center of the ring, the drum beat joined by a quiet string orchestra, growing slowly in volume, playing an E flat major chord. The bass rumbles through the seats as fans look around, wondering what's going on. Bryan: I don't know what this is... any help? A torch lights under the MegaTron, glowing brightly. Yale: This is? Okay, I'm getting word that we're having some sort of glitch in the production truck, and that they've lost their control over the area. Bryan: This is bizarre. Yale: You're telling me... A trumpet sounds a call, louder than the other instruments, as the buzz in the arena grows. The safety lights are out, leaving the single spot and the torch as the only light in the arena. The MegaTron flickers with snow for an instant before going out, and flickering to life once more. Two bloody wings lay in a spotlight on the screen. The wings are large, almost as if they're angel's wings. In moments, two wings fall from the rafters in the arena, almost into the same position as the ones on the screen. The fans ooh at the sight, as the note changes, turning into a B flat major chord, the timpani still hitting it's note. A second torch lights, followed by a third, a fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth. They form an arrow, pointing toward the ring. A voice speaks in a haunting vibrato. Voice: I was sent to be your savior, but, it was you... who saved me. The stringed instruments fade, followed by the timpani. The spotlight moves from the wings in the ring to the entrance way under the MegaTron. A minute goes by with silence. Bryan: What is going... Silence no more. A single guitar note fills the ears of the thousands in the arena, the millions watching around the world, and the man who wears a cloak, who's just emerged from the MegaTron, standing in the spotlight. A second guitar note wails again, a perfect vibrato, as he grabs the hood. A voice begins to sing, giving away the man's identity. IT'S ONLY THE END OF THE WORLD...AGAIN. The cloak comes off, as the perfectly painted face of one Jason O'Neil comes into view on the MegaTron. The stunned audience sits in silence as he looks around into the faces in the darkness. This is it. The moment he's waited for for six years. The moment where every fan in attendance stood on their feet, the moment where each and every person around the world felt goosebumps rise on their skin, and the chill down their spine. The moment where he'd might be accepted into their arms and hearts, finally. It did not disappoint. Bryan: JASON O'NEIL IS HERE! Yale: I'M SCARED, BUT, DAMN! IT'S REALLY HIM! The lights went up as Dax Riggs continues to sing Endsmouth, but, the fans barely hear it. O'Neil barely hears it as the fans scream and cheer and boo and hiss for him. He spent six years degrading this company, and now, for just one moment, for just one night, he was their hero and villain. Tears stained his eyes as he stood, visibly moved. The make-up under his eyes, which took more than an hour to put on his face, began to run. The stoic O'Neil pulled off the cloak, and fell to his knees, worshiping the fans who hated his every move. His tears fell on the ramp, as he bowed to each and every fan at ringside. Bryan: Fans, we're just going to shut our mouths and let you enjoy this. Yale: Thank God, Bryan. I'm speechless. Bryan: And a bit choked up. Yale: You are too! Bryan: And justifiably so! The prodigal son has returned! Jason O'Neil stood on wobbly knees and soaked up the reception he's received. Despite it all -- the destruction of the Jhub, the taunts thrown at Steven Caldera, the dismantling of Jeff Wylde, NBK, Toxic, Twizzy, Tristain Mayhem -- this was home. His home. He stops at the closest group of fans and hugs them, saying thank you to each and every person he touches. Steven Caldera looks annoyed in the ring as O'Neil sets his sights on him, walking to the ring with a smile on his face. A few steps later, and a convenient microphone placement later, O'Neil stood poised to enter the GCW ring for the first time in years. Caldera: Thank God you did that. I didn't want to have to gladhand your introduction and say things I really don't think I mean. O'Neil smiles as the crowd boos, though a segment cheers. He climbs into the ring and smiles at his nemesis and former boss. O'Neil: What, no plaque? No discount card? No trophy signifying my place in GCW history as the wrestler who's had the most impact even today? C'mon, Caldy. I figured I'd at least get a coupon for half off a dinner down the street, or something. Smirking, Caldera raises his arm to speak, as O'Neil crosses to him, ripping the mic from his hand. O'Neil: Hang on. I'm going to let you finish here, Steven, but, I gotta say something first. The fans applaud as Caldera's face turns red. O'Neil holds a hand out to the fans, and takes a deep breath. O'Neil: I left this company in 2002, and, everyday I haven't thought about anything else. When I wrestled Angelo Deville, I thought of GCW. When I counted the ring lights as Silvio Fucking Rochelli pinned my ass to the ground and I did the jay-oh-bee, I thought of GCW. When I sat at home, convinced my career was over, I thought of GCW, and goddamnit, I missed each and every one of you with all my heart and soul! The fans cheer, a loud, explosive prolonged cheer as O'Neil soaks it all in. O'Neil: Damn, that feels good. I may have acted as though I never wanted you to cheer for me, but, the fact is I did. And while you booed me, I know each of you respected me. I know each of you respected me, even you Caldera, and so... I thank you. O'Neil reaches a hand out to Caldera, and the fans cheer again. Caldera raises an eyebrow, looks at the hand extended to him and shakes his head. O'Neil: Man to man, Caldera. I'm thanking you. Sincerely. Truely. No backstabbing. The fans cheer as Caldera looks side to side and then begins to extend a hand, quickly ripping the microphone from the hand of the former Dark Messiah to a tremendous chorus of boos. Jason O'Neil looks dumbstruck as Caldera turns away. Caldera: Are you kidding? I can't trust you. You know that. I know that. I'll reach out, and you'll lock me into that pretzel move you do, and I'll be embarassed. No. Not happening. O'Neil: I mean it. Thank you. O'Neil advances a step forward while Caldera takes two back into the corner, ready to leave the ring. O'Neil: I want to bury the hatchet, Steven. All the years of hating this company was just me hating you. It was never about GCW. It was all about you. About my seething hatred for you. About how I'd love to grab your neck, and squeeze until your head pops. The fans cheer again as Caldera grabs his collar, pulling it just a bit looser, clearly feeling what O'Neil is describing. O'Neil smiles as he continues. O'Neil: But, I'm over it now. Because you gave me the greatest fifteen months of my career, and while I made millions for being Deville's whipping boy, and my stock was never higher, without you, without GCW, I'd be nothing. I'd do anything for this company, Steven. ANYTHING. Because these fans deserve that. They saw me at my worst, ready to destroy every superstar in the back. And now? They deserve to see me at my best. Caldera smiles, taking a quick step forward, catching O'Neil off guard as he retreats two steps in shock at the sudden mood change. Caldera: Anything? Truely, anything? O'Neil: Anything. Caldera holds his hand out, as O'Neil looks around. He backs off and moves away from the President of GCW as the fans cheer. Caldera: Anything, eh? What if I gave you what you've wanted for years now? What if I handed you a GCW title shot and the paycheck that goes along with it. O'Neil could barely contain his smile as he let go and looked around the ring as the fans stood bewildered why the president of GCW would hand a title shot to his biggest rival, without so much as a consideration to the other people in the company. Caldera: Drop the act, O'Neil. I know how much cash it took to get you here tonight, three months after you were inducted. I know you've got a price. You don't care about these fans. You care about money. And I've got it. I'll pay you. All I ask is that you destroy Andy Murray in the process. O'Neil laughed as he bowed his head. O'Neil: That's what it's about? You've exhausted the Establishment, and now, you're going to your biggest enemy to get done what you couldn't? Maybe it's about time we cut the bull and get down to brass tacks, Stevie. The fans cheered a bit, but, most looked on, wondering what would be said next. O'Neil: Face it: We know each other too well. The fact that I like money is moot here. Yeah, I wanted to get paid and paid well. I deserve it. I helped put this company on the map. I'd be lying if I said I didn't deserve to live just a little better for the next few months because you decided to put my name in the executive bathroom at Caldera Enterprises. Laughter as O'Neil backs away from Caldera, and into the corner. Caldera fastens the button on his double breasted jacket, and waits. O'Neil: And now, you want me to take on Andy Murray, and you're willing to let me have what should have been mine seven years ago? What's the catch? A rusty, weak, skinny Jason O'Neil faces the winner of Dangerous Games, who'll become the "chosen" one of Steven Caldera, and I'll get embarassed on National TV, put my tail between my legs and go home? Caldera: You know me too well, Jason. That's exactly what I'm counting on. I'm counting on your penchant for high places to come into play as you splatter Andy Murray across a parking lot somewhere. And then, while you recover, I put that belt that somehow winds up on your shoulders off of it, and onto a more deserving champion. O'Neil nods as the fans cheer. O'Neil: You're on. O'Neil reaches out and shakes Caldera's unexpecting hand, and thrusts his arms into the air as "Phantom Green" plays once more. Bryan: Looks like we have a main event for Dangerous Games, David! Yale: Certainly do! Jason O'Neil vs. Andy Murray? One has to wonder what kind of rust O'Neil has on his body! Bryan: This is definitely a different looking Jason O'Neil than we've ever seen, who is clearly thirty to forty pounds lighter than he'd been while he was in GCW last. Does he adapt a different style, and has he even wrestled since 2006? Yale: We'll find out at Dangerous Games, for sure! O'Neil raises his arms into the air once more as Caldera stands bewildered in the ring once more. ---- Killswitch vs. Bishop Steele Due to technical difficulties caused by Scott Stevens taking a poop in the production truck, this match could not air. ---- Hit the Lights The opening chords of Chromium's 'Ex Amino' echo across the arena, drawing a typically negative reaction from the fans who are not particularly looking forward to seeing SurReal emerge from behind the curtain. SurReal strides out from the back, stopping at the top of the ramp gearing himself up for the contest ahead with his head bowed. Bryan: As far as I’m aware SurReal isn’t competing tonight, so I guess he must have something to say! Yale: SurReal ALWAYS has something to say JB! Bryan: That’s true, the Irishman is certainly not shy of voicing his opinion. Whilst the commentators have been discussing him, SurReal, dressed in a pair of ripped jeans and a GCW t-shirt advertising the upcoming Dangerous Games extravaganza, has made his way down the ramp to ringside and motioned to one of the ring crew for a microphone which is hastily supplied. Yale: So you think he’s going to bitch about his loss at Warpath? Bryan: Dave, I would almost bet my house on it! The crowd’s jeering knocks up a notch as SurReal, now standing tall in the centre of the ring bring the microphone to his mouth, ready to speak. SurReal: So… Chants of “Asshole! Asshole!” ring out, and one fan in the front row, a blatantly overweight young man kitted out in an Andy Murray t-shirt, hurls some personal abuse at SurReal which stops the Irishman mid-sentence. Glaring at that one fan, with his brow raised he slowly brings his hand up to his face, his index finger extended, covering his lips he makes the point that the time has come to shut up. The fan looks sheepish, lowering his head to avoid any eye contact. SurReal: So… I know what you are all thinking. Warpath, I took on three rookies, for someone of my experience, someone of my ability, it’s a no-brainer, SurReal takes the win! And you’re right, I SHOULD have taken the win, there is no excuse for not having my hand raised in victory. There is NEVER an excuse for losing to goddamn rookies! But, I’m not a bitter man… Yale: Uhh… yes he is! SurReal: I won’t begrudge Carson his victory, the kid did good! After our victory against Mortifera he wasn’t exactly impressive, so I sent him a message by Jackhammering him in the center of the ring. The message was to up his game for Warpath, and what can I say? It worked. He was much more impressive and tonight he goes up against the worst rookie this company has to offer… Lucas Quinton. SurReal almost spits out the name of his nemesis such is his distaste for the new GCW Television Champion. Bryan: I guess SurReal is still bearing that grudge stretching right back to Rampage when both Quinton & SurReal made their GCW debuts! SurReal: So tonight Cade Carson, I’m going to ask you to up your game again. You earned your shot at Warpath, now do us all a favor and rip that title away from that worthless sack of monkey sh… The Tribal War Machine never gets to finish his assessment of Lucas Quinton as he is cut off by all the lights in the arena shutting down. The crowd reacts as all wrestling crowds do in this situation with a buzz of excitement and anticipation. They don’t have to wait too long for the purpose to be revealed, as a strangely distorted voice calls out over the PA system. Tick! Tock! Time is almost up Warrior! All your friends are gone, and only enemies remain. Like glass cutting across your skin. The dripping of your blood is your memories leaving your soul. Remember the shards of your past SurReal, those you try hardest to forget will be the ones that cannot be put aside! You have wronged me Warrior, and my retribution is almost upon you. I am neither here, nor there, but soon I shall be revealed, and vengeance shall be mine! How Long? Not Long! Because what you reap, so shall you sow! The lights flicker back on and there are a few screams, and even a few cheers, as the crowd take notice that the ring is now empty. Yet hanging high above it, bound to a massive glass cross, is an unconscious SurReal. Bryan: What the? Yale: Umm, you think maybe someone should try and get him down from there? As they speak ring crew scramble desperatley to find a way to get SurReal down from the cross. Bryan: What just happened Dave? Yale: I really have absolutely no idea JB! ---- Give Me Your Best, I Will Put On A Show Bryan: With Dangerous games right on the heels of everyone in GCW, there's been a real draw to the shows. Yale: Yeah, I mean, tensions are high, and everyone wants to get fired up or the Main Event. Bryan: Some are making enemies, some are making friends, and others, that's right Dave, others are making debuts. We have somewhat of a celebrity with us tonight... Yale: Really? Bryan: Kevin Jacobs is in the audience to give us the scoop. The camera cuts over to Kevin Jacobs sitting next to a familiar face to the entertainment industry. A well built, extremely lean, but childishly smiling guy with a fading black leans in to listen to Jacobs talk. Jacobs: JB, Dave, I am here with someone that many of us know already from WEC, Kris Michaels. The arena goes wild with cheers celebrating the celebrity guest. Michaels, now on the megatron, gets pats and nods from the couple people around him. Jacobs: Kris, you are a very successful MMA fighter, running up and down the middleweight rosters in WEC and remain undefeated in the UFC. I have to ask, what brings you to GCW? Kris licks his lips and smiles, putting his hand up to his face, elbow resting on the arm rest. Michaels: MMA is great! I love doing what I do, and that is why I'm not giving it up, but I feel there is something else out there for me, and that's when I went into negotiations with President Caldera. Jacobs: So, you're going to be stepping into the ring? Michaels: Yes, sir, but I do have to say I'm a little scared. Jacobs: Scared? Michaels: I'm used to a cage, Kevin! Jacobs: Yeah... Michaels: You can fall out of a ring! The crowd, along with the arena, laughs. Michaels: But no, seriously, I'm looking forward to my first match. I want to show the world that MMA fighters have a personality too. I want to show GCW's wrestlers that MMA fighters can scrap with the best. I want to show GCW that Kris Michaels is the next era of GCW... The crowd erupts in cheers but some boos make their way through. Kris looks around and smiles waving in all that the crowd offers. Jacobs: Those are big claims and big words, but you're not the only one to make those claims when first coming in. Look how far Bagwell has come... Michaels: MMA toughens athletes, and it's going to take a lot more than guys that play with dolls, freaks in masks, or wanna be players to take me down! Kevin and others around him begin to laugh as Kris continues. Michaels: I told Caldera, give me your best and I will put on a hell of a show. Jacobs: That is all we can ask for. Thank you Kris and hope to see you around the ranks. Michaels: You will. The camera cuts over to the announcers once again with the backdrop of chearing fans. Yale: I like this guy. Bryan: He's definitely confident but anyone running in a cage on a regular basis is crazy. Yale: He's going to put on a hell of a show... ---- Cheap Labor vs. Mysterious Masked Mexican Bryan: Up next, we've got what is most likely going to be a spectacular display of high-flying expertise! Yale: No doubt about that, J.B. Two of GCW's newest acquisitions will be squaring off against each other in what I predict will be a wild ride. Bryan: The two competitors we speak of are Cheap Labor and M3, or the Mysterious Masked Mexican. From the looks of things, we're ready to get things underway! Strobe lights mounted to the Megatron begin to flicker in unison with the first stacatto notes of "Re-Education (Through Labor)" by Rise Against. As the lead guitar starts undercutting the quarter notes with the song's main riff, the strobe slows to a stop as the drum fill crescendos into a climax. Andrews: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first'¦ Hailing from St. Joseph, Michigan and weighing in tonight at 180 pounds, he is Cheap Labor! BOOM! A wall of yellow pyro erupts across the entrance ramp and Cheap Labor leaps through this barrier, landing squarely on his feet and hitting the ground running. He is racing to the ring like a hooker runs from a baptism. "HEAR THE SOUND OF THE HEARTBEAT POUNDING AWAAAAAAYYYY..." He slides under the bottom rope and is propelled halfway across the ring by his own momentum. He gallivants around the ring, stirring the crowd into a frenzy. Bryan: There's the first of our two competitors for this match. Cheap Labor employs a rather unorthodox style in the ring. Let's see how well it works for him against his opponent, tonight. Yale: M3 has impressed me since he arrived in GCW. His high-flying prowess is second-to-none, and I wouldn't be surprised to see him take this one with relative ease, J.B. As 'After Dark' begins, M3 emerges from backstage in full luchador garb with the feathered head-dress and the cape. At regular intervals he stops to pose and milk the adulation of the crowd, before sliding into the ring and posing at all four corners. Andrews: Introducing next, hailing from Tijuana, Mexico and weighing in tonight at 237 pounds, he is M3, the Mysterious Masked Mexican! After removing the costume, he is left shirtless with colourful long tights on and tassels on both biceps, as well as his boots. Hal Jenkins calls for the bell, and the match gets underway. M3 and Cheap Labor head towards the center of the ring and engage in a collar and elbow tie-up. Labor breaks away and catches M3 in an arm wrench, twisting the arm half over, grinding his forearm into the shoulder joint. Bryan: Cheap Labor with the arm wrench! He's attempting to work the arm early, Dave. Yale: It's a smart move on the part of Cheap Labor. Most of M3's offense is delivered with his arms. So take out the arms, and you've effectively cut his arsenal in half. M3 struggles against the arm wrench, trying to power out of it but to no avail. So he decides to do what he's best at. Making a run towards the nearest corner, he sprints up the turnbuckles and flips does a back flip, landing on his feet before sending Cheap Labor over with an arm drag. Cheap Labor gets back to his feet and rushes at M3 once again, only to be sent flying with another armdrag. Both men get to their feet once again, only for Cheap Labor to nail an armdrag of his own, following through, holding on to the arm and locking it into an armbar. Twisting and grinding that arm against its joints, he applies as much pressure as he possibly can, contorting it in odd positions. Bryan: Cheap Labor goes back to the arm. Yale: He's really cranking on it, J.B.! Stepping over and straddling M3's body, Labor sits down on the Masked Mexican's lower back, pulling back and twisting on the arm in what appears to be a variation of a Camel Clutch. Screaming in agony, M3 attempts to crawl his way over toward the ropes, struggling to get out of the hold. Each time he gets close, Labor drags him back to the middle and sits down on his lower back again, driving his weight forcefully into the masked wrestler's lower back. Bryan: Look at him, Dave! If he keeps this up, he might make short work of M3 tonight. Yale: Maybe, but I happen to know that mat wrestling isn't exactly Cheap Labor's strong suit. Bryan: Who told you that? And how do you know it's true? Yale: I have my sources, and they've never misled me before. Deciding that the ropes are too far out of reach, M3 begins to twist and wiggle beneath his assailant. Trying to slide out from underneath Cheap Labor, he sees that it's futile for him to do so. Using his speed and flexibility to his advantage, he manages to reach up and trap Labor's arms and flip him onto his back for a quick roll-up pin. 1! 2! Kickout! Bryan: What a counter by M3! Cheap Labor didn't see it coming at all! Yale: Maybe we underestimated him a little bit. Either way, that was a Hell of a counter by the Masked Mexican. It looks like this match is starting to turn in his favor. Getting to his feet, M3 takes a moment to shake his arm and get the circulation flowing back through it as Cheap Labor tries to get over the shock of being pinned so quickly. He makes a dash at M3, swinging at him with a clothesline. M3 ducks underneath the blow, taking Labor down with a drop toehold, sending him face-first to the mat. Springing off the nearest ropes, M3 goes for a Lionsault. Labor sees it coming and rolls out of the way, but M3 lands on his feet. Almost as quickly as he does, Labor sweeps his legs out from under him, sending M3 crashing to the mat, landing hard on his back and shoulders, gasping for air and clutching at his chest. Bryan: M3 landed hard on the mat, Dave! Yale: It knocked the wind out of him, and now he's struggling for air. This is Cheap Labor's chance to take over. Cheap Labor quickly leaps towards the ropes, springing off the middle rope with a springboard legdrop, bringing his leg crashing down over M3's chest. Labor goes for the quick pin, hooking the far leg. 1! 2! Kickout! Getting up, he drags M3 to his feet by the mask, clubbing him across the chest with a forearm smash. M3 responds with a stiff knife edge chop that echoes throughout the arena. He then follows it up with another, then another, backing Cheap Labor into the corner. M3 takes a moment to play to the crowd, soaking in the cheers he receives from them before backing up and taking a running start, nailing Labor with a spinning wheel kick that flattens him against the corner. A collective, 'Ooooooooh!', erupts from the crowd as Cheap Labor slumps down a bit, seemingly out of it. M3 hoists Labor up, sitting him on the top rope, catching him with a right hand to the jaw just for good measure. Bryan: What's M3 got in mind now? It can't be good for Cheap Labor, whatever it is. Yale: You took the words right out of my mouth, J.B. Taking a look around the arena, and a deep breath, he leaps to the top rope and springs off, landing on Labor's shoulders and sending him flying towards the middle of the ring with a Top Rope Hurricanrana! The force of impact plus the momentum behind it sends Cheap Labor rolling back up onto his feet, stumbling around as he tries to gather his wits.
