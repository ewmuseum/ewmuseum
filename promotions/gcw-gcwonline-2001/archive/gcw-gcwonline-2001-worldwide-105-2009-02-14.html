The Scottish King of Cool is out of it completely, dazed enough to absorb most of the pain from a hellacious Mayhem move. Bryan, too, has been winded, but looks a lot less worse for wear than Murray. Yale: What a big move that was for Mayhem! Bryan: He could very well have cracked Andy Murray’s skull with that DDT, Dave. Yale: I hope for Murray’s sake that he’s not hurt too badly, but Bryan Mayhem’s name is surely scrawled all over this one now. How can he come back from a move like that? Bryan: We’ve seen this man fall through a steel cell and still pick up the win, but he just got dropped on his head on the concrete floor! If Andy Murray walks out here tonight on his own accord he deserves a medal! ”LET’S GO MURRAY!” CLAP! CLAP! CLAP CLAP CLAP! ”LET’S GO MURRAY!” CLAP! CLAP! CLAP CLAP CLAP! The arena is buzzing, electric, but Andy Murray’s body still lies motionless. Mayhem’s struggling to his feet, clutching onto the bottom of the steel barrier only to have his hands kicked away by a couple of rabid Murray fans. The Lunatic does eventually managed to push up to a knelt position, and after throwing his head back he glances once at his downed opponent and then once at the open casket. A sick grin etches across his features. Yale: Bryan Mayhem’s got that look in his eyes, JB… The former GCW Champion clambers to his feet with the grace of a rhinoceros. He grabs Murray by the back of the neck and pulls him across to the casket. Draping Andy’s body over the edge of the coffin, Mayhem reaches into the bed of the truck and picks out a handful of dirt. He looks to the fans and gives a foreboding thumbs down. Bryan: This is it! It’s over, Ladies and Gentlemen… Yale: Andy Murray, it was nice knowing ya! Kneeling down, Mayhem bundles Murray over into the casket and violently throws the handful of dirt down into his face. After snapping the casket lid closed he hops up onto the back of the truck and grabs the spade. ”BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Jeers rain all around, but Bryan Mayhem does not give a damn. He wraps his hands around the shovel and brings it down into the dirt… Bryan: What a massive win this is going to be for Bryan Mayhem! You wanna talk about a sleeping giant reawakening? Well this is it, folks! Yale: Murray put up a good fight, but the record books are going to show this as a win for Bryan Mayhem and a loss for Andy Murray. The Scot just couldn’t compete tonight with the ruthless streak of The Lunatic… Mayhem pulls a shovel-load of dirt from the pile and hesitates. As the timekeeper readies his bell, wayward specks of dirt and dust float down from the spade and settle on the polished pine surface of the coffin. Bryan: Just do it already. Bryan Mayhem grins… … and then something interesting happens. ”RAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!” Yale: HEY! Bryan: HOW THE…!?! Yale: I CAN’T BELIEVE IT! … and neither can Bryan Mayhem. Bryan: HE’S UP! THE SCOTTISH KING IS UP! Yale: HOW ON EARTH!? Coughing and spluttering, his face red raw, Andy throws the coffin lid open and hands over the side, wiping Mayhem’s handful of dirt from his face. Bryan: I don’t know how that man is even conscious, by sweet Jesus, what a twist! Yale: I thought Andy Murray was dead and gone! Wow! How did he survive that fall?! Pissed-off, Mayhem drops the shovel and jumps down from the truck. With fury in his eyes he grabs Murray and violently yanks him from the coffin. Murray’s now splayed out across the concrete, and Bryan Mayhem picks him up and throws his head between his thighs. Yale: Well it doesn’t look like Murray’s “miraculous comeback” is going to matter after all. Bryan: Mayhem’s setting up for The Oblivion Bomb. Oh dear… Yale: He should’ve stayed in the coffin! Now he’s only gonna find himself subjected to further Mayhem punishment… Bryan: Mayhem reaches under Murray’s stomach, looks to lift him up… … but Murray makes himself dead weight. Bryan can only lift Murray a couple of inches off the ground before he’s forced to let go. Clubbing Andy in the back, Mayhem looks to lift him again, but this time Murray let’s out a primal roar and tosses the gigantic Mayhem over his shoulder, nearly blowing out his back in the process! The crowd ignites… ”RAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!” Bryan: WHAT A MOVE! WHAT A COUNTER! Yale: Andy Murray is almost cockroach-like in his surviveability tonight! Jeesh! Bryan: And now Mayhem’s down! He’s writhing! Can Murray take the advantage! Bryan squirms on the concrete, not entirely finished just yet. Murray arches his back in pain after the pressure of delivery a back body drop to a man 70lbs heavier than he, but doesn’t waste any time in picking Mayhem up and throwing a punch… … which Mayhem catches! Clenching tightly to Murray’s fist, Bryan forces Andy’s hand downwards before stepping forward and headbutting him square in the forehead! Murray reels backwards, turning away from Mayhem, but Bryan’s attempt to pull Murray round by the shoulder is rebutted by a swift elbow shot to Mayhem’s jaw. Bryan: ANOTHER counter! Yale: Neither of these men can maintain their advantage at the moment! It’s as if they’re telegraphing the punches before they’ve even been swung! Mayhem staggers this time, holding his jaw. He looks up. Andy looks up. And for a moment, a second, their eyes meet. There’s a realisation between the two – an understanding, almost – and the moment lingers as if it had been unstuck in time. … and then… Bryan: HEX BREAKER! HEX BREAKER FROM MURRAY! Yale: Where did that come from!? Bryan: An EXPLOSIVE blockbuster from Andy Murray! And Mayhem’s down! Yale: Murray’s hit his finisher, but did you see Bryan Mayhem there? He just… stood there. ”MURRAY! MURRAY! MURRAY!” The chants buzz around inside his head. The Admiral of Awesome looks on at Mayhem, almost trance-like in his concentration on the fallen grappler. He snaps out of it and remembers the task at hand, grabbing Mayhem and with an almighty heave placing him in the casket. Hunched over, breathing heavily, exhausted; Andy Murray kicks down the coffin lid, but not before Bryan Mayhem has voluntarily folded his own arms over his chest… Bryan: … did you see that? Yale: Yes. Yes I did. What’s going on here!? Bryan: I-… I really don’t know, Dave. That was surreal to say the least, but Andy’s on his way to the cab… Still a little dizzy from the earlier DDT, Andy walks around the side of the truck as fast as his ailing legs will allow him. Momentarily he stumbles into the side of the truck but the cheers of the fans are enough to pull him back to life. Soon enough he reaches the front and climbs up the steps. Hanging out of the cab, one hand on an unseen lever, Andy looks over his shoulder one more time before yanking back on the lever. And the bed is raised. And the dirt tumbles. Bryan Mayhem is buried alive. DING! DING! DING! Bryan: It’s over, Ladies and Gentlemen! Andy Murray is your winner! Yale: I can’t believe what just happened though, JB. Andy Murray, still hanging out of the cab, cracks a smile and raises a victorious arm in the air. The top of the mountain is close, and there’s but one man left in his way. The GCW logo, followed soon after by a black screen. Until next week.
