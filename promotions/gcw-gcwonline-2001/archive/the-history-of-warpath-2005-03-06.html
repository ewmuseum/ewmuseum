The History of GCW Warpath Warpath - April 2001 The first ever GCW Pay-Per-View event took place in April of 2001. The show was titled Warpath to honor the eight man, one night tournament to crown the first ever GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Madison Square Garden hosted the event as we saw Thirteen, Paladin, Toad, Toxic, John Reed, Octavian, Steven Caldera and The Jhub battle it out for the biggest prize in sports entertainment. The winner of the tournament would leave the World Champion, while the losers of the semi finals would compete for the right to become the first ever United States Champion. In addition, a legendary feud got started as Mike Hardy and Chameleon went one on one in a historic ladder match for the cruiserweight title!
