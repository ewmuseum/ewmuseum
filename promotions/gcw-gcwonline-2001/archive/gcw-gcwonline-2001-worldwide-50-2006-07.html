GCW WorldWide #50 (Predictions Thread)

16 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Global Championship Wrestling's weekly show hits the half-century mark on WorldWide #50 airing from Vancouver, British Columbia in our neighbour to the north Canada. With the huge PPV Warpath coming to close just a week ago, and events shaking up the roster what will be the aftermath of the event? What will the road to Dangerous Games have in store for Dies Irae, although after wavering has appeared to given a death blow to their nemesis in The Ascension after losing their leader Eva VanLorne via concussion from the hands of Vivica J. Valentine. Is The Ascension no more? There is only one way to find out... tune in and see these great matches: Non Title Singles Match Tessa Windsor vs. Dawn McFarlane Once they were tag partners, now they find themselves on opposite sides of the coin. Fresh after a Crusierweight Championship win over the leader of Dies Irae Shadow, Tessa Windsor looks to continue her good ways against the most dominant stable in GCW by going against the most recent former GCW World Heavyweight Champion Dawn McFarlane. Fresh off her World Title loss, Dawn has a chip on her shoulder and something to prove. Will it be enough to overcome the winning ways of Tessa? Singles Match Johnny Borealis vs. Seymour Almasy After a triumphant return to GCW, Johnny Borealis is looking to show everyone why he was the #1 Contendor to the World Championship before his hiatus from GCW. His opponet? None other than Seymour Almasy, whose very first GCW match was against Johnny Borealis. Things have seem to come full circle, with Almasy now being different man than he was before in Dies Irae... can he repeat his previous outcome? Or will the fresh Borealis show Almasy whose boss. Debut Match Michael Balthazar vs. Dorian Ryan vs. Jacob McKail A triple threat match will be used to showcase one of GCW's hottest rising talents against two of GCW's newest signing. Jacob McKail electrified the Warpath PPV by starting at the beginning of the Television Championship gauntlet and mowing through his competition before he fell short of capturing the title from Danielle Northsky. Michael Balthazar and Dorian Ryan have other plans, plans of making an impact of their own by taking out the break out star in their respective debuts. Can anyone stop Jacob McKail? Hardcore Championship Match Virgil Ambrosi (c) vs. Jayce Bradley After a heartbreaking loss to Blaise McClancy at Warpath with some much needed controversy, Jayce Bradley is looking to past behind him and take a step towards becoming a champion. Dies Irae member and current Hardcore Champion Virgil Ambrosi is fresh off a title win himself at Warpath and has other plans. With the Hardcore Championship on the line anything goes. Can Virgil repeat his current success with a win over a man who has Dies Irae locked in his crosshairs? Singles Match Bryan Mayhem vs Brandon Youngblood Undefeated thus far in his GCW career, Brandon Youngblood appears to be on top of the world holding victories over both the United States Champion Vivica J. Valentine and the leader of Dies Irae Shadow. With people starting to pay attention, can Youngblood continue on his winning ways against a GCW mainstay in Bryan Mayhem... who comes into this match with a chip on his shoulder and nothing to lose? Tag Team Debut Match The Jackson Brothers vs. Firey Passion After recently signing contracts at GCW Headquarters in Columbus, Ohio both these tag teams look eager to bring life to a division that seems to be manhandled by the likes of the Knightmare Brigade. With both teams seeing each other for the first time in their debut matches, and UNITE most certainly looking on, which team will be the first to strike blood in the hungry tag division? TV Title Match Danielle Northsky (c) vs. ??? After making an appearance on Warpath, the masked man and Danielle Northsky cross paths again on WorldWide... but this time for the Television Championship. Who is this masked man? What will happen if he becomes victorious? Debut Match La Sombra vs. Marvin Harris With another PPV come and gone, new blood has once again began to flow through the veins of Global Championship Wrestling looking for their big break. Former RPW and PCW star Marvin Harris joins the GCW roster making his first appearance against Total J Cup break through star La Sombra. With high expectations and a lot of heart both men see this as a must win situation. Who will start their career undefeated? And who will start their career with an uphill climb? The Showstarter Shayne Steele vs. Jordan White In tonight's Showstarter, two familiar faces will be gracing GCW fans with their presence with a return chance with the company. Shayne Steele, who was seemingly released after Warpath has been granted a special exemption by Steven Caldera and will be returning to the GCW roster to face Jordan White in his GCW return to the squared circle. With a new lease on life, who will be hungrier for a win? Who do you believe will win these matches? Which match are you the most looking forward to? Who is your current favorite GCW Superstar after Warpath?
