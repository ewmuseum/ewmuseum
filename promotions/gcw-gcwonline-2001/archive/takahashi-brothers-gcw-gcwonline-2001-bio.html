Tag Team Highlight Moves Elevated jawbreaker-sees one wrestler use an inverted suplex to raise their opponent off the ground and onto the shoulders of their partner behind them. Both wrestlers then drop to a kneeling/sitting position, so that the wrestler at the front can hit the stunner style jawbreaker. Backdrop suplex, inverted DDT combination-This maneuver sees an opponent get pushed upwards in a belly to back suplex lift by one attacking wrestler then as the opponent falls to the mat the second attacking wrestler would apply the inverted headlock forcing the opponent's head into the mat. Little Tokyo-A move in which one wrestler hoists the opponent on his shoulders in the electric chair position, while another wrestler climbs to the top turnbuckle and delivers a flying bulldog on the prone opponent. Oregatokare- when two wrestlers both hit a STO on a single opponent at the same time Trademark Moves Jakked up!- Yoshiyuki hits a devastating yakuza kick to his opponent, only dazing him for a moment. Yutaka runs from a parallel angle hitting a clothesline. Grinded- Yutaka applies a single leg crab on the opponent while Yoshiyuki applies a Fujiwara armbar. (Double submission) Ball and Gag-One wrestler would begin a Russian legsweep as another wrestler hit the opponent with a clothesline, forcing the opponent hard back down to the mat. Famous- one of the wrestlers wrap a forward facing opponent's legs around his waist and apply a gutwrench hold to lift the opponent up off the ground. At this point the other, who is situated on the top turnbuckle would then jump down to hit the opponent with a top-rope leg drop to the back of his exposed head, forcefully driving the opponent's face and body back down to the mat. Entrance Music This Fire Burns ? Killswitch Engage Ring Entrances This fire burns by Killswitch Engage begins to pump through the PA system and immediately the fans jump to their feet and explode in a set of mix reactions as the Artificial Fog begins to thicken just underneath the Mega Tron. With the curtain of mist shielding the audience from seeing what’s behind it, the crowd now plays the waiting game for the Takahashi Brothers Even through the darkest days This fire burns always This fire burns always This is the proving ground Misery begins to rise Turn away from yesterday Tomorrow's in my eyes (Oh!) Yoshiyuki & Yutaka Takahashi emerges from the Clouded Wall and people welcome him with a standing ovation that just sends shivers down his Spine. Yutaka lets off one of his oh so wicked grins before taking a few steps forward lets off a Thunderous Roar to the GCW spectators; who excitedly return the favor, getting rid of any pre-match jitters that he might have. Trying to get in sync with the song, Yoshiyuki and Yutaka stands at the center of the entrance ramp and rocks away to their theme song, waiting for the opening he wanted. I will not be denied in this final hour I will not be denied, this day is mine This passion inside me is burning (Is burning) This passion inside me is burning (Is burning) Feeling that the time is right, Yoshiyuki taps the heel of his boot with his Bokken a few times before placing his right hand in his pocket and Yutaka raises his Kendo Weapon in the air. This is of course the signal to let the Water on either side of the ramp jet Thirty Feet into the air before lightly showering the back of Yuktaka’s and Yoshiyuki's head. As soon as the indoor rain stops, Yoshiyuki raises his head and looks to his followers with a look that expresses the calmer side of rage, this look only lasts for a moment before they sprints down to the ring as fast as they can. Sliding into the Squared Circle, Yoshiyuki shows off his athletic ability by flipping onto his feet before absorbing the audience’s reaction and begins to pace about inside the ring. Yutaka slides in and climbs the turnbuckle. Yoshiyuki gives his Bokken to the official and begins to prepare himself for his match.
