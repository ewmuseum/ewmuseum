WorldWide 11, 4 May 2005

WorldWide 11 Birmingham-Jefferson Convention Center Arena — Birmingham, Alabama ---- WorldWide #11 The lights dim in the arena, and the Birmingham crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on reading "Mr. Danger World Champ" and "Caldera 4 PM" The cameras continue to fly as the words "Birmingham, England" appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue. Bryan- We're sold out in Birmingham for another edition of Global Championship Wrestling's WorldWide! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan flying solo again from another packed house, and this crowd is geared up to see the first title defense by Jason Dread! The World Heavyweight Champion puts his title on the line against the mad scientist of the Praetorian Guard, Mr. Danger! Mike Hardy and The Jhub team up to take on Octavian and Luke Hanson, the US title is on the line again, and much more! Also, we hope to find out a little bit more about what's been going on between Michael Stevens and Mike Hardy, and there have been rumors that there's going to be another huge surprise here tonight! But for now, let's head backstage, where I'm getting word Kevin Jacobs is reporting an pre-show attack! The camera shot switches to the backstage area, apparently just inside the arena from the loading docks. A pair of heavy double doors lie wide open, affording the camera a view of the parking area. Four GCW Security guards and three paramedics are scrambling around the scene, where a tense Kevin Jacobs stands amid the chaos, one hand to his ear in a vain attempt to hear over the commotion. Jacobs- Thanks, JB, I'm at the back entrance where it appears Johnny Lexicon has been attacked just moments before the show went on the air! I've asked around and nobody is sure just yet who was behind this attack, but he's lost some blood and is believed to have a concussion. Lexicon is definitely out of tonight's opening bout, I'm sorry to say, and I'll keep looking for information as the night goes on. Hopefully we can find out who did this. The show heads right back to ringside, where JB looks upset at the scene. Overhead, Rabble Rouser by KMFDM cues up. The crowd buzzes in discussion over the attack. As Rabble Rouser begins hammering into the air, Jonathan Borealis appears on stage, as Bryan attempts to salvage the situation. Bryan- Well, folks, newcomer Jonathan Borealis is on his way to the ring, but he doesn't have an opponent! I guess he hasn't received word about the Lexicon attack yet, and we're probably just going to have a no contest here. Today is Borealis' birthday, folks, and he doesn't have a match to show for it. I apologize for this, folks, but let's just hope Lexicon is all right! Borealis steps into the ring, sensing something amiss. Referee Tim Vale approaches and appears to inform him of the situation. Borealis reacts with anger, pointing an accusing finger at Jenkins. The crowd begins to boo in disappointment over the lost match. Bryan- Well, Borealis doesn't look happy that he doesn't get to wrestle here tonight... Wrestling on WorldWide is a tremendous opportunity to display your talents here in GCW, and he's not going to have that chance here tonight. Suddenly, Tech Nine by Big Bad Wolf hits, and Borealis spins around to the stage. The crowd stands in unison as Trey Tremendous emerges on stage with a tremendous smile on his face. The crowd reacts as Tremendous pats the heavily dented steel chair he has cradled under his arm. As the camera zooms in on the chair, faint traces of fresh blood can be seen on the seat. Tremendous shrugs with a sinister laugh and begins walking to the ring. Bryan- Wait a minute, what's this? Trey Tremendous is on his way to the ring... I'm not sure I understand this! Is Tremendous going to take Lexicon's place here? Borealis actually smiles in the ring, happy to have been given a chance at a match after all. The official pokes his head through the ropes and a ring attendant shouts something to him. The official seems confused but does not attempt to stop Tremendous as he enters the ring. Bryan- Folks, I can see that chair Tremendous is carrying, and it looks like the same damn chair he's been using for weeks! And it's got blood on it. One can only assume that's Johnny Lexicon's blood! I guess we won't have to wait long for our answer to the Lexicon mystery. Trey Tremendous, who has been having his troubles with Lexicon lately, wasn't scheduled to wrestle here tonight, and I guess he took it upon himself to get onto the show! I guess we have a match! Jonathan Borealis vs. Trey Tremendous The bell sounds, and Vale orders Tremendous to lose the chair. Tremendous shakes his head and raises the chair threateningly at Jenkins, who scurries away. But Borealis cuts Tremendous off with a hard right to the head, and the chair falls to the mat. Vale kicks the chair out of the ring as Borealis and Tremendous begin trading shots to the head. Tremendous takes over on the smaller Borealis and throws him into the turnbuckle. Tremendous drives his shoulder into the midsection of Borealis a few times before lifting him off his feet and setting him on the top rope! Borealis quickly goes for a high risk move as he hooks Borealis for a superplex! Borealis quickly counters with a shot to the gut, before shoving Tremendous off to the mat below! Tremendous staggers to his feet after the impact, and Borealis flies off with a second rope cross body! Borealis nails the move and hooks a leg, but Tremendous kicks out at one. Both men rise and Borealis stays on the attack. Borealis nails a European uppercut, staggering Tremendous into the ropes. Borealis sends Tremendous into the ropes and catches him with a flying back elbow! Borealis hoists Tremendous back up and sends him face first into the turnbuckle, before launching him across the ring with a perfectly executed Japanese armdrag! Borealis charges a rising Tremendous, but Tremendous counters with a shoulder block, staggering Borealis. Tremendous charges but Borealis leaps onto his shoulders, taking him over with a hurricanrana! Borealis immediately heads for the top turnbuckle while Tremendous struggles to his feet. Borealis takes off with a huge spinning heel kick off the top, but Tremendous catches him and plants him with a modified powerbomb! The crowd oohs as Tremendous covers, earning only a two count. Tremendous pulls Borealis up and sends him into the turnbuckle. Tremendous nails Borealis with four hard rights to the head, then pauses before the fifth, deciding instead to go with a rake to the eyes. Borealis drops to a knee, but Tremendous hoists him up and delivers a hard snap suplex. Tremendous then heads to the top rope, measuring Borealis as he rises. Borealis staggers right into a moonsault from Tremendous, who hooks a leg after rolling through for another near fall! Tremendous pulls Borealis back up and sets him on the top turnbuckle. Tremendous steps onto the apron and climbs behind Borealis, raising his arms triumphantly to the crowd! The fans respond with a chorus of British boos. Tremendous spins around and goes for a frankensteiner, but Borealis drops him out of midair into a Japanese powerbomb! Tremendous lands hard on his head but Borealis is unable to hold the move for the pin. Both men lie hurt on the mat as Vale begins the ten count. He reaches four before Borealis manages to get to his feet. Borealis wearily pulls himself onto the turnbuckle as Tremendous uses the ropes mid-ring to drag himself up. Tremendous gets to his feet and turns, only to catch a huge cross body from Borealis! They hit the mat with tremendous impact, but Tremendous floats through and counters with the pin on Borealis! Tremendous instantly props his feet on the ropes behind him as Vale makes the count! 1... 2... 3! Borealis can't escape the extra leverage, and Tremendous steals it! Winner: Trey Tremendous The crowd boos as Tremendous slides out of the ring. A furious Borealis leaps to his feet and shouts at Tremendous, who simply grabs his chair and gives it a loving hug. Borealis turns and points at Vale, kicking the ropes in a vain attempt to explain the situation. Vale simply ignores him and steps out of the ring, leaving a furious Borealis alone as the show cuts away from ringside. We cut backstage again, where the EMTs are loading Johnny Lexicon onto the slightly ridiculous English ambulance. Kevin Jacobs waddles through the crowd, moving people aside by girth more than determination. As he approaches the prone Lexicon, another figure breaks through and pushes him to the side. It's clearly Jordan White, Trey's manager, and he gets to Lexicon quickly. Lexicon, holding a red rag over his forehead, looks more angry than hurt. Johnny- What the hell was that?! That kid's your responsibility, Jordan! Jordan- I don't even pay attention to my own problems, you know that. Johnny- Well pay attention to this--I want Trey at Warpath. Jordan- You are an old friend, so I think I can manage something. And, yeah, sorry about the match tonight. I've got you covered. Johnny- I wasn't worried. Jordan- Even better. Jordan turns and cuts back through the crowd, pushing Jacobs out of his way after having used up all the screen time they'd allotted the segment in the first place. The scene fades backstage, and the new GCW Hardcore Champion, Alex Blaq, is seen leaning against a wall in the hallway. His arms are folded across his chest, his left leg crossing over his right. His head is aimed low, but his eyes don't look towards the floor, where the Hardcore Title lays. It seems that he's trying to be relaxed, but it's evident that he is anything but. His eyes follow everyone who walks past him, glaring at them knowing that at any given second that person could turn and attack, thus; Alex was ready to attack. He was ready for anyone to step forth and try to take from him what he had taken from Jhub. Well, he was ready for almost anyone. A door opened, and his head instantly turned towards it. Part of him grew nervous as he read the name plate on the wall -- sure, he would never acknowledge the part of him that did, but nonetheless; it happened -- which stated, "LeStatt Knight." Quickly, Blaq scooped up the championship belt that he had so recklessly thrown to the floor -- the reckless treatment it received in the hands of Alex Blaq was quite evident despite him holding it for only a week -- and rested it against his shoulder. A second later, LeStatt Knight appeared; he was dressed in his wrestling gear, the United States Championship hung from his shoulder gallantly; He more than ready for his match against Antony Livestra. Blaq gained his composer without moving from his "relaxed" position. He straightened up, flipped his head back to push the long bangs from his vision, and held hardcore title close. LeStatt started down the hall towards Blaq. Their eyes locked, and Blaq was ready to strike at any given second. Blaq stepped from the wall, and into the middle of the hallway, centering himself in LeStatt's path. The confrontation was eminent. LeStatt walked at a steady pace, while Alex stood as firm as a brick wall. The hardcore title slipped off Blaq's shoulder, and let out a clank as it hit the ground. Blaq's breaths grew heavy, the glare of those icy blue-grey eyes were fixated on LeStatt Knight, who continued straight at Blaq. Cracking his neck, Alex murmured, "C'mon you sonuvabitch... c'mon." The two were just a few feet away when Knight responded with a calm, "I don't have the time for you." Alex was surprised, almost dumb-struck. It wasn't necessarily the words that struck him, but how calm Knight had spoken it. Before Alex even realized it, Knight was behind him, and on his way towards the guerilla position. Blaq let out a low grow, as his composure regained itself. Looking down, he saw the hardcore title on the floor, shook his head, and kicked at the title, sending it into the wall. "You god-forsaken fool..." the hardcore champion growled, "You'll regret that." ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The camera cuts backstage to see Toxic walking towards his locker room. He arrives at his dressing room and steps inside. Voice - Here comes the ogre to eat up my goats! Toxic looks around the room to see where the voice is coming from. Toxic - I know you're in here Twizz. Where are you? Twizzy - I'll never tell. Toxic's expressionless face doesn't change. Toxic - Where are you? Twizzy - Can't you see me? Or have all those pills made you blind? Toxic - What pills? You're the one carrying the prescription bottle around with you all the time. A small rattle, like the sound of a bottle of pills being pulled out from one's pocket, escapes from somewhere in the room. There's a long pause. Twizzy - Touché, salesman. Toxic - What? Where are you? Twizzy - You're the skilled prodigy, shouldn't you know? Toxic - Where are you? Twizzy - Why don't you come find me? Twisted's reply comes in a more serious and angrier tone than most of the Twistodium fiend's fans are used to hearing from him. Toxic turns over a table, sending his gear flying, and then jerks open a locker door, furiously searching for the addict. Toxic - WHERE ARE YOU? Twizzy - You think I'm not in the same league as you just because of my age? Well I've got Twistodium...what do you have you dirty goat monger? Toxic grins. Toxic - I have enough natural ability and youth that I don't need a bottle of hormones to carry my sorry ass around a ring. Twizzy - I don't need them! I could stop taking them if I wanted to! Toxic - Sure. And then you'd go back to being exactly what you were three years ago...the company door mat. Now...WHERE ARE YOU?! Toxic turns just in time to see Twizzy charging towards him with a steel chair. Twizzy swings the chair but Toxic puts his hands up and rips the chair out of the smaller man's hands. Toxic raises the chair. Twizzy throws his hands over his head and drops to the floor. Toxic simply lowers the chair and laughs. Toxic - Look at you. You're pathetic. You're cowering. Twizzy slowly raises his head. Toxic - You know I thought I would take great pleasure in destroying you. When GCW re-opened all I could think about was getting my hands on you and tearing you apart. But now I don't think I'm even going to waste my time on a washed-up *bleep* like you! Toxic walks back out of the room. Twisted stands up looking furious as the show switches to a close-up of a familiar face. A contemplative looking Mike Hardy stands in a hallway outside his five-star locker room, clad in dress slacks but no shirt, swaying side to side as he strokes the stubble on his chin. Kevin Jacobs stands beside him with a microphone, and begins immediately. The British crowd explodes with a huge mixed reaction, obviously suspicious of Hardy. Jacobs- Mike, lately you've been seen with Michael Stevens each week, and you even teamed with him in the ring last week. You were in a verbal battle with President Caldera, your now former manager, and we want to know just where exactly you stand. Hardy closes his eyes, thinking deeply to himself. Jacobs waits patiently for a few moments, before starting to feel awkward at the silence. He slowly pulls the microphone back and tries again. Jacobs- Are you officially hiring Michael Stevens as your manager? Again Jacobs offers Hardy the microphone, and again Hardy remains silent. Jacobs sighs, letting an embarrassed chuckle slip out as he glances nervously at the camera. Jacobs- Uh, Mike, we're live here, and the people want to know what your thoughts are on your future. This seems to snap Hardy out of it, and Hardy answers with a tense voice. Hardy- My future? That's easy. I'm going to be heavyweight champion of the world. It's only a matter of time before it happens. But I've learned lately that you don't always have to wait for things to come to you. Sometimes you have to take them. Hardy turns and opens the door to his locker room, pushing out of the scene. Jacobs manages to slip his foot in the doorway, stopping it inches before it slams shut. Jacobs- Mike, just tell me one more thing... Is Michael Stevens your new teacher? Hardy scoffs, smiling at Jacobs. Hardy- Who said I need a teacher? With that Hardy nudges Jacobs away and closes the door. A confused and embarrassed Jacobs motions to the camera, and it cuts back to the ring. Bryan- Well, fans, the Mike Hardy saga continues, and we're certainly hoping for more answers than that. I have heard that President Caldera has been trying to contact Michael Stevens all week, but Stevens has not returned his calls. Caldera will try to have a word with Stevens tonight, but I can't imagine Stevens will go through with that. Hopefully we'll find out just what the hell he's thinking by the time this night is over. United States Title Match LeStatt Knight vs. Antony Livestra w/ Rocco Diaz MAMBO!! The Mambo Kings enter first, waving their Tag Team Title Belts in the air furiously, taunting the crowd on the way down. Diaz pats Livestra on the back reassuringly, as the confident tag champions enter the ring. O'Fortuna hits next and the lights dim. The Birmingham crowd explodes as the United States Champion LeStatt Knight emerges on stage. Knight slowly stalks to the ring with a sinister smile on his face, causing the Mambo Kings to huddle in apprehension. Knight reaches the ring and slides in, where he is promptly attacked by both tag champions! The crowd boos as referee Josh Briggs struggles to force Diaz out of the ring. Diaz and Livestra stomp away on the US champ, who powers his way to his feet while covering up beneath the assault. Livestra and Diaz force him into the corner and continue to wail away with hard stomps and fists to the face. The crowd continues to boo as Briggs grabs Diaz around the waist in an attempt to drag him away, but Diaz turns and shoves Briggs off before going back to the attack! Diaz and Livestra together whips Knight to the other turnbuckle, and Livestra drops to all fours in front of him. Diaz charges from his corner and leaps off Livestra's back with a high leg clothesline! But Knight catches him on the way in and unceremoniously dumps him over the top rope to the floor, where Diaz hits his head on the steel steps on the way down! The crowd explodes as Livestra gets to his feet, and Briggs calls for the bell. Knight charges Livestra and crushes him with a clothesline! Livestra staggers back to his feet and charges with a clothesline of his own, but Knight sidesteps him with a drop toe hold, and Livestra lands over the second rope! He bounces right back up and spins around, only to catch a big DDT from the champ! Knight pulls Livestra back up and whips him hard into the turnbuckle. Knight then hoists Livestra into a seated position on the top, and climbs the turnbuckle! The crowd begins to buzz as Knight hooks Livestra on the top rope, looking out confidently to the frenzied crowd. Diaz suddenly appears on the apron again and reaches for Knight in an attempt to stop him, but Knight dispatches him with a headbutt between the eyes! Diaz falls to the apron and Knight drops back, planting Livestra with a top rope Knight Fall! The crowd explodes as Knight furiously hooks a leg and nods his head to the tune of the three count! Easy win for the US Champion, who retains yet again! Winner: LeStatt Knight Knight gets to his feet and is quickly handed his United States Title belt. He raises it to the crowd, but Alex Blaq appears out of nowhere! Blaq takes a swing with a steel chair and flattens Knight, causing the crowd to erupt in boos! Blaq throws the chair to the canvas and begins screaming at the fallen US champ as Shadow on the Sun cues up. Blaq looks down and sees the US title belt lying next to night. He picks the title up and slowly raises it over his head, before gingerly resting the belt over Knight's motionless midsection. Blaq begins heading to the back as Knight pulls himself up. He staggers into the corner and leans out, glaring furiously at Blaq. Blaq sneers and motions to his waist, calling for the US title. From behind his cascading black hair, Knight's eyes seem to glow a fiery red, and a sinister grin comes over his face... Michael Stevens stands outside side a door for a moment. He seems to be pulling himself together really quickly, before finally taking a deep breath and throwing open the door, and stepping inside. Steven Caldera sits behind his desk, doing some paper work. A smile crosses Michael's face as he looks down at the boss. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens: Hey how's it going Sit-N-Spin? Caldera looks up from his work, glaring angrily at Stevens. Caldera- Mr. Stevens. So good of you to drop by. I've only been calling you all week. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Ha-ha, we don't have to be that formal Wheelie, come on. We're buddies, remember the good old Establishment days? Steven Caldera: How could I forget? Come in, sit down, we have some things to discuss. For starters, we can talk about how you've gone behind my back and tried to corrupt my student. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : I think I'll stand, after all someone's got to be able to in this office. And hey I didn't try and corrupt anyone, I was just teaching the kid some of the harsh truths about this business you neglected to tell him about. Steven Caldera: Harsh truths, eh? Such as how it's real hard to win when your "buddy" pays off referees to fast count you out of the match? Yeah, I know all about that, "buddy." Don't for one second think you can out-manipulate me, because that's a battle you can't win. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens: I have no idea what you're talking about. See that's the problem with you Steven, you're paranoid. You think everyone's out to get you, and your friends. I've been teaching Hardy, because frankly the kid needed someone with a back bone to tell him what's what, not some lame ass who can't even stand on his own two feet and look someone in the eye he's making insane accusations at. Caldera suddenly leaps from his seat, looking quite healthy and agile. With shocking force, Caldera turns his table over, shoving it out of his way, and he leaps right into Stevens' face. Stevens takes a step back, and chuckles. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Why Lt. Dan, you got magic legs. Steven Caldera: All right, you son of a bitch, listen and listen good. I don't know what you're up to, but I'm going to get to the bottom of it. I know you all too well, Spitty, and you'd be sorely mistaken if you assume I'm not going to figure you out. I don't know what you want with Hardy, but whatever it is, it isn't good. But I've got two legs back under me again, "buddy," and that means you can't sneak around the building hoping I don't see you. I'm watching you, Stevens, watching you like a hawk. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Oh, I'm frightened of the big bad boss man. Listen Steve, I like the kid, I think he's got talent, that's all I want from him. I don't see any reason why we can't both mentor the kid, I mean really, once upon a time you actually thought I wasn't so bad. Steven Caldera: Sure, you were the chosen one. You were the golden child. You had everything I thought I wanted in a world champion. Brains, talent, but more than anything a willingness to do anything to win. To throw the rules out the window and act without the slightest thought given to right and wrong. But I've finally grown up, Mike, I've learned that I'm not a wrestler any more, I'm a promoter. And that means I need a star that has something you never did; integrity. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Who needs integrity when you're the king of the world Steven? Honestly, who cares how you win anything? No matter what happens it all goes down in the record books as a win. You used to know that, but now you're just pathetic. Feeding that poor kid a line of bullshit that would make a maggot gag. Integrity, I don't think so Steven, I think you're just using the kid to relive your glory days through rose colored glasses. Steven Caldera: You know, it's people like you that are ruining this business, Mike. You throw the rules aside and you trample all over the foundation of wrestling itself. You're the type of person that cheapens what it means to wrestle. Every time you pay off a referee, every time you sneak a chair shot behind the referee's back, you cheapen this company a little bit more. Well, if that's the kind of person you want to be, fine with me. But that's not the kind of person Mike Hardy is, though he may not see it yet. You're a disgrace, Michael Stevens, and I don't know what to say other than I'm glad you never won the world title, because it would have been stained forever. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : I'm a disgrace? I'm only doing everything you ever taught me Steven. You turned your back on me a long time ago, left me alone to figure out the rest of this world. Whether you want to admit it or not Steven, you helped make me this way, if I'm a disgrace it's because of your teachings. Now I see why you never called me to come back, it was because you couldn't stand to see your own handy work in action anymore. Octavian was already a monster when you got ahold of him, but you helped turn me into this. So yeah Steven whatever, watch me like a hawk, I don't care, I'll keep doing what I'm doing. I'm a champion and you know it. Stevens turns starts walking out, just as Michael gets to the door and opens it, he turns his head to look over his shoulder Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Oh yeah, Steven, one last thing. You really care about the kid don't you? Steven Caldera: I care about doing the right thing. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : But seriously deep down, you like the kid, maybe like the son you've never had. Caldera is silent, staring curiously at Stevens. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Well, yes or no, just tell me alright? Steven Caldera: Yes. Michael "Spitfire" Stevens : Thought so... Michael heaves something of a heavy sigh, and walks out the door, closing it quietly behind him. After the door closes Caldera raises an eyebrow, his anger fading to total confusion. With an exhausted sigh he turns to see his desk leaning against the wall. The cameras depart as Caldera tends to the mess, trying to gather himself... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Junkie Man ran into the office of Steven Caldera like a madman on a mission to see his boss who can do things for him, special things. He was shaking wildly and looked to be as energetic as a cheetah with a slowly exploding time bomb in its cerebral cortex. El Drug-O talked quickly in a speech that no one can understand as it sounded like he had marbles in his mouth. Twisted - Callie, ih neeg uhmash vitch Tuhcish! Caldera looks at the drug addict who lives in his office at GCW headquarters. Steven Caldera - What did you say? The Co-Dependent spit out what was in his mouth, which ended up being about a bottle and half worth of Twistodium pills. He then screamed the previous statement. Twisted - Caldy, I need a match with Toxic! Steven Caldera - Shhh... inside voice. Quieter: Twisted - I need it really badly! Why not have a stellar grudge match at Warpath? Twizzy thought for a moment. Twisted - Because people could get hurt? Steven Caldera - It was a rhetorical question. Anyway, so the match is on? Steven Caldera - That it is. Toxic vs. Twisted at Warpath. TnT. The Addict jumped into the air with joy like in those old Toyota commercials from the mid-80s where they would scream, "Oh, what a feeling!" Twisted - Oh, what a feeling! See? Twisted - Goat... is... Good! He ran up to Steven, grabbed his head, and took a large whiff of his boss' hair. Twisted - Smells like home. Then he ran out the door. Steven Caldera - He will be the death of me if Toxic isn't the death of him. The Jhub & Mike Hardy vs. Octavian & Luke Hanson Might of Rome hits, and the British crowd stands in unison. Octavian and Luke Hanson appear on stage, looking massive and confident. The duo swagger towards the ring, as Octavian pauses to point out a fan to Hanson, who joins in laughing. The two continue to the ring and step inside, taunting the crowd along the way. My Own Summer hits next and the crowd explodes for The Jhub! The former hardcore champion emerges and heads to the ring, walking with a purpose. The Jhub seems unusually serious tonight as he slides into the ring and stares down Octavian and Hanson. The staredown is broken by The Hand That Feeds and Mike Hardy! Hardy bounds down the ramp with a smile on his face, looking cocky and brash. Hardy slides into the ring and poses for the crowd on the turnbuckle, and a tremendous mixed reaction continues. Hardy hops down and looks at The Jhub, who returns a suspicious glance. The Jhub steps out of the ring to the apron, allowing Hardy to start the match. The bell sounds and Octavian turns to his corner, leaving Hardy alone with Hanson! Hanson smiles and the two walk to the center of the ring. The crowd buzzes as Hanson looks down at Hardy, laughing heartily. Hanson suddenly raises his arms into the air, calling of a test of strength, but knowing his hands are well out of reach for Hardy. The crowd boos as Hardy raises his arms but finds himself well short. Hanson laughs again as Hardy looks upward, looking for a way to lock up. Finally, Hardy cuts off Hanson's laughter with a hard boot to the gut! The crowd cheers as Hardy begins hammering a doubled over Hanson with fists to the skull, but Hanson cuts him down with a headbutt. Hardy springs back up from a knee to nail Hanson with a dropkick to the thigh. Hanson swings a vile clothesline at Hardy, but Hardy ducks and nails another dropkick to the back of Hanson's left knee! Hanson drops to a kneeling position and Hardy nails three more hard rights before hitting the ropes. Hardy bounces back and nails a bulldog on the kneeling Hanson, and the crowd erupts! Hanson groggily pulls himself back up as Hardy and The Jhub exchange another suspicious glance. Hardy turns back to Hanson and charges with another bulldog, but this time Hanson holds him in midair! Hanson rises and leaps off the mat, dropping back and driving Hardy into a vicious back suplex! The ring trembles under the impact and Hanson gets to his feet, smiling once again. Hardy reaches out for The Jhub, but Hanson grabs him by the knee and effortlessly pulls him back into his corner. Hanson pulls Hardy up and smirks as Hardy plants an elbow in his gut. Hardy fires off two more low elbows, but Hanson simply cuts him down with an elbow of his own to the back of the head. I've grown tired of all your stupid games that you play, and how you dismiss me as being a man who doesn't uphold to his words! Do you think I was ****ing joking to you when I said I would put you through a worse hell than I did that waste of a life Tristain Mayhem?! Do you honestly believe I wasn't serious?! I was dead serious, Jason. It's too bad it took all of this to happen for you to finally figure it out. I'm not totally heartless though Jason...I have a proposition for you. The crowd lessens it's volume once again, attuning in to what Octavian has to say. Octavian - Everything that I've just shown that I've taken from you I can give back...it's a very easy thing to do. In return however, you will walk into Warpath and out Coliseum Cage Match and hand over the GCW World Title to me. When that happens, you will not show your face in GCW again for three months thereafter. When you do that for me, I can give back your entire world. So now you're faced with a decision - keep the title that you struggled and worked your life to obtain just how you've wanted it and sacrifice everything up until that point, or give up this piece of gold and leave it for the real men to hold in esteem. Only then can you have your life and possessions back to the way it is. Your future depends entirely upon you, Jason...and you have a very limited time to make a decision on what you want to keep. The beauty of all this is...no matter what happens, I win. Gaius Octavian...always...wins. And THAT, Jason...is EXACTLY...what you dread! Octavian drops the microphone and drops the World title down onto the broken and bleeding body of Dread in one swift move. "Might of Rome" plays all over the arena as Octavian slowly departs the scene of his crime, no one is left in the arena sitting, as everyone chants in unison "Octy sucks!" It only fuels his glee, as Octavian makes his way up to the ring ramp, pausing at the top to turn towards the ring and raise both his arms in triumph. Octavian lets out a feral scream as the last show we are treated to is that of an overhead shot inside the ring, where inside lays a broken chair, a bloodied sledgehammer, and a broken and bloodied Jason Dread, with the blood-smeared World Title draped over his lifeless body.
