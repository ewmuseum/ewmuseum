Kid Cool Biography Real Name Francis Nicknames Cool, Kid Hometown Boston, MA Birthday 14th May 1982 Height 6'1 Weight 209lbs Biography In May of 1982 a young baby was brought into this world by a loving mother and father, however things arn't always what they seem to be. The baby was quickly taken away to be looked after and the father quickly left the hospital to be back with his wife 60miles away, and the mother was left alone with the baby. At the age of 6months the child was put up for adoption and was taken into care, he was always known as baby until his foster parents named him differently. Named Frank Eriks the young baby quickly developed into a talkative toddler, curiousity being his way of life, as far as I remember that curiousity got Frank into more trouble than I dare think about. At school Frank was a likeable child, his quick mouth got him into more trouble than most but he did his work got his grades to an average standard. Watching Television at home Frank followed many of the great starts, Ultimate Warrior, Denrol, Kyle Niker. He loved the whole concept of wrestling. There were so many great matches to watch and awe over. One of the most memorable matches was during the time of the WWA he forgets who was there but it truley was a memorable day. With no education, Frank acquired many jobs never getting anywhere in life, so with a deep breath he found the WWA through a friend. Frank took a step into a world that weirdly seemed familiar yet new to him, he signed up for the WWA academy and within a week he was accepted and began training. Now its not like the other wrestlers hated Frank, they just found him really difficult to work with and he annoyed the hell out of them with his constant story telling and over enthusiasm. You can't blame the kid for being excited following his dream right. His style resembled a previous WWA wrestler and to get him out of thier hair, WWA trainers set up a promotion match between him and another WWA rookie superstar, of course it was setup so that Frank would be unleashed on the WWA. As a gentle screw you to complete the deal they amended Frank Eriks details ever so slightly, they have now changed his name to Francis Erik Schon. Frank doesn't get the joke but everyone else does. Kid Cool was born. Kid Cool is exactly what the name says, a kid, he is not mature for his age he is a moody, egotistical and far too many hormones. Now don't get my wrong the Kid can have a sensible side to his personality, we've just yet to see it. The Kid can talk, and he can talk for the whole of the U.S he goes on about stories from his youthful past. Anyway that pretty much sums him up. So how does a kid who has no to little talent end up being a superstar of the GCW? Easy its all to do with being fired by the WWA and annoyance. Other Feds WWA, FUSE Strengths Taking Hits Weaknesses Taking too many hits Wrestling Style No Wrestling Ability Strategy Kid Cool would usually run from a fight rather than actually wrestle. He has little to no strength and about as much agility as a pig. The thing with Francis however is luck... He is one lucky SOB and when the gods favour him, nothing can go wrong! Special Moves Any wrestling Move Cool pulls off is Special! Entrance Music Journey - Don't stop Believing Ring Entrances As the first few lyrics of "Don't stop believing" hit the PA system the fans turn their attention to the entrance awaiting Kid Cool. Cool is pushed out from the back much to his annoyance and looks bemused at the fans cheering and shouting jeers. He smiles meekly as he looks at the ring ahead of him and gulps. He walks slowly and finally reaches the ring, sliding in and begins to complain to the referee about some injury he sustained during the walkthrough to the ring. The referee shakes his head and orders Cool to his corner, begrudgingly Cool obeys.
