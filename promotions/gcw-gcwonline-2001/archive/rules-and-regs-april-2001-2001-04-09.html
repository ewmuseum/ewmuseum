Rules and Regs (April 2001)

9 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The federation’s own rulebook at its opening: how to apply, how roleplays are scored and how titles change hands.
GCW Rules and Regs

1. Applying- To apply, click on either the Single application or the tag application, and fill our the required information. Do not roleplay until you have received a confirmation e-mail from the president. If accepted, you will be added to the roster after your first roleplay. This is done due to the overwhelming number of people who apply but don't follow through. Do not apply unless you intend on becoming a part of GCW. Also, we promote originality and creativity in GCW, so we do not accept real characters. Only original characters are accepted. Being original goes way beyond just your name. If you have an original name, but you're music is Limp Bizkit and your moves are a chokeslam and the Last Ride, you will not be accepted. Music should relate to your character; don't choose music just because it's 'cool'. Choose your moves wisely; be original, and do not rip off a real wrestler in any aspect of your character. Keep your wrestler's size within reason, as well. A 7'5 wrestler is a bit extreme. Be practical.

2. Content- We don't mind profanity or sexual comments in roleplays. However, refrain from using racial slurs. While profanity and sexuality is accepted (to an extent), excessive use of them shows lack of imagination and will only detract from the roleplay. Do not use catchphrases that are used by real wrestlers. This will not be accepted.

3. Roleplay length and frequency- Roleplays have no length minimum or maximum. However, a 10 line roleplay usually won't get the job done. Make roleplays long enough to get the point across. However, if a roleplay becomes too legthy, it becomes boring and tedious, and will not be enjoyed. Find a happy medium. Roleplay as often or as little as you want, but do not fall off the face of the earth. If you need time off, e-mail President Caldera to inform him, and you will be written out of the storylines. Also, do not roleplay too often. . . After three roleplays a day, you lose originality, and become more of a nuisance than anything.

4. Storylines (Creative Input)- President Caldera enjoys creating exciting and intricate storlyines to keep GCW interesting. He is always open to suggestions. While your ideas might not be used, you never need to hesitate to pitch ideas.

5. Winners and Losers- Winners and losers of matches are based on involvement and quality of roleplays. On occasion, a winner might be decided with consideration of a storlyine, but if this contradicts the quality of the roleplaying, President Caldera will contact you with the idea first. Nobody wins 'em all. . . Don't apply if you will quit after one loss.

6. Quitting- If you lose interest in GCW or cannot continue, please let President Caldera know at least one week in advance. This gives him the opportunity to write you out of the storylines.

For more info, e-mail President Caldera
