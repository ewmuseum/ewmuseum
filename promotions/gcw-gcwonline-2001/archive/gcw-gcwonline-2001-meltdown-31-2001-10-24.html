Meltdown 31, 24 October 2001

24 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 31 Tacoma Dome — Tacoma, Washington ---- Meltdown #31 (The lights in the arena dim, as the crowd begins to buzz. The Meltdown opening plays, followed by pyro exploding from the stage! The crowd goes crazy as Meltdown begins.) Bryan- Just four days away from Devil’s Night, and things couldn’t be more volatile! Tonight, everyone simply hopes to survive long enough to settle all scores at our next Pay-Per-View this Sunday! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan alongside David Yale… Yale- And we’re ready for an incredible night! I can’t wait! Let’s get right to it, JB! Andrews: Ladies and gentlemen, coming down the aisle... Bryan: Wait a minute! Yale: Look! (Dimensioner and Xcalibur have Blayze in the back. Dimensioner is pounding on the bloody face of Blayze. X picks Blayze up. PILEDRIVER! RIGHT ON THE CEMENT!) Dimensioner: You like that? Blayze: hh....uh...mmm... (D starts stomping on Blayze. X walks over a barrel and pulls out a 2x4. D picks Blayze up and whips him towards X. X Swings! A HOME RUN! Blaze starts to bleed from his head, as he lays motionless) Bryan: Look at that destruction by the Sick Freaks! Yale: Blayze was quiet, almost like he took Dimensioner too lightly! (The Dark Messiah and Twisted are talking off to the side. Twisted's Cell phone rings) Twisted: Hello? Yeah. He's right here. ::puts phone down:: Its for you. TDM: Me? ::takes phone:: Hello?...What the hell do you want?...Well, I would be happy to take a donation. ::pauses:: Thats all? ::pauses:: How much for the job? ::Pauses:: I can do that. ::Pause:: Don’t worry. It will be done. ::hangs up:: Twisted: Who was that? TDM: Someone who is making our dreams...come true. Twisted: You mean...? TDM: Yes. I mean. Bryan: What does that mean? Yale: Who knows? Knowing O'Neil though...it cant be good. Bryan- Well, we’ve started out Meltdown with a backstage assault by the Lost Soul Militia! I guess we should have expected tonight to be like this! (The camera heads backstage once more, and we find Badger Benandanti standing with Kevin Jacobs. The crowd cheers as they spot him on the MegaTron.) Jacobs- Badger, tonight- Badger- Shut up, ya big fat tub of goo! Tonight, I meet Octavian, a man who has accomplished some great things… He’s won tournaments, he’s beaten the best, he’s the world champion… Of course, I did all that a long time ago! This guy thinks he can measure up to the smallest professional athlete in the business? Tonight, Kevin Jacobs, GCW sees the real Badger Benandanti… if ya know what I means. (Badger walks off, leaving Jacobs alone. The camera then fades to commercial.) (Camera cuts back to ringside where Scotty Michaels and The Shocka are standing in the ring. The bell rings and the two men tie up.) Bryan: “And there’s the bell. They tie up and Scotty Michaels breaks the tie up with a thumb to the eyes of Shocka. Michaels follows it up with a few quick jabs. There’s a kick to the gut from Michaels. He hooks Shocka up and takes him down with a nice vertical suplex.” Yale: “And Michaels is really starting off well here.” Bryan: “Michaels sends Shocka off the ropes but it’s reversed and Michaels goes into the ropes. Shocka ducks down and sends Michaels high into the air with a back body drop, but Michaels lands on his feet! Shocka turns around and Michaels delivers a beautiful sidekick!” Yale: “Scotty is really using his agility and speed in this match. There’s a cover.” ONE! TWO! Bryan: “Kick out by Shocka!” Yale: “Michaels is looking impressive.” Bryan: “Michaels sends Shocka into the ropes and catches him with a reverse elbow. Michaels pulls Shocka to his feet. Michaels takes off and hits the ropes and comes back and takes Shocka down with a cross body.” ONE! TWO! TH- Yale: “Shocka just gets his shoulder up.” Bryan: “Michaels delivers a DDT to Shocka. He pulls Shocka to his knees, but Shocka just gave him a low blow. What a cheap shot from Shocka. Shocka pulls Michaels into an inside cradle!” ONE! TWO! THREE! Ding! Ding! Ding! Andrews: “Here is your winner…SHOCKA!!!” (Shocka gets his arm raised and leaves the ring as a furious Scotty Michaels looks on.) Bryan- The Shocka steals one from Michaels here tonight, but I don’t think we’ve seen the last of these two! (The show fades to commercial.) (The show returns, and Toxic and NBK make their way to the ring. Toxic takes over early with a big back body drop followed by a powerslam. Toxic sends NBK into the ropes, and pounds him with a few knife-edge chops. But NBK hits a DDT out of nowhere, scoring a two count. NBK takes over with a big suplex followed by a spinebuster, earning another two count. NBK then delivers a massive German suplex, and covers for yet another two count. NBK seems frustrated that Toxic won’t stay down, and he lifts him back up. NBK sends Toxic into the corner, and follows him in. But Toxic takes NBK down, and slingshots him into the turnbuckle.. and into the referee! Both men are down, until Toxic slowly gets back to his feet. But Luke Hanson runs out from the back, sliding in behind Toxic. Hanson hits Toxic with DeFueler, leaving him out cold. NBK rolls over for the cover, and scores the three count! NBK pins the US champion, in a major upset! Hanson walks to the back to a chorus of boos, as he prepares to meet Toxic in a BARBED WIRE match at Devil’s Night!) (The show returns from commercial, and Paint it Black plays as Twisted and The Dark Messiah make their way to ringside) Andrews: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the Aisle, weighing in at 264lbs, accompanied by The Dark Messiah...TWISSSSSSTED! (boos thunder from the stands, TDM stops and stares at the people booing him, and smiles, and keeps walking. Twisted climbs into the ring, and gives TDM a high five) (The crowd gets on its feet as "Small Hours" by Metallica blares over the PA system. The crowd goes nuts as Tristian Mayhem, comes out with Nanci close behind. He walks slowly to the ring) Andrews: And his challenger, weighing in at 251 lbs. TRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRISTAIIIIIIIIIIIIN...MAYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY-HEMMMMMMMMMMMM Bryan: Wow, listen to these fans-Whats Messiah Doing? Yale: He's blocking the entrance to the ring for Mayhem! What the hell? (Mayhem looks at TDM, and motions for him to move. TDM looks into Mayhems eyes, and moves out of the way. Mayhem looks at him all the way into the ring.) Bryan: TDM just moved out of his way? Is there something going on here? Yale: Maybe Messiah just wanted a photo op! (Twisted and Mayhem stare one another down from each corner of the ring. The ref calls for the match to begin, and Twisted charges right off the bat, but Mayhem easily makes a fool of him right off the bat as well, moving out of the way, and letting him charge the turnbuckle.) Bryan: There's that Cocky, Mayhem Arrogance! Yale: Whatever works for him. (Twisted stops himself, but is clubbed in the back by Mayhem causing him to hit to lose his composure, and he immediately pays for that with his head being driven over and over into the turnbuckle. The ref stops Mayhem, but Mayhem keeps his grip on Twisted's head, dragging him in a head lock to center ring before delivering a bulldog.) Yale: We could have a cover here! Bryan: No way! Its too early! (Twisted looks for an early cover, flipping Mayhem over, and hooking the leg... ) 1... KICKOUT! Mayhem stands up cockily, and yells at The Dark Messiah outside the ring) Bryan: Mayhem has got to forget about the Messiah, and worry about Twisted! Yale: Mayhem can take on both men, blindfolded! Bryan: Well the way these matches have gone recently, anything goes! Yale: Twisted is back up! (Twisted grabs Mayhem up by the head, and pulls his fist back... BUT Mayhem COUNTERS, nailing Twisted with a knee to the gut. Mayhem turns the tides easily with a wave of punches that drives Twisted all the way into the corner. Twisted , too dazed to escape the corner, is driven all the way to the canvas, and Mayhem proceeds to stomp the downed Twisted into the corner some more, and the Ref tries to stop it... 1...2...3...4...fiv...Mayhem stops just in time, and does a cocky walk back to center ring, leaving the lifeless Twisted in the turnbuckle.) Bryan: Mayhem is on FIRE! Yale: Its going to take more than that to keep Twisted down though! (Twisted suddenly springs to life, pulling himself up, charging, and clotheslining Mayhem with force. Mayhem goes down hard, landing pretty vertically on his neck, but shows guts getting right back up. Guts don't mean anything though as he is instantly knocked back down to the mat with a solid right hook. Mayhem lands on his knees, and holds his jaw in pain, but Twisted doesn't give him the chance to stop, quickly getting on him, picking him up, and sending him to the ropes. Yale: Look at Twisted! He is going for more than a win, he wants respect! (Twisted easily back body drops Mayhem using Mayhem's running momentum to sending him pretty far. Mayhem hits the mat hard, landing near the apron, and rolls out of the ring. Twisted gives chase, and the Ref begins the count. 1... Mayhem starts walking avoiding the slow Twisted to slow the momentum down. ... 2... Twisted breaks into a run to catch up with Mayhem ... 3.... Mayhem SUDDENLY TURNS AROUND, and stops Twisted in his tracks with a back elbow, knocking him to the floor. ... 4... He picks Twisted up, and slams his head on the apron... The ref angrily yells at him as he counts... 5... Mayhem follows up by whipping Twisted into the guardrail. ... 6... Moving fast, and ignoring the angry ref, Mayhem picks up Twisted again... 7... Mayhem goes to whip Twisted at the stairs... 8... TWISTED REVERSES!! Mayhem Hits the stairs hard!! He falls to the ground in agony. ... 9... Twisted rolls into the ring as Mayhem fights his way to his feet... te.... NO~! ) Bryan: I don't know who wants it more. Twisted? Or Mayhem? Yale: Im suprised that O'Neil hasn't gotten involved in this. (Mayhem rolls back in at the last second. Twisted quickly makes his move to the rolling Mayhem laying in some boots to his upper torso. Mayhem tries to roll back out, but TWISTED grabs him by the ankle, and drags him back to center ring. Mayhem moves to his back, and kicks Twisted in the face, knocking the pear shaped man to the canvas. Both men are up right away, but Mayhem beats Twisted to the punch, knocking the wind out of him, and lifting the fatty up to deliver a scoop slam. Mayhem goes for the cover, and Ref does his thing... ) Bryan: Mayhem could have it... (1... 2... KICK OUT!) ...but no! it was close Yale: That was more like 2 and 7/8ths there. Twisted is feeling it right about now! (Mayhem mouths off to the Ref a bit, and picks Twisted up, whips him to the ropes. Twisted bounces of the ropes, and comes back to a waiting Mayhem who goes for a clothesline, but Twisted ducks, and bounces off the other ropes coming back at Mayhem with even more speed! BUT MAYHEM CATCHES TWISTED WITH A POWERSLAM!! Bryan: What a Powerslam by Mayhem! (He hooks the leg, and the ref slides to the mat... 1... 2... th...KICKOUT~! ) Yale: OH! Almost! (Mayhem yells at the Ref in frustration, showing him how to do a real 3 count. The ref yells back and Mayhem picks Twisted by the hair ignoring the Ref, and he sets Twisted up for a hanging brainbuster. Mayhem lifts Twisted up, and holds him up there for about 6 seconds before Twisted somehow SLIPS OUT, FALLING TO HIS FEET BEHIND Mayhem !! Mayhem TURNS AROUND...TRASH COMPACTOR!! He turns Mayhem over, and looks for the pin... ) Bryan: This should be all! Listen to these fans boo! (1........ 2............................... ) Yale: THREE!!! (THREE!! NO!! WAIT! KICKOUT!! SD KICKS OUT AT THE LAST POSSIBLE SECOND!) Bryan: Twisted almost had the upset there! Yale: Upset? These are two evenly matched competitors! (Twisted picks up Mayhem and sets him up for a Russian leg sweep... BUT Mayhem COUNTERS OUT OF NOWHERE, TURNING AND NAILING TWISTED ACROSS THE THROAT WITH A SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE THAT HAS MORE FORCE THAN NORMALLY DISPLAYED BY MAYHEM !!!!) Bryan: I think Mayhem knocked Twisted out of his BOOTS on that one! (Mayhem gets to his feet, and drags Twisted up as well. He brings him through the ropes, to the outside apron, and PERFORMS A RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP OF HIS OWN TO THE OUTSIDE FLOOR!! The ref starts to count... ) Yale: They're right in front of us again! (1.... Mayhem rolls on the ground in agony as the fall hurt him as well... 2 ...Twisted lies on the ground, obviously at a loss of breath, and momentarily paralyzed... 3... Mayhem slowing brings himself up... 4.... He picks up Twisted, and rolls him into the ring... 5... He then climbs the apron, and the ref stops counting as Mayhem walks along the apron to the closest turnbuckle. He climbs the turn buckle... AND LEAPS OFF WITH A FLYING ELBOW DROP DIRECTLY INTO THE CENTER OF TWISTED'S CHEST!! ) Bryan: This match has been all Mayhem so far! Twisted should think twice before talking about the Woman of Tristain's Dreams! Yale: Very True! (The Ref slides to the mat, but Mayhem doesn't go for the pin! Instead, he climbs the turn buckle yet a second time! He leaps off, going for a splash... BUT TWISTED MOVES OUT OF THE WAY JUST IN TIME! Mayhem hits the canvas hard, and immediately begins rolling around the ring holding his torso. Twisted slowly makes it off his back... and soon to his feet. He moves over to the downed Mayhem and lays in a few hard boots before picking Mayhem up. ) Bryan: Mayhem went to the well one too many times with that splash, and Twisted took full advantage! Yale: I'll say! Twisted throws Mayhem! (He sends Mayhem to the corner, and follows crushing him between the turn buckle and his shoulder upon impact. Mayhem falls like a wet noodle to the canvas, but Twisted grabs him by the wrist, and lifts him up before he can stop completely and repeats the process.) Yale: Twisted is like a man possessed! (Twisted, with a dazed Mayhem in the corner, lifts Mayhem up to the top turn buckle, and sits him atop of it. He starts to climb up, latching onto Mayhem, and looks like hes setting up for a vertical suplex... BUT BAM! BAM! BAM! Mayhem nails Twisted in the face a few times, almost knocking Twisted down to the mat, but Twisted hangs on. BIG MISTAKE! Mayhem puts Twisted's head between his legs, and lifts him up...SPIKE PILEDRIVER!!!! OH MY GOD TWISTED IS DEAD! TWISTED'S HEAD BOUNCES OFF THE MAT LIKE A POGO STICK) Yale: TWISTED IS DEAD! OH MY GOD! GET THE PARAMEDICS OUT HERE! Bryan: Mayhem wisely goes for the cover! (1~!................. 2~!....................... ) (TDM gets in the ring and hits Mayhem in the back of the head. The Ref calls for the bell. TDM picks Mayhem off Twisted, and kicks him in the stomach. THE REPENTANCE! TDM HIT THE REPENTANCE!) Bryan: What the hell is he doing? Yale: I don't know. (TDM rolls out of the ring, and sets up a table.) Bryan: Oh, no...are we going to see what we did last week? Yale: It sure looks that way. (TDM Punches Mayhem in the head a few times and picks him up. He Puts Mayhem on the top turnbuckle backwards. TDM From behind Grabs Mayhem in a full nelson. TDM Stands up, AND JUMPS TO THE FLOOR!!! RECURRING NIGHTMARE! INTO THE TABLE!!!!!) BRYAN: OH MY GOD! WE NEED HELP OUT HERE! RIGHT NOW! Yale: Mayhem is broken in half, and look at the face of O'Neil!! (TDM stands over Mayhem on the ground, and smiles. He bends down, and makes the sign of the cross with his hand over the head of Mayhem. Twisted comes to, and looks at TDM, then at Mayhem. TDM Gets in the ring, and raises the hand of Twisted) Bryan: This man, is sick. Yale- And hold on a second! What’s this? (The crowd’s already intense boos grow even more as Octavian struts to the ring. With a smile on his face and his title belt over his shoulder, he climbs into the ring. Twisted and O’Neil greet him as he steps through the ropes, and is handed a mike.) Bryan- What the hell is Octavian doing down here?! Octavian: Good. You did what we talked about. TDM: Of course. The sinner, got what he deserved. He had a chance to repent, but... Octavian: Whatever. Here. There's some extra there for you. Go now. TDM: Octavian. Understand something. What I did wasn't for you. Maybe Mayhem will think twice before he sins again. Octavian: Great, good to hear, now excuse me I'm busy. (Octavian starts to walk away, but TDM stands in his way.) Octavian: I said get out of my way. TDM: Someday, Octavian, you too will pay for your sins. The only question to be answered then will be...Can you handle me? Octavian: (laughs in TDM's face) Yeah, sure, kid. Bye now. Bryan: What was that? Octavian paid the Messiah off? Yale: It sure looks that way to me. Unless O'Neil left his wallet laying somewhere, and Octavian, being the good Samaritan he is, gave it back. Bryan: I doubt that. With the money in Octavian's pockets, there is no telling what he can and will do! I don’t know what O’Neil was thinking, but Octavian is still in the ring, as the Militia leaves! Yale- And he’s all alone with Mayhem and Nanci! This can’t be good! Nanci is in the ring checking on Mayhem, but her boyfriend’s out! Octavian- Mayhem, tonight you found out what I can do to you… without even touching you. Now you find out what I can take away from you… (With that, Octavian drops the mike, and grabs hold of Nanci. The crowd boos heatedly as he shouts at Nanci, and bends her over.) Bryan- What- Good God! Octavian is manhandling Nanci! He lifts her up… PILEDRIVER!!! Good Lord! Nanci just got piledriven by Octavian! He could have broken her neck! (The crowd goes ballistic as Octavian stands once more. He holds his world title belt into the air as Might of Rome plays.) Bryan- This is just sick! Fans, we’re being asked to go to commercial, we’ll join you in a few moments! (The show fades to commercial as EMTs rush to the ring.) (Meltdown returns, and James Bryan is shown with a long face.) Bryan- Well, fans, during the break we got an update on Nanci following that terrible attack by the champion… She’s all right, we hear, no serious damage… But Tristain Mayhem is livid. We’ll bring you updates on that situation as the night progresses. (Luke Hanson emerges from the back yet again, Bridget at his side. My Own summer hits, and the reaction is explosive for the returning Jhub. Hanson takes over right away with a big boot followed by a massive scoop slam. Hanson then begins to focus on the Jhub’s knee, using simple yet effective methods such as elbow drops to the knee. The Jhub’s knee is obviously still not 100%, as Hanson dominates. Finally, the Jhub nails a blatant low blow, and fires back on Hanson with some right hands. The Jhub then nails Hanson with a DDT for the first near-fall of the match. However, Hanson takes control back with an amazing back body drop that sends the Jhub about ten feet into the air! Hanson then backs up the turnbuckle, and sets the Jhub up for a DeFueler from the top rope! But Toxic charges out to the ring, and cracks Hanson in the back with a chair! The Jhub then stands up, and hits Hanson with an awesome Downward Spiral from the top rope! The Jhub covers, and is victorious in his return match!!) (After the final break, Octavian and Badger head to the ring for their non-title match. The match is extraordinary. Badger starts things off with a flying headscissors followed by a dropkick. Octavian slides to the floor for a breather, but Badger dives over the top rope to connect with a suicide dive! Badger rolls Octavian back into the ring, and sends him into the ropes for a powerslam! Badger shows his deceptive power, and covers for a two count. Badger sends Octavian into the corner, but Octavian ducks out and locks Badger into an abdominal stretch. Octavian then hooks Badger up and brings him over with a beautiful German suplex with a bridge for a near-fall! But Badger fires back with some hot right hands, sending Octavian down to the mat! Badger ducks down to lift Octavian up, but Octavian pulls Badger down, and maneuvers him into the Roman Rapper! Badger screams in pain, but manages to get to the ropes! Octavian, however, refuses to break the hold! The crowd then erupts in cheers as Tristain Mayhem charges to the ring, taking Octavian down with a bulldog from behind! Octavian rolls out of the ring, as the referee begins his count… Not stopping the match. Badger slowly gets back to his feet, glaring at Mayhem. The referee finally counts to ten, awarding Badger a count-out victory. Meltdown then fades to a close as Octavian and Mayhem stare each other down, and Badger watches on in frustration…..)
