Chris Storm Biography Real Name Scott Drake Nicknames The Rock Superstar, the Last Great Technical Wrestler, the Iron Man Hometown New York City Birthday Fuck Off Height 6'5 Weight 252 Fed Titles Tag Team Titles (April 5th, 2007 - June 6th, 2008) & (February 4th, 2009 - June 21th, 2009) Fed Awards Longest Tag Team Title Reign (Over One Year as Tag Team Champions), Only Two Time Tag Team Champions, Honorable Mention Wrestler of the Year (2007), Handler of the Year (Banned & Exiled~!), Honorable Mention Storyline of the Year (2007) Biography Chris Storm is nuts. Actually, let me rephrase that. Chris Storm is FUCKING nuts. A true sociopath in every sense of the word, Chris Storm is unable to see past the cloud of bullshit he fills his own head with. His belief; everyone is holding him back, everyone must pay, he is damn good enough to do it. Stupid enough to run into any fight head on, smart enough to try and make it at his own pace. He truly is one fucked up individual. One could look back to the parents and wonder what they did wrong. 'Oops, the condom broke' is certainly a good start. The biggest mistake his parents made, though? They allowed Scott Drake to try out for the high school wrestling team. The hype-aggressive sport of high school wrestling was the perfect outlet for Scott's unreasonable anger at the world. He pounded opponents into the mat and pinned one after another until he landed a scholarship to wrestle in college. It was at the college level that his ego started to grow at the nth level. Success yeilded fame, which in turn yeilded girls. Natural talent quickly went to the wayside for booze, women, and a quick score. Aww, poor thing. Not really, the dumb fucker deserved it. It was Junior year when Drake was kicked off the wrestling team for fighting (the coach) and subquentially resulted in the subspension of his scholarship. No sooner there after, Scott dropped out of college. He would never go back to finish. Which means he's now ripped (from the training), angry, and dumb as shit; a dangerous combination. With no education and a horrible attitude problem, there was little hope for Scott Drake. He seemed destined to live out the rest of his days as an angry drunk on the side of the road. Sadly a friend of his pointed him toward professional wrestling. I'm sure every professional wrestling fan who has ever had to endure a Chris Storm promo would gladly kick the piss out of that friend if given the chance. In an odd twist though, despite his poor manners and lack of any social skills, Chris Storm still has managed to gather a close knit of friends in the form of his personal bodyguard Arizona and his fiance Tessa Windsor. Obviously he was quite successful at professional wrestling, otherwise you wouldn't be viewing this, and now the man who calls himself Chris Storm has found his way into the Global Championship Wrestling to make his own legacy as a champion. The fact of the matter is, Chris Storm hasn't entered a promotion yet without making some kind of impact. He was the mecca of the WIW:NG, he proved to be just as effective as a tag team wrestler with his long time girlfriend (and fellow professional wrestler) Tessa Windsor, and he showed how he could do anything to win in RPW. With such an impressive track record for success and a vast pool of pure talent, there isn't much to stand in his way. That is, of course, except for the other twenty-nine superstars who will be planning to do just that. But fuck them. Chris Storm will stomp each one into the goddamn mat, one by one. He's the Rock Superstar, and he's ready to start living like one. Other Feds WIW:NG, PCW, DWO, UWF, RPW, LoC Other Fed Titles WIW Grand Slam Champion & Hall of Fame member. PCW Tag Team Champion & Longest Reigning Tag Champion. RPW First Ever Mid South Champion, PTC Global Champion, & PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion Other Fed Awards TONS~! Strengths Freestyle & Greco-Roman wrestler, countering, reversing Weaknesses Arrogant, loves to talk trash during matches, tends to taunt. Wrestling Style Greco-Roman/Freestyle Strategy Though he is a clown with Banned & Exiled~!, make no mistake about it. Chris Storm is still one of the very best technical wrestlers in the business. He's gone toe to toe with the greatest and held his own every time. When he's in the ropes, he is all business and can just as easily break a jaw as crack a joke. He loves takedowns, looking to score them anytime the opponent is on their feet. He'll keep his weight on top of his opponent when he has them down and try to keep them carrying him. The idea is to wear the opponent down through Olympic style wrestling and suplexes until he can get them ready for the Tombstone Piledriver. He won't do much brawling, it's not his strong suit. Rather he wants to ground you and keep you there. The easiest way to ground you is with his suplexes. The suplexes are special for Chris, given his background in Greco-Roman wrestling. Where most wrestlers simply lift, Chris arcs his body and lifts from his waist. This allows for more impact by creating a higher lift and greater angle of throw. For him, wrestling is a science of motion, mechanics, and delivery. Each move has a clear purpose, every motion helps to fulfill the move to it's fullest. All eyes turn to the entrance ramp where Chris Storm suddenly appears. Wearing his usual; a black Punisher hoodie with matching black leather pants, Chris Storm steps out onto the ramp on his journey toward the ring. Andrews: Making his way to the ring, from New York City, weighing in at 250 pounds. . . CHRIS STORM! Bryan: Yale: His head tilted down, Chris makes his slow descend toward the ring, bouncing from foot to foot. His arms shake at his sides, his neck rolling as he goes. The crowd desperately moves in as close as the guard rails will allow, but Chris doesn't seem to notice them. His body just keeps on moving until he reaches the ring apron. With a mighty leap his sends himself onto the apron with grace that a man his size should not possess. Bryan: Yale: Stepping between the ropes, the crowd finally starts to die down as Storm makes his way toward the far ropes. Upon reaching them he throws both arms up to entice another huge pop from the crowd, again bringing them to their feet. He flips back the hood covering his head and looks out at the sea of people before finally turning his attention to the task at hand.
