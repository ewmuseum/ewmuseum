'Scandalous' Ryan Stephens Biography Real Name Ryan Stephens Nicknames Streak, The Smartest Man in Wrestling, The CEO, The Sultan of Suave Hometown Austin, TX Birthday 8/19/72 Height 6'2 Weight 220 Biography Stephens is the personfication of evil. He's evolved over the years into a very successful business man. There's no limit as to what he will do to others in order to obtain what he wants. That said, he doesn't always want a win in the ring. He brings a calculated indifference to the ring and is usually more focussed on his business ventures. He's smart, coniving and methodical in the ring and is usually a top-notch competitor when he wants to be. He hears all and knows all as he lurks backstage in the affairs of others -- he might be the smartest man in wrestling. The catch? He became this way after he ran off his wife citing her as a liability to his elusive title run in the NWC. Before he was a fiery youngster with all the heart and desire in the world only wanting to make himself a legend. At times he's conflicted because somewhere deep within that fiery youngster burns to get out of his entrapment from within the man in the business suit.
