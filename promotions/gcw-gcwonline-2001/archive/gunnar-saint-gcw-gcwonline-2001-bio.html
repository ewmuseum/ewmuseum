Gunnar Saint Biography Real Name Gunnar Saint Nicknames The Southern Slayer, The Hellhound Hometown Perdition, KS Birthday 10/31/82 Height 6'6 Weight 278lbs. Biography You'll know about me when I feel good and fuckin' ready to tell you. Strengths Brutality, toughness, strength, focus Weaknesses Not technical, headstrong, loner, overzealous Wrestling Style Confederate Ass-kicking Strategy Brutality, pure and simple. He looks to either ground and pound his foe or corner them, so he can pound on them. He can match strength with just about anyone and doesn't stray from the idea that bum-rushing his foe will win him the match. He only goes to the top when he knows things are in control. Speaking of that, he always shoots up to the top in one fluid motion like Sean O'Haire or RVD. He is very agile for a man his size, but rarely showcases it. Finisher Move The 13th Ghost Finisher Description Styles Clash setup, but instead of falling forward, he plunges to his knees, spiking the opponent's head into the mat. Finisher Setup Move Maximum Overdrive Setup Description Spear of massive power and impact. Special Moves Amittyville Horror, Springwood Slasher- Shining Wizard to standing foe in the corner. Crystal Lake Killer- Pumphandle Piledriver. The Shape- A swanton bomb. Right Hand of Doom- A combination of punches ending with a huge right-handed spinning backhand. Other Moves Cenobite- He lifts the foe as if he is going for a vertical suplex or jackhammer. Instead of going into what looks like a powerslam executing a jackhammer, he rotates so the foe comes down on his shoulders like a fireman's carry, as he slams them. Ride of the Horseman- Looks like he is executing a Kryptonite Krunch or Air Raid Crush, but instead of falling to his bottom, he leaps and lands on one knee, slamming the back of the foe's neck across his thigh. The Descent- A Straightjacket falcon arrow. -Standing release tigerbomb -An Arn Anderson like Spinebuster -A standing enziguiri -Head and arm suplex -T-bone suplex -Release northern lights suplex -Anaconda Vice -Samoan Drop (Can be set up in any way possible.) -Sitout Catatonic -Sitout Rock Bottom aka Bronx Bomb -Various punch, kick, elbow, or knee combinations -Judah, look it up so I don't have to explain it. Entrance Music "Won't Back Down" by Fuel Ring Entrances The lights go out and a series of black stitches appear on a black background on the tron. The stitches begin to glow red, as "Won't Back Down" starts up. As the song kicks into high gear, Gunnar comes marching out to the entrance way. His shaggy black hair hidden by the black hood of his sweatshirt, which also casts a shadow over his face. Red strobe lights flicker, as he makes his way to the ring. Gunnar leaps up to the apron and with a quick flick of his head, lowers his hood. Glaring harshly at his opponent the entire time, he slips into the ring and quickly removes his hoodie. A quick tug of the ropes and he is ready for battle.
