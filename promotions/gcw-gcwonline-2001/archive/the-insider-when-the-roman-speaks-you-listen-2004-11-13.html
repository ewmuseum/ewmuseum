When the Roman Speaks, You Listen!

Greetings all! Your first and former World Champion Octavian here. I am writing this article for Ben who suggested I write to you all and explain why exactly I incorporate my nationality into my character and why I am leaving GCW for the next month. Well, first I will talk about Octavian and where I got the idea for him from. See, Eons ago, I was asked to join an E-fed called ACWF, where I met Ben, Danger, Khan, and Jhub. My character was the Rock, and I was so stubborn that I ignored Danger's continued suggestion that I create my own character, that I never won a single match, because my promos were not original. I quit ACWF, and Ben being the cool guy that he is, offered me a position in a little promotion entitled CWU. However, He told me to come up with my own character because he saw potential in me, and so what better character to come up with than an extention of who you are? (with a hell of alot more money!). I thought alot about Italian History (I am 100% for you curious people out there in Internet land and I am damn proud of it) and I came up with the name of Octavian. History knows him better as Augustus Caesar, the First Roman Emperor, and perhaps the greatest ruler of them all. And thus, the superb heel known as Octavian was born from this great man. Now, it just so happens that I quit ACWF after I came home from my very first visit to Rome back in november. Guys, the city is so beautiful and awesome! [detail withheld] Also, the girls are fucking incredible! And this brings me to why I won't be around for a month or so. I am returning to the city I love so much [detail withheld]. Now, alot of you might be thinking; "He's nuts! Why go back so damn soon?" Well the answer is simple people! [detail withheld] [detail withheld] To answer your questions of insanity on my part, all I can say is that you have to go there before you know how wonderful it is. Alot of you might think it is a "Catholic" city, and even though I am [detail withheld], religion is not the motivation behind my love of the city. Whether you are Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant, Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, or any other religion, you will leave Rome with a newfound respect for this once mighty Empire! So, I'm glad to have had the opportunity to discuss this with all of you guys, and remember that Octavian will be back with a vengence, and that Title will be mine again! Never forget the words of Julius Caesar: "Veni, Vidi, Vichi!....I came, I saw, I conquered." Arrivederci Amici! (See ya later friends!)

-Alf aka Octavian
