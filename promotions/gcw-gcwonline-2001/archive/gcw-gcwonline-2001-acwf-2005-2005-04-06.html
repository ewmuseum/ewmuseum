ACWF 2005 Stadio Olympico, Rome, Italy ---- Introduction The GCW logo flashes across the screen, fading quickly to black. Dramatic orchestral music begins to play in the background, and a close up of the GCW World Heavyweight Title belt appears on screen. The shot pans over the shimmering gold belt, as the voice of Steven Caldera begins to narrate. Caldera- To be GCW World Heavyweight Champion is to be the best in the business. It means you have no equal, you have no peer… The video of Octavian locking The Jhub into the Roman Rapper at Warpath in 2001 plays, followed by a shot of him raising the GCW title into the air. Octavian- Being World Champion means you’re the one person everyone else wants to be… Another shot of the GCW title glimpses by, followed by the video from Dangerous Games 2001, as The Jhub pins Rich Rollins for the World Title. Jhub- The GCW World Title is the most valuable thing in my life. It symbolizes having risen to the top of the game, risen above everyone else… Next comes a video of Jason Dread pinning Tristain Mayhem to win the title at Rampage 2002, while his voice appears in the background. Dread- Winning the GCW World Title immortalizes your name, guarantees that you and your legacy will live forever. Finally, the voice of Steven Caldera returns, speaking a little higher and with more intensity now. Caldera- Tonight, six men vie for the most prized possession in all of sports-entertainment. Tonight, six men will do whatever it takes in the world’s most dangerous match to become the Heavyweight Champion of the World. Tonight… at A-C-W-F! The MegaTron is framed by giant steel girders distorted to form the massive letters ACWF! The rest of the stage appears normal, with the long steel ramp leading down towards the ring. The distance between the ring and the first row has been increased, and the entire ring area appears much larger. The fans are packed in the arena and going wild, ready to see a new champion crowned! Toxic lifts Twizzy off the guardrail and hoists him to his shoulder with uncanny strength, and glares out at the anarchy unfolding in the arena. Fans in the front row are held at bay by security as they try to leap out and save their hero. Toxic shouts “FUCK YOU!” to the crowd, and then whips Twizzy down. Toxic drives Twizzy’s head right through the table with a sadistic Toxic Waste, destroying the table and Twizzy! Bryan- OH MY GOD! Toxic Waste through the table! Twizzy is out! I think his head bounced off the concrete! Danger- HAHA! There isn’t enough Twistodium in the world to save Twizzy now! Toxic hasn’t forgotten about his long-running feud with Twizzy, and he’s taken some vengeance against Twizzy here in ACWF! Bryan- Damnit, this isn’t fair! Twizzy could have had this match won, but Toxic has cost him the opportunity to become heavyweight champion! Toxic slowly gets to his feet, favoring his back heavily. He slowly raises his arms into the air in triumph, glaring down at the fallen Twizzy. An army of referees rush to the ring and surround Toxic, ordering him to leave. Toxic snarls at the officials and raises a fist, but thinks better and hops back over the guardrail, leaving through the crowd. Bryan- That’s just plain jealousy right there! Toxic wanted to be in this match, he wanted the shot at the World Title, and he didn’t want Twizzy to get the chance! This miserable son of a bitch just ruined Twizzy’s chances at being world champion! Danger- Toxic may have signed his suspension papers right there, but I don’t think he cares! Toxic wanted his face in the main event, and he wanted to take out Twizzy. And he got just that! The crowd continues its boos, as the cameras watch Toxic exit through the chaotic crowd. The camera switches back to the ring, where The Jhub is crawling towards the platform! The fans’ attention turns back to The Jhub and they begin to cheer as The Jhub rolls himself onto the platform. Octavian and Dread are on their feet but still delirious, and Hardy is crawling towards the ring. Toad still lies unconscious to the side of the forklift, and Twizzy is motionless in the wreckage of the timekeeper’s table. Bryan- My God, these men have destroyed everything at ringside here, chains have been snapped, bodies broken, tables destroyed, all in the name of the GCW World Heavyweight Title! What more can these men do?! Dread turns to Octavian and nails him with a hard right hand. Octavian desperately fires back, then abruptly drops Dread to the floor with a double leg takedown! The crowd erupts even further as Octavian attempts to turn Dread into the Roman Rapper! Bryan- ROMAN RAPPER! Danger- If he gets this on, Dread’s finished! Dread fights the hold, as Octavian leans to his right in an attempt to turn Dread. Dread suddenly flips the other way, kicking out with his legs, forcing Octavian off his feet and over the guardrail! Octavian lands in a thud in the first row, and Dread looks to the ring. The Jhub is on all fours on the platform, trying to gain his bearings. Hardy reaches the chains as The Jhub presses the button, and the forks begin to raise him towards the belt! Dread begins staggering towards the ring as Octavian pulls himself over the guardrail, crawling towards the ring! Bryan- My God, Jason Dread narrowly escaped doom right there, and now Dread, Hardy, and Octavian are heading to the ring, while The Jhub tries to get to the belt! Danger- It’s a race against time here, JB! The Jhub might have it! These men are so exhausted, they can hardly stand! Dread reaches the platform first, followed closely by Hardy. Dread hops onto the platform where The Jhub is still on his knees. Dread begins hammering The Jhub in the skull with hard right hands as Hardy rolls onto the platform. Octavian gets there on all fours, put the platform raises just out of his reach! Octavian crawls to the driver’s seat and stops the forks about halfway up, five feet in the air. He then crawls out towards the back of the forklift, and begins climbing. Bryan- There are four men on this forklift now, and this is getting dangerous! And they’re all getting closer to the title! Danger- But Octavian stopped those forks, and everyone’s out of reach! And Octavian is climbing towards the roof! The Jhub, bleeding from the eyebrow now, stops Dread with a hard shot to the lower abdomen. The Jhub gets to his feet and sees Hardy up on all fours on the edge of the platform. The Jhub kicks Hardy hard in the gut, causing Hardy to drop. The Jhub then turns back to Dread, hitting him with a European uppercut! The Jhub sees Octavian crawling to the roof, and reaches up to meet him! Bryan- Octavian is almost to the roof, and The Jhub is going to meet him! My God, this is intense! Danger- Who’s gonna get the belt, JB!? The Jhub begins pulling himself up, but Dread hops up beside him! The Jhub nails Dread in the face with a few sharp elbows, but Dread slips an arm and a leg behind The Jhub, and drops him with a side Russian legsweep right onto the wooden platform! The crowd gasps as The Jhub painfully rolls off the platform to the floor below! Danger- WHAT A MOVE! Dread just took The Jhub out! Bryan- The Jhub is down and in a bad way, and now we’ve got Octavian on the roof, reaching for the belt, with Dread and Hardy below him! Dread painfully pulls himself back up, and heads for the roof. Octavian sees him coming and drops to a knee, nailing him with a right hand to the skull! The crowd has come completely unglued as Dread fires back, staggering the Roman! Dread fires again, and Octavian drops backward onto the roof. Dread begins pulling himself up, but Hardy suddenly nips up on the platform! Bryan- HARDY! Mike Hardy just nipped up! Danger- WHERE DID THAT COME FROM?! Hardy moves in behind Dread, and hooks his head! The crowd explodes as Hardy flips Dread over for The Waterfall! Dread somehow lands on his feet, and flows right into a devastating DDT on Hardy! The crowd gasps as Hardy hits the platform and rolls off to the pavement. Bryan- MY GOD, Hardy attempted the Waterfall, and Dread countered into a DDT! This is unbelievable! Dread quickly slides onto the roof, where Octavian is waiting. Both men get to their feet and Octavian nails Dread with a right hand! But Dread fires back with all his might, unfazed by Octavian’s blow! Dread begins swinging away with every last ounce of strength, backing Octavian towards the edge of the forklift! Bryan- Dread is on fire here!!! He’s got Octavian reeling! The fans begin leaping up and down in the stands, cheering wildly for Dread! Dread stops and leaps in the air, nailing Octavian with a standing dropkick!!! But Octavian somehow catches Dread’s legs, and instantly turns him over! Danger- ROMAN RAPPER! HE’S GOT IT! Bryan- Octavian has the Roman Rapper locked in on top of the forklift! And there’s nobody left to help Dread! Octavian screams out in victory as Dread flails wildly on the roof. Octavian rears back with all his might as the mixed crowd explodes. Octavian holds Dread in a sickening position, rocking back in the most vile form of the Roman Rapper he’s ever applied. Finally Octavian drops Dread and stands over his broken body, knowing he’s just a few feet from victory! Bryan- Octavian is just below the belt! He’s just a few inches short, but he might just get it! Danger- FOUR TIME CHAMPION! He really is the greatest ever! Octavian reaches up for the belt, but his fingers only lightly graze the gold. Octavian looks down at Dread writhing in pain below. Octavian places a boot on Dread’s back, and uses Dread as a boost! His fingers graze the belt but Dread squirms out, and Octavian falls just short. Angrily, Octavian reaches down and pulls Dread into a bent over position, hooking him up for a powerbomb! Bryan- OH MY GOD! Octavian is going to powerbomb Dread off the side of the forklift! HE’LL KILL HIM! Dread struggles, but Octavian stays firm. He lifts Dread high into the air, and Dread’s arms flail wildly in protest! Dread reaches out for something to hold on to, and out of brilliance or just pure desperation reaches for the GCW title belt hanging just inches from his head! Octavian suddenly brings Dread down, driving him with all his strength into a vicious powerbomb onto the wooden platform below! The crowd explodes as Octavian is the only one left beneath the belt! Bryan- POWERBOMB! MY GOD, Octavian just destroyed Jason Dread! Danger- Hey, WAIT A MINUTE! Octavian looks up, and his eyes grow wide. The wire holding the belt swings idly above Octavian’s head, but the belt is nowhere to be seen! Octavian looks down in horror at Dread’s broken body below him. The camera looks on from above at Dread lying motionless on the platform… …With the GCW World Heavyweight Championship belt draped over his shoulder! Bryan- WAIT A MINUTE! LOOK AT THAT! Danger- NO!!! The crowd explodes as the image of the title belt resting on Dread’s chest is shown on the MegaTron. Octavian, wide-eyed and furious, looks around desperately for some confirmation. He gets just that as the bell rings, and the crowd explodes in elation! “Just Got Wicked” begins to play overhead, and Octavian drops his head in disbelief! Bryan- JASON DREAD HAS DONE IT! Andrews- Here is your winner… And the NEEEEWWWWWWW GCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION… JASON DREAAAAAAAAAD!!!!! Bryan- YES! YES! YES! Danger- NO! NO! NO! Octavian sneers and slides down the back of the forklift, angrily storming away from the ring. Dread, hardly able to stand, rolls off the platform and staggers about, finally finding support in the side of the forklift. Bewildered, he looks around at the chaotic crowd, and hears his music playing above. Finally, he looks down at the title belt, which he has instinctively clutched to his shoulder. He takes the belt in his hands and studies it carefully, a confident smile crossing his lips. Finally he lifts the belt high into the air for the crowd to see, and the arena begins chanting his name! Bryan- The crowd is on fire here, as Jason Dread has become Heavyweight Champion of the World for the second time here tonight at ACWF! He’s defied all odds, he’s overcome the injuries and somehow gutted out one of the most impressive victories I’ve ever seen! Dread, still hurting badly, staggers to the chains and holds the belt up for the crowd to see. Cameras flash wildly, illuminating the arena, as an incredible fireworks display begins to explode high in the air over the Olympic Stadium! Dread turns to cross the ring, but finds Mike Hardy standing before him. Then, slowly, The Jhub staggers to his feet and glares at Dread, who drapes the GCW title belt over his shoulder. His music fades as the crowd quiets, unsure of what might be about to happen. Bryan- Uh-oh, Mike Hardy and The Jhub are up, and they aren’t happy about this! This was The Jhub’s match, and many felt Mike Hardy was destined to win the title here tonight! But Jason Dread has won this match! Hardy glances back at The Jhub, then back at Dread. Dread stands confidently with the GCW title displayed gloriously over his right shoulder, an eyebrow raised in curiosity. Hardy suddenly begins walking right at Dread with a quick pace, his arms raised! Dread takes a half step back, but Hardy abruptly wraps his arms around Dread, embracing the new champion! The crowd explodes with applause as Hardy raises Dread’s arm in the air. Dread seems uncomfortable with Hardy’s display of affection but plays along, looking at The Jhub. The Jhub finally shrugs and raises Dread’s other arm in the air, and “Just Got Wicked” cues up again! Bryan- WHAT A MOMENT! The Jhub and Mike Hardy, in the ultimate show of sportsmanship, are presenting to the world the new GCW Heavyweight Champion! Danger- This is terrible! My pick was robbed in his own hometown, and these people are cheering about it! Bryan- Octavian came close, but in the end it was a brilliant, heads-up move from a man who had just gone through hell to capture the GCW title belt! Hardy and The Jhub look at each other and shake their heads in disappointment. Hardy and Jhub hug each other consolingly and begin to head to the back together, leaving Dread to enjoy his victory. Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen, in all my years I have never seen anything like ACWF Six! This was the most amazing, brutal, emotional match I have ever seen, and these six men put their bodies on the line for the greatest prize in professional wrestling! But let it be known right now that Jason Dread walked into Rome tonight and became heavyweight champion of the world, in a match that will definitely put his name in the history books as one of the greatest champions of all time! Danger- I won’t take anything away from him, JB, this was a hell of a performance from an amazing wrestler! I didn’t think he’d walk out of this thing alive, much less win, but he proved me wrong! Dread steps back onto the forklift platform, dented and splintered from an hour of abuse. He hits the controls and the forks begin lifting him high above the ring. The crowd stands in unison to applaud the new champion, as gold and silver fireworks begin exploding overhead. The platform reaches its peak as Dread stands tall above the ring, holding his new GCW title belt high for the world to see! Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen, that’s our show! For Mr. Danger, Kevin Jacobs and all of GCW, thank you for joining us! Jason Dread is champion of the world! This has been ACWF!!!!! The GCW logo flashes on the bottom of the screen and the show fades out, leaving Jason Dread standing high and tall, presenting his GCW World Heavyweight Championship to the camera…………
