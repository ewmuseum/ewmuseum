Nerezza Biography Real Name Nascosto Nerezza Nicknames Nezz, informally. Hometown Manse Island Birthday May 29th 2002 Height 6 feet, 7 inches when stood straight - often slumps. Weight 256 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography In 1982, a man was born. Nothing of major importance happened that should concern anyone until 2002, when he was reborn. Taking the name Nascosto Nerezza, he formed an alliance with another man, the mischievous Gaz Green. For years, the two joked, pranked and laughed their way through the wrestling world, from PCW, to OSE, RPW, to PRIME. It was during their stint in PRIME that Gaz decided that wrestling was no longer the fun it used to be, with locker room politics and policies on not troubling other workers growing too strict. Nerezza revealed that he too was growing tired of the enforcements. While Gaz returned to his native Las Vegas for a life of vice, Nerezza diverted his attention to his own island in Japan. Thus, P.W.A. went out not with a bang, but with a whimper. Before he left the USA, Nerezza was contacted by another old associate, Hessian. When Hessian began his business, Nerezza had donated a notable amount of money to him. Hessian then gave him back his investment, plus interest? Nerezza returned home, grinning from ear to ear with the money he knew just what to do with. Construction began on the island he now privately down, renamed Manse Island. Turning it into his own private resort, a huge dome was built over it fitted with ultraviolet filters. For the first time, a vampire could freely walk in daylight. But this wasn’t the end. Nerezza soon began seducing local people with the promise of immortality, offering a sample of his blood to those who promised themselves to him. The desperate, the wealthy and his personal favourite, the desperately wealthy, soon flocked to him, begging him for the miracle elixir. Terminally ill family members of huge businesses, people who surrendered their life savings in greed, it didn’t matter ? fortunes were handed over by people seeing to cheat death. Nerezza’s so-called “miracle” was soon spreading, to the point that his island developed from a personal paradise to an establishment for his “children”. His visitors remained and bit by bit, it transformed into a cult hailing Nerezza as a saviour to the lost. The idea amused Nerezza and he played along ? along with a select group of vampires, humans joined the island, surrendering what they lacked in money to servitude to him, finally accepting a religion where the results didn’t require death and good faith to see. The cattle, the zealots, all arranged, Nerezza felt like the deity he was hailed as. But there was still something lacking ? challenge, prey and predators. The island placed him at the top of the food chain and soon, ennui set in. Even God ran out of ideas in seven days and while Nerezza was no real God, he soon succumbed to the blandness of it all. Leaving the island for a while, Nerezza attempted to contact Gaz Green ? but upon reaching Gaz’s hotel, he saw his old partner clutching a child in his arms, standing next to a woman with a bulging belly. Telling Gaz the reason was a social visit and mentioning nothing of his island or current situation, Nerezza left the man once known as The Jester in peace and swore that even if Gaz or his family were ever to fall under misfortune, he wouldn’t allow them the option of living eternally under his tyranny. Literally plucking a name from a hat, Nerezza had his right hand man, Jonathan Dyango, arrange his entry into GCW. His life on Manse Island remains unknown to the general public and he prefers to keep the two lives separate, with the exception of Dyango handling his business with the cult and simultaneously managing him. Nerezza hopes the world of wrestling will once again bring him a challenge, especially after the time away from Gaz and more in the company of his own kind changed his body as well as attitude towards the world. He has no interest in winning titles at all, now focusing on having an enjoyable match to relieve himself. His charisma and persuasion skills have greatly increased over time, winning over people who fail to see the fangs guarding the silver tongue... Other Feds PCW, OSE, RPW, PRIME, NTICW Other Fed Awards PCW: 5x tag Team Champion with Gaz Green as P.W.A. Wrestling Style Innovative, unorthodox, distracting Finisher Move Forze Del Male Finisher Description Set up by Nerezza taking the opponent by the right wrist with his right hand and delivering a stomp to the back of their knee to make them kneel down, Nerezza pulls them to a sitting position, holding one hand behind their back and facing them. Taking the opponent's other hand as if to do a basic surfboard-style pull, he then executes the move proper - rolling onto his side and landing on his neck and shoulders, facing up, while his legs scissor the opponent's head. With one final heave, Nerezza pulls with his legs as hard as he can and yanks their arms, flipping the opponent backwards. The end result is Nerezza in a sitting position, his legs split apart and the opponent on their back, shoulders pinned to the mat and their hands pinned and crossed on the mat, with Nerezza's own head pushing into the small of the back. Finisher Setup Move Stomp to the back of the knee Setup Description No explanation needed, though sometimes comes from ducking a clothesline Other Moves The Lecter Lecture - with the victim sitting and Nerezza standing behind them, Nerezza takes their left wrist in his right hand and pulls it so their face rests inside the elbow. He then takes their right wrist in his left hand and pulls it so their armis crossed with the other and pulls tight, forcing his knee into the back of their head. The object of the move exploits a loophole against biting, as one cannot bite one's opponent but there is no rule about biting oneself. The Dio Drop - After setting someone up in a Tree Of Woe position, Nerezza pummels them with a series of high speed lefts and rights, If the opponent doesn't drop to the mat, Nerezza snapmares them onto their back. To finish the routine, he ascends the top rope, does a pose and screetches, then jumps off and lands feet first on the opponent's stomach or chest. Sasori Jissatsu (Scorpion Suicide): Nerezza's submission finisher. With the victim face down on the mat, Nerezza picks up their ankles, sits on the floor and folds his legs so they rest on the back of the opponent's thighs. He then leans fowards, trapping the opponent's lower legs in his and lays on their back, stomach first. To finish, he takes their wrists in his hands and brings them up into the air where he bends them forwards towards the victim's head, causing immense strain on the shoulders. The Rude-plex: Grabbing his opponent's hands in his own and locking his fingers between theirs, Nerezza delivers a headbutt or two as a means of softening the opponent up, then bridges backwards, dragging his opponent through the air and onto their back.
