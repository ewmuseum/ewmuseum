Team Baked Biography Fed Titles Pffffft, Like these two could win titles? Fed Awards Or Awards? Biography Stoners. Get high every night, sometimes in the morning, before every match, sometimes during. Kicked out of High School, they just became sort of Drifters. They'd crash at friends houses, and eventually became wrestlers by wandering into the ring after an indy show and jumping around. Their charismatic persona's were easy to put over, and here we are. Two childhood friends, big time potheads. Rarely sober. Getting into trouble all the time, with everyone, everywhere. Fun loving duo. Like Beavis and Butthead, or Bill and Ted. Or Wayne and Garth. Or the Wayans brothers, any combination, yet white. But not white chicks, because that film sucked. Total Extremists. These guys can't fight for shit so they just jump around a lot, throw crazy punches. They're more of a defensive team, since while high, they seem to be impervious to pain. Other Feds Or be signed ANYWHERE else? Other Fed Awards ..Or Any other Awards? Finisher Name JOINT Production Finisher Description Inspired by the 'Most Overused Finishers.' thread on PTC.
