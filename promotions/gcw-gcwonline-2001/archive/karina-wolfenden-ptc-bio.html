Strengths Crisp striking, devestating finishers, supreme aerial ability, focused, relentless machine. Weaknesses High center of gravity, high-risk style, simplistic approach, vulnerable against technicians (can be easilly out-grappled). Wrestling Style Killyouinthefuckingface Style (fierce striking based with sky-high bendy-flips and flashy-twists). Strategy Relentless, violent onslaught. Finisher Move Deadlights. Finisher Description Flying Triangle Choke (feigning a Tiger Crush, she ensnares the neck and arm, brings them down with a crude huricanrana, and tightens the hold on the canvas for the tap/pass out. Will often throw in a few death-rolls to break an opponent faster). Finisher Setup Move Out of the blue. Special Moves Goodnight Moon (Asai Kneedrop). Other Moves To a standing opponent Tiger crush, snap kicks to thigh and side, crescent kick, spinning back-fist, knees from a Muay Thai clinch, enziguri kick, enziguri scissors kick, overhead bicycle kick, spinning enziguri kick, springboard spinning enziguri kick, superwoman punch, slap, moonsault kick, running pump dropkick, dropsault, rolling koppou kick, repeated Mongolian chops, knees to an opponent held in a butterfly lock, 720 DDT. Opponent in corner Kick onslaught, running cartwheel kick, running cartwheel knee, flying double-knee to the chest (usually followed immediately by an inverted lung-blower), furious barrage of stomps to any opponent down at the foot of the turnbuckles (holds onto ropes for balance while stomping away). To a downed opponent Top rope double-stomp, curb-stomp, Asai moonsault senton, standing twisting 450 Splash, standing corkscrew senton, standing 450 splash. Flashy shit Toss in the Corkscrew Impaler and Sunset Lungblower for flash, or for BIG pay-per-view matches she'll generally just try to up her usual moves a notch with an extra flip or twist. Very, very, very rarely will use a mist that fits in with her current colour-scheme (currently black). Special spots Top rope double-stomp to opponent draped over the middle rope (bouncing off to land on apron), hanging headscissors (huricanrana over top rope, then keeps legs locked and holds onto top cable to choke opponent). Alternate/Uber-finisher The C-4 is a tumbling Kudo Driver, to be used when death or paralysis is intended. With an opponent's arms tightly hooked for a Gory Special, Kari flips backwards into a seated position, pulling her opponent down onto the back of their neck. Entrance Music Velocity Shift by Overseer. Ring Entrances "Oh, sugar, you just gone and done the dumbest thing in your whole life…" As the prophetic whispered words straight out of Sin City fade, ringside is plunged into darkness. As camera flashes slash through the blanket of black, a frenetic techno pulse erupts throughout the arena, followed by a cascade of drums. SUPERCALIFRAGIALISTICWHENWEDROPWEGOBALLISTIC~! Velocity Shift by Overseer by is then fully unleashed, accompanied by a fit-inducing light show. Through the curtain emerges Karina Wolfenden, encased within a six-strong event security detail; pale face peeking out from under a hood, scar masked by a scaly black lightning bolt. United States title draped over her shoulder and buds nestled in her ears, she’s deaf to the seething volley of jeers which the crowd rain down upon her. Andrews: From Albany, New York… weighing in at 164 pounds… she is the GCW United States Champion… the K-Wolf… KARINA WOOOOOOOOOLFENDEN!!! Approaching the ring in her own time, Karina remains blinkered to the anger etched into every face along the aisle as she loosens her shoulders and tightens her gloves. As she reaches the ring, Karina hops up to apron then springs onto the top rope, balancing on tip-toes and finally looking out into the baying crowd for the briefest of seconds before dropping down into the ring. Body impatient, itching for a fight, she bounces on tip-toes in her corner, fists clenched and arms shaking themselves loose.
