Joe Milone Biography Real Name Joe Milone Nicknames Primetime Hometown Beverly Hills, California Birthday February 27th Height 6'5 Weight 275 Biography Joe Milone first entered the Sport in 1997 and wrestled through various federations, and winning numerous title belts. An injury in 2001 forced him to step away from the ring and persue other endevors. But the passion he had for professional wrestling could never burn out and he decided to step by into the ring by signing with GCW. At first he was assigned to the GCW dojo where he met Defoe Smith, who would become his greatest ally and friend. They were both called up to the main roster shortly thereafter where they formed Primetime Enterprises and ran roughshot though the fed until they were eventually fired for insubordination. After traveling to GDW they quickly captured the tag belts before decding to make a return to GCW. Upon the return to GCW, Milone went on to capture the Heavyweight Championship on 3 occasions and competed in a memorable feud with Jimmy Thunder. GCW closed it's doors and Primetime moved on to the Bloodbox capturing the Tag titles before Bloodbox closed its doors for good. After Bloodbox folded, Milone toured the US indies and Japan for a while before once again persuing other interests.
