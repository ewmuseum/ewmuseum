Yale- Wait, Dread gets a head of steam, and leaps at Mayhem, driving the head of that sledgehammer into Mayhem’s skull! What a move! An elbow drop using the sledgehammer! Bryan- This is already going too far! Dread gets back to his feet, finally setting the sledgehammer down! (Dread smiles again, raising his arms into the air. The crowd begins booing him, and a drink even flies down through the crowd, landing soundly on Dread’s back. Dread ignores the unruly fan, and heads back to Mayhem.) Bryan- And listen to the boos from the crowd for Jason Dread! Dread lifts Mayhem back up, but Mayhem is almost all dead weight! Dread sets Mayhem up in the corner, and simply begins to shout at him. Dread manages to leap all the way over Mayhem, who hits the other side! Mayhem bounces back, and Dread leaps again! But this time Mayhem catches him in midair! Bryan- But Dread drops back, and takes Mayhem over into a hurricanrana! What a move! Badger- Yeah, yeah, so what. I know Kung Fu. Bryan- Dread rushes up to the top rope, not wasting a single movement! Mayhem isn’t moving, as Dread takes off! (Dread flips back quickly, soaring through the air with an amazing moonsault! Camera flashes light up the crowd as Dread lands perfectly on Mayhem.) Bryan- WHAT A MOVE! A perfect moonsault from Dread! Yale- But Dread may have hurt himself there a bit! Dread is clutching his left knee, and may have injured it on the way down! (As both men lie on the mat and the count begins, a double feature appears. We see a slow motion replay of Dread’s moonsault, and the landing clearly shows him falling with most of his weight on his legs.) Badger- It’s still not Kung Fu. Yale- Will you please shut up? Bryan- Dread’s knee is hurting here, and both men are finding themselves counted down! But both men are slowly getting to their feet, as Dread pulls himself up in the ropes. Mayhem slowly crawls for the corner, pulling himself up as best he can. Yale- Dread takes a moment to shake off his knee, as Mayhem rises in the corner. Dread sees and opening, and charges! He dives in with a low shoulder, but Mayhem ducks out of the way, and Dread goes shoulder first into the steel post! Bryan- Dread staggers back out of the corner, clutching his shoulder… as Mayhem hits the ropes! Mayhem hooks Dread up… HAMMER ON! HAMMER ON! Big running DDT on Dread! (The crowd explodes with a huge chorus of cheers, as both men once again find themselves down.) Yale- And both men are down! But that right there could be it for Dread! Come on, Jason, get up! Badger- Did you know that I know Kung Fu? Bryan- Badger, with all due respect, we’re trying to watch this match! Badger- I’m bored outta my mind here. Octavian came to aid Dread, but Badger is keeping this one fair! Octavian begins to battle back, and Badger and Octavian are in a fist fight on the outside! Yale- But hold on! Here comes Toxic! Badger rolls Octavian into the ring, and follows him in, but Toxic is on his way! Toxic slides into the ring, and crushes Badger with a quick clothesline! Badger never saw him coming! Bryan- Dread and Mayhem are both down, but the count has stopped as the official attempts to restore order! (The crowd boos as Octavian and Toxic stomp away at Badger. However, the boos turn to cheers as President Caldera and Khan rush down the ramp! The fans go crazy as Octavian spots Caldera and Khan, and bails out. Toxic, however, continues assaulting Badger.) Yale- CALDERA AND KHAN ARE HERE! Octavian has bailed out, and armed himself with a steel chair! But Caldera has a chair of his own, and Toxic doesn’t even see them coming! (Khan spins Toxic around, and begins pummeling him with right hands. Badger gets to his feet, as Khan assaults Toxic, finally pummeling him out of the ring.) Bryan- Khan dispatches of Toxic, as Badger gets to his feet and begins shouting at Octavian to come back and fight! But Octavian is hesitant! (Octavian attempts to get to the apron, but bails out as Badger moves in. The crowd cheers wildly for Badger, as Caldera and Khan watch from the ring. Dread and Mayhem both lie hurting on the mat, as Khan begins climbing the turnbuckle. Badger raises his arms to the cheering crowd, and turns to face his allies. However, as soon as he faces Caldera, Caldera swings the chair, connecting with a vicious shot to Badger’s skull!!) Bryan- WHAT THE HELL?! (The crowd instantly begins to boo, confused and angry.) Yale- What the- Bryan- CALDERA JUST LEVELED BADGER! What the hell did he do that for?! Badger is out cold from Caldera’s chair shot, and Khan is perched on the top rope! (Khan soars through the air, moving the chair under his legs. Khan then lands perfectly with a leg drop over the chair, hitting Tristain Mayhem clean on the face. The crowd boos in total shock and incredulous hatred, as Khan and Caldera head to Dread.) Bryan- What is this?! Why have President Caldera and Khan destroyed Badger and Mayhem!? Will someone please tell us what the hell is going on!? Yale- Look! Caldera is pulling Dread up to his feet, as the referee is forced to count! (Dread warily looks up at Caldera with a smile. Caldera helps Dread to his feet, as the referee makes the ten count. The crowd boos in anger and Octavian and Toxic watch in shock as the referee makes the final counts… eight…. Nine…. Ten! Mayhem doesn’t move an inch, and the bell rings.) Bryan- DAMNIT! Andrews- Here is your winner… and the NEW Global Championship Wrestling Heavyweight Champion… Jason Dread!!!!! Dread smiles widely as the official hands him the world title belt, which he greets with a grin, a kiss, and a warm embrace before slinging it over his shoulder. I gave him chance after chance, opportunity after opportunity, shot after shot… and never once could he capitalize. In the end, Octavian flat out outsmarted him, and in the end it was ME who gave Tristain Mayhem the Heavyweight Championship of the World. I MADE TRISTAIN MAYHEM! (The crowd boos heavily once more.) When he saw Nanci was growing with his child, he moved on to something better for the time being. He asked me if I would give Double Cool my blessing, and I did. But he then proceeded to piss on my title belt, piss on my company, and piss on me when he won the tag team titles. He gave up on what truly mattered, telling me that he was in love. Well, Tristain, love has no place in my company, and love has no place in this world! Bryan- This is sickening! And we knew that the only way to make things they way they were… Was to bring back the ESTABLISHMENT! (The crowd erupts with an awestruck chatter, as Octavian and Toxic are taken aback. Bryan- Can you believe what he just said?! President Caldera has turned his back on everyone here tonight, and the Establishment is back! Yale- GCW will never be the same, JB! Finally, things are the way they should be again!! Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time! For David Yale I’m James Bryan…. Good Night Everybody! The Establishment heads back up the ramp, as Badger and Mayhem lie in pain at ringside, as Octavian and Toxic simply discuss the events to each other.
