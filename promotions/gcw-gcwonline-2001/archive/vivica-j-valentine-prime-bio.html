Vivica J. Valentine Biography Real Name Vivica Juliet Valentine Nicknames The Fearless Phenom, The Bulletproof Blonde, The Headstrong Heroine, The Woman Without Fear Hometown Highland Park, Illinois Birthday October 31, 1982 Height 5'4' Weight 129 Fed Titles GCW World Tag Team Champion (6/21/09 - 6/26/09), GCW World Heavyweight Champion (12/16/07 - 08/16/08), GCW United States Champion (02/21/06 - 10/30/06, 3/27/10 - 12/6/10) Fed Awards 2008 GCW Wrestler Of The Year, 2007 Dangerous Games Winner, 2nd Longest Reigning World Champion In GCW History, Longest Reigning US Champion in GCW History Biography Today, Vivica J. Valentine is a household name in the wrestling community. With GCW Hall Of Fame credentials and resume comparable to any other top female in the sport, you would think life had always came easy to the woman who made her impact as "The Fearless Phenom" on X:TV. Those awards came with unrealistic expectations and great sacrifice, and her life was far from handed to her. Born and raised in Highland Park, Illinois outside of Chicago, Vivica Juliet Valentine grew up in the type of environment that many kids would dream of. The daughter of two wealthy parents, the world was at her fingertips. However, the little girl whom everyone fully expected to be prim and proper (including her unfortunate parents) instead grew up an elite athlete. At the age of nine, Vivica was already a standout prodigy in the sports of Swimming and Gymnastics. Recruitment to private schools starter early, and her proud parents were more than willing to pay whatever the price may be to put their daughter on the way to fame. However, as Vivica would prove time-and-time again in her life, she was never one to seek fame. Blowing off invitations, Vivica enrolled in Highland Park High School, her local public school disappointing dozens with her fierce loyalty to her childhood friends. Fame continued to follow her despite her decision, becoming a 3-Time All-American in both Swimming and Gymnastics while maintaining a 3.8 GPA and being elected Homecoming Queen. Even with no desire to live in the limelight, she carried an undeniable charisma from a young age that seemed to draw people to her. With scholarships from all the top colleges in the country, the weight at the top become too much to bare. Be it her parents forcing her into stardom or her simply not being able to have personal conversations with friends without it becoming public knowledge, the burden of juggling high school work and politics with brushes of fame and dozens of suitors proved to be too much for the teenage phenom. At age seventeen she simply fell off the face of the Earth, packed her necessarily belongings into her high school backpack and ran away to the streets of downtown Chicago to try and start all over again. A simple life without all the drama and expectations. She picked up work quickly as a waitress at a high end restaurant, which is where she met Christian Zenith, a college business prodigy making waves in the world of Wall Street with brilliant yet eccentric ideas and an undeniable reputation as a kid who loved to party. Things went quickly for the couple, Valentine was fascinated by his intelligence and influence while Zenith had never met a woman who was so real with him. In only a couple weeks she'd moved into his father's estate while he flew back and forth between Chicago and Yale University. It was Christian Zenith himself who introduced her to the real world of professional wrestling. As a child she was often sneaking over to the houses of friends to watch episodes due to her parents feeling such a violent bloodsport was unbecoming of a prim and proper princess, and as a result it became a passion. Standing next to Christian in GLOBAL Wrestling was a dream come true for a lifelong fan, but there is where Zenith and her differed in opinion. Professional wrestling to the businessman was nothing more than an eccentric hobby. Another way to pass the time until taking control of his father's company and fortune while living out fantasies of power and influence over men and women who dedicated their lives to the sport. Controlling the stable Hunting Grounds, Christian introduced Valentine to Lia Ambrosi, Dawn McFarlane, and Tessa Windsor... three women whose passion for what they did was equal to that of Vivica's. While it was only a matter of time until Christian grew tired of the sport, Vivica craved to someday live out her dreams in the same vein as the three women she became close friends with. So much so that when Zenith decided to call it quits, Valentine moved in with best-friend Lia Ambrosi where she would learn the tricks of the trade to herself become a professional wrestler. Shamed by her newly reunited parents by following an interest so "barbaric", it wasn't long after training that her family simply ceased to care about her well-being and cut off communication all together only to resurface occasionally whenever the circumstances looked favorable upon them. Global Championship Wrestling became Vivica's first (and only) experience in professional wrestling. While flashes of brilliance were definitely shown (such as winning the GCW United States Championship in her first singles match), her ability was raw and unrefined, gaining a reputation as being the most inconsistent and vulnerable member of the stable Dies Irae lead by Lia Ambrosi herself. She became outcasted by her Dies Irae teammates (besides Lia Ambrosi) for what they felt as not being on their level of ability. Her struggles ended up landing her in a legendary losing streak, and her career in the industry severely in jeopardy. Unlike the performance of her younger self, Valentine eventually ended up standing up to the challenge and catching up to the steep learning curve winning Dangerous Games in 2007 before beating her own mentor Lia Ambrosi for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship at the following NC-17. It was there that the legend of "The Fearless Phenom" was born, holding onto the World Heavyweight Championship for a record breaking reign that saw such contenders as Jay Terror and Hall of Famer Jordan White fall to her feet. More titles and accolades followed as Vivica J. Valentine has become the longest consecutive member of the GCW roster. Her struggles with the likes of LeStatt Knight, Andy Murray, and The Establishment have become tales of lore around the industry. The record books of Global Championship Wrestling have become her legacy. GCW WorldWide has become her story. The story of a girl from Highland Park who refuses to be anything but the best in the world. Valentine is your high risk, gambling, high flyer who has slowly evolved into a flyer/striker hybrid but still excels mostly in high-octane offense revolving around her inhuman balance learned from years as a world-class gymnist. Valentine) [Karina Wolfenden] The C4: (Flip Over Kudo Driver) STRIKES: She'll use mostly stiff kicks and chops, switching to closed fists when angered or frustrated. She's been known to use high impact kicks as a game-changer such as a Super Kick or Shining Wizard. She's also known to sneak in a quick pin or two to catch her opponent off-guard, as well as using her entire body as a weapon against much larger opponents. GRAPPLES: Due to her size, Valentine will stick mostly to a high-speed no-lift offense against opponents regardless of size. It has become her comfort zone to the point where hitting a move such as a Body Slam or a Suplex against anyone comes off as a desperation rarity. She'll mainly stick to quicker high impact moves like a Hurricanrana, DDTs & Variations (Tornados, Flips, Etc.), Hip Tosses, Arm Drags, Drop Toe Holds, and Neckbreakers. SUBMISSIONS: Besides the moves listed above, the only submissions Valentine would attempt would be directed towards cutting off the flow of oxygen to the brain to take down a much larger opponent, or towards taking out the legs of an opponent to keep them from being able to keep up with her high-octane offense. Ankle Locks and Heel Hooks would be her go-to submissions against all opponents. AERIAL: This genre is where Vivica excells more than all the others. Her lack of size, gymastics background, and lightning speed give her the ability to pull off nearly any aeiral strike the imagination can muster. Springboards and verticle leaps are the very backbone of her offense, using the entire ring as well as her entire body to inflict as much damage as possible as fast as possible. With a showmanship flair, Valentine prefers to add a little extra to her aerial assaults in the form of flips and corkscrews. Instead of a Leg Drop, she'll do a Front Flip Leg Drop. Instead of a Suicide Dive, she'll do a Corkscrew Suicide Dive. The crowd loves her acrobatics, and their reaction fuels her desire to go on.
