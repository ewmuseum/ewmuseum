Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #17 – Tampa, Florida

Introduction -by Steven Caldera

The lights dim in the arena, and the Tampa crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Tremendous x2” and “Iron Man” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Tampa, Florida” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: With over sixteen thousand fans in attendance, Global Championship Wrestling is live in Tampa for another edition of WorldWide! Hello once again, everyone, I’m James Bryan, back at ringside with Pete and The Jhub, and gentlemen, we are just two weeks away from Dangerous Games!

Pete: That’s right, JB, a thirty man over-the-top battle royal for the number one contentionship to the GCW World Championship! It doesn’t get much bigger than that.

Jhub: …Right. Well, JB, I’m excited as hell to be here, because it means I get to watch these morons killing themselves to gain momentum going into that match, while I sit here at ringside with you guys, drink a few beers, go back to the hotel with the attractive young lady behind me, and cruise into Dangerous Games with ease.

Bryan: Folks, tonight we’re expecting two major announcements! First, from President Caldera regarding the list of names entering the Dangerous Games, and then the big one, the confirmation of what we’ve all been waiting for, the showdown between Jason Dread and LeStatt Knight!

Jhub: This thing is getting really personal, JB, and tonight we’re going to find out when and where these two will be getting it on. You can only expect they’ll be going at it at Dangerous Games, but you know it’s not just going to be another match. This is going to be an all-out war, guys, and I don’t think one arena can hold those two.

Pete: And you know LeStatt isn’t happy after losing the United States title to Trey Tremendous last week!

Bryan: LeStatt has been rather livid lately, but I don’t think it’s because of the United States title. He’s so hellbent on getting at Jason Dread and the World Championship that he’s turned his back on everything else, setting his focus entirely on the GCW Champion.

Jhub: Dread’s in for what could be his biggest challenge yet, other than fighting me, of course.

Bryan: In addition to all that, fans, we’ve got Mike Hardy in action, the Television and Tag Team titles are on the line, and we’ll see LeStatt and Dread in the same ring in the evening’s main event!

Evil Intentions -by Roberto and Eva

The cameras catch LeStatt Knight walking from the parking garage, which brings a chorus of boo's down inside the arena. He is clad in a black "wife-beater" and black slacks. His hair is tied back neatly and a set of sunglasses cover his bright blue eyes. A black a red gym bag hangs from his right shoulder. It is obvious that he is alone in the lot as the sound of his boots crashing against the pavement echoes through out. Just as he reaches a large set of double doors the door flies open almost hitting Knight. He gets a quick look of frustration but it quickly changes to a slight grin as Eva Van Lorne steps out and runs right into Knight. Slowly Knight reaches up and takes off his sunglasses and looks down at Eva. Eva's eyes slowly moves up Knight's body until they meet his own.

Knight: Hola senorita como estas?

With quick recovery Eva steps back as her gaze manages to get to his face. A slow sultry smile appearing on those lips which had the men in the crowds screaming for a kiss and making cat calls. Camera pans out a few feet to reveal the ice queen dressed in an extremely short hip hugging white lycra mini dress.

Eva: Lestatt, it is a pleasure to see you again. I could almost say I missed you last week.(sly flirtatious tone) Must have spoiled me to those locker room rendevous.

Knight smiles and nods his head in agreement before putting his sunglasses in a pocket in his pants.

Knight: Spoiled indeed but it seems you have plenty of dogs that are just drooling at the chance to have a one on one with you. Be it Borealis, Johnny Lexicon, or Trey Tremendous. I am sure that you couldn't have missed me that much.

Knight lets his gym bag lower from his shoulder and drop to the pavement. He slides his hand over his hair and adjusted the rubberband that kept it together.

Knight: Although with as often as we seem to run into each other its almost as if its all meant to happen. That is if you believe in thing ssuch as fate and destiny. Do you believe Eva?

Eva's face becomes a mask of amusement as she actually laughs and places a hand lightly on his chest.

Eva: Those boys Lestatt? Come now I would hope that by now you would realize they are nothing more than puppets to tugged about on my strings. And Lexicon is gone, who knows when he will show up again. ( pause as sly smirk appears) I guess the poor guy just couldnt handle losing his company like that.

Eva allows her hand to trail down his chest slowly before falling away.

Eva: ( husky tones) As for fate or destiny....I do not I am controlled by destiny or fate Lestatt...I make my own.

Knight: So it would seem Eva. Tell me though if they are merely puppets then what does that make me in your eyes? To know and understand what you look like through others eyes is a cherished thing ... so tell me Eva do I seem like a puppet to you?

Knight searches her eyes staring deep into them. His confidence flows from his body like water from a stream. an eyebrow slightly raises above the other and he awaits his answer.

The crowd seems to kind of hush all of them watching intently to see what the diva would say in response to that. Eva's icy blues never waver as she allows those lips to tilt upwards into a geniune smile.

Eva: No Lestatt, I do not think you are a puppet. You were my springboard by all means yes. Afterall it was my match with you that launched me here. ( lifting her hand to straighten his collar as her eyes dart away and lift to his once more) I don't know, perhaps it that which has given me some sort of respect for you.

Eva steps back to lean a hip against the door frame arms crossing across those rather abundant mounds.

Eva: Then again Lestatt one can ask you the same question. You have played several men for the fool the past few weeks. So what am I to you? Am I to be another one of those who ends up on your hit list?

Before he can answer her hand lifts, index finger pressed against his lips as she leans in and purrs in his ear.

Eva: Don't answer that. I like the mystery around you....

Eva drops her hand and moves back once again smiling at him in a way no one has seen before. Slowly she turns and takes a step back in as if she were going to walk away from him. Knight's face is emotionless, not even that cocky smile he is so famous for. He licks his lips slowly before saying anything more.

Knight: Don't worry Eva ... you weren't going to get in that easil anyways. Just remember that surprises are around every corner.

Knight now winks at Eva and slowly leans down to pick up his bag. It is obvious Knight lets his eyes travel down Eva's body taking her in completely, and then again as he raises up after picking up his bag.

Knight: Nice seeing you again Eva ... hopefully it is not the last.

She cocks her head over her shoulder to catch him giving her the once over only to smile and call back as she walked away.

Eva: Oh, I can asure you..it will definitely not be the last.

Knight smiles and slightly nods his head before walking past Eva and into the arena doors. The camera stays with Eva for a brief moment as she watches Knight walk away.

Eva: Not the last at all.

High Tide, Low Tide -by Caldera & Chris

Back in the arena, “Established” cues up overhead, and the crowd explodes! President Caldera, clad in a perfect Italian suit and a bright smile, struts towards the ring with a spring in his step, casually slapping the outstretched hands of the fans seated by the aisle.

Andrews: Please welcome the owner and President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera!

Bryan: And we’re kicking off WorldWide with a bang, as we get a rare appearance here at ringside from President Caldera! He’s here to settle some of the speculation about Dangerous Games, and you know whenever Caldera has something to say, he always brings a surprise.

Jhub: Yeah, Caldera’s always got a trick or two up his sleeve. I’ve known this guy for going on fifteen years, and I still can never tell what he’s thinking.

Pete: Caldera loves to shake things up, but I thought he was staying out of GCW’s affairs!

Bryan: I think that’s the idea, but everyone wants to know what to expect at Dangerous Games, and this is the man to settle it.

Caldera nimbly slides through the ropes and accepts the microphone from ring announcer Joey Andrews, then turns to the crowd. As the music fades, the cheers continue, and Caldera can’t help but laugh in spite of himself. He gives a dismissing wave to the crowd and raises the microphone, finally gaining some room to speak over the mayhem.

Caldera: Thank you Tampa!

The crowd gives Caldera the obligatory cheap pop, and Caldera chuckles at himself.

Caldera: You know, Dangerous Games is only two weeks away now, and it’s one of my favorite events of the year. Something about the total anarchy, seeing bodies being thrown every which way, it just makes my soul happy. But this year, there’s been a lot of speculation regarding the show in general. So I’ve come out here to settle a few of those questions.

Caldera pauses as the crowd applauds again.

Caldera: First of all, there’s this little problem between Jason Dread and LeStatt Knight.

The crowd boos wildly at the mention of Knight’s name, but Caldera presses on.

Caldera: Now, of course, I’m staying the hell out of that one. So where and when they get it on is up to them. But I will warn them not to kill one another, because whoever is World Champion following Dangerous Games has the winner of a thirty man battle royal to contend with!

The crowd explodes in cheers, excited about the big match.

Caldera: Naturally, neither Dread nor Knight will be involved in the battle royal itself. Which brings me to my good friend Mike Hardy…

The fans erupt in a huge burst of cheers, chanting “Hardy! Hardy! Hardy!”

Caldera: …and Rich Rollins.

The cheers immediately turn to boos as the arena expresses its distaste for Rollins.

Caldera: These two guys have somehow managed to arrange a sixty minute Iron Man match for Dangerous Games. Now, I’ll be doing my part to get Mike ready for this, because I know as well as anyone that Rich Rollins has never lost an Iron Man match. I’ve got every confidence in Mike Hardy, and I have no doubt he’ll end the Renegade’s streak at Dangerous Games, but the fact of the matter is he can’t be expected to wrestle in TWO hour-long matches in one night. So Mike was forced to make a choice, and he chose to fight Rollins. So that means Rich Rollins and Mike Hardy won’t be entering the battle royal.

Bryan: I think we all expected that, there’s no way these guys could wrestle for over two hours.

Caldera: Now, with a roster already below thirty, and with these four guys not entering the match, that leaves the big question: Where will the rest of the entrants come from? Well, as you all know, I am a man of business before anything else. And in my search for a solution to this nagging problem, a rather interesting business opportunity presented itself. Now, I think we all have come to expect a few surprises out of Dangerous Games, but this one might be my all-time favorite.

Jhub: Get on with it, already!

Pete: Shane Lane. It’s Shane Lane.

Jhub: Say that name one more time, I dare you.

Caldera: As you all know, I’m not just a promoter, I’m a wrestler, too. And once upon a time, I wrestle for a promotion called the AWA…

The crowd begins to buzz, anticipating a big announcement.

Caldera: And in this promotion I met a man… A man I do not particularly like. In fact, you might say I hate this man, and everything he stands for. But damnit, I respect him. I think you all met him once, and I don’t recall you particularly caring for him, either. However, this man gave me a call at the office last week and made me an offer I simply couldn’t refuse. So, without further ado, allow me to present the first official entrant into the 2005 Dangerous Games… LOW!!!!!

The crowd leaps in the air, booing with all the breath they can muster. A huge burst of shock erupts from the arena as the one and only Low appears on stage! (You might add your music and attire here as well.)

Bryan: OH MY GOD! You’ve got to be kidding!

Jhub: Not this asshole again! I can’t believe Low is actually walking back into a GCW arena! This guy’s got a lot of balls!

Pete: We haven’t seen him since Warpath 2002! This guy waltzed into GCW like he owned the place, made enemies with pretty much everyone, and made life a living hell for a lot of people!

Bryan: This man wasn’t in GCW for long, but in his short time he became one of the most hated men in GCW history! And President Caldera has just given him a spot in the battle royal? What the hell is going on here?!

The boos continue as Low struts to the ring, smiling a conniving smile. He climbs into the ring and looks out to the crowd, laughing in spite of their hatred. He then turns to Caldera, accepting a microphone from a nearby ring attendant. Low ignores the boos still drowning out the sound of his music and addresses him directly.

Low: Who would have thunk?

Low chuckles.

Low: 'The more things change, the more they stay the same'. Whoever came up with that, boy was he right. It seems like we can't go through a year without our little TV confrontation, right, Steven?

You brought it upon yourself, Caldera, when you ended my first AWA World title reign in 2001. Yet I quickly regained that title from you, and ever since then, you made your occasional comebacks to the AWA. Heck, last year you even managed the damn guy who beat me for the World title.

But the bottom line is this, Caldera - you are a footnote in AWA history, while I am an eight-time World Heavyweight Champion. And, my friend, I know you can't relate to that, but some of those title reigns went beyond the eight week mark!

Low laughs.

Low: Now, I am a student of this sport, and I've had an eye on GCW for a while. Seems like you're having a problem filling your next big show. So, considering that we are a pair of, shall I say, surviving soldiers that have fought the wars others can only dream of, I decided I was going to make you an offer you can't refuse.

Why? Because you know that no matter how huge my ego is, no matter how 'not-so-great' my first GCW run was, no matter how much your locker room hates me, people will react to me... people will pay to see me... people will pay to watch the American Wrestling Alliance, as they have for the past five and a half years.

The AWA has a proven track record. We're around for the long run. We're not doing a bunch of six month seasons over the course of four years. We've been drawing money, we've been drawing crowds, we've been making superstars ever since 1999.

You want to run a big show? You want to fill the gaps in your main attraction? Here's my offer - and I talked this over with President Gray, so rest assured this is all valid. You will get access to some of the biggest and best stars the AWA has to offer. You will get the opportunity to advertise AWA superstars on your show. You will get the chance to complete your big match.

In return, The Legendary Low gets to finish what he started more than three years ago! I get to prove that what I said all those years ago is the truth, and that is that I am better than anything this company has to offer!

What that means? It means that Dangerous Games has just become a lot more dangerous for all of GCW!

The crowd explodes in boos, as Caldera simply stands in the ring with an odd smile on his face.

Bryan: What on earth is Caldera thinking here? Why is he just letting Low stand there and say these things about GCW?

Pete: Caldera’s spent so much time out of the ring he’s gone soft, JB!

Caldera: You know, I’m glad you came out here to clear that up for us, Low, because I’ve got a few things to get off my chest as well.

Caldera begins to loosen his tie, and the fans begin to buzz. Low raises an amused eyebrow, watching Caldera with great curiosity.

Caldera: Now, I know you’re quite proud of the AWA and everything you’ve accomplished there. But I’m not allowing you to bring your AWA thugs into MY ring for ratings. Sure, it’ll be fun to see my GCW guys go at it with your AWA guys. Hell, it would be really interesting to see an AWA guy win it and fight for the GCW title. But deep down inside, my friend, there’s something else burning inside me that’s led me to make this deal. Low, you and I have had some epic battles, but never here on my home turf. I know all these people are excited to see you back in a GCW ring, if only to see you get your ass kicked…

The crowd cheers in approval, but Low simply laughs them off.

Caldera: But frankly, I’ve become a little… claustrophobic sitting back in that office week after week. This ring is calling to me again. I simply can’t stay away from it. I want back in, Low, and I can’t think of anyone I’d rather come face to face with than you!

The crowd explodes, and Low’s face tightens, realizing he’s been slightly outmaneuvered.

Bryan: WHAT?! Steven Caldera is going to enter the battle royal!?

Jhub: That’s what it sounds like.

Low simply shakes his head, smiling again, still confident.

Low: You? You’re going to enter the battle royal? Well, if you thought this would be some sort of clever ploy to catch me off guard and save some face against me, you’re mistaken. Steven, if you want to enter this match, if you think you can single-handedly keep me from winning this battle royal and lead GCW to a victory over the AWA, that’s just fine. It will be my pleasure to face you down in the ring one more time!

The fans roar in approval, as Caldera pulls his tie off, then slips his jacket off his shoulders and tosses it out of the ring.

Caldera: Low, you bring the AWA. You bring your friends. But just know that this is GCW, and this match is about GCW. But when it comes down to it, my friend, just remember that this is my company, and I will see to it that you and I come face to face in this ring. And when we do, GCW will understand what you and I are all about. See you at Dangerous Games.

“Established” cues up again as the crowd explodes, and Caldera and Low remain in the ring, staring each other down. Caldera slowly extends his hand, and Low cautiously accepts it, the two men shaking hands for the last time before tearing each other apart at Dangerous Games!

Bryan: Well, that solves the mystery! The AWA will be filling out the Dangerous Games, but Caldera did this just to get his hands on Low again!

Pete: I’m not sure I like Caldera getting involved. Are we sure this isn’t just some plot to win the thing himself?

Jhub: Somehow, I doubt that. Caldera’s just trying to glorify himself in the ring again, and he’s brought in his best opponent to do that.

Bryan: It should be quite a sight come Dangerous Games!

Renegade Politics -by Andrew

We abruptly cut backstage to the GCW interview pit as Kevin Jacobs stands holding a microphone in one grubby little paw. To his immediate left is the one and only Rich “The Renegade” Rollins.

The fans immediately begin to seethe in contempt as Rollins’ face fills the screen, his eyes hiding an ill-conceived grin that threatens to overtake his confidence. Trying to cut through the crowd’s hostility, Jacobs begins his spiel.

Jacobs: You’re only three weeks removed from the announcer’s table Rollins, and already you’ve managed to stir up a whole mess of controversy in your violent crusade against Mike Hardy. I guess the question on my mind is… why Mike Hardy?

The Renegade shoots a subtle sneer at Jacobs, looking him dead in the eye.

Renegade: I’ve already told you, Kevin Jacobs. You, and every last one of these ignorant sheep out here who mindlessly pay our bills, I already told you all. (the crowd reacts rather negatively, but Rollins pushes on) He’s got all the ambition in the world Kevin, but he refuses to apply himself. He wants to be in the main event, does he? He wants to bring home his first World title?

Rollins laughs, a harsh sound.

Renegade: He wants to be remembered, that’s all. But what good ‘ol Hickenbottom doesn’t understand is that winning himself a shiny new belt won’t make him famous. I’ll make him famous… whether he wants me to or not.

Jacobs: (dryly) How magnanimous of you.

Renegade: Don’t get smart with me, asshole. You get more airtime on a week-to-week basis than anyone, you realize that? We go out and mutilate ourselves every week, and what do you do? Hold a f*ckin’ microphone in my face some more and ask me how I feel.

Now firmly frozen in place, Kevin Jacobs can manage little more than a strangled response.

Jacobs: And if I told you that Mike Hardy wasn’t in the building tonight…?

Renegade: What?

Rollins’ brow furrows into a deep scowl as he clutches the microphone around Jacobs’ hands, jerking it from his grip with little resistance. Jabbing an index finger into Jacobs’ breastbone, The Renegade almost oozes with venom.

Renegade: He’s not here tonight? He’s wrestling Capo for Christ’s sake! If Hickenbottom thinks that a few staples in the ass are excuse enough to put me on hold, he’s got another thing comin’ come Dangerous Games.

Clenching the mic tight enough to turn his knuckles white, The Renegade finishes:

Renegade: I promise you th-…!

Voice: Rich Rollins!

His focus shattered, The Renegade turns around as a new face casually saunters into view against the WorldWide backdrop. His suit tailored with a thread-count higher than most men’s annual income, “Scandalous” Ryan Stephens offers a slick grin as he slides into position next to Rollins.

Unperturbed, Rollins addresses the newcomer.

Renegade: Ah, Mr. Stephens. To what do we owe this pleasure?

Stephens: You saw my little proposal last week, Rollins?

The Renegade merely shrugs.

Renegade: I saw a lot of things last week. Including that shit hole you tried to call your office. Perhaps you should refresh my memory.

Stephens remains very calm and poised as per usual. He just patiently eyes Rollins and rubs his fingers together, the universal symbol for money. Rollins shrugs again and smirks.

Renegade: You like to let your bank account do your talking, don’t you?

Stephens: Actually, I enjoy the face time. Hell Rich, with the ridiculous talent around here… let’s be honest, this company NEEDS my face time. My bank account just extends its’ helping hand as a nice form of persuasion.

Renegade: I see. (he hesitates) Well, I won’t deny that as a wrestler I could always use a little more cash in my pockets, but I trust you about as far as I can throw you Stephens.

Stephens: That’s probably a fair assessment, but you aren’t even in the battle royal Rich. You might as well put some stock in the winner, am I right?

Rollins just laughs smugly at the comment.

Renegade: You really think you can buy me, don’t you Stephens?

Stephens gives a slight nod.

Stephens: Every man has his price, Rich. Besides you’re one of the few bums around here that could actually wrestle his way out of a paper bag. I can’t trust most of these guys to cash in on their end of the agreement merely because they might not be capable of such a task. (he pauses) But you Rich, with you I would be more inclined to think that I wasn’t wasting my investment. I didn’t get wealthy making stupid decisions, my friend.

A small light flickers in the pupils of The Renegade’s cold eyes, but to Stephens he simply frowns.

Renegade: I think you’re barking up the wrong tree, hombre. I ain’t nobody’s “friend.”

Stephens holds up his hand apologetically.

Stephens: Suit yourself. Let me know when you change your mind.

Content with the banter, Stephens eyes Rollins again, making certain that his argument haw sunk in. Certain that is has, “Scandalous” Ryan Stephens casually disappears with an evil smile draped across his face.

Turning back around to a face a rather confused Kevin Jacobs, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins chuckles.

Renegade: F*cking politics.

And with that, he leaves Jacobs holding a live mic on an empty stage.

Singles Match – One Fall

Twizzy vs. Shawn Johnson -by Andrew

As “Summer‘s Overture” by Clint Mansell hits the loudspeakers, a mixed reaction of cheers and catcalls fill the arena as Shawn Johnson makes his entrance, his face focused and attention squarely on the ring. Climbing through the ropes, he lightly shifts his weight from one foot to the other in anticipation, waiting for his opponent.

Bryan: Shawn Johnson is in terrific shape here tonight. I hope he brought a full tank, because it doesn’t look like our resident Addict is ready to be sent home tonight.

Pete: Nobody goes on a full tank nowadays… not with these gas prices!

Clint Mansell fades away to be replaced by “Life‘s Sweet Drug” by Slash‘s Snakepit, and The Twisted One bursts through the curtain to a warm ovation from the fans. Staring a hole in his opponent in the ring, Twizzy points a finger and immediately begins trash-talking as he makes his way down the aisle.

Twizzy: Goat… IS…

Crowd: GOOD~!!

Sparse applause.

Bryan: Johnson isn’t about to take any of that… he’s tearing out of the ring and making a bee-line for Twisted!

Pete: And here comes Twizzy!

Bryan: Johnson ducks an immediate clothesline from the Co-Dependant and comes back in with a pair of solid right hooks, rocking Twisted back on his heels! And the referee rings the bell!

DING DING DING!

Twisted slumps back against the guardrail, fending off the hyped-up Shawn Johnson’s assault. Blocking another right hand, Twizzy grabs Shawn’s head and slams his face into the security guardrail, stepping back to take a breath.

Pete: That-a-baby! Did I ever tell you that Twizzy has a doctorate in dentistry?

Bryan: No, you failed to mention that.

Pete: Yeah, look! He just rearranged Shawn Johnson’s teeth!

The referee calls the count at eight, threatening to throw the match out if both competitors don’t get into the ring. Grabbing hold of Johnson, Twisted rolls the rookie under the bottom rope and follows suit, taking the action into the squared circle proper. Setting his opponent up for a powerbomb, Twisted flashes a grin to the heated crowd, drawing a few scattered chants from the mostly jabbering fanbase.

Bryan: And Twizzy goes for the powerbomb…

Pete: Reversal!! Shawn Johnson with a backdrop, but he holds Twizzy’s legs at the knees! What is he…?

Bryan/Pete: POWERSLAM!!

Shifting Twizzy’s momentum, Johnson holds tight to his opponent’s legs and flips him around back over his head, forcing The Twisted One to crash-land back on the mat. Rolling through it into a cradle pin, the referee moves into position.

Bryan: One! Two! Th… kickout! Twizzy was dazed, but only momentarily! And a swift European uppercut sends Shawn Johnson back against the ropes!

Pete: Twizzy’s got stamina, Bryan. Huffing mothballs all day will do that to you.

Bryan: (sigh) Not to mention kill some brainc…

Pete: Belly-to-belly suplex! Twizzy swooped in and took Shawn Johnson down with…

Bryan: Look! Johnson holds on through the impact and won’t let Twisted go! And he stands them both up… BELLY-TO-BELLY, right back at Twizzy! He just tossed the Pill Popper’s own offence back at him!

Pete: But Twizzy refuses to be exterminated! He holds on to Johnson, and they stand up a third time… belly-to-belly again! Jesus, Twizzy looks pissed.

Finally gaining the upper hand, Twizzy backs against the ropes for extra leverage and comes in with a savage stomp on the back of Johnson’s head, who is rolling around in the middle of the ring. Grabbing the rookie’s legs, Twizzy crosses them over his knee and sits back, locking in a picture-perfect sharpshooter. The crowd starts to get noisy with excitement as Shawn Johnson writhes in panic, desperately reaching for the nearest rope.

Bryan: That sharpshooter is locked, and there is no escape for Shawn Johnson tonight, Pete.

Pete: He can try all he wants, Bryan. All Twizzy has to do is leeeeeaaaannn back a little more…

Twizzy leans back in his posture, putting more pressure on Johnson’s ribs and knees.

Bryan: And the hold sticks! Shawn Johnson is in trouble here folks, and is screaming… what is he saying, Pete? (silence) Pete?

Pete: Hmmm? Oh, sorry Bryan. I was too busy watching the veins pop out of Shawn Johnson’s neck! Lookit those throbbing vessels, Bryan! I could take his heartbeat from here!

Shawn Johnson, his face red with strain, falls limp on the canvas. The referee slides into position and raises his arm once… and it smacks back down on the canvas. His arm goes up a second time… and again, it falls lifeless, prompting Twizzy to shake the sweat off his head in concentration, synching back the sharpshooter as far as possible. The referee lifts Johnson’s arm a third and final time… and Johnson’s hand lashes out at the ref, grabbing the lapel of his striped shirt! Straining with the effort, Shawn Johnson literally grasps the referee for support, climbing up the front of his shirt with both hands as Twizzy shakes his head in disbelief, trying to keep the sharpshooter locked in.

Pete: (laughing) Only in America would three men in that position be construed as live entertainment, Bryan.

Bryan: And Shawn Johnson just will NOT give up! Clinging onto the referee for survival, Johnson whips his body, knocking Twizzy off balance! The sharpshooter breaks!

Coughing from the sudden release, Johnson lays on the canvas, his lower body burning with pain from the immense pressure. Twizzy uses the ropes to pull himself back up and immediately makes a cover on his prone opponent, hooking both legs. The referee goes for the count.

Bryan: One! TWO!! And a kickout from Shawn Johnson! But Twizzy isn’t finished… he hoists Johnson on his shoulders, then lifts him in a gorilla press slam…

Pete: PILL POPPER! That came out of nowhere and caught Johnson like a sack full of bricks!

Bryan: And the cover! ONE!!

Bryan/Pete: TWO!!

Bryan/Pete: THRE… NO!!!
