The Formula Biography Biography Chas and Jonny grew up in Tuscan Arizona, they went to the same school and were basically best friends from the age of about nine, they shared the same passion, wrestling. Jonny though however had different ideas to Chas about which was the proper way to conduct ones self in the ring, favoring mostly heels Jonny like the idea of being a bad guy, he always cheered the villains in movies and got in to moderate trouble as a child. Chas was different. He was a purist. Chas would always do things by the book even if it meant that he would miss out some opportunities, he couldn’t stand the thought of doing wrong and always preached to Jonny the correct way of doing things. When Jonny would get up to no good Chas wouldn’t be far behind he was kind of like what Screech was to Zach. Chas had always been a good wrestler, he made the school team and was a fierce competitor whilst Jonny was the star quarterback he too had a passion for wrestling, they decided to try out for a small Mexican based promotion called Counter Combat Wrestling. At first they were there as a singles competitor, in which they didn’t get much achieved, both were good workers and pretty strong on the mic but the Mexican federation was more Lucha based so they never really made an impact. The duo then traveled to Japan where once again they had minimal success and came home with nothing more then a depleted bank balance and a few scars from the whacky matches they were booked in. Months passed and both Chas and Jonny decided that maybe a career in wrestling wasn’t meant to be, they saw an add in a local paper that was hiring security guards and thought why not we’ll give it a go. They got more then they bargained for. They met a chap called Barry Brimstone, a 50 somewhat character that had the charm and panache of Hugh Hefner. He gave them a job minding a local club that Barry was friends with and let them train in one of his gyms for free, that is where he saw the two in action. Barry approached the two saying that he had been watching them train for a while and liked what he saw, he also mentioned that he friends in the wrestling business and if they were up for it he would put the word round. The two jumped at the chance, but Barry told them that they should join forces as a team due to the fact that it is to hard to make these days as a singles competitor, at first the two were unsure but the thought of carrying on that humdrum security job reassured them it would be the right thing to do. So there we have it two friends joining together to tackle the world of tag team wrestling, will they do it? I suppose you’ll have to tune in to find out.
