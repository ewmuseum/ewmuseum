WorldWide 117 Iowa Veterans Memorial Auditorium — Des Moines, Iowa ---- Alex Colt vs. Primetime Joe Milone DARK MATCH (So make sure you turn on a light) As a bonus to the fans in attendence this week, GCW offered a dark match pitting GCW newcomers Alex Colt and "Primetime" Joe Milone. Since their developmental contract signings a few weeks ago, neither superstar has truly stood out over the other and have both been overshadowed by a third newcomer in Bishop Steele. Both superstars began the match in the ring with little reaction from the fans. Hell - some fans weren't even in the building yet and decided to use the match as extra drinking-in-the-parking-lot time. These fans obviously weren't extremely concerned that the loser of this match would, in fact, have their singles contract terminated. The match began with a very standard exchange of fists between Milone and Colt before Colt could gain the upper hand. Colt then proceeded to dominate Joe Milone technically, keeping the contest on the ground. Much to Colt's dismay, this is exactly where Milone wanted to be. Colt attempted a Figure Four Leglock and that ultimately proved to be the turning point of the contest when Joe Milone was able to reverse the leglock and then transition it into a modified Texas Cloverleaf. Colt wouldn't tap out immediately, but the move surely inflicted some serious damage. Milone then captured the upper hand and used his brute strength to slam Colt up and down each side of the ring. At six foot five, you can bet he was a force to be reckoned with against the much smaller Alex Colt. It wasn't until "Primetime" looked for his signature Walk of Fame finishing manuever that things really spiced up. A frightening HHHOOOWWWWWWLLLLLLL bore throughout the arena and an angry Wolfeinator galloped down to the ring. Wolfeinator gave Milone a big headbutt and sent the Beverly Hills native to the outside of the ring. Keep in mind - Wolfe has a BIG head. But the 'einator wasn't done there. He went on to actually bite the leg of Alex Colt. So much so that Colt's leg fell off his body. The referee had no choice but to call the match and flee for his life. The result? Well since neither Milone or Colt were able to defeat one another, both of their GCW contracts were terminated. And Wolfeinator...well, let's just say he's a little happier now than he was at the start of the night.
