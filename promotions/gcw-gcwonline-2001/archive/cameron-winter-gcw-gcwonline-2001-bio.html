Cameron Winter Biography Real Name Cameron DeAnn Winter Nicknames Cami Hometown Deland, FL Birthday May 8, 1986 Height 5'11 Weight 170 lbs. Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Other Feds N/A Other Fed Titles N/A Other Fed Awards N/A Strengths see below Weaknesses see below Wrestling Style technical Strategy Cameron is very straightforward in the ring. She thinks about wrestling much like chess, where she attempts to keep her strategy flexible, according to what her opponent offers her. Her strategy is to ruffle her opponent by keeping her cool in the face of whatever they can throw at her, which is often a difficult prospect for a rookie wrestler. She does know, however, that she has a lot of technical skill, and is well studied in counters for some of the more popular moves. Her strategy is, in general, to get in and out as quickly and as cleanly as possible. Strengths: Cameron is a very focused individual, and usually a quick thinker. She is often good at reading people, and anticipating what their reaction to a given set of circumstances will be. This has served her well in life ? and she's finding out as she begins her wrestling career that it likewise serves her well in the ring. Cameron likes the idea of a challenge, and treats the ring something like a chess game, where she measures moves and counter-moves very carefully. She thinks things over both in and out of the ring. Practices for her often consist of mapping out all the different ways she can string moves together. While she doesn't have a huge repertoire of them, she is very technically adept within her field. She is not as agile as some women wrestlers, but she is deceptively strong. Weaknesses: The problem with Cami's personality is that it's very Type-A. As mentioned above, she's always trying to think ahead of her opponent ? but if her opponent does something she doesn't expect them to do, this can leave her open to a counter. Cami is usually confident (not to the point of arrogant), but if that cool exterior is shaken by someone throwing her a curveball she didn't expect, she can become rattled and lose her focus. She is by no means used to the idea of huge crowds and big personalities, and this may affect her focus for some time. Finisher Move Winter's Discontent (impact finisher); Snowblind (submission finisher) Finisher Description (Ki Krusher - can be executed a number of different ways, either in the middle of the ring, or with the opponent on the ringpost, or with both opponents on the ringpost). Setup Description Winter's Discontent - snap kick into a side headlock; Snowblind - This move comes out of nowhere. All Cameron needs is a moment to slap it on ? it's quick and devastating, and she is adept at ending matches quickly with it. Special Moves see below Other Moves Cameron learned to wrestle in a very mat-based submission style, and is a stickler for the rules. She won't cheat, even when her opponent is cheating, because she believes very strongly in winning a match via honorable combat. This unwillingness to bend can often lead to problems when the stipulation isn't to that end, or when the referee is willing to let things slide. General Moves: Bow and Arrow rear naked choke dragon sleeper gorilla clutch hold paradise lock (http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves/MilanoParadiseLock.wmv) STF with grapevine katahajime bakatare sliding kick busaiku knee kick lariat (can flip smaller opponents 360) rolling back chop tomahawk chop backdrop suplex snap and spider belly to belly suplexes (her snap doesn't go over the head; spider involves opponent on top rope, and the suplexer bending backwards almost into a tree of woe position to throw the opponent into the ring.) butterfly suplex (usually has to be on smaller opponents) bridging dragon suplex (smaller opponents) bridging and release German suplexes release tiger suplex generally, most kinds of suplexes you can think of, she could probably do, potentially modified for the size of the opponent tree of woe These are by no means all of her moves, just some of the more common types that she would use. If you can think of something that fits this general style, please feel free to use it. Cami's Top Five: These are moves that you will see Cami use very often - just about every match, with rare exceptions. They are in no particular order. 1. lariat - hit right, she can sometimes flip smaller opponents all the way over. 2. half-nelson backbreaker (http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWQ5kTs6x1g) - An excellent way to soften an opponent up for a Snowblind later in the match. 3. top rope kneedrop - to the back or solar plexus 4. This: (don't ask me to describe it) http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pJmrOSHMhwI&NR=1 - Also useful as a setup for a Snowblind 5. piledriver Special Moves: Cameron is a devastating submissionist, and has a number of submissions that may not necessarily be match-enders, but can be used quite effectively to wear down an opponent's body endurance. She will therefore often allow her opponent to break these submissions after she's had them on for long enough for them to start hurting, rather than try to immediately end a match with them.
