Mindy Mercer Biography Real Name Mindy Mercer Nicknames The Caffiene Fiend Hometown Verona, Italy, now fighting out of Toms River, NJ Birthday July 19, 1985 Height 5'8' Weight 122 Biography Mindy Mercer had a fairly short wrestling career from 2004 to 2005, though she turned heads en route to winning a World Championship during her year long run with Global Wrestling. Upon losing the title, Mindy would go over her options that her newfound stardom had given her. Finding herself in numerous marketing deals, she was soon doing commercials and starring in bit roles on TV shows. In April 2007, Mindy found herself burned out on acting, however. Having regained a drive to compete but with little remaining interest in wrestling, she turned to the next best thing: mixed martial arts. An undefeated record at 7-0 in amateur competition made her future in the sport look promising, until relationship problems and a couple of injuries started her on a three fight losing skid. It would take her leaving her boyfriend of four years and ten months of sidelining to fully recover from a knee and back injury to get her to decide that another try at pro wrestling would probably be the best move for her to make. Other Feds Global Other Fed Titles Global World Title, Global Television Title Strengths Speed, frustrating striking, unrelenting pace, heart. Weaknesses Physically weak, thus limiting her moveset against larger opponents, has always refused to tap and will normally risk injury rather than submit. Wrestling Style High Flyer Strategy Early Going Tends to mix up lots of strikes and quick moves that require as little grappling as possible. Her movement and method of attack looks more akin to that of a kickboxing match. While not all that powerful she makes that up with her accuracy. Sometimes she'll even go for the Sugarshocker early on and try to catch her opponent being disrespectful of her lack of power. Early-Midway Point Slows her attack to throw in more typical wrestling moves. Lots of pinfall attempts are used here to keep her opponent on the defensive and if she can she'll latch onto a leg or an arm and try for a quick submission as soon as a kickout occurs. Midway Point This is where Mindy's pace spikes the most. She uses lots of high risk maneuvers at these parts of matches and moves which require more work to pull off. Middle-late Point Lots of high impact moves here. Normally likes to abandon the striking game by now and string together fast moving high risk attacks and a special move or two. The simpler of her Cruiserweight specific moves (IE Hip Toss/Implant DDT) might be attempted regardless of the opponent, as long as, of course, the size difference isn't massive (IE against wrestlers 270 lbs. and up). Nearing the End Here, of course, is where her finishers normally go. The Crash is the more bread and butter of her two finishers but against a tough opponent she'll go straight for the Bitter Jawbreaker, which people rarely kick out of. If someone does manage to kick out of it, she'll bait opponents into climbing the turnbuckles with her and then performing the move again from the top rope. Nothing in particular sets up either move; as long as something fits the context of one of her finishers, it's a potential setup for the finish; whether The Crash is used after a hurricanrana from the top rope, or if the Bitter Jawbreaker is used from Mindy simply clinging to an opponent's arm while they punch. Finisher Move Bitter Jawbreaker / The Crash Finisher Description Mindy puts a foot under the chin of the opponent while grabbing one of their arms. She then leaps back first to the mat with her leg locked straight up, pulling her opponent neck first onto her boot / Shooting Star Legdrop Finisher Setup Move None - all of her moves can be performed at any time. A couple of pink spotlights swirl and circle around the arena. Slowly, the piano fades, however, and the pink lights begin to become red. Then out of nowhere, the heavy hitting opening of 'Blinded By Hatred' by UnSun kicks in full gear, just as an energetic Mindy Mercer comes bounding out into view. Lost between heaven and hell They were able to kill each other Only screams were coming out of their mouths Only screams it was like a bad dream The crowd immediately roars to life as Mindy appears, and she gestures with her arms to them, trying to get them out of their seats. As she gets closer to the ring the red spotlights start to converge over her, keeping her lit in that dark crimson color as she waves and pumps her fists to the crowd. It's too late to reclaim the lost dreams The merciless world made them victims It's too late for the tears that've been shed They'll never grow up, blinded by hatred As she bounces her way down toward the ring, Mindy stops and lifts off a candy necklace from around her neck. She then moves to a security barricade and reaching over to give it to a lucky fan before her attention turns to the ring. Mindy hops in place for a moment, shaking out her limbs before taking two swift leaps; the first one gets her up onto the apron, and the second vaults her over the top rope and into the ring. Sometimes everything was alright They were able to talk together But only, only for a while Their love has made everything better The lights return to normal and Mindy climbs to the second turnbuckle in one corner, playing to the crowd a little bit longer before she settles down in her corner and her music fades.
