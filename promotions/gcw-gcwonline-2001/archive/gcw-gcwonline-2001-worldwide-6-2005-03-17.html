WorldWide 6 Madison Square Garden — New York City, New York ---- WorldWide 6 The GCW logo flashes on screen, and the WorldWide intro begins to play. Rage Against the Machine’s ‘Wake Up’ begins pumping into Madison Square Garden, and the New York fans explode! Fireworks explode on stage in a tremendous eruption as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd. One camera finds a sign reading ‘It’s About Time!’ before finally settling on James Bryan at ringside. Bryan- Welcome to WorldWide, ladies and gentlemen! GCW is back on the air- Danger- -Finally… Bryan- -And we’re live in historic Madison Square Garden for the biggest match yet in GCW! Tonight, six men get a shot at the last remaining spot in the ACWF match for the GCW World Heavyweight championship! Six men will compete in a lumberjack elimination match, with the five men already entered in the ACWF match to keep things under control on the outside! Danger- Well, I sincerely doubt we’ll be seeing any semblance of control in that match, JB! It’s going to be total anarchy, plain and simple! Bryan- There are some very personal rivalries involved in our main event tonight, and they’re sure to explode, but one thing’s for sure… This is going to be a very intense night as there’s only one spot left, just one chance for these six men to fight for, just to get one step closer to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! The camera pans backstage as its faces a parking lot. It’s dark as if the lights were purposely shut off. Suddenly the lights flash on in rows leading up towards the entrance. Just then a smooth black limo pulls to the front of the building as the limo driver escapes his seat and runs around to the back and opens the stretch door. Out pops a couple of exotically dressed women. They are quite striking as they all reach their hands into the limo and pull out a well-dressed casual looking man. Looking quite slick in front of his stunning ladies stands the President himself, Steven Caldera. He takes a few steps from the limo and wrings his neck out from the ride to the arena. The lovely ladies reach right back into the limo and pull out yet another casually handsome man yet this one is sporting more of a James Bond look. He pops his head out revealing his long black hair slicked back to perfection with an Italian cigar in the crease of his mouth. Unlit, the cigar rests there until it is released from his mouth and put in his under vest pocket. It is Michael Hardy. Looking quite fixed and well mannered, he struts up to Steven. Although his strut isn’t quite perfect, he nearly looks as though he’s really living the life now. The ladies charge ahead in their high heels and begin to open the entrance doors. Steven and Mike stand face to face as Mike reaches back into his vest pocket and retrieves the unlit cigar. He places it back in the crease of his mouth. Caldera-- After you Mike. Mike- No, no, Steven, I insist, after you. Steven just gives Mike a big smirk as he adjusts the collar of his fine Armani jacket and casually walks into the building. Mike then for a second quickly makes sure to see if Steven is not looking and throws the cigar out of his mouth and across the parking garage. He then adjusts his jacket collar as well and continues his strut into the building. Bryan- Well it seems as though Mr. Caldera has shown Mike the life of luxury. Danger- Good, poor Mike was ready to move out and rent a trash can with that Sesame Street puppet. You know the green thing in a can?…. Bryan- Oscar the Grouch? Danger- Yeah but I heard you have to have money in order to rent out rooms. HAHAHA! The camera shifts to the backstage area, to a scene of new comer Johnny Lexicon walking through the halls. He has a cell phone planted to his right ear, and a slight smirk on his face. As he walks, he seems to be pretty care free especially for a guy that is about to make his big debut in a few moments time. He rounds a corner, but as he does a door flies open and he almost runs into it. Johnny- ( yelling into the phone ) Damn it I’ll call you back … ( hangs up the phone ) You know you need to … His voice trailed off, as he caught the eye of the man who came out of the locker room in front of him. At first his face went blank, but quickly he regained that arrogant smirk that he had a moment ago. Johnny- Well hello LeStatt, long time no see. LeStatt Knight stood in front of Johnny now, sunglasses covering his eyes and draped in black. The U.S. Title lay wrapped around his shoulder, glistening in the light. Knight uses a hand to remove the sunglasses from his eyes and look directly at Lexicon. LeStatt- It hasn’t been long enough Lex, it’ll never be long enough. Johnny- Aw now come on Knight, is that anyway to treat an old friend ? LeStatt- Get out of my way Johnny Boy, I want nothing to do with you, I was done with you in 3W. Knight now brushes past Lexicon, and tries to make his to his next destination. Lexicon gets a bit of an angry look on his face as Knight ignores him, so before LeStatt could get too far he made his next comment. Johnny- You quitting on me again Knight ? typical. Knight stopped dead in his tracks now, his body is seen heave up and then fall back down as he took in a deep breath, then released it. He then slowly turned his head, his hair falling over his face. LeStatt- You are funny amigo, I’ll give you that. You still feel the need to try and get under my skin ? Well news flash, I don’t want anything to do with you and your juvenile attempts at getting into my head will only succeed in making think even lower of you. Johnny- Oh don’t give me all that high and mighty Bull(censor) ! I know you better than anyone wandering around back here, so you can’t hide behind that mask of yours. Knight now turns completely and walks up to Lexicon, standing merely centimeters from his face. LeStatt- Say what you wish Johnny … if I were you I’d be more worried about your debut match with Casey than I would with me at the moment. If you can prove you are capable of beating an imbecile like Torpid, then maybe we can talk. Johnny- How about I just make your ass tap out right here and right now ? LeStatt- You know what Lexicon ? I am already tired of this bickering, if you want me … then win tonight. You win tonight then next week you meet me in the center of that damn ring and try to take this United States Title away from me. Then I’ll prove that I am better once and for all. Johnny- You’re on Knight … I will go and crush Torpid just for you. Johnny gives a slight chuckle then turns away from Knight and walks his own way, obviously going to find the ring as his match was up next. Knight just remained standing for awhile, staring in lexicon’s general direction. A creepy smile spread across Knight’s face right before his sunglasses are slid back on. LeStatt- Good luck Johnny … good luck. With these final words Knight walks off, going to wherever he needed before being interrupted. The show then heads back to ringside, where our opening bout is almost ready! The arena sits as quiet as it will ever get. People waiting, watching... expecting. BANG! Praise by Sevendust begins to play. BANG!BANG! The entrance explodes, the words 'Johnny Lexicon' slam into the big screen and he steps through the curtain. Dressed for him match he marches to the edge of the platform and looks out at the sea of faces. The fans that know him cheer, the fans that hate him boo, but not a single fan is silent. Casey Torpid suddenly streaks from the back and pummels Lexicon from behind with a silver briefcase. Johnny goes down to his knees and Casey delivers a harsh kick to his kidneys. Johnny starts rolling down the ramp, Casey runs a hand through the twin blue mohawks on his head and follows Lexicon's descend casually. Johnny stops on the mats and immediately starts getting to his feet, Torpid drops the suitcase and approaches Johnny with a right hand. Lexicon blocks the shot and hammers home one of his own, Johnny swings again but Torpid ducks and rolls into the ring. Johnny mounts the apron and walks along it to the turnbuckle. The ref puts himself between the competitors and calls for the match to officially start. Johnny climbs into the ring and they lock up. Johnny powers Casey into the corner and then hits him with an open handed chop to the chest. And another, and then another. Casey explodes and launches a knee into Johnny's gut, and then drives his right elbow into Johnny's head. Torpid hops up onto the middle rope and launches a drop kick into Johnny's shoulder, sending him to mat hard. Casey is on his feet and moving to capitalize, Johnny swings his foot around and hooks Casey's ankle, with his other foot he kicks out at his opponents gut and forces him off balance. Casey falls to the mat as Johnny curls his legs in tight to his chest and spring boards to his feet. The fans erupt as Johnny hammers a kick into Torpid's side and then grabs his head by both mohawks and pulls him up to his feet. Johnny says a few words in Torpid's face and he takes a swing, Johnny leans out of the way and hooks the outstretched arm, pulling Casey about he hooks again and hefts the man in an overhead nelson slam, driving Casey to the mat hard. Johnny scrambles to his feet as Casey rolls for the ropes. Casey is up as Johnny grabs him and throws him into the ropes, he hooks them as Lexicon's drop kick falls short and he goes to the mat. Casey hops up and springboards off the middle rope with an asai moonsault. He pulls Lexicon up and sends him to the corner. Huge body splash makes Johnny's knees weak. Casey starts pulling him back up, Johnny hammers a right into Casey's gut, then a left and another right. He surges to his feet, grabbing Casey as he goes and then brings his groin back down on his knee. Casey reels and stumbles in agony as Johnny stands, spins, and delivers a vicious snap roundhouse that takes Casey down. Johnny falls into the rope and takes a deep breath before charging forward and dropping an elbow, scrambling up and dropping another. Casey rolls out of the ring. Johnny mounts the top turnbuckle as Casey stands up on the outside. Johnny leaps with a suicide splash but misses. Casey uses the guardrails for support as Johnny writhes on the ground. Torpid stomps him once, twice, thrice before grabbing him the shoulder length blond hair and lifting him into the ring. Johnny rolls into the center and lays flat on his back, eyes closed as Torpid climbs the turnbuckles. Johnny is up! He crashes into the ropes and knocks Casey off balance, Torpid straddles the top turnbuckle in pain as Johnny charges in, leaps onto the middle rope and hooks Casey's head under his arm, he surges and falls backwards, Casey holding onto the rope valiantly. He stands now quickly and falls off with a back elbow drop into Lexicon's gut. Johnny groans as Casey gets to his feet a little slowly. Casey calls for Johnny to get up, and Lexicon is only to happy to oblige, pushing himself up as Casey spreads his feet. As soon as Lex raises his head Casey is on him with a right hook. Johnny takes it and answers with one of his own. Back and forth they fire shots, Johnny being the bigger of the two taking an immediate advantage, clubbing one last shot down on Casey's head Johnny grabs his arm and throws him into the ropes. Casey comes back fast and ducks under the clothesline, he comes back again and jumps over the attempted scoop slam, he bounces into the ropes and can't avoid the precision super kick that sends him to the outside. Johnny falls to a single knee and watches Casey roll around on the floor holding his jaw a smirk spreading across his features. The ref's count reaches six before Casey is on his feet, Johnny doesn't risk the count out and dives through the ropes, tackling Casey. Johnny straddles his opponent, holding his head with his left hand and hammering him with rights. Casey struggles and finally manages to swing a foot up and hook Lexicon's head. Freeing himself from the point of no return Casey claws towards the ring. Johnny shakes his head and is on Torpid in a flash, pulling him and practically throwing the man into the ring before climbing up himself. Casey catches him on the apron through and hooks his head, dragging him up and inside with a vertical suplex. Johnny arches his back in pain as Casey pulls himself up on the ropes. Casey stands and turns as Johnny charges at him with a spear. Torpid moves and Johnny goes into the corner hard. Casey hammers his back with fists and kicks before pulling him up backwards and hooking his head upside down, he spins and drops with a neck breaker. Johnny lays still as Casey crawls over him for a cover. Johnny kicks out at the two count and frustrates Casey into getting up and giving him a swift boot to the chest. Casey starts climbing the turnbuckles, as soon as Torpid's back is to him Johnny gets to his feet and grabs Torpid from behind, pulling him off the turnbuckles and slamming him into the mat, with both legs in hand Johnny steps and flips before sitting down into a Boston crab. Casey howls but Johnny's only reply is to wrench his legs even harder. Somebody’s coming down the ramp, the as of yet undebuted Damien Banks storms around the ring and throws somebody out of a chair. Folding it up he climbs the stairs, Johnny notices the new comer and releases the hold he was sure was going to win the match for him. Banks steps into the ring and looks into Johnny's emotionless face, the folded chair casually resting on his shoulder. They say a few words that nobody can hear, suddenly Casey is tilting a bottle of booze back and flips Johnny around. Clang! Johnny takes the chair shot to the back of the head and falls on his face just as Casey ignites his lighter and. FWOOSH!!! The pillar of flame erupts from Torpids mouth and into Banks’ face. The man falls down clutching as his seared flesh as Casey looks a little confused. The bell rings and the ref declares Lexicon the winner. Torpid's eyes go wide and he turns them on Banks, he charges at the man but the ref hooks his arm. Notices his vulnerable location Nick takes that moment to slip out of the ring. Winner: Johnny Lexicon Casey howls obscenities at him and even goes so far as to throw his zippo at the man as WorldWide fades to a commercial break. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Kyle Niker and Connor Pearce approach a locker room door. Both men are dressed in a style that can only be compared to boxing corner men. Kyle has a towel draped around his neck and a pack of surgical utensils at his side, while Connor is sloshing around a spit bucket that oddly enough, already seems full. Before Connor pushes through into the room, Kyle grabs his arm... Kyle- Hold on a second, man. Maybe we should just kinda talk through how things should play out tonight? Connor- What do you mean? Kyle- Well, neither I, nor you really want to be babysitting Zippy. There's nothing I would love more than to abandon him to Lestatt tonight. Connor-*intrigued* Go on... Kyle- So we could do our natural thing and just no show...but then we wouldn't get our contracts. Connor- In theory...but maybe Caldera is using this as a test to see how assertive we are. Maybe he just wants us to leave Zippy to the wolves, stand up for our own interests? Is that what you're getting at? Kyle- Yeah...but then again...maybe he really does want us to look after the little guy... Connor-*sighing*Oh, that the world were not so ambiguous. Suddenly, from behind Connor, the door crashes open, knocking the spit bucket from his hands, delivering the contents into Kyle's face. There stands Zippy with a smile plastered to his face and that stupid "zippy tail" (as he calls it) dangling before him. Zippy- Hi!! Kyle- *sighing* Hey Zippy... Zippy- I'm bored... Quick! Put in Tom and Jerry episode 15!! No! Better yet! I wanna play tag! Kyle, who is now wiping the spit from his face, and Connor's let out a sigh in unison. Obviously, they've played tag many... many times throughout the week. Connor- Zippy, shouldn't you be getting ready for your match instead of playing tag? Zippy- Nope! I wanna play tag! Kyle- We've played it at least twenty times this week, can't we give it a rest? Zippy- Nope! Conner and Kyle look at each other before letting out another sigh. Before either of the two can answer, Zippy skips up to Kyle and taps him on the shoulder. Zippy- Tag! You're it! No tag-backs! Zippy hurries and dashes from the locker room, and back down the hall. Kyle and Conner look at each other, sigh once more, and follow him out of the locker room towards the ring. O’Furtuna hits as LeStatt Knight emerges from the curtains to a medium pop from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring and enters. Knight awaits his opponent as Zippy’s Song comes booming from the overheads. The crowd goes ballistic awaiting the arrival of the Beloved Zippy. Zippy’s song continues to run as no one exits from the curtains. Knight has a look of sure confusion as Zippy’s Song nears its end. Suddenly it fades out as the crowd is heard with much conversation as if they all stood in confusion as well. Suddenly there are screams heard from the deepest and highest of the nose bleed section as everyone in the audience turns to see a spotlight aimed on a crouching Zippy, hidden Wrestler. He is crouching on a seat pretending to be a fan simply watching the event. Just then, two more spotlights cues up on Kyle Niker and Conner Pearce. They are seen recklessly looking for Zippy. Zippy’s eyes glow in shock as he quickly yanks a GCW program book from the Fan sitting to his right. He opens the program and puts it in front of his face. Conner and Kyle come searching through the crowd for Zippy as they slowly approach him. Yet it seems to be that they don’t notice the man with the program to be Zippy. They end up walking directly past him and Zippy quickly hands the program back to the fan and tags Kyle from behind as Kyle tips forward directly into Conner as they both tip and fall. Popcorn explodes into the air as Zippy takes off back through the closest exit. Knight remains in the ring not seeming to be amused by this charade. He simply waits for Zippy patiently. Suddenly Zippy, quick with his speed, is in the middle bleacher section directly below Conner and Kyle above still rising to their feet fighting through a hysterical crowd. They stop and approach the nosebleed guardrail and look from there to spot Zippy as Zippy directly below them sticks his head out and looks up. Niker and Pearce can’t seem to locate him as they get frustrated and hang their heads low. When they hang their heads they see Zippy giggling from the section below them as their eyes begin to glow in joy. Zippy simply sticks his tongue out and spits saliva into the air and takes off once again. Kyle and Conner set off in different ways for the exits. Knight remains patient and calm although he still hasn’t found a humor for the immaturity taking place. Zippy’s song blares once again as he comes storming out from the back and slips and nearly tumbles down the entranceway. Conner and Kyle appear from the midsection bleachers and realize Zippy is towards the ring now. They storm back through the exits and a couple of minutes later emerge from the back as well. Zippy is seen sitting on the barrier wall with fans seen laughing behind him as Zippy catches his breathe. Kyle and Conner quickly say something to each other as they slowly walk around the ring in both directions causing Zippy to run to the back of the ring behind the aprons as he begins to think where to take off to. He turns to jump the barrier again but Kyle quickly rampages full speed at Zippy and takes off into the air and tags Zippy’s leg as he comes crashing to the pads below. Zippy unintentionally flips face first over the barricade wall. Niker gathers to his feet and speeds off. Zippy returns to his feet and the pursuit begins. Conner sees Kyle on the opposing side of the ring taking off in the other direction. Kyle mouths out to Conner that he Tagged Zippy. Conner, as joyful as can be, smirks and turns to take off. But Zippy who seems to be standing directly behind him just reaches out and taps his shoulder to catch his attention. Conner’s smirk immediately turns to a sour grin. He turns to quickly tag back Zippy but when he turns to tag Zippy back, all he sees is nothing. He focuses around and comes upon a pair of feet seen kicking desperately and quickly disappears under the apron to the ring. Kyle, nearly in the back again, is voiced to return back to the ring from Conner. Kyle lets out a cry of agony and comes stamping down to the ring in pouting fashion. Kyle approaches Conner as they voice back and forth and nod in acceptance. Niker and Pearce separate to opposite side of the ring. They nod once more and take off under the ring. A loud scream is heard as a cloud of smoke powers itself from under the ring. The crowd sits and awaits the devastation that could have taken place. Zippy crawls from under the ring with a fire extinguisher wrapped around his foot. Conner comes shimmying out from under the ring wiping his eyes from the powder to which has temporarily blinded him. The crowd is laughing tremendously. Zippy turns and smiles to see Conner’s face powdered fully. Zippy quickly stands up as Kyle reaches out from under the ring and slaps Zippy’s ankle. It trips Zippy up as he takes a spill. Kyle quickly exits from under the ring and stands to his feet. He stands in shock and as a last minute idea takes off into the ring. Knight remains patient and sees Kyle running into the ring. Zippy hops onto the apron and enters through the ropes. The bell sounds as Zippy chases after Kyle directly past Knight. Knight sees Kyle pass and see a speedy Zippy approaching as Knight extends his arm. Zippy’s face becomes traumatized as he puts on the brakes. But it’s too late. His face slams directly into Knight’s arm as he falls like a sack of bricks. The crowd cracks up as Knight, as cocky as they come, reaches his foot out and places it on the chest of Zippy. 1……2…..3…..As simple as that the match is over. Winner: And Still GCW United States Champion, LeStatt Knight Knight walks over and takes his US title from the time keeper handing it to him from the outside. Knight looks as Zippy, then continues on with no expression. He exits the ring to a crowd of applause and laughs and makes his way to the back. Kyle and Conner enter the ring and see Zippy laid out. They reach their faces over his limp body as Zippy sits up fast and makes Kyle and Conner jump back in freight as Zippy gathers and tags Kyle and takes off for the back. The crowd applauses Zippy as Conner and Kyle simply look at each other, shrug, and take off after Zippy. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ WorldWide returns and ‘Established’ hits overhead. The crowd explodes as President Caldera slowly struts on stage, clad in his perfect Armani suit and an expensive pair of sunglasses. After a moment of basking in the cheers, he turns and points to the entrance, where Mike Hardy appears behind him. The cheers intensify as Hardy appears wearing his own perfectly tailored Armani suit! Hardy removes his own pair of designer sunglasses and tucks them into his jacket pocket as he and Caldera walk side by side to the ring. Andrews- Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome Mike Hardy, and his manager, President Steven Caldera! Bryan- Here they come! The most dominant duo in GCW today! Mike Hardy has been on an incredible run after weeks of futility, and tonight he’s got a shot at entering the ACWF match and getting a shot at the GCW World title! Danger- Well, like so many before him, he’s getting a title shot and he has only Steven Caldera to thank! It seems Caldera always has someone he’s guiding to the top, and I’m not buying this whole ‘I’ve changed!’ thing. Caldera is the same old Caldera, and this time he’s just helping a different guy! Bryan- But Hardy’s done it all himself, Mr. D. Caldera has imparted unto him his knowledge, and Hardy has turned it into this incredible run! Hardy hops onto the turnbuckle and poses to the crowd as Caldera takes a microphone. His music fades as the crowd chants ‘Hardy! Hardy! Hardy!’ Caldera- Ladies and gentlemen, I’ve come out here tonight to do a little housekeeping. It seems my house is a bit out of order, and I think it’s time to set a few things straight. So, first off, I would like to call one Tristain Mayhem to the ring! The crowd boos as Mayhem emerges on stage to no music. He has a slight smirk on his face as he saunters to the ring, pausing only to give the fans the finger. Mayhem slowly gets to the ring and Hardy parts the ropes, welcoming Mayhem in. Mayhem scoffs at Hardy and simply slides in under the ropes, stopping right in front of Caldera’s face. Mayhem reaches out for the microphone but Caldera snatches it back, and shoves Mayhem away. Caldera- No no, you’re done talking. This time you’re going to listen. Tristain, you and I were once close friends. As such, I welcomed you back to GCW despite this new ego of yours. Well, no more. You’re a waste of damn space, Tristain, and I’m not going to take it any more. Danger- Is that really necessary? Bryan- Well, we’ve known Caldera to take care of people before, but this is the first time I’ve ever seen him fire someone on camera! I promised not to manipulate this company any more, but damnit, I’m in control! Now, on to more important business. Tonight, six men get a chance to compete in the ACWF match for the GCW World Title, coming up right here next week! And one of those men is to my left… Mr. Mike Hardy! The crowd explodes with cheers, but the sound is cut off by ‘Do The Evolution.’ The crowd begins to boo heavily as Toxic storms out onto the stage. Toxic- You know, I was hoping the two of you would give me a chance to appeal to reason one more time. Mike, I’ve been watching you these past few weeks. Hell, I’ve seen you up close and personal… The crowd begins to chant ‘You lost! You lost! You lost!’ Toxic’s face tenses up in anger, but he pushes on. Toxic- And I’ll admit, you’re on a roll. I just hope you realize that the only reason you are where you are, Mike, is because Steven Caldera put you there. There might not be any Establishment any more, and these people might be too stupid to see it… The crowd explodes with boos, and Toxic seems satisfied. Toxic- …But you have to see, Mike, you’re a puppet. The only reason you’ve gotten this far is because Caldera manufactured it that way. But I wanted to give you, Steven, one last chance to see things my way. I wanted to give you a chance to do the right thing. You can either call off this match tonight and award me the last spot in ACWF the way it should be… or I cripple your little buddy there once and for all. The crowd boos as Caldera looks at Hardy. He mutters something to Hardy and shrugs, and Hardy grabs the microphone. Hardy- You’ll cripple me? You’re not getting anything handed to you, Toxic. And all I have to say to you is that you’d better hope you don’t end up alone in the ring with me… or I’ll cripple you! The crowd explodes with a shockingly strong array of cheers. Toxic takes a step back, surprised by the support for Hardy. Toxic finally smirks and glares back at the ring. Toxic- That’s the way you want it then, is it? Fine. But Steven, remember this. The blood spilled in that ring tonight won’t be on my hands. It’ll be on yours. With that, Toxic drops the microphone and turns to leave. A number of fans are gathered on the ramps above the parking area, standing in the rain to watch the upcoming brawl. Jason Dread stands in an open area of the parking lot, wiping the water off his face as he waits for his opponent. A bloody Dread hauls himself to his feet, and stares painfully at Octavian as he approaches. Octavian is clad in black jeans and a black shirt, and wears the Imperial Roman Championship belt around his waist. Octavian pulls the belt off and grabs Dread by the throat, shoving the belt in his face as he screams at him. Octavian pulls Dread between his legs, and looks up at the screaming fans above. Octavian gets back to his feet and raises the Imperial Roman Title belt in the air, glaring down at the broken and bloody body of Jason Dread. The street fight never really got under way, but it’s definitely over!
