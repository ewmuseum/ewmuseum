Dorian Ryan Biography Real Name Dorian Seth Ryan Hometown Detroit, MI Birthday May 16, 1982 Height 6'1 Weight 230 Fed Titles 1x GCW United States Champion Other Feds MSW, PCW, NWCC, NWCP, DWS Other Fed Awards Winner of the NWCP Golden Ticket Match, NWC Hurricane Cup Finals Strengths Speed, able to vary offense Manager Araceli Negra Weaknesses Susceptible to submissions, tendency to take risks Wrestling Style Japanese Influenced Technical Wrestling Strategy In the beginning of the match, Dorian is going to try and avoid the opponent and use his speed to his advantage. He will basically be using a hit-and-run style offense. He knows that early on, especially against larger opponents, that this is goign to be what gives him an advantage, as he's not the most powerful wrestler out there. As the match gets going, you're going to see him be a little more daring with his moves. This is where his aerial offense is going to come in. He knows that even power wrestlers are less than likely to be able to prevent a fast high risk offense if they've been slowed down;. Dorian will try to get the opponent on the ground near the end of the match, going from his aerial offense back into a technical base. As the Muscular Bomb requires the opponent be grounded before he executes it, he knows that he has to have the opponent on the mat long enough to begin the wheelbarrow transition. If it's a wrestler that he's having a hard time keeping down, he'll start throwing in kicks to the outer knee and thigh to soften them up. If he's unable to throw the Muscular Bomb, he has the AMF that he can also rely on as a secondary finisher at times.
