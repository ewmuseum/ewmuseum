GCW — Card

Meltdown 15, 27 June 2001

27 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 15 Duluth Entertainment Center — Duluth, Minnesota ---- Meltdown #15 (The lights dim in the arena, and the crowd begins to buzz. Suddenly, pyro explodes on the stage, lighting up the giant GCW logo. The crowd goes crazy as the loud explosions continue, lighting up the arena. James Bryan chimes in over the mayhem.) Bryan- Last Friday night we saw the crowning of a new World Champion, and we’ve got a lot of controversy to settle tonight! Fans, I’m James Bryan here with David Yale, and we’re live on Meltdown! Yale- Shane Lane is the champion, JB! That’s all that matters! All I care about is that tonight, we will get to see the in-ring return of Khan! I can’t wait! This guy is gonna rip the US title away from Luke Hanson right here tonight! Bryan- Hanson certainly does have a tremendous challenge here in our main event tonight! (Suddenly, Born to be Wild hits. The crowd explodes to their feet as Rich Rollins walks to the ring. He signals to the fans, and is carrying a book of some sort. He steps into the ring as the crowd cheers on.) Andrews- Please welcome. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins! Yale- What is he doing down here!? Bryan- This is the first time we’ve seen the Renegade since he lost the world title at Dangerous Games! Rollins- Now, I suppose everyone’s wondering why I’m out here tonight. Well, I have two reasons for coming to the ring right now. First. . . Is to shamelessly plug my new book, Rich Rollins: Life of Hard Knocks! (The crowd cheers as Rollins holds the book up to the camera, smiling.) Rollins- And that will be out in bookstores everywhere TOMORROW! As for the second reason. . . The second reason involves two pieces of crap, and a belt that belongs to me! (The crowd cheers.) Rollins- Jhub, you and Caldera fooled me, I admit. But that’s in the past. The reason I’m pissed at you now is that you lost my belt before I got a rematch! So what I’m proposing is that you come on down here, you and I fight, and the winner gets a shot at Shane next week! (The crowd cheers. The Establishment Theme hits, and they begin to boo like hell. President Caldera walks to the ramp, microphone in one hand, a book in the other.) Caldera- Now, now, now, Mr. Rollins. You lost that belt, you have no say in when you get a number one contender’s match. I will decide who will face who for such an honor. But, I do rather like the idea. So Rich, I’ll tell you what. Next week, here on Meltdown, we will see a fatal fourway match for the number one contendership to the world title! (The crowd cheers.) Caldera- And Rich, you and the Jhub will be involved. As for the other two. . . Well, the other two will be decided as the night progresses. Oh, and Rich. . . If you ever waste my airtime to plug your book again, I’ll have security run your ass out on a rail! (The crowd boos.) Caldera- But while we’re on the subject of books, I would like to shamelessly plug my book, also coming out tomorrow! (The crowd boos, but laughs at the hypocrisy.) Caldera- So Rich, get yourself ready for next week! (The Establishment Theme hits again, as Rollins raises his book into the air, and leaves the ring.) Bryan- Wow, a fatal fourway, next week on Meltdown! What a night that will be! Yale- And who could President Caldera be thinking about for the other two spots? I mean, there are so many guys in the back that deserve such an opportunity. Bryan- Well, fans, don’t forget, Sunday, July 22, live on Pay-Per-View, GCW presents Annihilation! (The Annihilation promo ad appears on the screen.) Bryan- It’s going to be the biggest GCW Pay-Per-View yet! I can’t wait to see what will happen there! ( (Debonair by Dope hits, and the crowd explodes in boos. Khan walks to the ring, removes his robe, and steps inside.) Andrews- The following contest, set for one fall, is for the GCW United States Championship! Introducing first, from Hokkaido, Japan, he weighs in at 270 pounds. . . Khan! Bryan- Well, Khan is a former United States Champion back in the CWU, and he can certainly get things done in the ring! (Khan stares out at the crowd, until Fuel hits. The crowd goes crazy as Hanson walks to the ring, looking very focused.) Andrews- And his opponent, from Atlanta, Georgia, he weighs in at 376 pounds, and he is the GCW United States Champion. . . ‘The Fueler’ Luke Hanson! (Hanson enters the ring, and stares down Khan. Hanson hands over his belt, and the bell rings.) Bryan- And here we go! Khan is simply staring Hanson down from across the ring, but Hanson doesn’t seem to be intimidated! Hanson rushes in on Khan now, but Khan sidesteps him, and shoves him into the turnbuckle! Yale- And Khan takes Hanson’s legs out from under him with a leg sweep! Hanson hits the mat, and Khan is already on him with one, two, three quick chops to the neck! But Hanson shoves Khan off, and springs to his feet! Bryan- Khan stands stoically on the other side of the ring once again, as Hanson moves in! Khan sidesteps him again, but Hanson leaps to the bottom turnbuckle, and he’s not falling for the same thing twice! Yale- Khan gets to his feet, and Hanson jumps off with an axe handle smash! Khan goes down now, and Hanson stomps away! Khan rolls back and nips up, catching Hanson off guard! Bryan- Khan grabs Hanson’s arm and wrings it over, and now catches Hanson with a mean kick to the sternum! Hanson goes down, and Khan gets a head of steam off the ropes! He runs back in with a dropkick to the back of the head! Yale- And Khan is showing us why he’s the best! Khan heads for his favorite spot on the to turnbuckle, and this is where he’s dangerous! Hanson slowly gets to his feet, and Khan leaps off the top! Bryan- But Hanson catches him in midair! And he brings him down into a powerslam! What a move by the champion! Here’s the cover! ONE! TWO!!! THRE- Kickout by Khan! Yale- Hanson lifts Khan right back to his feet, and sends him into the ropes! Khan bounces back, and ducks a big boot from Hanson! Khan comes off the other side, and leaps onto Hanson’s shoulders! (The crowd oohs as Khan sits on the big man’s shoulders.) Bryan- And Khan is unloading with punches to the skull! Wait, Hanson lines Khan up, and hits a big powerbomb- No, Khan reverses into a hurricanrana! Impressive agility from Khan! This is really size against speed here! Yale- And Khan has the upper hand! Khan is heading up to the top rope once again, as Hanson slowly gets to his feet! Hanson is up, as Khan measures him and takes off! Missile dropkick! But Hanson stays on his feet! Bryan- Khan is getting frustrated, as he climbs to the top rope again! Another missile dropkick! But Hanson still doesn’t go down! He’s staggered, but still on his feet! And Khan is going up a third time! Yale- Hanson is stunned, as Khan takes off for a third time! Oh, but Hanson catches him with a giant boot to the face on the way down! My God! (The crowd oohs at the impact) Yale- Cover! ONE! TWO!! THR- Kickout by Khan! That is impressive, JB! Bryan- Great resilience by Khan. Hanson lifts Khan to his feet, and lifts him all the way up! Guerilla press! Hanson has Khan all the way up, in an impressive display of strength! And Hanson drops Khan right on his face! Yale- Hold the phone here. . . Hanson is going up to the top now! Khan is lying prone in the ring, as Hanson measures him off! Here it comes. . . FIVE-STAR FUELER SPLASH! Bryan- But Khan rolls out of the way! Hanson goes crashing to the mat! And Khan has a tremendous opening here! He’s slowly getting up, and heading for the turnbuckle again! Hanson is still down, as Khan goes up! Yale- Khan finally gets to the top rope, and takes off! Guillotine leg drop connects! What a move by Khan! That could do it right there! ONE! TWO!! NEW CHAMPION- KICKOUT by Hanson! Wow! Bryan-
