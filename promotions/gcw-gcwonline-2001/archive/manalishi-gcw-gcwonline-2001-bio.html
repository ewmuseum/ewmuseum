Manalishi Biography Real Name Chris Kannon Nicknames Chrissy-Poo Hometown Madison, Wisconsin Birthday March 17, 1986 Height 5' 9' Weight 185 Fed Titles Television Title Fed Awards N/A Biography Appearance: Chris Kannon is a smaller wrestler, standing only 5'9' and weighing in at 185. Since he last appeared in GCW, he has put on more muscle, adding to his power, but still, he's a small wrestler in a business full of giants... Kannon now has shoulder length blonde hair, and a long red (natural, not dyed) goatee. Out of the ring, he wears black combat boots and torn jean or camo shorts. In the ring, he wears a green lucha mask and black karate gi pants. For a shirt, he still likes to either where his custom made shirts (Chris will take magic markers and draw his own shirts of other GCW wrestlers) or he will wear a shirt his old favorite of Frank Zappa sitting on the toilet. Bio: Chrissy-Poo was raised in the Mid-west. Then he became the color commentator on the Universal Wrestling Federation's Wednesday Night Underground television show based out of Chicago. When UWF cancelled the show, leaving Chris out of work... he turned to GCW and stepped into the ring for the first time as a pro-wrestler. Oh yeah, he once worked in a federation called SCW and took over the masked persona of Lucha Libre legend, El Chupacabra, becoming El Chupacabra II. SCW sucked though and he jobbed hardcore there. But back to GCW... Chrissy-Poo would turn more bad ass and become Chris Kannon, and he won the TV title from Bryan Mayhem/O'Neil. After losing the title, Kannon took about a year off to build up some muscle and trained intensely until his return to GCW... now! BUT THAT RETURN DIDN'T GO ANYWHERE! Fast forward a few more years and Kannon returned as the masked "Manalishi" alongside his tag team partner in the Schizophrenic Demonoids, Kenny Mercury. RECORD ------ World Wide: Aiden Jackson def. Cross Body Block X-Factor Face Plant thingy Swinging Neckbreaker Cactus Clothesline Monkey Flip Running Bulldog Thrust/Super Kick Axe Kick Top Rope Favorites -------- Flying Elbow Senton Bomb Missile Dropkick Super-Rana Tornado DDT Dragon-Rana Flying Enzuguiri Submission Favorites -------- Texas Cloverleaf Leg Cradle Camel Clutch Notes -------- Chrisy-Poo is by no means limited to this arsenal listed above. Chrissy-Poo is scrappy as hell and being very small, he has to make up for his size disadvantage with innovative moves that he makes up on the fly using any means neccessary (weapons... ala Sabu/Cactus Jack) ...MATCH WRITING AID... Section 1: Start off moves. Chrissy-Poo uses his speed advantage to try and out maneuver his opponents. He can easily slip out/dodge of his opponents' moves, then turn around with a dropkick or enzuguiri or something. He uses a 'hit and run' tactic at this point of the match... ie... Opponent goes for a clotheline, Chrissy rolls out of the way, pops back up behind them, then lands a dropkick. The opponent will be able to easily recover, thus getting up and going right back for Chrissy, thus why he must back off and start the cycle all over again. Section 2: With the Advantage Chrissy-Poo usually doesn't have the advantage But if he does he his opponent down somehow... he'll usually go to the top rope and try and drill some kind of move into the opponent, looking for a big pay off, since its uncommon Chrissy gets his opponent down for any extended period of time more than a few times per match. Section 3: On the defensive One of Chrissy-Poo's defensive strategy's is to pull some kind of counter out of nowhere to catch his breath. Chrissy-Poo can take quite the beating, playing into his ultimate underdog gimmick. While the opponent plugs away at Chrissy minute after minute... Chrissy might just suddenly hit them with some kind of counter... DDT/Bulldog/Huricanrana etc... This makes the opponent lose stamina and gives Chrissy a chance to go up top, again looking for a big pay off. Section 4: Ending Minutes Usually by this time, Chrissy-Poo has had the piss beat out of him, and the crowd is rallying behind him. He can slip out of something or counter a big impact move with a lightning quick thrust/super kick to his opponent. Dropping them just long enough to set up for his finisher. Also, whilst getting piss-pounded, Chrissy looks for opportunities for quick roll up and the like... Section 5: Finishers The F-Poo: The F-Poo is almost always used after Chrissy-Poo quickly turns the tides at the very end of a match when things looked the bleakest. With a small opening after a Thrust kick or some other counter move, Chrissy goes for the F-Poo, a Death Valley Driver. Unfortunately, Chrissy-Poo hasn't figured out that he's not strong enough to actually successfully hit this move. This leads to Chrissy straining to pick up his opponent in the Death Valley Driver position... leading to one final big jerk, sending Chrissy's head and back ramming into the opponents crotch... causing much pain and giving Chrissy an opportunity to hit a different finisher... The Acid Drop: Rocker-Dropper/Fame-Ass-er from off the top rope onto the opponent standing in the ring... usually done immediately after the F-Poo fails. His true finisher. Arabian Face-Buster w/ chair- When available... Other than those... many times Chrissy will win with some kind of weapon or roll-up, pulling a win out of the bag when things like the worst for him. Section 6: Cheating and Retaliation Chrissy-Poo never 'cheats' per say... but he will use weapons when available. No eye raking/choking etc though. Section 7: Manager's/Partner's interference If Chrissy-Poo is ever in a stable... they will probably interfere, yes. Chrissy doesn't have a manager, though Section 8: Chain Moves To be honest... Chrissy-Poo is usually getting the crap kicked out of him way too often to really use these, however, when he does get a chance... -Monkey Flip into the turnbuckles, setting up a tree of woe where Chrissy hits a running dropkick from the reverse corner. Section 9: Strengths To be honest, everyone's larger than him. This leads to a speed advantage. Chrissy-Poo also has a huge 'heart' and a great amount of determination. He can pull counter moves out of anywhere... though not usually amazing, they get the job done. Section 10: Weaknesses Always has a huge weight/height/strength disadvantage. Can be man-handled and tossed around. Takes all sorts of crazy bumps (not self inflicted... he is just everyone's bitch)... GCW fans actually have a running pool on how long it takes before someone kills him. Summary: Chrissy-Poo is the ultimate underdog. He starts off with hit and run tactics... but the opponent usually catches up and overcomes them... leading to Chriss getting destroyed. Now and then he'll try a roll-up leading to a kick out and more beatings. Using a big counter or a weapon, Chrissy-Poo gains a quick advantage, allowing him to use The Acid Drop or some kind of weapon to pick up the win. He's not a great cruiser in the likes of say Ultimo Dragon, Juvi, or Rey Jr. Think more of Sho Funaki or Spike Dudley... but more high flying than Spike and less 'wrestling' than Funaki.
