Twizzy Biography Real Name Davis Leonard Branxton Nicknames The Junkie Man, The Co-Dependent, The Addict, and The RX Bandit Hometown New York City, New York Birthday 7/31/70 Height 6' 4 Weight 255 Fed Titles World Heavyweight Champion and Tag Team Champion (x2) Fed Awards 2003 Dangerous Games Winner Biography Davis Leonard Branxton always fought with his family. His father was a drunk, his mother was a spectator, and his younger sister was just as little and scared as he was. In high school young Davis played numerous sports including baseball, football, and wrestling. He excelled at wrestling and as he graduated with no prospects, no college to go to, and no family to give him support he ventured into the professional wrestling business. Davis, feeling tossed away by his family, gave himself the moniker of Trash, which he wrestled under until his late twenties when his career finally began to turn around. He had been wandering the independents and taking date after date after date traveling all over the world, never staying in one space for long (or even short) amounts of time. Davis got married to a beautiful woman in New York City, where his apartment lay, at the grand age of twenty-five. The union did not last long as his wife, Helen (aka Hell), couldn’t take her husband drifting all over the world. She didn’t trust him and therefore became untrustworthy as she slept around and then divorced Davis, taking half of his money. Fuck her though, right? Right. Anyway, Davis decided to stop messing around and get hired on salary to a promotion. That promotion came in the form of the CWU, run by two lovely brothers and business partners. In the CWU Trash made splashes. Always a nice and friendly guy, but wanting power he joined a group called the New Breed with three other men seeking power. At the company’s first major event Trash’s three "partners" disbanded him in favor of an old friend of theirs. Trash immediately took war on the New Breed, sometimes even by himself. Before the battles could come to a closure the promotion folded. However, when God closes a door he tears the slender roof off of the house, and Steven Caldera, who had worked with the Krotzers before, and now ran his own promotion, GCW, snatched up whatever talent he could. One of those workers was the lovable and heroic Trash. Trash tried his hardest to make waves in GCW, but couldn’t seem to cut the mustard. He had a good match to a GCW veteran, John Reed, but lost however, at his first Pay Per View event, Dangerous Games. At the same event a battle royal had been presented and out of many men Trash came in second, which is something that kept him going in the hard times. Trash met many people he would consider friends and in a year at GCW he did a lot of different things. He won the Tag Team titles with Dan Rivero and then won both from "The Main Man/Event" later on. Trash ran into troubled times though and created a much darker in ring persona. He hated the business and what it had done to him. It had lost him most of his money, it had lost him his wife, it had never given him much success, and it didn’t give him any respect. However, he was addicted. It was like a drug that he just couldn’t kick. He changed his moniker to Twisted and waged a war on drugs, the drug of the wrestling business. He began to tell people that it was too late for him, but young souls like Toxic, Tristain Mayhem, or Mike Hardy could still be saved! Twisted formed a group with other lost souls and called themselves the Lost Soul Militia. In the group lay Dimensioner, X-Calibur, "The Dark Messiah" Jason O’Neil and others. Jason O’Neil and Twisted especially became allied and won the Tag Team titles together as well, but again Twisted was at odds with his partner and he and an old friend won the titles from Jason O’Neil. This was the last bit of success for Twisted for numerous months. Twisted was trying harder than ever, but was losing left right, front, back, and center. He lost to almost everyone! He almost lost to Icebabe. Icebabe. No lie. So Twisted went to the doctor to see what the problem was and it seemed that Twisted had developed an injury called Jobberitis. The only known cure was a drug that wasn’t yet passed as legal by the American government (or any other government for that matter): Twistodium, produced by the company Drugs N? Stuff. Twisted began to take the drug regularly and became highly addicted. The drug helped him like nothing else and Twisted (who gave himself the new nickname of Twizzy) was doing everything a great wrestler could: win. Twizzy was beating everyone he saw and was getting the fans cheers like never before. Twizzy had found his calling. The government has not yet passed Twistodium for over the counter pharmaceutical usage, but Twizzy has not been stopped yet. He has become an official spokesperson of Drugs N? Stuff alongside Barry Bonds and Scott Weiland. Eventually Twizzy even won the GCW World Championship in the battle royal at Dangerous Games that he had gotten second place in one year ago. When GCW closed down Twizzy moved off to threat, and when that closed too Twizzy? well? no one is quite sure what happened to him, however it is known that the Junkie was seen sleeping on Steven Caldera’s desk with a blanket over him when the boss walked back in after being away so long to restart the company’s wrestling product. Now that GCW is back together Twizzy is ready for action! What else does one need to know about this marvel of a man? Hmm, well, nothing more really needs to be known about the Co-Dependent, but about the drug Twistodium in general. No one actually knows what is in the product. What is known is that it is a type of steroid, it comes in original tablet and lemon, grape, or blue raspberry powder form, it is extremely addictive, it makes the user extremely obsessed and fascinated with goats, and it makes the user absolutely, totally, non-stop, plain and simple, Tweety Bird in Groucho Marx glasses drinking a martini while licking his own balls crazy. But hell, none of that really matters. All anyone needs to know in life is that Goat? is? Good! Other Feds IWO and thReat Other Fed Awards thReat Resident Addict Strengths Hardcore and experience Weaknesses Age and knees Wrestling Style Head/Neck Technician, Hardcore and Unorthadox Strategy Twizzy mainly reacts to his opponent's body type. He tries to keep light wrestlers grounded, medium wrestlers close and heavy wrestlers away. In fact, with a type of wrestler that's too heavy or skilled for Twizzy to take on directly he may find a way to cheat, but it's not a prefered technique. He does like hardcore matches though and in this case uses everything available, even getting quite creative, to weaken his opponent. In every case Twizzy's offense mainly focuses on the head and neck.
