Oliver Ranken Biography Real Name Oliver Ranken Nicknames 'The British Bully' Hometown UK Birthday 01-01-1977 Height 5'9 Weight 269 Biography July 1999 Name: Oliver Ranken Height: 5' 6" Weight: 249 pounds Fighting out of: London, England by way of Norfolk, Va. A wild haired madman inside the *** cages, refined proper Englishman outside Oliver Ranken has brought some real heat on himself since debuting two months ago. Ranken's harden chin and vast knowledge of chokes places this newcomer to MMA an instant threat in the Heavyweight division. January 2002 Name: Oliver Ranken Height: 5' 6" Weight: 256 pounds Fighting out of: Manchester, England by way of New York City, NY. In the last three years of his stint in *** Mister Ranken as he has become referred to has stepped up to the upper echelon. His hand speed has become threating and is starting to catch up to his ground game. Ranken is carving his lot in *** with incorporating slams to his ever growing arsenal. August 2005 Name: "The Truth" Oliver Ranken Height: 5' 7" Weight: 265 pounds Fighting out of: St. Louis, MO by way of London, England Starting over is never easy to do but Oliver Ranken has pulled the feat off in spades. Easily disposing of his first three challenges here in ***. Following his last fight one of the *** interview crew dubbed Ranken "The Truth" mostly because of his straight from hip interviews but the name stuck because of his fierce punching power and ever innovating ground game. March 2008 Name: Oliver Ranken Height: 5'9" Weight: 269 pounds Hometown: London, England Oliver Ranken joins ringside Wrestling at the start of the month with not much known about his wrestling acumen. His debut while in the Championship tournament was practically a cake walk for this Mixed Martial Artist turned wrestler. The stand offish British grappler's abilities will be tested inside the squared circle for the first time in his life as he tries to earn a spot near the upper tier of Ringside Wrestling's finest. June 2008 Name: Oliver Ranken Height: 5'9" Weight: 269 pounds Hometown: London, England After a five match contract with rW expired Oliver Ranken followed the suit of the Americans he had renouced for so long. He took the money and ran. Evolution Pro Wrestling's money to be exact and it is with Evolution Pro that Oliver Ranken plans to make his stand with. Since being inside the once hallowed halls he has brought Dallas Green into his camp. As well as started a war with the entire gimmick genre of wrestlers. Some say the bad ass Brit has bitten off more than he can chew. Some might not be familar with Oliver Ranken that is. More to come... Other Feds Ringside Wrestling, Evolution Professional Wrestling, Relentless Wrestling Strengths Superiorplexs and ability to hit one of the many from any position. Ground Game, Ranken likes to impose his will with many wear down holds. Weaknesses Time, Ranken's feeling the pressure he knows he's got to make something happen now. Ranken's hold school, no high risk nothing to fast paced. Wrestling Style Mauling Grappler Strategy Ranken's a very direct grappler. He enjoys using every inch of leverage his two hundred and sixty-nine pounds can maneuver out of any situation. His massive thighs make his explosion through his suplexs earn them the moniker "Superiorplex". He also utilizes his arsenal of judo throws and knowledge of pressure points to his advantage. One of Ranken's usual ring strengths is his ability to just wrestle. Now on the backside of his career Ranken could be tempted to put his balls to the wall in order to win. Something a little more out of his comfort zone. A Ranken trademark through the years has been his slow starting in matches. For wrestling historians look back to Greg "The Hammer" Valentine, he usually starts off taking the beating. It's almost like if you don't slap him around a little he thinks it's not a real match or something. Maybe it's just his ploy? I have often thought about having an opponent base his entire match start around not going after Ranken. Making Ranken come after him, again taking him out of his comfort zone.
