Biography Orin is a very notorious drug dealer that has reached a higher financial status and has decided to after 5 years of distributing, he's decided that he wants to take his life elsewhere. Growing up in a very tainted household, he decided to run away at the age of 16 which lead him to the lifestyle that he lives that involves running a highly respected, highly feared drug operation out of his hometown of Flint, Michigan. In an attempt to venture out of this lifestyle, he decides to go elsewhere with his profession and become a professional wrestler.
