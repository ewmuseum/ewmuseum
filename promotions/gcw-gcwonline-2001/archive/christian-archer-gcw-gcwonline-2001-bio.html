Christian Archer Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Icon, Arch (friends) Hometown Delphos, Kansas Birthday March 17, 1986 Height 6'2 Weight 290 Biography The Icon's story will be told. Yes, indeed. Strengths Strength; Determination; Intelligence; Fighting the Good Fight Weaknesses Speed; Unwilling to Cheat; Stamina (plays into long matches) Wrestling Style Power / High Impact Strategy Christian Archer's strategy is simple - overwhelm his opponents with immense strength and an unwillingness to accept defeat. He is relentless, at a moderate pace (he's no speed demon), and rarely gives his opposition time to collect themselves. His best defense is his close ranged offense, controlling his smaller adversary as much as possible. The focus of his attacks tend to be the lower back and neck, and he'll utilize a plethora of "wear down" holds in order to sap an opponent's energy. Despite winning being his ultimate goal, he will not cheat in any manner to achieve victory; he will, however, aggressively attack without mercy, he just has certain limits. Ten lights, five on each side of the curtain, slowly begin to illuminate, to the breaking point of unbearable, the ramp in front of them. As they dim, you can see the immense figure of a man, standing proud, hands placed on his hips. An elongated chant joins the background music. Interrupted, albeit briefly, by tambourines colliding. Andrews: This is how I do! With the light dimmed low, the man, Christian Archer, is easily recognizable. His massive frame is covered by a black "Outsider Style" shirt, a nearly replicable pose from moments ago adorning the front, with "The Icon" scrawled across the back in "jersey" style. He wears his usual white, blue, and red tights, along with wrist and finger tape. Behind him, Miss Jordan Archer, dressed in an exquisite, yet provocative gown, gracefully slips through the black curtain. Here our soldiers stand, from all around the world Waiting in line, to hear the battle cry All are gathered here, victory is near The sound will fill the hall, bringing power to us all Jordan follows Christian closely, her face etched with a sincere smile. Her husband, however, sports a much more hardened face. His focus remains on the ring, at the task ahead of him. He leaps onto the ring apron, leaving his wife at ringside, and cuts through the cables into the ring. Many stand against us, but they will never win We said we would return, and here we are again To bring them all destruction, suffering, and pain We are the Hammer of the Gods, We are Thunder, Wind, and Rain Immediately climbs the furthest turnbuckle, and raises his powerful arms high into the air. Archer jumps down, heads for the neutral corner, and begins bouncing back and forth on his toes. He finishes stretching while awaiting his opponent's arrival or the start of the bout.
