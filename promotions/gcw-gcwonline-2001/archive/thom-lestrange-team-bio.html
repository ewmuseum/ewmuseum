Thom LeStrange Biography Real Name Thomas James LeStrange Nicknames The Englishman Hometown Bedford, England Birthday 9th April 1981 Height 6'1 Weight 212lbs Biography Personal history: In the wrestling world of today it seems grapplers with "nothing to lose" are ten-a-penny. It's easy to get lost amidst the rough childhoods, the familial deaths, the mental illnesses, the unrequited lovers and the outcasts. Show me all of these, and I'll show you a man who also has nothing to lose, but for all the opposite reasons. I'll show you man that appears to have absolutely everything to lose, but in fact is so secure there is no possible way in which he could lose a thing. Thomas James LeStrange was born into an English family entrenched over five-hundred years in the English aristocracy. He attended Beford Preparatory School and then Bedford School itself, just as five previous generations of the LeStrange family had done so, before studying Modern Languages at Cambridge. At Cambridge he strolled into the Blues rugby team, excelling as a fast, powerful inside centre. His degree completed, the world was his oyster; a talented young man with silver spoon firmly jammed in his mouth, with a superb education and supreme athletic ability. If anything, the world bored him; where was the point when everything came to him so effortlessly? He appeared to be suffering from a severe state of what Baudelaire coined 'ennui' - world-weariness. How could the wrestling world not appeal? Constant excitement, exposure and fame to feed his narcissism, a healthy measure of pain to remind himself that he was human after all (the temptation to believe himself as some kind of demi-god was ever present, it must be understood), and a chance to mete out a little of the pent-up frustration engendered by a life of unbridled success that makes each day seem mundane. It is important not to be fooled by any mask that Thom may don. His favourite, the innoucuous, harmless, loquacious showoff English toff, is particularly disarming; whilst he may act naive, his mind is ever-scheming. He may lay great claim to being a Gentlemen, and take great self-importance from his belief that his manners are far superior to others, but he is far from being so honourable; he will doing anything in his power to get that which he desires; no level is too low for him to stoop to. He can be rude, crass, inappropriate, and downright devious. Furthermore, his personal life is hedonistic to say the least - he is a renowned womaniser and frequently over-indulges with substances (athough it must be noted that he is able to exercise extreme self-discipline and is thus not an addict nor does he have a problem - he has an addictive personality in the very short-time, but not in the long run). Do not let his charming exterior fool you; anything he appears to be is simply a mask, a character, and what bubbles underneath is cold and calculated. Physical appearance: Facially, Thom has what some describe as "enigmatic good looks", akin to those of actor Christian Bale, to whom aesthetic comparisons have indeed been made. An incredibly handsome man, he has a strong jaw into which his soft features are set, the focus of which are undoubtedly his perfectly-shaped mouth and intense, invasive and brooding brown eyes. His hair is short but not close-cropped. Tousled but impeccably managed, his brown locks are worn forward with a hint of a side-parting. (For reference of facial features see this picture. Physically, Thom is of about average height, standing at 6 feet and 1 inch, but is very broad of shoulder. A former Blues rugby player at the University of Cambridge, he is fast, agile and yet broad and strong, and this is reflected in his physique, which is not polished or well-defined, but rather compact and bulky. His is not a brute, overwhelming strength typical of larger wrestlers, but rather one of explosiveness and speed off the mark. The majority of this strength is to be found in his muscular legs, particularly his thights, and thus his wrestling arsenal is comprised largely of moves which utilise this - springboard assaults and other non-acrobatic aerial moves, and fast attacks such as powerslams and suplexes. He also employs submission manoeuvres to neutralise the size disadvantage that he finds in a wrestling world where his otherwise-impressive proportions are of little consequence. Thom's in-ring attire is fairly simple. His upper body remains unadorned but for his wrists, which he tapes with black tape. He wears maroon close-fitting rugby shorts, with black cycling shorts underneath, giving a two-tone maroon/black appearance. To protect his knees he wears black kneepads, and his ensemble is completed by short black wrestling boots, completely covered by maroon kickpads/boot covers which stretch all the way to his kneepads, leaving no part of his lower leg exposed (for an example, see Chris Jericho, Rob Van Dam, etc). Out of the ring, Thom will usually wear a suit, tailored for him at a boutique in Jermyn Street, London. For all public appearances he insists upon wearing a suit, waistcoat and tie, but when travelling and out of the limelight, when a more comfortable attire is required, he will wear his most comfortable suit with an open-collared shirt. His favourite colour and cloth combination is grey Prince of Wales check. A picture of Thom's favourite style of suit can be found here, although it must be noted that this is an "off-the-rack" suit, and NOT one of Thom's tailored suits, and is for illustrative purposes only. Obviously, when in the gym or when exercising, Thom will wear athletic apparel, but is rather disinclined to do so. Other Fed Awards SAWF Ring of Honor Wrestling Style Fast, aggressive, and agile. Other Moves Suplexes: Belly-to-belly suplex Butterfly suplex Cobra clutch suplex Fisherman suplex T-Bone suplex Slingshot suplex Aerial: Somersault flip Diving Headbutt Springboard dropkick Suicide dive Flying clothesline Submissions: Boston crab Dragon sleeper Cobra clutch Other: Tilt-a-whirl slam Tilt-a-whirl powerbomb Ring Entrances "Reach out, touch faith..." Two eerie harmonic notes ring through the arena, and every light in the building descends instantaneously into darkness. The haunting unison vocals of "Personal Jesus" by Depeche Mode, amplified and delivered through the Public Address system, pierce the pitch-black gloom. As the final note of the introduction echoes about, the lights slide back up to their normal level of luminescence. Without warning, a sheet of sparks begins to cascade from the very top of the MegaTron, accompanied by the pounding bass riff of the song. From behind this conflagration, much to the chagrin of the booing crowd, saunters Thom LeStrange. He pauses at the top of the ramp, raising both arms at his sides in a gesture of welcoming, almost as if basking in the indignation of the assembled fans. "...Your own personal Jesus. Someone to hear your prayers, Someone who cares. Your own personal Jesus... Thom begins to walk towards the ring, and as he descends the ramp he breaks gradually into a run, gaining in speed and finally taking off and under the bottom rope of the ring, pulling himself up to his feet as he slides across the mat. Once again, he raises his arms up to the side in a supercilious gesture, spinning a slow three-hundred-and-sixty degrees before coming to face the MegaTron again, assuming a focused two-point stance, hands on thighs, patiently awaiting his opponent.
