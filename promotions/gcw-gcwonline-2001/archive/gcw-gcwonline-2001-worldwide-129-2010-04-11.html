WorldWide 129 Staples Center — Los Angeles, California ---- WW129: Dropping Hints And Picking Up Steam "I've worked my way from the bottom, beaten the names that some believed couldn't be beaten, and won multiple titles in the process. I know it may not mean much to you but I've given my heart for this company and I think it's time that I get what I finally earned." Smoke follows Jay Terror into the room, trailing behind his brisk, direct course to the desk as the door swings behind him. With a Marlboro firmly clenched between his teeth, Terror puts both palms down on the surface as he leans forward, looming over his seated host. "You know, Jay. Some people don't think you ever had a heart to give, but I always knew there was one ticking around in you somewhere, even if it might be a few sizes too small." Garbage Bag Johnny pushes the ashtray on his desk forward towards Terror before getting the urge to spark one himself. GBJ: So what is it this time? Jay Terror: Is 'Statt really on his way out? GBJ: That's what he said. Of course, circumstances have changed, but I'd still set some money aside for a parting gift if I were you. I hear he likes tequila. Terror refrains from smirking and taps his cigarette on the head of Garbage Bag Johnny's desk, beside the ashtray. Jay Terror: I'm not here to bid my farewell, I'm here to cash in my shot at his title. I may not be as savvy as Steve was, but I ain't near stupid enough to stand in the way of Knight's revenge, and I hope you're not that stupid either. I understand how a hierarchy works and winning at Dangerous Games was my opportunity to inflate my rank. On top of that, I defeated another legend. Jay takes a long drag on his cigarette, eyes focused on the commisioner. Jay Terror: I know I'll get my shot but I'm sick of being cut in front of. Garbage Bag turns on his heels, shooting a confused look at Jay. GBJ: Jay, there ain't nobody in line right now. You got to just roll with me on this and wait for it to form. He procures his AWC Transatlantic Championship Title, blows some dust off of it, and hands it to Jay. GBJ: I know you just lost your other title, so if you can't wait for a shot at the GCW World Heavyweight Belt, you can hold onto this one for a while. The Rampage Tournament's coming up, and I've got to keep my eye on a lot of new faces around here to see who looks promising. I already know what you can do, so roll with me a little longer, and I'll leave you to doing it. Terror grabs the dusty belt, not knowing, or caring, about the history behind it.
