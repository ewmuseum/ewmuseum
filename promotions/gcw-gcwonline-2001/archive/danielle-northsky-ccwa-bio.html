Danielle Northsky Biography Real Name Danielle Lynn Northsky Nicknames 'Dani', 'Sirius Star' Hometown Black Hills, North Dakota Birthday August 2,1984 (21 years old) Height 5' 6' Weight 140 Biography Raised by a Catholic Orphanage, Danielle was always run into the ground. Told from a young age her parents were both drunkard and her mother was a whore who died at the hands of one of her John's. Miss NorthSky always believed there was more to it. Not allowed to be with the other Children her age or allowed to be seen by any visitors, Danielle spent time in the basement reading old books and with TV to watch. Making a small gym out of odds and ends in the basment she kept her body form and fit. This was also a neccessity of occasionally having to deal with the other orphans who didn't like the fact that one of the 'indians' was being kept there also. She was found by her Grandfather at the age of 12, and he took her away after dealing with all of the paperwork. A hard man who was very much against the outside abuse of the Amerindians of the area, his attitude quickly spread to Dani after the harsh treatment she had had. She found out the things that the orphanage hadn't told her, like the fact that her family had been killed shortly after she was born by a group of white supremist with a score to settle with the local natives. She quickly picked up scraping and fighting, and threw in with a local Martial Arts Dojo to learn to protect herself and her aging grandfather. When the time came, wrestling came quickly too when she was looking at ways to do something other then work fast food and that in her local area. Smart, beautiful and strong she uses all to get what she wants and needs done. Now ready to debut in the big time after a few stints in the indys, she's had chosen the CCWA as her starting point. In her first match in the CCWA, she was in a gauntlet for the right to be one of the two contenders for the World Title. Her opponent she came in after was a large, 7 footer with a gothic gimmick. This was the first one to talk about taking Dani's soul, and he fell to her. However, she lost to another man in the finals of that gauntlet. She had impressed the CCWA though, and immediatly was put into contention for the North American Title with the other gauntlet round's second place winner. The next few challenges she faced she beat most of them, and those that she did beat, usually ran away, having been beaten by a much smaller woman. Just as she, as their North American Champion, the second highest belt they had, got rolling though... the CCWA went bankrupt and then closed. Dani didn't let grass grow under her feet though, she knew that the rookie sensation was a commodity that many others would look into, and out of many offers, cW made one that she liked the most... However, her time with cW was only on a developmental contract. cW tried to use her and promised her even a shot at their Classic Championship if she beat GW's Elia Smith at their Atlantic City Event. Dani's used her own means to get what she wants though, arranging for an FHM photoshoot where she not only appeared in revealing clothing, but topless... Then when cW started talking about such things as making their lower tier title not a real division and other things that she didn't agree with, she decided to end her developmental deal and head where she had the only challenge so far in her career. One loss in CCWA due to outside interferance. One loss in cW due to outside interferance. The only person to push her to the limits and not require help, even though Dani had beaten her was Elia Smith from Global Wrestling. So she followed ther to Global when her developmental deal with cW didn't continue as a mutal split. In Global she was just begining to really succeed, having won their platinum title, converging it with the TV and Crusier weight titles... and getting main event slots... But the company suddenly turned against it's talent and closed, canceling everyone's contract as a power struggle in the comittee tried to force everyone to re-sign with less pay. This led to her and her new allies, the KnightMare Brigade: Native American Nations to leave Las Vegas' hometown fed and find a new home Fortunatly for her, GCW heard of Global's antics and swooped in with a sweet deal to seal her and her friends into place. Special Notes about the KB:NAN You can concider this as a note about all 5 members of the KB:NAN. This is a stable with a long standing tradition of over 6 years in wrestling and currently has factions in at least two federations. PTC federations with factions are currently GCW and 4WW. The regular name of the stable is the KnightMare Brigade and it started long ago in what was known as the GIWF (now the UWF in PTC). Each faction has a sub name, this one is the Native American Nations, as all members but Ukyo are of noteable Native American Blood. The leader of this faction is Blaise McClancy, with Gray Brand serving as his second. The other line in the heirarchy is not known to be public. the KB:NAN is an honor based stable, no matter what role their playing at the time: Heel, Face or Tweener. They do not change tactics as they change roles in a match, angle or feud. One rule is paramount to this honor code... 'Bell to Bell' This rule is the one that must be followed to remain in the KB:NAN, with few exceptions. What is this rule? Basically as long as the rules of the match aren't changed the KB:NAN will never interfere in a match in between the opening and closing bell. Before the match? Fine. After the match? Fine. During the match? It would be a violation of the honor code to touch anyone who is in that match. This means that valets/managers, tag partners, and other stable members are not allowed to enter the ring. Valets and Managers can be at ringside, but you will find that they do not get invovled. A match is seen as a contest and this rule can not be broken. Exceptions: If the rules of the match specifically allow it (Hardcore or no-dq matches) and permission has been granted by their main sponsors that are in charge of all factions of the KB, then it would be allowed. And the final exception would be if continued battles with the same opponents show that tactic to be one that the opponents regularly use to teach them a lesson. Also would be approved by the sponsors of the KB stables. Other Feds CCWA North American Champion (never lost title before they closed), Global Platinum Champion (converged with the Cruiserweight and TV championship too) Wrestling Style High Risk / Daredevil with a dash of technical. For a regular match, they don't bother much now that she's returned to wrestling) The music of Queensryche's 'Blood' begins to play over the loud speakers. As it starts, the red blooded Native American woman, Danielle Northsky... The Sirius Star, walks out from behind the curtain. She stands there and poses for a moment, looking down at the ring. Thank-you for the lies, the lies you sold us. Thank-you for this vision of violence. Thank-you for this song you gave me to sing. Thank-you for the capping day... ... of a culture that lay dying. Yes it's us dying. Maybe it won't be long, now that it's all gone on. She starts to make her way down to the ring. She doesn't high five anyone or recognize the crowd, just focues on getting to the ring. An occasional glance around keeps her on her toes when it comes to her surroundings. It's all gone wrong and there's blood on our hands. She makes her way down to the ringside area until she gets to her corner of the ring. We saw them lying there with their arms around each other, staring through their fear and the miles between our heads. You could have told the truth. It was your decision to sacrifice integrity and compassion for greed. Now it's all gone wrong and there's blood on our hands. Quickly, Dani climbs up onto the ring apron and then into the ring itself. She climbs up onto the corner and poses again for the crowd. As they give whatever reaction they're going to give her, she unties the breast plate and takes it off, dropping it to one of the ringhands. She then unties the skirt and with a flourish, removes it and balls/rolls it up in a fluid motion and also drops it down into the waiting set of hands. Upon her getting down to only her high sandals and what most would call a bikini, a different reaction comes from the crowd, which Dani tries her best to look like she's ignoring it.. with a slight smirk on her face. She finishes it with a short moonsault to her feet fromt he corner to the ring mat, and then gets ready to start the match up. Entrance Music 'Blood' by Queensryche
