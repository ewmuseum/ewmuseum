Meltdown 49 Qwest Center — Omaha, Nebraska ---- Meltdown #49 (The lights in the arena dim, before the Meltdown theme cues up. The GCW logo appears on the MegaTron, before pyro explodes from the stage! Fireworks light up the arena as the fans go wild, and the arena explodes! Meltdown begins with its usual bang, as the cameras pan over the frenzied crowd.) Bryan- Hello everyone, and welcome to Meltdown! We’re just under two weeks away from Dread Reckoning, and we’re getting well on our way on the road to Warpath! Inside, we find Jason Dread, Michael Stevens, Toxic, Khan, and President Caldera all sitting comfortably in the room. Dread sits on a bench, holding his world title belt. Stevens and Toxic sit on a couch, Stevens proudly shining both of his title belts. Khan stands ominously behind Caldera, who stands observing his army.)
