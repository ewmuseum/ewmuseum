Tim Shipley Biography Real Name Timothy Shipley Nicknames "The Cavalier" Hometown Milton Keynes, England Birthday 6 February 1985 Height 5' 11 Weight 225 lbs Fed Awards GCW Rampage Tournament Winner (2010), #1 Seed for Rampage (2010); PTC Wrestler Rankings #3 (July 2010), #15 (June 2010); WWR Best Heel (June 2010) Biography Five years ago in Core Wrestling he was a college student struggling to make ends meet. He hit the big time, grabbed the Tag Team belts with Tony Aliso, then jumped ship to AWC. Aliso faked his own death before his shocking mob connections came to light. Innocent, naive Tim Shipley was never the same again. Forced into (very) early retirement by spinal injuries sustained at Aliso's hands, Shipley retreated from the spotlight. That lasted barely two weeks. He resurfaced a new man in LA, a new man far less pleasant or amiable than the boy he had previously been. As Shipley himself put it, "sinewy nights and long, lank, undead mornings" stretched out in front of the impressionable youngster. He got out in time. Some never do. A cold reality check on return to his parents' place in Albany made necessary his return to the ring. Kate Barnard, childhood sweetheart if Tim had had his way, consigned to the psych ward in the aftermath of the Tony Aliso trauma. Her mother dead, her father unable to take it. Numb to the world around him, Tim knew only one thing, and that was to make it right. He took the job in Ringside Wrestling. What John Mark Blackburn didn't know is that Ringside was merely a platform for Shipley. He had grander goals, and so emerged Just Wrestling, the hottest non-televised promotion now spreading its wings across the States. But as Tim Shipley made good on his promise, and rescued Kate from herself, it came to pass that as she fell in love with him he fell out of love with her. The two existed in an apartment of tense limbo, until a violent climax saw Shipley jailed for battery. It was a fabrication. Kate had inflicted the injuries herself, and soon enough Tim was released. But the guilt returned as Kate was worse off than ever. Jody Monroe, with whom Tim had been openly flirting for months in the Just offices, learned of Kate's plight and sided with her. Shipley found himself spearheading a major wrestling promotion without a shred of confidence in himself. And so it was that he returned to the ring. It was the road he knew. To redeem himself, and to rediscover what truly made him tick. There was only one way. (updated to joining HOW, late 2009. Further updates soon!)
