Khaos Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Black Dragon, The Deliverance of Pain, The Khaotic One Hometown New York, New York Birthday August 10, 1975 Height 6’6” Weight 350 Biography Khaos’s career began in 2003 when he was then known as Big Bad John in Evolution Pro Wrestling things were going well for him winning the Intercontinental title for the first time in his career and riding high in the ranks until he was repeatedly attacked by a man known as Psycho Fan a man who was relentless in making Big Bad John’s life a living hell costing him the Intercontinental title, each night he would constantly attack him from behind, but the attacks started to take its toll and weighted so heavily on him that he just snapped and went berserk thus turning him into Khaos a man hell-bent on causing destruction and mayhem wherever he goes. He got his revenge on him by throwing him into an electric panel causing him to go into convulsions thus solidifying Khaos’s career of mass destruction. But later on at Fridays Aggression someone put some kind of powder in his drink in a bad attempt to poison him, despite the incident Khaos quit EPW for personal reasons and wanted to be alone EPW had closed its doors several months later due to the lack of TV deals. Khaos moved back to New York City where it had been three months since he left and EPW had reopened its doors back in 2004 Khaos re-signed with them after long contract negotiations thus ready to once again resume his quest for the gold by capturing the Intercontinental title once again from Ace, the man who he later found out poisoned him. By then in June 2005 EPW was bought by CORE Wrestling, Khaos was still the Intercontinental champion and along with many other EPW superstars led an invasion to try and take over CORE rather than submit to the merger sometime during that period Khaos lost the Intercontinental title to Jason Slade after holding it for nearly for year, months later however EPW was unsuccessful with the invasion and thus was absorbed into CORE never to be heard from again. Khaos spent the next few months in CORE winning the Classic Championship only to lose the title the following week to John "The Prophet" Adams in a controversial decision which forced Khaos to cut ties with CORE altogether leaving him once again promotion less. Towards the beginning of 2006 he went off to Japan and signed with Tokyo Wrestling Federation for the next two years where in just a short amount time he won the TWF World Title and had dominated the scene going undefeated, since that time he has not lost the title. He runs his underground club Tears of Blood the hottest spot for the Goth/Punk community alike in New York City as well thanks to his long time friend Cory O’Brien for keeping it up while he was overseas. However in Mid April of 2008 he got a call from Cory, he told him that EPW had been bought out by LUSH and was looking to make a comeback once again, but surprisingly he opted not to be released from TWF when EPW reopened, he only gave this answer; "I will strike down when least expect it, and now is not the time to do so." On September 21, 2008 would be a date that will go down in EPW history as Khaos made his presence felt with his return to the company he called home by making one of the biggest entrances in EPW, and aiding his half-brother John "The Prophet" Adams to a victory over The Illuminati leader The Master, Mephisto. However the cohesiveness would not last. On Friday, September 26, 2008 During Aggression Khaos revealed his true colors by turning on his half-brother by smacking his skull with a steel chair. The following week on RiOT Khaos revealed that he had no intention of ever joining forces with his half-brother and that he told the world that he would do what no other wrestler could ever do, and that was eliminate John Adams from wrestling period even if it costs him his own career and even his own life. But Khaos needed no introduction in fact most EPW fans knew of his past and they had now recognized that no other EPW superstar has worked the hardest than Khaos, holding the Intercontinental title not only twice but for almost a year becoming the greatest Intercontinental champion in EPW history, he had set his sights on the biggest prize, to become World Champion for the first time in his illustrious career. But it seems fate had been against him for EPW had once again closed its doors, this time for good leaving Khaos unemployed once more and for Khaos who has kept himself in shape had been out of wrestling for two and a half years waiting for the right time to come back to the squared circle but why, why wait that long? Seems that no one but Khaos knows for sure, but wherever Khaos goes wherever he ends up mayhem and destruction will be sure to follow The Khaotic One. Other Feds EPW, CORE, SNM Other Fed Titles x2 EPW Intercontinental Champion, CORE Classic Champion Strengths Khaos has a very high threshold of pain thus making him tough to deal with as an opponent. Weaknesses His bad temper, this can contribute to him losing focus on the match and quite possibly getting DQ’ed. Wrestling Style Powerhouse, Roughneck. Basically Kane and Undertaker style of fighting. Strategy Khaos can be described as being very relentless on offensive, moving at a slow but deadly pace. Vs Smaller: These guys can be trouble for Khaos especially if they get the jump early on and try to take him off his vertical base, he should have no trouble if he takes them out early before they have a chance to pick up speed. Vs. Equal: When it’s an even playing field you can expect Khaos do his usual slow paced methodical style offense. Vs. Larger: No one more than Khaos relishes these kinds of challengers because for him it’s a chance to show up the giants by trying to match power vs. power. vs. Technical wrestlers: Much like the smaller guys this can spell trouble for Khaos, especially if they take him out early with submissions making him weak against technical wrestlers. vs. Hardcore or brawling type wrestlers: This is another area Khaos excels at Hardcore wrestling, brawling, death matches, etc this is where Khaos can truly be at his element and toe to toe with even craziest most hardcore of superstars. Vs. Women: For Khaos fighting women is just another day at the office for him, he treats them as he does the men, rough and tough with no apologies, not holding anything back.
