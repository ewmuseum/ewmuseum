Mark Ace Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Ace of Spades, Complete and Utter Ace-Hole Hometown Miami, Florida Birthday 14th July 1981 Height 6'4 Weight 278lbs Fed Titles None yet Fed Awards None yet Biography Cocky? Not a chance! Living with such good looks, charm and offensively great teeth boxed in only 6'4 foot of man it's a given that some pride would bubble over such a volcano as Mark Ace. Blessed with the luck of the devil Mark glides through life knowing that while he might not be the chosen one, he'll certainly befriend the chosen one... stab him in the back and steal his wife of 7 years. Untrustworthy is a strong word, but Mark Ace gives off the impression that in a previous life he got Eve to enjoy more than just an apple. Deep inside Mark might be a heart of gold, but he'd probably sell it to finance his many delicious vices and probably wouldn't even notice it was gone afterward. In the ring he's poetry, shaking the dust from classic heel manoeuvres and bringing them kicking and screaming into the 21st century and then dragging them on back to 1984 along with Mark's cut, slash, steal and run attitude. Born into middle class America in 1981, Mark experienced Miami at it's most deplorable and loved every second of it's trashy hot neon glow. Fighting his way out of high school with an impressive football career ahead of him, Mark turned away from his expected path after an unfortunate but fondly remembered night with the Coach's wife. Boxing was Mark's next port of call and he excelled in smaller tournaments that would help his stamina and give him a love of the squared circle that was hard to destroyed. After an ugly car accident and even uglier D.U.I charge Mark's arm was temporarily paralyzed causing him to give up his promising Boxing career and turn to a 9 to 5 job like everyone else in Miami... and with his connections and slowly recovering yet useless right arm, Mark took the obvious job to satisfy his need for both the things he'd lost, Male escort. Making an impressionable young man realize the cut and thrust life that the world expects is never a good first lesson, but Mark learned it quickly and took life in the stride that only liars and complete jerks can master. While the 90s weren't the best time for most, Mark reminded himself it was a new tomorrow and took the new road of professional wrestling when his arm finally recovered. With long stints in the Miami Wrestling Federation and a short stint with Purely Hardcore Wrestling, Mark was brought into the new millennium with the WTFL. Mark impressed many during his stint with the WTFL, but ultimately disappointment was around the corner again when Mark was badly injured in a Retirement Match and the WTFL closed it's doors in 2007. Mark once again took to the road, wandering America and turning up occasionally in backwater towns, fighting in small unknown wrestling circuits and learning, always learning... Other Feds MWF, PHW, WTFL Other Fed Titles PHW Middleweight Champion, PHW Tag Champion, WTFL Xtreme Champion Other Fed Awards MWF's 'Up and Coming' Star Award, PHW's Wrestler of the Year 2000 Strengths Cockiness, a deep belief that despite all evidence to contrary he's an absolute winner. Weaknesses See Above Wrestling Style Boxing based fighting with old school classical wrestling and typical heel moves. Strategy Mark's moves echo that of The Rock's earlier fighting style, with the same cocky punching but with more power based grapple moves such as powerbombs and piledrivers. Mark's movelist is however as varied as his wardrobe as he will pretty much employ any move towards winning. Writers should note that Mark isn't the fairest of people in matches but doesn't cheat constantly... he's a bad guy but he's still a talented wrestler. Finisher Move 'The Ace of Spades' Finisher Description The move which is also refered to as a front flip piledriver (Or Petey Williams of TNA's ''Canadian Destroyer' ) begins in a position in which the victim is bent forward against Mark Ace's midsection, the Mark grabs around his opponent's midsection latching onto the opponent's back, with his head to one side of the opponent's hips, keeping his legs around the opponent's head. Mark then pushes off the mat with his legs to flip the opponent over. As both wrestlers flip Mark uses his bodyweight to land in a seated position driving the opponent's head down to the mat between Mark's thighs and cracking their heads off the mat Finisher Setup Move A low kick to the stomach is all he needs, baby. Purple and dark blue lights fill the darkened entrance as those really lame errupting fireworks start to sprout along the ramp. Strutting from backstage with the attitude of a man entirely pleased with himself comes Mark Ace. Andrews: Making his/her way to the ring, from Miami, Florida! weighing in at 279lbs pounds. . . MAAAARK ACE! Bryan: Yale: Stretching his arms to accept the frankly indifferent mutterings of the crowd, Mark walks to the ring with an aura of calm... giving the occasional finger gun salute to members of the audience. Mark climbs to the ring steps and removes his shirt, spins it around and then just before throwing it to the crowd... stops and drapes it neatly on the turnbuckle Climbing into the ring, Mark runs to the opposite ring corner and jumps to the middle buckle, spreading his arms once more and giving a winning, if not a little patronising, grin. Bryan: Yale: Thumping his chest a few times, Mark shadow boxes a few times in the corner of the ring and generally gets himself warmed up. Special Moves "Purple Rain"- Mark shows a rare display of aerial attitude (!) by making his way to the top tu-.err. second turnbuckle with his opponent motionless on the canvas and dives off. driving a closed fist onto the NOSE of his opponent. Other Moves Float-over DDT Flowing snap DDT Running swinging neckbreaker Running thrust lariat Samoan drop Boston Crab Spinebuster Snap German Suplex Snapmare followed by a rather dirty kick to the spine Low kick to the back of the opponent's knee then a running bulldog His punching is where Mark stands out, with professional boxing training he can deliver punishing haymakers and uppercuts. The technical side of his moves is more grapple based with Mark rarely going to the top rope unless the opponent is down and out. Submissions aren't his strong point but the more basic ones are used at any obvious point. Mark has good strength aswell as physique and takes his opponent down quickly with punishing fast strike, hard hitting moves.
