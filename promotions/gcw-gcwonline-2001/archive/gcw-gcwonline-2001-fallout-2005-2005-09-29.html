Introduction

A dark force sits atop Global Championship Wrestling. An evil presence that appears to be indestructible, with an insatiable desire to create misery and destruction.

But a bright light bears down upon the darkness. A new challenger has broken through and threatened the dominant champion.

Tonight, the challenger attempts to destroy the darkness once and for all and bring light and respect back to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship.

But the champion will stop at nothing until his foe begs for mercy and taps the mat in submission.

Tonight, Michael “Spitfire” Stevens gets one more chance to make history. One more chance to bring down the unbeatable LeStatt Knight. One more chance to defeat the darkness and wrest the GCW World Title from the iron-clad grip of the most dominant champion in GCW history.

Tonight, Global Championship Wrestling presents… FALLOUT!

The GCW logo flashes across the screen, and Led Zepplin’s Kashmir begins to play. Amid the ominous tones, the dim faces of LeStatt Knight and Michael Stevens cross the screen in turn. They are followed by an intense looking Rich Rollins, a confident Seymour Almasy, an angry Trey Tremendous, the sexy Eva VanLorne, and a cocky grin from Jonathan Borealis. Finally the scene is broken by a bright explosion of orange fireworks from the stage! The camera fades in on the FallOut stage, as the Toronto crowd erupts in cheers! The frenzied crowd applauds wildly as the fireworks display fades into a puff of smoke, and the cameras begin scouring the crowd.

Bryan: Welcome to Toronto, ladies and gentlemen, as GCW is live for FallOut! Tonight, two of the greatest superstars in GCW history go head to head in a Submission Match for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship, and this intensely personal rivalry comes to a head! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan here at ringside once again with Pete and The Jhub, and guys, it’s finally time for FallOut!

Jhub: I’ve been looking forward to this one for a while now, JB! We’ve got an absolutely massive show here tonight, and we’re going to get our first big-stage looks at some of the great new talent in GCW. But of course, topping it all off are two huge matches. Rich Rollins finally faces the undefeated Seymour Almasy one on one, and LeStatt Knight defends his title against Michael Stevens in a submission match!

Pete: Stevens shocked the world by coming into Annihilation with a new sense of intensity, and he really took it to LeStatt Knight! He came within inches of winning the title there, guys, and I think tonight is his night. Knight may be armed with that intimidating new submission hold, which he’s calling the ****, but I still think Spitfire is going to get it done.

Bryan: Both of these men have been on an absolute roll. Stevens won the Dangerous Games, and he’s been on fire since then. But of course, the World Champion hasn’t lost a clean decision in eight months! Something’s got to give tonight, and the only way this match can end is by making your opponent tap out.

Jhub: Hey, and what about Rollins and Almasy? Seymour Almasy showed up at Annihilation to challenge Rollins, and Rollins has made himself scarce ever since. But he’s got no way out of this one. Almasy is undefeated in GCW so far, and he’s looked very impressive at every turn. But Rollins remains one of the most talented stars in GCW history, and the man is a Hall of Famer. I’ve wrestled Rollins several times before, and I’ll vouch for the fact that he’s one tough son of a bitch. That’s gonna be a good one.

Bryan: And you’d better believe that match is going to have huge ramifications on who the next challenger to the GCW World Championship is going to be! But we’ve also got a bitter rivalry between Mike Hardy and Octavian to settle, two big tag team matchups, including a Tag Team Title defense, and a Cruiserweight Title tournament to conclude!

Pete: And I’m looking forward to that one, JB! Rei Tiu and Aiden Jackson have fought hard for several weeks now to get to this point, and tonight we’re going to see a hell of a cruiserweight contest to crown the new Cruiserweight Champion!

Bryan: Right… Well, fans, FallOut is ready to begin!

Singles Match – One Fall

Bryan Mayhem vs. Gabriel White -by Jeff

“Blood In Your Eyes” thunders throughout the arena as the crowd explodes into a chorus of cheers, as pyros explode on the edges of the ramp leading to the ring. Gabriel White steps out from the back as the crowd continues to cheer. He slowly and methodically takes one step out, then stares out across the crowd as his music continues to play. He raises his arms in the air then rushes the ring, sliding under the bottom rope. He springs to his feet, and hit the ropes on the cross side, springing off as the lights fade down and “Demon of the Fall” rings out. The crowd immediately shifts their cheers to jeers 12 cloaked men appear carrying candles, walk slowly to the ring. They encircle the ring, as White looks cautiously out over them. A loud explosion followed by a shower of red sparks has Mayhem appearing at the top of the ramp. Without so much as an acknowledgment to the crowd or the man awaiting him in the ring, he marches to the ring without hesitation.

Jhub: Well it looks like this one is about to get started.

Pete: ‘Bout time I was getting tired of the fireworks, and music and whatever the hell these guys in the dresses are.

Bryan: They’re cloaks you moron.

Pete: Whatever. Can we wrestle please?

Both men stand in the ring facing one another from opposite sides as the bell sounds. The lights come up and cloud of smoke fills the ringside area where the cloaked men all disappear.

Pete: Serves ‘em right. Dress wearin hippies!

Bryan: Cloak wearing hippies….they were cloaks.

Back in the ring to the two men lock up in a collar and elbow tie up, with Mayhem easily forcing White back into a corner. The referee, Hal Jenkins, steps in between for a break where Mayhem slowly backs away arms in the air, then swiftly bring his right hand down, disrespectfully slapping White in the face.

Jhub: Well I don’t know how smart a tie up as, Mayhem outweighs White by about 75 pounds or so.

Pete: He learned not to do that again.

White flies out of the corner with a flying forearm smash that lands flush on the forehead of Mayhem send him back a few steps. White moves in to continue the momentum, but is stopped short with a boot to the gut. Mayhem raises White’s head back up and starts screaming at him in tongues.

Bryan: Sounds like Mayhem is possessed in there…

Jhub: Or drunk.

Mayhem again slaps White, this time with such force it sends the smaller man to the canvas. White jumps back to his feet, face glowing red from the previous slaps, and obviously his pride hurt as well. Mayhem smiles at him from across the ring, and slowly moves towards him. White initiates another collar and elbow tie up, but this time follows up with a hip toss, into a arm grapevine.

Jhub: Not a bad tactic by White. Keep Mayhem in the defensive and preferably on the mat.

Pete: Or get lucky and see what happens…

Mayhem quickly reaches for the ropes with Jenkins calling for the break. White continues to twist the arm of Mayhem, as referee Jenkins calls for the break again.

Jenkins: 1…..2……

White pulls back on the arm, as Mayhem screams out in pain, still trying to get the hold broken, as Jenkins says something to White again.

Bryan: Looks like White may just go for the disqualification here.

Jhub: Well he is certainly on the fast track for one.

Jenkins: 3…..4……

White releases the hold, and jumps to his feet as Mayhem rolls outside the ring, shaking his arm trying to get the circulation flowing again. White thrusts his arms in the air as the crowd cheers for him as Mayhem circles the ring still. White rushes the ropes near Mayhem, and drops down for a baseball slide, but Mayhem evades. Now with both men outside the ring, Mayhem takes control. He lands right after right to the head of White. White retaliates with a few of his own, but Mayhem taking the upper hand. Mayhem grabs the arm of White and whips him along the rail crashing him spine first against the security wall. Mayhem approaches White and again grabs his arm and whips him off the wall, only this time jerking him back in for a devastating short-arm clothesline.

Pete: Damn near turned him inside out with that one!

Jhub: Was indeed a very powerful maneuver.

Bryan: Well referee Jenkins looks like he is going to let this one go, as long as it stays in the arena. He hasn’t even begun to count these men out.

Jhub: As well he should! This is Fall Out!!!

Mayhem walks back towards the ring, but changes his mind, and moves back to where White is lying. He reaches down grabbing White by the head and lifting him to his feet. Mayhem sets him in a suplex position, but is blocked with a leg hook as he tries to lift White. Mayhem again tries for the suplex, but is blocked yet again. White counters with a back body drop, which leaves Mayhem lying on the hard floor outside, and him crashing to his knees, using the security rail to hold himself up that high. White pulls himself up and turns to Mayhem who is also trying to get to his feet and delivers a boot square to the side of the head, which barely phases Mayhem who is still trying to get to his feet. White delivers another boot to the temple, this one sending Mayhem back down to the floor.

Jhub: This Mayhem can definitely take some abuse, there is no doubt about that. One of those boots from Mayhem would have been enough to level a normal person.

Bryan: As evil as he may be, nobody is doubting his fortitude.

White grabs the hair of Mayhem pulling him up, and slams his head against the ring post, causing a stream of blood to start from the forehead of Mayhem. The crowd explodes into cheers as Mayhem is busted open. He hits the floor, but is quickly pulled back to his feet and rolled into the ring as White follows. White lifts him again, whipping him off the far side ropes, and connecting with a huge power slam. White hooks the leg as Jenkins hits the mat to count.

Jenkins: Only two!

Bryan: Close two count there.

Pete: You gotta know that mayhem is primetime. Its gonna take more then a simple power slam to put him away.

Jhub: Wow, to think I actually agree with you….

White gets to his feet, and climbs the turnbuckles to the second rope, and dives off with an elbow, landing across the chest of Mayhem. White pulls Mayhem to his feet and whips him into the corner. White rushes into the corner but is met with a boot, from the rising leg of Mayhem. White staggers backwards as Mayhem explodes out of the corner with a clothesline sending White to the mat. Mayhem leaps to his feet and thrusts his arms out, and head back before letting out a scream heard throughout the arena.

Bryan: This man is an animal…

Jhub: Not right in the head for sure. I mean is this really the final battle of good and evil?

Pete: I want a hot dog…

Mayhem yanks White to his feet, and tucks his head between his knees and delivers a massive power bomb that leaves the ring shaking and White in a broken mass on the canvas floor.

Bryan: Get me one too…

Mayhem uses his foot to move the arms of White in to his side as he touches his head, realizing the blood that is in his eyes, belongs to him. He grins sadistically as he pull White back to his feet. He hooks him in a belly to back position, and delivers a German Suplex, leaving White motionless. He pulls him to his feet again, and slaps a huge hand around the neck of White.

Bryan: Chokeslam! Could Mayhem be going for the Oblivion Bomb already?

Jhub: May as well, not like White is moving…Pete: Get me one too…

Pete: But I wasn’t going to…

Pete sighs dejectedly as he leaves the commentators area.

Bryan: Match has been fairly even until that powerbomb damn near crippled White.

Mayhem stands over white who lies there on the mat, not moving from the devastation he has just experienced. Mayhem pulls him to his feet, and again slaps a hand around the neck of White.

Bryan: White will never be the same after tonight!

Jhub: Maybe White is playing for the wrong team…

Bryan: You mean he’s….gay?

Jhub: No you idiot…I’m talking good and evil, heaven and hell.

Bryan: OH! THAT team!

Mayhem raises White into the air, holding him there briefly before slamming him down to the mat, for the second time. The crowd boos immensely as Mayhem grins down at White, and goes for the cover.

Jenkins: 1…2…thr….Only two!

White fidgets a shoulder up but can’t muster more then that, as mayhem in disbelief jumps to his feet and stares at White then at Jenkins who again motions only a two count happened.

Bryan: Wow?! Where did that come from?

Jhub: I don’t know but maybe it was some kind of higher power he received a small surge from. Kind of like being struck by lightning, but only a little piece instead of the whole bolt….

Bryan: …

Mayhem yanks White to his feet, and whips him off the near side. He lunges at him with a clothesline but White anticipates it and ducks underneath. He springs off the far side, and comes back with a diving lariat of his own. Both men now lie on the mat neither of them moving. Referee Jenkins calls for a microphone as he stands in the center of the ring.

Bryan: Jenkins asking for a microphone, I wonder what’s going on here.

Jhub: I don’t know, we haven’t reached the time limit yet…

Jenkins stands in the ring still and has gotten the microphone he requested.

Jenkins: Hey Pete, grab me one too…..

He tosses the microphone back out of the ring, as Pete shows up on the tron in line at the concession stand, slapping a hand across his forehead.

Bryan: Now THAT is great stuff….

Jenkins moves to the corner and climbs the ropes and takes a seat on the top turnbuckle, still not counting as the men lie motionless on the mat.

Bryan: I wonder how long Jenkins is going to let this go on?

Jhub: Three options. One – until someone gets a win. Two – until the time limit expires. Three- until Caldera fires him.

Bryan: Good answers! And it looks like White is slowly getting to his feet in the ring. White stumbles to a corner, as Jenkins hops down off the turnbuckle, as prepares to officiate again. White slowly climbs the ropes all the way to the top. He take a minute to steady himself as he reaches the top.

Bryan: Don’t look to comfortable up there.

Jhub: Look how big he is! What do you expect?

White leaps from the top landing a heavy leg drop across the chest of Mayhem, but landing flush on his tailbone as he does so. White rolls off Mayhem, and grabs his backside, as he crawls around the ring. Pete shows back up at the announcers booth with hot dogs for all and stares at the ring.

Pete: Wow…I really don’t want to know what I missed….

Bryan: ….

Jhub: ….

White crawls to where Mayhem is lying, and drapes an arm over his chest and collapses there.

Jenkins: One…

Bryan: Quick kick out by Mayhem there. Hey Pete, did you get any ketchup packets?

Pete: No damnit! Go get your own!

Mayhem rolls to the edge of the ring, and rolls out. He digs around under the mat and fetches a ladder.

Bryan: Hey this isn’t a….

Jhub: Shut up Bryan. Go get some ketchup….and grab me some.

Bryan: No!

Mayhem sets the ladder up outside the ring and rolls back in just far enough to grab White’s arm. He drags him to the edge of the ring and pulls him out, both men visibly exhausted. He climbs up the ladder, pulling White up the other side, both men heading to the top.

Bryan: Good lord that ladder has to be 17feet in the air.

Jhub: Gotta love industrial size ladders.

Pete: I wonder….Hey Bryan…grab me some ketchup too.

Referee Jenkins, rolls out of the ring, and heads back to the announcer’s booth and grabs a hot dog. He sits there chewing on the hot dog as the two men reach the top of the ladder.

Jhub: Don’t you think you should be there officiating.

Jenkins: Oh I can officiate from here. They are just gonna kill themselves on the ladder anyhow. I don’t want them falling on me, or spattering their brains all over me. Hey Pete did you get any ketchup?

Pete: No, Bryan is going to get some though.

Jenkins: All right! Grab me some too!

Bryan: I’m not going to get and damn ketchup! Stop asking.

Jenkins: What crawled up his butt?

Both men have reached the top of the ladder standing there trading blows back and forth. Each shot sending on man reeling backwards and almost off the ladder. Jenkins finished his hot dog and heads back down to the ringside area, opposite side of the ring as the ladder and watches the two men swaying dangerously 17 feet in the air.

Bryan: This is not going to have a pretty outcome.

Mayhem grabs Whites head and slams it down on the ladder, causing White to almost fall from the ladder, but Mayhem gabs him holding him up top. Mayhem hooks the head of White under his arm.

Bryan: My god… he’s not going to….

Jhub: We may as well get the EMT’s out here….

Pete: That hot dog wasn’t bad….

Mayhem lifts White over the ladder and suplexes him into the ring. Both men crash through the ring canvas as it gives way to the weight and collision.

Bryan: HOLY HELL!

Jhub: Through the ring!! Get the EMT’s out here now!

Both men lie motionless in the ring when suddenly one arm starts to move as Jenkins slides in amidst the carnage and begins to count.

Jenkins: ONE! TWO! THREE! Ring the bell!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! One of those men managed to cover the other, but it couldn’t have been on purpose! Those men are out of it!

Jhub: Jenkins calling for a microphone again, and it’s a good thing! Nobody can see a thing! Who won?!

Andrews: Here is your winner, Bryan Mayhem!

Jhub: Well, that was fun. This Mayhem cat scares me.

Bryan: Oh, man, this was ugly! Let’s get out of here!

Making the Sell -by Jeff

“Where you want it?”

“Over there is fine.” Ricky replies as he points to a corner in the locker room.

The man wearing a brown uniform, with gold logos, has a dolly full of boxes as he wheels it to the corner. He stacks the boxes against the wall, and approaches Ricky.

“Sign here please”

“Sure thing” Ricky signs the electronic shipping order as he continues talking. “Tonight is the night. Tonight I make ALL of GCW understand the power. The refreshing goodness.”

“Right.” The man takes the electronic clipboard and leaves the locker room.

Ricky walks over to the boxes of water stacked in the corner and grins. The door swings open and in walks Michael Stevens, startling Ricky a bit.

“Ricky what the hell are you doing here?”

“It’s Fall out! I had to be here! No way I was gonna miss this!”

“No. I mean in my locker room.”

“Oh...well see…since I wasn’t on the roster for tonight, technically I didn’t get a dressing room.”

“That better not be what I think it is… “

“Just hear me out. Here try one!” Ricky cuts open the box of water and tosses one to Stevens.

“Ricky, they’re hot. I’ll wait. Why do you have those here? Did they ship them here?”

“No even better! I bought them! This way they can see me advertising it on Fall Out! Kind of like unofficial advertising. When they see me I am sure to get a deal!”

“Ricky…take your water to the hallway, and get out. I have some things to tend too”

Ricky shakes his head at Stevens and heads out into the hallway, where he sees Eva heading his way.

“Eva! Here try some of this crisp refreshing water!” Ricky extends a bottle out to her, as she just glares at him and pushes on by.

“Man…this may be harder then I thought. 0 for two. Ah well, rejection is key to advertising! Or something like that.”

Ricky stacks the boxes in the hallway outside of Stevens' locker room, and covers them with a piece of blue plastic. He looks around then heads off down the hallway where the maintenance crew is working.

“Hey guys, you have a marker on you?”

The foreman tosses Ricky a marker and nods to him, as he heads back down the stacks of boxes. Ricky marks in huge black letters on the side ‘Caldera Enterprises’ “Maybe nobody will steal it this way!” Ricky mumbles. He heads back to the crew and returns the marker.

“You fellas want some nice, refreshing, ice…well its actually kind of warm still, but its water! It’s the best wa…”

“No Habla Engleis” comes the reply.

Ricky sighs dejectedly as he hangs his head and heads back down the hallway. He takes a seat on top of the boxes, and stares at Stevens' locker room door, which is still shut tight. Aiden Jackson is seen entering the building and moving past Ricky.

“Hey rook, didn’t know you would be here tonight” Aiden speaks as he passes.

“Wasn’t sposed to be, but I had some things to try and work out. Speaking of which, can I interest you in some tasty beverage refreshment?”

“Sure, I’ll take one”

Ricky hops off the boxes, and uncovers them retrieving a single bottle from underneath. He passes it over to Aiden who takes the bottle and nods to Ricky as he heads down the hallway. Ricky smiles to himself as he sees things are starting to look up. His smile turns to disappointment, as Jackson tosses the unopened bottle in the first trash can he passes.

“That sonofa…” Ricky is interrupted as Stevens peeks out his door, looks left then right, then right at Ricky.

“Ricky…dude, go mingle or something” Stevens shuts the door as Ricky stares perplexed. He grabs 4 bottles of the delicious water and heads off. He passes several open doors, and dark rooms, when he suddenly sees a light come on and the door shut. Ricky approaches the door, and knocks. The door swings open and Borealis is standing there.

“Hey there…” Ricky starts.

“Shhh” Borealis stares down at Ricky’s hands, and notices the water. He snatches two of them away, and slams the door.

Ricky stares blankly, not knowing whether to be happy or upset.

Bryan: Well here it is folks! Up next we have the Number one contenders match for the Tag team title shot.

Jhub: I am really liking these Regulators. These guys don't quit.

Pete: Well persistence is one of the keys to success. Let's see if they succeed tonight against the new tag team Bloodlines.

'See who I am' By within temptation cues up and begins flowing out of the sound system announcing the arrival of the newest tag team in GCW the Bloodlines. Joshua Curtis steps out of the shadows followed by his partner Mizuno Ami. The crowd greets them with a mix of cheers and boos not sure if they are going to like this new tag wrestling sensation. The pair strut down to the ring with an air of confidence. Joshua leap onto the ring side and sits on a rope to allow his partner to slip through. Immediately she climbs on his shoulders as he climbs a turnbuckle both of them raising their hands pulling more cheers from the audience.

Bryan: These two certainly look confident about this match tonight.

Jhub: Yeah, but lets see if that confidence helps them beat the Regulators.

Bloodline's music fades out as 'Regulators' by WarrenG/ Nate Dogg pulses from the speakers. Immediately the crowd begins cheering with wild enthusiasm. Bryan: OH MY GOD!! OH MY GOD!!!

Yale: HOW IN THE HELL DID HE DO THAT?!

Bryan: LeStatt Knight just countered Michael Stevens right into that damn hold! He’s got Stevens on the mat and locked in! But can he hold on?!?!?!?!

The crowd desperately cries out, trying to aid Stevens. Stevens kicks his feet in agony and flails his free arm in a vain attempt to find a way out, but finds himself completely trapped.

Bryan: There is no way out of this hold! Knight’s got him cinched in perfectly! OH MY GOD!

Knight screams out in absolute torture, pulling back one final time with all the strength in his body. Stevens screams out in pain as Briggs kneels beside him, desperately searching for a word. Stevens begins pushing back with his knees, suddenly finding some leverage against Knight!

Yale: WAIT A MINUTE! He’s doing it, JB! He’s got Knight off balance!

Bryan: Could it be?! Stevens might break this hold!

Stevens continues pulling with his back and pushing with his knees, pulling Knight from his seated position over his back! The crowd explodes wildly as Stevens momentarily appears to be free, until Knight shuts him down. Dropping down with all his weight and twisting Stevens into an even more vile position, Knight arcs his back as far as he can, bending Stevens’ back almost completely in half. Stevens, unable to even manage a breath to scream in pain, closes his eyes tight… and taps out.

Bryan: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!

Yale: AMAZING! I CAN’T BELIEVE IT!

The crowd, exhausted, plummets into disbelief. Knight maintains the hold until he hears the sound of the bell, and finally releases Stevens. Both men lie motionless on the canvas, Stevens appearing to be completely unconscious.

Andrews: Here is your winner… and STILL GCW World Heavyweight Champion, LeStatt Knight!

Bryan: Oh.. my… God.

Yale: That was… I don’t know what to say, JB!

Bryan: Damnit, I thought he had him! It looked like Stevens has Knight right where he wanted him!

Briggs is handed the GCW title belt, which he brings to LeStatt. A weary and hobbled Knight reaches out with his left arm for his belt, but retracts it instantly, screaming again in pain. “O’Fortuna” begins to play once again, and is joined by the unmistakable sound of silence from the stunned fans.

Bryan: Michael Stevens wrestled a hell of a match, but he’s fallen short again! I… I just don’t know what to say!

Yale: I’ll tell you what happened, JB! Let’s take a look at that replay!

A still shot of Stevens holding onto the Armbar is shown, finally playing as Briggs begins making his count on Knight.

Yale: Stevens had Knight down, and Knight couldn’t escape. I don’t think Knight ever lose consciousness! With each fall of his arm, Stevens pulls harder and harder, until finally he pulled himself into a position where Knight could counter him! Knight played possum until Stevens overextended himself, and turned it right into the hold, and that was game over!

Bryan: But to shut out that much pain and lie still, that’s just… that’s inhuman! Stevens was absolutely torturing LeStatt Knight, but Knight had the presence of mind to stay in the game and make the counter! Amazing!

Yale: Absolutely, JB! Stevens wrestled a good game, but I think we can finally say it for sure… LeStatt Knight is invincible!

Knight rises to his feet and Briggs raises his arm in the air. Typhoid Mary slides into the ring, immediately coming to her man’s side. Knight places an arm around her and shifts his weight onto her shoulders, and she begins helping him out of the ring. Knight never looks back at Stevens as Mary helps him stagger back up the ramp.

Bryan: Stevens started this thing off on fire, Dave, but LeStatt really dominated in this one! At Annihilation, Stevens controlled Knight the entire time. But this time Knight was in charge. Either way, the outcome was the same. LeStatt Knight wins, and is STILL the Champion of the World!

Yale: I am impressed, JB. Michael Stevens is a world class athlete and a hell of a competitor, but he simply couldn’t beat LeStatt Knight. There was nowhere for him to go, nothing he could do to escape that hold. I can only imagine what was going through his head when he tapped out and submitted away his shot at the GCW World Championship.

Bryan: God, it’s something Stevens has worked for all his life, it’s something he’s fought hard a long to accomplish, but tonight simply wasn’t his night! I guess it just wasn’t meant to be! Stevens has transformed himself into a new man and taken his wrestling to a whole ‘nother level, but he simply didn’t have enough to defeat the GCW World Heavyweight Champion.

Yale: He’ll be back, JB. But for now, he looks like he needs some help! He’s completely out of it!

EMTs finally arrive in the ring to aid Stevens, who lies face down in a puddle of blood, completely motionless. The EMTs circle Stevens and go right to work, as Mary and Knight finally come to a stop at the top of the stage.

Bryan: There he is, fans! Like it or not, he’s still the champion! And I ask you this, Dave, if Michael Stevens couldn’t beat him, who will?

Yale: If this is how Knight wrestles from now on, I don’t think there’s a man walking that could defeat him!

Bryan: Damnit! Well, there you have it, fans, LeStatt Knight is still the GCW World Heavyweight Champion! That’s it from Toronto! Ladies and gentlemen, for Pete, The Jhub and David Yale, I’m James Bryan, so long from GCW FALLOUT!

The GCW logo appears on the bottom of the screen, and the camera affords two final shots. First, the EMTs snapping a neck brace on a motionless, defeated Michael Stevens. And finally, the slumping, bloody, battered and broken body of LeStatt Knight, holding the GCW World Title belt in the air once again, reminding everyone that he is still the Heavyweight Champion of the World………………
