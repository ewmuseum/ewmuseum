He’s one of the top technical wrestlers this business has ever seen, which I’m sure you’ll take credit for, too, Caldera. Caldera: It’ll suffice to say that I was pretty damn good in my day, JB. Yale: Show some respect! Bryan: In any event, Jordan White is in some serious trouble here. Octavian is dominating this match, and White’s showing no signs of being able to contend! Jay Terror’s betrayal has really messed this kid up. Slowly pulling himself back to all fours, White can do nothing as Octavian steps in and delivers a crushing kick to the ribs. The blow rocks White back to the canvas, and he rolls out onto the apron for safety. Octavian takes his time in approaching as he watches White cling to the ropes in an attempt to stand. He gets to his feet, only for Octavian to hook him in a front facelock and deliver a big vertical suplex back into the ring. Octavian floats over and hooks a leg! …1! …2! …KICKOUT! Bryan: Wow! Octavian very nearly scored the win there after a big suplex, and White’s just about out already! Yale: It didn’t take much, but a little bit of Octavian goes a long way, JB. It’s pretty clear that White’s just completely out of his element here. Caldera: And this guy was nearly this company’s number one contender? I take a lot of flak for supposedly hand-picking my challengers, but come on, guys, please explain to me how this guy would have been better than Jay Terror. Bryan: I’m not taking anything away from what Terror did in the last few months, but the way he finally got his title shot was just wrong, and you know it. Octavian drags White back to his feet and delivers a European uppercut. The blow sends White staggering into the ropes, but he quickly bounces back and throws a weak right hand. Octavian blocks the shot and pulls White in, delivering a high overhead belly to belly suplex! The crowd oohs as White bounces off the mat again, and again Octavian hooks a leg. …1! …2! …KICKOUT! Bryan: Wow, huge move from Octavian, but White won’t stay down! He’s refusing to give up here, but he’s had almost zero offense in this match so far. Yale: It doesn’t look good, JB. Octavian’s just toying with him here. Willing himself back up again, White faces Octavian with a defiant but labored look in his eyes. Octavian, showing no response except the slight grin on his lips, simply kicks him in the gut and hoists him onto his shoulder! The fans stand as Octavian turns, presenting the limp White to the crowd. Bryan: Oh no, Roman Devastator coming! Yale: Good, finish off this pointless match! After parading White around the ring for a few moments, Octavian goes to drop him on his head. But White manages to slip off behind him and shove him into the ropes! Octavian bounces back and White launches a huge superkick right at his face! Somehow Octavian gets his hands up and, almost by accident, catches White’s boot before it can find its mark. Octavian immediately trips White to his back and hooks the legs, trying to turn him into a full crab! Bryan: White went for the superkick, but Octavian’s countering him into the Roman Rapper! White’s fighting it with everything he’s got! Yale: But he’s going over, JB! He can’t fight it off! Caldera: This is going to be fun! Despite his resistance, White cannot overcome Octavian’s strength. The crab is applied and the crowd cries out in shock as White, caught in the center of the ring, writhes in agony on the mat. Bryan: IT’S ON! This match is over! White’s got nowhere to go! Yale: Haha! What a great night! Jordan White just got his ass kicked by the Hall of Famer! Caldera: And I guess our number one contender wants to take a closer look after all! Bryan: Oh, son of a bitch, now he shows up! Beaming, Jay Terror struts out on stage, moving quickly in hopes of getting an up-close look at White’s torture. Octavian sees him coming and arches back further, eliciting an even louder cry of agony from White’s throat. Bryan: Terror’s coming out here to watch White submit! This is just humiliating! Yale: This is The Establishment, JB! They’re riding high again! White’s hand raises, appearing to be moments from tapping the mat to end the pain. But Terror steps into view, smiling and taunting him from ringside, and suddenly White’s face changes. The twisted expression of pain fades into a violent scowl, and he suddenly begins clawing furiously towards Terror! Terror, shocked, raises an eyebrow but holds his ground, shouting at Octavian to lock him down. Bryan: Whoa, hold on a second here! Terror got right in White’s face, and now White’s scrambling to get his hands on him! With the Roman Rapper still applied! Yale: Where did this come from!? Bryan: White was utterly lifeless in this match until he saw Jay Terror, and I think Terror’s taunting might have sparked something in him! Octavian fights all he can, but White succeeds in dragging himself to the ropes. He slaps a hand on the bottom rope, enabling him to pull Octavian all the way to the edge of the ring. Briggs begins the count but Octavian refuses to break the hold; but White, ignoring the pain, takes a huge swing at Terror through the ropes! Terror manages to dodge the blow, but he scurries clear of White’s reach. Bryan: HA! And Jordan White just threw a punch at Jay Terror before Octavian even broke the hold! I don’t believe it! Caldera: Like it matters! The damage is done here! White’s just lost his head. Octavian reluctantly breaks the hold and lifts White to his feet, but White explodes upward with a sudden left hook. The shot stuns Octavian, and White unloads with a rapid flurry of fists to the face! The fans roar to life as White sends Octavian hard into the ropes. Octavian comes back with a big lariat, but White ducks beneath it, slips behind, and delivers a colossal German suplex! Bryan: Good God, White damn near suplexed Octavian out of his boots! Where did this come from? Yale: White’s not just lying down any more! Octavian might have fallen asleep here a bit, and now White’s making him pay! Octavian struggles to his knees, his eyes glazed as Terror encourages him from nearby. White hits the ropes again as Octavian stands, and he launches himself at Octavian with a huge Yakuza kick! The wildly thrown boot connects square in Octavian’s face, instantly knocking him cold to the mat! The crowd goes absolutely wild as White stands with a furious look on his face, staring glassy-eyed out at the camera. Bryan: OH MY GOD! White just took Octavian’s head off! Octavian’s out! Yale: I’ve never seen anything like that before, JB… Octavian’s out! Silent with concern, Caldera can only watch as White takes to the ropes and begins to climb. Bryan: And now White’s going up for the finish! This is exactly what you told him NOT to do, Mr. President! You wanted him to be more like you, more like Octavian, but this is his response! Yale: Don’t do it! White throws his arms into the air, feeding off the frenzy in the arena. He then points down at Terror before taking off, pumping his legs once before dropping them right over Octavian’s neck! The pop is monumental as White drops and makes the cover, his eyes fixed on Terror as Briggs makes the count… …1! …2! …3!!!! Bryan: IT’S OVER! White nailed the big leg drop! It’s over! “DNA” cues up again as White slowly gets to his feet. Briggs raises his arm, but quickly bails out as he sees Terror climb to the apron. Andrews: Here is your winner, JORDAN WHITE! Bryan: White did it! He looked like he was mailing this one in until Jay Terror showed up, and suddenly he roared to life! And just like that he took Octavian down! Wow! Yale: What a comeback! That looked like it was going to be a total beatdown, but White was the one that delivered the ass kicking in the end! Caldera: Excuse me. Caldera casts aside his headset and moves to Terror’s side, forcing White to retreat a few steps in the face of superior numbers. Bryan: Oh no, and now Caldera and Terror and bearing down on White! Looks like they’re going to exact a bit of revenge here for this huge win by White! Yale: Serves him right! Didn’t he know this wasn’t about him!? As White readies himself for a fight, the roar in the stands suddenly intensifies. Vivica J. Valentine, her World Title belt around her waist and a steel chair in her hands, rushes down the ramp at full speed! The fans go wild as Valentine slides into the ring, sending Caldera and Terror retreating back to the floor. Bryan: THE CHAMPION IS HERE! Valentine to the rescue! Yale: Oh, come on… why on earth does she feel the need to help White?! Valentine points an accusing finger down at Terror as Caldera helps Octavian out of the ring. With Valentine armed and on the higher ground, The Establishment thinks twice about their assault and begins to back away from the ring. Bryan: And The Establishment is getting the hell out of Dodge! This night did not go as planned after all! Yale: It’s just one match, and it doesn’t change the fact that Jay Terror is the one with the title shot, not Jordan White! Bryan: But White’s sent a message, Dave! He’s not just going to sit back and take this lying down after all! With the crowd going absolutely wild, “Image of the Invisible” cues up for the World Champion. Valentine continues to shout obscenities at The Establishment as they back up the ramp, before turning back to check on White. But White merely turns his back on Valentine and drops out of the ring. Bryan: Well, Valentine came to White’s aid, but White doesn’t look like he’s sticking around to say thanks! Yale: Wow, what’s going on here, JB? White was victorious, and Valentine came to help him out, but I guess he’s still a bit emotional… Bryan: Of course he is! But there’s clearly more going on inside Jordan White’s head than we might understand. Valentine raises an eyebrow in confusion as White, never making eye contact, begins to walk away from the ring. Valentine watches him leave with a lowered face, but then shrugs and moves to pose for the crowd. Bryan: What on earth is going on with White? He looked like he had no interest in this match until Jay Terror showed up, and then he suddenly took Octavian apart! And now he’s given the World Champion the cold shoulder! Yale: Well, Valentine doesn’t seem to mind much. She did what she came to do, she ran off The Establishment, and even after a Jordan White victory it’s Vivica J. Valentine standing tall at the end of the night! With Valentine hoisting her title belt into the air for the benefit of those with flash photography, White vanishes behind the stage without a glance backwards. For David Yale, I’m James Bryan… Good night!
