RUMORS Monday, October 03, 2005

Former TV Champion at CEI?
Columbus, Ohio- President Caldera may have left GCW programming, but he hasn't stopped making an impact.

As usual, Caldera Enterprises security was airtight this afternoon, allowing out only the information they wanted released. However, what was provided leads us to believe that a former GCW Television Champion has inked a return deal with the company, and will be returning on WorldWide this week.

Again, no name was released, nor any hint as to who it could be. Zippy remains under GCW contract, NBK is currently active in Japan, and we can safely rule out former Cruiserweight champion Demented. That said, the remaining list of names is long, and speculation has turned up no solid answers.

So Caldera Enterprises has their way again, and the world will have to wait and watch WorldWide this week to get the answer. However, it has been confirmed by the company that a former TV champion is returning to WorldWide this week, so be sure not to miss it!

Sunday, September 18, 2005

Coyle O'Conner Injured?
Vancouver, British Columbia- New GCW superstar Coyle O'Conner was reportedly injured during his matchup with Aiden Jackson in Round Two of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament on Thursday night.

Reports remain sketchy regarding the seriousness of the injury, but O'Conner's name has been removed from all GCW booking for the next 4 weeks. Sources report Coyle is expected to miss in the neighborhood of 6-8 weeks.

The injury reportedly came when Aiden Jackson delivered the Criminal Justice, a massive falling DDT. Coyle suffered a hairline fracture in his vertebrae, but is expected to make a full recovery and return to the ring before long. The injury will not require surgery.

"We are sad to lose Coyle for a while, we were very excited about him and what he brought to the Cruiserweight Title division," a GCW spokesman said today.

The move was set up by the interference of O'Conner's rival X. The two were set to wrestle at FallOut, but that match has been removed from the card due to Coyle's injury. When asked if X and Jackson, who appeared to be working together, had planned the events leading to Coyle's injury beforehand, the spokesman replied "...it's something we're looking into."

Wednesday, September 14, 2005

Identities of Mysterious Men Revealed?
Several times now a mysterious duo has appeared on GCW Television over the past several weeks. Most of their time has been spent bickering amongst each other, but nonetheless it appears they are making progress towards entering GCW.

The two have been granted an audience with President Caldera today. However, their identities still remain a mystery to all but a few at Caldera Enterprises. The big news coming out today is that they are two former GCW stars, and big names at that.

Some sources name them as Badger Benandanti and Shane Lane, two former GCW Champions. However, this is far from confirmed. Other reports have offered names like The Outlaw Kid, Chameleon, Branson King, and even Jason O'Neil. But all seem to be idle speculation thus far.

Keep watching GCW TV, as the duo is likely to appear again soon.

Tuesday, September 06, 2005

Toxic Fired?
Tacoma, Washington- With GCW still on tour in the Pacific Northwest, the company has been running house shows throughout the region in the past weeks. Fans at Tacoma were treated to a special surprise that went terribly wrong.

Former X-Wrestling Champion Toxic made his return at the Tacoma house show, wrestling in one of the opening bouts. It has since been learned that Toxic had his suspension lifted tentatively, and was permitted to return to the ring to work off some rust.

Toxic wrestled Tacoma local talent Jeff Higgins. When he reached the back, he was reportedly confronted by President Caldera. Caldera was upset with the way Toxic had wrestled the match. Toxic had indeed given less than his full effort during the match, until the end where he unloaded on Higgins and hurt him badly. Toxic attempted to blow Caldera off, but the President kept at him.

Caldera and Toxic exited public view to a locker room, but shouting was heard. The arugment appeared to get very heated, and moments later officials entered the room to stop the fight. Caldera was led back out of the room, where rumors immediately began to fly that Caldera and Toxic had come to blows.

Toxic was immediately suspended again and sent home.

What this does to Toxic's career can't be known right away, but his contract is coming up in December. Toxic was not available for comment, and Caldera has apparently issued a hush order on Toxic, restricting him from talking to the media about his status with the company.

This certainly seems to be another nail in the coffin of Toxic, whose career with GCW appears to be nearing an end. The twenty-one year old phenom is still heralded as a potential legend in the business, and his combination of size, strength and agility make him a rare commodity. But his poor work ethic and refusal to cooperate with management is making him hard to handle.

The latest rumors out of Caldera Enterprises suggest the company is seeking buyout options.

Wednesday, August 24, 2005

Huge New Signing?
Columbus, Ohio- The wrestling media was in a frenzy today as a long black limosuine arrived at Caldera Enterprises in Columbus. The passenger arrived with their collar raised high and a hat low over their face, and was hurriedly surrounded by CEI security and lead into the building. Many pictures were snapped, but not one revealed the identity of the person.

The media was tipped off by an anonymous source, who called in and reported that a big name wrestler would be arriving at CEI today to negotiate a contract with GCW.

President Caldera refused to comment, and a CEI spokesman denied any knowledge of a free agent visit.

No CEI information reveals any new signings, and inside sources say that things were very tight-lipped around the office today. However, someone intent on hiding their identity was definitely spotted in Columbus today, and apparently met with President Caldera for several hours in secret.

More as it develops.
