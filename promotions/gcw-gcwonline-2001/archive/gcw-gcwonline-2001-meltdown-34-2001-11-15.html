(Badger pulls the Jhub up, and both men turn around. But Rollins explodes with a superkick, leveling the Jhub.) Bryan- Whoa, the match is over, but Rollins just leveled the Jhub! Now Badger is fighting both men, but he’s a little outnumbered! Yale- Outnumbered, out-classed, out-matched, either way he doesn’t stand a chance! Dread and Rollins are pounding away at Badger! But wait, Badger is fighting back! There’s no quit in this man! Badger sends Rollins down with a headbutt, and begins going toe to toe with Dread! Bryan- Dread and Badger fire away, as the Jhub slowly gets to his feet! Rollins is back up, but the Jhub catches him from behind! The Jhub takes Rollins down, and steps through… SHARPSHOOTER! Sharpshooter on Rollins! Yale- And Badger spins Dread around… BADGERCLUTCH ON DREAD! Dread and Rollins are both caught… and they’re both tapping out!! (The crowd goes crazy as both Rollins and Dread begin to tap from the pain of the submission holds. After a moment, both men release their opponents. BadgerTheme hits, as the Jhub and Badger nod to one another.) Bryan- The Jhub and Badger gave it their all here tonight, but in the end, Danger’s plan didn’t work! Dread and Rollins have been left lying! Dread must face Badger, and Rollins must face the Jhub, all at NC-17 in ten days! (The crowd continues to cheer, as Badger and the Jhub walk together to the back. Rollins and Dread slowly try to recover, as the show cuts to the NC-17 promo. After playing, the show cuts to commercial.) (Meltdown returns, and heads to ringside.) Bryan- Well, here we go. This should be a most interesting match, ladies and gentlemen, and should really set the stage for NC-17! (Might of Rome hits, as the crowd reacts with explosive boos. Octavian, title belt in hand, emerges from the back with Mr. Danger. The two stop at the end of the stage, as gold pyro erupts from the rafters. After a pause, the two then continue to the ring.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, the team of the GCW Commissioner, Mr. Danger, and the GCW Heavyweight Champion, Octavian! Bryan- Well, this Octavian is an awesome force, that’s for sure. And he has been hellbent on destroying Tristain Mayhem and poor Nanci! But we’ll get the truth at NC-17, Dave… President Caldera, earlier tonight, announced that Nanci has submitted to a blood test, and the results will be announced at NC-17! Yale- And they will prove once and for all that Octavian is that baby’s father! (Octavian and Danger enter the ring, and Octavian raises his belt to the air. The crowd boos even more, as Might of Rome fades. Paranoid hits next, and the crowd goes crazy. Together, Tristain Mayhem and President Caldera waste no time in heading for the ring. They both bolt for the ring, and slide in, going right after their opponents!) Bryan- Whoa, and this one is underway! Mayhem is going right after Octavian, and Caldera and Danger are going at it! Hold on, Mayhem has already tossed Octavian to the outside, and he’s following him out! Caldera is taking it to Danger in the corner, and this crowd has come alive! Yale- Mayhem is stomping away at Octavian out here on the floor, and Octavian is scrambling to get away! Caldera is simply hammering away at Mr. Danger, and he’s got him pinned in the corner! Mayhem finally takes Octavian by the arm, and whips him right into the steel steps! (The crowd goes ballistic as Octavian hits the steps, and flips head over heels to the other side.) Bryan- Caldera finally pulls Danger out of the corner, and delivers a massive belly to belly suplex! What a move! Danger is hurting, as Mayhem follows Octavian to our announce table here! Mayhem closes in… but Octavian spins around with one of our monitors, and cracks Mayhem over the head! Yale- Whoa! Mayhem collapses, and Octavian pulls him up onto our table! Watch out, JB! Mayhem is lying prone here, as Octavian climbs up onto the Spanish announce table! Oh, no, watch out! (Yale and Bryan scramble for cover as Octavian leaps from the Spanish table to the English, landing on Mayhem with a big elbow drop. The table shatters under the pressure, destroying both men.) Bryan- Good God! Our announce table has been destroyed! Octavian with a big elbow drop has taken out Tristain Mayhem! Octavian is hurting, too, but he’s starting to stir! Good Lord! Yale- Back in the ring, Caldera is still taking it to Danger! Caldera has Danger in the corner, and is trying to lift him up onto the turnbuckle! But Octavian is up on his feet out here, leaving Mayhem in a heap on what used to be our announce table! And now the Champ has a steel chair! Bryan- It’s absolute chaos out here! This is hardly even a match! Octavian slides the chair into the ring, and climbs back in! Caldera has Danger set up top, and is trying to bring him down! But Octavian moves in, and cracks the President in the back with the chair!! Yale- And Caldera falls all the way back to the mat! And now Danger is still on the turnbuckle! He takes off!!! Elbow drop from the commissioner to the president! Wow! Bryan- Tristain Mayhem is busted open out here, and is hardly moving! We may need some help for him! Can we get some help for Tristain Mayhem, please!? Hold on, Octavian picks Caldera up, and tosses him overhead with a monstrous Roman-Greco throw! Yale- See? These men are the future of GCW, not President Caldera!! Caldera’s finished! Hold on, the referee is trying to restore some order here… oh, but Danger takes him down from behind! He never saw it coming! Octavian and Danger together, now, are stomping away at Caldera! Bryan- Wait just a damn minute… (From the back appear Jason Dread and Rich Rollins once more. The crowd’s boos intensify as the two appear on the ramp. But their boos turn to cheers as the Jhub and Badger make their way out again, charging from behind!) Bryan- Wait, Badger and Jhub! Badger and Jhub are here! Badger takes down Dread, the Jhub takes down Rollins! Good God, it’s total anarchy! Yale- No fair, they came from behind! But Danger and Octavian are still pounding the President! Mayhem is slowly getting back to his feet, but he’s finished, JB! Bryan- Mayhem has managed to pull himself up to the apron, but he’s in a real bad way! Wait, now Octavian is giving Danger some orders! Danger is going up top again! What’s this going to be? Octavian gives Caldera one more boot, as Danger measures the President! Yale- But wait! Who the hell is that?! (Without warning, someone leaps over the barricade from the crowd, and hops onto the apron. The man wears all black, including a black mask. The man reaches up and gives Danger a shove, sending the commissioner all the way over the top to the floor below!) Bryan- What?! There’s a masked man that just jumped in from the crowd, and he’s assaulted Danger!! Could this be who I think it is? Yale- It’s a crazy fan! Bryan- No, it’s got to be Caldera’s backup! The man who Caldera is bringing out at NC-17 to help him with GCW! He’s just attacked Mr. Danger! (The crowd goes crazy as Danger lies hurting on the floor. Dread, Rollins, Badger, and Jhub continue their fight in the entrance ramp, as Mayhem slowly slides back into the ring.) Bryan- Octavian has spotted the masked man, and is shouting angrily at him! But wait, Caldera is back up! Caldera is back up! LOW BLOW! Caldera hit Octavian with a low blow from behind!! Octavian is bent over in pain, and Mayhem is up in the ring! Yale- Mayhem runs in… HAMMER ON!!! (The crowd erupts with an enormous pop. Octavian’s head bounces off the mat, as Caldera revives the referee.) Yale- Oh, no! Bryan- OH YES! Mayhem has taken down the World Champion, and he’s got the cover! The referee moves in! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!!!! He did it! (The eruption that follows the three count is deafening. Fans goes crazy as Mayhem rolls off the Champion, and Caldera kicks him out of the ring. Paranoid plays, but is barely detectable over the pandemonium.) Andrews- Here are your winners, President Caldera and Tristain Mayhem! Bryan- Tristain Mayhem has just pinned the Heavyweight Champion!! Tristain Mayhem has just pinned Octavian! Could this be a sign of things to come?! Yale- Damn it!! (Danger crawls over to regroup with the champ, as the Jhub continues to battle Rollins, and Badger continues to battle Dread.) Bryan- Tristain Mayhem has pinned the World Champion here tonight! (Mayhem spots the timekeeper heading to give Octavian his title belt back. Mayhem reaches through the ropes, and snatches the belt away.) Yale- Hey!! (Mayhem glances at it, before holding it high over his head. The crowd goes ballistic as he raises the World Title belt over his head.) Bryan- Tristain Mayhem has pinned Octavian, and is holding the World Title belt! Yale- Put that down! That’s not yours! Bryan- We’re out of time! For David Yale I’m James Bryan… Goodnight! (The show begins to fade as the cheering fans watch Mayhem hold the belt, as Octavian and Danger lie in pain on the floor…….)
