Brecca Xanthus Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames "The Colossus of Rhodes", "The Behemoth", "The Golden Goliath", "The Nemean Lion", "The Strongest Man on The Face of The Planet" Hometown Monolithos, The Island of Rhodes, Greece Birthday July 9th 1985 Height 7'4'' Weight 487 pounds Biography Not much is known of Brecca's youth, what is known is that he had a rather rich upbringing by the way he acts, speaks and socializes. All that is known is that he grew up living and loving his Manager Alexia, Who shows to have almost a full control of Brecca. That means if she wants it, Brecca will get it for her. Alexia, who speaks fluent English is the Behemoth's mouth piece to say, she speaks for him when he needs a translator, she has done so when Brecca gained international fame for his feats of strength, which includes pulling 747s with full capacity, eighteen wheelers, and multiple buses. She can speak for the giant in five foreign languages which includes English, French, German, Italian and Spanish. Once Brecca gained international fame for his record shattering feats of strength press constantly pestered the couple, Alexia takes the brunt of it in stride as Brecca would rather keep his privacy at times. Brecca seems to have vast training in Greco-Roman styles of wrestling, Alexia claims he would be a World Champion but "There isn't a weight class large enough for my giant." Alexia, to the liking of the press stated Brecca would participate in professional wrestling training on the mainland of Greece. Much of the giants popularity died down over the two years of training. To his liking he returned to Rhodes in privacy and returned to his life as Alexia contacted multiple wrestling organizations. She claims "As long as it brings competition, my giant will be happy." So now Brecca waits, patiently in his paradise. Waiting for a Federation he sees fit for his presences. Other Feds N/A Strengths His World class Strength, and his insane Durability Weaknesses His size slows him down at times aswell as his narcissistic actions. Wrestling Style Greco-Roman wrestling, unmatched power Strategy Working on the opponents back, doing anything to set up his submissions or if needed, The Final Labour. Finisher Move The Final Labour Finisher Description Brecca throws a running opponent high in the air vertically as they fall back he catches in a snap Samoan drop, his weight ontop of the opponent. (The smaller the opponent the higher they are thrown into the air.) Finisher Setup Move The Final Labour is usually out of nowhere. Setup Description N/A Special Moves Lock of Heracles- A Bear hug (used commonly as a finish) Lock of Atlas- A sickening torture rack (Also commonly used as a finish) Other Moves Basics - Simple chain wrestling, scoop slam, hip toss, Sidewalk slam, beals, choke lift, backdrop, belly to belly suplex, german suplex, stalling vertical suplex (crowd will count seconds) as well as just throwing opponents at will.
