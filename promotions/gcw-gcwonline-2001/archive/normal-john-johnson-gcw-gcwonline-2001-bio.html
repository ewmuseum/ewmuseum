Other Feds Just Wrestling Other Fed Titles Just Wrestling Champion (current) Other Fed Awards Just Wrestling Homecoming Tour co-MVP (w/ Hush) Wrestling Style Counter Grappling All-rounder Strategy John's main strength inside the ring is his ability to counter moves. This is his major strength inside the ring. He has a natural ability of understanding his surroundings, his body and momentum. Using the moves of an opponent to turn it inside out and in his favour is almost an artform when it comes to Johnson and most times it results in a monstrous shift in momentum. It is not used as an escape from a hold or takedown, but it is turned into an offensive manoeuvre by Johnson. Sometimes it can almost be seen as John being strategic in inviting his opponent to strike him. After a few minutes into a match John will begin to take on some of his opponent's personae in the ring. It's not something he is conscientiously is attempting to do, but within the confines of the ring he is attempting to get into his opponent's head and understand them. Like when Oprah is talking to someone and she starts speaking in their accent. He's just trying to understand. He might pick up some of their mannerisms but will definitely pick up their wrestling style. He does, however, like to steal opponent's finishing moves on occasion (more particularly when in a feud) just to rub it on that they're being defeated by him.
