Caden Alexander Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Cade (More to come later) Hometown Bixby, Oklahoma Birthday September 25th, 1979 Height Six feet even Weight Two Hundred Thirty-One Fed Titles None at this point Fed Awards None at this point Biography To be filled out as I have time and as we go along. Other Feds PRIME, Core, AW, NEW, XGWO Other Fed Awards Numerous Wrestling Style Stong Style based well rounded wrestling Finisher Move Perfect End & Crossface Chickenwing Finisher Description 180 Degree Sit-Out Pedigree {http://www.yucubetoo.com/moves/180_sitout_pedigree.gif} & Crossface Chickenwing w/Legs wrapped around opponents mid section Finisher Setup Move Blue and Black Attack Setup Description A Bulldog that can be done from several varations. Either a running bulldog, off the second or top rope, reversing out of a move, etc. Other Moves Trademark/Signature Moves: {1} Miracle-Buster: Spinebuster {2} Cross Arm Lungblower (Carlito Style Backbreaker only with opponents arms crossed) {3} Triple Snap Suplexes (Guerrero's Three Amigos) {4} Apron DDT (http://www.yucubetoo.com/moves/ApronDDT.gif) {5} Flying Armbar (http://www.yucubetoo.com/moves/FlyingArmbar.gif) Some Basic Moves: *Notes: {1} Moves are always subject to size of opponents. {2} Though he is a cruiser-weight sized guy and is athletic he DOES NOT flip around and act like a cruiser. He can, and does, do some flyer moves but he doesn't use a ton of them and he doesn't do anything real fancy in reguards to taking to the air.* Striking Offense: knife-edge chops, clothesline, short arm clothesline, forearm smashes, knee lift, elbow strikes to the back, chop block, basic kicks and stomps, lariat, european uppercut, spinning back elbow, lots of forearm strikes, flying forearm strikes, dropkicks, of course basic punches and kicks, standing side kick, sissors kick, etc. Pin Combinations: Backslide, inside cradle, small package, victory roll, Sunset Flip, etc. Suplexes: Exploder suplex, belly-to-belly suplex, German suplex, Release German, Release Belly-To-Belly, Wristclutch Suplex, Leg Capture Suplex, Northern Lights, Vertical, Reverse Vertical, Double Underhook Suplex, Slingshot Suplex, Superplex, etc. Just about any suplex you can dream up he can execute. Submissions/Wear Down Holds: Boston crab, Dragon Sleeper, abdominal stretch, , Mexican surfboard, Gory Special, front face lock, hammerlock, armbar, hammerlock w/ body scissors, wristlock, top wristlock, key lock, figure four, grapevine leg lock, Indian Leg lock, camel cluth, Abominal stretch, etc. Back/Arm Breakers: Backbreaker, Pendulum backbreaker, Gutwrench backbreaker, tilt-a-whirl backbreaker, Shoulder breaker, Single Arm Breaker, Jumping Arm Breaker, etc. Drops and Slams: Body slam, scoop slam, powerslam , pump handle slam/drop, Military Press Slam/Drop, standard powerbomb, Samoan Drop, Fall-Away Slam, etc. Tosses and such: Hip toss, Arm Drags (Mexican, Japanese, American and yes they are different), Back body drop, monkey flip, atomic throw, Airplane spin/throw, etc. DDT's/etc: DDT, Veritical Suplex DDT, Reverse DDT, Jump Swinging DDT, Atomic Drop/Inverted Atomic Drop, Side Russian Legsweep, Front Russian Leg Sweep, Swinging Neckbreaker, Neckbreaker Drop, Hangmans Neckbreaker, Running DDT, Running Neckbreaker, etc. Aerial: Crossbody, Top Rope Elbow, Missle Dropkick, Top Rope Leg drop, Flying Clothesline or Forearm, Flying Sunset Flip, etc. *This is a small sample for now but I'll add to them as I go along. 3 Strengths: {1} Great balance and strength, using them with his knowledge of leverage, allows him to pull off moves against much bigger opponents that the average wrestler couldn't. {2} Ring technican. He always knows where he is in the ring and really does a great job of forming a strategy for matches that he employs. {3} Very effective, quick, stiff striking offense. Mainly in the forms or punches, elbows, forearms, chops, and kicks. The kicks are some basic ones you won't find him doing a lot of fancy martial arts type stuff. {4} Very well trained and prepared wrestler. This allows him to reverse many moves. He is especially good, due to his own knowledge of it, good at reversing or countering submissions. Due to the fact that he isn't someone who will lay in a submission hold forever, or to the point of injury, and is willing to tap out to avoid injury he has adapted and learned to avoid being put into the situations as much as possible. Still he can be placed into them and he will tap out if there is no way for him to escape, reverse, or break the hold. 3 Weaknesses {1} Can be a bit too hot tempered and thus cause himself to lose focus. This leads to reversals of fortune in a match and even can get him disqualified from time to time for simply snapping and going overboard. {2} Despite being fairly agile and quick, what you'd expect from a guy of his size, he is no match for a cruiserweight styled wrestler in this department. They can move around much faster than he can and their taking to the skies can often pose a problem for him. He has to keep them grounded and dictate the pace. {3} Old school upbringing makes it very hard for him to face a woman. He won't back down from a match but will significantly alter his style and approach against a woman. He simply will not strike her the way he would a man choosing instead to out wrestle them and use counters, reversals, and chain moves to set up his submission finish. You'll never find him using his power finisher on a woman. This is a weakness because it can cause him to take a great deal of punishment from a quick striking female and can often cause him a lose cause he is waiting for a moment to captialize on, rather than creating one. {4} His lack of trusting people, and thus being a sarcastic asshole to many, by nature often leads to situations where he is vastly out numbered. He is always going to have more enemies than friends. Simple Do's and Don'ts of the ring: {1} Very much the sportsman. He likes to keep his matches clean and win by being the better wrestler but isn't above the occassional cheap tactic to get out of a jam. He'll use the occassional thumb to the eye, ropes for leverage, things of that nature but for the most part likes to keep his matches clean. {2} Will chain together moves as much as possible. He has a very good knowledge of putting moves together that work in succession. Won't go out of his way to do it but he'll do it as often as the oppurtunities present themselves. {3} Doesn't like to be overly showboating by nature. He'll play to the crowd a bit here or there and sometimes the arrogance in him will come out a bit in a match but for the most part he stays very focused in the ring and keeps on task. {1} Never, ever, ever gets in the face of, or complains at, the ref. He respects them and the hard job they have so he will never lay his hands on, or spend time arguing with, a ref. {2} Will not, for any reason, use his striking offense on a female. Refuses to hit a woman for any reason. {3} Will not stay in a submission so long that he gets injured. He isn't the kind of guy who is so arrogant to think that he is above taping out. If there is no way to reverse a hold, get to the ropes, or something to break him free in a reasonable amount of time he will tap out and avoid injury. Believes in the live to fight another day mentality. Entrance Description: 'Over and Under' by Egypt Central begins to play into the arena. There are no frills, flashing lights, pyro, or any of that. Just the music blaring as he makes his way down to the ring. He likes to give the fans five and such on the way down, even taking the time out to look around and play to the fans in whatever way he feels like on that particular evening. (The entrance is fairly sparce cause I plan on writing my own matches and will change the entrance, in terms of the minor stuff, from match to match.) Character Description: Head: Dark blue eyes. Short black hair. Think Ken Kennedy in style for the hair. Always has a goatee. Almost all the time has that Five O'Clock shadow thing going on for the rest (From the sideburns, which are always kept pretty short to the area around his goatee which he likes to keep neat and well groomed). Usually is in a good mood so you'll find a small or at least a happy look on his face. Isn't an overly brooding or angry person on a regular basis. Upper Body: Muscular frame that fits his body type. He isn't some freak of muscles or anything like that but has the right amount of muscle on his frame. Isn't overly cut up or anything like that. His body fat percent would be something between 12-14 percent at all times, so while he is in good shape, great wrestling shape even, he isn't all cut up, toned, and defined. Think Jericho, Edge, Carlito in this nature. Has a tattoo of his wifes name (Dawn) on his chest above his heart. Lower Body: Pretty much looking at the same thing here as with the upper body. Muscular lower body, thinking something like a Angle here due to his style in the ring, but isn't just ripping with tone or definition. Ring Attire: Black and Blue wrestling suit, the short kind (one piece deal like Curt Hening 'Mr. Perfect' wore). The blue should be a light to mid range blue that complements the black very well. The suit, the majority of it, is the blue color with the outlines of it (like the straps and what not) being black trimmed in white. He wears black wrist tape, both wrists, and Bike brand elbow bads that are black trimmed in the blue. On the bottom, or shorts part, of the suit are cool designs of some sort. He has knee pads on that are black trimmed in white. His boots are white trimmed in the blue and the black with black laces. Entrance Music 'Over and Under' by Egypt Central
