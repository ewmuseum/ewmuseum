WorldWide 141 Joe Louis Arena — Detroit, Michigan ---- WW141: Plans and Prices In his luxury suite, Triumph Frost sits on a couch in a salmon dress shirt and designer jeans, still sporting fresh bandages upon his face. His beautiful wife Alexis, in a white Dolce dress, comes over and hands him a cold beer. He cracks it open and takes a sip, and then rests it along his forehead. He takes in a deep breath and stares at the ceiling. Triumph Frost: What a goddamned stupid… She takes a seat next to him and pulls off the bandage from his forehead. Triumph Frost: What the hell are you doing now? Alexis quickly grows frustrated. Alexis Frost: I’ve done this before, you know. Triumph extends his arm, keeping her at bay. She finally relents. Triumph Frost: It’s just a goddamned scratch. He shakes his head as he takes a sip of his beer. Triumph Frost: I can take care of myself, you know. He takes a sip from his beer, and simply mutters under his breath… Triumph Frost: Fuckin’ Murray… He lets out a deep breath, and then holds up his thumb and index finger, now speaking to no one in particular. Triumph Frost: I was this…fuckin’…close… Alexis rubs away at his shoulder. Alexis Frost: It’s alright. You did good… Triumph shakes his head from side to side. Triumph Frost: Fuck good! I will crucify him… Triumph smashes his fist on the table. Triumph Frost: ...and I swear to fucking Christ, I will hammer in the nails myself! His words carry down the hall and are intercepted by a man whose ragged jeans come into view first. Triumph Frost: The fuck you want? Jay Terror: Heard you gave a cheap handy. He says, jokingly of course. Jay Terror: Could hear you down the hall. Gotta’ say, for a teenager, you got your head on straight. Triumph sports the grin to end all grins, and turns slightly to Alexis. Triumph Frost: Give us a few, will ya? Alexis nods and gets up off the couch. As she passes Terror, she gives him a weary eye, but he pays no attention to her. Triumph Frost: Have a seat, have a beer. Terror snatches a drink and clicks the tab open. Pulling his bandanna down, he takes a sip and points at Triumph. Jay Terror: You know, I was pulling for you out there. The look on Murray’s face would have been enough to retire on. Triumph grunts, and takes a swig. Triumph Frost: Fucker got lucky, is all. Triumph gazes off into the distant for a moment, as if miles away. Triumph Frost: But he'll get his... Terror looks into the same distance but can’t seem to find the same solace. Triumph Frost: Get the fuck outta here with that bullshit. Jay Terror: Trust me, I don’t need friends. ‘Sides, I don’t share my cigarettes as easy as this. He takes another gulp from the beer. Jay Terror: All I’m saying is you like to fuck with people. I like to fuck with people. Maybe we both end up fuckin’ with the same person. S’all. Jay laughs now. Jay Terror: Friends? That’s rich. Triumph nods. Triumph Frost: You know, you're alright for the fake thug bitch that you are. Triumph sips from his beer and leans back. Triumph Frost: So maybe I help you out. Maybe we break that bitch's legs. Maybe we put him down for good. Maybe. Triumph leans forward. Triumph Frost: Just don't go gettin' any fuckin' ideas on me. Jay’s cigarette is near the filter and he takes a smaller pull to save it. Jay Terror: I’m just dealing with what I’ve been given. Fact is, I could smash Murray with a bat on my own time. I could shatter his skull for all it’s worth. Figured you were like me though. Figured you wanted in on the action. Triumph takes a last puff and stubs the cig out on the table. Triumph Frost: You act like I need you for all this shit. Why not just cut out the middleman, take that prick out myself. He takes a sip from his beer. Triumph Frost: And say that I do . He’s shitting through his stomach in a bag for the rest of his life, you get to jerk off on that shiny belt of yours or whatever you do with it. The question becomes… His eyebrows raise up slightly. Triumph Frost: What would I get in return? Jay takes his stub and smokes it to the filters face before tossing it onto the floor. Jay Terror: I’m a powerful man these days. Jay leans forward in his chair. Jay Terror: Name your price. A sly grin crosses Triumph's face. Triumph Frost: I've got my plans. He finishes off his beer. Triumph Frost: Now, if we're done... Taking the hint, Jay grins and takes in the rest of his own beer. He stands and makes his way toward the door. Jay Terror: You know where to find me.
