Dorn Tracer Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames "The Archon", "The Ace" Hometown Washington D.C. Birthday November 18th 1978 Height 6'2'' Weight 240 lbs Biography This could probably take awhile. Short story: Dorn's the smack-addicted maniacal son of a former SWAT team member who also had a gambling problem and allegedly killed Dorn's mother. Dorn's brother Michael died of mysterious circumstances overseas, which sent Dorn spiraling into bouts of depression and drug use. He started hearing voices around age 18, and he believes it was the voice of the All-mother, En'Flarim the Golden Goddess. He began to research into the Occult and discovered a fanatical group of sun-worshippers who believed intergalactic deities were pulling the strings of the universe. Being a gifted athlete and natural showman, Dorn decided the best way to get their message out was by being the poster child of it, in front of as many people as possible. Wrestling gave him that venue, and it was in the smokey halls of the arenas that RUSH Wrestling resided in that his reign of terror began. It hit its apex in the defunct Primetime Championship Wrestling, where Dorn won many championships, including being its last World Champion. After PCW folded, Dorn disappeared... His presence was made known again some years later in DC, where he and a group of followers drove around to various churches, graveyards, and congregations in a giant black RV called Starship Tracer. They would picket the functions, calling for the death and doom of unbelievers and laughing at the misfortune of families. Their gang tactics tended to be violent as well, with Dorn himself injuring many people when they try to confront the group. He believes his calling is to bring about the death of the world, and will try to drag as many with him as possible. This is where the story begins, for all intents and purposes. Other Feds PCW, RUSH Other Fed Titles PCW World Heavyweight Champion x1, PCW Television Champion x1, PCW Rising Star Champion x1 Strengths A seasoned technician. High-flying tendencies. Uses brutal submissions and tactics to break an opponent's body and spirit. Very confident in his abilities. Clinically insane. Weaknesses Clinically insane. Arrogant. Maniacal. Has a tendency to underestimate his opposition. Not the strongest ox in the box. Wrestling Style Brutality, Submissionist, Technician Finisher Move The Zodiac Killer Finisher Description Pumphandle hoist into a sit-out Tombstone Piledriver Finisher Setup Move Kick to the gut, usually. However, if Dorn can get the opponent on his shoulder, chances are the Zodiac is coming out. Special Moves Shooting Star Press, HER RAAGGEEE!! (Takes a picket sign and blasts his opponent with it.) Other Moves Dorn is a seasoned veteran of the ring and a well-established technician. As the years have gone on, however, Dorn has figured out ways to just flat-out hurt people. You think that's an armbar he's locking on? May the Goddess have mercy and kill your children so they cannot suffer you any longer. He's kicking you in the face with one leg as the other crosses over and secures the hold. Nice STF, right? Woops, he's rolling over and presses his knee into the small of your back. Nice knowing you, die in a fire. Dorn will typically utilize brutal tactics, cheating, submission holds, and various drivers and suplexes. It's probably best not to let him get an advantage. Ring Entrances Dark, foreboding chants in an unknown language followed by "Requiem For A Tower". Dorn's followers come through the entrance first, wearing black linen robes. They split up and kneel on either side of the walkway as Dorn very slowly walks through them, his eyes never leaving the ring. He ascends the steel staircase, walks to the middle of the ring, and just faces the east part of the crowd. He then slowly raises his arms and his eyes to the sky in a welcoming gesture as a circle of flame suddenly starts forming on the ceiling. When the circle completes, Dorn lowers his arms, throws off the robe, and prepares for the opposition.
