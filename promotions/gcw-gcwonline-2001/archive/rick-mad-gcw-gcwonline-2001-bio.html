Rick Mad Biography Real Name Rick Mad Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Birthday 7/21/1973 Height 6'5 Weight 267 lbs Biography Rick Mad grew up in Philadelphia, an exciting city for a wrestling fan such as himself. ECW, based out of Philly, was starting to take off as Mad became an adult and moved away, but the promotion was what inspired him to become a professional wrestler. He moved to Canada and trained there, and later claimed to be trained by Stu Hart (although this was not the case). Following that he toured Mexico and Japan as often as possible before debuting in the United States. In professional wrestling, Rick found most of his success in a company called PCW. He won the Tag Team Titles with his partner Bob the Cameraman in a comedy duo known as the Wifebeaters, and later went on to win the PCW World Heavyweight Title. PCW closed years ago and Rick never quite made it like he did there. Now, Rick is looking to regain his former glory in the wrestling business. After several on-again-off-again stints in a company called WCF, he's looking to team with WCF's biggest star ever, Logan. Logan and Rick Mad have been rivals for as long as they've known each other and this is their first serious attempt at being a tag team. Other Feds TGW (Currently), WCF, PCW (Past) Strengths Rick's main strength is his experience in the ring. He's been wrestling for quite a while and knows all the rules and how to use them to his advantage. Weaknesses Rick's most obvious weakness is his age. His body isn't what it used to be. Wrestling Style Technical Strategy Rick will try to wear his opponents down as much as possible. Lots of pinfall attempts and submission holds. Midway through the match he'll start with the high impact moves, and only go for high flying at the end if he doesn't have any other options.
