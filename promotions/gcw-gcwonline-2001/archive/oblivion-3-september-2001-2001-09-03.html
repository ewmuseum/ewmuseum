Oblivion, 3 September 2001

3 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
                   

(The GCW logo flashes over the screen, and pyro explodes in the arena.  The crowd jump to their feet, cheering as Oblivion begins.)

 

(Lance Star’s music hits, and the newcomer heads to the ring.  He struts as he steps through the ropes.  Next up, Jnightmare emerges from the back, and he is booed all the way to the ring.  He climbs in, and the match starts.)

 

Bryan- And there’s the bell.

 

Yale- There are two tough competitors in the ring tonight.  I mean you got Lance Star and JNightmare.  This will be one hell of a match-up.

 

Bryan- Yes it will…We got a rookie debut here as Lance start stares down JNightmare in the ring.

 

Yale- Lance Star looks like he will be a rising star soon to come in the GCW.  I mean he looks like he knows so much.

 

Bryan- Watch out.  This fight is now boiling in.  JNightmare throws some lefts at Star.  But Star is coming back with lefts of his own.

 

Yale- JNightmare is another rookie in the GCW.  He will prove his points here.  He is ok but I don’t like him.

 

Bryan- Yale you bearly like anyone!

 

Yale- So, your point being?

 

Bryan- Nevermind. Oh god…Lance Star just did a spinning heel kick that immediately takes down JNightmare.

 

Yale- JNightmare, quick to his feet and is grabbed and sent into the ropes by Star.

 

Bryan- JNightmare on his way back now, and is hit with a hellacious clothesline.

 

Yale- That was perfectly executed.

 

Bryan- As if there was any other way to execute that move!

 

Yale- Shut up Bryan!  There goes Star kicking into the right side of JNightmare’s ribs.  Star is punishing JNightmare here in his GCW debut.

 

Bryan- He is giving a lot here.  Star picks up JNightmare and sends him to the turnbuckle.  Star charges as JNightmare lifts himself up and rolls over Lance Star and pins him.

 

(Ref counts……One…..Two…..)

 

Bryan- Kickout!  Wow we almost had a big upset there.

 

Yale- That almost went down in the books as the shortest match in GCW history.

 

Bryan- No Yale.  There was that time Mike Hardy rolled up Chris Jordan with the school boy to get an easy three count for the Cruiserweight Belt.

 

Yale- Bryan just shut up.  I was being sarcastic.

 

Bryan- Well anyway.  Back to the action in the ring.  Lance Star picks up JNightmare into a scoop slam.

 

Yale- Star is climbing uptop.  Wait!  What is this!  A Corkscrew Legdrop.  But JNightmare moves out of the way.

 

Bryan- If Star had connected there this thing would have been over!

 

Yale- But he didn’t so this match continues.  JNightmare has control now as he scoops up Star and sends him hard into the turnbuckle.  That had to hurt.

 

Bryan- JNightmare is going to take control of this bad situation for Star.  JNightmare picks Star up above his head and OUCH!  Gorilla press slam!  That has to hurt.  He just dropped Star right on his face!

 

Yale- This is an action packed match!  JNightmare walks over to the feet of Star and grabs one leg.

 

Bryan- ONE LEG BOSTON CRAB!!!  We haven’t seen that move in a long time Yale.

 

Yale- Star is yelping in pain as he is reaching for the ropes.

 

Bryan- That is a very painful hold.  Star is really close to reaching the ropes now.  He is within an arms reach.

 

Yale- Star is inching closer and closer and yes!  Star makes it to the ropes.  JNightmare needs to break the hold.

 

Bryan- Wait…..JNightmare won’t break the hold.  Referee Andrews is in there to try and pry JNightmare off.  Referee Andrew finally gets JNightmare off Star.  Star is clinching his back in pain now.  JNightmare lifts Star to his feet.  He bring Star over to the ropes and places him down on the top rope.  JNightmare climbs up next to Star.

 

Yale- What’s he gonna do?!  OUCH!!!  A big time hurricanrana!  Star is out…JNightmare with the pin.

 

Bryan- Ref counts…….One…….Two……Thr…..NO!

 

Yale- Star was bearly able to kick out of that one.  That was a two and a half.

 

Bryan- You have got to give Star a lot of credit.  This rookie has a lot of spunk.  I know many veterans in this business that are still unable to kickout of a hurricanrana.  JNightmare is picking Star back up again and sends him into the ropes.

 

Yale- Star heading back now and he ducks the clothesline!  JNightmare is stunned as he turns around.  DDT!

 

Bryan- Star just nailed JNightmare with a DDT.  It was a DDT with authority!  And just like that Star is back in control.

 

Yale- Star heads uptop and look out!  Frog Splash!  Star with a quick cover on JNightmare.  Ref counts…….One…….Two……No.  JNightmare was able to kickout of that one.

 

Bryan- What a great match.  Star picks JNightmare to his feet and charges off the ropes.  Star coming back now.  He jumps and catches JNightmare with a flying head scissors!

 

Yale- Outstanding move by Star.  JNightmare gets to his feet.  Star is standing there taunting the crowd.  But wait!  Low blow by JNightmare.  Star is holding himself in pain.

 

Bryan- JNightmare charges to the ropes.  Both men jump at each other with a double crossbody!!!  Both men are down now.  Ref is counting to ten.  One man better make it up before ten or this thing is over.

 

Andrews-  One…….Two……Three……Four…..

 

Yale- I doubt either men have it in them to get up.  This has been a hard match.

 

Andrews- Five……..Six…….Seven……

 

Bryan- Wait both men are starting to move now.

 

Andrews- Eight…….Nine…..

 

Bryans- And both men are up!  That was close.

 

Yale- But they start off where they left off.  JNightmare with a left.  Now Star…..now JNightmare.  Each men are exchanging blows as Star gets the better of JNightmare.  JNightmare is reeling back into the ropes now as Lance Star jumps with a spinning heel kick that sends JNightmare right over the top to the outside!  Outstanding!

 

Bryan- I think this match is just about over.  Ref counts.

 

Andrews- One…….Two…..Three….Four…..Five

 

Yale- But Star breaks up the count and heads outside after JNightmare!  The ref follows Star outside as Star rolls JNightmare back into the ring.  Star rolls back into the ring.  The ref hops back in.  Star picks JNightmare up to his feet and send him for a ride towards the turnbuckle.

 

Bryan- But Wait!!!  The ref was in the way and now the ref is down and out.  JNightmare is stunned in the corner as Star charges with a Spear!  A spear to the gut of JNightmare!!!

 

Yale- JNightmare falls to the ground as Star heads outside.  He goes over to the time keeper’s table and snags the ring bell!!!  What’s he gonna do with that Bryan!!!

 

Bryan- My bet would be that he hits JNightmare in the head with it.

 

Yale- Star heads back into the ring and waits upon JNightmare to rise.

 

Byran- JNightmare is up but he is in for a Nightmare of his own!  JNightmare turns and ducks the bell shot!!!  Wait low blow again to Star.

 

Yale- Star drops the bell as JNightmare retrieves it and bashes it over the skull of Lance Star.  Star is down and out.  JNightmare disposes of the bell and covers Star.

 

Bryan- The ref is really slow to recover.  But surely enough he does and counts.

 

Yale- One……………..Two………….Thre….NOOOOO!!!!  Star kicked out of that!  And JNightmare can’t believe it!

 

Bryan- JNightmare is walking over to the ref and starts arguing the count.  But in the mean time Star is recovering.  Star is up.  JNightmare is still arguing the ref, he doesn’t even know Star is up.  JNightmare turns around and OUCH!!!

 

Yale- A Tilt-a-whorl backbreaker!!!  JNightmare is down.

 

Bryan- Star climbs to the top.  He is slow to his climbing but makes it.  Star taunts the crowd as he jumps!

 

Yale- OH MY GOD!!!  CORKSCREW LEGDROP!!!  IT’S ALL OVER NOW!!!

Bryan- OUT STANDING MOVE BY STAR!  Star with the pin!

 

Yale- One…….Two…….Three!!!!!   He got him!!!  It’s all over!!!  What a match that was.

 

(Lance Star’s Music hits again as Lance Star jumps on the turnbuckles and lifts his arms in victory.)

 

Bryan- Lance Star gave a hell of a fight and he is able to walk out of Oblivion victorious!  How about that, Lance Star’s debut in the GCW and he walks out with a victory over JNightmare.  This will be put in the books folks.

 

Yale- Star is an outstanding wrestler!

 

(The crowd gives Lance Star a standing Ovation.)

 

Bryan- Lance Star with a well deserved standing Ovation from this crowd.  What a great match.  Yet we still have more to come on GCW’s Oblivion!  Stay tuned.

 

(Commericials hit.)

 

{ In Bloom hits as Jeremy Caide and Jennifer walk out to the ramp. The fans in the arena give Jeremy a decent reaction as mostly cheers are heard. They walk to the end of the ramp and Jeremy gets in the ring. Jeremy awaits LeStatt Knight.}

 

Yale: New comer Jeremy Caide will take on Lestatt Knight one on one.

 

Bryan: Lestatt has proven his ability several times and Caide looks like a promising athlete.

 

{ "More Pain" hits as the lights dim. The dark shadow of LeStatt Knight walks out. He slowly walks to the ring. He hops to the apron and overlooks the fans. }

 

Yale: Jeremy from behind, sends Knight to the floor.

 

Bryan: What aggression by the new comer in the first few seconds!

 

Yale: Both men on the out side. Knight with an eye gouge. Caide staggers back and Knight with a vicious clothes line on the ramp!

 

Bryan: The back of Caide's head just bounced off the steel ramp. Knight now on the attack as he lifts Jeremy up and drops him chest first on the guardrail.

 

Yale: Knight grabs the hair of Caide and drags him to the ring. Knight hops in the ring and stomps away.

 

Bryan: Caide trying to escape back to the corner but is met with a forearm to the back of the head.

 

Yale: It looks like Knight is going to put Jeremy on the top. Knight going for a superplex but can't lock on. Jeremy fights back and pushes Knight off.  Jeremy jumps and connects with a flying clothes line!

 

Bryan: This might do it! 1..2..NO!

 

Yale: Can't keep him down!

 

Bryan: Jeremy back up and hits a swinging neck breaker on LeStatt. Jeremy jogs to the ropes and drops a knee right on the forehead of Knight.

 

Yale: Knight slow to get up but Jeremy with an ax handle to the small of LeStatt's back. Both men back up and Jeremy whips Knight into the ropes, Knight comes flying back missing a clothesline and gets hit with a knee to the ribs.

 

Bryan: Caide runs into the ropes and knees Knight in the head!

 

Yale: That's got to hurt!

 

Bryan: Great offense by the new comer!

 

Yale: Knight on his knees. OHH! LOW BLOW!

 

Bryan: Wake up ref! We need to start giving eye exams.

 

Yale: We do, maybe this one forgot to put in his contacts. Anyways, Knight is now in control. He grabs Caide and lifts him up into a suplex.

 

Bryan: Knight stomps away at Jeremy. Knight picks him up and plants him with a DDT!

 

Yale: Knight going to the top! Jeremy not moving and Knight takes off hitting A HUGE SPLASH FROM THE TOP!!!!

 

Bryan: This might be it! 1...2...3NO! NO! NO! The rookie will not stay down!

 

Yale: You can see by the expression on Lestatt's face that he thought it was over! Knight back up and it looks like he is going to put him on the top rope again.

 

Bryan: Jeremy still trying to fight back but Knight stops him with knife edge chops to the chest. Knight sets him up on the top and sets him up for a superplex! Jeremy trying to fight it, but Knight punches Caide in the ribs and lifts Jeremy up and drops him down with a HUGE SUPERPLEX!!

 

Yale: KNIGHT FLOATS OVER! 1....2...3NO!

 

Bryan: The rookie kicked out again!

 

Yale: Knight isn't happy with that count. He pushes the ref into the corner and Caide sneaks up and rolls him up!

 

Bryan: 1..2..NO! Couldn't hold him down!

 

Yale: Jeremy kicks Knight in the mid section and tries to lift Knight up but he reveres!

 

Bryan: Caide lands on his feet! He turns around and there's the Knightfall!

 

Yale: It's over! 1....2....3!!! LeStatt Knight picks up the win!

 

(We fade to commercial.)

 

{ I Disiapear blasts on the sound system as Mike Hardy steps through the curtain. The fans go crazy as the Cruiserweight Champion walks to the ring }

 

Andrews this match is scheduled for one fall and is for the GCW Cruiserweight Championship! Introducing first from Cameron North Carolina! Representing System-X! Mike "Hickenbottom" Hardy!

 

Yale: This is Hardy’s first defense and it looks to be a good challenge against young Chris Edge.

 

Bryan: Edge is the new kid on the block and he has a lot to prove tonight.

 

{ Hardy gets in the ring and holds the title towards the fans. Edge’s music hits as Edge walks out to a mixed reaction. He smiles and runs to the ring. He walks around Hardy and shows off to the fans }

 

Yale: And we are under way! Hardy and Edge lock up, Hardy with a reverse chin lock. Edge tries to break free but Hardy won’t let go. Edge elbow Hardy, and runs into the ropes.

 

Bryan: A big cross body from the challenger and we have a cover! 1..No note even a two count.

 

Yale: Both men back to there feet, Hardy fires with right hands to the face of the rookie! Edge back into the corner and Hardy is really going after him. Edge fights back but Hardy with a drop toe hold and Edge is down.

 

Bryan: Hardy sprints to the ropes and connects with a huge close line! Hardy with the cover! 1....2.No! Only a Two count. Hardy grabs Edge and what a snap suplex! Hardy now in full control. Hardy looks to the fans! He could be, yes he’s going to the top!

 

Yale: This is where Hardy does what he does best, and that’s fly!

 

Bryan: Hardy perched on the top rope waiting for Chris to stand, Hardy jumps and tries a hurricanranna but Edge blocks and drops him to the mat!

 

Yale: Great move by the rookie! Power bombing Hardy straight into the mat!

 

Bryan: Both men are down and Edge is the first to get up. He drops kicks Hardy, and Mike falls between the ropes.

 

Yale: They are right in front of us now, and Edge looks to be in control. Edge whips Hardy into the railing and it looks like it hurt Hardy’s back!

 

Bryan: We all know the problems Hardy has had with his back and that looked like it didn’t help!

 

Yale: Chris Edge back on the attack and he fires chops to Hardy’s chest.

 

{ Whoo’s from the crowd as the smacks of Hardy’s chest echo’s in the arena }

 

Bryan: Edge climbs back in the ring and goes to the top rope! We could see some really higgh flying now!

 

Yale: Hardy stands and Edge flies with a cross body right on Mike Hardy!

 

Bryan: Both men look to be hurt. I think Hardy got the most of that one.

 

Yale: Chris slow to get up but grabs the apron and slides in the ring. Hardy is just not moving out there! Chris goes back to the out side and picks up Hardy. He whipes him into the guardrail but Hardy reveres and clothes lines Edge to the concrete!

 

Bryan: WOW! I never though Hardy had that left in him! Hardy back up and what determination from the champ! Hardy runs to the guardrail and moonsaults hitting Edge!

 

Yale: That had to hurt Hardy’s ribs again! He just can’t risk his life like this!

 

Bryan: Death is the only way out! Hardy struggles to stand. Edge is having a second wind and is back on the attack! He fires right hands and tosses Hardy back into the ring.

 

Yale: Nice back breaker by the rookie! Edge with a standing splash. We have a cover! 1.....2....NO! Hardy kicks out again! He won’t quit Bryan!

 

Bryan: That’s right! Edge pulls Hardy up but Hardy counters with a jaw breaker.

 

Yale: Nice move by the Champ! Hardy and Edge both slow to get up but the fans are behind Hardy one hundred percent!

 

Bryan: Hardy now back up and on the prowl! He fires with chops, and right hands! Hardy has Edge reeling! Hardy with a kick to the mid section and a perfect DDT!

 

Yale: This could be it! 1...2.. 3NO! No! No! The youngster is still in this one!

 

Bryan: You can tell that Hardy though that was it. He looks determined to get the job done now! Hardy stands Chris back up and there’s the Bottom feeder! Edge should be out now!

 

Yale: Hardy takes off his shirt and the fans go nuts! It’s time for the swantonbomb!

 

Bryan: But wait! Is that The Great Tiger coming to ring side?

 

Yale: Oh good lord he has a Chair! No! Don’t do it!

 

Bryan: Great Tiger just clocked Hardy square in the back! The ref was checking on Edge! He didn’t see it!

 

Yale: Edge slowly gets up and crawls towards Hardy who is laying on the mat! He covers!

 

Bryan: No not like this! 1...2...3!!! EDGE WINS! EDGE WINS!

 

Yale: The Great Tiger gave Edge the win! Chris Edge is the new champ!

 

Bryan: That chair shot to Hardy’s back was incredible! Hardy might be injured! Wait a second! Great Tiger climbs to the ring as Edge walks out with the Cruiserweight title.

 

Yale: The damage has been done! Tiger picks up Hardy and give him a Tiger Driver!

 

Bryan: Some one call security! Hardy’s back can not possibly take much more damage!

 

Yale: Tiger kicks at Hardy’s back!

 

Bryan: Stop already! Tiger has gotten his point across!

 

Yale: Here we go! The System is in the house! Toad runs in and Tiger escapes through the crowd as Hanson runs to get him. Toad checks on Hardy.

 

Bryan: Hanson goes to help Toad. They both grab a arm and help Hardy to the backstage.

 

Yale: Hardy might be hurt, that chair shot was terrible!

 

Bryan-  Fans, we’re out of time from Oblivion.  Join us this Thursday for Meltdown!
