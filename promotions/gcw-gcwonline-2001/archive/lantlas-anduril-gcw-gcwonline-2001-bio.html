Lantlas Anduril Biography Real Name Lantlas Evenstar Musada Durin Diee Anduril Nicknames Lanty, Elf-Boy, Moon Hometown Munich, Germany Birthday Unknown Height 6'7 Weight 240 Biography "The Elven Warrior" has left his mark in the wrestling industry everywhere in which he has ventured. Sporting long flowing blue hair, carrying a cast of weapons ranging from flails to longswords, and accompanied by his short fiery Romanian escort Devon Drake, he certainly needs few words to make his presence known, and usually follows as such. Lantlas trained in a rigorous military base in Munich, Germany with his late brother Neovan. Coming to the American scene around the turn of the century, he did not get discovered until being signed by Joey Cranston of HHW in 2003. His several years of tenure there climaxed with a world championship victory over tyrannical owner Joey Cranston. He also held the tag team championships a federation record 11 times. Usually noted as the prime of his career, he was signed by Luis Malave of Pure Class Wrestling shortly after HHW fell after eight long years. After enduring a brutal war of words with previously unbeatable Ace Anderson, Lantlas ventured a slow journey from the bottom of the card to the main event, capturing the championship from Ace Anderson after a year of nearly untouchable title defenses. Winning the championship, along with the tag team title and the International title through a title-for-title match, went on to post a record of 45-5. His tenure in PCW is most remembered for becoming acquainted with the beauty of PCW known as Emily "Jade" DuPrae. Eventually becoming engaged to the brunette knockout, Lantlas, Jade, and Emerald traveled the road together, and anytime someone in the business went anywhere near Jade, they wouldn't have to wait for the blue-haired warrior for long. Lantlas's career since the decline of PCW has been erratic, with stints in Global, an exceptional run in Encore before the federation's short and untimely demise, and an undefeated run in A-RUSH wrestling. Most recently and successfully, he entered the world of EPW. Becoming the Soul Survivor and winning the EPW championship, he sported an undefeated 14-0-2 record, and when EPW closed, he was still holding the championship gold. Currently a free agent, he looks for an escape from reality.
