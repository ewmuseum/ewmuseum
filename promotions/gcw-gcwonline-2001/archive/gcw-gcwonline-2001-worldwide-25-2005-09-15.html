Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #25 – Vancouver, British Columbia

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Vancouver crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “TREY GOT ROBBED” and “Pete Sucks!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Vancouver, British Columbia” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: With another capacity crowd on hand, Global Championship Wrestling invades Canada for WorldWide! Hello, sports fans, I’m James Bryan back with you again at ringside with The Jhub and the legendary Pete, and guys, we have another huge show on tap tonight. We’re live in the sold out General Motors Place for another huge show as we prepare for FallOut in just two weeks!

Jhub: And it’s going to be a huge one, JB! LeStatt Knight versus Michael Stevens in a submission match for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! A grudge match of epic proportions between Mike Hardy and Octavian! The Marauder gets a TV title shot against Mike Savige! And that’s just the beginning! We’re under the impression that more matches will be announced this evening.

Pete: And we’re going to see LeStatt and Stevens back in the same ring together for the first time since Annihilation! After what happened in that match, you know sparks are going to fly in this evening’s main event!

Bryan: That’s going to be a huge one, folks, and we’ve got a United States Title Match, too! Johnny Borealis has his first defense here tonight against Mike Hardy, and guys, we might just see a new champion here tonight!

Jhub: Normally I wouldn’t agree with you, because Borealis has really stepped up his game, but Hardy is due, guys. Hardy is due for a big win, he’s due to make a big impact, and if he can keep Octavian in the back of his mind and focus on Johnny B, he could very well walk out of here the new GCW United States Champion.

Pete: And don’t forget about the second round of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament! Aiden Jackson goes one on one with Coyle O’Conner for the right to go to FallOut and compete in the finals for the GCW Cruiserweight Title! That’s going to be a hell of a fight, guys! And don’t forget, next week Rei Tiu goes at it with Zodiac for the last finals spot.

Bryan: That tournament is really heating up, and we’re going to advance one and say goodbye to another here tonight. But for now, fans, let’s head to the ring!

Three’s Company, Five’s a Crowd -by Jamie and Lokie

Regulators theme hits the speakers hard, as the arena erupts in a loud roar. Blake Cassidy along with Warz appear at the top of the ramp and begin making their way down the ring.

Bryan: Well you can't blame the Regulators for storming to the ring like this.

Jhub: You're right there. But, you can't blame Ryan Stephens for what he did either. He did what he had to do to keep those tag titles.

Bryan: It was dirty...and you know it!

Standing next to Warz in the ring, Cassidy is tossed a microphone.

Cassidy: First of all, I'd like to point out this cut over my eye. Thanks to Jake Summers and one of the tag titles I can't continue my eyebrow exercises every morning!

Warz quickly snatches the microphone out of Blake's hand.

Warz: We're here tonight for a reason. Not to complain about what happened. We're here to call out Jake Summers and Ryan Stephens!! We're calling you out...cause we DESERVE A GOD DAMN REMATCH!!!

The crowd yells with a mixed reaction.

Warz: We had the match won! It was over! Those titles should be around our waists!

Cassidy: Yeah, we should be on the new back to school lunch pales!

Somehow Blake Cassidy managed to obtain another microphone.

Cassidy: We should be doing the photo shoots with the tag team title belts in different poses! We should have the new action figure toys that come with miniature belts...

Warz: Enough! Summers...Stephens....you owe us a rematch at Fallout!!!!

The remix version of "I Am" by Dale Oliver begins blasting throughout the arena. Gold pyrotechnics erupt down the entrance ramp as "Golden Boy" Jake Summers emerges from the darkness below the GCW Mega-Tron. Dressed in Levis and a long sleeve white TAPOUT t-shirt, he raises both fists confidently into the air and begins to make his way towards the ring. As he makes his way down the aisle, he is met with cheers, but yet some are booing. He finally enters the ring and calls for a microphone.

Summers: You guys may have had that and you very well could have had this.

Summers pats the GCW World Tag-Team Title draped across his shoulder.

Summers: But face it in this business should of, could have, or would of amount to jack shit! Simply stated you either get the job done or in your cases you don't. Now given the fact the big guy here is about to foam at the mouth, I realize you two are a tad upset over what took place last week, but you need to get your story straight. I didn't hit anyone with a damn title belt, but I guess since I'm Stephens' partner I'm being accused as well.

Cassidy and Warz nod in agreement.

Summers: As for your eyebrows exercises, I think the crowd can agree with me when I say, you needed to shave that damn uni-brow anyhow.

The crowd laughs along as Jake chuckles slightly. Meanwhile Warz tries to calm Cassidy who is stomping about.

Summers: Seriously though guys, there's nothing I can do to change things. What's done is done. I don't have the power to go back and reverse the decision. The only thing I can do is say I'm sorry and apologize for what took place.

Warz pushes Cassidy aside and steps almost nose to nose to Jake.

Warz: You know that's bullshit.

Summers: Speaking of crap. Dude you need to take a step back. Two words! Mouth Wash!

Summers takes a step back and waves his hand in front of his face fanning Warz's breathe away.

Summers: Like I said, I'm very sorry that Stephens hit both of you with a title belt, but he's his own man. I can't control his actions.

Cassidy: Sorry doesn't cut it pal! You can give us a rematch! That's the least you can do.

Summers: We are a tag team, I just go can't make a decision unless we both agree upon it. And well, as you can see he's here at the moment.

Warz: He didn't confer with you when he knocked the hell out of us. Why should you have to confer with him? It's an easy decision. Quit avoiding the situation. Either give us a rematch or....

Suddenly "Wherever I May Roam" begins blasting over the PA system and The Guild make their way to the ring.

Bryan: With all five men out here, things could get interesting.

As The Guild enter the ring, Summers, Cassidy, and Warz stare them up and down.

Cassidy: Why are you guys out here? This doesn't concern you.

The Rogue Paladin calls for another microphone.

Paladin: You two had your chance and like Summers said you failed. It's The Guild time to shine!

Cassidy: Like you guys shined against us two weeks ago? Need I remind you that you lost the number one contenders match?

Cassidy smirks.

Meatwrecker: Yeah you guys won, but what good did it do you? You crashed and burned when you got your chance.

Paladin: My partner is right, if anyone deserves a title shot at Fallout its The Guild!

Warz: Once a loser always a loser.

Meatwrecker: You choke artists should know something about that.

As The Guild and The Regulators continue to argue, Jake takes the opportunity to get out of the ring. Meanwhile the four men in the ring turn towards him.

Paladin: So what do you say Summers, give us a title match at Fallout!

Cassidy: We deserve a rematch.

Summers: I'll think about it!

Bryan: Looks like The Guild and The Regulators prying has worked somewhat on Summers

Jhub: Yeah the man has cracked some. Thinking about it is better than a flat out no!

Old Friends -by Phyl & Jeff

We open up on Michael Stevens finishing up lacing his boots. The crowd explodes with cheers as he appears on the screen. He stands, and takes a sip of water from the bottle sitting on the table in front of him. He sets it back down, and starts doing some basic stretches, a little warm up before his match later. He takes a couple hops to continue loosening up, when there’s a knock on the door. Not looking back he just shakes his head.

Michael Stevens: Yeah?

The door opens as Stevens glances back over his shoulder. The figure of Ricky Thunder walks into the room, and the crowd gives the new recruit a modest pop. Stevens glances over his shoulder at the sound of the door, and a huge smile breaks out across his face. Turning around and walking forward Stevens extends his hand, the two men shake and do the one arm man-hug-shake.

Michael Stevens: Holy hell, Ricky Thunder as I live and breathe. I haven’t seen you in years man.

The two both take a step back immediately in silent agreement that they’d been too close together too long. Long live the unspoken rules of male contact.

Ricky Thunder: Yeah, it’s been a while, how you doing?

Michael Stevens: Ugh, I don’t even want to think about it brutha, what about you? I’d heard Caldera signed a Ricky Thunder, but I thought you’d retired, figured it was some other asshole stealing your name.

Ricky Thunder: Nah, it’s me, couldn’t stay away. I’ve been ok, hanging out on the beach a lot.

Michael Stevens: Same old Ricky. Good to have you here man. Still having the dreams?

Ricky gets a little bit of a “deer in the headlights” look on his face, and shakes his head.

Ricky Thunder: Yeah…

Michael Stevens: Sucks, so you ready for your debut tonight? I’ve heard some good things about that guy from Lights Out.

Ricky Thunder: Oh damn, they turned off his power too, poor bastard. I should ask if he wants a loan or something.

Michael Stevens: Hehe, DEFINITELY same old Ricky. Listen bro, I’d love to love to chat, but I’ve got to get ready for my match, and you should probably be getting ready yourself.

Ricky Thunder: Nah, I’ll be fine….WAIT! What the hell is that?

Ricky points to the bottle of water on the table that Stevens had previously been drinking from.

Michael Stevens: Uh, a bottle of water.

Ricky Thunder: Oh man, how in the hell can you drink that, seriously? Dude I only drink the greatest water on the planet, I’ll have my people call your people and they’ll send you some.

Michael Stevens: Ricky, since when do you have “people”?

Ricky Thunder: Well not really people, but I will send you some then!

Michael Stevens: Hehe, whatever Ricky, but like I said man…

Ricky Thunder: DUDE! You got a mirror in your dressing room, this is SOO much nicer than mine. Hey what’s that over there?

Ricky begins walking around the room looking at everything and pointing them out to Stevens. The Spitfire shakes his head sadly obviously realizing he’s not going to get Ricky out anytime soon, as the scene fades away.

Singles Match – One Fall

Gabriel White vs. X -by John

Both men have entered the ring. X explodes towards White, taking him down to the mat with a surprise football tackle. He begins raining short range punches down on White’s face. A quick reversal and White takes the advantage. He attempts to throw rights, lefts, and forearms down to X’s face from the mount position. X puts his arms up, blocking the blows, and rolls White back over. X stands up, moving away from White and trying to gain his composure.

Bryan: Both men looking to gain a quick advantage here.

Pete: X being the smaller man needs all the help he can get.

Ms. Scabbia says something to X, which receives a nod in return as Gabriel White gets to his feet.

X rushes White from behind, and connects with a dropkick to the back while Gabriel has his back turned. White flies back towards the ropes, bouncing off. X lunges, looking for a spear, but White ducks, turns and grabs X from behind… White spins X, follows with a boot to the gut and a DDT to put X on the mat.

Bryan: The newcomer, Gabriel White, with a spot on DDT to get the momentum going in his debut match.

Jhub: It won’t last long.

Pete: You’re always so hard on the new guys.

Jhub: Because they haven’t proven themselves yet.

White drops an elbow, landing right on X’s temple. A second attempt misses, leaving White grabbing his arm.

Both men are up to their feet, but X is the first to strike. He goes low with a dropkick, connecting with White’s right kneecap. White drops to one knee. X follows up with a dropkick to the face, putting Gabriel White flat on his back. The enigma, looking to soften up the knee, places a target right back on White’s right knee. He grabs the leg and drops an elbow right into the knee. X then wrenches back, looking to rip the knee from the socket.

Pete: That looks like it hurts.

Jhub: Nah, you think?

Bryan: Nevertheless, X has been looking pretty impressive lately.

White begins smacking the mat in both pain and frustration. He reaches forward, gripping the enigma around the face, as Ms. Scabbia encourages X to pull harder. A single right hand only causes X to pull even harder on White’s knee. White again strikes at X’s head, causing X to wrench the knee further and further back. Only when White slams both of his fists, full force, into X’s temples does he release the hold.

Bryan: Wow, now that is a hold breaker right there.

Pete: Ring a ding ding ding dong.

Jhub just sighs.

White hobbles to his feet, as X pulls himself up using the ropes, holding his head the entire way.

White attempts a lariat, only to have X duck at the last second. X hits the ropes, ducks a second clothesline attempt, spins, and again dropkicks White’s right knee. White falls to his face. X immediately grabs the downed White’s foot, lifts his leg high into the air, and slams him, knee first, into the mat.

X holds onto the leg, this time choosing to apply a figure four leg lock.

White is in no position to grab the ropes. He tries sitting up to alleviate the stress. X punches White in the face, sending him back to the mat. His shoulders touch.

Referee counts.

1…

Bryan: Close call for White right there.

White picks his shoulders up. X leans back, placing even more pressure on White’s already tender knee. He again falls to the mat.

1…

2…

Bryan: Another close call, and X seems to have this match in control.

White sits up. Realizing he has no other alternative… he begins rocking his body back and forth, trying to flip X over onto his stomach and reversing the pressure. He rocks once, twice, three times… turning X over successfully on the third flip. X; however, was ready. He uses White’s own momentum to continue spinning, leaving the newcomer in the same submission predicament.

Only this time… White can grab the ropes.

Bryan: Gabriel White grabs the ropes, finally putting an end to the submission by X.

The ref forces X to break the hold. X refuses to argue. He pulls White to his feet and Irish-whips him to the corner. White stumbles, falling on his face before he hit the other side of the ring. His knee is hurt. X pulls him off the mat and places him on the top rope. He wants to hurt White, and figures a superplex is one of the best ways to go about it.

X climbs to the very top, looking to take White a good ten or twelve feet down to the mat. He pulls back, but White blocks. A shot to the gut stuns White, and X again looks for the superplex. Again… White blocks. This time, he uses X’s own momentum against him. Still seated on the top turnbuckle, White holds X high in the air, as if to hit a vertical suplex to the floor. Unwilling to kill himself just to get the upper hand… White dumps X right on his head on the canvas. White stands, takes a second to gain his balance… and leaps.

Bryan: He’s too big to do…

Pete: I don’t think he cares right now.

He drops an elbow square on the chest of X, causing X to yell out in pain.

Bryan: What a move by Gabriel White. That could have turned the tide in his favor!

White groans in pain, climbing back into the ring. The official begins his ten count on X, who remains motionless.

Bryan: And it looks like Gabriel White is going to win this one by countout! What an impressive victory!

White gets to his feet, smiling and shouting “HE HAS BEEN SAVED!” The crowd, uncertain about this, offers him a mixed reaction as the count reaches five. X begins to stir, but shows no signs of being aware of what to do.

Pete: This was a valiant effort by X, but White is going to pick up a great debut victory here! Not a bad match!

Jhub: White may drive me nuts outside the ring, and I may want to stick a screwdriver in my temple when the man talks, but he showed us that he’s a great athlete!

X suddenly begins to rise with more authority, as the count reaches 8! White, seeing this, moves to the ropes, but the official stops his count and backs X away. The moment the official turns his back, a figure springs over the guardrail! X places his hand on the bottom rope to drag himself in, but Coyle O’Conner appears wielding a steel chair! Coyle swings for the fences and nails X in the spine, dropping him back to the floor! The official turns, only in time to see O’Conner’s back as he races back through the crowd!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! Coyle just ambushed X!

The official counts nine… and ten! The bell sounds, and White shrugs, raising his arms in satisfaction. His music cues up again, and the crowd cheers for Coyle as he races out.

Andrews: Here is your winner, Gabriel White!

Bryan: A big debut win for Gabriel White here tonight, but give the assist to Coyle O’Conner!

Jhub: Think this was a preemptive strike from Coyle? He wants to send X a message to stay out of his Cruiserweight Title business!

Bryan: Absolutely. Well, a big win for White here, and-

White turns to leave, when suddenly the arena goes dark. A red light begins to glow on the ring, and White freezes, spinning rapidly. An eerie chanting begins rising out of the speakers, and suddenly a wave of dark cloaked figures appears around the ring from out of nowhere!

Bryan: Wait a minute… What the hell is going on here!? The ring has been surrounded!

Pete: Where the hell did these guys come from?! They’ve got Gabriel White pinned in the ring!

Jhub: I think White’s the one that needs to be saved right about now!

The black cloaked figures, totaling twelve in all, remain circled around the ring. Finally, one black figure, much larger than the rest, throws back his hood, revealing a familiar dark face…

Bryan: Bryan Mayhem! Oh my God, Mayhem has come for White!

Jhub: You never should have picked on this man, Gabe!

The crowd remains hushed as Mayhem speaks, his voice booming over the sound system.

Mayhem: Take a look around you. My minions surround you. You think you can bring the light to GCW? I am the one who brings the darkness. You think you can block us out? Think again. At FallOut, Gabriel White, you will find out that the darkness can never be defeated. My minions will always be watching you. I suggest you leave here and never return. Because if we find you at FallOut… you may not live to preach again.

With that, Mayhem lets out a terrifying laugh, and the arena is plunged into darkness again. The laugh echoes for a few moments, until slowly the lights return. White stands alone in the ring, and the minions have disappeared. The black figures are nowhere to be seen, leaving only a startled White to stagger to the back.

Bryan: Oh God, that was creepy! Gabriel White said he’s come for GCW’s salvation, but it looks like Bryan Mayhem doesn’t want it to be saved!

Jhub: That guy gives me the willies, JB.

Pete: Yeah, that Mayhem is one crazy dude.

Jhub: No, I was talking about White. Preachers scare me. He’s not Catholic, is he?

Bryan: Oh God, can we change the subject now?

Reunited -by Jay & Donny

The camera fades in and we see a lonely graveyard at night. The moon is high in the sky and all is silent. The camera slowly pans around the scene and catches sight of a large mound of dirt beside a hole marked with a tombstone which reads “Here Lies ‘The Violence’ Vince Jones. The camera moves forward and catches sight of Vince Jones slowly stirring to consciousness while laying in an open casket set in the hole. Vince finally opens his eyes as he begins to shake off the cobwebs and holds his head in pain.

Vince Jones: Ah damn! What the hell happened? V’s head feelin’ like sh*t here!

He finally takes notice of his surroundings and looks around in shock.

Vince Jones: Huh? What the hell is V doin’ out here?

All of a sudden a lone figure steps up to the foot of the hole with a shovel thrown over his shoulder laughing to himself. Vince Jones, still a bit groggy, looks up at the cloaked figure in disdain.

Cloaked man: Comfortable down there, shawty?

Vince Jones: Who the hell are ya, huh? You the one that put me here??

Cloaked man: Ah come on Vince... After the beating I gave you at the final LOW pay-per-view your going to forget me so soon? Dropping you in that casket at Unearthed was only the start for us Vince. Could you really believe I would just disappear from your life after the federation closed its doors?

As the cameras swing around the mystery cloaked figure slowly removes his hood. Down inside the newly dug grave Vince Jones recognizes the face as Donny Diamond and he begins to panic.

Vince Jones: You son of a b*tch!

Donny Diamond: From the look of things Vince it doesn't seem to me that you will be making it to the after party you planned. In fact it doesn't look like you will be going any where's for a while. None of your boys can come here to lend you a hand, because for some reason "yo boy" Reno is out heading to Vegas because he thinks that slut of yours needed a ride. So it looks like V Jones is in a little bind out here in the middle of no where with the Living Nightmare.

Vince Jones: What you want, huh? What you want outta, V? Get V out this damn hole, Donny! Get V out this damn hole right now!

Vince begins pacing around back and forth like a crazed man in a frenzy down in the hole.

Donny Diamond: Wow who would of thought we'd ever see the day when Vinnie would be begging, beggin Double D to get out of this little problem...

Walking around the grave a couple of times, Donny Diamond stops to kick some loose gravel down to Vince. Kneeling down at the edge of the grave Diamond watches on as Vince remains in the distant corner.

Donny Diamond: Remember what you told me the day we came face to face in LOW? You said no one makes a fool out of V Jones. Well guess what fool? I have made a fool out of your gangsta ass, week after week. So instead of trying to toss some cash my way to fix this problem, you grow a set and we finish this for good. On the next addition of World Wide you meet me back inside of a GCW ring and we make it official.

Vince stops in his tracks and looks up at Donny in disbelief.

Vince Jones: What?? You and The Violence, one-on-one in the ring again??

Vince tries to regain his composure and remain cool.

Vince Jones: You got a death wish or somethin'??

Donny Diamond: If your lucky and your able to some how scavage a victory over me, Donny Diamond will be gone from your little nightmare. Because as you should know by now Vince, the only way to get a win over me is to kill me. Take a look at that long list of people who tried to take the Living Nightmare down and you will see it is next to impossible.

Saying his piece to Vince, Donny kicks a ladder down into the grave before walking off as the cameras fade.

Wanker-slapped -by Chris

The MegaTron quickly cuts backstage. Here we find Chrissy-Poo, one of the GCW's newest signees. Chrissy-Poo sips a blue raspberry slurpee as he strolls down the hallway of the arena. The 5' 9" 160 pounder is wearing his black combat boots, torn off camouflage pants and a black 'Dio' t-shirt. Chrissy-Poo flips his shaggy hair out of his eyes as he gasps for air, right after downing like... a pint of sugar-flavored ice.

Chrissy-Poo: Damn man... Last week I'm out of the tournament... this week I'm stuck hanging out back here, on deck in case Caldera needs some fresh meat to feed to someone... Oh well, at least I got this baby.

Chrissy-Poo takes a quick sip of his drink as he continues down the corridor.

Chrissy-Poo: I know it's not going to be easy being the smallest guy on the roster... but I just have to like... channel the powers of my tiny-man, bad-ass heroes... Like... CHRISSY F*CKING JAMES F*CKING DIO! Next match I have I'm going to Holy Diver someone's ass! I'm going to be the Last In Line no more! I'm like a Rainbow In The Friggin' Dark! I'm going to climb the GCW ladder and become the Man On The Silver Mountain! Yeah, so... LOOK OUT!

Chrissy smiles just before he sucks down a huge gulp of slurpee... then... BAM, it hits him! Chrissy-Poo's face scrunches up in pain, his free hand immediately grabs his head.

Chrissy-Poo: AH! GOD... BRAIN FREEZE!

Just then Chrissy-Poo turns the corner and... WHAM! He careens right into GCW superstar, Angel. Chrissy-Poo, weighing exactly 100 pounds less than Angel, is sent flailing backwards. Chrissy lands on his backside on the floor... while his slurpee lands all over Angel's face and clothes. Chrissy-Poo looks up at him innocently as veins start to pop out of Angel's head.

Chrissy-Poo: Sh*t, dude... I'm sorry, man--

Chrissy-Poo: I'm sorry.

Angel: You don't have ANY respect! This is a slap in my face... You little arse-hole, I've been working for years on my body... YEARS... I've been training for half a lifetime just to get where I am today... and look at you... They sign some little piece of crap like you... I can't STAND it.

Chrissy-Poo shoots back up.

Chrissy-Poo: "Hey man, look, I said I was sorry...

Chrissy-Poo: "But I think I've had just about enough of your bullsh*t. Now if you don't mind--

Chrissy-Poo lays on the floor unconscious as Angel stares down upon him. Angel spits upon Chrissy's fallen form and stomps away.

Angel: No respect...

The MegaTron fades out.

Singles Match – One Fall

Donny Diamond vs. “Poe” Dunn -by Carrie & Phyl

Bryan: GCW continues to grow, I tell you what Jhub the roster just continues to grow.

Jhub: Steven's certainly doing his job, and doing it well.

Pete: Couldn't we just get Badger or Dread back? Why do we need all these new guys?

Bryan: That's not always the way it works Pete, you should know that. This match we've got coming up right now is between two of the new incoming talent Poe Dunn, and Donny Diamond. In fact Dunn's already in the ring waiting.

"Hallow" by Submersed, blasts over the P.A. system as the massive figure of Donny Diamond starts down the isle towards the ring. The big man ignores the crowd as he walks, looking entirely focused on Poe Dunn inside the ring. Diamond walks up the steps to the ring apron, and steps through the ropes. Dunn takes a step forward jawing a little bit at Diamond, but The Nightmare simply ignores him.

Bryan: Diamond is a veteran, but he's still got a lot to prove here in GCW. And Dunn, well he's a virtual unknown, so of course he's going to try his hardest here.

Jhub: Both of these guys are just big tough SOB's. This shouldn't be much more than a knock down drag out brawl.

Pete: Actually Donny Diamond is fairly technically proficient, he could surprise you.

The bell sounds and the two men lock up in the center of the ring. Dunn takes the quick advantage with an Irish whip sending Donny into the ropes. Dunn catches the smaller man coming back in with a hip toss that nearly sends the Nightmare out of the ring. Dunn raises his arms to the air, as the crowd seems mixed in their opinions of him.

Bryan: Dunn taking early control, but he's not following up, this could cost him just like it did Angel last week.

Jhub: Yep you've got to stay on your opponent the crowd won't win you matches, but they can sure make you lose.

Dunn turns around just as Diamond is standing and hits the smaller man with a short arm clothesline dropping the Nightmare. Dunn follows up with a quick elbow drop, pops back up, bounces off the near ropes, and comes back in with another, hooking a leg and going for the cover. However Diamond is able to kick out in two.

Jhub: There, see that's what I'm talking about, you've got to focus on the match not the outside distractions, Dunn is really taking over here.

Dunn gets back up and begins arguing with the ref, firmly insisting that it was indeed a three count.

Jhub: Maybe I spoke too soon.

While arguing Diamond makes his way back up to his feet, but doesn't go on the attack, instead waits for Dunn to turn. The crowd steadily builds a big cheer for Diamond as he waits, with Dunn finally turning around only to get nailed in the stomach with a huge boot to the stomach doubling the big man over. Diamond quickly continues by driving forearms into Dunn's back pushing Poe down to his knees. Diamond then takes a couple steps back and charges in with a big field goal kick to Dunn's face nearly spinning the big man over.

Bryan: What a boot to the face by Donny Diamond!

She viciously kicks at the back of his knee before twisting it to lock in a leg grapevine, however Almasy is able to catch her and roll her into a small package..1...Mary rolls Almasy over pinning him…1...Almasy kicks out as both pop up, Mary charges but Almasy leaps over her to hit a sunset flip..1...2...KICKOUT as Mary claps her knees around Almasy’s head and pushes up using his legs for leverage on her own pin…1..2...KICKOUT! Both warriors pop back up their feet as the crowd roars it’s approval for the pinfall sequence.

Bryan: That was incredible!

Jhub: A series of real quick near falls there. Those might not have won the match but you’ve got no idea how much it takes out of you to get out of a pin attempt.

Mary and Almasy begin circling each other warily looking for an opening. Mary comes in for another lock up but Almasy was waiting and he nails a VICIOUS palm strike right between Typhoid Mary’s eyes stunning the wild woman.

Pete: DEATH TOUCH!

Jhub: What? This isn’t a video game.

Pete: Hey, Almasy calls it that the Death Touch, I’m just saying…

Almasy steps in and attempts a boot to Mary’s gut but she pulls her hands away from her face at the last second to catch Almsay’s foot. Mary spins the Final Fantasy around, and nails a kick of her own to his stomach doubling him over. Mary goes for a piledriver, but Almasy blocks, another try, Almasy blocks again this time lifting her up, hooking both legs with his arms and driving her down to the canvas with a backdrop driver! Almasy lays back across Mary for the pin, 1...2.…KICKOUT!

Bryan: Mary just refusing to go down here. These two must be exhausted.

Jhub: Indeed, they’ve been firing on all cylinders the enter match and that has to getting to them. Almasy didn’t even have the energy to hook a leg.

Almasy uses the ropes to pull himself back up to his feet, and takes a few minutes to breathe in the corner, while Mary slowly gets to her feet. Mary charges in but Seymour sidesteps and Mary goes chest first into the corner, she stumbles out only to be spun around by The Final Fantasy who boots her in the gut doubling over and quickly nails her a straightjacket piledriver!

Bryan: TIME COMPRESSION!

Mary nearly bounces a foot in the air off her head from the impact and crumples to the mat on her stomach, but Almasy wastes no time rolls her over and goes for the cover. 1...2.…3! SEYMOUR ALMSAY WINS!

Jhub: Good match.

Bryan: Are you kidding me Jhub, that was incredible. Mary and Almasy took each other to the limit here.

Jhub: Woulda been better if we coulda seen her rack. Still yeah, it was pretty good. If Mary would’ve just calmed down a little bit and thought before she went charging in this one might have come down different.

Bryan: That would’ve been a major upset, but Almasy manages to pull out the win here, but wow Typhoid Mary showed a lot of people she’s for real here in GCW, and I’d expect she’s going to be back next week with a vengeance.

A Decision Made -by Jamie

Bryan: Jhub, I'm getting word that Sally Ward has caught up with Jake Summers backstage. Lets go to her and see if Summers has come to a decision.

Scenes quickly fade backstage to where, Sally Ford, with chest pooched out, stands near on of the many soda machines twirling her blond hair.

Sally Ford: Mr. Summers... Mr. Summers.... Jake!

Jake Summers abruptly stops in his tracks, turning his attention the the lovely GCW backstage reporter. Eye her up and down he begins to make his way over to her.

Sally Ford: Earlier, Jake you were called out by The Regulators whom demanded a rematch after Ryan Stephens actions last week. Furthermore moments later The Guild came out as well asking for a title shot at Fallout next week.

Jake Summers: Indeed they did... and since then I've had a decision to make. Whether or not to give The Regulators a rematch.

Sally Ford: It appeared earlier your tag team partner didn't exactly see fit to give in to their demands. Any comments?

Jake Summers: Stephens made his bed and now he has to lie in.

Sally Ford: So are you saying you’re going to give The Regulators their rematch?

Summers ponders his decision.

Jake Summers: Yeah it's the right thing to do.

Bryan: Whoa... Ladies and gentlemen it appears that Summers and Stephens will defend their titles once again against The Regulators.

At that moment, Ryan Stephens enters the scene to which Sally approaches.

Sally Ford: Ryan, what so you of having to defend your tag titles against The Regulators once more.

Stephens looks surprised. He turns to Summers.

Ryan Stephens: I told you I wasn't defending the titles. There's no way I'm going to defend the titles agains The Regulators again. Whether I cheated or not isn't to be questioned. They lost and that's all that matters.

Sally turns the microphone back to Jake Summers.

Jake Summers: It's too late I already agreed to give them a rematch.

Ryan Stephens: We'll see about that. I'm going to talk to Caldera about this.

If upon cue Steven Caldera enters the scene.

Ryan Stephens: Caldera, there's no way I'm defending the tag titles at Fallout. Especially not against The Regulators.

Bryan: It would appear as if Ryan Stephens has a little bit of the limber tail.

Jhub: Stephens is not a coward. Whether you agree with his actions or not last week, what he did was for the better of his team.

Caldera scratches his chin.

Caldera: Well, I have to admit, Mr. Summers, your partner here has a point. You’ve already beaten the Regulators. They should have to earn their shot.

Stephens: Thank you!

The crowd boos as Stephens smiles, and Summers looks a bit upset by this decision.

Caldera: But The Guild, on the other hand… you’ve never beaten them. And frankly, I think it’s high time you gave them a shot!

The crowd cheers wildly at this, and Stephens gets red in the cheeks.

Stephens: But-

Caldera: Hang on, I’m not finished yet. Now, The Regulators do happen to be a very impressive Tag Team, and frankly I think you’re lucky I’m not making you face them too. So here’s what I’m going to do. At FallOut, it’s gonna be Ryan Stephens and Jake Summers defending the GCW Tag Team Titles against The Guild!

The crowd cheers, but Summers even appears upset at this.

Summers: What about the Regulators? They asked for a rematch, and as far as I’m concerned, they can have one.

Caldera: Indeed, but I’m afraid I’d need you two to agree on that. And it doesn’t seem like you do. Now, I’m not in the habit of getting in people’s business, but it seems I simply have to step in here. Mr. Stephens, you have a title defense with Mr. Summers here against The Guild whether you like it or not. As for the Regulators, Mr. Summers, don’t worry, they’ll be taken care of. Because at FallOut, the Regulators will have a chance to EARN a rematch, when they take on GCW’s newest tag team… The Bloodlines!

Summers shrugs, apparently satisfied for the moment. But Stephens finally explodes.

Stephens: You can’t do this, Caldera. I’m the leader of this tag team, I’m the one who made these tag titles mean something! You can’t just do whatever you want with them! These titles belong to ME!

Summers is taken aback by this, stepping aside and standing next to Caldera. Caldera, for his part, merely grins and begins to walk away.

Caldera: Mr. Stephens… Good luck at FallOut.

Bryan: Wow! Jake Summers wanted to give everyone their shot, but Caldera came up with a compromise!

Pete: Looks like Ryan Stephens wasn’t in the mood to compromise, though!

Jhub: The Regulators are going to be pissed next week! They demanded a tag title shot at FallOut, but they get the Bloodlines instead?! Who the hell are they?!

Bryan: The Bloodlines are GCW’s newest tag team, and they’ll be here next week! The Regulators are going to meet their opponents then! You’d better believe they’re going to be in a fighting mood at FallOut after President Caldera just cut them out of the FallOut title picture! But rest assured, The Regulators will have another shot at the tag titles soon!

Pete: But for now, the shot goes to The Guild!

Tag Team Match – One Fall

Michael “Spitfire” Stevens & The Marauder vs. “The Machine” Mike Savige & LeStatt Knight -by Ben

Bryan: Well, fans, it’s main event time. The winning team in this match is going to have a hell of a lot of momentum going into FallOut, and the losers are going to be on the run.

Jhub: No doubt that the mental edge going into FallOut is going to be won or lost in this match. Someone is going to step up and really take control of their destiny. If Michael Stevens and The Marauder can pull this one out, they’ll be in a great position to win their matches and leave FallOut with some gold. But if Knight and Savige pull it out, they’re going to be very hard to dethrone come FallOut.

“Victory” cues up, and the crowd leaps to its feet in cheers. The crowd screams wildly as the masked Marauder appears on stage. The Marauder trots towards the ring, his eyes focused on the ring, but pauses to slap hands with the cheering fans along the ramp.

Andrews: The following tag team contest is set for one fall! Introducing first, from parts unknown, weighing in at 256 pounds. . . The Marauder!

Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, whatever you want to call him, The Marauder has been waiting for this opportunity for a long time! He’s finally on the brink of making a huge impact in GCW, and tonight is his first chance to really get a leg up on his nemesis Savige, and the GCW World Champion LeStatt Knight!

Jhub: Well, Lexicon is a fine athlete, JB, but he hasn’t done anything to show me that he can compete on the big stage! He’s a nice guy and the crowd loves him, but that only takes you so far! This guy has a huge chance tonight, as you said, JB, to really cut loose and show people what he’s made of! And if he can do that, he might just have enough to pry that TV title off Savige at FallOut.

Marauder slides under the ropes and leaps to the middle turnbuckle, flexing to the crowd as they frantically snap photos. Marauder hops down, and “I Want to Conquer The World” cues up overhead! The erupts grows rapidly into a full-on roar as Michael Stevens appears on stage! Running his hands across his bright red hair, Stevens smiles and nods confidently, strutting to the ring with pride.

Andrews: And his tag team partner, from Greenwich, Connecticut, weighing in at 249 pounds. . . Michael “Spitfire” Stevens!!

Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, Michael Stevens’ star is rising! He dominated the World Champion at Annihilation! He’s dominating the competition in the Primetime Central Gauntlet tournament! He’s been virtually untouchable ever since winning this year’s Dangerous Games Battle Royal! And at FallOut, he’s got the chance to take that final step!

Pete: But that’s a huge step to take! LeStatt has been just as dominant, and one of these guys is going to have to give, JB! It’s a submission match! There are no disqualifications! It’s just the two most dominant wrestlers in the world beating each other until one of them finally taps out.

Jhub: Stevens is on the brink of greatness, guys! I’ve watched him grow from an insecure sneak into a dominant, intimidating technical genius. He’s always had the potential. But now is the time Michael Stevens needs to step up and take the final plunge from greatness into legend.

The roar persists as Stevens slides into the ring and heads for the ropes. Stevens climbs up the middle of the ropes and raises his arms to the air, posing for the frenzied fans. Stevens hops down and paces to the opposite side, repeating his pose for the rest of the arena. As he hops back down and his music fades, Stevens slaps hands with Marauder and the two turn to the stage. “Machinehead” cues up next, and the arena is plunged into boos. The TV title belt appears over a massive shoulder, glistening in the lights. Mike Savige grins widely as he stares down the ramp at Marauder, eyeing him intently.

Andrews: And their opponents. Introducing first, from New York, New York, weighing in at 255 pounds, he is the GCW Television Champion. . . “The Machine” Mike Savige!

Bryan: The Machine, indeed! This guy is as intense and as maniacal as they come, ladies and gentlemen, and he is the reigning TV champion. This guy loves to brawl, he loves to get down and dirty and pound away, and he’s got his sights set on The Marauder in what has become a very personal, intense rivalry.

Jhub: And that’s why I fear for Lexicon at FallOut, guys. Lexicon’s great, and he matches Savige’s intensity at Annihilation, but can he do it again? Does he want it bad enough? We’ll have to wait and see.

Savige stops short of the ring, patting his TV title belt in mockery of Marauder. “O’Fortuna” hits next, and the lights dim in the arena. A red light grows on stage, and a lone figure appears. The GCW title belt rests idly around the waist of LeStatt Knight as he slowly stalks towards the ring. A few feet behind, Typhoid Mary finally appears, carefully following Knight to the ring. The boos begin to rattle the roof as the fans serenade Knight to the ring.

Andrews: And his tag team partner, from Mexico City, Mexico, weighing in at 266 pounds, he is the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. . . LeStatt Knight!

Bryan: Well, there he is, folks! The man who has dominated every man he has ever stepped in the ring with, save one! LeStatt Knight is the champion of the world, and he’s got something to prove now.

Jhub: Hey, Stevens dominated Knight at Annihilation, that’s for sure. But in the end, it may have been a mistake. Because Knight is motivated now, he’s intent on taking it to another level and really putting a hurting on Michael Stevens. Spitty’d better be ready for the fight of his life at FallOut, because that’s what Knight’s going to bring.

Knight passes Savige, who shivers as a tingle runs up his spine. He licks his lips happily and follows Knight to the ring, where the two climb inside. Mary stops short of the ring, looking a bit worn after her match with Almasy. Stevens and Marauder give their opponents room but don’t leave the ring. Knight and Savige each surrender their title belts in turn, and Knight steps out to the apron without another word, leaving Savige to start the match. Stevens and Marauder slap hands again and Marauder steps out, allowing Stevens to begin for their side.

Bryan: Well, fans, there’s the bell, and this main event match is underway! It’s gonna be Stevens starting things off with Mike Savige.

Jhub: You know, another way to look at this, JB, is that Stevens should just take it easy in this match and let Marauder do all the work. He can’t risk getting seriously hurt just two weeks before FallOut!

Pete: But the same goes for everyone in the match!

Stevens and Savige lock up in the center of the ring, and Savige immediately takes over with a side headlock. Stevens attempts to wrench his way out, but to no avail. Savige finally takes Stevens to the mat, still holding on tight. Stevens flips backwards and slaps on a scissorlock, breaking the hold and pinning Savige down! Savige finally powers his way free and both men rise, where Stevens immediately drags Savige down with a side headlock takedown of his own.

Bryan: And there’s a nice opening flurry from Stevens, who is going to keep going back to that technical prowess that was on display at Annihilation. Don’t forget folks, Stevens and Knight are going at it again at FallOut in a submission match, and I daresay Stevens is going to have the advantage in that one!

Jhub: You got that right! Stevens is a master of submission, but Knight is more of a stand up brawler. Frankly, I don’t think this match is tailored to Knight’s style, and I wonder just what the hell he was thinking when he made the challenge for that match!

Bryan: He was thinking about revenge, and revenge only. Knight wants to save face, and he wants to do it by making Stevens tap out!

Savige powers back to his feet and backs into the ropes. Savige shoves Stevens off and across the ring, where he hits the ropes. Stevens bounces back in, but Savige drops to the mat and Stevens springs over. Stevens flies in again, but Knight is waiting with a high kick over the ropes! Stevens ducks the kick and immediately answers with a stiff right hand to the jaw, knocking Knight down! The crowd explodes in cheers as Stevens turns, only to receive a massive clothesline from Savige.

Pete: Hell yeah! Stevens just dropped Knight in one shot! But Savige is back on top!

Jhub: Somehow, I don’t think Knight minded…

The camera moves in close on Knight showing a trickle of blood forming upon his smiling lips. He licks the blood off his lips, as if savoring it, as Savige pulls Stevens up and takes him over with a vertical suplex. Savige rises again and waits for Stevens to stand, charging and dropping him with a big boot to the face.

Bryan: God, high impact offense from Mike Savige here, and Stevens is in trouble!

Savige pulls Stevens off the mat and glances back at Knight. Knight smiles, his attention entirely on Stevens. Savige attempts to pull Stevens into the corner, but Stevens breaks free and nails Savige with a high dropkick. Savige stumbles into his corner where Knight tags him on the back and slides into the ring. The crowd boos wildly until Stevens responds with a diving tag to Marauder, who comes in full speed!

Pete: Knight tagged himself in! But here comes Marauder! This is gonna be good, JB!

Marauder meets Knight with a high flying forearm, staggering the champion! The crowd explodes as Marauder begins pummeling Knight, backing him into the ropes. Marauder finally whips Knight to the other side, and catches him on the way back with a massive back body drop! The cheers continue as Knight springs back to his feet, only to catch a headbutt to the nose.

Bryan: God, Marauder is on fire here! He’s coming at the World Champion with no fear, no intimidation!

Jhub: Take a good look, Mike Savige, this is the man you have to face in ten days!

Marauder whips Knight again, but Knight reverses. Marauder bounces back and ducks a clothesline, hitting the ropes again, but finding Savige waiting with a knee to the kidneys. Marauder, unfazed, turns and nails Savige with a hard right! Savige drops to the apron and Marauder turns, right into a big back heel kick from Knight.

Pete: Ouch! Marauder took that one right in the jaw!

Bryan: The Marauder is on fire here tonight, he really wants to make a statement, but that momentary distraction from Savige cost him.

Knight pulls Marauder up and nails him with a hard right hand, then whips him into the ropes. Marauder bounces back and ducks a clothesline, hitting the brakes and coming back hard. Knight turns, walking right into a massive clothesline from Marauder! The impact turns Knight inside out, and he lands with a thud on his chest in the center of the ring!

Bryan: OH YEAH! ALL ABOARD!

Jhub: That was incredible impact! The Marauder might have just knocked out the champion of the world!

Marauder instantly heads for a neutral corner, stepping out of the ring and climbing the ropes!

Pete: Here comes the Jolly Roger!!!

Bryan: WHAT AN UPSET!

Marauder dives with the big elbow drop, but Knight’s body suddenly jerks out of the way. Savige pulls Knight away from outside the ring, and Marauder finds nothing but canvas. The crowd boos mightily as Savige returns for his corner and begins shouting at Knight, desperately looking for a tag.

Bryan: Damnit! Savige pulled Knight out of harm’s way, and Marauder came up empty on that Jolly Roger elbow!

Jhub: And both of these guys really need a tag here! Who’s gonna get it first!? If Savige makes the tag, he’ll be all alone with a helpless Marauder! But if Stevens gets it first, he’ll have time to work over his opponent at FallOut!

Knight slowly pulls himself together, glancing back at Marauder, who remains motionless other than his subtle writhing on the mat. Knight streatches out to Savige, but Savige’s extended hand suddenly snaps back! The crowd explodes as Michael Stevens pulls Savige off the apron and whips him hard into the steel guardrail! Stevens begins waylaying Savige as Knight stands angrily, but referee Josh Briggs cuts off his exit!

Bryan: OH YEAH! Stevens stopped the tag! He saw Marauder wasn’t going to make it in time, and cut Savige down from behind!

Pete: Savige is in trouble here! Knight is furious!

The frenzied crowd explodes as Marauder suddenly gets his second wind and slides in behind Knight. Marauder moves into position and immediately drops Knight with a hard side Russian legsweep! The fans continue cheering as Marauder keeps the hold applied, rolls over, and pulls himself and Knight back up for another one! But this time Knight stays firm, suddenly dropping forward and forcing Marauder face first into the canvas!

Bryan: OH GOD! A reverse legsweep from Knight, and Marauder might be out cold!

Jhub: Wait a minute, what the hell is Knight doing?!

Knight lunges onto Marauder’s back, sitting on his back. He then reaches forward and applies a dragon sleeper, pulling back on Marauder’s spine with the vile hold! The crowd gasps in horror as Knight mercilessly contorts Marauder’s body.

Bryan: OH MY GOD! WHAT A SICKENING SUBMISSION HOLD FROM KNIGHT!

Stevens hears the commotion and turns, just in time to see Marauder tap out. The bell sounds and boos fill the arena. Stevens grows wide eyed, suddenly frozen by the sight of Marauder trapped in Knight’s vicious hold.

Andrews: Here are your winners, Mike Savige and LeStatt Knight!

Bryan: Someone get him off of there!

Jhub: What are you waiting for, Spitty?! Help your damn partner!

Knight refuses to release the hold, staying on top of Marauder with the vile submission hold. Marauder’s muffled screams fade as his body suddenly goes limp, having passed out from the sleeper hold. Stevens, halfway up the apron, simply stares in awe.

Bryan: My God, what’s Stevens thinking!?

Jhub: He’s thinking that could be him at FallOut! Knight just made Marauder tap out in seconds! God, what a deadly hold! If Knight applies that to Michael Stevens at FallOut, Stevens will be done for!

Pete: Marauder’s out, guys! He’s out cold! Someone needs to get in there and help him!

The boos continue to rain down as security rushes past Stevens, shoving him out of the way. A few security members finally tear Knight away, letting Marauder’s body collapse to the mat. The new air gives Marauder new life, and he manages to curl up, clutching his back in agony.

Bryan: The Marauder was on fire here, and he really took it to the World Champion, but it looks like Michael Stevens left him hanging here!

Jhub: Marauder’s gonna be pissed when he wakes up…

Pete: I don’t understand it! What’s Spitty’s problem?!

Jhub: He’s been intimidated by the champion! Knight just scared the pants off Michael Stevens!

“O’Fortuna” begins to play again, adding an ominous soundtrack to the terrifying moment. Knight is handed his World Title belt again, which he raises into the air with a menacing glare at Stevens. Stevens remains frozen, his face blank, as if imagining what Knight has in store for him at FallOut.

Bryan: My God, LeStatt Knight has made a huge statement to Michael Stevens tonight! Watch out at FallOut, Spitfire, because LeStatt Knight wants to make you tap out! Ladies and gentlemen, for Pete and The Jhub, we’re out of time in Vancouver! Join us next week! Oh God…

The GCW logo appears on the screen as Stevens and Knight engage in a tense staredown. Stevens breaks first, slowly backing away, leaving to regroup as WorldWide fades to a close………
