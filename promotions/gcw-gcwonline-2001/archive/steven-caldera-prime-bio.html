Steven Caldera Biography Real Name Steven Caldera Nicknames The Boss Hometown Columbus, OH Height 6'4 Weight 245 lbs Biography Steven Caldera was born in Columbus, Ohio, the eldest son of a well known family that was destined to do great things. Caldera had opportunities in any field imaginable, but had only one true passion: professional wrestling. Caldera forwent many lucrative careers and plunged himself into training to become a wrestler. His talent was evident from the early parts of his career. Trained by a local promoter named Mr. Danger, Caldera rose up the ranks to rapidly win his first title during his rookie year as a wrestler. Hitting the national circuit with the American Wrestling Alliance, Caldera saw his star continue to rise. Over the course of the next two years he won several titles, but in doing so suffered a traumatic knee injury. Caldera has had several surgeries over the years that have allowed him to wrestle for short spells, but the damage forced him into an early retirement. Caldera caught on with the CWU as an on-air personality, and his savvy for the business built him a sizable following in the crowd and the locker room. Despite cultivating a reputation for an iron will and a dominating ego, Caldera's input in the company helped it grow many times over. However, when the owners forced the ever more popular Caldera out, it became clear that his destiny was to found his own promotion. In January of 2001, the dawn of the new millennium, Global Championship Wrestling was born. GCW took the world by storm, and after a two-year hiatus from 2003-2005, GCW returned and evolved into the largest, most recognizable professional wrestling organization in the world. During its eleven year lifespan, GCW toured every populated continent on Earth, broadcast over 200 events, and played host to over 200 of the best wrestlers in the world (and James von Doom). Not always popular with the crowd, Caldera believed that fans were most excited when the bad guys were on top and the fan favorites were trying to knock them down. More often than not, Caldera was eager to play the role of bad guy himself. With several iterations over the years, The Establishment was the most enduring and impactful stable in company history. Despite his domineering approach, the method appeared to work, as GCW built one of the most loyal rosters and fan bases of any wrestling promotion. Caldera left the company in 2011 to focus on other aspects of GCW's parent company, Caldera Enterprises, Incorporated. Since the company closed its doors for good in 2012, Caldera has begun international philanthropic work, and donates generously to the arts. But some say, deep inside his Columbus, Ohio offices, Steven Caldera still plots the next GCW comeback.
