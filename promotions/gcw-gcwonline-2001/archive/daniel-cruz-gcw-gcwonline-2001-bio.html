Daniel Cruz Biography Real Name Daniel Cruz Nicknames "The Canadian Icon" Hometown Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia, Canada Birthday June 11th 1974 Height 6'0 Weight 234lbs Biography Daniel Cruz grew up in Nova Scotia, Canada. Since the age of four Daniel Cruz loved the sport of Professional Wrestling. Whenever an independent or professional organization would come to town, Daniel Cruz would drive his parents crazy to take him to the event. Even though they despised the sport themselves, they always took a very young Daniel. As time moved on throughout the years, Daniel Cruz got involved in more sports such as Hockey, Golf, and Baseball. Although he excelled in all of these sports and enjoyed playing them, Professional Wrestling was the one sport he always dreamed of being a part of. Right out of High School, Cruz moved to Toronto where he attended University. At least that's what he told his parents. Daniel did attend University to become a Chartered Accountant, but what he neglected to inform his family was that in the night time he was attending a Wrestling Training School in downtown Toronto. His parents wouldn't want him having any part of a training school if they knew it would affect Cruz's studies at University. And that’s exactly what happened. After a year and a half of balancing a life in University in the day time and learning the tricks of the trade in Pro Wrestling in the nights, Daniel Cruz dropped out of University and moved to Calgary when he received an offer to join an independent circuit that traveled around Alberta. His family wasn't impressed with his decision but respected he was doing what he loved most. Daniel Cruz traveled around Alberta working for various organizations and winning numerous small time championships. He received additional training while in Alberta mainly from "Ruthless" Eddie Gilbert who became a long time friend of Cruz. Daniel Cruz was becoming recognized all over Canada, USA, and Japan for his high impact matches. Each and every night Cruz delivered a high energy in his matches that was full of hard impact and lots of agility. Cruz's submission skills were also a big part of his matches that the fans immediately took notice too. One person who instantly took a shining to Cruz was Riston Simon. Simon was a man with a lot of money and used that money to launch Extreme Pro Wrestling. EPW was in the beginning stages at the time and turned into one of the longest running and highly rated organizations of all time. Daniel Cruz was a main event star and shared many heated feuds with Simon himself. During this time Cruz held the Extreme Pro Wrestling Heavyweight Championship two times. He was also a three time Television Champion. After years of EPW running strong, CEO Riston Simon decided it was time to turn the organization into something even bigger. After a long hiatus, FATE was constructed with Riston Simon running at the helm. Again "The Canadian Icon" was the spotlight star winning the Battle Royal that night becoming the first person ever to hold the FATE World Heavyweight Championship etching himself in the record books forever. As time passed along with legendary matches and feuds going down in history, "The Canadian Icon" decided it was time to officially call it quits. Daniel Cruz made more than enough money to enjoy his retirement. He always promised himself he would retire young so he could enjoy his time off with no aches and pains. But the retirement life would only last so long. Daniel Cruz took a shining to Global Championship Wrestling. Cruz would watch every episode and purchased every Pay Per View event. Eventually, the quite life of retirement became too much for Cruz to handle. After two months of negotiating with GCW Executives, Daniel Cruz signed on with GCW on April 6th 2011. Other Feds FATE, EPW, and numerous small promotions during the rookie years. Other Fed Titles Extreme Pro Wrestling 2x Champion, Extreme Pro Wrestling 3x Television Champion, FATE World Champion Other Fed Awards N/A Strengths Excellent at submission victories. Cruz has been known to sneak out a submission victory out of no where. He has done this multiple times over the years, even in matches when it looked like the chips were down, Cruz was able to lock on a special submiss Weaknesses Cruz has some high launch aerial moves that he likes to perform. However there are times it takes him too long to setup the special jump/dive. The time delay results in Cruz giving the opponent too much time to rest. Wrestling Style Mat Technician/Submissionist Strategy Daniel Cruz's strategy is to always keep the match in his area of expertise. This usually consists of quick technically sound maneuvers that will bring the opponent onto the ground. Cruz usually picks out one part of the opponent and works on it throughout the match. Regardless if its the opponents back, leg, or arm, Daniel Cruz typically softens the limb throughout the match and applies various submission maneuvers to force the opponent to tap out or hit them with The Cruz Control. The crowd instantly stand to their feet trying to get a view of the entrance way. The Canadian Flag slowly lowers down from the rafters and stops at top of the stage. Red strobe lights begin to flicker and flash while a florescent light image of Maple Leafs light up the ramp way and flicker inside of the ring. Moments later "The Canadian Icon" Daniel Cruz walks out with his arms out to his sides, palms facing up. He stands at top of the entrance way taking a few minutes to check out the screaming crowd in front of him. He gives a cocky grin and mumbles a few words before walking down to the ring. With his head held high in the air, Daniel Cruz walks down to the ring with a very confident strut. Cruz suddenly stops to bicker with the crowd who seems to be yelling something at him that he didn't like. Cruz slowly turns his back to the fans and heads towards the steel steps. Casually walking up the steps, Cruz enters the ring and stands center of the squared circle looking all around the arena. He raises both arms in the air in a victory stance one final time before the music slowly fades out and the lights come back on.
