HEADLINES Wednesday, November 09, 2005 GCW Announces Next International Tour
Columbus, Ohio- Global Championship Wrestling has announced its next international tour. After WorldWide spent several weeks in Europe earlier this year, the show heads East to Japan for a series of events early next year.

Dates in Yokohama, Osaka, Kobe and Nagoya have been officially scheduled. WorldWide will appear in all of these cities, and house shows will be run in the surrounding towns during the tour. This culminates in GCW's next Pay-Per-View event after NC-17, Rampage, in Tokyo on January 29th, 2006.

GCW heads out on a tour of the east coast in the United States in December so that the stars can be close to home for the holidays. Following the new year, GCW heads to Japan for a month long tour of events.

OFFICIAL TOUR SCHEDULE:

January 5th: Osaka
January 12th: Nagoya
January 19th: Kobe
January 26th: Yokohama
January 29th: Rampage in Tokyo

Friday, October 28, 2005 The GCW Weekend Update
Hello, everyone, Kevin Jacobs here with the weekend update. We're just hours away from Devil's Night, and there's a lot going on behind the scenes to get ready.

-While the six guys that have made it into the amazing main event are exceptionally talented, some of the GCW brass are privately worried about the match. With the winner most likely taking on LeStatt Knight in the NC-17 main event, the company has a lot riding on that match. There are fears in the company that Knight has set up the match so that nobody who comes out of it are capable of dethroning him on GCW's biggest stage. Those six men are world-class athletes, but not yet proven on the same level of the World Champion. The current opinion backstage is that the main event of NC-17 could turn into a massacre unless someone takes a major step forward at Devil's Night.

-GCW security has come under heavy fire, and head of security James Haley will likely be fired early next week. President Caldera is reportedly furious about security's complete inability to protect his wrestlers, in particular The Marauder. Marauder has been beaten savigely over the past few weeks, without a single clue as to who might be behind it. Marauder will face his attacker at Devil's Night, but Caldera Enterprises plans to take steps to ensure this never happens again.

-The GCW booking commitee is very impressed with the work of both Vince Jones and Typhoid Mary. While neither has made major strides forward, they are expected to be getting the chance to do just that. Jones, while considered a loose cannon and highly unpredictable, is nonetheless impressing the management with his intensity and hard-hitting style. Typhoid Mary, struggling to gain momentum, has still made an impression with her smooth moves and high ring IQ. Expect to see lots more of these two over GCW television heading into NC-17.

-Speaking of NC-17, the big show is coming up quick. Madison Square Garden has been sold out for months, but GCW is seeking ways to add seating to the venue and release at least 500 extra tickets. The hype continues to grow, and I myself am growing more and more excited about it every day.

-After a much-needed slowdown in hiring, GCW is ready to begin searching for new talent again. GCW is looking at several promising new prospects, and are considering giving spots to at least one. After that massive wave of great new talent, those who couldn't hang with the incredible talent in GCW have been sent packing, and the company is excited to bring in some eager new stars.

-Despite rumors that Seymour Almasy was considering leaving the company after his rivalry with Rich Rollins had been settled, it appears as though Almasy has agreed to a longer-term commitment. Almasy is working on a verbal deal due to his commitments elsewhere, but his name now appears on house show bookings well into December. The schedule for 2006 hasn't been released yet, but the latest word is that Almasy's name appears at least into January. Expect to see plenty more of the phenom.

-After a troubling time in his career, Johnny Borealis is reportedly clean and sober. Struggling with a painkiller addiction, Borealis sought help and managed to kick his problem and remain on the road. While Borealis remains one of the more hated men on camera, GCW tips its hat to Borealis for managing to straighten out his life.

-Trey Tremendous is doing well rehabbing at his home in Detroit. Devil's Night takes place in the Motor City this weekend, but Trey will not be in attendance. Trey suffered a major knee injury at Annihilation a few weeks back, and only made things worse by competing in an ACWF match at PTC's Super Series. Trey remains in high spirits and is under a long-term contract with the company, but don't expect to see him again until some time in 2006.

-Rumors that President Caldera was interested in purchasing a stake in the Dallas Mavericks are completely false. Caldera told me personally this week that he loves Mark Cuban's take on the team, and instead chooses to continue to support the team by buying his courtside season tickets every year. Rumor has it that he might be in the running to buy the Dallas Cowboys once Jerry Jones decides to hang it up, but that won't happen for a long time.

-Chrissy-Poo's Darth Vader costume this week came directly from LucasArts. The suit was the very same suit worn by Hayden Christenson in Star Wars Episode III. However, being shorter than Hayden, he was forced to pad the bottom of his boots to fill it up.

-No word whatsoever on the identity of the man in the Caldera Mask that has been appearing at WorldWide lately, but most are confident that Mike Hardy will get the job done and unmask him at Devil's Night.

-Some GCW stars are upset about not being booked for Devil's Night. But rest assured, every single member of the GCW roster as of right now will be in competition at NC-17. Despite their not being active this Sunday, expect everyone to be on hand and quite busy gearing up for the big event.

-The Falls Count Anywhere stipulation was suggested by Sam Wolack and confirmed this evening by the GCW committee. Coyle didn't seem worried about it, but was definitely curious to know what Wolack has in store for him at Devil's Night. Wolack, formerly X, definitely has a plan, but we'll just have to wait and see.

-Apologies to everyone who wanted to see yours truly in costume this week at WorldWide, but I had just returned from a business meeting and didn't have time to dress up. For anyone who's curious, I'm going as the Kool-Aid man for Halloween. Oh Yeah!

That's it from Denver. The crew is now off to Detroit for Devil's Night! I hope everyone tunes in this Sunday for the show, and have a safe and happy Halloween! Until next time...

-Kevin Jacobs
Senior GCW Interviewer

Friday, October 21, 2005 News after Chaotic WorldWide
After pure destruction at the 29th Edition of WorldWide, Eva VanLorne reportedly disappeared at the end of the show. Many speculations have arisen amongst the fan-based community, such as a kidnapping. Many have come to believe that prime suspects in the disappearance of Eva include LeStatt Knight, Jonathan Borealis, Mike Savige, and some are even speculating that Ricky Thunder may have something to do with it. No word has been officially released about Eva, reguarding her location, or whether she was even kidnapped or not.

Also, the chaos at WW29 led to The Marauder visiting the hospital for a short stay where he released himself by fleeing the scene. Doctors reported that Marauder may have suffered a mild concussion and reported that he should stay overnight for an analysis.

Wednesday, October 19, 2005 Another Addition to the GCW Team
Caldera Enterprises reported on October 19th at 3:00 PM that Cassian Jaymes is their newest acquisition to the GCW Staff. Cassian Jaymes graduated from Mizzou majoring in Journalism. Caldera reported that Cassian was hired to get the inside scoop on superstar relations. Cassian has conducted interviews with several big names including Ricky Williams, Terrell Owens, Peyton Manning, and Tom Brady as he recently ended his contract with ESPN. Cassian Jaymes claims he is not scared to bring out the truth in the superstars of GCW, Caldera also stated that he was quite confident in Jaymes’ ability to gain the answers that everyone wants to know. Although Cassian Jaymes has been signed to the Global Championship Wrestling Staff he will not be appearing in backstage interviews for the time being.

Big Story: Jake Summers Update
Phoenix, Arizona- Conspicuous in its absence from last week's WorldWide broadcast was the scheduled match of Bryan Mayhem vs. Jake Summers. The match was blacked out from the broadcast, and for the first time GCW has released a statement as to why.

It appears as though the career of Jake Summers was unceremoniously ended at WorldWide. After the match had ended, Summers had been the recipient of a fractured skull, a broken left leg, and a severe concussion at the hands of Bryan Mayhem. The match was so brutal that even the lenient executives at X:TV, owned by Caldera Enterprises, no less, refused to air it.

Mayhem apparently entered the ring with destruction on his mind. Summers never got out of the gate, as Mayhem promptly overpowered him and delivered a massive powerbomb, going to work from there. The rest of the details of the match were not released to the public, but fans in attendance reported it as being the most brutal beating they had ever seen.

Mayhem was fined heavily by GCW as a result, and a suspension is possible. However, when asked about it, Mayhem simply replied, "I was bored, so I thought I'd kill someone."

Summers is expected to spent several days, if not weeks, in a Phoenix area hospital, as a result of the beating. He has been removed from the GCW roster, and is expected to be released this week. However, as with all GCW stars receiving releases due to in-ring injury, all of Summers' medical bills will be paid for by GCW, and he will be given an impressive severance package to help get him back on his feet.

This is shocking news, and GCW offers its best wishes to Summers as he recovers. The career of one of GCW's brightest young stars has been ended by the maniacal Bryan Mayhem, who will likely forever be considered one of GCW's most dangerous wrestlers.

Monday, October 10, 2005 GCW Releases Two, Adds 3
Columbus, Ohio- Following WorldWide this week, GCW made two more releases.

Gabriel White and Angel have both been released from GCW. Caldera Enterprises explained that White had been a no-show for several weeks. Angel, though he appeared at WorldWide this week, has not been performing at the expected level. The two will not return to GCW.

As always, the departured opened the door to new additions. Varsity, Ehren Moore and RipeFist were officially signed and added to the roster today. Varsity and Moore are expected to debut next week. RipeFist appeared on WorldWide this week, and makes his in-ring debut in a tag title match with his new friend Ricky Thunder.
