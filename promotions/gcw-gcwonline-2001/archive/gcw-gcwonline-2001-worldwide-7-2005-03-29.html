WorldWide 7 Olympic Arena — Torino ---- WorldWide 7 The lights in the arena dim, and the Italian crowd begins to buzz. The steel GCW logo behind the MegaTron begins to glow neon blue, and the screen lights up. The letters A-C-W-F fade onto the screen, and the crowd cheers. The face of The Jhub appears next, and the crowd goes wild! The Jhub fades out and Jason Dread appears next, earning more cheers. The next still image is of a bloody but smiling Mike Hardy, who gets an enormous pop from the Italian crowd! Hardy fades out and Toad’s image appears, wielding a steel chair, and the reaction turns sour. Twizzy appears next in a still image of him standing in the ring over Toxic’s body, holding the shards of his broken piggy bank, and the crowd erupts. Finally, the confident face of Octavian appears in an extreme close-up on screen, and the crowd EXPLODES! The reaction is mixed but easily the loudest of them all. After a few moments, it becomes apparent that this isn’t a still image, but a live shot of Octavian backstage. The camera backs out to find Octavian clad in a perfect suit, proudly wearing the Imperial Roman Championship belt around his waist. The belt sparkles as Octavian addresses the crowd in his native tongue of Italian, while English subtitles appear on the bottom of the screen. Octavian- In just seven days, we will travel to my home town of Rome to do battle for the Heavyweight Championship of the World. And I, Gaius Octavianus, will prove not only to my home fans but to the world that I am the greatest athlete in professional wrestling today! Octavian pauses to listen to another explosive reaction, still mixed but extremely loud. Octavian- Tonight I will show each and every one of you involved in that ACWF match, but especially Jason Dread, why I have the homefield advantage. I’ve competed in and won an ACWF match. I’ve never been beaten in my home town. And the Roman people, the great people of Italy, will be there to cheer on their hero, their champion… their GOD! Octavian smiles and raises his arms into the air confidently, and this time the reaction comes in much more negative. Despite the boos Octavian winks at the camera as the image fades out, and the GCW logo flashes on screen. After a moment, ‘Wake Up’ begins to play and the arena explodes in fire! Blue and silver pyro explodes on stage as fireworks pop overhead in the rafters. The lights come up and the camera opens to a long shot of the packed crowd, with the caption ‘Venice, Italy,’ on the side. As the cameras fly over the cheering Italian crowd, the familiar voice of James Bryan chimes in. Bryan- Global Championship Wrestling truly has gone Global, ladies and gentlemen, and we’re here in beautiful Venice, Italy, kicking off GCW’s first ever tour of Europe! Buona sera, everyone, James Bryan here alongside the legendary Mr. Danger, and we’re set for the last episode of WorldWide before ACWF next week! Danger- And I’m pumped, JB! I’m here in Italy, doing the job I love to do with the company I love to do it with, I’ve got the rest of the week to visit Italy, and then I get to sit ringside at the biggest show of all time! It doesn’t get much better than this, JB! Bryan- We’ve got well over twenty thousand fans here tonight to get their last glimpse of GCW before we go to crown a new Heavyweight Champion next Wednesday in Rome! Next week, in the Asphalt, Wrenches, Chains and Forklifts match, six men go at it in the most brutal environment ever imagined for a chance to become champion of the world! Danger- Well, we just heard from Octavian, and undoubtedly he’s got the home field advantage, and I think the smart money’s on him to win it all, JB! He won ACWF Four, he’s the only three time world champion in history, he’s wrestling in front of his home town fans, and he’s the Imperial Roman Champion to boot! Bryan- No doubt Octavian is willing to do whatever it takes to become champion of the world, but he might not have the upper hand like you think. That title he has around his waist doesn’t mean anything, and it seems his fellow countrymen don’t all worship him like he thinks they do. But without doubt, especially after what he did to Jason Dread last week, Octavian seems poised to take himself to the limit to get what he wants. Danger- Speaking of Dread, he’s here tonight, and he’ll be at ACWF next week! Dread has been looking great since his return, and he was probably once one of the favorites to win the GCW World Title. But after the assault perpetrated by Octavian last week, I don’t think Dread will be in the kind of shape necessary to win the match. Bryan- Dread has indeed looked fantastic in the past few weeks, but I think you’re right, Danger, Dread’s suffered a major setback after being assaulted by Octavian last week. You can never count the former World Champ out, and Dread wants badly to prove to Octavian that he deserves to be mentioned alongside the greatest champions ever. Danger- Then of course you’ve got The Jhub, and we all know he won’t be winning this thing… Bryan- Come on, you want to talk about favorites? Here’s your guy. He’s a veteran of the ACWF match, it’s his match! He invented this match, but this time he faces his biggest challenge to date. He’s got to face five other men in this match, but if anyone knows ACWF, it’s The Jhub. Danger- But he’ll have to get through Toad! Toad has been quietly dominant in GCW to date. And not only is he going to prove to the world that he’s the best wrestler, he wants to get his Hardcore title back from The Jhub, too! Bryan- Twisted is the fifth man in this match, and the fan favorite is hell bent on getting his World Title back. You wouldn’t know it by looking at him, but you’ve got to believe Twizzy is going to come to play at ACWF in an attempt to regain the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Danger- He’ll come to play, all right… Play with his freakin’ piggy bank! He’s positively out of his mind, JB, but I hate to say that gives him a tremendous advantage. Bryan- And, of course, the shocker! Mike Hardy defeated five other men last week and earned the last spot in the ACWF match next week! Hardy has never been in this kind of main event environment before, and he’s never received a shot at the World Title! And you’d better believe he’s never been in this kind of hardcore environment before. But even you, Mr. Danger, cannot deny that Mike Hardy is on a roll! Danger- He’s on a roll, all right, but he’s got too many people gunning for him, JB. You think those other five guys want to see little Mike Hardy come in and leave with the World Title? I don’t think so. Hardy’s on a roll, but I just can’t see him leaving ACWF a winner. Bryan- But you have to credit his heart, Mr. D. Mike Hardy is going to bring everything he’s got to try to shock the world and become GCW champion. The fans are behind him, and the entire world is backing Mike Hardy in this one. Danger- He has no chance. My money’s on Octavian. Who you got, JB? Bryan- I don’t want to pick… Danger- Come on, who ya’ got? Bryan- OK, if I have to choose, I’m going with Hardy. Danger- A fool and his money, JB… Bryan- Well, now that that’s out of the way, we’re ready to start the show! For the first time in a few weeks, Peter Maxwell made his way out to ringside. As per the usual, there was no music, no pyro. Nothing but the boos of the fans cascading from the entire arena. Maxwell stopped at the rampway, and smiled at the fans greeting. He stopped a moment, and closed his eyes to soak it all in. The boos turned to cheers, as the unlikely hero in NBK bounced out of the crowd, and jumped into the ring, staring down Maxwell. For his part, Maxwell stood his ground, but he smiled at NBK in the ring, as the Natural Born Killer put a microphone to his lips. NBK- I’ve been doing a bit of thinking, Pete. And since I was screwed last week out of the ACWF match next week, I’ve really got nothing going on. So, I thought to myself, who could I take some of this anger out on? Who doesn’t have a match for ACWF VI? Maxwell shrugged, and said something toward the ring, but, couldn’t be heard. If you’re a lip reader, you’ll know. It wasn’t complimentary towards Mister Carter, and quite honestly, should have been censored. NBK- So, I looked up and down the roster, and the flight itineraries, and saw that Peter Maxwell wasn’t even going to bother attending the event. Can you believe that? The man who destroyed the Jhub wasn’t even going to bother to watch an event that he’s undefeated in? The crowd booed at the mention of the Maxwell/Jhub match that cost GCW quite a bit of time away from the spotlight as Steven Caldera fought off the demons of the PTC, and federal government. Maxwell smiled, and basked in the boos again, taking a bow, and thanking NBK for the memories. NBK- And when you went off to become a big superstar in some shitty dead promotion, most of us were languishing back, waiting for GCW to return, so we could hold you personally responsible. Understand this, Maxwell. I don’t give a rats ass about these fans. They can cheer me, or they can boo me, and I’m not going to lose any sleep at night. But if these fans love me because I want to tear your jerky head from your neck, then let them cheer. The fans exploded for NBK, probably for the first time in a long, long time. NBK nodded toward Maxwell, who’s face turned from enjoyment to anger. NBK- So, Caldera was gracious enough to grant me a match with you, at ACWF VI, with just two stipulations. The fans waited with baited breath. NBK- This match is going to be a no holds allowed match. That means, that all those headlocks you’ve been perfecting the last few weeks? Can’t do them. Wanna put me in an armbar? Can’t happen. Wanna try and get me to submit to a figure four leglock? Sorry, Charlie. No moves, means no moves. It means that just you and I, outside the arena. Mano e mano. Man to man. We’re going to settle this thing once and for all… HARD CORE STYLE. Maxwell turned livid and started marching down the aisleway, visibly pissed. The fans blew the roof off the joint, finally realizing that they were going to have their first look at the Dark Messiah since GCW re-opened. Maxwell kicked the guardrail, and stood halfway between the ring and the ramp. NBK- But, that’s not all, Pete. I also got it in writing that Caldera has mandated that each wrestler MUST have theme music, and MUST have an entrance video. Since I knew you’d be resistant, I took the opportunity to pick some music for you that I think’s fitting. Let’s take a look at the new entrance for Peter Maxwell! From the rampway, fourteen dancers in sequined tops and pants came running out like cheerleaders, each one dancing to a techno beat that was playing in the background. They surrounded Maxwell, as he looked to the ring in anger. And then it happened. The beat turned from an anomolous one to one even more famous. Maxwell’s face turned pale as he turned around and found himself eye to video screen eye with a man he hated more than life itself. Justin Timberlake. Don’t be so quick to…walk away… (Dance with me) I wanna rock your body…this day… (Dance with me) You don’t have to admit you…wanna play… (Dance with me) Just let me rock you…to the break of day… (Dance with me) Maxwell covered his mouth, as the dancers around him shook their asses. The former Dark Messiah looked like he was going to launch into convulsions, as he tried to run from the ring, but was cut off by the dancers shaking their tits in his face. There was only one way out of this, and Maxwell knew it. He grabbed the closest dancer by the arm, and lifted her into the air. The arena froze as Maxwell had the girl in the air by her shoulders. And to the shock of the entire audience, Maxwell spun the dancer around, set her on the ground and started to pop ‘n’ lock. He stood and gave his best Justin Timberlake impression, and as far as Justin Timberlake impressions go, it was pretty good. NBK, 15,000 fans in the arena, and millions around the world stood in utter silence as the former Hardest of the hardcore bastards (Sorry, Toad) danced along to Justin Timberlake’s “Rock Your Body”. With reckless abandon, the Dark Messiah lip synched with the song, to the horror of NBK, and the fans in attendance. The gag that NBK tried to pull had backfired in his face, and it was his turn to turn red with embarassment. It was obvious that someone told Maxwell what was going to happen, and Maxwell played it up huge. The song came to an end, and the fans in the arena had no choice but to stand up and boo. Everyone hates Justin Timberlake. It’s a fact of life. And the fact that Peter Maxwell had taken the time to learn the entire dance routine that was planned out to be used against him made him an even bigger tool in the eyes of the GCW faithful. Maxwell- Come on, NBK. Jizzle is my nizzle, for shizzle, dizzle. You didn’t think I could throw down? Well, nigga, yo ass just got served. …BITCH. Maxwell threw down the mic, flipped NBK a bird and walked off while NBK stood in stunned silence in the ring. The most uncomfortable moment of the GCW chatroom couldn’t compare to this feeling in the arena. So, let’s go to commercial, ok? Thanks. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The music of a former Champion, a role model, an energetic, fun-loving, comedic man begins to play over the loud speakers. Yes, “Life’s Sweet Drug” by Slash’s Snakepit fills the arena as The Junkie Man himself, Twisted, walked down the ramp in his black baggy jeans and an old John Reed T-shirt. Twizzy waved to the crowd and walked up to the ring apron. He jumps up on it and then entered the ring through the ropes. He walks briskly to the other side and reaches through the ropes as he is handed a microphone. Immediately putting it to his face, he shouts- Twisted – The power of Twistodium compels you! The crowd roars with cheers. Twisted – So I’m going to be in a match for the GCW World Title at ACWF win five other characters. Octavian… A loud reaction for the long time GCW veteran. Twisted – Jason Dread… The crowd cheers what… you… dread… Twisted – Toad… Some booing for the core of hardcore. Twisted – The Jhub… Cheers up the wazoo for Jhubin. Twisted – and Mike Hardy… Lots of cheers for GCW’s resident underdog. Twisted – At ACWF the six of us are going to be set in a ring made of death, blood, and icky, morbid things like that. I, for one, am looking forward to it. I’ve got my bottle of Twistodium in one hand and I’ll have a wrench in the other. Whoever leaves the match the champ is going to have my respect, sure. I know that I have faith that I’ll leave with the title, and probably a few welts and bruises along with it. The crowd cheers a bit for Twizzy’s confidence. Twisted – So to those five guys in ACWF, just know that I’m coming… all over your face! The crowd pops wildly at the innuendo. Twisted – But what I really want to talk about is Toxic. The crowd boos loudly. Twizzy started to mock his rival. Twisted – You’re not gonna get the World Title. He then looks right into the camera, sticks out his tongue, and spits. Twisted – After ACWF Tox, you and I will have words! You made me break my treasure and that isn’t going to stand! So, World Title or not, after ACWF I’m coming… all over your face! The crowd pops again at the joke, but mainly at the threat made by Twizzy. Twisted – Now I’m about to wrestle Michael Stevens, a man I respect. He has the coolest hair in the world! I want bright red hair like that! I mean, hell, he’s the Dennis Rodman of pro wrestling! How cool is that? Twizzy smiles brightly. Twisted – But “Spitfire” you’re going to lose this match, I assure you. I’m not just good at reading treasure maps, selling T-shirts, and being charismatic. I can also wrestle you so good that your hair’ll turn white! The audience pops. Twisted – So unless you’ve got a goat liver back there that you want to trade to me for a forfeit win then you better get down here and be ready with some hair dye! Again, the crowd pops. Twisted – Because Goat… is… Good! Cheers fill the arena as Twizzy tosses the microphone out to ringside and waits for Michael Stevens to walk out. Twizzy stands in the ring, stretching out a bit as he prepares for his opponent’s entrance. It doesn’t take long, as “I want to rule the world” by Bad Religion begins blaring from the speakers and unleashing the notes to the crowd. ‘Spitfire’ then steps from the curtains, sneering at Twizzy as he makes his way down the ramp. As Stevens reaches a few steps from the apron a fan throws something at him, hitting him in the shoulder. When Stevens turns to look at the fan, Twizzy charges and lands a baseball slide, knocking Stevens down and rolling around. Twizzy stalks Stevens, whom quickly reaches his feet. Twizzy throws a hard right hand, which sends Stevens backwards. Now Twisted grabs Michael’s head and tosses him head first into the steel steps, causing him to fly back holding his nose. Twizzy goes for a clothesline, but at the last second Stevens ducks, hitting a drop toe hold, planting Twizzy face first onto the floor. Stevens shakes the early cob webs out quickly, and picks Twizzy up and slides him under the bottom rope, before sliding in himself, and the bell rings. Twisted is the first to get to his feet and drops a right hand to the head of Stevens, but Michael fires back ferociously with a hard right of his own. Twizzy cocks back and lands another, then Stevens does the same, back and forth they go, until Stevens leans in and drives his knee into Twisted’s gut, doubling him over. He takes advantage of this with a sharp forearm uppercut, then quickly hits a knife edge chop, sending a loud slapping sound ringing through the arena. Stevens now gets a hold of Twisted and tosses him to the ropes, as he returns Michael misses a clubbing blow and turns to get laid out by a running clothesline to the chest. Immediately Twisted leaps into the air and hits a leg drop, and quickly goes for a cover. 1 … The referee doesn’t even get to, two before ‘Spitfire’ kicks out. Twizzy just kind of smiles as he brings Stevens back to his feet. He lines Stevens up and delivers a forearm to the face, then another, and another, before pushing Stevens to the turnbuckle. Twizzy charges Stevens, but he moves at the last second sending Twisted into the turnbuckle chest first. Twisted backs up a few steps because of the blow and Stevens immediately takes advantage with a falling neck breaker. Stevens snaps back up, and quickly drives an elbow down hard on Twizzy’s chest. He sharply stands up only to drop another elbow, and then a third for good measure. Stevens stands now and only takes a moment to stare out at the crowd, before grabbing Twisted, who was already trying to pull himself up. He delivers a couple quick jabs, that drive Twisted into the turnbuckle., then he tosses Twisted to the opposite turnbuckle, and the impact makes the ring shake a bit. Michael doesn’t stop there, he stalks Twisted to the other side and gets ready to toss him again, this time that the force he uses cause him to fall to his knees as he tosses Twisted, who hits chest first and immediately is driven backwards and falls to his back, holding his chest. Stevens runs to the corner now, and quickly walks up to the second rope, sitting on the top one. He looks down at Twisted a moment before leaping off, Bret Hart style and driving his elbow into the head of Twisted. Now Stevens gets to his knees, and just rest for a moment, thinking up his next plan of attack. Slowly he pushes himself up, and surprisingly so is Twisted. Stevens drives a boot into the stomach of Twisted, then a second one. Stevens grabs Twisted and tries tossing him to the ropes, but Twisted holds on, and pulls Michael close, driving a quick boot to the stomach , then hitting a DDT! Both men go down, and lay there. The referee begins his mandatory ten count now. At the count of seven, both men pull themselves up, Stevens reaching his feet a bit sooner. He goes for an attack but Twisted stops him in his tracks with a back elbow to the side of the face. Michael goes for it again but is stopped in the exact same way. Stevens gets angry and charges, Twisted drops to one knee and drives a fist to Spitfire’s gut, then quickly stands up, and driving Michael face first into the mat with a bulldog. Twisted wastes no time in lifting Stevens back up, in a front face lock, then he swings his arm over his own head, setting up for a suplex, but instead he spins around, into a variation of a neck breaker. Twisted stands up, and jogs to the turnbuckle, then climbs it much to the joy of the crowd. He stands tall at the top turnbuckle, smiling at the crowd. He then concentrates back to his opponent, then leaps off attempting a double knee drop, but Michael Stevens rolls out the way ! Twizzy lands knees first on the mat, and he howls in pain as he grabs at his legs. ‘Spitfire’ crawls over to the nearest ropes, and pulls himself to his feet. A smirk creases his lips, as he watches Twizzy writhe in pain. Stevens hovers over Twisted now, before driving a boot down onto his knee. He then reaches down and grabs the right leg of Twisted. He drives a boot into the side of the knee first, then he steps over with his left leg and drops his body weight down on the knee. Stevens quickly gets back up and drops down again, causing more pressure and pain. Twisted now rolls over onto his stomach, trying to quiet his own scream of pain, though this doesn’t stop Stevens who goes to his feet. Spitfire reaches down and grabs one foot of Twisted, then lifts it up and slams it down hard onto the mat. Stevens quickly turns Twisted over, then sets him up and puts on the Reverse Fujiwara Armbar. Immediately Twisted begins howling in pain, and shaking his head no as the referee begins asking him if he has had enough. The crowd are all on their feet cheering the addict on now. Twisted begins slamming his fist onto the mat, not quitting but trying to get the pain out of his mind. After a few moments, he reaches out with one hand and pulls himself closer to the ropes, a great showing of strength. He then claws out with his other arm, grasping at the mat and pulling closer once again. The crowd begins chanting out Twisted’s name. “TWIZZY! TWIZZY! TWIZZY!” He slowly inches his way towards the ropes, and the crowd getting more and more pumped with each and every centimeter he grows closer. Cameras are flickering and chants are being screamed. He gets to one arm’s length now, as he fights back the pain. He then pushes his body up one last time, and is just about to dive for the bottom rope when Stevens stands up slightly, but not letting go of the hold and drags Twisted right back to the center of the ring. The crowd dies down knowing this should be the end as Stevens sinks in the hold to a very visible extent. Stevens begins yelling with all his breath for Twisted to give up, and Twisted yells with what he has that it won’t happen. Moments seem like an eternity with this kind of pain, and just as it seems like Twisted is about to give in … “BANG! BANG! BANG!” The big screen flashes on, as letters begin to flash onto the screen, only stopping when they have spelled the name “JOHNNY LEXICON.” The entire crowd stands, if for nothing than to stop from being confused. Michael Stevens face is one of confusion, but he doesn’t completely break the hold. He then begins yelling at the referee, who is now standing. Lexicon reaches the ring apron, and now Stevens releases the hold. He leans over the top rope screaming at Lexicon the newcomer to GCW. The referee is also trying to get Lexicon to return to the back, as he has no business here. Lexicon doesn’t back down though, instead he climbs up the steel steps and onto the apron, where he stands face to face with the former United States Champion. Lexicon doesn’t say a word, only gives a slight, but arrogant smile. It is Michael Stevens who is furious and is jawing away. To Steven’s surprise the crowd begins going wild, he looks to his left and to his right a bit annoyed that the fans would cheer this new guy. Then Lexicon laughs and points right behind Stevens. He doesn’t get a chance to turn, as he is forced to turn around by a now standing Twizzy, who kicks him in the gut and then delivers the PILL POPPER!! The crowd goes nuts as Lexicon drops from the apron and begins walking back to the locker room. Twisted lays an arm over Stevens, and although a bit confused by what just went on, the ref drops and counts 1 … 2… 3 !! Winner: Twizzy The crowd explodes with cheers as ‘Life’s Sweet Drug’ cues up again. Twizzy sits up with a dumbfounded smile on his face, staring at Lexicon. Lexicon ignores Twizzy as he slowly backs up the ramp. Twizzy shrugs and slides out, slapping hands with the fans as he leaves the ring. After a few moments Stevens manages to sit up, and sees Lexicon still standing on top of the stage. Stevens begins muttering obscenities as he pulls himself up. Lexicon simply shouts ‘Come and get me!’ and turns to leave. Stevens nods in agreement, and steps out of the ring to find him. As the scene cuts to the parking lot, where tourists mass around an entrance to us far away, we are immediately greeted by the long, luminous image of a stretch limousine as it pulls slowly into the Venice parking-esque lot. As the limo comes to a complete stop, the driver steps out in his uniformed black suit and drudges to the back door. Nevertheless, right as he approaches, the door swings open and smacks dead into his abdomen. As the limo driver loses his wind, out steps the slick Rocco Diaz, one-half of The Mambo Kings. He is dressed in an ostentatious teal sequence button-up shirt that is left buttoned to about his belly button that lays over a pair of teal dress pants. His chest hair rushes from beneath the fabric as he runs a hand over his oiled head of jet black hair. He takes a long whiff of the air around him and cringes... Rocco Diaz – Mang, I hate Italy. As the driver stands, limply holding out a hand to usher Rocco into the arena in the background, the Cuban throws a scowl and checks himself before moving from the doorway... Rocco Diaz – Tony, let’s do ‘dis. As Rocco moves, Antony Livestra exits the door of the limo, dressed in a white t-shirt that says “I LOVE ITALY” with flamingo pink bellbottoms. His large, muscular frame tightens the tee as he flips his ear-length Rick James curls a little. As the driver shuts the door and breathes deeply, regaining his breath, The Mambo Kings begin to walk slowly to the arena double doors. However, soon GCW’s very own Kevin Jacobs catches them with a microphone held tightly in his fist... Kevin Jacobs – Rocco...Antony...Tonight is your WorldWide debut in GCW. What are you thoughts about debuting, being as we are only one week from ACWF VI? This stops Rocco, who turns with one hand slicking back his oiled hair. However, his partner doesn’t stop walking and Rocco has to comically halt Antony and bring him back before chuckling to speak... Rocco Diaz – Let me tell choo sumting, mang. My thoughts are simple. My thoughts are ‘dat we are one week from ACWF. But, ‘da world of GCW need to look at ‘dis as one week till ‘Da Mambo Kings are....uh...how choo say it, Tony? Antony stares a hole through Kevin Jacobs as he speaks into the mic... Antony Livestra – Unleashed, mang. Rocco Diaz – ‘Dats right. Choo know? ‘Da Mambo Kings gunna’ do a two-step on ‘dis whole friggin’ ‘stablishment, mang. Kevin Jacobs – A two-step? Rocco Diaz – Don’t interrupt me, mang. Tony an’ myself are not two men to push aside. We from Cuba— Antony Livestra – CUBAS! Rocco Diaz - --Where ‘da peoples of the ‘Mericas try to bring my country down. Antony Livestra – DOWN! Rocco Diaz – So, choo tell everyone ‘dat we are here. We watch tonight. But, in one week. (He chuckles.) In one week, we return the favor to the ‘Mericas. Rocco and Tony! Antony Livestra – ROCCO AND ME, MANG! Kevin Jacobs – Well, as I’m sure you’ve heard, Steven Caldera has been cracking down lately on unnecessary roughness and insubordination. What do you plan to do? Rocco Diaz – What do we plan to do? Tony, show him. As if on command, Tony Livestra turns and goes at Kevin Jacobs as if to attack. Jacobs curls backwards in defense, or more like hiding from the storm about to rage, however, Antony moves past him to the limo driver who is waiting impatiently by his limo. He looks around as if Tony is going to someone else, but the stiff right hand into his chest speaks differently. Antony proceeds to land several punches into the limo driver’s gut. As Rocco walks over, Antony lifts the skinny limo driver into a gorilla press and tosses him atop the limousine’s hood, creating a large crater in the metal. Rocco walks over and chokes the limo driver with a strong right hand before Antony yanks the limo driver off the hood, his clothes slipping off of him as he goes cold, and Antony spins him and launches him straight through the driver’s side window. Glass shatters into thousands of micro shards as Rocco laughs. After a moment of strutting and high-fives between the tag team, Rocco turns back to Kevin Jacobs who now turns to scramble from the scene as if the team might turn on him at any second. Rocco struts and does a little dance before raisng his arms in cowardly triumph and shouts... Rocco Diaz – Whoah, wait, mang! Hey, Jacobs! You tell them. Tell everyone that Rocco givs’ his regars’. GCW – ‘DA MAMBO KINGS ARE HERE – CHOO IN TROUUUUUBBBBBBLLLLLLLE! As Antony laughs goofily behind his partner, Rocco devilishly grin at the camera as if he can’t get enough of his little sadistic assault. The scene fades to black and commercials. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ “Established” cues up in the arena, and the Venice crowd explodes! President Caldera emerges from the back sporting a noticeable lump on his head. Caldera wears a pair of blue jeans and a black shirt rather than his normal suit, and seems to be in a serious mood. He offers the crowd a polite smile as he heads to the ring, walking gingerly the entire way. Bryan- Welcome back to WorldWide, ladies and gentlemen, and we’re being joined now by President Caldera! Caldera is furious after being assaulted by Toxic last week, and I’ve got word he’s coming out here to address the twenty-one year old phenom. Octavian immediately pulls Hardy up and nails him with a huge Roman Devastator, and the crowd now begins booing Octavian completely! Octavian gets to his feet again and angrily shouts at the motionless Hardy before reluctantly leaving the ring. Octavian continues to shout obscenities as he snatches his title belt back and begins leaving the ring area. Toad has left The Jhub hurt on the outside of the ring, where security has broken up the fight. Toad reluctantly begins to leave as well, as a slightly wounded Jhub slides back into the ring. The Jhub looks down at the fallen Mike Hardy lying on his back in the middle of the ring. The Jhub raises an eyebrow to the crowd, and moves towards Hardy. The crowd begins to buzz as The Jhub grabs Hardy’s legs, and steps through! The Jhub stops here, staring right down at Hardy, who has begun to come to. Hardy looks up at The Jhub standing ready to lock him in the sharpshooter. The Jhub mutters something to Hardy and Hardy responds, as the crowd boos heavily! The Jhub finally reaches down and pulls Hardy back to his feet, holding him in a solid handshake. The crowd cheers as ‘My Own Summer’ hits again, and Hardy and The Jhub stand face to face. Hardy attempts to turn away but Jhub holds onto his hand, pulling him back into the staredown. Hardy’s face grows serious as he realizes the next time he sees The Jhub, it’ll be for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship……… Winners: The Jhub & Mike Hardy
