Biography |Overview| Dr. Kasidy Williams Drake III (PhD), is a professional wrestler, entrapanuer and biochemist known primarily for his appearences in Core Wrestling from 2004 to 2005 as his alter-ego, Smiley, and as an authority figure in the Atlantic Wrestling in 2006. He also had a brief run for Bubba McCoy's Global prior to its closure in 2007 under his own name. Drake also founded and operated Drakewerx Pharmacudicals, a publically-traded drug company from 2005 until he was voted off of the Board of Directors and resigned in 2010. Drake discovered the Prometheus Serum, a legal performanc enhancing drug that intensified adrenal gland activity providing users with boosted strength and agility. The drug became heavily-used by several wrestlers including Drake himself ultimately resulting in the death of former AWC Transatlantic Champion, "The Mongoose" Alexander Strider and Drake's own rapidly deteriorating health. In January of 2012, Drake applied for and was granted a wrestling license from the Philadelphia Athletic Board leaving the door open for his return to pro wrestling. |Early Life| Kasidy Williams Drake III was born to Gina Monroe and Kasidy Williams Drake Jr. on March 15, 1981 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. His father was a also a chemist who died when Drake was very young in an unsolved murder which has since become a cold case. His mother who suffered from severe xenophobia and could not go out to make a living married an ex-boyfriend, Butch Scott, a construction worker from New Jersey. The relationship between Gina a Butch quickly turned tumulteously abusive and became riddled with alcohol abuse soon after they married. Drake, a young and scrawny nerd with little courage of his own could only watch as his mother and step-father constantly threatened each other with knives and guns in many a drunken argument. Drake's lack of a social life in school and poor luck with women throughout his teenaged years turned him into a very repressed and distant young man when he entered the California Institute of Technology at age 16 in 1997 double-majoring in biological sciences and chemistry. The ills of his dark childhood and the pressures of being a sixteen-year-old in one of the nation's top schools weighed heavily on Kasidy as he wrote in his 2006 memoir "Smile With Style: Achievement, Addiction and Attonement". Drake writes of this period "I began to dream of suicide by the time I was a sophmore. I'd spend so much energy planning how I was going to do it. I just didn't want to be sad and lonely anymore. In the end, I was too much of a coward for pain to do it". In spite of himself, Drake excelled in his academics and became completely obsessed with his schoolwork to distract him from the psycholigcal issues he refused to confront. "I just didn't have time to worry about those things," he continues to write in his book. "I was in a competitive environment and my brain has always been strange. Even in the worst of circumstances it always wants to absorb information-- disect it. If I was going to be too much of a wuss to put myself out of my misery, I decided, I was at least going to do one thing right in my life and that was my work". Drake impressed many of his professors and although by the time he graduated from CIT in the year 2001 he was having a mental breakdown from repressing so many of his feelings. |Discovering the Serum| Drake's ever-growing bubble of stress within him began to show signs of bursting after he witnessed the 9/11 attacks on television during his internship with GlaxoKlineSmith in New York. Somehow the thought of that many people dying within a flash and the state of his mother who had recently began to show the signs of severe early-onset alzheimers. He quit his internship and decided to return to the only place that seemed to sufficently distract him from the pain-- school. Having never truly developed a mechanism for dealing with major life-altering events like his mother's illness and the terrorist attacks of that year he poured himself into school once more attended graduate school at Drexel University in his hometown of Philadelphia and completed a rigorous dual masters/PhD program in biochemistry. He began doing extensive research on the human adrenal gland after accidentally piecing together the early pieces of the formula that would later become the Prometheus Serum and change the lives of many. The title of his doctorate was "The Potential for the Willful-Manipulation of the Human Adrenal Gland and Its Applications". The paper was widely-panned by fellow scientists who pointed out that the "catalyst" substance Drake describes in his thesis is purely theoretical. After completing the program at Drexel early in early-2006, Drake found himself now over-qualified for most positions he would have liked to enter into having served no internship and now 25 he was forced to work a lowly entry-level position at Maxwell Pharacuedicals based in Philadelphia. He tried to get his superiors to look at his research into adrenal manipulation but was again met with jeers. Still, with nothing left to care about in his life besides his research he poured himself into figuring out a way to prove them wrong. In 2006, the space shuttle Discovery returned to Earth after a near two-week mission at the International Space Station. Upon safe re-entry an unknown granite-like material was found lodged into a crevice in the re-entry capsule. Nicknamed Prometheite since it had come to Earth for the first time in STS-166's firey re-entrance as though fire from the Gods, it was discovered to be a powerful explosive and the US military contracted Maxwell Labs to explore its possible applications. Drake, who was not on the team initially volunteered once he discovered evidence that it may share properties with the theoretical "Element X" he had described in his thesis. After some up-close observations he decided the substance would work and stole a sample to figure out a way to make a snythetic version. For this, he was fired. Drake brewed the first batch of the Prometheus Serum in his apartment building in June of that year. With no test subjects at his disposal, he tested it on himself. To his delight it worked and afforded him near-superhuman strength and speed but also seemed to induce severe mental side-effects including paranoia, schizofrenic tendencies and periods of extreme rage coupled with random spurts of violent behavior. Once the serum wore off, however, Drake returned to normal. Wanting to find a way to improve his work he took the only high-paying job he could get at the time and became a backstage interviewer for Core Wrestling. He schemed to double-dip and work as a masked wrestler as well under a pseudonym, Smiley, while under the influence of the serum. |Core Wrestling| Smiley made his wrestling debut on June 29, 2004 on Core Wrestling Smash 57 against Layne Sorensen. With no real training he mostly brawled during his match-up but with the use of the Prometheus Serum he was able to overtake Sorenson by submission despite Sorenson having training and experience and Drake having none. His win turned heads and he began to engage in bizzare segments during shows displaying all possible signs of pure insanity. He lost the following week to Brock Rockhard after attempting to incoroperate more wrestling into his moveset unsucessfully. He continued to do backstage interviews for the promotional simultaneously when he was sober. For several months no one-- not even managment knew that the dorky and scrawny interviewer who often was abused by some of his subjects was becoming an increasingly sadistic brawling monster in the ring. The only person Drake told was his best friend and landlord, Dexter "Dex" Williams. Dex began to vocally worry about Drake's growing side-effects from the serum but Drake had begun to learn that he really enjoyed competing and refused to stop. He also met his first girlfriend, Cassidy DeMoore, an author. Though still haunted by his past and the consequences of what he was doing to himself in the ring, he was making tremendous amounts of money and was the most content he had ever been. Continuing to refine the formula he became ever-more violent and crazed in the ring winning a tag team match with Tyler Jackson against Tony Aielli and Hawke Poe at Smash 59 and Artemis on the final edition of Core Smash on July 27, 2004 with his signature move, the Last Smile. He went on to win #1 Contendership for the Core Classic Championship defeating Hawke Poe once again on PPV on All's Fair In Love and War 2004. The following week he defeated Veronica Paige, love interest of the top star of the company, Jason Hughes, and captured the Classic Championshp on the first edition of Core's newest premiere show, Pride. Drake continued to care for his mother behind the scenes and struggled to maintain a stable relationship with Cassidy as his sanity and physical health in his sober state continued to decline. Drake, now became a full on addict of the drug and his insane alter-ego "Smiley" began to creep more and more into his everyday life. Drake became increasingly aware that he was being monitored by some unseen force finding himself often followed by blacked-out cars and looked at a little too long on the street. He lost the Classic Championship to Brian Wes the following week during an incident where he was unable to brew the Prometheus Serum. Cassidy left him after finding out the truth about his wrestling lives and he lost again on the next editon of Pride to Adoph Nilsson and again in a tag team match two weeks later. Drake, in a drug-induced rage snapped and killed Cassidy during a halucination. At this point Drake met former NGW champion, Calvin Riley, who had formed a cult and was running a sovereign island off the East Coast as a country. Riley, who had assembled some of the world's most renowned scientists claimed that he believed he could bring Cassidy back to life with the serum. Riley offered to try but only on the condition that Drake hire him as a manager and become his slave. Riley treated Drake like an animal having him make his ring entrance in a cage and acting like his handler. Smiley entered the Core Wrestling Ringmaster tournament with the winner earning a shot at the top title, the Legacy Championship. Drake made it to the finals after defeating former-champion (at the time) Jason Hughes in the semis. He lost in the finals to Nick Worrell and at that point the damage being done to Drake's body from the serum took its toll. After discovering Riley had lied about being able to revive Cassidy the two of them fueded eventually having a match on an edition of Pride. Victorious but ultimately heartbroken over his love's demise and his lost at Ringmaster, Drake retired soon after establishing Drakewerx Pharmacudicals deciding to rededicate himself to fixing his Prometheus Serum. |Atlantic Wrestling Club and Global Wrestling| A few months after his departure from Core and with Drakewerx putting the Prometheus Serum on the market despite knowning its long-term effects, Drake was contacted by a former Core wrestler, Tim Shipley, whom Kasidy had become good friends with on the road. Shipley informed him that the relatively-new promotion, the Atlantic Wrestling Club, was having a huge financial crisis due to over-inflated contracts and was looking to sell a stake in the company. Drake, decidedly retired but still deeply bitter about the failures of his own wrestling career purchased a 51% stake in AWC and appointed himself General Manager. Drake also tried to manage several tag teams while in the AWC including UNITY, a duo of female luchadors from Compton, and Rainbow Flag, a gay tag team that had recently made headlines for being outed as lovers in their hometown of Leetown, Missouri. Both teams ultimately flopped and Drake then introduced a powerhouse from Sierra Leone named LeVar Kintu. He went even further to ensure this protege would be successful and coerced him into using the Prometheus Serum, the current incarnation of which seemed relatively stable. Drake and Kintu caused havoc in the AWC for several months and Drake began to use Kintu as his own personal patient zero trying to find out why the serum hadn't harmed his body in spite of nearly-crippling Drake. There had been no deaths from the medication on the market and he thought that he had at last comleted his dream of creating a stable formula. Kintu, however, died abruptly along with former AWC Transatlantic Champion, Alexander "The Mongoose" Strider. This infuriated AWC management but there was nothing they could do as Drake had purchased most of the company's stock. Meanwhile, the aquisition was starting to loose Drakewerx massive amount of money as AWC, still pretty new, was not drawing large enough crowds and merchandise sales to offset their arena and production costs. Drake left the AWC and vanished from the wrestling scene for several more months until, in a final bout of rage and frustrations in the AWC and his own company's Board of Trustees which had given him a vote of no-confidence, Drake purchased the other 49% of AWC's stock and then shut down the company out of spite. Kasidy stepped down from his position as CEO and Chairman in 2008 and decided to give wrestling one more go. His body was weak and he couldn't get his body weight up past 200. At this point in his life he knew that he was going to die young because of the serum and had given up drugs in honor of Caissdy. Wrestling under his own name, he went on a decent winning streak being blessed and cursed by a side effect of the serum. His adrenal glands began to secrete dangerously low amount of adrenaline but when that adrenaline was released, it was ten times more potent than the normal human beings giving Drake quick burst of the power that had once made his a champion. In spite of his valor, Drake was once again rebuffed by fate when Global closed later that year. He was reinstated by Drakewerx in 2009 amid the near-collapse of the company. Unable to help them, it soon became clear that Drake's ownership of AWC was creating a black hole in the company's finances. He was again fired in 2010 and the new CEO of the company Jack Greene sold the company back to its original owner, David "Pearl" Harber. Drake has not been seen or heard from since. Despite his apparent disappearence, on August 9, 2011, several families of people who had suddenly begun dying from using his serum filed a class action law suit against him and his former company.
