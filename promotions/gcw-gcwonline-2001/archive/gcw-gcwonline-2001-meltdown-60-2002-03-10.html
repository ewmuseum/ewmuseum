Meltdown 60, 6 June 2002

Meltdown 60 Oregon State Fairgrounds Pavilion — Salem, Oregon ---- Meltdown #60 Established hits in the arena to kick off Meltdown, and the crowd explodes with boos. The GCW logo flashes on the bottom of the screen as President Caldera emerges onto the stage, wearing a beautiful new business suit. A thin frown rests just below his clean features, causing the usually regal President to appear quite grim. James Bryan chimes in as the fans boo the President’s immediate arrival. Bryan- Welcome everyone to Meltdown! Thank you for joining us following Shockwave, which turned out to be a truly hellacious night! We’ll catch you up on all that went down, but we don’t even have time for an introduction, as President Caldera is already in the ring! Yale- Don’t sound so annoyed, JB! This is the man who proved once again that he is the real boss of this company, and he can dictate each and every event that occurs in GCW! Caldera, having entered the ring, looks out to the fans. His music fades but the boos do not, and Caldera stands silent, forced to endure the raging anger of the crowd. After a few moments, the boos start to subside, and Caldera begins. Caldera- You know, even I have been known to make mistakes. Even I have made decisions that I regret. Even I have done things against my better judgment. The crowd quiets further at this confession, now curious to hear the President out. Caldera- But one thing I certainly do not regret is raising Octavian fifteen feet into the air at Shockwave to capture his third GCW world championship! The fans explode with boos once again, as a smile comes to Caldera’s face. This time he seems to relish the boos, which once again subside momentarily. Caldera- But there are things I regret. I regret the day I trusted Badger Benandanti… At the mention of Badger’s name, the crowd explodes with cheers. Caldera- I regret trusting Michael ‘Spitfire’ Stevens… More cheers follow the former United States Champion’s name. Caldera- And I regret each and every moment of weakness I have had, when I’ve had Mr. Danger by the throat, and all I’ve had to do is squeeze. In case you haven’t heard, there is a strong possibility that tonight could be the final GCW show of all time. The end of the empire, the end of an era… the end of the Caldera era. This meets with a mixed reaction, some fans excited to see Caldera go, others not wanting the company to end. Caldera- Almost every month, I have had to right some wrong. Almost every month I’ve run this company, I’ve had to make my presence felt and I’ve had to alter the direction of my creation. Do you have any idea what that’s like? To run a company full of men who are totally incapable of representing it the way it deserves to be? Rich Rollins, Shane Lane, The Jhub, John Reed, Tristain Mayhem, Jason Dread, Badger Benandanti… So many names are forever transcribed in the annals of the GCW world title. Hell, The Jhub’s name is in there twice! Do you have any idea what that means to the title I created? And if you look though that list of names, you’ll see that each and every time that belt has changed hands, I have been there to move it. But I’m tired of having champions who need help to be at the top. I’m tired of seeing weakness reign in the higher echelons of my company. I’m tired of having to take matters into my own hands. Why should I focus on maintaining a respectable representative for my company, when it’s infected with a virus like Mr. Danger? That is why I have intervened for the last time… That is why Octavian is and forever will be your GCW World Heavyweight Champion! The crowd erupts with an incredible array of boos, as a flashback hits the MegaTron. JB narrates as it plays. Bryan- Well, fans, in case you missed Shockwave, President Caldera did indeed return to GCW television to help Octavian defeat Badger and The Jhub to capture his third GCW title in a hellacious ACWF match. After seeing Caldera raise Octavian to victory, the camera heads back to the ring. Caldera- But I guess I haven’t really answered the big question yet, have I? Is tonight the last ever GCW show? The answer is… Take a Look Around hits, and the fans leap out of their seats. Cheering wildly, the crowd greets Mr. Danger, who struts to the ring looking a bit worried. A microphone in his hand, Danger quickly heads to the ring to confront a rather aggravated Caldera. Bryan- And it seems that Mr. Danger has a thing or two to say before Caldera makes his decision! At Shockwave, Danger came down to take out Caldera, and even ran him down with a car! You can see Caldera is a bit banged up as a result, but he still managed to foil Danger’s plans to keep it a fair fight, and managed to crown Octavian the champion. Danger enters the ring, and Caldera begins imposing some noticeable restraint. Danger ignores the crowd as his music fades, and begins addressing Caldera. Danger- Look, Steven, you and I don’t like each other. I can live with that. And I know that at the moment I have a lot of say as to what goes on in this company. But I can’t force you to keep this thing going. GCW runs from your pocket, it’s in your hands. But what I can do is ask you to keep going, not to give up. There are a lot of people who love to see our show every week, and would love to continue to see our show every week. So please, before you give up, consider the people you employ, and the people you entertain. The crowd cheers, agreeing, but Caldera seems infuriated. Caldera- The people?! The people?! The people I employ are a dime a dozen! Those slobs that set up the ring could be replaced in a heartbeat! These wrestlers are talentless pickpockets! And these people who come to my shows are by far the lowest forms of scum I have EVER seen in my life! The fans explode with boos as soon as they hear this, but Caldera doesn’t miss a beat. Caldera-It’s real easy for you to talk about people losing their jobs from your little pedestal where I can’t reach you! It’s easy for you to come out here and speak to me like that when you can’t be fired! But what you don’t realize, Danger, is that I can still kick your ass! Caldera begins to approach Danger, but Danger backs down, trying to calm the President. Danger- Hold on, hold on. Look, I know you’re angry, but there’s got to be another way. Caldera- Damnit, I can’t fire you, but I can end the whole damn company! It’s the only way to be rid of you forever! So as soon as tonight’s show is over… GCW… IS FINISHED! With that, Caldera slams down the mic and rushes past Danger, angrily moving away. The crowd explodes with shock, murmuring loudly. James Bryan- Octavian has his finishing submission move on Stevens, and its right in the middle of the ring!!! THERE IS NOWHERE TO GO!!! David Yale- He’s not giving it up, he won’t quit!!!! James Bryan- Stevens reaches the ropes, and Octavian breaks at the four count. Both men slow to get up, and Octavian in control. Octavian scoops up Stevens and HERE IT COMES!!! David Yale- The ROMAN DEVESTATOR!!!!!! James Bryan- MY LORD THIS HAS TO BE IT!!! Here is the cover. ONE..TWO..THREE!!! David Yale- It’s over!!! OCTAVIAN WINS IT!!!! ( The bell rings as “Might of Rome” hits. The fans give a standing ovation for all the men in this match, and for the champion Octavian for quite possibly the last time ) Joey Andrews- Here is your winner, and STILL, the GCW WORLD CHAMPION, OCTAVIAN!!!!!! James Bryan- In what has got to be one of the damndest matches I have ever seen, Octavian pulls off the win. And what a note to close out GCW! David Yale- Octavian wins the right to be called the last champion in GCW history, and congratulations Octavian! James Bryan- Thanks to all who have helped make GCW a success!!! ( Octavian’s “Might of Rome” is cut short and Steven Caldera’s “Establishment” theme cues up. The fans boo as Caldera makes his way to the ring. ) David Yale- And we are not done yet!! Here comes the boss to put an end, once and forall to his creation.. Octavian stands huffing and puffing in the ring, as Caldera steps through the ropes with the GCW title belt. Octavian takes the belt from Caldera with a smile, and throws it over his shoulder. Caldera is handed a mic as Might of Rome fades, and the frenzied crowd finally quiets. Caldera- Gaius, the night is over, and you’ve proven yourself to the world. You will now be known forever as the first, last, and greatest GCW champion of all time. Congratulations… Octavian and Caldera share a heinous handshake as the crowd boos. However, Take A Look Around hits again, and the crowd explodes with cheers! Mr. Danger appears on stage, microphone in hand, his face looking a bit more confident than his first appearance of the evening. Caldera, annoyed with the interruption, turns to face Danger amid the carnage in the ring. Danger- Now hold on a second. You said that as soon as the show went off the air, GCW was done forever. But we’re not off the air yet, and I’ve got one last idea to try to see to it that we go on the air one more time. Now, despite the fact that me might never see it, Dangerous Games is scheduled to take place on June 30th at Arco Arena in Sacramento. I’ve got an idea that might be able to persuade you to keep us going. Caldera, confident that he cannot be swayed, leans against the ropes to listen. Danger- I’ve drawn up a little contract for you. Should you choose to sign it, you will officially book a match for the main event at Dangerous Games. In that match, you, Steven Caldera, would team up with the World Champion, Octavian, to take on myself and a partner of my choosing. Should I win, you will lose all powers to regulate the longevity of GCW to the board of directors of the company. Caldera- And should we win? Danger- And should you win, I will officially resign from Global Championship Wrestling, and step aside to allow you to dissolve the company forever as you wish. Bryan- Wow, what a situation that would be! The President of the company fighting to kill his own creation! Caldera discusses this with Octavian, actually looking slightly interested. Danger- This way, Steven, all you have to do is beat me. You’d get to prove to the world once again that you are the better man, and you could go out in style. This is what you wanted, isn’t it? To fight me one last time? Octavian nods to Caldera in approval, shrugging nonchalantly. Caldera finally cracks a smile, and looks down at Danger. Caldera- An interesting thought. OK… fine. You’ve got it. I’ll sign your little contract next week on Meltdown. But don’t think for one moment that I won’t pull out all the stops, Danger. This match will go down on my terms. This match will belong to me. And when it’s all over, I will have finally destroyed you, and I will finally be rid of this no good company once and for all. The crowd boos slightly, but actually begins cheering as they realize that GCW will not close its doors tonight! Established hits as Danger and Caldera lock eyes in an intense stare, and the GCW logo appears. Bryan- GCW SURVIVES THIS NIGHT! But how much longer will it last? At Dangerous Games, it will be Steven Caldera and Octavian fighting to destroy the company they created! Until next week this is a thankful James Bryan for a relieved David Yale saying good night, and join us next week for more GCW! The camera fades out in typical GCW fashion, zooming out away from Caldera, Octavian, and Danger, all locked in a heated glare…….
