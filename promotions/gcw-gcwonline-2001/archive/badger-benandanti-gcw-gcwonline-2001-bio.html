Badger Benandanti Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Durham, NC Birthday 5-18-70 Height 5'11'' Weight 212 Fed Titles GCW World Heavyweight Champion, GCW Hardcore Champion, CWU World Heavyweight Champion Biography The second man to be enshrined in the GCW Hall of Fame, Badger Benandanti was the most unique wrestler in the history of the organization. Renowed for his unorthodox wrestling style, quick dry wit and silent midget sidekick, Badger will forever be respected as one of the best wrestlers ever to grace this company's ring. Badger got his start in the Cyber Wrestling Union while Steven Caldera was the acting head of the company. He quickly gained the attention and adoration of the crowd by displaying a wrestling style, later dubbed Bad-Fu, unlike anything they had ever seen. Using a mix of jujitsu, lucha libre and his own unique twists, Badger's star rose quickly. Before long he became entangled in a heated feud with CWU Commissioner Steven Caldera. Caldera made it his mission to hold Badger down, but he could only delay the inevitable. In a four man triple tiered cage match, Caldera somehow managed to steal the CWU World title for himself. The historic feud, which saw the two battling in back allies and with barbed wire, came to an end the next night when Badger defeated Caldera one on one to win the CWU World Title. After the CWU folded, Badger left North America to wrestle in Japan. There he found even greater fame, becoming one of the top names in the country. Months later, however, Badger began to miss America and returned, where he was immediately offered a contract to Global Championship Wrestling. After weeks of negotiating, Badger agreed to return to the ring. He made his debut on an episode of GCW Meltdown, making an immediate impact by attacking GCW legend Rich Rollins. Badger went on to be one of the biggest names in the company. Arguably the best match of his career came in February of 2002, where he took on Khan for the Hardcore title. Khan's unique martial arts approach made him the perfect opponent for Badger, who ultimately prevailed in one of the top five matches in GCW history. It was only a matter of time before Badger got his shot at the GCW World Championship. In a shocking turn of events, Badger united with Steven Caldera to capture the GCW title at Warpath 2002. THe union would be short lived, as with Badger's title reign, but he undoubtedly left his mark on the championship. After GCW's closing, Badger Benandanti left for Japan again. His memory remains, however, and we dedicate this page to his memory. From his antics with the mute midget martial arts guru General Tso to his death-defying maneuvers to his timeless interviews, Badger Benandanti captivated CEI audiences like no other. Perhaps the greatest pure entertainer ever to grace the ring, Badger will forever be loved by his fans and coworkers.
