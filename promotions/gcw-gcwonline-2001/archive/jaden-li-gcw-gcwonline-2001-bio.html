Jaden Li Biography Real Name Jaden Li Hometown Chinatown, San Francisco, California Birthday May 12, 1986 Height 5'10 Weight 215 Biography Jaden Li was born in Chinatown, San Francisco, California in 1986. His father was Chinese who was the chef and owner his own Chinese restaurant, Dragon Express while his mother was an American who was a banker at a bank. Quite the normal family, no? At the age of 9, he lost both his parents in a fatal car accident. They were driving home after attending to a party that the bank was holding as it was a celebration of Jaden’s mother’s promotion. The driver came out of nowhere and side-swiped both of Jaden’s parents off the road and into the guardrail, killing them and the driver instantly. It was soon later discovered that the driver was drunk. A grieving Jaden was sent to live with his Aunt, Mei who soon started to take over his father’s Chinese restaurant. Jaden depressed and unmotivated to live, Mei didn’t want to see her nephew in this pathetic state anymore. That’s when he started to learn Jeet Kwon Do from’you guess it, Aunt Mei. It seemed that she picked up the martial art while she was in California. It’s still a mystery today on who taught his Aunt this martial art. His Aunt believed that this would take his mind off his parents? death and it successfully worked. Now, we’re at the present. Jaden has grown into a young adult at the age of 19, as he’s a senior at the Dynasty Sun High School. He’s the leader of the gang who claim themselves that their the representatives of the High School. Representatives, as if any other gangs from other highs school in Chinatown want to challenge them or what, then they will be there to throw down. His gang is not the bullying neither ordinary as they have a strong sense of honor since their leader, Jaden enforces it heavily. They’re basically popular in their High School as someone can say they’re even popular than the popular students. Of course, some of the students don’t like this. Among the jealous thou there’s a great hatred for the gang especially for Jaden. The Dynasty Sun High School’s Vice President, Chow Gung thinks that Jaden and his gang are nothing more then “bad seeds” that needs to be getting rid of. This isn’t the only reason why that he’s after Jaden. He’s after Jaden because of an interesting mishap. Let’s leave it at. VP Chow Gung would do anything and I do mean anything to see that Jaden and his friends are expelled from the school. On an interesting note, Jaden is said to be a genius. Would never know that with all the times he skipped his classes. But it’s true. Go figure, eh. With all the fights and troubles of high school, Jaden has a secret dream on becoming “The Most Dominant Fighter” in professional wrestling just like his idol, Kenny Cage. He’s been a Kenny Cage fan ever since he was a kid. When he retired undefeated from UTCW (United Titans Championship Wrestling) as the UTCW Heavyweight Champion. This inspired him try out for professional wrestling. He hopes he can be like his idol, but perhaps he hopes to surpass him. Only time will tell if Jaden has the stuff to be??The Most Dominant Fighter?
