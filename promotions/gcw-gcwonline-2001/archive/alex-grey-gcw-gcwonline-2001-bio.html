Alex Grey Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Ales Grey or Grey Hometown Maine Birthday 10/08/80 Height 6'0 Weight 215 lbs Biography Once a member of GCW Alex Grey wrestled as the man named Payne, when GCW Closed Alex fell into a brooding pit of anger and despair, He would fight for the next two years against his bitter rival Lestatt Knight, their violent feud carried from GCW and spilled into xCw, and then was concluded in DWA, since then the two have had a mutual trust in one another. After falling off the map for a time Alex reappeared recently in EPW, Becoming the Evolution Champ he held the belt until the fed closed. Now Alex has come home, trying to forget his past has ultimately led him to where it all began, GCW. Strengths Experience, Alex has been wrestling for years, straight up wrestling, Hardcore, submission, he’s been up and down the wrestling road many times and he’s not afraid to step into the ring with anyone. Weaknesses A Back Injury that may play up once in awhile, Also while he’s wrestled them before he’s not likely to match up as well against a Cruiserweight high flier wrestler, he’d find it hard to defend against since he rarely even climbs the turnbuckle himself Wrestling Style Technical/Brawler Strategy With Alex in the ring you can assume he’s been around long enough to have a well developed ring presence, know where the ropes are to get his foot up on one during a three count, he’d be a superb technical wrestler and an experienced Brawler after all he’s done in his career, and he’s fearless, having said that he’s far from perfect, merely mortal he can screw up like everyone else, he can still lose match and lose it good naturedly. Firemans Carry Entrance Music "Undying" by Demon Hunter Ring Entrances As “Undying” by Demon Hunter hit’s the speakers Alex Grey pushes his way through the curtains and stops atop the ramp, looking like a man who’s come home after a long time away he smiles and takes it all in as his music plays “Ever since the day that I was made I've been deciding the end And I was made of wood and stone and won't diminish or bend So when the heavy hand of death is here to take me away I'll be the solid grip of time, forever holding my stay Nobody ever made a force that took a beating like me I call the earth and every scum to come and try to fight me Cause when I made the choice to live beyond the dirt that we tread I felt the curse of mortal limit fall before it was said? Making his way down the ramp his White Trench coat billows out behind him, a salute to the days when he wore it before, sliding into the ring he removes his jacket tossing it aside, removing his shirt, his upper body has more than its share of scars and burns from a very turbulent wrestling past, focusing on his opponent he folds his arms across his chest and waits.
