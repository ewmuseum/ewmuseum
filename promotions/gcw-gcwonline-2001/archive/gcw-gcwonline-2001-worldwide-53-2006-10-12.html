GCW's WW#53 "The Road to NC17" Predictions

8 October 2006

GCW's WW#53 "The Road to NC17" This Week: GCW comes to Phoenix, Arizona for the first WorldWide after Dangerous Games! http://www.gcwonline.net/bigshows/index.php?pg=results for full DG Results "()" encases a specific Superstar's place in DG Card for Thursday, September 12th Phoenix, AZ The First step to GCW's Landmark PPV "NC 17" MAIN EVENT LeStatt Knight(Unranked) & Eva VanLorne(21st) vs. Shadow(1st) & Vivica J. Valentine(22nd) The GCW World Heavyweight Champion has a new challenger. Shadow outlasted 29 other entrants and won the 2006 Dangerous Games, earning her a shot at Knight at NC-17. But following the controversy in Knight's last defense, doubt hangs over his head. Eva VanLorne and Vivica Valentine renewed their epic rivalry at Dangerous Games, and are ready for another chance to battle. But with the dark past between Eva and Knight, is there any way they can co-exist for the better good, or will their own long-standing rivalry cost them a major victory? CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Tessa Windsor(6th) vs. Blaise McClancy(16th) Despite his brief appearance in Dangerous Games, McClancy has been granted a Cruiserweight Title shot. Tessa set herself above the competition at Dangerous Games with an impressive performance, and her sights are suddenly set extremely high. But she must focus if she wants to retain her coveted Cruiserweight Title against Hazard. HARDCORE TITLE MATCH Virgil Ambrosi(11th) vs. Dorian Ryan(24th) By virtue of his amazing victory over the monster Bryan Mayhem in the DeadPool match, Dorian Ryan earned this shot at the Hardcore Title. Can Ryan make it two great hardcore victories in a row? Will Shaman continue to seek revenge for Ryan's assault at Dangerous Games? And can Ambrosi continue his reign against the rolling Ryan? TELEVISION TITLE MATCH Jordan White(9th) vs. Bryan Mayhem(5th) Jordan White had an incredible run in the Dangerous Games match, lasting over an hour. But there were none more impressive than Bryan Mayhem. Mayhem eliminated a record 7 people from Dangerous Games, looking nearly unstoppable all night long. Mayhem's now got a shot at the Television Title, and White has the unenviable task of trying to defend against this behemoth. TAG TEAM MATCH La Sombra(7th) & Ciel(10th) vs. Fiery Passion (17th & 18th) In a match that will set the winner on the fast track to a Tag Team Title opportunity, La Sombra and Ciel take on Fiery Passion. Hope Clarkson and Keiko Yoshimoto are oping to establish themselves as major players in GCW, and can do so with a victory at WorldWide. SINGLES MATCH Shaman(unranked) vs. Peter Vetra(15th) Shaman made his GCW debut at Dangerous Games, twice interrupting the match and eliminating Dorian Ryan from competition. Shaman finally makes his in-ring debut against Peter Vetra, who will try to welcome him to GCW with a loss. SINGLES TITLE MATCH 'All F'n That' Jeff Harris(Unranked) vs. Rufio(13th) The Ruffian has made few friends in GCW since arriving, and has the task of welcoming Jeff Harris to GCW in his first match. Harris' much-anticipated debut will either end in success, or in a coup for Rufio.
