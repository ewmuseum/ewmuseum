Clyde Fox Biography Real Name Clyde Evan Fox Nicknames The Prince of Emo Hometown Ottawa, Ontario, Canada Birthday August 1, 1987 Height 6' 2 Weight 190 lbs. Fed Titles GCW Television Champion (2) Fed Awards 2009 Rampage Tournament Winner, 2009 GCW Segments of The Year Award Biography Clyde Evan Fox spent most of his life trying to find out who he really is. After being separated from his family via falling into a snow bank, Fox had longed to have a family. As luck would have it, Fox would be found and brought up by step parents. It wasn't until his late teens that Fox was reunited with his biological family. But this is just the beginning of the fairy tale. Clyde learned the ways of the warrior through his father. Little did he know, but Clyde came from a long line of fierce fighters. He had those patented Fox genes running through his veins. It's not an overstatement that Fox is a very special lad. Like his siblings and father before him, Clyde will forever remain a child trapped inside an adult body. Candy, fun, video games, boobs, and wrestling are his passions, and his family focused on honing most of those interests. His abilities are actually quite deceptive once you get him in the ring. He was trained by his father, who also had an extensive background in wrestling. After living in his parents basement for so long, Clyde finally landed his first real job... ever. Not too shabby if you ask him. Now at the tender age of 23, Fox has been acsending the GCW ranks. Clyde's career victory to this point was winning the 2009 Rampage Tournament. But he won't stop there. Oh no, he's trying to tear GCW a new one. Feel lucky... you get to witness it. Wrestling Style Acrobatic / Speedster / High Flyer Strategy Fox's game plan is all about deception. Like a Transformer, he is more than meets the eye(s). Surprisingly, he has a lot of stamina which can sometimes lead to his success by tiring out his opponent. Sometimes, things can seem like a video game to him, where he must inflict the ultimate amount of damage in order to win.
