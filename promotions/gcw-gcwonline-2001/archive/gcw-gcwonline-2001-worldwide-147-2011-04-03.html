WorldWide 147 1st Mariner Arena — Baltimore, Maryland ---- WW147: Extreme Makeover Somewhere in the streets of West Baltimore, something unprecedented is occurring and the locals don't quite know what to make of it. A mustachioed man wearing a three-piece suit, a sheriff's hat, and dark aviator shades inspects his broken down vehicle by the side of the curb. As residents stare at him from their porches, perplexed by the exotic automobile, he reaches into one of his suit pockets and dials a number on his burner. This man is Garbage Bag Johnny, the thick mustache the only remaining facial hair he has, hair cut short beneath his head gear. The vehicle is his Segway. GBJ: What do you mean you're not sending a tow truck? If you can tow a car, this should be a piece of cake! Garbage Bag listens to Triple A on the other end, but from his facial expressions (now more visible without his beard), it's clear that the conversation isn't working out in his favor. GBJ: So what am I supposed to do? Push this Segway all the way to Hamsterdam? The intoxicating purr of a freshly-tuned Mustang rumbles into earshot. Rolling around the corner and cruising by with all the subtly of a baby blue rocket ship, a '67 Shelby pulls to a stop next to the distressed Commish. The passenger window lowers and Rich “The Renegade” Rollins' scruffy mug peeks out. Rollins: Hey... Tom Selleck? The hell...? You need a lift, brother? Garbage Bag admires the marina blue finish and softly whispers "unicorn" under his breath. Rollins: I'm lookin' for a guy who goes by the name Garbage Bag John-boy, you heard of the guy? Garbage Bag lifts his shades for a closer look. GBJ: That's me. You from Triple A? The Renegade squints, cocking his head in confusion. It takes a second. GBJ: There it is. Realization dawns. Rich Rollins laughs, slapping the dashboard in delight. Rollins: Holy shit, Johnny. That's quite a clean shave you got there. What's her name? GBJ: Tessa Windsor. The mention of Tessa's name brings a confused, humorless expression to Johnny's face momentarily before he focuses back on the busted Segway. GBJ: Can you be a friendly ghost and pop the trunk? Damn thing blew a gasket. Shooting the Commissioner a cocked eyebrow (YES a cocked eyebrow! What of it?!) Rich Rollins complies, pulling a lever that pops the trunk. Johnny offers his thanks as he hefts the busted mobility device into the back and slams it shut. Rollins: Get in, John-boy. I think you and I have a few things to discuss. After carefully situating the Segway in the trunk of the car, GBJ circles around and hops into the passenger seat next to Rollins. GBJ: I'll say. I was too busy icing the knee to catch your return from the dead. How was the other side, by the way? Obviously dodging the question, Rollins puts the 'stang into gear and pulls off. He indicates Johnny's knee with a flick of his chin. Rollins: You got a bad knee? That shit sucks. I'll tell you, nothin' worse than losing the ability to stand on your own two feet. Like Terror, for instance. (he grins) I'll bet that shit sucked when he couldn't stay up at NC-17. GBJ: It's seen better days. Garbage Bag examines the interior of the sweet ride as it speeds on towards, presumably, the arena. GBJ: Listen, man. I appreciate the ride and all, but I can't imagine you were cruising around Baltimore looking for strays. You said you had something to discuss? The Renegade nods. Rollins: I want to put my boot so far up Jay Terror's ass that Steven Caldera feels it in his nuts. Also, bagels. Lots and lots of bagels. With raisins in 'em. The commissioner chuckles. GBJ: Well, hell. I ain't gonna stop you. Laws of physics might, but I'm only concerned with the law of the land in GCW. Wear him around like a snowshoe for all I care. Can't say he doesn't got it coming. Rollins: Law of the land, huh? This is an alien concept to Rich Rollins. Law? Sounds too much like order. Order sounds too much like ordeal, and the whole ordeal makes The Renegade's head throb. But this guy? Rollins sizes up GBJ with a quick glance and makes his decision. He kinda likes the guy. Rollins: All right, Johnny. You and me, we can do a lot for one another. You want order? I don't do that shit. You want anarchy? We've got business. Hell, we're practically from the same neighborhood! We're both Chicago kids, am I right? So long as I can get my hands on the guy who stole my property and choke him with his own goddamn bandana, I'll be your best friend. Ha, friend. I don't use that word often, John-boy. Whaddaya say... pard'ner? GBJ: You know, Rich. There was a time when I thought that I could get through to Jay, but lately, he's been worse than ever. Keeping an eye on Terror's like a full time job on top of all the paperwork that's always piling up on my desk. Garbage Bag massages his temples and sighs. GBJ: I swear. That's my least favorite part of the job. Rollins grins as he flicks the brim of Johnny's dapper officer's hat. Rollins: Don't you fret, Sheriff! Garbage Bag shakes his head and readjusts his hat. GBJ: Something tells me this is gonna mean extra paperwork. A sharp, almost crazy laugh bursts from Rollins' throat. He swats at the radio dial, hits the volume, and turns that shit up to TWENTY-TWO motherfuckers. Rage Against the Machine's “Wake Up” (ironically enough, the theme for WorldWide... coincidence?) blasts into the streets as The Renegade hits the pedal. Rollins: I think we're gonna get along just fine, John-boy. ---- Introduction The lights dim in 1st Mariner Arena and the Maryland crowd begins to buzz.
