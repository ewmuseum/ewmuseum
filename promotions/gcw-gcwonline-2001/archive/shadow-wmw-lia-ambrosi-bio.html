Lia Ambrosi Biography Real Name Lia Ambrosi Nicknames The Ebon Magdalen Hometown Zdice, Czech Republic Birthday 18 January Height 5'9 Weight 143 lbs. Fed Titles World Champion (former); Cruiserweight Champion (former) Fed Awards 2006 Dangerous Games Winner Biography [[Inactive]] Other Feds Global TV Champion, Global International Champion, WMW Ace Superior Champion Wrestling Style shoot/technical Finisher Move Fool's Gallows; Canto VII; Stygian Stitch Finisher Description Fool's Gallows: (submission) Elevated Texas cloverleaf. Named for the picture of the Fool or hanged man in most tarot decks where he's hanging from a tree, by one ankle, and the legs form a figure 4. Canto VII: (strike) springboard busaiku knee kick. Similar to a springboad enziguri, except it's a busaiku kick. A powerful knee kick that can be done to either the front or back of the head. (Named for the canto of Dante's Inferno in which Dante and Virgil descend into the fourth circle of Hell, where the greedy are punished). Stygian Stitch: (submission) iron cross- rarely used- a hyperflexing neck crank involving trapping the opponent's hands and forcing the head towards his or her chest. The technique is performed with the opponent lying on his or her back, and the combatant performing the neck crank perpendicularly face-down in a side mount position above the head of the opponent, with the opponent's head resting towards his armpit. The combatant traps one arm using the legs, and the other using the arms. By using the pinned arms and legs as a point of leverage, the combatant can forcefully crank the head towards the opponent's chest. Finisher Setup Move n/a Setup Description Some sort of strike is used to set up both of the submissions, most likely a shotei or ridgehand strike to knock the opponent senseless. Any of the palm strikes on her movelist will probably be used to either the sternum or face, potentially the neck to knock the person to the ground. She'll follow up the opening blow with whatever she needs to knock them to the ground and get them in position for the submission. Special Moves see below Other Moves Basic: Opening gambit moves, used to start softening up opponents with basic blows that hurt, but don't do too much damage. This is not the flashy stuff, it's the small guns, comparatively. back elbow chop clothesline diving elbow smash elbow to the midsection eye gouge face rake forearm to the back kick to the groin kneedrop hanging kneebreaker reverse neckbreaker Russian legsweep short-arm clothesline swinging neckbreaker armwringer back rake drop toehold Aerial: I placed this class of moves second after basics, because they are essentially moves that will not be used as the meat and potatoes of her arsenal. They are important only as ways to soften up an opponent, and will not under any circumstances be used when there's an element of risk, but as a follow-up when she's already got the upper hand in an exchange. Ground Aerial (execute from the mat): dropkick sunset flip dropkick to the back of the head dropkick to the face (this usually happens during a tag match, when her partner holds up the opponent's face, and Shadow nails them) dropkick to the knee Reversals/Pinning Predicaments: Reversals will become a pretty important part of her style, simply to show off technical skill in the ring; when she's in a tight spot, her flexibility comes in, and in comes a reversal. backslide schoolboy small package *outside hook kick (used as a reversal to a DDT, or the setup for a vertical suplex. Once the setup is in place, Shadow uses her great flexibility to arch her back and catch the opponent in the face [probably around the chin area] with a hook kick using the leg that is farthest from them- hence, outside hook kick) *backflip cradle backward bridge hurricanrana thingy (yeah, I don't know a better name for it Tongue)- blatantly ripped off from Masato Yoshino's From Jungle, which can be found here: http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves/FromJungle.wmv hurricanrana counter- again, ripped off, this time from K-ness's Judah, which is here: http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves2/Judah.wmv (You'd be just as well watching the video, because I don't know how in the hell to describe it). Technical: Again, these are not finishers, but are used to exhaust an opponent to the point where her real striking and submission strengths can come in. It's for that reason that the technical moves are really more abbreviated, and are used mostly as takedowns to buy time for things that she's better at. Mid-match moves. handstand throw (can also be altered into a throw when in powerbomb position) armbar takedown dragon screw side suplex *dragon screw leg whip Japanese armdrag takedown *Sambo Suplex (Rock Bottom without the lift- a simple forward throw). STO (not used terrifically often, but used as a homage to her husband, Virgil. Tag team matches between the two of them might see a double STO). Blizzard suplex (http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves3/blizzardsuplex.wmv) Belly-to-belly suplex variations (overhead; snap [http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves/snapbellytobelly.wmv]; trapping [http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves2/trappingsuplex.wmv]; *spider [http://www.wrestlingencyclopedia.com/Moves/spiderbellytobelly.WMV]). The spider version is one of her trademarks. Northern Lights suplex Submissions/Holds: These moves are more along the lines of moves that Shadow would use to finish a match. Some of them, such as the wristlocks and the 'smaller' submission holds are only used as a way to soften up joints for harder to escape moves, while others can be used as a finisher in a pinch. front facelock headscissors submission *wristlocks (supinating: grabbing the hand and twisting the thumb outward- a weak hold, usually used to force the opponent to the ground; hyperflexing: pressing the palm of the hand down towards the forearm, twisting outward- can be rather painful with leverage applied; z-lock/kote hono gaeshi- a pretty strong hold: opponent's arm extended, grab palm of hand, bend elbow slightly, and twist wrist inward towards the body and then torque downwards. Can only be broken by opponent dropping to the ground). Chinlock iron cross (rarely used- a hyperflexing neck crank involving trapping the opponent's hands and forcing the head towards his or her chest. The technique is performed with the opponent lying on his or her back, and the combatant performing the neck crank perpendicularly face-down in a side mount position above the head of the opponent, with the opponent's head resting towards his armpit. The combatant traps one arm using the legs, and the other using the arms. By using the pinned arms and legs as a point of leverage, the combatant can forcefully crank the head towards the opponent's chest. ) kneebar- much similar to the armbar, only done by straightening and then hyperextending the knee triangle choke *katahajime juji-gatame (crucifix armbar) *flying armbar (see this page for a picture: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujigatame#Juji-gatame. This move is basically very specialized to Shadow, and rarely used, but exciting to watch in action). Rear Naked Choke hammerlock Aoki Hikari (http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves/AokiHikari.wmv)- specifically employed to be rather embarrassing for the person receiving it, as Lia enjoys the irony of her victim's body being used against him or her. Shoot-Fighting/MMA: This is the absolute meat and potatoes of her movelist, to be seen in every single match from here on out. It's a mid-match sort of thing, though in this particular match, it will probably come out pretty early. These blows are hard. They are meant to produce sound when they impact, meant to have the crowd perking up its ears and "ooh"-ing with the force. They are used to soften up for submissions, but can also be hard enough to end a match on their own. Hand Strikes: Leopard's Paw- fingers curled down, but not into a fist. *Shotei (palm strike) Spear Hand strike (flat hand (like a chop) but forward with the tips of the fingers) *Ridgehand strike (inverted chop, thumb tucked down to the palm, and hit with that side of the hand. The bones are bigger and stronger. And the hand is in natural position on a sweep in. For a ridge hand you can pull it back like a punch and swing in an arch and hit them, or swing up in an uppercut without any loss of speed or movement.) chop to the neck palm blow to the throat (especially when opponent is downed) Leg/Foot Strikes: back heel kick enziguri roundhouse kick ax kick jumping kick (savate uses this move all the time) mule kick (If a leg is caught on an attempt at a boot to the midsection, the kicker rotates underneath their own axis [typically clockwise if the right leg is immobile, and counter-clockwise if it's the left], and then boots the holder in the face.) toe kick to the shin (or anywhere else low on the victim's body) *snap kick (a piston-action savate kick- start with the knee bent, the leg pistons out, hits the target and comes back to its original position. Can land in any area of the body). Gamengiri calf kick *busaiku knee kick jumping heel kick wheel kick calf kick toe kick to the face on a downed opponent toe kick to the spine on a sitting opponent leg lariat *springboard enziguri (a very flashy move- you can add about as much height as you want to this, depending on how much pop you want to get out of it.) Cartwheel kick (http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves/cartwheelkick.wmv) handstand springboard enziguri (http://www.puroresucentral.com/moves2/handspringenzigieri.wmv) Combos: Part of Shadow's strength with this new movelist will be chaining moves together in devastating combinations. Here are a few. *ascending combo (Starts off with a toe kick to the shin, then a boot to the opposite knee, followed up with a palm blow to the sternum with one hand and an elbow to the face with the other. This is all executed in very quick succession.) arm wrench and hook kick Reverse STO into Koji Clutch Triangle Choke + Wrist Lock Irish-whip into ropes + calf kick (adds to the velocity of the move, and gives Shadow time to get some lift on the calf kick) TRADEMARKS: These are marked above, but I'm putting them down here with their names.
