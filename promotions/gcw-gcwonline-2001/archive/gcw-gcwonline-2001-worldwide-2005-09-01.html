Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #23 – Portland, Oregon

Promotional Video: The Formation of a Diabolical Creature -by Brandon

A creation of violence, the essence of mayhem, finally out of the fight clubs I’ve arrived to a permanent settlement. A realm, in which I will be rewarded by the flow of cash into my bank account. A kingdom, an empire of wrestlers to defeat, one by one I will destroy them all as quickly as I can, reigning to the top of the ladder as the king of the empire.

The scene opens in the empty arena of Portland, Oregon. The banners of Global Championship Wrestling are draped across it, the blue glows in the arena as the lights shine against them and the ring is setup in the center of the stadium, the focal point of the arena, and everyone’s eyes will be on the ring. The ring is an unfamiliar region for me; I have grown fighting in the octagonal ring.

Jesse, the brother of Aiden Jackson, sat there in a seat next to me. We looked around the arena then began with casual conversation.

“How does it feel?” he said to me as he put his foot up onto the seat in front of him.

“Weird, it’s a different environment than I know. It’s an environment that I am unfamiliar with myself, I mean I’ve been training and I’ve found good methods of fighting just as I had in the octagon. I’ve found other methods in here though, using the top rope to my advantage. Well, we’ve seen enough of this place haven’t we and I know you’re itching to see my locker room.” I said to him.

“Yeah, you guessed it.” He replied.

We hopped the guard rail walking up the ramp-way to the backstage, men rushed around preparing for the show in chaos. I felt tingles run through my body, the feeling that I would have next week when I actually debut in my first match in GCW. The feeling overcame me for a moment and I zoned out. I finally snapped back to reality when my brother spoke to me.

“Here it is.” He said.

“What? Sorry, I kinda zoned out.” I said.

“I said here it is.” He said back to me looking at me funny.

“What?” I said as I laughed softly.

I looked at the doorway of the locker room, it read Aiden Jackson. To see my name on something was amazing, and knowing exactly why it was there. I knew that locker room was my place of calmness, my place of preparation before I entered the dungeon of chaos in which I would pursue glory.

“This is it.” I said as I opened the door.

“Wow” He said in awe as he looked around the locker room.

The locker room was decorated with a nice leather couch and big screen television, already sitting there was my dress for the night. He looked at them then turned to me.

“What is that?” he said to me.

“What I wear when I wrestle, it’s just a pair of long black shorts with chains on them like a chain wallet you know?” I replied.

“That’s not quite you at all.” He said.

“It fits all the music I listen to I guess, it’s a rock star style of and outfit, for me it’s better than those little girlie tights.”

“Ahh, yeah I know what you’re saying.” He said to me.

“Well, I think we’ve seen everything this is going to be a good ride indeed, you’ll be there tomorrow right?” I said.

“Yeah, of course I’m not gonna miss this for the world bro.” he replied.

“Well man, let’s grab a bite to eat. That sounds good right?” I questioned him.

“Yeah, that sounds great.” He answered.

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Portland crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Champion Superhero” and “I Fear Stevens” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Portland, Oregon” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We’ve got twenty thousand on hand in the Rose Garden in Portland, Oregon, as GCW comes to you in the wake of Annihilation! It was an amazing broadcast, ladies and gentlemen, and tonight we’ll feel the fallout!

Jhub: Speaking of FallOut, that’s the next big show we’re heading towards, and rumor has it we’re going to have a few matches signed for that show right here tonight! But before we can get there, we have to talk about Annihilation.

Bryan: And for anyone who doesn’t know, LeStatt Knight remains your heavyweight champion of the world! But it didn’t go down the way we thought it would.

Pete: Hell no! LeStatt ended up pulling through and winning the match, but Stevens beat the hell out of him in the process! We saw a new side of Michael Stevens at Annihilation, and quite frankly I’m a little intimidated by him now!

Jhub: I heard that! Stevens showed that he could be a sick bastard, too, when the situation calls for it, and LeStatt was caught totally off guard. But in the end one mistake by an overzealous challenger allowed LeStatt to remain GCW World Heavyweight Champion.

Bryan: And of course we saw the most amazing ladder match in GCW history, where pretty-boy Jonathan Borealis confirmed the rumors, stabbed his friend Trey Tremendous in the back, and walked out the NEW United States Champion.

Pete: That was a dark moment for the fans, JB, because Trey fought his heart out. But the good news is, Rich Rollins didn’t go home with any gold at all!

Jhub: That’s because Eva VanLorne, right off the heels of losing the Tag Team Titles, stuck her nose, and a few other things, in the match and stole the TV title belt! For some reason President Caldera is letting her keep the damn thing, but she’s the new champ.

Pete: And of course, we’ll never forget what Eva did to The Jhub-

Jhub: What’s next, JB?

Bryan: Well, fans, you’ll hear more about Annihilation as the night goes on, but tonight we have a huge lineup for you. In tonight’s main event, GCW’s newest superstar Seymour Almasy will be in action, taking on the new United States Champion Jonathan Borealis in a non-title bout. Almasy shocked the world at Annihilation by showing up in GCW to challenge the Renegade, and we hope to get more information on that situation before the night is over.

Pete: Let’s go backstage!

Superpowers -by Ben

A pair of heavy steel doors swing open, and in steps Michael “Spitfire” Stevens. The crowd explodes at his image on the MegaTron as he casually struts into the Rose Garden. Carrying a black duffel bag in his left hand and a bottle of water in his right, Stevens peers down the hallway from behind his sunglasses, trying to get his bearings.

Bryan: And here’s the man who almost became champion of the World just four nights ago! Michael Stevens is in the arena!

Stevens struts down the hall, sporting a million dollar smile on his face. He comes upon a huddled group of ring technicians, quietly discussing their assignments for the night.

Stevens: Hey guys! How’s it going?

The foremost of the group immediately goes pale, his eyes wide in fear. He turns and walks directly away from Stevens, trying to act casual by hurrying as fast as his feet will carry him.

Stevens: What’s his-

The rest of the group immediately follows suit, leaving Stevens alone in the hall.

Stevens: …Problem? Hmm.

Stevens shrugs and continues down the hall, whistling “O’Fortuna” as he walks. Stevens suddenly screeches to a halt, and the camera turns to peer down the hallway where Stevens is staring. There referee Josh Briggs stands with a security guard, going over a few details for the night.

Stevens: Hey! Briggs! What are you-

Briggs, hearing Spitfire’s voice, immediately shoves a handful of papers into the guard’s hands and scurries off. Stevens cocks his head to the side, now totally bewildered.

Stevens: Something I said?

Stevens shrugs it off again and takes a few more steps down the hall, finally breaking off to his right. He knocks on a wooden door, where the words “Steven Caldera” are clearly printed. The fans applaud at the sight of the name, waiting for Stevens to enter.

Stevens: Caldy! You in there?

A muffled voice answers from inside, and Stevens pushes inside. President Caldera sits behind his desk, arms folded in front of him, staring out into the room with an odd look upon his face. A hastily drawn chair sits on the far side of the desk, where Stevens quickly seats himself. Caldera continues to stare past him, but addresses him warmly.

Caldera: Michael, how are you? Recovering all right?

Stevens: I feel great, actually. Never felt better. But something really odd is going on around here. Did I do something to piss everyone off?

Caldera: Don’t ask me. I thought your match was great, despite your little slip up there at the end.

Stevens finally turns and looks behind him to where Caldera is staring. There in the corner, huddled on the floor in a terrified silence, is GCW interviewer Kevin Jacobs.

Stevens: What’s he doing down there?

Caldera: The hell if I know. The moment he heard your voice, he dove out of his chair and started hiding. I think it might have something to do with your performance at Annihilation.

Stevens: Well, actually, that’s what I wanted to talk to you about. I left you a message asking for a match tonight, you know, to keep the juices flowing. Now, I understand if you’re pissed that I didn’t win the title, or if you don’t want me to hang around because I let you down, but I just had hoped to wrestle again tonight to prove to you that I’m through making mistakes-

Caldera: Michael, calm down. I tried to get you a match tonight. But nobody wanted to wrestle you. Not a single solitary soul would step into the ring with you. Hell, I even asked Hardy, and he called in sick. I’m sorry, Mike, but nobody wants to step into the ring with a sick, sadistic bastard like you.

Stevens seems taken aback by this, almost a bit offended.

Stevens: Well, surely there’s got to be somebody to fight tonight.

Caldera: Hey, don’t look at me. You think I’m crazy enough to fight you after that?

Stevens seems legitimately disappointed, slumping down in his chair.

Stevens: Nobody will even come near me tonight, will they?

Caldera: I’m afraid you’ll have to find some other way to amuse yourself.

Stevens frowns, until a lightbulb pops up over his head. He stands, snatches up his duffel bag, and walks towards the exit of the room. He places a hand on the knob, but pauses and looks down at Jacobs.

Stevens: BOO!

Jacobs screams out in terror and leaps from his spot in the corner. Jacobs dives around Caldera’s desk and takes refuge behind the President, unwilling to show his face again. Caldera glances down blankly at his lead interviewer, then back to Stevens.

Caldera: Don’t abuse it.

Stevens laughs to himself and nods, suddenly excited about his newfound powers. Stevens swings the door open and steps out of the room, off to cause some mischief. The camera fades back to ringside, where The Jhub is laughing hysterically.

Jhub: Ha! That was great! Oh, to be Michael Stevens tonight!

Bryan: Well, Stevens put up one hell of a fight on Sunday night, and I can’t blame people for being a little wary of him. Any man who can match wills with LeStatt Knight has to be a little unstable.

Pete: I just hope he doesn’t come down here.

Jhub: What, you forgot to wear your Depends tonight?

Pressing Issues -by Rob

“O’Fortuna” begins playing from the sound system causing the fans to stand and boo with everything they have. Soon after the music starts up, the curtains fly open and out walks LeStatt Knight, GCW World Title draped over his shoulder. For once his hair is pulled back and tied neatly, black sunglasses cover those normally icy blue eyes. A half unbuttoned, white dress shirt is covered by a black Gucci jacket, his slacks, which match the jacket flow freely and slightly go over the black dress shoes that he wears.

Bryan: Here he comes, fans! The man who defeated Michael Stevens to retain the GCW World Heavyweight Championship at Annihilation is on his way to address the crowd!

Jhub: Well, we just watched that impressive video package featuring new star Rei Tiu, but now we’re suddenly snapped back to the reality of our situation, that while the cruiserweight tournament is getting underway, LeStatt Knight is still World Champion and still as evil as ever.

Pete: And even more angry than usual considering what happened to him at Annihilation!

The champ doesn’t smile as he comes down to the ring, and as a matter of fact barely pays attention to the thunderous boos he receives. Knight reaches the ring and climbs up to the apron before climbing in between the top and middle ropes. He strolls to the opposite side of the ring where the time keeper greets him with a microphone, which he snatches from him and clutches it in his right hand. Knight walks to the center of the ring, but is forced to wait for the crowd to stop cursing him. When they do Knight puts the microphone to his lips.

LeStatt Knight: It is not every show that I come out here and address issues that are plaguing my mind. It is not often that you people get to see your World Champion waltz down that aisle to speak a few words. Tonight is a special occasion though, yes indeed it is. Now before I go into exactly what makes this occasion so special, let me take you all back to what happened last month at Dangerous Games.

Knight points towards the screen where clips of Dangerous Games are shown, different spots throughout the world title match between him and Jason Dread. Eventually it gets to the end where LeStatt strapped Dread down and threatened to burn him and his inevitable quitting. The montage stops with a picture of LeStatt holding the world title high above his head.

LeStatt Knight: Take a look at this picture. (The fans boo like crazy.) Take a mental note, if you will because that right there is very, very important to what I have to say here tonight. Now I am going to take you back to World Wide a few weeks ago where I defended my World Championship against one Bryan Mayhem.

The screen flashes to the match Knight spoke of, but going right into the series of vile chair shots from LeStatt to the skull of Mayhem and ending with the unorthodox “flaming chair” shot, then ending once again with Knight holding the World Title high above his head.

LeStatt Knight: Again look at this picture! Do you see it? I know you do its large enough for the entire world to see. Remember this one as well, it will also be very important. Now one more clip … Annihilation, Sunday night.

Michael Stevens vs. LeStatt Knight begins showing, or at least fast clips of it. Going from Steven’s barrage to Knight’s snapping his own arm back into place, to the wild finish of the Shooting Star Press into a Knight Fall. Again the montage ends with LeStatt Knight holding the title as he walks up the ramp, Typhoid Mary to his side. The screen goes black now and Knight lifts the microphone up again.

LeStatt Knight: Now I am sure some of you are wondering why I showed you those clips. Its not because I am conceited and love watching my own matches, its not because I want to gloat about those victories either, because I let my actions speak for me. Those clips all have one thing in common … LeStatt Knight leaving as the GCW Heavyweight Champion of the World! Maybe I should slow that down for all of you to comprehend? L-E-S-T-A-T-T … G-C-W … C-H-A-M-P. That’s right I AM the champion, and no matter how much you all may hate me for what I have done, the one thing I’ve NOT done is cheated for my victories. I have won everything I have on my own. Was RaZiel out there when I defeated Michael Stevens at Annihilation? NO! Did I have some sick “Establishment” behind me when I made Jason Dread say those three fateful words? Of course not. I do things for myself, BY MYSELF.

Now the thing that I want to make perfectly clear, once again, is that I am the Champion because it seems that some people have forgotten that. GCW has a whole slew of guys, older and new that have staked their claim on a World Title that is around MY waist. One guy calls me a paper champion and all I can say to that is that he has never faced me before … and frankly isn’t worth the time of day to do so now.

A second guy is some Boy Wonder who has illusions of grandeur. He made a living in the cruiserweight division and now wants to step up to the World Title? Well while the thought of having a world title shot may seem like a dream come true, I promise him and the rest of the world that I would quickly turn that boyhood dream into a never ending NIGHTMARE!

Does it end there though? Of course not, we also have a former World Champion who seems to think he is still some top notch superstar when he really has done nothing of significance since 2001. Since when has beating Tristain Mayhem, John Reed, or Jason Dread been anything to be proud of? This guy was around at the right time and now is nothing more than a wanna-be.

All of these “superstars” want this title but seem to forget exactly who it is that holds it. This is no longer the day when a sorry excuse for a champion stands on top of the mountain and is only in that position because of the backing of some self proclaimed “Establishment” or some Army. I have worked harder than anyone since this company re-opened, I almost single handedly put GCW back on the map! (The crowd boos now. Knight smirks finally) You all boo like it isn’t true yet when I was holding onto the United States Title you were all cheering my name! It was your votes of confidence that made Steven Caldera understand that his BEST athlete was stuck in the U.S. Title scene and inferior talents were going for the company’s greatest prize! So continue to boo me, it just proves the type of hypocrites and band waggoners that you are! I am the face of this company, love me or hate me. In fact I offer this question, who deserves to go head to head with ME for the GCW World Title at Fall Out? (The fans all begin screaming out different names and Knight listens with mock interest. )

Fascinating answers, believe me but I really did not care who you thought. Tonight I am out to do more than just remind everyone of Who I am and what I am capable of. Tonight I have come out here to tell the world, first hand that the Main Event to Fall Out has already been signed, sealed, and delivered. Earlier this week I flew to GCW headquarters and met with the booking committee and demanded that the ONE deserving individual gets a shot at MY Title. I told them that we have unfinished business. Ladies and gentleman The Main Event at GCW’s Fall out will pit me, LeStatt Knight against … Michael “Spitfire” Stevens, in a “SUBMISSION MATCH.”

The fans all stand and explode into cheers at the mention of this ground breaking news. Knight smiles at the crowd’s reaction, perhaps a genuine smile. He adjusts the world title to better stay atop his shoulder before continuing.

LeStatt Knight: The battle is set, Michael Stevens … I’ll see you at Fall Out.

LeStatt Knight drops the microphone and heads out of the ring. With that arrogant smile he walks up the ramp only stopping to hold the World Title up over his head one last time before disappearing into the back.

Bryan: What a challenge! LeStatt Knight wants Stevens in a submission match? God, wouldn’t that be incredible?

Jhub: Knight has already beaten Jason Dread in an “I Quit” match, but make no mistake, this is not the same idea! Knight and Stevens were locked in a technical war at Annihilation, and that’s how it’s going to stay! The man with the best technical prowess will come out on top, but they’ll have to make the other man submit away the GCW Title!

Pete: And I don’t think either one of these lunatics would consider tapping out! Man, if Stevens accepts that challenge, I shudder to think what will happen to these two at FallOut!

An Audience… -by Brian

Steven Caldera sits calmly behind his desk, watching the live feed from the ring on a nearby monitor. So far the show has gone well; or at least as well as possible given the unpredictable nature of the business. A sudden knock at his door pulls his attention from the screen

Caldera: What?

From beyond the door a voice echoes out.

“Sire, we seek an audience.”

A puzzled face crosses Caldera’s face and he turns in his seat to face the door.

Caldera: A what?

“An audience…”

A strange request indeed; his interest piqued, Steven obliges.

Caldera: Sure. Come in.

The door swings open silently allowing two men to enter the room, their faces covered yet instantly identifiable by the cloaks which seem to constantly swirl around them. The Rogue Paladin and Meatshield Trainwrecker take up positions directly in front of Caldera’s desk, seemingly oblivious to the incredulous look on Caldera’s face. Paladin raises the hood of his cloak; meanwhile Meat simply crosses his massive arms across his equally massive chest.

TRP: Sire.

Caldera: Sire?

TRP: Indeed. If GCW be a kingdom, would you not be the patriarch? The King as it were. Or is there some other form of address that you would find more suitable.

Caldera: A simple “sir” would do nicely.

TRP: Sir, you honor us with this meeting. I seek your counsel or many issues.

Caldera: And what might those issues be?

TRP: Foremost, we thank you for the opportunity afforded us at Annihilation; however we were in no way prepared to win that battle. Your trial by fire has indeed opened our eyes.

Steven Caldera seems to mull this statement over for a bit trying to figure out exactly what the hell the Paladin is talking about.

Caldera: And your point would be?

TRP: We lack the required level of experience to be successful on a champion’s level. We are, rather bluntly sir, noobs. We need XP, and lots of it.

Caldera: And?

TRP: In the few bouts we have had, we have already faced all the tag teams GCW currently has to offer. To continue to battle the same competitors time and again will not help us achieve the desired effects.

Caldera: I’m going to assume that you have idea to rectify this situation.

TRP: Indeed. Regardless if we win or lose tonight, we are not ready to face the Tag Champions just yet. I suggest that we compete in single melee for a period of time, to receive a wider breadth of experience. I believe that there is a Cruiserweight tournament going on. I should like to enter.

Caldera: I see. But what about your silent friend here?

TRP: After our debut victory Dimensioner has taken umbrage with our triumph, believing it to be fluke. My compatriot would like nothing more than to prove him wrong, again.

Meatshield Trainwrecker takes this opportunity to loudly crack his rather large knuckles as a hint of a smirk casts out from beneath the hood of his cloak.

Caldera: Well, I will see what I can do. Is there anything else?

TRP: Just one small thing sir. The craft table seems to be woefully under stocked as far as Cheetos and Mountain Dew go. You should see to chastising the catering staff at once.

Steven releases an annoyed sigh and rolls his eyes.

Caldera: I’ll look into it.

TRP: Than we shall take our leave of you.

With a flourish, The Rogue Paladin’s face disappears behind the hood of his cloak as both he and Meatshield Trainwrecker exit Caldera’s office. As soon as the door closes, Steven puts a hand to face and begins to massage his temples in an attempt to stave off the headache that has only very recently surfaced.

Caldera: Cheetos and Mountain Dew? What the hell was that?

Zodiac vs. Typhoid Mary -by Kris

Bryan: All right, fans, it’s time to kick off the GCW Cruiserweight Title Tournament! And we’ve got a doozy here in the first round! Newcomer Zodiac is going to go one on one with LeStatt Knight’s latest squeeze Typhoid Mary, and this should be a hell of a way to kick off the tournament.

Jhub: There you see the brackets, and it looks like a hell of a lineup. There’s a lot of talent in this field, but only one of those people can emerge the GCW Cruiserweight Champion. And it looks like the Rogue Paladin’s request has been made, and he’ll be facing Rei Tiu in the first round next week!

Can I Play With Madness by Iron Maiden cues up, the lights all around the MegaTron flickering about randomly, sparks jetting into the air in short, unpredictable bursts until Typhoid Mary steps out to a round of cheers, her fists beating the air as she bounces down to the ring and rolls in under the bottom rope.

Bryan: Typhoid Mary making her way into the ring, full of energy and ready for her match up.

Jhub: I'll bet she'd like to be full of-

Bryan: Did you know Typhoid Mary and Zodiac are both former champions of unaffiliated promotions.

Pete: Are we even supposed to mention unaffiliated promotions?

Jhub: I'd give Mary a promotion.

Hole in The Sky by Pantera cues the golden fire works and a mixed round of cheers, confidently Zodiac takes to the ramp, her eyes focused on Mary, now posing off the middle rope.

Pete: It's like these two ladies are polar opposites, Zodiac looks focused.

Bryan: Indeed they are, and as I understand this isn't their first altercation.

Jhub: Was it the slap in the face on Sunday that clued you in?

Bryan: Well... Yeah.

Pete: For anybody who wasn't able to catch Annihilation:

**Clip plays for the home viewers of Annihilation's announcement of the new Cruiser Weight Divison.**

Bryan: The tension is high as these two dangerous ladies get ready to start this match.

Circling each other slowly the two women size each other up, they lock up, Zodiac hits the hip toss. T Mary gets to her feet and Zodiac hits the forearm chop, Mary fires back and ducks Zodiac's return. Into the ropes and Mary hits a drop kick.

Pete: Important first move, these cruiserweights set a fast and furious pace, every move counts.

Bryan: Zodiac is far from out, back on her feet here she sends Mary for a ride with a scoop slam!

Zodiac tries to capitalize with a single leg crap, but Mary counters with a monkey flip and spring boards to her feet. The fans cheer, Mary goes into the ropes and spins with a cartwheel kick, Zodiac dodges and goes into the odd ropes, Mary lands and rolls smoothly under Zodiac's clothesline. Zodiac continues into the ropes, Mary sets a wide stance and swings her momentum wide as Zodiac launches right into a huge knee to the mid section.

Jhub: I love that Brazilian sway.

Zodiac avoids the repeated stomp attempts from Mary and rolls into a crouched position. She leaps for a spear but Mary dodges, tucking into a neat roll Zodiac plants a stance and catches Mary trying to capitalize with a backwards splash that takes them both down. Zodiac scrambles to straddle Mary, three quick jabs before Mary rolls her legs up and hooks Zodiac, pulling her back and into a head scissors choke hold.

Bryan: This crowd is just as intense as this match!

Jhub: Who cares about the crowd!

Zodiac manages to free herself and both women rise to their feet at the same time. They circle quickly before locking up, Zodiac pulls a headlock and spins Mary to the mat, laying across her back and applying a lot of pressure to Mary's neck. Mary fights it until Zodiac lets the hold go, slow to get to her feet Mary suddenly explodes and throws Zodiac's arms away, she fires a right and a left, hits the ropes, Zodiac moves back and avoids the momentum, forcing Mary to pull up her speed and Zodiac pounces with a quick jab.

Bryan: Great tactical work from Zodiac there.

Zodiac finds a beat to her punches but Mary sways and dodges, so Zodiac changes to a wider stance and mixes in a side kick, Mary blocks and hits a leg sweep. Mary drops an elbow. Mary hits the ropes but has to drop the attempt as Zodiac rolls into a crouched position. She dashes forward with a sweeping side kick, Mary catches it under her arm and they pause. Enziguri kick takes Mary to the matt.

Bryan: That nearly took her head off!

Pete: Zodiac the first to take the top rope! And she launches! Oh!

Jhub: Mary tried to avoid the moonsault and got kicked in the head!.

Bryan: Zodiac took the landing fairly hard but she's getting to her feet now.

Zodiac fixes her boot quickly before moving in on Mary, she reaches down and Mary flips over, catching Zodiac's arm in one hand and holding her there as she swings both feet up for a kick to the head.

Jhub: An eye for an eye, or a boot for a boot?

Zodiac reels. Mary springboards to her feet. The crowd goes insane. Mary takes the top rope and quickly launches the body splash. Zodiac goes down. Mary covers. 1... 2. Zodiac kicks out and tries to roll away.

Bryan: Zodiac with a decisive kick out, she's not out of this yet.

Mary bounces from foot to foot and calls the slow rising Zodiac to get up faster. Her lips flying though the few words heard over the cheering crowd are foreign gibberish. Zodiac takes her time. Mary moves in with a kick, Zodiac catches it and tackles her to the mat, hooking the knee she steps over, locking in the single leg crab. Mary spits Brazilian curses but doesn't tap.

Zodiac leans back, trying to force out the tap but Mary wont give it to her, leaning back even further Zodiac twists her own body to try and hook Mary's arm and lock in her finisher. Mary's hips roll, her leg almost bent out of shape, but she breaks the hold and the two detangle quickly. Zodiac is up quickly and pulls Mary to her feet.

Jhub: Amazingly Mary was able to avoid the Annihilator and break the hold.

Pete: At what cost, she can hardly hold her own weight on her left leg.

Zodiac tests that leg by throwing Mary into the ropes, she comes back and Zodiac hooks her head, runs up the ropes and lands the ariel bulldog with picture perfect precision.

Bryan: Out of nowhere Zodiac hits her Mystical Requital, and we might have a winner!

1...2...3!!

Pete: Zodiac's done it!

Jhub: NO! Haha!

Bryan: Her foot was on the ropes, the ref saw it at the last second!

Pete: Listen to this crowd go insane for these two amazing women.

Bryan: I think Eva has herself double the competition.

Mary is almost completely out of it as Zodiac pulls her into a standing position. Zodiac throws her into the corner. Zodiac runs in and jumps, but Mary drops and rolls underneath her. Zodiac hits the turnbuckles hard. Mary straddles her from the middle rope, jumps and catches Zodiac's head in her legs for a stunning hurricanrana.

Jhub: It's beautiful.

Mary curls her position and hooks Zodiac's legs on landing for a small package.

1..2..Zodiac reverses by rolling Mary onto her back and holding her legs down.

1..2.. Mary pushes the weight off and rolls back onto her feet.

As Zodiac stands Mary launches a drop kick that takes Zodiac to the mat hard. Mary gathers a leg and curls it around her body, twists herself on her hips, leaning back to hook her hands around Zodiac's head and locking in Meds Time... Jhub: Is it just me, or does The Renegade look a little… lighter tonight?

Renegade: Cut my f*cking music. Now.

“SEY-MOUR~! SEY-MOUR~! SEY-…”

“SEY-MOUR~! SEY-MOUR~! SEY-MOUR~!”

Renegade: Yeah, f*ck you too.

Renegade: Are you happy now, Almasy? “SEY-MOUR~! SEY-MOUR~! SEY-MOUR~!”

Almasy: You know, Rich…I didn’t want to come out here tonight. I would have been perfectly content to wait in the back until it was time for my match with Borealis. But, no, like always Rich, you have to stir the pot.

Almasy: Still with the insults. The only reason I’m here is to settle the score between us. I’m with Global Championship Wrestling as long as it takes to take you down, and perhaps longer if I keep getting welcomes like this…

Almasy: But nonetheless, Renegade…all I want’s a match. When’s good for you?

Bryan: Wow, Almasy cut right to the chase! And from the sound of it, the GCW fans sure want to see it happen!

Rollins finally stands still, raising the mic to lips that tremble with unrestrained outrage.

Renegade: A match? You wanna go toe-to-toe with The Renegade, and that’s it? Why the hell should I have to prove anything to you? You don’t come waltzing into my backyard and start demandin’-…

His response resonates with cold finality.

Renegade: No.

The fans voice their displeasure eagerly. Almasy remains as stoic as ever, shaking his head sadly.

Almasy: Don’t be a coward, Rich. I know you’ve wanted this ever since Clyde chokeslammed your sorry ass over my kneecap, and here the opportunity stares you right in the face. Almasy: That’s bullshit Rollins, and you know it.

Arching a suspicious brow, Rollins regards Almasy carefully before speaking.

Renegade: What sort of wager we talkin’ about?

Almasy: (grinning) Tonight, in my match against US Champion Johnathan Borealis… if I beat Borealis, I want your signature on a contract guaranteeing a match with me at FallOut, no questions asked.

Renegade: And if you lose?

A slow smile spread across the Final Fantasy’s face.

Almasy: If I lose, then I’ll compensate Steven Caldera for the remainder of my contract and leave GCW... tonight.

The scowl on Rollins’ face has all but disappeared, replaced by a wicked grin so wide that it almost seems out of place that he has no curly black mustache to tug at.

Still in the ring, still grinning like a Cheshire cat, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins watches his departure with hungry eyes.

Bryan: WOW! It’s all on the line tonight, folks! Almasy wants to silence Rollins for good, and he’s willing to risk his newfound career in GCW to do it! This is monumental!

Pete: Almasy has buckets of confidence and a truck full of talent, and I have no doubt that he can back himself up… but against Borealis? That’s an opportunity you don’t get every day.

Pete: … Jhub?

Jhub: Hmm?

Pete: You really, really do need to retire.

As Almasy flies through the air, Borealis turns and nails him with a dropkick on the way down!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! What an amazing move from the United States Champion! Borealis showing incredible agility and quickness there, nailing Almasy out of the air with a dropkick after that huge back body drop!

Jhub: That was a great combination move, and I think Borealis might have taken Almasy off guard a bit. I can only assume Almasy had enough respect for the competition in GCW to be ready for a tough outing against Borealis, but Borealis is taking his game up a notch now!

Borealis moves towards Almasy, but comes to a sudden halt. He quickly spins on his heel and darts to the far corner, where he leaps onto the second rope and flexes his arms for the crowd. Again the fans meet him with a roof-rattling chorus of boos, and again Borealis seems confused. Borealis steps down and angrily kicks the middle rope, turning back to Almasy.

Jhub: Still no dice, buddy.

Borealis scoops Almasy up, but Almasy rises with a sharp European uppercut that takes Borealis off his feet. The crowd explodes as Borealis scrambles away from Almasy, who gives chase all the way to the far turnbuckle. Borealis turns back over as Almasy moves in, and he hooks Almasy by the tights, pulling him hard into the corner. Almasy stumbles and flies shoulder first into the steel post, and Borealis follows with a school boy!

Bryan: Roll up from Borealis! ONE! TWO!!! Almasy narrowly escapes, and we almost had a pinfall there!

Jhub: That was a big opportunity for Borealis, but Almasy was able to wriggle out of there. Almasy’s got to be careful, though! Borealis does have the size and power advantage, and had Borealis been in better position there on that roll up, he might have scored the pinfall.

Pete: That was just a cheap move by Borealis! Almasy’s playing cleaner than that.

Borealis climbs to his feet, looking more focused now. He waits for Almasy to stand and immediately drops him with a high dropkick, snapping Almasy’s head back violently. Borealis immediately dives in for a hook of the leg.

Pete: Here’s another pin attempt! ONE! TWO! And another kickout by Almasy!

Jhub: Borealis is being too cocky with his pinning attempts, he needs to respect the ability of Almasy! Almasy may be giving up some weight, but he’s still a world-class athlete and is strong and flexible enough to kick out of a half-assed cover.

Borealis pulls Almasy back to his feet and lifts him off the mat, sitting him down on the top turnbuckle. Borealis reaches up and nails a deafening knife-edge chop, eliciting a surprised “Whoo!” from the crowd. Almasy grimaces in pain as Borealis climbs in front of him, hooking him up for a superplex! Almasy struggles for a moment but Borealis powers him to the top rope, immediately pulling him down and into the huge suplex!

Bryan: SUPERPLEX by Borealis! What a move! That could do it!

Jhub: Almasy’s hurt! Make a good cover now, kid!

Bryan: Here’s the hook of the leg! ONE! TWO! THRE—NO! Kickout by Seymour Almasy!

The crowd explodes with cheers as Borealis sits up, repeatedly pounding the mat with his fists in protest. Borealis glares at Briggs, but receives to fingers in his face in reiteration.

Bryan: Almasy is showing great resilience here in his debut match, but a very frustrated Johnny Borealis is still in control!

Pete: I dunno, he’s starting to lose it, JB. What’s it gonna take to keep Almasy down?

Borealis gets to his feet and drives Almasy face first into the top turnbuckle, bouncing him out of the corner. Borealis quickly springboards off the second turnbuckle and leaps back, but Almasy springs around himself and cuts Borealis out of the air with a perfect spinning roundhouse kick to the face! The fans explode in cheers as Almasy hooks the leg!

Bryan: COVER BY ALMASY! ONE! TWO!! No, Borealis gets his foot on the ropes! Borealis was saved narrowly by the ropes there, after getting hit with that big move that Almasy calls the Jecht Shot!

Jhub: That was a hell of a kick there, but Borealis is showing flashes of his own resilience! But he needs to recover quickly, or Almasy is going to take the advantage back!

Almasy lies on his back, still recovering. Both men begin to move at the same time, with Borealis pulling himself up the ropes and Almasy crawling to the other side. Almasy and Borealis reach their feet at the same time in opposite corners, and Borealis is the first to move. Borealis heads toward the opposite corner, but Almasy greets him with a stiff mule kick to the face! Borealis staggers back, but quickly recovers and flies in again. This time Borealis leaps straight to Almasy’s shoulders for a hurricanrana, but Almasy steps out and drives him to the mat with a vicious powerbomb!

Pete: OH YEAH! Powerbomb by Almasy!

Bryan: Almasy flips over into the cover! ONE! TWO!! THREE-NO! NO! Borealis JUST kicked out!

Jhub: That was close! How on earth did he kick out of that one?!

Borealis, stunned, remains motionless on the mat. Almasy wastes no time in climbing through the ropes and heading down the apron to the corner, where he springs up to the top turnbuckle.

Bryan: Wait a minute, Almasy is going up! He’s gonna put it all on the line here!

Almasy immediately rises to his feet, spins in place, and springs back with a soaring moonsault! The crowd explodes as Almasy turns all the way over and lands with a perfect leg drop over the neck of Jonathan Borealis.

Bryan: LUNATIC PANDORA! Almasy nailed the big legdrop!

Pete: That’s it, Johnny’s dead!

Almasy winces in pain and rolls onto his stomach, unable to capitalize. The chants of “HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!” fill the arena as the fans stand and applaud, flashing their cameras endlessly.

Bryan: That was simply amazing! Seymour Almasy has brought some absolutely incredible talent to Global Championship Wrestling, and this Portland crowd is on their feet in awe of his athleticism!

Jhub: You can’t deny it, JB, Almasy is something special! But he hasn’t put Borealis away yet! He can’t get over for the pinfall after that big impact!

Borealis remains motionless as Almasy clinches his teeth and shuts out the pain. With a firm but pained movement he flings a single arm over the champion’s chest.

Pete: Here’s the cover! One! Two! KICKOUT! I don’t believe it! Johnny kicked out!

Bryan: Jonathan Borealis simply refuses to die! He wants this match bad now, and it’s going to take something more to put away the GCW United States champion!

Pete: More than that!? That was amazing! What else could Almasy do?!

Almasy painfully pulls himself back to his feet, pulling a limp Borealis up behind. Almasy backs Borealis into the ropes and shoots him off to the other side. Borealis hooks the top rope and comes to a halt, but Almasy charges right after him. Almasy takes off with a flying forearm, but Borealis sidesteps it and Almasy lands hard on the top rope.

Bryan: Oh no, Almasy missed that big move, and he’s been hung out to dry on the ropes! And Borealis is looking to capitalize!

Borealis slips in behind before Almasy can slip back to the mat, grabbing him by the legs. Borealis quickly wrenches Almasy off the top rope and brings him down to the canvas with a face-first powerbomb! The crowd explodes with terror as Borealis flips Almasy over and hooks the leg for the easy cover!

Jhub: This could be it! ONE! TWO! HE KICKED OUT!

Pete: I don’t believe it!

Jhub: Seymour Almasy kicked out! I have no idea how he got out of that, but he’s still alive in his debut match!

Borealis collapses to the mat, exasperated and exhausted. With a grimace he pulls himself to his feet, when suddenly a roar of cheers comes up from the crowd. Eva VanLorne appears on stage, bolting towards the ring!

Bryan: Wait a minute! Here comes Eva!

Pete: Oh no, she’s come for payback! Watch out Johnny!

Jhub: Great, not her again… cover your balls, fellas!

Eva glides to the ring and stops by the apron, ducking down out of sight. Borealis, meanwhile, has slipped out of the ring, and walked back to the timekeeper, where his United States title belt rests once again! Borealis snatches the belt back and turns back to the ring, smiling confidently.

Bryan: Oh, now Borealis has his damn title belt, and he’s looking to use it! Don’t ruin this match like this, kid! It’s not worth it!

Borealis smiles and crosses the ring, standing behind Almasy while he rises. Almasy staggers about, but Borealis follows him, holding the belt at the ready. Briggs screams at Borealis from amid the monstrous boos raining in, but Borealis ignores the official and the crowd. Almasy finally stand and Borealis charges, but Eva leaps onto the apron and snatches the belt out of his arms! Borealis flies in and immediately gets wrenched down to the mat, where Almasy locks in his signature submission hold!

Jhub: CACTUAR CROSSFACE! Almasy has it locked in!

The crowd explodes with cheers as Eva locks eyes with a screaming Borealis. She blows him a kiss and drops his belt, exiting through the crowd as Almasy bears down! Borealis flails his limbs in a vain search for the ropes, but coming up empty immediately gives the signal to Briggs. The fans leap to their feet in applause as Briggs calls for the bell!

Bryan: THAT’S IT! It’s over! Almasy wins!

The reaction is deafening, almost drowning out the sound of “Otherworld” as it begins to play again. Almasy’s arm is raised and he quickly takes to the turnbuckle, posing with a smile to the crowd!

Winner: Seymour Almasy via Submission

Jhub: What an amazing main event! Borealis and Almasy went toe to toe, but in the end it was Borealis who made a mistake, didn’t watch his back, and just like that Seymour Almasy put him away!

Pete: That was a pretty impressive showing from both of those guys! But Seymour Almasy has made a statement here tonight on GCW WorldWide!

Borealis, clutching his arm in pain, rolls out of the ring and crawls to his title belt. He gives the belt a big hug and then looks up the aisle where Eva has departed. Borealis furiously gives chase, leaving Almasy alone at ringside to celebrate his victory. Almasy crosses to the opposite corner, posing for the other half of the arena.

Bryan: That was a big-time debut from Seymour Almasy, and this GCW crowd now knows first-hand why this guy is one of the most respected athletes in all of professional wrestling!

Jhub: Hey, that was a great win, but let’s not go overboard. That was just his first match. You can bet now that everyone in Global Championship Wrestling is going to want to make a name for themselves by wrestling Seymour Almasy!

Almasy turns his glance up to the stage, where a familiar form is standing. Rich “The Renegade” Rollins has strode out to the top of the stage, clapping his hands slowly in mockery of Almasy’s win.

Pete: Hey, wait! There’s the Renegade! What’s he doing out here?!

Bryan: We’re not gonna find out! We’re out of time! For Pete and The Jhub, I’m James Bryan, join us again next week for WorldWide!

Almasy and Rollins lock eyes in a venomous stare. Almasy remains perched on the turnbuckle, glaring up at Rollins, while the GCW faithful continue to chant Almasy’s name as the show fades out……..
