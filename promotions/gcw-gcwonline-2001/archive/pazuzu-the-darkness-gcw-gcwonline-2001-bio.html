PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS Biography Real Name PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS Nicknames The PAZUZ Hometown 7th Layer of Hell Birthday 5,000 B.C. Height 7' Weight 305 Fed Titles Television Champion (03/17/10 - 04/11/10) Fed Awards #5 in GCW Stock Exchange (287.3 points) 04/02/10 Biography PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS is an ex-demon from the 7th Layer of Hell, and the 1,233rd child of the infamous devil and underworld ruler, Satan. After coming to earth in December of 2009 to experience the human realm, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS got drunk and inked a GCW contract. He did not remember it the next morning. Without any previous in-ring experience, he beat his first opponent, Bishop Steele, on WorldWide 124, relying on his super-inhuman strength and thousands of years of experience slaying angels. However, his father didn't take kindly to the idea of his son in the human realm and placed a curse upon his head via the Amulet of Kroog~! Due to this curse, PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS no longer has his demonic powers and has been forced to develop his newly-formed human body into a workable pro wrestling machine. He won his first pay-per-view match at NC-17, 2010. A week later, he won his first GCW title, capturing the Television Title from Tempest in a match that many considered a massive upset. After defending the title on WorldWide 128 against SurReal, he lost the title to a reinvigorated Tempest on WorldWide 129. Along with his insidious imp, Gnark, PAZUZU enjoys wanking it, long walks on the beach, and philosophical debatse on the finer aspects of beef jerky. Other Feds Are you fucking kidding me? Who would hire this guy? Other Fed Awards Ranked #8 in PTC, March, 2010. Ranked #14, April, 2010. Strengths POWER, ENDURANCE, SPEED, RELATIONSHIP WITH JESUS CHRIST Manager Gnark Weaknesses TECHNICAL WRESTLING, LAZINESS, RELATIONSHIPS, ALCOHOL, VIENNA SAUSAGES Wrestling Style Power Strategy PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS uses a number of powerful moves to keep opponent off-balance. A constant barrage of these would will even the toughest of opponents into submission. Unfortunately, P-ZU is generally lackadaisical at this point and will attempt to converse with Gnark, banter at the fans, share family albums with the referee, or otherwise engage in some form of distracting activity. Conversely, on the defensive side he is very tough and in general cannot be physically overwhelmed. Unless your mom sits on his face. Finisher Move Shit, Here Comes the Lawsuit Finisher Description Points opponent's neck at a murderous downward angle and throws them as hard as he can at the mat...after drinking a 5-Hour Energy, thus doubling the impact. While laughing at you. And your weak character.
