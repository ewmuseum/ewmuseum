Paul Samson Biography Real Name John Paul Samson Nicknames Mr. Muscle Hometown Pico Mundo, CA Birthday June 11, 1984 Height 6'11 Weight 365 Other Feds NFW, Global Other Fed Titles 1/2 NFW Tag Champions, NFW United States Champion Wrestling Style Powerhouse, Brawler Finisher Move Samson Slam Finisher Description Running Powerslam Finisher Setup Move Setting em' Up Setup Description Samson will Irish whip his opponent into the corner, while there he will drive his right shoulder into the midsection three times. When the opponent begins to stumble out he will toss them onto his shoudlers for the Samson Slam Other Moves John Paul Samson was a total surprise to his parents, as they were not aware of him until the day his mother (Debra) went into labor. Paul Samson was sixty-five-years-old at the time, and his wife Debra was fourty-eight. When Debra had stopped having her period, she had thought it was merely due to menopause. There was no way she could be pregnant, she was fourty-eight years old! When she started gaining weight, they figure it was because of her age. Older women always put on weight, right? Paul reassured her it was merely because her metabolism was slowling down, she couldn't hold onto her girlish figure all of her life. They kept this mirage up for seven-months. On June 7th, 1984 Debra Samson had an incredible pain, and began leaking. Nervous she called her doctor, who told her to come right in. Paul was at work, and she needed a ride! As though luck were upon her side the nice young couple across the street had just pulled into their driveway. Debra ran out of the house and across the street to grab the attention of her friendly neighbors. John and Linda were their names, and they were what Paul and she had called 'Good People'. They would give you the shirt off of their back if you needed it, and so when Linda caught Debra running their way out of the corner of her eye she stopped to see what was the matter. Debra then told them she was experiencing incredible pains, and considered she might have Appendicitis. John and Linda climbed back into their small car, allowing Debra the room in the back to lay down. In probably five minutes they arrived at the hospital, and in another five Debra was being checked in while John and Linda waiting in the waiting room. After about twenty minutes, a doctor had come out to speak with them. First he asked if Paul was here, and when they told him that he wasn't, the doctor laid the shocker upon them. Debra was twenty-nine weeks pregnant, and she was in labor. When Paul finally made it to the hospital, John and Linda gave him the news. He was shocked to say the least, but seemingly excited. They had never had a child, and thought it would never happen. After being debriefed Paul found a nurse, who let him into the room with his wife. There, the doctor had explained to them what was going on. The baby may have complications as it was not full term, but he did have a chance to live. Debra had been checked into the hospital at 1 PM on June 7th 1984. At 6 PM June 11th, 1984 the son of Debra and Paul Samson was brought into the world. The doctors were surprised to find a completely healthy child just as much as the parents were. After some deliberation, Paul and Debra chose to name their son John Paul Samson, John because had it not been for their neighbor their son would have never been born and Paul after his father. And So there was John Paul Samson. John's life was well for the first fifteen years, excelling in sports and academics at school. John was a very happy teenager, watching wrestling during the week with his father, and going to life shows whenever they were close. But it was that summer before his Freshman year in High School that he and his father met with an unfortunate accident. They had been on their way home from a local wrestling show when a drunk driver blindsided the front end of their car. The accident put John's father into a wheel chair, where he remained for the rest of his life. On June 10th 2003, the day before John's nineteenth birthday, his father passed away. As a result John never attended High School graduation, but allowed the certificate to be sent in the mail. As far as he had seen it, if his father could not be there to see him get his diploma, he didn't want to be there either. This is when John (ignoring the advice of his friends, teachers and family) chose to make a career in something his father would have loved. Professional Wrestling. For the next three years John trained hard, and the results paid off. New-Found Wrestling scout noticed John at a local Pico Mundo show and signed him to a contract. When John realized he was on his way to the big times, he decided it was time for a small change. He needed a name with a ring to it, and what better to use then his father's name. Paul Samson. John knows if his father were alive now, he would be proud of him, as he is doing something with his life that his dad would love. John is wrestling. Entrance Music Do You Call My Name by Ra
