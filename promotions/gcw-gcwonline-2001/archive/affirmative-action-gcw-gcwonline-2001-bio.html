Affirmative Action Biography Biography A combination of 3 South Central athletes who turned to wrestling when their NFL and NBA hopes fell through due to questions about their character. While training to be wrestlers they saw white guys who hadn't been there as long, who didn't have half the in-ring ability they did get pulled into the PRIME's, the SCCW's even the GCWs. Sure they felt like grabbing some homies and some Uzis and wrecking shop, but they didn't. Instead they procured a lawyer turned failing talent agent, Roland Washington. Roland's quest is one men like Ron Simmons and Butch Reed put forth, to bring equality to wrestling. More specifically Global Championship Wrestling, I mean what bigger stage to balance the lack of diversity in wrestling than a Global stage? The three men are Junious Slaughter or June Bug, Craig Slaughter or CJ his younger cousin, Orlando Atkins or Big O. Any combination of the group can and will compete. Each brings different qualities and style to the matches so each combination is unique. June Bug, he's your all arounder. One of the most prized Varsity wrestlers in the country as Parade listed him in their top 5. CJ is lighter and more the high flying bump machine of the crew. He knows his homies are there to back him up so he'll do anything to grab a highlight. Big O is the powerman of the squad. He holds six weight lifting records in his high school district. The book is he has the biggest upside but doesn't take to coaching. All three resent authority, except for Ro-Ro, he's sold them on him being in this business for them and them alone. To see that they get a fair shake to compete for all the championships the white wrestlers are getting chances to compete for. Omar (Big O) Atkins- Press slams, Press power slams, power slams, bear hugs, general violation style mauling Entrance Music Bow Down by Westside Connection Ring Entrances The Megatron lights up with the image of two hands forming the gangland A symbols as the opening beat to 'Bow Down' by Westside Connection. The fans start belting out their disapproval as three young black men walk out on the ramp each with black poncho style tops. Across their backs in red is Affirmative Action. From alongside of them Roland Washington struts out bobbing his body around to the beat gleaming from ear to ear as he points to his small army of men. From left to right stand CJ, the high flier, Big O, the power, and June Bug, everything else. While they profile for the angered fans in attendance Roland continues to dance down towards the ring. As Mac 10 starts off the second verse June Bug takes the lead and the crew follows single file as Affirmative Action starts to the ring. Roland makes his way around the ring snatching the microphone from Andrews' hand and continuing his strut around the ring. As the squad reaches the ringside area they split and slide under the bottom ropes simultaniously pop to their feet and each throw their right arm into the air.
