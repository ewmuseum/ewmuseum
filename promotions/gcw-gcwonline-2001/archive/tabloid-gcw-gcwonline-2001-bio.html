Tabloid Biography Real Name Not known Nicknames Global Enquirer Hometown Hollywood, CA Birthday Gives his age as 28 Height 5'9 Weight 195 pounds Biography Like the National Enquirer or Star, Tabloid is a trainwreck that one simply cannot help but watch. Known for wearing a mask to hide his identity, Tabloid's path to the top is littered with secrets spilled to the right sources and sold to the highest bidder. A man who is a broker for information as others might be for diamonds or valuables, his successes in the wrestling world have been as much due to acumen outside the ring as skill in it. With the tenacity of the paparazzi, he searches for weaknesses in the lives and ring skills of his opponents, prepared to exploit either for victory. Behind this, though, lies an insecure man, forever wondering if anyone will expose his identity, and thus open him to the same sort of reprisal that he visits upon those unfortunate enough to cross him. Strengths Mind games, a quick-hitting finisher, technically sound. Weaknesses Paranoia, inexperience, lets the crowd get to him. Wrestling Style Technical wrestling with cheating and other stuff as needed Strategy Two-fold. Outside the ring is as important a battle as inside. Using any dirt he can dig up on his opponents, he engages in psychological warfare before and during the match. Purely wrestling based, Tabloid employs a technical style, with elements of rulebreaking when needed. He will take the occassional risk, but nothing more elaborate than his finisher. His goal is to break down and disorient his foe, hit his Guilty Pleasure to drive the wind out of them, and go for the pin. Finisher Move The Guilty Pleasure Finisher Description Full-nelson front Russian legsweep. Finisher Setup Move Anything. Setup Description ditto. Special Moves Sunset Boulevard, aka Amazing Red's Code Red, and Smile for the Camera, a camel clutch with fishhooking. Other Moves Striking moves like a clothesline, forearm smashes to the face, dropkicks, and general kickery. Your basic technical stuff, like hiptosses and armdrags and whatnot. Similarly, basic submissionery like leglocks and armbars and chinlocks. High impact moves that don't involve lifting, such as DDTs and bulldogs. Aerial stuff no more complicated than a top rope senton backsplash. Also eligible are the top rope elbowdrop, top rope fistdrop, top rope kneedrop, and top rope legdrop. No top rope moonsault, would make his finisher seem inferior. Moves he'll use more regularly include the following. Tabloid has a pretty nasty Piledriver that he uses to disorient opponents. It won't usually win a match, but is good for a nice long two. Likewise, he has a running Yakuza kick that he throws for a nearfall. Other more powerful moves include a top rope diving double kneedrop (both knees land on the opponent), a reverse neckbreaker, and a running dropkick delivered to a kneeling opponent's face. See the above sections for his best moves.
