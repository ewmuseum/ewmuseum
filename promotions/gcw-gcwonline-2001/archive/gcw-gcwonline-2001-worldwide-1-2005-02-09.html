WorldWide 1, 9 February 2005

9 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WorldWide 1 Nationwide Arena, Columbus, Ohio ---- Introduction: Welcome Back The GCW logo flashes briefly on the screen, before quickly fading out. The shot opens into a wide view of a frenzied crowd. Every last one of the seventeen thousand people in Nationwide Arena are on their feet, literally leaping in enthusiasm. The arena lights are dimmed, save for a single spotlight in the center of the ring. Standing there, clad in a perfectly pressed new tuxedo and looking fit and young, James Bryan stands with a microphone in his hand. From beneath a thick brown goatee he smiles, his eyes darting across the crowd as the camera zooms in for a closer look. Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen… Welcome to Global Championship Wrestling. He states the words slowly and methodically, but the crowd explodes nonetheless. With another sly grin, Bryan continues. Bryan- Thanks to each and every one of you who are here tonight, and to each and every one of you watching at home. With your support, Global Championship Wrestling will once again become the greatest wrestling promotion there is. So, without further ado, Global Championship Wrestling presents… WORLDWIDE!!! Bryan’s last words bring an enormous explosion from the rafters, as blue and silver pyro begins pouring down from the ceiling! ‘Wake Up’ by Rage Against the Machine begins booming in as the lights in the arena come up, and a string of blue flames runs down from the rafters to the stage, colliding with it to create an incredible blast of fire rising in front of the MegaTron. The fireworks finally subside, as the cameras begin panning rapidly over the crowd, displaying each and every fan to be going wild with anticipation. James Bryan’s voice returns overhead from the broadcast booth, sounding much more enthusiastic this time. Bryan- Welcome back, everybody! After nearly two long years, Global Championship Wrestling has returned! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan, back with you again at the broadcast booth here at ringside! And it’s my distinct pleasure to introduce to you my new broadcast colleague, and the PRESIDENT of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! The crowd’s incredible reaction continues as Wake Up blasts into the foreground again, and the cameras focus on the stage. The new stage is large and raised from the ground, with an enormous steel version of the GCW logo framing the MegaTron above the entrance. A moment passes before a tall, fit figure emerges onto the wide, long stage. Clad in a fine Italian suit and a broad smile, Steven Caldera opens his arms in welcome to the crowd. The roar is massive for Caldera as he heads for the ring, slapping hands with the fans on his way. After just a few long strides he reaches the ring and slides inside, where he is promptly handed a microphone by ring announcer Joey Andrews. Caldera nods in appreciation as the music subsides overhead. Caldera turns in a full circle, taking in the spectacle, as cameras flash over the ring to capture the momentous occasion. Caldera slowly observes the ring itself, then places a hand on the top rope and gazes upward to the bright lights shining down on the ring. After a few moments Caldera raises the microphone to his mouth and begins to speak. Caldera- I’ve always known that promoting professional wrestling is my calling, my purpose for being. But these past few weeks have brought a wealth of doubts to my mind. I found myself, as we grew closer and closer to this moment, wondering if I still had it in me. If I was still capable of running an efficient and entertaining wrestling promotion. And with that doubt came a lot of reflection, realizing how much I’d changed in the past few years and wondering how it would effect me. But now, standing beneath these lights again, on the biggest stage in the world of professional wrestling, there’s no doubt about it. Steven Caldera and Global Championship Wrestling are back! The roar is incredible in response to Caldera’s words, as every fan in the arena jumps to their feet again to applaud the owner. Caldera- Before I came out here tonight, I planned on promising to run this company right, swearing never to manipulate things to my advantage again. But the moment I stepped through those ropes, something dawned on me. I’m the owner of this company, and I have the right to do whatever the hell I want! The crowd, still supportive of Caldera, erupts once again. Caldera- Now, that doesn’t mean I’m not a little older and a little wiser. I’m not the same stubborn son of a bitch you all used to love to hate. But I do feel that I know better than anyone how to handle matters out here, so let it be known that my authority is absolute, and there is absolutely nobody back there that can circumvent it. That said, I promised everyone an announcement, and here it is. In regards to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship, we have a new title belt, and a new belt deserves a new champion. In case anyone out there hasn’t heard yet, we’ll be crowning that champion in six weeks, in a six man ACWF match! Another loud explosion from the crowd, anxiously awaiting the legendary match. Caldera- Now, with six open spots for that match, it leads to a very interesting question. Who gets in? Who gets the shot? Well, I pondered this for a long time and considered all the ways we could decide six contenders to the title. But in the end, I decided that there’s only one guy qualified enough to decide the participants for that match… me! So the task before each and every member of the GCW roster is this… Impress me. I want everyone to wrestle their hearts out. Because every match counts, every win and every loss matters. The six guys that impress me the most are the guys that will have the privilege of competing in ACWF Six for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! As the crowd applauds again, the thin strains of Might of Rome begin creeping into Nationwide Arena. The cheers immediately sour as a magnificent form appears on stage. Caldera’s head snaps around with a knowing grin as a familiar face begins heading for the ring. Octavian, former 3-time GCW champion, is wearing a magnificent gold-chain male vest over a solid black silk shirt, black pants and a menacing scowl. Clearly more muscular than the last time he was seen, Octavian appears to be in superb condition, and walks smoothly to the ring, ignoring the boos from the crowd. With a light step he climbs the steel steps with a dignified air, steps through the ropes, and produces a microphone of his own from his back pocket. Caldera meets eyes with Octavian with a firm stare, as Octavian begins to speak in his Roman accent. Octavian- It’s- Octavian hardly begins before his voice is drowned out by the jeers of the crowd. The fans are on their feet expressing their disgust with the Italian Stallion, but Octavian remains patient. After a moment the boos subside, and he presses on. Octavian- It’s good to be back in this ring with you again, Steven. I can’t tell you how good it makes me feel. Because for the past two years I have been waiting very patiently for this day to come. Most people never thought they’d see GCW again, but most people don’t know you like I do. I know you better than you do, Steven, and I always knew you’d bring GCW back from the dead. I knew your ego wouldn’t allow you to stay out of the spotlight forever. And now, as always, I am here to remind you that your power is not absolute. That as long as you exist, I will be there to fight against you. You and I are opposites, Steven, and we will always be in conflict. So don’t be so arrogant as to think I won’t do everything in my power to take this company for you. Your purpose in life is to promote wrestling. Mine is to take it from you. The crowd boos, and Caldera is observably affected by this. But his voice remains strong as he responds. Caldera- I had a feeling you would be the first to come out here, Gaius. I just knew you wouldn’t be able to wait your turn to get your voice heard. So I’ll tell you what I’m going to do. Rather than fight you, or argue with you, or try to stop you, I’m just going to sit back and listen to you. Then, when you’re done, you’re going to turn your Roman ass around and walk straight back to your locker room, and wait there until I tell you to come out. The crowd cheers in response as Caldera simply backs into the corner, tucks his microphone under his arm, and looks at Octavian in impatience. Octavian- Fine then, I’ll tell you what I want. I want what’s mine. I want the GCW World Heavyweight Championship back! The crowd boos at this predictable answer, but Octavian ignores them. Octavian- You see, when I heard you say that you were giving six men a shot at that title, it occurred to me that this simply isn’t fair. It isn’t fair because of everyone back there, Steven, nobody has the credentials to carry that title that I do. I’m a three time World Champion, Steven… The ONLY three time champion! I held the title for seventy three days! I successfully defended that title more times than anyone else! And, most importantly, Steven… The last time I won that title, I had it TAKEN from me by you! I never lost the GCW World Title, Steven, it was stolen from me. And now I order you to give it back! The crowd’s boos drown out all sound in the arena, as Caldera contemplates his next move. Before he can respond, however, a new sound fills Nationwide. Just Got Wicked begins booming overhead, and all eyes turn back to the stage. Clad in blue jeans and no shirt is Jason Dread, who is received with a mixed reaction from the crowd. Dread ignores the fans reaching out to touch him and slides right into the ring, accepting another microphone from Joey Andrews. Dread takes a position directly between Caldera and Octavian, glancing at both of them. His music fades as he finally turns his back to Caldera, facing directly at Octavian. Dread- You know, I never realized until just now how good it feels to be standing in front of a GCW crowd… The fans explode in appreciation, though Dread’s focus remains locked on Octavian, who studies him curiously, a little upset at being interrupted. Dread- And to subject them to that kind of bullsh*t isn’t something I’m prepared to do. Greatest champion of all time, eh, Octy? Let’s think this one through a bit. When did you get your big break? When you became leader of the Establishment. Oddly enough, I got to the top as leader of the Establishment, too. You held the title for three months… I held the title for three months. Sure, you beat me by a few days, but the fact is my reign will always be listed right next to yours as greatest ever. But there’s a difference between you and I, Octy. You were handed the GCW Title by the man behind me. Me? I worked from the bottom up to get there. I had to wrestle with Dan Rivero for two months before getting a shot at the spotlight. You say you’re Caldera’s opposite? Well then I’m yours. Because we’ve done the same things in this company, Octy, but I’ve done them all by myself. Are you really mad at him for taking away your title? How can you be mad at him for taking something away from you that he gave to you in the first place?! The crowd bursts out in cheers as Octavian glares angrily at Dread, taking a step forward. Caldera instantly springs forward to stop any potential outburst, stepping between the two. Octavian sighs and steps back a few feet, his eyes burning a hole in Dread. Octavian- You? You could never have been if it wasn’t for me, Jason Dread. I created the Establishment. I created your identity. And you stole it from me. Considering the fact that you simply followed the trail I blazed, I should think you ought to show a bit more respect to the man you owe your life to. Dread, angered by the remark, tries to push forward but is restrained by Caldera. Dread- I’m sick and tired of having to listen to you talk about how you’re the franchise of this company. Tell you what. You want to prove yourself? Earn your way into the ACWF match. If you want to prove that you’re really better than me, that you’re the original, then earn that shot into the ACWF match and win the title for yourself. I guarantee I’ll be seeing you there. Octavian- I have already earned the right to compete for that title as the greatest champion of all time. And you, Steven, are not the only man capable of deciding who deserves to be there. I- Octavian’s voice is immediately drowned out by a familiar drum beat. The heavy tones of My Own Summer flood Nationwide Arena, and the Columbus crowd reacts with the biggest explosion of the night! A few moments pass before The Jhub comes jogging out on stage! The explosion is deafening as cameras begin to flash again, bathing The Jhub as he basks in the glow of the arena. The three men in the ring all retreat to a neutral corner as The Jhub heads for the ring, smiling all the way down. He slides into the ring and perches himself on the remaining turnbuckle, throwing his arms up in the air to the delight of the crowd. Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen, this is a shocker! I had no idea The Jhub was back with GCW, but here he is before our very eyes! This crowd is going absolutely wild, and this situation may start to get out of hand! The Jhub hops back down and expertly produces a microphone from his belt. Caldera smiles, apparently having been in on The Jhub’s presence, but Dread and Octavian stare in amazement as The Jhub observes the ring’s occupants. Jhub- Octavian… ya got your Green Card yet?? The crowd erupts in appreciation as Octavian simmers in anger. Jhub- And Jason Dread… last time I saw you, you were lying on your back, staring up at the lights as yours truly left with his new world title belt! Dread shakes his head in disbelief as the crowd applauds again. Jhub- And Steven… you're still a choade...but I’m damn glad to be back! But how could you disrespect me by calling yourself the authority on ACWF? After all, everyone knows The Jhub is the Mohammad, Moses, Jesus, Vishnu, and just flat out GOD of the ACWF! More applause from the crowd, as The Jhub smiles back at the camera. Jhub- You see, I was doing just fine sipping martinis and getting massages from blonde Swedish women all day long, but when I heard those four letters, I knew it was time to come back. After all, cold hard pavement and a nice powerful forklift beat a pair of double d’s any day, right? The crowd chuckles in disagreement, but continue to applaud The Jhub. Jhub- Ok, ok, I'm full of it. There's nothing better than a nice pair of tig-ass-bitties. The crown erupts in agreement. Jhub- So I’ve got to say, guys, before either one of you start tearing each other apart for the title of best champion ever, maybe you should consider what you’re getting into. Because I’m here tonight to tell you all that The Jhub will be in ACWF Six, and any man stepping through the chains is going to find himself screaming to get out by the time I’m done with them! Caldera- Well, it seems we’ve got a bit of a problem here. Just because you three think you deserve the shot doesn’t mean I’m just going to hand it to you. You three all have great résumés, but you’re going to have to earn it all the same. Anyone that wants in is going to have to fight for it. Octavian, you’ve got a match tonight. You two? You want to impress me? Then I’ll tell you what. You can do it tonight. Because tonight it’s going to be The Jhub versus Jason Dread! The crowd explodes in excitement as The Jhub glares across the ring at a confident Jason Dread. Caldera- Now, if you don’t mind, I have a show to run. So what do you say we get this thing on the road?! Established cues up, signaling an end to the conversation. Octavian is the first to bail out of the ring, quickly heading for the back. The Jhub and Dread lock eyes again, before Caldera ushers The Jhub out. The Jhub and Caldera begin walking towards the back, with The Jhub saying something to Caldera. Dread finally raises his arms to the crowd for one more pop, then steps out of the ring and heads for the back. Bryan- Wow! Steven Caldera has set the story for WorldWide tonight, as The Jhub, who we didn’t even know was going to be here, takes on Jason Dread one on one! I will be joined by President Caldera in just a few moments, as he heads to the back for the final preparations for WorldWide. In the meantime, let’s take a look at what WorldWide holds for us tonight, here in GCW’s hometown of Columbus, Ohio! An image of Niklas Bauer appears on the screen, showing the Hardcore title belt below him. Bryan- First up tonight we have the match for the GCW Hardcore championship! The massive German Niklas Bauer has earned the first shot, but so far nobody has stepped up to face him. I can’t say that I blame everyone for not wanting to step into the Hardcore environment with that man, but surely someone wants a shot at the belt. And, of course, as soon as that match ends, the 24/7 rule goes into effect, and the belt can be decided any time, anywhere! Bauer’s image disappears, replaced this time by a profile of NBK, his intense glare facing a shot of none other than Tristain Mayhem. As soon as Mayhem’s picture hits the MegaTron, the crowd explodes in anticipation, though a noticeable shroud of boos falls over the reaction. Bryan- And how about this, ladies and gentlemen! Former GCW Champion, the man that dethroned Octavian, Tristain Mayhem makes his return to GCW here tonight! There’s more than one person backstage with a bone to pick with this guy, and it’s going to be a tremendous moment when he steps back into the ring! The next shot up shows three images, from left to right, Twisted, Mike Hardy, and Octavian. The image of Twisted elicits an explosion from the crowd, as JB continues. Bryan- And in a Triple Threat match tonight, we’ve got Twisted, Octavian and Mike Hardy going at it! Twisted and Octavian have never wrestled, ladies and gentlemen, and tonight’s their night! But Mike Hardy wanted to get his name thrown into the hat for ACWF, and he’s managed to get in the middle of those two. I don’t know what he’s thinking, but the undersized former cruiserweight champion is going to have a hell of a time against two of GCW’s best. Finally, Toxic and Toad appear on the MegaTron, earning yet another enthusiastic eruption from the crowd. Bryan- And one of the most storied rivalries in history continues tonight, as the youngest world champion ever, Toxic, goes up against the Hardcore Legend himself, Toad! I can’t wait to see this one! ---- Not Quite As Intended... The shot heads backstage into a hallway, where Steven Caldera walks with a large grin on his face. The most powerful man in wrestling? No, no, no. The richest human in entertainment? Much too egocentric. A controversial and brilliant creature whose cunning is the only thing truer than his sophistication. Spot on. Steven Caldera can’t keep back the smile as these through flow through his head. GCW’s head honcho had just finished his speech concerning the promotion and its World Championship, last held by its youngest title holder, Toxic. Caldera walks down the backstage hallway toward his temporary office for the night. He could not wait to get back out to ringside to continue the show with JB. But wanted to savor the moment for awhile. Maybe he would savor it with a fine cigar and then carry on more business. He hears an odd light buzzing noise as he begins to open the door to office, but thinks nothing of it. When the door is fully open he sees the cord and what that cord is attached to. “Can I, please, once, open my office door without seeing you in it?” Steven Caldera shouts. Twisted, a former World Champion, is on his knees in Steven Caldera’s office with a vacuum cleaner while trying to clean under the couch. The head of the cleaning unit won’t fit. “It’d be nice if you weren’t in here doing something weird, either.” Twizzy continues to try and get the cleaner under the couch. “It won’t fit. Your couch is faulty.” He stands up and strokes the whiskers on his chinny-chin-chin. “I know what to do,” he states as he claps his hands together. He starts to walk out of the room, but Steven Caldera stops him. “Wait. Where are you going?” Steven asks. Twizzy looks at Steven, confused. “To the get the ax, obviously.” “Why the hell would you need an ax?” “The couch isn’t going to fit through the doorway.” Steven blinks. Twisted blinks. “Why exactly,” Caldera begins, “are you in here cleaning at all?” The Junkie smiles, “I’m trying to impress you, Caldy. Just like you said.” “That’s not really what I meant.” “But I really want that title shot.” “I know, Twi-“ “I really want it Caldy. I need it. I can’t let Toxic get that title.” Twizzy’s eyes darken and grow wide. “He can’t have it. He took it from me and he can’t have it.” Twizz moves in closer to Steven to whisper in his ear. “I hate Toxic. He’s so evil he could strain matzah ball soup. Yeah, the soup with the ball.” Twisted’s head begins to shake rapidly. His hands latch onto Steven’s face and he looks directly into Steven’s eyes. “I have a secret weapon though! I have the ultimate power! Space, time, Richard Linklater movies. They’re all a simple children’s toy compared to Twistodium. It’s magical. It’s all-powerful. Nothing compares. No-thing compares, to it. The power of Twistodium compels you!” Twizzy walks away leaving a baffled Steven Caldera. “That guy is messed up.” ---- LeStatt Knight vs. Niklas Bauer GCW returns from commercial to a long shot of the crowd, still excited over the opening segment. Bryan- Welcome back to WorldWide, ladies and gentlemen, and I’ve been joined now by the man who will be spending the next few weeks here at ringside with me, Steven Caldera! Caldera- Thank you very much, JB, it’s great to be down here right next to the action. It’s too bad your former broadcast partner couldn’t be here tonight, but I’m glad to do the job! Bryan- Yeah, it’s not that bad… This is a tremendous achievement, though, Mr. Caldera, and I’m excited about some of the faces backstage. But I have to ask you about something… of all the stars I saw back there earlier, I never saw Toxic. The big man is supposed to be wrestling here tonight, where is he? Caldera- Hell if I know, JB. I haven’t heard from him all day. Last I heard he was on vacation in Cancun. He’s definitely not here yet tonight. He’d better make it here, because losing for not showing up isn’t very impressive. Bryan- Certainly not! Well, this is a historic night, ladies and gentlemen, and we’re going to get underway with our first match, with Niklas Bauer going for the Hardcore Championship! "Wer bist du" by the Megahertz sounds and the Donner Tier Niklas Bauer makes his way from the backstage area to the ring carrying and waving the German flag in the air. Along side with him is his translator Troy Newman. The fans come to their feet in boos for the Germany’s strongest man. Joey Andrews- The following hardcore contest is set for one fall, and is for the GCW Hardcore Championship! Making his way to the ring being accompanied by his manager Troy Newman weighing in at 318 pounds from Werneuchen, Germany…Niklas Bauer! Both men make their way down to the ring, Bauer with a stare at the ring acting as if there is nobody in the audience. Both men make it through the ring ropes and Bauer waves proudly his nations flag to the chants of "USA" that have made it across the arena audience. He then hands the flag to Troy who continues to wave the flag in honor of their great country. But he just hands it back to Bauer for him to wave it in his respect to his heritage. Troy grabs the microphone out of the hands of Joey Andrews as Joey was about to make his way out of the ring and walks to the center of the ring and puts the mic to his mouth and begins to speak. Troy- Let me introduce to you the man that is Germany’s strongest man and the strongest man and will be the most dominate man is this company…Niklas Bauer. Bauer raises both is arms in the air to the boos of the crowd and after the Bauer haters quiet down Troy tries to talk again. Troy- You all have the sheer pleasure in watching the Thunder Beast annihilate everybody and anybody who steps into the ring with the most dominant man in the world. There is not a single person smart enough, strong enough, good enough to have the guts to take on Niklas Bauer. There really isn’t such a person. Niklas Bauer represents the power and strength of Germany during the time of the World War. Nobody wanted to take Germany on because they knew no matter how hard of a fight they put up they would fall down even harder. And that’s how it’s going to be here in GCW. Niklas Bauer is going to take care of all the other superstars here on his way to claiming the World Title and nobody will be able to stop him. Troy asks Bauer for the flag as they exchange the microphone for the German Flag. Bauer- Ich bin der stärkste Mann in der Welt!!! Caldera- Well, I don’t recall telling him he could wave that flag out here, but I guess there’s no sense in telling a three hundred pound man he can’t have his way. Bryan- Well, was there any luck in finding a challenger for Bauer here? He’s not just going to be handed the Hardcore title here, is he? Caldera- Absolutely not. I set up a match, and I’m going to deliver on it. Andrews- And, introducing his opponent… O’Fortuna hits overhead, its ominous tones playing as the arena dims. The crowd stands as a familiar dark figure appears on stage and begins stalking to the ring. Andrews- From Mexico City, weighing in at 266 pounds… LeStatt Knight! Bryan- Wow! Here comes one of the most sinister men in the business, and he should prove to be quite the match for Bauer! A very confident move by LeStatt Knight! Caldera- Hey, he said he wanted the match. You call it confidence, I call it insane. But hey, LeStatt’s a big guy in his own right. He can handle himself. The bell sounds, and the Hardcore title match is underway! Knight moves right at the massive Bauer with a series of devastating right hands, catching the German off guard. Knight sends Bauer into the ropes and staggers him with a big clothesline, but Bauer stays upright. Knight hits the ropes and comes back with another clothesline, making good contact but still unable to topple the big man. Knight comes back a third time, but this time Bauer picks him up with a huge spinebuster! Bauer gets a quick two count, but the resilient LeStatt Knight kicks out. Bauer easily picks Knight back up and lifts him high overhead with a huge guerilla press, before slamming him right back to the mat. Knight rolls out of the ring in pain, and makes a b-line for the timekeeper’s table. Knight shoves Joey Andrews out of the way and collects a steel chair, turning to see Bauer still in the ring. Knight slides back in as Bauer dares him to swing. Knight obliges and nails Bauer with a huge chair shot to the skull, but Bauer shrugs it off and nails Knight with a huge clothesline! Bauer then picks up the chair and nails Knight with five solid shots to the side before dropping for a near-fall. Unable to believe that Knight had kicked out, Bauer picks Knight up and tosses him back out of the ring. Bauer follows a wounded Knight around the ring, but Knight spins around and catches Bauer with a drop toe hold, sending Bauer face first into the steel steps! Bauer holds his face in pain as Knight pulls the top section of the steps free and carelessly tosses them right on top of Bauer! With the steps on top of Bauer’s head, Knight reaches back into the ring and grabs the steel chair, and proceeds to wail away on the steps with incredible volume! Bauer escapes from below the steps holding his ears, but Knight picks the stairs up again and once again throws them right at Bauer, this time nailing the German in the head! Bauer goes down and Knight covers for a two count. Knight rolls Bauer back into the ring and lifts him to his feet, planting him with a DDT for another near fall. Knight angrily slides out and grabs the steel chair again, placing it on the mat and waiting for Bauer to stand. Knight charges Bauer, but Bauer slips behind him and takes him down with an incredible Das Holocaust full nelson slam! Knight is out as Bauer makes the cover and gets the three! Bauer is the new Hardcore champion! Bauer is awarded his belt and he heads for the back, confidently displaying the belt over his massive shoulder. The camera gives us one last view of LeStatt Knight, who amazingly is on his feet and actually smiling at Bauer as he walks away the champion. ---- Toad vs. Toxic Toad arrives and heads for the ring to a tremendous reaction from the crowd. He steps into the ring and onto the turnbuckle and awaits his opponent. After a few minutes, however, Toxic does not arrive. Toad becomes confused and agitated, demanding the referee do something. The official just shrugs, as the crowd waits patiently for Toxic. Finally, as Toad is about to leave, Toxic’s music hits and the big man steps onto the stage! Toad backs up as Toxic produces a microphone and speaks on the way down the ramp. Toxic explains to the crowd that he was detained while trying to sort out just why he wasn’t being awarded a shot at the World Title, as the reigning champion. It seems he’d been stripped of the title, and like Octavian is being forced to earn a shot. Well, if he has to impress Caldera, he’s going to do it at Toad’s expense! Toxic slides into the ring and the bell sounds, and immediately begins exchanging blows with Toad. Toxic delivers some devastating shots, but Toad takes them and just keeps coming. Toad finally ends the exchange with a boot to the gut and an Irish whip to the ropes. Toxic bounces back and takes flight with a surprisingly agile flying shoulder block! Toad goes down but quickly springs up, only to receive a forearm to the face from Toxic. Toxic whips Toad hard into the corner, and follows Toad in with a devastating clothesline in the turnbuckle. Toxic takes Toad by the head and leads him across the ring, sending him face first into the turnbuckle. Toad bounces out and nails Toxic with a fist to the face, sending the big man down in one shot! Toxic rolls out of the ring as Toad follows him to the floor, but Toxic quickly sends him to the mats with a swinging neck breaker! Toad screams out in pain as Toxic quickly collects his opponent and rolls him back into the ring. Toad staggers to his feet but is quickly picked up by Toxic and sent careening to the mat with a belly to belly suplex. Toxic kneels next to Toad for a cover, but instead decides to nail Toad in the face with a series of big fists that bust Toad’s eyebrow open. Toxic lifts Toad back up and pulls him up over his shoulder, easily handling the 300 pound man. Toxic spins around a few times, displaying his awesome strength to the crowd, before powering Toad into a sidewalk slam for a two count. Toxic pulls Toad back up and whips him into the ropes, lowering his head for a back body drop, but Toad counters it into a Toad Slam! The crowd goes nuts as Toad heads for the top rope and signals for the Toad Jam! Just as Toad takes off, Toxic nips up and catches the 300 pound Toad in mid-air! Toxic hoists Toad over his shoulder and then brings him right down into a perfect Toxic Waste! Toxic makes the cover for the definitive three count, resulting in a huge chorus of boos from the crowd. Toxic stands and poses to the crowd after his big victory, leaving a bleeding Toad lying in the middle of the ring. Bryan- Wow, I’d say that’s a pretty impressive victory, wouldn’t you, Mr. President? Caldera- Hey, I’ve never doubted Toxic’s abilities. This kid is only twenty one, ladies and gentlemen, and he’s one of the very best.
