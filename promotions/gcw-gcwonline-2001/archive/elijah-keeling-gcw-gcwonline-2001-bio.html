Elijah Keeling Biography Real Name Elijah Keeling Nicknames The Planet's Only Carbon Neutral Wrestler, The Gore-Bore, Eli. Hometown Washington, D.C. Birthday March 15th, 1985. Height 6'1 Weight 221lbs. Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography "You look at that river gently flowing by. You notice the leaves rustling with the wind. You hear the birds; you hear the tree frogs. In the distance you hear a cow. You feel the grass. The mud gives a little bit on the river bank. It’s quiet; it’s peaceful. And all of a sudden, it’s a gear shift inside you. And it’s like taking a deep breath and going, 'Oh yeah, I forgot about this'." - Al Gore, An Inconvenient Truth. Other Feds N/A Other Fed Awards N/A Strengths Cunning, ring awareness, frustrates opponents by staying cool under pressure and ducking out of the ring to buy some time, has a tongue on him that often talks him out of trouble. Weaknesses His size can lead to him being bumped around a lot and outmuscled, as he relies on a lot of cheating he runs the risk of a DQ, borrows from different styles without really mastering one. Wrestling Style Sneaky/cheating, methodical. Strategy He's the tortoise rather than the hare... albeit a sneaky, cheating tortoise that nobody can stand. He'll bide his time, using ring-smarts and frustrating tactics to deal with an opponent's onslaught, then when the moment is right, go for an eye or a low-blow to get himself in the game. He'll methodically target the neck with nearly everything he does. When an opponent tries to make the big comeback he'll always try to catch them out with a quick inside cradle or roll-up. Has no qualms about winning a match in unglamorous or less-than-heroic circumstances. It's no difference if it's by the NVP in the middle of the ring, or by DQ when he gets smashed with a chair. The end will justify the means. In summary, he's bump-fodder until he sees a chance to target asoft-spot, then exploits the chance with all he has. Finisher Move The Nobel Violence Prize. Finisher Description Double-Underhook Piledriver, usually preceeded by a blind low-blow. Finisher Setup Move See finisher. Setup Description What that said. Special Moves GORE! GORE! GORE! - this doesn't involve any kind of Spear, or even a wrestling move... he just loves Al Gore and wants people to know it. RECOUNT! RECOUNT! RECOUNT! - his frenzied reaction to a close 3-count. Other Moves The Carbon Footprint - Garvin stomp to a face-down opponent. The Assault of Reason - With an opponent cornered he'll land a string of boots to the gut while lecturing the opponent on carbon emissions. For larger opponents that he can't do the NVP on, he'll resort to an inside cradle or any rope/ballshot assisted pin that gets the job done. When in control of the match he'll target the neck to set up the NVP, so moves he'll bust out will be: DDT, Enziguri, Snapmare/rolling necksnap combo, Guillotine legdrop, Kneedrop (leaping high) to the back of the neck, Swinging neckbreaker, Side Russian legsweep, Backdrop suplex, Yanking back on an opponent's hair to pull them into a forearm to the neck, Hotshot, Rear chinlock, Half-nelson suplex, Wheelbarrow suplex into a hotshot, Rope choke, Rope eye-rake. He's also not beyond a suicide dive to the outside, varying between simple and showy depending on how cocky he's feeling. Entrance Music Cult of Personality by Living Colour (preceeded by Al Gore's opening monologue from An Inconvenient Truth) Ring Entrances All at once, the arena’s lights shut out, plunging ringside into darkness. Into the blackout, the voice of Al Gore begins to speak, reciting his opening monologue from An Inconvenient Truth. "You look at that river gently flowing by. You notice the leaves rustling with the wind. You hear the birds; you hear the tree frogs. In the distance you hear a cow. You feel the grass. The mud gives a little bit on the river bank. It’s quiet; it’s peaceful. And all of a sudden, it’s a gear shift inside you. And it’s like taking a deep breath and going, 'Oh yeah, I forgot about this'." Cutting off the words of the former Vice-President, the dizzying guitar of Living Colour’s “Cult of Personality” starts up. With the camera still showing a flat black screen, the drums kick in, and the intro runs its course. "Look in my eyes, what do you see?" A lone spotlight then flicks on, and the camera is shown zoomed in on the smug face of Elijah Keeling, eyes closing and head dropping back and he breathes in the boos and whistles. Andrews: Hailing from Washington, D.C? he weighs in at 221 pounds? he is the planet’s ONLY carbon-neutral wrestler? ELIJAH KEEEEEEEEEEEEEELING!!! After licking his lips, Keeling casually makes his way down to the ring, covering his ears to block out the screams of the fans either side of the aisle. With still just the spotlight on him, up the ring steps Eli strides, before vaulting over the top rope and into the ring, holding his arms out to the baying crowd as he spots his landing. Slipping out of his linen suit jacket, Keeling checks his black elbow pads and wrist tape, before untying his linen trousers, dropping them around his black-booted feet to show a pair of black, blue and silver tights, based around the swirling smog pattern from An Inconvenient Truth.
