Hatchet Biography Real Name Unknown Nicknames The Dead Body Man Hometown Souhtwest Detroit Birthday 9/6//84 Height 6'2 Weight 240lbs Biography Hatchet is what you could call a diehard Juggalo ryda. Growing up in the hometown of the Insane Clown Posse he grew up with love of the clown in his blood. And just like his idols he had the love for the wrestling business and at the age of 18 he started training at the JCW Wrestling Academy. Now nearly ten years later he has stepped up his game and has set out on the road too spread the clown love around the world and plans too bring the juggalo army everywhere with him.
