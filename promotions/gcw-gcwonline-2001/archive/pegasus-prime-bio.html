Pegasus Biography Real Name Robin Porter Nicknames The Bronco Hometown Boise, Idaho Birthday October 10th, 1988 Height 6'1 Weight 188lbs Biography Robin Porter was born and raised in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Grewing up in a poor family lead him to some trouble in his life. he had to fight for everything he own. His two loving parents did everything they could to provide for him and his older brother. Pegasus gimmick is basically a humble, passionate person who has this burning sensation within himself to accomplish his dream and that is to become something greater than greatness. Other Feds (1x) WRW X-Division Champion, (1x) AW Champion, (1x) AW United States Champion, (1x)AW Tag Team Champion w/Clearly Other Fed Awards (3x) WRW Roleplay of the Week Strengths Using the Turnbuckles, ropes, and other surroundings. Manager Michelle Queen Weaknesses Powerhouse Wrestling Style Highflying, Technical, and Extremist Strategy -Pegasus is a world class athlete and likes to use his highflying background and technical skills as an advantage -Pegasus loves to take thing over the limit -His Speed and Technical style to get over in his match ups. He uses an array of different slams and clotheslines on his opponents, wearing them out. As well as minor submission moves to dim his opponent down. -Despite his size, Pegasus is a pretty fast hand to hand competitor. -He tries to keep a quick pace with the match -Pegasus uses a series of chain-style wrestling at the mid-point of the match. -He's compared to Kurt Angle mat style with Alex Shelly movement. Finisher Move The Glory Road Finisher Description Taking his opponent's chin over his shoulder, Pegasus drops to the ground, sending his opponent to the mat while snapping their neck against his shoulder. Similar to the Diamond Cutter/RKO. Finisher Setup Move Pegarana Setup Description Bouncing off the ropes, Pegasus does a cartwheel and lands on his opponent's shoulders, facing the same way as his opponent. The wrestler then spins 180 while still on his opponent's shoulders to the front and hurricanranas them to the mat.
