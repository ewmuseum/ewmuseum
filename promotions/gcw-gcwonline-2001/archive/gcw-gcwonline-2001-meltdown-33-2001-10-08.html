GCW — Card

Meltdown 33, 8 November 2001

8 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 33 Casper Events Center — Casper, Wyoming ---- Meltdown #33 (The lights in the arena dim, as the crowd begins to buzz. Slowly, as the Meltdown music begins, the lights come up, until the stage explodes with red, white, and blue pyro! The crowd goes wild as the pyro shoots to the ring, sending the turnbuckles up in flames. The pyro finally quiets, as the camera flies over the frenzied crowd. James Bryan chimes in as the Meltdown intro draws to a close.) Bryan- In front of yet another sold out crowd, GCW brings to you another edition of Meltdown! We’ve got an incredible event planned for you tonight, as we head in to the biggest Pay-Per-View event ever, NC-17. Yale- NC-17 will be coming to you from the sold out Madison Square Garden on November 25, and it’s going to be great! But here tonight, we’ll find out just a little bit more about that event! (The camera immediately heads backstage, where Toxic stands with his US title draped over his shoulder. He holds a Styrofoam cup of coffee in his hands, as he nods in agreement with the young lady beside him. The young lady is newcomer Lindsay Walker, who has introduced herself to the champion. Toxic nods the whole time as Lindsay talks, thought it appears that Toxic’s attention may be focused on Lindsay’s low-cut shirt than the conversation.) Lindsay- So, here I am. Toxic- So you are… (A small figure appears next to the coffee table, but is blocked by Toxic and Lindsay. Lindsay turns to the figure and cheerily introduces herself, though the new person does not respond. Toxic then takes a glance over, and jumps as he sees Bridget pouring herself a cup of coffee.) Toxic- Whoa! Hey there! I’m not coming near you this time! Bridget- Didn’t Luke warn you enough? Stay the hell out of my way! (With that, Bridget throws her newly poured coffee right into Toxic’s face. Toxic howls at the scalding pain, as Bridget laughs. But Lindsay then steps in between Bridget and Toxic, getting into Toxic’s face.) Bridget- What do you want? (Lindsay simply stares Bridget down, until Bridget finally laughs and walks away. Lindsay turns to tend to the wounded Toxic, who has finally wiped the coffee off his face. The camera leaves the two and heads back to ringside.) Bryan- Well, again Bridget has done her best to make Toxic’s life miserable. Yale- And Toxic wouldn’t dare touch her, because he’s too afraid of Luke Hanson! Bryan- Come on, Dave, he’s not afraid, he’s just not looking to be jumped from behind any more! Well, fans, as we gear up for NC-17, we have to take a look at what will be taking place. Already we know that we’ve got two cruiserweight matches, and President Caldera takes on Commissioner Danger in a street fight! Yale- And, at that, President Caldera stated last week that whoever it is he has to back him up will be arriving at NC-17! Bryan- But for now, we’re heading to ringside for the most bizarre matchup in GCW history! (The Tom and Jerry theme song hits, and the crowd jumps to their feet with smiles on their faces. Zippy emerges from the back with his Skeletal Finger Light glove, followed by his sister Gidget, who wears a Freddy Kruger glove. The two prance to the ring, arguing the whole way.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 223.2 pounds, Zippy and his Skeletal Finger Light Glove! And their opponents, at a combined weight of 124.6 pounds, Gidget and her Freddy Kruger glove! Bryan- I have no idea how this match is going to work, but I’m sure these two have it all figured out! Yale- Look at these two idiots! I can’t believe they’re letting them do this on the air! (The two siblings step into the ring with their gloves on, and the bell sounds.) Bryan- Well, here we go! Zippy runs right at Gidget, shining his light on her face! But Gidget turns away, slashing blindly behind her with her razor-fingered glove! Yale- I think those fingers are plastic, JB…. Bryan- Whoa, Gidget flails out with her glove, and catches Zippy with a shot to the face with the glove! (Zippy flies back to the mat, hitting hard with a grossly exaggerated bump. He moans in pain, though Gidget’s plastic glove seems a bit bent from the blow.) Bryan- Oh, for Pete’s sake… Yale- Gidget move in, but Zippy gets his glove up, shining his light right into Gidget’s eyes! (Gidget catches the beam in the face, and proceeds to fall to the mat as if blinded. She scrambles to get her vision back, in an act worthy of an Oscar.) Yale- This is the most pathetic match I’ve ever seen, JB! Bryan- Zippy gets back to his feet, as Gidget rises herself! Zippy moves in, but Gidget charges and takes Zippy down with a mighty shove! Gidget hops on top of her brother, and covers! ONE! TWO! Zippy gets a shoulder up! Yale- Man, oh man… Bryan- Zippy gets back to his feet, as Gidget buries her glove right in Zippy’s gut! Zippy hunches over, and Gidget claws him in the back of the head! (Zippy hits the mat again, in yet another hilarious act. The crowd laughs along as Gidget takes her glove off, sets it down on the mat, and heads for the top rope.) Bryan- Zippy is in trouble here! Gidget takes her glove off, and is heading for the top! Zippy is finally back to his feet, and Gidget takes off! There’s a big cross body! (Gidget comes crashing down on top of Zippy, and Zippy’s glove flies off. Zippy’s glove lands right on top of the Freddy Kruger glove, as Gidget begins slapping Zippy.) Yale- Wait a minute, one glove’s on top of the other! Bryan- The referee looks dumbfounded… but he’s counting! ONE! TWO!! THREE!! Oh, for the love of God, it’s over! (The crowd explodes with rather shocking cheers, as the Tom and Jerry Theme hits again. Gidget stops hitting Zippy, thinking she’s won.) Andrews- Here are your winners… Zippy and his skeletal finger light glove! Yale- So Zippy’s glove pinned Gidget’s glove? Bryan- I guess so! (The crowd continues to laugh as Zippy gets to his feet, and grabs his glove. Gidget angrily snatches her glove from the mat, and follows Zippy to the back.) Yale- Boy, am I glad that one’s over! Bryan- An exciting match, indeed! (As Zippy and Gidget head to the back, so do the cameras. They find President Caldera, who states that Zippy and Gidget is one of the most intense rivalries in the industry, and the only way they can settle their differences is in a Hell in the Cell at NC-17! After booking such a sadistic match for such idiots, Caldera goes on to talk about Danger, and how he doesn’t stand a chance at NC-17.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall.....Introducing first, representing Hellz Franchise! She is ICEBABE! Yale- We are here to see a great women's match. Bryan- We all know who's going to win, and that's the more experienced Icebabe! Andrews- And her opponent representing "Simply Perfect", She is Angela HATHAWAAAYY!!!! ( Angela walks out with a very good reaction. ) Bryan- I change my mind! The more beautiful girl will win, and that's is Miss Hathaway! ( Angela struts to the ring. The ref steps on the bottom rope and helps Angela in the ring ) DING DING Yale- Icebabe charges at Angela and takes her down with a body tackle! Bryan- Icebabe hammers at the beautiful face of Miss Hathaway! Please stop Icebabe! Please! Yale- Icebabe pulls Angela up by her hair and throws her into the corner! Icebabe is just slapping away at her! Bryan- Well, well, look who's coming down to ring side. Why it's NBK! Yale- NBK is right by the ring and looking in at Icebabe. ( Icebabe notices NBK and walks toward him ) Bryan- Icebabe just gave him the finger! Hahahahha! Yale- Angela runs from behind and hits a bulldog! We got a cover! ONE-TWO-TH-NO! Icebabe gets a shoulder up! Bryan- Angela pulls Icebabe up and snaps her down with a hair suplex! Yale- That was neat. Bryan- Very. Yale- Angela pulls Icebabe up, Icebabe counters and rolls her up! ONE TWO-NO! Only a two count! Yale- Icebabe picks up Angela and hits a DDT! Bryan- Icebabe goes to the top rope! Yale- NBK hopes to the apron and Icebabe sees him. Bryan- Angela pulls the ref by here and NBK throws Icebabe off the tope rope! Yale- Angela hops up and drops Icebabe with a mean spike pile driver! ONE TWO THREE! Angela Hathaway picks up an impressive win here tonight on Meltdown! (Angela celebrates her big win, as the camera heads backstage. Luke Hanson stands with Kevin Jacobs, and looks quite pissed off. Hanson complains about not having a match, and challenges Toxic to a rematch for the US title at NC-17. Toxic then bursts out from behind, clobbering Hanson with a chair. Toxic shouts that the hair shot was his acceptance, and walks away. Toxic and Hanson at NC-17!) (When the show returns, the camera once again go backstage. Commissioner Danger stands with Barry Goldman, and receives quite a few boos. Danger states that, as his first act of power, he’ll be setting up a miniature Hard*core title tournament, to crown a new champion since Toad left with injuries. There would be two semi-final matches on Meltdown in the coming weeks, with the finals at NC-17.) Bryan- Here comes, a match, made last week, due to some friction backstage. Yale- That's one of the strengths of President Caldera, Bryan. Andrews- Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is for one fall, and it is for the GCW Women's Title. Coming down the Aisle, weighing in at 132lbs. She is the GCW Women's Champion, Here's... KAAAAAAATTTTTTAYYYYYYYYYYAAAAAANAAAAAA! (eyyyy....ahhhhhh... eyyyy....ahhhhh....The beautiful people...the beautiful people...eyyyy...ahhhhh....) (Kat walks down the aisle to a few cheers from the crowd. she gets in the ring and holds up her women’s title with a smile on her face) Andrews- Making his GCW Debut. He is a member of the Lost soul Militia. Weighing in at FOUR HUNDRED AND FIFTY POUNDS, and standing SEVEN FEET FOUR INCHES TALL, He is the Angel of death ...Here...is...the GRRRRRRIM...RRRRRRRRRRREAAPERRR! (The Lights in the arena go dark...thump...a hush falls over the fans...thump...suddenly, a bright white spotlight shines from the entryway...thump...and a tall figure, appears on a horse. The Grim One makes his entrance, in a white horse, dressed in Armor. The lights behind him, bathe him in a white light. He rides down to ringside, and gets off the Horse.) Bryan- Look at that entrance! Yale- Never mind the entrance, look at that man! (The Grim One climbs into the ring. He stands still, and raises his arms slowly. As he does the lights in the arena come up.) Bryan- Wow. I...I don’t know what to say! Yale- That’s a first! Look at the look on Kat's face! Bryan- She doesn't look that worried. (The bell rings as Katanya walks up to The Reaper. She starts talking smack. She rears back and slaps the Reaper!) Yale- He didn't move! (The Reaper cocks his head to the side, as Kat still talks. She rears back to slap the Reaper. The Reaper grabs her hand. He picks her up by the arm, and turns her over, and spanks her!) Bryan- What the hell is that? Yale- I don't know (The Reaper grabs kat around the waist. He puts her on his shoulder and spins her around. He picks her up while spinning her, and throws her to the ground!) Bryan- That's at least a 10 foot fall! (Kat holds her stomach after the fall. The Reaper backs down, and gives Kat a chance to stand up. Kat gets up and goes after the Reaper, punching him in the face. The Reaper feels each blow) Yale- Wow! Kat is really hammering it to the Reaper. (Kat goes off the ropes...Flying clothesline to Reaper! He's still on his feet! Kat off the ropes again. Another clothesline! The Reaper is still on his feet! Kat to the ropes one more time...Clothesline...but the Reaper catches her!) Bryan- uh oh...I don’t think Kat's gonna like this! (The Reaper picks Kat up over his head again. POWERSLAM) Bryan- WOW! Kat is DONE! (Reaper with the pin. One. Two...THREE!) Andrews- The Winner and...New GCW Woman's Champion? The GRIM REAPER! (After the match, the show heads backstage. We find Tristain Mayhem talking to President Caldera, who seems to be giving him a bi of advice. Caldera seems angry at Mayhem’s actions as of late, but finally tells Mayhem not to believe Octavian. The show then heads to commercial.) (Meltdown returns to find The Dark Messiah with the new Women’s champion.) Reaper: The first seal is broken, Man called O'Neil. TDM: Good. Keep that title. Defend it with Pride. ::knock at the door:: TDM: Come in. ::Caldera walks in the door. Caldera shakes O’Neil’s hand, and smiles at him and the Reaper.:: Caldera: Gentlemen, how are ya. TDM: Very well, sir. Caldera: Good. Mr. Reaper, I Hate to tell you this, but i need to take that title back. I can't have a 7'4 man defending a womans title. So if I may... ::Caldera goes to grab the title, and the Reaper growls at him.:: Reaper: President called Caldera, I warned you about the seven seals. This is the first. TDM: Gabriel, give it to him. Caldera: Yeah, listen to him, Grim. Reaper: Here is your title, but remember, the Apocalypse has begun. ::Reaper hands the title over, and Caldera looks up at the Reaper, and walks out. The Reaper just stares at Caldera as he walks out.:: Rivero and Reaper head to the ring to battle for the TV title. Rivero takes over early, hitting O’Neil with some sharp suplexes. Rivero sends O’Neil into the turnbuckle, and catches him with a big splash that earns him a two count. But O’Neil sees a telegraphed back drop, and counters with a piledriver! O’Neil covers for a two count. O’Neil takes over, hitting Rivero with a big swinging neck breaker for another two. But Rivero manages to counter another piledriver with a slingshot into the turnbuckle, and both men go down! Rivero gets to his feet, and all the way to the top rope, where he hits the Rivero Elbow! But O’Neil kicks out at two!! Rivero can’t believe it, but his attention is drawn away from O’Neil as Twisted and Reaper make their way to ringside. However, NBK emerges from the back with a chair, clobbering Twisted! But Reaper takes over on NBK, sending him into the barricade. But a masked man then emerges out of nowhere, assaulting NBK, helping Twisted! The battle continues outside, as O’Neil hits a Penance! Rivero is out, but the referee is too busy trying to restore order outside. As O’Neil covers, Lance Star appears from the crowd, and hits O’Neil with a Star Sizzler from the top! Star rolls Rivero on top, and the referee turns just in time to make the three count! Rivero retains the TV title, and along with NBK welcomes Lance Star to the team of Simply Perfect! But the Militia storms the ring again, and Rivero sends O’Neil down with a shot with the belt! Star and NBK double team Reaper, but Twisted then hits Rivero from behind. Twisted and the masked man stomp away at Rivero, until Simply Perfect has been leveled… The masked man then pulls his mask off, revealing himself to be… Misdirection!! Backstage, Katanya is found shouting at someone on a cell phone. She sees someone coming, and quickly hangs up. President Caldera approaches her, and hands her the Women’s title back, explaining that she is once again the champion. Katanya simply snatches the title away from him, and leaves. After a commercial break, we head to ringside with Jason Dread! Dread gets into the ring with a microphone, and talks about the future of GCW, and how it lies with him. But the Jhub walks to the stage, shouting to Dread that the Jhub is the past, present, and future, and Dread is nothing! The Jhub then hits the ring, and the match is on. Dread takes over early with some right hands, and hits the Jhub with a back drop. Dread then hits the Jhub with a brainbuster for a two count. He then locks the Jhub in a figure four, working on the Jhub’s injured left knee. The Jhub manages to turn it over, forcing Dread to head to the ropes and break the hold. Dread comes back with a leg drag on the Jhub, and an elbow to the knee. But the Jhub gets the momentum back when Dread misses a moonsault! The Jhub fires back with some lefts and rights, and hits Dread with a big STO! The Jhub then goes for the sharpshooter, but Dread sees it coming and scrambles to the ropes. The Jhub picks Dread up, and hits him with a back breaker followed by a sharp elbow drop for a two. However, Rich Rollins then makes his presence known, walking to ringside. The Jhub sees him coming, and turns his attention to the outside. Dread slowly recovers in the ring, as the Jhub and Rollins argue. The referee tries to get Rollins to leave, but Rollins refuses! But suddenly the crowd erupts as Badger Benandanti hops the barricade, and slides into the ring from behind! Dread goes after the Jhub, but Badger hooks him from behind and plants him with a monstrous BadgerSlam! Dread goes out like a light, as the Jhub turns around! The Jhub covers Dread, but Rollins pulls the referee out of the ring, and decks him! The match ends in a disqualification victory for the Jhub, as Rollins slides into the ring and begins to pummel the Jhub! Dread slowly begins to recover, but Badger climbs back in to take him on! But the tide turns as Octavian emerges from the back, and hits the ring for his team. Right behind him, however, is Tristain Mayhem! Mayhem explodes on Octavian, hammering him with rights and lefts! Octavian, Rollins, and Dread finally bail out of the ring, leaving Badger, Mayhem, and the Jhub stare them down. After the final commercial break, we find President Caldera in his office. Just off camera stands a tall, dark figure. The two watch a monitor together, studying the events of the last match. Caldera explains a few things to the man, leading us to believe that this man is Caldera’s backup and future heir! Heading to ringside, Tristain Mayhem and Rich Rollins head to the ring once more. Mayhem opens up with some offense, letting out some frustration. Mayhem takes Rollins down with a back body drop, and covers for a two. Mayhem then hits Rollins with an awesome reverse DDT, earning another two. But Rollins turns things his way when he leaps onto the turnbuckle, and connects with a short moonsault! Rollins heads to the top rope, and hits a guillotine leg drop for a two count. Rollins then mounts Mayhem and hammers away, until Mayhem shoves him off. Mayhem gets back to his feet, and Rollins goes for a superkick! Mayhem counters by catching the Renegade’s foot, but Rollins comes right back with an ensiguri! Mayhem goes out, and Rollins heads to the top! Rollins takes off with an Anarchist Elbow, but Mayhem rolls out of the way! Rollins hits the mat hard, and Mayhem gets back to his feet. As Rollins rises again, Mayhem charges in and plants him with his big running DDT, Hammer On! But Mayhem is too worn out to cover. He finally manages to sling an arm over the Renegade, but Rollins kicks out at two! Mayhem gets to his feet, and climbs the turnbuckle! Rollins gets back up, but before Mayhem can take off, Rollins leaps with a dropkick, sending Mayhem from the top turnbuckle to the floor! Mayhem is hurt badly, but slowly gets to his feet as the referee holds Rollins back. But Octavian leaps over the guardrail next to Mayhem, and clobbers him with a steel chair! Octavian rolls Mayhem back into the ring, and Rollins covers! Mayhem somehow gets a shoulder up, leaving Octavian and Rollins in shock! Rollins picks Mayhem up one more time, but Mayhem pulls Rollins down with a small package! Rollins can’t get out, and Mayhem earns the three count and a huge victory! Rollins goes crazy, as Octavian joins him in the ring. Octavian then pulls Mayhem to the center of the ring, applying the Roman Rapper. Rollins grabs a microphone from Andrews. He holds it to Octavian’s mouth as the champion punishes Mayhem. Octavian shouts that Nanci’s child is his, as Mayhem tries to break free. Meltdown draws to a close as Octavian finally releases Mayhem, who lies broken in the ring….
