Sage Black Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday Dec. 4th, 1980 Height 5'11 Weight 187 Biography His appearance matters too, you shallow monsters. Scrawny, head somewhere between shaved and shaggy. Main distinguishing feature is the spider tattooed on his hairline above his left eyebrow. Also, dresses in expensive suits these days, very rarely dropping into the furcoats and leather pants of old. It is, needless to say, a sad day. Sage Black used to be Jordan White. As Jordan White, he was a druggie bastard through all of his L.A. high school career and only straightened up slightly to become a shitty masked wrestler named The Marauder. He eventually lost the mask and just used his own name. He has a career of some fame, and a fairly bad reputation. Things happen, he retires. He gets an extremely profitable company because a guy he managed beat some other guy in a wrestling match. He liquidates the company and claims all the assets. The guy he manages, Trey Tremendous, leads a successful and brief career in GCW, claiming the US title from LeStatt Knight. Jordan ends up running him out of the country, sometime after Jordan ends up marrying some chick named Danielle. Under a stress deluge--being conned into wrestling by an old manager gone bastard, his own halfbrother's halfbrother trying to kill Danielle, a town he founded for a group of his worshipers turned against him by said bastard exmanager, and a bunch of other shit that might get mentioned here and there--he decides to avoid falling back into heavy smack abuse by giving up the name Jordan White. He pins it on some sucker named Frank Black, in the process of deciding he might as while try to make some money off the whole deal. Taking the name Sage Black and quickly getting Jordan accepted into the newest iteration of the Establishment. Here, he was mostly tolerated until he outright insulted Caldera's judgment and claimed that Jordan's victory over Rollins had been fixed. This lead to a termination of the part of whatever his job was that required him to be an on-air presence. He hasn't been seen on television since, but is rumored to have retreated to a secluded city he purchased some time ago, where he gets wasted and lets other people run his organization as the money piles up. Of note: Sage is mindbogglingly rich. It's not even funny. I don't even try to put a number on it, for fear of losing the mystic nature of his wealth to something so simple as a precise, actual string of digits. Management Style Between derisive and friendly. Never very managerly. Special Moves 149.24 - A way better version of Jordan White's superkick. Often thrown leaping out of a crouch, very high impact. Other Tactics He lounges with the commentators; smokes, drinks, eats. He won't interfere unless he genuinely just wants to see the opponent suffer. If he steps in, he's stepping in with that scary superkick, or a top rope corkscrew moonsault into a 'rana for when he's feeling fancy. He really likes talking shit to Jordan and about Jordan, but will avoid putting on the microphone and sharing it with the world most of the time.
