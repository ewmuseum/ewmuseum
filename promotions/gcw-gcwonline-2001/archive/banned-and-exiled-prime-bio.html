Biography Banned & Exiled~! are the greatest tag team ever! They rock harder than any other, try are cool than any other, and yeah, they are awesome. I mean what is better than TWO WIW Hall of Fame wrestlers tagging up and doing whatever they want? (which usually include goofing off, annoying the staff, and harassing the hell out of Tessa) Other Feds n/a Other Fed Titles They claim the title of unofficial 2007 PTC Tag Team of the Year Other Fed Awards Both are WIW Hall of Fame members Wrestling Style Quick Tags & Dirty Heel Tactics Finisher Name Banned~! {A Superkick (You Fucking Suck!) from Markus into a German Suplex from Storm} Finisher Description A Superkick (You Fucking Suck!) from Markus into a German Suplex from Storm Setup Move Exiled~! {Shining Wizard (Record Breaker) from Markus as Storm hits a Spinal Tap} Setup Move Description Shining Wizard (Record Breaker) from Markus as Storm hits a Spinal Tap Special Moves Tap~! (Boston Crab/Camel Clutch combo) Move Set Spike Piledriver (Storm jumps, Stone holds) Tap~! (Boston Crab/Camel Clutch combo) The basic idea is that they are old school in the idea that they will cheat any chance they can and will try to keep the smaller opponent trapped in their corner. They also keep tagging at a regular pace to stay fresh. Biggest thing to note, they both have microphones in their corner so they can call the match for each of their tag matches. Entrance Music 'We Are The Champions' by Queen Ring Entrances The lights dim down, a hush falls over the crowd. A pair of familiar voices ring out over the darkness. "Weeeeee are the champions......AGAIN!" 'We Are The Champions' by Queen kicks in leading to a booming chorus of "B & E!" from one side of the arena and "SUCKS!" from the other. The unlovable (unless your name is Steven Caldera) duo of Markus Stone and Chris Storm appear from behind the curtain and step out onto the entrance ramp. Both are wielding microphones, ready to call the match with their unique spin. Andrews: And introducing... Storm: Hold it buddy, we don't need a hack trying to introduce the greatest thing since baby jesus. Boos (and still some cheers since they are frickin' awesome) come raining down hard and fast. Andrews lowers his microphone, grumbling to himself as he exits out of the ring. Stone: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages. Prepare your eyes, check your pulse, and grab onto the edge of your seats. Tonight, in that very ring, the greatest tag team ever, weighing in at a combined weigh of four-hundred and eighty pounds, we are BANNED & EXILED~! The fans once again boo for everyone's favorite Tag Team Champions as the two toss their microphones aside and get ready.
