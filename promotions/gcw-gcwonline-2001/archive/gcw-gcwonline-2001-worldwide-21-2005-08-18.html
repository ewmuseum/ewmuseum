Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #21 – Sacramento, California

This Means War -by Phyl

Michael Stevens sits with his face buried in his hands. The seat rests in a deserted hallway some empty little corner of the arena. A moment or two goes by without Stevens moving. He sits silently, the only sound his breathing. After a short time the sound of footsteps echo through the hall. Stevens doesn't move, assuming whoever it is will just continue past After another minute LeStatt Knight enters the scene followed closely by his bodyguard RaZiel the two men stop in front of Stevens looking down at him for a moment.

RaZiel: ( with a slight chuckle ) Well LeStatt it seems they let anyone in these buildings now a days.

LeStatt: (Now smirking slightly) Yeah garbage day isn't until tomorrow, and this trash really stinks.

The two await for Stevens to give them any kind of acknowledgment but at first he barely even moves, or even blinks.

LeStatt: Don't pretend to ignore me Michael ... LOOK AT ME!

Stevens slowly looks up, glancing first at RaZiel then at Lestatt, heavily he sighs.

Michael Stevens: Piss off, I'm not in the mood.

Without bothering to see if they go, Stevens simply lowers his head back into his hands.

LeStatt and RaZiel glance at each other and both nod in unison.

LeStatt: Poor Mikey has alot on his mind si? You may want to get your head straight Boy because come Annihilation you will need every bit of that energy.

RaZiel: Ya know LeStatt it looks like his girlfriend just (censor) his best friend ... maybe we should leave him alone.

Stevens twists his neck from side to side cracking it. Slowly he stands looking directly a Knight. His eyes filled with barely contained rage.

Michael Stevens: Are you deaf? I said piss off, go away. And Knight, muzzle your dog before I grind him into dust.

LeStatt: ( With a shrug ) Suit yourself Stevens, just remember that if you slip up I will break you in two.

LeStatt and RaZiel now turn their backs and begin walking away, but after they get but three or four steps away, LeStatt stops and digs into his pocket, pulling something from it.

LeStatt: Here’s a gift from me to you though Michael ... maybe it'll help you through these hard times.

LeStatt then throws the object and it lands at Michael's feet. The object is a pink pacifier hanging from a gold chain.

Stevens looks down at his feet. His eyes grow and his lips curl away from teeth. With a snarl Stevens leaps forward his hands outstretched as if to strangle Lestatt.

Michael Stevens: You bastard, I'll rip your damn throat out!

RaZiel steps in front of the charging Stevens, who causually pushes him to the side fury lending him strength to the move the bigger man. He reaches Knight and just begins to wrap his hands around the champion's throat when RaZiel grabs him from behind, chicken winging one arm. Knight pins the other arm and the two men muscle Stevens face first into the wall.

Michael Stevens: I'll <censored> kill you both

StartRob: LeStatt: YOU ARE IN NO POSITION TO THREATEN ME! Look at you Stevens, you are pathetic!

RaZiel: What should we do to him? Maybe tell him how much fun Sheena is having?

LeStatt: No, not yet ... although she has been a model citizen let me tell you. No Stevens we have something else we want you to do.

Snarling like a beast Stevens is able to push himself away from the wall, knocking both Knight and RaZiel back. Stevens once again goes after Knight, but RaZiel catches him in the kidney with a hard right hand. The impact takes Stevens down to this knees. RaZiel immediately pounces on his using his massive weight to pin Stevens down.

Michael Stevens: I swear to god Knight, if you harm a hair on her head, I'll kill you.

Stevens continues to struggle violently but just can't push the huge man off him. After another moment Stevens stops fighting. Putting his forehead to the ground, he sighs.

Michael Stevens: What? What do you want? You've got me ok, what do you want?

LeStatt: I want your full attention Stevens. You have done nothing but make a mockery of everything I have fought so hard to win and I am sick of it. I have done whatever it takes. So listen here you sorry waste of space. TONIGHT it’s my pleasure to inform you that your opponent will be RaZiel and you will beat him in that ring, if you don't? ( he chuckles ) well ... I am going to show Sheena what a REAL man is all about, comprendes?

Michael Stevens: Oh you've got my attention Lestatt, I hope you're prepared to deal with it. And I want you to remember what I said, harm a hair on her head, and I'll kill you. I don't care if I go to jail, I'll rip your heart out. Now get your mutt off me.

LeStatt: Save your threats Stevens ... They won't save you from being destroyed at Annihilation. (RaZiel now slams Steven's face into the ground and stands up. RaZ and Knight now back up a bit. ) GOod Luck tonight ... puto.

As the two men walk away Stevens lifts his face from the ground. A small trickle of blood leaks from his forehead, as he stares with intense hatred out at Knight and Raziel. The camera focuses in on Stevens's face as his lips curl away from his teeth, after a moment though his snarl fades away to a smile and he begins chuckling slightly. The scene fades away with Stevens chuckling just so slightly.

Standing he wipes the blood away from his face, and retrieves the necklace from the ground. Clutching it tightly, he pulls a phone from his pocket and dials a number.

Michael Stevens: Yeah, it’s me, listen I’m going to need a few things, and damn quickly, got it?

With that the scene fades to a close.

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Sacramento crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Ragin’ Roman” and “We Want Sheena” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Sacramento, California” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: Arco Arena is packed to the brim as GCW grows ever closer to Annihilation on Pay-Per-View! We’ve got twenty thousand strong here to witness another edition of WorldWide, as GCW’s tour of the west coast continues! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan here at ringside with The Jhub and Pete, and gentlemen, I for one am getting more and more excited about Annihilation coming up in just two weeks.

Pete: I sure as hell am! We’ve got two matches confirmed for Annihilation, and we’re going to see Rich Rollins defend both of his titles against Jonathan Borealis and Trey Tremendous in a double ladder match, and LeStatt Knight faces Michael Stevens for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship!

Jhub: It’s gonna be a hell of a show, guys, and once again yours truly will be at ringside to see it all up close and personal. We’ve got two matches already, but tonight we’re sure to end up with more! I talked to President Caldera earlier today, and he promised that anyone seeking a match for Annihilation would be granted one. So I’d like to take this opportunity to formally challenge you, Pete!

Pete: What?!

Jhub: Ha! Just kidding. If I wanted to kick your ass, I’d just wait by the buffet table with a crowbar.

Bryan: On a more serious note, fans, we’ve got a packed show tonight, as Michael Stevens will be in single’s action against an unnamed opponent, Octavian makes his long-awaited return to the ring against Twizzy, and we’ll see the debut of plenty of new superstars!

Jhub: Finally, a tag team other than Allied Chaos gets a chance in GCW! It’s about damn time.

Bryan: For right now, fans, let’s head backstage!

Enter Summers -by Jamie

As the match concludes, quickly the cameras cut to the backstage area, where we find ourselves outside the makeshift office of Steven Caldera. Suddenly we find ourselves inside the room; Caldera is reclined in a chair setting behind a large desk. In front of the desk, standing is a sandy blond haired man with his back to the camera.

Steven Caldera: If you'll just sign your name on the dotted line and you'll be all set for your development contract will be all set.

Unknown Guy: Developmental contract?

Steven Caldera: Is there a problem?

Unknown Guy: Well for starters...

Before his newest signee can say anymore, Caldera stops him and explains.

Steven Caldera: Look, I realize you were once somebody, a former star if you will. However you're going to have to face the fact you've not been in the ring in almost a year and from a GCW standpoint you're a nobody at this point. Heck you should be grateful that I'm taking a chance on you by giving you this opportunity let alone the shot to earn a full contract.

Unknown Guy: You're kidding right?

Caldera: This may not be the most flattering arrangement to you, but it's not like anyone else was knocking on your door.

Unknown Guy: I guess you do have a point.

Caldera: Now that’s more like it. As I was saying you do have the opportunity to sign a full contract after you've fulfilled your three match deal. That is if you make the cut...

Unknown Guy: The question isn't if, but when...

They both chuckle.

"What the heck!" yells an off screen voice.

Instantly we change scenes, finding ourselves in the hallway outside Caldera's office. Near the door, stands "Scandalous" Ryan Stephens eavesdropping and in an awestruck state due to what he's just overheard.

Ryan Stephens: That voice I know it. Nah it can't be him!

Suddenly the doors burst open and GCW's newest signee is standing face to face with Stephens. The unknown man is of his late twenties and of a muscular build. His face is offset by his chiseled cheekbones and the piercing blue eyes, that he wipes his sandy blond hair from. He’s wearing a sky-blue button up shirt and his worn khaki dress pants, tattered at the end of the legs hangs over his black Chuck Taylor low-top Converse.

Stephens: Jake!

Jake: Stephens!

They both are equaled surprised to see one another, let alone to find themselves standing outside the office of GCW owner and president. Stephens extends his hand, to which the latest GCW signee, Jake accepts.

Ryan Stephens: "Golden Boy" Jake Summers, what in God's name are you doing here? I thought you were like dead or something.

They both laugh.

Jake Summers: Not hardly, but things are far from being golden these days. Could be changing though?

Ryan Stephens: What do you mean?

Jake Summers: I just signed a developmental contract with GCW. Only three matches and little money, but every little bit helps.

Ryan Stephens: Say what? NWC is dead and gone, but you had a lot going for you man. Sure the place closed abruptly, but you had outside interests and other opportunies as well,... The real-estate business and all. However now... (momentary pause) No offense, but now from where I’m stand you look like shit man!

Summers surveys himself in his current state and eyes Stephens, whom is dressed is a neatly pressed three piece suit.

Jake Summers: Had is the million dollar word! Life is a delicate balance between order and chaos. One small disturbance, one minor infraction is all it takes and everything changes. Along comes a sound of thunder.

Ryan Stephens: A sound of thunder? What in hell are you talking about?

Jake Summers: The last year has been pretty hard on me and it grew into total mayhem in the last six months.

Stephens flashes a look that could only be explained by, "No shit Sherlock!"

Ryan Stephens: You don't say...

Stephens flashes that arrogant sarcastic smirk he's known for.

Jake Summers: The real-estate business. No more! The wife, the kids! No more! Three months in jail and now all I have is a bankruptcy to deal with and a pending divorce to go through. With no money coming in, the child support and alimony is going to drain me of what little funds I still have from the hidden accounts in the Caymans.

Ryan Stephens: Geez, she clean you out that quick? Well I’m sorry to hear that, but...

Could he be any more insincere? For Christ sakes, the guy just lost his wife and a business he built from the ground up.

Ryan Stephens: …. but a developmental contract?

Jake Summers: How did you…

Stephens cuts his former ally off in mid-sentence.

Ryan Stephens: Sorry I was just on my way to discuss some matters with Caldera and I couldn't help to overhear. As I was saying, you’re a former NWC regional heavyweight champion, a former two-time NWC J-Crown champion and on nine separate occasions you wore gold in the National Wrestling Council.

Stephens is a walking encyclopedia when it comes to facts from the former organization he and Summers both worked for.

Jake Summers: All of that is history. Things aren’t what they used to be. It's not like I planned on having to be in the ring again, but my real-estate licenses were revoked and jobs weren't exactly tossed at me. Wrestling is what I know most and for now I’ll take what I can get.

Ryan Stephens: That's a shame. A shame indeed, but I may be able to help you.

Summers is interested, but yet the look on his face is one of concern as well. He's hesitate to trust Stephens, but at this point he doesn't have a lot going for him. And his far from being accustomed to being the curtain-jerker anywhere.

Jake Summers: What do you have in mind?

Ryan Stephens: This business is a lot like poker. You've always gotta have an ace up your sleeve. I've got some business to discuss with Caldera, but for now go take care of whatever else you need to. I'll catch up with you later and fill you in on the details.

As Summers begins to walk off, scenes fade back to ringside...

Tag Team Match – One Fall

The Guild vs. Dimensioner & X Referee: Timothy Vale -by Dan the Man

'Wherever I May Roam' by Metallica starts up as the lights in the arena dim.

Blue and red laser lights splash over the crowd, slowly at first, fitting with the slow intro. But as the song kicks in, the lights to the arena kick in full blast and The Rogue Paladin and Meatshield Trainwrecker walk out onto the stage, not getting much of a crowd reaction. But the two try to play up the crowd as they make their way down to the ring.

Bryan: And here we go folks! We've got a great tag team match in front of us as The Guild makes their debut against X and Dimensioner. And this is going to be a match where, frankly, I don't know what will happen. We've got X and Dimensioner, opponents last week, taking on The Guild that we see right here.

Jhub: You know, I'm not sure what to think of these guys. On one hand I want to beat them up and take their lunch money. But on the other hand, if they're as committed to wrestling as they are to their games, we may see some good action out of The Rogue Paladin and Meatshield Trainwrecker.

Pete: Mmmm...meat shield...

The Guild have gotten down to the ring as Disturbed's 'Down With the Sickness' infamous drums kick in and the arena goes pitch black with only flashes strobing in and out, illuminating parts of the audience.

'Boo!' goes the crowd, because that's Dimensioner's music and Dimensioner is bad.

Jhub: And right now we've got a monster of a man, Dimensioner, coming down to the ring.

Pete: That's right. You know, even though Meatshield outweighs Dimensioner, the Demented One has several inches and isn't quite as...well, quite as fat.

Jhub: Maybe when this match is done you two can have a pie eating contest.

As Dimensioner reaches the halfway point down to the ring, his music is cut off by the sudden burst of 'The Ecstacy of Gold composed' by Ennio Morricone, and the crowd begins to become a little bit lighter in their reaction as X can be seen making his way right through the crowd.

Pete: And the Egg-O-Matic One is making his way through the crowd. And Dimensioner looks a bit annoyed at the stealing of the show.

Bryan: I think, Pete, you meant to say 'Enygmatic One.' But you are correct. He's parting the crowd, entering opposite his partner, and sandwiching The Guild.

Jhub: He's in with the people. He's a regular Don Quixote.

Pete: Mmm...meat shield sandwich...

As X and Dimensioner close on the ring, The Rogue Paladin seems to be planning something. He takes Meatshield Trainwrecker by the arm and points to X, and then he himself looks out to the imposing Dimensioner. The big man is almost to the ring, while X is still making his way through the crowd. It looks like The Guild is going to try to take advantage of the situation as both rush to the side of the ring that Dimensioner is close to.

Before the big man can act, The Rogue Paladin vaults over the top rope directly at him and lands a cross body block that lights up the arena as the fans give a nice sized pop. But Dimensioner does not go down easily. He is looming over The Rogue Paladin as the much smaller man is still getting up from off the ground. By the time Paladin is ready to defend himself, it's too late as he is met with a huge right hand that sends him reeling.

Dimensioner is rearing back with a second, but he is caught completely off guard by Meatshield who shoulder checks Dimensioner from behind and knocks him clean into the guardrail and the audience cringes.

Dimensioner is up in just a second and rushes over to Meatshield and throws a big right hand. Meatshield gets the block up, though, and responds with a shove to the chest. What Dimensioner didn't know was that The Rogue Paladin was knelt behind his knees and now Dimensioner is completely off balance and falls backwards down to the ground!

Pete: Check it out, JB! Revenge of the nerds!

Bryan: Oh, but look out. It seems that the X factor is coming into play.

Jhub: Guys. These puns have to stop. Now.

But, as per Bryan's words, X has entered into the equation. He's running up along ringside as Meatshield is facing away from him and The Rogue Paladin can't see through Meatshield to see him coming.

Before either can react, X cuts the corner and jumps up on the guardrail. He vaults up over Meatshield, grabs meatshield's head in a bulldog, and brings the big man down right on top of The Rogue Paladin!

Bryan: The Guild is down, X makes a remarkable entrance, Dimensioner is getting up, and the crowd is going nuts!

Jhub: And the damn match hasn't even started yet!

Referee Timothy Vale is calling to the wrestlers to get to the ring or he'll DQ them all!

By this time, the brawling has broken out between Dimensioner and Meatshield, and X and The Rogue Paladin. X and Paladin are closest to the ring, and they hear Vale's words. They look to the ring, then to each other, and back to the ring. Both immediately run as fast as they can and slide into the ring. The bell rings three times, and the two come up punching like they haven't missed a beat.

Bryan: And finally, Timothy Vale has restored some sort of order here.

X has gotten an upper hand on Paladin with a powerful headlock. He grinds down on Paladin's skull. The Rogue Paladin, looking for any way out of the hold, backs to the ropes and throws X to the opposite ropes as hard as he can. X is sent running, and comes back with a full head of steam and lands a running dropkick to The Rogue Paladin.

Outside, Dimensioner has gotten the upper hand on Meatshield Trainwrecker, and leaves the slightly smaller man leaning against the guardrail as he heads to the ring.

Meatshield looks a little winded, but is resiliant and pulls himself up as he makes his way to the ring.

Back inside, X lands a remarkable uppercut to The Rogue Paladin that sends the smaller man to the ground. X then runs to the turnbuckle, climbs it, and jumps out to do a senton bomb. With a shudder on the mat, he lands it and covers! Vale goes for the count:

1!

2! And the shoulder gets up!

Jhub: X looking for a quick pin here. And that's not a bad idea, considering the fact that his team isn't as well oiled as The Guild.

X leans The Rogue Paladin into the turnbuckle, and throws him across the ring to the opposite turnbuckle.

X then faces the crowd and works them up as he looks like he's getting ready to hit a big move.

But, Dimensioner has gotten ahold of Paladin. He begins choking Paladin, who is up against the turnbuckle, from behind. Vale, seeing this, gets on the other side of Paladin and yells for Dimensioner to let go.

X turns around, his back to the turnbuckle, and gets ready to charge to the opposite corner. But he sees what's going on and walks over as well, yelling at Dimensioner.

Bryan: He doesn't look happy.

Jhub: Well yeah. He was setting up The Rogue Paladin for some big move and Dimensioner got in the way. So much for teamwork.

Pete: Oh please. Dimensioner was playing on a team. He was doing his part and X should recognise that.

Annoyed, X slaps Dimensioner for the tag and gets out on the apron as the big man gets in with The Rogue Paladin.

Still not having let go of Paladin's neck, Dimensioner clasps both hands on the neck and lifts the much smaller man up in the air.

Legs kicking, The Rogue Paladin tires to pry Dimensioner off of him, but it's too late! Dimensioner brings him down to the ring in a double chokeslam that rocks the entire ring and the crowd goes crazy with boos.

Bryan: Did you see that impact! He must have broken The Rogue Paladin in half!

X is calling for Dimensioner to go for the cover.

But Dimensioner picks The Rogue Paladin up and throws him to the opposite corner, where he runs into Meatshield and knocks him off the apron!

Dimensioner follows up with a running lariat right into The Rogue Paladin, who is slammed into the corner and slumps down as the big man looks bloodthirsty.

X is trying to rile the crowd into support for his partner, but Dimensioner ignores them completely as he picks up The Rogue Paladin with both hands.

He lifts him over his head in a gorilla press and drops The Rogue Paladin right down on his front side.

He then drops for the pin, but doesn't see Vale shaking his head.

Pete: What's this, now?

Bryan: Referee Timothy Vale is saying that The Rogue Paladin isn't the legal man!

Pete: What?

Jhub: He's right. Paladin tagged Trainwrecker when he ran into him and knocked him off the apron. Which means that Dimensioner should watch himself!

Right on cue with The Jhub, the crowd begins going nuts.

Dimensioner, covering The Rogue Paladin, doesn't see the huge bulk of Meatshield Trainwrecker poised up on the turnbuckle.

Bryan: You've got to be kidding me.

Jhub: I think I see the support beam bending on that thing!

Finally, Dimensioner looks up the other way at Timothy Vale, wondering where the count is.

The Rogue Paladin, seeing death from above, rolls out of the way as Meatshield Trainwrecker half jumps/half falls off the turnbuckle right at Dimensioner.

Dimensioner sees the shadow and turns his head just a little too late as Trainwrecker comes crashing down on top of him and Dimensioner as the crowd goes nuts!

Bryan: Did you see that impact!

Pete: I guess Meatshield rolled, what, a sixteen to his sneak attack check?

Jhub: What did I say about the puns?

Pete: And that means that Dimensioner crit failed on the spot check-

Jhub: Pete! That's got to stop now!

Back in the ring, Dimensioner has rolled out of the ring and Timothy Vale has started the count when X rushes in as the crowd goes crazy.

Meatshield is looking out at Dimensioner when he gets a dropkick to the back of the head that bounces him off the rope. He turns around and gets a superkick right to the jaw for his trouble.

X is the only one standing in the ring as he looks at his downed opponents and signals to the crowd and they lose it yet again.

Bryan: I think X just got a devilish idea.

X grabs The Rogue Paladin and picks him up off the mat. He puts Paladin's head under his arm and stands so that Paladin is between him and Meatshield.

In one fluid motion, X vaults into the air, does a flip, and simultaneously brings his leg down on Meatshield's throat and drills Paladin's head into the mat!

Jhub: That was the Power 9 that he executed with a modified leg drop, just for one Meatshield Trainwrecker! An incredible move by X!

Bryan: That it was. But he's going for the pin, and neither he nor The Rogue Paladin are the legal man.

Timothy Vale is tapping X on the shoulder, trying to tell him this fact, as Dimensioner is making his way back into the ring.

Dimensioner sees Meatshield Trainwrecker laying on the mat and picks him up. Going over to the ropes, he doesn't think twice about Irish Whipping Meatshield to the opposite ropes...right at X and Vale.

Vale sees Meatshield just in time and dives out of the way. But by the time X sees the big man barreling down on him, it's too late. He braces for the impact, but Meatshield is just too big as he sends X right through the ropes and to the outside!

Jhub: What did I say about poor teamwork?

Meatshield is on his way back and Dimensioner sticks out an arm to clothesline him. Meatshield hits the ground and Dimensioner goes for the pin.

1!

2!

3-NO! The Rogue Paladin breaks it with what seems to be his last ounce of strength and Dimensioner looks less than happy.

Bryan: And I would not want to be The Rogue Paladin right now.

Pete: Looks like he failed his 'crap your pants' check.

Jhub: For the love of...Pete...

Dimensioner bears down on The Rogue Paladin and looks ready to make the kill. He grabs the smaller man by the neck and brings one big fist back.

But behind him, Meatshield has pulled himself up. Signaling to the crowd, as Timothy Vale is trying to pry Dimensioner off of The Rogue Paladin, Meatshield pulls a bag out of his pants and puts it in his hand as he hits Dimensioner in the head! Dice go flying everywhere and Dimensioner looks stunned.

Bryan: Saving Throw! Saving Throw!

Jhub: And Dimensioner is in trouble, as his opponent is still outside the ring because of him!

Meatshield reaches and lifts Dimensioner up, setting up for the team finisher.

Pete: And it looks like he's got some muscles under that fat as he's got Dimensioner up. But can he finish?

The Rogue Paladin seems to pry himself off the mat and jumps up to complete the move!

The crowd roars as they hit the d20 and Dimensioner hits the mat hard!

Timothy Vale goes in for the count and it's 1! 2! 3!!!

The bell rings and The Guild celebrate their first victory here at GCW!

Bryan: An incredible match and with teamwork like that, these two will go far in GCW history!

Earth.

You mean…us.

Right. Of course. I mean us. Exactly. The awesomeness that is us.

Right.

Bryan: Wait a minute! We’re being joined here by the former three time World Champion Octavian! Perhaps we’ll get to hear a little of what’s on his mind!
