Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #18 – Washington DC

Introduction -by Steven Caldera

The lights dim in the arena, and the Washington crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “DANGEROUS” and “DREAD” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Washington DC” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We’re only a few days away from Dangerous Games, and we’re here in the nation’s capital for our final episode of WorldWide before the big event! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan alongside The Jhub and Pete, and we’re coming to you from Washington DC as we prepare for one of the biggest and most exciting events of the year!

Jhub: And not a moment too soon, either. Honestly, are we really supposed to just sit back and wait for President Caldera to get off his ass and air this show just because he wanted to get some puss-

Pete: Whoa! Kayfabe alert!

Bryan: Perhaps we should start this show over…

Introduction… Again -by Steven Caldera

Bryan: We’re only a few days away from Dangerous Games, and we’re in the nation’s capital for-

Jhub: Another exciting episode of WorldWide! Thanks, JB! I, for one, am damn excited for this night, because I get my last look at the competition before Dangerous Games! Last week I got a good look at that loser Twizzy, and tonight I’m hoping to check out the rest of the field, including these supposed AWA schmucks that are coming in.

Pete: President Caldera certainly made a curious deal to bring in his archrival Low and his associates, but I’m sure it should be interesting, and we even get to see President Caldera enter the match himself! What would happen if Caldera won it?!

Bryan: I don’t think victory is his plan, Pete. We’ve also got Trey Tremendous and Michael Stevens heading to a collision for the United States title, we’ve got Jonathan Borealis taking on not one but TWO other men for the television title in a handicap match, the historic Iron Man match between Rich Rollins and Mike Hardy, and of course, the big Heavyweight Championship showdown between Jason Dread and his former best friend, LeStatt Knight.

Jhub: Big fan of Knight’s, aren’t you, Pete?

Pete: I must have had a stroke or something last week. LeStatt Knight is going to get what he’s got coming to him this week at Dangerous Games, I’ve got no doubt.

Bryan: Well, fans, tonight we have a huge show, including a gauntlet match to decide the number thirty entrant into the battle royal!

“Established” cues up, and the crowd explodes! The perfectly tailored form of President Steven Caldera appears on stage, wearing a tremendous smile as he struts to the ring.

Andrews: Please welcome the President and Owner of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera!

Bryan: And we’re starting this show off with a bang, as President Caldera is on his way to fill us in on Dangerous Games!

Jhub: I have to admit, I’m a little excited to see what he has to say about the battle royal, JB. We really don’t know much about it, other than the AWA will be there, Toxic will be entering at number one, and the winner of the gauntlet match tonight will enter at number thirty.

Pete: Are you in that gauntlet, Jhub?

Jhub: You’ll just have to wait and find out.

Caldera enters the ring and accepts the mic from Andrews, turning to the applauding GCW fans to address the arena.

Caldera: Thank you, Washington DC!

The crowd explodes in cheers, responding to the blatantly cheap comment.

Caldera: You know, we’re just a few days away from Dangerous Games, and-

Before Caldera can even get started, “Do The Evolution” hits overhead, and the arena is plunged into boos. The massive form of Toxic appears on stage, wearing a pair of black jeans but no shirt, his chiseled physique heaving under the lights. Caldera simply laughs in disbelief as Toxic snaps off his sunglasses, staring down the boss.

Jhub: That didn’t take long…

Toxic: I’m sorry to interrupt you, Mr. President, but there’s something that’s been bothering me for the last week that I simply had to get off my chest here tonight. I’ve been thinking all the way back to ACWF, where I took you apart limb from limb. I physically dominated you, just like I dominated Twizzy at Warpath. And yet, beating you doesn’t feel like a victory. Hell, most everyone I’ve run into has told me they thought you won that battle.

The crowd applauds Caldera, who simply offers Toxic a knowing grin.

Toxic: Now, last week I made a little business deal to take the night off, and in return you stuck me at the number one spot in the Dangerous Games. Well, that’s just fine with me. I have no problem disposing of twenty nine other guys on my way to winning, because when I do, there will be no doubt that I deserve to be GCW World Heavyweight Champion!

The crowd boos, as Toxic’s voice begins to grow in intensity.

Toxic: But one thing I cannot stand the thought of is being in the same match as you, with the chance to prove myself to the world, only to have your sorry ass tossed from the ring before I get my hands on you. I knew I needed a way to assure myself at least a few moments in the ring, just the two of us. Which led me to GCW headquarters in Columbus last week. And it led me to the office of a good friend of yours… and the man who this little show is named after!

Toxic turns to the entrance, and the crowd rises in anticipation. Another muscular, suited figure appears on stage, and the fans resume their merciless boos aimed at the stage.

Bryan: MR. DANGER!

Jhub: I knew he’d show up before long!

Danger slips the sunglasses off his face and smiles at Caldera, then nods in Toxic’s direction.

Toxic: After I left last week, I didn’t feel like celebrating any more. So I took that cash that I got from Mr. Stephens and bought myself a favor from Mr. D here, who just so happens to remain on the GCW board of directors.

Danger: And that favor, Steven, much as I hate to do this to an old friend… was to name YOU as the number TWO entrant into MY match!

The crowd boos mightily, and Caldera is taken aback. He simply chuckles to himself, his eyes falling to his thousand dollar shoes in contemplation.

Bryan: WHAT?! That’s not fair! Caldera just entered the match to get at Low!

Jhub: But he forgot about Toxic! And now he’s stuck in the ring with the big man again.

Pete: He’ll be lucky to make it long enough to even touch Low!

Caldera: Wow, Corey, it’s great to see you again. You know, I was wondering when you would show up. I had asked you to come by to promote the show you made famous in the first place, and I was starting to wonder when you’d accept my invitation. So I’m number two, am I? That’s fine. This match isn’t about winning for me, it’s about tying up loose ends. You want me one on one again, Toxic? Looks like you’ve got me. But I warn you: I’m in this match to wrestle Low, and there will be no chance in hell I’ll be leaving that match before I get my hands on him. I’m not going to take it easy on you this time, my friend. You just inserted yourself in between one of the greatest rivalries in professional wrestling. You have no idea what you just got yourself into.

The crowd cheers wildly for Caldera, who seems quite confident as he stares blankly down at Danger and Toxic. Toxic seems angered by this, but Danger can’t fight back a laugh.

Danger: Well, Steve, that’s all I have to say about the situation. Mr. Toxic, best of luck to you. You’re going to need it.

“Established” cues up again, and Danger simply pats Toxic on the back and leaves. Toxic, confident but fuming, glares at Caldera, who simply shrugs and turns back to pose for the crowd.

Bryan: My God, President Caldera is entering at number two? He and Toxic are going to go head to head one more time at Dangerous Games, and Caldera had better hope he lives long enough to get his hands on Low.

Jhub: Toxic is motivated, guys, he wants to win that match and prove himself to the world. But you just have to wonder if this move is biting off a little more than he can chew. Nobody has even won the Dangerous Games entering even remotely that early, and I’m not so sure Toxic can do it.

Pete: Hey, Toxic’s a talented guy, but I just don’t see this working out well for either one of these guys.

Another Unexpected Guest -by Phyl

Again Allied Chaos was given the night off. He was considering just cutting the two of them loose, they weren’t much good to him, but it was always fun to have a couple extra lackeys around, he’d decide later. He stood kilted, with a plain red tee-shirt. He stood waiting, she should be here soon enough, Sheena always had a knack for not being exactly on time. As she quietly opened the door and slipped inside, he found himself admiring her in a way he hadn’t for years. She really was a gorgeous woman and her new attitude was something he could really sink his teeth into, who knew she had a brain?

Sheena Ramone: What’s with the kilt?

That brought a laugh as he looked her up and down, the color of the bridesmaid dress was hideous.

Michael Stevens: You’re commenting on the way I’m dressed?

Sheena Ramone: Hey, lay off, I bought it for two dollars at a thrift store.

Michael Stevens: Fair enough I suppose. Anyway I’m getting in touch with my Irish roots, in fact I think it’s time I started getting back to basics in just about everything.

Sheena Ramone: Well, that’s good I guess.

Michael Stevens: Damn skippy babe.

Sheena Ramone: And what exactly gives you the right to be calling me babe?

Michael Stevens: Why not? I said I was going to be getting back to the basics, and basically you’re a babe.

Sheena Ramone: You know I actually think you meant that to be a compliment so I’ll try and take it that way. Anyway you seem in good spirits tonight, which is great, because I’ve got something to ask you.

You just know that couldn’t be good. Oh well, nothing was going to spoil the mood, beating Jason last week had given him the purpose and drive he’d been lacking for a while now, and he was feeling damn great for the first time in a long time.

Michael Stevens: What?

Sheena Ramone: Well, you know how I said you owe me, and I’m not the only one you owe?

Michael Stevens: Yeah? And I still say it’s a load of horse nuggets but whatever you say.

Sheena Ramone: Good, because there’s someone here to see you, I think it’s time you gave a little back to him, just like you’re giving back to me.

Michael Stevens: I swear to god Sheena if you brought that damn midget Bruce I’mma fire you again, I never liked the little bastard, only reason I kept him around so long is I won him in that poker game with Shane Ransom a few years ago.

Sheena Ramone: It’s not Bruce, but…

Michael Stevens: Good, let me guess then, he’s waiting outside the door like any good little puppy, waiting to be allowed inside. Damnit Alex, get the hell in here.

The door opened almost immediately and it was him, Michael Stevens cousin Alex Morgan. A long time ago he had a chance at being a top star in the business, drugs and injuries cut that dream off really quickly, but he was still huge, and had a passion for the business. He’d been Michael’s official bodyguard for a quite a while, and he probably wanted his job back. It was really hard not to like the guy, loyal to a fault, and not that bright he was always eager to please, and not a bad bodyguard, at least when he was sober. He was looking pretty good, in better shape than Stevens had seen him in years, maybe he’d actually gotten himself clean, that’d be a miracle.

Alex Morgan: Hi, Mike.

He even sounded like a big dumb dog with that low rumbling voice. Despite the irritation he felt at Sheena’s gall for bringing him here, he realized he’d actually sorta missed the lug, and seeing him was kinda fun.

Michael Stevens: Hello Alexander. It’s good to see you again. You look to be doing fairly well.

Alex Morgan: Oh I am Mike. I’ve been clean and sober almost a year now. I know you fired me cause I was a screw up, and I’m sorry man. But if you could just give me another chance, I really want this Mike. You know how much I love wrestling, and I just want to be a part of it again, I swear give me the shot and I won’t let you down, please Mike.

The poor sap actually apologized for Stevens being an asshole towards him. Feeling more than a little sorry for the guy, Stevens shook his head almost sadly.

Michael Stevens: Look big guy, I’ll give you the shot, just try to look tough ok? And don’t like trip over anything, that just makes you look dumb. Oh and don’t talk, cause that just proves it.

Sheena Ramone: MICHAEL!

Michael Stevens: Oh yeah, what I mean is, sure you can have your job back. Just don’t screw up ok? Now I’ve got myself a big match with Jason tonight one on one, so I need to get prepared. But look, there is something you two can help me with. See Alex as I was telling Sheena I’m getting back to basics, and to start everything off I’m heading out to the ring in a few minutes to lay out my plans for the next few weeks, I want everyone to know that I’m back. To do that I’m going to need a real show of force, now I don’t want you guys at ringside tonight for the match, but when I go out for my little speech, I want you to with me ok? Just stand aside and let me do my thing, follow my lead and everything is gonna work out just fine.

Sound good?

Alex Morgan: Sure Mike, anything you say.

Sheena Ramone: I guess.

Michael Stevens: Great, now everyone shut up and let me organize my thoughts.

Dangerous Games -by Jason and Rob

Kevin Jacobs stands in the middle of the ring, smiling that same cheesy smile he always does. The fans cheer at the sight of him, which only brings that smile to a new level. After they settle down a bit Kevin lifts the microphone that he has placed in his hand to his lips.

Kevin: Hello Washington D.C.! ( cheap pop ) I am glad to be here tonight as GCW presents another spectacular edition of WorldWide. We are but days away from Dangerous Games which promises to be nothing short of perfection. Tonight I have the honor of interviewing perhaps the most dominate man that the GCW has EVER seen, former United States champion … LeStatt Knight!

Boos rain down from the crowd as “O’Fortuna” plays throughout the arena. When the next challenger for the World Title steps from behind the curtains, the boos grow even more forceful. As always Knight basks in the fan’s hatred towards them, extending his arms and smelling the air as if it were a early spring day. He chuckles as he walks down the ramp, with new insults every step down. Wasting little time he slides into the ring and walks over to Jacobs, so still has that same smile plastered on his face. The fans don’t die down right away, but anxious to probably get Knight off camera, they hush to hear what would be said.

Kevin: Welcome LeStatt, tonight there are just a few questions that the world would like to know, first of all with Dangerous Games only days away how are you feeling?

LeStatt: First of all Jacobs wipe that ridiculous grin off your face … it’s making me sick to my stomach. ( Kevin’s smile fades ) That’s better. NOW as for Dangerous Games, I have never felt better. This is the opportunity that I have been waiting since early 2001 for. Waiting to see MY name in the Main Event spot battling for the world title … a world title that I have always known would look great around MY waist. There is no doubt in my mind that after Dangerous Games is over, we WILL have a new heavyweight champion.

Kevin: You speak of the length of time you have been apart of GCW. If my memory serves me right you came in only two months or so after GCW officially opened, even being the third TV champion. What is it that you believe kept you from reaching this point until now?

LeStatt: ( with a slight rise of his eyebrow ) Do you really have to ask that question Kevin? Is it not obvious that Steven Caldera purposely held me back for that whole time? What his reasoning was I can never understand but it was him that kept putting me in dead end feuds, matches, and situations. Throwing Luke Hanson off a scaffold was just about the highlight of my career … that or beating JNightmare one on one. How sad is that really? I was never given my fair shake … and now it’s time for me to collect my dues, with interest.

Kevin: well since the GCW opened back in the beginning of this year you have certainly made a name for yourself. You have lost but two matches, while beating the likes of Trey Tremendous, Luke Hanson, Michael Stevens, Khan, Twisted, Mike Hardy, and Natural Born Killer. Your United States Title reign was record breaking. Now looking into the future the World Title and more specifically, Jason Dread is in your sights.

LeStatt: I don’t dwell on my past accomplishments Kevin, I can’t afford to. Sure I have accomplished a lot but it’s not over, not by a long shot. The World Title is the ultimate prize but it is not the end of the road. I will do something nobody has ever done … I am going to give the GCW something they have never seen before. ACWF was fun, watching as two morons battled each other, and argued over a title that was never theres. War Path was amusing to watch the look in Alex’s eyes every time he landed a blow yet I would just stare right back at him with fire in my eyes … then when I chose to I destroyed him. NOW comes Dangerous Games and Jason Dread. Thus far things have been relatively quiet between us. Sure I bashed his head with a steel chair and broke our so called “friendship” but hey (censor) happens. I believe tonight is the night that we make things interesting for our match up at Dangerous Games.

Kevin: What kind of interesting?

LeStatt: Well it occurred to me that we needed to add some flare to this match up. Sure a one on one encounter can be epic but there is something more we can do to make it an even greater battle. I don’t believe pinning someone ALWAYS makes you the better man … one lucky move can win a match for you. It’s pounding your opponent to the brink of their very mind crumbling inside of their head. You break, tear, and claw their very soul until they just simply can NOT go on anymore.

Kevin: Hard Core match?

LeStatt: Hardly Kevin.

Kevin: Last man standing ?

LeStatt: That’s been done.

Kevin: Then it’s … ( Knight cuts him off )

LeStatt: AN I QUIT MATCH!

The crowd explodes into a mixed reaction, some hating Knight’s arrogance, others excited at the possibility.

LeStatt: That is the one match that can break a man down … because if you tap out to a man that you hate that will eat at you until the day that you die. ( Knight’s head lowers a bit ) TRUST me, I know. I don’t need Jason’s consent for this either, because the match has already been signed sealed and DELIVERED! Dangerous Games will mark the end of that coward Jason Dread, I will make damn sure of …

As usual we are treated to a interruption in the middle of this interview, like countless others. Instead of being recent and familiar music we are treated to, however, what comes over the loudspeakers is that of something from GCW's past. A past better forgotten. Everyone in attendance hears the cueing of "The Safety Dance" and that can mean only one thing.

Disco...f'ing...duck.

GCW's own former hardcore champion, and the only mallard to ever grace the ring steps a webbed foot out onto the stage ramp and the crowd goes ballistic with cheers! Raising the roof all the way down to the ring, Disco is met with the reception only a returned loved champion would receive, completely unfitting for anyone who used to ally himself with a 15 year old kid. Needless to say, Knight is in the ring, rather unimpressed by the intrusion. With typical fervor, Disco tries to do a standing leap onto the ring apron, sadly missing by about a half foot and crashes down on the floor of the arena. Another cheer...who doesn't love Disco Duck?

Aside from Lestatt, who furiously yells in the duck's direction as he finally clambers into the ring. With one last pose reminiscent of The Incredible Hulk, Disco stands alongside Kevin Jacobs, in opposition of Lestatt.

Kevin: We've been joined by Disco Duck, whom GCW has not seen the likes of for several years now! Why have you chosen to make a return tonight in the middle of this huge announcement about Dangerous Games and its main event?

Disco Duck: Quack quack quack! Quaack qua-quack!

Nothing's changed about Disco in the least. Kevin Jacobs translates.

Kevin: Disco says that he's come because he's tired of seeing you talk about the shot he should be getting at the World Title.

Lestatt laughs in the face of our fine feathered friend.

LeStatt: You've got to be kidding me! I've not come all this way to be the ass of one of your jokes. Stick to where you're used best, Disco, doing all those personal appearances that the big stars like me don't care to take and being a general nuisance to all of the under card "talent". I have no use for you.

Disco: Quaaaack?! Qua-qua-quack, quack quaaack quaaaaccccckkkkkk!

Kevin nods in understanding.

Kevin: He says that he was a champion while you were still getting your ass kicked by Capo, and deserves a lot more than you will ever get. This sounds to me like a blatant challenge.

Disco nods in agreement, and extends a wing towards Lestatt in as of a menacing manner as a man in a giant duck suit can convey. Once again, Lestatt has a hearty laugh at the challenge from this blast of GCW's past.

LeStatt: Alright Duck, you want a fight with me to prove once again why you've been the ass of GCW since you've been here? You've got it. My request to have a match before Dangerous games may have been declined, but I'll choose to get a little exercise on you instead of waiting until Dangerous Games. You made a really stupid mistake, Duck.

Disco: Quack quaaaack, quack qua-qua-qua-quack quack?!

Kevin stands silent, as the look upon LeStatt's face becomes impatient.

LeStatt: What did he say Jacobs?

Kevin takes another second to process everything.

Kevin: He asked; What'cha gonna do...when this mallard runs all over you, brother?!

The crowd pops huge as Disco does plenty of muscle man poses in the direction of Lestatt, who wryly smiles at Disco. He does a throat slitting motion towards the Duck, whose face conveys nothing but mild confusion. Lestatt leaves the ring and walks to the back, leaving out last parting scene that of Disco posing in the ring to the wild delight of the fans that are sadly happy to see him back and in action.

Tag Team Title Match – One Fall

EVL vs. Bosom Buddies -by Steven Caldera

Summer’s Overture cues up, and the crowd rises. Shawn Johnson and Lopsang emerge on stage, and the crowd actually greets them with a rather large pop. Lopsang remains stoic in the face of the lights and the crowd, but Johnson can’t help but greet the fans’ enthusiasm.

Andrews: The following tag team contest is set for one fall, and is for the GCW Tag Team Championships!

Bryan: Well, here come Shawn Johnson and Lopsang, unfortunately titled the “Bosom Buddies,” and they’re set for a Tag Team Title shot against EVL here at WorldWide!

Jhub: Bosom Buddies? Well, I won’t bother, a butt-sex joke would be too easy there.

The duo climb into the ring, but the lights suddenly go out in the arena. Their music abruptly cuts out, and the crowd is plunged into silence.

Bryan: What the-

Pete: We’ve lost power! Are we still on?

Jhub: Lost power? You haven’t been doing this long, have you, Pete? We don’t lose power in the middle of a live show. If the lights are off, someone turned them off!

Pete: Ok, who then, smart guy?

The Sickness by Disturbed suddenly begins rumbling into the pitch black arena, and the crowd begins to boo. The MegaTron slowly flickers to life, a pale and fuzzy image appearing on screen. The massive form of Dimensioner, standing in profile, fades in, as Lopsang and Johnson stare on in confusion.

Dimensioner: There will be no tag team title match here tonight. Miss Eva is too busy, and the two of you haven’t proven a thing. However, there is a tag team that IS deserving of this title shot… The SICK FREAKS!

The long-time GCW crowd gasps as another figure slowly fades in on screen. The faint silhouette of X-Calibur appears behind Dimensioner, smiling a wide, sinister smile.

Dimensioner: If the two of you want your tag team title shots, come to Dangerous Games. But be warned, The Sick Freaks will be there too!

With that, the MegaTron flickers to black, plunging the entire arena into darkness. A lone spotlight shines down on the ring, singling out the Bosom Buddies, where Shawn Johnson stands a bit concerned. The pair consult each other in the lonesome darkness, as the crowd chants for Lopsang and Johnson.

Bryan: The Sick Freaks? Dimensioner wants a tag title shot?

Jhub: I was really hoping we’d seen the last of those two… Don’t they ever give up?

Mind Games -by Andrew

Wielding a baseball bat he stole from a merchandise kiosk only moments before, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins charges down a service hallway and through a security checkpoint, ignoring the burley yellow-shirted guard at the gate. Heading deep into the backstage area, he makes a sharp left turn so quickly that he startles a group of sound techs casually enjoying a cigarette.

Tech: Hey Rollins, what’s the… hey!

Without breaking stride The Renegade snatches a lit cigarette from the techie, flicking it at the techie standing next to him. The men scramble, obviously not interested in becoming the focal point of Rollins’ obviously foul mood.

Crossing the narrow hallway in great leaping strides, Rollins finally reaches the door to his personal locker room, the sign reading “The Renegade” now tilted at an awkward angle. Pushing the already ajar door fully open with the tip of his bat, Rollins lets out a low whistle as he addresses the darkened room beyond.

Renegade: I gotta say kid, I’d be impressed if I weren’t so goddamned agitated right now. What do you think this is-…

Slowly pulling himself into the room, The Renegade stops dead in his tracks. Hanging on the far wall, the white poster board still stained with the dried blood of Mike Hardy, is a familiar sign:

“Mike Hardy… Future World Champ!”

Cautiously stepping forward, The Renegade wields his weapon against the shadows as he comes closer to the very sign he pounded into Hardy’s back two weeks ago. With his free hand he touches the poster as if he were unsure it was really there, but as his fingertips touch the cardboard surface his face tightens. Taking it by the bottom edge, Rollins jerks the sign off the wall and tosses it on the floor.

As it falls to the ground a new message is revealed, scrawled on the opposite side:

“See you at Dangerous Games… Iron Man.”

A slow grin overtakes The Renegade’s expression as we fade back to ringside.

Laying Out The Plan -by Phyl

“I Want To Conquer The World” by Bad Religion blasts through the arena as Michael Stevens heads down to ringside, somewhat strangely dressed in a kilt and a tee-shirt. Following closely behind him is Sheena Ramone, pink hair flowing past her shoulders, lime green bridesmaid dress. Towering over both and bringing up the rear, a huge man long black hair tied in a pony tail, skin tight black shirt poured over his muscular chest, and khaki cargo pants. The trio is met with a huge chorus of boos heading down towards the ring, Stevens and the huge guy pretty much ignore the crowd, whilst Sheena cackles and points at members of the crowd.

The trio reaches ringside and Stevens immediately slides in, Sheena waits patiently for Stevens to hold the ropes open for her, but he merely walks to the center of the ring where a microphone is already waiting. The big man walks up the stairs to the apron, and steps over the top rope, before holding open the ropes for Sheena who then follows his lead up the stairs and through the ropes. Watching the two with amusement, Stevens chuckles into the microphone, and gets shot an evil glare from Ramone, he shakes his head. All the time with the crowd jeering.

Michael Stevens: Boo, yell, I don’t give a flyin’ crap. You can hate me all you want, it’s not going to change that one simple, and inescapable fact, I just don’t care. But I’ll tell you all out there what you’re going to do, you’re going to shut the hell up for five minutes so I can say my piece, and after I’m done you can go back to grooming each other, and eating the bugs out of one another’s hair.

The boos grow even louder.

Michael Stevens: Hehe, guess I hit a little close to home. Oh well. Seven years ago, I started out in this business, a young kid without a care in the world. I didn’t care who loved me, who hated me, or who just plain didn’t think about me at all. I just put my head down and tried to put out the best matches I possibly could, if I could screw with some people along the way, and show them just how stupid they were, more’s the better.

But about a year in, something happened, I started to chance. I like so many other’s in this business I started to buy into all my own hype. Even worse I started living for it, the praise of the wrestling rags, the glowing words from whatever promoter I was working for on any given week. I won’t lie, I loved it, it was kinda cool knowing that at least somewhere out there I was appreciated. I started just being completely stupid, doing whatever it would take to get noticed, I dreamed of working in a company like GCW.

All this in less than two years of wrestling, I couldn’t even buy a beer yet, and I thought I was the king of the world headed for the big time. Through the next three years I was a part of some great companies, I won myself a few titles, and it was all good. Then I met the guy that promised to change my life for the better, one Steven Caldera.

(The fans cheer at the mention of the bosses name.)

Michael Stevens: Yeah that’s right, kiss his ass, I still think there’s room for another few lip prints. Taking me under his wing, he put me with his top guys, The Establishment. I thought I’d finally made it. The only problem was, for all Steven promised me, he always without question failed to deliver. I was supposed to be his new protégé, the man who’d be the future of his company. And yet still assholes like Octavian and Jason Dread soaked up all this attention.

The fan’s boos turn into cheers as United States champ Trey Tremendous walks out from the curtains and looks on at what has happened.
