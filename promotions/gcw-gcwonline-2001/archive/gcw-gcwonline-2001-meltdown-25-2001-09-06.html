GCW — Card

Meltdown 25, 30 August 2001

30 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 25 Arena at Harbor Yard — Bridgeport, Connecticut ---- Meltdown #25 (The GCW logo flashes on the screen, and we quickly cut to scenes from last weeks Meltdown. First we see Reed’s ceremony, where Octavian stands in front of him.) Octavian- The CWU is gone now. And before you go off handing out title shots, maybe you should remember the man, President Caldera, who pays your checks every month. And maybe you should remember the man who gave you that title shot in the first place. . . me. Don’t forget those of us who really made this possible. Now that you’re champion, everyone in the back wants a shot at you. And a few of us in the ring want a shot. So you just be careful what you say when you say you’ll be a fighting champion. Because I may just take you up on that. (We then see the main event.) Bryan- Wait a minute! Tristain Mayhem is here! The United States Champion is here! Mayhem hops onto the turnbuckle, as Rollins rears back to hit Octavian with the chair! Yale- But Mayhem snatches it from Rollins’ grasp! Rollins turns, but Mayhem is already gone with that chair! Mayhem may have just saved Octavian! Bryan- Octavian is up, as Rollins turns to see him! Wait, Octavian scoops Rollins up, and turns him over!! What’s this?! Octavian drops him right on his head! That’s the Roman Devastator! Yale- My God what a move! Octavian has hit the Roman Devastator on Rich Rollins! Rollins is out! Octavian drags him to the center of the ring, and covers! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!!! Bryan- Octavian has done it! Octavian defeats the Renegade! But not without the interjection of Tristain Mayhem! (The Meltdown logo then appears, followed by the Meltdown theme. Suddenly, pyro explodes from the ring and stage, as the Arizona crowd erupts in cheers! The pyro continues to explode as the show begins.) Bryan- Welcome everyone to GCW Meltdown! I’m James Bryan alongside David Yale, and we are set for another big night in the new era of GCW! Yale- That’s right! Last week, Octavian barely defeated Rich Rollins, but Tristain Mayhem interjected! And now this week Mayhem and Reed are teaming up to take on the two men who were enemies last week, Rollins and Octavian! Bryan- That match should certainly be interesting. I don’t know how Rollins and Octavian are going to be able to cooperate! In addition, the cOd will be taking on the Sick Freaks in tag team action, and Toad gets another shot at the Hard*core title! (Bawitiba hits, and the crowd immediately begins to boo. Dan Rivero emerges from the back, his TV title draped over his shoulder. He taunts the crowd, throwing his arms in the air. Slowly he struts to the ring, patting his TV belt.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Brooklyn, New York, he weighs in at 279 pounds, and he is the GCW Television Champion. . . Dan ‘The Man’ Rivero! Bryan- Well, Dan Rivero, since winning the TV title, seems to have grown a rather large head on his shoulders! He’s issued a challenge to John Reed for next week, as I hear Reed may have accepted! (Rivero steps through the ropes, and snatches a microphone from Joey Andrews. The crowd continues to boo him.) Rivero- Shut the hell up! I’m the Champion of all Television, so I’m gonna make myself seen on Television! Now, I’ve issued a challenge to John Reed for next week. Well, Johnny Boy, I hope for your sake that you stay away. Because if you do show up, by this time two weeks from now, I could be wearing my belt, and yours! (The crowd boos as Rivero hands the mike back to Andrews. Last Resort plays next, and Stone emerges to little reaction. Stone heads to the ring, walking with a purpose. He steps in and the bell rings.) Bryan- Both men circle the ring and they tie up. Stone gets Rivero in a headlock and Rivero battles back with elbows to the gut! Rivero battles out of the hold and sends Stone to the ropes, sleeper hold locked in! Yale- Stone is fading slowly. Stone gets off his feet and JAWBREAKER! Now look what he did, a ten count begins. Referee- ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! Bryan- Both men slowly get to their feet and each share rights! Stone gives Rivero three right hands but Rivero ducks the forth and turns Stone around, and sends him to the ropes. And Stone with a clothesline! Stone hooks the leg, ONE, TW-- NO! Shoulder is up. Stone is furious. Stone backs the referee to the corner and Rivero is slowly up. Yale- Stone turns around DAN DROP! What a move! Dan turns Stone over and hooks the leg, ONE, TW-- KICKOUT! Stone kicked out! That little-- Bryan- Rivero climbs up to the top rope and Stone is up! Dan doesn’t notice and flies off with an Elbow Drop, Stone catches him and Sit-Down Powerbomb! ONE, TWO, THR-- SHOULDER UP! What a maneuver by eWa member, Stone. Yale- Stone is back on his feet and stomps away on our Television Champion’s stomach! Stone picks up Rivero by his hair and locks up. Stone has him up for the Crippler! Rivero flips out and lands on his feet. Rivero pushes Stone to the ropes and THAT’S IT! Another great offensive move by Dan. Bryan- Rivero climbs up the turnbuckle slowly. He jumps off! NO! Stone rolled out of the way and Rivero hits the mat with his elbow! Stone quickly covers, ONE, TWO, THR-- NO! Rivero gets up and lifts up Stone’s leg and MONKEY FLIP! Stone hit the turnbuckle headfirst. Stone; staggered, backs up and Dan has him in a full nelson. No, Stone kicks Rivero’s groin and down goes Rivero. Yale- Stone picks up Rivero, he has him up BRAINBUSTER DDT! What a move. Stone quickly covers, ONE, TWO, THREE! NO! ROPE BREAK! Stone gets up and drags Rivero by his leg and goes for a Leg Lock but Rivero kicks Stone and he goes threw the ropes to ringside! Rivero gets to his feet and climbs out to ringside. Rivero picks up eWa member Stone and sends him to the guardrail, back first. Rivero charges but Stone flips him over to ringside! Stone climbs over the guardrail and picks up Rivero. Rivero quickly burst with left and rights. Stone clotheslines him down and taunts the fans. Bryan- Rivero is up and Stone charges. POWERSLAM OVER THE GUARDRAIL! The referee checks on both men. Rivero pushes the referee away and down goes the referee! Rivero crawls towards us, no he passes us and grabs his Television Title. Stone gets up and charges, Rivero is up and WHAM! Yale- Cha-Ching. Bryan- Rivero drops the title and rolls Stone into the ring. Rivero jumps up and is perched on the turnbuckle. RIVERO ELBOW! The referee is slowly up, ONE……TWO……THREE! Andrews- And your winner, DAN ‘THE MAN’ RIVERO! Bryan- Rivero is on a roll here! He’s defeated Stone, and is still the TV champ! What a match! (The camera heads backstage. President Caldera sits behind a table, leaning forward towards the person on the other side. On the other side sits Tristain Mayhem, his feet propped up on Caldera's table. Caldera looks angry, though Tristain looks calm and uncaring.) Caldera- So let me get this straight. . . You gave the kid the United States title belt? Mayhem- Yeah, I gave him my belt. Caldera- Wait a minute, maybe I'm not getting this. . . You gave the kid the United States title belt? Mayhem- That's what I said. I gave him my belt. Caldera- First of all, you have him MY belt. You just get to wear it because you beat the piece of crap the Jhub. And second of all. . . You gave the kid the United States title belt? Mayhem- You gonna stop repeating yourself? Or do I have to listen to this echo all night? Caldera- Tristain, we need to discuss a few things here. . . (Caldera glances at the camera.) Caldera- Would you excuse us, please? (The camera fades to commercial.) Bryan- Next up, Luke Hanson verus JNightmare! Joey Andrews is inside the ring with a microphone in hand. (“Fuel” hits. Luke Hanson walks down the ramp with his Tag Team Title on his shoulder. Luke climbs into the ring.) Andrews- The following contest, set for one fall. Introducing first, he’s a member of System-X, weighing in at 376 pounds… LUKE HANSON! (“96 Quite Bitter Beings” hits. JNightmare walks down the ramp as the fans boo. JNightmare finally gets into the ring.) Andrews- And his opponent, weighing in at 265 pounds… JNIGHTMARE! Bryan- The match is under way. JNightmare and ‘The Fueler’ share left hands. JNightmare knees Luke in the gut and a short bulldog! JNightmare quickly covers, ONE, T-- NO! He kicked out. JNightmare picks up Luke and sends him to the ropes and kick to the gut and DDT! Yale- Woo-Hoo! Go JNightmare! JNightmare picks up ‘The System-X Steamroller’ and sends the Tag Team Champion to the corner and JNightmare charges, BOOT TO THE FACE BY HANSON! JNightmare is out like a light. Hanson falls and hits the groin of JNightmare, what an odd maneuver. Bryan- JNightmare quickly rolls away as both men are out on the mat. Hanson quickly crawls and hooks the leg, ONE, TWO, THR--! He kicked out at the last second! Yale- Hanson picks up JNightmare, sends him to the ropes and Powerslam! Hanson stays with the cover, ONE, TWO, THR-- NO! Another kickout! Luke picks up JNightmare by his hair and sends him to the turnbuckle, shoulder first! Bryan- JNightmare backs up, Luke grabs him by the waist, German Suplex! He stays with it, ONE, TWO, THR-- NO! Another kickout! This man won’t stay down! Luke Hanson is up and climbs to the top turnbuckle, JNightmare is there and gets Luke’s legs, SIT-DOWN POWERBOMB! JNightmare keeps the cover, ONE, TWO, THRE--! NO! Last second! Yale- Nightmare seems to be getting a bit frustrated, as he lifts Hanson up to his feet! Nightmare bounces off the ropes, and charges Hanson! BAM! Hanson gets a surprise boot into the air, and takes Nightmare down! Bryan- Big move from the Fueler! Hanson drops to a knee, as Nightmare slowly tries to get up! Nightmare is halfway up, but Hanson grabs him and underhooks the arms! Here it comes! Yale- Wait a second! Hold on there! (The crowd begins to boo as LeStatt Knight bursts into the ring. Just as Hanson tries to lift Nightmare, Knight hits him with a chop block from behind. The crowd boos as the bell rings, and the match ends in disqualification.) Bryan- LeStatt Knight! LeStatt Knight is back from his injuries, and for some reason he’s taken out Hanson! Why would he do that?! Yale- I have no idea! But this match has ended! Hanson will win by DQ! But Knight is stomping away at the fallen Fueler! (Knight stomps away at Hanson, as Nightmare slides to the floor and walks to the back, a bit angry. More Pain hits, and the crowd boos as Knight leaves Hanson lying.) Bryan- Whatever Knight has against Hanson, he’s going to have hell to pay for this unprovoked assault! (Again we head to Caldera's makeshift office. Mayhem is still sitting with his feet propped on the table, as he chews on a toothpick. Caldera is talking, but Mayhem seems to be ignoring him.) Caldera- You're unclean. You're immature. You're irresponsible. You're unsavory. You're juvenile. You're disgusting. You smell. You're uneducated. You're ill-mannered. You're ill tempered. You get into trouble daily. You're a poor role model. You're a slob. You cost me more money than you make me. And you have nothing to say for yourself. (Mayhem simply sits in his chair, staring off into the distance.) Caldera- Well, what do you have to say? (Mayhem slowly turns his head to Caldera, and seems to be shocked to see him.) Mayhem- Oh, hey Prez. Did you say something? (Caldera's head hits the desk with quite some impact. He mutters to himself, sitting in disbelief. He finally raises his head just long enough to speak.) Caldera- We made you a new belt. You can pick it up outside. Get out of here. (Mayhem smiles and gets to his feet. He swings open the door and leaves without a word. Caldera rests his head on the desk for a moment, before the camera returns to the ring area. We Will Rock You hits, and the crowd begins to boo. The Sick Freaks emerge and head to the ring.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 495 pounds. . . Dimensioner, X-Caliber, the Sick Freaks! Bryan- Well, the Sick Freaks have been on a bit of a losing streak as of late, but they’d be right back on top if they can defeat the cOd here tonight! (As the Freaks enter the ring, Natural One hits. The crowd reacts with a very intense mixed reaction as Heckler and Dero emerge. They strut to the ring and begin to climb in.) Andrews- And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 632 pounds. . . Big Daddy Dero, the Heckler, the cOd! Bryan- Well, Dero and Heckler are renewing their past in the cOd! But don’t forget, Dero takes on President Caldera next week on Meltdown, and if he wins he gets a shot at Reed! (The bell rings, and Dero starts things off with Dimensioner.) Yale- There's the bell and it looks like we're going to see Dimensioner start things out with Heckler. Bryan- They lock up, and Heckler pushes Dimensioner back into the corner. Heckler goes for a punch, but Dimensioner comes out of the corner and reverses! He whips Heckler into the turnbuckle and look at him go! Yale- Dimensioner is punching away at the midsection of Heckler, and Heckler is absorbing all of it! Bryan- Dimensioner throws Heckler into the opposite corner, and follows it up with a clothesline! Heckler goes down, and crawls over and makes the tag. Here comes Dero. Yale- And look out! Dero just ran into a big boot, and he goes down! Bryan- Dero back to his feet, and a scoop slam by Dimensioner. Dimensioner is a house of fire, and he drops an elbow. Dimensioner now, applies a chin lock, trying to slow this match down. Yale- You've got to wear your opponent down JR. Dimensioner drags him over to his corner, and there's a tag to X-Caliber. Bryan- X-Caliber comes in and there's the double team. Both men double suplex Dero and X-Caliber goes for the cover! One, two, AND NO! Yale- X-Caliber picks him up and throws Dero to the ropes. Dero reverses, comes back and a backdrop! X-Caliber in trouble! Bryan- The tide turns, and Dero with a spinning heel kick! Down is X-Caliber. Here is a cover, one, TWO, AND NO SIR! Yale- X-Caliber needs to tag his partner. Dimensioner wants back in! Bryan- Dero tags in Heckler, and now they are working together. a double DDT and X-Caliber is out. Heckler for the cover, one, two, and NO! It was Dimensioner making the save! Yale- Uh-oh Ross! Dero comes into the ring and he nails Dimensioner with a right hand, and here we go! Bryan- Dimensioner and Dero are going at it! This is an all out war, as they rumble off towards one side of the ring, but meanwhile look at Heckler! It looks like he's going for the top rope powerbomb, and WHAM! It's the Final Insult!! What a move! Yale- What a devastating maneuver! X-Caliber is out! Bryan- Heckler for the cover. One, two, and THREE! This one is over. Heckler and Dero get the win! (The bell rings, and the crowd reacts again with cheers and boos. Heckler and Dero stand, looking angry.) Andrews- Here are your winners, Dero and Heckler! (Dero is handed a mike, and he speaks, if a bit out of breath, with strength.) Dero- You know, I’m getting really sick of this. Week in and week out me and Heckler come out here and destroy people, but we keep getting ignored. Well, next week I take on President Caldera in a cage! And when I beat him, I get a shot at John Reed. Well, Johnny Boy, take a good look at the Sick Freaks. Because after five minutes in the ring with the Core of Destruction, they’ve been decimated. So John, this is a warning. Big Daddy Dero is coming for you. Be ready! (Natural One plays again, as the crowd begins to boo now. Dero and Heckler leave the ring, and the Sick Freaks behind.) Bryan- President Caldera and Dero next week in a cage. They’ve met three times now, and Caldera’s won two. But I don’t like Caldera’s chances in this one! (The show fades to commercial.) (We return to the ring area where the cage lowers over the ring. Toad comes out, followed by Dread. The bell rings, and the match begins. Toad takes control early with some mean rights and lefts, pounding Dread into oblivion. Toad takes Dread by the hair and sends him headfirst into the cage bars! Toad stomps away at Dread in the corner, before breaking off to taunt him. Toad moves back in, but Dread takes him down with a drop toe hold and sending Toad’s face into the bottom turnbuckle. Dread pulls Toad into the center of the ring and slaps on an STF. Toad manages to power out of the hold, but Dread stays in control. Dread lifts Toad up, and hits a vertical suplex. Dread then heads for the door, trying to escape. But Toad catches him on the way out, and takes him down from behind with a pump-handle slam! Toad lifts Dread up, and sends him into the ropes. Dread bounces back and takes a devastating powerslam from Toad! Toad then heads to the corner, where he begins to climb. He gets halfway up, but Dread gets to his feet and pulls him down. Toad lands straddling the top rope! Dread then springboards off the bottom turnbuckle, and leaps into a clothesline on the vulnerable Toad. Dread follows this up by heading for the door. But Toad somehow gets to his feet, and hooks Dread from behind. He lifts Dread up, and hits a reverse suplex! Toad hits the ropes, and comes back with a big splash to disable Dread. Toad then begins heading up the cage. Dread slowly gets up below him, and climbs next to him. The two men brawl on the side of the cage, before both lose their grips and fall to the mat below. While both men are down, Dan Rivero runs in from the back! The TV champ scales the cage from the outside, and gets to the top! The crowd is booing intensely, as Rivero takes off, and nails the Rivero Elbow on Dread! The crowd is confused, as Toad slowly crawls to the door. Toad gets out and wins the Hard*core title for the fourth time! The crowd goes crazy as Dread recovers, and begins searching for Rivero. The show fades to commercial as Toad celebrates his big win.) (We return to Meltdown, and Iron Man begins in the arena, and the crowd begins to boo like crazy. The guitars hit, and red pyro erupts from the stage. As the smoke clears, Rich Rollins emerges to the boos of the crowd. Rollins slowly heads to the ring, looking grim and serious.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Chicago, Illinois, he weighs in at 225 pounds. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins! Bryan- Well the former world champion is here not only for Reed, but for Mayhem! Rollins and Mayhem have been having some trouble, all because Mayhem wants to make it big! (Rollins steps into the ring, and hops onto the second rope. He glares at the fans, as the give him some incredible heat. Iron Man stops, and Might of Rome fades in. A huge mixed reaction follows, as Octavian struts from the back.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, from Rome, Italy, he weighs in at 285 pounds, and he is the Commissioner of GCW. . . Octavian! (Octavian stops short of the ring, and gold pyro explodes from the stage. Rollins glares out at Octavian, who stares right back. Octavian finally hops onto the apron, and steps into the ring. He locks eyes with Rollins once more, before turning to the crowd. Might of Rome fades out, and the Small Hours hits. The crowd erupts in a HUGE pop for the United States Champion. Mayhem emerges with his new belt, and heads to the ring.) Andrews- And their opponents, first, from Birmingham, England, he weighs in at 251 pounds, he is the GCW United States Champion. . . Tristain Mayhem! Yale- What a response for Mayhem! He really has momentum going into this one, and if he can pull out a win, he may be on his way to the big time! (Mayhem gets to the ring and doesn’t hesitate in sliding in. He walks right past Rollins and Octavian, and hops onto the turnbuckle. Camera flashes erupt from the crowd as Mayhem looks out at them. Small Hours stops, and Break Stuff hits. Another enormous reaction ensues, as John Reed emerges from the back. Rachel trails close behind, holding John’s title belt for him.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, from Chicago, Illinois, he weighs in at 247 pounds, and he is the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. . . John Reed! (Reed gets to the ring, but Rollins has already assaulted Mayhem. The bell rings as Reed and Octavian take to the apron, and Rollins and Mayhem duke it out.) Bryan- Well, we’re already underway! Rollins really hates being shown up by Mayhem, and he’s looking to take it out on him now! Mayhem is trying to fight back, but Rollins caught him off guard! Yale- Rollins hammers Mayhem down, and sends him into the ropes! Mayhem bounces back, and Rollins catches him with a big back body drop! Mayhem quickly gets back to his feet, and Rollins meets him with a boot to the gut! Bryan- Rollins stomps away at Mayhem, and sends him into the ropes again! Mayhem bounces back, and Rollins flies with a leaping back elbow! But Mayhem catches Rollins in midair! (The crowd cheers as Rollins realizes he’s in trouble.) Yale- Rollins is caught, and Mayhem brings him straight down to the mat with a slam! Rollins is hurting, as Mayhem takes a moment to recover! Mayhem gets to his feet, as Rollins reaches out to the Commissioner! Bryan- But Mayhem pulls Rollins back into the middle of the ring! Rollins springs to his feet, but Mayhem begins pounding away with some hard right hands! Mayhem backs Rollins into the corner, and continues pounding! Yale- Right after right, left after left, Mayhem is hammering mercilessly at the Renegade! Rollins goes down from the barrage, as the crowd goes crazy! Mayhem takes a step back, and NAILS Rollins in the face with a dropkick! Bryan- Big move from the youngster, but now Reed is shouting for a tag! (The crowd chants ‘Reed! Reed! Reed!’ Mayhem looks at them questioningly, but finally gives Rollins one more boot and tags in Reed.) Bryan- There it is! The World Champion is in the ring! Rollins gets to his feet, but Reed is in and he’s on fire! Reed hammers away at the Renegade, and sends him into the ropes! Rollins bounces back, and ducks a clothesline from Reed! Yale- He comes off the other side, but gets CRUSHED by a big clothesline from Reed! The crowd is loving this! Reed lifts Rollins back to his feet, and sends him into the ropes again! Bryan- Rollins bounces back again, and ducks another clothesline from Reed! Rollins bounces off the other side, and leaps! Spinning heel kick from the Renegade, and down goes Reed! (The cheers turn to boos immediately.) Yale- Reed goes down, as Rollins looks to Octavian! Rollins extends his hand, but I don’t think Octavian wants in! Finally, Octavian tags Rollins, and the Commissioner is squaring off with the World Champion! Bryan- Octavian heads right for Reed, and immediately snapmares him over! Octavian slaps on a rear chinlock, putting all of his weight on Reed! Don’t forget, Reed and Octavian were formerly in the Establishment together! Yale- That was a long time ago. Besides, Reed has what everyone wants now, he has no friends! Octavian releases Reed from the hold, only to lift him to his feet! Octavian bear hugs Reed, and takes him over with a belly to belly suplex! (The crowd oohs the move.) Bryan- Octavian covers Reed! ONE! TWO!! TH- Kickout by Reed! Octavian lifts Reed up again, and sends him into the ropes! Reed bounces back, and Octavian takes him down with a huge Greco-Roman throw! Yale- Octavian is in firm control of the World Champion right now! The Commissioner pulls Reed to the center of the ring, and tries to turn him over! He’s going for the Roman Rapper! Bryan- Reed is fighting it, but I think he may not be able to resist! Wait a minute, Mayhem is in the ring! Mayhem charges and takes Octavian down with a stiff clothesline! The referee urges Mayhem out, but he’s bought his teammate some time! Yale- Reed slowly crawls towards his corner, as Octavian shakes the cobwebs out! Octavian is looking to Rollins for a tag, as Reed heads for Mayhem! Octavian manages to get his tag to Rollins! Bryan- Reed leaps out for a tag! But Rollins grabs his boot and jerks him back! No tag was made! Rollins lifts Reed up to his feet, and takes him over with a suplex! Rollins floats over for a pin! Yale- ONE! TWO! THR- Kickout! Reed is still in it! Rollins is now heading up to the top rope! Rollins wants to put Reed away! The Renegade finally gets to the top, and measures Reed! But wait, here comes Mayhem! Bryan- Mayhem heads to Rollins, but the Renegade sees him! Mayhem charges, but Rollins comes off with a cross body on Mayhem on the apron! Both men topple to the ground outside! (The crowd oohs at the move and the impact.) Yale- Reed is looking for a tag, but Mayhem is down on the outside! Here comes Octavian in the ring now! Octavian lifts Reed to his feet, and kicks him in the gut! He lifts Reed up. . . PILEDRIVER! Bryan- Octavian hits a piledriver on Reed! Reed could be out cold! Mayhem and Rollins are finally up on the outside, and are going at it again! Rollins seems to be getting the best of Mayhem this time! Yale- Reed is out cold, Octavian is the only one left standing! Octavian now gives Reed a boot to the gut, and another! Reed may be out cold, but the Commissioner is still stomping away! Bryan- Octavian is showing no mercy here! What’s gotten into him? Octavian finally stops, and takes Reed by the legs! The referee is trying to restore order, but it’s futile at this point! Yale- Octavian turns the helpless Reed over, and he’s got him in the Roman Rapper! Octavian has the World Champion in trouble! But I think Reed is out cold! Bryan- Rollins now has Mayhem set up for a clothesline, but Mayhem sidesteps him! Mayhem shoves Rollins off and right into the steel post! Rollins goes down hard, as Mayhem sees what’s going on in the ring! Yale- Octavian is rearing back on Reed, and I can’t believe the Commissioner would do something like this! Reed is defenseless, but Octavian doesn’t care! But Mayhem is climbing into the ring! Bryan- Mayhem hits the ropes, and charges Octavian! HAMMER ON! Mayhem hits a huge leaping DDT on the Commissioner, right out of the Roman Rapper! Octavian goes down, and Mayhem covers! Yale- ONE! TWO! THREE!!! But Octavian wasn’t the legal man! (The crowd erupts in enormous cheers. The Small Hours hits again, as Mayhem’s arm is raised.) Andrews- Here are your winners, John Reed and Tristain Mayhem! Bryan- It got too chaotic to keep track of anything! Mayhem came in and hit Hammer On on the Commissioner, and got the pinfall! What a match! Yale- Mayhem already has his belt, and he’s heading for the back! Rollins is up, and he looks furious! Reed is still down, as is Octavian! Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time from Phoenix! Join us this Sunday for Oblivion! For David Yale, I’m James Bryan saying goodnight! (The show ends as Mayhem and Rollins stare each other down from a distance. . . . . . . .)
