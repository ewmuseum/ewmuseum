WorldWide 108 Cow Palace — Daly City, California ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Los Angeles fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: We are in San Francisco, California tonight for another edition of WorldWide with nineteen thousand in attendance! Yale: And I have to tell you JB, I’m feeling really good this week! The Boss is back and all is as it should be! Bryan: Steven Caldera is indeed back, and he made himself felt, getting a measure of revenge against Vivica J Valentine, the woman who put him on the shelf for months. Yale: That was no way to treat her boss, and only by Mr. Caldera’s good grace does she still have a job. Things have gotten out of control since Mr. Caldera has been away, but now he’s going to put them right. And that includes getting rid of that Primate in attendance! Bryan: You have to believe that is the plan with the main event we have tonight. Vivica J. Valentine teams with Lindsay Troy for the first time officially as the two try to compete in a four way tag match; if you can call it that. Yale: It will be Troy/Valentine taking on Banned & Exiled~!, Jay Terror teaming with Brad Jackson, and in an interesting twist LeStatt Knight teams with Andy Murray! I don’t know how those two will work together. Bryan: After Knight burned Murray by naming CJ Woods the number one contender and trying to embarrass the poor kid on television, I can’t see how, and I have a feeling that is the plan. Yale: Well speaking of tag team action, we also have two new ones making their debut tonight. Bryan: While Affirmative Action have already made themselves known, most notably to Chris Bagwell, the team of Uncle Truck and Friar is a complete unknown. You have to be positive that the winner of this match will really be on the fast track to getting at shot at the champions. Yale: That may be the case but I’ll tell you right now, no team can touch Banned & Exiled~! Bryan: Also making their debut will be Mindy Mercer, Ash Bombay, and Brock Alyas. The three will be competing in our opening bout in a triple threat match. Yale: It will be interesting to see which of them will start their GCW careers on the right foot. You never know, we may have the next Jay Terror among them! Don’t forget he started as lowly as they did at one point, but look at him now, on top of the world! Bryan: Someone who is on top of the world is Brian Fisher, and the new Television champion will be in action against the ever enigmatic Face. Yale: That’s a tall order for a new champion, as Face is just unpredictable. But if anyone can do it, it is Brian Fisher. He has been absolutely impressive ever since he was recruited by Tyson DeBough. Bryan: Another hot newcomer, CJ Woods, will be in action as well, taking on Dan Black. I have to tell you though, I don’t know how CJ Woods can even compete tonight after the beatings he took from Bagwell and LeStatt. Yale: The kid is a fighter, I’ll give him that, but I’m with you JB. He has to be feeling the effects, and going against someone like Dan Black is a tough task on its own. He’s called the Bulldozer for a reason, and if CJ Woods isn’t careful out there, he may just get flattened. Bryan: CJ Woods will also be taking with Sally Ford tonight, so that will be interesting to hear for sure after he saved her from Bagwell. Also tonight, we have Garbage Bag Johnny taking on Shaman again. These two had a hell of a fight last time, and with Garbage Bag on such a roll, this one has the looks of another classic. Yale: I don’t like him, but even I have to acknowledge the impressive streak he’s on. For those of you not aware, GCW’s very own Garbage Bag Johnny because the first non-contracted member of PRIME to win their Duel Halo match, awarding him the famed “golden ticket” and I have to tell you, I hope he uses it to force Lindsay Troy back where she belongs! Bryan: With the odd friendship him and Vivica seem to be striking, I can’t see that happening. Besides, he needs to make sure he stays focused on Shaman tonight and not worry about any Golden Ticket. Shaman is a perfect specimen of muscle and knows so many ways to hurt you, even if he has been acting odd as of late. Yale: As of late? He’s had a screw loose since the day he signed with this company! Bryan: Be that as it may, let’s send it down to Andrews to kick things off. ---- True Leadership "Established" hits, and at once the arena is plunged into furious boos. After a pause, President Steven Caldera appears on stage, adjusting his tie as he smiles broadly. Andrews: Please welcome the Owner and President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! Bryan: And we're kicking things off with the President of the company! We haven't seen Caldera since Dangerous Games when Jay Terror lost the World Title, but he returned last week to answer the arrival of Lindsay Troy! Yale: It's about damn time, too! I don't know what Caldera was doing during his time away, but now that he's back I have a feeling things are going to get a little better around here! Bryan: I was happy without Caldera around, quite frankly, and I think most of the crew would agree with me. Yale: Then why haven't you all quit yet? It's because no matter how much you hate the man, you have to respect him and his accomplishments! You have to acknowledge that he's built the best wrestling promotion in the world! You work for this man like everyone else, JB, and you should show some respect. Bryan: Whatever. Caldera carefully steps into the ring and accepts a microphone from Joey Andrews. His music continues to play as he closes his eyes and relishes the spotlight for a moment. Bryan: He certainly still feels at home in the ring. There are three certainties in life: death, taxes, and Steven Caldera can't stay away from GCW. Yale: Quiet, JB, don't spoil it. Finally the music fades and Caldera opens his eyes to look out at the crowd. Caldera: Did you miss me? The crowd responds with an emphatic "no." Caldera: That's all right, everyone here knows how we feel about each other. I didn't come back for you people. I came back for one reason and one reason only, and that's to make sure Lindsay Troy's stay in Global Championship Wrestling is an unpleasant one. The crowd erupts in cheers at the mention of Troy's name. Bryan: You just had to know Caldera wouldn't be far behind when Troy arrived two weeks ago, Dave. Yale: I could only hope, JB. But Troy's presence on GCW television is an affront to everything this man stands for. Caldera: Now, I've been getting a lot of questions about my absence. Why did you leave after Jay Terror lost the World Title? Why did you come back when you did? Why did you allow Lindsay Troy back in GCW? Well, tonight all your questions will be answered, starting with Lindsay Troy. Yale: See, Caldera's a man of the people, JB. Bryan: He's just rubbing our faces in it. Caldera: I may hate Lindsay Troy's breathing guts, but I'm a businessman first and foremost. And let's face it; you people want to see Lindsay Troy team with Vivica J. Valentine to take on Banned and Exiled~!, don't you? The crowd thunders its approval of the idea. Caldera: Well, if you people want to shell out fifty bucks each for the privilege of watching the greatest Tag Team in GCW history annihilate Valentine and Troy on Pay-Per-View, who am I to deny you that? I will gladly take your money and sit back and watch as Chris Storm and Markus Stone keep the GCW Tag Titles in The Establishment where they belong. I'm not here to run Lindsay Troy out of town. I'm here to make sure she gets embarrassed before she takes her sorry ass back to that junior league where she came from. Another massive chorus of boos follows. Caldera: But now that I'm back, there's something else that needs to be done. Something I've been hoping to do for some time. You see, when Jay Terror lost the GCW Title to Jordan White at Dangerous Games-- The fans interrupt with a rush of applause; Caldera pauses until it subsides. Caldera: --After Jay lost, I disappeared. People were speculating that Jay would be kicked out of The Establishment, or that the group was falling apart. But you are all too narrow-minded to see the truth. But don't take it from me. Take it from the man himself. Bryan: Oh, this should be good... Caldera: I'd like to present to you the man that masterminded the greatest moments in Establishment history, and your next GCW World Heavyweight Champion... "The Established" Jay Terror! The crowd grumbles as "Established" hits again, and out comes Jay Terror from the back. He pauses on stage for a moment, observing the crowd with contempt, then starts heading for the ring. Bryan: Well, here comes the former World Champion! Jay Terror has struggled in the ring since Caldera left, but it seems their friendship hasn't ended! Yale: That's the perfect word for it, JB. That's the difference between Terror and all the other former members of The Establishment. Caldera actually likes this kid and wants him to be the man, and Terror has responded by building the most lethal group Caldera's ever had control of! Terror enters the ring and Caldera greets him with a hug. Terror pats Caldera on the back and looks out at the crowd once more. Bryan: He certainly looks pleased with himself. His life is sure to get easier with Caldera back. Terror takes control of the microphone as a heavy wind of boos sweeps across the arena. To the world's surprise, he offers merely a grin for their efforts and speaks loudly for all to hear. Jay Terror: Tell me Daly City, how the hell are you feeling seeing two of the most powerful minds in this industry standing side by side once again? The boos get louder and their smiles get wider. Jay speaks above the roar nearly shouting. Bryan: That's an answer if I ever heard one. Yale: He's right though. Look at those two guys in the ring. Damn! Bryan: Might wanna' wipe that brown spot off your nose. Jay Terror: Just what I like to hear! You see, it's been too damn long since Steven Caldera came out and showed his face to you undeserving people. It's been too damn long that he's been behind the scenes pulling off some of the biggest twists seen on television. Finally though, finally, here he is - the man that taught me all I know. Terror paces around the ring for a minute to feel the pulse of the crowd. Jay Terror: For the past few months, I've been leading some of the toughest competitors on this roster toward something that they could only imagine. Guiding them somewhere they dreamed of going. Where might you ask? Just look up. You see, each and every one of these guys that stands behind myself and Steven are no different than us. At one point Caldera wanted to make it up the ladder and take a look at where he is now. When I was fresh on this circuit the only place I wanted to be was in the spotlight fighting some of the toughest men and women the world had to offer. Take a look at me now. You see, under Caldera, people go places. People that have talent and a desire to become better make it up that ladder of success and into the ranks that I now live in. He pauses and looks across the ring at Caldera. Jay Terror: That is why I've been guiding DeBough, Fisher, Jackson, and now Banned & Established~! Steven Caldera taught me how to rise, who to mess with, and what to do when you're at the top. He's shown me how to win, how to win titles, and how to beat the best out there. Hell, if it wasn't for his insight and keen intellect I doubt I would be where I am. Bryan: Well that's for sure. It's easy when the boss is pulling strings for you. Yale: That's not true. It was his knowledge that helped Jay grow out in the ring. Bryan: His knowledge? Caldera's may have knowledge but I know he isn't putting it into Jay's head. Just look at all the ridiculous things he's done while Caldera was out. Jay Terror: But you know what, I'll be the first to admit that I'm holding this team back. I mean, we have the Tag Team titles, the Television title and surely Brad or Tyson will be gaining the US title from Taylor soon. Of course, that leaves one more - the world championship. Sure, chances are that if I had defeated Murray at the last pay per view I would be challenging Knight for it now but sometimes the ball doesn't exactly land on the table right. To us, it's a minor setback. You see, holding onto that strap was one of the most comforting feelings I've ever felt. Never before have I been so content with myself. Losing it - well, we can say that it was not the experience I meant to have happen. Especially not to that rag-tag Jordan White. Yale: Especially not to him at all! I bet if Jay could name the biggest thorn in his side it would be White. Bryan: Considering their history, I could agree with that fully. In second place might just be Murray. Jay Terror: But things move on and that leads me to my final statement. I know I might have to wait for Murray to take that title out of Knight's old hands, but when he does, I'll be gunning for it again. I'll be fighting harder than I've ever fought before doing the things that Steven Caldera has taught me over time and I will take it back. And when I do - tell me how you feel about the greatest version of the Establishment ever created. Jay smiles and hands the microphone back to Caldera who nods approvingly. Caldera: Jay, I wanted to bring you out here tonight to thank you for what you've done with The Establishment. And to answer the question, I left because I knew it was time for Jay Terror to take the next step in his career, to become the leader this organization needs and deserves. Jay, you took that step, you became something greater. But there's more work to be done. I'm talking about something even bigger than winning the GCW World Title back. I'm talking about becoming the greatest leader The Establishment and GCW have ever seen. I'm talking about becoming the new GCW icon, the one that's going to lead this company into a new era. Caldera pauses for a second as the crowd jeering starts up once again. It is quite obvious by the volume level this time around that the crowd in San Francisco is none too happy about his return. As always the case with the Establishment, this only fuels their fire and causes the shit eating grins across all of their faces to grow, that is until "Bittersweet" by Fuel hits on the PA system. Bryan: Thank god! Yale: I wouldn’t quite use that choice of words. In fact if I had to say anything about god right now JB, it would be that Tessa better believe in him if she’s going to try and size herself up against not only Jay Terror, but the boss as well. Indeed the bite sized superstar is marching her way down to the ring looking mighty furious. She grabs a microphone from ringside before rolling into the ring, standing only inches away from Terror. The two have a momentary stare down before Tessa finally glances away and directs her attention towards Caldera. Windsor: I seriously hope for your own sake that you are not out here building your company around that guy standing beside you. Even though Tessa has barely said anything, the crowd cheers wildly simply for the fact that someone was out here to end Caldera’s countless praises of Jay Terror. Windsor: A guy who talks much more action than he actually does. I hate to break it to you, but the fans here in San Francisco know that his stock is dropping faster than Wall Street these days. There is no use wasting valuable company time trying to play it up otherwise. When’s the last time he even had a match? Terror spits out the word ‘tonight, for which Windsor already has words. Windsor: And if you call that match tonight an actual plan to reveal just how ‘high and mighty’ Jay Terror is, you’re absolutely kidding yourself. We obviously know how that’s going to play out. Tessa looks back over in Terror’s direction, giving Caldera enough leeway time to cut back in. Caldera: Go on then Ms. Windsor, humor us with some predictions. He attention snaps back towards Caldera, who arrogantly towers over Windsor, eagerly awaiting what she has to say about this evenings Main Event. Windsor: You really want to know what I think? I mean it’s pretty out there in the open. That Jay Terror is overrated. That tonight obviously you’re going to continue to play the numbers game and hand him another undeserved victory. That the only reason he’s even standing out besides you right has something to do with his lips. I’m done playing coy, Caldera. I’m done pretending that I just haven’t earned my chance at the pretty little belt around Knight’s waist right now. If you think it’s going to be as easy as putting the champion up against Jay Terror in the right setting with the right people watching on, I’m here to tell you that you’re dead wrong. If Terror wants that belt so damn bad, he’s going to have to go through me first. Bryan: Wow! That's pretty damn bold of Tessa! Yale: Is she really doing this? Does she have any idea what she's getting into here? Vivica needs to come out here and tell her what happens when you mess with Jay Terror when the GCW Title is at stake! Finally Terror speaks up, after regaining his breath from the huge fit of laughter he had just broken into. Terror: That ought to be easy enough, all I need to know is the direction of the nearest mall. While you’re at it, you can pick me up an outfit that’ll accent that belt you’re going to ‘fight me to the death’ over. Caldera gets a chuckle out of this as well, which only infuriates Windsor further. Without warning she rears back and catches Terror in the chin with a left. Bryan: Whoa! Tessa just clocked Terror! Yale: Oh, big mistake! Terror staggers back with a scowl on his face. The crowd explodes as Tessa urges him in for a fight, but Caldera, who has positioned himself behind her, delivers a forearm to the back of the head. Tessa stumbles forward and Terror delivers a kick to the gut, followed by a swift DDT. Bryan: Oh, damnit, Caldera hit her from behind! And Terror takes her down! Yale: She sucker-punched him, JB! That's exactly what she deserves! Bryan: Tessa was damn bold in coming out here and standing up to Caldera and Terror, but she should have brought some backup! Seated on the mat and rubbing his jaw, Terror looks up at Caldera in disbelief. Caldera straightens his jacket and helps Terror up, and together the two stare down at a writhing Tessa. Finally "Established" hits again, and the two slowly begin their exit. Bryan: I really admire Tessa's gumption here, but I think her temper may have gotten the better of her here! Yale: It was just straight up stupid, JB! This was supposed to be the moment of Jay Terror's coronation as the new GCW Icon, but Tessa had the audacity to come out here and stick her nose in it. What business does she have out here? Bryan: She's a phenomenally talented wrestler, Dave, and she's trying to move up in the world. And damnit, she deserves it, and she didn't want to sit in the back and listen to another self-congratulatory speech by Jay Terror. Tessa's trying to break through the glass ceiling installed by The Establishment, but I think she just learned it takes more than just guts to succeed. Yale: If she's smart she'll just stay out of it from now on, JB. But coming out here wasn't smart to begin with, so I suspect Tessa's in for a lot more pain at the hands of The Establishment! ---- Time to Dance The next cut to the backstage area shows Mindy Mercer doing her last minute stretches before her match. She has one leg lifted and placed against the wall as the camera closes in behind her. The particular angle is probably enough for the male viewers to sit up a bit straighter in their seats, but it's short-lived. Mercer: Huh? Who's there? She puts her leg down and turns around to blink at the cameraman. Mercer: Hehe, yer pretty good sneakin' up on people fer someone wif a big camera on their shoulder. She giggles, oblivious to just what the man was filming. Moments later, Sally Ford approaches. Ford: Hey, Mindy. I don't suppose you could give us a minute to talk about your match before you head out to the ring? Mindy turns to Sally and smiles widely. Mercer: Why of course I can! Ford: Great. I'll start by saying that it's always a pleasant surprise to see a woman of your stature become so successful as a wrestler. Former Global Television and World champion and now looking to make your mark in GCW, why do you choose wrestling? Mercer: Well y'know somethin', Sassy Sally, wrasslin's always been a way fer me ta show my serious side and that I'm not jus' some Italian bimbo who's too afraid of breakin' her nails ta be any good at fightin'. Believe it or not I come from a fightin' family, so it's only right I do them proud and make myself the bestest wrestler I can be! Ford: And to continue along on that path to being the... bestest... you've taken out a tough competitor in Daria Ngozi, and now you have arguably a tougher test in two veterans in the sport. Your thoughts on your performance against Daria and on your opponents tonight? Mercer: Daria Ngozi was my first test an' she gave me a run fer my money, but now I gots a real challenge, 'cause I'm fightin' two guys! I've had triple threat matches before but this'll be mah first one in a while so I'm awful nervous, but I'll be damned if I don't come out of this one with my hand raised at the end. Ford: Your cousin Michael is another veteran wrestler, and he's beaten one of your opponents for tonight, Ash Bombay. Does that weigh on your mind at all, that you want to match your cousin's success against him? Mercer: Well a wittle bit. Yeah, mah cousin beat Ashy before in a tag match, so I'm hopin' not ta let him down 'cause I know he'll be watchin' this ta see if I win. HI MIKEY! Mindy sticks her face obnoxiously close to the camera and grins cheesily for the crowd watching at home. Ford: Right. And your other opponent, Brock Alyas, is a rather large competitor. Mercer: Well I dunno much about Brock, I jus' know he's not Brock Lesnar and nothin' can be worse than Brock Lesnar. Mindy nods sagely. Mercer: I've beaten big opponents before though, so I'm not too worried. I mean, ta every advantage there's a disadvantage, so I'mma jus' pick apart all of his disadvantages an' not let him expose any of mine! Ford: I believe you're match is coming up any second now, so I'll just ask you if you have anything else to say before you head out to the ring. Mercer: One more thing ta say. Hrm... Mindy taps her chin and seems to be pondering hard. REAL hard. Finally, she exclaims... Mercer: WHAT'CHA GONNA DO, brothers?! What'cha gonna do, when Mindy Mercer an' her six inch platform heels have a Dance Dance Revolution all over YOUUUUUU?! She bounds off camera, leaving behind a perplexed Sally Ford. Yale: Did she just say what I think she said? Bryan: What do you think she said? Yale: That she's not only likely to be snorting Pixy Stix before all of her matches, but her endurance comes from playing a game mastered by emo kids and people who otherwise don't know how to really dance. Bryan: ... Yale: What? Bryan: ...anyway. Mindy appears to be ready, so that means we should be ready for the first match of the evening to get under way next! ---- Not Yet Steven Caldera grins as he waltzes through the GCW hallways once again. He motions to hi-five a passing crew member, but the young man flinches at the sight of Caldera's raised hand. Caldera simply snickers and steps to his office. He swings the door open, but doesn't make it two steps before bumping into Brad Jackson. Caldera: Hello, Brad. Jackson folds his arms over his chest. He's still in his street clothes, dressed in black from head to toe. Jackson: Boss, I need to talk to you. Caldera nods, gesturing to the empty seat across from his cluttered desk. Caldera: Sure. Jackson grins, settling into the chair with his customary slouch. His silver Zippo finds its way into his hand, and he starts clicking the lid, fidgeting for a moment before spilling his guts. Jackson: So, Shockwave's coming up, right? Caldera: Jackson, if this is about your match- Jackson frowns, picking up something in Caldera's voice. Jackson: What? Oh, man… I know. You want to clear it by me first. Something sick, right? Glass tables? Exploding C4 Ropes? Right, we can't do anything too extreme to take away from the Main Event… I gotcha. I'm cool with a straight match. Garbage Bag Johnny won't- Caldera: …be your opponent… Jackson: …know what's coming to him. Jackson stops, realizing that Caldera has just interrupted him. He pauses for a moment, replaying the conversation, shaking his head in disbelief. Jackson: Whoa… whoa… wait. I think the chair shots are getting to me. Did you just say what I think you said? Caldera: I did. Jackson shoots to his feet, all traces of good humor gone in an instant as his features darken in anger. Jackson: Who the hell am I facing then? Bryan Mayhem? Shaman for the eleventh time? Caldera: Jackson, calm down. You'll get your shot at Johnny, just not right now. You'll be facing someone else at Shockwave. I would've thought you'd be happy about this- Jackson shoves the chair, slamming it against Caldera's desk before stomping towards the door, seconds away from going nuclear. Jackson: If you won't sign off on the match, I'll kick the shit out of that hippie freak in the parking lot, unsanctioned. I'm sick of this shit! I don't need it! And with that, he storms out of the office, slamming the door hard enough in his wake to crack the glass in the window beside it. Caldera: Neither do I. Bryan: Wow, Caldera's back but he's not giving Jackson Garbage Bag Johnny at Shockwave? Yale: In case you've already forgotten what we've seen tonight, JB, Caldera is a man with a plan! If we won't be seeing Johnny versus Jackson one on one, you can bet Caldera has something even bigger in store! Bryan: But Brad Jackson is itching for a fight, and he's off to find Johnny! I don't think Garbage Bag Johnny's plan to stay out of the way is going to last much longer. ---- Mindy Mercer vs. Ash Bombay vs. Brock Alyas Bryan – The first match we have this evening is a three-way dance and for two of the competitors it will be their debut. Although new to the scene Ash Bombay has built quite the resume for himself in federations ICE and PDW. The other new-comer has recently been released from Scotland Correctional after a four-month bid for…a drunken disorderly and possession of illegal firearm charge? This sounds interesting. Yale – Sounds like my kind of guy! Bryan – Anyways Brock Alyas has quickly earned himself a name in the New Frontier of Wrestling and there’s no doubt about it – this guy enjoys hurting people. Unfortunately for Mindy Mercer, she’s also involved in this match-up against a well-rounded Bombay and a money hungry Alyas both looking to make their debut one to remember. Mercer’s last match-up as you may recall was against Daria Ngozi where she picked up the decision. The camera panned on the entrance for this evenings first bought and the lights faded signaling the competitors to make their way to the ring. Joey Andrews was set-up in the middle of the ring and the ring bell was rang three-times signaling the first match-up of the evening. Andrews – Ladies and gentleman! Please rise to your feet for the FIRST match up of the one-hundred and eightieth installment of WORLD WIDE brought to you by GLOBAL CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING! Huge crowd pop for this one. The fans were sick of waiting to see some pandemonium. Andrews – The follow contest is scheduled for one-fall and it will be a three way dance! As the lights dim, harmless sounding piano chords begin to gently fill the silence over the speakers. A couple of pink spotlights swirl and circle around the arena. Slowly, the piano fades, however, and the pink lights begin to become red. Then out of nowhere, the heavy hitting opening of 'Blinded By Hatred' by UnSun kicks in full gear, just as an energetic Mindy Mercer comes bounding out into view. Andrews – Making her way to the ring weighing in at one-hundred twenty two pounds and wrestling out of Toms River, New Jersey… The crowd immediately roars to life as Mindy appears, and she gestures with her arms to them, tring to get them out of their seats. As she gets closer to the ring the red spotlights start to converge over her, keeping her lit in that dark crimson color as she waves and pumps her fists to the crowd. As she bounces her way down toward the ring, Mindy stops and lifts off a candy necklace from around her neck. She then moves to a security barricade and reaching over to give it to a lucky fan before her attention turns to the ring. Mindy hops in place for a moment, shaking out her limbs before taking two swift leaps; the first one gets her up onto the apron, and the second vaults her over the top rope and into the ring. Andrews – MINDY MERRCERRR!!! The lights return to normal and Mindy climbs to the second turnbuckle in one corner, playing to the crowd a little bit longer before she settles down in her corner and her music fades. 1 the ring. "One" by Metallica blares through out the arena as the lights go dark. Blue strobe lights fill the entrance way. Andrews – The following competitor making his way to the ring weighs in at two hundred twenty pounds and hails from Oklahoma City, Oklahoma… Bombay gives a arrogant strut towards the ring as he flashes his trusty half smile and blows kisses to the female admirers in the audience. He steps into the ring and gives a quick pose for the fans then he turns his attention to his opponent. Standing tall, Ash glares at his opponent briefly, before closing his eyes to take in the rage portion of the song... Andrews – ASHHH BOMMMBAYYY! As if drawing power from the hateful energy of the song, Bombay's fists begin to shake with his own fury, to an explosive climax in which Bombay throws open his eyes and lets loose a bellowing war cry! Snarling at his foe, Bombay brings up his fists, ready for battle. The camera panned back towards the entrance and the lights faded throughout the entire arena as the bass lines of Twista’s rendition of "Break Ya Neck" punish the sound system. Some of the crowd reacts to the theme music but they weren’t familiar with who would be clearing the curtains. Wasting no time, the menacing figure of Brock Alyas made his way through the curtains and towards the ring while smoking a cigarette. Andrews – Making his way to the ring weighing in at two hundred sixty eight pounds… hailing from Detroit, Michigan… Not the prototypical crowd pleaser Brock didn’t take a minute to slap any hands instead he walked with a purpose on his way to the ring with only the thought of showing GCW how big of a mistake they made when signing him. The expression on his face was obviously intimidating Bombay but Mercer looked like she was ready to reconsider her career. Andrews – BRROCK AAALYYASSSS! After flicking his cigarette into the crowd at one of the front row fans, Brock stepped through the second rope and took his place in his corner. The referee this evening, Hal Jenkins calls a good, solid match and he quickly checked Brock for foreign objects in his baggy jean shorts and much to Brock’s disapproval was convinced everything would hopefully remain clean.
