Paddy O'Shea Biography Real Name Paddy O'Shea Nicknames The Man From The Caravan, The Unrepentant Fenian Bastard, Hatchets (by certain family members) Hometown Galway, Ireland Birthday 5/11/69 Height 5'9 Weight 180 lbs. Fed Titles - Fed Awards - Biography Paddy hails from the sleepy town of Athenry in the mountainous regions of Galway, Ireland. Son to a life long alcoholic, Paddy quickly learned how to cut corners and earn easy money, whether this was through deceit or force. His childhood was one of confusion with his mother and father speaking only rarely to each other because of their lifelong hate for one another. They couldn't compromise on one thing - One of the key differentiations has been the relationship game. Paddy has eight other siblings (with Old Papa O'Shea cutting corners too with the condoms) whilePaddy is notoriously introvert among females. O'Sheas perception of the family unit is therefore a paradox and Paddy found commitment quite difficult. However, for the same reason, Paddy is known to be overly trusting and eager for friendship. As a teenager, his father taught him (in some of his few moments of soberness) some of the worst lessons that can be taught - take what you can and take what you can't. Growing up, Paddy was known to sell, wheel, deal and flog anything of his fancy, just to fuel his desire to take despite having no commitment to the consumerism. As he entered his twenties, he moved into the circle of bare-knuckle boxing in his hometown. His status was quickly raised a result of his shockingly powerful left hook and high pain threshold. However it took nearly ten years for him to get anywhere. At the age of 30, his status caught the attention of a PCW scout who was actually in the local city of Cork to watch an up and coming star. After a shaky trial period, Paddy found his feet in the sport of wrestling only to have his career postponed by a fractured foot. With only a terminated contract in his pocket, Paddy turned his new talent to the AWC where his success led to a successful run in GTT5 and a spell in PRIME. Paddy left wrestling in early 2006, his decision fueled by a combination of a continuing knee injury and a general distaste for the sport...of course, this distaste was merely temporary and he soon realised wrestling was the only thing he truly embraced. Entering GCW in mid-2007, Paddy had problems with his visa causing it to be revoked. A year later, Paddy managed to independently negotiate an agreement allowng him to stay in the US as long as he continued to be apart of a mainstream promotion. A set of bad knees had made this difficult but luckily GCW decided to give the old dog one last shot to prove he can still draw the crowds. Other Feds PWA, AWC, PRIME Other Fed Titles 1 X PCW Rising Star Champion, 1 x AWC Alliance Champions (w/Crimson O'Malec and The Farmer) Other Fed Awards First ever PCW Diamond in the Rough winner, 1 x AWC Countdown Trophy Holder Strengths Quick; Powerful punching ability; Unwavering determination and heart Weaknesses Age; Injury prone; Weak in the air Wrestling Style Brawler Strategy Paddy is an exceptionally dirty fighter but has the character for the fans to embrace it. He lives off the fans support and so is the type of wrestler who'll fight tooth and nail.
