Something happened in that huge main event title match to remind these two of why they are such great friends and teammates, and it’s showing. Yale: No way, JB, there’s animosity there. Knight takes the upper hand on White, backing him into the ropes. He whips White to the other side, but White simply takes off, delivering a flying forearm to the face of Rollins! Come on, Briggs, do your job! Knight immediately drags White down into a brutal reverse neckbreaker. Rollins clambers back onto the apron and extends and enthusiastic hand, which Knight obliges with a tag. Rollins steps in and pulls White up, delivering a hard slap across the face. White stumbles back into the corner, where Rollins begins laying in hard boots to the gut. Bryan: In comes Rollins now, and he’s really tearing into White! This kid came within a hair of being World Champion a few months ago, but now he’s up against a highly motivated Rollins! Bryan: In comes the opportunistic Caldera, and Rollins is going for the knockout blow! Yale: This is gonna be great, JB!
