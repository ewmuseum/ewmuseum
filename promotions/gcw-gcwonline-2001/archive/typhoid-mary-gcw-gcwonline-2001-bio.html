Typhoid Mary Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Yuma, Arizona Birthday February 14th 1982 Height 5'8 Weight 153 Biography Standing at an average 5ft8' Mary doesn't seem intimidating at all but her psychiatric records would say otherwise. She is or at least has been technically, clinically insane but adorable none the less. She's athletically muscular but not overly, she's slender and curvaceous with a caramel complexion courtesy of her African American and Native American parentage. Her appearance is wild and funky, kinda goth mixed with club kid. Her usual trademark attire is a slashed up fishnet body stocking which she wears beneath a slashed up cheerleader outfit along with fingerless lace gloves and knee high combats loosely strung up. Her hair is usually just left to cascade about her heart shaped face and the huge raven curls frame her innocent doe eyes. Mary is a recovering psycho, she just wants to be happy but it never works out right. She loses the plot a lot, she has a lot of razor blade scars from failed suicide attempts and crys for help. She can be a clown one minute and dark the next. She almost always speaks in the third person and occasionaly in the fourth. She has no siblings only 3 cousins all of whom wrestle too but she sees infrequently.
