Caldera's Corner

Catching Up

Well, it's been a while since I've written, so I figured I'd go ahead and write. I've been kinda out of the loop, it being Easter and all. So happy Easter to all my Christian friends, and happy Passover to all my Jewish friends. To everyone else; screw you. You get your [detail withheld]. (I'm kidding, of course.)

Things for the Pay-Per-View are shaping up nicely. I've been brainstorming all week, and I've got some great stuff lined up. I've been writing a lot over the past week, and it's turning out great. Everyone that's been with me for a while knows what to expect. . . But I think everyone will really be blown away.

If you notice me being a bit out of it, it's because [detail withheld]. Shit happens, I'll move on. . . maybe. . . but for now, I'll wallow in self pity for a while.

In all honesty, I don't know what else to add. I promise I'll do a real column soon. Until then, keep up the good work, and get ready for a great PPV. -President Caldera E-mail President Caldera
