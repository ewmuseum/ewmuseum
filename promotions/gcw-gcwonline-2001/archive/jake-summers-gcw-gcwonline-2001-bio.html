Jake Summers Biography Real Name Jacob Alexander Summers II Nicknames 'Golden Boy' Hometown Panama City Beach, FL Birthday July 04, 1976 Height 6' 2' Weight 235 Fed Titles Current GCW Tag Champ w/ Ryan Stephens Fed Awards None Yet Biography Below is his title history. I'll add a more detailed bio as time goes on. Jacob Alexander Summers II was born July 4, 1976 to Jacob & Margerie Summers. Like many kids Jake spoiled, but in his case it went far beyond that. Priveleges do come with wealth. Jake's privelege was that fact he was able to travel to various prestigous amateur tournaments across the country. Needless to say having the best trainers at your whim did pay off as Jake went on to win many. The success carried on through highschool and afterwards. Having the ability to succeed on every level of the game is what earned him the nickname 'Golden Boy' from his peers. His amateur accolades earned him an opportunity for Olympic hardware. However thanks to failing a substance test, Jake saw his medal revoked and amateur status trickle down a urinal drain. Upon returning to the states, Jake finished college at the University of Michigan with a bachelors in business management and a minor in real-estate. Soon after graduating college and with a bachelor's degree to fall back upon, Jake broke into the pros in 1997; having been trained by the world renowned hardcore superstar, 'Hard Rock' Darren James, at James' own Jamestown Massacre Dojo. Summers excelled at this 'School of Hard Knocks' and soon made his debut in New Hardcore Wrestling Federation with James by his side. With James as his partner, Jake went to win his first title, NHWF Tag Team Champion. Success continued to follow Summers as he broke out into singles action in his next stop, Rebels Championship Wrestling Assocation in 1998. To make a long story short success and Summers has gone hand in hand. However he is not without heartache as well. At least not one that you'd see back in the late 80's and early 90's when wrestlers were overly gimmick. Instead Jake is enduring an inner battle. On one shoulder you have the angel and on the other the devil. Both battling for Jake's soul as he struggles between doing right and doing what he always has in years past. Today Jake clashes with trying to be a role-model for his two sons and teaching them right through example.
