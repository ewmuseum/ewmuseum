Anthony Monroe Biography Real Name Anthony Vincent Monroe Nicknames Tony Hometown San Diego, California Birthday 10/14/1982 Height 6'3 Weight 250 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography Well, for starters, Anthony was born and raised under money. He lived his life on the beautiful shores of Daytona Beach Florida. He had everything he could ever dream of. Money, Friends, Good Looks, and Family was all around him. *When Anthony was about 12, he had a surfing accident one morning. Anthony said at the time he could remember everything that happened, until it happened. He said he was surfing along on just and average wave when out of no where he was hit, after that...dark. The accident had left his right forearm completely smashed and shattered. The doctors had no idea what to do for him, there was even talk of completely severing his right arm. Peter, Anthony's father, would have none of this. His through his money and power at the doctors, AND IT WORKED. The doctors, in a very confidential surgery, Anthony's doctors removed Anthony's fragments of bone from his right forearm and somehow managed not only to piece it back together, but they also coated it in some kind of super liquid metal. The documents and staff have all vanished...as if....paid off. Not even Anthony can tell you what happened during that surgery or how long it lasted. After that there was nothing he couldn't have or do. His father was dewtermind to give him everything. However he never used that power, he knew the money he used was not his...it was his fathers. Unlike most kids with single parents Anthony grew up with his Father, Peter. Peter was a wealthy man, making his money from doing nothing. Just simply helping people keep track of their overseas bank accounts. No one really knows if Anthony's father made is money legally...sometimes computers do make mistakes right? This always stayed on the back of Anthony's mind when he was growing up, but when your a kid it doesn't matter. It only started to way on Anthony's mind when he turned about the age of 18, right after he graduated High School, Anthony started smoking pot. Anthony's father knew this and hatted it, but didn't want to tell his son outright. Instead he decided to take Anthony along with him on his business trips thinking this would take Anthony away from the people and the party's in sunny Daytona Beach. And it did, but it also showed Anthony the real way business was done 'overseas'. Now although Anthony's father hated drugs...he was no angle. Like I said no one knows what Anthony's father did to make all his money...but Anthony knows. This made Anthony want to become something, something to most people would sound weird, but Anthony wanted to become a cop...just a regular street cop. Anthony's father actually encouraged it. He paid for the college and the training, for all the fancy gear. Why? It was because he wanted Anthony to have a better life...not financially...but MORALLY. Anthony graduated collage top of his class. A nice young rookie, fresh for duty. Now this is where the shit hits the fan. Anthony's on his way home from his first night shift, tired, red-eyed...the way you get after your first 12 hour shift in anything. HE arrives at his home... or his father's home doesn't matter. As he pulls up the white granite driveway, he starts seeing something hanging out in front of the door. His hart sinks as he gets a little closer. 7:30 AM and what a way to start the morning, hanging from a rope....is his father. After this point it gets a little cloudy in Anthony's memory...what he can remember is that his father is dead, no clues, no witnesses, no nothing. The police told him they had nothing...nothing was to be found. Anthony was devastated and furious. His highest achievement had just let him down on the one thing he NEVER wanted to lose. The Present...Anthony has just quit the DBPD (Daytona Beach Police Department), it's the day after and Anthony is about to swear on his life to find out what happened to his father....but his money starts to drop, needs to find a way to make money....BOOM...WRESTLING!!!!!
