Annihilation 2001 Allstate Arena, Chicago, Illinois ---- ACWF II: The Jhub vs. Khan The camera cuts to the loading dock area. A large ring like structure is set up on the asphalt floor. An area about twice the size of an average ring is boxed in by chains, supported by tall concrete pillars in each corner. Above the area is a wire mesh panel, supported by the concrete pillars. Hanging from the center of this panel, twelve feet above the floor, hangs the GCW US title belt. In one corner of the ring area is an ominous forklift. In each remaining corner is a steel wrench. The crowd cheers as the image appears on the MegaTron. Andrews- The following contest is the ACWF match for the GCW United States Championship! The winner must utilize the forklift to gain the title belt! The crowd cheers even louder, expecting an incredible match. My Own Summer hits, and they crowd erupts with a deafening mixed reaction. The Jhub appears on the MegaTron, walking slowly from the backstage area out into the loading dock. Andrews- Introducing first, the challenger. From Stamford, Connecticut, he weighs in at 270 pounds. . . The Jhub! The crowd continues with their cheers and boos, until My Own Summer fades out. The Jhub looks entirely focused, as he stares up at the title belt. Suddenly, Debonair hits, and the crowd explodes with another mixed reaction. Some surprising cheers are heard as Khan appears in the area, walking through a lot of parked cars opposite the ring area. Andrews- And his opponent, from Hokkaido, Japan, he weighs in at 280 pounds, he is the GCW United States Champion. . . Khan! Khan stares at the Jhub. They stare each other down for a few long moments, before the bell rings and the match begins. Bryan- And here we go! We’ve seen only one of these matches in the past, and it was hellacious! Khan emerged victorious in that match the first time around and the first Annihilation. . . Will he be able to retain here tonight? Yale- This is going to be amazing, JB! The Jhub runs in first, but Khan dodges him! Khan shoves the Jhub off, and the Jhub goes headfirst into the concrete pillar!
