Brock Alyas Biography Real Name Brock Alyas Nicknames the Brick Shithouse, the MEAN Chiropractor, the Suplex Specialist, the Detroit Lion (although I'd avoid calling him that for a little while) Hometown Detroit, Michigan. Birthday February 2, 1980. Height 6'2 Weight 268.lbs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Fuck you. Pay me. Other Feds No Mercy Wrestling, NFW Other Fed Titles N/A Other Fed Awards NFW Rookie of the Mid Year (2008) Strengths Brute Force, Ring Knowledge, the Drive to win. Weaknesses Speed, Stamina, Right Knee. Wrestling Style Powerful Technical Innovator Strategy Underground pit fighter turned wrestler, Brock's very well versed in all areas of combat. A meancing stiff delivery makes Brock one of wrestlings most ruthless and feared competitors. Each and every night those in attendance witness a real life fight in the ring when Brock's entertaining. He doesn't care about your past injuries in fact, if he finds out you've suffered from one that's the first point from which he'll attack. If you're healthy, he knows where the soft spots on you are and often exploits them. Brock's matches have been known to be very brutal and leave fans wondering if he realizes what the sport is all about. He's not a fan of foreign objects...that is if you don't consider his left and right one. He's short but stalky and has no problem getting even the biggest guys over for the clean, T-Bone suplex. I wouldn't wrestle Brock Alyas if my life depended on it. Because, if I were to wrestle Brock Alyas - my life WOULD be on the line. Finisher Move Extra Mile EXPLODER! Finisher Description Wrist Clutch Exploder Suplex with a devastating after affect. (Isn't typically a move kicked out of) Finisher Setup Move High impact SPEAR! Setup Description Although it's more commonly referred to as a GORE! Brock's very picky about copyright issues that prevent him from calling it that. Although, he does feel as if it is a great surname for the Spear. Special Moves Northern Lights Suplex, Dragon Suplex, Judo Flip, Belly to Belly Suplex, Waistlock German Suplex, T-Bone Suplex (and all of it's buttery modifications) Other Moves Basic Striking, Takedowns. - Forearm / Elbow to face - Overhand Punch - Kick to Gut - Headlock Takedown - Snap Suplex - Belly to Belly Side Suplex Mid-Match/Wear-Down Maneuvers. - Sidewalk Slam - Reverse Suplex - T-Bone Suplex - Belly to Belly Suplex (of ALL variations) - Northern Lights Suplex - Head And Arm Suplex - Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex Signature/Highlight Maneuvers. - Capture Suplex - German Suplex - Falcon Arrow - Fisherman’s Suplex - Death Valley Driver - Half Nelson Release Suplex - Full Nelson Release Suplex - Tiger Suplex - Half Tiger Half Rear Naked Suplex Entrance Music "Fuck You Pay Me" by Young Joc
