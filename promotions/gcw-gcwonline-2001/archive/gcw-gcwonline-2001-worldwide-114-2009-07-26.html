WorldWide 114 Wachovia Center — Philadelphia, Pennsylvania ---- Bishop Steele vs. 'Primetime' Joe Milone vs. Alex Colt The three wrestlers in the ring are already glaring hostilely at each other, just waiting for the bell to ring. The second it dings, both Bishop and Colt go after the much bigger Milone. Colts gets to him first, and nails Milone with a flying forearm but doesn’t quite manage to knock him down. Bishop follows up by clotheslining Milone out of the ring, drawing a small pop from the audience. Bishop takes a minute to pose for the lightly cheering fans, but pays for his showboating when Colt forces him down with a standing dropkick. Not wanting to give up the advantage Colt follows through with a barrage of stomps, stubbornly following Bishop as he tries to roll away. Through a flurry of blows Bishop slowly manages to make it to his feet, but is promptly brought down once again when Colt plants a vicious roundhouse kick to Bishop’s face. Bishop goes down in a heap, and Colt quickly covers him, 1… 2… and its broken up by Milone! The big man has climbed back into the ring and is ready to repay the early humiliation he suffered. Hoping to repeat his earlier success, Colt lunches himself at Milone, but this time he is caught midair! With a sadistic grin Milone easily lifts the smaller man up, then slams him down in an atomic drop. Colt feebly tries to kick Milone’s shins out from under him after his collision with the mat, but it is clear that the wind has been knocked out of him. Contemptuously Milone slaps his feet away and forces Colt to his feet. Colt struggles to break away from Milone’s grip, but he is easily hoisted up into the air above Milone’s head. Grinning maliciously throws Colt out of the ring and onto the guardrail. Colt lands onto the rail with a sickening thud, and falls to the floor gasping in pain. But back in the ring, Bishop has recovered and surprised with Milone with a schoolboy rollup! 1.. Milone kicks out with ease, throwing Bishop off of him. Both men scramble to their feet, but Bishop is significantly faster and manages to capture Milone’s head before he can force his way up. Without prelude Bishop falls to the mat, driving Milone’s head down in a vicious DDT. The impact is not quite enough to take Milone out. Shaking his head in an attempt to rid himself of the dizziness he is feeling. But Bishop calmly waits for Milone to get to his feet before devastating Milone with the Last Chance. The meeting between Milone’s face and Bishop’s foot has caused Milone to lose two of his teeth. He lies motionless on the mat and Bishop, smelling blood, drags him to the center of the ring. Bishop sinks to his knees, and quickly covers Milone. 1…….. 2…… 2.5….. AND Colt breaks it up! Apparently colt was content to let the two bigger men battle it out while he recovered. He hip tosses Bishop back to the ground as soon as he stands, then follows that with a series of spinning heel kicks, clearly hoping to wear Bishop down with series of blows. After the fifth kick Bishop finally goes down, and Colt races to the turnbuckle. He climbs onto and points to the crowd. The audience has been slowly swelling during the course of the match, and it lets out a roar in recognition of the smaller man. With a flourish Colt jumps off the turnbuckle, intending to finish Bishop with a moonsault….and misses…completely misses Bishop. Bishop is quick to capitalize on Colt’s mistake and literally lifts Colt to his feet. Wihtout hesitation he nails Colt with Redemption, and Colt looks to be done.
