Meltdown 48 Will Rogers Coliseum — Fort Worth, Texas ---- Meltdown #48 (A black screen. No audio. Suddenly, snowy static slowly begins to fade into view, twitching and distorting violently. Electric hissing noises emanate from the background, as the blurred image of a single man slowly begins to fade into the snow. The picture remains nearly unrecognizable, as a robotic voice begins, barely speaking over the static.) Voice- Are… you… ready… for…… Low? (The man’s face does indeed appear to be Low, but the scene quickly blips back to blackness. The GCW logo then flashes on the screen, as the cameras cut to the stage inside the Igloo, where red and blue pyro explodes from the stage! The Pittsburgh fans go wild as Meltdown begins with a tremendous bang, and the cameras begin to soar over the arena. They find signs reading ‘Jhub was screwed!’ and ‘Low is coming!’ The cameras continue to fly as the GCW logo appears alongside ‘Live from Pittsburgh’, as James Bryan begins.) Bryan- Just four nights ago, GCW invaded the Ice Palace for SubZero! And just four nights ago, the gang mentality of the Establishment truly ruled, as they left the night with both the World and United States Championships! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan as always alongside David Yale, and thank you for joining us on the first night of the road to Warpath! Yale- Well, JB, it’s tough to look so far into the future when you just came off such a great night at SubZero, but Warpath is so huge, it’s impossible to ignore! But before we move on, we just have to acknowledge the fact that Jason Dread is STILL the GCW World Heavyweight Champion! Bryan- Well, it took the whole Establishment to put the Jhub away, but they did manage to screw the Jhub out of his title shot! Quite frankly, Dave, the Jhub had the world title won about five times! Just knowing, after what I did to all of you fans, the put downs, the hate…it makes it all worthwhile. (The Fans scream) Bryan- What class! Maybe I’m not on top like Jhub, or Rich Rollins are. Hell, All I’ve ever had in GCW is this tag title around my waist right now. But that doesn’t mean that I’m not one of the best there is, was and will forever be in the GCW. (Fans cheer, and O’Neil walks around the ring a bit.) My time has come and gone, folks. The last chapter in the book of O’Neil has been written. All that’s left is for me to ride off into the sunset. Ride off a winner…Thank you for the memories… (Established plays and from under the MegaTron, President Steven Caldera stands with the Establishment. Yale- Caldera and Khan head up the ramp, as Octavian, Badger, NBK, Rollins, and The Jhub assault the remaining members of the Establishment! Toxic, the US Champion, and the World Champion are getting destroyed in the ring! This isn’t fair, it’s five on three! Bryan- It was five on two a minute ago, and you were loving it! The Jhub promised us a surprise tonight, and somehow he has found a way to unify these five men to destroy the Establishment! Yale- Someone stop this! (The crowd continues to cheer as the five men in the ring stop their assault, and face Caldera and Khan on stage. Caldera yells down at the ring, though Khan restrains him from moving. The Jhub cockily invites Caldera back, as the crowd cheers for more.) Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time! For David Yale, I’m James Bryan, join us for Oblivion! (Khan and Caldera glare back in shock at the ring, as NBK, Octavian, Badger, Rollins, and The Jhub stand tall. The opposition has unified, as Toxic, Stevens, and Dread lie in pain after a five on three mugging. The camera affords us one last shot of the five men in the ring, and then Caldera shouting in frustration and anger, before Meltdown fades to a close……..)
