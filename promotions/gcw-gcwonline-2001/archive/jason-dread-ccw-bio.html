Jason Dread Biography Real Name Seth Cantrell Hometown Southern Tracks, Arizona Birthday August 17th, 1978 Height 5'11 Weight 192 Fed Titles GCW Hard*Core Champion, GCW Television Champion, GCW World Champion (x2) Fed Awards 2001's Most up and coming wrestler. Biography Jason Dread, born Seth Cantrell, was born in Southern Tracks, Arizona on January 23rd. As a small child, he grew up watching the great wrestlers of all time, and frequently mimicked whatever moves he possibly could with any nearby stuffed animal. This was shunned upon by his mother, who after his father died when he was 11, completely restricted Seth from watching any type of wrestling. This did not stop Seth from going forward with his dreams which were to become a professional wrestler! At the age of 19, Seth moved out of his home and to the outskirts of Southern Tracks, one hour away from Phoenix. It was at this time he came unto the knowledge of a former wrestling great of a decade ago who was willing to train any people interested to becoming professional wrestlers. Seth, along with only one other pupil appeared at the doorstep of "The Iceman" Rick Davis, and took him up on the offer to train for free. Because there were only two men to train, everything went by fairly quickly, with both Seth and the man who grew up to become his closest friend, Eric Inez, learning what it took in under nine months time period. Both were found to be incredibly gifted inside the ring, and with the huge size difference between the two, put on a fantastic match for the head of an up and coming Mid-western wrestling organization, clinching them with jobs in their first wrestling company. Six months time passed in this organization, before Seth, dubbing himself "Khristian Khaos" encountered his first real wrestling injury, as his suicidal high flying nature nearly cost him all movement below his neck from a springboard 450 degree splash that went terribly wrong. Three months of recuperation later, Khaos wasn't cutting it with the fans, as the now slower nature of his matches took hold of the fans, sending them into a rally of boos whenever he came to wrestle. He went back to Davis, and slowly he began a new persona. He trained harder, put on more muscle, and soon Jason Dread was born. On the first month back he became an extremely popular star with his new look and attitude, getting over and receiving a push very quickly. He became the federation's World Champion when everything began crashing around him. His best friend, "Bane" as he called himself, broke his neck during a ladder match with a fellow wrestler, and the company was on the verge of bankruptcy. Two weeks after that incident the company filed for bankruptcy. As Jason Dread returned back to Southern Tracks, he was visited by Rick Davis' daughter who told Jason about the malignant cancer Davis had developed in the past two years. Dread quickly went to visit Rick, who was literally lying on his deathbed. The last words spoken to Dread by the Iceman were "Never give up on yourself. I may be going, but my spirit will always be with you." Dread left the room and his mentor and tutor died five minutes later. Davis was an inspiration to Dread, and has returned numerous times in the past to his burial spot to sort things out when seemingly the worst situations are at hand. With no job, one friend, albeit crippled, and slowly running out of money, Dread was being backed against the wall as for what to do with his life. When it seems as he was backed to the edge of the road at the age of 23, he received a phone call from Global Championship Wrestling - and the rest is history. One Hard*Core Championship won on his debut, a TV title, two World Championship runs, and a Hall of Fame induction later and Dread planned on closing his dealings with GCW for good. He has since retired from full time active wrestling choosing to do the very occasional Japan and Independent bookings as well as running a very small but successful training center for those young and willing to learn the sport. Deep down Dread hopes to find the one pupil to personally mentor just as The Iceman did for him. Ring Attire: Jason Dread's jet black hair is cut short, only long enough to where he has a small amount of bangs lying over his forehead. The tips of these bangs are colored a deep crimson. His face is slightly handsome, except for a small, but noticeable scar under his right eye. He chooses not to wear a shirt in the ring, but wears tight taping around both of his arms, near his shoulders to show off his not extremely huge, but toned muscles. His pants are custom made baggy black jeans that on the side, down each leg the word "Dread" is printed in a fiery print, which is laced with printed-on barbed wire. He does not wear any elbow pads, and plain black wrestling boots adorn his feet. Other Feds AWA, $W, UCW, etc. Other Fed Titles Too numerous to mention Strengths His resiliency. Dread is able to take an insane amount of punishment for a smaller wrestler and in one fell swoop pull out a huge desperation counter with a quick follow up or even a superman against an opponent. In addition his left ankle has always been a trouble spot but it's a rare occurance to have someone work over that particular area. Wrestling Style Technical/High Flyer Strategy Outside the ring and on the camera, Dread is a proud man. This can often get him into trouble when around the wrong people and this quality is brought out. In the ring and fights however, Jason Dread becomes an evil and ruthless machine, capable of inflicting the most amount of punishment available in the smallest amount of time possible. He can take anyone apart technically, then spring to the top rope and deliver a completely innovative high flying maneuver. He is also very resourceful, being a near master of in-ring strategy and presence. He knows when he is trapped, and when to throw that low blow or hit that one move which will get him out of any jam. He loves to sneak out of the ring whenever the opportunity arises, grab a foreign object such as a chair, and use it to his advantage whenever he feels the need to. Stooping this low however, is pretty much only employed when he is near a losing situation. Normally though, he will stick to what he knows best and was taught - straight wrestling. Since retiring Dread works primarily as a straight face and will seldom use cheap shots and underhanded tactics unless incredibly overwhelmed or his opponent has resorted to using them first. Despite that flashy moves performed with chairs and other assorted items are still a specialty of his and he will employ those tactics at any time if the match stipulations allow. If they do not Dread will only resort to those tactics as retaliation as well. While playing by the rules he strictly adheres to an "Eye for an eye" Philosophy and will sometimes go out of his way for payback from an unruly foe. Finisher Move No Moment Finisher Description The victim lies on their back on the mat, with Dread standing over top of them. Dread picks up the victim's legs as if he were getting ready for a wishbone legsplit, but he suddenly spins around so his back is facing the opponent, while holding onto the victim's ankles. Dread then steps over so the victim is back on their stomach, and he has the victim's feet/legs locked under his armpits, with their lower body going under his legs, but their upper body and head are still on the mat (essentially a Boston crab of Walls of Jericho, except Dread faces the opposite direction). He then reaches down to the opponent, still lying on their stomach, then grabs their arms and picks them up into the air, suspending them. While suspended, Dread swings the victim back and forth, putting extreme pressure on the back, up through the upper neck of the victim. Finisher Setup Move Tres Flores - Cobra Clutch Bulldog. Can be used in a variety of ways including running, elevated, as a high impact counter manuever. Setup Description Dread locks the victim up in a Cobra Clutch, but then runs forwards a couple of steps and delivers a bulldog, inflicting heavy damage on the neck of the victim. Also used as an alternate finisher. Special Moves Deadshot - Fireman's carry with a push off of the victim into a high angle diving reverse DDT Other Moves - Dread picks the victim up as if he were going for a standard suplex, but then he releases the victim forward, and drops the victim back first across his knee.) - Spinesplitter DDT (Dread grabs the head of the victim in a DDT position, but instead of falling backwards, he goes straight down, leaving the victim still standing, with their spine/upper body bent down to the ground at an odd angle. The body of the victim resembles an upside-down 'V') - Double Underhook Piledriver - Release German Suplex - Double Springboard Hurricanrana (runs at turnbuckle, climbs up and springs off both sets of rope on both sides of the turnbuckle, then flies at the victim and delivers a hurricanrana.) - Russian legsweep as a default counter to a standing move. For a desperation counter Dread will follow this up with a Fujiwara armbar to slow his opponent day or in hope to snag a quick and sudden submission - Rolling Senton (Opponent lies on the mat, Dread runs at the victim, does a forward roll, and as he gets back up from the first roll, does a flip splash onto the victim. Think Rob Van Dam.) - Japanese Arm Drag combination ending off with an inverted suplex. - Running Moonsault off of the 2nd turnbuckle - Reverse neckbreaker on opponent sitting on top turnbuckle - Any other quick/snap moves that can suit taking an opponent down quickly or delivering punishment to a certain focused body part.
