GCW — Card

WorldWide 15, 2 June 2005

2 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The lights dim in the arena, and the Lisbon crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The Spanish crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Knight Is A Bitch” and “Hardy Was Screwed!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “San Antonio, Texas” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: Just four days ago, Global Championship Wrestling returned to Pay-Per-View with Warpath, and in one tremendous night the face of this company was changed! Welcome back, everyone, I’m James Bryan here at ringside in the SBC Center in beautiful San Antonio, Texas, for another edition of WorldWide! And with me this week, replacing my now-departed partner Rich Rollins is another GCW legend, the former Hardcore Legend himself… Pete!

Pete: Hardcore legend? Hey, thanks, JB! What can I say, people are afraid of me. …AAH!! WHAT WAS THAT?!

Bryan: Just a fan, Pete… They’re just a few inches behind us, you know.

Pete: Oh… right. Sorry.

Bryan: Well, Pete, tonight we start down the road towards one of the most exciting events in GCW, Dangerous Games! We’ve got a lot of action heading into Mr. Danger’s very own show, but nothing more exciting than the Dangerous Games themselves! The thirty-man battle royal for the number one contendership!

Pete: I love watching Dangerous Games, JB, as long as it’s from a safe distance. Bodies flying left and right, it’s out of control.

Bryan: But perhaps bigger than that are the ramifications of the night’s main event! In case you missed it, fans, Jason Dread is STILL the GCW World Heavyweight Champion, but after the match his “friend,” the US Champion LeStatt Knight, left him bleeding and broken in the middle of the cage! The match took its toll on Octavian and Dread, but in the end it was LeStatt Knight left standing!

Pete: I couldn’t believe that when I saw it, JB. Anyone who wanted to climb into that cage is crazy.

Bryan: Tonight we’ve got a preview of Dangerous Games as we witness a battle royal for the GCW TV Title, Dimensioner returns to singles action, and Rich Rollins wrestles in his first GCW match in nearly four years!

The show cuts backstage, where a pair of heavy steel doors swing open. From the parking garage comes Mr. Danger, clad in an expensive suit but looking rather down. His face cold and a bit angry, he pushes through the doors and storms past workers who quickly sprint out of his way. He turns the corner slowly, letting out a deep sigh. From behind comes Kevin Jacobs, wearing a pair of black windbreakers and a GCW shirt, and he places a hand on Mr. Danger’s shoulder to stop him. Danger, annoyed, slowly spins around and rolls his eyes, sighing heavily.

Jacobs: Mr. Danger, I just wanted to know about the status of the Praetorian Guard. Luke Hanson was the only member who was a winner last Sunday at Warpath, and Octavian is back in Italy undergoing treatment for injuries suffered in the-

Danger: Please, just stop. This is not news to me. I was there, I saw everything. Octavian didn’t win the World Title, and he’s not here tonight. Alex Blaq came up short against that bastard LeStatt. At least Luke did what he needed to do. But frankly, Kevin Jacobs, as far as I’m concerned, there IS no Praetorian Guard. It was just the last ditch-effort of a fading star like Octavian to maintain that last shred of stardom he had!

The crowd cheers, as Jacobs seems surprised.

Danger: Mr. Jacobs, Octavian asked me to help him because he had this antiquated notion of what it means to be a star in this business. Well, he’s gone, and for all I care he can STAY gone. Frankly, I’m ready to evolve. I’m ready to step into the year 2005. I’m done playing those games. Luke, Alex… to you I say ‘good luck.’ I wish you all the best. But Mr. Jacobs, Dangerous Games is coming up. That’s MY show. And I’m not going to play second fiddle to a washed-up star like Octavian. I’m going to get back to what I do best.

Jacobs: And… what’s that?

Danger: Be myself.

Danger smiles and pushes to his right into a door. The camera steps to the side to watch him go in, but he pauses to look Jacobs up and down with a smirk.

Danger: Thanks for dressing up tonight, by the way.

With that, Danger steps inside and lets the door slam behind him. The crowd explodes in cheers as the name on the door becomes clear… “Steven Caldera.” With that, the show heads back out to ringside.

Bryan: Wow! Mr. Danger didn’t have anything nice to say about Octavian, and it seems like Octavian’s latest group was rather short-lived!

Pete: Sounds like a good career move by Mr. Danger there. Octavian isn’t even around, Danger needs to use his talents where they’ll be appreciated.

Bryan: And what’s he doing in Mr. Caldera’s office? Hopefully Kevin Jacobs can get to the bottom of that.

As the capacity San Antonio crowd continues to rock the arena with their unique brand of enthusiasm, the opening chords to Rage Against the Machine’s “Born of a Broken Man” splits the air. With the post-Warpath afterglow still ringing in their heads, the crowd immediately turns ten shades of hostile as the former two-time GCW World Heavyweight Champion and Hall of Famer, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins, saunters out onto the stage.

Bryan: And we kick things off with a full tank, folks! Rich Rollins is in the house, and he looks… well, he almost looks pleased with himself.

His steps calculated and deliberate, Rich Rollins slowly makes his way to the ring as the bloodthirsty crowd paves his path with startling animosity. His attention sharp and unshakable, The Renegade climbs up the steel steps and slips between the ropes, producing a microphone tucked into the back of his worn blue jeans.

Standing center stage, The Renegade closes his eyes and absorbs the heat radiating from the GCW faithful.

As the tension slowly fades from the audience, Rollins raises the mic to his lips…

“HARDY! HARDY! HARDY! HARDY! HARDY!”

The Renegade pauses, a disturbing grin cracking his eerily calm façade. The sudden chant starts small but quickly gains momentum, engulfing the SBC Center within seconds.

“HARDY! HARDY! HAR…”

Renegade: Michael … <I>Hickenbottom</I> … Hardy …

“HARDY! HARDY! H…”

Renegade: … is finished in the wrestling world. He just doesn’t know it yet.

The chant finally breaks, but only so that the crowd can vehemently assault the ring with a chorus of boos.

Renegade: What we have here folks is a classic case of wasted potential. At Warpath, I witnessed an absolute tragedy take place in this very ring. From that table right there … (<I>he stabs a finger at James Bryan sitting alone at the announcer’s table</I>) … I saw the most pathetic display of athleticism in the form of one very misguided child… the one-and-only Hickenbottom Hardy.

The Renegade raises a hand to cut off the crowd’s bitter retort.

Renegade: Before you allow your ignorance to blind you, let me remind you of something… I was <I>there</I>. I was there at the first episode of Meltdown that kicked GCW into the spotlight, when the final nail was finally slammed on the CWU’s coffin and evolved into something better than we had ever imagined. I was <I>there</I> before “The Establishment” became a household name, before Steven Caldera polluted the World title that I made famous by setting it on Jason Dread’s shoulder.

The crowd responds, and it isn’t very encouraging.

Rich Rollins merely laughs.

Renegade: That’s right, I had completely forgotten… the average Texan attention span is about as reliable as its politicians.

Another layer of savage cat-calls roar in disagreement, but Rollins continues.

Renegade: Did you forget that your precious World Champion used to be just another key on Caldera’s chain? Of course you did… but Jason Dread isn’t the reason I’m here tonight. No, tonight is all about me and the child who one day dreamt he was a professional wrestler…

Gripping the microphone tight, The Renegade bores holes into the camera with his unflinching gaze.

Renegade: … until ‘ol Rich Rollins finally slapped his sorry ass back into reality. Poor Hickenbottom, always the bridesmaid and never the bride. I’m sure you could have made some lucky man a happy customer as a housewife, Hickenbottom. Besides, we all know that it takes a <I>real</I> man to go out and do the <I>real</I> work. (<I>the crowd tries to rise again, but Rollins pushes on</I>) You could have it all, kid. This entire fabrication of reality, this childhood dream we call Global Championship Wrestling, it could have been <I>your</I> dream, Hickenbottom. It could have been your own personal playground, an empty canvas waiting for someone like you to come along and turn it into a masterpiece, but you just couldn’t deliver when the heat was on. And believe me kid, I was there… “Spitfire” flat-out smoked your ass.

Bryan: (<I>snorts</I>) Sure, with the help of one misguided Anarchist Elbow.

Slipping his trademark red bandana off his scalp and letting his long brown hair fall in bundles down his shoulders, The Renegade turns his attention toward the entrance.

Renegade: You were given the opportunity to shine, Hickenbottom. The powers that be granted you the opportunity to finally make good on your one true mistake - your faith in Steven Caldera - by defeating his former protégé on the biggest stage of them all… and you f*ckin’ choked. I did you a favor at Warpath, don’t you see that? I ended your suffering at the hands of Michael Stevens, and tried to end your humiliation as Caldera’s newest whipping boy. I gave you the exit you were looking for, Hickenbottom… I just gave you a choice.

Bryan: What is he talking about? Rollins ruined one of the most important matches of Mike Hardy’s career at Warpath!

Renegade: The choice is simple; in one month, GCW will have its third Dangerous Games battle royal to determine the #1 contender for the World Heavyweight Championship. That’s all fine and dandy… but a battle royal is <I>not</I> a competition. No, a true test of a man’s strength is in a single one-on-one bout, tossing pride and prejudice aside for the chance to finally know… really <I>know</I> … if you have what it takes. Well, Hickenbottom? Do you?

The rabid mass of humanity in attendance grows suddenly quite, waiting for the inevitable.

They don’t have to wait long. Within moments “The Hand That Feeds” blasts into our eardrums, heralding the arrival of Michael Hickenbottom Hardy.

Bryan: What a way to kick off WorldWide! The tension has been building since The Renegade returned last month, and Mike Hardy has heard enough!

With an obvious purpose in his stance, Mike Hardy basks in the warm ovation from the San Antonio crowd as NIN continues to swoon throughout the arena. Still in the ring, Rollins begins a mocking round of applause for the new arrival.

Renegade: What a performance, Hickenbottom. I’ve never seen a more determined - or mediocre - half-assed wrestler in my entire career. What, were you expecting to <I>bore</I> Michael Stevens into submission at Warpath?

Hardy: Rollins… just up. Just. Shut. Up.

“HARDY~! HARDY~! HARDY~! HARDY~!”

Having produced his own microphone, Mike Hardy remains on the stage with an expression of complete frustration sitting behind his eyes.

Hardy: I gave Michael Stevens my heart and soul in this ring, Rollins.

Bryan: Yes you did, Mike.

Hardy: Ever since this company opened I’ve done nothing but give it everything I’ve had. My very being is tied to this business so intimately that nobody can unravel my resolve. Not even you.

The Renegade grins.

Renegade: Finally, the kid shows some backbone. Words aren’t worth the air pumping through your lungs, Hickenbottom… the only measure of success in this world costs more than mere threats, and your track record hasn’t really been all that kind to you, has it?

Hardy: You’re one to talk. Nice work in the last Golden Turnbuckle Tournament, by the way. You lost in what… the first round?

A direct hit. His good humor instantly evaporated, Rich Rollins absently begins to pace back and forth in the ring.

Renegade: It’s not wise to provoke a living legend, kid. I’ve been around the block a time or two more than you have, and you best not forget it.

Hardy: How could I? You’re your own best self-promoter, Rollins… and I’m getting damn sick and tired of listening to the same old song and dance week in and week out. It’s not even worth the “young lion challenges the older lion” analogy, because quite frankly Renegade, your stay in GCW has already worn out its welcome.

Bryan: ‘Atta boy, Hardy! Rollins looks ready to boil a chicken what with the steam coming out his ears!

Renegade: I’m sorry, but was that another thinly-veiled threat?

Hardy: No, Rich Rollins. That was me, Mike Hardy, blatantly telling you to kiss my ass.

The crowd pops like Pete at the buffet on Sundays.

Renegade: All right Hickenbottom, you want a second chance at proving yourself? Fine. You and me, one-on-one at Dangerous Games. I am <I>the most</I> dangerous game to be played, kid… if you want me, all you have to do is say…

Hardy: Yes.

Hardy almost spits the word as he cuts The Renegade off, sending the live crowd into another round of jacked hysterics.

Hardy: I won’t play your games Rollins, but if you wanted a fight then all you had to do was ask.

Renegade: I wasn’t asking, you ignorant ass. In fact, just to make things exciting… why don’t we settle on a little wager right here tonight, since you seem to be so sure of yourself?

Hardy: Depends on the wager.

Renegade: It’s simple. I have some unfinished business to take care of, and his name is The Jhub. After I make him my own personal bitch tonight, I get to decide a stipulation for our little showdown at Dangerous Games. Supposing I lose… you pick the stipulation. What do you say, Hickenbottom?

Hardy considers for a fraction of a second, then responds.

Hardy: You’ve got yourself a deal, Mr. Hall-of-Famer.

And with that, Mike Hardy drops his microphone on the stage and backs up as “The Hand That Feeds” hits once more to a roaring ovation from the crowd. Leaving a fuming Rich Rollins behind, Hardy acknowledges his fans one more time before disappearing into the back.

As his opponent finally leaves, a sick smile slowly snakes across The Renegade’s lips.

Bryan: And what the hell is he so happy about all of a sudden? I think The Renegade just might have an alternate agenda here with Mike Hardy, but I’m not even going to try and second guess Rollins. Let’s head backstage.

One of GCW's newest additions, Bryan Marshall was having a disagreement with a coke machine. In fact, Marshall was pounding his head against the coke machine. Since Marshall was relentlessly denting the coke machine he failed to notice the man approaching him from behind. Leaning in a doorway was Ryan Stephens complete with Armani suit and evil scowl.

Stephens: What did it do to you sugar britches?

Marshall wheeled around. A look of sheer psychosis shot towards Stephens who met his glare with a cold stair of his own. Furious, Marshall pointed at Stephens, veins pulsating from his neck.

Marshall: I'm about to kick your ass Stephens.

He took a step towards Stephens who merely smirked and held his ground.

Stephens: Easy killer. There's plenty of time for that later.

Marshall paused the attack and shrugged at Stephens as if to say, I'm listening.

Stephens: You're quiet. Now that's a change from the beginning of this week.

Before Marshall could rile himself back up Stephens continued.

Stephens: Here's the deal compadre. You and I both know that we're pretty big threats to this here television title. Perhaps we should stay out of one another's way until we're the last two standing or at least approach that match with that idea in mind.

Throughout the week Marshall hadn't been able to get a good read on Stephens, and he certainly liked the idea. He nodded his approval and spoke up fearlessly.

Marshall: So basically I'm getting your assurance that you won't go for me until we're the last two standing if that scenario occurs?

Stephens just nodded calmly.

Marshall: Well I know I can kick your ass one on one so sounds like a good plan to me. One less person I have to worry about sneaking up on me.

Stephens: You don't know me very well.

Stephens smirked.

Stephens: Don't get used to this, it's a one time thing. I don't need your psychotic ass pulling out some ridiculous move that's going to get us both injured and let God forbid, a girl take this match.

Marshall: But once it's you and I, anything goes?

Stephens: If you can get that far kid, I wouldn't want it any other way.

Marshall nodded and smiled. He liked the idea of mauling Stephens' cowardly ass if it came down to just the two of them. Stephens pointed at the coke machine.

Stephens: Don't waste all your energy hot shot.

Stephens walked off with a perplexed look on his face.

Pete: I wouldn't trust Stephens as far as I could throw him. He's betrayed so many people along the way, what makes Marshall think this little agreement will be any different?

Bryan: Unbridled confidence and the fact that he doesn't know much about Stephens. Hell GCW doesn't know that much about Stephens.

Pete: I have a feeling we'll find out later tonight J.B.

The show returns and finds a thick, flowing head of hair in view. As the camera zooms out, the face and body of the GCW United States Champion LeStatt Knight comes into full view. The crowd in the arena explodes in boos, crying out against the treacherous Knight. Knight is simply smiling to himself, seated in a quiet corner of the hallways, until a voice calls to him from across the way.

“LeStatt! May I have a word?”

Kevin Jacobs rushes to the side of the U.S. Champ a bit out of breath. Knight pushes some of the loose strands of hair from his sight and looks down at Kevin with a slight smirk on his face. Knight licks his lips and raises an eyebrow at the same time crossing his arms over his chest.

LeStatt: Ask what you will Kevin.

Jacobs: First of all I will say congratulations to you on your United States Title defense as it was a grueling battle between you and Alex Blaq, do you have any words for the young superstar?

Knight chuckles then waves his hand.

LeStatt: Grueling you say Kevin? No that match was a joke. I gave that boy every opportunity to put me away and he couldn’t get the job done. I pulled off a total of what? ten offensive moves and put that pathetic dog to sleep. Alex was never in my league and he never deserved to be in that match … but I guess it is the GCW way to put over useless hacks such as Blaq.

Jacobs: Well I thought he…

LeStatt: And who the hell cares what you THOUGHT Kevin? You only thought Alex gave an impressive performance because I made him look good. Anyone can suplex a man, body slam or even piledrive a man … but to actually DEFEAT a CHAMPION, well it takes far more. Alex Blaq now knows his place and it sure as hell isn’t at the top with the likes of me. He’ll be lucky to one day have his name in the same sentence as Capo and the Television title.

Now Kevin my time is precious so why not stop jerking me around? You know what you wish to know … it’s what the WORLD wishes to know. It’s that oh so famous question after a night such as the one we just witnessed. It’s a word that is very short but very powerful … and in my case VERY damn annoying.

WHY?

Why did I interfere in the World Title match? Why did I help Jason Dread keep his title only to shove it down his throat moments later? WHY did I betray my … ( Knight’s smile widens ) “FRIEND”. That is really what you wish to know isn’t it Jacobs?

Jacobs: Yes Knight … your actions were not expected, why did you do that?

LeStatt: You, Dread, Octavian, Caldera, the fans … you all are now hanging on my words. You are awaiting me to spew out some long and worn out speech about why I did what I did as if I had been conspiring to pull this off for months right? I must hate Jason Dread for some unknown reason? Maybe I did it to spite the fans? Come on I MUST have a reason right? Someone who supposedly had it all, respect, the adoration of the fans, and the most successful Championship reign in GCW history MUST have some sort of a reason for throwing it all away! Well the answer is …

Knight froze just as Jacobs microphone got right up against his lips. Jacobs was indeed hanging on every word that was coming from Knight’s mouth, just as LeStatt had said he would. It seemed to be what gave Knight his will. Knight’s expression went blank again and his eyes shimmered.

LeStatt: You’ll find out soon enough…

Knight then walks passed Jacobs who seemed shell shocked. Kevin doesn’t move nor turn his stare from the spot that Knight was just standing. Jacobs didn’t move until knight was around the corner and out of sight.

Jacobs: Son of a …

TV Title Battle Royal

The shot returns to find newcomers Ashton Hardcastle and “Psychotic” Bryan Marshall in the ring. The bell sounds, and the match begins! Hardcastle and Marshall tie up, and the slightly bigger Marshall powers Hardcastle back into the turnbuckle. Marshall nails Hardcastle with a sharp knife-edge chop, but Hardcastle fires one back of his own. Marshall stops Hardcastle with a stiff headbutt and snapmares him out of the corner. Marshall hops onto the turnbuckle and dives off with a legdrop towards the back of Hardcastle’s head, but the Teen Heart Throb moves out of the way and Marshall falls hard to the mat.

The buzzer sounds, and out comes Kyle Niker! The crowd bursts into a mixed reaction as Niker slides into the ring, leaping into the air in celebration. Niker moves in behind Hardcastle and sends him down with a stiff axehandle, then nails Marshall with a hard right hand. Niker pulls Hardcastle to his feet and sends him into the ropes, but Hardcastle bounces back with a perfect spinning heel kick! Hardcastle turns around, only to be driven down by a big lariat from Marshall.

Again the buzzer sounds, and in comes Erik VanLorne! The crowd is split on the tag champion as he hits the ring and goes right after Marshall. VanLorne and Marshall exchange hard rights, and VanLorne takes over. VanLorne hooks Marshall up and nails a hard vertical suplex, but Niker immediately mounts VanLorne and begins pounding away with a flurry of right hands. Hardcastle flies in and dropkicks Niker in the head, sending him right back to the mat.

Hardcastle gets to his feet and poses for the crowd as the buzzer sounds again. The massive form of Dimensioner appears on stage, and the older crowd cheers the familiar face. Dimensioner hits the ring and stalks Hardcastle into the corner. Hardcastle sticks a thumb in Dimensioner’s eye, backing the big man off. Hardcastle hops onto the second rope and takes off, but Dimensioner turns him right into a massive powerbomb! The crowd cheers until VanLorne slaps on a sleeper from behind. Dimensioner reaches back and snaps VanLorne over his shoulder, dropping a big fist into his face.

The buzzer fills the arena once again, and out comes Jonathan Borealis! The crowd explodes in a surprisingly loud chorus of cheers! Borealis smiles and slides into the ring, only to catch a big boot from Dimensioner! Dimensioner pulls Borealis back to his feet and whips him into the ropes, but Borealis hooks the top rope and stops. Dimensioner charges, and Borealis drops a shoulder, tossing Dimensioner high over the top rope! The crowd explodes but Dimensioner lands with one foot on the apron, barely clinging to the top rope! Borealis immediately springs off the middle rope and nails Dimensioner in the head with a vicious sidekick, sending the big man off the apron to the floor! DIMENSIONER IS ELIMINATED.

The buzzer sounds, and out comes the second half of the tag champions, Eva VanLorne! The crowd explodes in cheers as Eva, scantily clad as always, blows a kiss to the fans and slides into the ring. Borealis pauses, unsure of how to approach her, but is cut off by a wild headbutt from Marshall. Marshall shoves Borealis into the corner and begins laying in a series of headbutts to the skull, finally knocking Borealis to the mat! Marshall rubs his head and turns to Eva, who is waiting with a shot to the head! Marshall looks down and delivers a headbutt right to Eva’s chest, prompting a raucous cheer from the fans. Eva falls on her ass in shock, but Erik cuts Marshall down from behind with a forearm. Niker is in trouble in the far corner with Hardcastle, and the buzzer sounds again!

Connor Pearce charges down the ramp to the applause of the crowd, and he powers Hardcastle away from his partner. Pearce nails Hardcastle with two hard shots to the gut, then calls for Niker’s help. Together, Allied Chaos hooks Hardcastle up and suplexes him right over the top rope! Hardcastle somehow manages to land on the apron, then slings himself back over with a double cross body, taking out both Allied Chaos members. EVL is double teaming Marshall, while Borealis pulls himself up in the corner.

The buzzer signals the entrance of the next wrestler, Ryan Stephens! The crowd explodes in boos as Stephens smiles confidently and climbs into the ring. Stephens waits in the corner, observing the action, before finally grabbing Eva from behind and throwing her hard to the mat. Erik gets to his feet only to catch a hard snap suplex from Stephens. Hardcastle, putting the boots to Allied Chaos, doesn’t see Stephens as he sneaks in from behind, grabs him by the belt and tosses him over the top rope! ASHTON HARDCASTLE IS ELIMINATED. Stephens smiles to the crowd, soaking in the boos. He turns back to see Bryan Marshall back on his feet, and the two stare each other down.

The buzzer sounds again, and both men turn to the stage. The crowd explodes in the biggest chorus of boos yet as Capo appears on stage! Wearing a pair of trademark shades and the TV title belt around his waist, Capo sneers at the men in the ring. He finally reaches the ring and climbs in, but refuses to remove the belt! Marshall charges in as Stephens backs off, and Marshall and Capo exchange blows. Allied Chaos together begin to recover, pulling themselves up the ropes. Borealis explodes out of the corner and nails them with a double clothesline, sending both of them over the top rope! KYLE NIKER AND CONNOR PEARCE ARE ELIMINATED. The crowd cheers Borealis on as he locks eyes with Stephens. Stephens moves in, but EVL double team him from behind! The crowd cheers as Erik props Stephens up, and Eva begins wailing away!

Capo manages to drive Marshall down to the mat, only to get hit with a sharp sidekick to the gut from Borealis! Borealis begins laying in hard kicks from all directions, dissecting the former TV champion! Capo drops to a knee, both hands protecting the TV title belt still around his waist. Borealis lines him up for the knockout blow, but Bryan Marshall nails him with a low blow! The crowd boos as Marshall rises again, pulling Capo back to his feet.

Capo stops Marshall with a knee to the gut, then wrenches Marshall over the top rope. Marshall lands on the apron and instantly rolls back into the ring. Capo moves in behind Erik and drags him off of Stephens, nailing him with a DDT! Eva shoves Stephens away and heads for Capo, staring down the man with the belt. The crowd cheers Eva on as he gets in her ready stance, and Capo simply chuckles. Borealis sneaks in from behind and pulls the TV title belt off of Capo’s waist, prompting the shades salesman to spin around! Eva promptly drops to a knee and delivers a cruel low blow to Capo, and Borealis promptly takes Capo by the head and tosses him over the top rope! CAPO IS ELIMINATED!

The crowd explodes in cheers as Capo hits the mats, rolling around in pain. Borealis drops the title belt out of the ring, turning to Eva. Eva bats her eyelashes, causing Borealis to hesitate just long enough for Eva to nail him with a big right hand! The crowd oohs at the sound of the impact, and Eva continues wailing on Borealis. Borealis slumps down to the mat, and Eva winks at the crowd! Stephens suddenly rises behind her and gives her a shove, sending her over the top rope to the floor! EVA IS ELIMINATED. The crowd explodes in boos as Stephens arrogantly blows her a kiss and turns back into the ring.

Marshall is back on his feet, and he blows by Stephens to go after Erik. Erik nails Marshall with an elbow to the gut, but Marshall shrugs it off and powers Erik into the ropes. Marshall attempts to lift Erik over the ropes, but VanLorne hooks on a front facelock and holds on for dear life. Marshall loses his grip and drops Erik, and Erik promptly hoists Marshall up to toss him! Stephens immediately moves in, lifting up Erik and dumping both men over the top! ERIK VANLORNE AND BRYAN MARSHALL ARE ELIMINATED.

Stephens slaps his hands together as if having taken out the trash, laughing to himself heartily. He then turns, realizing Borealis is still in the ring! Stephens pulls Borealis to his feet and nails him with a stiff right, jawing with the crowd in between shots. Stephens finally whips Borealis into the ropes, but Borealis bounces back with a high knee to the face! Stephens staggers back into the ropes and Borealis charges! Borealis lands a high leg clothesline, sending both men over the top! The crowd leaps to its feet, watching as both men land on the apron and hold on!

The fans sigh as Borealis and Stephens pull themselves back into the ring and to their feet. Borealis nails a short European uppercut, followed by a hard Russian legsweep. Borealis immediately pulls Stephens back to his feet and whips him into the ropes, but Stephens counters. Borealis bounces right into a huge tilt-a-whirl backbreaker called Money in the Bank! Stephens nails the move to the chagrin of the crowd, and he snaps back up with a cocky grin. Stephens pulls Borealis up and moves him to the ropes, shoving him over the top!

Borealis holds on, but Stephens keeps the pressure on. Suddenly, Bryan Marshall appears back on the apron with the TV title belt in hand! With a crazy look in his eye, he charges down the apron and nails Stephens with a shot to the side of the head! Stephens collapses to the mat, and the crowd erupts in cheers! The officials at ringside swarm Marshall and take the belt, forcing Marshall away from ringside!

Borealis clutches his back in pain as both men stagger to their feet! Borealis suddenly charges and hits the ropes, coming in with a huge superkick! He beat Alex Blaq because he got mad, not because of wanting the hardcore title. That was just a nice bonus. And then, the twins. Erik and Eva, the brother and the hot chick, are the best tag team in a singles-focused company, and that still means something. Namely, means they've got two more pretty gold belts to add to the line-up. Namely, means I'm well enough off to draw a line in the sand.

He looks desperately around for sand to draw a line in and, failing that, just continues with his rant.

Jordan: Namely, means Anarch's the place to be for all those guys trying to catch a break and change the face of things around here. The lackluster need not apply, since you're the ones we're set to take over from. It's not a threat, it's just simple evolution. The tired--the Octavians and the Stevenses and the Dreads and the Knights--have all had their time in the sun, and are more than ready for their time out to pasture. No reason to ignore the inevitable, so you crazy guys ready to takeover have yourself a new home--in the real future of GCW, the one that won't use asinine catchphrases or spend a half-hour to say one sentence that matters.

Jordan raises the mic high, Anarch's agents standing behind him with the full amount of respect the three lower-tier champions can expect to get. The fans salt-and-pepper their reaction again, but keep it loud. That's what matters.

Jordan: I love you guys!

Lexicon’s music hits again, and the mixed reaction resumes. As the Anarch crew exits the ring, the shot returns to James Bryan and Pete at ringside.

Bryan: Well, there certainly seems to be a new power rising in GCW, and these guys might be spearheading it, Pete!

Pete: Trey Tremendous and the rest have certainly taken GCW by storm, and it might not be long before they really take over. Trey is taking on LeStatt next week!

Bryan: Well, fans, we’re almost ready for the evening’s main event, which is a rematch almost four years in the making. We’re going to take you back to NC-17 2001, for a trip back to ACWF III and the last GCW Match of Rich “The Renegade” Rollins.

The GCW logo flashes across the screen, before heading to a taped shot of The Jhub and Rich Rollins. The text “NC-17 2001 remains at the bottom of the screen, where The Jhub and Rollins lie in the bed of a truck in a full parking lot, both obviously feeling the effects of a long match.

Bryan- Rollins slowly rolls out of the truck, which has taken a lot of damage itself! The Renegade takes a moment to catch his breath, and reaches back in for the Jhub! He pulls the Jhub out, picks him up, and places him over his shoulder! Where are they going?

Yale- Rollins is carrying the Jhub, and I think he’s headed back to the chained ring area! Rollins carries the Jhub through the rows and rows of parked cars, and finally hits the clearing! Rollins continues carrying the Jhub, until they finally get back to the chains!

Bryan- Rollins sets the Jhub down in the chained area, and picks up a piece of that broken chain! The Jhub is finally starting to get up, but Rollins is wrapping that chain around his fist! The Jhub gets back to his feet, and staggers around to face the Renegade!

Yale- WHAM! Rollins sends the Jhub down with a chained fist! What a shot! And look, the Jhub is busted wide open! Man, he’s bleeding badly! And he may be out! Rollins now begins to look around the area… what’s he looking for?

Bryan- Rollins walks back out the chained area, out to the wall! He reaches back behind a stack of steel chairs… he’s got a table! The Renegade has a table! Rollins pulls the table back to the Jhub, and sets it down! What’s he doing with it?

Yale- Rollins climbs back through the chains, and drags the table to the forklift! I get it! Rollins sets the table down over the forks, and heads back for the Jhub! He rolls the Jhub over onto the table with a few boots to the head, and climbs on himself!

Bryan- Rollins has made himself a platform on that forklift with the table, and both men are on! Rollins now reaches down, and presses a button in the forklift! And there they go! The forks are raising both men higher and higher over the pavement! This doesn’t look good for the Jhub!

Yale- They finally stop, but they must be fifteen feet above the pavement! Rollins now lifts the Jhub up, and sets him up… Oh, no, he’s not going to…

Bryan- Rollins has the Jhub set up for a powerbomb! He’s going to try to powerbomb the Jhub fifteen feet to the pavement! He could kill him! Don’t do it, Renegade! Don’t do it!

Yale- Rollins tries to lift the Jhub, but the Jhub holds on! He tries again, but he still can’t get him up!

Bryan- Wait, the Jhub pulls the Renegade’s legs out from under him, and Rollins hits hard! The Jhub steps through, and turns him over…

Yale- SHARPSHOOTER! The Jhub, bloodied and beaten, has the Sharpshooter on Rollins fifteen feet above the pavement! The referee looks on from below, as Rollins screams in pain!

(The Jhub yells out as he musters all his strength to rear back. Blood dripping from his face, the Jhub pulls as hard as he can, causing Rollins to scream out. With nowhere to go, no ropes to save him and no way to crawl, Rollins finally gives in to the pain, and taps out!)

Bryan- THAT’S IT! Rollins tapped out! Rollins tapped out! The Jhub has won ACWF Three!!

(The crowd inside goes crazy. My Own Summer plays, as the referee presses yet another button on the forklift. The forks begin to lower, bringing the Jhub and Rollins down. The Jhub releases the Renegade, and waits for the forks to lower. He finally hops off, collapsing to the cool pavement below.)

Andrews- Here is your winner, the Jhub!

The flashback shows several shots of The Jhub celebrating, and the last image GCW had seen of Rollins, where the Renegade is loaded onto a stretcher and carted away to the hospital, not to return until May of 2005….

Bryan: I remember that match very well, Pete, and it was quite a spectacle. These two men put each other through hell, but The Jhub finished the Renegade off, and Rollins was out of GCW until just a few weeks ago.

Pete: And I shudder to think what this match holds for The Jhub. Rollins looks a little mad about that, JB, and he’s looking for payback here in this match!

The Jhub vs. Rich “The Renegade” Rollins

“My Own Summer” hits overhead, and the crowd explodes in cheers! The Jhub struts out on stage, limping slightly but in good spirits. The Jhub slaps hands with the crowd as he approaches the ring, apparently favoring his back following his powerbomb into the dumpster on Sunday. He looks to be in decent shape as he steps onto the turnbuckle and poses for the crowd, which flashes their cameras over him furiously. He steps into the ring and backs into the corner, waiting anxiously for his opponent.

“Born Of a Broken Man” hits, and the arena plunges into boos! Rich Rollins, clad in worn blue jeans and his trademark red bandana, slowly saunters on stage with a cocky smile across his face. Licking his lips in anticipation for the match, Rollins savors his first walk to the ring in years, eyeing The Jhub the entire way. The boos remain relentless as Rollins hops onto the apron and slowly climbs inside.

Referee Josh Briggs checks The Jhub for weapons, then crosses the ring to check Rollins. Rollins scoffs and warns Briggs off, but finally relents and plays along. Satisfied, Briggs glances at each man. The Jhub and Rollins remain in their corners, each confident and anxious for the match to begin. Briggs finally calls for the bell, and the crowd explodes in cheers!

Rollins and The Jhub stare each other down, each with a confident smile on their faces. They slowly meet in the middle of the ring, The Jhub jawing furiously. Rollins simply smiles and delivers a hard shove, backing The Jhub into his corner. The crowd boos as The Jhub simply laughs, moves back to the center and shoves Rollins. Rollins falls back into his corner, and The Jhub simply opens his arms in a welcoming fashion. Rollins comes back in again, this time delivering a sharp slap across The Jhub’s face!

The crowd boos heavily as The Jhub staggers in pain, but suddenly jerks up and nails Rollins with a hard right, taking the Renegade right off his feet! Rollins springs right back up but finds himself caught in a flurry of stiff rights from The Jhub. The Jhub backs Rollins into his corner and whips him hard to the other side, where Rollins hits the turnbuckle hard and falls to the mat! Rollins pulls himself back up again, but The Jhub whips him hard into the ropes. Rollins bounces back with a flying forearm, but The Jhub has it well scouted and ducks, sending Rollins flying through the ropes to the floor!

The crowd explodes as The Jhub hops onto the turnbuckle with surprising intensity, fired up as he motions to the crowd. Rollins slowly sits up, checking his lip for blood. With a confident grin he smoothly rolls back into the ring, watching The Jhub like a hawk. The Jhub gives him room to enter, but moves in the moment he hits his feet. Rollins blocks a right hand and steps to the side, sending The Jhub headfirst into the turnbuckle. Rollins begins laying in heavy boots to the gut in the corner, before sending The Jhub into the ropes. The Jhub bounces back and Rollins lowers his head, sending The Jhub over the top with a back body drop to the floor!

The crowd explodes in boos as Rollins mounts the turnbuckle and motions to the crowd, mocking The Jhub. The Jhub angrily pulls himself up and slides back into the ring, meeting Rollins in the corner. The Jhub hits Rollins with a right hand that echoes throughout the arena, shocking the Renegade. The Jhub pulls Rollins out of the corner and throws him to the mat, mounting him with hard right hands. Briggs attempts to pull The Jhub away, but The Jhub simply shoves him off. Rollins manages to counter and roll The Jhub over, but both spill through the ropes to the floor.

Both men get to their feet, and The Jhub immediately whips Rollins hard into the steel steps! Rollins bounces over top and lands hard on the floor, while the crowd begins to come unglued. The Jhub kicks the steps out of the way and moves in on Rollins, but Rollins kicks out and catches The Jhub in the midsection. Rollins manages to get to his feet and charges with a clothesline, sending The Jhub right into the front row!

Fan scatter as Rollins hops back onto the apron, breaking Briggs’ count. Rollins grabs him by the collar and shouts at him, warning him not to end the match prematurely. He then shoves Briggs away and looks back, seeing The Jhub pulling himself up. Rollins springboards off the second rope and leaps into the stands, nailing The Jhub with a huge moonsault! The crowd explodes in cheers, chanting “Holy Shit! Holy Shit!” Both men lie reeling on the concrete floor, while security frantically tries to keep the San Antonio fans at bay.

Rollins staggers to his feet and pulls The Jhub back up, nailing him with a right hand that sends him back over the guardrail. Rollins steps onto the corner of the guardrail, watching as The Jhub tries to pull himself up. Rollins takes off from his perch and nails The Jhub with a knee to the face, then stands and swipes a beer away from a fan who gets too close. The arena continues to rock and roll as Rollins pulls The Jhub to his feet and rolls him back into the ring.

The Jhub manages to get to his feet as Rollins slides inside. The Jhub backs into the corner and Rollins moves in, but The Jhub hits him with a mule kick to the face. Rollins staggers back and The Jhub charges, knocking him down with a lariat. The crowd bursts out in cheers again, and The Jhub immediately slaps on a rear chinlock. Rollins struggles and finally pushes up to his feet, hitting The Jhub with a pair of sharp elbows to the gut. Rollins shoves The Jhub off into the ropes, but The Jhub comes back in with a hard shoulder block, sending Rollins to the mat!

The Jhub hits the ropes but Rollins stays down, and The Jhub leaps over him. Rollins suddenly nips up, managing to catch The Jhub with a big hip toss! The Jhub rolls back to his feet and bounces into the ropes, not missing a step. Rollins leapfrogs The Jhub and both men hit the ropes, but this time Rollins nails the flying forearm! Rollins nips up again, waiting for The Jhub to rise. Rollins sends him right back down with a sharp standing dropkick, then applies a dragon sleeper!

The Jhub reaches out for the bottom rope with his foot, but Rollins drags him into the middle of the ring. The Jhub pounds the mat with his fist in frustration, struggling to move. The Jhub arches his back up, forcing Rollins to his feet, before suddenly slipping around, taking Rollins over into a northern lights suplex! The Jhub bridges into a cover! 1… 2… Rollins manages to slip out.

Both men get to their feet, and The Jhub takes Rollins down with an arm drag, holding him in an armbar. Rollins wastes no time in turning over and pushing himself to his feet. Rollins reaches across and nails The Jhub with a stiff forearm, but The Jhub wrenches his arm and sends him back to a knee. Rollins screams out in pain before rolling forward, nipping up, and reversing the armbar! The Jhub turns into it and punches Rollins right between the eyes, breaking the hold instantly. Rollins backs into the ropes, and The Jhub delivers and Irish whip.

Rollins bounces back and The Jhub drags him into a powerslam, hooking the leg! 1… 2… Rollins kicks out again. The Jhub pulls Rollins back up and nails a perfect side backbreaker, hooking the leg again! 1… 2… Another kickout. The Jhub drags Rollins to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle with authority, and Rollins flips over, hitting the turnbuckle with his back and landing seated on the top, facing the crowd! The Jhub charges in and climbs behind Rollins, pulling him to the top rope!

The Jhub hooks Rollins up and pulls him off the top rope into a massive back suplex! But Rollins changes direction in mid-air and turns into a cross body block, driving The Jhub into the mat! The fans erupt in shock as Rollins rolls off, recovering in the corner. Rollins pulls himself up and climbs the turnbuckle, measuring The Jhub! The crowd stands in unison, knowing what to expect next! Rollins takes off with a towering Anarchist Elbow, but The Jhub rolls out of the way! Rollins lands hard on the mat and instantly clutches his elbow, and The Jhub rises to take advantage!

The Jhub painfully drags Rollins to the corner and slides out of the ring, then pulls Rollins spread-eagled around the steel post! The crowd winces in pain as Rollins’ eyes cross. The Jhub takes the Renegade by the leg and sends his knee into the post, and Rollins tries to pull away. The Jhub reaches in and pulls him back to the turnbuckle, forcing his knee into the post again. The Jhub turns and spots the dislodged steel steps to his right, and picks up the top section! The Jhub backs up and gets a running start, driving the steps into Rollins’ knee! The Renegade screams out in pain as the crowd continues its raucous support for The Jhub. The Jhub then folds Rollins’ leg up, leaps into the air, and applies the figure four around the ringpost!

Rollins shoots up and screams out in pain, reaching through the ropes in a vain attempt to break the hold. Briggs leaps out of the ring and begs The Jhub to release Rollins, but The Jhub simply ignores him. Rollins bites down on the middle rope in an attempt to shut out the pain, and the crowd cheers The Jhub on as he continues to tear apart the Renegade’s knee!

The Jhub finally releases the hold and Rollins immediately drags himself out of the corner, clutching his knee in pain in the center of the ring. The Jhub shoves Briggs out of the way and pushes the timekeeper out of his seat, snatching up his steel chair! The crowd goes crazy as The Jhub slides into the ring with the chair, stalking Rollins into the corner! Rollins pulls himself up, not putting any pressure on his left leg. The Jhub raises the chair up, but Rollins suddenly shoots out of the corner, spearing The Jhub to the mat!

The fans boo wildly as Rollins hits The Jhub with three sharp rights to the skull, then rolls away to drag himself back up. Still favoring his left leg, Rollins waits in the corner with a furious scowl for The Jhub to get back to his feet. The Jhub stands and turns right into a superkick from Rollins! The crowd explodes as The Jhub hits the mat, but Rollins curls into the fetal position, clutching his wounded leg. Rollins grinds his teeth and finally rolls over to cover The Jhub! 1… 2… Kickout!! The crowd explodes with applause as The Jhub shoots his shoulder off the mat, and Rollins beats the mat in frustration.

Rollins pulls himself back up, glaring down at the fallen Jhub. Rollins steps out of the ring onto the apron, measuring The Jhub. Rollins pulls back on the top rope and slingshots himself into a legdrop over The Jhub’s neck! Rollins pushes The Jhub back to the mat, hooking his leg angrily! 1… 2… Kickout! Rollins rolls back into a seated position, face blank in disbelief. Rollins finally smiles and glances over at the steel chair that The Jhub brought into the ring! Rollins limps to it and snatches it off the mat, turning right back into a protesting Josh Briggs. Rollins shoves Briggs out of the way and stalks The Jhub, who is pulling himself up the ropes!

Rollins bangs the chair on the mat and holds it at the ready, and The Jhub staggers around! The Jhub suddenly steps right into Rollins and plants him with a vicious Downward Spiral! The fans explode as both men lie in pain on the mat, with Rollins positioned in the center of the ring! The Jhub slowly pulls himself up and stares down at Rollins, before positioning himself at Rollins’ legs. The crowd explodes as The Jhub sets Rollins up for a Sharpshooter!

The Jhub tries to turn Rollins over, but Rollins reaches up and drags The Jhub into a small package! 1… 2… NO! The Jhub narrowly escapes, and both roll into opposite corners. The crowd, exhausted from the excitement, remains standing in awe. Rollins staggers to his feet as The Jhub stumbles towards him. Rollins spins around and delivers a second huge superkick!

The Jhub somehow catches Rollins’ leg and snaps him over into a leg drag, then steps through and turns him right into the Sharpshooter!!! The crowd explodes, leaping in the stands as Rollins screams out in pain. Rollins frantically scrambles towards the ropes, reaching desperately for an exit. Rollins comes within inches of the bottom rope, before The Jhub stands and pulls Rollins into the center of the ring! Rollins seizes the extra slack, pushing back with his arms and then shooting out with his legs, forcing The Jhub through the ropes and back out to the floor!

Rollins slowly pulls himself back up as The Jhub gets back to his feet. The Jhub reaches in and grabs the steel chair, hopping onto the apron and climbing onto the turnbuckle! The Jhub stands perched on the turnbuckle, waiting for Rollins to stand! Rollins finally staggers into the middle of the ring, and The Jhub takes off! Rollins springs into the air, countering with a huge superkick that sends the chair right into The Jhub’s face! The Jhub lands hard on the mat to the chaos coming from the crowd.

Both men lie in pain on the ring, while Briggs frantically tosses the chair out of the ring. Rollins slowly pulls himself onto the apron and begins dragging himself up the turnbuckle, while The Jhub remains unconscious on the mat. Rollins slowly steps onto the top turnbuckle, but The Jhub begins to move! Rollins waits for The Jhub to rise, then takes off! Rollins lands on The Jhub’s shoulders, dropping back into a hurricanrana! But The Jhub remains standing, pulling Rollins back onto his shoulders! Rollins begins firing frantic right hands to The Jhub’s skull, but The Jhub lifts him up for a huge powerbomb! Rollins slips his legs out and drops down, driving The Jhub into the mat with an enormous DDT!!!!

The crowd gasps at the counter, while both men remain motionless on the mat. Rollins suddenly lifts his legs up and nips back to his feet, staring out at the crowd with intensity! The fans cry out in shock, still on their feet as Rollins turns back to The Jhub. The Jhub slowly pulls himself back up, but Rollins nails him with an overhead belly to belly suplex! Rollins nips up again, waiting for The Jhub to rise! The Jhub slowly pulls himself back up, and Rollins moves back in!

Rollins swings a big right, but The Jhub ducks it and hooks him for the Downward Spiral! The crowd erupts, but Rollins reaches around and hooks his head, springboards off the second rope, and twists around into a scintillating reverse neckbreaker! Rollins rises to a knee, staring down at The Jhub as he lies in the middle of the ring. Rollins then looks up at the turnbuckle, nodding his head in readiness. He steps onto the apron and leaps onto the turnbuckle with ease, measuring The Jhub!

Rollins stands with perfect balance on the top rope, raising his arms to the air. He takes off, nailing the Anarchist Elbow with deadly precision! The fans fall into their seats soberly, as Rollins hooks the leg! 1… 2… 3!!! The Jhub doesn’t move an inch as “Born of a Broken Man” cues up once again! Rollins rises and Briggs raises his arm, quickly leaving in disgust.

Winner: Rich “The Renegade” Rollins

Bryan: HE DID IT! Rich Rollins finally got his revenge on The Jhub, ending what is arguably the longest-running rivalry in GCW history!

Rollins smiles at the crowd, leaning against the turnbuckle for support. He chuckles and calls for a microphone, in a good mood following his victory.

Bryan: And poor Mike Hardy… according to their agreement, the ball is in The Renegade’s court now, as he gets to pick the stipulation for their showdown at Dangerous Games!

Snatching a tossed microphone out of midair, Rollins takes a minute to catch his breath as the crowd begins to grow restless. An aura of anticipation settles over the SBC Center as The Renegade slowly grins, soaking it up.

Renegade: Hickenbottom…

The fans eagerly tone down.

Renegade: … they used to call me the Iron Man.

Bryan: Oh my god…

Renegade: As a great wrestler once said, “To <I>be</I> the man, you’ve got to <I>beat</I> the man.” Well kid, you’re gonna have to beat the man at his own game… in a sixty-minute Iron Man Match!

Bryan: OH MY GOD~!!

Pete: That’s a lot of minutes!

Renegade: Hopes and dreams won’t win you any World titles Hickenbottom, and I’ll keep beating it into your head until it finally sticks. I promise you <I>that</I>.

Bryan: First it was Vincent Reed, then it was Khan and Octavian… nobody has ever beaten Rich Rollins in an Iron Man setting! The deck just got stacked, and it’s not looking good for Mike Hardy!

The Renegade lets the microphone drop to the canvas as “Born of a Broken Man” screams through the San Antonio arena. Rolling out to ringside, Rollins flashes the rowdy Texan assembly one final defiant sneer as he marches back up the ramp and through the curtain………
