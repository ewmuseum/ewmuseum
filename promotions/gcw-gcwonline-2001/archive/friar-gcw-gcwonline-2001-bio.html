Friar Biography Real Name Leon Christmas Nicknames the Hellhound Hometown Boulder, CO Birthday 6 August 1977 Height 6' Weight 224 lbs. Biography Before I wore this mask, I was a real man. I had dreams, goals, family, and friends. My life was what you would call impressive and average, all the same. It was beautiful. But in 1997, right around my 20th birthday, I was given an opportunity to change the world. Not by joining the Peace Corps, Colorado State Police, or even the US Army. Late one September night I met a man who unexpectedly became my Teacher. Through lessons and conversations I became his Student in the fight of Good against Bad. The battle between Light and Dark. So here I stand. Twelve years removed from the regular world. Twelve years spent training with the Teacher. Twelve years hiding and fighting amongst caves in the Himalayas, make-shift rings in Tanzania, and underground parking garages up and down the Rocky Mountain Belt - for what? To ensure that my face is known everywhere as the man who can stop bad things from happening to good people. Not the face that belongs to Leon Christmas. Friar. The face of Good. The face of Light. The face of GCW. Strengths Mentally resilient, physically tough, unexpectedly strong, nimble, inspired Weaknesses size, professional experience, consumed by righting the wrongs Wrestling Style High-flyer / Submission Strategy Friar uses his body as a weapon for Good. He will stop at nothing to keep the Light on the favored side of the battle. In doing this, he finds himself sometimes looked down on or shunned for his violent yet just actions. He does the things nobody wants to because it is not his choice to make. It it his duty. General Strategy: -Hero type persona; willing to bleed to save GCW from falling into the Darkness -plants offenders early and often with the Gift DDT (see moveset). this is his Stunner - uses it as a statement and in backstage areas, etc. In-Ring Strategy: -uses the Gift as a finisher often -Great athlete but lack of competition at the highest level often leaves openings for opponents Finisher Move Tibetan Necklace Finisher Description Inverted Chickenwing Crossface Finisher Setup Move The Gift Setup Description Jump-Swinging DDT performed with many variations Special Moves WRECK-LESS X PRESS (WXP): Shooting Star Press but Friar twists to X his arms and legs as his hits his opponent Other Moves General Moveset: Snap DDT, Snap Kick, Standing Side Kick, Enziguri, Armbar, Surfboard, Emerald Fusion, Japanese Armdrag, Short-arm Lariat, Snap Suplex, German Suplex Light Heavyweight: Slingshot Plauncha, Friarana (Dragonranna), Backflip Splash, Broken Gift (jump swinging DDT from second turnbuckle), Moonsault, Running Heelkick Heavyweight: Satan's Gift (Springboard Jump swinging DDT), hip toss into neckbreaker, fujiwara arm-bar, single-leg Boston Crab Super Heavyweight: Broken Parts (360-elbow smash that 360's the opponent into the turnbuckle followed by a running knee smash), running 3/4 neckbreaker, The Gift (Jump swinging DDT), Diving Spear Submission: Boston Crab, front face lock, fujiwara armbar*(favored hold), leg grapevine, cross armbreaker, Friar's Clutch (arm triangle choke), hammerlock, armbreaker Hardcore: figure-four around ring post; The Gift onto/with chairs, tables, glass, bats, cinder blocks, and garbage cans; WXP with opponent on table underneath chair [EXTREMELY RARE]
