Meltdown 16, 4 July 2001

4 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 16 River Center Arena — Baton Rouge, Louisiana ---- Meltdown #16 (The camera opens into another sold out arena. The crowd buzzes, until fireworks explode on stage, and the fans go crazy. The camera pans over the arena, as the show begins.) Bryan- Welcome everyone to another edition of Meltdown! James Bryan here alongside David Yale, and fans, we have word that here tonight, President Caldera has promised some big surprises tonight! Yale- This should be interesting! Every time President Caldera promises something big, he delivers! I can’t wait to see what’s going to happen here tonight! (The Establishment theme hits, and the crowd explodes in boos. President Caldera walks to the ring.) Andrews- Please welcome the President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! Bryan- I guess we’re about to find out what he has in store! (Caldera snatches the mike from Andrews, and looks out at the crowd. They begin chanting ‘Asshole! Asshole!’ Caldera waits until they quiet, and begins.) Caldera- You know, I’ve been feeling a bit down as of late, and I’ve finally figured out why. I own a multi-million dollar company, I have cash coming out of every orifice, and I’m one of the most powerful men in the business! (The crowd boos in disgust.) Caldera- And yet, I want something more. Now, its no secret that I am a two time world champion. . . But lately, I’ve been thinking I could make it three. (Suddenly, Freak on a Leash interrupts Caldera. Shane Lane marches to the ring to a chorus of boos, and takes a mike from a ring hand.) Lane- Now wait just a damn minute there, Mr. Caldera. I am the world champion! And of all the people who may get a shot at me, you are certainly not on the list! Caldera- Shane, you need to remember something. It’s because of me that you’re at the top right now. And so I can bring you down just as quick. But Shane, I don’t want to quarrel with you. Here’s what’s going to happen. Tonight, there’s a little match for the number one contendership to the world title. The winner of that match will face Shane Lane and myself at Annihilation for the World title! (The crowd boos, as Shane smiles. The crowd then explodes in cheers as Born to be Wild hits. Rich Rollins walks halfway down the ring, mike in hand.) Rollins- Now hold on just a second. I’m sick and tired of having to listen to you two dips chatter on and on about how great you think you are. Quite frankly, that belt still belongs to me, and I’m going to take it! Caldera- Rich. . . There’s only one belt I see in your future. And you know what? You might just be getting a belt here real soon. . . (The crowd boos as they see someone sneaking up behind Rollins. Rollins turns, and gets the US title belt to his face. Khan stands over Rollins, and begins stomping away at him. Lane runs up the ramp, and begins stomping away as well.) Bryan- Damn it! That was a blatant attack from behind by Khan! How much worse can things get? Hey, wait a minute! Here comes System-X! (Toad, Hanson, and Reed come running down the ramp, and go after Khan, Lane, and Caldera. They brawl for a moment, until officials run out the break the fight up.) Yale- Well that’s that! I guess this battle is over! Lane, Caldera, and Khan are retreating to the back, while System-X checks on Rollins! Wait, Hanson and Reed are having a few harsh words with each other again! (The crowd oohs as Hanson and Reed get in each others’ faces, for no apparent reason. Toad tries to break them up, as Rollins recovers.) Bryan- Well, fans, this situation is getting out of hand! We’ll be right back after this. (The show cuts to commercial as Hanson storms off, leaving Reed and Toad with Rollins.) (The show returns from commercial, and the crowd goes nuts as Break Stuff hits, and John Reed walks to the ring. Rachel trails closely behind him, and he steps into the ring.) Andrews- The following contest is a fatal fourway match set for one fall! Introducing first, from Chicago, Illinois, he weighs in at 275 pounds. . . John Reed! (The crowd cheers again as Reed’s name is called. Fuel plays next, and the cheers continue. Luke Hanson heads for the ring, staring at John the whole way.) Andrews- And, from Atlanta, Georgia, he weighs in at 376 pounds. . . ‘The Fueler’ Luke Hanson! (Hanson steps over the top rope with ease, and stares at John. They go face to face, until Rachel steps in between them. She backs John off a bit, as Born to be Wild hits. The crowd explodes as Rich Rollins makes his way to the ring.) Andrews- From Chicago, Illinois, he weighs in at 225 pounds. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins! (Rollins hops into the ring, and leaps onto the turnbuckle. He stress out at the crowd, who cheers even more. Finally, My Own Summer hits, and the crowd gives a mixed reaction to the Jhub.) Andrews- And from Stamford, Connecticut, he weighs in at 270 pounds. . . The Jhub!
