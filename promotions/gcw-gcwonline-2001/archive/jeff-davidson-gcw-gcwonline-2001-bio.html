Center as the crowd comes to the edge of the seats to see the new youngster make his way to the ring. Out steps a young kid who seems to have an enormous about of energy and greats a few of the fans on the way down the ramp to ringside. Andrews: The first match of the evening is set for one fall with a 15 minute time limit. Making his way to the ring from Kansas City, Kansas weighing in at 220 pounds... “The Kansas City Kid” Jeff Davidson!!! The young man continues making his way around the ring greeting the fans and pointing out a few younger fans who seem to be in attendance and shaking each one of their hands as he seems to be a man of being a role model. Taking his time seizing the moment he walks up the steel steps and up onto the ring apron. Heaves himself over the top rope and climbs up the turnbuckle and appeals to the thousands in attendance to try and get the crowd behind him early before the match even starts. Descending one corner and casually walking to the adjacent corner and mounting it continuing to gather the crowd behind him hoping he can pull off the upset victory. He descends the second turnbuckle and walks over to his corner and gets the shake down from the referee making sure he’s not withholding any objects that would cause him to get disqualified.
