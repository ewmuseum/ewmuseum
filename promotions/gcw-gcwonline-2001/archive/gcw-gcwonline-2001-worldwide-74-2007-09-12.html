WorldWide 74 Cincinnati, Ohio ---- Introduction Ambrosi: I’ve given this a lot of thought, and there’s one guy out there that I have a lot of respect for, a guy that’s on his way up in the world and definitely deserves to be in the World Title conversation. So I’d like to invite out the man who I’d like to offer tonight’s title shot to… the GCW United States Champion, Jimmy Thunder! The crowd roars as Thunder’s music promptly cues up, and the US Champion appears on stage beaming. He adjusts the US Title Belt over his shoulder and marches towards the ring to a great ovation. Bryan: Wow! Lia Ambrosi has extended this week’s challenge to US Champion Jimmy Thunder, and here comes the Aussie hero! This man made a huge impact at Tsunami by defeating Shaman in front of his home town crowd in Sydney, and now he’s got a chance to win the biggest one of them all! Yale: I don’t think this is a smart move by Lia! This guy is the strongest wrestler in the history of the industry, he’s on a hell of a roll, and he could really deal Lia a beating! Bryan: I think we’re beginning to see President Caldera’s plan backfire a bit, Dave. Caldera wanted to wear Lia down, but she’s stepping up to the plate and she’s going to show Jordan White what a real World Champion looks like. Thunder enters the ring and accepts a microphone from a ring hand. His music fades as he steps near Lia and allows her to continue. Ambrosi: Jimmy, I’ll be totally honest with you. I respect you a lot, I think you’re a great wrestler and you’ve got a ton of momentum. So tonight I’ve decided it’s you that deserves the World Title shot. But before you answer, know this; this isn’t just some casual encounter. This is going to be the fight of your life. I’m proving a point to that weasel Jordan White, and that means I’m going to give you the absolute best of Lia Ambrosi. So I hope you accept my offer and take your crack at greatness, but know that as long as there’s breath in my lungs I won’t let you leave here with my title. The crowd cheers as Thunder nods appreciatively. He looks around at the screaming crowd as they urge him to accept. He slowly raises the microphone and begins to speak, but only gets out a brief sound before “Established” cues up. The crowd explodes in boos as President Caldera walks out on stage and marches towards the ring, shaking his head while chuckling to himself. Bryan: Oh, my, what’s he doing out here? President Caldera isn’t wasting much time in inserting himself into tonight’s affairs. Yale: Hey, this is the World Title we’re talking about here, JB, President Caldera has a right to supervise. Bryan: This was Caldera’s doing, after all, this idea that Lia would have to defend her title every week until Dangerous Games, but it looks like he might be thinking twice about the wisdom of his idea. Caldera climbs into the ring and produces his personal microphone from his side pocket. His music fades but the boos do not, and Caldera hesitates as the jeers continue to rain down. He finally raises the microphone and addresses the two champions in the ring. Caldera: Well, isn’t this heartwarming. Lia, I didn’t come out here to disagree with your feelings about Mr. Thunder here, there’s no doubt he’s a hell of a talent. We’re all very glad to have him here in GCW. But Mr. Thunder, before you answer Lia’s challenge, know this: it’s a bit of a tradition around here to see United States Champions leave their title behind in pursuit of the biggest one of them all. So if you accept this challenge, if you want to take this shot at the big time, you must walk out of this ring right now without the US Title over your shoulder. Feel free to accept this challenge, but I won’t have both of these titles in the ring at the same time. You can still walk out of here with your title… or you can place that belt in my hands and come back to face Lia later tonight. Bryan: Wow! What the hell is Caldera trying to pull here? Yale: Hey, it’s a fair deal, JB. Caldera just wants to make sure the US Title and the World Title keep their distance from one another, and Jimmy Thunder shouldn’t have the chance to leave here a double champion. Bryan: Something smells here. The crowd buzzes as Thunder begins thinking hard. He looks down at his US Title belt, then over at the World Title as it rests over Lia’s waist. Bryan: This is without a doubt the biggest decision of young Jimmy Thunder’s career! He’s made huge strides in beating Shaman for the US Title in front of his hometown crowd, but he’s got a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity here! Does he give up the US Title just for one shot at the big one? Yale: And I’ll admit, JB, it’s pretty clear Caldera’s got an agenda here. But what could it be? What does he gain from either preventing Jimmy Thunder from wrestling here tonight, or from taking the US Title off him? Thunder finally extends the title towards Caldera. Caldera smiles and reaches out for it, but Thunder keeps an iron grip on the belt. Caldera’s eyes open wide as he struggles to get the belt from Thunder, who sneers at him while staring hard. Finally, after a tense moment, Thunder glances over at Lia, nods, and releases the belt. Caldera smiles and slings the belt over his shoulder as Thunder climbs out of the ring and turns back to Lia. Thunder’s music cues up again as Thunder draws his hands over his waist, showing Lia precisely where her belt will be by the end of the night. Bryan: There it is! Thunder has just given up the United States Title! Yale: He didn’t deserve it anyway, JB, he’s Australian. Bryan: Thunder fought really hard to beat Shaman for that belt, but he’s willing to let all that go for this one shot at the title! It’ll be Jimmy Thunder challenging Lia Ambrosi for the World Title in tonight’s main event, and Thunder will either leave here with the biggest prize in the game, or completely empty handed! Yale: It’s a huge gamble, but I like his chances, JB! Thunder’s got a huge size advantage and Lia might not be able to counter it! We could see both the US and World Titles change hands here tonight, JB! Bryan: And what does this mean for the US Title? Caldera obviously has plans!
