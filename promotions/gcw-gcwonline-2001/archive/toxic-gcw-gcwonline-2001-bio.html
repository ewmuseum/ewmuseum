Toxic Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown San Jose, CA Birthday November 22, 1983 Height 6'10' Weight 310 Fed Titles World Champion (X-Wrestling); United States Champion; Tag Team Champion; Hardcore Champion Biography Toxic got his start in professional wrestling at the age of 17. He was signed the same year he graduated from East Side Union High School in San Jose. During senior year, Toxic was an all-state amateur wrestling champion. At the national finals that year, he was disqualified from the competition after a physical altercation with one of the judges. Still, he was good enough to catch the attention of CWU owner Nathan Krotzer. Toxic made his first appearance on CWU television in September of 2000, when he took on Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins and Gigas in a triple-threat steel cage match for the CWU Intercontinental Title. Rollins won the match and Toxic left the company the same night after an argument with Krotzer. Three months later, while working the independent circuit, Krotzer contacted him and the two quickly made up. In December of 2000, Toxic made his return in a CWU World Title match against Vince Reed. Reed escaped with the victory, but that was not the big story of the night. On that same night Toxic met Steven Caldera, the man who had been hired as commissioner of the CWU. Caldera and Toxic became fast friends, and Toxic didn't hesitate to jump ship when Caldera left the CWU to start his own comapny: Global Championship Wrestling. Upon his arrival in GCW, Toxic joined forces with Caldera, Octavian, and John Reed to form the original version of The Establishment. At GCW's first ever PPV, Warpath, Toxic was entered into the GCW World Title tournament. He defeated The Toad in the first round, only to lose to The Jhub in the second round. This earned him his first shot at the GCW U.S. Title. However, it was not to be as Thirteen ended up overcoming the big man in the match. Thirteen ended up being released from the company on the same night and at Shockwave Toxic got his second shot at the vacant title. This time though, Luke Hanson ended up walking away with the title. The next night Caldera's new Establishment turned on Toxic. That summer, Toxic injured his ankle in a victory over future World Champion, Tristain Mayhem. This was the first major setback for Toxic and he was forced ot the sidelines for over a month. Toxic returned in September 2001 with a vengeance. With the crowd behind him, he finally captured the U.S. Title, in another victory over Tristain Mayhem. In October 2001, Toxic entered into one of the most exciting feuds of his career with Luke Hanson. Toxic defeated Hanson twice (Devil's Night, NC-17) to retain his U.S. Title. Right after NC-17, Toxic joined forces with Mr. Danger, Octavian, and Jason Dread to form one of the most feared groups in GCW history. However, it was not enough to help Toxic keep his title at Christmas Chaos, during a gruelling four-way match with The Jhub, Mike Hardy, and The Toad. The Jhub ended up walking out with the title. Toxic turned on Octavian in January 2002, but the feud was brief as GCW closed for the first time shortly after. GCW returned in July 2002 with a 30-man battle royal for the GCW World Title at Dangerous Games. Toxic was the runner-up but was eliminated by Twisted. Toxic would be named the number one contender but would not get his shot as GCW closed again. Over a year later in March of 2003, Caldera opened up X-Wrestling, with most of the staff and wrestlers of GCW returning. At XW's first ever PPV, Evolution, Toxic and Twisted clashed in a Last Man Standing match. The match was fierce but Twisted was not able to answer the ten count after Toxic powerbombed him from atop the MegaTron. Toxic walked away with his first World Title, and at age 19 was the youngest World Champion in the history of Caldera Enterprises. His reign would be cut short as XW/GCW closed for what appeared to be the final time in April 2003. Two years later, in March of 2005 GCW re-opened. Toxic was stripped of his belt and forced to try to earn a spot in the 5 man ACWF match for the title. Toxic failed to make it into the match Toxic, however, was still bitter that not only had Caldera stripped his title without a match, but that he wasn't included in the 5 man main event at ACWF VI. The bad blood finally came to a head when the two went at in a Last Man Standing Match at ACWF. Toxic walked away with the win, but not before receiving a beating at the hands of the boss. Toxic and Twisted finally got to settle their feud in April, at Warpath, with Toxic getting the decisive victory. At Dangerous Games, Toxic was eliminated by Caldera in the early stages of the battle royal. Toxic no-showed the following WorldWide and was suspended indefinitely by Caldera. Toxic made his return at Devil's Night 2005, revealing himself as the mystery attacker of The Marauder, and then defeating him in a hardcore match.
