WorldWide 8, 14 April 2005

WorldWide 8 The O2 — London, England ---- WorldWide 8 The GCW logo flashes across the screen, before the letters ACWF appear on screen. Quick clips of the highlights from last week appear on screen one by one; Allied Chaos winning their contracts, NBK’s car getting hit by the sanitation truck, the appearance of Khan, LeStatt Knight retaining his title, Toxic destroying Caldera, and several of the high spots from the main event, concluding with Jason Dread capturing the title belt! Finally, the shot heads to ringside, where “Just Got Wicked" is cued, and the crowd explodes! Bryan – Welcome to London, England, ladies and gentlemen! GCW has invaded Europe as we embark on the Road to Warpath! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan alongside Mr. Danger here at ringside, and we get to start off this edition of WorldWide with a visit from our new GCW World Champion, Jason Dread! Jason Dread defeated five of GCW’s most legendary talents to become the first world champion of the new generation! The camera changes view from Bryan and Danger, to a clip of ACWF- Octavian holds Dread in a sickening position, rocking back in the most vile form of the Roman Rapper he’s ever applied. Finally Octavian drops Dread and stands over his broken body, knowing he’s just a few feet from victory! Bryan- Octavian is just below the belt! He’s just a few inches short, but he might just get it! Danger- FOUR TIME CHAMPION! He really is the greatest ever! Octavian reaches up for the belt, but his fingers only lightly graze the gold. Octavian looks down at Dread writhing in pain below. Octavian places a boot on Dread’s back, and uses Dread as a boost! His fingers graze the belt but Dread squirms out, and Octavian falls just short. Angrily, Octavian reaches down and pulls Dread into a bent over position, hooking him up for a powerbomb! Bryan- OH MY GOD! Octavian is going to powerbomb Dread off the side of the forklift! HE’LL KILL HIM! Dread struggles, but Octavian stays firm. He lifts Dread high into the air, and Dread’s arms flail wildly in protest! Dread reaches out for something to hold on to, and out of brilliance or just pure desperation reaches for the GCW title belt hanging just inches from his head! Octavian suddenly brings Dread down, driving him with all his strength into a vicious powerbomb onto the wooden platform below! The crowd explodes as Octavian is the only one left beneath the belt! Bryan- POWERBOMB! MY GOD, Octavian just destroyed Jason Dread! Danger- Hey, WAIT A MINUTE! Octavian looks up, and his eyes grow wide. The wire holding the belt swings idly above Octavian’s head, but the belt is nowhere to be seen! Octavian looks down in horror at Dread’s broken body below him. The camera looks on from above at Dread lying motionless on the platform… …With the GCW World Heavyweight Championship belt draped over his shoulder! Bryan- WAIT A MINUTE! LOOK AT THAT! Danger- NO!!! The crowd explodes as the image of the title belt resting on Dread’s chest is shown on the MegaTron. Octavian, wide-eyed and furious, looks around desperately for some confirmation. He gets just that as the bell rings, and the crowd explodes in elation! “Just Got Wicked” begins to play overhead, and Octavian drops his head in disbelief! Bryan- JASON DREAD HAS DONE IT We switch back to the ring ramp, where Dread's music still plays, and for the first time since ACWF he's seen actually walking down the entrance ramp, a large smile easily visible on his face. He stops at the top of the ramp and takes the GCW World Title belt from his shoulder as raises it high into the air, eliciting another HUGE pop from the fans! He re-shoulders the title and walks down the ramp to the ring, a slight limp noticeable in his step. James Bryan - In short, ACWF six was an incredible match filled with everything that we'd hoped for. Action at every turn and a closing of the match where Jhub and Mike Hardy presented Jason Dread the World Title showed once again that not only was GCW back, but it's here to stay! Dread has entered the ring by this time, where he stands on a turnbuckle, once again holding the World Title high into the air! The crowd pops huge and the lights dim, as flashes from cameras illuminate the arena. The champion hops off of the turnbuckle and stands in the center of the ring. He pulls a microphone out of his pocket and brings it to his lips. As he begins to speak, a deafening cry of "Ja-son Dread! Ja-son Dread!" stops him before he can say a word. Waiting a few more seconds for the chanting to die down, Dread pulls the microphone back and begins. Dread – For those of you who are new here – my name is Jason Dread...and THIS is the GCW World Title! Dread raises the belt one more time. The crowd pops huge once again. Dread – It was over 3 years ago when I lost possession of this belt, and every day since I've thought about what I would do if and when the opportunity came back to get back the opportunity to hold this all over again. Nothing did prepare me for the opportunity which did present itself...ACWF. I'd seen bits and pieces of what others have been through, and even got in the ring opposite of the few who have stepped into that devilish match...and one thing is for certain...none of them were the same afterwards. In all my years in this business have I ever been taken to the physical and emotional levels which I'd been taken to as it came to ACWF. On one hand, Octavian was always there to try and keep me away from him and the World Title. He attacked me weekly, he cost me match after match...and then hit me with a car. Minutes later he drove my head through the windshield of said car. And look at how much good that did! Another pop comes from the crowd, this time at the expense of Gaius Octavian. Dread – I'd always said that Octavian knew that I was and will forever be the future of Global Championship Wrestling, and that he was deathly afraid to take me on in a straight fight. He'd never successfully done so before, but even with the weight of ACWF on his side, he still needed to take "precautions", so to speak, to make sure that the World title's reputation would once again rot in his clutches. He's always wanted this belt for all the wrong reasons...and fate has a certain way of weeding out the crap like Octavian. If he was supposed to win this title, he would have. He didn't...it obviously wasn't in the cards for him. And I've heard plenty of crap over the past few days about in what fashion I'd gotten a hold of this title. That it was not of my own merit, that I would never have had it if not for a mistake on Octavian's part...well anyone can make up an excuse. Just look at the end result. I stand before all of you a man determined to bring dignity and respect back to this title...back to GCW! With the mention of common good for GCW in mind, the fans start another chorus of "Ja-son Dread! Ja-son Dread!". He stops and beams openly, the smile masking all other features of his. As they die down again, Dread continues. Dread – I stand before you a determined man...and man who will not make all the mistakes which I've previously done in my Establishment days. I will not treat this title as just an object to enhance my own personal well-being...I've made that mistake before and refuse to do it again. GCW deserves a champion who will not treat it's greatest award by such...and all of you out there who support GCW deserve much more to be proud of! I've been through the roller coaster of life, and with that trip it brings many learning experiences. As said before, this is now my chance to do everything the right way...the way everything should have been done the first time I acquired the GCW World Title. I will finally be the champion who is worthy of his praise. I will be the man who can be put down into history as respectable in every means. I will not fail any of you this time around...with that, you have my promise. I thank every one of you for supporting me genuinely...and from the very bottom of my heart. Long live GCW! The crowd erupts once again for the champion, before “Might of Rome” cues up! Boos explode from the British crowd as the large figure of Octavian appears on stage. Clad in a fine suit and his Imperial Roman Championship Belt, Octavian gives Dread a sarcastic round of applause. Dread simply grins up stage at his rival, as the music quickly fades. Octavian looks out at the crowd, relishing the incredible wave of hostility radiating his direction. He smoothly produces a microphone from his jacket pocket and glares at Dread. Octavian- Yes, yes, that’s very heartwarming, Jason. I wanted to offer you my congratulations on your tremendous World Title win last week. You left with the belt, Jason, not me. Well done, indeed. The crowd cheers, but Octavian cuts them off with an intense shout. Octavian- But you did not beat me! The cheers instantly turn to boos as Dread simply clutches his title belt over his shoulder. Octavian- You did not beat me, Jason, you capitalized on a mistake. I made one small mistake, I had one tiny mental lapse, and you took advantage of it. Well, good for you. You won the title in what can only be described as the biggest fluke since John Reed! The crowd explodes again, raining boos down on Octavian. Octavian- Well, I’m not concerned with the outcome of ACWF. I proved that I am the most dominant man in professional wrestling, and at the end of that match, I was the only one still standing! You may have won the battle, Jason, but this war is far from over. You may be World Champion right now, but I remain the greatest champion in the history of this company! The crowd explodes with boos, as Octavian takes a few steps towards the ring. Octavian- The fact remains, Jason, you haven’t proven a damn thing! When I embarked on my second world title reign, I held that title longer than anyone else has managed. I survived challenge after challenge, defeated everyone that stepped into the ring against me. Enjoy that belt while you have it, because it won’t last! The crowd explodes with boos, as Dread’s blank face simply shakes side to side. Octavian- You still have a long way to go to challenge my records, my legacy! And as long as I have breath in my lungs, I will not rest until that title belt comes back around my wait where it belongs! Jason, you won the first battle, but now I will bring this war to you with a wrath and a fury that you have never seen before. Tonight, Jason, I will begin to make my war against you, and it will start with the gathering of my legions. My Empire will begin to form tonight, and it will continue to grow as we approach Warpath. Then, Jason, on GCW’s biggest stage, on the show I made famous, I will face you man to man, and I will regain the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! The crowd explodes with boos, as Dread paces about for a moment, thinking. Bryan- That sounded like a challenge to me! Danger- Sure did! Dread didn’t think Octavian would do this, but Octavian wants to go one on one for the title! Dread- You want a shot at this title at Warpath, then? Fine with me. But make no mistake, Octy, I know who you are. I know how you play this game. You will not take me off guard, you will not get the better of me. Assemble whatever troops you want, because at Warpath, I will win this war! With one last parting smile, Dread drops the microphone. "Just Got Wicked" cues over the loudspeakers again, and the crowd pops huge as the GCW World Champion stares down his adversary, who offers a vile, sinister smile to the champ as the show fades to commercial… ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The camera moves to follow a walking Barry Goldstein, and a large man with shoulder length blond hair wearing a black leather racing jacket and a pair of blue jeans stands with his back to the shot. He's talking to another not so large man, and they seem to be in the middle of a heated discussion. "Excuse me." Goldstein interrupts. Johnny's head snaps to look over his shoulder as the smaller man walks off camera and disappears. "Johnny Lexicon, you raised a few questions last week." Johnny fixes his hair and straightens his jacket for the camera as Pete launches into a full fledged interview. "I think the fans of GCW are interested in your involvement last week regarding Michael Spitfire Stevens and Luke Hanson, and more importantly your relationship with Lestatt." Moves the mic to Johnny. Lexicon clears his throat and taps the mic. "I don't do interviews." Turning away Lexicon leaves a stunned Goldstein undecided whether to chase or not. The camera pans to watch Johnny walk down the hallway when something darts out from around the corner, it's hard to see what going on and then with the sickening clang of steel on skull Johnny drops like a sac and a form moves to stand over him. The camera zooms in enough to make out Damien Banks, his gums flapping as he spits trash talk at a groggy Lexicon. "What happened?" Goldstein’s voice floats from off camera. Banks looks at the camera and smirks before raising the chair again and bringing down with attitude. He steps to the side and flips the chair off to land across Lexicon's chest. Making a gun with his fingers he pops a cap in his prey and struts out of the scene. The camera man moves up as Johnny starts to get up, on his knees he takes the chair in both hands and looks down at it, as the scene moves in to show the smear of Lexicon's blood across the now dented seat. Trey Tremendous vs. Damien Banks Banks makes his way to the ring to a good sized chorus of boos from the crowd. He’s followed by the debuting Trey Tremendous, a well-built black man with abundant piercing and tattoos. He wears baggy jeans and a wife beater, and the crowd rises to get a look at the new arrival. Tremendous steps into the ring where he is immediately met with some rights from Banks. Banks sends Tremendous into the ropes, nailing him with a hard clothesline on the way back. Banks pulls Tremendous up but Tremendous quickly counters with a knee to the gut, followed by a perfectly executed snap suplex! A stunned Banks gets to his feet and backs into the ropes, where Tremendous promptly clotheslines him over to the floor! Tremendous quickly gets a smattering of cheers from the crowd, as Banks gets to his feet on the outside. Tremendous moves to follow, but the official cuts him off. The crowd boos as Banks smirks and circles the ring, catching his breath. Tremendous finally shoves the official out of his way and springs over the top rope, nailing Banks with a cross body block! Tremendous earns more cheers as he pulls Banks up and rolls him into the ring. Tremendous nails Banks with a series of hard forearms, backing him into the corner. He whips Banks to the other side, nailing him with a nice high-leg clothesline for a two count. Tremendous pulls Banks back up but Banks rakes his eyes, ignoring the official’s reprimands. Banks pushes Tremendous into the corner and nails him with some hard boots, before backing into the opposite corner. Banks charges and nails a leaping splash, staggering Tremendous. Banks pulls Tremendous into a dragon sleeper, before dropping him hard into a backbreaker for a close two count! Banks pulls Tremendous back up and nails him with a hard right to the skull, before hitting the ropes behind him! Banks flies back with a leaping bulldog, but Tremendous spins him around in midair and turns it into a sit-out powerbomb! The crowd applauds as Tremendous holds him for a near-fall. Both men get to their feet as Tremendous measures Banks. Banks hits the ropes and nails a rising Banks with a flying forearm, sending him back to the mat! The crowd begins to rally behind Tremendous as Banks pulls himself up again. Tremendous then charges the corner and leaps to the middle rope. He then springboards to the top rope, before coming off with a huge missile dropkick! That’s his finisher, called Absolute Power! The crowd cheers him on, but before Tremendous can continue, some commotion catches his eye to his right. Danger- "Somebody’s coming out of the crowd!" Trey Tremendous backs off into the corner looking at Banks pull himself up on the ropes groggily. Bryan- "That's Johnny Lexicon!" Danger- "And he's got that bloody dented chair!" Johnny flies into the ring on his belly and hops to his feet with practiced grace, the chair in both hands swings around like a big fat baseball bat. It slams into Banks back with force and he hangs off the middle rope like a rag doll. Bryan- "Lexicon is taking out his retribution!" Lexicon pounds one down on Banks head and shoulders before he backs off and locks eyes with Trey for a long moment. Slipping out of the ring he backs away towards the ramp. The crowd thunders and chants as Damien glares at him from his hands and knees. Danger- "An eye for an eye JB, I like this kid, and listen to this crowd go wild for him!" Bryan- "It's never that simple, these are egos clashing." Johnny stops at the top of the platform and makes a gun with his fingers aiming it at Banks before popping it off and grinning. That now even more dented chair still clutched in his free hand. Tremendous looks around in confusion as the bell rings, and the match is thrown out. Winner- No Contest Row after row of production vans, advertising the GCW logo on the wall of the exterior, are aligned in the back lot of the arena. The London Bridge is visible in the background, festooned with bright lights adjoining with multiple wires. Nevertheless, from a large gate that barricades the private back lot from the streets of London, a light pink ’59 Cadillac, adjusted to accommodate hydraulics, edges onto the neatly trimmed concrete. The Caddy bounces several times as it approaches an empty parking space, surpassing taken spots for GCW logo vans, exotic sports cars, and others of the sort. The white caution sign in front of the parking space reads “Reserved”. As the Cadillac parks, Kevin Jacobs appears from out of nowhere with a camera crew. He tightly grasps the GCW mic. The car suddenly lowers to it’s normal height. The engine is cut off. The driver’s side door bursts open and gives a little jerk backwards before Rocco Diaz steps out of the beauty of an automobile. He immediately steps over to Jacobs. Simultaneously, the passenger door opens and Antony Livestra steps out. The odd things is that the two men are dressed differently. Rocco wears khaki slacks with a buttoned up pink Polo. Pink shades disguise his Hispanic features. Meanwhile, Antony is wrapped in black dress pants under an untucked silk shirt flamingo pink, of course. Nevertheless, there respective chest hair emits from underneath both of their own shirts identically. Antony joins his partner of the side of the car, pushing aside Jacobs forcefully. Kevin Jacobs – Well, The Mambo Kings have arrived and they are-odd. Something about you is different? Rocco takes a long time to remove his sunglasses, as the camera seems to pan down their bodies, checking out the attire. Rocco quickly snaps,placing his shades in his pocket. Rocco Diaz – Hey Yo, Snapperhead! Up here, mang! In response to your question, Jacobs, Tony an’ I decided it t’was time for a change. New clothes, new car, new manicure, pedicure, and we thought it was time to stop dressing alike. It’s time for a change. Choo’ll be hearing ‘dat a lot in the coming weeks. GCW is ready for a change and ‘Da Mambo Kings are gonna’ giv’ it to ‘dem. Jacobs purses his brow a little before beginning his next question. Kevin Jacobs – Well, do you mean to start tonight? You do have your match tonight against Allied Chaos for the long vacant GCW Tag Team Championship— Rocco cuts him off. Rocco Diaz – Hey, mang! We know what we’re gonna’ do. We know what tonight is. We know who we are facing. We know what’s it’s for. All choo need to know is ‘dat when Mambo Kings go to ‘da ring, just watch. Antony Livestra – WATCH, MANG! Jacobs backs up a little out of fear when Antony chimes in his two cents, yet seems to shout more so then chime. Kevin Jacobs – Alright, do you have any thoughts on how you’re going to approach the match? Rocco Diaz – Tony and I have been chilling ‘dese couple of days, mang. We need no preparation. We just go and do the second thing we do best. Kick ‘da ass of any flash-in-the-pan ‘Merican ‘dat stands in our way of what we want. Like ‘da day we come to ‘Merica. Some lil’ piece of dogsheet try to jack my perfectly shined suede shoes. His attitude needed to be changed. Change. See it’s just something needed in everybody. Antony Livestra – EVERYBODY, MANG! Kevin Jacobs – Well, finally, do you have any words for Allied Chaos tonight? Rocco ponders on this question a moment, then looks directly into the camera. He swiftly snatches the mic from Jacobs and speaks harshly. Rocco Diaz – Choo two need to take a good look in ‘da mirror. Come to terms with chor fate tonight, mang. ‘Den I want choo to go into chor locker room quickly after ‘dat. Lock ‘da door, bend over, ‘den kiss chor ass good-bye, mang. Because, choooooo in trooooouuuubbbbbllle! As Rocco drops the mic, he exits the frame and walks off. Jacobs quickly bends over to pick it up, but as he stands, Antony snatches the mic even more forcefully then Rocco did. He shouts into the mic. Antony Livestra – DEEZ CUBAN MISSILES IN YOUR MOUTHS, MANG! IN YOUR MOUTHS! Antony practically finishes his statement by ripping the mic cord from the camera and throwing it clear over the hood of their Cadillac. He slowly walks off, leaving Kevin Jacobs in a bewildered and confused shot. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "The Hand that Feeds" cues up as Michael Hardy emerges from the back to a magnificent chorus of cheers. He slaps hands with screaming fans who reach fully extended to touch Hardy's infamous skin and bones. He reaches the ring and slides under the bottom rope and stands up receiving a huge applause and a chant of "Hardy, Hardy, Hardy!!!" Mike was just about ready to reach for a mic from a guy outside the ring but first he approaches the turnbuckle and lets off a few poses for his die hard fans. He flexes and jumps off the turnbuckle and makes sure to stop at all four corners posing the same images. Then he spins around and makes his way to the ropes and reaches out for the mic. He retrieves the mic and waits for the chants and screams to die down a bit. Hardy- I now know what love truly feels like. It's a passion for something or an intriguing moment where you can't think of something or anything else. I found my true love in life. I found it here in this very ring. My true love, is and always will be, WRESTLING! The crowds explodes into deafening cheers in the support of Mike's love for the sport. Hardy- And all I can say is that one day my love will devote itself to me and let me be what I have dreamed to be since the moment I signed my contract...World Heavyweight Champion... Once again the crowd erupts chanting "Hardy, Hardy, Hardy". Hardy- Jason Dread, you deserve what you currently wear, that belt belongs to you for the determination and the inspiration you hold and let loose on others. You have made me want to strive for more and be all I can, prove to all that I’m not the choke artist people claim I am. I look up to you Jason, you're exactly how I pictured myself being once I've hit my prime. Your simply an incredible wrestler with an incredible amount of heart. And for that I as well, celebrate your victory. Mike places the mic down and begins clapping as the crowd starts up a chant, "What you fear, What you fear, What you fear..." Suddenly “I Want to Conquer the World” by Bad Religion hits, and the crowd, begins to boo, as Stevens steps out onto the ramp with a microphone in hand. He smiles out towards the crowd, soaking in the boos, chuckling just a bit, before finally raising the mic to his lips. MSS: Ahh, Mike, Mike, Mike, first off congratulations my friend, you did an amazing job in ACWF last week. You were so close, you could’ve had it, but you fell just a little bit short. No one can blame you for it, you were up against some of the stiffest competition in the business. The only problem is, you didn’t win. You know it should be you with that strap around your waist. ACWF was your time, your chance, you were going to succeed finally, but it didn’t happen. Why do you think that is Mike? You had the so-called greatest mind in the business behind you, our beloved President Caldy, but you still failed. Crowd screams and jeers at that one. MSS: The problem wasn’t with you Mike, you’re young, naïve, but you’ve got the potential for greatness. If you would’ve had someone behind you that was willing to give you exactly what you needed, you’d be the champion right now. Steven is not that man. You know all about sacrifice already, I’ve seen you put your body through the kind of hell most people would consider suicide, you’ve got the drive and the determination, but you don’t have that final piece of the puzzle, and you need someone to teach you. But Steven will never teach you that. The one thing you’re missing, the only thing standing between you, and world champion glory. The willingness to do anything and everything it takes to get a victory. Sure you’ll break your own body, but are you completely willing to take someone else out? Are you willing to dig your thumb into someone’s eye, until they’re so blind they can’t see where you’re coming from? No, not yet you’re not. You could be though, if only Steven would have the courage to tell you that’s what you need. He knows it, and so does everyone else, but lately he’s too worried about everyone loving him to give you all the training he can. He won’t teach you how to go that extra mile, because he wants these twits in the crowd to cheer his name. Jason Dread is the champion right now, because he just like me has the killer’s edge. He’ll do whatever it takes to attain the gold. He’s got the brain of a champion, and you could too, if you’d just listen. Steven won’t teach you how to do it, but I will Mike. Let me be your guide Hardy, I’ll teach you everything Steven already has, and more. I’ve give you the champion’s instinct, I’ll teach you how to obliterate anything and everything your path. Now I know you’ve got a lot to think about Mike, you don’t have to decide right away. Do whatever you’ve gotta do to think. But whatever you decide I’ve got a little something for you. I know Steven’s been trying to get you hooked on his stretch limos, and thinks of that nature, but I figured I’d give you a gift that’s a little more your style. Check it out. Stevens turns his gaze to the big screen, where we’re treated to a visual of the parking lot, where two gorgeous women stand next to a brand new blue and silver 2005 Suzuki Hayabusa, with a big red bow on it. MSS: That’s right Mike, a Hayabusa, the world’s fastest motorcycle and it’s all yours. Let me teach you or not, still unlike some people who I won’t name, I won’t take away gifts given if you decide to not to stick with me. Well Mike, I want you to think it over, I’ll talk to you next week, alright? Hardy gives Stevens an odd look as “I Want to Conquer The World” cues up again. The crowd boos Stevens massively as he slips out of the ring, leaving Hardy to ponder his offer. Bryan- Wow… I didn’t see that coming. Mike Hardy has been on an absolute roll as of late, and now Michael Stevens is trying to recruit him! Danger- Well, Stevens is a tremendous athlete, but does he really have that much to offer Hardy? And what is his real agenda here? Stevens is the ultimate sneak, he’s always plotting… What’s his reasoning behind this? Bryan- I don’t have a damn clue. But for now, fans, we’ve got a very special interview which was pretaped last week in Kansas City… Starring my partner, Mr. Danger! Danger- Thats right JB! I sat down with Kevin Jacobs and discussed the highlights of my career, as well as the ups and downs of my life. I'm very thrilled that KJ wanted to devote a two part interview to yours truly. I hope everyone enjoys it. Bryan- I know I've been looking forward to it! For our fans out there that don't know what my broadcast partner is all about, lets take a look.... The camera fades from the broadcast booth to black. "Take a Look Around" by Limp Bizkit begins to play and we begin to see highlights of Mr. Danger's career. We see him holding up the IWA World Title belt in the ring. Shots of being Dero Bombed by Big Daddy Dero thru a broadcast table are seen next, as well as various other highlights from his career. Kevin Jacobs is doing a voice over of the highlights. Jacobs- The one man that has literally done it all in this business.. He's won world titles.. He has been the brains behind two wrestling promotions that he owned and made successful.. He's the owner of a major Las Vegas resort... And now he's a GCW commentator.. Let's take a further look into the life of Mr. Danger.. (Camera fades in and we see Danger and KJ sitting in the living room of Danger's house. ) Jacobs- First of all I want to thank you for allowing us the time to conduct this interview. I know you're a very busy man. Danger- It's my pleasure Kevin. I would be glad to enlighten the fans minds about my life and career. Jacobs- Well let's start with the beginning. How did you get involved in wrestling? Danger- When I was a kid I was always very athletic. I was involved in everything. Football, basketball, baseball, soccer, you name it. I was very competitive as a kid and young adult. When I decided that college wasn't my thing, I wanted to do something crazy. I had always loved wrestling growing up, and I thought what the hell. I dropped out of school and went to wrestling school. I trained long and hard and it all eventually paid off. Jacobs- So now as a trained wrestler, where did you look for work? Danger- I started working the independent circuit on a regular basis almost immediately. I remember my first match was against an established local star and I was the appointed "jobber". They paid me 20 dollars to go out there and get destroyed. After that I just started working as many local shows as I could in the Kansas City area. Wrestling was big back then in KC. Bob Gigel, the regional promoter, saw that I had something the other kids didn't have. I had that spark, and he knew that one day I was gonna be famous. Jacobs- So where did you go from the independent scene? Danger- Well, I bounced around for awhile and ended up in a fed called the DUL. I worked my way up and even won their World Title. But soon after I did so, the promotion closed down. However I was never beat for the belt. Jacobs- As your career went on, you found out that you were really good at being a professional wrestler. But when was it that you found out that you were going to be truly great as the head of a wrestling promotion? Danger- After DUL closed, I bounced around for awhile and then said to myself, I think I could do an even better job at running my own fed than all these other guys. I had the money, and I had the support, so I decided why not? I opened up my first fed in November of 1996. It was a rip-off fed called the NWO. The New-est World Order. We started out slow, but quickly build a cult-like following. Jacobs- So you created the NWO on your own, started it up from nothing, and when did you start to hit it big-time? Danger- Well the first month or so was slow. We were trying to sign new talents as well as promote two shows a week. We were quickly building a fan base, and a very loyal one at that.
