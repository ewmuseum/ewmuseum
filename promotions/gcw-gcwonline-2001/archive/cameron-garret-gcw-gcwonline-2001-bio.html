Cameron Garret Biography Real Name Cameron Garret Nicknames The Amazing Superstar Hometown Tacoma, Wa Birthday 08/22/1987 Height 6'5 Weight 225 Biography Standing at 6’5" and weighing in at 225 lbs, Cameron Garret is a pure specimen. All of his life he has wanted to be a pro wrestler, spending his hours after school wrestling in the backyard with his friends or spending it a the gym working on his physique. An all American in three sports, it is not surprising that Cameron Garret is good at anything he put his mind too. Whether it was playing cornerback on the football field or if it was catching during baseball season Cameron was the star on the field. That was until his sophomore year of high school where he made varisty wrestling. It was at this point he knew his future and his passion would be. Growing up in Tacoma, Washington, Cameron came from a good family. Middle class hard working folk, his father an investment banker, his mother a stay at home mother. Overall his childhood was a success and he was able to enjoy his home life. At the age of 18 when he graduated high school ranked 5th in his class Cameron decided that wrestling was the sport he wanted to pursue. He took a free ride to UCLA and their wrestling team. After one year on the team he realized that was not on the right path. At this point he dropped out and enrolled in the Stu Hart Wrestling School. He went on to graduate from their at the age of 20, starting out campaigning in the Canadian circuit for a little while until the thrill ran out. At this point he did two short stints for about two years each in both Mexico and Japan. His time over seas has made him into the perfection wrestler he is today. He has now brought his talents to GCW to set out and become the best wrestler there is in the industry today.
