Cary Turkington Biography Real Name Cary Turkington Nicknames none Hometown unknown Birthday He's somewhere between 30 and 40 Height 5'9" Weight 176 Biography Cary is a bum born into the bum life. Recently he's been existing as a Vodka dumpster, but that all changed when Paddy O'Shea needed a tag partner and found Cary in the arena's parking lot. Cary absolutely loves vodka. Usually he'll polish at least a liter of it a day. Wrestling Style Goofy, sometimes drunkard brawling style. Doesn't really have the strength to lift people up, mostly just punches and runs around. Finisher Move Hair of The Dog Finisher Description This is a submission. Cary, over a flattened opponent, pulls his pants down to reveal his hairy, dingle-berried ass. He then sits on the opponents face and wraps his legs around his opponent's arm, essentially locking himself on there. Most opponents tap out to this not because of pain, but rather the fact that there are 1+ year old dingle berries still in the hair. Finisher Setup Move Anything that gets the opponent flat on his back Special Moves Pride of the Cossacks: Opponent is flat on their back when Cary gets up on the third turnbuckle. Standing straight up, he pulls a flask from his jacket and starts to chug. When he's done, he throws the flask into the crowd and jumps off the turnbuckl Other Moves Snipe Hunt: Cary throws opponent into ropes; opponent comes back to receive a straight, spear-like head butt from Cary. This will occasionally stun or hurt Cary as well. Instincts (note: use only when Cary is drunk on a show; if you're writing my match, I'll let you know if it's in the cards): Cary grabs opponent, dry humps until thrown off. Cut Off (note: use only when Cary is drunk on a show; if you're writing my match, I'll let you know if it's in the cards): Cary pukes on opponent. Cary's fighting strategy: In case you haven't been able to tell you, Cary doesn't really know how to wrestle. He takes a lot of punishment in matches and only swings punches or does other strikes (rarely kicks, and if he does it's to the shin or groin). If there is an opportunity to be cheap, he will be. If you write a match where he's performing a bunch of real moves, I will not be very happy with you, so just keep it to him getting his ass whipped, performing his small variety of signature moves, and punching-biting-scratching-kicking-etc. Entrance Music No music; see entrance Ring Entrances There is not set entrance with Cary. If he's in a tag match, he'll come out with his opponent and use his video and what not. For example, if he arrives with Paddy, then expect to see Paddy's entrance, music, etc. If he's by himself, he'll usually just wander out through the crowd or from anywhere but the ramp, trying to slink into the match. No music, no video, nothing--just comes out.
