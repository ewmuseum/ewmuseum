Aiden Jackson Biography Real Name Aiden Jackson Nicknames Prophet, Malachi Hometown Saint Louis, Missouri Birthday October 7th, 1980 Height 6'1 Weight 212 Fed Titles GCW Cruiserweight Champion Fed Awards None Biography Malachi grew up in a family of 6, 2 parents and 3 brothers. Malachi the smallest of the lot was always picked on in the Jackson family. Their parents were strict Catholics and had the children enrolled in private schools until they got to the college level where they were free to go to which ever college they so chose. Sadly enough Malachi never actually made it to the college level. Malachi wasn’t out of high school yet when the day happened. Malachi’s parents were slain after leaving a Broadway feature at Saint Louis? Fox Theatre. Malachi became an orphan; luckily enough his brothers cared enough for him to take him into their household. Malachi’s oldest brother, Jesse, decided to adopt Malachi, whose real name is Aiden. Malachi had been mad at the world even though his life had been in order. Malachi pursued the career his parents would have always wanted him to, a pastor within a church. Malachi easily grew tired of the practices going on in the church and began to fall away from the career wanting revenge, to seek vengeance for his parents. Sick and disgusted with the world Malachi finally got his way and killed the man known as Michael Mason. No one is really sure how Malachi found him and killed him but he did. Michael Mason was a man who lived in the poor parts of town and probably was just looking for money from Aiden’s parents. As they read the verdict to Malachi, he just stood there and laughed. Eventually, Malachi was declared legally insane after screaming the name of his parents in his cell at night and claiming to see the ghost of Michael Mason, also noting that God talked to him. The truth was this man was far from insane, he was so mentally sound. Eventually, a powerful man by the name of Isiah James, the owner of a wrestling organization, and a long time friend of Malachi bailed him out by paying off the owner of the mental institution. In return, he asked Malachi to join the wrestling organization because of Malachi’s psychotic nature and Malachi gladly accepted promising to stay loyal to Isiah. Recently though, Aiden was released from this organization sent to other endeavors for his life. Basic Outlook of Aiden Jackson: He mixes styles with technician, power, and highflying/luchadore styles. He uses power against the smaller opponents that he comes in contact with mixing his style of technicality in also, he tries to rip his opponents apart with the power moves then end the match with the agility type moves. Smaller opponents for Aiden range up to 6'3 in height and 235ish in weight. Aiden will try to pull a more advanced style of luchadore wrestling against a bigger opponent to wear them down, Aiden trains day and night to gain endurance to out wrestle any of his opponents. Aiden will mix agility with technical wrestling when he wrestles the bigger guys. The bigger guys range widely usually they are about 6'5 and 250-260 pounds as a minimum.
