Martha from Florida asks: “So, are you gonna win at NC-17 or what?” Murray: First of all, thank you, Martha from Florida, for stopping this thing from becoming a trainwreck. I salute you. He pauses. Murray: Back to the question, though. I guess we'll find out tomorrow night. There's not one wrestler on the GCW roster who can say with any credibility that they're going to win their match tomorrow night, and I'm no different. I may be the longest reigning GCW World Champion of all-time, and I may be the single most happening dude on the planet, you never know what's going to happen when me and V are stood in this ring tomorrow night. The King stomps down on the mat for emphasis. Murray: Unfortunately, I cannot promise victory. What I can promise, however, is that tomorrow night, I'll keep doing the same thing that I've been doing since the day I first walked into this business as a bright-eyed teenager. I'll keep busting my ass to make sure that everyone who's ever bought a ticket just to see the Scottish King of Cool perform feels that every penny they've spent has been worth it. Certain people would have you believe that my time as Champion's coming to an end, but it's taken me fourteen years to get this high, and I don't feel like coming down yet. Win or lose, I'll still be able to walk out with my head held high. A rippling applause circles the arena as the King shows some defiance for the first time in weeks. Kevin Jacobs is already looking for the next question. Jacobs: Well I never! Two in a row. From Patrick in Delaware: “How has breaking Valentine's WT record affected your NC-17? Do you feel that there's anything left to do with the belt now that you're the longest reigning champ in history?” Murray: Well, I guess taking this record's made her just that little bit angrier. I mean, I've effectively written her out of the record books, and we all know that she's a great competitor, but is she ever going to have another 240-day title reign? I think not. Preparation-wise? I would say much has changed. Poor, deluded Vivica apparently wanted to kill me before I took her precious little record, so that ain't changed. I'd be lying if I said I haven't been hitting the gym that little bit harder, though. What she and I have is personal, and the fact that I took her record is almost immaterial. A pause. Murray: I suppose it's quite easy to say that I've nothing left to prove as GCW Champion, and hell, maybe I don't. Just because I've written my name into the history books doesn't mean I'm just gonna sit back and rest on my laurels, though. Is there anything left for me to accomplish? Of course there is. There are a roster of men and woman who'd love to try and take this belt from me, and until one of them proves that they can, I'll stand my ground. Another strong answer from GCW's biggest fan favourite is met with a positive response. Jacobs: Here we go again. From Ben of Texas: “Andy, man, you gotta help me. Ever since I wrote-off my fourth car at 5mph last year, no insurance companies will come within a mile of me! What do I do?!” Murray: Well, Ben; personally, I just saved a bunch of money on my car insurance by switching to Geico! Andy flashes a cheesy smile and a thumps-up as a six-figure sum deposits itself in his bank account. Jacobs: Chris and Shane in New Jersey ask: “We pop our collars like pros, go through two bottles of fake tan a day, take a cupful of steroids with every meal and wash them down with a protein shake, fist pump like no one else on the planet, and put $50 worth of product into our hair every morning... and STILL the girls won't sleep with us. Where are we going wrong?!” Murray: Stop telling people you're from New Jersey, that'd be a start. NEXT! Jacobs: Back to the routine, now; from Jenna in North Carolina - “is there anything you would have done differently in 2009 if you had the chance to go back?” Murray: I'd never have let that woman into my life, simple. There's no need for elaboration - the fact that Andy bows his head for the first time in the evening tells the story. Kevin, meanwhile, has ran out of paper. He reaches down into the box and takes out a few more. Jacobs: Cara in Georgia asks; “is Jay Terror really as much of an asshole in real-life as he is on WorldWide?” Murray: Well he's not exactly a bundle of joy, I'll tell you that much. Yes, even behind the scenes, Terrible Jay is as terrible as his name would suggest. Jacobs: From Walter in Kentucky; “what are you feelings on LeStatt Knight presently?” Murray: Awful, awful example of humanity, but the toughest competitor I've ever fought. For my money, LK is the only guy who can call himself “the best in the business” and get away with it. Our war taught me things about myself I never would've known if our paths hadn't crossed - I don't like it, but I owe the guy. He pushed me to the end of my tether, and I emerged a better man because of it. For that, he will forever have my respect. Jacobs: You guys are 1-1 in World Title matches - any chance of a post-NC-17 rubber match? Murray: Just let me get over this massive hurdle first, then we'll talk about that. Kevin nods slowly, unravelling the second to last slip in his hand. Jacobs: Henry in Wyoming wants to know, “how does the challenge at NC-17 compare with some of your previous battles?” Murray: She took my heart, tore it into little pieces, and stomped them under her boot... but you know what? I've learned from it. If Vivica J. Valentine thought that turning on me like that was going to neutralise my threat, then she was right... Looking downtrodden, Murray leans his arms over the top rope. Confusion and awkward conversation spread throughout the fans, before Murray finally raises his head, smiling. Murray: ... but not any more. Maybe I was stuck in a mire for a couple of weeks. Maybe I was selling myself short and leaving myself open to her assaults, but if I hadn't gone through those weeks of sorrow then I wouldn't be standing here now, telling each and every one of you that the 2009 Wrestler of the Year is ready to make 2010 his own... starting tomorrow night. A loud cheer, growing to a roar. Murray: I've never faced a battle this personal before, so it's only natural that there are gonna be a few doubters and naysayers doing the rounds. But they said I wasn't brash and outspoken enough to take down Chris Bagwell. They said I was too much of a do-gooder to beat LeStatt, that I lacked the grit to overcome Terror's power and authority. This is what I do, Kevin, only now the shoe's on the other foot. I've got my doubters, sure, but I shed that “underdog” tag the moment I pried this belt from LeStatt's iron grip. I may never face another challenge this personal in my career, and honestly, even I'm not 100% sure what to expect. All I know's that I'm ready, and when that bell's ringing and Joey Andrews is announcing the winner, nobody will be able to say that Andy Murray came to NC-17 and didn't give his all. Voices cheer and hands clap in support for the man in the ring. Finally, he has given them a reason to get behind him again. ”AN-DY MURR-AY!” The same humble smile occupies his features as Murray again bows for his crowd. Jacobs: Alright, how about one more for the road? Murray: Hit it. Slowly, Kevin Jacobs unravels the final piece of paper. His eyes widen and brows rise as he reads the question a second time. Murray: ... well, what is it? Jacobs: Oh, man. I don't know if you wanna hear this one. Murray: Try me. Jacobs: I dunno, it's pretty close to the bone. Murray: Kevin, I'm getting impatient here. GCW's Head Interviewer raises his hands, as if to say “don't shoot”. He pulls the microphone back to his lips and prepares to speak. Jacobs: From Boom in Northern Ireland... Kevin pauses. Jacobs: “T' BE SURE, T' BE SURE, T' BE SURE! HAVE YE SEEN ME POT O' GOLD YE FECKIN' EEJIT?! I T'OUGHT FER SURE THAT I LEFT IT B'SIDE ME BOWL O' LUCKY CHARMS AN' ME LUCKY FOUR-LEAF CLOVER! BEGORA! TAP O' THE MORN T'YE!” Murray: ... Jacobs: Yeah, I think it's best if that one goes unanswered. Shivering, Jacobs drops the paper. Jacobs: Well Ladies and Gents, thanks again for your time. Unfortunately it's time to wrap things up here... Andy last words, Andy? Murray: Thanks for coming, enjoy the show tomorrow... and remember! If Shaman ever tries to communicate, get the hell outta there before your brain explodes. Jacobs: Ahhhh, so that explains Bishop Steele... Having let his mind wander, Kevin recomposes himself. Jacobs: Uhhh, thanks for joining us, folks! We'll see you tomorrow night for the biggest event of the year, NC-17! Goodnight.
