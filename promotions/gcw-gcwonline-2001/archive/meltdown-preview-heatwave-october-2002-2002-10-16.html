GCW returned to ABC, and recorded one of its highest ratings ever. But with the shocking news of a new President and Steven Caldera totally out of the picture, where is the company going?

The United States title is to return at Heatwave, and either men have been given the chance to capture it! Who will walk out of Heatwave with the title? And who will challenge Twisted for the biggest prize in the world today? The only way to find out is to tune into Meltdown this Thursday!

Number One Contender's Match: No Disqualification Toxic vs. Rich "The Renegade" Rollins Toxic and Rollins have one of the most long-standing rivalries in GCW history. The challenge was laid down by Rollins, and now the match is set. One of these two will face Twisted for the GCW title at Heatwave. But with no disqualifications in the match, will either of them survive? Round One: United States Title Tournament Luke Hanson vs. Mike Hardy With Hanson's impressive showing at Dangerous Games, and Mike Hardy moving up the ranks, both men are in line to capture the coveted United States title. With this level of talent gunning for the belt, there's no telling who could capture it.

Round One: United States Title Tournament LeStatt Knight vs. The Tyrant These two are ready to destroy each other to reach the GCW United States Title. Knight is re-motivated and is ready to prove his worth; The Tyrant is quickly becoming one of the most beloved names in the business. This one could explode easily!

Round One: United States Title Tournament Chance vs. Kippo Both of these young stars are shooting for the United States Belt. But with only a small bit of GCW experience, can they survive in a talent full of well-known talent?

Round One: United States Title Tournament NBK vs. Michael Stevens Two talented young studs are meeting in this first round match, and both are fully prepared to go all the way. But NBK has Luke Hanson hunting for him, and Stevens has the Renegade looking to extract some revenge. Can either of these men survive without eyes in the back of their heads?

PLUS: We find out the status of the new President, more contracts are signed, and we get an update on the PTC Golden Turnbuckle Tourney! Meltdown Archives
