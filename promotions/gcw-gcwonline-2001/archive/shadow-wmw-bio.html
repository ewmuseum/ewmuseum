The Megatron shows a mist-covered moon and slowly, as the music finds a definite beat, the moon bleeds into an ugly red. Then, a scene racing through a city, details unclear save for the edges of buildings here and there, finally sweeping up to what seems to be the top of a skyscraper and a hooded figure standing on it, cape billowing in the wind behind. With that, a voice begins to speak. ASPIRAT PRIMO FORTUNA LABORI ME DUCE TUTUS ERIS VOX POPULI VOX DEI AD MAIOREM DEI GLORIAM... AD INFINITUM Slowly, the shot tightens and zooms on the face of the figure, swathed by a long sheet of black hair’Shadow. The crowd immediately reacts negatively, and the music kicks into a heavy beat, accompanied by an explosion of silver and white at either side of the ramp. A spot flickers on, illuminating the tall, lanky form of Shadow standing in the middle of the stage, hands on her hips. You know just who I am Don't be so distant Cause when you're lost I am solely there to share your grief Wailing for your sorrow Is only my way to comfort you Reminders of innocent youth Waiting for morrow you're lonely I name your solitude I speaketh the truth Andrews: And now... from Zdice, Czech Republic, she is your leader of Dies Irae... The Black Magdalen... SHADOW! Kamelot’s “March of Mephisto” continues to play as Shadow makes her way down the ramp. The fans boo her enthusiastically, but Shadow seems to pay them absolutely no attention. Now tell me all about your pain Down to the detail Don't say it's love Your fragile heart feeds my contempt Shadow, in full-on cocky mode, ascends the ringsteps and vaults over the top rope with the grace one would usually associate with a feline stalking its prey. She stands in the middle of the ring, raises a fist to the sky, and receives nothing but boos for her offering. Her eyes scan around the arena at the jeering fans and slowly, a deliciously pleased-looking smile comes over her face. Entrance Music 'March of Mephisto' by Kamelot
