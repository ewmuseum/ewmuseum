Jimi Thorne Biography Real Name Andrew James Hendrix Nicknames The Highway Child Hometown Northampton, England (Raised in Highway, Texas) Birthday June 20, 1987 Height 6'2 Weight 244 lbs Biography Andrew James Hendrix was born on June 20th, 1986 to Maxx and Amy Hendrix. Up until the age of 11, Andrew grew up with his parents in their home in Northampton, England. On November 14, 1997, a little more than two weeks after his father's birthday, tragedy struck when Maxx and Amy crashed into a telephone pole, in route from a Legends Signing, held in England. His father, the renowned wrestling journeyman was killed instantly by the accident, while his mother, famous for her time in United Kingdom Wrestling, as well as being one of England's top female wrestling attractions, spent almost a week in intensive care before she passed on. Alexandr Bapho, a close friend of the families, while originally never wanting to be a parent, immediately took the boy in and raised him on his farm in Highway, Texas. Overwhelmed with grief and depression, Andrew eventually made the best of the situation, excelling in school, as well as baseball and graduating with honors from Highway High School. Upon graduation, he received a scholarship to attend Rice University to play College Baseball, but he had no desire to attend. By the time he was done with High School (and after two years of begging) he was allowed to travel back to England to train at the United Kingdom Wrestling dojo, just as his mother did. Also, somewhere around this time, Andrew trained at the Icebox Gym and Dojo under the world famous Ewan Edwards. Other Feds United Kingdom Wrestling, various indy/spot shows around England Strengths Injury free do to being new to the business, natural instinct of technical and chain wrestling (see below), great understanding of basic professional wrestling, natural athlete. Weaknesses Inexperience, goes against natural instincts stated above and overthinks his strategy, the longer the match goes, the more desperate he becomes to win (i.e. taking a massive gamble), making tons of rookie mistakes in a match, Wrestling Style Old School Babyface Strategy Sticking to the "basics" of professional wrestling, regardless of his opponents style (obviously having it's advantages and massive disadvantages at times), the longer the match goes, the more brutal Jimi becomes (relying on punches, kicks, and favoring headbutts) to try to finish the match. Going along the lines of that, the longer the match goes, the more likely Jimi will attempt massive gamble, strategy wise, and try for something that could honestly cost him the match. Generally, Jimi will also overthink the match or his strategy and go against his natural instinct. Finisher Move Crown of Thornes Finisher Description One Move, two ways to win... (Firstly, the submission aspect of it, think of a full nelson that is reversed... the opponent is facing towards Thorne. Thorne slides his arms in underneath his opponents and lacing his hands around the front side of his op Finisher Setup Move Locking his opponent up in a Setup Description Exactly put, a reverse full nelson, with his opponent facing him, he will shove his arms underneath his opponent's, then bring them up his shoulders and around his neck. Basically a full nelson, applied backwards, thus a Reverse Nelson.
