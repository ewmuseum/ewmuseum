Josh Cameron Biography Real Name Josh Cameron Nicknames SoCal Shooter, The Giant Killer, The Pitbull, JC Hometown Long Beach, California Birthday May 11, 1984 Height 5'8 Weight 185 Biography Josh became interested in mixed martial arts at an early age. His father thought it would be a good way for Josh to learn some discipline and quickly enrolled him in Kick boxing and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. He took to both martial arts pretty quickly, and excelled- more so in the Jiu-Jitsu. Along with the MMA, Josh became fascinated with amateur wrestling. He was a high school standout and was recruited to UCLA with a full athletic scholarship. During his time at UCLA, he was a two-time All American and was a member of the U.S. National Team. After becoming a member of the National Team, Josh caught the attention of Jason Ramsey, the head trainer of Tenchu-Do, and a man with a rich appreciation and history in amateur wrestling. He invited Josh to train in the Tokyo Tenchu-Do facility. Josh quickly accepted the offer and began to train with Jason Ramsey one on one as well as personal training with Garret Bishop, a phenomenal mixed martial artist. He occasionally visits the dojo as a guest speaker. Competions, trophies, and awards followed Josh after his Tenchu-Do training. He has competed in the USA, Canada, Mexico, Japan, and all over Europe for several different fighting promotions. He's regarded by experts as one of the top five submissionists of today's MMA scene. Throughout his time in MMA, he was dubbed 'The Giant Killer', because he often took challenges far out of his weight class and succeeded in making men 80 lbs. heavier than him tap. Josh takes a lot of pride in being such a beast at such a small size. During his short tenure in professional wrestling, he's earned a reputation as a very skilled submissions expert. He is associated with Tenchu-Do and LIONS FC-- two very prestigious groups. ---------------------- Personality: JC is a cocky, brash, arrogant, womanizing, egocentric... asshole. He owns an MMA clothing line and has made a massive amount of money between professional wrestling and MMA. He loves to flash his money and show off for anyone who cares to see. Under the surface, he's a very insecure person who is tremendously self-conscious about his physical size. He feels the need to constantly be fighting for respect from peers. It's at his most effective while he has them in his guard or when he has side control. Gator Roll: A traditional "gator roll" throw with a choke. Video below. Trademarks Arm Trap Belly-To-Belly: Josh will secure a belly-to-belly with one of the opponent's arms trapped in the grip. With a Gable Grip secure, Josh hoists them into the air, pivots, and lets his feet leave the ground as he drives all his body weight down with the impact. Step-Up Enizguri: After jumping and posting off the opponent's thigh or hip with his left foot, he quickly swings his right foot around into the back of the opponent's head. General Style/Psychology Josh is a basic, yet very technically sound professional wrestler. Because of his size disadvantage in most matches, he usually wrestles defensively and focuses on utilizing his speed and proficiency with joint manipulations. His extreme cockiness plays to his advantage in wrestling defensively, because he can usually lure an opponent in long enough to shoot in and take them to the mat, where he is, without a doubt, it control. Entrance Music Gangsta Nation by Westside Connection Ring Entrances Gangsta Nation by Westside Connection blares over the sound system and Josh Cameron immediately struts into the view of the crowd. He bounces to the music with his trademark smirk as he peers at the crowd from behind his aviator glasses, obnoxiously chewing his gum. He lifts the logo up from his SoCal Shoot Gear hoodie, showing off his brand. The bulldozer known as Seth Landell follows behind JC, staring intensely towards the ring. While Landell pays little to no attention to the crowd, JC doesn't even seem all that interested in getting the ring. He's leaning over the rails and inviting girls to the afterparty. Landell uses the ropes to stretch his back while JC slowly pries himself away from the crowd and slides in under the bottom rope.
