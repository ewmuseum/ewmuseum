NC-17 III Madison Square Garden, New York, New York ---- Introduction: Welcome to NC-17 What does NC-17 mean to me? The voice of James Bryan, lead GCW commentator. An exterior shot of the legendary Madison Square Garden appears, brightly lit and packed, yet venerable and patient. It’s the pinnacle of everything we work for. It’s the reason we’re here. It’s the biggest stage in all of sports-entertainment. All roads lead to this event, every drop of blood and sweat spilled in that ring all year long is done so in hopes of reaching this moment. Inside, slow-motion black-and-white images of the GCW superstars appear, quiet and intense. Vince Jones; Jayce Bradley; Aiden Jackson; Michael Stevens. NC-17. This event earned that name because nothing is held back, no moments of hesitation or pause to consider consequences. Careers are made or broken here, and every ounce of energy and every drastic move will be put on the line. This is the greatness of NC-17; a night for the legends, a night where respect and courage are honored, and yet violence and intensity are on display. Another black-and-white shot, this time of Tristain Mayhem crawling out of the ring at NC-17 2001, only to have the door of the steel cage slammed upon his head. Dreams and lives have been shattered… Then a triumphant shot of Octavian, proudly holding the GCW Title belt above his head. …legacies and greatness won forever. The live crowd flashes briefly on screen, before giving way to the GCW logo. It sits quietly on the screen until the four symbols appear behind it: NC-17. This is the night where Global Championship Wrestling presents the best it has to offer. This is the night where the greatest athletes in the world put on their greatest performances. This is the night where the weak fall, and the great triumph. This… is NC-17! ---- Introduction: The Show Kicks Off With a sudden explosion of silver and gold pyrotechnics, Madison Square Garden erupts in a flurry of bright lights and booming cheers. Knight pulls back even further, but Stevens does nothing to suggest he’s willing to give up. Painfully stretching for the ropes, Stevens finds himself hopelessly out of reach. Stevens attempts to kick his heels into Knight’s back, but the leather bounces harmlessly off Knight’s muscled frame. The crowd desperately tries to rally to his cause, screaming his name and turning the volume up higher than ever, and Stevens still refuses to submit! Bryan: But Michael Stevens is not giving up! He just refuses to die! LeStatt Knight might just have to kill Michael Stevens to keep him down! Yale: And Knight will do it, too! These two have damn near killed one another here tonight, but Knight’s got it won! Just don’t let him put you out for good, Spitty! Give it up! The crowd, sensing Stevens beginning to fade, slowly changes pace. Finally the crowd begins pleading with Stevens, as if the people themselves are willing him to submit! The crowd pleads for mercy, begging for the torture to end, sickened by the ghastly amount of blood gushing from the face of Michael Stevens. Bryan: This thing is getting totally out of hand! Michael Stevens won’t give up! Someone’s just got to stop this damn thing! It’s all over, just save Stevens from himself! There’s blood everywhere, Stevens is being ripped apart! This has got to end now! Briggs watches as Stevens suddenly goes limp, his movement stopping completely. The crowd quiets into an ominous hush as Briggs raises Spitfire’s arm, letting it drop lifelessly to his side. Yale: Finally, Stevens is out! Get Knight off him! Bryan: That’s one! Two more and Knight retains! Just end this thing! Briggs lifts Stevens’ arm again, and again it falls without a twitch. Bryan: There’s two! ONE MORE! Again Briggs lifts Stevens arm, and for the third time it drops lifelessly to his side. But as it falls, Stevens lunges up with a sudden burst of power! The crowd explodes as Stevens manages to thrust his legs beneath him, and he begins powering his way up! Bryan: YOU HAVE GOT TO BE KIDDING ME! Yale: IMPOSSIBLE! He’s breaking the hold, JB! He’s doing the impossible! Knight tenaciously holds on with the dragon sleeper, even as Stevens manages to get his feet beneath him and power back up! The fans come positively unglued as Stevens begins to struggle, bent backwards from the vile hold by Knight. But Stevens unleashes a booming scream as he summons every last ounce of his energy. He pulls forward, lifting Knight clean off the mat! Twisting Knight down in front of him, Stevens drops straight down to the mat, driving Knight’s skull into the mat with sickening impact! Bryan: INFERNO DDT! THAT WAS THE DAMNEDEST THING I’VE EVER SEEN! Yale: How the hell did he do that?!?!? Bryan: Stevens just pulled Knight right off the mat, and that took more strength and power than any mortal man could possess! Knight is out cold! But can Stevens capitalize?! Yale: He’s moving, but he’s too dazed to make the cover! He doesn’t even know where he is, JB! But Stevens answers by abruptly nipping up, snapping to his feet! The fans unleash the biggest eruption they can muster as Stevens begins pacing around the ring, circling Knight like a vulture! Yale: ARE YOU KIDDING ME?! Bryan: And just like that, Michael Stevens has come back to life again! Knight is down! But he’s moving again! Somehow, some way, LeStatt Knight refuses to stay down! The crowd cries out in astonishment as Knight rises to his feet again! But Stevens, unflinching, delivers a stiff kick to the gut, hooks his head, and drives him into the mat with another DDT! The cheers rise again as Stevens lies in total exhaustion next to Knight, neither moving an inch. Bryan: ANOTHER INFERNO DDT! Stevens nailed a third one! The first one didn’t keep Knight down! The second one didn’t keep Knight down! But the third one might have! But can Stevens capitalize?! Yale: He’s lost too much blood, JB! There’s no way he’s got anything left! Stevens suddenly lunges to his feet, shocking the crowd once again! Stevens begins circling about the ring once more, finally stopping in front of the steel chair. He eyes it carefully and the crowd roars its approval, but Stevens inexplicably kicks the chair out of the ring! The confused crowd watches on as Stevens turns and watches Knight begin to rise again! Bryan: Stevens was eyeing that chair, but he got rid of it! He wants to win this thing fair and square! Yale: Mistake! Knight used it, Stevens needs to level the playing field! And these DDTs aren’t keeping Knight down! KNIGHT IS UP AGAIN! Somehow, some way, Knight is on his feet again! He stumbles around and walks right into another hook from Stevens. Bryan: Stevens has him hooked again! Can he finish Knight off!? Stevens pulls Knight straight up into the air, holding him vertically over his head! Circling about for a moment, Stevens gets a running start and leaps onto the second rope, keeping Knight perfectly balanced in the air! Stevens flies back into the ring and drops straight onto his back, pulling Knight’s entire body on top of his head with an amazing springboard DDT! Bryan: YESSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Yale: WHAT… A… MOVE! Bryan: That was the biggest damn Inferno DDT I’ve ever seen! That’s number four! IS IT ENOUGH!?!? Stevens calmly turns and hooks a leg, and the entire arena quiets, screaming the count in perfect unison! Bryan: ONE! Crowd: ONE! One. Bryan: TWO!!!!! ………………… Bryan: THRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Madison Square Garden reacts with a pop of monumental proportions, screaming, cheering, exploding with delight. Stevens, spent and near unconsciousness, somehow finds the strength to rise to his knees. His head dropping, Stevens closes his eyes as if in a dream. But “I Want to Conquer The World” cues up once again, and Stevens knows it’s happened. Bryan: YES!! MICHAEL STEVENS HAS DONE IT! Winner: And New GCW World Heavyweight Champion, Michael “Spitfire” Stevens Stevens opens his eyes and looks up from behind his crimson mask, and finds a towel dangling in his face. He looks up to find a grinning Josh Briggs offering him the clean white linen, which Stevens accepts blankly. Stevens wipes the blood from his face, though it almost immediately begins to flow again. Standing on willpower alone, Stevens staggers into the center of the ring, almost tripping over the motionless body of the now former champion. Bryan: Michael Stevens has accomplished the impossible here tonight! He has beaten the unbeatable LeStatt Knight! He has dethroned the most dominant champion in GCW history! LeStatt Knight has just been defeated cleanly and without question by the NEW Champion of the World, Michael “Spitfire” Stevens! The crowd applauds wildly, some actually crying tears of joy. Briggs is handed the GCW Title belt, and he turns to Stevens. Briggs promptly hands the belt to Stevens, who accepts it and holds it in his hands, observing it with a blank, dazed look on his face. Finally he looks up, a familiar wry grin crossing his lips. Walking calmly to the turnbuckle, Stevens steps to the second rope and thrusts the title into the air, earning a huge chorus of appreciative cheers! Bryan: There you have it! WHAT A MATCH! Yale: I… I’m exhausted, JB! That was the bloodiest, most intense one on one match I have ever seen! Those two did things to each other than would kill a normal human being! But there was so much on the line in this one, neither of them would go down! But I’ve got to hand it to him, JB, Michael Stevens pulled it out! Bryan: Hell yes he did! You’re looking at the gutsiest, most fearless man ever to step into the squared circle! Michael “Spitfire” Stevens is the new champion of the world! Stevens hops down, where he finds the ring empty. LeStatt Knight is nowhere to be seen, having somehow exited the ring during Stevens’ celebration. Stevens, confused, scans the arena for Knight, but comes up empty. Shrugging, Stevens slings the title over his shoulder and staggers to the opposite corner, where he climbs and poses for the fans once more. Bryan: We’ve just witnessed the conclusion of one of the most personal, intense rivalries in GCW history, and Michael Stevens has come out on top! Yale: I don’t know that this will ever be over, JB. These two have been through an absolute war, and that’s not something that will heal quickly. But the fact is, after months of domination from LeStatt Knight, and after what was perhaps the most brutal beating I’ve ever seen here tonight, Michael Stevens refused to quit, and pinned LeStatt Knight in the center of the ring! Bryan: It started at Dangerous Games! Michael Stevens went through twenty nine other men in a resilient victory that earned him the respect and admiration of the fans! He earned his title shot there, but after two bloody, brutal, intense battles with LeStatt Knight, he came up empty handed! Then at Devil’s Night, he revealed himself to be the rather odd RipeFist, winning the Buried Alive match and earning one last shot! And this time, Stevens would NOT be denied! Yale: I gotta say, JB, like him or not, you have to be impressed with Michael Stevens! He’s fought long and hard for this moment, and here on wrestling’s biggest stage, he’s become Heavyweight Champion of the World! Stevens slumps off the turnbuckle and moves back to the center of the ring. Dropping to his knees, Stevens lowers his eyes to the title belt again. Holding it close to his chest, Stevens appears to spend a quiet moment for himself, whispering under his breath. He then shoots to his feet, suddenly stomping about the ring with a burst of energy! Bryan: THERE YA GO! If you’ve got anything left, kid, celebrate this moment! You’ve earned it! Yale: He sure as hell has! Bryan: Michael Stevens is the Champion of the World! What an amazing contest! Michael Stevens has overcome perhaps the greatest champion in GCW history! He’s defeated the single most dominant athlete in the world today! He has done what no other man has been able to do! Michael Stevens has defeated LeStatt Knight! We’re witnessing the birth of a new era in GCW, with Michael Stevens leading the way! Yale: You’ve got to give it up for LeStatt, though, JB. That son of a bitch scares the crap out of me, but you have to respect what he’s done! That man went ten months without being beaten, he tore through this company, grabbed the GCW title and had the most dominant run of all time! He destroyed Michael Stevens and everyone else that came after him! Stevens is the new champion, but he’s just ended the single greatest title reign in GCW history. Bryan: There’s no doubt about that! LeStatt Knight shattered every record, he destroyed every precedent! He has set the bar extraordinarily high for Michael Stevens and every other champion to come after him. From now on, LeStatt Knight is the man every world champion will be measured against! Stevens drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring, wiping the blood from his hands on the canvas as he exits. He then turns to the crowd and flashes them an exhausted, slightly vacant stare, and begins his appreciative circle around the ring, slapping hands with the frenzied, ecstatic fans at ringside. Bryan: He’s tired, he’s expended every ounce of energy he’s got, but Michael Stevens is taking his rounds, thanking these fans for sticking with him these past months! Yale: He used to be one of the most hates guys in the industry, JB! From United States Champion and leader of the Establishment to World Champion and leader of GCW! He’s come a long way! Bryan: But he’s about to embark on the hardest journey yet! If Michael Stevens thought getting the GCW title was hell, wait until he finds out what it’s like to try to keep it! Everyone in GCW will be gunning for this man now. Stevens has a giant bulls-eye on his back as of this moment! We’ve learned quite a bit about this man in the past few months, but we’re going to find out exactly what kind of man Michael Stevens is as he begins his reign as World Champion. Yale: And while he’s been fighting for this title, JB, the talent in GCW has been getting better and better! There’s a whole army of young, hungry talent just waiting for a shot at this title. Michael Stevens is going to have a lot of people coming after him right away. Bryan: Well, for now, let’s just let this man bask in the moment! He’s endured absolute hell, he’s overcome a challenge so great that perhaps no other man could have done what he did. LeStatt Knight was in the midst of a dominant run the likes of which we may never seen again! But Michael Stevens has ended it tonight with four, count ‘em, FOUR Inferno DDTs! Stevens reaches the ramp and slowly begins his exit, his eyes once again scanning the arena for LeStatt. But the former champion has completely vanished, having snuck off while the attention was on Stevens. Stevens again turns his attention back to the screaming, cheering fans, barely able to keep his feet as he inches towards the stage. Yale: LeStatt wouldn’t stay down, JB! He was like Michael Myers or something, he wouldn’t die! Stevens hit him with DDT after DDT, and Knight wouldn’t stay on the mat. But Stevens finally hit him with that amazing move, and was able to keep him down for the historic one, two, three! Bryan: That was an amazing match, one of the all-time greats! But it’s ended with Stevens on top! He’s hardly got enough left to keep his feet, but Michael Stevens is going to walk out of here like a man! Stevens finally turns and stops on stage, his music still blaring, the fans still cheering. Stevens raises the GCW title belt above his head one more time, and a huge spray of gold and red pyrotechnics begin raining down over the stage. Fireworks explode in the rafters in celebration of his title win, serenading the world champion as he smiles proudly atop the stage. Bryan: There he is! The new champion of the world! Ladies and gentlemen, we’re out of time from Madison Square Garden! Thank you very much for joining us on this historic night! For Kevin Jacobs, David Yale and the entire GCW team, I am James Bryan. . . THANK YOU AND GOOD NIGHT! The GCW logo appears on the bottom of the screen as Stevens finally turns to exit. Still holding his arm in the air, the new heavyweight champion of the world walks out of view, his belt and his head held high. . . . . . . . . . . . . .
