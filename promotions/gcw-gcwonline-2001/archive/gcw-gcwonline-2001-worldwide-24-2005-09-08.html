Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #24 – Seattle, Washington

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Seattle crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “TREY 4 CHAMP” and “Octavian = Guido” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Seattle, Washington” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We’re live here in Key Arena for another exciting edition of GCW’s WorldWide! Tonight this sold out crowd witnesses a HUGE show as GCW continues to prepare for FallOut! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan alongside Pete and The Jhub once again, and guys, tonight is the biggest episode of WorldWide ever!

Pete: That’s right! Seven big matches are on the lineup for tonight’s huge three hour edition of WorldWide, and I’m excited as hell to be part of it! And what big show would be complete without a World Title defense!?

Bryan: You heard it, fans, tonight LeStatt Knight puts his World Title on the line against Trey Tremendous, and guys, we might just see a new champion here tonight.

Jhub: Trey’s got the tools, he’s been on fire ever since coming to GCW. And he’s got something to prove after losing his US title belt. But he’s facing the most evil, sadistic son of a bitch in the company, and tonight could be the biggest night of Trey’s life.

Bryan: On top of that, the cruiserweight tournament continues with the rest of our first round matches, the Television Title is on the line again, and Ryan Stephens and Jake Summers put their Tag Titles on the line against the Regulators!

Pete: It’s a packed lineup, JB, and I’m definitely looking forward to it.

Ask And Ye Shall Receive -by Tony

"Machinehead" by Bush hits over the PA and instantly the crowd erupts into boos as the Mike "The Machine" Savige steps out onto the stage, a slight frown on his face and the TV title draped over his left shoulder. He glances about, his eyes unreadable under his mirrored shades.

Bryan: The newly crowned Television Champion Mike Savige on his way to the ring! He looks like he's got something to get off his chest here guys!

Pete: Well I don't think he came out here to chat about the weather!

Jhub: Or discuss politics, of course he's got something on his mind JB! That's why these guys come out here!

Savige pauses and jaws at a vocal fan, Savige lunges towards the fan as if about to strike him and the fan recoils and Savige points and laughs before continuing on to the ring shaking his head and smirking. He tosses the TV belt in under the bottom rope and then rolls in after it, scooping it up in one smooth motion as he stands. He motions for a mic and it's tossed to him, he snatches it out of the air and turns to the back and draws his hand across his throat and his music fades out. He waits for the boos and jeers to die down a bit before raising the mic.

Mike: Last week I did exactly what I said I was going to do when I came into this company. I said I was going to shake things up, I said I was going to save this company, I said I was going to make things exciting! And by God, this (points to the title on his shoulder) is damn exciting!

He looks around as the boos intensify.

Mike: Yeah, yeah, you're booing me, but you're here aren't you? Each and every one of you came here tonight to see what would happen to me! You can deny it out loud all you want, but you wage slaves can't lie to yourselves! Now shut the hell up, cause your TV champion has something to say!

So do the fans, it sounds like a chant of "Ass-Hole! Ass-Hole!". He lets them go for a moment before cutting them off as if they weren't saying anything.

Mike: I've given you a reason to tune in each week. And just by looking at some of you, you need some kind of reason in your pitiful, meaningless existence.

The "Ass-Hole" chant starts up again, and again Mike cuts them off.

Mike: I came out here last week and did exactly what I said I was gonna do. I kicked Eva VanLorne's cute little ass! I've given you a TV champion who deserves to be on television. But there was one thing that ruined my victory last week, and that was you Marauder!

He takes off his shades and glares into the camera as the fans cheer for the masked man's name.

Mike: I was all set to teach little Eva a lesson, and you had to come in and rain on my parade. Ever since I came into this company you've stuck your nose into my business, since day one! Frankly I'm (bleep)ing sick of it!

Pete: He looks pissed.

Jhub: Really? Thank you for that brilliant observation Pete. I suppose next you're going to tell us he's standing in a wrestling ring?

Pete: I hate you.

Mike: And we all know not a damn thing was settled at Annihilation! We took each other to the limit, but what did that prove? Not a damn thing! I've had it up to here with you Marauder, Johnny Lexicon, whoever the (bleep) you are under that mask! This thing isn't over Marauder, not by a long shot, we need to settle this thing once and for all. And FallOut is right around the corner. So, here's what I was thinking. Since I intend to be a fighting champion, and I do have this overwhelming urge to kick your ass, I'm thinking the Marauder versus the Machine at FallOut! I'll even put the TV title on the line if you like!

The fans go nuts at the prospect of another title match at FallOut. Savige looks about and nods a bit, the smirk back on his face.

Mike: And here's the kicker. We conduct this match in the parking lot. No ring, no rules, just you and me, man to man, in a straight up street fight.

Bryan: Whoah! A parking lot brawl at FallOut for the Television title! That's gonna be huge.

Jhub: Savige must be real fired up to demand a street fight! He wants to get it on in that concrete jungle out there? That’s vicious.

Mike: I'm not a patient man Marauder, I want an answer before this broadcast is over! If you really are Johnny Lexicon under that mask, then I have no doubt you'll accept and I'll see you at FallOut!

And Angel, don't think for a second that I'm looking past you or that I've forgotten about you. I know you're here tonight and I know you want my title, and that's just too damn bad! And if you think, even for a second that my bloodlust for the Marauder is gonna give you some kind of advantage, that I'm going to be distracted or sloppy, well, you're dead wrong. If anything my hatred for the Marauder is going to put you at a huge disadvantage! Because I'm gonna see that masked freak's head on your body, and I'm gonna tear you a new one! Angel tonight you meet a devil!

And Marauder, remember, you have until the end of the broadcast to give me an answer!

The Machine drops the mic and glances around, an intense look on his face as "Machinehead" starts up again and he puts his shades back on and leaves the ring amid the boos and jeers of the crowd.

Bryan: The Machine throwing out a challenge to the Marauder to meet him at FallOut for the TV title in a parking lot brawl!

Jhub: And like I said, those matches are dangerous and with the hatred that these guys have for each other, they could very likely kill each other at FallOut!

Pete: And don't forget he gave Marauder until the time we go off the air tonight to give Savige an answer! That's gotta be weighing on the Marauder right now, considering he's got a match with Bryan Mayhem tonight!

Bryan: And Savige has his first title defense against Angel! Things just got more interesting for FallOut! Will the Marauder accept Savige's challenge? We'll just have to wait and see!

Promotional Video: Getting Ready -by Geoff

My body crashes through a plate glass window with enough force to knock the planet from its axes. Thousands of glass shards zip by me, under me. Through me. I can feel my flesh being ripped, staining some of their points a grotesque red. I float for what seems like a century before I land.

Crash is the more operative term.

Call me Rei. Giving you the play-by-play in Hollywood style slow motion.

The sand is cool, welcoming me with a cushioned fall that invites a serene unconsciousness. Too bad for it and me that I feel the landing as it shakes up my internal organs. I notice a steadily rising pain in my leg and reach down to remove the glass shard from my thigh. A slurping sound emanates and more blood flows from the gaping wound.

I try and think back to what exactly was going on. Broken glass. Desert sand. I suddenly feel for my six shooters and am disheartened to find the holsters empty. They have to be around here, somewhere.

Through the enormous hole in the window frame, the sounds of a saloon pour into the night.

A moment ago, there was silence.

I hear the piano player inciting sing-a-longs from the drunks. Beer mugs being slammed. Broken bottles being smashed. The moans of prostitutes and their clients from upstairs. A western saloon, in all its glory. Catering to the needs of every drunken gambler the world could offer. I’m just one more for the money pot, and I listen as the party carries on without me.

But I’m special.

I have to be.

It’s not every man that gets thrown through a window and has the opportunity to lay in the sand, thinking about what just happened. I could’ve been shot. Fed to the wild pigs. Stabbed to death by a deceiving prostitute. The possibilities are limitless.

So what do I do? I’m asking myself in hopes of something better than what I’m already doing. Giving up. Letting fate take the reigns. That’s not me. I don’t conform to the inability to change my hand. That might account for my addiction to poker.

Or it might just be ignorance.

“You don’t cheat me out of a game, outlaw.” Gruff voice. Gruff man. I can see fire as I stare into his coal eyes. He hates me as much as I hate lying here waiting to die. “I’m gonna teach you why, you son of a b*tch.”

And I’m lifted with elegant strength to my feet. Punched. Kicked. My face hits the sand, it filling my mouth with its body. I spit and lick my shirt. He’s stronger than I anticipated. All that alcohol must have lit a fire in his stomach.

SMASH! The sickening thud of his fist and my cheek bone meeting for a date. His ring leaves a lasting impression.

“Just shooting you is too damned good for ya. I’m gonna beat you and when I get tired, I’ll shoot you with your own gun.” That, at least, answers the question about my guns.

CRACK! Wood splinters as I’m heaved into an old broken table. My arm is sliced open by a jagged piece jutting from the side.

I’m just waiting.

His foot rams into my stomach repeatedly, causing me to cough. He lifts me up again and punches me, sending me sprawling back in the dusty street.

The wind has picked up, I notice. Makings of a sand storm. Through it and blood-filled eyes, I see people at all angles watching, cheering us on. Begging for violence.

I wince as his massive fist crashes into my kidneys. His boot grinding into the small of my back. The cheers and drunken incomprehensible increase in volume as blowing sand stings my face and open wounds.

“I’m going to kill this piece of sh*t.” He has their full support as he turns me over, face to face. I see his composure break when he notices no expression. I’m an outlaw. Expressionless and stone faced. I spit sandy blood. He draws away quickly and backhands me, sending more precious body liquid into the brown earth.

I’m still waiting.

I make my way to my feet slowly, watching as he goes back inside the saloon. I arch my back, hearing the creaks and groans. Craning my neck from side to side, I turn just in time.

SPLASH! An unopened bottle of beer smashes against my shielded face. The pain in my forearms is excruciating, now.

The man is resilient. I have to give him that.

A table leg caves in my midsection and I crumple to one knee, shifting my weight because of the throbbing in my thigh.

Waiting, still.

He tosses the table leg aside and helps me back up. “Piece of sh*t ain’t even gonna fight back.” He shoves me and I’m back to one knee. “Are you ready for your match, Rei?”

… What?

Everything is blurry. Voices are slowed to a crawl and repetitious. I begin to think unconsciousness is setting in, then it happens …

“Hey, Rei. Are you alive?”

Sh*t. Day dreaming, again. My lids are heavy as I strain to get to my feet, stretching to my full six foot frame. At least I’m already dressed. But I have to admit that there’s something stylish about being late to your debut match. “Yeah, you get janitorial detail when you’re done.” That slipped out loud.

The man holding the door steps in. Rich Rollins stands before me, staring down at me with a frown. I already know what’s next. “Sorry kid, cleanin' up your mess ain't in my job description. By the way, your music's playing.”

“I wasn’t … Nevermind. Uh, thanks.” I contemplate patting his shoulder, but think better of it. The expression on his face is not one of friendliness. I simply slide past him and on to GCW success.

Anticipating the clothesline, LeStatt hooks the arm and takes Trey over and down with a deep arm drag that tosses Trey halfway across the ring, but Trey immediately bounces back up and tries again. LeStatt delivers another arm drag, but still Trey is not deterred. Swooping back in for a third clothesline attempt, Trey Tremendous pulls out at the last second as LeStatt once again goes for the arm drag, but finds no arm to latch. Caught in mid-air, LeStatt crashes to the canvas as Trey hops over his body, bounces against the ropes, and dives down for a rolling senton splash. He arcs up, but LeStatt rolls out of the way, and Trey catches nothing but canvas. Rolling to his feet, a heaving Trey Tremendous shoots a look at LeStatt Knight, who now stands opposite him across the ring.

The applause detonates like a dirty bomb throughout the entire arena as they stare one another down.

Bryan: Woah buddy, you can feel the frustration tonight!

Jhub: They’re trying to carve enough of a niche for themselves so either man can set the pace, but so far we’re at a stalemate! Maybe I was wrong… LeStatt looks pissed.

Pete: They’re slowly circling one another now, looking for an opportunity to strike.

Jhub: Trey is so f*cked.

They suddenly launch themselves at one another, locking up with a vengeance. Slamming his forehead into his opponent’s face, LeStatt ducks down and comes behind Trey as the challenger stumbles, finding himself once more in a reverse hammerlock. Shifting his weight, Trey leans down and scoops up LeStatt’s leg with his free hand, hefting the larger Champion on his shoulders. Without hesitation Trey drops backward, slamming LeStatt into the canvas with a Samoan drop that shakes the ropes as the fans cheer on undying support. Suddenly finding himself with the advantage, Trey lifts a stunned LeStatt to his feet and shoots him into the ropes.

Bryan: Trey now, whipping the World Champion into the ropes… LeStatt bounces back… tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! LeStatt just hit Trey’s knee like a ton of bricks, and Trey goes for the cover!

The referee slides in.

Crowd: ONE~!

…

Crowd: TWO~!

…

Crowd: TH-…

Pete: And LeStatt kicks out! But Trey STILL isn’t giving him a moment’s rest, and he’s already on top of him and pounding his face with rights and lefts!

Bryan: The referee is threatening Trey with a count, but the challenger won’t let up! He’s beating the living hell out of LeStatt!

LeStatt Knight desperately tries to cover his face as Trey Tremendous continues to rain fist after fist into his flesh, but as the referee’s count reaches four, Trey quickly hops off and raises his arms in triumph as the live crowd roars in approval.

Pulling himself to his knees, LeStatt gingerly touches a finger to his nose, which is now freely flowing crimson. Spitting a mouthful of blood LeStatt rises, meeting Trey’s taunts with a few fists of his own that rock the challenger backward into the ropes. Staying on top of his opponent, LeStatt pushes Trey into the ropes and bounds him across the ring, waiting patiently for the return. Coming off against the opposite side, Trey rushes back in as LeStatt readies himself…

CRASH~!

“HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!”

Bryan: OH MY GOD, LESTATT JUST BACKDROPPED TREY OVER THE TOP~!!!

Pete: He just crashed back-first into the security banister! Good god, he isn’t moving!

Jhub: We need to see that again!

The MegaTron comes alive with an Impact Replay (brought to you by Verizon Wireless), showing in painstaking slow motion as LeStatt bends down and grabs Trey’s legs, hefting him up and over in a high arc as Tremendous sails over the top. Fans in the front row scatter at the last second as Trey crashes down on the security rail, falling backwards and smacking his head against the mats below.

Bryan: Just look at the awesome power of LeStatt Knight! Jonathan, sitting here and watching this match, what's going through your mind when you see the world champion? You and he haven't had much of encountering each other. But with him as the world champ and you as the US champ, I can't see that from being too far off.

Borealis: JB, I can say this right now and I can say it truthfully. As of this point in time I have no intentions of going after LeStatt Knight or his world title. I've got my own title to defend and to hold onto, and I intend to do just that. But I will say this. Knight is a hell of a champion, if not one of the best I've ever seen. And I would relish the chance to meet him some day, preferably for that world title he's got on. But that's down the road and around the corner.

Bryan: Holy Christ, Trey still isn’t moving! The referee has gone down to check him out, and it doesn’t look good!

Pete: He’s still conscious, and he’s begging the referee to let the match continue… but he can’t stand up! Trey is a sitting duck-…

Jhub: LOOK OUT~!

In a wild moment of reckless abandon, LeStatt Knight comes flying to the mats below from the top of a nearby turnbuckle, taking out ref and wrestler alike in one massive splash that wrenches bodies at awkward angles. Flopping to one side, LeStatt painfully holds one arm as he writhes on the floor, the ramifications of his decision to go airborne just now catching up to him.

Bryan: Good lord! It looks like it took more out of LeStatt than he bargained for! That’s the same arm that was dislocated at Annihilation, and he may have just dislocated it again!

Pete: LeStatt, you moron! Those mats are as flat and hard as my girlfriend’s chest, what the hell did you expect?!

Jhub: From your girlfriend? Same as usual, a bl-…

Bryan: Christ guys, NOT NOW~!

Wobbling to his feet, LeStatt hefts himself against the apron to stay vertical as Trey lolls about on the mats. With the referee completely out of commission, LeStatt rears back and slams his shoulder into a ring post, but all it does is force a blood-curdling cry of agony from his throat. Slowly regaining his senses, Trey spots LeStatt trying to realign his socket.

Jhub: Uh-oh.

As LeStatt prepares himself for another go at the turnbuckle, Trey hits a dead sprint down ringside and launches himself upon the security rail. Carefully cat walking the last three feet between himself and LeStatt, Trey leaps off with his signature Absolute Power dropkick, squashing the World Champion against the apron as both men crumple to the ground.

Bryan: ABSOLUTE POWER! LESTATT IS DOWN… GAH~!

Quickly pulling himself vertical once more, Trey Tremendous shoots one look over at the unconscious referee and immediately goes for the ring apron. Bending down, he roots underneath the GCW runner cloth as the fans foam at the mouth for retribution. When Trey finally pulls out a steel chair, they blow like Krakatoa.

Bryan: I have a feeling that things are about to get real personal, real quick.

Pete: The ref is down! Trey knows his opponent is wounded, and the look in his eyes… man, am I glad I’m sitting behind this booth!

Jhub: Trey’s about to get medieval, baby~!

Hefting the chair high, Trey practically snarls at LeStatt as the World champ clutches his arm on the ground. Gripping the chair legs with both hands, Trey raises the chair and instantly snaps it back down, bringing the full weight of his swing across LeStatt’s injured shoulder. A few nearby fans groan out of sympathy, but it’s an afterthought as Trey hefts the chair again and slams it back down, intent on destroying his opponent’s arm completely. Breaking off from his prey, Trey Tremendous suddenly rolls into the ring and sets the chair up seat-first against the middle of the ropes, once again shooting a look at the referee.

Bryan: Trey is taking full advantage of the situation here, and I don’t even want to know what he has in store for our World Champion.

Jhub: Oh yes you do, JB. And either way, I think we’re about to find out!

Climbing back to ringside, Trey lefts LeStatt none to gently as he climbs back on the outside of the apron, half carrying his opponent to the center. Driving a stiff forearm into the Champion’s face for good measure, Trey leans LeStatt backwards over the second rope, resting his head against the seat of the chair on the other side.

Bryan: Oh god…

His body suspended between the second rope and the chair, LeStatt can do little other than bleed as Trey Tremendous quickly stalks to the nearest turnbuckle.

Bryan: Oh GOD…

Perching himself upon the peak of the post, Trey Tremendous’ eyes glaze over for a second as he looks down at his helpless opponent. The sheer weight of the crowd’s overwhelming excitement shakes the arena to its foundations as Trey takes a few deep breaths.

Then he jumps.

Bryan: OH GOD~!!!

CRASH~!!!

“G-C-DUB~! G-C-DUB~! G-C-DUB~! G-C-DUB~!”

Coming down across LeStatt’s exposed throat like a derailed freight train, Trey Tremendous delivers a top rope leg drop that drives LeStatt’s skull into the chair, causing it to ricochet halfway across the ring upon impact. His tailbone screaming in protest, Trey Tremendous scoops up LeStatt’s broken form in a tight cradle, waiting for the three count.

Unfortunately, it’s a three count that never comes. The referee, having finally awakened and seen the chair skitter across the canvas, waves frantically at the timekeeper.

DING-DING-DING

Andrews: Your winner via disqualification, and STILL GCW World Heavyweight Champion… LESTATT KNIGHT!

Trey slowly stands, sneering wildly at the official. The referee immediately scrambles from the ring, and Trey turns back to Knight. Knight picks himself up slowly, blood dripping from his face, and stands to stare at Trey. The two exchange a furious glare, until Knight actually cracks a smile.

Bryan: Oh, God, look at this… I think Knight enjoyed that!

Pete: Man, this guy is sick, JB.

Bryan: Fans, we’re out of time from Seattle! Join us next week on GCW WORLDWIDE!

The show fades out with Trey Tremendous and LeStatt Knight locked in a vicious stare. And in the background, the faint noise of Johnny Borealis snickering can be heard as the show fades out. . . . .
