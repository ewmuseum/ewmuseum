CJ Woods Biography Real Name Craig Woods Nicknames the New York Lumberjack, Unbreakable, Paladin of the Public, the Innovator of Impact Hometown New York City, New York Birthday 2nd of February, 1981 Height 6'4 Weight 289 lbs Biography CJ was a promising young star who was looking like he was set for big things. Able to match the main event style even though he'd traditionally brought himself up through hardcore backyard promotions. In 2005, CJ's girlfriend fell pregnant with the first of what would be two children. Leslie was born early 2006. Urged by his wife to settle into a normal lifestyle and accept the IT Consultant job her father was promising CJ if he were to come off the road. CJ did so, for the sake of his unborn child. Stepping away from professional wrestling. As time wore by, CJ tried to do the right thing by his family. But he was never truly happy. He wasn't doing what he thought he was destined to do. Wrestle. 2009, a New Year's Resolution occured, with his wife, to do whatever it took to be happy. He and his wife were having difficulties being civil to one another and majority of the times together they fought like cats and dogs. CJ made the decision mid-January 2009 that if he were to truly be happy he would have to bite the bullet and say goodbye to his wife, step away from being with his children full-time, because that would be best for everyone. And he has. Stepping away from his wife, with six days a month to spend with his children, CJ has turned back to professional wrestling, to find himself once more. The Innovator of Impact CJ made an impact in his debut match, sliding past the veteran of GCW, Shaman against the general concensus. From there CJ continued to brand himself as an easy-going carefree kind of guy, that fans have quickly drawn a liking to. In his third match, GCW World Champion LeStatt announced him as the number one contender to the World title as a slap in the face to Andy Murray. Coming close on several occasions, he wasn't able to surpass the inevitable beating which was always on the cards and LeStatt retained in CJ's FIRST main event and FIRST title shot at any GCW belt. Earlier in the show, Chris Bagwell triggered what would be something that would resonate over the next few shows. The brutalisation of CJ Woods began, week in and week out. Who beat down on the Innovator of Impact? Chris Bagwell. LeStatt Knight. Gruelling contest with Dan Black. Injured himself out of competition at Shockwave. ANDY MURRAY (complete with sledge hammer). LeStatt Knight (2). What next? Record: W3-L4-D0 WW105 vs. Shaman - W WW106 vs. Face - L WW107 vs. LeStatt Knight [c] - L (GCW World Championship match) WW108 vs. Dan Black - W Shockwave vs. Tyson DeBough vs. Face vs. AXEL Action - L (American Dream match) WW109 vs. Andy Murray - L (Last Man Standing match) WW110 vs. Brock Alyas - W (Rampage tournament match) Strengths hunger; power; stamina Weaknesses speed; ring rust; hunger Wrestling Style Brawler/Technician-Bump taker with a hardcore twist Strategy CJ works the torso, trying to soften them up. Various back and mid-section workings with traumatic and powerful slams and breakers to try and get that area softened. His theory is if you make it harder for them to breathe you can remove their stamina, therefore he will work on the guts, ribs and back to try and slow them down with bruises and breathing difficulties. CJ can take a Hell of a beating and will take a lot to keep down. (see Main Event: WW109). His will and hunger will destroy him someday. He will put his life on the line for the fans and is somewhat of a modern day Mick Foley. He has that type of total disregard for his body (as long as he is athletically able to do things). He's in it for the fans.
