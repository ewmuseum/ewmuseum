Bryan Mayhem Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Lunatic Hometown Belmont, England Birthday ?????? Height 6'11' Weight 348 Fed Titles World Champion, 2x TV Title, 3XTag Team Champion, 4X Hardcore Champion Biography The past is the past, and Mayhem choses to keep it that way. The oldest of three boys(one being the Legendary Hall of Famer Jason O'Neil) Mayhem was raised in a world of abuse, molestation, ritual body carvings, and torture. The son of a pyschopathic farmer and a prostitute, Bryan learned of his violent tendencies at a young age. Forced to protect his younger siblings from a ravenous father, Bryan killed him by beating him over the head with a blunt object. After the murder, and the slow drug induced suicide of his mother(his only love ever) The O'Neil's were split up. Jason was sent to the US, where he lived a relatively normal life. Jeffrey, the youngest, and physically deformed was sent to numerous parts of Europe to be studied. Jeffrey's whereabouts to this day are unknown. Bryan jumped from foster home to foster home in England, only to be excommunicated from all schooling and family love. At the age of 18, Bryan returned to Belmont, and bought the very house he grew up and learned the meaning of pain in. The O'Neil's stay separated until Bryan saw his younger brother on TV as a professional Wrestler. Bryan, seeking the opportunity to revive the ties that bind headed for the states. Jason and Bryan would later for The Agents Of Oblivion. Jason's pure wrestling skill, and Bryan's demonic thirst for blood made them one of the world's most dangerous duos. Bryan, in a fit of jealousy would turn on his brother, snowballing one of the longest running, most violent feuds to this very day. Bryan's return to GCW, after a year's hiatus, a year following his most successful year of his career which included a World Title Run, has fueled his rage to finally end his brother. The O'Neils are Hellbent, and HellBound, and one day the Clash of the Titans will erupt...and may God have mercy on their souls...... Other Feds EHFW, PCW(for like a week) Other Fed Titles Hardcore titles, tag titles, US Titles Wrestling Style Brawler/Hardcore/Sadistic Finisher Move Oblivion bomb Finisher Description AKA The Last Ride Finisher Setup Move Chokeslam WW Entrance The arena is succumbed by darkness as a red light erupts from the ground under the Megatron. "Puritania" by Dimmu Borgir erupts over the PA system. Smoke billows up from the floor and mixes with the red light. The crowd's reaction is mixed as The Lunatic Bryan Mayhem makes his way from backstage.. Smoke and flames surround Mayhem as he stands tall, eyes straight ahead. He slowly and methodically makes his way down to the ring as the flames and smoke continue to kick up from the stage. The red light follows him down the aisle. The Megatron is littered with images of matches involving Mayhem, death, mushroom clouds, and Belmont. He stands in front of the ring, staring straight ahead, glancing at the ref, than the announcer. He pulls himself onto the apron. The lights slowly light the arena as the Lunatic stands perched, ready for battle, ready to spill blood. Special Moves Lucifer's Grip Other Moves In his return Mayhem has developed a submission hold known as "Lucifer's Grip" The opponent lies face down, Mayhem digs his knee into the small of the back. He pulls his opponents left arm under their own throat with both hands and begins to pull upwards. The effect is hyperextending the opponents elbow and shoulder, along with choking them out with the crux of their own arm. In his return as well, the slow and methodical Mayhem is done. He has become very aggressive, almost rabid. He may be older, but he shows no signs of slowing down. Ring Entrances The lights are extinguished and a bright ray of red light points directly at the megatron. The screen fades from a dark nothingness to a a vision of a mushroom cloud, followed by the picture of a vaguely famaliar mansion, than immediately to an unmarked, exhumed grave, with the mist of the now warm body billowing into the cold air. 30 men, all bald, pale, with small goatees and mustaches wearing black cloaks, with a rope tassle around their waists, resembling demonic monks walk in uniform, like an army of darkness, left, right, left, right, left, right, down the aisle all carrying candles, lit dimly. A loud static sound is heard throughout the arena, almost defeaning, the crowd on hand silent, not aware of what's happening. A rhythmic double bass drum erupts, and in unison strobe lights flash the arena. The images of death and destruction on the screen are succumbed by a blackness once again. Words appear on the screen. WE'LL DO AWAY WITH YOUR KIND. COUNTDOWN TO EXTERMINATE THE HUMAN RACE. 4 3 2 1 The cloaked men begin to sway in unison as "Puritania" by Dimmu Borgir blasts through the arena. The megatron once again keying in on that exhumed hole in the earth. Coming from behind the screen is another cloaked man, only his face is dripping blood, but his cloak and tassle is still the same. A wall of fire erupts behind him as he stands there, mic in his hands. He points at the crowd, than towards his minions in the aisles. They stop their swaying and get to their knees and begin, in unison, a ritual bow. The bloodied man, with loud Black Metal behind him begins to speak into the mic with a distorted crackled voice. "Let chaos entwine, on defenseless soil" "Remove errors of man and sweep all the weakening kind" A loud eruption shakes the venue, and walking in from the wall of fire is a very recognizable figure. 6'11" 325 lbs long black deadlocks, and the most evil of painted faces. The crowd erupts at the return of.........BRYAN MAYHEM!!! "I am war, I am Pain, I am all you've ever slain" "I am tears in your eyes, I am grief I am lies" Mayhem makes his way slowly down the aisle, each candle being extinguished on its own as Mayhem passes it. The crowd is in a frenzy, it has been a year, and Mayhem has made his return!" "Bygone are Tolerance, and presence of Grace" Scavengers are sent out, to cleanse the filth of Human Parades." The exhumed hole on the megatron begins disappear on the screen as images of fire, blood, and Belmont make its way into view. Mayhem's pace is methodical, knowing his time of retribution is only a matter of feet in front of him. He stares deep into the souls of the ring, and clenches his fists. "I am pure, I am True, I am all over you." "I am laugh, I am smile, I am the earth Defiled." Mayhem gets to the final two candles. All the cloaked men are still bowing in a ritual unison. Mayhem turns his back to the ring and faces the men that guided his entrance back to the ring. Mayhem raises his hands, and in remarkable choreography each of the cloaked men rise to their feet in unison. "I am the cosmic Storm, I am the Tiny Worms." "I am fear in the night, I am Bringer of the Light" At the word Light, the music cuts off, the lights in the arena come on, as all the cloaks fall empty, including the man with the mic. Nothing left but a pile cloth, no human life supporting them. Mayhem turns his head, and stares back into the ring...
