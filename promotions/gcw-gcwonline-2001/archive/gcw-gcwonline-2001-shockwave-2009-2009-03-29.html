Shockwave 2009 Aloha Stadium — Honolulu, Hawaii ---- Introduction A bolt of electricity crackles across the screen as the GCW logo flashes quickly. Soon a low rumble turns into a loud bang as a dozen pillars of fireworks scream through the air, illuminating Aloha Stadium. The capacity crowd thunders to life as the stage erupts in golden flames, until all that is left is a thick plume of smoke crawling across the stands. Tonight, Andy Murray gets his first GCW title shot, and his stellar GCW ascension comes to a climax! The crowd roars as Murray lands on his feet and drills Knight in the small of the back with a forearm. Amazing! Bryan: And now Murray’s in serious trouble here! Knight’s taken Murray’s biggest shots and he’s not staying down. Yale: Just as I said! He instinctively grabs Knight’s head to counter, but hesitates when he realizes the pad is gone. Knight seizes the opening and elbows Murray in the gut. Bryan: Whoa, Murray had a chance to fire back, but he didn’t want to use the exposed turnbuckle! Yale: What a pansy! He won’t even use Knight’s tricks against him! These are some of the most basic wrestling moves in the book! It’s cheating 101! Bryan: And that’s why Murray’s the most respected athlete in GCW! He’s reached this point without being dirty, without being a jerk, without hurting people! Yale: Unfortunately for him, LeStatt likes hurting people! With a snapping motion Knight tosses Murray backward through the air, delivering a huge German suplex that causes Murray’s neck to land on the exposed turnbuckle! The crowd roars as Murray slumps to the mat, leaving Knight grinning above him. Bryan: OH GOD! OH GOD! What a move by Knight! Yale: HAHA! German Suplex into the turnbuckle! I think Murray’s knocked out, JB! Bryan: No, I can see him twitching in pain! What a sinister move by the champion! That may be it for Murray! Knight steps over Murray’s prone body on his way to the turnbuckle. He begins to climb, reaching the top rope and then turning to face Murray below him. He rises to his full formidable height, perched with perfect balance on the top rope. Bryan: And now Knight’s going up! We’ve seen this before! Yale: This is the move he used to beat Jordan White!! Knight prepares to take off with the frog splash, but Murray suddenly leaps to his feet and dives into the top rope! Knight loses his balance and falls, landing spread-eagle over the exposed steel! Bryan: Oh yeah! Murray counters! Knight’s seeing stars! Yale: Hey! That was a dirty move! Murray used the steel! Bryan: He did not! He did what he had to do to counter, and it was Knight’s own dirty tactics that cost him! Yale: Sell it however you want, JB! Murray had to make a choice, and he made the only choice he could! Even in agony, Knight’s winning this battle! Murray climbs the turnbuckle in front of Knight and quickly applies a front facelock. The fans rise in anticipation as Murray lifts Knight off his seat and delivers a towering superplex! Both men lie in pain on the mat as Briggs checks in. Bryan: HUGE MOVE! What a powerful maneuver by Murray, whose power is matching up to Knight’s just fine! Hell, I’d argue Murray’s had the more impressive showing so far! Yale: Murray’s been impressive, sure, but do you really think this is going to keep Knight down? Bryan: We’ll see, here’s the cover! Murray finally reaches over and drapes an arm over Knight’s chest. …1! …2!!! ….KICKOUT! Yale: NO! See? It’s just not enough! Bryan: Just a near-fall, but those near-falls are coming awfully close! Murray’s just one big move away from capturing the GCW World Title! Looking for respite, Knight rolls out to the apron and begins pulling himself up in the ropes. Murray climbs to his feet and moves in, but Knight ducks under the ropes and drives his shoulder into Murray’s gut. Knight grabs him by the neck and thrusts him toward the exposed turnbuckle once again, but again Murray counters, and this time whips around with a violent clothesline that cuts Knight off his feet and down to the ground! Bryan: OOH! What a clobbering blow from Murray, who again avoided the threat of that steel turnbuckle! Landing awkwardly on the ground, Knight ends up with his head beneath the turnbuckle, his body sprawled out on the mat. Murray takes time to recover, the blood loss now beginning to take its toll. He tries to clear the blood from his eyes again, finally pulling Briggs over to wipe his face on his shirt. Yale: Well that wasn’t very gentlemanly… Bryan: I bet he’ll pay for Briggs’s dry cleaning… Satisfies, Murray sees Knight crawling back up to the apron. The crowd explodes as Murray flies back into the ring, and Knight grins in satisfaction. Bryan: Oh God, where’d that come from!? Knight is stealing this opportunity away from Murray by refusing to keep it clean! Yale: And why should he?! What did you expect?! Bryan: Damnit, you’re right, I was dreaming if I thought Knight wouldn’t pull out all the stops! But someone’s got to get that chair away from him, he’s got sinister intentions! Bryan: And Knight is just berating Murray here. I don’t think we really saw just how much Knight hates this man until now! Yale: I don’t think he hated him coming in to this match, JB, but this pathetic display of misguided honor in a match this big has Knight absolutely disgusted! Knight wants an opponent who will challenge him, who can take him to even greater heights, but he can’t reach where he wants to go if he’s held back by a PUSSY! He finally readies himself for the knockout blow, savoring the moment before he crushes Murray’s skull. Bryan: Oh God, here it comes! Knight’s lining him up! Yale: It’s all over! Bryan: Murray’s got the advantage! This is it! The title can be his! Yale: But he’s not gonna do it! This is what it takes to be champion, and Murray won’t do it! Almost imperceptibly working his arms out of the ropes, Knight screams violently at Murray to swing. Murray stands with the chair in his hand, sneering with hatred as he inches toward his adversary. Bryan: God, Knight is BEGGING Murray to hit him! He’s screaming at the top of his lungs for Murray to deliver the chair shot! Murray begins to step away, but Knight’s screams get the better of him. He shockingly frees himself from the ropes the moment the chair hits the mat, grabbing Murray before he knows what’s coming.
