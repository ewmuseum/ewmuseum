Jared Sykes Biography Real Name Jared Sykes Nicknames The Black Sheep, The Dragonslayer, The Burning Star, King Blueberry (and about 10 nicknames that go with it) Hometown Boston, MA Birthday 12/14/1978 Height 5'10 Weight 198 lbs. Biography Jared Sykes was born as a result of science's attempts to grow an emo d-bag in a laboratory. They succeeded. Now he's a wrestler. Isn't this awesome? Also, aliens scare the hell out of him. Not the illegal kind. The probe-your-butt kind. Other Feds SCCW (PTC), PRIME, SCCW (NWC), MSW (NWC), LSW (NWC), HIW (NWC), Atlantic (NWC Region), South (NWC Region) Other Fed Titles PTC -- SCCW Livewire Champion, SCCW Strength In Numbers (tag team) Champion, SCCW Gateway Champion, SCCW Universal Champion, PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion ... NWC -- MSW Cruiserweight Champion, LSW Cruiserweight Champion, NWC J-Crown Champion, HIW Maui C Other Fed Awards Only Grandslam Champion in SCCW (PTC) history Wrestling Style Japanese light-heavyweight w/lucha influence Finisher Move Lightening Helix Finisher Description Anaconda cross Other Moves Wrestling Style: Japanese light-heavyweight/American independent Set Up Move: Burning Star Lariat (Diving uppercut lariat ) Finisher: Omega-13 (Impact driver) Other Moves: MOVES: His primary offense is focused on the upper torso, with a smattering of aerial moves thrown in for a bit of variety (and because he's a cruiserweight, and that's what they do). He's agile, but not flippy. He'll pull out a moonsault, but don't expect any 450's or 720's out of this guy. Standard Set: Slingblade Springboard enzugiri Final cut Yakuza knee kick (Busaiku knee) Arm-trap belly to back suplex Neckbreaker across the knee Inverted overdrive Charging dropkick to an opponent seated in the corner Charging double knee strike to an opponent seated in the corner Moonsault reverse DDT (rare) Jared is also incredibly fond of and well-versed in neckbreaker and cutter variants, having used a few as finishers for the majority of his career. Trademarks: Burning Star Lariat - Comes in two forms, but is basically a diving uppercut lariat. The only variation involves a deep wind-up spin, where he'll almost drag his hand on the canvas before exploding up with this. This is THE trademark move, and can also setup the Lightning Rift. Crack the Sky - Slingshot cutter from the ring aprong to a dazed or hunched-over opponent near the ropes. More a desperation counter than anything else.
