The Jhub needed to be synonymous with show-stealing greatness, and I wanted a match that could meet my needs. I thought back to my roots, back to the common thread most of the original GCW crew shared; the American Championship Wrestling Federation. The place was a dump, of course, because Steven Caldera was champion, but it seemed like as good a starting place as any. I then walked out to the alley behind the hotel I was staying at in Chicago and began searching for any implement of destruction that could fit the acronym. Asphalt, chains, wrenches... all pretty easy. The answer came to me later that night at the arena. They were bringing Pete into the arena, and the only way they could haul that tubby seamstress around was on a forklift. Eureka! But if for some reason you want to see what happened to ACWF after my retirement, they're there for you. If you're not familiar with ACWF, well, prepare for an education.
