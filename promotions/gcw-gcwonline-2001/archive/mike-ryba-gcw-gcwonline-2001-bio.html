Mike Ryba Biography Real Name Mike Ryba Hometown The Jersey Shore Birthday February 25th 1982 Height 6'6" Weight 276lbs Biography Ryba isn't new to wrestling, being in multiple federations and having his own success, but also in others and his fair share of failures. Throughout the years he has been known to be a reasonable, caring man but if pissed off can become a very vulgar, hard man to deal with. He started his wrestling in the XWL, where he went his fair share of titles ranging from the Tag Team to the World (or Xtreme) title. After XWL he did his fair share of job searching, and although he managed to capture other titles...the feds were not around long after he managed to win. His only other claim to fame would be the SAWF...where he and Michael Mercer won the Tag Team titles. Holding them and defeating anyone who dared to challenge them. But eventually at one point both men walked out to the ring on one of the televised shows, and announced that they will be dropping the Tag Team titles, citing the reason of being 'not enough challenges.' Ryba proceeded to lob his title and Mercer proceeded to urinate on it, before shaking hands with Ryba and going on their separate ways. Although Ryba didn't follow in the same success of Mercer, who went on to win the SAWF World Heavyweight championship and remaining so till the company went down. But Ryba made his own history as being the youngest to hold the IC Title (according to the records upon his win, there is no validity to the statement) Ryba then joined PDW when it first opened. Although his first match there was a loss to Nightmare, after that night Ryba went on a winning streak that lasted him all the way into the first Pay Per View, where he captured the PDW Platinum Championship and taking the title of Platinum Status and PDW's First Platinum Champion. But as time went on, his drive for wrestling begin to die when his partner was sent out of PDW, and someone he considered a friend betrayed him. From there, he was stripped of the title for desecrating it, and then took his leave. His second run in PDW came in similar fashion, losing his first match and then going on to win the Platinum Championship for a second time. As he held on to it, due to his disliking of whom had a shot at his title, Ryba walked away from PDW only to make one appearance, before departing ways with the company for good. With his hiatus from the ring now, Ryba had begun to patch his life where holes were making it tough to live. He has since gotten engaged and still resides in Jersey.
