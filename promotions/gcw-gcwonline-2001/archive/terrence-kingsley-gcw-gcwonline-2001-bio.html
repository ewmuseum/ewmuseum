Terrence Kingsley Biography Real Name Terrence Kingsley Nicknames The Pit Viper, The Curse Hometown Li Grand Zombi Birthday September 7, 1984 Height 6'1 Weight 235 Ibs. Biography A snake oil salesman and a cold blooded killer have one thing in common; they are unrepentant. Nicknamed "The Pit Viper", Kingsley carries himself in the ring much like a predator. Unlike others inside GCW, he is an alien entity, a mystery coming from the swamps of unincorporated Mississippi. As such, he carries a certain unquantifiable mystique based on both intrigue and danger. His actions in SCCW are legendary for their calculating nature and sheer savagery; ending the careers of Thad Denver (Spacely), Coral Avalon (Baron Von Blackberry), Lane Stevens, lynching Mackenzie Malone with a noose of barbwire, brutalizing GTT7 winner Alexandra Pierce, and breaking Amy Campbell's ocular cavity before piercing her cheek with a rusty nail. As Gateway Champion, he's devastated all comers, brandishing his views of absolution as reason for why he does the things he does. He speaks of having a conscience, but his actions show he has none. Kingsley has no sense of self preservation and is completely willing to sacrifice his health if it means he can harm his opponent. He can be pounded into the mat until all that is left are bone fragments and tufts of hair, yet his warped views make him fight to the bitter end. This refusal to quit gives him an iron will, evening out for his lack of intense strength or athleticism. He wrestles methodically; every motion is deliberate, and his actions are systematic. He wants to grind his opponents down to the point of breaking them, both physically and mentally. All anyone knows of his life before wrestling have come from interviews. He's an ex-con, having spent an indeterminate amount of time at Parchman Farm in Mississippi. His father was a leader of men, but the furthest that has been explained involves the man's pastor tendencies. As a child, he apparently fought animals for profit and never finished school. Other Feds Just Wrestling, Sin City Championship Wrestling Other Fed Titles SCCW Gateway Champion Strengths Savagery, toughness, and unflinching resolve. Weaknesses Savagery, toughness, and unflinching resolve. Wrestling Style Methodical, Psychology Based Rasslin' and Brawlin' Strategy Strategy? Terrence Kingsley doesn't have a general strategy. Plans make one predictable. And if the Pit Viper is anything, he is unpredictable. His goal isn't to win matches, to hoist championships, to be considered the best; he is a man of black and white who resolves only to devour and destroy no matter the personal or professional cost to those involved. There is no flash to his arsenal. He doesn't care if you scar his body, just that he scars your body and psyche as well. He never yields. Never cedes. He wields his silver tongue as a sword. His capital is toughness and grit, and he is wealthy in both. If his unflinching stare doesn't unnerve you, breaking your fingers or kissing you so he can bite your tongue will. Even so, don't mistake him as just another of the nonsensical lunatic fringe that fill these halls; everything he does is with vigor and purpose and murky brilliance. Weapons of choice: glass, followed by concertina wire.
