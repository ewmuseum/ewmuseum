Marshall Hamilton Foxe Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Agent Foxe, The Agent Hometown Richmond, Virginia Birthday May 16th, 1981 Height 6'3" Weight 251 lbs. Biography Not much is known of Foxe's childhood due to his involvement with the United States Government, and the fact that he was given a new 'civil' identity by the government. The basics of his life are known. Marshall Hamilton Foxe was born on May 16th, 1981 - which makes him 27 years of age at the time of writing. His mother and father both raised him well until the age of 19 when they both passed away in an unrelated deaths due to natural causes. At this point, Foxe was known to have completed college and began to work in an administration role for the US Government. Details of his promotion and development towards an actual Secret Agent are sketchy, although rumours do state that he had 'more than a working relationship' with a 'higher-up-figure' which catapulted his career. From the age of 19 till now, Foxe had served the US Government in an admin role, as an agent, and perhaps several other roles. However, it was recently in late 2007 that certain 'secrets' had been exposed - thus resulting the US Government to end its 'secret affiliation' with Agent Foxe. Foxe was also known as David Kallstrom to the normal public - an identity provided by the government as to keep his work and personal life seperate. According to his personal friends, Kallstrom was a self employed national delivery driver, thus explaining the many days he spent away from home. In March of 2007, Kallstrom's girlfriend, Kim (surname unknown), was held up in a hostage situation inside a bank in Virginia. Due to the nature of the crime, and rumours have it that the hostage-takers were 'more than normal criminals', the US Government was appointed to take control of the situation. Foxe/Kallstrom had realised that his girlfriend was a hostage, and although under strict orders to not do so, blew his secret identity when he shot a police officer and civilian when not allowed to enter the bank - an incident seen by the public, and more importantly, Kim. Foxe was now caught in the middle of two identities, and was being charged for two murders. Amazingly, Foxe was then given a Presidential Pardon due to his knowledge of sensitive information and was allowed to walk free on two conditions he must meet to keep his life safe - he doesn't talk about his time spent as an Agent, and he must walk away from his job. Foxe, however, isn't willing to let go that easily. He has this dream that one day he will work for them again, but he needs physical and mental strength. Enter the world of wrestling. Why? God knows. But Foxe somehow stumbled upon this world, having never wrestled a match in his life, to find redemption. Having met former World Wrestling Alliance (WWA-Online.net) superstar Freddie Arson along the way, Foxe has picked up several vital skills from him including a basic moveset. His first attempt at the world of wrestling began in June 2008 when he inked a deal at Evolution Pro Wrestling. From five contests, Foxe suffered four losses and one no contest. Foxe was a mentally defeated man and questioned his ability in the game as he walked away from EPW at the beginning of August, less than two months with them, without fulfilling his contract length. Six months on, Foxe decided to give it a go again. With an improved knowledge of wrestling from his on-call trainer, retired and former WWA Television Champion Freddie Arson, he believes he is a much better physical and mental shape to make a success of this. It wasn't long until he agreed a deal with Ringside Wrestling who had given him the opportunity to prove himself. Foxe was quick to make enemies at rW but, more importantly, he was making quite a name for himself with his technical wrestling ability. He then teamed up with rW Ringside Champion, Eugene Fitzpatrick - acting as his bodyguard which earned him main event spotlight on a number of occasions. However, it was not long until rW folded and closed its doors leaving Foxe, yet again, without a home. And so the story begins.
