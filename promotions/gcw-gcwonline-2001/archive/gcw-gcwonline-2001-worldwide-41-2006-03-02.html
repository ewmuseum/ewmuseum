So, What's Going On, GCW?

28 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I'm glad you asked. Just two weeks away from Rampage, GCW's first quarterly Pay-Per-View of 2006, and GCW is anything but together. The literal chaos from Christmas Chaos has nearly shattered and broken the company, and yet, Steven Caldera was able to once again pull the peices together with help from new Vice President of Talent Relations and Television Peter Speer. And yet, things are in more disarray than ever. The US title has changed hands twice in the last three weeks, moving from Eva Van Lorne to Sam Wolack, and then around the waist of Vivica A. Valentine, a newcomer from GW. If that wasn't enough, the injury that Eva Van Lorne suffered after Christmas Chaos in her match with Wolack was no where near as severe as feared, and now, Ms. Van Lorne wants her gold back. The Television Champion, Chris Kannon (no relation) has been absent from shows for the last three weeks, due to visa related problems while GCW is on their trip through Japan. And, to make things worse, sad news from the Michael Stevens camp as Stevens was forced to vacate his GCW World Championship, due to a freak injury that forced GCW's Worldwide to be not shown on US television. No word on when or even if Stevens will return to a GCW ring, but, sources say the leg injury is severe enough that Stevens may have to hang up the red boots. Yet, with all the problems, GCW's Rampage, originally for the #1 contendership for the title leading into Warpath will instead present the winner with the GCW World Championship. So far, Dawn McFarlane and Shadow have qualified for the tournament with the following matches taking place this week on worldwide: Lestatt Knight, former GCW Champion takes on GCW newcomer and PTC workhorse Blaise "Hazard" McClancy. Seymour Almasy will take on Kimbusa, Eva Van Lorne and Scott "The 06 Icon" Taylor in a fatal fourway to decide just who will advance with the second seed in the Rampage tournament. Almasy's had a hellish schedule in recent weeks in GCW, and with his GTT5 commitments, Almasy may not have enough left in the tank to take on EVL, Kimbusa and Taylor. The PTC Global Champion's spot is also up for grabs as he takes on Mooch from Team Baked and Meatsheild Trainwrecker from the Guild. Can the champ live up to his legacy as the best in the world? Could the stoner Mooch pull off the upset? Or will the Meatshield find some phat loot before the match, and gear himself for sweet PvP combat? In his second singles match in GCW, Virgil Ambrosi takes on Chris Kannon for not only the TV title, but also for Ambrosi's spot in the Rampage tournament. Despite being off for the last month, will Kannon be able to take Ambrosi, who went through a brutal match with Jason O'Neil the week before? Vivica Valentine will defend her US championship against Ehren Moore, as well as her Rampage spot. Will Moore keep playing pass the potato with the belt? Will Valentine have a successful defense of the belt? Will Eva or Wolack get involved somewhere? And in the final title match of the show, Danielle Northsky will put her Rampage spot on the line against GCW Cruiserweight Champion Jayce Bradley. Bradley, who advanced far in GTT5 will have his first test in a few months for his title in the form of Northsky. And if that wasn't enough, last week on Worldwide, Steven Caldera shocked the GCW world when he announced he was returning to TV full time after taking the last 6 months off. There are rumblings that GCW talent isn't happy about this, and that there may be another confrontation this week on Worldwide, if Caldera shows his face on TV. Tune into XNet TV this Thursday at 8PM for GCW's 41st edition of Worldwide with James Bryan and David Yale!
