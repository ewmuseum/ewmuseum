Carmella Ignito Biography Real Name Carmella Ignito Nicknames The Second Generation Goddess, Simply Caramella Hometown Toronto, Ontario Canada Birthday July 11th, 1992 Height 5'7 Weight 130 Biography Carmella Ignito did not experience the typical upbringing also being the daughter of 3 former FWA World Champions. Her birth mother is Gabrielle Ignito one of the most celebrated female wrestlers in the FWA who married Jennifer Ignito another very accomplished female wrestler. A one night stand with Shawn Cortez (that may or may not have been taped by Jenny) who was a long time friend of both women resulted in Gabrielle falling pregnant with Carmella. Though Shawn is still an active part of his daughters life, she was raised by her two mothers alongside Micah. She always seemed destined to become a wrestler being surrounded by so many and her mothers regularly took her to wrestling events after their retirement. Though Carm was quick to pick up these skills, she sought an identity of her own and began to train in both Judo and Muay Thai seeking a career in either sport. But it wasnt long before the allure of all that her parents had done as well as Micah venturing into wrestling that she sought to follow in her familys footsteps...thus her career in GCW is born. Strengths Her flexibility allows her to escape from and perform unique moves. Her martial arts skill set gives her a unique offensive style Weaknesses Her flexibitity allows her body to be contorted and twisted increasing the pain inflicted. Overconfidence; being the daughter of three World Champions as well as growing up around many others has inflated her ego Wrestling Style Well trained in the martial arts of Muay Thai and Judo with a solid base in technical wrestling Strategy Using her drop dead gorgeous looks to distract from and hide her somewhat brutal in ring skills based around Muay Thai and Judo Wearing her opponents down with a wide array of strikes and submissions Finisher Move Divine Sin Finisher Description Coming from either the top rope or springboarding into the ring she leaps at her opponent and hits an Inverted Lunglower Finisher Setup Move Sweet Caramel Setup Description CM Punks Pepsi Twist Special Moves Suffer For Penance (Judo Tobinagi takedown into an armbar), Suffering For Penance (a Muay Thai clinch where the victim is helpless against a barrage of knees) Other Moves Various kicks, knees, elbows and punches Various Judo takedowns (sweeps, throws, etc.) Spider Twist - adopted from her mother Gabrielle Grounded Heel Hook - adopted from her father Shawn Jujigitame - adopted from her mothers wife Jenny Hammerlock DDT Snapmare Driver Entrance Music Reach by The Butterfly Effect
