Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #20 – St Louis, Missouri

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena and the St. Louis crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Rollins = Thief” and “Marry me Eva!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “St. Louis, Missouri” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: Welcome everyone to the twentieth edition of Global Championship Wrestling's WorldWide! We're here with another packed house as GCW continues to head towards our next big Pay-Per-View event, Annihilation! And tonight we expect to get the official announcement regarding the inevitable showdown between GCW Champion LeStatt Knight and the number one contender, Michael "Spitfire" Stevens. Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan, and with me as always here at ringside are my analyst Pete, and my color commentary expert, The Jhub.

Jhub: Thanks very much, JB, it's always a pleasure to be here in the front row at WorldWide. I've gotta admit, I'm excited to get this World Title match confirmed, because Stevens versus Knight has the potential to be one hell of a match.

Pete: Hey, I'm excited about that, too, but how about the title versus title match tonight? Eva VanLorne defends her newly won United States Title against the new Television Champion, Rich Rollins, and someone is leaving here a double title holder tonight!

Bryan: And we've also got LeStatt Knight's first ever title defense in the evening's main event! Knight has vowed to be a fighting champion just as he was as the US Champ, and he's putting the title on the line tonight on WorldWide! Rumors have been running wild as to who his opponent may be, but that name still has not been released!

Pete: LeStatt and Eva are the big story in GCW right now, ladies and gentlemen. If they both win their matches tonight, between the two of them they'll hold the World, US, Television, and Tag Team titles! All the gold in the company! That, fans, is something unprecedented in the history of this company.

Jhub: Well, I dunno, Double Cool held a lot of-

Bryan: Changing the subject rapidly, fans, we're also going to see Twizzy and Jonathan Borealis go at it, as well as the debut of newcomer X! All that and more tonight on WorldWide!

Bacon -by Dan

We fade in to Jonathan Borealis in his locker room, tense and pacing back and forth with his eyes looking down and his fists clenched.

Jonathan’s jaw is held tight and every now and then he shakes his head as he paces back and forth...back and forth.

"Son of a bitch...

That son of a bitch. I can't believe-"

With one, short, thunderous BANG, Borealis' fist crumples part of a locker and retracts as if he was never there as he continues to pace back and forth.

Back and forth.

"I can't fucking believe it!!!"

Finally, he sits. His once pretty eyes glazed over in a straight ahead stare at a tile on the floor and his shoulders hunched up, making him look very different from the Johnny Borealis we've grown to know. A definite gleam in his eye shows overwhelming fury that he can barely contain as he visibly shakes and tries to calm down.

"...my fucking championship..."

On cue, there's a knock at the door.

No movement. No voice. Borealis might have been made of stone for all we know.

There's another knock and the muffled voice of Jordan White can be heard on the outside.

"Johnny-boy. Open up."

Borealis responds in a whisper that we can barely hear at all.

"Go to Hell."

But even if White did hear it, it wouldn't change his actions much.

The door opens smoothly and Jordan White, in his usual attire, finds himself staring halfway across the room at Jonathan Borealis' back.

He's holding a white paper bag in his hand and he begins to walk over to Borealis.

"What's got you so terribly, terribly sad? I haven't seen an emo trip like this outta you since...

...well, since you hacked off part of your whack-off hand, Johnny-boy."

Jordan White takes a seat right next to Jonathan Borealis and pats him on the back.

"Don't worry, kidd-o. It was just a TV title."

"That's not the point."

"With you? Of course not. It's never the title. It's almost always some stupid bullshit about how you lost it or how you lost in general or how you let yourself down again and again, whine, bitch, and moan."

For the first time, Borealis' eyes leave that tile on the floor and look sideways at Jordan White.

"You above all people should know not to call me 'kidd-o'."

"I know, tiger. I know.

And that's one of a couple hundred reasons why I'm not in there, wearing LeStatt Knight's belt right now. I got so fucking sick and tired of having to prove myself to different groups of people over and over again. There are few things more frustrating than having people treat you like a rookie just because they never heard of you.

Or in your case, because you act so juvenile.

Bacon, I trust?"

Jordan pulls a wrapped burger out of the white bag.

"What?"

"I asked if you want the one with bacon on it?

See, this is why I like Wendy's better. Their bacon's just got a little more kick to it than the leading cheap-shit burger place."

Borealis actually seems to crack a bit of a smile.

Then he gratuitously grabs a burger.

"Plus they don't have some retarded clown to sell their food. Just some old racist.

I really shouldn't be eating this, though. I do have a match tonight."

Without thought, Borealis takes a large bite out of the burger and returns to looking at that damn tile on the floor.

Jordan has retrieved two more burgers, a baked potato with butter, chives, and sour cream, and a Biggie Coke from the bag as well.

"Eh, eat it, chief. You and I got a lot to talk about..."

Singles Match – One Fall

Dimensioner vs. X -by Phyl

Bryan: This opening match should be a good one Pete, Dimensioner versus a very recent GCW recruit the mysterious X.

Pete: Who?

Bryan: X.

Pete: The Eliminator? Isn’t that the guy who’s always trying to get the crest of Birdman?

Bryan: No idea what you’re talking about.

Their conversation is cut off by “Down With The Sickness” by Disturbed. The crowd immediately fills the arena with a huge chorus of boos. The big man treads down to the ring a quirky sick little smile on his face. Once he arrives he steps over the top and into the center of the ring. He turns towards the entrance way, awaiting his opponent coldly folding his arms across his chest.

Bryan: Dimensioner looking focused here. He certainly doesn’t want a repeat of last week.

Pete: Won’t happen JB, end of story. Hardy got lucky last week, and there’s no chance in hell this new Eliminator kid is going to have a snowballs chance in hell.

Bryan: That’s X Pete.

Pete: Bah, whatever.

Jhub: Ever get tired of being an idiot Pete?

Pete never gets to answer the question as “The Ecstasy of Gold” begins to play throughout the arena. The crowd gives a modest cheer for the relatively unknown X as he heads out towards the ring. Wasting little time the man known simply as X sprints down to the ring and slides in under the rope. He pops up just as the bell sounds.

Bryan: And we’re under way, GCW is expecting some big things out of X, and this is going to be his chance to prove himself against a man who outweighs him by nearly a hundred pounds.

Pete: Fat chance.

Jhub: Pete, unless you’re talking about you, you’re not allowed to say fat.

Dimensioner grins evilly and lunges at X, but the smaller man is too quick, and side steps lifting a knee just in time to catch D in the stomach. The big man continues a couple steps forward holding his stomach, but soon turns to face X with the same evil grin on his face. Shaking his head the big man charges again, only to end up eating another knee to the stomach.

Bryan: Ha! This kid is just way too face for Dimensioner here.

Pete: Yeah, but once Dimenesioner gets his hands on X, this match is over.

Jhub: Hmmm, Pete saying something intelligent, it’s amazing.

Dimensioner stands for a moment holding his stomach, before slowly turning. Gone is the evil smile, the big growls at the smaller and slowly begins stalking forward. X wastes no time however, and goes for a quick seated dropkick into Dimensioner’s knee. This takes the big man down, and X immediately follows up with a single leg grapevine.

Bryan: Smart move by the new comer here by taking out the bigger man’s legs. Dimensioner won’t have so much of a power advantage if he can’t stand.

Pete: Pfft, just wait…see?

Dimensioner uses his feet foot to launch the smaller man across the ring. X flies into the corner and collapses after his back slams into the turn buckles. Dimensioner slowly begins to get up to his feet, as the camera cuts to the entrance way where another newcomer Maverick stands watching the action in the ring.

Pete: Who the hell is that big goon supposed to be?

Jhub: Careful Pete, he might hear you.

Bryan: That’s another new recruit to GCW the huge Maverick. He’s clearly out here to take a peek at the action maybe scout his future opponents.

Dimensioner turns right into a spinning heel kick from X, staggering the big man into the turnbuckle. X mounts the ropes and starts pummeling Dimensioner with hard right hands, but Dimensioner shoves him away. Dimensioner charges with a clothesline, but X ducks and springboards off the ropes, landing a short moonsault that takes Dimensioner off his feet.

Bryan: Wow! Impressive move there from X, and Dimensioner is in trouble here!

Jhub: Looks like another loss for Dimensioner. Maybe I’ll get in the ring with him next week.

Dimensioner staggers back up as X mounts the turnbuckle and dives off, but Dimensioner cuts him down with a brutal clothesline out of the air. Dimensioner pulls X up by the hair and drills a couple hard right hands into the side of the man’s skull. Lifting him up in the air like a rag doll, he tosses the kid back into the corner, further damaging his back. Stomping on the smaller man a few times for good measure, Dimensioner finally pulls the kid back up to his feet, lifts him up further and DRIVES him down across his knee with End of Reality.

Pete: HA! Told ya. See, one two three, and it’s over.

Bryan: Indeed, Dimensioner with a big win here, basically destroying the debuting X.

Jhub: Never mind, I don’t think I’ll be facing him any time soon.

Bryan: Fans, Dimensioner proved to be a bit too much for X here tonight, but watch this kid, he’s going to get better as the weeks go on!

Introducing… -by Mav

The camera opens with Kevin Jacobs standing in a dark room within the arena. Next to him is a guy in a white cloak and the hood draped around his shoulders not covering his head, and the man himself Maverick, who is towering over Jacobs, like a dark shadow.

Jacobs: Well I am here with The Druid, manager of the GCW newcomer Maverick, welcome here to the GCW Maverick & Druid!

Druid: It is our pleasure to be here!!

Jacobs looks pretty nervous as he looks at Maverick who is just standing there with no expression on his face and his cold eyes fixed onto the face of Kevin Jacobs.

Jacobs: Maverick you have signed a contract with GCW and this is being your first night here on World Wide, are you looking forward to tonight even though you don’t have a match this week??

Maverick doesn’t say anything and The Druid is quick to answer.

Druid: Maverick doesn’t speak much Mr Jacobs, I am basically his mouth as well as his manger here in GCW, but yes I can tell you Maverick is looking forward to being a part of GCW just like myself. He does what he does when I say and ONLY! When I say, but I do think even though this is our first night here tonight with this company we will hit an impact here tonight Mr Jacobs I can assure you that.

Jacobs: Err! . What exactly are your goals here Maverick??

Maverick still stands there motionless.

Druid: Mavericks goal is to put on the hurt, to destroy anyone who gets in his way. Yes I want to see this evil human being become the GCW Champion one day in the future but whoever the champion is when Mavericks time comes better be well prepared as he is not the sort of guy who loses in a match.

Kevin Jacobs wipes the sweat from his forehead with a tissue.

Jacobs: Err … May I ask why Maverick hasn’t stopped looking at me since this interview began??

Druid: I can see he is drawing a sweat on your forehead Mr Jacobs, and I can see in your eyes just how nervous you actually are at this moment in time. Look into the eyes of hell Mr Jacobs … you look very closely, you can see just how evil this man is, you can see just how much he is going to take this company by storm, and most of all Mr Jacobs you ……

Quickly Kevin Jacobs drops the microphone and runs out of the room in a panic.

Druid looks up at Maverick and says.

Druid: See how you struck fear into his heart Maverick, you see how afraid he was of you, it is only a matter of time until you get blood on your hands, just be patient, it will come!!

Druid looks where Jacobs just ran.

Druid: Oh yes it will come!!

Welcome to the Machine -by Tony~!

"Machinehead" by Bush hits over the PA and the crowd turns towards the entraceway wondering what's going on as this isn't the music of anyone they know. The song plays for a few moments before a lone figure steps onto the stage and walks to the top of the ramp. A slight smirk on his face as he looks to the left and then the right surveying the crowd from behind a pair of mirrored shades. He's decked out in a leather jacket, a Johnny The Homicidal Maniac shirt, black jeans and black Sketchers. He raises his eyebrows and shakes his head slightly and then heads to the ring, ignoring the hands that are clamoring for a high five.

Bryan: Well I'm not sure what's going on here. This isn't on my program.

Pete: Don't look at me, I'm just as confused as you are.

On his way to the ring the back of his jacket can be seen. It's got a airbrused image of a half Terminator face, and the other half is that of the man wearing the jacket. He walks up the steps and pauses on the ring apron, again pausing to look over the crowd, and he chuckles a bit to himself before stepping into the ring. He walks across with a practiced ease and asks for a mic. Once he has it he steps to the middle of the ring and draws his hand across his neck in a "Cut the music" gesture and the song quickly fades. He waits for a moment for the confused murmuring of the crowd to die down.

Man: Now I'm sure you're all wondering who I am and what I'm doing here.

Bryan: You got that right pal! We got a wrestling show to run here!

Man: My name, as you will all get to know, is Mike "The Machine" Savige, and I have come to save GCW!

Pete: Save GCW? What the heck is he talking about? This company doesn't need saving! Who is this guy?

Savige: Save it from what you may ask? Save it from mediocrity. Save it from the doldrums. Save it from the status quo! I have come to drag this company up from the bowels of boredom to make it the top notch, five star company it can be! And how? Simply by being myself. You see, unlike most wrestlers who slap a fancy name onto their own name, I didn't nickname myself the Machine. No, that name was given to me when other promotions saw what I could bring to the table. A mix of technical know how, and the ability to roll with the changes. The ability to adjust at the drop of a hat, or in this business, the drop of a body.

He pauses and paces around the ring for a few seconds before taking off his shades and tucking them into an inside pocket of his jacket. He raises the mic again.

Savige: I have come to save you, the fans from the same old boring wrestlers and matches. I mean really, how many times can you watch Rich Rollins, Mike Hardy, The Jhub, Michael Stevens or Twisted? Let's face it, I'm the best thing to hit this company since...since...

He stands there for a minute gazing off into the distance looking confused, then shakes his head and waves his arm as it shooing away a fly.

Savige: ...hell, since this company opened!

This earns the ire of the fans since he just dismissed the entire history of the company in one sentence. Especially since his previous sentence dismissed five of the top wrestlers in the company, and most of these fans have been following the GCW since the first incarnation back in 2001.

Savige: You see, I'm going to do something that no one else has been able to do. I'm going to give you a reason to get off your asses and come to the shows every week, I'm gonna give you a reason to sit on your fat asses at home and turn on the TV. I'm gonna give you a reason to buy pay-per-views! I'm going to bring entertainment back to GCW. I'm going to deliver matches that you're going to want to watch. Simply put, I'm going to make not just GCW, but wrestling fun again.

The fans are really giving him what for now. Booing and a few random chants of "Ass-hole!" have started. He seems not to hear it, or if he does, he doesn't seem to care. He pushes on before the chants and jeers even have a chance to reach a fever pitch.

Savige: I can promise you that in the coming weeks, you will see what a real wrestler is like, what a real wrestler can do for this company. How I plan to breathe life into this company and make it great. In short, I'm going to add a touch of class to this company and take it from good to great!

He paces again, still ignoring the jeers of the crowd, who have now decided that this guy in the ring is nothing more than an aorrogant jerk.

Savige: This is not GCW saying welcoming Mike Savige. This is Mike Savige saying to GCW "Welcome to the Machine!"

With that "Machinehead" starts back up and Mike walks to the ropes, puts down the mic and jumps from the ring, heading back up the ramp smirking as the fans along the rampway jeer and yell at him. He seems to revel in their hatred and stops to point and laugh at a fan who only gets more animated in his jeering of The Machine.

Bryan: Well, he certianly didn't read "How to Win Friends and Influence People"!

Mike pauses at the top of the ramp, turns back to the crowd and bows, tossing off a quick salute and disappears backstage, the smirk still firmly planted on his face.

The Favor -by Carrie

The live audience goes wild with mingled boos and loud cheers as the image of one Eva VanLorne shows up on the megatron. As usual that davastating GCW diva is dressed in all white. Tonight her gear was more scandalous than usual. The low riding white lycra short showing off that shapely rear as the camera followed her path to Lestatt Knight's locker room. Without knocking she presses open the door and peeks in.

Eva: LeStatt?

LeStatt turns his head slightly as he sits on the floor with his back turned to where Eva is standing now. He is stretching out, obviously just keeping himself ready.

LeStatt: Que pasa Eva?

Eva's brow perks with that well known interest as she slinks inside and saunters around to other side of him for a better view.

Eva: Hey lover. I have a favor to ask....( lowering her lashes coyly as she settles on the bench before him and rests her elbows to get more face level with him) That is if you aren't ~too~ busy later?

LeStatt brushes the hair from his face to uncover his icy blue eyes. An eyebrow lifts up and then a soft chuckle escapes his lips.

LeStatt: No Not busy Eva, just finishing some stretching. What can I do for you senorita?

She smiles in that saucy manner at him as she lowers herself from the bench to straddle his hips. Arms wrap around his neck as she kisses the side of it and pulls back to look him in the face.

Eva: ( chuckling huskily) With my brother unable to be here with me I have no one to escort me to the ring. I was thinking with me being the US champ and you being the king of the ring at this time...( giving him that cute pout) maybe you would be so kind as to escort your lady to the ring? Exposure never hurt anyone.

LeStatt: Is that what you had in mind? If I didn't know any better Eva I'd say you were using me to get your name even further into the papers. ( He now gives a devilish grin. ) Tell me, if I agree to this exactly what is it that I am getting out of this?

Eva grins mischievously at his question to her motives. Wiggling in his lap to get closer she leans in to murmur against his ear.

Eva: Oh Lestatt, you ~know~ I will make it worth your while. Please....for me? ( Pouty batting of the lashes)

LeStatt: Well ... I guess tonight is your lucky night since I don't have a real threat to my title tonight. I can walk down that aisle with you, give those damn fans something to boo about. BUT ... I am sure you can handle Rollins alone right?

Eva snorts lightly at the mention of Rollins. Maneuvering about to lock her legs around his waist she purrs.

Eva: Rollins is a chump not a champ. I have handled bigger men than him. Besides...I can handle you can't I? I mean....if you catch my drift? ( winks at him)

LeStatt: Comparing me to Rollins is like comparing Batman to Robin. Sure Robin was popular but he was nothing more than a side character in the grand scheme of things. Besides ... if you handle Rollins tonight you MIGHT get the chance to really HANDLE me ... good luck Hermosa.

Grinning she goes in for the lengthy kiss causing the men to scream boos at LeStatt and cat call her to kiss them like that. Pulling away she climbs out of his lap and gets up heading for the door. At the door she turns and winks at him.

Eva: LeStatt, darling, the only place I want to handle you is in the bedroom. Speaking of which...I will see you after the show.

LeStatt watches as Eva walks out of the room and disappears.

LeStatt: Women ...

He chuckles again now and goes back to his stretching. Back to the show!

Singles Match – One Fall

Jonathan Borealis vs. Twisted -by Ben

“Life’s Sweet Drug” hits overhead in the arena, and a mixed reaction greets Twizzy. Smiling and clutching a small orange bottle, Twizzy stumbles towards the ring, seeming caught off guard by the volume of the reaction.

Andrews: The following contest is set for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 241 pounds, Twizzy!

Bryan: Welcome back, fans, and we’re at ringside for our next bout! Former World Champion Twizzy looks to get back on his winning ways against the former TV Champion Jonathan Borealis.

Pete: And Borealis is livid, guys, at the loss of that belt last week. Twizzy had better watch out in this one.

Twizzy sets the bottle in the corner and rolls under the ring, sitting in the opposite corner to wait for his opponent. “Rabble Rouser” cues up in the arena and the crowd cheers as the former TV Champion comes rushing down the ramp! Borealis hits the ring and springs to his feet, not wasting any time.

Bryan: The bell sounds, and Borealis charges! The former TV Champ is fired up and wants a win badly here tonight!

Borealis leaps across the ring, catching Twizzy unawares. Twizzy slumps back into the corner as Borealis snaps off relentless right hands, pummeling the former world champion. Twizzy finally manages to sneak out the back door and answer with a headbutt, but Borealis shrugs it off and slings Twizzy right back into the corner, continuing with the hard rights!

Bryan: Wow! I've never seen Borealis this aggressive before!

Pete: He's taking out a lot of frustration here from losing the TV title last week, and you'd better believe a win over Twizzy puts him right back in the title picture!

Jhub: But what does this mean for Twizzy? Does he even know what's going on?

Twizzy loses his footing and drops to the mat, instantly nailing Borealis with a sharp headbutt to the lower midsection. The crowd groans as Borealis slumps over and stumbles out of the corner, allowing Twizzy to stand.

Bryan: OOH! Dangerously low shot there from Twizzy.

Pete: That's why I don't wrestle any more.

Twizzy darts out of the corner, but Borealis twists him into a massive powerslam! The crowd explodes as Borealis hooks a leg! One... Two... Twizzy kicks out. Borealis gets back to his feet and hits the ropes again, leaping over the still fallen Twizzy. Twizzy hops up as Borealis bounces back, and Twizzy drops Borealis with a hard clothesline. Borealis staggers back up, only to catch a hard vertical suplex from Twizzy. Twizzy snaps back up and dives into the corner onto his belly, snatching up his Twistodium bottle and popping off the top.

Jhub: Oh, come on, Twizzy, focus on the damn match.

Pete: Hey now, Jhub, the fans used to love Twizzy for that stuff!

Jhub: But it's getting out of hand! The guy can't focus, he can't even stand up straight. I hope Borealis puts him out of his misery here.

Twizzy shoves a few white pills in his mouth, stands, and turns right into a massive standing sidekick from Borealis. The crowd cheers once again as Twizzy crawls into the corner, where Borealis catches him by the ankle. Twizzy scrambles up and grasps the top rope with both hands as Borealis pulls back on his feet, trying to tear him away. Borealis pulls Twizzy off the mat but Twizzy hangs on to the top rope for dear life!

Bryan: Borealis is desperately trying to pull Twizzy out of the corner, but he's got a death-grip on that top rope!

Pete: He's just trying to get away! I don't think Twizzy expected this out of Borealis!

Borealis gives up on trying to pull Twizzy away, and instead props Twizzy's legs over his shoulders and steps in, suddenly spinning in place and driving Twizzy face first into the mat with a vicious inverted powerbomb! The crowd explodes in cheers as Borealis flips him over for the cover.

Bryan: What a move by Borealis! Reverse powerbomb into a cover!

Pete: ONE! TWO! Kickout by Twizzy!

Jhub: I may not like the guy, but you've got to give Twizzy credit for kicking out of that one.

Borealis pulls himself back up with a confident smile on his face, and mounts the turnbuckle. With his hands on his hips he poses to the crowd, earning a huge pop for his trouble. Borealis then hops to the top and flips back with a big moonsault! Borealis soars through the air but comes up empty, as Twizzy rolls out of the way. Borealis bounces off the canvas and Twizzy instantly rolls him over, draping an arm over his chest.

Bryan: One, TWO... Borealis kicks out after that failed moonsault attempt, and Twizzy did have the presence of mind to make a cover there.

Pete: I don't get this guy, JB. Sometimes he can't even stand up straight or remember his own name, and sometimes he shows flashes of being a great ring general like that. This guy really needs to get his act together.

Jhub: He should go ask Trey Tremendous for the name of a good rehab clinic.

Borealis is the first to his feet, with Twizzy up behind him, favoring his... nose. Twizzy turns right into a hard knife-edge chop from Borealis that staggers him back into the ropes. Borealis charges but Twizzy drops his head and tosses Borealis over the top rope! Borealis hooks the top rope on his way over and twists in mid-air, slinging himself feet-first back through the ropes, catching Twizzy with a hard kick to the kidneys as he slides gracefully back into the ring!

Bryan: OH, what a move! That was an amazing display of control and agility there from Johnny Borealis!

The crowd explodes with cheers as Borealis poses again, flashing a bright smile. Borealis heads back for the top rope again, waiting for Twizzy to rise. Twizzy slowly pulls himself back up, but suddenly shoves the referee into the ropes, causing Borealis to lose his balance and crash to the turnbuckle.

Pete: Dirty move from Twizzy! What's gotten into this guy, JB?

Jhub: Must be a bad dose of Twistodium. Listen to these people now, guys! They recognize what's happened to Twizzy! They're booing him, and he deserves it!

Twizzy, confused by the boos, staggers about in a dizzy circle, trying to get his bearings. He finally sees Borealis seated on the top rope and begins to climb in front of him, hooking him in a front facelock. Twizzy attempts a superplex, but Borealis holds on tight. Borealis begins throwing quick jabs to Twizzy's gut, breaking the hold, and then steps back to the top rope. Borealis dives and pulls Twizzy into a sunset flip, powerbombing him off the top rope into a pin!

Bryan: GREAT MOVE! ONE! TWO! ANOTHER KICKOUT!

Pete: Sheesh! Where is Twizzy getting this?

Bryan: I don't have a damn clue, but Johnny Borealis is beginning to get a bit frustrated here!

Borealis stands and kicks the bottom rope in frustration, but closes his eyes and calms himself. He opens them with a confident smile back on his face, and he pulls Twizzy back up. Borealis whips Twizzy to the ropes but Twizzy counters, and Borealis bounces back with a shoulder block. Neither man leaves his feet, and they lock eyes, each with a goofy smile on their face. Borealis bounces back into the ropes and charges again, but this time Twizzy ducks and hooks Borealis from behind, nailing him with a modified Trash Compactor!

Bryan: OH NO! Twizzy hit his old move, and Borealis is out!

Jhub: That move hurts like hell, guys, that's gotta be it for poor Johnny Boy.

Twizzy stands and looks down at Borealis, scratching his head as if trying to remember what to do next.

She worked her mouth trying in vain to speak as that beautiful bloodied creature curled up into a ball and just screamed a scream so long and so loud it could almost shatter eardrums. She was in shock, confusion was evident in her eyes as she began to rock back and forth despite badly bruised ribs.

Eva: Erik....Petre

She would just repeat the name over and over again.

LeStatt looked confused, bewildered, and frustrated now. He stands up now and begins backing away as medical personnel rush to the scene. He doesn't stop backing up until he runs into RaZiel. RaZiel's large hand comes down on his shoulder.

RaZiel: What happened LeStatt?

LeStatt: I ... I ... Don't know ...

As the medics rush into to surround Eva she begins screaming at them not to touch her as a blanket is tossed around her shoulders to cover her ravaged form. She struggles weakly as a shot is administered. Admist all the chaos, other wrestlers begin spilling onto the scene including a newly arrived Erik Vanlorne looking the worse for wear himself.

Erik: What the hell is all the commotion abou-

HE freezes as he catches a glimpse of Eva's form being lifted by one of the larger medics onto a gurney.

Erik: EVA!! ( his enraged gaze turns to Lestatt) What the hell did you do to her!?

He rushes to Eva's side hands clasping onto hers as he continues to scream at everybody in his sights.

Erik: WHAT DID YOU DO TO HER!?

LeStatt's stare goes cold now, as if he had nothing but hate and anger inside. He takes a couple steps forward but is stopped by RaZiel. Knight refrains from attacking Erik and instead just calls out.

LeStatt: Erik when you can tell us who "Petre" is then maybe someone can answer your question.

Knight's voice was flat and emotionless but something about it ws eerie. RaZiel begins pulling LeStatt back trying to get him to just walk away at this point.

All cameras pan to Erik's face as it suddenly loses all color. He stood there stunned for a moment staring at Lestatt with a startled look in his eyes before they shoot down to Eva as the gurney is shoved towards a newly arrived ambulance. She is hoisted inside as Erik is shoved away, the doors closing her in. Sirens begin to wail as the ambulance slowly makes it way through the gathering crowd of wrestling and security staff alike.

Erik: ( staring at Lestatt) Petre......it couldn't have been. I was..

Suddenly an aid appears, and from behind comes a running Steven Caldera. Caldera spies Eva on the ground and grows wide-eyed, scouring the scene for a clue as to what happened. Completely shocked and at a loss, Caldera charges the cameraman.

Caldera: Kill that camera right now!
