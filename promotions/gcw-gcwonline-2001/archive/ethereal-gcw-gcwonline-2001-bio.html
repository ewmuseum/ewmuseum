Ethereal Biography Real Name Skylar McGovern Hometown Brookeville, Maryland Birthday 09/24/1981 Height 6'0 Weight 245 Biography At age fourteen Skylar McGovern had developed a peculiar knack for solving mystery. Four years later Skylar met a fork in the road. He could either attend the University of Maryland and study criminal education, or he could bypass collegiate education and accept a job with a Baltimore based law enforcement firm. Placed in the detective agency, Skylar was taken under the wing of established detective, Kevin Johnson. With the guidance of Detective Johnson, Skylar thrived in his role within the firm. Within one year of his first assignment Skylaw was a statewide renowned detective, with a reputation for speedy resolution and a unorthodox attention to detail. At age twenty-two, however, after three years of unfaltering success, the tables turned. With word of Skylar's abilities traveling to detective agencies across the country, he was attracting more and more attention. In the winter of '02 Skylar recieved a summons to the scene of a crime in New York City. Eager to increase stature as a detective, Skylar accepted the summons and arrived in New York only five hours later. As he approached the warning tape, he was informed by the chief of police that it was a double homicide with no clear motive. Having heard this hundreds of time before Skylar stepped over the tape without hesitation. He approached the body with the chalk line screaming confidence, but as he set eyes upon the corpse, he immediately emptied his stomach. On the ground with glazed eyes unclosed, lay Skylar's longtime friend and mentor, Luke Glaze, whom Skylar had touched base with only days before. Years before, when Luke went north to attend school at New York University, a vow was exchanged between the two friends. Regardless of the distance that was ever between them, they would remain close. Death however, was another story. With the image of his dead friend's mangled body and distorted face scorching his retinas, Skylar stepped back over the caution tape, without even a glance at the second corpse. Not only was he leaving the scene of crime, he was ending his career as a detective. A month later Skylar was informed that the crime was solved and a motive was discovered. Luke was killed over a drunken fight about college football. The killer, a drunken NYU fratboy, had broken every bone in Luke's face. After pouring half a bottle of Jack Daniels into his already alcohol-littered body, the frat boy, C.J Gibson, died of alcohol poisoning. Learning that his best friend's death was caused by such a trivial matter, however, brought no rest to Skylar. Feeling guilty for having no remorse for any of his past cases, Skylar spent the next year researching his past cases, and learning the stories of the people involved in each one. Bothered by the cruelty he was subjected to over the course of his fairly short lived, Skylar discovered a violent side to his personality. After a few bar fights, Skylar realized that these bar fights gone wrong were exactly the kind of thing that called his attention years ago. Skylar hung a punching bag in one of his apartments, and took his frustrations out on that. An old friend introduced him to wrestling, as a prospect of taking out his frustrations, so he contacted Mr. Caldera...
