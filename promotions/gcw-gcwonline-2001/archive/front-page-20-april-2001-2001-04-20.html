News/Notes- 4-19-01- Again, I apologize for the lack of a real card this week. However, Oblivion will be a real card, which will be up Sunday Morning. Then, that night, Warpath will go up, and it will be INCREDIBLE. I promise. The roster is updated, as well. 4-18-01- My sincere apologies to everyone who has worked hard for this last Meltdown before the PPV, but due to a hectic schedule, I'm going to have to write Meltdown in summary form. I will do my best to get the real copy out by tonight, maybe tomorrow. So you can read the results now, or hold out until then. But Warpath WILL be done on time and in whole, so get ready for a great card! 4-17-01- Oblivion begins this Sunday. It will be posted that morning, followed by Warpath that night. 4-15-01- Caldera and I have come to the concluson that, it would be easier if everyone got AOL Instant Messenger. We don't require that everyone gets it, but if you do, here is how you do. Go to www.aol.com, and scroll down until you see the People and Chat section. Click on the AIM icon and it will take you to another page. Scroll down on this page until you see a button that says "Get it Now". Click on this. It will bring you to the name screen. To make it easier for us to communicate, we want your name to be formatted as followed: {Wrestler Name} GCW. Here is an example. I am John Reed so my name is John Reed GCW. After you have it downloaded, send both me, [detail withheld],and Caldera, [detail withheld],an email statin your wrestler and your name. Thanx. 4-15-01- Sorry we've been out of the loop, but it is Easter, after all. Anyway, things will pick back up now. The first pay-Per-View is only a week away, so everyone needs to get cooking to make it a great card. 4-11-01- Meltdown Results up. Udated Meltdown Card. 4-7-01- I added a Title History Page, check it out. 4-5-01- Changed the graphic on the top of the page. Added anew poll. 4-5-01- Next Wednesday's card is updated. It's shaping up nicely, if I do say so myself. If anyone would like to get involved in something for the PPV, e-mail President Caldera. I want the PPV to be huge, ad if you want a part in it and are not already scheduled for a match, let me know and we'll think something up.
