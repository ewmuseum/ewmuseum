WorldWide 85 Maishima Arena — Osaka ---- Introduction WorldWide opens with a simple shot of Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins: 1978-2008. His image fades into the grim faces of James Bryan and David Yale sitting at ringside. No fancy introductions, no pyrotechnics, just the announce crew.
