Christian Zenith Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Prodigy, The Heir To New York Hometown Manhattan, New York City, New York Birthday July 12, 1982 Height 6' 5 Weight 254 lbs Federation Awards N/A Biography ABOUT CHRISTIAN... Grew up in Chicago as the only son of world famous billionaire Jake Barringer. Attended Trinity School in Manhattan. Member of Skull & Bones. Graduated with honors from Yale University with a degree in business management. Heir to Zenith Corporation who spends more of his time partying and pursuing eccentric hobbies (such as wrestling and MMA) than in the office, but is still viewed on the world stage as a respected and brilliant businessman, often referred to as the bright future of American business. Admitted Freemason. Accused Illuminati. Fluent in English, Spanish, French, German, Mandarin, and Japanese. A PSYCHOLOGICAL EXPLORATION... The super-confident exterior of Christian Zenith often hides a brilliant mind. As a Yale honors graduate, his work ethic and superior intelligence compensate for whatever physical shortcomings and lack of natural ability he may have. A quick learner with the best one-on-one training that money could ever buy, Christian is a formidable opponent in his own right. Wrestling, like life, is a mind game in which he has a superior advantage in manipulating the thoughts and emotions of his opponent to his will. While his professionalism is sometimes lacking, his OCD tendencies tend to make him a super-organized planner who would run in terror at the thought of anarchy. He's a natural charmer with undeniable charisma and a smooth talker who places his words as well as the categorized suits in his closet. He's the type of man who could talk a fish into buying a drink, and is a born winner. Losing in any aspect just doesn't cut it in the face of his astronomical expectations.
