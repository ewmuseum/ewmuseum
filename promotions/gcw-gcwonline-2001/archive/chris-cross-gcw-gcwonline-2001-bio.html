Chris Cross Biography Real Name Christopher Cross Nicknames "God's gift to Wrestling"; "The Innovator of Awesome" Hometown Chicago, Ill. Birthday Feb. 24th, 1975 Height 220lbs Weight 6ft, 0inchs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Chris Cross grew up in a well-to-do middle class family in the suburbs of Chicago, where he lived with his mother and father, in a happy and healthy home. Chris grew up happy, reaching 10th grade before life began to fall apart. Soon his father found his way to cocaine and major drug addictions and Chris Cross was without a father, due to overdose, and a mother whose life was shaken by the lose of her spouse. Chris left home at seventeen, and found a shelter in his local friend, and aspiring wrestler's home. After months of living under the roof of this friend, Chris found an interest, and soon developed skills. After leaving school, Chris and his new found best friend began to study under local famed wrestlers, and soon broke into the business, wrestling in local gymnasiums in the New Found Wrestling Alliance, which soon fell, leaving Chris with nothing but a paycheck that had bounced. Chris worked at fast food establishments, and moved furniture to pay for his wrestling classes, and once again broke into the business, this time in Golden Championship Wrestling, with his friend Starr. After a successful run in Golden Championship Wrestling, both tag team with Starr, and singles division, the GCW feel, and it again left Chris with a void to fill, which he did with the Non-Stop Wresting Alliance. After two top-notch years in the NSWA, and yet he had made no progress championship wise, making a middle-class title; the Middle-Weight Title, famous, Chris and Starr both walked away from what made them household names. Soon enough, the void again needed filling, this time with a little-known federation; the One World Coalition. Here Starr and Chris Cross parted ways, in a horribly sad ending, in which Chris Cross put Starr through a table which was light on fire; ending Starr's short-lived career in OWC. Chris Cross however went on to win the World Title, and form new friendships which are alive to this day...how ever OWC is not. After the closing of OWC, Chris Cross has turned too Global Championship Wrestling to fill that ever present void in his life! Chris Cross vows to become the biggest star GCW has ever seen; and he will! Other Feds Extreme Wrestling Entertainment: United States Title, International Title. One World Coalition: Orignal One Title, World Title, United America Title Strengths His confidence. When he knows it's over, he gains confidence, and begins to destroy his opponents with amazing moves! Weaknesses His confidence. When Chris Cross thinks it's over...it might not be and that can land him in major trouble! Wrestling Style High Flying; Brawler Strategy It's an all and out brawl if Chris Cross knows he doesn't have his opponent beat before the match starts. If Chris is confident, he may begin to show off his athletic side, which he has for being a smaller person in hight. A Missal Drop kick is merely the tamest areal assault Chris Cross has in his arsenal. Expect to see high flying DDTs and neck breakers. If Chris Cross knows he can win, don't blink for a second, because you'll miss some amazing moves! Finisher Move The Cross Fire Finisher Description Chris Cross reverses an Irish Whip, ducking an aerial move, and tucking his opponent's jaw under Chris' right arm, Chris flips froward, holding his opponent with his left hand across the back, landing in a side-slam, after turning through the air 360 degress Other Moves -The Cross Out Sometimes referred to as a Spinning Sidewalk Slam, Chris Cross has adjusted the move to call his own. Chris grips his opponent by the throat, and twisting his own body, causes the opponent to fly through the air in a 180 degree motion, both left and right, before Chris slams his opponent to the mat. -Super Kick -Inverted 180 DDT -Cradle Powerdriver Ring Entrances The lights in the arena quickly dim as "Wherever I May Roam" begins to play over the public announce system, and a large stream of pyrotechnics explodes from the turnbuckles and both sides of the stage, filling the top of the entrance stage with smoke, which Chris Cross emerges from. Chris walks down the ramp slowly, as pyrotechnics again explode behind him, creating a silhouetted image of himself. As he enters the ring, the smoke clears, and Chris stands on the nearest apron, jumping down, as his feet hit the mat, again pyrotechnics explode.
