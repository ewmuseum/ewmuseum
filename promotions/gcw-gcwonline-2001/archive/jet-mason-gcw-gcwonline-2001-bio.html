Jet Mason Biography Real Name Jet Mason Nicknames None Hometown Cornelia, Georgia Birthday August, 7th 1987 Height 5'11" Weight 208 lb Biography Jet Mason (born August 7th, 1987) is an American professional wrestler, known in ring by his real name, Jet Mason. Mason currently competes in several independent promotions, most notably New Osaka Wrestling (NOW), where he is the current NOW Jr. Heavyweight Champion. Mason also has wrestled for promotions such as Great Lakes Wrestling (GLW), and Southern Wrestling Alliance (SWA). He initially debuted as Tex Mason Jr. in the promotion Southern Wrestling Alliance, teaming with his father Tex Mason. He wrestled for SWA until his fathers retirement before leaving to GLW in 2004. In mid-2004, Jet left GLW and traveled to Japan, wrestling for the upstart independent league known as New Osaka Wrestling. In 2005 he joined forces with NOW legend and ex-Jr. Heavyweight Champion Noroshi Yamatsu to form the masked tag-team called The Masked Riders. As part of The Masked Riders, Mason and Yamatsu wore masks to hide their identity. Yamatsu later turned on Mason, forming a new team with American professional wrestler Johnny Marvelous, revealing Masked Rider #1’s true identity. The two clashed at NOW’s flagship Pay-Per-View titled No Turning Back where Mason and Yamatsu wrestled in what NOW fans believe to be one of the promotions best matches of 2006. After the death of Debra Mason, Jet took leave of absence from professional wrestling for eighteen months. In the down time he spent time with his then girlfriend and manager, Lindsey Calibre (Lindsey Carmini) and once again trained under his father at SWA. 2007 saw Jet return to NOW and rekindle his friendship with Yamatsu. The two went on to team up and form a stable with fellow NOW star FREDO called New Era. After the stable disbanded in 2009, Jet went on to pursue a singles career. On April 2nd, 2010 Jet Mason defeated The Lone Dragon for the NOW Jr. Heavyweight Title. On September 29th of the same year Mason dropped the title to Yamatsu, and left the organization. Mason was born in Atlanta, Georgia but relocated to Blue Ridge when he was three years old. Following his father around the nation, and later the world, Jet received an in-home tutor for his schooling. He received his high school diploma from Southeast Atlanta High at the tender age of fifteen. Following his fathers footsteps, Jet began his training under his father and SWA great Larry Titus. After very few months of training, Jet had grasped a contract with the federation in 2004; when Tex Mason retired from professional wrestling due to injury and prostate cancer. Currently, Tex Mason is hospitalized at Saint Joseph’s Hospital in Atlanta.
