GCW — Card

WorldWide 2, 16 February 2005

16 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WorldWide 2 American Airlines Center, Dallas, Texas ---- Introduction With a low rumble in the background, the GCW logo flashes on screen. Moments later, a spinning globe fades onto the screen, as the faces of several GCW superstars flash in and out in the background. Finally, a view of the American Airlines Center in Dallas appears, and the ring explodes into flames! The lights in the arena come up and cameras start panning wildly across the screaming Dallas crowd, as Wake Up blasts in the background. Pyro explodes on stage in a fantastic display, and the familiar voice of James Bryan kicks in on cue. Bryan- We’re in the sold out American Airlines center in Dallas, Texas tonight for another edition of GCW’s WorldWide! Welcome back everyone, I’m James Bryan being accompanied at ringside by the President of Global Championship Wrestling, Steven Caldera! Caldera- Thanks very much, JB! It’s good to be back in Dallas, a place I used to call home, and I can’t help but be excited about tonight. Bryan- Well, we’ve got another tremendous show lined up! Tonight, the Hardcore title is on the line, we’ve been promised a few new debuts, and Tristain Mayhem might finally make his return to the ring! Caldera- I talked to The Jhub earlier tonight, and he’s anxious to get in there with Mayhem. And, frankly, I’m interested in seeing if Mayhem’s still as good as he seems to think he is. The camera stops with the ring in focus, finding that it is carpeted in red and surrounded by hulking men in plain black shirts. In the center of the ring is an odd black podium, about waist high, covered in a red silk cloth. Bryan- But to kick things off tonight, fans, as we get a look at the rather odd setup in the ring, former three time GCW champion Octavian is going to unveil his new title belt! Caldera- I have no idea what Gaius is thinking here, JB. I take this as a slap in the face, because the arrogant bastard sure as hell never asked my permission to do this. He’s welcome to carry whatever belt he wants, but rest assured it is not recognized by this company and it certainly doesn’t make him champion of the world! ---- The Imperial Roman Championship ‘Might of Rome’ slowly fades into the AAC, and the Dallas crowd is instantly hot. Without pause Octavian emerges onto the stage, clad in a tremendous suit and an expensive pair of sunglasses. The smile on his face is tremendous as he ignores the massive crowd and heads for the ring. Andrews- Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome Octavian! Bryan- Well here comes Octavian, and even you have to admit, Mr. President, that Octavian looks great. Caldera- No doubt. He’s put on some weight, he’s in great shape, I can’t deny that. His suit isn’t as nice as mine, though. Bryan- Of course. Octavian steps through the ropes and approaches the podium in the center of the ring. Octavian reaches into his jacket and produces a jewel-encrusted gold microphone, prompting a scoff from Caldera at the commentator’s table. Octavian removes his sunglasses and slips them into his breast pocket before speaking in his Roman accent. Octavian- You know, I had originally wanted to do this in Rome, so that my new belt would not become encrusted with a thick layer of Dallas filth… The crowd explodes in boos, and several noticeable expletives can be heard amid the noise. Octavian- … But this is something that simply must be done. For anyone that isn’t aware, last week I came to this ring and demanded that President Caldera give me back the World Title I never lost! Octavian turns and glares at Caldera, but Caldera simply grins back. Octavian- But of course, he wouldn’t listen to reason. Now, in a little over a month, I will be at ACWF to take back the world title that is rightfully mine. But a man with my power is not accustomed to waiting for things. So I had this belt made. I feel it reflects my power, my wealth, and all the things I have… that all you people don’t. The crowd boos as Octavian swiftly removes the cloth covering the podium, revealing an amazing belt below. The crowd actually gasps at the sight of the belt resting gloriously on the pedestal. Made of solid gold and bordered in platinum, the belt sparkles in the lights. The center of the belt is spackled with diamonds, and written in perfect script at the bottom are the words ‘Imperial Roman Championship.’ Bryan- WOW! Look at that! I’ve got to admit I’m impressed! Caldera- The man’s greed knows no bounds, JB. Octavian- It’s worth over five million dollars, US. That’s why I’ve invited my friends here to circle the ring and protect my new investment. Of course, once it goes around my waist, nobody will EVER remove it. So, you all have the privilege of witnessing the crowning of the first ever Imperial Roman Champion… Octavian! The crowd boos as Octavian takes the belt in his hands, his arms drooping from the weight. He slowly wraps the belt around his waist and secures it, before turning back to the crowd to pose. Octavian- Now, as Imperial Roman Champion, I must issue my demand to President Caldera to give me a spot in ACWF- As Octavian finishes the end of his sentence, immediately Just Got Wicked by Cold hits over the PA system, and the fans give a largely mixed reaction! Out from the back steps Jason Dread, microphone in hand. Inside the ring Octavian starts shouting at the guards which surround him angrily, his exact words lost in the music. Dread slowly starts walking down the ramp to the ring while he speaks, the music fading to silence. Dread- Octavian, Octavian, Octavian...it's good to see that for the SECOND week in a row you've managed to either bore the living hell out of everyone out here, or just flat out put us to sleep! Naturally, a pop arises from the crowd, irritating Octavian even more. Octavian- Listen here- Dread- I'm NOT finished! Surprised by the interruption, Octavian refuses to retort, and instead slowly drops the microphone away from his mouth to listen for the moment. Dread- You know what I find funny besides your obviously outdated banter, your disturbingly old features, and the fact that you haven't bathed in anything other than goat piss in the past 3 years... This elicits a good response from the crowd, whom starts laughing at Octavian's expense. Dread- ...is the fact that no one cares anymore about your problems with Caldera and your Boo hoo, I didn't lost my title cry, cry, cry, bitch, bitch, bitch, moan, moan, SHUT THE *Beep* UP! A HUGE pop comes from the crowd. At this point, Dread is standing outside of the ring, and begins climbing up the ring ropes. Octavian's guards cautiously watch his every move. Dread- Maybe you've forgotten this, but you aren't the only person who has ever had a title lost by reasons out of their control. Let me share a little story about what happened to me two years ago, when I was a bigger man than you will ever be. You see, at that time I was the number one man in GCW...I had it all. The World Title, a group a strong-willed fighters under me, and the man with all the control of the company in my hands... Octavian- Let me stop you right there, Jason. I remember that perfectly! You were on the top...then you hit rock bottom when Caldera did the same thing to you that he did to me. He lost your belt and sent you packing. Haha...those were greats times, Jason. Dread's confident smile changed to a deep scowl, as he now stand opposite of Octavian and his cohort inside the ring. The crowd becomes hushed as Octavian continues. Octavian- You see, we're not that much different...but the main difference is that when I lost my belt, I didn't hit that Pity Me depression state... The scowl intensifies on Dread's lips. Octavian- ...you should have killed yourself when you had the chance, Jason. It'd save you a lot of trouble in the long run with competing with the likes of me-. Dread, now infuriated, bursts into a tone of voice showing nothing but hatred. Dread- No Octavian, there is a HUGE difference between the two of us. When you lost, you got up and left this company with your tail between your legs like a scared little bitch! Not to mention that yeah, Caldera lost my title for me, but you don't see me coming out here EVERY single night and using that as a basis to bitch and have an excuse as to why you're a f*beep*ing failure NOW! The fans ERUPT in cheers at Jason Dread, who us breathing heavily in the ring, sets the mic away from his face and staring Octavian right into the eyes. The Roman takes just a second to compose himself, and lets out a slight chuckle as he begins his first sentence. Octavian- Jason, Jason...it's that type of attitude that shows me just how infantile you are. It's a mindset like that which shows me that you never deserved a single thing that you ever got handed to you in your stay here in Global Championship Wrestling. Octavian walks towards Dread, shining his belt he is wearing around his waist as he walks. Octavian- And it damn sure makes me realize that you will never come close to getting a shot at this title...representing everything in GCW that I never lost to begin with. At this point, Octavian is standing directly in the face of Dread, staring him right back. Both share heated scowls. Octavian- This belt also symbolizes something else...it shows what you never should have truly won to begin with...because unlike me, you're a petty fraud... With a coy smile, Octavian steps away from Dread, as the crowd boos in reaction to what he's just said. Octavian- ...So, Jason, I suggest you get a real good look at this belt around my waist, because this is as close to a title you will ever see as long as I'm around. With a slight pause, Dread retaliates. Dread- I can't seem to say this enough...shut the hell up! Octavian is once again taken slightly by surprise. The crowd gets into it, cheering on Dread. Dread- You don't impress me with all your gold and your diamonds, Octy. What I see here is a man that suddenly realized he would never get to the top of the mountain again, and is compensating by hiding his... inadequacies with a really big belt. I think you know that with me gunning for the world title again you don't stand a chance...and you don't want to face that music, Octavian. And that, my Roman friend, is EXACTLY...what...you...dread. With that, Just Got Wicked hits overhead, and Dread nimbly slips out of the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Octavian seems to be shaking in fury, his coronation having been ruined by the verbal assault of Dread. Dread backs his way up the ramp, laughing at Octavian a bit on the way. Bryan- Wow! And Jason Dread seems to have rained on Octavian’s parade a bit! Caldera- Serves him right! I’d had about enough of that self-serving crap. Kudos to Jason Dread for stepping up and taking Octavian down a peg! Bryan- Well, fans, while Octavian clears the ring, we’ll take a commercial break, but when we come back the Hardcore title is on the line! ---- Toad vs. Niklas Bauer WorldWide returns to the entrance of Niklas Bauer to the tune of 'Wer bist du' by Megahertz. His translator follows close behind as the crowd boos the massive German. Bauer slides into the ring and flexes for the crowd, and Out With the Old cues up next. The reaction is tremendous as a very fit looking Toad makes his way to the ring with the Hardcore Title belt around his waist. Toad stops short of the ring, drops his title belt, and begins searching under the ring. The bell sounds as Toad is still searching under the ring, and Bauer moves out to greet him. As Bauer sticks his head out through the ropes, Toad pops up with a fire extinguisher in his hands, blasting Bauer in the face with a huge plume of dust! Toad leaves the extinguisher and finds a baseball bat, which he brings into the ring. Bauer turns and takes a blind swing at Toad, but Toad hits him directly in the gut with the bat. Bauer is double over and Toad hits the ropes, going for a quick Toad Slam! But Bauer rises up with Toad on his back, dropping the champ to the mat with great impact. Toad springs back up but is instantly caught in a tremendous guerilla press from Bauer, eliciting a huge gasp of awe from the crowd. Bauer drops the three hundred pound Toad back to the mat and begins posing for the crowd again. Toad painfully gets back to his feet, but Bauer quickly grabs Toad by the head and tosses him out of the ring. Bauer follows Toad out and grabs a steel chair from the timekeeper’s table. Bauer swings the chair and nails Toad in the skull, covering Toad on the outside for a two count. Bauer angrily grabs Toad and pulls him up towards the ramp, but Toad fights back with some hard right hands. The two exchange blows while making their way up the ramp. Bauer finally gets the upper hand by sending Toad headfirst into the steel frame over the entrance, and Toad goes down. Bauer covers for another two count. Bauer pulls Toad back to his feet and picks him up overhead in another guerilla press. Bauer moves towards the end of the ramp to toss Toad off, but Toad counters, dropping behind Bauer and giving him a mighty shove, sending the German off the ramp and through a table on the floor below! Toad swivels his hips and takes off, nailing a perfect Toad Jam onto the floor! The official takes a moment to climb down, and makes the three count! Toad is handed his title belt and he painfully climbs back on stage to celebrate. Bauer shows amazing healing powers by getting back to his feet, meeting Toad with an angry glare as the champ leaves the area. Winner: And Still Hardcore Champion, Toad Bryan- What a hell of a Hardcore matchup that was! Toad has proven that he’s the Core of Hardcore once again, and the eleven time champ is rolling. Caldera- Well, Bauer is a hell of a wrestler, there’s no doubt about that, and it didn’t take him long to get back to his feet, even after that huge fall. That man scares me, JB. Bryan- He’s a freak of nature, there’s no doubt about that. Any man that can pick Toad up over his head with ease should be feared. ---- Welcome to my Knightmare The camera leaves the commentating table and heads backstage. With his back turned to the camera, LeStatt Knight stands in his locker room. He is shirtless, and water drips down from his back. His long black hair is tied back in a rubber band and falls to the middle of his muscular back. He is perched over two dressers which he has pulled close to each other, and doing ‘dips’ to get a bit of a warm up before he goes out for his match tonight. His voice is softly heard as he counts his reps out loud. Knight- 70, 71,72, 73, 74,75. The camera pans around to get a shot of his face. A few loose strands of hair fall over his face, and water drips over his face, settling at his lips. His icy blue eyes are staring straight ahead, focused perhaps like never before. The veins in his arms seem to grow longer with each rep. Knight- 96, 97, 98, 99, 100. He stops with his exercise, and drops off the dressers he had himself perched on. LK walks over to a fold out chair that’s over near his bathroom door and grabs the towel that is draped over it. As he begins wiping his face with the towel he hears a knock at his door, and although he seems annoyed he walks over to the door and opens it up. His annoyed look turned to a genuine smile as he saw the only person who could make him smile that way. Knight- Mary, how’d you get in here ? Mary- Wells Statty when yaz looks as nestles scrumptious as Mary does, you can get anywheres ! Knight- I bet … well come in. Knight’s girlfriend, known to most only as Typhoid Mary walked in wearing an outfit, that left little to the imagination but also being very unique made mostly of fish net and combat boots. Her Raven curls bounced freely about her face, as she looked up at LeStatt with a dopey grin. Knight- So Senorita, what brought you here? Mary- Wells I hads me a night off ats the federally … er … Federamation …. Um … Knight- Federation dear. Mary- Yeah That!! Well I had me a nights off, and thought I’d supwise yous babes ! so SUPWISE ! hehe Have ya had your match yet ? Knight- No actually I am getting ready to go out now. Mary- Wells hurry your cutes butt up, so we can have us some naz-D fun ya heard ? Knight raises one eyebrow, then gives Mary a devilish smile. Knight- Don’t worry Hermosa, we’ll have our fun, but first I have to go … go and destroy some has-been who will be lucky to walk after tonight. Mary- Woo Hoo, that’s my Latino Heeeeat! Knock ems dead ! Knight lowers his face and kisses Mary passionately, and after a few moments pulls back and grins. Knight- Yeah … Dead is the word alright. Knight then turns and begins heading out the door to go to the ring for his match with the Legendary Khan, he only smiles and whispers. Knight- Welcome to my Knightmare. ---- LeStatt Knight vs. Khan WorldWide fades back in as JB and Caldera talk about the debut of Khan. First out is LeStatt Knight, the ominous tones of O’Fortuna playing him to the ring. Knight waits in the ring a moment before Debonair hits next. Khan walks out on stage looking bigger and meaner than ever, his eyes focused directly on Knight. Khan hits the ring and instantly attacks Knight with a huge spinning heel kick that connects with Knight’s face. Knight bounces back up but is sent back down by a dropkick, followed by a cover and a one count. Khan waits for Knight to stand and delivers a big floatover suplex, earning a two-count. Khan rushes up to the top rope as Knight regains his balance, and Khan soars with an amazing moonsault into a cross body block, garnering another nearfall. Khan continues the offensive with a whip to the ropes and a brutal powerslam. Khan attempts another whip, but Knight reverses and catches Khan with a huge roundhouse kick to the face. Khan staggers back up but Knight nails him with a brutal death valley driver. Knight mounts Khan and begins nailing him in the face with repeated right hands, not stopping until he has drawn blood. With Khan’s face now bloodied, Knight pulls him up and whips him to the corner. Khan counters by jumping to the second rope and leaping back at Knight, but Knight picks him right out of the air with a big Knight Fall! Knight covers and gets the three! Knight looks down at the bleeding Khan and nods in satisfaction before heading to the back. Winner: LeStatt Knight Bryan- Well, Mr. Caldera, it seems your former henchman’s return has been spoiled by an impressive outing by LeStatt Knight! Caldera- Well, Khan looked great, he brought that trademark fury in the beginning of the match, but Knight was just too much. Hell of a win. Bryan- Well, it seems LeStatt Knight might have earned a little respect from you tonight, Mr. Caldera. Any thoughts on who the early leaders are in the race for the six spots in ACWF six? Caldera- It’s only week two, JB, it’s too early to pick. But every win counts, and LeStatt definitely got moving in the right direction tonight. Everyone’s looked impressive so far, and there are quite a few names I- ---- Man at Work Caldera trails off as a picture appears on the MegaTron. The cameras have located a section of the crowd that has burst into cheers, many of them standing to look at someone walking down the aisle. There, stumbling down the steps without a lot of grace is Twisted, carrying a tray of blue cotton candy. With a goofy grin he begins tossing the cotton candy out to the fans, who jump wildly to try to catch them. The entire arena begins to cheer the former world champ as they see his image on the MegaTron, and JB can’t help but laugh. Bryan- What the hell is he doing? Caldera- I have no clue. Twisted reaches into his tray and pulls out a microphone, which catches the cheers of the crowd in the background. Twisted- Hey everyone! Get your Twistodium flavored cotton candy right here! Free Twistodium flavored cotton candy! Twizzy tosses a few more sticks of the puffy blue candy out to the crowd, as security rushes down the stairs to try to pry the fans off Twizzy a bit. Twisted- And Caldy, I know you’re watching, I just wanted you to see how I’m bringing joy to the people of Dallas here! I thought about what you said about giving the fans what they want, and I know they want this delicious treat! I wove it myself! And each bite contains sixty-three percent Twistodium! And, to prove to you that I’m not just some greedy Roman guy like Octavian, I’m giving it away for FREE! Come and get it! The crowd cheers as Twizzy tosses some more of the candy out to the crowd, and the camera finally returns to the announce table. Bryan- You’ve got to be kidding. Is this impressing you? Caldera- Actually, yeah. It impresses me that a guy that dumb hasn’t been run over by a cement mixer while crossing the street. ---- Jason Dread vs. Toxic Meltdown returns with a nice view of the downtown Dallas skyline, finally centering on the American Airlines Center. Bryan- Welcome back to WorldWide, fans, and getting ready for what should prove to be a tremendous matchup. Caldera- Toxic and Jason Dread are up next, JB, and you’d better believe there’s a lot on the line in this one. Both men won big matchups last week, and one of them is going to leave Dallas on a big streak. Bryan- Well, Toxic looked dominant in his first match against Toad, and Dread sent a message to The Jhub last week that Jhub isn’t the only one that can play the ACWF game! Toxic enters first, receiving one of the loudest reactions of the night. The crowd boos Toxic heavily as he makes his way to the ring, flexing his impressive physique to intimidate a few fans on the way. Out next is Dread, who gets a great round of cheers. Dread gets right in Toxic’s face, though Toxic stands at least ten inches taller than Dread. Dread starts things off exchanging right hands with Toxic, but Toxic quickly gains the upper hand and back Dread into a corner. Toxic continues pounding on Dread until the official cuts him off. Toxic shoves the referee out of the way and goes back at Dread, but Dread counters with a dropkick to Toxic’s knee. Dread hits Toxic with a European uppercut and then hops onto the second rope, coming off with a hard flying clothesline that takes Toxic to the mat. Dread immediately hooks Toxic in a side leg lock, punishing the big man’s knee. Toxic finally kicks Dread off and hobbles back to his feet. Dread moves back in with a few kicks to the kneecap, but Toxic picks Dread up by the neck with both hands and tosses him hard into the turnbuckle. Dread counters with a kick to the head, followed by a bulldog. Dread covers but Toxic powers out before a one count. Dread locks Toxic back in a leg lock, but Toxic uses his long arms to reach the ropes. Both men get back to their feet and Dread charges for a clothesline, but Toxic powers him down with a sidewalk slam, getting a two count. Toxic picks Dread up overhead and takes him back down with a powerslam. Toxic then locks Dread in with a Texas Cloverleaf, punishing Dread’s back. Dread comes close to tapping out, but after a long struggle finds the ropes to break the hold. Toxic nails Dread with a backbreaker for another two count. As Dread recovers, the crowd boos as Octavian appears and heads to ringside. He stops shorts of the ring and watches as Dread struggles to his feet and Toxic pulls him into the T-Bar, punishing Dread’s back further. Dread begins to fade, but comes back with some hard right hands to the head. Dread nails Toxic with a big standing dropkick, staggering the big man. Dread then hits the ropes, but Octavian nails him in the back with the Imperial Roman title belt, staggering Dread without the official seeing. Toxic hooks Dread up and plants him with the Toxic Waste for the three count! Octavian retreats to the back with a smile on his face, leaving Toxic to celebrate his big win. Winner: Toxic ---- Mike Hardy vs. The Dark Messiah Bryan- Welcome back to WorldWide folks, and Mr. Caldera, you’ve got to be impressed with Toxic’s win, interference or not! Caldera- Well, just like in baseball we need to place an asterisk by Toxic’s name in the win column, but he did look great. But you’d better believe Jason Dread is going to be looking for revenge. Bryan- Well, I hate to keep asking, but when are we going to find out who is going to get the first spot in the ACWF match? Caldera- Well, I’ll tell you what, JB, if it’ll make you happy, I’ll announce a match for next week that will have a spot in the ACWF match riding on it. How about that? Bryan- I think everyone would love to see that! Caldera- Then consider it done as of tomorrow. Broken Promises cues up overhead, and the crowd cheers as Mike Hardy appears on stage. Hardy, not scheduled to wrestle, is dressed in his wrestling attire anyway. He has a somewhat angry look on his face as he heads to the ring. Bryan- Well, here comes Mike Hardy, fans, though I didn’t know he was scheduled to be here tonight. Caldera- He’s not. In fact, I haven’t talked to Mike all week. I heard he was looking for me, but I never got a call. Hardy slides into the ring and motions to cut his music. He snatches a microphone from Joey Andrews and turns to speak directly to Caldera. Hardy- All right, I need to clear something up right now for you, Steven. I really feel like I deserve a chance to get into the ACWF match like everyone else. But when you’ve got officials who forget I’m even in my match, and I get cheated out of a big win because of it, it makes getting noticed very hard. Then, after all that, you don’t even bother to give me a match this week? I’ve got to tell you, Steven, I’m a little upset about this. I mean, how am I ever going to win the world title when you don’t even seem to know I exist? I spent half the week trying to see you in Columbus, and all I got was ‘He’s too busy, he’s not available.’ Well, here we are, Steven, face to face, and I want someone to notice me! Suddenly, the arena is plunged into darkness, and some odd violin music begins to play in the background. The violins pick up their crescendo, joined now by the more bass sounding cellos and upright bass. The note is just barely out of tune, as they play, louder and louder. Mike Hardy stands in the ring confused as pictures of people from GCW’s past play on the MegaTron. Eric “All That” Allen. Michael “Spitfire” Stevens. Badger Benandanti. Alex Grey. Jeff Wylde. Bryan Mayhem. With each passing face, Hardy looks more and more concerned. He knows that each hadn’t been in the ring in quite a while, and he doesn’t want to suffer the same fate. But what the hell is going on here? Suddenly, the violins stop. The lights fall to nothingness for a few seconds as the fans start to show a bit of panic from the unexpected surprise. And then, it happens. Everyone in the arena knows what is coming. Everyone…except Mike Hardy. The ring seems to explode with green pyro, as Mike Hardy turns around. A new song kicks in through the arena as Hardy makes eye contact with the man standing behind him. “It’s only…the end…of the world…again” Hardy stared in disbelief as The Dark Messiah stands in the ring. Caldera- WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING HERE?! Bryan- Unbelieveable! The Dark Messiah is back in GCW? Caldera- I sure as hell never invited him back! What the hell does that son of a bitch think he’s going here?! Hardy went on the quick offensive as the lights came back up. He threw a right to the side of TDM’s face. TDM blocked it and returned with a kick to the midsection, doubling Hardy over. TDM lifted a leg and caught Hardy with a kick the the face, sending him down onto his back. TDM smiled at Caldera, who was now standing at ringside, watching the ring. TDM pulled Hardy up by the hair, and stared at Caldera as he did so. He pointed to his nemesis, and slashed across his throat. But all the occupation with Caldera left TDM open. Hardy took advantage and put fist to testicles, sending the painted one to the canvas with a thud. The fans cheered in appreciation as Hardy showed his resolve. He put heavy boots to the top of TDM’s head, opening a sizeable gash on his forehead with rage. Hardy backed off for a second and taunted TDM, as the crowd went banana. Caldera smiled his approval as a referee finally made his way to the ring, after the unexpected attack. Hardy went on the offensive again, coming off the ropes, and dropping an elbow to TDM. TDM sat up for a second, trying to collect his wits when Hardy threw him to the canvas, and got a one count. Hardy stood again, and lifted TDM off the canvas. He tried to drop the Dark one with a DDT, but TDM backed him into the turnbuckles, and launched a shoulder into Hardy’s midsection. Air came out of Hardy’s lungs as he struggled for breath. Another fierce shoulder knocked the wind out of Hardy, as TDM stood, and put the boots to Hardy’s chest, repeatedly. Struggling to breathe, Caldera became concerned. TDM was clearly in control again, and was now standing on Hardy’s chest, making sure he didn’t catch his breath. Just before the five count TDM stepped off and glared at Caldera. Hardy coughed and wheezed, trying to catch his breath. TDM grabbed him off the canvas, and send him off the ropes. Hardy returned, and TDM lifted him high into the air, and slammed him down with a horrible sounding spinebuster that made every fan in the building wince. TDM sprung up and wiped the blood out of his eyes, flicking his fingers at Caldera, spraying his two thousand dollar suit with blood. Hardy turned over, and struggled for the ropes. TDM waited, and pulled him backwards, and locked him into the Fall from Grace. Hardy struggled against the mighty dragon clutch, but, it was academic. Hardy waved his arm, and the bell rang. TDM was victorious, and to celebrate, he lifted Hardy off the canvas, and threw him outside the ring like a rag doll. He walked over to the corner, and grabbed a microphone, almost daring Caldera inside the ring. Caldera shook his head, and crossed his arms, waiting for the uninvited guest to speak his mind before having him escorted out of the building. TDM – Gee, Steven. I thought you’d be happy to see me. I thought all of you would be happy to see me here today. Boos rang through the arena, as they remembered the things the Messiah had done to GCW in the past, including trying to kill GCW’s biggest star: The Jhub. TDM – Quite frankly, I can’t blame any of you for booing me. After what I’ve done to this company in the past, I’m sure I’m the last person Steven Caldera wanted to see tonight. But each time I’ve tried to kill this place, it’s come back, stronger than it was before. The fans cheered TDM’s compliment, as he nodded to Caldera. Caldera raised an eyebrow, wondering where TDM was going with this. TDM – And that’s a testiment to you fans. No matter what this guy does, you all embrace him, as if he’s some sort of God. But remember, he’s closed this place three times now, shutting each of you fans out. TDM didn’t ruin GCW. Steven Caldera did. The fans boo this time, but, it’s unsure if it’s directed at Caldera or TDM. Caldera looks around at the fans, and a few point at him, accusitorily. He simply shrugs and turns his attention back to the ring. TDM – Third time’s a charm, eh, Stevie? No, you fans need someone you can respect. You need a bastion of light and a bastion of hope. And I’m going to be that man. Tonight, you saw the last of my wrath in the ring. What you saw me do to Mike Hardy in the ring, will be the last time you see me use a power move… …or a Spinebuster… …or a submission move. From now on, it’s nothing but headlocks… Boos. Armbars… More boos. …And hip tosses. If I’m truly going to kill GCW, then I’m going to destroy the very product that Steven Caldera grew to be so proud of. I’m going to bore each and every fan into changing the channel every night until you simply get so sick of GCW, you’re not going to buy tickets, you’re not going to buy products from the advertisers, and you’re not going to watch what will become a god awful excuse for a wrestling program. Caldera’s face turns red, angered at TDM’s words. TDM – There’s nothing you can do about it, Steven. I’m as much a part of the fabric of GCW as Jhub, Dread, Tristain, and Toxic. And when one thread starts to unravel, the rest are soon to follow. Enjoy GCW, fans. In six months, you’re not going to remember it. Caldera…Can you handle…that? TDM tossed the mic to the ground as the now even more prophetic “Endsmouth” by the Agents of Oblivion played through the arena, and the CD seemed to skip at the opening line… It’s only the end of the world…again… It’s only the end of the world…again… It’s only the end of the world…again… ---- Tristain Mayhem vs. The Jhub Bryan- GCW returns here to WorldWide, and I’ve been left all alone here at ringside, as President Caldera has gone to the back with Mike Hardy. Hardy was helped to the back during the break, and he did not look good, ladies and gentlemen. Caldera was very concerned about Hardy, and I’ve got to say I’ve never seen Caldera as angry as he was when The Dark Messiah arrived just moments ago. But the show must go on, ladies and gentlemen, and it’s time for our main event! The Jhub comes down first to an amazing reaction, and he poses for the crowd for a long time. Mayhem is out next to an incredible chorus of boos, and Jhub starts a ‘Mayhem Sucks!’ chant with the crowd. Mayhem is angered but remains cool as he enters the ring. Mayhem gets on the microphone and announces that, after careful consideration, he’s decided that The Jhub isn’t main event material, and he won’t be wrestling here tonight. Mayhem begins heading to the back, but The Jhub chases him down and brings him back to the ring, where the bell sounds and the match begins! The Jhub immediately nails Mayhem with a series of right hands, finally sending Mayhem to the mat with a big haymaker. Mayhem springs back up, but The Jhub nails him with an inverted atomic drop, followed by a blatant kick to the balls. Mayhem goes down and The Jhub covers, but Mayhem kicks out at two. The official admonishes The Jhub as he pushes Mayhem into the corner and delivers a series of big boots to the gut, before bealing Mayhem all the way across the ring! Mayhem slips out of the ring and starts to head for the back, but The Jhub catches him and brings him back into the ring. Mayhem comes back with a rake to the eyes, then yanking The Jhub down to the mat by the shirt. Mayhem drops a leg over The Jhub’s face and covers him for a one count. Mayhem whips The Jhub into the corner and follows it up with a big running clothesline, sending The Jhub back to the mat. Mayhem pulls The Jhub to the center of the ring and delivers a mean headbutt to the lower abdominal region, though The Jhub reacts as if the shot was placed further south. Mayhem pulls The Jhub up again and delivers a big DDT, earning a two count. Mayhem sends The Jhub back into the ropes, but Jhub counters with a sunset flip for a two count. The Jhub comes back with a double leg takedown and steps through, shocking Mayhem by hooking on the Sharpshooter! But Mayhem is too close to the ropes and he instantly grabs hold, breaking it before much damage is done. The Jhub continues with a pendulum backbreaker, then comes off the middle rope with an elbow drop for another two count. The Jhub pulls Mayhem back up but is met with a series of knife edge chops to the chest. Mayhem chops Jhub back into the corner, then begins to bite The Jhub in the face! Jhub pushes Mayhem off, but his cheek has begun to bleed. Mayhem catches The Jhub with a powerslam, but The Jhub escapes the pinning predicament with a foot on the bottom rope. Mayhem sends The Jhub into the ropes and catches him with a dropkick right to the groin area. Mayhem then covers The Jhub with his feet on the second rope for added leverage, but The Jhub still kicks out. Mayhem gets back to his feet and pulls The Jhub up, but The Jhub counters again and steps through for the Sharpshooter! Mayhem struggles, scrambling for the ropes with The Jhub still holding on. Mayhem pulls his upper body up the ropes with The Jhub still holding his legs. Mayhem finally frees a leg and kicks Jhub in the face, shoving off the ropes and sending The Jhub to the mat. Mayhem rolls through by rolling The Jhub up, and placing his legs on the top rope! The official doesn’t notice as he makes the three count, and Mayhem steals the win! Winner: Tristain Mayhem Bryan- Oh, you’ve got to be kidding me! Tristain Mayhem has robbed The Jhub here tonight, folks! He said he was going to pull out every dirty trick in the book, and he certainly did! Mayhem slides out of the ring as The Jhub angrily springs back to his feet. The Jhub points an accusing finger at the official as Mayhem raises his arms in victory. However, his smile fades as he sees President Caldera standing at the top of the ramp, with spots of Mike Hardy’s blood still on his white dress shirt. The crowd cheers as Caldera glares down at Mayhem, while The Jhub looks on curiously from the ring. Caldera- Wow, what a great victory, Tristain. I don’t know if you think winning that way is going to impress me, but I can assure you it has not. Now, there’s a lot I have to say to you, Tristain, and I’ll get to that. But for now, I promised JB a match to select the first entrant in the ACWF match. So, I’m here to announce a tag team match for next week in Hartford! The crowd cheers as Mayhem gets a concerned look on his face. Caldera- In this tag match, the man who scores the pinfall will earn the first spot in the ACWF match! That man’s partner will earn another shot the following week. As for the participants, I think The Jhub deserves a shot, doesn’t he? The crowd cheers, as The Jhub nods in agreement. Caldera- So it’s going to be The Jhub, teaming with Jason Dread… to take on Tristain Mayhem… The crowd cheers in excitement as Mayhem jumps in anger. Caldera- …And the Italian Stallion, Octavian!!!!! The crowd explodes in shock as Mayhem’s face suddenly becomes very somber, realizing that he’ll be teaming with his arch rival next week! Bryan- WOW! Tristain Mayhem and Octavian together!? There’s no way that can possibly work! Caldera- And as far as we go, Tristain, there isn’t enough time tonight to say what I have to say. So if you’re a man like you seem to think you are, you’ll meet me in the ring at the top of the show next week, and we can finally say all the things that need to be said. Until then, I suggest you find your partner and talk strategy. That is… if you’ve got the BALLS for it! Caldera throws down his microphone and raises his arms into the air, as The Jhub steps onto the turnbuckle and poses for the crowd. The final shot is of Mayhem, glaring out at the crowd in anger. Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time! Mayhem and Octavian team up next week, and Mayhem and Caldera finally confront each other! Don’t miss WorldWide in Hartford! Until then, I’m James Bryan, GOOD NIGHT! The show fades out as Mayhem and Jhub lock eyes, prepared to meet again……..
