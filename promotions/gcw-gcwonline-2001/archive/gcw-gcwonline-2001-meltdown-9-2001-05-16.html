Meltdown 9, 23 May 2001

23 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 9 Allen County War Memorial Coliseum — Fort Wayne, Indiana ---- Meltdown #9 (The crowd begins to murmur as the lights in the arena dim. Suddenly, pyro lights up the stadium, and the crowd goes nuts. Explosions rock the arena, as the camera pans over the packed crowd.) Bryan- We are just three nights removed from Shockwave! It was a night to be remembered, and rivaled Warpath in its intensity! We saw some of the most shocking events ever on Sunday night, and hopefully tonight we’ll get some answers! Yale- Well, for any of you that don’t know, President Caldera turned his back on Octavian on Sunday, and in the end we saw Caldera, the Jhub, and Khan together again! But what prompted this? Bryan- We’d all like some answers, of course, but for now, let’s take you back to Shockwave. (The final moments of the iron man match are played.) Bryan- The Jhub had come to the ring, but in the end, Caldera, the Jhub, and Khan assaulted Octavian, and cost him the world title! Yale- But things didn’t go quite as planned! That damned Renegade snuck in and scored a quick pinfall, capitalizing on the handiwork of the President! Bryan- That’s right, Rich Rollins is the new world champion, and he is here tonight. We hope to speak with him. But first, I have word that someone is making his way to the ring. . . (Physical hits, and the crowd boos like mad. President Caldera, Khan, and the Jhub make their way to the ring, looking quite disturbed.) Andrews- Please welcome the Jhub, Khan, and the GCW President, Steven Caldera! Yale- I can’t wait to hear what he has to say, JB! Let’s hear his explanation! (Caldera is handed a microphone. A brief ‘asshole’ chant begins, but is quieted when Caldera begins to speak.) Caldera- What a difference one night can make. Just last week, the two men who now stand before me were taking pride in giving their boss a beating. But now, after one night changed it all, they stand beside me, my allies. So I suppose we should get right down to the obvious question. . .Why? Well, don’t take it from me, let’s hear from the men themselves. (Caldera hands the mike to the Jhub. The crowd’s boos become incredibly loud.) Jhub- So you people want to know why I am standing side by side with the President? Well, it’s simple. This is where the power lies. Steven Caldera is the judge, jury, and executioner of GCW. With that kind of power, who would want him as an enemy? (The crowd continues to boo.) Jhub- Now, we’ll get to Rollins and all that later. For now, I want to address my good buddies, the Toad and Luke Hanson. First off, congratulations, guys, on taking home the gold last night. (The boos lighten.) Jhub- But quite frankly, that gold doesn’t mean a thing next to the power I have now. So you can take your belts, and you can take your System-X, and you can shove them up your asses. . . (The boos intensify) Jhub- . . .Because the Jhub and System-X are through! You two jackasses can have System-X. I’ve moved on to bigger and better things. (Jhub hands the mike to Khan, as the boos intensify.) Khan- I am not a man of as many words as the Jhub, but he put it well when he said that this is where the power lies. What you people fail to realize is that President Caldera and I have been together since day one, an unstoppable force no-one can match. The brief charade of the past month, however, seems to have been for naught. Because I did not get the job done. But heed this warning, Renegade. You may have won the battle, but we will finish the war one on one, and I will make things right again. (Khan hands the mike back to Caldera.) Caldera- This brings us to a little unfinished business. Some might wonder where Toxic, John Reed, and Chris Jordan may stand with all this. Well, I’d like to invite them to the ring right now, so we can discuss this matter. (The crowd reacts with both boos and cheers as Toxic and John Reed head for the ring. Reed is favoring his shoulder and left knee. Toxic’s head is bandaged. They step into the ring, looking tentatively at the Jhub and Khan.) Caldera- Now, guys, I want you to know something. You past few months of loyal service have not been forgotten. Even though, John, you failed to take the Hard*core title away from Toad on Sunday, that’s okay. And Toxic, even though you’ve failed twice at taking the United States title, that’s also okay. But what is not okay is the fact that you two keep looking to me for help. (The crowd boos, as Reed and Toxic stare at each other.) Caldera- You two saw the Establishment as a free ride. Well, guys, that just doesn’t fly with me. So I’ll tell you what. I will give you two one chance to apologize to me for the months of mooching. I will give you one chance to walk out of here with your heads held high. So go ahead. (Caldera hands Toxic the mike. Toxic whispers something to Reed, who nods. Toxic and Reed both proceed to nail Caldera with punches.) Bryan- Woah, there’s their answer! Toxic and Reed put the boss down, but Khan and the Jhub are on them! Khan and the Jhub have come in from behind, and are taking them apart! What the hell has happened to the Establishment? Yale- I don’t know, but President Caldera is right! Those two were just good for nothing moochers! And I’ll bet Chris Jordan is just hiding under a table somewhere! (Physical hits again, as Khan, the Jhub, and Caldera stand over the beaten Reed and Toxic. They head for the locker room, as officials help Reed and Toxic to the back.) Bryan- Well, folks, tonight we will see the Jhub and Khan team up against Rich Rollins and a mystery partner! We’ve been notified that Mr. Danger is not here tonight, but managed to appoint a tag partner for Rollins anyway! Yale- Of course Danger isn’t here! He was taken out on a stretcher at Shockwave, thanks to our President! Bryan- With some help from Octavian and a giant hell in a cell, I might add. Fans, we’ve got a big night ahead of us, and hopefully a few more questions will be answered!
