Foster Nackedy Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Guy With the Bad Name Hometown Arabi, LA Birthday 7/7/1977 Height 6'4 Weight 251 Biography When Foster was brought up in Arabi, Louisiana, life was easy. That is, until both of his parents were injured and slipped into comas. When his mother eventually died, Foster looked everywhere for help and he found it at the local aquarium. He felt at home with the fish and water animals. He loved all of them, and formed a protest when the city tried to shut down the aquarium. In the protest, he was severely beaten. He took defense classes to improve his fighting skills, and became a great martial artist. He fought in a local martial arts tournament and placed 3rd, giving him enough money to restart the aquarium. But now, with the city closing in again, he must get enough money to keep it going. With this knowledge, Foster turned to the Federation of KNF to gain money for his cause. After a respectable Ultimate Title reign, Foster gained more than enough money to keep the Aquarium going. Foster wanted more, and he went to NWC member fed LCW to get it. After abandoning all of the fans who loved him, he won the Big Apple Heritage Title and had a respectful reign, then was stripped of the title when he won the NWC United States Title from Nixon at Legacy. Foster did not have a successful defense, and he went back to LCW, having a harsh losing streak. Right when it seemed that his best times were behind him, something happened to change his life. Pat Gray, Foster's mentor and good friend, was hospitalized with mysterious and unfounded conditions. This episode showed Foster the error of his ways, and he began to try to mend relations with his family. With Pat dead, he was intent on proving to everyone that he really has changed. And he did. He got back on the winning track and defeated Andy O'Brien for the Big Apple Heritage Championship before the league folded. Soon after, the NWC:S formed and drafted Foster as their number one pick, 4th overall. Zooming to the spotlight there, he got into a long and memorable feud with Kaylo Laven revolving around his then girlfriend Magen, who he married in January. That feud ended in a match at Southern Comfort, where the results of Magen's paternity tests would hang from a ladder. Foster won the match and found out that he was the father of Magen's child, a baby that would be born two months later when Kaylo kicked Magen in the stomach. In May Foster won the NWC:S Championship, his destiny, and was shocked to lose it only a month later to Sean Laughreah, but the shocks weren't over for the night. His child, only five months, was attacked by Anton Dufresne and almost killed. This sent Foster into a rage that has not been quelled yet, even with justice being served when he paralyzed Anton Dufresne with a vicious steel pipe shot at Civil War: Patriot Games. This incident caused a change in Foster, as he spent the final days of NWC in a dark state, trying to make everyone feel his pain. He played no role in the end of the NWC and ended up trying to stay on in one of the feds formed out of the dead NWC. In his third match there, he broke his leg during a match and was sidelined for over a year. After separating from his wife because she made him choose between her and returning to wrestling, he joined a small fed in Wyoming which only lasted for a few months, but the important thing here was Foster met a new girl. He dated her for about six months before going to Chicago to wrestle in the UWF. He won the TV Title there and looked perched to return to his former dominance when Magen, his wife, filed divorce papers wanting sole custody of their young child, Connor. In the end, Foster did the only thing he could think to do: he got back with Magen and quit wrestling. But the bug came back, and Foster obliged. Joining the CWMC and effectively ruining his personal life, Foster busted through the ranks in a surprise trip to the semi-finals, losing to Illustrious Face Eater and Digital Mortality. Trying to feed off of his momentum, Foster signed a 90 day contract with PRIME to test his mettle, but only got on TV for a few weeks during a storyline with his brother. After going back under the knife for a few standard knee operations, Foster signed to GCW for what he thinks will be one last hurrah.
