Meltdown 22, 8 July 2001

8 July 2001

Meltdown 22 Celebration Arena — Decatur, Alabama ---- Meltdown #22 (The lights in the arena dim, and the crowd begins to buzz. The MegaTron lights up, as scenes from the past few weeks are shown.) (Suddenly, red and blue fireworks explode from the stage, and the crowd goes crazy. Meltdown begins with a bang, as its music plays and the camera flies over the fans.) Bryan- Tension is running high in the GCW locker room, and tonight things could explode! The CWU and ACWF factions have been in control as of late, but tonight Commissioner Octavian and President Caldera have promised to take control back for GCW! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan, alongside David Yale, and we’re set for another edition of Meltdown. Yale- What I’d like to know is. . . What is the deal with Kurt Richards? Rumors have been flying around as of late, and I’d like to know what’s going on! Bryan- We’ll get that answer tonight when Richards takes on Mike Hardy. Also tonight, President Caldera and the Jhub form an unlikely alliance to take on Mr. Danger and Big Daddy Dero! Dero and Caldera will go one on one at Heatwave, a match signed at Oblivion! Yale- Dero narrowly escaped LeStatt Knight on Monday, but he’ll have to tangle with Caldera and the Jhub tonight! Also, Toad defends his Hard*core title, and more! Bryan- And here we go! The Jhub looks like he’ll start things off with Dero! They tie up, and fight for position! And it looks like the taller, stronger Dero is taking over! Dero powers the Jhub back into the corner, but the referee pulls Dero out! Yale- Dero backs off, allowing the Jhub to get to his feet.
