Meltdown 5, 18 April 2001

18 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 5 Allstate Arena — Rosemond, Illinois ---- Meltdown #5 (The camera shot begins as the GCW logo fades into view. Slowly, the packed arena fades in behind it, dimly lit and buzzing with anticipation. Suddenly, a loud bang and explosive pyro lights up the arena, as the Meltdown theme fills the area. The fans jump to their feet as the camera pans around the crowd, finally resting on the announce table.) Bryan- We are just four short days away from Warpath, and tonight, things reach the breaking point, as the Establishment and System-X square off in what should prove to be all-out war! Yale- We are so close I can hardly stand it! In just four days, we’ll be crowning three new champions, including the first World Heavyweight champion! Bryan- Not to mention a ladder match for the cruiserweight title, and our first United States champion will emerge from the tournament! (Physical hits, and the crowd boos like hell. All four members of the Establishment emerge from backstage and head to the ring. They climb into the ring, and John Reed is handed a microphone.) Reed- I- (The crowd begins chanting ‘Sellout! Sellout!’) Reed- I am not a sellout. I just have good business sense. (This shuts the fans up) Reed- And I am here, standing in the ring with two of the greatest wrestlers there are, and the most powerful man on the planet, to remind everyone that this Sunday on Warpath, the Establishment has three competitors going after the World title. Bryan- And none of them earned it. Reed- With the collective talent of the Establishment going in to Warpath, there is no way we can lose. (Reed hands the mike to Toxic. A background ‘Toad!’ chant begins. Toxic cringes and begins.) Toxic- Our slimy friend the Toad will not be a factor in the tournament! In fact, I wanted to be sure to get my hands on him on Sunday, so I was granted permission from our great leader to take on Toad in the first round of the tournament. Toad, I’ll see you in four days. (The fans boo, as Toxic hands the mike to Octavian.) Octavian- Hello, my American friends. What you people fail to realize is that there is absolutely no way that the Italian Stallion is leaving Madison Square Garden without the gold around his waist. (The fans boo. This disturbs John Reed, who glares at Octavian for a moment. Reed whispers something to Toxic, who just shakes him off) Octavian- So after I leave the first GCW Pay-Per-View the World champion, all of you American idiots will be forced to kiss my Roman ass! (The fans boo as he hands the mike to Caldera.) Caldera- This much is for sure. Just as Octavian has his own corporate Roman empire, GCW is my empire. I am the judge, jury, and executioner in this company, and that much cannot be disputed. (The fans boo. A slight ‘Asshole!’ chant begins.) Caldera- And what this means is that the tournament will be won by one of the three men in this ring right now. These three men represent the pinnacle of the business, and one of them will be the first- (My Own Summer hits, and the fans jump to their feet. The Jhub walks out to the stage, microphone in hand.) Jhub- I’m sorry to cut you off there, prez, but I couldn’t resist the opportunity. You know, it’s funny you mention the opportunity the Establishment has to win the tournament. I will admit, three of you against two System members is a bit much. But you know what, the Jhub likes adversity. And stacked odds make me dangerous. So I think we’ll toss another log on the fire. (The fans cheer, waiting for something big.) Jhub- Since I kicked Toxic’s ass last week, I get to choose my first round opponent for the tournament, correct? (Toxic paces in the ring) Jhub- And you know what, I think there’s someone in that ring right now who deserves a slot in the tournament, too. So Mr. President, I need to correct you. When you say, ‘One of those three guys will win it,’ you should say. . .’One of these FOUR men will win it.’ (The fans cheer as Caldera gulps.) Jhub- That’s right! Steven, I want YOU in the first round of the tournament! (The fans go ballistic as My Own Summer hits again. Toxic and Octavian try to console the President, as Reed slips out of the ring and heads to the back.) Bryan- Woah! The Jhub will square off with the President in the first round of the tournament! Yale- That’s not fair! President Caldera isn’t an active wrestler!
