Jake Reed Biography Real Name Jake Reed Hometown Boone, NC Birthday 7/1/1982 Height 6'1 Weight 221 Biography In Jake's words, an excerpt from 'Lance Jacob Reed'... "At 15 when I made my in-ring debut for my father’s Blue Ridge Wrestling promotion he convinced me to take on the Lance moniker. He fed me some bull shit about a stage persona, separating the man from the wrestler, shit like that. Looking back on it I think he just wanted to win, wanted to best my dead mother 15 years after the fact. Jake became the passionate side of me, the man who lived outside of the world of wrestling. Lance became the man who would do whatever he needed to on the inside of the ring, risking his body daily to win matches. My father built Blue Ridge Wrestling into a respected training school and prominent regional wrestling promotion by using my blood, sweat, and tears. By 18 I could no longer shoulder the burden of being the main attraction of my father’s traveling circus so I packed my bags and headed to Japan. In Japan I studied mixed martial arts under Jinsei Kobayashi and I was Jake once more. I had a few pro fights over there, ended up going 3-2 with 2 victories by submission. When the MMA bookings dried up Jin hooked me up with a local wrestling promoter and I started working shows. In Japan I had a bit of name recognition from MMA so wrestling as Lance was out of the question. Wrestling in Japan was pure bliss because there I was Jake? just Jake. Not the son of Bill Reed, not the brother of Travis, and especially not Lance. I fought with passion inside the squared circle and the Japanese people loved me. I buried Lance, buried what he was and what he represented. As Jake I still flew high and took risks but not because I had to, not because I was forced to, but because I wanted to. I didn’t really talk to my family much while I was in Japan. I returned to the states to find Blue Ridge shut down and our training school turned into a fitness center in order to make ends meat. My older brother Travis blamed me for the closing and relations with my family were nearly nonexistent for the next year. Travis had always been the big man in Blue Ridge. He was the champion, the face of the promotion, but it was no secret that the fans of the late 90’s were less enthralled with Travis? old school approach than with my all out “attitude”. Travis was on the poster but the fans came to see me. When I left for Japan, taking my drawing power with me, the promotion died a slow death under Travis? lone watch. To this day he has never forgiven me for that. Time heals all wounds. I used to think that was bull shit but in my case it proved true. Eventually my father came back into my life, he managed my career for a time and lead me to one of my few singles titles. It took Travis a little longer to come around. Dad died in 2004 and we left a lot of things unreconciled but his death did serve as the kick in the ass Travis needed to finally start returning my calls. It took some time but Travis and I sorted things out and we even reformed our old tag team, Generation Gap." More to come...
