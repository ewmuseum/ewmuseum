Special Notes about the KB:NAN You can concider this as a note about all 5 members of the KB:NAN. This is a stable with a long standing tradition of over 6 years in wrestling and currently has factions in at least two federations. PTC federations with factions are currently GCW and 4WW. The regular name of the stable is the KnightMare Brigade and it started long ago in what was known as the GIWF (now the UWF in PTC). Each faction has a sub name, this one is the Native American Nations, as all members but Ukyo are of noteable Native American Blood. The leader of this faction is Blaise McClancy, with Gray Brand serving as his second. The other line in the heirarchy is not known to be public. the KB:NAN is an honor based stable, no matter what role their playing at the time: Heel, Face or Tweener. They do not change tactics as they change roles in a match, angle or feud. One rule is paramount to this honor code... 'Bell to Bell' This rule is the one that must be followed to remain in the KB:NAN, with few exceptions. What is this rule? Basically as long as the rules of the match aren't changed the KB:NAN will never interfere in a match in between the opening and closing bell. Before the match? Fine. After the match? Fine. During the match? It would be a violation of the honor code to touch anyone who is in that match. This means that valets/managers, tag partners, and other stable members are not allowed to enter the ring. Valets and Managers can be at ringside, but you will find that they do not get invovled. A match is seen as a contest and this rule can not be broken. Exceptions: If the rules of the match specifically allow it (Hardcore or no-dq matches) and permission has been granted by their main sponsors that are in charge of all factions of the KB, then it would be allowed. And the final exception would be if continued battles with the same opponents show that tactic to be one that the opponents regularly use to teach them a lesson. Also would be approved by the sponsors of the KB stables. Entrance Music 'Ceremony' by the Cult
