Jimmy Thunder Biography Real Name Jimmy Thunder Nicknames Jimmy T Hometown Murphy Idaho via Sydney Australia Height 6' 10 Weight 390lbs Fed Titles United States Champion - Television Champion Fed Awards None yet. Other Fed Awards Core Wrestling, Ringside Wrestling Wrestling Style Street Fighter/ Brawler Finisher Move Thunderstruck Finisher Description A verticle suplex that turns into a brain buster Finisher Setup Move Crack of Thunder Setup Description Whips his opponent into the turnbuckles then nails him with a running clothesline. Entrance Music Your Disease by Saliva Ring Entrances The lights go out as the sound of thunder crashing echos around the arena. A light shines on the entrance to show a silhouette of a large man who then begins to raise his arms over his head and then drops them quickly by his sides which triggers off one huge explosion in front of the light. "Your Disease" by Saliva blasts through the arena's speakers as an enormous cheer from the crowd starts when Jimmy Thunder walks out. He continues on down the aisle while the crowd begin to chant "THUNDER! THUNDER!" in unison as Jimmy doesn't take his eyes off the ring before him. His stone like facial expressions give out no emotion as he climbs up the steps and into the ring before preparing for combat.
