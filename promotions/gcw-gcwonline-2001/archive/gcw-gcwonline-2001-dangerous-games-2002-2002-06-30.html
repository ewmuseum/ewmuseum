Dangerous Games 2002, 27 July 2002

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dangerous Games 2002 Arco Arena, Sacramento, California ---- Introduction: Welcome Back The lights in Arco Arena dim, and the fans begin to cheer wildly in anticipation. As the arena slowly grows dark, the GCW MegaTron lights up, casting shadows over the stage. From a bright white background, the GCW logo slowly fades in to view. The fans continue to go wild, living up to their title as the nation’s loudest arena. After a moment, the logo suddenly fades out, and the MegaTron goes black, plunging Arco into darkness. The cheers continue, but are suddenly drowned out by the explosive pop of a huge ring of fire high above the ring! Blue and red pyro erupts from the rafters and flies up the entrance ramp, before gold and silver sparks begin falling from the top of the MegaTron! The entire crowd leaps to its feet as the house lights go up and the cameras begin flying over the crowd. A familiar voice begins to speak over the crazed fans, as Dangerous Games begins. Bryan- Global Championship Wrestling has returned, and tonight Dangerous Games will crown a new GCW Heavyweight Champion! Hello everyone, I’m James Bryan once again at ringside with my broadcast colleague David Yale, and Dave, it’s good to be home! Yale- And it’s good to be employed, JB! We’re here in the country’s loudest arena in Sacramento, a fitting place to hold the comeback Pay-Per-View event for the greatest wrestling company in the world! And what better way to return to action than with a huge battle royal! Bryan- Twenty men from all over the world have come to Dangerous Games tonight to compete in this tremendous event, and while many of them may leave here tonight with GCW contracts, only one will leave with the GCW Title! Yale- And to top it off, JB, we’re going to hear from GCW President Steven Caldera in just moments! And I really can’t wait to hear what he has to say about the second run of his company! Right on cue, Established hits on the speakers, and the crowd remains on its feet. An explosive round of both boos and cheers erupts as Steven Caldera himself appears on stage, wearing his finest Italian suit and smiling broadly. Yale- And there he is, JB! The man himself! Bryan- Steven Caldera was the man who started this company over a year ago, and tonight he has shown up to prove that he intends to continue on! But all those accolades aside, David Yale, he has also proven in the past year that he is a low, low human being that can not, under any circumstances, be trusted! Yale- How could you say that about your employer, JB!? I, for one, trust Steven Caldera with my life! Bryan- Then you’re a dead man. Caldera steps through the ropes and into the ring, finding that the boos from the crowd have taken over. Obviously, the fans have not forgotten who he is. However, he straightens his tie and smiles yet again, not willing to let the crowd get him down. After a moment he raises a microphone to his mouth as both his music and the crowd settles down. Yale- Here we go! Caldera- Boy, is it good to be back home! This statement elicits at least a modest array of cheers. Caldera- You know, after GCW closed down, I realized as I was trying to clean things out that I simply wasn’t done yet. GCW is my home, it’s where I belong, and I could never go anywhere else. Once GCW is done, so is Steven Caldera. So selfish as it may be, I decided to keep things going, simply because I wasn’t ready to ride off into the sunset. Yale- Fair enough for you, JB? Caldera- But that being said, I need to clear some things up. I know that I didn’t exactly adhere myself to you people the first time around. I came out here tonight to address the fans of Global Championship Wrestling. You are the people that really keep this company rolling. You are the people who really own GCW. You are the people who have stuck with us and kept us going through the good times and the bad. And for that, I thank you. And despite the fact that it was your money that funded this company, I was still coming out here each and every month and demanding total control. And for that… I apologize. Caldera’s smile having faded into a look of total sincerity, tucks the microphone under his arm and begins applauding upwards towards the crowd. A shocked Arco Arena in turn begins applauding and cheering for Caldera, though it seems obvious that quite a few are still weary of the President. Yale- Wow! Bryan- How about this change of heart! But I’m still not sure I buy it, Dave. Yale- And I’m not sure I want to! Caldera- Now I know you have no reason to trust me, so let me just say this and we’ll get on with the evening. This time around, there will be some changes. No more intervention from me. There will be no President on Meltdown. We won’t have any commissioner, no joint stockholders, no authority figures interfering with the course of the company. The referees, the ring crew, they will be the ones in charge. I’ll stay at home and sign checks and make deals, but I won’t be here with my hands on the strings any more. After I leave this ring tonight… you may very well never see me again. This creates an explosion of cheers from the crowd, some happy to see him go, while others excited to see him honestly changing. Yale- WHAT?! Bryan- WOW! That is absolutely huge, ladies and gentlemen! Steven Caldera has just promised to steer clear of the goings on of his own company! But I still don’t believe it! Caldera- Whoever wins the GCW World Heavyweight Championship title tonight will do so based on merit and skill alone. No more interference from me. Whoever goes home with the title tonight will truly deserve it. That being said, I think it’s about time we get on with Dangerous Games! The fans begin to cheer again, as Caldera turns to step out of the ring. However, as he places his left leg though the ropes, he stops abruptly. Might of Rome hits, and the arena immediately becomes overwhelmed with boos. Octavian emerges from the back clad in his elaborate golden entrance attire, complimented by one very important piece of gold… the GCW World Heavyweight Title belt. Caldera backs up and stops in the center of the ring, watching Octavian make his way to the ring. Bryan- Wait a minute! Caldera was about to take his farewell march up the aisle, but he’s been stopped by Octavian! I was wondering about this! Caldera helped Octavian capture the GCW title for a third time before the company closed, and they have been close friends for years! Caldera has changed, but has Octavian? Yale- Well, we’re about to find out! But what I want to know is what Caldera is going to do to reimburse Octavian after announcing that he was no longer GCW champion! Bryan- Well, Octavian is obviously confused here as we are, because he’s still carrying the GCW World Title belt! Octavian, grimacing, hops onto the apron and enters the ring, as his music cuts out. Octavian walks straight to Caldera and snatches his microphone away. The crowd continues to boo as Might of Rome fades out, and Octavian immediately begins speaking in a very heated tone. Octavian- What do you think you’re doing? Huh? What do you think just happened out here, Steven? You thought you could just walk away? Nobody walks away from me, Steven. Nobody. My father couldn’t. And you certainly won’t. You see, you and I have been a team since day one. And ever since, we’ve dominated everywhere we’ve gone. But you need to understand one thing, Steven. And that’s that at no point did I consider myself below you, or subservient to you in any way. We were partners, Steven. No, we weren’t partners… I was YOUR boss! I always was number one, Steven. I am the one who has kept this company running for so long. Without me, you’d have been torn apart countless times by all the people in the back who to this day are still waiting to rip you limb from limb. But no, I protected you, and in return to awarded me this belt. That’s how it was, that’s how it is, and that’s how it always will be. You and I, united to control this company with an iron fist! The crowd explodes with boos as Yale and Bryan sit in awe. Caldera’s face has grown grim, but he suddenly reaches out and snatches the microphone back from Octavian, causing Octavian’s eyes to flash red. Caldera- First of all… This no longer belongs to you. Caldera places his arm on Octavian’s shoulder, and quickly grabs the GCW title belt. Octavian is infuriated as Caldera slings the belt over his own shoulder, his face looking intense but not making eye contact with the former champion. Caldera- We’ll find out how much you deserve it tonight. I don’t doubt that you’re a great athlete, but you don’t have my help any more. Caldera finally raises his eyes to meet Octavian’s. The Roman looks as though he is prepared to destroy Caldera that very moment, but is showing some restraint. Caldera- Now... If you so much as lay a hand on me, you’re fired on the spot. If I see you trying in any way to cheat your way to win here tonight, you’re fired on the spot. I will have no more of this crap from you Gaius. I am putting my foot down here and now. Now I’m going to take MY belt and head back up that ramp, and I won’t be back. But just because I’m not here doesn’t mean I’m not watching. I can end your career with a simple phone call, Gaius. So do not test me. Bryan- Good God! The crowd explodes as Established cues up again. Caldera walks right by Octavian and nimbly exits the ring. Octavian continues to stare out in the same direction, the anger and rage building up as though it might explode at any moment. The crowd continues to cheer for Caldera as he marches up the ramp, his face looking proud, the GCW title still over his shoulder. Yale- I don’t believe it! This can’t be true! Reconsider, Steven! Bryan- Steven Caldera has really shocked us all here tonight, ladies and gentlemen! He’s turned his back on Octavian, and he’s promised not to get involved any more with the company! Tonight we will crown a worthy champion! Caldera suddenly stops, turns back to the ring, and shouts for Octavian. Caldera- Oh, and one more thing, Gaius… Octavian warily turns to face his former friend, still looking as though he wants to kill the President. Caldera- You might want to know when you’re entering tonight’s battle royal. You were right about one thing, Gaius. You’re number one. Yale- WHAT?!?! Bryan- My God! Caldera’s last act has been to stick Octavian as the number one entrant! He’s going to have to go through nineteen other guys to get his belt back tonight! Caldera smiles at an enraged Octavian, who begins angrily storming around the ring. Caldera turns again and exits the arena, as five referees emerge and head to the ring. Caldera stops senior referee Josh Briggs and hands him the GCW title belt to be presented to the winner. Caldera then takes one quick glance out at the cheering fans, and leaves Arco Arena. Bryan- Octavian is number one in tonight’s battle royal! And we’re just moments from getting underway! Octavian’s face now grows serious, and as he removes his golden entrance attire he begins to look more and more determined, wisely not allowing his temper to get the best of him. Yale- So if Octavian is number one, who is number two? Bryan- We’re about to find out! Dangerous Games is getting started! ---- The Dangerous Games Match Ring announcer Joey Andrews takes a perch on the ring steps, a safe distance from Octavian. The crowd cheers wildly as he announces the match. Andrews- Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is a fifty man battle royal for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! Every ninety seconds, another entrant will make his or her way into the ring. Competitors are eliminated when they are thrown over the top rope, and both feet touch the floor. The last man standing will be awarded the GCW title! Having finished his explanation, Andrews steps back to the floor and the arena grows quiet, awaiting the introduction of the number two entrant who will go at it with an angered Octavian. Bryan- There's the bell! This one is under way! We're just awaiting out number two entrant! After a moment, I Disappear hits and the crowd explodes with cheers! Michael Hardy rushes down the ramp towards the ring, never hesitating a moment. Hardy slides into the ring immediately and locks up with Octavian. Bryan- It's Mike Hardy! Yale- Oh, poor Mike is dead! Bryan- Hardy goes right after Octavian, but Octavian is waiting with some big right hands! Octavian begins hammering away at Hardy's skull, before whipping him off to the ropes! Hardy bounces back, and ducks a clothesline from Octavian! Hardy bounces off the other side, and ducks a back elbow! Hardy hits one more time, and comes flying off with a huge clothesline, taking Octavian down!! Yale- Wow! Octavian scrambles back to his feet, but Hardy nails him with a big right hand! Where is this kid getting his intensity from!? Bryan- Octavian is staggered into the ropes, and Hardy charges him! Hardy connects with another big clothesline! Octavian is sent flying over the top rope, and the crowd explodes with cheers! Octavian manages to hook the middle rope and land with a thud on the apron, before rolling back into the ring. Yale- Whoa! Hardy almost eliminated Octavian there! Bryan- This place would have come unglued if that had happened! Octavian gets back to his feet now, but Hardy moves in and lays in a big knife edge chop, sending Octavian back to the corner! But the clock is ticking down, let's see who number three is! The crowd chants with the clock on the MegaTron as is winds down.... Five! Four! Three! Two! One!! A loud buzzer goes off, and Burn by Nine Inch Nails cues up. The crowd explodes with boos as Demented rushes out from behind the stage and charges down to the ring. Hardy, having left Octavian lying in the corner, turns to see his arch nemesis sliding under the ropes, and moves in to meet him. Bryan- It's Demented! Demented is number three! Bryan- We all know the history between these two! They've had one of the most brutal feuds ever! And Hardy meets Demented with a big right hand! Demented gets to his feet and fires back, but Hardy quickly shoots him over to the ropes! Yale- Demented bounces back, and Hardy takes him down with an incredible high leg clothesline! Bryan- And this crowd is solidly behind Michael Hardy here so far! Demented gets back to his feet, and Hardy charges with a clothesline! But Demented ducks, and Hardy walks into a huge dropkick from Octavian out of nowhere! Hardy goes down, and Demented and Octavian immediately begin stomping away. As they beat Hardy down, the buzzer again begins to wind down, with the crowd chanting along. It finally hits zero, and the buzzer sounds. Kippo's music hits, and the crowd erupts as the Almighty Kippo emerges from the back! Kippo walks briskly to the ring, seemingly confident but not quite ready to get into action yet. Bryan- Kippo! The Almighty Kippo, former Television Champion is back and in at number four! Kippo is taking his time heading to the ring, as Demented and Octavian continue pounding away at Hardy in the corner! Yale- But wait, Octavian turns and nails Demented! Demented staggers back, and Octavian moves back in with a big kick to the kidneys! Demented goes down to a knee, as Kippo finally climbs into the ring! Bryan- Octavian doesn't even give Kippo a glance, as Kippo moves around the edge of the ring to the corner! Hardy is struggling to get back to his feet now, as Demented manages to connect with a knife edge chop to Octavian! Yale- Octavian quickly fires back, and nails Demented with a huge right hand, sending Demented right over the top and down to the apron! There's the Roman intensity of old, JB! Octavian is going to win this thing, I can feel it! Bryan- Octavian turns and nails Hardy with a huge knee to the gut, sending him back to the mat! And Kippo is staying in the corner, watching on! Once again, the crowd begins chanting down from five, as the clock runs out. The buzzer sounds, and the music hits. Mike Scott, newcomer to GCW, emerges from the back and rushes down to the ring. With great energy he springs into the ring, but is quickly assaulted from behind by Kippo. Bryan- Mike Scott is number five, and is already in trouble with Kippo! Octavian is back in the corner choking Hardy with his boot, Demented is still hurting in the corner, and Kippo is taking it to Scott in the other corner! Yale- Demented finally struggles back up to his feet, and moves in with a big forearm to the spine of Kippo! Kippo goes down to a knee, but Scott quickly nails Demented with a big right hand between the eyes! Bryan- Demented staggers back, but Octavian comes flying from across the ring and nails Scott with a HUGE clothesline, sending him over the top rope and out to the floor! Mike Scott has been eliminated! Yale- Fifth one in, first one out! Welcome to GCW, Mike! Ha! Bryan- Octavian turns and nails Kippo with a right hand now, but Kippo blocks a second one and nails Octavian with a big knee to the gut, followed by a huge swinging neckbreaker! Great combination there from Kippo! The clock begins to wind down again, and the fans rise to their feet to watch the entrance. The buzzer sounds, and Hellz Movie cues up. The fans begin to boo as Capo emerges from the stage area, and rushes to the ring. He slides in with great energy, and goes right for Octavian. Bryan- Capo is number six! And he goes right for Octavian! Yale- It's almost as if Octavian has a huge bulls-eye on his back! Kippo and Capo are now double teaming Octavian.... hey, Kippo and Capo, that's catchy! Bryan- Hardy is back on his feet now, and he moves in to begin stomping away at the former champion! Octavian is getting beaten down by three men here! But Demented moves in and destroys Hardy with a huge clothesline! Yale- Demented begins stomping away at Hardy here, as Octavian manages to take Capo's leg out from under him, dropping him to the mat! Octavian powers his way back to his feet and nails Kippo with a big shot to the skull as well, following it up with a big boot to the face! He's back in control! Bryan- Kippo and Capo are down, Demented and Hardy are going at it in the corner, and Octavian is catching his breath in the center of the ring. And here comes another entrant! The crowd begins to murmur as the buzzer sounds again. After a brief pause, My Own Summer cues up, and the crowd explodes with cheers! The Jhub, looking better than ever, emerges from behind the stage area and marches to the ring with a determined look on his face! The fans cheer his every move as he heads to the ring, locking eyes with Octavian as he nears it. The Jhub quickly climbs onto the apron and steps inside the ring, moving right at Octavian! Bryan- It's The Jhub!!! Yale- WHAT?! He's not supposed to be here! Bryan- The Jhub is back in GCW, and he's in at lucky number seven! And he's gone right for Octavian! The Jhub is pummeling away at Octavian here, hammering him with hard right hands, backing him into the ropes! Yale- Oh no! Damn it! Come on, Octy! Bryan- Octavian is getting pummeled now, as The Jhub backs him into the ropes! He's got Octavian hung up in the ropes, as he backs up for a big clothesline! The Jhub charges, but Demented comes out of nowhere and grabs hold of his arm, spinning him around and leveling The Jhub with a big DDT! Yale- Ha! There we go! Octavian is really hurting here, as he slumps down to the mat! Kippo and Capo now have split off, and are going at it with Mike Hardy in the corner! Bryan- Poor Mike is getting destroyed here tonight- Yale- ....But that's nothing new to him, is it? Heh. As The Jhub painfully begins to get to his feet despite Demented's continued onslaught, the timer winds down and hits zero. Hero hits, and Chance emerges from the backstage area to a passive reaction from the crowd. Bryan- And Chance is heading to the ring at number eight! This kid is a hot new prospect making his GCW debut tonight, and he's already got a ring full of incredible talent to tend with! Yale- Chance moves right for the ring and is immediately charged by Demented! Bryan- Demented went for the quick elimination with a clothesline, but Chance ducks it and nails Demented with a huge right hand! Good Lord! Chance is hammering away here with some big right hands! Demented is getting pummeled! Yale- Wow! How about this Chance guy coming out with all this energy! Bryan- Kippo and Capo are destroying Hardy in the corner still, but The Jhub is getting back to his feet! The Jhub moves in behind Capo, and nails him with a big forearm to the spine! Kippo turns around, and gets nailed with a huge swinging neckbreaker! Yale- Octavian is struggling back to his feet again, but Mike Hardy explodes out of nowhere with a dropkick to the side of the face! Where the hell did that come from?! As Hardy slumps back down in pain, the timer winds down yet again. The buzzer sounds, and Rivero's music explodes into the arena. The crowd erupts with boos as Dan Rivero emerges from the back and sprints down to the ring. Bryan- It's Dan Rivero! Yale- Oh God No! Bryan- Dan Rivero is back in GCW! Yale- No no no no no no no no!!! Bryan- Rivero slides under the ropes, and goes right after The Jhub! Rivero rushes into the ring, but The Jhub steps aside and gives him a big heave, sending him right over the top rope! The crowd explodes as Rivero goes flying, but he manages to cling to the second rope on the way down, and only one of his feet hits the ground. Rivero quickly rolls back under the ropes, only to catch a stomp from The Jhub. Bryan- Whoa! Rivero was almost eliminated right off the bat! Yale- Oh, that would have been great, JB! Bryan- Rivero is catching a beating here from The Jhub, as Kippo and Capo regroup on the other side! Octavian is back on his feet now and watching the action from a neutral corner, as Chance now puts the boots to Hardy on the remaining side! Yale- Chance is really taking it to these GCW veterans, JB! I kinda like that! Boy, wouldn't it be great if he eliminated Rivero? That would be great. Again the clock ticks off its remaining seconds before it resets. As it does, Just Got Wicked cues up to a massive response from the crowd! Jason Dread emerges from the stage and slowly begins stalking to the ring, reveling in the incredible array of cheers and boos. Action in the ring actually stops as Kippo, Capo, The Jhub, and Chance all turn to watch Dread walking towards the ring. Bryan- Jason Dread! Jason Dread is here in Sacramento for Dangerous Games! And all attention is now focused on the former GCW World Heavyweight Champion! Could Jason Dread leave here tonight with his second world title? Yale- Keep in mind that Jason Dread has had some brutal battles with some of the men in the ring right now, including Demented, who defeated him just a few months ago! And Demented slides under the ropes and moves out to meet Dread! Bryan- Demented is greeted with a big right hand from Dread, and the two begin their brawl on the outside! And Octavian charges The Jhub from behind in the ring, nailing him right in the back of the head! Yale- Whoa! The Jhub almost went over there! Bryan- Octavian is hammering away on The Jhub here, as Dread rolls Demented back into the ring and then steps inside himself! Dread has officially entered the battle royal! Yale- But Kippo and Capo immediately go after Dread! And Dread greets both of them with quick stomps to the gut! Jason Dread has come in on fire here! But now Chance charges in from behind and nails Dread with a dropkick to the back of the knee! And Dread is down! Bryan- But now Hardy moves back in with a dropkick to the back of Chance's head! And Chance tumbles into the corner! Hardy now goes right for Capo, who begins firing back with some heavy right hands! Yale- But now Demented is back up, and he connects with a big right hand to Hardy's kidney's! And The Jhub and Octavian are still going at it in the corner! The crowd, now on fire with the entrance of Dread, begins chanting down as the clock ticks away its final five seconds once again. The clock hits zero, and the crowd becomes still as they wait for a few moments before the next entrant appears. Debonair cues up and begins blasting into Arco, accompanied immediately by an incredible reaction from the fans! The action in the ring seems to be oblivious as a familiar dark figure begins slowly marching to the ring.... Bryan- KHAN! Yale- Oh My God!! Bryan- Khan is back in Global Championship Wrestling! Khan is here in Sacramento for Dangerous Games! I don't believe it! As the crowd yells out in shock, Khan suddenly breaks into a sprint. He leaps seven feet from the ramp onto the apron with great ease, and hops up onto the turnbuckle, before quickly launching himself halfway across the ring and nailing Demented with a huge missile dropkick! Bryan- My God! Yale- Did you see that?! Bryan- Khan just flew from one end of this arena to the other in no time flat! Simply amazing! So much for the knee and back injuries I suppose! Yale- Kippo turns around and charges right at Khan, but Khan immediately drops him with an incredibly stiff clothesline! Capo follows suit, but catches a lightening quick standing dropkick to the chin! Khan is leveling the entire ring here! Bryan- Hardy is getting hammered by Chance in the corner, as The Jhub chokes Octavian over the second rope! Khan quickly moves over to the corner now, and Chance spins around to catch a vicious standing sidekick! Khan has leveled almost the entire ring! Yale- Hardy now begins to pull himself back to his feet, and comes face to face with Khan! Watch out! Oh, poor Mike! The fans all jump to their feet and explode with cheers as former GCW Champion Tristain Mayhem emerges on stage! Khan turns his attention away from Hardy for the moment and faces Mayhem, who locks eyes with him quickly. Mayhem happily struts his way down to the ring, as Khan patiently awaits his arrival. Bryan- Oh Man! It's Tristain Mayhem! One of the most controversial figures in GCW history is here for Dangerous Games! And he's here at number twelve! Mayhem steps onto the apron and Khan moves in, but Dan Rivero catches his arm from behind and nails him with a knee to the spine! Yale- But Khan spins right around and nails Rivero with a monstrous forearm! Thank you Khan! Rivero goes down, but Mayhem takes the opportunity to drill Khan with a big shot to the back of the head! Bryan- Khan drops to a knee, and Mayhem turns and heads right for Octavian! Mayhem pulls The Jhub out of the corner, and immediately begins stomping away at the Ragin' Roman! My God, there are some scores to settle here! Yale- But The Jhub doesn't appreciate Mayhem's involvement! The Jhub now moves back in, and pulls Mayhem away from the corner, sticking the boots to Octavian himself! And now Mayhem is looking a bit angry! Bryan- And Mayhem pulls The Jhub out of the corner again! These two are locked in a rather heated staredown here! The crowd begins to buzz as Mayhem and The Jhub glare at each other. After a pause, the two simply turn back at Octavian, and both begin laying the boots to Octavian in the corner! Bryan- And now both men are assaulting Octavian! But give the man credit, he was number one and he's hanging in there! Yale- Khan moves in and grabs The Jhub from behind! Oh, we knew this was going to happen! The Jhub turns around, and gets nailed with a huge chop to the throat! The Jhub goes down, but Khan is assaulted from behind by Kippo, Capo, and Chance! And Hardy is putting the boots to Rivero in the other corner! As three men struggle to beat Khan down, Octavian manages to get a shot in on Mayhem and power his way out of the corner. As Octavian and Mayhem exchange blows, the clock runs down and the buzzer sounds again. Trailer Trash begins to play in Arco, and the fans erupt with boos as Luke Hanson walks out onto the stage. Hanson marches proudly to the ring, watching the action carefully. Bryan- Hey! It's Luke Hanson! I had no idea he was here tonight!! But he's coming in at lucky number thirteen, JB! Bryan- Hanson reaches the ring and easily steps over the top rope, and charges across the ring! HUGE boot to the face of Mayhem! Good God! Big chokeslam on The Jhub from Hanson! And The Jhub is down again! Man, I'm loving this! Rivero and The Jhub are both getting their asses kicked! As Hanson stops to study the action in the ring, the clock ticks out again. The buzzer sounds, and Life's Sweet Drug hits. The crowd explodes with cheers as Twisted appears from the stage, and walks briskly to the ring. Twizz locks eyes with Hanson immediately, as the fans cheer him on. Bryan- Twisted! Twisted is here at number fourteen! The former tag team champion is heading to the ring! Yale- This guy is nuts, JB! I can't stand him! Bryan- I don't know why anyone would want to do that! It's every man for himself, and any chance you get to eliminate someone, you should take it! Bryan- Hey, wait, Octavian is on the outside! Has he been eliminated? Yale- No, no.... He's just taking a breather, JB!
