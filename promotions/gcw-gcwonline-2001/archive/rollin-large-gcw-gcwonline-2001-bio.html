Rollin Large Biography Biography When you are the size that these two men are, you really don't have much choice when it comes to being involved in sports. Both men actually met when they were competing against each other in gimmick matches for indy promotions in Florida. While the matches drew in crowds, Bill and Antoine could not stand that they were looked at as freaks. The crowds showed up, and they were entertained, but they never showed them the respect that they had for the other members of the roster. It was the Light Heavyweights that brought the fans to their feet, the heavyweights that made them wince, and then there was the comedic relief. They never felt as if they belonged, and the rest of the roster did nothing to ease them of their fears. It was then, after three months in a heated feud that no one cared about, that they decided to band together and do something different. The head promoter got a good laugh out of their suggestion, to be treated as equals and to be allowed to compete with the 'normal' members of the roster, but quickly waved the idea off as a joke and denied their request.
