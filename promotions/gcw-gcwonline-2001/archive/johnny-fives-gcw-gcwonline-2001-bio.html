Johnny Fives Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames "Jiffy" / Johnny Boy / JF Hometown Lake Providence, LA Birthday March 9, 1990 Height 6'3' Weight 202 lb. Fed Titles Working on it. Fed Awards Sexiest man named Johnny Fives ever to join GCW. Biography Nathaniel Banning grew up in one of the poorest towns in the United States with only his mother and younger brother Valentino. His mother never married, and didn't have a job, but strangely enough she was never at a loss for money. Of course, being a very negligent woman, she never took care of her children and indeed Nate had to become the man of the house from an early age. Living in a town once called the poorest place in America by TIME magazine, Nathan had to struggle to earn money for he and his brother, which resulted to a number of odd jobs. Doing his best to stay above the legal line in a town with little to do but have sex, do drugs, and get into gangs was quite difficult, and Nathan never had enough money to keep the roof and lights and also keep his little brother fed. And with the eventual departure and mail forwarding of his mother, it was clear that they'd always be just on the edge of falling apart at the seams. Going to the state for help would result in splitting up the two Bannings, and with no other family to take them in, the young brothers had no options available to them other than a life of hardship. But all of that changed, though, on that fateful day that Cameron Winter knocked on his door.... Other Feds A bunch. Other Fed Titles A bunch. Other Fed Awards Yeah, let's go with that Strengths See below. Weaknesses See below. Wrestling Style Street Fighting with basic wrestling Strategy Strategy: Hitting his opponents until they stop getting up. Unlike his tag team partner, Johnny doesn't mind lying, cheating, faking, stealing, generally doing whatever it takes to win. The conflict of personalities where this is concerned can lead to mistakes in matches. Strengths -- Nathan has a lot of fire and energy, and doesn't know when to quit. He'll always fight to get out of anything be it a submission or a powerbomb. Though not having much formal training, Nathan has a lot of experience in street fighting and has a basic understanding of how the human body works and a thorough understanding of how his own body works. In addition, while not a high-flyer by nature, Johnny is just crazy enough to try never-before-seen brawling maneuvers that he can surprise an opponent that takes him lightly. Weaknesses -- But training is still training. Nate lacks the expertise of the fundamentals of wrestling, meaning that he can be taken advantage of by wily veterans. Not only that, but having never watched wrestling before, one can fill the arena with what he doesn't know about the sport. Nathan is taller than Cameron, but the woman has the greater wrestling knowledge and has to cover for him much of the time; not to mention that she's stronger than him. In a singles match, if Nathan cannot take control from the beginning, it's very difficult for him to (re)gain control without a mistake from his opponent. Andrews: [And the opponent] Coming to the ring from Lake Providence, Louisiana; weighing 202 pounds... JOHNNY FIVES! Though the crowd gives the newbie a chance, the lack of experience and esteem built up with the paying customers results in a lukewarm reaction. With long legs and a quick gait, it doesn't take long for Fives to reach the ring accompanied by his half-sister Cameron Winter. The ring stairs guide the rookie up to the apron, whereupon the African-American wrestler hops over the ring ropes into the squared circle.
