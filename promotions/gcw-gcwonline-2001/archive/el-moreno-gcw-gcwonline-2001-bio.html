El Moreno Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames none Hometown San Diego, CA by way of Tijuana, Mexico Birthday June 20, 1985 Height 6'4 Weight 205 Biography Growing up in Mexico he became a huge fan of Lucha Libre. His earliest memories were that of attending matches in his hometown of Tijuana, Mexico. Though he moved to the United States at age 12, he still attended several matches, as well as becoming a fan of Pro-Wrestling in the United States. He drifted away from it as he got older, getting more into Mixed Martial Arts and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. He also trained to achieve his lifelong goal of becoming a police officer, like his father and his grandfather. He burst onto the scene relatively quickly, almost instantly scoring spots in big organizations. No one knows much about what switched his life goals, but he has certainly made quite an impact in his brief tenure in the business. Using his submissions expertise he has made many an opponent tap out on the independent circuit before finally landing in the bigs. Other Feds none Strengths Strong Arsenal of Strikes, Solid Submission Base from MMA Training Weaknesses Adaption to modern American Pro-Wrestling, Can be overpowered. Wrestling Style Lucha origins with Submission focus Strategy Moreno is a trained mixed martial artist, trained mostly from time in the military and police foundations training. He has a wide array of strikes that he can use and often will. His submission and ground fighting is also exceptional. In terms of striking he is trained in traditional boxing and Muay Thai Kickboxing. He favours using his hands and will often do so. However, he has powerful kicks and knee strikes which he will use on occasion. The straight jab is an effective weapon that he will use in the ring. His submission game is built mainly on chokes and compression holds. He will focus the majority of his submission on offense on chokes and neck based submission holds. He also grew up watching wrestling, so he has knowledge of modern wrestling submission holds as well as actual Jiu Jitsu and Judo submissions. He does know arm and leg joint submissions, but will use these more sparingly. He lacks significant wrestling training and as such will struggle in some parts of the modern American Pro Wrestling game. His chain wrestling isn't all that strong, nor is his knowledge of pro wrestling maneuvres. Basically, he has adapted things he learned from fight training to the ring, so the majority of his offense other than submissions and striking is based on throws and suplexes. As an MMA competitor he is rather large for a 205 pound man, but compared to the behemoths that occupy the rosters of most pro wrestling promotions he is somewhat small. Although he is strong and athletic, those that are significantly bigger or heavier than him may be able to overpower him. In these cases he will need to use technique to defeat his opponents. Although he is neutral, in ring he shows very few heel tendencies. He has a deep respect for competition and discipline from hsi time in the military and also from watching traditional Lucha Libre. So he is unlikely to cheat or use weapons. However, he shows a slight viciousness and stiffness in his striking and submission game.
