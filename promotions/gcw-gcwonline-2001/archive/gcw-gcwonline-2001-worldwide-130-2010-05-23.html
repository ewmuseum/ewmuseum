WorldWide 130 Sydney Superdome — Sydney, New South Wales ---- WW130: The Coronation of a New Number One Garbage Bag Johnny sits behind his desk working meticulously with a stack of toothpicks and a tube of paste, working on some kind of craft. The glue drips and pools on the table as he glues toothpick squares to other toothpick squares. The commissioner has glue on his hands and the sleeves of the suit he borrowed from Steven Caldera. A toothpick snaps. GBJ: Fiddlesticks! Garbage Bag sighs and looks at the pile of toothpicks, tossing the halves of the broken pick to the wayside. He begins counting toothpicks from the pile. He counts aloud. GBJ: Alright, we got one...two...three...five... His attention is suddenly snagged away from the toothpicks by the entrance of an entranced Tessa Windsor, who stands by idly in the door way curiously watching on. Windsor: Need some help there? Her head tips in the direction of…well…whatever it was he was trying to make. GBJ: Oh, hey, Tessa. GBJ surveys his mess. GBJ: Sure, pull up a chair and we can glue some toothpicks together. She obliges, nabbing the chair closest to her along with a handful of toothpicks. Windsor: So I’m presuming there is a method to the madness. Or are we just randomly gluing toothpicks together to see what we come up with? GBJ: First you gotta glue four of them in a square. And then you gotta glue four other ones in a square. Then you glue the squares to the other squares until you got a wall of squares five squares wide by five squares high. Got it? She nods, already reaching out for the bottle of paste. Windsor: Seems easy enough. She pulls the glue her way, as she continues on with her chatter. Windsor: So can I ask what the deal with these stars is? GBJ: Cutting right to the chase, huh? And here I was thinking you just came by to pay me a visit and help me out with this little project here.
