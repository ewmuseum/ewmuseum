Darren Slade Biography Real Name Darren Slade Nicknames Dr Extreme, King of Kaos Hometown San Francisco, CA Birthday September 10, 1980 Height 6'3'' Weight 230lb Fed Titles None Fed Awards N/A Biography Darren Slade was the son of a United States officer, and a very prominent English diplomat. It was their love that brought him life, and it was their lifestyles that drove them apart. Just a year after he was born, his mother and father divorced, and he was granted custody to his mother. His father was crushed, but unwilling to allow his son to go on without a mother. He would move back to the United States where he lived in New York, pursuing a career in acting. He rarely if ever saw Darren. As a child, Darren stayed mostly to himself, although he had a few close friends. He had an arrogance about him that came from his upbringing. His mother was wealthy and confident, and then built up Darren to be the same way. He thought he was better than everyone else, and worked hard to prove it in athletics, academics, and even in life itself. Although he displayed a love for his studies and an energy to succeed, he still had no idea what he wanted to do as a career. His mother worked as an educator. She was a former diplomat and advisor to the Royal Family, but found politics to be boring and repetitive. She moved back to education to teach at Cambridge University where she specialized in foreign affairs and foreign language. It was here that Darren became fluent in several different languages. Darren actually attended many schools in his scholastic career. Namely, four different elementary schools, three different high schools, and two different universities before he was handed a bachelors degree. The fault was Darren’s and Darren’s alone. He never truly found a school he liked, and was constantly badgering his mother to move him to another. His mother got tired of the constant moving, but did as his son wished. It was mostly Darren’s fault, who continually was unsocial with the other children. His arrogance lead to distancing of himself from other children, and eventually he became a loner. His university life was rocky at best. He was strangely attracted to the subjects of philosophy and psychology, and that lead him to believe that it would be the career of his future. An incident in one of his philosophy classes Sophomore year, however, would scar that entire idea. He got into a shouting match with his professor about the idea of a perfect family, and Darren chastised him for being such a narrow minded man. Darren was reprimanded for his words and disrespect for his instructor, but not expelled. Darren would do the University one better, and transfer out to a smaller college. There, he strayed away from the subject matter he used to love, and instead went back to athletics, lettering in both fencing and boxing. He loved to play the flute, and he began to hone his skills in music courses, but he never thought it would be his future. It was just something that kept him busy. He graduated three years later with his bachelors degree, and began working on his doctorate degree in Sociology. It would be his mothers love for the United States, however, that would bring Darren back to his father, and back to the United States. He returned to much fanfare, as most of his loved ones had not seen him in years. They were equally surprised by his grown up son, who had an amazing array of knowledge and a great working vocabulary with English. Still, Darren’s nature kept him distant from his father’s family. While he explored the vast opportunities that America had waiting for him, he realized that he had a passion for action and adventured. He became enamored with American kick boxing and UFC type shoot fighting, and soon began exploring different fighting styles. He settled on a drunken boxing style and submission holds, enjoying the idea that his opponents would submit to his own will. As he perfected his styles, his father encouraged him, finding a true common interest between the two since they met. His mother was appalled by this change in direction, but could do nothing to stop it. Darren eventually made his way to several independent promotions where he flaunted his flashy and hardcore style, implementing a high impact/power wrestling style. Strengths High impact and high reward/risk style. Able to comeback. Weaknesses Frustrated by technical wrestlers, does not work well in holds and grapples Wrestling Style Hardcore, High Impact Strategy Slade is a high impact/hardcore wrestler hybrid. His offense is very spot heavy and uses a variety of strikes. He does not fare well wrestling more technical wrestlers and gets frustrated when placed in different holds and mat based wrestling. Wears down opponents with strikes and high impact moves, and follows that up with a variety of aerial moves from the top rope. Has a tendency to have to make a comeback in matches because of the risks he takes. i) Slade is whipped into the ropes but avoids opponents strike, bounces off the ropes on the other side and counters with a flying forearm smash ii) Slade counters an opponents hold or grapple, and spins opponent into a double armed DDT iii) Slade ducks under a clothesline or punch and slides behind opponent and drags opponent into 3 rolling german suplexes. iiii) Slade and opponent are bouncing off the ropes avoiding each others running strikes and Slade suddenly catches opponent in sleeper hold Finisher Move Second Opinion Finisher Description Slade puts opponent into powerbomb position, lifts them over his head but then drops them into a necbreaker Finisher Setup Move SPIKE!
