Toga Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames "Your Friendly Neighborhood Klepto" Hometown Aye Lik Pi Frat House in Boston, Massachusetts Birthday October 6, 1985 Height 5'7 Weight 163 lbs. Fed Titles GCW Hardcore Title (1) Biography He's a kleptomaniac who loves to party. He is more interested in having fun than he is in being serious, even in the ring. This has caused him to have one of the worst wrestling records in history. He typically isn't very bright, and has a child-like innocence to himself, and everything he does. His sexual orientation is unclear, but due to his ignorance on the subject, he may not even have a preference to begin with. He most often wrestles in a black sleeveless shirt, with a matching skirt. His hair is typically spiked up, with flight goggles up on his forehead. Tube socks and generic sneakers cover his feet, along with big knee pads and elbow pads. He is managed by a nerdy bombshell named Lollie, who's favorite color happens to be green, and the majority of her wardrobe shows that fact off very well. She isn't one to flaunt her own sex appeal, ever. She cares a great deal for Toga and has a very professional attitude, though she isn't against having fun sometimes. She is the brains behind the operation. She isn't afraid to get her hands a little dirty, but she, however, does not wrestle. His best friend just so happens to be a VCR... that he stole from the, then, POW Gladiator Champion, Rodnie Steel's, locker room at one of POW's Pay-Per-Views, Evolution Reborn '06. He likes to carry his broken VCR to the ring in a "Brave Little Toaster" backpack that also has a game of Chutes and Ladders in it, and a shiny toaster once given to his as a gift, among other odd trinkets or toys. His favorite movie is "The Brave Little Toaster," and he enjoys long walks on the beach and candlelit dinners.... on days that end in "Y." Other Feds Powers of Wrestling (PoW), Flaming Pig Wrestling: SmokeHouse (FPWSH) Other Fed Awards PoW's Best Gimmick 2006 Strengths Fast, Agile, Funloving, Good Endurance, Hard-head, Very Awkward to Wrestle Against Weaknesses Ignorant, Too Funloving, Physically Weak, Too Nice, Easily Confused, ADHD, Just Plain Awkward Wrestling Style Aerial, Speedy, Humiliation, Dirty Strategy He doesn't really have one. He just goes out and tries to do whatever he thinks might be fun at the moment. Usually his "play-time" is cut rather short by his opponent. Inside the backpack is a broken and battered VCR along with a game of Chutes and Ladders. He smiles at the audience for a second, before bursting into a sprint down the ramp, past Lollie.
