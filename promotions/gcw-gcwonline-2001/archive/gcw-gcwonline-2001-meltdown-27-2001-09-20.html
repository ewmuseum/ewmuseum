Meltdown 27, 20 September 2001

Meltdown 27 AT&T Center — San Antonio, Texas ---- Meltdown #27 (The camera opens to San Francisco, where the crowd has packed itself into the stands. The entire GCW roster and staff is standing either in or around the ring. The crowd is silent, as Steven Caldera stands in the center of the ring with a microphone.) Caldera- Ladies and gentlemen, we do not wish to give the perpetrators of Tuesday’s attacks the satisfaction of stopping American life. As a result, Meltdown will take place tonight as planned. But we do wish to take a brief moment of silence for those who lost their lives in the terrible tragedy befalling New York and Washington on September 11, 2001. (With that, the roster and the crowd all bow their heads. The bell tolls once, twice, three times. . . And then the arena is silent. The camera watches as not a sole moves, each with their thoughts and prayers extended to the victims and their families. After about thirty seconds, Caldera and the roster raise their heads. The arena begins to applaud loudly, as the roster begins to head to the back. The GCW logo then hits the screen, and the Meltdown intro plays.) (The show returns after the intro, where the crowd is on their feet and going crazy. Pyro explodes from the rafters and the stage, as the show begins.) Bryan- Following one of the most terrible tragedies in American history, GCW is on the air tonight in defiance of fear! Tonight, GCW will bring you its programming after only one week of suspension, and tonight we will hope to entertain you, with the thoughts of the victims in the backs of our minds. Yale- That’s right! All of the nation is rallied this week, but GCW refuses to allow these people to bring down our form of entertainment! So tonight we will bring you our programming as planned, and we’ll have a good time despite the tragedy. (With that, Break Stuff hits.
