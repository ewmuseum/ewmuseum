WorldWide 128 EnergySolutions Arena — Salt Lake City, Utah ---- WW128: The Sun Sets, The Stars Shine, And Thus Begins The Night "I've worked a long, long time to get to this point. Honestly? There are still moments when I still see myself as that nobody who showed up all those years ago. I'm not sure I'll ever get rid of that little, sad bastard inside of me completely...but FOUR times? FOUR? I don't think any have been this sweet." The new GCW World Heavyweight Champion sits on an office chair, the gold of the title belt shining brightly as it rests in his lap. LeStatt Knight grins and for once it doesn't appear to be the mischievous kind. The living legend of GCW seems to be in a very, very good mood. "The gold fits you, big guy. You ready for tonight, I take it? You've just about done and said it all--anything I can think of, anyway. What comes next? What's it gonna be the fourth time around?" Across a large, oak desk from the GCW World Heavyweight Champion, Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny is beaming. His legs are crossed as he leans back in his chair and rests his feet on the desk's surface. He's wearing a dark gray suit with a mustard shirt underneath, looking more dapper than ever as he puffs on a bent billiard tobacco pipe. Knight: I'm intending to do the same as I've always done. I'm going to do the unimaginable; I'm going to shock the world. LeStatt's eyes locate the World Title, he admires its beauty. The champ taps the gold three times with his right hand. Knight: Once you reach a certain point, a person must change his situation up. Things cannot become 'the norm'. I can promise you one thing, GCW will never be the same again. The camera pans from the gold in LeStatt's lap across the gold nameplate in front of the champ on the desk. It reads "Commissioner GBJ." The Commissioner laughs lightly. GBJ: I don't doubt it, man. You put a hell of a show on last week earning number four, and I want to thank you for that. Couldn't help but feel that heading into Denver, not too many people had faith in my ability to pull off the job. I respect the hell out of you, LeStatt, and as the man with the keys, I hope I can do half as much for GCW as you've done, so if there's ever anything I can do for you, my door's always open. Knight: There is something that you can do for me Johnny, something very important. GCW is everything to me; it's been my life for the better part of nine years. Some bad, many great but all of them are memorable. I do not want to see that lost. I want to see GCW flourish no matter what circumstances surround it. Just tell me if you are the man that can make this happen, can you assure me that GCW will prosper? Garbage Bag Johnny does something he doesn't do often, and for a second, it freezes his speech as a chill visibly crawls up the commissioner's spine. He looks LeStatt Knight square in the eye. GBJ: LeStatt...consider it a Garbage Bag Guarantee. GBJ holds up a finger as the rest of his body disappears behind his desk. The Commish shuffles through his cabinets. In one hand, he balances two shot glasses. The other hand follows with a fifth of Patron. Garbage Bag measure out some shots and slides one to the champion. GBJ: Toast to the first ever four time GCW World Heavyweight Champion? Knight takes the glass in his hand and brings it off the table. His smile spreads across his face again. Knight: I can drink to that. The champ lifts his glass and extends his arm out in front of him. GBJ smiles as well and returns the gesture, the shot glasses lightly clicking as they hit each other. Knight: But also…to the future. The champion and the commissioner both throw back their shots and handle them like veterans. Garbage Bag clears the table back into his drawer, but he notices a small relic near the back. He pulls out a pair of handcuffs. GBJ: Hey, remember when I cuffed myself to you in Dangerous Games a couple of years ago? Knight: I'll never look at handcuffs the same way again. GBJ: Thought it'd at least get me second. GBJ grins wryly. GBJ: You want to keep 'em? Might be your lucky charm. Not that you really ever needed luck. GBJ tosses the handcuffs, and the champ snatches them from the air. Knight looks them over briefly before wrapping his fist around them. He looks back up at the commish. Knight: I appreciate the thought... LeStatt slides the handcuffs back to Johnny across the desk. Knight: ...but I won't be needing any luck tonight. ---- Introduction The lights dim in the EnergySolutions Arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Dallas fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: Welcome to Salt Lake City, everybody, for what promises to be another incredible edition of WorldWide under the rule of new commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny. Yale: I've got to say that he's alright in my book after last week. He may not be Steven Caldera, but Johnny was able to book the match that finally, FINALLY got the GCW World Title off of Andy Murray. Bryan: 269 days, Dave. That's a hell of a title run, and I have no doubt that Andy Murray will go down in the history books as one of the greatest World Heavyweight Champions of all time. Yale: But not THE greatest, JB. The greatest GCW Heavyweight Champion is the current champion, the FOUR TIME CHAMPION, LeStatt Knight. Bryan: Knight won an epic battle at WorldWide 127, and his fourth title reign not only breaks a record--it sets one! And now Knight and Murray are set to square off on the SAME side of the ring in tonight's main event. I'm anxious to see if these two can go from giving everything they have to beat each other to working on the same team. Yale: Jay Terror and Clyde Fox are just as disjointed a pair though. I'm not sure I follow Garbage Bag Johnny's thought process here. Bryan: He's a man without a plan, or so he'll have you believe, but before we get to the main event, we've got a lot of ground to cover. We've heard the words "aggressive expansion" thrown around a lot lately, and with the Rampage tournament coming up, it's a great time to see if any of these newcomers have what it takes to make a name for themselves in GCW. Yale: We'll see that as soon as tonight! Recent hires Rick Mad and Logan take on some GCW veterans in Mortifera, and T.A. Giles and Slash are two out of three competitors when they step in the ring against each other and Bishop Steele, who is also still looking to gain a foothold here in GCW. Bryan: Maybe we'll get more details about the tournament tonight, but I'm sure all competitors, new or old, who have yet to really break through to the top will be working hard to get noticed for Rampage. Everyone has to know that winning the Rampage Tournament can be a great career boost. Look at Andy Murray! Look at Clyde Fox! Yale: Yeah, yeah, yeah. Two hacks got lucky and won the tournament, and then they experienced greater success in GCW. I want someone DESERVING to win for a change. Bryan: Well, I'm sure your definition of deserving varies greatly from mine, then, but with GBJ being so tight lipped about the tournament, let's focus on wrestlers who can raise their stock tonight! We've also got two titles up for grabs. The unorthodox PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS defends against SurReal, which just goes to show, if you complain enough, you get what you want. Yale: I'll be sure to keep that in mind. I've been hinting for a raise for far too long. Bryan: Well, if you'd have to fight a horned, crazed beast like PAZUZU, you might reconsider. Yale: I'd rather face PAZUZU than be in Phil Atken's shoes, though. The bumbling Scotsman goes head to head against Hardcore Champion Bryan Mayhem in a first blood match. I sure hope Phil brought some bandages. Mayhem's going to rip him in half. Bryan: Maybe Atken will get lucky, but Mayhem is looking scary in that hardcore division. I was skeptical when I heard Garbage Bag announce the disbanding of the tag belts, but the Hardcore Title does fit perfectly around Mayhem's waist. Yale: Let's not forget, we also have Tim Shipley's first match against newcomer Aaron Fujita. Shipley's got to be pissed that he's just another roster member. He spent last week getting trashed. I should've done the same thing. Bryan: Well, Shipley has as storied a career in the ring as he does behind the desk. Aaron Fujita's going to have to bring out his youth and energy to topple the intelligent style of Tim Shipley. Another interesting match puts two U.S. Title contenders against each other as AXEL Action faces the recently returned American Dream holder, Digital Mortality. Yale: I bet Digi's thinking twice about cashing in that American Dream now that Vivica J. Valentine has the United States Championship! She beat Dan Black, and Black was sent packing! That just goes to show, it's all in the timing, and Digital Mortality might be too late. Bryan: We'll see about that. Mortality is a cunning, cunning man, and if I were Vivica, I'd watch my back. Yale: I'd watch a lot more than my back if I was Vivica, if you know what I'm saying. Bryan: If you're into that sort of thing, I guess. Yale: We also have a rubber match from NC-17 as one of my personal favorite wrestlers, Markus Stone, gets a rematch against Tessa Windsor, and I hope Stone gets his revenge. I also hope Tessa has a wardrobe malfunction! Bryan: Revenge? Wardrobe malfunction? Come on! You saw what Markus Stone did to Tessa last week. That was a travesty and a cheap shot! I've heard Tessa isn't even cleared to compete tonight after Stone's traitorous attack! Yale: I guess we'll have to wait and see, won't we? Bryan: We won't have to wait too long, though. Stay tuned for a night of incredible action here in the EnergySolutions Arena in Salt Lake City, Utah! ---- A challenge of The lights in the arena shut off and flames erupt on the stage circling it. "Hollowed" by Submersed begins over the speakers as a red light fills the arena. Out onto the stage steps "The Living Nightmare" Donny Diamond with the flames reflecting off from his deep wine colored attire and coat. He crosses his arms in the form of an "X" across his chest before raising them into the air. Bryan: I thought the rumors said Donny Diamond was retiring? Yale: You do know they're called rumors for a reason right? As he steps down onto the aisle flames run down each side as fog covers his footing. He stops for a moment to look at the ring before making a small run and sliding in under the bottom rope. Bryan: Oh, I know...maybe since his new era didn't start as planned; he'll come out and blame SurReal some more. Yale: Or if we're lucky, he'll come slap some respect into you! Climbing to his feet Donny leans against the ropes and begins removing his sunglasses and trench coat. Afterwards, he walks back into the middle of the ring and makes the "X" formation again as flames erupt from the ring posts. The lights return to normal and the music comes to an end as he walks to the far side of the ring and motions for a microphone. Donny Diamond: So last week an interview aired with me saying that a new era was going to begin and that I would gain my first win in GCW. A few fans in the front row can be seen laughing. Donny Diamond: Well as we saw with the closing to my match, that didn't exactly happen. Instead I was on the receiving end of Bryan Mayhem's Oblivion Bomb. This is the second time in GCW that I have been given a chance to become a champion, but instead I find myself cheated once again. Bryan: Like I said earlier; Donny Diamond was going to come out here and blame someone else for his loss. Yale: Well in fairness to The Living Nightmare, there was someone else in the ring last week. He leans against the ropes and flips himself over the top onto the outside where he lands on his feet. Lifting up the apron he searches under the ring for a moment before pulling out a bat. Knocking it across the ring post a couple of times, he grins before rolling back into the ring. He picks the microphone up from the mat and begins speaking again. Donny Diamond: I had it all set for victory last week, that gigantic jackass had his leg stuck in a table. I was ready to hit me a homer and send the trash packing back to the office of that garbage we call a Commissioner. Until YOU had to insert yourself into my business, and it's because of YOU that I'm not the Hardcore Champion right now! Bryan: This cannot be real! Is he seriously going to blame Bishop Steele for his loss? Yale: Well in his defense Bishop Steele hasn't won a match in...forever? Donny Diamond: I'm the kind of guy who solves a problem before it can evolve into something bigger. So here's what we're going to do; next week on WorldWide the two of us will come out here and there will be a winner. This isn't a challenge to you Bishop Steele, and this isn't me asking our Commissioner for a match, this is me giving you a chance. You want to be taken seriously in GCW? Then next week come out here to this ring, bring your A-Game and pin my shoulders down to the mat! Make it happen, Johnny, because if you don't next week I will make my way to your office and choke you out myself! Bryan: Now this is something the fans of GCW need to see! Two wrestlers looking to not just get a win on their records, but looking to make a statement! Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny, you have got to make this match happen! Yale: I got to agree, this match has “awesome” written all over it! Dropping the microphone to the mat, “Hollow” by Submersed begins to play again. The lights in the arena dim as Donny Diamond lifts the baseball bat into the air. A bolt of lightning comes striking down onto the tip setting it ablaze as the cameras roll to commercials. ---- Getting Trashed Masked behind the cover of a fichus plant, Garbage Bag Johnny lets loose a burning growl after swigging some more from the Patron stashed in his bottom drawer. The commissioner is heard quickly screwing the cap back on the tequila bottle, but a knock at the door hastens his pace as he shoves the bottle back in the drawer before kicking it shut. GBJ: Door's open. The door swings open and the stench of rotten trash fills the air. Trailing behind that fetid odor is a ragged man, homeless in appearance dressed in tattered wrestling gear in Dallas Cowboys colors. His skin is covered with black splotches, obviously dirt. His light blonde hair is greasy and matted to his skull, tinged with brown from many nights of sleeping on the ground. A tangled mass of facial hair hangs from his chin. It's caked in garbage and dried face paint and reeks of spoiled food. AXEL: Johnny. Garbage Bag Johnny stands up, wafting away the stench with a stack of official papers as he circles the haggard man standing at the door. He doesn't really know what to make of the man, who only vaguely resembles AXEL. GBJ: God damn. I ain't even ever been this low. AXEL: I know. I've hit rock bottom and you're not the first to point it out. Not quite sure why he's being inspected so thoroughly, AXEL crosses his arms over his chest. The situation is a mildly uncomfortable one at best, as he's not used to being in such an unclean state; especially not around people. GBJ: I was wondering where you were at last week, but I didn't know you were this bad. That sonuvabitch took you for all you had, huh? AXEL: They took everything, even my dog. Black's lawyer called me the day after I got home from Arlington and said I had a week to find somewhere else to stay. Two days later, I was evicted and the repo man came for my things. He even took the clothes I was wearing at the time. As you can see, this was all I had left. GBJ: Well, you can't go out there looking like that for your match tonight. Let me see if I can get you dressed up in something proper...and be sure to hit the locker room for a shower before you go out there. Can't have those fleas spreading around. AXEL Action shakes his head, attempting to run his fingers through his hair. This only illicits an agonized grunt as the knots in his hair are tugged. He knows GBJ is right, but the last thing on his mind is a shower, much less a match that he wasn't even aware he was booked in. AXEL: I don't know anything about any match, but what I do know is that I came here to ask a favor. You see, the paperwork pertaining to that lawsuit states that Dan Black gets approximately three quarters of each of my paychecks until further notice. That's barely going to leave me with enough money to keep myself fed. So, I was wondering if you could give me some extra work to do aside from wrestling my matches? You know, an odd job here and there to supplement what little pay I'll be getting. GBJ strokes his chin, thoughtfully. GBJ: I'm sure I can find something for you to do. Can I trust you with an important task? AXEL nods, his dingy hair flopping about as he does. AXEL: Yes. It doesn't matter what it is, I'll do it. GBJ: Alright, man, now I'm going to need you to pay attention, because there's no margin for error here. GBJ leans in really close and AXEL nods. GBJ: Once you get out of the office, head down the hallway to the right. Take the second right you can, head down that hallway for a ways and make the first left, you got it? Once you make that left, there should be a catering table at about 40 paces with a meat and cheese tray lined up. Make me as many cracker sandwiches as you can. I want three slices of meat and three slices of cheese on both, but mix it up so it goes meat, cheese, meat, cheese, meat, cheese. You got all that? AXEL: So let me get this straight...you want me to make you some cracker sandwiches, arranging the filling so that it alternates meat, cheese, meat, cheese and so forth? GBJ points to his head and then points to AXEL's. GBJ: Bingo. If anything else comes up, I'll page you. GBJ reaches into his pocket and tosses a page to AXEL Action. GBJ: You get the job done right, and I'll get you in on something real important next time. AXEL nods, catching the pager in his left hand. Scratching the back of his head, he blinks. AXEL: Should I shower first? I'm assuming you don't want my filth all over your snack. GBJ: I'd say so. Garbage Bag Johnny gets up, measuring AXEL, before digging through his gym bag and tossing AXEL an extra change of clothes...nothing fancy, just a pair of red running shorts and a white t-shirt. GBJ: We're about the same size, so that should fit you. The commissioner then reaches into his pocket and pulls out his wallet to pick out a crisp five dollar bill. He straightens the bill out and hands it to Action. GBJ: Half now...half when the job's done. Taking the clean clothes in hand, AXEL smiles for the first time in weeks. His teeth are tinted yellow, with bits of garbage and food in between. He's even developed a bad case of halitosis, being unable to brush and all. AXEL: Thank you so much! You won't regret this. You really won't. Action folds the clothes up and places them under his arm so that he can hold on to them. After all, wrestling tights don't have pockets and one hand is being taken up by his new pager. Taking hold of the fiver he is offered, he nods appreciatively and turns to head toward the door. He looks back over his shoulder, his features still twisted into that less-than-gleaming smile. AXEL: Once again, thank you. This means a lot. The commissioner tips an imaginary cap at AXEL Action, and Action leaves to get to work. GBJ shakes his head pitifully and sighs at the low AXEL has hit. With AXEL out of sight the commissioner reaches for his bottom drawer again, unscrewing the cap to his Patron. GBJ: Poor kid. The commissioner throws the bottle back and shakes as the gulp disagrees with his throat. Bryan: AXEL Action doesn't look like he's in very good shape right now. It's just like Dan Black to sue the man for everything he's got and then leave GCW. Yale: He's got to pay for his sex rehab facility bills somehow. Bryan: Speaking of sex rehab facilities, we've got Mortifera and newcomers Rick Mad and Logan up next! ---- Logan and Rick Mad vs. Mortifera The arena goes dark as the sounds of hammers falling are heard. Each strike of the hammer upon the anvil light sparks burst from around the ring entrance. Andrews: Making their way to the ring, at a combined weight of 740 pounds. . . MORTIFERA! Bryan: Well folks, it looks like it is time for the first match of the night. Yale: First slaughter of the night. This is not the way I would want to make my debut, against a very angry Mortifera. Bryan: After losing the Tag Team Championship to Bryan Mayhem at NC-17, you have to believe those two are going to be out to prove a point tonight. Suddenly a huge fireball explosion erupts around the entrance way as 'Surprise You're Dead' by Faith No More blasts over the PA system. Shaman and Samuelsson step through the curtains as they both raise their arms, the word 'Mortifera' explodes onto the MegaTron in metal letters with jagged sharp edges and flames burn in the background behind them. Shaman and Samuelsson both climb up the side of the ring. Both nod to each other as they both easily step over the top rope looking around the empty ring. They both await the match to begin as neither man is showing any type of emotion. Bryan: They look calm and collected, but I can't imagine they want anything more than to rip into someone right now. Yale: I hope the new talent GCW has brought in is ready. The arena lights dim as Evenflow by Pearl Jam hits. The crowd begins to cheer as green lights flash at the entry ramp. Andrews: Making their way to the ring, weighing in at a combined 530 pounds. . . RICK MAD AND LOGAN! Bryan: These two had to be excited to get their contracts, but I imagine that excitement was quickly extinguished when they discovered who they were facing on their first night. Yale: First night or not, no one wants to face to stand opposite Mortifera. Rick Mad and Logan step out from behind the curtain and become engulfed in the mass of the GCW fanbase. Rick walks to the ring, slapping a few hands as he goes while Logan stays nearby, jumping up and down as he soaks up the energy from the building. The whole arena is illuminated by green light. The two climb up the ring stairs and enter the ring while chants of 'YOU'RE GOING TO DIE' rain down on them. Yale: Not a good sign. Bryan: I thought Mormons were supposed to be nice. Yale: Even Mormons can tell a beating when they see one. Rick then climbs up one turnbuckle while Logan climbs the opposite. Both raise their arms as green pyro blasts from the other two. Climbing down, the two move to their corner to wait the silent moments before the bell. DING-DING! Shaman steps out of his corner while Logan joins him in the center of the ring. Bryan: At his size, Logan isn't use to being the smaller one in the ring, so it will be interesting how he treats this. Yale: Godzilla would feel small against Shaman. Cameron Wrigley steps back and allows the two big men to get right in each other's face. To the shock and awe of the EnergySolutions Arena, Logan comes right out of the gates with a big right hand to Shaman. Granted Shaman didn't even flinch, but still, shock and awe that he actually attempted it. Shaman reaches back to answer with one of his own but Logan is quick to get out of the way and tags in Rick Mad. Yale: Well that didn't take long. Bryan: It feels like Rick Mad is being set up. All Logan did was poke the wasp nest for him. Yale: Giant three-hundred and thirty pound Native American wasp. Without a hesitation Rick Mad bolts into the ring and runs straight up to Shaman, delivering his own right hand. For the second straight time the Native American refuses to budge. For the second time the person opposite Shaman runs away before the increasingly agitated Mojave Warrior could strike back. Before Logan even realizes what is going on, he is tagged back into the match. Yale: This is getting us nowhere. Cameron Wrigley signals for Logan to enter the ring just as Rick Mad makes his exit through the ropes. Resistively Logan does as he is told and enters for his partner. Wrigley again steps back to allow the action to continue. Shaman stands back, still in the center of the ring, waiting for his next opponent to come at him. Bryan: Looks like they will be keeping on the same train of thought. Indeed, Logan rushes Shaman just as he did before and reaches back with his right fist. This time, however, Shaman catches the fist and uses it to pull Logan in close to him. The crowd starts to rise up, feelings a big slam coming, but Shaman uses his momentum to latch on an air-tight sleeper hold. Yale: And there we go; Shaman is going back to that technical aspect he's been working on. Bryan: We'll have to see if it has been polished since his last outing. A giant sigh releases from the crowd, who was hoping to see a big impact. For Logan, however, getting slammed down with all the might of the Native American may have been more enjoyable. As of now, his windpipes are being pressed into the back of his throat, keeping any precious oxygen from passing through. The crowd in Salt Lake City boo as Cameron Wrigley almost seems to be enjoying himself as he checks to see if Logan will be able to keep going. His legs kick wildly, letting the referee know this match is far from over. Bryan: They are in the center of the ring, so unfortunately getting a rope break will be near impossible. Yale: He'll have to use strength to try and break out of this hold. Bryan: Against Shaman, that is no easy task. Rather than looking for a way to power out of the Sleeper Hold, Old Man Logan allows his body to go limp and drop to the canvas. Even for Shaman, a sudden drop of two-hundred and seventy pounds is enough to cause him to jerk forward. Logan wraps his hand around the back of Shaman's neck as it passes over his shoulder and forcefully brings it down in a Stunner. Yale: That's one way to bring him down! Regrouping, Logan scrambles to his feet and makes it to his corner where Rick Mad is waiting. Rick Mad knows he has a small window of opportunity and hurries into the ring. Shaman has already gotten back to a crotched base, but Rick Mad throws up a Yakuza Kick regardless. Shaman is able to catch it just as he reaches him, whipping him to the canvas with a Dragon Whip. Bryan: The momentum shifts, and it looks like Shaman is tagging in Jorge. Yale: If they thought they had problems with Shaman, Jorge Samuelsson is even bigger. Bryan: And possibly just as nasty. But he won't be trying to show off any technical ability. He wants to slam you, and slam you hard. Rick Mad pulls himself off the canvas and looks over to see the looming seven footer staring back at him. He crawls toward the corner where Logan awaits, but stretching out his hand he finds himself inches away from his partner. He goes to push himself further but his progression is halted when Jorge buries his boot into Rick's back. Like whipping his boot on the canvas, Jorge drags Rick back toward the center of the ring. He lifts his boot off of Rick Mad's back and grabs him, bringing him off the canvas. Again the fans slowly begin to rise up to see what is about to happen. Wrapping him up with both hands, Jorge brings Rick Mad up onto his shoulder and drops his entire weight backwards for a Back Body Drop. Bryan: Oh my! That's four hundred pounds sandwiching him into the canvas. Yale: Even as big as he is, that has to hurt. Jorge rolls off his flattened victim and grabs both of his legs as he comes back to his feet. With both legs hooked, Jorge drags Rick Mad over to the corner where he tags in the awaiting Shaman. Jorge lays into Rick Mad with boots, but Shaman simply hooks one leg and flips Rick Mad over for a Single Leg Boston Crab. Bryan: Rick Mad is in trouble again. Yale: But it looks as though his partner is about to bail him out. Seeing Rick in trouble, Logan bolts into the ring and comes right at Jorge with a Flying Forearm shot that sends the Modern Viking through the ropes and tumbling to the outside. Shaman releases Rick Mad upon seeing his partner take a fall, but Logan places a boot to the back of Shaman's skull before he can fully rise. The Mojave Warrior crashes face first into the canvas but before Logan can add to his offense Wrigley lets him know he has to return to his corner. Yale: Old Man Logan was able to buy his partner some time, but I don't know how much good it did. Bryan: Rick Mad is able to get to his feet, so at least he'll have a better chance than when he was flat on his stomach. As Shaman is getting back to his feet, Rick Mad steps in behind him, throwing the Native American back for a Russian Leg Sweep. Keeping his hands in place, Rick Mad tries to lift his leg to wrap over Shaman's neck for a Triangle Choke, but his muscles refuse to cooperate with him. Having to abandon his submission hold, the quick eyes of Rick notice Jorge back after Logan's attack. The seven footer climbs over the top rope with ease and seems to be making a beeline for him. Bryan: Jorge Samuelsson is fuming over that surprise attack from Logan, and it looks like he's going to take it out on Rick! Yale: But Rick is making his exit stage left, right between the ropes! Too slow to surprise him, Jorge can only wildly swing as Rick Mad makes a dive between the bottom and middle rope to the outside. Fans crush up against the guard rail to try and get closer to the action, hoping it will slip over to the outside. The Modern Viking, however, only screams for Rick to get back into the ring; that is, of course, until Logan comes up behind him and dumps Samuelsson over the top rope. Yale: It looks like Rick has some company outside the ring now. Bryan: Rick Mad and Logan are certainly using veteran skills to keep this match much more competitive than we thought. Yale: You almost get the impression that Mortifera may have underestimated them. Bryan: I highly doubt that, Mortifera has never underestimated an opponent. They approach each match with the same intensity. After sending Samuelsson out to join his partner, Logan turns to find Shaman now back on his feet. A kick to the gut doubles him over, leaving Old Man Logan open for a Desert Spike from the Native American. Yale: Logan is down and out for the count! Bryan: That's all good and well, but he's not the legal man. Rick Mad is, who is still on the outside taking it to Jorge Samuelsson. Indeed, as fans press close to watch, Rick Mad has Jorge up against the guard rail, pounding into him with lefts and rights. Each fist seems to nearly send the seven-footer over the railing and into the crowd. Security tries to keep people back for their own safety, but the fans refuse to listen, and instead reach out to just touch their favorite GCW superstars. Finally, Jorge is able to get enough space to pry his forearm between his self and his attacker. Using his massive forearm to block a right, Jorge shoves Rick Mad back and answers with a right of his own that nearly rocked him out of his shoes. Bryan: What a big impact by the Modern Viking! Rick Mad nearly fell out of his boots on that one. Yale: He has some space to work now, that's not good for Rick Mad. Rushing him, Jorge shoves Rick into the apron and follows it with a sandwiching Clothesline. Hovering over him, Vivica's words could be heard loud and clear over silenced and terrified crowd even without the microphone... "Respect me now." Bryan: What a heinous ending to WorldWide 128--to LeStatt Knight's last GCW Match! I can't believe this. We need some EMTs out here...something. Yale: I'm not so sure Vivica J. Valentine didn't just sign her own death warrant. Do you think LeStatt is going to let this slide? No way. Bryan: We're going to cut out here as LeStatt gets treatment for that burn, but we'll be in L.A. next time, folks, and I don't know if we'll see Knight at WW129, but for Vivica's sake, she might want to hope that she just wasn't convincing enough. Yale: Fat chance. Bryan: Good night, folks, and thanks for watching GCW WorldWide.
