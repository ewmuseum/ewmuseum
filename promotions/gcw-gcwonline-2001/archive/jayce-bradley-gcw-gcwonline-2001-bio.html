Jayce Bradley Biography Real Name Jayce Bradley Hometown San Antonio, Texas Birthday December 5, 1986 Height 5'11' Weight 215 Fed Titles Former Cruiserweight champion, Former Television champion Biography Excerpts from archived interview: Jayce grew up in a nice suburban home. His father had a nine to five that paid him quite generously, his mother was a stay at home mom that sold Mary Kay products to her friends and family to garner a bit of extra income’her way of contributing to the household and getting a few bucks to stash away for a rainy day. Life was good, and the relationship between Jayce and his older brother, Dominic, was one where Jayce looked up to his older brother for guidance. He; like most other kids, wanted to be just like his older brother. Jayce mimicked Dominic in every which way, shape, or form. Jayce idolized his older brother, and Dominic did everything he could to give his younger brother someone to look up to and be proud of. Of course, life is never that perfect. Things changed, and no one took it harder than Jayce. When Dominic started high school, he had the size, strength, and build that would land him a starting spot on the junior varsity squad. He took it, and after the first two weeks of the season; due to an unexplainable injury to a starter on the varsity squad, he was rewarded with a back-up spot on the team’s so far undefeated team. It was nice, but with all the practices and time spent studying film on the following week’s opponents’yeah, Texas takes their football real serious. Well, that did not leave much time left to spend with a little brother that was starting his second year of junior high school. It was something Jayce had no control over, and something he chose not to dwell on. While he still idolized his brother, he was nowhere near his brothers size, and could not play football if his life depended on it. Jayce had to find other ways to occupy his time, and that was about the time when he started watching wrestling a bit more. Jayce and Dominic had spent many a night doing homework in front of the television, watching their favorite National Wrestling Council superstars as they attempted to out-wrestle; and more importantly, out-think their opponent. Jayce’s favorite wrestler was the "Cruiserweight Sensation" Jeremy Thomas, while Dominic preferred to cheer for "The Devil Himself" J.W. Oswald. Dominic always liked to believe that the bigger man should always prevail. He believed that cruiserweights had no business wrestling against the bigger, stronger wrestlers? which was why they received their own division in the rankings. It was a sad way of thinking, and Jayce never agreed with him. Jayce always believed that it was the smarter man, the faster, more agile wrestler that could overcome his bigger, slower adversary. That night he was proven wrong, but over time; as Jayce started watching more and more wrestling, Jayce decided that he was going to prove his brother wrong. He was going to prove to his brother that the smaller guys had every bit as much of an advantage as the big burly wrestlers. He was going to become a wrestler and prove it to him, himself. Of course, his parents would not hear of it. With his grades, Jayce had the opportunity to go on to law school or med school. He was on the honor roll, and something like that’in his parent’s eyes’could cause him to lose focus on a future that would be more promising than being on the road for most of the year for little to nothing as far as wages went. Like most kids though, Jayce paid them no mind. Without the money to fund his exploits, Jayce started showing up in random backyards, doing insane stunts to garner a cult following of other kids in the neighborhood. Dominic himself had a huge hatred for the people that took part in backyard wrestling. Jayce had to tell his brother, himself. Dominic was not pleased at all when he heard the news. The two had a good talk, and Jayce was ready to give it all up...but something happened that night that led Jayce to believe that there was nothing left for him. He no longer had a reason to give up what he was doing, his brother had been murdered. He continued on with his backyard wrestling exploits until he entered a tournament for Primetime Central called the Infinite Guantlet 13, and from there went on to sign with Global Championship Wrestling. Which leads us to today. Wrestling Style High Flying, Brawler that pushes himself to the limits Finisher Move The Final Solution Finisher Description Jayce grabs his opponent in a stunner type position close to the corner, he then lifts his feet onto the top turnbuckle, pushing off and flipping himself over his opponent. With the amount of force that he uses to push off the turnbuckle, Jayce's momentum lifts his opponent off of the ground and flips him over to the point where he lands on his head in a DDT type maneuver. Finisher Setup Move The Dawning Setup Description An Inverted Atomic Drop, that leaves his opponent stunned long enough for him to grab him in the stunner position from behind.
