Timmy Lester Biography Real Name Timothy Barrett Lester Nicknames "B-Movie" "The King of the Cutting Room Floor" Hometown Hollywood, CA by way of Utica, NY Birthday 9/14/1977 Height 6'4 Weight 250 lbs. Fed Titles none yet Fed Awards none yet Biography APPEARANCE: Dirty blond hair with the "bed-head" look, hazel eyes, always has five o’clock shadow. Out of the ring and off the set, he’s rarely seen without a cigarette or sunglasses. Medium build, he has defined musculature, but he wouldn’t be mistaken for a bodybuilder or roidhead. A little hair on the chest and some on his abdominal area. Small tribal tattoo on his left shoulder blade. RING ATTIRE: Plain black spandex tights with "B-Movie" written near the waistline on the back in blue lettering with gold outline. Black elbow and knee pads, gold boots. HISTORY: Timothy Barrett Lester was born in upstate New York in the late 70s. As a boy, he was somewhat rebellious, always getting into fights at school, getting caught with cigarettes as a teenager and the like. He took this rebellion to a new level in 1994, dropping out of high school to pursue a career in professional wrestling. As "T.L. Skyhook," he tore up the indie wrestling scenes in New York state, New England and the mid-Atlantic, dazzling people with his then-innovative aerial offense. He received enough acclaim in his early career to get a tryout in Greensboro for the storied CSWA in 1998, but before he had the chance to get down to North Carolina, he blew out a knee after landing wrong during a missed double axehandle in his last scheduled indie match in Lowell, MA. His tryout was cancelled. Lester fell into a funk for a year, traveling the country doing odd jobs for various people and attending comic book conventions. During one such con in Los Angeles, he saw a casting call for a cult movie. He showed up and auditioned for a supporting role in low-budget film. He nailed it. Six months later, he was a B-movie superstar after his turn as Sgt. Rhodes in Men Are from Mars, Zombie Cheerleaders Are from Venus. Over the next decade or so, Lester made a huge name for himself as a B-movie fixture, starring in such cult classics as Van Helsing’s Brood, Porno Mummy and Carnal Litigation. He garnered the nickname the "King of the Cutting Room Floor," as he made several cameo appearances in big-budget films that tend to appear in the deleted scenes rather than in the main films. He also had a reputation for doing his own stunts, which made him an attractive option for cheapskate producers not interested in hiring more people than necessary. As the years went by, Lester grew weary of his life as an actor, burning out and getting frustrated with the level of pay associated with the amount of work he put into acting. Despite years of acting and doing his own stunts, his knee healed completely and was almost as good as it was nearly ten years prior. He arranged another tryout with a national company, this time, Global Championship Wrestling, and there was no injury to take him out this time. "B-Movie" Timmy Lester is back and ready to make the splash he was poised to make in 1998 now in 2008.
