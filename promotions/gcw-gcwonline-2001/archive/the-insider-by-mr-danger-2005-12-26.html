What this is A guest column by a former Commissioner on his time in the federation and on e-wrestling generally.
                   

By Mr. Danger

Hey there GCW, former Commissioner Mr. Danger here to speak my mind in the world of ewrestling, WWF, and real life. Let's start out with GCW. I've been involved with GCW since damn near the start of it. Probably about April early May. Since that time I would definatelly say that things have gone downhill. No matter how great a efed is, eventually it will run its course. And I have a feeling we are seeing that happen here. As the old saying goes, all good things must come to an end, well, that about says it. The way I see it, our days are numbered, but we've got at least a couple more months to go! As far as my character goes, things are really going to get hot in the weeks to come. Mr. Danger has been in ewrestling for about 5 years now, and I think its time for one last run. Big Daddy Dero has been there with me since day one, and I'm sure he will enjoy this last run with Danger as much as I will. A lot of history there, a lot of issues to settle once and forall. As for everyone else. I see nothing but improvment. The main guy that stands out in my mind is Octavian, who came from a worthless, pathetic jobber Rocky wanna be in the ACWF, to one of the greatest characters and roleplayers I have honestly ever seen. To this day I still get a kick out of a roman from a rich family going into the wrestling business in america. I just love the angle. And this guy rp's now compared to one year ago as the rock just don't compare.. Other than that, to put it quite honestly nobody seems to impress me that much. Everyone else seems to ramble on about meaningless crap. Or maybe its just because I am not interested in their angle.. well, you guys need to learn to MAKE YOUR ANGLES INTERESTING!!! do shit in your rp's that will draw people's attention, and make people want to read them. Octavian does a good job of that, and so does Tristian Mayhem might I add. Most of the other RP's i just skim or dont read at all anymore.. cause if you've seen one, you've seen em all. Just a little tip there guys, dont take it personally. On the other hand I think this is definatelly going to be Danger's last run in the ewrestling world. Things are getting boring, and if it doesn't pick up I'm outta here. The real world is just too tuff right now. [detail withheld] so I've got lots of shit to be doing. [detail withheld] I'm not saying im an expert, but give me a fucking break. Most of these people as I have observed don't know a fucking thing about poker or blackjack. So if you don't have a clue how to play, why throw away your money. it amazes me.. What else was I going to talk about. Oh yeah, real wrestling. WWF. I'm the biggest Kurt Angle mark in the world, and if he doesn't win the title from Austin at Unforgiven im going to be pissed off!!!! he was screwed at summerslam, and I am sick of seeing austin as champion.. at what the hell is with austin and his WHAT?? WHAT??!! Damn that is annoying as hell. I really thought it was funny when Kurt Angle was mocking him the other night, saying it. That was good shit. As for the new guys, the only one im really impressed with is Rob Van Dam. This guy is going to be heavyweight champion in less than 5 years. you can take that to to bank. He's reminds me a lot like Shawn Michaels, and a lot like one of the hardy's combined. He's going to make it big if he doesnt kill himself first.

Well thats all i have to say, and in closing just let me say the chiefs will go all the way this year!!!!!! WHOO HOOO! Rams suck cock.

-- Danger
