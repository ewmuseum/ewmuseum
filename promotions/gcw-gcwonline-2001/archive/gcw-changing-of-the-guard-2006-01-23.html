Changing of the Guard

23 Jan 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is GCW’s founder hands the running of the fed to another handler; posted on the GCW forum (announcement thread 578) and e-mailed to the forum’s members; this is the e-mail copy dated 23 January 2006, from the Janitor’s own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The mail’s automatic unsubscribe footer is not carried; the sender’s address is marked, and one passage is withheld as a private exchange.

Not the 2007 leadership story served under a similar title. Served whole, as written, bar the marked places.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: gcwonline.net [detail withheld] Date: Mon, Jan 23, 2006 at 12:56 PM Subject: New announcement: Changing of the Guard Well, obviously, things need to change here in GCW in order to keep them going. And one of the major changes will come at the very top. As of this moment, I am no longer in charge of GCW. I still own GCW, and I'll take the reigns back some day, I'd imagine. However, I simply don't have the time, energy, or dedication necessary to run GCW the way it deserves to be run. Therefore, I'm turning things over to Pete. I don't talk much about my personal life here in GCW; I don't like to mix reality with fantasy. But the fact is, the last part of 2005 was awesome for GCW... but bad for me personally. [detail withheld] I will remain here in some capacity; I'll still be helping with angles, helping book and post shows when possible, and just pitching in around the house when I can. But, for all intents and purposes, Pete is now in charge of Global Championship Wrestling. Pete assisted me with GCW in a major way during our first big run, and has run highly successful feds before. He's fully equipped to run this fed. He's also going to be decidedly more dickheaded about things... And I mean that in a good way. Gone will be the lax attitude towards deadlines. People are going to start pitching in or get tossed. But I'll let Pete elaborate on his policies himself. The simple fact is, I need to take some time to get myself in order. I'm not going anywhere; you can still come to me with questions and expect to get answers. However, Pete is the man now, and he'll be in charge of booking, posting shows, and getting things done. If he sees fit to adjust schedules and/or deadlines, that's his call. I hope everyone will do their best to stay committed to GCW, and to help Pete run an awesome fed. I know GCW can succeed still, which is why we've come to this agreement. I have no doubt that GCW will thrive and continue to grow with Pete at the reigns. I've finally recruited some help on finishing up WW37, so that will finally be airing. Once it does, Pete will take over and book the next show. Rampage is still on, but will be pushed back. Other than some changes Pete will surely be instituting, I'd imagine you will see little to no major changes in the fed. Everyone waiting to debut will still do so as planned, and all angles are still more or less in tact. However, it would help Pete out if everyone would drop him a line and let him know what their plans are, so he can be on top of things. Bear with us during this transition period, but it should go rather smoothly. Thanks for sticking with GCW, everyone. I promise this will get things back in order, and we'll continue to be the best fed out there. You can view the full announcement by following this link: http://gcwonline.net/forums/index.php?topic=578.0
