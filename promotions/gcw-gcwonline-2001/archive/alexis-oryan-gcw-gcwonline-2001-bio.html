Alexis O'Ryan Biography Real Name Alexis O'Ryan Nicknames The Ice Queen Hometown Chicago, Illinois (Born in Vancouver, BC, after age 9 lived in Tampa Bay, FL) Birthday November 4th, 1992 Height 5'7 Weight 140 Biography The younger half-sister of Colin O’Ryan, Alexis is the daughter of two former wrestling World Champions, named for her mother’s mentor and exposed to wrestling at a young age, as her mother had been, despite her father’s protests. Due to her father’s frequent absences while still on the wrestling circuit her brother helped raise her. After her father retired he slipped into a drunken depression that further broke the already cracked marriage and her parents divorced when she was nine. After the divorce her mother took a job helping at a close friend’s wrestling school in Chicago, and encouraged Alexis to continue the legacy. However Alexis chose a small rebellion. She wanted to be a wrestler, but not as a legacy, so she set out to find her own path, coming once again in contact with the brother she had not seen much of for the past ten years. Strengths extensively trained in various styles Weaknesses nieve, one track mind toward opening with her Wrestling Style Joshi Puroresu, MMA with a bent toward Shaolin Kung Fu Strategy Wears revealing outfits to the ring in order to distract her opponents. She's a lot stronger and tougher than she looks and uses her appearance to her advantage. Finisher Move Broken Heart Finisher Description Package Piledriver WW Entrance During the slow intro of the song the arena is dark, only the tron is lit, fog across the screen forming into little icicles. Black words float across the screen from opposite directions. "Alexis O’Ryan" and "The Ice Queen". As the tempo crashes into a faster beat the screen shatters out into flashes of Alexis wrestling and posing, seductively smirking at the camera, then rolling her eyes sarcastically. At the same time a spotlight comes down, looking as if it was made from the shattered icicles, with a slightly blueish hue. As it comes down Alexis appears on the entrance stage beneath it, one hand on her hip, surveying the crowd. As the lyrics start she begins her walk down to the ring, the arena lights coming up, going side to side approaching men in the first rows of the audience, just to brush them off with a disgusted face, sometimes not even getting that close before looking like she reacts to something with a grimace. She mouths along with the chorus "You make me wanna die!" aiming the phrase over and over at members of the audience. After the third time the phrase is repeated in the first chorus Alexis completely brushes off the audience and runs to the ring, hopping up on the apron. She prances on the outside, swinging around on a ring post. She hangs off of it for a second, looking with mock hope at the audience. Then she wrinkles her nose and emphatically mouths "No!" She climbs up the ropes backwards until she sits on the top one. She lifts one leg slowly and seductively, then almost lazily swings it over the rope. Putting her hands behind her she leans back on the rope and with a contemptuous look she sits back up and swings the rest of the way into the ring, hopping off of the rope. She behaves more like the wrestler she is, warming up a little, until the chorus comes again. She swings her arm around, pointing at the entire audience, singing along "You make me wanna die!" Special Moves The Cold Shoulder (Spear), Head over Heels (Pump Handle Piledriver) Other Moves Breathless (Opponent is on his knees, you take to his back... pull his arms up and wrap them, cross wise, infront of his neck and you lean him back, driving your knees into his back and cutting of his air to his neck.) Kicks from pretty much any angle. Punches. Open hand hits (palm attacks, side of the hands, etc.) Bulldogs Piledrivers Entrance Music You Make Me Wanna Die by The Pretty Reckless
