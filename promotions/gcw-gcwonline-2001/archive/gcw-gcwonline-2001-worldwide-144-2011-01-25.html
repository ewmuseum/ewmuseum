WorldWide 144 Wachovia Spectrum — Philadelphia, Pennsylvania ---- WW144: The Only Good Thing to Come Out of Scotland After a few polite knocks on a skybox suite, Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny opens the door a tad and walks in. The commish, hands in his pockets, surveys the swanky surroundings. There's a large flat panel television on the wall, even though it's not in use. The real view is through the glass slanting forward, making up the entire wall of the room. The view overlooks the capacity crowd and a direct shot of the ring in front of the entrance ramp. GBJ: Nice digs. A lonely figure is seated in the center of the box, motionless under a plume of undulating smoke. The figure raises a hand to motion the Commissioner forward. A heavy exhale sends a cone of gray air upward, exciting the smoggy space. Slowly the figure turns, revealing a half-spent cigar pressed between the smiling lips of GCW's founder. Caldera: After ten years, I figure I've earned the right to watch my creation in style. Cigar? Drink? GBJ: Why the hell not. Johnny examines the label on the bottle of Glenlivet sitting on the end table. He pours himself a glass and tops off his boss' drink. GBJ: Must be nice to see the action from up here. It's hard to keep tabs on everything from an office, but I think we got a pretty solid night lined up tonight. Caldera raises his glass to his Commissioner and nods in appreciation. Caldera: I miss the office. Something about being in the trenches, being constantly hounded by dozens of hungry wrestlers aching for a moment's attention from the boss-- those were the days. But now I've got you for that, and I've got my eye on... well, on the big picture. I suppose that requires a different perspective. Caldera gestures toward the arena, packed from ring to rafters with excited GCW fans. GBJ: I'm just glad to step out of the office for a second. It's starting to smell like Jay's 27s in there. Johnny tries to hide all the tiny signs that look to unnerve his confident demeanor. He calms his fidgeting hands and sips from his drink. GBJ: He's come a long way this year, though, huh? Caldera takes a long pause, puffing contemplatively on his cigar. Caldera: ...He's done exactly what I expected of him. He's taken charge in a way no GCW World Champion before him has managed. Tell me, Johnny. Does he frighten you... as much as I do? The Commissioner slams back the rest of his drink with no recoil. GBJ: Jay? Nah. He's not even close to having me figured. Caldera: You're doing fine, man. Go run your show. The Commissioner breaths a sigh of relief or, more accurately, a cloud of whiskey, his expressions not hiding the gratitude from the compliment. GBJ: Enjoy the show, boss. Garbage Bag Johnny sets down his glass. Caldera watches the Commissioner leave and turns back to his seat. He resumes his position looking out onto the crowd, carefully igniting another cigar. He sits at attention, basking in the warmth of the electric arena atmosphere.
