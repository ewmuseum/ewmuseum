[ The camera fades in and the GCW logo appear on the screen. The theme music for Oblivion is cued. Next we see “Rattlesnake” Jake Stone and Mastermind Sean Morgan sitting behind a set back at GCW studios. ]

Jake Stone: Hello everybody and welcome to another edition of Oblivion. I’m Jake Stone here along with Sean Morgan, and here tonight we are going to recap Oblivion which took place earlier today in Chicago, Illinois.

Sean Morgan: What a show the GCW superstars put on tonight in Chicago! In the opening match it was “Golden Boy” Jimmy Taylor getting the pinfall victory over Kris Krowe.

Jake Stone: Next Sunday at Dangerous Games in Kansas City, Jimmy Taylor and his partner Dan “The Man” Rivero will get a shot at the tag team titles currently held by Assassin One and Mikado.

Sean Morgan: I am definatelly looking forward to that one! Tonight, Dan Rivero was victorious as well as he defeated Big Dawg.

Jake Stone: Mike Hardy wins the first blood match against Chameleon. The best of seven series will continue on Meltdown with match number 6.

Sean Morgan: This rivalry between Hardy and Chameleon, or shall I say Demented, is getting hotter day by day. As we all know, last Wednesday on Meltdown Katanya was kidnapped by Demented. What wil happen next in this ongoing love story?

Jake Stone: Capo defeated Stone tonight to retain the Television title, and in the main event Hiroshima Genryu wrestled Luke Hanson to a no contest when Shane Lane got involved from the get go!

Sean Morgan: Lane and Genryu pounded away on Hanson, the US champion. The match never got officially underway, and Hanson took another beating at the hands of these two.

Jake Stone: Also at Dangerous Games, the feud between these two men will culminate in a match for the GCW US title. Shane Lane goes one on one with Luke Hanson.

Sean Morgan: Mr. Danger sat down tonight backstage at Oblivion with David Yale for a special interview. Let’s take you to that interview now.

[ Camera fades out and into the interview with Mr. Danger from Oblivion earlier. ]

David Yale: Thank you for granting us this interview time Mr. Danger. The fans, as well as I would like to get a few things cleared up before Dangerous Games next Sunday on pay per view.

Mr. Danger: Like always David, I will speak my mind and tell you like it is. Shoot away.

David Yale: I think that over the past few weeks it has been obvious that Rich Rollins and yourself have had some differences. Are you on good terms with our world champion at this time?

Mr. Danger: Well David, I’ve made some mistakes in the past few weeks. Yes, I hit Rich Rollins with a steel chair a couple of times, but what the people don’t realize is that it was an accident. I never hit Rollins intentionally, and everyone around here including yourself David, jumps to conclusions.

David Yale: Another issue I would like to touch on is trust. Mr. Danger, you are the commissioner of GCW and with that title comes a lot of power. The fans as of late don’t seem to trust you anymore. Current polls asked of our fans indicate that you have sold out. They indicate...

Mr. Danger: (interupting) You know, that just really makes me sick. I have come in here since day one with the same exact motive. I am trying my hardest to make this federation the greatest ever, and all anyone does is doubt me. I’ve also heard what some of the wrestlers are saying, how I cannot be trusted, and how I’ve sold out. That is just really hard to swallow. I’ve busted my ass to do so much for this league, and in return I get no support from anyone. I am the same man I was two months ago, and maybe it isn’t me that has changed, maybe everyone else has changed, and I’ve stayed the same.

David Yale: One more question. Next Sunday at Dangerous Games, the pay per view that is named after you, and taking place in your hometown of Kansas City, you will be the special referee in the main event for the World Title. Rich Rollins defends against Establishment member The Jhub, and just what can we expect from you?

Mr. Danger: You can expect me to call the damn match right down the middle. I’m as fair as fair can be David, and that’s what my job will be next Sunday. I am going to go out there and to the best of my ability call it right down the line. I am going to show no favoritism to Rollins, and definatelly to The Jhub. I’m just going to do my job, all personal feelings aside.

David Yale: Well Mr. Danger, all eyes will definatelly be on you next Sunday. Thank you for you time.

Mr. Danger: Anytime.

[ Camera fades out and back to the set of Oblivion at GCW headquarters. ]

Jake Stone: Well, Mr. Danger says that he is still the same, and he hasn’t changed. What do you think Sean?

Sean Morgan: I can understand a guy making a mistake once in awhile. Maybe I can undetstand twice. But Mr. Danger has ‘accidentally’ went out there and pummeled Rollins more than three times. I smell a conspiracy, and next Sunday at Dangerous Games Rollins had better be on the top of his game, cause Lord only knows what will happen.

Jake Stone: And as we leave you tonight, we have some footage to show everyone. Maybe this will give everyone a new perspective on Mr. Danger and just exactly what this man is capable of. Good night!

[ Camera fades out ]

 

 

[ The following footage is from ACWF Deadly Decisions pay per view. November 26, 2000. Finals of the World Title Tournament. ]

Jim Ross: Dero bounces Caldera's head off of our announce table, and NO! GET BACK IN THE RING, PLEASE!

Jerry Lawler: Dero throws Caldera right ontop of our table JR! THIS ISNT GOOD!

Jim Ross: Dero gets up on the table, and NO!!!!! NO!!!!! DONT DO IT!!!!! DONT DO IT DERO!!!!

[ The crowd explodes as Dero gives the sign for the Derobomb. ]

Jim Ross: Get back King! GET BACK!

[ Dero is ready to Derobomb Caldera, and Caldera grabs onto Dero's legs and he can't pick him up. Caldera then takes Dero's knee out, and Dero falls off the table in pain! ]

Jerry Lawler: YES!!!! Caldera twisten that knee, and Dero falls to the floor!

Jim Ross: Dero is down, and Caldera gets off the table. Steve picks him off the floor, and lays him out on the table! Caldera gets up on the RING APRON!!!! LOOK OUT!!!!

[ TABLE SHATTERS AS CALDERA NAILES DERO WITH A FLYING ELBOW ]

Jim Ross: GOOD GOD! GOOD GOD! These men are broken in half!!!!!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: What a move JR! Hebner is out here trying to get these men back in the ring where they should be!

Jim Ross: Caldera is barley moving, and he staggers to his feet. He pitches Dero into the ring, and he makes a cover!!! ONE......... TWO....... AND NOOO!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: Dero kicked out! How the hell did he do that?!

Jim Ross: Caldera can't believe it! He slowly gets up and grabs Dero. He whips him to the ropes, and Dero REVERSES! Caldera ducks a clothesline, and spins Dero around. Dero DUCKS a clothesline, and picks up Caldera and gives him a piledriver!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: What a series of moves that was!!!! Dero just drove Caldera into the mat, and here is a cover be Dero!!!

Jim Ross: ONE, TWO, and NO!!!!! Caldera kicks out. Dero pulls him up, and grabs the wrist of Caldera again. Caldera swings Dero around, and OH NO!!!!!

[ Caldera reversed and threw Dero right into the corner, SMASHING referee Earl Hebner in the corner. ]

Jim Ross: The referee is down, and OUT! All 300 plus pounds of Dero smashed him in the corner, and we have no referee!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: This isn't good JR!! No ref, anything can happen now!

Jim Ross: Dero grabs Caldera and a swift kick to the gut! He puts him down, and HERE IT COMES! YES!!! THE DEROBOMB!!!!! MY GOD WHAT IMPACT!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: These fans are going nuts, but there is no referee!!!!

Jim Ross: One, two, three, four, five, six!!!!! No referee to count the fall!!!!! Hebner is out!

[ Dero goes over to Earl Hebner and is shaking him. Hebner is out cold. Just then the fans ALL turn their heads to the enterance. ]

Jerry Lawler: Everyone is looking at the ramp, and LOOK WHO IS HERE!!!!!

Jim Ross: ITS MR. DANGER!!! AND HE HAS ON A REFEREE SHIRT!!!! THESE FANS ARE GOING NUTS!!!!!

[ Mr. Danger rushes to the ring, and Dero then pushes Hebner out of the ring and covers Caldera. ]

Jim Ross: Danger down for the count!!!! ONE..... TWO..... AND YEAH-NO!!!!!!!! NO!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: A fair count, but Caldera got the shoulder up!

Jim Ross: He had a whole 30 seconds to recover from that move! And now Dero in control. Mr. Danger has took over as referee and he is shouting instructions at Dero. Dero picks up Caldera and is going to Derobomb him AGAIN!!!!

Jerry Lawler: Oh no! This will be it for sure!

Jim Ross: He's got him down, and up!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: Caldera is up, and NO! He breaks away, and falls to the mat on his feet. Spins Dero around and a DDT!!!!!!!

Jim Ross: DDT!!!! Dero is layed out. Caldera covers!!!! Danger down... ONE, TWO, NO!!!!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: Dero is out!!!! WHAT A MATCH JR!

Jim Ross: These men are giving EVERYTHING they have tonight! Both men staggering to their feet, and Caldera is complaining about the count. Caldera is arguing with Danger, and Dero is comming from behind Caldera!!!! LOOK OUT!

[ Dero is winding up for a big clothesline, and Caldera ducks. Dero accidentally nails Mr. Danger and he falls to the mat in pain. ]

Jerry Lawler: Caldera knew he was comming somehow, and ducked!!! Mr. Danger is down, and holding his jaw! Caldera kicks Dero in the midsection, and grabs his head! FACEBUSTER!!!!! Dero is out, and Caldera slides to the outside!! He picks up a steel chair!!!!! WHAT'S HE GONNA DO?

Jim Ross: Dero is slowly getting up, and Caldera gets in the ring with the chair!!! Mr. Danger is still down, but slowly getting up!!!! Dero gets up, and WHAM!!!!!! WHAT A SHOT!! Dero falls to the mat, and is busted wide open from that chair shot by Caldera!!!!!

Jerry Lawler: Mr. Danger is up, and he didn't see it!! Caldera makes the cover!!!! Danger gets down to count!!!

Jim Ross: NO!!!! NOT LIKE THIS!!!!! ONE...... TWO........

Jerry Lawler: NO!!!!!!! DERO KICKED OUT!

Jim Ross: I CANT BELIEVE THIS MAN!!!!! Big Daddy Dero somehow, kicked out right before the three and Caldera is pissed!!!! And uh-oh!!! Mr. Danger sees the chair in the ring still, and there is blood on that chair, as well as on Dero's head!

[ Mr. Danger picks up the chair. He sees that blood is on the chair as well as on Dero's head. He is shouting something at Caldera who is just looking innocent. ]

Jim Ross: Danger is putting two and two together, and he knows Caldera used that chair! He and Caldera are arguing about it!!!

Jerry Lawler: Look out! Dero is very slowly getting up, blood dripping from his face! He is a bloody mess!!!!

[ As Dero gets up, Danger and Caldera are arguing. Mr. Danger then winds up the chair, and goes to hit Caldera.. Just at the last second he stops in mid air, turns around.. and smashes the chair over Big Daddy Dero's head. The crowd gives a "uuuu" then is silent ]

Jim Ross: (pauses) WHAT THE HELL IS THIS????

Jerry Lawler: Did I just see what I think I saw?????

Jim Ross: MR. DANGER!!!!!! JUST SMASHED THE CHAIR OVER DERO'S HEAD!!!!!! Mr. Danger throws the chair to the outside, and points down at Dero and is laughing!

Jerry Lawler: What is going on JR? I can't believe this!!!!

Jim Ross: Caldera picks up Dero, and hooks both arms! He lifts him up in the air, CALDERA DROP!!!! Danger for the count. One, two, three!!!!!

[ The Sickness by Disturbed begins to play. The fans are totally disgusted as they boo and start throwing trash at the ring. Mr. Danger runs to the outside and grabs the belt. He comes back into the ring, and he and Caldea embrace with a big hug. Mr. Danger then gives Caldera the belt, and raises his hand. They hug again. ]

The Fink: Here is your winner, and the NEEEEWWWWWW ACWF World Heavyweight Champion, STEVEN CALDERA!!!!

Jim Ross: I can't believe it!!! THEY SCREWED US ALL!!!! Mr. Danger and Steve Caldera were together all along, and this makes me SICK!!!! Big Daddy Dero is bloody and beaten at the hands of Steve Caldera and Mr. Danger!! Caldera is the new champion of the world, but at what cost?

Jerry Lawler: Danger and Caldera are both walking up the ramp laughing!!! What fools everyone here is!!! They never saw this comming! What a fool you are JR!!

Jim Ross: Ah shut UP! Mr. Danger, shocks the world here tonight in Kansas City!! What a DEADLY DECISION he has just made!!! Right here in his hometown!!!!! They played each and every one of us!! Fans, our time is up. For Jerry Lawler, I'm Jim Ross saying goodnight from KC...... This is just sickning!!!!!!

[ We see a shot of Caldera holding up the belt at the enterance to the back. We then see a shot of referees helping Dero up who is laying in a pool of his own blood in the ring. Camera fades out and the show ends. ]
