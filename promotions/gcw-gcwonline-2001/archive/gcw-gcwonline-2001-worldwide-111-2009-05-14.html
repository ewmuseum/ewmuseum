WorldWide 111 Wachovia Center — Philadelphia, Pennsylvania ---- I'm coming to fuck you up! For the first time in as long as you can remember, the first thing you see when you flick over to WorldWide isn't the Global Championship Wrestling logo flashing against a black backdrop. There's no Rage Against the Machine, no highlights packages, no dramatic shots of the arena and no fireworks. No flashy introductions, now showmanship, no pomp. Just one man standing in the middle of the ring, microphone in one hand, steel chair in the other. "AN-DEE MUR-RAY!" CLAP! CLAP! CLAP-CLAP-CLAP! "AN-DEE MUR-RAY!" CLAP! CLAP! CLAP-CLAP-CLAP! The roar of the crowd brings the slightest of smiles to his troubled facial features, but The Scottish King of Cool isn't here to mess around. Rising the microphone to his lips, Andy nods appreciatively. Bryan: Well uhhh, welcome to WorldWide ladies and gents. After a rather abrupt start it looks like The King's got a few things to get off his chest... Yale: The show hasn't even started yet and already Andy Murray's out here to flap his gums! How inconsiderate of him. Murray: Wow. Thank you, Philadelphia. His Scottish tones sound hoarse, maybe even a little bit deeper than usual, but that's hardly surprising given his physical condition. With bandages covering much of his right forearm and a large plaster stuck across his forehead, Murray bears the scars of a two month war with the GCW World Champion. And more specifically, having his head thrown through a car windshield last week. Murray: In case there's anyone out there that can't hear all these people, my name's Andy Murray, and I am your number one contender. But right now, there's only one goal in my mind... He pauses. Murray: To destroy LeStatt Knight. The crowd immediately shower jeers down on the ring at the mere mention of the Champion. Yale: What?! When is this guy going to learn, JB? You'd think that having his head plowed through a car window would have knocked a little bit of sense into him. He was beaten and bloodied by LeStatt's hand last week! He stuck his nose in LK's business last year and it ended in tears, and ever since Shockwave things have been going steadily downhill for The Scottish King of Cool. You brought me to breaking point at WW100, you brought my surge through the competition at Dangerous Games to a shuddering halt with one violent thud. You made me knock the living crap out of a guy I once called an ally, and just last week you did your best to make sure I wouldn't even make it to Rampage. Guess you could say you've been forcing me to do things your way since the day we first crossed paths, 'Statty... I'm finishing it TONIGHT! No more mindgames, no more trickery. Just you and me, face-to-face, man-to-man.
