WorldWide 75 The Q, Cleveland, Ohio ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Cincinnati fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: With another capacity crowd on hand, Global Championship Wrestling invades Cincinnati for the final stop on the way to the most anticipated event of the year! Dangerous Games is just around the corner, and tonight every superstar in GCW is revving up for the opportunity of a lifetime! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan at ringside once again with David Yale, and Dave, we're just days away. Yale: And I've never been so excited, JB. Dangerous Games is always the biggest, most exciting, exhilarating event of the year, and this year stands to be bigger than ever! It's a wide-open race for that coveted main event spot and title shot at NC-17, and to top it off, we're hearing rumors that a large number of spots were left open for surprise entries! Bryan: Absolutely, the list of confirmed entrants was released to us this week, and though we cannot reveal it on air, we can confirm that only sixteen names appear on the current GCW roster! That means almost half the field is going to be unannounced, and that means we should be prepared to expect the unexpected! Yale: But that said, the hugeness of this match cannot be overstated, JB. Literally everyone in GCW, old and new, has the chance to skyrocket to the highest peak in professional wrestling in one night! Someone's dreams are going to come true next week at Dangerous Games, and it's literally anyone's ballgame. Bryan: Speaking of dreams coming true, dreams will be made or shattered in that evening's main event, as Lia Ambrosi defends against the red-hot Jordan White! White is oozing with confidence right now, he's coming off a massive win over Rich Rollins, and Ambrosi's got to be getting tired. She's got yet another main event title defense tonight to survive before she even gets to Dangerous Games. But if she leaves here tonight with the title, she's going to have a tough challenge ahead. Yale: And White's up to the task, JB. He's proven he can be a superstar, he can win on the biggest of stages, and they don't come a whole lot bigger than the one he'll be on next week. But Lia Ambrosi, for her part, has a lot to prove herself as she tries to place herself in the same class as The Establishment's LeStatt Knight as all-time great champions. She has to win against White to continue on a path that, I hate to say, is leading her towards the GCW Hall of Fame. Bryan: But we've got an enormous show for you tonight, folks, as Brad Jackson takes on Bryan Mayhem, Vivica J. Valentine takes on Shaman, and so much more! ---- Double Trouble The camera finds Kevin Jacobs scurrying about backstage. He rushes through a busy hallway, dodging and weaving through hurried GCW crew members to fight his way to the back door. He pushes out the door and the camera follows him into the parking garage. Within moments a figure enters the scene, sporting a sparkling gold belt over her shoulder. The crowd roars as Lia Ambrosi appears on the MegaTron, heading into the arena. Jacobs cuts her off with a microphone thrust towards her face. Jacobs: Lia! Lia, with only one more week until Dangerous Games, you've got one more title defense to get through. You've already beaten President Caldera and Jimmy Thunder, who do you have lined up for tonight? Ambrosi: Oh, I'm sure that'll work itself out. The fact of the matter is, Kevin, nobody has really shown me that they want it. Jacobs: Well, rest assured everyone in GCW wants what you have, but in light of the greatness of this current title reign, they might feel a bit intimidated. One man who does not appear to be intimidated is Jordan White. What are your plans for him next week at Dangerous Games? Ambrosi: White isn't afraid, but he should be. I'm going to prove to White and The Establishment that LeStatt Knight isn't the only one with a real claim to being the best of all time. I'm going to put White down and I'm going to be in the main event at NC-17 again this year. And another thing-- Lia stalls as her brow furrows. From across the way comes President Caldera, sporting a grim smile and a dapper suit. Jacobs moves aside to allow Caldera into the shot. Caldera: Well, Mrs. Ambrosi, thank you for joining us. Strolling in a little late, aren't we? Ambrosi goes to answer, but Caldera raises a hand to cut her off. Caldera: Save it. You're in the main event again tonight, and I won't go back on my word to allow you to pick your opponents. But seeing as how you're so damn confident about your abilities, and how you think so little of the next World Champion... maybe one opponent isn't enough for you tonight. The crowd boos, sensing another Caldera screwjob coming. Ambrosi shifts her weight and adjusts the title belt over his shoulder, still unconcerned. Caldera: So you can still pick your opponents... both of them. Because tonight, just to get you ready for the challenge that awaits you at Dangerous Games, you'll be defending that title in a Handicap Match. Consider it a favor... you're going to need all the practice you can get. Caldera smirks and struts away, leaving a stunned Jacobs next to a blank-faced champion. Bryan: What?! Oh, that's just not fair! Yale: Hey, fighting Jordan White is like fighting two men! Caldera's just trying to get her ready! Bryan: Wear her down is more like it... Jacobs: Champ, I- Lia doesn't respond. She simply turns to Jacobs and smirks, as if a lightbulb has gone on over her head. She walks away, leaving Jacobs scratching his head as the shot returns to ringside and the commentating team. Bryan: Well, Dave, tonight's main event just got a little bigger, as Lia is going to have to defend her title against not one, but two opponents! Yale: Hey, she didn't sound like she was sweating Jordan White that much, JB. If she can handle the number one contender that easily, two lesser opponents should be a breeze! ---- Ray Holland vs. Shawn Porter The Arena darkens into a dim white and green lighting as “You Ain't Know” hits the PA system the fans erupt in boos. Shawn Porter comes out in his usual wrestling attire, he's wearing a Black and Green Underarmour full body tights with the name "Porter" written across the back of it. The lights begin to flash at the top of the ramp while Porter is under it. Porter puts his hands out absorbing the momentum. Porter begin doing the Motorcycle (Dance), and then raises his arm as he walks to the middle of the ramp, he drops them with force as a big bang of pyro goes off behind him. Shawn walks down the entrance ramp until he reaches the ring. Shawn looks around. Shawn nods and smiles as he climbs up onto the ring apron. Shawn climbs through the ropes, and looks over at his opponent for the night. Ray Holland isn't what you'd expect in today's modern era. His body isn't oiled and well toned, instead he stands there in a black singlet with both straps dangling around his very noticeable gut. There was no pyro, no fancy music, just a man walking down the ramp and into the ring. The crowd explodes as Porter and Holland close in on each other, this match could set the pace for the rest of the evening. Bryan: Listen to this crowd! This crowd is letting Shawn know that they can't stand him, there’s no doubt about it! He has to drown it out though, and focus on winning. He knows that a win here can get him back into the thick of things, while a loss to a relative unknown could set him further back from the Television title scene than he'd like to be. Yale: Like dark match further. Oh wait! Porter begins jawing at Holland, spewing bitter sounding insults at his smiling opponent. Holland abruptly breaks the tension by connecting with a big right hand to Porter's head, catching Shawn off guard. Porter backs up toward the corner, as Holland follows him in and gets caught with a huge belly to belly suplex! The crowd explodes in shock as Holland painfully rolls to his feet, staring in amazement across the ring at his adversary. Holland begins to move in once more, but Porter makes him think twice when he drops down into a ready position and motions for him to take another step. Holland stops and Porter smiles as he relaxes a bit, before the two start to circle each other in the center of the ring. Bryan: Holland doesn't look like he's ready for a match like this. Yale: He doesn't look ready to be in that ring period. Bryan: You do have a point there. Holland spits on the canvas before rushing forward so the two could lock up. They execute a collar-and-elbow tie up, and Porter quickly launches Holland across the ring with an arm drag! The crowd explodes with boos as Shawn raises his arms, while the newcomer pounds the mat in frustration, the smile never leaves Porter's face. Bryan: Porter is coming out aggressively here, folks, and it might be starting to affect him a bit! Holland slowly rises to his feet and nods to Porter, his face growing a little firmer now. Porter just shifts his weight from one foot to the other, taunting Holland with a knowing nod. Holland smirks and moves in again, meeting Porter with another tie-up. Porter immediately goes for a snapmare, but Holland blocks it and brings a sharp elbow down across Porter’s face! The crowd groans as Porter hits the mat, and Holland immediately follows with a series of vicious fists to the forehead. Holland shoves Porter violently to the mat, taking the time to get in an extra kick to the man's back as the crowd showers him with boos. Yale: Holland may not be much of a wrestler, but the man isn't someone you want to go toe to toe with. Bryan: The man's sloppy, there's no way he can come out of this with a win. Porter has too much experience to let that happen. Yale: How much would you like to bet, Caldera made sure Holland learned at least the basics? Bryan: Caldera invested in this man the moment Holland signed on the dotted line, there's no way he wouldn't make sure he would get a return on that investment. Caldera's not a foolish businessman. Porter painfully pulls himself back up, and Holland is waiting with a brutal kick to the gut. Holland slings the hobbled Porter into the turnbuckle, sending him shoulder first into the steel post. Porter bounces out, but Holland immediately scoops him up from behind and sets him on the top turnbuckle. Holland begins to climb, but Porter counters with a sharp back elbow, edging Holland back to the mat. Porter slides off the turnbuckle and lifts the newcomer onto his shoulders, but Holland manages to keep Porter off balance enough that he staggers back into the middle of the ring. Shawn is in no position to block the stiff right hand that Holland drives into the side of his head, so he just drops Ray off to the side as he tries to shake the cobwebs from his head. Bryan: Well, that was a huge move for Holland there, because who knows what Porter was trying to set him up for. Yale: We'll never know. Porter manages to stop the ringing in his ears, but Ray snaps him down onto the canvas with a headlock takedown. Bryan: Holland has Porter grounded now, and he’s starting to wear Porter out a bit, sap some of his energy. Yale: Absolutely. Porter really needs to set the pace of this match, because if he keeps fighting Holland's style of brawling, he can end up on the losing end. Bryan: This is usually Porter's style of match, but I'm not sure he was ready for what Holland has been dishing out. Porter manages to push himself back to his feet, but once more Holland snaps him back down. Porter this time manages to flip his legs up and wrap them around Holland's waist, which Holland immediately counters by reaching out and grabbing the bottom rope. Porter springs back up, only to find a huge clothesline coming his way! Porter quickly ducks down to evade the move, then shifts his weight onto his left foot and extends the other into a Yakuza Kick that catches Holland square on the jaw as he recoils off the ropes. Bryan: Yakuza Kick from Porter! Yale: Could we be seeing a turning of the tide here? Porter steps back and waits for Holland to rise, and Holland turns to Briggs to complain. Porter charges in and hits an unsuspecting Holland with a hard clothesline, toppling Holland over the top rope to the floor! The crowd pops huge for the move, as Porter grasps his jaw in pain, and Holland slowly pulls himself up off the floor. Bryan: Wow! Big move from Porter there, and Holland is hurting! Yale: What? I can't even hear you this place is so loud! Holland, his face turning red with anger, pulls himself upright, only to find Shawn sliding under the rope with a baseball slide, toppling Holland back to the floor! The arena begins to shake again as the fans cheer wildly, helping Porter find the strength to pop back to his feet! Porter pulls Holland up, promptly sending him headfirst into the steel steps! Bryan: Good God! Ray Holland just had his cranium crushed at the hands of Shawn Porter! I think he dented the damn steps, Yale! Yale: This is amazing! Porter's got the momentum back, and with this kind of a reaction going on, he needs to start picking up the victories! Holland, eyes glazed over, manages to push himself onto a wobbly base. Porter simply nails him with a sharp standing dropkick, sending Holland head over heels over the steel steps, where he lands in a heap on the back of his skull. Porter gives one final kick to the top section of the steel steps, dislodging the top porting right on top of Holland. Porter takes a step back, then takes a running leap off the lower portion of the steps, dropping all of his weight onto the steps, crushing Holland below! Bryan: Holland is really in trouble here! Porter is putting his body on the line in an effort to win this match! Yale: He has no choice, how would it look for him to lose to some guy that just walked into the ring straight off the streets. Holland painfully tosses the steps off himself and scrambles away from Porter, who stands leaning against the turnbuckle, rubbing his back. Holland reaches the announce table and drags himself halfway up, prompting Porter to charge. Holland narrowly ducks a lariat and scurries into the ring like a roach, as Porter stops himself before he crashed into the announce table. Holland staggers back into the center of the ring, trying to ignore the pain as he watches Porter slide under the bottom rope. Holland takes off trying to catch Porter off guard, but Shawn is ready and catches him with a dropkick, nailing Holland square in the gut. Both men hit the mat with a thud. Yale: Whoa! Porter had that one scouted a mile away! Bryan: Scouted, Holland has never been in the ring, what kind of tape could he have studied for this match? Yale: I mean in general. Porter has been around long enough that he should know how to handle something like that! Porter quickly pulls Holland back up, whipping him hard into the turnbuckle. Porter charges in after him, but Holland throws a vicious mule kick into Porter's face, cutting him down with a sickening crack of leather against skin. The crowd plunges into a shocked silence as Holland rears back and catches Porter unaware with a stiff shot right across Shawn's jaw. Yale: WHOA! 1... Bryan: Wow! Holland stunned Porter and now he has a leg hooked! 2... Yale: I can't believe this! Shawn Porter is out! 3... sfx: Ding! Ding! Ding! ---- Business, of the unfinished kind! Green eyes swept the hallway, noting that everything was as it should be- everyone rather busy at the moment, and no one in particular noting her presence. For a woman as busy as Lia Ambrosi was on this evening, that was a good thing. She shifted the heavy weight of her bag (holding within it GCW's World Title), and then moved it wholesale to her other shoulder. It was a gesture of nervous energy- tonight, she'd get to go out and defend her title again, and despite the fact that she'd have to wait quite awhile to do so, her body was already jumping at the chance physically. It was because of this feeling that she needed a little time to herself before she actually emerged for her interview later in the evening. She made a beeline for the Choosers of the Slain locker room, her eyes not deviating from the path for any reason whatsoever, no matter how particularly loud or ridiculous the exclamations (and curses) of the crew became. With a sigh of relief on her lips, she turned the knob and slipped into the locker room, feeling along the wall in the dark for the light switch. Once her fingers found it, she flicked only the first of two upwards- the place didn't need to be lit blindingly, after all. "Good evening Lia." Her body tightens up but she fights hard not to let the fact that she was indeed startled show much.  Slowly she turns her head to where the eerily familiar voice originated.  Surprised?  Perhaps but at the same time maybe she should have guessed.   Knight:  How is the GCW World Champion doing? Knight stands at the far end of the room, his back pressed against the wall and his arms crossed over his massive chest.  For once he is not in his usual wrestling attire but in a very nice, clean, and expensive solid black suit.  A smirk forms on his face as he awaits Lia's response. Lia's shoulders remain tense, and her eyes never leave Knight as she sets her bag down on the chair that is sitting beside the door. Her fingers meticulously dust minuscule specks off of her street clothes, and then come to rest at her side. Despite the fact that she's eyeballing him like a hawk, she shows no fear, and not even much surprise. Lia: I'm passably well this evening, though I'll confess I hadn't planned for visitors. You'll forgive me, I hope, for not having set out the tea. Knight chuckles as he moves from his place on the wall and uncrossing his arms instead placing them into his pants pockets. Knight:  Well it's fine, I don't even like tea ... its no Tequila let me say that.  ( Knight takes a step forward)  I'd apologize for interrupting your alone time but the fact is I'm not sorry.  Of course I'm sure you are wondering exactly what I'm doing here, not like we have become good friends or anything yes? Lia looks at him in a way that can best be described as 'unamused.' Lia: Did you not get my Christmas card? She folds her arms over her chest. Lia: Of course I'm wondering what you're doing here. And of course you're not sorry, because you seem to have somehow gotten the idea into your head that reading someone else's name on a locker room door is no deterrent to you walking in whenever the hell you feel like it. Knight:  Of course it isn't a deterrent.  ( he doesn't miss a beat)  You see Lia I've been around here a long time as you already know.  GCW was built around me so in all actuality you would not have such a splendid locker room if it weren't for me.   Knight slowly begins to take step after step closer to the World Champion but the steps are so SLOW that it almost seems like hes gliding.   Knight:  However that is neither here nor there.  I have no reason to go through my list of accomplishments with you.  I am here for another reason, a far more important reason.  That of course being Dangerous Games.   Lia's expression remains unamused, and neither does her stance change at all in response to his attempt to get into her personal space. Lia: Well, I'm glad I don't have to offer a lengthy diatribe explaining how grateful I am that you're so wonderful. I'm not sure I could find the words. Her phrasing is spat out with quiet venom, and acidic sarcasm. Lia: So I presume you're here to tell me that you're the man with the plan at Dangerous Games? Knight:  On the contrary ... I'm here to speak about Jordan White.   Knight stops just a step or two away from Lia, his icy blue eyes never falling from her own stare.  These two GCW superstars have never been known as the kind to back down to anyone and its not about to start now.  The levels of confidence and superiority in these two are at all time highs. Knight:  I have stood back for the most part and watched as this continuing struggle has raged.  Lia vs. White or maybe it's Lia vs Caldera?  Your battle to settle this once and for all is quickly coming up and I just have to wonder are you REALLY prepared for what Jordan White is going to bring to you? Tell your manufactured contender that I'm not only well aware but well prepared for whatever it is he's going to bring me. She scoffs at him, shaking her head. Lia: Really, I'm surprised they didn't put you in a mailman's uniform. Since when did you become anybody's glorified messenger boy? LeStatt lowers his head slightly and takes in a deep breath.  Obviously showing a mock sense of disappointment. Knight:  Well maybe I've become soft ya know?  Maybe my time is up in GCW?  Hmmm ... I wonder if people will look at me the same way they now look at guys like Twizzy?  The ones who stay around far past the times that they are worth a damn anymore.  OH what have I become?   Knight lifts his face again but is not smirking now.  Although the comments were meant as a joke he looks as serious as he has since Lia walked through that door.  Knight:  Ya know it's time to cut the bullshit.  I didn't come here for Jordan and I didn't quite come here for Dangerous Games and I damn sure didn't come here for the tea.  If we want to be real here?  I've come for NC-17.   Knight's eyes seem to grow brighter at the very mention of the show and is that a half smile that momentarily cracks on the face of the World Champ?   Knight:  We both know what happened last year Lia when you took that World Title from me.  Problem is that there was another pest involved, someone who doesn't even deserve to be mentioned in the same breath as either one of us. I may be one arrogant asshole but I know talent when I see it ... I can show respect.  Lia, Champ ... you and I have unfinished business. Lia's arms fold around her abdomen, and a certain snap has come into her eyes. Either the Champ is amused, or envigorated by the thought of facing Knight again- and it's almost impossible to tell which it is. She looks up at Knight, holding his gaze for a moment as a slow smile envelops her features. Lia: I see. I suppose the fact that you were the one pinned doesn't make any difference? The look on Knight's face, even if it was momentarily, was the look of a man who was just punched in the gut.  He grits his teeth ever so slightly before saying anything else.  Knight:  You won ... that's all I will say about that.  Problem remains though that there was someone else involved and that means more than you may want to admit.  When the dust settles at Dangerous Games and I've cut through GCW ... I hope it's... Knight cuts himself off, obviously rethinking what he was going to say Knight:  (a smile forms on his face) The world will finally get to see whom is the greatest GCW has to offer. Lia's stance loosens slightly- but then, she's never been particularly on edge around Knight. It's part of the respect they share- neither has ever harmed the other outside the context of a wrestling ring. Lia: I'll admit that it was different than a one on one match, Lestatt. It was the first time she had referred to him by his first name Lia: But that's all the leeway I'll give. She arches a brow, a surefire sign that she's about to fire off something slightly cryptic. Lia: I'm sure the rightful victor will come out on top. The two stand in silence staring at each other for a moment.  There is an understanding between the two superstars, one that did not need any words.  Knight slightly gives a nod of his head.  Knight:  Enjoy the rest of your evening Champ.  I'll be watching.  Knight now goes to brush by her but stops right at her side.  He leans down now and whispers... Knight:  Good Luck.
