WorldWide 121 Teater Bowl — Northern Ohio ---- Daria Ngozi vs. James Mercier vs. Bishop Steele vs. Marshall Hamilton Foxe Daria Ngozi, looking for a successful return to Global Championship Wrestling following a lengthy spell on the sidelines, was first to enter the ring, enjoying a warm reception from the small portion of the crowd that remember her previous GCW escapes. First to join her was James Mercier, a man who's been largely disappointing since his GCW debut, followed by the debuting Marshall Hamilton Foxe and Bishop Steele, who'd been relegated to the dark match having performed on the main card consistantly since his debut. The match started off scrappily with Steele and Nogzi showing initial signs of dominance. Ngozi soon used her superior agility to outwit Steele, though, but found herself on the receiving end of a Marshall Hamilton Foxe cheapshot. The rookie never really got a chance to establish his authority on the match as a resurgent Bishop Steele came after him as James Mercier and Ngozi brawled outside the ring. Each superstar managed to get a few moves in, but there was only ever going to be one winner. With Steele and Mercier having wiped each other out, Ngozi finished Foxe off with her patented Genocide finisher for the one, two, three. A solid, if rusty, performance from Ngozi, who will now surely be looking to re-establish herself as a contender in the Television division.
