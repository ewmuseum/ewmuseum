Alexander Edwards Biography Real Name Alexander Edwards Nicknames "The Seventh Deadly Son" "The Demon Seed" Hometown Chicago, Illinois via Hertfordshire, England Birthday 26th June 1992 Height 6'1 Weight 224lbs Biography The Edwards family has been a dominant force in Catch Wrestling and Professional Wrestling for over a century, producing six generations of World Champions. Alexander Edwards is the seventh generation of the Edwards Legacy. Born in Hertfordshire, England on June 26th 1992, six minutes before his twin sister Amanda, Alexander is the eldest son of two former World Champions and FWA Hall of Famers, the sixth (and in the opinion of many, greatest) generation; "The Legacy" Ewan Edwards and "The Queen of Wrestling" Jillian De Silva-Edwards. From the day he was born, the was no question about Alexander's future, he would be a wrestler, and come hell or high water he would be the greatest who ever lived. His bedtime stories were tales of the glorious victories of his Father, Mother and the countless ancestors who came before. When most toddlers were learning the basic pleasantries of please and thank you, Alex learned how to hold himself with the dignity, stoicism and integrity of a true champion. In time-honoured tradition Alexander's true training began the moment he could walk. Daily tutelage from his parents on the finer points of chain wrestling and the submission arts. His skills developed at a rate that surprised even his parents, it was apparent that Alexander had the ability to surpass every generation that came before. So Ewan and Jillian kept pushing his training further and further. Wrestling was quite literally Alexander's entire life. But as Alexander grew older he began to wonder if there was more to life than being who his family wanted him to be. At the age of 13, under the pretence of wanting to "broaden his skill-set," Alexander left his parents behind and relocated to Chicago to live and train with the black sheep of the Edwards family, Ewan's long-time tag-team partner and brother-in-law "The King of Puroresu" Shawn Cortez. Cortez had little to teach Alexander in terms of wrestling, but rather, taught him that he doesn't need to be Ewan to be a wrestler, taught him how to break from the Edwards mould and be the person he wanted to be. Alexander remained in Chicago for five years. At the age of 18 it was time for him to return to England and claim his destiny and storm the world of professional wrestling as the seventh incarnation of "The Legacy." But when Alexander returned it was to break his family's hearts, and become the first member of the Edwards clan to decline the traditional moniker "The Legacy," shrug off the burden he had been carrying from the day of his birth, and walk his own path. A path that has led him to Global Championship Wrestling. Strengths Alexander is a master of technical chain grappling and submission wrestling. Despite his young age he posesses the instincts of a seasoned veteran due to being trained from childhood by his parents. Weaknesses When angered he can become uncontrollably vicious, missing opportunities to win in order to inflict further suffering on his opponent. He has virtually no aerial offense, and is inexperienced in Hardcore-style matches. Bryan: Yale: Alexander takes a moment to survey the crowd from the stage, before beginning to saunter down the ramp. He walks with both intense focus and an arrogant swagger, acknowledging his fans with a dignified nod instead of a high-five. Alexander inevitably encounters a small but vocal group of fans intent on berating him, wearing T-Shirts bearing the slogan “The Legacy Continues” in support of Alexander's father Ewan. Alexander flashes them a roll of the eyes and a half-hearted laugh before calmly ascending the steps. He pauses on the ring apron, leaning casually on the ring ropes, once more surveying the crowd. Bryan: Yale: He steps into the ring and climbs to the second turnbuckle, but does not taunt or pander to the crowd, he simply stares upwards, almost with apprehension, or even disgust. After a moment, Alexander slowly lowers his gaze and looks backward over his shoulder, a sinister and strangely familiar smirk crossing his face, a smirk that will most likely remain throughout the match. He leaps from the ropes and begins pacing the ring and rubbing his wrists with great enthusiasm.
