Dangerous Games 2006 Thomas and Mack Center, Las Vegas ---- Final Thoughts This segment was pre-recorded earlier this morning at the Thomas and Mack Center in Las Vegas: The setup is simple, two chairs and a coffee table in a room backstage. Yale: I’m backstage here, about an hour or so before Dangerous Games is set to go on the air, with Seymour Almasy. Seymour, thank you for this interview. Almasy: I’m not doing it for you, but I appreciate the sentiment. Fire away. Yale: Well then, I guess the most basic question is… why Brandon Youngblood? Why have you targeted this man? Almasy: A fair question, I suppose. Especially considering Brandon himself doesn’t get it. In a way, I’m paying him the highest of compliments. Yale: Come again? Almasy: I didn’t expect you to understand, so I’ll explain it for you and everyone. Brandon Youngblood calls himself the Only Diamond in Our Sport. He’s a former Jolt Triple Crown Champion. He is one of the finest wrestlers alive. I’ll admit this freely, because it’s Yevon’s truth. When push comes to shove, could I beat him? Absolutely. But why should we bother? Why should Dies Irae, the most dominant faction in this company’s history, waste it’s time trying to fight Brandon Youngblood where he’s his strongest? Brandon Youngblood was always a potential enemy. Him in GCW is the single greatest roadblock to our total dominance. Once Youngblood goes, the floodgates open, and you may as well call this place DICW. Yale: But why your tactics? Why the bottle of rum to a man known to be a former alcoholic. He gets enraged. He lets his emotions rage out of control. And when he does? I’m there to capitalize. To put it simply, Brandon Youngblood makes things personal. I keep them strictly business. Yale: With all due respect, how can you justify everything you’ve done to the man as business? And even if you can, how can you possibly not be mad at him for what he did to you two weeks ago?
