Mike Savige Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Machine Hometown New York, NY Birthday 4/14/76 Height 6' 6' Weight 255 Fed Titles GCW Television Champion Fed Awards None Biography Mike's a cocky bastard, his personality a cross between the Rock and Curt Hennig, who while not thinking he's better than everyone else, he knows exactly how good he is and never misses an opportunity to tell anyone within earshot. However, he can back it up, using a mix of technical wrestling moves that can confuse his opponent, submission holds that can tie an opponent up, and has the know how to find his opponents weaknesses and use them to his advantage. While not the biggest or strongest guy, he knows how to use the skills he has to take down anyone who stands in his way. However, he's not above bending (or breaking) the rules to get the advantage. After seeing the infamous Hulk Hogan/Iron Sheik title match in Madison Square Garden in 1984, Mike knew what he wanted to do with his life. His parents just thought it was a pipe dream and he'd grow out of it, but as he got older, they realized he was serious and made a deal with him. He had to finish college before he entertained the thought of wrestling, that way if it didn't work out he had something to fall back on. As soon as he graduated college he enrolled in Killer Kowalski's wrestling school and learned the ropes, literally. He spent a short time in the indies (winning one minor title) where he was discovered by a talent scout for the IWO where he spent a few years, earning the IWO World Heavyweight title a record six times. He earned the nickname of the Machine due to his technical prowess that earned him comparisons to wrestlers like Bret Hart and Chris Benoit. When the IWO went belly up, he moved on to the SWO where he met his manager (and future wife) Cindy 'Sinnamon' Matthews, and a man who would become his long time friend Jeff Wylde. He snagged the SWO World Heavyweight title early on, but the SWO also ended up going out of business. He tried out for EPW but quit before ever wrestling a match because they saw him as nothing more than 'enhancment talent' (read: a jobber). After quitting EPW he and Cindy got married, their relationship havng gone from professional to personal. He then traveled to Mexico where he wrestled under a mask as La M?quina (spanish for The Machine) for the AAA promotion. After a knee injury knocked him out of action for six months he returned to the States where GCW caught his eye and now the Machine is back in the ring and ready to kick some ass! Mike is light skinned (about the same complexion as the Rock), he keeps his hair cut short and his face clean shaven. Physcially he's not huge, but he is well defined and despite the smirk he almost always wears, well let's just say even if he wasn't married he wouldn't have trouble getting a date. He wears a pair of mirrored shades and a leather jacket to the ring, and his in ring gear consists of white athletic gloves with black trim, black tights with a silver and white barbed wire pattern running up and down both legs, and black boots. He's got tribal tattoos on both arms that run from wrist to shoulder, and the one on his right arm extends over onto his chest. He's got another tribal design across his stomach that circles onto his back.
