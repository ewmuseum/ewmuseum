WorldWide 14, 26 May 2005

26 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The lights dim in the arena, and the Lisbon crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The Spanish crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “A Tremendous Champion” and “WARPATH” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Madrid, Spain” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: Global Championship Wrestling concludes its tour of Europe with the final episode of WorldWide before Warpath! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan back at ringside with Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins at we roll towards what could be the biggest event in GCW history!

Rollins: Well, without me on the show that’s impossible, but damnit I can’t help but be a little excited. There’s nothing like GCW Pay-Per-View, JB, and GCW’s first Pay-Per-View in three years should be exciting. Plus, I’m happy to be getting out of Europe and back to the States!

Bryan: Indeed, Warpath comes to you from Houston, Texas, and it’s going to be an incredible show! The Jhub takes on Luke Hanson, Mike Hardy goes at it with Michael Stevens, LeStatt defends his title against Alex Blaq, and, of course, the huge Coliseum Cage match for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship!

Rollins: That’s right, and my sources tell me that Octavian will be revealing more information about that big main event right here tonight on WorldWide!

Bryan: You have sources?

Rollins: Everywhere.

Bryan: I see.

Camera pans in on that gorgeous blond bombshell who entered the ring with the US title holder last week. Passing by a couple of staff members she offers a cool hello before stopping at her locker room door. Dropping her duffle from slender shoulder she opens the door and tosses the bag in nonchalantly before flipping the light on and kicking the door shut behind her.

It was obvious she was rather pleased with herself still after the unfolding of last week's event. Hands slide to the hem of her shirt tugging the snug material upwards so that she can wriggle free from the constricting thing. If it weren't for the slight chuckle that came from behind her, that shirt would have come all the way off.

"Well senorita we haven't even had a first date yet ... moving a bit fast aren't you?"

Eva spun around her eyes wide in shock to see the man perched up on a table at the far wall. LeStatt Knight, the man she faced last week for his U.S. Title was about the last person she'd have expected to be here. His hair fell down covering half off his face, though his slight smirk and his icy blue eyes were easily recognizable. As always the man was cloaked in black, slacks, wife-beater, boots. Eva knew she couldn't hold that surprised look for too long, she could not give him that satisfaction.

Immediately that shirt would be whipped off as she turned her back to him and tugged out a whitebeater pulling over her semi bared form as she replied in icy tones.

" No we haven't..," Turning to regard him with a coy smirk," Is that why you are here to ask me out on a date? Or is there something else I can help you with?"

Gone was the surprise on those immaculate features. Replacing that once surprised look would be one of the ice queen, full of confidance. It was a look met with a half smirked look of darkness as LeStatt casually slid from his perch to walk along the far wall while regarding her like some predator would its prey.

"I am sure there is plenty that you can help me with Eva but..." Knight pushes the hair from his face and looks Eva up and down. "Your help is not something I require right now chicita. It seems that when it comes to you and me there has been plenty of HELP already ... not only did your brother help you find a way outt of thhat ring last night but it seems I inadvertently helped you make a name for yourself, isn't that right?"

He is only a few paces from her now with his icy stare locked into a set of eyes that perhaps could rival his own. His smile growws a bit wider, probably at the very thought that this woman could be as dangerous as he knew he could be.

That slender brow would perk in amusement as she stifled a laugh.

"Come now LeStatt, what upsets you more, the fact that you had to defend your title against a woman..or the fact that I could have beat in the ring that night. We both know I was holding my own, what my brother does is of his own accord."

Ice blues met ice blues. There was no hint of fear in her gaze, in fact, if he looked close enough she was looking rather predatory herself.

" You come any closer LeStatt and I might begin to think you have a thing for me. " His grin had widened. " Mmm....perhaps you have a big thing for me? Is that why you snuck in here? For a chance to catch me alone and....sweep me off my feet?"

LeStatt chuckles at this comment.

"Trust me, mamacita, I'm not sweeping anything that has been used by Johnny Lexicon. Although there is something intriguing about you Ms. Eva, that I will not deny. I am not here to try and get you by my arm like some trophy nor am I here to impress you with juvenile pick-up lines like the man that I will rip apart tonight in that ring. What I did come here to say Eva is ... Gracias senorita."

Her face turns cold as stone at the mention of Johnny. Those eyes narrow fire evident in them. She is obviously resisting the urge to retaliate in some manner.

" Danke? What exactly are you thanking me for LeStatt? After all it was you who helped to launch my career as you see it, though... what you believe and what I see are two very different things."

Eva steps around him moving across the room to grab a bottled water. Turning she leans against her table and crosses her arms eyeballing him.

" How about you stop playing coy with me and get to the point? I have things to do....like get dressed. Unless, of course, you wish to stay and watch?"

LeStatt raises an eyebrow and then licks his lips before continuing.

"Ah it seems I struck a small chord ... pity because you shouldn't get upset over someone like Johnny, trust me I learned the hard way. As for what I should be thanking you for, well it's simple really. Your actions last week proved something to me, something that I once knew but sadly forgot."

Knight walks over to the table where Eva is standing, or leaned over onto. As he walks behind her she doesn't move almost as if enjoying the attention. A smile shortly forms on her lips, but she wipes it away as she turns her head slightly to now eye this man. Knight leans in a bit towards her now, his face no longer holding that arrogant smile.

"Evil is as evil does right?"

That smile does not fade as again prow perks with interest. He was implying something, but leaving it just below the surface.

" Mmm, and the good die young.."

Right about then there is a knock on her door followed by the arrival of Erik VanLorne on the scene. His pale gaze locks on LeStatt and Eva nestled so close together. His own brow rises in question as Eva gently removes herself from LeStatt’s form.

" Hello Erik, LeStatt was just coming to congratulate me on my debut. Apparently I was a big..bang....Isn't that right LeStatt?"

Knight's eyes now fix on Erik, sure he is aware of Eva's movements but he is concentrated on the brother. A smirk so small that it's hard for almost anyone to notice momentarily creases those lips.

"A bang you were Eva ..." Knight walks pass Eva and towards the door but stops when he is side by side with Erik, then he looks back at Eva with a fire in his eyes. "Also remember chicita ... the foolish die painfully."

She merely smiled arrogantly. " Funny you should say that...."

Her gaze turns to Erik giving him a look. Erik finally speaks in soft tones to LeStatt.

" I believe you have over stayed your welcome, comrade. "

" It is alright Erik, he was just leaving."

LeStatt looks at them both a dark smile spreading across his lips as he nods his head towards Eva.

" Another time senorita."

LeStatt now walks out the door leaving Erik and Eva standing in the locker room alone. Erik watches Knight out then turns his gaze to his sister obviously not quite sure of exactly what just happened.

Eva smiles.

" I think I made a new friend."

Erik frowns.

" I am not liking your choice of friends."

Eva laughs as she walks over and shuts the door in the cameraman's face.

Toxic vs. Luke Hanson

“Life’s Sweet Drug” cues up, and the crowd explodes in cheers! Twizzy, clad in a black and white striped shirt that seems to have ice creams stains on it, struts down to the ring with a distinct orange bottle in his hand. He waves to the crowd and rolls under the ropes, sitting cross-legged in the corner. He pops the top of the bottle and throws his head back, pouring a few pills into his mouth. The cheers continue as “My Own Summer” hits, and another striped-shirted figure appears on stage. Wearing a pair of chinos and an expensive pair of sunglasses, The Jhub slaps hands with the crowd as he struts to the ring and climbs in.

Twizzy stands and smiles, extending his hand in friendship. The Jhub lowers his glasses and eyes the addict cautiously, and Twizzy happily extends the Twistodium bottle to The Jhub. The Jhub raises an eyebrow as the crowd explodes in cheers, ushering him to accept. The Jhub shakes his head, but Twizzy insists, shoving the bottle into The Jhub’s shirt. After a pause, The Jhub reaches into the bottle and extracts a single pill. With a deep breath he tosses it into his mouth and swallows, just as Luke Hanson’s music hits.

Hanson, massive and looking to be in a foul mood, storms to the ring and stops just short. Sneering angrily at Twizzy and The Jhub, Hanson shouts a few obscenities before “Do The Evolution” hits overhead. More boos rain down heavily upon another massive hulk of humanity, and an equally angry looking Toxic slowly saunters to the ring. Hanson hops onto the apron and circles the ring, eventually stepping over the top rope, eyeing The Jhub the whole way. Toxic reaches the ring and locks eyes with Twizzy, before finally sliding in.

Twizzy happily calls for the bell, and he and The Jhub back into opposite corners. Toxic and Hanson reluctantly eye each other, before finally meeting mid-ring. The two lock up, jockeying for position. The two come to a stalemate and break the collar-and-elbow tie up, backing away into their neutral corners. Toxic turns and spits at Twizzy, causing boos to pour down upon the ring.

The two circle each other before tying up again, and the taller Hanson begins moving Toxic back into the corner. The Jhub steps in the way and attempts to push the two apart. He finally backs Hanson away, and Hanson angrily swings a fist at The Jhub. The Jhub dodges the blow and backs away, pointing at the GCW logo on his shirt with a smile. Toxic seizes the opportunity to charge out of the corner and hit Hanson with a hard lariat, staggering the big man into the corner.

Toxic begins hammering Hanson with hard rights in the corner, before Twizzy steps in from behind and grabs his arm! Toxic spins around in anger, prompting Twizzy to scramble away from the furious big man. Hanson immediately steps out with a forearm shot to the back of Toxic’s head, dropping Toxic to a knee. Toxic rears back up to his feet, headbutting Hanson under the chin and sending him right back into the corner.

Toxic turns and goes for another big right, but again Twizzy cuts him off! This time Toxic flies around with a back elbow to the temple, flooring Twizzy to a chorus of boos. Toxic goes back to work on Hanson, and Twizzy abruptly sits up! The Jhub, shocked, cocks his head to a side, wondering how Twizzy recovered so quickly. Twizzy simply smiles as Hanson lowers his shoulder and drives Toxic all the way to the opposite corner, ramming him into the turnbuckle with authority.

Hanson pulls Toxic to the ropes and whips him across the ring, lowering his head in the center of the mat. Toxic steps to the side and lifts a leg, leaping over Hanson and nailing Twizzy with a boot to the face! Hanson jerks upright and turns with a vicious clothesline to The Jhub! The crowd explodes in boos as the two monsters look at their fallen foes, before finally turning back to each other and tying up once more.

Instantly The Jhub sits up, prompting a huge rain of cheers from the fans. With a satisfied smile, The Jhub grabs Twizzy’s prescription bottle resting on the mat, examining it with satisfaction. He turns to Twizzy, who is also on his way back up. They nod to each other and move towards Hanson and Toxic! Twizzy spins Toxic around and plants him with a Pill Popper! Hanson turns, only to get a huge Downward Spiral from The Jhub! The crowd explodes as each man covers their foe, making a double three-count in perfect unison! The crowd chants along as their hands hit the mat for the one… two… three!

The bell sounds and My Own Summer hits again. The Jhub and Twizzy stand and regard their fallen foes. The two finally shake hands, and The Jhub picks up the Twistodium bottle. Together they turn their backs on the ring and head out, discussing the powers of Twistodium as Hanson and Toxic recover in the ring.

Winners: The Jhub and Twizzy

With a grimace, Caldera stands from behind his desk and stretches his legs. Still sore and a bit swollen from surgery a few weeks ago, Caldera gingerly limps across the room. As the camera pans right, the twisted form of Mike Hardy appears seated on the floor. His legs crossed in an odd position and his arms extended to his sides, Hardy sits in meditation, eyes closed and chest heaving with each deep breath.

Hardy: How much longer do I have to keep this up?

Caldera: Until you have your moment of enlightenment.

Hardy: My legs are going numb.

Caldera: Good, it’s working then.

Hardy lets a huge breath of air escape his lungs, and his eyes snap open, an agitated look on his face.

Hardy: Shouldn’t I be lifting weights or something?

Caldera: Eyes closed!

Hardy sighs and closes his eyes, returning to his meditations. After a pause, the heavy door to the office opens, and the crowd explodes in cat calls as Eva VanLorne enters in a huff. Caldera smiles, obviously happy to see her. Behind her is her brother Erik, who closes the door behind him.

Caldera: Ms. VanLorne, lovely to see you again.

Hardy hearing Eva’s name, raises an eyebrow. He opens his left eye a slit to peek at the ever-scantily clad young lady, but Caldera immediately catches him and gives him a scolding snap of the fingers, prompting Hardy to jam his eyes closed.

Caldera: What can I do for you, guys?

Eva: Mr. Caldera, it seems as though I’m not really getting the… respect that I should around here. I was wondering if you could… help me with that.

Eva steps forward, pulling her shirt back to reveal a low-cut tanktop below. She stops inches from Caldera, looking up seductively. Caldera admires her with skepticism, but appears to be willing to play along. Erik simply rolls his eyes and leans against the door.

Caldera: And… uh… How can I do that?

Eva: Well, I’ve noticed that all these people seem to respect is gold. Now, I didn’t manage to beat LeStatt last week, but that doesn’t mean I can’t win gold. I mean, just because I have breasts… huge, perfect breasts… doesn’t mean I can’t wrestle, right?

At the mention of the word ‘breast,’ Hardy’s eye cracks open again. Caldera knowingly takes a step to his left, positioning himself between Hardy and Eva. With a grunt Hardy tries to inconspicuously reposition himself as Caldera continues to speak.

Caldera: Well, I… can’t say it does. Seems like you were holding your own against LeStatt last week. But I’m afraid there isn’t much I can offer you.

Eva: Are you… sure?

Eva smiles and drops her shirt, tossing it on the ground next to Hardy. Hardy picks up the shirt and inhales deeply, smiling for a moment before dropping it and continuing his discreet movement.

Eva: I was thinking that Erik and I might really like a shot at the GCW Tag Team Titles.

As Caldera considers this, Hardy finds his route around Caldera blocked by Caldera’s desk. Hardy attempts to stand, but finds his legs still twisted into his meditation position. He frantically begins pulling at his legs in an attempt to free himself and get a look at Eva, but appears to be fighting a losing battle.

Caldera: Well, you know those titles are vacant right now. But I’ll tell you what. At Warpath on Sunday, I’ll let you and Erik take on Allied Chaos for those belts. Is that satisfactory? …EYES SHUT!

Hardy frantically snaps his eyes closed and extends his arms, just before Caldera snaps around to see him out of position. Caldera does a double take before returning back to Eva, who is smiling happily.

Eva: Mmm, Mr. Caldera, you’re the best.

Eva steps around Caldera, her hand on his chest, and heads for her fallen shirt. She bends at the waist to pick it up, inadvertently extending her backside within inches of Hardy’s face. Hardy’s jaw drops as he is afforded a peek up Eva’s highly cut skirt, but Eva quickly snatches up her shirt and exits the room, Erik trailing behind with another roll of the eyes. Caldera clears his throat and brushes back his hair, turning back to Hardy. Hardy still sits on the floor, his legs crossed, and a blank look on his face.

Caldera: So… is the meditation working?

Hardy: I think I just had that moment of enlightenment you were talking about.

The shot returns to ringside, where JB and Rollins each seem to be reeling from Eva’s effects.

Bryan: So… um.. tag title match at Warpath, eh?

Rollins: Get ahold of yourself, JB. I know women seem threatening to you, but she won’t hurt you, I promise.

“Rabble Rouser” begins to play through the arena and Johnathan Borealis walks from the stage with a microphone in hand. He doesn’t waste time going down to the ring, just slides in and stands in the center of the ring with a grin on his face.

Borealis: Tonight the world gets the great opportunity and PRIVILAGE or watching a changing of the guard. Tonight I, John Borealis am stepping into the ring with one of the most feared competitors today in LeStatt Knight for his United States Title. Also tonight is the night that I will take that title from Knight. Most fans may not know this but LK and I go way back, all the way back to 3WA.

Now another thing you fans may not know is that the LeStatt Knight that you all know and love was a joke back in 3WA! That’s right he got his success from riding the coat tails of another loser in Johnny Lexicon. The bad part for Knight was that everyone KNEW that’s what happened and they disrespected him. The GCW fears LeStatt Knight, only I see nothing to fear. Tonight is the night that I expose LeStatt for what he really is …

“O’Fortuna” lifts the fans from their seats in applause. The United States Champion walks from the curtains with a stoic look on his face. He doesn’t acknowledge the fans in one bit, on his way down to the ring he unstraps his belt and lets it fall to the floor right before he slides into the ring. Knight walks right up to Borealis who seems amused.

Borealis: Well look who finally shows up … the guy I am going to beat tonight. I am glad you came out here for this little conversation though Knight because there are a few things that I want to get off my chest. Actually I just need to ask you one question really … how does it feel to…

Knight: Shut your damn mouth! ( the crowd goes wild ) Johnny Borealis … you know … well … ah the hell with it.

LeStatt aggressively flies at Borealis with a roundhouse kick that Johnny narrowly ducks underneath. Borealis adjusts nicely and attempts a side thrust kick but Knight blocks the blow with his forearms and pushes Borealis’ leg away. Johnny spins away from Knight regaining his balance and setting up again perhaps thinking of a new way to get in. Knight smirks at Borealis as the two begin to circle each other. Knight goes in for a tie-up but Borealis maneuvers behind Knight and wraps him in a rear waist lock. Knight throws a few elbows back but Johnny is perfectly placed behind him so knight stops for a moment before crossing his feet then spinning all the way around to look eye to eye with a bit surprised challenger. Knight wastes no time and drives a head butt into the face of Borealis who only loosens his grip but a second blow to the face does the trick and Borealis releases his hold on Knight.

Knight unleashes a series of chops to borealis’ chest which send him backwards into a corner. Knight immediately sends a closed right hand to the skull of Johnny which makes a sickening cracking sound through the arena. The referee rushed to Knight quickly screaming at him to back off. Knight smiles and puts his hands up in mock innocence before driving a boot into Johnny’s gut, then a second before backing up a bit before running in with a hard clothesline, but Borealis ducks out of the way causing Knight to go chest first into the turnbuckle. Knight springs backwards holding his chest, inadvertently he walks right into a drop toe hold by Borealis’. Knight’s face slams into the mat which makes Knight grabs at his nose and mouth, checking for bleeding.

Borealis reaches down and pulls Knight up by his long hair, when back on his feet Johnny lashes out with a hard right hand which sends Knight’s head back in a whiplash effect. Johnny drives into LK’s body with a knee, doubling Knight over. Johnny lifted his elbow up high before driving it down onto the back of LeStatt’s neck dropping the U.S champ to one knee. Swiftly Borealis dropps down with a falling drop kick that connected with the face of Knight sending him to his back.

Borealis drops down with a quick elbow drop to LeStatt’s chest now then pulls himself up only to drop down and plant another elbow onto his opponent’s chest. Borealis stands up and throws a hand in the air drawing a slight mixed reaction from the crowd. Knight is already returning to his feet, Johnny helps him by pulling him up with his hair. Knight this time doesn’t wait for Johnny to attack, Knight drives and elbow into the gut of Johnny which catches the challenger off guard. LK quickly hits a spinning elbow which makes Borealis lose his balance a bit and tumble to the side, but not off his feet. Knight rushes in with a boot to the midsection then immediately hits a swinging neck breaker!

Wasting no time Knight mounts Borealis and begins to hammer away with a barrage of right hands that are all finding their mark on the forehead of Johnny. Knight doesn’t stop this attack until the referee peels Knight from Borealis, who following the attack rolls over on his stomach grasping at his head. Knight rips himself from the ref’s grasp and stalks Johnny. Knight lifts Johnny up and plants a few forearm shots to his head before Irish whipping him to the ropes, Borealis bounces off the ropes, ducks under a big boot attempt, then he leap frogs Knight, but right as he spins around he is caught by a vicious super kick which plants him back down to the mat. Knight walks to Borealis’ fallen body and places his right boots squarely on his chest then screams for the referee to count.

1..

Johnny kicks out right after the ref puts his hand down for the one count. Knight smiles knowing full well that wouldn’t be enough to keep Johnny down. Borealis rolled to the ropes and quickly pulled himself up to his feet, even if a bit wobbly. Knight lets Johnny stand back up almost as if inviting him to try to mount some offense. Johnny obliges with a hard right hand, Knight then returns the favor with a right of his own. Knight aims to hit a haymaker but Borealis ducks underneath the blow and reaches back grabbing Knight by the back of his head and neck and falling down into a inverted neck breaker.

Quickly both men get to all fours and begin crawling towards each other. As they reach each other they, in unison, get up to one knee with John swinging first and connecting with knight’s cheek. Knight then rips into Borealis’ chest with a hard chop heard throughout the arena. Johnny lands another punch then LK hits a chop, and back and forth they go, getting more vile with their blows with each new swing until Johnny hits Knight so hard that he had to shake the cobwebs out, this gave Borealis the opportunity to hit Knight again, then again and a third time which sent Knight tumbling to his side.

Borealis pulls himself back to his feet but falls back into the corner trying to catch his breath. Catching his breath, Johnny notices Knight pull himself up to his feet which is his cue to go back in. Johnny hits a stiff kick to Knight’s side then follows up with another on the other side, Borealis goes for a straight kick but Knight catches his foot … ENZIGURI by Johnny !

Knight drops to his knees holding the back of his head. Borealis smiles and readies himself before swinging his foot around, going for a beheading kick, Tajiri style but Knight rolls out of the way and quickly springboards himself back to his feet. Borealis seems shocked and the fans are going crazy. It is obvious that Knight still has to regain all of his composure though as he closes his eyes briefly and shakes his head. Borealis goes for a round house kick but Knight drops down with a falling drop kick of his own taking out the leg that Johnny was standing on, forcing him to crumble holding his knee.

Knight’s smile fades as a look of total concentration comes over his face. He immediately begins putting the boots to that now injured knee a scream following each kick. Knight then lifts that bad leg up and wraps his body around before locking legs and falling back into a figure-four! Borealis immediately begins howling in pain, screaming the word “NO!” over and over again.

Knight’s teeth can almost be heard grinding as he putts as much pressure on the hold as he possibly can, perhaps trying to break Borealis’ legs. Borealis tries to gain control and begins to slowly rock from side to side, trying to pull the classic figure four reversal. Knight senses this happening and lets the hold go and quickly gets back to his feet. Borealis grabs at that knee as he rolls around on the mat. Knight helps John up who puts all the pressure on his good leg. Knight grabs John by his wrist and goes to Irish whip him but instead pulls him back in and ducks down lifting Borealis on his shoulders before delivering a hellacious Death Valley Driver! Knight quickly goes for the cover.

1…

2…

Johnny lifts a shoulder up at the last possible second! Knight sits up and at first seems frustrated but lets out a low chuckle then stands up, bringing Borealis with him. Knight delivers a kick to the gut then immediately turns it into a diving power bomb! He goes for the cover.

1..

2..

AGAIN JOHN KICKS OUT! Now Knight shows his frustration by mounting Borealis and again driving fist after fist into his face. This time he stops himself and suddenly gets this sadistic smile on his face. Knight leaps to his feet and jumps outside where he forces the time keeper from his chair and takes it, folding it up and diving back into the ring with it. Knight slams the chair on the mat and awaits Borealis to turn around. The fans are screaming like crazy as Borealis drags his body to his feet. John turns and somehow ducks a chair shot then he leaps into the air and kicks the steel chair right back into the face of Knight who crumbles to the mat! Borealis’ leg couldn’t take the pressure and buckled as well, both men laying on the mat now.

Borealis crawls to the ropes and begins pulling himself up. Knight has gotten to his knees and has noticed that blood is dripping from a gash on his forehead. Knight’s breathing becomes erratic at the sight of his own blood, then he growls, almost animal-like. Knight gets to his feet but his eyes are still fixed on the blood. He takes a few steps backwards then turns around but he is met by a SPIKE DDT!

The crowd is going ballistic at this exciting United States Title match! Knight is almost completely motionless except for the occasional blink of his eyes. Borealis now begins pulling himself back to his feet. When he reaches the standing position he looks up at the turnbuckle then after a few moments begins to ascend it. It takes him some time to get to the top with his injury and all but he finally does … that’s when knight makes his move. Knight, who is now on one knee pushes himself the rest of the way up and runs towards Borealis, Knight climbs up the ropes like a cat then hooks Johnny’s head and arm and dives backwards delivering a top rope KNIGHTFALL !

Both men are down but the impact caused knight’s arm to barely be draped over Johnny’s chest, and the ref dives down.

1…

2..

3!!

The bell sounds as Knight defends his U.S. Title successfully, but neither man moves from their spot. Each are laying in pain trying to regain some form of consciousness. The referee drapes the U.S. Title over Knight’s shoulder and the fans stand and applaud the action.

Winner: LeStatt Knight

Ok, things were just getting weird now. He was dressed up like a general, with the weird flared pants and everything. Shined boots, a corn cob pipe, baton, maybe he was trying to look like MacArthur. Either way it was weird, and you could definitely tell the Allied Chaos boys felt the same way. They sat in chairs that were just plain too small for them, but hell he was the teacher, the blackboard behind Stevens covered in stick figures was supposed to make that clear, apparently.

Michael Stevens: Alright kids THIS is Mike Hardy.

The baton cracked against the blackboard, at a little figure who looked scrawny even by stick figure standards.

Michael Stevens: And THIS is you two.

Again a crack, this time pointing to the two sticks with abnormally large smiley faces.

Michael Stevens: Now the most important thing to remember is that there are TWO of you, and only one of him. Got it?

Kyle Niker: Yeah I think that’s pretty clear.

Michael Stevens: Good good, now THIS is me.

Who the hell draws a stick figure Godzilla and claims it’s themselves…weird.

Michael Stevens: OK guys I’m not going to be in the ring to help you all out, and if I know Steven there’s a good chance he’ll ban me from ringside, but still that shouldn’t be a problem for you. Like I said there’s two of you and only one of him, this should be beyond easy.

Hardy’s a little pipspeak of a no talent schmuck. He’s tiny, inconsequential, but the one thing he has going for him is that he’s the luckiest SOB I’ve ever met. Watch that, try and find his rabbit foot or whatever, it wasn’t lucky for the rabbit, so make it unlucky for him. Now he’s also quick so you’re going to be quicker. He’s pretty dumb, so that shouldn’t be too much of a challenge for you, ummm I mean, well yeah, not a challenge, sure.

And remember I can’t stress this enough there are TWO OF YOU. See what I mean, that’s one more than Hardy has, which means you’ve got two more fists and two more feet than he has, it’s called double team, do it often. Remember I’ve gotta take this guy on all by myself at Warpath, and while of course I could beat him with both hands tied behind my back, a broken leg, and a blindfold, I want you two to make sure and work him over pretty good.

You two should beat him no problem, but make him suffer first, wear him down so that by the time he gets into the ring with me he’s already hurting, that’s your goal tonight, winning or losing doesn’t matter so long as you hurt him.

Conner Pearce: But if it’s so easy for you to beat him, don’t you want him healthy so it’s more of a challenge?

Michael Stevens: HA! There’s no way Hardy could be a challenge, it’s just the kid’s got stamina see, and I don’t want to spend five hours beating him up and down each side of the ring, understand? Besides it’s that whole lucky thing, I want you two to make sure he doesn’t have the speed to catch me with any of that jumping bean shit he likes so much. The only weapon he’s got is his body, break his body, he has no weapons and I can put him away at my leisure, like it should be, see?

Kyle Niker: Yeah I think we’ve got it boss.

Michael Stevens: EXCELLENT! Now I’m going to turn to figure two here.

Flipping the board over he revealed a teaming mass of stick figures and stick tanks, all seemingly burying under a mass of small chalk dots.

Michael Stevens: THIS is why you never fight a ground war with Russia in the winter….

As Stevens continued to blather on without seemingly much purpose we fade away into the beautiful bliss of commercials.

With a tremendous smile, the new GCW Hardcore champion struts through the hallways backstage. Cheers rise up from the crowd as Trey Tremendous struts past workers and crewmembers, largely ignoring their hellos. Tremendous turns the corner and finds himself standing in front of two striped men in the midst of a highly philosophical discussion.

Jhub: No way, chocolate ice cream is definitely superior to strawberry.

Twizzy: I’m afraid I must disagree with you, good sir, strawberry is better. Better flavor, better color-

Jhub: Color? What, you got something against brown things?

Tremendous: Excuse me, gentlemen, champion coming through…

Trey attempts to move past, but The Jhub places a hand on the champion’s shoulder, stopping his progress. The crowd gasps, unsure of what to expect. Trey is taken aback, stepping away a few steps.

Jhub: What’s the rush, buddy? Not in the mood for chit-chat?

Tremendous: It’s not that, it’s just that your addicted friend here doesn’t have the kind of stuff I’m into.

Tremendous: That’s not QUITE what I meant…

Before long, Kevin Jacobs steps in from the side of the screen, leaning into the conversation.

Jacobs: Did I hear someone say ice cream?

Jacobs smiles hopefully, licking his lips in anticipation. The Jhub, Twizzy and Tremendous simply stare blankly, unsure of how to respond. Finally all three shrug in unison and turns their backs on Jacobs, prompting him to lower his head and shuffle out of the scene.

Jhub: Look, Trey, I just wanted to say… good luck with that belt. I’m sure you know you’re defending it all the time, and being hardcore in GCW means you have to be one tough son of a bitch. Or just plain nuts like Twizzy here.

Twizzy raises an eyebrow and considers this, before finally nodding in agreement.

Jhub: But just now when you came around that corner, you left yourself open for chair shots, ladder shots, kendo sticks, TV cameras, and pies.

Tremendous: Pies?

Twizzy: Pies.

Jhub: So just remember, a little advice from someone who knows… Watch where you step, because you never know who might be waiting around the corner to nail you in the face with a chair.

Tremendous eyes The Jhub intensely, prompting Twizzy to wander off to avoid a possibly confrontation. Tremendous simply nods and eyes The Jhub as he walks away. The Jhub smiles and pushes through a nearby door into the locker room. The moment he enters the room, he’s promptly knocked to the floor… hit in the face with a chair.

Hanson: I’m sorry it had to come to this…

Hanson eyes the fallen Jhub, placing a few more kicks thoroughly into his rib cage. He backs away and calls for a mic, his anger more than visible on his face. He begins to speak.

Hanson: Jhub...I am sick of you. I am sick of seeing you here weekly, I am sick of your half-assed attempts to make a difference, and I'm sick of the reminder you are of how easily the 'mighty' fall. I guess what I'm trying to say is, I want you out of GCW.

Leave on your own accord or a stretcher, I don't care, I just want you out. And so, I'd like to propose that our match Sunday be a dumpster match.

You, and the garbage, and the closing of the lid...all one big metaphor for things that I don't care to go into, because it should be obvious to anyone with half a brain.

So whenever you pull yourself up, make your way to a Whirlpool or massage table backstage and mull this over. Because I'm pretty damn sure the powers that be would love to see another gimmick match on the card, and the possibility, or in this case likelihood, of this being your final match in GCW will probably sucker a few more viewers into buying Warpath.

I'll see you Sunday, Jhub. I can't wait to be the guy who puts you out of your misery.

Hanson steps on The Jhub on his way out, placing his four hundred fifty pounds on The Jhub’s ribcage. The shot returns to ringside, where JB seems furious.

Bryan: Damnit, that Luke Hanson ambushed The Jhub again. That’s an intensely personal feud that is going to explode at Warpath, in what I guess will be a dumpster match!

Rollins: Oh, don’t sound so upset, JB. Jhub should have listened to his own advice.

Bryan: But what the hell is Hanson thinking, ambushing his former best friend like that?

Rollins: I only have one thing to say about that situation, JB…

Bryan: What’s that?

Rollins: Vanilla is better than chocolate and strawberry.

Mike Hardy vs. Allied Chaos

Allied Chaos appears on stage, looking confident and excited about their handicap match. With a huge leaping hi-five, the two head to the ring with smiles on their faces. They slide into the ring and execute another precision hi-five, before “The Hand That Feeds” hits next. The crowd EXPLODES in the biggest eruption of the night, and Mike Hardy bounds out on stage. Clad in his extravagant new ring attire, Hardy heads for the ring while slapping hands with the Spanish fans at ringside on the way.

The moment Hardy hits the ring, Allies Chaos is on him. The bell sounds as Niker and Pearce hammer away on Hardy, who manages to power to his feet. Allied Chaos whips Hardy to the ropes, and takes him down with a huge double clothesline. Pearce scoops Hardy up and powers him overhead, while Niker hops on the turnbuckle. Niker takes off, grabbing Hardy’s shoulders while Pearce drives him down into the mat for a huge spike powerbomb!

The crowd oohs in pain as Hardy lies motionless on the canvas. Niker and Pearce begin leaping in the air in celebration, before Pearce shakes his head and settles Niker, keeping their heads in the match. Niker scoops Hardy back up and sends him into the ropes, and Pearce tosses Hardy high into the air! Hardy manages to change direction in mid-air, and he nails Pearce in the face with a huge missile dropkick!

The crowd cheers as Niker moves in and catches Hardy with a stiff right to the face. Niker hammers away on Hardy as Pearce gets back to his feet, and the two put the boots to Hardy in the corner. Hardy finally manages to nail Pearce with a hard right! Hardy alternates right hands for Allied Chaos, finally backing Niker out of the corner! Hardy turns and sends Pearce to the mat with a Caldera-style European uppercut!

Hardy backs into the ropes and charges Niker, dropping him with a surprisingly vicious clothesline! Pearce staggers back to his feet, and Hardy hits the ropes again. Hardy spring back and leaps onto Pearce’s shoulders. Pearce steps forward and drives Hardy down again, but Hardy twists through Pearce’s legs and takes him over with a reverse hurricanrana!

Hardy gets to his feet as Niker stumbles backward. Hardy hooks him and promptly drives him into the mat with a huge Waterfall! With a smile, Hardy pulls Pearce on top of Niker, and heads for the top rope! Hardy bounces a few times, then leaps with an enormous shooting star press! He nails both men and pushes Pearce of, covering Niker for an easy one-two-three!

Winner: Mike Hardy

Hardy gets to his feet and leaps onto the turnbuckle, celebrating with the crowd and his music explodes into the arena once more.

Bryan: He's done it again! Two weeks in a row Mike Hardy has pulled a miracle out of thin air! This kid's star hasn't even begun to shine, Rollins.

Rollins: Well, you got one thing right.

Bryan: And what's that?

Rollins: He's just a kid. Nothing more, nothing less.

Bryan: Oh, come off it, Renegade! He destroyed The Dark Messiah...

Rollins: ... which was supposed to be my victory...

Bryan: ... and single-handedly just took down both members of Allied Chaos! For Christ's sake Rollins, what is it going to take to get you to start singing this guy's praises?

Rollins: Sing his praises? Sing his... excuse me? Am I supposed to be impressed that he managed to overcome two burnt-out wrestlers who like to label themselves a tag team? Is that enough to get you going, Bryan? Is it enough to get you to sing like a canary for Hickenbottom's mediocrity?

Bryan: I...

Rollins: He's better than this curtain-jerking bullshit, Bryan. The kid is talented, but he doesn't challenge himself. He doesn't push himself. There was a time when Hickenbottom ruled the undercard, a time when the cruiserweight championship was synonymous with Mike Hardy. But you know what I was doing during that time, Bryan?

Bryan: This isn't about you, Rollins. This is about Mike H...

Rollins: I was winning World titles, my friend. I was carving out my own slice of history here in GCW while all Hickenbottom could do was lick my table scraps. It's pathetic and insulting, and this kid is either incredibly naive or just plain stupid to think that he can make it to the top of this business by bowling over puppets like Allied Chaos. He wants to make a name for himself? He'll have to do better than Michael Stevens, that's for damn sure.

Bryan: Oh, and where do you propose he find such a "worthy competitor," Renegade?

Rollins: He won't have to look far, Bryan. I promise you that.

Bryan: I suggest you keep your mind focused on calling the next match, Rollins.

Rollins: And I suggest you stop talking like a bitch, or I'm gonna smack you like one.

Bryan: ...

Rollins: Don't look at me. Look straight ahead.

Bryan: ...

Rollins: That's right, play-by-play man. Stick to the script, it's better for your health.

The shaky camera ran into the locker room and froze at the sight in front of it. Alex Blaq, bloody and beaten hung by his chained up wrists by the doorway of his locker room. Blood poured from his face and a large spot on his chest. EMT’s rushed into the room and froze at the horrific scene. On the wall next to Blaq was a message written in black ink, it read:

“Win it all … or LOSE everything”

The EMT’s broke from their daze and went to pry Blaq’s body from it’s “crucifixion”. One EMT grabbed a towel and wiped the blood from the man’s face while another grabbed a towel and began wiping away the blood that was seeping from his chest. The gashes that formed on Blaq’s chest caused the EMT to jump back.

“GUYS LOOK!”

The EMT’s stopped what they were doing and looked at the cuts which were already bringing more blood. Their faces moved in a bit closer and the one with the towel wiped the blood away once more then he whispered.

“L…K?”

The shot instantly leaves the room and returns to ringside.

Bryan: Oh… my… One can only assume that LeStatt Knight had something to do with that, but I didn’t think he was capable of doing something like that…

Rollins: You have the shortest memory of anyone I’ve ever met, JB. LeStatt Knight was one of the sickest men I’d ever seen in the ring. And now just because he’s all buddy-buddy with the golden boy World Champ and the crowd is singing his praises, you think he’s changed? Hell no! LeStatt Knight is a vicious human being, JB, and frankly I get off on that.

The show heads backstage once more…

Knock, knock.

"Yo Jordan, it's the federales."

"It isn't the federales, Trey."

"You sure? Might wanna let me check, if they're lookin' for you."

"Haha, jackass. You're the one that's prone to lose a title anytime--I think I'll handle it."

Jordan stands and pulls his fur coat over the cast on his arm, opening the door a crack and quickly stepping out. It closes behind him and he stares down the major agents of Anarch--Johnny Lexicon and the Van Lorne twins. Lex cocks a grin. "Jordan, have a minute? I'd like to talk to you about something." Without giving him time to respond. "See my legal agencies have discovered something very interesting, it looks like... Well... Trey is still mine."

"...that kinda thing's already legal?"

"Of course. Trey signed a contract to my label, turns out the hardcore champ is an Anarch."

"Oh Christ, I thought you meant, well, you know. Wasn't sure if you'd come for alimony payments or divorce papers, but--you said that good old Trey's riding under your evil banner?"

"Did I stutter?"

"A little--not as bad as you used to. Still dance aimlessly around the point, though, so hop to it."

"I have a deal. Me and Trey, at Warpath for the Hardcore title. I win, you become my two newest Anarchs," he says unperturbed.

"Two? I know you don't want a jack-off manager in your entourage, Lex. What's in the fineprint?"

"There's no fine print, as an Anarch you'll enjoy all the luxuries life can offer. Women, cars, insurance, premium benefits, the best doctors money can buy keeping you at top shape in the ring."

But you belong in the ring Jordan, there could be a lot... a lot of money in this. Of course if you refuse, I'll have to pursue legal action, my lawyers will keep him tied up and out of the ring for months. It's up to you."

"Hm. That gaping maw by your side have the contract to sign?"

Johnny nods to Eva. "Do I ever come unprepared?" Taking the contract he holds it out to White along with a black and gold pen. Lex's grin says it all.

"I'll get back to you on that" Jordan steps into the locker room, and noises ensue--noises like talking! After a few moments he steps back out, handing the signed contract over to Lexicon.

"Lexi, can I give you a word of advice?"

Might of Rome hits in the arena, and the Madrid fans explode in boos. Octavian emerges on stage with a big smile on his face, and he struts towards the ring. Bryan: And it’s time for our main event, fans, but before the match Mr. Rollins tells me that Octavian has something to say about the Coliseum Cage match.

Rollins: I can’t wait to hear what he’s come up with, JB. This should be interesting.

Bryan: Octavian has designed his own cage for the main event at Warpath Sunday night, and Jason Dread might be in serious trouble.

Octavian: Mr. Dread, I wanted to explain to you exactly what it is you have to look forward to this weekend in Houston. It’s no secret that I’ve been working long and hard to design a cage worthy of Rome. And I have done just that. If you would, please, roll the footage.

The lights in the arena dim, and the MegaTron flickers to life. Might of Rome plays again in the background, and the dim image of a massive steel structure appears on screen. An unmarked wrestling ring sits in the center of a darkened room, a circular structure surrounding it. The lights rise and the structure comes into full view. Made of black steel girders, the cage resembles the Roman Coliseum. The walls are several feet wide, and the openings every few feet near the top of the cage seem large enough to stand on.

Text appears next to the cage, indicating it to be twenty feet high and twenty feet in diameter. An overhead view appears, revealing that the ring is surrounded by a steel mesh platform that rests flush and even with the ring, so that the mat and the steel mesh are level. The very top of the cage is lined in gleaming barbed wire.

The lights in the arena come back up as the MegaTron dies out. The crowd murmurs frantically as Octavian nods in sadistic anticipation.

Rollins: The damn thing looks just like the Coliseum! And I know, I got to see it when we were in Rome a few weeks ago!

Bryan: You were here?

Rollins: I was always here.

Octavian: In that structure, Jason, you will meet your end. That GCW World Heavyweight Championship will come back around my waist in the only way it should: inside the Coliseum. Now, send out my opponent, and I will give you a preview of what you’ll be seeing on Sunday!

They struggle and Trey throws Octy to the mat hard. Octavian is up quickly and looking at Trey a little different. They lock up again and Octy goes into the ropes he comes back and ducks a clothesline, pulls up his momentum and turns to hammer Trey with a hard right. Trey fires back and they trade blows until Trey tackles Octavian and straddles his waist, raining down with vicious rights and brutal lefts until the ref can get Trey to get off.

Octavian shakes his head and starts getting up slowly, touching his now busted lip and bleeding nose with a look of fury. Trey moves to close in on him but Octy kicks him the gut and slams a right hand in hard. He spears Trey where he stands and takes his turn straddling and pummeling. They break off and get up, they lock up and Trey sends Octy into the corner and follows with a big drop kick. Octavian stumbles out and Trey grabs him in a belly to belly suplex. Tremendous is on fire as he ascends the turnbuckles and lands a HUGE elbow drop.

Trey is on his feet almost instantly as Octavian writhes in pain at his feet. Trey reaches for his foe when Octavian throws a closed fist into his junk, a collective wince goes through the male audience and Trey is stunned long enough for Octavian to scramble to his feet. Trey goes for a ride into the ropes and takes a clothesline down to the mat. Octavian stomps on him again and again until he feels comfortable enough to stand Trey up and hooks his head under the arm. He raises an arm to the cloud before going for the DDT, but wait, Trey holds his ground and heaves. Octavian’s eyes are round as saucers as he's lifted and the flap jacked right on his face.

Octavian holds his mug as Trey runs and jumps onto the middle rope for a moonsault, but Octavian moves. Trey is first up and throws a hard running kick into Octavian’s side. Octavian rolls and comes up on his feet, they lock up and Octavian tosses Trey into the corner before hammering him with fast right hands. Trey takes every one, Octavian is suddenly distracted by a grinning Jordan White. He says something that the camera's microphones can't pick up, Octavian gives a puzzled expression the moment before Trey explodes out of the corner. He pushes and hammers Octavian with a feral intensity.

Octavian is reeling when Trey manages to lock on a reverse headlock and hooks his opponents knee and lifts. He hammers Octavian with the culpable and the crowd explodes. Octavian collects himself while the ref reaches a six count and Trey slides back into the ring. Trey fights it and reaches for the ropes, the pain must be insurmountable, He looks ready to tap but holds out, Jordan is shouting from the outside and Trey reaches for the ropes but it's no use.
