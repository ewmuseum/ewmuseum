Octavian Biography Real Name Gaius Octavianus Nicknames The Italian Stallion, The Ragin' Roman Hometown Rome, Italy Height 6'5' Weight 285 Fed Titles 3x GCW World Heavyweight Champion; HardCore Champion Fed Awards Hall of Fame Biography Octavian's records may have since been broken, but no wrestler set the bar higher during GCW's original tenure. A little-known but fast-rising star at GCW's outset, Octavian exploded onto the scene as the clear-cut leader of the new promotion, and went on to become the most decorated star of the 2001-2002 era. Though born into great affluence, Octavian came from humble beginnings. He began his career under a different persona in Mr. Danger's ACWF in 2000 alongside Steven Caldera. The two became fast friends, and the two soon departed for the CWU. Caldera took over the company and saw great potential in the raw talent of Gaius Octavianus. Caldera urged Gaius to be himself and draw on his heritage, and soon after Octavian was born. Though Octavian had left his family's empire in Italy, he quickly returned to his roots as a powerful ancestor of Roman royalty. His improvement was exponential, as he quickly moved up the ranks and won the CWU's Intercontinental Title. Shortly thereafter he fought through a one-night tournament at NC-17 2000 to become the number one contender to the World Title. The CWU closed before Octavian could get his shot, but Caldera opened GCW in 2001 and quickly put the spotlight on his favorite star. Octavian tore through yet another tournament, winning his second in as many months, to become the first ever GCW World Champion. Despite their shared roots, Caldera and Octavian did not always see eye to eye. Though Octavian had begun his GCW tenure as the leader of Caldera's Establishment and the hand-picked World Champion, Octavian began to suffer from nagging injuries that caused him to pull back from his support of The Establishment. Rather than let his superstar rest, Caldera replaced him, causing Octavian to lose his World Title to Rich Rollins. Still, Octavian did not stay out of the hunt for long. After recovering from his injuries, Octavian came back and quickly set his sights on the World Title again. John Reed had made his dream come true by winning the title from Rollins, but found Octavian waiting in the wings. Reed fought hard, but Octavian was not to be denied. Many critics had credited Caldera with Octavian's first World Title win, but Octavian silenced them all with a clean and brutal victory over Reed to win the World Title a second time. Octavian then embarked on one of the most legendary reigns in GCW history, becoming the first man to manage to hold the highly contested belt for longer than a few weeks. He was challenged by Tristain Mayhem, whose debauchery was personally offensive to the regal Octavian. The two battle ferociously in a very personal series of matches, with Octavian defeating Mayhem at Devil's Night and again at NC-17. Mayhem finally managed to get the better of Octavian at Christmas Chaos, but the reign of Octavian was already cemented as the best GCW had seen. Octavian continued his career in GCW, never taking his sights off the gold he'd become obsessed with. After Jason Dread was chosen as the new leader of The Establishment, Octavian briefly feuded with President Caldera once again. Still, the two could not stay apart for long. At Shockwave 2002, Octavian took on World Champion Badger Benandanti and The Jhub for the GCW Title in an ACWF Match. Octavian and Caldera reunited and Octavian made history, capturing his then-unprecedented third GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Octavian was one of the best all-around athletes in GCW history. He got his start as an excellent technician, but quickly learned and excelled in nearly every aspect of in-ring combat. Though his temper was notorious and occasionally got the better of him, it also made him one of the most feared men in wrestling. He was known as a powerful, relentless machine whose hard work and conditioning were unparalleled. He enjoyed punishing his opponents in the ring, and his quest to be on top of GCW was so consuming that he worked harder and longer than anyone else in order to continue his endless pursuit of the GCW World Title.
