Keegan O'Conner Biography Real Name Keegan O'Conner Nicknames Chaos, The Fiery One Hometown Los Angeles, California Birthday 03/25/1980 Height 6'6 Weight 285 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Keegan grew up with a life of priviledge in Los Angeles, California interested in contact sports. As a result he attended the best schools and had the best trainers to pursue what he wished. At the age of thirteen Keegan started boxing and stuck with it through out high school. By the time he graduated he was competing in the Golden Gloves. He wasn't bad, but he could have been a lot better. One night, after his match was stopped due to the referee's discretion, Keegan took his anger out on the unsuspecting referee. As a result, the young man was barred from competing in the boxing ring. Rather undecided about what to do next, Keegan was approached by a wrestling promoter for a small time deal out in Pico Mundo California. The promoter told him he could be just what they were looking for. Well Keegan took the man up on his offer, and went to work immediately for the company. It was a small time deal, but it was satisfying for him. A few years went by before Keegan had even known it. It was then, one night in the ring across from John Samson that Keegan was really noticed. A scout for New-Found Wrestling had been in attendance, and had cornered Keegan after the show, offering him a contract. Without hesitation Keegan signed, and joined his opponent that night as being the first two employees of New-Found Wrestling. Keegan would go on to become the first ever NFW Heavyweight Champion, where as Samson became the first NFW United States Championship. NFW was sold, and purchased by Keegan's professional wrestling trainer, Joshua Kosidlo. For a brief period of time NFW and Global were partnered together in a development deal. Keegan and Samson were the first two people to be promoted to the Global roster. After a handful of tag and singles matches Keegan would later be admitted to rehab for drug and alcohol problems. By the time of his release from rehab Global was already closed. After the closing of Global, Keegan was without a job. Encore Entertainment came knocking and Keegan signed a decent deal with them. His time in Encore would be cut short after failing a drug test for steroid use. When his suspension was lifted the company had already folded. After a month of getting his life back together Keegan began talks with GCW. He has since been signed to join his old friends John Paul Samson and Delylah.
