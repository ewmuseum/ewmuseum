G.G. Dupree Biography Real Name GOOD GAWD! Dupree [Birth Certificate Lists: Geoff Gerald Dupree] Nicknames the Southern Manager Extroirdinaire; Mister Mouth-O-Matic Hometown Houston, Texas Birthday undisclosed Height 5'9 Weight 165 lbs. Federation Awards n/a Biography - Born Geoff Gerald Dupree to Clarissa Morrison from San Antonio, Texas and Henri "Hank" Dupree a native of Quebec, Canada. - Grew up with family in Houston, Texas after his father perished in a logging accident and his mother became mentally unstable and unable to care for him. - Grew up in a strict household with his older grandparent’s and never saw a wrestling match until the age of thirteen. - Graduated high school and enrolled in the University of Tennessee in Knoxville as a communications major. - His college roommate took him to see his first wrestling match his freshman year and both decided they wanted to become professional’s in the sport. - Both enrolled in the Tennessee Pro Factory, a wrestling school run by local wrestler/manager/booker Freddy Freebird. - His roommate excelled in the program and became "Glorious" Bradley Gaines eventually becoming successful, while Dupree wasn’t picking up the basics well enough to really move on to the next level of training. - Was convinced by Freebird that due to his size [at the time a whopping 140 pounds] and lack of natural athleticism that he would never realize his dream of being a pro wrestler, but Freebird was impressed with Dupree’s overflowing charisma and the way he was able to put together half decent promos. Freebird hooked Dupree up with long time manager "Eloquent" Eddie Hearns who gladly took Dupree under his wing and trained him in the ways of a manager. - Took his act to Nashville, Tennessee after gaining his communications degree and was introduced to a budding wrestler named Red Hicks. They became fast friends and decided to stake out on their own as a manager/wrestler team, with Red adopting the persona of the masked "Nashvillain" and Geoff taking on the name G.G. [he says G.G. stands for "GOOD GAWD!"] Dupree. - Helped Red/Nashvillain make his bones throughout the South until Red was banned from wrestling in the South due to an infraction, which no one knows the real details about besides Red, G.G., and six Board of Ethics members. - Went on to manage other wrestlers when Red disappeared such as: Lou Lucky, The War Machine, John Stars and Corporal Ulysses Bars [the Stars & Bars tag team], The Dark Horse and Diablo de Ville [the Guerilla Warfare tag team], and most notably "Glorious" Bradley Gaines his former college roommate and a star in the South. - Late last year after severing ties with Bradley Gaines, G.G. contacted his old friend Red to try and convince him to dust off the old Nashvillain mask once again and to this time make a serious run up North where he would be guaranteed heat because of his pro Southern outlook. Other Feds n/a Other Federation Awards n/a Weapon Details white parasol umbrella allegedly loaded & a Confederate flag. Management Style loud, annoying, abrasive, nosey, all around pain in the ass Special Moves every move you make is special when you're livin' under God's great Southern sun. Other Tactics Always carries one of two things to the ring: number one is the old Confederate flag, which he’ll wave around at any opportunity and it has also been known to make an appearance in matches in the Nashvillain’s favor. The second thing is a white parasol umbrella, which has the Confederate flag airbrushed on the top of it, it also has been known to come into play during matches and some have speculated that it is loaded with something far heavier than usual umbrellas. Much to the chagrin of the general population, G.G. never seems to be too far from a microphone and on numerous occasions he’s been known to snatch a mic ringside during one of his client’s matches and either praise and give encouragement to N.V. or to berate and insult his opponents. . . .right over the loudspeaker in the middle of a match!
