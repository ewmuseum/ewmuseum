WorldWide 142 Quicken Loans Arena — Cleveland, Ohio ---- WW 142: A Surprise In A Vent, A Surprising Event Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny's office is decorative quite festively for the holiday season, however, it's unclear which holiday (or amalgam of holidays) the decor represents. His wax statues have all been mummified. The fichus plants are covered in neutral ornaments. Some Grand Funk is playing on a cassette tape on GBJ's desk. The commissioner himself is dressed up in a DIY costume. He looks like a cross between himself and a nihlist take on Santa Claus. He's got the suit down, but it's made out of garbage bags and tape, and underneath the bags, he wears a hooded sweatshirt with the hood up. Garbage Bag sits in a throne instead of a cushioned office chair, and he looks back and forth between two open filing cases on his desk while he holds a manila folder open in his hand. GBJ: Let's see. Naughty or nice? Muffled Voice: NAUGHTY! GBJ’s head spins around, not entirely sure where that came from. He continues to flip through the folders. GBJ: What about this one... Muffled Voice: NAUGHTY! Johnny tosses the folders on his desk, as he was sure he heard a voice coming from somewhere. He scans the room with his eyes before picking up the files and shuffling through them once more. Muffled Voice: NAUGHTY! NAUGHTY! NAUGHTY! They’re all nau-- CRASH! Garbage Bag Johnny sits there, stunned, as the air vent grate directly above his monstrous desk comes crashing down. He looks up and notices a set of pure white eyes surrounded by some rather questionable eye shadow. Before Garbage bag Johnny addresses the man in the vent, he presses his speakerphone button and talks over the intercom to a secretary he mistakenly thinks he has. GBJ: Sheila, can you please get Aaron Fujita. I've got a vent that needs fixing. Garbage Bag puts the files aside... all except for one. Written on the folder is the name of the man slithering out of the fallen duct: CLYDE FOX. GBJ: Clyde... I've got to say, I'm a little surprised to see you. What are you doing in my vent? Fox pulls his tattered cape across the lower half of his face as if he was Batman. Fox: You’re not the first one to ask me that... and... there’s so many ways I could answer you. Fox rises to a ‘normal’ postured stance. Fox: The first thing that you should know is that using your office door is for conformists. I, on the other hand, am a deviant. I make my own rules. I make my own entrances. If I choose to crawl up Sally Ford’s crab infested skirt and have her walk in here that way, then I reserve the right. Kapeesh? GBJ opens his mouth to respond, but the emo one keeps on rolling. Fox: But enough about that. I’m here for one simple reason. Where is my United States Championship belt and why haven’t you identified me as such? GBJ: You fought a good match last week, and hey, a win's a win, but the title doesn't change hands on count outs. I was rooting for you--god knows you need something to get you out of this funk--but sometimes that's the way these things shake down. In fact, take a seat. Let's talk about it. With no other chairs in the office, GBJ taps his thigh, offering the Prince of Emo a spot on his lap. Fox: Uhhhhhhh....... Fox’s looks around. Fox: Uhhhhh ummmmmmmm........ Clyde continues to search around for a chair. There’s still nothing. No magic chairs appear this time. Fox: Yeahhhhh..... uhhhhhhhh.... Clyde awkwardly throws a thigh over GBJ’s desk, pushing some of the cluttered mess to the floor. Fox: Wait, that’s uncomfy. Clyde stands back up. Fox: But being comfy is being a conformist. And I’m totally not that. Clyde sits back down making the toilet face. Fox: I think I’m good here. GBJ: Alright... well, if you're such a non-conformist. How about you sit on my throne, and I sit on your lap, and I'll tell you all about the holiday I celebrate while everyone else is celebrating Christmas. It's called Saturnalia! Fox’s eyebrows twitch. Fox: What in powdered sugar is that!? GBJ rises from his throne and motions for Clyde to come on over. Fox: Will this influence my chances to be crowned United States Champ? GBJ: It just might. See, Saturnalia started out as a Roman holiday or something, and people threw kickass parties in honor of Saturn, the God of Agriculture, in hopes for a successful harvest in the upcoming year. That's where Christmas got the tree idea from and a lot of ideas, too. There were singing mummies and dancing and everything. It was way better than Christmas. Garbage Bag motions harder towards the chair. GBJ: Come on, take a seat, and I'll sit on your lap so you can tell me all about what you want for Saturnalia... if you promise to be a good boy, I'm sure you'll get what you want. A new video game system perhaps? Fox: A new video game system? What are you implying? That I took my systems out in my side yard and smashed them? And then proceeded to post the photos on Facebook? Anyways, that’s beside the point. I don’t need presents for Christmas anymore. I’ve grown up. I’m beyond that. Fox pauses and smiles as he reflects inside on the big boy words he just used. Fox: The only thing I need is my United States Title. And... just like the rest of the people in Cleveland, you don’t seem to be so accommodating. GBJ doesn’t look too impressed. Fox: I won’t promise to be a good boy. Because I’m not a good fucking boy. Get it through your head. The old Clyde Fox is dead. Fuck Saturn Claus or whatever the hell you are. And if this is the only way I can prove it, then feast your eyes on this. Clyde pulls out a Miami Heat LeBron James jersey from behind his back. He slings it over his shoulder as he proceeds to pull out a small amount of what is assumed to be baby powder from his pocket. GBJ: Is that... baby powder... in your pocket? Clyde doesn’t bother to respond, but instead, he pats his hands together and throws the baby powder up in the air. Fox: Fuck Cleveland. Fuck Saturn Claus. Fuck your United States Title. And fuck you Garbage Bag Johnny! Clyde Fox storms out of GBJ's office, knocking over one of the mummies on his way out. As he slams the door, the Commissioner sadly shakes his head and sighs, holding Clyde's manila envelope. GBJ: Looks like this one's going in the naughty file. ---- Introduction The lights dim in the Quicken Loans Arena and the Northern Ohio crowd begins to buzz.
