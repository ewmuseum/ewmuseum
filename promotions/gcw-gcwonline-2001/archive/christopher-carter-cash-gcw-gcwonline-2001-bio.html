Christopher Carter Cash Biography Real Name Christopher Carter Cash Nicknames The Man in Black, The Redneck Rocker, Triple C Hometown Nashville Tennessee Birthday 01/04/1985 Height 6'0 Weight 230 lbs Biography Chris Cash is the grandson of the legendary country singers, Johnny and June Carter Cash. He take after his Grandfather in almost every way. His style of clothes, singing style, religion, and the ability to get into trouble. Also, like his Grandfather, Chris considers himself to be a multi-facceted entertainer. A singer, songwriter, and wrestler. He is very arrogant about his abilities. While he could have gone into the music business, following his Grandparents, and Aunt Rosanne, Chris decided that wrestling would be more of a challenge for him. Chris Cash is out to prove that he isn't your typical Rocker/Wrestler combo...no, he is better than all the rest. Other Feds Powers of Wrestling, Flaming Pig Wrestling: Slaughterhouse Other Fed Titles PoW Tag titles (twice) FPW: Slaughterhouse World Champion. (first, last and only) Strengths very ring savvy Weaknesses Arrogant, would rather boast/taunt his opponent than get the win Wrestling Style Technical, submission Strategy Won't hesitate to cheat to win, although he doesn't need to being a sound technical wrestler. Izak often assists if possible/needed. Finisher Move "Root of Evil" Finisher Description Rear naked choke, starts standing and then on the ground with leg scissors Other Moves "Ring of Fire" (Rings of Saturn) "Leg-acy" (Flipping guillotine Legdrop) "Dropping The F-Bomb" (Swanton from Top rope) "Cash-Flow" (Flowing DDT) "ReMake" (Mimics one of his opponents moves/taunts) "Train A-Comin" (Corner high knee followed by a bulldog) "Cash Talks, BS Walks" (Ric Flair strut) Ring Entrances We hear the words, "Hello, I'm Chris Cash" come across the loud speakers. As soon as that is done, Johnny Cash's song, "Country Trash" plays. Chris Cash walks out, with his bodyguard, Izak lurking to the left behind him. The duo poses on the stage for a moment, as the crowd boos before continuing. Cash has a headset microphone on, and sings his music live as he walks down to the ring, much to the disgust of the fans. Izak contiunes on behind him, glaring at any fans that might cause Cash trouble as he sings. Chris Cash enters the ring, sets down the microphone, and gets ready for the match, along with Izak.
