WorldWide 140 Scottrade Center — St. Louis, Missouri ---- WW140: Devil's Night Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny's office is decked out for Halloween: orange and black streamers, fake cobwebs, cardboard cutouts of witches, vampires, and an assortment of hokey cartoon depictions of some of history's most storied monsters. He sits at his desk, picking at a bowl of fun sized candy bars, and he's decked out for the occasion as well, in full costume as Honest Abe. GBJ: I always wondered why they called these tiny candy bars fun sized when they're the least fun size available in bar form. I guess some mysteries remain unsolved. A knock at the door disturbs the commissioner's contemplative snacking, and he hurries to answer it, bringing the bowl of candies with him. Johnny swings open the door to reveal the hulking frame of Bryan Mayhem, who does not say "Trick or Treat." GBJ: Sweet costume, man. You've earned yourself your pick of treats! The commissioner extends the bowl of candy to Bryan Mayhem. Mayhem stands straight up his arms folded. He looks down into the bowl than back at GBJ. GBJ looks back at him than back at the bowl. He pulls it away and turns back towards his desk. Mayhem: I have come here to let you know to cut the crap with this trick or treating and costume garbage. Devil’s Night is MY night. I have buried men alive here, I have ended careers on this show. I don’t find this night humorous one bit. GBJ: Easy there, killer. I get it. You don't have a sweet tooth. Garbage Bag withdraws the offer for candy, replacing the bowl on his desk. GBJ: Anyway, if you came here to pay me back for the emergency crew I had to call in to fix the ring you broke last week, it's on me. Watching ol' Frosty crap himself was more than worth it. Mayhem’s eyes are wide with shock. His fists clench and his teeth begin to grind. Mayhem: I’m not sure if the pot has went to your brain, but this is not a goddamn joke. Don’t you understand what this night is to me? GBJ: Of course, man. I know what you're all about. 2008--you put Octavian in the ground, man. A bona-fide GCW legend. No victims tonight, but I guess you're saving it for Frost at Dangerous Games, huh? Mayhem: I guess you’ll just have to wait and see. All I am saying is that I won’t be held accountable for what transpires. Mayhem gives GBJ one last glare and walks out of his office. The Commissioner takes off his Lincolnesque top hat and scratches his head. GBJ: Note to self...find out what kind of food Mayhem likes and get that next year. ---- Introduction The lights dim in the Scottrade Center and the Missouri crowd begins to buzz.
