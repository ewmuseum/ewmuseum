'Psychotic' Bryan Marshall Biography Real Name Bryan Marshall Nicknames The Mad Man Hometown Providence, RI Birthday 04/25/75 Height 6'0' Weight 240 Biography Bryan Marshall is as crazy as they come. He once used to be a man that would look into the mirror every five to ten minutes. But during his first stint as TV champion in the UWF he was severely injured. He was on the wrong end of a piledriver. Marshall couldn't feel his legs for a week and it took hims months to even be able to move out of bed. Marshall came back to the UWF and worked twice as hard. His recovery was unbelieveable and he was destined for a title shot. When his World Title shot came he was screwed out of the title by the same man that put him on the shelf. Marshall was forced to watch as the owner of the company paraded around with the man that nearly killed him with his world title. Marshall snapped. Since then Marshall has been a few french fries short of a happy meal. He enters through the crowd and the whites of his eyes can be seen from a mil away. Marshall does whatever it takes to win a match. By hook of by crook. Marshall comes to the GCW looking to start off his reign by capturing the TV title.
