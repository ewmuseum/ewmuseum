Jason Warr Biography Real Name Jason Warr Nicknames The Warr Machine, Warr Hammer, Peacemaker Hometown Albany, NY Birthday 03/03/83 Height 6'1' Weight 225 Fed Titles GCW Television Champion Fed Awards GCW King of Kings. In turn > PRIME's. Biography HSW, AWC, and Warr. It was a relationship headed straight for.. something that ends relationships. 'I hate his guts' said one HSW Representaive who chose to remain nameless. 'He Signs, wins, and when his contract his up he dissapears into the abyss? FUCK JASON WARR.' 'I'd hire him back,' said an AWC Delegate. 'Just so I could fire him the next morning.' After his first AWC Loss in Mid-January, Warr dissapeared into New Yorks Underground wrestling scene. Touring with various small time promotions, and the entire time losing his technical background in favor of a hardcore based style. No one knows why he chose to dump his lucrative contract to work for peanuts and spill pints of blood at a time. Will you find out? Duh, why the hell would I be foreshadowing..? Other Feds I sort of just sat around. Other Fed Awards Right, the sitting. Wrestling Style Innovation, Extremist. Finisher Move World Warr III Finisher Description Spinning Throwdown Piledriver Finisher Setup Move KICK. TO. MIDSECTION~! Setup Description Even Ben ain't that Retarded. Other Moves Strikes: Quick Punches(Fast paced, not much power.), Leg Kicks, Knee strikes. High Spots: Any sort of Springboard, Hurricanrana's, Moonsaults, If it can be done off the top rope, Warr will do it. Submissions: Warr is not a master technician when it comes to submissions, honestly. He knows a few, Triangle Choke, Guillotine Choke, Rear Naked Choke, but thats about it. Won't pull off a submission in a normal match, so when he does try and submit an opponent it can be considered a finisher, not a match break. Ground Moves: Not comfortable on the ground at all. His entire personality defies being stuck, hence it's his weakness. Unlike most character mine DOES have a weakness, and being trapped in a submission or something on the mat spells doom. Grapples: He's strong for his size, but obviously has his limits. If he's with someone under 300 Pounds he can hit Powerslams and other assorted Bombs and Drivers. Innovation is the secret. Entrance Music Manowar: 'Hail and Kill'
