WorldWide 10, 27 April 2005

WorldWide 10 The O2 — London, England ---- WorldWide 10 The lights dim in the arena, and the Amsterdam crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on reading “Emporer Octavian” and “Jackass!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Amsterdam, Holland” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue. Bryan- We’re live here in beautiful Amsterdam as GCW’s tour of Europe continues! We’re on the Road to Warpath as GCW approaches its first Pay-Per-View extravaganza! Hello again, everyone, I’m James Bryan, and I’m all by myself here tonight at WorldWide! As I’m sure you’re all aware, my former broadcast colleague Mr. Danger left the announce table last week in the evening’s main event, and much to this announcer’s surprise made an alliance with Octavian and his band of thugs! The shot heads to a flashback of last week, showing Mr. Danger’s entrance into the ring. It then rapidly cuts to Mr. Danger nailing The Jhub with the Dangerous Drop, Octavian pinning Jason Dread, and Octavian, Hanson, Alex Blaq and Danger celebrating their victory in the ring. Bryan- This is definitely a major development as Octavian continues his quest to become GCW World Heavyweight Champion! The Jhub and Luke Hanson are having their problems, and in a story that broke after we went off the air, Alex Blaq and LeStatt Knight came to blows in a backstage confrontation! Things are really getting intense here in GCW! Most importantly, however, it seems US Champion LeStatt Knight has chosen his side, and he helped The Jhub and Jason Dread in their match last week. Hopefully tonight we’ll get more answers as we continue the Road to Warpath! The shot heads backstage, where GCW Interviewer Kevin Jacobs is slowly trudging through the halls. Ring crew workers and producers scramble about wildly through the plain white corridor, as Jacobs’ eyes dart to and fro, obviously searching for something. The camera backs away in front of Jacobs as he walks down the hall, before finally reaching a black door. Jacobs pauses and finally pushes his way inside, where the camera follows. Seated behind a desk in this plush makeshift office is President Steven Caldera, writing furiously. His black suit jacket is hung over a coat rack at the front of the room, his tie secured neatly around his neck. Caldera looks up at Jacobs and smiles, as Jacobs sighs loudly. Jacobs- Still can’t find him, boss. Caldera- Really? I was told LeStatt got here a few hours ago. The crowd cheers wildly at the mention of the name of the GCW United States Champion. Jacobs- Nope, he’s nowhere to be found. I guess I can’t get that interview you wanted. Caldera- Don’t sweat it, KJ. If he doesn’t want to be interviewed, you won’t find him. Why don’t you go find Mike instead? Jacobs- Hardy? Sure… I saw him talking to Michael Stevens a few minutes ago. I’ll go- Caldera cuts him off abruptly, dropping his pen. Caldera- Stevens? Jacobs, suddenly concerned, backs towards the wall. Caldera wheels himself backward and around the desk, revealing his black and silver wheelchair. Both knees bulge under his dress pants from heavy swelling, obviously still recovering from surgery. Caldera- What’s he doing talking to Stevens? Jacobs- He’s… they’ve been spending quite a bit of time together lately, boss. And they’re teaming together in a tag match tonight. I thought you knew. Caldera- I’ve been stuck in this damn office for weeks! I can’t walk… Nobody tells me anything. And who the hell made that match? I sure as hell didn’t. What the hell is he up to? Jacobs- I really don’t know, boss. I’ll go get him. Caldera- You do that. Jacobs quickly slips out of the room, leaving a perturbed Caldera alone in the room to ponder this new information… Johnathan Borealis vs. Damien Banks New York, New York cues up and Damien Banks enters to a mild reaction from the Dutch crowd. Banks slides into the ring and waits as the arena hushes. A guitar can be heard over the PA, starting out loud and distorted but fading after a few seconds. The guitar almost fades out as it hits once, twice...three more times. The stage explodes in a mix of fireworks, noise, and pure adrenaline as "Rabble Rouser" by KMFDM kicks in full. Johnathan Borealis emerges on stage before an awed crowd and heads for the ring. Borealis slides in and Banks catches him immediately with a hard clubbing forearm to the back. The bell sounds and Banks whips Borealis into the ropes. Borealis bounces back and answers with an impressive spinning heel kick to the face! Banks falls and bounces off the middle rope back to his feet. Borealis instantly leaps and nails a high standing dropkick to the face, sending Banks over the top rope to the floor! Borealis moves to pursue Banks, but official Tim Vale cuts him off. As Borealis argues with the official, Banks slips back into the ring behind him. Banks moves in, but Borealis senses his presence and spins on his heel, nailing Banks in the head with a roundhouse kick! Banks is staggered, and Borealis begins pummeling him with fast kicks to the midsection. Borealis backs Banks into the ropes and sends him to the other side. Banks bounces back and Borealis sends him high into the air with a quick press, catching him on the way back down with a falling DDT! The crowd gasps as Banks painfully rolls onto his back, and Borealis makes the cover! 1… 2… Banks kicks out! Borealis pulls Banks back to his feet, staying on him. Borealis kicks Banks in the gut and sets him up for a piledriver! Banks pulls Banks up and spikes him to the mat, driving his head into the canvas. The crowd gasps again as Borealis gets to his feet and hops onto the turnbuckle, raising his middle fingers in the general direction of the crowd. The crowd, shocked, actually laughs as Borealis hops down onto the mat. Banks is waiting with an Irish whip, but a surprised Borealis counters. Banks bounces back and Borealis catches him up on his shoulders, spinning him down into a tilt-a-whirl piledriver! He perfectly executes the move he calls Euthanasia and Banks in out. Borealis makes a cover! 1… 2… 3! Borealis wins convincingly in his debut! Winner: Johnathan Borealis Borealis slides out of the ring as his music cues up, raising his arms confidently into the air. He mostly ignores the crowd as he swaggers back up the ramp, leaving Banks motionless in the ring. Vale checks on Banks and motions to the back, calling for aid. After a few moments a pair of medics pushing a stretcher charge down the ramp, and the show fades to a commercial break as they haul Banks out of the ring. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The arena back doors open and Johnny Lexicon steps inside, he stops and waits for a moment before a gorgeous looking blond woman follows him in and hooks her arm in his. Together they walk down the hall for a moment before Johnny says a few words and heads for his locker room. The blond haired woman takes her purse in hand and walks the opposite direction. The camera pans to follow her progress as she steps up behind a familiar looking man at the candy bar vending machine. “You must be new.” The man says. With a smile the woman extends her hand to him. “Hi handsome, I'm Eva.” “Trey.” They shake hands and the scene fades. The shot fades back into the hallway, where a rather stressed out looking Kevin Jacobs is still walking briskly down the passageway. Busy workers still shoot from side to side to avoid the chubby Jacobs, who frequently raises his shirt sleeve to his face to wipe the sweat from his brow. A man with long black hair passes by, and Jacobs lights up. He stops and squints towards the man, thinking it might be LeStatt, but finally shakes his head and moves on. The first step he takes is halted by a collision with someone moving the other way. The camera moves forward to see The Jhub, now covered in the coffee he was drinking. Angry and steaming (literally), The Jhub sneers at Jacobs. The crowd explodes as they see him on the MegaTron, as Jacobs tries to talk his way out of a beating. Jacobs- Sorry, Jhub, didn’t see you there… Jhub- Whatever, that’s fine, fat ass. Jhub steps forward to move on, but Jacobs cuts him off. Jacobs- Have you seen LeStatt Knight by any chance, Jhub? Jhub- No, as a matter of fact, I haven’t. Not since he did the right thing and helped us out last week. He didn’t exactly finish the job, but hey, we can’t all have the basketball sized testicles of The Jhub, now can we? Jhub moves to leave again, but again Jacobs steps in his way. Jacobs- How about Mike Hardy? Where’s he? Jhub- Damnit, I’m not getting paid to be your little helper. Last I heard, Hardy was in his locker room, probably cruising down the Hershey Highway with Michael Stevens, if you know what I mean… The crowd laughs as Jacobs is taken aback. Jacobs- So you know about that too, eh? Jhub- Of course I do. Everyone knows but Caldera, it seems. I’d go tell my little buddy to stay the hell away from Spitfire, but I’ve got my own business to attend to. Seems I’ve got a seven foot sack of trash to dispose of, and he’s in the evening’s main event. He seems to think he’s the biggest man in GCW, but frankly I just don’t understand what he’s talking about. I used to share a dressing room with this guy. The guy is hung like a housefly. The crowd bursts out in laughter, as Jacobs snickers to himself. Jacobs- Uh, Jhub, I think Luke’s talking about his physical size… You know, he’s tall? The Jhub considers this, scratching the goatee on his chin. Jhub- Oh. Yeah, I guess he’s kinda tall. The Jhub shrugs and moves on, finally pushing past Jacobs. Jacobs simply shakes his head and pushes on, continuing his errands for the boss. Twizzy vs. Johnny Lexicon The scene moves back to ring side where Johnny Lexicon's music is blasting out of the speakers and he's mounted the middle turn buckle to flex and pose. “Life's Sweet Drug” by Slash's Snakepit starts to play and Johnny gets to the middle of the ring. Twisted appears from the back to a thunderous chorus of applause, with a confident walk he heads down to the ring. The ref checks them and signals for the bell and this matchup is underway. They charge to center of the ring and lockup, Johnny overpowers Twizzy and tosses him against the rope. Hard shoulder block. Follows with a chop, and another, throws him to the ropes again and lowers his head to scoop Twizzy up. Twisted hits him with a right boot and bounces off the ropes again for a leaping missile dropkick. They move to lock up. Twisted reverses into a standing side arm bar, Lexicon reverses into a hip toss take down, Twisted reverses into a monkey flip, Johnny goes down hard but holds onto Twisted's hands. Folding back up and over he straddles the Junkies chest and hammers down with a few hard rights before Twizzy hooks a boot on a shoulder and pulls him off. Both men take a second to get to their feet, but Lex doesn't give Twisted long before charging and hammering him with a fist, and another. Twisted goes into the corner and Johnny hammers him again. Twisted answers with a trio of shots to the gut and an uppercut that sends Lex stumbling back. Twisted throws him to the ground with a shoulder block and grabs his legs. He hooks for a Boston crab but Johnny pulls him down and gives him a boot to the mouth. Johnny scrambles to his feet and hits Twisted with a Star Struck clothesline out of nowhere!! Johnny holds onto the momentum by springing to the top rope and launching a huge moonsault!! The first pin of the match is broken at a 2 count. Lex pulls Twisted to his feet and chops his chest, throws him into the ropes and scoop slams him. Johnny goes to capitalize but Twisted with a drop toe hold. Both men are up, they lock and Johnny overpowers but Twisted turns it into a lariat and sends Lex down, he stomps a few times. Trey Tremendous is at ringside... Trey Tremendous is at ringside? Twizzy backs into the corner and waits for Lexicon to rise. Twizzy charges in but Lexicon explodes upward with a high leg clothesline! Twizzy goes down as Lexicon notices Tremendous. Lexicon glares at Tremendous suspiciously, as Tremendous just grins back. Lexicon shouts something at Tremendous, and turns right into a massive Pill Popper from Twizzy! Twizzy, confused at the presence of Tremendous, covers Lexicon! The ref gives it the three count and this match is over. Johnny fights to get to his knees as the ref raises Twisted's hand in a snap victory. Trey backs up the ramp with a finger pointed at Lex. Winner: Twizzy ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Kevin Jacobs enters the locker room area, hoping to find Hardy and Stevens somewhere in the dressing rooms. He begins to hear crashes and rumbling noises coming from down the hall, and soon finds their source to be behind a door marked "The Jhub." Confused, as he’s just seen The Jhub in the halls moments earlier, Jacobs approaches the door. Jacobs knocks a few times and, receiving no answer, enters on his own accord. The room is a mess. A table has been smashed to pieces, all of which have been pushed together in a square-like pattern. The Jhub's personal effects have been thrown on top of the kindling: a few shirts that are surely worth have of Jacobs's yearly salary, a suspiciously empty wallet, a copy of the Poop Wars Volume 3: The Shit REALLY Hits The Fan DVD, and, most importantly, Jhub's own little black book. Ouch. The camera pans back and encompasses Luke Hanson's hulking frame, clad in his customary jeans and Docs, as well as a fireman's jacket and helmet, squirting lighter fluid onto the small pile. He smiles down at Jacobs upon noticing his arrival. Hanson- Hey, KJ! How's business? Give me just a second and we can go talk out in the hall. Hanson backs away, pulls a Zippo from his pocket, fires it up, and tosses it onto the pile, which immediately flames up. Hanson escorts a gaping Jacobs to the hall, closing the door behind them. As soon as the doors close, the fire alarm can be heard from within the room, and the sprinkler system sounds like it’s soaking what’s left of The Jhub’s belongings. Hanson- Alrite, Kev, what can I do for you? Whatcha wanna know? Jacobs stammers a bit before getting his words out. Jacobs- I--uh--how long are you going to let that burn? Hanson- Let what burn, Kev? It's just garbage, nothing of value...to me. We'll get someone on it before it burns the building down or does something of an equally catastrophic nature. I just want Jhub to acknowledge that I made a challenge to him. He doesn't even have to accept. Just acknowledge that I made it. If he'd done so before now, I wouldn't have done this. Like Smoky the Bear says, only Jhub can prevent forest fires. Next? Jacobs- There's smoke coming from under the door, and I think the walls are about to-- Hanson- Kev, my friend, you worry far too much. Now, granted, if you play with fire, you're going to get burned...as LeStatt Knight will become all too aware of tonight. He's got a solid history in regards to taking me down, but never just man-to-man. There's always been something or someone else that gets in the way of it being between the two of us. Tonight, I think he's going to find out--rather abruptly--that I am not to be taken lightly. Maybe I won't take the belt from him, but I'm going to beat him--mark my words. Now then, I'm starting to get a little bit warm. What's say we get out of here? Hanson starts to walk off, alerting a few security staff of the small blaze occurring in the Jhub's dressing room. Jacobs begins to cough from the smoke he's inhaled as security rush to the Jhub's locker room and the scene fades. Jordan- Hey Johnny. His voice precedes three knocks at the door of Lexicon's room. Cigarette lit, Jordan White waits beside of the door, kicking his fur coat around at his ankles. Jordan- I know you're in there. He knocks again with more force and shakes his head. Jordan- Bastard. Johnny- Who? Lexicon approaches from off-screen, holding a water bottle and pulling the keys to his locker room out of his pocket as he talks to Jordan. Jordan- Not you, for once. Trey. Johnny- What's the kid doing? Jordan- Maybe that girl you brought in here earlier. Hard to tell, but their eyes were definitely caught in a dreamy--dare I say, faraway--stare near the tech room about five minutes ago. The door swings open and Lexicon turns his head, raising an eyebrow. Jordan smirks. Johnny- And why are you telling me? You're supposed to be his manager. Jordan- So? There's some stuff you figure a guy wants to know. This made the list, I guess. Johnny- You serious about this? Jordan- Bottom of my hear. They stare for a moment--Jordan's face a defiant grin in light of the determination that scars Lexicon's grimace. Johnny- I hope you're not right, Jordan. Jordan- That'd be a first. Johnny stomps away from his locker, letting the door close on Jordan's foot. He looks around, then disappears into the room. The shot then heads to the locker room area, where Jacobs is standing down the hall from The Jhub’s torched locker room. Leather-suited firemen walk in and out as Jacobs waits patiently with his back to the action, standing in front of a door labeled “Michael Stevens.” The crowd boos as they read the sign, and after a moment the door opens. Michael Stevens emerges from the room, followed closely by a smug looking Mike Hardy. Stevens sneers as he sees Jacobs lying in wait, and rolls his eyes. Stevens- Oh, great, what do you want? Jacobs- Mike, Caldera wants to see you. Stevens- We don’t have time for him. We’ve got a match to wrestle. Stevens turns back to Hardy, and begins whispering to Hardy. For his part, Hardy does not look to be overly happy about his situation, but is trying to keep a happy face. Jacobs stammers and tries to interject, as The Jhub appears far in the background. The Jhub takes a sip of a fresh cup of coffee, but promptly spits it out all over the floor as he sees the smoldering remains of his locker room. Jacobs- Guys, what’s going on here? Really, Mr. Stevens, I think the world wants to know what your interest in Mike Hardy is. Stevens angrily spins around, looking at Hardy with a raised eyebrow. In the background, The Jhub can be seen arguing with a fireman, and he promptly throws his cup across the hallway. Hardy- You want to know what’s going on? I’m training to become World Champion, that’s what. Steven’s laid up right now, he can’t help me out. Spitty here has offered to help out while Caldera’s recovering from surgery. Simple as that. Stevens- You see, Kevin? I don’t mean any harm. I’m just trying to help Mike here realize his full potential, go that extra mile, aim for the stars, and any other awful generic inspirational clichés you can think of. Hardy- Frankly, Spitty, I don’t know how the hell this match was made tonight, I sure as hell didn’t see it coming. But Kevin, I’m in a match and I’m not about to lose it. I got swindled last week, and when I find out just what the hell was going on there will be hell to pay. But for now, I have a match to win. So if you’ll excuse us… Hardy pushes past Jacobs and turns down a hallway away from the chaos emanating from The Jhub’s locker room. The Jhub has his arms in the air, now berating a security official. His shouts occasionally grow loud enough for the camera to pick up, as Stevens sneers at Jacobs. Jhub- …poop wars… Stevens- Listen, Jacobs, don’t go sticking your nose in my business. If you cross me like this again, you’ll be fighting off a horny gay midget before you can say “Curiously Bruce.” Jhub- …sprinkler… Stevens pushes past Jacobs and follows him down the hall, while Jacobs simply shakes his head. The Jhub motions towards his smoking locker room and then towards his crotch, while his last words are picked up as the show fades to commercial… Jhub- …Penis envy! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Michael Stevens & Mike Hardy vs. Allied Chaos Allied Chaos come to the ring to a strong mixed reaction from the crowd. They slide into the ring and pose for the crowd, until “The Hand That Feeds” cues up. Mike Hardy emerges on stage to an absolute explosion of cheers, and stops on stage with a forced grin. “I Want to Conquer The World” hits next, and boos rain down upon Michael Stevens as he struts out to take a spot next to Hardy. The two charge down the ramp together and hit the ring, and the bell sounds. Hardy meets Kyle with hard forearms to the head, while Stevens knocks Connor down with a hard lariat. Stevens shouts something to Hardy and they whip Kyle into the ropes. Kyle is met with a double back elbow from Stevens and Hardy, and they then drop a pair of elbows into Kyle’ sternum. Official Hal Jenkins stands aside and watches as Connor gets back to his feet and knocks down Stevens from behind with an axe handle. Stevens rolls out of the ring, as does Kyle, leaving Connor and Hardy alone. Hardy nails Connor with a kick to the gut and slaps on a side headlock, which Connor instantly counters into a back suplex. Hardy nimbly nips up and beats Connor to his feet, waiting with a hard knife-edge chop. Connor staggers into his corner and tags in a waiting Kyle, while Hardy glances to Stevens, who has finally made it to their corner. Stevens extends his hand for the tag, but Hardy ignores him and locks up with Kyle. Hardy slips behind Kyle and abruptly rolls him up into a school boy. Kyle kicks out at one, and meets Hardy with a hard standing dropkick. Hardy painfully gets up and Kyle drops him into a snapmare out of the Mambo Kings’ corner, where he tags in Connor. Connor climbs back in and places a boot on Hardy’s back, holding him up, while Kyle springs off the second turnbuckle and flips over Hardy’s back, catching his neck and wrenching him in half on the way down ala Mr. Perfect! The crowd oohs as Connor powers Hardy back up and places him seated on his shoulders, dropping him into an electric chair drop! But Hardy flows through and spins Connor into a pinning predicament! Connor powers out quickly but Hardy rolls back towards Stevens. Stevens again extends his arm for the tag, but again Hardy turns away and ignores him. Connor charges Hardy with a clothesline but misses, and finds himself in front of Stevens. Stevens takes a swing at Connor, but the big man blocks the shot and nails Stevens with a headbutt to the face! Stevens drops off the apron to the floor, while Hardy flies in with a leaping forearm shot to the skull! Connor collapses into the corner and Hardy sprints to the opposite turnbuckle. He grins smugly at Kyle and charges, but Kyle reaches into the ring and grabs Hardy’s hair, jerking him to the mat! The crowd boos as Jenkins continues to watch idly. Hardy painfully gets up as Kyle holds a chunk of Hardy’s hair in the air proudly. Connor explodes out of the corner and drops a rising Hardy with a hard lariat. Connor pulls Hardy back up and tags in Kyle. Kyle climbs to the top rope as Connor holds Hardy up. Kyle takes off with a missile dropkick, but Hardy ducks and Kyle takes Connor down! The crowd cheers as Hardy springs to his feet, having caught a second wind. Kyle stomps his foot on the mat in frustration, as Hardy hooks him from behind and takes him over into the Waterfall! Kyle nimbly counters and lands on his feet, but Hardy drops him with a double leg takedown, and launches him into the air with a slingshot! Kyle flies into Hardy’s corner, where Stevens is waiting with a steel chair! Stevens NAILS Kyle in the skull, cutting him out of the air. The crowd gasps as Hardy, kicking Connor out of the ring, apparently didn’t notice the shot. Hardy turns and sees Kyle lying on the mat, and Stevens back down on the floor. Hardy shrugs and climbs to the top rope, measuring Kyle! Motionless, Kyle is a sitting duck for a perfect shooting star press from Hardy! Jenkins makes the count… One, two, three, Kyle isn’t going anywhere. Winners: Mike Hardy and Michael “Spitfire” Stevens Hardy raises his arms into the air, but seems confused as to the lukewarm reaction his victory is receiving. Stevens climbs into the ring and raises Hardy’s arm, and the crowd begins to boo mightily. Suspicious, Hardy snatches his arm away and seems to be accusing Stevens of wrongdoing. Stevens shrugs and smiles, happy that his team has won. Hardy shrugs and smiles, also apparently satisfied to be back to his winning ways. Stevens raises Hardy’s arm again, and the two celebrate as the show heads backstage again. The door to the locker room flies open and Trey starts immediately pulling his pants up around his waist. “What's this?” Lexicon thunders and storms towards Trey without hesitation. Quickly fixing her dress and scrambling out of the way, Eva heads for the door as Lexicon charges Trey. Before he can defend himself Johnny grabs Trey and throws him into a locker hard. He sends a swift boot to his gut and then pulls him back up by his head. The scene switches to the hallway where Trey comes flying out of his locker room and hits the opposite wall hard and goes to his knees. Eva backpedals away as Johnny storms out after him and drops a huge fist across his back and then sends a sharp kick into Trey's side. “You ever touch what's mine again Trey and I'll make this look like a tickling contest.” He finishes the statement with a slap across Trey's head and turns to Eva. “Johnny I...” She is unable to finish as he takes her arm roughly and leads her off camera. The scene zooms into Trey's face as he gets back to his knees and touches at the blood leaking from his lip. With a sneer he slams his fist into the floor and starts getting to his feet. The shot then heads back to ringside, where James Bryan sits alone. Bryan- Well, fans, we’ve had a rather eventful night so far, and it seems Spitfire’s manipulation of Mike Hardy is continuing. You might notice I’m still out here all alone, and it seems they haven’t quite found a replacement for Mr. Danger yet. If anyone out there wants a job, just drop us a line… I sure could use the help. The show heads backstage again, where Steven Caldera sits in his wheelchair with his arms crossed over his chest. His tie is gone and his collar unbuttoned, his face grim as he watches a replay of the Hardy/Stevens tag victory on his small TV monitor. After a few moments, a sweating Mike Hardy springs through the door, a large smile on his face. Hardy- That’s how to do that! Did you see me? Hardy glides across the ring and seats himself on Caldera’s desk, pleased to see himself on the monitor. Caldera angrily pauses the TV and wheels around to look Hardy in the eye. Caldera- What the hell do you think you’re doing? Hardy- …Sitting? Caldera- No, what are you doing with him? Damnit, Mike, no good can come of this. Hardy snickers, unsure of how to take this. Hardy- Come on, boss, it’s just while you’re laid up. Before long your leg will heal up and we’ll be back to kicking ass together again. But in the meantime, Spitty’s teaching me some good stuff. I lost last week without his help, and this week I won with him in my corner. And he didn’t even do anything. Caldera- Didn’t he? Caldera hits play on his remote control, and the replay flickers to life. The voice of James Bryan calling the action fades into the background, as Hardy watches the closing moments of the match. He sees Stevens nailing Kyle with the chair, laying him out cold while the official worked on Hardy. Caldera hits the button again and the TV clicks off, leaving Hardy to state blankly at the screen. Caldera- He’s no good, Mike. Michael Stevens is the most manipulative guy I’ve ever met. He’s a talented wrestler but he’s only in it for himself. You’ve got to see that. I don’t know what his agenda is, but I promise you it can’t be good. You saw for yourself; Stevens cheated to help you win. Hardy- Well… Is that so bad? I mean, we won, didn’t we? And nobody said anything. We were standing, they weren’t. Did he really do anything wrong? Caldera, shocked, angrily glares at Hardy. Caldera- Yes, he did something wrong! He cheated! That’s not what a true champion does, Mike. You want to be the real deal? You play by the rules. You want to really go down as a legend? You have to win fairly. Michael Stevens isn’t going to help you do that. He’ll help you win, but it’ll be empty. Winning that way doesn’t mean a thing. You want to play it that way? Fine. But I promise you Mike Hardy the cheater won’t ever… EVER… have the spotlight in my company. Hardy- What’s wrong with you? You used to be the best of the cheaters! You used to change the rules as you went along to get your way! You helped me realize I could be great, Steven, but Spitty has shown me that there’s more than one way to play this game. I’m sorry if I’m too progressive for you, but I simply don’t think I’m doing anything wrong. Caldera- Mike, this isn’t being progressive, it’s being stupid! Stevens is just going to keep leading you down the wrong path, and when he finally runs out of uses for you and he’s accomplished whatever the hell he’s trying to accomplish, he’ll turn on you at the drop of a hat. Hardy- Hey man, I don’t need this advice from you. If you don’t want to be a part of the hottest commodity in GCW any more, fine with me. But don’t let me catch you crawling back to me when I win that GCW Title. It’s gonna be mine before long, Steven, and then you’ll see you were wrong to hold me back. I’m going to do whatever it takes to become as powerful as I can. And it seems clear now that you can’t help me do that. With that, Hardy storms out of the room and slams the heavy steel door behind him. Caldera winces at the loud clang and sits back in his wheelchair. Slowly, his eyes facing the floor, he wheels himself back behind his desk and resumes his paperwork. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ We return from commercial to see many signs being waved in the Arena. “Amsterdam loves the Jhub,” “Dread=True Champion,” and “Mr. Danger is a Sellout.” The crowd awaits in anticipation for the World Champion to emerge from backstage. However, they get exactly the opposite. “Might of Rome” cues up to a chorus of boos from the audience. The fans are on their feet throwing empty water bottles at the most dominant Champion in GCW history. He is dressed in an Armani black suit, his hair is slicked back, and he has on his trademark Gucci sunglasses, and the Imperial Roman Championship draped over his shoulder. His bulging biceps appear to make his suit a little tight. He has an evil smirk on his face as he struts to the ring. Following right behind him clapping his hands together is Danger. He is also wearing a black Armani suit, and the duo looks better than ever. Danger climbs the steps to the ring, and holds the ropes open as Octavian goes under the ropes and grabs a mic. The boos are almost deafening. Danger- Give it up, for the Greatest GCW Champion in HISTORY!!! The crowd gets even more riled up, and Danger just laughs and high-fives Octavian. Danger- Everywhere I've gone all week, people are asking me.. Why did you do it Mr. Danger? WHY? Well, I'll tell you why. I got sick and tired of sitting behind that commentators booth, having everyone think I'm just another old washed up has been.
