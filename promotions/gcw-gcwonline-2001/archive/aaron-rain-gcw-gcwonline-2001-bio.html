Aaron Rain Biography Real Name Aaron Rain Nicknames The Saint Hometown Calgary, Alberta, Canada Birthday March 31st, 1980 Height 5'10' Weight 220 Other Feds Metal-Tanc Wrestling (United Kingdom), Latino Wrestling Organization (Central/South America), Worldwide Entertainment Wrestling Other Fed Awards Various titles in each. Wrestling Style Super Junior (technical and flying), though there's experience in hardcore Finisher Move Arm Capture Hangman's Clutch Finisher Description three-quater chancery/crossface combination submission Finisher Setup Move no particular set up Other Moves Aerial Rain's initial style, more reminiscent of the typical United States Cruiserweight that anything else. Has since dialed down the high flying moves in favor of a more technical style, he occasionally pulls one of these out in dire circumstances. - Shooting Star Press - Bloodshed: Springboard Front Flip Legdrop - Asai Moonsault - Plancha - Diving Hurricanrana - Diving Elbow Drop - Gift From Above: Frog Splash Submission Moves aquired by studying tapes of the great technicians of the past. Rain is always looking for new ways to apply these as quickly as he can. - Shapshooter - The Welt: Full Nelson/Body Scissors combination - Texas Cloverleaf - Crossface - Half Boston Crab - Dragon Sleeper - Anaconda Vice - Triangle Lancer (Head and shoulder lock variation) - Figure Four Necklock (Oponent is neck to the ropes facing away. Rain is sitting on the top rope. He raps his legs around the opponent's neck in the shape of a '4' and falls backwards, forcing the opponent's neck to support his weight) Impact Typical. Often prefers to concentrate on moves that directly impact the neck and head. - Pendulum Neckbreaker (set up like a regular backbreaker, except the knee is driven into the neck rather than the back) - Reverse DDT - Codebreaker: Alabama Slam - End Of Days: Raised Reverse DDT into Sit Out Powerbomb - ZZT: Brainbuster - Swinging Neckbreaker - Gift From Beyond: Pepsi Twist - TigerDRIVER: Typical Tigerbomb, except that the opponent is dropped with force to the back of their head - Empty Spiral: Rain stands face to face with the victim and grabs both their arms, crossing them in front of the victim's body. Rain bends the victim down and traps their head in one of the his arm pits similiar to a facelock. Rain releases one of the victim's arms and reaches through the victim's legs. Rain lifts the victim up so they are upside down. Rain falls to the ground and drops the victim on the top of their head. Counters Signature counters. - Clothesline duck-under into Empty Spiral or Super Kick - Powerbomb into hurricanrana - Powerslam into Reverse DDT or End of Days - Sharpshooter into Sharpshooter Kicks and Combinations Because of his past belief that 'the fans aren't worth it'; worth putting an honest effort into winning without cheating, Rain once restricted his arsenal. These are moves that he worked in during his early years, primarily his time working frequent tours of Britain and Mexico. A stunning mix of stiff kicks, punches, and quick combinations that have not been seen from Rain in quite some time. - Double Stomp: Two feet planted firmly into whatever part of the body Rain picks to land on. Can be done basically out of no where in any position. His favorite is when the opponent is stuck in the Tree of Woe and holding their upper body up. Rain climbs to the top turnbuckle where they're hung up and jumps down onto the upper body with the Double Stomp. - Super Kick - Missle Dropkick - Any other random kicks Poem: Opponent in sitting position ==> shin kick to the back ==> spinning back kick to the chest ==> Lionsault Apologetic: Opponent runs at Rain ==> backdrop opponent onto apron ==> kick opponent off of apron ==> Plancha Open Heart: Opponent in corner ==> Repeated Rain forearms to the head/chest ==> Charging dropkick to the chest In Utero: Tornado DDT ==> Bloodshed Entrance Music 'Sick' by Dope
