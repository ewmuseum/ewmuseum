Johnny Borealis Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Johnny B, Your Guilty Pleasure, The Greatest Thing To Grace The Ring [citation needed], The Swaggerer Hometown Aurora, NY Birthday 4/2/82 Height 6' Weight 195 Fed Titles World Champion, 3x TV Champ, 3x US Champ Fed Awards Dangerous Games 2005 Most Eliminations, Dangerous Games 2010 Most Eliminations (tied with Chris Bagwell), Rampage Winner 2011 Biography Take your left hand and cover it in peanut butter. This is Johnny Cage from Mortal Kombat. Take your right hand and cover it in marshmallow fluff. This is JD from Scrubs. Your face is Chris Jericho. Go ahead and smear your hands all over your face, basking in the amazing deliciousness and sinful goodness that you will never, ever, ever get off. It stains your soul. It sticks in your hair. It completes you. This...is Johnny Borealis. He's got his own reality TV show cuz fuck it why not. He's obsessed with being a star on any and every level, constantly trying to master something different. His in-ring skills are commendable, but his focus leaves much to be desired. Oh, and he loves sex. Other Feds A bunch of places that don't measure up to GCW. Other Fed Titles Various championships here and there. Strengths Conditioning, agility, playing possum, feeding off crowd energy Weaknesses Overestimates his own strength, will overemphasize showmanship at times at the cost of effectiveness, hates to use submissions, will execute moves that cause him harm, overprotective of his face, runs his mouth all the fucking time Wrestling Style Grappler/Risk Taker Strategy He shows off. He's a douche. And the crowd loves every minute of it. Finisher Move Northern Lights Out Finisher Description A double wristlock Northern Lights brainbuster. Special Moves The Highlight Reel (modified Shooting Star Press), Lit Up (Superkick), Tornado DDT, Sweet Dubloons of Saskatoon! (NLO off the turnbuckle) Other Moves STRIKING Johnny stays away from brawling and if someone is trying to punch or kick him he will look for catching an arm or leg and hitting a reversal. His one striking strength comes from his speed; when he does jab or kick, it's usually a smaller, faster strike that can open up an opponent. Borealis will dropkick often, as it is both flashy and effective. Enzuigiri Spinning Heel Kick Johnny is a fan of the Superkick which he calls Lit Up , mostly when he needs to rip momentum back his way. Face it, Superkicks look great and pack a whallop. It is flashy, it can give momentum, it can bring the crowd to its feet, AND it can either be quickly executed or built up (example: HBK would stomp and get the crowd clapping). *NOTE: The commentators tend to use the name of the move as a verb rather than a noun, such as, "[opponent] just got Lit Up!" or "Borealis just Lit him Up!" GRAPPLING - Borealis is a grappler, with great core strength and familiarity on his feet. He's comfortable locking up with opponents of any size, since he can squirm around and outmaneuver larger wrestlers and he can match the agility of most smaller wrestlers. Facebuster Russian Leg Sweep Spinning Neck Breaker Snap Suplex DDT (just about any variation) Stump Puller Piledriver Rolling German Suplexes Belly To Belly Snap Suplex Snap Mare (really anything with the word 'snap' in it) Spinning Drop Toe Hold 3/4 Turn Neckbreaker Inverted DDT Fireman's Carry Ankle Pick takedown * NOTE - Johnny will never hit a Northern Lights Suplex. It would be pretty dumb of him to do a move so similar to his finisher when he could just hit his finisher instead. POWER MOVES - Borealis is built more for speed, agility, and endurance. However, like most professional wrestlers, he has made sure to add some power to his resume. While he won't be gorilla pressing a 350 lb opponent, Johnny will definitely try to pull off a move that requires all his strength. After all, Johnny realizes that many big, powerful moves will get the crowd on their feet. This can get him into trouble against larger opponents, but it's worth it if he can pull it off. Running Sit Down Powerbomb Pump Handle Slam Jumping Piledriver Death Valley Driver Brainbuster IRISH WHIP MOVES - Being smaller, Borealis utilizes momentum when he can. He's a big favorite of throwing opponents into the ropes and capitalizing on redirecting their movement. Head Scissor Takedown Reverse Bulldog (Hart Breaker) Sidewalk Slam Samoan Drop Spinebuster Lou Thesz Press Lit Up (Superkick) to a running opponent Flying Clothesline Backflip Dropkick Side Slam Springboard to elbow Knee to the gut followed by Abdominal Stretch Cross Body Block Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker or Slam Moonsault off the middle rope into a running opponent TOP ROPE MOVES - Johnny is at home on top of the turnbuckle (it makes sense, seeing as that gets the crowd pumped and that he is literally above his opponent). He's not a high flyer so to speak, so most of his moves from up top are fairly simple in execution. The Highlight Reel - The extent of his top rope complexity, Johnny will hit this Shooting Star Press when he has an opening and needs the crowd to pop. This can lead to some backfiring, as sometimes he rushes it and his opponent isn't quite ready to stay down. Johnny makes this his own by hitting the move, using the impact bounce to turn over onto his back, and finally hook the opponent's head and leg in an inverted cradle pin. Front Flip Legdrop Frog Splash Cross Body Block Missile Dropkick Diving Clothesline * NOTE - Johnny would not do a diving headbutt (or any headbutt for that matter). His face is his money maker. TURNBUCKLE GRAPPLES Sweet Dubloons of Saskatoon! - A variant of the Northern Lights Out that Johnny will hit off the turnbuckle. This move is extremely rare, and he'll probably only bust it out for Pay Per Views, title matches, or a reversal if someone tries to land a Tornado DDT off the turnbuckle. The extra elevation for what is essentially a double wrist-locked Northern Lights Super-brainbuster is extremely dangerous (as his opponent is falling onto their face from the top turnbuckle without their arms to protect them). Tornado DDT - Coupled with his penchant to play possum, Johnny will often times catch his breath while opponents look to set up a turnbuckle move of their own, and he favors reversing it with a Tornado DDT. Springboard to Back Elbow Turnbuckle Splash Headscissors off the top rope Superplex Bulldog off the turnbuckle SUBMISSION - Submission holds, in Johnny's view, are boring. Does anyone fondly miss Dean Malenko, the Man of 1000 Holds? Roddy Piper always excited Johnny growing up...until he hit his Sleeper Hold finisher and put his opponent, and the crowd, right to bed. But, they do have their value in wearing somebody down...just don't expect Borealis to lock more than one in during a match. Single Leg Boston Crab with Reverse Chinlock Octopus Stretch Ankle Lock Crossface Ring Entrances The shrieking voice cuts through the arena like a fucking knife. "CALL 9-1-1 NOW!!!" Skrillex's "First of the Year" blasts in right at the bass drop. The fans are still cringing from the cacophony as a quick burst of pyro draws all eyes to the entrance way. Bryan: Clad in black baggy cargo pants, black Doc Martens, and a pair of Oakley's, Johnny Borealis carries himself onto the stage exactly the way he wants: with all eyes on him. The crowd's reaction is more than mixed, and it's not warm. Andrews: Making his way to the ring...from Aurora, NY...weighing 195 lbs...Your Guilty Pleasure...The Swaggerer...JOHNNY! BOOOOOOORRRREEEEAAALLIIISSSSSS!!! The swagger is there, alright. Every step is a gift to the crowd. The man is lean, refined, and charged up to deliver. The smirk on his face comes from how amused he is at himself; his whole purpose of existence is to get the crowd loud and on its feet, and he couldn't give less of a shit if it's because they love or hate him. Yale: Bryan: Sliding into the ring, he hands the "HDTV" Title off to Joey Andrews and cracks his neck, sizes up his opponent, and looks ready to wreak some havoc.
