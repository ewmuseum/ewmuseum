Kevin Sandusky Biography Real Name Kevin Sandusky Nicknames 'Kev', ''Bola', 'Hey you! Get off of my sister!' Hometown Tucson, AZ Birthday 2/18/1980 Height 6'2' Weight 242 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography It's complicated, really. He's a bastard, literally. I guess the primary things to know are that he's an impeccible physical specimen, with an extremely sound mind for the business. Raised almost from birth to be a wrestler (his step-father is a luchadore) he's steeped in the traditions from south of the border. A tour of Japan as given him a steely shinto edge. And most importantly, a series of backstabs and broken promises by promoters and federations have left him with a bad attitude and a jaded streak a mile wide. Gimmick: 2nd generation wrestler looking to force the 'big break' that has eluded him so far. Driven, egotistical, and good enough to tell just about anyone how much better he is and be truthful. PS: He's a prick, and staring at your sister's boobs right now. Appearance: Kevin has a mischievous, almost boy-like face and close cropped (yet meticulously messy) sandy blonde hair. The kind of look that is capable of disarming a women's defenses and garning the instant trust of men unaware; and equally capable of the kind of smarmy and superior sneer that could rally a democratic convention to start screaming 'Class War'. To the ring, he wears a pair of straight legged black jeans over a pair of Classic black boots with red wingtips. He wears a tight black shirt with red sleeves and black MMA forearms pads. One of his trademarks is a braided silver belt with a silver skull as the buckle. Out of the ring, he tends to wear motorcycle leathers, as his main mode of transportation and 2nd obsession is his motorcycle. He rides a customized black and red Aprilia Mille that was given to him as a gift after a tour in Japan. Other Feds RUSH Syndicate Champion, XUDF/XUW Galactic Tag Team Champion (w/ Shayne Steele) provisional Wrestling Style Puro/Lucha hybrid Finisher Move Sandusky Slam Finisher Description Falling full nelson slam, with the added nuance that he steps across with his right foot, causing the opponent to pitch back across his hip and land with full weight on the base of their skull. This finisher was his estranged father's finisher. Kept not to honor him, but because it's so damn effective Finisher Setup Move Latino Leap Setup Description 360 Leaping Leg Lariat (His stepfather's finisher, kept and used to honor him) Other Moves Brainbuster Suplex Arm-drag variations: Lucha guys can arm-drag you from anywhere Monkey-flip Contagion: A black mist (ala Kenji Mutoh) Terminal Velocity Shooting Star Senton (450 backward rotation) **uber-death Puro finisher** Next Generation: Top rope somersaulting 1/2 twist leg drop Christos e desnucadera: Lucha style Full-nelson submission into inverted face-first powerbomb Entrance Music 'Land of No Sun' by Destrophy
