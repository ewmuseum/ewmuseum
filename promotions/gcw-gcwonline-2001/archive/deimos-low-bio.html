James von Doom Biography Real Name James von Doom Nicknames The Puppetmaster Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday 03/09/83 Height 6'1 Weight 235 Fed Titles none Fed Awards none Biography Von Doom has had a long career in wrestling and has competed in various organizations. Most fans and wrestlers consider him to be somewhat crazy or eccentric. He has recently developed a bizarre strategy of making himself appear weak to his opponents even though he is quite a cunning fighter and match strategist. He will feign weakness, throw matches to make his rivals think he is weak and not a threat to them, he will lie and make bogus alliances with people, and often lures his opponents and allies into a false sense of security while simultaneously convincing them to turn on their allies and close friends. He makes complex webs of false alliances while only having a true alliance to a small people. He was the first in wrestling to intentionally lose matches and was seen as weak by his fellow wrestlers. He also bizarrely seeks to be hated by the other wrestlers, bookers, and other wrestling personalitis. He uses lies and deception after admitting to his fellow wrestlers that he would do so, lies about conversations and alliances and even lies about past experiences in his life. His straight talking attitude toward other wrestlers has made him a security risk as well. Regardless, James is considered one of the most devious wrestlers in history.
