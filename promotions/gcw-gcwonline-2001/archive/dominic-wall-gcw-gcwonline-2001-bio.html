Dominic Wall Biography Real Name Dominic Wall Nicknames Dom, D-Wall Hometown Miami, FL Birthday December 11 1986 Height 5'11 Weight 205 Fed Titles GCW TV Title Biography The quick version is that Dominic never knew his birth parents. He was adopted as a baby by Adam and Janelle Ricci. He grew up loving and playing football and wrestling, becoming All-State in both in high school. He's lived in Dallas, Texas, Jacksonville and Miami, Florida. He had scholarship offers for both football and wrestling to numerous colleges but instead joined the Army instead. After his four year committment was over, Dominic left the service and had been working a few jobs and preparing to finally head to college. However things would take a drastic turn when GCW legend, LeStatt Knight, approached Dominic and offered to train him in wrestling and get him a job in GCW. The long version? Well isn't that what RP's are for? Strengths great cardio, very fast, surprisingly strong, explosive, great submissions, very calm and collected for someone of his inexperience Weaknesses inexperienced, not overly powerful, leaves himself open to counter attacks because of lack of knowledge of moves he is performing Wrestling Style submissionist, high impact Strategy Dominic is confident in what he knows, striking and submissions (which he learned in his Army days doing combatives training) so he will try to make the opening moments of a match a distance game by using leg and body kicks to try and wear down his opponents. He's also a skilled striker with his hands, possessing fast combinations and a deadly overhand right. He's unafraid to stand in and bang because he has so much faith in his striking. He is inexperienced but is a quick learner and with the help of LeStatt Knight, Dom is gaining a wider arsenal of high impact moves such as slams and suplexes. Many of these moves will be open to reversals however because of his ignorance to the technical side of most of the moves but he still loves to attempt them. He will not attempt many high risk moves, only high impact. Although he is quick and agile enough to run with most cruiserweights. All of the above is just the build up however because what Dominic really wants to do is make someone give up or better yet, pass out. He is an expert submissionist, training and competing extensively while in the Army. He'll quickly kick someone in the stomach and instead of moving in for a knee lift or a DDT, lock in his guillotine choke and cut off someone's air supply. If you can think up a submission, Dominic knows it or will attempt to learn it and use it in a match. Finisher Move The Last Breath Finisher Description Guillotine Choke WW Entrance Almost at the same time that the beat of 'Till I Collapse' by Eminem kicks up, Dominic Wall steps through the curtains and into the spotlight of the GCW stage. He stands tall, staring out into the massive sea of people that make up the evening's crowd and a smile forms on his face. After soaking in the moment for a few seconds, Wall leaps into the air and howls. "LET'S GO!" Dominic moves down the ramp with a pep in his step and an intensity plastered on his face. He outstretches his hands to allow any fan wanting to get a little bit of a GCW athlete to get what they want. When he reaches the ring, Wall leaps up onto the apron, grabs a hold of the top rope and launches himself over it and into the ring. Now he waits for the bell to ring, for his moment to come. Special Moves KnightFall(NOT a Frog Splash): Edge's Downward Spiral OR Mortis'(Kanyon) Flatliner Other Moves KEY NOTE: Because of certain childhood issues, Dominic refuses to hit a woman in the face. No punches, elbows, kicks, etc. He will perform submissions, clotheslines, slams, etc. because those are part of the sport but it should be shown that we won't strike the female wresters in the face. It could be used down the road against a nasty, heel female perhaps that she pushes him to do it but it'd take alot.
