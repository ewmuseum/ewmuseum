Sunday, July 28, 2002

GCW: Meltdown Preview

With just a few days to go until the return of GCW to ABC, the superstars are preparing to pick up where they left off at Dangerous Games!

Rich Rollins eliminated himself from the battle royal after receiving a slap from Michael Stevens... Rollins is scheduled to be at Meltdown, so perhaps we'll get an explanation!

Twisted has yet to comment on his GCW title victory at Dangerous Games, but he is confirmed to be at the show, and will be in action in the main event!

And The Jhub and Octavian, two favorites to win the battle royal last night, also will be in attendance! They brawled throughout the entire night, so it seems entirely possible that they will explode once again.

Also, the status of the other GCW titles will be announced, contracts will be signed, and much more! So don't miss Meltdown, this Thursday night on ABC!

Source: GCW Meltdown

Steven Caldera Returns to Columbus

Columbus, Ohio:

Steven Caldera landed in the GCW corporate jet in Columbus International Airport this morning, and intends to remain there for months to come.

GCW, meanwhile, heads south to San Fransisco for the first episode of Meltdown in the new era.

It seems that Caldera's sincerity has been confirmed, at least for the moment. He has been working out of GCW headquarters all day, and is not scheduled to fly to San Fransisco any time soon.

Caldera really is staying out of GCW's affairs.

However, with a roster full of men that have yet to sign contracts, how will Caldera accomplish confirming a roster without being present? Rumors of a new staff member being hired as a middle-man are running rampant, and Caldera himself has been especially cryptic when approached about it.

Meltdown will be quite interesting, as several superstars plan to ink their contracts before they step into the ring again.

Source: GCW HQ

Thursday, July 25, 2002

Heatwave Sold out in moments

Charlotte, North Carolina-

Tickets went on sale for GCW's August Pay-Per-View, Heatwave, just hours after the conclusion of Dangerous Games.

And only minutes later, the show had sold out.

Heatwave, announced a few days before Dangerous Games marked GCW's return, will be GCW's first true Pay-Per-View return. This incredible event has garnered great anticipation, and the result was the fastest sellout in GCW history.

Fans from all over the country were found in Charlotte to purchase tickets personally, and internet users likewise logged on to Ticketmaster to get their seats. GCW has announced that it intends to find ways to expand the floor seating, and will release at least another 300 tickets in a few weeks.

However, with Heatwave already sold out, the anticipation will grow as we approach GCW's full blown return to Pay-Per-View.
