Natalie Burrows. Biography Real Name Natalie Burrows. Nicknames 'The Southern Belle' has become an unofficial moniker. Hometown Durham, North Carolina. Birthday April 29, 1988. Height 5'9 Weight 173 lbs. Biography The only child of a traveling insurance salesman (Robert) and a homemaker that chose to give up her career as a seamstress in order to stay at home (Angela), Natalie's life was largely unremarkable as she grew up in Durham, North Carolina. A straight-B student, the teachers never had a problem with the little blond and she was well-liked by her peers for her sunny disposition. This affection extended into the local independant wrestling scene; while he never climbed through the ropes, Robbie was a fixture of the indies, the guy that kept records and wrangled arrangements for different promotions to have their shows. Add onto that a friendship with Morgan Reid, one of the most notable talents of the time and region, and it was no surprise that Natalie became a fan... although she wasn't going to get into the business at first. Rather, she wound up on track for a scholarship with Duke University in their teaching program. Everything in Natalie's life was as good as it got until one afternoon when she was suddenly called to the principal's office in the middle of her Chemistry class. It was the news she received that shook her world to its very core. On his way to an appointment with a prospective client, her father had been in a car accident. A drunk driver in a truck t-boned his old sedan after running a red light. As seems to be common luck, the intoxicated speeder walked away without so much as a scratch while the Burrows patriarch left the scene of the accident in a body bag. The community did what they could to help out, of course, but there was only so much that they could do. The one question that needed asking that no one could bring themselves to voice was a simple one, yet dastardly complicated all at once; how was the small family Robert left behind going to cope with his death? Hitting the bottle, ironically enough, was her mother's answer to that question, a decision that would ultimately lead to Natalie taking her share of the inheritance and leaving one afternoon while Angela was at the local watering hole after months and months of arguing. There wasn't much left after paying off her father's debts - something else Angela neglected to do - but it was enough to enroll her in wrestling school (with a discount provided by Morgan Reid, one of the two wrestlers that trained her) and buy a ramshackle old plantation house in Placid Valley. Training for two years with the sort of fervent passion that is rarely found, Natalie set her sights high for her debut… a near-impossible task. Debuting in a hardcore elimination match with eight other competitors, not only did the underdog Southern Belle walk out the winner with half of the eliminations to her credit, but she did it without picking up a single weapon-- the only competitor to ever do so, in Hardkore's twenty-plus years of operation. She then went on to win the Hardkore World Women's Championship from Yuku Shiro in a grueling half-hour match that proved, concretely, that her success was not a fluke… although an injury soonafter involving her leg cost her that title in her first defense. Also of note is her run in the Volatile Wrestling Alliance, where she worked her fingers to the bone in a lengthy path to the Women's Championship there. VWA is where she picked up the unofficial moniker 'The Southern Belle', and also persevered in the face of tragedy, having lost her mother right before the match with Sloan Sawyer in which she would win contendership to the title she was after. Going on to defeat Amy Chastaine at Crisis of Faith, Natalie would be the last woman to hold that coveted title, VWA going under due to a power struggle between Jude and Shane Lawson soonafter. Undeterred, Natalie made her way through a couple of flash-in-the-pan companies before making her way to the GCW… where she intends on continuing to build her legacy. Other Feds Hardkore World, VWA, ACW, TGW. Strengths Thanks to a lifetime of leg-based athletic activities-- soccer, most specifically-- Natalie has proven to pack quite the whallop, especially considering how nicely she speaks and how she presents herself. She's quick on those educated feet of hers, a Weaknesses Her mat and submissions game is average at best, and while she can overcome a larger opponent? It is by no means easy. Should a considerably larger opponent catch her and slow the pace of the match gone, then her chances of success drop quite sharply-- an Wrestling Style Unconventional striker-- think RVD sans flips/top rope work-- with a dash of power, when appropriate.
