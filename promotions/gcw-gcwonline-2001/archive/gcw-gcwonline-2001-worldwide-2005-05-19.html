GCW — Card

WorldWide 13, 19 May 2005

19 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The lights dim in the arena, and the Lisbon crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The Greek crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Tremendous!” and “Octavian 4 Emperor” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Athens, Greece” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: With another capacity crowd on hand GCW continues its Road to Warpath from Athens! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan with you again at ringside with the legendary Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins, and Rich, tonight is one of the last two shows before GCW’s first Pay-Per-View event of the new era, Warpath!

Rollins: That’s right, JB, and I for one am not looking forward to it one bit. In case you haven’t been watching the news, The Dark Messiah, the man I was supposed to face at Warpath in my return to the ring, has been fired thanks to that wanna be Mike Hardy. Looks like I’m stuck back here with you until I come up with a plan ‘B’.

Bryan: Well, you’re probably the only man in the company that’s upset about that, because for the firs time in a long time, GCW doesn’t have to fear Peter Maxwell! Mike Hardy did the unthinkable and destroyed Maxwell right here in this ring last week, and quite possibly saved GCW in the process!

Rollins: Why don’t we talk about tonight?

Bryan: Tonight we’ve got another tremendous show in store for you, as several fresh faces get shots at the big time, and The Jhub gets a shot at the Hardcore Title!

Rollins: And we’ll get to see if GCW can produce a decent show without me in the ring.

The laughter echoed down the hallway, at first boisterous, dying down into a low chuckle as the two men approached the door. They looked at each other, exchanging dumbfounded glances. Last week Michael Stevens had been supremely pissed, tossing around chairs kicking the walls, it wasn’t a great time to be around the guy. And now Kyle Niker, and Conner Pearce were standing outside his dressing room, listening to him laugh. It was odd to say the least.

Kyle Niker: Uh, you think we should go in?

Conner Pearce: I guess so, I mean if we stand out here, his coffee’s gonna get cold.

Kyle Niker: Yeah, and the doughnuts might get stale.

Conner Pearce: Right on. Hey Kyle why in the hell are we getting him coffee and doughnuts again?

Kyle Niker: Duh, we’re trying to cheer him up cause we was so pissed last week. No one can be annoyed in the face of a jelly-filled Long John

Conner Pearce: Oh yeah.

They stood for a moment, both staring at the door, as the laughter continued to come from behind the closed door. After a minute Kyle looked over at Conner.

Kyle Niker: You gonna open the door anytime soon? My hands are kinda full of fried doughy goodness.

Conner Pearce: Whoopsie.

Opening the door, the two find Stevens shaking his head almost sadly as his chuckles are finally dying away. He turns smiling at both men.

Michael Stevens: Guys, glad to see you.

Kyle Niker: You are…I mean yeah of course, we brought you some doughnuts and coffee, teach.

Michael Stevens: Excellent, just set them down somewhere; I just got some damn fabulous news. Steven screwed up big time, he’s actually going to let me step into the ring with that upstart twit Hardy at Warpath. I’mma grind his precious little golden boy into dust, and make him beg for mercy. It’ll be glorious, I’ll teach that twerp a lesson he’ll never forget.

Conner Pearce: Sure you’re gonna be up to that Mike? I mean you’re ribs and all?

Michael Stevens: What’s that supposed to mean?

Kyle Niker: He didn’t mean anything…

Michael Stevens: You know what I don’t care, it doesn’t matter. I could twist that doofus into a pretzel with both arms tied behind my back and a system full of a horse laxative. Nothing can bring me down right now, not even having to wrestle Timothy Leary’s retarded cousin Twizzy again. Christ I think I’ve wrestled the guy more in the last couple months than I’ve jerked off, and believe me without Sheena around, that’s a damn lot.

Conner Pearce: Umm…

Kyle Niker: Uh yeah.

Michael Stevens: Hehe, don’t worry about it guys, things are looking up around here, I’ll destroy Hardy, then I’ll focus on some other things that I’ve been considering lately, and we’ll get you guys up and running as the most dominate tag team in GCW. After all, the belts are up for grabs again since those two guys who I couldn’t understand for the life of me got fired.

Now, how about a doughnut, I hope you guys didn’t get any of the jelly filled crap.

Kyle Niker: Son of a…

The show then heads back to ringside, where Joey Andrews stands ready to announce the opening bout.

Trey Tremendous vs. Toxic

Bryan – Well we’re going to kick WorldWide off with a good one as the self-proclaimed future of GCW takes on GCW’s fastest rising star.

Renegade – Toxic hasn’t been in the ring in over a month. He better hope he can shake off the rust for this.

“Big Bad Wolf” blasts through the arena as Trey Tremendous receives a largely mixed reaction from the crowd. Trey carries a steel chair to ring with him, much like two weeks ago. “Do the Evolution” hits next and the crowd is more than generous with their jeers for Toxic. Toxic ignores them and charges towards the ring. He slides under and Trey immediately charges at him with the chair. Toxic slams a hard fist into the chair knocking it back in his face and sending the newcomer down.

Toxic slides the chair out of the ring before tossing grabbing Trey with one arm and throwing him across the ring. Toxic shakes his head at Trey, who is slow to get up, before barraging him with heavy rights and lefts. Trey sinks into the corner of the ring and is not able to move before Toxic’s knee connects with his face. Toxic aims another knee strike but Trey ducks out of the way and Toxic’s knee connects with metal this time.

Toxic stumbles backwards and Trey quickly takes him down with a chop block. Toxic gets to his feet and Trey flips him down with a headscissors, and rolls into the pin. Toxic shoves Trey off of him after 2. Trey is quick to respond with a dropkick and a spinning heel kick that brings Toxic to his knees. From this point Trey is able to hit an easy DDT and roll through with another cover, giving him another 2 count.

Trey whips Toxic into the ropes and attempts a powerslam, but Toxic reverses his weight, making it impossible for Trey to lift him. Toxic instead throws Trey high across the ring with a belly-to-belly suplex..

Trey staggers to his feet only to be put down with a hard clothesline. Toxic drops down for the pin but Trey kicks out quickly. Toxic lifts Trey up and drives him hard into the mat with a spinebuster. This is quickly followed up by an Irish whip and a full-nelson backbreaker.

Toxic pulls Trey up and slams him down hard with a pumphandle slam. He signals for the Toxic Waste and lifts Trey up on his shoulders. However, Trey squirms out, hits the ropes and brings Toxic down with a beautiful springboard moonsault. He hooks the leg and nearly gets the three count before Toxic gets his shoulder up.

Trey hops up onto the top-turnbuckle and flawlessly executes a flying cross-body which takes Toxic down again. As Toxic gets to his feet he’s met with a hard flying forearm that puts him down again.

As he slowly gets to his feet, Toxic catches a Trey spinning off the top the turnbuckle and nailing the Absolute Power.

Trey doesn’t wait any longer and hooks Toxic up in position for the Culpable. Toxic tries to power out of the move but Trey uses the momentum to hit a modified version of the move. Toxic lays motionless and Trey covers him for the 1…2…3!!!

Winner: Trey Tremendous

Bryan – What an upset! Trey Tremendous is walking away with the victory and this has to be a huge blow to Toxic on his way to Warpath.

Renegade – Toxic doesn’t take his losses lightly and I guarantee you he’ll be back for revenge as soon as he gets the chance.

“Big Bad Wolf” hits again and Trey heads back up as Toxic glares a hole in his back. WorldWide heads to commercial.

Winner: Trey Tremendous

The camera cuts backstage to see Twisted walking down the hallway to his locker. He opens the door and is somewhat startled to see Toxic standing there.

Twizzy – I expressed shock and disgust at the heathen standing before me.

Toxic – Huh? What are you doing?

Twizzy – I’m narrating my life…I sternly replied.

Toxic just shakes his head at the junkie man.

Toxic – Did you get to the part about me kicking your ass at Warpath?

Twizzy – Of course not stupid, that hasn’t happened yet…I answered with disdain.

Toxic – You know that’s really irritating?

Twizzy – Not as irritating as finding a big smelly goat-monger in my locker room…I replied with a small sense of satisfaction.

Toxic – Alright I’ve had enough.

Toxic makes his way towards Twisted, his eyes glowing.

Twizzy – That’s why I brought this stereo…I said and pointed across the room. Just to remind you…I said and walked across the room.

Twizzy pressed play and instantly MC Hammer jarred the room.

Twizzy – You can’t touch me!

And immediately one of the most ridiculous scenes in GCW history ensued as Twizzy did the Hammer dance.

Twizzy – Stop! Twizzy time! You were heading for an a-sskicking thanks my Twistodium, but Caldera came on screen and shouted from a podium…

Toxic – Podium?

Twizzy – He said we couldn’t touch each other until we got to Warpath…so now I’ll have to wait to kick your mangy ass! You can’t touch me!

Twizzy, distracted by his own dance moves, doesn’t notice Toxic heading over to where his Twistodium lies. Toxic picks up the small bottle of pills and shoves them in his pocket. He then produces a different, although similar-looking bottle, and places it on the counter.

Toxic – Percasets should do the trick.

Toxic smiled and exited the room, as “Can’t Touch This” died down.

Twizzy – Where’d he go? I queried at the empty space before me.

Twisted walks over to the counter and pops the lid off the pill bottle. He immediately pops about five pills down his throat.

Twizzy – GOAT IS GOOD!

(We return to the ring to hear "Just Got Wicked" playing over the PA system, and in the middle of the ring stands a beaming and very proud Jason Dread! He stands confident, GCW World Title slung over his right shoulder. He brandishes a microphone in his right hand, which he bring to his lips to speak, background music dying down in the background.)

Jason Dread – I stood in the ring last week at a clear disadvantage to my situation at hand. I'd had everything stolen from me in my life, given and ultimatum to give up my GCW World title, and had underhanded booking work against me in the sense that I was to go one against four as I squared off against every member of the Praetorian guard in a battle royal.

Going in I understood the peril of being in such a match, but I wasn't about to back down. I'm not going to live in the legacy of former champions GCW has seen, where I take the low road through a challenge. I told every single one of you from the day I regained this title that I would hold it in esteem that I nor many others have before.

(This, of course, gets a fairly decent pop from the crowd.)

Jason Dread – And I want to answer a challenge given to me several weeks back by the man whom has used every underhanded tactic in his book to get under my skin...Octavian, get out here now!

(The crowd pops and the alluding to an Octavian/Dread conflict! After a few moments silence, "Might of Rome" cues up, and Octavian steps out from the back all alone, to a chorus of boos from the crowd. He has a mic in hand as well.)

James Bryan – This is an uncharacteristic move by Jason Dread, calling out former three-time World Champion Octavian. I wonder was exactly he has to say?

Jason Dread – Octavian, two weeks ago you left me with a choice as I lay in this very ring a broken and bleeding man. Either I could surrender my GCW World Title to you and regain everything you've stolen in my life, or continue to fight a battle that may never end because this title around my shoulder is the one thing all of your money and power can't get you.

Tonight, Octavian, I have an answer...

(A smile preys upon Octavian's lips and he preempts Dread's next words with some of his own.)

Octavian – Before you give me that answer I think I have some things you might want to think about, Jason...

(A prerequisite boo from the crowd.)

Octavian – You were my protégé for a period of many months before you gained that title for your first time, and you got to know my better than what most do. You saw first-hand what I did several times over with the likes of Tristain Mayhem...you know how badly I can hurt someone physically and emotionally. You're not stupid, Jason...the question I ask of you is...

...why do you think I've been as generous as I have to you?

You know of everything that I could be doing at the moment to you. I could be down in that very ring right now choking your life away or simply finishing that job I started on your neck. You're completely oblivious to all this, Jason.

Dread – Say what you will, Octavian, but you know for fact that I have the upper hand now. I beat every single person in your Praetorian Guard-

(Octavian cuts Dread off.)

Octavian – And once again you had the help enlisted from Lestatt Knight and Jhub to get your job finished for you! You're the same as you ever were...you're weak and require everyone else around you to do your jobs for you.

(Dread smiles, spotting an opening in Octavian's speech.)

Dread – And you're not?! I don't have a group of three flunkies fluffing my balls whenever I say the word. I'M not the one who has made up a fancy group name such as the "Praetorian Guard", and have never used said group in a means of mugging one lone man whom you couldn't get the job done with all the way back at ACWF six!

Octavian, you're as much as a hypocrite as you are shallow shell of a man.

(Obviously livid at Dread's words and the crowd's hearty response to them, Octavian throws a mild screaming fit at the top of the ramp. All the sudden, he stops, and a smile comes over his face once more.)

Octavian – Think what you will, Jason. None of that even matters at this moment, as I will always be the one who shows everything in action.

(Octavian turns to leave, leaving everyone with just one more message before he departs.)

Octavian – By the way Dread, your "upper hand" that you have is right behind you...

(We turn our focus to the ring, where Dread stands momentarily confused, then turns around only to be laid out by a cheap shot from Mr. Danger! Danger bails from the ring quickly before Dread can recover, which he does quickly. He stands to his feet to watch as Mr. Danger runs up the ring ramp to the backstage area. Dread throws his microphone down, and World Title in hand, charges out of the ring after Mr. Danger.)

James Bryan – And in the fray, Octavian seems to have gotten away pretty easily. Once again for the 3rd week in a row, Octavian has found some way to get at the World Champ using any means at his disposal. Dread is in pursuit of Danger at the moment, and hopefully he finds that little weasel!

Twizzy vs. Michael Stevens

“I Want to Rule the World” hits and the fans start to boo as Michael Stevens makes his way to the ring. Upon entry, the music cuts out and “Life’s Sweet Drug” rips through the arena. The fans immediately leap to their feet with cheers. Twizzy wastes no time slapping hands with fans as he cheerfully heads to the ring.

Bryan – Well Twizzy sure looks cheerful tonight

Renegade – Yeah…give him five minutes and he’ll be staggering like a drunk. Those percasets do a number on you. It only takes a couple knee surgeries to find that out.

The match gets underway with Twizzy and Stevens locking up in the centre of the ring. Twizzy gets the upper hand and shoves Stevens into the corner. He follows it up with some hard right hand shots before whipping Stevens across the ring and connecting with a clothesline.

Stevens gets to his feet only to be taken down again by a big swinging neckbreaker from Twizzy. Twizzy gets to his feet and raises his arms, inciting cheers from the audience. Stevens uses the opportunity to aim a dropkick but Twizzy senses it and ducks out of the way, letting Stevens land awkwardly on his knee. Stevens rolls around on the canvas clutching his knee.

Twizzy allows him to get to his feet before bringing him down hard with a running bulldog. Twizzy covers and gets a two-count before Stevens kicks out. Twizzy lines Stevens up for a clothesline but misses. Stevens turns around and with a kick to the guy sets Twizzy up for the Inferno DDT…only to have Twizzy spin out of it and drill Stevens with a HUGE Screw Loose.

Twizzy goes for the cover but suddenly sits up to scratch his ear. He stands up and bumbles around the ring for a moment before beginning to furiously scratch his entire upper body. With Twizzy distracted by his own skin, Stevens rises up behind him and takes him down with a reverse DDT.

Stevens covers him and Twizzy is only barely able to kick out before the three. Twizzy’s gets to his feet and swings wildly at Stevens who easily dodges the attack and drops him with a quick rolling armbar.

Twizzy is able to reach the ropes and Stevens breaks the hold. Stevens telegraphs a heel kick but a sluggish Twizzy only manages to partially move and catches it on the right cheek bone.

Twizzy slowly pulls himself to his feet and with a vicious, incoherent spin manages to catch Stevens with a hard elbow shot to the face. Twizzy tries to hook Stevens up for a vertical suplex but becomes woozy and drops to one knee. This leaves a big opening for Stevens who quickly pulls Twizzy and puts him down and out with the Inferno DDT, rolls through and gets the 1…2…3!

Winner: Michael Stevens

The crowd boos as Stevens raises his arms in victory.

Renegade – I told you those were powerful pills.

Bryan – Well Toxic’s managed to sabotage another of Twisted’s matches here tonight and the fans are not pleased.

Twizzy, still in a haze, stumbles off through the crowd as WorldWide heads to commercial.

( LeStatt Knight stood in the hallway which isn't a big surprise, the shocking part about this is that he is smiling. This smile comes from the fact that he is standing in the hallway chatting with a GCW crew member. Knight is stretching and such, preparing for his latest GCW U.S. Title defense, something he has gotten quite used to lately. )

Knight: Well Paul I don't quite know what to say about this match I have tonight... I mean I guess I could be nervous, but for some 'strange' reason I am not.

Paul: I know I wouldn't get into it with no woman, have you ever seen Fatal Attraction or Basic Instinct ? The hell with that, women can be ruthless.

Knight: ( with a chuckle ) Well that's the difference between us amigo, I've dealt with some unstable senoritas in my day... this one named Ashura, I swear that she'd eat her young.

( Paul and Knight laughed together at this comment but their conversation was cut short as fellow GCW superstar and GCW Hard Core champion, Alex Blaq walked into the picture. His face was solemn and his eyes were fixed on

Knight. Knight's smile dropped some but he did keep an arrogant grin on his face, one that would irritate any normal person. )

Knight: I'll talk to you later Paul... ( Paul nodded and walked off, leaving just the two wrestlers. ) Que paso Alejandro ?

( Alex's expression didn't change and he did not lose eye contact for even one moment. )

Alex: I hope you know that at Warpath, it's --( Knight cuts him off )

Knight: Listen Blaq, I know that we are going into the ring at Warpath so you don't have to remind me. Quite frankly at this point in time I have no room for you in my thoughts. I have a title defense tonight, so you are going on the back burner for now. When it is our time Alex then I will give you my full concentration, but for now just keep running around with Octavian and your Mickey Mouse club.

Alex: I am growing tired of your constant disrespect towards me Knight. I am the Hard Core champion and I easily took it from the Jhub first match -- My first match against a GCW legend, NOW GIVE ME SOME DAMN RESPECT!

( Knight laughs out loud having to cover his mouth to hold in the rest which finally brings a small reaction to Blaq's face as his eyes widen. )

Knight: You beat the Jhub for the Hard Core title and all of a sudden you think you are some kind of god around here? News flash Alex the hard core title means nothing in the grand scheme of things. The Jhub? Well he has fallen greatly since the old days, and hell I never liked the guy anyways. This is what I'll do for you though Alex, see at Warpath it's already written that LeStatt Knight will STILL be the United States champion, so you might as well forget about that changing. After Warpath though I will let you in on something spectacular, see I figure that after Warpath it should be my turn to shine at the top... meaning I will be gunning for the world championship, Octavian or Jason Dread it doesn't matter to me. So when I WIN the world title perhaps I'll hand you down the U.S. Title, since you'll NEVER come close to beating me any other way.

( Knight eyes Blaq for a moment then brushes by him, but Blaq grabs him by the arm which brings an angry look on Knight's face. )

Alex: You have no idea, do you? You're just as ignorant as every god-forsaken waste of life in attendance. You have absolutely no idea what I'm capable of, Knight -- no idea at all. In fact, no one knows exactly what I am capable of, and no one knows my limits. So, listen well, Knight -- I can break you. I can shatter your entire world, break the very foundations of your life and leave you as nothing more than a wretched abominable mass of a man that once was... and you'd be called nothing more than a mere, and pitiful example.

( Knight rips his arm from Blaq's grip. )

Knight: If you're feeling froggy then jump Alex... ( Knight taps his cheek ) Hit ME ! Come on Alex, you want respect so bad? Then take your best shot but if you do know that you better knock me out or kill me, because if you don't I will do everything in my power to end your pathetic excuse for a career. So what's it going to be !?

( Knight places his arms behind his back and gives Blaq the opportunity of the first blow, but Alex just stands motionless. )

Knight: COME ON !

( Blaq did not move so Knight knowing nothing would happen just snickered. )

Knight: Just as I thought... don't come at me with threats until you are ready to deliver. See you at Warpath Blaq.

( Knight begins to walk away again, this time getting about five paces from Blaq when Alex made his final move. )

Alex: What's her name? It's Mary... right? Knight, do you know what it's like to lose the one person in your life that meant the entire world to you? Do you know how much it hurts? I'm sure you don't, but I can show you.

( Knight stopped frozen in shock that this man would stoop to that level. His fists clenched up and the sound of teeth grinding could be heard. Knight spun around with hatred in his eyes and malice in his heart. His chest heaved up and down as he took the steps to face Blaq once again. There they stood, eye to eye and nose to nose. )

Knight: I will bury you Blaq... I will bury you in the grave that you have dug yourself. Send my regards to Octavian.

( Knight went to turn around but stopped suddenly and with a quick jerk launched a right hand at Blaq which connected with his nose sending blood flying to the wall behind them. Blaq fell to one knee, then Knight sent a round house kick to the side of Blaq's head causing him to fall back, only being kept up by the wall. Knight said nothing else, only staring down at Alex for a moment before walking away, more than likely for his Title defense. The camera went to Alex's face which was covered in blood, but beneath the blood was a smile, a sinister smile that was not normal for a man who just got his ass kicked. )

Alex: Hook, line... and sinker.

Luke Hanson vs. Jonathan Borealis

Luke Hanson hits the ring to a massive chorus of boos. He steps over the top rope and glares at the ramp as Borealis’ music hits and he sprints to the ring.

Borealis slides into the ring and the match gets underway with Hanson putting the boots to him. Hanson hits some quick power moves and goes for the pin. He gets a two count before Borealis manages to kick out.

Hanson swings a heavy right hand but Borealis ducks it and connects with a back heel kick. Borealis quickly follows this up with a standing dropkick that brings the big man to his knees. This is followed by two snap kicks to the side of Hanson’s head which finally puts him on the mat.

Borealis decides to head up top and connects with a flying elbow. He rolls into the pin and gets a two count before Hanson powers out.

Borealis goes for a DDT but Hanson reverses into a hard spinebuster. Hanson whips Borealis into the ropes and hits him with a big boot. Hanson attempts the same thing but this Borealis ducks and Hanson’s boot connects with the ref instead.

Borealis attempts to take advantage with a running cross-body but Hanson counters into a powerslam.

Frustrated, Hanson heads outside the ring and retrieves a steel chair. He climbs in the ring and drills Borealis with the chair. Hanson goes for the pin but the ref is still down.

Borealis is lifted onto the top turnbuckle. Hanson moves in but is met with a brutal kick to the face. He stumbles back and Borealis takes off and connects with a Spike DDT onto the chair. H

Borealis slides the chair out of the ring and hooks the big man’s leg. The referee slowly crawls over and administers the 1…2…3!!!

Winner: Jonathan Borealis

Bryan – WHAT AN UPSET!!!

Renegade – Hanson’s gonna be pissed when he comes to.

The crowd erupts in cheers as “Rabble Rouser” blasts through the arena. Borealis is all smiles as he makes his way to the back.

A locker room door opens up. From within steps that gorgeous blond who showed up on Johnny Lexicon's arm only a couple of weeks ago. "Yayyyy" go all the guys in the crowd, because they all want to make love to 3WA's hottest vixen. Pulling the door shut, Eva fishes around in her small purse presumably looking for change as Johnathan Borealis shows up on the scene.

Borealis struts in, wearing his ring attire but still sporting his perfectly awesomely styled hair. "Yayyyy" go all the girls in the crowd, because they think they all have a chance with God's Greatest Creation.

Borealis: * whistles long an low* Hot damn sand on sand, what have we here? New candy for the old eyes? Of course, if anybody else calls your eyes "old," I'll break their fingers one by one.

Eva looks up and perks a brow.

Eva: Can I help you with something?

Borealis: No but I bet I can help you. You got a name or should I just call you sex kitten?

With slight entertainment, but with an icy chill, Eva extends her hand.

Eva: Eva VanLorne....and you are?

Borealis: The man of your dreams, Johnathan Borealis. But you, and only you, can call me Johnny.

Eva: Another Johnny?

Borealis: The ~only~ Johnny you need to know. You Lexicon's latest arm piece?

Eva: Hardly. I am a wrestler around here.

Borealis looks stunned for a moment, but then he bursts out laughing with outrageouse humor.

Borealis: YOU!? A wrestler? Baby, I seen some female wrestlers in my time. The ones that are any good at all rival Toxic in their good looks. It's only the ones that are a little fragile, and a touch reluctant to play with the big boys, that look anywhere near as good as you.

He steps closer crowding her in her doorway.

Borealis: But...you can play with me anytime. I promise I won't hurt you...

He takes off his sunglasses and winks.

Borealis: Much.

Eva does not look pleased.

Eva: Move * shoves at his chest rather harshly.*

Borealis: Relax baby, It was only a joke.

Eva smiles, the suddenly her hand snakes out with a vicious slap across his face whipping Borealis's head to the side. He shakes it off rubbing the red mark, but with a smile that shows he might be even more turned on than before.

Borealis: You have no sense of humor little lady. Why don't you stop bickering, and let Johnny take care of you.

Before he could react Eva's knee makes a solid connection with his groin taking him by surprise. Johnny yelps with pain and doubles over falling against the wall as he cups his injured crotch. Eva merely steps up to him leaning down to look him in the eyes with a smug smile.

Eva: How is that for a sense of humor? See you in the ring big boy.

Shoving his face away she straightens up and heads for the curtain area.

After a few seconds, Johnathan regains enough composure to sit up on the ground, one hand still on his crotch, and he is noticeably trying to keep his lunch down. He looks after where she left and actually has a bit of a smile on his face.

Borealis: The ring it is.

Damn...what a woman.

Trey Tremendous struts through the backstage hallways, recipient of another booming chorus of cheers from the crowd as his image appears on the MegaTron. A huddled group of crewmen, busy discussing the production of the show, pass by, but stop to offer Trey a big “congratulations.” Trey offers them a slight nod but pushes on, smiling quite happily with his ‘tremendous’ victory earlier in the night. Before long he turns the corner and collides with another large body moving in the opposite direction. Steam billows upward as a full cup of coffee goes flying across the white walls. Alex Blaq, Hardcore title around his waist, backs up a few steps, swearing loudly. Both men grimace at the searing hot liquid now covering their bodies, and Blaq is the first to react.

Blaq- What the hell was that?! Watch where you’re going, ya’ son of a bitch!

Trey cocks his head to the left, raising an eyebrow in response to the insult.

Blaq- Oh, what, you have something to say, rookie? Why don’t you make yourself useful and go get me a towel, all right?

Trey takes a step forward, a thin grin rising across his lips.

Blaq- What, you want something? You’re dealing with the Praetorian Guard right now, buddy, I think you’re a little out of your league. So why don’t you use your head and take a step back, all right?

Blaq shakes his head and turns away, brushing himself off angrily. With a conniving smile, Trey notices official Tim Vale approaching from down the hall.

Blaq- I’m getting real tired of dealing with these people…

Blaq is cut off as he feels his Hardcore title belt unsnap and fly off his body. He turns in confusion, and finds Trey holding his belt! Without warning, Trey blasts Blaq in the face! Blaq falls hard, as Vale rushes forwards to step in between the two. Before he can act, Trey is on the floor with a cover on Blaq! Vale shrugs and makes the count! 1.. 2.. 3!! Trey Tremendous is the new GCW Hardcore Champion!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! Trey Tremendous just pinned Alex Blaq!

Rollins: What a night for this kid! First he beats Toxic, then he pins the Hardcore champ? He’d better get the hell outta there, he’s gonna have some angry people looking for him!

Tremendous smiles and grabs his newly won Hardcore title belt, turning to leave. Before he does, he pauses and turns back to Blaq.

Tremendous: Sorry about the coffee.

Trey laughs as the crowd cheers mightily, and the show heads back to ringside for another title bout!

LeStatt Knight vs. Eva VanLorne

“O’ Fortuna” begins to play and the crowd erupts with joy at the sight of the current US Champion, LeStatt Knight. Trotting down the ramp he slides into the ring and hold his title belt in the air, as the crowd continues to cheer wildly. Looking across the ring at Eva, LeStatt smiles and nods towards the crowd. He turns to hand over the belt to the time keeper, and gets clobbered from behind by Eva. And the bell sounds.

LeStatt falls into the ropes, and Eva grabs one arm, Irish whipping the champion into the ropes. She checks him coming back in with a clothesline, and mounts Knight, driving several punches into his temple, before hooking the far leg and going for a pin. Knight easily kicks out after one, and Eva pulls him back to his feet by the hair. She goes for an eye rake, but LeStatt pushes her away, and into the corner.

LeStatt doesn’t continue the advantage and waits for her to come out on her own. The two begin circling each other, than lock up. Knight gains the upper hand for a moment, going into a side headlock, but Eva squirms out going for a release German suplex, but LeStatt lands on his feet. Eva turns right into a spinning heel kick from the champ sending her down to the canvas. Knight immediately heads to the top rope looking to continue the punishment as Eva gets to her feet. LeStatt comes off the top for a double ax handle, but Eva catches him coming in with a defensive dropkick right to the gut. LeStatt crumbles. Eva continues her advantage grabbing the champs legs, before dropping a knee into his groin, as the predominately male crowd groans. Hooking the leg she goes for another cover, but LeStatt again kicks out this time in two.

Eva goes to the pull Knight up again by his hair, but he catches her with a quick punch to the ribs doubling her over. Knight hooks her and drops her down with a power bomb. Knight goes for a pin, but Eva kicks out quickly. Frustrated the champ begins pulling her up by the hair, but falls victim to his own strategy, and Eva nails him with a hair shot to the berries. Yeah, as in twig and berries. Once again, the male audience clutches their junk in inarticulate fear.

Eva then up to her feet bounces off the ropes, and nails and ax kick on the doubled over champion. She goes for a pin, but again LeStatt is able to kick out. Eva is now the one looking frustrated as she heads up to the top once again. She comes off with a HUGE 450 splash, and once again goes for a cover.

1

2

3...NO! LeStatt kicked out at just the last second. Eva looks pissed as she slams her hands on the mat and begins to argue with the ref. As she’s arguing the champ is getting to his feet, albeit slowly. Suddenly, Erik VanLorne, Eva’s twin brother, comes out of the crowd. Eva turns to lock up with LeStatt, desperation leading him strength LeStatt sends her into the ropes where Erik is waiting with a chair. Erik cracks Knight across the back with the chair, and he stumbles forward right into a DDT from the challenger. The official immediately calls for the bell, and this match is over, but Eva has made her point!

Winner: No Contest

(We are looking down an empty hallway backstage in the arena; at the far end we can hear some commotion. Barreling down the hallway runs Mr. Danger, obviously still being pursued by Jason Dread from earlier in the evening! Danger runs past the camera and down a parallel hallway. Seconds after Danger disappears, Dread is seen chasing behind him! He stops at the end of the hallway, faced with the decision of going either in the direction Danger went down, or the opposite way. Stuck at his 50/50 chances, a man slowly walks down from the hallway Danger just came from. Upon seeing him, Dread immediately grabs him by his shirt collar, pushing him against the wall. His voice comes out determined and raspy.)

Dread – Danger...where is he?!

(The poor man in question is obviously scared out of his life. He tries to speak, but no words come from his mouth as the much larger man's hands are nearly around his throat. Dread takes a moment in to notice how he's interrogating the man and releases him from his grasp.)

Dread – Let me try this again, did you see a man run down that way at all?

(The man struggles at first, but chokes out a reply.)

Dread – Yeah, he was running towards outside I think...the loading docks more than likely.

(That was all Dread needed to hear. Without another word to the man he bolts down that direction in hot pursuit. The camera fades on the scene.)

The Jhub vs. Alex Blaq

The crowd jumps to their feet as Jhub makes his appearance from the curtains. Making his way to the ring he slaps hands with a few fans but just as he makes it half way down the ramp, Alex Blaq charges down and nails Jhub with a forearm to the back of the head. Jhub goes tumbling forward and Blaq goes on the offensive. Jhub turns around and walks right into a kick to the gut which doubles him over. Blaq drops to one knee and delivers a sharp uppercut to Jhub’s face, sending him to the floor holding his face. Blaq quickly grabs Jhub by the head and brings him to his feet.

Blaq swings a right hand but Jhub blocks it and strikes with a punch of his own, drawing cheers from the capacity crowd. Blaq tries to drive a boot into Jhub’s gut but Jhub catches the attempted kick, quickly Jhub spins Blaq around then leaps forward taking Blaq down with a thunderous clothesline. Jhubin leaps to his feet and screams at the crowd who stand and cheer for their hero. Jhubin knows to not waste too much time and goes to lift Blaq to his feet.

Jhubin connects with a few jabs to the chin before grabbing Blaq by the wrist going to throw him into the apron but Blaq reverses slamming Jhub back first into the ring apron. The force causes Jhub to fly forward face first into the mats. Blaq leaps into the air and drives a leg onto the back of Jhubin’s neck. Blaq now mounts Jhub and begins firing away with a barrage of right hands each doing considerable damage.

The crowd boo’s Blaq as he gets to his feet once again, but not without bringing The Jhub with him. Blaq drives an elbow to the side of Jhub’s head, then a second, and a third. Blaq smiles as he sees Jhub fight back the urge to scream in pain. Blaq now whips Jhub face first into the steel post, a sickening thud is heard as Jhub’s face connects with the steel and his body falls to the floor.

Blaq stalks the Jhub who quickly reaches all fours. Blaq sends a boot to the ribs of Jhub who is turned over by the force of the kick and lays sprawled out on his back now. Blaq now places a boot on Jhub’s throat and brings all his weight on that one foot, choking the life from Jhub, whose body flails around wildly. Blaq’s face remains emotionless as he unleashes this brutal attack on the GCW legend. Blaq now lifts a boot and drives it into Jhub’s gut, causing all the air to leave his body.

Blaq now reaches down and lifts Jhub’s almost lifeless body up and tosses him into the ring. Blaq doesn’t follow though, Alex instead goes to one knee and begins digging around underneath the ring, pulling out two steel chair and two long chemical lights. Blaq slides them into the ring, then follows suit. Jhub has now pulled himself to one knee. Alex lifts one chemical light and rushes Jhub, Blaq swings but misses as Jhub dives out of the way. Jhub amazingly thrusts his body forward and connects with a diving right hand which forces Blaq to drop the chemical light.

Jhub picks him self up again and drives another right hand into Blaq’s face which sends him back into the ropes. Jhub rushes forward, taking Blaq’s arm then tossing him to the ropes, Jhub ducks under a clothesline attempt then leaps into the air with a flying shoulder tackle, taking Blaq off his feet much to the delight of the crowd. Both men get to their feet rather quickly but it’s The Jhub who gets the first blow but Blaq fires back with a punch of his own. Jhub goes for a hay maker, but Blaq ducks under it sending Jhub swirling around, so swiftly Blaq goes in and hooks Jhub’s arms. After a few moments of struggle, Blaq lifts Jhub off the mat and slams him back first onto one of the two chairs with a Full nelson slam!

Jhub grabs at his back as he rolls in pain. Blaq spits in Jhub’s direction before going to pick him back up. Blaq drives a knee into his stomach then he hooks Jhub’s head, before lifting him up into a suplex, but instead of falling back he holds the Jhub’s body there for a few moments, a true show of strength, before coming down with a vicious brain buster. Blaq rolls over and goes for the cover.

1 … 2…

The Jhub kicks out, and the crowd goes crazy! Blaq merely nods his head as if expecting such and outcome. Blaq climbs to his feet and immediately goes after a steel chair, then he waits for the Jhub. The crowd screams at the Jhub to look behind him as he climbs to his feet not knowing that Blaq was ready to pounce. When Jhub reaches his feet he slowly turns around to see an oncoming Blaq, out of instinct Jhubin leaps into the air with a drop kick sending the chair backfiring into the face of Blaq!

Both men tumble to the mat and the crowd explodes with cheers. Blaq seems out, and Jhub slowly crawls to the ropes to begin his ascent to his feet. Blaq begins moving soon after, climbing to his feet as well. After much cheering and a few moments both men reach their feet, both a bit groggy. Jhub is the first to swing but his attempt is caught by Blaq who then pulls Jhub forward for a short armed clothesline, but Jhub ducks under that and hooks Blaq from behind in a falling reverse DDT !

Jhub immediately gets to his feet with new found energy, and the crowd alive and well. Jhub then walks around Blaq’s body and lifts his legs up then hooks him into the sharp shooter! Blaq screams in pain and the referee is there to make sure that Blaq taps out. Blaq continues to shake his head no and every time he does, The Jhub pulls harder.

The fan’s cheers turn into boo’s as they see Luke Hanson leap from the crowd and slither into the ring where the Jhub can’t see him. Luke looks down and picks up the two chemical lights then winds up and shatters them on the back of Jhub’s head, the impact sounded almost like an explosion! Blood began to pour from the back of Jhub’s head. Hanson pulls Blaq back to his feet and screams some orders to him.

Blaq picks Jhub’s motionless body up and places his head between his legs. Blaq then muscle’s Jhub’s body up and leaps up and drives Jhub down head first into a piledriver. Blaq then covers The Jhub and Hanson forces the referee down to make the count.

1…2…3!

Winner: Alex Blaq

(Our view cuts to that of outside of the arena. A quick pan around shows it is the loading docks to where Dread was chasing Mr. Danger earlier. We hear the pants of Danger as he runs down the docking area to escape Dread. Without seeing him, we hear Dread's voice.)

Dread – Why don't you just give up, Danger?! You know I'm going to find you...I guess you haven't had enough the past two times I've been in the ring with you? This time I'm going to break something, just like Octavian tried to do to me!

(Danger is cornered, surrounded by the concrete docking area. Through the light fog from outside, we see Dread slowly step into our view, Danger clearly in his sight. He walks methodically towards Danger, a wry smile across his face.)

Dread – And you had better believe that I'm going to enjoy every minute I have with you. I've grown sick of these fucking games...now it's your turn to pay.

(Dread is but mere feet from Danger at this moment, who in self-defense throws a fist straight at Dread! It might have hit its mark if Dread had not deftly caught it in his right hand. The smile on Dread's face never wanes as he tosses the World title that was on his shoulder off to the side. In a lightning-quick move, Dread kicks Danger hard in the gut, doubling him over! As Danger struggles for air, Dread grabs him hard around the throat! Danger struggles even more so now, the tightening hand not feeling so great.)

Dread – What to do first, you little rat? How about I start with touching my fingers together through your throat?

(The smile on Dread's face now leaves, and is replaced with a scowl and his hands tighten even further. Seconds pass as the only sounds we hear is Mr. Danger struggling for air against the pain. As Dread rears back his opposite hand to lay a fist directly into the man prone in his grasp, a large object falls from above the two of them, missing Dread's head by merely inches!!! Dread's drops Danger, looking downwards at what barely missed him, as we do the same.

It was a cinder block!

Dread looks upwards to the overhead on the dock, and looking over, smiling, stands Octavian! By this time Danger has regained some of his bearing as has run away from Dread, leaving him staring in utter shock as Octavian cackles like a madman!)

Octavian – You've never the upper hand, Jason...I prove that once again. If I wanted to I could have just killed you right now, but that's not my plan...at least, not for the moment.

(Dread, unable to speak, grabs the World title off of the ground and backs up slowly. The wide-eyed gaze as he takes in what just occurred completely uncharacteristic of him.)

Octavian – I've had every opportunity to take you out, but the real reason why I haven't is not that I've not been able to, as you've thought. It's that I'm waiting for the right...opportunity. That opportunity will present itself very soon though, Jason. I personally can't wait for it...for Warpath, I mean.

You've accepted my standing to have the Coliseum Cage match be in any format I want, and you better believe everyone can't wait to see Jason Dread versus Octavian three, with this being the end of the road for one of us. Look at it this way – I'm perfectly healthy, have everything I need on my side, and am devising the match which will ultimately become your gravesite...unless you make the right decision and hand over the GCW World Title to me.

Then and only then will you be saved from a certain death by my hand.

(Dumbfounded as the pieces fall into place, Dread merely looks towards the World title in his hand, and to the overhang at Octavian once more. Behind Dread, a car is driving towards him. Octavian watches as the automobile, now discernable as a limo, pulls up behind Dread and parks with the passenger door towards him.)

Octavian – I told you that I'm a man who always gets what he wants all the way back in our Establishment days, Jason. If you don't hand the World Title over to me before that bell rings at Warpath, then I'll have won anyways. All my waiting will have paid off, since I'll dismantle you deftly while all eyes are upon us at Warpath.

I can't lose, Jason, and that look on your face clearly expresses to me just how right I am.

(Octavian looks past Dread and at the limo.)

Octavian – I see your ride is here that I called for. I'm not entirely heartless, and even commissioned the local limo company to give you a lift back to your hotel room. I figure you'll need a little bit of thinking about the right decision to do before you manage to squeak out an answer for me.

(The limo driver holds open the door to the car, awaiting Dread to enter. Jason takes one last look at Octavian, then enters the limo without another word being said. The drive returns to the front of the car, and drives away from the dock with Dread. The limo fades out into the distance, turns a corner, and completely disappears from our current view.)

Octavian – Gaius Octavian ALWAYS wins...

(His voice trails off in the nighttime sky, but is followed closely by laughter...)
