Yutaka Takahashi Biography Real Name Yutaka Takahashi Nicknames Panda Bear, Yuko, Taka Hometown Tokyo, Japan Birthday Unknown Height 6'2 Weight 251 Other Feds AW World Heavyweight Champion (x1) Other Fed Awards Roleplay of the month: December Strengths Powerhouse, Technical, and Hardcore Wrestling Style Powerhouse-Technical Strategy Strategy -Yutaka is a bigger athlete and likes to use his power and size as an advantage -He is a Powerhouse and technical sytle to get over in his match ups. He uses an array of different slams and clotheslines on his opponents, wearing them out. As well as minor submission moves to dim his opponent down. -Despite his size, Yutaka is a pretty fast competitor. -He tries to keep a quick pace with the match -He doesn’t usually go onto the turnbuckle much but can perform diving shoulderblocks off the turnbuckle -Yutaka maybe bad but he don't cheat at any point in the match. He may have some outside help from Yoshiyuki but he will not perform dirty moves during the match up. Yoshiyuki uses a serious chain-style wrestling at the mid-point of the match.
