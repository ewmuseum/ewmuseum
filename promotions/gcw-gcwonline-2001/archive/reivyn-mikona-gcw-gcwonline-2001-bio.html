Reivyn Mikona Biography Real Name Reila Vynesa Mikona Nicknames Thunderfoot Hometown Tokyo, Japan / Ft. Lauderdale, Florida Birthday 8-15-1986 Height 6'1' Weight 140 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Reivyn, a partial Seminole Indian/African breed of girl, grew up in Ft. Lauderdale with her rich, Casino owner father, Cody Mikona. She has always been known as a bit of a brainiac, landing Valdictorian of her high school, and a big time scholarship to Harvard. Always a bit of a health nut, her father enrolled her in various martial arts forms growing up, to help keep her in top form, as well as being able to defend herself. Several things untold happened around her seventeenth year of life, things to eventually be explained. Soon after, Reivyn had become intrigued by a few things she had witnessed in the so called 'Underground', one of those things being fighting. After a few back alley, amateur fights, that brought in a few dollars,(that a mega millionaire's daughter doesn't need.) she soon found herself enlightened about 'The X'. While waiting to take her chance in college, this young Daddy's Girl has decided to go a bit against her formal grain, and give a shot at The X. During her time with The X, Reivyn became associated with The Saikyo System, and a younger member by the name of Lotus, who became her partner in the ring. After the closure of The X, Rei continued to work out of public with the S-Unit, and now, few years later, her reason for turning up in professional fighting is highly unexplained. Other Feds The X Other Fed Awards The X Tag Champion (With Lotus) Wrestling Style Martial Artist/Submisisonist Finisher Move Th'DreemKetchr Finisher Description Reivyn lands a hard kick to her opponents ribs, doubling them over. She quickly follows it up by jumping and hitting a double knee to the face, while slamming a double elbow into the back of their head. Finisher Setup Move Tha Sur13 Setup Description A quick succession of 12 medium to weak kicks and elbows, ended with a 13th strike that is a jumping axe kick to her opponents shoulder. Other Moves Trademarks: HardKor! - Managing to get her weight in on a smaller, or larger-but-weary opponent, Rei locks her victim into a muay thai clench, working them back into the corner as she begins to brutalize their legs and mid-section with a flurry of knees, anc shin-kicks to their lower leg. At first sign of possible breaking, or too much assault, she'll attempt to finish the move with a jumping knee to the face. The Skullbreaker - Catching her opponent kneeling/sitting/slumped, Rei will make a short sprint and leap at her opponent, attempting to grasp at their head and draw it into a flying knee. S'called Aikido, Bitch! - Managing to counter any punch against a forward moving opponent, Rei will pull them against their weight and employ a wrist throw. Barely giving them time to hit the ground, she stomps a foot into their ribcage and then drops upon the arm, wrapping her body around it like a snake in an attempt for an arm-lock submit. Rufie Colada: Face-up guillotine choke, with repeated knees to the sides and midsection. Basics: 1. Shin kicks - Various parts of the body, preferrable the thighs and mid-section. Head kicks are usually reserved for a weary, or off-guard/inexperienced opponent. 2. Knees - Reivyn will employ the use of her knees as probably her second most used tactic. working primarily on the legs and mid-section, she'll never pass an attempt for a good knee to the head. 3. Elbows - Used primarily for distraction, Rei will often use her elbows along with quick jabs to distract her opponent, setting them up for kicks and knees. 4. Spinning Back Kick 5. Axe-Kick - A personal favorite kick for Rei, who was a huge fan of Andy Hug. This is probably her most devestating single kick, putting out an extremely high KO to survival ratio. 6. Sweep-Kick - A move often employed against larger, more powerful opponents, who Rei would prefer to kick while down, or to get into a submission-war with. 7. Basic Counter-throws - A valued technnique of Reivyn's is to use her opponents momentum against them, and she will often attempt to trip up/hip-toss/arm-lock and flip and aggressive opponent. 8. Small-joint locks - Rei will often employ locks against fingers, toes, wrists, ect. against her opponents, who are usually larger than she it. She has no qualms with breaking said bones if an opponent doesn't submit, and will only attempt to further the attack if said bones do become damaged. 9. Rope-rebound kicks - Reivyn will often use the ropes or turnbuckles as a means to put some spring into her kicks. But rarely goes up-top for a power move unless desperate and clawing for a means to win. Entrance Music Crash / Concrete by Theatre of Tragedy
