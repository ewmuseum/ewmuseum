Rikki Roxx Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Maestro of Metal, The Maharajah Of Rock, The Heir Of Hardcore, The DragonEater Hometown West Hollywood, California Birthday March 17, 1978 Height 6'2 Weight 190 lbs. Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards Your Rock God. Biography Growing up in Detroit Rock City, Michigan listening to the greatest music God ever made... HEAVY F'N METAL, a young Richard Weisenheimer made it his dream. No, his fate to become one of the greatest metal singers ever to grace the microphone. The greatest bands of all time... bands like Poison, Night Rider, Motley Crue, Whitesnake, Cinderella, and The Scorpions... became his inspiration. He would be like them one day. He would have all the fame. During a performance in a night club in Los Angeles, California in his band DragonEater... the man now known simply as Rikki Roxx's life changed forever. Right before intermission a man screamed five words that are nothing short of blashpemy. "Heavy Metal Is For Faggots." Sending Rikki into a blind rage, he lept into the audience... starting a riot that shut down the club for the better part of the week and putting his name on the front of every tabloid in the greater L.A. area. After a couple hours in prison (where he was bailed out by a mysterious woman who would only go by "mother"), all the charges were dropped and Rikki Roxx became an overnight bad boy sensation. Rock & Roll all night, and party everyday. That was his motto, he was living the high life... and who had the balls to tell him any different? He did start the biggest riot of the last 4 months of 2006. It was in a dream that Rikki saw the next door in his life open before him, and it was almost as if The Heavy Metal Gods themselves spoke to him. The next step, is to show the entire world that heavy metal isn't dead. Now a member of Global Championship Wrestling, Rikki is taking his High Notes, killer riffs, and pounding bass to your homes. Where he is out to show you that not only will Heavy Metal never die. He will show everyone.. Heavy Metal is NOT only for faggots. Other Moves Totally rocking out. Entrance Music Hard Rock Hallelujah by Lordi Ring Entrances HARD. ROCK. HALLELUJAH. HARD! ROCK! HALLELUJAAAAAAAAHHHH! "Hard Rock Hallelujah" by Lordi echoes over the arena as the fans rise to their feet... then head to the aisles and towards the concession stand. As they head up the stairs to get their refreshments they miss a huge light show going on at the entrance under the MegaTron. Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, and Orange lights alternate along with matching lasers as smoke fills the entrance. Right as the lyrics kick in suddenly a figure powerslide through the entrance and goes clear past the fog flashing with different colors as he holds up a metal sign on each hand with his tongue out. The saints are crippled On this sinners´ night Lost are the lambs With no guiding light The walls come down like thunder The rocks about to roll It´s The Arockalypse Now bare your soul Andrews: Coming down the aisle hailing from West Hollywood, California. Weighing in at 183 pounds... The Maestro Of Metal, The Maharajah Of Rock And Roll, The Heir of HARDCOOOOOORE, RIKKKKIIIIIIII ROOOOXXXXXXXX! Getting off of his knees Rikki Roxx looks into the crowd in appreciation of the couple dozen people in their seats still cheering for him. Holding the metal sign into the air on his right hand he heads down the aisle towards the ring. His blonde mullet flows behind him wrapped in a black headband. The lights shimmer off of his overly oiled not-so-chiseled chest under an undersized chrome studded leather jacket. Upon reaching the bottom of the aisle, The Maharajah Of Rock And Roll pumps his fist into the air with enthusiasm before jobbing... I mean jogging up the stairs and onto the apron. All we need is lightning With power and might Striking down the prophets of false As the moon is rising Give us the sign Now let us rise up in awe Rikki Roxx sighs before stepping intp the ring. With a quick run Rikki Roxx power slides across the mat on his knees before coming to a stop just before the ropes. Looking into the crowd he spots a girl in the front row cheering him on. Pointing at her he winks with a smile before using the ropes to pull himself back up to his feet. Walking over towards the turnbuckle nearest to him he climbs up to the top rope. He puts his hand like a visor over his eyes as if he's looking at every single person in the crowd moving his head back and forth. Putting his arms into the air he salutes the crowd with the double devil horns before he bangs his head for a minute to the rockin sounds of Lordi. They are now prepared for what everyone loves to see. A car wreck... or the equivalent there-of in a Rikki Roxx match.
