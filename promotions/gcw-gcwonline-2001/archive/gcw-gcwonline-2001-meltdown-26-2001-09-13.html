Meltdown 26 Yakima SunDome — Yakima, Washington ---- Meltdown #26 (The GCW logo flashes on the screen, before we switch to scenes from last week’s Meltdown.) Bryan- Octavian is showing no mercy here! What’s gotten into him? Bryan- Mayhem hits the ropes, and charges Octavian! HAMMER ON! Mayhem hits a huge leaping DDT on the Commissioner, right out of the Roman Rapper!
