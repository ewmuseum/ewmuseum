Firey Passion Biography Biography Firey Passion was formed in March of 2006. It orginally consisted of Keiko Yoshimoto and Faith Von Almen in the 4WW. But Faith went out on an injury after ticking off the fhead leader of the Knightmare Brigade in Patti-Anderson McClancy. Thus Keiko inlisted the help of her best friend, lover at times, and student Hope Clarkson also known as Pain and Pleasure. Since then Keiko and Hope had been tearing up the 4WW tag team division before the doors closed. Now Firey Passion has a new goal to dominate the GCW tag division and the singles division. KEIKO'S FINSIHER MOVE: Heaven, Puragtory, Hell, and Defeat Finisher Description: The best description of this move is think of Rey Mysterio's 619 x 3. Keiko will trip her opponent into the second rope. Keiko will then bounce off the ropes from theo ther side of the ring leaping into the air when coming to the other side. She will grab the top rope and swing her body around kicking her opponent in the face and sending them to the bottom rope. Keiko will then swing around again this time using the second rope to swing her around knocking her down opponent down to the canvas. On the last swing around she will use the bottom rope to kick her foe in the face whom is now on the canvas. After the three kicks Keiko slides back into the ring on her back and does a kip-up only to jump back in a standing backwards moonsault onto her downed opponent. Each swing around the ropes symbolizes Heaven (1st spin), Purgatory (2nd spin) and Finally Hell or the last spin. From there Keiko is back on the outside of the ring. She leaps to the top rope springing off of it and lands on the attackers neck butt first. She then pins her now victims hands with her knees and school girl pins for the victory.
