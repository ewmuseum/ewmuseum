WorldWide 138 Alltel Arena — North Little Rock, Arkansas ---- WW138: Hypothetically Speaking The camera opens up on Garbage Bag Johnny, hard at work kneeling in front of his slanted desk. With one of the legs broken, the Commissioner uses some good old fashioned ingenuity to prop his desk back to normal with a metal block. GBJ: There, this oughta do it. The commissioner sits back at his desk, admiring the levelness. His usual decor of the AWC Transatlantic Title is hung up behind him, but his attention moves to a VHS tape that sits on his desk. He wears a puzzled expression on his face. GBJ: Now onto more important matters, like this footage here...but where in the hell am I going to find a VCR? Cutting edge technology my ass. Suddenly, stumbling int GBJ's office, carrying a 1990's era Magnavox VCR and a case of Sierra Nevada is none other than GCW Hardcore and Television Champion Triumph Frost. He looks into the room for a moment. Triumph Frost: Shit. Wrong room. He turns to leave... GBJ: Hey, wait a minute there, champ! You're the TV Champion, right? Any chance you got a VCR I could borrow? Triumph looks down at the hunk of ancient technology in his hand. Triumph Frost: You mean this hunk of shit? I was gonna throw it into oncoming traffic, sit back and watch the fun. If I gotta be around inbred bumfuckin' rednecks, might as well blah blah et cetera. Triumph's voice trails off, his attention drifts away. GBJ: That sounds like good fun and all, but before you do that, you mind if I used it first? Triumph Frost: Yeah, whatever. Triumph tosses it on the ground and it bounces on the carpet. He rips open the case of Sierra Nevada and pulls out a beer, placing the case on the floor, and plops down on GBJ's office couch. GBJ: Wow, a whole other side of the room I never even knew was there. GBJ examines it. There's a couch, a television, and now thanks to Triumph Frost, a VCR. GBJ roots around back behind the monitor, but after enough rooting, he throws the VHS tape against the wall. GBJ: God dammit! None of these connectors match. Garbage Bag Johnny emerges from behind the television and plops right next to Triumph Frost. He cracks open one of Frost's Sierra Nevadas without asking, using a lighter to pop off the top. The commissioner takes a swig. GBJ: You know, I don't care what anyone else says about you. Your taste buds are alright. Garbage Bag raises his bottle for a toast. GBJ: We may not be friends, but here's to being taste BUDS, what do you say? Triumph pauses for a moment, before he gives in, and shares in the toast. Triumph Frost: Fuck it. When in Nazi Germany, or however that saying goes. GBJ takes a swig of his beer and turns to his newest drinking pal. GBJ: Stop me if you've heard this one...why did the golfer wear two pairs of pants? Triumph stops mid-sip, and produces the blankest stare in the history of blank stares. GBJ: Because he wasn't a golfer at all! He was a double champion wrestler! And he needed the pants to hold up both of his belts! Clearly uncomfortable, Triumph rolls his eyes and rubs the back of his neck. Triumph Frost: Yeah. Great... Garbage Bag Johnny slaps Triumph Frost on the back, he's at his hokiest right now, for some reason. GBJ: Why, I remember the first time you came into my office, you were huffing and puffing about not having a title, and now you have so many titles, you don't know what to do with them. Betcha you wish you had two waists, huh? Well, doctors can do amazing things nowadays. Triumph Frost: You mean like those conjoined twin chicks? The ones with two heads? I saw a thing about them on TV. Holy shit, that was weird. Triumph takes a sip of the beer, and stares off into the distance. Triumph Frost: Like...really weird... He seems to be miles away. Garbage Bag attempts to reel him back in with a hypothetical. GBJ: You like movies, right? You seen the new Batman? Triumph Frost: With the English prick? GBJ: Shipley? No. Anyway, pop quiz, hotshot. Let's say you're Batman--and this is hypothetical, because you're not really Batman--and I'm the Joker, also hypothetical, and I'm all hopped up on goofy pills or whatever, and I've kidnapped the Hardcore Title and the TV Title, and you can save one of them, but the other one has to die. What do you do? What DO you do? Triumph pauses, mired in thought. He fishes out a pack of Lucky Strikes, and pulls one out and lights it up. He takes a drag... Triumph Frost: Hmm... He thinks. Ponders. Wonders. The smoke billows out from his mouth in a jet stream. Then... Triumph Frost: Fuck 'em both. I don't really care. GBJ: You'd make a pretty shitty Batman, then. Garbage Bag Johnny takes his jacket off and rests it over the couch. Underneath, he reveals that he's wearing a black and white striped vintage referee's outfit, the kind that the GCW refs used to wear before Caldera sprung for those flashy green ones. GBJ: Good thing I'm not the Joker then. I'd pants you twice, metaphorically speaking, of course. GBJ pauses for a sip and lights up a cigarette of his own. GBJ: Hey, you want me to show you how to make a pencil disappear? Triumph gives another blank stare. Triumph Frost: I hope that doesn't mean you wanna fuck me, 'cause really...that just ain't gonna happen. Garbage Bag Johnny laughs, prominently displaying his referee jersey by tugging the collar out. GBJ: I wouldn't count your chickens yet, champs. Triumph Frost: ...I have no idea what that means. Triumph makes a gun with his fingers, and simulates a shot, complete with two clicks and a wink. Triumph Frost: But whatever you say, chief. He holsters his imaginary gun, and takes a sip from the brew. Triumph Frost: So which stupid twat you got for me this time? The Indian trog? Hot Topic? Ugly Jesus? That one shitbird? The dickhole that doesn't use contractions? SuperTard?That...other shitbird? GBJ: To be honest, I don't know. That's why I was trying to watch that tape. Triumph takes another sip. Triumph Frost: Then you shouldn't have broken it, stupid. GBJ: Ah, those things are nearly indestructible. I guess, supposedly, you can see the Man in Black's real face on that footage, and even though he's completely shirking chain of command going after Hot Topic--I mean, Vivica Valentine--like that, I kind of wanted to show a token of my gratitude. Triumph Frost: Does this have anything to do with me? GBJ: I guess it could. I mean, you're the one who had the VCR. Triumph Frost: What is it you do around here, anyway? GBJ: I do a lot of things. Garbage Bag pauses, completely deadpan. GBJ: I'm not sure how to answer that. Triumph Frost: You say that like I care. GBJ: Then why'd you ask? Triumph thinks for a moment. He finally ends up just frustrating himself. Triumph Frost: You gonna tell me who I'm fighting or not? Christ... GBJ: Alright, well, I was thinking of giving it to whoever was on the tape, but I'm not going to give out title shots to Men in Black. Hell, could be you under that mask, and there's no way short of trick cameras to pull that off. Or it could be Vivica, going after herself--trick cameras and all that--and if people think you're insufferable with two belts, well, I wouldn't want to see Viv with two. Truth is, I don't know who you're facing, so if you've got any suggestions, now's the time. Triumph, meanwhile, finishes peeling the label off his bottle. He notices GBJ stops talking. Triumph Frost: Huh? Oh. Yeah, cool and whatever and shit. GBJ: You're no help. First you bring me a VCR but no RCA to coaxial adapter, then you don't have any suggestions. GBJ paces across the room, picks up the slightly damaged VCR tape. GBJ: Yet you're still here, sitting on this couch I guess I have. Why? Triumph says nothing for a moment. He grabs a fresh Sierra Nevada and cracks it open. He takes a sip, and lets another long pause linger. Triumph Frost: Don't got anything better to do. GBJ: Well, that makes one of us. The Commissioner looks down at the tape. GBJ: Wait a minute. If this is We Are Marshall, where's the surveillance footage? Triumph rises up from the couch. Triumph Frost: Whatever, not my problem. He downs the beer, and tosses it behind him, letting it shatter on the wall. Triumph Frost: Thanks for nothing, asshole. With that, Triumph exits and stuff. GBJ: God dammit. Garbage Bag shakes his head, looking at the cover of the VHS box before flipping it over and reading the description. GBJ: I heard McConaughey gives the performance of a lifetime in this one. Ah, well. Wonder what's on this television.
