Brock Shepherd Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday 4th of July, 1981 Height 6'2 Weight 265 pounds Fed Titles Coming soon! =>;P Fed Awards These are too. =8-D Biography I enjoyed the good life. Fortune. Fame. Ladies. I enjoyed it ALL! And then it was stolen away from me by Fiend. He destroyed my hand. My beautiful right hand. He took it away from me and everything changed from that moment on. In EWA I was a blue chipper. I was the Intercontinental Champion three times. That's nothing to be sneezed at. Three times. I was the biggest thing since sliced bread and then the place ran out of money paying some schmuck Ron Irvine to run the place. Some 'creative accounting' left me a bunch of other guys jobless so I wandered in seach of a place. Big Money Wrestling came and went. Wasn't my scene. Too many chiefs and not enough indians if you know what I mean. EYE? Tournaments aren't my cup of tea and they weren't paying what they were talking about paying so that's when Brock goes walking. Action! Wrestling. Finally a brand after my own heart. I was excited. I was real excited and I was put in a program with a guy on the rise, to put my name on the map and help push him along further. HardCase. That's when things went all downhill, right? HardCase hired Fiend in the storyline to try and shake me off of his tail. So Fiend comes down and is supposed to work my arm over, but the guy doesn't work my arm over. He breaks my wrist. He doesn't just break my wrist, he fucking shatters it into a million pieces. Then at the PPV I'm supposed to be in a handcuff match with HardCase. I am. And we're doing this thing and we go up on the big screen. A big work to put us both over. Me with my broken wrist in the cuff and I topple down and dangle from the top, HardCase clinging on coz he doesn't wanna drop. I'm hanging by my shattered wrist. The cuff destroys the ligaments and tendons and my hand dies there on Pay-Per-View. My hand dies. The blood flow was cut off and the nerve endings slinked into my arm and the feeling goes. The doc cuts it off. Reed Young, owner of A!W, cuts ME off. Pays me out coz he says people aren't ready for an amputee. I'm fired. Gone. But in the way of a good story the hero returns. But don't always count on a hero. Tradition tells you the quiet ones are always the ones to watch. Strengths Brock is infintely close with his move set, his confidence inside of his wrestling style is far greater than it ever has been as he's completely reconfigured it so he can be confident with it. He has an enormous amount of stamina and courage, Manager April Monday Weaknesses He has one functioning hand. He has no idea of when enough is enough. A big mouth Favours his left hand side constantly due to it being the functioning side which leaves it open as a target. Wrestling Style Southpaw Bump Taker/Brawler Strategy Ring Technique Brock is a very talented wrestler who has had to completely re-assess his style due to an injury that cost him his hand. Completely. Brock's right hand has been amputated and is currently replaced by a soft, maleable prosthetic hand so it feels just like a real hand, but it is often concealed within a black leather glove. His stlye has to change dramatically, once a powerful wrestling superstar it has taken a lot of effort for Brock to maintain strength in his right arm, though his right forearm is slowly losing strength the upper arm remains stronger than ever. Allowing him to use it somewhat in his wrestling style through using it as a support base under legs and whatnot. He cannot grapple with the prosthetic hand which has caused a major upheaval in Brock's wrestling style. Brock has a very distinctive technique now due to the hours on end of re-preparing himself to return to the ring. He likes to call himself a Southpaw Bump Taker/Brawler. a. Favourtism Obviously favouring his left hand more so, Brock still uses his right hand for strikes which has a sickening rubberiness due to the prosthesis. A brutal knife edge chop can be delivered once Brock straightens out the prosthetic. It normally remains in a fist which he uses to deliver a punch, which has no substantial affect on opponents as accuracy is a problem. His left side is by far the stronger side as he uses it constantly for his offence. This, however, is a weakness. If anyone were to work over his left-hand side Brock would be virtually powerless as the brunt of his wrestling style would be opened wide and useless seeming as though his right hand is un-useable and his right arm isn't much better aside from being able to use it for clotheslines and an elbow shot. b. Grappling Any moves which require grasping with both hands is completely out of the question. He can use his right arm to stabilise opponent's for moves. Suplexes are virtually a no-no, although he can still do the basic suplexes, they are very slow in execution and easily reversible. There are a couple suplexes that Brock can constantly and easily execute, and they're listed. A vertical suplex is slowly executed, and poorly, too. One of the simplest moves and most basic moves in a wrestler's repertoire and unable to be executed by Brock. *****NOTE: ANY DOUBLE HANDED GRAPPLING MOVES ARE OUT OF THE QUESTION!***** Most submission moves are out of the question. Below is a list of moves Brock can successfully achieve during a match and has virtually mastered them as a result of hours of training to get himself back inside a wrestling ring. Brock is particularly fond of the Fireman's Carry which he has worked hard on and is a basis for most of his moves as long as there is not too much grappling required. Double underhook are also something that Brock has made a key element to his offence c. Ring Psychology Since his accident, Brock has developed a fear of height and no longer goes to the top rope. You will find the furthest he delves is second rope aerial displays. Ladder matches are not his forte and he will try to avoid them at all costs. If Brock were to go to the top rope it would be for an extra special reason and he'd probably be shaking like a leaf. If someone were to go near him he'd probably drop down and low blow himself on the top rope out of fear. *****NOTE: TOP ROPE FOR EXTRA, UBER SPECIAL OCCASIONS ONLY!***** d. Knees Having his own disability, Shepherd relies heavily on basic yet brutal moves to try to slow down his opponents by wearing out their foundations. Their knees. Brock will focus wholly and soley on one particular knee to make sure his opponent is limping. He uses simply yet effective knee strikes to hurt the joint. He also has some power moves which would fall into the knee strike category as well as the two submission moves which he's perfected. For the record, it's easier for him to work over the right knee. e. The Bump Taker Brock is kinda like a heavyweight spot wrestler. He likes to set himself up as the underdog and will take long lasting beatings to prove his stamina and get the crowd working. His offence comes in flurries and once he has an advantage he will try to slow down the pace and begin to concentrate on the knee area. Brock WILL bleed, in fact he busts open as easily as a peach throw at a bed of nails. Blading is not an issue to Brock, he loves nothing more than giving his own blood for an audience. f. The Cheater Brock will use the blind side of the referee to his advantage. He's not so much a low blow styled guy, but if he can use anything else to his advantage he's use it. Mainly his cheating comes from his valet, who is a qualified wrestler herself. Her name is April Monday and she is not afraid to get in the ring with the big boys. Truth is, she could have a successful career of her own but that's another story for another day. The cheating mainly relies on Brock distracting the referee so April can cheap shot them with some sort of foreign object as she does not do the patent "valet-lowblow-dude" routine. She will hit them with a steel chair or something to that extent. She's been known to carry salt with her and throw it into an opponent's face on behalf of Brock. Normally toward the later stages of the proceedings. A favourite trick for the pair is replacing the prosthetic hand. April distracting the referee and Brock exiting the ring to retrieve it from under the ring apron or April throwing a replacement hand to him which is shaped into a fist and made of lead. Its simple... Brock punches them and knocks them the fuck out. He normally covers this up with a pinfall OR quickly tangling them up into a one armed ankle lock to protray they've passed out from the pain. With Brock, weapons of other descriptions is virtually a no-no. He cannot use any weapon which requires both hands. Like a chair. Or a baseball bat. He has be known to use a police stlye extendable baton and often has one handy in his bag for required events. April has been known to hide it on her person out of sight and use it also. It's a handy little device which is easily hidden and easily and quickly used. Wearing his patent neon green wrap around shades he simply bursts by her, storming the ring with a hurried pace. April follows, shouting belittling comments to fans on her way down to the ring. Brock rolls beneath the bottom rope and steers himself toward the far left turnbuckle to which he climbs, standing on the second rope he snatches off his shades and tosses them into the crowd. Folding his arms across his chest and sneering at them. If his opponent is already in the ring he'll stand on the second rope until the bell tolls. If he is first to arrive he stands in between the turnbuckles furtherest from the stage and leans back on the top rope.
