Houngan Columbus Biography Real Name Baron Columbus Hometown Jacmel, Haiti Birthday Unknown Height 6'8 Weight 335 Biography How Houngan Columbus made his way to the GCW is not entirely decided upon. Rumored to be exiled from his home country because of his radical practices of Voodoun (or voodoo), Columbus and his endeavors in black magick have left him blacklisted in various indy circuits. While main eventing a match in a flea market in Alcoa, TN, Columbus delivered his Samedi Knuckle to a local wrestling under the "Doink The Clown" guise. Columbus was disqualified later in the match for refusing to break a chinhold after the count of five. Columbus 'retaliated' by invoking a hex on the local wrestler... who keeled over and died on his way out to his car, after the show. Who trained him has yet to be answered. Columbus does not grant interviews. Dave Meltzer once wrote in a 2010 edition of the Wrestling Observer: "There are some guys who supposedly lose themselves in the gimmick. In the case of independent Houngan Columbus, wowing and frightening audiences along the southeast circuit, there might not even be a gimmick in the equation." Other Feds PHW, WTFL (defunct) Wrestling Style The power, strength, and endurance of the Undertaker blended in with the theatrics of Baron Von Raschke. Finisher Move Voodou Vice Finisher Description Whether deliberate or unexpectedly, Houngan Columbus latches his left hand onto the cranium of his opponent and squeezes with all his might either forcing them to submit instantly or to render them unconscious. WW Entrance The entrancing sound of "I Put A Spell On You" by Screaming Jay Hawkins is heard, in accordance with the lights going off. The crowd is left sitting in the dark, murmuring. A large spotlight falls on the entrance way and after a ruffling of the curtains, Houngan Columbus emerges, right arm extended holding his jar of jelly beans. He twirls the large wooden switch in his right hand effortlessly, using it to tip the bill of his top hat. From left to right, he crosses the stage, scanning the crowd, mouthing hexes, shaking his switch..swatting at thin air. Andrews: Making his to the ring, from Jacmel, Haiti! Weighing in at 330lbs... the High Priest, Houngan Columbus! Bryan: Yale: Columbus begins his descent towards the ring, the spotlight fixed on him, reflecting off his serengeti shades. Approaching ringside, he plants one last kiss on his jelly bean jar before stowing it underneath the nearest turnbuckle. He steps through the ropes, stretches his arms up high, holding the switch like a title belt, bellowing in Haitian French. Bryan: Yale: He skeptically hands the switch to the referee, as well as his top hat and sunglasses. The house lights are resuscitated. Houngan crouches down on the canvas, closes his eyes, conducting a final prayer. He punctuates it abruptly by SCREAMING at the top of his lops, and rising to his feet, fully prepared for physicality. Special Moves Chicken Bone Breaker (tilt-a-whirl backbreaker), Juju (mist to the eyes), Samedi Knuckle (heart punch) Other Moves Powerbombs and chokeslams mixed in with lariats and chinlocks. Arm wrenches. Seldom leaves his feet or mounts the top rope. Often utilizes theatrics like smoke and talcom powder.
