Bishop Steele Biography Real Name Bishop Steele Nicknames True Perfection, The Greatest Ever, Simply That Damn Good Hometown Miami, Florida Birthday 04/28/1985 Height 6'4 Weight 255 Biography Grew up with a wealthy life. His mom is a lawyer and his father is Surgical Doctor. He has always gotten everything he has wanted growing up. From a brand new car on his 16th birthday to a new house once he went to college. While in college he studied Business Management for hopes that one day he would own his own business. Once he hit his final year in college, Bishop had the drive and determination to graduate and be sucessful. Once he graduated school he used money from his parents to start his own clothing line which is known worldwide to this day. Perfection Inc has been recognized as the most illustrious clothing line for the rich and famous to date. From womens clothing to mens apparel. Bishop has turned it into a Multi-Billion dollar clothing line that has him ranked in the top 10 most richest men in the United States. Bishop has always had money and has never been ashamed to flaunt or show off what he has. He knows he is rich and lets eeveryone know about it. Later in life Bishop and his father were forced to bury his mother after she passed from Multiple Mialoma Cancer. When this happened Bishop and his father were devasted. Bishop's clothing line hit a down time, cause he wasn't able to design clothing cause his mind was twisted from the lose. A year later Bishop returrned to designing and his clothing line hit a new high out selling every major fashion in the industry. Bishop later invested his time in other activities and took up wrestling. Once he started wrestling he enjoyed it and looked to pursue a career in the sport. Now he wrestles for the GCW and is looking to make a impact in this federation, cause he already knows he will make a impact with the ladies. Other Feds HIW and WWX Strengths Great Charisma, Good technical wrestling skill and very versatile Weaknesses Showboats to much and has a big ego. Wrestling Style Technical/Grappler Strategy Usually Bishop trys to take control of the match hitting the opponent with a serious of moves to throw them off balance. Once he gets control, its just non stop action from Bishop with technical/power moves to eventually set him up for one of his devastating finishers.
