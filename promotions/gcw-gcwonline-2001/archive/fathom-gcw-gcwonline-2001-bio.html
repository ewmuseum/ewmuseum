Fathom Biography Hometown Denver Birthday 6/24/86 Height 5'11 Weight 200 lbs. Biography Fathom has fallen from grace after many years in prison. Originally a PTC superstar, Fathom was a member of the elit and competed all across the world until the fateful night he found himself on the other side of the law. What he did? Good question, one that you will never know. What you will know is that Fathom is back in the wrestling game and looking for gold and pain. His pain or yours, that is up for discussion. Want to know more? So does everyone... "Take me dowwwwn, six underground..." "Six Underground" resonates throughout the arena, fans portraying the mixed reaction which is expectant for Fathom. Seconds pass, growing the anticipation of the world before the enigmatic steps through the curtains to the indifferent fans giving him praise and hate throughout the arena. Announcer: Coming to the ring at this time, from Denver, Cooooooloraaaaaado, weighing in at 200 pounds and standing 5-foot-11-inches, this is Faaaaaathooooooom! His eyes look around, locking onto certain fans as his snarl matches his general attitude as he walks down the path towards the ring. He shakes his hands and stretches his neck in preparation for the onslaught of the match, sliding into the ring and stepping onto the middle turnbuckle to look out at the crowd, still menacing the fans in the audience. He drops off the turnbuckle, turning to land and face the referee who begins patting him down.
