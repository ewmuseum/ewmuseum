Michael Stevens, NC-17 2005), GCW Hall of Fame inductee (2007), GCW Grand Slam Champion, Dangerous Games 2008 Winner Biography LeStatt Knight or known to some as Roberto Abitua was born November 10th 1979 in Mexico City, Mexico. At birth his mother died leaving her husband with the pain of raising Roberto and his twin brother Damen. Not able to handle the pressure and the pain of losing his wife, Roberto's father blamed the children for his wife's death and shot himselff in the hospital. The two children were separated from there, Roberto being sent to Spain to live with his wealthy uncle Juanito and Damen being sent to the United States to live with their grandmother. Roberto was brought up in a nice home with all the things any child could ask for, loving parents, good schooling, and toys like you wouldn't believe. It was when he was very young that Roberto began learning to speak different languages and different forms of martial arts as well as amateur wrestling. When Roberto was fourteen he met Cynthia Martinez, a young and beautiful girl who shared the same school as he did. They dated for close to three years before Cindy became pregnant when the couple were 17 years old. Knowing their families wouldn't accept this other wise the young couple got married, a few months later they had their daughter, Starr Abitua. When Starr was less than two years old a tragic car accident took her life along with Cindy's. Roberto fled from Spain after this on a trail that led him to a twin brother he never knew he had. Damen was a polar opposite of Roberto, into drugs, gangs, etc. and Roberto with his money and ability joined right in. The two thought they were invincible until a Drug Lord in Detroit, Michigan forced Roberto to watch his brother die. Roberto went into hiding aftter that not to be seen for over a year. It wasn't until a man helped Roberto learn to harness the aggression and rage built inside of him. This is when Roberto met his long time friend David Guerrero AKA RaZiel. It was RaZiel who brought Roberto into wrestling getting him in with an upstart promotion called Global Championship Wrestling under the name of Khris Krowe, a masked entity with very few words. His career began very rocky in GCW under the Krowe name and soon Roberto fought hard to get a makeover of sorts. At Dangerous Games 2001 he lost the mask and entered the famous Battle Royal as LeStatt Knight. Although Roberto nor RaZiel felt Roberto got the fair treatment he deserved they built on the GCW experience and went to prosper in other places such as XCW, 3WA, and even becoming World Champion in Dragon Wrestling Association after beating his most hated rival, Alex Grey, known in the GCW world as PAYNE. Roberto has learned many lessons in his lifetime and although he is only 27 years old, his eyes tell a much older story. Roberto has learned to love since the tragedy that came down onto his family, falling for the controvertial Typhoid Mary. The two have since had their first child together, a son named Joaquin Roberto Abitua. Although He has been in a few other promotions throughout his time in wrestling, Roberto has always thought of GCW as his home. Not only is it where he got his start but it's also where he found his greatest villain (turned greatest ally). Since returning to GCW in 2005 the name "LeStatt Knight" has become more than anyone ever could have imagined. In the two years since GCW's reopening Knight has become nothing short of a legend, holding the US Title for (at the time) a record setting time, two time World Champion and has been inducted into the GCW Hall of Fame. He has notched wins against the best GCW has had and is now considered perhaps the most dominate entity in GCW's history. Knight was a part of the most prolific version of Steven Caldera's Establishment ever seen with members Rich Rollins, Octavian and Branson King. For all of their star power however many saw Knight as the heart and soul of the group. He has been GCW's rock, through the good times and the bad. The group has his disbanded because of Rollins' death and Caldera's bringing aboard Jay Terror and immediately giving him a World Title shot. Knight saw this as a slap in the face/disrespect and left The Establishment. Although he has not had his hands on the World Title in almost two years it remains his main goal. After feuds with Jay Terror, Chris Storm and most recently Andy Murray, LeStatt has finally gotten back to where he believes that he belongs ... In the hunt for the World Championship. After winning the 2008 Dangerous Games Battle Royal, Knight will face off against newly crowned World Champion, Jordan White in the Main Event of NC-17, a place Knight is very accustomed to. Since GCW's rebirth in 2005, Knight now will headline 3 of the 4 NC-17 events that have been held and the other was a co-main event in an unforgettable match with Jay Terror. Also with the win at DG, Knight has basically accomplished every feat in GCW. He's the first Grand Slam Champion, Hall of Fame and now DG winner. Now with NC-17 in the books LeStatt Knight's illustrious career has gone to yet another new height. It was a night of firsts for the Hall of Famer; the first time he beat Jordan White, the first time he won the GCW World title at NC-17. From there he went on to battle his most imposing challenger yet, Andy Murray. The two began a rivalry at WW100 that soon spilled over once Knight became the champion. Knight would win a match over The Admiral of Awesome at Shockwave (09) but would fall to the challenger at Rampage, starting a Title reign for Murray like none GCW has ever witnessed. Knight would disappear again for some time, some speculating that the Hall of Famer had called it quits. Dangerous Games however proved he wasn't finished as Knight returned. His comeback was spoiled by an unlikely person however as GCW fan favorite, Clyde Fox, eliminated Knight in quick fashion. What followed was one of the most unusual pairings in GCW history. Knight and Clyde Fox would feud for months and after alot of pranks and scare tactics the two would collide at NC-17 VII where Knight would walk out victorious over a very game Fox. At NC-17 long time GCW owner, Steven Caldera stepped down and handed the company over to a very unlikely person, Garbage Bag Johnny. In his first show as the head man in GCW, GBJ announced a stunning Main Event of defending World Champion, Andy Murray who was fresh off a heated and personal battle with former lover Vivica Valentine. The Champ would defend his title against the man he took the belt from: LeStatt Knight. In a fast paced and explosive match, LeStatt Knight managed to put an end to Murray's impressive run at the top and capture his record breaking FOURTH GCW World Heavyweight Championship. Somewhere, Octavian is crying. Coming out from his record breaking run, a new challenge emerged ... The desire to call it quits. The greatest superstar in GCW's history, decided to retire at the top of his game, to the shock of the wrestling world. The one who it shocked the most? Vivica Valentine, one of Knight's most hated rivals. Valentine refused to allow LK to ride off into the sunset, demanding one final match. It took some convincing and some vicious attacks but eventually, Knight agreed to meet Valentine at Shockwave 2010. To her surprise, Knight vacated the World Title the week before their match just to spite Vivica. In the end, the match was as legendary as one would expect from two of the best to ever lace em up in GCW. Valentine fought valiantly and viciously but, as usual, Knight was one step ahead and he toppled Valentine to win his final match in GCW. LeStatt Knight is gone. But his legacy remains and inside of GCW's walls, it'll always remain alive. Other Feds DWA, 3WA, XCW Other Fed Titles DWA World Heavyweight Champion, 3WA North American Champion Other Fed Awards DWA Hall of Famer, DWA Wrestler of the Year (03) Strengths Extremely Powerful and even though he is a rather large man, hes still stronger than he looks, Incredibly high pain tolerance (evidence by his having his arm disloacted and still winning a match), can move around very well for a man as large as he is. Weaknesses Although he is beginning to learn more mat wrestling it is still his weakest link. The key to defeating Knight is to wear him down on the mat because if you attempt to take it to high impact moves or a standing brawl ... not a good choice.
