That moment of distraction almost costs her, as Shaman shoots forward into a kick to the midsection. He hooks a front facelock, and lifts Pierce vertical in a delayed suplex. JB and Dave mention the Desert Spike here, but Alex lowers her knee into his head, once and then again, landing on her feet behind him for a desperation hangman's neck breaker. Both of them lie in the ring as Briggs makes his count. Speculation becomes whether Alex Redding would advance to face the Azuma-Aaron Fujita winner without having to compete. As if on cue, there comes the Willing Villain, wandering down to ringside. He sets up a chair in the aisle, taking a seat. He leans back, crossing his legs at the ankle, as James Bryan grumbles about the potential for Redding to pick his own opponent. The Vanguard champ answers the referee's count at seven, and pulls Alexandra to her feet. A hard Irish whip puts the Spider into the corner. He follows in immediately, burying her with a clothesline that engulfs the much smaller woman. She slumps against the ropes, and he takes her arm again, firing her across the ring, and this time he's a hair slower in following, trading immediacy for power. She floats over this time, catching the big man as she passes with a lungblower. She drapes an arm over his chest, but the count doesn't even hit two. Shaman rolls over onto his stomach. Shaw manages to position herself in his line of sight, ducking low to deliver a mocking Native American chant. That earns her a swipe from the big man's hamhock fist, which probably doesn't come nearly close enough to merit such a loud squeal. Alex gets up to her feet, slingshotting herself over the top into a legdrop across his back. Shaman is left draped over the apron, and Alex slides off onto the floor. Her pump kick catches him on the side of the head, and the momentum leaves him hanging upside down. She cinches his head, and delivers a hangman's DDT to the floor. Kathryn pulls up the apron skirt (the same one she was checking earlier in the night, as a matter of fact), and the two women pull a table from underneath the ring. It takes them both to slide it into the ring, and Shaman has already gotten back to his knees when Alex circles back his way. She bounds off the steps to hit the kneeling Vanguard champion with a headscissors, but his massive power comes into play again as he blocks the attempt, coming up to her feet with Pierce still on his shoulders, and he hits a thunderous powerbomb onto the apron. This would be where we get a lot of "Good Lord, she's been broken in half" type commentary, and we get a shot Alex Redding, no longer chilling in that chair, but leaning intently forward. Shaman rolls his opponent into the ring, draping himself exhaustedly over her. He gets one. He gets two. She gets her foot on the bottom rope. Enraged, Shaman rips her off the mat, hooking a front facelock and hefting Alex vertically -- time for the Desert Spike to end this. He never gets her vertical for the suplex, as she manages to drop behind him with his head caught and falls backwards to hit a flatliner that has the Vanguard champ briefly airborne. Exhausted to the point of being insensate, it takes Desade a moment to crawl over to him, hooking a tree trunk leg. He kicks out at two and seven-eighths, to the shock of the crowd and the dismay of Kathryn Shaw at ringside, who immediately begins rooting under the ring again. Alex sits up, the only sign of her frustration coming in the form of a single smack of the mat before she lifts her hands to sweep sweat-soaked hair out of her face. She pivots to her feet, lifting the table to prop it up in the corner. Shaw, meanwhile, has pulled on a pair of yellow rubber gloves straight out of a 1950s dishwashing soap ad, and she uses them to handle a coil of barbed wire that you'd think was a live snake the way she's treating it. The involvement of the table and the barbed wrie pulls Redding to his feet, and Alexandra spares him a glare before she turns back to her opponent. She receives a brief, cheeky wave in response, fingers waggling. She pulls up the Vanguard champion by his hair, perhaps losing herself in that moment, as she attempts to Irish whip him corner-to-corner to drive him through the table. Unsurprisingly, he reverses the move, but she's able to slide to a halt before she hits the table, and when he follows, she puts him face-first into the thing with a drop toe hold. The wood cracks but does not break, and Alex is off like a shot, again bounding to the top rope and again running it, this time meaning for a weirdly angled frog splash to put him through. Shaman moves aside at the last minute, and Pierce plummets through the broken table in an explosion of debris. Shaman pulls her out of the wreckage, and up into a standing headscissors. He yanks her up into a powerbomb. The first one thuds against the mat, then he deadlifts Pierce again, pitching her into the refuse that used to be a table. Grabbing the Spider's husk by the ankle, he yanks her into the middle of the ring, hooking a leg. He gets one. He gets two. Shaw blatantly and willfully interjects herself, sliding into the ring and clobbering Josh Briggs with what might be charitably called a diving forearm. There's nothing Briggs can do -- hardcore matches are by their very nature no disqualification -- but that doesn't mean she faces no repercussions, as a hot-tempered Vanguard champion slides out of the ring after her to give chase. The pursuit is short-lived -- long strides plus perilously high heels are a losing proposition -- and it ends in the ring with the vicious Native American yanking Kathryn to her feet by her tawny brown hair. He slaps on a front facelock, literally yanking her out of her shoes as he pulls her vertical for the delayed, stalling suplex that begins the Desert Spike. Unfortunately, this leaves him wide open when Alexandra gets to her feet, and she saves her frenemy with the flying double-knee facebuster she calls the Spider's Kiss. The rescued brunette is rolled from the ring by our referee as the Spider bounds over the debris and up to the top, unwilling to presume that the Kiss would keep Shaman down. She calls her layout moonsault double stomp the Stake Through The Heart, and it ISA thing of beauty. What follows after is not; she pivots up to her knees, clutching her ribs as she reaches for the wire. She wraps it around Shaman's leg and up to his head to assist in her STF. Still, the Vanguard champion hold on, even with the wire in his mouth. It isn't until Alexandra adds a bit of broken table pressed against his eye socket that the big man is left with no choice but to tap. As the beleaguered Briggs goes to raise the weary Medusa's hand, Shaw shoves him aside to do it herself. In the aisle, the Willing Villain has folded up his chair, and he bangs it against the steps to get her attention. Pierce looks away from her handiwork, and any regret on her face fades as Redding climbs to the apron. The two exchange words, and Shaw circles around to prop open the ropes. Even with the weapon in hand, Redding thinks better of it, hopping back down. As "Wars" blares again to close the show, we get a shot from the ground as Alex climbs to the middle turnbuckle, blood literally on her hands but one step closer to her prospective match with Terrence Kingsley.
