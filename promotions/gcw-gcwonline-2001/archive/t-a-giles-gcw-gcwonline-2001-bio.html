T.A. Giles Biography Real Name Travis Giles Nicknames T.A., Giles, The Adept Hometown Brandon, Canada Birthday April 8, 1975 Height 6'2 Weight 195 lbs Biography What is known of T.A. Giles is rather scarce. He grew up in a rural city in Manitoba and, for unknown reasons, left when he was twenty-something for New York. There, he would meet the future "one that got away (but didn't)," Natalie, a girl who would become the mother of his daughter. While Natalie and T.A. never married and are no longer romantically involved, they lived together -- in a state of high tension, nonetheless -- for the sake of Jessica, their daughter. For a ten year period, T.A. worked at a downtrodden gym in New York City, training with a variety of amateur wrestlers and visiting professionals while longing to be one himself. Being accepted into GCW would be his first opportunity to wrestle professionally.
