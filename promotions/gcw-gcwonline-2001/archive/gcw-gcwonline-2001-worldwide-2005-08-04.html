Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #19 – Boston, Massachusetts

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Boston crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “NEW CHAMPION!” and “Spitfire 4 Prez” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Boston, Massachussetts” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We're back once again in the packed Fleet Center in Boston for Global Championship Wrestling's WorldWide, and we're just days removed from one of the greatest broadcasts in GCW history! Hello, everyone I'm James Bryan back here at ringside with The Jhub and Pete, and gentlemen, last week we witnessed one of the most incredible single events in memory!

Jhub: Well, aside from the fact that I didn't win Dangerous Games, I can't disagree with you, JB. Top to bottom, that was the most exciting event I've ever taken part in, and though I may be just a bit bitter about the whole damn thing, I can't help but be happy I was there.

Bryan: Fans, we hope you didn't miss it, because Dangerous Games ended up destroying everyone's expectations and turned into one of the most special shows we've ever produced. And tonight we've got some huge new stories developing, as we've got a new number one contender! Twenty-nine men and one woman entered the ring right here in Boston, but only one survived! That man was Michael "Spitfire" Stevens, who is next in line for a shot at the GCW Champion!

Pete: And it pains me to admit that we have a NEW Champion in GCW, guys. Despite an amazing contest and one of the most brutal bouts I've ever seen, LeStatt Knight got Jason Dread to say "I Quit" and captured the GCW Title.

Bryan: In case you missed it, fans, LeStatt Knight pulled out all the stops, and I can't even bring myself to utter what happened. But the fact remains that, after months of domination, LeStatt Knight is at the top of Global Championship Wrestling.

Jhub: Well, we still had a lot of questions regarding LeStatt going into the match, and we sure as hell have plenty more, but we learned just how sick and twisted this SOB really is. And I guess we learned just how valuable the GCW Title is, because these two were willing to throw their friendship out the window and kill one another just to get it!

Pete: If I wasn't terrified of LeStatt before, I am now. But he wasn't the only star of the night. We also saw an amazing sixty-minute Iron Man match, where we saw Mike Hardy take a step towards greatness, but he fell just short of putting away the Hall of Famer, Rich Rollins.

Bryan: This Boston crowd gave Hardy everything they had, and Hardy gave them every ounce of energy in his body, but in the end the legendary Rich Rollins proved that he's still one of the greatest talents on earth. If you want to see a real wrestling great, folks, you need look no further than the Renegade.

Jhub: Hey guys, if I may, I'd like to talk about GCW's resident diva, Eva! She lasted a hell of a lot longer than I did in the battle royal, and was tossing men out left and right, myself included! That chick is beautiful AND talented, and a hell of a kisser, I might add.

Pete: That was a classic moment, I must say, Jhub.

Jhub: You liked the tittie-twisted, too, did you, Pete?

Pete: Ha! That was great!

Jhub: Yeah. I have to be in court next Tuesday on sexual harrassment charges. It was worth it.

Celebration and Mind Games -by Phyl and Rob

“I Want To Conquer the World” by Bad Religion begins to blast over the PA system and Michael Stevens appears at the top of the ramp, with Alex Morgan and Sheena Ramone in tow. The crowd seems pretty even split in their reaction. The fans just can’t seem to decide whether or not they love him or hate him.

Bryan: And here he comes! The man who entered at number seven and went all the way to win the 2005 Dangerous Games! This is your number one contender, ladies and gentlemen, the man who will meet LeStatt Knight for the GCW Heavyweight Championship at Annihilation!

Jhub: And he’s starting to win the crowd over, too, JB. These people may hate arrogant jerks like Stevens, but they know talent when they see it, and they respect what Michael Stevens did at Dangerous Games!

Pretty much ignoring the crowd Stevens walks down towards the ring. Interestingly enough he’s wearing a duffle bag over his shoulder. Tossing the bag into the ring, he takes a minute to walk around the ring and grab a microphone, before sliding into the ring himself. Alex and Sheena remain outside of the ring, Sheena blowing some kisses to fans, while Morgan crosses his arms over his chest and just watches. Waiting a moment for the crowd and the music to die down, finally he lifts the mic up to his lips.

Stevens: And here I am, it’s my night ladies and gentleman. I’m proud to present to all of you….ME! Winner of the Two Thousand Five Dangerous Games Battle Royal, number one contender of the World Heavyweight Championship, and in just a few short weeks you’re world champion, MICHAEL STEVENS!

Alternate boos and cheers .

Stevens: You know it’s kinda funny. I took some time this week to think about things, and it appears to me I’m finally getting what’s coming to me. Just before GCW closed three years ago, Steven Caldera had hand picked me to be the future of this company, forget Toxic, forget Octavian, forget everyone, with the Establishment crumbling, I was the only man left standing. Of course that’s all metaphor, the realty is that at Dangerous Games I proved I was the last man standing. When the smoke cleared and the dust settled I was the man only man left in the ring. I’m your number one contender, and that’s F’n spectacular.

Still I didn’t come out of Dangerous Games unscathed, Luke Hanson put me in the hospital, and tonight I’m going to settle up that score. Big lumbering bastard’s going to pay for what he did to me, pay for what he’s done to a lot of guys just like me. He thinks because he’s bigger than everyone that he can constantly throw his weight around, I’m going to prove him wrong, cripple him if I have to, why quite simply because I can.

But that’s not the reason I’m here, it’s celebration time, and I want you my devoted fans, to share in this moment with me…

Stevens is cut off by “O’ Fortuna”. The crowd immediately begins jeering crazily, as Lestatt Knight makes his appearance. Stevens glances behind him where Alex is about to step forward, but cuts the big man off with a waved hand. Knight with world title draped over his shoulder, walks down the ramp paying no mind the to fans looking like the cat that just swallowed the canary. Upon making his way down to the ring, he steps up the stairs and stands on the apron. Before he can move forward Stevens speaks.

Stevens: Wait, wait, wait. I prepared for this, I knew you’d come out here, cause well you’re jealous we’ve established that. So before you step into the ring, and get all in my face and everything, just give me a second.

Knight looks amused, but doesn’t enter the ring, he stares at Stevens somewhat curious. Stevens drops the mic and bends down over his duffle bag. Unzipping sticks his entire head in the bag as well as his hands. A moment later he comes out of the bag with a wreath of garlic around his neck, and holding in one hand a cross and in the other, a rigid pink dildo. The crowd laughs heartily at that, especially the dildo. Stevens looks down, realizes his mistake and tosses it aside before reaching into the bag again, from the mat the microphone is still able to pick up his words as he mutters.

Stevens: Damn, wrong one. Where is it, damnnit, didn’t pack the stake. Oh well.

Removing his spare hand from the bag again, he picks up the microphone and stands. Brandishing the cross out in front of him he looks straight at Lestatt.

Stevens: Ok, cool I’m ready now.

LeStatt stands near the ropes with a smug look on his face. He breaths in deep and slowly exhales as he shakes his head from side to side. LeStatt begins taking steps around the ring, basically circling Steven's position. Its almost as if he is searching for the right words to use. Knight stops as he came full circle, before looking at Mike directly he drops the World Title to the side. Now he pierces into Michael with those icy blues.

LeStatt: What the hell are you doing Stevens? ( The crowd boos just at the sound of Knight's voice. ) Are you a wrestler or a comedian? Do you really think that getting a few laughs from these moronic fans is worth anything? Maybe you think that if you make THEM happy then they will love and cheer you even more? Really Mikey ... explain to me what this clownish behavior is suppose to prove?

Stevens: Hmmm, pretty good question. I think I shall respond in kind, what's walking around with a stick up your ass trying to be all weird and mysterious supposed to prove? That you're really a vampire? I got that, explains the complexion. I'm just being prepared working with what you gave me.

Knight looks out at the crowd in disgust as they chuckle at the joke made by Michael. He closes his eyes for a moment as someone would who has a headache.

Stevens: Garlic getting to you, excellent, knew it would work.

LeStatt: I hope you enjoy being this clown Stevens. I hope you enjoy being some circus act for these fans and for Steven Caldera, because its all you are going to remain while you are stuck in this juvenile state. I know your type Stevens, I've beaten them time and time again. Sure you can pull out all the Anne Rice jokes you want, call me gothic, devil worshiper or what ever numerous lame jokes you can think of ... but you know nothing about me. Do I go about talking about killing people? Being Satan reincarnated?

Knight takes the microphone away from his mouth for a moment as if to give Michael a chance to respond.

NO! Thats not who I am, but yet you all seem to stand by that sad stereotype.

LeStatt: You Stevens ... act the clown, but could be so much more.

Stevens: Stevens: Actually, last time we talked you did mention something about hacking some dude's head off. Remember? You know why people keep sticking to the sad stereotype LeStatt, because you surround yourself with it. I don't think you're the devil, I don't think you're that evil, I'd barely even call you naughty. You're a pathetic little man hiding behind a image meant to creep everyone out. I'm not buying it Skippy, you keep living the stereotype, I'll keep mocking you. That's how it works.

LeStatt Knight smiles now, a genuine smile which eventually turns into a chuckle. Slowly he walks forward until he is standing right in front of Stevens ... and thats when that smile fades.

LeStatt: Good Michael, I hope you remain this confident all the way through Annihilation ... or maybe... ( Knight looks Stevens up and down, then again gets a look of disgust ) Just maybe it is not me that is pretending?

Stevens: So you really are a vampire? Not being able to see yourself in the mirror would explain the haircut.As for confidence, how could I not have it. You're a freakin cartoon character.

LeStatt: Regardless of what you say here and now Mikey, this 'cartoon character' has something that you want. Stevens winning Dangerous Games was an accomplishment, one that maybe I should applaud you for. ( Knight takes a step backwards now, places the microphone between his arm and his body, clapping his hands a few times. ) Things change a bit now though. Now you don't have twenty-nine other people saving your ass ... Now it is just you and me. Its too bad your ignorant high school antics over power your in-ring ability ... or else this MIGHT have been a good Main Event.

Stevens: I think the chances of a good main event pretty much went out the window when you won the title. Don't worry Knight my boy, I'll try not to make you look too bad.

Knight suddenly gets a fire in his eyes as he rushes forward and shoves Stevens with all the power he has in him. Steven's flies backwards, not stopping until he reaches the ropes, obviously a bit shocked either by the shove, the power or both. Again Stevens checks over his shoulder quickly, Alex looks to be getting ready to rush the ring, but Stevens holds out a hand for him to stop.

LeStatt: GET YOUR HEAD OUT OF YOUR ASS MICHAEL! This is what I am talking about! You are pathetic! You have all the god given talent in the world inside that ring, any casual fan can see that, but you don't utilize it! You'd rather spend your time and energy being some damn stand-up comedian! Look at this ... ( Knight walks over and snatches his World TItle from the mat and shoves it forward in Steven's face. ) DO YOU WANT THIS!? is THIS what you strive for!? If so then you need to know what it takes to get it! If you want to be a jokster then lose some pounds and go open a cruiserweight division or maybe go and battle Trey for that United States title again and again, since its become a joke since hes won it anyways. BUT if you want to be in the big time, and step up to the plate then its time to grow the hell up!

Chuckling Stevens steps forward, taking the garlic wreath from around his neck, and tossing it aside. He comes nose to nose with Knight, still grinning from ear to ear, while Knight fumes.

Stevens: And just like that, you're angry, working off emotions not logic. I'd say someone just got the advantage.

Stevens brushes by Knight, and slides out of the ring, where’s he’s joined by Sheena and Alex. Chuckling he begins heading to the back. Knight stands alone for a moment and turns his head slightly, then picks up the loose Garlic. A devilish grin spreads across Knight's face now as he turns around.

LeStatt: Stevens... (Mike turns around to face Knight) You might want to keep this, wouldn't want something unfortunate to happen to you before Annihilation. ( Knight throws the Garlic which lands at Steven's feet. ) Oh and ... Sweet Dreams.

Stevens looks at Knight strangely for a moment, before finally shaking his head and turning around. “I want to Conquer the World” starts up again, as Stevens continues to the back.

Starting Over -by Ben

With a grunt, Mike Hardy pulls himself up for another sit-up. Shirtless and dripping with sweat, Hardy earns a huge explosion of cheers as he appears on the MegaTron. Hardy’s face is tight and frustrated as he continues his tireless workout, screaming in pain as he punishes himself further.

The door finally swings open, and a fluffy white towel flies through the air, landing on Hardy’s face. Hardy snatches it away in anger and snaps to his feet, but lowers his guard when he recognizes the immaculately dressed figure leaning in his door frame.

Hardy: What do you want?

President Steven Caldera enters the room to cheers of his own, closing the door behind him. Hardy crosses to a bench and seats himself with his back to Caldera, pulling off his sneakers and beginning to lace his wrestling boots.

Caldera: I just wanted to see how you were doing. I had hoped you were ready for your match tonight.

Hardy: I’m ready.

Hardy answers shortly, and Caldera scoffs. Caldera seats himself beside Hardy, facing the other way, and speaks softly and kindly.

Caldera: Listen, Mike, don’t be so hard on yourself. Do you know how many people Rollins has beat in Iron Man matches over the years? Dozens. You know how many have even come CLOSE to beating him?

Hardy turns his head to the side, half curious.

Caldera: Just one.

Hardy looks down in a moment contemplation, before finally scoffing and returning to his boots.

Hardy: All this work, and for what? All this pain and suffering, and I still can’t win the big matches. I didn’t win ACWF, I couldn’t beat Stevens, and now I couldn’t beat Rollins. Every time I come so damn close, and then somehow, for some reason, things don’t go my way. I’m sick of being the damn lovable loser, Steve. Because frankly, I’m not feeling a whole lot of love right now.

Hardy throws his boot to the cold concrete floor in anger, and Caldera shoots up to his feet. Shouting with a surprisingly commanding voice, Caldera’s words prompt Hardy to stand in shock and back into the lockers.

Caldera: Damn you, Mike! What the hell is wrong with you?! I’ve spent months training you now, getting you ready for the big time, and you still don’t get it?! It’s not about whether you win or lose, it’s how to play the game!

Hardy: Spare me, Steven, I heard that when I was in third grade.

Caldera: And it’s no less true today! Listen, Mike, I’ve been doing this for a long time, and so has Rollins. And when guys like us look back on our careers, after a decade in the sport, you realize that it’s not how many matches you won, its how many people you touched. And damnit, Mike, these people in Boston tonight and at Dangerous Games didn’t come to see GCW, they came to see YOU!

The Fleet Center explodes in cheers, so loud that Hardy actually hears them from his locker room. A thin smile manages its way over Hardy’s lips, but Hardy remains silent.

Caldera: You’re upset that you’ve lost THREE matches? Kid, I stopped counting my losses when I got to the hundreds. You can’t keep worrying about winning and losing, because that’s a short-term thing. Wins help you get to the top, sure, but you’ll get there when you’re ready to get there. In the meantime, you can’t take the easy way out to shave some time off. That’s what that son of a bitch LeStatt did.

The crowd boos mightily at the mention of the GCW Champion’s name.

Caldera: He stabbed his friend in the back just to get a title shot sooner than he would have. He’s got the title, Mike, but he’s hurt a lot of people in the meantime. So you have to ask yourself a question, Mike. Do you want to do this the right way, do you want to be one of the good guys? Or do you want to say to hell with the right way, to hell with the fans, and to hell with me, and look for a damn shortcut?

Hardy looks down, unsure of how to answer.

Caldera: Because I guarantee you, Mike, when you look back on your career some day, you’ll have wished you had done things the right way when you had the chance, because that’s not ever something you can get back.

Hardy grins and looks up.

Hardy: So let’s just start over, Steve. I’ll be the student, you be the teacher, and we’ll start from the beginning.

Caldera: You’re still young, my friend. You’ve got your whole career ahead of you. Don’t get discouraged. Let’s just go out there and take things one match at a time.

Hardy steps forward and extends his hand, gripping Caldera with a firm handshake.

Caldera: You’re going to be champion of the world some day, Mike. I know that for sure. So it doesn’t matter when it happens. Because it’s going to happen eventually.

Hardy nods and heads for the door, exiting for his match. The door slams behind him, leaving Caldera alone in the locker room. Caldera smiles and looks at the floor.

Caldera: …Some day soon…

Singles Match – One Fall

Mike Hardy vs. Dimensioner -by Phyl

“The Hand That Feeds” cranks up in the arena and the fans come to their feet cheering wildly for Mike Hardy. The young superstar trots down to the ring, basking in the warm reception from the crowd. He’s still moving a little slowly after the beating his took at the hands of Rollins, but he’s still getting around pretty good. Sliding into the ring he stands raising his hands to the air, and receiving another warm round of a applause.

A few seconds later, “Down with the Sickness” starts and the massive Dimensioner heads down to the ring. Ignoring the jeers from the crowd, he strides down to the ring with a purpose, and arriving steps over the top rope. Quickly the bell sounds and the match is under way.

Big D charges Hardy, but the smaller man is too quick, and able to slide between the big man’s legs. Hardy quickly pops up, and hits Dimensioner in the back of the knee with a kick. This staggers the big man, who turns only to receive a boot to the gut from Hardy. After a couple more boots Dimensioner seems to get fed up, and grabs Hardy by the hair, pulling him forward while at the same time driving his fist into the smaller man’s skull.

Bryan: Dominating power there by Dimensioner, and this doesn’t look good for Hardy!

Jhub: This Dimensioner is one Sick Freak.

Three more shots later, Dimensioner practically tosses Hardy across the ring and into the corner, knocking the breath out the kid. Dragging Hardy to the center of the ring by the hair, Dimensioner goes for the pin, but only gets a two count.

D stands pulling Hardy up with him. Applying a double handed choke, Dimensioner begins to lift Hardy up into the air. However, Hardy squirms out of the man’s grip and blasts Dimensioner in the face with a dropkick. The big man stumbles into the ropes, but comes flying off them with a HUGE clothesline that spins Hardy around in the air.

Dimensioner pulls Hardy back up, and goes for the End of Days, but Hardy slips out of his grip, and lands on the mat behind the big man. Quickly taking advantage of his position Hardy back kicks Dimensioner in the knee causing the big man to slightly kneel, right into position for the Waterfall! Hardy goes for the cover, 1...2...3! Hardy Wins!

Bryan: Amazing display by Mike Hardy here, beating the mountain of a man Dimensioner.

Pete: The kids got real talent and I don’t think anyone can die that anymore. His only problem now is that he always seems to fall short in the big ones. Still if Hardy can keep putting wins like this up, he’ll be back in the main event scene in no time.

A Dreadful Conversation -by Phyl and Jason

We come to focus on the inside of the locker room, specifically on a door. We hear a light knock on said door, and a split second later the door is pushed open. Slowly gimping in is Jason Dread, supporting himself on two crutches. The crowd in attendance gives a supportive but disappointed cheer. He goes on without notice.

Dread - Mike...you here, man?

A voice comes from the distance, which we can obviously peg down as Michael Stevens.

Stevens -Yeah, what the hell are you doing here?

Dread looks at Stevens, and sighs. For the first time we also get a glimpse of Stevens as his attention turns from a bag lying on the floor of the locker room to Dread.

Dread - I think you know as well as I do. You're not as dumb as you're acting, man.

Stevens - Actually I've got no clue, figured you were still in the hospital or something, or had died of shame. You quit, or did you forget?

Dread - Michael, can you drop the facade so we can talk for a few minutes man to man. We've had our share of problems as well as triumphs in the past. This is far from the time for a falty attitude. You to me, man to man. That's all I ask.

Stevens seems to think and consider this for a moment. Sighing heavily, from his seated position he pushes a chair out towards Dread with his foot

Stevens - Alright Seth, have a seat man.

Dread hobbles slightly off his position on the crutch and gives a small smile to Michael Stevens. He sits on the chair, obviously in a much more comfortable position. After a few seconds of silence, Dread looks up to Stevens whom is staring down at him.

Dread - As you have probably already heard, after tonight I'm not going to be around for awhile. Possibly longer than I've thought. It's not because I've died of shame more than reality bit me a little harder than I would have wished it had. Everything that I've been through physically for the past two months has taken it's toll...I can't wake up in the morning without feeling that same sharp pain in my spine as I have ever since that bastard Octavian tried to kill me. But I wish that was it...there's always the matter with Lestatt...

Stevens -You know, I've wanted to talk to you about that Seth. How in the fuck could you let someone like Lestatt play you so badly. Seriously man, I thought you were smart enough to know that you can't trust anyone in this business, your friends are only your friends until they want something of yours, right? Seriously, we've known each other how long, and do you trust me any further than you can throw me?

Dread stops to think for a moment, but continues with the answer to the question posed.

Dread - As a person, Michael, I trust you like a brother. What most people don't know is that we've known each other for a lot longer than we've ever given off, and the days before GCW were good days indeed. Hell, we were unbeatable...we were Immortals.

But as the man you front out, no, I wouldn't trust you shaking me after I've taken a piss. What I did with Lestatt was make the very wrong mistake of blurring those lines which I see in you and many others. What I failed to realize is that there WAS no line. What you see is what you get.

You're right, I should've been more conscious of my actions. I shouldn't have let him get the better of me. I guess I became too soft, and definately let my better judgment fall flat on its face.

He gives a small chuckle.

Dread - And look where I am now.

Stevens looks somewhat uncomfortable, shifting in his seat. He stares hard at Dread for a moment, finally shaking his head sadly.

Stevens - Yeah, look at you now, sorry man. I really don't know what to say Seth, sorry? What can I do? Can I help at all? Seriously man, is there something I'm supposed to be saying here, I honestly have no clue what the hell is going on. Seems like everything's spinning around and I'm the only one who notices. It's crazy.

Dread - Michael, I didn't come here to ask for your pity or your help. I didn't come here to preach about the wrongs of your ways. I want you to know, man...

Dread smiles warmly at Michael, and for the first time we see him stand on his own two feet, despite the pain.

Dread - I know you're the man who is here to fill the void that I'm leaving. I've known you longer than I have most anyone in this business and I can tell you straight up that now is YOUR time to shine.

Stevens stands as way, extends his hand to shake. Dread smiles and completes the handshake.

Stevens - You know brother, that means a lot, and you know I'm pitying you. It's just a weird time, stuff in changing all over. Tell me one thing though, I just like you have stared down some sick SOBs in my day, what makes Knight so much different? I mean really, even when you just talk about the guy, I can see it in your eyes. There's something there, what is it?

Dread - I don't know, Michael. I've wondered this many time over the past couple of days...maybe it's the fact that he didn't set me on fire when he had the chance that's the most unnerving of all...

Stevens - Indeed, man. I hate to do this but...I kinda need to start getting ready...

Dread - It's fine man, I understand. The time's come for you to write another chapter in your story. You've got my blessing, Michael...and if you need anything you know where to reach me.

Stevens - Same here, bro, you know that right?

Dread - Of course, I wouldn't expect anything less.

As Dread grabs his crutches and starts to hobble out of the room he turns for one final thought.

Dread - One last thing, Michael...have fun while you can, but don't underestimate Lestatt when he's in the ring with you. Whether he's simple insane and purely a genius I don't know...but the bottom line is that he is a dangeous individual. Take care of yourself...I don't want to hear about you joining my ranks anytime soon.

Stevens - You know me, I always have fun. And Knight, bah, I dunno, he's just a guy to me still. Even seeing you like this I guess it still hasn't sunk in, he just hasn't done anything to me, you know? Oh, and believe me I won't be anytime soon, you know I've always been the tougher one.

Stevens chuckles.

Dread - I wouldn't expect anything less from a fellow Immortal, my good friend. Don't expect me to fade off into the sunset just yet, you never know how soon a pheonix can rise from the ashes.

Dread gives Stevens one last parting and knowing look before he exits the doorway and into the unknown future. Stevens gazes to where Dread previously stood for a second afterwards, taking in the effect of the visit. Stevens smiles to himself as he turns to his bag once again...

Television Championship Match – One Fall

Jonathan Borealis vs. Rich “The Renegade” Rollins -by Phyl

Rich Rollins heads down to the ring to the strains of “Born Of A Broken Man” by Rage Against The Machine. The crow showers him with boos as he heads down to the ring, a huge smile on his face. Obviously still pleased with himself over the brutality he inflicted on Mike Hardy at Dangerous Games. Sliding into the ring, he raises his hands to the air, and receives more boos for the trouble. As his music dies away, “Rabble Rouser” starts up, and Jonathan Borealis sprints down to the ring to a huge round of applause. Dropping the title on the apron, he slides in, and pops up as the bell sounds.

Bryan: There’s the bell, and this Television Title match is underway! And how huge is this match, with the fast-rising Borealis taking on a Hall of Famer for the Television Title?!

Pete: Biggest TV Title match ever, JB!

Rollins immediately charges at Borealis hoping to gain the early advantage, but Jonathan sees him coming and drops the “Renegade” with a drop toe hold. Following up the advantage Borealis drops a quick elbow to Rollins’s back, and cranks in a hammer lock. Rollins writhes in pain on the mat seeking to escape the hold, but Jon has it locked in tight, and Rollins can’t escape.

Finally after a time in the hold, Rollins is able to catch Borealis in the side of the head with a back elbow. Both men quickly pop up to their feet, and Jonathan goes for a swift kick to the Rollins midsection but thinking quickly the veteran is able to catch Borealis’s leg and spin him around. Going from there Rollins grabs Borealis around the waist and nails a German suplex. Going for the bridge he gets a one count before the TV champ is able to push up one shoulder and stop the count.

Bryan: Big move there from Rollins, but the champ is hanging tough!

Jhub: This Borealis kid is tougher than he looks, JB. He eliminated half the damn field in the battle royal, and he’s going toe to toe with a living legend.

With both men getting quickly back up to their feet they meet in the center of the ring. Back and forth Rollins and Borealis go with punches to each others skulls. Finally the champ is able to get the advantage and Irish whips Rollins into the corner. The “Renegade’s” back slams into the corner driving the wind from his body, and Borealis follows him in with a big running splash. Rollins begins to stumble forward, and the champion takes him down with a snap mare take down, before cranking in a chin lock. Jonathan places a knee in Rollins’s back, with applying the pressure to his neck, really pushing the veteran’s pain threshold.

Pete: Borealis is back on top here! He’s got Rollins in a bad way!

Jhub: Oh yeah! Now we’re talking! Stevens just one-upped Knight there with an even bigger chair shot on Hanson!

Pete: I can’t watch this… I’m closing my eyes.

Jhub: I’ve got five to one LeStatt wins.

Knight offers Stevens a grim smirk from beneath his flowing black hair, and waits as Hanson somehow begins standing again. Knight motions for Stevens to back away, and Stevens obliges as Knight hits the ropes, springboards off the middle turnbuckle, and flies through the air with a massive baseball swing, snapping Hanson’s head back violently and taking him off his feet again! The crowd explodes in applause for the amazing move as Stevens tucks the chair under his arm and claps slowly for the champ.

Bryan: GOOD GOD! Another damn chair shot, and they’re just beating Hanson senseless in an attempt to best each other. This is just cruel!

Jhub: It’s a cruel sport, JB! Come on, who’s in on this? Who’s gonna have the best chair shot?

Stevens readies his chair and measures Hanson, but Hanson doesn’t move. Knight nods in satisfaction and turns to leave, but Stevens calls after him. Knight stares back in disbelief as Hanson begins to rise again!

Bryan: Wait, Hanson is getting up again! God, how is the big man still alive, much less moving?

Jhub: Who knows?! But who cares!? I wanna see the knockout blow here!

Stevens smiles and raises his chair, waiting for Hanson to stagger to his feet. Hanson rises and Stevens takes off, springboarding off the middle rope and swinging the chair around his back! With his free arm Stevens hooks Hanson by the head and drops straight back, driving Hanson into an Inferno DDT onto the steel chair! The crowd explodes to their feet, cheering wildly for the amazing move!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! What a move by Michael Stevens! That’s got to be it! Hanson is out cold!

Stevens smiles and stands, spinning on his heel to mock Knight. He never sees Knight, only the flash of Knight’s chair before it hits him in the face. Stevens is turned inside out from the shot, flying head over heels and landing face down on the mat in a heap. The arena plunges into boos as Knight calmly drops the chair and kneels beside Stevens.

Knight: You win.

With that, “O’Fortuna” begins to play again and the GCW Champion slowly exits the ring, leaving Hanson and Stevens lying unconscious in the ring.

Bryan: Dear Lord, Knight just about decapitated the number one contender, and he’s leaving both men lying!

Jhub: There’s a new champ in the house, guys! Like him or not, he just inserted himself into this main event and destroyed not one but TWO men!

Knight slowly stalks up the ramp, finally turning back to eye Stevens. The camera offers one last glimpse of Knight, his eyes burning red from beneath his jet black mane.

Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, we’re out of time from Boston! For Pete and The Jhub, I’m James Bryan, join us again next week on WorldWide!

The GCW logo appears as Stevens slowly pulls himself back to his elbows, glaring out angrily at the GCW World Champion, his mind already plotting against him and concocting a way to win the GCW World Championship. . . . . . .

…Pete: Can I look now?
