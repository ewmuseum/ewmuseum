Keiko Yoshimoto Biography Real Name Keiko Yoshimoto Nicknames The Dragon, The Samurai-Rita Hometown Seoul, Korea, residing in Oahu, Hawaii Birthday May 4, 1977 Height 5'6 Weight 140 Fed Titles Tag Team Biography Keiko was born on May 4th 1977. She was born in Seoul, Korea but fearing the North Korean lifestyle her family sought refuge in Tokyo, Japan. Their Keiko became highly interested in forms of combat. At the age of ten she defeated four boys that were bulling her and her classmates. Her father refused for her to join a dojo. However she ran away despite her parent’s protests and joined the Yu Wong samurai dojo. Yu trained her and became a father figure to her. Keiko was a fast learner. She mastered karate, tae kown doe and others with ease and by the time she was 16 she was beyond black belt status. Yet she did lose some her fair share of fights as well. Sensei Wong also trained her in the form of weapon’s combat. Keiko’s favorite thing to use was a sword. Yet, she learned how to fire guns and uses other knives and nunchucks. She also mastered with dagger, and sai’s. On her 20th birthday Keiko received the greatest gift of all. She was granted a warrior by her mentor. He also let her have her status and nickname The Dragon for that was the Chinese/Japanese year she was born in. She was also given a house in Japan to where she had to defend at all times the honor of that house. The House of Yoshimato and Wong were the two most powerful houses in all of Japan. At the age of 23 Keiko suffered a tragic loss. A jealous house called the Wang’s emerged and sought out to destroy Yu Wong. In a long battle Yu was struck down by Mi Wang a very strong female warrior. At the time Keiko was out of the country in Paris. When she came home and learned the news she became hostile and sought out to challenge Mi alone to a fight. The two fought for one whole day when finally Keiko killed Mi with Yu’s sword. Keiko returned home and found a note left by Yu. He gave her his sword and house. The dujo was hers as well. She was to train and make more warriors, for he knew of her gift of second sight. He knew people would come after her and destroy her now. So Keiko did just that, and after a year of searching she found her warrior’s Hope and Faith Clarkson. Little even with her second sight did she know that how bad Hope and Faith were. Yet she knew these girls proved a vital importance in her house and she trained them, while Christine Lockhart trained them in the arts of being a wrestler. Over time Faith learned her combat’s in gun fighting while Hope advanced in martial arts and like Keiko discovered a fetish with swords, daggers, and samurai weapons. Keiko had a new mission. Her students lost a match and are now slaves to Sexy Amanda. She knows of Hope and Faith’s past and she knows Amanda will use the hurt to her advantage. Taking a vow and pledging to her god and Yu Wong, she’s joining DU so she can protect them and get them free, and hopefully in the end, change their ways of life. However destiny has a funny way of interferring....(see Current History) in November of 2004 Keiko aligned her self with Christine, Hope Clarkson and her then wife Faith Von Almen to form the strong stable called The Family. However as quickly as it formed it was quickly torn apart. It was torn apart when new Divas known as the Kuno Sisters (Kawanaha and Kit) entered the picture. After Kawanaha and Keiko teamed together to save Jaguar Yoshiko, the two had a small make out session in the ring. Ever since then Keiko has changed everything about her ways including her appearance. She’s become more athletic, more tanned, and most importantly completely forgetting most of what Yu Wong has taught her. The only thing she still does that is in relative to her Samurai ways in her martial arts and weapons training. This is mostly to keep her skills sharpen, and to win good graces with her desire, Kawanaha. She also still trains Hope in sword play, but harbors some bad feelings for her now as both Hope and herself are fighting for Kawanaha’s affections. Keiko has now also given up her red dragon named Ariel. She’s given ownership to Hope. She no longer wants the hassle of taking care of her. If Keiko isn’t residing in her dojo in Seoul, Korea or in the farmhouse torturing her slave in Christine, she is to be found at Kawanaha’s private beach mansion in Hawaii. Recently both Hope Clarkson and Keiko Yoshimoto have undergone training into the Red Sky Dojo under the training of Master Bruce Skylar Other Feds 4 Winds, Divas Unleashed Other Fed Awards Top contendeter for tag Team Titles in 4 WW, Nearly won South Wind Title Wrestling Style Martial Arts/HIgh Flyer/Tech Finisher Move Heaven, Purgatory, Hell, and Defeat Finisher Description The best description of this move is think of Rey Mysterio's 619 x 3. Keiko will trip her opponent into the second rope. Keiko will then bounce off the ropes from theo ther side of the ring leaping into the air when coming to the other side. She will grab the top rope and swing her body around kicking her opponent in the face and sending them to the bottom rope. Keiko will then swing around again this time using the second rope to swing her around knocking her down opponent down to the canvas. On the last swing around she will use the bottom rope to kick her foe in the face whom is now on the canvas. After the three kicks Keiko slides back into the ring on her back and does a kip-up only to jump back in a standing backwards moonsault onto her downed opponent. Each swing around the ropes symbolizes Heaven (1st spin), Purgatory (2nd spin) and Finally Hell or the last spin Finisher Setup Move Chop Seuy Setup Description A combination of kicks, chops, and punches all over the oppoents body, then tripping them into the ropes Other Moves KEIKO'S RING ENTERANCE: Ring Entrance: A spotlight hits the top of the ramp asthe opening to Zion by Fluke The Korean Japanese Flags are displayed on the video wall before it shatters into clips of the Dragon in various wrestling matches and weapons training events. Keiko leaps out from under the ramp slicing her sword in mid air. She lands with a yell and the crowd eats it up. She then pauses and gestures to the ring curtain area. Coming out of the ramp dressed in a similar black samurai robe is Hope Clarkson. Her tag team partner. And now coming down the aisle accompanied by Hope Clarkson from Seoul, Korea and one half of the tag team Firey Passion...She is The Dragon, Keiko Yoshimoto!!!! As the two walk down to the ring, red and yellow pyros light up the ramp to resemble dragon fire. Keiko semi acknowledges the crowd as she walks to the ring. She knows though she has to remain focused on the prize at hand. On some levels she still omits the fact that she is a heel, but she remains neutral to all. There’s one thing she's focused on, her opponent and how to beat them. All the way down to the ring she has her eyes focused on that ring. She tries to use her gift of second site to see if she can find a defense and to find any cracks in the facades that her opponent or opponent's are showing. Although she doesn’t use it regularly, she’s not that good at it. After climbing into the ring steps and stepping into the ring, Keiko stands center ring with a now smile on her face. She begins to belly dance all the while removing her Samurai robe from her tanned body. She reveals to the crowd her white spaghetti string tank top with a black Playboy Bunny symbol on it and red thong bottom. Also she is wrestling barefoot and not using wrestling boots. Keiko sets the holster for her sword at her feet as she dances. After the stripper showcase, The Dragon stands all smiles with one hand on her hip and the robe in the other. The lights really make her skin glow. She tosses the robe to the outside of the ring to Hope and grabs her sword from her holster. Keiko hears the fans making a small chant for her and she wants to give them what they are asking for. Keiko smiles and then goes into a showcase of her weapon abilities by doing tricks with the sword, before spitting out a mist from her mouth that turns into a flame. She then rests in her corner waiting for the bell. Hope removes the sword from the ring and goes back to the corner with Keiko. Hope begins to rub Keiko’s shoulders getting her psyched up for her fight. The Dragon Slayer This move can ONLY be done if Keiko is tossed into the ropes and she uses this as a counter. Keiko is tossed into the ropes. She dodges whatever her attacker is going to do and bounces of the ropes from the other side. She then leaps into the air with a flying headscissors on her attacker. From there she will spin her body around the attackers necks sending chops all across attackers chest and sides. After she tires of this she will drop the now victim onto the mat with a hurricarana like maneuver into a pinning combination hooking both legs. She then lands behind her confused attacker and nails a dropkick to the back of the head sending them face first into the turnbuckles. If it's a group of people the attackers are all in a line and she hits the lead into the others sending them back, even out of the ring if necessary.
