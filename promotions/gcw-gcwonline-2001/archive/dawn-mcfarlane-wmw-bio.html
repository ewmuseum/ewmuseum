Dawn McFarlane Biography Real Name Dawn Catherine McFarlane Nicknames The Steel Rose, Perfect Dark, The Lioness, Mistress of the Mat Hometown Toronto, ON, Canada Birthday Oct 23rd, 1981 Height 6'0' Weight 151lbs Fed Titles GCW World Champion Fed Awards 2006 Rampage Winner Other Feds WMW, Global Other Fed Awards Global Cruiserweight Champion, Global TV Champion, 2005 Mistress of the Mat Wrestling Style Power/Technical Finisher Move Open your Eyes Finisher Description Final Cut/Eye of the Hurricane (Front face lock, spins into position for a reverse DDT and then drops an elbow across her opponents throat while dropping them down Finisher Setup Move Falling Angel Setup Description Top Rope Clothesline Other Moves Trademarks: Dragon's Fang - Sit Out Asai DDT Rarely Used The Black Rose - Flatliner Rarely Used Lighting the Candle - Dawn executes a handstand on her opponents shoulders, standing perfectly upright on her hands before rolling down for a curling drop kick into her opponents abdomen. Only practical on large opponents. Crown of Thorns - A Series of Rolling Suplexes. First a Dragon Suplex, then a Japanese Ocean Suplex and finally a Japanese Ocean Cyclone with a bridge for the pin. The Higher you Fly - Running Knee lift into opponents back while they're face first against the turnbuckle. The Farther you Fall - Side Russian Leg Sweep so that opponents head hits lower turnbuckle. Kissed by a Rose - A running Knee Lift, then grabs her opponents head for a Diamond Cutter when they bend down. Once too Often - A Dragonscrew Leg Whip trained specifically to counter superkicks. Tsunami Bomb - Japanese Ocean Bomb. Suplexes: - Butterfly Suplex - Gargoyle Suplex - T-Bone Suplex - JOS (Japanese Ocean Suplex) (With or without bridge) - JOC (Japanese Ocean Cyclone) (With or without bridge) - Northern Lights Suplex - German Suplex (With or without bridge or release) - Dragon Suplex - Tiger Suplex Throws and Grapples: - Arm Drag Takedown - Bulldog - Exclamation Point DDT - Hip Toss - Reverse DDT - Snap Suplex - Swinging Neck Breaker Dawn Tends to use Kicks and Strikes to wear her opponent down for Suplexes and Throws. Kicks and Strikes: - Back Heel Kick - Backflip Kick - Drop Kick - Enzugiri - Flying Wheel Kick - Handstand Crescent Kick - High Circle Kick - Inside Crescent Kick - Knee Scissors Kick - Lariat - Low Leg Sweep - Low Drop Kick - Muay Thai Clinch with followup Knee Lifts - Over the shoulder High Kick - Ridgehand Clothesline - Rolling Sobat Kick - Running Enzugiri - Shining Wizard - Spinning Back Fist - Spinning Heel Kick - Standing Thrust Kick Submission: - Guillotine Choke - Triangle Choke - Dragon Sleeper - Rising Sun Sleeper (Wall of Jericho, but with victim upside down) Highrisk/Agility: - Asai Moonsault - Flying Head Scissors - Frankensteiner - Moonsault - No Hands Plancha - Running Turnbuckle Moonsault - Second Rope Leg Drop - Springboard Leg Drop - Suicide Dive - Top Rope Victory Roll Entrance Music 'Animal I have Become' by Three Days Grace
