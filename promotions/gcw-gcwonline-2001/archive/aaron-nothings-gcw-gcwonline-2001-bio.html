Aaron Nothings Biography Real Name Aaron Nothings Nicknames "Nothing" "Unstoppable" "Tenacious" "The Godfather of Just" Hometown Cleveland, Ohio Birthday 29th of October, 1974 Height 6 foot 2 inches Weight 223 and 1/4 pounds Biography Nothings always had a drug addiction, his teen years riddled in cocaine use rendered his high school chances at gaining a scalorship to the lack of colleges he applied to. He was down and out you might say, his life on the thin red line of death. Then he met Candice, perhaps the love of his life, and he turned himself around. He found a job, even if it was cooking at a local resturant, grabbed his own apartment, and swayed away from the cocaine, and other drugs he had found himself wrapped up in(tar, alcohal, marijuana, and crack.) Then he joined the Marines. He scaled to the top of his squad, his tactics where not the best, but his leadership ability painted the yellow brick road as he climbed the mountain of ranks. He went to war, Candice wasnt into the whole idea, her tears carrying the both of them, the light in the relationship slowly dying. And the unthinkable happened. Nothings was shot in the head by a twelve year old child, the bullet cementing itself into his cranial cavity and then back out. He was dead. There was white everywhere, and then he blinked, the first sign in thirty mintues that he was alive. And so he lived on, six months in rehabilitation, his memory of the accident gone, besides the hollow silohette of the boy. He landed in Cleveland, the thoughts of his fiancee on his mind. He came home to nothing, the girl of his dreams dead by her own hands, atleast thats what her parents wanted him to believe. Nothings passed on from woman to woman, noe of which really lasting as he went back to his cooking job, his life taking that long narrow dead end road. His addictions met up with him again. One night he was wasted, his mind long gone from the earths surface when he showed up to a Just Wrestling event his friends had found tickets for in New York, the beginning to his end. His military background fit right in as the underachieving promotion was looking for sign ups for the next Touring Event. Nothings had nothing to gain, nothing to lose. And so he was signed, his drugamatic problems paving the road to his uneventual turn of success. Nothings still waded in the tides of his addictions, his premiere addiction burning a hole through the opposition. He captured the JWC from Max Danger, his reign of supreme lasting three plus months till a Lone Ranger of sorts in Wone unseeded the unstopable. Nothings hit rock bottom afterwards, banging men and women, drugs penetrating his flesh, his mind long gone since that night of more then twelve years ago. Rekindling the flame in his soul, Nothings recaptured his success and rolled on to recapture the JWC from GCW's Andy Murry who one week prior obtained his second world title, and rising star Terrance Kingsley in one of the greatest matches to touch Just Wrestling in the year long adventure. Nothings was on top of the wrestling world, but his social life was taking a fall, and recently it hit rock bottom again, as he waited in an air port, the second love of his life murdered and the child that turned out to be Nothings after all(not naturally his, but legally his). With the wieght of Just in his left hand and the wieght of the world in his right hand, can he find a middle realm where the to can mengle leaving Nothings the young gun of the 1990's?
