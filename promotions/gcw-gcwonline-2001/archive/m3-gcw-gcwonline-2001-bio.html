M3 Biography Nicknames The Unknown Luchador Hometown Tijuana Birthday 08/03/82 Height 6'1 Weight 237 lbs Biography M3 is a luchador in the great Mexican tradition and as such is always hidden behind a mask. He has been competing in various Mexican promotions since the tender age of 15 and has decided to try his hand in the United States as he approaches the peak of his abilities. The only problem is that he doesn’t speak English anywhere near fluently enough to negotiate and gain a roster spot in one of the big American federations. Therefore he has enlisted the help of Jorge Ramos - El Gato Más Loco (The Craziest Cat), a renowned manager and overall wrestling savant, to help him in his quest to break through north of the border. Unfortunately, GCW were unwilling to hire his Manager in an official capacity due to budget constraints so M3 is forced to compete alone. Strengths Crowd favourite, extremely quick in the ring Weaknesses Lacks the strength of many wrestlers, poor English Wrestling Style High-flying Strategy Get in and out quickly. M3 works a breakneck pace that often throws his opponents off their game. Finisher Move Paso de frontera Finisher Description Double springboard hurricanarana pin Special Moves Wheelbarrow bulldog, wheelbarrow armdrag Other Moves Armdrag variations Satellite headscissors Hurricanarana variations Springboard moves (dropkick/neckbreaker/armdrag/senton/leg drop/headbutt/splash) Spanish Fly(C4) Neckbreakers Any and all top rope moves. Entrance Music 'After Dark' by Tito & Taruntula Ring Entrances As 'After Dark' begins, M3 emerges from backstage in full luchador garb with the feathered head-dress and the cape. At regular intervals he stops to pose and milk the adulation of the crowd, before sliding into the ring and posing at all four corners. After removing the costume, he is left shirtless with colourful long tights on and tassels on both biceps, as well as his boots.
