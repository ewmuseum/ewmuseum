Warpath 2001 Madison Square Garden, New York, New York ---- Introduction The lights dim and the crowd grows in anticipation. The MegaTron lights up, and the fans begin to cheer as the faces of eight men flash on the screen. Thirteen, Paladin, Toad, John Reed, Toxic, The Jhub, President Caldera, and Octavian all make brief appearances on the screen. A voice begins, filling the arena. Voice: A new era has begun in the world of sports entertainment. Global Championship Wrestling is one month old, and has already reached the pinnacle of the industry. And tonight, one man will fight through the ranks, to reach the peak of the best company in the business. Tonight, eight men wage war for the richest prize in the industry. The camera returns to the capacity crowd, and the MegaTron dims, completely darkening the arena. Voice: And now, GCW proudly presents. . . Warpath! The arena is suddenly lit up with fireworks and explosions. The crowd is going wild, as the camera zooms in and out. The elaborate stage area is filled with random items of war, and is lit up with pyro coming from every direction. The fans continue cheering in anticipation, as the show begins. Bryan: We are live, coming to you from the sold out Madison Square Garden in New York! We are pumped up and ready for GCW’s first ever Pay-Per-View extravaganza, Warpath! The anticipation has been growing for weeks, ever since President Caldera’s first press conference, where he announced his new company! Yale: This is it! The night we’ve all been waiting for! Eight men are lined up for a one night tournament for it all! The stakes really can’t be any higher than this, J.B.! Bryan: Before this night is out, we will crown a new World Champion, and a new United States Champion! Not to mention a ladder match to crown the first Cruiserweight champion, and much more! Yale: This is going to be an incredible night! I’m ready to go! ---- Preparations The camera switches to a shot of a luxurious locker room. The traditional lockers and benches are replaced with antique dressers and recliners. The camera pans up from a coffee table of condiments to reveal President Caldera, Toxic, and Octavian in their luxury locker room. The crowd begins booing noisily as they realize who is on the MegaTron. Toxic is pacing nervously behind a large couch, as Caldera and Octavian sit in chairs, drinking champagne Caldera: Well, boys, this is the big night. The night where we finally crown the first GCW World Champion. This is a big night. You know, I won my first world title in a tournament just like this? I might like to do it again! The Establishment will be walking out of here with the World title, and I’m going to punish that idiot John Reed in the first round. And Mr. President, good luck against the Jhub.
