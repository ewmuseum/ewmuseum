DVD 2 Aloha Stadium — Honolulu, Hawaii ---- The Best of GCW Originally released in 2003, this program was broadcast on X:TV as a farewell to GCW. After closing its doors in 2002, Global Championship Wrestling was presumed to be dead. Though its parent company, Caldera Enterprises, had met with continued success, the wrestling promotion had been written off by the industry as a thing of the past. This broadcast was compiled based on the votes of loyal GCW fans, who selected their favorite matches from GCW's first run. Now, for the first time on DVD, witness all the greatest moments from GCW's original ride. The DVD features the original pre-match commentary by GCW founder Steven Caldera.
