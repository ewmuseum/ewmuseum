Markus Stone Biography Real Name Markus Stone Hometown Oklahoma City, Ok Birthday 11/25/1980 Height 6'0 Weight 236 Fed Titles Tag Team Champions (Banned & Exiled~!)x2 Fed Awards Longest Reigning Tag Team Champions (Over one year as champions) Biography Markus Stone first got his start wrestling for WIW. Owned by Jason Chae, Markus Stone experienced a good deal of success in this company. He also made a good number of friends very quickly. SoZe, Jole Tempest, and Shane Slade quickly come to mind. However, it was there that he also met Chris Storm. It is somewhat strange that the two became close friends, however, because Storm had ended the career of a long-time friend of Markus'. Super Starr, whose real name was Michael Evans, trained with Markus Stone. His career was cut short in a horrible accident, as Chris broke his neck during a match. Markus won the WIW Television title almost immediately, and after a few defenses gave the title away. Not long after, he was placed in the main event scene. Although Markus only held the World title once, he was constantly in the main event. This later lead to his being inducted into the WIW Hall of Fame. Another iteration of WIW, WIW:NG, saw Markus returning once again to the main event scene. However, at this time Markus began showing signs of unstability. His personal issues were many, although he kept them well hidden from friends and co-workers. He soon had to leave the federation in an attempt to resolve these issues. When he tried to come back, Markus found the federation had closed it's doors. With that done, Markus hung up the name Zero, which he had used his entire WIW career. He decided to make a name for himself from then on, and not some moniker. A few applications later, and Callisto Bane called him. Markus had a job with the DWO. A new fire was ignited in Markus, and he had a good deal of success with the federation. However, he also had a very hindering issue. Markus' right knee was severely injured from years of in ring abuse. To be frank, he shouldn't have been wrestling in the first place. Markus pushed through the pain, with some help from a virtual pharmacy of painkillers, and steroid injections. These, however, also proved to be an undoing. The medications that he was taken took their toll on Markus' psyche, and made his depression/paranoia tenfold worse. It is a vicious disease that he still struggles with today. When Markus finally did take time to attempt surgery, his knee had degenerated so much that his only option was to have metal plates bonded to the bones in his knees. After joining GCW, Markus and Tessa Windsor were able to lure Chris Storm into joining as well. The duo formed the most formidable tag team in GCW history, and would eventually win the Tag Team Championship and set the record for longest title reign in GCW. When they lost their titles to LeStatt Knight, due to Windsor's interference, Markus decided to take a break for a bit of time. During his time away, Markus aggressively rehabilitated his injured knee, and seems to be in prime condition. Him and his best friend, Chris Storm, have returned to the GCW, primed and ready for another shot at breaking another GCW record. Other Feds WIW, DWO Other Fed Titles WIW Television title, WIW World title Other Fed Awards WIW Hall of Fame, DWO Rising Star award Strengths Quickness, very tenacious fighter, and he cheats like crazy. Good submission game, and talented at reversals. Weaknesses Markus is pretty cocky, and will get caught playing to the crowd often, or just mocking his opponent. He is still susceptible to submissions as well, and his style doesn't translate well into much larger opponents. Favors his leg a lot, and is an obv Wrestling Style Technical/Brawling Strategy Now that his knee no longer gives him trouble, Markus Stone has gone from being a slower, more methodical wrestler to a balls to the wall, smash-mouth style that would probably be more suitable to a wrestler slightly larger than himself, so there will be times when he tries going toe to toe with an opponent to very poor results. He will eye poke like crazy, as that's his favorite move in the world. He does like to put opponents into the corners and work them over from there, lots of knees and elbows when he gets them there. He'll irish whip to catch his opponent in suplexes, powerslams, and the like. He also isn't as wary of top rope moves anymore, but he still doesn't go up there very often, fearing to be relegated to Ric Flair fate (go to the top for the sole purpose of being thrown off). He eye pokes like crazy. Oh yeah, and Markus Stone eye pokes like fuckin' crazy. Can't say it enough times.
