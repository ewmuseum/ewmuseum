Meltdown 54 AT&T Center — San Antonio, Texas ---- Meltdown #54 The lights in the arena dim, and the GCW logo slowly fades onto the screen. The crowd explodes into cheers, as pyro suddenly erupts from the rafters! The lights come up and the cameras fly over the excited crowd, as fireworks of all colors radiate from the MegaTron. The Meltdown music booms into the arena as the fireworks calm, and James Bryan starts the show. Bryan- The Road to Warpath has really begun to heat up, and tonight the entire show is guaranteed to take shape! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan as always at ringside with David Yale, and we’re set for another big night on the road to the biggest Pay-Per-View extravaganza of all time! Yale- Tonight should be huge, JB, because tonight Michael Stevens has a shot at becoming the new GCW World Heavyweight Champion, as he takes on The Jhub! Bryan- Plus, Low takes on Toad in Hardcore action, and much more! Right now we’re going backstage to kick off Meltdown! The show indeed heads backstage, and finds two men standing tall in the Establishment’s locker room. Michael ‘Spitfire’ Stevens and Steven Caldera earn the rage of the crowd as they appear on the MegaTron, each sitting comfortably in a leather recliner. Stevens lightly sips a glass of cool iced tea, but both men share equally intense faces. The boos slowly begin to die down as Caldera, dressed in his usual tie and suit, begins to speak. Caldera- OK. We all know what happened here two weeks ago. The Establishment no longer has Jason Dread. But at the same time, we also don’t have an official leader. Sure, I take care of the legal stuff, but we need a strong athlete to get things done in the ring. Someone who can inspire and rally the troops. Someone who is a natural leader and a talented wrestler. Someone who can truly be at the top of the mountain, the best of the best. And Michael, that is why I’ve asked you here tonight. Stevens seems confused, but nods in agreement. In his lap rests the GCW United States title belt, which he sets his left hand on for comfort. Caldera- Look, you’re the United States champion. You’re one of the best in the business. Hell, you are the best in the business! I won’t bother sugar coating this thing for you, Spitty. I need someone who can really take charge. I need Michael Spitfire Stevens to lead my Establishment. And what better way to start your new reign as leader than to become the first ever United States and World Champion by beating The Jhub tonight? Stevens seems pleased with this proposition, and nods enthusiastically. Stevens- You want the world title back in the Establishment? I’m your man. Dread was just holding me down anyway. He was overrated. I’m the man to lead us into the spotlight again, and you can count on me, Mr. Caldera. Caldera nods emphatically, as Stevens quickly stands and leaves the scene.
