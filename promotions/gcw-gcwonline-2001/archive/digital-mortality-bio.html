Entrance Music "Souls of Black" by Testament (Icy Gaze Mix) Ring Entrances The lights in the arena are suddenly off, plunging it in complete darkness. The camera flashes are the only things that light the way for the fans in the arena. Before long, static appears on the big screen and it can be heard from the PA system. Some of the fans cover their ears to protect themselves from the unwelcoming sound. The static disappears and there in the middle of the screen is a huge blinking eye darting every which way, almost as if looking at everyone in the arena. When the ominous voice stops giving its message to the masses, it goes straight into the main riffs of Testament's "Souls of Black". Digital Mortality walks out to burning deep red lights. Andrews: Making his way to the ring, from San Juan, Puerto Rico, weighing in at 230 pounds....DIIIIGGGIIIITTTALLLLL MOOOORRRTTTALLLLIIIITTTYYYY!!!!! The fans boo loudly as the one known as the Man in Black walks slowly to the ring.
