Mr. Danger Biography Real Name [name withheld] Hometown Kansas City, MO Birthday 03-28-1967 Height 6'2 Weight 235 Biography Mr. Danger started his career wrestling on the indy circuit in the late 80's. Hard work and dedication landed him a job in a regional promotion, the DUW, in which he eventually captured their World Title in early 1996. Shortly thereafter, he left the promotion in hopes of starting up his own wrestling company. The ACWF was born in early 1996 and operated for almost a year and a half before closing its doors. For the next few years, Danger persued other business opportunities. In the fall of 2000, Danger would re-assemble the ACWF for a short second stint. The company managed to stay open only for a few months, but it was in that short time that Danger noticed a young rising star in Steven Caldera. At Deadly Decisions, ACWF's first and last PPV, Steven Caldera became the ACWF World Champion with help from Mr. Danger. In early 2001, Danger assisted Caldera in the launch of GCW. While serving the on air role as the GCW commissioner, Danger also played a major part backstage in assisting Caldera with day to day operations of the new company. It was in 2002 that Danger decided to leave GCW to once again persue other opportunities. In GCW's second stint in 2003, Danger would return briefly in the role as color commentator on GCW's new flagship show WorldWide. This lasted only for a few episodes and Danger would again vanish from the company, this time for over 5 years. In the summer of 2008, Danger would make his return to GCW only this time as an in ring competitor. Other Feds DUW, ACWF Other Fed Titles DUW World Champion Other Fed Awards ACWF President and Owner Wrestling Style Technician / Brawler Finisher Move Dangerous Drop Finisher Description Jacknife Powerbomb Finisher Setup Move Kick to the gut Other Moves Danger is a veteran with over 20 years in the business. You won't see him doing lots of running or other high speed energy moves. As a technical brawler, most of his moves are going to be punches, jabs, kicks, irish whips, clotheslines, etc. Moves that can make an impact without alot of work. The technical side of him will have him execute moves such as armbars, leglocks, basically any type of submission hold, as well as other old school wrestling holds such as hammerlocks, hip tosses, etc. Ring Entrances Danger's pyro hits as he is halfway to the ring on the enterance ramp. He will stop, look up at the ceiling as he raises both arms into the air (much like Chris Jericho) then 3 bursts of blue and red pyro will explode behind him. Danger always takes the time to give high fives and shake hands with his fans.
