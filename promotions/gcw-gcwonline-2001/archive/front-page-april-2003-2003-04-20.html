Global Championship Wrestling is back! GCW announced its reopening earlier this month, and the time is finally upon us.

Meltdown is scheduled to return to the airwaves on Wednesday, January 8th! And already GCW has a tremendous show planned, as Toxic takes on Rich Rollins in the evening's main event. In addition, President Steven Caldera has promised to reveal the details of the company live in Sacramento!

Don't miss Meltdown, and continue to check the GCW website. GCWOnline will be receiving great attention over the next few weeks, and should prove to be the best yet for GCW.
