Triumph Frost Biography Real Name Triumph Frost Nicknames The Future Hometown Pacific Heights, San Francisco, CA Birthday November 25th, 1991 Height 6'6" Weight 256 Fed Titles GCW Hardcore Champion, GCW Television Champion, GCW World Heavyweight Champion Fed Awards Held GCW TV & Hardcore Titles Simultaneously, 2010 Dangerous Games Runner-Up, 2010 Rising Star Biography Born to an affluent family, his father was a Japanese businessman who built an empire in his home country, while his mother was an American-born self-made fashionista. His father wanted Triumph to have his upbringing in his native Tokyo, but when he was unexpectedly killed in a plane crash when Triumph was five, his mother decided to raise him in San Francisco instead. Being half-Japanese and half-white was a bit tough at first, but Triumph quickly learned to use his name as a beacon of power (in addition to being physically intimidating) and enforce it on others. He excelled in sports such as soccer and baseball, easily dominating on both fields, but his athletic interest lay elsewhere. He quickly took an interest in martial arts, even at the protest of his mother. Using his vast wealth, he had experts in various fields flown in from all across the world, people that were skilled in methods such as Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, boxing, and wrestling. However, when he was done gaining the expertise he desired, he had them admonished from his property, sometimes in a most rude manner. His mother died when he was fifteen, and he was suddenly left with the legacy of what his father had built. Just as quickly as he inherited it, Triumph sold the company off immediately to various controlling interests and kept the money for himself, which raised the ire of all of his family members. With his sizable fortune, however, he was able to take care of such protest by hiring people to keep his family away from him indefinitely. At sixteen, he acquired his high school equivalency through a loophole in the California education system. With the rest of his daunting free time, he lounged around exotic beaches and traveled the world. At seventeen, he met his current girlfriend, Alexis Thorpe, a native of Huntington Beach, California, while traveling through France. At eighteen, he married the then-nineteen-year-old Alexis in Australia in front of a small crowd of friends. When they arrived back in the states, Triumph started his rounds in underground bare-knuckle fighting circles. Having been successful, but with Alexis looking for something safer for him to take part in, she became his manager and began shopping him around various places. Having discovered wrestling, she decided to try and put Triumph's skills to good use. Now, with him looking for a new challenge, he is willing to take on all comers in any venue, at any place, and at any time. Other Feds N/A Strengths Superior athleticism; strong, smart, and mean as hell; deceptive speed; belying abundance of technical skills Manager Alexis Frost Weaknesses Overtly arrogant; easily distracted; a bit of an attention whore; nonchalance; rarely plans for anything and doesn't know anyone's name nor care to learn it Wrestling Style Varied Finisher Move Sympathy for the Devil Finisher Description From the top-rope, an aerial powerbomb that mid-air is switched into a double-underhook facebuster (Pedigree). This move can only be done on certain people due to weight constraints. Other Moves Special Moves: The Vicious End - Better known as the Goru-Raku Gatame, it is where the opponent is on his stomach, and Triumph criss-crosses his opponent’s arms and pulls back in a Camel clutch position, essentially choking his opponent out with his own limbs. Reppageki - Known roughly in Japanese as “Violent Tearing Attack”, it is a half-Dragon, half-Tiger suplex. (Essentially a half-nelson/half-underhook belly-to-back suplex.) Bushinzan - In Japanese, it translates roughly to “Weapon God Slicing”, and is an inverted Death Valley Driver. Shinryudan - Again, roughly translating to “Divine Dragon Judgment”, it is an inverted Boston crab. Divine Intervention - Holding his opponent in his arms horizontally, he feigns a fallaway slam and switches it mid-air into a Spike DDT. Other moves: Submission moves: Scorpion Deathlock, STF, juji gatame (cross-the-body armlock), Boston crab, Figure Four Leg Lock, fujiwara armbar, Dragon sleeper with knee pressed into spine Attack moves: Jumping DDT, Northern Lights suplex, Michinoku Driver slam, Exploder Suplex, Tigerbomb, Spinebuster, Fisherman buster, rolling snap suplexes (usually three in succession), Brainbuster Top-Rope moves: Somersault legdrop, shooting star press, 450 splash (rare, only to outside) Entrance Music "What Does Your Soul Look Like (Part 2)" by DJ Shadow
