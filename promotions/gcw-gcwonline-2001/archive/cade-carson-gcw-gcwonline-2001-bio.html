Cade Carson Biography Real Name Jason Cadence Carson Nicknames "The Tainted One", "Kitanai" Hometown Detroit, MI Birthday 02/07/85 Height 6'1 Weight 231 lbs. Fed Titles GCW Television Title Biography Born on February 7th, 1985, to a wealthy businessman and his doting wife, for the first eleven years of his life, Cade lived in the lap of luxury. With his job taking him on business trips to far off places, Cade's father often compensated with gifts and money for all the missed quality time between parents and child. So while Cade had always gotten everything he wanted, he'd never developed a bond with his mother and father, and in fact, felt more comfortable confiding in his nanny-of-the-month. On August 3, 1996, Cade's mother and father departed for yet another one of their weeks-long sojourns, this time to a conference in Japan. When Cade had said his goodbyes to his parents, as he stood at the gate of the departure terminal for private flights, he hadn't realized that that would be the last time he would ever see them again. With his parents missing -- the exact cause of their disappearance remains a mystery to this day -- and no other family to live with, Cade had been placed in foster care. His father's wealth should have afforded Cade someplace much nicer than government child services, but just as mysteriously as his parents' disappearance, was the disappearance of all their wealth and savings. So not only was Cade an orphan at age eleven, but he was a poor one as well. Thus, foster care was his only alternative, at least until his sixteenth birthday. He bounced around from family to family, always leaving without warning, and never being given a reason as to why he could no longer live with the previous foster family. It was during this time that he'd met the man who would become a mentor to him, a father figure to replace the one that he'd lost when he wasn't even a teenager. The man's name was Father Michea, and through his teen years and into his early adult years, he molded Cade into the man who would one day enter the world of professional wrestling. That part of his journey begins now ... When he signed his first contract, Cade decided that he'd honor both of his parents by using a variation of both their surnames as his in-ring alias. Cade is short for Cadence, his mother's maiden name. And deciding he wanted to carry on his family's name, he kept his real surname of Carson. Top rope moonsault 15. Missile dropkick Ring Entrances The lights go out, descending the entire arena into darkness. A moment passes, then another, before the opening lyrics of "Cry Little Sister" play over the arena speakers, a shrill noise cutting through the darkness like a knife. Suddenly, the lights begin to strobe as a figure emerges from the back, his white full-length robe like a ceremonial gown covering his body, his face hidden underneath the folds of his hood. He walks with purpose down the aisle, ignoring the fans as he passes them by on his way to the ring. His eyes remain focused on the ring, a pair of dark brown orbs that appear almost reddish in the eerie light. Sliding under the bottom rope, he makes his way to his corner, where he stands feet wide apart, arms down by his side. Suddenly, he lifts his arms in the air, throwing his head back in the process. The hood comes off, revealing a shaven head marked by ritual tattoos, including a red cross that traverses the top of his skull, from the beginning of what would be his hairline to the back of his skull. Likewise, when the robe is removed, his entire body is covered in similar markings. Lowering himself to his knees, he brings his hands together, then, bowing his head, he closes his eyes and utters something silently, then touches his hand to four corners of his body -- the sign of the cross -- before opening his eyes, and standing to his feet.
