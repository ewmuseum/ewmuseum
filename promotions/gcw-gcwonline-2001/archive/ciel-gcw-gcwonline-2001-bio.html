Ciel Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames CL // Sky Hometown Fort Lauderdale, FL Birthday 4/29/1979 -- 27 years old Height 6'5' Weight 230 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography (More of a personality description than a bio, but hey; there's not much to his history that matters. He was born, grew up peacefully as a ward of the state of Florida, became a wrestler for having little life ambition. Very unremarkable man, but incredible wrestler.) If there is a gimmick to Ciel, it's that he doesn't have a gimmick. He's past the point in his career where he feels that he has an image to live up to. There is very little ego with this man, but he has no problem standing up to those who need to be corrected. A man of principles, he does not mind lending a hand to his fellow wrestlers if they are also good people who deserve it. Heels tend to get a cold, dismissive shoulder, while faces are treated with respect. As it extends to management, Ciel will of course have his opinion of things but will generally not rock the boat unless he is messed with first. Affecting his behavior is that Ciel is a family man. His three daughters (and live-in wrestling students) watch what he does, and he wants them to be proud of him. Self-image is not a concern in the sense of fellow workers; it is the family that trumps all else. The eldest daughter is 16 the youngest is 6; they pass judgment on wrestlers like everyone else, but they hold their own father to a higher standard than all others and he comes through for them. Ciel has spent his years in wrestling developing his body and style to the point that he possesses myriad attack options for every opponent. Having 5 years of experience under his belt, he's not a full veteran, but is nonetheless a very intelligent wrestler. He is like a chess master, seeing multiple different possibilities for attacks before doing them, and each branch then opens up more possibilities, all of which he can immediately perform. There is no delay to his chaining of attacks, and the movements that he does are decided upon on the fly so that his opponents have a very difficult time anticipating what he will do. Knowing that different opponents will have different body sizes and levels of agility and counter/reversal/escape ability, he will alter his moves to make it most difficult to escape. This can include cradling all suplexes, dead-lifting against agile opponents whenever possible to prevent 'flip-outs', and frequently changing one move into another to keep counters and escapes to a minimum. All of this comes with great fluidity and speed, making Ciel one of the fastest technical wrestlers in wrestling. In a match, there are things that Ciel will not do, period. These include: closed fists (even back fists), cheating, head drops, and harassing the referee. He's an honorable enough wrestler, and anything short of a clean win is unfulfilling to him. To that end, he will also refuse to end a match with a countout victory. One thing about Ciel is that though he is primarily a submission wrestler, he will attempt pinfalls constantly. These pins, however, come out of his holds; the purpose of these pinfalls is to make the opponent use their energy simply to kick out, he never pins out of desperation, but rather as an overall part of his strategy to wear down his opponent. Other Feds None listed for desire to start over Other Fed Awards None listed for desire to start over Wrestling Style Adaptive hybrid wrestling (self-taught) Finisher Move Various submissions Finisher Description Ciel is a submission expert, possessing a proficiency with jointlocks and holds that rivals the dangerous holds used by mixed martial artists. He doesn't bother with wearing down an opponent's body part before applying a match-ending submission because he doesn't have to. Any submission, properly applied, will end the match. It doesn't fit very well into the aura of sports entertainment, but Ciel never fully went along with that anyway. Training with Shawn Stewart has improved his submission ability tenfold, so opponents should always be wary about being locked into a hold with Ciel; even the simplest technique can end a match. Finisher Setup Move No setup listed Setup Description Ciel is a chain-wrestling expert. He easily transitions holds and throws into other throws and holds, chaining wrestling moves together with uncanny ease. Other Moves ENTRANCE Never one to have a complex entrance, Ciel may have changed his music as time has gone by, but one thing that he's never changed is its simplicity. As LTJ Bukem's 'Moonbeat' steadily crescendos to the full melodic volume, Ciel steps out from the back. Last seen as a full-time wrestler over two years ago (if only that), he has a bit of unfamiliarity with the crowd to work through. However, the crowd gives him a generally-warm reception as he walks slowly, calmly to the ring. His hands are held in front of his chest, palms meeting, while his eyes are cast downward. Facial expression reveals a tranquility, almost a pre-match trance. Ciel is a picture of control, serenity. Reaching the ring, Ciel stops in front of the apron and sets his hands on the bottom rope. While he jumps into the ring, he goes through the bottom and middle ropes from the floor, rather than climbing onto the apron first. Standing up near the corner, Ciel proceeds to step up to the middle set of ropes and give a single, halfway subdued wave to the crowd before hopping down to the mat, ready to start the match. MOVELIST ~Strikes~ Palm Strikes Running Gamengiri Dropkicks Spinning Back Chop Flash Elbow Drop ~Impacts~ Powerslam Snap Suplex Inverted DDT Bulldog Lariat Samoan Drop ~High Flying~ 2nd Rope Legdrop Top Rope Missile Dropkick Springboard Clothesline La Quebrada Frog Splash ~Examples of Chain Holds~ Manhattan Drop -> Fisherman Suplex -> Falcon Arrow -> Belly to Back Wristlock Atomic Drop -> Blue Thunder Powerbomb -> Tomoe Hishigi Backdrop Driver -> Shinbreaker -> Dragon Screw -> STF Jawbreaker -> Chest-Cracker -> Sankaku Jime Northern Lights Suplex -> Death Valley Driver -> Keylock Corner Gamengiri -> Corner Legdrop Bulldog -> Armbar Scissors Towerhacker Bomb -> Face First Piledriver -> Inverted Achilles Tendon Hold ~SUBMISSIONS~ Anaconda Vice: With the victim on their back, Ciel lies across their chest on his side so that his legs are away from the body. Taking the arm nearest his head, he bends it up toward the opponent's head with his far arm behind the head and trapping the victim's wrist. Ciel's near arm is in the pit of the victim's elbow and locks with his own wrist. Ciel then pulls inward toward his own body, applying pressure to the wrist and elbow. It is an effective variation on the Key Lock, and the two moves can be transitioned between each other with little effort. Armbar Scissors: More commonly known as the Rings of Saturn, the victim is lying face-down on the mat. Ciel attacker scissors the victim's near arm with his legs, reaches over the back, and grabs the victim's other arm with his hands and pulls back on it with an armbar. For proper application of pressure, Ciel turns the victim onto their side. Back-Mounted Neck Crank: This move resembles a Camel Clutch, but Ciel straddles the victim while kneeling on the mat. His application is over the upper-middle of the back, so that as he reaches under the chin and pulls back with a deep seat, the pressure is completely upon the neck. A simple and deceptively effective submission; Ciel can roll backwards into a Reverse Achilles Tendon Hold if he wishes. Belly-to-Back Wristlock: The victim is sitting on the mat. Kneeling behind the opponent, Ciel grabs one of the victim's arms at the elbow and bends it inward toward the shoulder, but no damage is done. Ciel then pulls up on the arm and pushes it into his chest, bending the wrist downward as the back of the hand is pressed against his body. This applies a lot of pressure onto the wrist as he continues to pull inward; to prevent escape, he scissors his legs around the victim's exposed ribs of the trapped arm and the free arm itself, then falls backward to the mat. Crucifix Armbar: While the victim is on their back, Ciel applies a scissor lock on the arm with his legs and falls backwards while holding the victim's arm. Ciel applies pressure by pulling down on the victim's arm so their elbow is hyperextended across his body. To prevent escapes, Ciel takes advantage of his long legs and crosses his ankles, pulling inward tightly against the far shoulder. This keeps the victim from effectively using the free arm, and restricts the potential range of motion below the shoulders. Fujiwara Armbar: The victim is laying on the mat face first, Ciel is to one side of the victim with his back to their body. CL locks his arms around the victim's near arm so that the elbow is locked underneath his armpit. Ciel keeps the elbow locked and applies pressure to the arm, but leans back across the victim's body, and applies pressure to the victim's shoulder as well. Ciel bridges himself on the opponent's body without turning them onto the side, so maximum pressure is achieved. Gyaku Kobitsuri: Ciel is so proficient in submissions that he has actually invented some of his own; this is one of them. Sitting over the opponent's stomach and hips while kneeling on the mat, Ciel drops his weight onto the body. This causes the opponent to instinctively react by sitting up (if only slightly) to alleviate the immediate pressure. Ciel takes advantage of this by reaching underneath the armpits of the victim, locking his hands on top of the opponent's forehead. From here, Ciel falls flat onto the chest and uses this lower point to pull the opponent's head so that the crown (rather than the back of the head) will be upon the mat. CL's own head is used to push at the chin and assist in the proper form. When the victim's head is properly positioned, Ciel then leans back and straightens his arms for further hyperextension of the victim's neck. With Ciel's arms straightened and tensed, the opponent's shoulders are restricted to prevent them from using their arms effectively to escape. Inverted Achilles Tendon Hold: The form of this technique very-closely resembles a Single Leg Crab. The difference in the two is that a Single Crab actually applies pressure to the back, as does the full Boston Crab (a Single Crab simply targets one side of the back instead of the whole back). Ciel's jointlock, used here, is a legit leg submission. It chains wonderfully into an STF or a Reverse Achilles Tendon Hold. Key Lock: The victim is lying on their back and CL lays across their body as if for a lateral press. CL grabs the arm nearest to their head, the attacker applies a wristlock with one hand, the attacker applies more pressure by reaching through the victim's bent arm with their other arm and grabbing the arm applying the wristlock. The attacker pulls upwards applying more pressure to the elbow. Mounted Belly to Belly Double Armbar: Another submission invented (or at least innovated) by Ciel. The victim is face-up on the mat. Ciel sits on the chest and traps both the victim's arms underneath his own, trapping in the armpits. While squeezing the arms, Ciel leans back as far as he can. This puts a tremendous amount of pressure on the victim's elbows as they are hyperextended on Ciel's own arms. Ciel can choose to release and perform a Gyaku Kobitsuri. Reverse Achilles Tendon Hold: The victim is on their stomach, Ciel grabs one leg by the ankle pushing it forward so the knee is bent. He always achieves extra pressure by putting his arms underneath the knee and pressing all of his weight down with his chest, driving forward with the legs. A very damaging submission, it allows for quick segueway into an STF, should Ciel wish to do so. Reverse Crucifix Kneebar: The victim is on their stomach; CL grabs one of the victim's legs, applies a scissor lock around the victim's leg and falls to the mat, trapping the victim's heel in his armpit. Ciel applies pressure to the victim's leg by pushing down and trying to hyperextend the victim's knee across his attacker's body. Sankaku-Jime: A more popular variation of this move is called the Triangle Choke, but there is a difference. Ciel is lying on his back, with the victim's right arm trapped against CL's body. Ciel lifts his legs onto the shoulders, the right leg resting across the top of the back and his left used to scissor his ankle and pull down on what is a legal chokehold (more of a sleeper variation, really). The victim's arm remains pinned against Ciel's body, and he turns slightly onto his left side just enough to keep his own left shoulder off of the mat. The victim may tap out or pass out from lack of oxygen. Standing Achilles Tendon Hold: The victim is on their back as Ciel grabs one of the legs so that fhe foot is in his armpit. Ciel then puts his arm under the knee of the victim and lifts up the victim's leg while leaning back. This applies pressure to the victim's foot and ankle, and Ciel steps onto the ankle of the opponent's free leg, pressing down hard so as to prevent them from using that limb to attack him. By simply turning the victim over while maintaining the jointlock, and sitting down, this can become an Inverted Achilles Tendon Hold. STF: With the victim on their stomach, Ciel lefts a leg and scissors it between his own. He then drops forward and reaches forward to apply a crossface hold. Ciel can chain into the Back Mounted Neck Crank by releasing the leg and sitting on the back. Stepover Armbar: Also known as an Ashigatame in judo, the opponent is kneeling on the mat. Ciel steps grabs an arm and steps over with his near leg, dropping to a knee with his far leg. The victim's arm is now trapped between Ciel's legs, underneath the bent near leg. Ciel twists the arm so that the elbow is pressed against the bottom of his thigh and pulls upward to hyperextend the knee. An effective submission in its own right, but easily chain into the Armbar Scissors. Tomoe Hishigi: A very awkward-looking dislocation technique, Ciel has the victim's hips trapped with the shoulders down and legs in the air (as it would appear that he completed a powerbomb). Rather than holding for the pin, he traps the hips and pushes the body toward the head, dangerously hyperextending the neck for dislocation-pressure applied by the combined body weight of Ciel and his victim. If he chooses to relieve the pressure, an Inverted Achilles Tendon Hold is readily available from this position. Entrance Music 'Moonbeat' by LTJ Bukem
