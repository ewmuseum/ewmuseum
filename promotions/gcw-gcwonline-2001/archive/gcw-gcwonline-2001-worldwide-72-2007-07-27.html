WorldWide 72 Adelaide, Australia: Adelaide Entertainment Centre ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena, and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Australian fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: We've got another packed Australian house on hand for the last stop before Tsunami! GCW is nearing the end of its exciting and historic tour of the land down under as we conclude with the final episode of WorldWide here in Perth! Hello again everyone, I'm James Bryan at ringside with David Yale, and Dave, tonight's show should be a huge one! Yale: Thanks, JB, and you're absolutely right, this one's shaping up to be a big night. We've got so much going on in GCW, it's tough to know where to begin! We've got a new United States Champion! And that's thanks in part to the major return we saw last week, as the Hall of Famer Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins made his return to GCW! Bryan: It was a shocker, no doubt, as Rollins returned to his role as a thorn in The Establishment's side! Tonight Rollins will be on hand to discuss his return, and the Renegade told me earlier today that he's got a major challenge to lay down! Yale: Whatever he's got in mind, you can bet Jordan White and The Establishment are going to have an answer for him! Bryan: Tonight we've got a huge lineup, as we'll see the debut of a few new faces, the Television Title is on the line, we've got two huge tag team bouts, Australia's own Jimmy Thunder takes on the legend LeStatt Knight, and so much more! Let's get things started! ---- Treacherous Waters Yale: Well, we're about to get started here tonight, JB... Bryan: Indeed. Yale: That ought to be her lesson, then—if you cross the boss, you don't get what you want. Almost as though Bryan is psychic, the lights suddenly flicker. A hard-edged techno beat sears through the arena's speakers and is shortly accented by a plaintive violin. The lights dim over the stage, and the Megatron lights up with a gold and silver swirl. A moment's rotation of the image turns it into a desert scene straight out of a fantasy novel- huge, dusky gold rock formations jut out in the distance, and the horizon gleams a silver hairline. The sun's rays make visible heat waves that rise up from the uneven ground. No wind stirs the sands. The Crüxshadows' "Winterborn" slowly fades into a soft vocal line.
