Vince Jones Biography Real Name 'The Violence' Vince Jones Nicknames The One Man Dynasty, NYC's Most Rough, Rugged, and Raw, 'The Conqueror, the Killa, and the King' Hometown New York, New York Birthday 04/02/1976 Height 6'7' Weight 269 Biography **Updated on Nov 29th** People in Vince's Life (Past and Present) 1.) Jasmine 'The Crown Jewel of Wrestling'- She is the valet and girlfriend of Vince Jones. The two of them are involved in an open relationship with one another. Vince and Jasmine go way back to their childhood growing up in New York. 2.) Reno Banks 'The Agent of the Stars'- Vince's agent. He and Reno have a love-hate relationship, but the one thing that unites them is their desire to make it to the top. 3.) Keshawn Jones (K.J)- The younger brother of Vince. He and Vince at one point in their careers were both apart of a tag team in another federation. He and Vince had a falling out while Vince was a member of Core Wrestling. Vince had high expectations for his younger brother to sign with Core and watch his back; but, K.J turned his back on his older brother to sign with WMW. 4.) 'Old School'- His real name is unknown to us. He is a middle-aged black man who works as a bartender at a bar called Spanky's that Vince Jones frequents in the downtown New York. From what we know about him he seems to be sort of a mentor or confidant of Vince. 5.) K-9, 'The Big Dawg'- The ringleader of the once prominent EDW street gang that Vince Jones was once a part of in his teenage years in NYC. His whereabouts are unknown to us. 6.) ?? An Old Man??- At one point in Vince's past he came across an older man that appears to have taught Vince some of the things that he knows about wrestling today. The question is who is this old man and why does Vince Jones say that he wants to make him happy? What is his connection to Vince Jones? 7) Jack 'The Hellraiser' Wright- He seems to be a rival of Vince Jones from back in his NYUFC days. He seems to have been the champion of this NYUFC while Vince was there. 8) ??Vachon?? - Vince Jones is heard uttering the name Vachon and mentions that he is dedicating his win to Vachon. Who is Vachon and what is so special about this Vachon? ** 9) Big Lex: The former employer of Vince Jones back when he fought in the NYUFC. -- ------------------------- Vince Jones was born in New York and resided in a rundown apartment on a not so pleasant block in the city. He was born to a David and Jamisha Jones. At a very young age his father walked out on young Vince and the rest of the family. This left young Vince, his younger brother Keshawn, and the rest of his family struggling even more to survive in the city. Years passed and Vince Jones soon came across a group that he would begin to call family, a street gang known as the EDW. He along with the leader, K-9, Keshawn, and others terrorized the streets of New York City. Time passed and the crew began to become arrogant in their ways. In doing so, they planned a bank heist which went totally wrong and landed each and everyone of them in prison. Vince Jones spent two years in prison, but was eventually released. The reasons for his release have been a well-kept secret. Upon his release from prison Vince struggled to find a job that would keep him out of trouble as well as keep food on his table to survive. The details of Vince's life from this point on are shadowed by mystery but rumor has it that he may have joined an Underground Fight Club in New York which may have also been the center for other illegal dealings and activity. It is believed he fought in prize fights to make money. It is also rumored that he may have had other affiliation. It is rumored that after spending time in this Underground Fight Club as its champion he soon tired of the lifestyle and saw more a profitable use of his ability as a wrestler. The reason for Vince Jones becoming a wrestler is another well-kept secret, but whatever that reason may have been he appears to be determined to pave his way to the top of the business as well as deal out pain along the way. Jasmine getting hurt while at ringside will leave him momentarily distracted at times or cause him to Wrestling Style all-arounder, brawler, hardcore Strategy Section 1: Start off moves. Vince Jones will usually start off a match taunting his opponent and talking trash to the crowd. Sometimes he comes into a match firing on all cylinders with brawling tactics at other times he'll start off a match rather slow and methodical. It all depends on his mindset throughout the week leading up to the match itself. If he is the underdog in the match he will fight sometimes methodically in the beginning. If he gains the advantage he'll lose focus on being cautious which may lead to wrecklessness on his part. Section 2: With the Advantage With an extreme advantage or when he just wants to see his opponent writhe in pain he'll resort to using the anklelock submission as a means to torture his opponent. Vince Jones will not hesitate to gloat. With a slight advantage Vince Jones may try to pour on the offense to wear out his opponent. Section 3: On the defensive Vince Jones will often look for openings for low blows and other desperation tactics to regain control over a match. He will use brawling tactics as well. If his opponent is coming off the ropes and has the advantage he'll resort to a quick mafia kick to the face of his opponent to slow him or her down again. Section 4: Ending Minutes He will try to nail his opponent with the Lou Thesz Press and Knuckles if his opponent is charging towards him off the ring ropes. If he's trying to make a comeback at this point he'll try to turn up the heat and use brawling techniques. If he's in control he may look towards beating his opponent into the ground or just trying to injury whoever it might be to make a point and prove his dominance. Section 5: Finishers All of these moves can be considered interchangeable finishers, but his favorite and most famed finisher is 'The Silencer' which he will look to land by any means necessary his most brutal match-ups against opponents he especially hates. 'The Thug Killa'- its an Emerald Fusion or a sidebody DDT. He'll usually execute it on his opponent from a standing position. If his opponent is charging at him from off the ropes he may sometimes catch them on the rebound, lift them up in the air, and nail it that way as a counter move finisher. 'The Neck Cutta' is one of Vince's signature moves which will usually lead up to 'The Silencer' later on in the match. It is a flying clothesline from off the top turnbuckle. He'll usually perform this move when his opponent is down on the mat and appears slow to get back up to his or feet. If he's feeling especially cocky at the moment he might taunt the crowd before performing it. If he's furious he'll flash the hand sign of his old New York City gang the EDW. 'The Silencer' - its an inverted brainbuster DDT. He'll kick his opponent in the midsection, grab them by their head and turn them into what loks like the Scorpian Death Drop position that Sting from WCW used to do. Then he'll lift his opponent into the air, jump, and drill their head into the mat. Looks like a backwards Christian 'Unprettier' with his opponent looking up at the ceiling rather than down at the mat. If Vince is totally dominating and knows he's in total control at the moment he may taunt the crowd and flash the hand sign of his old New York City gang, the EDW, before delivering the move slightly slower. After doing the move he might sit on the mat acting as if he's brushing dirt off his shoulders because he knows the match is over. If Vince is desperate for the win and/or scared he may lose the match he'll perform the move and try to land it as quickly as possible without any showboating or taunting. The move can be done to his opponents through tables, onto the concrete floor, ontop of metal folding chair. In some extreme cases he'll perform it off the top turnbuckle to the outside of the ring leaving himself slightly banged up as well. Section 6: Cheating and Retaliation Vince Jones will cheat whenever he gets a chance. He often resorts to using his Louisville Slugger baseball bat to take down his opponents when the ref isn't looking. When situations become extremely desperate he'll go as far as to 'accidentally' take out the referee or have one of his managers distract the ref for him to use foreign objects outside of the ring to get the advantage. If he has his opponent backed up into the corner he'll often times climb up the turnbuckle behind his opponent and lift them up into a Hangman's chokehold. In retalliation to cheating against him, taunting, or even the crowd being obviously on his opponent's side he'll often resort to his NYUFC pitfighting roots outside of the ring. He'll use whatever he can get his hands on to weaken or attempt to injure his opponent for the opponent for the upperhand. He'll use tables, metal folding chairs, steel stairs, cables, ladders, barbed wire, and anything else to attack his opponent if the ref knocked out cold or is distracted by either his valet Jasmine or his agent, Reno Banks. Vince Jones is pretty sadistic and doesn't really care about the well-being of his opponents. In his most wreckless moments he'll go as far as to perform hardcore moves that leave himself banged up; but, he saves those for his most hated opponents when he's looking to injure more than to even win. Section 7: Manager's/Partner's interference Jasmine 'The Crown Jewel of Wrestling'-Vince Jones has a valet/girlfriend named Jasmine who will interfere in Vince's matches by distracting the ref or the opponent. She'll slide foreign objects into the ring to Vince when the ref is distracted at times. Rarely will she ever try to get physically involved in a match because she's not a fighter by any means. Sometimes when she gets overwhelmed by emotion she may try to get physically involved in a match as a mistake. She will, on the otherhand, not hesitate to get physically involved in a match if Vince happens to be fighting a female wrestler because she's not very fond of other females. Reno Banks 'The Agent of the Stars'- He sometimes accompanies Vince down to ringside for matches, but its rare. He usually will only come down to ringside with Vince Jones and Jasmine if its a pay-per-view match or an extremely high profile match as a way to promote the fact that he is an agent of a huge wrestling star. He will usually talk to the ref as a way of distraction. He will also talk trash to Vince's opponents by stating how superior an opponent Vince Jones is or by trying to recruit Vince's opponents as new clients for him to represent and make money off of. As far as getting physically involved in Vince's matches, he often brings to him a briefcase either holding a foreign object like mace, brass knuckles, etc. or one loaded full of bricks or anything else that will pack a punch so he can try and knock out Vince's opponent if times call for it. Stables: Vince Jones will often try to have stablemates if he has any come down to ringside with him to watch his back or make an attack later on in a match for him. On the flip side, Vince Jones is slow to really lend help to his stablemates because he is consumed with the well-being of himself over anyone else. Section 8: Chain Moves Can't really think of any chain moves at the moment. For the most part he's gonna be taunting his opponents and trying to belittle them by slapping them in the back of their heads, kicking them, circling around them and talking trash, laughing at them, and even talking trash to and taunting the crowd. He'll sometimes even kneel down to get really close to talk trash to them. Sometimes to really taunt his opponent if he thinks they're slow to get up he might even act as if he's checking to see if his opponent's still alive as a way of taunting the person. Sometimes he may stand over top of his opponent and yell for them to get up and keep fighting. Section 9: Strengths Vs Smaller: Vince will resort to a power attack moves looking to pound his opponent. He'll resort to brute strength and brawling. Because he has average knowledge and skill of technical wrestling he'll sometimes fight a smart match and try to ground his opponent by attacking their legs with anklelock submissions, dragonscrew legwhips, etc. Vs. Equal: He'll use a somewhat balanced attack, but will slightly lean towards his hardcore and brawling skilll. Vs. Larger: He will fight a majority of these matches trying to attack the legs of his opponent to break them down as long as he keeps his cool. If he feels he can brawl he'll brawl with his opponent, but he'll try to ground his opponent in an attempt to show superiority. While grounded he'll resort to mounted punches to the face, mounted punches to the back of the head, slamming his opponent's head into the mat repeatedly, stomps, and chokes. Overall: Great at brawling because of his life on the streets. Extremely good in hardcore type battling because of his background in the NYUFC (New York Underground Fight Club) he fought in before he got into wrestling. Average when it comes to technical wrestling. He has a knowledge of technical wrestling, but will more often resort to brawling, hardcore, and powermoves. He can use high-flying moves, but his arsenal of those is extremely limited to the flying clothesline, suicide dive through the ropes, flying elbow drop, and a rare vaulting body press off the top ring rope . Section 10: Weaknesses in general: Crowd noise against him can sometimes motivate Vince, but will often distract him and force him to the point of wrecklessness in his fights. Jasmine getting hurt while at ringside will leave him momentarily distracted at times or cause him to become overly aggressive. The sight of his own blood will push him into the realm of wreckless abandon (can be good for him, but can also be bad and make him chaotic), beautiful valets of opponents can distract him, trash talking opponents gets to him sometimes, arrogant opponents get under his skin, overly strange or mysterious opponents can weird him out and make him fight a bit cautious. vs. New Yorkers or other thugs: When Vince Jones fights a fellow New Yorker or thug opponent he can be overly aggressive trying to prove that he is the only wrestler worthy of representing New York or thug life. vs. Technical wrestlers: Against technical wrestlers he will sometimes try to beat them in their own game by using an assortment of technical moves which we'll lead to his opponent getting the upperhand on him because Vince is not as proficient in that type of wrestling. vs. Hardcore or brawling type wrestlers: When Vince Jones is facing a hardcore wrestler or brawler type wrestler he will often try to one up his opponent in an effort to try and show that he is the dominant wrestler when it comes to brawling or hardcore fighting. Vs. Smaller: Vince is especially arrogant and overly confident against smaller wrestlers. He'll toy around with them and may lose his focus if he has the total advantage. If a smaller wrestler begins to gain control over the match he will sometimes become frustrated and fight overly aggressive leading to mistakes. Vs. Equal: Doesn't respond well to someone with equal size and/or equal mat wrestling ability. He'll tend to make mistakes as a result of the fact that he doesn't have a decisive advantage over his opponent. Vs. Bigger: He can be overly aggressive against larger wrestlers which leads to mistakes or Vince trying to attempt power moves that his opponent may be a bit too large for him to attempt. This occurs more often than not when he becomes enraged because he'll sometimes become wreckless. When he has the upperhand against a larger opponent he will sometimes begin taunting them to flaunt his momentary superiority over them. When he gets angry it will either motivate him or make him wreckless. Sometimes Vince will resort to overkill on extremely weak opponents and look for injuring his most hated opponents. Vince Jones, in his mind, sees himself as the greatest wrestler walking the face of the Earth and will not hesitate to show and tell it. In the end, he looks to win by any means necessary and lets his cheating slip from his mind later. Powerbomb from off the ring apron **= trademark moves Entrance Music 'Thug Luv' by 2Pac and Bone Thugs N' Harmony Ring Entrances The lights in the arena dim and a small fog begins to rise lining the rampway leading down to the ring. All of a sudden a loud, booming, demon- like voice bellows out the message 'PREPARE TO ENTER MY HELL, MY REALM, AND MY GAME...' as the words flash up on the jumbotron screen in pulsing red letters. The message quickly fades away and the voice bellows out the message '...ENTER THE VIOLENCE' as the words appear on the jumbotron screen embossed by flames. All of a sudden a loud gunshot rings out through the arena and 'Thug Luv' by 2Pac and Bone Thugs N' Harmony begins to blare throughout the arena. 'The Violence' Vince Jones emerges from the entrance and makes his way to the edge of the rampway with a black baseball bat in hand followed by the lovely Jasmine. He stops and raises the baseball bat in the air and flashes the hand sign of his old gang from New York as red pyro explodes behind him. He slowly makes his way down ringside totally oblivious to the flickering camera flashes and the hatred of the crowd. He slides into the ring, climbs the nearest turnbuckle and looks out at the crowd as if he's in a trance, drawing in energy from the hatred the crowd has for him. He finally awakens and points the baseball bat out at the members of the crowd and once again raises the baseball bat above his head and flashes the hand sign of his gang. He then climbs back down to the mat and awaits his opponent.
