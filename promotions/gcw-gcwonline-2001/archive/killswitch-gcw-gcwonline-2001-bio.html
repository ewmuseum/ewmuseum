Killswitch Biography Real Name Tojo Suzuki Nicknames The Instrument of Genocide, KAMIKAZE, Innovator of Awesome Hometown Tokyo, Japan Birthday July 4, 1979 Height 5 ft. 10 in. Weight 176 lbs. Biography Killswitch lives life on the edge, literally. Orphaned at birth, not much is known about Tojo Suzuki, the man known as Killswitch. All that is known is that he was born in Tokyo, Japan on July 4, 1979. Being a problem child, and having the rage of a bull, he was never adopted and was raised in the orphanage. Tojo broke into the fighting scene around the age of fifteen to make ends meet. He established himself into an elite professional fighter but retired from the sport after six years after sustaining multiple injuries, mainly to his neck. Needing money, and having no family for support, Tojo began training to become a professional wrestler. He debuted with the New Japan Championship Wrestling Federation after two years of training under the alias Crimson Hawk. One year ago, after hitting his head on a cabinet door, Tojo received a CAT scan which revealed several brain tumors resulting from a rare neurological disorder called Lamplington's disease. He was given an 'expiration' date of no more than two years. From that day, Tojo began to hate life and everything to do with it. Knowing he can die any day now, he takes many 'risks' inside and outside of the squared circle. He walks around with a chip on his shoulder and barely speaks to anyone. And whenever he does talk its usually one word sentences or he's usually complaining about how bad life is. The funny thing about the whole situation is that Tojo doesn't really have the disease. He was misdiagnosed due to X-Rays generated by a broken, outdated CAT scanner. BUT HE HASN'T FOUND OUT YET. Other Feds PRIME, SCCW Wrestling Style High-Flier Finisher Move Murder.Death.Kill.Homicide. Finisher Description Double rotation moonsault. Special Moves Shining Wizard and The Sling Blade (Wrist Clutch Exploder) Other Moves Diving Double Foot Stomp, Springboard moonsault from the inside middle turnbuckle to the floor, Double jump moonsault, Tilt–a–whirl headscissors armbar, hurricanrana variants, Lariat, Turnbuckle neckbreaker, Skytwister Plancha. Entrance Music "New Born" by Muse Ring Entrances A black screen. The opening rifts of "Newborn" by Muse begins over the speakers. "Time is running out" appears across the titantron in medium-sized, white letters, along with an alarming pulsating beep. The words flicker upon the screen for a few minutes before another set of the same words appear directly underneath the first. Upon command, the words continue down the screen, like an annoying computer porn popup that you cannot get rid of. After three minutes of this display, a loud pyro explosion scares the bejesus out of everyone in the arena as the titantron returns to a black state, as if it is out of order. At this time, the guitar solo of the song cues and the man known as Killswitch steps through the curtain.
