SoCal Stretching Crew Biography Biography Very little is known about The SoCal Stretching Crew thus far in GCW. We know that the two met in Japan while JC was touring with LIONS FC and that they are both from southern California. Other than that, the two are a new team and looking to establish themselves. Landell hits the ropes as JC shoots the opponent up in a slingshot and Landell connects with his trademark discuss lariat. Landell hits the ropes as JC shoots the opponent up in a slingshot and Landell connects with his trademark discuss lariat. ---------------------------- Other than their finish, the Crew doesn't have a lot of fancy tandem moves; they keep it pretty simple. Generally speaking, the two are going to focus on injuring the arm and neck to maximize the effectiveness of their respective finishing moves. Landell is going to use moves that compliment his explosive, high impact style, whereas JC is going look to shoot in and stretch. JC is a lot more playful and charismatic in the ring than Landell. JC likes to taunt and trade armdrags and things like that. He's incredibly fast and hard to get hold of. Landell just wants to take their head off by any means necessary. Every move he goes for is aimed for the kill. Entrance Music Gangsta Nation by Westside Connection Ring Entrances Gangsta Nation by Westside Connection blares over the sound system and Josh Cameron immediately struts into the view of the crowd. He bounces to the music with his trademark smirk as he peers at the crowd from behind his aviator glasses, obnoxiously chewing his gum. He lifts the logo up from his SoCal Shoot Gear hoodie, showing off his brand. The bulldozer known as Seth Landell follows behind JC, staring intensely towards the ring. While Landell pays little to no attention to the crowd, JC doesn't even seem all that interested in getting the ring. He's leaning over the rails and inviting girls to the afterparty. Landell uses the ropes to stretch his back while JC slowly pries himself away from the crowd and slides in under the bottom rope.
