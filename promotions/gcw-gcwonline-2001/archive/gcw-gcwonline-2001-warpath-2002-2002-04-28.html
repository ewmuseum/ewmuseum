GCW — Pay-per-view

Warpath 2002, 28 April 2002

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Warpath 2002 Madison Square Garden, New York, New York ---- The Jhub vs. Badger Benandanti Andrews: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! The crowd explodes with cheers and rises to its feet in anticipation of the main event. The lights begin to dim as the entire arena focuses on the stage, waiting for the first participant. After a few moments of tension, BadgerTheme explodes into the arena, and the fans go wild! Badger Benandanti emerges onto the stage, his face ridden with confidence. The fans greet him with explosive cheers as he walks down the steel ramp towards the ring, his slight wry smile not covering his intensity. Andrews: Introducing first, from North Carolina, he weighs in at two:hundred pounds, he is the number one contender and challenger to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship... BADGER BENANDANTI! Bryan: Badger Benandanti defeated The Jhub five weeks ago on Meltdown to earn this shot at the World Heavyweight Championship! He had no idea that he'd be facing The Jhub again, but all Badger cares about is winning this title! These two men have tremendous respect for each other, but there really can only be one icon in the business! Yale: Well, it's a well known fact that I hate Badger, but if there's anyone I hate more than Badger, it's The Jhub! I really could care less who wins, JB, as long as they destroy each other! Badger reaches the ring and hops straight up to the apron. With one fluid movement he climbs into the ring and hops onto the turnbuckle, and raises his arms in the air. The fans flash pictures quickly, as Badger stands looking intensely into the crowd. After a moment he hops down and spins in a quick circle, surveying the area. After a moment BadgerTheme cuts out, and Badger begins lightly hopping in the ring to stay loose. The fans sit for a tense few moments, before My Own Summer explodes! The fans leap to their feet again, cheering in unison for the world champion. The Jhub emerges onto the stage, walking slowly. The GCW World Title belt rests over his shoulder, and he is clad in brand new ring attire. He instantly makes eye contact with Badger, and the two share an intense glare as The Jhub heads to the ring amid the cheers of the fans. Andrews: And his opponent, from Stamford, Connecticut, he weighs in at 270 pounds, and he is the reigning and defending GCW World Heavyweight Champion… The Jhub!! Bryan: And listen to this incredible response for The Jhub! The man who ended Jason Dread’s three month reign at the top of the game! The man who disintegrated the Establishment! And tonight, he’s going one on one with Badger Benandanti, a man he has never defeated! Yale: I’ll give both these men credit, JB! They’re both incredible athletes. The Jhub is the world champion, but Badger beat The Jhub to get this shot! I think this one is pretty much impossible to call! The Jhub reaches the apron and slowly hops upward, never losing eye contact with Badger. He cautiously steps through the ropes, and Badger, still locked in their stare, steps back to allow him room. The Jhub quickly heads to the other corner, raising his title into the air in the turnbuckle. The fans explode with yet more cheers, as the Jhub finally turns around and raises the belt in Badger’s direction. Badger smiles and nods in The Jhub’s direction, as My Own Summer fades out. The official takes the belt from The Jhub, who remains focused on Badger. The official raises the belt in the air, as the bell sounds and the match begins! Bryan: And what a staredown this is! The two biggest icons in sports entertainment today are locked in a climactic stare! After all that has gone down tonight, after such a tremendous event, this is what it all boils down to! The GCW Heavyweight Championship! Yale: And this crowd is going wild! I can’t even hear myself think! Bryan: And The Jhub makes the first move! The Jhub charges in and nails Badger with a big right hand! And Badger fires back! The crowd is going crazy here, as we have a slugout in the opening moments of this match! Yale: And The Jhub is getting the better of it! Bryan: The Jhub is backs Badger into the ropes, and quickly whips him to the other side! Badger bounces back, and ducks a clothesline from The Jhub! Badger comes off the other side, and launches himself in with a flying clothesline, taking The Jhub down! The Jhub is the first one down! Yale: Man! And listen to this crowd! They’re going totally crazy! Bryan: The Jhub quickly gets back to his feet, but Badger hits him with a knife edge chop! And another! Badger is laying in those chops, and he backs The Jhub into the ropes! Badger whips The Jhub to the other side, and The Jhub bounces back to duck a clothesline! The Jhub hits the other side, and comes back with a huge clothesline, sending Badger down to the mat! Yale: Good God! What incredible impact on that one! But Badger is getting right back up! Bryan: The Jhub nails Badger with a stiff right hand, and Badger staggers back into the turnbuckle! The Jhub moves in, and delivers a series of three quick stomps to the chest, sending Badger down to a knee! The Jhub finally rears back and hits Badger with a big right to the face, and Badger falls out to the floor! Yale: Ha! Look at that coward on the outside! Bryan: Badger is no coward, Dave, and he’s getting back to his feet again! The Jhub hops down to the floor, and Badger immediately grabs him by the head and sends him face first into the steel post! The Jhub falls down onto the guardrail, where Badger begins firing away big right hands at The Jhub’s unprotected face! Yale: And the crowd is still on its feet! Not a single person in Madison Square Garden is in their seat! Bryan: The Jhub lowers his head now, and charges out, spearing Badger and sending both of them into the steel steps! Good Lord! Both men topple down amid the steel, and this crowd has come absolutely unglued! The Jhub is the first to his feet, as Badger pulls himself up on the steel guardrail! Yale: And The Jhub explodes, charging Badger with a clothesline! But Badger drops his head, and back drops The Jhub clear over the steel rail to the crowd beyond! Good Lord! Bryan: Security scrambles to regain order here, as Badger hops over the rail and snaps off another quick right hand! The official is following them out into the fans, and it looks like he’s letting anything go in this match! Yale: That’s the way it should be, JB! Bryan: Absolutely! The Jhub and Badger are exchanging big right hands again, and this time Badger takes over with a knee into the gut! Badger leads The Jhub further into the crowd, as fans scramble around to see the action! But The Jhub shoves Badger forward, and Badger goes chest first into another guardrail in the crowd! Yale: Oh man! Badger is hurting here, as The Jhub grabs him around the waist and flings him aside, sending Badger sprawling back over the guardrail into the ringside area! This is intense, JB! Bryan: These two men want this title more than anything! But there’s so much more on the line here than the world title! There’s a great deal of pride on the line as well! And Badger knows that, as he gets to his feet and slides into the ring! Yale: The Jhub climbs back into the ringside area and hops up into the ring, where Badger is waiting with a big elbow to the back of the head! Badger leaps to his feet and begins stomping away at The Jhub’s skull, and this one is getting serious, JB! Bryan: These two are really tearing into each other here, as The Jhub is powers to his feet! Badger now backs out and hits the ropes, but The Jhub ducks the clothesline! Badger hits the other side, and The Jhub sticks him on the way back with a huge standing sidekick to the jaw! Badger is down, and The Jhub quickly goes for the first cover of the match! Yale: ONE! TWO! No! Badger kicks out! But The Jhub is now in firm control of this match! Bryan: The Jhub scoops Badger up to his feet, and sends him face first into the turnbuckle! The Jhub now locks on a side headlock and flips Badger over to the mat, driving him downward into the canvass! The Jhub is slowing down the pace here, and now we’re seeing the technical ability of the champion. Yale: The Jhub is using one of the most basic moves in the book here, but it’s wearing Badger down! But Badger’s managed to turn to the side, and push his way up to his feet! But The Jhub quickly turns and snaps Badger right back down to the canvas! Bryan: But Badger quickly nips up, freeing himself from the hold! The Jhub gets to his feet, only to catch a dropkick to the face from Badger! The Jhub goes down, and Badger quickly drops and elbow on The Jhub’s chest! The Jhub quickly tries to get back to his feet, but Badger hits him with a sharp kick to the kidneys to send the Jhub down again! Yale: Badger is quickening the pace again here, as The Jhub again manages to get back to his feet! Badger now begins to head up to the turnbuckle, as The Jhub spins around! Bryan: Badger takes off, and nails The Jhub with a big missile dropkick! Perfectly executed, and Badger dives out for a cover! ONE! TWO! Kickout by The Jhub! But Badger quickly gets back to his feet and snatches the Jhub back up, staying right on the champion! Yale: Badger now steps behind The Jhub, and plants him with a big belly to back suplex into the mat! Badger floats over, and hooks the leg again! ONE! TWO! And another kickout from The Jhub! Bryan: Both of these men seem like they’ve got plenty left, as they’ve got the richest prize in the business driving them right now! Badger pulls The Jhub back to his feet again, and sticks him with a right hand! The Jhub stumbles back into the corner, and Badger follows: Suddenly, the crowd explodes into boos. Badger stops and begins backing away from The Jhub as he sees President Caldera on stage. Caldera, frowning, begins heading down the ramp to ringside. Bryan: Hold on a minute, what the hell is he doing out here? Yale: All right! Bryan: President Caldera is making his way down to the ring! What business does he have down here? The action in the ring has stopped, as Badger gives Caldera the evil eye! But The Jhub doesn’t see Caldera coming, and he explodes out of the corner with a big clothesline, sending Badger down! Yale: Caldera is coming around here towards us! And… he’s got a steel chair from the timekeepers! Caldera opens the chair and places it firmly on the floor, taking a seat facing the action in the ring. Bryan: Caldera has taken a seat in the chair here, and he seems like he's watching the action from ringside! The Jhub has finally spotted Caldera here, and he’s heading over in his direction! This crowd has come alive here, as The Jhub steps out of the ring and gets in the president’s face! Yale: Back off, Jhub! You’d better not lay a hand on your boss! Bryan: But Badger is up in the ring! Badger hits the ropes, and dives over the top rope, taking The Jhub down with a huge suicide dive! Both men are lying in a heap there on the ground now, after that incredible move from Badger! Yale: Caldera narrowly escaped that one, as he heads for higher ground! Badger is getting to his feet on the outside, and he pulls The Jhub up, rolling him on top of our announce table! Time to leave, JB! Bryan: The Jhub is lying prone on our announce table here, as Badger hops up onto the barricade! This can’t be good! But The Jhub kicks out, kicking Badger’s leg out from under him and sending him down spread:eagle over the barricade! Good God! The crowd oohs in pain as they watch Badger writhe over the barricade, and The Jhub get to his feet. Yale: And now The Jhub is getting back up again! This is an incredible see:saw battle, JB! Bryan: The Jhub pulls Badger up, while he remains standing on our table! And: OH NO! DON’T DO IT! The crowd explodes as The Jhub quickly drops back, planting Badger with a Downward Spiral through the table! Both men lie in pain amongst the wreckage, as Caldera begins making his way back over to survey the damage. Bryan: GOOD GOD! A DOWNWARD SPIRAL THROUGH OUR ANNOUNCE TABLE! Both men have been destroyed! And here comes that damn Steven Caldera again! Yale: Man oh man! The Jhub is out, Badger is out, and they’re both covered with the remaining pieces of our table! This place is a disaster area, JB! Bryan: It didn’t take long for these two to utterly destroy each other, as Caldera now moves in on The Jhub! And damnit, Caldera is stomping on The Jhub! The Jhub is totally defenseless, and Caldera is assaulting The Jhub! The crowd erupts with boos as Caldera begins assaulting the helpless Jhub. However, the boos suddenly fade to cheers as NBK appears from the backstage area! NBK turns around, and close behind follows Jason O’Neil! The fans go wild as the two head to the ring area, NBK moving much faster than O’Neil. Bryan: And hold on a minute! Here comes NBK! Yale: And that’s Jason O’Neil right behind him! HE CAN’T BE HERE! Bryan: Yes he can, Dave! NBK helped him to win his career back earlier tonight! O’Neil is back in the company! And they’re coming down here to take care of Caldera! And Caldera’s Establishment is in ruins as of tonight! There’s nobody to help him! Yale: Mr. President! Run! Caldera doesn’t see them! Bryan: NBK gets to Caldera, and hits him in the spine from behind! Caldera goes down, and NBK is on fire here! This crowd is going absolutely crazy, as NBK assaults the President! And Badger is finally getting to his feet, with The Jhub right behind him! Yale: But what’s O’Neil doing? O’Neil has stopped on the other side of the ring, and he’s just watching! Bryan: Badger and The Jhub have both made their way back into the ring, but they’re both still hurting from that big Downward Spiral through our announce table! NBK is assaulting President Caldera here at our side! Khan, Toxic, and Stevens were all defeated here tonight! There’s nobody to save the President of this company from NBK! Yale: The Jhub has pulled Badger back to his feet in the ring, and he’s laying in some stiff right hands in the corner! Caldera is still getting hammered here by NBK! And Jason O’Neil is just watching on! Bryan: The Jhub now pulls Badger out to the center of the ring, and hooks him up for another Downward Spiral! If he hits this it’s over! But Badger nails him with an elbow to the side of the skull, and kicks The Jhub into the ropes! The Jhub bounces back… BADGERCLAW! Badger catches The Jhub with the BadgerClaw! Yale: Oh, NO! Badger has it locked on here, and The Jhub is in trouble! We could have a new champion! This crowd has come unglued here! Everyone is on their feet, as Badger holds The Jhub in the BadgerClaw! Bryan: Badger has this match won! The official is checking on The Jhub! But The Jhub drops down and takes Badger off his feet… SHARPSHOOTER! The Jhub counters the BadgerClaw into the Sharpshooter! Badger is caught! The Jhub is going to have this thing won! Yale: I can barely hear you, JB! This crowd is exploding! Badger is screaming in pain! The Jhub is going to retain the title here! Bryan: WAIT A MINUTE! What the hell was that?! Jason O’Neil just pulled the official down, and has yanked him out of the ring! O’Neil nails the official with a big right hand, and the referee is out! And Badger may have been just about to submit! Yale: The Jhub had it won, but Jason O’Neil has pulled the official out of the ring! NBK didn’t see it, as he finally leaves Caldera alone here and grabs that steel chair! NBK slides the chair over to O’Neil, who grabs it and climbs inside! Bryan: O’Neil is armed with a chair here, but The Jhub doesn’t see any of this! Badger is screaming in pain, the Jhub had this thing won! But the official is gone! NBK now grabs hold of Caldera, and rolls him into the ring as well! We’ve got total anarchy here! Yale: O’Neil has that chair, as The Jhub finally senses the commotion and releases Badger! The Jhub turns around, and sees Caldera! The Jhub heads right for the President, and begins stomping away! Bryan: And here comes O’Neil with that chair! Caldera is being dismantled here! The Jhub: wait a minute! WHAT!?!?!? The crowd explodes with boos as O’Neil measures The Jhub from behind, and clobbers him with a monstrous chair shot. The Jhub drops to the ground in pain, totally motionless. The fans become incredibly angry at O’Neil, who drops the chair, smiling at the fallen Jhub. Yale: I Don’t Believe it, JB! Bryan: Jason O’Neil just destroyed The Jhub with a huge chair shot! The Jhub is down and out at the hands of Jason O’Neil! But NBK is furious! NBK nails O’Neil with a big right hand, and we’ve got a brawl here! NBK and O’Neil are going at it here! What the hell is going on!? Why did O’Neil assault The Jhub!? Yale: Badger is getting back to his feet here, and he’s spotted the steel chair! NBK is assaulting O’Neil here, following that incredible chair shot on The Jhub! NBK is really wailing away here, as Badger gets to his feet! Bryan: And so does Caldera! The President is back up, and he’s slowly heading for NBK! But Badger is back up to his feet, and he’s got that steel chair! Yale: Watch out, Caldera! Bryan: Badger moves in with that chair, and swings! The crowd explodes, thinking the chair is headed for Caldera. However, at the last moment, Badger adjusts him aim, and clobbers NBK in the back of the head with the chair! NBK falls right to the mat, as Badger smiles in Caldera’s direction. Bryan: OH MY GOD! DON’T TELL ME! Yale: BADGER JUST HIT NBK! Badger hit NBK with that steel chair! NBK is out! Badger just saved O’Neil! And Badger is smiling at Caldera! Bryan: Please don’t tell me! Please say it ain’t so! The Jhub is getting back to his feet, but he’s now faced with the trio of Badger, O’Neil, and Caldera! I don’t believe this! The Jhub has no idea what has just happened, as he dives in and nails O’Neil with a big forearm! The Jhub is fighting for his life! Yale: The Jhub nails O’Neil with a big right hand, and then snaps one off on Caldera! The Jhub is battling everyone in sight! But Badger moves in from behind… BADGERCLUTCH! Badger locks on the BadgerClutch, and The Jhub has nowhere to go! Bryan: The official is back on his feet on the outside and sliding in! Why the hell is he letting this go!? Yale: Caldera’s the boss, JB! You don’t cross the boss! Bryan: Badger has the BadgerClutch locked on The Jhub! Please don’t tell me this is happening! This can’t be true, Dave! Badger has the BadgerClutch on The Jhub, with Jason O’Neil and Steven Caldera at his side! Yale: But The Jhub spins around, and tosses Badger overhead with a big northern lights suplex to counter the hold! The Jhub is still fighting! Here comes O’Neil! Bryan: DOWNWARD SPIRAL! The Jhub nails O’Neil on the way in with the Downward Spiral! O’Neil is out! And here comes Caldera! But The Jhub takes Caldera down, and turns him over! SHARPSHOOTER ON THE BOSS! Yale: The Jhub is still fighting! He’s fighting for his title! He’s fighting for his life! Bryan: The Jhub refuses to die! But Badger gets back to his feet from behind, and locks on the BadgerClutch! The Jhub is locked in the BadgerClutch, but he won’t let go of Caldera! He’s still got Caldera in the Sharpshooter! But I think The Jhub is fading! Yale: And the official is just watching on! Caldera is screaming in pain, this crowd has come unglued, and the Jhub is fading at the hands of the BadgerClutch! But he refuses to let go of the Sharpshooter! The Jhub is fading fast! Bryan: The Jhub will not submit to the BadgerClutch, but the blood isn’t flowing! The Jhub is going out, even though he still has the Sharpshooter locked on the President! Caldera is screaming in pain here, but Badger almost has The Jhub put out! The crowd, going absolutely wild, watches as the official, too scared to oppose the President, moves in to check The Jhub. Bryan: The official is in to check on The Jhub! He raises his arm, and it drops! The Jhub could be out here! The official check again, and The Jhub is out! One more time and we have a new champion! Yale: The official raises The Jhub’s arm a third time…. And down it goes! The Jhub is out cold! Badger has defeated The Jhub! BADGER IS THE NEW CHAMPION! The bell sounds and the crowd explodes with total shock. BadgerTheme hits, as the official hands the GCW World Title belt to Badger, who tosses The Jhub aside, finally freeing Caldera from the Sharpshooter. Andrews: Here is your winner… and the NEW GCW World Heavyweight Champion… Badger Benandanti!!! Bryan: I do not believe this! Badger Benandanti has put The Jhub out with the BadgerClutch, and he’s captured the GCW World Title! We’ve got a new world champion! But what the hell has happened!?! What is going on here?! As BadgerTheme plays, Badger proudly places the GCW Title belt over his shoulder, and reaches a hand down to Caldera. Bryan: I do not believe this! Badger reaches out for Caldera, and helps him up to his feet! Badger is shaking hands with Caldera here, as Jason O’Neil moves in to embrace the President as well! I do not believe this! Yale: Believe it, JB! We’re seeing it right here! The rebirth of the Establishment! Woo:hoo!!! Bryan: That’s what this is, Dave! Steven Caldera has Badger Benandanti and Jason O’Neil is his ranks! The Jhub has been screwed again! Damn them all to hell! I never thought I would see the day where Badger and Caldera could ever be on the same page, but we’re witnessing it here tonight! Yale: I’m in shock, JB! Badger, O’Neil, and Caldera have united here tonight at the expense of The Jhub! The Jhub has lost the GCW World Title! And Badger has joined the Establishment!!! Bryan: Badger has sold his soul to Steven Caldera here tonight, all for the GCW World Title! Fans, we’re out of time here from Warpath! I’m James Bryan for David Yale, join us next week on Meltdown!!!! The crowd, now totally immersed in boos, continues to jeer at the new Establishment as they raise each others’ hands in the ring, as The Jhub lies totally motionless in the ring, still unaware that a new storm has fallen upon Global Championship Wrestling……..
