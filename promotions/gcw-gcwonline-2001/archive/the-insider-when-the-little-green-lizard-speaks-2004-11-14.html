When the Little Green Lizard Speaks, You Listen!

-Ahoy fellow GCW members. I, the one and only, have been asked to do an Insider about whatever I want. Talk about freedom. If you have ever read a Ross Report, then you know what I'm doing here. So here I am. Lets talk about how great GCW is. First we have some of the greatest talent. From Shane Lane to Rollins to Jhub to Rivero. All of us have worked hard. Some harder than others, some catch a break and get a title, the others wait and get involved into classic feuds. Our fine staff makes this federation tick like a clock. We have two of the greatest card writers, two of the greatest of all times. Caldera and the man known as Danger! Assembled together we are the GCW.

-Ego. Every one in this game has an ego. "My role play was better!"" I should of won!" Then you decide to post something on the OOC board. My ego is always held down. I'm not in here to win. I'm joking I always want to win, I want to be world Champion! My time will come. Look at Mike Hardy. He has lost the most matches in the GCW. Sorry Mike. I respect people like Mike Hardy. He losses a few matches but still comes back to GCW to role play. He tries to win. He roleplays to win. Then he started winning. He out Role played me quite a few times. Look at guys like the Jhub and Toad. Chameleon and JD Billion beat them for the tag titles. Ya right, I couldn't out Roleplay them. I think I need more than an eight grade vocabulary. Those two didn't complain about losing. Some people let this stuff get to there head. In my case, I let what's in my head out into a roleplay.

-Why I have turned character so many times. Well as you know I started as a rich snob. Then my posse left me. I was then just a heel with no reason. Then on the great night of June First, [detail withheld]. Well then the next few days I was off trying to find my head, and I still am. Anyhoo, I was home and trying to type a roleplay when I couldn't. I needed a new attitude. I then thought of my old days as a sadistic crazy wrestler. So here I am today.

Random thoughts

- Toad, your not heel, your not a good heel, saying " Greetings Toad fans" Then saying "Shut up" Doesn't work. Or maybe it's just your attitude.

- Austin, I mean the Jhub. It doesn't work either to have only top face and like twelve top heels. Why Jhub 3:16? Sell out!

-Shane Lane, You better win that US strap. If you don't I'll personally run to Caldera's house and change the ending of the match. Texas is far away.

Fueler- A little more RP'n will make me happier. So much talent.

Capo - I'm stealing your title if you don't RP.

-I'm liking the new Dan "The Man" guy. He is doing really well.

- I believe that I am doing an excellent job as my new physco guy.

-If Danger turns heel, I'm ditching the physco character and becoming a biker who is known as the "Canadian Bad Ass" EH?

- The Renegade needs to role play. If he doesn't I'll have to challenge him to a world title match.

- I just read Christmas Chaos that Caldera wrote in a different fed. It was really good. I am excited to see who will won. Octavion is gone. I haven't heard from Khan in a role play for a while. I think it will come down to a couple of men. Luke, Toxic, Reed, and Lane. Or maybe Caldera will pull out a wild card from his sleeve and have a guy like Zippy win. It would be a proud day if Zippy won. God bless you Zippy!

- I like being able to know what city we are going to in our events. Then it gives me an idea. I think the city of the event should be posted on the card.

- The CWU invasion. What invasion. It's like two or three guys. There was a bigger Latin invasion last year with Ricky Martin and the other guy. I think the CWU, the new one, really sucked. They had good talent, bad results, bad storylines, and really bad staff. I was a part in the CWU. I was missed used. I quit because of well, it sucked. A lot of talent but the results didn't even make you want to role play. Every week it was a summery. Trash, Diminsioner and Shadowgade, I am glad you three are here. Keep up the role playing and leave the bad luck of the CWU back in CWU.

- GCW is the best.

- I am looking forward to the PPV. I can pretty much guess what will happen. I hope Shane wins the US title. Zippy gets the TV title. Then I run out and steal it. J/K. Jordan might pull an upset and beat toad. Maybe. I gotta go with the CWU guys to win the tag match. I hope the US title match has a very interesting stipulation. How about cage, or like belt on a pole match. Something to make Shane win! Shane Rulz! My match is going to be awesome. With an exception to the main event I believe that my and Hardy's match will be worth reading. Buried alive. It's gonna rule. Danger Will Robinson. I don't trust our commissioner. Something is gonna happen.

- I have had lots of fun doing this and I hope you liked this lets call it a GCW Report. I am Demented and Chameleon. As the good preacher once said, You mess with a Crazy man, Your fucked! Until the moon rises to a full blue. May the Force be with you.

-The Chameleon
