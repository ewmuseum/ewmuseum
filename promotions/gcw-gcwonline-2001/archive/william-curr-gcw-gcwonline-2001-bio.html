William Curr Biography Real Name William Donald Curr Nicknames "Courageous" William Curr Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday 5/10/83 Height 6'3 Weight 235 lbs. Biography William Curr was born and raised in Chicago, Illinois. He was an avid wrestling fan growing up, and this interest brought on the decision to join the middle school wrestling team when he was 12 years old. It was hear that he built a foundation to work upon, and continued to as he developed more so during his high school years. Curr received a full ride scholarship to Chicago State University and wrestled there for his Freshman and Sophomore years, compiling a 16-5 record. Unfortunately, he had season ending surgery to his neck, sustained during his first match during his Junior Season. He went on to finish his degree but did not continue his collegiate wrestling career during his Senior year. Instead, he rehabbed and conditioned. He received word that he had been offered an opportunity to wrestle at a small time promotion called RVW. Ridge Valley Wrestling found in Chicago, where Curr wrestled for a few months before being called up to a high caliber federation, Core Wrestling. During his short run in Core, before the doors shut for good, he found his way to the top of the lower card, receiving a match for the Classic Championship. He won the match, but failed to retain the belt after his first defense. This point marked the end of his time in Core, and for a few years he found himself out of work. During the first few days of May, 2011, Curr received word that GCW, the hottest federation in the world, was interested in signing him to a contract. It was official on May 4, 2011 when WorldWide 149 aired, that Curr was indeed a new talent of GCW. Other Feds cW (Core Wrestling) Other Fed Titles cW Classic Champion 1x Strengths William has an ability to grapple with the best, and can lock in holds that damage critical areas of the victim, such as the back, neck, legs, arms and shoulders. Weaknesses When William encounters an opponent that will not be an easy take down, he struggles to accomplish the game plan he sets out for each night. Wrestling Style Technical/ Submission Strategy William focuses on an area and works on it, usually applying many different locks and submission holds to get the job done. He will be looking to take the match to the mat, and also will look to score big moves such as suplex's, which will dramatically play an impact on the effectiveness of the Sharp Shooter, the move he looks to finish each and every match with.
