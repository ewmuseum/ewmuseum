House Show Report

Solid Stones vs. The Paramedics

The Paramedics made their GCW debut, as did the Stones. The Paramedics showed their veteran tag skills, dominating the match early with quick tags. However, the Solid Stones were able to come back, hitting a double team cross body for a quick three count and the win!

Drew Syphen vs. Mike Hardy

Hardy started things off quickly with some high flying offense that took Syphen off guard. Syphen took the match over with some unorthodox offense, but Hardy managed to come back following a countered superplex. Hardy then went for a Devil's Ride, but Syphen moved out of the way. Syphen then quickly lifted Hardy up and nailed a DiSyphener for the three count.

Cruiserweight Title Match: Demented vs. Kippo

Demented took over early, but Kippo came back after a cross body followed by some stiff technical wrestling. Kippo then nailed Demented with a DDT, but Mike Hardy returned to the ring. Kippo was distracted by Hardy, allowing Demented to hit the Strike from behind for the three count. Hardy then entered the ring and hit Demented with a big Bottom Feeder, and left both men lying.

Toad vs. Michael Stevens

Stevens controlled the match early, keeping Toad grounded with some impressive techincal work. However, Toad managed to destroy Stevens with a big clothesline, and went to work. After a powerslam and a spinebuster, however, Stevens would not stay down. Toad dropped Stevens out of a guerilla press, and then went to the top and hit the Toad Jam! However, Stevens was able to flip Toad over as he hit, and held the tights for a three count. Toad then hit Stevens with a Toad Slam after the match and left Stevens in a heap.

Non-Title: Blood Games vs. Alex & Jeff O'Neil

Jeff Wylde made his way to the ring announced as Jeff 'Wyldeman' O'Neil. The crowd reacted well to the Revolution, but also enjoyed Blood Games' control of the match early on. The tag champions assaulted Jeff with some great teamwork, but Jeff managed to tag in Alex after a piledriver. Alex went to work on Blood Games, bringing down Logan with a big dropkick and Dylan with a clothesline. Jeff then returns to the ring and tosses Dylan out of the ring, allowing Alex to hit the Euthanasia on Logan for the victory.

Toxic vs. NBK

Toxic starts things off early after a big kick to NBK's face. Toxic manhandles NBK for a while, in an impressive display of power. However, NBK displays a great deal of power as well after reversing a suplex, and holding Toxic up in the air for ten seconds! NBK plants Toxic with the suplex before hitting him with a 38 Special! Toxic is barely able to get a foot on the ropes, however, as NBK goes after Toxic again. Toxic pulls NBK back up and goes for a Toxic Waste, but NBK counters and drops Toxic into The Natural! However, Toxic twists free, gets to his feet, and counters a lariat into the Toxic Waste for a three count and the win.

Badger Benandanti & The Jhub vs. Khan & Steven Caldera

The World Champion and the contender to it teamed up to take on the Establishment in the night's main event. Badger started things off with Khan, and Khan took control. Khan tagged in Caldera, who hit Badger with a back drop before tagging Khan back in. However, Badger was able to drop Khan with a heel kick, before tagging in the Jhub. The World Champion assaulted Khan, before locking him in the Sharpshooter. However, Caldera charged in with a bulldog, sending the Jhub down. Caldera returned to his corner where Khan tagged him in, and Caldera went back to work on The Jhub. The Jhub managed to get back to his feet, however, and after an STO tagged Badger back in. Badger slapped the BadgerClutch on Caldera, but Khan entered the ring. The Jhub cut him off and dropped him into the Sharpshooter, as Caldera tapped out to the BadgerClutch. Caldera and Khan retreated as Badger and The Jhub stared each other down, before General Tso ran to the ring with a bottle of vodka for the two to share...

Source: Louisville, Kentucky

Unique Critique

Here it is, the official Unique Critique. I'm going to go into more detail with this one, and use the format that Pete did recently.

Caldera - I rule.

Khan - K struggled to get back into the swing of things, but I'm starting to see some of that old Khan shine through. He's never had a whole lot to say, but he still gets his point across. In reality, my only suggestion here is to keep plugging away, you'll be in tip top shape soon. ****

Reed - He's starting to roleplay a bit more. I've talked to him quite a bit recently, and I sincerely hope his attitude is changing. That's the advantage the newer talent has over Reed. They have better attitudes, and that is a huge part of success here in GCW. I won't rate him just yet, but I hope to see him improve. N/A

Jhub - Jhub has really come on strong. He gets more reads on his RPs than anyone. I'm surprised at the momentum this guy has. To be honest, [detail withheld] he would be world champion. Damn that handbook! ****1/2

Rollins - I've always been a big Rollins fan. He hasn't lost a step since I first met him over a year ago, and he's adapted well to the changing tide. His upcoming feud could steal the show at Warpath. ****1/4

Octy - This guy will sit on top for as long as he wants to. It truly blows my mind to see how far he's come since being the Rock in the ACWF. He's got a huge feud coming, and like Pete said, he's never too far away from the world title... ****1/4

Toad - Danger pops in every now and then just to tell me to give this guy the world title. Toad is definitely looking at a push. No real suggestions here. Toad is honestly one of the funniest RPers around, though Tyrant is giving him a run for his money there... ****

Toxic - The Establishment angle hasn't done wonders for him, but with Warpath coming, he's got a good singles feud coming up that should do him wonders. If he does well at Warpath, as I have reason to believe he will, then Toxic is looking at finally being a solid main eventer. ****

Hardy - I won't rate him based on some personal stuff that's going on... But he'll always be Hardy. :-)

Hanson - Hanson has come so close to the top of the mountain and fallen back again on many occasions. However, his upcoming feud should definitely get both he and NBK into a big time spotlight. Hanson has what it takes, but he needs to show it. ***3/4

Capo - He's hung in there since the beginning of GCW, and has sat in the same spot for longer than anyone. In reality, Capo has come a hell of a long way, and can definitely start moving up the ranks. He really deserves a push more than anyone. However, he does need to start showing a bit more enthusiasm for RPing. If he tries hard enough, he'll get there for sure. ***1/2

Twizz - I love Twistodium. Twizz has struggled to find himself, beginning with Trash, but he's definitely found himself now. Great RPs, great character. This month begins some added emphasis on Twisted. ****

Dread - A stellar writer, a great mind. But his title reign has grown a bit stale. In reality, I don't think he feels that his title has been in jeopardy, though believe me, it has been. However, Dread may be down, but I don't think he's out. He's rolling right through the Fedwars tourney, and we all understand his preoccupation there. That's big news if he or Hanson can win it. Still, he needs a new spark to keep his belt. ***3/4

O'Neil - That piece of crap retired. F him.

Wylde - Wylde has also come a long way, both in his RPs and his card writing. The bastard works his ass off for GCW. [detail withheld] he's really come on strong. ****

Badger - Badger has really reinvented himself with his new direction, and is one of my personal favorites to read. I've always enjoyed writing his matches, though I can never seem to get his character right... That aside, Badger is really on top of his game. ****3/4

Demented - Was a great TV champ, deserved the belt for sure. But he's willing to give up too quickly. If he has faith in himself and keeps on truckin, he'll be a big star. I've been really impressed with his new stuff. ***3/4

NBK - I really do need to book this guy more, he's good ratings. Now eeking his way into the main event, he's going to be tough to hold down. ****

Payne - Not around just now, but based on his old stuff, I'm very impressed. Some of his upcoming stuff should be stellar. I'm looking forward to watching this guy work. ****

Lunatic - Very impressive. However, his old style doesn't work too well in the more traditional, realistic GCW. He's a great storyteller, though, and is definitely a future top star. ****

Spitfire - F U, Phyl. ****1/4

Evangelist - I honestly though this guy was Khan in disguise when he first arrived. I'm impressed with where he's come, under the wing of Jason Dread. Definitely has a future, and I'm looking forward to seeing him in his first real feud this month. ***1/2

Knight - He's kinda tweaking himself a bit, and needs a definite definition of his character. However, once he finds his niche, he's going to be huge. This guy has been waiting to explode for six months, and I think the timer's about ready to go off. ***1/2

Kippo - I don't know as much about Kippo as Pete, but I can see the potential. In reality, I can definitely see that Kippo would be a better heel. He just seems a natural heel to me, and now that he's starting down that road, we'll see what this guy is made of. ***

Tyrant - Once we learn a bit more about him, he'll be huge. For now, he really is the biggest thing to hit GCW in a while. Tyrantmania has hit me... I asked Pete to make a Tyrant T-Shirt just so I could wear one. I'm impressed. He's yet to really prove himself in a big time feud, but I don't have any doubts. ****

Syphen - GCW has a basic tendency to veer away from longer RPs, but I personally don't condemn them so quickly. I think Syphen is a great new character for John, and he's going to take it a long way. He just needs to get established, get people familiar with the spy character, and he'll be set. But he's on the right track. ****

Low - I think I'm a bit more understanding of this guy just because I feuded with him in the AWA for four months, and he's kicked my ass many a time before. I agree, he hasn't quite lived up to the hype. But in reality, I don't think he should be judged based on three RPs he did that didn't even cont towards anything. He's just getting himself back into the whole e-wrestling thing. Once he gets himself situated and pointed in the right direction, everyone will find out what he's about. So I won't rate him for now. N/A

Logan & Dylan - Good writers, but the whole office smashing thing is tough to buy. In reality, I fear that he's trying to be both goth and rebel, like some odd amalgamation of Steve Austin and Gangrel. He took immediate aim at the Establishment, which is fine, but a tough thing to walk in and do. However, the talent is there, so once this team finds its niche they'll be big tag names. ***

Women - They want to be booked more, I want to book them more. But both sides want to see the other one work first. In reality, (this being aimed at one person in particular) I handle the women's division the same as men's wrestling. I have to see the desire to wrestler before I spend card spots on shows for you. No differences here. **

Now, onto the Divisions:

World Title: Right now, I like the fact that we've now had two champions hold the title for more than a month. However, with so many people in legitimate contention for it, it's tough. In reality, a lot of people might start finding it hard to get into the main event scene, simply because I'm going to make it harder. The world title, handled the right way, can be incredibly valuable. ***1/2

US title division. Problem with this is that so many people in the US title division are also considered world title contenders, Stevens included. Look for the US title to become more valuable and more sought after. **

TV title - I really enjoy the TV title. Tyrant will be a great champ, and should have some good opponents. A definitely stepping stone to the main event... Look at Dread. ****

Tag- Starting to take shape behind the scenes, but non-exhistant until I introduce some. *

Hardcore - Despite what Pete says, I like the Hardcore title. Toad is a mainstay in the division, and Badger has really stepped up the division a notch, making the belt quite valuable. His match with Khan at SubZero really boosted the division. However, it's not a true division, just a belt that random people might grab. The belt is in limbo most of the time, but it's still fun to have around. ***1/2

Overall: I'm impressed with where GCW is headed. The sky truly is the limit, and I can't wait to see where things go from here. My retirement is now the furthest thing from my mind. I'd initially planned on leaving after Warpath, but there's too much to be done. This month will definitely be the best ever in GCW.
