across his right knee, which is slightly dipped down) Brandon is always considered perhaps pound for pound the most powerful wrestler on any roster, along with being one of the most aggressive performers in the world. He has a strong amateur wrestling background and can wrestle the mat with the best of them. He can escape almost any hold and can escape moves with ease due to a strong vertical base and a brilliant center of gravity. Rarely makes mistakes and is a complete student of the squared circle. He is a machine in the ring. Once he smells blood, you are as good as dead as he will cross the line to rip your body apart before finishing you off. Almost always dictates the pace of the match up. Blindingly fast within close quarters, giving him an advantage to dictate his opponent. Aerialists chances of taking the skies are significantly diminished against him. Pound for pound he might be the best wrestler in the sport today. Entrance Music Wait by Earshot
