Ehren Moore Biography Real Name Ehren Anthony Moore Nicknames Private First Class, Ehren, Moore, The self-proclaimed Best Physical Specimen in GCW. Hometown Born: Des Moines, Iowa Resides: Washington, DC Birthday September 18th, 1980 Height 6'4' Weight 238 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Ehren was born to Steven and Linda Moore on September 18th, 1980 in Des Moines, Iowa. Ehren was the second of three children. He has an older brother, Kyle. Kyle is 26. Ehren also has a younger sister, Michelle, age 21. Ehren had a fairly normal life. He grew up on the east side of Des Moines, progressign at an average pace. He entered school at age 6 and kept average grades. He was always a high energy kid, which made his decisions to join sports in junior high believable. He was also very good in academics. Ehren entered high school and immediatly jumped into as many clubs and sports as he could. He made the football team, the Spanish Club as well as many other good-cause clubs. His freshman year went off without a hitch, earning many sports achievement awards. But the next year when he got his license, Ehren got into some trouble. He began to mingle with the wrong crowd. His sports abilities began to deteriorate, his grades dropped. He began to party....and party hard. He got charged with reckless driving, underage drinking, and tons of curfew violations. Ehren's entire sophmore year was a disaster. As Ehren entered his junior year, he realized that something needed to change. He began to get back to his old ways. His grades jumped back up, and his behavior got better. But he knew the peer pressure would eventually get to him, so he began looking for things to keep him away from the parties. He eventually found something. He discovered the art of proffesional wrestling. Now of course he had watched in when he was six when Hulk Hogan and Andre the Giant were in their prime, but it hadn't appealed to him then like it was now. He loved the physicality of it. He really got into it. He was a total mark. He continued to watch throughout the rest of high school. But he soon became less interested in it as he neared graduation. A local Marines recruiter came to Ehren's school. Ehren was hooked instantly. He signed up and was set for the first four years after school. He entered his basic training a month after graduation and was off. After the September 11th attacks, three years into his service to the Marines, Ehren decided he would rather be frontline, and wanted to go to the Army. So after his fourth year in the Marines, Ehren moved to the Army. His physical condition was amazing after his time with the Marines. He breezed through basic, and was off to active duty. Ehren eventually became a Private First Class, before being deployed to Iraq in early 2003. He was activated to frontline combat soon after and was into the real fighting. On his 23rd birthday, Ehren was wounded by a bullet to the right bicep. Ehren was discharged and put through a little bit of reconstructive surgery. He was soon back to his new home in Washington D.C. and back to watching wrestling. He had now gotten the urge to get into the ring, and as part of his physical therapy on his arm, Ehren began to buff up. He eventually reached an eight percent body fat level, weighing 240 pounds. Ehren was ready. He took a few independant bookings before working for a small promotion out of Baltimore for a bit. He's now found his way to the GCW and looks to aspire. This isn't Ehren's first public appearance though. He can be seen in Slipknot's video for their 2004 hit single 'Duality'. Appearance: Description: Ehren is semi built. His upper arms are well defined, as well as his abs. His frame is that of an upper average sized man. His upper body is very toned. He has short, but not really really short hair. Its dark in color on the sides, brownish-black to be exact. It's buzzed short on the sides, fading up into hair about an inch or so long on top, blonde in color. He will normally have it spiked up. He also has a chin-only goatee. It's fairly thick, but not too long. He has one piercing: his left eyebrow. The jewelry is a simple small hoop, red white and blue in color. His ears are normal size, as well as his lips. He has baby blue eyes. On his right upper arm, he as a tatoo of the letters 'USA' with the American Flag filling each letter, as well as barbed wire wrapping around each letter. In the back, Ehren will normally dress in very comfortable clothing. Things like mesh shorts and wind pants, ARMY t-shirts, GCW t-shirts, or just plain colored shirts. A shirt he is seen in alot is his dark blue, white and black camoflague cut-off sleeve shirt. He will arrive in the arena dressed in a pair of Khakis and a semi-dressy shirt. In the ring, Ehren will wair his favorite camo shirt, with matching shorts. The shorts are loose, and sit on his waist, and hang down below his knees. His shoes are simple two-toned black Vans. He wears generic black ACE bandage knee supports. The Discharge: Ehren lifts his opponent up for an Alabama Slam, with his opponent behind him, upside down, belly to back. Ehren hangs onto his opponent's legs, and drops to his knees, nailing them with an inverted/modified Tombstone Pileriver *also used as his finsher* sharpshooter, fisherman suplex, implant ddt, suplexes in general, death valley driver, T-Bone Suplex, neckbreakers, sit-down powerbomb, ddt, top rope elbow drop, low blows, eye rake, chokes MATCH WRITING AID Section 1: Start off moves. Ehren likes to get the advantage quickly. He most usually likes to make his entrance first, so that he can be waiting for his opponent. He'll begin laying the stomps to his opponent as they slide into the ring, or start laying forearms to their back as they climb into the ring. He will keep on them by coming off the ropes with running dropkicks as they are trying to get up to their feet. He also will whip them to the ropes and bring them down with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker - if they are the right size. He will do the backbreaker spot normally twice, before seemingly setting it up again, only to start hitting a series of two or three neckbreakers. Section 2: With the Advantage With the advantage, Ehren is most likely to try a lot of high impact moves. He likes to use suplexes to begin to weaken his opponent's back for his Basic Training powerbomb. Ehren will also being to start showing off his 'skills' by coming off the top with missile dropkicks, flying clotheslines, falling fistdrops, etc. He will also start to showboat by completing a move, and then playing to the crowd. This is the other wrestlers chance to make a comeback. It's one of Ehren's weaknesses. He will probably hit a high impact powerslam, and then go for the whole foot-on-the-chest cocky pin, only to get a one-count of course. At this point, Ehren likes to send his opponent into the ropes and hit them with moves on the rebound. He likes to hit a flapjack neckbreaker, where he shoves his opponent straight into the air, and then catches their neck, dropping them with a diamond cutter. He will also look to hit a few samoan drops, back body drops, hip tosses, drop toe holds, etc. Section 3: On the defensive When Ehren is getting it layed on him, he'll tend to try to cheapshot his way out of it. If he's in a headlock, he'll hit hard closed fists to the temples of his opponents. He'll try dirty roll-ups, holdign the ropes or tights. He'll try to get in a cheap low blow to get the advantage, but never seems to get caught. As big as Ehren is, if his opponent is a veteran, or really pissed off at him, mainly only in a feud ender, you'll see him scamper around on his hands and knees trying to get outside, where his friends the weapons are. Most of the time he looks fairly amusing doing this, and it almost never works, always getting caught. Section 4: Ending Minutes Near the end of Ehren's match, he'll start to really work on ONE specific area of his opponent's body. It's either their neck or their back. That's it. If he's looking to hit the Basic Training, he'll hit a long series of suplexes, and powerbombs. Anything from Belly to back, t-bone, northern lights, german, snap, vertical, and a very very cocky one armed hanging suplex. He will also try powerbombs. regular, sit-down, sit-out, tigerbombs, double-powerbombs, turnbuckle powerbombs. This is to work their back down. If Ehren is going to look to hit The Discharge piledriver, he'll start hitting high-impact neck damaging moves. Things like, neckbreakers of all sorts: swinging, hanging, top rope. He will also start to hit DDTs. He likes to stun his opponent and then hit the ropes, coming back and leaping into the air, catching their head and driving them to the mat with a STRONG swinging DDT. He will also nail an implant DDT, and a double arm ddt ala Mick Foley. And don't be surprised if he sets his opponent on the top turnbuckle, hooks his head, and brings him down with a HARD implant DDT. Section 5: Finishers The Discharge: (DMG: ***** Pin/Tap: ***** Special Finisher) - Ehren lifts his opponent up for an Alabama Slam, with his opponent behind him, upside down, belly to back. Ehren hangs onto his opponent's legs, and drops to his knees, nailing them with an inverted/modified Tombstone Pileriver. Ehren will use this in average matches, but only to show dominance. This move is NOT to be kicked out of. Due to the opponent's inability to protect their head and neck, it becomes very high impact. It is able to be reversed, but its only a slight chance. The opponent would be able to take Ehren's feet out from under him, but Ehren doesn't really hesitate at all before hitting this one. He sets it up and BOOM. If Ehren succesfully lands this one, its over. The 1 - 2 - 3 is mandatory. Basic Training: (DMG: *** Pin/Tap: *** Normal Finisher) - Ehren approaches his opponent from behind and lifts their left arm into the air, draping it on his neck. Ehren places his left hand under their left leg, and his right hand on the right side of their waste. He lifts them into the air, and in one quick and powerful motion, he spins them to a sitting position on his left shoulder, just like a powerbomb, only off to the left. Without missing a beat, he brings his left arm to warp around his opponent's chest and slams them to the mat, falling to his knees. Ehren uses this as a normal finisher, to finish off his normal week to week matches. It has the ability to be countered. As Ehren lifts his opponent up, they could do a backflip over Ehren, and land on their feet behind him. It can be kicked out of, but not in unmeaningful matches. If it gets kicked out of in a 'who cares' match on WW, I'm not happy. If it's a hyped match-up, with some outside segging to set it up, then possibly. But it will probably only be kicked out of if we've had a real good feud, and this is our ender at a PPV. Eternal Suffering (DMG: *** Pin/Tap: *** Average Finisher) - Ehren kicks his opponent in the gut and hooks their head and arm like a suplex. He also reaches down and hooks their right leg with his left arm and lifts them up and holds them for a few seconds, before falling to his rear, dropping his opponentto their back, in between his legs, head closest to Ehren. Variation: He will just do the entire move without any hesitation at all, just one quick motion. This is a move that Ehren will only use to finish a match if it's against Joe Nobody. Only in a match that is a sure-fire win for him. It's not very high impact, and can be easily reversed with the long hesitation involved. He might use it as one of his 'with the advantage' moves for larger matches, but will NEVER try to use it as a finisher in a good match. He's smarter than that. Section 6: Cheating and Retaliation Oh boy does he cheat. Not with the dirty pins like most heels though. He likes to show off his power by finishing matches with his finishing moves, but that doesn't mean he won't grab a chair to set up a finisher. He will hit low blows to shift momentum, and will gladly run outside and grab a chair when he has the advantage. He love's the classic Ric Flair eyepoke, or nut grab, and will not hesitate to hold his Sharpshooter for the four count after the ref has called for a break. He's failry sneaky too. He loves to make 'friends' with another wrestler to take out another opponent, only to quickly turn his attack to that same 'friend.' Another cheating thing he likes to do, in three way dances, he likes to avoid physical contact, unless they other two wrestlers are down. He'll stand outside the ring and watch the other two go at it. Once he finds his opportunity, he'll slide in, take a few shots until he's figured out and then bail again. Got it?
