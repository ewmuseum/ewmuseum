Meltdown 35, 22 November 2001

22 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 35 Teater Bowl — Northern Ohio ---- Meltdown #35 The show begins with a bang, as James Bryan and David Yale set the stage for NC-17 this Sunday! The huge show should be truly spectacular, and tonight's event will set the scene for GCW's biggest Pay-Per-View ever! The action begins with a Hard*core contest, with the winner taking on Dimensioner for the Hard*core title at NC-17. Biggs and Agent Steel go at it with great athleticism, but Biggs takes over early with a fantastic hurricanrana from the top rope. Biggs then hits a superplex, and follows it up with many incredible high-flying moves. He finally pulls out a table, sets Steel up top, and hits a DragonSteiner through the table, pinning Steel after dominating the entire match. It'll be Biggs vs. Dimensioner at NC-17! We head backstage, where Mr. Danger, Rollins, and Dread wonder where Octavian is. Next up, Lance Star takes on former Simply Perfect member NBK. NBK starts things off with a big powerslam for an early nearfall. But Star comes back with a sprinboard moonsault, and rolls NBK up for a two count. NBK, however, comes back with an early 38 Special, a big inverted powerbomb from the top. The referee drops to count, with Star out cold, but Rivero appears out of nowhere, and pulls the referee out. Rivero hops into the ring, and levels NBK with the TV title belt. Rivero and Star double team NBK, though NBK wins by disqualification. Rivero and NBK will go at it for the TV title at NC-17. Backstage, Tristain Mayhem and Nanci arrive. Nanci asks Mayhem to leave, as the groom isn't supposed to see the bride before the wedding. Mayhem leaves to prepare for his match, as Nanci begins to dress for the wedding. Tristain Mayhem takes on Dan Rivero next. Mayhem opens up with a flurry of quick offense, but Rivero takes control with a big body press. Rivero dumps Mayhem to the outside, and begins to work him over with a whip into the steps, and a body press over the guardrail. Rivero rolls Mayhem back inside, and hits him with a piledriver, earning him a near-fall. Rivero then heads up top, preparing for the Rivero Elbow! But Mayhem dives into the top rope, sending Rivero down onto the turnbuckle. Mayhem then prepares for a superplex, but Rivero holds on and shoves Mayhem off. Rivero then stands for another Rivero elbow, but NBK makes his way back to the ring! NBK, without the referee noticing, shoves Rivero off the turnbuckle! NBK retreats to the back, as both men in the ring get to their feet. Mayhem then hits the big running DDT, Hammer On, for the three count and a huge win! Mayhem then heads to the back to shower and dress for his wedding! Backstage, Kevin Jacobs approaches Nanci's locker room, attempting to get an interview. But when he gets there, the door is locked and some odd noises are emanating from within. Mike Hardy, Capo, and Chris Jordan lock up next, in a preview of the Cruiserweight matches to come. Hardy stands aside as Capo and Jordan go at it. Capo levels Jordan with a big forearm, and follows it up with a belly to back suplex. But Hardy flies off the top rope out of nowhere, sending Capo all the way to the floor with a flying headscissors! Hardy then hits a huge Bottom Feeder on Jordan, before climbing to the top and hitting the Swanton Bomb for a quick victory! However, Chris Edge and Lance Star rush to the ring, assaulting Hardy. But Chameleon charges to the ring to even things up, and the three teams brawl until they are separated by security! Up next, Rollins, Dread, and Danger head to the ring, followed by the Jhub, Badger, and Caldera. Jhub starts things off with Dread, and Dread takes over early. Dread sends the Jhub down with an early spinebuster, followed by a dropkick. Dread tags in Rollins, but the Jhub takes Rollins down and locks him into the Sharpshooter! Dread rushes right back in, and hits the Jhub with a bulldog out of the Sharpshooter to free Rollins. Rollins dives out and tags in Danger, while the Jhub tags in Caldera! Caldera rushes in and takes Danger down witha clothesline, and then decks Dread on the apron! Dread goes down, but Caldera turns around only to receive a low blow from Danger, followed by a DDT. Danger tags in Dread, who immediately punishes Caldera with a Tequila Sunrise. Caldera resuses to give up, and Dread releases him. Dread then drops a leg, and heads to the top rope. Caldera staggers to his feet, and Dread hits him with a huge cross body! Dread covers, but only gets a two count. Dread then tags in Rollins, who immediately hits Caldera with a superkick! Rollins covers, only to have the count stopped by the Jhub! Rollins goes after the Jhub, but when he turns around he gets a big knee to the face from Caldera! Rollins makes his way to his corner and tags Dread back in, as Caldera dives out and tags Badger! Badger comes in with a head of steam, and takes Dread down with a clothesline. He then nails Rollins on the apron with a dropkick, and then takes Dread down with a snap suplex! Badger covers Dread, but Danger breaks the count. Badger runs Danger off, as the referee attempts to separate them. Dread gets to his feet, but the Jhub sneaks in and hits the Downward Spiral on Dread from behind! Badger moves back in and covers, but Dread somehow kicks out at two! Badger then lifts Dread up and sends him into the turnbuckle, but Badger follows him in, and Dread moves! Badger hits the buckle hard, and Dread dives out to tag in Rollins. Rollins moves in, but Badger slips behind him and locks on the BadgerClutch! Danger rushes in to save him, but Caldera moves in and takes him down with a clothesline! The crowd goes crazy as Rollins finally gives in, and the match ends! Dread, Rollins, and Danger retreat as Badger, Caldera, and the Jhub stand tall in the ring. The show finally returns, to find that a huge altar has been constructed in the ring. A priest stands in the center of the ring, flanked by Mayhem's manager Tom, and President Caldera. Paranoid hits, and Tristain Mayhem walks to the ring wearing an impressive suit. The crowd goes crazy for him as he walks to the altar, and shakes Caldera's hand. Mayhem's smile cannot be paralelled as Here Comes the Bride plays. From the back emerges Nanci, but her dress seems to have changed. She now wears an all black dress, and her face is covered by a black veil. Mayhem seems confused, but it does not change his demeanor. Nanci finally gets to the altar, and the Priest welcomes the crowd to the wedding! After a few moments of the obligatory prayer, the Priest asks Mayhem to take the vows. Mayhem says I Do, and the crowd goes crazy. The Priest then asks the same of Nanci. But Nanci remains silent. Mayhem begins to looks worried, as the Priest reiterates the vows. Again, Nanci remains silent. Mayhem turns to Nanci, asking her what's wrong. But Nanci responds only with a huge kick between the legs! Mayhem falls to the floor, as the crowd boos with shock! Nanci begins to stomp away at Mayhem, before Caldera intervenes and pulls her away! Nanci pulls away and escapes, pulling her veil off. But Nanci isn't Nanci at all... it's Katanya!! Katanya retreats to the back, as Caldera helps Mayhem up. Then the MegaTron flickers on. Octavian stands in front of the camera, as the crowd boos his presence. The camera then pans aside, to reveal Nanci tied to a chair and gagged! Octavian laughs at Mayhem, admiring his captive. Mayhem, realizing the setup, rushes to the back to try to find Nanci, as the show slowly fades to a close.......
