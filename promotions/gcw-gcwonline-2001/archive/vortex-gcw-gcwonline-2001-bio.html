Vortex Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames The Unforgiving Hometown San Diego, California. Birthday Unknown Height 6'9 Weight 280lbs Biography Vortex can't remember much from his previous life. Having been married and having a daughter, both of them being tragically killed by a drunk driver who was rumoured to be a member of senate and a big player in wrestling entertainment. Vortex was driving the car when the crash took place, and having suffered third degree burns and lacerations to the face, he also suffered amnesia and even though the doctors expressed that it would be temporary, it has been months and only glimpses of his past have come back to him. He is seeking revenge, his past torn away from him by the stupid actions of another and his life now in tatters, Vortex has only one thing on his mind. He plans to tear his way through the wrestling fraternity until he finds the answers he needs to move on, and he is willing to do whatever it takes. Other Feds SCW - Steel City Wrestling. Strengths Vortex is powerful so it takes alot to get him down. It is also easier for him to damage his opponents through using power moves. Weaknesses He is driven by revenge so he is vulnerable to deceit and lies. He is big and powerful, so he would have a hard time against a faster, more technical wrestler. Wrestling Style Power Finisher Move The Unknowing Finisher Description Vortex pulls his opponents head underneath his armpit, his forearm wrapping around his opponents neck. Vortex wraps his free arm around his opponents abdomen before lifting him up over his left shoulder. With his opponents back now against his shoulder Special Moves Side walk slam, Chokeslam, running clothesline, Big boot, standing choke hold. Other Moves Cross-face, sleeper hold, crucifix slam, belly to belly suplex, vertical suplex, uppercut punch to the chin. Entrance Music "Paint it Black" by the Rolling Stones. Ring Entrances The lights around the arena dim leaving only the flashing of lightbulbs in cameras flashing around the stands. "Paint if Black" by the Rolling Stones beings to play quietly from the speakers and suddenly, the sound of glass in the rooftop smashing breaks the gutteral silence and two stray forks of lightening crash down from the ceiling, hitting the exact spot near the entrance way to the top of the ramp. Wearing his typical black trenchcoat, Vortex begins his journey down toward the ring with the smoke left by the flash of lightning still billowing around the wrestler's feet causing an effect where it appears to look like he is walking on air, levitating toward ringside. His walk is slow, his hands soaked in the black of his fingerless gloves stoic against his hips. His scarred and disfigured face hidden beneath a deathly black veil of oil slicked raven hued hair. Without acknowledging the crowd, Vortex makes it to the ring and slowly trails up the steel steps before lifting one leg over the top rope, trailed by the other. He enters the ring and stops directly in the center of the mat, one of his hands lifts and he pushes his hair away from his face displaying the scars and burns of his past for everyone to see and out of no where, white flames sprout out from each of the corner posts, the music fades to nothing, and the lights return to normal.
