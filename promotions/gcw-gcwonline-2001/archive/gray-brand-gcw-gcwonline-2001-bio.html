Gray Brand Biography Real Name Grayson Stiqayu Brand Nicknames 'Gray Ghost', Gray, The Gray Man, Sungmanitu Hometown Suquamish, Washington Birthday He's known to be near 29 years of age. His birthdate is only known to his family Height 6' 4' Weight 265 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Gray Brand is the older brother of Joshua Brand. Actually his half brother. Gray was born with Gray Hair and Eyes, and looks a few years above his 28 years. He has always been invovled in wrestling, but he's stuck it out in the Pacific Northwest, Idaho and the others in the Native America area. He grew up in Saquamish, Washington on the Kitsap Pennisula, learning all about Chief Seattle and the tribes. He's an expert fisherman, especially salmon and others from the area in Washington. He's also a heavy skier, specializing on the runs at Mt. Ranier and Mt. Baker in the local area. He's the more level headed of the two, and is the full blooded American Indian of the two also. He's more laid back and takes things in stride. He's been teaching at one of the many wrestling academies in the area, as was one of the original teachers of Dani Northsky. She looks to him and his brother as her 'surrogate cousins', even though their not not related in the slightest. He has recently also been connected with Dawn MacFarlane, although most say that people are seeing things that aren't really there. Gray's middle name is the luteshootseed (the language of the Suquamish and Dumwamish tribes) word for Wolf. It's actually spelled normally with no capitals and with a questionmark on the end: stiqayu? ... However he's americanized it for the sake of driver's licence and other identification. He normally doesn't go by or answer to Grayson, only his brother has seemed to get away with calling him such. He'll turn a deaf ear to others who try it. Special Notes about the KB:NAN You can concider this as a note about all 5 members of the KB:NAN. This is a stable with a long standing tradition of over 6 years in wrestling and currently has factions in at least two federations. PTC federations with factions are currently GCW and 4WW. The regular name of the stable is the KnightMare Brigade and it started long ago in what was known as the GIWF (now the UWF in PTC). Each faction has a sub name, this one is the Native American Nations, as all members but Ukyo are of noteable Native American Blood. The leader of this faction is Blaise McClancy, with Gray Brand serving as his second. The other line in the heirarchy is not known to be public. the KB:NAN is an honor based stable, no matter what role their playing at the time: Heel, Face or Tweener. They do not change tactics as they change roles in a match, angle or feud. One rule is paramount to this honor code... 'Bell to Bell' This rule is the one that must be followed to remain in the KB:NAN, with few exceptions. What is this rule? Basically as long as the rules of the match aren't changed the KB:NAN will never interfere in a match in between the opening and closing bell. Before the match? Fine. After the match? Fine. During the match? It would be a violation of the honor code to touch anyone who is in that match. This means that valets/managers, tag partners, and other stable members are not allowed to enter the ring. Valets and Managers can be at ringside, but you will find that they do not get invovled. A match is seen as a contest and this rule can not be broken. Exceptions: If the rules of the match specifically allow it (Hardcore or no-dq matches) and permission has been granted by their main sponsors that are in charge of all factions of the KB, then it would be allowed. And the final exception would be if continued battles with the same opponents show that tactic to be one that the opponents regularly use to teach them a lesson. Also would be approved by the sponsors of the KB stables. Other Feds None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Power House/Suplexer. Thinking wrestler but not mat based, thus not listed as a 'technical wrestler'.
