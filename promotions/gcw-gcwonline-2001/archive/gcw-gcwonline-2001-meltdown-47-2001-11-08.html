GCW — Card

Meltdown 47, 21 February 2002

21 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 47 Columbus Civic Center — Columbus, Georgia ---- Meltdown #47 (The GCW logo flashes onto the screen, before the shot heads to the darkened arena. The crowd goes crazy as pyro suddenly explodes from the stage and the MegaTron, and the lights come up. As the pyro display settles, the cameras pan over the crowd. They zoom in on a sign reading ‘Jhub 4 PM!’ and ‘SH*TFIRE STEVENS.’ The cameras continue to fly, as James Bryan chimes in.) Bryan- With just four days remaining until SubZero, GCW comes to you with our last stop of our Canadian tour! We’re live in Winnipeg here tonight, and things couldn’t be more volatile as we prepare for SubZero, live on Pay-Per-View this Sunday! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan, as always here at ringside with David Yale for Meltdown! Yale- And JB, I really can’t wait until SubZero this weekend. I mean, we’ll be in the Ice Palace to see the coldest man in GCW, Jason Dread, absolutely annihilate the Jhub! And you know what? He's going to give us a little preview here tonight! Haha! Bryan- Yes, Jason Dread and the Jhub meet in a ridiculous match later tonight, NBK teams up with Badger to take on the Establishment, and the Evangelist puts his Television title up for grabs! But for now, let’s go backstage with Kevin Jacobs! (The cameras leave the ringside area, and find Kevin Jacobs standing in the hallway backstage. His oily hair seems even messier than usual tonight, as he stands nervously next to a tall man. As the camera zooms out, the man comes into view. Spiked hair, scowl on his face… Toxic. The crowd boos heavily as soon as they recognize the former United States Champion, though Toxic brushes it off and turns to Jacobs.) Jacobs- Toxic, last week here on Meltdown, you turned your back on your friend Octavian and joined the Establishment. Toxic- It’s only business, Kevin. Octavian is making this far too personal. In the end, Kevin, I did what I had to do for myself. I can’t go around making decisions based on what Octavian thinks. I’m in this game for myself, Kevin, and that’s why I did it. Jacobs- Well, you’re going to have to explain that to Octavian himself in the ring this Sunday at SubZero. Toxic- That’s fine with me. If Octavian wants a fight, he knows I won’t back down. Besides, he was in the Establishment before. He knows how it works. If you aren’t with us, you’re against us. And Octavian chose not to join us. So I guess I’ll just have to solve that little problem… won’t I? (Toxic grimaces at Jacobs once more, before turning and walking away. Jacobs nods back at the camera, and the shot heads back to ringside. The SubZero promotional poster appears on screen, as the crowd cheers in anticipation of the event.) Bryan- Well, fans, don’t forget to join us this Sunday at the sold out Ice Palace in Tampa, Florida, for Sub-Zero! It’s going to be an incredible event for sure, and the only way you can see it now is on Pay-Per-View! Andrews-The following contest is for the GCW Television championship and is scheduled for one fall. ("Burn" hits and the fans start booing as Demented steps out onto the stage and pyros go off around him.) Andrews-Coming to the ring first, here is the challenger. Representing the Agents of Oblivion, from Las Vegas, Nevada, weighing 225 pounds, Demented! Bryan-Demented getting a shot at some TV gold here tonight, and a chance to bring more gold to the Agents. Yale-This could be a big night for not only the Agents but Demented himself. ("Facing Hell" hits and The Evangelist makes his way to the ring.) Andrews-And his opponent, weighing 295 pounds, he is the GCW Television champion, The Evangelist! Bryan-After a successful title defense on Oblivion against Bruce Moore the Evangelist set to defend against Demented. Yale-The Evangelist better not underestimate Demented, he may be a bit smaller than the Evangelist, but he's no less dangerous! Bryan-And both men lock up as the match starts and the Evangelist with the early advantage shoving Demented to the mat! Yale-Demented back up, and he charges into another tie up. And there's a knee to the midsection! Demented now with repeated shots to the midsection of the Evangelist. Bryan-But the Evangelist with a forearm across the back of Demented stops that offense real quick! The Evangelist now unloads with a right to the jaw! Demented goes down hard. Yale-The champion now stomping away on Demented, and now drops a knee right across the face! And now a blatant choke hold right in front of the referee! Bryan-This is the man who's supposed to deliver the sinners from GCW? Yale-Well sometimes extreme measures are needed to get the job done. Demented being pulled back to his feet now, and he gets dropped right back down with a DDT. Bryan-Demented struggling to get back to his feet now, and the Evanglelist helps him up and whips him off the ropes, and catches him with a tilt-a-whirl powerslam! What impact! Yale-The Evangelist is big and powerful there's no doubt about that! And he has the tools to get the job done, and so far he's getting the job done against Demented. Bryan-The Evangelist has Demented locked up now and just dropped him in a Cobra Clutch slam! And he goes for a cover. One, two, and Demented kicked out. Yale-Still way too early to go for a cover here. And Demented with a thumb to the eye! Bryan-I don't care how big you are that's gotta hurt! And he just took the Evangelist off his feet with a jawbreaker! Yale-The Evangelist right back to his feet however, but Demented takes him down with a spinning heel kick! And the champion is right back up again! But again Demented on the attack with a superkick! Bryan-And that kick sends the Evangelist sprawling into the ropes! And Demented sends the both of them over the top with a running clothesline! Both men go tumbling to the floor! Yale-But the action doesn't stop. Demented just suplexed the nearly 300 pound Evangelist. A nice display of power by the Agent. Bryan-And there's a shot to the throat! And Demented has taken over in this match. He tosses the Evangelist back in the ring and heads to the top rope! Yale-The Evangelist slowly getting back to his feet, and here comes Demented off the top rope with a flying sunset flip! He's got him over! One, two, and no! The Evangelist managed to kick out! Bryan-And there's a headbutt to the stomach, the champion lunged right into Demented to take the advantage back. He takes Demented down with a slam, and then drops an elbow. Yale-And now Demented just got dropped on his neck with a German suplex by the TV champ! He continues to work over the neck of Demented by dropping him with a neckbreaker! He follows that up with a piledriver. There's a cover. One, two and Demented gets the shoulder up! Bryan-The Evangelist takes Demented up for a suplex, and holds him up! What power by the Evangelist. All the blood rushing to Demented's head, and finally the Evangelist drops him! Yale-Demented came down hard to the mat! And now the Evangelist going upstairs. He leaps and drops a flying elbow right to the heart of Demented! Bryan-He calls that the Rock of Ages, and there's a cover! One, two, not quite three as Demented managed to kick out! Yale-I thought he had him there, but Demented is tougher than he looks! Both men back to their feet now, and a whip off the ropes, double clothesline! Bryan-Both men had the same idea and they took each other down! It's going to be a race to get to their feet as the referee starts issuing the mandatory ten count! Ref-1...2...3...4... Yale-Neither man moving yet, and we both know JB that the first man to get to his feet will have a better chance of walking out of here with the gold. Ref-...5...6...7... Bryan-Demented getting back to his feet, and he hits a standing moonsault onto the Evangelist! One, two, th--no! The champion kicks out again! Yale-And now Demented pulls the Evangelist back to his feet. What's he planning here? He's got him up, sidewalk slam! Bryan-Another pin attempt. One, two, no another kickout by the Evangelist. Yale-Sends the champion into the ropes, but the Evangelist reverses. Catches Demented with a huge powerslam! He takes him up now with a crucifix powerbomb! Drops him in the middle of the ring! Bryan-But Demented somehow gets right back up! Both men slugging it out in the center of the ring! The Evangelist getting the advantage, backing Demented up and sends him off the ropes, spinebuster! Yale-And the champ with a cover! One, two, thr--kickout by Demented! Both men are really going at each other here! Bryan-Demented with a thumb to the eye again. He sends the Evangelist in and goes for a hurricanranna, but no! The Evangelist catches him in mid air and powerbombs him down! There's a cover! One, two, three! He got him! Andrews-Here is your winner, and still GCW TV champion, The Evangelist! Yale-The Evangelist retains the title by pulling a powerbomb out of nowhere! ("Facing Hell" hits as the Evangelist gets the belt back.) Bryan-The champion with his second successful title defense and...wait a minute. What the hell is this? Yale-It's Jeff Wylde! Where did he come from? Bryan-Jeff Wylde came from out of the crowd and he's got a chair! The Evangelist hasn't seen him yet! He turns around and Wylde just whacked him with the chair! Yale-The Evangelist goes down hard! And Wylde whacks him again! Right across the back! Bryan-The referee trying to stop him! And Wylde just laid out the referee! And Demented getting to his feet, and he gets nailed too! Yale-Wylde warned us this was going to happen! Ever since Pete put that $100,000 bounty on his head, he said he was going to wreak havoc at GCW TV shows! Bryan-Now he's dragging the Evangelist out of the ring and he's bringing him over by us! He's ripping apart our broadcast table! Yale-I'm getting out of here! (Bryan and Yale evacuate the broadcast table. Wylde takes the Evangelist and hits the Wylde Ride through the table.) Bryan-Dear God! The Evangelist has been broken in half! (Wylde asks for a mic.) Wylde- Pete! You fat bastard! You want to put a bounty on my head? I warned you this was going to happen. This is only the beginning! (He drops the mic and heads back into the ring. He takes the chair and clocks the rising Demented again.) Yale-Wylde has gone wild! He just took out Demented. We've got a fleet of referees and officials out here, including William St. Clair and Tony Salazar! Bryan-Wylde holding them all at bay with that chair! He won't let anyone in the ring! A referee is trying to get in! Wylde just laid him out! Yale-St. Clair and Salazar coming in from behind! Wylde sees them! He just laid out Salazar! And St. Clair gets a shot too! Wylde has lost it! Bryan-Wylde backs out of the ring and escapes through the crowd, and folks we'll clean up this carnage and have more action when we get back! (Meltdown goes to commercial.) (Meltdown comes back on and Jeff Wylde is walking through the back with his chair in hand and he walks up to what looks to be Commissioner Pete's office.) Wylde- Let's see if $100,000 is worth this. Bryan-Well we've got a new table and Jeff Wylde just went into Pete's office. What's he planning? Yale- Your guess is as good as mine. (The cameras head elsewhere in the backstage area. LeStatt Knight stands with Barry Goldman, and the crowd receives Knight on the MegaTron with a chorus of boos. Knight smiles at Goldman, who begins.) Goldman- LeStatt Knight, in just a few days you meet John Reed at SubZero. Reed is a former world champion, so you can’t possibly be taking him lightly! Knight- The key word there is former. He is a former world champion. He hasn’t been able to do anything of significance for months, Barry. His star is falling, and mine is rising. And on Sunday, I’m going to see to it that I put an end to John Reed once and for all, and make certain that LeStatt Knight gets some attention around here for once. Goldman- You can’t look past Reed, but where are you going from there? Knight- Of course I’m looking past Reed! He’s not worth a second glance! I’m not wasting my time on him. Reed, on Sunday, you will be put in your place forever. Then, Barry, to answer your question… I’m moving up. (Knight’s cocky smile turns to a glare as he steps away from Goldman and leaves. Goldman watches him go as the cameras return to ringside.) Bryan- Not a wise move to overlook the former world champion, Dave! Yale- Oh, come on, were you even listening? What has Reed done to make Knight worry? Nothing! Andrews-Ladies and gentlemen the following is a triple threat match and is scheduled for one fall! ("Demons Surround Me" hits and Capo heads to the ring.) Andrews-Coming to the ring first, weighing 257 pounds, Capo! ("Born To Be Wylde (GCW Remix)" hits and Jeff Wylde emerges to boos. He's still holding his chair from earlier.) Andrews-His opponent, being accompanied to the ring by Roadblock, from Sydney, Australia weighing 260 pounds, he is the Wyldeman Jeff Wylde! Bryan-We all know about the price put on Wylde's head by commissioner Pete at Sub-Zero. Whoever eliminates Wylde from the battle royal gets $100,000. Yale-That is no way to treat the best TV champion in GCW history, JB. ("Beethoven's 9th" hits and the crowds boos further intensify.) Andrews-And their opponent, representing the Agents of Oblivion, from Belmontshire, England weighing 266 pounds, he is one half of the world tag-team champions, Jason O' Neil! Bryan-Well, where is he? (The music fades and restarts.) Andrews-Jason O'Neil! Bryan-And Capo just attacked Wylde from behind! They were waiting for O'Neil but Capo grew tired of waiting! He's got Wylde in the ropes and sends him off nailing him with a huge back body drop! Yale-Roadblock is yelling at Wylde to get his head in the game! But Wylde was taken by surprise from behind by Capo and now Capo drops Wylde on his noggin with a brainbuster! Bryan-Capo takes Wylde over with a suplex, and Wylde staggers to his feet and gets nailed with a DDT! Yale-Capo taking the early advantage in this one and still no sign of O'Neil. Bryan-You may have spoken too soon, look at the stage! (The crowd goes almost totally silent and the action in the ring grinds to a halt after Capo nails Wylde with a piledriver. Everyone is mostly silent as Jason O'Neil hobbles down to the ring to no music. He looks like five miles of bad road.) Bryan-It's O'Neil all right but he looks like hell! He can barely walk! Yale-He looks like who did it and ran, came back and did it again for good measure! Bryan-O'Neil making his way into the ring now, and it looks like each step is pure agony! But Jason O'Neil finally makes his way into the ring. Yale-Looking at O'Neil I think I can speak for everyone here by saying he should not be in there. I mean listen to the crowd, they're in shock at the condition of O'Neil. Bryan-A shocked silence has fallen over this Canadian crowd. Wylde is still down in the ring as O'Neil walks over to Capo. WOW! Out of nowhere a boot to the face of Capo! He just knocked Capo out cold! Yale-And now he's walking over to Jeff Wylde and drags him over the limp body of Capo! He's telling the referee to count! Bryan-And the referee does just that. One, two, three. And just like that this thing is over. O'Neil now, leaving the ring. Andrews-The winner of this match Jeff Wylde! Yale-Roadblock pulling Wylde to his feet, and trying to tell him what happened. Capo staggering back to his feet as well now. Bryan-The crowd still in a shocked silence as O'Neil makes his way back up the ramp still limping. And wait, look at the top of the ramp. It's Payne! Yale-O'Neil just reached the top of the ramp and Payne just punched O'Neil in the head! And now Payne is just assaulting Jason O'Neil! Bryan-He just slammed his head into one of the MegaTron supports. I think O'Neil may have been busted open by that shot! And now Payne slams O'Neil on the steel ramp! Yale-Payne has had it in for O'Neil for quite a while, and he's taking him out here! Bryan-O'Neil is busted wide open and now Payne, he's not going to do what I think he is! Yale-He's setting him up for a Lesson in Payne on the steel stage! And there it is! A Lesson in Payne! Bryan-Dear God! Jason O'Neil may be dead! And now Payne tosses O'Neil off the stage through a table! Jason O'Neil is broken in half! Yale-And look at Payne's face! He's got an evil sadistic grin on his face! Look at Capo, Wylde and Roadblock, they don't know what to make of this. Bryan-The fans are sitting here in a shocked silence! I'm in shock! ("South Texas Deathride" hits as Payne stares at the unmoving body of O'Neil. He finally turns and walks back through the curtain.) Bryan-Fans we have to take a break but we'll be right back! (Meltdown fades to commercial showing Capo, Wylde and Roadblock looking at each other in shock.) (Meltdown returns from commercial break with "During the break" in the bottom left hand corner of the screen. We can see Jason O'Neil being loaded into an ambulance with a neck brace on.) Bryan-During the break folks the EMT's came out and carted off Jason O'Neil after the assault by Payne. (The scene changes to the end of the match.) Bryan-O'Neil came out after the match had started when Capo was wailing on Jeff Wylde. He stepped into the ring, booted Capo in the face and dragged Wylde on top of Capo to give Wylde the win. (Scene cuts to the assault by Payne ending with Payne tossing O'Neil off the stage and through the table.) Bryan-O'Neil then staggered from the ring and was attacked by Payne who hit him with a Lesson in Payne before tossing him off the stage and through that table. O'Neil is being taken to a local medical facility and we'll have an update on his condition as soon as we can. (Meltdown returns from commercials and is again outside Pete's office. Jeff Wylde comes out with a smile on his face. He's holding a can of gasoline.) Yale-We're back here on Meltdown and there's Jeff Wylde coming out of Pete's office. If you can call it that. Bryan-He's got a can of gasoline. What the hell is he doing? (Wylde turns to the camera with an evil sadistic smile on his face.) Wylde-Is this worth a hundred grand Pete? (Wylde tosses a match onto Pete's desk and the desk goes up as it has been totally soaked with gasoline. All of Pete's food goes up as well. Jeff walks away whistling to himself. He hears voices behind him and starts running when he sees Pete leading a bunch of GCW officials.) Pete-There he is! (Jeff takes off and reaches the parking lot where Roadblock is waiting in Jeff's car. The passenger door is open. Jeff gets in the car and the car screams out of the parking lot before Jeff has even closed the door.) Jeff-GO! GO! GO! (Pete and the officials look after the car in disgust.) Pete (yelling)-This isn't over Wylde! Not by a long shot! Bryan-Jeff Wylde is taking this bounty on his head personally! Yale-Wouldn't you? Bryan-But I think he's taking it too far. Yale-That's a matter of opinion. Bryan-Anyway we have a match to get to, so let's go to the ring. Andrews-The following tag team match is scheduled for one fall. ("Badger Theme" hits and the crowd goes wild.) Andrews-Coming to the ring first, from North Carolina, weighing 220 pounds, Badger Benandanti! Bryan-The self proclaimed master of Bad-Fu, his own form of Kung-Fu, Double B teaming up with of all people, the US champion NBK! Yale-NBK who will be defending that very title against one of the men he'll be facing here tonight in Michael Stevens at Sub-Zero. Just three days away on pay-per-view. ("Unforgiven" hits and NBK emerges onto the ramp.) Andrews-His tag-team partner, from Dallas, Texas, weighing 265 pounds, he is the GCW United States champion, NBK, the Natural Born Killer! Bryan-And there he is, the US champ the Natural Born Killer. Yale-NBK looks to be all business tonight JB! Bryan-Let's not fail to mention that we found out last week that it was our own world champion, Jason Dread who dropped the Jhub 20 feet to the mat at Rampage, allowing NBK to win that US title he's got wrapped around his waist. Yale-The arrogant Jhub got exactly what he deserved. ("Established" hits and the crowd starts booing.) Andrews-And their opponents, representing the Establishment, at a total combined weight of 534 pounds, Michael "Spitfire" Stevens and Toxic! Bryan-Toxic became the newest man to sell his soul to Steven Caldera last week and join the Establishment! Yale-You'd better watch what you say about Caldera JB! He don't forget he threatened to fire you just a couple of weeks ago. Bryan-The Establishment has reached the ring and Badger and NBK don't waste any time! Badger beating on Toxic and NBK takes on Stevens! Yale-This is starting out as a full scale slugfest here! Badger is stomping Toxic down in the corner and NBK just tossed Spitfire over the top rope to the floor! Bryan-And look at this, Badger just whipped Toxic in the direction of NBK and NBK just clotheslined Toxic! He nearly turned Toxic inside out with that shot. Yale-The referee sends Badger out and it's going to be Toxic and NBK starting things off. NBK goes in for a full armdrag twist and snaps Toxic down to the mat. He then drops a leg across the arm, and now bends the arm behind Toxic in a hammerlock. Bryan-Smart wrestling by the champion. Control the pace of the match and wear the bigger man down. Toxic is a big guy and if NBK and Badger want to have a chance against him they need to dictate the pace of the match. Yale-There's a hangman DDT by NBK and a tag to Badger. And a double suplex to Toxic! Double B stays on him by dropping a leg across the throat of Toxic and goes for a quick cover, but Toxic kicked out at one. Bryan-Badger takes him over with a fisherman's brainbuster! Badger sends Toxic to the ropes now and nails him with a back heel kick! Yale-He nearly took Toxic's head off! And there's a T-Bone suplex and Toxic wisely rolls over to his corner and tags in Stevens. Bryan-And look at NBK! He wants in there in the worst way! He's practically begging BadBen to tag him in. And Badger complies! Yale-Both men meeting center ring. NBK and Spitfire talking trash nose to nose now. And Stevens just shoved NBK! And NBK just nailed Stevens, and he stays on him now pummeling Stevens with rights! Bryan-He sends Spitfire to the ropes and catches him with a vicious spinebuster in the middle of the ring! What impact! He's staying right on top of him and pulls him up only to drop him with a Death Valley driver! Cover by NBK! One, two, kickout by Spitfire! Yale-Spitty showing a lot of resilance here, and a little bit of dirty pool as he just jammed his thumb into NBK's eye! Bryan-And you condone this? Yale-It's only cheating if you get caught JB. Bryan-Well, either way there's an armdrag and he pulls NBK over to his corner and tags the newest Establishment member Toxic back in. Toxic climbs to the top rope and the near 300 pounder drops an elbow right to the arm of the champion! Yale-And Toxic is now dishing out the beating. Not wasting any time he just turned NBK inside out with a belly to back suplex! He nearly threw him into the next providence! And there's a piledriver! Bryan-NBK holding his head and writhing in pain on the mat, and Toxic just stomped him in the head! Yale-Don't hit him there Toxic! Hit him where it will hurt! Bryan-You're such a sweet guy. Yale-I know. Bryan-Toxic continues his assualt on the US champion. He grabs NBK and chokeslams him to the mat! He goes for a cover. One, two and Badger breaks up the count! Yale-Toxic drags NBK back over to his corner and tags Stevens back in. Stevens unloads to the face of the Killer. He takes NBK and sends him into the ropes on the far side and nails him with a stungun! Bryan-That top rope digging right into the throat of NBK and the US champion is in trouble here! If Stevens keeps up this kind of offense at Sub-Zero in three days we could have a new US champion. Yale-Stevens staying on NBK, slapping on an abdominal stretch. The referee getting in position to ask NBK. Bryan-And look at this, Toxic taking Stevens' hand helping wrench NBK even more. Yale-And they let go when the referee looks. Spitfire punches NBK in the ribs before grabbing Toxic's hand again. Bryan-And this time the referee sees it! And kicks their hands apart! Yale-Can he do that? Bryan-Yes, he's the referee, he's supposed to enforce the rules when they're broken. Yale-A Russian leg sweep takes NBK down and Toxic gets tagged back in. Toxic picks NBK up in a gorilla press slam. What power by Toxic! Bryan-And there's an overhead suplex, but that may have been a mistake as he tossed NBK over to his corner and there's a tag to Badger! Yale-Badger comes storming into the ring and takes down Toxic with a clothesline and a forearm shot to the face of Stevens on the outside knocking him off the apron! Bryan-Toxic gets back up and Badger takes him down with a dropkick! He nabs Toxic as he gets back to his feet and nails him with a reverse DDT! Yale-Cover by Badger! One, two, and Stevens just pulled Badger out of the ring! Bryan-And here comes NBK! He's taking on Toxic inside, and Stevens just rammed Badger head first into the steel steps! Double B may be out of it! Yale-Stevens in the ring now and the referee is trying to restore order! He sees Badger trying to get back in the ring! How in the hell is BadBen even standing? Bryan-I don't know but the referee is trying to keep him out of the ring! And the referee needs to turn around! Yale-And look out, NBK just got dropped in Toxic Waste! And wait, not another one! Another Toxic Waste! Bryan-Dear God! NBK might be out cold! And here comes Stevens, Inferno DDT! And a cover! No not like this! Yale-Toxic getting rid of Badger by tossing him out of the ring! The referee sees the cover! Bryan-No! Dammit! Not like this! Yale-Here's the count! One, two, three! The Establishment wins! The Establishment wins! Andrews-The winners of this match, Toxic and Michael "Spitfire" Stevens. Yale-A huge win for the Establishment as we go into Sub-Zero this sunday! Bryan-Stevens and Toxic leaving the ring now as Badger and the referee check on NBK, the Killer's eyes are glazed over, but he seems to be okay! And fans don't go away because when we come back it'll be the Jhub taking on Jason Dread. Yale-With our esteemed president Steven Caldera as the guest referee, Toxic as the timekeeper, and Khan as the guest timekeeper. Bryan-The deck looks to be stacked against the Jhub, and we'll find out next!
