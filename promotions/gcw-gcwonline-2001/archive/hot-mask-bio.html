Hot Mask Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Sultan of Smut, Czar of Spank, Rasputin of Pootin, The God Damn God-Damner, et. al. Hometown Gary, IN Birthday 1/12/1983 Height 5'9 Weight 200 lbs. Biography One time, Hot Mask slipped into some cat pee during a match and still won the match. And this other time, Hot Mask made Dustin Diamond dress up in a phallus costume just because he said so. OOH OOH and this OTHER time, Hot Mask won a wing-eating competition and only had to eat ONE wing because everyone else was scared of him! Hot Mask is a verifiable horse's ass, and has been pretty much unseen since his departure from the UWF and the unwinding of Awesome and Win. Now, with a beefed-up physique and a renewed desire for competition, Mask is out to make amends with the wrestling audience he deserted and go full-force in the GCW -- with a whole new arsenal of cock references at his disposal. Other Feds Neon, UWF, AWE Other Fed Titles Neon Television Champion, UWF Television Champion Other Fed Awards UWF Champion of Champions winner Wrestling Style Technical/Comedic Finisher Move Brown Star Press Finisher Description Second-rope Bonsai drop onto opponent's face Finisher Setup Move The Facial WW Entrance Slaying keyboard begins to blast through the PA speakers, signaling the start of the theme from the greatest television show of all time: Perfect Strangers. From behind the curtain, Hot Mask walks out to a rousing or not-so rousing chorus of cheers as he does the Tootsee Roll momentarily to the tune of the music. Trotting down to the ring (ACTUALLY TROTTING,) he rolls underneath the bottom rope and mounts the opposing turnbuckle. Actually mounting it, and humping it while holding one arm in the air for balance. Once finished with this display of childish behavior, he stands poised for the match to begin. Special Moves Beavis & Butt-Head, Schiavo Special Other Moves 1) Bite. To any body part that might be sensitive to intense pain. ANY body part. 2) Choke. Will opt for a choking maneuver over pretty much anything. Might even try to choke you after landing a punch, even if it's only for a second. 3) Stomp. Regular, Garvin-style, double-style, on opponent's toes. The possibilities are endless. 4) Eye Gouge/Poke/Spike/Fist/Toe Stomp. The eyes are a funny thing: they allow the opponent to see. Eliminate this instrument, and you shall go far. 5) Tree of Woe, followed by punching their face, grinding his crotch in their face, spitting in their face, stomping their face, or running really fast into their face. 6) Bronco buster. With obligatory homoerotic pelvic thrusts. 7) Bully moves. Wedgies, melvins, noogies, arm burns. Rarely effective. 8) Inverted atomic drop. If opponent is too big to lift (275+ lbs.), a knee to the balls works just fine. 9) Various "audience becomes part of the action" spots: Mask will hold the opponent's head in place and encourage a fan to come up and belch in opponent's face, put opponent in headlock and allow a fan to spank the bent-over opponent, etc. 10) Enjoys trying to make a referee inadvertently attack his foe, by tripping them so they fall down on a lying opponent, making fun of their mother so they're prompted to punch him but accidentally hit the opponent instead, etc. 11) The Facial - Mask takes the opponent to his knees, then proceeds to mount around his/her face and fall forward, causing their face to be creampied. Usually a set-up to the Brown Star Press. 12) Beavis & Butt-Head - Mask will sharply kick the opponent in the crotch and toss them down to the mat. With opponent lying face-down, Mask then bends over and rams his ass into the back of the opponent's head, slamming it repeatedly into the mat. Similar to a push-up facebuster, but looks funnier. 13) Schiavo Special - Mandible claw. Awful bulemia joke. Alternate to finisher. Holds: Stretch Muffler, Indian death lock, Fish hook to mouth, Face claw, Armpit claw Attacks: Bell clap, Stinger splash, Bionic elbow, Knee drop, Elbow drop, Headbutt, Mule kick, Heart punch, Rake (eyes, back, etc.), Hip attack Throws: Single arm DDT, Cutter, Wheelbarrow driver, Inverted stomp facebreaker, Facebuster, Guillotine drop, Shoulder jawbreaker, Monkey flip, Shin breaker, Ranhei, Complete Shot Entrance Music "Nothing's Gonna Stop Me Now" - David Pomeranz
