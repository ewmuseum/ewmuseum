Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #26 – Calgary, Alberta

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Calgary crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “TREY GOT ROBBED” and “Pete Sucks!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Calgary, Alberta” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We’re live with another sold-out and jam-packed edition of GCW’s WORLDWIDE! We’re just three days away from FallOut, and a main event submission match that might just change everything in Global Championship Wrestling! Hello, fans, I’m James Bryan back at ringside with The Jhub and Pete, and we’re set for one more show before FallOut this Sunday.

Jhub: And we’re live in CALGARY…… ALBERTACANADA. And damnit, I’m excited to be back in the city where Caldera and I got our start. Guys, there aren’t too many more famous wrestling towns in the world than Calgary, so it’s a fitting place for tonight’s WorldWide.

Pete: And it’s only fitting that tonight in Calgary we’ve got a Submission Lock Challenge match! LeStatt Knight and Michael Stevens will put their respective submission holds on display in tonight’s main event, in an attempt to get the psychological edge on their opponent going into FallOut!

Bryan: That’s going to be a hell of a main event, folks, but there’s lots more on tap. Seymour Almasy takes on Trey Tremendous tonight, and guys, I think Trey’s losing his mind a little bit.

Bryan: And we’ve got the second round two matchup in our Cruiserweight Title Tournament, as Rei Tiu takes on Zodiac, in what could be a hotly contested matchup. The winner takes on Aiden Jackson for the GCW Cruiserweight Title at FallOut!

Pete: Aiden had an impressive win over Coyle last week, thanks in no small part to X! But if you’ll recall, fans, Coyle was reported to have been injured and out for six weeks earlier this week. But Coyle is HERE tonight, and he’s decided to forgo surgery and return to the ring right away to take on X at FallOut! Coyle can’t wrestle here tonight, but he will be in action at FallOut!

Jhub: That’s a tough bastard right there. Stupid, but tough.

Bryan: Well, fans, we’re ready to get this show on the road.

“The Violence” In Chill Mode -by Jay

The camera switches to the arena crowd where 'The Violence' Vince Jones is seen sitting in a ringside seat in the crowd with a scowl of anger plastered across his face. The attractive, blond, curvy GCW interviewer, Sally Ford, is standing next to him with a bright smile on her face facing the camera as the crowd is going nuts over the action they'll see tonight.

Bryan: Let’s go to Sally Ford to find out!

Sally Ford: Wow! These people sure have a lot of love for you here tonight, Vince.

Vince Jones: Yeah, yeah, yeah...whateva.

Sally Ford: Sorry about the loss in your debut against Ricky Thunders, Vince. Have any clue where you go from here?

Vince Jones nods his head and laughs to himself.

Vince Jones: Yeah. Yeah. That sh*t might be true. Ole Donny boy might've had somethin' to say 'bout V's thoughts and feelings. He still might have a lot to say 'bout V's thoughts and feelings; but, let me ask you a lil somethin' somethin' Sally. Where he at now, huh? Where the hell "The Livin' Nightmare" at now? That b*tch might've got the drop on V last week and dropped him in a hole with a damn casket and all; but, where the hell he at now?

Sally looks at the delighted Vince Jones with an expression of concern on her face.

Sally Ford: Are you implying that you might've had something to do with the rumors of Donny Diamond possibly being on his way out of GCW?

Vince shrugs his shoulders at the questioning and just chuckles to himself.

Vince Jones: Maybe...maybe not. V ain't sh*t here! All he did was ask you a simple question tryin' to stir up a lil conversatin' and all.

Sally Ford: I see. So, what brings you to WorldWide tonight anyways, Vince?

The smile vanisishes from his face and he ooks up at her in disdain.

Vince Jones: What the hell do you think V here for tonight, huh?? He obviously out here tryin' to relax a bit, tryin' to sit back and watch his fellow G-C-Dubbas in action. V tryin' to enjoy that in house experience. He tryin' to get his adrenaline pumpin'. He tryin' to see some action. He tryin' to see some good competition and all. Tryin' to enjoy a lil bit of popcorn up in this joint. Ya know?

Vince turns and sees a man sitting next to him with a bag full of popcorn in his hands and snatches it from him. The man turns towards him in anger.

Audience member: Hey buddy! That's my...

Vince Jones lunges at him like he's about to hit him and the man quickly leans away in fear of being hit.

Vince Jones: Shut the hell up! You interruptin' V's interview here!

Audience member: Sorry...

Vince Jones: Betta be sorry!

Sally Ford: Well...that was interesting.

Vince Jones just shakes his head in disgust at the thought of the guy he just stole his bag of popcorn from.

Vince Jones: Its a damn shame how rude cats can be these days. What's the world comin' to and all?

Sally Ford: Yeah. Well... I'm sorry; but, I myself and all these people out here aren't stupid, Vince. There has to be more to your appearance here tonight. You're not even scheduled to fight here. Why make a trip all the way out here to Calgary from your home in New York?

Vince Jones: Well, its like this, Sally. Sometimes you gotta watch from the sidelines and see what you can see, observe. Sometimes you gotta sit back, relax, and chill in this game and in the meantime watch and wait. Right now V takin' it easy. He on that down low roll; but, when V makes his move cats gonna know, heads gone turn, and a G-C-Dubb b*tch made's gonna get struck. Until that time 'The Violence' is chillin' on that silence mode. 'Nuff said, Sally! Now get the hell on outta here! You startin' to crowd V's space!

Vince Jones: HEY!! You still here?? Roll out before V. Jones gotta send you on your way!

Sally Ford leaves Vince's side as the camera moves back to the announce team.

Bryan: Well, fans, we all have heard the rumors that Donny Diamond got roughed up backstage at a house show recently, and it seems like Vince Jones might have had something to do with that!

A Thunderous Interview -by Jeff

Scene fades in with Ricky Thunder standing backstage beside Sally, who is holding her GCW microphone, and obviously wanting to ask him some questions. The shot pans in as the crowd pops slightly, at the shot of Ricky.

Sally: Ricky, if I may, your reactions on last week?

Ricky: My reactions? Look at me Sally! Look at this THING on my neck.

Ricky points to the white brace wrapped around his neck from the actions of Vince Jones following their match.

Sally: What kind of prognosis has the GCW medical team given you and will this in any way affect your performance here tonight?

Ricky: Prognosis, out 4-6 weeks with a displaced vertebrae. Affect my performance here tonight? Obviously.

Sally: So Ricky, are you stating that you are unable to wrestle here tonight?

Ricky: Oh I am able, and I will be there, by any means necessary. How does this affect me? This affects only three things, and that is my will to get through this, my drive to track down the man responsible for this and most importantly, my want, for vengeance.

Sally: Ricky going into tonight’s match, what can we expect to see from you? Last week there were some high flying moves as well as a technical assault, will your mobility be limited by the injury?

Ricky: My mobility limited? I don’t care if break my freakin neck completely! I am here to give these fans what they paid for! [The crowd explodes into a chorus of cheers] You think that a small injury like this will sidetrack me? You think some street punk can keep me down? Vince Jones, I’m coming for you! I’m not stopping until I find what I’m looking for, and you know as well as I do what that is.

Sally: Ricky, one final question for you. Have you given any thought to the cruiserweight division? The tournament is currently underway and after fallout, a champion will be crowned.

Ricky: I haven’t given much thought to titles yet. I am here simply to do what I love, and give these fans their monies worth. If Caldera sees me fit to be a champion then he will move me into that division, otherwise, I have my mind set on righting a few wrongs.

Ricky stares into the camera and the scene fades out, neither him nor Sally speaking another word.

Fire in Your Belly -by Carrie

Lights: Dim.

Room: Secluded changing room.

Spectre: Eva, dressed casually in a pair of black pants and a tank top steps into the approximate center of the room, her eyes following somebody move in from off camera. A song starts playing, soft at first, without rhyme or reason, The Marauder steps into the circle of light provided from above for him and Eva alone. The rhythm finds it's way over the speakers, the beat kicks in.

Eva: What song is this?

Marauder: It doesn't matter.

Eva: But I like it, its got a great beat.

Marauder grins: Move with it...

As bidden, Eva starts to sway with the music, exotically at first, The Marauder catches her hips in hand.

Marauder: Not your hips.

Eva frowns and starts swaying back and forth her knees bending slightly as she bobs to the music.

Eva: Like this?

Marauder nods as they start moving from side to side, heads rising and dropping in time to the tune. They quickly find the tempo and Marauder removes his hands.

Marauder: Close your eyes.

They match, perfectly synched up to the music.

Eva: I can't hear the words...

Marauder: You're not supposed to... The words are different for everybody, you have to feel them.

Eva scowls again, eyes closed.

Marauder: Keep moving... picture a ring. An empty ring.

Eva nods to this, playfully trying to peek and giggle but Marauder's closed eyes and straight mouth urge her into compliance.

Marauder: Put your brother in that ring, put Savige in there for taking your belt.

Eva frowns.

Marauder: Keep moving, keep pace... put Summers and Stephens in that ring... Put Borealis in there... Go ahead and add Lestatt and Mary.

Her frown furrows in even deeper.The tempo of the music has been gradually gaining momentum.

Marauder: Put your hands up... Like this.

Taking her hands he puts them up like a boxer, he taps her legs and moves her shoulders, Eva falls into the stance easily, still moving to the music.

Marauder: Listen to the words...

There's a few moments of silence as the undecipherable lyrics whisper, The Marauder raises his right.

Marauder: Right.

Eva reacts quickly, swinging a blind right. Marauder catches it in tune to the beat and it falls back.

Marauder: Left.

Perfectly in synch with the rhythm Eva slaps her fist into Marauder's palm.

Marauder: Right... Right... Left. Right... Right. Left. Right... Right... Right... Left.Right.

Eva's expression contorts quickly caught between anxiety, confusion, and rage. Sweat starts to glisten on her forehead and loose strands of hair escape the tight pony tail. But The Marauder pushes her on in unison to the music, beat for beat, it quickens and so does he, until finally he calls for right and doesn't catch it. Eva spins around with the momentum for a heel kick The Marauder avoids.

Eva pirouettes perfectly and drops into a leg sweep, The Marauder hops over and moves in quickly, eyes still closed she blocks a one two punch combo and launches back with three of her own, perfectly timed to the beat The Marauder blocks them before taking a knee to the side. He yelps, Eva's eyes open and she falls back breathlessly holding her belly.

Marauder: Can you feel it?

Eva: I... feel what?

Marauder: The tingle... the burning.

Eva: I feel something.

Marauder whispers: The focus... When everything becomes lost, the pressure, the people, the fans, even the beat doesn't matter anymore because it's in you, the motions, the fight. It's like watching yourself and wondering if you'll ever be that good, perfect clarity, with it anything and everything becomes possible. Legends are smelted in the crucible of competition and the focus is your hammer with which to fashion your own legacy.

Eva looks at her hands reverently.

Marauder: It helps to find the focus when you wrestle with a clear head, you can never win with a broken heart.

Eva: My heart's not-

Marauder: Your heart is your greatest advantage and most crippling handicap, Eva. Entire wars have been won on bravery and lost on despair. But I've learned from my mistakes, the heart will not be controlled or mastered. Hate, love, rage, compassion all come from the same place, if you know your heart, you can use it.

Eva frowns at him looking a bit confused before sighing and nodding. It looks like the diva finally understood. She strolls forward and pulls Johnny into a hug with a smile.

Eva: Thanks.
