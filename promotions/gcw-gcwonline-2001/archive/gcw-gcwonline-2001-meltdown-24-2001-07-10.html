Meltdown 24 Nationwide Arena — Columbus, Ohio ---- Meltdown #24 (The GCW logo flashes on the screen, as Meltdown begins. Scenes from Heatwave play as we prepare for the show.) Bryan- Wait, Toad takes off from the top! TOAD JAM ON THE CHAMP! Toad hits the Toad Jam on the World Champion! And Reed could have it here! Toad pulls Reed on top of Rollins! Yale- ONE! TWO!! NO! Eric Allen hits an elbow drop of Reed from the top! Rollins narrowly escapes defeat! Reed and Rollins are both hurting! But here comes Toad again! Toad goes after Allen, while Hanson has Toxic on the other side! Bryan- Hanson lifts Toxic up, and delivers that mean underhook powerbomb! Toxic is down and out, and Hanson now has his sights set on Rollins! Rollins is slowly climbing to his feet, but Hanson is stalking him! Yale- Wait, Rollins turns. . . Superkick! Rollins senses Hanson coming, and hits a superkick on the Fueler! Hanson is down, but Reed is rising! Allen is trying to break free from Toad, but Toad has him in a bear hug! Bryan- Rollins turns to Reed, but Reed scoops him up! WAIT A MINUTE! WAIT A MINUTE! Cradle Tombstone!!! Reed hits a cradle tombstone on Rollins! Toad has Allen held back! Toxic is down! Andrews- Here is your winner, and NEW GCW Heavyweight Champion. . . John Reed! Bryan- Reed has done it! GCW has destroyed the CWU here tonight! What a night! What an upset! (The camera once again flashes the GCW logo, followed by the Meltdown theme. The camera hits the arena, where the Houston crowd is going wild. Heatwave was an incredible event, and we hope to follow it up with another here tonight. Yale- GCW struck back decisively at Heatwave, JB, and it looks like the CWU is gone for good! In what was one of the greatest upsets of all time, John Reed defeated Rich Rollins for the World Title, and brought it back to our side! Bryan- It was an amazing match, as was the entire night! Big Daddy Dero did manage to defeat President Caldera at Heatwave in the street fight! But he and Heckler turned their backs on the ACWF, and reformed the legendary group, the Core of Destruction! Yale- Commissioner Octavian defeated the Jhub in a bloody battle for the Commissionership, as well, and Tristain Mayhem proved his amazing ability by defeating LeStatt Knight in that ladder match! Bryan- Knight was injured in that match, fans, but how severely we’re not sure of yet. We’ll update you on his condition as we get the news. Well, fans, it’s time to head to ringside, where a special presentation is to be held in the ring! (Master of Puppets hits, and the crowd awaits someone’s arrival. President Caldera steps out into the arena, and the crowd begins to cheer. Caldera’s head is bandaged from his match at Heatwave, but he seems to be in a good mood. A red carpet has been rolled down the ramp, which Caldera walks on. He steps into the ring, where a large podium is standing. A red cloth rests on top of it, and Caldera eyes it with a smile.) Andrews- Please welcome the President of GCW, Steven Caldera! (Caldera takes a mike from Andrews, and looks out to the crowd.) Caldera- At Heatwave last Sunday, the armies of GCW defeated those of the ACWF and CWU for the last time. The world title once again rests in the hands of GCW, the hands of John Reed. (The crowd goes crazy at the mention of the name.) Caldera- And so, tonight, we have prepared a little ceremony for Mr. Reed. But before we bring him out, I’d like to introduce the man who, at Heatwave, remained Commissioner of GCW. . . Octavian! (The crowd goes crazy as Might of Rome hits. Octavian slowly struts to the ring, his head also bandaged. He wears a nice suit, despite his match with Rollins later. The commissioner steps into the ring, and takes another mike from Andrews.) Octavian- Thank you, thank you. At Heatwave, I showed that insignificant Jhub the fury that lies in this Roman’s heart. The ACWF and CWU was sacked at Heatwave, and tonight, we are proud to present this ceremony in favor of John Reed. And so, without further ado, I would like to introduce the Heavyweight Champion of the World. . . John Reed!! (The crowd explodes as Break Stuff hits. After a moment, Luke Hanson, Toad, Mike Hardy, and Rachel Reed appear. The crowd goes crazy as they spot System-X., but still wonder where Reed is. Finally, the System members point to the entrance, where John Reed, limping slightly, emerges. He wears the CWU title belt over his left shoulder, his tag belt over his right. System-X proudly walks to the ring. Hanson holds the ropes for Rachel, then for John. The System climbs into the ring, as the crowd continues to cheer with all their might. Octavian hands his microphone to Toad, who smiles at the crowd.) Toad- Hello, Toad fans! (The crowd cheers.) Toad- Now, I’m not about to come out here and hog all the airtime from my good buddy John. But there is one thing I’d like to say first. At Heatwave, there was only one major problem. And that’s the fact that Jason Dread is still wearing my Hard*core belt! I may be half of the tag team champions, but I still want that damn hard*core title back! (The crowd cheers.) Toad- Well, enough about that. . . Back to John. I suppose we could hand the microphone to Mr. Mike Hardy, the Cruiserweight champ. . . (The camera zooms in on Hardy, who wears a big smile.) Toad- Or we could head over to your favorite jackass and mine. . . Luke Hanson! (Hanson glares at Toad, but finally breaks out with a smile.) Toad- Or we could just go straight to the man himself. . . John Reed! (The crowd cheers enthusiastically for Reed, and Toad finally gives in. Toad hands the microphone to Reed, which earns a loud reaction. Reed smiles, and waits for the cheers to die. Caldera and Octavian applaud with the fans as they stand in a corner.) Reed- I have to say, from the bottom of my heart. . . Thank you. Thank you to everyone in this ring, everyone in the crowd, and everyone watching at home. Thanks to my fans and my friends, I have reached the top of the world. I’ve dreamt of this all my life, and I’m finally here. So thank you, all of you. (The crowd applauds warmly.) Reed- Now, first matter of business, Luke, you gave me a run for my money in that number one contender’s match, and I’m sure you’d have taken this belt from Rollins like I did. So I would like to ask a favor. As long as I’m world champion. . . I’d like to ask that you take my tag team belt, and defend it with Toad for me. (Reed extends his tag belt to Hanson. Hanson looks at Reed questioningly for a moment, but finally takes it. The crowd cheers as Hardy helps Hanson strap the belt on.) Bryan- Every System member is holding gold now! Reed- Now, I plan on being a fighting champion. And so, I need to decide on who gets the first title shot. Luke, you could have been world champion as easily as me. . . So I think it’s only fair- Caldera- Hold on a minute, John. Before you go any further, I think we need to take care of something. You are the GCW world champion, yet you are carrying the CWU title belt. So, if you would be so kind as to hand it to me. . . (Reed looks confused. Cautiously, he hands Caldera the CWU belt. Caldera hands it back to Octavian, and then walks to the podium in the center of the ring.) Caldera- As GCW champion, you need to be carrying the GCW belt. And so, John Reed. . . (Caldera pulls the red cloth off of the podium, revealing an incredible gold belt. The belt shines in the light, and is truly impressive.) Caldera- I present to you the new GCW World Title belt! (Caldera hands Reed the belt. Reed takes it with a smile, and walks to the turnbuckle. He steps to the second rope, and holds the belt up to be seen. Camera flashes explode from the Houston crowd, capturing the image forever.) Octavian- Now, before you go, John, there’s just one more thing we have to do. (Reed hops down, and Octavian presses through the System members to approach him. Octavian extends his hand, and Reed takes it. The crowd cheers as they shake. However, as Reed pulls away, Octavian holds on and pulls him back. Toad, Hardy, and Hanson jump at Octavian, but Reed calls them off.) Octavian- The CWU is gone now. And before you go off handing out title shots, maybe you should remember the man, President Caldera, who pays your checks every month. And maybe you should remember the man who gave you that title shot in the first place. . . me. Don’t forget those of us who really made this possible. Now that you’re champion, everyone in the back wants a shot at you. And a few of us in the ring want a shot. So you just be careful what you say when you say you’ll be a fighting champion. Because I may just take you up on that. (The crowd seems hushed as Reed and Octavian stare each other down. Octavian shakes Reed’s hand once more, as Break Stuff hits. Caldera steps between Reed and Octavian, pushing Octavian away. Reed poses with his new belt once more, and finally walks out with the System.) Bryan- Well, it seems like party time is already over for John Reed! The world champion does hold the one thing everyone wants, and it seems like the Commissioner may have already taken an interest in Reed’s newfound glory! Yale- Well, Octavian has held that belt once before, I’m sure he’d like to hold it again! But for now, Reed is going to have his day! (The show fades to commercial as Reed and System-X head to the back.) (Meltdown returns, and we head backstage. Dan Rivero is standing with Kevin Jacobs, and he is holding his TV title belt.) Well, boys, the difference between you and me is that you guys cry that you aren’t getting noticed. . . While I go out there an make myself get noticed. You have to make your own fame in this business, and that’s what I did at Heatwave. So Knight, Stone. . . Good luck, boys. The Man is running solo. (Rivero walks off, smiling with his TV belt. But it seems now that Mr. Knight has been put out of commission for a while, and I am without a challenger for this belt. (Fans shout names like Hardy, Hanson, and Toad as potential challengers.) Mayhem- I want someone big to fight for this thing. I want someone who deserves it. Someone who will give me a better fight than Mr. Knight. I want someone. . . Important. (Again, the fans shout suggestions.) Mayhem- And I think I know the guy I want. But you’ll just have to wait and see. But for now, I think I’ll scout some competition. . . So I’ll just join JB and David Yale for commentary! (The crowd cheers as Mayhem steps out of the ring. He walks to the announce table and sits next to Yale, who again sighs at their uninvited guest.) Bryan- Well, we’re being joined by the United States Champion here, Tristain Mayhem, as we get ready for another matchup. ( hits, and Great Tiger emerges from the back. Piledriver! Bryan- Big move there, and that could be it! Allen covers! ONE! TWO! THR- Kickout! How the hell did Great Tiger kick out of a piledriver? Mayhem- That moves hurts, too, man. Bryan- That’s got to be an upset! Mayhem- I’ll say. Eric Allen is a tough cookie, even if I could kick his ass. Bryan- Great Tiger has defeated Eric Allen here in his debut! Allen is on a bit of a losing streak! (Tiger does not stop to have his arm raised. He adjusts his mask and walks to the back, never showing a bit of emotion.)
