'The Deathscythe Outlaw' Kyo Kushinaga Biography Hometown Unknown Birthday Unknown Height 6'5 Weight 265 Biography 'The end doesn't exactly mean the end. It instead means the birth of a new life. Or in my case, the rebirth of a better self.' -Kyo Kushinaga If you want to know what the outlaw is all about, then the only thing you need to know is that the Deathscythe Outlaw is a wise-cracking, cigarette smoking, alcoholic drinking, gun-shooting, sword swinging badass who doesn't take shit from anybody. Try to order him around and you'll receive a hard kick to the nether region, and an unforgettable ass kicking. He's pretty much the type of guy who says the hell with authority. He doesn't trust anybody. If you want to earn the outlaw's trust and respect, then you'll have to prove yourself worthy in a fight against him. Give him a worthy fight, then he might consider you an ally. Fail, then he will stomp your ass into the ground. Oh, there is one more thing. Try not to piss him off. If you do, then I have only this to say. ...It was nice knowing you... Other Feds Global, 4WW, PCW, WIW:NG, DWO, LOW, ESW, FWX Other Fed Awards Global: 1x Platinum Champion 4WW: 2x West Wind Champion, 1x East Wind Champion, 1x North Wind Champion PCW: 1x Tag Team Champions WIW:NG: 1x Television Champion DWO: 1x Cruiserweight Champion and 1x Heavyweight Champion ESW: 1x Heavyweight Champion FWX: 1x Light Heavyweight Champion (undefeated when the fed closed), 1x European Champion (undefeated when fed closed), 1x Tag Team Champions Strengths Kushinaga is a superb striker, he has great stamina, he has speed especially to pull off lightning fast punches and kicks on his opponents, and his strength can rival even that of the biggest fighters in the league Weaknesses Kushinaga lacks in the technical department, and he also doesn’t do quite well with defense since he’s more of an offensive fighter Wrestling Style Martial Artist - Striker Strategy Kushinaga always starts his fights at a slow pace, he do this to reserve his energy for the more crucial moments in the fights. He goes to work on his opponents with his striking skills, he strike his opponent in their vital area, which includes, the head, the chest, the neck, and the back (however, if he’s facing an opponent that’s taller than him then he’ll target the legs too). In the middle of his fights, Kushinaga will unleash some of his reserve energy and start to do power moves to inflict more damage on his opponents, he’ll also start to do aerial moves to keep his opponents off their feet as well confuse them. Near the end of his fights, Kushinaga will unleash all his energy, as his moves will become faster and fiercer than they were in the beginning of the fights. He’ll sometimes do the Outlaw Destroyer on his opponents before he applies the Deathscythe Vice on them (however, if his opponent counters the Outlaw Destroyer then he’ll quickly go for the Deathscythe Vice). Now, this is quite rare, if the Outlaw Destroyer neither the Deathscythe Vice has no effect on his opponent then he’ll go for the finisher that he considers forbidden. He first takes off his right gauntlet and reveals the demonic tattoos on his right forearm then once he sees an opening he’ll go for the move known as Ryuujin’s Roar to take his opponent out. Entrance Music "This Fire Burns" by Killswitch Engage Ring Entrances || Kushinaga's Ring Entrance || Killswitch Engage’s "This Fire Burns" begins playing on the loud speakers, as clips from the fighter’s previous fight starts to roll on the giant screen. Some fans have already begun to boo this individual on the account of his bad attitude and the means of his brutality to those that he faced in the square ring. But most fans were cheering for him, as they seemed they didn’t mind his attitude neither means of brutality. “Deathscythe Outlaw” Kyo Kushinaga steps from behind the curtains. He stands on stage where he doesn’t dance to his music or anything in that matter to pump up these fans. Kushinaga instead just stands there and has a bore look on his face while he’s looking at the fans. He can hear the fans? boos. He can also hear the fans? cheers. He can even hear some female groupies saying that he was hot and other sexual compliments. Kushinaga slips his right hand in his pocket. He pulls out his pack of his cigarettes and his silver lighter. Kushinaga puts a cigarette in his mouth and lights it up at its? end. He takes a long drag from his cigarette then breathes the smoke out from his mouth. Kushinaga savors the taste of nicotine and looks out at the fans through the smoke from his cigarette with the same bore look. He puts his hands in both pants? pockets and starts to head to the square ring. Kushinaga glared at the place that he considered the “Graveyard” in which this was the place that he put his opponents, or lost souls to rest. He closes his eyes while he makes his way to the ring. It’s almost like he was visualizing the entire fight in his mind’s eye. Kushinaga could see everything clearly. But he snaps open his eyes which ended his visions. Kushinaga didn’t want to see the fight’s end. He considered it bad luck to see the outcome of the fight. He rather be surprise then know everything that was going to happen. Kushinaga stops before the ring. He does a quick jump and lands on the ring’s apron. Kushinaga does another quick jump, as he jumps over the top rope and lands gracefully in the ring. While he’s walking to his corner, he turns and glares at the official. Kushinaga can see that he was making the official feel unease with his glare. Once at his corner, Kushinaga takes one final drag from his cigarette and breathes the smoke out of his nose this time. He removes the cigarette from his mouth and rubs it out on the turnbuckle behind him. Kushinaga then unties his black bandana from his forehead and ties it around the base of his long raven-grayish hair in long ponytail. He takes a seat on the turnbuckle and waits for his opponent to show.
