Meltdown 51 Aloha Stadium — Honolulu, Hawaii ---- Meltdown #51 (The lights in the Allstate Arena dim, and the fans begin to buzz. The MegaTron flickers to life, and the text ‘March 5th, 2001’ appear on the screen. The fans begin to cheer as the shot moves to a crowded room, the shot in nothing but black and white. President Caldera stands at a podium, as reporters flash pictures of him. He begins to speak.) Caldera- I am here tonight to announce the beginning of a new wrestling company. One which I believe could become the greatest in the industry. Global Championship Wrestling will open on March Twenty-First, Two Thousand and One. And it will take the wrestling industry by storm. (With that, the shot quickly zooms in on Caldera’s face, in yet closer to his left eye, before shooting right through to another series of text… March 21st, 2001. The shot switches to the opening pyro of the very first Meltdown, where an excited crowd is going crazy. James Bryan’s voice can be heard in the background, opening the very first show.) Bryan- Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the first ever GCW event, Meltdown! The suspense has been building ever since President Caldera’s press release announcing GCW’s opening, and we have finally arrived at the night we’ve all been waiting for! Fans, I’m James Bryan, sitting at ringside with my broadcast colleague, David Yale! (The flashback finally fades to a black screen. The entire arena remains in total darkness for several moments, the Chicago crowd cheering wildly. Slowly, the GCW logo fades in on the screen, and the cheers become even more intense. Finally, the Meltdown music hits, and pyro explodes from the stage. The crowd leaps to its feet, every single fan screaming in anticipation. An incredible pyro display explodes from the MegaTron, before two rockets scream down overhead the ramp, hitting the ring and causing gold flames to erupt from the ring turnbuckles. The flames cause golden sparks to begin to pour from the rafters high above, littering the ring with golden glimmers. The pyro finally ceases after a long and incredible display, and the cameras begin to fly over the crowd. The cameras catch close up shots of signs reading ‘Jhub 4 Champ’, ‘Badger was Screwed’, and ‘I’m a Sell Out.’ The GCW logo appears on screen again, with ‘Chicago, Illinois’ next to it. The Chicago fans continue their incredible reaction, as James Bryan chimes in as usual.) Bryan- Happy Birthday to Global Championship Wrestling! Thank you very much for joining us on this historic night, fans, as GCW gears up for a month long celebration of its first year of existence! And this marks the one year anniversary of the very first night myself and my broadcast colleague David Yale sat in this very spot at ringside for the very first episode of Meltdown! And Dave, that is a very good thing to be a part of. Yale- JB, it truly is a pleasure to be back here! I may not like you all that much, but damnit, it’s pretty special to be at ringside to call the first year’s anniversary of Global Championship Wrestling! Last year at this time, JB, you and I were right here at ringside, opening up the company! And now we’re a year old, and we truly are the greatest wrestling company in the world! Bryan- And on this historic night, we have an incredible lineup for you! I’m sure everyone saw Dread Reckoning on Pay-Per-View a few nights ago, and if you did, you know that Kurt Richards returned for one night only, in order to lay down a challenge to Octavian! Octavian takes on Kurt Richards right here tonight! Yale- And, of course, Jason Dread is STILL the GCW World Heavyweight Champion! Here tonight, though, either Badger Benandanti or The Jhub will become number one contender to that title! But I have it on good authority that Caldera did not sign that match! So who the hell did?! Bryan- Well, that and many questions will be answered tonight! We’ve got a night full of incredible surprises, the debut of Low, Kurt Richards’ last match with GCW, several titles on the line… it just doesn’t get any bigger than this! (With that, the cameras cut to the stage, while ‘Established’ begins to boom into the arena. The crowd’s reaction remains enthusiastic, but now quite bitter as Steven Caldera appears on stage. He wears an elegant black suit and tie, perhaps his best and most expensive ensemble. He seems to be in good spirits as he walks down the ramp to the ring, despite the crowd’s less than favorable reaction.) Andrews- Please welcome the President of GCW, Steven Caldera! Bryan- Well, we’re kicking off the show with the President of GCW, fittingly enough! And while he may not be the most loved man in the company, he certainly has done a great deal with GCW in the past year! Yale- Well, despite what these idiots in the crowd think of him, I know that President Caldera is one of the greatest owners, greatest stars, and even still one of the greatest wrestlers in the world! Caldera smiles widely, despite the continued boos from the crowd. He takes a quick survey of the arena, spinning around slowly. He finally comes to a stop, and begins to speak. However, he is quickly cut off by an enormous ‘Asshole!’ chant from the crowd. Caldera pauses, letting the chant subside. He thinks about this for a moment, before beginning.) Caldera- You know, this marks exactly one year since the first time any of you ever called me an asshole. And I find that rather special. So thank you very much. (The crowd seems displeased with this reaction, but begins to quiet.) Caldera- And in that year, look at the empire I have amassed. I now own and control the greatest wrestling company on the planet. I went from being a mere millionaire just one year ago, to the billionaire you see before you now. I hold the World Championship, the United States Championship, and both tag team titles in my pocket, thanks to the greatest organization ever, the Establishment. Because it seems that certain things are still capable of getting around the Establishment. Caldera- Now, I know for certain that I did not authorize this match.
