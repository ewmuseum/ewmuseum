KJ Newfoundland Biography Real Name KJ Newfoundland Nicknames Fallen Angel, The Detrimental Dragon Hometown Atlanta, Georgia Birthday May 18, 1986 Height 6'2 Weight 225 lbs Biography K.J. started out in the wrestling business wearing a golden mask that protects him from the laughter of the people around him. Newfoundland was taught that he was ugly and he needs to wear the cursing mask as long as he breathes. K.J. first wrestling gig ended horrible after he was forcefully banned from the company after he nearly killed eight people. Six of the people where fans that got in the way of his and the body count got K.J. canned and was police escorted out of arena and out of the city. On route of leaving the city, the police curser was stopped in the middle of road after there was a figure in black standing in the road. The second officer went out to see if the person was okay and ask why he was standing in the middle of the road. The officer was answered by the person pointed behind him to the curser. As the officer turns around, he sees the officer head exploded in the curser and K.J. kicks the door off the hinges and walks out holding the officer pistol. Officer never stood a chance against KJ and the person in the black. Officer was barbequed on the spot by light bolt from the clouds in the night sky. The man explained to K.J. that he was searching for him for the longest time. The man revealed that he was Shredder; Creeker's twisted brother. For the next couple months K.J. learn from Shredder and his family. K.J. learned the dark arts from Shredder in hopes to revive his dead girlfriend that was killed in front of after some jealous person shot her in cold blood. He actually began to make the Rivers' family able to be in the same room without killing each other. During a match K.J. was severely cut and burned and was bed ridden for couple months. In a fever control dream, K.J. was contacted by his dead father. This same father tried to save him but couldn't and ends up dieing from a gun shot to the skull by Newfoundland's insane step mother. The worst part was the fact that K.J. father was actually a demon and told K.J. if he wants to find a way to reborn his girlfriend fallow his instructions to the letter. K.J. had to use a wooden sword to steal sins from people and after so much he would learn they way to revive his girlfriend. After couple weeks of sins hunting, K.J. comes up to his last mission and it happens to be in a graveyard that was located underground. For this mission Kody Gordert went with him because Newfoundland told him that this going major battle and you need help. As the “joint” group travel through the graveyard the came up to the place. The weird thing is that fact nothing tried to attack them and they would find out that this will show K.J. the way. In the tomb was a giant Kodiak bear and Kody couldn't stop from making the stupid joke. Kody calls out as he pulls out his saber "BROTHER!" After couple hours of fighting Kodiak finished off his "brother" with explotion to the chest from a jar filled with gas that was located in the tomb. K.J. brushed off the dust on the name tag to see who he is going to take his final sin from. It wasn't a male at all! The person in the coffen was....his MOTHER! K.J. refused to do this deed because he will never do this to his mother and the wood sword did't like this fact that its not going to get a sin that it wants. The sword exected its control over Newfoundland body and K.J. falls to his knees screaming to the heavens "Forgive me mother!" As K.J. yelled this a bright light engulf the tomb that blinded Kodiak. K.J. was floating in the air and he felt happy. For the first time in long time and then his mother appeared in front him. His mother was angel and she told her that it wasn't his felt that she had to leave the living world to come back. For most of Kay Jaque life, he blamed himself for making mother die in child birth. Mother talk to K.J. and explain to him about everything; about dad, why he was sent off to do this deeds, and why he is here. As his mother touched his mask another bright light happen and caused K.J. to go back to the tomb. Kodiak rubbed his eyes and stared at Newfoundland with a dumb struck look on his face. For the first time ever, Newfoundland was maskless and he wasn't bad looking. After being demasked for the good, he went off and won couple more tag team championships and the world championship. If it is a bigger opponent, he tends to attacks the legs or the arms. His thinking is if you cannot stand then you cannot pick me up and if get lift your arms then you cannot over power me. If it is a smaller opponent or roughly the same size, he would attack the torso and or head. He has no problem taking a cheap victory by going for a possum pin or a roll up if someone was distract during his match.
