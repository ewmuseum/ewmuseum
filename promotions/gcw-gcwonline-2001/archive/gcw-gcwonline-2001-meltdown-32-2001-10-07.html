And he deserves it! Mayhem doesn’t lay off, as he moves back in with more boots to the gut! Danger is getting a royal beating from a very emotional young man! Mayhem finally picks Danger up, and rolls him into the ring! Yale- I haven’t even heard a bell yet! The match hasn’t started! Mayhem throws back the apron banner, and begins looking under the ring! Oh, no, he’s got a steel chair! Mayhem slides into the ring, as Danger slowly gets to his feet! Wait, the referee is begging Mayhem to stop! Bryan- But Mayhem just shoves him out of the way! Danger gets to his feet, but Mayhem is right there! (Mayhem brings the chair down over Danger’s skull, and the crack is heard around the arena. A piece of the chair flies off, landing somewhere in the crowd. Danger hits the mat, totally leveled. The crowd goes crazy, as they see blood trickling down Danger’s face.) Bryan- GOOD GOD what a chair shot! Danger goes right down… I don’t think this match is even going to take place! Mayhem just came here to beat the hell out of Danger, and that’s what he's doing! Danger is out cold, but Mayhem isn’t done! Mayhem lifts Danger to his feet, and hits the ropes! Yale- HAMMER ON! Good lord, a big leaping DDT on Danger! Danger is broken and bloodied! What did he do to deserve this?! Bryan- Oh, come on! Here come the officials to the ring, but Mayhem is already leaving! (Paranoid by Black Sabbath fills the arena, signaling the end of Mayhem’s rampage. The crowd explodes in enthusiastic cheers, as Mayhem leaves the commissioner lying.) Bryan- Tristain Mayhem isn’t done yet! He still wants to become champion of the world! Yale- But he’s not gonna do that, JB! He’s too worried about Nanci… and the fact that she’s carrying his worst enemy’s child! Bryan- I still don’t believe him, Dave! Well, fans, as Mayhem heads to the back, and the officials tend to Danger in the ring, GCW would like to extend its thanks to all the men and women in our armed forces, who right now are fighting for out freedom here in the USA! (Danger is helped out of the ring, but he is still delirious from the beating. Referees guide him to the back, as the show fades to its final commercial break.) (Meltdown returns to action, heading back to ringside.) Bryan- Well, fans, in just a moment, we’ll be ready for our main event, as Badger and the Jhub take on Octavian and Jason Dread! (Iron Man begins to fill the arena, as the crowd is instantly hot. Rich Rollins emerges from the back, in his first Meltdown appearance in a month. Rollins marches to the ring, ignoring the jeering crowd.) Bryan- Rich Rollins is back! At Devil’s Night, he assaulted Badger and the Jhub, making his first appearance since Badger Benandanti returned to GCW last month! But you’ve got to wonder where his allegiances lie! Yale- I can’t wait to hear what the former two time champ has to say! (Rollins circles the ring, stopping at the announce table. He throws his arms into the air one last time, receiving one last chorus of boos before taking a seat, and putting on a set of headphones.) Yale- Renegade, welcome back! Rollins- Thanks, Dave… I’m kinda glad to be back. Anxious to bust a few skulls. (BadgerTheme hits, and the crowd erupts with an enormous pop. Badger Benandanti walks out onto the stage, alongside the Jhub. The two march right for the ring, smiling and slapping hands with the fans on the way.) Andrews- The following tag team contest is set for one fall! Introducing first, at a combined weight of 470 pounds, the Jhub and Badger Benandanti! Bryan- Badger and the Jhub haven’t always been friends, but tonight they are teaming up against the World Champion, and one of GCW’ brightest, if misguided, young stars! Rollins- Well, these two clowns saw what I’m all about last Sunday night. They’re just in for even more of a beating tonight. (Jhub and Badger step into the ring, basking in the adoration of the fans. BadgerTheme fades out, and Might of Rome hits next. The crowd instantly grows angry and heated as Jason Dread and Octavian step onto the stage. Golden pyro erupts from the rafters, as the pair march to the ring, admiring Octavian’s belt.) Andrews- And their opponents, at a combined weight of 497 pounds, Jason Dread, and the GCW World Champion, Octavian! Bryan- And fans, we won’t soon forget what Octavian has done to Tristain Mayhem and Nanci! This man is obsessed with destroying Mayhem, and tonight he went so far as to force himself upon Nanci! Yale- Come on, JB, we all saw it! Nanci jumped on Octavian! And who wouldn’t, what with Octavian’s rugged good looks! Rollins- Are you coming onto him? Yale- I was just saying- Rollins- Shut up. These two are very impressive, JB, and I don’t know why everyone keeps complaining about Octavian’s victory at Devil’s Night. (Octavian removes his belt, as Dread steps into the ring to start things off. The boos quiet at the bell sounds, and the Jhub prepares to start things for his team.) Bryan- Here we go! Dread moves right in, and ties up with the Jhub! Dread slips behind the Jhub with a rear waistlock, but the Jhub counters with a snapmare! Dread immediately gets back to his feet, warding the Jhub off! All four of these men are very technically sound, and this should be a great contest! Yale- Dread and the Jhub tie up again, but this time Dread takes the Jhub over with a deep arm drag! The Jhub hits the mat hard, but rears back and kicks Dread to the mat! Both men spring back to their feet, and this time it’s the Jhub with an arm drag! And he holds on with an armbar! Bryan- The Jhub cranks back on Dread’s arm, but he’s not down for long! Dread turns over, getting to his feet! Dread finally slips behind the Jhub, clamping on a hammerlock! But the Jhub simply hits Dread with a devastating elbow to the face, and Dread lets go! Yale- The Jhub hits the ropes, and charges the staggering Dread! Oh, but Dread clotheslines the Jhub right to the mat, and the Jhub is hurting! Dread gives the Jhub a harsh stomp, and reaches over for a tag! And here comes the world champion! Rollins- Yeah, the guy’s good. Of course, I beat him a few months ago for that belt… Bryan- Renegade, with all that’s going on, we’re all wondering who’s side you’re on! Rollins- You’ll find out. Bryan- Octavian steps into the ring, as the Jhub slowly rises to his feet! Octavian greets him with a sharp knife-edge chop, sending the Jhub into a neutral corner! Octavian slowly approaches the Jhub, and lays in another chop! The Jhub is in trouble! Yale- Octavian takes the Jhub by the arm, and gives him an Irish whip into the opposite corner! The Jhub hits hard, and bounces out! Octavian grabs him in a front facelock, and tags Dread in again! Dread comes back in, as Octavian holds the Jhub’s arm in the air! Bryan- Dread lays in a big right, as Octavian steps out! Already this new team is working well! Dread reaches down, and gives the Jhub a big scoop slam to the mat! Dread leaps up, and hits a big leg drop! Very well executed by Dread! I hate to admit it, but Jason Dread is an extraordinary athlete! Rollins- Yes he is. Of course, I would have had him had it not been for Badger. Yale- Dread covers the Jhub! ONE! TWO! Kickout! Dread glares at the referee, and slowly lifts the Jhub back up! Dread sends the Jhub into the ropes, but the Jhub hooks the top rope, stopping his progress! Dread charges the Jhub, but the Jhub ducks, launching Dread over the top rope to the floor! (The crowd cheers as Dread hits the mats, and the Jhub reaches over to tag in Badger.) Bryan- And here comes Badger! Dread slowly gets back into the ring, and Badger is ready! Dread steps through the ropes, and Badger meets him with a stiff left! And another! Badger finally sends Dread into the ropes, and catches him with a beautiful dropkick! What a move! Yale- Dread hits the mat, as Badger heads for the top rope! Dread slowly gets to his feet, but Badger is already up and waiting! Dread spins around to see Badger waiting, and Badger soars with a cross body from the top! Dread hits the mat, and there’s the cover! ONE! TWO! Kickout! Bryan- Jason Dread isn’t ready to give this one up just yet! Badger gets back to his feet, pulling Dread up with him! Badger hooks Dread up, and tosses him overhead in a scintillating belly to belly suplex! Dread hits the mat hard, and Badger covers again! Yale- ONE! TWO! Another kickout! Rollins- Keep trying, Badger… Yale- Badger lifts Dread up one more time, and sets him up again! There’s a vertical suplex! No, Dread slides down behind Badger, and picks him up… Belly to back suplex from Dread! Great move, and once again the momentum swings in favor of Dread and Octavian! Bryan- Dread slowly reaches to tag Octavian, but Badger is already recovering! Dread leaps out, tagging in the champion! But Badger reaches out, making the tag to the Jhub! The Jhub and Octavian meet in the center of the ring, and Octavian throws a clothesline! Yale- But the Jhub ducks, and brings Octavian down from behind with a reverse neckbreaker! The Jhub hits the big move, and covers! ONE! TWO!! No, Dread leaps in with the save! But Badger is back! Badger spears the rising Dread, and both men spill out to the floor! Bryan- The Jhub gets back to his feet, as Badger and Dread begin to brawl on the floor! They’re really going at it out there! These two men want to rip each other apart! The Jhub looks out at the two, and then back to Octavian! The champion is slowly rising, as the referee tends to the brawl on the outside! Yale- The Jhub is calling for the Downward Spiral! Oh no, look out Octavian! The Jhub lifts Octavian up, and… wait, where is Rollins going?! Bryan- The Renegade has just left our announce table, and is heading for the ring! The Jhub has Octavian set up for the Downward Spiral! But Rollins slides in behind him, and spins him around! SUPERKICK! DAMNIT! Rollins just Superkicked the Jhub! (The crowd reacts with intense boos, as the Jhub hits the mat. Rollins bails out of the ring, as Octavian covers the Jhub. The referee turns from the Badger/Dread brawl, to see the cover.) Bryan- Damn it! ONE! TWO! THREE! It’s over! Rollins assaulted the Jhub! (The bell rings, as the crowd boos in disgust. Rollins slides back into the ring, nudging the Jhub to the floor. He helps the champion up, as he is handed his belt. Dread and Badger finally separate, as Dread slides back into the ring. Badger runs to check on the Jhub, as the three men in the ring celebrate.) Andrews- Here are your winners, Octavian and Jason Dread! Yale- I knew it! Rollins has helped the Champion! (The boos intensify as Octavian, Rollins, and Dread raise each other’s arms.) Bryan- What a force this is! Rich Rollins has joined them tonight! Octavian, Dread, and Rollins together! This can’t be happening! Yale- The three greatest talents in GCW are together at last! Bryan- Fans, we’re out of time here from Meltdown! Join us next week! Until then, I’m James Bryan for David Yale… Goodnight, everyone! (The boos continue as Octavian, Dread, and Rollins celebrate in the ring, leaving Badger and the Jhub on the floor. The show fades as the GCW logo appears on the bottom of the screen, before everything goes black……)
