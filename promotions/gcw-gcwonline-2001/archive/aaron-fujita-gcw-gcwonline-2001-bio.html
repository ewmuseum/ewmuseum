Aaron Fujita Biography Real Name Aaron Andrews Nicknames The Atmospheric Phenomena, The AMERICAN Dream Hometown Plainfield, IL Birthday August 28th, 1990 Height 6'2 Weight 225 Fed Titles Television Champion Fed Awards 2011 American Dream, 2011 Rampage Semi Finalist Biography Aaron Andrews, born in the midst of a significant F5 tornado, was raised in North-Central Illinois. Blame it on his time of birth or whatever you may but his love for atmospheric science seemed to come natural. Then again, so did his love for professional wrestling. As Aaron graduated high school and looked toward his future he saw two things. Training to become a wrestler. And going to college. It isn't commonplace to find anyone in the wrestling industry who is either working towards or already has a Bachelor's Degree. Aaron looks to change that thought. A junior in the meteorology program at Northern Illinois University, Aaron seems to have a chip on his shoulder. Not to mention the fact that he is already hated by a majority of the world's population for being a weather man. Aaron doesn't come into GCW looking to impress; in his eyes he is already the elite of the elite and better than you. Other Feds GCW, FUSE, PRIME, rW Strengths Quickness, agility, wrestling knowledge Weaknesses wrestling knowledge, cockiness Wrestling Style Technical/Flyer Finisher Move Quasi-Geostrophic Theory (QG-Theory) Finisher Description Aaron approaches from the back. He drapes his opponent's left arm over his shoulder and grabs their right leg with his right hand. Fujita lifts them up and spins them upside down before dropping to the mat and planting them with a piledriver.
