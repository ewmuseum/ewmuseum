Shawn Porter Biography Real Name Shawn Junior Porter Nicknames The Rev Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Birthday January 7, 1983 Height 6'3 Weight 231 lbs Fed Titles None Biography Shawn Junior Porter was born on January 7th, 1983 in Philadelphia. Shawn grew up in a poor community, he had to struggle as a lad and he'll never forget that. Shawn was an excellent athlete as a teenager and adult. he was a All-American in football and wrestling in Philadelphia. Upon graduating from high school, Porter attended the Clarion University of Pennsylvania, where he continued to wrestle at an amateur level. He was a National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I champion in 2002 and 2003, national runner-up in 2004, and a 3-time NCAA Division I All-American. In addition, Porter was the 2004 USA Junior Freestyle champion, a 2-time USA Senior Freestyle champion and the 2005 USA International Federation of Associated Wrestling Styles Junior World Freestyle champion. In the course of his amateur career, Porter faced several future mixed martial artists, defeating Billy Blanks, Mark Coleman and Mark Kerr. Upon graduating from college, Porter continued to wrestle. In 2006, he won a gold medal at the World Championships in Atlanta, Georgia. Following this victory, Porter began training for the 2008 Summer Olympics under Kurt Angle at the Pennsylvanian Foxcatcher Club. Shawn Porter uses his technical style in respects to Chris Benoit, Kurt Angle, Rey Mysterio, and Harley Race.
