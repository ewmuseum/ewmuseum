Devils Night 2008 American Airlines Arena — Miami, Florida ---- Introduction An ominous rumble of thunder bristles through the smokey arena air. Suddenly a bolt of brilliant lightning strikes the stage, setting off a string of explosive red fireworks rushing down toward the ring. The turnbuckle erupt in flames and the fans erupt in cheers, and the house lights come up to reveal the massive crowd.
