Dave Mullett Biography Real Name Dave Mullett Nicknames "Delicious" Dave, The Mighty Mullett Man... Hometown Leeds, West Yorkshire, England Birthday 21 May 1975 Height 5 '9 Weight 210lbs Biography Dave Mullett was born and raised in Leeds, England. As a child he was entranced by British wrestling, in particular Big Daddy and Giant Haystacks. He cites meeting Mick McManus in his early teens as the moment that made him decide to be a wrestler. After brief stints as a boxer, Mullett turned semi-pro as a wrestler in 1992. He toured up and down the country with various independant wrestling promotions, often jobbing to more estblished names. Around this time he met and wrestled William Regal (or Steven as he was known then) who convinced him Japan was where it was all happening. In Japan, audiences were more appreciative of Mullett's no nonsense stiff style, and consequently he became very popular. As an Englishman, he adopted a snobbish heel persona and managed to wind up several superstars, producing some great fueds. Rather than return to England Mullett took a gamble and toured the world, wrestling in Germany and Mexico. Now he arrives in the US, bringing all the loyal MullettManiacs with him! Strengths Brawling, ring psychology like leaving the ring to taunt etc. Weaknesses A high flyer, relentless high power moves. Wrestling Style Stiff, brawler, cheater. Strategy Weak Finisher Move The Mighty Mullet Kick Finisher Description Superkick Finisher Setup Move A jawbreaker Setup Description Jawbreaker Special Moves Atomic Drop, German suplex, Back body drop, Running shoulder barge. Other Moves Knee to the groin, Backbreaker, Schoolboy pin, Stalling brainbuster, Leg lariat, Bulldog, Leg drop, Russian leg sweep, Eye rake on the ropes. Entrance Music "Won't Get Fooled Again" by The Who Ring Entrances Mullett largely ignores the crowd, he would however take notice of his MullettManiacs if there's any in attendance. A stroll to the ring and a slide under the bottom rope and Mullett is ready to fight.
