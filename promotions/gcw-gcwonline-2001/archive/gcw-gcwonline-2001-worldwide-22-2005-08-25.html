Global Championship Wrestling – WorldWide Episode #22 – Oakland, California

Introduction -by Ben

The lights dim in the arena, and the Oakland crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the MegaTron as Wake Up by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. The WorldWide intro bursts onto the screen, and is capped with a tremendous explosion of pyro! The crowd erupts as the fireworks continue to explode, tearing across the arena. The cameras fly over the screaming crowd, zooming in on signs reading “Where’s Trey?” and “LeStatt: Give us back our puppies!” The cameras continue to fly as the words “Oakland, California” appear on screen, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in on cue.

Bryan: We’re just four days away from Annihilation, and this California crowd is rocking the Oakland Arena for WorldWide! Hello, everyone, I’m James Bryan alongside Pete and The Jhub once again as Global Championship Wrestling prepares to return to Pay-Per-View! And as we look forward to the huge World Title match at Annihilation, with LeStatt Knight defending his title against Michael Stevens, we’re going to see the two in a Prisoner Exchange here tonight!

Pete: It’s unprecedented, JB, but we’re going to see it here tonight! It’s been weeks of constant one-upsmanship between these two, as Michael Stevens has tried to use every trick in the book to come at the World Champion. But LeStatt Knight has been up to the task, and the result is both men are in possession of the other man’s manager!

Jhub: Knight made the first move by abducting Michael Stevens’ lovely valet Sheena, and Stevens hit back last week by snatching Knight’s bodyguard RaZiel! And guys, tonight, they’ve agreed to trade back their prisoners. But after seeing these two go at it the past few weeks, you know they’ve both got something planned for tonight.

Bryan: Neither of those men can be trusted, that’s for damn sure. But guys, tonight isn’t just about those two, we’ve got a full load as we head into Annihilation. Several new matches were signed last week, and we’ve got at least one more to confirm this evening, as “Scandalous” Ryan Stephens issued a demand for a Tag Team Title match at Annihilation. Tonight, not only will we find out who his partner is, but the details of that match, which President Caldera has reportedly granted!

Pete: Well, it’s been a long time since the Tag Titles have been defended, and it’s great to see them back in action, but the current tag champions, EVL, aren’t fit to defend! Erik VanLorne hasn’t been heard from in weeks, and Eva is still on the shelf after that brutal beating a few weeks ago on this program.

Jhub: Well, I’ve heard rumors that Eva is going to be at Annihilation, and that’s one tough broad. My money says she fights.

Bryan: And tonight, ladies and gentlemen, our main event will see Trey Tremendous and Mike Hardy team up to take on Rich Rollins and Octavian! That’s a huge match with big ramifications on Annihilation, as all four of those men seek momentum heading into the show.

Pete: But conspicuous in his absence from that match is Jonathan Borealis! I saw him backstage earlier and he was rather upset that he wasn’t included in that match in some way. I hear he’s going to ask President Caldera to include him in the match!

Jhub: I don’t know what that Borealis kid has going through his head right now, but I wouldn’t trust him as far as I could throw him. He’s been acting a little weird since he heard he has the chance to leave Annihilation a double champion.

Tough Love -by Rob & DD

The fans begin to boo mightily as "O'Fortuna" begins blaring from the speakers and flooding the ears of those in attendance. It takes only a moment for The World Champion to glide from behind the curtains. He walks to the ring with a purpose, not glaring at the fans as usual. He slides into the ring and screams for a microphone, which is quickly handed to him. The fans boo's are as loud as thunder as Knight goes to put the microphone to his lips. Knight looks out at the crowd as if he were sick, then with a thunderous voice, the champ bellows.

LeStatt: I did not come down here for you or your entertainment. This is about something far more important than your insignificant wishes! (LeStatt is fuming as he turns his attention to the ramp way) MICHAEL STEVENS! (The fans cheer in approval at the name) Forget later, I want RaZiel back right here and right now! That tramp of a lover of yours is in the back this very instant. You give me RaZiel and you can take her and let her show you the new tricks I've taught her!

Nothing happens and Knight places his hands on his hips awaiting Michael Stevens to come down and answer this "challenge". As the seconds go by Knight seems to grow more and more furious.

LeStatt: COWARD! You come down here and do what is demanded of you! You want Sheena back and you can have her, why wait until later!? Let's go right here at this very moment.

The lights in the house go out and the arena is plunged into darkness. The crowd are buzzing with anticipation and then "CAN I PLAY WITH MADNESSSS?.." screams out from the speakers and there is a hush of confusion, some cheers from those who recognize the theme. The house lights come back on and at the top of the ramp is a woman dressed like a cheerleader turned stripper that got caught in a combine harvesters blades. Explosions hit as she wanders down to ring side with out a care in the world. She climbs the steps and enters the ring and smiles at LeStatt before signaling for a mic of her own. The crowd are buzzing still but hush while she prepares to speak.

Typhoid Mary: Holaa babababooshka. Surprised?

Mary grins from ear to ear.

All of the color and emotion from LeStatt's face is wiped out in this instant. Its almost as if he is frozen in time. Those icy blue eyes of his just stare at the woman that stands in front of him, confused, excited, but most of all angry.

LeStatt: What the HELL are you doing here?

Mary takes a step back and looks from side to side mocking shock and confusion.

Typhoid Mary: Now that's no way to treats ya one and only. Then again ya likes to kick ya one and only around, which is parts of the reason Mary's here silly. She camed to keep an eye on her chacha bear and gets out summa dat ring rusties too. Ain't ya happy pappy?

It is obvious that Knight is trying to control his temper. He slowly cracks his neck, first to the left and then to the right. His eyes close as he takes in a deep breath and open them as he exhales.

LeStatt: You have no business being here Mary. You know where we stand, you are not my mother and you are not my guardian. If you insist on staying in GCW then fine, but you BETTER stay out of my damn way because if you don't ... (his voice trails off)

Mary narrows her eyes at LeStatt and mock pouts while he speaks. When he finishes her eyes go wide.

Typhoid Mary: Or you'll what rico freaking swavey, huh huh huh huh huh, or you'll what? You and Mary been there done that, got the hospital gown. (she stops poking him in the chest) Megh, how bouts you leave Mary to do what she likes, cause she can and all the Ty-sons and Ty-daughters out there wanna see Mary back in Los Ring. So nerr. She's here, she's quee...well she's here, so get used to it!

Mary shakes her curls from her face and puts her hands on her hips defiantly.

Knight stares down as Mary with a cold look in his eyes, but Mary stares back at him defiantly, something not many have done to the champ. A grin spreads across his lips.

LeStatt: Have your fun Mary, just remember what I've told you. Now if you'll excuse me I have someone IMPORTANT to find.

LeStatt now brushes passed Mary and climbs out of the ring and slowly walks back up the ramp to the boos of the crowd.

Mary turns to watch LeStatt walk up the ramp. She goes over to the ropes and hangs over them idly boucing up and down and mooning the entire arena behind her.

Typhoid Mary: Don't you walk away from Mary damn you. There is bidniss to settle and scores to score damnit! (LeStatt continues on out of sight and Mary leans back up pouting) Fine, you runalong. Mary ain't leaving this damn federation neither just cause you don't want her here. Poo on you Seenor. Mary's staying she gonna prove to herself she's still got it and she aint leaving G dub till she's satisfied herself in every ring they got damnit! (the crowd pop and Mary turns to them like she'd forgotten they were there. She then smiles wide.) Hold on to ya husbands ya wives, ya boyfriends, ya girlfriends, ya popcorn and ya monster gulpers and ya back catalogue of peewees playhouse peoples. Typhoid Mary is back in town.

Mary drops the mic as her music hits and she skips up the ramp to cheers and confusion.

Bryan: What the hell?

Jhub: I can’t keep track of all these people, JB. Typhoid Mary? I remember her… I thought she and Knight were… Never mind.

Pete: That’s what I thought, too! What’s the deal with these two?

Bryan: Well, if she’s close to LeStatt Knight, you know she can’t be trusted. Something very odd is happening here tonight, guys, and I get the feeling that Michael Stevens should be extra careful what he does.

Negotiations -by Dan

Knock knock.

Steven Caldera doesn't even bother looking up from his desk.

He's a little too buried in a pile of papers to even think about how much he hates paperwork.

Knock knock knock.

...

This time an annoyed Caldera averts his eyes up to the door, staring in a way that says 'go away.'

POUNDPOUNDPOUNDPOUNDPOUNDPOUND-

Caldera: Alright! Who is it?

The easily identifiable voice is heard through the metal door.

Borealis: Johnny Borealis. We've gotta talk, Steverino.

Looking extremely annoyed, more than normal, Caldera answers with gritted teeth.

Caldera: Come in...and don’t call me 'Steverino'.

The door opens and Johnathan Borealis stands there, looking annoyed as well.

Clad in his black jeans and a white tank top, Borealis' pefectly styled hair and expensive looking sunglasses, the fans give a nice sized cheer, especially the women as he steps into Steven Caldera's office.

Borealis: We've gotta talk, boss.

Caldera: You know, two weeks ago I was 'Mr. Caldera.' Now we're suddenly on pet names, are we? Johnny?

Borealis: Look, I work my ass off here, don't I?

I go out there all the damn time and give everything I got.

I entertain, I work the fans, and I beat people up.

So explain to me why I'm always getting shafted.

Caldera: Shafted? Johnathan, you've got a triple threat match at Annihilation! You could, in theory, walk out of that ring with your old TV Title, the US Title, or both. How are you getting shafted?

Johnathan seems to calm down a bit from his original storm upon entering.

Having a seat across from Caldera, Borealis leans back in the chair and cuts right to the chase.

Borealis: Tell me one reason why I'm not in the main event tonight. So I can get my crack at Rollins tonight. Tell me why you're keeping me out of WorldWide two weeks in a row now.

Caldera: Johnathan. I'm not keeping you out of WorldWide.

There's just this little thing called 'you don't have a match tonight.

Borealis: This is crap, Caldera! Aside from Trey Tremendous, you don't have one superstar in that freaking match! At least not one that earns his keep.

Mike Hardy is a raving joke. Octavian is a has been's has been. And Rollins! Don't even get me started on Rollins!

Without someone like me in there, your ratings are gonna drop so much that it'd be cheaper for the company if you guys just ended the show before the main event.

Caldera: Johnny, I-

Borealis: It's crap, Caldera!

Caldera: Johnathan, calm down-

Borealis: I work for this company, and I always deliver! If you think-

Caldera: I've got a position for you in that match, Johnathan.

Borealis, having stood up to gain argument leverage, slowly sits back down to Caldera's calm level.

He looks down at the floor for a couple seconds and then looks up, having gained back his boyish smile and normal Borealis-like ways.

Borealis: I'm listening.

Caldera: You're, uh...you're in the match...

You're, the uh...

You're the special guest referee!

The crowd loses it as Borealis smiles for a moment.

Bryan: Did you hear that?

Jhub: This heats up the night more than it was!

Borealis leans forward, intent on listening to this promotion.

Borealis: Is that a fact?

Caldera: Yeah. That makes things a bit more interesting.

You will referee the match of Trey Tremendous and Mike Hardy versus Octavian and...Rich Rollins. Does that seem like a better deal to you?

Borealis: ...yeah. I think that'll work.

With a smile on his face, Borealis gets up out of the chair and begins to walk to the door when Caldera interrupts.

Caldera: Johnny, I have one thing to ask you.

Borealis: Yeah.

Caldera: When was it that you started to become such good friends with Trey Tremendous? He doesn't seem anything like your type of partner.

Another boyish smile from Borealis.

Borealis: What can I say? War makes strange friends of us all.

Caldera: Yeah...so does raw, unadulterated greed.

Giving Caldera a quick wink and a 'what, me?' look, and heads out of the office.

Preparations -by Vin

In small clusters of beads slowly oozing from it's source, baby oil runs down the face, neck, and chest of Coyle O'Conner. Just moments before the match, he is in a strong, overpowering mindset of pure perfect performance. He is setting himself up for the zone, it's a place where he wants to be; somewhere that'll prepare him, guide him, and side with him. Taking his debut match more serious than anything in his entire life, Coyle stands patiently yet quietly, just waiting to be called out to the ring.

"I can feel it, it's coming, and it's gonna be good..."

Just barely, Coyle can hear what seems to be the prelude of the match. He can here statistics being discussed and fans screaming loudly. Hearing all of this doesn't only pump him up, but rather, it has him set. He knows exactly what he has to fight for, and that's for glory. Waiting for his name to be called, he has thought of every little thing that has wronged him in his life, only to spice up the energy that's about to spontaneously explode in the ring in front of thousands of people. Slowly but surely, Coyle is ready, and so is GCW. As they begin to have a bust-down on introducing Coyle O'Conner, a backstage operator is helping Coyle over to the entrance. There was a swift pat on the back which caused Coyle to turn around. When he did, he stared at the operator blankly...

"This is it..."

Andrews: The following contest is set for one fall! Introducing first, from Newport, Rhode Island, weighing in at 215 pounds. . . Coyle O’Conner!

O’Conner slides into the ring, where his music quickly cuts out and is replaced by “Control” by Puddle of Mudd. Angel appears on stage with a cocky sneer on his face, already earning a few boos from the crowd. Angel struts towards the ring, ignoring the fans as he stares down O’Conner.

Bryan: And here comes another youngster, the twenty-one year old Angel! This kid is the greenest of the green, but he wouldn’t be in GCW if he didn’t have talent! And tonight he’s got a big chance to win a match on national television!

He twists in the hold, but Coyle rolls through, and pops back up with a dropkick laying out Angel. Both men are back up to their feet quickly with Angel goes in for another lock up, but Coyle sidesteps and catches him in the back of the head with a clothesline. Angel stumbles forward into the corner, and turns only to be caught with a splash from O’Conner.

Bryan: O’Conner taking over here in the early going.

Pete: Pfft, I’m not going to waste my breath on these two. Lets seem them stick around a little longer then I might care.

Coyle continues the punishment laying in several hard punches to Angel’s skull, dizzying him. Following up the punches Coyle lays in a couple quick knees to Angel’s gut.

Bryan: Coyle O’Conner here looking to make a big man for himself in his GCW debut he’s just taking the punishment to Angel here.

Pete: …

Angel continues to get pummeled for a moment, raising his arms to block the blows, before finally reaching forward and digging a thumb into Coyle’s eye. Momentarily blinded, Angel is able to switch positions with Coyle putting him into the corner, and following up with a hard chops to the chest. The thwack of his arm across Coyle’s chest echos through the arena as the crowd “WHOOS!” with each blow. Finishing up with the chops Angel takes O’Conner down with a drop toe hold, before scampering up to the middle rope and coming up with an elbow to the smaller man’s back.

Bryan: And now it’s Angel fighting back. Those chops were just vicious.

Pete:…Not listening, don’t care, where’s the hotdog guy?

Jhub: Yeah like you need another one fatty.

Angel pops back up and pulls Coyle up by the hair. Angel goes for another arm wringer, but again Coyle rolls through popping up this time with a spinning heel kick, Angel ducks and goes for a release German suplex, but Coyle lands on his feet, as Angel turns he’s caught with a monkey flips that sends him head over heels into the corner. Coyle quickly follows him in tying Angel in the tree of woe.

Bryan: What a quick series of moves and reversals by this two rookies here in GCW this is just damn exciting.

Coyle runs to the far corner and comes back in with a baseball slide dropkick right to Angel’s face, pulling him down off the tree. Coyle pulls him to the center of the ring, and goes for a pin, but Angel kicks out quickly in two. Coyle pulls Angel back up, and continues the assault with forearms across back and shoulders. Angel takes over with another thumb to the eye, and whips O’Conner into the ropes. Angel sets for a back body drop but Coyle sees this and just rolls over Angel’s back, continues to run and leaps up onto the middle rope. O’Conner comes off with a spring board splash, but Angel catches him and DRIVES him into the mat with a spine buster. Angel goes for a pin, but O’Conner easily kicks out.

Bryan: Back and forth action here, both of these guys want to impress Steven Caldera tonight.

Both men are quickly back up to their feet, as Coyle charges in only to be caught with a belly to belly suplex. Again Angel goes for the cover, and again Coyle is able to kick out. Angel attempts to pull Coyle up by the hair but gets caught in the stomach with some vicious punches from the rowdy Irish. Losing his hold on Coyle’s hair, Angel stumbles back a step only to be caught with, a jumping uppercut, with a trip. Angel goes down and Coyle covers…1.…2...3! Coyle wins!

Bryan: Hot opener from these two, as O’Conner picks up the win in his debut match, still an impressive showing by Angel as well.

Rei Tiu -by Geoff

Cue static.

Call me Rei. No extended personal profiles or crappy, boring web logs. No Rei Tiu History 401, or at least not yet. I'm fresh off the boat.

…

Fresh off the plane, actually. I was flown in first class for no better reason than to watch a GCW show that I'll soon be a big part of. I already have dreams I can't even describe.

So I'm elated and my exultant self can hardly defend from the child-like grin slowly forming on my lips. I'm like a kid on Christmas getting the largest present, and then shoving my ass in everyone else's face. A disturbing image brought to you by Rei Tiu and associates.

But enough of that for now. My temporary abode. It's not so humble, but it works. It's somewhat quaint and tidy, average-sized and bare except for the GCW posters of old adorning the walls and the dirty mop buckets abandoned in the corner. I could be offended, but that's my job; to offend.

I've got my single black luggage bag resting on the bench under my arm, two days worth of clothes folded with careful precision inside. The small television and vcr combo bolted to the wall above me sings with bad audio as the crowd erupts in a chorus of cheers.

Go ahead and guess as to how long I've been sitting and not moving. Breath shallow. Face transfixed but mind wandering with constant confusion. It's been at least fifteen minutes with nothing to show.

I have questions seeping in my mind, and I can almost feel the steam in my head. A monotonous parade of them all with the same base and conclusion.

Call me Rei. Wanderer mass confusion plus jubilance is me, right now. Add a dash of humor and a sprinkle of wit, and you've got the full package.

I run my hand through my tu-tone hair, feeling the softness and texture of it. Clad in a simple back tee and a pair of denim cut-offs, I feel dressed for success. Never mind the suit.

”Dressing up is inevitably a substitute for good ideas. It is no coincidence that technically inept business types are known as 'suits'." I forget who said it, but you get the point.

Above me, the television sounds with edgy distortion as bodies crash to the wrestling mat. Two GCW superstars squaring off for fame and fortune. For the thrill and to satisfy their need for an end. I find it funny how wrestlers always try to think of creative ways to spout their gospel, but in substance, it's all the same. It's always about destroying each other's pride and making the other guy cry uncle.

Call me Rei. Finding the funny, sunny side in a world where violence is the puppeteer.

I suddenly yawn and stretch myself to the limit. My thoughts are shattered, and like glass, form a million other pieces that are more dangerous. I get up slowly and walk closer to the television screen, staring at it so intently one might think I'm trying to find it's soul. I can't tell who's in the ring, not that it matters, anyway. No one knows me and I don't know anyone else. I'm the freak in the trench coat that everyone shies away from. My voice is husky, my five-o-clock shadow rough and grainy. My eyes are like black coals, set off by the extreme contours of my well-chiseled jaw.

Actually, that's my evil twin. I call him Dirty Rei.

Anyway, my mind changes wavelengths quicker than a country bumpkin changes an all Boy George radio station. Like a movie you can't miss a second of, otherwise "rewind" becomes your new best friend.

I'm still staring almost blankly now at the television screen. The nifty GCW logo in the corner reminds me that Global Championship Wrestling is my new home. Or will soon be. The contract isn't official, as of yet. Formalities were dispensed in the offices of Stephen Caldera, and I'm given what he and his compadres referred to as a "Grand Tour" of the show and how it works before business ensues. This "Grand Tour" consisted of a walk out of the office, down the hallway past open locker room doors and into a makeshift locker room, complete with discarded wrestling gear and a slightly unpleasant aroma I've been trying to ignore. They call it a preview, but with this dirty room I'm in, it feels much more like a peepshow.

Despite how bad it sounds, I'm impressed. Caldera and crew let the action sweep you off your feet, not the hospitality. Even though I haven't been introduced to anyone outside the executive suite, I feel connected.

Call me Rei. Never washing my hands simply because a sign told me to.

I pry myself away from the television screen and survey. The walls are stormy grey, depressing in nature, and the floors are tile that is stained beyond cleaning. I glance at the ceiling and notice the several missing plates and yellow insulation hanging out like entrails. The television erupts again, in illegible cheers, and a knock at the door brings me back to the here and now.

"Mr. Tiu?" The extremeness with which he prolonged the name made me cringe with distaste.

"Call me Rei." I say this as I open the door and am met with ... a stranger, not surprisingly. His glasses are down at the tip of his nose and his receding hairline is dotted with beads of perspiration. Flush red like a tomato, he smiles cordially.

"Very well, Rei. I'm in a bit of hurry, so do you have the tape?"

The tape. The tape? I immediately smile at my lapse. An introduction video, of sorts. Similar to one of those group meetings where admitting the problem is the first step. A video they planned on airing specifically in house tonight at the show. A planned five minutes of airtime, but the video camera has been running for the past thirty minutes.

"Rei?"

"Wait right here a minute." He nods and I close the door, stifling a laugh. I turn towards the video camera rested on a bench and stop it. I grab the tape and pop it in the vcr, rewinding it.

~Play~

Rei sits on the bench, staring into nothingness. The sounds of GCW television are faintly heard in the background ...

~Fast Forward~

Rei sits on the bench, staring into nothingness. The sounds of GCW television are faintly heard in the background ...

A huge smile forms across my face. If this wasn't so funny, I'd be worried.

~Fast Forward~

Rei stands up and walks off camera. The sounds of GCW television are faintly heard in the background ...

I stop the tape and snatch it out of the television. I glance up in time to catch the endings of another stupid Trojan commercial before I make my way to the door. Tomato-face is tapping his fingers against the outside wall as I open it.

"Excellent. Five minutes, right? And you kept your tones pleasant? This gets aired during commercials in a few and I don't have much editing time."

"Don't worry, chief. I was well behaved." I pop a cheery smile and wave as he heads back the way he came. I watch him walk down the hallway, his stride cheerful and loose. Closing the door, I press my back against it and watch the fuzzy television screen, waiting for my glorious introduction.

Call me Rei. Waltzing into GCW with a smile.

…….

After a moment, the camera returns to ringside, where JB, Pete and Jhub sit intently watching their monitors.

Jhub: That was… interesting.

Bryan: Yet another newcomer to GCW getting ready to make his debut before long, Rei Tiu! I can’t say I know anything about the man beyond that right there, but it should be interesting to see him in action.

Pete: What was his name again?

Giant Hunting -by Rob

LeStatt and Stevens both slightly turn to the side as if in deep thought, then after just a few moments later they both spin around, with Knight delivering a super kick to the head of RAZIEL, making him tumble to the side, and at the same time Stevens hit’s a super kick of his own to the face of Sheena. Knight and Stevens now look at each other in shock and then down at the ground where …

Bryan: That’s not RaZiel!

Pete: That’s not Sheena!

Jhub: Typhoid Mary?

Bryan and Pete: Alex?

Bryan: Oh my god LeStatt and Michael Stevens were aiming to trick the other into getting the upper hand here but it all has backfired on them! How did they both know of the trick though!?

Pete: It’s a double double-cross!

Knight and Stevens just stare at each other now and appear ready to go at it but their attention is detoured as they see their other friends moving now. Knight walks to Mary’s side and Stevens walks to Alex’s side. The crowd then explodes as SHEENA is seen slide into the ring behind Knight!

Jhub: And there’s Sheena! Where the hell did she come from!?

Bryan: I don’t know but it doesn’t seem like Knight knows she is there.

Mary is pulled to her feet by Knight, and LeStatt checks to make sure she is concious. Mary somehow notices that Sheena is behind Knight and pushes him to the side and then rushes towards Sheena as well and lands a hard right hand to Sheena’s face, driving her back. Mary then steps back and dives forward, tackling Sheena through the ropes, both of them spilling outside the ring.

Stevens begins moving forward to go after Sheena and Mary but LeStatt steps in his path and the two stand face to face, nose to nose. No punches are thrown, no words are even spoken as they simply stare each other down. Knight takes a step back as he notices that Alex has gotten up and came to Michael’s side. Knight puts his hand up as if trying to de-escalate the situation. Alex and Stevens seem to have sinister smirks on their faces. Boo’s fill the arena as the big Man RaZiel leaps over the guardrail and makes his way inside of the ring right behind Alex.

Bryan: RAZIEL!

Pete: BASTARD!

Jhub: Now we’re talking!

RaZiel quickly spins Alex around and wraps his hand around his throat before lifts him up and then tossing him across the ring causing him to slide under the ropes and crash to the outside. RaZiel then walks away, climbing over the top rope and stalking Alex on the outside. RaZiel grabs a handful of hair and brings Alex to his feet before Alex dropping to a knee and hitting a low blow, Alex gets up quickly and then hit’s a DDT on the floor! On the other side Mary is driving violent kicks to the ribs of Sheena before stepping back and driving a field goal type kick to Sheena’s face, causing her to roll around holding her face.

Inside of the ring LeStatt and Stevens are watching the carnage go on. Surprisingly they aren’t even attempting to jump in, but enjoying the show. Neither are paying attention to each other until their gazes meet again and they lock eyes. Almost in unison, grins form on each of their lips.

Bryan: These two are enjoying this violence!

Jhub: I am enjoying watching two beautiful women go at it!

Pete: How on earth did RaZiel get free? And Sheena?!

Jhub: Stevens and Knight must have succeeded in setting them free before hand! They both one-upped each other, and now all hell is breaking loose!

Referees all now rush to the ring area, a few pulling Mary and Sheena from each other and the other TEN pulling RaZiel and Alex away. No one goes into the ring though as Knight and Stevens continue to simply stare each other down, while slowly circling each other.

Knight: It seems that you are a bit more intelligent than I thought Michael.

Stevens: It seems that you aren’t as big of a moron as I suspected LeStatt.

Now the two drop their microphones and stop moving, just stare. Again almost in unison they begin to applaud each other. They smirk at each other as they applaud before Knight moves backwards until he reaches the ropes and back flips out, landing on his feet. Slowly Knight backs up the ramp, still keeping his eyes locked with Steven’s. Mary and RaZiel meet up with Knight on the ramp, each saying random things towards their new enemies. Alex and Sheena have also now joined Stevens in the ring and are pointing out at Knight, RaZiel, and Mary. Stevens and Knight are the only two still silent. Mary and RaZiel walk through the curtains but Knight stays back for a moment and then puts up three fingers. Michael just nods and puts up three fingers of his own.

Bryan: THREE DAYS!

Pete: Annihilation is coming fast guys and this match is going to be huge, I for one hope Stevens destroys LeStatt!

Jhub: This should be classic Pete, I for one can not wait for Annihilation this Sunday!

The GCW logo appears on the screen, and WorldWide fades to a close as Michael Stevens and LeStatt Knight remain engaged in a historic staredown, just days away from their confrontation at Annihilation……….
