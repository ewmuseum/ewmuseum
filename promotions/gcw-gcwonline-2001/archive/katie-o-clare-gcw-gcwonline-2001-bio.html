Katie O`Clare Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames the Monarch Butterfly; the diminutive Irishwoman Hometown County Kerry, Ireland (staying currently in Burlington, VT) Birthday September 7, 1974 Height 5'2' Weight 106 Fed Titles GCW Vanguard Title Other Feds SAWF Other Fed Awards SAWF Women's Champion (2x), SAWF Cruiserweight Champion (2x), SAWF Tag-Team co-champion (Yin/Yang with Liz Ryan) Strengths She moves with lightning quickness and agility, uncorking numerous chained aerial and technical moves like a well-oiled machine. She is expert in crippling submission hooks while her gumby-like flexibility helps her tolerate the same longer than most. Weaknesses Small and thin to the point of being outright scrawny, she cannot lift any opponent weighing 230 pounds or more, though she can use such moves as a hip toss or armdrag. She cannot take nearly as many high-impact moves as others before she's out of it Wrestling Style Cruiserweight technical/high-flyer, submission Strategy Katie always seeks to outmaneuver opponents with her extensive array of mat grappling and aerial moves. Completely lacking in brute power, she has to rely on leverage and momentum instead. While she is profiicent in a number of impact power moves, she only uses them on opponents she knows she can pick up-- usually only women. Her small size lets her get under an opponent and uproot their balance, while keeping her own center of gravity very low. She can chain together many aerial attacks into a blistering assault from all directions to confound and confuse the opponent, setting them up for a painful hook or submission hold; she is proficient in many despite preferring to use the Viceroy as her trademark finisher. Katie fights cleanly and honorably, but will uncork a dirty move or two if roughhoused excessively, or to answer another dirty move. Having been a referee herself for some time, she understands how to turn the tables on an enemy that relies on allies distracting the official during a match.
