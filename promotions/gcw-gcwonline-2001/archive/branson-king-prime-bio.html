Branson King Biography Real Name Branson King Nicknames The Top Dollar Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday 7-30-1974 Height 6' 1 Weight 245 lbs Fed Titles First GCW Tag Team Champion (Wall$treet Hu$tlers) Fed Awards None Biography Branson was born into a life of great wealth and opportunity. Growing up, he had everything a young boy could dream of and much, much more. After he graduated college at Yale University with a degree in money management, he decided to prove to his family he could achieve sucess on his own. He hired Sammy Sampson, a personal trainer from the Bronx to tune his body in shape for wrestling. After two years of vigurous training King joined an independent federation called Global Championship Wrestling. There, he created a dominant stable known as the Wallstreet Hustlers. The Hustlers had many ties, and many underground negotiations that made them a sucess. They were even accused of having ties with Steven Caldera and the original Establishment. None the less, the group slowly departed and seperated. JD Billion, the first to leave has not been heard from since. Branson found himself in another independent federation, this time in Scotland. There was once a rumour that Chameleon was also performing in Scotland, but nothing has been confirmed. Due to creative differences, the Scotland based company was closed and left Branson without a home. He ventured from company to company but never found somewhere to call home. IWO, Eternal, etc... none could accomodate his taste for power. Finally, in 2003 he left the wrestling circuit. Now, in 2007, he has returned to his roots in Global Championship wrestling to finish what he once started.... Global domination! Wrestling Style Technical, Submission, High Flyer Finisher Move The Top Dollar Finisher Description A moonsault off the top rope.
