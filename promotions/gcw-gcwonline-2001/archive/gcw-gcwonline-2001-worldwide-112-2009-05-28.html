WorldWide 112 Hammerstein Ballroom — New York City, New York ---- Introduction There is no fireworks tonight, not unless they want to burn down the building. Tonight is no normal WorldWide. It is the 112 edition and it is live from the Hammerstein Ballroom in beautiful New York City. The fans have packed the building to the brim. The cameras cut to the announcer's booth as James Bryan is sitting next to David Yale. Bryan: Hello and welcome to a very special edition of WorldWide. We are just one week away from Rampage and we are live from the world famous Hammerstein Ballroom. Yale: Never in the world will you find any more of a lively crowd than here in the Hammerstein. There has been a lot of history here and we fully expect GCW to add to that. Bryan: Speaking of history, our show is filled with history tonight. Even our main event has a bit of history behind it. We have a rematch from Warpath as Vivica J. Valentine takes on "Established" Jay Terror and Jordan White. There won't be a World title on the line, but you can expect an intense fight. Yale: Also tonight will be the last two Rampage matches. There is only two more spots left and we'll find out who fills them tonight. Bryan: And that means Brian Fisher will be in action, defending his Television title against AXEL Action, the man he defeated for the belt. Yale: The other Rampage match is between two people who know each other very well; our United States Champion Zacharia Taylor and Dan Black. Bryan: With Tyson DeBough and that American Dream still hovering over him, will Zacharia be able to focus? Yale: It will be important to Black if he can't. Bryan: Plus expect tag team action and much, much, more. So stay tuned as this should be one for the books. ---- Through the Backdoor WorldWide 112 is ready to formally kick off, and the anticipation in the sold out Hammerstein Ballroom is palpable, as most every head in attendance is turned toward the entrance way. The crowd dies down a bit, as that entrance way remains empty, but the quiet remains only for a moment or two until… Bryan: Looks like we have some kind of disturbance in the crowd. Cameras and producers scramble to catch a glimpse of what those in attendance are in a tiff over, and when we can finally settle in on an image, it’s of Brian Fisher, television title draped over a shoulder, and Tyson DeBough, making their way through the crowd from a balcony, down onto the floor, up over the guardrail and underneath the ropes into the ring.
