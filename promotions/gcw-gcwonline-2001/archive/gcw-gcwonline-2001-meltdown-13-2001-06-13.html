GCW — Card

Meltdown 13, 13 June 2001

13 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 13 Carlson Center — Fairbanks, Alaska ---- Meltdown #13 (The lights in the arena fade, and the crowd begins to buzz. Suddenly, explosions light up the stage, and the crowd goes into a frenzy. The packed arena stands on its feet, as the camera pans over the fans. James Bryan chips in.) Bryan- We are just four days away from Dangerous Games, and tonight we will be coming to you live from another sold out arena! We have a huge lineup here tonight, as we prepare for one of the biggest GCW events yet! Yale- It’s going to be a huge night, JB! The Jhub gets his shot at Rich Rollins, and believe me, if my sources are correct, Mr. Danger will have a surprise for Rollins that night! Finally, the Jhub will capture GCW gold! Bryan- Not to mention the big battle royal, which is really being hotly contested! Let’s go to ringside. ( hits, and the Real Deal emerges to a generous reaction. They walk to the ring, and step in. Jimmy Taylor gets on the microphone.) Taylor- Now, despite our big tag team title match coming up this Sunday. . . (The crowd cheers.) Taylor- I have been watching with great interest as the CWU guys have shown up here in GCW. Well, little do most people realize, I myself was once part of the CWU. Bryan- What? Taylor- My name is Chris Van Burren, and I wrestled in the CWU for five months. And now that the CWU is here, I may as well refer to myself by my real name. So from now on, Jimmy Taylor is Chris Van Burren, the way it ought to be. (The crowd is confused, as the Game hits. The crowd boos as Mikado heads to the ring, the lights dimmed.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one-fall. Introducing first, weighing at 232 pounds, hailing from Parts Unknown, He is one half of the GCW World Tag Team Champions, Mikado! Andrews- And his opponent weighing in at 279 pounds, hailing from Brooklyn, New York, Dan "The Man" Rivero! Bryan- There's the bell and this match is under way. Mikado doesn't let Rivero off the ground. He is putting the boots to him. Yale- Mikado now picks Rivero off of the ground and whips him into the ropes. Rivero comes back and takes a strong clothesline. Mikado goes for the cover. ONE TWO and Rivero gets the shoulder up. Bryan- Mikado lifts Rivero up and hooks his head and arm. He lifts him up and drops him down with a suplex. Mikado floats over with the cover. ONE TWO Rivero gets the shoulder off the canvas. Yale- Mikado picks Rivero off of the ground once again and whips him towards the ropes. But Rivero reverses and whips Mikado into the ropes. Rivero lifts Mikado up and tosses him high overhead with a back body drop. Bryan- Rivero picks Mikado up and kicks him in the stomach. He shoves him under his legs and picks him up. He drops Mikado down into a huge piledriver. He goes for the cover. ONE TWO No! Mikado lifts the shoulder up. Yale- Both men climb to their feet. Mikado charges Rivero, and Rivero picks him up and drops him down in a Samoan drop. Rivero climbs to the top rope. He leaps. ELBOW DROP! Bryan- He covers. ONE TWO THR Mikado kicks out. Rivero stands and starts stomping away at Mikado. Remember these two team up with their partners this Sunday to fight for the GCW World Tag Team Titles. Yale- Mikado climbs to his feet and misses a right hand. He goes for a clothesline, but Rivero ducks and grabs his head. Rivero drops him down into a neckbreaker. Rivero covers. ONE TWO No! Mikado kicked out! Bryan- Rivero is arguing with the ref. Wait, Mikado sneaks up on him and rolls him up! He's got the tights! ONE TWO THR NO! Rivero kicked out! Rivero is furious and he goes to work on Mikado again. Yale- Rivero picks Mikado up off of the ground and whips him into the corner. He follows him in and hits a monstrous clothesline. Rivero lifts Mikado up onto the turnbuckle and climbs to the third rope himself. Bryan- Mikado starts battling it though. And Rivero gets pushed off. But he lands on his feet. Mikado stands! He leaps. Rivero catches him on his shoulders. BIG RED BOMB! Rivero covers! ONE TWO THREE! We got a winner! Andrews- Here is your winner, Dan "The Man" Rivero! Bryan- Big win for Dan Rivero here just four days from his tag team matchup! Yale- Yeah, well, when they step into the ring with Assassin One and Mikado, they won’t even make it out alive! It’s just a matter of time! We’ll be right back! (The show drops to commercial. When it returns, the camera is focused on ringside. Take a Look around hits, and the crowd reacts with both cheers and boos. Mr. Danger walks to the ring with a serious look on his face. He steps into the ring and takes a microphone.) Andrews- Please welcome the Commissioner of GCW, Mr. Danger! Danger- Now, I’m assuming your boos mean you saw Oblivion, and you saw the footage that was played at the end of the show. Well, yes, that did happen, but that was months ago, and it doesn’t mean a thing. It’s nothing but propaganda from the President, and none of you should listen to him. He’s just trying to tear me and Rollins apart, and. . . (Suddenly, the MegaTron lights up. The same scene from Oblivion is played. . .) Jim Ross: I CANT BELIEVE THIS MAN!!!!! Big Daddy Dero somehow, kicked out right before the three and Caldera is pissed!!!! And uh-oh!!! Mr. Danger sees the chair in the ring still, and there is blood on that chair, as well as on Dero's head! [ Mr. Danger picks up the chair. He sees that blood is on the chair as well as on Dero's head. He is shouting something at Caldera who is just looking innocent. ] Jim Ross: Danger is putting two and two together, and he knows Caldera used that chair! He and Caldera are arguing about it!!! Jerry Lawler: Look out! Dero is very slowly getting up, blood dripping from his face! He is a bloody mess!!!! [ As Dero gets up, Danger and Caldera are arguing. Mr. Danger then winds up the chair, and goes to hit Caldera.. Just at the last second he stops in mid air, turns around.. and smashes the chair over Big Daddy Dero's head. The crowd gives a "uuuu" then is silent ] Jim Ross: (pauses) WHAT THE HELL IS THIS???? Jerry Lawler: Did I just see what I think I saw????? Jim Ross: MR. DANGER!!!!!! JUST SMASHED THE CHAIR OVER DERO'S HEAD!!!!!! Mr. Danger throws the chair to the outside, and points down at Dero and is laughing! Jerry Lawler: What is going on JR? I can't believe this!!!! Jim Ross: Caldera picks up Dero, and hooks both arms! He lifts him up in the air, CALDERA DROP!!!! Danger for the count. One, two, three!!!!! [ The Sickness by Disturbed begins to play. The fans are totally disgusted as they boo and start throwing trash at the ring. Mr. Danger runs to the outside and grabs the belt. He comes back into the ring, and he and Caldera embrace with a big hug. Mr. Danger then gives Caldera the belt, and raises his hand. They hug again. ] The Fink: Here is your winner, and the NEEEEWWWWWW ACWF World Heavyweight Champion, STEVEN CALDERA!!!! Jim Ross: I can't believe it!!! THEY SCREWED US ALL!!!! Mr. Danger and Steve Caldera were together all along, and this makes me SICK!!!! Big Daddy Dero is bloody and beaten at the hands of Steve Caldera and Mr. Danger!! Caldera is the new champion of the world, but at what cost? Jerry Lawler: Danger and Caldera are both walking up the ramp laughing!!! What fools everyone here is!!! They never saw this coming! What a fool you are JR!! Jim Ross: Ah shut UP! Mr. Danger, shocks the world here tonight in Kansas City!! What a DEADLY DECISION he has just made!!! Right here in his hometown!!!!! They played each and every one of us!! Fans, our time is up. For Jerry Lawler, I'm Jim Ross saying goodnight from KC...... This is just sickening!!!!!! [ We see a shot of Caldera holding up the belt at the entrance to the back. We then see a shot of referees helping Dero up who is laying in a pool of his own blood in the ring. Camera fades out and the show ends. ] (The MegaTron fades to black, and the crowd boos and murmurs.) Bryan- Well, we’ve seen that before, and I can’t say that I enjoy seeing it. . . Danger- Oh, come on! Don’t listen to Caldera! He’s turning you people against me! Why can’t you see that?! (With that, the Establishment’s theme hits again. The crowd explodes in boos as President Caldera walks to the stage.) Caldera- Wow, that brings back memories, doesn’t it, buddy? I just find it interesting, because if Mr. Danger can screw one guy out of a title to help Steven Caldera, he sure as hell can do it again! Danger- First of all, don’t call me buddy. (The crowd cheers a bit.) Danger- And second of all, that footage is ancient, Steven. I came here for one reason and one reason only, and that’s to make sure you didn’t get a big head on your shoulders. Needless to say, your head is ready to explode, and I’m here to see to it that someone can bring you down to size! Caldera- Oh, man, you are the funny one, Danger, you always have been! But hey, we’ll let the fans decide for themselves. Just watch out, Renegade, because last time Danger and I were in Kansas City. . . It wasn’t good for Dero. . . And it won’t be good for Rollins. (The Establishment’s theme hits, and the crowd boos. Mr. Danger sullenly heads for the back, as the show fades to commercial. When it returns, Paranoid hits, and Andrew Hawk heads for the ring.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall and is a non-title match. Introducing first, weighing in at 275 lbs, hailing from Los Angeles, California, Andrew Hawk! (Hawk steps into the ring, as Fuel hits. The crowd goes crazy as the US champ heads for the ring.) Andrews- And his opponent, weighing in at 376 pounds, hailing from Atlanta, Georgia, He is the GCW United States Champion, "The Fueler" Luke Hanson! Bryan- Well folks, there's the bell and this Non-Title Match is under way. Hawk ties up with Hanson. Hanson gets Andrew Hawk into a side headlock. Yale- Hawk pushes Hanson into the ropes and takes a shoulder block. Hanson bounces off of the ropes and hops over Hawk. Bryan- Hawk stands and hits a nice arm drag take over on Hanson. Hanson back up, he charges at Hawk, Hawk with another arm drag. Yale- Hawk backs Hanson into the corner. And he is placing those knife-edge chops right across Hanson's chest. (Crowd lets out "Whoos" with every chop) Bryan- Hanson reverses now, and he is placing chops on Hawk. Hanson whips Hawk across the ring, but Hawk reverses. Hanson now in the corner as Hawk moves in. Yale- Hawk climbs onto the second rope and begins to lay down a Ten Punch on Hanson. (Crowd counts as Hawk hits Hanson) Bryan- Hawk hits number eight and nine. But Hanson reaches down and pulls Hawk up and drops him down into a sit down powerbomb. Hanson covers. ONE TWO and Hawk gets the shoulder up. Yale- Hanson picks Hawk up off of the ground and whips him into the ropes. Hawk ducks a clothesline and hits the brakes. He waits for Hanson to turn around and he plants a spinning heel kick on Luke. Bryan- Hawk goes for the cover. ONE TWO TH Hanson gets the left shoulder off the ground. Yale- Hawk lifts Hanson up and body slams him back down to the canvas. Hawk positions Hanson, and he climbs to the top turnbuckle. Bryan- But Hanson stands and shakes the ropes. Hawk falls spread eagle onto the top turnbuckle! Yale- Hanson climbs to the second rope and hooks Hawk's arm. He pulls him off. SUPERPLEX!! Hanson floats over with the cover. ONE TWO THREE! NO!!!!!! Hawk got his shoulder up! Bryan- Hanson picks Hawk off the mat and whips him into the ropes. Hawk comes back with a nice dropkick. Yale- Hawk picks Hanson up and grabs his head. He runs, walks the turnbuckle and comes down with a running Tornado DDT! Cover. ONE TWO THR Hanson gets the shoulder up. Bryan- Hawk kicks Hanson in the stomach a few times and climbs the turnbuckle. He is perched up top. Hawk leaps. Elbow drop! Here's the cover, ONE TWO THR Hanson kicked out! Yale- Hawk cannot believe it. He picks Hanson off the ground, and positions him between his legs. He pulls Hanson up. And there is a nice Fall Forward Powerbomb. Bryan- Hawk goes for the cover. ONE TWO THR Once again, Hanson gets his shoulder off of the canvas. Hawk climbs to his feet and he goes through the ropes to the apron. Yale- Hanson is back up also though, and stops Hawk from reaching the turnbuckle. Hawk shoulders Hanson in the gut and leaps over the ropes. Bryan- Hawk grabs Hanson's legs and completes the Sunset Flip. ONE TWO Hanson reverses the pin! ONE TWO Hawk reverses! ONE TWO Hanson reverses again! ONE TWO and Hawk escapes just before the three count. Yale- Hanson stands and moves in on Hawk. Hanson drops down with an elbow to the back of the head. Hanson climbs to his feet once again and drags Hawk to the middle of the ring. Bryan- Hanson grabs Hawk's legs and locks an STF onto Hawk! The ref is checking to see if Hawk is giving up. Hawk is struggling to reach the ropes. He is writhing in pain. Yale- Hawk just does make it to the ropes and Hanson breaks the hold. Hawk climbs to his feet and ducks a clothesline from Hanson. Hawk kicks Luke in the gut and hooks his head. He drops him down with a DDT! Bryan- Hawk lifts Hanson to his feet and bounces off of the ropes. He comes back and goes for a crucifix, but Hanson counters! Hanson has Hawk on his shoulders. And there is the DeFueler! Hanson covers. ONE TWO THREE. He got him. Andrews- Here is your winner, "The Fueler" Luke Hanson! Yale- Wow! Those two just put on a great match for us. Bryan- Well, lets now go to ringside for another amazing battle in the Hardy/ChameleonDemented best of seven series. Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall, and is the sixth match in this best of seven series! Introducing first, weighing in at 222 pounds,hailing from Cameron, North Carolina, Michael "Hickenbottom" Hardy! (I Disappear hits and Hardy sprints to the ring. As he slides into the ring Chamoflauge hits.) Andrews- And his opponent, weighing in at 195 pounds, hailing from Las Vegas, Nevada, Chameleon! Bryan- Well, these two young superstars have embarked themselves in a best of seven series. But what has really been interesting is Hardy's interest in! Katanya. That young woman seems to be the only thing on his mind. Yale- Well, he should have Chameleon on his mind. He is going to have his hands full. Bryan- Well, there's the bell and this match is officially under way. They pace around each other, and now they lockup. Hardy has a side headlock on Chameleon. Yale- Chameleon shoves Hardy into the ropes and ducks a clothesline. Hardy comes back, gaining speed, and hits a shoulder block. Bryan- Hardy stands and runs into the ropes, he comes back and Chameleon catches him with a nicely executed drop toe hold. Chameleon picks Hardy off the ground and goes to work on his left knee. Yale- Chameleon picks Hardy up and grabs his jaw. He leaps and falls to the ground. Nice Jawbreaker. He goes for the cover. ONE TWO NO Hardy gets the shoulder up. Bryan- Chameleon is furious. He raises Hardy from the ground and whips him into the ropes, Hardy reverses and Chameleon goes into the ropes. Yale- Chameleon goes for a clothesline but Hardy ducks and reaches backward, he grabs Chameleon's head and executes a neckbreaker. Cover, ONE TWO THRE- Chameleon just gets the shoulder up. Bryan- Hardy raises Chameleon off of the mat and tosses him over the top rope. Hardy leaps onto the turnbuckle and waits for Chameleon to turn around. Yale- Hardy leaps and lands on Chameleon's shoulders. He rocks him over and executes a nice hurricanrana. Bryan- Both men are hurting from that. Hardy struggles to his feet and Chameleon is up as well. Yale- Hardy hits a few right hands. The ref is counting these two superstars out! Ref- ONE! TWO! Bryan- Chameleon blocks a few punches and hits a few of his own. Hardy is being backed towards the entrance ramp. Ref- THREE!! Yale- Hardy is standing on the edge of the entrance ramp and Chameleon comes charging. Oh My God! Drop toe hold onto the steel entrance ramp. Ref- FOUR!! Bryan- Hardy rolls into the ring and restarts the count. He moves back to Chameleon and whips him into the side of the ring. Hardy charges and goes for a springboard DDT. Ref- ONE! Yale- Chameleon grabs onto the ropes and Hardy falls flat onto his back, straight to the floor. Chameleon slides into the ring and as Hardy climbs to his feet, grabs him by the hair. Ref- TWO! Bryan- Hardy now on the apron, shoulders Chameleon in the gut and springs over the top rope. Chameleon side steps Hardy, and Hardy flies right into the ref. Yale- Hardy did that intentionally! That cheater! Bryan- I doubt that David. Yale- Whatever, but Hardy rolls out of the ring and grabs a steel chair! See? He is a cheater! He slides into the ring and Chameleon starts stomping away. Bryan- Well, Hardy makes it to his feet and runs towards Chameleon. Chameleon has the chair! Hardy doesn't see it! Yale- Chameleon cracks that chair upside Hardy's head! He drops the chair in the center of the ring and picks Hardy up off of the ground. Bryan- Hardy gets whipped into the corner. He springs upwards and Chameleon follows him in. Hardy lands on Chameleon's shoulder, upside down. Yale- Chameleon forces Hardy down, and into a Tombstone position! Bryan- Chameleon walks over to that chair and is standing right over it! Don't do it! You'll break this kids neck! Yale- That's the idea! Chameleon has that look in his face and--WAIT!! Bryan- Hardy pulls himself up and is sitting on Chameleon's shoulders! He pulls Chameleon with his legs and hits a hurricanrana DDT onto the chair! He calls that The Bottom Feeder! Yale- NO!! Hardy is climbing to the top! He is perched on the top rope. He leaps! Bryan- SWANTON BOMB!!! Hardy hits the Swanton Bomb and covers! But the ref still isn't up! Hardy stands and stomps Chameleon a few more times. Yale- The ref is up. And Hardy notices. Hardy drops and covers again. Bryan- ONE TWO THREE NO!! Chameleon got his shoulder off of the ground! How in the hell did he do that?! Yale- I don't know, but at least he did. Hardy is furious. He goes back to the turnbuckle and leaps up top once again. Hardy is about to hit the Swanton Bomb again. Wait!! Bryan- Is that? It is! There is a video of Katanya on the megatron! She is tied to a chair! Hardy notices and he jumps down to the floor. He is sprinting to the back! Yale- Hardy is backstage. He is still in a match. Ref- ONE! Bryan- No! Hardy is being counted out! Well, we have a camera caught up to Hardy. (The camera switches and Hardy is seen sprinting down a hall. He opens a door and finds nothing.) Hardy- Katanya!! Where are you?! Yell!! (Hardy checks a few more doors and yells again. The camera switches back to the ring, showing Chameleon back on his feet.) Ref- TEN!! Ring the bell! (DING DING DING) Andrews- Your winner of the match, due to a count out, Chameleon! Bryan- Hardy was cheated! He went to the back to find Katanya and got counted out. Well, Chameleon is making his way to the back with a small smirk on his face, but don't go away folks. We have a hardcore match coming up. (The scene switched to John Reed's dressing room. He is on the ground doing push ups. He does some and springs into a sitting position. He takes a bottle of Mountain Dew and chugs some. The camera switches to a shot of Trash walking down a hall. He looks determined to hurt John.) Bryan- Well folks those two stars will go at it in a hardcore match next! (Show fades to a commercial) (Freak on a Leash hits, and the crowd explodes with boos. Shane Lane and Hiroshima Genryu head to the ring, Lane flexing the whole way.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, from Beverly Hills, California, he weighs in at 224 pounds. . . Shane Lane! Bryan- In four days, Shane Lane meets the Fueler Luke Hanson in a towel match for the United States title! What a match that will be! (Lane steps into the ring, hops onto the turnbuckle, and flexes for the fans. They continue to boo, as My Own Summer hits. The Jhub heads to the ring, accompanied by President Caldera.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, from Stamford, Connecticut, he weighs in at 278 pounds. . . The Jhub! Yale- And the Jhub gets his shot at the World Title against the Renegade at Dangerous Games! It’s going to be great, JB! (The Jhub steps into the ring, glancing at Lane. Fat Lip hits next, and the fans jump to their feet. Toxic walks to the ring, the crowd cheering him on.) Andrews- And their opponents, introducing first, from San Jose, California, he weighs in at 298 pounds. . . Toxic! (Toxic stops halfway down the ramp, as Born to be Wild hits. The crowd explodes again, as the world champ walks down the ramp. He meets Toxic, and they hit the ring.) Andrews- And his tag team partner, from Chicago, Illinois, he weighs in at 225 pounds, and he is the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins! (Rollins and Toxic slide in, and Jhub and Lane meet them right away. The bell rings, and the match begins.) Bryan- And here we go! Rollins and the Jhub are going at it, while Lane and Toxic head to the apron! The Jhub takes over on Rollins, and shoots him off into the ropes! Rollins bounces back, ducks a clothesline. . . Off the other side, Rollins comes back with a clothesline of his own! Yale- The Jhub goes down, but Rollins lifts him right back up! Rollins gets a head of steam, and the Jhub throws a clothesline! Rollins ducks, and comes back from the other side! He connects with a flying elbow! Bryan- The Jhub is in trouble, and Rollins tags in Toxic! The big man comes into the ring, and lifts up the Jhub! But the Jhub shoves Toxic away, falls back, and tags in Shane Lane! Yale- Oh, man! Lane versus Toxic! Lane charges in, but Toxic takes him down with a big right hand! Lane gets back up, and Toxic takes him right back down again! Lane is in trouble here! Bryan- Wait, now President Caldera is on the apron! The referee turns to usher him off, but Toxic sees him! Toxic heads for Caldera, and sends him flying off the apron with a big boot to the face! (The crowd goes crazy as Caldera flies off the apron. The Jhub hops down to help him up.) Yale- Lane is back to his feet, and he hits Toxic from behind with an axe handle! Toxic is stunned, as Lane sends him into the ropes! Toxic bounces back, and Lane takes him down with a deep arm drag! Bryan- The Jhub is back to the apron, as Caldera regains his composure! Wouldn’t it be a shame if he wrinkled that suit? Yale- Watch it, now. . . Bryan- Toxic is back up, and Lane backs him into the corner with a few quick right hands! Lane pounds away with stiff right hands and boots to the gut, and he has Toxic in trouble! (The crowd boos, as Lane backs out of the corner and flexes again.) Bryan- Oh, come on! Yale- Woah! Toxic explodes out of the corner with a big clothesline, and about decapitates Lane! Toxic slowly heads for Rollins, as Lane heads for the Jhub! Bryan- Lane tags in the Jhub! Toxic is almost to Rollins, and. . . HEY! President Caldera pulls Rollins down from the apron! Toxic was there for the tag, but Caldera pulls Rollins off! The Jhub pulls Toxic into the middle of the ring, and slaps on a half crab! Yale- Wait, Rollins just hit Caldera! Rollins and Caldera are going at it out here by our announce table! Wait, Hiroshima Genryu is here, and he’s assaulting Rollins from behind! There’s a double-team on Rollins! (The crowd boos, until Mr. Danger runs out from the back with a steel chair. He gets a few cheers as he heads for Caldera.) Bryan- Mr. Danger is here! The commissioner is here! And he’s got another steel chair! Danger runs to the double-team. . . And swings the chair! Yale- Whoa! Danger levels Hiroshima Genryu! Genryu is down! Caldera spots Danger now, and he’s high-tailing it out of there! Danger gives chase! Bryan- The Jhub releases Toxic now, and slides out to the floor! Now Shane Lane is in, and working over Toxic! Danger is chasing Caldera, as Rollins grabs a steel chair! Caldera is running around the ring, and is almost back over here now! Yale- Danger is giving chase to Caldera, and he slides into the ring to cut him off! But Rollins intercepts Caldera, and takes him down with a chair shot to the face! Oh my God! Caldera is down! (The crowd goes crazy.) Bryan- And Mr. Danger sees Lane going after Toxic! The Jhub is over to Caldera, as Rollins slides into the ring with his chair! He’s shouting out at the Jhub, slowly backing up! Yale- Danger takes Lane down with a chair shot! Toxic rolls out to the floor, as Danger backs away from Lane! (The crowd begins to buzz, as Danger and Rollins back into each other. Danger, startled, turns around, swinging the chair.) Bryan- Wait. . . OH, NO!!! NO, Damn it! Yale- Mr. Danger just blatantly hit Rollins with that chair! Rollins is down, as the Jhub climbs back into the ring! (The crowd boos like hell, now convinced about Danger. Danger looks down in shock, as the Jhub rushes in and takes him down. They wrestle out to the floor.) Bryan- This is not good! Danger and the Jhub are going at it on the outside, but Toxic is up! Toxic goes after the Jhub, and takes him down! But Genryu is helping Shane Lane get back up! Lane is up, and on the apron! Yale- Lane climbs back into the ring, as Rollins slowly tries to get back up! Rollins looks out to Danger, who’s busy with the Jhub! But Lane is up behind him! Lane locks on an abdominal stretch! Bryan- Wait. . . SUPERSTAR DRIVER! Superstar Driver on the World Champion! I don’t believe it! Lane rolls Rollins over, and covers! ONE! TWO!! THREE!!!! He got him! (The crowd boos, as Freak on a Leash hits. Genryu pulls Lane out of the ring, and they head back up the ramp.) Andrews- Here are your winners, Shane Lane and the Jhub! Yale- Shane Lane just pinned the world champion! Bryan- Damn it all to hell! Mr. Danger cost Rollins that match! I don’t know what to think any more, fans! Toxic and the Jhub are still going at it, as Mr. Danger climbs back into the ring to check on Rollins! Yale- Rollins is slowly pulling himself up in the corner, as Danger tries to console him! But Rollins just pushes Danger out of the way! Rollins slowly gets to his feet, as Danger tries to reassure him! Bryan- Wait a minute! SUPERKICK! Rollins explodes out of the corner and superkicks the commissioner! (The crowd goes crazy as Danger drops to the mat, out cold.) Yale- Wow! The Renegade has made himself clear as to what he thinks of Danger! Bryan- But Mr. Danger is the special referee for the World Title match in four days! This is not a good situation, folks! We’re out of time from Meltdown! Join us for Dangerous Games this Sunday, LIVE on Pay-Per-View! (The show fades out as Mr. Danger lies motionless in the ring.)
