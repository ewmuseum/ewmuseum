Meltdown 40 Houston County Farm Center — Dothan, Alabama ---- Meltdown #40 (The lights dim in the Astrodome, where thousands of fans sit ready for a tremendous event. The MegaTron flickers to life, and the GCW logo appears. The cameras then cut to scenes from Christmas Chaos and its amazing events.) Yale- Hold on, Dread caught Badger! The crowd goes absolutely crazy, with an enthusiastic ‘Holy Shit!’ chant breaking out following Dread’s breathtaking fall.) Yale- OH MY GOD!!! Bryan- GOOD GOD ALMIGHTY!
