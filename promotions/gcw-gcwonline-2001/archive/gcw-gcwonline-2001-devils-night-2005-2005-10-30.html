Introduction: The Devil’s Knight -by Ben & Rob

Abashed, the devil stood and felt how awful goodness is…

The Devil’s Night, October 30th, is a night of evil and mischief. A night where the armies of the devil march across the earth, wreaking havoc and spreading sin. But tonight, the devil’s greatest weapon has seized control of Global Championship Wrestling. An event of his own, a night where his malice and hatred will rule all that occurs, with his watchful eye endlessly fixed upon each and every terrified victim.

The GCW World Heavyweight Champion. A malevolent instrument of destruction, eager for another challenge. Having brushed aside everyone that opposed him, the Devil’s Knight waits, patiently biding his time until another rises to meet him. But his blood lust is unquenchable, so tonight he leads five innocent lambs to slaughter, and only one lone wolf will survive. Then, voraciously he will descend upon his newest challenger, eager to begin another bloody and devastating struggle for supremacy.

The devil has unleashed his greatest weapon, and tonight he sits unchallenged, the undisputed champion of all things good and evil. With his vile imagination he concocted his plan, and on his sinister whim six men enter the ring with the intention of burying one another alive. Five men will find themselves six feet under. Only the most merciless of them will remain standing.

And as he stands above his buried rivals, the last one above ground; if he survives, he will raise his eyes skyward, and see the Devil’s Knight smiling… For another victim has been named.

The lights dim in the arena, and a low murmur rises in the crowd. The camera opens to a straight-on shot of the stage, merely a hulking silhouette in the shade. Finally, after a pause, the dark overture of “O’Fortuna” begins to play. After the booming opening chorus, the sinister notes spark the lighting of a pair of massive torches above the stage. Finally a dark, blood-red light rises, dimly illuminating the set.

The MegaTron sits directly on the stage, bordering the entrance on the left. On the right hangs a massive “Devil’s Night” banner, both framing in the massive stage. Usually made of steel, the stage on this night is dark dirt, and in a neat row, spaced evenly except for a wide opening in the entranceway, are five deep graves.

Behind each grave is a large pile of dirt, and behind each pile a small, golf-cart sized bulldozer, its open face perfect for rapidly filling a grave. Stuck ominously in each pile of dirt are a pair of old rusty shovels, desperately waiting for their chance to be used to inflict pain and terror.

The stage is suddenly lit by a large ring of torches, perched above the façade. There, directly above the entrance ramp, raised on yet another pile of dirt, towers a devilish throne, made of twisted black wood and padded with purple silk. To the right sits a smaller, more subtle throne, but still ghastly and ominous in its own right. Behind the massive setup rise two massive red torches, their flames casting large shadows down upon the fans.

Finally, after a few moments, a dark figure appears on top of the stage. Circling the large throne is the GCW World Heavyweight Champion himself, LeStatt Knight. The fans erupt in boos as Knight, now standing in a small, faint spotlight, grins from beneath his matted black hair. Clad in black pants and an open black shirt, the GCW World Heavyweight Title belt over his shoulder, Knight turns and looks over his shoulder.

From behind the smaller throne comes Typhoid Mary, looking smug and excited as she glides to Knight’s side. His music fades but remains in the background, as Knight produces a microphone and addresses the crowd in a grim but sadistically eager voice.

Knight: Ladies and gentlemen… Welcome… To Devil’s Night. Welcome… to MY night.

With that, the torches all around the ring suddenly flare high in the sky, raising the lights in the arena as “O’Fortuna” continues to serenade the beginning of Devil’s Night! The official Devil’s Night introduction begins to play on the MegaTron, finally earning a huge eruption of cheers from the fans! The cameras begin flying wildly over the raucous Detroit crowd, come in costume, others wielding signs and GCW merchandise. The shot finally rests on James Bryan and David Yale. Bryan is dressed in his usual sharp suit and black tie, but Yale appears to be in his James Bond tuxedo once again, though his lovely ladies didn’t make the trip.

Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, WELCOME to Devil’s Night! I’m James Bryan here at ringside with the incomparable James Bond for this historic night, a night which will see six men desperately trying to bury one another alive in an attempt to win a shot at the GCW World Heavyweight Championship! We’ve got a packed house here at Joe Louis Arena in downtown Detroit, and this crowd is ready for a tremendous show!

Yale: For the past two months, JB, we’ve been talking about NC-17, and how Devil’s Night is the last major stop before the event of the century. But now that I’m here, I see this incredible setup, I hear the screaming fans, and I see those five graves, I can’t help but be excited about Devil’s Night! This is going to be an amazing event, and I can’t wait to get it started!

Bryan: We’ve got some big matches lined up for the evening, folks, including an unprecedented main event! Tonight, five men will be Buried Alive, and just one will remain standing to challenge LeStatt Knight for the GCW World Heavyweight Title! Mike Savige, Aiden Jackson, Rei Tiu, Bryan Mayhem, RipeFist and Chrissy-Poo will do battle in an attempt put one another six feet under and get a shot at the champion.

Yale: Just thinking about it sends shivers down my spine, JB. This is gonna be intense.

Bryan: Plus, we’ll see Rich Rollins taking on Seymour Almasy, Johnny Borealis puts his United States Title on the line against Eva VanLorne, and so much more! But let’s get things started right away by heading to ringside, where Coyle O’Conner gets a shot at Sam Wolack in a Falls Count Anywhere match!

Ricky Thunder: I’m almost scared to ask...so I wont. Just show me...

Ricky Thunder: Monster...? Is this the same costume?

RipeFist: It's the same, but not the same, it's gone through a transformation, become something....MORE!

Without further adieu RipeFist reaches forward and tears the sheet away revealing....the same exact costume as before.

It's....the...same...

RipeFist: NO, it's completely different look at the face Friend Thunder...if you dare.

Upon closer inspection we can see the head of the outfit has indeed been altered. It looks like a two year old went mad with safety scissors and crayons turning what was once a smiling face into something that might vaguely resemble an angry scowl...if you squint, and stand about twenty feet back

RipeFist: Of course not friend Thunder it never had whiskers don't be silly.

Ricky Thunder: Did...is that...Ripey...why did you color on this thing?

RipeFist: I realized how people could not cower before this awesome creation of stark brutal fear, it smiled. Of course it's hard to be scared of something that's smiling, so I made it growl, can't you see it's gonna get you...

*Ricky stares in disbelief at what he just heard*

RipeFist: Like a fox or even A SQUIRREL!

RipeFist laughs at his little joke playing slapping Ricky on the shoulder

Ricky Thunder: Ripey...listen to me. You are in a buried alive match against some of the best in this company. 1 Mistake and you are done...I am goin to step in here and say no...

RipeFist: Friend Thunder, don't you think I know this, I cannot afford to make a mistake, I must use every advantage possible, this is exactly WHY I must do this.

RipeFist looks crestfallen and slumps down in a chair, looking up in dejection, wide eyed he looks at Ricky.

I have told you my take on things...if you want to go out there in that ridiculous setup and end up crippled, its up to you. I'm done.

Without saying another word Ricky turns and walks out the door shaking his head in disgust. The door slams behind him, as RipeFist continues to sit saying nothing doing nothing merely staring out towards his squirrel costume.

Last Minute Plea -by Carrie & Gary

RipeFist sat alone, mournfully gazing at his beloved squirrel costume. He smiled sadly looking into it's eyes, all his work turning the goofy smile into something resembling a fierce grimace had been for nothing. Truly a sad day indeed

A knock at his door was quickly followed by the entrance of Eva VanLorne. She looked ready to talk serious business but found herself pausing at his mournful stature.

Eva: What is wrong with you Ripey?

Without bothering to look to at his visitor RipeFist reaches out towards the costume softly stroking it's fur.

RipeFist: Ricky told me I shouldn't wear it...

Eva sighs as she comes over to stand beside him. For a moment she looks between him and the mass of fur before speaking.

Eva: Well Ricky is right. You want to be taken seriously tonight. That costume will inhibit movement and proper grasp. Besides..you don’t want to hide behind more coverings do you?

RipeFist: I even made it look more scary...See? He's not grinning anymore.

I felt so strong wearing it, the darkest fears of man realized. I was less a man than a god. It was such incredible power, but I'm not supposed to wear it. It's not fair.

Eva looks at the squirrel and half smiles.

Eva: Well he does look more menacing I will give you that. Still you have no need to be in a costume to be powerful. Power comes from the heart and will to succeed. You want succeed right?

RipeFist: The power of Fruit-Fu shall overcome all who stand before it. I know this, yet for the first time since I inherited the mantle of master upon Yore's death, I find myself questioning...

Couldn't the power of omelets be just as great? Chili Dogs seem to have a profound and deadly effect on Friend Thunder...

Eva merely makes a bland face and sighs with exasperation as she places a grip firmly on his shoulder and forces him to look her in the eyes.

Eva: Look Ripey, you don’t need fruit fu, or egg fu or even chili fu or any other kind. What you need is spirit fu. The will to go out there and simply kick some asses and take some names. You got me? NO fruit fu. Just wrestle...and win.

Boldly RipeFist leaps to his feet, the sudden movement nearly knocking Eva down. Raising a fist to the air he points one finger to the air.

RipeFist: You're right Lady Eva. It's only through belief in myself that I shall prevail. I don't need a costume to be imposing, the power of Fruit-Fu is all that I need. Power and determination to do what's right in every scenario.

Thank you Lady Eva for setting me straight, your kind and inspiring words will not be soon forgotten I assure you of that. However I apologize, you must excuse me I must mediate on goals, only through visualizing the cherry, may the owl pick the treat after all!

Eva is clearly not pleased with the mention of Fruit Fu yet again. Throw her hands up in defeat she merely shakes her head and heads for the door.

Eva: Have it your way then Ripey. You want people to take you seriously then you have to be serious and fruit fu isn't the way.

Do us both a favor....and go into this match playing it straight. None of that damn fruit fu. Think about it will ya? As for meditating, yeah, I have my own match to get ready for. Dont let me down ( turning back to look at him) please.

Eva turns back and continues out the door. RipeFist seems to have not noticed until the sound of the door clicking home rings through the room. Turning his head he looks towards the closed door.

RipeFist: Lady Eva?

Fades.

Prelude to a Funeral, Part 1 -by Geoff

Halloween. Samhein. Bringing in the Celtic New Year with lots of cheap imitation and vitiated candy. A time when adults dress in Peter Pan costumes and try to pretend that they are defining what is cool.

Samhein, in all actuality, is the time when enlightened ones celebrate the spirit world and the connection between it and our own. It’s time for shedding the weaknesses and cleaning the slums from our souls. Enlightened ones, or Wiccans if you’re hip on that, bring a parchment to their sacred fires of Samhein and reduce it to ashes. Supposedly, the weaknesses fade away and the prayers rise with the Gods and Goddesses into the atmosphere and the black, acrid smoke.

It’s a time for accessing spiritual growth and divination. Examining the already treaded paths and peering ahead to determine future development.

Supposedly, the veil between the two worlds, the spirit and our own, and is at its thinnest at Samhein. Allowing communication between the living and the deceased. It almost sounds like a cheap plug for a new invention.

[Are you down in the dumps because your Aunt Mary died in that horrible train accident?]

Eh?

[You know, the one where her body was crushed and her severed head was found several feet from her body, twisted into an everlasting look of pure terror and agony?]

…

[Didn’t get to say all you wanted to her in life, did you Tommy? Well, cheer up. Samhein is here! If you act now, you can finally tell her EVERYTHING you wanted to tell her! Isn’t that amazing?! ]

And as always, there’s a catch.

[All you have to do, Tommy, is a few simple installments of two thirds of your corn, milk, and your children. How’s that for easy?!]

Yeah. Damn marketing ploys.

Anyway, Samhein was a time of great significance to the Celts … and a bunch of really strange shi* happened. And some other strange shi* happened.

And we now have fat Batmans and midget Robocops running around purloining candy. That could probably pass for strange.

I am Rei. Celebrating the eve of Samhein in Detroit, Michigan. You already know what’s going on.

The much hyped Devil’s Night is upon us, bringing in the evening with a bunch of mat slamming and glistening sweat. The grinding flesh. The blood. Tears. Pure violence served in monolithic quantities before the peaceful celebration.

Outside the arena, the night air is cool. A gusty breeze running benign fingers through my hair and whispering sweet nothings while nibbling my lobes. I’m at the back of the arena, duffle bag in tow. Even from here, the decibel level inside the arena is sending shockwaves. Obviously, the hype had worked. It’s a sold out show, neon digital sign speaking in all capital letters with pride.

Call me Rei. On the outside looking in.

I’m thinking as I make my backstage entrance. The phone call. Rebecca had somehow managed to get in touch with me in Detroit, even though I hadn’t talked to her prior. She’s like the mafia.

I couldn’t get away from her if I wanted to.

“Rei, how are you?” A question that burrows deep in the ocean floor. I want to tell her about GCW. I want to convey everything I’m thinking. Pointing out with a laser pen my life on a blackboard, outlining the important and emphasizing everything. I want to give her a play-by-play of my life, compete with highlight reels and instant replays.

But what’s the point? She’s my center. Wrestling is my life, and Rebecca Hendley is the governing body that keeps the rotational orbits in line. With her gone, there’s utter chaos and my planet-lifes crashing into each other.

So I’m a bit of a wreck. What else is new? Another rhetorical question. My life is like that.

I am Rei. Jealous of Forrest Gump. I wish my life could be like a box of chocolates. Everything is sweet.

“I don’t know. I’m … tired, I guess. How are you?” Bullshi* answer that she lets me get away with. I’m thanking the heavens. I can’t find the words or muster the energy to match the speed at which my mind is running through how I feel.

“I’m okay. I’m really tired.” As well she should. Work at the museum is a hybrid vampire-zombie, sucking her arteries until they’re dry and consuming her flesh to further throw salt in the wound. I can picture her tired eyes, lids threatening to snap shut. The corner smile I get when I playfully flick her nose, and how she bites my cheek with a pleasurable clamping of teeth. It sends shivers pulsating through my neck. “I just wanted to call and tell you to be safe.”

Ha. Safe. Everywhere I go, irony bares its little fangs. Devil’s Night has me in the main event in a buried alive match. Apparently, I earned the right to compete in that after beating Typhoid Mary. Aiden finally manages a rake to the eyes to free himself, and both men get to their feet. Rei squares up and nails Aiden with a straight kick to the heart, then springboards off the nearby dozer with a spinning heel kick that drives Aiden right back into the dirt! The fans cheer wildly as Rei stands, quietly observing his fallen opponents around him.

Bryan: And listen to this crowd! They’re solidly behind Rei Tiu, who is standing tall right now in this Six-Man Buried Alive Match!

Yale: Savige is bleeding and battered, Aiden is out, and RipeFist doesn’t look like he even knows where he is! This might be Rei’s night, JB!

Bryan: He’s still got to bury the rest of these guys, though, and that will be no easy task!

Rei pauses for a moment, then turns back to Savige. Rei pulls Savige out of the dirt and hits him with a low knee, then delivers a mean snap suplex into the dirt towards the next empty grave on the left side of the stage. The fans rise and begin to buzz as Rei pulls Savige up again, leading him towards the grave with big right hands to the face. Savige manages to absorb the blows and keep his ground, but Rei slowly positions him at the edge of the grave!

Bryan: And Rei is bringing Mike Savige towards that grave! He’s going to bury the biggest man left in this match, and that may be a good idea!

Yale: But Savige won’t go down, JB! He’s staying up! Rei might not have enough behind those blows to knock down the big man!

Indeed, Savige seems to get stronger with each shot. Suddenly he fires one back, though Rei keeps on throwing punches. The two exchange blows as behind them Aiden Jackson crawls into the dozer resting on top of Chrissy-Poo’s grave! Aiden swings it around and guns forward, heading right at Rei and Savige!

Bryan: OH NO! WATCH OUT!

Savige, seeing Aiden coming, dives out of the way. Rei spins around just in time to get struck with the speeding dozer! The small vehicle carries enough punch to throw Rei violently into the grave below, and Aiden immediately leaps out for the next dozer over!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! Aiden Jackson just ran over Rei Tiu with that damn bulldozer! Rei’s in the grave!

Yale: Rei must have been crushed! But look! He’s still alive!

The camera peers down into the grave, where Rei is slowly pulling himself to his feet. But moments later, Aiden moves the dozer above forward, pushing the massive pile of dirt downward. Rei is crushed beneath the weight of the dirt, which fills the grave on top of him!

Bryan: GOOD GOD! REI TIU HAS JUST BEEN BURIED ALIVE!

The crowd unleashes its full fury at Aiden as he drops lifelessly out of the bulldozer. Lying on top of Rei’s grave, Aiden begins to laugh, chuckling with satisfaction as the boos rain down.

Yale: Wow! Just like that, Rei is gone!

Bryan: Rei Tiu was in firm control of this match, as he was for just about the entirety of it! But it took two cheap shots from Malachi to finish Rei off!

Yale: The first cheap shot didn’t finish Rei off, but the second one sure did! First Aiden laid an unsuspecting and perhaps overly trusting Rei out with that shovel to the face! But somehow Rei miraculously came back and took control, beating the hell out of Aiden for it! But Aiden hit him from behind with that dozer, and Rei is gone!

Bryan: Hell, Rei was still coming even after that! But he just wasn’t quite fast enough! And these fans are absolutely livid! Well, give Aiden credit, he hung in there, but damnit, he’s completely deceived his “friend” Rei Tiu and buried him alive!

Yale: Hey, that’s what I was saying from the beginning! That’s why Aiden’s my pick!

Aiden slowly stands, locking eyes with Savige as he crawls to his feet. Two graves now remain; one on either end of the stage. Aiden and Savige suddenly turns their attention to RipeFist, who lies in a heap on the entrance ramp.

Bryan: We’re down to three now, with RipeFist somehow hanging on and staying out of sight, the Cruiserweight Champion Aiden Jackson and the former TV Champion Mike Savige! One of these men will meet LeStatt Knight for the GCW World Heavyweight Championship!

The camera returns to Knight during this brief lull in the action, and he appears to be satisfied with the carnage so far. Surveying the scene below, Knight takes a satisfied drink of his wine and relaxes on his throne.

Yale: Look at the World Champion! He seems a little too happy with this thing so far. That guy is one evil son of a bitch. But he is the best!

Bryan: And now it looks as if Savige and Aiden are going to finally take RipeFist out of this match! Savige had better watch out, though, we all know what a good ally Aiden makes…

Aiden and Savige head for the ramp with the fans still booing mightily. Together they hoist RipeFist to his feet, but RipeFist suddenly explodes with energy. Nailing Aiden with a back elbow to the face, RipeFist begins pummeling Savige with incredible right hands! RipeFist backs Savige onto the stage as the fans erupt with cheers, fully in control of the big man! Aiden charges with a clothesline from behind, but RipeFist ducks it and Aiden clobbers Savige! Aiden spins again only to catch a boot to the gut and a massive DDT in the dirt!

Bryan: Whoa! Where the hell did this come from?! RipeFist is taking over!

Yale: What!? There’s… no way!

The fans are screaming now as RipeFist pulls Aiden up and drives him face-first into the side of one of the small dozers. Aiden bounces back with a right hand to RipeFist’s bandaged face, but The Fist shrugs it off and nails Aiden with a huge standing sidekick! Aiden slumps into the dirt as RipeFist turns again, only to be lifted off the ground by Savige and driven straight down with a brutal spinebuster!

Bryan: Oh God! Just like that, Mike Savige has derailed RipeFist’s comeback attempt! And Savige is in control now!

Yale: Yeah, but he needs to act quick. We’ve seen how quickly things can change in this match. Savige needs to bury one of these guys quick!

RipeFist lies motionless in the dirt as Savige plucks Aiden back up. Leading him across the graves of Chrissy-Poo and Bryan Mayhem, Savige pulls Aiden to the last open grave on the right side of the graveyard and doubles him over. Savige stands at the edge of the grave, holding Aiden prone for a powerbomb!

Yale: Here we go! Mike Savige is going to bury Aiden Jackson right here and now!

Bryan: Good God, if he does that he’ll kill him! He’s going to powerbomb Aiden Jackson into the grave!

Savige suddenly finds himself airborne as Aiden rears back! Aiden back drops Savige through the air and all the way down into the grave! The fans cry out in shock, booing Aiden hard, as he drops to his knees and begins to crawl towards the dozer behind the massive pile of dirt!

Bryan: Aiden just countered! Savige is in the grave, and Aiden’s about to bury another one! And these fans are booing the hell out of him!

Yale: I won’t say he doesn’t deserve it, but this guy is just doing what it takes to win this match! All this talk about Rei Tiu being the next big thing, what about Aiden Jackson? That’s twice now he’s gotten the better of Rei! Why aren’t we talking about Aiden!

Bryan: We will be if he manages to bury Savige alive here!

Aiden reaches the dozer, but Savige is already on the move. Knowing where he is, Savige immediately pulls himself out of the grave and begins crawling up the mound of dirt above it. Aiden just takes a seat in the dozer as Savige rises above him. Before Aiden can react, Savige reaches down and hooks him in a front facelock, promptly powering him out of his seat and down onto the dirt with a massive suplex!

Bryan: Wow, what an astonishing display of power there by Savige! He managed to pull himself together, and he just overwhelmed Aiden Jackson there!

Yale: Savige has Aiden on the ropes here! But can he put him away?! Only two graves left, JB! And at least one of these guys is going in there!

Savige pulls Aiden up and sets him up for the powerbomb again. This time he powers Aiden straight up onto his shoulders and thrusts him straight down into the grave! But Aiden somehow hangs on and pulls himself back onto Savige’s shoulders! Aiden begins throwing furious right hands into the top of The Machine’s skull, staggering the big man!

Yale: Wait, Aiden’s fighting back! I don’t believe it, Aiden’s fighting back!

Bryan: Savige is in trouble here!

Savige plays his last card, shoving Aiden forward off his shoulders. Before Aiden hits the ground, Savige catches him under the arm and drives him straight down into the grave!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! Savige hit the Mean Machine right into the grave!

Yale: Wow! Savige’s modified Rock Bottom just destroyed Aiden Jackson! But they’re both down in that grave now!

Both men lie in a heap inside the grave, and somehow they both begin to move together! The fans cry out in astonishment as the camera peers down into the grave, watching both struggle to their feet.

Bryan: God, how is Aiden moving after that? For that matter, how is Savige moving? These guys refuse to die!

Savige, his face suddenly furious, boots Aiden in the gut and underhooks him! The fans explode as Savige pulls Aiden off his feet and drives him straight into the dirt with a massive Machinehead! The arena comes unglued as Savige begins crawling out of the grave, leaving Aiden motionless and unconscious below.

Yale: MACHINEHEAD! Aiden’s out, JB! Wow!

Bryan: Mike Savige might have just finished off Aiden Jackson for good! He’s heading for the bulldozer!

Savige quickly crawls into the dozer and guns it forward. After a moment’s hesitation, the pile gives way and crumbles down on top of Aiden! The grave fills to the top amid the explosion of the crowd, and Mike Savige collapses onto the grave of Aiden Jackson!

Bryan: THERE IT IS! AIDEN JACKSON HAS JUST BEEN BURIED ALIVE!

Yale: I don’t believe it, JB! Savige just buried my pick! Damnit! And now we’re down to just two! Mike Savige and… RipeFist.

Bryan: And I don’t like RipeFist’s odds in this situation! Savige might just be the next number one contender to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship!

Savige slowly rises, managing a thin wry smile amid his exhaustion. The fans greet him with a mixed but massive reaction, and Savige just stand there soaking it in.

Bryan: And listen to these people! They recognize that tonight Savige has become one of the most dominant men in the business! Aiden Jackson just wouldn’t go away there at the end, he hung tough and gave a world-class showing in this match, but it was the power and the unrelenting force of Mike “The Machine” Savige that won out in the end! Incredible!

Yale: And now all he has to do is bury RipeFist! And that should be no problem for Savige!

Savige slowly struts towards the still motionless RipeFist. But RipeFist suddenly nips up to his feet, much to the crowd’s surprise! Savige has no time to react as RipeFist suddenly begins tearing into him again!

Bryan: WHAT?! RipeFist is back up and has the big man reeling! What the hell is going on here?!

Yale: This is amazing, JB! Where is RipeFist getting this from!?

Savige swings a massive arm at RipeFist, but RipeFist suddenly wrenches him down into a brutal reverse armbar! The fans suddenly explode with cheers, recognizing a familiar move!

Bryan: Counter! Armbar by RipeFist! And… good God, that’s…

Yale: The Reverse Fujiwara Armbar, JB! He’s going to rip Mike’s arm out of its socket!

Savige lies screaming in the dirt as RipeFist pulls back with precision. Knight, suddenly fascinated, sits forward in his seat, leaning close and watching with wide eyes. RipeFist finally releases Savige and stands amid the frenzy in the Joe Louis Arena… and turns his gaze up to LeStatt.

Bryan: What’s going on here, Dave?!

Yale: I have no idea, but RipeFist just let Savige go and is staring at the Champ!

Suddenly, the MegaTron goes black. The fans begin to buzz as the word “RipeFist” appears in bright red letters on the MegaTron… and RipeFist begins to untie his wrappings!

Bryan: Oh My God! RipeFist is unmasking!

Yale: Eew, I don’t want to see that hideous face! What’s this idiot doing, JB!?

Knight looks up at a monitor with awe as the letters in “RipeFist” begin to move. The fans quiet and wait as the letters rearrange… then suddenly EXPLODE with shock! The arena come absolutely unglued as even LeStatt Knight’s jaw drops at the sight of the new word on the MegaTron.

S-P-I-T-F-I-R-E

Bryan: OH…MY…GOD!!!!

Yale: IMPOSSIBLE!

With that, RipeFist removes the last of his bandages and spins around to face the crowd. The MegaTron lights up with a close-up shot of his face… the face of Michael “Spitfire” Stevens!!!!!

Bryan: It’s HIM! I DON’T BELIEVE IT!

Yale: MICHAEL STEVENS?! RipeFist… is… Spitfire! I can’t believe I didn’t see it all along, JB!

The arena continues cheering wildly, screaming out with joy to see Stevens standing on stage! Stevens looks up at Knight, whose face is blank with disbelief.

Bryan: The champion doesn’t know what to think! Knight is in total shock here! And so am I!

Yale: Me, too, JB! This is amazing! Michael Stevens was RipeFist all along?! But why? And how?!

Suddenly, Knight’s lips begin to move. They slowly twist into an unmistakable smile, suddenly beaming with glee. Stevens simply smiles back as Knight stands, moving to the edge of his throne. Standing above Stevens, Knight points to Savige and utters two words that boom throughout the arena.

Knight: DESTROY HIM!

The fans buzz with confusion as Stevens turns and looks down at Savige. Immediately he springs into action, apparently obeying the order!

Bryan: Good God, Knight just told Stevens to destroy The Machine!

Yale: It’s not fair! Stevens has spent the whole damn match lying around with Savige has been burying people alive! This is totally unfair, JB!

Bryan: And Knight looks somehow pleased to see Spitfire! I… I just don’t know what to say about this!

Stevens pulls Savige up and begins peppering him with stiff right hands. The fans cheer along as Stevens backs Savige towards the final empty grave on the left side of the graveyard. Savige feels the grave coming and suddenly begins firing back, taking over on Stevens! But Stevens lowers his head and tosses Savige back into the dirt with a belly to belly suplex! The crowd erupts again as Stevens stands quickly and goes right back to work.

Bryan: Wow! Stevens just suplexed Savige, and he’s got the big man on the ropes! I can’t believe what we’re seeing!

Yale: Stevens can’t win this! There’s no way, JB!

Savige somehow picks himself up out of the dirt and grabs Stevens by the leg, tripping him down. Savige stands and promptly slingshots Stevens back and into the MegaTron façade. Stevens bounces off the steel and Savige immediately drops him with a vile clothesline!

Bryan: Oh, God, Savige just silenced this crowd with a massive clothesline, and he’s not ready to quit!

Yale: Savige seems like he’s the only guy in this arena not surprised to see Stevens! He’s just accepted it and is continuing to fight for his life!

Savige slowly picks himself up, his face and body covered in dirt. He hoists Stevens to his feet and leads him to the grave, eliciting a nervous cry of protest from the fans. Savige powers Stevens onto his shoulders and then into the air, holding him prone with a huge guerilla press! He tosses Stevens down into the grave, but Stevens changes direction in mid-air! Grabbing Savige around the head he pulls him down with him, driving Savige into the grave with a huge Inferno DDT!!!

Bryan: OH MY GOD! WHAT A COUNTER!

Yale: Savige is dead! He’s gotta be!

Bryan: Michael “Spitfire” Stevens just DDTed Mike Savige straight to hell! I don’t believe it!

The fans come undone as Stevens emerges from the grave, pulling himself out. Getting to his feet, Stevens circles to the dozer and gives one last glance to the watchful champion. With that, Stevens pushes the dozer forward, dropping the massive pile of dirt into the grave and covering Savige! The fans explode as the bell sounds, and Stevens crosses to the center of the graveyard.

Bryan: HE’S DONE IT! STEVENS JUST BURIED SAVIGE ALIVE!

“I Want to Conquer The World” cues up for the first time in a long time, and the fans respond with their biggest explosion of the night. Stevens surveys the filled graveyard around him, then turns his gaze up to Knight.

Andrews: Here is your winner, and the NEW Number One Contender to the GCW World Heavyweight Championship. . . Michael “Spitfire” Stevens!!!!

Bryan: Absolutely amazing! He did it, Dave! Michael Stevens came in and survived this hellacious match! And he’s going to get one more shot at the champion!

Yale: This isn’t fair! It was supposed to be RipeFist! But RipeFist was Stevens! I must be blind!

Bryan: Michael Stevens has shocked the world and the champion tonight! Incredible!

Stevens and Knight lock eyes, and Knight slowly begins to applaud with respect. Stevens shoots him back a contemptible grin, eyeing him hard.

Bryan: This is simply unbelievable. Michael Stevens has unmasked, and he’s won this match! Bryan Mayhem, Chrissy-Poo, Rei Tiu, Aiden Jackson and Mike Savige all buried alive! Those men put forth an amazing effort, but it was Michael Stevens who outsmarted everyone and has come through with a win! And we’ll see him face LeStatt Knight again!

Yale: It’s not fair! After all that, Stevens STOLE this one! But I guess in the end he’ll get what he deserves! Because we’ve seen that Michael Stevens cannot beat LeStatt Knight! He’s just going to get destroyed again!

Bryan: God, this has turned into one of the bloodiest, most intense rivalries in professional wrestling today! Michael Stevens is coming back for more! And this crowd loves it!

Stevens turns away from Knight to pose for the shocked and screaming crowd. Cameras flash wildly as the former RipeFist stands tall over the graves of his opponents, standing as the challenger to the GCW World Championship once again.

Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, we’re out of time from Devil’s Night! For David Yale, I’m James Bryan, thank you for joining us! Now it’s on to NC-17, where Michael Stevens will challenge LeStatt Knight… ONE MORE TIME!

The GCW logo appears on the bottom of the screen as Stevens again looks up at Knight. The two hold the stare for a long time, until Stevens slowly raises a finger at Knight, shouting five spite-filled words: I’ll see YOU at NC-17!!!!
