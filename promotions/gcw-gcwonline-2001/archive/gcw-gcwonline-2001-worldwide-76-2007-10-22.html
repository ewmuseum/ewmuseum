WorldWide 76 New York City, Madison Square Garden ---- Introduction Bryan: We’re live at historic Madison Square Garden in the heart of New York City, and a packed house in on hand to witness the aftermath of the biggest event of 2007! Another amazing Dangerous Games has come and gone, and tonight we’ll celebrate the 2007 winner, Vivica J. Valentine! Bryan: Down goes Octavian, but White took his eye off of Knight for a split second, and that momentary lapse in concentration is all Knight needs! LeStatt is so focused, so driven right now, I’m not sure I’d want to be in the ring with him at all. Yale: Yeah, we saw Jay Terror take exception to LeStatt eliminating him at Dangerous Games, and Terror got right in Knight’s face about it tonight. If I were Terror I’d just consider myself honored to have been in the ring with LeStatt and keep my mouth shut! Knight takes White by the hair and pulls him out to the ropes. Knight whips White, but White drops to the mat and slides to a halt. He quickly springs up as Knight charges again, but White connects with a high knee to the face! Knight goes down and White backs into the ropes, springing himself forward to drive a fierce leg over Knight’s neck. The crowd roars as White pulls Knight up and delivers a belly to back suplex. White stands and finally notices the crowd going wild over his every move. The moment seems to stun White, who looks out at the vast crowd in confusion. He finally steps over to Lia and tags her in, then steps to the apron quietly. Bryan: There’s the tag! Here comes the World Champion! Yale: Is it just me or does White seem a little intimidated by this crowd, JB? Bryan: Their boos at Dangerous Games certainly made it hard for him to get started, but he’s definitely not used to being cheered! The way this whole night has transpires, Jordan White’s world has been turned upside down. Knight slowly gets back to his feet and retreats to his corner, staring down Lia. Lia grins and cracks her knuckles, beckoning Knight forward. But Knight simply smiles and shakes his head, turning and tagging in Octavian. Bryan: Well, we had a chance to get another look at Ambrosi and Knight, and we all know there’s incredible history there dating back to last year’s NC-17 where Lia pinned Knight, but Knight isn’t going to engage her just yet! Yale: Make no mistake about it, there’s no fear there, JB. Knight wants a piece of Lia! But he’s going to toy with her a bit, and we’re going to get our first look at Octavian in two years! Slowly climbing through the ropes, Octavian keeps his eyes fixed on Lia. He briefly casts a glance out at Caldera, who shouts a few instructions his way. Octavian nods and quickly returns his stare to the World Champion. Yale: See, JB? That’s why Octavian is a three time World Champion right there! He’s a phenomenal athlete, but he knows how to accept help when it’s offered! If Jordan had just listened to Caldera, he might be World Champion right now! Bryan: I agree with you that he’s an amazing athlete, Dave, and we shouldn’t take a thing away from his skills in the ring. He really is one of the best of all time. But White has made it clear that he’s not going to be selling his soul any time soon, and he’s content to know that he lost on his own terms. Octavian and Lia circle the ring a few times before tying up mid ring. Octavian immediately takes over with an arm drag, which he turns into a standing armbar. Lia quickly adjusts and powers back to her feet, but Octavian grabs her by the hair and yanks her back to the canvas, still holding onto the armbar as the crowd expresses its feelings. Bryan: Well, that was some straight forward offense there from Octavian, and Dave, despite the fact that Octavian is one of the premier technicians in the world, his mean streak is well documented. Yale: This guy may seem calm, cool and collected, but somewhere underneath all of that is the Ragin’ Roman, and he’s every bit as intense and brutal in the ring as his partner is! Bryan: I just can’t get over the team in the ring right now, it’s the two greatest stars of the two eras of GCW! But Jordan White and Lia Ambrosi are closing in on cementing their own status as all-time greats, and they might deserve to be in the same conversation! Yale: Maybe some day, but Jordan White couldn’t get the big win, and Lia Ambrosi has never beaten either of these men in a one on one situation! Again Lia manages to get back to her feet, but Octavian powers her back into the corner. He drives a hard knee into Lia’s midsection and then whips her across the ring, where Lia hits hard. Octavian follows her in with a clothesline, but Lia ducks out of the way and Octavian runs sternum first into the top pad. Lia drops to the mat and snaps him down with a small package from behind… …1! …2! …Save from LeStatt! Knight drills Lia in the back of the head with a straight stomp, breaking the pin. Bryan: Whoa, Lia damn near pinned Octavian there! But LeStatt was paying close attention and got there in time to make the save. Yale: You really think there’s any way Lia and White win this match? This is the big debut of the new-look Establishment, JB, and there’s no way Lia and White will be able to get along long enough to pull of any kind of miracle. Bryan: I don’t know, Dave, I’d think any team with the World Champion and the former number one contender on it would be the favorite over anyone! Octavian quickly takes over on a stunned Lia, lowering his shoulder and lifting her off the canvas. He steps back towards his corner and drives her into the mat with a hard spinebuster, then reaches up and tags in LeStatt. Knight smiles as he climbs through the ropes and immediately scoops Lia off the canvas. He brings her to the side and whips her across the ring, but Lia hits the ropes and holds on to stop her progress. She shakes a finger at Knight and then turns to White. White and Lia exchange an awkward stare, before White finally accepts a tag. Bryan: And now it’s Lia denying Knight the chance to face her! Those two are clearly playing mind games with one another, and I think that’s destined to be a stalemate. Yale: You can see how uncomfortable both Lia and White are in this situation, JB… Bryan: Maybe so, but it’s clear from their conversation earlier that they’re both willing to put their differences aside, forget their highly competitive history and come together on the common thread they now share. White steps through the ropes and recklessly charges Knight. Knight charges too and they meet with a double shoulder tackle. Knight wins the exchange, sending White rebounding off and into the ropes. But White quickly recovers and uses the momentum to fly back with a spinning heel kick that takes Knight to the mat. Knight quickly hops back up but White delivers a leaping forearm to the face, sending Knight staggering into the neutral corner. Bryan: And you can see Jordan White is still sticking with his careless, wild style that he chose to utilize at Dangerous Games, and so far it’s paying off! Yale: It might help him in this sort of situation, JB, but with the big prize on the line you should listen to President Caldera, every single time! The camera flashes to Caldera on the outside, shaking his head disapprovingly. White looks out at him and spits on the mat, and Caldera frowns, eyes ablaze. White meets Knight in the corner and drives a few heavy forearms into Knight’s head before picking him up and lifting him onto the top turnbuckle. White begins climbing in front of him as the crowd rises from its seats. White leaps, lands on Knight’s shoulders and drops back, executing a perfect hurricanrana! Knight flies down onto his back and White leaps out for the cover! …1! …2! …KICKOUT! Knight escapes to the crowd’s chagrin, and White flashes a glance over his shoulder at Ambrosi. Lia offers a half-hearted hand, but White ignores the offer and goes back to work. Bryan: GREAT move by Jordan White that time, and he damn near had Knight pinned! Yale: Knight escaped, and White didn’t look too interested in tagging in Lia there. Bryan: Things have been uneasy but civil between Ambrosi and White, and you know, despite the highly competitive situation those two have been in for the last few months, there wasn’t a lot of personal animosity there. Yale: That’s what you think, JB. Just because nobody started making "Yo Momma" jokes doesn’t mean it wasn’t personal. There’s nothing more personal than the World Title, and you can bet your bottom dollar Jordan White still wants what Lia Ambrosi has, Establishment or no Establishment. White pulls Knight back up, but Knight grabs hold of White’s belt and drops to the mat, dragging White forward. White flies towards the mat, hitting his head hard on the middle turnbuckle. The blow stops him long enough for Knight to reach up and make a tag, letting Octavian back into the ring. Octavian immediately pulls White up from behind and hoists him into the air, drilling him with a huge German suplex, bridging into a pin! …1! …2! …KICKOUT! Lia has stepped halfway into the ring, but a save is not needed as White kicks out. Bryan: Good Lord, what a devastating suplex from GCW’s only three-time World Champion! Yale: He looks like he’s in the best shape of his life, JB, and he wants to get back to the top of GCW more than anything! Right now Jordan White is going to suffer because of it! Octavian pulls White off the mat and hooks him in a front facelock. He lifts White off the mat with a high vertical suplex, holding him in the air with impressive strength. Octavian turns and shouts a few words at Lia before dropping back, delivering the big suplex. Caldera applauds fondly from the outside as the crowd boos mightily. Bryan: Wow, amazing power there from Octavian, and it’s The Establishment in firm control here! This crowd is doing its best to get Jordan White back into the match… and what a strange turn of event this is indeed! Yale: I still don’t understand White’s decision to leave The Establishment, JB. Bryan: Hey, it seems pretty clear Caldera was about to kick him out, anyway. White has decided he’s only going to do things on his own terms! Yale: But he should have been a good employee and taken the boot like a man. For robbing Caldera of the pleasure of kicking him out, he’s going to have to suffer this beating at the hands of his replacement, a man who has PROVEN he can win the big one… three times! Octavian pulls White back up and White tries to throw a few punches, but Octavian stops him with a knee to the gut that he follows with a snapmare. Octavian drops and applies a rear chinlock, and in response the crowd begins clapping in unison in an attempt to rally White. Ambrosi shrugs and begins clapping along, perhaps half sarcastically. Bryan: White’s in trouble here, as Octavian’s got him grounded, and we saw first hand that, for all his amazing talents in that ring, the mat is definitely Jordan White’s Achilles heel! Yale: And he’s in there with one of the all-time best, JB! And if you want to see what President Caldera wanted for Jordan White, look no further than Octavian! Here’s a man who started out small and allowed Caldera to teach him! As a result Octavian became one of the best technicians of all time, just like Caldera, and he went on to win World Championships, just like Caldera! Bryan: Right! Just like Caldera! Octavian’s cemented himself as a Hall of Famer and Jordan White wants the same, but he wants to be his own man and I applaud him for that! Come on, kid, win this thing! The rallying fans seem to inspire White, who begins to work his way off the canvas. Octavian keeps the hold locked in even as White powers to his feet, and then fires a vicious back elbow. The blow catches Octavian right on the orbital bone, immediately stunning him. White breaks free and charges, bouncing off the ropes with tremendous momentum. But Octavian uses his momentum and pulls him into a towering guerilla press! Bryan: White escaped, but Octavian’s got him! What power! White suddenly squirms free again, this time hooking Octavian’s head on the way down. The crowd explodes as White delivers an amazing counter DDT, and both men lie motionless on the canvas, gasping for air. Bryan: WHAT A MOVE! White just delivered a huge DDT out of nowhere, and Octavian is out! Yale: That’s where he’s dangerous! White isn’t worth a damn on the canvas, but as long as things are moving he can strike quickly! Bryan: And now he’s got a chance to make that tag, and I think he’s a bit more willing this time! Suddenly enthused, Lia reaches into the ring, eyeing Knight across the way. Knight does the same, but Octavian makes no movement. White slowly begins clawing his way across the ring as the fans cheer him on, and Lia waves him forward. Bryan: White’s moving! He’s inching closer, and Octavian hasn’t budged! That DDT took him right out! Lia’s going to come in and finish this match off! Yale: This is a disaster! Come on, Octy, do something! Caldera pounds on the apron in an attempt to rally Octavian, but he still shows no signs of moving. White nears his corner, and Knight suddenly springs into action, Giving up his hopes of a tag, he climbs into the ring and charges, grabbing White by the heel and pulling him away from Lia. The crowd boos hard as Knight drops White in the middle of the ring, but stands to find Lia charging! Lia delivers a massive clothesline that sends them both flying over the top rope, where they land right on top of Caldera! The crowd roars as all three sprawl to the canvas. Yale: HAHA! Brilliant move from Knight! Bryan: Knight saw it was hopeless and prevented White from making the tag! But Ambrosi finally decided to get herself some of LeStatt Knight! Slowly pulling himself up in the ropes, White absorbs the chaos surrounding the ring. Octavian finally begins to recover, and White stands at the ready, measuring him patiently. Bryan: This crowd is going wild! White’s measuring Octavian for the superkick! Yale: Watch out! Someone do something! Octavian gets to his feet and spins around, only to find White flying forward. White delivers a huge superkick, but Octavian ducks and White flies right by. But White never quite recovers, finding another boot flying right at his face. A massive superkick connects under the chin of White, sending him flying back to the mat. An astonished chorus of boos explodes from the crowd, as Octavian stands and smiles at the man across the ring. Yale: JB! I don’t believe it! Bryan: RICH ROLLINS! MY GOD, THE RENEGADE IS BACK! Rollins stands in the ring, basking in the vicious boos being thrown his way. Octavian drops and hooks White’s leg, but referee Josh Briggs looks reluctant to count. Rollins has a few words for Briggs that seem to convince him, and Briggs drops for the count. Bryan: God, Rollins superkicked the head right off of Jordan White, and Octavian’s got the cover! …1! …2! …3!!!!!! "Established" hits overhead amid the fierce anger of the fans. On the outside, Ambrosi and Knight are still trading blows, neither seemingly aware of the action in the ring. Knight rolls into the ring and Ambrosi gives chase, failing to notice Rollins and Octavian until it’s too late. She stands and gets immediately mauled by both men, who drive her back into the corner. Knight stands and together they all begin stomping Lia into the canvas. Caldera recovers and climbs in behind them, thoughtfully nudging White out of the ring. Bryan: It’s over! This match is over! And now Knight and Octavian… and by God, Rich Rollins are beating the hell out of the World Champion! Yale: This is amazing, JB! Are we really seeing this?! Bryan: This CANNOT be happening! Rich Rollins, and all he stands for… he would NEVER join The Establishment! Can this really be happening!? Knight and Octavian hold Lia up and pull her to the center of the ring. Rollins steps in front of her and blows her a kiss, then raises his boot and delivers another brutal superkick. Lia slumps to the canvas, leaving the four men in the ring exchanging glances. Bryan: God, another superkick! I can’t believe my eyes! And where is Valentine in all this?! Her friend just got her face kicked in! A sickened groan wells up in Madison Square Garden as Steven Caldera, Octavian, LeStatt Knight and Rich Rollins come together in the middle of the ring for a big group hug. They then begin exchanging high-fives as "Established" continues to play. On the side, Rollins and Caldera come face to face, and after a pause they heartily shake hands, earning another pop of boos. Bryan: I don’t believe this! Rich Rollins and Steven Caldera are shaking hands! How the HELL are these two on the same page?! Yale: I don’t know, but President Caldera told us earlier tonight that The Establishment is only going to get better! Do you realize what this means?! Caldera no longer has the top two champions in GCW history… he’s got the top three! Bryan: This is disgusting! Rich Rollins has just returned! I thought the son of a bitch retired after White beat him at Tsunami! Yale: You can’t kill the Renegade, JB! He’s back and he’s joined The Establishment! WOW! The boos continue to grow in intensity as WorldWide begins to fade to a close. Caldera, Octavian, Knight and Rollins join hands and raise them into the air, standing triumphantly over the broken body of the World Champion. Bryan: Ladies and gentlemen, we’re out of time from Madison Square Garden! For David Yale, I’m James Bryan… The Renegade has returned, and he’s sold his SOUL! As WorldWide comes to a close, President Caldera walks to the corner and approaches the camera. Just as the show fades out, Caldera has a few parting words that just make the air: Caldera: ……And it only gets better from here.
