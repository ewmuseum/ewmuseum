Oblivion, a 2001 edition (page ob7)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

(Debonair by Dope hits, and a dark image appears on the screen.  It slowly zooms in, and a flare of light illuminates the image.  The man is Khan, and the camera quickly switches to screen shots of Khan’s past battles.  The music continues playing as we remember Khan beating the Jhub for the US title in the first ACWF match, Khan destroying three other men to retain his title at the last CWU event, watched as he battle Rollins and Octavian in the Iron Man Match, and fought the Jhub in the ACWF rematch.  The camera retreats once again to an image of Khan, and slowly backs away as Debonair fades out for the last time. . .)

 

(The Oblivion cinema hits with its music, and the Sacramento crowd goes crazy.  Blue and red pyro lines the stage, and red fire explodes from the turnbuckles.  The camera rests on the announce team as the crowd continues its frenzy.)

 

Stone-  Welcome everyone to another edition of Oblivion, and things really couldn’t be and bigger at this point!  The drama continues with the CWU growing in numbers by the week!  I’m the Rattlesnake Jake Stone, alongside Sean the Mastermind Morgan for what should be a great show!

 

Morgan-  Well, what I really want to know is what Mr. Danger’s big surprise?  He’s been alluding to it all this week, and was watching from the ramp at the end of Meltdown. 

 

Stone-  Well, speculation has been running rampant this week as to what his announcement will be.  I think that tonight he will take his side on who he will support in this battle!

 

Morgan-  Well, we’ll find out later tonight, but for now, we have word that there’s something going on out back!

 

(The camera switches to the parking lot area.  Several cars are circled around each other, leaving a small opening in the center.  Inside this ring of cars stands Kurt Richards, who is holding a steel chain.  Kevin Jacobs appears and tries to get a word with him.)

 

Jacobs-  Blockbuster?  Blockbuster. . . What is going on out here?

 

Richards-  I just found out that Caldera turned down my rematch request for Meltdown.  I wanted Octavian again next week, but now I’ve been told I have to wait until Heatwave to get him in the ring again.  Well, if I can’t fight him in the ring, I want him out here tonight!

 

(The crowd cheers in anticipation.)

 

Jacobs-  I don’t know that the Commissioner will even be here tonight, Kurt.

 

Richards-  He’ll be here.  And so will I.

 

(The camera returns to ringside where Awake by Godsmack hits.  Icebabe heads for the ring to some tremendous boos.)

 

Andrews-  The following contest is set for one fall.  Introducing first. . . Icebabe!

 

(Icebabe steps into the ring, and grabs a microphone.)

 

Icebabe-  Now, I guess you all are aware that next week on Meltdown there’s a little battle royal for the Women’s title.  Well, I plan on walking out of there with that belt. . . But I’m afraid Emma wont be walking at all!

 

(The MegaTron then lights up to a scene backstage.  Emma lies on the ground, being attended to by officials.  The crowd boos as they see this.)

 

Icebabe-  Yes, I’m afraid Emma met with a little accident a few minutes ago.  So I guess that means. . . I win by forfeit!

(The crowd boos, as Icebabe turns around, taunting the crowd.  Suddenly, the boos turn to cheers as Gidget, Bridget, and Katanya rush the ring.  All three ladies begin to pound away at Icebabe, but they are followed closely by the referees who quickly break up the fight.)

 

Stone-  Wow!  Icebabe apparently attacked Emma backstage, but that didn’t sit too well with the rest of these ladies!  This Icebabe is out of control!

 

Morgan-  Come on, we don’t know that it was Icebabe!  Don’t jump to conclusions!  Icebabe is tough, though, and she’s going to be the women’s champion by next Wednesday!

 

Rivero vs. Impact is next.  It is a great match, even between the two.  Impact goes for the Impaction, but Rivero counters with a low blow.  Rivero climbs to the top, and goes for the Rivero Elbow.  But Impact rolls out of the way, and gets the pin afterwards.  After the match, LeStatt Knight, Raziel, and Stone run out and beat down on Impact.  WINNER-  Impact

 

(Camera comes back from commercial.  You see the backstage area.  Eric “All That” Allen is cutting a promo with Kevin Jacobs.) 

Jacobs: “Eric, in just a few moments you will face John Reed, what are your thoughts?” 

Allen: “Well Jacobs my thoughts are simply-“ 

(Allen is cut off as Toxic accidentally bumps into him; he is wearing a GCW shirt.  The crowd cheers.) 

Allen: “Watch where you’re going.  Just another GCW nobody.” 

(Toxic turns around and looks at Allen.) 

Toxic: “You better watch your mouth you piece of trash.  I ruled the CWU long before you were there.  And if you don’t watch yourself I’ll rule your ass.” 

(The crowd cheers.  As Toxic begins to walk away Allen shoves him into Jacobs and runs away.  Toxic turns around and stares angrily as Allen takes off.  Camera cuts to commercial.)

 (The show returns from commercial.)

 Stone: “Here we go.  It’s a battle between the GCW and CWU.

 Andrews: “The following contest is scheduled for one fall.”

 (Break Stuff hits.  The crowd goes nuts as John Reed enters the stage area.  He walks down to the ring with Rachel Reed.)

 Andrews: “Entering first from Chicago, Illinois.  Being accompanied to the ring by Rachel Reed.  Representing the GCW.  He weighs in at 246 pounds.  JOHN REEEEED!!!

 (Reed steps through the ropes and pulls on the ropes as All That hits.  The crowd boos louder than Allen’s music.)

 Andrews: “And his opponent.  Representing the CWU.  Weighing in at 235 pounds.  ERIC “ALL THAT” ALLEN!!!”

 (Allen steps through the ring glancing over his shoulder momentarily.  Reed jumps him.)

 Stone: “Reed jumps Allen and has knocked him on the ground and is stomping away on him!”

 David: “Reed looks pissed.”

Stone: “Reed sends Allen off the ropes and delivers a devastating spinebuster.  Reed is on fire early.”

 David: “Reed mounts Allen and is pummeling him with rights and lefts.  Reed picks him up and takes him up and over with a vertical suplex.”

 Stone: “Reed is pounding away on Allen again here. The ref is pulling Reed off Allen.  Reed is arguing with the ref and Allen sneaks up from behind and rolls Reed up.  He could have him here!”

 ONE! TWO! TH-

 David: “Reed kicked out just in time.  Both men are up and Reed rushes and takes down Allen with clothesline.  Reed is choking Allen now.  Reed has lost it.  The ref is pulling Reed off of him and Reed and the ref are arguing again.”

 Stone: “But here comes Allen.  Allen just pushed Reed into the ref and the ref is down.  But Reed is back to his feet.  Allen runs towards Reed but Reed catches him…STUN GUN!  STUN GUN!”

 David: “Reed has him here.”

 Stone: “Reed hooks Allen’s leg.  There’s no ref!  This should be over.  Reed has gone over to revive the ref.”

 David: “Allen is staggering to his feet.  He walks over to Reed and spins him around.  Right hand from Allen.  Right hand from Reed.  They’re brawling all over the place here.  Reed is getting the upper hand.  Allen is staggering.”

 (Suddenly Fat Lip hits.  The crowd goes crazy as Toxic sprints to the ring with a chair and wearing a GCW shirt.)

 Stone: “TOXIC IS HERE!  Toxic has come for Allen!  He’s got a chair!  Toxic slides in the ring.  Allen with a right hand to Reed sending him back a few steps back.  Toxic spins Allen around and whinds up…WAIT A MINUTE!!!”

 WHAM!

 Stone: “TOXIC JUST NAILED REED IN THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR!!!  WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE?!!!”

 David: “Toxic picks up Reed on his shoulders…TOXIC WASTE!”

 Stone: “Toxic pulls the ref over and Allen makes the cover.”

 ONE! TWO! THREE!

DING! DING! DING!

 Andrews: “Here is your winner…ERIC “ALL THAT” ALLLLLENNN!”

 (Toxic raises Allen’s hand.)

 Stone: “This can’t be.  I still don’t understand.”

 (Toxic grabs his GCW shirt and rips it open revealing a CWU shirt underneath.  The crowd boos and starts to litter the ring.  Toxic throws the GCW shirt down and spits on it.)

 David: “Toxic has just spit in the face of the GCW.  Now look at this.  Toxic is rubbing that saliva-covered shirt in Reed’s face.”

 (Toxic and Allen turn to walk away.  Rachel Reed climbs in the ring and tries to help her husband.  Toxic turns around and spots her and starts walking towards, as does Allen.)

 Stone: “Now c’mon.  She didn’t do anything.  And Toxic just grabbed Rachel Reed by her hair.  Allen has a chair.  Hold on a second.  Toxic has Rachel on his shoulders and Allen just slid that chair underneath Toxic.  DON’T DO THIS!  TOXIC WASTE ONTO THE CHAIR!!!  DAMMIT!  SHE COULD BE PARALYZED.”

 (Fat Lip plays. Toxic and Allen walk away as EMTs rush to help a motionless Rachel Reed.  They load her onto a stretcher.  Fade to commercial.)

Mayhem vs. Danger is next.  Lots of speculation as to what Danger’s surprise is.  Mayhem heads for the ring, followed by Danger.  The bell rings, and Danger walks right into a small package by Mayhem, who gets the three count for a fast win!  Danger is furious.  I guess we won’t be seeing Danger’s surprise tonight!  WINNER-  Tristain Mayhem

 

(The show returns from commercial, and goes out back again to where Richards still stands waiting.)

 

Stone-  Welcome back, fans, and out back, Kurt Richards is still waiting for Octavian to arrive!  The show’s almost over, I don’t think he’s coming!

 

(Mike Hardy passes by the camera, and Richards spies him.)

 

Richards-  Hey, Hickenbottom!!

 

Hardy-  What do you want?

 

Richards-  You seen Octavian around anywhere?

 

Hardy-  Maybe, maybe not.  Look, I don’t want to be involved in this, Kurt.

 

(With that, Richards leaps over the circle of cars, and assaults Hardy.  Hardy fights back bravely, and a brawl ensues.)

 

Morgan-  Wait a minute, a fight has broken out back there!  Richards wanted a fight so bad, he had to pick one with Hardy!  Hardy wasn’t doing anything!

 

Stone-  Richards and Hardy are really going at it here!  The GCW officials are out there to break things up, but Hardy wants more of Richards!  The fight finally gets broken up, as Richards walks back to the cars!

 

Morgan-  But wait, look who’s there!  Octavian is waiting right behind Richards!  Richards sees him, but Octavian takes him down with a punch to the face with Blockbuster’s chain!

 

Stone-  Octavian is wailing away at Richards, with that chain wrapped around his fist!  Richards has been beaten down, as the officials run in to pull Octavian off of Richards!  They pull the commissioner away. . . But looks who’s back!

 

Morgan-  There’s Hardy again!  Hardy climbs onto the hood of a car, and looks down at Richards!  Hardy dives. . . Swanton Bomb!  Hardy hits a cheap shot on the fallen ambushed Blockbuster!

 

Stone-  Hardy may have hurt himself more than anything there, but he proved a point!  Hardy and Octavian have formed an unlikely alliance here for a moment to take out Richards!

 

The camera returns to ringside, where Welcome to the Jungle hits, and the crowd begins to cheer.  Trash emerges and heads for the ring, waving to the fans as he goes.)

 

Andrews-  The following contest is set for one fall, and it is for the GCW United States Championship!  Introducing first, from San Jose, California, he weighs in at 265 pounds. . . Trash!

 

Morgan-  What a brawl that was!  Octavian will be getting his on Meltdown, though!  So I guess it’s back to ringside, as Trash heads for the ring!

 

Stone-  Well, I for one am wondering where Trash stands on the whole CWU situation.  I mean, he initially came in with the CWU, so it stands to assume that he would go back!

 

(Trash hops into the ring and awaits the Jhub.  My Own Summer hits, and the Jhub emerges to a huge mixed reaction.  He struts to the ring, carrying his belt over his shoulder.)

 

Andrews-  And his opponent, from Stamford, Connecticut, he weighs in at 270 pounds, and he is the GCW United States Champion. . . The Jhub!

 

Stone-  And, of course, the Jhub has been everywhere and done everything. . . Now with this odd alliance with Mike Hardy he has, we’ve got to wonder where he stands as well!

 

Morgan-  Well, it sounds like the fans are confused as well!  So the Jhub steps into the ring, and hands his belt to the referee!  The Jhub and Trash meet in the center of the ring, and start trash talking!

 

Stone-  This should be an incredible matchup!  There’s the bell, and there they go!  Trash fires off a quick right, and it’s returned by the Jhub!  Back and forth they go, and we have a fistfight!  It looks like Trash is getting the best of it!

 

Morgan-  Trash backs the Jhub into the ropes with those stiff right hands, and sends him to the opposite side!  The Jhub bounces back, and Trash levels him with a shoulder block!

 

Stone-  The Jhub gets right back to his feet, and Trash gets a head of steam off the ropes!  Trash runs into the Jhub, but neither man goes down this time!  Trash bounces off the ropes again, and hits another shoulder block!

 

Morgan-  But neither man budges!  Trash bounces off the ropes again, and meets with the Jhub in the center of the ring!  But the Jhub catches Trash off-guard with a back body drop!

 

Stone-  Trash goes flying, and lands right on his back!  The Jhub turns around, and immediately slaps Trash in a triangle arm lock!  Trash screams out in pain, and looks for the ropes!

 

Morgan-  Trash flails his legs out, and finally gets to the ropes!  The Jhub keeps the hold on. . . But breaks it at the referee’s insistence!  The Jhub gets back to his feet, as Trash tries to recover!

 

Stone-  The Jhub seems to be in firm control here, as Trash gets to his feet!  The Jhub moves back in, but Trash rises with a quick knee to the gut!  The Jhub is doubled over, as Trash hooks his head, and hits a big DDT!

 

Morgan-  Both men are down, as Trash recovers from the Jhub’s early onslaught, and the Jhub shakes the cobwebs out from that DDT!  Now-  Wait a minute, what’s this?!

 

(The crowd begins to boo as Mr. Danger heads for the ring.)

 

Stone-  What is Mr. Danger doing back out here?  He’s just mad that he lost so damn fast!  He shouldn’t be out here ruining this main event!

 

Morgan-  He’s up on the apron, now, but he’s been stopped by the referee!  Trash and the Jhub are both slowly getting to their feet, not seeing Danger!

 

Stone-  The Jhub gets up and charges Trash, but Trash ducks it!  He hooks the Jhub from behind and runs. . . Trash Compactor!  Big move by Trash!  We could have a new US champion here!

 

Morgan-  But Danger still has the referee tied up!  Trash has the cover, but. . . Wait, who’s this?!  Two men just jumped the railing, and are climbing into the ring!

 

Stone-  Is that who I think it is?!  That’s Big Daddy Dero!  And the Mangler!  They were part of the old ACWF!!!  Mangler gets to the top rope, and takes off!  Big elbow drop to the back of Trash’s skull!

 

Morgan-  Trash is hurting after that move, as Dero steps over the top rope!  Dero lifts Trash up, and bends him down!  Oh, no!  Dero picks Trash way up. . . Derobomb!  Big move from the big man!  Dero and Mangler have destroyed Trash!  But why?!

 

Stone-  Mangler drags the Jhub on top of Trash, as Danger hops down from the apron!  The ref turns. . . ONE!  TWO!  THREE!!  The Jhub retains!

 

(The bell rings and the crowd boos in confusion.  My Own Summer plays again, as Trash rolls out of the ring in pain.)

 

Andrews-  Here is your winner, and STILL GCW United States Champion. . . The Jhub!

 

Stone-  What the hell is this?

 

Morgan-  I’ll tell you what it is!  It’s Danger’s big surprise!  It’s the ACWF in GCW!!!

 

Stone-  And the Jhub is in on this!?  Why?!  Danger, Dero, and Mangler are retreating up the ramp, leaving the Jhub with his belt in the ring!  Fans, we’re out of time.  Is the Jhub with the ACWF or not?!  Join us on Meltdown for more!!!  Goodnight!

 

(The show ends as Danger and his ACWF back up the ramp, leaving Trash in ruins.)
