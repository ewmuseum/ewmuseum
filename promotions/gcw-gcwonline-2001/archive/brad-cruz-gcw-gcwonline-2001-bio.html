Brad Cruz Biography Real Name Bradley Hegan Cruz Nicknames "The SoCal Prophet" (Main), "The Golden Cruzsader", and "Silver Screen Seraphim" Hometown Hollywood, California Birthday July 27, 1984 Height 6'0 Weight 227 Fed Titles GCW Hardcore Champion, POW SoCal Champion of North American, & MOW:RTW Tag Team Champion Biography Bradley Hegan Cruz, The beginning… Brad Cruz is ego maniacal wrestling star, quite capable on the microphone and in the ring. Cruz is known for his trademark mouth, a mouth that has gotten him into trouble along with amassing plenty of fame and infamy. He is a bit of a joker and seldom takes his opponents seriously outside of the ring, but in the ring he is quite stiff and takes what he does quite seriously. Known for his high flying daredevil theatrics, a strong technical base, and a complete and total faith in his abilities that will not waver, makes Brad Cruz quite the formidable opponent. Born July 27, 1984 in Garden Grove, California Brad Cruz had a happy and productive childhood. His mother was a former stage and minor Television Actress… while his father was a high school and later college wrestling coach. Both parents had a heavy hand in steering young Brad’s path into Drama and Wrestling while he was attending Garden Grove High School and later USC for College, but from a very young age Brad had a passion for professional wrestling. To Bradley it was the perfect mesh of athletic competition and pageantry, a paring that his parents forced on him in completely separate pieces. In just a few months into his college education he began training with “The Raging Aztec” Sergio Chavez and Canadian hero “The Unstoppable” Jimmy Dibbo. Chavez trained Cruz teaching him the art of aerial and submission combat while Dibbo gave him perspective and strong fundamentals. One year later while sporting long blue tights and shoulder length brown hair Cruz made his debut on the indies for the “Sports Entertainment Alliance” ran by his trainers Dibbo and Chavez. He squared off in singles competition against a young upstart from Blackpool, England who went by the name DeToX. Brad would score the victory with a bridging tiger suplex. MOW:RTW, Madmen of Wrestling: Rock The World After a year of scratching his way through the indies Cruz was signed to a contract within the dynasty of MOW promotions. Specifically he was signed to their third tier promotion RTW, Rock the World. He began wrestling under the name “The Natural Disaster” Hurricane. He made his debut with two other new RTW superstars, Thanatose and Renegade in a triple threat match. Cruz would pick up the win hitting the “Hurricane Driver” (screwdriver) on Renegade for the pinfall, a move he would later lose to Thanatose who also favored the screwdriver. These matches marked the beginning of a long line of feuds to come with RTW’s best which included: Thanatose, Renegade, Hawaiian Bryan/Bishop, Apex, Gambit, Hanover Fists and many others. Brad was also a founding member of Fatal Fury (stable) and later a member of XL (stable). Bradley earned his first championship in RTW becoming the RTW Tag Team Champion with his partner Renegade. Cruz would stay with the promotion until it dismantled. Brad made a brief return to the indies and later began touring overseas in Europe and Japan while finishing school in the fields of Theology & Religious Studies and Drama. The Natural Disaster Formerly Known as the Hurricane, POW (Powers of Wrestling) Part 1 Awhile after RTW’s demise Cruz signed with POW, Powers of Wrestling. He drastically changed his look when he buzzed and bleached his hair and adopted a somewhat less then admirable attitude in the ring. He even went as far using his real name dubbing himself “The Natural Disaster Formerly Known as the Hurricane” Brad Cruz. he quickly went from a fan favorite in MOW:RTW to a cocky Villain in POW. This run was short lived due to his attitude backstage. The SoCal Prophet, POW Part 2 Brad made his return now as “The SoCal Prophet” Brad Cruz at the expense of POW’s Chet Blackstone by hounding and insulting him for weeks on end until defeating him in a singles match. After his ordeal with Chet, Brad began interjecting himself into other people’s segments, which was the case with Reese. Bradley unprovoked, verbally assaulted Reese on several occasions until physically attacking him on an episode of Chronicles aligning himself with Heartless whom Reese had a deep personal hatred. This carried into a match at Final Glory where Brad was taken out by his own partner AKA. The feud continued into a match at No Limits in a singles match against Reese, Brad would lose by submission via "Spinalsnap" (Liontamer), but Brad would score a win in a rematch the very next show at Chronicles 76 with a Tiger Suplex, thus ending their feud. POW SoCal Championship of North America Part 3 Due to the buzz coming off of Brad’s feud with Reese, Cruz was viewed as a top contender for the North American title which was held by World and North American Champion Sebastian Roach. The two stars (who had gotten along in the past) suddenly turned on each other with a brutal exchange of words leading into to their match at Chronicles 77. The match was a technical feast with roach pulling away close to securing win, but due to the interference by POW superstar Vision it left Cruz a opportunity to hit a low blow and the then finisher “Spectrum Wind aka Cruzifix Slam” (Olympic Slam) for the win. This win ended the longest title reign in the history of POW and crowned Brad Cruz with the POW North American Championship Brad’s first singles title, but in typical fashion it wasn’t good enough for Cruz and he re-dubbed the title the “POW SoCal Championship of North America”… a moniker that would only be used until losing the belt at Evolution Reborn to “Big Daddy” Hector Mercado via frogsplash. Notable Feuds in POW included: Hanover Fists, Darkangel w/ Judge, Chet Blackstone, Shockwave, Michael Reese, AKA, Brod, Sebastian Roach, Hector Mercado, Nate Wiley, and Toga. Other Feds MOW:RTW, Madmen of Wresting & POW, Powers of Wrestling Other Fed Titles GCW Hardcore Champion, POW SoCal Champion of North American, & MOW:RTW Tag Team Champion Strengths Strong for his size, Risk taker , Fast, Aggressive, Dirty, and Technically Sound. Weaknesses Size, Risks don’t always pay off, and Hot Headed. Wrestling Style High Flyer, Techincal, Striker Strategy He favors working the head, neck, and spine while every other body part falls into a wild card category. He has a chip on his shoulder and is constantly trying to one up his opponent with what he views as his superior speed and skill… failing that he’ll start getting ugly simplifying hit move set with stiffer and less fanciful moves with: chops, boots face washes, and elbows ETC. He is no stranger to cheating and will if he feels it’s necessary. He’s always looking for short cuts but very willing to put in some brutal ground work. Finisher Move 1. Cruzifix Slam 2.
