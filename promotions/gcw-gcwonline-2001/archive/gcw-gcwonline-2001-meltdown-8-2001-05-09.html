Meltdown 8, 9 May 2001

9 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown 8 Alerus Center — Grand Forks, North Dakota ---- Meltdown #8 (The lights dim, and the crowd begins to murmur. Suddenly, the stage lights up with a loud explosion, and the crowd goes ballistic. The camera flies over the fans as the show begins.) Bryan- We are just ten days away from Shockwave, and we are just ten days away from the triple threat iron man match! We are just ten days away from Hell in a cell! We are just ten days away from our President and our Commissioner getting it on! We are just ten days away from a new US champion! And just ten days away from a fatal fourway cruiserweight battle! All that and more at Shockwave! Yale- And, as a gift to our champion, we’ll be coming to you live from Rome, Italy! I can’t wait! I love Europe! Octavian is gonna take out Khan and Rollins in his home town! It’ll be great! Bryan- Tonight we’ll see the Renegade and Khan go one on one, in what should be a great battle of the titans! Not to mention the first round of the United States title tournament, and the tag titles to be decided! Yale- And Toad goes up against three other guys in a hardcore fourway! This is a big night, JB! (The camera moves to the parking lot out back. A limo pulls up, and an attendant opens the door. The fans boo as Octavian steps out of the limo, his belt over his shoulder. Toxic follows him out, followed by Chris Jordan and John Reed. President Caldera climbs out last, and they head into the arena.) Yale- The whole Establishment is here tonight! Bryan- Only Toxic is competing tonight! Why are they all here?! Yale- They can do whatever they want! (The camera returns to ringside, as Countryboy Can Survive hits. Mike Byrd heads to the ring to little reaction.) Andrews- The following contest is a fatal fourway match for the GCW Hard*core championship! Introducing first, from Durham, North Carolina, weighing in at 300 pounds. . . ‘The Legend’ Mike Byrd! (Byrd enters the ring, and It’s All About the Flame hits. The Flame heads to the ring, and hops in amid modest cheers.) Andrews- And his opponent, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing in at 279 pounds. . . The Flame! (The crowd’s cheers intensify as Real American hits. Zippy heads for the ring with a big smile on his face.) Andrews- And their opponent, from Mianus, Connecticut, weighing in at 222 pounds. . . Zippy! (Zippy steps into the ring, and he and the Flame begin to chat. Out With the Old hits last, and the crowd jumps in cheers. Toad walks out to the ring, as pyro goes off behind him.) Andrews- And their opponent, from Biloxi, Mississippi, weighing in at 310 pounds, he is the GCW Hard*core champion. . . Toad!! Bryan- Toad steps through the ropes, and there’s the bell! The Flame gets a jump on Toad, and Byrd goes for Zippy! Remember, the first man to score a pinfall wins! Yale- Toad begins to fire back at the Flame, as Zippy plants Byrd with a knife-edge chop! (Born to be Wild hits, and the crowd bursts into cheers. The Renegade runs to the ring and slides in.) Andrews- The following contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 225 pounds. . . Rich ‘The Renegade’ Rollins!! (The Renegade tosses his bandana to the crowd, as Debonair hits. More cheers follow, as Khan walks into the now dimmed arena. He slowly gets to the ring, removes his robe, and climbs in.) Andrews- And his opponent, from Hokkaido, Japan, weighing in at 278 pounds. . . Khan! Bryan- These two men met a few months ago in a different company, and Khan suffered one of his few defeats at the hands of the Renegade! Yale- He’ll be looking for redemption here tonight! Bryan- There’s the bell, and we’re underway! Khan and Rollins circle the ring, before meeting in the center! Khan has the size advantage, and takes over! He backs Rollins into the corner, and plants a kick to his midsection! Yale- And another! Khan pounds Rollins with those martial arts kicks, before backing up! He charges, jumps onto Rollins, and flips over! Tiger-Wall flip! He catches Rollins in the face, and Rollins staggers out of the corner! Bryan- Khan gets back up, and charges in! But Rollins catches him with a back elbow! Khan is stunned, and Rollins takes him down with a quick clothesline! Rollins goes for a quick cover! Yale- ONE! No, only one! That’s not gonna get the job done, you idiot! Rollins lifts Khan up and sends him into the ropes! Khan bounces back, and Rollins catches him right in the face with a perfect dropkick! Bryan- Khan is staggered back, and he falls through the ropes to the floor! Rollins heads for the turnbuckle, and he’s climbing as Khan gets to his feet! Khan turns to Rollins, who takes off! Yale- WOW! Cross body from the top from Rollins! The aerial attack from the Renegade is taking Khan off guard! The air is normally Khan’s territory! Bryan- Good strategy by Rollins! The Renegade gets back up and tosses Khan into the ring! Khan gets to his feet, as Rollins mounts the turnbuckle again! Khan turns, as Rollins jumps! Yale- But Khan meets him in midair with a dropkick! Rollins goes down, and he may have the wind knocked out of him! Khan recovers, and pins him! Bryan- ONE! TWO!! No! Rollins gets a shoulder up, and now Khan helps him to his feet! Khan sends Rollins into the ropes, and the Renegade bounces off! He comes back, and Khan jumps, and takes him over with a hurricanrana! Yale- Great move! Rollins rolls all the way out to the floor, as Khan gets a head of steam off the ropes! The Renegade gets back to his feet, as Khan leaps to then top rope, and springboards off! Bryan- INCREDIBLE suicide dive from Khan! He leapt ten feet into the air and landed on Rollins! The Renegade is down, and Khan is slowly getting back into the ring! Yale- The referee has begun the ten count on Rollins, but the Renegade is slowly getting to his feet! Khan is taking a breather in the far corner, as Rollins pulls himself into the ring! Bryan- Khan moves back in with a swift leg drop that catches Rollins in the back of the head! Khan flips Rollins over and covers! ONE! TWO!! The Renegade kicks out! Yale- Khan doesn’t hesitate in heading back to his favorite spot on the top! Rollins slowly pulls himself up, as Khan gets ready to take flight! But the Renegade throws himself into the ropes, and Khan loses his balance! Bryan- Khan lands sitting on the turnbuckle, and that’s gotta hurt! Rollins slowly heads for the corner, and he climbs up in front of the fallen Khan! Rollins hooks Khan up, and tries to superplex him! But Khan holds on! Yale- Khan blocks it, and now he’s attempting a suplex! But Rollins is hanging on! Finally Rollins fires a few right hands, and lifts Khan up! SUPERPLEX! Rollins got it, and both men are down! Bryan- Rollins slowly tosses an arm over Khan’s chest! ONE! TWO!! THR- Wow! Khan barely gets a shoulder up! Both men are really hurting, but the Renegade has things going his way! Yale- Rollins gets back to his feet, and bounces off the ropes! He comes back with a quick, hard elbow drop to Khan’s chest! Rollins spins over and mounts Khan, hammering away with a flurry of punches! Bryan- Rollins is in command, as he pounds away at Khan’s skull! Finally he gets back up, and Khan gets to his knees! Rollins backs into the corner, as Khan gets up! The Renegade charges! Yale- Rollins moves in, but Khan ducks and lifts him up! Samoan drop! Beautifully executed by Khan, and Rollins is hurting! Khan covers! ONE! TWO!! THR- Rollins kicks out! Bryan- This has been a very give-and-take match here! I don’t know who the better man is! The fans are loving every minute of this! And, wait, Khan nips up! A quick recovery by Khan has the crowd going crazy! Yale- Khan flips to his feet and heads for the ropes! Khan climbs the turnbuckle, as Rollins gets to his feet! Khan takes off with a moonsault! He connects with Rollins, they both go down, and Khan lands with a pinning position! Bryan- ONE! TWO!! THRE-NO! Nearfall by Khan, and how did Rollins kick out of that? Yale- I have no idea, but look who’s coming down to the ring! (The crowd boos loudly as Octavian struts down the ramp with a steel chair.) Bryan- Damn it, what the hell is he doing down here! Octavian has no business in this match! Yale- He’s the champ, JB, everything’s his business! Khan has spotted him, and he’s turned to face him! Rollins is still hurt from that moonsault, but he’s pulling himself up with the ropes! Bryan- Octavian and Khan are exchanging words here, as Khan backs up to beckon the champ into the ring! And Octavian is sliding in with his chair! Yale- Octavian charges Khan with the chair, but Khan ducks! Octavian follows through, and clobbers the rising Renegade! Rollins goes down, as Octavian turns to Khan! Octavian lays Khan out with a chair shot! Bryan- There’s the bell, and this great contest has been ruined by our arrogant champion! Octavian moves back to Rollins and hits him one more time! There’s another shot for Khan! Yale- He’s proving that he’s the one true iron man, JB! And. . . Wait, here comes the Jhub! And he’s being followed by Mr. Danger! (The crowd goes crazy as Mr. Danger and the Jhub hit the ring. Danger stays on the outside, as the Jhub catches Octavian from behind.) Bryan- The Jhub is assaulting the champion! The Jhub is assaulting Octavian! Octavian is in trouble, as the commissioner watches from the outside! Yale- The Jhub is laying waste to the champion, but. . . Yes, here comes the cavalry! John Reed, Toxic, and Chris Jordan are on their way, followed by the President! Bryan- Jordan and Reed assault the commissioner, as Toxic slides into the ring and pummels the Jhub! Reed and Jordan climb in, and the Establishment is laying waste to Khan, the Jhub, and the Renegade! Yale- The President has Mr. Danger, and he’s sent him into the steel steps! Caldera lifts Danger up and rolls him into the ring to join the beating! Bryan- I’m getting damn tired of this sight! I’ve seen enough of the Establishment’s muggings! Yale- This is the way it should be, JB! The Establishment reigns supreme again! And- (Yale’s voice is drowned out by cheers. The Establishment looks around to see what’s going on, and someone runs in from the crowd and begins attacking Reed and Toxic.) Bryan- Wait, WHO THE HELL IS THAT?! Is that who I think it is?! Yale- That’s 8-Ball! What in the hell is he doing here?! I thought he was the CWU champion! Why is he here?! Bryan- 8-Ball is assaulting the Establishment! Rights and lefts, and everyone is going down! Now Khan is up! So is Rollins and Jhub! The Establishment is getting assaulted! Yale- This isn’t fair, JB! 8-Ball doesn’t belong here! The Establishment is in real trouble! Caldera has bailed out of the ring, and is watching his troops get decimated by the men in the ring! Bryan- 8-Ball is here to help in the fight! Finally the odds are even! Ladies and gentlemen, we are out of time! Join us next week for Meltdown, and then it’s on to Shockwave on Pay-Per-View!! (The show ends as the crowd cheers on 8-Ball, the Jhub, Khan, Rollins, and Danger as they assault the Establishment.)
