WorldWide 92 Bradley Center — Milwaukee, Wisconsin ---- Introduction The lights dim in the arena and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Milwaukee fans go wild!
