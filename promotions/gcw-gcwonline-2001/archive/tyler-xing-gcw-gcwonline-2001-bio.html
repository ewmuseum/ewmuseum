Tyler Xing Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames Nightwater Hometown Beijing, China Birthday 19/08/1980 Height 6'2 Weight 223 Fed Titles - Fed Awards - Biography One word describes 'Nightwater''s life in the present day - 'honour'. After a troubled past, including entanglements in the Chinese Mafia and the loss of his father, Tyler Xing lost his vision, and his concept of life evaporated. Drawn into a petty life of crime, he was rescued by the teachings of a master of wushu shi fu, a source of Chinese Martial Arts, and taught a new way of life - based on the three principles of Chin Woo: Wisdon, Philosophy and Courage. He is now a student of many of the Wushu forms, and has found a pinnacle of spirituality in its teachings. He is disciplined through the rigour of his teachings, and enlightened by the wisdom of the great masters. He comes now into the world of wrestling to widen his philosophy and spread the three great principles of Chin Woo. His adopted Wushu name is 'Nightwater', coined for his subtleness, like the moving shadows of the sky-water after the red dragon gives in to the white spirit of the night. =========================================================== Born in 1980 to an English Mother and Chinese Father, Tyler was educated in the west from the age of 4, but brought up on the principles of Chinese culture. The general elements of health, discipline and principled dedication were instilled at his father's behest, and when not in school, Tyler spent most of his days at his father's house on the outskirts of Beijing. At the age of 18, Tyler was sent to America to do a degree in Economics and International Relations, at the request of the Chinese Government. Tyler did well at his university studies, gaining a worthy qualification, and returning to China expertly trained in subjects that would push the boundaries of Chinese academia. However, upon returning to China, Tyler had been so disappointed with life in America, he wanted to distance himself from the continuing westernisation of China, and followed in the footsteps of his uncle and grandfather, both who had been prominent figures in Wushu, the Chinese Martial Arts. His father, though disappointed in his son's choices, put him in touch with his uncle's master, Han Sho Phat, who had adopted the name 'Thin Ice' for his astoundingly short temper, and cold disposition as well as his solid grasp of the principles of Wushu in all their forms. At 21, Tyler entered into the training at the Wushu Shi fe temple in North West China. Based at the foot of the mountain of dragons, Tyler trained under the disciplined tutelage of his master, learning not only the physical excellence promoted by Chin Woo, but also the mental precision and discipline instilled by the 10 concepts: Man; Character; Carriage; Words and Deeds; Trustworthiness; Punctuality; Justice; Service; Welfare; and Fraternity. Though having been tought these concepts, and the three principles of Wisdon, Philosophy and Courage by his father, Tyler Xing found new meaning in the Chin Woo criteria under his master. He became an adept pupil, and his master decided to send him to the United States of America to extend his training into more practical pursuits. Tyler now comes to the GCW looking to put into practice the teachings of his master, spread the ways of Wushu and Chin Woo, and to extend his mental and physical conditioning. He looks to be embraced by a culture that he tried to get away from only five years before, and the acceptance of a people he has felt at odds with his whole life...
