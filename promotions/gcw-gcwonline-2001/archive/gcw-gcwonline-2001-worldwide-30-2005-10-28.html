WorldWide 30 Budweiser Events Center — Loveland, Colorado ---- Introduction Normally at this time the lights in the arena would dim, music would play, and signs would be shown. People would be screaming at the top of their lungs as cameras panned the crowds, and fireworks went off. James Bryan and David Yale would be welcoming you to another edition of WorldWide, and people would be ready to see their favorite GCW stars, coming to the ring to battle each other in modern day gladitorial contests. But, this wasn't a normal WorldWide. The camera was focused on three men, standing in the middle of the ring. To the left stood a smaller, masked gentleman, just a tick shorter than the man to his far left, wearing a mask of Steven Caldera. In the center, stood the bane of all GCW stands for, Dan Rivero. A microphone in hand, he apparently has made good on his word to return this week. Outside the ring stand ten security personnel, waiting for a cue from the back to storm the ring, and take these interlopers back from whence they came. Rivero: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Worldwide! A few fans cheer, but, most of them boo, as clearly, they don't like Rivero. Then again, who does?
