Darek Machida Biography Real Name Darek Machida Nicknames Dee, Macho Hometown Detroit, MI Birthday Feb 22 1992 Height 5'8 Weight 185lbs. Biography Darek Machida is a half Mexican, half Japanese American who was born in Detroit, MI. His father left him when he was born and his mother was unable to take care of him so he was forced into foster care as a young boy. Growing up in the Detroit area was tough and being a foster child made things even more harsh for the young kid. IN the early years he tried hard to do well in school but because of his asian descent and small frame, he was picked on alot. Darek would come back from school day after day with black eyes, bloody noses and even the occassional broken bone. His grades began to drop as things got worse and at the age of 12 he began to skip school all together. Everything came to a head one day during his freshmen year in High School when he was being made fun of during his lunch period. Three kids that he had been dealing with since Junior High School were at it again, throwing every Asian and Mexican racial slur they could at him until he snapped. BANG-BANG-BANG! He only broke the nose of the closest kid to him and yes the other two got in a few good licks on him before school security came and broke it up but it didn't matter, Darek felt great. Even though the school suspended him. After that day Darek didn't hesitate to stand up for himself, fighting anyone who'd give him any trouble. This brought alot of respect his way which also brought the wrong kinds of friends. Darek would join up with a gang out of Detroit that he refuses to name for obvious reasons. He stole, beat people up at first but that quickly escalated to armed robbery and drug dealing. This lasted for a little over a year until the day came when he was shot by a rival gang member, almost dying in the process. It was at this time that a man who worked at a local gym, who supported the foster system took an interest. Jesse Gonzalez, 41, married father of two. He read about Darek's shooting in the paper and made a call to the hospital where he was staying to arrange a visit. Of course Darek didn't want to hear what Jesse had to say in the beginning, he was almost 15 and had lived on his own since he could remember. No one was going to help him ... or so he thought. Jesse offered Darek a free membership to his gym and a means of releasing alot of his anger. Fighting. Jesse agree'd to train Darek in boxing, martial arts and wrestling, all things Jesse himself learned at a very young age. Long story short, Darek agree'd after two more sessions with Jesse and began working with him as soon as he was able to. The rest? As they say, is history. Darek moved in with Jesse and his family. He enjoyed it, being cared for but something was still off to him. Like it wasn't HIS life he was living SO once Darek hit 17 years old he decided to get emancipated and went out to do his own thing. Which had led him here. 17 years old, angry, full of promise but little focus. Strengths Quick, Great stamina, toughness is off the charts, high pain tolerance. Weaknesses Small, Well rounded but not great at any one thing, can't match strength with many wrestlers. Wrestling Style Well rounded but likes to brawl if he can.
