The Official Rampage Prediction Thread

11 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Card: Match 1: Seymour Almasy and a Partner to be Named vs. The Guild Team Baked and the Guild faced off last week in a match that was decided by a haiku! The Guild moved on, and will now face Almasy and his partner at Rampage for the Tag Gold! Can the Guild roll 20's? Or will Almasy's Active Time Battle System reign supreme? Match 2: Kraig Carter vs. Jason Warr vs. Bryan Mayhem In a match for the #1 Contendership of the TV title, GCW vet Bryan Mayhem takes on newcomers Kraig Carter and Jason Warr. Will Carter and Warr actually show up? Will Mayhem finally get his revenge on Chris Kannon, after being cost the TV title before Christmas Chaos? Match 3: Shadow vs. Rich Rollins The first Rampage tournament semi-finalists square off against each other in the third match of the night. Shadow barely pulled off the near-upset by the underdog Jayce Bradley while Rich Rollins had more than a handful with Virgil Ambrosi. Can Shadow do what she couldn't at Super Series and put Rollins away? Will Rollins continue his reign as PTC's best with a victory over Shadow? Match 4: The Foundation vs UNITE vs. Futureshock vs. Team Baked Relative newcomers to GCW, three of the teams are looking to make a name for themselves by being named #1 contenders to the GCW tag titles. Team Baked is just looking for a way to keep themselves in the sticky icky purple kine budz. Match 5: Dawn McFarlane vs. Seymour Almasy Almasy could make GCW history by wrestling 3 matches in one night. Dawn McFarlane will do anything to make sure that doesn't happen. Both had narrow escapes on Worldwide with McFarlane eeking out the W over US Champion Vivica J Valentine, and Almasy stopping former GCW World Champion LeStatt Knight. Do either have enough left in the tank? Will Almasy be able to go the distance? Match 6: Scott Taylor vs. Kimbusa A match that's been brewing for years, Scott Taylor hates Kimbusa. Likewise, Kimbusa is not fond of Taylor. Taylor feels robbed after losing his OSW Championship hopes, thanks to Great Dragon and Kimbusa in particular. This will be a war! Main Event: Winner of Shadow/Rollins vs. Winner of McFarlane/Almasy This is it. It's for all the marbles. Michael Stevens is out, and GCW needs a champion. Who will step up and take the gold? Can Shadow or McFarlane be the first female in GCW history to be World Champion? Will Almasy win yet another title? Can Rollins unify the GCW Championship and the Global Title*? *No, not actually. Figuratively.
