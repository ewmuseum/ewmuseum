TyDezno Biography Real Name [name withheld] Nicknames TyDezno, The Pearl, Paragon of Perfection, MARK of PERFECTION Hometown Toronto, Canada Birthday May 25, 1986 Height 6'2 Weight 230 Biography Marked with a jagged past but a hopeful future, TyDezno is here to proved to his adversaries that he has the greatness inside of him to go all the way to the top. With is beautiful fiancee, Jasmine, by his side he CAN accomplished his heart's desire... simply to be the best! He is young, talented and engaged to be married to his High School Sweetheart, Jasmine. Trained for a year at Elite Wrestling Training Ground where he was the eUniversal Champ 2x. TyDezno is a high-risk technical, high flyer who is methodical and strategic in his actions in the ring. Even more so, he is arrogance and smug with a dark past he is trying to get rid of. Often times, he will have flashbacks in the ring with will trigger a rage... taking it out on his opponent. The fans hate him and he hates them. His only ally is his fiancee who can at times keep him at bay.
