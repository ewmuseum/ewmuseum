WorldWide 127 Pepsi Center — Denver, Colorado ---- Introduction The lights dim in the Pepsi Center and the crowd begins to buzz. The GCW logo flashes across the screen, as "Wake Up" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. Finally a heavy guitar riff explodes into the arena, and the Denver fans go wild! Bright blue fireworks explode over the ring as two screaming bolts of light stream down to the stage. The stage erupts with silver pyrotechnics as the cameras begin flying over the frenzied crowd, and the voice of James Bryan chimes in right on cue. Bryan: We're live and a Mile High as WorldWide returns for the first event of the 2010 schedule! Welcome everyone to Denver, Colorado as GCW prepares for the first historic night of a new era in professional wrestling! Hello, everyone, I'm James Bryan, happy to be back on X:TV with David Yale, and Dave, where do we even begin? Yale: So much happened at NC-17, JB, it's tough to cover it all! Do we start with the fact that Andy Murray successfully defended his World Title belt against Vivica J. Valentine? Or LeStatt Knight's win over Clyde Fox? Or the fact that Jay Terror beat Jason O'Neil's ass and proved he's the next big thing? Bryan: I think we have to start with the one thing that affects all others, and that's our new Commissioner! At NC-17, as promised, President Steven Caldera stepped down and departed the tour! He is not here tonight, and if he holds true to his word, we won't see him again. Instead, he's promised that Jay Terror will be next in line, and to ensure that, Caldera gave the Commissionership... I can't believe this... to Garbage Bag Johnny! Yale: I was absolutely shocked when Caldera sent both Shipley and Zenith packing and gave it over to that filthy, no good hobo...and I mean that in the best way possible. I don't think anyone saw this coming, and I for one am anxious about what this means for the future of GCW, but I trust Caldera's choice. Bryan: Well, you can't deny that on his first night in charge of GCW, Garbage Bag Johnny has a huge event in store for us! And it begins with Digital Mortality returning to action against the former MMA star Kris Michaels. Yale: Michaels is still learning the wrestling game, JB, but he's coming on fast. Still, Digital Mortality, the Man in Black himself, is back with a vengeance. This will be a blockbuster opener. Bryan: Up next we'll see the new Television Champion Tempest put her belt on the line in the first of several title matches! PAZUZU, THE DARKNESS won a big three-way match at NC-17 to earn this shot, and Tempest defeated Cheap Labor to capture the title. Yale: Cheap Labor was so humiliated that he up and left the company this week, JB! But PAZUZU represents an altogether different challenge. This guy is about as bizarre as I've seen, and the Sparkly One will have her hands full in this one. Bryan: And the United States Title is also on the line, as Dan Black puts his belt up against an unnamed opponent! Yale: Everyone thought AXEL Action would beat Big D at NC-17, but Black proved them all wrong! Whatever Johnny has in store for him, I'm sure he can handle it. Bryan: There have been rumors about who his opponent will be, but we won't speculate here. I'll just say that Big D had better not get cocky. Yale: Bryan Mayhem is also in action here tonight, JB, but we're not sure what to make of his situation. Mayhem is apparently steaming, JB, because Commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny retired the Tag Team Titles this week on GCW Radio! That means Mayhem, who had to endure a brutal handicap match with Mortifera to win the titles, finds himself empty handed! Bryan: Well, rumor has it Johnny has something in mind for Mayhem, and I sure hope it's good. He does not want to be on Mayhem's bad side, because the big man is returning to championship form. He'll be in the ring with Donny Diamond and Bishop Steele, in what could be an ugly Hardcore Iron Man Match. Yale: Well, speaking of Mortifera, Shaman's liable to be in a bad mood too, JB. He may have lost the tag titles but he stands to lose a lot by seeing those belts retired, too! He'll be going on on one with Tessa Windsor, and hoping to draw blood. Bryan: But is there anyone hotter than Tessa right now? Yale: ...Not when she's wearing that half shirt... Bryan: The match between Windsor and Markus Stone stole the show at NC-17, and everyone's talking about where those two are headed. And with Windsor coming out on top, she may finally be ready to make the big leap to the top. She'll need to get by Shaman to keep the momentum going, though! Yale: It's not going to be easy. Bryan: But we have to talk about our main event. Garbage Bah Johnny has set an absolutely massive headlining match, as Andy Murray puts his title on the line for the second week in a row... and he does it against three-time former champion LeStatt Knight. Yale: Knight beat Clyde Fox in a match where Clyde acquitted himself admirably, I must admit. But Knight looks like he's back in top condition and ready to go at it with Murray once again. Bryan: These two obviously know one another very well, and it was Knight that Murray took the title from over nine months ago. Murray's incredible reign was extended at NC-17, but will it come to an end here tonight at the hands of Knight? Yale: I have to say, JB, this confuses the hell out of me. Isn't this supposed to be Jay Terror? Isn't that why Johnny got this job? Bryan: Terror does still have a title shot in his pocket thanks to his Dangerous Games win, but we'll have to wait and see what the agenda is. For tonight we've got this massive title match on tap, and it should be another classic war. Yale: Murray's been absolutely untouchable during this reign, JB. Knight almost looked like he was going soft... I mean, he actually showed Fox the slightest bit of mercy, which is more than he would have done a year ago. Bryan: So you don't think he's got what it takes? Yale: He's LeStatt Knight, of course he does. I'm just saying that I think the time has come for Jay Terror to become the new Icon of GCW, and Knight should enjoy his moment in what may be a fading spotlight. Bryan: In any event, it's a new year and a new era in Global Championship Wrestling. The Caldera Era is over, and now we're ready to move into an unknown future for the first time! It's an exciting and potentially earthshaking night in GCW, and it begins right now as we kick off WorldWide! ---- Prepare for War The fact that the Megatron's feed doesn't cut backstage tells the people something's about to happen, and then it does. Clutch's unfamiliar grooves rip through the arena as “The Mob Goes Wild” prepares to explode. After a few seconds of single-note riffing the song kicks in, hitting full-flow with a burst of pyrotechnics from the Megatron's edges. And the fans? As soon as an unmistakable Scottish mug appears on the screen there's only one thing they can do. ”RAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!” He's dressed in a black suit - sans tie, jacket open, top buttons undone - and a pair of polished black brogues, with the GCW World Heavyweight Title strapped around his waist and a pair of mirrored aviators resting on his nose. Smarter than usual for The Scottish King of Cool, but not so much that you'd confuse him for Steven Caldera or Christian Zenith. With a huge grin across his face, Murray begins his journey down the ramp, slapping hands with the masses as he goes. Bryan: The soundtrack may be different, but the Champion remains the same! Andy Murray is in the house and the Denver crowd are losing it! Yale: This man exorcised some serious demons at NC-17 just ten days ago, and tonight it looks like there's a smile on Andy Murray's face once again. This moment is his to enjoy, but he'd better not let himself get too caught up in his glory. LeStatt Knight awaits... Bryan: He may have gone through one of the toughest nights of his life at NC-17, but he's got another ahead of him tonight! Andy Murray and LeStatt Knight have waged war across the world, and after Murray's Rampage 2009 title victory over Knight, LK's coming back for vengeance. Yale: Defeating Valentine may have legitimized Andy's position as the longest-reigning GCW Champion of all-time, but he's got a serious struggle on his hands if he wants to keep that reign going. LK is hungry; the hall-of-famer is coming off a huge victory over Clyde Fox, and there's no question he'll do whatever it takes to seal an unprecedented 4th GCW World Title. Leaving his shades with a younger audience member, Murray hops up onto the apron and climbs into the ring. Walking to the opposite side of the ring, he throws an arm in the air and takes a moment to showboat for the fans, before eventually taking the microphone that's handed to him. He stops in the middle of the ring, waiting for the buzz to die down. Murray: Denver, Colorado; how the hell are we doing tonight? The pop is cheap, but the Champ couldn't care less. His grin only broadens as the fans raise the decibel. Yale: Same old Murray, always kissing the fans' asses. Murray: It's been a long three months, hasn't it? Look at all that's happened since Dangerous Games. First, Jay Terror comes back, wins the big one, then has his shot stripped 'cause Daddy Caldera's still got a hard-on for kicking Jason O'Neil's ass. GCW becomes reacquainted with Bryan Mayhem, Tessa Windsor, and later Michael Stevens and Phil Atken. The Tempests of the world have broken into the Television Division as the Oliver Rankens and Killer Bees vanished without a trace. Then Shipley shows up and all of a sudden, Caldera decides he doesn't wanna be the boss no more, but it's not the Englishman that ends up getting the job... The allusion to GBJ draws a mixed reaction from an undecided crowd. Murray: … and Valentine? ”BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” He doesn't need to elaborate. Yale: Listen to these fans, JB! They should be showing some appreciation for the effort Valentine put in at NC-17. She came as close to dethroning The King as anybody else has. Bryan: It was a hard-fought battle, Dave, there's no doubt about it. It'll be a long, long time before these people will consider forgiving Vivica, though. After the past couple of months, she has a lot of sins to atone for. Murray: Believe me, I didn't come down here tonight to brag and gloat. I've no interest in rubbing my victory in anyone's face tonight, because as far as I'm concerned, Vivica J. Valentine is dead. She's shown herself to be no better than those she spent years chastising. The biggest mistake of my career was letting this woman inside my life, and I promise you, GCW, I won't let it happen again. Applause from fans glad to see The King back on his feet begins to ripple around the arena. Murray: At NC-17, I buried my relationship with Vivica J. Valentine once and for all. I know the loss is only going to drive her even closer to insanity, but you know what? I couldn't care less. She and I are finished. Though we may work for the same employer, Vivica is no longer on my radar. She dug her own grave, now she can lie in it. For me, it's onwards and upwards. NC-17 has come and gone; now's the time for new beginnings and fresh starts... but you know what? I'm the longest- reigning World Champion of all-time, and victory tonight will see me equal LeStatt's record of 8 successful defences. Why change a winning formula? A smile draws itself across Murray's face. Murray: Critics will come and go, and I'm not gonna be GCW Champion for the rest of my life, but you know what? As long as you people continue to be gracious enough to let me keep wrestling for you, that's enough. But I can assure you, GCW; anyone that wants to try and take this belt from me is in for the fight of their life. The challengers are only gonna come quicker now that I've reached this stage in my reign, but I'm not gonna give it up without a fight. Bryan: A fight's exactly what he's gonna get tonight, Dave. Yale: Absolutely. After defeating Clyde Fox, there's nothing that LK wants more now than to capture his fourth World Title and make his accomplishments even more unsurpassable. He's hungrier than ever. Murray: It starts with LeStatt Knight, an--... The voice of the Junior Mafia speaks over the PA system forcing a unanimous boo from the crowd around Andy Murray. "Who Shot Ya" by The Notorious B.I.G. plays proving that it isn't LeStatt Knight here to speak his piece -- it's Jay Terror. The Established Heir steps out from behind the curtains with a large bandage strapped across his head and his signature sunglasses over his eyes. Rich Rollins' bandana hangs around his neck and sways side to side as Terror makes his way down the ramp with a microphone in hand. Yale: Jay Terror's in the building ladies and gentleman and I'm sure he's here to stake his claim for the world title! Bryan: I can only imagine. Winning Dangerous Games gave him a title shot at some time in the future and I bet he's wondering why his card wasn't pulled tonight. Yale: Not only that, but Terror and Knight have a past. They've been in their battles and, as it stands, they're tied with one win each. I'm sure that Jay is going to make his case with that somehow involved. Bryan: Whatever he has in mind, I'm sure he won't hold back. He fought a brutal fight all around the arena at NC-17 and came out victorious against Jason O'Neil. It's exactly what Steven Caldera wanted which can only be good for Jay Terror. Yale: It's the best news yet. He's claimed his stake as the best in GCW, retiring another legend, and it would only make sense for him to battle Murray. This is going to be good! Terror walks up the ring stairs and ducks under the top rope. He walks past Andy Murray with a yellow grin on his face and takes his spot across the ring from the Scottish King of Cool. Jay Terror: Shut up, Andy! BOOOOOOOOO!!! Jay Terror: I know you don't make matches around here, but the fact that you're facing LeStatt Knight once again is making me sick. I mean, come on? Knight? I've heard people talking about how you're the greatest champion that GCW has ever seen but all you've done is face people like the suicide bitch and this retired hack. I'm sick of it, Murray. Bryan: He has a point. Murray has defended his title seven times and it seems like LeStatt Knight and Vivica Valentine have been the key takers. Yale: Murray doesn't make his matches. Besides, the Murray-Knight fight was the fight of the year! Jay Terror: Last night I fought Jason O'Neil through sky boxes, the kitchen, hallways, and the ramp until I finally proved what I really am -- the best there is today. I showed that GCW has a face and there's a bandana wrapped around it. I've done my time and made my sacrifices to be where I am now, and I'm repayed how? Again, I'm simply watching you fight some undeserving legend. Andy, I know that we've never seen eye to eye but I think it's time that you used your words and talked to Johnny about a real fight. A fight between you and me. Bryan: The last time they fought Terror lost. Doesn't he remember? Yale: That was before his rise, JB. Terror's ascended to a true star and he's ready to take what's rightfully his -- that title. Bryan: I don't think Murray can make that happen exactly. Terror has a better chance by simply waiting in line like the rest of the roster. Jay Terror: And if you're feeling queezy about making decisions then fine; I'm decalring it now. I'd like to take my name and toss it in this main event tonight. I won the Dangerous Games battle royal for christ's sake, this is as good as an American Dream coupon if you ask me. Now please, Johnny, let's get this new leadership kicked off right with some hard justice! Yale: Garbage Bag Johnny will set things straight! Come on, Johnny! Bryan: I'd be surprised if the new commissioner could walk a straight line. Hard justice responds with slap-pop bass, hammer-ons and pull-offs sending the crowd into a loud mixture of strange reactions. It's still unclear whether to cheer or boo the new commissioner, and it becomes even more unclear when "Garbage Bag Johnny Will Win Zero II Hero" hits and GBJ doesn't appear. Instead, two beautiful women, each one carrying a fichus plant, walk out from backstage. The crowd can get behind this. The women set the plants down on either side of the end of the ramp. Bryan: Will someone turn this entrance theme off? Yale: Your taste in music is worse than Eddie Fowler's. As the crowd expects the arrival of commissioner Garbage Bag Johnny, eyes pointed towards the entrance ramp, a surprise comes from the skies. Veering around from behind the Megatron, the commissioner, clad in a suit not too dissimilar from one of Caldera's, navigates a Martin Jetpack through the air. The Denver crowd can't help but pop as pyros shoot off. Speaking of shooting, Garbage Bag Johnny, high above the arena floor, aims a t-shirt cannon hither and thither and yon, blasting balled up GCW t-shirts to lucky fans. The mixed reaction grows slightly less mixed. Yale: Wait a minute! This is more extravagant than the send-off he gave to Steven Caldera. What's going on here? Bryan: You're asking me? Murray and Terror both look up at the hovering commissioner as he prepares the jetpack for its descent, landing in between the fichus plants at the top of the ramp. An event staff member tosses the commissioner a microphone after he drops his t-shirt cannon carefully to the stage. GBJ: Hello, Denver, Colorado! The crowd gets more receptive. Terror looks on impatiently. Murray can't help but laugh, no matter how hard he tries to hide it. GBJ: First of all, I want to clear the air on a minor issue. I know that when I left GCW the first time, I was a mess, and I did some things I'm not very proud of, and I appreciate those of you who showed some support for me last week at NC-17, but I don't blame the rest of you. You have to break a few eggs to earn enough trust for a man like Stevie C to put you in charge of day-to-day operations of his company, and if I had some kind of magical machine that reconstructed broken eggs, you could bet your bottom dollar that I'd be using it right now. The crowd cheers again. Terror fumes. Yale: I can't believe this! Bryan: I can't believe it either! If this has all been a power play on Garbage Bag Johnny's part, I can't even fathom why he'd want control of GCW, but this is the Garbage Bag Johnny that the fans voted Most Missed Wrestler of 2009. Yale: Somebody call Mr. Caldera right now! Bryan: Good luck with that. Caldera's made himself extremely hard to reach. GBJ: Now, before we get ahead of ourselves, here, I'm not here to pull a fast one on Caldera, and I ain't even here to hold you back, Jay. Yale: He better not be! GBJ: But I know Steve's pretty high up on you. Hell, he made it clear to me that there really ain't anything I can do to you if you don't want to hear me out, but hear me out. Caldera'd wring my neck if he heard me saying this, but deep down, he ain't such a bad guy. He cares about GCW, but he just doesn't always show it. And for some reason, he cares about you, but as much as he wants to see you standing tall with Murray's gold there, he knows that unlike him, you can't put GCW ahead of yourself. He knew that Zenith couldn't do that. He knew that Shipley couldn't do that. But I can, because as much as GCW missed me, I missed GCW. The crowd is sold now. Any doubt over their support for the new commissioner has lifted, and GBJ soaks in the largest pop he's received since his return. Yale: What a suck up! That's for guys like Andy to do. But if that happens--if you do end up on top of the mountain--I'm here to make sure you don't start thinking that you're bigger than GCW. You earned a shot at the strap, man, but you're not getting it tonight! Terror stomps around the ring, and he doesn't even notice as the champ points and laughs at his tantrum. GBJ: If you don't want to cooperate with me, Jay, that's your call, but just so you're aware, I ain't taking directions from you. Not you, not anyone.
