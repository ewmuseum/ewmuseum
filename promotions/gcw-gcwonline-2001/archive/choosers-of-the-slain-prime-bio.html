The pulsating guitar of "Engel" by Rammstein plays over the arena as Choosers Of The Slain make their out of the curtain. To the thunderous ovation of the crowd Vivica J. Valentine and Lia Ambrosi stop just a couple of steps in front of the curtain, wearing matching black and cobalt blue pants and shirt outfits. Posing they stand back to back, looking down towards the ring beore pointing straight out towards the ring simultaneously, setting off twin fire pyros on both sides of the entrance. Wer zu Lebzeit gut auf Erden wird nach dem Tod ein Engel werden den Blick gen Himmel fragst du dann warum man sie nicht sehen kann Andrews: Coming down the aisle from Zdice, Czech Republic and Highland Park, Illinois. Weighing in at a combined weight of 274 pounds... Lia Ambrosi and Vivica J. Valentine... CHOOOOSERS OF THE SLAAAAAIN! The cobalt blue and white lights flash in the darkness to the beat of "Engel" as the duo makes their way down to the ring. Slapping hands with all the fans reaching out on their way down, halfway to the ring both take off in a sprint at the same time and dive under the bottom rope into the ring. After sliding across the mat the two go to opposite turnbuckles and point into the crowd. Lia Ambrosi puts her hands on her hips before doing a backflip off the top rope at the same time as Vivica. Erst wenn die Wolken schlafengehn kann man uns am Himmel sehn wir haben Angst und sind allein Gott wei? ich will kein Engel sein After landing the two begin to discuss stratedgy in the ring as the song fades into the rabid cheers of their adoring fans, getting prepared for the match ahead.
