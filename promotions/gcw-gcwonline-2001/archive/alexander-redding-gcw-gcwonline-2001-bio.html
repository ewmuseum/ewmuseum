Alexander Redding Biography Real Name Alexander Redding Nicknames Red, The Willing Villain Hometown Kitchener, Ontario Birthday October 13, 1984 Height 6'2 Weight 220 lbs Fed Titles United States Champion Biography Sit down with the man over a pint and hear what makes up his life. He wouldn't bother you with the details of birth date, parents, etc. He'd tell you about the man he was at this moment, and maybe touch on the ex-fiancee who dared him to make something of himself. He'd laugh about the nights on the road performing in front of 50 drunks and the pranks he'd play on his friends. You'd nervously sip at your drink as he told you the escapades Grady Patrick had gotten him into, only to smile when he said, 'but I'm alive to tell the tales, ain't I?' Dig up the courage and ask him why he did this for a living and he'll tell you it is all about respect. An autobiography is written by old people reflecting on life. He was too busy to bother reflecting. Other Feds jOlt Strengths Athleticism, Pride Manager Grady Patrick Weaknesses Pride, Temper, Loyalty to Manager Wrestling Style Athletic, Showman Strategy He carries two strategies, the first for competitors larger than him. He will try to keep his distance to start, preferring to land a few leg kicks before launching into an assault of forearm shots. Then come the chop blocks and trips/sweeps. The eventual goal is to weaken the knees/thighs before he feels comfortable working in tighter and pulling off grapples. But he is prideful enough to stray from this strategy when challenged or provoked into trading shots, tests of strength, etc. Secondly, weakened or smaller opponents. He will try to bully and force his will when he can. To him it is a game of pride, and dominance is the way to earn his competitors' respect. For a heel, he still has tendencies to showoff for the crowd, hitting high spots or chained maneuvers in later stages of the match. The finisher is something he could pull off at anytime, but is best saved for the end. He is out to leave a mark on the locker room and the crowd. His pride is something that will see him try to struggle to the ropes to break a submission rather that immediately tap. Wants to think of himself having a resilience like Mick Foley, but it is probably three chair shots to the head less. And then there is Grady Patrick at ringside with him. He will not hesitate to gain the advantage for Alex where necessary. Grady will heckle the ref, trip the competitor with his cane, and sit in with the announce team talking about his favourite subject - himself. Grady will yell at Alex if he is behind, and they are usually backhanded words of encouragement.
