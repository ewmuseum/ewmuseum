Hope Clarkson Biography Real Name Hope Clarkson Nicknames Pain and Pleasure, Blonde Ambition Hometown Kansas City, Mo Birthday June 4,1979 Height 5'6 Weight 135 Biography past: Bio: Hope Clarkson was born on June 4th, 1979. She was born and 'raised' in Kansas City, MO. She was the oldest sibling and the only girl. She had five brothers who were very abusive to her. Hope learned quick growing up that she had to fight to survive the life on the farm. Not just from her brothers, but from her parents as well. She had many fights with them’some resulting in her actual winning, but most times she was not so lucky. Her bones were always getting broken, but she remembers the day she broke her brother Chris’s leg throwing him from the hayloft to get away from him. Hope’s parents didn’t want her when she was born. They wanted all boys. Yet they didn’t want to put her up for adoption, so they decided to torture her and make her life a living hell. An every night ritual before the brothers were born was to beat her with a leather strap with every inch of her life. Hope shivered in her bed crying herself to sleep every night. Once all her brothers were old enough they too got in on the beatings. Every night Hope was beat within an inch of her life out in the barn where no one could hear her screams for help. And there was nights it was all Hope could do to crawl out of the barn. As they beat her, they told her she was nothing, and she’ll never amount to anything. Hope never received love at all during her childhood. She felt the world hated her, and for that she hated the world for hating her. She was in and out of fights all throughout her school years. She fought the boys, the girls, hell even some teachers got a few punches from the hellcat. She was expelled left and right from school, causing even more beatings at home. On her 17th birthday and her senior year of high school she finally confessed her home life to a high school guidance counselor about her home life and why she was so violent. The counselor didn’t believe her. He wanted to put her in a mental hospital. Hope knew she had to get away from this life if she were to ever find love and make a name for herself. That day after school with the clothes on her back and little money in her pocket she ran away. She was in an out of barfights, and it wasn’t until a fight with a trainer on night she was recruited to join an all female wrestling camp for the MFWA (Mid-West Female Wrestling Association) The trainer she fought became her trainer and like a mother to her. Her name was Christine Lockhart. Christine took her under her wing and became her savior. Hope was finally feeling love. Soon Hope was wrestling all pro in the MFWA and even though she was still a bad girl, the fans loved her. She was the kind of person you loved to hate. Everything was going good for Hope?. Until Faith arrived. Faith Von Almen came into the MFWA by Christine almost the same way Hope did. Christine too took her under her wing training her. Jealously soon became of the two women and they were constantly fighting the other. Most times they robbed a title they were holding from the other. However the jealousy came to an end one night. Deep down Hope and Faith shared an attraction to the other, but didn’t want to admit it because they were fighting for Christine’s love and respect. However on July 10th 2000 the jealousy ended and the love for each other blossomed. It was a Women’s World Title Match. The match had a catch though; champion Faith challenged Hope to a Leather Strap Cage Match. Each woman carried a leather strap into the ring to use on her foe. The match started like always with the women taking the fight to the other blooding the other. Faith had changed the pace though trapping Hope in a corner and beating her and beating her. Hope’s mind flashed back to her childhood, of all the beating she had ever received. Hope then crumbled to the mat crying stop daddy, stop mommy’please no more. Faith stopped her beating. Hope was sobbing uncontrollably. Christine on the outside was holding her head. She knew something like this would happen. Faith finally showed love for her beaten rival helping her up and holding her in her arms, trying to calm her. Hope looked into Faith’s eyes, accepted her apology and then kissed her for the first time that night. In fact the two women got so lost in love they began doing it in the ring. Because of that, that match was never aired, and Christine had the task of prying them apart. Something she really didn’t want to do because she finally got them to love the other. Hope and Faith went on to become a dominant team using their built up hatred of the world to win matches. Hope had kept that leather strap that she used in that match, as a symbol to overcoming her fears. Christine helped her with that along with Hope. Now they use it on there foes. Hope takes great pleasure in beating one with a leather strap as opposed to being beat by one. Hope’s title reigns included: 8 Time MFWA Tag Team Champion w/Faith 6 Time MFWA World Champion 4 Time MFWA Hardcore Champion 3 Time MFWA Horizon Champion (like a step down from World) Hope and Faith also loved sex. They loved it so much they produced several lesbian sex DVD’s and have done some private catfight videos for several companies. They’ve appeared on the Jenny Jones show and on Jerry Springer to promote the porn and wrestling fields. On January 1st 2004 they were married and Christine preformed the ceremony. Once Hope’s parents found out about it, they tried to get Hope away from her and out of the MFWA. When security arrived she said she had no idea whom these people were. She told them Christine was her only true mother, and she had no father or brothers. Her parents were placed in jail overnight, and Hope never seen her parents or family ever again. Now they want a bigger challenge, and decided to join Divas Unleashed. Hope and Faith have had there down time in DU as well. After being freed from there slave match clause with Sexy Amanda and the Dark Inferno by Christine and Hope’s Sensei Keiko “The Dragon” Yoshimato. It wasn’t until then that Hope and Faith stopped using hate and started using love. It was then the whole group decided to form The Family in November of 2004. At one time this stable was thought to be one of the dominant stables in all of DU even some saying more powerful than The Occult. However this is one of the few fairytales that have a bad ending. (See Current History) current: bad form came in Kawanaha and Kit, The Kuno Sisters. Upon their presence in DU, The Family crumbled. Kawanaha sat her affections on Keiko and Hope, while Kit sank her talons into Faith. Before DU and anyone knew it, The Family went its separate ways. Keiko first left after being kissed by Kawanaha setting her hidden desires a flame. The Dragon used everything in her power eventually making herself evil like to make Kawanaha like her and sleep with her. However the big shock was with Faith. After Faith getting a small sample of Kit, Faith demanded a divorce from her wife Hope. Since Missouri doesn’t honor gay/lesbian marriages the wedding was never really official. However the emotional trauma on Hope lingered. With her depressive state she was easy pickings for Kawanaha to move in and show her love. Now Hope is fighting with Keiko, her one time teacher, to bed Kawanaha, and battling Faith in court for her share of what they acquired when they were together. One good thing has come out of all of this. Keiko had given complete control of Ariel to Hope. This mostly amounts to the strange bond Hope and Ariel share, that no one can understand. Hope however still fights the good fight, and would help anyone that asked of her help. Yet know single, she wants a Hawaiian Goddess and if need be she will destroy her once friends (with the exception of Christine) to get what she wants. However again, Hope's life has tkaen yet another turn for the worse. Under an assupmption, Kit Kuno, sister to Kawanaha done some research and found out that Chrisitne was actually Hope's mother. Hope found out right before a big match for in the 4WW against Patti Anderson. SInce that match Hope has not been seen. Now she wants to return to take out her aggression. Hope has tagged with her teacher with Keiko Yoshimoto to form Firey Passion, and along with Keiko has taken some training classes by Master Bruce Skylar.
