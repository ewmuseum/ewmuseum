I gave in... so here's the handbook.... [Mainstream Wrestling Federation Handbook Version 3.0] MWF OVERVIEW: I. Titles II. Booking III. Shows IV. Supercards V. Commentators/Referees/Other Personalities, VI.. Creation Form ** I. Titles MWF Mainstream Championship: The big one. The most prestigious title in the federation, to be defended once per month and on every PPV. It's Mainstream and not Heavyweight ... well for one simple fact...any man on the MWF roster can fight for this title. We do not restrict weight classes for big gold. Hell, we only have two belts in the federation.
Midstream Championship: Kinda catchy name for a title that is usually named something like Skyweight, Cruiserweight or Junior Heavyweight title. This title will be defended twice per month and on every PPV. It is also the belt which signifies the number one man in the category of 230 pounds and under.
** II. Booking The way I book is pure and simple. You rp, angle and be active.. and do it well ... you'll win. You stay inactive ... you lose. Simple dimple. And strats? Well, you can write a 56k strat and lose. Cos I am not looking for a writing headache. Something short like an entrance and some spots will be fine. They almost _never_ decide a match.
** III. Shows MWF Manic Mondays: On every couple of weeks, this is the flagship show of the MWF. I don't foresee too many matches a week with a small roster. MWF Flashwire: Simple. Basically the preview show for Manic Mondays with the rps from all you guys.
** IV. Supercards MWF Wrestlekade -- The only supercard currently planned, it will feature an 8-man tournament to crown a MWF Mainstream Champ, and a Four Corners 8-man elimination match for the MWF Midstream Title.
Other ppvs will arise... but not too many. We're a small fed... don't expect more than 4. ** V. Personalities Jack Firestone: Owner/commissioner. Full owner of the MWF, so he basically does whatever he chooses. He's run the MWF and has a smart head for the business.
Jack Cafferty: Veteran play by play man from the MWF days of old. He's been calling matches for almost 2 decades now and is indeed a fan of wrestling and sportsmanship. Jack is in his early 50's and knows the game well.
Leroy Jones: The very verbose color man of the MWF. Leroy brings a savvy, view on things to his match calling and seems to never run out of things to say. At times he can be very annoying and other times, not. Articulate in his own way,
Leroy certainly brings a new dimension to calling a match. Leroy is in his late 30's and is African-American. Ian Maxwell: 6'7", 295 lb. head of security. Ex-wrestler who has been hired to keep the MWF wrestlers, fans, officials in line.
Jim Stone: Referee. The very model of a referee. Very strict in the rules that have been placed before him. And will not back down from any of his decisions even if it means him being physically accosted.
Mike Tracy: Referee. Young gun referee who seems to be rather lenient. Sometimes his leniency can cause problems but most wrestlers know that when he's ref'in a match, they are bound to get away with some things.
Marcia Brown: Buxom yet very assertive interviewer. She's not some dumb bimbo who was hired just for good looks. No, Marcia is a field veteran with the wrestling circuit and MWF especially. She's always asking the hard questions
... and backs down from no one. ** VI.
