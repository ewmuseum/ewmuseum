A federation founded around July 1994 that belonged to the Summit Wrestling Alliance; its Television, Intercontinental, and World Six-Man Tag Team titles all changed hands or were contested on its own broadcasts between 1995 and its last surviving show in March 1996. Nine wrestlers are recorded across three served documents. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Electronic Wrestling Federation was already running by July 1994 and marked its first anniversary with a special e-per-view that same month in 1995, one of the member federations of the Summit Wrestling Alliance. It billed itself plainly in its own recruitment posts as “one of the most consistent (and nasty) Summit feds” and, a month later, as “the SWA's nastiest fed.”

Its house style leaned into parody as readily as into a straight title picture. A March 1996 broadcast staged an entire angle inside a hospital lobby after a battle royal put two members of the Hurley clan — a wrestling family including Gramma Hurley, Tetsuo Hurley, “Nature Boy” Budro Hurley and Hulk Hurley — in the emergency room, played as a soap-opera send-up complete with a doctor fending off Gramma Hurley's advances.

Its World, Intercontinental and World Six-Man Tag Team titles all changed hands or were contested on its own broadcasts across 1995 and into 1996. One angle pitted a five-man team, fronted by the Honor Guard's own Thomas Williams, against a rival stable calling itself Asylum, the feud built on a former champion's own claim that holding gold meant nothing next to being a man the fans actually respected — a pointed dig at the reigning Intercontinental Champion, William the Conqueror, whom other wrestlers had already beaten down in the ring without ever taking his title.

No closing notice survives; its last recovered broadcast ends mid-storyline in March 1996 with no indication the promotion was folding. A departed community member, writing well outside the promotion's own record, still spoke of it as an active fed as late as September 1997, but that mention is not itself the EWF's own attestation of anything past its last held broadcast.

Affiliations

The Summit of E-Wrestling

Member of EWF (1994–96) (at least 30 November 1995 – 14 October 1996, as the four served editions of the alliance's own membership list run)
This EWF, the Electronic Wrestling Federation, appears in all four surviving editions of the alliance’s own membership FAQ, spanning November 1995 to October 1996. Its own blurb dates it against the alliance’s own calendar: it “got going during Summit VI,” and by November 1995 it had “quickly amassed over 100 wrestlers.” Its wrestler the Electric Youth is entered “representing the EWF” in another member’s interfederation tournament in September 1996. This is a separate promotion from the held Elite Wrestling Foundation and the held World Electronic Wrestling Federation (1990–92), which used the same initials in different eras.
