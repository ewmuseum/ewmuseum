EWF (The Electronic Wrestling Federation, 1994–96)

"Ground-Zero: Part Two (fwd)"

15 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Cut to an EWF interview area. Hanging over the EWF logo is a battered Scottish flag. Standing in front of that we see the Scottish Soccer Hooligans: Tully and Willie, and their manager Jimmy Lacrosse) Jimmy: "As most of you know by now.....those of you who speaka de English, that is, which I suspect isn't a whole lot, I put out a challenge last week to the entire EWF tag team ranks for a round robin tournament.
Well you know how many teams had the balls to step forward and face the
Scottish Soccer Hooligans and me, Jimmy Lacrosse? Well its a number that describes what most of you would probably rate on a scale of one to ten in both intelligence and beauty.........ZERO!!"
Tully: "Bunch of bleedin haggis pussies they is! Why a newborn pup has got more balls then most of this league."
Willie: "Speakin of new, boyo, check out me new tattoo." (Willie rolls up his sleave to show a rather tasteless tattoo of a man getting his eyes gouged out with a spoon)
Tully: "Bloody lovely it is!" Willie: "'Ere, take a closer look....." (Tully comes in for a closer look and Willie bashes him in the face with his elbow.)
Tully: "AHHH, me nose! You broke it again, you bugger!" Willie: "Ay, Jimmy! You cares to take a look at me tattoo?" Jimmy: "I'll pass, I left my...uh...glasses in my other jacket...... Yeah that's it....." (suddenly Jimmy is interupted by an EWF assistant who comes on camera and hands him a note.)
Jimmy: "What's this? (reads it) Well, well.....seems the EWF has granted us a TV Tag Title shot against Random Violence."
Tully: "But ain't we already 'ad them belts?" Jimmy: "Yeah yeah, I know....been there, done that. But I guess we'll take them anyway. After all its not like the rest of this league is beating down our doors to get a piece of us."
Willie: "Bloody lucky for most of them, otherwise we'd be ripping off there bloody heads and spitting down their bleedin throats."
Tully: "Or ripping off their arms and beating them with the stumps." Willie: "Or sucking out their eyes and kicking them where the sun don't shine." Tully: "Or..." Jimmy: "I get the point! Lets just save it for Random Stupidity. Hopefully they'll prove to be at least a minor diversion for a moment or two." Willie: "At least until we break their bloody kneecaps!" Tully: "Right you are, laddie. What say we go and celebrate with a pint or two." Willie: "Great idea! C'mon Jimmy, we'll make a man of you yet...." (The Hooligans walk out and start to drag Jimmy with them) Jimmy: "Uh guys, I don't think I'm really in the mood for...." Willie: "That WASN'T a request....." (fade out) ============================================================================== (Scene: Commissioner Presseur's Office. Gaijin and the Master of Illusions are standing in the background. A man in a business suit it arguing with the commisioner.)
Lawyer: Random Violence is Intercontinental material. The EWF is deliberately stifling them by relegating them to fighting for the TV titles. They are former Intercontinental champions and have defended their belts not once but three times against such teams as the New Medics who are considered World title material, Dai Kaiju and Bull Baker, top singles wrestlers and Arthur Newcastle and The Wall. This latest action from the
EWF Championship Committee is a blatant attempt to destroy their credibility. Gaijin not only survived the eight man TV Tag Team Challenge but eliminated two top wrestlers, El Hijo Hurley and the Insurance Policy.
(Clip of Gaijin landing a superkick into the Insurance Policy's chin that takes him down for the pin.)
Gaijin: (Grins manically) Shawn Michaels! Eat your heart out! And I mean eat your heart out.
Presseur: (Calmly) Due to their 'less then legal' methods of victory the championship committee decided to insure they had true wrestling skill by moving them to the TV title.
(Gaijin looks like he's going to attack Presseur but Master of Illusions stops him.) Lawyer: According to EWF policy, we demand a rematch against the Spanish
Inquisition for the Intercontinental tag team titles. Pure science. Nine
Planes of Hell. Ladder match. Loser Burns Himself On A Stake match. Random
Violence doesn't care. Those Intercontinental belts are the property of RV and they intend to get them back.
Gaijin: (Turns to the camera) We will be the EWF's Intercontinental tag team champions again. It'll be a cold day in hell before we allow the infidel Spanish Inquisition to continue to hold those belts. You can hide behind your Championship Committee, Torquemada and Fang. We'll teach you the true meaning of an inquisition and I guarantee you that burning on the stake will be considered fun compared to what we have planned for you.
Presseur: (Rises from his desk) I believe you have a TV defense to fight. Gaijin: (Growling) We took the TV titles from the SSH once. We beat them once. We can beat them again.
Presseur: That was with Backbreaker. Master of Illusions: Never bring that name up again Commissioner. I'm every way his superior. He was simply a wrestler. I am more. I am the master of illusions.
(Gaijin and the Master of Illusions disappear in a cloud of smoke) Lawyer: They'll retain the belts. They'll become the top ranked tag team in the EWF. Then we'll see about maybe making them contenders for the World tag team championship. We will talk again. (Leaves)
============================================================================= RANDOM VIOLENCE v.s. THE SCOTTISH SOCCER HOOLIGANS [EWF Television Tag Team Title Defense] The Hooligans waited in the ring as Random Violence materialized by the ring entrance chanting "IC! IC! IC!" attempting to get the attention of the championship committee. The champions knew they'd have to do that in the ring as well.
Willie McDawg didn't seem to be interested in what they had to say and he greeted Gaijin with a plancha to the floor and then a big whip into the stairs. At the referee's demand, LaCrosse got his man to bring it into the ring and the Hooligans went to work. McDawg hit punch and kick combinations and a bulldog before tagging in to Tully Reeker. To
LaCrosse's delight they then hit the "Tie-Breaker" top rope leaping knee-->bridge suplex combo but only for a 2 1/2 count.
Gaijin went nuts at this point shrieking and howling to the dismay of many ringside fans. He hit a drop kick and then a leaping spin kick before locking in his "Primal Agony" oriental spike. Several painful minutes later Reeker got to the ropes and Gaijin tagged in to the Master of
Illusion (TMI). TMI got a 2 1/2 count with the "Dreamtime" crucifix and then unloade with drop kicks, elbow smashes, and a gut-wrench suplex for 2
1/2. TMI went for a DDT to end it but Reeker rammed TMI into the Hooligans corner and as LaCrosse distracted TMI with comments including: You two call yourselves RANDOM Violence!? The days of the week are less predictable then you guys! and GEEZ! What'd you guys take up at wrestling school.....SPACE?
McDawg used this opportunity to rock TMI with stiff kicks to the mid-section and then another Irish whip. McDawg followed in but ate TMI's boot allowing a tag to Gaijin. McDawg greeted him with an eye gouge and then knee smashes that dropped Gaijin to the mat. McDawg hit a moonsault that Gaijin reversed for what would have been the three count has LaCrosse not distracted the referee showing him the TV Titles and yelling about how they should have been polished for him.
The Hooligans took this opportunity to set up a table outside the ring and nail the Suicide-Sault double moonsault onto Gaijin, with a timekeeper's bell, through a table. Reeker covered but Gaijin shocked the crowd, and actually drew some applause, kicking out of the daredevil move. Reeker then went for a top rope leg drop but Gaijin got out of the way. Gaijin used his last bit of energy to hit a leaping clothesline. He covered and, with an assist from TMI who drop kicked McDawg to the floor, Random
Violence had retained their titles in a wild match. =============================================================================== [TH] Impressive showing by both the challengers and champions. Presseur better considering signing Random Violence to face the Inquisition at II.
[AB] You have a point. The champions have gone back to what made their first incarnation so deadly-they're wrestling. I wouldn't want to be Fang and
Torquemade walking around with that bullseye on their heads.
[TH] Our next match was set to be Actagon against the Annihilator but because he's a coward Actagon no-showed.
[AB] What are you talking about? You know as well as I that Actagon's plane was delayed because of mechanical problems.
[TH] It's all the same. [AB] Anyway, Annihilator made the arrogant mistake of issuing an open challenge in the ring to anyone who thought they could take him. Bad move as there was an incensed Electric Youth waiting in the wings to tear anyone apart.
=============================================================================== THE ELECTRIC YOUTH v.s. ANNIHILATOR The Youth charged the ring only to be met with a flying drop kick from the
Annihilator that sent him to the arena floor. Mike Falkner grabbed his man to shake some sense into him and afterwards it was brutal. EY nailed a clothesline, then a press slam, then a fallaway slam. He followed that sequence with a leaping Electrocution piledriver for a three count. Post match EY hurled the Annihilator over the top to the floor before being lead to the back, admist massive mark pop fever, by Falkner.
This was just not the Annihilator's night. Already sprawled out on the floor, Backbreaker came to ringside. Backbreaker rocked King Louie with a martial arts combo and then a backbreaker before grabbing the house mike.
[BB] ANNILATOR! You can cower behind your Reign of Terror pals. You can hide behind your pretty little manager. But I'll bet in a fair one-on-one match you couldn't beat your grandmother. If you want to prove me wrong, you know where you can reach me. You and me. One on one. Anytime. Any place. Any stipulation.
He then stomped on Louie on his way out as the fans were really enjoying this. =============================================================================== [TH] What was that crap? Who allows imbeciles like Backbreaker to just run out during a match and attack a defenseless manager who is distraught over seeing his man brutalized by the Electric Puke?
[AB] Hey Annihilator started all this by putting Backbreaker through a table for no reason at Survival of the Fittest.
[TH] I'll remeber your condoning this in the future. [AB] Our next match was requested by the Intercontinental Champion, William the
Conqueror. Why? We'll let him explain for himself...
=============================================================================== William the Conqueror is irate. Rather than taking his ease in his usual chair before his roaring fire, he is pacing in front of the mantel. The Irish Wolfhounds and pacing with him and looking unhappy as well.
WTC: Cactus Jake Mason, or whatever you call yourself. You have made a bad mistake and bad enemy. NO ONE interferes with my matches, you pusilanimous wretch.
My rings skills are matchless and I do not need your help, nor that of anyone else. You need to be taught a lesson, and I am the consumate teacher. Mr. Mason, I hope you don't find the experience too 'jarring'.
Showtime. This is not over. Sometime, sometime soon, we should settle this in the ring again - without the intervention of Mr. Mason or anyone else. Arrange it with the officials.
Despite the fact that I don't like you much, you were robbed of a conclusive resolution - and I was robbed of the chance to see you squirm in pain and submit to the Boston Crab.
Mason, first I deal with you! Good evening. =============================================================================== WILLIAM THE CONQUEROR v.s. CACTUS JAKE MASON This match had all sorts of fun going on before the match ever began. Cactus Jake Mason came to the ring to "Shitkickers" by House of Pain but the music was cut off by none other than Roker Showtime.
[Roker] Hey Cactus Jake Mason! (The camera pans over to Roker Showtime standing on top of a table at the top of the isle) [Roker] You seem to think it's pretty funny hitting people over the head with chairs. I bet you'd love to come down here right now and just put me through this table like you did a month back. Then do I have a match for you. Table Break Match... Interfed Invasion... you and me putting each other through tables until you can't stand. And just to show how serious I am...
(Roker jumps high in the air and elbow drops the table in half. Countless jobbers come to help Roker up, but he pushes them all away and grabs the mic)
[Roker] Mason, this ain't even close to the amount of pain I'm gonna inflict on your pathetic body. You wanted me in the ring again. Then pay the price!
(Roker storms off) Mason burst into hysterical laughter and grabbed a microphone to respond. [CJM] That's too funny *as he lets out a crazy sounding laugh* but you'll be goin' through those tables soon enough. Then we're gonna see who's laughin'! Now as for my opponent tonight. it should have been that low-life peice of garbage Dingleweed, but instead...
(Cactus looks over to what seems to be an overweight out of shape fan w/ a mustache and beard and sporting a
"EW F'N C" t-shirt who is majorly cursin' out cactus. This guy is chuggin' beer, wearin' sunglasses, a baseball style cap, and is really yellin' out the verbal assaults) [CJM] Listen you fat f**k, if you think you can do this sh*t night after night...then get your ass in here! If not, then pull up the front row and sit down.
(The fan clams up and sits down) [CJM] As I was saying before I was interrupted by the missing link...Instead my oppotent tonight is the one and only, former world champion and current intercontinental champion, William the Conquerer.
(Another devilish grin comes upon Cactus' face.) [CJM] This man is a great wrestler in both the singles and tag ranks but recently he hasn't shown it. If it wasn't forme he wouldn't even have that gold around his waist. Now he thinks he can redeem himself by kickin' the sh*t out me...
(*Cactus snaps his head back to get the hair out his eyes*) [CJM] THE ONLY SHIH*T KICKIN' HAPPENIN' TONIGHT, IS GONNA BE...me kickin' your ass! (Cactus drops to his knees and goes BANG, BANG down the aisle as William, unphased by all the pre-match hysteria confidently stirdes towards the squared circle)
Oh, there was an actual match too. Mason decided not to let this one start out in the center of the ring as he charged William and hit a planch-line over the top to the arena floor. William knew better than to stay out there with Cactus and after reversing a whip into the ring stairs slid into the ring.
William then showed everyone why he is among the elite technicians in the sport today. He first gave Cactus a taste of his own medicine refusing to allow him into the ring-first he hit a neck snap clothesline and then he brought him back into the ring via a suplex. A series of elbows, knees, and slams to the back softened Mason up and even got a 2 1/2 count.
William went for the crab but Mason was able to avoid it with a stiff kick. Mason charged but was brought back to the mat with a leg sweep and William went right back to work. Leg drops, headbutts, and a splash to the back found Mason ready for the crab. Luckily for Mason he got to the ropes before too much damage could be done. William went to pick Mason up for a suplex but his attempt was thwarted when Cactus rammed two of his fingers down the Conqueror's throat causing him to gag.
Mason capitalized on this bizarre submission move by hitting a double underhook DDT. He then went into his brawling mode punching, stomping, head butting, and even getting a groin shot past referee Anderson that allowed him to hit a face first piledriver for a near three count. Mason then gave the crowd a BANG, BANG and went to go back to work on William but got kicked in the jaw and then swept to the mat.
William quickly executed a swinging neckbreaker before sending Mason hard into the turnbuckle. Wehn he stumbled out and to the mat William awaited and locked the Boston Crab on until the referee was forced to stop the match and award it to the Intercontinental Champion. William pushed his luck refusing to break the hold until threatened with a reversal of decision-he had proven his point though. Mason was slow to return to his feet.
Before William could get all the way to the back, however, a mysterious man leapt the barrier kicked the champ in the stomach and spewed black mist into his eyes and then hit a dragon suplex onto the concrete. This drew out Barry Bromowitz who drove the attacker off quickly and helped his friend to the back.
============================================================================== [AB] Another attack-this is getting ridiculous. [TH] William looked really out of it after that dragon suplex to the floor. [AB] Otherwise, an impressive showing by William the Conqueror. [TH] Do you expect anything otherwise? [AB] He must be getting a little edgy now as he has yet to hear if he's involved in Interfederational Invasion and if so who he's facing.
[TH] Typical Presseur garbage. Right now though it is my pleasure to show this piece from two men looking to visit the EWF for our next supercard.
[AB] Who? [TH] You'll see.... ============================================================================== The camera fades in on a brick wall that is covered in ragged, and torn posters of various e-wrestling superstars of yesterday and today. We pan across to see Doom and Disaster, the Original Gangstaz, Animal Instinct, the T-Birds, Death Warmed Over II, and Blackjack. All of these teams have large, red slashes across them. Suddenly rumbling is heard from behind the wall and following a large scream the bricks shatter and come flying towards the camera. A pale, streamlined man with a blonde buzz cut, and a dark skinned, asian man with a black ponytail step forward into view. To anyone who's followed e-wrestling over the past two and a half years they are immediately recognizable as Blitzkrieg, and Napalm, WAR.
[Napalm] Well, well, well...Once again it seems as if we have to travel to another federation to actually find ourselves a fair shot at a good fight. First it was the EWC, then the MIW, then JCWF/USeW, and now we're coming to
Interfederational Invasion to kick some EWF ass. Tell about it Blitz. [Blitzkrieg] No matter what politics we've had to overcome, no matter what suspensions have been levied, no matter what stipulations we've endured-WAR has always reigned supreme. Legends have fallen before our feet, champions have crumbled without hope, and federations have cowarded in our presence.
[Napalm] We're not just here to talk though we are here to come full circle and end the career of the one man who double-crossed us and lived to talk about it.
[Blitzkrieg] He has fled from federation to federation, fired managers, and now he has even gotten his family involved. We are here to settle unfinished business, no matter what the cost.
[Napalm] Tyler "T" Wolf you may have left Sly Foxx and Kid Kodiak behind in the EWC but you can not run from your destiny.
[Blitzkrieg] I feel sorry for your brother Brandon-he's going to feel unimaginable pain just because he came from the same sorry womb as you.
[Napalm] So if you're half the man you once claimed to be you will agree to step into the ring and meet your destiny.
[Blitzkreieg] At Interfederational Invasion meet us in a bunker match. And trust me, this time parlor tricks and pyrotechnics won't save your marked hides. The hunt is on.
[Napalm] When the fire falls... [Blitzkrieg] ...and the lightning strikes... [Both] WAR shall be victorious!!! [The two men flex for the camera as we fade to black.] ============================================================================== [AB] I can't believe that these two are still harping on one of the first matches they ever fought in their entire careers.
[TH] That's a sorry way to attempt to save the Wolfpack from the beating of their careers. [AB] We'll try to get words from the brothers Wolf next week and find out if they'll meet WAR at II. Now though we've got the Television Title Match as the loose cannon of the EWF-champion, Dai Kaiju faces the newest enigma, and winner of the Rookie Rumble, Oscar.
[TH] First though a word from a very interested party. =============================================================================== (The camera opens on Mr. Wrestling IV sitting in a hospital waiting room while Godzilla on Monster Island is on the television overhead)
[MWIV] Now you folks might be wondering why I'm sitting in a hospital waiting room. It's not like a chokeslam onto the cement floor can hurt me. Well
I'm here because Ryan got messed up by the EWF Television Champion, Dai
Kaiju. I hate hospitals! I hate the food here, the smell of the cleaning materials and I especially hate the ugly nurses with nothing better to do than give me a hard time. And you, Dai Kaiju, are the man responsible for my foul mood. You may think you're big and bad because you kicked
Apocalypse Unlimited around, but the fact of the matter is we let you do it. We went in there to save our friend Barry Bromowitz, not to lock horns with you. So if that meant Ryan had to get stitches, so what? If that means William and I had to be chokeslammed, big frickin' deal. What matters is Barry is ok. Now the way I hear it, Barry doesn't give a damn about slapping your pathetic ass around, Dai Kaiju. But that's not the point. The point of the matter is you pissed off the wrong crew. You pissed off the man that hit Derek Machismo so hard, I gave him a new
$&*#!^% personality. I schooled a man that later became the IeWS World Heavyweight Champion. What makes you think a second rate monster such as yourself has a chance against me? You may think the EWF is your own personal Tokyo, but you're wrong; dead wrong! The EWF is where the Apocalypse is happening! We decide who wrestles here. WE decide who holds what title. WE have decided you're time is up! And *I* have decided to personally punch your ticket. Dai Kaiju, you painted a big bull's eye on your ass and Mr. Wrestling IV is just the man to shoot it off. Dai Kaiju, you are going the way of the IV-gotten. I will be your
Destroyer. Count on it!
(fade to black) =============================================================================== DAI KAIJU v.s. "MAGIC" [EWF Television Title Match] This "match" if can even call it that was bizarre, and chaotic from the start. The man who was last called Oscar, came to the ring to "Slam" by Onyx, wore an Orlando Magic jersey, and was called "Magic". The champion was then lead to the ring by his manager, Dr. Dragon, and that's when things fell apart.
>From one side of the arena Jewbay "The Crippler" Soetoe charged out with a pair of brass knuckles and leapt at Magic. From the otherside of the arena
Mr. Wrestling IV came charging at Dai Kaiju with a barbed wire baseball bat. Not one of the four men backed down and chairs, loaded basketballs, tables, and the objects already mentioned all played a part in the brawl that followed and spilled all over the arena. This was wild. Blood, stiff shots, and total anarchy had many wondering if Dai Kaiju or any of the others had lost it again but no one could really be sure.
4-1-1 and the jobber brigade rushed to the scene and eventually pried the men apart but not before referee Hatrick ruled the match a no contest.
=============================================================================== [AB] Um, I'm not sure what was more confusing, Oscar/Magic or the altercations that went down after the match.
[TH] Well I just got word that next week the following will occur. Jewbay Soetoe will face Oscar/Magic, and Dai Kaiju will face Mr. Wrestling IV. The winners of those two matches will then fight later on the card-the TV
Title WILL be on the line.
[AB] Wow, next week's card is going to be incredible. There's more than enough action for two full cards already. Our next match pits two light heavyweights: "Heaven's Best" Michael Gabriel and Backbreaker.
[TH] Maybe Gabriel can teach Backbreaker some manners after that stunt he pulled earlier on tonight. First, let's hear from the one and only, "Heaven's
Best". =============================================================================== ("Heaven's Best" Michael Gabriel, mirror, posing. You know how it goes.....) [M.G.] Now before all you moronic fans get any ideas, it should be PERFECTLY
CLEAR who the *REAL* star of the Rookie Rumble was! It should be a NO-BRAINER as to who impressed you and the EWF Chhampionship Committee the most! And since most of you cretins out there have no brains, you obviously see it my way....
(Admires himself for a moment or so before continuing....) [M.G.] Now as for you, Mr. Backbreaker..... you and I are not strangers. Remember the Survival of the Fittest? Remember how the Annihilator robbed you of the honor of being pinned by me? Well, now you have been given a second chance -- a second chance for YOU to fulfil *MY* destiny! The word came down from God above, Backbreaker..... and He has commanded you to be
*MY* stepping stone on the way to the top! (Turns away from the mirror to look straight into the camera) [M.G.] And when all is said and done, Mr. Backbreaker, I will impress you,
I will re-impress all the idiots in the peanut gallery, and God's Glory will rain down on me once again because I will have once again shown just WHY I am.....
(Drops to one knee and does a double bicep pose) [M.G.] Heaven's *BEST*! (Kisses both his biceps before the tape ends) =============================================================================== (Scene: Gym. Backbreaker's outfit has changed. He's wearing simple black wrestling pants and black boots. A makeshift wrestling ring is in the middle of the room and Backbreaker comes off the top rope with a wooden board and snaps it in half in a 'Spinesnapper'. Turns to the camera.
Backbreaker looks intense) Backbreaker: My days with Random Violence are over. You don't get it do you. As soon as Chimera, now the self-proclaimed Master of Illusions interfered, my contract with Random Violence ended. I'm a free man now and
I'm fighting for myself. The bloodlust is gone. You may be the master of illusions but I'm the master of execution. You may be biased because of my past history. You may not like me because of it. But I demand and will receive your respect.
(Backbreaker grabs another wooden board from the middle of the ring) Backbreaker: Annihilator! After attacking me from behind and then cowering behind your manager you've refused to fight me. You continue to claim to be an excellent wrestler but I challenge that claim. I want a rematch
Annihilator. Pure skill against pure skill. Because I'll show you that in the ring I am the master and the squared circle is my domain.
(Clammers to the top rope and hits another 'Spinesnapper', breaking the board) Backbreaker: I'm waiting Annihilator. But now, to the task at hand. 'Heaven's Best' Michael Gabriel. Heaven must have puked you out when they sent you to the EWF. You're arrogant, incompetent and not even fun to watch. Time to see who truly is the best...
