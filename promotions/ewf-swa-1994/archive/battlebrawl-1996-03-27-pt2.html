The match starts out under regulation rules... your typical, run-of-the-mill match. However, after five minutes have passed, and every five minutes after that, a new stipulation will be added to the match, and these stipulations are CUMULATIVE! That means, they build up, like the oily grease in your hair for some of you out there!
[RD] The first plane is the regulation rules... the planes afterwards are as follows -- baseball bat match, where baseball bats will be suspended from above two turnbuckles for use in the match; no count-out and no disqualification, but only for the legal men; barbed wire ring, where barbed wire will be wrapped around the ringropes to make a simple Irish whip a deadly maneuver!
[TR] How about electrified wire? That's the fifth plane and it results in high voltage streams of electric current being sent through that barbed wire! After that, we go to Texas Death, where a fallen wrestler has ten seconds to get to his feet to continue the match! Plane seven is a street fight match where falls now count anywhere!
[JC] Plane number eight is the weapon-of-choice match, where each member of each side gets a weapon chosen by that side prior to the beginning of
this card! The final plane will be Finisher vs. Finisher with the previous seven planes' stipulations accumulated as well! [TR] As you can see, not a match most wrestlers would want to participate in! However, the challenge was issued to al Nassir's men by Lord Lionheart to prove himself, and I hear al Nassir has even put on the tights to join his men in this slugfest!
