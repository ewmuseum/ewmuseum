EWF (The Electronic Wrestling Federation, 1994–96)

"Ground-Zero: Part One (fwd)"

15 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============================================================================ (We're not done from the site of Battlebrawl yet...) ============================================================================ (When the dust settles in the ring, and Bromowitz, et. al., leave the Youth alone for a second...)
[Youth] HEY ASYLUM!!! YOU WANNA PLAY THAT WAY??? _FINE!!!_ Cause the next time I get my hands across the neck of that sorry excuse for a world champ, I don't care if it's in the ring, in the locker, or in the blasted parking lot...
This has a lot more to do with my friend... You hurt him, yeah, but he will be back, and he'll gladly take you out!!
You see, Fontaine... I know what it is to be a _champion_, and that's not synonymous with holding a world title belt, or any title belt. If you want to make the EWF World Title belt an eight-man tag belt, that's fine by me...
Cause this is about being a champion, no, being a MAN... The sport of e-wrestling was a lot simpler when I had my first world title... We didn't worry about bringing Insurance Policies, tag teams, or anybody that could do actual damage. We brought those who inspired and cared...
It ain't like that no more. I can't bring Debbie Gibson to the middle of this situation because I know she'd become a target. You don't fight man on man...
You never have. You saw how I took apart that Intercontinental "Champion" William the Conqueror. Yeah, he has the belt, but he has no respect from these fans either. They saw what happened when Roker Showtime put him down just as bad as I did the week before...
I want to introduce you to our fifth man... Some of you know him as a man who has been one of the few to put down the 600 Pound Gorilla...
THE HONOR GUARD -- THOMAS WILLIAMS ("Stars and Stripes Forever" comes on...) [Williams] ASYLUM, WAR HAS BEEN DECLARED. War was declared the day your man decided he couldn't handle being beat 1-2-3 in the center of the ring. And the sad thing of all is that you can't change that. You don't want pinfalls;
you want people out of the damn sport!! FINE... Just know this... You wanna start claiming about people watching YOUR back?? YOU DAMN WELL GONNA NEED EVERYBODY YOU CAN <BLEEPING!> FIND!! [Youth] That's right. Cause I want that world title belt, and I'll make sure Presseur knows that. My wallet's open for some fines, big boys... Since that's the way you guys want to play it, you're no longer safe...
[Both] ANYWHERE!!! =========================================================================== [Interview with Elisabeth Wiatt & Barry Bromowitz in the locker room area] [EW] Barry, what are your thoughts on the occurrences at Battle Brawl? [BB] Oh, there's a lot to talk about Battle Brawl. First of all, everyone's asking about Dai Kaiju and the TV Title match, the fact that he put me in the elevated full nelson and wouldn't let go. I think exactly what happened is that Dai Kaiju got more of a fight than he expected... and he gave me more of a fight than I expected, but the fact is, he realized that the only way he was going to keep his belt was if he kept me in that full nelson. And when he knew he had to let go and face me, he decided to take the easy way of holding onto that TV title. But you see, that doesn't bother me, because he did me a favor by not sticking me with a
4th rate title.... however, as far as messing with the AU... that's another story, but I'm sure IV and Kid will deal with that as they see fit.
Secondly, we had that battle royal with all the new talent off the league. Elisabeth, what do you think of our new wrestlers? [EW] I think we've got a few good new prospects in there. [BB] You know what I think? I think most of these schmucks are a goddamn embarrassment! I think Pressuer thought of a new way to get at me. He's going to make me ashamed of being the best wrestler in the EWF by filling it up with a bunch of morons who cashed in at the 2 for 1 sale for discount on bad gimmicks down at K-Mart. I'm not going to name any names... but you guys know who you are. I know you can't measure up to the best wrestler in the EWF... but damn it, at least try! I never thought I'd see the day I wished that fat tub of crap the Dark Rider or
"Mr. Handout" Derek Swisscheesemo was around... but that's another story entirely. Lastly and most importantly, we've got the World Title match.
See, the World Title is the most important thing to me right now in my career. I've made it my goal to win that belt before the year is out.
Steve Fontaine, you bet your ass I'm gunning for you. You say anytime, any place? Fine, big man... (pulls out a contract) Here it is. Next week for the world title. If your so confident, bring it on. I'm not hard to find. Just keep in mind, I don't make many mistakes, I made one last time letting you put me in the hospital once... I'm not letting that happen again.
[End of tape.] ============================================================================== A raging mosh pit is threatening to spill out of the dance floor and overrun the camera. On stage a band that seems to be named
"Carpal Tunnel Syndrome" is pounding out something with a frenzied beat. Suddenly, a figure climbs onto the stage, the camera zooms in and we see that it is a blond man with a buzz hair cut, a leather vest and a sneer;
the overall effect is something like what Billy Idol might have looked like had he been a pro-wrestler. EWC fans, and quite a few others probably, recognize him as Generation-X. He takes two steps, uses a monitor to get some altitude and comes crashing back down into the crowd. A moment later, he comes crawling out of the mosh pit with a grin on his face.
He accosts a passing waitress. GEN-X: Ey, luv, give us a pint of black and tan. He then continues, the camera tracking him, until he arrives at his booth, another man is there waiting. The other man is larger and wears his hair in a bristling mohawk. This is Fifi Pistol, Gen-X's friend and sometime tag-team partner. The table is strewn with empty pint mugs, and as the camera dollies in, Feef finishes another and slaps it down.
FIFI: MAKE THAT TWO, EH? The waitress nods over her shoulder as she heads towards the bar. OFF-CAMERA VOICE: So you get one more shot at Steve Fontaine, eh, X? GEN-X: What? Eh, Feef, what in 'ell is this kneebiter talking about? FIFI: EWF, mate. Fontaine left the EWC, remember? GEN-X: Too bloody roight, dropped a cage on his head din't I? And we wrote a song about him, eh? FIFI: That's right. He went to the EWF. He's the world heavyweight champion there. GEN-X: THAT git? Bloody 'ell. VOICE: And the EWF has their Interfederational Invasion coming up. Both Fifi and Gen-X slowly swivel their heads to look past the camera to where the voice must be located.
VOICE: Umm, and they invited you to challenge him for the belt... FIFI: That's right, X. EWF gold. GEN-X: And a chance to remind that wannabe wrestler who ran him out of the EWC in the first place. I like it. The barmaid arrives with the black and tans and Feef and X each take a hearty swig. GEN-X: Thanks luv, eh, better bring another round. 'Eh, bring one for the voice, too. VOICE: Thanks. GEN-X: Don't let it go to your 'ead. =============================================================================== (The camera fades into a quiet arena, that is suddenly filled with a sea foam green light spectacular. With that, boos filled the places, as the good doctor leads his charge down to ringside. Along with the rest of Asylum is a parade of beautiful women accompanying the champ. The final two women can not be made out, as "The Stingray" appears in his sequinned robe and slowly makes his way towards the ring)
[Shinn] Welcome one and all to the greatest show on earth! Tonight we will bring you things that will dazzle, shock, and amaze! This crazy circus that we call
Asylum is on top of the world, and the fun is just starting. It is only fitting that... [Fontaine] (interrupting) I NEED the mic! Boy oh boy, what a night this is! As the doc said, I am ON TOP of the WORLD. NOT ONLY did I WHOOP poor little Starfire, I have a LOVELY SURPRISE that will SHOCK the world of e-wrestling! Ladies..... (The final two women reveal themselves to be none other than Awesome Amanda and DEBBIE GIBSON! The crowd goes absolutely wild!!!)
Need I say any more? EWE, AU, you want to GET CRAZY with US? Don't you know WE'RE LOCO! Your women saw the the champ in all his glory last week and RUSHED to MY SIDE! After all this time, I finally know what you mean EY, I can FEEL the POWER and ENERGY!!!!!
(Fontaine kisses Gibson up and down her arm) WHOOOOOOOOOAAAAAA!!!!! I've got YOUR WOMEN! I've got THE TITLE! I've got the BIG MEN protecting my back(Fontaine chops the chests of the IP and then
Harvester)! "THE CAVITY" is in ON A ROLL. And good old DDS is leading the way! (Fontaine throws down the microphone and quickly struts proudly across the ropes) How else can I put this? (singing) I'VE GOT THE WHOLE WORLD IN MY HANDS, I'VE GOT THE WHOLE WORLD IN MY HANDS!!!!! [Shinn] As you can see, the champ is better than ever. EY, Barry, and all your little hooligan friends, if you want to interfere in our matches, then you will PAY THE PRICE! The ladies are on OUR SIDE now!!!!! As for the EWF's hiring of Generation X, I must say that is just hilarious. Very funny, huh?
It's a conspiracy I tell you, but it's goin' right back in yo' face. Mr. Fontaine is long cured of his fear of X and he WILL get back at X for the little cage incident.
[Monroe] Beijing Buttf*cks, we will be ready this time and we will extract some revenge on you little girls.
[IP] If you dare try anything on us, or even if you don't, you're gonna get an all expense paid trip to the HOSPITAL, courtesy of ASYLUM!
[Harvester] WE GUARANTEE!!!!! (They cackle like mad, as Fontaine takes his shock stick to the screen, and with that, it cuts to static)

[TH] This one comes as a result of an altercation in the rookie rumble at Battlebrawl. ============================================================================= Setting:The thrown room of a dark and dismal, dimly lit castle, with only 2 burning torches to the sides. Spider webs are strewn across every wall.
Sitting in the center, Arachknight sits in his thrown, contemplating many a thought in anticipation of his coming match. Next to him, stands The Black Widow,
Arachknights mananger, her white hair flowing down her extraordinary body. BW: Leviathan, you have made a grave mistake...throwing my Arachknight out of the Rumble. We have big plans for the EWF. And anyone who gets in our way to the top, will answer to the power that is Arachknight. The scene in your locker room, only a taste of what he can do.
(the camera closes in on the stern, evil looking face of Arachknight, untill it closes in on the void of darkness within his eyes. 2 men suddenly appear to the left of him. One is about as tall as Arachknight,the other towers over both.)
AK: (breathing deeply) Leviathan, a spider is cunning and strong. It waits for it's prey,then attacks with no mercy! Leviathan,you have done just that. (He picks up a small doll of Leviathan and places it into the giant web) You have fallen into my web! And on Ground-Zero, it will be the fall of the giant! (laughing maniacally)
BW: Soon all of your men, Strange, will fall prey at the hands of my warriors! (fade out) ============================================================================== (Cut to the EWF interview area, where Dr. Anthony Strange and the huge 7'6" Leviathan are standing....) [Strange] "I'm going to say this once, you eight-legged freak of humanity....you're trying to make a reputation off the WRONG MAN...and as big and bad as you look, you're about the size of Ed Carr in comparison to THIS *gestures to Leviathan*
Therefore, EWF, better get the bill ready, cause the exterminator has arrived.. to get rid of a few pests..." [Leviathan] "Spider....must....DIE!!!!!" (Leviathan proceeds to tear the EWF logo off of the wall, and break it into pieces, and then storms off the set. Strange merely laughs and leaves behind him)
============================================================================== LEVIATHAN v.s. ARACHKNIGHT Leviathan received a gift package in his locker room and, without Strange around to advise otherwise, opened it only to be sprayed with an acidic gas in his eyes. This only served to enrage the giant who was then brought out to ringside by Dr. Strange as "911 is a Joke" blared. "Spiderman" by the
Ramones then began to play and the lights in the arena then cut off. When they were back on Arachknight was seen plummeting onto Leviathan from the rafters. Leviathan just shrugged it off and delivered a massive chokeslam. The two powerful warriors began to exchange stiff power moves without either seeming to be very affected. Leviathan then took control using a power choke to slow the tempo before hitting a gorilla press, suplex, and falling head butt.
Leviathan then went for the kill with his second rope powerslam but Arachknight reversed it and hit a belly to belly suplex off the ropes. He then went to work hitting shoulder breakers, powerbombs, and top rope knee and leg drops but was unable to get more than a one count. The fans were going nuts as Arachknight quickly became frustrated with his inability to hurt Arachknight. The mysterious one went for a leaping bulldog but
Leviathan caught him and drove him to the math with an unexpected falling powerslam for a sudden three count.
This wasn't over though as The two men seen in the interview, Scorpio and Sentinel, the Bloodhawks charged the ringside area and, after taking out Strange, began to lay into Leviathan. The resilient one fought the duo off for a decent amount of time before Arachknight blew mist into his face and executed a spiked Spider-driver. He then grabbed the microphone and stated:
[A] (in a low growly sort of voice as his pitch black eyes widen) Leviathan, you have not seen the last, of The Arachknight.
The fans were now well against this unit that beat a hasty retreat as Tombstone and Darren Machismo charged the ring to prevent any more harm from being done. ============================================================================ [AB] I would not want to be Arachknight when Strange's men find him and his cronies. All the magic acts in the world aren't going to save his hide.
[TH] He seemed to be doing just fine out there tonight. But here's a piece that intrigues me...
============================================================================= (Camera opens on a large chest. Camera pans back to reveal the massive "Arabian Nightmare" Mustafa Assad. "The Arabian Dream" Abdul Kemal steps out from behind him and prepares to speak.)
[AD] My friend here, Mustafa is very, very angry. At Battlebrawl he was interrupted in his glorious onslaught by a phony American. He infiltrated a "rookie" royal rumble and dared to insult the intelligence of all those involved. You my friends, Luke and John....are nothing more than pretenders...and of all people you pretend to be Americans!!! Your own stupidity is so obvious..and that will be your downfall.
You have made Mustafa mad...and that is not a pretty sight. Soon we will meet in that ring again..and Mustafa will most definetely come back into the ring if he is knocked out. Yes my friends...the Sultans of Slam have declared war on the All-American Boys...east will meet the wanna-be west..and things will never be the same in the EWF again!!!! Tell them
Mustafa! [AN] All-American Boys....we begin with you! We torture you...we beat you...and then we throw you aside!
[AD] Is that all you have to say today, Mustafa? [AN] No....there is one more thing. At the rumble at Battlebrawl...TWICE I threw a man out and TWICE he came back in with the help of his little woman! Riker Showtime...you pathetic excuse of a wrestler. Abdul and I know how pathetic American wrestlers are..but you showed no honor that night. I hope our paths cross one day...one on one.
[AD] Hehe....and if I must keep Sister Mary distracted..then I will do so and it will be ALL HER PLEASURE!!!! HAHAHAHA!!!!
(Camera fades as the Sultans of Slam laugh hysterically.) ============================================================================= [AB] I don't know what's worse, the Nordiques pretending to be the All-American Boys or the fact that the Sultans of Slam are playing along with this charade. [TH] You're such a cynic. Well fans of great action can rejoice in knowing these two teams will face each other next week on Wetworks.
[AB] Our next match pits one third of the Triple Threat, the Enforcer, making his singles debut against the exciting Heatseeker.
============================================================================== THE ENFORCER v.s. HEATSEEKER Both men came down to the ring ready to fight and make a name for themselves at the expense of the other. Heatseeker began mouthing off at the Enforcer who responded with a big glob of spit and this one was off.
Heatseeker was the faster of the two and it sure showed early on. The masked one hit armdrags, hip tosses, a side slam, drop kicks and an inverted DDT but couldn't get more than a one count. Heatseeker went for his Guided Missle finisher but it was too early and he ate canvas.
The Enforcer then hit a viscious clothesline and a jawbreaker before trying to hit his "Sentence" finisher. Heatseeker shifted his weight to avoid it but he still got caught in a powerslam for a 2 count. Enforcer then charged the corner but Heatseeker got his knees up the momentum swung again. He hit an avalanche and then came off the top with an elbow smash for a 2 count. A punch combo set the Enforcer up for a small package but that was reversed and then the two exchanged pinning combinations for no more than 2 counts.
Heatseeker hit european uppercuts and then a leaping elbow. This time the Guided Missle found its mark but the Enforcer was able to get a foot on the bottom rope right before the three count fell. Heatseeker picked the
Enforcer up but got nailed by a desperation leaping DDT. A stunned Heatseeker was then hit by a northern lights bomb and the Enforcer has grasped victory out of the jaws of defeat much to the dismay of the fans.
============================================================================== [TH] Impressive singles debut for the Enforcer. The Triple Threat will be a force to watch in the weeks to come.
[AB] That match was the Heatseeker's to win all the way-one concentration lapse cost him the match-I for one would like to see a rematch.
[TH] Forget it-the Enforcer is moving on to bigger and better targets. [AB] When we return it's the Television Tag titles up for grabs as Random Violence defends against the Scottish Soccer Hooligans. ============================================================================== (Camera fades in on a deserted park bench, leaves blowing in the wind. A tall man in a dark overcoat walks up and takes a seat on the bench before reaching under it and taking out a large manilla envelope and a mini-tape recorder. He then turns on the tape recorder.)
Voice: Good afternoon, Jim. (Cut to an over-the-shoulder shot as Jim removes an 8x10 photo from the envelope. The picture shows a large group of men in overalls.)
Voice: These are the Hurley Clan, an up-and-coming force in the wrestling world, specifically in the Electronic Wrestling Federation. They are also the most insidious threat to the IQ of the wrestling public, and therefore the US public, that we have seen in some years, which has occaisoned your unit's re-activation.
(Jim turns to the second picture in the envelope, that of an elderly gray- haired woman.) Voice: This is "Grandma" Hurley, the head of the Hurley Clan and...apparently, the brains behind their operations. Your mission...should you choose to accept it...will be to organize and manage a force to counter-act the
Hurleys operations at the EWF's next SuperCard... (Jim turns to the last photo in the envelope, a picture of Nick Pumpernickle, Paris Burns, Jean Presseur, & Michael Connorson, Jr. all shaking hands underneath the EWF logo.)
Voice: ...Interfed Invasion. As usual, should you or any of your team be captured, the Commissioner will be forced to deny all knowledge of your actions....Good luck, Jim...This tape will self-destruct in five seconds.
(Jim gets up and walks away as the tape disintegrates in a cloud of oily smoke and the camera fades to black.)
