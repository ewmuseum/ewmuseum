Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

APCW's surviving record opens with a recruiting post, 28 March 1999, billing itself as “The Ultimate Fantasy Alliance” and run by two co-presidents seeking “elite roleplayers.”

Its one surviving broadcast, a pay-per-view called Malicious Meltdown, aired in three parts on 9 June 1999. It opened with the federation's own president firing a longtime wrestler on camera — “get your fake ass OUT of APCW” — before announcing a last-minute stipulation change to that night's Hardcore Championship match: a bank across the street had agreed to host it.

Snakes, the two-time reigning Hardcore Champion, defended the title inside that rented bank against Brian Baylor, tapping out once his own leg was trapped in the vault door; the federation's own president fired him on the spot. The Television Championship changed hands the same night: Apocalypse's own longtime rival ran in and struck him with the title belt, handing the pinfall to his challenger, the Hardcore Kyd. The World Tag Team Championship was retained by disqualification.

The show closed with a twenty-man battle royal for a future World Heavyweight Championship match, thrown open early to every entrant by the promotion's own commissioner and turned into a mass brawl; a masked debuting wrestler, Consumption, outlasted runner-up “The Future” Brandon Thornton and promised to unmask at a later show.

Whether that unmasking, or the promised title match, ever happened is not in the surviving record; that same 9 June 1999 broadcast is its last surviving document.
