GLOW — Roleplay

"The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake vs. Vincent Lockheart

25 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We simply see Jeffrey Drake's mansion begin to come in over the horizon in Beverly Hills, California..the luxurious home of one of the best talents in the business today, albeit, underrated. The place has obviously been kept up with. We can see the brash gardens, the marble path leading to the front door. The door suddenly opens, and none other than "The Cornerstone" himself emerges from the door. He is wearing a slick black Armani suit with matching sunglasses...his hair tied back..as usual. Drake smirks and motions for the cameraman to come inside. Drake then exclaims...) ["TC" JD] Live...From "BURNING HILLS" CALIFORNIA...the greatest underrated talent in the world today..The Corrnnnerstonnne Jefffffreyyyyyyy Draaaakkkke! Remember that, because if I have it my way, you'll be hearing it for a long time into the future....unlike the name of my opponent...what is it? Vincent Lockheart? Please. This guy needs to get a better nickname because last time I checked, "Lockheart" honestly, truthfully sucks.. Point Number One. (Drake nods his head in a mocking style) ["TC" JD] Tsk...Tsk...Tsk...it really is a sin when you think about it. The guy's mental block is so bad, he can't even present himself in a decent package...he needs to go back to the bush leagues, if ya ask me. Point Two. (Drake shakes his shoulders.) ["TC" JD] Kinda seeing a pattern here? Good...the pattern? VINCENT LOCKHEART IS AN UTTER LOSER! Now, I may not be perfect...huh? WHAT THE HELL? OF COURSE I'M PERFECT! Scratch that..anyway, let's be honest....I'm the man that this business relies on to hold them up...I'm that one brick holding everything sturdy so it doesn't collapse on itself...Vincent over there....well...he's a common brick..his job is to get stepped all over by people who are better than him..namely...moi. I smartened up..unlike this bozo. Vinny is in the jungle now..and he's in unchartered waters...he's in MY WORLD now...and in my world...I have a BAD OBSESSION...what is it? Good question...here's an even better answer....its called kickin' some ass and takin' some names. Its all relative. Oh, Lock-And-Key-Heart...one more thing, numbnuts... ...welcome to the jungle ...bitch ...You're gonna die. ["TC" JD] Buckle up, jack... ...Its gonna be a bumpy ride. (Camera slowly fades out with Drake walking deeper into his "Burning Hills" mansion.)
