"The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake Speaks on a Few Issues

29 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please comment/criticize this rp [The camera fades into the infamous mansion of GLOW superstar "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake in his hometown of Beverly Hills, California...as the camera zooms into the luxurious main hallway, Drake is just coming down the stairs, but instead of wearing his usual t-shirts and jeans, we see that he is wearing a slick black Armani suit, with matching Gucci shoes. This seems to be a major turnaround for him...but, instead of screaming the cameraman for breaking and entering, he just smiles, comes down the steps, and leads the cameraman into the den, where Drake calmly takes a seat, and explains many different things, about his new lifestyle, or whatever he seems to be doing, and his match this Saturday, in which he will team up with his good friend, and partner, "Centerfold" Jeffrey Keenan, to take on two randomly chosen opponents.] ["TC" JD] Yeah yeah, you don't even need to tell me, I look a little different, right? Well, being the "star" I am, no matter how jaded I might be, I think its time I took command of the bucking bronco better know as my career! I've been outta' action for a couple-a weeks, but don't let the thoughts of "ring rust" flow through your simple little minds, because I am in the best shape of my career, and I plan to finally "show my stuff" and get the recognition I deserve! [Drake smiles a cocky, arrogant smile] ["TC" JD] It all begins when me and the only man I can trust in this damn business, The "Centerfold" of pro wrestling Jeff Keenan take on two randomly chosen opponents. Isn't this lovely? Its gonna be nearly impossible to work out a stategy because me and Keenan don't even know who the hell the two nimrods we're fighting are! But, rest granted...whoever they are.... ....They're gonna wish they were someone else after the match..put your money down on that! Because, when you're the "Cornerstone" of pro wrestling, you get ta' thinking...I'm the guy holding this all ta-gether, so where's the respect? But, I'll guarantee you this.... ....It'll sure as hell be comin' soon! Two random opponents, no matter who they are, should be no sweat, because as soon as the two unlucky buffoons get in the ring and look across at the team better known as Dangerous Ambition.... ....They're gonna soil themselves! And no... ....There's no bathroom break during the match! Ya see, its elementary...Keenan and myself are just gonna use this match as a stepping stone to bigger and better things, because when you get right down to it, we haven't exactly gotten our fair share.... ...So, it looks like we're gonna have to take it... NO MATTER WHAT! One of us is gonna be getting some singles' gold, because we're both qualified lightweights, we're daredevils in the ring, and, you have-ta admit it, ....WE'VE BEEN KICKIN' A WHOLE LOTTA' ASS LATELY! ....Why ruin it now for the two worhtless saps steppin' in the ring to steal our thunder? ....Doesn't make much sense, does it? ....Didn't think so. [smirks.] ["TC" JD] You all are gonna see.... ....That I'm gonna take center stage.... ....And I'll be damned if someone pulls the curtain on me. Oh, and remember this...come Saturday, when the opponents are chosen.... ....I pity the bastards. [The camera fades out with Drake grinning, but then standing up, and showing the cameraman the door.] [name withheld] --- Handler Of: "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake Former INWI Trans-Atlantic Champion Former Co-Holder of the N.E.W. World Tag Team Titles Former Co-Holder of the INWI TV Tag Team Titles
