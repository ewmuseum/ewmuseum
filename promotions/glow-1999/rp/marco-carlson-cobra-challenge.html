GLOW — Roleplay

Marco "The Cobra" Carlson

15 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Marco Carlson, a relatively new character of mine, is starting to make it big in the GLOW...here's his most recent flash. I'd just like to hear some of your opinions on it. ***************** [The camera fades in to show a wide angle view a UFC style octagonal ring with a plain grey mat and chainlink fence standing alone in a sea of pure black. The mat has obviously seen its share of action; blood and sweat stains are randomly dotted about it. You can hear the door to ring swing open. A figure appears out of the surrealistic background, and stands in the center of the ring. The camera slowly zooms in to show Marco "The Cobra" Carlson, wearing a black Krugan's shirt and white shorts. He looks out into the distance, and begins to speak.] MC : "The Octagaon"...this is where the best fighters in the world come to test their skills...Karate, Taekwon-do, Sambo, Jiu-jitsu...all styles engage in a no-holds barred showdown to find out who the best fighter is. Two men come in, and the door is shut. The last man standing is the winner. No rules, no countouts, no pins...just pure fighting. I've been in here before, and I've won. I've knocked men out with a single kick, and I've made grown men cry like babies with a simple twist of their wrist. I've seen teeth knocked out, blood spilled, bones shattered and ligaments torn to shreds...if it's in the human anatomy, it's probably been injured in here. [Carlson turns to face the camera] A lot of people claim to be the best, but very few ever get to prove the chance that they are. This Saturday, I get to step into the ring with someone who calls himself "Simply the Best", and he happens to be the GLOW Heavyweight Champion. James Pillow, I'm not going to take you lightly. You've got to be doing something right if you've got that belt around your waist. But I've got an advantage coming into this match...I've been training in the martial arts for over 20 years. I've been around the world and I've fought with the best. I've fought the Red Army in Sambo. I've made Gracies tap out. I've stunned the arenas of Bangkok with a single roundhouse. I've been in the Octagon and come out on top. Pillow, you've never been anywhere close to the level of competition I've been. I've fought the best in all the world, and I've beat them all. You've been stuck in the same wrestling ring fighting with the same moves, using the same style for years...you'll stand no match against a true student of the fighting arts. All my studies, all my time in the ring and in the gym, all my time perfecting my technique, my whole life devoted to fighting...it all culminates on Saturday night. I wish you the best of luck, James...may the best man win... [The scene fades back to black as Carlson walks out of the ring] The Tommy AKA Colonel in the RSPWF Army Former UIWF VP Handler of Jimmy Wallace and Marco Carlson
