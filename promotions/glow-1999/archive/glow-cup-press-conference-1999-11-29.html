GLOW Cards

GLOW Cup Press Conference

29 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The setting is that of a press conference. Many reporters are sitting with their cameras and notebooks ready. Many flashes break out as King Apples, the owner of the GLOW, walks his way to the podium.] KA: Ok, easy on the flashes. No then, the reason this press conference was called to order was to announce the pairings in the G-Cup. At this time, the only matches on the pay per view will be the G-Cup matches, because I have yet to find someone suitable to defend my World Title against. [A reporter shoots up her hand and Apples shoots her a stare.] KA: What? Reporter: Why don't you find anyone suitable? Is it just because you don't want to defend the belt? KA: Lady, first off...this wasn't a conference open to questions. If you had read the booklet you got when you walked in, your dumb ass would of known that. Second of all, I am the owner of this organization and if I don't want to defend at this pay per view, I won't. The bottom line is that there are people suitable, but they are all in the G-Cup. Now shut your trap as I announce these pairings. [Apples unveils the first part. The first four brackets are shown.] KA: Ok, here are the first four brackets, on the left side of the set up. In your first match, it will be Setzer Van Strife, who by the way is my pick for this tournament, and he will face off against "The Hammer" Alex Cote . In your second match, you will see "Devastating" Higor Rosa vs. Jonathan Hammer. In the third match, it is Token White Boys vs. "Doomsday" Vaden Duggart.. And in the final match of this bracket you will see Victor V. Hill vs.Bobby Pride. [Some of the reporters take some quick photos as Apples unveils the other side of the bracket.] KA: In the right side of the bracket, your first match will feature Ryan Walters against "Incomparable" Dylan Rapture. In the second match, you will see Balial, that bible thumper vs. "The Cobra" Marco Carlson. In the third match it is "No Remorse" John Davies vs. Rio Spade. And in the final match, you will see "Drifter" JD Carter vs "Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno. [Apples slides the two brackets together as more pictures are taken. ] KA: Now then, as always, I have an open contract for myself. The dumb ass lady here in the front is too impatient to wait for me to announce other things. If I feel that the person is ample for me to beat on at the pay per view, I will put the strap up as always. I am a fighting champion. [A clamour is heard in the back of the room.] KA: What the hell is going on back there. This is a closed press conference. [Flash bulbs begin to pop yet again as the former owner and CEO of GLOW Enterprises makes his way to the middle of an aisle, microphone in hand. Russell is clad in a beige Gucci suit, his blond hair spiking into the air. His green eyes sparkle in the light as he smirks at Apples. He speaks.] JR: Hey there, Kingfish. Good to see your sorry ass after a few months. See what happens when I leave the company to you? Nobody is seen for a few months while you spend hard earned cash in Jamaica. No matter, though, my fruity little friend, because I have quite the opponent for you. A man nobody would ever think would come near you again... Hell, nobody would think he would associate with me, either. But it's a wonder what hatred can do to a person. [In unison, the members of the media turn towards the door in the back of the room. The doors swing open and time stands still as there is no figure in sight, and for a brief moment, it appears that Jason Russell may have inserted his mighty foot in his large mouth.] [But in a showing that _HE_ makes the decisions regarding when and if he'll show up, the shadowy outline of a figure appears in the doorway. As he enters the room, the lights up above and the flickering flashbulbs eliminate the shadows and finally, identification is known. . . doubt has been cleared. . . and mystery has been solved.] [Enter: Brandon Bloom.] [The media parts like the sea to Moses, and Bloom walks with confidence and purpose to the stage. His eyes are fixed on Apples. There is not a nod, a wink, or even a smile. Only determination, rage. . . . . . and hate.] [Bloom's appearance has changed slightly. Battles in the ring and father time is starting to etch its existence on Bloom's face. Wrinkles have formed near the eyes on his tanned skin. A dark shadow with a touch of gray lines his jaw and cheeks. The top of his black hair is grown out, reminiscent of a surfer-cut. A black T-shirt clings to his muscular form, tucked into his Structure carpenter jeans,wrapped with a black belt with a silver buckle. The bass sound in the floor comes from each step Bloom takes in his black Doc Marten boots.] [Bloom strides up the steps and approaches the podium. He takes his glare away from Apples and focuses on the media who seem eager to snap his picture and record every word.] BB: Just in case ya don't know, my name is Brandon Bloom. For those of ya who do, I'm not a ghost. . . this is real. And I am here for one reason. In my hiatus, so much has transpired. I lost my business. I made bad investments. I divorced from my wife, and the money I once made in my prominence is now gone. The only reason why I continue to exist, and stand before you folks here today is because of two things. The first is my son. The second, is to come back to the only thing that can provide a living for my son and I. And that is wrestling. [Crowd cheers. Bloom pauses and waits for the applause to simmer before resuming his speech.] The people who know my history know why this would come as a shock to many people considering who my co-workers will be. But since my "name value" is not quite as high as it used to be, it seems that I will have to work my way back up the ladder in the very few organizations that offered me a contract. I know why Jason Russell wants me here. To destroy "King" Apples. Russell and I have nothing else in common. And I know why "King" Apples offered me a contract. He wants to humiliate an arch-nemesis from his past. Apples, you and some of these people are well aware our history, and the hatred that exists between us. But I accept your contract because I need to provide the best life I can for my son, and I want to be a hero in his eyes. I don't want him to think of his ole' man as a failure. This is my last chance at redemption, proving not only something to my boy, but to me as well. Redemption is the key word. Redemption for my son. Redemption for me. And redemption against you. [Apples looks slightly surprised at the showing up of Brandon Bloom.] KA: Well first of all Russell, last time I checked, you were banned from GLOW festivities without purchasing a ticket. And well, there are no tickets for this. So we'll make this quick. As for you Bloom, last time I checked, you were off getting married or finding your smile or spanking your weasel. Something to that affect. However, an Apples-Bloom II would be intriguing. But, the question is, are you worthy of this title shot? JR: Anyone is worthy to be in the ring with you, Apples. _Somebody_ has to carry the match after all. In this particular one, it'll be none other than the man standing next to me. [Apples smiles and strokes his chin.] KA: Well you pussy, I am always out for a fight. If you want it, you got it. A title match. You and me. At the G-Cup. I am always willing to pound the crap out of a "Fella." We always knew you "Fella's" liked to play with one another in interesting ways. And I am sure I can quickly make you lose your smile again and walk off. BB: The only thing that's going to be lost in this match, Apples, is that career of yours that should have ended years ago. [Apples is outraged and leaps over the podium, heading straight for Bloom, who is ready for him. The two begin to trade right hands with each other, both men being rocked after each blow. The two bring each other to the ground, fists flying everywhere. Finally, GLOW security separates the two.] [Apples quickly is back to his feet when the two are separated.] KA: Bloom...you really stirred it up here. At the pay per view, you and I will indeed go at it. In a NO DISQUALIFICATION....FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE MATCH. And just bring yourself Bloom, because if anyone interferes on your behalf...I will throw you out of the GLOW forever. And I can guarantee nobody will come in on my behalf. This is me and you. BB: That's the only way I want it. [Apples and Bloom are separated, and leave in opposite exits of the room as the reporters flash more photos.]
