|_|______________________________________________________ [The camera fades in to show the face of Marco "The Cobra" Carlson deep in concentration. The shot is completely zoomed in, and his face fills the entire screen. His eyes are closed, but they do not show any signs of tension. His eyebrows are pointed downwards ever so slightly, and his brow tenses and relaxes, evidence to the deep concentration with which he is engaged. His nostrils flare open as he deeply inhales. He hesitates a moment, and exhales. He continues his rythmic breathing pattern for a few more cycles as the camera slowly begins to zoom out. As the view moves farther and farther back, we can see more of Carlson's body. He is standing on top a thin pane of glass balanced on two balloons, which are placed about a third of the way in from each end. Carlson's posture is a perfect horse stance. As the camera zooms as far out as it will go, Carlson begins to move. While keeping his eyes closed, he throws a series of middle punches and blocks, and then executes a side kick well over his head. He holds this posture, and it looks like Carlson is doing a split while standing on one foot. He raises up on the ball of his one planted foot, and turns 180 degrees with his leg still in the air. He slowly lowers his leg to its natural position. Just as his leg touches the top of the glass, he presses off of it and does a spinning roundhouse kick in the air, going a full turn and a half before touching down on the glass. His movements are precise and controlled, and the two balloons barely move upon the impact of his landing. With only a second's pause, he leaps in the air again and does a full side split at the apex. He lands on the glass again, with no damage to it or the balloons. The camera zooms in slowly, focusing on the face of Carlson. His slowly opens his eyes, looks into the camera and begins speaking.] MC : It's been a long time since I have stepped into the ring for any sort of competition. I thought I might have gotten a little rusty... [Carlson hops off the pane of glass, and looks at the platform on which he formerly stood. There are no signs of any wear or tear or damage of any sort.] MC : ...but I'm probably wrong on that one. This hiatus from competetion hasn't been a break by any means. In these past few months, I've trained harder than I ever have before. I am in the best physical condition I have ever been in, and my skills have been honed to the point of perfection. Bailal, you may have thought you drew an easy opponent in the first round. You probably thought I have been living on easy street, working on my golf game and visiting family. I can't blame you, because that's what 99% of society would do when confronted with a break. But I am that 1%...I am a fighter. I have devoted my life to the perfection of the martial arts. Everything I do in life is centered around the improvement of the combat sports. That's the only way a fighter can be if he wants to be successful. Bailal, you are a devoted man, but only to your god. You are a religious man, not a fighter. I respect a person who can be trully devoted to a noble cause, but I have time respecting you as a fighter. You can pray all you want, but nothing will save you when you step into that ring but your skill as a fighter. We have but few precious days left before we meet in the ring... I'm ready...are you? No longer does sportsmanship, compassion, and spirit exist...in their places lie greed, corruption, and self-preservation. We are not gods...we are but mortals...and until you realize this fact, you shall never ascend to the heights that you are capable of. Throughout my time in this strange land of professional wrestling, I have found that influence only comes with notoriety. And notoriety only comes with success. However, one can not be successful by veiling their failures, and I will do no such thing. I can say that I have not accomplished everything that I've wanted to. I can also say that I have not gained that level of notoriety that I seek. I can even say that in some people's eyes, I am remembered not for a match that I won, but rather, one that I lost.And as I hung suspended in the air from a cross that I had been fixed to by my bitter enemy, I began to think... I began to think about my efforts and results that were springing from them. As I fought for noble causes and what I thought was right, I was bypassed by men who had a different kind of success on their mind. Material success drove them to the top...and they were recognized for it time and again. And as I said before, success leads to notoriety, which ultimately evolves into influence. I saw that these men were dictating their surrounding because they had material success...something that is tangible and easily seen. That was two years ago and I have had plenty of time to think things over. I want everything that comes with it as well...the accolades, the responsibility...and the notoriety. For until you have the pulpit, the gospel falls on deaf ears. It doesn't matter how I have to do it or even who I have to hurt because I know that it will all be justified in the end. Change filters down, not up or sideways...and change MUST occur. For I speak the gospel, and the gospel is the truth...
