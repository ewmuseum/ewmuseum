Hammer as always is dressed in dark blue jeans, black combat boots, and a black longsleeved Tool t-shirt. His hair is covered by a black baseball cap turned around the other way. It says KoRn. His black goatee is neatly combed. He looks at the camera and a small smile crosses his face.] JH : Well I figured you guys would find me some time soon. I'm guessing you guys want a word with me. [The camera moves up and down confirming this.] JH : All right then, it's been a couple of months since the world has seen or heard from Jonathan Hammer. I decided to pack up and head back to Detroit. After the matches with Tucker and Bane I needed the time to just relax and kick my feet up and for the record regain some blood. God damn boys you damn near killed me. So I rested and worked out. Regain some of my strength and I wrestled a few matches here and there to keep the ring rust at a minimum. Today I feel as if I'm in the top shape of my life. With the GLOW now opening up I feel as if a piece of my life has been put back together. [Hammer takes off his hat and straightens his hair and puts the hat on again.] JH : In the first main event of GLOW it will be myself taking on the talented James Pillow. The man who is simply the best or so he speaks. Well James you are going to need to be the best when we step into the cage. I know you remember that I'm simply the best when it comes to extreme matches James. You step into my world for the GLOW World Title. I'm going to use the cage as a chesse grater with your face son. The more you scream the more I will laugh James. Don't take it personally it's just the fact that I want this title more than life itself. I admit that I've never been a world champion in my life. I've yearned for a world title of some sort. Now I get another chance and I aim to please. James good luck you will need it. The INWI is gone and the GLOW is here to stay. Pillow welcome to the House of Hammer!
