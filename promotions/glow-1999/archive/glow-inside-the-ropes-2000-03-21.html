*********************************************************** Vandal Gomez *********************************************************** [We open to see a young boy, bundled up in a winter coat, mowing a lawn. Sweat slowly drips from his forehead, even though it is very cold outside. He finally stops, looks at the lawn satisfied, and walks over to the mailbox near the street. He opens it, and pulls out an envelope. With a smile on his face, the boy flips the envelope over and tears it open. Inside is a twenty dollar bill. The boy pockets the money and the envelope, walks back over to the lawnmower, and starts dragging it down the street. Fade to black for a moment, and then reopen inside of a house. It seems that we are looking down, from the stairs, at the front door. Suddenly, the front door swings open, and in walks the boy]
Boy : Mom! I'm home! [The boy starts walking up the stairs, towards our view. He passes as if the camera were not there. He opens a door and walks in, and we follow. The door seemingly leads to the boy's room. Inside the room are posters of popular bands, a nicely made bed with dark blue sheets, and a desk. The floor is a bit of a mess, with a few action figures and pieces of clothing scattered throughout. The boy sits on his bed, pulls out the twenty, and shoves it between the cushions]
Boy : [In a whiny, womanly voice] "Don't mow his lawn" she says. Yeah, right. How can someone that pays me twenty bucks to mow his lawn be so "evil". Mom's out of her mind.
Woman's voice: Johnny! Dinnertime! [The boy springs up and bolts out the door, rushing downstairs. We follow, at a much slower pace, and see him disappear around the corner. As we turn the same corner, we see a dining room, with three plates of food. Sitting in front of one is the boy who has been identified as Johnny. In another is a young girl, a few years younger looking than the boy. Finally, from another room, in walks an older woman, in her mid-thirties by appearance, with a gallon of milk in her hand. Woman : So, Johnny... what did you do today? Johnny [J]: Nothing, Mom. Just mowed some lawns and helped Ms. Henderson move her couch. [The woman, identified as Johnny's mother, come back into the room and sits down] Johnny's Mother [JM]: Mowed a lawn? It's the middle of winter. Who would want you... Our parents wouldn't let us near the house, but we would go anyway, to see the "monster". And everyday, he would peak out of his window and look down at us. As if he wanted to kill us. And we would run away. I had so many nightmares because of him. The pale white skin... the burning flesh. That couple had spawned the Devil. At least that's what I always thought.
J : Mom... you're not scaring me. [Johnny trembles at this last statement, his bottom lip curling, tears coming to his eyes] JM : I don't want you going over there ever again. EVER again. That couple died three years ago. All that's left in that house is the monster. And none of us know what he is going to do. Lonely all his life. Locked in that house away from the sun. What might happen if he comes out? What might happen to you if he decides to take you? I'd be so lost.
I love you, Johnny, and I don't want you to get hurt. I don't want anything to happen to you. And I don't want you going over there anymore.
You hear? J : Yes, Mom. I'm sorry... [Johnny's mother stands up and grabs Johnny almost out of his seat, her arms around him tight. Tears are slowly starting to roll down her cheeks.

Johnny is mowing the lawn, a car passes by, the mailbox sits closed... and at a window on the far right side, a dark visage stands.

And we close in on the mailbox, sitting on the corner. To the side of the mailbox, where a name stands out in black letters on the white box. "Gomez". Fade to black]
