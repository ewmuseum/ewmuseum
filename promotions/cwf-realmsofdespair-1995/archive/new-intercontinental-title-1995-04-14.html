CWF New InterContinental Title!

14 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a shocker indeed. Dr. Teeth was supoosed to go up against Kevin, Mr. Perfect B. However Dr. Teeth was injured from his clash with
Mr. Perfect in the previous week, therefor he asked his good friend "G
Graeme" to step in and take his place.
"G Graeme" stunned the world by taking the title away for "Kevin Mr. Perfect B." and more importantly he may have lost his friend. After the match "Dr. Teeth" left down the aisle right after the three count was had. "G Graeme" was visibly upset, look for these two to clash at Cage
Rage II.
The other side of the story is the "Detonator". The crazed former-Irishman is out for blood (as usual) to get at "G Graeme". The
"Detonator" and "G Graeme" are both in Cage #1 at Cage Rage II and look for them to meet in a bloody brawl.
