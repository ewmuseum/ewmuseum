CWF House Show Report

29 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The CWF put on another night filled with action in a house show held last weekend. The night included 4 headline matches and 3 undercards or wimp matches. The fans I spoke to said they enjoyed it and were going to purchase tickets for the next house show to be held in three months time. The card went as follows:
Match 1: Outta Prison Chisolm vs Vince Fragassie (wimp match) Prison Chisolm won relatively easy. Match 2: Badd Brad vs Dusty Parsons Badd Bard and Dusty Parson continued their feud by battling it out for 15 mins with some great scientific moves and wrestling. At this point all hell broke loose and both men were counted out and brawled to the dressing room.
Match 3: Bill the Rock vs Joe Smithingston (wimp match) The Rock toasts Joe S. in 34 seconds. What a thriller! Match 4: Omar Kadafi vs The Black Mask (wimp match) Omar makes the masked man submit in under 3 minutes. Match 5: INTER-CONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH: "G" Graeme vs Dr. Teeth (G Graeme champion) The match was a brawl from the start. The G had the upper hand quickly and kept the crowd going through out. Dr. Teeth let his temper get the best of him and was DQed for biting and refusing to let up about 10 mins into the match. A bloody G Graeme was helped to the room by the Golden Boy who came out to a massive cheers
MATCH 6: EURO FORCE (Lord Tony & Nat Roma) vs The Detonator & The Glasgow Ranger The bad blood was hot in this encounter and former friends and foes of all four men went at it. The match was set as Lord
Tony and The Detonator were to square off but the crafty english lord was far too smart for the psycho Detonator. As the match continued the double teaming by Glasgow and the Detonator got increasingly dirty. It got even worse when Sweet Lenny came to ringside. After Nat Roma was smacked with a chair all hell broke loose as The Natural One came down to assist his men in hurting Nat Roma. Just as they were to crush his leg G Graeme and the Golden Boy bursted out of the dressing room to save the day. With the ref unconscience no decision was ever announced.
MATCH 7: WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Johnny Funk (champion) accompanied by the Detonator vs The Golden Boy (challenger) The two CWF superstars put on an exciting show as they battled, wrestled, brawled and just plain fought their way through 10 minutes of action. Johnny Funk and the crazed Detonator were up to their old tricks. The Natural One will do whatever it takes to keep Johnny Funk champion and sending Detonator down to the ring with him ensures that. 12 minutes into the match Sweet Lenny struts down the aisle and distracts the refree as the carzed former Irishman Detonator slams Golden with a steel chair, the ref turns and Funk takes the pinfall.
Stayed tuned, I'll post when I get some more information or results.
