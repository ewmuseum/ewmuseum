The Entire GAWA Staff --------------------------------------- - 1. The Intro to GAWA - --------------------------------------- GAWA... A name that has lingered in the name of one wrestling fan for nearly all his life... GAWA is a federation that orignated from it's talented and creative creator, Banjamin Xavier. Xavier has been a wrestler for nearly 6 years, and a wrestling fan since he was 5. At age 24, he went after the only thing that had never been achieve in the Awesome Internet Wrestling Federation, and in VERY few other federations as well; The Grand Slammy Winner. In the federation known as EWO, Banjamin first competed in. However, after his third match, he was forced into retirement. From there on he picked up his pieces, and moved onto the federation called AIWF. Within the first 2 months, he already captured his first AIWF Title; The Canadian Title. From there on, he went to capture the Intercontinental Title, United States Title, and Television Title. By September 1998 he had done almost everything he could, and he went on to go for that World Title. However, Banjamin was in a car crash that landed him in the Hospital for 1 month, which he couldn't compete in the match. As luck would prove, after that month, Banjamin picked up the pieces once again, and captured the Television Title within 2 weeks. Within 2 more weeks, he went on to win an 8-Man Tournament to win the AIWF World Title. After that, he went on to Tag with his good friend, Riddler. Within their first Tag Team Title win, to their last & final win, ending up being the 3X AIWF Tag Team Champion with Riddler, he ended up capturing once again the United States Title, as well as the Hardcore Title. His career was then put on a drastic hold as he was brutally beaten one nite, and since then has been paralyzed. Since then, Banjamin decided that he can't WRESTLE and stay in great shape, so he decided to hit the ring as a President & Owner. His long-time friend, who also has major back problems, Eric Draven Nygma, is helping him run the GAWA. GAWA finally found a Network to broadcast his federation, and today, he brings to you, still, with much pride, GAWA. If you don't know, heres.... --------------------------------- ------ - 2. What To Expect From Us - --------------------------------- ------ If and hopefully when you join GAWA, you can expect alot of things. First of all, you can expect fun. GAWA provides you with fully written & detailed cards. Although some weeks we may be a little late, we _NEVER_ go more than 3 days before we get the card out! Myself & the GAWA Staff all think of the best angles, gameplans, storylines, and matches to help you enjoy your stay
_that_ much more. Sometimes, just sometimes, you never know what to expect from
GAWA..and sometimes neither do we!!
And last but not least, you can expect to get GREAT competition around here! We may not have _THE_ biggest stars e-wrestling has to offer, but we _DO_ have some of the greats, most of the time you may never hear of them! Ever hear of Striker Phrost? Probably not! He's a former GAWA Extremely Hardcore
Champion, former GAWA Canadian Champion, and current GAWA All American Champion! How about Aswan Jaguar? A former 3X GAWA All American Champion! All these people, plus MANY more await you in GAWA! But if you can't compete here, we don't push you..we JOB you! Sorry, but that's what happens. If you're gonna join, please STAY for the trip. If not, then we'd like you, but you're not our
#1 priority. Either way..we'd love to see you in GAWA, but PLEASE stay! We hate seeing people leave, and all those contracts tend to get messed up sometimes! --------------------------------- ------ - 3. Small Helping of Roleplaying - --------------------------------- ------ Here in GAWA and like all the other _GOOD_ feds, ya gotta Roleplay. I'm not going to go on and on about how you should roleplay because I know you'd kinda get sick of it sooner or later, but we do appreciate it if you roleplay about every other day! If you need some, here are some tips to help you out
_that_ much more.
1. Try and give people a full description of the scenery, the clothing being worn and other things that can paint a picture in the readers mind. _BUT_ don`t go overboard. Remember, the most important thing is to keep it intresting so people will want to read it.
2. I always try to stress this lots, but it's simple in 4 easy words; Try and be original! There's so many Rock, Austin, and whatever other Big 3 wannabe's out there, it truly does get boring. Be creative with your quotes, and not rearrange those 'Monday Nite' ones.
3. Say what you need to say in an intresting way. Plot your RP out before you do it. List what you want to say (maybe you can type it or jot it down), how you might say it and anything else you could put into your RP.
4. Try to keep away from inring interviews every time you roleplay. It is OK every now and then but if you continually use a house show setting to roleplay, it may tend to get a little old. Try to think up new and different places to set your role-plays in! My _personal_ favorites are usually in somebody's house..But you can use _whatever_ you want.
In GAWA, I want you to at least RP once a week. The more you RP, however, the more involved you`ll get and the more likely you`ll win and have a lotta fun. In the begining of GAWA, I started it out as a close system RPing.
Then, about once everyday, I send out the RPs in a GAWA Wire-like packet. Remember; All Roleplays are sent to _ME_! ( [detail withheld] ) --------------------------------- ------ - 4. The Road To Success - --------------------------------- ------ GAWA currently offers 4 different titles for it's members! Of course, we're considering either a GAWA Welterweight Title or a GAWA Tag Team Title.
Whatever we choose, it _IS_ for the best of the GAWA! Now, onto the GAWA Titles! Great American Wrestling Association All American Title- This title is for the
_BEST_
wrestler in all of GAWA. Winning this title isn't easy, and thus far, we've only had 5 All American Champions. They will forever be etched into the GAWA
History Books..Heck, why not name them now? Aswan Jaguar, The Prophet, The
Dream, Saber, & Striker Phrost. These 5 are the cream of the crop of GAWA. Can you achieve it? Few can, and to achieve it _IS_ great success!
Great American Wrestling Association Extremely Hardcore Title- This title is for the baddest, meanest, Most HARDCORE man in the GAWA! Whenever you see this title defended, it's always defended in some sort of Gimmick match! So far we've had some unique encounters thus far, and there's plenty more to come!
Great American Wrestling Association European Title- This title is NOT for the
European ones, however, this title is the second highest regarded title in the
GAWA. To hold this title, you must be of great caliber. This title is usually defended every Sunday Nite Fight.
Great American Wrestling Association United States Title- This title is the lowest regarded title in GAWA, but it isn't exactly easy to achieve! To win this, you gotta be be able to put on a heckuva performance..and WIN!

Draven Nygma, Zed Roberts, and William Johnson Jr. I can be contacted at

We have 3 cards per week: 1.) GAWA Sunday Nite Fight - Obviously held on Sunday, this is our Main card of the week. Actually, it's the only card of the week that doesn't feature wrestler vs. jobber! This card is the one that builds angles, furthers storylines, and usually has at least 1 title match per card, and I'd say it has an average of 3 title matches per week. We also usually have 1 or 2 gimmick matches a week, and that's about it.
2.) GAWA Wednesday Super-Stars - This is our biggest House Show card of the week. Obviously, it's held on Wednesday's. To participate in this match, you just have to roleplay on Wednesday, and you wrestle & win. It's that tough, really.
3.) GAWA House Show - This is the lesser known show of the week, as it is held on Thursday's. To win, the same rules apply as Super-Stars; To win, ROLEPLAY!
Our GAWA PPV's are held once a month/every 5 weeks. Whatever time we decide to throw a PPV, GAWA knows about it. We have a few PPV's that are halfway copied off of WWF, using the title IYH, but NOT using the term In Your
House, as the World Wrestling Federation does. We do have a few spectacular
PPV's, and they are; WrestleKrazy & StarrCadia. More come as the future progresses.
However, the Sunday Nite Fight the week BEFORE the PPV is usually a small one, featuring 1 title match, and about 5 matches in all. It's just funner to keep the HUGE matches for the PPV, and a small prelude in the Sunday Nite
Fight!
