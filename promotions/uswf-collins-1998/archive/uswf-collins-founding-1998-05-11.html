A New E-Fed Based Solely On Roleplaying

11 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We are a new, young fed with over 30 wrestlers at this point. I'm not gonna yell at you to join my fed "or else" and I won't tell you that "I HAVE THE BEST FED EVER!!!" because that just is not true. We have yet to hold our first card, but when we do "hit the air" and start "broadcasting" or television shows, we could be ONE of the best. If you want a fed with class... If you want a fed with pride... If you want a fed based soley on roleplaying with no simulators whatsoever, please check out the USWF. Thank you for your time. Corey Collins Free Chris Benoit! Free Ric Flair! Replace "remove" with "dfr" to mail me! President Of The United States Wrestling Federation Visit my homepage at http://darkforcerising.simplenet.com " Do you know Koko B. Ware's mom's first name? Tupper." - Bobby Heenan "I'm so quick, I could spit in the wind, duck, and let it hit the old lady behind me!" - "Rowdy" Roddy Piper
