If You Can't Roleplay, Then Don't Bother Reading!

15 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you are looking for a great new fed set to debut on May 24 with its first pay-per-view (Made In America) that is run ONLY on roleplays, then swing on by! Like the subject reads, if you can't RP for beans, don't come over because we only determine winners by roleplay quality with NO simulator use. Once again, we start Sunday May 24, and we have some matches for the PPV already. If you are interested in a new young fed, come over to the USWF!
