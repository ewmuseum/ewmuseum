USWF (Collins, 1998) — Card

Made In America

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the first PPV of the United States Wrestling Federation, Made In America: ***** Match 1 - Voices In Your Head Vs. Big Daddy Cruel & Bone Breaker Kid (Tag Team Title Tournament) Although The Kliq had a definate size advantage with BDC, it slowed them down. Loco started off with BDC and his speed advantage took the big man down many times. BDC would soon tag out to BBK, and Psyche would come in. Psyche was the bigger man here, and just took the beating to BBK. It was all even until Razor Ramoon came to the ring. As the ref was distracted by Big Daddy Cruel, Razor went to hit Loco with a chair. He missed, nailed Bone Breaker Kid, and The Voices In Your Head grabbed a pinfall victory. Winners - Voices In Your Head Match 2 - AfterShock Vs. Juan Clement In perhaps the most lackluster match of the evening, things pretty much swayed back and fourth. Until The Clement boys showed up, and swayed the odds. In the end, it was Juan Clement winning with a spinebuster. Winner - Juan Clement Match 3 - Ricardo Clement Vs. "Bad Ass" Dan Miles Dan Miles started this match off with an impressive barrrage of moves. He used everything from the piledriver to the inzuguri. He was going for the pin until Bad Ass Buddy Brown came down to the ring and distracted the referee. As this was happening, a masked man looking to be 7'1" came in and gave running chokeslams to both Clement and Miles. However, Miles rolled on top for the pin. Winner - Dan Miles Match 4 - The Dominators Vs. The Rock And Roll Express (Tag Team Title Tournament) This had to be the shortest match of the night, with The Domiators grabbing a win in less that two minutes! The RnR Express proved that they truly do need to retire as directed by others, because they just can't keep up with the USWF's younger group. Buddy Brown nailed The Bad Ass Bomb on Ricky for the pin while Satre pinned Robert after The Lift Off at the same time. Winners - The Dominators Match 5 - Rage Vs. Marko Clement Rage started this match off with an offensive flurry, knocking Clement out of the ring. The two battled past the guard rail and into the crowd. After a fierce fight, both men were counted out. Winner - No Contest Match 6 - Maximus Vs. Sportsmaster Maximus started out this contest by dominating Sportsmaster...but soon,Sportsmaster took over the match. Maximus made a short comeback at the end, but Sportsmaster showed their his poise by retaining control of the match, and quickly ended things with a belly to belly suplex. Winner - Sportsmaster The USWF will crown its first, undisputed Heavyweight Champion in a one night, eight man, single elimination tournament! Two men will have to tackle the task of wrestling THREE times in one night! Match 7 - The Rebel Vs. Bad Boy Razor Ramoon (Heavyweight Title Tournament) This match was totally dominated by The Rebel. Since he burst on the scene and has formed a makeshift stable to be with Tiger, The Rebel has been a force to recken with. This was a very quick match, with Rebel grabbing a close win after a double underhook DDT. Winner - The Rebel Match 8 - Terrorist TJ Miles Vs. Shane Johnson (Heavyweight Title Tournament) A very even matchup with TJ Miles and the USWF's newest recruit, Shane Johnson. Johnson has been on an uproar since he came to the USWF, and it didn't stop tonight. Johnson finished Miles off in about 10 minutes after nailing his top rope tombstone labeled "The Showstoppah" and getting the pin. Winner - Shane Johnson Match 9 - Tiger Vs. Canibus (Heavyweight Title Tournament) Whether Canibus never knew about this match, or if he was scared, he didn't show up for his match against Tiger, giving him an automatic bye to the next round. Winner - Tiger Match 10 - Dave Martens Vs. Hellscream (Heavyweight Title Tournament) Tiger stayed at ringside to watch this one so he could scout his opponent. Early in the match The Rebel (Tiger's partner) came down to watch the match with him. Suddenly, Rebel jumped on the apron and distraced the ref while Tiger lambasted both wrestlers with a steel chair, which ended up with Martens covering HellScream. Winner - Dave Martens Match 11 - The Rebel Vs. Shane Johnson (Heavyweight Title Tournament) A great match between two up-and-coming athletes in the USWF. Tiger came down with Rebel, but it wouldn't throw Shane off track. At one point Rebel had pinned Johnson, but another ref came down and told the original ref that Rebel had his feet on the ropes for leverage. The match was restarted, and Johnson nailed a heart punch A.K.A. The Stud, and then nailed The Showstoppah and pinned Rebel. Winner - Shane Johnson Match 12 - Tiger Vs. Dave Martens (Heavyweight Title Tournament) Martens came down with a bandaged head from the chair shot he took earlier from Tiger. Tiger took it to his head with fists and even went outside and used the hammer from the timekeeper's bell on him. A match totally dominated by Tiger climaxed when Tiger nailed a double underhook DDT on a chair and pinned him. Winner - Tiger Match 13 - The Dominators Vs. Voices In Your Head (Tag Team Title Finals) In perhaps one of the greatest tag team matches of all time, four of the USWF's most gold-hungry competitors stepped up the plate for the USWF Tag Team Titles. Satre and Brown proved for the second time tonight why they are infact one of the greatest young teams to burst on the scene. This match could easily qualify as match of the year, as it went past the 30 minute time limit mark, but was re-started. Within 10 minutes of the match re-starting, The Dominators nailed Total Domination and won the Tag Titles. Winners - The Dominators (New Tag Team Champions) Match 14 - FINALS --- Tiger Vs. Shane Johnson (Fireworks blast off after a 10 minute break as the main event is set to erupt. The USWF comentators (William Newfield and Al Callard) make their first live television appearance to a large crowd pop. They warm-up the crowd before sitting down at their ringside seats.)
Ken Abbot: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the main event!!!!! (Huge crowd pop) This match is scheduled for one fall to a finish and is for the undisputed United States Wrestling Federation Heavyweight Champion!!! Making his way to the ring first.... From Kerla India, weighing in at 245 pounds, here is Tiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiigeeeeeeerrrrrrrr!!! (Tiger walks to the ring with Rebel, gets in the ring and plays the crowd a little bit) And his opponent...... From Las Vegas, Nevada and weighing in at 230 pounds.. Accompinied by Natalie, here is Shane "Biiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiig" Johnson!!! (Shane and Natalie walk to the ring to a nice crowd pop, get in the ring and Shane jaw-jacks with Tiger)
William Newfield: Hello folks, this is our first appearance here in the USWF, and this is gonna be one helluva match!
Al Callard: Thats right, Willy, we have two young "up-and-comers" here tonight in Shane Johnson and Tiger and they are ready for a big night in a battle for the big gold of the USWF.
WN: They lock up and Tiger is pushed to the corner. Johnson backs off and they lock up again. Johnson backed into the corner this time, but there is no clean break from Tiger!
AC: Tiger with a couple of knees to the ribs and Shane has the wind knocked out of him. Johnson pushed to the ropes and thrown to the far side. He runs back, Tiger drops down and is back up and nails a clothesline. Johnson gets back up quickly and backs up.
WN: Another collar-'n'-elbow tie up, and Johnson whips in Tiger. Tiger back off and he gets kicked in the stomach. He is doubled over and Johnson suplexes him hard. Tiger slowly getting up and Johnson dropkicks him in the knee. Tiger is picked up and put in a sleeper.
AC: Johnson won't win it this way, but he sure as hell can wear down the native of India. Tiger standing up and goes to his side and is now in a headlock and back suplexes Johnson. Johnson is on his back and Tiger is pounding on top of him! A slew of fists on the top of Shane's head and he could be busted open if the ref doesn't get in there.
WN: The ref is now backing Tiger off of Shane and Shane may need some help... The ref is seeing if he is OK, and it looks like it's a yes. Johnson is being helped up by the official and...
AC: He speared Tiger!! He speared him right through the ropes and onto the floor!!! Tiger has unleashed an animal inside of The Big Johnson! He clotheslines Tiger over the railing and they are in the crowd. He goes for a chair but that fan won't let him have it! Someone get that guy out of here or he is gonna get hurt... Wait, he looks at the next chair and has....
WN: A hotdog?! He is beating Tiger with a hotdog! That could do some serious damage! He now has a chair and the hotdog is on Tiger's forehead... Oh my!!! He could have that hotdog on his head permanantly! Johnson nailed Tiger with a chair and smushed a hotdog onto his forehead.
AC: The ref might wanna get in there with some water and a washclothe because if that ketchup, mustard or relish gets in Tiger's eyes it'll be over! Tiger now thrown over the railing and they are back in the ring.
WN: Johnson looks like he could be... Yes! He is going for The Showstoppah! He has him setup, but no! The Rebel just hit him with a chair, Tiger catches him in a top-rope Victory Roll and.. One... Two... Three!!
AC: We have a new champion!! A cheap win but a win at that! Wait, Tiger is pointing at that guy that we saw earlier with the chair and the hotdog! And he is jumping the railing! SECURITY!!! Here comes the cops, but Tiger says no and this huge man is in the ring and celebrating with Tiger and Rebel! What the hell is this? Better yet WHO the hell is this?
WN: As Shane and Natalie go to the back and this trio celebrates in the ring, we remind you to join us for Tuesday Time Bomb this Teusday night for some BIG USWF news! Goodnight everyone!
