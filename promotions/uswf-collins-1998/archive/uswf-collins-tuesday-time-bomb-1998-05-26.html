USWF (Collins, 1998) — Card

Tuesday Time Bomb

26 May 1998

May ~26~ 1998 Match 1 - Thunder & Lightning Vs. The Apocolyptic Warriors A pretty slow match, and we didn't really know what to suspect especially with the lack of each team's presence in the USWF. It was very even throughout the match and we could be looking at a pair of USWF Tag Team Champions in both teams. The Apocolyptic Warriors came away with the win after War Dog nailed Thunder with a piledriver and pinned him. Winner - The Apocolyptic Warriors Match 2 - The Messiah Vs. Ice Lord This match lacked crowd support because as with the first match, we have not seen a lot from either of these men. The Messiah showed that he would be dominant in his USWF tenure and ran right through Ice Lord. The Messiah finished it off with his version of The Niagra Driver labeled The Crucifix. Winner - The Messiah Match 3 - Rogue Warrior Vs. Tracker Tracker dominated this whole match until the end where we would see some interference from The Apocolyptic Warriors. As Tracker went for The Dallas DDT, War Dog tried hitting him with a chair, but Tracker quickly dodged and Warrior was nailed instead. Tracker dropkicked War Dog off the apron and pinned Rogue Warrior. Winner - Tracker S P E C I A L A N N O U N C E M E N T (President Corey Collins walks to the ring with some papers in his hand. He grabs the mic, and starts to talk.)
Corey Collins: Hello everybody!! Folks, tonight, we have a very special announcement.. It is concerning the remaining titles of the USWF, and we will start with the Television Title.. Starting at Friday Night Fury, an eight man tournament will be held to decide the TV Title! Now, let me tell you the line up: On this week's FNF, we will see Tracker face Dan Miles and The Messiah face Ice Lord. The rest of the tournament will be posted later this week. And as for the other tournaments, if you would place your attention on the video wall, they should appear... (The crowd and Collins looks to the video wall. The following appears:) United States Title - One Night Battle Bowl At Orgy Of Violence The Rebel\Joe Satre vs. Tiger\??? Dan Miles\TJ Miles vs. Tracker\The Messiah Thunder & Lightning vs. Fort Knox\Mark Curtis War God\Buddy Brown vs. Loco\War Dog The finals will be an 8 man battle royal for The US Title. (Underneath that, the following appears:) Lightweight Title Tournament Ice Lord vs. Tracker Dan Miles vs. Joe Satre TJ Miles vs. Lightning (Underneath that, the following appears:) The three victors of each match will decide the champion at Orgy Of Violence.
Collins: There you have it, and thank you VERY much!! (Collins leaves to a nice crowd pop... GET YOUR ROLEPLAYS IN!!) Match 4 - TJ Miles Vs. Ricardo Clement The Terrorist would not be the actual Terrorist tonight. Ricardo Clement took a commanding lead over TJ after he missed a flying legdrop off the top rope. Ricardo laid a beating on TJ, and finally pinned him after a powerbomb. Winner - Ricardo Clement Match 4 - Fort Knox Vs. Joe Satre In a very anticipated match up, two of the USWF's brightest stars locked it up tonight. Earlier in the night the cameras spotted President Collins speaking with some old friends in the crowd who would eventually play a role here. Who were they? The Public Enemy along with Chris Furman! As Satre had The Rings Of Satre locked on, one PE member (Swap Meet Louie) distracted the ref by jumping on the apron while The Ghetto Boy nailed Satre with a somersault legdrop. Fort rolled over on top of Satre for the three count. This wasn't over, though. TPE started brawling with Satre and Buddy Brown (who had made his way to the ring) in the ring. Soon after, The Dominator's 7 foot friend came down and tried beating down on The PE. All three of them tumbled out of the ring, and brawled until Dan Miles made his way to the ring and started his match with Buddy. Winner - Fort Knox Match 5 - Buddy Brown Vs. Dan Miles This was the match for the title of "Bad Ass." Miles had a quick upperhand with his early attack on Brown after his brawl with TPE (they also stayed at ringside during this match as did Satre and his "friend"). This match didn't last very long, though, because as Brown was about to nail The Bad Ass Bomb Swap Meet and Ghetto attacked him! DQ win and the name for Miles, right? Wrong! Because Ghetto quickly turned to Miles and started beating the living hell out of him too! Soon enough Satre and the monster were in there and it was a four on one brawl. As the show ended, for some reason, The Apocolyptic Warriors came down and started pouncing on The Dominators. Maybe it was a clue that they wanted a title shot....
