A roleplay-only, no-simulator e-fed founded 11 May 1998 by a self-described operator who crowned undisputed Heavyweight and Tag Team Champions at his own first pay-per-view two weeks later. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A second promotion also billing itself the United States Wrestling Federation opened under President Collins, its own founding post dated 11 May 1998 stating plainly, “We have yet to hold our first card.” That first card, Made In America, ran on 24 May 1998 and crowned an undisputed Heavyweight Champion in a one-night, eight-man tournament in which two men had to wrestle three times each.

Tiger won that tournament, closing the night by pinning Shane Johnson, the promotion's own newest recruit, after Johnson's finisher attempt was broken up by interference from Tiger's own ally, the Rebel — a swing that let Tiger catch Johnson in a top-rope victory roll for the win. The same tournament turned on the Rebel's own interference twice more: once to give him a quick win over Bad Boy Razor Ramoon, and again when he and Tiger ganged up with a steel chair on Dave Martens and Hellscream, leaving Martens to score the pin. As the new champion celebrated, an unidentified huge man jumped the guardrail to join Tiger and the Rebel in the ring; the surviving record does not say who he was.

The same card's own tag final crowned the Dominators — Buddy Brown and Satre — over Voices In Your Head in a match the broadcast called one of the greatest of all time, restarted after running past its own thirty-minute limit.

Two nights later, President Collins used a special announcement on Tuesday Time Bomb to launch tournaments for the promotion's remaining titles at once: an eight-man Television Championship bracket, a one-night United States Title Battle Bowl, and a Lightweight Championship tournament. The surviving record runs to that same broadcast, 26 May 1998; how any of those three tournaments played out isn't recorded.
