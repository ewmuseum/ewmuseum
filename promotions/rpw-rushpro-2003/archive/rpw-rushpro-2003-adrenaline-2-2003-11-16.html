The scene opens to a vacant RPW locker room. After a quick pan around the room, it turns out to not be vacant after all. RPW's Resident Moron, Ken Kaze along with George is seen with his opponent from last week's Adrenaline, Kohana, who is accompanied with his father, Chief Squatting Dan. Kohana is tying up his wrestling boots as Ken Kaze paces in front of him.  The group seems to be conversing on something, most likely their match together against Civil Disobedience, which was announced prior to Adrenaline. Ken Kaze:  You know, Conan.. I was thinking... Kohana:  It's Kohana. George:  .............. Ken Kaze:  Oh, shut up George! It's a common mistake! Sorry, Kohana. Ken blushes at his mistake. Kohana:  Don't mention it.  Happens all the time. Feeling a little relieved, Ken proceeds with what he was saying. Ken Kaze:  So anyway… I was thinking. We should have a captain for our tag team. Kohana stares blankly at Ken Kaze, in awe of such an absurd declaration. Kohana:  ...Why? Ken shrugs at first, trying to ponder an advantage for his idea. Ken Kaze:  Just because it would be nice to have a captain, you know? Kohana:  No, not really. Ken looks to his right at his trusty companion, George, who is sitting on a chair with a cigar in his "mouth." It seems as if a hole was cut out in order to place the cigar there. Painted around this hole are lips, and above the lips is a painted nose and eyes. Ken stares willingly at George as if he were looking for support. Ken Kaze:  You effing suck, George! I picked you up from the streets and gave you a home! And this is the thanks I get? Can't get the slightest bit of support from you, can I? Ken sits down in a chair on the opposite side of the room, obviously trying to ignore George, and pouts.  Just as he finishes tying his boots, Kohana's father, the Chief, walks up to Kohana to speak with him privately while Ken sulks. Chief:  Kohana... just have team captain. Kohana:  What?  Why?! Chief:  It make him happy.  You no want unhappy team going into match. Kohana:  But dad… that's the dumbest thing I've ever heard!  Team captain of a tag team?!  What does that mean?  There are only two of us!  What advantages would I have over Ken if I were captain?  None!  Do you think Civil D has a team captain?  I DON’T THINK SO!  Chief:  It give no advantage... it just make him happy before match! Ken Kaze:  Thank you, Kohana! Thank you! Ken gets up to his feet and looks at George. Ken Kaze:  Did you hear that George? We're gonna have a captain! Ken begins to chant in a singsong voice. Ken Kaze:  We're gonna have a captain! We're gonna have a captain! We're gonna have a captain! Kohana:  I'm glad you're happy.  So now all we have to do is pick a captain. Ken looks at Kohana for a second. Then, he glances at George with anxiety in his eyes. Next, he glances at Chief who is scratching his "package" while glaring around the room. He then completes a full circle of rotation as he once again lays eyes on Kohana. Ken Kaze:  So... ...who's it going to be?! Kohana:  I guess I could be captain.  Great, we have a captain now!  Everything cool? Ken looks at Kohana with a little bit of his anxiety loss, but the fact that they now have a captain rekindles the fire in his eyes. Ken Kaze:  Great! We now have a captain! Kohana:  Yeah, that's great!  So I'll see you in the ring... okay? Ken grabs George as he begins to exit the locker room. Ken Kaze:  Yeah, okay! See ya, Kohana, Chief! As Ken steps out the door, his foot snags on a bundle of wires causing him to trip. Trying to use George to prevent the fall, he places George against the wall opposite of him attempting to brace himself. However, his momentum is too great as he continues falling forward, eventually into George, onto the ground, rolling away down the hall screaming and shouting obscenities at the bundle of wires that "caused" the fall.  The scene then fades away, the echo of Kaze’s screams still present.
