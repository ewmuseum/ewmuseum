Saturday, January 03, 2004 [RPW] Sunday Adrenaline Live at the Halton Arena! 12/21/03 12/21/03 Sunday Adrenaline Live at the Halton Arena! Due to Holidays this was Adrenaline’s last show of the year and it was a huge one! Big names, big matches, and one big bad Wolf! Magnum Wolf was the first to land airtime as he tries to lie his way into the building. The RPW had banned him from entering the show but the Wolf would not be denied as Magnum scored a ticket that had accidentally been dropped by a fan after Wolf was thrown into him when security threw Magnum Wolf out. Overall: Great use of Magnum Wolf here. This was one of the best segments of the show. The camera really seems to love Magnum. The night continued on as two big PTC names, Jackson Rust and Tessa Windsor, are seen backstage. It seems Jackson Rust had been plotting against Chris Storm. The Real Big Easy shares his plan with Tessa; to release Oompa Loompas into the building and terrorize Chris. Overall: Good Jacko/Tessa work here. The two are a great pairing. Nice job making past reference to pull it all together. A RPW star and former PCW star made his return as a video for Violence Jack played. He let Gideon Poe and Peter Maxwell know where he stood as both men was labeled as targets for him when we made his in ring return. Overall: Easily my favorite segment of the night. Violence Jack did a great job and really was on tonight. This in ring return is truly something to look forward too. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid came pleading to Rush Mason for Hardcore matches. Rush wanted nothing to do with the hardcore image for RPW and let H.I.T. know that he was better than hardcore matches. Once more the Trapezoid asked for his favorite match but when Rush still refused to give in H.I.T. promised to make Rush see the light. Overall: Good to see H.I.T. on screen. The Hardcore division is something most federations don’t take seriously but with someone like Trapezoid in the lead RPW could have a very successful hardcore division if other promising stars step in. Back in Rush’s office he was paid a surprise visit as an envelope was delivered to him. The scene cuts out as Rush is opening it. Overall: On the short side but in the grand scheme of the show it was needed. Finally it’s time for the first match and gold is on the line! The “Jewserweight” Ilan Levin took on Brianna Washington and Karina Wolfenden. The match was insanely high paced. Each move was a beautiful motion of poetry. At first it looked like this was going to be a handicap match as the two friends Brianna and Karina doubled Levin. But soon the thrust for gold was too much and the two were exchanging moves. There were plenty of near falls and even more high flying moves in this action packed match. Though it looked to be ruined when Magnum Wolf appeared taunting Wolfenden. Karina got herself a small dose of revenge on Magnum as she went sailing off the ropes and nails Magnum. After more near falls and high risk moves from the three lightweights it was Karina Wolfenden with her Three Sixty Alien Drop on Ilan Levin to take the win and the Eclipse Title. The three competitors shared a hand shake after the match as a show of good sportsmanship. Overall: INCREDIBLE MATCH! This was worthy of a PPV main event. If you see no other parts of this week’s Adrenaline than watch this match. It was three of RPW’s very best giving it their all. Karina left with no doubt she deserved that belt. Backstage the Oompa Loompas finally caught up to Chris Storm. The Perfect Storm tries to fight them off but after his Cardboard-Throw-O’-DOOM~! failed to scare off the midgets Chris was left with no other option but to run away in fear. Overall: Funny segment with Chris Storm. Nicely building up the heat between himself and his two ex-best friends. In Marah’s apartment we see the effects of last week’s attempted attack on Akiyama. Jason’s friend was bruised up and both were left angry. As the two talked Rush Mason called up Jason asking to see him and Marah was on his way to Adrenaline! Overall: It was very nice to see how last week’s actions ended. The Marah/Akiyama hatred is really developing nicely. Once more Chris Storm found himself being surrounded by Oompa Loompa. This time the Perfect Storm was in company with an intern for RPW. The intern did not share Storm’s fear of the midgets and left him to defend himself. Chris is left once again with no choice but to run. Overall: Another funny segment. This whole Rust/Windsor/Storm involvement seems to be providing the comedy relief for the RPW shows. Boos filled the arena as El Diablo appeared. The Mexican Devil teased the fans with threats of quitting. But it was just the Eclipse division that El D was quitting and not RPW like the fans had hoped. Once more El Diablo promises that he will indeed be the first ever RPW World champion. Overall: El Diablo is solidifying himself as a main eventer. His mad quest to be the first ever World champ certainly gives him the claim he needs to the title and after PCW he showed he has the talent to become one. In the back Peter Maxwell and Tristan Belmont are exchanging harsh words to each other. The two blame each other for last week’s short comings. Though at the end the two came to a mutual agreement after their talk and the show continues on. Overall: Deepens the problems between Peter and Tristan. The two battled wits and it came out a draw showing that Tristan maybe new but he is no rookie. In the crowd the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid appeared again. This time promising to bring RPW to the threshold. Overall: Another short segment but it served it purpose on the card. H.I.T. is going a good job building up his much wanted hardcore division though it might be for nothing is Rush doesn’t approve of it. Back in the ring for handicap action now as Jackson Rust takes on Sweet Illusion members Sam Alotti and Alyssa Sanders. Jackson Rust did his part in holding the two ladies off as a great match took place. But when things stopped going S.I.’s way a chair was brought into play. This prompted Tessa Windsor to appear and save her friend from a sure beat down. The two managed to fight off the two members of S.I. That is until the rest of Sweet Illusion came out to help the number battle. Out numbered Tessa and Jackson was victim to yet another S.I. beating. The match was declared a no contest. Overall: Good match though it ended in a no contest. This thing between S.I. and Jackson and Tessa seem to only be getting hotter and hotter. When will it end? For Tessa and Jackson’s sake it better be soon. These girls kick ass. Finally arriving at Adrenaline, Jason Marah was in store for a huge let down as Rush revealed that Marah had tested POSITIVE for drugs. Jason threw a fit but Mason had him thrown from the building. Before he was drug out Jason asked for one final match against Akiyama but Rush would not grant it. Overall: Huge twist here. I don’t think anyone saw that coming. Though Jason Marah was thrown out of the building I have a feeling the hate between him and Akiyama is far from over. Just when Rush Mason thought his night would finally start going easy he was sadly mistaken as Magnum Wolf entered his office. Putting on a act worthy of an Oscar, Magnum made sure to sell his injuries he took from Karina’s assault earlier. Though when Mason started talking about a payment for his injury Magnum suddenly forgot his was in pain and looked to strike a bargain with the owner. Magnum laid down his wants; a huge contract from RPW with top money. Rush informed him that he promised certain people that he would not hire Magnum however. Rush was defeated though when Magnum Wolf threatened to sue. Trying to strike a compromising Rush offered him a match at the PPV Genesis against Karina were a contract for over 20% higher pay was on the line for Wolf. It wasn’t until Mason took shots at his ego that Magnum finally agreed and the match was signed. Overall: The Karina Wolfenden and Magnum Wolf fued is easily my favorite in RPW at the moment. It has history, it is still something new each week, and it captures your interest. I can’t wait for the PPV to see how this one turns out. Finally Gideon Poe was shown on television. The fans let the Shade know he was missed with a huge respond to him. Gideon reflected on what it will be like to come back and wrestle after being paralyzed and having a broken neck. Poe seems to have no fear and after a talk with his pet eagle Roland, Gideon is ready for his match later on. Overall: It is always nice to see the emotions of the character shine. After the events that have plagued Gideon Poe these past weeks it is nice to see him share his thoughts and feelings. I’m sure everyone is looking forward to his in ring return. Another big name made his appearance midway through the card as Totakoxe Salvador finally appeared backstage with his father. Tota expresses his worries about his match with Gideon Poe against Tristan Belmont and Peter Maxwell. Tota is given a necklace that belongs to his mother to encourage him. Tota’s father tells him to express his worries to Gideon Poe which surly enough after leaving the locker room Tota bumps into the Shade. Gideon puts his worries to rest and assures him that they will get their revenge on Belmont and Maxwell. The two friends leave to go to the ring for their match which is scheduled for next. Overall: I really hope they make use of the necklace later on down the road or part of this segment will seem pointless. Though I like how Tota and Poe are coming together as a tag team. Before the match is able to take place a short exchange between Ken Kaze and Kohana takes place. Ken is seen talking to his friend George the Trashcan when he sees Kohana. Ken tries to talk to him about tag team moves, names, and a theme music but Kohana does not seem too interested in the tag team details. Overall: Short exchange between the two. It was funny and I can’t wait to see those two in tag team action again. Hopefully they can lead a successful Tag team division in RPW once more teams start to take shape. Still before the match Jason Marah is being escorted out of the arena. It isn’t until Marah realized the girl who delivered the test results to Rush was an ex-X wrestler that the guards escorting him out of the arena attack him also revealing to be friends of Akiyama. Jason was left a mess leaving Cyclonia and Akiyama easy taunts. In the end Akiyama revealed new power allies and Marah was left in pain and unemployed. Overall: Really nice but one question. Isn’t the Irish gang the O’Grady family? So why is Marah using Yakuza before? Always wondered that. Uh back to the overview…really nice segment here with the fued rolling along very nicely. Finally the match is set to start. Peter Maxwell and Tristan Belmont are the first to enter the ring. The roof is nearly blown off the arena as Totakoxe Salvador and Gideon Poe appear and make their way to the ring. Tota and Belmont start off the match. Tota explodes on Belmont and Poe waste no time pulling out the high spots. The match is intense with the deep seeded rivalry here. Belmont and Maxwell both focus on the next of Poe during the match. Tota was on fire all match and really giving the fans their money’s worth. Just as things were going Belmont and Maxwell’s way though their team work fell through and the two was attacking each other. Maxwell even nailed Belmont with his Clouded Mind DDT. The referee has no choice but to call for a no contest when Maxwell locks his own tag team partner in Fatal Mistake. After the match Tota gave Maxwell a chair shot to the back of the head. Tota snaps on Maxwell and locks him in the The Tota Destruction camel clutch despite the bell ringing. Tota is finally pulled off Maxwell by Poe. Belmont and Maxwell were completely destroyed tonight. Overall: Great match that almost stole the whole show. The four gave it their all but Peter and Tristan just was not on the same page. Tota and Poe looked awesome during the match. After the match the camera switches to the medical room where Tessa Windsor and Jackson Rust are receiving medical attention after their beat down from Sweet Illusion earlier. Chris Storm appeared to farther damper their mood as he announced that he had Peter Maxwell sign Chris Storm vs. Tessa Windsor for later on the show. Overall: Good build up for the upcoming match. Also nice to see what happened to Tessa and Rust after their match. Outside the arena an impromptu fight takes place between Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid and Carys. Carys, with shovel in hand, seemed to have the H.I.T.’s number up until the Hardcore was able to score one of his trademark mathematical moves. Thinking he had dealed with Carys HIT tries to leave only to catch a shovel to the back of the head. Finally HIT is able to hit his Trigonometric Series on Carys and walk away. Overall: Rather short fight with very limited moves. Not the best start to what could be a great Hardcore division but I have a feeling this will not be the end of these types of fights in RPW. Back in the ring the former Schmoopies face off against each other. Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor try to fight but having worked along side each other for so long it was nearly impossible for one to surprise the other with a move. That is until Storm reached into his bag of tricks and started playing dirty. The two exchanged near falls before Chris decided he had enough wrestling and went for the steel chair. Giving Tessa too much idle time the Tessa Bear was able to take back the advantage and nearly put away Chris Storm. Tessa tried for her Fatal Storm but Chris Storm reversed it into a Powerbomb followed by his Bite the Dust submission hold. With the match seemingly won it was a surprise when the Oompa Loompa once more chased after Storm causing the Perfect Storm to release the hold and flee the match causing himself to be counted out and giving Tessa Windsor the win. Overall: Nice little match. At least it didn’t end in a no contest which I thought it would. The Oompa Loompa angle has really been well played out. Very nice match though from the two former tag team partners. Hopefully we can see a real match between the two in the future. In the Celestials locker room they celebrate their recent victory over Jason Marah. The celebration was short lived as one of the members of the Celestial told how though they beat him down and got him fired it still was not enough. They needed to defeat him in the ring to finally prove Marah was never a match. Cyclonia excuses herself to go see someone. Overall: Nicely done and great job of keeping up with everyone. It was very interesting and well thought out. Back in Rush’s locker room Karina Wolfenden pays him a visit. She yells at him for giving Magnum the chance. Rush tries to rationally explain things but Karina is too far emotional to even listen to his words. Karina even threats to quit. After telling Mason she can’t beat Magnum she makes her leave. Overall: Incredible segment. Magnum Wolf was not even there and it helped put him over more as a heel. Great emotion coming from Karina in this one. I am left not having any clue what will happen from here on out. The camera cuts to the parking lot were Jackson Rust and Tessa Windsor is planning to leave. Rust and Tessa seem to share a moment up until Tessa reveals she still loves Chris Storm. Rust gives her a hug and looks to try for a kiss but Tessa pushes him away. Jackson leaves Tessa alone which turned out to be a huge mistake as Chris Storm was waiting for the right moment. He covers Tessa’s mouth and gags her until she passes out. Jackson sees what is going on and tries to make the save but it is too late. Chris Storm nearly runs Rust over with his car and leaves him but only after rubbing salt in his wounds. Overall: Big turn around here, just when Chris had been playing a fool all night he turns around and kidnaps Tessa. I can’t wait to see what happens with this next week. In a shocking twist Cyclonia ask Rush to give Jason’s job back. Rush refuses but does agree to give Marah and Akiyama an unsanctioned match at the PPV. Cyclonia leaves and Rush is visited by another person who is ready to debut on RPW television. Overall: I can’t wait for the PPV now. I am really enjoying the Marah/Akiyama fued thus far. I am sure they will do a great job at the PPV. Finally it’s time for the MAIN EVENT! The Peter Maxwell Classic. Peter Maxwell is the first person to appear and once again has the World title with him. He lays it down on the mat and motions for the first wrestler to appear. When none does Peter makes a surprise announcement that the entire thing was a joke and Peter Maxwell is making himself the first ever World Heavyweight Champion! Gideon Poe comes out with Totaxedo Salvador and exchanges words with Maxwell until El Diablo appears claiming that he is the only one deserving of the belt. Suddenly Tristan Belmont appears with steel chair in hand and lays out Tota. A brawl breaks out among all the men. The lights go out and a voice calls out stopping the fighting. The lights come back on revealing it was just a kid! In a shocking turn of events it is announced that the 16 year old kid is actually the new owner of RPW, Little Al! Quick to make matches for the PPV and next week Little Al announces at Genesis there will be an Elimination match to decide the number one contender involving Maxwell, Poe, Tota, Diablo, and Tristan. Also next Adrenaline Poe and Tota will face off as will El Diablo and Tristan. Plus Peter Maxwell will fight SILVIO ROCHELLI! The show comes to an end as everyone is left shocked, especially Peter Maxwell! Overall: WOW! Who would have ever guessed the new owner would be a 16 year old kid. But what a great show he has booked for next week! I can’t wait to watch next week’s Adrenaline. Source: RPW
