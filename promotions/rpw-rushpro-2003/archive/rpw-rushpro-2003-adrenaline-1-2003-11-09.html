The opening pyro is immediately upon the viewers with a thunderous flash.  It seems no expense has been spared to provide the fans with a thrilling and visually stimulating introduction to Rush Pro-Wrestling.  There are pyrotechnics of every color, but the reds (the trademark color of RPW) stand out the most, lacing the Halton Arena with a red tint as explosives after explosives ring out, the crowd in a frenzy at the opening of this federation. Panning down to the announce table, we see an unusual sight.  There is only one man sitting, organizing his papers and watching in awe at the very impressive display.  He is very unremarkable.  His brown hair is neck-length, but well-groomed and he’s clean shaven, as well as fairly built for his small stature.  The only remarkable fact about this man is that he happens to be none other then Tyler Valentine, a former RUSH Wrestling World Champion during the establishment’s run in 2002. Tyler: Friends, I welcome you to the FIRST ever broadcast of Rush Pro-Wrestling.  I cannot explain to you how excited I am to be a part of this grand occasion.  As a former RUSH employee, I jumped at the chance to be involved in some fashion.  Unfortunately, a bum knee has stopped me from ever wrestling again, but I’ll be damned if I’m not gonna raise some hell of my own at this table! The pyro still isn’t quite done but it trails off, ending with one final loud bang as the crowd gives an extraordinary pop and that’s just for the light show. Tyler: Now, for anyone unfamiliar with the story of RPW, it’s a bit long, but in a nutshell, here goes:  Rush Mason, with money received from friends (namely, a large donation from Jeremy Howard, to be repaid in a percentage of quarterly profit) started this very federation. For this part of the broadcast, Tyler’s words are on the PA system, which is nifty for any fans not knowing the backstory to the federation. Tyler: Now, it was tough for everyone involved to see Rush trying so hard, but without a TV deal or sponsorship.  He was about to give up, but he stumbled upon Fox Sports South and they stumbled upon his idea and…well, what happened happened.  So, we gotta thank Fox Sports South for being able to broadcast to many more people then originally possible. There’s a small pop, but everyone gets the gist that TV was made to say that last line anyway, so it doesn’t need a giant hearty pop. Tyler: We had the TV deal but no place of operations and Rush wanted to stay local, near his home down here.  Of course, naturally the University of North Carolina-Charlotte was an immediate choice and luckily enough, they agreed to give us a great Sunday night primetime renting space for advertising and some of the gate.  Once again, many thanks to the University for that. Many of the fans being UNCC students, they Uni gets a pretty good pop for its help towards getting RPW off and running. Tyler: So, with that out of the way, it’s time to get things going.  We at RPW thank all of you in attendance for being here tonight.  Have a good night and enjoy the show, folks. With that, Tyler goes off-line on the PA and gets a standing ovation from the crowd, who for the most part know the troubles with his knee and how they ended what was once a very promising career.  Valentine gives a wave to the crowd and then settles into his seat, as do the fans, getting comfortable for the ride. The owner and president of Rush Pro Wrestling, Rush Mason, sits quietly as the shot opens.  He’s got a desk and chair in what looks to be a small office wing of the arena.  Of course, since RPW will be renting out the Halton every Sunday night, it’s no big deal that Rush has begun to get himself settled in already.  Of course, this is his first peace and quiet of the night, having been involved in this, that, and the other thing.  He’s been in the Halton Arena since noon today preparing for this giant occasion and finally there’s a minute of peace.  He leans back in the chair, gazing at the monitor enjoying the silence… A loud knock on the door disturbs Rush's peace, but before he can even answer to tell the person to come in, the door swings open and in steps El Diablo dressed smartly in a white shirt and black pants for RPW's opening night. Diablo walks straight up to Rush's desk and stands in a pose, like he expects to be hugged by Rush, only this isn't the case. El Diablo: Now do I look good or what? Again before Rush can respond, Diablo stops posing and starts talking again with a big smile on his face. El Diablo: Rush, it's good to see you again. It's been a long time since you first signed me up for RPW, and to be honest I'm glad you came to me, I was getting bored of sitting at home and doing nothing. Rush: Like I’ve said from day one, Raul, it’s my absolute pleasure to have you here.  I really do appreciate you and the pay cut you took to be here.  I can’t be sure, but my psychic powers tell me that you’ll be rewarded once the six-month report comes in and we renew all the contracts. Rush: Of course, I don’t really have psychic powers, I’m just saying…you know, like…yeah…so what did you need? Diablo's eyes gleam as he waits for Rush Mason's response to his gigantic request.  Of course, “requiring” something of your boss, especially a 6’4 280 pound boss, isn’t always the best move, but then again, El Diablo has never been known for common sense. Rush: Hmm…you require a spot in the main event, eh?
