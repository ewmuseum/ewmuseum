Unwanted Arrival Slowly a black stretch limo pulled into the parking lot of the RPW Arena in North Carolina, the windows tinted so that nobody can see in. The engine stopped running and soon enough the front door opened where a skinny white man stepped out. The man couldn't be any taller than 5'7 and must have weighed a feeble 135lbs, his hair was covered by a ridiculous gray and black drivers hat which matched the suit he was wearing, and on the suit was a name tag reading "Mark", he was certainly a professional. He slammed the front door shut and walked all the way to the back of the oversized limo and opened the door. Inside the limo was none other than El Diablo, the man personally responsible for the cancellation of Adrenaline by the Fox network. The limo he was in had 16 seats made of pure leather and the carpeting in the limo was pure velvet. Also in there was a stereo system which was playing loudly as the limo pulled in and of course a television on the right hand side which was located right by the alcohol and glasses. Diablo was wearing a custom made Armani tuxedo and had a glass of champagne in his hand which he promptly put to his lips and drank down. He slammed the glass down but not hard enough to break it and stepped out of the limo. "Ah Mark, today is a good day," Diablo exclaimed, "Today marks a new era for RPW, today marks a new era for El Diablo. Today there are no restrictions, and today RPW needs me as they need a top drawing star so the fans will come to watch while RPW suffers it's financial trouble," he finished with a smile. Mark just nodded as the well dressed El Diablo kept talking to him. "While RPW is suffering financially, I am doing great. I was on a fat check in PCW and spent very little of it, I'm on one of the highest salaries here in RPW and have spent little of it. So quietly I have built myself up a small fortune, I have near $5 million put aside and so now I can live it large at RPW's expense," The Mexican Devil Remarked, "But why would I do that? Why would I want RPW to suffer? BECAUSE RPW HAS MADE ME SUFFER! I should be RPW World Champion, I should have been from the very beginning, but RPW held me back, and so now I am holding RPW back," Diablo stated with malice. Diablo sat back down in the limo as Mark stayed by the limo door listening to every word El Diablo was saying to him, as after all it was his job to. And I know everyone is wondering now, why if I have this money am I still wrestling? Mark slammed the door shut and walked to the front again and turned on the engine, where the music started once more. A Fresh Start The people in the crowd were silent, as they saw former PCW star Matt Jackson enter silently backstage. It was his first night in Rush Pro Wrestling and he couldn’t wait to begin his tedious work of earning back the title the Bastard Child. It wouldn’t be hard to make these people hate him, to have them loathe him in the same way he loathed them. He had been out of the game for months. Yet he didn’t care, it was like riding a bike, once you learn – you never forget. Tonight was going to be the night that he hoped back on and began to peddle. He missed it – the challenge and games that he used to get to play. The psychotic torment he used to infringe on others. Something inside of Matt craved the burning sensation he felt when he was praying on the weaker. Everything changed when PCW closed though; he was just a regular guy, which was a sigh of relief, as he no longer had to deal with the troubled Silvio Rochelli who acted as if he was handled by a fourteen year old. It wasn’t the same though – each morning he woke up wanting something. He knew the thing that he wanted. He wanted to once again rehash the torment, he knew he was physically superior to most people and he just needed to prove it once again, but no one was willing to give him the chance. No one wanted a liability with the mind of a psycho path. They were fearful to sign the former student of Angelo Deville and Jason Snow; for fear that he had become too deranged. They were probably right about him. His chance hadn’t disappeared forever…RPW was his chance now. They longed to sign him for the cheap asking price he wanted, but little did they know he was willing to sign such a cheap contract, because he wanted to once again invade and destroy the minds of those who stood in front of him. Tonight was the night it all began. He had begged for his former girlfriend to fix-up a contract for the former A! superstar Jason Deline – to step into the ring with the student of Max Danger, his former idol. He knew he didn’t need to idolize Danger anymore and he was going to prove it tonight by beating his student. Tonight was a fresh start. Karina Wolfenden vs. Violence Jack Dimmu Borgar's "Puritania" played, the theme that meant Violence Jack was about to enter the Dale F. Halton Arena's ringside. There may have been no camera running, no sound truck parked outside, no production crew frantically running about behind the scenes... But one thing that hadn't changed was the fans' response to the theme; They jeered, HARD. The curtain was pulled apart for Jack's arrival, allowing him to march out straight onto the stage as his theme played out all around him. Booing meant nothing to him, he couldn't care less about kids giving him the finger, and even the lewd questioning of his mother's virtue failed to get any reaction. Violence Jack wasn't walking down to the ring to be wound up by Charlotte's natives, he was there to win the Eclipse Title... ...And use it as a symbol for his beliefs. "Ladies and Gentlemen," Jerry Quecard boomed, "the following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is for the RPW ECLIPSE CHAMPIONSHIP!!!" Into the ring Jack stepped, finally pulling down the hood of his robe to reveal his marked face. "Introducing first, the CHALLENGER!" Jerry continued as Jack paced around him, unsettling the ring announcer. "Weighing in tonight at 234 pounds, he is Boston's BASTARD Son... He is VIOLENCE JACK!!!" Jack's arms thrust skywards, sending the robe falling down behind him. Despite their hatred for him, the RPW fans couldn't pass the photo opportunity, and their camera flashes acted like strobes, flickering the ring with light. Quecard tried not to look Jack in the eye as the challenger turned to him, giving a disapproving look to the RPW staffer. "Hide YOUR Face FOR Ever" No matter who was in the ring, the dreamy words of Sandra Nasic always made the RPW fans explode, and there was no exception for Violence Jack. "Dream AND Search FOR Ever" RPW's barely adequate PA system struggled to do the song justice as it boomed out around the Dale F. Halton Arena, the sound guys seemingly feeling the theme was deserving of a little extra volume. The fans didn't cheer it for the band, or the music, they cheered for the woman it ushered out every week, their Eclipse Daredevil, RPW's X-Athlete, the K-Wolf... ...Karina Wolfenden. "And his opponent," announced Jerry, struggling to be heard over the pumped-out music and roaring fans. "From Albany, New York... Weighing in at 153 pounds... She is the RPW ECLIPSE CHAMPION, KARINA WOLFENDEN!!!" At RPW's first ever card, in the main event of their first ever TV show, Karina Wolfenden won the RPW Eclipse Championship in her usual do-or-die style... Now, 10 weeks later, with the TV cameras gone, at the start of a new story for RPW... She'd fight to defend it with all she had. The curtain was sent flying, the tell tale sign Wolfenden had arrived, and not one to disappoint, she bounded out onto the stage. With the Eclipse Championship slung casually, yet with pride, over her shoulder, the whirling dervish of blonde, pink and black made her way down to the ring. Gloved hands tried to slap as many fan's hands as possible on the way to the ring, but although she wanted to show her appreciation to the supportive crowd, there was a larger task at hand. A larger, and far more dangerous task. Last week, Little Al - under the influence of Magnum Wolf - took rules out of the Eclipse Equation. Count-Outs were no longer a factor, and the ending couldn't be left clouded by a Disqualification... Eclipse was now all abut getting the pinfall or submission with everything at your disposal, a philosophy that seemed to suit Jack far more than the K-Wolf. Tentatively, Karina hopped up on to the apron, clearly showing caution. She'd seen what Jack was capable off, and Kari was going to do all she could to avoid being another of his victims. Then, rather than watching him like a Hawk, the K-Wolf turned her back on him, thrusting the Eclipse Title into the air. That's right, she turned her back on probably RPW's most dangerous man. The faintest of smiles on VJ's face said it all, and he made a lunge straight for Wolfenden, trying to send her flying from the apron into the guard rail. "No ya don't," Karina then quipped flinging her right elbow back to catch Violence Jack right in the mouth. Eclipse was all about using what you had, and Kari certainly had her wits about her. Instinctively, VJ grabbed his mouth, giving Karina the chance to lasso him with her belt, pulling his head over the top rope... Then dropping down to the apron, yanking his throat down across the cable with a Hotshot. The belt ensuring he couldn't escape, Jack was flung back as Kari released it's strap, the challenger gasping for air. With a sly punch of the air, Kari turned then leapt up onto the apron, raring to follow her trap up. Jack was back up to one knee, holding his throat... Then as soon as he stood, Karina sprung up, using the rope's elasticity to launch herself feet-first at Jack, so both feet could be pumped right into his face. Karina wouldn't know an Exploder Suplex from an Ganso Bomb, or an Emerald Fusion from a Diamond Dust, but she knew how to kick someone in the head. Violence Jack was sent flying backwards, both hands slapping down onto his face while Karina managed to land, crouched, but on her feet. Anyone who'd been formally trained would have gone for the pin, just to force Jack to kick out if anything, but the K-Wolf was far from formal. Before she would have even had a chance to cover anyway, Jack rolled onto his front and began to get to his knees... ...And everything the K-Wolf did from there, the whole crowd could of called. After flinging herself into the ropes, Karina ran up behind Jack, planting her left hand as a pivot so her body could be swung around into a Handstand 619, only for Jack to duck her legs. Kari swung through and landed on her knees in front of a rising Jack. Thinking quickly, she rested her forearm pads against the canvas as and thrust her body vertically up, flinging her right foot over her head with a Scorpion Kick, catching an off-guard Jack on the jaw. It seemed her feet were locked onto Jack's jaw, and he couldn't shake them. Stunned by the kick that hit him seemingly out of nowhere, Jack dropped down to his knees, setting it all up again. Karina bounced off the ropes in front of him, seemingly trying to build up extra speed, but as she passed him, the K-Wolf planted her hand and spun her body around... Landing her Handstand 619 Kick to the back of Violence Jack's head instead. "Umph," Jack expelled as he was knocked down flat against the mat, having been caught totally off guard by Kari's improvisation. As dangerous as Violence Jack was, the K-Wolf was keeping him subdued, her kicks preventing him getting a telling blow in. Karina looked around the ring, wondering what to do next before she hopped through the ropes and out onto the apron. Jack rose, grabbing the back of his throbbing skull while Wolfenden executed a springboard onto the top rope, then flew off towards him. Keeping her body vertical in the air, Karina looked as if she's flown too high to hit a rising Jack, but as she thrust both feet out to her right hand side, they caught Jack perfectly in the chest. The impact sent a winded VJ flying back, falling through the ropes and down to the floor while Karina just managed to land safely on her feet. A giant pop went out as the fans saw Jack hit the mats at ringside, while Karina punched the air and tried to squeeze even more noise from the already lively crowd. On the floor, a stunned Violence Jack shot up, although he looked unsteady on his feet. Knowing she had to keep the pressure on, Karina crouched, waiting for her opponent to get back onto the apron. The Champion was in the ring, and the Challenger needed to beat her, so Jack crawled back onto the apron, pulling himself up using the ropes. Without having anything planned, Karina charged at the ropes, literally flinging herself like a rocket through the ropes and into Jack, connecting with both fists to his abdomen. Kari's momentum sent her falling down to the floor along with Violence Jack, both crashing into the mats by the aisle. "KA-RI-NA!" the fans started up, giving her their full support in trying to beat the detested Violence Jack. Her improvisational move looked more effective than it was, and Jack was able to rise alongside the K-Wolf. Not wanting to be stuck on the outside, Karina went to grab Jack to roll him back inside, but was shoved away... Right into Superkick range. Jack thrust his right foot up towards her chin, only for the K-Wolf to execute a lightning-quick backflip, taking her out of range. Realising that getting close to Jack wasn't such a good idea, Karina reverted back to her usual tactics, springing up onto the apron. Jack went to charge her, but the K-Wolf leapt off onto his shoulders and flung her body back for a Huricanrana... But Jack wasn't moved from his feet. Not wanting to be left hanging, Karina demonstrated her own strength by sitting back up, allowing her to go for the Huricanrana once more, only for the same to happen. Knowing he had the Eclipse Champion hanging, vulnerable, Jack didn't wait for Karina to pull herself up, and spun around sharply to his left, swinging the K-Wolf's helpless body into the steel guard-rail. Kari just got her hands up to protect her head, but being whacked against the metal took it's toll. Even through her legs went limp around his head, Jack held on, building up steam and swinging her body once more into the rail. "Booooooooooooooooooooooooooooo!" echoed through the Dale F. Halton Arena, united in their hate for Violence Jack as he dumped Karina down onto the mats. Hurting for where each metal bar slammed against her, Karina tried to crawl towards the ring. Despite the barrage of kicks he was subjected to, Violence Jack didn't seem effected by any of them, shrugging the effects of every connection off in a matter of seconds. With Kari crawling, Jack stalked her, moving around so he could pull her off the floor with a Gutwrench... And lift Wolfenden through into the rail, her back taking the impact. Karina was again dropped to the mats, her face showing the pain from Jack's crude offence. Before she even had a chance to recover from the move, Jack's knee was thrust at her face, Karina taking it right on the cheekbone... The impact sending her head back and into the steel once more, the guard-rail seemingly acting as Jack's tag team partner. Laying on the mats, Karina grabbed the back of her head as the sharp pain pulsed from it, dulling her senses enough so that Jack could leap up, then crash down into her with a vicious Elbow Drop. Taking a step back as he rose, Jack just gestured for his opponent to get up and fight him, and act that endeared him even less to the RPW fans. Not wanting to stay down, vulnerable on the floor, Karina stumbled up and towards the ring... But Jack grabbed the back of her hair, yanking her straight back down with the Osaka Send-Off. Wolfenden felt the concrete through the thin mat as her head hit the floor, the impact causing her teeth to jar. Her head throbbed, pulses of pain working their way from the back of her skull around to her eye sockets. Jack looked to her face, and just from the expression he knew she was weakened... And he could use everything in his sick mind to keep her that way. He grabbed a handful of the K-Wolf's pink and blonde hair, yanking her to her feet to avoid losing clumps of her mane. Before she could fend him off, Jack tossed her into the railing, then pushed her throat down across the top bar. Kari's limbs lashed about as Jack's pressure cut off her air... Which she had been gasping to get into her lungs. It wasn't long before she stopped struggling, apparently having passed out. Jack released and took a step back, not following up for some reason. Suddenly, Karina's eyes shot open and she spun back to face her challenger... Only to take a Thrust Kick to the side of the head that sent her flying back over the guard rail and into the crowd. Violence Jack wasn't stupid. He knew Karina was one of the fittest people in RPW, and there was no way she'd have passed out after 15 seconds. She was sly to fake it, but he took the credit for knowing she had. Looking out into the tightly packed crowd, Jack knew following Karina into them would be a bad idea... He had no friends there, and they'd do everything they could to protect their favourite. Instead of pursuing, Jack instead approached the rail and waited for Karina to crawl back towards it. Dropping down to his knees, Jack reached through the bars and grabbed a handful of Karina's hair... Then yanked her forward so that her forehead hit into the steel. His left hand had a tight grip on her pink fringe, and as much as Karina struggled, VJ managed to keep her face pulled against the bars... Allowing him to send a series of right hands through the gap and onto her face, her hands unable to properly block any of them. As he released her hair, Karina slumped onto the floor, rolling onto her back. Thinking quickly, Jack grabbed the top of the rail and lifted it off the floor, pushing it so it was held over Karina's face... ...Then slamming it down. Knowing she didn't have a chance of pulling her head out of the way, all Karina could do was close her eyes and rapidly twist her head, pressing her left cheek hard against the cold concrete. The crowd gasped, praying it wouldn't crush her head... And they got their wish, the bottom of the rail having been stopped by it's supports just a few millimetres away from the K-Wolf prominent right cheekbone. Jack looked visibly frustrated, and grabbed the rail, lifting it up again but deciding to throw it down sidewards into Karina's body... ...Causing her to quickly roll back and thrust her feet up as a defence. Seeing his arms weren’t winning the battle against Karina's legs, a snarling Jack released, but not without taking the chance to send a quick stomp to her face as she pushed the railing away with her feet. Again by the hair, Jack pulled Karina up and dragged her towards the ring.... Ramming her spine-first into the apron instead of throwing her in. While Karina winced and tried to hold herself up, Jack slid into the ring and stood... Making it clear to everyone that he, the challenger, was the one standing tall in the ring. Rising to Jack's inadvertent challenge, a dogged Karina pulled herself onto the apron using the ropes, determined to stand up and face him... But VJ was quick to pounce, pulling Karina up over the ropes and onto his shoulders... ... And into a Torture Rack. Before Karina could even offer the weakest of blows in opposition, Jack swung her down into the Detonator Delta, the impact bouncing Karina over onto her back. A confident Jack didn't even move around to cover the K-Wolf securely, instead choosing to just lean back and grab the nearest leg. 1... 2... 3-NO!!! Jack reacted to the slapping of the 3, but then had his victory hurriedly cancelled as the official spotted Karina's foot on the ropes... The only way she'd of ever 'kicked out' from the vicious move. Standing, VJ glared at the referee, visibly intimidating him, but Faircount stood firm, holding up the 2... Although he was backing away the whole time. Turning back to Karina, Jack dropped to one knee and again pulled her up by the hair. Kari's legs looked weak below her, and noticing the fact, Jack let go of her mane as soon as she was vertical... Sending Karina slumping back down to the mat. Almost disappointed with her, Jack shook his head, but gave her no mercy as he yanked her into a front facelock, then in the blink of an eye span Karina roughly down onto the canvas with a Fisherman's Swinging Neckbreaker - an Ode to Azathoth. Jack was cold and methodical, knowing each of his moves packed enough punch to keep the Eclipse Champion subdued. As the challenger paced around the broken body of their favourite, the crowd cheered Kari on rather than jeer Jack, trying to inspire something through the pain and grogginess... But the emergence of a grinning figure on the stage changed that, causing the Dale F. Halton Arena to direct their hate up at the man... ...Magnum Wolf. Wolf knew there were no disqualifications, meaning there was nothing to stop him sliding into the ring and delivering the knock-out blow himself... Other than his own conscience, and that was hardly a tough barrier to break through. Soda cups rained down on him as he strutted down to the ring, attracting the attention of both Violence Jack and the referee... Neither of whom seemed too please to see him. As he reached ringside, Magz walked around to the time-keepers table and grabbed one of the spare chairs, folding it up and tapping it with satisfaction against the ring post... Taking in the metallic chink. "Tie 'er up," Wolf yelled to Jack, who was still more than frosty in his reception of Wolf. Violence Jack knew he could beat her without Wolf's help, he had her virtually unconscious already... But Jack was a brutal and malicious man, so if he was going to take Karina's belt away, why not add a little more insult into the mix by allowing her nemesis to help. Kari's limp body was easy to pull up, allowing VJ to effortlessly carry her to the ropes. After draping her arms over the top rope, he pushed her down while yanking the middle up... ...Clamping Wolfenden's thin arms between the tight cables. The vice-like grip of the ropes digging into her arms seemed to be the only thing keeping Karina up as she slumped down, almost sitting on the bottom rope. Wolf slid into the ring, not taking his eyes off Karina, who's face was nearly all obscured by her pink fringe while her head was hung forward. Jack walked back towards Karina and grabbed her hair, roughly yanking her head up so that she was looking right at Wolf... ...And just the mere sight of him seemed to bring her back to life, furiously lashing her legs about and trying in vain to free herself. A frustrated Chris Faircount tried to get Wolf out of the ring, but Magz knew the stipulations of Eclipse matches, as he'd recommended them to Little Al himself. Jack remained knelt in front of Karina, wanting to watch her face as the chair shot blanked it... The chair shot that Wolf was preparing to deliver. Like a batter ready to swing, Magnum pulled the weapon back over his shoulder... ...THEN BROUGHT IT CRASHING DOWN OVER THE HEAD OF VIOLENCE JACK! Jack hadn't even seen the chair as it swung down, and only realised what was happening a split second before impact, reading the expression of Karina's face. The hard blow right to the back of his head sent Jack's eyes rolling back, and his body dropping backwards onto the mat. Not knowing whether to cheer or jeer, the crowd just settled for shocked silence, trying to read Wolf's body language to see what he was up to. Looking at her nemesis in amazement, Karina couldn't form any words, but her expression clearly questioned "Why?". Magnum looked down at Jack, tossed the chair away, shoved the protesting referee aside, then left... Not even looking at the bemused Karina, thus shrouding his act in mystery. Without looking back, Magnum walked up at the aisle, even dropping his head to avoid seeing the snared K-Wolf on the RUSH ProVision. Karina kept trying in vain to free herself, knowing that Faircount couldn't intervene given the rulings. She could see a furious Jack coming to and trying to pull himself up, the chair only a few feet away from him... If he got back in the game before she could escape, Karina knew she'd be waking up in hospital, if at all. Suddenly, her struggling stopped, and she composed herself, steadying her breathing and talking to herself under her breath. Jack got to one knee, grabbing the top of his head where the dull throb penetrated even his high pain threshold. Seeing he was nearly up, Karina made her move, thrusting herself upwards and kicking off from the mat with her powerful legs, sending her back-flipping over the ropes so that they freed their grip on her arms. Landing perfectly on the apron, Karina grabbed the shaking top rope and sprung onto it with a lightning-quick Springboard... ...Then hopped onto the just-risen Jack's shoulders, quickly snapping her body back into a Huricanrana, guiding Jack's head onto the chair with a twist of her hips. Reaching back, Karina grabbed both of VJ’s legs then leant forward as far as she could without losing her balance, locking her challenger in tightly. Faircount slid over and saw both VJ's shoulders down, prompting a count. 1... 2... 3!!!! They may not have understood how it came to happen, but the crowd stood and one and gave out an inspiring cheer for Karina as she released her grip and dropped forwards down onto the mat. Behind her, Jack wasn't moving, just looking up at the arena lights with his fists tightly clenched. Seeing that she wanted out of the ring quickly, Faircount was quick to hand Karina her belt, and fulfil the obligatory raising of her arm... Allowing her to hurriedly slide out under the bottom rope and stumble to the back. "The winner of this match," Quecard boomed from ringside, "and STILL RPW ECLIPSE CHAMPION.... KARINA WOLFENDEN!!!"
