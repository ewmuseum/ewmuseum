Front Page Updates

7 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own front page as the Wayback Machine captured it on 7 June 2004 at its first address, rpw.sheepsden.com (our capture, lot 0178): the webmaster’s eight short update notes of 14 March to 9 May 2004, each with its own date line and heading, newest first. The page’s seven longer news posts are served on their own pages; its navigation menu, images and links are not reproduced.
Sunday, May 09, 2004 Adrenaline finally airs. After a hellacious week on the UNCC Campus, thanks to a watermain break, Adrenaline finally aired. As a result, the coming weeks PPV, has been pushed back a week, to May 23rd. THere's too much to talk about that happened, so go read the show, DAMNIT! Monday, April 26, 2004 You call that Cancer?! On the latest edition of Adrenaline, Rush Mason's proposed Cancer turned out to be something a bit more benign. It's not Cassius Deamont, nor is it Status Quo, but he's definately a threat to RPW. ALso: The new World Champion had lots to say, and I'll finish this later. I'm tired of looking at text. Tuesday, April 20, 2004 NEW RPW WORLD CHAMPION?! Nah, Just kidding. OR AM I?! Seriously. It didn't happen...OR DID IT?! HMMM? Nah. Come on. Who could beat Gideon Poe? Magnum Wolf? Nerezza? Violence Jack? They'd all met Poe before and come up short, why would tonight be any different? UNLESS THERE'S A SWERVE?! EVER THINK OF THAT YOU COCKSUCKING FUCKERS?! Thank you, Gabe. ADrenaline was action packed, and there is indeed a new RPW World Champion. Just kidding... OR AM I? Tuesday, April 13, 2004 Kannon Effect: Part 1 The Twentieth Edition of RPW's Adrenaline came and went Monday Night, thanks to the Easter Holiday. Christian Kannon, known through the world formerly as Xavier Kannon, had a profound effect, even putting himself in the Main Event against Gideon Poe and Jason O'Neil. O'Neil barely made it to the match, thanks to a horriffic injury that could have further impact on his dream and desire to be the RPW world champion. Carys took on Quinton Dufresne, for the Eclipse title, Violence Jack and Scott Matthews continued the war of words in the ring, Chris Storm was suprised when his investment didn't pay off, and Tessa Windsor very nearly had her head taken off her body. So much action to talk about, why not read it yourself? Monday, April 05, 2004 Financial Backer Revealed! Just who is this attractive woman in the picture to the left? You'll have to read Adrenaline to find out. Let's just say that things are about to get real, real interesting in RPW now. Also: Jason O'Neil and Chris Storm wrestled to find out just who can lay claim to the best technical wrestler in the world today, A new recruit from the former OSE showed his face, and gave the fans at ringside some High Sixes; Tessa Windsor faced her most grueling day as an RPW wrestler, and Magnum Wolf sicked the entire crowd. Don't believe me? Click here. It's the first segment. Thursday, April 01, 2004 Adrenaline 18 Pete got lazy and forgot to link it. Sorry, you little jerks. Here ya go. Monday, March 22, 2004 Adrenaline #17 - The FINAL Adrenaline?? One week after Maximum Overdrive, a shocking announcement was made, that Rush Mason has not only returned to RPW, but also has taken control of the company. He also had big news for everyone in RPW about the future of the company. Magnum Wolf proved to be the Magnum Wolf of old, as he took on Vangellus Oxios, Little AL's favorite proved to be an "Old School" Hero, and Chris Storm got himself into quite a bit of hot water. Invincibleness went crazy, and Gabe had a rap battle with an unlikely opponent. Check it out! Sunday, March 14, 2004 Overdrive in the Books! Aside from minor technichal difficulties during the Peter Maxwell and Dread Pirate Roberts match, Maximum Overdrive went off with nary a hitch. Tristan Belmont met with a new superstar in RPW. Did Matt Jackson's transformation go off well with Kohana? Or Did Kohana make his presence known? Could Chris Storm pull off his match with Chryste? Or is there a new champion? Did Poe make it out of the evening alive? Are there new tag champs? What happened during the Maxwell/Roberts match*? Did Magnum Wolf actually fight Karina Wolfenden? All these questions and more are answered HERE! *Note: All replays and future editions will contain the match.
