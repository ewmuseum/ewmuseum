What this is The fed’s own TV Archive index with its Champions and Contenders page, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 10 December 2003 (our capture, lot 0178). The page’s navigation menu, images and links are not reproduced; its text is. It lists the cards of Adrenaline #1 and #2, the news and rumor headlines of 8 November to 3 December 2003 and the three belts’ holders and contenders as they stood that December: the RUSH Heavyweight and Tag Team titles with no holder named, and the Eclipse title held by Ilan Levin.
