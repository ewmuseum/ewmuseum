DO NOT DISTURB "I wouldn't go in there if I were you..." Ever since his plans for Brian Arson crumbled around him 7 days ago, Christian Alexander Kannon-Hall had not been the most approachable of men. Not only had he seen Magnum Wolf once more make a bold statement on Adrenaline, but the one woman who could have soothed his mood was off managing Gaz "The Jester" Green, a man that it could safely be said that Kannon couldn't stand. Now was not the best time to be an RPW backstage worker, especially one being nagged by all parties. "Well what am I meant to do, E-Mail him?" The already stressed runners and production workers didn't need the further complication of an un-approachable boss, but it looked like that was what they were going to get. "I've got 90% of the locker room chipping in to buy out Wolf's contract," the highly-strung worker began, gesturing to the thick batch of notes and memos in his hand, "there have been 3 reports of Karina Wolfenden threatening to kick wrestlers’ balls out of their throats, Gaz Green has supposedly forced half the tag division out of their locker rooms to make way for his 'entourage', security have a guy held for selling fake tickets on campus, and the lighting director is asking why the strobes only half work... In that they're off." And the show hadn't even begun yet. "Well if it's alright by you," the other worker appealed, "I'd rather not be out of a job when Kannon snaps and fires everyone who's pissed him off... I still have next year's tuition to pay!" Left alone, the runner stared at the thick wad of paper that was causing his hand to ache, then to Kannon's door, behind it an ominous silence, far from the usual frantic scene it was most Sundays. Gulping, the young man took a step forward and went to knock... Before pulling short, worried what barrage of abusive language he'd be dismissed with. "Best just to dart in, drop off, and get the hell out," the young man decided, taking a deep breath and wishing his heart would stop pounding. "Here we go, this'll be easy..." As soon as the handle was twisted, the door was pushed open and filled with a fast pace from the worker... "GET OUT!!!" ...Who was then expelled as quickly as he entered by Kannon's supremely dominant roar. Staggering back, the runner's eyes then shot wide open as a paperweight was hurled in his direction, causing the young man's sense of self-preservation to kick in. As he hit the deck promptly, the pile of papers went sent flying up into the air, a few plucked out by the paperweight, which then crashed against the wall, sending lumps of plaster cascading down to the floor. "Fuck this..." the panicked worker surrendered with, scurrying down the corridor on all fours while the papers he was meant to deliver floated down onto the floor. SLAM! Making sure everyone heard his door close, Kannon went back to his self-imposed isolation, hoping that people had gotten the message. SUPER PREP TO THE RESCUE! Lifting the emerald lenses of his sunglasses over his forehead, the Jester peered forward, his eyes roaming over the platoon of people standing before him, each of whom different from the next. Like a squadron of soldiers, they stood motionless, their legs parted to the hours of five and seven… And not ten and two like some of the females parading themselves here in Rush Pro Wrestling. Getting back to the point, these persons stood with their arms tucked behind their backs and the only signs of movement featured came from their eyes swinging in a pendulum motion as they tracked their commanding officer, Gaz "The Jester" Green. "For the past six months you people have kept this picture of perfection looking like an A-list celebrity and I know this isn't Hollywood, where are we again?" Questioned Gaz as he tilted his head in the direction of the gentleman standing at his side. Adorning an iron pressed suit stood the Jester's agent, who replied to his client, "Sir, Charlotte, North Carolina, Sir!" "Well for that, I apologise. This place has a strange perception of the word 'talent'. Take this one hometown lad these people… and I use the term people loosely… idolise. Each and every week he climbs to the second turnbuckle and then bang, vertigo hits. Like a drama queen he stands there screaming, 'AAAAARRRRRGGGGGHHHHH!' before putting his fingers to the side of his head like some Kurt Cobain re-enactment and then does this mid-air toilet break, which I think is suppose to be a leg drop. IDIOT!"   The entourage of personnel starred blankly in response. They had no clue what the movie star was rambling on about, unlike the live crowd who erupted into a chorus of jeers, their opinions however quarantined to the ringside area and thus falling on deaf ears. "Now listen and listen good. You're employed by ME and nobody, I mean nobody else. So, should I find one of you make-up artists so much as lend blusher to another then I will personally see to it that you will never work in Hollywood again and that your next job is face painting in some chapel of rest. That's about it!" Sneered Gaz. If he were to work for RPW then he would want to look his best, which didn't include being overshadowed by any other, male or female. "Now… If there aren't any questions…" Said Gaz allowing for a brief pause. "This is only a two hour show so I suggest we first get acquainted with my locker-room and after you make-up people have weaved your magic and you clerical squires have double checked that my contract has more zeros than the binary code of a television set, then I shall acquaint myself with Eleanor Hall's humble abode!" Concluded the former PCW and OSE starlet with a smirk. Looking every bit like a tour the various professionals headed up by Gaz Green ventured from the chill of the open planned parking area and into the warmth of the Halton Arena. The Jester cockily strutted forward and into the building passing security on his way, even offering a cheeky wink as he did so, security however could muster no more than puzzled glances as one, two, three… ten or so individuals passed them by. "I don't pay you people to stare, it's not like you haven't seen a pair of monkeys before!" He stabbed back at his followers. Security offered a little more than puzzlement this time as they glared angrily at Gaz in retort to his comment. Gaz though had his back turned and continued into the arena with a skip when suddenly he stopped abruptly, had the Jester felt the eyes of security burning into his back or was it something else? It was definitely something else, seven foot of something else actually. "…" "Isn't that Hessian?" Murmured a voice amongst the Jester's staff. The Goliath stood silently before the entourage his log-like arms crossed over his chest. The Mercenary Of Satan looked to be pondering as you'd expect from somebody who had severed all ties with humanity, but this was the first time in a long time that the paths of both student and mentor had crossed. That's right, Hessian schooled Gaz Green during his Primetime Wrestling Academy stint just in case that question should surface during Adrenaline 30's feedback thread. "Ah! Long time, no see squire!" Said Gaz nervously. Hessian's presence made the comic feel a little uneasy. However, this encounter seemed different, not because of the audience but Hessian's state of mind. There was a sense of weakness and the Jester preyed on the weak - Just ask Tessa Windsor and more recently, Christian Alexander Kannon. The Jester had masqueraded for weeks that he was above RPW and everything associated with it, and now he stood before the biggest dog in the yard. No question, he was intimidated, but Hessian seemed uninterested by his company, allowing his opportunist student the chance to strike. "Something seems amiss Angelus, and it isn't a David Boreanaz lawsuit neither. What can it be? Can it be the Minion The Footstool? Nah, that isn't it. What about the rest of your mask, which you seem to have mislaid? Nope. Oh what can it be Kelsig?" Said Gaz in a patronising manner. "Could it be your monotone one liners, ETERNAL DAMNATION this and ETERNAL DAMNATION that" Like a snake striking, Hessian's hand shot forward, his fingers wrapping around the scrawny neck of his victim, Gaz "The Jester" Green. The behemoth lifted his prey towards himself, the tips of the Jester's feet scraping back and forth along the tiled floor as he did so. "I don't remember you being so talkative in class… Squire… Would you like to feel Eternal Damnation?" He asked. "NOT AT ALL!" Cried the comic. "Err, I was just commenting on the lack of gold around your waist, specifically that Global .Jpeg Championship you were robbed of…" "GLOBAL WHAT?" Roared the Titanic Satanic Insaniac. The Jester could only splutter in response as the giant's hand began to clench, but something stopped him from squeezing every last breath from his victim. "LIKE, SO PUT HIM DOWN OR ELSE!!" Hessian tilted his head left to see one Eleanor Kannon-Hall standing in a confident pose, her hands locked to her slender hips. Had Gaz been saved by a Kannon? "LISTEN VAN KELLOGS, PUT MY CHAMPION DOWN. HE HAS AN IMPORTANT MATCH TONIGHT, OKAY?" Barked Ellie in a pissed off preppy tone normally unheard unless Gap were closed early. As ordered, Hessian reluctantly released Gaz Green, who immediately went about camouflaging his ordeal with a tug of his emerald jacket and the last say, "You were lucky the lady showed up when she did" "Yes, lady luck indeed shone down upon you, Green. However, lightning never strikes in the same place twice and I'd much prefer to see you ravaged by the pack of wolves you have the misfortune of facing later tonight!" Hissed Hessian in retort, for it was the Goliath who would have the last laugh in this exchange. With that, Angelus Von Kelsig departed, leaving Gaz to deal with Eleanor, "Like, who are these peeps?" "Err, my personal staff…" And even before he could explain further Ellie had cried two words that sent a chill down his spine, and it wasn't Magnum Wolf. "YOU'RE FIRED~!" From make-up artist to clerical assistant to agent, the cries of "WHAT?" Resounded from each and every one of them. The Jester's new manager had just fired his entire team; taking on Ellie now didn't seem quite the good idea. With no Christian Alexander Kannon around Gaz had no real need for Eleanor and this was only the first week of their arrangement, but apparently she had a need for him. "You can't really fire them, can you?"
