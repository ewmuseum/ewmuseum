Finders Keepers The show opens on a small crowd trying to filter into the Dale F. Halton Arena in time for 'first-bell'. Checking their watches and craning their necks to see over those in front, the impatient fans jostle for position. Then, breaking up the hurried hum, a man's vocal protesting is heard from just inside the entrance. "I TOLD YOU, I HAVE A TICKET... BUT I LO-... UM, THE DOG ATE IT!" With 2 bulky security personnel dragging him by the upper-arms, a red-faced Magnum Wolf is reluctantly being expelled from the building... RPW seemingly keeping good it's word not to let him purchase a ticket. Magnum: Get Mason on the radio, he's my gulp pal! Yeah, we get on like a cross on fire... Even though he's black! That's the kinda swell guy I am. Some of my great grandfather's best slaves were blacks, ya know? Unimpressed by Magnum, the security duo continue to drag him through the crowd, while Wolf struggles, sending several fans tumbling over. Magnum: Look! They can sue you know! How's your boyfriend going to react when you get home with a million pound lawsuit? Both security guards look to one another and nod... Before literally tossing Wolf head-first out of the crowd. Magz crashes down onto the ground with a 'hmph', before bellowing out to all who'll listen. Magnum: BRUTALITY! BRUTALITY I SAY! The crowd who're still filtering into the building turn their noses up at Magnum, while those he knocked over dust themselves off, snarling at the unemployed wrestler. Security chuckle at seeing the down-trodden Wolf trying to clean himself off. "As we said.... No ticket, no way in." The pair walk back inside, happy with themselves, while Wolf mutters to himself under his breath. Dragging himself up, Wolf turns and flashes a seething look towards security... But his attention is caught by something left behind by the crowd. Magnum: Oh, SCORE! He scrambles over and grabs the dirtied ticket that was knocked free from one of the crowd during Wolf's struggle, before hurriedly checking it's date... It's for tonight. Magnum: .... EHEM! With looks that scream 'NOW can we hit him?', the security duo turn around, fists clenched... Only to find the beaming smile of Magnum Wolf directed right at them. Magnum: I believe I've found my ticket! They drop and shake their heads, before stepping aside for the grinning Magnum Wolf to enter the building with a skip in his step. Magnum: Kari, Kari, Kari... What fun we'll have... Here Comes the Oompa "Tessa! Tessa!" The Louisiana-based accent of Jackson Rust's voice shatters the serenity of silence, as the camera quickly opens up to see Jackson dashing across the floor, decked out in an old DWO t-shirt, and a pair of black, denim jeans. His roan-coloured hair flails in the air, as he makes his way to Tessa Windsor (pushing random staff-working from his path). Tessa stands before a refreshments table, her hand buried in a cooler full of ice and assorted drinks, before she emerges with a bottle of water in her small hand. With a friendly smile, she watches as Jackson comes to a halt right before her. Tessa Windsor: "Hey Jacko, what's up?" Tessa hands the bottle of water over to Jacko without bothering to ask if he wants it. Rust takes the bottle gratefully, as she then reaches back into the cooler, grabbing another bottle of water for herself, and twists the cap open to take a sip out of it. Jackson Rust: "Just about the coolest idea ever!" He grins as a Cheshire cat would, quickly placing the bottled water into his jeans' pocket, and taking Tessa's cold hand into his own, before hurriedly walking forward - Tessa's shoes not providing much grip as she slides across the cement floor, giggling as Rust quickly stops, and turns to her with a smile. Jackson Rust: "Y'know, if ya walked by yourself, this would be so much easier." Tessa Windsor: “Ah Man but then I’d have to work. I think I should take this as my night off! You can do all the work for me….starting now!” Tessa plants her bottom on the floor with a giggle as she looks up at Jacko with widened blue eyes. Tessa Windsor: “Hmmm, this is kinda comfortable.” With a sigh, Jackson winks at Tessa, before leaning down, and wrapping his arms underneath Tessa's, pulling her up somewhat. Quickly acting, before Tessa would slouch back down, he heaves her from the floor, and into his arms… holding her by the upper back, and under the crook of her knees. Tessa Windsor: "I could get used to this, y'know…" Jackson Rust: "Don't." With the word, Jackson tosses her in the air slightly, before crouching down, allowing Tessa to freefall (with a shriek, of course) back into his arms, before he stands once more. Jackson Rust: "See what I mean?" He sticks his tongue out to her this time, before leisurely walking across the floor, before finally reaching the door to the arena. Tessa Windsor: “Are we there yet?” Jacko nods his head in a yes response which leads Tessa to leap out of Jackson’s arms and plant her back against the nearest door. Tessa Windsor: “Soooooo….what’s this big plan of yours?” Tessa toys around with the cap on the water bottle as she looks up with a twinkle of excitement in her eyes, while she waits for Jackson to explain his plan to her. Jackson Rust: "Last week… I was watching the monitor, and Chris and Alyssa were doing the normal 'we're both on our periods' routine, and then BAM! Storm admitted a fear." Tessa Windsor: "What is it?!" Jackson Rust: "Midgets… Oompa Loompas…" Jackson deftly pushes the arena door open, allowing a train of Oompas to walk through the door, and into the building, as Tessa looks down at the short people, with a sly smile adorning her face. Jackson Rust: "Catch my drift?" Tessa nods her head but looks rather confused as to what good this will do. Tessa Windsor: “Do you really think this will work? I mean it seems sorta…well…..weird…that someone would be afraid of ….well Oompa Loompas?” The aforementioned midgets line up in a hemisphere around Rust and Windsor, as if they were in the military. Unmoving and disciplined they stood, as Jackson began to speak. Jackson Rust: "Hey, why are people afraid of heights? Because they think they'll die or something. Imagine being mauled by a hoard of Oompas… the orange and green blitzing of small people, barraging your body until you can't stand. Imagine them standing over your carcass, using your freshly spilled blood as new war paint, before going off to get their vengeance on Wonka. Now… would that not scare you? Hmm?" Tessa looks over at the Oompas and then at Jacko. Her face shrinks into a puzzled look as she shrugs her shoulders still confused as to what is so frightening about a bunch of midgets. Tessa Windsor: “Well….Uhh….I suppose so…” Jackson Rust: "EXACTLY! So, Oompa brigade! Formation: kick-Storm's-ass! Now!" The midgets comply, turning around 180 degrees to face forward. They take a dashing stance, angered scowls upon their painted faces. Oh yeah, they wanted blood… bitches. Jackson Rust: "On your marks… get set…" Tessa Windsor: "Go!" On her word, the midgets run… forward, in short strides (as their legs are only so long, y'know?). Armageddon has arrived. Tessa Windsor: "What are they going to be doing, exactly?" Jackson Rust: "Hopefully mauling Chris into little itty bits of confetti?" Tessa Windsor: “Ah man! You mean I don’t get to do that?” Jackson Rust: "Unfortunately. However, if the midgets fail their mission… he's all yours. But, I'd much rather engrave 'Death by Oompa' onto his gravestone, y'know?" Tessa Windsor: “Eh it works, long as I get to beat the crap into his new toy and her pals.” Tessa smiles as she follows Jackson off down the hall behind the Ooompa Loompas as the scene fades out. Jack's Back The video image slowly focuses in from black to a grainy picture, slightly wavering and gritty like that of an old B-grade film. The atmosphere is morose and gloomy, that of a decrepit old cemetery falling on dusk with a slight glare from a setting sun in the background. The hills beyond the graveyard are set with leafless trees and jagged peaks, overcast by a bleak gray sky. The camera slowly rotates to the right to present the rows upon rows of aging tombstones and skeletal dead trees that compose this dismal environment. At the far end of this burial ground is a crypt that towers over the surrounding area like some dark sentinel. The whole scene has the aura of an amateur-produced horror film. The crunch of leaves to the left prompts the camera to swing wildly back to its original position. Emerging from behind a fungi-crusted tree is a figure garbed in a black robe with hood drawn, the garment embellished with a series of obscene occultish symbols and effigies and the cuffs trimmed in silver and gold. At the very approach of the gaunt figure, the atmosphere seems to darken even further. He pauses and slowly lowers the hood of his robe. The solemn face of The Bringer of the Black Gospel, Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan, comes into view for the first time since his last RPW Adrenaline appearance, his face decorated in similar markings as his robes. Taking a deep breath of the cool New England air, Shanahan gazes down the cemetery at the foreboding crypt and exhales slowly. A sinister grin slowly crosses his thin lips as he continues to stare at the structure, seemingly ignoring the camera set on his stocky frame. VJ: Men look for solace in the strangest of places. Most go to church, bowing blindly before an altar to worship a mortal they choose to call savior. I can understand such an ambition, though to condone it is something I cannot do. To seek escape from this darkened world is surely a worthy aspiration, but to do so by worshipping a meek coward and ignoring reality is not the way to go about it. Weakness is not a virtue, and to believe it to be is simply asinine. No matter what that book of lies may lead one to believe. Shanahan pauses in his speech and looks to the camera for the first time. His eyes seem devoid of emotion or concern, his stature and words almost robotic in their coldness. VJ: I find my ‘solace’ in other places. It’s places like this that I come when I desire retreat. Here I can hear the souls of the dead cry for redemption, and it’s the most beautiful symphony of suffering one can ever hear. Nothing soothes the mind like the chorus of a thousand agonizing souls. Or at least….such is my opinion. He cracks a more devious grin, this one lined with a sense of morbid amusement. The first real emotion displayed yet by the Boston native. VJ: Very few feelings can match such a glorious sound, but a few weeks ago I experienced one of those few. When myself and Peter Maxwell drove that heathen Gideon Poe’s skull into the mat, I swear I could hear his spinal column nearly fuse and the vertebrae almost split. To see that weak fool writhing on the mat, twitching like a slaughtered lamb on an altar of sacrifice….it was truly magnificent. The Great Old Ones were proud that night. He shakes his head forlornly, recalling the aftermath. VJ: Alas, that coward Rush Mason opts to again take the easy way out. Just as he so faintheartedly decided to take legal action against Maxwell for what he did a few weeks back, he panics and does so yet again. And now here I find myself, suspended for doing what I have been decreed to do by the masters, and kept off television. And in typical fashion, RPW rallies support for their fallen hero by initiating a ‘touching’ sit-down interview with that gimp. And just like the religiously faithful with their beloved Messiah, the public eats it right up. Poe shows that he still lives, and subsequently vows to gain his retribution by striking down those who nearly destroyed him and his way of life. A couple weeks later, he wheels his crippled self out to the ring, and in a ‘miraculous’ display of conviction, pulls himself from his wheelchair. Just like the Son Himself, Gideon Poe is resurrected and will vanquish the most evil forces of RPW. Namely myself and that overzealous rebel Peter Maxwell. Jack’s dark grin dissipates, replaced by a snarl. Hatred visibly flares up in his eyes, burning into the camera lens. VJ: Well folks, I’m here to bestow upon you a terrible revelation. In life, the forces of good do not always overcome the forces of evil. Often times the light is swallowed by the darkness. It’s time to stop kidding yourselves and open your eyes to the harsh realities of this universe. And that, in itself, is my very purpose for being in RPW. There is no escaping it. You see…..like it or not, humanity has been cast from the grace of the Father. A day will come when fires will rage across the Earth, and mankind will reap the full backlash for its sins. This race is doomed to rot like disease-ridden rats, and there is no savior or salvation to prevent it. VJ: Gideon Poe’s devotion is no different. He may have risen from his tomb with a promise to crush his enemies, and in turn that of the public he champions, but in the end it will be a futile mission. We proved Poe’s weakness on Adrenaline when we nearly turned his spine into an accordion, and it won’t end there. Shanahan scoffs and shakes his head, slowly caressing his goatee. VJ: Poe….let me speak directly to you for the first time. Even at 100%, you were powerless to stop us from nearly destroying you forever. What in the name of Great Cthulhu himself makes you believe you stand any sort of chance against me when you still need help to even pull yourself out of bed in the morning? What sort of flawed logic are you trying to entertain here?? Hell…..make no mistake. You still only breathe on this wretched Earth because I chose to show you remorse. And trust me….that remorse did not please the Black Pharaoh. And thus, it won’t happen again. Again he looks away momentarily, his eyes set on the crypt silhouetted against the now nearly-hidden sun over the horizon. VJ: You could just as easily be a vegetable right now, spending the rest of your days in a hospital room kept alive by IV’s and machines. An intelligent man would realize this, count his losses, regroup, and simply leave things be and move on. But you can’t do that, can you? Every time you close your eyes, you see the children in the audience who cried as they carried you out on that stretcher, and you feel you need to be their hero yet again. Face it, boy…..this goes beyond simple vengeance for you, no matter what you try to sell on a big screen in some Barbara Walters tear-jerker of an interview. It always does for your type. I was there once, I know that. I don’t need the fame or the recognition anymore, Gideon. I’ve proven what I’m capable of time and again, and I’ll take it a step further when the time is right. When you overstep your bounds, I will not hesitate to finish the job myself and that wretched Maxwell started. And just as Christ was crucified 2000 years ago, such will be your own destiny. You can bear the cross on behalf of RPW, and those weak-minded buffoons you represent in the stands. Bruce draws a leather-bound tome from his robes. The title is scrawled in Latin print across the cover, though it’s initially indistinguishable. VJ: You can’t stop what I bring to this organization, chump. You will come to embrace what I have brought, else you will be left broken and crushed in my wake, just as my other enemies have been. And should you continue to follow your ridiculous crusade, the only thing that will save you is the Angel of Death. Mark my words, boy. He cracks a sinister grin as he flips slowly through the pages. VJ: As for you Maxwell…..your time of atonement will come. I’ve grown weary watching your antics every week on Adrenaline while I’m treated like some pariah by the organization. Your ‘rebellion’ is a farce. And though we may see eye to eye on some things, your betrayal is far from forgotten. I have other fish to fry, but don’t forget about me. Then you can join Poe and the rest of this despicable company groveling before me. You should thank RPW for this suspension. Though, of course, it only delays the inevitable. VJ casts a glance above the camera, beckoning toward the crypt. VJ: We’re finished here. We have more pressing matters to attend to…. With that statement, the image goes black. No Rush for Hardcore The door to Rush Mason’s office bursts open. Mason looks up, sighing, expecting to see any of the usual suspects. He is thus somewhat surprised, and relieved, to see the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid in front of him. His relief is lessened considerably, however, as the Trapezoid speaks. The HIT: Mr. Mason…I’ve been quite patient. I’ve gone out there and wrestled in any match you wanted. Now, I have a request. Rush Mason: What do you want? Mason expected him to ask for another shot at the Eclipse Title. HIT had taken his losses to Karina Wolfenden quite hard. The Trapezoid’s intentions were entirely different, however. The HIT: I want hardcore wrestling. The answer caught Mason off guard. He shouldn’t have been so surprised. He knew who the HIT was, knew the exercises in insanity that he had competed in. The convictions in HIT’s eyes made what Mason would say next all the more difficult. Rush Mason: I’m sorry to have to tell you this, but hardcore really doesn’t fit in with the direction we’re trying to go with RPW. The appeal of hardcore wrestling died out in 1999 and 2000. The people don’t want to see it. Fans want high flying and technical wrestling now, which you’re both capable of. Sure, we’ll throw the occasional ladder match out, but that’s the exception, not the rule. Trapezoid looks at Rush, obviously upset. The HIT takes several seconds to try and gather his thoughts, and finally does so. The HIT: You don’t get it, Mason. By not allowing hardcore wrestling, you are taking away my greatest asset to this company. You don’t prevent Akiyama from using the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse or his other power moves, do you? Rush Mason: No. The HIT: You let Karina Wolfenden fly all over the ring. You allow all of the other athletes of RPW to use their strengths. Yet, you deny me mine. I realize that under normal circumstances my abilities are illegal. But, you can grant me the matches in which I can shine. You have this power, Rush. USE IT! Mason looked at the Trapezoid’s face, a visage marked by scars. He made one final attempt to appeal to the logical side of the HIT, if such a thing existed. Rush Mason: Why the hell do you want hardcore wrestling? Shit, I wouldn’t have hired you if I didn’t think you were capable without all that bullshit! You’ve got an encyclopedic knowledge of wrestling moves. What the hell do you need to swing a chair for? The question had been asked of the HIT many times, and in fact, was one that HIT had asked himself.. He had an answer, and he was ready to give it Rush Mason. The HIT: I’ve had over 400 stitches. Second degree burns. And more minor injuries than you can shake a kendo stick at. But, you know what? It’s brought me success. It’s what I’m known for. I’m the HARDCORE Isosceles Trapezoid. Hardcore is what I’m known for. Hardcore is what’s made me a success. And I’ll be goddamned if you’re going to take my livelihood away from me, Mason. Trapezoid turns to leave, but Mason’s voice hits him as he makes it to the door frame. Rush Mason: What are you going to do? The HIT: You’ll know it when you see it. Early Delivery Without a knock the door of Rush Mason’s office is pushed open. A girl no more than a teenager walks in, wearing tight blue jeans and a black jacket covering her upper body. She wears a black baseball cap over her pony-tailed hair, making her face barely visible. Mason looks up, concerned about the intrusion. Rush Mason: Can I help you? The girl lifts her left arm forward, showing Mason and the camera a manilla envelope. Courier Girl: Umm…ya, I got a package here for Rush Mason. Here… The girl steps closer to the desk and tosses the envelope on top of the papers that already clutter the desk. Mason picks it up and flips it over to the addressed side. A white printed sticker indeed confirms that the package was meant for him and a stamped address in the upper left hand corner reads “World Anti-Doping Agency” followed by their HQ address. Rush Mason: Yeah, thanks for this. Mason begins to open the seal but a rough “ahem” from the courier girl grabs his attention. Mason looks up to see her with her hand out, obviously expecting a tip. Rush smirks and takes a wallet out of his pocket. He opens it up and takes out a large number of green bills and places it in the palm of the waiting teenager. Upon further inspection, it seems that the tip is nothing more than a wad of one-dollar bills. Courier Girl: Pfft…cheap skate bitch. She slips the money into her pocket and leaves the room as Mason continues to open the envelope.
