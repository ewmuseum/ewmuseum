Gideon Poe Biography Nicknames The Shade; Dismas - Apparantly he was the 'good thief' who repented his sins before being crucified alongside Jesus. I'm not sure how I got lumbered with it though. Maybe I was crucified in a past life. That would explain my occasional back aches. Hometown Not to sound like the typical and proverbial 'miserable son of a bitch' that you can find outside any Hot Topic store but, do you really give a shit? Either way, it's Bangor, Maine. Although nowadays I can be found inhabting Salem, New Jersey. You need an Birthday The 10th of February 1980. I think it was a Thursday... but probably not. Why? You got a present? Thought not. Height Six' Four Weight Two hundred and thirty of the most unsightly, tattooed and scarred lbs you're ever likely to see. Fed Titles Pffft Fed Awards Yeah right Other Feds RUSH, PCW, PWC Other Fed Awards ..... Wrestling Style Hybrid blend of unorthodox and innovative martial arts and technical manouvers. Likes to go for the big moves and put the opponenet down for good. Very methodical and creative, uses a lot of his speed to his advantage and is surprisingly strong. Ignores c Finisher Move Cadaver Theory Finisher Description Inverted Swining Neckbreaker - a'la Nova's Spin Doctor. Executed in a very quick wild snapping style also impacting the opponents face into the canvas.
