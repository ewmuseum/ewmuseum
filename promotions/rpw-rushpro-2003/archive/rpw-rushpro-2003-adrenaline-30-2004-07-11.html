The Circle Opens Stop. For just a moment, go no further. Adrenaline may be starting in North Carolina, but something of interest is happening in Massachusetts. His hazel eyes regard the contract, which has been sitting there on his desk for at least a week, almost contemptuously.  It shouldn’t be this difficult.  Is he getting cold feet this late in the game?  He hopes not.  Whether the damn thing gets signed or not, he imagines he would still have a world of trouble trying to get out of the deal no matter how chilly his toes might be.  Everything has already been set up, and one does not simply back out of the biggest undertaking of one’s life without severe consequences.  The contract is written out to the final draft, made out to the exact specifications personally negotiated between he and Christian Alexander (he liked Xavier so much more) Kannon.  He knows exactly what is on the document he should have completed by now; There is no logical reason to feel so uneasy. But he does, just the same. He recalls the confrontation between he and an old friend on the night he told his old friend what he intended to do.  The idea had been brought up once before but never merited any serious conversation, because to his old friend it was simple foolishness and he treated it as such.  There was no place in the business for him and the notion that he would try to claim one was “Madness,” his old friend said, almost unbelievingly.  “It’s utter madness and you cannot seriously be considering taking part-” This was too much.  He interrupted, and part of him enjoyed the hurt look on his friend’s face.  “Oh, spare me the guardian angel bullshit, please.  I don’t need you looking out for me no matter how helpless you obviously think I am.  I can take care of myself well enough, Wiz.” “I don’t doubt that, but I don’t think you’d be so dedicated to this plan of yours if you had any idea what you could bring upon yourself.” His old friend paused, probably hoping the momentary silence would allow the words to sink in, before asking “Have you any idea?” “Yeah, I’ve got a clue.  I’m not stupid.  Me and him were pretty close, you know.  We kinda had to be,” he said. He didn’t think his old friend looked particularly convinced.  That was probably because he didn’t think he sounded particularly convinced.  Briefly he wondered just who he was trying to convince here.  The question was oddly troubling, but since now was not the time, he put it aside, possibly for later consideration. “I don’t think you do,” his old friend said.  “He will find out, eventually.  One way or another he’ll find out, and then he’ll come for you.” He rolled his eyes.  “Him,” he said.  “It’s always about him, isn’t it? Why the hell should he get to have all the fun?  He is gone.   It’s my time now.” His old friend, who had once saved his life, stared at him fixedly, as a psychoanalyst would at an unstable patient.  From behind the silver-rimmed spectacles he always wore, his eyes shined with uncanny intelligence.  “You realize of course who you sound like.  I wonder how many other similarities will appear in time, if you decide to go through with this.” The first thought that came to him was to punch his old friend’s nose back into his above average brain.  Questioning his logic and calling him crazy was one thing.  But he wouldn’t be compared to him.  Never. “We’re not talking about this anymore,” he said flatly, and turned to walk away. His old friend said his name.  He ignored it and started walking.  He didn’t look back.  He didn’t stop until he was home. That had been months ago.  He hasn’t spoken with his old friend since.  The time between then and now he used to make all the preparations necessary to his plan and then inform a certain Mr. Kannon of his wish to join the ranks of Rush Pro Wrestling.  Mr. Kannon, who except for a few castoffs from the old PTC-WT alliance had precious little real talent to work with, was happy to talk business with this young man with a famous name.  A deal was made with the ease of two veterans all too familiar with this side of the business they love, which was ironic, since he Suddenly realizes that he’s been having a staring contest with a legal document for the past five minutes while he obsesses over ancient history and snaps his attention back to the present.  Christ, he’s letting the Wiz get into his head now, which is the last thing he needs given the circumstances.  Life’s gotten too mundane, just too fucking dull.  He needs some action, he needs to live like he knows that simple square of canvas can let him.  His toes are starting to warm up.  The pool is always cold when you dip your feet, yet once you take the plunge into that clear, wonderful water, you forget all about what was keeping you out at all in a single moment of icy revitalization.  Or you drown. Is it risky business?  Of course.  He doesn’t need a registered genius to tell him that.  But the water looks oh so nice, and the threat of God knows what varieties of trouble might be waiting for him does nothing to silence the call, that undeniable magnetism of the ring felt only by the rookies who know nothing of the way it can ruin a life, and by the veterans who have seen it happen for themselves time after time, but still come back for more. He remembers something the man he calls father once said: “It’s all just a game, and you can either play it or be played with.  Players and pawns is what it comes down to, really.  Players may lose a round or two, but they’re always around at the end, a little smarter, ready to go again.  Pawns are manipulated and discarded.  Even the most powerful piece on the chessboard, the highest champion, is still at the mercy of the player.  If you’re a player, no king or queen can beat you.  It’s the other players you have to worry about, and those are in very short supply.” An apt little credo, something you’d expect him to say.  But what is he, a player or a pawn?  Will entering the game mean salvation or damnation?  There’s only one way to find out. He smiles grimly, practically salivating at the possibilities.  Low pay, cutthroat competition, and plenty of danger.  This is the real deal.  This is just what he needs. What you see is RPW rebuilt in my vision, free from the corruption of Christian Alexander Kannon, free of favouritism and screw jobs. What you see is me finally taking what I deserve, and becoming just as selfish as those who've gone before me... DO you like what you see, Kari?" As Kari crawled over to check on the motionless Tessa, Magnum seemed insulted that she wasn't reacting to him... Her concern for a fallen opponent greater than that of her desire to argue with Wolf. "RPW stands on the Eve of it's Destruction, Karina," Wolf hissed, trying to grab her attention. "Every crime that has been perpetrated against me will be punished, and those who've tried to poison the wrestling world against me will see their lives and dreams crumble... In a little under a month's time, I will be King, and my reign will be merciless. If I'm left with no promotion to rule, then so be it, because RUSH Pro Wrestling would have brought it's demise upon itself." Finally sick of Wolf's preaching, Karina began to crawl up, looking at her rival with eyes narrowed out of hate. "Will you listen to yourself?" she hit back, words tired and pained. "You sound like a clichéd super villain! What are you going to do next? Launch an Asteroid at Metropolis?" Kari arguing back only riled Wolf further, turning his voice into little more than a harsh, angry whisper. "Oh, you want to know what I'll do next?" Magz hit right back with, picking the chair back up off the canvas. "I'm going to hear you beg, that's what I'm going to do next..." Karina knew that Wolf couldn't touch her, even with the chair he held in his hand... But it didn't take long for Wolf to clarify. "I'm going o make you beg for the health of Tessa Windsor, and I want you to know that it's all your fault..." CRACK! For the third time in as many minutes, Wolf slammed the chair down across Tessa's back, not even getting a reaction from her limp body. "What the fuck is wrong with you," a despairing K-Wolf roared, surging forward but stopping just an inch or so short of Wolf. "What has she ever done to you?" "That doesn't sound like begging to me, Missy," Wolf smirked back, knowing that he finally had the upper hand. "Now, I know this won't the first, or thousandth time a guy has said this to you... ON YOUR KNEES!" Knowing that Magnum wouldn't think twice about battering the hurt Tessa again, Karina slowly dropped to her knees, looking up at Magnum with spite. "You'll pay for this, you know that..." "Do I?" the dominant Wolf chuckled back. "No, Kari, what I do know is that you're about to beg for Tessa Windsor's ability to walk, and you're going to apologise for all that you've done to me, and you'll do it because there's no other way of stopping me. You can't hit me, unless you value that little bitch above the RPW World Title, and I wouldn't advise anyone else trying to help, since in 3 weeks time, I'll hold their career in my hand... ...Now, my sweet, sweet Karina... BEG!" The jeers from the crowd grew in volume, but stood no chance of bringing even the tiniest bit of guilt into Magnum's mind. "Beg," he continued, "because you're the only person that stands between Tessa, and life in a wheelchair... You, and you alone... Because nobod-" He'd tempted fate one too many times, and his words had been proved false. "Epic" by Faith No More blasted out around the arena, and for the first time ever, RPW fans greeted Peter Maxwell with relief and cheers. An enemy of their enemy was their friend, and at that moment in time, Wolf was by far their arch-nemesis. Hearing the theme, Magnum stopped mid-sentence, dropping the chair in unison with his jaw. His eyes no longer hidden behind glasses, Maxwell burned holes of intense hate into Wolf with his stair, each step possessing enough intent and intensity to make most men quiver. He'd been made to play the bad guy all his life, a life that drove him into a vicious cycle of betrayal and selfishness. But now, now he got to play the hero. "Pete... You don't want to do this," Wolf assured him, backing away to the ropes as Maxwell slid in, picking Tessa up and handing her safely over to the EMT's who'd been poised at ringside. "Think about this, Pete... This moment right now decides your future..." Maxwell stepped between Wolf and Karina, gesturing for RPW's champion to rise from her knees, no longer needing to be submissive to Wolf. Wolf was silent, his gaze darting, scanning for an escape route while Maxwell removed his padded gloves. Magz watched on open eyes as Maxwell linked his fingers before cracking his knuckles, each one being felt up Wolf's spine. "I'm not yours, I'm not hers, and I'm not the fans'," Maxwell informed Wolf with cold, emotionless words, before casually cracking his neck. "I'm RPW's... And for that fact alone, I do this." "Pete... Pete..." Wolf continued to plead, holding his hands up over his face... ...But leaving just enough of a gap to see the huge figure of Peter Maxwell nearly decapitate Karina Wolfenden with a spinning Lariat. Son. Of. A. Bitch. With Magnum nearly in hysterics at having fooled the fans again, the crowd let out a jeer of pure hate, some fans even needing to be held back by security. Before Karina could even react to the incapacitating move, Maxwell crouched down and pulled her dazed frame up, dragging the RPW Champion into a front facelock. Grabbing her waistband, Peter easily lifted Karina up, before collapsing under her to nail a nasty-looking Brainbuster Suplex, leaving an out-cold K-Wolf to slump down across the mat. "A wise choice, Mr Maxwell," Wolf applauded, his grin as wide as ever. "The first of many who will decide it's better to be with me than against me. Peter Maxwell isn't an honourless mercenary, he's a man who LOVES RPW... He knows that those in charge are ruining the company, and that a new era is soon to begin. We will steamroller RPW, and build it in our vision, build it the way it SHOULD be. No 'Golden Children' hogging the titles, no personal vendettas getting in the way of business, and a few less over-hyped and over-paid failures!" Having no words to go with his betrayal, Maxwell simply pulled his famous pair of dark glasses from his pocket, masking his deceitful eyes. "See this as a sign, people," Wolf continued to preach. "The unworthy RPW Champion at my feet, a fitting way to usher in my re-" Having seen enough, RPW's owner finally made his presence known, not even needing music to draw attention to him. Reacting to his mere sight, the fans exploding, seeing him once more as their salvation against the powers that threatened RPW's future. "So is this rally going to be RPW's future?" Kannon questioned, managing to remain calm. "Peter Maxwell fighting your battles for you? Aren't you going to lead by example, Magnum?" "Who are you to talk to me about leadership?" Wolf scoffed back, shaking his head at RPW's figurehead. "The man who's steering RPW towards bankruptcy lecturing me about leading! HAH!" "Maybe if I didn't have over-paid and unwanted people such as yourself on the roster, our finances would be a little better," Kannon suggested, heading down the aisle under the watchful eyes of Maxwell and Wolf. "Maybe then I'd have some spare money to actually allow RPW to grow, to invest, to finally do things how I wish." "Oh, and your way always has to be right, doesn't it!" Kannon recognised the voice right away, and dropped his head, knowing what it likely meant. Onto the stage behind him walked Brian Arson, changed out of his wrestling gear and wearing an old Xavier Kannon t-shirt... More out of mockery than homage. "Did you ever stop to think that your way is the wrong way?" Arson pressed, while Magnum looked mightily pleased with events. "Or are you so caught up in your own hype that you can't see when you're screwing something up?" Turning back to the stage, Kannon took several focussed strides towards Arson, ready to fight his corner. "Maybe it's just people not following orders properly that makes plans fall apart, huh?" he shot right back at his protégé, knowing he was angry about suffering his first loss earlier in the night. "Maybe if people cared less about their own agenda, more things around here would work." Taking his forceful suggestion as an insult, Arson's anger swelled, while Magnum and Maxwell seemed hooked on the unfolding events. "That's what you do best, isn't it?" Arson snarled back. "Pushing things off onto others... Like your battles and personal grudges. But to be fair, there is one thing that you do very well, which I can't help but admire..." Before Kannon could even react, Arson doubled him over with a kick to the gut. To the tune of thousands of furious fans, Arson then gabbed Kannon in the all-too-familiar standing Headscissors, before lifting him, grapevining the leg... And drilling Kannon's head down onto the stage with the Hallmark. Finally breaking his silence, Magnum's words warmly greeted Arson's actions. "Seeing as Mr. Arson is now out of favour with the current management," Magnum's silver tongue tempted, "maybe he would feel more welcome in a group which intends to change things around here." Without speaking, Arson stepped over the downed Kannon before walking down the aisle, security trying hard to keep the fans at bay. Up the stairs Arson walked, before spring over the top rope and into the ring. Shaking his head in a mixture of pity and disgust at the fallen World Champion, Arson walked over to Wolf, untying his hair. "That's the best offer I've had around here," he grinned back at Wolf, sealing the deal with a handshake. And then there were three. "Are these the actions of happy and content workers?" Wolf then questioned the crowd with, drawing even more hate from the baying mob contained behind the rail. "Do you now see that the current regime isn't working? Look at your Champion, knocked out cold. Look at your figurehead, felled by a man supposed to be his inferior student!" "I think you're forgetting someone," determined female words then accused from the stage. Standing there in opposition to the trio in the ring was Eleanor Kannon-Hall, unfaltering as she approached her fallen husband. "Oh, please," Wolf sighed, "is the eye candy all that stands in our way?" Eleanor's intervention did it's job, however, as the EMT's were able to slide into the ring just long enough to rescue the out-cold Karina Wolfenden. "Eye candy with RPW's booking power at her disposal," Eleanor hit back with, while Gaz Green walked tentatively out behind her, trying to convince his manager not to get involved. "Listen to the Green guy, hotcakes," Wolf suggested, laughing off her suggestion. "I don't remember asking you to talk," Ellie confidently swatted him down with. "But since you 3 seem so pally, how about we see how you get on in a 6 Man Tag Match?" Cocking an eyebrow at them, Eleanor paused to allow the fans to show their reaction, a loud pop for the prospect of the now despised trio having to show what they could do in the ring. "After all," Eleanor continued, showing how strong she could be when needed... While Gaz seemed less willing to be out beside his manager. "I'm sure that Karina wants her chance for revenge..." "Her and who's Army, Barbie?" Magnum scoffed. "Who's going to fight beside someone like her?" "Gaz will," Ellie then smiled back at Wolf. "I wil-" Gaz went to echo, before realising what he was saying. "WHAT? Hey, this isn't my figh-" As Green's manager, Eleanor had made her decision, but hadn't finished yet. "And just for old times' sake," she continued, getting the fans behind her, and scarily, Gaz too. "I'll throw Nerezza into the mix too... I'm sure he'll have bags of bloody fun with you three!" Not exactly thrilled at the prospect, Wolf went to argue back, but found Eleanor a worthy verbal opponent. "I'm sure you'll have no objections, gentlemen?" "Er, yes, actually!" Gaz answered from behind her. "Oh, shush," she ordered, causing Gaz to roll his eyes as Ellie turned her attention back to the ring. "You've made your impact, said your piece, and next week you'll get to back it up... Against Karina Wolfenden, Gaz Green, and Nerezza." Gaz and Nez may have been far from the fun-loving Pranksters that wrestling fans once knew, but the prospect of them fighting side by side once more, even as singles wrestlers, thrilled the crowd... Especially give the opposition. "Line them up, toots," Magz smirked back, "and we'll knock them down." Magnum Wolf had stepped up, and made an even bigger mark on RPW, but an unlikely figure stepped up to try and thwart him... Would her plan work? Could Gaz co-exists with others? Could Karina recover from another punishing week? Could Nerezza even be persuaded to fight for another? Was Maxwell's loyalty to Wolf as strong as it seemed? And could Arson's ego be a match even for Wolf's? Lots of questions, all of which would be answered in a week's time.
