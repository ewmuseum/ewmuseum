THE HILLS ARE ALIVE WITH THE SOUND OF MUSIC The lights came on. The cameras rolled. The mics went live. The fans cheered. Then, "Rock is Dead" began to play. A week after the Kannon Effect, the fans were expecting the new Champion - Karina Wolfenden - to head out and open the show. Failing that, Invincible Gabe bursting through the curtain and boasting about never having been beaten for the belt. If not them, maybe Gideon Poe, commenting on his valiant attempt to regain the RPW World Title. Instead, they got Magnum Wolf. And they didn't like that fact one little bit. Far from the theatrical man they were used to, Magz just stormed out through the curtain and carried his fast pace down the aisle. No pausing to pose, no jawing at the fans, no cutting a quick boastful promo to the ringside cameras. He wanted in the ring, and he wanted on the house mic... Quick. "MAG-NUM-SUCKS! MAG-NUM-SUCKS! MAG-NUM-SUCKS!" the familiar chant echoed, having no effect on it's focussed target. 7 days ago, he thought he'd won, thought it was "his time". Karina Wolfenden had walked away, giving him the #1 contender's slot. Invincible Gabe had been taken out, freeing up a spot in the main event. Everything had fallen into place... Then SHE ruined it all. The thorn in his side, the blot on his landscape, the itch he couldn't scratch - Karina Wolfenden. "Rock is Dead" faded down, leaving only jeers and a fuming Magz in the ring, where his tight grip held the house microphone. "You hate me, I hate you, we know the deal, so just shut up and listen for once in your lives," Magnum snarled at the crowd, without his usual, playful tone. "I know that in my career, I've been known as a hot-head. I've had my run-ins with management and earned a reputation as a 'backstage troublemaker'... But what I have to say now, I say in all seriousness... I DEMAND THE RESIGNATION OF CHRISTIAN ALEXANDER KANNON." Probably not the best thing to say about the man who held the keys to his wrestling future. Naturally, nobody in the crowd had the tiniest bit of sympathy for the despised star, and immediately leapt on his words with fury. Kannon had saved RPW, propelled it forward, while all Wolf had done was torment one of their favourites and play them for fools. Kannon had a legion of fans, Wolf had himself. "Ugh, you people are such marks," Wolf scoffed with disgust. "Sure, he's come out here and said how you people make this place what it is. He's booked the matches you want to see. Pampered his little "Golden Children" Kari and Poe... But now look what he's done!" Whereas Magz had shown signs of sincerity before, this time the words really did sound honest. Last Sunday hurt him deep down, crushing his spirit. He was screwed over, and nobody could convince him otherwise. "If the head of any other company had been as underhand as Kannon, he'd be shunted out before he had time to drink his morning coffee," Wolf exclaimed. "And in no other promotion in this country, would such a miscarriage of booking justice leave me - the #1 Contender - unable to face the World Champion..." Pausing, Wolf's face soured before he turned his head and spat down on the canvas. "...Karina Wolfenden." Not caring about the context, or the insult to her name Wolf expelled, the mere mention of the K-Wolf exploded the fans, finally rattling Magnum. "For bringing this promotion into disrepute, I hereby urge Kannon to do the honourable thing, and stand down," Magz continued, managing to compose himself. "Unless you want to stay and screw things up some more? Maybe ban tag teams from going for the Tag Titles? Or how about nobody under 230 winning Eclipse? Why just quit at letting your own PERSONAL feelings get in the way of what's right, huh?" Maybe there was some truth and logic in Wolf's words, but nobody listening had that opinion. "MAG-NUM-SUCKS! MAG-NUM-SUCKS! MAG-NUM-SUCKS!" "I think we all know that Karina Wolfenden's name wasn't drawn from any machine," Magz hissed. "I think it's clear to everyone that there wasn't even another draw. SHE was put in that match to wind me up. Well, Mr Kannon, it's good to see that you are using your power and authority wisely, it really is." He was saying what some were thinking. Maybe Kannon didn't draw another name at random, and maybe his anger at Wolf's attack on Gabe did influence his naming of the K-Wolf as the replacement... But to the RPW fans, the end justified the means. "Have you nothing to say?" Wolf then pressed, focussing his attention on the curtain. "Or will you stay silent in your shame?" Wolf expected Kannon not to show. He wasn't even sure RPW's figurehead had arrived yet... But "Rock is Dead" playing for a 2nd time told him someone was about to come out. Angry at the theme that he'd adopted being used by another, Wolf watched on wide-eyed as Christian Alexander Kannon appeared on the stage. Sick of Wolf using his old music, RPW's figurehead had decided to reclaim it, and what better time than with a fuming Wolf stood in the middle of the ring. "You don't need a grand entrance," Wolf yelled up to the stage, struggling to be heard above the fans. "Just do the right thing and leave this place in more capable hands..." In the ring, Wolf smirked, happy to drag Kannon's name though the mud. On the stage, however, Kannon looked in his element, and not like a man who was about to resign. "Do the right thing?" he hit back, amazed that a man like Magnum could say such a thing while keeping a straight face. "Did you do the right thing when the OSE Locker Room demanded you were fired? Did you do the right thing by conning the RPW fans into thinking you were some sort of hero on a mission of redemption? Did you do the right thing by dragging up Karina Wolfenden's private life to try and break her? Did you do the right thing by ambushing Gabe last Sunday." "THERE IS NO PROOF IT WAS ME!" Magz ranted back, not too happy at Kannon's rebuttal. "By all rights, RPW should have paid up your extortionate contract months ago and thrown you out," Kannon's passionate words continued. "I don't like you, the fans don't like you, the crew don't like you, and nobody on the roster would shed a tear if I fired your whiny ass tonight." "NOW JUST YOU HOLD ON A MI-" Magz went to protest, his words lost in the pops for Kannon. "I'm talking now," Kannon bluntly told him, putting the uppity contender in his place. "Now, you are the #1 Contender to the RPW World Title, that much you can claim. But you agreed to the stipulations of your match with Karina. You don't touch her, she doesn't touch you. You don't step into this ring for any singles matches against her, neither does she against you." "Well," Wolf quipped, "you've well and truly fucked this one up, haven't you?" Ignoring Magz, Kannon continued. "But, as much as I hate to say it," Kannon sighed. "In 7 weeks time, at our next extended PPV, you will go 1-on-1 with the RPW World Heavyweight Champion, as the contract stated... Whoever that Champion is will all depend on just how worthy of a Champion Karina Wolfenden turns out to be." "Oh, let me guess," Wolf sneered. "She'll defend against, shock horror, Gideon Poe? Or maybe Brianna Washington will come back to face her? Anymore of her buddies I've forgotten?" "Although Gideon Poe is more worthy of an RPW Title shot that you'll ever be," Kannon shot right back, "he won't be challenging before your shot. After winning his match last Sunday, Steven Silver will be given a shot, as will Violence Jack, amongst others." "Phew," Wolf smirked, "I won't have to face Skankarina again then." "Oh, but you will..." a beaming Kannon revealed, enjoying toying around with the strung-out Magnum. "Tonight, in fact." "LOOK," a frustrated Wolf roared as his feet stamped the canvas, "I am not someone for you to screw around with, I'm the closest thing this cess-pit has to a megastar, and it's about ti-" Again, Kannon just cut Magnum off, not allowing his unpopular star to ruin his flow. "Tonight, I'm going to give you the chance to do something you've failed to do so far in RPW..." Kannon teased. "...Make a few friends." "Yeah," Wolf hissed back, "because that's how this place runs..." "I'm going to let you pick 3 partners," Kannon revealed, frustrating Wolf by completely blanking any comebacks. "And Karina will do the same. 2 teams, 8 wrestlers, 1 match." "You're giving me a chance to show your..." Wolf began, before pausing to spit again. "World Champion up for the fluke-win spot-monkey she is? Fine by me. Just don't expect the superstar alliance my talents will form to go easy on whatever freakshow that skank blows to get on board." "Will there be anything else?" Kannon then directly asked Wolf. "Oh, I think everyone has seen and heard enough already," an almost chuckling Wolf smirked. "Where you're concerned," Kannon immediately snapped back, just as "Rock is Dead" began to play. "That's very true." Naturally, Wolf managed to retort with a smarmy comment of his own, but it was drowned out by the music and cheering, casing Wolf to slam the mic down in frustration. SOLITARY CREATURE Hidden away in her haven of a locker room, Karina Wolfenden heard every word Magnum Wolf had said. The walls of the Dale F. Halton Arena couldn't contend with the fury in Wolf's voice, amplified by the house mic, and gave way to the sound. She also head Kannon's retaliation, putting her back in conflict with Wolf just 7 days after she thought it was all over. Maybe a different Champion would have left their dressing room to confront Wolf, or reject their boss making plans for them. Maybe Gideon Poe would have just shrugged agreements aside, strode down to the ring and taken Wolf on then and there. Maybe Gabe would have verbally battled Kannon to cancel the match. But she wasn't them... She was passive rather than aggressive, solitary rather than aligned, silent rather than ranting. "Great," she bemoaned, realising she now had to find 3 partners to take into battle alongside here. Who was there to choose? Brianna was still on the injured list, having tweaked her knee as part of Karina's double bluff against Wolf. She lacked the nerve to even ask Poe to side with her just a week after winning 'his' title... Although he wished her well, it would be salt in his wounds to walk out with her. For someone so popular with the fans, she was pretty much a loner backstage. "Maybe they'll come to me?" she muttered, a trace of faint hope in her voice. Since winning the RPW World Title, the weight on her shoulders had caused her to slump. She could no longer walk away as she'd done with Magnum at The Kannon Effect, she could no longer slip in and out of the arena unnoticed, and she couldn't escape the feeling of responsibility. She was playing catch-up to those who's footsteps she was following in, and there was still quite a way to go. But she had a choice... Let the pressure get the best of her, or relish the moment and truly appreciate the honoured belt he had. INDESTRUCTABLE It was a miracle that Peter Maxwell could still walk. In professional wrestling, everyone expects to take the odd "big bump", but they're usually a fall from the top turnbuckle, or at most a suicide dive over the top and into the crowd... What Peter Maxwell's body endured at the Kannon Effect made even the most hardened wrestler flinch. By all rights, Maxwell should be in traction. But instead, he was about to endure an even worse fate - Magnum Wolf. "Petey, old pal!" the slimy contender boomed along the corridor, snaring Maxwell in his words. "Good to see your spine is still in working order." Really not in the mood for Wolf's antics, Maxwell slowly turned, staring harshly over his black sunglasses at the approaching wrestler. "What?" he bluntly scoffed, a warning that Wolf didn't even register. "Hey, you know me," Wolf beamed back, not even realising that knowing him usually meant they hated him. "It's never about what you can do for me, it's about what I can do for you!" Peter Maxwell could be forgiven for being sceptical. "This I gotta hear," Maxwell muttered, wondering how Wolf could turn his selfishness into a favour. "As your friend," Wolf boldly began, showing he had no shame, "I feel it's my duty to stop this downward slide you've been having..." Yep, that's the kinda thing Wolf actually says to peoples' faces. "...I mean, you used to be the MAN around here, although admittedly that was before I signed my contract, but now? It SADDENS me to see what you've become, Petey." "Call me Petey once more," Maxwell thought, clenching his taped fists, "and I'll break your face." "You were in the biggest matches RPW has had," Magz continued, his poisoned tongue failing to paralyse Maxwell, "people pictured you with the World Title around your waist... Hell, you came SO close to RULING this place... But now? Well, forgive my honesty, but you've become Poe's bitch." In Maxwell's mind, he was ramming Wolf's face into the wall, then kicking his ribs in. But on some levels, he wanted to hear what Wolf's offer was, knowing deep down he'd fallen from his earlier glory. "It's pretty obvious to see that Kannon isn't appreciating your talents," Wolf continued, not even registering Peter's cold glare, "so I'm taking it upon myself to give your career a relaunch. Everyone knows I'm headed to the top of this place, and we're on the eve of the Era of Magnum, and I want to offer you a place by my side... Starting tonight, in our Main Event!" Suspicion was over-coming Maxwell. He knew Wolf couldn't be trusted, but on the other hand, he knew Wolf lacked the merest hint of an ally... So the proposition made sense. "Why?" "Just think, Pete," Magz grinned as his hand slapped down on Maxwell's shoulder. "Tonight, you have the chance to pin the RPW World Champion..." Wolf then spat on the floor. "...1-2-3 in the middle of the ring. I've beaten her enough already, so I'm offeri-...No, I'm GIVING you a win over the RPW World Champion..." Again, Wolf turned away and spat with disgust on the concrete. "...Sound good to you?" "Who else will be on our team?" Maxwell enquired, still not relaxing around Magz. "Don't worry," Wolf assured him, "I've sorted that all out, not that we'll need them. I mean, you fell about 3000 feet through a stage and half-way down to hell, what's that skanky, multi-color, raver-whore going to do to us? Blind us with a glow stick? Pete... ...You're INDESTRUCTIBLE!" Brought around by Wolf's words, Maxwell finally unclenched his fists, before sliding his glasses back up his nose. "Okay," his cold words agreed, "I'm in."
