Adrenaline 4, 30 November 2003

30 November 2003

Revealing We start Adrenaline with Peter Maxwell in the ring. He’s pacing back and forth, looking around while boos greet him in the ring. Maxwell: Mason! I have tried, and I’ve tried to be the nice guy here, pal. I’ve given you chance after chance, and you’ve still proven that you don’t give a damn about this company, you don’t give a damn about these piece of shit fans, and you don’t give a damn about your talent you’re trying to lock up in contracts. Boos get louder, and Maxwell stands in the middle of the ring. He enjoys the feeling of the heat from the nine thousand strong crowd, and puts a hand on his hip. Maxwell: Are you finished? Upon being acknowledged, the crowd boos even harder. Maxwell raises an arm up, pumping the crowd’s heat up to insane levels. Maxwell: COME ON! GIVE IT TO ME! It doesn’t seem possible, but the crowd gets even louder with their anti-appreciation for the man in the ring. An insane look comes into the eye of Peter Maxwell as he walks to the ropes and leans over the top rope a bit. Maxwell: Because it’ll be the last thing you people here in the Halton Arena have to boo for. EVER. The crowds reaction turns from boos to one of confusion. Maxwell: That’s right. Tonight, marks the end of RPW. After just 4 weeks, RPW is over. Again, the confused reaction from the crowd gets louder. Just a few minutes ago, the ringside announcer, Matt Elia had been in the ring, telling people of all the action coming up this week. That plans were in the works for a pay-per-view in January. That plans were together for a NASCAR sponsorship. And now Maxwell had just turned their excitement into words they didn’t think they’d hear. Maxwell: It’s true. I’ve proven it all over the world. Look at this. Maxwell points to the fairly new ArenaTron, as a video package rolls. The first shot is one of Jason O’Neil and Angelo Deville wrestling in FSW. Deville pins O’Neil which gets a huge pop from the crowd. A graphic then pops up that says FSW: Killed. Next, Jason O’Neil is in the ring again, this time with Sebastian Toomes. O’Neil’s just delivered his former finishing move, the “Fall from Grace” and pins Toomes. Again, a graphic pops up. PWC: Killed. In the next image, Jason O’Neil is wrestling a man known to fans of the former GCW as Twisted. O’Neil kicks Twisted in the face. O’Neil Reverse DDT’s another man, and footage is shown of the most insane wrestling move of all time. Jason O’Neil grabbing Jordan Carter, and dropping him off of the roof of an arena. A graphic comes up yet again: GCW/XW: Killed. The video package turns to a highlight of last weeks action, when Dorn Tracer and Totakoxe Salvador fighting in the ring as a bloody Maxwell watches on. Another picture of Maxwell and Violence Jack dropping Gideon Poe on his head appears, and slowly, a graphic fades in… RPW: D.O.A. Maxwell looks at the crowd. The reaction is much more clear this time. The boos are louder than they could have hoped. They seem to echo through the arena, out the doors, and across the globe, as people watching on Fox Sports Southeast join in. Maxwell: I am a killer of feds. Look at the proof. I’ve killed every place I’ve been, and I’m not going to stop with RPW. No, no. Because tonight, I’m going to bring out a man that’s going to kill Rush Mason. Not literally, of course. But financially, Rush Mason is going to have no choice but to kill off his very creation he named after himself. Rush Pro Wrestling…WILL BE NO MORE. “It’s Goin’ Down” by the X-Cutioners blasts through the arena as Rush Mason emerges from the middle of the Tron. The cheers seem louder than the boos as Mason raises a fist to the crowd. He’s got a microphone in his hand. His face shows a bit of worry, but nevertheless, he makes his way to the ring. Maxwell claps, and even goes over and holds the ropes open for him. Cautiously, Mason makes his way into the ring. Maxwell: Good of you to join me here for RPW’s last hurrah, Mason. Mason: Shut your mouth. Right now. Cheers blow up the arena, and Mason slowly, and cautiously walks towards Maxwell. Mason: You’ve been telling me for weeks about this “financial backer”. And, sure. I’m interested to know who it is. But lets get one thing perfectly clear, Maxwell… Mason is now standing eye to eye with Maxwell. Even dressed in a suit, Mason is bigger than Maxwell. He stands just a bit taller, and definitely wider, a presense that used to intimidate opponents in the ring. Maxwell just smiles into his face, as Mason snarls. Mason: RPW is bigger than you. If there’s one thing that you’re not going to do, that’s going to be to “kill RPW.” I will decide when that happens, and when it does, it’s not going to be on the whim of some punkass bastard like yourself. More cheers come from the crowd as Maxwell starts to back off from Mason’s glare. He clutches his chest and feigns a heart attack. Mason reaches out and grabs Maxwell by the lapels, and pulls him into his face. Mason: Don’t you think I’ve dealt with people like you from the beginning? If I had a nickel for every little bastard that said he was going to hurt me, or kill something I worked for, I’d be a wealthy man. And if I had a dollar for every no-talent wrestler with delusions of grandeur, I’d be Bill Gates. Another huge pop for Mason’s comments as Maxwell struggles free of Mason’s grip. Maxwell backs off, and straightens his tie and jacket. He smiles at Mason and laughs. Maxwell: Oh you pompous dick. The difference between me and them is, I can back up what I’ve been claiming. You wanna meet the financial backer, Mason? Do you want to look into the eyes of the man who’s going to destroy RPW?! Well, fine. I was going to give you ONE LAST CHANCE to right the wrongs, but you’ve forced my hand Mason. From here on out, the BLOOD IS ON YOUR HANDS! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN… Suddenly the lights in the arena dim, cutting Maxwell off. The crowd turns to the entrance as a majestic tune fills the building. Maxwell’s face grows dim as a huge guitar riff brings the lights back up, and a flurry of gold pyro explodes from the stage! The crowd rises as a lone figure emerges into view, his arms wide open, sporting an expensive suit. Maxwell is noticeably shocked as he recognizes the figure standing in front of him. Tyler: Is that who I think it is?! The crowd seems to recognize the man before Valentine, but Maxwell seems to be steaming. Tyler: That’s Steven Caldera! I don’t believe it! The former owner of Global Championship Wrestling, the former manager of Kimbusa, and Peter Maxwell’s former employer is here in RPW! Caldera, beaming at the warm reaction from the fans, turns a full circle as the flaming guitar of “Rock the Night” plays him to the ring. He adjusts his tie and begins slapping hands with the fans as he heads towards the ring. Maxwell begins pacing angrily as Caldera approaches, though Caldera seems unconcerned with Maxwell at the moment. Mason claps as Caldera makes his way through the ring. Tyler: And did you see that explosion of pyrotechnics when Caldera came out? Does that mean… Does that mean Caldera’s RPW’s financial backer?! Rumors have been flying that Maxwell’s former boss may be bankrolling RPW, and this entrance seems to back that up! Caldera reaches the ring and steps inside, heading straight to the turnbuckle to raise his arms to the crowd. The fans cheer wildly, happy to see Caldera back on television after nearly a year’s absence. Caldera reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a microphone as his music quiets. Maxwell remains in the opposite corner of the ring from Caldera, glaring at him steadily. Caldera: Man, it’s good to be back in a wrestling ring again. The crowd cheers warmly again as Caldera finally looks right at Maxwell. Caldera: For anyone that may not know who I am, my name is Steven Caldera. I used to run this little company called Global Championship Wrestling… But it met with an untimely demise. Then I briefly ran something called X-Wrestling, but that too was a rather ill-fated venture. You see, there was a man who worked for me a while ago who got it in his mind that I was out to get him. He thought I was doing my best to hold him down. So he set out to destroy me and everything I’d worked for. Well, he didn’t manage to keep me down, but he did manage to destroy my companies. The crowd boos in Caldera’s favor, but seem unsure of Caldera’s point. Tyler: Caldera was a rather successful promoter, but both of his promotions died out pretty quick. Caldera: Unfortunately, I’ve never managed to get back at the man that ruined me. Which is why when I saw that man standing in an RPW ring… Caldera says this as he stares right at Maxwell, who actually begins to smile. Caldera: …I knew I would have to call in a few favors and get myself a contract. But I’ve done a little more than that… The crowd begins to buzz, anticipating the blockbuster announcement Caldera may be about to make. Tyler: I don’t believe this! Could it really be Caldera that’s been backing RPW? Maxwell suddenly tires of listening to Caldera and moves towards him, stopping right in Caldera’s face. Caldera’s smile disappears, but he does not back away. Maxwell: Steven, What are you trying to prove? I am sorry I had to ruin your career. I’m sorry that I’ve destroyed you not once, not twice, but three times. I’ve left you not only in ruins with your career, but I’ve left you in financial ruin. Tyler: Does that mean…? Maxwell: So for all you idiots that thought Steven Caldera was the “big shocking announcement”, let me make it perfectly clear: Steven Caldera is an opportunist. If there’s something with him reaching a prominent level in a certain place, then Caldera will be there in a heart beat. You’re a parasite, Caldera. The crowd boos, though Caldera does not say anything to dispute this statement. Suddenly, Maxwell nails Caldera with a knee below the belt, doubling Caldera over. The crowd roars with boos as Maxwell begins pummeling Caldera with right hands. Rush Mason escapes the ring as Maxwell grabs Caldera by the throat. Tyler: OH MAN! Maxwell is beating the hell out of Caldera! I guess Caldera’s not the backer after all! The boos abruptly turn to astonishment as another figure enters the scene. A mammoth man hops over the barricade from the crowd and nimbly rolls into the ring behind Maxwell. A ‘Holy Shit!’ chant rises from the crowd as they recognize the humongous form now in the ring. Tyler: KIMBUSA! Kimbusa is here in RPW, and he’s right behind Peter Maxwell! Maxwell, hearing the change in reaction, turns around to see Kimbusa. Kimbusa moves in and drops Maxwell with a boot to the gut. Kimbusa backs up as Caldera clears the way, and Kimbusa charges at Maxwell. He connects with a hard clothesline that crumples Maxwell to the canvas. Maxwell rolls himself out of the ring as Kimbusa charges on. Kimbusa stops and helps Caldera to his feet, as Caldera picks up his microphone again. Caldera: Remember him, Pete? Remember the man that nearly killed you? We’ve still got “business” with you pal. At the end of the night, Kimbusa and I are going to be in this ring waiting for you. I’ve already talked to Mason, and he thinks it’s a great idea. So you bring whoever the hell you want, we’re here to take you and all your little friends on. And I suggest you show, because if you don’t, we’ll be waiting for you in the parking lot, and there won’t be any witnesses back there. Caldera then throws the mic down, as his music cues up again. Maxwell, fuming, turns and storms out of view. Caldera raises his arms to the crowd again as Kimbusa hops out and slowly begins sauntering towards the back. Tyler: I don’t believe this! Steven Caldera and Kimbusa are in RPW, and Caldera has laid down a challenge to Peter Maxwell! What a huge shocker this is! Fans, tonight, we've got a shortened version of Adrenaline, thanks to UNCC Basketball. Don't worry though! Next week will be a Three Hour Spectacular! We guarantee more action than you can shake a stick at! An intimate meeting with Violence Jack The video image slowly focuses in from black to a grainy picture, slightly wavering and gritty like that of an old B-grade film. The atmosphere is morose and gloomy, that of a decrepit old cemetery falling on dusk with a slight glare from a setting sun in the background. The hills beyond the graveyard are set with leafless trees and jagged peaks, overcast by a bleak gray sky. The camera slowly rotates to the right to present the rows upon rows of aging tombstones and skeletal dead trees that compose this dismal environment. At the far end of this burial ground is a crypt that towers over the surrounding area like some dark sentinel. The whole scene has the aura of an amateur-produced horror film. The crunch of leaves to the left prompts the camera to swing wildly back to its original position. Emerging from behind a fungi-crusted tree is a figure garbed in a black robe with hood drawn, the garment embellished with a series of obscene occultish symbols and effigies and the cuffs trimmed in silver and gold. At the very approach of the gaunt figure, the atmosphere seems to darken even further. He pauses and slowly lowers the hood of his robe. The solemn face of The Bringer of the Black Gospel, Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan, comes into view for the first time since his last RPW Adrenaline appearance, his face decorated in similar markings as his robes. Taking a deep breath of the cool New England air, Shanahan gazes down the cemetery at the foreboding crypt and exhales slowly. A sinister grin slowly crosses his thin lips as he continues to stare at the structure, seemingly ignoring the camera set on his stocky frame. VJ: Men look for solace in the strangest of places. Most go to church, bowing blindly before an altar to worship a mortal they choose to call savior. I can understand such an ambition, though to condone it is something I cannot do. To seek escape from this darkened world is surely a worthy aspiration, but to do so by worshipping a meek coward and ignoring reality is not the way to go about it. Weakness is not a virtue, and to believe it to be is simply asinine. No matter what that book of lies may lead one to believe. Shanahan pauses in his speech and looks to the camera for the first time. His eyes seem devoid of emotion or concern, his stature and words almost robotic in their coldness. VJ: I find my ‘solace’ in other places. It’s places like this that I come when I desire retreat. Here I can hear the souls of the dead constantly cry for redemption, and it’s the most beautiful symphony of suffering one can ever hear. Nothing soothes the mind like the chorus of a thousand agonizing souls. He cracks a more devious grin, this one lined with a sense of morbid amusement. The first real emotion displayed yet by the Boston native. VJ: Very few feelings can match such a glorious sound, but a couple weeks ago I experienced one of those few. When myself and Peter Maxwell drove that heathen Gideon Poe’s skull into the mat, I swear I could hear his spinal column nearly fuse and the vertebrae almost split. To see that weak fool writhing on the mat, twitching like a slaughtered lamb on an altar of sacrifice….it was truly magnificent. The Great Old Ones were proud that night. He shakes his head forlornly, recalling the aftermath. VJ: Alas, that coward Rush Mason opts to again take the easy way out. Just as he so faintheartedly decided to take legal action against Maxwell for what he did a few weeks back, he panics and does so yet again. And now here I find myself, suspended for doing what I have been decreed to do by the masters. And in typical fashion, RPW rallies support for their fallen hero by initiating a ‘touching’ sit-down interview with that gimp. And just like the religiously faithful with their beloved Messiah, the public eats it right up. Poe shows that he still lives, and subsequently vows to gain his retribution by striking down those who nearly destroyed him and his way of life. Just like the Son Himself, Gideon Poe is resurrected and will vanquish the most evil forces of RPW. Myself and Peter Maxwell. Jack’s dark grin dissipates, replaced by a snarl. Hatred visibly flares up in his eyes, burning into the camera lens. VJ: Well folks, I’m here to bestow upon you a terrible revelation. In life, the forces of good do not always overcome the forces of evil. Often times the light is swallowed by the darkness. It’s time to stop kidding yourselves and open your eyes to the harsh realities of this universe. And that, in itself, is my very purpose for being in RPW. There is no escaping it. You see…..like it or not, humanity has been cast from the grace of the Father. A day will come when fires will rage across the Earth, and mankind will reap the full backlash for its sins. This race is doomed to rot like disease-ridden rats, and there is no savior or salvation to prevent it. VJ: Gideon Poe’s devotion is no different. He may have risen from his tomb with a promise to crush his enemies, and in turn that of the public he champions, but in the end it will be a futile mission. We proved Poe’s weakness on Adrenaline when we nearly made his spine into an accordion, and it won’t end there. Shanahan scoffs and shakes his head, slowly caressing his goatee. VJ: Poe….let me speak directly to you for the first time. Even at 100%, you were powerless to stop us from nearly destroying you forever. What in the name of Great Cthulhu himself makes you believe you stand any sort of chance against me when you still need help to even pull yourself out of bed in the morning? What sort of flawed logic are you trying to entertain here?? Hell…..make no mistake. You still only breathe on this wretched Earth because I chose to show you remorse. And trust me….that remorse did not please the Black Pharaoh. And thus, it won’t happen again. Again he looks away momentarily, his eyes set on the crypt silhouetted against the now nearly-hidden sun over the horizon. VJ: You could just as easily be a vegetable right now, spending the rest of your days in a hospital room kept alive by IV’s and machines. An intelligent man would realize this, count his losses, regroup, and simply leave things be and move on. But you can’t do that, can you? Every time you close your eyes, you see the children in the audience who cried as they carried you out on that stretcher, and you feel you need to be their hero yet again. Face it, boy…..this goes beyond simple vengeance for you, no matter what you try to sell on a big screen in some Barbara Walters tear-jerker of an interview. It always does for your type. I was there once, I know that. I don’t need the fame or the recognition anymore, Gideon. I’ve proven what I’m capable of time and again, and I’ll take it a step further when the time is right. When you overstep your bounds, I will not hesitate to finish the job myself and Maxwell started. And just as Christ was crucified 2000 years ago, such will be your own destiny. You can bear the cross on behalf of RPW, and those weak-minded buffoons you represent in the stands. Bruce draws a leather-bound tome from his robes. The title is scrawled in Latin print across the cover, though it’s initially indistinguishable. VJ: You can’t stop what I bring to this organization, chump. You will come to embrace what I have brought, else you will be left broken and crushed in my wake, just as my other enemies have been. And should you continue to follow your ridiculous crusade, the only thing that will save you is the Angel of Death. Mark my words, boy. VJ casts a glance above the camera, beckoning toward the crypt. VJ: We’re down here. We have more pressing matters to attend to…. With that statement, the image goes black.
