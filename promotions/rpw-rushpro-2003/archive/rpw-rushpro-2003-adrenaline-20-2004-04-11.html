POWELLING WOLF The sun still just hung high enough in the sky to tell it was early evening, still a little while before Adrenaline's first bell. Magnum Wolf couldn’t help but smile as he dragged the RPW cameraman eagerly along with him. This was just one more way he would stick it to his hated nemesis. She wouldn’t be able to forget her abortion. And if she had somehow, by any means, managed to forget it between last week and now… She sure as hell wouldn’t be able to once she arrived at the Halton. “Yes, that’s it, perfect! Start filming,” Wolf ordered the cameraman. The RPW official complied with the request, and began filming, well, the outside of the arena. Fans began to make their way to the stairs, waving happily at the camera and the chance to get on television. “Excellent, now, come on out everyone!” The Alpha Male grinned maniacally to himself, as he gestured to a group of cars, and the cameraman swung the camera around to follow Wolf’s pointing hand. What he saw almost made him drop the camera. A group of protestors, primarily female, emerged from a group of cars, carrying signs. Signs that said things such as “Karina Kills Children” and “RPW = Rejecting Pregnancy Wrestling”. Magnum Wolf had brought a protest to the doorstep of Rush Pro Wrestling. “Don’t stop filming! I want as many people as possible to see this! I want as many people as possible to see just what their lovely little K-Wolf has caused!” The protestors formed a line in front of the steps to the Halton, waving their signs and chanting. Most fans simply ignored the protest, however, a noticeable few simply turned around and headed back to their cars. As the camera followed one such car leaving the Halton’s parking lot, a long, black limousine pulls up. “Who the hell’s that?” the cameraman asked, with good reason. Unlike more affable promotions, limos were not common sights at RPW shows. “Oh, just the sponsor of this little protest. I organized it, sure, but you don’t think I have the money or time to coordinate such an effort, do you? No, so I found a friend to do it for me.” The terms “Magnum Wolf” and “friend” seemed to go together about as well as oil and water. However, as soon as the chauffeur opened the passenger door to the limo, a man stepped out, and eagerly shook Magnum Wolf’s hand. A man that had not been seen for several months. One-half of the only team to capture the PCW, FSW, and OSE World Tag Team Championships. And, more importantly, a stablemate of Wolf’s in FSW. William S. Powell had come to Rush Pro Wrestling. “So good to see you again, Magnum. How’ve things been treating you?” Powell’s usual cocky smirk rested on his face, eyes looking into those of the Alpha Male. “Got a woman that’s a pain in my ass, but that’s pretty typical. Yourself?” Both men laughed, somewhat dryly. “Not bad at all. Oh, I haven’t told you the good news yet…I’m on the roster!” Wolf smiled. He had simply called in William to lead a protest. Magnum realized quickly he had underestimated Powell’s reach. Still, he had to know how the hell Powell had become a member of RPW. “But how,” Wolf asked, curiously, “did you get hired by a company run by a man who’s wife you attacked, a man that fired you once for sexual harrassment?” Powell smiled at the thought. His hiring not only would help his long time friend Magnum, but it would be a way to stick it to the man who fired him from his position in PCW. Two birds…one stone. “Well, you saw all the other former Old School Entertainment talent being hired…I was one of them. The last one hired before Kannon signed, as a matter of fact, and from what I hear, he was none too pleased about it.” Wolf pondered the situation a bit, before deciding that he could leave Powell do this. He had other things to do…other plans to enact, other ways to ruin the life of Karina Wolfenden. “You’ll be okay here, right? Just keep the protest going. And when Karina Wolfenden shows up…” “Don’t worry,” Powell said, a gleam in his eye. “If Karina decides not to cooperate, I’ve got a very special bunker buster that I can drop on her mountains…” The Alpha Male whistled as he walked into the Halton, unbothered by the protestors. Behind the misogynistic exterior, Powell was an excellent competitor. Wolfenden would have her hands full… …And probably her mouth full, if Powell got his way. Wolf disappeared into the arena, as Powell took his place in front of the steps with the protest movement. Wolfenden was due any moment. The Master Debater cracked his knuckles, and began to bounce in place. Loosening up. Things could happen the easy way or the hard way. Karina could give in and let Powell have his way with her…or attempt to fight him, be beaten down, and then Powell would have his way with her. It was Powell’s typical cockiness. He obviously had no clue what he would be in store for when the K-Wolf arrived at the arena. But would she? Magnum Wolf had called in a man she had never even met, all for the purpose of ruining her life. The next salvo in the war between Magnum and Wolfenden was about to be fired. Powell only hoped that it could be fired down Karina’s throat. THE ONCE AND FUTURE KING Through the minimal light that crept into the Dale F. Halton Arena, RPW's fans checked their watches. It had hit the hour, and the 20th edition of Adrenaline should have already kicked off in it's usual energetic fashion. But, instead, all they saw were shadows in the dark, and mutterings passed up from ringside of movement in the ring. Had the lighting department fucked up? Were the cameras even rolling for the PPV audience? Or was it all part of the show. "C'mon!" some of the fans pleaded, some having arrived hours in advance for meet and greets with the roster. Then, without so much as a pyro burst or countdown, every light in the arena suddenly flicked on... And revealed two figures in the ring. The pop was instantaneous, and fitting. Standing in the centre of the ring, trademark cheeky smile across her lips, was Eleanor Kannon-Hall, who playfully turned to the corner of the ring and pointed. Sitting on the top turnbuckle, as casual as anything, sat RPW's unlikely savoir. Reluctantly, he'd been forced to play PCW's on-screen owner, the power-hungry Scientologist known as Xavier Kannon. But now, he had to do it for real. No gimmicks, no scripts, just the fate of a wrestling company, it's fans, and it's employees on his shoulders. That man was Christian Xavier Kannon, and these were to be his first words. With cheers, chants, and screams encircling the ring, a smiling Kannon hopped down into the ring - who's canvas seemed to have been slightly softened - and joined Eleanor. Side by side they stood, microphone in Kannon's hand, just looking out into the sea of fans. They were there because he'd stepped in and saved their beloved promotion. Despite attacking Poe and the DPR, they still popped like mad... But he knew fans were fickle, and he had to keep them all happy. "Hi!" Ellie chirped, leaning forward to grab a word into her husband's mic. Wolf-whistles and howls flooded the sound channels, the young woman having many fans in RPW already. "I would have sorted out some huge pyro display, maybe a Hollywood light-show to kick this thing off," Kannon began, raising his voice above the loud ambient noise. "But this isn't about fireworks. It's not about glitz, not about show... This is about putting on a product. I didn't step in so we could up production values through the roof, I'm not here so we can blow a wrestler's salary on special effects. I'm here to keep RPW in business, to keep it on the up, and make sure that nobody else EVER walked into the ring and says this place is closing." The fans were suitably impressed by his first words. If they wanted overly-lavish effects and piped-in cheers or boos to make the crowd sound anything but dead, they'd have waited for a PCW show to come to Charlotte. Week in and week out, the fans came back to see the roster, see the wrestling, and anyone who secured that for them would be a hero by default. "But to those saying Xavier Kannon is back, I'm afraid your very wrong," he then stated, a hint of sadness in his voice. "We might look the same, we might have the same tattoos and share the same birthday, but he's not the man who bankrolled this place. I'm Christian Alexander Kannon, my name was on the cheque that helped make RPW more than just a dream, and while I was there at the start, I'll be there at the end... ...Or rather, make sure that it never ends." The charisma that helped make Xavier Kannon a star was returning. The career threatening injuries had come as close to healing as was possible, fatigue had been purged from his body, and Kannon was looking the man he once was. He looked 28 again, he looked like a man who had more years before him than behind... And he once again looked the kind of man you could believe in. "Now, last week, at the end of Adrenaline, I stamped my name on RPW," he continued, words now sounding more business-like as Eleanor turned to look up at her man's face. "In the ring you may have seen good guys and bad guys, your side and theirs, solution and threat... But to me, all I saw were 5 men and a boy acting above their station. RPW's fate will not be decided by it's wrestlers. It won't be decided in matches. RPW's future is in it's fan's hands. You pay the wages of everyone who calls this place their home... ...And your love will not be tainted by power-struggles." On the mic, Kannon looked as natural as a bird in flight. The words came smoothly, backed up by subtle yet effective mannerisms, and changes in tone that gave the mere words body and substance. "But RPW's future will be the only thing that isn't decided in the ring," Kannon then tantalisingly revealed, enjoying stating his sentences with little teasers. "From now on, if you want your voice to be heard on Adrenaline, you need to earn it... In the squared circle here." There was the expected pop as the pumped RPW fans reacted to the prospect of more hard-fought in-ring action. "Anyone on a wrestling contract is being paid to show up to Adrenaline," Kannon divulged, passion for wrestling returning to his voice. "It's my money, it's your money, and it's our advertiser’s money. Now, I'm just speaking for myself here, but the others will agree with me, I'm sure... But I don't pay people to talk. I don't pay people to care about their own little agendas. I don't pay people to do the minimum required to get by. I pay people to be STARS, I pay them to be ICONS. I pay the to put on the best wrestling show money can buy. So talk all you want, but you'd better all back the words up out here, where it matters." His "State of the Union" was going down well, despite his bemusing actions at the end of last week's show. "Those rules apply to everyone, including me," Kannon then added, causing a few to hush. "I've come out here and said my piece, and now I'm billing myself for it. I was going to give you all a World Title main event tonight, pitting Gideon Poe against Jason O'Neil..." Knowing exactly what the reaction would be, Kannon paused before the huge pop even began, everyone thrilled at the prospect of seeing the enemies battle. "But I'm sure that after last week, both of them wouldn't mind getting a few licks in on me," Kannon beamed, coming alive in the spotlight. "So I'm going to wedge myself in between them... And give you Gideon Poe against Jason O'Neil against ME... For the RPW World Heavyweight Championship." If his aim was to get the RPW fans whipped up into a frenzy before the first bell had even rang, Kannon had succeeded. They were lapping it up, eating from the palm of his hand. "So I've said my piece, set your main event... Now, just sit back and enjoy the show." THE PATH OF MOST RESISTANCE A call to her cell phone had told Karina Wolfenden what would await her outside the Dale F. Halton Arena. The concerned friend urged her to take one of the sleepy, back roads onto the site and avoid any confrontations... But Kari wasn't in a mood to skulk about or shrink away. She was in a mood to roar. Through mirrored red sunglasses, seen through the opened visor of her red and black helmet, Karina sat astride her Ducati 748 and looked down at the lights. The Charlotte-based Campus was alive at night, a beacon of activity in the placid evening... And it was where Karina Wolfenden was ready to ride into, with no shame, only defiance. A roar was expelled from the powerful machine as it's tyres clung for dear life to the rough tarmac, propelling the K-Wolf with it as acceleration threatened to tear her body from it's steed. Each rev was a warning shot to Wolf and his allies as Karina stared into the full beam in front of her, waiting until it lined up the entrance to the Halton Arena's grounds... Where the despised and corrupt William S. Powell would be waiting with his orchestrated demonstration. Karina didn't care if they were expressing their opinions, or if they were just poor college students given 20 bucks to hold a sign for an hour, she'd fire a warning shot across their bows. Drenched in amber from the harsh outside lighting, Powell was already trying to work his slimy charm on one of the young women in the crowd. "So I said, I can't tell you anything about weapons of Mass Destruction, Miss Campbell, but if you bend over the desk and hike your skirt up, I'll show you how a weapon of Ass Destruction works!" The girl looked thoroughly repulsed, soured, as if she wanted to vomit. "Yeah, that's exactly the expression her face had during my little 'demonstration'... You know her?" It was easy to see how Wolf and Powell were able to get along so well. The sickened woman took her placard and shoved it back into Powell's chest, before storming off with a look of thunder about her. "Hey, at least I didn't knock her up," Powell cockily appealed to her. "Saves the problem of a love child!" Tilting his head just a little, William observed the young woman's ass as she stormed off up the hill... Where a light was growing in size, along with a gutsy roaring sound. "Tsk, these Students and their noise," Powell sighed as the engine's growl continued to slice through the calm evening. "And where is Karina, she should be here by no-... Oh..." Powell's narrow eyes turned to the foot of the hill, where the scarlet Ducati sent a cloud of dust up off the un-even tarmac. Powell's body was singled out by the bike's headlight, it's powerful beam causing his stars and stripes tie to shimmer. "Don't take me now, Lord," Powell muttered as the machine shot forward, "I still haven't given my intern her birthday cigar..." Flinging his body from the bike's path, Powell immediately felt the moisture of the damn grass soak into his thin white shirt as he rolled. Spluttering as he wiped a smear of mud from his lips, Powell watched the scarlet Superbike flash past, pummelling his ears with it's harsh roar. The crowd who'd gathered at Powell's request all turned and looked down at him as he pulled himself up and began to brush the grass and leaves from his smart clothes. "You see that lack of regard for human life?" Powell preached while adjusting his tie, trying to keep up appearances. "THAT is why we must protest against her, until she learns." His followers and employees went back to the chants and sign-waving as Powell seethed under his breath, angered at being thwarted. HAUNTING He sat in his locker room. The night was young and business was to be taken care of. Minion had left shortly before to do some signings for the fans outside who couldn’t get a ticket in. He had enough time before any sort of business tonight. Rubbing his hands together, Hessian stared at the floor from the comfort of his steel chair, thinking. …The Global Title…it is my new objective. To capture it and make it my own. I have spent too long and fought too hard and for what? To hear the screams and see the blood spilling? It is time I took a title, they will realise who I am and what I can do. I have slain the immortal…I will slay the Global Champion…I will become immortalised…no one will slay me… His thoughts filled his head and occupied him so heavily he almost missed the guitar riff that suddenly filled the air. Turning his head and glancing around the room, the guitar music seemed to be coming from all around, loud enough to be heard, but quiet enough that its source could not be judged. He recognised the tune almost instantly, and was soon on his feet searching for the source of the strumming. What song was he hearing? Seasons In The Abyss. “This has got to be a damn joke…who’s there!” Hessian yelled, lifting ceiling panels and moving lockers aside in search of the source. The song it seemed was emanating from all around, or perhaps from within the confines of his own mind? “STOP IT! WHO’S PLAYING THAT INFERNAL MUSIC?!” Hessian screamed, throwing his arms up in the air and bringing them down on the steel chair, heavily denting the seat. Suddenly, the music seemed to shift, filling the entire room but moving closer to the door. Hessian moved with the music, closer to the door. Quickly grabbing the door the Goliath ripped it open, throwing himself into the hallway in time to hear the sound of “Seasons In The Abyss” by Slayer drowning out and fading to silence, accompanied by a raspy laughing. Looking around him down the empty corridor Hessian shrugged his shoulders and slammed a fist into the wall as he shouted obscenities down the hall. Heading back into his locker room, the Mercenary of Satan contemplated what had just happened. He was sure he hadn’t just daydreamed it, and in fact he had heard the theme of………of that demon. Was this a sign? Or simply a prank? Hessian could only hope he’d find out soon. CHRIS STORM vs. KWOLF So You Wanna Be A Rock Superstar? Live Large? Big House? Five Cars? As the sounds of Cypress Hill burst from the ringside speakers, the RUSH ProVision began to play Chris Storm's video reel. Rapid-fire clips of hard Suplexes and painful submission holds flashed on the screen, separated by the shining image of his beloved Mid-South title. Out onto the stage Storm strolled, opening his bomber jacket to show the decorative plate of his Mid-South championship belt. With a smirk across his lips, Storm's view panned the crowd, not caring that all were hurling abuse at him. Shaking his head at their ignorance, Storm set off down the aisle, teasing a slap to the few out-stretched hands, before pulling away jus to rile them. "Our opening bout is for the RPW MID-SOUTH CHAMPIONSHIP!" Jerry Quecard expelled from his powerful lungs, "and is scheduled for one fall!" Storm bounded up the steps, before athletically springing over the ropes and into the ring, pausing for someone to show the briefest hint that they were impressed... Only leaving him to fume as his athletic prowess was ignored. Sliding his jacket off and dumping it in his corner, Storm exchanged words with the hostile fans at ringside before un-buckling his belt and taunting them with the coveted prize. "In the ring, the CHAMPION!" Quecard then announced, his grand words still unable to whip up any support for Storm. "From New York City, weighing in at 253 pounds... He is the PERFECT STORM... THE LAST, GREAT TECHNICAL WRESTLER... THE HUMAN SUPLEX MACHINE... THE IRON MAN HIMSELF... CHRISSSSS STORMMMMM!!!" Even Storm seemed impressed by Quecard's introduction, likely inspired by Kannon's orders and mandates. As Storm begrudgingly handed his belt over to referee Mick Matslap, "Followed the Waves" by Auf der Maur began to brew deep in the speakers. Much to Storm's aggravation, the arena lights dimmed and took on a crimson hue... A scene which was then complimented by a giant pop as Karina Wolfenden strode out onto the stage. Still clad in her black, red, and white bike leathers, and with her red hair remaining tightly pulled back into a ponytail, the K-Wolf stormed down the aisle... Not even extending her gloved hands to the fans. "And his opponent," Quecard then hurriedly continued, seeing the speed at which the K-Wolf was approaching. "From Albany, New York... Weighing 156 pounds... KARINA WOLFENDENNNNN!!!" As the lights came back on, Storm stepped to the middle of the ring and guarded it as if it were his own. Undeterred by Storm's command of the squared circle, Karina leapt straight up onto the apron and grabbed the top rope. Before Storm could react, Kari hopped to the top rope with a Springboard, before launching herself at the Champion with a Pump Dropkick. Matslap called for the bell as soon as Kari entered the ring's airspace, not caring much for the Champion. Unable to block Karina's immediate attack, Storm took both her pumped feet in the chest, sending him flying back as Kari crashed down onto the mat. Obviously spurred on by Storm's comments towards her, as well as the actions of Wolf and Powell, Karina seemed ready to fight, and her fists remained permanently clenched. Protesting against what he felt was an unfair start to the match, Storm ranted at Matslap as he stumbled up... But Karina was right on him. With a stinging Snap Kick, Karina landed her foot to Storm's chest, causing the Champion to cringe. As soon as her foot had landed, Karina sent a right hook to Storm's chin, not hitting it hard, but the desire was there none the less. The fans exploded with each contact made, getting behind the K-Wolf on what was her chance to finally get herself back on track. Then, arcing her right foot up high over Storm's shoulder, Karina landed a Crescent Kick to the Champion's right cheek, knocking him off balance. Seeing Storm's solid stance disrupted, Wolfenden surged forward and toppled him, immediately straddling the despised wrester and raining down right hands. But alas, Karina was neither a brawler nor an Ultimate Fighter, and it wasn't long until Storm saw a chance to counter. Blocking a furious yet wayward right, Storm pulled Karina forward and tucked one of his legs between their bodies, allowing him to take Kari over with a crude Monkey Toss. Spinning on the mat, Karina turned without even thinking, and surged back at Storm... Only to be mowed down with a Straight-Arm Clothesline that snapped her head back. Looking down at Karina, Storm shook his head in disappointment, as if he was expecting more. He then got his wish as Karina spun again on the mat and thrust both feet up into his ribs. Caught off guard, Storm stumbled a pace back and gave Karina room to execute a kip-up... But the moment her feet touched down, Storm up-rooted her with a punishing Belly-to-Belly Suplex. As their bodies thumped against the mat, Storm immediately hooked Karina's leg. 1... 2-NO!!! Much to the Champion's frustration, the K-Wolf wormed a shoulder out before the 2 even hit. Grabbing a handful of Wolfenden's hair, Storm yanked her up, only for the full-of-fight Kari to score a right hand to his mouth. Although Storm was only dazed for a fraction of a second, the K-Wolf didn't hold back and launched herself into a Roundhouse Kick, which was just what Storm was expecting. Catching Karina’s leg at an awkwardly high angle, Storm then launched her up and over with a Capture Suplex, sending Kari's back crashing against the mat. With no chance to manoeuvre and soften the landing, Kari took the full brunt off the move, numbing her body. Springing up, Storm was as jubilant as if he had just won, having taken great pleasure in thwarting Kari. On the mat, Kari held her back and slowly began to rise, only for Storm to capture her with a front facelock. As soon as Karina stood, Storm hammered her back with a forearm, dropping her right back down again. Not deterred, the stubborn Karina again climbed to her feet, only to take another hard forearm that dropped her to one knee. Despite the hard forearm she knew was awaiting her, Karina was focussed only on getting up. Then, before Storm's forearm could hit, she flung her right leg back, arcing it over her back to catch Storm on the chin. Taken by surprise, Storm grabbed his mouth and stumbled back, while a single-minded Karina just charged right for him, her temper getting the better of her judgement. While a calm and calculated attack might have taken advantage of the situation, Kari's charge only gave Storm her momentum to use as he pleased. Reacting before Karina even hurled a fist his way, Storm stepped forward and whipped Karina over with a Powerslam, shaking the canvas with it's thumping impact. Again, Storm covered right away and waited for Matslap to catch up. 1... 2.. 3-NO!!! Ruining any hopes of an early win for Storm, Karina shot a shoulder free, breaking the pinfall. Again, Storm grabbed Karina by the hair and yanked her right up... Before soaring to the corner and ramming Wolfenden's head into the turnbuckle. Feeling the core of steel through the pad, Kari was dazed after impact, and couldn't resist as Storm whipped her to the opposite corner. She was flung fast, too fast to try and use it to her advantage... But that wasn't likely to stop Kari in her state of mind. Even though she was too close to get any kind of clean landing, Karina leapt up for the middle buckle, nearly catching her right foot under the rope. After finally securing her footing, Karina hopped to the top and prepared to fly off at an on-rushing Storm with a Moonsault... ...But he was already standing behind her, able to sneak up while Wolfenden battled to balance. Despite the huge array of Suplexes in his arsenal that could have broken Kari in half from a great height, Storm instead chose something a little more basic... He shoved her. Without even a fraction of the footing she'd need to resist the push, Karina had no chance of staying put and was sent tumbling down from the top. Down she plummeted, just managing to land on her feet but having to soften the landing with a roll, which unfortunately sent her crashing into the steel guard rail. Storm had to stop himself from laughing out load as he saw Kari crash land, before he ignore Matslap's orders and rolled outside. Strolling around the corner, Storm looked down at Kari with pity, before stomping her back down to the mats. The fans at ringside hurled abuse his way, but Storm just shrugged it all off as he pulled Karina back up. Aiming at the ring post, Storm then whipped Kai at the steel... Only for her to grab the pole and swing back onto the apron... Where her left foot slipped and nearly sent her tumbling off the apron. Seeing a chance to show off, Storm leapt onto the apron in front of Wolfenden, then pulled her down with a hop-up Hiptoss Slam. Kari's back crashed down onto the mats, hard enough to feet the unforgiving concrete below. Not what she had planned when her body rode it's swing around the post. Grabbing Kari by the collar, Storm pulled her up and rolled her back into the ring. Pointing down at the broken K-Wolf, Storm riled the fans up even further, but didn't notice their roar of displeasure was helping bring Karina to. Distracted by the fans, Storm turned away from the K-Wolf and began jawing at some of his detractors in the front row. Rolling out under the bottom rope, a pained Karina pulled herself up and grabbed the top rope. Yanking back on the cable, Karina tried to launch herself onto the top with a slingshot, but her left leg dragged behind, having to settle for the middle rope instead. "Dammit," the groggy Karina muttered, attracting Storm's attention once more. Acting quickly, Storm simply reached up and grabbed a handful of Karina's hair, before launching himself forward and pulling Wolfenden down with a crude Bulldog. Holding her face, Karina rolled away and instinctively tried to get back to her feet... An act which Storm was more than happy to help with. Gripping Karina tightly, Storm then thrust her up with an over-head Belly to Belly Suplex... ...Leaving the booing fans to hush as their favourite crashed down onto the canvas. With a smile plastered across his lips, Storm crawled over to Karina and covered, hooking her far leg while Matslap slid over. 1... 2... 3-NO!!! Karina kicked out, but without a fraction of a second to spare. Angered, Storm yanked Karina up and thrust his knee into her ribs, knocking some of the fight out of her. With the K-Wolf doubled over, Storm grabbed her for a Gutwrench, before heaving the limp Kari over... Only for her to come back to life and flip through, landing on her feet. With Storm just managing not to collapse down to the mat, Karina spun her body around and dropped the Champion with a Flat-Drop DDT, the only move she could have pulled off given their body positions. Although the move showed there was still some fight in her, Karina's counter wasn't as effective as it needed to be, merely inconveniencing Storm. Determined to win the race up to their feet, Karin scrambled up before she was ready, and nearly collapsed back down again. Seeing Storm pull himself up in front of her, Karina didn't even think as she lunched forward and flung a right hook at him, a punch that Storm could have ducked with his eyes closed. Catching Karina as she was about to fall, Storm grabbed her in a waistlock... ...Then flung her over with a Release German Suplex. Although the release angle and speed of the move gave her no chance in hell of doing so, Karina's stubbornness took control and she tried to flip through, and act that only caused the K-Wolf to land on her knees, doing more damage than the Suplex was likely to. Grabbing her knees, Karina cringed as she felt Storm's shadow be cast across her. Taking hold of both Karina's arms, Storm pulled her up into a Double Underhook, then seamlessly heaving her over with a Butterfly Suplex, keeping the arms gripped so all the impact was taken by Wolfenden's tail bone. The hard knocks she'd taken from Storm took their toll, and Karina was hurting all over her body... And cursing her rash attempts to land a blow on Storm. Unable to fight him off, Karina was again pulled up and grabbed in a half-nelson. Kari knew there was a second or so window to try and counter, but had nothing in the tank as Storm pulled her over with a Half-Nelson Suplex, folding her up on impact before she just slumped out onto her front. All around the ring, RPW fans were jeering Storm, but his ice-man cool demeanour just filtered them all out. Drawing a finger across his throat, Chris signalled the end of the match, then heaved a limp Wolfenden up. Without any struggle, he lifted Wolfenden into a torture rack position, the set-up to his infamous RyoPlex. Knowing what was coming, the fans increased the volume of their jeers, but it did nothing to halt the Champion. Thrusting her up, Storm held Kari in a reverse Military Press for longer than usual, almost taunting her, giving her a chance to counter... But there was nothing there. Then, in the all-too-familiar motion, Storm dropped her down and increased the angle of Kari's descent to land a sickening RyoPlex, folding Karina up once more. As Storm sat up, Karina remained doubled over, her shoulders down on the mat while her feet touched the canvas either side of her head. Unable to resist, Storm then spun around and pulled his body over Kari's, pinning her in a position he'd more then likely occupied during a porn shoot. 1... 2... 3!!!! A smirk crossed Storm's lips as the bell rang, and he pushed Wolfenden all the way over, causing her to collapse down onto her front. "The winner of this match, and STILL RPW MID-SOUTH CHAMPION...
