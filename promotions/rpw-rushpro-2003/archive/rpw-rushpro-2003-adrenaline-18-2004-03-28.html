I KNOW SOMETHING YOU DON’T KNOW! The show was just beginning, and the fans were charged to see their favorite stars. At this point in the show, they wanted their Karina Wolfenden. They wanted their Gideon Daray Poe. They would even be willing to accept a little Dread Pirate Roberts. What they didn't want right now is exactly what they got. Magnum Wolf. There was a certain bounce in Wolf's step. As though he knew something that no one else knew. And perhaps that was true enough. He was whistling to himself, and almost skipping he was so giddy. He was out in the parking lot, heading into the arena. As he walked past the front door, Wolf hit the underside of the security guard's chin, and crowed. "Buck up, chum......p. Things are only going to get better!" The security guard eyed him carefully, and replied, "I'm doing fine right now. What the hell do you mean?" "Well, I know you're masking your pain... but don't worry... Magnum Wolf is going to get himself another win tonight. I'm going to even up the score with that little pink haired hussy Skankarina," was the answer from Wolf, who didn't bother to stand around and wait for another response from the security guard. He just made his way on into the building. Coming from his lips were the notes to "Nobody Knows the Troubles I've Seen". Even the incredible chorus of boos that he heard when he mentioned getting a win over Karina tonight didn't deter his smile from beaming brighter than it had since he was a face. Down the hall he went, as though he was on his way to a very specific place. He stopped in front of a door, and smiled to himself. LITTLE AL is all that was on the door. The fans once again fell into a chorus of boos, and Wolf just swatted at the air as if to make them be quiet so that he could do his business in here. RAP RAP RAP! There was no response from inside. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! Still no response from inside. This brought about cheers from the people in the crowd. This response ALMOST took away Wolf's smile... but not quite. He calmly turned back to the door, and kicked it in. After dusting off his shirt a little bit, he gazed into the room to find Little Al staring at a computer screen with a phone on his shoulder. The look on Al's face as he turned to see Wolf kicking in his door was that of pure amazement. Actually, beyond amazement. Who would have the nerve to kick in HIS door? "What... the... fuck...?" was all that Al could choke out. "AL! Great to see you, buddy! I know there's a match between Hessian and Karina for tonight... but trust me that that match will be cancelled and there's going to be one between myself and Karina. Because, you know, she owes me a win and everything," Magnum explained casually. Little Al stared right through Wolf, "Are you serious, Magnum? You don't think I have enough to worry about right now? Between Rush Mason's black ass coming back and trying to ruin things for me, and everything involved in trying to keep this federation afloat... now you want to fuck with my card? Jesus, man!" "There's nothing to worry about, Al. You'll still get a great match, and I'm going to get my win! No problems, right?" asked a very concerned for Al's well being Magnum Wolf. Ok, so it was fake. "Wait a minute... how exactly are you going to get that match? Didn't you try this stunt last week?" asked the young entepreneur. Magnum smiled wickedly all of the sudden. He pet his mustache carefully, and winked, "It's like this... I know something that you don't know about Karina Wolfenden. In fact, I know something that NONE OF YOU know about Karina Wolfenden. But I can't tell you what it is..." "Ok, fine, I don't car----" "FINE! I'll tell you! It's like this... Karina is not as... WAIT A MINUTE! I will not be tricked into giving away my secret by you!" Magnum angrily jumped up, and turned to leave the room, "I don't appreciate your RUSE Mr. Al. Good day to you sir." Little Al stared at the door as Wolf uselessly attempted to put the door back onto it's hinges. Obviously, it wasn't working, so Wolf gave up and threw the door back on the ground while making a grunting noise. Little Al was amazed. "Maybe hiring that guy was a mistake in the first place." Little Al looked at the pile of papers on the table and sighed. “I better get this place cleaned up. Damn Mason.” AGAINST DOCTORS ORDERS Loud screaming could be heard coming from the parking lot arena. The door from the parking lot to the arena was slammed open and Chris Storm appeared in full wrestling gear carrying his wrestling bag with him. From behind him a man dressed like a doctor appeared. “You are not cleared to wrestle; you are still suffering the effects of the concussion.” He yelled to Chris. The Perfect Storm paid him no attention and continued to walk. “I have a score to settle tonight with a certain Dave Gibson and his bitch.” Backstage reporter Caroline North was on hand to see the whole thing transpire and rushed over to Storm to try and get a word in with him. “Storm, are you saying that you will indeed accept Shawn Steward’s challenge for a match later on?” she asked him, holding the microphone up to his lips. Chris Storm stopped for a second and looked at her. When he finally stopped, his hair shifted to the sides showing a bandage over his forehead. “I’m not only accepting his challenge, I’m going to break him and Old School in half. They want to mess with the Last Great Technical Wrestler they can end up like all my other enemies, gone!” He started to walk off again but Caroline cut him off again, “how is your condition after the attack from Kohana and Tessa Windsor?” “They will get what is coming from them as well. Next week I’ve got plans for the two of them, trust me.” Chris shot back getting annoyed at the thought of his enemies once again out numbering him. Caroline North had one more question she wanted to address before she would allow Chris to return to what he was going about doing, “what do you make of this new relationship between Matt Jackson and Tessa Windsor?” The question brought a complete stop to Chris. It seemed he nearly stopped breathing as well. “Let Matt Jackson fuck up his career over a girl. He can be the next Schmoopie. Everyone knows after I left Tessa she’s just been trying to find another guy to replace me but the call couldn’t cut it like I could.” “Matt Jackson seems confident that he can though, even suggesting he maybe better than you.” Chris looked at her with a half smile; he wanted her to bring that up. “Better than me?” Chris opened his wrestling bag and pulled out the Mid South title. “Do you know what this is Caroline?” he asked her in an I-know-but-I-want-you-to-answer tone. “It’s the RPW Mid South title.” She answered correctly. “And how did I win it?” It did not take long for her to answer “in a tournament.” “And just who did I beat in that tournament?” Chris knew where this series of questions would lead. Caroline had to try and remember the order but answered back “Matt Jackson in the first round, then Totakoxe in the second, and finally Tessa Windsor in the finals.” The Champ rubbed his chin and smiled slyly. “I defeated Matt Jackson and Tessa Windsor to earn this title.” Chris slung the Mid South title over his shoulder to free up his hand. He reached into his wrestling bag and pulled out the Global title. “I won this as well. That’s two titles that prove I’m the best damn Technical wrestler on this roster. Let Matt talk about how he did better than me in PCW. This is RPW, think Rush Mason cares what either of us did there? You think Little Al cares what we did in PCW? I’ll tell you a secret, THEY DON’T!” Chris tapped both titles “they only care about here and now. They care that I’ve helped RPW’s rep as the best league in PTC. We can claim we have the most talented roster because I’m known as the best wrestler there now.” “Tessa, Kohana, Matt, Dave, and Shawn all are going to pay for their actions as of late. I’ll see to it myself. I’ve yet to be stumped in RPW and I don’t plan to start now.” Chris tossed the titles back into the bag and moved past the reporter to finish what he came here to do. SCHOOL’S IN SESSION! The opening drum lick to “Paradise City” hit, and Dave Gibson’s theme music began in earnest. In the ring, a lectern had been set up at the center of the squared circle. A pair of stools also had been positioned inside the ring, along with a chalkboard with “School’s In Session” written upon it. And with that, Mr. Old School began to walk toward the ring. Wearing another immaculate three-piece suit, which still did not distract total attention from the neck brace he wore, Gibson headed for the ring… surprisingly alone. Wielding only a microphone, he sneered at the fans who booed his mere approach to the squared circle. Had they already forgotten Gibson’s heroics in the last days of OSE? His attempt to wrest referee duties of the final pay-per-view from Tempest? His literal brush with death as he battled Hessian at End Game? Ingrates… But that was a rant for a different time. At the moment, Gibson basically surmounted the ringside steps, entering through the ropes and raising the microphone as his music quickly subsided. “My name is Dave Gibson… and I AM OLD SCHOOL!” Even before Gibson could complete his catchphrase, the boos showered down upon him. Gibson turned slowly, surveying the hatred glares and words of the fans seated at ringside. “Oh, I see… Just because I’ve thrown away the tattered shorts… the “Shut Up And Wrestle” shirts… just because I’ve tried to BETTER myself… you people think I’m a sellout. A bastard. A traitor. Well you know what? I don’t have to worry about pleasing you people any more. Because I’m a manager. A personality. Whether you cheer me, or you boo me, I don’t give a damn, because my check will be just as fat the next week! “Oh… make no mistake. I know that a lot of the scrubs back in that locker room are concerned about RPW closing, but as for Dave Gibson? I’ll be just fine. “Remember that Rush Mason was brought in by ME for the three-team war against the Red Army and Asylum. He owes me. And… when this shithole closes… I anticipate that I’ll be well-compensated… with more money than most of you peons make in a year’s time!” Gibson laughed at his final remark, as the boos broke out in greater ferocity. “But we’re not here to talk about me. Oh no. This is “School’s In Session.” A chance for a hardened soul to come out here, and give his own take… on what it means to be Old School. And this week’s guest… they don’t get more Old School than him. See, this is a man… who believes that what you do inside the ring matters more than what you can do with a microphone. This is a man who believes professional wrestling is about more than backstage politics… or a flashy gimmick… or engaging your opponent in a damn Lincoln-Douglas debate! This is a man whose hands and feet should be registered as lethal weapons, a man who gives no quarter and expects none in return… he is the uncrowned OSF World Heavyweight Champion, and the next great superstar in this business… ladies and gentlemen, I give you the first member of Dave Gibson’s managerial stable… so stand up with me and ALL HAIL the King of the Cage… SHAWN STEWART!” Stewart’s name is met with a mixed reaction from the fans as the Panther walked to the ring, dressed in a simple warmup suit. Moving at a brisk gait, he ignored the jibes and the cheers, rolling into the ring. All business, he shook his manager’s hand and then took the microphone from him. Shawn's eyes cut through the air, his piercing gaze moving slowly. After a moment, he took the microphone in his right hand and lifted it. “Where has Shawn Stewart been?” he began slowly. “That's what I've heard people asking. That's what people have been saying. Let me make something very clear; since the close of OSE, what's gone on in my life, by and large is none of anyone's business except mine.” The crowd didn't respond all that well. “Just because I used to be in OSE and now have shown up here, that doesn't mean you're obligated to know the details of my life.” Shawn took a moment to stroke his goatee, as if to aid his thoughts. “But, to be fair, what I'm doing here is a valid question. What am I doing here? When last you saw me, I was a prop for the return of Hessian and a fight between him and Clyde. Was I thrilled with that? Hardly. So my departure from the world of wrestling shouldn't have been a surprise, though it was to many people.” Shawn stopped and stepped forward to the edge of the ramp. “Why am I here? I'm not a wrestler by a long shot. I'm a fighter, through and through. During my time off, I will tell you I spent a lot of time on the mats and even a few times in the octagon. So my return is to remake my name in this world, but not in the way you're used to. This is for the way I compete, the way I fight. This is to re-establish who I am and why you care.” The crowd relented in its reactions, almost as if absorbing the words of the King of the Cage. “So the fact is that RPW lies before me like a lamb for slaughter, not for people like you're used to seeing. It's ready to be taken apart, bit by bit by someone that is completely different from anyone else.” Stewart took just a moment to lift a finger towards his face. “You're looking at a man that doesn't punch for the point of buying time to get that next move in, you're looking at a man that punches to hurt the person on the other end. You're used to submission holds to make the other man quit. With me, you'll see holds designed to injure.” Shawn lifted his eyes upward. “Why am I back? I have a promise to fill to an old opponent, I have something to do for him that he never did do. Shawn Stewart is back with a purpose and right here, right now, Shawn Stewart is ready to begin down that path.” With that, Stewart dropped the microphone, and Gibson raised his arm, pointing at his client. The fans once again responded in mixed fashion, with some cheering Stewart while others jeered his association with Gibson. Yet all could respect this potentially devastating union. REGRETS He still had pain all over his body. Moving wasn’t just a hassle, it was a struggle. Jimbo had to go through some medical clearance that resulted in Jimmy Diablo’s painkillers benig confiscated for being legal off-shore (he used them in American Samoa) but not in the contiguous United States. Propaganda beat the ever-loving hell out of Jimmy Diablo just yesterday in the PCW Face-to-Face challenge. To be going from that to yet another match tonight against Vangellus Oxios? That wasn’t a good thing. The fact that he was even standing after the punishment that he suffered last night was a miracle, as well as a testement to his tenacity (and to the effectiveness of ice baths). Even so, a rather large part of him was dreading his booking decisions made last week during a euphoric alcohol-induced state of consciousness. "You're looking ready to go," Jimbo joked. Jimmy Diablo, dressed in jeans and a white T-shirt, took great effort to sit down. Getting up would be even harder. "I feel about as good as Bobby Brown right now." "Ouch," said Jimbo, wiping some bread crumbs from a sandwich he ate earlier. His blue silk shirt didn't need to be yanked down from its place in perfection by sesame seeds from the great burger that Marlene in Catering gave him. The great thing about wrestling is that it's not real, right? What the hell are you thinking, you dumbass mark? Of course it's real! People don't get injured, lose blood, and kill themselves for wrestling as a special effect. Jimmy Diablo had serious health issues going into every match that he might wrestle in the future. Did that matter to him? No, that would require some damn sense. No, Jimmy just continued to show up to the shows even though there'd probably be a time in the near future in which the rod in his neck gets knocked out of position, the vertebrae drift from their alignment, and his entire head goes irreparably limp. Diablo seemed willing to kill himself. And for what? "So," Jimmy asked, "did you call this Oxios guy like I asked?" Jimbo searched the pockets to his black cotton shorts. "Hold on a sec, JD, I'm looking for that ID badge that says Secretary on it." "Dammit, Jimbo, he needs to know about my condition," Diablo asserted. Jimbo gesticulated wildly, throwing his arms into the air. "It won't matter! You're the one who's here, it's on you for assumption of risk. You think anyone's going to take you seriously if you're like, I'm going to make a return to wrestling and show everyone that I can still do this! But please go easy on me, I don't want my neck to get messed up or anything." Jimbo was the brains of the pair. Sad part was, he was only of average intelligence. Diablo slapped his right thigh. "So, what, instead of fulfilling my purpose in life and possibly die, you want me to give up all my pride and spirit and just pretend to live?" Must... Stop... The Melodrama! Enter the Diamond. "Excuse me?" Adrienne Diamond knocked on their door, inviting herself in. Jimmy looked over, not quite paying attention to the voice that interrupted them. "What-- oh, a lady. Who are you?" Ms. Diamond, a little more casual in the gray RPW T-shirt and her own faded blue jeans, stepped forward and offered her hand. "I'm Adrienne Diamond. I work for Vangellus Oxios, your opponent for tonight." "What's he want?" Jimbo asked. He got defensive for Jimmy; someone had to look out for him since Diablo seemed incapable of looking out for himself. Adrienne shook her head, "He doesn't want anything, really. I came here on my own." Jimmy Diablo grasped her hand. "So what might you be wanting?" Ms. Diamond had a folder in her left hand, tucked behind her back. His eyes shot open. A few more moments and he crumpled the paper. "What is it, JD?" Jimbo asked. His words fell on deaf ears. Taking a lighter out of his pocket, Diablo set fire to the pages with clear intentions to keep whatever he found from getting out. Finally turning his attention to Adrienne, Jimmy said, "Where did you find that?" "Your reaction could be taken as corroboration of the evidence, Mr. Flores." I'm not sure how much Mr. Diablo will tell you, though, if you don't already know." The woman was out of the door quickly. She had a brisk walking pace at all times. He didn't even look his friend in the eye. His match wasn't too soon to come yet, but he just left the locker room, not really wanting to talk about it. And even though they were new and most likely faces, the fans would still only give them moderate cheers because: 1. They were new. And… 2. They were facing The Forsaken. Both are small, feminine, and like playing with dolls. Making fun of him is so never gonna get old. Backing against the ropes, Helms ran towards Clyde, before jumping into the air, flipping himself forward as he dropped a leg across Clyde’s throat. The fans cheered the move, as Helms could feel the energy flowing through the arena, pumping adrenaline through his veins. While Clyde held his throat, Matt took to the second rope of the nearest turnbuckle, waiting for Walkins to stand. But whatever he was aiming to do would never happen, as James ran along the apron, pushing Helms off the turnbuckle. Clamping on a side headlock, Clyde wrenched it as he held Matt Helms in position near the ropes. APOLOGY NOT ACCEPTED After being teased and tormented for weeks by a certain individual, most people wouldn’t feel any shame in beating down that individual for a bit of revenge. Tessa however was not like that. For whatever reason, after last week Tessa had felt a certain guilt run through her mind after help beat down on her former tag partner and fiancée, Chris Storm. Perhaps she still had feeling for him, or perhaps she felt guilt for putting the odds against him, but either way she felt she need to apologize, even if was against her better judgment. “Chris!” She called out as she saw him walking backstage. It was obvious she was having second thoughts as she tugged nervously on the bottom of the tank top she included in her usual pink attire. The Last Great Technical Wrestler did not even bother to look over at her as she ran up to him. “Chris, do you have a minute?” she asked with a hint of hope in her voice. Chris would not reply to her, instead he continued to walk. Tessa kept up with him and stepped in front of him stopping him. Chris brushed his golden locks away from his forehead and stared at her. With the blonde hair moved aside Tessa could see the bandage on his forehead. “You nearly made me miss a show.” He muttered, turning away from her as he continued down the hall. “It could have been worse!” Wasn’t the best response to come from her mouth, but it was about all she could think of at the moment as she trailed behind him. Chris sidestepped Tessa and started walking toward where the ring is again. “Yeah it could have been what I have planned for you. Now if you don’t mind I have to break someone’s face.” “Well seeing as though you apparently can’t stand the sight of me anymore that very well might have been a good thing for you.” Whether or not Chris liked the words coming from Tessa’s mouth, he had to face the fact that what she was saying made a whole lot of sense. Chris stopped and looked at Tessa face to face. “By the time the next PPV comes I can promise that the whole Chris Storm & Tessa Windsor thing will come to an end one way or another. Do you understand me?” He shifted his Mid South title and continued on his journey towards the ring. Quite frankly tonight his concern was not focused on Tessa; it was focused on getting even with Dave Gibson and Shawn Stewart. But Tessa refused to be let Chris brush her off like this, after all she didn’t have to apologize to Chris, she chose to, so she felt that he should at least show her some sort of courtesy. “Yes I understand that you want me gone.” Tessa replied in a quiet tone, obviously feeling a bit disappointed that despite all the two had been through in the past together, Chris had grown so much of an ego that now he felt that even she didn’t deserve to be in his presence. “But what I don’t understand is why. What did I ever do to you?” He looked at her surprised, “What did you ever do?” Chris seemed taken back by what she asked. “Try being a cancer to my career! Have you forgotten the Schmoopies? If Matt Jackson is smart he’ll avoid you too before you destroy his career…” Chris stopped and thought about what he just said, “Ok so he would need to develop a career before you could destroy it but still you get my point.” Chris tilted his head causing his hair to move away from his eyes. “Simply put Tessa, you are not a wrestler. Staggering away, Nerezza allowed The Shade the time he needed to get back to his feet. Powerless, Poe could only hope for the 5-count to come fast enough. Poe sunk to the ground, gripping his neck and spluttering. Nerezza took up his position just behind him, fingers spreading again as the air raised into the air. Pools of blood were seen oozing between the shiny gloves, collecting on the mat.
