Intro The fans began hurling and tossing debris immediately at El Diablo. Some screamed in horror, some yelled in anger, some cried. Maybe Gideon Poe really was just a 23-year-old kid barely out of adulthood who took on more than he could handle when he sought revenge against those who had wronged him. The Mexican Devil was about to end it all, put an end to the corruption he swore was being acted out against him, and win the world title. Gideon Poe was about to be Liberated, and God knows that maybe it would be a blessing in disguise. Diablo yelled at the top of his lungs and pulled Gideon forward. Both men fell to the canvas as Gideon’s neck soared down to the canvas like a dart. Then the fans gasped. Somehow Gideon managed to roll backwards off El Diablo’s shoulders and land on his feet. The fans quickly broke into cheers as El Diablo himself went to the mat and felt nothing connect to the canvas after him. The Mexican Devil knew what had happened, but Gideon was already standing there waiting. El Diablo looked up and saw Gideon standing there. Diablo’s eyes turned the same crimson colour as the liquid staining the canvas. He quickly hauled himself up and charged at Gideon. Gideon was already positioned as the fans screamed at the top of their lungs in their final act of hope. El Diablo charged a few steps, as he did Gideon quickly lunged forward and thrust his foot out and super kicked El Diablo straight in the throat with a hard harsh sickening soft thud! CADAVER THEORY! El Diablo flew through the air as a blood, phlegm and saliva shot out of his mouth before he crashed to the canvas. Motionless, lifeless, and defeated. The fans bellowed at Gideon who was now standing tall and lonesome in the ring. He turned and seen El Diablo laying lifeless on the floor, and took one last look at the fans who were destroying their vocal chords by cheering and screaming at him. Gideon then walked to Diablo, dropped………. And made the cover! The referee dropped and made the count. 1 Everyone held their breath. 2 Everyone prayed. … … … … … … … … … … … … … 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The entire Halton Arena of North Carolina erupted and exploded in a ballistic unified cheer as the titanium steel roof blew off from its foundations. Gideon rolled off of El Diablo as the referee raised his hand high and mightily in the air. “10:45 Amsterdam Conversations” by Funeral For A Friend burst through the Halton Arena Public Access system. The winning cheers from the fans were more than enough for Gideon to get the adrenaline to rise to his feet and rest against the turnbuckle. Gideon looked out across the arena as the defining cheering pitch never dipped an octave. Poe barely knew what was going on. The look in his eyes was one of uncertainty, confusion, doubt, and almost skeptic fear. Above all, disbelief. That all faded however as the referee approached him slowly and stood in the center of the ring with the RPW World Title belt. Gideon looked at him almost mystified and limped to the center of the ring slowly, almost apprehensively. The referee smiled and nodded at Gideon who wiped the blood from his eyes. The referee handed the World Title belt to Gideon and than yanked his hand high up into the air. The fans outdid themselves once again and drowned out Gideon’s theme music as the ovation roared even louder as a set of dazzling fireworks shot up from the ramp ways lighting the arena with sparkling intensity. Gideon dropped both hands and then dropped to his knees. The referee patted him on the back and left the ring, leaving Gideon to stare at the magnificent beauty of the gold in front of him. Nobody could see it, but for the first time in years Gideon Poe began to cry. He’d come back from short-term paralysis to achieve a dream, which had always seemed too far out of reach to even contemplate. Gideon stayed there for a few moments, letting the arena lights reflect off the gold before his eyes. Poe then slowly looked up and for the first time properly took in the sight of the fans blaring and cheering his name. For once, Gideon Poe finally had a reason. A reason to truly feel alive. On that note, Gideon got his feet, ascended the turnbuckle and threw the title high above his head with both hands. The fans went ballistic with jubilant cheers of joy. Gideon had never won a single title before in his life, now he’d captured the most coveted. The fans loved him for it. He had represented hope in the sign of overwhelming adversity and had come through it alive, and also a champion. As Gideon then thrust the title into the air one more time, allowing the Halton Arena to be ignited in a dazzling flash of camera lights being clicked in unison, it became clear to everyone. Gideon Poe was the RPW World Heavyweight Champion. No matter what happened from here on in. Everyone would live through and remember tonight. For once, in RPW’s tenure, good had finally overcome evil. With that, the RPW copyright logo flashed at the bottom of the screen as Gideon Poe walked to the center of the ring and held the title up again for the fans. Even as he was smiling with tears slowly drying on his face, the always intense look in his eyes showed he knew what was awaiting him, but for tonight, all he had to do was celebrate. Celebrate as the first ever RPW World Heavyweight Champion.
