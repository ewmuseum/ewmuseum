EoD Updates Announced

28 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A post of the federation’s news desk as the Wayback Machine captured the front page its site then kept at www.ewnweb.com on 2 August 2004 (a fourth address for the RPW site, which the wing already holds at three): the post’s title, date and text. The poster’s line is not carried, as for the wing’s other news posts.
After the dust had settled at the end of another eventful Adrenaline, RPW's figurehead Christian Xavier Kannon confirmed a quartet of matches for the upcoming 'Eve of Destruction' show. 'In regards to the RUSH Pro Wrestling Title,' he began, knowing that both participants in the match were less than fond of him, 'I've taken a special 'precaution', something I felt necessary given the pair involved. It's well known that Karina and Magnum cannot stand one another, and that in 18 months neither has managed a decisive win... But Eve of Destruction WILL change that. I know that Magnum is a sneaky enough cheat to steal a win, just as I know that Karina's style itself is suited to pulling wins from nowhere. Both methods lead to controversy, which is something the belt has had enough of.' How did RPW's figurehead decide to remedy the situation? 'The main event on Sunday will be a 2/3 Falls contest,' he explained. 'Karina has enough wits about her to stop Wolf playing the same trick twice, while not even Magnum can call foul if Wolfenden pins him 2 falls in a match to win... This isn't a gimmick, it IS the end of their rivalry, no matter who takes the belt, and whether they like it or not.' As well as adding a stipulation to a match that had been brewing since the hotly-disputed 'The Kannon Effect' main event, Kannon confirmed another match booked over a month ago... The tag team contest between The Marvellous Brothers and the duo of Vangellus Oxios & Skylar Green. Despite being the earliest booked match for Eve of Destruction, it has had minimal buildup due to the fact that the teams have purposefully been attempting to stay away from each other until the night of the match. The Marvelous Brothers hold two wins over Oxios and Green, but with the latter two acting more as a team and Mike Marvelous trying to force his brother into a technical style with which he is somewhat unfamiliar, will they be able to increase that lead to 3-0, or will the tandem wrestlers chalk one up against one of the premiere teams in the RPW Tag Team Division? And while 2 of RPW's tag teams do battle, members of 2 of wrestling's hottest tag teams past and present will do battle, as Gaz 'The Jester' Green will square off against former RPW Champion, Gabe. Gaz will bring the managerial expertise of Eleanor Kannon-Hall with him to ringside, but will the presence of Scott 'Rampage' Matthews and an arena full of pro-Gabe fans cancel her out? With no count-outs and no disqualifications, look for crimson to mix with emerald as the pair go to war. When asked about his own match, and his physical state going into it, Kannon seemed reluctant to comment. 'All I can say is that I'm facing Brian, as he requested,' Christian explained. 'I am not an active wrestler in RPW, nor do I intend to become one, but sometimes circumstances take over.' With a champion who refuses to talk to him, a #1 contender who's mission is to change every choice he's made, a broken body which may not last a full match, and the constant pressure of RPW's worrying finances to worry about... Eve of Destruction could be a reference to Kannon's mental state just as much as Wolf's proximity to RPW's greatest prize. ...And we all know what he's been capable of in the past.
