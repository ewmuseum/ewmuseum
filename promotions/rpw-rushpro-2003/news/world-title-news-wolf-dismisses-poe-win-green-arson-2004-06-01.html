World Title News; Wolf Dismisses Poe Win; Green; Arson.

1 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Adrenaline went off air, RPW World Champion - Karina Wolfenden - made it perfectly clear who she wanted her first title defence to be against. 'I think just about everyone backstage heard her,' revealed one of the light technicians working nearby. 'The words 'Steven Silver' and 'Next Week' could clearly be made out, and she didn't sound cautious about saying them.' Many behind the scenes supported the claim, revealing that the K-Wolf was less than pleased with the disrespect Silver paid to RPW's 1st World Champion, and newly crowned Eclipse Champion, Gideon Poe. 'I think the gist of the conversation was 'If he wants to be a star here, throw him to me',' added another of RPW's tight backstage crew. 'It takes something special to rile her, and Silver's words certainly did that... Just ask the guy who has to fix the door she slammed on the way out.' With Silver hardly likely to turn the shot down, and Kannon already planning to manoeuvre him into World Title contention, it seems that in 5 days time, the K-Wolf will have to show the world that she's ready to hold the coveted belt. It also seems that Silver isn't the only talent backstage getting on Gideon Poe's case. After his capture of the Eclipse Championship, following in Karina's footsteps and winning double singles gold in RPW, Poe was immediately ripped into by none other than Magnum Wolf. 'Poe won the Eclipse Title? SHOCK HORROR!' the sarcastic Alpha Male sneered. 'Who'd of thought it, huh? Now both Kannon's 'Golden Children' have belts... Excuse me while I vomit.' While Karina was keeping quiet on the subjects of Wolf and Silver, she couldn't resist taking a jab at Gaz 'The Jester' Green as she exited the Halton Arena. Asked to respond to his derogatory comments about his career, the K-Wolf just dryly replied, 'it's good to see his movie career is going well. I'm especially looking forward to his bit-part in Weekend at Bernie's 3 : Bernie in Space.' Burn. Even with Gaz, Silver, and Magz all rubbing people up the wrong way, there was still room for one more to make himself unpopular backstage. After debuting under the moniker of 'the next Xavier Kannon', Brian Arson seems to have let the push go to his head. 'I thought Kannon would have learned not to hire complete pricks after PCW,' scoffed one roster member, who asked to remain nameless. 'As if the locker room wasn't bad enough with the likes of Wolf and Storm, we now have this guy strutting about like he's won something? It's good to see people still have to pay their dues around here. Not.'
