The Third Time's a Charm

16 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finally, on Adrenaline 31, RPW fans will see a match that has been three weeks in the making: Invincibleness~! versus Skylar Green and Vangellus Oxios. The latter team has been having trouble winning matches since they have formed (though to be brutally honest, both of them have lost more than they have won over the past few months even in singles competition), but the fact still remains that on Adrenaline 29, Green and Oxios were booked to wrestle Invincibleness~! in what was to be yet another 'training' match for the two singles wrestlers to prepare them for their pay-per-view meeting with Dave Gibson's representative tag team, The Marvelous Brothers. Skylar Green missed the show due to illness, and while Oxios and Invincibleness~! were in Kannon's office complaining about no longer being booked, and with the sudden arrival of El Spiffy!, the match was made for AD 29: Invincibleness~! against Vangellus Oxios and El Spiffy! That team went into the match trying to win, but Invincibleness~! triumphing after Rampage destroyed Spiffy. The next week on AD 30, Vangellus Oxios and Skylar Green faced Forsaken. Green was pinned by James Walkins in short order without either man even tagging in their partners; Oxios, believing his partner to have been extremely lazy in the past two weeks, arranged for Invincibleness~! to face El Spiffy! and Skylar Green in a tag team match. That match saw Green and Spiffy! acting more as a team than Oxios could or would with either man, and thanks to an inventive and somewhat spontaneous double-team Yakuza Kick/Flipping Piledriver by Spiffy! and Green to get the upset win. Now, with a few days remaining until Adrenaline 31, RPW is announcing that Invincibleness~! will finally square off against the team of Vangellus Oxios and Skylar Green in a tag team contest. What makes this match newsworthy is that we sent Caroline North to spy on Van and Sky during a training session at a nearby wrestling gym. If their work was any demonstration, the announced tag team match could be the sleeper match of the night. Caroline North is putting her intuition to the test in calling the match so; we shall see if her hunch is true. When contacted for commented, Mike Marvelous had this to say: 'Who in the HELL cares about Skylar Green or Vangellus Oxios? Nobody! Why are you even covering them? I thought RPW news was for important things or people? Hell, you didn't even yet ask me about how the Marvelous Brothers made Mick Pepper and Mouse look like wrestling school rejects at Adrenaline 30? Screw Karina versus Tessa, Mike and Malcolm Marvelous were the stars of the show! Magnum Wolf can go to hell, the Marvelous Brothers made fifty times more of an impact than him!' Caroline North reportedly walked off in mid-tirade, which forced Marvelous to stay on topic: 'You know what? It doesn't matter one way or the other. Mike and Malcolm Marvelous have already beaten Invincibleness`! (Make sure you anti-tilde that name, North), and in a MUCH more convincing manner than Forsaken could. After all, we defeated them in only ONE match, whereas Billy Bob and Jimbo needed two back-to-back matches to score even one pinfall on them. 'And Oxios and Green? They could improve their tag team skills tenfold; but ten times nothing is still nothing. We'll have no problem defeating them at the pay-per-view. In fact, I'm beginning to regret even agreeing to that match. Maybe I could find harder opponents, like The Legend of Pete and Half-Ton...'
