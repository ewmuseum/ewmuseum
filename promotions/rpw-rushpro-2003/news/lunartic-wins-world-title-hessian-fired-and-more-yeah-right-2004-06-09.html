Lunartic Wins World Title, Hessian Fired And More (Yeah Right!)

9 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
... Only kidding! Adrenaline 26 finally aired and the statement, ‘Better late than never’ couldn’t be more appropriate. What a show. Chris Storm continued to levitate towards the nearest recording camera all night whilst Brian Arson’s delusions of having talent… Sorry, not talent, Brian Arson’s delusions of being hailed as ‘The Next Xavier Kannon’ were apparent for the second week in a row. Oh, and the poster boy for jobbing James Edwards once again saw stars, and believe me, it wasn’t from the comfort of a NASA observatory. The man impressing the world of Geocities had this to say after his gruelling contest with Eclipse Champion Gideon Poe, “Blah, blah, abused my character, blah blah… Wait that’s redundant. I used commas!” As Edwards made good use of his microphone time the sound technician and cameraman noticed Rush Pro Wrestling’s Christian Kannon emerging from his neighbouring office. Like a pack of Magnum Wolves they pounced, beckoning for his thoughts and views with regards to the show, “I have one word. WOW. With the talent we have at our disposal we shall continue to offer the viewing world coverage of the best wrestling –Period-” The seemingly happy RPW owner went on to thank those talents by name. Mentioned were Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor, Hessian, Gideon Poe, Karina Wolfenden and Aaron Roberts, alias Magnum Wolf. Actually the latter failed to make it into this select list of names, perhaps Kannon feels violated by Wolf’s recent accusations of racism within the company. That, or the Alpha Males ‘Callisto Copying’ of the former PCW World Champions entrance theme, ‘Rock Is Dead’ by Marilyn Manson. When asked about the recent appearances of movie star Gaz “The Jester” Green, Kannon sneered, “Mr. Green is not contracted to Rush Pro Wrestling or any other promotions as far as I’m aware. If you would like to know whether I’m interested in signing him to this promotion, the answer is YES! I have a janitor vacancy left to fill after the departure of Rush Mason.” Whilst on the topic of the Emerald Menace, Kannon had lots to say, “LOOK, I already have the better member of P.W.A. on my talent roster. Seriously, why would I want his former leprechaun parasite of a partner lounging about backstage? And Gareth, should you be watching from the comfort of your trailer, I have two simple words, STAY AWAY!!!!” Like I said, Christian had lots to say on this topic, “As of next week I shall be tightening up security, so, should any unwelcome guests decide to execute their own one man invasion, they’ll be forcefully ejected quicker than Chris Storm’s bedroom activities!” With that a stern faced Christian Kannon marched off. To stir the pot RPW did try to get a retort from Gaz Green himself but the Jester was unavailable. Apparently he was on the set of his latest blockbuster comedy, ‘Ron-away Groom’ The Wayan Bros. spoof to 1999’s Julia Roberts flick, ‘Runaway Bride’.
