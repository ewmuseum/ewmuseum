Mystery Signing from Rush? Or is it RUSH?

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It seems that a week cannot go by without some sort of identity crisis in RPW. Every Adrenaline, someone’s asking questions about a new owner, financial backer, mystery partner or new arrival. It’s like a gimmick or something. This week, sadly (or fortunately, depending on how you like your sports entertainment), is no different. Whether he did it on purpose or not, Rush Mason set off a frenzy of speculation after AD20 had aired, thanks to the revelation that he’d secured one of RPW’s last singles contracts. Because of Mason’s declared intentions to offer the contract to an infamous ex-member of RUSH, RPW’s late parent organization, fans have been throwing around guesses that range from the ridiculous to the “oh shit, that kinda makes sense” logical. There are a number of names that once graced the RUSH roster who have not worked regularly since its closure – a closure that occurred, ironically, at the hands of current RPW owner, Christian Kannon. Chris Matthews has been working exclusively overseas and has scoffed at the idea of returning, even for a RUSH-related company. Brock Newbludd was last seen in Old School Fighting, and proved as successful as the organization itself. There is even talk that the “cancer” is in fact a veiled reference to another virus, Ebola III – now known by his given name, Kevin Sandusky – who hasn’t been seen since one of the last shows of Ron Starr’s New Jersey-based Pro Wrestling Circuit. Sandusky was rumored to have a RPW contract months ago. Despite the relative obscurity of RUSH’s former members, many of them have connections to Rush Mason through his period of ownership in the defunct semi-national promotion, and through his competitive days as a part of Primetime Championship Wrestling. PCW had several working arrangements with RUSH, including talent exchanges. This culminated in a full buyout at the hands of Christian Kannon, then competing under his ring name of Xavier Kannon as PCW’s owner. Many RUSH veterans vowed revenge for the purchase and never got it, leading many to wonder if this “cancer” is in fact arriving with malicious intent. Aside from Rush Mason and Christian Kannon, the only other RPW member with ties to RUSH is current Heavyweight Champion Gideon Poe. While everyone else is led to blind speculation, only those three know what the atmosphere was like in RUSH, and only they can provide informed insight as to who this “cancer” might be.
