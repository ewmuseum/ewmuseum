Dave Gibson Makes Open Challenge

20 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Late Tuesday morning, Dave Gibson, the leader of the Old School Elite, walked into Christian Kannon's office building, intent to speak with the head of Rush Pro Wrestling. He said that the Old School Elite had already participated in Lumberjack and Gauntler matches, but that they had not had the opportunity yet to show their solidarity and dominance in a team-oriented match. Gibson noted that none of the Old School Elite were booked for Adrenaline 32, and made this suggestion: the Old School Elite face another team in a five-on-five tag team match. Kannon seemed interested in the idea, but didn't know who would be right for the match. Many of the money-makers that he'd want in that type of match were already booked, though we won't mention names at this point. Gibson acknowledged that, and proposed that any of RPW's wrestlers be allowed to volunteer for the match if they wanted to wrestle. If there were no people willing to sign up, Kannon would just assign some random team to face the Elite, notifying wrestlers as they came to the arena. Christian Kannon accepted the proposal, and what we now have is a confirmed 10-Man tag team contest with only one side having any representatives so far. A notice was e-mailed to all RPW wrestlers, and anyone who wishes to enter the match may do so. We'll update you on all entrants, and any statements from and reactions to them.
