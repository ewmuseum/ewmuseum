Sit Down Interview with Chris Storm

6 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently Chris Storm was able to sit down with RPW and talk about his life, career, and RPW. On Life Storm: Life huh? You want me to share my thoughts about life? Well pain is life. No not some stuff gothic description about how my life is so painful. No I mean the pain I inflict on others. That is what it is all about. Just how much I can hurt someone else. I don't feed off it, I don't hungry for it. I just enjoy it. I think it is amusing to watch someone suffer great pain. It doesn't matter if it is physical or mental pain. You still know that look when someone is in pain. Who's life am I currently inflicting with pain? Sure some would say Tessa Windsor and Jackson Rust. But I have not even started with either of them yet. Oh no I am just using them as a tool to cause pain on someone else. Joe Morris. The supposed super hero of RPW. He hates mistreatment of woman. He hates sexual messages, he hates viguar language. He hates people like me. On Joe Morris Storm: So this guy hates everything I made my career out of. I mean I'm sitting backstage and I see this guy insulting everything I am. I'm not going to sit back and watch am I? So what do I do? I go and cost him his little match he had that night. No big deal right? Then when I'm with my toy Alyssa Sanders he decides he wants to save her from a vile man like myself. So now he's insulted my life and messed with my girls. You don't do either. So that is when I decide I'm going to go and do everything he hates and watch him as he can do nothing to stop me. Which he hasn't yet. And at the PPV I'll be sure to put Joe Morris' RPW career to an end. On Tessa Windsor & Jackson Rust Storm: Now I know people have been thinking I've been at Tessa and Jacko's throats since they entered because I have bad blood with them. But they need to understand that I have not even begun to extract my revenge from them. Oh no what I have done to them right now has just been so I can get under Joe Morris' skin. Just wait until I start getting serious with them two. They will both wish neither of them had ever entered RPW. In fact I believe I will start at Genesis. Have their end on RPW's beginning. On his Career Storm: My career? What can I say about the Perfect Storm's career except for Perfect? What other wrestler has landed himself in not one but two Hall of Fames for leagues that have been ranked #1 in PTC. I am a Hall of Famer for WIW and DWO. Hell I held every single title in WIW. I'm their second every Grand Slam champion. But my greatness isn't limited to just PTC. Even in PCW I held the record for longest Tag Team title reign beating their supposed "Greatest Tag Team" P.W.A. who had held that record. And do you know how hard it was to pull off that title reign when you have to carry a female partner in every match? On Recent Times Storm: Now I will admit as of late my career hasn't been what I wanted it to be. I couldn't seem to find my niche in PCW and I tried DWO but they could make more promises then they could challengers for me. I went to CDC trying to land a place in OSE but I soon realized that was not the type of place I needed to be. I needed to have more pride in myself then be in some school for the retarded. So when RPW offered me a spot yeah I took a cut from that million I was making in DWO or WIW. I just make enough to live a comfortable life. But if it means one last chance to climb my way to the top then I will pay any price. Even my own blood. On RPW Storm: I look around and remember when we had a much smaller roster. We have what, 32 people on he roster now? We had only 20 people even show up our last show. I remember hearing Rush saying we was lucky at least 20 showed up this time. Hell Magnum Wolf appears on our shows more than most the roster and he isn't even employeed by us! Magnum and Maxwell are the only two people really even doing anything for this show. Those two put the people in the seats. No one cares about that Hot Topic Poe or Argentine Tota. Most of our fans don't even know where Argentine is! But I will give RPW some credit. They let me enjoy all the air time I want and let me tell you when my face is on the camera the people react. They want me and RPW is the only league in PTC that can give that to them. So apparently they are doing something right. On Peter Maxwell Storm: Thus far Peter Maxwell has been the man here. Every time I have needed a match signed I go to him. Why? Because I know Rush Mason won't get it done. He's nothing more than a paper owner. Peter is the man that really gets stuff done. I mean what type of owner really is Rush? He sold RPW to some 16 year old kid! He wants us to fail as a promotion. Thankfully Peter is doing his best to save us. Though if he would have been smart he would have went ahead and told El Diablo to take a walk and gave me that World title. But hey I never said Peter was a genuis. On Magnum Wolf Storm: The guy is golden. He may not be a part of RPW but everytime I hear he's on the screen I stop what I am doing to watch. I can't wait for Genesis when Magnum ho slaps Karina and gets his contract. Then maybe I can have someone backstage I could actually have an decent conversation with. After all I won't dare try to talk to Poe. I don't think anyone could have much to say when they are reducing to having to talk to their pet eagle.
