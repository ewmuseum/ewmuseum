Jane Says...

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...Book me a ticket to Charlotte. With speculation growing as to who will be revealed as RPW's financial backer, rumours are swelling about numerous wrestling promoters being seen in the Charlotte area. People are already claiming Ron Starr helped Mason fund RPW, and have been posting the news on wrestling sites all over the web. Now, Jane Reagan has been added into the mix, after several RPW fans claim to have seen the former OSW owner spirited away into a limousine last night. "We were just on our way home," the young man claimed, while is girlfriend confirmed the events, "when this redhead with shining green eyes came out of the restaurant over there. I watched OSE... Well, OSW, since OSF sucked an' all, so I recognised her right away. That was Jane, man, she might as well have just given the thumb's up to confirm it. Reagan's here in Charlotte." With the increasing numbers of OSE talent in RPW, it wouldn't be too far fetched to think that Jane had some sway in RPW, but with members of Ron Starr's defunct PWC promotion also being on the roster, there seems to be no clear pick as to who backed the promotion. Unless the husband/wife team are finally back together?
