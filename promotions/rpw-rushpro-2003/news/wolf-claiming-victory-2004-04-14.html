Wolf Claiming Victory

14 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"See that, LOOOSERS!?!?! I won!" Those were the jubilant words of Magnum Wolf as he left the Dale F. Halton Arena on Sunday night. After a campaign lasting months, Magnum finally saw Karina told to leave the building by new figurehead Christian Alexander Kannon. "I knew that not even Skankarina could be so shameless!" he later commented to anyone who would listen. "If you ask me, she deliberately tried to get herself dismissed to avoid the humiliation of losing to me. But, then again, what do I know? Oh, that's right, EVERYTHING!" But while Wolf was claiming victory, after over a year of trying to best the K-Wolf, Kannon wasn't buying into his boasts. "Karina Wolfenden was put on 'voluntary' leave," he confirmed, although it's debatable as to how much she even consented, let alone volunteered. "She will be back when she matches my expectations. And when she is, it's open season on Wolves." Although RPW's financial backer was said to be infuriated by the actions of Wolf and Powell, who's actions threatened to disrupt Adrenaline, he remained tight-lipped on the matter. "Powell was signed before I stepped into my role, and as for whether I would have signed him myself? No comment. As for Magnum, his vast talent will be rewarded accordingly, just as his attitude and actions will be punished." When pushed further on how he planned to settle the dispute between Karina and Magnum, Kannon refused to give any solid plan. "They'll have the chance to settle their score for good, I'll see to that."
