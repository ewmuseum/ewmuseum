Adrenaline and Genesis Postponed

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Due to scheduling conflicts, Adrenaline this week has been cancelled. Sources say that most of the RPW talent had prior commitments that must be honored. In a sign of belevolence, new RPW head "Little Al" gave the entire roster the week off, with pay. "I figured these guys wanted to take some time, go home, and spend time with their families. I've gotta go see my mom, and have to study for finals." Little Al said. "Don't worry. It's not a sign of impending danger or anything. I'm not used to working weekends. That's my arcade time."
