Poe still missing after AD

28 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A post of the federation’s news desk as the Wayback Machine captured the front page its site then kept at www.ewnweb.com on 2 August 2004 (a fourth address for the RPW site, which the wing already holds at three): the post’s title, date and text. The poster’s line is not carried, as for the wing’s other news posts.
The events that transpired shortly towards the end of the latest ADrenaline involving Steven Silver and Gideon Poe have raised an immediate set of anxious alarms in the RPW camp. Reports after seeing the footage which aired at ADrenaline confirmed the fact that Steven Silver and Gideon Poe did leave the arena without notifying anyone, and, as confirmed from the footage, the two were believed to leave to settle their differences in a physical manner before their No DQ match at Eve Of Destruction. The exact whereabouts of where the two men left to settle their differences was still uncertain after Adrenaline went off the air. However, the one thing that was confirmed to be certain was the fact that Steven Silver was the only one to return to the Halton Arena. Several reports from RPW roster members and production crew workers confirmed that Steven Silver returned to the arena shortly after Adrenaline went off the air. “The guy was cut up bad alright” one production crew members stated. “Whatever those two got up too, it wasn’t a technical wrestling escapade, put it that way. The guy had cuts and bruises swelling all over his face, but the fact that he was grinning like a Cheshire Cat throughout it all only confirmed to us that he’d got the upper hand over Gideon. Especially when he showed us the T-Shirt” When asked exactly about the T-shirt, the production crew members confirmed to us what several other workers and roster members had already confirmed. “He had Gideon’s T-Shirt, the one he had worn that night, some old Pink Floyd shirt. I’ll tell you one thing, Gideon couldn’t have taken that thing off to go skinny dipping, that shirt was torn and ripped to hell. Steven must have had to rip it straight off Gideon’s body. The thing was covered in blood, mud stains and a bunch of other shit. Some of the lighting crew who were packing up their equipment said he hung it outside his locker room while he was collecting his stuff, like symbolic or something. Well, that was the last straw for most people, they started ringing round the local hospitals like they were ringing up to confirm their winning numbers for this weeks lottery.” It turns out that several RPW Staff members rang the local hospitals in North Carolina well into the early hours of the morning. Christian Kannon himself was reported to have rung round several of the local hospitals, and even ones ranging from outside North Carolina, due to the exact whereabouts of where the two went still being unknown. It was later reported that no patient under the name of Gideon Poe had been checked in that night. It’s reported that RPW’s staff are taking this matter very seriously, and have failed in several attempts to contact Steven Silver since Adrenaline went off the air. RPW is demanding hat Silver get in contact with the immediately. No information has been given yet whether or not their match at Eve Of Destruction will be cancelled.
