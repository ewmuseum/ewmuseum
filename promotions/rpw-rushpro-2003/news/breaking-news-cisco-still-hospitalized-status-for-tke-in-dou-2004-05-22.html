BREAKING NEWS: Cisco Still Hospitalized; Status for TKE In Doubt!!

22 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Adrenaline, Cisco Milloy suffered a severe allergic reaction during his demonstration of the Six Finger Crisco ULTRA product for a group from Wal-Mart. Days later, on the eve of The Kannon Effect, Milloy is still in the hospital recovering. "Mr. Milloy was admitted to Presbyterian Hospital Sunday night after suffering a severe case of anaphylactic shock," said an RPW insider speaking on condition of anonymity. Sources at the hospital said that Milloy experienced the allergic reaction after consuming peanuts--a substance he is highly allergic to. Hospital sources have called it one of the worst cases of anaphylactic shock they've seen. "We've seen people with serious allergies, but Mr. Milloy's reaction was more serious--and quicker--than most," one hospital source said. Cisco experienced severe swelling in his face and extremities. As the swelling worsened, his throat began to close up and he went into shock. Cisco actually stopped breathing briefly during the hospital ride and had to be resuscitated. He's been kept hospitalized all week undergoing a battery of tests to insure no permanent damage has been done to his system. The medical mishap has Cisco's status in doubt for The Kannon Effect, scheduled to air live on pay-per-view this Sunday. Cisco is scheduled to compete against Steven Silver and Shawn Stewart in a three-way dance, as well as participate in the six-way main event for the RPW World Championship. "It's up to the doctors, I guess," the RPW insider said of Cisco's availability. "Right now we're working on the assumption he'll be there... but if he's not here by the time talent is scheduled to report--two hours before showtime--we'll proceed accordingly."
