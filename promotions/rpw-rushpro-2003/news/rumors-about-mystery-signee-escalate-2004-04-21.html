Rumors About Mystery Signee Escalate

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After AD21, speculation into Rush Mason’s mystery signee has heated up even further. With the clues dropped during a segment with Mason and his partners-in-crime, Jason O’Neil and Peter Maxwell, rumors as to this newcomer’s identity have started to take on more substantial shape. The most notable detail was the allusion to time spent in PCW, Mason’s old stomping grounds. There has been a sufficient amount of PCW/RUSH crossover back when both coexisted, so there are still a plethora of names out there. Since mention of PCW, names that have been added to the fray include Hiro Saotome, who fits Peter Maxwell’s description of sucking in both PCW and RUSH, and Dante Jackson, whom has long been known as a friend of Rush Mason out of the ring. There have also been some interesting names coming up. When talking about people who nearly killed RUSH, one name is always mentioned – Chris Sloboda. However, “Boda” is currently under contact with the new PCW, which does not feature the same working agreement with RPW as the old version did with RUSH. A suggestion that has been gaining a great deal of steam lately has been that of a former part-owner of RUSH during its early days who has recent dropped from active competition, but may be looking for a return. Once involved in the company’s top angle before his departure for a competitive spot in PCW, the well-known midcarder left for fWo’s Survivor competition and never looked back. However, with his personal life and lack of commitment ruining that chance, an open contract might be his last shot in the game. Will we see Tim Walden in RPW? The only way to find out is to tune in next week.
