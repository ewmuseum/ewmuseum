The Kannon Effect.

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three days ago, Christian Kannon was unveiled as the new figurehead of RPW. As it stands, no one is exactly sure what Kannon's capacity will be in RPW. The one certainty, however, is that the emergence of the former head of PCW, as well as the man who is without a doubt one of the more famous men in wrestling history has attracted new fans to RPW. For the first time, since RPW opened it's doors last November, The Halton Arena has sold out in just two days after realeasing tickets. There was also an increase in media credentials asked for at the show. Could RPW finally be making it's way to the big time? "Aye, he was bloody great in PCW, behind the desk and in the ring. I have to admit, it's been a tough secret to keep." says Matt Jay, the official RPW spokesman. "But this one's official - the name on the bottom of every single pay slip, contract and most action figures is Christian Kannon. No more XK, although for those with difficulty adjusting, Mr. Kannon is an easy way around it." There has also been an increase in the companies carrying Adrenaline, RPW's weekly television show. At the start of buisness on Monday, RPW had already recieved guarantees from Time Warner Cable to branch out into Florida, Alabama, Tennessee and Kentucky, as well as more cities being added through North and South Carolina. RPW can currently be seen in 14 markets through the Carolinas and Georgia. By the time Adrenaline hits the air, it is expected that the show will have the potential to reach nearly one million people. "The man has a magic touch. Look at what he did for PCW. Look at how the ratings for OSE shot up. And now he's here, in RPW as the man with the plan. F'ing marvellous! It's also great to be able to bring RPW into the homes of more fans. Previously, you had to get a bloody ticket and see it in person, but now you don't even have to leave your house." The direct result is that Christian Kannon can be directly linked to this explosion of interest in RPW, and he's not even spoken a word yet. More on this as it comes available.
