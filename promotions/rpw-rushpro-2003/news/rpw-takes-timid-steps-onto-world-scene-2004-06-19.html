RPW Takes Timid Steps Onto World Scene

19 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With talks between RUSH Pro Wrestling and the UK's 'The Wrestling Channel' still ongoing, Christian Alexander Kannon has decided to pursue other foreign ventures in an attempt to improve RPW's scant finances. 'The US scene has proven very hard to break into,' confessed RPW's spokesman 'Angry' Matt Jay. 'Even with name-power behind you, networks and major sponsors will always be reluctant to give full backing to a new promotion, especially one who's financial security is constantly in question.' Although RPW Adrenalines are expected to start airing on UK satellite TV in late July, once the final details have been hammered out, it's rumoured that The Wrestling Channel wants to show RPW's product from the beginning, meaning British audiences will be over 6 months behind... Not really helping the talent investments made in the last few months pay off. 'Even if we can secure 2 slots a week until they've caught up,' Jay continued, 'it's going to be well over 3 months until the channel starts showing up-to-date Adrenalines, which we feel are the best product we've put out so far.' So, not content with just the United Kingdom, Christian Alexander Kannon has used his connections to help spread RPW's name further a field. 'We're still probably years away from the kind of tours that some promotions are able to put on,' RPW's figurehead commented when asked of his global plans for RPW, 'but it won't hurt to have as many people around the world know about RPW as possible.' Through business relations made when he worked with Japan's failed PURE promotion around the time of PCW's last Red October, Kannon has started work on finding RPW a 'twin' federation in the land of the rising sun. 'It's an arrangement that can be beneficial in the long run,' Kannon explained. 'The days of mergers are dead, but the current state of the market is wide-open to cooperation. Just the mere positive acknowledgement of RPW by another promotion overseas is better publicity than we can ever pay for.' Despite RPW's continued weekly loss, many are expecting Kannon's bold international ventures, along with the recent appearance to tSC talent on Adrenaline, to help turn the finances around. 'Currently RPW is in talks with a promotion working out of Tokyo called AEON, and will be sending 2 of our roster over to their Monday show,' Kannon revealed, 'while we will be accepting one of their rising stars - Ryuuji Asakawa - for an appearance on this coming Sunday's Adrenaline.' The two wrestlers sent in return? 'I'm very serious about this venture, so are sending the pair of Karina Wolfenden and Peter Maxwell,' Kannon confirmed. 'Our World Champion, and one of those who helped keep RPW afloat in it's troubled early days. Although Karina has picked up a few heavy knocks recently, she'll be guesting in a 10-person Tag Team contest on Monday, while Maxwell will handle the publicity side.' Although the K-Wolf has previously shunned all attempts by RPW to use her as 'advertising', her recent change in attitude has seemingly softened her resolve. 'Karina knows that as RPW's Champion she has responsibilities,' Kannon continued, 'and she's realised that in the past 2 weeks... To the point where she paid for the return flights involved with her own credit card.' So RPW Adrenaline will be without the K-Wolf for the first time in it's history, something which Magnum Wolf will surely exploit. Finally, Kannon announced the opponent he'd selected for Asakawa. 'This Sunday, Adrenaline will feature Ryuuji Asakawa versus Brian Arson,' Kannon revealed. 'Should relations go well between RPW and AEON, then Arson is one of RPW's next generation who will step into the larger spotlight.'
