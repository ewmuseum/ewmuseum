Kannon Scraps AEON Deal

26 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a frank, and rather agitated interview after last week's Adrenaline went off the air, RPW owner Christian Alexander Kannon made it known to all that negotiations with fledgling Japanese promotion AEON were being terminated immediately. After the young talent they sent - Ryuuji Asakawa - failed to show for RPW's weekly PPV, Kannon let it be known just how he felt. 'Un-fucking-professional,' he seethed, looking stressed at the end of a long night. 'I already had some of my talent unavailable for the show, and then I send Karina and Maxwell over to their show, only to get fuck all back in return. Ryuuji Asakawa against Arson would have been a great match, but seeing how AEON are not returning my calls, I'd bet they ever even booked him on the flight.' Clearly not happy with the Adrenaline that RPW turned out, managing to fill the show with what talent he had at hand, Kannon looked relieved that his tired roster had a week off. 'A 14 day break should have them all raring to go next Sunday,' Kannon revealed. 'All the knocks should have been shaken off, and all the cuts healed. Talent have been asked to cut back on their Indy bookings, so what you'll see is RPW at it's best.' During the show, the man Ryuuji Asakawa was slated to face - Brian Arson - looked less than happy at having to sit out yet another show match-wise. 'Brian was understandably angered,' Kannon admitted, despite some pressing him hard on the youngster's backstage attitude. 'He was stuck as a lackey to guys like Kano and Kris Kanton, and he saw RPW as liberation from that kind of card-placement... And it will be. He's slowly building as a performer, but I'm not going to put too much pressure on him too early.' In contrast to Kannon's take on the matter, Arson's parting words were a little more blunt. 'Another night when I might as well have watched at home,' the man slated to follow in Kannon's footsteps scoffed. 'We had main eventers sitting around, Champions who could have defended, and I'm stuck waiting on some Funaki-level curtain jerker? Yeah, I had a GREAT night...'
