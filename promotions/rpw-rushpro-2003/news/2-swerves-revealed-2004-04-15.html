2 "SWERVES~!" Revealed

15 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the appearance by the trio referred to as THE PHANTOM SWERVE~! at Adrenaline, many Independent Wrestling journalists were quick to name 2 of the 'masked' men. #1 was identified as Lee Wilkinson, a little-known brawler on the United Kingdom's Indy circuit, while #2 was played by William - or "Willie" - Smythe, a veteran of the world scene, who never managed to live up to the likes of Steven Regal or Johnny Smith. With RPW soon to receive air-time on the UK's Wrestling Channel, Christian Alexander Kannon had been rumoured to be eyeing up some British talent for the fans to relate to, having seen how Davey-Boy Smith and Tom Billington helped create a solid fan base in the 80's. Adrenaline has been called their "try-out" show, and barring any Teddy Hart egos and incidents, both are expected to debut under their own names soon, possibly as a mis-matched tag team.
