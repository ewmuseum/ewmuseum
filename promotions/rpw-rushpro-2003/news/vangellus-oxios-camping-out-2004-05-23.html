Vangellus Oxios Camping Out

23 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's nearly 2 AM here when I write this, and Vangellus Oxios is already at the Halton Arena. Though he's unable to stay inside the building itself, he's camped out around the back where the wrestlers normally enter the building. One would normally expect this behavior of a ticker-purchaser, but instead Oxios is waiting here because he is eager to get his hands on Hessian. The PTC Global Champion attacked his manager at Adrenaline, causing the normally scatter-brained and airy Oxios to become upset enough to challenge Hessian to make their pay-per-view match a hardcore rules outing when it was not booked as such to begin with. Oxios's manager is Adrienne Diamond, only seen sparingly on RPW television but playing a far greater role in his career; and though it is corny, she is also his friend, and Oxios doesn't take well to his friends being assaulted, the same as anyone. Diamond was attacked last week on Adrenaline 24, suffering numerous injuries that will keep her out of the Halton Arena and away from her duties for weeks, if not months. Hessian has not been contacted for comment.
