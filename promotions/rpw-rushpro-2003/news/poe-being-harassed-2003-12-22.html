Poe being harassed?

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Several reports from various reliable back stage sources have surfaced as of late, all dictating that the returning fan favourite Gideon Poe, may be going from one problem right into another. This time, the problem seems to suggest that Poe is being harassed by more than one anonymous person…. Via his cell phone. As Adrenaline 7 went off the air, several backstage crew members and roster members spotted The Shade leaving the arena shouting various obscenities and laughing sarcastically into his phone. One ring crew member was reported in saying: “Yeah, he was leaving through the car park when I saw it happening. At first I thought he was talking to himself, but then I seen he was speaking into his cell phone. I remember him saying something along the lines of “Why don’t you bastards actually come say these things to my face”. He stopped right by the ticket booth then, and looked taken back a bit, his face went a little pale, I remember that much, and then he just laughed and said “Next week at the Halton arena is absolutely fine by me.” Other reports have suggested that they witnessed Gideon receiving these disturbing phone calls throughout the day before his match at Adrenaline, and some claim he was forced to take the sim-card out of his phone before his brutal tag team match. If this is all true, who could these anonymous phone calls be from, and if the sources which claim to be true suggest that there is actually more than one person behind these calls, who could the people involved be. It seems all of these answers will be revealed next week at Adrenaline 8, live from The Halton Arena.
