Tessa Windsor... New RPW Champion?

10 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a week after losing the Mid-South Title in a fiercely contested bout with Shawn Stewart, Tessa Windsor could very well win an even bigger prize at Adrenaline 30... Without lifting a finger. After seeing Tessa put on the best performance of her decorated career at The Kannon Effect, defeating Chris Storm to the shock of many, Kannon made the decision to grant Windsor a World Title shot the week after she dropped the strap. 'To say the boss was impressed would be an understatement,' revealed RPW spokesperson Angry Matt Jay. 'He drafted up a contact to give her a World Title shot to reward that performance, effective as of the Adrenaline after she lost the belt, excluding 'Super Adrenalines'. So, this Sunday it's the K-Wolf against Tessa for the Gold... Hopefully...' The only problem with that match seems to be Karina Wolfenden's physical condition. After matches with Shawn Stewart, Steven Silver, and Brandon Youngblood left Karina in need of a week off to recuperate, last week's borderline savage encounter with Violence Jack left the Champion unable to leave ringside unaided. Should she be deemed unfit to compete on Adrenaline, since Tessa is contracted to the match, the K-Wolf would have to forfeit the belt over to Windsor. 'It's not something that we'd be happy to do,' Jay confessed, 'but it's what we're obliged to do. At The Kannon Effect, Gabe was unable to compete, thus forfeited the belt, and his spot in the Main Event. Since this weekend's match is a signed and sealed singles match between Tessa and Karina, our only option would be to crown Tessa as the new Champion if Karina can't defend.' Since winning the belt, Karina has defended it with all her heart, leading many to worry about her physical condition. 'She's stubborn, that's the politest way of putting it,' Jay continued. 'And I would NOT want to be the Doctor who deems her unable to compete, as he'll likely join her on the injured list. But she's as tough as she is stubborn, so I wouldn't count her out just yet.' So Tessa Windsor is one failed medical away from being RPW World Champion... But should Kari not be able to defend, would Tessa want to take the belt in such a fashion? Could Jack stake a claim to the belt after leaving the Champion unable to compete? And what says Magnum Wolf about the situation, since he's the #1 Contender scheduled to face the Champion at Eve of Destruction? Adrenaline will either have a High Flying World Title Match, or the answers to the above questions.
