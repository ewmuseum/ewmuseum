The Fury of the Manbeast Unleashed

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This past Adrenaline saw Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan dismantle beloved fan favorite Gabe, culminating in Gabe being knocked unconscious by a dual Violence Jack-Ethan Knight attack and the subsequent R'lyeh Anthem that sealed the win for the Bringer of the Black Gospel. After the match-up, many noted the angry glare Scott 'Rampage' Matthews had locked on VJ as he made his exit and left the 19 year old broken on the mat. Considering that an aura of constant rage is the hallmark of the Manbeast, it was obvious Rampage felt extraordinary contempt toward VJ for the beating he delivered on Invincible Gabe. Reports state that as Rampage carried his outspoken yet revered partner to the back, many noticed a blank look in the eye of of the big man. After the show, Matthews was overheard in the office of the administration, bellowing his fury and demanding a match with the Bringer of the Black Gospel. The behemoth apparently made his point to the cowering company brass, and now a contest that many fans have been aching to see has been made. This coming Sunday will see Scott 'Rampage' Matthews take on Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan in a brutal battle for pride and retribution that many pray will be the one to bring VJ's top-tier ambitions to a screeching halt. Outrage swelled to a boiling point this past Sunday night following the Gabe/V-Jack match and security chief Pete Blackbeard was overheard saying he feared a riot was on the verge of breaking out. There's no question Violence Jack was a loathed figure in Rush Pro Wrestling due to his outrageous beliefs and vile acts in the promotion's past, but decimating and attempting to cripple the man many consider RPW's most popular only made his own popularity plunge further. Matthews, inarguably the most purely savage and destructive force that V-Jack has battled with yet in RPW, is the great hope of many disgusted fans who see this as the best chance to silence the Baron of Brutality and his diabolic plans for Rush Pro Wrestling. Shanahan, on the other hand, is said to view this as his road back to the top. Defeating one of RPW's most physically daunting personalities would certainly be a considerable statement for his cause. "Bullshit, just plain bullshit," stated one fan leaving the Halton Arena when asked about the Gabe - Violence Jack encounter. "Kannon coming back as the backer was historic and Cisco coming in to confront Gibson was just awesome, but that shit with Jack just had me pissed off the rest of the night! The kid's alright I heard, thankfully. I swear to God, if Violence Jack ever wins the title from Gideon Poe, I'll keep coming back here just to see him lose it and laugh in the fucker's face! Especially if Gabe were the one to take it!" While not being billed as the main event, higher-ups in the company power structure are said to be excited at the possibilities of the match and are already hyping the contest as huge. One side sees a massive, vicious monster fighting on behalf of the company's favorite underdog and having all of RPW's fan following in his corner, himself just coming off a huge win over former Eclipse champion Karina Wolfenden. His opponent is a ruthless top contender and sadistic death-match veteran who has again proven his prowess at drawing the ire of wrestling enthusiasts everywhere. This one is set to explode on the April 11th edition of Adrenaline. Rumors are stating that it has been agreed to ban both Gabe and Violence Jack's cult forces from ringside, and that the rules will be relaxed to allow the two to pummel each other to their hearts' content. More on the match stipulations in the coming days.
