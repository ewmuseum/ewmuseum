Farwell In Trouble?

29 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A post of the federation’s news desk as the Wayback Machine captured the front page its site then kept at www.ewnweb.com on 2 August 2004 (a fourth address for the RPW site, which the wing already holds at three): the post’s title, date and text. The poster’s line is not carried, as for the wing’s other news posts.
A doctor’s report sent to RPW headquarters today revealed that James Farwell has been suffering from internal injuries, which may have been present for over a month. While the extent of the injuries are unknown to anyone outside head RPW officials at this time, it could be safe to say that Farwell’s performances haven’t been suffering due to age, as many wrestling fans have noted in the past month, but rather these injuries that coincide with the timeframe in which his performance began to diminish. James Farwell has been advised to take a couple weeks off in order to heal, thus The Forsaken have been pulled from active duty for the Eve of Destruction Pay-Per-View event. Many have been questioning this move, as it leaves the RPW Tag Titles off the show, and with a division that has been suffering it could prove to be the proverbial final nail in the coffin. Christian Kannon supports his decision, however, citing that he’d “rather have a healthy wrestler that can give 100% over an unhealthy wrestler that only gives 40%. If it means giving a week or two off here and there, it’s a small price to pay.” While Farwell may be missing action for a week or two, Clyde Walkins could be on a permanent vacation from wrestling. Earlier in the day, reports arrived stating Clyde Walkins had quit RPW, leaving his tag partner James Farwell in a further mess. “Farwell’s been left high and dry,” one anonymous source stated. “Not only has he had health problems that have had a direct impact on his wrestling ability, but being one-half of RPW’s Tag Team Champions and having Clyde quit has put him and the rest of RPW in a very unlikely and awkward position.”
