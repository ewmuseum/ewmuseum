Hessian vs. Chris Storm PPV Match Named!

21 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As seen on RPW Adrenaline 31, Chris Storm was challenged to a match at the upcoming supercard, Eve Of Destruction. The stipulations and even name of the match were kept under wraps however...that is until now. Details straight from the desk of Christian Kannon reveal that Hessian and Chris Storm will face off at Eve Of Destruction... ...In a Technocore Match. The stipulations for this match are as follows: --- 1. Chris Storm must win by either knocking Hessian out, or by the Referee stopping the match outside the ring. 2. Hessian must win by either submission or pinfall on Chris Storm, or by Storm being unable to continue the match, due to punishment given in the ring by Hessian. 3. Neither wrestler, under any circumstance may go into their environment (Storm being in ring, Hessian, outside) to simply weaken an opponent, then move the opponent to the outside to attempt to pick up a cheap win. No pinfall/submission/KO will be called. 4. The ring will be specially fit and created to allow optimal movement into and out of the ring. A normal ring stands 40'' off the ground. The ring for this match will sit a mere 24'' off the ground to allow easy entrance into and out of the ring. 5. Once the upper hand is achieved by each member there will be a 45 second count administered by a specially appointed ringside official. If the attacker does not take the victim into the appropriate environment in that time, then the attacker will lose the match. Special Enforcers will be in attendance. --- As the 24'' off the ground measurement is required, a second ring will be placed beside the entrance to accomodate both Storm and Hessian. With the PTC Global Title Match at the Survival I-PPV fast approaching, fans will be glued to the action to see two former Global Champions go at it in a match you can catch only on RPW: Eve Of Destruction!
