Kannon Losing Patience

20 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While the RPW fans cheered for the near-instantaneous return of Karina Wolfenden to Adrenaline, one man wasn’t - the one man who matters - Christian Kannon. After enforcing leave upon her last Sunday, Kannon told her only to return when she could put in the performances that made her famous on the Indy scene. Kannon, for one, does not believe that she’s back in the right frame of mind. “I try to be more personable than most bosses,” Kannon confessed, “but that doesn’t mean I’m immune to losing my patience… And the Wolf and Karina situation is really testing it right now.” Throughout their RPW tenure, the rivals have gone back and forth, both physically and emotionally. Karina tried to bar Wolf from joining, due to his abysmal backstage reputation. Magnum nearly drove Karina crazy by conning the fans into believing he was their brave hero. Kari walked away while Violence Jack had his followers beat Wolf into a pulp. And then, to top it off, Wolf went lower than anyone could imagine, and dragged up Karina’s private past for all to hear… Only for Kari to walk right back out of suspension and cost Magnum the RPW World Title. However, it looks like things will come to an end soon. “Both need to get over their whole thing, and move on with their careers,” Kannon decreed. “How long are they going to keep it up? Neither will back down, and neither will let the other get the final word. So, something has to be done.” Although nothing specific has been announced, it appears Kannon wants to do the right thing for both RPW and it’s 2 stars… Both of whom he’s reported to admire. Even Wolf, despite the backstage problems. But how well Wolf will respond to Kannon’s words after the controversial end to Adrenaline, or if Karina will even be allowed through the doors this Sunday are still to be seen. So… Well, tune it. And stuff.
