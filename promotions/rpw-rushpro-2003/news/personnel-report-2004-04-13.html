Personnel Report.

13 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fast to make an impact on the roster side of the equation, Christian Kannon has decided to release some of the inactive talent in RPW. "It's tough to have these people here in hopes that they're going to perform. RPW's done with an open format, so if they want to wrestle, they're more than welcome to. These people have unfortunately not taken advantage of that platform, and are now currently free agents." The list is rather spectacular, and include members of RPW that have long been forgotten, or haven't even shown up on Adrenaline in quite a long time. Dismissed from their RPW appearance deals are the following: Surge. Katriana Dimoni. Erika Cruz.* Team Tradition.* Ilan Levin. Tristan Belmont. Dorn Tracer.* Totakoxe Salvador. Murdoch. Cyclonica and Akiyama.* The Legend of Pete.* Half-Ton. Simon Iscariot. Jacob Yoder. Jason Marah.* Malphas. Chryste. In addition to this list, Alyssa Sanders has moved to a non-wrestling role within RPW, and has taken a job as the head of the RPW promotions department. Also, Cyclonica and Akiyama are currently touring Japan, after a freakish injury suffered by Akiyama. It is rumoured that they may return eventually, but have been given time off. Jason Marah has also moved into the Television Production department, and will be teaching three classes at UNCC in Television Production. "It's a good opportunity for me. I'm not really ring at the moment, but I'm more than happy to help behind the scenes." Marah stated recently. The Legend of Pete has also been moved to his former role in PCW and GCW of Company Seamstress. Team Tradition, Erika Cruz, and Dorn Tracer were left over, however have the option of returning at a later date. Cruz has since moved on to another promotion, but has not ruled out a return to RPW. Chryste and Malphas were also on a one year contract, however, the contract was a mistake as both were only supposed to be in RPW for two months. The situation was immediately rectified by the RPW offices and the LOW offices, and the two are still in excellent standing with RPW officials, who have nothing but good things to say about the PTC Wrestler of the Year and her beau. "We wish her luck in LOW. It was an excellent opportunity for her, and she's always had a great mind for things behind the scenes. She'll do well there." Kannon however, did not sound reassuring when he said that there would potentially be more roster moves coming. "If someone proves to be worthless in the next few months, then I will not hesitate to remove them. I can't afford to pay people that aren't going to perform to my expectations."
