RPW's Financial Strain Bigger than Thought.

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Breaking news from the RPW Offices is that after Adrenaline, Christian Kannon held a meeting with all active RPW superstars. There, quite a few wrestlers left with a pink slip, among them: Chris Crawford Matt Jackson Quinton Dufresne 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst The Legend of Pete Halfton Colby Korver and Marcus Olson, a tag team yet to make their debut. Kannon was vocal in his thoughts about the future of RPW with the internet staff as well. 'If people aren't going to have a hand in trying to make RPW money, then they will not be under contract to RPW anymore. We've already let go one round of wrestlers, and we're ready to release another 10 at a moments notice.' Dave Gibson was said to be less than thrilled with the fact that Dave Hurst was released. 'Not that I like the guy, but why hire him, and then fire him a few weeks later? It makes no freaking sense.' Kannon also announced that Jason O'Neil had taken a position in the front office of RPW as the Director of Media and Talent Relations.
