Challenges Issued and Accepted for Adrenaline 25 and Beyond

9 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, you probably missed it, but the team of The Marvelous Brothers defeated Vangellus Oxios and Skylar Green at Adrenaline 26. To anyone able to view the match, it was obvious that the latter team's lack of chemistry led to their downfall. Green was decent enough, but Oxios attempted to take the whole match himself, which resulted in his being pinned with the Up In Smoke by M2 while Green and Mike Marvelous battled on the outside. After the match, Skylar Green attempted to leave the ring. Oxios stopped him by taking the microphone and challenging The Marvelous Brothers to a rematch. But not just any rematch, a rematch at the pay-per-view, which is six weeks away! The Marvelous Brothers accepted, making it the only confirmed match on the card so far. As usual, Green must go along with it because due to his habitual 'going with the flow' for matches suggested, the management signs him to anything that comes up. That was not the end of it, though. Akiyama took the stage behind The Marvelous Brothers, having a microphone of his own. He said that he voiced his displeasure with Skylar Green for becoming so weak, whatever that was supposed to mean. It most likely draws back to their common history as allies in PCW, but we won't go into that. Akiyama then said that Green and Oxios needed to work better as a team if he was going to keep from losing the rematch (no mention of concern over Oxios's winning or losing), and made the challenge that the team of Skylar Green and Vangellus Oxios face Re:plica on the next show. Oxios accepted that match, and it was made for Adrenaline 25. When asked for comment after the show, Green said, 'I really need to get more proactive in making my own matches.'
