Triple Duty Again?

31 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Feburary 15, Chris Storm made RPW history by being the first wrestler to have three matches in one night. Now on April 4th he looks to do it again. The Perfect Storm is set to take on Tessa Windsor on Adrenaline this week in a non title match. This will be the first time the two have faced off in solo action since Feburary 15th when Chris defeated her and became the first ever Mid South champion. On that night Chris had to face two other matches before he got to her. Now he faces her as his first match of the night! Last in the Main event Chris finally gets his chance to shine as he takes on Jason O'Neil. In what will surly be a technical masterpiece, the two will lock up in a non title match that is for respect and honor. Chris Storm is calling this one of the most important matches of his RPW career. If he can take the victory he can prove his worth there. Finally after two grueling matches Chris must step on a plane and fly to Boston, Massachusetts where he will try and defend his PTC Global title against not one, not two, but three competitors. Can Chris find the energy to repeat his past and win all three matches or will it be too much for him. April 4th is going to be a LONG night for sure for the Last Great Technical Wrestler. If he can survive he will have surly earned the name Ironman.
