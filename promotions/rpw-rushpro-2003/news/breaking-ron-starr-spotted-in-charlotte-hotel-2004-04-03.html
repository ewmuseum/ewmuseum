BREAKING: Ron Starr Spotted in Charlotte Hotel

3 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Three sources have confirmed that Ron Starr has been spotted in Charlotte staying at the Charlotte Renisannance Suites. After checking the community calendar, and seeing that there were no ComicCon or RPGCons in town, it has been confirmed by one RPW source that Starr will be unveiled on Adrenaline as the Financial Backer of RPW. Reactions from the RPW Roster was mixed when informed of the situation. Nascosto Nerezza: Wonderful. And I suppose next, you'll say he's accompanied by Elvis Presley? Gideon Poe: "I think everything that needs to said about Ron Starr has been said. Everyone knows Ron was a big ambassador of "Give head to get ahead" as he so cutely put it. Unfortunately it didn't work that well for him in PWC, and it won't work this time. Thankfully the only thing Ron Starr has to worry about holding back these days is how much cash he needs to spend on his designer Buddy Holly haircuts and tinted retro bifocals" Invincible Gabe: "WHO THE FUCKING-ASS-DICK-CUMDUMPING FUCK CARES about Ron Starr?!?! Let's talk about me. I "financially backed" Ron's mom last night and the bitch didn't pay up! Think of it as a fucking testament to my greatness! I'm the Almighty Master of Ron Starr's Mom!...and didn't he go around for years wearing pantyhose on his head?" Dread Pirate Roberts: Inconceivable. The financial backer has been around for several months in a row, no children have been reported eaten, and there's not a generic talentless monkey frontlining the federation out of some ridiculous sense of friendship or loyalty. It can't be Ron Starr. Peter Maxwell: I don't give a shit. I'll punch him in his fat head, and push a fucking Warhammer figure into his eye. If it's him, he's seriously making a big mistake. BIG. MISTAKE. Carys: "If he comes near me with those mother fuckin' slimeball hands. Trying to make me the next Sam Alotti. I'm going to cut them off and ram them down his throat." Chris Storm: "Ron? There is no God if he is the financial backer. If I have to work for that guy then you can expect one unhappy Double Champion." Tessa Windsor: That's sure a surprise. It seems that most of the roster are quite upset about Ron Starr potentially taking over RPW. It's expected that he will be announced tomorrow night, possibly at the outset of the show.
