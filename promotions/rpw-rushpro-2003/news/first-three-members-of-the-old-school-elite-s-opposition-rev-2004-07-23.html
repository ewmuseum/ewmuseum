First Three Members of the Old School Elite's Opposition Revealed!

23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday night, it was reported that Dave Gibson set an open challenge to all within RPW. The challenge: to wrestle the Old School Elite in a 5 on 5 tag team match. Anyone who wished to enter the match had only to e-mail Christian Kannon and their participation would be confirmed. We can report now that the OSE's opposing team is 3/5ths complete. The first confirmation came from RPW's own former PTC Global Champion and resident Titanic Satanic Insaniac, Hessian. In his e-mail, which was sent only minutes after Christian Kannon's mass e-mail to all RPW wrestlers, Hessian reaffirmed his willingness to crush Chris Storm in all possible matches. Quoting from the e-mail, Hessian had this to say: 'Chris Storm has felt my wrath in several different matches, and there will yet another to come at Eve of Destruction. Important to the context of this message, though, is that Storm has on numerous occasions attempted to utilize his backup, the Old School Elite; The Gauntlet Match at Adrenaline 29, and the Lumberjack match on Adrenaline 30. Storm, for being the Last Great Technical Wrestler, can't seem to stand on his own two feet when faced with an actual challenge. When he can outsize his opponents, he doesn't mind much; when faced with a bigger, more dominating threat, he fails (more than usual) to live up to his own billing. 'The match will be a 10-Man tag team match. I've already dealt with the Old School Elite multiple times now, and soundly defeated them at every turn. This time, however, they will have to deal with the fact that I will once again beat Storm to a pulp in front of them, and there won't be anything that they can do unless they get tagged in. And Storm will learn what it means to show mercy, as I leave him just awake enough, just alive enough to come into the Halton Arena on Eve of Destruction to suffer the final nail in his coffin. 'If for no other reason than Chris Storm, I enter this 10-Man tag match. Just make anyone else who joins aware that just because we're on the same team doesn't mean that we're teammates.' Two days later, Skylar Green responded to the e-mail, saying that he would participate. He entered separate from Vangellus Oxios, and his e-mail was short and to the point, simply saying, 'I'm in,' with 'About the 10-Man Tag' as the subject of the e-mail. And just minutes ago, Vangellus Oxios e-mailed Christian Kannon saying that he would be the first to join the OSE Opposition. When a reply e-mail from Kannon informed Oxios that Hessian was the first to enter, Oxios countered with, 'I'm better than Hessian. A pay-per-view win will attest to that. Since I always come in ahead of Hessian, that means that I technically entered the match first.' Kannon could reportedly be heard uttering things under his breath that we need not repeat here, but Oxios was entered into the match. So far, the match is: Old School Elite (Shawn Stewart, The Marvelous Brothers, Pain, and Chris Storm) vs. Hessian, Skylar Green, and Vangellus Oxios (two more slots to fill) We'll keep you posted and let you know who else joins the match.
