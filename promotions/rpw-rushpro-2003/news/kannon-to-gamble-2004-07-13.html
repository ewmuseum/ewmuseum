Kannon To Gamble?

13 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Despite the cost-cutting measures put in place since Christian Alexander Kannon took charge of RUSH Pro Wrestling, the financial state of the company still seems allergic to being in the black. Although the wage bill is down, and advertising receipts are up, the company is still stranded on the slope to security, and in need of a push. How that push will be obtained, and who it will be given by, has been an intense topic of discussion behind the scenes. 'It's fair to say that everyone involved creatively in RPW has their own opinions,' an anonymous member of staff remarked, 'some want to saturate the shows with the most 'over' talent, some favour more development contacts to make new stars, and one had even suggested going to one show a fortnight. Mr. Kannon, however, seems more focussed on delivering something right away to get people talking.' Not situated in one of the most fashionable areas of the country, RPW has always struggled to attract a new and profitable fan base, and has so far avoided 'stunts' that other small promotions try to pull. 'So far everything has been built slowly,' the source continued. 'Solid and dependable workers have been signed, rising stars have been pushed, but there is a genuine fear that these investments will not pay off in time. Because of that, Kannon seems to favour something that will get the maximum amount of publicity in the shortest amount of time.' It appears that RPW is ready to gamble. 'All I will say is that RPW's status will be my primary concern in any choice I make,' Kannon's rather reserved statement on the matter began. 'Some fans might not approve, as could some of the roster, but if it breaks us even for the week, I'll do it... Ideals can wait until our debts are paid off.' Could this decision mark a change in direction for RPW, catering for a larger audience? And would such a change lose the promotion it's small but loyal fan base? Only time will tell, but already some involved seem displeased. 'I just hope we're not going to end up like TNA,' the source finished with, 'having a talented roster who give their all, only to be upstaged by Reality TV stars and Basketball players...'
