Doubts Over Wolfenden

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As seen on Adrenaline, Karina Wolfenden walked out on the show after a heated exchange with Rush Mason. Backed into a corner by Magnum Wolf, Mason was forced to concede, and a match pitting Wolf against Wolfenden was made... Where if Magz wins, his reward is a hefty RPW contract. Upon hearing of this, the K-Wolf confronted RPW's then-owner, and stormed off when her protests were ignored. 24 hours later, and it's still not clear where Karina, RPW's Eclipse Champion stands, and if she'll even return to face her nemesis. "He promise, he lied," were the words she muttered to backstage personnel on her way out, "I'm going off-line." RPW's new head, the wealthy teenager known only as "Little Al" has said the match stands, not wanting to bother with all the paperwork needed to abort such a match. Given the storied history of the duo referred to as Wolf/Enden, it's no surprise that such a move by Mason would infuriate the K-Wolf. Since the beginning of the year, Wolf has done his best to get at Karina on every level of her life, and even being partly to blame for her firing from OSW. During the year, Wolfenden has gotten the better of Magnum on several occasions, but neither have been able to deliver a knock-out blow to end such a heated rivalry... If there IS anything that could end it. Naturally, having RPW's only Champion AWOL with the belt, having already missed a promotional appearance earlier today, is troubling for the young company. However, any move to strip the highly popular superstar of the belt would not go down well with fans, especially given the reaction when she won the belt back last night. Once again it seems Magnum Wolf has instigated a serious backstage problem, and one where a simple push on Ilan Levin could separate RPW from one of it's most marketable talents.
