Choice Words for UNCC

10 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Akiyama is one known for his superior strength and his even superior ego at times, but his recent action has left some people questioning his motives. RPW reporter Caroline North reported not only to RPW, but to UNCC when Akiyama made a statement backstage after the show that would bring both parties down on him. "I enjoy a good drama here and there, but this is getting ridiculous," North reported him telling her as he left the Halton Arena after Adrenaline 23 with Re:plica. "This turns more and more into an episode of "Saved by the Bell: the College Years" every time I show up for work, between "Screech" Storm and Windsor to the prankish shennanigans some of the goofier people around here pull off. Invincibleness... Spiffster... whoever. RPW's got a hell of a long way to go before it can even get into superstar promotional status. And I can guarantee you that it won't get anywhere free riding off the labor of interning college students. Believe me... working here is NOT an educational experience. I feel dumber showing up at shows most of the time... it's either the fans or the fighters that do it, I forget..." A journalism student at UNCC herself, she was insulted at the way Akiyama spoke out about RPW, not to mention UNCC. In response, Akiyama shot back with this: "I'm sorry... but I fail to see any potential out of this place. It's a party school, plain and simple. There's no academic value here whatsoever, and I can prove it. I can guarantee you that I can take a pair with no formal training whatsoever in American studies and run them through an entire semester and bank straight As. Re:plica..." North reported the twins called Re:plica looked up at their master with a surprised expression, an unusual one opposite to their usual creepy demeanor. Akiyama nodded, stepping out of the building with his disciples. "Watch and wait, child... watch and wait. Re:plica will tear this place apart." After hearing this report, both RPW officials and UNCC officials were insulted by his remarks, yet intrigued by the wager he proposed. There'll be more to this on if they'll accept it or not...
