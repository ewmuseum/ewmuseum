The Forsaken; Belt Beatings And Other Stuff

8 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So I'm not good at titles. Sue me. Actually, don't. I need all the money I can get for prostitutes. My one weakness... Anyway. After Adrenaline aired, The Forsaken treated a few fans outside of the arena to an impromptu Q&A session, in which they discussed their title win, as well as the reasoning behind why they no-showed last week's Adrenaline, and why Clyde Walkins used a belt to the head to defeat The Freak Show. Without further ado, I give you the answers to those questions, which no one really gives a damn about anyway. You bastards. 'Why weren't we at Adrenaline last week?' James was heard to remark. 'Well, the main reason was that nobody told us we had a match. Here we are, the NEW RPW Tag Team Champions, and our own federation fails to tell us that we're even supposed to be at the arena. Seriously, there's some incompetence issues floating around the main offices of RPW.' When Clyde brought up the fact that James left his cell phone turned off after a four-day celebratory drinking binge, Farwell changed the subject. 'As for why we beat The Freak Show in the way we did...well...we did it because that's what we do! We can beat any team in the world...ANY TEAM...so why do we have to keep on proving it by fighting the undercard? The simple fact is that we are the best team in the world today, and when we're put up against The Joke Show, what are we supposed to do besides club them in the head with a belt? Do you actually expect us to stoop down each time a team wants our belts? Hell no! So as long as RPW keeps sending us crap, we'll keep flushing it.' As for what Clyde Walkins had to say in the matter...well, his comments directed towards The Freak Show were a little odd, to say the least. 'When the day is done and the dust has settled, I will stand above your fallen body and know that I am the victor. For in this life that we live there are no certainties, and when you least expect it I will sack you in the balls and take the easy win.' Wacky old Clydie.
