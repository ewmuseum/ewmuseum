Wolfenden Speaks; Issues Warning

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just days after storming out on Adrenaline, the K-Wolf herself, Karina Wolfenden has spoken about recent events regarding Magnum Wolf. "The match was made, the match can't be changed, so I have a choice; Fight or Run... And I refuse to run from him anymore," she purposefully stated. "In little over 2 weeks, he'll fight for an RPW contract, and given nowhere else will take him, he's pretty much fighting for his career... So I'm going to do everything in my power to make sure he isn't gettin' that win. Fair went out the window months ago, there'll be nothing for him to hide behind." Her words marked a noticeable change from Sunday's events, as it seems she's finally drawn the line against Magnum, seeing the match at Genesis as a way to finally remove the spectre of Magnum from her life. When asked how she felt RPW's new figurehead, still known only as "Little Al", would react, her words were just as blunt and unforgiving. "Fine me all you like afterwards, but don't get in my way until I'm finished." No word has been heard in reply from Magnum Wolf, but as one of the few to ever see the truly aggressive side of Karina Wolfenden, he'll know that sneakiness maybe even beyond his means may be needed to repel her wrath... And a protective cup wouldn't do any harm either.
