Trio Sign New Deals; Match Announced

25 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This morning, RPW owner Rush Mason announced that 3 of his roster had already put their names down to the new "written deals" he has offered. Those 3 were Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters, aka Civil Disobedience, and the K-Wolf herself.. Karina Wolfenden. "Good place, good people, they keep the 'undesirables' out... I like the place," Karina commented briefly, "So as long as they keep the ropes up, I'll keep coming to play on Sundays." Mason also revealed that Eclipse Champion Ilan Levin is on the verge of signing a deal, as are Kohana and Ken Kaze. "The roster admit to the benefits, and once a few more sign their name, we expect the rest to follow." It looks doubtful, however, that Brotherly Love will sign the new deals that would disrupt their fWo schedule. No word is known yet on what this will mean in regards to their RPW future. In other news, the newly re-signed trio of Civil D and the K-Wolf will team together on Saturday to face Alyssa Sanders, Carys and Sam Alloti, who've formed a close until over the past few weeks. Other matches rumoured to be appearing on Adrenaline are AJ Trinidad vs. L'Diable Blanc, and Ilan Levin’s 1st Eclipse Title defence. More news on RPW's contract situation as it emerges.
