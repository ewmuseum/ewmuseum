Contract Problems Arise

23 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When RPW was launched, Rush Mason had a grand vision. RPW would be an open forum for wrestlers all over the globe to come and showcase their talents. Stars from many federations across the USA and Japan came, heading to Charlotte on a Sunday to work for RPW, but many were also working Indy bookings during the week. At Sunday's 3rd edition of Adrenaline, many of RPW's road agents were complaining of wrestler fatigue, as a large amount had wrestled for various Indy promotion's Saturday shows... This led to several slow moments in the ring, and a general feeling of tiredness towards promos which led to some very brief appearances on camera (especially from those carrying visual injuries from the previous night). Naturally, RPW management wasn't happy. But, rather than take a harsh line, Rush Mason has revealed a plan to reward wrestlers for their loyalty to his product. "There's a reason some of RPW's talent work as many bookings as they do," he explained, "There are the few who truly love it, but it's down to money. They can make enough to see them through the week with a big Saturday show pay-off, and the money from their RPW appearance is a healthy bonus. So what I'm having drawn up is a new written contract I plan to offer the roster, where for an increased basic amount, and a larger cut of merchandise, they'll agree to work no bookings on Saturday, and won't work again until Tuesday so they can fulfil RPW promotional dates on the Monday following Adrenaline. These contracts will grow as RPW grows, so the more they put into the product to help it flourish, the larger their pay-off will be." Mason will be keen to sign some of his bigger names to the new deals, with Dorn Tracer, El Diablo and Civil Disobedience likely to be top of his list, as well as the Eclipse Division, who's wrestling style leads to nagging injuries which can only be made worse by working 2 nights in a row. Whether such talent as Brotherly Love who already work a cluttered fWo schedule will sign the new deal, especially given that Ruahh~!! falls on a Monday is doubtful unless a compromise can be made. "Signing means RPW will be your home," Mason added, "And we'll be taking good care of our contracted talent, both in the ring and out. There won't be any bias towards those who decide to stay on an open contract, but not being at 100% for every show could make their position very difficult. Once we have a good core of our on-air talent under written contract, we'll be looking to open the doors even further for Indy talent. If they can wrestle and entertain, we want to give them a shot, let them be heard, give their style an audience... That's what RPW is about, letting it's wrestlers show what they can do without hindrance." Updates are expected this week on the progress of these deals, but with the added security they'll offer, many of RPW's top names are said to be in favour of the deal and looking to sign as soon as possible, to make RPW their "home".
