Lee Released, Poe's Future In Doubt

25 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While many in RPW are looking to improve and solidify their contracts, Rush Mason has relieved one wrestler from his duties to the Federation all together... That wrestler being former PWC and PURE talent 'Sinister' Steven Lee. "He'd made it clear that RPW wouldn't rank highly in his priorities," revealed Mason, "And the best thing about an open door policy, is that people can go out of it as quick as they come in." However, one wrestler who's more than happy to commit to RPW is having problems of his own. Gideon Poe, currently injured after an attack headed by Peter Maxwell, is looking to sign a written deal, but the agreement he has presently might not even hold at the present time. "It may seem harsh," Rush Mason regretfully stated, "But with the open, pay-per-appearance deal most of RPW are on, we're not obliged to pay medical and rehab costs... And it's more trouble than it's worth to deal with injured talent. Poe is one I'd love to have tied to RPW with a written contract... But with his body in the state it is, I can't even have him back right now under any deal." These words are sure to trouble Poe, who will be looking for revenge as soon as his body is capable, but whether he'll be able to exact it in RPW is still to be seen.
