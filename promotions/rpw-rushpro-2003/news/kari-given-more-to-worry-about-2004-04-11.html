Kari Given More To Worry About

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While it's still unsure exactly what role Christian Alexander Kannon will play within RPW, one thing is for sure... He certainly isn't going to be big on diplomacy. The first target of his talent concerns was a rather unexpected one, as RPW's figurehead had a few choice words to say about firm-fan favourite, and the woman who main evented RPW's last "Super Adrenaline" - Karina Wolfenden. "Some people on the roster I'll admit I didn't expect much from," his rather blunt statement went, "but Karina wasn't one of them, which disappoints me. I remember seeing her just over a month ago when she basically flipped-off gravity and just ignored physics completely, and the fans loved it. Now? Given her last 2 performances against Hessian and Rampage, she's one of those I feel needs a little "adjustment"." When reminded of the vast size difference between the K-Wolf and her most recent opponents, Kannon didn't seem too bothered. "It's not a matter of how big or how small, win or lose," he dismissed. "There was no shame in losing to them, but the manner of her defeats is what's bothering me. When I saw her battle Wolf, I saw a never-say-die spirit. I saw something in her eyes during those matches that told me any investment in her - whether it be money or air-time - would be a good one. Now? The Karina Wolfenden I watched last week was just a cheap imitation." His solution to the apparent problem? "Easy, I give her another big-time match and see how she responds," Kannon revealed. "At Adrenaline, I'm giving her Chris Storm. He's no 7 footer, he doesn't top 300 pounds, but if someone goes against him without focus, then they'll leave in pieces. If the Karina Wolfenden I want in RPW turns up, we'll have one hell of an opener this Sunday... If not, she'll find out what happens when people's personal lives get in the way of business... My business." When the topic of Magnum Wolf was brought up in relation to Wolfenden, Kannon's reaction was far from caring. "If she hates Wolf so much, why is she letting him win like this?" As soon as he was informed of the match, RPW's Mid-South Champion - Chris Storm - began to shoot his mouth off... Seemingly enjoying working his opponents over verbally pre-match. "People need to stand up and give Magnum Wolf a standing ovation for all he's done," Storm snapped. "He tries to bring a little morality into RPW, the fed I represent as a Champion, and he's vilified for it... While she's built up as some kind of Heroine? As an adoptive father, I find her actions sickening... But not half as sickening as the Suplexes I have ready for Sunday." Will Storm face off against the increasingly depressed Wolfenden RPW has seen as of late, or will his and Kannon's words, along with Wolf's actions, stir the fight up inside Karina and give RPW a new Mid-South Champion? Tune in on Sunday to see.
