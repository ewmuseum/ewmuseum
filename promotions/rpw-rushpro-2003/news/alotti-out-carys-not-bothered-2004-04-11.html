Alotti out, Carys not bothered

11 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Samantha Alotti is scheduled to be out of action for two or three weeks after damaging the muscles in her neck last week on Adrenaline, so doctors are stating. The recipient of the brutal springboard piledriver from the top rope from Eclipse Champion Carys is currently quoted to be “like, totally in pain” and doesn’t intend to be out of the local hospital in time for this weeks Adrenaline, maybe not even next week. Doctor Jonathon Daniels stated; “ People who fall on their necks at a velocity such as that don’t get out of hospital quickly. Given the lack of build of Ms. Alotti, she’s lucky not to have seriously damaged something. In my opinion, wrestling is a particularly…” Yeah, we cut him off there. Started to get bored. Sorry. Carys is said to “not care in the slightest” about the injury she has inflicted upon the young Englishwoman and quite frankly, who can blame her? She’s not the most likeable young girl in the world now is she?
