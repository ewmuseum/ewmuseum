Vangellus Oxios Medical Condition Discovered

7 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After making an appearance at the Georgia Tech on Thursday evening, Vangellus Oxios got into a car accident. Following the event, the Van World Order founder and CEO was leaving on his way to the airport to return to his United States address in Belmont, North Carolina. His own driver, who as escorting him in a indigo blue Ford PT Cruiser, drove off of the road into a tree. Oxios sustained minor injuries, as did the driver. Both were transported to a nearby hospital for treatment. The driver was treated and released into police custody, as he was found to have a blood alcohol level of .223, almost three times the legal intoxication mark. As an interesting note, a medical report has been leaked from the hospital. It indicates that as part of a routine battery of X-rays, an medical oddity was confirmed in Vangellus Oxios. The notations made by his doctor, Ms. Salia Tenser, confirms that Oxios's skull is three-eights of an inch thicker than normal. A separate medical professional, Dr. Evan Westell, suggests that the stress on Oxios's brain might explain his odd behavior, as the blood vessels could be constrained, resulting in a smaller amount of oxygen than normal reaching his brain. However, it was also suggested that Oxios could suffer injuries to his head with minimal injury that would cause major brain damage to another person. Given that he has no major injuries, Vangellus Oxios is expected to appear at Adrenaline this week.
