Before Today #11 : Magz, Lies & Videotape (Part One)

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/01/04 'Oy, kid,' discreetly whispered the mischievous Magnum Wolf, 'you got the bottle?' Through the view-finder of his video camera, Wolf was watching Karina Wolfenden, zooming in on her smiling face as she breezed around backstage at PWC TV... The last PWC TV she'd wrestle at, if Magnum had anything to do with it. This tape wasn't for his own 'private' collection, that wouldn't begin until he finished the drill-hole though her changing room's wall, this was all for money. The money of Karina's father, Lawrence Wolfenden. Huddled out of view beside Magnum, his apparent lackey gave a positive grunt, which was followed by the shaking of what sounded like a pill bottle. 'Goooood,' the almost sinister whispering of Magnum continued, 'now don't screw this up... or I'll be buying a night's worth of Jersey hookers with your $20!' When Magnum told Lawrence Wolfenden that he'd record a special 'expose' of how the PWC was really ran, he had no intention of simply filming some of the more unsavoury characters backstage... Wolf wanted to make a tape that would have Lawrence yanking his daughter from pro-wrestling faster than he could gloat about it. It was Karina's punishment for not sharing Magnum's ideals. The floaty Karina stopped and pirouetted, seemingly looking around for someone, all the time completely oblivious to the fact her movements were being archived. The view-finder was gradually filled more and more with Karina's face, zooming in to show her emerald eyes scanning around. 'As you will see, Lawrence old pal,' Wolf's hushed commentary continued, 'the Pro Wrestling Circuit is a seedy place full of shady individuals, the type that give the likes of me a bad name.' There was a fumbling sound as Wolf pushed his accomplice away from him, sending him into the theatre, so to speak. 'I'm sure this is NOT the sort of place you'd want your daughter frequenting on a Saturday night.' His accomplice crept into view, skulking up behind Karina while trying hard not to look at the hidden camera. 'Oh, I recognise THAT guy,' a falsely surprised Wolf commented, 'that looks like Harry the Steroid Pusher, a kinda regular around here and NOT a nice guy... Don't let his dorky look fool ya.' Magnum zoomed in on his accomplice, who was doing his best to be inconspicuous... Crossing his arms tightly across his huddled body, eye darting around. 'Of course, I've told him to get lost many times... As I for one abhor the use of drugs,' the silver tongue of Wolf added. 'But let's face it, Karina isn't as strong willed and street-smart as I...' Karina's turn brought Magnum's lackey into her sights, and she stepped towards him, open and friendly. 'Oh dear,' Wolf sighed, laying on the false sincerity as Karina began to speak to the man he dubbed 'Harry', 'it looks like Karina is about to be sucked into a downward spiral of Steroid Abuse. I hope you like kids, Lawrie, because you’ll probably have 3 grandchildren to look after when their Mommy finally checks into rehab.' Away from the view-finder, a rather different story was unfolding. 'Hi,' was her smiley greeting to the seemingly nervous man, 'have you seen Alloti about?' Wolf let out an ‘Ahhhhhhhhhhhh’ from his hidden place, before adding his own translation. 'She's speaking in Druggy Code,' Magz accused, 'she wants some 'Alloti', the STRONGEST kind of metabolic steroids. Looks like we're too late, she's well on the way to shooting up before breakfast every morning.' Wolf's shady accomplice didn't answer her enquiry, and instead stooped close towards her. From his pocket he whipped out a plastic medication bottle and shook it in front of her bemused face. Kari's expression turned to disgust and she went to push him away, but not until after the recording light of Wolf's video camera stopped flashing. 'Oh, Karina,' the seedy Private Eye gloated, 'what would your Daddy say?' Kari dumped Wolf's accomplice down onto his ass before striding past him, letting out a sickened sigh. When her footsteps could be heard no more, he turned to where the camera concealed and gave a thumbs up. Emerging from his hiding place, Magnum gave a hearty chuckle, having gotten some great material for Lawrence. 'I don't think my client would be too happy watching his daughter fraternise with drug dealers,' the Private Investigator beamed, already working out the kind of fee he could charge for the footage. 'Good job there are fine, upstanding people like me to protect them from society's scum.' He quickly turned to his rising accomplice, who sill had the medication bottle in his hand. 'Shame on you,' Wolf then spat out as he clipped the kid behind the ear, 'BAD drug dealer.' Wolf then looked back to the tarpaulin covered, discarded packing crate where he was hidden. 'Now, to find my next vantage point...' An enthusiastic Magnum strolled off, while his accomplice followed, rubbing the sore spot where Wolf clipped him. 'Scorsese, eat your heart out...'
