The Worst Vacation Ever

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/04 While vacationing in Hawaii with the funds made by providing a place to stay in my Boston apartment for the few men who qualified, I finally began to realize something. Maybe men really aren’t as great as I make them out to be. I mean lets face it, men either have the looks or the brains. They aren’t capable of having both at the same time, unlike women. This point was especially proven as I walked across the sandy white grounds of some beach in Hawaii. I couldn’t recall the name, mainly because I probably wouldn’t want to considering nothing I saw there amazed me any. I was surrounded by losers, DAMNIT! This wasn’t the way I should be spending my vacation which I worked so hard to fund. After all, it isn’t easy keeping a new guy company every night! Yet here I was, surrounded by losers with their tongue hanging out of their mouth as soon as they caught sight of me. I couldn’t blame them for acting that way though. There didn’t seem to be a single decent woman on this beach either. I was in hell, plain and simple. “Excuse me miss, but I don’t believe you belong on this beach.” A deep feminine voice came from my side. Turning my head back to see who the voice belonged to, the only person in my proximity was a chubby woman whose head was shaped just like the pineapple she was munching on. “NO SHIT! It seems as though I am the only decent looking woman within miles of this place.” I replied with a snap, as I quickly turned my head back forward, only to bump my head up against the worst possible thing, a chest covered in hair and sweat. “KILL HER! SHE MUST BE A CULT LEADER IF SHE IS THIS GOOD LOOKING!” The hairy man shouted as he put his full forced effort into leaning forward and grabbing the ponytail I had pulled my silky black locks into. “What the fuck? WHO the hell are you guys and WHAT the hell is your problem?” I said as the hairy man pulled me up to my feet. It was obvious the man wasn’t in much shape considering he was already out of breath just from pulling me up to my feet. “THOU SHALT NOT SPEAK TO THE HOLY GUARDS OF PRIMET….I MEAN WAKUKU BEACH, THAT WAY!” The hairy man shouted in my ears as if he were the drill sergeant and I were his pupil. “PEOPLE LIKE YOU ARE NOT WELCOME HERE!” “Errr yeah….if you say so.” I replied with a roll of my ocean blue eyes. If these guys were trying to make a point they were failing very miserably at it considering it was nearly impossible to pay attention to them. Every time I set an eye on them, I had to keep from gagging. “Keep your mouth shut!” The female shouted. “It’s because of people like YOU that popularity contests happen! If you come here EVERYTHING will become a popularity contest!” “If you say so fatso.” I muttered considering this was the last thing I pictured to happen on the vacation which was not meant to be spent this way. “The name is Colleen! And the dude who is about to shut you up is named Hugh!” The fat woman screamed as her saliva squirted all over the place with the pronunciation of each syllable. “Isn’t that great? Now would you mind getting over your jealous rage? I have better things to do than listen to your rants about how life isn’t fair because people don’t give you fucked up islanders as much as they give us mainlanders.” I replied in a casual tone to hint that I knew exactly what this was all about and that I really didn’t give a shit if the rest of the world didn’t give a shit about their people. I couldn’t blame them. Who would want to waste their time on such an idiotic group of people who weren’t capable of understanding they meant nothing to the rest of the world. “NEVER! NOT UNTIL YOU MAINLANDERS START TO GIVE US THE RECOGNITION WE DESERVE!” The hairy man shouted, with a rage which equaled up to Rampage’s. “How do you expect recognition when there is nothing worthy of recognizing you for?” I questioned as a man clad in business attire came strolling up to the scene. The eyes of the two islanders lit up with glee as they caught sight of the business man and immediately I found myself free from the hairy man Hughes grip. “Well good afternoon there! Have I got an offer for you that you can’t refuse! My name is Prest…I mean Tom…and I’d love the give you two the recognition that you deserve! Forget the mainlanders! They will be nothing once us three are in business together.” The business man said as he immediately planted his suitcase in the sand and began to dig the little cash he had out of his pocket while the two other two islanders remained speechless with their mouths dropped wide open as if this man were the god he perceived himself to be. While the two of them were off fantasizing about the recognition they would never get I decided it would be best if I hightailed it and booked the first flight back home. There was no way I was staying on this hellhole of an island anymore. No longer was I going to pay hundreds of dollars to see a bunch of hot men that clearly on an island, because clearly that was nothing but a ploy to get people to their island. I suppose I could just settle with what I had in Rampage for the moment. Though taking a trip to wherever that dude Malphas came from might not be an idea. After all Malphas seemed like the type of guy who could take me to peaks that rival the Himalayas. Maybe there were more guys like him wherever he came from….NAH! I guess Rampage would have to do for now. Then again, was he really that bad of a choice? I mean with a temper like that just imagine what he could do in bed.
