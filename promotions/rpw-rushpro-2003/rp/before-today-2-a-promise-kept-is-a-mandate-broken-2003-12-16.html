Before Today #2 : A Promise Kept Is A Mandate Broken

16 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/12/03 She'd promised. While most wrestlers make event preparation a laboured process, Karina Wolfenden took her usual approach, same as any other show. No sports or travel bags, just a lone black & white camouflage QuikSilver rucksack she'd picked up from a small store in London a few years ago, cheaply. Into it she dropped a pair of high-impact elbow pads, a towel, and a copy of 'Maya' by Jostein Gaarder. The phone rang, just as it had all day, endless well-meaning people warning her about a call... This call. Ignoring it, not breaking the trend of the day that led to a stream of answering machine messages, Karina simply pulled her blonde and pink hair back into a tight ponytail, and waited for the click. *Click* 'Karina, this is Jane,' stated the stern and authoritative voice of OSW's owner Jane Reagan, 'I've been told you're still gong to appear tonight...' For all intents and purposes, Jane Reagan was Karina's boss. After the small, New Jersey promotion PWC folded, Karina decided she wasn't quite done with wrestling, and in a complimentary twist of fate, it appeared wrestling wasn't done with her. More well known than she thought, or wished, herself to be, Karina got the call to the big leagues, and OSW TV was her next port of call. 'You know the dictate that management released this week,' Jane continued, while Karina zipped up her red and black leathers, then slung her rucksack over her right shoulder, 'And I won't insult your intelligence by assuming you didn't understand it.' She was a hit, and despite the initial unease at 10,000 fans cheering her on, Karina excelled, bettering herself with every Daredevil move. OSW fans liked their small taste of the K-Wolf, and Jane responded by offering a full-time deal. But the desires of the 'Nationals' would never eclipse an obligation to the Indies. True to her word, Karina fulfilled all the Indy dates she'd agreed to before going Old School full-time, but it was the last of them that threw up the dilemma. 'Any and all OSW stars are forbidden from working the same show as Magnum Wolf,' Jane harshly reminded Karina, although she was too busy slipping her left arm through the strap, then grabbing her helmet off the kitchen counter, 'He's the one and only forbidden, and he's been advertised on the same card as you. This isn't flexible, this is an ultimatum... If you wrestle tonight, then the final contract I have drawn up in front of me has a meeting with the shredder. I wouldn't expect it from any of our big names, so I certainly wouldn't expect it from hopeful debutants.' David Richardson, a good, fun-loving wrestling fan had invested most of his savings in his dream; A small wrestling promotion operating out of Albany, New York. He raised the funds by running his own record store for 15 years, the shop he worked at from the age of 13, and the shop that fuelled one of Karina's passions from a young age. He gave her Bif Naked, the Breeders and Belly, now she was giving him a high-flying exhibition. She'd promised. 'It's nothing personal,' Jane continued in the background, while Karina breezed past the phone, picking up her keys from beside it, 'In fact, it's been nothing but a pleasure working with you so far, but given Magnum's actions before and after his firing, especially the comments on the Internet, we're having to go zero tolerance on this.' Jane finished her sentence as the door closed behind Karina. She didn't need OSW's money, considering that everything was more than paid for well before any wrestling earnings were factored in, but she needed her pride. Her word wouldn't be bought, wouldn’t be given an ultimatum, and wouldn't give in to zero tolerance. 'I'll call you again in the morning,' Jane then sighed, realising Karina wasn't there to pick up and talk, or that she was screening, 'And hopefully we'll be talking about future plans rather than saying our goodbyes.' Jane's line hung up, and the minimalist apartment was left with a volume level to match. If there was one person Karina wasn't doing this for, it was Magnum Wolf. In 24 years, he'd been the only person to intrude upon her personal life, to hate her that much that he'd try to turn those closest to Kari against her... He nearly succeeded, and in return for instigating the K-Wolf's nadir, he'd become a sworn foe. He'd be at Richardson's show as well, despite a less than glowing reference from Karina, and any words directed her way would likely be met with a kick to the mouth. Despite the price, despite the company she'd be keeping, and despite the small pay-off, she'd keep her word. From outside, the gutsy roar of her Ducati tore through the early evening, before accelerating off into the Albany night. Karina had kept her promise, and if Jane did the same, Kari would be saying her goodbyes to OSW the next morning. And she did, with no regrets.
