An attraction

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/04 I'm not exactly a romantic guy. Hell, a guy with a rage complex isn't exactly anything but an unstable angry mess. In my case, a big unstable angry mess. But every now and then, something stirs the pit of my stomach and draws me in. Or tries to. I didn't get drawn in, but for the love of God, I wanted to. I don't know how I held my ground. I guess I wasn't in the mood or maybe I was just aggravated at Gabe... again. But when Alyssa walked in, man, I couldn't keep my eyes off of her. I'll admit, I'm no novice with women, and I hear she makes her way around the male circuit, but regardless of all that, well, she certainly flipped a switch or two with me. That hasn't happened in years, and I never expected it to. What do I do? I wrestle to send money home to my mom and sister so they can live well. I haven't bought anything new in years. My boots, my kneepads and elbowpads, my gloves, they're all at least three years old. My Jeep Wrangler is older than that. I don't get my hair cut because I don't want to pay for it, so I never bothered to even think that some female would have any sort of interest in me. I'm well aware of the fact that she could have been playing a game with me. Trying to entice the big man, tie a string around his neck, draw him in and all that sort of stuff. It sounds so damn corny, but I think there was something deeper than that. I mean, that there wasn't just some kind of animal lust. Sure, that was there, too, but it was a little more. Hell, I'm probably just crazy. Scott Matthews is a big man. He's a tough man. And he hangs out with Gabe. So why put up that kind of facade? There's plenty to gain from it. I mean, she could try to bend a monster of a guy to her will and drag Gabe along for the ride. It might be insurance against us challenging for those tag straps. It might be a million different things and to be real honest, I don't care. I did make her leave, I know that. But the way I did it, I don't know why. I guess that doesn't matter either. I do know that under it all, I wanted to indulge myself. No kidding, no two ways about it, but I can't stand that seductress act. That's why I don't think it was any kind of ulterior thing, I think it was more of an honest attraction. If it had been purely a seductive act, I would have been madder than hell and probably have tossed her out on her butt instead of smacking it. Then again, who am I kidding. She could pull that act on any guy she wanted and tie him around her little finger without much of an effort on her part. If she is as, err, experienced as they say she is, she'd enjoy it and just tie a guy's head into knots. Who am I? Just some big guy that can't get a grip on himself. They call me a beast. They call me Rampage. Beasts don't love. I shouldn't disillusion myself.
