Do You Remember Me? (Part 2)

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 30th, 2004 Chapter 4: The Fall Out With Tessa For the second time in my life, I found myself jobless. Now PCW had been paying me a lot of money to be a part of the Schmoopies. Quitting a job that good was a damn hard thing to do. Actually no, I lied again. It was the easiest thing I could have ever done. Sure, the money was great but my relationship with Tessa got shot to hell and my wrestling career fell into the shitter. The relationship with Tessa got messed up real badly in PCW. We haven’t been the same since, which was over a year ago. She was big on PCW; it was the big times for her. To me it was nothing special. I had actually applied to a wrestling school instead but they turned me down because I already had a job for PCW. Image that, turning down a major promote like PCW just so you can go back to school. Sadly, if I had been allowed to go to PWA, Primetime Wrestling Academy, I might still be with Tessa Windsor. What broke us up? Well she couldn’t believe I wanted to leave. We got into a whole big argument about it one night. I just couldn’t stand that she agreed on everything the office gave to us without even letting me open my mouth. She agreed to some of the lamest stuff I had ever heard. But she had her reasons. If you were making that, much money I doubt you would be in a big rush to disagree with you boss either. That’s the difference between Tessa and me. She did this for the glory and money. I wrestle because I love it. I absolutely love going out there and slamming on a Hammerlock on someone then arching back and throwing them with a Suplex. Oh man it’s a rush standing over a man on his back and be legally allowed to punch in his skill. I’m just a violent type of guy though. I’ve ended several careers in my time just so I could have their spot on the card. Tessa never could hurt anyone though it wasn’t in her nature. Chapter 5: Storm goes to DWO I actually had my departure for PCW planned out in advance. I finally had it with the bad booking and angles. I had some friends over at Dominate Wrestling Online, or DWO as it was known, that I got to know when I did some cross-promotional work when I was in WIW. I talked to Callisto Bane, the owner, and got my contract all hammered out. By April, I had left PCW and was free to work the DWO. It wasn’t until June that I finally got to wrestle. Why did it take me two months to appear when I already had a contract and an angle planned out? Because the owner dicked me around as much as she could. DWO is the only company I have ever left on bad terms. Never in my life had I seen a less professional place. They cared more about lesbian sex (the owner is a lesbian, she runs DWO with her wife Lexy Bane) and porn than the wrestling business. For two months, I had to sit on my ass without pay just so Callisto could work with their top draw Chryste. She couldn’t get her ass out of the spotlight and let anyone else have it. So I had to wait and ended up getting royally fucked over for my entire angle. I was supposed to work with Chryste since we were both huge in Primetime Central. Instead, I was placed to help put Markus Stone, a buddy from WIW, over. I had no problems working with Markus I enjoyed it. But I saw they were stringing him along too. He had knee problems so they used him as a feeder to other stars. I’ll talk about Chryste for a second. She and I were a love-hate relationship. I loved working with her, it was always gold; but at the same time, I hated her because it came so easy to her. She could go out there and entertain everyone without dropping a bit of sweat. I always had to bust my ass. She was the only professional to really come from DWO. Maybe that is why she went off to better things once they closed. *Editor’s note. I recently got to work with Chryste in RPW. It was an absolute blast. Sadly, we had twist and turns thrown at us. An unexpected tournament came up that we had to work around. Chryste never complained once about anything the RPW office threw at us. She just kept making the best of it. Our program wasn’t too well received but the match with her at Overdrive will always go down as one of my all time favorites. I just wanted to say thanks to her for willing to help me and finally give me the feud DWO screwed me out of. Chapter 6: The Road to Retirement DWO turned out to be less fun than PCW. After my first month of real competition there I bolted. The major promote, probably the largest at the time, Old School Wrestling (who at the time was being called Old School Entertainment) had just opened a wrestling school. I was one of the first to join. For the first time in years, I had been pumped about wrestling again. Finally, I would get the chance to work with the legendary trainer Tempest, who was a major OSE star and head teacher of PWA. Sadly, things didn’t go as planned. For a month, I busted my ass in CDC. In that whole time I never had a single wrestler there even look my way and give me help. I was there to learn Damnit, but none of them wanted to teach. The straw that broke my back with CDC was when Tessa got hired straight to OSE. PCW had just closed and OSE was making a mad rush to scoop up all its talent. Here I am busting my hump to get a job and learn and she just strolls in and gets a big contract. It made me want to throw up. I’m a serious wrestler who loves the business. She just does it because she can. I left CDC. Are you starting to notice a pattern forming here? It seems I can’t stay a place for more than a few months before I start hating it. I think I just had bad luck. I needed a place I could find my niche in like I did with WIW. There needed to be a league that would allow me to do my own thing and actually let me run with the ball. With three bad leagues behind me, I did the only thing I could think of. I retired. It pained me greatly to retire. I did not want to leave. Whenever I feel asleep, I could hear the fans cheering, in my dreams I suplexed anyone who got in the ring with me. Each morning I woke up, I muttered to myself how I needed to get back into the business. That’s why I stayed in such great shape. I’m actually in the best shape of my life. Chapter 7: RPW Calls Finally, I had a break though. My phone rang. I can remember answering it and thinking it was a prank phone call. A new league had opened up. It was called Rush Pro Wrestling. They wanted me to be on the starting roster. They told me what to be but I didn’t have any problem with it. I was willing to be ordered around if it meant stepping back into the ring. Hell I didn’t want to let myself get rusty and lose my career. That was about six months ago. Since then I have been on every RPW show I could. I have yet to miss a show for personal reason or injury. I even had a pretty bad concession and I stilled wrestled. Course like a bad rap, Tessa Windsor followed me. OSE closed and she contacted RPW about a career. Within a month, she was with me in RPW making life a misery for me. I have gotten where I am because I am the best at what I do, wrestling. I know Freestyle, Greco Roman, and Technical wrestling. Tessa has gotten where she is because, well I don’t know. But for some reason whatever it is she has, it gets her to one up me every turn of my career. In RPW, I have sworn I would not allow her to do it again. I would prove I was the better wrestler and should have gotten the OSE career, not her. I should have been making the big bucks instead of retiring. It is her fault, all because she doesn’t care about the wrestling business. But I’m going to teach her, the hard way.
