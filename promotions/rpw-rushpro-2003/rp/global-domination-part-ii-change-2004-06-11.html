Global Domination: Part II - Change

11 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-06-04 He was the dealer of fate to the immortals, the 7’2” Mercenary of Satan. He was the greatest wrestler in PTC today, the most intimidating man to be faced in the squared circle. He was the PTC Global Champion. …He was Hessian, the man sitting in the corner of the room, hugging his knees and holding back the tears. Hessian thought to himself, trying to concentrate while the four walls seemed to be closing in around him. Today was to be a day he showcased his supremacy over men such as Saij and Digital Mortality, before physically doing so in the ring. On attaining the Global Title something inside him changed. A choking pile of cold, hard cash had extinguished the hellfire that burned inside him. Certainly Hessian wasn’t made of money but it was this that had tainted him, pulling him from the dark recesses into a world where the bright lights shone his name for all to see. The Global Title had brought him straight to the top, and for once the Mercenary of Satan was tempted by the bright lights and fame. Money was the root of all evil, but in Hessian’s case it was not that he bought out companies or indulged in the slave trade, quite the opposite. The evil of money was the fact that it had lured him from his dominating position at the top of the Dark Gimmick hierarchy. After fighting for so long to preserve honour within the bloodline and claim his rightful place at the top, Hessian had thrown it all away…and sold out. For so long he was content with simply wrestling and being his evil old self, even holding the OSW Tag Team Titles and the Hardcore Title he simply used them as a lure to draw in more prey. Yet this Global Title was more than just a lure, it was a trap. Hessian was the last person thought to succumb to such temptation. Yet there he sat, in realization that his actions throughout the past month were those of a sell-out. The money-grubbing, the cockiness, the odd changes in behaviour. It was as though he was possessed by the same demons as the legendary Tyler Nelson, although his greed was on a much lesser extent. Getting up off the floor, Hessian approached the Global Title and picked it up off of the ground, dusting it off and looking it over. The gold shimmered across his face and he felt himself go into a slight daze. Just looking at the title made him feel warm inside. It had consumed him, just as Vampir Nosferatu had done all those years ago, turning him into what he was today. Gazing at the title for a few more seconds, Hessian suddenly gasped and threw it down once more, this time on the office desk before him. “This title is a curse.” Hessian whispered softly to himself. “I’ve given up everything I loved and stood for…and for what? A piece of tin and grief from some fucking peons? This isn’t who I am.” Sure enough, as he won the title it began to change him. He wasn’t the hell raiser he had been with a new belt; Hessian didn’t have the PTC Global Title. The PTC Global Title had him. Even as he dismissed it and fought against its enticement he couldn’t help but glance at it again and again, overcome by its beauty. It was because of this title that he had sat through the press conference. Being in the public eye so prolifically and even answering such brutally bland questions hadn’t entered into his thoughts, all that was important was the Global Title that lay before him for the entire world to see. Now that he was alone and thinking for himself. There were no PR agents around, no managers, no representatives…no one. Just Hessian. He felt worn and dirty, like everything he did was not of his doing or to his satisfaction. He thought back to Adrenaline, the same show in which Violence Jack broke his nose. Shuddering, the images flashed in his mind again of the promos that had aired showing Hessian advertising for a local Red Cross and car dealership. He felt his legs shaking slightly and took a seat at the desk, bowing his head and sighing as he reflected back on what could only have been a bad decision. Hessian stared at the title, sneering at the thought of those adverts, and everything he had done. What came to mind most often was Minion the Imp. Hessian had spied him in the crowd of reporters, yet he hadn’t come to the press conference with Minion. In fact in the past month Minion and he had only ever interacted a few times backstage. His head ached as he thought about all they had been through together. Hessian was projected into the spotlight with Minion by his side, without him he would have very well died with NTICW. One of the most charismatic and easily recognisable managers in wrestling history, and Hessian turned his back on him. Once again the greed was to blame. After his success in Old School Wrestling, the last thing Hessian expected was to be signed to Rush Pro Wrestling. However he made the move from OSW, a global corporation and one of the greatest federations ever to grace the wrestling world to the RPW, a federation that could barely afford to keep its wrestlers never mind splash out on luxuries. Hessian made the transition without a complaint to be heard. That didn’t mean he wasn’t against it. Sure the cream of the PCW, OSW and PWC rosters were there but his decision to sign was partly thanks to one person…Minion the Imp. His manager. Winning the Global Title was like a bad wake-up call. For the first time in many months Hessian held a title and he did it on his own. Minion was barely around to manage him, and perhaps in a moment of extremely poor judgement and cockiness, Hessian made the mistake of severing the link between the two. Minion however couldn’t bear to part with his greatest success and stayed loyal in the hopes Hessian would see sense. Today proved that it might have been too late, as Minion took his leave once more, possibly for the last time…while Hessian only now realized the error of his ways. “I’m going to change this!” Hessian said to himself. “I’ll be the champion I want to be, I’ll do it the way I want to…I will reclaim my lost self…” Easier said than done. Standing up, Hessian glanced across the room at a mirror hanging above a small sink. Staring at himself it was obvious nothing had changed. His attire still screamed evil and intimidating, inside however he felt about as intimidating as Tessa Windsor did before being powerbombed over the top turnbuckle onto the steel steps. Cursing under his breath, Hessian approached the mirror and removed it from the wall, taking a seat and staring at his reflection. His face looked old and worn. He didn’t have to look at it twice before shutting his eyes tightly, feeling the sting as tears tried to form once again. He would not succumb to his own urges, subconscious or otherwise. He would not show that vulnerability. There was only one way to prove himself. He had to find himself again, not spiritually…but mentally. He would have to once again harness the demons that made him the Goliath. Looking to the sink, Hessian’s eyes widened and moving over to it began to empty his pockets of all the money on his person. As the small heap of cash lay before him, Hessian reached into his pocket and pulled out a lighter. Taking one of the notes, the Global Champion lit a flame under the paper, watching as it began to burn. Dropping it in the pile of money Hessian watched as the money began to smoulder and burn, turning to ashes in so little time. Yet as Hessian watched the last of the notes burn into nothing, he felt nothing change inside. His lip quivering, Hessian sighed and ran a hand through the short crop of hair on his head. Looking back to the desk Hessian saw the mirror next to the Global Title, and a marker pen lying in an in-tray next to that. Taking a seat, Hessian grabbed the marker pen and tested the felt tip on the glass. Surely enough it left a black mark, and with that Hessian began to sketch something onto the mirror. His mind was working a mile a minute, and the stress was showing in his eyes. Glancing at the Global Title, the Mercenary of Satan sneered and continued sketching. Finally he capped the pen and tossed it back into the in-tray, examining the fine piece of work etched onto the glass………a pentagram with all the occult trimmings. Scanning the room, Hessian spied his next necessity in the in-tray once more, a safety pin. Producing the needle, the Global Champion Showed the extent of his desperation…pricking his finger and allowing the blood to flow to the surface. Tossing the safety pin aside Hessian squeezed his finger until an adequate drop of blood dripped into the middle of the pentagram. Sucking the excess from his fingertip, Hessian held an open hand over the pentagram and closed his eyes, praying in silence before taking a deep breath and calming down. He felt his heartbeat slowing down, and maintaining a slowly and easy rhythm in his breath, opened his mouth and in a droning chant. “Om vitriu qhui hjing ptuih…lliuht dhiru nyuryi pas-…RAARGH! FUCK IT!!” Hessian roared out, pulling his open hand up into a fist and slamming it down hard onto the mirror, breaking the glass and leaving a rather ugly crack on the wooden grain below. Checking his hand was free of any cuts, the Global Champion examined the broken glass, seeing his disfigured face in each of the shards and sighing, almost whimpering. Gazing over the broken glass, Hessian picked up a piece and held out his wrist. It took little more than a couple of seconds for him to drop the glass and jump to his feet. “WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH ME?!” He yelled, careless of who might hear then pausing for a moment to repress a guttural bellow. “This is beyond fucking belief. I’m behaving like a fucking wannabe…” he said in a quieter, more sombre tone. Looking to the doorway now in disappointment, Hessian knew he didn’t have long until he would have to face the press again. In his current state how could he do such a thing when surely he would be ripped apart by the media and left vulnerable for his match? There was only one solution left he could think of…and standing by the door, the Global Champion slowly opened it, peering out into the busy hallway and passing a glimpse over the people moving to and fro and going about their daily business. And then he saw him. Walking along the hallway with a cocky grin on his face, none other than Digital Mortality made his way towards Hessian! The PTC Global Champion was awestruck to see one of his opponents in the same conference hall he had made sure to keep him away from. Instinct kicked in immediately, and as soon as he was close enough…Hessian reached out and grabbed hold of the UWF wrestler, dragging him into the room and slamming the door shut. Now was the proving time. This was the moment he would define himself. “Well, well, well. A surprise to see you here considering you should have been…how should I say…diverted?” Hessian said softly, with a sinister hiss in his voice, while Digital Mortality looked shocked. Then he remembered his talk with Minion earlier and grinned. “Well, if it isn't the great and powerful Hessian. Did you think your cheap trick would be enough for you to take the limelight? Remember how many people have lost this belt in their first defence.” He said confidently. “My cheap trick worked...it appears as though someone spilled the beans so to speak...no matter. I'm going to ensure I defend the Global Title first time...and I do believe victory will be ensured after the actions that are about to transpire here.” Hessian growled, his eyes flashing a dark sinister red. The first time they had done so since he tore the heart from his bastard father Clyde. “What? Are you going to kill me right here? Right now? What could you do that has not already been done to me? Besides, I have some information that could come in useful when we are in the ring. Or is it that you are so afraid to face me in the ring that you have to resort to beating people up before the real fight. I don't think you understand who you are messing with.” Digital Mortality said with an eerie air of confidence that caught Hessian off guard. “Hah. Yes Hessian, I know about your little heart problems. I know about your little broken nose, I just hope that you don't need your medication in the middle of the match, because it would be a pleasure to beat you in your prime.” Hessian froze. His eyes widened in a look of combined rage and shock. “...How the...you...what in the-...how the fuck do you know about that?” Hessian said, getting face-to-face with Digital Mortality and showing a scary amount of rage in his face. “Well champ…” Digital Mortality began, only to be interrupted as the door opened and in popped the head of what would turn out to be a very unpopular Minion the Imp! “Oh hey guys, thought I’d find you in here. Hey Hessian hope you don’t mind, Digital Mortality kinda stumbled in when I was telling myself about all your little foibles.” Hessian let go of Digi and stared at Minion, his face now a bright red beacon of unadulterated anger. “Ah-uh-uh, don’t make a move just yet. I should just tell you before I get my ass out of here that I didn’t do anything to weaken your chances without getting a little equalizer first to…y’know…make the match more interesting.” Minion said, glancing at Digital Mortality to see a look of shock on his face. “Yeah, turns out when I was talking to Digi he’s prone to revealing the fact his left arm is just waiting to be snapped thanks to a shitload of injuries. Oh, and his right leg? You might wanna focus on that…turns out it broke and if you smack it hard enough you’ll disable the dumbfuck. CHECK YA LATER BOYS!” And with that Minion the Imp left the room as both Hessian and Digital Mortality lunged at it screaming ‘YOU LITTLE BASTARD!’ yet missing him by an inch. Looking unnerved and in disbelief, Hessian sank back into his chair and stared at the Global Title while Digital Mortality left, in the same amount of shock. Not another word was spoken, only a confused glance shared between the two before Hessian found himself alone once again. What had Minion just done? His long-time friend and manager had sent him up the proverbial shit creek without a paddle. His deepest, darkest secret had just been unearthed to who was a complete stranger to him, before and after the defence. The Global Champion had just made the transition from the top of the food chain to the very bottom…unless he capitalized on Digital Mortality’s weaknesses first…and prevented him spreading the word of Hessian’s vulnerability. In trying to find his true self once more, Hessian had been thrown a very unwelcome surprise. He had tried to channel his inner-demons via the pentagram yet gave it up almost instantly due to its ridiculousness. Self-harm was completely out of the question…and burning the money didn’t work at all. Though, surely destroying that which he loved most was the solution to his problems? It became very clear to Hessian that the money he had burned was worth nothing compared to the amounts that would keep piling in. Disposing of a small handful of notes meant nothing when his income was barrelling in so often. Then it clicked. Staring at his precious Global Title now it all became clear to Hessian. As the dust settled from this confrontation the Mercenary of Satan understood it all. It was impossible to become the monster he once was. It was stupid to think he could. The PTC Global Title…all it stood for…was far greater than simply being a Goliath. He would only ever have once chance at being Champion…and he would not waste it for anything. This type of glory came once in a lifetime, even a rematch wasn’t guaranteed to bring it back to him. This moment, this past month…the glory was too much. No matter how hard he tried he could not be the Titanic Satanic Insaniac he was before when the temptation of the title was so strong. Only if he lost the title could he redeem himself and only then could he become the monster feeding on the raging hellfire that had been extinguished…or had it? Hessian had to have the title. He needed it, and no one could take it away from him. It was the sole material possession of his that made the loss of his Dark Gimmick seem so far away. It was the Global Title alone that made Minion’s abandonment seem like nothing out of the ordinary. Hessian had to have it, for it filled the empty place inside. The PTC Global Title was his making…it would ultimately be his undoing. Looking at the Global Title one more time, Hessian sighed forlornly…and then looked to the broken glass on the table before him. He stared longingly into those soulless eyes that gazed at him. He stared back at a man he no longer knew. Walking back to the press conference, the Global Champion was a torn man. He took his place before the representatives and the media…then stared at the title and smiled, just a little. Somewhere inside he still hurt a little; but somewhere inside he knew what he had to do.
