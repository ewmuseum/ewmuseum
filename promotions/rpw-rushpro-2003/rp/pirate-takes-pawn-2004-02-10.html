Pirate Takes Pawn

Date: Timeless All the lights were extinguished, the curtains were drawn, It was five in the morning, but no birds were in song. They had fled long ago from such losers as this, In search of real men, those who stood while they pissed. Although none rightly knew and fewer rightly cared, Tossing in his ring-bed, the perfect one was scared. In his dreams he suplexed Lucifer and the Lord, In-ring, in reality, he left the fans bored. This waking nightmare was new, ere he thought he'd rocked, But having messed with the pirate, now he'd be mocked. Cold sweat drenched his body as it did in the ring, Pasty goo (from his hair?) dribbled down his drab chin. Vainly he clamped shut his eyes to block out the vision, But it invaded his thoughts like Hessian did Minion. Wildly he wriggled wondering what was amiss, And he yawned with a mouth that had never been kissed. Simple pleasures di'n't matter to one such as he, For technic'ly he was sound . . . as Chrystal's bum knee. Jerked erect on the canvas that served as his bed, He caught sight of Chryste's panties and threw back his head. Shame welled deep inside him and he started to choke, But he beat it down earnestly, stroke by slow stroke. With the hands of a master he caressed his pride: I'm a virgin by choice; I have nothing to hide. But still, as would happen, when the tool was alone, He wept, prayed and begged God for a woman to bone. The known emptiness at heart now rife with his rage, He fell on proven tactics to shatter this cage. 'Upside-down, two-fisted, thumb-pressed-against-finger,' He recited by rote to soothe his demeanour In back-lettered order vast positions he knew For grand masturbation, the sole 'lease of his spew. He bragged no man alive wanked on level with him, For he'd spent his life practicing, summ'ning the djinn. No woman could attest to the strength of his horn, But he came with a thunder -- he was The Vaseline Storm.
