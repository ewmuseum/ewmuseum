Guilty Until Proven Innocent

4 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/04 Some people find it amusing when the blame falls on them for something they wanted nothing to do with. They laugh it off until it comes back to bite them in the ass and they suffer the consequences that rightfully shouldn’t be theirs to bare with. That’s why I tried to do. Even if I knew the consequences that would occur if I didn’t stop Chris Storm from winning that Mid South Belt. I knew I’d be at blame for not giving it one hundred percent in some people eyes….and well in others I was still to blame, only because I was once associated with Chris Storm and I didn’t keep him in line in some peoples eyes. No matter what, the blame all fell on me even if I couldn’t take anymore of the pain so I had to give up and let the three count happen, and none of the blame fell on him for being relentless. And when he gloated to others about how great he was….it wasn’t his fault that the fans didn’t like it, it was my fault. Even if I didn’t agree with one word mentioned in his rant, and even if I did what I could to keep him off TV. But why do things have to be this way? Why do people have to be so narrow minded as to having to place the blame on the first person they can find that is associated with the person who the guilt should belong to? Why should I be punished for doing what I can to prevent an issue? Why should all the threats and the questions fall on me? I wish I knew, I really do. I wish I knew how to show that the blame shouldn’t fall on me for Chris spewing off how great of a Mid South Champion he is. I wish I knew how to stop him from letting his ego get the best of him. And most of all I wish I knew how to stop this from hurting my career. What’s next? Losing my friends due to an issue that shouldn’t be an issue to me at all? I wish there was someway to stop it from happening, but the only way of doing that is going back in time and erasing it all and well that really isn’t a choice at all, is it?
