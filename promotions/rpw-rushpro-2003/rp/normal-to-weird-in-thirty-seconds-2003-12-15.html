Normal to Weird in Thirty Seconds

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/03 Brianna Washington sat on the cold weathered bench located a few feet down from a convenience store where people were frequently shuffling in and out, getting their lottery tickets, cigarettes, and other on the go items. She pulled her beige winter jacket up over her face as a mean wind picked up sending chills all over. The winter weather was beginning to pick up and the sky threatened snow….but the weatherman had said no flurries would occur until Friday so Brianna was happy. “God if he doesn’t hurry up with that car….” Brianna mutters to herself thinking of the long time she’s spent on the bench freezing her ass of waiting for Milo to drive by with HER car. “Maybe one of these days I will learn not to succumb to his little begging fit. God sometimes it feels like I’m his mother. No Milo don’t do that, here let me help you out, and all that other stuff.” she says, grabbing her stomach and wincing as her monthly joy attacks her body causing her to cramp. A few seconds of quietness pass and a weird homeless looking man comes and plops himself down right beside her, staring at her from the corner of his eye trying to remain inconspicuous. Brianna notices but doesn’t feel like yelling at him, half afraid that it will cause a headache, and half afraid that he’ll take the rusty bottle of whiskey in his right hand and hit her with it. “Some colds weathers we’s gots here, wouldn’t you say missy?” Brianna turns to respond, being the courteous person she is and is instantly thrown off her train of thought as he flashes her a toothless smile, his craggy gums sending chills down her spine. She has to turn her head just to prevent herself from throwing up. “Yeah it’s a cold one.” she replies trying to hold the feelings of a lurching stomach down. Awkward silence befalls them and the old man fumbles in his jacket pulling out a raggedy pair of gloves, which only adds to his grunginess. He proceeds to put them on his hands, despite the fact that the tips are missing leaving his fingers dangling in the cold. “This Christmas season is always greats isn’t it? I means peoples is nice and everything just seems more calm and friendly.” “That’s one of my favorite parts about it. People coming together that you’d never imagine…it’s a shame it doesn’t work like that all year round.” Brianna replies, starting to somewhat enjoy the conversation with the man, having completely forgotten about having to wait for Milo. “What really tops the cake is alls those great songs. Jingle Bells, O Holy Night…and uh….umm….” his face goes into a serious thinking pose as he tries to remember others, but nothing comes to mind. “Well you knows what I means.” Brianna shakes her head in agreeance as he fumbles with the whiskey bottle yet again, this time take a big drag from it, clearing out the liquid from inside. He lets out a small satisfying burp and rubs the stray droplets from his mouth setting the bottle aside. “So why are you sitting out heres?” “I’m waiting on my ride.” “I’s surprised no guys has come to offer you a ride. You know I have a cart parked over there, I wouldn’t minds givin you a lift.” At first Brianna goes to laugh, until she realizes he ‘s actually serious. He gives her a failed attempt at a wink as he moves closer to Brianna forcing her to jump from the bench to create some much needed space. Just as she does a black Saturn pulls up and stops right in front of her. Quickly Brianna races to the door and jumps inside. “I know I’m a bit late, but why’d you jump in the car so quickly? You that cold?” he asks as he checks his hair in the mirror, fearing that a strand had fallen out of place. “You see that guy over there?” “You mean the one winking at you?” Brianna looks over and sure enough the man is still trying to pass off his winks. She turns quickly away fearing that staring too long would give him the idea that she’s interested. “Well I’ve spent the last ten minutes with him and….I’ll yell at you later.” At a loss for words she shakes her head furiously as Milo starts laughing hysterically, finding all of this very amusing. “Say goodbye to your boyfriend.” Milo jokes as he starts to pull off. Without responding Brianna reaches over and gives him a good solid punch in the arm which causes him to whimper. The Scene Fades…..
