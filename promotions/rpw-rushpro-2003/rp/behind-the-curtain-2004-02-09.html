Behind The Curtain

9 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-02-04 It’s the buzz you get standing there behind that curtain. It’s the buzz every wrestler gets knowing he’s about to face the world and show it what he’s got. The pressure mounts and keeps mounting every second as you prepare yourself for the challenge ahead. In the case of many they have to go over and over in their mind the moves, the risks, the performance…they know they have to reach that standard set by others before them, by the greats of this sport. Each and every one of them want to see their names nailed to the pages of history and be remembered as being not just a wrestler…but being the best wrestler. The world is watching and judging, demanding to be entertained, and it’s up to that wrestler to put on the best friggin’ show that they can. It’s the weight of the world on their shoulders that causes the buzz, and that’s exactly what they crave. It’s the same for a lot of people in the business. The wrestlers have the most pressure put on them because they’re what the people came to see…but the owners, at least the ones that care, the backstage crew, the announce team…every damn one of them get the buzz knowing the world is cheering on their work and their product. The desire for greatness pushes them all to their limits, everyone working as a single unit, giving their all to give the people what they want. That is wrestling and that is what the buzz is. That rush of adrenaline as they try to land the moonsault perfectly across the torso of their opponent, while also perfecting the arc at the peak of the move. It’s the electricity flowing through their veins as the technicians execute the pyrotechnics perfectly in time with the wrestler’s entrance moves. It’s the shaking nerves covered by the loud confidence as the announce team try to keep up with the fast-paced chain wrestling before them. The wrestling business is one big rush. Success is on everyone’s mind and their effort in the ring shows their determination to make it. Everything they ever do building up to that single moment when they stand behind the curtain as I do now. Their palms moist with sweat, their voices shaking ever so slightly as they go over the match in their head, and their stomachs churning vigorously as their nerves increase. Everyone feels that rush and this Imp is feeling it right now…but not because I’m going out there to face the world…fuck that, I had the hottest most spiciest muthafuckin’ curry for dinner last night and this little Imp is burstin’ for a shit!
