Face it

18 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 18th 2004 So, I was led from the Yamamura Inn like the child I was supposed to be. The warm smell behind me as I stepped over the threshold into the open air was still overpowering. The light around me was near-blinding. I’d relied on my favourite red sunglasses to protect myself from the sun’s rays, seeing it is only the eye that takes punishment from ultraviolet. But I didn’t have them, all I could so was look at the ground with eyes sewn shut for fear of the brilliance around me. Helplessly, I wandered, guided by his hand taking mine. “This way…” I heard those words over and over again. I had no clue where I was. Were there people around me, staring at me? At us? A gray-skinned, white-haired, six feet and seven inch tall thing being led by the hand like an unruly son by an even taller, even more muscular man to whatever spot he desired. And if they were, would I even want to open my eyes? I’d see all those faces staring at me in shock, or mocking me. It didn’t matter so much in the ring, the audience were usually in darkness and I wasn’t really concentrating on them. I could smell the forest… and more blood…we must be near the spot I usually fed. Could Hessian smell blood as well as I could? Or had I left tell-tale stains over the leaves and trees? Had I unwittingly left a signature to be found? Maybe, somewhere in the back of my mind, I had done it subconsciously. Maybe I wanted to be found. Maybe I knew that sooner or later, the loneliness would have eaten at me. Either way, I had been found. Someone had a plan for me. “Open your eyes, son…” Hessian spoke, releasing my hand. He couldn’t tell if I had or not. Nobody, NOBODY would see my face again. But my eyes were certainly open. He’d taken me to a thicker area of the forest, where the branches blocked out a lot of the light. The cool shade was exactly what I needed. Hessian was not alone, either. His ever-near companion, Minion The Imp, was by his side, giving me a look that was full of irony… he stared at me with confusion, trying to work out what I was. Considering he was a small purple pygmy with an overly large… libido, he was nothing to be taken with a fleeting glance either. “What the fuck is up with your head?” he squeaked, picking up a dead branch and poking me in the chest with it. “Last time I saw you, you were some anorexic piece of pale shit!” “In order for me to be accepted, I made my body a little more… human. At a price.” I began with a heavy heart. “Aside from my gray skin, my body has become almost human again. But my face… my face shall always remain behind this mask of hair.” “Aw, fuck off and let me see!” He demanded, trying to part my hair and peek underneath. I don’t know how much he saw, but from the look on his face as I bit the twig in half, I had the feeling he’d seen enough of my face to never look beneath it again. I even saw a look of unease in the eyes of Hessian as I turned to him, covering up my teeth with my snowy locks. “I’d advise your friend not to do that again, Father. Those who have seen this face are now digested. But tell me… why have you brought me here?” He didn’t answer. Hessian was abnormally quiet. Even Minion was relatively silent. Hessian was loud, he roared when he spoke. Minion would never shut up. At least, not until the ride… ~ Driving to UNC-Charlotte… ~ Minion had never looked more ridiculous. Seated on a stack of Yellow Pages and wearing platform shoes that would make the Spice Girls and Gary Glitter cry, he steered the rented car along the road…barely. Hessian and I sat in the back, succumbing to the swaying and bending like grass in a hurricane. Neither of us particularly wanted to complain to Minion – he was doing bad enough with full concentration. “You still haven’t said why you found me, Father. You mentioned we would spill blood like water, that we would slice flesh and crush bones to powder… but not who.” With a sigh, Hessian kept his eyes forwards. “MY Father.” He growled. The deduction came slowly but as Sherlock Holmes once said… “When you have eliminated the impossible, whatever remains, however improbable, must be the truth.” So, to eliminate the impossible. Hessian’s father would be long dead. Hessian was the sole descendant of the Von Kelsig family and it looked like it would end there. The only other man I could think of made sense yet still seemed a long shot. Angelus Von Kelsig was also the bastard son of… “CLYDE?!” I yelled, almost exploding with excitement and shock at once. “You can’t be serious… he’s not a warrior anymore. He received peace. The torture ended for him, he doesn’t have the desires anymore.” “AND THAT IS PRECISELY WHY HE SHOULD DIE!” bellowed Hessian, causing Minion to swerve the car violently. “HE HAS THE POWER TO WIPE THE ENTIRE WORLD OUT AND HE’D RATHER SIP PINA COLADAS WITH HIS LITTLE INFANT!” More questions and answers. More questions than answers. Was Hessian jealous of Clyde’s unused potential? Was there something at risk for Hessian? And why did he call me when surely he was powerful enough to take Clyde on by himself? My questions… and yours… will be answered in due time.
