Date: 5/28/4 I took a drag off my cigarette and flicked it away. Yup, still dead. I'm Jack Noir and this is my head. A bit dusty, a little shopworn, and don't mind all the Sports Illustrated swimsuit pinups on the walls... just a super spectacular guy with one hell of a lousy Public Relations department. What was I doing there passing time with a dead... if you're feeling generous about descriptions... man? Well, it wouldn't be too far from the mark if I called him the third best company in the old flea bitten mansion. Second-best was kinda busy at the moment fixing the electrical system and I knew better than to get between her and a screwdriver. First best? Well, I DO talk to myself a lot... I was mildly impressed at how little the flashlight beam shook as I took in the details. Walls? Check. Floor? Check. Ceiling? Check. One body, half-eaten and liberally smeared across previous entries? Check. Strange how naturally the description stopped there. Really, once you cover those base points, what more do you really need to know? The pattern on the drapes? For those morbid types that do want to know, it was the same pattern as everywhere else... speckled red with weird wriggle tracks. Poor loser. Whatever snacked on him obviously wasn't in a rush. Some of the bloodstains were completely dried and crusty, while others had had barely enough time to coagulate. Which meant that if I wasn't careful, I might trip over it as it slept off the hundred pound pot roast it had powered down. Wouldn't that be embarrassing? SNICK! The safety catch on my Beretta sounded unnaturally loud in the oppressively dark room. 'Hey! I found something!' Which meant that the staticky squeal from my radio was enough to make me squeak several octaves higher than my vocal cords are normally capable of. Backing carefully into the hall, I fumbled for the walkie-talkie, almost dropping the flashlight in the process. “Be quiet!” I hissed. “What?” “Be. Fucking. Quiet.” “What? I can’t hear you! Speak up!” I clicked off the radio. Well, if there was still something in there, it knew about me now. Did I mention that the darkness was oppressive? Because it was. Almost smothering. If this was a movie, there’d be scratchy violins playing. I tried not to remember what usually happens to characters that separate early from the main group in horror flicks. There was this faint, slithery-scratch kind of sound at the edges of my hearing. Like a damp mop being dragged across a hardwood floor… with tentacles… and sharp teeth… and an appetite. Isn’t it great that I don’t have much of an imagination? A lesser man might admit to feeling creeped out right about now. But not ol’ Jack, steady as a rock. Yeah, right. In an earthquake, maybe. I hunkered down behind an overstuffed chair and trained the beam of the flashlight… and my gun… at the gaping doorway. Anything that came out of there, I don’t care if it was a fricking bunny rabbit, would be sucking lead first, and enjoying a leisurely autopsy second. The scritching noise stopped. Some might say just outside the range of my light, but not me. Nope, just a rat… with forty seven eyes… and huge claws… At that moment, every light in the house kicked on at once. There was this gibbering squeal and the scratching noise went into overdrive like it was scrambling away. I blinked my smarting eyes and darted to the doorframe, determined to add a few more picturesque patterns to the room’s décor. I aimed at… Nothing? Well, not actually nothing. The mess from before was still there, but nothing in addition to said mess. What’d it do? Ooze through a wall? I flicked the radio back on. “… and there were these neat geometric designs like all over the room! And big, bubbly glass jars with colored stuff going through them! It’s like a cheap horror movie set in here! Coolness!” Ugh, my idiot employer was STILL babbling.
