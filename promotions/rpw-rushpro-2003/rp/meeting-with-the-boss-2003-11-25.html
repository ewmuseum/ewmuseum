Meeting With The Boss

25 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/25/03 Hip Hop… it's more than just music. It's a culture. A culture, that no matter what some people may say or think, transcends every country, color, creed and race in the world. From Hong Kong to San Juan. From Stockholm to Sydney, Hip Hop has taken over. It is the voice of the youth, the voice of a youth that wants to be heard, but no one wants to listen to. If you over 25, Hip Hop ain't for you, go listen to some rock n roll, go read a book, because you're past it… To say Duzza likes hip hop, is an understatement of gigantic proportions. Duzza doesn't like hip hop, he lives and would probably die for it too… Duzza is hip hop. The picture changes to Duzza, sitting in a office building lobby, with headphones on, blaring out a Lloyd Banks mixtape. Duzza is dressed in a black leather Avirex jacket, baggy black jeans, tan Timberlands and a matching t-shirt. Long gone are the cornrows that everybody and their mother was rocking last summer, now Duzza has a fade, with a black Yankee's fitted cap covering his head. A receptionist calls out to Duzza Receptionist: "Mr Williams, Mr Mason is ready to see you now… Mr Williams… MR WILLIAMS" After hearing her shouting, Duzza quickly looks over and switches of his ipod. Duzza: "Sorry… did you say something?" Receptionist: "…yes… Mr Mason is ready to see you now" Duzza: "Oh dope, ‘bout time too… got me out here in the lobby waiting for his ass, like he don't know me…" Duzza walks into Rush's, plush office, where Rush is sitting behind a desk, covered with documents. The office is what you'd expect, more from a well-paid CEO of a company, than the owner of a up and coming wrestling organization. There is a big window, behind Rush, and paintings on the walls, even a liquor cabinet, incase he ever feels the need to get drunk at work. Rush looks up at Duzza and sighs to himself, before speaking. Rush: "Ahh… Duzza… It's been a while" Duzza: "yo what up baby… yeah yeah, true, but that's life ya know" Rush: "Indeed… so, you know why we're here right?" Duzza: "Yeah man, I did get ya message, we gonna do a lil, official contract signing…" Rush: "Good good… so Duzz, where's the other degenerate at nowadays?" Duzza: "… the other what?" Rush: "Degenerate… it means… oh forget it… Statuz Quo, where is Quo?" Duzza: "Ohhh… well ya coulda just said Quo, dunno why you gotta act all clever and whatnot, I mean-" Rush: "Just answer the question Duzz… please" "Duzza: "Oh right, my bad… I dunno where he's at man, last time I saw him, he was on his way to Atlanta…" Rush: "Ok… well… look… since we go back, and I know that you don't really have any job opportunities going for you, I decided to offer you a job" Duzza: "wow… way to make a brother think even less of himself" Rush: "Anytime my friend" Duzza: "You like dissin' me don't ya?" Rush: "Well… when we used to hang out, it was my sole purpose for living… so yes, I'd say so" With that, Rush reaches out from a draw in his desk and passes a contract and a pen to Duzza Rush: "Just sign here Duzz, and you're officially part of RPW… even thought you have been hanging around since day one" Duzza quickly looks over the contract while Rush fixes himself a drink from his cabinet Duzza: "… wait a second… this is a janitors contract!" Rush: "wait… you can read?!" Duzza: "Hell yes I can read… I can't believe you would try to play me like that!" Rush: "haha…heh… hmm…err… it was a mistake, you know how it goes, here's the proper contract… that was a test…and you passed…well done" Duzza: "…bastard" Duzza then looks over the contract, making sure it's not a ring boy's contract or something and signs it and gives it back to Rush Rush: "Well Duzz, it's…great… to have you on RPW…" Duzza: "Thanks boss" Rush: "Now Duzza… now you're a RPW employee, you need to act more responsibly than you used to, we have a reputation to uphold… that means, no more shootings in clubs, no more fights with the KKK, no more breaking into toy stores or muggings of old ladies… you got that?" Duzza: "…awww man" Rush: "I said, you got that?" Duzza: "…yes…" With that, Duzza gets up to leave and starts to walk out of his office, when Rush calls out to him Rush: "Oh Duzza… I saw you at the show the other week, having a conversation with Murdoch" Duzza: "the Irish dude?" "Rush: "Scottish..but yeah him… Duzza, seriously, don't get into anything with him… he could kill you with his bare hands man…" Duzza: "…whatever" And with that, Duzza puts his headphones back on, and walks out the door, now an official member of RPW.
