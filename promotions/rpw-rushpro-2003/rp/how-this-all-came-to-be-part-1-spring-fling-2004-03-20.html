How This All Came To Be: Part 1 'Spring Fling'

20 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/04 As of late I had done a lot of thinking, thinking about the past and how it led up to what it was doing to me now, and what it would do to me in the future. The thinking all stretched back to one night in particular though, the night of April 20th, 2002 where I not only lost a belt that fit firmly around my waist, I lost a partner in both the ring and life. It was this evening which forever haunted my mind in constant repeat mode. The setting, the emotions, and each word all exactly the same as they were that day. My groggily blue eyes struggled to open as I looked up to see the familiar face of my best friend Jackson Rust. I’m really not sure what happened before, other than a brawl that I had outside of the ring with the man whom I accidentally married. It was obvious from the position that I was in that I lost the brawl considering a winner wouldn’t be handcuffed to the ring post as clueless as I was to the moments. The answer to all the questions running though my head at the moment were quickly answered as the big screen lit up with the image of a bloodied and beaten Chris Storm being drug down the halls by the tag title bearing members of the now five time PCW Tag Champion team, P.W.A.. The image brought tears to my eyes as I could do is sit and watch while Jacko did what he could to free me from the shackles that cost Chris and I the Tag Titles that we had given our all to get. “You okay Tessa?” Jacko questioned repeatedly as he helped me to my feet, but the question went in one ear and went out the other. I wasn’t worried about myself; I was worried about Chris and what would happen to him. The odds were two against one, there would be no chance he’d survive without me, yet there was nothing I could do but watch and hope that he’d make it out okay. Each snicker that fell from the mouths of Nerezza and Gaz caused another piece of my heart to crumble. Each step they took made me wonder if I’d ever wrestle with Chris by my side again. It seemed like the longest minute of my life, until the clock ultimately ticked down to the last few seconds. The seconds that brought the sight of Chris down on his knee’s before a brick wall as his blood trickled down his cheeks and stained the concrete below him. Things only got worse as Nerezza clamped his black glove shut, capturing Chris’ golden locks within his grasp and forcing him back up to his feet. I trembled as the two members of P.W.A. exchanged a few words before a moment of silence, a moment that caused a chill to run up my spine as I waited. I knew what was about to happen, it was something the two were all too infamous for doing. So I gulped and silently hoped that something would go wrong, something would stop this from happening. But as much as I tried to block out the horrific sight that laid before my mind, the internal clock inside of my head wouldn’t let me look away as it ticked down the time. Three… Two… One… CRACK! I turned my head away from the screen to soften the blow my heart was about to take, despite knowing all that just happened. I knew it was too late, I’d never live this down for as long as I lived. I let Chris down, I let myself down, I let “The Schmoopies” down. Things could only go downhill from here.
