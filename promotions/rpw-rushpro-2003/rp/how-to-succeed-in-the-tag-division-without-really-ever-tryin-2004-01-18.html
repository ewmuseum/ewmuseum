How To Succeed In The Tag Division....Without Really Ever Trying.

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/04 Men Most girls would say who needs them. The answer I would provide to them is me. What most girls fail to realize is just how great they are. All you have to do is wrap them around your finger and you can get every little thing you could ever hope for. It’s a nearly flawless plan. Should they realize what you’re up to all you have to do is find another guy and perform the act all over again. Call it whorish if you must, but if it fulfils your needs than what is there to complain about? I suppose most would point out that in my three months of being in RPW all I’ve managed to win is one match. What they leave out though is that the match that I won was the most important of all. Just look at what I’ve got around my waist now and tell me all those losses wasn’t worth it. I dare you to. Go on and criticize me for using what god gave me to win that title. So I cheated a bit? Who cares? Right now I’m willing to bet that you wish you were in the same shoes….or should I say pants as me. I can’t blame you for that. But right now the extra space in my pants is reserved for someone special. Who you may ask? Well it really shouldn’t be that hard of a guess. When you’ve got a belt to defend you’re obviously going to try and weaken the enemy. For us the enemy is the tag team closest to getting out titles. The tag team consisting of Gabe and his much more sane partner Scott. So what makes me think Scott would fall for me? The explanation is a lot less complicated than you think. You see as tough as Scott tries to act I know that he can be broken down into nothing. I’ve dealt with guys like him before and they are a lot softer than you could ever imagine. It’s as simple as getting it his head with some foul play. I mess around with Gabe, Scott gets jealous and then BOOM! No more friendship, and no more threats to our title. It may take a little dedication to a pack of fools, but it will be well worth it in the end, very well worth it. And trust me it will happen. No one can resists a piece of eye candy as mouth watering as this, and no one will. Scott will fall for me, even if I have to resort to drastic measures to make him do so.
