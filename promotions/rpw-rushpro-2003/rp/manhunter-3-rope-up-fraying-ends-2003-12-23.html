Manhunter - 3 - Rope Up Fraying Ends

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 23rd, 2003 ''I've got a better idea...'' Kat Dimoni never was one for thinking things through, especially not when it came to men she could gain something from - but this one was persistant enough to sober her. ''Why...'' She was trying to pose a question - moreso a command in the form of a question - while struggling to squirm away from Tristan Belmont's embrace. ''...don't we have a conversation? You know...'Hey! I just slept with you about sixty times over, what's your name?' type discussion. I'm sure you're well-versed in that sort of thing.'' Belmont quickly caught on and smiled proudly as he stepped back, arms folded - he'd worn her out! As he rubbed his pinch, he nodded his head, acquiescing to the question that was put forth by the raven-haired beauty that had drained him of all his energies. He scanned the room with his eyes, pointing to an empty bottle-clad table, indicating that such place would be the conversation location. Both he and Dimoni laughed, remembering that a few hours ago they were at the same location, however talking hadn't been the subject at hand. Both walked to the table, and after having pulled Kat's chair and seated her well into it, Belmont took a seat at the opposite direction of her and peered into her eyes. ''What is it that you want to know from me?'', he said as he could not stop himself from looking up and down at the goddess that sat before him. She grinned, knowing full well that's he was being ogled by the man that had shown intensity which she had never felt before. The physical tension in the room was at its highest, however, in order to regain energies and know more of one another, she persisted with the demand for conversation, still having no idea exactly who and how refined mister Belmont was. ''Who are you, exactly?'', the Russian-Italian beauty asked. Chuckles from the man erupted in a well mannered type. ''Why, it seems as if last night I was the same asking such questions and it lead to the bed, shall we repeat history my dear?'' Belmont was confident of his abilities, but the woman across from him was stubborn and had quite some will power when she had something she wanted on the mind - in this case, information. ''I don't like repeating myself.'' Kat was matter-of-fact underneath stilted eyebrows. ''I'm Katarina Dimoni, and the ID I swiped at about 3am says you're Seth Belmont...but what's your intent with me?'' He smiled, displaying his perfectly placed and whitened teeth. The question she had just asked him had stumped him in reality. Before, he would've said nothing, judging her like he judged every one else, maybe as a fan or just another dumb broad but no, not now...not her. He wanted something she had; he wanted her plain and simple. ''After what we just did, I think it is quite obvious what I want you for. You came into my hotel room looking for wine; I guess we both got something out of that venture...wouldn't you agree?'' Belmont looked down at his gold watch for the time, before looking back up at Dimoni and running his left hand through his dark hair. ''As for that taken ID, I will have to demand you give it back.'' He lent his hand forward across the table. The closer his exposed skin got across the table, towards her, the tougher fitting the sexual urge inside of him got. Nevertheless, his martial arts training had given him enough will power and strength to be able to hold himself back. She went to work kissing the tips of his fingers and slowly slipped his wallet between them, chuckling. ''Some people have called me a 'man-eater', mio caro...how can you be so trusting that I won't just rob you and run?'' ''Because you never run from something that gives you what I gave you last night.'' He smiled and she returned it, but with a raised eyebrow. ''You're awfully confident, are you that cocky at work?'' Dimoni smirked, ''I might do best to avoid you there.'' She still had his arm held by the wrist, unconsciously nipping at the end of his pinky and staring straight back into the dark eyes of something unusually close to evil, with evil thoughts of her own brewing with it. ''Alright, another question...'' Her blue stare was nearly blinding, if not in how bright and cold it was then by the intensity behind it. ''After I leave this morning, am I coming back? I might let you make the decision for the moment...'' ''Yes, you'll return. I look you in the eyes and see that through your hard front, what happened is something you will not let go of so soon. It is not only by my will that you shall return but by yours.'' He was confident. Maybe too confident, but that was what Tristan Belmont was all about. He had lived through many and had gone through more to be what he was today. Dimoni was seeing one side to him, the side that no one knew...the rather kind side. ''About avoiding me at work then, you would probably not recognize me. The passion that I display stays here when time comes for my metamorphosis to begin. I am not the man you know so far, but I'm the lover you have experienced.'' Rather an ambiguous answer by Belmont, which left Dimoni wanting more, with many more questions at hand. No one had seen the metamorphosis Belmont was talking about but himself, and that change was from the lusting man to the evil incarnate that he truly was. Katarina only regarded him again with her brow raised. ''You seem to play about control,'' She sighed, almost upset thinking he was so willing to challenge her, ''Arrogant enough, almost, to make me stay away just to have you beg...'' Belmont sneered, looking down at his hand which was trapped in her soft caress. Control was one of the most important aspects of any relationship, whether it be love of lust. In this case, the two craved for the power to feed the overwhelming egos that grew in their heads, and with no side willing to budge, but both sides infatuated with one another, sparks were bound to fly. ''The door was never locked on your way in, and don't let it be on your way out if you choose it.'' She remained as stone in her bench, opting to throw a sly smile as Belmont got up, tired of his sitting stance. He walked around the table, letting the tip of his fingers caress the wooden surface. Finally, he reached the back of Dimoni's chair and pulled it out from under the table, lifting her up along with it. She looked into the man's eyes and saw one thing - unadulterated lust. In one swift movement, Belmont pushed the raven hair beauty off her balance and onto the table, before laying himself over her. He had pinned her there, sending the empty bottles down to the carpeted floor. Belmont went in for a kiss when she did one thing that shot away all attraction - she grabbed him by the jaw and forced it to stay in place while she had her word. ''I'm sure you could use a little rest, so take it.'' Kat frowned, still pinned between Tristan and the cool wooden table-top. But as she struggled to keep her will-power intact, the little Russian managed to speak softly, though her words were harsh and challenging. ''Why are you so afraid of conversation? Worried you might end up liking more than my ass?'' He smiled as he pulled his visage away from her grip, finally getting completely off of her. Still, he was smiling. She had mistaken him for a sex starved fiend who could not utter a single phrase without sounding stupid. Belmont was far from being a brainless jock. ''I only fear your ignorance, love.'' He turned his back and walked back to the bed, where rested the shirt she had ripped off his torso the previous night. Seemingly without any care for his invitee, Belmont put on his black muscle shirt and, without even looking at her, continued the verbal war. ''Sparking up interesting and meaningful conversations would imply that both sides have a brain to function on, and so far, apart from moaning and asking for more, you've not shown any sign of intellect.'' He turned around, a cocky grin drawn upon his lips. ''What's the matter, scared you'll become addicted like a filthy whore is to heroin?'' He'd almost won...he'd come closer than any man had before. Kat sneered, trying not to show the shock and near-defeat behind her eyes. ''Dare to imply any ignorance on my part again and you'll find that, unlike yourself, I've got a hell of a lot more than whore references in my trickbag.'' She wasn't sure whether or not that was a physical threat, but one thing was certain - it got Belmont to turn around...only to find the minute Dimoni glaring up at him, entirely unafraid and not willing to budge an inch. ''Through all this berating, you still stand on your two feet and stay here in my apartment. And I must say, Miss Dimoni...I'm rather happy to find you have a backbone.'' Neither side would budge, but Tristan Belmont being the analyst he was found himself losing more ground to Dimoni on the psychological aspect, becoming more and more fascinated by her. He could not put his finger on it but she brought him calm, she brought him joy. ''As for that little threat, know that from past experiences I have faced many that would have eaten your weight in food, so I'm sorry to say that I cannot take you seriously on that one. Tough luck.'' He winked very cockily, which only inspired more anger in Dimoni. ''Now that we've spoken, is there anything else you would like to know, because I have admit that this shirt is becoming a burden on my back and I'd be most grateful for you to tear it off again.'' ''Or...'' She grinned, slipping a hand up his chest...a lovely diversion before her tone turned ice cold. ''You could take it off on your own and have a rousing round of Hide and go Fuck Yourself.'' He smiled at her, massaging the spot where her hand had connected. Slowly, he nudged his head towards the door and said. ''Well now that you've made your choice don't let me stand between you and the wonderful hallway, love.'' Again, he turned his back to her and walked over to his side of the bed where his dress pants were. He bent over, picked them up and put them on leg-by-leg, before buckling them. ''As for that little game you suggested, no thanks. Now please, dispose of yourself.'' ...She didn't move. Not an inch. ''Are you done spouting commands? A man who prides himself so much on being such a gentlemen should never ask a lady to leave.'' Dimoni grinned, ''Especially not when she's struck such a nerve.'' It was a slight surprise when she stepped over and forcefully kissed him, but at that point neither were sure WHO was winning. It was either Belmont for reverse psychology, or Katarina for some odd form of double reverse pychology. But the sure thing was that Tristan was fairly fed up, even to the point of pushing her away...which only caused her smile to become wider than ever before. ''Angered you enough to be cast away...that is an accomplishment.'' Belmont grinned towards Kat, as he caressed the bottom of her chin. ''It is not anger to dispose of an object that his served its purpose, my dear.'' He winked at her as he turned his back to her yet again and headed towards the bathroom, beginning to hum a little tune. Too much was going through Belmont's mind. He wanted her around, but he wanted her gone. He wanted to love her, and at the same time wanted to hurt her. It had been a while since someone had gotten to anger Belmont in such a manner. He wouldn'tt admit it, but he was not as cold-blooded with this girl as he would've liked to be. Instead of following, Dimoni stepped out of the opposite - the exit - door. She'd return, but wouldn't allow Belmont to know that.
