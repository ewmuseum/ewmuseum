Limo Ride

3 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-3-03 Caldera hadn't felt this good in a long time, and it was showing. Kimbusa, sitting across from him in the limo as is speeds along some anonymous highway towards Caldera's private jet, stares at him with a suspicious glare. Caldera, for his part, is staring out the tinted window with the curiosity of an infant on his first car ride. Caldera's smile reflects back on the window pane, a smile so wide as to prompt Kimbusa to break his usual silence and comment on the situation. "What are you so happy about?" he asks with the tiniest hint of amusement in his voice. Caldera turns away from the window and smiles back at Kimbusa. Caldera, dressed as usual in his finest Italian tailored suit, loosens his tie and turns back to the window. The limo speeds past an Ohio interstate sign, the first clue to Caldera that they are approaching their destination. "This is where I was born, you know. Ohio. Born here, got my start here, everything. This is my home," Caldera says with some sort of giddy joy in his voice that is quite uncharacteristic. "You've also spent the better part of the last year here, so this can't be the source of your… bliss." Kimbusa spits out the last word as if it had been a swig of sour milk, but still seems amused by Caldera's childlike state. Caldera shrugs. "I can't explain it, Kimbusa. Something about where we are right now. I like it. I'm finally going to get back at the man that ruined me. For once, I'm going to take the fight to him in his territory. Makes me feel like I'm twenty years old again, just making my break into the business." Kimbusa cocks his head to the side in curiosity. "I have sought revenge before," he states coldly. "When I am preparing for battle, I do not spend my time prancing about as you do. I calculate, I plan, and then I exact my vengeance in one swift, bloody stroke. The pleasure comes after the victory, not before the battle." Caldera turns back to Kimbusa. There was a time when statements like these chilled Caldera to the bone. But Caldera was so used to Kimbusa's cold-blooded nature by now that he almost found it funny. "Once this is done, my friend, so am I. There will be no time to revel in our victory, because once we're done we're gone. We're going to retire to a beach somewhere in the south pacific and stare at beautiful women and drink beer all day and all night. This is the last hurrah for Steven Caldera. So yeah, I'm going to wring all the pleasure I can out of this moment, because we're preparing for our last stand." Kimbusa, shockingly, actually chuckles at this. "Yes, I suppose. Once this man is destroyed, however, I cannot come with you, of course. I have sworn an oath to help you, but once your career is over, I would very much like to return home." This actually causes some concern in Caldera. Kimbusa had seemingly left his home behind, and now considered America his permanent place of residence. Kimbusa had battled for years now since leaving Japan to quell the demons that followed him. To return home would be to invite danger. "Home? You mean… Japan?" Caldera says, still not believing Kimbusa. The sumo simply nods. Caldera raises an eyebrow. "Of course, I'll send you wherever you want to go. The world is yours. But why would you want to go home?" It is Kimbusa now who turns to stare out of the window. His face grows dark against the tint of the pane, and Caldera rises to attention. Caldera had also learned long ago that Kimbusa is volatile by nature, and while Caldera would not be physically threatened, the cleanliness of his limousine might be. Kimbusa simply closes his eyes. "I'll tell you on the plane."
