Before Today #18 : Not The End, By Far

5 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/04 Karina might not have beaten Magnum Wolf, but she scored a victory over her father, who regretfully submitted to her. Things were never going to be exactly the same again, they never could be. Lawrence would always think Karina resented him for his harsh and over-protective reactions, while Karina had begun to appreciate just what she had, and just how quickly it could be taken away. When she regained consciousness after the match, Kari had looked through blurry eyes to ringside, where the uncharacteristically softened face of Lawrence Wolfenden smiled to her. He'd seen that Karina could take care of herself, he'd seen that his daughter had carved out her own little bit of fame... And he'd heard the fans cheer out of respect, not out of Neanderthal lust. She was a Wolfenden once more, and reclaimed all that came with the name... Even the mundane. As she found ways to amuse herself in the Albany night, Karina Wolfenden was, to put it bluntly... Bored. After the showdown with Magnum, she was given a show off, 'Family Time', which she was grateful for... But she joined PWC for a reason, fun, and 'sitting on the bench' for a week wasn't it. Just a week after she was flying off rails, over ropes, from turnbuckles, from ladders... Karina was left waiting for a tape of PWC TV to wing's it's way to her. But it wouldn't be the same, she wouldn't be part of the action while looking through a TV screen at it. No plasma screen, surround sound, cinema system set-up could even bring the footage close to resembling the first hand experience. The week Karina spent with her family, as if they'd never ever had a fallout since she was born, was a nice little release from what she'd been through over the past 2 months. No having to check prices to the nearest cent, no having to arrange bills in the order of which she'd be least late paying... But just as when she was a child, it just wasn't 'her'. Karina Wolfenden just wasn't a girl born to play happy families. Why does she stay then? As much of a free spirit that she is, it would have been a little rude to fly off in the middle of 'Family Fortnight' to hit the slopes in Austria or wherever, or even take a ride down the east coast for a few days to see what fun she may find. No, for them, her family, she'd stick it out for another week. After all, her mother had been caught in the middle of the Wolfenden family squabble for weeks, she at least deserved this time with her daughter. 'I haven't seen you in years...' Karina said affectionately, wandering out under one of the beacon-style lights that were dotted around the Wolfenden estate. In her hands Karina cradled a skateboard, showing it's years but carrying a classic grace all the same. 'Let's see if you get better with age.'. She glided the board down onto the smooth concrete and tentatively placed her right foot on it, the black rubber of her sneaker's sole sticking to the surface like glue. Almost immediately the feeling came back to her, like riding a bike, and she was off... Nothing special, just riding around the paths that wound their way through the ornamental gardens bordering her family home. In an Ani DiFranco t-shirt over a long sleeve grey top, coupled with baggy black jeans, Kari wasn't dressed like the high society types who last occupied the lawns and paths at the previous night's garden party. 'This'll pass an hour or so,' she sighed. 'But... But it's no hitting a WolfTamer...' was then added in resignation, accepting that it would only be a short and cheap fix. A K-Wolf in captivity was without it's adventurous optimism. With a casual grace, she followed the paths through the chilly twilight, her pink fringed hair being pushed back tight along the flanks of her head. Shifting her weight in the blink of an eye, Karina effortlessly executed a perfect kick-flip, as if she'd been practising every day of her break. For the first time on her ride, a smile broke out across her pink lips, a small bit of pride welling up at scoring the trick with such ease. But it was no Sinful Indy Air, both the ring and slope varieties. Like a heroin addict having to settle for a half-dose of methadone, Karina just wasn't fulfilled. Out of the blue, Kari then busted out a heel-flip, almost as if she was hoping an element of surprise would make it all the more enthralling, even to her. But alas, it was still a poor substitute. What was a casual skate against the sheer adrenaline of opening up the throttle on her Ducati, what was a kick-flip compared to racing through soft, powdery snow at breakneck speed, before watching the ground rotate below her as she soared off a ramp... ...And what was a heel-flip next to tucking her body in close and spinning through the air to hit the Teardrop Explodes 450. Karina stopped to check her watch, but all it did was remind her that it had only been 5 minutes since she'd last checked. However, there was one thing that gave her heart, gave her the drive to get her through the fake comfort she'd have to display... ...In 6 days, she'd be wrestling again.
