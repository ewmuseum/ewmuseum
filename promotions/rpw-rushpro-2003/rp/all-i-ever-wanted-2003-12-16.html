All I Ever Wanted...

16 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sometimes… we think we want one thing… but soon we realize what we truly desire… is the complete opposite. The last time you saw me, I had betrayed my father. Impounded a sledgehammer into his skull… and at the time I quite enjoyed doing that. My ambition for that action? Simple; I wanted him to listen. He had stopped listening to me, his guide, his oracle, his Divine Prophetess, his very own flesh and blood daughter, the one that’s been by his side since the news was declared. His OTHER “daughter” left his side as soon as things went sour, but I remained. Because I wanted my father to fulfill his destiny. But he could not do so… if he did not listen to reason. Instead, he listened to the fans, the humans. He listened to their cheers as he laid waste to their villains, failing to realize that he was only doing their dirty work. He thought he was doing the right thing… but instead the mortals he saw out there only appealed to the mortality inside of him… placed there by the mortal parents who raised him. He never really loved the people for the cheers they gave him… he only appreciated the feeling of nostalgia that came with relating with mortals, reminding him of his own childhood. He listened to his mortality, when he should’ve listened to his godhood. And so… I wanted him dead. Of course, putting a sledgehammer into the Neo Void God’s skull isn’t enough to get the job done… as you see. He came back, and continued to be the people’s pawn… without me. Akiyama had banned me from his castle, my home on Sado Island. He had banned me from being near him, banned me from PCW. And of course I had no problem with it. By that time I was fed up with the inner workings of the wrestling world, and went back to what I was truly in love with… PAIN, the causing, and experiencing of it. I traveled Japan, coming across different people, meeting with different souls and learning their pain, tasting their pain, and causing their pain. But it wasn’t the same… it jut wasn’t the same. In Japan there were two big trends going around the shadowed underworld scene at the time. One was Celestialology, of course started by my father and myself. It was still going… still is today. The other… Miyabi, started by our sworn enemy… Inphino Blitz. Celestialology was a more civilized form of evil, organized, refined, a kingdom of sorts with subjects and ruler. Miyabi, on the other hand, was anarchistic, disorderly, and chaotic. It was like one big rave orgy, and Blitz was the big dick in charge. I couldn’t stand to see such… unorganized chaos. I had ambitions of the same amount of anarchism for Celestialology, but we lacked motivation… ever since my father left the following. Blitz was present in the movement, and had backing from figures in the media in both America and Japan. Celestialology was just Japanese… and then it didn’t have the support Miyabi had. It was looked down on as a cult, where Miyabi became a genre itself, and a lifestyle. But I didn’t feel right trying to deal with Miyabi… or Celestialology… not then… Lucky for us though, Blitz split on the Miyabi. It’s still going… just under new management. And once again… I was alone…so alone… I had a lot of time to think… figure out what I want, what I need, what I desire. The list isn’t that long. All I want is to make people suffer… make people understand the pains of this world. That, is my life goal. But… all I ever wanted, all I ever needed… all I ever desired… was HIM.
