Before Today #16 : Flying As She Falls

5 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/04 Karina Wolfenden should have learned her lesson. There was an instinct inside the young woman that told her to never back down, to always bite back, to give as good as she got. It wasn't a bad attitude to have, and one that had led to many playful exchanges and pranks... But in the PWC, she was treading new ground, stepping into the unknown, the realm of Magnum Wolf. Where as Karina would always find the words to shoot back, or pull out a prank to get even... She had no idea the depths that Magnum Wolf would stoop to in order to say he won. It was a dangerous game, and for the first few weeks it was indeed a game, but the stakes had risen too high, and Karina had lost... ... Big time. The match between them was her chance to get back at the table, with a fresh stack of chips to play with. Crouching on the guard rail, Karina was ready to fly, her body perfectly balanced despite footing that would have most swaying or falling. Behind her, the PWC fans chanted her name, waiting for her next Daredevil spot, while in front of her was a red Ducati 748 Superbike... An antagonistic doppelganger of the bike Karina was forced to sell to make ends meet. But she was ignoring the bike, it was the dazed man slumped over it that Wolfenden's burning emerald eyes had locked on to. This was the match people said was a step too far for the eager rookie, a bout in which she was the under-dog both physically and psychologically. Magnum Wolf had broken her down away from the ring, and this was the match in which he was supposed to do the same in the ring. So far, people were wrong... Surprisingly so. A wronged Karina came out all guns blazing, the ferocity of her kicks as they thumped against Magnum's toned body making even the most hardened fan wince. She'd been emotionally backed into a corner, had all she held dear taken away, and it had only magnified the fight in the timid-looking, plain-faced girl. 'Ughhh,' Magnum gargled, before spitting a mix of phlegm and blood down onto the ramp. 'Why am I sitting on a freagin' bike?' he then thought, not realising that just 10 seconds ago Karina swung her foot harshly onto his jaw, sending him stumbling into, and partially over the bike. There was only 10 feet - maximum - between the guard rails of the short aisle, but the logistics and geography of the venue weren’t going to ground Karina. The bike was about 6 feet away, with Magnum's shaking and heavy head 5 feet above the ramp. The distances entered Karina's brain, instantaneously creating a flight plan... Which was then sent down to her legs. Powerful thighs thrust her from the rail, launching the K-Wolf high into the muggy arena air. Catching a flash of pink out of the corner of his eye, Magnum sat up, exposing his chest for Karina... A chest that may as well have had a target tattooed onto it. Keeping her body vertical in flight, Karina pulled her legs up to her chest, forming a ball, until she flew closer to Magnum, each second being stretched out in flight. With a powerful burst, Karina thrust her legs out to the right, pumping both feet brutally into Magnum Wolf's chest with great delight. 'Guhhh,' Magnum wheezed as the air was forced out of both his lungs, just before the kinetic energy passed on to him by Karina sent him flying backwards off the bike. The sound all around Karina was deafening, the spot having drawn just about everyone to their feet... The symbolism of the K-Wolf smashing Wolf from what should have been her bike not being lost on many. In mid-air, she turned back to see Wolf flying backwards onto the hard ramp, a sight that brought a smile to her magenta lips. But as Wolf was falling in flight, so was Wolfenden, and she needed to realise it quickly. Hurriedly, her head snapped back around, facing front, to see the sight of the opposite guard rail growing larger and larger before her. The sheer speed and arc of her leap meant there was no changing course, and no way to land sooner... It was going to be a crash landing whatever she did, so all the K-Wolf could do was prepare. The whole action from leap to impact hadn't even taken 5 seconds, but for Karina her flight was timeless, she always had ample time to react and hit her moves... But with a painful impact imminent, that only prolonged the thought that the landing wasn't going to be too pretty. Behind the rail, the fans along the aisle had backed away, their reactions also being applaudable. Descending on her rapid approach, Karina pushed her body back, going for a feet-first landing... Or rather, aiming her legs between the wide gaps in the cheap railings, giving her a few extra feet in which to make her landing. Her left foot threaded the needle, the sole of her sneaker squeaking as it slid along the wooden floor... But her right was less lucky. With the angle of her flight, Karina had to contort her right leg to guide it through the railings, and when her sneaker's rubber sole hit the varnished wooden gym floor, the friction stopped it dead... Channelling nearly all of her weight through the twisted knee joint. 'Aghhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh,' came her pained cry as the knee gave it’s own silent scream, the pain taking control of her body away from Karina. The rest of her crashed into the railings and ramp, her left shoulder taking the brunt of the fall on the latter. Those fans who'd watched her right leg still looked sickened by the angle they’d seen it adopt for that agonising fraction of a second, and the referee could tell by their faces... And Karina's... That the match couldn't continue. So much for revenge in the ring. As the bell rang, an agonised Karina flung her body back onto the ramp, holding her head in her hands and wondering if she'd just been the only passenger aboard the final flight of the K-Wolf.
