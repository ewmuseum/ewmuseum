Before Today #10 : Meet The Parent

22 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/01/04 'Your car is ready at the door, Mr. Wolfenden.' Freddy Jarvis loved his job. As a wrestling fan in New Jersey, he was just begging for a promotion to open up on his doorstep... And the 19 year old from Trenton was granted his wish when the Pro Wrestling Circuit opened up. Ron Starr had brought wrestling to his town, and he rushed to be a part of it. Be it ring crew, sound tech-monkey, or even a t-shirt seller, Freddy would be there... ...And there he was, on a day that changed Karina Wolfenden's life. Dressed from head-to-toe in PWC branded gear, Freddy's job was a general backstage dogsbody... Not glamorous, and hardly even a paying job, but when he had nothing to do, he could sneak up onto the balcony and watch the action beside some of the wrestlers. His idea of job satisfaction was fetching Sebastian Toomes his towel, or passing on Angelo Deville's drink order. But that night, he was Lawrence Wolfenden's guide. 'This way, Sir,' the ever polite Freddy nodded as he opened the doors for Karina's father. 'Did you enjoy the show?' Although Lawrence was officially at PWC's TV taping as a potential investor, his visit had little to do with finance-injection or promotional deals... He was there to see where his only daughter was 'working', and although he wasn't convinced at all that he wanted her there, her passionate pleas had temporarily appeased him. 'I didn't get to see much of it,' the stern voiced Wolfenden answered, his tone making it clear he didn't want a conversation with the backstage 'Gopher'. He'd looked at the men and woman backstage all night, and as much as he searched, he hadn't found any that repulsed him... In fact, he'd even found 2 that went to the same University as his own son, Max. But to say he'd seen the whole of PWC's backstage spectrum would be a lie. Karina was careful in who she let him see, so there were no conversations about what position his daughter was conceived in with Sebastian Toomes, and certainly no meeting with Magnum Wolf. The outside doors opened, and Lawrence was greeted by the sight of his silver Mercedes, ready to drive him back to Albany. 'Mr Wolfenden,' suddenly lassoed him back inside, 'I think there's something you should know, it's about Karina.' Watching from across the parking lot, Karina could neither hear nor see who was talking to her father. Sitting astride her crimson Ducati, she wanted to see her father leave, just so she could breathe a sigh of relief to mark the end of a stressful night. As well as having to prepare for - and compete in - her match, she had to arrange the PWC's personnel like Chess pieces to avoid any unwanted encounters. But the most unwanted of them all was occurring, and it was occurring while she was watching. Inside the building, standing only just out of the K-Wolf's line of sight was Magnum Wolf, having smartened himself up for the meeting... If it wasn't for the eccentric hair and bushy moustache, he might have even out-dressed the wealthy Lawrence. 'What about Karina?' Lawrence's steely voice boomed, always treating every strange man as a threat to his beloved Karina. 'Well,' Magnum pondered, doing his best to sound sincere while the Wolfenden patriarch paced towards him, 'I'm not sure how to say this, but... Well... She's been lying to you...' From her distant vantage point, Karina watched her father walk back into the building. 'Where are you going?' she whispered to herself inside her helmet. 'Just get out of there and go home...' Hurriedly, Karina slid her rucksack free and pulled out her cell phone, hitting the 1st speed-dial to bring up 'Dad' on the screen. The ringing increased her heartbeat, but there was no answer... ...Because the phone in Lawrence's jacket's inside pocket had been turned off. 'How do you mean, lying?' the protective parent demanded to know, forcing Wolf to back away and hold his hands up, submissively. 'Hey, I'm a friend here, buddy...' he assured Lawrence, although not even the smallest measure of trust was being shown. 'Truth is, I like your daughter...' Seeing the look on Lawrence's face quickly morph into one of anger, Magz again had to act quickly to keep him at bay. 'No no no no, not like that!' a panicked Magnum told him, 'no need to go all 'Meet the Parents' on me. I just mean she's a good kid. WAY to good this this sleazy dump.' Wolf's words were chosen perfectly, and Lawrence visibly reacted to the implications of sleaze. 'Sleazy?' he asked in demanding fashion, depraved images already entering his mind. 'What do you mean? And don't you dare be cryptic, understand?' Lawrence scared Magnum, but it didn't stop the mischievous wrestler using his silver-tongue to great effect. 'I bet she's told you how this place is some well-ran, professional place where everyone has been to College or are just your average family guy, huh?' Wolf probed, placing his hand on Lawrence's shoulder as if he was a friend, a gesture that drew a stern look in reply, so Wolf apologetically sliding his hand away. 'Well, I'm sure she hasn't told you that the owner has been implicated in 'improper relations with a minor', huh? Or that we have someone here who's got more connections to the Mob than Tony Soprano? And that's without even mentioning the screaming and grunting you can hear from Sebastian Toomes' locker room when he works his charms on some of our female talent...' As he spoke, Wolf could see the melting of Lawrence's harsh expression, slowly realising that his daughter was working in a place she knew he'd never approve of. Despite the thoughts spiralling out of control in his head, Lawrence didn't trust strangers... Especially not above his own flesh and blood. 'Are you calling my Karina a liar?' he growled, testing out Magnum. Lawrence wasn't sure how Magnum would answer him. Most would have backed away and found an answer to appease him, but Wolf had something else in store... Reaching down into his bag to pull out his video-camera. 'I'll prove it to you,' was the self-assured reply. His days were back-to-back meetings, and he was known as one of the toughest negotiators in his field... But Magnum Wolf had actually managed to silence Lawrence as he contemplated it all. He felt bad about doubting his daughter, but he knew Karina, and knew she'd let nothing get in her way once her heart was set on something. 'What did you have in mind?' he then asked Magnum, now receptive to him. 'I'll make my own, completely un-bias 'expose' of this place,' the Private Eye revealed, 'so then you can see what Karina didn't want you to see... And more.' It was a betrayal of Karina's trust, but it was for her own good... Or so Lawrence told himself. 'And what do you get out of this?' Lawrence questioned, looking Magnum up and down, mistrust still rife. 'There will be my usual fee,' a grinning Wolf revealed as he handed over his business card, 'but what's money when it comes to Karina's safety, huh?' Lawrence took the card and studied the text. 'Magnum Wolf - Private Eye : No Job Too Small, No Fee Too Big' it read, not presenting the most professional image of Wolf's part-time, one-man operation. 'My contacts are on there,' Wolf continued, 'so get in touch with my secretary... Who, er, sounds quite like me... In a week's time, and I'll have some footage that you won't be able to see anywhere else.' Karina tried her father's phone again, only to be diverted to his voicemail once more... Not leaving a message. 'Don't let it be Toomes,' she prayed, 'don't let it be Toomes...' Finally, her father briskly stepped out into the New Jersey night, before swiftly getting into the car, which in turn drove off as soon as the door slammed shut. She may not have known who her father was talking to, but he was gone now, so she could relax. He'd seemingly accepted her latest thrill-seek, and the K-Wolf was perfectly happy to leave it at that... ...But not nearly as happy as Magnum Wolf, who's smug grin just out of sight from Karina broke out into full-blown laughter.
