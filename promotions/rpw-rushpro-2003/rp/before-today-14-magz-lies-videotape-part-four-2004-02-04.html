Before Today #14 : Magz, Lies & Videotape (Part Four)

4 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/02/04 'What do you mean 'I'M LEAVING'?' 'It's not open for discussion,' Lawrence coldly ordered, not caring for his daughter's protests. 'You're not staying here, and that's all there is to it.' 'Err,' a wide-eyed Karina began, 'I don't think so.' This was far from the first argument the hot-headed daughter and over protected father had engaged in, but they were usually in locales a little more glamorous than a PWC locker room. 'If you think for one second that I'm going to let my only daughter frequent a place like this,' Lawrence announced, looking with displeasure at the dank surroundings, 'then you're more of a daydreamer than I thought.' 'Oh, I'm sorry,' Kari sarcastically shot back, her body language showing she wasn't going to shy away from the argument. 'I wasn't aware that I was your property.' 'Stop being so childish,' her father snapped back, trying to put Kari well and truly in her place. 'Pack your things, then we're going.' Just 2 days ago, a tape was brought to Lawrence by his butler, the tape Magnum Wolf had promised him. Acting the concerned friend, Wolf wanted to save Karina from a wrestling life, so made his own 'expose' of the Pro Wrestling Circuit... The content of which had been heavily scripted by the callous Private Eye. Lawrence bought it, literally too. 'Can't,' came her blunt and smarmy reply. 'I have a contract here, legally binding.' 'I've already talked to Mr. Starr,' Lawrence Wolfenden smiled, showing his keen business sense in making sure that all bases were covered. 'He's accepted my buy-out.' 'You did WHAT?' Karina exclaimed, distraught that her own father would over-ride her life. 'You've no reason to still be here,' Lawrence decreed, 'so you're leaving with me... Leaving this whole 'profession' behind...' 'Don't say it like that,' Karina ripped back, hating the way her father's tone of voice would belittle things he disapproved of. 'And I DO have a reason, it's because I WANT to be here.' Since her teens, Karina had been the main reason Lawrence's hair had turned grey around the sides. She could have grown up to be a clingy daughter, trying hard to win her parents' approval... But instead, Karina flew the nest, spread her wings and discovered the world... ...A world her father's business and expectations didn't occupy. 'Tough,' the obviously frustrated parent shot back, showing Karina he wasn't going to negotiate. 'Your whole life has been about what you 'want', it's time you actually did something constructive for once.' For so long Lawrence had sat back and watched Karina's passport collect stamps as if it were a hobby, let Karina throw herself fully into every one of her passions. He tried not to ask too many questions, but now he wasn't asking... He was telling. It was time to clip Karina's wings. 'Constructive?' she pondered. 'You mean constructive as in coming to work for you?' 'At least it's safe,' the over-protective Lawrence told her. 'And devoid of the low-lifes around here.' 'Safe? You mean BORING!' a despairing Karina exclaimed, gesturing an overly dramatic yawn to back her words up. 'And where are these 'low-lifes'? because I certainly haven't seen them. Then again, I'm not quite as snobbish as you.' 9 to 5 was Karina's idea of hell. Wearing a skirt-suit would be akin to wearing a noose around her neck. She didn't want to be an 'employee', she even feared it. When Karina looked at a clock, she always thought that time went too fast, constricting her... Stuck working for her father, she'd be counting down the seconds from the instant 9am hit. 'This isn't a case of being a snob,' Lawrence grunted, still keeping a superior tone to even that sound. 'It's a case of protecting my daughter, when she obviously has no considerations or standards herself.' 'What? Afraid that your dinner parties might go a little quiet when you say 'my daughter wrestles in gyms on a Saturday'?' Karina hissed, determined not to let her father's social status rule her life. 'And what do I need protecting from? This place has more security than any club... Or would you rather I spent my Saturday nights getting hammered in some club and waking up next to a stranger the next morning with a hangover an-' His thoughts on her life troubled him enough, without Karina hitting any more nerves. 'ENOUGH,' Lawrence roared, his domineering voice knocking some of the fight from Karina, lowering her eyes. 'For years I've watched you waste your life... You dropped out of college, you've never had a paying job, I never even know which COUNTRY you'll be in when I hear from you...' His harsh tone had Karina silenced, her lips ready to move but no words there to act as ammunition. '...The hair, the clothes, the piercings, the attitude, and now this carnival... This... FREAK SHOW! It has to stop, and it stops now.' 'You can't make me,' Kari answered, the beginnings of a tear forming in her right eye, while her words wavered. 'Oh, I can,' Lawrence nodded, an all-conquering pose having been adopted. 'Believe me, I can.' She knew he could, but Karina just hoped he wouldn't go that far. 'I'm the one who pays your credit card bill every month, I'm the one who has to finance your little jaunts around the world,' he ranted, forcing it into Karina that her independence is bought by him, and him only. 'I pay the rent on your apartment, I'm the one who paid money into your trust fund for 18 years... And I'm the one who can stop writing the cheques, close the accounts and cancel the cards.' Karina didn't want this to be the end of her leisurely life. She knew that most people had to work, that the majority of people her age had found employment, began to settle down, carved out their place in life... Karina never thought that narrow. If she got an e-mail saying friends in Switzerland were planning a party, she could get the credit card out and be on the first free flight. It was a privileged life, and one she wanted to hold on to. 'You mean stop me doing everything I love?' Kari argued back, not wanting to give in to Lawrence's idea of how she should live. 'I'm never going to be one of your 'employees'... Just because you're my father, it doesn't mean you control me.' There was a hypocrisy to it, and she knew that... But she couldn't help it, Kari just didn't think like most people, and certainly didn't live like them. 'And does it mean that I have to sit back while you associate with drug dealers?' Lawrence boomed, finally bringing up the tape as he saw Karina was on the back-foot. 'While you surround yourself with the bottom of society's barrel? While any scum GROPES my only daughter.' She remembered the previous week... It was odd she thought, but not suspicious. 'How did you...?' a shocked and curious Karina half said, half gasped. 'Oh, Mr. Wolf showed me some VERY interesting footage,' a self-assured Lawrence revealed. 'And it's on video-tape, so there's no arguing your way out of it.' Then it hit her... The reason for her father' sudden explosion had been down to Magnum Wolf. Deep down, she was applauding his ingenuity, just as a chess player would acknowledge an inspired move by their opponent. Deep down, that's what their rivalry boiled down to, a pair of strong wills locked in a calculated duel, both looking for checkmate. 'You'd trust HIM over your own daughter?' Karina yelled in reply, realising what levels Wolf had stooped to. 'He set the whole thing up, the guy's an A-Grade CREEP!' 'I believe him,' Lawrence bit back. 'because he didn't make sure I only saw the good side... And why would he lie, exactly?' 'Because...' Karina began, before realising that Lawrence would never buy the truth about Magnum, he'd just think it was an excuse. Lawrence hadn't seen what was really happening, and if Karina had explained her back and forth relationship with Wolf, he certainly would have yanked her out with him. Magnum Wolf was a game, a toy to play around with and frustrate... Like dangling string in front of an over-eager kitten. But he'd taken it up a notch, the question was... ...Could she follow? 'Listen, Karina, it's done.' Lawrence stated, hammering the point home with his stare. 'No daughter of mine is going to hang around a dump like this. When I leave here, which won't be soon enough, you're coming with me.' 'And if I don't?' Karina asked, less than bold with her head dropped down. 'Oh you will,' he confidently assured her, almost smiling. 'If you want an apartment to go back to, and if you want your cards to be worth more than the plastic they're made of.' 'I'd rather have nothing,' Karina potently answered, thrusting her head up so she could stare right at her father through teary eyes, 'than be in debt to someone like that.' It was the choice Karina knew, deep down, she should have made years ago. It was time to stand on her own two feet, to let Karina Wolfenden control Karina Wolfenden's life. 'You'll change your mind soon enough,' Lawrence grunted, not buying into her apparent resistance one bit. 'It's a little late to finally decide to stand on your own two feet.' 'Then it's about time I learned,' Karina bluntly replied, her words biting with conviction. 'Look, this hasn't suddenly turned into a debate...' Lawrence roared, but his words no longer kept Kari at bay. 'I am leaving, you are coming with me. No more wrestling, no more disappearing off. You are 23 years old, stop wasting your life.' To Lawrence, it may have been true, but to Karina, her life hadn't even wasted a day. She spoke 7 languages, she had seen more of the world than most would in their entire lifetimes. She'd seen the northern lights in Sweden, she'd been one of the first to see in the Millennium from the Pacific island of Taveuni, she'd toasted Christmas with friends over snow-chilled Vodka in Lapland. ...Not a second was wasted to her. 'Wasting?' she audibly enquired, standing up to her domineering father. 'So doing things I enjoy is wasting? Being happy is wasting? Being FREE is wasting?' 'Don't talk back to me,' he snapped. 'Now, forget all that junk, you're com-' 'Go,' Karina demanded, pointing to the door while sniffing back her tears. 'I want you out, now.' 'What is this?' a bemused Lawrence exclaimed. 'Don't tell ME what I shoul-' 'I'm not coming with you,' her cold words assured him. 'So go, or I'll call security, and have them escort you.' When Lawrence walked through the door, he'd take Karina's finances with her... All she had to do to keep everything was back down, say sorry, tell him to stop. But her lips remained sealed, it was time to be truly independent... And if she needed to effectively be an orphan to do so, it was something she had to do. 'I'll give you a week...' Lawrence ripped back as he left, seemingly knowing she wouldn't be able to live without him. 'Good luck in the real world, Karina.'
