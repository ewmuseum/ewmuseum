From Here To Back Again: Silvio My Dear Friend

19 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/04 The ghosts of my time after PCW still haunt me, coming from all directions never allowing me a moments rest. It seemed that I was at my lowest moments when the word started going around that Fusion would be the last card that was placed under the PCW banner. Times were tough and my depression was deep on the final night on the airship. After all this was going to be my last time on the airship. My last time to see friends; the last chance I had to finish my business with Silvio. A bottle of vodka was sitting on the desk in the corner of my cabin. My binge had been going hard after the show, but the time for a much needed stress reliever was at hand. I pulled a box from under my bed and removed the cloth from the top of it. The contents were simple: a needle, heroin, and a rubber strap to tie off my arm. As soon as I put the strap in place, my veins bulged calling out for their escape. It was quick in coming, the needle passed into my skin and the drug began to take its course. I was young and in a business and a situation I did not fully understand. The powerful silver gun found its way into the back of my pants and the bottle of booze was gripped tightly in my hand. If I had a purpose at the time I did not understand it, but this was a night of depression. In my entire time on this earth, one thing stood out above the rest. Wrestling was the only thing that I had ever been good at. Now my world was toppling around me. Everyone else had a place to go. Old School Wrestling was hoping to expand and now the talent pool was rolling in. Even my enemy Silvio Rochelli had been granted asylum in this new country. Yet I was alone, left in the cold to fend for myself like a wolf being separated from the rest of the pack. That was not going to happen, because I wasn’t going to allow it. People fail to realise that a lone wolf on the verge of destruction is the most dangerous one from the pack. I grabbed my red trench coat from the bed and hung it on my fragile body. My trade mark blonde hair was spiked and my combat boots were in place. For the last time ever I was becoming the assassin, the CIA agent that was nothing more than a ploy to get people to direct their gaze onto me. It was never something people respected. When Xavier Kannon brought me into destroy the company that he built, it was my only task. Something stopped me from doing it though; perhaps it was Tessa, the beautiful girl of my dreams. Or disillusions of grander that were placed in front of me by the secret VPs of PCW. I did not destroy it. In the end I did not have to destroy it. People like Ron Starr did that task for me. I stood in my cabin for a minute more, allowing the heroin to make its final rounds in my blood stream. A high that I loved and a binge I couldn’t stop were the things that let me out of my cabin. The dimly lit hallway seemed as if it was converging on me. Walls acting like human chests, heaving and collapsing with each breath. Vodka was spilling down my throat without my knowledge, leaving a burning sensation in my stomach. None of it mattered anymore. It seemed to never end as I marched down the halls of the airship. My destination was known to me, but for some reason I could not find it. An hour before the needle had passed into my skin; a call had been placed to one Silvio Rochelli from one Matt Jackson. I asked him to meet me in the boiler room of the ship, to discuss matters. He knew I wasn’t happy, but he agreed anyways. Heat blasted out from under the door and I knew I was in the right place. A crescent smile cut my lips and showed my glimmering teeth. I reached out and grabbed the handle to the door, my hand was trembling, but the mixture of vodka and heroin was relaxing it. Once I pulled the door open, it felt as if I was kicked in the face. Steamy air bombarded me from all sides and knocked the breath from my lungs. Silvio stood on the walkway with an imposable presence. He was dressed with his usual cheap second-hand business suit and the ever so cocky smile. In his left hand was a briefcase black enough to match his heart and hair. Fear would not be the word to describe me at that instant. I didn’t fear the wop; I loathed him and hated him for the torment he had forced me to endure. I was nineteen barely a year out of high school and this forty year old business man was treating me worse than a piece of trash. “Hello Matthew, it’s good to see you again. I’m sorry you didn’t get an invitation to OSW. Our games would have been so fun continuing,” his voice cackled. “But business is business and you aren’t as good at it as me.” “Talent is talent as well Silvio and you just don’t have any,” I replied. “You got into OSW because you kissed the right peoples asses, me on the other hand happen to have the thing you don’t. When it’s all said and done you will long be forgotten and I will still be standing over you.” He chuckled and took a few steps towards me. I could feel his breath attacking my skin and place me on the defensive. “Matthew, don’t feel bad. You could never get over me, it’s just the way the world turns,” he began. “I do have a small severance package for you though; from my company. After all you made me big without even know what you were doing.” I couldn’t believe he was serious. He was offering me money, which was a slap in the face. I pressed the vodka bottle up to my lips and took a few more drinks. He stood there, looking at me looking for an answer. But I didn’t have one in the form of words. A fist flew into his face and his nose snapped. “I have a going away present for you too,” I screamed. “The only problem is you aren’t going to be alive long enough to fucking see it you worthless wop. See that’s the deal with you Silvio, you are too conceded to realise that I’m not your little bitch. I’m not some kid fresh out of the trailer park. The reason you think you are better than the rest of us is because you grew up with nothing and now that you dad sucked his way to the top. You think you are something special for inheriting it. Now look at yourself, bunching yourself up in a ball. Holding your nose and feeling your blood stain your face.” I kicked my boot into his mouth shattering his teeth. The pain on his face was a joy as he choked on the shrapnel in his mouth. “You are a fucking greedy wop attempting to cheat his way to the top like the rest of them Silvio. No more, my connections have brought me here. Angelo Deville is a masterful planner he’s been at this much longer than I. Simply put, you are going to be gone and no one is the wiser. Someone is already in place to act as you.” My smile grew as a smashed my boot down on the side of his head. Angelo’s plan was in genies. Silvio Rochelli was going to die in the boiler room and some other fucking wop was going to take his place. My part was simple to pull the trigger. I heard his groans and yelps, but I was lost in my own world. My torment and pain was all about to end. Again my boot smashed into his head and than found its way to his chest. The snapping of his ribs was a sound so sweet. I knew his lung had been punctured, but that wasn’t enough for me. I wanted to end his life. The cold steel meshed with my sweaty hands. “Good-bye Silvio, it was fun while it lasted,” I laughed. A pawn in some greater plan was a possibility. Angelo might have been using me for some higher purpose the answers to those questions are still unknown and my mentor is a zombie, so I will never receive them. I had a second thought for a moment. Angelo had been the one to supply the booze, the heroin, the pistol. The entire plan was his, but I wanted this more than anything. BOOM! I expected more recoil and more blood. None of it was present, just a dead body. A man whose life I had actually taken. The feeling in the pit of my stomach was disgusting and my face was covered in perspiration. “Not bad at all,” a voice from the shadows said. My eyes darted in all directions as Angelo Deville appeared. He wore a mask of calm and a suit of immaculate white. He walked over to the corpse and tapped it with his shoe. “We need to dispose of this, but you did well kid.” “It’s finally over?” “Yes my lost lamb. You did the job perfectly he will never bother you again.” His words were comfort. Silvio Rochelli was no more. My worst enemy was dead and gone. My hell was just beginning.
