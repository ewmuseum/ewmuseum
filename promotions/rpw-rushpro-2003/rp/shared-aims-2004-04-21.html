Shared Aims

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/04 Not too long ago, that little wretch, David Gibson tried to do away with me. That amateurish poser of a “heel” attempted to seal me away in walls of stone. Some bitch gets in my way and he tries to send me away to the pokey. Yeah, fucking right. He knew full well I would get out. He knew for a fact I would escape. Hell, this is wrestling. When the bad guy throws another guy in jail, they always come back the next week. I knew it. He knew it. But he did not see me coming last week when I took that weapon to Stewart’s misbegotten cranium. That was just too great a moment for me. Then Cisco tries to be my good buddy just because his goals coincide with mine. Well, fuck that. He got the hell beat out of him, too. I am RPW’s one true miscreant and as horribly cliché as this sounds, I live for the backstabbing. I live to raise a little hell every week and I cannot wait until I make Mr. Milloy pay in spades for his ignorance later in the evening. My mind buckles one day at a time since I have started my one-man quest to turn RPW on its head. So many odd characters, left and right have confronted me and that trend does not give the impression of being finished anytime soon. Already, I must deal with Dave Gibson and his charge, Shawn Stewart. Now Cisco Milloy looks to intervene simply because his and Dave’s definitions of the words “old school” clash. News flash, fuckers: “old school” died when Terry “Hulk Hogan” Bollea decided he would shake his head and prance around like a geriatric buffoon for the cash rather than the people. I merely wish to get my hands on Dave Gibson because he’s a fucking leech. Sure, I may be no better than he is in the fans’ eyes, but I am not living vicariously through an inexperienced simpleton. He should not be in this world, yet here he is with a man he respects, yet is completely oblivious and trusting. Trust was a concept that I decided to set free the moment bookers decided to make me injure my best friend. Shawn is a puppet and he does not see it, so I fear that I must batter my views into his head until he sees Dave for what he really is. Cisco, to me, is nothing more than fodder. He may be much younger than I, but his best days ended when the letters O, S, and W sank into the Earth. OSW perished and apparently, the three participants in this little tête-à-tête do not understand it. RPW, while a very shitty product in its own right, is a godsend compared to the backstage bedlam that led to OSW’s downfall. So not only must I inflict pain upon Mr. Milloy, Stewart, and Gibson, but now I must make them see that OSW has and will always be dead. And now that you know where I stand in this little affair and you have been enlightened, I must ask that you leave. Your very presence sickens me to my very core.
