End of Act One

16 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 16th 2004 Life… or unlife, couldn’t be better. About one month ago, he sat in a dismal basement, with only the footsteps from the floorboards above him and a small animal many people didn’t believe exited for company. Now, he sat in a dismal basement, with only the footsteps from the floorboards above him and a small animal many people didn’t believe exited for company… but with a gigantic grin on… and off his face. Slowly, bit by bit, more about the Human Horror Film was being revealed. He motives since the collapse of OSE and subsequently, P.W.A. seemed to border on a vainglorious “for the drama of it all” reason, as though he were an actor playing the role fate gave him with relish. From the stage beneath the Yamamura Inn to an audience of none, he shammed his way into Clyde’s favour. When the demon appeared to him, Nerezza was apprehensive to say the least. Clyde granted him a choice… sadness now, or sadness later. ~ Several weeks ago ~ “Who’s there…?” Nerezza called out. Dragging himself to his feet, his motion and sound disturbed Fluffy, causing the chupacabra to perk his own little head up from his cosy spot by the pipes. But the room was indeed getting warmer. A warmth… no, a heat that only Hell could produce. One of it’s children had paid a visit. “Son…” the voice came. “You’re too late.” “…I am not your son.” Nerezza growled, already on guard. “Although, you’re only out by a single generation. Why are you here?” “Hessian plans to kill me. Quite simply, kid, he’s coming here right now. Already on his way over, on the ferry. When he gets here, he’ll probably destroy the whole place so you have nothing to stay behind for. Then he’ll bring you to a little company called RPW.” Clyde explained, but was cut mid-sentence by a snappy vampire. “RP…W? I already know what the W stands for. But my days for wrestling are over. P.W.A. has disbanded, Gaz is in movies and I’m sitting here waiting for the end to come, whenever it may. Until then, leave me to my solitude.” He hissed. If he were a cat, his back would have arched and his fur would be standing up. If he were a rattlesnake, his neck would have bent into an S shape and his tail would have been shaking rapidly. But he was Nerezza. Clawed-up hands and a distinct tone of threat in his voice would have to suffice. “Well, kiddo, you have another option.” The demon smirked. Oh yes… one of THOSE smiles. Like when you corner an insect and you have a kettle of boiling water in one hands and a magnifying glass in the other on a hot summer’s day. “You can either do me a big favour and go undercover for me, get my idiot son’s trust and then when I say so, you shove the knife in his back. OR…” “Or…?” “Or the entire inn… you, Fluffy, the family and guests – and everything within a 1 mile radius becomes ground zero for a forest fire – and you KNOW I can do it.” The vampire knew his “Grandfather” wasn’t joking. While not a single drop of blood was shared within the family, the basis of the so-called bloodline was more of a spirit… a feeling, an attitude granted to those with inhuman souls, whether their bodies were human or not. Clyde was of the latter… one of Hell’s very own. He’d been burned to a crisp, dropped from insane heights, hounded by Men In Very Very Very Very Very Dark Grey and blown up by Xavier Kannon’s Surface To Freak missiles. Yet every time, Clyde had come back. Same burned face, same shredded body, same soul so dark, it practically absorbed light like a black hole. With the proverbial match in one hand… which was actually a literal ball of flame… Nerezza had no choice but to co-operate with the demon’s desires. As fast as he had arrived, Clyde disappeared as soon as Nerezza had nodded his head in agreement. Mere hours later, Hessian would show up seeking Nerezza’s aid against Clyde. The inn, no matter which way it happened, would become a slaughterhouse. The only bonus is that Hessian would grant each of them a quick death before he made artwork of their corpses. ~ The present ~ Blood is indeed a hard thing to clean up. When it dries, it turns red-brown and only seems to stain more rather than vanish. Nerezza’s white, almost silver hair, was covered in blood. His own, Clyde’s, Hessian’s and even Minion’s. But it was blood he would refuse to ingest for reasons he would keep to himself. In one night alone, such revelations had happened. Things yet to unfold. The reunion with Gaz Green, who had been noted on more than one occasion to be sitting in RPW’s audience. The girl he saw, who caught his eye amongst the many. And the man he saw… the next one to suffer. Who he would meet next, in which order, not even he knew. After all… an actor may only follow his script. But the very reason he was still around, the motive for returning to the ring, the raison d’etre… was how an actor performs his lines. The gestures, the voice, the personality put into it. Just the same as Robert Englund turned Freddy Krueger into the wisecracking anti-hero of the later Nightmare On Elm Street movies… just as Daveigh Chase had added a childlike sadism to Samara Morgan in The Ring… just as Peter Cushing and Christopher Lee had elevated Dracula beyond the history books and even Stoker’s rendition of Vlad Tepes… Nerezza had his own role to play. And the show… must go on.
