The Preliminaries: Signing up

14 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/14/03 (Repost of Free Agent RP) Fourteen days following the New Year. The weather was generally pleasant, with some clouds in the distance that would indicate the approach of rain. The RPW registration desk sat in the middle of the 15x15 room across from the glass fourth wall that allowed the early morning sunlight from the world outside to filter into the sterile office. Behind the neatly organized desk there was a middle-aged brunette in big plastic librarian's glasses, looking a bit heavy-set and stressed, but dignififed. Her dark blue women's business suit went well with the deep brown cherrywood desk, and made her seem all the more 'with it'. Seven chairs lined the fourth wall, with an extended magazine rack along the wall to the right adjacent to the windowed-wall opposite the door into the room. All the chairs were empty; being only 9:32 am, not many people would have been checking in for anything yet. Stepping into the room, the triumphant blaring of a brass set that played '2001: A Space Odessey' alerted the entire room (all one of the people in there) that Vangellus Oxios had entered the building. He's a black American citizen of Greek and Canadian descent. Dressed to the nines in a navy gray-pinstriped three piece suit complete with the black Cole Haan shoes, having his short wavy black hair slicked back with gel for a 'wet look, Vangellus struck the kind of image of a professional man who knows what he wants out of life and will go after it. The secretary, far too jaded by egotistical fighters to be impressed by this, and simply glanced at Vangellus through the corner of her eyes for a moment. After a disinterested blink, she returned to her morning routine of listening to the Golden Oldies station on the radio, eating her Nutri-Grain bar, and looking over her boss's agenda for the day. 'Greetings,' said Vangellus, walking over to the desk. His theme music was apparently coming from outside, as it stopped when he moved further into the room and allowed the black-framed glass door to close behind him. The secretary nearly ignored him, her response only being another glance up at him. 'I'm sure you've heard of me: Vangellus Oxios. Here's my card.' Vangellus removed a business card from his inner left breast pocket. He smiles, revealing pearly white teeth. They're all still there, even though he's taken a quite a few blows to the face. The face is his meal ticket, much like it was for Oscar De La Hoya. The card had Vangellus's name, e-mail, fax, and cell phone number inscribed, along with his signature logo that appears on all of his clothing. The logo is that of twin dragons, wrapped around a sword in the design of a Claymore; the sword ends in a hilt which sprouts flames that envelop the sword and the dragons. Quite elaborate for a logo that's usually small, but the publishers of the cards and his tailors don't mind doing it for the money that he pays them. Taking the card, the secretary's eyes were firmly glued to her Gateway computer. The card was set upon the desk, face down. She was not at all impressed. 'Yes... anyway, I'd like to register, please.' The secretary moved her left hand over the keyboard and pressed two buttons along the top. F3 and F5. Moving her right hand over the black and chrome mouse that matched the color scheme of the computer, she dragged the accessory along the square mousepad from upper left to the lower center. Clicking twice, she then proceeded to type something in. Leaning forward, Vangellus snaps his right thumb and middle finger at the unresponsive secretary. Getting no reaction, he does that four more times. 'Would you stop that, please?' the secretary requested, though more in a way that was telling him to cease rather than asking him to cease. Coming back to his erect standing height, Vangellus raised, then lowered her eyebrows. 'I had to make sure that you weren't in a trance. It's generally respectful to give other people some kind of response when they talk to you.' 'Lose the ego and I might be a little more attentive.' She had a point. Vangellus did walk in like he owned the place. Theme music to step into a new room? Who does that with a straight face? With a frustrated sigh, Vangellus slowly said, 'Fine, now would you help me?' The secretary pointed at the computer screen with her left index finger. I have to pull up the registration files, hold your horses.' A few more moments of typing, and the secretary was ready. 'Okay, name?' Vangellus slapped his forehead with the palm of his left hand. 'Sweet glittery crap! I just told you AND gave you a card!' 'And?' 'So, you can't fault me for being self-confident if you're going to be stupid and inattentive.' The secretary hit the button on the monitor. 'Oh well, the computer's off and I don't care to pull the file up again. You'll have to come back some other time.' Another sigh of frustration from Vangellus. 'I know that you just turned off the monitor. Don't insult us both.' He took a roll of cash from his pocket and slipped the secretary fifteen hundred dollars. In truth, he had much more money than that; such a frivolous waste of cash was just a drop in the Olympic-sized swimming pool for him. 'Maybe this will make you more personable. Able to slip me into your busy day now?' The secretary took the money and slid it into her purse underneath the desk. Her attitude immediately changed, noticeable in that she turned tbe monitor back on. 'Registration, yes, I can help you get set up with that. I'll add it your name.' Name: Vangellus Oxios Looking up at Vangellus, she asked, 'Are there any nicknames or titles by which you're addressed or referred?' Vangellus nodded and answered. Aliases: 1) Sky High Oxios 2) SHO 3) 'The Most Marketable Competitor in the World' 4) Your Hero, Your Nightmare (Your Icon), Your Wet Dream Vangellus made many names for himself fighting in different formats. He started in American boxing, then made the transition into kickboxing, and ultimate fighting. All of these names he has really given himself. Sky High Oxios is something that was completely dreamed up for his budding professional wrestling career. 'That last one might not go through,' the secretary said of Vangellus's final nickname. 'Hey, I'm all about truth in advertising. It's an accurate statement, it has to go through.' 'If you say so... Age?' Vangellus nodded his head. Age: 26 The secretary input the numbers into the computer, then skipped the next section. 'We both know your gender, I won't bother asking.' A grin. 'That's the initiative I like to see, Miss....' Fishing for a name, Vangellus got it. 'Gladys.' 'Gladys. Nice to meet you.' Sex: Male 'How tall are you?' 'American or metric?' Vangellus asked. Gladys shook her head. 'Doesn't matter.' Height: 6'4' 'And how much do you weigh? Weight: 222 lb. 'And your hometown or current town of residence?' Hometown: Oxios Grand Estate of Excellence, Crete The next question was a little more than is usually asked on a registration form. 'What do you like? Activities, things... what do you like to do?' 'Why do you need to know that?' Vangellus scratched his head. 'I'm just signing up to wrestle, aren't I?' Gladys waved off the question. 'That's not the point. We run a resource site. People can read up on you, your background, and your techniques. We control all information regarding you, so anything that you want revealed to the public will be applicable in the rest of this registration. Likewise, omit any information that you want kept private.' 'Ah, don't worry, I've got nothing to hide.' Thinking for a second, Vangellus continued. 'Well, I've always been a big follower of the Three W's: Wine, Women, and Winning.' 'In that order?' A shrug of Vangellus's shoulders was the immediate response. 'The priorities might switch around every now and then.' Furrowing her brow, Gladys said, 'Is there any way you can give this section a little more substance? Something that doesn't make people want to hate you when reading it?' 'Fine...' Likes: 1) Sirloin Steak 2) Training 3) Autograph Sessions 4) His Fans 5) Luxury cars, particularly the Bentley, Porsche 6) Vacationing at remote tourist spots around the globe 7) Winning 8) His private jet built by the Lexus manufacturers 'Wait a minute, since when did Lexus make jets?' Vangellus laughed. 'They'll make anything if you pay them enough for it.' 'Well, now we've got to cover the things that you don't like.' Dislikes: 1) People who act like he owes them a part of his life because they like to watch his fights 2) Fish 3) Random women that throw themselves at him; quote Oxios, 'I don't want it if it's that easy!' 4) Naked pictures of men; quote Oxios, 'Seriously, you people need to stop that.' 5) Losing 6) Staying inactive for too long without resting 7) Fighters who aren't worth his time to 'school' Gladys looked up at Vangellus. 'You're not one of those mysterious types, are you?' 'Not that I'm aware.' 'Well, certain people like to keep their past a secret. And by certain, I mean many people.' Vangellus exhaled a long breath. 'Some people want to be wrestlers, but don't want everyone to know it. Odd, it's not exactly a profession that's compatible with anonymity. Go figure.' Parents: Alexander Oxios (father), Deborah Oxios (mother) 'Brothers or sisters?' Vangellus shook his head. 'Technically no.' Siblings: Danielle P. (sister); Javier P. (brother-in-law) 'Why doesn't your sister count?' 'I have to provide a reason? Geez. Since when did you care about my life story?' 'Don't worry, we'll get to that later. Any significant other?' Vangellus jokingly replied, 'If you're offering, you're not really my type.' 'Seriously...' 'Seriously.' The secretary released a slightly frustrated sigh of her own. Significant Other: None 'I guess there aren't any children running around, since you haven't been with anyone.' 'Yeah. I can't have a lot of baby-mamas running around. Or even just one at home. I'm around all the time, wouldn't leave someone waiting for me.' Children: None 'Okay, the next question deals with your fighting style. Who taught you?' Mentors: Amateur Wrestling -- Dr. Richard D. Basic Professional Wrestling -- Guy 'American Kamikaze' Walker Boxing -- Cornelius Johnston Kickboxing -- Quyen Tran The Way of the Whole Freakin' SHO -- Self-developed 'What's next?' Vangellus asked. 'Now,' Gladys answered, 'You get to brag about your fighting style. Explain what it is.' 'Very well, take this down.' Fighting Style: Vangellus uses a self-taught fighting/wrestling hybrid technique called 'The Way of the Whole Freakin' SHO'. Though the attacks do have power to them, this style incorporates a lot of flash and elegance into its movements. Everything is as pleasing to the eye as it is damaging. There are seven components to the Tao of SHO, described by Vangellus himself. 1) The Tao of Power: 'There should be no attack that is not intended to deal damage; if it does not hurt, it does not need to be utilized in a fight.' 2) The Tao of Speed: 'Slow and steady may win the race, but it will get you beaten to a oozing bloody pulp in a fight. You have to be able to think, move, react, anticipate, and attack just as fast, if not faster than your opponent. Temperance in all things, maybe, but since your opponent's goal in life is to reward you with a trip to the hospital, it's in the best interests of your health to give more hits than you take. ' 3) The Tao of Defense: 'As great as attacking may be, and it is great, sometimes the best thing to do is to sit back and block, reverse, counter, or dodge your opponent's moves. Get a feel for the way that they move, spy potential weaknesses to be taken advantage of. In a match, just a tiny crack in the opponent's defenses, properly utilied, will open up the floodgates.' 4) The Tao of Agility: 'If you can't stretch, can't jump, can't do a flip, then the odds are that you're going to be caught in a situation that you won't be prepared to deal with. Your mind needs to be limber in order for you to quickly analyze a problem and come to the proper and necessary conclusion to win, but your body must to be able to do what your mind needs it to do in order for that conclusion to succeed. The Whole Freaking SHO style is not for everyone. I tailor made it for myself, but I know that others will try to follow in my footsteps. Just make sure you're physically capable of doing at least 80% of the gymnastics that I employ.' 5) The Tao of Adaptibility: 'Your attack strategy can't remain the same for all situations. You'll need to change things depending on your opponent's strengths and weaknesses, the place of the fight, and your own physical condition. You should be able to alter your plan quickly and effectively to suit the task at hand. To that end, you really shouldn't even have just one finisher. If they never see the end coming, it makes you that much more dangerous.' 6) The Tao of Creativity: 'Part of being a great competitor is being able to surprise your opponents. You can't do that if you if you only perform the basic moves, or do moves that everyone else does. Perfect your own special attacks, and lay waste to the unsuspecting 'competition'.' 7) The Tao of Psychology: 'This is more important than any one of the previous Taos. To use this Tao effectively is to sway the fight in your favor. Psychology can be used for you, or against your opponent. In the first case, you may need to spice things up a bit. Always develop new and exciting moves. If you're bored with the techniques that you use, what's the point in using them? Note that for the purposes of The Whole Freakin' SHO style, fighting with an audience is always better. The audience, when they get behind you for the cool-looking and damaging moves that you do, will support you. Their cries of love and support are a booster to confidence, and make you feel stronger on a psychological level. With a crowd of people cheering your name, you can completely forget your injuries and rise to victory. As for the second case of usage of the Tao of Psychology, always taunt your opponent when you're sure that you have a free moment. It will anger them, or if not throw them into a rage, it will at least throw them off of their game a little. I don't care how stoic a person may ACT, underneath, it'll get to them. Besides, there's no reason to be better than others if you don't brag about it. When not being the cocky in words, it's always good to change your fighting stance in order to affect your opponent. Turning your back or crossing your arms behind your back are two good ways to mock the opponent. The disrespect will make your opponent angry, and they'll charge or try some type of attack. You now control that person, so you're the one in the position to dictate whether or not he or she stays on their feet for much longer. All in all, The Way of the Whole Freakin' SHO is a great technique. As long as I live longer, it'll surpass even Jeet Kun Do in the terms of being loved and attempted by all. 'That was long-winded.' 'I didn't think that I was working under a time limit or anything.' '... Next, list any signature attacks that you do.' Signature Moves: 1) Pepsi Plunge -- soft drink 2) Max Factor -- cosmetic line 3) Southwest Air -- airline 4) Mach 3 -- Gillete razor 5) X-Boxed -- video game console 6) The Clapper -- appliance 7) Nike Shox -- athletic shoe 8) Roca Driver -- clothing line 9) PS1-2-3 -- video game console 10) Pathfinder -- sport utility vehicle Gladys looked up from the monitor. 'You're not going to give descriptions?' 'What? And spoil the surprise when I actually debut them?' 'Why are they all named after consumer goods or services, anyway?' Flashing the money roll again, Vangellus said, 'Marketing, my dear. If I sell the naming rights to a move that I make, I can charge the company every time I do the move. It's ingenius!' Vangellus stretched his arms up above his head. The ceiling was only eight feet, so he had to lower them enough that he wouldn't mess up the textured pattern. 'This is more tiring than I thought.' 'Well, you're almost finished. What are you like? Your personality, I mean.' Personality: Vangellus is arrogant, or confident, depending on whom you ask. He knows his strengths and weaknesses; goes to great lengths to hype the former and downplay the latter. He's accustomed himself to the finer things in life, and won't settle for anything less. Though there is a problem with ego, he can make an attempt to interact with people from different walks of life. Proud of his heritage, he wears the flags of his mother's and father's respective countries on his elbow pads, which he always wears for fights. Vangellus will give anyone the 'benefit of the doubt' in assuming that they are kind and helpful; it is apparently up to the person to prove him right or wrong. He does have a habit of thinking that he is better than everyone, though, and seems disturbed and shocked when people don't know who he is.Though single and not looking for anyone, he loves to flirt. Physical competition is a big thrill for him, and he seeks out new opponents to defeat. His entire image is that of being the most marketable fighter in the world, so he'll accept challenges from anyone, and endorse any product that he likes or appreciates. 'What's your wrestling attire?' Appearance: Red denim vest with 'SHO' emblazoned across the back. The Vangellus Oxios logo on the left breast. Black cotton sweat pants with the trademark Oxios logo on each leg. Enters matches wearing a boxer's cloak, which reads across the back, 'SHO Mania: Get it or get out of the way.' 'And lastly, we need to know your background.' Vangellus nodded. 'Alright, write down every word I say verbatim.' History: Okay, so where to begin? I grew up in the lap of luxury. The bastard son of Bruce Wayne and Lois Lane, you can imagine that I had my share of problems. Mom was always getting herself kidnapped just to have an affair with another guy. She always called him the 'Man of Steel', and apparently that nickname had nothing to do with the fact that bullets bounced off of him. *Wink wink nudge nudge* Dad... he was never home in the first place. Running a multi-billion dollar a year company during the day and dressing up like The Amish Avenger at night (but you could tell that he wasn't with all those gadgets he used), I never saw him. He tried to get me into that crime-fighting thing when I was seven, but I told him that I bruised easily and was looking more to be a lawyer. He'd capture them, I'd put them away. But he was all like 'No, we need to take matters into our own hands. Too many times has the legal system let the citizens down.' Putz. So while mom's off having an affair with some flying tool, dad spent any of his free time out of the house. He had three other kids that he wanted to spend more time with. Not that they were his, oh no, he just kinda decided that they were going to be his chosen children more than me. Some circus performer, the police commissioner's daughter, and two other punks from the poor side of the tracks. Just because they wanted to take the 'Death Wish' approach to crime, he cuts off contact with me. The bastard. As you can imagine, after I singlehandedly won the Wimbeldon tournament at the age of seven, I became legally emancipated. I started dating Demi Moore, ended apartheid in South Africa, and somehow found time to crack the code for the human genome. During a trip I took to Spain, I saw Barcelona and said, 'This would be a good place for the Olympics.' And I made a few phone calls. BAM! 1994 Summer Olympics from Barcelona, baby. Fast forward to my EIGHTH birthday, I graduated from Harvard. I had a Master's in Biochemistry and International Economics, and then there was Harvard Law and Harvard Medical, which I cleared up over the summer. Following a year's break to handle my business, I cut Demi loose, introduced Jennifer Love Hewitt to acting, won a few Gran Prix races in Monaco, became the first person to release five albums simultaneously across different genres (rap, rock, country, classical, and latin), and buffed up to 300 pounds of muscle for my career as a professional K-1 fighter living in Japan. But man, recently, I've just wanted to get away from all the hectic celebrity lifestyle. I came to the island of Crete (which I now own and am ruler for life), fixed it up into technologically advanced paradise, and now the rest is history. I'm ruling that place like a god and still do the occasional 'shadow-producing' for the Neptunes (you think they're THAT good? HA!). ..... Yeah. So that means that I grew up in Michigan with my mother and father. Simple life, no real hassles. Actually, I was kind of a science geek in the younger years. My older sister (ten years older) was the better-loved child, in my opinion. She was the athletic one. She was the popular one. I just stayed inside and worked on homework all day. Of course, things change. Whereas I completely skipped college and lived off my folks from the ages of 18 to 22, Danielle went to school in America at the Michigan Institute of Technology. Met some guy there, got married, had kids, and led a successful life in the field of automobile design. In high school, where I was at the time that she was living life to the fullest, I joined the wrestling team. It was the only sport that wouldn't let me be weak for being a small guy, and I really needed that kind of push. Thanks to the coach and school history teacher Dr. D., I learned confidence, I learned how to wrestle and control another person's movements, and I learned what it was like to be social, and in the eye of the crowd. Though only getting up to 166 in high school despite decent weight training, I was still able to get on the varsity team. We went to nationals and competed in tournaments that had everyone knowing our names. I still talk with some of the guys in the old team, though they've settled into quieter lives. Me? From high school, I went into professional wrestling for about two months as the protege and tag team partner of the American Kamikaze. Yeah, it's scripted, but it still hurt getting slammed repeatedly, so it's far from fake. But I did that, was a big independent name as Sky High Oxios. I traveled to Japan, Mexico, Germany, Australia, South Africa, England... a whole lot of places. I was introduced to different cultures and different sports. It was when I was in Mexico and saw a really inspired boxing match between a couple of no-name rookies, that was when I said to myself 'I can do that.' So I came back to the states and became a boxer. Got trained by former Unified Welterweight champion Cornelius Johnston. I was undefeated in amateur wrestling and amateur boxing; I needed a challenge, so the next step was professional boxing. I only lasted three fights before almost quitting. Not that I didn't win and dominate my opponents, but there's way too long between fights. A month? Two months? I'm an active, slightly ADD-afflicted young man; I can't wait that long to fight again. With that, I did kickboxing on the side with Quyen Tran as my trainer. He also's trained Duk Kim, the current world kickboxing champion. I reached the pros first, he came after my time. Needless to say, going through all these sports undefeated, I was beginning to get bored. I mean, I love to fight, but if no one could even begin to challenge me, then what fun was there in it? Yeah, I think I'm better than a lot of people, but if I don't even have to prove it, then there's all the enjoyment just sucked right out. I actually quit fighting altogether following a motorcycle accident which screwed up my jaw, ribs, and left leg. I was coming home from a New Year's party. Usually I'm a fairly responsible guy, but I'd had just a little too much to drink that night. Took a curve too fast, wrapped the bike around a tree. Needless to say, I drive cars now. During the time off, I had the chance to rediscover the finer things in life with all that free time. The folks lived with me in Crete. I'd saved my earnings and fell in love with the little Mediterranean island. Bought a home there and everything. Now my estate plays host to some boxing matches, and 'Wild On' specials. Coming more to the present, I needed to get back out there and make some money. Not only was I now supporting my parents, but I was starting to pick up a tab of luxuries that needed to be paid off. The expenses added up, and I could only touch so much of my savings in one year. I was still in good shape, so I set my sights on RPW, which is a good place to start to get back into wrestling... For being a 'small' federation in some regards, the name talent was there. Violence Jack. Gideon Poe. Tessa Windsor. Magnum What's-His-Face. Wolfenden. Now THAT looked like it was worth my time. Looking at tapes from the RPW wrestlers, I tried to develop a full technique that would be the best for both effectiveness, and getting the crowd to like me. I'd learned from the previous professions that it's not always about how good you are, but how well the fans appreciate you. With that in mind, I created my own style that's as much about flash and flair as it is about hurting the other person. And now, I'm a wrestler who sells the naming rights of my special techniques to companies for a little extra cash while marketing myself better than Michael Jordan EVER could. It's all in a day's work when you're the World's Most Marketable Wrestler. Vangellus cracked his knuckles and neck as well. The registration process actually took a bit of time. He hadn't expected it to last so long. At most, he was anticipating a short explanation of just who he was, and then leaving triumphantly. But he actually had to work! 'So I'm in now. When do I start getting paid?' Gladys shook her head with a laugh. 'Oh, ho, you're far from 'in'. All this information needs to go up to the personnel department. They're the ones who will decide your fate.' 'What? I'm still not official after all that money I gave you?' 'Not at all. But thank you for the spending money, I had my sights on a luxury handbag.' Uttering an expletive under his breath, Vangellus turns on his heels. 'Well then, glad I could be charitable. You have a nice day.' He obviously didn't mean that, and it showed in his tone. Rather than staying and putting up with Gladys for another second, Mr. Oxios dragged himself toward the door and shoved it open. Immediately, his music kicked up again. 'Cut it out! I didn't get the job yet.' Instantly, everything stops. The horns die down individually, going off-key before the noise ceases. Apparently, he hired an orchestra to play him in and out of the building. Sadly, he wasted his money.
