Heart to Heart

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/12/03 The mid afternoon sun shone down through the unguarded innocent sky beckoning down into the view of Trent causing him to squint his eyes slightly and withdraw a pair of sunglasses hanging loosely from the V-Neck of his T-shirt. “Sky’s brighter than a damn evening in Iraq” Trent says nonchalantly as he slips his sunglasses on. Across from the small round coffee table a chuckle emanated from a man who seven weeks ago couldn’t’t even wiggle his toes. Gideon then slipped his own pair of sunglasses on and then took a deep breath. The crimson streaks in his long black silkily hair dazzled off the suns rays with glistening vitality . Trent was amazed how quickly his complexion had returned since leaving the hospital after Gideon walked, what Trent knew, were the longest 12 yards of Gideon’s life. “You’re appetite increased much” Trent asked while reclining slightly and folding his hands across his chest. “A little” Gideon replied while looking at the sun. “Hospital food through a straw will do that too you though” he smiled. Trent did as well, slightly. The mental image of his friend and guitarist laying crippled on a bed swallowing liquefied mashed potatoes through a straw didn’t really make his stomach rest easily, or his mind for that matter. “And training?” Trent asked. Gideon this time turned and looked at his long time friend and vocalist. “Good. After the hospital I pretty much spent two days on leg exercises. I’ve got thighs like a cheetah right now” Both men laughed at this, causing a few customers sitting outside the coffee shop to stare at them with confusion and anxiety. “Still hard though” Gideon continued. “I mean I can still nail a roundhouse, can still back flip and land on my feet, can still lift 230 plus pounds. But 20 minutes afterwards I feel like my spines been compressed to the size of a French fry.” “I know what you mean” Trent responded. He knew that Gideon knew he had no idea on what he meant describing the situation. But he felt Gideon knew he understood the basics, and was thankful he didn’t respond with one of his usual sarcastic mannerisms. Trent’s trail of thought was then broken by a small chirpy voice come from behind him, “Anything else?” Trent and Gideon looked up as a short brunette waitress placed a tray with two cups of coffee on the table before then. “That’s all thanks” Trent smiled affectionately while Gideon nodded in thanks. The waitress returned the smile, taking a few extra moments to stare at Trent in a comfortably dazed manner, before walking back inside the coffee shop. Silent moments slipped by as the men relaxed in confortable silence, Trent sipping his coffee while Gideon mused over the small amusing tweaks and annoying mannerisms and habits of the people around him, dipping his ear in and out of conversations concerning tax, politics, who was sleeping with who and who was going to win the superbowl before Trent broke back into conversation. “So, the paralysis genuinely was short term?” “Shorter then most of Vex’s relationships anyway” Gideon smiled while turning to Trent. Both men chuckled at the comic antics of they’re bass players past. “How is he anyway, and Maynard?” “He’s OK. Got in a bit of a ruckus the other night at a bar involving a girl. Nothing new there. Except this time the guy he thought was her boyfriend turned out to be her father.” “Jesus” Gideon gasped while a grin appeared over his face. “Yeah, 21 year olds look younger by the day. As for Maynard, he’s still pissed cause the rumour that Pearl were going to sponsor him with equipment turned out to be only that… a rumour.” Gideon chuckled while staring at his coffee cup and tracing his index finger over the rim of it. “They missed you ya’know. Still do.” “I know” Gideon said replied without looking up. He could see Trent smiling out of the corner of his eye. “Well, they did-” “I’m scared Trent” “Want to send you…..” The casual way Gideon slipped those three vital words into the conversation almost brushed by Trent like the cool afternoon breeze. He stopped talking and simply stared at Gideon feeling the pit in his stomach sink. He was hoping this was a ruse or a joke, but the way Gideon calmly looked at his coffee cup strongly suggested otherwise. “Say that again?” was all Trent could manage to squeeze out. “I’m scared” Gideon repeated with the calmness of an actor who has rehearsed his lines to perfection. “O-Of what?” “Right now?” Gideon this time looked up and leaned back in his chair. “Everything Trent, everything. I’m afraid of walking, I’m afraid of running, I’m afraid of breathing. Most of all …. I’m afraid of going back there.” “There? RPW?” “Yes” Gideon responded as if Trent should have known full well where “there” was. “You seen what Bruce said while I was in the hospital. I was at my best when I faced him and Maxwell, and they snapped my neck like a pretzel.” Gideon stared hard into Trent’s eyes now, sometimes Trent hated the way those eyes locked you into their gaze. “At my fucking best!” Gideon brought his fist down against the table hard enough to rattle the crockery and spill some of the coffee onto the table. A few people looked with shattered patience at the two men, neither even cared to notice. “Now I’m far from my best Trent. I’m never likely to be 100% again.” “Well don’t go back!” Trent whispered almost harshly, leaning in towards Gideon as he did so, making the two look like a young couple leaning in towards each other for their first kiss. “Walk away dammit! For once, maybe that’s the right thing for you to do. Maybe this time, it’s the ONLY thing you can do.” “I can’t! Can’t you see Trent. I’ll never get an ounce of sleep again. I’ll never be able to rest at ease. I’ll never be able to stop the laughter ringing through my ears, the silent hush of the fans as they watched my neck crumble beneath me! I’ll never be able, to get rid of Maxwell’s voice as he leaned down to me, breathed into my ear and whispered “I told you that you didn’t stand a chance …. Kid” Trent hung his head down and looked away from Gideon. He understood what he was saying, but that didn’t mean he had to like it, and it didn’t mean he had to play a part in it. “If I keep hearing those things ringing in my head for much longer they’ll lock me away in Juniper Hills Mental Hospital back in Derry before I even see my next birthday.” Trent didn’t respond. “But I’m scared. One more wrong tumble on my neck, and it’s over. Not just wrestling, but the band, walking, moving… my life Trent, my life.” “Well what am I supposed to do?!” Trent finally snapped back. People turned once again to look at them. “What, am I stopping you from talking about how Bush is ass fucking this country four ways from Thursday?!” Trent shouted to the onlookers, who gasped in shock before turning back to their chairs, some mouthing equally insulting comments back to Trent, who barely registered them. “Look, you’re afraid of Maxwell, you’re afraid of going back, you’re afraid of never sleeping again. Well you’re not the only one buddy.” Gideon knew immediately where this conversation was going, and immediately felt guilty and stupid for not seeing this coming sooner. He suddenly wished he could turn time back to10 minutes ago before he even decided to venture into this conversation. “There’s three guys who’ve known you for more years than we’d like to think about. Three guys who pray every night to, no doubt, three different God’s that you leave the ring with enough energy to walk and breathe the next morning without having to resort to painkillers and Oxycotin.” Trent paused to breathe while brushing some of his hair out of his face. “Now, you’ve never missed a practice, never missed a show, and never let this industry come too much between the band, and I don’t think I’ll ever know how you do it, but you deserve a sainthood for doing it.” Gideon smiled a little, and so did Trent. “However!” The smiles soon faded. “ If you think you’re the only one who’s scared about you going back into the firing line, then you’re wrong. We’re scared that once you go back in, you’re not going to come back out the same man.. That the next time you go back in, you’re not going to be able to pick up a guitar. That the next time you go back in, you’re not going to be able to pick up the pieces of your life.” Gideon nodded understandingly, and felt his heart buckle under the revelations Trent was pouring out onto him. “You’re scared of the exact same things pal, and whether you like it or not, there’s people going into shit creek without a paddle than just you. We’re there too. Me, You, Maynard and Vex. Cadaver Theory. All for one and one for all and all in the name of rock’n’roll …. Or something like that.” Gideon laughed and Trent joined him, it felt to good to laugh after all that was just said, all the stress of several years having just been poured out and emptied slightly within the last 15 minutes. “So, no matter what happens. You’ve still got us, it might not be a lot…… but tough shit” Gideon grinned, nodded and patted Trent firmly on the shoulder. “Nah, it’s more than I deserve, put it that way.” Trent nodded while drinking a bit more of his coffee. “I know, now put your hand down. If you wanna look like you play for the other side of the team that’s fine, but don’t make me look like I’m your quarter back.” Gideon lent back and laughed as if this was the funniest thing he had ever heard.
