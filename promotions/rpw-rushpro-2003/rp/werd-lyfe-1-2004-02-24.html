Date: Timeless 'Stop!' No, it wasn't Maxwell. It wasn't Roberts, either. I don't even know why you'd think that, silly, given the pitch and tone of the voice. Shrill, high, and piercing, the woman's call froze The Dread Pirate Roberts, yanking him to a sharp halt like an icecube sliding down his back. No one should be born with a voice that narking, that grating, and still boast of a tongue into their middle years. It was almost like a crow's alarming cawing, but instead of just rubbing nerves the wrong way, it was like a sandblaster bearing down on them. As Roberts turned a questioning look to the source, he quickly surmised that the clerk had a temperament to match the voice. 'Where do you think you're going?' she demanded as she came around her l-shaped desk, for all the world as if she was descending from her throne, disturbed for no good reason. Her bearing was that of a queen interrupted from her busy life of leisure to oversee an execution, except now that she was riled up, it would be her swinging the axe. Dyed raven hair bounced around her shoulders as she stalked up to him, crossing her arms and huffing for effect before she was even halfway to him. She levelled a glare at Roberts that should have incinerated him where he stood, though with her eyes squinted so tightly he wondered if she could even see him at all. Intrigued, he waved a gloved hand in front of her face to test the idea. She bristled with indignation, ready to launch into a tirade, so he yanked his hand back as if bitten and spoke before she could. 'I'm here to visit a friend, my dear lady,' he said, masking his apprehension with benevolent good cheer. 'And of course,' he continued smoothly, flourishing a bouquet of carnations from behind his back, 'I also brought you a gift, to assist in your arduous task of brightening such a grim place. A mere trifle beside your elegant beauty, to be sure, but every little bit helps.' He hoped his smile seemed genuine. The woman squinted at the flowers as if willing them to wilt, then turned a much harsher look on Roberts. 'What's with the mask?' She poked an accusatory finger into his chest. 'You're trying to rob us, aren't you?' By 'us', Roberts assumed she meant the hospital. He couldn't help but look around in bewilderment at the empty, sterile halls and waiting rooms, their only possessions heavy medical machinery he would need a truck to cart off and horribly out-dated magazines not even the fatally bored would deign to read. Knowing she must be joking, he decided to play along. He hung his head in shame as he confessed 'Oh yes. You've caught me. I've just been dying to get my hands on some catheters; getting up every single time you have to go to the washroom is for the birds.' He shrugged in mock apology for being such a scoundrel. It sailed right over her head. 'I should've known,' she whispered, then threw her head back and screamed, 'SECURITY!' Alarmed by her obstinate stupidity, Roberts reached out and grabbed her by the arm, his eyes wide and his mouth doing its damnedest not to pour forth the laughter that she so richly deserved in her face. 'You misunderstand! I was just joking with you . . . You know: haha, jokes, we laugh, all that good stuff?' She didn't look convinced. 'Then why are you wearing the mask? What are you trying to hide?' 'Why,' he began, lifting a hand to rub at his chin as his face split with a mischievous grin. He belied it by sounding as innocent as he possibly could. 'To hide my beautiful face, of course. It simply wouldn't do to have all of the nurses and female doctors distracted from their surgery and other duties because I was too inconsiderate to shield their lust. There would be anarchy.' Roberts was sure that would get a laugh, or at least an admonishing look. Instead the woman somehow narrowed her eyes even more, then nodded ruefully as if her suspicions had proven true. 'Only a thief would try to be so falsely disarming,' she stated matter-of-factly. 'I am not so easily fooled.' 'Please miss,' The DPR sighed hopelessly, spreading his arms out to the sides. 'I bear no ill-will toward you or your hospital. My friend has just awakened from a coma and I'm dying to see him and speak with him once more.' That was true enough. He eagerly wanted to tell Jason that he had managed to secure a match against that bastard Maxwell for Super Adrenaline. He would have his revenge for what had almost been done to him, and gain redemption for what had almost killed O'Neil. Jason would be exuberant, a feeling he must be sorely missing in a stagnant environment like this. It didn't seem he would ever get to share the news, though, if this turnip had her way. 'A likely story,' she muttered with a snort. Then, under her breath, she added, 'I can't believe he went to the trouble of finding out about a patient here. He's good, yeah, but no match for me. Katrina sees through all deception. Oh Yeah. Can't touch this.' She gasped when Roberts suddenly drew his sword, her eyes flashing knowing confirmation of his villainy. Fighting the urge to run her through and finally deliver her from her tormenting life of evil conspiracies, he held it out to her hilt-first, so that she could grasp the sword. 'Here: take my sword. It is worth a fair sum to any, and more than life itself to me. Take it so you know I will come back,' he paused long enough to give her a suggestive wink, 'and if you're still feeling so frisky, you can search me to make sure I'm not trying to make off with any of your priceless soaps and sponges.' Practically dripping with suspicion, she reached out to the blade as if expecting it to suddenly spin around and impale her. 'What trickery is this?' She examined the blade thoroughly, twisting it this way and that, then bit it, as if expecting to find her teeth sinking into plastic. Harrumphing loudly, she handed it back to him with a knowing sneer. 'As I thought! That blade couldn't cut butter!' Snatching the sword back, he was about to prove her wrong by piercing her own lard when, from completely out of nowhere, a raving lunatic in a straight-jacket bounded onto the scene, pushed along from behind by a tired looking orderly of considerable size. The maniac was cursing up a blue streak to match the fuzz covering his pointy head. The words were simple enough, but he shouted with such a fervour one would think he was spreading the Lord's word. As he foamed and spat, he seemed to be addressing the orderly. 'I swear all fuckin' day because it makes my ass bad! I fucked your fuckin' mom, bitch! Then I raped your dad! I'm the sensationalist motherfucker with a million ripe rhymes Who'll cuss you out not once or twice but a jillion fucking times!' The DPR listened with intent concern, riveted by the mental midget. The clerk shook her head; the orderly seemed embarassed. 'You get paid to shackle me in cloth and it makes your fuckin' day! You think it fuckin' makes you tough? Wake the fuck up, bitch -- YOU'RE GAY!!11! I'm the almighty fuckin' master of your motherfuckin' mom! You're a male fuckin' nurse! Could you get more fuckin' wrong?!' He railed against the orderly holding him as if he expected the beast of a man of tumble against his 100 pounds of skin and bones. The man ignored him as best he could, but the philippic was obviously beginning to wear on him. The DPR couldn't blame him in the least. He'd have snapped the idiot's neck long ago. 'I didn't know this place doubled as an asylum,' Roberts commented, nudging the clerk. 'Why's this retard here?' 'The police brought him in last night, saying they couldn't put up with him. They had him in general population, but even as all the guys were beating and raping him he kept right on talking shit. Nothing stops his mouth. It just goes on and one, senselessly. They figured we could better deal with him because we have heavy sedatives.' She sighed, shaking her head again. 'As you can see, they've worn off. We're releasing him back into the population today, but for obvious reasons we're restraining him until he's out of the building. If you ask me, I think he's faking the whole thing.' She nodded and nudged him back. 'For attention.' 'What's his name?' 'Gae Ceamen.' 'Gay Semen?! Ahahahahahahahaha!' Hearing the mocking laughter, the blue-haired monkey turned his waist-high glower to The DPR, then turned out his garbage filled mouth. 'Tremble at the fuckin' name, bitch! I'm a fuckin' bad mofo! Like your mom trembles for my fuckin' dick! With fuckin' pleasure! She's a ho! What's with the fuckin' mask, bitch -- Did you lose a fuckin' bet? Oh fuck! Wait! I get it now -- You're a fuckin' ass pirate!' 'Ignore him,' the clerk pleaded, tugging The DPR away. Or trying to, at least. Roberts ignored her pawing and took a step toward the foul-mouthed moron, arching an eyebrow in disbelief. 'Are you talking to me, you filthy Rico-imitating pissant?' Gae spat at him before launching into another stanza of pathetic nonsense. 'Do you see your fuckin' mom here, bitch? I sure fuckin' don't! The only fuckin' way I talk to her is with my fuckin' dick! In her throat! Of course I'm talking to you! That's why I keep fuckin' sayin' bitch! Now quit lookin' at Invincible Gae like you want him to scratch your ass itch! 'GAY. 'LIFE. 'BITCH!' As the tirade died with Ceamen swivelling his head around like a demented white Booker T about to do a spin-a-roonie, the orderly shook his head at DPR as if telling him not to bother. The DPR, though, found this darling attempt at wit too entertaining to pass up. After all, he pwned poetry. There was no way he could let even this shameful display go unremarked. Clearing his throat, he engaged the twit in his game. 'What's with all the cursing, man? What are you -- ten years old? You've the size to match the age and your shocking humour's oversold. Once the novelty wears off and all that's left is stupid you, You'll be lucky to land a job catching peanuts at the zoo. 'Luridness rapidly wears thin when it is posed to those with brains; Those aren't wings borne of your barbaric wit, you fool -- those are midcard chains. You're a blue haired little muppet with the charm of a headless chicken; And what's worse: you're physically challenged -- you're the black Woody Allen. 'You think you're dropping nukes but all you bring are stinking bombs. You're the only virgin ever to have mastered all our moms. In the future forget my name when you must press your weak attack. And oh yeah... Grover called... He wants his pubic hair back.' Patting the raging Gae's dyed sideburns with a sympathetic hand, Roberts moved past him and began making his way down the hall. Muffled outrage spewed around the large orderly's hand as he pushed the gagged maniac out of the hospital, in the process saving not only all of those within earshot from hearing more of his inane babble but also sparing him from having to expend the last of his wit coming up with more than a couple of dozen lines. The DPR whistled contentedly to himself as he moved down the hallway, but again he was stopped by a sharp cry from the clerk. 'Hold on!' she shrieked. 'You're not going anywhere until you take that mask off! I know you're a robber!' She came up and poked him in the back. Roberts was in no mood to berate yet another halfwit, so he seized the opportunity and crumpled to the ground when the finger touched him, as if all his bones had turned to dust. The clerk gasped and snatched her finger back, regarding it and the power it must hold with significant awe. It didn't last. Master of scrutiny that she is, the woman quickly surmised that this must be a ruse. 'Get up,' she hissed, poking Roberts with her foot. He jiggled with the impact but otherwise was stone-still. 'You're not fooling anyone! Get up!' Another nurse who happened by ran to a phone on the wall and called a red alert, and in moments a swarm of nurses was on the scene milling about like angry ants defending their home. Amidst the yelling there was a clatter and the sound of wheels on the lineoleum as a stretcher was hauled in. The clerk shook with rage, yelling at the nurses that the man was a fraud, and not to buy into his act. She stamped her foot like a small child, jutting out her lip in a pout and screaming that it wasn't fair. 'GET UP, DAMN YOU!' she screamed at The DPR, kicking him again, then throwing herself on his limp form, pummeling him with her delicate fists. The nurses began shouting at one another, then three pairs of hands were restraining the clerk, lifting her from the poor, injured Roberts and smushing her against the floor as others helped The Man in Black onto the stretcher and began running down the hall with him, checking his pulse as they went. They rounded a corner and brought him into a small room, then hurriedly went about trying to remove his shirt and trying to stick electrodes on him. 'Woah!' he shouted as he sprang from the bed, flailing his arms to knock all of the groping hands away. 'Easy now, ladies . . . There's enough Wesley for everyone.' 'Lay down!' one ordered. 'What's going on here?' another demanded, grabbing at his arm and trying to force him back to the stretcher-on-wheels. He resisted, shaking her hand off and raising his arms defensively. 'There's no need to be alarmed. I must have just blacked out for a second or something.' He began backing away from them, suddenly realising that they weren't going to let him leave of their own volition. 'I'll show myself out.' Balking towards them to throw them off guard, he turned and bolted from the room as they all jumped back. As he ran, he heard a herd of footsteps following, accompanied by urgent shouts to not let him escape without an examination. 'You can't leave! It's too dangerous!' came one of the voices, but Roberts let it get drowned out by the sound of his own rapid footsteps. Knowing it was a ruse, he didn't share their concern for his well-being. He had about the same amount of concern that the rotund little women would catch up to him. Crashing through a door marked 'stairs', he ascended them three at a time until he was at O'Neil's floor, then he hurriedly rushed to his friend's room. Looking back to make sure none of the nurses were there, he opened the door and scrambled through, then closed it behind him and leaned against the door. He expected silence to envelop him, or perhaps the low hum and beeping of machines helping Jason breathe and function, but instead there was a voice. A low, gruff voice, sounding agitated at his intrusion. He raised his eyes, suspicion fluttering up into his stomach as he instinctively reached for his sword. It didn't make sense for panic to grip him, but for some reason it did. His head scrolled up in slow motion. Just a doctor, he told himself, but he knew that wasn't right. Something was wrong. At first he saw O'Neil, sleeping peacefully in the bed, tubes and wires extending away from him like tentacles. Beside the bed, looming over him, was the one man Roberts feared he would see, grinning sinisterly at him like a wolf bent on defending his new-fallen prey. Today, Roberts knew as he embraced the rage within himself and shattered the silence with the metallic schwing of his sword being drawn, would be the day the villainy ended. Today, right here, right now, Peter Maxwell was going to die.
