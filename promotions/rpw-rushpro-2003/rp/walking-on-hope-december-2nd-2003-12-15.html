Walking On Hope - December 2nd

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Unless he walks the distance of those bars Trent, we can’t let him go. He may be rid of the short term paralysis, but his legs have been asleep and unused for the best part of the month, his legs have basically woken up from a coma and still have jack shit of an idea what’s going on” “That your professional diagnosis Doctor?” Trent asked as he look into Dr. River’s eyes, who was in charge of Gideon’s rehabilitation and physiotherapy process. “No, that’s my common sense diagnosis” he replied back, before walking over to where Gideon sat in the wheelchair at the foot of two long steel hand rails several feet apart from each other. The man who the world had come to know as The Shade had never look so much unlike himself. His usual long silky sleek jet black hair with it’s usual array of vibrant dyed streaks of colour now hung in a pony tail, clumped together lifelessly. The streaks faded to dismal shades that the original colours would usually hide themselves embarrassingly from. Gideon himself worse a pair of baggy black denim jeans. His bare chest’s various tattoo’s glimmered under the hospital lights like test subjects under a microscope. “Okay champ, you ready for this” Rivers asked. Gideon looked up at him, his eyes yelling that he could sense the fake hope covering his words. Rivers hung his eyes slightly, not wanting to look at those peering hypnotic blue eyes. Hair colour may fade, legs may not walk, anger may rise, but those eyes of his never lost an ounce of their beauty and majesty. Gideon wheeled the wheel chair closer to the bar, and reached his hands out and gripped one in each palm. Rivers began to walk to help Gideon to his feet, and he quickly shot him a look that said “Not if you want to see this months pay check”. Rivers backed back to his position. “You can do it baby” Trent said. Trent sounded like a cheerleader to him, and the quick image of Trent in a cheerleaders outfit both amused and disturbed Gideon, almost forcing a smile from the man who had locked himself away in the unmerciful darkly lit world of rehabilitation since he heard Rush Mason may terminate his contract if his physical ability didn’t improve quickly. Gideon took a deep breath and seized the bars tightly before hauling himself out of the chair, his face clenched in frustration and determination as the wind gratingly exhaled through his gritted teeth. Trent watched on with clenched suspense as Gideon rose from the chair and stood straight up on his feet, his arms slightly extended at the sides catching his balance. It was here that Trent noticed how much weight Gideon had lost since his rehabilitation state. It’s a long way back to the borderline, Gideon thought to himself. The borderline of being just Gideon Poe. If he overcame this walk, which seemed so bitterly ironic to him, a couple of fucking yards that even 3 year olds could walk with ease, if he could make this walk, something would be waiting for him. Mainly the expression of Maxwell and VJ when they seen him walk again. Walk right up to them and make them wish their mother’s had kept their legs closed all those years ago. Gideon lurched his right foot forward slowly, seemingly dragging an anvil behind it as he did so, following it through with his left and quickly gripping the bars as he did so. “Walk you bitch, walk” Trent whispered under his breath. Gideon lumbered his right leg forward again, letting go of the bars in the process and hauling his left leg forward again in quick succession. “That’s it, haul your damn ass” he whispered again that the words barely passed over his breath. Gideon latched onto the bars again as he lunged his right foot forward. Rivers looked at Trent and smiled, probably at the fact that Gideon had broken his current record of 4 steps with 5. Like that was anything to crack a bottle of Champagne over. Gideon looked ahead at him, his forehead doused in sweat which glimmered sickeningly under the hospital lights as he draped his left leg forward without the aid of the bars before stumbling slightly and using them to catch his balance. “That’s enough for one day, I think…” “No!” Trent interrupted Rivers. Rivers looked at him with short wearing impatience. “Trent, this-” “Now now!” Trent shoved past the doctor, and stood in front of the bars. Gideon looked up at him. His silence of words replaced by his defining screams of determination and fading hope simmering away inside. Trent rested his hands against the bars, and leaned in slightly/ “You want to let them win?” Gideon faced the floor. “Don’t you dare fucking look away from me ! I may not be your father but I can damn well treat you like a child if you’re going to sulk into a tantrum over this!” Gideon rolled his head up, looking at Trent with emotions Trent didn’t even want to try and decipher. He doubted any dictionary ever could either. “You want those bastards to win? I know you’ve stopped watching RPW, but I haven’t! They’re running it apart. Maxwell, they might as well give him the keys fucking North Carolina the way he’s playing everyone in their palm. You remember Pete Maxwell. The man you respected, the man you looked up to….. The man who played a part in SNAPPING YOUR FUCKING NECK!” Gideon eye’s lit up and he bolted his right leg forward snapping his hands away from the bars. “You want to live under the microscope?” Gideon shifted his left leg forward. “You want to be fed by someone who’s first name you don’t even know?” Gideon trusted his right leg forward and stamped it down. “You want to ever walk again?” Gideon looked up at him, his hair now loose of the ponytail and sticking to his sweat ridden face. “Walk, run, wrestle, play live, fight, make love? Even get revenge? Then come and walk over here.” Gideon raised his right leg, and struggled before setting it back down. Trent felt that hope slipping away again. “Come over here, and I’ll tell you how you were always nothing. How you were the reason that you held Cadaver Theory.” Gideon looked up slowly this time, his eyes questioning. “How you being forced into wrestling held us back!” Gideon’s expression turned to anger, and Trent was thankful that Gideon couldn’t tell these lies were hurting him as much to say as it was for him to hear. “How when you had the chance to turn back, YOU DIDN’T!” Gideon trusted onwards, letting go of the bars, right foot, left foot, right foot, left foot, One…Two…One ….Two. “You want me to tell you how you were ALWAYS the weakest link!” Gideon stared him dead in the eyes, nearly at the end, nearly towards Trent. “You want all of this…….. …..Then come and get me!” Gideon pursued forward, his legs now in rhythms, not holding onto any bars, not faltering, not wavering. “…Now WALK!” Gideon lurched forward, his face emblazoned in crimson as his eyes become slits under the pain and anguish running through his body at simply being to keep his balance walking. Trent seen River’s mouth almost sag to the floor as he held his clipboard. Closer Gideon came, inches and feet disappearing under his feet as his finally ran at Trent as he drew near. Trent’s eyes widened as Gideon reached out his arm and grabbed Trent by the collar and reared his right arm back in a clenched fist, Trent closed his eyes and prayed. His prayer was broken by the heavily grunting voice through exasperated breath. “Trent…” Trent opened his eyes, surprisingly taking note at how Gideon’s voice remained to keep all it’s deep well spoken intense integrity. Gideon smiled down at him through his long hair which encompassed most of his face. “Lies make Baby Jesus cry” Gideon then winked before collapsing into Trent who barely regained his senses to catch his lead guitarist and good friend. Trent then looked at Rivers as he slipped one of Gideon’s arms around his. “Good enough?” Rivers slowly nodded, still somewhat in awe. “Thank God for small mercies” Gideon turned his head to the left as he hung onto Trent for support, and smiled slightly. A grin that was smiling at the present….. As well as what was going to happen in the future.
