Cadaver Theory: A Breed Apart

13 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/11/03 ' This is about as much fun as batch of fucking herpes!' All three boys looked at Maynard, who was leaning his head out their changing room door where he could hear the small crowd mumbling between themselves to kill time and boredom. 'I hate suspense, seriously, I'm sure it causes early heart attacks or some shit like that. It's not healthy, it's ... it's..... Jesus, screw it, Vex you got any weed?' Vex smiled at Maynard while putting down his bass guitar. He reached down beside the chair he was sitting on in the small cramped dressing room and tossed him his drum sticks instead, which Maynard dropped clumsily, the three of them noticed this was because his hands were shaking quite furiously. 'Your mother teach you how to throw with that arm shithead?' snapped Maynard. 'You kiss your mother with that mouth Glam boy?' replied Vex effortlessly. Gideon looked up from tuning his guitar and spared a glance at Trent, who was chuckling quietly to himself. The band, as a whole had been officially together for three weeks. They'd been practicing everyday for those three weeks, 5 hours a day easily before hitting the town each night, not that there was such a thing in Bangor, and hitting the town for these guys consisted of , what would in many years, become a lost art, of chasing. None of them actually wanted to be the ones to ask an adult if they could go into the liquor store and buy them alcohol, of course they would have the money, and they'd even let the adult, whoever that may be, keep the change, It rarely worked. Yet Trent was usually the best at it, and whenever they did get lucky with whatever cheap alcohol they did manage to get, it was because of Trent. He was good with words and good with people, the qualities of a good front man I suppose you could say. Older college girls were his speciality, mainly because he looked their age, and mainly because if he didn't get them to buy their chosen poison, he'd usually get a number out of it. That was mainly why tonight's gig was occurring, all four of them were out of money, completely. 'Should have practiced more! It's too early, way too early!' wavered Maynard, his voice a clear sign of how nervous he was. The three of them were possibly set to take into consideration what he had just said. After all, the band on before them, Royal Roulette, had been pretty good, hardly any covers besides Black Sabbath's 'Children of The Grave', which even Trent had found himself tapping his foot and singing along too, much to Maynard's anger. 'Drummer couldn't hit a bulls ass if with a bass guitar, let alone a symbol'. All three knew he was lying when he said this, even Maynard knew himself. However, time for consideration was then cut off from the loud cheesy game show host voice of Randy Oswald, the guy who they arranged this gig tonight with. 'Okay ladies and gentleman, are you ready for the next act' bellowed Randy's voice. The place was filled with people of what had started to become known as the 'alternative' lifestyle of the surrounding areas of New England.. The four of them smiled at the humour of Randy addressing them as ladies and gentleman. 'I said are you readdddddy?!' A small cheer erupted. Mainly because by now the crowd wanted to hear anything but Randy's nails on a chalkboard sounding voice. 'Well here they are, from Bangor , Maine, just down the little old road from here, playing tonight for your pleasure! CABANNA THEORY!!!' Maynard's eyes turned red the instant the words escaped Randy's moth. He bolted from the door and ran down the small hallway, where a few steps at the end of it led to the stage. 'Cadaver theory! It's Cadaver Theory you dumb fat redneck!' The three other member were chasing after Maynard and finally caught up to him as he uttered the insult at Randy. The look on his face pretty much confirmed that they were about to be thrown out, when suddenly the crowd began breaking into hysterics and clapping and cheering. Maynard suddenly began smiling, and nodding at his new found approval from the crowd, his nerves suddenly disappearing. Randy himself acknowledged this, and bit his tongue on whatever he was about to say. 'Nice going smooth talker! If we ever want another gig here we've got to tear the fucking roof off now!' whispered Trent violently into his ear. The three of them then walked down the small wooden steps. Randy already staring daggers at them as they finally began plugging their instruments in. 'Hello folks, you all having a good time tonight?' A good responsive cheer. 'You all enjoy Royal Roulette earlier?' A louder cheer. It was a rule of thumb to get the crowd psyched up for forthcoming acts, and acts that had already been on. It was thanks to a booking error that Cadaver Theory were on after Royal Roulette, even more pressure on them to do a great performance. 'Good, well, we're Cadaver Theory, and we're...' '1-2-3-4!!!' Maynard suddenly shouted while tapping on his drumsticks, already tired of Trent's talking and Gideon, Maynard and Vex suddenly burst into one of their own songs, which Trent had named 'Running from Deliverance'. An adrenaline charged number that instantly got the crowd head banging and moving. Trent quickly gave Maynard a cold stare, which the drummer winked at, before turning to the microphone. 'Everything I try to say, always seems to get swept away! All my emotions you force me to keep at bay!' Trent began banging his head and tapping his foot to the drum beats Maynard was pounding out. 'The art of writing your own stuff' He recalled Maynard saying to him the other week. 'Nobody can tell if you fuck up when you play them for the first time' He hoped he was right. Time seemed to speed up. Trent's voice never wavered, Maynard's beats were in time, Vex's bass was liquid and solid, and Gideon's playing was fluent as always. Yet as much as Trent forced himself to look at the wall ahead, and as much as he wanted to hang his head while singing, it wasn't going to prevent the fact.... that the crowd was dead and still, motionless. 'You enjoy that one?' Stupid question Trent, very fucking stupid question his mind replied back. Silence. 'How many of you like REO Speedwagon?' asked Vex on his microphone, his attempt at humour was met by a bottle thrown at him which he narrowly avoided. Trent closed his eyes tight, he knew what was coming. A swarm of bottles began being tossed up at them, the crowd had a pretty deadly accuracy. Soon enough the stage became a target for every movable item in the bar. Gideon was content enough to let this go. He had no problem with packing his equipment up now, as much as it would eat away inside of him, but he'd put it down to a bad first gig and he'd swear to live to fight another day with this band. If the crowd were going to be assholes, so be it. That was.... until Randy Oswald began strutting over to the stage, a big broad grin on his face, seeming oblivious to the bottles that were hitting and staining his white shirt. No way.... not in this fucking lifetime was he going to let that scumbag end their gig. If their gig was going to end, it was because they were going to end it. Gideon rose to his feet, quickly plugged his guitar back in. The crowd weren't going to settle for their own stuff, not yet anyway. He knew what. That was why he suddenly found himself, without even hardly realising, that he was pounding out the opening chords to 'Breed' by Nirvana. For a split moment, everything fell silent and still, like a lullaby sung to a tired baby. They were quickly replaced by a defining cheer by the crowd and Randy Oswald's face turning a sickly shade of pale as Trent charged at him and pushed him with all his might into the crowd below, letting the crowd body surf the screaming and yelling Randy to the back of the bar. Trent would say he tripped, at least to Randy. Yet all four of them knew it was simply the adrenaline kicking into him as he truly slipped into his front man persona, which he was simply born with. Gideon noticed as he looked at Maynard that he was sticking his tongue out while almost snarling with his teeth at Royal Roulette who were watching from the side of the stage while flipping the drummer off. Whatever work Trent had done to make a unity with the band while getting the crowd to cheer for them had quickly dispersed. Gideon continued playing, looking at the crowd swarm over and around each other. Over the next few years with the band Gideon would see such sights as circle pits, slam dancing, and aerial roundhouse kicks being pulled off through the air and often wonder whatever the fuck happened to dancing. But right now, this moment, he knew nothing, at least hardly nothing was going to steal the limelight from this moment. 'Thank you' Trent said as they finished the song. The crowd roared back. 'Now, if you would be so kind as to drop all your drinks, cans and bottles, we're about to play another one of our songs' The crowd laughed, Gideon noticed one kid smash a glass bottle over his head and cheer. He thought that was a good sign... he hoped anyway. So they broke into one of their own songs 'Ruthless Reflection'. This time the crowd moved, and stayed moving. The rest, as they say, is history. After the set had finished Trent, Gideon, Vex and Maynard would sign their first autographs. Gideon's would be writing on a girls bare naked breast while hoping his blushing face didn't show too much. Maynard would finally get to third bass with a girl, Trent would taste his first Aftershock compliments of the barman, and Vex would make off with Royal Roulettes jack cables. All in the name of Rock' n' Roll as they say. Cadaver Theory were finally here.
