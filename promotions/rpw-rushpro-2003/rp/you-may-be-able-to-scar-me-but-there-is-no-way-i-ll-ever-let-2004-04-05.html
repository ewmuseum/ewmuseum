You May Be Able To Scar Me, But There is No Way I'll Ever Let You Beat Me.

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/04 It was a perfect day. It seemed nothing could go wrong. The sun was shining brightly and there were no clouds in sight. The snow had melted away and instead of a fiancée, I was a wife. On the other side of the window Christa, Alex, and Evan stood in the front yard playing a friendly game of baseball. Inside the house Chris and I lay inside my bed, wrapped up in white silk sheets. My head lay on his chest with my ocean blue eyes shut tightly, still tired from the explorations of a new world the night before. As my eyes began to open, Chris’ started to stir. His eyes opened and up he popped, pushing my head up from his chest. He jumped out of the bed and pulled a pair of his faded blue jeans over his smiley face boxers. He seemed in a rush, like he was forgetting something that he suddenly remembered yet he seemed relax, a grin on his face and his blue eyes lighting up the room. “Chris, where are you going?” I said as I brushed a lock of my light brown hair out of my eyes, looking over at Chris curious as to what the rush was. “Well Tessa Bear…” He started up as he pulled the zipper of his jeans up and then began to put on a brand new pair of black dress shoes that he had bought the day before. “Well what?” I cut in as I climbed out from under the sheets dressed in a pair of pink pajamas that Chris had bought for me just a few days after we had started dating. I walked over to him and wrapped my hands around his arm. “I’d hate to break your heart, but darling you’ve just became yet another victim of the Perfect Storm.” His arrogant grin lit up the room as he peeled my hands off his arms and then shoved me aside. “Huh? But…” My voice started to quiver and the words began to eat away at my mind. This couldn’t possibly be true. Something like this couldn’t be happening, but it was. “Indeed you do have a nice but and I’m damn sure the rest of the world would love to see it and well fortunately enough thanks to me they will finally get the chance to see just that and more.” His arrogant smile once again crept across his face. He winked at me showing that he truly had gotten what he came for as he reached behind the family portrait of the children, him, and me, and pulled out a camera. Waving his hand with it tight in his grip he began walking out of the room without a care in his heart. He got what he had come for, and now he was on his way. The only thing I could do was fall face first into the bed and cry. Cry because I had been so stupid and become yet another victim and been blind to what should have been so obvious. So there I laid my tears soaking the pillow and closed my eyes fading into a world of numbed thoughts. It’s almost scary how the things we fear most of dreams, can become a hellish reality. I could almost shoot myself for thinking that nightmares don’t come true, because the truth was that they do. It was contrary to everyone’s popular belief that the dreams that you wish, will come true, it was more like the dream that you wish would come true, didn’t. And the ones you never want to relive would come back to bite you in the ass while laughing at your clear ignorance and saying “You thought that just because you were taught otherwise it couldn’t become a reality?” My naivety had once again gotten the better of me. Just because I wanted to think that I’d live happily ever after, didn’t mean I would, and Chris proved that to be true when he walked out nearly a year ago. Ever since then all I’ve gotten to hear is how stupid I was to believe that he actually cared for me, and well I have to agree. Perhaps I was quite stupid to think a man like him would fall in love with a girl like me. But I’m not anymore stupid than Chris, who thinks that just because he beat me once, he can beat me anytime. If he thinks he can push me around like a puppet just because he’s beaten and bruised me both emotionally and physically, than he’s damn wrong. It’s just a shame for him that he won’t realize this until it is too late, and I’ve taken all that he has left, his pride and his Mid South Title.
