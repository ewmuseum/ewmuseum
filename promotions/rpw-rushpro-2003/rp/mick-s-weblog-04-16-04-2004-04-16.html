Mick's Weblog - 04/16/04

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/16/04 Hello everyone, and welcome to my official blog! For those of you who just came and are wondering just who the hell I am, my name is Mick Pepper, and I'm a wrestler in Rush Pro Wrestling as part of the tag team The Freak Show; along with my friend John, but we call him Mouse. Anyways, since a very few of you might be wondering about what's up, and some of you might be new fans, well, here's what's up. Both myself and Mouse have each had out first match in the RPW - a single's match each. Mouse won, whereas I had to do the J.O.B., as they say. A little miffed too. Not because I lost, but because that Silver guy wrestles quite stiff. Maybe it was just giving the new guy his lumps, but he didn't seem like the kind of guy to know how to control himself in the ring, you know? But that's past, lets look at the future! Coming up on Sunday, we have our first tag team match against Team Tradition. The rumors in the back say that these guys are probably not gonna be here much longer, I hope they don't decide to screw the match up because of that. Cause I wanna use this to get our name out and circulating. After all, we have what, 3 teams total? And we're the only team that's actually a team, the other guys are just singles teaming up. Doesn't sit right with me, but management isn't listening. A good showing in a tag match, with a good reaction from the fans, might change their mind on the situation. Here's hoping. Anyways, be sure to tune in Sunday to see the Rubberman and the big guy wreck havok on Team Tradition, and start the journey towards RPW gold. This is Mick, keep it real!
