Blink.Breathe.See.Repeat (Waking up in my own paralysis)

24 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... Sleep, slumber, therapeutically relaxed I'm there, swirling through those emotions, those elements as I awake, awake from the abyss. Yet I don't open my eyes, something, someone, no, definitely something - telling me not to open them. Warning me. If I ever want to keep my sanity, my will power, my self being in tact, then I can't open them, can't open these crystal blue eyes that have enchanted so many before. Something else says I must. My conscience? Pleading with me. DON'T! I must. YOU CAN'T! Got to open them. NO! Got to wake up! YOU MUSN'T! Got to face whatever's there. NO! I open. I awake. I face. Emulsion, white walls, stagnant smells, the echoes of footsteps on pale linoleum floors. Tubes, wires, monitors. NO! I remember. Blink Therapeutically numb. Breathe Unwantingly numb. See A tear rolls down my face, blurring my eyes. I taste, it, bitter, salty, corrosive. It rolls down my neck, tickling, teasing, tantalising. It slides down past my neck, down across my neck. ...... and I feel it no more. I feel nothing no more. BLINK Lost. BREATHE Gone. SEE Waking up..... in my own paralysis.
