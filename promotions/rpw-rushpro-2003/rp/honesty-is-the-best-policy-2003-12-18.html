Honesty is the best policy

18 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 18, 2003 This is how Chris Storm’s day would go if he was an honest person. Thankfully, he isn’t an honest person and doesn’t have to worry about any of this. The sun rises in the air casting light into the North Carolina apartment window of Chris Storm. The Perfect Storm rolls over trying to fight the sunlight. Alas, he has to wake up as his alarm clock goes off. He rolls over back to the alarm clock and shuts it off finally sitting up in his bed. Suddenly a strange woman rolls over and wraps her hands around Chris’ waist. Instead, Chris gets out of bed wearing only a pair of boxers. The woman sits up looking at Chris with puppy dog eyes. “Come back to be, I need someone to cuddle with.” She tells him. Chris just looks at her funny. With all the insensitiveness he can muster up, he replies to her, “Gaw you are a lot uglier in the light. Why the hell did I ever pull you? I must have been drunk off my ass. Get the hell out of my apartment and don’t let the door hit you where I stuck you.” The woman sits in the bed completely flabbergasted. Tears start to form in her eyes and she runs out of the room crying wearing just her bra and underwear. Chris just shrugs and finishes getting dressed in a black shirt with red flames and black leather pants. He grabs a brush off the dresser and looks at himself in the mirror. “I am one sexy bastard. Look at me I’m too hot for my shirt. I’m too sexy for my pants. No wonder I go clubbin’ in the VIP room.” He says with a smirk as he laughs. He tosses the brush back down and walks out of the room. Not bothering to eat breakfast he heads out of his apartment and to the elevator were he meets his neighbor tenant. Chris looks over at the person and smiles brightly. His ego can almost be seen as he tells the neighboring tenant, “Sorry if I kept you up last night. I was up all night slamming this bird from the VIP room. Though I must say that I’m pretty sure you didn’t mind since you probably whacked it imagined yourself pulled a chick that hot. Course since you look like something a pit bull chewed on I doubt you could ever get a girl that doesn’t look like yesterday’s shank.” The person stares at Chris pissed. He throws down his paper and marches back to his apartment slamming the door behind him. Chris just shrugs. “I guess some people just can’t take the truth.” The ding for the elevator sounds and the metal doors open. Chris steps into the elevator joining a little old lady who was already inside it. Chris looks down at the little old lady. She gives him a bright smile. “Man you are one old bat. So tell me, what was Jesus like as a teenager?” Storm asks her. The old lady is so offended that she presses for the nearest floor and exits the elevator. Chris just brushes the incident off and presses for the elevator doors to close. Finally, he reaches the ground level and exits the elevator. Chris leaves the apartment building and looks over to the valet parker. Chris nods to the person signaling for him to bring up his car. The valet parker nods back and leaves. Shortly there later he returns with Chris’ car. The parker gets out and holds the door open for Chris with one hand and his other hand extended for a tip. Chris looks at his extended hand and laughs. “What you want a tip? Invest in a blow up doll because parking cars won’t get you laid.” The teenager’s jaw drops as Chris slams the car door nearly catching his fingers. Chris speeds off in his car and into North Carolina traffic. After several minutes of stop and go traffic some jerk with a horn starts laying in on his horn. Chris looks in his rear view mirror at the well sized guy behind him in the truck laying on his horn. Having enough of it, Chris slams on his brakes and throws his car in park. He gets out of the car and starts tapping on the window of the truck. The man rolls down his window. “What the hell are you doing dipshit? Get back and your car and drive!” the man yells at him. Chris pays him no mind. “Look that horn is annoying the hell out of me. Either you stop it or I introduce your face to it. Got me?” He says calmly. The big man in the truck starts beeping his horn again just to annoy Chris. He smiles and looks at Chris. “And just who do you think you are?” he asks. “I R MINI-BODA!!!!!” screams Chris in his ear. The man nearly falls over in his seat. Chris smirks and walks back to his car, puts it in drive, and leaves the man in the truck stunned to what just happened. After a short drive, Chris arrives at the University of North Carolina campus. He parks the car in a spot marked for the handicap and gets out. Just as he was parking the car one of the campus police cruisers pulls up and notices him taking the handicap post. The policeman gets out his car and catches up with Chris. “Sir you need to move your car it is in a post reserved for the handicap.” The policeman tells him. Chris lowers the shades he had put on and looks at the cop. The policeman finally notices who he is. “Oh my you are Chris Storm? I remember watching you in WIW! You were great. Oh, you wouldn’t mind signing an autograph would you. I know you wrestlers are big into thanking your fans.” Chris lets out a slight laugh. “That is just what we tell the fans so they buy our stuff. You think we actually believe it? Man you must be handicap. Get out of my face I need to report to the arena for the show. That is a good one, the fans making the wrestler. Last time I checked no fan gave me this god-like talent.” Disgusted by his lack of friendliness the policeman lets Chris go. But while Chris is walking away he makes sure to put the ticket on his car. As Chris walks to the arena to get ready for the upcoming show one of the RPW interviewers spots him and runs up to him. “Chris, Chris can I get a word with you?” he asks. Chris sees him running up to him and stops. “Whoa you are ugly. Get away from me, your ugly might be contagious.” Chris tells him and walks right on past the interviewer. The interviewer brushes off the comment and tries to get a word from Chris. “Chris can you tell me what is going on between the former Schmoopies?” he asks. Now Chris stops and turns to face him. He smirks and lets out a cocky laugh. He had only one comment about this question. “Oh you will see, trust me this week will be very interesting. No matter what I will stand on the top at the end. Because after all I am Chris Storm it’s my job to be on top.” With that said Chris enters the arena. Still Sunday is about three more days away and Chris has plenty of time to put his plans in action whatever they will be. Joe Morris, Chris Storm, Sweet Illusion, Jackson Rust, Tessa Windsor, how much bigger can this get?
