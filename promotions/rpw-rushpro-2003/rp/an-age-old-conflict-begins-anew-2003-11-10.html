An Age-Old Conflict Begins Anew

10 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 - 10 - 03 Once upon a time, there was a country on the Arab Peninsula… The visage of a tranquil field, caked in the sun's browning light, fills your vision for miles. The glowing blades of grass dance like sensual figures in the brisk morning wind as nature's sweet voice courses through the gusty airwaves. Clouds float steadily through the fiery sky like puffs of smoke, casting their shadowy gaze upon the landscape. … which housed a race of people known as the "Ker'Mali". Astronomers. The sworn enemies of the powerful Byzantine Christian army at the time. The view quickly shifts to the glowing horizon, where the awakening sun peeks over the valley, covering the land in its light. All seems very peaceful to you. You feel very at ease… almost comfortable in this strange land. For many years, the two religion-fueled armies collided for a variety reasons ranging from trade to territory. But the REAL reason, the people knew… it was their beliefs. Suddenly, the sound of a distant rumble can be heard… like an approaching thunderstorm. Bits and crumbs of the landscape begin to jump about slightly as the impending, ominous sound begins to slowly creep closer and closer. The ground quakes harder as the rhythmic rumbling increases. Neither side could gain a distinct advantage with field battle and minor sieges. They knew they must commit to a huge climatic battle… right in the enemy's homeland. And the Christians were the first to acknowledge this. Like a great beast, a huge black shadow climbs up over the horizon, casting out a multi-headed silhouette upon the field. Upon closer inspection, through the sun's cascading light, you can see that it's a gigantic legion of heavy cavalry, adorned in shining steel armor and brandishing huge tower shields with the cross of Christ upon the face. Their horses wear metal faceplates and boast huge banners from the sides of their bodies. The army of horsemen looms across the horizon, stretching for miles. A few hold the mighty banner of the Byzantine Empire. The sounds of war echoed louder than ever before upon the winds. Wanting to finally crush the heathens, the Byzantine Cataphracts marched forward, with the intention to slaughter the entirety of the Ker'Mali. However, the Astronomers were prepared… However, upon the opposite horizon, a huge city looms towards the sky. Like a golden beacon of hope, the majestic city casts an aura across the field like no other. And below it, complimenting it like gemstones, stands the Ker'Mali Protectorate: The elite group of horsemen for the Ker'Mali. Decked out in obsidian black armor and carrying huge double-headed axes, the Ker'Mali armor looks honorable yet fearsome. However, the Byzantine army doesn't bat an eye. Ready to defend their homeland, the Ker'Mali stood on the brink of destiny, their capital looming in the background. They knew if Nota Ur were to fall, their entire legacy would come to an end. The mighty Cataphracts of Byzantine stood ready as well, carrying out what they believed was God's will: The slaughter of the Ker'Mali. For what seems like ages to you, the armies stand only 500 feet apart and stare at each other. Not a sound but the wind can be heard now that the Cataphracts have come to a halt. Legends speak of a hero that arises… to lead the Ker'Mali to their ultimate victory… Upon what appears to be a 15-foot tall black dragon-esque beast rides out a most imposing figure. Donned in royal Ker'Mali armor and brandishing a huge silver sword, the man stares through his opened cross helmet with a smirk and crystal blue eyes. A man of few words and great deeds, this hero was to lead the Ker'Mali into battle and defeat the entirety of the Byzantine Cataphracts. He was to ride into battle upon a blazen dragon, whose roar could crack the earth wide open. A mighty man indeed, this hero was the ultimate Ker'Mali hero… The hero raises his sword to the sky, and suddenly, everything seems to move in slow motion. The hero brings down his sword and gives a mighty cry, but only seconds later, its completely muffled by the huge yell of war as the Ker'Mali Protectorate charges down the battlefield. On the opposite end, the Cataphract commanders order lances readied and the first wave sent forward. The battle has begun. The sound of clashing steels and pained death cries fills the air as meteors come whistling from the heavens above, crashing down to the battlefield and flattening friend and foe. Products of the mighty Ker'Mali Templamur, the huge fiery rocks instill fear in the mighty Byzantine army… but instill no retreat. Wave upon wave of meteorites pummel the Cataphract army, but still they fight on, clashing with the Ker'Mali horsemen. Bodies drop to the bloodstained field left and right as cries of agony and death fill the air. The dragon-riding hero cuts through the enemy ranks, tearing them asunder with his mighty sword. However, things suddenly turn around… as meteorites now crush Ker'Mali soldiers. What should have been a glorious Ker'Mali victory… turned into a terrible defeat by betrayal. The hero stops in his tracks and looks on in horrible as legions of Ker'Mali horsemen are obliterated by the fiery rocks. The Cataphracts quickly regroup and pounce on their advantage, slowly breaking through the Protectorate ranks. The hero tries in vain to hold them back… but their numbers are too great… The Templamur, fearing the masses would turn on them for encouraging war once it was won, decided to crush the Protectorate at its finest moment, solidifying the peoples' fear in them. They had betrayed their soldiers… to keep their titles. Their beliefs were caput as their greed shown through. The hero turns towards the great Ker'Mali capital and stares with a great, burning hate in his eyes at the Templamur Citadel, standing taller than any other building. He does naught but stand as a meteorite comes crashing down upon him… then… blackness… The combined effort of the Christians and Templamur killed the Ker'Mali, nearly erasing them from history. The remaining Astronomers wandered the world, searching for any haven they could get from the murderous God-servants. Having their home, families, and purpose… it looked to be over for them… until… From the blackness, a figure suddenly creeps into view. With his eyes to the floor, one can barely make out the black trench coat he's wearing and the luster of his bald head. A new hero has arisen, promising to lead his people back to their past glory… and promising to crush any who resist. An age-old conflict burns once again. But this time, not between armies… but between one man… The man lifts his head and reveals himself to be Dorn Tracer. His crystal blue eyes shine in the darkness, the scowl on his face complimenting his dark look… … and his sworn enemy. The figure suddenly disappears and is replaced by Totakoze Salvador, standing defiantly like some mythological man of wondrous deeds. One fights for justice and the safety of his people, and the other hopes to shoot that grand dream down in what he believes is a crusade to rid the world of evil. Such was the battle all those years ago. And it's funny, isn't it? The Christians were wrong then… but they won. This time… They're still wrong… But now… they'll LOSE. The hero will not be crushed by the Templamur, or beaten by the Christians. In En'Flarim's violent light, a great wrath will be brought upon the heads of those who try to stop him. Woe unto them… and unto you, Tota… The conflict begins again. [End.]
