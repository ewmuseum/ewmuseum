Before Today #13 : Magz, Lies & Videotape (Part Three)

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/02/04 From his concealed vantage point, a grinning Magnum Wolf watched it all go down through his video camera’s view-finder. It was to be the final scene of his PWC exposes, the final trigger that would have Lawrence Wolfenden yanking his only daughter from the Pro Wrestling Circuit... And signal a victory for Magnum Wolf. Why? Because he didn't like her, simple as. 'Oh, this is just golden,' an ecstatic Magnum whispered to himself, hearing his lackey fumble through his lines just 10 feet away. Through a view-finder, the nervous fan was seen trying to block a bored Karina's path, keeping her in the conversation. 'So, they have you wrestling now?' the lackey blurted out though his almost scared laughter, just wishing it was over so he could get his money. 'Aren't you afraid you'll get hurt? I'd hate to see you... Damaged.' Magnum almost burst out laughing, and had to fight himself to stay silent and keep his camera on the action. Even through the small screen on the camera, Karina's body language was easy to read. She was used to doing as she pleased, and blowing off anything that didn't interest her... And having to deal with apparently horny fanboys certainly didn't grab her interest. With a sleazy manner that even Wolf would have been proud of, the man took a few steps towards Karina... Who simply crept her right hand down into her low-sung rucksack. 'Beauty is such a terrible thing to waste,' the man then confessed, apparently taking on the role of a bad Shakespearian actor. 'So it's important to know your place as a woman.' The line nearly had Wolf falling over with laughter inside his hideaway, finding his lackey's acting the height of hilarity. 'You go, slick,' he chuckled under his breath, waiting for the moment he'd been filming for... The moment his 100 bucks were paying for. With wide, Bambi-eyes, the man tilted his head to the side, looking right at Karina... While his right hand crept up onto her left breast. Karina didn't pull away, and instead just watched his hand settle, closing her eyes. 'Hmmmm,' the naive, and soon to be unfortunate, lackey pondered, 'I might be in here...' He leant forward, trying to plant a kiss on Karina... Who thrust her right knee sharply into the man's groin in chillingly casual manner. 'Ugh,' he groaned, a dull, agonising throb beginning to resonate from a crushed teste. His moaning got deeper as a tear ran down his left cheek, coupled with an overwhelming urge to vomit brewing deep in his stomach. Just about staying on his feet, Wolf's lackey could only watch on helplessly as Karina then whipped her rape alarm from her rucksack. Raising an eyebrow, just to rub the man's grave mistake in further, Kari thrust it out beside his ear... ...And pressed. 'SCCRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!' With an agonised cry, the man dropped to the floor, grabbing his left ear and thrashing his pained body about on the floor. All around, those caught off-guard by the high-pitched noise caught their breaths, having jumped in shock. Without saying a word, Karina simply took a few steps back, leant to the side to grab a quick look at the man's shell-shocked face, then turned on her toes and walked off. After Karina had departed to stunned silence from everyone around, Magnum Wolf scurried out and looked down at his moaning and groaning lackey. 'Not a bad job there, kid,' he beamed, not offering any aid. 'I'll just edit out her reaction, and I have the perfect ending!' His words were only met with another sobbing groan from the balled-up man at his feet. 'You okay?' Coughing and spluttering, the man rolled over, looking up at Wolf with pleading eyes. 'Where... Were... You?' 'Sorry, bro,' a beaming Magz casually dismissed him with, 'got here as quick as I could. Now, to get this baby ready to post, then it's one less Karina, and a few thousand more dollars in my bank account!' Magnum scooted off, while his lackey called out after him. 'My money?' 'What kind of man would I be if I gave you money, instead of the medical attention you need, huh?' Wolf exclaimed, before strolling off... Not even attempting to call for help as his lackey finally winced and vomited all over the floor.
