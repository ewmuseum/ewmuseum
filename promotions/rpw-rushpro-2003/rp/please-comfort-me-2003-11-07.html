Please Comfort Me

7 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/7/03 At twenty years old I've already learned how to deal with things the hard way. The real hard way if you ask some guys, but I really would not suggest asking them considering they would probably be too intoxicated to answer your question the right way. As a matter of fact most of the guys I hooked up with would probably who on I earth I was considering I don't talk to any of them much longer than one evening. But what does it matter? Boys are nothing but toys to me. I mean come on they seem to think that I care that they offered me a shoulder to cry on earlier today when I had found out that my father had passed away. Too bad they don't realize that I really don't care. Everyone dies at some time, today just happened to be that time for my father. Besides how could I be upset when he left behind a winning lottery ticket worth millions of dollars? I mean for fucks sake this is just the kind of luck I need. Now I won't even have to worry if my career in Rush Pro Wrestling is a complete failure. If worst comes to worst I'll just buy me a couple of male strippers and be in heaven for the rest of my life. Then again I guess I'll have to outsmart these lawyers first. "So Ms. Sanders, when your father passed away were you aware that he had purchased a winning lottery ticket the evening before?" Questioned my father's lawyer who was a rather good looking guy. I was not sure what attracted me to him more, his platinum blonde hair or his smile which caused his blue eyes to light up like fireworks on the forth of July. "No, I was not aware in the least bit. I never thought he was the gambling type." I replied with a complete lie. I knew my father gambled. I was aware that he had purchased a winning lottery ticket. The only thing I wasn't aware of is how he had died, but I really did not care to learn that. He was dead now which meant I inherited the fortune that would have been his, that was all that mattered to me. "You poor girl, I can't imagine how it feels to lose your father at such a young age." With sympathy overflowing from his heart that could not have been beating much longer than mine he stood up from the kitchen chair that had been facing me for nearly twenty minutes now and offered a hug. Of course I couldn't reject the chance to be in the arms of such a young handsome fellow so I leaned forward into his arms and melted into them, hoping that this might convince him to stay a bit longer. I mean what kind of sane guy wouldn't like to spend some time with a girl as beautiful as me. "It's the worst. I just wish I had someone to stay here with me and keep me company." I whispered into his ears while false tears slid down my cheeks. Hopefully this would convince him to stay, if not I'd have to resort to other measures. There was no way I was going to let a man as handsome as him just walk away. "If you like I'll stay here and keep you company." He offered while patting my back, attempting to comfort me from the sadness which was never really present in the first place. I wiped away the tears I had forced from my eyes and offered him a smile as I parted from his grip. My only hope was that this would make him feel like he was doing his job of cheering me up and would convince him to stay longer. "Thank you so much. You don't know how much it means to me to have someone here for me." I reached out and hugged him once more in gratitude, though I really was not grateful that he was here to console me, I was more thankful that he was staying which would give me a better chance to check him out. "It's no problem, I just ask one thing of you." He said as he looked into my eyes which startled me and left me wondering if perhaps he knew what I was up to. "And what is that?" I questioned, trying my best not to blow my cover considering I knew if I didn't do a good enough job of faking this sorrow he would leave in an instant which would leave me in a true devastated state. "Can you get me a glass of wine?" He replied with a smile, therefore releasing all the nervous tension that had begun to build up in my mind which could not stop focusing on him. "Of course! Why don't you make yourself at home inside of my room and I'll be right in with more than enough wine for the both of us." I giggled causing a smile to develop on both of our faces before I turned around and headed towards the kitchen. With a silent snicker I headed towards the refrigerator with an extra skip in my step. I had just set myself up for the perfect night, I was an absolute genius. All I needed was plenty of wine and the right words and I'd be in any girls heaven. "Oh Aly you are such a genius. Tonight is going to be the greatest night ever. Too bad I don't even know his name yet. Then again, does that really even matter? Tonight is going to be the greatest night EVER! I just hope that poor guy doesn't mind the major hangover he's going to have tomorrow. Then again how can he complain when he's going to wake up with this beauty by his side?" Caught up in thoughts I finally begun to realize that I had already had every thing I needed in hand within a matter of seconds, so what was I waiting for? Surely he had begun to think that as well so I shouted to him before he came looking for me. "Ready or not, here I come with all the wine you could possibly want!" I shouted out as I walked towards my room down the hall. Tonight was going to be perfect…too bad he'd be too trashed to remember it in the morning.
