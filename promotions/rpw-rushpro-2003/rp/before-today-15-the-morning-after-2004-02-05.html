Before Today #15 : The Morning After

5 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/04 As Karina’s tired eyes opened, the display from her digital bedside clock branded itself onto her retina. 11:43, later than she ever slept and half the day already gone. Sorrow masked her face, the path her mascara ran when mixed with her tears still dried onto her cheeks. ‘Ugh, what’s the point?’ Karina groaned rhetorically as it seemed she was about to rise, before just pulling the covers back over herself. The events of the night before didn’t just weigh heavy on her mind, they squashed it flat. When she rose yesterday morning she was Karina Marie Wolfenden, daughter of Lawrence and Sandra, not a care in the world and living a life of leisure... An existence oh so different to that of the Karina who woke with a splitting headache on the gloomy Sunday morning. A sad eye glanced out of her bedclothes, sheets huddled to Karina as her only defences, and looked once more to the clock where the date read “MAR 02”... Rent day was yesterday. Would the money even be in her account? Would her cards work? What did she have left? Behind the clock stood the bottle of Vodka she used to drown her sorrows, the impressive volume gone explaining why it was so an hour late that the Boarding Beauty had risen. Then, she finally decided to venture out of her comfort zone, and a foot swung out onto the floor. ‘Here’s to the first day of the rest of my life,’ she quipped, putting a depressed perspective on the positive saying. Tossing the sheets aside, Karina sat on the edge of her bed, a thin pillar of light that funnelled through the small gap in the curtains cutting across the thigh of her pyjama bottoms. With her head throbbing and body aching in accordance, Karina slowly rose and walked to the window, turning away and squinting as the curtains were ripped open, getting the hit of the lighting change over with. Shying away from the midday sun that only intensified the sharp pain running through her skull, Karina walked into the shadows that darkened the path to her en suite bathroom. Bare feet tingling as their soles touched the cold tiles, Karina looked into the mirror. Eyes that sparkled the previous day looked dull and muted, once bright shiny hair instead dull and frizzy. ‘I look how I feel.’ Karina's head shook in self pity as she turned on the tap, then made a bowl with her hands, splashing the water up into her face before shaking it off... An act that only aggravated her throbbing head. The water washed away the black tear streaks, but not the sadness, that remained. Standing, staring into the mirror, the Boarding Beauty looked a shell of her former self... No optimism, no determination, not even a look that said she wanted to persevere. With a hollow exhale, she wandered back into the bedroom, her one ray of hope being nullified as she looked at her answering machine where the red digital display proudly boasted 0 messages. She was orphan, no parents to speak of and only her meagre PWC earnings to survive on. For the first time in her life, she hated herself, hated how her love of a conflict led her to take such drastic measures, hating that she couldn’t just tell Magnum Wolf that he'd won. The situation was of her own making. Never one to second guess any of her actions, Karina now instead found herself asking so many things about what she did that night... Her self-assured persona eroded by fear. Should she of just left PWC? Why did her father talk to her that way? Was there some compromise she could have suggested to avoid all this? ‘I guess what’s done is done,’ she commented, leaning with her elbows on the windowsill, staring out into the brightness of the outside world from her shadowed den of self-doubt. Should she be angry at her father? After all, he was duped, tricked and manipulated by Magnum Wolf. Shown a highly edited tape to showcase the wrongs of PWC... But why would he take Magnum’s side over hers? Why wouldn’t he listen? Well, it was all academic now, she'd made her decision. Her father is no longer in her life... ...But Magnum Wolf was. In 6 days time she'd once again be in the same building as him, the man who broke her family apart through lies and misinformation. The bank account of Magnum Wolf may have a few extra thousand in it for what he did, but Karina was going to make him pay for every last cent of it. He expected her to run, he expected her to quit, he expected her to surrender. But she didn’t. As low as Karina felt, as much as she regretted not trying harder to talk Lawrence around... She held firm. Karina Wolfenden was finally a wrestler.
