The Things We Take For Granted

4 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/04 When I decided to make pro wrestling my career, I know ahead of time there were going to be aspects about the people and the business that I didn’t like. The same could have been said about any other career I chose to go into. The one thing that always bothered me though were people who took things for granted. I suppose I’d find this type of people anywhere I went, but I think I found the greatest abundance of them in the business of professional wrestling. It never really crossed my mind how many people in the business took things for granted until a portrait from my past spoke to me. It came to me in the form of a picture of Chris, the children, and me that still to this day rested on the mantle above the fireplace despite the happy days being long since over. It amazed me how Chris could just disregard two children who looked up to him as a role model just because he decided that he no longer wanted to be in my company. What amazed me even more is that he still somehow managed to fill the void created by abandoning the children, with two belts that apparently meant more to him than anything. Some people would kill to have such kids as wonderful as Christa and Evan, but Chris didn’t care. All he cared about was his belt. “Someday he’ll come to his senses.” I sighed under my breath as I diverted my attention away from the picture. Chris wasn’t the only on who took things for granted though. There were plenty of others who I could name off the top of my head. Just look at the people Chris would face this Sunday when he defended his Global Title. One of them decided the pressure was too much so he swallowed a bottle of pills. It would be a pity if he didn’t make it to the match due to his fear of defeat. It’s too bad Jess doesn’t realize how many people wish they had the pressure that came with that spot, mainly because they envied a chance to hold the spot he did. Of course to me that wasn’t a tragedy, it was mere stupidity. It would like me deciding to hang myself because I thought the pressure of facing Bob the Temp and Dawn McFarlane was too much, despite never facing them in the ring. I wasn’t the only one who thought that way though. I’m sure many others would agree with me on that point, mainly because I’m sure others would agree with me that Jess was taking what he had for granted. But hey it didn’t seem any one in that match really seemed to care all that much about it, which had me wondering if Dawn and Bob felt the same way about the match the three of us would have to decide which one of us would advance on to round two to face either Karina Wolfenden or Limp. “I wonder if Dawn and Bob really care about this, or are they just doing to get their fifteen minutes of fame?” The question never ceased to leave my mind as the time continued to tick down, bringing it closer to the time where the three of us would finally face off in the ring. Don’t get me wrong, I enjoyed the spotlight just as much as the common person, but I’d be insulted if Bob and Dawn were in this tournament for that reason alone. I wasn’t obsessive about the business, but at the same time I couldn’t stand to see someone win something they didn’t care about. They’d be taken what they had for granted, which is something I wouldn’t forgive myself for if I allowed them to win and let that happen. Come to think of it, I already couldn’t stand Bob. He represented just about everything I couldn’t tolerate in a guy. His attitude was one that I had begun to recognize as the rookie male attitude, the one that the males who won’t get anywhere carry. You know, the attitude that basically claims that just because he’s facing a girl, or two girls for that matter, he’d have it easy……what a joke. It’s no wonder this guys stuck managing a nobody Bob can say what he wants though. When it all comes to an end and he’s left jobless because he couldn’t beat two girls, he’ll see that taking his manager job for granted really was a big mistake. But if we want to get onto the topic of mistakes, we can talk about what Dawn is doing right now. I almost wanted to pity her for falling into the same trap I fell into. The trap that I was reminded of everyday when I woke up in the form of two children questioning, “When is Chris coming back?” Perhaps it was jealousy that bit my soul whenever I heard of Dawn and the man she had fallen in love with, or maybe even pity for the girl. But all I knew is that I didn’t want to hear another thing about her seeming not to really care all that much about the match because she was too busy romanticizing with some guy. I know I probably should be glad for Dawn that she very well might have found the love of her life in that guy, but that’s kind of hard to do when the guy I once loved and still do in a way is too busy trying to end my career while telling me how useless I was and still am. “You don’t know how lucky you are Dawn.” Though Dawn wasn’t around to hear my whisper, I hoped that perhaps she knew what I was thinking this very moment as I once again directed a glance towards the picture that depicted the fair tale life I once had. I was hoping that perhaps she understood how much a chance to advance in this tournament meant to me, and how much more jealous I’d grow if she took that away from me. It’s not that I wanted her to forfeit or anything, I’d just hope that she wouldn’t cheat to win when she already has everything I wish I had. And even though Dawn seemed to have nothing she took for granted I’d always see it that way anyways, just because she had the perfect life. She had everything I wanted, and everything I took for granted when I had it. Perhaps that’s why that every time I heard the mention of her name, people would seethat unusual gleam of jealousy that no one would expect to come from me, someone who they related to as a compassionate person who cared about everyone. But what they don’t understand is that everyone always has that one person they wish they could be. For me that one person was Dawn, which is why I’d make sure that seeing as though I couldn’t be Dawn, I’d take from her life what I could have, the chance to advance to the second round of the Cruiserweight Tournament.
