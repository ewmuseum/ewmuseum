Beginning of the Fall

19 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 19, 2004 It has long been common sense to avoid the dark alleyways. You never know what scum makes its home in the shadows. Those narrow spaces have been home to hundreds of crimes. Void of light, damp and filthy, an audible thud echoed from the alleyway. The alleyway stood between an old gym and a long abandoned apartment complex. On one side of the alleyway a black car was parked. Nothing unique about the car, it was like hundreds of others. It would be hard to pick out in a street littered with traffic. The sounds of the thuds start to get mixed groans of pain. A voice rang out over the sounds emitting from the alleyway. “Maybe next time you will understand orders a little better.” The voice contained a thick Italian accent. He said it like it was not a question but rather a fact. An even louder thud followed as a man could be heard coughing violently. There was a flicker of light, from a solo light bulb hanging over the back exit stairs of the old gym. Four figures could be made out. One standing to the side, he was mostly shrouded in shadows. The other three completed the triangle, with one before the victim while the other held him. A gym bag laid on the ground beside the victim, its contents spilled out onto the grimy concrete. It was clear this attack came as a surprise to the victim. There was another momentary flicker of light. The victim’s face could be seen for a split second. His blonde hair stuck to his face, those blue eyes were dimmed with pain. The victim was a man who was supposed to be in the highlight of his career, Chris Storm. Chris did not look to be in his highlight. It was clear, whoever these people were they meant business. Chris was being held with both arms hooked behind his back. Sweat dripped from his body, not from the beating but from the fact that he had been working out intensely for his upcoming Global title match. He knew he had to be in his best shape ever to defeat the two men he had to face. Like never before Chris had pushed his body. Sadly it was the intense workout that made Chris too weak to even fight back against these shadow men that assaulted him. “When you told to take a dive it is not a suggestion. You understand?” The person standing off to the side said. He had been the one talking before. Whoever he was, he seemed to be very well educated, speaking in proper grammar. As if a cue, the man standing before Chris started to rain down with more vicious punches. One of Chris’ arms had been released. The hand that had previously been holding the arm back shot up and grabbed Chris’ hair, jerking it back. Chris’ head was forced to tilt back so that he would have to look up at the man assaulting him. The air seemed to snap, the man who delivered punch after punch cracked his knuckles. Like a beacon of Chris’ spirit, the light bulb flickered weakly again. The spit second of light had been enough for Chris to see the face of his attacker. He had a thin, long face that seemed to be ghostly white. His hair was white, slicked back and made into a ponytail. The white hair served as a reminder of how much he hated white hair. The sight of this man’s face burned into Chris’ memory banks. Chris just knew one day he would get his revenge back, even if he could not do it now. “Are we getting through to you, numbskull? Or do we have to reiterate our point again?” That Italian accent became like poison to Chris’ ears. Each time he heard it he knew what would come next. SMACK, SMACK, SMACK! Three brutal punches, following each other, came down on Chris’ tilted face. Blood rolled down his lip, dripping onto the ground. A small red trail remained behind. The iron grip on Chris’ hair yanked again, forcing his head back up. The hair had nearly been ripped from the skull. The fist came up again, looking like a train coming out of the tunnel. Chris flinched, readying himself for another assault. The fist never came though. Instead, an assault of laughter came. It had not been enough for them to physically beat him, they had to humiliate him and take away the last ounce of dignity he had. Chris may not have been able to stop the battering of abuse but he would not allow them to walk away with his dignity as well. His head still forcefully tilted back. He mustered all his energy he had left inside him and spat on the man before him. “Take a dive to Adam Jacobs? Ha! Bet against me and it’s your own fault. Don’t you know who you are messing with?” he fired back at them. He could not hold himself up but at least he could hold his head up proud. “Yeah, yeah, we know who we are messing with, Chris F’ning Storm. Well Mr. Storm you do not seem so high and mighty now.” The voice rattled with a hiss. With a simple motion of his head he wiped the spit away. The other hand came back and delivered a smack to the side of Chris’ face. The person that had held Chris the entire time released him. No longer being held up, Chris dropped face first onto the disgusting ground. His ocean blue eyes had no life in them. It was just a void, like endlessly stretching water then seemed to go on forever. His senses failed him. All he could do was listen to them talk. “So what do we do with him now?” One of them asked. The voice seemed to be coming from behind him; Chris deduced it came from the man that had been holding him. “The boss said to just teach him a little lesson. After all, he has to be able to pay us back.” The words came from the man that normally talked for them. “But I want this fucker to learn not to mess with my girl!” Chris could not see what happened, but he could feel it. The man who had just yelled at him had driven his foot into Chris’ ribs. The impact forced Chris to roll over onto his back, staring up at the starless sky. Chris could not keep his eyes open anymore, with the last of his energy his eyelids shut. The image he saw before his eyes closed would never leave him. The light bulb tried to flicker again but it faded out. With their job done, the three men entered their car and drove off leaving Chris to bleed in the dark alleyway. The light bulb that served as a beacon for Chris’ battered spirit again flickered. The alloy became brighter slowly. The shadows were chased off by the light emitting from the soft white bulb. Somewhere inside him Chris found the strength needed to sit up. He turned and came to his feet. An unexpected coughing fit had him doubling over, spewing blood and phlegm. Chris picked his head up and looked at the brick wall of the abandoned apartment building. He saw that image before his eyes had closed. It would haunt him in his sleep. Whenever he closed his eyes he would see it. The image of a woman crucified had been spray painted onto the brick wall. The woman was frightfully familiar. Maybe it was the fact that she looked so familiar that made it haunting. Regardless it had cost him many hours of sleep. Chris grabbed his gym bag and pulled himself upright. He leaned against the wall of the apartment building for support as he made his way back to his own car. Disgusting sewer rats came up from their mucky homes to lick up the blood lift behind by Chris. He threw his gym bag in the passenger seat and grabbed the keys from inside the bag. The roar of the engine was the opposite of how he felt at the moment. Chris slowly drove back to his apartment, in his current state he was in no rush to get anywhere. Even in the dark of night the roads were cluttered with cars. New York was the city that never slept, no wonder there was so much crime. A used 1996 Cadillac Deville pulled into the parking garage of the apartment building Chris stayed at. Chris stumbled as he walked, the lost of blood already started to affect him. He had to walk with a hand against the nearest wall so he would not fall over. When he entered his apartment the sight before him was not what he had hoped to see. His knees went weak and his stomach turned. He thought he was going to be sick. Chris’ New York apartment had been trashed. Everything of value was destroyed, a broken shell of its former self. His leather couch slashed, his 25 in’ TV smashed, and video collection ripped up, it nearly drove Chris to the brink of tears. He did not notice he left the door wide open. “Ring, ring, ring..” The sound of the phone ringing made Chris’ heart jump. His hand shook as he reached for the phone. Chris took a deep breath and steadied his hand. “Hello?” his voice was unusually weak and low. “We’ll be looking for our money soon. You have two weeks to come up with the money. As you already noticed, we rearranged your apartment for you. This is a reminder that we are not playing around Mr. Storm. $15,000 is what you owe. Good-bye.” The line went dead. The voice had a strong Italian accent, but he had not heard it earlier. It made Chris wonder just how many people were after him now. “Man this place is thrashed! Why didn’t you tell me you was throwing a party, I feel ripped that I didn’t get invited.” Chris turned, holding up both fist ready to fight. He knew he was in no shape to even defend himself but Chris’ pride would never let him show his true weakness. Luckily it was no mobster or two-bit thug. In fact this man could never make it in the mob even if he tried. His manager, Arizona walked in through the open door. Like the bum he is, Arizona did not even bother to close it. “So champ had fun training for that Final Fantasy dude and his bitch?” Arizona asked. When he got closer to Chris he could see the already formed bruises and welts on his face. “Oh man, it does look like you had fun. What happened, you stood in the way of a train?” Arizona pulled out a kitchen chair for Chris to sit in. Chris gladly accepted, letting his weary body collapse in the chair. Never had it felt so good to get off his feet. Chris could let his head drop with his faithful manager around. Arizona walked back into the kitchen and wetted a wash cloth for Chris’ busted lip. “I got jumped after I left the gym. Three grease bags jumped me. They try it again and I’ll shave their mustaches off. You won’t be able to tell them from their mothers.” Chris just snarled as he remembered the events that transpired only an hour ago. Arizona pulled a chair up in front of Chris. “Are you going to be able to compete in the Global title match?” He knew his friend was in bad shape but he could not help but think about the match that could easily be the biggest of his career. It is not every day you get to go after the Global title and Seymour Almasy. Chris nearly jumped out of his chair. Arizona had to stop him by placing his hand on his shoulder. The blood started to rush at the idea of forfeited. The damp cloth turned crimson red. “If you are insisting that I can’t beat Seymour or AWS Man then you are sadly mistaken!” Chris was screaming at his friend as he pointed at him. “Whoa champ, I simply meant are you sure those thugs won’t be coming for you again?” Arizona tried to calm him down. “Chill man, right now wrestling should not be your top concern. You just got messed up by the Mafia. When the Mafia starts messing with you, you’re in some deep shit.” Arizona’s words of warning fell on deaf ears. Chris Storm was born a wrestler. He grew up a wrestler. At times it seemed Chris did not know a world existed outside wrestling. Now had been one of those times. “The Mafia can suck my dick! They want their $15,000 from me…” “$15,000! You owe them $15,000! How in the bloody hell do you owe them that much money?” Arizona’s jaw hung to the floor and his eyes bugged out. Chris became very uncomfortable. Arizona could tell Chris was thinking about it, the wrinkle between his eyes gave it away. “Remember the Adam Jacobs match?” Chris started off. “Well it turns out some Mafia bitch put some dough on Jacobs. Come match time I had these two jokers in suits tell me ‘it would be in my best interest to take a dive’. I told them to suck my cock. Turns out that wasn’t the smartest thing to do.” The pieces of the puzzle came together. “So when you won and they lost their money they blamed you, and want you to pay them back? Damn that sucks. Well I gotta go.” Arizona said quickly standing back up. Chris looked shocked. “What? You’re leaving! Where are you going?” He asked. Arizona turned and looked at him as he walked out of the door. “Dude you’re a marked man. I’m getting the hell out of here before they see me too!” The door slammed leaving Chris alone in his wrecked apartment. There was nothing but silence. Those fists came down on him like runaway trains. Now the train derailed in his apartment, destroying everything he held of value. But the train had passed now leaving only silence in its wake. He walked over to what had been his coffee table in front of the leather couch. Broken shreds of glass littered the floor, broken wooden pieces clustered around them. Chris dropped down to his knees and held up two of the larger pieces of glass and looked at them. His reflection stared back at him. Chris saw what he looked like now. No not the proud Chris Storm that once stood. The Chris Storm that knew he could take on the world and suplex his way to the throne was gone. Instead there was a man as broken as the things he treasured. His face was beaten badly. Large welts colored his face black and blue. He could hardly even open his right eye. A small cut had been opened over his eye and on the corner of his mouth. In a fit of rage Chris smashed the glass on the floor. He hated what he saw. The man in the reflection could never defeat Seymour Almasy and AWS Man. The man in the reflection would be luck to even get a shot at the Global title. It was not Chris Storm that looked back at him, he refused to admit it! Chris picked himself up from his knees and dragged his body into what had been his bedroom. All his clothes had been pulled his dresser and closet and ripped to pieces. His bed has been flipped over. Chris was too weak to even bother to flip the bed back over. He walked to the window and looked out at the dark city. He knew out there somewhere his opponents were going through their own lives. What was Seymour Almasy or AWS Man doing right now? Despite the hardships that faced Chris he started to laugh. His thoughts drifted to RPW. He punked out Seymour which helped finally land his spot in a Global title match. What made him laugh though was that Seymour Almasy was openly obsessed with facing Chryste. Chris faced Chryste week in and week out over the past two months in RPW. He may not be able to lay claim to the Global title just yet, but he knew the fact that Chris had a match against Chryste got under Seymour’s skin. He would be sure to expose that to weaken the champion. His breath steamed up the window. Chris had let out a big sigh. He was tired; the day had been very long. Now Chris just wanted to sleep. Maybe he would close his eyes and all it would disappear. He would wake up and everything would be back to normal. There would be no smashed coffee tables, no ripped clothes, and no slashed couches. It would just be Chris rejoicing in his career filled with promise. Something he worked on day in and out since the doors of WIW closed. “Please God, I know I never do this, but just once I need your help. I need the strength to carry on. I need the strength to defeat my enemies. Please God, grant me this and we can go back to ignoring each other’s existence. Amen.” Chris closed his eyes and lay down to sleep on a bed that had been flipped over. The man once so proud, now reduced to begging for help from someone he never acknowledged existed before. But this was far from over. Get your rest Chris, you will need it.
