I'll Be Back...

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-20-04 To Whom it May Concern: I should apologize to all my fans out there, and really to anyone who supports RPW. I came aboard to this promotion to lend a hand in making it a success, but I let my personal vendetta take control of me. I admit it, I came here to get even with Jason O'Neil, and I'm letting it cloud my judgment. I showed up to Adrenaline a few weeks with Kimbusa, and was told at the door that Mr. Maxwell wouldn't be needing us that evening. To be told that our services couldn't be used to help make this fledgling promotion a success was a slap in the face directly from Peter Maxwell. I know he's looking for retribution as well; I once told him he wouldn't be main eventing my promotion, and now he's turning the tables. For the past few weeks, Kimbusa and I have gone our separate ways, searching for a little clarity. I write to the fans of RPW now to assure you all that we have come to a few conclusions, and we're ready to do what we came here to do. I was wrong to have stayed away. Had I tried hard enough, I could have walked through that door and confronted Maxwell. This is about the most personal feud I've ever been a part of, and quite frankly I'm ready to end it. Unfortunately, it seems I had forgotten how to do that. My first instinct was to take it to the lawyers. But then Kimbusa reminded me of something; I came to RPW to rediscover Steven Caldera the wrestler, not to reinforce Steven Caldera to pencil pusher. I was once heralded as one of the best wrestlers in the world. I think it's time I reminded the world, and myself, that Steven Caldera is still capable of putting on a hell of a show. I'm not sure what the future holds now. I'm not sure how to get myself back into the mix. Kimbusa will be back, although when I can't say. I myself have a few loose ends to tie up first, and then I'll throw myself at the mercy of the RPW board and ask to come back. I'm not coming back for vengeance. If I get my shot at Jason O'Neil and Peter Maxwell, you'd better believe I'd destroy them both without a second thought. But I need to be in this for myself first. I'm here to prove to myself and to the world that I am still a world-class athlete. I don't know when, I don't know where, but Steven Caldera will return. -SC
