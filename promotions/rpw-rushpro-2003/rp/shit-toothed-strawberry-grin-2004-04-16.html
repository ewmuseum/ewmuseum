Shit-Toothed, Strawberry Grin.

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/16 It had been raining when I last saw him… a lightning storm, even. Fitting for what the meeting was worth: tumultuous, angry, bitter, quick. I’d hoped, while hovering over his still living carcass, that a sudden power surge would fuck up the equipment still keeping him alive. Unfortunate that it never happened. Better luck next time… assuming there is one. I’d hope there isn’t, but hoping has proved fruitless, hasn’t it? Anyway, I stood above him sopping wet, beads of moisture rolling off my tearless face onto his rotting frame, wishing him death. His yellow eyes peered up at me as he gummed in satisfaction, the cock-sucking smirk he wore serving what he thought well; “I’m still alive, you little shit.” I ran my hand through my hair coolly and slung the handful of water down onto his face in defiance. A polite, innocent, shocked expression. “Sorry, Grandfather. Didn’t mean to get you wet.” He reacted as if poison has touched his skin, squirming and coughing beneath the covers of his temple of sheets. I turned and dropped my peacoat on the “visitors” chair, concealing a smirk. Should he have seen it, the meeting would have been cut short. The fuck was quick to the nurse-call button. Apparently I was a disturbance on many other occasions. Regardless, I couldn’t end the little shindig early this time: business. “The fuck you didn’t, you Goddamn pissant. If your whore of a mother kept her legs clo—” “ARE YOU FEELING BETTER, GRANDFATHER?!” I was quick to cut his diatribe off. My mother and I have a very good relationship, and if he’d gotten out his typical ‘your mother is a whore, you shouldn’t have been born, I hope you get syphilis and kill yourself thanks to dementia’ speech, the nurse would have had to been called in. Feet have to be surgically removed from asses, you see. “Heh… I’m dyin’, ya little cocksucker. Other than that, I’m fuckin’ gravy.” The man cursed like a sailor. Dropping onto the chair, I used one armrest as a recline and stared at the man I had come to loathe with intensity, watching the Brillo of chest hair that seeped through his nightgown rise and fall with his raspy breaths. From within the back pocket of the jeans I wore, I produced a slip of paper. “Mmm… I figured as much.” He coughed, “It’s always about money with you. Your mother was a mooch and your father was a stupid fuck of a gambler, you know that? Damned from conception.” Angered then. “Whatever, old man. I upheld your bullshit end of the bargain.” The paper was handed over. With printed-up lenses, he examined it briskly, checking out the name of the author and the company from which it came. With a raised eyebrow, he smirked and crumpled the check into a ball of paper. A soft thud, it connected with my forehead and dropped onto the crotch of my pants. “My bullshit end of the bargain? You, maggot, came in here with money from a bullshit company. I pay for your fucking education and this is what you come back with? Disgraceful.” I shook the hair from my face and pocketed the week’s pay. “For a dying piece of shit like you, you sure have a lot of words to say.” He rolled his eyes and reached for a bowl on the desk that sat next to his bed. Fingering around for one of those individually wrapped pieces of strawberry candy (the asshole had no teeth, but he had some strange drawing to those sweets), he drew back and empty hand and turned back to face me. There he saw my opened mouth, an oval of red lying on the tip of my tongue. Drawing it inward, a crunch between the teeth, a satisfied grin. “Tasty.” He smirked evilly. I had an intuition that he wouldn’t give me the inheritance, even if I worked for it (the sole condition of earning it) before I had entered the building that night. I wanted to tell him to fuck off, though. That was this business at hand. I’d hoped I’d kill him of a broken heart, not something typical like cancer. “I’ll be sure to buy all I can with your inheritance money.” I smiled. “Great, and I’ll be sure to throw in a buck or two for the sorry guy who has to haul your ass to the crematorium for baking. How much do you think I can get for the soap that’s likely to come from your shit-festering remains? I think ‘Scent de Asshole’ is popular among the young people these days.” He reached for the button to call for the nurse. “Or I can bury you in my backyard, but I don’t think the roses or azaleas would much like the weeds that are likely to surface. Hell, I can tolerate the smell of manure, but you…” And it was pushed. He screamed in false agony, cursing my name, yelling out all the atrocities I never committed. In fumbled the nurse, her attire stretched beneath the girth of her childbearing frame. I felt sorry for her, and didn’t struggle as she began to whisk me out of the room. Grabbing hold of my coat, I slung it over my shoulder and continued my verbal assault. “I’ll laugh when you die, ‘martyr.’” “Get ‘im out of here!” “I’ll piss on your grave to match the shit in your teeth, you fucking asshole!” At the frame of the door, I extended my middle finger. The last goodbye wave I’m likely to ever give that worthless son of a bitch. Every day I clamber into the apartment and check the messages on the phone, hoping that it’s the nurse with the announcement that will make life that much easier. Until then, so it goes.
