Before Today #04 : First Impressions (Part One)

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/12/03 February 1st, 2003. 'Rowan University - Esby Gymnasium' Magnum Wolf read the sign and shuddered. His career had come to this. From being a cult favourite in FSW, making nearly as much by selling his own merchandise as he did from his downside, to wrestling in a New Jersey Gym. He'd have turned the car around, driven back to Arizona, and basked in his relative fame there until the freebies ran out... Well, he would have, if he was driving the car. 'So, I thought you big time wrestlin' stars'd be drivin' ya Mercedes 'round ta shows?' questioned the well-meaning driver as both men felt the transition from tarmac to gravel. As the vehicle slowed, a rumble of thunder sprinted across the sky, an ominous sign for Wolf's career. 'Was a good job I was drivin' this way, or ya mighta missed ya show!' Part of Wolf thought that might have been a good idea... This was the 'bargain bin'. The kind of shows that wrestlers do for charity in their home towns once a year, just to make it look as if they still care about the business above their pay. 'My Ferrari is at home,' Wolf sarcastically scowled in reply to the Jersey native's annoying question. Before the car had even come to a complete stop, Wolf had the door open, desperate to escape it's stale and stuffy confines. Magnum had dreamed of his name being shown in lights outside Madison Square Garden, anything less and he knew he wasn't being appreciated. But, to his bemusement, his name hadn't carried very far up the wrestling ladder, and it was in the Indies that he remained. There were no bright lights to spell his name outside the Esby Gymnasium, and a laminated card sign reading 'Inaugural PWC Show' taped above the entrance almost brought a tear to his eyes. 'Well, this fucking sucks the big one,' he muttered, stepping out of the Ford Contour and into the miserable night. His dingy, tan trench coat was pulled up over his head, keeping his hair dry as he scanned for any sign of a welcoming committee. 'No Limo, no Driver.. Not even a frickin' ring-rat to blow me before the show...' The driver struggled to force his bulk out of the car, but his deep breathing and the creaking of the Contour's chassis were drowned out by a distant, yet gutsy rumble that was heard getting louder. 'I told you that 3rd Burrito you forced down your gullet was a bad idea,' snapped Wolf to the driver, re-living the memory of the journey from smelly, fast-food filled hell. Looking innocent, the driver closed the driver's-side door and waddled around to Wolf's side, hoping his charitable act would be repaid with a free pass backstage, so he could meet the likes of Sebastian Toomes and Angelo Deville. Trying to make it look like he'd never met the portly, geeky looking man before, Wolf began to cross the un-even parking area... Finding his path blocked by a large expanse of puddle. What a grand life being a pro-wrestler was. 'You,' he barked back to the fan, 'Throw that XXXXL jacket down here, will ya? These boots cost $300!' Before the guy can even tell him to shove it, the distant roar rapidly became an ear-splitting torture, and a flash of Red appeared in Magnum's peripheral vision. From the sound, one could have been lead to believe that a bearded, tattoo-laden man beast would be approaching on a hog of a chopper... But the complete opposite roared towards Magnum, or rather the puddle right in front of him, zigzagging madly through the parked cars. A tall, let slender figure, decked out in figure-hugging black, red, and silver leathers commanded a scarlet Ducati 748 Superbike, throwing it around beneath her as if it were weightless. A daredevil nature was then displayed for all to see, as the mighty machine was flung into a power-slide the second it emerged from the narrow gap between 2 vehicles. As the tyres' screech shredded the still night, the Ducati nestled snugly into a slot, barely 3 feet wide, right by the entrance. The engine cut off, and the night was quiet once more... ...Except for the growling of Magnum. 'Dayyyyyummmm!' exclaimed the impressed driver, keeping his eyes firmly on her leather-encased form, 'Where does a chick learn ta ride a bike like that!' Magnum, however, didn't reply. The bike's twisting route through the cars sent it right through the massive puddle at Magnum's feet, right as it launched into it's slide. The wave of dirty water expelled flew up 7 feet high, with every water molecule seemingly attracted to Magnum. 'Ptew,' he spat, saliva mixed with grey, gritty liquid flying back into the puddle. From head to toe he was soaked, and even his famed moustache had been tainted by the murky puddle-water. The woman slithered off the mighty machine that had rumbled between her thighs, catching the eye of everyone in sight... Apart from Wolf, who was looking down at a ruined pair of boots. 'This day just gets worse....'
