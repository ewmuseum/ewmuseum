ETD: Into The Shadow

22 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Timeless Julie had never before met a man so handsome and beguiling. Seated in her receptionist's chair in the small Absolute Fitness gym, she scolded herself for even thinking about him. She knew she was beneath his notice. He was so suave and powerful, so charismatic, caring, and thoughtful. So talented. So famous. Whenever he walked into a room even the lights seemed to swoon and sigh before brightening in his presence. He was like a king, and she just a lowly serf forever denied his true attentions through the difference in their stations. Still, she couldn't help imagining herself in his loving embrace, wrapped up in the delightful poetry she was sure he spouted as easily as other men grunted and swore. Anything more than the professional greeting he gave her whenever he showed up for his workouts would be rapture. She ached for his touch, for his soft breath against her ear, as she knew all women did. Therein lay the problem; with all of the women he met undoubtedly throwing themselves at his feet, how could he notice a pathetic wretch such as herself? She sighed and steeled herself against the tears threatening to erupt. He would be there soon and she didn't want him to see her cry. What would he think of her then, blubbering and carrying on like a schoolgirl? She didn't even want to think of the disappointment that would well up in his beautiful eyes. But then . . . What if it made him concerned for her? What if he swept her up in his powerful arms and asked her what was wrong, told her not to worry? A shudder ran through her body at the image of it massaging her mind. Taking a deep breath to calm herself, she looked around to make sure no one had noticed. No one had. No one ever noticed anything about her. Very few people even bothered saying hello to her when they arrived. Except him. He was such a gentleman. What would he do if she just threw herself at his feet? She let her imagination run wild as a silly grin stole onto her face. She knew it was just flightless fancy and that a man so important would never ravage her in such a way, but hey, if she couldn't have it in truth at least she still had her daydreams. And her other dreams, at night, alone in her large bed. Yes, he was undoubtedly the man of her dreams. And who could blame her? Any woman would kill for such a man. Her heart nearly leapt up out of her throat when she heard his voice outside, good-naturedly rebuking some careless passerby who wasn't watching where they were going. 'Get out of the way, wretch!' She could hear his contained ire and it infected her for a moment. How dare some careless oaf get in his way? She thought of running outside to help him belittle the thoughtless ruffian, but before she could bring her legs to move he was sweeping through the doorway. She sat ramrod straight behind her desk, smiling up at him, her mind going in a thousand directions at once, all jumbled into a hopeless mass of things she wished she could say but would never have the courage to. 'Can you believe the nerve of some people?' he asked as he spotted her and began striding purposefully over, the dashing smirk on his face sending her heart aflutter. He looked as handsome as ever. His hair was neatly combed, and he was already wearing the form-fitting shorts and shirt that would in no time be drenched with his blessed sweat. It took all her effort to keep her eyes schooled on his. She felt helpless in the crosshairs of that intelligent gaze. Gesturing toward the door, he continued, 'A man can't go five feet without some dirty little urchin demanding some of his hard-earned money.' She nodded frantically before he could possibly think she'd take the little devil child's side. The man screwed up his face and made his voice ripe with mocking scorn. 'But mister, please, I'm hungry.' He shook his head ruefully before returning to his regular voice. 'Bah! Of course the little brat is hungry! I'd be hungry too if I was a lazy little bastard who sat around on my arse all day refusing to work! Same as you, right, Judie?' 'J-Julie,' she stammered, crushed that he had forgotten her name. 'Huh?' Brow furrowed, lips drawn down in a frown, he looked to both sides before returning his gaze to her, obviously incredulous that someone as low as her would think to correct him. She wanted to die of shame. 'Uh, right . . . Julie.' He shrugged in apology and leaned over the counter. 'Is my friend here yet?' 'Yes sir, Mr. Storm,' she said, nervously rummaging through papers on her desk as if the answer was written somewhere among them. 'That silly pirate is waiting for you in the aerobics room.' Storm rapped his knuckles on the desk and gave her the double-gun salute before turning around and walking away. 'Thanks,' he called over his shoulder without looking back, 'you've been a doll, Judie.' Julie sighed. 'Roberts! Roberts!' Storm glared around the empty expanse of the room with his hands planted firmly on his hips. Thin mats covered every inch of the floor and some were even piled against the wall to one side. Floor-to-ceiling mirrors on every wall had Storm glowering back at himself from all different angles. A chin-up bar hung down from the twelve foot ceiling, as did a pair of ropes with circlets dangling from their ends. Well, the circlets would have been dangling, at least, if one of the mats hadn't been folded and tied between them in a makeshift hammock. Within this resourceful setup The Dread Pirate Roberts stirred at the sound of his name being called. Stretching, yawning, and somehow even sighing all at the same time, he poked his head up over the side and arched an eyebrow at the raging Storm. The mat teetered precariously with his movement, threatening to spill him to the floor. 'There you are!' Storm fumed. 'What the hell are you doing up there -- sleeping?!' The black mask disappeared down between the sides of the mat once again, then the whole hammock swung around, dumping The DPR to the floor. He landed on his feet and immediately dipped into an elaborate bow, spreading his arms wide. Sheathed in his suit of black silk, his scabbard swept behind him, he flashed Storm a mischievous grin. 'Well I was,' he answered smoothly, 'until you started yelling.' The Global Champion stormed across the room waving an angry finger. 'There's gonna be a lot more yelling if you don't smarten up! You do remember that you're wrestling your first match this week, don't you?' Straightening, Roberts waved a dismissive gloved hand. 'That's not until Sunday.' 'Sunday's only a few days away,' Storm growled. 'More like a week.' Now directly in front of Roberts, Storm struggled to contain his frustration. 'And you think that's enough time to whip you into shape to face the XUW World Champion? Yesterday you couldn't even master a headlock takedown! A freakin' headlock takedown!' Storm called up a mental image of him suplexing God in order to calm himself, but it did no good. 'Today you're sleeping!' The Man in Black narrowed his eyes and cocked his head. 'What was that bit about the XUW World Champion?' 'The Shadow. Your opponent.' Biting off each word, Storm began trembling with ire as The DPR continued to stare at him unconcerned. 'The Shadow is one half of the XUW World Champion!' 'He's only half a man?' Roberts asked, suddenly grinning again. 'This will be easier than I thought.' 'No, you idiot. He and another wrestler, some fat guy, are joint Champions. They each hold half the Championship!' 'So?' 'So they're both World Champion!' 'No, they're both half of the World Champion.' Seeing that Storm was about to erupt again, The DPR placed a comforting hand on his shoulder. 'Don't you see, Chris? A champion would be something to cause worry in a wrestling match, just as a man with wit would be something to cause worry in an argument . . . but a half-champion? Think about it. Would you be worried by a half-wit?' Remembering who he was speaking to, The DPR amended, 'Do you think I would be worried by a half-wit?' Shaking the hand off of him, Storm threw both of his up and began pacing. 'You're freakin' doomed! Doomed!' 'Come now,' Roberts scoffed, 'it isn't so bad as that.' 'It is!' Storm cried, still pacing. 'You're up against The Shadow, a World Champion, and you've never even wrestled a match! You're doomed!' 'It may be true that I've never wrestled a match,' The DPR allowed, scooting over to pace alongside Storm, earning himself a glare, 'but I'm far from doomed. I'm being trained by you, the PTC Global Champion, RPW Mid-South Champion, and dare I say, The Last Great Technical Wrestler. Do you think this Shadow fellow had such an excellent tutor? With you in my corner, how can I lose? I'd say he's the one who's doomed.' Storm paused, considering. Rubbing his chin, he donned a half-smile. 'Well, yeah, I suppose there's that. There's no way The Shadow had a teacher like me.' He was coming around, but still didn't sound convinced. 'See, there you go.' Roberts grin finally took hold on Storm and they both laughed. 'Aren't you the one always saying you could train a monkey to become RPW World Champion?' Smiling, Storm nodded and shrugged. 'And isn't RPW better than XUW?' More nodding. 'So then this match is already won . . . unless you think a monkey has a better shot than I do.' Roberts laughed again. Storm didn't join in. He blinked, then adopted an angry, hopeless scowl. Throwing his arms up and pacing again, he roared, 'You're doomed!' Dim candlelight washed the small room in a flickering yellow glow. Bare-chested yet still wearing his mask, The Dread Pirate Roberts lay at the focus of the four small candles arranged in a semi-circle behind him. Face-down on the cold cement, his panting breath condensed to form a wet round spot in front of his mouth. Sweat drenched every inch of his body and made a considerable puddle beneath him. Storm had practiced with him deep into the night, intent on molding him into a wrestler of consequence. He lacked confidence that it would ever work, but had agreed to meet Roberts again the next day to continue the training. Roberts couldn't help but think that was extremely kind of the Global Champion, even if he was only doing it because of a bet they had made. Storm said he didn't hold out much hope for The DPR ever becoming a champion like him, but Roberts didn't take the discouraging words seriously. That was just Storm's way. Still, Roberts wanted to win. He loved the fight, loved the battle, but he loved victory even more. He didn't know if he had what it would take to defeat The Shadow, a decorated wrestler of the highest esteem, and it was for that exact reason he had challenged him. 'We triumph without glory when we conquer without danger,' Roberts quoted under his breath, then forced out another agonizing push-up. He knew that winning wasn't everything. The desire to put up a good fight, the need to give it all you had, the want of victory was. When he finally straightened his arms he wanted to just crash to the ground, but he lowered himself slowly, counting backwards from five, ignoring the flames searing his already numb and burning muscles. A mumbled cry breached his lips as he settled on the ground once more, clamping his eyes tight and turning his head to the side. He would not lose this fight for lack of want to win. He would not lose this fight for lack of preparation. He would win this fight and all others set before him. Behind him, from the darkness, a shadow began to emerge. It stepped carefully into the light, testing the air with its many tentacles, slipping up the wall and growing ever bigger and more dangerous. It could hear Roberts panting; it could smell his exhaustion. It was drawn to his fear like a hound to blood. A monstrous thing of many legs and arms, it rose up on the wall in front of him and began ghosting over to where he lay. It promised destruction and doom. It vowed to lay waste to the unfortunate pirate, to make him suffer like he had never suffered before. Inching along the wall, sucking away the hope and light, it threatened to devour his entire being. It had no mercy. It felt no remorse. It wanted only to punish and consume. The Shadow was upon him.
