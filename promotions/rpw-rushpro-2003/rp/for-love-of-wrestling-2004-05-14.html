For Love of Wrestling

14 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, May 14, 2004 They can try and keep me away but they never will. This is where I belong. It is what I do. Kannon can tell me to stay at home, think about what I want, try and keep me away from the arena. It won’t happen. Fine me, I don’t care. Suspend me; I’ll just keep coming back. Fire me, I’ll move on and work somewhere else. I am a wrestler. He can not keep me from wrestling. No one can. It is what I do when I wake up, it is the reason I wake up. Each and every morning is just another reason to step between the ropes and refine my skills, talent, and my very style of life. I have to show up on Sunday. Kannon does not understand it but I’m trying to help him. I’ve been helping him. I am his most valuable ally he has in RPW. If he is serious about turning RPW around and making it the most respected wrestling organization out there, he has to trust me and give me the ball to run with. No other wrestler can do what needs to be done. Pride needs to return. In order to have pride you must respect yourself and the ring itself. That ring protects us, but they don’t understand that. Without the ring, we would not be here. Sure people like Gabe, Maxwell, Violence Jack, they just see ropes, a canvas, and maybe ring post. But I don’t. I see honor. There is competition in a physical form when you gaze at a ring. I can see it. I understand it. That is why I need to have the ball. People think I can’t do it, that I will fall on my face. I won’t. I love this business to much to let that happen. This business is everything to me, my life line. I need it and it needs me. Without me, the ring loses the respect it had and thus loses all purpose. Do you think Hessian has respect for the ring? He doesn’t have respect for anything other than money. Does Karina respect it? Far from it, she uses the ring. She abuses the ring. It is not some place to play and spend your weekend. If she wants to go play, then do it on her time, not on mine. RPW is dying. I can see it. It’s dying because we have lost our way. What was supposed to be a great and honorable wrestling business is a joke, the court jester among the sea of fools. Names like Gabe, Spiffy, Oxios, Skywolf, Dave Hurst, and many more bring shame and mockery to the ring. That can not be allowed if we are going to survive. But they don’t want to survive and they don’t want to wrestle. They want to tell jokes and make people laugh. Are we here to make people laugh? No. We are here to wrestle. So why am I the only person on the roster that actually does that? Not only do I wrestle, but I do it damn good. I’m good enough to crown myself a former Global title and the current Mid South title. What is the problem here? Even the most serious of wrestlers are trying to be stand up comedians. Does Kannon not see by giving the poison that is Gabe, air-time that he kills this company? The people think the only way to get ahead in RPW is to make the crowd laugh. Well I for one will not reduce myself to a mere joke. My name is Chris Storm and I am a wrestler. A wrestler Damnit! So you see it is vital that I show up on Sunday. I have to try and do what I can to save Rush Pro Wrestling from becoming another side show freak like UWF or XUW. I must bring us up the hill to where we belong, on top of the mountain. OSW, PCW, FSW, and many more will weep in their graves because they will know we have surpassed them. And I will be the one that lead it. I just need my chance. I will get my chance.
