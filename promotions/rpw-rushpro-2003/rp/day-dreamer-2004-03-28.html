Day Dreamer

28 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 28, 2004 Have you ever had a song express exactly what you were feeling? Chris felt that way today with “End of the World” by R.E.M. You know the one, the one that goes “it’s the end of the world as we know it and I feel fine.” Yeah Storm’s feeling it, can you dig him sucker? With all the recent events going on in his life it felt like it really was the end of the world. Yet somehow Chris still carried on with life. He found his strength threw wrestling and the pride he took being the Global Champion. So while it maybe the end of the world as we know it, Chris Storm felt fine. Today he again avoided working out with Arizona so that he may focus on actually helping another individual, the Dread Pirate Roberts. The two spent the day in the gym while Chris taught the Pirate the fine art of wrestling in hopes he would not screw up too badly in front of RPW. The man of more nicknames than his handler cares to remember threw his wrestling bag over his shoulder as he left the gym. His clothes were soaked and his sweat weighing the champion of more titles than his handler cared to remember down. Man what a lazy ass handler if you ask m…wait…anyways back to Lanc….Chris Storm. Chris Storm came out of the gym coated in sweat after just finishing another training session with the Dread Pirate Roberts, whom Chris was hell bent on making a winner come this Sunday in his match against Shadow. Storm should have been focusing more on his own training though with his Global title defense approaching. It had proven to be difficult enough to defeat two other men to simply win it. Now Chris would have to defeat two men and a woman just to keep it. One could not blame Chris for it though. With his recent string of bad news he wanted to forget his problems and focus on others. It showed with his strict training with the Dread Pirate. You could say Chris was working out some of his aggression. It may have given the Dread Pirate a mild concussion but it did relieve aggression. For now Chris thought it would be best to take a little break before returning to the hustle and bustle of life as a wrestler. The Global champion crawled into the back of his Coup Deville and closed his eyes. The Dread Pirate Roberts would not mind a short nap break and Chris for one welcomed it. Chris slipped away from the concrete jungle to a tropical paradise. The soaring skyscrapers turned to lush palm trees. Rolling concrete became white sand that went to a large body of water. Those baby blues opened to admire his new surroundings. He was not in the backseat of his Coup Deville parked behind a gym; he was sitting in a life guard tower watching over a wondrous beach. He was just about to close his eyes again and allow the sounds sooth his problems away but a sight caught his attention. This was more important than a calm and relaxed life. This was more important than resting on a beautiful beach. THIS WAS TESSA WINDSOR RUNNING ON THE BEACH NAKED! Every luscious curve bounced in the open air, exposed for all eyes to see. There was only one set of eyes there to see her however; the pair of Chris Storm. Chris watched from his tower the beautiful diva enjoying her freedom. Her soft white skin seemed to glow in the light of the sun. Two soft mounds bounced up and down with each step. Chris’ head bounced with each step. His neck would surly be stiff tomorrow. He knew this was a dream. Everything was just an illusion created by his mind. A giant wave came crashing down on Tessa soaking her from head to toe. Water cascaded down every inch of her vivacious body. At that moment Chris realized he did not give a rat’s ass if this was a dream of not, this was fucking sweet. Without a moment lost Chris hopped over the rail on his life guard tower and landed on the sand. Chris ran a hand through his golden locks to ensure each strand was where he wanted it. With his mane just the way he wanted Chris set out to try and hit that. “Well hello beautiful, it’s amazing finding you here. If you need anything” Chris made sure he emphasized the word “ANYTHING at all, just let me know.” He smiled broadly and stuck out his chest to look bigger than he really was. Not surprising the whole time Chris spoke his eyes stayed locked on those two perky peaks. Tessa just smiled, not caring that his eyes stared at her ravenously. This was Chris’ dream after all. She opened her mouth slowly to speak, “Think you could be any less original Chrissy?” The body belonged to Tessa Windsor but the voice clearly was not. Chris stepped back in shock and horror. “So you don’t like the indirect approach. Maybe the direct approach is the way to go, it is my dream after all” he said to himself showing a smudge of arrogance. “So Tessa what do you say we go out back to the life guard tower and you blow me?” If that had been the real Tessa Windsor Chris would have been slapped across the face or at least kneed in the groin. But this was NOT the real Tessa but a very horny dream created by the mind of a perverted handle…wrestler. “You’re just coming onto me because your gay, you gay little bisexual gay man. GAY!” came from Tessa’s mouth. Chris raised an eyebrow. That defiantly was not Tessa’s voice. Tessa had an angelic voice that sounded like a hundred clarinets. This sounded like a hundred chainsaws. The Perfect Storm stepped back and rubbed his forehead. This dream sucked balls and not in the way Chris wanted it to either. The verbal banter continued from the vision of beauty. “Get away from me you walking STD! Blah gay! Blah blah STD blah gay blah Chrissy blah blah blah you’re so old! Blah too old! Blah blah you old gay STD Chrissy blah!” Before you ask, yes she really did say ‘blah’ that many times. Thankfully they were on a gorgeous tropical beach where there are no sharp objects or Chris would have stabbed out his ears just to escape the wretched voice. Out of the corner of his eye Chris spotted a coconut that had fallen from one of the trees. He had the urge to beat himself over the head with it but two incredibly beautifully and incredibly naked supermodels came running out from the lush tropical forest. The two seemed to move in slow motion ala ‘Bay Watch’. “Finally this dream gets a little better.” Chris told himself. He looked over at the naked form of Tessa. Water rolled down her sexual curves, clinging to her nude body. Man he wanted to bang her brains out but that voice made him want to drive a spike through his head. NOT THAT ONE! Having enough of this dream Tessa, Chris turned and started to sprint away toward the two super models. “Hey get back over here; I’m not through bloody nagging to you yet! Nag 1: to find fault incessantly: COMPLAIN 2: to irritate by constant scolding or urging 3: to be a continuing source of annoyance.” The voice yelled at him. Chris looked over his shoulder at her “why don’t you define annoying bitch while you’re at it!” Automatically the voice shouted back “annoy vb: to disturb or irritate esp. by repeated acts…” the voice became a distant memory for Chris as he trucked it over to the other, hopefully less annoying, naked super models. The Perfect Storm stopped right before him, flashing them both a pearly white smile. The two twin super models had a rack that had to be fakes, it being a dream that would obviously make them fake, but hey it was Chris’ dream and he wanted large breasted naked supermodels. “Hi ladies, what a beautiful day we are having today, but not as beautiful as the both of you.” If you asked Chris what color eyes they had he would not be able to tell you. His eyes were at a toss up over whose breast he should stare at more. “Have you seen my belt? I can’t seem to find it anywhere.” The first one asked. Oh Kami her voice was worst then Tessa’s! At least Tessa had a female’s voice. This supermodel had a male voice, an annoying and whiny male voice at that. Chris raised his eyebrow at her, “we can play hide and seek later babe.” “Oh maybe she has it!” the first model said running off toward Tessa. Chris looked at the other one hoping she had a female voice that would not make his ears bleed or make him wish to cause bodily harm to himself. “So what’s wrong with your voice? Let me guess, you sound like Minion the Imp or Gabe? Do you talk like Vince Jones? Let me know now so I can wake myself up now.” “My life is so depression!” the woman screamed. Her voice was that of a man’s as well. Sadly enough Chris could stand her man voice more than Tessa or the other super model. “That’s nice, why the hell do you have the voice of a man? This dream has been pretty messed up.” Chris asked. He hopped she would answer him but with the way this dream was going she would be more likely to grow a second head first. And no, that would NOT turn him on. “The life of my life died! My life is a continuing spiral of pain!” she screamed at Chris before running back into the tropical forest going on about how depressing her life was. Chris Storm turned and looked at the other supermodel and Tessa. His eyes lit up. They were making out! Two hot, wet, naked chicks were making out! Chris hurried and made his way toward them to get a better view of the action. The two broke their kiss and looked at Chris staring on. Tessa snarled and hissed at him in that painfully annoying voice, “you better stop staring or I’ll get my boyfriend to beat you bloody. He kill you gay STD old man! He’ll go Irish on your ass!” “Oh let me just tell you, I fear an Irish man. Let me guess his name has Mc in it. Why am I not surprised? Well let Ronald McDonald try it, he can get an ass whooping too.” As if things could not get strange enough in this tropical paradise, Dogbert walked up behind Chris. “Look at me! I’m cute, what with my little black nose, soft furry ears, and adorable little face.” Tessa Windsor and the supermodel took at each other, “he IS cute.” The two naked beauties took Dogbert by the hand and walked off with him down the beach. “Man this dream sucks, I’m out of here.” Chris said as he slapped himself suddenly. Chris shot up in the back seat of his Coup Deville. He looked around trying to regain his sense of location. The Global champion was back in his concrete jungle home, far from any beaches lost in tropical paradises. “Could that dream have gotten any weirder?” Chris asked himself as he got out of the car. He looked at his watch “7:35 P.M.” Chris must have slept a little longer than he had planned on. Something inside him wondered if the Dread Pirate was still inside waiting for him to return and force him to train more. “Ah what the hell, if he’s not there I could use the training myself.” A weird almost 70’s porn song started to play. Chris opened his bag and retrieved his cell phone. “Yeah?” Chris paused to listen to whoever was on the other side. “Patti killed someone?” He paused again. “They found her teeth mark AND fingerprints on a messed up corpse? Wow, she’s hardcore. Good let the bitch rot in a jail cell. It makes for one less opponent for me to face.” The person on the other side cut Chris off. “Hey look she was the one stupid enough to murder someone. She should have known killing a man would make it hard to attend a match, especially when you’re on the electric chair.” Chris paused again. “What do you mean she might get off on self defense? Fucking legal system, this is why I’m moving to Canada.” Having enough of this conversation, Chris hung up the phone. “Ah fuck them. I don’t care if any of them show up or if they all do. I won this belt for a reason and I’m keeping it for the same reason, I’m the best Technical wrestler walking on the earth today.” He may have cancer and he may be up for what some have said will be one of the harder matches, but Chris Storm was as cocky as ever. Could the Perfect Storm keep his title? Oh course he could, otherwise he would not be here now. With the match drawing near Chris was like a kid in a candy store at the thought of getting to wrestle again. It truly was his life.
