Reacquainted

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/12/03 That old all to familiar touch came back to him as his finger tips glided gently across her neck. He closed his eyes and let the memories of past months come flooding back to him, his fingers locking around her base, like an old key that still fits into a forgotten rusted chamber door. He groped and fumbled with every inch of her figure. His right hand cradling her body, his left hand tantalising and teasing her lavish seductive neck. It had been to long since had cradles Tanya in his tern muscular tattooed arms. The people watching him doing so seemed nonexistent to him in this euphoric moment. Some people thought naming you’re guitar after a woman and treating her like one was crazy, well maybe it was. That didn’t matter to him though. Gideon shook the moment off and looked into the sea of buzzing fans revolving around each other. Tanya gripped tightly to his body as his spider like fingers blistered with lightning efficiency across her fret board. “I AM…. YOU’RE ONLY… MOTHER FUCKING RHETORIC REASON!” Gideon tiled his head to the right and glanced at Trent at the front of the stage screaming the lyrics like a besotted demonic poet. His microphone cable wrapped tightly round his body. For a moment the wires made him look like a cheap imitation Egyptian mummy to Gideon. Cadaver Theory were pulling in the crowds to the small clubs more and more, and Gideon and the rest of the guys didn’t kid themselves into denial. They knew his exposure on RPW, especially after his rehabilitation following his temporary paralysis played a part in it. “Sway to the rhythms that brought you down to me!” Gideon looked at Vex to come in for the backing vocals. The red headed Axl Rose stopped playing his bass line and came in right on cue. “Dance with Devil and kid yourself that you’re free!” All three men then stopped and let the ever hormonal Maynard thunder through with his trademark stomach busting Drum solo. Gideon smirked as the drummer coughed dramatically while trying to smoke a cigar at the same time. As far as Gideon remembered, Maynard didn’t even smoke, and was known to get more than verbal against people who did while he was eating at a restaurant. Either way, May was entitled to his bit of showmanship with the bands new found fame, as small to some as it might be. Maynard quickly spat the cigar to the floor, stamped it out with his right leg before returning it to bashing the bass drum, and quickly hit a thunderclap drum roll allowing the rest of the band to come back in. They did with poetic finesse and grace. Gideon once again looked into the crowd and once again asked himself “Whatever happened to dancing?” as the blurring images of circle pits and slam dancing occurred before his eyes. Time passed, moments rendered, seconds ceased seemingly in an incessant blurring haze. The next thing he knew Trent was shouting “Thank you folks, goodnight. Our album is on sale near the bar, catch us next time. We were Cadaver Theory!” “Time flies when you’re having fun” Gideon thought. He walked off stage, his guitar slung behind him, the neck of Tanya pointing at a downward diagonal angle making him look like a Samurai warrior with his weapon by his side. Gideon exited through the small fire exit where he saw Vex, Maynard and Trent all unloading a few cans bottles of beer from the back of Vex’s pickup in celebration. Gideon stopped for a few moments and let the cold night air numb his body in a soothing arctic grip. “Yo Gid, come and grab one, you earned one buddy!” shouted Vex. “Fucking-A. He even tuned his guitar this time!” came the voice of Maynard as he perched himself on top of the pick up. Gideon grabbed a beer before looking up to Vex. “Hey May, how’s your throat?” All three members broke into hysterical laughter at the cigar incident which occurred only moments ago. Maynard quickly and automatically produced his middle finger in an ancient gesture that seemingly has been around since Christ was born, and then went back to drinking his beer. The cool calmness of the night was then shattered by a polyphonic ring tone of “Frantic” by Metallica. Gideon quickly felt an cold sharp chill dig and grate into his spine which wasn’t caused by the cool night air. He quickly stormed to the backseat of the truck, snatched his bag out and fumbled for his phone while the other continued talking amongst one another. He quickly answered it. “What?” “……He wishes to see you soon” “Really? Tell him to tune in to RPW Television, he can see me along with thousands of others.” “…In person” “ We arranged this already!” “Things change. He wishes to speak with you at the next RPW Show” Gideon looks behind him, notices his band mates looking at him with deep concern, before noticing Trent is not among them. Suddenly a hand snatches the phone from behind him, and he doesn’t need to turn around to know who it is. “Hello. This is the concern factory, unfortunately you’re attempted order can and will not be placed through asshole” Trent then snaps Gideon’s phone shut and tosses it back into the back of the truck. He then sighs before leaning against the truck as Vex and Maynard join them. “You got this situation under control?” Trent asks in a strict demanding tone. Gideon nods. “Next RPW show, things will get straightened out. These assholes will know what they’re dealing with then. And no, you don’t need to be there” Trent laughs as Gideon seemingly reads his mind. “Fair enough. Anyway gentleman.” All three men look at their leader, front man, and somewhat spiritual adviser. “Next stop. New Jersey!”
