Hellbent Haiku

22 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/22/04 I miss you, old friend You were one of the good ones Always at my side Folks like you are rare Always there to give me strength In times of despair We traveled long roads We competed with the best We had seen it all We had paid our dues While collecting memories Of all the good times The titles and fame The glory and the honor Now lost in time’s sand For they came along Forced us to fight face-to-face Then it soon followed The day good times left Fate had dealt us a cruel hand And soon, the house won We fought valiantly With tooth and nail, we bled And now you lie dead Confined in a chair For all the rest of your days All because of them The fans are just sheep Never making up their minds About what they want What now do I see? My voyage about to start It’s all for you, friend I set out to right The wrongs that shattered us both For the years to come And to RPW, the ties that bind, now a noose Wrapped around your neck -Fin-
