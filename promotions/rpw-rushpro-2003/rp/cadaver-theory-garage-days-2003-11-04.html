Cadaver Theory: Garage Days

4 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/11/03 'Nocturnal Reaction?' came the young voice of the unmistakable Vex Lennon, his eyes lighting up at the possible answer as to what to name the band in which he played bass guitar for. 'Sounds like something an Albino would have an allergy towards' replied Maynard Sanders while sitting behind his drum kit twirling a drumstick through his left hand. His long purple hair easily rivalling any glam rockers hair from any decade. The blunt shoot down of Vex's choice had clearly not disheartened his attempts though. 'Lost In Static?' he asked suggestively once again. 'Yeah.....' said a young Trent Scott approvingly as he reared his head back from off the mic stand at which it had been resting on. 'Yeah?' Vex asked excitedly. 'Yeah.... that's where your brain seems to be these days' Trent said in a now dry sarcastic tone causing a loud light hearted laugh to erupt from the three young teenage boys sitting in Maynard's garage in the baking heat, as well as Vex giving Trent the proverbial middle finger while smiling sarcastically back to the vocalist. The insult session between the three young rockers then came to a sudden pause as the sound of savage barking invaded their ears, causing each member to look at each other with a sly menacing smile on their faces. 'Who's Mitch eating now Maynard, the fucking pizza boy?' asked Trent while positioning his mic on the stand for no other reason than to cure his long term boredom. 'Ah, who cares, just someone passing by in the street probably' replied Maynard, seemingly more concerned on seeing how long he could twirl the drumstick between his fingers. The three were about to agree with him, until the savage barks suddenly turned to faint coarse whimpers. Maynard looked to Trent, Trent looked to Vex, who in return looked at Maynard to complete the triangular shape of shocked and concerned looks. The three fledgling rockers quickly bolted up from their seats and headed towards the door of the garage, Trent and Vex being the two reaching it first and struggling against each other body's to be the first out. 'Jesus Trent, lose some weight you damn redneck son of a....' Vex suddenly stopped in the middle of brushing himself off and cursing Trent to come to a shocked stand still by his side. 'Well I'll be damned' mouthed Trent in almost a whisper. Right before them, Mitch, Maynard's prize Doberman who had earned the fear and respect of every postman in the Bangor Postal Service and who would tear apart any stranger who Maynard didn't approve of, lay on the hot concrete floor of Myanard's drive, paws out front, eyes looking sympathetically upwards while whimpering affectionately at a boy of their age. His greasy black hair barely reaching past his ears and his definition of a goatee nothing more than sprays of faint stubble across his chin, resting there on his knees, his right hand extended so that his index and middle finger were pointed outwards locking contact with Mitch's eyes. Trent couldn't help but grin as the boy seemed oblivious to them standing there. 'Alright assholes!' came the sound of Maynard as he came charging out of the garage in an overly dramatic fashion with a paintball gun grasped in his hands. 'Who's ready to...... WHAT THE FUCK HAVE YOU DONE TO MY DOG?!' This time the young man did look up and also lowered his hand slowly while rising to his feet. Maynard quickly raised the paintball gun and aimed at the young guy's head until Trent's hand bolted out to the side and slapped it down. 'Put that damn toy away May, what you gonna do, colour him to death?!' Maynard ignored Trent while never taking his enraged eyes off the stranger before him. It wasn't until then that all three men noticed the guitar case resting down by his feet. The dog silencing stranger then produced a piece of crumpled paper from the back pocket of a pair of faded creased blue denim jeans. 'I'm here about the position for the band. I play guitar' announced the stranger in a deep voice. 'Positions filled asshole, thanks for coming, now get the fuck off my lawn!' snapped Maynard in response. 'Is it filled? Is it shit May, shut your hole' responded Trent in a light hearted tone. 'Hey, what's your name buddy. You got a name right, cause I'm fine with calling you Dr. DoLittle, but with band credibility the way it is nowadays..' 'Or lack of' chimed in Vex. 'Or lack of in our case, yes, thanks Vex. We may need to change it.' responded Trent while slipping an arm around Vex, the two looking like the perfect comedy duo. 'Dead man walking sounds like a good and accurate name to me right now' snapped Maynard once again, still clutching the paintball gun. 'Nah, my names Gideon' announced the now identified Gideon as he predicted the inevitable cluster of smirks and giggles spreading across the three faces of the boys, even Maynard tried to hide his. 'G-Gideon what son?' asked Trent. Gideon rolled his eyes slightly. 'Gideon Poe. Gideon Daray Poe if you want the full uncut version'. That was too much for Trent as the singer dropped to his knees while clinging onto Vex's leg to bury his face against, still somehow trying to hide his overblown laughter while Vex simply laughed while looking up at the sky. Even Maynard dropped his paintball gun and rested against the nearby garage wall to break into his own fit of hysterics. All the while Gideon nodded slowly while grinning, playing along with the hysterics. 'Yeah yeah, blame my parents, not me'. Trent then slowly rose to his feet, wiping a few tears away from his cheek with his arm. 'And what about that there Gideon Daray Poe' he said while pointing at his guitar case. 'Can you play what's in there. Gideon looked down to his guitar case before looking back up to the three of them. 'Well why don't you tell me?' Trent smiled, he liked the boys attitude, he was 'spunky' as Trent would say. He then motioned his arms towards the garage door in a mocking manner. 'Well then roll up, roll up, step right this way sir. Don't be shy, everyones a winner!' Maynard and Vex rolled their eyes while walking in as Trent followed, leaving Gideon to make his own way in. 'There's a guitar amp over there, Trent's uncle used to play and he gave it to us. You can plug in there' Vex said while slipping his bass strap around his shoulders. Gideon then opened the case and produced a magnificent crimson red Marlin Stingray. It's body almost glistening under the hot summers day. 'Not bad kiddo', not bad' chirped Trent. Gideon seemed to pay no attention as short snapping burst of static ringing through the garage indicated he had plugged the guitar in. He then stood up straight, guitar around his body resting against his chest looking as though it's sole purpose was to be crafted for that one young mans body. 'Whenever you're ready' said Trent. Gideon nodded, before cranking the volume up on the guitar to full. He then looked at Maynard behind the drum kit. 'Anything?' asked Gideon. 'Anything you want handsome' replied Maynard sarcastically. Gideon smiled, and without warning grasped the neck of the guitar and violently ripped into the opening of Metallica's 'Blackened'! 'OHHHHHH HEEEEEEL YEAH' yelled Trent as Vex quickly recovered from the suddenness of the playing and broke into the bass line. All the while Gideon looked from the guitar to Maynard as he finally managed to gather what was going on and thundered through the on the drums. 'Blackened is the end! Winter it will send!' Trent growled out the lyrics like a wolf snarling for fresh meat, he couldn't help but faintly grin at how fluid the music was being played before him. 'Throwing all you see, into obscurity!!!' Vex rose to his feet and began banging his head back and forth frantically along to the bass and drum beats. Gideon smiled as Maynard banged frantically through the beats of the song. 'FIRE!!!' Gideon to began banging his head back and forth through the heavy riffs of the song that he played with the fluency that a poet would have when reciting a sonnet. 'To begin whipping dance of the dead! BLACKENED..... IS THE ......END! To begin whipping dance of the dead! COLOUR OUR WORLD BLACKENED!' All four young boys then began thrashing their heads in an unrehearsed unison that would have brought any crowd to defining cheers. It was during this frantic playing that Trent began to slow his movement while singing, focusing on Gideon the whole time. Looking at him as his fingers moved stealthily and suddenly across the fret board like a spiders running across a wall. Trent, young as he was in age, old and mature as he was in mentality, didn't for one second believe that it was coincidence that himself and the others were having their most intense, brutally satisfying technically sound practice on the day that Gideon decided to audition. Gideon was the final screw needed to hold and support and link them together, the final piece of the jigsaw the brought all the foundations and colours into full scope. 'Okay, okay cut it!' Trent said and stepped away from the microphone as he turned it off. The other three looked up at him with anxious and disappointed looks. They seemed worried that if they didn't keep playing, that beautiful cohesion they had just experienced with Gideon would fade. That of they didn't keep playing they would lose it, lose it forever. Although, none of them would admit to that, not right now anyway. Yet Trent knew, and he knew the others secretly deep down knew, that that cohesion would always be there, as long as Gideon was there. 'That was pretty good, not bad at all' Trent lied. It was the greatest and most exhilarating experience he'd ever had, not even seeing Pearl Jam earlier that summer had lit a fire underneath his ass like those 2 minutes of playing did just then. Vex and Maynard nodded, so did Gideon. 'Anyway, we've got a gig in three week at TJ's, a small club just outside of town. If you want the gig....' He looked briefly at the others, who nodded slowly, Maynard taking a few seconds before doing so. '... Then it's yours man' Gideon smiled while brushing his hair out of his face. 'Excellent' 'Yeah, just don't try any f that Voodoo bullshit theory on my dog again mother fucker!' shouted Maynard, but smiled faintly while doing so. 'Bullshit theory? Lucky I didn't make him into a Cadaver Theory' Gideon laughed, the other didn't however. Instead, a hush silence fell over them as faint smiles spread across their faces. Trent then looked at Gideon, with an excited yet hard stare. '...What did you say?' ================== And so it was born. So they were born. Cadaver Theory.
