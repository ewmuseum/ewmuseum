It's MY House

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/04 For the first time in what seemed like years, but was in reality only a few weeks I had finally gotten to set foot back on the territory of my home. Quite as expected it was exactly how I left it, but then again there really was no reason it would be any different. My mother dared not to change a thing because of the differences between her and I and the kids made sure that when I go home I’d be greeted by the same mess I was used to. But just because I was finally back at home, didn’t mean I would receive a cheery homecoming. In fact it only took a few seconds after I stepped in through the front door for the tirade of unnecessary questions to begin. Of course, the one leading this charge to find me guilty of being the worst person of earth was none other than my mother. “And where on earth have you been these past few weeks?” She called from the top of the stairs, her cold blue eyes directed straight towards me as she made her way down the stairs, clad in her bathrobe and a pair of flannel pajamas that she had raided my closet for. “Does it really matter all that much?” I questioned as I made it a point to steer my body towards the coat closet, away from my mother who continued her charge down the stairs. “Don’t you talk to me that way young lady! I want answers and I want them now.” My mother persisted as she continued her journey of making my homecoming a living hell by stepping onto the tiled floor of the hallway and making a beeline towards me with that “I won’t tolerate this crap” look on her face that I was all too used to. “Young lady? I’m twenty two for crying aloud and this is MY house last time I checked so I’m allowed to go and come from it as I please. Besides, it’s not like I wanted to be where I was anyways.” I retorted after hanging up the light pink fleece that had been given to me for my birthday by the two children I had adopted. Boy did it ever come in handy while I was gone. I wish that when I received it back in July that I knew how great of a gift it truly was, because then I would have showered the kids in more than a million thank you’s. Then again there was no use in worrying about it now with Christmas past, yet all of gifts still sitting under the rotted brown tree. Maybe there would be another gift under there that would be just as handy as the fleece was, but then again would the children even care to hear my thanks after missing out on Christmas. “Where were you that was just oh so miserable that you had to miss out on the holidays with your family?” She questioned with hateful eyes. For once she was mad at me for a reason that was actually understandable. I almost felt like the devil himself for not having an answer, though I knew exactly why I was gone. It was tearing me apart though not being able to answer for fear that the name brought up in the answer would open up an even greater hell. “I had no way to get home mom.” I shot out, not even thinking of how obvious of a lie it truly was. “You can’t possibly tell me that there was not a train, plane or bus that could bring you back home.” She replied, trapping me in a corner. She was right, there would be no reason for a sudden halt of transportation like that. So what could I possibly say now? “Alright fine, I was off with a friend who needed some company.” I replied with yet another lie considering Chris didn’t need any sort of company with the way he whored himself around. Not to mention the last thing I would consider him to be now was a friend. “So for three weeks partying with friends was a lot more important than being with family and taking care of the two kids you are supposed to be raising?” My mother shouted at me as I walked away, hoping to take shelter in my bedroom upstairs. “YES!” I finally shouted in frustration, knowing that even if that wasn’t the truth I would have to admit it if I wanted the wrath of my mother to come to an end. “You see what that job of yours has done to you?” She continued on though, regardless of me being the one to surrender first. “It’s absolutely killed your morals! All you ever do now is slut yourself around and forget all about those two kids that you said you’d take care of and raise to be….” “Actually mom, not really. But seeing as though you feel like your living with that bad of a person I suppose you can leave right now and find yourself somewhere else to live. After all, it was this little moral less girl who gave you a home when you needed it most. ” I replied in a calm yet angered manner after growing tired of her lies. “You wouldn’t dare throw me out of the house…” She replied in a low voice as she began to realize that I was serious about what I had said. “Oh yes I would, so say your goodbyes. Tomorrow brings a new day for you…along with a new home, because you’re no longer welcome here.” I grinned after finally putting up the guts to stand up to her as I slammed the door in her face. No longer would I put up with her crap, just like I wouldn’t put up with Chris. It was time for the soft hearted Tessa to show the tougher side, the side that I was no longer afraid to show.
