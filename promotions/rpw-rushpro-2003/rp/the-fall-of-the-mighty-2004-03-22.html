The Fall of the Mighty

22 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Mar. 22, 2004 Chris found himself in a dimmed area. The lighting made it difficult to see even the dangling golden locks before his eyes. He tried to focus his eyes but he was unable to make anything out. It was very quiet, yet something was in the air. He could feel himself being weighed down by something. There was some force that made Chris Storm feel like he weighed a ton and a half. Most of the burden seemed to be coming from something dragged over his shoulder. Chris looked at what caused him the pain to see a brilliant reflect of himself. The light was so bright Chris had to shield his eyes. He could see the source of the magnificent light, the PTC Global title. How could such an item bring him such a heavy burden yet emit such a wondrous light? Despite the brilliance of the title, Chris removed it from his shoulder and placed it beside him. Most of the pressure had been removed but Chris still felt a great burden pushing down on him, like gravity intensified. Chris felt strangely ill. His stomach was nauseated and he felt very tired and weak. His head pounded like a hammer. He wanted it to stop. He thought of running away but what good would that do? He did not understand where he was or where he would go. So you wanna be a rock superstar And live large. Your big house, five cars His heart stopped. He knew those words all too well. When those words hit it was the start of ‘Rock Superstar’ by Cypress Hill, Chris Storm’s entrance music. But why was it played now? Confusion racked his brain. Chris turned his head to see a curtain. The curtains were openly partly; just enough for Chris to see what was beyond them. A massive crowd booed at the mere playing of his theme song. He stepped out through the curtain onto the stage. Before him stretched an entrance ramp that led to a ring. His eyes followed that ramp into the ring where he saw them. Each stood in their respected corners, now all they needed was for Chris to descend into the ring and take his respected corner. “And making his way to the ring, at a weight of 243 pounds; he is the PTC Global champion and the RPW Mid South champion, he is Chris STORM!” the announcer yelled. Unsure of what to do, Chris made his way toward the ring slowly. The fans threw trash at Chris, jeering him. Storm stepped through the ropes and walked into his corner. DING! The three other opponents went to work wrestling. Chris watched them move with grace. Their bodies performed like a fine ballot. He tried to follow them but his body would not reply. His body was swept over with a chill, like the entire arena dropped a few degrees. All the noises; the roaring crowds, the bodies tumbling, and his own heart pounding, came to a dead halt. He fell to his knees then face first onto the mat. There was an entire system shut down in his body. Chris Storm’s last breath escaped his lips. This was the end of his of his era, the end of his life. BUZZZ! Chris shot up to a sitting position. He looked around confused, was he dead? A bright light shined in his eyes. Chris covered his eyes with a hand and looked around. He was back in his apartment lying in bed. The morning sun came in through his window lighting up the entire room. “What a strange dream.” Chris thought. “I wonder what it could ever mean.” He had this ghastly feeling when he thought about it, like his stomach was turning. “It was just a dream.” He told himself. Chris forced himself out of bed and went ahead and got dressed in a pair of grey sweatpants, white wife-beater shirt, and grey Asics hooded sweater. After his traditional bowl of Trix cereal, Chris grabbed his gym bag and headed out to meet his manager and trainer, Arizona, at the nearby gym. “In a matter of days you will have one of the bigger matches of your career.” Arizona bellowed out. The Magical Bum paced back and forth in the center of the ring. “Jesse Jamester, Apocalypse, and Patti Anderson are all gunning for your title.” “Yeah I know, huge match could make or break my career blah blah blah. This will be so easy they should blindfold me to make it fairer.” Chris replied showing his arrogance. Arizona expected this from the egotistical Chris Storm. “Jesse Jamester and Apocalypse are both larger then you so what I was thinking is we practice your bumps. Thus far in RPW you really haven’t had an opponent who’s been able to pick you up and slam you down so I’m betting you are rusty on it.” Chris rolled his eyes at the idea of him not being up to shape with anything. “So you want me to run around and fall on my face? Who do I look like, AWS Man? I’m not retarded you know.” “You’ll be retarded if you go into that match unprepared. Now bounce of those ropes and do a front flip onto your back.” Arizona ordered to him. He grabbed the whistle and placed it between his lips, giving it a blow causing the ear piercing noise to shake the air. Chris rubbed his ear to make sure it was not bleeding. “Fine, fine I’ll do your stupid little bumps. But tomorrow I’m practicing my Ryoplex. I need to learn to hit it on larger opponents.” The Perfect Storm looked over his shoulder at the ropes behind him. He took a few steps back and ran into the ropes. He bounced off the ropes and ran at the opposite set. Building speed, he nailed the other ropes and leaped into the air. Chris ducked his head down and kicked his feet over landing on his back. He held out his arms and lands supporting the fall easier. “Again” ordered Arizona as he blew the whistle once more. Chris pulled himself back to his feet and looked over at Arizona half annoyed. He shook his head and ran at the ropes again. Bouncing off the ropes Chris performed a second front flip on the mat, again landing in a spread out position to reduce impact. “Again” Arizona repeated, starting to sound like a broken record. For the third time he blew that whistle as if Chris was some dog that was suppose to follow his ever order because of it. Standing up, Chris looked at Arizona and pointed at him. “Last one” he told him before running at the ropes and performing another one of his front flips. “Again” Arizona barked, blowing his whistle at him. This time Chris did not jump back to his feet. His eyes rolled shut and he remained motionless on the mat. A low and nearly inaudible groan escaped his lips. Arizona noticed something was very wrong. “Doctor!” he yelled, “someone please get a doctor!” He started to panic at the sight of his friend and client on the mat barely moving. The pain that Chris felt could be seen on his facial expression. Arizona kneeled by his side and placed his hands over Chris to keep him from moving. He looked around to see if anyone heard his cries for help. “Someone get a fucking doctor!” Arizona screamed, fear showed in his tone. From the main part of the gym, three men came running in to help. Chris tried to open his eyes, to see what was going on around him, but the pain was too sharp and piercing. He could feel everything getting cold and dark. Before Chris knew it he passed out. That night a single lamp was left on, sitting on a desk. A man sat at the desk with a pen in hand. The light gently brushed against his skin showing a look of concern. There were crumples of paper shattered around the desk. He tried again to put the pen to the paper. To Whom It May Concern: I don’t have much time left. I do not know when but I know the sand is dropping into the hour glass. Grain by grain another second ticks by. My fate is slowly but surely approaching me. I am going to die. Tear drops fell onto the paper creating tiny splashes. The hand could no longer write, it placed the pen down on the desk. The writer moved his hands up to his face and cupped his nose and mouth, covering his identity. The two hands moved up and down rubbing the bridge of the nose. The tears hit his hands and rolled down along the hands until they fell onto the desk. The hands moved down and came together just covering his mouth, revealing his identity. Chris Storm took a deep breath and picked up the pen again. His other hand moved to the back of his neck. He rubbed where the neck met the spine. His hand moved over a small lump that had formed. He fought back the tears as he put the pen back to the tear-dropped letter. A few days ago, as usual, I was in the gym practicing. I have a Global title defense coming up against Jesse Jamester, Apocalypse, and Patti Anderson. Well Arizona told me that since Jesse Jamester and Apocalypse are bigger than me that I should practice my landing. He figured since I’m not use to larger opponents that I will get slammed a lot. I bounced against the ropes and performed front flips so I could land flat on my back but when I hit a sharp pain went through my body. I thought I messed it up; I got really scared that I might have accidentally injured myself. There was no way I could have predicted what the doctor had in store for me. I have cancer. It turns out there is a lump that formed at the top of my spine. I must have accidentally landed on when I took the fall. The lump was cancerous. They ran some test, staging they called it. They wanted to see just how far the cancer has spread in my body. When I talked to the doctor later, he told me I was going to die. He gave me anywhere from three months to even a year. They need to see how I respond to the treatment first. I go back tomorrow. I am scared. I am really scared. I don’t want to die. Chris could not hold back the tears anymore. He turned away from the desk and buried his face into his hands. Something in Chris did not want to believe it. A voice kept telling him it was all just a bad dream. He had allowed himself to be stressed after all the arguing that happened between himself and PTC. His reign as champion had started very rocky, Chris did not want to defend against Patti Anderson and it caused a large argument between him and management. That voice told him it was just the stress from that which triggered the bad dream. He would go to sleep soon and when he woke up it would all be gone, there would be no lump or cancer. Chris could go into his defense with a clear mind and come out with the title. No, it did not work like that. Chris knew in his heart it was no dream. He just could not believe this was happening to him though. He was in perfect shape. Wrestling had toned his body amazingly. He trained like a madman when he had matches such as the upcoming Global title match. It had not mattered in the end. He had noticed a sudden drop in his weight. Arizona started to complain about how Chris needed more and more breaks during practice. All the signs were there but Chris never noticed. Chris stood up and walked over to the bed. All the emotions that ran through him had drained every last ounce of energy. He plopped down on his bed and lay down. With his clothes still on, Chris closed his eyes. “Dear Lord it’s me again. I know I said I would not bother you anymore but something has come up. I’m really scared. I don’t want to die. You have to help me. They call you a Savior; well right now I need one. Please, you can’t let me die.” Tears rolled down his eyes. Chris had to stop, unable to continue. Through his life he faced many opponents. Now he faced one he was not sure he could defeat. It seemed too much for the Last Great Technical Wrestler to handle. He had gone from being on top of the world, winning the Global title, to crashing back down to earth. He needed to gather himself together if he wanted to retain that title when he had to face off against his three opponents. The emotional drained Storm succumbed to sleep. The champion would not dream of suplexes and arm bars. He found himself in a hard place in his wrestling career. He had overcame a lot in his life but though it all he beat the odds. Could he beat this and three other capable opponents? Could he even survive it?
