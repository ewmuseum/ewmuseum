So What's All The Hub-Bub With That Big Guy Who Can't Stop Following Me Places And That Fat Cow?

18 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/18/04 Alyssa 'Fat Cow' Sanders I watched the replay of me kicking her fat ass up and down the ring. It was sweet. KICKWHAMIMFUCKINGBETTERTHANYOUPERIOD~! Coo-coo Ca-choo, the bitch was done! The way she kept throwing her stuff in my face. She was practically screaming out, 'OH, INVINCIBLE GABE~! I WANT TO BE THE MOTHER OF YOUR CHILD RIGHT HERE ON THIS MAT!' Yeah, like I haven't gotten that offer before. Seriously. I kicked her ass and she wants me to put the King Kong Dong in her?! Yeah, honky-fucking right! If The Almighty Master of Your Mom wants a woman, she'll be almost as good as he is. She...was crap. She botched so many moves, even I broke a sweat making our match look good! Scott 'Rampage' Matthews, Gabe's #1 Fan I also saw the little encounter she had with my ward, Rampage. I mean, seriously. Who THE FUCK was she trying to kid? Trying to get with him? The man trained by Invincible Gabe has no time to waste by even giving a second glance to that mortal hooker. But there was something in his eyes. Something that told me he liked what he saw. What is that feeling people call? The one where they pay some slut a ludicrous amount of money to feel? Yeah, Wuv. I saw it on Futurama. (FYI, that future is a lot closer than you think. I have one of those crab-like Zoidbergs as a pet in my mighty intergalactic Bachelor Pad of Kickassitude) The Concept of Wuv Wuv. It only confuses me. I can't stand it, never liked it. I always thought that 'Wham, Bam, Thank You, Gabe!' was good enough. But...that Alyssa and the rolls of fat on her stomach shan't woo my ward away from our goal of taking those straps from the StarDyke Troopers! Even if she truly 'Wuvs' Scott, it is simply an emotion that doesn't kick nearly as much ass as...kicking much ass. Wuv is a waste of time and only delays MY chance to win the Tag Titles. I must find some way of smoldering that wuv-struck fire and get Invincibleness~! back on track, thus beginning Operation Invincible Gabe Owns You All and Proves His Mighty Masculinity by Taking Those Straps! As For The StarDyke Troopers... Just fork over the tag straps and quit sending your fucking whore and her penis-eating diseases to delay the inevitable! Now we return you to your regularly scheduled programming which will seem completely fucking bland now that the Guy With More Moves Than The HIT has shined down his infinite wisdom, thus shooting your IQ up by 1 billion points. This is the Eight-Foot Phenomenon called IN-FUCKING-VINCIBLE GABE singing off...
