No Choice (featuring Chris Storm)

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/03 “Come on Tessa, it’s time to get up.” The voice of Chris Storm roared across the living room of his New York apartment as my baby blues slowly crept open. The first thing that caught my sight was the clock which illuminated the dark room with red pixels which read 5:00am. “Are you kidding me? It’s five in the morning. If you want to get up that is great for you. But as far as I’m concerned it’s time for me to get back to sleep. So, goodnight to you.” I replied with a yawn as I buried my head under the pillow given to me. I might have been the kidnapped one, but there was no way in hell I was going to be the puppet. “I said get up!” I shouted at her. Tessa just rolled over not paying any attention to me. I smirked looking at her, she still did not understand the position she was in. I took the glass I had brought with me and dumped the ice cold water on her head causing her to shriek out and jump to her feet. “Good morning sleepy head.” “Good morning asshole.” I retorted, showing my true morning side as I rose to my feet and stormed out of the room. If Chris was trying to get on my good side, then he was failing miserably. However I wasn’t going to let him get on my nerves, despite not being to happy about being up first thing in the morning due to having cold water splashed on me. I followed her out of the room and grabbed her arm, forcing her to turn around. “Just where do you think you are going? We have a long day ahead of us today.” Tessa turned her head away and tried to not even look at me like she did not see me. Angered with her attitude I shoved her toward the bed causing her to fall on it. “I left you some clothes to change into. I expect to see you in them within thirty minutes. If I come back and you are not dressed, you will find yourself appearing on Adrenaline without them.” I yelled at her before leaving her alone to the sound of the door slamming. “Damn men.” I muttered to myself as I fell back onto the bed with a sigh. I looked over the pile of clothes I was instructed to wear and nearly died at the sight. What on earth did Chris think I was? From the look of the clothes left for me it was Barbie gone bad. There was no way in hell I was going to wear a leather mini skirt that barely covered my ass as well as a top which showed every little bit of cleavage that it could even if it was a innocent shade of pink. While the brat was getting dressed I made sure everything was going to be ready for the night. I made sure no one was looking even though I was in the safety of my own apartment and reached into a drawer in my kitchen. Out came a jump suit and hat that looked to belong to a crew member of the arena. I made sure I still had the security tape and small video recorder intact as well. I stopped to look at my watch. “Are you almost ready in there?” I yelled. “Ah the hell with it” I muttered to myself. I was tired of waiting for her, if she wasn’t fully dressed yet then it was just going to be a free show for me. I put the stuff away and turned to go back to the room Tessa was changing in. “Ready or not, here I come.” The voice of Chris echoed from outside the door. Within second the door flung open as Chris directed his cold blue eyes over in my direction. His face nearly dropped to the ground as his eyes caught sight of me, still dressed in the same silk pink pajamas as before. “Something wrong Chris?” I teased, though it probably was not the wisest of things to do considering Chris was in home territory. “I thought I told you to put on the fucking clothes I left for you.” The tone of his voice grew louder while his cheeks began to turn an angry shade of red. “I did.” I replied with a smirk as I slightly tugged at the waistline of my pants, revealing the pair of leather pants underneath them. “You never said I couldn’t wear anything over them though.” I chuckled as I marched proudly out of the room. “Clever” I smirked, “very clever.” Tessa just smiled at me until my smirk disappeared. I went to her and grabbed her fist throwing her on the bed again. This time I pressed my body against hers. I moved my face right next to her ear. “You think you are smart you don’t you? Well you forget I am still much stronger than you, you little slut.” I hissed at her. My hands darted to her pajama top and lifted it off revealing the pink top I had given her. My hand moved to the pants and pulled them down showing off the leather mini shirt. I grabbed the clothes and pushed away from her. Holding them in my hand I made my way to the door. “You can get these back AFTER Adrenaline. If you play nice that is and don’t give me any more shit.” I laughed at the teary eyed Tessa. “Oh come on now don’t be so sad. I’m sure that your little buddy Jacko will love to see you dressed like this? He might even get up the nerve to make a move on you, you little slut.” I tapped my chin thinking about it. “Nah…he’s too much of a wuss to ever do anything like that.” I said with a laugh as I left her alone in the room once more with the door slammed behind me, this time by Tessa. Slamming the door did little good for me considering Chris simply pushed it open once more and stormed right back in. “Slam the door in my face once more and I’ll be sure to return you the favor.” He growled as he made sure to move his face in close to mine so that I would get the point, “Then you stop using the word slut when your little pal Alyssa isn’t around considering we all know she is the only one that fits that description around here.” I replied, turning my face away from his while exiting the room. “And get me something decent to wear. There is no way in hell I’m wearing something like this all day.” I simply laughed at her. “You don’t seem to understand do you? Ah it makes no difference. Enjoy what I have bought for you, after all you might as well get comfortable in it seeing as it will be your attire for Adrenaline.” I told her. I put my hand over her face to farther prove my point and shoved her back. “You see I am in complete control here, so get use to it!” Tessa looked at me infuriated. I just laughed at her useless attempt to get angry. “Go on and throw a fit, see if it helps your cause any. If you think that outfit is bad I could come up with much worse! You are lucky I am even letting you wear anything at all. If I wished I could have you parade around here naked for my amusement.” I informed her. I waved her pajamas in her face to show how easy it was for me to remove her clothes. “You are such a heartless bastard. What has happened to you Chris?” I looked over at him as tears formed in my eyes while he continued to dangle my outfit over my head. Instead of swatting at the pajamas like a kitten I lowered my head and walked off in the direction of the living room in hopes of catching a quick nap to temporarily erase the reminder of the situation I was currently in. The thought of stealing the rest from her played in my head but I let it pass. She could rest for now it would give me more time to prepare for the upcoming show. I would have plenty that needed to be set up before the show and it all had to go flawless. The sound of Tessa settling into the couch echoed out in the room. I cast my eyes over to see her try to get comfortable on the couch while still trying to cover herself despite the revealing clothes, I had to laugh at her vain attempt. She could grab at the leather mini skirt and try to pull it down farther to cover the lower half of her ass but it would always ride back up. “SHUT UP!” I shouted at Chris as I tossed one of the pillows on the couch in his direction in hopes that it would shut him up. The last thing I wanted right now was to be mocked so I pulled the blanket over me instead of bothering to struggle with the skirt anymore and hid under there. I caught the pillow with ease and dropped it on the ground beside the couch. I leaned over by where her head was on the couch. Her eyes were closed so she never saw me move there. “Good night my little slut.” I whispered to her before walking off laughing. Tears started to roll down Tessa’s cheek through her closed eyelids. Under the blanket you could still see she was fighting with the mini shirt. “Good night asshole.” I replied, closing my eyes tighter while I heard Chris foots steps tap against the tile before they became too far away for me to hear. I could only wonder what Chris had planned for me and if there was anything I could do that would bring the old Chris back.
