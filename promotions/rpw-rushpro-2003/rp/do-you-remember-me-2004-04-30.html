Do You Remember Me?

30 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 30th, 2004 Chapter 1: The Beginning of the End Who am I and why do I do this? My name is Chris Storm. Yeah good job naming me parents. Do you think you could have given me a worst name? I should have changed it a long time ago but I got into wrestling. You know how it is. Once you get into wrestling and make it big, it’s hard to change your name. All the fans in WIW, that’s World Internet Wrestling for anyone who didn’t know, knew me as Chris Storm. There is another great name huh? So if I changed my name it would have meant starting all over. They had me wrestle as The Unknown for a while. It was just me under a mask to try and surprise people when I took it off and revealed I was heel. The Unknown was worst than Chris Storm though. I guess I’m just stuck with a crappy name. Like I said before, I’m a wrestler. Sure, at dinner parties people look at me funny when I tell them what I do for a living. “Yeah I drop people on their head with suplexes.” It pays the bills though. Ok so with the current gig I have, Rush Pro Wrestling, I’m not making that much. I’m only getting $1,500 a week to wrestle. RPW, that’s what we call Rush Pro, is a small place so they can’t play me top billing. Yeah I know I said earlier that I’m a big wrestler. So I should be making a lot more. WIW was paying me $150,000 a year. That’s twice what I’m making now. But my career has not been as great as I would have liked. I allowed my personal life to get in the way of my professional life. That was the biggest mistake I could have ever made. It started with WIW. I could tell Jason C. was having some problems. I just didn’t know how badly. Jason C. owned WIW along with an old wrestler named Haze. Normally C. likes to keep off the camera unless the ratings are bad. Well he had been on camera a lot the last few weeks. That should have been my first hint. Turned out he went broke. The investors didn’t like the product and pulled out. WIW closed. At least they placed me into the Hall of Fame before they closed. I was a Grand Slam champion there. That meant I held every single title there. Chapter 2: Meeting Love So I went from a quarter of a million a year to nothing. I was in trouble. Looking back, I think I’m part of the reason WIW closed. You see at that time I had met Tessa Windsor. She was wrestling for this creep Tom M.. He had her stuck in the Woman’s Division and she hated it. So I talked her into quitting and joining us. Backstage things started to heat up between us. Soon we were dating. She was supposed to align with my arch rival Erik Erratic but when she and I started dating the storylines was changed so that she and I would join together. That was the start of the Schmoopies. I’ll get to what a Schmoopies is later. When Tessa and I started dating the whole show started to revolve around us. I was the World champion and she was the Intercontinental champion. So together, we held the two highest belts. It basically killed a lot of interest I think. Yeah it was that whole personal life getting in the way of a professional life. With WIW in the shitter and me needing a job, I started to try and get my name out there. There was this major company in Atlanta called Primetime Championship Wrestling. It’s supposed to be a big time league. Now they offered me a contract but to this day, I still have a gut feeling that they only hired me so they could get Tessa to join since she was looking into that Five Star Wrestling place. I think that is one of the reasons I’m bitter toward her today. I didn’t like the fact that I was the World champ and Hall of Famer yet they only wanted me for her. Chapter 3: My time in PCW So I backed my backs and moved to Atlanta. Tessa and I got a place together. Turned out they wanted us as a tag team. Later we would be named the Schmoopies. That name actually came about as a way to try and get back at the people making fun of us. You see people didn’t like how lovey-dovey we were on television. Well ok, that is a lie. The management loved it. I should know because every month I threatened to quit if they didn’t let me change it around. Course each month Tessa talked me into staying. I couldn’t stand it that everyone was calling us the Schmoopies behind our backs. But we decided to run with the name to show them we would prove them wrong. Let me say this right now. I absolutely hated PCW with every fiber in my body. They paid me great money but they killed my wrestling career. I was one of the most serious wrestlers you could find, a real career killer for technical wrestling. But they made me this pussy. My wrestling career has never recovered from the image I got when I was in PCW. No one has taken me as a serious wrestling since then. I’ve just been this big jobber (a jobber is a term used to describe someone in the wrestling business that always loses). Needless to say, I quit PCW. I left on good terms with them thankfully. They even sent me an e-mail to join them when they reopened. I declined though. PCW wrote me out of the storylines by having Nerezza of P.W.A., our arch rivals, slam my head into a brick wall. Some times, I wish Rez actually had driven my head into the wall. It might have knocked some since into me. The next few career decisions I would make would not be my best.
