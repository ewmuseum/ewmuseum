Beachin' it Up

8 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/08/04 Scene opens on a hot, sandy beach somewhere in the sunny state of Florida. The sun is shining down brightly, sending it's warm rays across the beach, which is littered with men, women and children of all ages, all dressed in bright bathing suits ranging in style and colour. Women lay on towels with the back of their bikini tops unstrapped to prevent tan lines. Children scurry around in the sand, running from the torquoise waves as they roll quietly onto the warm sand, destroying sandcastles and wetting many a foot while making a gentle wooshing noise. Cries of joy and exasperation can be heard from the water as families and friends frollock in the warm, gentle waves. Throwing inflatable balls around, body surfing and splashing contests are among some of the most popular activies going on amongst the happy sun bathers. Seagulls glide peacefully over this tranquil, joyous scene, scarecely making a sound out of their orange, pointed beaks. On one side of the beach, teenagers are engaged in an exciting game of beach volleyball. Four on Four. Each team consisting of all males. Young, teenage girls sit on their beach towels, watching the game while chatting amongst themselves. The boys are heavily involved in the game, anxious to show off their skills to the young ladies surveying the game. Bodies jump and dive through the air, batting the white volleyball around as if everything depends on it staying in the air. One of them hits a vicious spike, sending the ball over the net, slamming into the sand. The scoring team cheers and gloats to the losers, who groan loudly and throw the ball back over the net. A large form walks over to the man made court and stretches out a massive hand, which snatches the ball out of mid air. The camera zooms in closer to catch a glimpse of a man who is without a doubt, the most stunning physical specimen on the entire beach. The teenage girls immediately take notice, widening their eyes and whispering amongst one another. The boys just stare in wide-mouthed awe at the massive, muscle-bound man grinning back at them. His body looks chisled to perfection, and his muscles twitch with every movement of his body. Is skin is tanned to a golden brown and his teeth sparkle above a large, defined chin. Behind him is a beautiful, raven-haired babe in a red string bikini. Her piercing, cold blue eyes stare out from under thin, stern looking eyebrows. Her small lips are twisted in a half smirk as she walks up closer to the muscular man, laying one hand on one of his massive shoulders, and one hand on her curvacious hip. The boys turn their attention towards the vuluptuous, raven-haired goddess but then quickly back to the man, who finally speaks, startling the young boys at first with the volume of his deep, booming voice. 'Hey dudes, I see you got a game goin' on. Mind if I join in?' There is a long delay in their reaction, but finally, one of the boys speaks up. His voice squeaks briefly at first. 'S..Sure. W-Who wants to sit out?' The other three boys simultaneously shout out an affirmative answer to the question, causing the large man to chuckle underneath his breath. It's a deep chuckle, resonating from his massive, barrel-like chest. Meanwhile, the smallest of the boys takes the initiative and physically leaves the court, solving the dilemma. The large man nods in approval and steps forward, taking the spot of the boy who just left the court. 'So...who's leadin'?' The same boy who spoke up first speaks up again, after running a hand through his scruffy, brown hair and thinking for a second. His mind has obviously been jarred by something or another. 'W-We are.' A small smirk crosses the lips of the man upon hearing this, and he cracks his knuckles loudly. The other boys cringe at the loud crack. 'Sweet. I've never been on a losing team before in my life, and I'm about to start now. So..what-do-ya say we get this thing started, dudes?!' They all exchange glances and shrug their shoulders before smiling confidentally and taking position. The other team exchange nervous glances and gulps. The large man puts the ball in position to serve, then turns briefly to his new found team mates. 'By the way. Hi dudes, my name is Rod. But you can just call me Surge.' With that, 'Surge' throws the ball up in the air and bats it over the net with amazing force. His hand makes a gigantic slapping noise among hitting the ball. The opposing team just barely keeps the ball from hitting the sand, batting it back over the net. One of the boys heads the ball up in the air, using a little bit too much force in his strike. The ball flies up really high and looks like it's going to go out of bounds, until Surge leaps into the air, gaining massive height. He extends one of his powerful arms and swings it down and forward, spiking the ball out of the air. The ball comes down hard and just misses the top of the net, slamming into the beach and sending sand flying up into the faces of the now-losers. The boys let out a cheer and crowd around Surge, patting him on his massive back and slapping five with his massive hands. The girls stand up and cheer as well, not noticing the angry, jealous glance being shot at them by Candy, Surge's girlfriend. She immediately walks over to Surge, grabs both sides of his face and plants him with a steamy kiss right in front of everybody. After the smooch is over, the raven-haired beauty glares seductively into Surge's eyes then leans in close, cooing softly into his ear. 'Good job, baby. You kicked those punk kids' asses' Surge smirks arrogantly and wraps one of his pythons around the tanned back of his bikini clad girlfriend, then looks at his team mates. 'Nice playin' with ya, dudes.' He turns towards the losers who are kicking at the sand and muttering under their breaths. 'You guys too. Even though you're big time LOS-ERS!' Surge shows his true colours all at once, chuckling heartily and flashing the L-Sign on his forehead and he walks away arm in arm with his gorgeous girlfriend. The teenagers look at each other with confused and angered looks on their faces while the girls get one last glance at the muscle bound adonis. Meanwhile, the man they call 'Rodzilla' walks along the beach with Candy Dickinson. As they walk along the warm sand, many of the beach goers turn their heads to make a double take of the attractive young couple. They are clearly the envy of the beach, since they both look like something out of a Fitness magazine. They both sit down close to the water and watch the waves coming in. Surge wraps one of his muscular arms around Candy and rubs her shoulder softly. He lets out a long sigh. 'I can't wait to find some REAL competition. Hopefully this wrestling thing'll be just the thing I've been lookin' for.' Candy smiles warmly and lays her hand on Surge's leg. She answers in her smooth, sexy rasp. Her voice is feminine, yet it has a strong, domineering quality to it. 'This is different, honey. It's not like those piss pore, tiny wrestling promotions in those musty, rathole bingo halls. This one is televised. We're gonna be famous, baby.' An arrogant smirk materializes on Surge's face once again. He nods slowly, as if answering a question that he just asked himself in his head. Or maybe agreeing with something he just thought. 'Well, they better be ready for Rodzilla.' Candy giggles and slaps his shoulder lightly and playfully. 'Whatever, 'Rodzilla'. I'm sure you'll turn a lot of heads. I know you turn mine.' She leans in slowly, staring through her mesmerizing eyes. Surge leans in to meet her halfway, grasping her in another lip lock. Candy smiles warmly again and puts her hand on Surge's shoulder, pushing herself up onto her feet. 'I'm gonna go find the ladies room. Be back in a bit.' Surge looks up and smiles back at her. 'Later, babe. I'll be waitin' right here when you get back.' Candy blows a kiss in Surge's direction, who pretends to catch it in mid-air and put it in an imaginary pocket. She slowly disappears from sight as she walks across the beach, becoming lost in the crowded beach. Surge leans back on his massive arms and stares out at the ocean. Suddenly, three beautiful girls walk up to him. One blond, one brunette and one red head. They're all incredibly hot and are wearing teeny weeny bikinis. They stand no more than 2 feet away and smile down at Surge. The blond is the first to speak. 'Hey...me and my friends were just getting ready to do some tanning and....we were wondering if you could rub some tanning lotion on our backs. We'd do it ourselves but we can't reach.' A huge grin slowly appears on Surge's face. 'Who's first?' Scene fades out as Surge stands up, takes the bottle of lotion in his hands and squeezes a small puddle of the white, greasy liquid into his hand. He rubs both hands together with a huge grin on his face and approaches the three bikini-clad babes as they lay down belly first on their towels, then proceed to unhook the back of their bras. The rest...well...that's up to your sick, perverted little imagination!
