Thoughts and Ramblings

20 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th of January, 2004 Why the hell are all these massive blokes comin’ after me? First the six foot eleven Neo Void God has a problem with me and gets me bloody fired. Then, when I beat his arse, another huge pillock comes my way. Jesus Christ, why can’t I just get a few of them flyin’ wrestlers that look have too strain their necks to look up into the face of munchkins. No chance they’d cause me this much feckin’ pain…unless they use a shovel of course, aye, that Carys one gave me a right migraine with one of them to the temple. But y’know, I’d rather her with a shovel than having VJ’s henchbitch trying to separate me spine from me pelvis. Argh, me back is in bits. I wish this armchair were one of them Easy-Boy chairs. Maybe I should take the next show off, last thing I need right now is bein’ dropped on me head. Aye, I think that’s something we could all do without. Eh, it’s not like RPW seems to be going anywhere anyway with us getting’ kicked off the TV and all. That fuckin’ Mexican had to ruin all the fun, didn’t he? Who knew there were vampires in Meh-Hi-Co? It’s a struggle to get up off me arse today. I’ve been beaten up worse than what Perdition gave me but for some reason, I’ve never felt as bloody sore as I am today. Maybe at twenty-seven years old I’m gettin’ too old. Maybe I should pack in the ringwork and have another try behind the desk as a booker or somethin’ like that. Meh, fuck it, I think I’ll stick it in the ring…still a few years left at me I think. Me back is bloody achin’ though. I got up to get a can of the black stuff out of the fridge – I barely made it past the TV set. Hmm…an extra week off is soundin’ rosy right now. As I sit back and look at some daytime TV with a half-empty can resting in my hand, I wonder if at twenty-seven years old, five years pro, am I really doing the right thing wrestling in the lower mid-cards in a wrestling company ran by a sixteen year old smart arse without a TV deal. I could be wrestling elsewhere; I heard Action, PWW and FAW were interested in my services and on a more permanent deal than RPW. Even Vintage Wrestling contacted me about me availability – so I have other options. RPW haven’t exactly been great to me by any stretch of the imagination, so I wouldn’t really feel any guilt leaving…a bridge I really wouldn’t mind throwing a match onto. Eh, doubt they’d see me as a big loss anyway. Maybe I’ll leave once I take out VJ and his beehatches. I wouldn’t really be pissing off anyone I like if I left…simply because there is no one I like there. Well, Lumi’s a nice girl but where was she when I was getting accused of all sorts? It might be a better option to leave the world of wrestling completely…just sever all ties. I could do that y’know, go back to Sacramento and run me nightclub there (‘tis called BLING, I reckon ya should check it out if you’re in the area). I’ve left Rio Espines to run the place in my absence; he says everything is going smoothly. I’ll take a trip up there next week to make sure the boyo isn’t lying, I wouldn’t be surprised if he’s converted the place into a whorehouse. I fucking hate it down here. I live in this piss-pot and all the people in this god-forsaken place are always tryin’ to get on me bad side…well, the blokes in the pubs do. Maybe they don’t like me accent, but what can ya do? I hate this bloody place. Oh, how I long for the beautiful green fields of Wicklow. Lad, ya wouldn’t believe how much I wanna go home. Don’t get me wrong, I like Americans and all but the place has really grown old on me. Everythin’ just goes too fast here for my tastes…things are just so much easier back home. Miles of green fields replace miles of grey, boring buildings. God, I feel like breaking into song with “The Fields Of Athenry”. I struggled to my feet and strolled over to the window overlooking the city. It just looks so depressing. People below in business suits making their way to work like robots, students skiving off of class for a boring day strolling around the streets looking for something to be enthused by. I let my head press against the cold glass and focus on the steam marks left on the window by the breath from my nostrils. I resist sketching a little smiley face onto the breath patch. Instead I draw a sad face…seems a little more fitting for my mood at the moment. I hear a quick double tap knock on the apartment door. It must be your man from the fast food joint. I try get there as quickly as possible; I think my grumbling stomach encourages me to do so. I open it and the spotty faced kid stands there with a paper bag in hand, this kid is actually probably older than my boss. We exchange a couple of polite words. “Four fifty” he says. I hand him a tenner, “Keep the change, lad.” He smiles and thanks me before departing. I just made his hour, which made my minute. Well, the thirty seconds till I took my chicken burger out of the wrapper and realised it was actually three chicken nuggets in between two buns. Ugh, I had half a mind to complain but yeah, today I’m lazy. The nuggets remind me of Shanahan and two of his followers. On their lonesome, ya wouldn’t think much of them. Not much of a threat, really. Put them together and slap a couple of slices of Perdition on either side and hey, they’re suddenly worth $4.50. Now, instead of a snack, they’re a meal. Individually they’re nothing more than run of the mill ‘rasslers…together they’re a force to reckoned with, a force that can put me out of commission whenever they please. Okay, it’s a pretty shite metaphor but work with me here. IF individually, they are indeed average wrestlers…then, I need to take each of them out individually. I need to tear them down…brick by brick. Half way through my nugget/burger or whatever ya want to call it, I try to figure out whether I should go for the temple or take out the knees first. VJ is obviously the temple. He’s the most important part of the equation and if you take him out, the whole group collapses. HOWEVER, VJ will be the most difficult to attack with his followers surrounding and protecting him at all times. So, what I need to do is take out the knees of this team. With the knees gone, the temple is extremely vulnerable. Without Ethan Knight, Horace Tully and Perdition, he is almost completely unprotected. Knight and Tully should be relatively easy to take out…these two haven’t got much to them…if they did, they wouldn’t be the ones following, they’d be the ones with the followers. Knight and Tully are the two I need to take out first. That’s one knee shattered. Then there is Perdition…this guy is a house of a man. Seven foot plus, this guy will be no walk in the park to finish off…but he doesn’t exactly seem like the brightest bulb. If I can get Perdition on his own, one on one, both prepared and ready I think I can take him…hopefully. If I can shatter that knee, then VJ is mine…ALL mine. Jack is a man I don’t think highly of. Never have, never will. If I can take out his legs from beneath him then I he is no match for me. Without Knight, Tully and Perdition, VJ cannot handle me…this, I am sure. This is what I need to do…this is what will free me of these heathen demons…and then maybe I’ll look upon my life in RPW with a new, brighter light. And that concludes my day of thought and ramblings. Thank ya kindly.
