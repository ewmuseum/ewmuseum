Before Today #17 : A Long Way Down

5 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/02/04 It had come down to this. Two rivals. One match. No rules. For 20 minutes, they'd both been beaten numb, to the point where Magnum's throbbing body couldn't even muster anymore pain to be felt when Karina's kicks hit, while the K-Wolf's surging adrenaline absorbed Wolf's punches... All that separated them was a ladder. On either side of the tool, they hung on for dear life, knowing that a fall would flick their tired bodies' switch well and truly off. Whoever fell would have to accept a loss, would have to listen to the other say that they'd beaten them fairly and squarely... It was an indignity that would most likely cause the loser to leave the Pro Wrestling Circuit, rather than having to hear it week in and week out. Magnum's fist clubbed Karina's jaw, but instead of a real pain, Karina just felt the impact jar her jaw-bone... A dull sensation, like being prodded out of slumber. In retaliation, Wolfenden took a risk by swinging her body to the right, flinging a powerful leg around the ladder to connect with Wolf's ribs. His sides, stomach and legs were already one huge, purple marbled bruise from relentless strikes... And the weak kick was like a raindrop in the ocean. Both took the chance to climb another rung, hunting for any extra leverage to give their blows a little more 'umph'. 'The weaker they are,' Magnum sneered through bloody teeth, 'the easier they fall...' Wolf reached over the ladder, causing Karina to flinch as if a punch were coming, but instead Magnum grabbed a handful of her pink fringe. 'And you're wayyy to easy,' he then added, with lavish amounts of pleasure, before yanking Karina's forehead down onto the ladder's top step. The steel sent a piercing pain around Karina's skull, causing her left foot to slip off it's rung. Wolf hung on tighter as the ladder wobbled, Karina's hugging of the rungs causing it to rock. 'And the bigger asshole they are,' Kari quipped back, screwing her eyes up as her vision slowly began to un-cloud, 'the harder I laugh when they fall.' Through the rungs, Karina thrust a gloved fist towards Magnum's groin, connecting with as much power as her muscles could muster. 'And you're a biggg asshole.' It was a low blow, but it was far from the sneakiest action of their rivalry... And Karina relished every millisecond her thinly padded knuckles crunched Wolf's left teste against his own thigh. A sickly groan was expelled by Magnum as his eyes rolled back. He had to fight the urge to let go of the ladder and grab the pained area, which pulsed agony up into his stomach. Down a rung Magnum dropped, his eyes glistening with the makings of tears, while an acidic sensation tickled the back of his throat, tempting his urge to throw up. Sensing her chance, Karina used her burning thigh muscles to shoot her up, until there was no further to climb on her side of the ladder. With Magnum slouched forward, mumbling about how 'The Package~!' had been smited and violated, Karina could see over him and down to the ring below, where a cheap wooden table was set up. Karina knew it would barely break her fall if she tried anything, but it would be perfect for driving Magnum through... And from that moment, there was no turning back. Showing a sub-conscious survival instinct, Karina's body resisted her, but through sheer will she overcame it. All her muscles were aching, but they were still moving, still working... So she'd put them to use. Karina swung her body up, using the top of the ladder like a gymnastics horse to land on Magnum's shoulders. They were both facing the same way, away from where Karina wanted them to go. Before Magnum could react, Karina's thigh's clamed around his head, muffling his ears so the cheers from the popping crowd couldn't reach him. 'Here goes nothing,' Karina whispered to herself, hoping that this wouldn't be the fall that ended it all. Backwards Wolfenden's body thrust, her back arcing sharply into a backflip. Her legs followed the flight path, their grip strong enough to peel Magnum from the ladder and send him flipping over and down with her. Kari's legs relaxed, releasing Wolf from their guidance, not that he could do anything about it. As soon as Kari's thighs freed Wolf's ears, he heard the briefest of cheers, before he saw the brown flash of a table. As expected, the wood did little to break their fall, especially Wolf's. His chest's impact cracked the wood, not even effecting his landing angle as he crashed front-first down into the shards and canvas. Beside him, Karina's landing wasn't much better, and far from the perfect landings on her feet she was used to making. All she could do was contort her body to reduce injury, not even thinking about putting her hands out to soften the impact... She knew her wrists wouldn't be able to take it. And there they lay, 2 rivals left motionless on the mat with the debris of the table strewn all around them... ...Before the ladder toppled and fell between them, acting as the final dividing line. The referee wanted to stop it then, but knew he had to go through the formality of a 10 count. Hurriedly, he counted both of them down, then called for the EMTs before even thinking about calling for the bell. Karina Wolfenden's sacrificial move hadn't beaten Magnum Wolf, and all Magnum Wolf's mid-games and distractions hadn't beaten Karina Wolfenden… …And thus, there was a calm.
