Where Bullets May Fall

10 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-02-04 He stood enveloped by darkness, head bowed and eyes closed almost as if in prayer. The void around him stretched far into the beyond, yet his presence somehow reduced the vast emptiness into a claustrophobic limbo. It seemed desolate, but in the midst of nothingness seemed to be a grand commotion. A muffled ovation comprised of thousands of voices, of monstrous screaming all pounding and pulsating through the void. He opened his eyes and looked down at the sidewalk, focusing on the tumult as it slowly became clear and crisp. Looking up at the sea of motion, he watched as it crashed into the many large reflective mountains, filled with just as many souls slaving away and staring out to the sea, wishing they could simply reach out and join it once more, delving back into obscurity. Too wrapped up in their own lives not one of them noticed the gleaming black gun he held in his hand. How could they when he was standing atop the tallest building? “It’s taken too long to get here…” The journey had been tough. Yet he had persevered through it all and now his reward was within reach. It all started with a simple notion: to dominate within the family. What should have been an easy task was almost a year in the making, and accomplishment was now just over the horizon. Sacrifice played a large part in getting to where he was now, and it all began on the night that Hessian died. A minor medical problem was exploited and exaggerated to allow himself time to gather his thoughts. In order to avoid being discovered and having his plan thwarted he waited until the mortician was closing in with the scalpel before sitting bolt upright and wrapping both hands around his throat. The bloodcurdling scream made him laugh, and continue to laugh even after the mortician had hit the floor unconscious, from both the fright and the throttling. Looking the gun over, Hessian then peered over the edge of the building at the mass of bodies below. A single bullet occupied the magazine of the Glock, and that was all he needed. The air was calm and to those far below him unaware of the danger lurking above, it was just another plain day. They had always been unaware of just what was stalking them until it was too late, and today was no exception. He raised the gun above his head, pointing to the heavens as though aiming at God. Holding the pose, Hessian bowed his head once more, closing his eyes. “…Now the time has come…” Now safely hidden in the shadows, plotting began to take over supremacy within the family. The world was convinced he was dead, and with time to spare before his prey entered into the OSW, he formulated his plan. In order to succeed, he had to sacrifice everything. In his mind the idea of deceit seemed the best option, which led to more deliberation as to how to deceive the demon. The answer came as a message delivered to him by none other than the Man In Black, Johnny Cash. On passing the very graveyard he was buried in, the tired and broken voice of Cash filled the air, emanating from a crowd of people in mourning as a loved one was buried. Committed to the earth and accompanied by the haunting rendition of “Hurt”, he watched on and listened closely. His eyes slowly widened and at that moment he knew what to do. He thought back to all he had done and accomplished thus far, all who had fallen and all that he had gained. He looked down into the mass of souls and grinned. The gun still pointing at the heavens, Hessian tightened his grip and pulled the trigger ever so slightly, almost as though toying with fate all the while grinning at everyone below. For so long he had been contained, restricted from unleashing a full force of hell. Now he was free, the time had come to break rules and bones in the name of Hessian once more. Pulling the trigger he looked up, knowing the bullet was already delivering a deadly kiss to the heavens. “...The apocalypse has arrived...” Identifying with the words he had heard and recalling the outlaw lifestyle of the Man In Black, Angelus Von Kelsig sacrificed everything about himself and donned typical western attire…taking on the identity of “The Wanted Man”. On Angelus’s OSW contract it was that forename that bound Jones to Von Kelsig. A little more than a month after Hessian had died before the fans in the middle of the ring, Angelus Jones made his debut at the Ascension Pay-Per-View. Thankfully the extreme change in appearance fooled every one of the fans, and left Hessian free to roam the locker room. As he re-entered the world of wrestling so to did his prey…the burned man. Luring the demon was as simple as stringing him along. Half of the fight was fought in the last stretch of OSW, and only Hessian knew it. Aside from the titles and glory, the real satisfaction came from knowing the demon that he had signed a deal with was unable to seal the deal and could do nothing about Hessian breaching the contract. No soul would be sent to hell. The psychological war had been fought and won, and now as Angelus returned as Hessian the time had come to destroy the body in a war of Eternal Damnation. He stood looking at them all, thinking. Unbeknownst to the swarming mass of bodies fate was rising, preparing to fall and strike one of them. He had wondered about this before, how a bullet could be fired straight up into the air…but how no one had ever stopped to think where it would land. Would it fly up and up and up into the atmosphere and beyond? Would it be blown away by a strong wind and fall to the ground out of sight of anyone? Or would it fall right back down into the middle of them…hitting whatever passed beneath it. Again Hessian grinned, now thinking about Clyde…and the RPW. “…Eternal Damnation is upon us…” He had sacrificed everything to get the demon. At times even the very essence of evil within him seemed to be smouldering away into nothing. Yet as he stood tall before the world and unleashed Eternal Damnation once more it was clear the evil within was as strong as ever. While the psychological front had been conquered, dominancy at the head of the family – the top of the food chain – was still slightly out of reach. To do so required a little something extra…it required a recruitment of sorts, from within the family itself. As the ferry neared Oshima Island, Hessian could feel that atmosphere surrounding him. He had been told of the Yamamura Bakemono, and knew he had found what he was looking for. Stepping off onto the shore, he waited for everything to quieten down again…then as he approached the Yamamura Inn the atmosphere changed. Death was close. As he entered the inn the occupants quickly realized there was more to fear than the Bakemono. The stench of death choked him as he entered the basement of the Yamamura Inn, and with open arms he embraced it. He had found Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The only thing left to do now? Destroy the head of the family. Destroy Clyde. The bullet had begun descending. Each second that passed was another off the life of someone below. Rapidly dropping to the earth below, it shot past Hessian who watched and grinned as the thick mass of bodies parted suddenly. Fate had struck. Hessian chuckled, backing away from the edge and walking away. The death of OSW and the arrival of RPW meant now Hessian could finally deliver fate into the hands of the demon. Suddenly the mass of bodies, the thunderous echo of screams and roaring and the light all manifested itself into a void of absolute black. Once again a plain of darkness had fallen. A bullet was descending on RPW.
