In The Mean Time - Reintroduction

2 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 - 02 - 03 A majestic silver beam of light cascades down to the Earth from the Moon, illuminating the jade forest that extends beyond the eye's comprehension. Piercing through the lush green forest is a rocky offshoot, extending from the cliffs towards the moon. Long torches have been stuck along either edge, basking the offshoot in a mixture of fiery orange and beautiful silver. Only the quiet sounds of night's creatures and the crackling of the flames can be heard. The whole scene is peaceful, tranquil… just the right time to be crowning a new Templar. Ten cloaked figures stand in the middle of the offshoot, arranged in duel columns while two men are at the head of it. One is adorned in an extravagant outfit, with thick golden shoulder pads and a tall golden headdress. His shimmering blue robe cloaks his body in darkness, creating a rather ghostly image. The other man, merely a bald figure with a black trench coat, kneels before him with his eyes to the ground. The tall man with the blue robe places his open hand over the kneeling man's head and begins to chant, "Tala ect-dora nes… Moon, fire, and darkness, heed my call! A desirable disciple, through esteemed service and devotion, has brought forth much glory to the Goddess! It is on this night, that another should ascend to the golden pantheon of Templamur." A murmur begins to arise from the group of men, a hushed whisper of doubt. The robed man smirks angrily and calls out again, "Should En'Flarim reject our offer of this man's soul to the higher plane, may she stop us now!" When nothing arises, the murmur falls to a hush. The robed man clears his throat and raises his hands and eyes to the sky, "Now, Goddess En'Flarim, shine this young disciple with your glory and power! Make him a Templar, so that he better serve his people!" He begins to chant something else, but suddenly, the kneeled man speaks. "She has spoken to me already… she's aware of my exploits…" The robed man suddenly looks down at the kneeled figure with a look of confusion on his face. The kneeled man raises his eyes, his clean-shaven face becoming illuminated in the moon's light, and smirks, "… and of your failure." "What is this?! You must be silent during the ceremony!" The man clamors, a little disoriented at this sudden break in the figure's silence. However, despite the robed Templar's objections, the figure rises to his feet. Once again, the Templar exclaims, "What are you doing?!" The man wearing the trench coat turns to the crowd of gathered disciples and booms, "En'Flarim's displeasure of the pantheon is known! The Templamur have too long been failures! They soil our good name and worry only of their wealth and status! I refuse to take the name Templar… but shall rise above and beyond it… to HIGH TEMPLAR!" The Templar looks absolutely aghast at this outburst and begins to back away. "You cannot… this is treason! Tendra is the High Templar! She will-!" "Die by my hand!" The coated figure bursts out, startling the Templar. Suddenly, the 10 other men throw back their hoods, revealing faces with various machine parts attached to their faces. The Templar gasps and backs even further away, "The Brotherhood!!" The men draw guns from beneath their cloaks and point them at the frightened Templar as the figure with the trench coat turns around and grins, crossing his arms across his chest. He steps up to the trembling Astronomy higher-up and takes him by the throat roughly, "No longer will the incompetent Templamur rule things… because now…" He draws a knife from within his coat and quickly thrusts it into the Templar's abdomen before whispering, "… I do." He then releases the Templar, watching the quivering mass drop to the ground. The Templar reaches towards the heavens, gasping and coughing up blood before lying still. The cloaked figure kicks the body over the edge and turns towards the Brotherhood, "Let tonight be the night… that the Astronomers took back what is there's!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ But what are your plans, Dorn? The man with the black trench coat, now identified as Dorn, strolls alongside one of the hooded men from the cliff offshoot as the two walk the forest lake perimeter. Dorn looks to the starry night sky before bringing his gaze back down, "I'll continue my inquisition through professional wrestling. The mass media coverage was enough to bring us back into the limelight, don't you think, Jones?" "Perhaps," The man known as Jones quips, uncertainty sneaking through his tone. "But PCW closed down, did it not?" Dorn nods and looks down at the grass as he walks, "Yeah, but a young upstart company contacted me not long ago, by the name of Rush Pro-Wrestling. They're small, but ample. I cannot be picky as it is, anyway. The revolution needs coverage and En'Flarim must be pleased." "The Templamur are unaware of the revolution and know nothing of En'Flarim's displeasure in them, but I'm sure Tendra knows." "Of course! She's not High Templar for nothing," Dorn chuckles. Jones clears his throat and continues, "Yes. We cannot allow her to interfere. The Brotherhood will keep her and the Templamur at bay, along with the Moonguards, but you must act quickly. Once the other castes and factions learn of the revolution's doings and the assassinations of Templar Yale, they'll be pissed." "I know, my friend," Dorn replies, "I'll act as quickly as I can. I'm bound to run into resistance in RPW, as I did in PCW, so it may take some time. Just keep the Templamur off of me, and En'Flarim's wrath will be brought once more." Jones nods, then sighs, "Let's hope so. Or it may mean the end of us all…" -[End]-
