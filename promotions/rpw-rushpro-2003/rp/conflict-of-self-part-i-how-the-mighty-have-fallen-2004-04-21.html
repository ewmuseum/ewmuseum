Conflict of Self: Part I - How The Mighty Have Fallen

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-04-04 It happens to the best of them. Walking down the street and minding their own business when the inevitable happens, and they hear their name. It’s not exactly what could be construed as a burden to most, but for those in the wrestling business who have planes to catch, meetings to attend and so forth it can be a frustrating waste of time. Hessian had no planes to catch and no meetings to attend; yet the frustration was still there. Why? “Hey Dad, is that Hessian!?” the boy asked rather excitedly. After Old School Wrestling closed down it became harder for the fans to follow their favourite wrestlers, although with a good majority of them signing with Rush Pro Wrestling it seemed to put the fans’ worries at rest. Being in the vicinity of the last show however, it was almost impossible not to be spotted, especially when you were wearing half a faceplate upon your weary face. “Well I’ll be, it is Hessian! Wanna go over and see if we can wrestle an autograph out of him, son?” the father replied, chuckling as his boy nodded, eyes bright and wide. Unaware of anyone approaching him, Hessian sighed. Turning his head every so often into the shop window adjacent to the wall on which he was leaning, the Goliath sighed again, looked back into the sea of bodies as they lumbered past almost like trawled fish. His stance was without care, yet the look in his eyes told the vigil onlooker he was perhaps a little more anxious than he cared to be. He had reason to be, after all…waiting next to an adult bookstore. Now, any normal wrestling fan hearing that for the first time would imagine the 7’2” Mercenary of Satan complete with flowing black cape and menacing attire looming over the public outside a sex shop, threatening death and agony upon passers-by. To add to their amusement possibly also the addition of dancing dildos and singing genitals by Hessian would complete the image. On this day however Hessian was without his regular outfit, instead favouring the rather bland combination of jeans and a bomber jacket, that in itself a strange notion Hessian almost blended into the environment. Becoming increasingly agitated as more glances were shot his way, Hessian turned to the entrance of the adult bookstore just as it opened, and the reason presented itself. The reason why he was frustrated stood before him, holding a shopping bag and grinning up at him cheekily. Why was he frustrated? Minion the Imp. “What the hell’s your problem? You look like you’re about to relieve yourself of constipation or something, lighten up!” was the jovial reply to the look on Hessian’s face. Glancing about the somewhat busy streets, Hessian turned back towards his companion and manager. “The problem is everyone looks over here and sees me standing next to a seedy store like this and you’re nowhere to be found. How do you think that makes me look?” Hessian retorted, scoffing at his 3’11” counterpart. “Hey man I got 30% off of the first volume of MIA: Midgets In Action! Whatever humiliation you go through is worth it for these beauties!” Minion said, removing one of the magazines and showing it off to Hessian. Not even wishing to gaze upon the smut Minion had purchased, Hessian grabbed the magazine and thrust it back down inside the shopping bag, closing it tight and growling at the so-called Walking Apocalypse. Minion was quick to pip in before his besmirching at the hands of the Goliath. “Oh, and since when did you give a damn what people think of you? Don’t be going soft on me now!” Those words were enough to stop Hessian in his tracks, prompting him to back up slightly against the wall again, looking over the sea of people passing by. For some strange reason unbeknownst to him Minion’s words had had an effect on him. Lost for words, Hessian simply snarled and turned away from the Imp to take his leave off down the street…and stumbled straight into the father and son who had been watching him. As the Mercenary of Satan opened his mouth to roar down the clumsy oaf that had bumped into him, he spotted the child by the man’s side and forced himself not to cause a scene. “Hey there, sorry to bother you! You’re from that Rush Pro Wrestling ain’tcha? Hessian isn’t it?” asked the man politely, trying to maintain the respectable father figure in front of his son. “That I am.” Hessian replied, a hint of anger still evident in his voice. Nudging his son, the father pointed to Hessian and smiled at his little boy. “You were right son, it’s Hessian! Now…what do you want to say?” he remarked condescendingly to his boy. The offspring waddled forward, clutching a piece of paper and a pen close to his chest, biting his lip and staring all the way up at Hessian with bright eyes. “Could I please have your autograph Mr. Hessian?” he asked, holding the paper and pen out. Sighing, Hessian took them from the boy and stretched his hand out, holding the paper flat out on his palm and clutching to the top of it with his fingertips. Glancing at the father, Hessian cocked an eyebrow. “Who do I make it out to?” “Hmm…make it out to Dad, Jeff and Son, Brad…then whatever you want to put, thanks!” came the polite reply once more from the father. For the first time Hessian was up close and personal with fans, and from the politeness of the son even in the face of the Goliath, his mind was fraught with thoughts of wonderment. Surely the child before him should be cowering, or at the very least have a look on his face that suggested Hessian was dangerous in some form? Instead the boy ogled the Mercenary of Satan with doe eyes as though he were his hero. Topping off the autograph with his signature Hessian knelt down, handing the pen to Minion and allowing him to scribble his name on the bottom as well. Taking the autograph, Hessian handed it to the boy, whose eyes lit up even more than thought possible. Looking up at his dad, the boy nudged him waving the autograph in front of him. As the boy did a little jig and read it over and over Hessian got back up and turned to the father. “How old is he?” Hessian asked. “He’ll be eleven in May.” The father replied. “Do you really let him watch all the violence I partake in?” Hessian’s queries were being influenced by wonder of the boy’s fear – or lack thereof – shown towards him. Brad’s father took a moment to think. “Well…” he began. “He’s been watching wrestling for a few years now and my wife and I have always told him that it’s dangerous and professionals do it and so forth. Thankfully he’s never tried any of the moves, and when he sees you on television doing all that supernatural stuff we try to tell him that it’s not real, even if we aren’t sure ourselves y’know, heh. So we just let him watch it, there’s no harm in it.” The father’s words struck Hessian as the look of the boy had. It’s not real was the specific phrase that kept circling around in his mind. As a dark gimmick he should have reduced the streets to a flowing river of blood and flesh…but instead he found himself taken aback by the boy, and a question that haunted him to his very soul. “Hey Mr. Hessian can you make me float like you made Nerezza?” the boy asked innocently, referring to an incident many weeks previously just after RPW’s Maximum Overdrive PPV where Hessian had somehow summoned the power to make his own vampiric bastard son Nerezza elevate in a bout of anger. Hessian ignored the father’s response trying to convince his son that was fake too, as he felt his stomach drop and something wrench in his gut. Quietly he uttered two words he didn’t want to believe were his own. “I can’t.” ---------- The flickering candlelight was the only salvation from absolute darkness. Sat before it, his head bowed, he placed a finger over the dancing flame. Slowly he began passing his finger over the fire, cringing as it burned. The warm glow emblazoned across his half-faceplate crafted of steel, the colours swirling in the silver almost epitomizing the pain he felt as the rest of his face hid behind a veil of shadow created by his hand positioned before the candle. His mood was made worse not by simple torment, more so the fact it stemmed from that fateful encounter with a naïve 11 year old. Hessian’s character, that which he was, his very being was questioned innocently by a child and for the first time he was questioning himself too. Too swept up in bloodbaths and violence he was never in contemplation until it was thrown in his face at the first given opportunity. Until now he was Hessian, ruthless destroyer of men and king of the dark gimmicks since the death of his own bastard father, Clyde. He was the Mercenary of Satan. Brought into creation by the vile hand of the infamous Vampir Nosferatu and unleashed upon the world. He was the man who had slain hundreds. He was the man who nailed his own tag team partner to a hanging crucifix and watched with glee as it hurtled through the canvas of the ring destroying Azrael Cain forever. He was the man who dragged the bloody carcass of his own mentor Vampir Nosferatu to the top of the OSW Jumbotron, thrusting a knife deep into his back and watching as the supposed immortal plunged deep down into the stage, never to move again. He was the man who bludgeoned “The Reverend” Jon Hull into eternal slumber with the force of two steel stairs from ringside. He was the man who took his own life and laid in wait for almost a year in order to finally pounce on the unsuspecting burned man Clyde, finally sending him to hell in a gory demise featuring the removal and annihilation of the demon’s heart… …He was that man…until now. What had changed? Where had the veil been drawn between fantasy and reality? He racked his brains going through past and present trying to determine where the constant had been interrupted. Surely he was overlooking some minute detail that provided the answer. He had to find it should he wish to avoid being subjected to apparent accusation by the boy and father earlier in the day. Glancing across the room at the grandfather clock he listened to the forlorn tick…tock…tick…tock of time passing by. Noticing the time, Hessian turned his gaze towards the bathroom door and winced. It was then that he realized what had changed… …Nothing. The walls seemed to crumble all around him as in his mind the answer formed in grotesque fashion. The great Hessian, a 9th generation mercenary, spawned by the greater evil to forge a path of death and destruction, powered on by a sinister bloodlust the likes of which could not be quenched. This was the dealer of fate to the immortals. This was the Mercenary of Satan. This was the Hessian the fans, the people knew. Up until now this was the Hessian that Hessian knew. Now, he was a miserable and lonely man sitting in the dark contemplating his existence and waiting for the dull chiming of the grandfather clock that would deem it time to accept fate. Staring at the clock as though it were his worst enemy, Hessian followed the hands as they slowly but surely made their way around the face, losing track of time slightly until the chiming boomed from within. Hessian sighed and without hesitation got to his feet and walked the short distance to the bathroom, dragging his feet along in some kind of protest, though no one was around to argue. Opening the door he entered, heading straight for the medicine cabinet and stopping before it with his hand poised ready to open it. Looking into the mirror for a moment he stared at his reflection, running his hand down the steel of his faceplate before opening the cabinet door and reaching in, taking hold of a bottle that required the grasp of his entire hand around it and removing it from within. Closing the door, Hessian opened his hand out with the bottle before him and read the name as he had done many times before. Lanoxin. It was at that moment everything seemed to click, and for all the wrong reasons. Why was he so desperate to find the change in his past that rendered void the opinion of the boy and his father? Why was he unable to pinpoint a time he could accept what they had said? His reality had been questioned, yet neither he nor they were wrong. What was the point of this long-winded worry and doubt? How many dark gimmicks were not of supernatural origin? How many required prescription drugs just to keep on stepping into that squared circle night after night? How many were so firmly rooted in their beliefs that they had completely ignored their physical limitations and state of reality? None it seemed…except Hessian. He was thankful those thoughts belonged only to him. No matter what he thought he was still the Hessian that everyone feared, the hell raising, war waging Titanic Satanic Insaniac that was gunning down everyone in his path. Popping the lid off of the bottle of pills, Hessian shook out enough for a single dose. Twisting the cap back onto the bottle Hessian placed the pills back in the cabinet and stared at the tablets for a moment before tilting his head back and allowing the handful to slide into his mouth. Cupping his hand under the tap he let water splash into it, and with enough to assist with the swallowing was moments from gulping it all down when he heard a knocking at the door. “Who ish it?” he said, juggling the pills between his teeth and contorting his lips so as to prevent them from falling out as he spoke, while keeping the handful of water from seeping through his fingers into the sink and down the drain. “What the fuck do you mean ‘who is it’? I’m the only other one here except the damned cleaning lady coming in ever so often…and you KNOW she ain’t gettin’ anywhere past the bed-…” was the disturbingly sultry reply by what could be none other than Minion the Imp. Hessian sighed and quickly drank up the water from his hand, clenching his fists and swallowing hard. He stayed rigid for a moment as the concoction of pills and water barrelled down his oesophagus, then swallowed again before splashing a little more water on his face and drying his hands off on the wash towel, turning his head back to the door. “It’s open, what do you want?” he said firmly. Minion waltzed in, holding a cordless phone and covering the mouthpiece with his impish little hand. “It’s for you…” he said, whispering slightly. “Some guy in Detroit calling about a debt you owe him? Didn’t leave a name…” Hessian was puzzled. Taking the phone from his Imp comrade he thought nothing of what the call could be about. Little did he know that what would follow would lead to his past being unearthed and far bigger problems being created for him. With doubts of his being plaguing his mind, the call he was about to take would not help in the slightest…or would it? Only time would tell for the Mercenary of Satan. To Be Continued.
