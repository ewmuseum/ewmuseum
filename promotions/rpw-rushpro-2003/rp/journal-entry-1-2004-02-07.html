Date: Feb 7, 2004 February 7, 20045:06 pm I rockHey everyone, RPW's very own Chris Storm here. I decided to keep a journal for my road travels and stuff. Some cool stuff normally happens on the road and between shows. Thought I would keep this journal incase anyone ever cared to read. Its Saturday which means Adrenaline is tomorrow. I for one am not looking forward to it. I'm hoping I can get in there and get it over with. Thanks to our laughable owner, Little Al, I have to wrestle not one match but two! Can you believe they treat a huge star like that? I deserve better than this. You don't see that bitch Karina Wolfenden getting booked double or our oh so great World Champ, Gideon Poe, do two matches in one night. I guess that just further proves my point about how I should be a champion here. I work harder than both our Eclipse and World champion. Lucky for me I get a chance to go one step closer to actually getting a title! I advanced to the second round of the Mid South tournament after beating that idoit Matt Jackson. I face Totakoxe Salvador this week as my first match. I know I can rip him a new one. I still owe him a good ass whooping after that handicap match anyways. The best part is Malphas and Tessa Windsor have to face off this week as well. When I beat Tota I get to face the winner of them in the finals. I'm hoping its Malphas, it would make our Best of 5 series go much easier.Speaking of that Best of 5, I think I showed the entire roster last week that I am the true Last Great Technical Wrestler after that Malphas match. You should have seen the look on his face and that slut Chryste's face. Man it was priceless when they saw I was wrestling Greco-Roman and Freestyle. Malphas could not even stop me. I know I can take this series in no time. When I do that forces Chryste into a match against me at the PPV!Oh I hope all my fans had fun this past week. I stopped by a local gym to practice and next thing I know people are swamping me asking for autographs! Ah I know you guys are nothing much mindless sheep that cheer for whoever they tell you, but man it was cool having so many people want my autograph. That hasn't happened to me since back in WIW. I might make it a regular thing if I can. I know the front office wants the wrestlers to make more personal appearances. At first I wanted to tell them all to fuck off but after this week, I dunno it might be cool.Well I gotta run, I am on the road on the way to a WMW show. I told them I would wrestle that loser from FAW, Jacob Adams, on their show. I'm using my new T-Mobile sidekick so I can hopefully keep up this journal more even from the road. Man I love this thing its so cool! I have the internet and email plus AIM. Hmm maybe I should start my own mailing list? The Perfect List? I dunno, maybe!Later losers, I gotta do what I do best and suplex some no namer through the mat!Current Mood: CreativeCurrent Music: 'Rock Superstar' by Cypress Hill
