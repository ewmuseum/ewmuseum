Celebratin'

15 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15, 2004 Scott felt good for the first time in a long time. He hadn't known what Alyssa would do until it was over. He hadn't been sure if Gabe could carry his end. He hadn't known a whole lot heading in and to be honest, and as tough and cunning as Rampage may be, he wasn't really that smart of a man. Now that he was a tag team champion, he knew a few things. At the moment, life was good. He sat on the barstool, his left elbow propped up on the bar with his big hand wrapped around a rather large shot glass. His other arm rests around the shoulders of Alyssa and off down the bar a bit was Gabe, sipping at a green bottle of... well, don't tell him this, but O'Doole's. Scott turned and rapped the bar with his knucles to call over the barkeep. 'Another vodka?' the fat, bald man asked. 'Or another Sex on the Beach for your girl?' 'She's fine. Give me a tequila shooter,' he replied. He watched the clear liquids pour into the glass and grabbed a napkin, covered the lip of the glass and smacked it on the bar. Scott tossed a few dollar bills on the bar when he heard a familiar sound. 'Sizzucka please! I'll kizzill you with my lizzittle fizzinger!' he heard Gabe shout. Apparently he'd reverted to his... shall we say 'gangsta' persona. Incredible. The big black man he was talking to was even less impressed. Gabe's antagonizer was big, but he wasn't anywhere near the size of a Manbeast. He stands up. bringing his arms down to his side and walks a few steps up behind Gabe. 'You better bizzack the hizzell up, sucka!' Gabe shouted, still on his track. That was apparently enough, as Gabe's big opposer grabbed him by the shirt and pulled a fist back. Surprisingly enough, at least to him, an even bigger hand clamped down on the grabbing wrist. His eyes tracked up and met the hard face of Rampage. That actually took care of that, but Gabe apparently couldn't resist a last shot. 'You bizzetta rizzecognize! GABE LIFE!' Rampage turned to find another bit of annoyance: there was a drunkard hitting on Alyssa. Scott's face split in a wolfish grin. 'Hey, honey,' the man slurred, 'why don'tcha come home with me?' 'Get away from me,' was her only reply. 'You won't like what happens if you don't.' 'Oh yeah, like what, ya lil'...' his words cut off when the same big hand came down on his shoulder. Scott spun the man around. 'Before I happen,' was all he said. With his free hand, he shot back the entire shooter and hooked the little man's belt. When the man flew through the front window, that was time to go. Gabe followed the man out with the semi-appropriate scream of 'Gizzeronima, bitches!'. Scott and Alyssa came out through the door, and they all headed for the old Jeep Wrangler sitting in the parking lot. Scott threw the door open and literally tossed Gabe into the back, boosted Alyssa into the shotgun seat and ran around to pull the driver's door open. The roar of the engine and a spray of gravel sent the Wrangler soaring out into the road and out before anyone could catch up. Inside, Gabe turned to rap his schtick out the back while Alyssa laughed. Scott's face was still fixed with the wolfish grin. There were worse ways to celebrate. And the night wasn't even over yet.
