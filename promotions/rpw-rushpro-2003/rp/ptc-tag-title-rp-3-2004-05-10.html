PTC Tag Title RP 3

10 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 10, 2004 To sit there and think that nothing was wrong at that very moment was a crime. Discomfited silence had filled Scott Matthews’ room for the past hour and the only sound that escaped was that of his breathing. Granted, Scott was okay now and he was simply gathering his strength Gabriel Brown simply let it all go in one ear and out the other. The thing that racked his 19-year-old brain was mental health: both his and Scott’s. Finding out all these new things about the goings-on of Alyssa Sanders and her beau and discovering all your assumptions, your quarrels, your insecurities were superfluous all along certainly didn’t help matters. Everything had gone so well. But then Alyssa came in and it started a whole sequence of events leading up to now. They fought through legit hardship not fabricated by any clever booker with an angle idea. This was real life and right now, it was kicking the fuck out of Gabriel, Alyssa, and Scott. Just when Invincibleness~! had earned a shot at the PTC Tag Team Titles, it seemed God, Allah, Buddha, Jesus; whatever mystical deity actually reigned supreme decided to take a big fat dump upon them. And in about five minutes or so, said deity would continue to unload. Gabriel Brown sat back in his chair while Alyssa was taking a quick nap. This whole episode certainly took its toll upon the young beauty and her mind eventually called her back to the Land of Nod. He’d been sitting around, doing next to nothing in this room while staring at Rampage. When he woke, he’d get up and they’d all leave, hopefully a little happier. With a bit of luck, the three would put aside all petty differences and just work together. But, hey: what’s a good story without that extra bit of conflict? Well, think back a couple paragraphs to that little comment about taking a dump. Well, that mystical deity is lighting a match now. Case in point: Mrs. Matthews, Scott’s mother and little sister, Stacy had heard about the incident when Gabe phoned them with the bad news. The two had come in to check on Scott, who was simply resting at this point. Gabriel took notice of Mrs. Matthews, wearing a black knee-length dress with a white blouse and black high-heels. Stacey had on more casual attire, ratty blue jeans, blue t-shirt, white sneakers. The mother-daughter tandem also had matching worried faces for their son and brother, respectively. “Is he all right?!” Mrs. Matthews said, frantically. “Yeah, what happened?” Stacey chimed in. Gabriel stared at them, then cleared his throat, relieve to give the mother and sister of Scott the good news. “Well…he’s gonna be all right. He woke up a while ago; he’s merely resting now. The alcohol poisoning isn’t debilitating him anymore, so when he gets up, hopefully we can go. We need to concentrate on our PTC Tag Title match for now.” While Stacy stood over her brother’s bedside and watched the big lummox take in large breaths, Mrs. Matthews didn’t look too happy. She had a look on her face that could only be described as a mixture of anger and sadness. Gabriel immediately took notice of this when he glanced her way. “Is something the matter, Mrs. Matthews?” he said, genuinely concerned. “How can you sit here and talk about your damned wrestling when stuff like this happens, Gabriel?” She said, accusingly. Gabriel stammered. The question had caught him off-guard and he was clearly not prepared to answer such a thing with a coherent response. “Uh…I…Mrs. Matthews…Kathy…I…I didn’t mean to imply work came…” “I don’t want to hear your damn excuses,” she said, bluntly. “You know, when you phoned us about this whole deal, do you know how worried I got? Do you have any fucking clue what you put me through with this crap, letting my only son go out and putting himself in such danger. You are irresponsible and all this comes back full-circle right to you, Gabriel.” “Kathy, you have to know I’d never…” But that was about as far as he got before Stacy had to give her insight to this situation. “She’s right, you know?” She forcefully added her voice into the conversation. “It’s ALL YOUR FAULT!” Now she screamed it out. “YOU! YOU! YOU! YOU’RE THE REASON HE’S IN HERE! YOU CALL YOURSELF A FRIEND, YOU MORON?! HOW THE HELL COULD YOU DO THIS?!” Alyssa was a light sleeper, even though she had been through everything just as rough as Gabe and Scott. The difference was she didn't feel like she was bearing the guilt of that Gabe felt like he had to shoulder. She came around slowly and looked up, seeing two other ladies she had only met a few times. “Kathy, Stacy,” she began. Big mistake. They rounded on her. “And you,” Mrs. Matthews began, “you supposedly care about him. How could you let this happen? You call yourself a girlfriend? What is WRONG WITH YOU?!” The whole degenerated from there. Gabe fired back, trying to defend himself. Alyssa was not only grumpy the way you get when you just wake up, but she was personally insulted. Both Mrs. Matthews and Stacy were flinging insults in ways that belitted even the Invincible One's usual tirades. To give a more medical update on Scott, the alcohol was out of his system. He was sleeping, resting his body and had an IV drip that was re-hydrating himself in order to manage to get his body repaired from the damage. There weren't any ill effects left anymore, so it was merely recovery phase. Or it was until this whole thing started. The IV stand flew past everyone and slammed base-first into the door. That stopped everything cold. Everyones' eyes went wide as they turned towards the bed. Scott sat up, his body bare to the waist where the sheet had fallen when he sat up. He ripped the needle out of his hand and threw it and the dangling tube to the floor. “Is everyone fucking done yet?” he asked angrily. The big Manbeast sat there, his eyes burning. This was Scott back and in shape. He wasn't still recovering, he wasn't still down on his back, he wasn't struggling to survive anymore. Rampage was back and he was pissed off. “I can't believe you two sometimes. Think about this for a damn second!” he added his own yells, cutting through the air with their deep bass. “You honestly think this has much to do with them? It's because I screwed up, you hear me?” Apparently they did. They sat there in shocked silence while Scott grabbed his clothes and shot a hard look around the room at the other people. He kept the sheet around his waist and marched into the bathroom attached to the room he'd been lying in. He emerged a few minutes later, looking like a bear coming out of hibernation. “Guys, we've got a tag title match to get to,” he said simply. “I don't think so!” Kathy Matthews stated. “You are not going anywhere, Scott, you are coming home!” “No, he's not,” replied Gabe, turning on her. “Who are you to be deciding this? You let him get...” she started again. “And you, you... hussy!” Stacy threw at Alyssa. “EVERYONE SHUT THE HELL UP!” as Scott again cut the air. “Will you two QUIT?!” he roared at his family. “You will quit blaming Gabe and Alyssa for what happened, you understand me? I'm going to this title match for one reason. It's not for the belts, it's not for the prestige, it's so I can send the big, fat bonus home and maybe Stacy can get a car.” Gabe and Alyssa knew very well what Scott had intended, but his family was speechless. He glared at them, his eyes burning. “I don't see where you get the idea I can just stop instead of going on to the next match. I go to get you guys the money you EAT ON, you hear me?” He turned towards the door, his eyes still burning. Gabe and Alyssa moved to follow him. It wasn't like Scott to leave his family without saying something. “You know,” he growled, “this wasn't their fault. This was the same thing that happened the day dad died. I'm providing for this family and you all can just deal. If you don't like how I do it, that's just too fucking bad. Go home. I sent a check last week.” With that, Rampage marched out. He didn't care how he felt, he didn't care how his body had been ravaged. He was back on his feet. He and Gabe had a match to win. He had a job to do. Being surrounded by plastic tubes and monitors wouldn't help any, but getting back in that ring, that would be the best therapy in the world.
