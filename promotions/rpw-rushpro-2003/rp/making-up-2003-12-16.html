Date: Months before The X closed... Akiyama wasn’t a hard man to find. A near seven foot tall 339 pound Japanese man tends to stand out in society. I had heard that he was working for a Californian based wrestling promotion, The X, with old acquaintances of his. Raizo Tenzan and Yuka Hamada… a pair from PURE who had taken a turn in The X with two rookies, Lotus and Reivyn Mikona. Together dubbed as the Saikyo System, they had a scheme plotted to rape the profits out of this foolish mortal, Jason Marah. At the time, none of that concerned me... I only was concerned with my father. -=-=-=- She found alone in his hotel suite that night in San Diego, the balcony windows drawn open and curtains fluttering inwards from a gentle breeze. It was dark in the room, and there was no sight of him from the doorway. The door was cracked open, giving Cyclonica the entrance she needed. She was dressed in a pair of black four inch high heels and a black thigh-high skirt, a tight black blouse buttoned up to the third button, long black hair in a ponytail. As soon as she stepped through the door, she closed it, locking it behind her. Then she turned back, walking slowly into the living quarters of the suite. Cyclonica looked into the living quarters, finding no one sitting at the sofa. She walked towards the bar, picking up a bottle of a deep, rich red wine. As lightning flashed in the distance, she caught the image of the nVg in the bar’s mirrored walls. Cyclonica spun around, finding her father sitting on the edge of the bed dressed in black pants, a tight black muscle shirt, his hair hanging low over his face. His hands were folded as he glared ahead, his silver eyes hidden in shadow and hair. Cyclonica stood there silently for a moment, not knowing how to speak. “…Father…” but she found the words. He didn’t make a move, or respond. She took this as leeway to pour herself a drink into a crystal glass, holding it as she slowly stepped towards the bedroom door. “Father,” she called out to him again. “I’m relieved to see you after so long. I’ve missed you.” Nothing from him. She sipped the wine, keeping her eyes on her father. She knew what he was capable of… what he would do to her if provoked in the slightest way. “I know… there are things between us now that we should address. First, I apologize for what started this whole thing. But I had a just cause at the time.” She was only a few steps from the doorway. She took another drink and continued. “You weren’t listening to me anymore, daddy,” her voice had grown vulnerable, a strange trait to hear from such a devious woman. “I was trying to help you, but you were only doing what you wanted to. No, you were doing what the people wanted YOU to do. They were telling you the wrong things… and all I wanted to do was help you get refocused.” She reached the doorway and paused. Akiyama still remained where he sat, not moving an inch as he burned a hole into his daughter. She drank more wine, lowering the glass by her side slowly. “After that, I couldn’t stop worrying about you. I couldn’t stop thinking about you.” She stepped into the bedroom, closing the door behind her. As soon as that door closed completely, Akiyama loomed to full height, Cyclonica pressing herself against the door quickly, almost in fear. “Daddy please!” she pleaded, strange behavior for the Divine Prophetess. “I only want to talk! I have no harmful intentions planned! Please… hear me out.” Akiyama’s body heaved in and out, heavy breaths from anger towards his daughter. But slowly, he sat back down on the edge of the bed, arms hanging down rested on his thighs. Cyclonica stepped away from the door slowly, setting her glass down on the floor before moving in closer. “When I first couldn’t stop thinking about you, I thought it was just lingering hatred for what you did to me. You betrayed me by listening to the people over me… and you betrayed yourself and what you believed in. I wanted to make you pay for forgetting… but I soon forgot about revenge… but never about you, father.” She drew closer to him, now only a foot away where she stopped. “It became an obsession almost… I couldn’t go a few days without wondering what you were doing, if you had realized the error of your ways, or were you still walking the people’s path blindly.” Cyclonica drew closer, now only a few inches from her father. She could see the whites of his eyes, the anger that filled him, fueled him, made him live… “All I wanted to know… is if you were still the being I knew so long ago… are you still my father, the Neo Void God?” She threw herself into his arms, burying her face into his large shoulder. Her arms wrapped around his neck as she stood there pressed against him. Akiyama stared ahead over her shoulder, his eyes still filled with darkness, though they were bright and silver. “Can you ever forgive me, father?” Her voice was muffled as it came from pressed against his body. Akiyama didn’t speak… but slowly… his hands moved up, and pressed against her lower back. At first it was stiff, and soon it began to relax, and it soon became a clear embrace. Cyclonica pulled her arms back from around his neck, resting them on his shoulders as she brought her face from his shoulder. She brought her hands to the sides of his face as she leaned in, her voice turning to a whisper. “… I love you, daddy…” Her lips met his, a peck not lasting more than a second. But there was something within that small kiss, something that sucked the very breath out of Cyclonica… something that knocked the wind out of Akiyama. She kissed him again, this time for longer. Cyclonica broke away, her breaths heavier as she wrapped her arms around his strong neck and straddled his hips. Akiyama’s hands pressed her closer, his daughter’s lips parting as she started to get more involved with the kiss. They both became lost in the passion… in the lust… in the love… -=-=- “I remember how I fell down on my knees and prayed before the god… clasping my hands tightly and bowing my head… subdued moans of thanks and love… I knew that if I had only bowed down in front of him, he would give me the pleasures of my desire. I wanted to use him… but I was wrong for thinking that. I am his servant, his Divine Prophetess… his own daughter. If I were to serve him with all my heart, I was to let him use me as he desired… allow him to thrust his will into me, and he will allow my abilities to flow freely into his lap and lead him to a climatic victory. “Blood is thicker than water… but semen tastes better than the both of them… “So says… the Divine Prophetess…”
