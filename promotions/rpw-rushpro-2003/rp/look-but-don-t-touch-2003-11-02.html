"Look, but don't touch"

2 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 2nd 2003 She's very friendly with the camera. It likes her. She likes it. But I don't like it when he looks at her that way. She stood against the canvas backdrop, in that little outfit. Dancing. Swinging. Posing. It's not wonder I picked her. Athletic, strong, feminine. A clash between the eye candy that most females in the business simply enter to be, but yet encompassing the skill that the near-androgynous vulgarities of "women" that occasionally grace the business carry. Beauty and the beast in one innocent little package. The way she moves sizzles with an underlying sultry message, yet on the surface she is as pure and wholesome as can be. Of course, I think she couldn't look any better. That little top of hers with the mesh, the strappy armwarmers and gloves, the skirt, tights and boots. Those sunglasses add a cool, classy touch to such a delicious little cake. But it's a cake only I can nibble at. Lumi… my Lumi… is very much like poison ivy. Look, but don't touch. Men have wandered up to her with little but the most basic of Neanderthal instincts on their minds. From the most primal attempts to take her to the sophisticated, rich charmers, all who try to take her from me will meet the same result. Failure. Those who persist will find out the true sting of the poison ivy. And right now, I don't like how that cameraman is asking her to pose. "Lift your nearest leg up a bit more, show some more thigh." He says. While I don't object to her showing off her looks, that pose was a little too much. "Okay, hands behind the head, move your body forwards." Continues the lecherous little rat. Look at him… grubby white shirt, hanging out. No tie. Even his fly is half open. Makes me wonder. Cigarette in one hand. Enough grease in his hair to keep McDonalds open for months on end. And he's just enticing her into revealing more. It sickens me. But I promised Lumi that I'd let the publicity shoot go uninterrupted. No matter what he does. "This is a bit… too sexy, isn't it?" she asks. She has every right to. She's sitting down, facing her right. One leg on the floor, the opposite hand touching the inner thigh, her head tilted back. "Don't talk, just smile, or better yet, pout!" he snaps. People of his class should treat my Lumi with more respect. But she does as she's told. Although that's my privilege. The collar and chains she wore in The X. Symbolic of my dominion over her. But she no longer wears them. We are now symbiotic. She represents me, I give her the strength to carve her own legacy. But she is still mine. "Okay… stand up and put your arms out." He commands. Her skirt is a little too high at this point. It's too dangerous for me to yank it down. The cameras see me, even if people can't. And he took a photo of her. … Unforgivable. ~ The shoot went well. I don't normally pose like that, but I was only posing how I did for a second. It's all done now. The cameraman said he'd give me some of the photos back for future reference. It was kinda embarrassing. The one he gave me back was taken at the wrong moment. I knew I should have yanked the skirt down. But he just kept on telling me to keep posing. He told me it was going to be the one that would look best. Well, I wrestle, not model, so that one can stay behind closed doors. I'm just glad about that man not getting involved. He gets too jealous when I'm around other guys, except Marah. I suppose we both owe Marah. But anyone else and he goes nuts. I thought he'd go for the cameraman. I had this bad feeling in my stomach when he showed me the photo, but nothing happened. I think I'm the last person to get a photo shoot. Which is kinda relieving now it's over. It reminds me of when you're in school and everyone has to go into a room for something and there's a big queue outside. You're the last one in it and you see everyone going in and coming out, clutching their arm or something. Then it's your turn. But this time… nothing bad happened. I'm just glad. I think that man may be a little more… social now. ~ So I left the studio after taking pictures of that chick. Just had to get the real money maker shot… she was so easy to get into place, snap. Got a few for RPW, plus a few for my private collection. But you know how the saying goes… what you gain on the swings, you lose on the roundabouts. I never thought my luck would be this bad. She leaves, then the camera falls onto me. I rigged up the tripod myself. Then it falls onto me like someone pushed it. Smashes my teeth. Now I have to see the dentist and hope this chip doesn't become permanent. That's also two hundred bucks of camera down the toilet too. But that's not the end of it. I had my car tuned yesterday, paid cash up front for it. Horn won't stop blowing. All I did was send it in to check the tracking on it. Then the radio goes crazy as I'm at the lights. Every time I try to turn it off, it sparks at me. People on both sides give me the finger and tell me to can it. Then the lights change and I can't go, no matter how hard I step on the gas. I finally get going and the gas won't let up… speed picks up and up and up. It's like the car has a mind of it's own. And the steering fails just as I'm headed for a wall. If I didn't bail when I did… So I lose the photos I had of the purple haired chick, my camera, my car and my teeth in the same day. Things can't get any worse. At least, that's what I said before the explosion. 40% burns. Skin grafts needed. Morphine's not stopping the pain. Can't even see. I'm beginning to wonder exactly what I did to deserve this. I wish that someone, a cop, a doctor, even God himself… who did I piss off to deserve this??
