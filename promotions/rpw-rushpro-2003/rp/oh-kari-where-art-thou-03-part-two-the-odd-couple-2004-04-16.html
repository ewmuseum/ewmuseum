Oh Kari, Where Art Thou? #03 : Part Two - The Odd Couple.

16 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/4/04 It was, for want of a better word, awkward. Either Jeff Ward was finally loosening his collar, or he'd just found the perfect way to annoy Karina... In return for the countless times her own will had ruled over the letter of RPW's law. 'My way?' she echoed, almost disappointed that he'd found a way to anchor himself within her apartment. Still wrapped around an over-sized cushion, Karina's emerald eyes peeked over with suspicion, trying to judge Jeff's next move as he sat uncomfortably upright. 'Well, my methods obviously don't meet your approval,' Ward commented with a shrug, about the most relaxed motion his body had made in months. 'So I'm always open to a new approach... Maybe I'll learn something.' With her voice muffled by the cushion, Karina muttered out of Ward's hearing range. 'Yeah, like where the door is...' mumbled her frustrations, wondering why the world was so eager to disturb her peace. 'Oh, joy,' then came false sincerity as her head was raised, 'fun, fun, fun here this afternoon.' 'You don't respect my job at all, do you?' Jeff then bluntly asked, putting Karina on the spot... A place she was far from comfortable. She may have been apathetic, and admittedly selfish, but she wasn't cold and heartless. For all Karina knew, Jeff could be working himself towards an early heart attack to put food on his family's table, or to help pay for a parent's care. 'I don't even know what you're job is!' she then honestly answered, her voice softening as advantage in the conversation drifted away from her. 'What is it you actually do?' Ward took his job seriously, even if others didn't. Despite their physical training, wrestlers lacked discipline, and had little to no business sense. Even staff were usually veterans of the ring, thinking their prowess in the squared circle would translate to a Gordon Gecko-esque success behind a desk. They thought demographics were something that played on a video-tron. His job was just as important as any main-eventer. His job sold shows far more than any commentary team. His 10 hour days should surely earn him more respect than the guy who helps put the ring up... But only he could see it. To everyone else, wrestling was about pads, ropes, and canvas. Not cufflinks, pin-stripe, and tie-clips. 'My job is to make sure everyone in RPW gets the best pay-off possible,' came Ward's admission, pride evident in his voice. 'Not everyone can afford a place like this, and only have to worry about where the snow will be best. I give them a little extra money for their kid's college fund, or a chance to pay their mortgage off earlier. Everything I do is for RPW, and the money doesn't stay in Mr. Kannon's pockets, you know.' There was little Karina could say in reply to that. Beneath the all-business demeanour of Ward, he was just another guy, doing his job... And doing it well, admittedly. He was also right, something that blocked off nearly all of Kari's arguments. She was privileged, although she always got 'you don't belong here' stares in any first-class compartment. For every dismissive remark she made about publicity seeking, there were probably 50 Indy stars who'd beg for such a chance to raise their profile. 'Exactly,' she then appealed back, having found the smallest hole in his statement to creep through. 'I'm not every other wrestler, I'm not after a big pay-off... I'm only in the whole Cruiserweight deal because you entered me... Remember? So can't you just forget about me? Let me do my own thing? Give the time, give the promotion to someone-- well, someone who cares, I guess...' Usually, Karina's words flowed with confidence, thought out before they were even prompted. But, in appealing to Ward, they appeared to be improvised, pulled from the air around her in an attempt to relay her feelings. 'All you had to do was say so,' Jeff's frank reaction went, making it sound as if Karina had been fighting an invisible foe. 'Maybe show up to a meeting and say you don't want to do it? Rather than leave me waiting for an hour.' It had once again been turned back on her, something Kari once more looked uncomfortable with. 'So how would you prepare for Dawn then?' Ward then enquired, seeing that no words were forthcoming from the Wolfenden camp. 'We never see you training at the Halton, you haven't given an interview since I've been involved in RPW, you barely seem to show interest in any match that doesn't involve Magnum Wolf...' 'I just...' Kari began, pausing when she realised she didn't really know the answer. Her normal life just filled the gaps, her lifestyle itself keeping her fitter than most of those she shared a promotion with. 'I just... Well, do what I do, I guess.' Jeff still hadn't gotten his answer, and just sat, wondering what she meant, while his expression hinted 'expand'. 'When the bell rings, I care, when it rings again, I stop,' her explanation began, realising that she wasn't going to make a great job of explaining it. 'I'm not a wrestler, I'm just someone who... Well, likes to wrestle, if that's what you even call what I do. I show up, do my thing, and leave. I do that for RPW, and I’ll do it for whatever extra-curricular stuff you put me in for. It's me, it's who RPW signed, and it's who you'll have to deal with.' Rather then feel the urge to combat Karina's entrenched stand-point, Ward instead seemed to appreciate her unwavering stance. Everyone had their own style. 'I'm not going t-... Why are there X-Rays on your wall...?' Ward then stopped in his tracks and enquired, bemused... And a little freaked. He knew Karina wasn't exactly the kind of girl to go home, crawl in to bed with hot mug of cocoa and digest a good book, but he didn't think images of broken and cracked bones would plaster her walls. It was rare, if not unheard of, for Ward to be distracted from business talk. Had he and Karina finally found a point and place where they could lower their guard? Rather than carrying the look of a woman who'd been 'found out', Karina's face lit up fondly. 'They're not just X-Rays,' she beamed, proud of the films that even had their own light-box built into the wall. 'They're [i]MY[/i] X-Rays.' That didn't exactly do much to calm Ward's turning stomach, especially as the 2 jousting bones he was observing were meant to be one. 'That's your head?' he then enquired, ill-at-ease as he looked inside her body. 'Yup,' the proud owner of the skull chipped in, taking a few seconds to admire the art she'd made from her resilient and quick-to-heal body. 'And my ankle, and my knee... Oh, and my left arm and shoulder... And my spine. Twice.' Ward's concerned glare wasn't broken by Karina's awkward smile, that she hoped would snap him out of it. He kept waiting for her to laugh and chuckle, 'gotcha,' but it didn't happen. 'Wow...' was all Jeff could exhale. 'Or as I call them,' Kari continued, knowing each one like the back of her un-broken hand. 'Switzerland, Austria, France, Japan and Liechtenstein.' 'You're very... Odd,' Jeff confessed, his words not sounding insulting, almost as if it were a compliment. 'You sound like my Dad,' was Kari's instantaneous response. After all, it wasn't the first time somebody had made the comment. She may not have gotten her indulgent, uninterrupted, and relaxing day... But in a rather corny-sounding way, she got much more. She saw the man behind the grey pinstripe, she got a glimpse into the real him, and for an ever-enquiring mind like the K-Wolf's, finding a real person under a business-like shell was always satisfying. 'So do you prepare for many big matches by just lazing around?' Ward then asked, adjudging that Kari has hardly been exhausting herself before his arrival. Before, such a line could have been taken as criticism, or a scathing, dismissive comment towards Wolfenden... But there was a light edge to Ward's tone, as if he wanted to finally slouch and relax, but was just waiting for the invite from Kari. 'Y'know, this place doesn't have a dress code,' Kari quipped, nodding towards Ward's tie. Feeling rather over-dressed in his surprisingly casual mood, Ward took a giant step and un-did his top button, before loosening the tie just a little to open up his collar. 'Make yourself at home, why dontcha...' Kari then added, jokingly, surprising even herself by her encouragement of Jeff to relax. It would be hard to explain what they did for the hour before Ward left, since they didn't really do anything. Not all conversations have to have a topic or purpose, as sometimes it's just nice to bounce words back and forth with someone you hardly know. It's like taking a sneak peek at a living, breathing encyclopaedia... You learn something, relevant or not. Ask Karina in 10 years what happened on the afternoon of April 14th, and she'd never be able to tell you. Ask her about Jeff Ward, and her answer would be instantaneous. 'Not the guy I thought he was to begin with...' Maybe come next week, Ward will have his defences back up, retreating behind his grey, authoritative ways. It wouldn't matter. For one day at least, he was human... No different to Karina, no different to Dawn McFarlane.
