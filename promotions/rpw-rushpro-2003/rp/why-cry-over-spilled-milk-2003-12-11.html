Why Cry Over Spilled Milk?

11 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/03 There comes a time in everyone’s lives where they must learn to accept what is reality and move on. For me however it seemed that this moment was forever set on repeat mode. To me reality is almost beginning to seem unreal. After all, shouldn’t reality be something that remains constant? It shouldn’t be something like my career and life during the past year. I mean who else there besides myself can claim they raised three children, two of who were taken away by an insane mother who kills one and returns the other. Then on top of that be accidentally married to the one person they cannot stand on this earth which leads to the one they love leaving and the company they work for closing suddenly and unexpectedly which sends you back down to the bottom after you worked so hard to get up to the top. It doesn’t end there though. Imagine having great hopes for future only to be hired by a federation who sees absolutely no potential in you. In hopes of making things better, you stay with that federation for four months in which the only thing good that comes out of is an annulment which ends the marriage between you and that husband which you have finally begun debating whether or not you can tolerate. I know it may seem too crazy and chaotic to believe but for me this has become a way of life I have begun to accept as reality. To me nothing is impossible. I’ve seen nearly everything that shouldn’t have happened happen. Perhaps that is why everyone but me was surprised as to how Chris reacted to my arrival in RPW. It certainly left everyone questioning what happened between the two of us that would cause this type of hate. However to tell the truth nothing happened between us. I don’t know what caused Chris to behave this way. My only assumption is that once you become a man whore, you’re always a man whore. The way I see it is that the love between “The Schmoopies” never existed, at least not a mutual love. I won’t deny that I loved Chris. I loved him with all my heart. However, I was apparently too blinded by love to see his motives. I should have known that the only thing I was to Chris was bragging rights. The explanation as to why he proposed was simple. What better way to use and abuse a girl’s trust than to marry her and tell her you never cared? For the first time in my life, I can actually say I am now thankful for the interruption of P.W.A. during our two attempts to become a married couple. The only thing I can really say now is that I am still confused as to how to feel about Chris. After all, maybe he really did care. I don’t know any guy that would go as far as he did just to get a laugh, but then again if he did care would he have just spat in my face the way he literally did on Adrenaline. I guess I really was what I never wanted to be. A victim of a love that never existed. Yet after all that happened on Sunday, I still hated it every time I had to step into the ring against Chris. Sure, I probably should have had enough anger built up in me to take out him and his new little pet without a problem, but my mind continued to remind me of all he did for me. The constant flowers, candy, gifts and taking a beating for me. He couldn’t have done all that just for the sake of a cruel joke. It almost seemed as though Chris had been brainwashed. It seemed the only logical reason as to why he wasn’t the Chris I had known and loved. But whatever the reason he hated me for now I guess I would have to swallow the tears and accept things the way they were. After all there is no use crying over spilt milk now is there? So what is the sense of crying for someone who doesn’t care for you, especially when you have a friend like Jacko Rust watching your back.
