Conflict of Self: Part II - Origin Of The Species

21 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-04-04 “Angelus Von Kelsig?” The voice on the other end of the line was gruff and worn. Hessian was slightly apprehensive as he listened to the heavy wheezing of whoever was calling him, waiting for him to answer. Thinking for a moment, the Goliath opened his mouth to answer only to be interrupted by the impatient caller. “Hello? Angelus Von Kelsig?” “Yeah, yeah…this is Angelus…” he replied. “This may come as a bit of a surprise to you Mr. Kelsig, but-…” The voice on the other end of the line was suddenly cut off and forced into silence as Hessian piped up. “…Wait, how did you know I was here?” he asked, his curiosity piquing and rightfully so. As far as Hessian was concerned the only people who knew he was staying in the hotel were the important people at RPW. Even people looking to get a hold of him from the PTC Global Title Division had to go through the offices at RPW to get in contact with Hessian. How then did an anonymous caller acquire knowledge of Hessian’s whereabouts? The caller paused for little more than a second to get their story straight before continuing. “My friend, there are television adverts, posters, promotional vignettes and all manner of advertisements showcasing the upcoming Primetime Central Global Title match included in which is yourself. It merely took one of my more avid wrestling fan-like accomplices to identify you, thus leading us to this conversation…” came the reply, which as vague as it was made a little sense…to anyone except Hessian. “Well listen here, friend. The only people who should be calling me here address conversations a little differently than you, so unless this is of vital importance I will bid you adieu…” Hessian said languidly. With everything on his mind the last thing he needed was some jackass prank caller. “It IS of vital importance Mr. Kelsig! I’m calling about a debt that is yet to be paid. Your father Jack Kelsig borrowed a lot of money from me some time back and I still await repayment!” The determination and persistence of this caller seemed to suggest a legitimate purpose however as he yelled out on the other end of the line. Hessian’s jaw was as close to dropping, as it would ever get. His father’s name was mentioned, his deceased father. Never before had anyone mentioned any member of Hessian’s family. He had never made any mention of them after all, and after that fateful night in October, 1996…he never wanted to mention it… ---------- Life was good. 25yrs old on this fine day and living the life of luxury in the family mansion. Everyone came to visit. Aunts and uncles and grandparents and cousins and nephews and all the rest attended the birthday party for the Kelsig lad. There was no particular reason for the massive celebration, the family were simply close-knit and at every special occasion added to the festivities by praising accolades and achievements held by each member of the family. On this day however all was not well. Angelus should have been celebrating a new contract to work alongside his father as well as his birthday, and indeed he did. His father however had spent the entire day looking over his shoulder as it were, worrying about something. Only Angelus realised this, but confrontation only brought his father to lie. “I’m just making sure everything’s going ok for your birthday, son!” was the idle reply Angelus received each and every time he asked. With that he shrugged off the worry and went about celebrating. Much of the day passed with joy and laughter as the entire family enjoyed party games including a piñata filled with dollar bills. A barbeque in the evening was the prelude to the opening of the presents. Much of the family looked forward to this as what should have been a mouth-watering feast turned into a banquet of barely edible food thanks in part to Angelus’ uncle who couldn’t cook, but mostly the blame lay on Angelus’ father who once again was not concentrating, and seemingly jumpy. Questioning only lead to more lies, and soon the family was hurried inside the house for Angelus to open his presents. Of course he received everything he had hoped for plus a little more. The keys to his father’s old Cadillac were the favourite with a $20 gift voucher from his grandparents taking up the rear as least appreciated present. Afterwards everyone enjoyed a tipple from the bottle of whiskey his uncle had given him, and Angelus happily joined in family conversation, while glancing at the clock until it would be time for him to head out for the night with a few friends. He quickly began tiring of the family as they babbled about this and that, and only the constant look of worry upon his father’s face stopped Angelus submitting to the foul tipsy state he was in. Still he waited patiently for 11:30pm and his escape from his now-boring family, most of whom had thankfully retired to bed for the night. As the clock struck 11 o’ clock however, Angelus stared at it as the chime inside seemed to make a muffled smashing sound. Getting up and shaking off the tipsiness, Angelus checked on the clock to make sure it hadn’t broken, and surely enough everything was going like clockwork. He thought for a moment and heard another smashing sound, followed by a thud directly above him! Thinking nothing of it other than one of his relatives drunkenly stumbling around, he collapsed back into a chair and sighed, waiting for time to pass. Little did he know that the smashing he had just heard was a deadly Molotov cocktail soaring through one of the windows upstairs, and landing in the hallway before the staircase had rendered escape impossible. What followed was the brutal murder of Angelus’ family. Some died from smoke inhalation, some died screaming as the fire cooked them alive. Hearing the screams Angelus raced to the stairs, only to be forced back by black billowing clouds of smoke. He had no choice but to leave the house, to get out before he too was burned alive. He was running on instinct, backing out through the front door and tumbling down the front steps, too confused and scared to think. Angelus watched, as the house erupted in fire, destroying everything within. Surely he would have passed out at that moment hadn’t he heard the laughing from behind. Looking back, Angelus set eyes upon a man…a man with an evil smile upon his face and a small army of goons surrounding him. A man that had just murdered his family… ---------- Hessian was silent, holding onto the phone and grimacing as he thought back to the night it happened. The silence proved a frustration to the man on the other end of the line who was quick to interject, snapping Hessian out of his forlorn recollection of memories he wished he’d never been cursed with. “Mr. Kelsig are you still there?” he asked innocently. “………Who are you and what debt are you speaking of? My father was never in debt…” Hessian asked, trying to hide the shakiness in his voice. Something he had never had to do before. He was dealing with thoughts he had never contemplated before nor had any want to. He was scheduled to compete against five other men in a match for the greatest prize in Primetime Central. He was already being pushed mentally and physically would soon be pushed just as hard. More mental pressure was the last thing he needed, especially when it came from the darkest corners of his past. “My name, Mr. Kelsig…is Jim Carlisle. In 1996 your father Jack Kelsig came to me and made a withdrawal of $15,000. He fully intended to pay it back of course, the only problem was I don’t think even he knew when he was going to pay it back. After his unfortunate passing it was obvious I would never receive my money. Then a couple of weeks ago one of my accomplices made the connection between you, Angelus Von Kelsig, and your father Jack Kelsig. Being a blood relation Mr. Kelsig you are entitled to pay off your father’s debt…and I expect it paid back in full.” Jim Carlisle spoke with a hint of aggression in his voice. Was everything this man said true? He thought back to all those times his father would leave on business trips, and after he returned made so many changes to the shopping budget and luxuries and so forth. He couldn’t believe it but the facts seemed to be there. Hessian was almost lost for words. “…What are you-…did you have anything to do with his death?” Hessian asked, not thinking of what he was saying. Carlisle chuckled while Hessian gripped the phone tight, feeling taunted by the man. “Heh, I assure you Mr. Kelsig your father’s untimely passing was nothing to do with me. I do not operate like that…you have my word. I just want my money back, and unfortunately it falls to you to pay me back. I apologise but it’s the way it works.” He replied, treating this as nothing more than another business transaction. To Hessian this was more important; this was personal. This was his life. For now though Jim Carlisle’s words sounded honest, and so Hessian sighed and continued. “Well…what makes you think I even have $15,000 to give you? What even gives you the right to ask me for that money?” Hessian said, trying to trick Carlisle. “Mr. Kelsig have you ever heard the expression, “Don’t bullshit a bullshitter?” I’ve done my research and with your success in the wrestling world I find it very difficult to believe you would have trouble coughing up 15 grand…” Carlisle broke off for a moment, and listening carefully Hessian could hear papers being shuffled. “…Especially when taking into consideration your salary when you worked for Old School Wrestling. Now unless you’re spendthrift, which I very much doubt…you must have money lying around doing nothing somewhere…what am I saying? Mr. Kelsig this was your father’s debt! Do you really want to dishonour your father by ignoring something which was such a burden to him? Surely to pay off his debt would be to put his soul at rest?” he said in an almost preachy manner. Hessian sneered at the mention of the soul and cleared his throat. “Do not patronize me Mr. Carlisle, and do not try to sway me with talk of dishonour. If what you say were true my father would not have dishonoured his family by turning to a damned loan shark! Now the money I have, the money I earned is going to stay just that, mine. If you want $15,000 you’re going to have to find it somewhere else. You’ve obviously done without it with no trouble for the last 8 years, I’m sure you can go without for-…” “NO TROUBLE?!” Hessian was interrupted and surprised as Carlisle’s voice boomed down the line. “No trouble? Listen here Mr. Kelsig…I run a business. I give money to make money. Do you realize just how long it took to make up for those lost earnings? Your father’s inability to keep his word meant I had to find money elsewhere. The only problem was that I couldn’t find money elsewhere. Mr. Kelsig you may find it interesting to know I was forced to take out a loan of my own…a loan that I spent the better part of three years trying to pay back. Do you know what that did to me? Do you know what it did to my family? You have no idea how this affected me, yet you feel comfortable in accusing me of inconveniencing-…” “OK! I apologise Mr. Carlisle it was rude of me to assume…” Hessian said, interrupting him before the loan shark completed his life story. The phone call was frustrating to begin with, now it was just bearable and no more. Wasn’t he talking to a loan shark that knew where he was when the rest of the world didn’t, Hessian would have hung up by now. “Ah…excuse me Mr. Kelsig. I shouldn’t have snapped like that. You have to understand I need the money, and like I said you have to be the one that pays it to me. Do you understand?” he asked in a more polite manner. Hessian knew what kind of person he was talking to however, and would not be swayed by the lies hidden in Carlisle’s voice. “Like I said Mr. Carlisle, I don’t have your money. I can’t help you. Now if you don’t mind I-…” before Hessian could continue, Jim Carlisle butted in with words Hessian should have laughed at…but instead found a cold shiver run down his spine. “You know it would be really unfortunate if you couldn’t make it to your Global Title match, would it not?” said Carlisle. At this point, Hessian would have laughed and hung up. Then that night or perhaps the night after when Carlisle came to exact revenge on him, he would feel the wrath of Eternal Damnation. His spine would chill and his gut would wrench as Hessian picked him apart both mentally and physically and left nothing but the splattered blood on the walls and a sickly smell of fear in the air. That is what Hessian, the Mercenary of Satan would have done. Instead he cocked an eyebrow and held back a sigh of disbelief and perhaps also worry. Thinking for a moment, the Goliath replied. “Are you threatening me? Mr. Carlisle you have just spent the last five minutes telling me how you never got your money in the first place due to my father’s passing…and now you threaten me? You’re either hypocritical or just stupid…” In saying this, Hessian was defending himself as best he could. The realisation of who he was and what he wasn’t was quickly dawning on him, and rather than fight it and stand up for who he was…Hessian was lying down and admitting defeat. He was accepting who he really was. Thankfully he still stood up for his right to wrestle. In his mind he may not be the immortal dark gimmick who commands the apocalypse or the monstrous and evil mercenary to the dark lord Lucifer himself…but he was damn sure still a wrestler. “Mr. Kelsig…Angelus. I simply said you would not attend your Global title match. I made no reference to your father or his passing or any connection to you. That being said you won’t make it to your match…if I don’t get my money. Do you understand?” “Listen. You don’t scare me. Threaten me all you want, but if you try anything I’ll-…” Hessian was determined in his reply, but stopped dead as Carlisle uttered words that struck Hessian hard. “Or what if something happened to your little Imp friend there? He looks very happy chasing after the cleaner ladies and receptionists here…we wouldn’t want to end that happiness would we?” “………You’re in the hotel?” Hessian asked, now in utter disbelief. Indeed, Minion had left the room a short while ago which only added to Hessian’s worry. “Why of course I am…where else would I-…” Jim spoke but was once again cut off by a shocked Hessian. “Wait, I was told you were in Detroit! How the hell can you be here?” “Oh you must have been mistaken. I told your little friend I was from Detroit. Now Mr. Kelsig…do we have a deal? Or does your little friend come with me?” Hessian listened carefully as in the background the animalistic howling of Minion erupted as he chased after the ladies working in the hotel. Something in his stomach contorted tightly as he heard his friend and manager’s muffled yells of “Come here you sexy little beasties! Minion wants to swat yo’ ass! WOOHOOOHOOHOO!” close to Carlisle. Hessian didn’t know what to do. It was obvious if he didn’t comply Minion would be in great danger. It was becoming scarily obvious also that Hessian could not burst into the lobby and rip Carlisle apart in a blinding rage of hellfire and apocalyptic carnage. After his recent revelations, Hessian was being faced with an entirely human problem. He could not kill or maim his way out of this one. This was a human problem and had to have a human solution. The root of this problem? Money. The solution? Hessian did not know. He was running on empty and had little to no choices but to comply. Sighing in defeat, the Goliath gave his reply. “…Ok, tell Minion to bring you up to the room. We can talk more in person.” … The path of destruction was being set. Stood in his way now, the incompetence personified of Primetime Central. Indeed, Hessian would be in full battle mode in preparation to wrestle the Global Title from Jesse Jamester and all other second-rate competitors jumping for a chance at the gold…wasn’t his mind still plaguing him. He had still not resolved the issue that had arisen from the incident with the young child and his father. Strangely though the incident with the two was not the cause, but rather a reminder. It all came about when the haunting began. Not believing for a moment that the burned man Clyde could be the perpetrator, Hessian hunted down the vampire Nerezza demanding he explain himself and his connection to the cryptic messages left for Hessian. A prankster in his prime, Nerezza shot Hessian down and then some. Speaking a truth Hessian feared, he was left shattered in the barren and empty hallways a broken man. His fate had been realized, and was now soon to be believed. The root of his fear however was not the humanity within, but the predator that would seek him out soon. If what Nerezza had said was true and he was not behind the haunting that the Goliath had been subject to…could the demon have returned? If the possibility were fathomable, Hessian would be in great danger. In a state of doubt and fear he was easy prey for Clyde, should the demon be hunting him down. Still, only Nerezza spoke the truth and as the one who helped carve Clyde’s heart from his chest, would surely not divulge the truth to the burned man. But what if he did? The world seemed to be ganging up on him. 5 men, supposedly the elite of their federation would try to defeat him for the Global title. Jim Carlisle, the man who was truly behind Hessian becoming what he is…or what he was…would be watching closely, ready to disable him shouldn’t his will be done after they met for the first time very shortly. Then there was Clyde. A creature that cared not for humanity or championships, but simply the suffering brought upon mankind. For now though, Hessian waited to find himself face to face with a creature not unlike Clyde. Although entirely human, Carlisle could and would dismantle Hessian as the burned man could, just in entirely different ways, as the Mercenary of Satan would soon find out. To Be Continued.
