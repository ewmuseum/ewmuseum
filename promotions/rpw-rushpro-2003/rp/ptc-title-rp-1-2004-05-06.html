PTC title RP 1

6 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6, 2004 You know, as far as people could remember, admitting weakness of any kind was possibly the hardest thing to do. Well, that and saying ‘no’ to sex, but that’s a whole other story. Right now, despite all that Invincibleness~! had gone through in their personal lives, somehow, they proved God wasn’t a big-ass meaniehead and have overcome challenges to get where they are now, that being the PTC Tag Title match itself. BUT…God did prove that he was at least a meaniehead from 9-5 when Rampage was stricken down with alcohol poisoning following then incident involving Alyssa, Gabriel Brown, and Scott Matthews. Gabe and Alyssa’s fighting was all that Scott could stand, they tried to get him out of the bar, but to no avail. He drank himself to dangerous levels and now here he was, as helpless as a newborn kitten lying up in a hospital bed with Gabriel and Alyssa never taking their eyes off him. I refuse to go into some long-ass description of what a hospital room looks like. You want to know? Dig up Hemingway and have him illustrate the entire scene to you. Why? Because he’s a good writer. Still don’t believe me? Take your eyes of this right now and go smack your face into a brick wall. Have a loved one, friend, roommate, SOMEBODY, take you to the hospital and get you checked in. That should do it. If not, may I suggest some tools or something? A good monkey wrench to the head is a surefire way of staying overnight, what with you being crazy enough to hit yourself just because an RP on the Primetime Central Forums told you to. They’ll be probing you crazy while trying to figure out just what the fuck hallucinogen you’re on. Once again, I digress. Perceptibly, it was a more solemn time for Gabriel Brown and Alyssa. And with them watching over diligently, Gabriel must finally put his demons to rest and stop his jealousy, otherwise Scott will just drink himself back here and next time, he may not wake up. “You know,” Gabriel started, somber tone and all, as he sat back in a brown chair next to Scott’s bed. “In all my years of knowing this man, I don’t think I’ve ever seen it get this bad. I mean, sure, there have been a few run-ins with police, but nothing so serious where he ends up in a hospital.” Alyssa Sanders, girlfriend to the behemoth passed out in the bed, had sat on the other side of the bed while wiping her nose with the 16th Kleenex tissue she’s gone through since this. “It’s hard to go through this,” she said, choking back another sob. “I…I…I just can’t stand to see him this way.” “Hey, hey, hey,” he got up and walked over to her. The man known on-camera as The Almighty Master Of Your Mom held Alyssa and tried to comfort her as best he could. He was no Rampage, clearly, but he made do with what he had. “It’s gonna be all right. I’ve seen this guy go through people in fights like a buzzsaw, this won’t keep him down, not at all.” She embraced him back as Gabe stood over her, her eyes still not leaving Scott’s form for a second. “What I’m really worried about, and I feel like the world’s biggest asshole from even mentioning it during such a clearly horrible time, but…the fucking hospital bill.” Gabriel let go of Alyssa for a moment and kicked hard at the wall out of sheer frustration. Yes, that dreaded hospital bill. An awkward silence filled the room momentarily as Gabe turned away and Alyssa looked over at her boyfriend. “I can pay for it,” she said quietly, but enough so that Gabe could hear her. He suddenly turned around with almost an offended look. “What the hell do you mean by ‘you’ll pay for it?’” She turned around in her chair to face her. “I mean I can pay for it.” “Since when, when the world is tilted over on its axis, Chris Storm can wrestle a good match, and money does INDEED grow on trees?” “No, dummy,” she said to him. “Since I don’t particularly wrestle right now, Kannon was nice enough to make me the head of marketing for RPW. Plus, I do happen to have some inheritance money when dad unfortunately passed away.” Okay, let’s back the fucking train up, Gabe thought. “You’re loaded?” “Well, not LOADED, per se,” she corrected him. “But I do have enough that we can pay for this no problem and not have to worry about RPW finding out about this incident. I promise.” “Okay, okay,” Gabe said, confused, as if she just called him a Snuffalupaguss for no reason. “Just what the fuck are you talking about? You mean…YOU…want to pay for something for once in your life? You ARE aware that you’ll actually LOSE money with this, aren’t you?” Alyssa looked back up at him. “You don’t know a damn thing about me and Scott going out, do you?” “Well,” he said, slightly agitated at the current circumstances. “I know that usually any money we make gets tossed down the drain because you and Scott go out constantly. That’s…kinda what started my little spiel back in the diner in the first place!” “No, no, no!” Alyssa shouted back. “That’s not how it happens at all, Gabriel. I’m the one that’s been paying for our dates. Our shows, our hotel rooms…YOUR hotel rooms, everything!” Okay, this picture wasn’t adding up at all for Gabriel. In his mind, he was going over many questions. Okay…SHE’S been the one paying for our stuff all this time? SHE’S the reason that we can afford half the stuff we have? Okay, now’s the perfect time to look like a big asshole. Way to go, fuckhead. “Please don’t tell me you’re joking…so all this time…this time you’ve been spending with Scott and myself…it’s all been YOU? And my reasons for being jealous were completely misguided? Fuckin’ perfect.” Gabe, defeated, took a seat in a chair and looked up at the wall, laughing to himself like he’d lost his mind. Yeah, watching a loved one in a hospital bed and finding out all the jealousy and hatred that you had for a third wheel was completely unnecessary was gonna do that to a person. “FUCK!” he shouted at top of his tiny lungs as he slammed a fist against the wall. “So all this shit, this fighting, him in the hospital bed…ALL MY FUCKING FAULT!” For a moment, it seemed like Gabriel Brown was in his own hell. It was he and his injudicious annoyance at Alyssa Sanders that caused the fighting which put him in here in the first place. Good fucking job, Gabe. And the kind of hell I’m talking about is bad hell. Like ‘You walk by a bunch of radios at Circuit City and you hear the song “Bia Bia” by Li’l Jon and the Eastside Boyz.”’ That kind of hell. He ran his hands through his hair, about to shed a tear at the events that had now just unfolded between Alyssa and himself. He felt like the worst kind of shit to ever exist. He was washed away in a sundry torrent of confusion and self-loathing. Gabriel looked over to Scott and sighed to himself at the fact that all this crap just had to happen between the three over the course of the past couple weeks. With Scott still resting, Gabe looked over to his best friend’s bedside and sighed to himself with Alyssa looking on. “You know,” Gabe said quietly. “You’re the lucky one right now, you big bastard.” He buried his face in his palms with Alyssa looking right at him, now worrying about the state of not one, but two people. What a fun day for Alyssa, yes?
